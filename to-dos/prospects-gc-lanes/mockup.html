Is this the best we can do? Is it intuitive and robust?

A is the honest minimum, and it answers the ask. Walked through as the assistant making the calls, and as the data under it, it has eleven weak spots. B fixes each.

Weak spot in AWhy it mattersWhat B does instead
Four tabs × four sub-tabsSixteen screens. Convert is a backfill tool for customers added from the Customers page; Activity would be three reports nobody compares.The new lanes get Follow Up and List. Activity is one report with kind chips. Convert stays where it is, Customers only.
A job is a sentence in the notesAn owner has several jobs over the years. The date that orders the queue is the job's bid day, and a sentence cannot be sorted, counted or handed to New project.A GC lead carries a jobs list (name, address, size, plans link, bid due). The queue has a Soonest bid order. Asked us ✓ hands the chosen job to New project filled in.
The demand is on another pageThe caller on Subs cannot see that Concrete needs 2 and Fire sprinkler needs 3. The bench's "Find 2 more" still has nowhere to look.A What the bench needs strip heads the Subs lane, from the bench's own rule (3 per trade), each trade a filter with its queue count. "Find 2 more" on the bench opens the Subs deck on that trade.
Converted ✓ on a subThe word means nothing there, and the press opens Add customer, the wrong record.Each kind's last press is named for what happens: Add to the bench ✓ opens Add a company filled in (vetting: new, as the bench expects); Asked us ✓ makes the customer and opens New project. The GC Customers tab's first step is already called Asked us.
Trades and range as notesThey cannot filter the queue, match a trade package, or fill the bench's fields on conversion.A sub carries trades (the bench's own trade names), range in miles and language, on the card as chips, in the list as columns, and straight into Add a company.
Nothing stops a doubleA company already on the bench typed again; an owner who is already a customer.Typing a name or phone checks gc_companies and customers: "Bexar Fire Protection is already on the bench · Open it". A GC lead may link to an existing customer (a trade customer who could hire us as GC) instead of making a new one.
The call script is a page awayv2.5556 wrote the script for exactly this call, and the deck does not show it.A Call script door on the sub's card, the company's facts filled in.
No sourceHiring learned which sources convert; the new lanes would start blind.Source on every new-kind card (a GC's sub list, PlanHub, a permit, a referral, the architect, drove by), and Source success per kind on Activity.
Conversion keeps no linkToday the customer is named in a comment's text, not a column. Add to the bench would be the same.converted_customer_id and converted_gc_company_id on the prospect, so the card and the list can open what it became.
A lead who texts has no kindMessages' Add to Prospects writes a customer prospect; a homeowner asking about a build is a GC lead.The sort card's Add to Prospects offers the kind. Default stays customer.
Lost bids go coldAn owner who gave the job to another builder is the warmest GC lead there is, and the Lost lane only keeps the record.Send the owner back to Prospects on a lost project's card: a GC lead with the job marked lost and a note of who won.

What A got right and B keeps: one page for all finding; one deck, one machine; four top tabs in the order the company finds things; the kind picked first; nothing changes for Customers or Hiring; one gate (prospects staff) for v1.

What B says no to: a Finding fold on the GC pages (two decks would drift, and the assistant who calls works one page); a kind filter instead of tabs (the caller works one kind at a time, with its own script and its own last press); one card per job (the person called is the owner, and two jobs would collide on the contact, the notes and the lock).

The owner's calls

  1. The tab names. Drawn as Jobs to GC and Subs, the owner's words. The cards say who the person is (Owner, Architect; Fire sprinkler). The other candidates were Owners and Trade companies; say so to swap.
  2. Who sees the new lanes. Drawn behind the one Prospects gate (dev, master, assistant, controller, an estimator with the flag). The GC team's own people may need Subs without the rest of Prospects; that would be a second flag.
  3. What Asked us ✓ does. Drawn as: make the customer, then open New project filled in. The lighter version makes the customer only and leaves the project for the day the plans come in, which is what the GC guide says today.
  4. The job list from day one. PR 3 is the biggest piece. For the first month a job could stay a sentence in the notes and the queue run on Coldest; the owner decides whether the bid-day order is wanted from the start.
  5. Lost owners back to Prospects. Drawn as a press on the Lost lane. It could be automatic the day a project is marked lost.
  6. By need on the Subs queue. Drawn as a third order beside Coldest and Never called. If the trade filter is enough, the third order can wait.
  7. The trade names. Drawn as the bench's own list with free typing. A fixed list is tidier and needs an owner to keep it.
Nothing here changes the Customers tab or Hiring. Every piece reuses a thing that exists: the deck, the bench, New project, the contact kinds. The one new table is the job list; the rest is columns on prospects.
