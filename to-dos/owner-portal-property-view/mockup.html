What is going on

1 · He is the customer — the GC is the payer
Umar Khan is the customer of record on all five Lenox Hl jobs (273, 858, 866, 881, 1009 — $30,387 open). Every bill is billed to the GC (bill_to_party = 'gc'). The portal lists what the viewer pays, so his balance is honestly $0 — and it then says “You're all paid up.”
2 · Sharing exists, but nobody ticked it
A bill can already be shown to the other party “for their records” (v2.3375): shown_to_party on each bill, ticked at Bill Customer or on the Bill tab. All 12 jobs here are off. Edit Job's Show the bills to … tick only covers the job's next bills — turning it on today would still show him nothing.
3 · The app has no sent notice on these jobs
Job 273's notice is awaiting approval (Sep 22) and 258's is a draft; this afternoon the closed months were noted as missed. No § 53.056 notice on either property is recorded as sent, and no filing exists. If paper went out, the app — and so the portal — does not know it yet.
4 · Even shared, the words would be wrong
The shared card tells an owner “Not yours to pay, and not in your balance.” After a lien notice that is the opposite of the letter, which asks him to hold back the amount from the GC and call us. And the portal has no word about the notice at all.
Terrell Rd, too: Syed Rizvi is the customer on 7 of the 8 jobs at 628 Terrell Rd — same picture. Job 258 has no customer at all (a different property record), so no portal could ever show it; set Rizvi as its customer.

A Your idea — a switch on the Pipeline row, next to the owner's portal globe

Only on a job whose bills go to the GC and whose customer is a different person — the owner. One click shows every open bill and the next ones on his portal (it stamps each open bill, not just the job's memory, so it works today). The popover says what he will see and offers the other jobs at the same property.
