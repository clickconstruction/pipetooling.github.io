2 · The amount — before and after

The owner: "if this number is set by the line items, perhaps we should not make it easy to change as a whole number and the user should have to go in and adjust line items." It is set by the line items (or the estimate the customer accepted), and today the box changes only the agreement — a typed $120,000 is a signed contract for $120,000 on a job that bills $123,600, and nothing says so.

The rule. The agreement's amount is the job's amount: the accepted estimate's total when there is one, else the line-item total. No typed override — the one case for one (an agreement for the original scope on a job whose line items grew through change orders) has not happened here, and adding a reasoned Agreement covers a different amount… later is easy where removing it once people lean on it is not. A draft that already carries a different typed number is flagged, never rewritten: the row wears an amber chip, the footer says it, Send all skips it, and one click puts the job's number on the draft. A sent agreement stays locked exactly as now. The full editor (⋯ More → Open the full editor) gets the same read-out, or the hole just moves one click away.

Is this the best we can do? — the first pass (2026-09-21) and what changed
  • The amount showed a third time. The first pass drew it big — 20 px — in the pane, while the list row and the document already carry it. The refresh's own critique of the pass before had been "the amount showed three times". It is now one line in the This job block, the same weight as the scope and the payment line.
  • A time-and-materials checkbox invented a field for a case the app already handles. A job with no amount already sends as time and materials, already wears the amber chip and is already kept out of Send all — and nobody asked to declare it on purpose. The checkbox and the new jsonb field are gone; the row reads what the agreement will say and where to add the number.
  • A differing draft was a warning box, not a state. The first pass never said whether such a row was still Ready — so Send all could still send the wrong number. It is now a flag in the same vocabulary as the others: an amber chip on the row, out of Send all, named in the footer sentence, with one click to put the job's number on the draft.
  • The hint line under the tabs repeated the chips. It also pushed the list down by a row. The reasons went into each tab's tooltip; the empty state keeps its sentence.
  • The header still repeated the counts. "109 without a contract · 82 need a look" plus a Drive clause with the In Drive count, next to tabs saying 109, 82 and 6. The header now says only what the tabs cannot: dollars of work, sent and filed this sweep, the floor, and checking Drive… until the tab appears.
  • The full editor was left with a typed box. Locking the sweep alone moves the hole one click away — ⋯ More → Open the full editor. Both get the read-out in the same PR.
  • "Fix email on the job" was drawn behind ⋯ More. A row with no email is the broken one; its fix should not hide in a menu. It stays in the open; More holds the editor and the one-job send.
  • Kept from the first pass: the read-only amount with its source and one door; Ready to send as the tab's name; the tabs scrolling sideways on a phone; the segmented ways as a real radiogroup; Preview PDF in reach.
