Contract sweep · two pieces drawn 2026-09-21, not built

The amount follows the line items, and the filter sits on the list

Two owner asks on Jobs → ⋯ → Contract sweep, from screenshots in one session. They touch the same modal but are separate changes and should ship as two PRs — the tabs are cosmetic, the amount changes what the office can do.

1 · The amount is the job's number, not a box

The owner: "if this number is set by the line items, perhaps we should not make it easy to change as a whole number and the user should have to go in and adjust line items." It is — the amount prefills from the accepted estimate's total, else the job's revenue, which the Job form computes as the sum of its line items plus rider fees. Typing here changes only the agreement, so a typed $120,000 leaves a signed contract for $120,000 on a job that bills $123,600 and nothing flags it.

Why it cannot simply be locked. A blank amount is a real option today — the box's placeholder is Blank = time and materials — so that becomes an explicit checkbox. A job with no line items needs a way to become Ready: add line items, or tick time and materials (today: type any number). And drafts already carrying a typed amount are flagged with a one-click way back, never rewritten silently. Drafts already sent stay locked exactly as now.

2 · The filter is the list's tabs, and each number appears once

The owner: "this area doesn't look great, can we improve it?" Three problems: the summary and the control say the same numbers side by side (109, 82) while the one that matters — ready to send — is only in the control; the control is a faint outline with no dividers, the unselected options read as plain text; and it filters the list on the left but floats over the agreement pane on the right.
