Contract sweep — ask the first question first

The sweep asks how to get a signature before it asks whether one is needed. Some of these customers already have a contract with us, sitting in Google Drive — and the only way to say so today is a small link in the footer, "Already signed? File it". This makes that a first-class door, in the sweep and on the job — and because the app can already read the jobs Drive, the sweep looks for the contract itself and hands you the link to confirm, instead of sending you to find it.

1 · The refreshed sweep — live: switch the two doors

The pane now opens with the job it is about, then one question with two answers. We already have one is a single field for the Drive link — already filled in when the sweep found the file. We need a signature is what the pane does today, tightened. Click J363 in the list for a job with nothing in Drive.

The first pass, and why it was replaced (2026-09-21)
  • It still made a person hunt. "We already have one" was a paste box: open Drive, search, copy, come back, paste — per customer. The app already scans the jobs Drive and matches files to these jobs with a stated confidence; that lived in a window under ⋯. Now the sweep runs it and hands over the link.
  • In Edit Job it drew a button that revealed a field. The ask was a field. When nothing is on file, it is just there.
  • Preview PDF was tucked into ⋯. Looking before emailing a $123,600 agreement should be one click.
  • The amount showed three times — the list, the pane header and the Amount box. The header lost it.
