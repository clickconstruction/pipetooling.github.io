State roll: who holds a license near us, drawn before it is built

A new tab on Prospects, beside Hiring and Permits · nothing here is built yet · every person, license number, company, phone and day on this page is made up.

What Taunya asked for, Nov 20. “I'm having an issue finding experienced plumbers and I really need these plumbers to be licensed … use that as a way to find people who were recently licensed or to find people whose licenses have just expired … collect the information of everybody who is licensed and then begin adding information to that.” On Nov 21: build it, and put it all under a new tab.
The one thing the state does not give. Neither roll carries a phone, a street address or an email for a person. State law has kept those private since 2023. The tab says who to look for and where they live by county and ZIP. How to reach each one is added by the office, by hand, and every row says so.

8. Still yours to say

  • The tab's name and place. Drawn as State roll on Prospects. It could sit under People instead.
  • Who sees it. Drawn for the people who hold the Hiring switch, and nobody else.
  • Apprentices. Drawn off. The state's file gives no age, and an apprentice can be 16.
  • How anyone is contacted. Drawn as a number a person adds and a call a person makes. No text goes out until OK to text says yes, the rule Prospects already keeps. A lawyer should answer before the first call.
  • Whether a person may be looked up. On Market the answer was companies only. Nothing here looks a person up.
  • Counties. Drawn as the five the permits cover.

9. The order to build it in

  1. The pull and the table. Plumbing, our counties, read every Monday. The Pulls view.
  2. Our own. Our people and subs against the state. Nobody is contacted.
  3. Everyone and the person's sheet. Search, the line of history, our notes, Leave off.
  4. Call this week. The tags, Pursue to Hiring, Not for us, the Reach block.
  5. Electrical and A/C. The same four views, turned on by trade.
