What is still to answer, and what it looks like

Diane's ask: make the Punch list page easy for a person to see the questions they still have to answer, and what a card looks like before and after it is built. This page is the static copy of the design canvas. On the canvas each frame also has a dark theme.

Today, as it is

The real app, drawn in the lens frame with invented data. One row is about 520 px tall at a desk and 1,111 px on a phone. What the card waits on is inside the Next line. The pictures are one press away.

The shapes

A is a third View, the cards that wait on a person, grouped by who. B is one card opened, with today beside what it becomes if built. C is A on a phone.

Is this the best we can do?

What is wrong with A, B and C, cause by cause.

  1. A answers the wrong question. The ask was the questions I need to answer. A lists fifteen rows for everyone, and each person has to hunt for theirs.
  2. A row says a status. Waits on the owner. His walk with one real superintendent. A person cannot answer a status. The row should lead with the thing to do.
  3. Two ways to answer on one page. Go ahead and Change sit beside the board's own Do, Later and Drop. A session would have to read both.
  4. A picture 54 px wide says there is a picture. It does not show the screen.
  5. Before and after only exists for work that is not built. Fourteen of the fifteen cards are built. There the honest picture is the built screen alone.
  6. Nothing says a picture is old. The My Bids screen changed the same day its pictures were drawn.
  7. An answered card stays in the list.

D · the refined cut

  • It opens on Waiting on me. The count rides the View pill. Everyone else's questions are behind pills.
  • The question comes first, as a sentence a person can act on, with one word in front that says what kind of thing it is: Look, Read, Walk, Decide or Use. The card's name comes second.
  • One way to answer. The board's own pick, which on a card with a question reads Go ahead, Change, Not now. Change opens the note. It is saved where Do, Later and Drop are saved today, with the name and the day.
  • One lead picture at a size that reads. See all opens the card's pictures page. A built card says Built. Work not built shows Today beside If built, and the mock-up side has a dashed edge.
  • An old picture says so: The screen changed after these were drawn.
  • Answered cards fold into one line at the foot.
  • B stays as the opened row.

Honest doubts

  • Me needs a rule. Each question has to name who it waits on, by role. Counsel has no login, so counsel's questions show to the office.
  • Change is not a pick today. The store holds Do, Later and Drop. A fourth value is one small migration. Without it, Change is a note with no pick.
  • A question on the card is one more thing to keep true. It has been written once for fifteen cards. Each later session has to keep it.
  • The lead picture is somebody's choice. A poor choice shows a dull crop.
  • D shows four cards a screen at a desk and two on a phone. Today's board shows fewer than two at a desk and less than one on a phone.
