5Is this the best we can do?

  1. An icon in the window's top row was considered. Those icons are destinations; a report is an act, and an icon needs a tooltip to say report. The words win.
  2. A button in the note composer beside Arrived, Leaving and Set % complete was considered. That row is the field's stamps and the office's note; a report has its own form and would make the row a third thing wide.
  3. The empty Reports pill reads as quiet text today, No reports yet on hover. With the button beside it, the quiet pill reads as "none yet, and here is how".
  4. The label is the Dashboard's Leave Report, so the field and the office press the same thing.
