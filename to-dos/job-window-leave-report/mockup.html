5Is this the best we can do?

  1. The first draft drew an outlined blue button at the row's end. It fought the pills it sat beside: a different shape, a heavier line, a label that did not say which pill it belonged to. The chip is the row's own shape, and it sits by the word it belongs to.
  2. The label shortened from Leave Report to ＋ Report because the pill before it already says Reports; the chip's hover word and its accessible name stay Leave Report, the Dashboard's label.
  3. A button in the note composer beside Arrived, Leaving and Set % complete was considered. That row is the field's stamps and the office's note; a report has its own form and would make the row a third thing wide.
  4. The empty Reports pill reads as quiet text today, No reports yet on hover. With the chip beside it, the quiet pill reads as "none yet, and here is how".
