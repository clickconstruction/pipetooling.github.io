Bid photos, and a Links column that is always there

The Bid Board's Links column draws an icon only when a link exists, so the icons do not line up and a bid with nothing shows a dash. The ask: a photos icon that is always in its place, grey until a photo folder holds photos, and maybe the same rule for the other links. Three directions are drawn. Each keeps the same column and differs in what a press opens.

Every bid, builder, person, count and file name on this page is made up. The photos are drawn tiles. Frames are 1,248 wide for the desk and 375 for the phone. Green dashes mark what is new.

Is this the best we can do?

The column holds up. The windows behind it are where a second look pays. Three things bothered me in A, and each one draws an alternative.

Five windows for one bid's files. A person who sets the folder often sets the plans next. In A that is close, press, a new window.

A blue link cannot be changed from the board. A wrong plans link sends the person to the gear and a long form.

A press that only makes an empty folder. Step 1 asks for a yes before the person can do what they came for. A person who says Not now has learned nothing, and a person who says yes and closes the window leaves an empty folder in Drive.

The owner's calls

  1. Which direction? My pick: C. The column as asked, one Files window, blue links straight out.
  2. Is the folder made by a press of its own, or by the first photo? You asked for a confirm. My pick: the first photo, with the button naming the folder it makes. No empty folders, one press fewer.
  3. Where does the photo folder live in Drive? My pick: Photos, under Team only, inside the bid's project folder. A photo saved from a text onto a bid already lands there. When the bid has no project folder, the app makes one in the company's shared Drive first.
  4. The bid folders made by hand sit in a personal Drive. Do they move into the company's shared Drive? The app cannot file into them as they are. My pick: move them, once the estimators are members of the shared Drive. Until then a photo waits in the app with its reason. This is card #240's open call.
  5. When a bid is won, what happens to its photos? My pick: nothing moves. The bid's folder already becomes the job's folder, so the photos ride along in Team only › Photos. Customer Pictures stays the job's own.
  6. Who may add a photo, and who may remove one? My pick: anyone who can edit the bid may add, a primary on their own bids included. The person who added it and the office may remove. A removal goes to Drive's trash with an Undo. It would be the first thing the app removes from Drive.
  7. Is the Bid submission slot drawn on an unsent bid? My pick: yes, grey. Five slots on every row keep the column straight.
  8. Photos only, or videos too? My pick: photos first. A video is held with its reason and can go in through Drive.
  9. Does the Files window replace Files & links in the bid's form? My pick: not yet. Both write the same columns. Retire the form's section after a month of use.
