Working hours

A mock-up, second pass, 2026-11-23. A dev or the controller sets a person's hours, a start and an end, in 15-minute blocks. Every name and figure here is made up. No wage or pay is drawn. Each screen is shown in light, then in dark.

Try it: the row itself

These two rows work. Days that share their hours are one line. Press the underlined hours to change the whole line at once. On the second row, make Wednesday a half day: press the hours, then Give one day its own hours, then Wed, then Half day, then Keep.

1. A half day on Wednesday, press by press

Tobin works five full days, 7:00 AM to 3:00 PM. From now on Wednesday is a half day. This is the standing week. A half day on one Wednesday only is not this: that is one day's move, or time off.

Afterwards. The two lines are edited on their own.

2. Where a dev or the controller sets them

People, the person's sheet, Pay & schedule. One new row, Working hours.

3. What the week reads as afterwards

The same hours, on the screens that read them. No pay figure is on any of them.

4. What someone who cannot set them sees

The owner reads the same lines with no press, and can open what the hours were before. The office has no Pay & schedule section, as today.
