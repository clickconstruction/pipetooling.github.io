Is this the best we can do?

  • My pick is B. Two bars on one scale show the two things the card compares, and the gap between them is the red part. Every number on the card is named and appears once.
  • The card goes amber before it goes red. An old percent is the usual cause of a gap. So the card first asks whether the percent is still right. It turns red only after someone says yes.
  • The crew's card speaks hours. A superintendent is never shown dollars, and he cannot check a money number. The office keeps the money on the Projects board and the Costs tab.
  • The card has a name. Job pace. A person can ask about it, and What is this? says what it compares and why.

The one question. When money is ahead of the work but the hours are not, should the crew's card stay calm? I would say yes. Materials are bought before the work they are for, and the crew cannot see or change dollars.
