Go anywhere: the search box opens a page, a Settings card, a guide or a window by name

The box the office already has, drawn beside the box as planned, for four searches. Today's rows never move. Under them, only when the typed words match one, come Pages, Settings, Guides and Punch list, three rows each and N more. Every row carries its role gate, so a person never sees a door they cannot open.

See it as The dev sees every door, the Punch list among them.

Every job, bid, customer, address and amount here is made up: Ridgeway Builders, Cedar Bend Holdings, J1418, B1418. The guides, the Settings cards and to-do #126 are the repo's own.
