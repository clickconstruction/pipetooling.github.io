4 Is this the best we can do?

The first pass, before the render. Each line is a path considered and the reason it was taken or cut.

Role sets the starting rules. Considered: attorney → each event on every matter, billing → the weekly digest where fees ride, paralegal → each event on only theirs, said in the pick itself. Cut. It adds a third thing to learn, it guesses wrong for half the firms (a billing clerk who wants each applied payment the moment it lands), and the paralegal preset lands on Only theirs, which hears nothing until the office names them. The two switches are two taps, and they are in the form now, so a preset saves nothing.

Role gates events. Considered: each person ticks which of the seven events they hear. Cut for now. Seven boxes per person is the wordy thing again, no firm has asked, and the digest already separates the money lines from the rest. It stays open as the owner's call 1, option C.

Drop the role. Considered. Cut: the office reads it on the Legal desk's list, and the Recorded by picker uses it to tell two names apart. It stays as a typed Title, optional, with one hint that says where it shows.

A timeline of the seven events. Considered: seven rows, a mark on the one that rides the digest. Cut: it is a list that tells. The same seven as subject lines in an inbox show the sender, the matter and the day, and the dim weekly digest only line at the foot says the one exception without a sentence.

One sample email only. Considered: the cheapest form. Kept, and joined by the inbox list, because one email shows the frame and the stop link but not the week, and the digest's point is that it is one email where the other rule is six.

The form is the row. Taken. The card the firm fills has the same fields and the same two switches as the card it becomes, so the first row teaches the second. The weekday and time appear under Weekly digest exactly as they do on a row.

Count instead of warn. Taken. Under Only theirs a count reads from the firm's own open matters: The office names Priya Natarajan as handling 1 of 3 open matters today. A zero is the warning.

The sample week is made up, and says so. Taken. The subject lines name sample accounts, never the firm's real matters: a sample Referral withdrawn: Cedar Bend Homes on a real matter would read as news. The handling count is the one real figure, and it is a count.

5 The owner's calls

1 · What is the role?

  1. A typed Title, optional, that gates nothing (recommended, as drawn). The hint says where it shows. The rules are the two switches in the form.
  2. A pick that sets the starting rules and says so: attorney · each event, every matter, billing · weekly digest, every matter, paralegal · each event, only theirs. The switches still show and still change. One more PR than A.
  3. Per-person event picks: each person ticks which of the seven they hear. A migration (a column of picks), the dispatcher and both desks. Two PRs more than A.

2 · How deep is the preview?

  1. The sample inbox and the first email opened under it (recommended, as drawn). The opened email is what shows the frame, the handling line and the stop link.
  2. The sample inbox alone, with a Read one › fold. Shorter on a phone, and the stop link is a sentence again.
