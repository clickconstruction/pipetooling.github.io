The same height as the five pills. Read left to right it is what happened to the material: blue came in, green is here, grey went out, amber is the gap. An amber badge counts what waits for a hand, the red one is the money that walked away this week. The map is a view of the green pill, at the row's end.

The badges come from the data, and they are the only marks on the row:

  • amber on came in: deliveries waiting in Unsorted to be put away.
  • amber on on site: boxes due a count, or a Can't find since the last count.
  • red on missing: the money that walked away this week. No badge when nothing is missing.

What a person gains: the row reads in one glance without reading. The colour says what kind of figure it is, the arrows say the order, the badge says where to go and how many.

What a person loses: nothing in height. The map is one press further in, at the row's end under On site, and a colour-blind reader leans on the words and badges, which is why both stay.
