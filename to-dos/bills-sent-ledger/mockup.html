Bills sent

The master asked for one place to see every bill that has gone out. He wants to know who got it and the minute it went. This page shows what he can open today, and the one list I suggest. Today on this page is Thursday, Nov 12. Every name and number is made up.

The short of it 1 · At a desk 2 · On a phone 3 · One bill's record What each line can say Where he finds it The calls

The short of it

The idea makes sense, and most of it already exists. My recommendation: do not build a new ledger. Build one screen, Bills sent, that reads the records the app already keeps.

Now

  • Every bill the app emails is already kept: who, the exact time, the address, and a copy.
  • It sits in Documents, under Sent copies, mixed with every other paper.
  • That list shows no amount, no address, no total, and no statement.
  • A bill that never went out is not on any list.

The master can open it today. Nobody would think to look there.

My suggestion

  • One screen with its own address. One line each time a bill goes out.
  • Each line says the minute, the bill, the job, the amount, the person and the address.
  • It says who pressed send, and whether the email arrived.
  • A bill with no send on record is shown at the top, not hidden.

No new record of the same sends. The first step needs no database change.

What the count found. I read the last 30 days of the live app, with every write refused. 39 bills went out. 36 went by email from the app, and all 36 have the full record. 3 were entered from Housecall Pro, with a day and nothing else. 9 more bills were made and never emailed. So the gap is the shape, plus three small holes. It is not missing data.

The calls Grace's and the master's

  1. Build it this way? One screen over the records the app keeps. Or a new ledger table, which I advise against.
  2. What a line is. Every send is its own line, and the total counts each bill once. Or one line a bill, with its sends inside.
  3. The gap strip. A billed bill with no send is shown at the top. Or it is left off the list.
  4. Who may open it. The four office roles that read Sent copies today. Or the master and developers only.
  5. The main door. The Billed header on Jobs, Stages. Or a line in the menu. Or the Dashboard.
  6. The Monday email. A block in the weekly Bridge email he already gets. Or no email, the screen is enough.
  7. By-hand sends. Add a press that says how a bill went and to whom. Or leave them as No send on record.
  8. Proof for counsel. Keep every delivery answer, not only the last. Or wait until counsel asks.

One thing only the master has. Which bill made him ask. I would test the first build on that bill before calling it done.
