Asked of it Is this the best we can do? five things wrong

  1. The same email shows up three times. Money waiting sits on the controller strip, the master strip and the dev strip. Roles are the wrong axis for a list: an email has many readers, but a reader has one day. The customer tab works because a homeowner meets each thing once, in order.
  2. It answers "what could a role get", not "what does Malachi get". Who receives a digest is set per stream on Emails & reports (recipients, cadence), not by role. A sample role is a guess; a real person is the truth — the customer tab already has that toggle (Sample · A person) and it should mean the same thing here.
  3. Subjects still say {{date}}. The point was to see the subject line a user sees. Sample data fills it: Money waiting — Tue Sep 29 · 27 unapplied deposits · $116,240.
  4. On day one the tab is mostly grey. 14 of 25 emails build their HTML inside the edge function over live rows, so nothing renders until each is lifted into a kernel. The draft hides that behind "built on the server". The tab should count it, like the customer tab's 36 rendered live · 5 open as the PDF line, and offer the honest fallback where it exists: Email me the real one (the streams already have test sends).
  5. There is no "when". Cards say daily or on event in small print. A team member's inbox is a clock: 7:00 crew day, 7:30 money waiting, Monday the week's money, Wednesday the GC ask. Order by the clock and the tab reads like the day it describes.
