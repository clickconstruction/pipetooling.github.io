Sub payment move / remove — the two pieces still to build

PR 2: the sub's portal · PR 3: Edit Job → Payments received

What already shipped (v2.3562)

On the office sheet (Jobs → Subs → Pay) every payment and backcharge row carries Edit · Move… · Remove (Edit and a ⋯ menu below 640px). Move lists the same sub's other sheets first and previews both sheets' paid and owed before and after; Remove asks why, with Wrong job → Move it instead, and can be undone for 30 days. Both write one row to people_labor_job_payment_events, and both sheets draw a grey trace line from it. The migration is pushed and the three functions are live.

What these two boards add

PR 2 carries that same trace to the sub's own portal, so a subcontractor who sees a payment disappear reads why instead of calling the office. PR 3 puts the same Move to job… on customer payments in Edit Job, where a payment landing on the wrong job has the same cause and no cure today.

Assumptions the drawing made

  • The portal shows that a payment moved or was removed, and who did it, but not the office's reason text — "Duplicate entry" is an internal note. Open question in the to-do: show the reason, or keep it internal as drawn.
  • A moved payment appears on the destination sheet's portal lines as an ordinary payment; only the sheet it left carries the grey line. Otherwise the same dollar reads twice.
  • The portal's ledger already filters to the last N days (buildSubPaymentLines, sinceYmd); a trace line obeys the same window, so an old removal does not resurface.
  • On the job side, Move to job… appears only on rows no sent bill has counted. A payment on a sent bill shows "unlink from bill #3 first" instead, because the customer was told a number. Stripe rows stay locked exactly as today.
  • The job-side amounts (J880, bill #3, the $2,400 check) are illustrative; only the row shapes and the lock states are drawn from the real table.
Matched to the shipped v2.3562 surfaces: the sheet's Payments table verbs and grey trace rows, subPaymentTraceLines' wording (Moved → 922 Michael Palmer · Taunya · wrong job), and the existing Edit Job → Payments received row actions (Remove, Unlink & remove, the Stripe lock).
