How a bill arrives, and the steps before the form

By email. Open the mail. Download the PDF. Upload it to the AP folder on Drive. Copy the link. Open the house. Type the form.

From the counter. The ticket comes back in the truck, or the tech photographs it and texts it. The photo lands on his Our team thread. Save it to Drive. Copy the link. Type the form.

On paper. The tray. Scan it. Upload it. Copy the link. Type the form.

The red steps are the ones no option below keeps.

AA Bills inbox on Materials the pick

A house that emails its invoices lands them on its own thread in the box the app already reads. The assistant forwards the rest with one press. The reader drafts a card from each PDF: the paper on the left, the fields on the right, every field marked read, guessed or typed, and one Approve that writes the invoice exactly as the form would. A card the reader could not settle is held with the reason and a door. The bar's button takes what is ready and lists what it held. Press a mark to read the why. The presses here work.

?Is A the best we can do?

  • Every field says where it came from. Read is green and quotes the paper. Guessed is amber and names what the app used. Typed is blue. A guess is never written silently, and the row keeps the marks, so a wrong one can be found later.
  • Approve is a person's press, and the write is the form's own. Job Summary, Held for suppliers, the aging heat map and the Lien desk read an approved draft exactly as a typed invoice. Undo deletes it as the form's Delete does.
  • The PDF is already ours. The box the app reads copies every file into our bucket today. No new Google scope, no Drive step, and the row's View opens our copy.
  • Four checks hold a card, and only four. No job named, a total that does not add up, a house we do not know, a bill that looks like one on file. Each held card says why and has a door. A statement, several invoices in one PDF, is held too, with each invoice a press.
  • Do what is ready. The bar's button names the count it will approve and lists what it held. A hold on one bill never stops the rest.
  • The lines are kept. Today's form throws them away. A later PR can say what a job's copper cost at a house this month, and C's match reads them without reading the paper again.
  • The one read that can pass and still be wrong is a guessed job. That is why a job is green only from a PO code on the ledger or an account reference on file, and amber from an address or a name, with the why on the card.
  • The new thing is a model call in one edge function, with a fixed answer shape, a key the owner adds, and cents a bill. The matching, the checks and the words are plain kernels with tests. Nothing else in the app calls a model.
  • Left out on purpose: approving with no person, reading paper from the tray (a Scan press on the Physical inbox deck feeds the same function, a small later PR), paying the bill, matching the bank charge.
