# The lien month: five zones across the top of the Lien desk (#235)

## The ask

The owner, 2026-11-01, in three messages, verbatim.

The policy: *"I think that Reminders should go out within the first five days of the month, Notices should go out the next five days of the month, and then liens should go out between the tenth and fifteenth every month. This is an office policy, not an app rule, but how can I have the app encourage this policy? Do you like this policy or do you think there's one better?"*

The bar: *"I think it would be kinda fun to have a bar across the top with five zones And the dates for those five zones, which is from day sixteen to day zero of the month, nothing to do, but for the other days the work from the above table and then we can show in those zones how many are outstanding that way It's easy to identify what we need to do and what the deadlines are. If this makes sense, do you wanna help come up with a mock up for this visual table of sorts? I'm not even sure where we should put this yet, whether it be in the lean desk or on the dashboard."*

The card: *"Save this as a punch list item because I think it needs more refinement, but it is a great idea and I do want to incorporate it."*

## Why the shape fits Texas

Every deadline in Property Code chapter 53 lands on the 15th. The § 53.056 notice for a work month is mailed by the 15th of its month (the third month after the work on commercial property, the second on residential), and the affidavit for that work is due on the 15th of the month after the notice's. So a notice mailed in notices week gets its reminder in reminders week of the next month, about three weeks later, and the lien follows ten days after the reminder. The reminder lands when "by the 15th" is close enough to be urgent and far enough to pay, which is better than the courtesy notice's day-7 clock (#231), which scatters the office's sends across the month.

The one weakness in the policy as stated is the lien window: days 10 to 15 leave nothing for a clerk's refusal, a wrong legal description or a weekend 15th, and § 53.055 wants the affidavit's copies sent to the owner and the GC within five days of filing. The recommended days leave two days spare and keep the 14th and 15th for whatever slipped.

| Days | Work | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 to 5 | Reminders (the courtesy notice, #231) | the lien is ten days out |
| 6 to 9 | Notices (§ 53.056, the run) | certified mail postmarked a week before the 15th |
| 10 to 13 | Liens (the affidavit) | filed with two days spare |
| 14 to 15 | Catch-up | whatever slipped, and the affidavit's copies out |
| 16 to the end | Nothing scheduled | what is queuing for next month |

The owner's own days (1 to 5, 6 to 10, 10 to 15) are the first thing to refine; the bar reads whatever the rule says.

## What exists today

- The Lien desk's **Do now** tab lists every step by its own deadline, inside 30 days, in one list: notices to draft, approve and run, affidavits, retainage, the sent notices' follow-ups. It says what is due; it does not say what this week is for.
- The **courtesy notice** (#231) is offered from day 7 after the packet and again every 7 days, so on the live desk the press shows on a different day for every job.
- **Letter two** (counsel's second certified letter) runs from day 10, overdue from day 14, on the same per-job clock.
- The desk's **§ Rules** sheet holds the house rules the desk already reads, so the days have a home.
- The **Dashboard** has cards per role; nothing there speaks of the lien month.

## The proposal

One bar, five zones, drawn in `mockup.html` beside this card four ways: on the Lien desk in reminders week, on the desk in liens week with a slip, as a Dashboard card, and on a phone.

**The bar.** Five zones across the top of the Lien desk above the Do now tabs, widths in proportion to their days. Each zone names its work, its days and its count, and reads by where today falls:

- **Ahead**: the count coming and its day, greyed. *10 coming · 3 to draft, 7 to approve.*
- **This week**: tinted in its colour, the count left and the count done, a thin progress line, a today mark, and one press that opens Do now filtered to that zone's work. *12 to send · 4 sent · Send 12 reminders ›*
- **Behind**: green with a tick when everything went. Red with the slipped count and the first job's name when it did not, and the slip carries into Catch-up.
- **Catch-up**: what slipped from the three zones before it, plus the affidavit copies to mail after a filing.
- **Nothing scheduled**: dashed, with what is already queuing for next month (*9 reminders · 6 notices · 2 liens*), so the size of the month ahead is never a surprise.

**Where it goes.** Both, in two sizes. The Lien desk is the home, full size, because each zone's press opens that desk's own list. The Dashboard gets a small card for office roles: one coloured strip, three counts, a link to the desk. On a phone the zones stack as rows.

**What it never does.** Send, file, approve or move anything. A window closing with work unsent is a red count in Catch-up, and that is the whole nudge. The owner's words: an office policy, not an app rule.

## What changes underneath

1. **The rule.** The five zones' days live in the desk's Rules sheet as the office's rule, editable by the owner, read by the bar and by the courtesy notice's clock. Where the sheet's rules are kept decides whether a migration is needed (a column on the house-rules row, or nothing if the sheet is already JSON).
2. **The kernel.** `lienMonthRhythm.ts`: given today, the rule and the desk's book, the five zones with their state (ahead, this week, behind), counts (left, done, slipped), the slipped jobs' names, and next month's queue. Pure, tested, read by the desk and the Dashboard both so they never disagree.
3. **The courtesy notice's clock.** `courtesyStatus` gains the rule: the press is due in reminders week of the affidavit's month, with the day-7 floor kept so a notice mailed on the 3rd does not get a reminder on the 4th. In between, the sent footer reads *reminder goes Dec 1 to 5* instead of nagging for three weeks. Letter two keeps counsel's clock.
4. **The bar** on the desk (`LienMonthBar.tsx`), the card on the Dashboard, the phone rows; each zone's press opens Do now filtered to that work.
5. **The guides.** `send-lien-notices-from-the-lien-desk` gains *The lien month* section; `send-a-courtesy-notice-before-a-lien` says when the press appears; the Rules sheet's guide names the days.

## Open calls for the owner (the refinement)

1. **The days.** The owner's 1 to 5 · 6 to 10 · 10 to 15, or the recommended 1 to 5 · 6 to 9 · 10 to 13 with 14 to 15 as catch-up. Recommended: the latter, for the two days of slack before the 15th.
2. **The reminder's cadence.** Once, in reminders week of the affidavit's month; or every month's reminders week while the notice stands and the lien is not filed. Recommended: once, since the lien goes in that same month and letter two covers the rest.
3. **Notices week.** Only the notices whose deadline is this month's 15th, or every notice whose window is open (a month's notice can go from the 1st of the month after the work). Recommended: every open one, counted, with this month's deadline ones leading, since an earlier notice collects better.
4. **Who sees the Dashboard card.** Office roles only, or the owner and the controller too. Recommended: everyone who can open the Lien desk.
5. **What the quiet zone shows.** Next month's queue (recommended), or nothing at all.

## Self-review

- Reuses: the desk's book (every count is already on Do now), the Rules sheet, `countedLabel` for each zone's press, the courtesy notice's status and the affidavit step's day.
- Risks: a rule stored per house needs a home and maybe a migration; the courtesy clock's change must not re-offer a reminder already sent; a slipped count must name the job, not just a number, or it is a nag with no door.
- Not in scope: automation of any kind; letter two's clock; retainage's own rhythm (the § 53.057 notice has its own deadlines and would be a sixth zone only if the owner wants it).
