What each zone reads

Ahead: the count coming and its day, greyed. This week: tinted in its colour, the count left and the count done, a thin green line for progress, the today mark, and the one press that opens Do now filtered to that work. Behind: green with a tick when everything went; red with the slipped count and the first job's name when something did not, and the slip is carried into Catch-up. Nothing scheduled: dashed, with what is already queuing for next month so the size of the month ahead is never a surprise.
