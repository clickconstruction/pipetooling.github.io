Retainage on our trade jobs is money owed to us

two variants: the owner's open pick

On our own trade jobs the GC holds back 5–10% of every pay application. The app knows the number twice — the saved AIA application's retainage_held and the Lien desk's lien_retainage_held — but nowhere does it show as money owed to us: AR lists billed bills only, so the held money has no line, no age and no release. The after gives AR a Retainage held section read from one source, drafts the release bill when the job closes, and ties the Lien desk's § 53.057 row to it.

Before Three screens each know a piece — none shows it as owed

  • Two numbers for one amount. Pay app #3 says $5,840 held; Edit Job's lien row says $6,400. Nothing reconciles them, and each screen trusts its own.
  • AR never shows it. Retainage is not a bill, so the money waits invisible — no line, no aging, no door.
  • The release is hand-made. When the job closes, someone has to remember the amount and type a bill from scratch.

After AR gains a Retainage held section — pick the one source below

  • One source, the other reads it. Whichever number the owner picks, the AIA window, AR and the Lien desk show the same held amount.
  • Owed and aging. Each trade job's held money is a line, aged from the day the job finished, with a door to the job.
  • The release drafts itself. When the job closes, the bill is drafted with the amount filled, through the existing bill flow, and the § 53.057 row links to it.

With A, C1385 shows $5,840.00 everywhere and the desk's drifted $6,400 is retired; a job that never saved a pay application (C1377) falls back to the typed number until one is saved. The to-do's opinion: A, so the window that produced the signed paper stays the truth.

With B, C1385 shows $6,400.00 everywhere — simpler (one typed box, no fallback), but the signed G702's held-to-date and the app's number can disagree, and someone must keep the box current per application.
