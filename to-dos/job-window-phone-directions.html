The questions for the owner

Where the three directions differ, these are the calls. The green words are the drawing's own pick.
  1. The tab strip. Every tab on one line that scrolls sideways (A, B), or the role's three tabs and a More ▾ (C)? One line that scrolls, with the ? and ✕ pinned.
  2. The icon row. Which doors earn a named press on the row on a phone, with the rest behind More ⋯? Calendar and Share. Money story, Supply house, the paid email, Send as a task, Write up a change and Edit go under More ⋯.
  3. The body's order. Keep today's order with the top tidied (A), or Where · Who · Now · the thread first with the rest folded under counted headings (B)? B, as the second PR, once A is on phones.
  4. Money on a phone. One line that opens the Bill tab, or keep the three tiles and the accounts chips? One line each.
  5. The bar. The same Status ▾ · verb · Note for everyone, or the role's main press (the crew's Arrived or Leaving big, Status under More ▾)? The same bar for now. The role's bar comes with C, if C comes.
  6. The kind chip. Does Kind not set stay in the phone's title, or move to the quiet line under it? The quiet line.
  7. The first step. A alone, as one PR? Yes: the tab strip, the title, the press row and the two folded lines, nothing below.
