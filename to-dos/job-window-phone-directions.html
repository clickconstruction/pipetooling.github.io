What changes
  • The name comes first. A title row like the desk's since v2.6539: the name and the status, a quiet line with the number, the customer and the kind, and at the right ✕ on the name's line with ⋯ under it on the quiet line, so the name and the status have the whole first line.
  • The seven tabs run on one line that scrolls sideways under it, 40 px tall, the active one kept in view, a fade at the edge saying there is more.
  • The header scrolls away. The title row and the tab strip go up with the page, so a thumb's first flick gives the whole screen to the job. A small ✕ floats at the corner the whole way down, and a scroll back up brings the tabs back. The bar at the foot stays put.
  • The icon row is gone. Every door is a named 48 px row in the ⋯ sheet, Share and Calendar first since they are the two anyone pressed, with the ? card as its last row.

  • The Street View banner draws on a phone as it does on a desk, under the tabs on every tab, Edit included, the address pinned on it, and scrolls away with the header. The street view ▸ fold is gone.
  • The photos are a banner: a row of 72 px tiles that flicks sideways, Add a photo the first tile, then every photo newest first with its day. A tap on a photo opens the viewer there; a tap on the first tile opens the camera on a phone, the picker on a desk. Today's three thumbnails and a + N tile go.

  • Nothing below moves. The bar is as today.
