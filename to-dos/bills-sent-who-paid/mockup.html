Bills sent: who paid, when, and what came in each day

The owner asked for two things on Bills sent. The leader should see at a glance who has paid and when. He should also see how much money came in from Stripe on any given day. This page shows the list as it is, the list with payment made plain, and a second view by the day the money came. Today on this page is Monday, Nov 23. Every name and number is made up.

The short of it

What I found

  • The page already knows when a bill was paid. It says so in small green words at the end of a line, beside Delivered in the same green.
  • The page reads how each bill was paid and never draws it.
  • A bill sent by text, or before Oct 5, says Paid since with no day.
  • Nothing on the page adds up what was paid. No tile, no chip, no word on the day band.
  • The list is in the order bills went out. No screen lists money by the day it came.

What I suggest

  • On the list: a stamp under each amount. It says Paid with the day and how, or Not paid with the days out.
  • On each day band: how many of that day's bills are paid, and what is still out.
  • A second view, By day paid: a month of days with what came in on each. Press a day to see its total and its bills.
  • The chip Stripe narrows the month to Stripe money alone.
  • One page, one switch at the top. No new door to learn.

What it costs

  • Four small builds, one after the other.
  • No new table. The page reads the payment rows the app already keeps.
  • No new access rule. The same four office roles open the page.
  • One fix is the owner's call: a Stripe payment made in the evening is filed on the next day.

Why paid does not read
  1. 1It is the last of five small things. Paid sits at the end of the grey row, after the pill, the sender and the delivery word.
  2. 2Two greens say two things. Delivered is about the email. Paid is about the money. They look the same.
  3. 3The amount looks the same paid or owed. The eye goes to the bold amount first, and it says nothing of payment.
  4. 4Nothing adds it up. The band counts what went out. No tile and no chip says what came back.
  5. 5Some lines have no day. A text, a Housecall Pro bill and an older email read Paid since. The day is found through the sent copy, and these have none.
  6. 6Part paid says nothing. A check for half the bill is on record. The line is silent until the bill is paid in full.

One more that cannot be drawn. The page reads how each bill was paid and throws the word away. For a Stripe payment the word it reads is empty anyway.

What changed
  1. A money column. The amount, then the stamp, then how. The eye runs down one column and reads who paid.
  2. Green means money, and only money. The delivery word turns grey and moves to the right edge of its row. A bounce stays red.
  3. The day and the way. Card · Stripe, Check 4417 · bank deposit, Cash · typed by hand. These are the job window's own words.
  4. Not paid is said too. With the days since the bill first went.
  5. Every line has its day. The page reads the payment by the bill, so a text and an older email get theirs.
  6. The band and the tiles add it up. Not paid narrows the list. Paid this week opens the second view.
  7. Cents are small. The dollars of every amount read first.

3 · By day paid the second view · press a day, press a chip

This view answers one question. What came in on a given day? The month shows a figure on each day money came. Press a day to see its total and the bills behind it. The drawing opens with Stripe pressed and Wednesday, Nov 18 picked. It can be pressed.

Bills sentPrintDownload CSV

What the figure is. Each payment at the amount it paid off the bill. A Stripe payment sits on the day the customer paid. A check sits on the day its deposit posted at the bank. A check typed in by hand sits on the day the person named. Stripe's fee is not taken off, because the app does not keep it.

4 · By day paid on a phone

The same view at a phone's width. The month comes first and the picked day follows. Each day is a press a thumb can hit. This drawing opens on every way of paying, with Friday, Nov 20 picked.

Bills sent
What to look for
  1. One deposit, two bills. A check that paid two bills is drawn as one deposit with its bills under it.
  2. A day with no money is a plain cell. Press one to read the empty words and the press they offer.
  3. Later days are dashed and cannot be pressed.
  4. The last tile is Stripe's next payout to the bank. It is less than the Stripe total, because the fee is taken out.
  5. A bill's number opens that bill's record, as on the list.

What "money from Stripe on a day" can mean

The app keeps one fact about a Stripe payment: a payment row with the bill, the amount and a day. The table says what each meaning would need.

MeaningWhat the app holdsWhat it would take
A · The day the customer paid, at the bill's amount my pickHeld for every Stripe payment. The Dashboard's Money in bar reads the same rows the same way.Nothing new. One flaw to fix: a payment made in the evening is filed on the next day.
B · The day the payout reaches the bankNot kept. The app can ask Stripe for the next payout and the last one. It cannot say which bills are inside a payout.A new read of Stripe's payout history. Then a second day for the same money, on the same page.
C · After Stripe's feeNot kept. No fee, no net amount.The same new read as B. The figure would no longer match the bills.
A refund made in StripeNot seen. The bill stays paid.The view cannot show it until the app records refunds.
A bank debit still on its wayNot seen. The payment appears on the day it settles.Nothing. The view says so in its foot.
A disputed card paymentKept as a case, with the payment it names.The line can carry a red word, Disputed.

The calls

  1. Build it this way? One stamp on the list and one second view. Or the list alone first.
  2. Which day is a Stripe day? The day the customer paid. Or wait for the payout day, which needs a new read of Stripe.
  3. Fix the evening payments? One small database change files a Stripe payment on the Central day. Older rows can be corrected once, after a list of them is read.
  4. Is the next payout shown? One tile with the amount and the day it reaches the bank. Or leave it on the Dashboard only.
  5. Does By day paid show every way, or Stripe only? Every way, with a chip for Stripe. Or Stripe alone.
  6. Are the days out shown on a bill not paid? Yes, as small grey words. Or the words Not paid alone.
  7. What should a Stripe payment be called? Card · Stripe, as the job window says today. Or plain Stripe, since the app cannot tell a card from a bank debit.

One thing only the leader has. The day he wanted to check when he asked. I would open the first build on that day and read him its total.
