Is this the best we can do? The second pass

  • The window opened on a question few people need. Draft 1 put Back after time away and Ended by mistake side by side as two equal cards. A mistake is caught within days and is rare. The cards pushed the date and the rows down. Now the date comes first and the mistake is one quiet link under it.
  • Assign Luis again assumed Luis is still here. The row offers the old leader by name only while that leader is active. Otherwise it offers Pick… alone.
  • The wage row keeps Change as the main press. After three years a raise is likely, so Keep is the outline press and Change is the blue one.
  • The Hire match reads the name or the email. A person can come back under a new name, so a match on the old email alone stops the form too.
  • The away line on the desk stays. It reads like noise beside two stints. But without it the gap between Nov 2026 and Nov 2029 has to be worked out by eye, and tenure questions start there.
  • At phone width each row's presses drop under its words, and nothing scrolls sideways.
