Job form · plan and mockup

The address pins itself

The owner, 2026-10-07: right now geocoding occurs when I open the /map page, but I would like it to occur when someone puts in an address in new job and edit job modals. How can I offer this tastefully? Behind it: 149 property records have no point, so no court map can place them, and the Map page only pins what it is opened for.

What is already there

  • Under the Job address input sits a quiet line, ON STATEMENTS, that shows how the address will read on paper and offers a one-tap comma fix. Advice, never law: nothing there blocks a save.
  • geocode-one already answers one address for the office roles and estimators: the cache first, then Nominatim, then Google, then Census, and writes the pin to address_geocodes. The Map page and the property record's parcel lookup use the same cache. Nothing in the job's own row changes.

The rules behind the line

  • When it runs. On blur, or 1.2 s after the last keystroke once the address looks whole (the statements line has found a city, or there is a ZIP). Never on every keystroke, so a half-typed street costs no lookup. Edit job reads the cache the moment it opens, with no lookup; an unplaced address shows Place it and does nothing until tapped or blurred after an edit.
  • What it says. The city and county the geocoder answered, so a wrong pin is visible — a Kyle address that reads San Marcos · Hays County is caught at the desk, not on the firm's page. Pinned from the street map (Nominatim) or from Google; Not right? Pin from Google is the existing refresh.
  • What it never does. Block the save, change the job's address text, or write anything on the job. The pin lives in the cache under the address; edit the address and the line resets and runs again.
  • What it writes. The cache row, and — new — the geocoder's county onto a linked property record that has none (county_source = 'geocoder', the rung the record already knows), so the firm's page reads Guadalupe · JP Pct ? before the precinct is drawn.
  • The backlog. The 149 old records are not waiting on someone to open each job: the precinct nightly geocodes up to 300 unplaced property addresses a night through the same cache, then classifies them. The job form keeps new addresses from joining the backlog.

Is this the best we can do?

  • First draft had a button. Pin on the map beside the address. Dropped: a button is a chore someone skips. The line runs by itself and the only chip is for an answer that looks wrong, or an old job that was never placed.
  • First draft ran on every pause. That geocodes 380 TX, 380 TX-123, Se… and spends Google calls on fragments. Now it waits for a whole-looking address or a blur.
  • First draft said only ✓. A tick hides a wrong pin. The city and county make the answer checkable in a glance, and they are the same words the court map needs.
  • First draft left the 149 to the job form. Nobody reopens every old job. The nightly carries the backlog; the form stops it growing.
  • Not done. The bid form's address (bids show on the Map page too) can take the same line later; the tech's sub-labor form keeps its plain address, since techs cannot call the geocoder today and should not be made to wait on it.
