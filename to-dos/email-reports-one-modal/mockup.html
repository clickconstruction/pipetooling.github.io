Smallest change: both existing bodies live on, under one title and one intro that names the two kinds. The preview/test toolbar folds under a disclosure so the schedules come first.

Option B — one list, by person

The unified answer: one row per recipient, a Digest chip and an Every-report chip, Edit opens both settings for that person. Schedules (name, days, time) become a small secondary editor.

Tradeoff: a bigger rebuild — the digest's per-recipient scope/filter/costs and the schedule editor both need a new home inside the row editor. Rows shown are examples.
