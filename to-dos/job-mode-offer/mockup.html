What you are asked to pick

  1. Which one: A, B, C or D. B is the recommendation. A's picture or D's timing can be added to B later, each as its own small change.
  2. Not now: for good, or back in two weeks? Today it is for good. You said you want to keep offering it, so the drawing brings it back in two weeks.
