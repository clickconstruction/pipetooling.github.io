What I thought through

  • A hidden row is never found The big one

    The card's whole job was to be seen. A row that starts tucked is seen by nobody. So the row has to earn its tuck.

    My take: it opens showing until the person has pressed a third once, or for five visits. It also shows after every switch. Only then does it tuck. Try First visits against Later visits above.

  • The pull is plain scrolling

    The app already switches off the rubber band at the top of every page. So there is no pull past the top to borrow, and nothing fights for the gesture. The row is simply the first thing on the page, and the page opens scrolled just past it.

    My take: good news. No gesture code, and it works the same on an iPhone and an Android phone.

  • Anything that jumps to the top shows the row

    A dozen places in the app jump the page to its top. Each one would land on the row. A page shorter than the screen needs a little height added, or the row cannot tuck.

    My take: mostly welcome. The top of the page is where the row belongs. It is the part of the build that needs the most care.

  • It lives in the app's shell, above the header

    Task Mode is not a Dashboard. It sends every page to the checklist. So the row cannot belong to the Dashboard. It sits above the header, where the app already draws its top lines.

    My take: it shows on the Dashboard, on the Job Mode page and on the Task Mode checklist. Other pages keep their top as it is.

  • There are four modes, and the row has three thirds

    Dispatch Mode is the fourth. It is a bar that follows a person to every phone, and a leader has it on before choosing. It is not a place to go.

    My take: leave it in the gear menu. The middle third switches Task Mode and Job Mode off and leaves Dispatch Mode alone.

  • Rides the scroll is the other way to hide it

    The row shows at the top, slides away going down and comes back on any scroll up. It is found more easily. It also covers the top of the page each time a thumb moves up.

    My take: Tucked is calmer and is the better fit, with the guards above. Feel both before you pick.

  • Who gets the row

    The old card went to leaders and superintendents only. Every role can hold Job Mode, and anyone can hold Task Mode.

    My take: leaders, superintendents, subs and helpers on a phone. The crews are the ones who need the gear to leave Job Mode today. The office keeps the gear menu.

What you are asked to pick

  1. How it hides: Tucked, Rides the scroll or Always there. I would build Tucked.
  2. When it starts to tuck: after the first press or five visits, as drawn, or another count.
  3. The handle: a small grey bar on the page's top that hints at the row, or nothing.
  4. Who gets it: the four field roles on a phone, as drawn, or everyone.
