My Bids: one press per bid

A person does two things with a bid here. They hide it, or they go into it. So a bid has two moves and no buttons: a press goes in and a swipe hides. The name and the due line take the full width of the box.

1 The bid

The edge is red for past due, amber for due within three days, grey for the rest.

2 A press opens the bid's window

This is the window Preview opens today. It shows the bid and has a door to every tab of it.

Not picked two doors

A press jumps straight to Submission & Followup, as today. Preview sits small at the bottom right.

Why the window
Today a press always lands on Submission & Followup. An estimator pricing a bid then has to find Counts or Pricing. The window's doors go straight to any tab.
Like a job
A job's name opens the job's window all over the app. A bid's name would open the bid's window the same way.
Space
With no press on the row, a bid is two lines on most phones. The name is never squeezed by a button.

Scroll and Back
A touch that moves up or down scrolls the page. The phone's Back swipe starts at the left edge and moves right, so a left swipe never starts it.

Try it

These two frames work. Swipe a row left with a finger, or drag it with a mouse. Press a bid to open its window. Press a report to open it in place. Press a heading to fold it. Tab reaches the strip's press on every row.
