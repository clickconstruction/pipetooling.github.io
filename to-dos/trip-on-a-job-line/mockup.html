What each one changes in the app

AA bill of its ownBA line on the job
What the press makesA bill in Ready to Bill, at once. No line.A line among the job's lines. No bill until one takes it.
Job totalThe same either way. It rises by the amount, and percent done is read over the larger total.
When it can go outNow. It is already a bill.When the office picks it. Alone with one more press, or with the next bill.
The job's own next billUnchanged. The trip sits beside it.Carries the trip unless the trip was billed alone first.
Changing it laterThe words and amount change on the bill, as on any draft bill.The line is renamed, repriced or removed in the Job window, like any line.
Taking it backDelete the draft bill. The trip reads not billed again.Delete the line before a bill takes it. The trip reads not billed again.
A second pressRefused either way. The trip already holds its bill or its line, and says where.
Where else it showsThe job's bills, Ready to Bill, Accounts Receivable.All of those once billed. Before that, in the job's lines and its left to bill.
What gets builtOne function shaped like the turnaway trip charge's. A trip mark on the bill. The Job window is not touched.One checked function that adds one line. A trip mark on the line. The trip's chip read through the line to its bill.
The sharp edgeThe app would hold two things a person calls a trip charge: the turnaway's and this. And a trip can never ride a progress bill.A Job window left open on another screen saves its own older list of lines and can drop the new one. A change order's Apply to job has this edge today. The build has to close it for the trip's line.

Why B

  • B covers what A is for. A's one real gain is a trip billed by itself, now. B gets there with Bill the 1 picked, a press the Bill tab already has.
  • B keeps a job's money in one place. Every dollar asked of the customer is a line. A puts some of it on bills with no line under them.
  • A was the smaller build. It follows a function that already runs. B has one sharp edge to close: a Job window open elsewhere must not write the new line away.
