The owner-sees signal, three ways

On a job billed to the GC whose customer is the property owner, the row's customer line carries the owner sees the bills switch (v2.3827). It reports what the owner's portal shows them of this job's money: nothing, until the office shows them the GC's bills. Every row below is drawn at the Pipeline's real sizes, measured on the live board at 1440 px.

"I think that the 'owner sees $0' is confusing and takes up valuable real estate. … Perhaps we should show an eye with a line through it where when the user hovers over it, they can get details about what that means in the form of a temporary modal." — the owner, 2026-10-23

0 Today

The pill sits after the 🌐 and the speech bubble. Three rows, three states: a job with no bill out yet, a job with open GC bills the owner cannot see, and a job whose owner sees them. Hover the pill: today's title is a browser tooltip.

As it is pill 110–140 px · wraps under the name on a laptop · 23 of 80 live rows carry it

What "$0" means. Not the job's money, not the GC's balance. It is what Nia Okafor's own portal would show her of this job right now: zero, because the bills are Ridgeway's and none is shared. Her page reads all paid up while Ridgeway owes $12,400 on her house. That case is the reason the pill exists.

A A crossed eye in the icon run, with a hover card the pick

One 15 px glyph where the pill was: the Stage Plan's own eye, where an eye already means on the portal. Crossed when the owner's page shows none of the bills, open when it shows them, hollow when some. Grey while there is nothing to show yet; amber the moment open bills exist that the owner cannot see. Hover or focus opens the card a beat later; a tap opens it on a phone. The door opens the switch's existing window.

Option A glyph 15 px · the customer line never wraps for it · the card is 300 px, over the row
  • Grey crossed eye on the Whitfield row: no bill is out, so there is nothing the owner could see. Nothing to act on.
  • Amber crossed eye on the Okafor row: $12,400 is open to Ridgeway and Nia's page says all paid up. The one case worth a glance stays visible, at 15 px instead of 140.
  • Blue open eye on the Marsh row: Theo sees the bills. The card says the figure with its label, for their records, no Pay button.
  • The card's first line is the fact, the second is why, then the figure, then the door. Esc or leaving closes it.

B The glyph, and the figure beside it only when it is not $0

The same eye. When the owner sees the bills, the open figure sits beside it in blue; when they see some, amber with the whole. $0 is the glyph alone. Same card on hover.

Option B glyph 15 px alone · 15 + ~48 px with a figure · ~110 px on a partly row
  • Shorter than today on every row, and the off rows are as quiet as A.
  • But the figure has no label. 👁 $9,200 sits beside a money cell where every number has one. The reader asks the same question the owner asked of $0. In A the figure sits in the card next to its words.

C The figure folded into the money line

Nothing in the customer line. The Progress & payment cell's last legend line carries a muted fragment after Left on Job. No new line while it fits; the fragment is the door to the same window.

Option C 0 px in the Job column · ~90 px on the legend's last line · +17 px to the row when it wraps
  • The customer line is as short as A's.
  • The money cell is already the row's tallest on most Billed rows (225 px of 250 live). A fragment that wraps there grows the row by a line. C can cost height where A and B only give it back.
  • A muted fragment in a legend is not a door anyone finds, and the amber case has nowhere to be amber.
