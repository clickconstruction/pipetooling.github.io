Punch list #328 · design only · nothing built

A place on a task, and a map of my tasks

My recommendation: let a task carry one place, a named dot on the map. The giver picks it from the places already used, or presses Use where I am while standing there. The doer sees the dot, the giver's photo and Directions on the task. Today opens on a map of the tasks that have a place. A task with no place is made and done exactly as today.

1 · The top of Task Mode 2 · The task as the doer sees it 3 · Giving a task 4 · A task with no place 5 · When the phone cannot help 6 · What a leader sees 7 · The plan

Now

  • A task is words, a day and people. It has no place and no job of its own.
  • Only the doer can add a photo. The giver cannot show the spot.
  • Task Mode is a list. Nothing says where anything is.

My suggestion

  • One optional row in the Add window: Place. One press picks a place used before.
  • The giver's photo of the spot sits beside the dot on the task.
  • Today opens on Where my tasks are, and the list wears the same numbers.

The app asks a phone where it is only on a press, and never saves where a doer is.

Why it has to be nearly free. The app already has three things like this that a person may skip, and they are skipped. Attach this location is on 2 of 211 Task Dispatch requests. No box has a spot on a site map, out of 102. No task has a photo yet. So the design rests on one press: a place used before, or Use where I am.

7 · The plan eight small PRs, and a stop after the fourth

The first four make the whole loop work: set a place, see it, get there, see them all. Then stop and count how many tasks got a place before building the rest.

PRWhat it addsSize
1The place itself: a name, a point, how sure the phone was, who set it and when. The words for distance, direction and accuracy. One migration, no screen.S

3Doing: the place on the card, the small map, Directions, How far am I?, and the three honest cases.M
