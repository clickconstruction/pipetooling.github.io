The owner picked option 1 (on the job) and wants to keep today's view too. There are three ways to keep both. Each one leaves today's dots exactly as they are, and each works the same in the job's pane, the Lien window, and counsel's portal.

Way A A switch on the strip: Steps · Windows recommended

A small two-way switch in the strip's corner. Steps is today's view, unchanged, and it's what everyone sees first. Windows is the new view. Each person's choice is remembered on their device, so an office assistant can keep Windows and the leader can keep Steps. The Months table follows the same switch. Try it:

Way B Stacked: Steps on top, Windows opens underneath
