Is this the best we can do?

The ask was a modal that uploads. This is a window that browses and uploads, because the person who opens it to add should see what is already there, and the person who opens it to look should find Add at the top. Two faces of one window, not two windows.

The ask named the Pipeline. Field roles rarely open Jobs. The camera on the row is the office's door; the Job Mode card's Photos line is the field's. One component, four doors (the row, the ⋯ sheet, the Job Mode card, the job window's Photos section).

The field already has a camera-first Leave report. The two doors differ in what happens after the shutter: a report pushes, emails, asks a percent and moves the thread; a job photo is quiet and findable. Both stay, and the guide draws the line in one sentence.

Free words, chips, or both. Both, lightly: the description box first (what the owner asked for, what search finds), then the stage chips the report form already draws (the job's own lines, nothing to maintain). No kind chip set until the owner asks; Before: as the first word does that work.

Reused, not rebuilt: the report form's shot, shrink, send and tiles (lifted into one hook); Documents → Photos' tiles, viewer, chips, day groups and search; the job window's Drive list; the one sweep (a fifth source) and the one view (a fifth branch); the job's own gate; the empty state, the retry panel and the counted press.

New, and only this: one table (job_photo_notes: the report photo's shape plus a description), one bucket, the window, the icon, the Job Mode line, the thread line, the guide.

The owner's calls, each with the recommended default

  1. Where the Pipeline icon opens. Recommended: the Job photos window. Alternative: the job window's Documents tab with an Add press on its Photos line.
  2. The field's door. Recommended: a Photos · N line on the Job Mode card, in this train. Alternative: the Pipeline only.
  3. The description. Recommended: free words per photo, optional, with the stage chips, editable later. Alternatives: required; a kind chip set beside the stage chips.
  4. Which Drive folder. Recommended: Customer Pictures, where a report's photos land. Alternative: Team only › Photos.
  5. Who is told. Recommended: one thread line, no push, no email. Alternatives: nothing; or the report push's subscribers.
  6. Several at once. Recommended: a box per photo with Use these words for the rest. Alternative: one box for the batch.
  7. Editing the words later. Recommended: the taker and the office may, in the viewer. Alternative: fixed once added.
  8. No signal. Recommended: the queue as the train's last PR. Alternative: Retry until the signal is back, the queue on a later card.
