What changed when I asked “is this the best we can do?”

My first idea was a separate “Follow up on” record with its own button. Looking at the real screens changed five things.

First ideaRefined
A new “Follow up on” button on the bid.No new button. The date is asked in the tap you already make after a call. One extra line, and skipping it behaves like today.
A new kind of record with its own history.The date rides the contact log entry. The log already says who called and what was said, so the history is free.
“On hold” as a special state beside the 7-day rule.One idea: every open bid has a next date. Seven days is only the default when nobody picks one.
Queue still sorted by longest silence.Queue sorted by what is due. A year-old silence is the least urgent call, not the most.
A date per bid only.The call window already saves a next date per builder, and the queue ignores it. Both now feed one queue: the builder’s date by default, a bid’s own date when it differs.
