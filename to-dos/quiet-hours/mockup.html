5 The rule, in three words

The company's hours gate what customers see. A person's quiet hours gate what reaches that person. Each push the app sends takes one verb.

Hold

The push is not sent now. The row is written as held, and the person gets the catch-up push when their quiet hours end.

Every push to a teammate about their own work: a work order, a bid marked for them, a report, a clause to approve, a check that came back.

Hold and hand on

A customer's reply. The quiet holder is held. The office's wait push goes as today, to the office members who are awake.

No customer waits on a sleeping phone, and no sleeping phone is buzzed.

Ignore

The Settings test push, which the person pressed to see one.

Everything that is not a push to a person: texts to customers, the morning run, Today's sends, the Needs you card, every email stream.
