My Time: the week and the eight weeks

A mock-up, 2026-11-23, for the two parts of the Dashboard's My Time card that were circled. Every person and every hour here is made up. No pay is drawn. Each BEFORE is the card as the app draws it today. Use the bar to switch the person, the moment, the width and the theme.

What each BEFORE mark means today

What is drawnWhat it means in the code
A bar's heightThe week's recorded hours against the person's week, cut at 150 percent. The whole bar is 30 px, so 5 percent is 1 px.
The dashed line100 percent. Nothing on the screen says so.
Red, overAnything above 100 percent. One hour over a 40 hour week is red.
Green, full85 to 100 percent. A salaried week at exactly its hours is green.
Blue, steady. Amber, light60 to 84 percent, and under 60.
The striped stubThis week, still running. It is coloured against the whole week, so it reads light until about Thursday.
On ShiftA salaried day with any time on it, a finished day included. The hours are hidden.
A dashNo time on the day. A day off, a day to come and today before the start all read the same.
