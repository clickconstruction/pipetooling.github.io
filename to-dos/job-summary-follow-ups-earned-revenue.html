Job Summary: what is left — the earned-revenue kernel — before and after

Jobs → Job Summary → View · Jobs (the totals tiles and the jobs table, searched to “96”)

What changes

Today an in-progress job’s Revenue is contract × % complete, however many of its hours fall inside the window, so a 90-day view compares 90 days of labor and overhead against value the job earned over its whole life. After, in-progress rows read the one earned-revenue kernel the Bridge already uses (and Crew P&L and the board will share): each approved field hour earns contract ÷ expected hours, so the window’s hours earn the window’s share. J963 (148.5 of its 262 hours in the window) drops from $26,280.00 to $14,895.34 and its true margin from 53% to 17%; J968 (every hour in the window) and finished J960 (contract) do not move. The tiles and the totals row follow, the Revenue tile’s sub-line says “earned by hours worked”, and the Revenue* asterisk finally lands on a one-line rule under the table. The kernel itself and the new cell tooltips are not visible.

Assumptions the drawing made

  • The to-do names no placement, so the after keeps the figure where it already reads (the Revenue column, the Revenue tile and the totals row) and adds only a one-line rule under the table for the Revenue* asterisk that today points nowhere; the row cell keeps its ‘earned’ sub-label so the table’s shape does not change.
  • Finished rows keep the contract (the owner’s 2026-09-03 pick); the kernel is applied to in-progress rows only, over the approved field hours inside the Worked-in window, with no-% jobs still assumed 50% (‘earned ½?’).
  • J963 is the job the to-do names and its $710.00 parts cost is the to-do’s real figure; the brief names job 960 Elaine Giesber. Customer pairings (TF Harper, Burd), addresses, contracts, hours, %s, labor, subs and overhead are plausible, not real; J963 is given 113.5 hours before the window (the amber + mark) so the two rules visibly differ.
  • Worked in is set to 90d and the search box holds “96” so exactly these three jobs show and the tiles and totals are over the visible rows; overhead is Day-share with the recommended dials (30 days · 20% carry · 14-day idle cap).
  • The real table has 15 columns and scrolls sideways at 1120 px; the mock keeps the seven that carry the story (Job, Revenue*, Gross, Hours · days, True profit, True %, %) and leaves out Labor, Subs, Parts, Margin, Overhead, Burn, Proj. margin and $/hr, none of which the kernel touches (Margin and $/hr would move with revenue but were cut to fit the width).
  • Rows sort by True profit descending as the app defaults, so J963 falls below J960 in the after; the Cut by panel is absent because Cut by is none; hover tooltips and the delta lines under the tiles (Compare to is none) are omitted.
