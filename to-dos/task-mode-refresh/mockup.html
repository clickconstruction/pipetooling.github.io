What the refresh changes

Each line is something in the drawing above. None needs the database.

One head that says where you stand

Today the word Checklist appears twice and Today twice. The refresh keeps the date and one green sentence, the way New task's bar says what Send will do.

A Next card

Nothing says what to do first. The first open task of the day is drawn open, with its place, its Done when… lines and the page's one solid blue press.

The map stays first

Where my tasks are already opens Today when an open task has a place. It keeps that seat under the head. Each numbered dot matches the number on its task, and an old task with no due day gets an amber dot in place of red.

A done task is one line

The finished card keeps a 48 px note button and a solid blue chip. Refreshed it folds to a struck line with a quiet ＋ Note.

Presses have names

🔕 becomes Mute alerts and FWD becomes Hand on. Both are quiet pills, like New task's Add row. ＋ Task gets its words back in the header.

Red means late, and only late

A task with no due day shows its age in amber and reads given Aug 20. Red is kept for a real due day that has passed.

The whole tag, and a fold

The blue tag is cut off at 190 px. Refreshed it is a muted line that wraps. The old list shows three rows and folds the rest.

Kept from the sunlight pass

The tick and Mark done keep their thick dark borders and big targets. Tints only repeat what words already say, since glare takes tints first.

Today and History as one bar

The two tabs become the joined bar New task uses for When, lit the same way.

What a task can hold

From the schema and the code on main.

It holdsWhat that is
Words and linksA title, with links that stand in the title as [1], [2].
WhenA start day. Once, on chosen weekdays, or so many days after it was last done, with an end day. A due day on a one-off only.
WhoOne or more people it is for. The giver. One person who hears when it is done.
Stays until doneOn for most one-offs. Off, a missed day drops away.
Done when…Lines to tick. The card reads its percent from the ticks.
Percent0 to 100 on a one-off or roadmap task. 100 closes it.
PhotosA photo of the spot from the giver. Photos from the doer at a percent, a line or the finish. A switch that asks for a photo at the finish. Images only.
WhereA named place with a pin, or a painted map.
NotesA thread of notes on each day, with the done, reopened and signed-off marks in it.
RemindersA time of day, the day before, and a nudge to the giver after so many days.
RoadmapA link to a roadmap task and its stage.
Not thereNo job. No tags. No reviewer of its own. No file that is not an image.

States: open, waiting on review, signed off, reopened with a reason. There is no snooze, no skip, no "does not apply" and no handed-on mark.

Calls that are yours

  • Review. Finished tasks are rarely looked over. Either the chip goes quiet, as drawn, or only a task whose giver asked to hear waits on review.
  • Red. Should a task with no due day ever read late? Drawn here it shows its age in amber.
  • What comes first. Drawn: today's open tasks in the office's order, then tasks sent back, then the oldest.
  • Sunlight. The August card avoided tints on purpose. The refresh keeps thick borders on the tick and Mark done and uses tints elsewhere.
  • The note press. You asked for it on the right half so a thumb misses the tick. It stays on the right, smaller.
  • Who may close or hand on a task without a yes. Tools 3 and 7 are drawn as tell the giver, not ask the giver.
  • A map on a task that has one. Drawn: the doer adds to that map and cannot swap it. Only a task with no map takes a new one.
  • A point and the task's place. Drawn: a point stays on the map. The task's own place still comes only from where the person stands.
  • Where my tasks are. A task with a map and no place has no dot today. It could show at its line's start.
