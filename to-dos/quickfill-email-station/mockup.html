But the lanes answer a different question. Needs a reply is a conversation nobody answered. The Inbox is what a person has not filed. An email answered yesterday still sits in the Inbox until it is archived, and this station would call the morning clean.

Half the crack. Follow Up and Next Actions are the person's own labels, which Messages never sees. Those two rows stay hand-typed, the history stays a retyped list, and work@ is still read by nobody.

B Live labels, per box and per person — the pick

The station reads Gmail's own state. One row per label with its live count and the oldest thread's age; under it the threads sitting past the limit (the owner's number, three days to start) with the day they arrived, the sender, the subject and a door into Gmail under that box's address. One line says what moved since the last look. The mark is the Roller's green Looked, and the note writes itself.
