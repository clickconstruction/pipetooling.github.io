## The ask, in the owner's words

> To send those 9 notices I would first have to have the right property records and names of owners saved for all those locations. Where in this pipeline should I add a prompt to offer that?

## The reading

- The § 53.056 notice goes to the **owner of record** at a mailing address; without one the desk cannot mail, and the Lien window's affidavit gate stops too. Today the owner is entered on the property record (Customers → Properties, or Edit Job → Property record since v2.3401) — three doors from the Pipeline, and nobody is prompted.
- The app already does the hard part: the property record's **paste-the-CAD-page** box (`cadPagePaste.ts`) picks out the owner name, mailing address, legal description and the homestead exemption from a copied appraisal-district page, and `CustomerPropertyRecordPanel` runs the parcel lookup (county, legal) as it opens. So a prompt is one paste, not a form.
- The owner belongs to the **property**, not the job: RMC Dudley Mason's 16 jobs sit at a handful of addresses; one paste covers every job at an address, now and later.
- A builder entered as the customer with no GC set reads as "we contracted with the owner" and gets no monthly-notice clock at all; the same prompt should ask which it is. (Live count 2026-09-14: 3 open jobs. The larger gap is the other way round — 42 of the 62 open GC jobs have a GC and **no customer row at all**, so the owner slot is simply empty.)

## The decision (second pass) — the app finds the owner, a person confirms it

Run live on 2026-09-14: `property-lookup` returned the owner of record, mailing address, legal description and county for 10 of 10 addresses in the pile (Bexar, Guadalupe, Blanco, Comal, Williamson, Medina districts; ~0.5 s each; one address with a "Null" ZIP included). Three of the ten are things a paste would have hidden: **a public owner** (J690's site is the City of Round Rock — a mechanic's lien does not attach to public property; the remedy is a claim on the GC's payment bond), **a landlord** (J650's site is Schertz Station Ltd; ATI Schertz is the tenant), and a REIT with a c/o mailing address (J523 · J883). So every surface below shows the roll's answer with its provenance and asks for one click (**Use**); the paste box appears only on a miss. New kernel piece: `ownerKind(parcel)` → public · company · individual, plus the landlord test (owner ≠ customer ≠ GC), carried as *reads-as* chips onto the list and the desk. New columns: `customer_addresses.owner_confirmed_at / _by`.

## The three moments (as redrawn)

1. **When a job is created (or edited) with a GC on it — the owner appears, nobody is asked.** The Property record row runs the lookup by itself when a GC is set (or the customer is a builder) and shows *Found on the appraisal roll* with owner, mailing address, legal, county and provenance, plus **Use** / *Not right — paste the CAD page…* / the CAD link. Use creates or fills the property record (on the GC when the job has no customer row — 42 of the 62 open GC jobs) and links the job (the v2.3401 path). Direct jobs get no row. When the **customer is a builder and no GC is set**: *"Is <customer> the owner of this site, or building it for someone?"* — the second answer sets the GC on the job (which also fixes the notice clock).
2. **When the job is Working — the Pipeline's Fix-ups card.** `buildPipelineFixups` gains **`owner-confirm` — "Owner of record to confirm · N"** counting GC (or builder-customer) jobs with approved hours and no *confirmed* owner; the chip opens a property-grouped list that looks every property up as it opens (cached, a progress line), shows the roll's answer per row with reads-as chips (public owner · landlord · likely homestead), **Use** per row and **Use all found**, the paste box only on misses. Shared with the GC-on-notice modal's Step 1. The card disappears when the data is clean.
3. **When the first bill goes out on a GC job — a quiet line in Bill Customer.** *"Owner of record for <address>: <the roll's answer> — not yet on the job. Use it so the lien notice can be mailed when it is due."* Never a block. The desk's *Needs the owner* pane reads the roll the same way, and a public-owner item reads *bond claim* and is not drafted.

The Lien desk's *Find the owner ›* stays as the exception.

Rejected: a hard block on Working or on billing; asking per job instead of per property; a free-text owner box (the paste is faster and gets the legal description too).

## Open decisions

- Should the card count every GC job with approved hours and no confirmed owner (35 tonight; 13 of them already past a first month's window), or only the desk's due items (7)? Proposed: the wider count — the list fills itself, so it costs nothing.
- **New (decision 5):** save the roll's answer with no click — a nightly run writing every unowned GC job's owner *from the roll, unconfirmed*, the desk drafting on it and a person confirming before *Record the run*? Proposed: build it as a Settings switch, off on day one, on after the first sitting shows the roll is right.
- Should the amber count wait for approved hours (35 tonight), or count every unconfirmed GC job, Waiting included (36)? Proposed: hours — amber means a clock is running; the lookup runs at creation either way.
- Where does the silent lookup run on the job form: GC and builder jobs only (27 of the last 30 days' 57 new jobs), or every job (57)? Proposed: only those; a middle path (run everywhere, show the box only when the roll's owner differs from the customer) is possible.
- When the roll suggests a **homestead** (`homesteadHint` = likely: an individual gets mail at the property; the roll has no exemptions), should the box say so on the spot ("a homestead lien needs a recorded pre-work contract — talk to the attorney before starting")? Proposed: yes, one line.

Bill Customer on a GC job with no owner shows the quiet line. Prod-safe: pastes write real property records — use TEST jobs for the live pass.
