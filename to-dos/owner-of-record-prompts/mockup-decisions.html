One correction to the first mock-up

The first page read the five desk jobs with no customer name as "the builder is the customer." The database says otherwise: on six of the seven, the GC is set and the customer row is empty (J258 carries the owners' names as typed text, no customer record). Across the board it is the same shape — 42 of the 62 open GC jobs have no customer row. So the "who owns the site?" paste is the whole answer for most of the pile, and the separate builder-as-customer question (a builder in the customer row, no GC) is a smaller case: 3 open jobs today, 4 of the 57 jobs created in the last 30 days. Both prompts stay; the numbers just swap which one is the main door.
