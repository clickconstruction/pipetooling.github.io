What the second pass changed

The first draft asked the office to paste a county page per property. The app already has property-lookup (v2.3004): address in, owner of record + mailing address + legal description out, from the statewide appraisal roll, keyless, about half a second. We ran it on the ten addresses in tonight's pile and all ten answered — including 9703 Lenox Hl whose ZIP reads "Null", and including three the paste would have hidden: a public owner (City of Round Rock — a mechanic's lien does not attach; the remedy is a bond claim), a landlord (Schertz Station Ltd owns ATI's building), and a REIT with a care-of address. So the design is now: the app looks up, a person presses Use. Decisions 1 and 2 shrink to "when does the count go amber" and "where does the lookup run"; decision 5 is new.

Also corrected: six of the seven desk jobs have a GC set and no customer row (42 of 62 open GC jobs are that shape); the builder-in-the-customer-row case is 3 open jobs.
