Why this moment

Texas counts the notice clock from the month labor was furnished, not the bill date — by the time the first bill goes out, the first notice is often already in its window. The office is already in the modal thinking about who pays; the owner's name is the same thought, one line lower.

Rejected

A hard block on Working or on billing (a late bill is worse than a late owner). Asking per job instead of per property. A free-text owner box (the paste is faster and gets the legal description too). A fourth surface — the desk's Find the owner › is the exception path, and it stays.

Is this the best we can do?

The first draft asked the owner question on every new job, put a free-text box on the Fix-ups list, and counted jobs. Each one made the office do more for less. What changed, and why:

Properties, not jobs. The list groups by address. RMC's two Bexar sites are two pastes, not two per job. The count reads "7 jobs on 7 properties" so the office knows how many pastes the sitting is.
One number everywhere. The chip, the list's footer and the desk's Needs the owner pile all read has_owner from the same RPC. No second definition of "missing" to drift.
The builder-as-customer question comes first. Five of seven jobs cannot even say whose site it is. Asking "who owns the site?" before "who is the owner of record?" fixes the GC and the notice clock in the same breath.
Ask only where the lien clock runs. A direct residential job with no GC never sees the prompt; Waiting jobs with a GC but no hours yet stay out of the count (proposed — the owner may flip this).
The homestead line on the spot. When the paste says homestead, the form says what that means for the lien before anyone starts work. One sentence; the attorney's words.
Green stays green. A saved row on the list does not vanish; the office sees its own progress through the sitting, and the footer counts down.

The plan

Three PRs, smallest first. No new table.

Data

Nothing new to store. The owner lives on customer_addresses.owner_* (the property record) with the per-job override in job_property_owners; the job links to its property by customer_address_id (v2.3401 adds the row from the job form). The paste parser and the county lookup exist.

One count. no-owner in buildPipelineFixups, fed by the desk's list_lien_notice_months().has_owner (jobs with a GC or builder customer, approved hours, no owner) — or a sibling list_jobs_without_owner() if the desk's 30-day window is too narrow for the card (decision below).
