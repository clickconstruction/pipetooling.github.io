So the office should never paste; the app looks every GC job up and the office presses Use.

What changed from the first draft, and why

The paste was the wrong primitive. It existed as a fallback for addresses the roll cannot place (v2.3016); the first draft made it the main door for 35 jobs. Ten live lookups in a row answered, so the main door is the lookup and the paste is the exception.

Confirm, not enter. Every surface now shows the roll's answer with its provenance and asks for one click. The office's job is to notice when the answer is wrong, which is the job a person is good at.

The roll knows things a paste would not surface. Public owners (no lien, bond claim), landlords (the tenant is the customer), out-of-state and c/o mailing addresses. The reads-as chips carry them into the desk.

Rejected. A batch job that saves every owner with no human click — considered as decision 5; recommended, but as a setting the owner turns on, not the default on day one.

Is this the best we can do? — second pass

One click for the pile. 35 lookups run when the list opens; Use all found writes them with provenance. The paste box appears only on misses.
Nothing asked that the roll can answer. The job form shows the owner; the only human question left is builder, or owner? on the 3 builder-as-customer jobs.
Provenance on every row. District, tax year and Prop ID ride with the owner, the way the property panel already prints them, so the day-of-filing check on the CAD page is one link.
The roll's blind spot is named. Tax year lags sales and the roll carries no exemptions, so likely homestead is a hint to confirm, never a fact, and the CAD link sits beside it.
Public owners stop a wrong notice. The City of Round Rock row would have received a § 53.056 notice under the first design; now the row says bond claim and the desk skips it.
Still one number. The chip, the list footer and the desk's pile all read the same RPC; the count means "unconfirmed", not "missing", and drops to zero the moment the office presses the button.

The plan

Three PRs, smallest first. No new table; one new column.

Data

Exists. property-lookup and the parcel kernel (txParcelRecord.ts: proposePropertyRecord, homesteadHint, ownerLooksLikeCompany, applyProposalToFields), the provenance columns on customer_addresses (v2.3004), the add-as-property path from the job (v2.3401), the desk's has_owner.

New. customer_addresses.owner_confirmed_at / _by (a roll answer saved by Use is confirmed; a batch save without a click is not — decision 5); ownerKind(parcel) in the kernel → public · company · individual (+ the landlord test: owner ≠ customer ≠ GC); the wider count RPC (list_jobs_owner_to_confirm(): GC or builder jobs, approved hours, no confirmed owner, with the first deadline for the sort).

PR train
  1. PR 1 — the list that looks itself up. The Fix-ups chip, the property-grouped list (OwnerConfirmListModal), lookups on open with the progress line and a cache, Use / Use all found, the reads-as chips, the paste box on misses, the migration for owner_confirmed_*. Clears tonight's 35 in one sitting.
  2. PR 2 — the job form. The Property record row runs the lookup when a GC is set (or the customer is a builder) and shows the roll's answer with Use; the builder-as-customer question on the after-create prompt; the homestead line.
  3. PR 3 — Bill Customer and the desk. The one-line Use in Send to; the desk's Needs the owner pane shows the roll's answer with Use instead of the Find the owner › door; a public-owner item reads bond claim and is not drafted.
