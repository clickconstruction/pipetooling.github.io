What changes under the hood

  • The dated timeline needs each notice's day 0 and its affidavit day. Both are on the desk already: the attorney’s letter's clock says where day 0 came from, and the affidavit entry carries the § 53.052 day.
  • The counts come from the notices on the step: the courtesy's hold reason, the attorney’s letter's state, the property record's gates. All read today, none written.
  • The words are a rewrite of the twelve briefs plus the four Reminders ones, each with a one-line meaning for every other move. Plain words, tested like the guides.
  • The rename (Grace, Nov 6): letter two becomes the attorney’s letter on every surface a person reads — the rung, the press (Send the attorney’s letter), the footer, the guides, § Rules. The code keeps its names. Rides in PR 1 with the words.
  • Payments on the ladder (Grace, Nov 6): a payment is a row on the timeline with the amount and the remainder, from the payments already on the job under the one-payment rule. A partial payment pauses the reminders as paying it down and makes the usual move Record what they promised; every later amount is the remainder; the legal day never moves. Paid in full closes the ladder, strikes the deadline through, and points at Release the lien when an affidavit is on file. To check first: that the server's affidavit list drops a job whose open balance reached zero.
  • The affidavit's week, a priority not a hold (Grace, Nov 6): inside the 7 days before the affidavit's day the desk's recommended action becomes the affidavit, and the courtesy's press stays on the footer for a send by hand. The legal holds, a homestead, no work month, a closed window, stay holds with their reasons. One line in the courtesy status.
  • One courtesy, then the attorney’s letter (Grace, Nov 6): the desk offers one courtesy reminder, never a second, and after a week of silence offers the attorney's letter. The press stays on the notice's footer for a resend by hand. The Reminders rail keeps its four rungs: Reminder due · Reminder sent · The attorney’s letter · Closed. In § Rules the source line becomes "Day 7 is the owner's choice". One constant and rung 2's words change; the owner confirms the default.
  • Size: two small PRs. The first rewrites the words and adds the meanings. The second draws the dated timeline and the counts. Neither changes a rule or a press.
