Why some rows are bright and some are faded

the desk on November 4, 2026

A faded row is not a blocked one. The ladder above the strip has four rungs, and when you press a rung the desk keeps that rung's rows bright and fades the rest to a third. Press the rung again and every row comes back. Try it below on the real rows.
