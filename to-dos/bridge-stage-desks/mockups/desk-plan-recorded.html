What is lost without itA plan on a non-link desk is remembered, not recorded. If Jorge never goes, nothing says the owner planned it on Oct 26. The Schedule block, if placed, says it. If it was never placed, nothing does.
What it risksTwo ceremonies on one page. The owner records a call on the link desk and a plan on a desk, and Friday reads two cards. The first-timer review already found the record step the hardest to read.

What it costs

  • One migration: one column, kind, on bridge_calls with its default and check. Every existing row reads as a call.
  • The call reader filters to calls for the held link, the streak and the verdict, so a desk plan never steals the link. One kernel change with tests.
  • Each desk gains the plan line, the sheet footer, the recorded stamp and the chip. Four desks, four PRs, or one shared strip.
  • The Last call card gains the second block.
Yes or no: record a desk's plan on bridge_calls with a kind marker, or keep recording with the lit link only?
The recommendation is no, for now. The lit link's call already records the week's plan with owners, and the Schedule, the percent window and the bill send record the moves themselves. A second ceremony adds a card to read and a migration, and the thing it saves is a promise the Schedule block already keeps. Say yes later if a Friday comes when the owner wants to know what he planned on a desk that was not the link.
