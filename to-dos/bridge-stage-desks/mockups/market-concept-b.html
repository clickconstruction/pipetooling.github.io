What the book says
The narrowing Three names carry 70% of the asks, up from 54% last quarter. If Burd cools, Market is the link within a month. The spread work is yours; the book just names it.
The pricing spend Knight has taken 6 priced bids for $0 won and no answer, ever. Every one cost estimator days the live GCs waited on. Keep pricing them, or stop — the book will show the answer either way.
What this desk cannot see Courting, reputation, invites that die before a bid exists. A GC enters the book at their first invite and not before. This page tells you where relationship time pays and where it burns — it cannot make the calls.
How this reads: a row groups the bids by their GC of record (gc_builder_id) and the estimates as one customer line — the same bids and estimates the Bridge reads, spanned to what it holds (this window + 90 days; a year needs the same read, wider). “Pays” is work won from that source in the span, by value. Fading = asks this quarter under half their quarterly norm. Dead weight = six or more priced bids, nothing won, nothing answered. New name = first-ever bid this quarter. 8 bids ride at $0 until a value is set.
