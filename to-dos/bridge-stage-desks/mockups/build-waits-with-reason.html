What the desk stops counting, and what it costs

  • Stops counting: the waiting job leaves the Stalled tile, the verdict's "hold $X and saw nobody" and the biggest money idle chip. The job's dollars show in their own tile, Waits with a reason, so nothing hides.
  • Keeps counting: the Bridge's Build card. Its pile is sold work not finished, and the job is still that. The wait changes who the desk blames, not what is sold.
  • Costs: one migration. A small table, one row per wait: the job, the reason, the words, who, since, and lifted when. The desk's kernel gains a fifth group and the write. The Stages card and the job window gain the chip and the fact row.
  • Not a status change. The job stays Working. Waiting already means not started, and the Pipeline's counts keep meaning what they mean today.
Yes or no: can a Build job be marked waits, with a reason, so the desk stops counting it against the crew?
The recommendation is yes. The desk already tells the owner to name why it waits. This gives that sentence a press, one migration, and the Stalled tile stops blaming the crew for a GC's delay.
