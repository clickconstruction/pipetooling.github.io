How this reads: every row names the read that proves it. Finished = the %s × contracts, less what bills or payments cover (the larger side is named). Ready day = the job's own ready-to-bill status change, kept with the day and who. Billed day = the day the bill went to the customer, else the day it was marked billed. Lag = billed − ready; cash-days = lag × the bill. Who sent it comes from the bill's own record. No weekly plan lives here — each row carries its own door, and the Bridge's call stays with the lit link.
