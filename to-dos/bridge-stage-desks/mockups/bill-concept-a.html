What the sitting costs. A bill starts its payer's clock the day it goes out, not the day the work finished. SPACEX pays at its ~33-day pace: pay app 3 sent 4 days late lands 4 days late. This window's late sends pushed ≈ $55k·days of receipts back — about one day of the company's cash float.

How this reads: ready day = the job's own ready_to_bill status change (job_status_events, who and when); billed day = the day the bill went to the customer, else the day it was marked billed (billedDay). Lag = billed − ready. Cash-days = lag × the bill. No plan to record here — the rule is daily, and the sitting rows carry their own door.
