The robots already estimate blind and the hourly routine already runs them. 4What silence means: a quiet robot and a sleeping Mac are two facts

Two clocks. A robot that holds a bid and has not spoken for ten minutes is quiet: amber on the bid, nothing for a person to do yet, the next round picks it up. A Mac that has not made a round for fifteen minutes, three rounds missed, is asleep: gray in the caption, and one line on the owner's Dashboard with the fix in the sentence. An estimator never hears about the Mac. The owner never hears about a slow sheet.
