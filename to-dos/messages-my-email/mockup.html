Punch list #230 · Messages · a mock-up, nothing built

My email: a person's own @clickplumbing.com mail beside the threads, one press to tag a mail onto a job or a bid

The tab is a tagging desk, never a second inbox. It reads a person's own Inbox and Sent live and keeps nothing. The app guesses the job from the subject, the sender and the conversation; the person presses Yes. A tag copies that conversation onto the job as lines in Messages, with the files and a court-ready .eml, and later mail in the conversation follows by itself. Everyone, address, job and sentence below is made up.

0 Today: the one way a mail from your own box reaches a job

Nothing new is needed for this. The reader takes a forwarded copy on bids@ and lands it on the mail's first sender, with the forwarder's note as a team note; a job number standing in the subject ties the line. It is a habit, and one the team has not been told.

Where the guess comes from, in order: the conversation is already tagged (then the mail lands by itself and never shows here); the subject names a job by its number or its name among the sender's jobs, the reader's own rule; the address is on one contact with one starred or one open job; the address's domain is a customer's with one open job. Otherwise no guess. The app never tags on a guess alone.
