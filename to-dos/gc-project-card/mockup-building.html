  • The head on a building job is three grouped sets, not one wrap: the job's (The plans, Papers, Questions, Schedule), the work's (Daily log, Punch list, Submittals, RFIs, Draws) and the money team's (Bill the customer, Bill day, Change orders, Closeout), each door with the fact that says whether to open it (Daily log · 2 missed, Draws · 1 to approve, in red when it waits on us). More ▾ holds the four rare presses. The price as signed joins the facts line for the money team.
  • The verdict band holds everything the strip's top did, in one tinted block: the verdict and why, Next, the four numbers, the four bars (work against time, money against work), and Bill day's line. An assistant sees the band without the two money numbers, the two money bars and the Bill day line, and nothing moves.
  • The clock is the strip's week squares as they are, with the month gaps, the finish as the last square and the milestones as marks; its summary (weeks to the finish, milestones on time) is the band's caption. The key is behind the link.
  • The trades are one table: stripe, trade and company, contract (money), done as a bar with its share, billed (money), what it waits on, next. A row that waits on us is red and says what (Draw 2 waits on our approval, Their submittal waits on us, needed today); › opens the statement of work's facts (price, retainage, the plan set it was based on, the two schedules of values, what they will not do) and the trade's open items. The tile, the heading, the scope, the statement-of-work card and the asks are one row.
  • The plans band says the Drive problem in one red clause with a How to fix it door, where today it is a three-line sentence.
  • The foot is the stage's end: how much is reported and the Closeout door, the money team's.

Is this the best we can do?

  • Fifteen doors became three sets and a menu, and each door carries the fact that says whether to open it. I weighed folding the work's set (Daily log … Draws) into the verdict band's Waiting on us number alone, since Next already names the most urgent one; it lost, because the office opens Daily log and Punch list every day whether or not they wait on us. A door is a place, a number is a state; both belong.
  • The bars live in the verdict band, not their own band, because they are the verdict's evidence (38% done where 42% was planned is why it reads Watch). A reader who sees no money sees two bars; the band does not reflow.
  • The trade row's Done bar replaces the tile's bar, and the statement-of-work card, today a block as tall as the row's whole table, opens under the row. On a job with twelve trades that is the difference between one screen and four.
  • Two keys stay, behind links. The week squares' key names six kinds; the rows' stripes name four. The words on each square's hover and on each row say the same, so the key is a first-month aid, as on the bidding card.
