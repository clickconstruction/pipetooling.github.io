You picked drawing B on Nov 13: one at a time. A press on Worth lists every row with no price, as it does today. One press on that list opens a window that asks about one name at a time. It shows the parts-book part that fits, what a supply house bill paid, a box for a price you type, and Not a part. The short of it

Now

  • A press on Worth lists the rows with no price. It stops there.
  • Pricing one row is three presses and a search of the parts book.
  • The search finds nothing for most names a crew typed.
  • A name the parts book does not hold cannot be priced on this screen at all.
  • A row that is scrap or tools reads no price for ever, so the cover can never reach the end.

My suggestion

  • Each row with no price shows its offers: a parts-book part, a bill's price.
  • One press accepts an offer. Enter moves to the next row.
  • A box takes a price you type. Not a part takes a row out.
  • An offer is never counted. Only a price you accepted is known.
  • Every answer has Undo.

The Parts Book is never written from here. A count, a box and a name are never changed.

Your calls

2 · May an offer come from any bill?

Yes. The site's own bills fit one row. Every bill the company has fits 39.

Only this site's bills.

3 · Does an offer count in known?

No. Only a price a person accepted.

Yes, as a second figure beside it.

4 · Does Not a part leave the cover?

Yes. The cover says how many were left out.

No. It stays as no price.

5 · Does a typed or bill price go stale?

Yes. After 90 days, like a parts-book price.

No. It is what was paid.

6 · Does a price belong to the site?

Yes. As a tie does. A name can mean another part on the next site.

To the whole company.

7 · Who may price?

The roles that tie today. They already read the desk's money.

The office only.

8 · Does it price the job's page too?

Yes. One rule, one price, on the Missing table and the desk.

The site's screen only.

9 · Does Worth open the window at once?

Yes, straight into the window.
