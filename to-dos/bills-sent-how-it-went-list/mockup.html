Bills sent: the office edits the list under How the bill went

The page Bills sent has one form, Say how it went…, and one list in it. Today the list has four choices and only a developer can change them. This page draws the form as it is, then three ways the office could add to the list and edit it, then three more fixes to the form that are separate from the list. Every name, job and amount is made up.

The short of it 1 · Now 2 · Option A, a line in the list 3 · Option B, chips 4 · Option C, Settings 5 · The window 6 · The form itself 7 · Old records 8 · Your calls

The short of it

Who opens the page

The office only. You have it right. Four roles open Bills sent: the developer, the master, the assistant and the controller. No field role opens it. The same four can press Say how it went….

What I suggest

Option B. The choices become chips in the form, with ＋ Add a way at the end and Edit list beside it. One press picks a way, a new way is typed where the person stands, and nothing covers the next field.

What must hold, whichever you pick

  • A bill recorded last month keeps the words it was recorded with.
  • A way is taken off the list, never deleted.
  • Every way, old or new, counts under Recorded by hand.
  • The list holds 12 ways at most and never fewer than one.

7 · Old records what a change to the list does, and does not do

Today a record keeps a short code for its way, and the screen looks the words up when it draws. So a rename would reword last month’s records. The first step of the build closes that: each record keeps the words too.

The office does thisThe formA bill recorded before itThe chips, the tiles, Print and the CSV
Adds Uploaded to the builder’s pay portalA new chip, for everyone, at the endNo changeA bill recorded with it is under Recorded by hand. Its line, Print and the CSV say the new words. The tiles count it on the day it went.
Renames Mailed to Mailed, first classThe chip reads the new wordsStill reads Mailed, on the list, on the bill’s record, in Print and in the CSVNo change to any count
Takes off FaxedThe chip is goneStill reads FaxedNo change to any count
Brings Faxed backThe chip returns where it wasNo changeNo change
Moves a way up or downThe chips follow the new orderNo changeNo change

Why every way counts under Recorded by hand. The chips sort a line by who says the bill went. Email means the app sent it and the mail server answered. A way the office typed is a person’s word. A person’s word under Email would sit beside lines that say Delivered 9:28 AM and look like the same proof.

8 · Your calls a word each will do

  1. Which option? A, B or C. I suggest B.
  2. Who may edit the list? The four roles that open the page, or the master and the developer only. I suggest the four: the people clearing the yellow box are the ones who meet a missing way, and every change carries a name and a day.
  3. May the four ways there today be renamed and taken off? Yes or no. I suggest yes. Emailed from a person’s own mailbox is long, and the office knows its own words best.
  4. Does every way count under Recorded by hand? Yes, or a way may name the chip it counts under. I suggest yes, every way.
  5. Is 12 the right cap? 12, or another number.
  6. R1, the day offers the day the bill was made? Yes or no. I suggest yes.
  7. R2, several bills to one person at once? Yes or no. I suggest yes, after the list is done.
  8. R3, the form reads the record back? Yes or no. I suggest yes.
