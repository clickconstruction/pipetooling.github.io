6 · Is this the best we can do?

  • A fifth door, "Dig in", holding all four. Lost. A drawer of unrelated tools is the old scroll with a lid. Each piece opens from the number it explains, so you find it when you are asking that question.
  • The Daily profit rate card back as it was. Lost. It put a red number beside a green headline. The information was right and the placement was wrong.
  • Profit after overhead as a fourth tile on the first screen. Lost. That is a fourth reading of "are we making money" at the top again. Three were the problem.
  • The what-ifs back as their own list. Lost. Two of the three were already rows on the usual fixes, so a list would print them twice.
  • A record press on the desk again. Lost. One call needs one place to make it. The list is there to read.
  • The Monday sheet behind a door. Lost. It summarizes a page that is now one screen.
Three things are yours to decide.
  • Which of the four come back. I would take all four. If only one, profit after overhead.
  • What profit after overhead reads. The old card read approved hours only, which is why it showed a loss while hours waited. It could read recorded time, as the headline does, with the same ≈ mark. I would do that, so the two can no longer disagree in sign for that reason.
  • Whether the fourth move can go on the call from the desk. Drawn as read-only. A press there would be quicker, and it is a second place that changes the call.
