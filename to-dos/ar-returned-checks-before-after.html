The second pass: is this the best we can do?

  • The lane is not a standing list. Four returns in six months does not earn a third tab every day. It draws while a case is open and goes when the last one closes.
  • "Customer told" is not a new store. It is the They said… promise the Pipeline already keeps. A promise newer than the bounce marks the step done.
  • "Replaced" is suggested, not typed. Applying the suggested deposit to the same bill closes the case by itself.
  • Cut: an email to the customer about the bounce (the office calls) and a returned-checks report (the lane and All are the report at this volume).

Your call Three things only the owner can decide

  • The clearing window. Hold the unconditional waiver until a check has cleared, and for how many days? Drawn at 7. A GC who wants it the day they pay would wait a week. Counsel's view is worth having.
  • A returned-check fee. Offer a fee line on the bill when a check bounces? Texas caps the fee and the amount is not yet verified.
  • Who hears about a return that is not on a job. The people told when a payment lands, or everyone who can open Accounts Receivable.
