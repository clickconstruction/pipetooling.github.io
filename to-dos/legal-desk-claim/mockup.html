The desk sizes a case by its open bills. Since v2.5849 How much? already adds the retainage the GC holds and leads with that claim — but anything more a dispute claims (materials left on site, damages, interest, attorney fees) has no home: on the one matter that needed it, those lines lived only inside the demand letters. The after adds a typed claim table on the Account tab, each line with its amount, its basis and the document behind it; the tile, the firm's cut and the settlement floor read the claim, and the packet prints the table.

Before The matter today — the claim stops at bills + retainage

  • The typed lines live in the letters. Materials on site and damages are a table inside a demand letter PDF; the desk never sees them.
  • The floor only knows the balance. The slider is a percent of the open bills — a floor meant as “most of the claim” can’t be said.
  • The print stops at the ledger. The packet's worth page shows bills and retainage; the firm re-types the rest from the letters.

After The claim table on the Account tab — two lines fill in on their own, the office types the rest

  • One table is the claim. Open bills and retainage fill in from AR and the job; materials, damages, interest and fees are typed once, not re-typed per letter.
  • Every line carries its paper. An amount, a basis (statute or contract clause) and an Evidence document the firm can open.
  • The tile, the cut and the floor agree. How much? reads the claim with the balance inside it; the floor can be a percent of either; the packet prints the table.
