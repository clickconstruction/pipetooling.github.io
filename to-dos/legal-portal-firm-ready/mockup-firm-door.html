6 Is this the best we can do?

  1. A new tab to Settings, or a window over the desk? A window. You are mid-review when you notice the firm is wrong, and leaving loses your place. Every other tool on the lien and legal desks opens as a window over them. Open in Settings stays for the full page.
  2. A third button in the header? No. Two firm buttons already sit there, and the name is where you looked. Folding all three into one firm control is the cleaner end state, so it is PR 3, after the demo.
  3. Rename the stand-in, or replace it? Renaming is right for fixing a typo, but it keeps the firm’s people, its link and its accounts. A stand-in’s test people would then get the real firm’s emails. So the window shows what is on the firm now, and offers Replace as its own path. For ZZ Test Firm, replace it.
  4. Can Replace be two saves from the page? No. Between retiring the old firm and adding the new one, no firm is active, and a failed add would leave none. The database allows one active firm, so one call must do both or neither.
  5. What about accounts already with the stand-in? Replace waits until they are pulled back, and lists them with their own Pull back…. Moving them silently to the new firm would hand a firm accounts it never agreed to take.
  6. Why the particulars in the same window? They are the other half of setting up the firm side, and the firm reads them on every packet. The count, like 4 of 8 filled, makes the gaps obvious before the meeting.
  7. Who may change the firm? Today only a dev, and the database enforces it. Opening it to masters is one policy line. That is your call, so the mockup keeps it dev only.
