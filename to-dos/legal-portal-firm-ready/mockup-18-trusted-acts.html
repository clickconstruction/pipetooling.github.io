The hourly limit

Today

30 acts per firm per hour, across every matter. A paralegal entering a month of costs on one matter locks the whole firm out: "Too many changes in the last hour."

Proposed

60 acts per matter per hour. The message names the matter: "60 changes on Lenox Builders in the last hour is the limit. Try again after 3:40 pm."

Is this the best we can do?

Should "recorded by" be typed, or picked?
Picked from the firm's own Notifications list, so the name is one the firm vouched for. If the list is empty the field reads "the firm" and the act still saves. The choice is remembered on that browser, so it is one pick per person, not per act.
Undo, or delete?
Undo with a reason. The stream is the record a court may read; a delete would make it unreliable. The undone row stays, struck through, with who undid it and why, and leaves every total.
Can the firm undo the office's rows?
No. Each side undoes its own. The firm asks in the conversation for anything else.
Is a client key enough against double saves?
With a unique index, yes. The first PR checks for the key before it saves. The second adds the index, so two requests in the same instant cannot both land.
Why per matter, not per firm?
The limit guards against a runaway script on one link. A real month of costs lands on one matter, and a per-firm cap punished the busiest matter's neighbours. Sixty per matter keeps the guard and frees the work.
