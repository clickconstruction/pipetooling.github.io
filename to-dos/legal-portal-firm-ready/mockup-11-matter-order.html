Is this the best we can do?

  • Which order? Largest balance first. A firm works the money: the biggest claim is the first call, and it is what the owner will point at in the meeting. The newest referral breaks a tie, because a matter just sent is the one the firm has not read yet.
  • Balance, or total demand with the firm's fees? The balance. Fees are the firm's own entries; ranking by them would move a matter up because the firm billed it. The balance is what the customer owes the company.
  • Sort in the function or on the page? On the page. The balance is the packet kernel's figure, built on the page from the records. The function cannot rank by a number it does not compute, and a second copy of the money rule would drift. The function's order stays as the fallback and the tie after the tie.
  • Say the order on screen? Yes, in the list's caption, so nobody wonders why a new referral sits third.
  • The digest email? It lists the matters in the function's order and carries no balances. It is left as it is.
