Is this the best we can do?

Does the firm see the reason, or only the count?
The count. A hold exists for something that is not the firm's business, such as a family matter or a note about our own staff. Printing the reason would print the secret. The count tells counsel to ask, and the office answers in the conversation.
Does this need a migration?
No. held_overrides keeps its column and its meaning, true means held. The reasons ride in the same JSON under _reasons, which every reader of the booleans already skips. An old false (share a pre-bill entry) is now the default, so it changes nothing.
Should a hold need a reason?
Yes. Records are discoverable either way. A reason written when the hold is made is what the office can defend later. The Hold back button stays disabled until a reason is typed.
What about the matter already with the firm?
Its pre-bill entries start going to the firm on the next portal load. That is the owner's decision taken on purpose. The release note says so, so nobody is surprised.
