Is this the best we can do?

  • Where does the matters list go? First. On a phone the firm opens the portal to find one account; a list it must scroll past the whole packet to reach is no list. With one matter it is a single card, so it costs little.
  • Hide the side rail on phones? No. The particulars are what a paralegal copies into a petition. They follow the matter.
  • Cards instead of tables on phones? Not now. The ledger and the jobs tables are records a lawyer reads in columns, and their own sideways scroll keeps the columns. The dates no longer break across two lines, so a row is one row.
  • Inline styles with a width check in JavaScript? No. The repo's pattern is classes in src/index.css with a media query (the Lien desk title bar, the Submittals road). The page's grid, the forms and the job timeline moved to .legalPortal* classes; the colors stay inline and literal, since the portal is pinned light.
  • Why 720 px? The side column needs 260 px and the matter needs about 420 px to read a ledger row. Below that sum, one column reads better than two.
  • Tabs that scroll sideways? No. The five tabs wrap to two lines, so Fees & steps is never hidden off the edge.
