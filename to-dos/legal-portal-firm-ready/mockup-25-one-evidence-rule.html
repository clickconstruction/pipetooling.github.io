One evidence rule

Two jobs of the sample GC's account. Job 1042 has two approved clock sessions in May and one session from June 12 nobody has approved. Job 1189 has two sessions on August 20 and 21, neither approved yet. The Evidence tab and the lien clock counted every session that was not rejected; the Chapter 53 timeline counted approved sessions only. So one packet said two things.

  • One rule: an approved session that was not rejected or revoked is evidence. Hours, the days worked, the GPS count, the sworn-account check and the lien clock all read it, as the timeline already did.
  • Sessions waiting for approval are not hidden: the row says "2 awaiting approval".
  • On the desk, job 1189's gap says the sessions are waiting, so the office approves them in Hours before it refers the account.

Is this the best we can do?

  • Count unapproved sessions and let the timeline catch up instead? No. An approved session is the one a person checked. The lien dates a firm files on must rest on records the office stands behind, and the timeline, the lien desk and the § 53.056 forecast already use approved only.
  • Hide unapproved sessions from the firm entirely? No. "Awaiting approval" tells the firm more evidence is coming and tells the office what to do. The count carries no hours or dates.
  • What about the job's own last work date when no session is approved? It stays the fallback, the same one the timeline uses, so the two still agree.
