Is this the best we can do?

Percent of what?
Of the open balance the day the firm proposes, by the same rule the packet uses. A percent fixed at referral would go stale as payments land. The page and the function compute it with one shared kernel, so the firm never sees one floor and meets another.
Dollar and percent together?
One or the other. Two floors raise "which one wins" on every proposal. The database refuses both at once.
Should the firm see the floor?
Yes. That is the point of a threshold: authority the firm can act on without calling. It is not shown to the customer anywhere.
What if the firm records Settled with no amount?
With a floor set, the amount is required. With no floor, it is optional, and the step moves the stage as before.
Reuse the sign-off plumbing?
Yes, reversed. The firm's proposal is a question with flavor settlement; the office's answer carries the question's id and signed off yes or no. One database function writes the answer and moves the stage together, so a sign-off can never land without the stage.
