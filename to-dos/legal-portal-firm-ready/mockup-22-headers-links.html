Is this the best we can do?

  • Does a Referrer-Policy on a JSON answer protect anything? Barely: a JSON answer opens no links. The real path is the page's own address, which carries the firm's key. Refined: the page sets a no-referrer meta while it is open, on top of the rel="noreferrer" its links already carry. The header stays on the functions as a cheap default.
  • Will fifteen-minute links break a firm that reads slowly? They would, so the page reloads itself at ten minutes before any link can die. The first draft only shortened the life; the reload is what makes it safe.
  • Why confirm Rotate at all? It is the more dangerous of the two buttons: it looks like housekeeping, and it silently kills every link in the firm's inbox. Now it asks, and it says to send the new link.
  • Anything else? Both functions now look the link up by its hash first and fall back to the raw column. That is harmless today and lets the hash-only migration land without a function change.
