Is this the best we can do?

  • Should sending the link also put the address on the email list? No. The two guards stay: nothing reaches an address until a person at the firm adds it and clicks the confirmation. The welcome email asks them to do exactly that as step one.
  • One address, or several? The firm's address on file is often a shared inbox; the attorney is often the person who reads. The card takes the address on file and typed ones, at most three, in one email (the rest on copy), so the firm sees who else got it.
  • Who is it from? From the company, signed by the person who pressed send, with reply-to set to them. A firm that replies reaches a person, not a no-reply box.
  • Does it need a stop link? No. It is one email a person sent once. Its footer says nothing else is emailed unless someone at the firm adds the address.
  • What happens after Rotate? The emailed link stops opening, so the card's Sent to … line starts over for the new link, and the Rotate toast says to send the new one.
  • Where does “Sent to … on …” come from? The filed copy in sent_documents (kind legal_firm_link, under the link it carried). No new column: the record of what went out is the one place it is kept, and the office can open the copy from it.
  • Is a passing failure line enough on the portal? For tonight, yes: it replaces a Saved that was untrue. A line that stays against the person until an email gets through is item 26.
