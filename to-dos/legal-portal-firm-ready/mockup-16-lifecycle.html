Is this the best we can do?

Should "settled" close the matter if the office never closes it?
No timer. The desk's With the firm list shows a settled matter with a Close button, and the Pipeline chip reads "settled · close it". An open settled matter costs nothing, and closing it too early is the bug we are fixing.
Should a backward step be refused?
No. The firm did the thing, so the entry is recorded with its date. Only the stage waits for the office. Refusing would lose the record of a second demand.
Is "payment plan" a step on the ladder?
It sits beside it. A plan can start after a demand or after a judgment, and when it defaults the firm sues. So moving into or out of a payment plan is never treated as moving back.
Should a pulled-back matter keep showing the whole packet?
First draft kept it. Refined: the customer's records leave the portal the moment the office pulls the matter back, because counsel no longer represents us on it. The firm keeps what is its own: its fees and costs, the conversation and the reason.
Can this ship without a migration?
Half of it. Settled staying open and the backward guard need only the functions and the page, so they ship first. The four new stages need the stage check widened. The pull-back reason needs two columns. Those ship second, after the page already knows the new stage names.
