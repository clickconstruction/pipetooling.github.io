Is this the best we can do?

  • Why not keep "sworn account" and just soften it? Because it is a conclusion. Whether a Texas sworn account under Rule 185 fits is the firm's call, and a portal that says it holds invites the first question in the meeting. The three facts behind it are what the firm needs, with their dates.
  • Keep the words parsed from the gap strings? No. The firm's print packet (item 1) read swornMissing strings back with startsWith. A wording change on the desk would silently empty the firm's facts. The packet now carries a structured record per job, and one kernel turns it into words for the page and the print.
  • Show the invoice's terms instead of the credit posture? The invoice row in the payload carries no terms or due date today; the printed bill says Upon receipt for every job. Printing a due date we do not hold would be worse than none. The row goes. Contract terms, interest and the fee clause are item 14, which adds them to the payload.
  • Two places for the same facts? No. Account's Jobs table carries the job's record. Paper keeps the paper: the agreement and its PDF. A footnote says where the rest is.
  • Is "crew on site with GPS" true for a field report? Yes. A report with GPS or a clock session with GPS each place a person at the job's address. The Evidence tab carries the counts.
