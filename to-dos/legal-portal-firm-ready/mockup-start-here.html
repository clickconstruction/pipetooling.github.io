What comes with each matter

  • Lien rights are kept. Each job's notice months and affidavit deadline run from the last day on site.
  • Notices go out on time. Each one is on file with the day it went.
  • The evidence is gathered. The agreement, bills, payments, crew time on site with GPS, notices and the demand.
  • Every call, promise and note is on the record, with its date.
  • Click keeps the office work. Payments, job changes and calls to the customer stay with Click.

Is this the best we can do?

  • First draft: an About page. The pitch bullets as one long page, and a help page beside it. Dropped. Nobody reads a long page, it does not ask anything back, and it says nothing about where the reader is.
  • Live figures, never typed ones. Step 1's numbers come from the same book the Lien grid draws, so they are never stale. The sample portal shows the sample's own figures.
  • The pitch, curated. Kept what a lawyer needs on the job. Dropped the sales close and the pilot; that is a conversation, not a page. "What is not finished" became the Lien grid's own note on precincts.
  • Answers the office receives, not an email. The firm's answers land on the firm's record, and the Legal desk's firm door shows a dot until someone in the office reads them. The firm can change them and send again.
  • The real portal, not screenshots. Each tour stop opens the real tab and rings it, so the tour cannot drift from the page.
  • Never in the way. Start here opens first only on a browser's first visit. Matters are one tap from every step. Until the firm answers, Matters carries one quiet line pointing back to step 4.
  • The rules readable without an account. The sign-off question opens the Texas lien rules guide right on the portal. Today the guide only opens inside the signed-in app.
  • Plain words, enforced. Every line passes the app's plain-words rule in a test: one idea, no more than 20 words, no dashes or brackets.
