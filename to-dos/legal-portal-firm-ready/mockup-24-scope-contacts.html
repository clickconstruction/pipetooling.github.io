The contact log, scoped to the matter

customer_contacts is the payer's general contact log: every call and email about any job, any bid, or the account. For a GC that log is mostly bid follow-ups and other jobs. The table has no job column, and the portal sent the whole log for the payer, filtered only by the held-before-first-bill date rule. Example: Sample Contracting, referred for job 1042, also has job 1057 with Click and two open bids.

The rule

The entry's textGoes to the firm?
names one of the matter's jobs by number: 1042 · #1042 · J1042 · job 1042yes
names one of the matter's jobs and another jobyes (it is about the matter)
names only the payer's other jobsno, it stays with the office
names no jobyes, as account history
the digits inside money, a decimal, a date, a phone or a longer number: $1042 · 1042.50 · 512-555-1042 · 10425not a job number

A job's number is its HCP number, else its Click number, as the office writes it. Numbers shorter than three characters never count. After this rule, the held-before-first-bill rule applies as before.

Is this the best we can do?

  • Why not keep only entries that name a matter job? Most real entries name no job ("statement re-sent, no reply"). They are the account's history and the firm needs them. Dropping them would empty Their word.
  • The bid follow-up still goes. It names no job, so the rule reads it as account history. Leaving it out would need a job or bid column on the log; the call-mode and bid follow-up writers know the bid, so a later migration could record it. Not tonight.
  • A false match? An account entry that happens to carry another job's number standing alone ("273 Main St" when job 273 exists) would stay home. That errs toward saying less about unreferred work, and the office can see every entry on the desk.
  • Does the office see the same? Not yet: the desk's own Their word shows the whole log. The office's Preview of the portal shows exactly what the firm gets.
