What changed, and the hard look

  • Gone: the Before release gap list, the worth-it line (after 33% and $350 costs), nothing held back, a firm has not been assigned, the theory and basis line.
  • New: the firm's name and handling person on the cover; the matter's stage and referral date; Balance owed · Fees and costs to date · Demand as of today (the office's contingency rows are not fees the debtor owes, so they are left out); a running balance on the statement; an exhibit index that says where each exhibit sits; each job's record as facts (signed agreement 2026-04-22 by Pat Sample, or bill sent, crew on site with GPS, no dispute logged); the firm's own fees, with a total, and every entry on the matter with who recorded it; the company's particulars for filing in the footer; page numbers and a running footer.
  • Is this the best we can do? Three things were asked and answered while drawing it. (1) Should the demand number be on the firm's paper at all? Yes: the firm writes the demand letter, and the figure it starts from should be the one the office showed. (2) Dates stay as 2026-06-03 rather than June 3, 2026: every table in the portal and on the desk reads that way, and a packet that switches would read as two documents. (3) The exhibits still say Where inside this packet and not a file, because the files (invoice PDFs, the demand letter as sent) are item 12; the index is built so a file column can be added without re-lettering.
  • Left for later items: interest and the fee clock (14), the property record per job (6; the sample has none), the debtor's legal identity (13), invoice and letter files (12).
