Is this the best we can do?

  • Company name or "the office"? The portal already calls the people the firm deals with the office (Ask the office, From the office on the packet). That stays. Where a line names the party to the claim, it takes the payload's company name: the particulars, the record of contact, a direct payment to the company, the email footer. No line names a brand the payload did not send.
  • One toast for every act? No. One line cannot be true for a fee, a step, a payment and an added person at once. Each act says what happens next, from one kernel with a test. A step names the matter's new stage, so the firm sees the step took.
  • "Record of contact" or "What they said"? Record of contact. It matches section C of the firm's print packet (item 1). It also covers what the office did, a visit or an email with no reply, which What they said leaves out.
  • Stage words in one home? Yes. The chip, the digest email and the print packet read one map in the shared email builder, so the browser and the sender cannot drift. The office's own labels (With the firm · new) stay in legalStageLabel for the desk and the Pipeline chip.
  • Announce held entries? No. Item 29 shares everything by default. The intro says what the record is, not what was kept back.
  • "Right away" was plain. Why change it? Its label, Tell me, read as a chat prompt. Emails: Each event / Weekly digest names the thing being set and the unit that triggers it. The desk keeps right away on its own list.
  • Left for other items: the theory line and the Terms with Click row are item 4 (facts, not theories), so this PR leaves them. The owner's-answers band keeps counsel's pile letters: they are the firm's own short call from its memo, with the statute beside each.
