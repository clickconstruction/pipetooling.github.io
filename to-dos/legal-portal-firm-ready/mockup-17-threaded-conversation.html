Is this the best we can do?

Should questions and answers stay inside the steps table, with answers indented?
First draft did that. It buried a three-line exchange among demand, suit and payment rows, and the "Office" column had to mean four things. Refined: a conversation table of its own on both sides, steps and money left in On this matter.
Does a withdrawn ask need its own stamp?
No. The desk already acknowledges an ask when it withdraws it, and the kernel reads that as withdrawn. The firm kept seeing it only because the payload dropped the stamp. Returning acknowledged_at fixes both bugs at once, with no migration.
What about answers saved before today, which carry no question id?
The kernel threads an unlinked office answer under the newest firm question before it that has no answer yet. That matches how the desk wrote them: answer, then acknowledge that question.
Should "fee seen" email right away?
A paralegal adding six filing costs would get six emails back. Refined: an acknowledged fee rides the digest only. Asks, notes, answers and applied payments go right away, because each one asks for or changes something.
