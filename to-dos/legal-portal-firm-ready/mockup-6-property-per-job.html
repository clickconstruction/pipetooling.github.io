Property per job

A GC-paid matter, the shape most of the firm's matters will have. Sample Contracting pays; it keeps two office addresses on its customer record. Job 1042 is a tenant finish-out at 1200 Sample Pkwy, Kyle. That property record sits on the owner's customer (Sample Holdings LLC, Hays County, non-residential), and the job carries an owner override naming the owner of record. Job 1189 is a house at 88 Oak Hollow, Buda, linked to its own record (residential, Hays County). Before is the current kernel; after is the proposed one.

Is this the best we can do?

  • Keep the payer's other addresses somewhere? No. The payer's own address is already in Who owes. The office addresses are not properties the jobs stand on, and showing them under Property record is what misled.
  • Guess a job's property from its address when nothing is linked? Only on an exact address match against the payer's records, and the row says it was matched. A street-only match stays a suggestion for a person to confirm; legal identity is never guessed.
  • Send the override owner's email? No. The firm needs the owner of record and the mailing address. The email is the office's courtesy channel and stays out of the payload.
  • An older function? The page reads a payload without the new fields. Linked records are then simply absent and each job falls back to an exact match or its job address. Nothing crashes, and the deploy brings the records.
