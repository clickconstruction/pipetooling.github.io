After Apply: a toast — 2 invoices marked paid 9/15 · $8,625.44 to Reece — and the house's table refreshes with the same Paid On dates.

1
The payment comes first, with the day it cleared.

Today paid_at is whatever moment you click Apply. A Paid on date (default today, hint = the house's payment day) is written to every invoice in the batch through the same paidAtPayload path the invoice form now uses — one date for the cheque, not seven timestamps.

2
The link stops overwriting the PDF.

Rename it for what it is (the remittance or receipt), and only write it to invoices that have no link — blank leaves every invoice's Drive PDF untouched. That's the bug fix; the rest is polish.

3
A table, not a checkbox list.

Due date with an over-by-N-days pill (red past 60), the job each invoice sits on, the PO — the things she cross-checks against the statement. Filter chips replace "Show all invoices": Unpaid (default), Past due, On job account, Paid too.

4
Select all, select the late ones.

A header checkbox for everything showing; the Past due chip narrows first so "pay everything overdue" is two clicks. Sorted oldest due first, which is also the order the statement lists them.

5
The total is the button.

A pinned footer shows count · sum · what's left unpaid, and the green button says the amount it's about to record. Today you tick boxes and hope the arithmetic matches the cheque.

6
Panel scrolls, header and footer stick.

The same v2.3473 / v2.3476 pattern — this modal has the same un-capped panel that clipped the invoice form; a house with forty unpaid invoices already scrolls the inner list inside a dialog that itself can't.

What it would take: one client PR on SupplyHousesTab.tsx plus a small kernel (supplyHousePayment.ts: selection sums, over-by days from due_date, the link-preserving payload) — no migration. due_date, paid_at, on_job_account and the job allocations are all already loaded for the table. The link fix alone is a five-line change and could ship first as its own PR.

Deliberately left alone

  • No new "payment" table or amount field — the app records which invoices are paid and when, not the cheque itself. Banking already has the Mercury transaction; this stays the invoice-side view.
  • Green for Apply — it's the app's "money went out" colour on this page already (the Make Payment button).
  • Partial payments — not modeled today (an invoice is paid or not), and nobody has asked.
