Sub papers: a work statement, a payment record and a statement of account

Punch list #350. Every company, sub, job, street, number and amount here is made up. The three papers are one set that adds up, drawn as the PDF pages a sub would be handed. Then the presses that make them, the send window, a payment over several papers, the sub's portal, and the names.

A the same figure on two papers purple, dashed§ a sentence that waits on counsel's words The names on the papers are the picks. The owner's words for them are in section 7.
As it is today. The only paper for a sub is the printed sub sheet: the job, the lines and a total. It has no number, no payments and no balance. A payment has no paper at all, and no number. One sending that pays several sheets is saved as one row per sheet, with the method typed into the memo. The sub's portal lists their open sheets and the last 90 days of payments, and its Print press prints that page. No paper covers a period, shows a sheet that is paid, or carries a number the office and the sub can both quote.
