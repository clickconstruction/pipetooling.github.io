What it takes

One client PR. No database change, no new load. The desk already holds every job, owner, property and GC it shows. The search kernel grows from "sent letters" to "every job on the desk", the call sheet gains a practice switch that removes Save, and the assistant's guide gains a short section on searching and practising.

Calls to make

  1. Practice on a made-up letter, or on a real one? Drawn: made up (Pat Sample, Sample Builders, job 000). A real job would need its Save switched off too, and a real owner's name would sit on a screen nobody called about.
  2. Show "Letters out now" before anything is typed? Drawn: yes, newest five. A caller is most often holding the letter mailed most recently.
  3. The word on the box. Drawn: "Find", because it now finds jobs with no letter too. The blue button keeps its name, ☎ Someone's calling ›.
  4. Forgive a slip? Drawn: when nothing matches, the last word loses letters from its end, down to three, and the box says which letters it used.
