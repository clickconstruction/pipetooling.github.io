Thanks for calling about the notice on 9703 Lenox Hill. First — you did nothing wrong by paying RMC. You didn't hire us, and this is not a lawsuit. I need to ask you three quick things, and then I'll tell you exactly where it stands.
1 · What they still owe
“Do you still owe RMC anything — a final draw, a holdback, anything at all?”
If yes → “Please don't send RMC that money until this is cleared. The notice lets you hold back $7,902.00 from it. § 53.081”
If no → “Then what you already paid is between us and RMC. The law doesn't ask you to pay twice for anything you paid before our notice reached you.” § 53.084(a)
2 · The 10%
“During the job, did you hold back the 10% Texas asks owners to keep? Is any of it still with you?”
Held → “Please keep holding it until we send you a release.”
Released → “When did it go to RMC?” … Sep 10 is inside the 30 days after Aug 30 → “Texas asks owners to hold that 10% for thirty days after the job is done. Since it went out before then, the property can still be reached for that amount. I'm not saying you owe it — I'm telling you what the law says so nothing surprises you.” § 53.101 · § 53.105
Released after the 30 days, nothing on file → “Then that's done — there's nothing to hold.”
Never held → the same § 53.105 line.
3 · Their contract
“When did RMC finish your job?”
Done Aug 30 → the 10% hold ran to Sep 29; the sheet used it in step 2.
What happens next
“If RMC doesn't pay us, we file a lien affidavit on the property by November 16 and send you a copy within five days. I'd rather not — we'd rather pick up a check from RMC. The moment we're paid you get a release the same day, and a copy goes to RMC.” § 53.052 · § 53.055
If they ask “can I just pay you?”
“That's your decision on your contract with RMC — not something the law makes you do. If you want to, call me back before you write anything and we'll do it cleanly: a check payable only to Click, a release the same day, a copy to RMC. If RMC will email us that you may pay us directly and deduct it, even better.” counsel's sign-off on this job first
Never say RMC's other jobs · “not paying subs generally” · interest, fees, theft of service · “the Code requires you to pay us” · a joint check · “you are in default”
