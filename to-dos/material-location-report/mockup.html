The paper, grouping A recommended by job, then place, then cage or box

The job's section, its places in name order, each cage or box under its place with its lines on hand, the job's Unsorted pile after the places, the boxes with no place yet last. The jobs with nothing on site close the paper in one block, a line each, so the material reads first. A cage or box and its lines never split across a page; a job's head and a place's head stay with the first block under them.

The paper, grouping B the other way by location across jobs

One section per place name, folded across jobs (two jobs each have an Arena), each job's cages and boxes under it with the job named on every box. It answers what is at the Arena in one place. But a place is a row of one job, so the same name on two jobs is only probably the same spot; the Unsorted piles and the boxes with no place sit in sections of their own; and a job's share is scattered through the paper.
