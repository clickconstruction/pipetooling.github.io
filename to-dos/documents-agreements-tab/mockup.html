The tab new

This frame works. Press a chip or type in the box. Agreements sits after Sent copies, since both are the office's ledgers of paper. Each line is one agreement: its kind, its name, who it is with, the job or the person, its state in that kind's own words, and its day.

The second row of chips opens on Sent and signed, so a voided paper stays out of the way until its chip is pressed. The count on Waiting on a signature is the list of what to chase.

Who sees what

The database decides which rows a person reads, and the tab shows only to roles whose rows it would hold. Today the office reads every job contract and every person's agreement. An estimator, a primary and a superintendent read neither.

The plan

Small steps, each one PR. Nothing ships until the owner says build.

PR 1

The rules, alone. One file that turns any kind's row into a line: its tag, its name, who it is with, its state and its day, the chips, the find words and the month bands. Unit tests. No screen.

S
PR 2

The read. One database view over the agreement tables, where each table's own row security decides what the reader sees, as the Photos tab's view does. Sorted, filtered, searched and paged by the database. A test bed proves each role reads only its own rows. Pushed right after the merge.

M
PR 3

The tab. Agreements after Sent copies, for the office. The lines, both rows of chips, the find box, Show more, the empty states and the retry panel. A press opens each kind's own window. A help guide, the release note and the docs.

M
PR 4

Search. The Documents Search tab gets an Agreements section under Jobs, fed by the same read, so one box finds a job and its paper.

S
PR 5

GC mode's kinds. Our contract with the owner, a trade's statement of work and a trade company's agreement join the view. Each shows only to the roles GC mode gives it to.

S

Left out on purpose

  • Drafts. A draft is not an agreement yet. It stays in the window that made it, as on every Documents tab today.
  • What is missing. A job with no contract, or one marked Not needed, is the Contract sweep's list on the Pipeline. This tab lists paper that exists and points there.
  • Accepted estimates and signed bid proposals. They stay on the Estimates and Bids tabs. The tab points there.
  • Papers that are not agreements. A W-9, an insurance certificate, a licence, an hours statement, a lien waiver.
  • A machine rental. The app keeps only its agreement number.
  • Any change to who can read a paper. No row security changes.
  • Saving a signed job contract to Google Drive. Today its PDF is kept by the app and Find a file cannot find it. That is the follow-up, as its own card.
