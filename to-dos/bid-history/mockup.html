What is new, and what is not PR 1 → 5

The ledger (PR 1)One table, one trigger on the seventeen tables that hold what people type. Ships first and alone: history starts the day it deploys.
The pane (PR 2)Actions, not rows: an import is one line. Filters by tab and by person; a search box for a fixture or a value.
The switch (PR 3)Under-cell lines on the four numbers people type: price, count, quantity, hours. Two lines at most per cell.
Put back (PR 4)A value reverts through the normal save, logged as a change by whoever pressed it. A removed row comes back with what hung on it.
Not built hereNothing before the deploy is visible. Cover Letter inclusions/exclusions saved per bid, the labor sync keeping renamed rows, and the cost-override table's missing FK are PR 0, separate and first.
Owner's callsWho sees whose edits (one company says everyone who can open the bid). How long to keep (drawn as three years). May a non-dev put a row back (drawn as yes, for whoever can edit the bid). Which Edit Bid columns count.
