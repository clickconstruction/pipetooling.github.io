Should the Short view get the day's row?

The Short view is one screen for a superintendent: the job card, what waits on him, tomorrow and one door to everything else. It has no row of squares. The long Job Mode page got one today.

Below are three kinds of day. Each is drawn three ways side by side: no row, the job's two squares alone, and the whole row. Every phone shows the first screen only, at the size of a real one. What does not fit above the pinned bar is cut off here as it would be on the phone, and named under the picture.

The pick

A · No row, for now my pick

The page was made to take things away for a man who said the screen overwhelmed him. Nobody is on the Short view yet, so nobody has said what is missing from it. The whole row pushes Tomorrow and Everything else off his first screen on a morning with stops.

The first superintendent on Short should be asked what he looks for first. That answer is worth more than a guess made today.

If something is wanted now: B

B answers the one real gap, Directions and the job's window before a job is on the clock. It costs the same height as C on a working day and nothing on a day off. It leaves out the four squares that are one press away already.

Paint a map on the short pages is the same question as call 11 of punch list #392. One answer can settle both.

How this was drawn

The morning and the day with no job left follow the real page, measured read only on the one superintendent's own day. The page on the job is drawn from the page's code and was not seen on a phone. A real phone shows a little less than these pictures, since its own status bar takes room at the top. Every name, job and address here is made up.
