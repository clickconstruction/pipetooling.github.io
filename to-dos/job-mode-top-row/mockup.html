What field people pressed, the last 31 days

Read from the app's press record for helpers, subs and the superintendent. The counts are left out of this page on purpose and are written here as words.

  • About one field account in five opened the app. Nearly all of their time in it was on the Dashboard.
  • The job's window was the door opened most, by nearly everyone who opened the app.
  • The Schedule tab came second, at a little over half as many presses.
  • Arrived and Leaving in the job window, and On my way and I'm here, came next.
  • Leave report, the Customers tab and the Inbox tab were each pressed a handful of times.
  • Paint a map, the wide card, was pressed once.

Sample and test accounts are left out. The card's own Clock In, the press bar and the blue square are not counted by the press record.

Is this the best we can do?

The pick: C, the day's row recommended

The job's window is the most pressed thing a field person has, and today it sits a scroll down the card. C puts it and Directions at the top of the screen for the job the card names. Paint a map and My spend shrink to named squares that never move. On a day with no jobs the left pair turns into the two questions that day has: my hours this week, and when I work next.

The row already reads the day for Paint a map's second line, so C needs no new read for the job. Photo on site is a bet: nobody in the field added a photo this month, and one press at the top may be why or may not.

Weighed and left out

  • The person's own quick buttons. Only a leader, an assistant or a dev has any. All but one of the field accounts would get an empty row. A leader in Job Mode keeps his buttons on the line under this row, as now.
  • Call the office, ask dispatch, ask estimating. Already in the header, one press each.
  • Call or text the customer. On the card once clocked in. Before that the app holds the number back on purpose.
  • Turnaway, Leave report, Clock In. On the press bar under the thumb.
  • Checklist tasks due today. No field answer in a month. A count here would read zero every day.
  • Materials on site as a fixed square. Nearly every working job holds no boxes. In C it shows only on a site that has boxes, with the count. In A and B it stays on the card.
