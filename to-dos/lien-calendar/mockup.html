Pay dot. When the customer is expected to pay — the GC’s word, a promise, or the pay-speed estimate.
Hollow flag. A § 53.056 notice this sub owes, due that day. One per unpaid month.
✓Check. That notice is recorded as sent.
Solid flag. The last day to file the lien affidavit (§ 53.052). Green, amber or red by how close and whether the money lands first.
Green run. Room — the money is due before the flag.
Red hatching. The flag comes first (file first), or a window that closed — the lien is gone.
Striped bracket + faint flag. Property kind not set: the flag is at the residential date, but could be as late as the faint commercial one. Set the kind to pin it.
3A number by a flag. On a GC row: how many of its jobs share that date.
Dashed dot. No pay date yet. Click it to record what they said; the dot fills in and the room or file-first reading appears.
Any dot is editable. Click it (or drag it along the axis) to change the date, say whose word it is, and leave a note — the same “They said…” the row’s chip opens.
