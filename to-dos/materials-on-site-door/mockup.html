Where the door to Materials on site belongs

Today the Job Mode card shows a link to Materials on site on every job. Almost no job has anything left on site. Below is the card as it stands, then three other places for the door. Each one is drawn on a job with boxes and a job with none. The dashed ring marks what changes.

One link, on every job, in the middle of the card

The link sits between the day's stops and the pace card. It reads the same whether the site holds forty cages or none. On a day with no schedule it is still there, and it opens whichever job the tally lists first.

Way A

Say what is there, and say nothing when nothing is Recommended

The link leaves the card. A job whose site holds live boxes gets one row in the job's head, under Job calendar. The row counts what is there and opens the boxes. A job with nothing on site shows nothing at all.

The first box starts with the sticker the office hands the crew. Once stickers are printed for a job, the row draws on that job and counts them. The crew picks the box, sticks one on and points the camera at it, and the app makes the box. A crew with no sticker opens Job detail, then Materials on site, then Add a container.

Is this the best we can do?

Way A, with Way B's place for the row

A door should appear when there is something behind it. Way A is the only one that tells a crew, with no press, that boxes are on this site and how many are due a count. It is also the only one that takes the line off every other job.

The row sits in the job's head, where Way B puts its press, since what is on site is a fact about the place and not a step of the day.

Hiding the door must not hide the first box. The office hands a crew stickers before material is left, so printed stickers are the signal. The row draws on that job before its first box, and the scan makes the box. Leave report stays as it is, since a row there would be the same noise on every job that the link is today. Job detail's Materials on site fold stays as the plain way in on any job.
