Permit news on Followup, with a red count to answer

The owner, 2026-11-19, after the first mock-up: “I like the idea of the permit information being on the follow-up page with potentially a red numbered icon on the follow up header title when new permit information comes through for the estimator to verify or ignore.”

This page draws that direction. The strip leaves the Bid Board. Followup gets a lens, Permit news, and a red count of the lines you have not answered. Each line asks the one thing that matters about the bid and takes its answer in one press. The first mock-up, with the board as it is today and the other ways, is mockup.html.

The owner then read this page and asked: “Is this the best we can do though? Are we making it intuitive yet easy? Are we structuring things visually well?” The honest answer was no, on five counts. The first band shows the card before and after. Every frame below it is the redrawn one.

Every bid, builder and plumber here is made up. Marcus is an estimator with three new lines. Dana is an estimator with none. Priya works in the office. The two presses at the top right switch the theme.
