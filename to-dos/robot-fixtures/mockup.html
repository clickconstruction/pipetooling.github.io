Robot fixtures: the robot pulls the fixtures off the plans, the estimator picks which to add to her count

The owner: the robots' ability to mark up a set of plans and find the costs of parts has been reasonable and accurate enough that we are considering offering to our estimators the ability to load in a set of plans and have the robots pull out and bid only the fixtures to help speed up their bidding… an augment to our current flow that is not invasive… make it easy for the estimator to review all of the text in the plans related to those fixtures… these items would need to look different such as a lightly shaded row so while the estimator is bidding she knows that they were added from the robots and not her.
