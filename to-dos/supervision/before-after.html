Supervision · before / after · 2026-09-19

One switch instead of team leads

The owner's finding: "it does not matter what leader is on a job, so long as there is someone competent jobs can progress, and helpers can't go do most of anything without oversight." Two levels per helper and per sub — supervision required, supervision not required — and everything else is read off the schedule and the clock. Six pairs, drawn from the live screens.

1 · Where a lead is set

Before: a modal of leader → member links, three of its eight leaders archived, an assistant leading a team, nobody opening it. After: one switch on the person's row, on by default for helpers and subs, off when the office decides they can run a job. Masters have no switch.
