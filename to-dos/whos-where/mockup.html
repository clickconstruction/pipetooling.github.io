The owner's ask: "a timeline of who is on what job in the form of floating heads … flip through the time both of day and of date." Every head comes from a clock session or a schedule block. Nobody maintains this page; it is what the day looked like.

1 · Tuesday, 10:40 am

Flip the date with the arrows or the week strip (each day shows its head count). Scrub the time with the slider or press ▶ to walk the day. Islands are jobs; heads are people. Solid = clocked in at that moment. Hollow = listed on the schedule for that job at that time, not clocked in. The ring says the role.

3 · What the week teaches, and the one tap

Beside the day, the page counts who shared islands with whom this week. That list is the org chart for hourly, per-job work. On each island the page guesses the crew lead (the master or sub present; else whoever has been there longest) and draws a faint crown. The office confirms with one tap — the only button on the page — and the Team leads link is written from what the picture already shows. No modal, no cards to build.

This is what the helper try-out loop was missing. When crews are visible and confirmed here, Try out can default its who with? to the crew a helper is actually scheduled with, and the verdict card knows whom to ask without anyone building a team by hand.
