Who's where: the org chart as a timeline

The owner's ask: "a timeline of who is on what job in the form of floating heads … flip through the time both of day and of date." Every head comes from a clock session or a schedule block. Nobody maintains this page and it writes nothing. The week is the front door — the org chart is who worked together — and a day at a moment is the drill-down.

1 · The week: crews, not jobs

People → Who's where opens here. Heads are clustered by who was together this week — Dispatch's linked crew blocks say who was meant to be, the clock says who was. Tie strength is days together. Under each crew, the jobs it touched. The crown is the master or sub on the crew: read off the schedule, never set. Flip weeks with ◀ ▶; tap a day in the strip to open it (panel 2).

4 · What it does not do

The first drawing had a crown-confirm button that wrote the Team leads link. It is gone. The lead of a crew is whoever the schedule and the clock say it is, on that day; nothing persists, nobody maintains a list, and the Team leads modal stays what it is (hours approval) untouched. The helper try-out loop reads the same rule to know whom to ask about a trial helper.

This is what the helper try-out loop reads. When Bryan clocks out of Oak St, the card goes to the lead the day shows — Mike — with no team ever built by hand. On Cedar Park's kind of day it goes to the office instead, with the reason.
