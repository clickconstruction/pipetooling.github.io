Tap a person to see their jobs and what is not on a job yet. See the charges lists them — date, merchant, card, amount — each with Put on a job (the same split window Banking and Tally use). Refunds come off; Internal Transfers are not spend.

How it counts The same rules as the job's cost

Who a charge belongs to

The charge's attribution — set automatically from the card holder, or by hand in Banking. A person record without a login counts as that person. A card marked as a company card with no one on the charge goes to Company cards. No attribution and no card holder: Not tied to anyone.

Fuel

The fuel family's tag — the accounting label's tag first, else the bank's category — exactly as the job window's ⛽ line and the cost timeline. Card purchases only; a refund comes off.

On a job / not on a job

What the job splits put on jobs, and the rest of the charge. A charge split half onto a job shows half under On jobs and half under Not on a job. A charge already on a supply-house invoice counts as on its job.
