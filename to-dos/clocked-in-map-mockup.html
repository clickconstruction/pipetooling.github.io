What is on it
  • Map left, stops right. Tapping a stop selects its pin; tapping a pin selects its stop. The stops are the strip's rows regrouped by place, most people first.
  • Pins carry a count, popups carry the people: name, clock-in time, elapsed, memo, then Open job (the same opener the strip uses) and Directions.
  • The office is the anchor diamond the jobs map already draws, with its distance ring; office sessions sit on it instead of a pin.
  • Bids pin violet, jobs blue, the Bid Board and Dashboard map colors.
  • Live: the modal reads the same sessions the strip ticks and refreshes over realtime. No new subscription, no new query.

QuestionProposed
A pin is…A job or bid with someone clocked on it. People at one place share a pin; the badge is the head count.
Office sessionsSit on the office diamond with the distance ring, never a pin of their own.
No job on the sessionNamed under the map as “Not on a job” with the Assign door the strip already has. Never placed by guess or by phone location.
Address will not geocodeThe jobs map's line: “1 job has no map location yet · JP…”, with the job link.
ColorsJobs blue, bids violet, office navy: the Dashboard and Bid Board maps' colors, so one legend reads across all three.
FreshnessLive from the strip's own sessions; the header says the time and “live”. Closing and reopening costs nothing.
Who sees itWhoever sees Currently In: the Dashboard, People → Hours and Quickfill mounts of the same strip.
Map providerGoogle when the browser key loads, OpenStreetMap otherwise, the existing fallback.
Open jobThe strip's own opener: the tabbed window for the office, the read-only pane for superintendents.
Not in scopePhone GPS, breadcrumbs, a “last seen” trail. The map says where the job is, which is where the clock says they are.
Is this the best we can do? One thing the second pass added: the rail orders stops by head count and the footer links to the full Map page for the everything-view, so this stays a five-second glance and never grows filters. One thing it removed: a count on the button, since the bar already says (7).

What building it takes

Client only, one PR, size M. A pure kernel (src/lib/clockedInMap.ts: sessions → stops, legend, unmapped line, colors, with tests), the modal (ClockedInMapModal, reusing the two shared pins canvases and the address-geocode hook the jobs map uses), the button in the strip's header cluster, a render smoke, the release note and fragment, a help guide (“see where everyone clocked in is right now”), and a row in the Dashboard architecture doc. No migration, no edge function, no new RPC: every address the modal needs is already embedded on the strip's session rows.
