Keep a mail's files as a set on a bid

Punch list #368. Made-up bids, names and files. A mail tied to a bid brings an addendum or a revised spec. One press files what it carries beside the bid's plans in Drive and adds the set to the bid.

1 · Where you reach it: four doors onto one press

The press sits on the mail itself, wherever the office reads that mail. Nobody has to know which mailbox it came from.

Door 1 · the main oneMessages, the mail's windowOpen a person's thread and press the mail. The press sits beside Open the bid.
