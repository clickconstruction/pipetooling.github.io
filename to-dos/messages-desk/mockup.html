Messages, worked like a desk

With #139's six additions, Messages is a complete record and a complete inbox: what came in, what we never answered, who went quiet on us, what went out and whether it landed, every line back to the first. Its highest and best use is not another lane. It is three things: the loop switched fully on (emailed replies land in the thread, the phone hears a reply, a call starts from a row), a page the office works one person at a time instead of reads, and a strip that says whether it is working, every number a door to the rows behind it.

The office's seat

Taunya opens Messages on her phone, presses Answer all, and six people are answered in five minutes. Mine shows what she holds. A call she just made is logged where she is, with the day they gave.

The job behind the ask

Nothing a customer says goes unanswered. Every send lands and gets an answer, or a nudge. A homeowner who texts books a visit or gets an estimate in one press, before they call the next plumber.

The whole office

Three switches still off mean a customer's emailed reply lands in someone's own Gmail, not the thread. The owner gets a read on whether communication works, not a page of activity. One Messages, one front door.

7The seven decisions

1 · Where the strip sits

Recommended: the top of Messages, four tiles, each a door, over the last 7 days. The Dashboard's Needs you card can carry the one number (answered within an hour) later if asked. Alternative: a Dashboard card only.

2 · Show's default

Recommended: Everyone, with Mine one press away and remembered per browser, so nobody's row hides by default. Alternative: Mine by default for assistants.

3 · Answer all's reach

Recommended: Needs a reply first, then the quiet ones offered as a second pass at the end. Alternative: one desk over both lanes from the start.

4 · What a logged call does

Recommended: a team line that clears Needs a reply; a day they gave holds Waiting on them until that day, like a promise in the flows. Alternative: a plain note with no day.

5 · A lead's doors

Recommended: both — Book a visit opens Add Job filled from the lead; Start an estimate makes them a customer and opens the estimate. Either takes them off Leads. Alternative: Start an estimate alone.

6 · The switch card's home

Recommended: on Messages for the owner and devs, folded to a line, Later holds it a week, gone when all eight are on. Alternative: a Settings card only, nothing on Messages.

7 · What Nudge does

Recommended: fills the step's words and opens the send window, one read and one press, like Text Greg on Nudges from the flow. Alternative: puts it on Today's sends for the morning.
