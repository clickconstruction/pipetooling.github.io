Is this the best we can do?

What changed because of the pass

  1. One view, not four reads. The first draft read each table in turn and merged on the client. One security_invoker view keeps every table's own policy and gives one newest-first page.
  2. The Drive line is a state, not a yes or no. In Drive, On its way, Not in Drive yet with the reason, Still on the phone, Our copy not in Drive, Shared with no copy here: the row's own words, each with its press.
  3. Texts and email joined the list for the office and the estimator, since a customer's picture on a text is often the one that matters, and their policy already reads those rows.
  4. The primary's list follows the ledger. The report photos' gate names a primary on every job; the view adds the ledger clause the box photos already have, so a primary sees their own jobs' photos only.
  5. Drive-only photos stay out, and the foot and the job window say where they are, rather than a nightly index nobody asked for.
  6. The viewer's doors name the source. Open the report, Open the box, Open the thread: the office learns which press the photo came with, not just where it sits.
  7. The job window's Documents tab gets a line, not a tab. The strip under the address already is the job's photo list; a second copy of it would be two lists of one folder.
