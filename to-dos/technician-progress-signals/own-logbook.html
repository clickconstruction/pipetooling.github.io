Second pass, after the render

Checked in the browser at 614 px, no sideways scroll. The strip reads before and after, and one press flips it to Stop showing with the day and who pressed. The phone frames fit at 343 px. What the pass kept and changed:

  • Kept the strip, not a lone button. A button alone says nothing about the state. The strip says who can see the page, every time the office opens it.
  • Print stays on Dario’s page. The summary sheet is the paper Dario takes to the board with the signed form. It is the one control from the office’s page worth keeping.
  • The menu row, not the dashboard, for now. The Your week card later in #255 brings the number to the dashboard. Two homes now would be one too many.
  • The read is new and narrow. Dario reads the page through one read that answers only for the person signed in, and only after Show. The office’s read and the licenses table stay closed to teammates.
  • Left for later: Show to everyone at once, and a note on Dario’s Dashboard the first time the logbook is shown.
