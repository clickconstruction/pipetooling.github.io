Taking the GC off hands the bid to the alternate

Wendi: "When taking the primary GC off of a project in bids, I would like it to auto select the alternate GC as the primary." The ★ card gets an ×. One confirmed press takes the lead GC off, the alternate becomes the bid's GC, and every letter, note and date stays with the GC it really belongs to.

Wendi's seat

The lead GC drops the project. Today the only door is change ▸ — a search over every customer in the system, to find a GC already on the bid one card down. She wants the press, not the search.

The job behind it

The ★ is just who runs the bid. When the lead drops, the record must say "Caldwell out, Rio Grande leads" — and keep Caldwell's letters and dates as Caldwell's. Today they silently re-badge onto the new GC.

The whole office

The board, Followup, Waiting to hear and the robots' backtests all read these tables. One atomic swap keeps every reader truthful with zero changes to any of them.
