4A process as its steps

A run reported as a sentence is a stepper left undrawn. The morning run that texts customers, the path a reply takes to a phone, a punch on its way to a paycheck, a photo on its way to Drive, a workflow step telling the next person: each is a chain of steps the app walks on its own and reports after the fact in prose, so the reader cannot see where it is, what it held and why, or when the next pass comes. The shape is the steps in a row with the current one marked and a count on each, and the held or failed ones as rows with their reason and door.

Lives in the app already as Quickfill's stations, GC Review's Check → Send → Word track, the sub portal's four-dot rail, the outbox door's Needs you · Waiting · Sent today, and the batch rule in batchOutcomeWords (a batch names what it held, with the reason and its door). 24 of the 131 places take it; five are drawn here.
