The shape is one bar with the parts as segments on one scale, or the sum drawn as its rows with each row a door to where it is kept.

Lives in the app already as the Legal desk's money bars (moneyBars.ts, v2.6105), the Pipeline row's paid · billed bar, the G702's scheduled-value and this-period columns, and the Workbench's Where the profit lives. 24 of the 131 places take it; every one is drawn here, the eight that matter most first, then the other sixteen, then three on the pages they land on.
