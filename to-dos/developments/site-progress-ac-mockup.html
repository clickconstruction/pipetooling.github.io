Is this the best we can do? the pass

The combinationAll three keep A's sum above C's cells. They differ in where (a Money look, or the Pieces card) and in how much cell each row shows (the whole cell as a row, the whole cell stacked, or a line that unfolds).
The recommendation: A1A Money look, the band on top, one row per live job with the cell's parts laid across (progress, money, dates, next), the paid jobs folded, tightest runway first. The Pieces card is left at five columns, and the two cards answer two questions: Pieces what and who, Money how far and how much.
Why not A2Thirteen of the real site's twenty-three live rows are bids, which have no bar; the cell would be empty on more than half the table, and the sixth column breaks the table at 1400.
Why not A3 aloneThe dates are the reason to look; folding them behind a press hides the one thing the owner's screenshot leads with. A1 can borrow A3's unfold later for the full ledger and the note.
What this draft does not solveThe same as before: the percent is weighted by value and says so; the lien notice state needs the Lien desk's reads; a bid not yet decided is one chip beside the stages, not on the bar.
