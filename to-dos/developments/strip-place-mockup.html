Is this the best we can do? the pass

What he misread, and why he is rightA tick is a mark of completion and a score is a tally of what is finished; on nouns like Pieces and Plans they can only mean the things are done. The last draft chose them to show the review happening and told the real meaning in a hover title, which is telling after the misreading. The mark nobody misreads is the one on the place.
The tailA fact with no subject on it. Two honest fixes: put the subject back (C's line under the lit look) or move the fact to its subject (B's amber on the Plans look). B does it in four words where the eye already is.
The recommendation: BNumbers for the order, the filled one for the place, never a tick, no score, no tail; the amber count with its noun on each look that has something waiting. A says nothing the cards do not; C costs a line and moves.
Beside it, straight inThe Plans card's own line gets the same subject fix: 42 pieces have no plans filed when no piece carries an older set, and 7 pieces are on an older set than Oct 20 when some do, instead of older or none for both. And the cap's hover tell drops the sentence about ticks.
What this draft does not solveWhether the strip should be pinned at all on a phone, where it is 88 px tall above the app's bottom bar; a separate ask. And the page's first look, when every count is new: the amber on What changed would read 211 new, so the first look keeps the plain count.
