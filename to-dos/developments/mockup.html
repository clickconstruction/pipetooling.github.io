The app already keeps it as a record on jobs (a named row, the house icon on the Pipeline, a filter, GC Review's By development). This mock-up extends it to bids, gives it a page of its own, and keeps the questions a leader asks about it on the record where the answer is read later. green outline = new

3 · The Development page new PR 3 · PR 5 · PR 6

A page at /developments/<id>, like a customer's page. It opens from the house line on a Pipeline row, the Job window, the Bid window, a Bid Board row, GC Review's By development, Settings → Manage developments, and from the search box by name. It leads with what changed since you last looked, because that is the question the owner asked: when one piece gets an update, which others does it touch.

Ask the estimatorsAsk Dispatch
Doors into this page: the house line on a Pipeline row's Job column · the Job window · the Bid window · the Bid Board row's muted line · GC Review → By development → the group's name · Settings → Jobs & dispatch → Manage developments · the search box, by name (⌘K).
Lead with what changed The feed is a reader over rows the app already keeps: a bid's sends and contacts (its log), its outcome, its plans link and plan date (the bid history ledger records the change), a job's stage moves, bills, payments and reports, and the questions. Since you looked is kept on the device at first, like Messages' 7 days; a stamp in the database only if the owner wants it to follow him between devices.
The plans line The owner's case in one line: a new set on one bid, and which siblings were priced or are working on an older set. It reads the bids' and jobs' plan dates and links, nothing more, and its press is the same Ask.
Not in it yet Harlow Ranch's 2 paid jobs and 1 bid share the address and never got the pick. The page says so with a count and one press, so a development fills up without 30 edits. The match is the members' own addresses, so the development needs no address column of its own; the head's address is the one its members share. The real example has 20 jobs and 13 bids in that state.
Who opens it The office set sees all of it. An estimator opens it from a bid and sees the bids, the jobs' names and stages, the plans and the questions, with no money on the jobs (a definer read, the way the Money lens reads a won job for them). Primaries, superintendents, subs and helpers do not reach it.
A page, not a window A person stays on it, it has views of its own, and it opens from bids and from jobs. That is the rule punch list #182 wrote for the Legal desk, and the Customer Hub is its precedent.
Not built, by design No money on the development itself (no bills, no development statement beyond GC Review's). No scheduling. No development on estimates. No per-development robot. A development is a record for finding and reading, as v2.1198 made it.

4 · Kept on the record
The question and every reply stay on the development page's Questions card and on the bid or job it was about, open first, then answered, newest first. The development's What changed feed lists the ask and the answer as two lines. Nothing is re-typed into a note.
One lane, read from three places: the inbox (the answerer), the record (anyone reading it later), the feed (the leader coming back to the site).
Why the request lanes and not a new table A question needs a to, an open state, an inbox someone watches, a push, an age, a thread and a close. estimator_requests and dispatch_requests have all seven, their notes tables carry the thread, and the people already watch them. A new table would be a fourth internal lane beside the robots' questions and the plans questions, with a fourth inbox nobody has yet.
Why not Messages Messages is one thread per outside person. The estimator is not a contact, and a question about a site has no customer to tie it to. Its slots are the wrong shape for an internal ask.
Why not a note A job note or a bid note has no to, no open or closed, and tells nobody. It is where an answer used to be retyped by hand.
What the lane gains development_id beside bid_id and job_ledger_id (still one reference per request), a read for the office of every request on a record (today the asker, the group and a dev read a request), and the record-side section with its Ask.

5 · Is this the best we can do? the pass

What a first-timer misreads Development beside Project (the bid's Project Name, the Projects container) and beside the customer. The page's second line says what it is in one sentence, and the picker sits beside Project so the two are seen together, each with its hint.
What the leader needs first Not the lists. What moved since they last looked, and what is waiting on someone. The first draft led with the bids table; the feed moved to the top and the counts line says 1 question waiting.
Tag or record A record, and it already is one (v2.1198 chose it over a free-text tag so grouping cannot fragment on a typo). The mock-up adds nothing that would make it a tag again: the bid gets a foreign key, the page a route.
Dead on arrival The real site has 13 bids with no development and 20 paid jobs without it. A page that shows 9 jobs and no bids on day one is dead. So PR 1 ships the share this address suggestion on the Edit forms and PR 3 the page's not in it line with its one press.
Does the question tooling duplicate Messages No, once it rides the request lanes: Messages stays the thread with people outside, the requests stay the asks between us. The first draft had a new table and a new inbox; cut.
What the rendered page caught The head said 2 open bids over a table of 3, and an open-bills figure no job carried. The plans line named BP430 twice. A Dispatch question about the job sat on the bid as if asked there; a question now shows on the record it was asked on and follows a bid onto its job, one direction. The not in it press sat inside the Jobs card and read as a jobs-only thing; it is its own line above the lists. The Pipeline's house would filter while the window's house opened the page, with nothing between them; the filter chip now carries the door.
Left out on purpose A development on estimates and on GC projects (no ask), a development statement with money (GC Review's By development already prints one), a timeline chart, scheduling by development, a robot that reads the site. Each is a later card if asked.
