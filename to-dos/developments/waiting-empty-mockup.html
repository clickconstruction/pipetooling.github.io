What a site waits on. The Pieces table already knows three kinds, in its Waiting on column: a question to the estimators or Dispatch, an answer from the customer on a bid that was sent, and a payment on a job that was billed. The tile counts the first kind, because that is the one answered in this app; the other two are counted by the tiles beside it (N sent under Bids, Open on bills). An empty card can show all three, each with this site's state and its press, instead of a sentence about the first. Three ways, then the pass.

Is this the best we can do? the pass

Show, not tell, for an empty countA sentence can only describe. The card's own shape (a cap, rows, a press) can show the kinds a site waits on and where this site stands on each. A does that with the three kinds the Pieces table already names, so the page has one idea of waiting.
The recommendation: AThe tile keeps counting questions (the kind answered in this app); the card shows all three kinds with the site's state, the two presses under Questions, the sent bids and the unpaid bills as rows when there are any. The head reads Nothing waiting when every kind is empty and Nothing waiting on us when only the other two have rows.
The pressesAsk the estimators and Ask Dispatch open the Ask sheet with the lane set (PR 4; on the spike the toast says so). Go to Questions stays the foot. The same two presses can sit on the Questions card's empty state, which today is also a sentence.
The other empty cardsEach names one kind already (Every bid is decided, Nothing billed is unpaid), so a sentence is short there; if the owner wants, Open on bills can show the jobs nearest to a bill (Working, Ready to bill) the same way.
What this draft does not solveWhat a site waits on from us that is not a question: a bid not yet sent, a job not yet scheduled. Those are the Pieces rows' own words, and a fourth kind here would make the card a second Pieces table.
