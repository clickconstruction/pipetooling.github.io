Should we also make it easy for a user to create a bid or create a job and then in the act of doing so have a modal pop-up where they can backfill it with information.

What the real site needs this for. Most of its jobs have no bid because the work was sold before the bids were kept in the app; the pairing tool finds a few likely pairs and the rest have no bid to pair with. So the missing half is usually a bid for a job that is already done (born won, worth what the job billed), and sometimes a job for a bid that was won, which the app already makes with New Job from a bid. One shape for both directions, from the row's gap, with the facts this record knows already filled in.

A · The full form, prefilled how New Job from a bid works today

The gap's press opens the record's own form (New Bid, or New Job) with every field it can copy already filled, and the pair made on save. Nothing new to learn; every field is there to change. It is the whole form for a record whose three facts you already know.

The gap's press (make the bid on a job's row, make the job on a bid's) opens a small sheet with the few facts the new record needs and cannot be sure of, each already filled from the record it is made from and marked with where it came from. What it does wellFour facts to glance at, not twenty, and each says where it came from, so the person is confirming, not typing. One press makes the record and the pair, so the row reads whole at once. The same sheet both ways, so there is one thing to learn. The real site's twenty jobs are twenty presses.
What it costsA bid made this way is thin: no counts, no sent date, no plans. It reads as won on the Bid Board and in the win rate, which is true but changes the numbers; the sheet says so. Undo must delete the new row, which only a dev or the bid's maker may do today.
What the sheet asks and what it copiesAsks: the name, the value, the day, the estimator (job → bid); the name, the revenue, the stage (bid → job). Copies silently: customer, address, development, trade, and for a job the budget copy New Job from a bid already makes. Never asks: due date, sent date, GC contact, plans, which it cannot know.

Is this the best we can do? the pass

What the press is forThe owner's word was backfill: the record exists in the world, the app lacks it. So the sheet's job is to show the few facts the app is guessing (the value, the day, who) and let the person correct them before the record lands, not to run them through the full form (A) or to land it unseen (C).
The recommendation: BA backfill sheet from the row's gap, both directions, the guessed facts up top with where they came from, the rest copied silently, one press that makes and pairs, Undo in the toast, the full form a link away. The sheet for the bid → job direction reuses what New Job from a bid already copies, so it is a shorter door to the same record.
What a backfilled bid should carryBorn won with outcome_at on the day given, the agreed value, the job's trade, customer, address and development, the estimator named, and a note line on the bid (made from J887 on the Development page) so the Bid Board and the win rate can tell a backfilled bid from a bid that was priced and sent. No sent date, which keeps it out of the sent counts and the pending pile.
Where it livesPair mode only, beside came from… and became…, so the table stays quiet off the mode and the three presses on a gap read as one family: pair with one that exists, or make the one that does not.
What this draft does not solveUndo of a made record is a delete, which the bid policies allow only to its maker and a dev today, so Undo may have to read open the bid and delete it for others. A backfilled bid has no counts, so Pricing and the robots read it as empty; it is history, not work. A job made from a bid that was sent to several GCs picks one customer, as New Job from a bid does.
