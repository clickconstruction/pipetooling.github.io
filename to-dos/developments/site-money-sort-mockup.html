How the Pipeline does it. Its rows sit in stage sections that never reorder; a sort changes the order inside every section, reached from a column header (the Progress & payment header sorts by percent complete, the Working header's ⇅ puts the next visit first) or the ⋯ menu's Sort group, with a Sorted: % complete × chip while a non-default order is on. The device remembers the orders that are settings; percent complete is a look and is forgotten on leaving. Minimising on this page is already two things: the What changed card folds to one line, and the Done fold hides the paid pieces behind Show them. Three ways to give the Money look both, then the looks themselves.

S1 · Stage sections that fold to their sums; the rows sort inside them from the column heads recommended

The rows sit under stage heads as on the Pipeline: Billed, Working, Waiting, Paid. A section head is one line that carries the section's sums (the count, what is left, what is past due), and its chevron folds the section to that line, so a folded section still tells. Paid starts folded; the device remembers the rest. The column heads are presses: Dates sorts by the tightest runway (the default), Progress by percent complete, Money by most left, Job by number; the chosen head wears the arrow and the card's head the Sorted chip. The band folds too, to one line.

What the device remembersWhich sections are folded, and whether the band is; the order inside the sections only when it is a setting (tightest first, by number), not when it is a look (percent complete), as the Pipeline does.
What it does wellThe Pipeline's own grammar: sections by stage, the order inside them, the sort from the column head, the chip that says so. A folded section is its sums, so minimising hides rows and keeps the reading. Nothing new to learn.
What it costsFour section heads on a table that had none; on the real site Billed has no rows today, so its head reads nothing billed is unpaid alone. Two chevrons on the card (the band's and the look's, below) must look different enough.

Is this the best we can do? the pass

SortThe Pipeline already taught the office its sort: the column head, the order inside sections, the chip. S1 keeps that; S2 drops the sections to let a sort cross stages, which buries the stage; S3 adds a menu for grouping nobody asked for yet.
MinimiseThree things fold, each to its own sum: the band (one line of the site), a section (one line of its stage), a look (one line of its chip). Each fold is a chevron on the thing's own head, each is remembered on the device, and nothing folded loses its number.
The recommendation: S1, with the looks' foldStage sections that fold to their sums, the rows sorted inside them from the column heads with the Sorted chip, the band foldable, Paid folded by default; and the chevron on every look's head so the whole page can be read as five lines.
The default orderTightest first inside Billed (the lien runway's own sort key), last work newest first inside Working and Waiting, number inside Paid. Percent complete and most left are looks, forgotten on leaving; the folds are settings, kept.
What this draft does not solveGroup by the bid (S3) waits on the pairs; a look folded by the device is one more thing the first-timer may not expect to find closed, so a folded look's line must say what is inside, never just its name.
