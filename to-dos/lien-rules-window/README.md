# Lien desk § Rules window, redrawn for the office

Grace, 2026-10-07: *"take a look at the lien desk rules, are they easy to read and follow a good structure? how could we improve it for a user?"* — then *"mock it up, then ask yourself is this the best we can do, then make the changes."*

## What was hard

- The window opened a third of the way down a 7,100 px page of 2,300 words, with the index 1,900 px above and no sense of place.
- Headings were the statutes' names, not the office's questions.
- The dates people came for were prose. One example box covered one month.
- *What the app does* listed screens; it did not say what to do.
- Seven rules counsel had not read were only listed at the foot.
- Nothing said what the rules meant for the job on the desk.
