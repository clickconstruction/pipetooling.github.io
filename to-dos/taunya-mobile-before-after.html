Is this the best we can do? is the second pass on the same surface: it asks what she is actually there to do and rebuilds the screen around that one job.

Caveat the ledger cannot remove: page minutes carry no device. Taunya's evening and weekend minutes and her three phone-specific reports (dispatch drag, Follow Up, supply-house notes) are the evidence she works from a phone; the dock and shell changes assume it.

Two things the capture found that are bugs, not design 1. Deep links bounce. Dispatch Mode's “back after 5 minutes → Schedule” jump (Layout.tsx, isDispatchModeReturnAfterAway) has no home-path guard, unlike the newer one-hour rule in assistantDispatchLanding.ts that only fires from / and /dashboard. Opening /jobs?tab=stages cold as a phone assistant landed on /dispatch-mode/schedule in two of three runs. Two rules, two thresholds, one missing guard.
2. The schedule hub's Day roster shows the sample and twin accounts (“Sample leader”, “Twin Estimator 1”, “Sample assistant”) to a real dev and to the assistant alike; the People rosters hide them (is_sample), the hub's loader does not.

#0 The shell hard every page

Nine header targets, three of them look-alike colored squares that do three different kinds of thing (open a page, open a modal, open a menu). Pages are split across the hamburger (Quickfill, Estimates, Jobs, Schedule, Projects, Bids, Prospects, Roadmap), the check-mark menu (modes and Documents, Materials, Checklist, Map, AR, Tally, Calendar, Help, Settings) and the gear. The dock holds Dashboard · Schedule · Inbox · Customers · PO: Customers is 1 h of her 189, PO is 8 minutes, while Pipeline (64 h), Quickfill (22 h) and Supply houses (10 h) are two or three taps deep. The header scrolls away, so a page's own tabs (Reports | Pipeline | Billing) are gone once she is in the list.

#1 Jobs · Pipeline hard 64 h 15 m · 34 % of her time

The Pipeline tab at phone width opens with New / Follow-ups / Forecast, the search, then Jobs on a map (the map, three distance tiles, 117 pinned jobs), three money tiles, and six Today's money opportunities cards. The stage strip and the sections (Waiting 20 · Working 42 · Ready to Bill 0 · Billed Awaiting Payment 70 · Collections 7) begin about 2,600 px down. Open Working and each job is a ~600 px card whose one big blue button is Ready to Bill, the status advance; the way into the job is the 24 px-tall name link. The strip wraps onto three lines.

#2 Dashboard hard 31 h 34 m

For an office assistant the first screen is the field crew's: a wrench, Clock In, quick clock, Job Report, then My Schedule saying “No blocks scheduled” twice. Needs you · 10, the cards she acts on, starts around 1,000 px. Below: AR / AP / Not billed tiles, the Jobs · Bids · Projects chips, the clock strip as a 613 px table (8 columns, sideways scroll), Crew Day (12 people), Recent Reports, Billing pipeline, My Time. 6,083 px in all.

#3 Schedule hub · Day hard 30 h 08 m

At phone width the hub renders Day / People / Jobs, + Schedule, ⋯, the date, then the whole roster grouped by role: Leaders, Assistants, Controllers, Primaries, Estimators, Superintendents, Subcontractors, Helper. Everyone is listed, “Free” or not, office roles before crews, and the sample and twin accounts among them. 4,200 px. Tapping a block opens that person's clock modal (Visual / Form, “What were you working on?”), not the block. The People tab (week pills, Add job / Copy to techs / Assign work, team cards) already fits a phone.

#4 Quickfill unusable 22 h 33 m

All 27 sections render open: 28,692 px, 567 controls, 239 text runs under 12 px, 30 inputs under 16 px (iOS zooms the page when one is focused), five tables, one of them 4,069 px wide. Each section's header is a 32 px title, a “Last marked” line, a chart button and a full-width “Marked at 9:17 AM by Taunya. Reloads in 11.8h. [Open now]” block before any content. The section jump strip scrolls sideways with two chips visible.

#5 Materials · Supply houses ok 9 h 51 m

Below it, Accounts payable renders two tables at 493 px and 10 columns (sideways scroll), and Job accounts a third. 7,457 px. #6 · #7 · #8 Estimates, Follow Up, Dispatch Mode schedule easy 4 h 23 m · 4 h 04 m · 3 h 24 m

Three surfaces already built for a phone. Estimates is a card list with 44 px buttons and a clear Ledger; its one tell is the “▶ —” expander at the foot of each card, which does not say what it opens. Follow Up is call mode: a phone-number button, four outcome buttons, Set callback; its tab strip wraps to two rows and “New Prospect” sits mid-strip. Dispatch Mode's schedule (the dock's Schedule) is the best phone screen in the app: two-week calendar strip with counts, person chips, block cards with the job-account chips, a floating add. The proposals above borrow its parts.

#10 People · Hours hard 1 h 21 m

#11 Customers unusable 1 h 03 m · a dock slot

Six stat tiles, eight filter chips over three rows, the search box on the third screen, then all 444 customers as 200 px cards with a PAID / BILLED / UNBILLED rail that reads “— — —” for most: 67,927 px and 3,370 tap targets on one page. It holds a dock slot. The Customer Hub a card opens into (Profile · Estimates · Jobs · Invoices, the journey, activity) already fits a phone.

#12 Jobs · Subs · Work ok 1 h 02 m

Work / Pay toggle, New work order, search, status chips over two rows, four stat tiles, then jobs as groups with a sheet card each: WINDOW, Set a window…, GC off, AGREED / PAID / OPEN, the “where it stands → next” timeline, a full-width Get it in writing, ⋯, and “$4,200 of work on a handshake”. About 500 px per sheet, readable, slow. The answer is the same card the Pay tab gets in #9: one line per sheet, the sheet's actions in a bottom sheet.

#13 The job window ok opened 452 times in 60 days, company-wide

Opened from the pipeline row, a dispatch block, the customer hub. The tab bar (Job · Edit · Bill · Costs · History · ×) is sticky and right. The body, in order: street-view preview, customer with phone and email, job-account chips, Their journey, then the activity feed whose composer carries Arrived · Leaving · Set % complete · Post note, the crew's buttons. Then Last work date, Last bill date, the Status stepper as small text links, Assigned team, Job start, Last work, Reports, Schedule and recorded time, Costs, line items, payments, invoices. ~2,400 px. The things an assistant does in it, change the status, bill, leave a note, edit a field, are on three different tabs and 1,400 px down.
