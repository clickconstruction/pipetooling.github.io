Before Every row is a nag with a door — none can be answered

The section recomputes on every open. Each gap offers one thing: a door to the surface that would change the fact (Contract…, Call mode…, Edit job…). When the fact is simply true and known — the work ran on a handshake, there is no paper to record — the office has nowhere to say so, the row nags forever, and the attorney reads a hole where the office has an answer.

  • No way to answerWork done without a contract can't be listed or explained — the only door records a paper that doesn't exist.
  • The app's own answer is ignoredThe job can be marked contract not needed on the contract strip; this section doesn't read it.
  • The narrative can't hear itNothing makes sure the known fact lands in the narrative — it has to be retyped, or it's lost.
