6 The rule, applied six ways

A desk is a page with its own address when a person stays in it, it has tabs or views of its own, and the page under it goes unread while it is open. A window is right when it is one act and the page under it is where you go back to. A third answer, a window with an address, is for a window that opens from everywhere and is worth a link.
