Notes on the canvas

Every name here is made up: the company, the client, the vendors, the site, the code name. No clause wording is drawn. The paper's words are grey bars.

Your call, 1c: Same place only records the match. It changes no job's address. Fix the spelling is a second, separate press.

Counsel's call, 2a and 2c: whether the paper prints the site. Drawn as a tick that can be left off. Before signing the vendor sees the code name only.

Your calls, row 3: one section holds our own people and the outside companies. A sub counts as an outside company. A job with no development keeps the site's controls in its own Documents tab.

Your calls, 4a: no way around the hold is drawn. A quote request belongs to a bid, so the site match covers bids as well as jobs. A held send goes by itself when they sign. The hold reads the job a send is tagged to, so a message with no job on it is not held.

Settled Nov 24, 3e: the office may turn secrecy on for a site. These are the same roles that can require a paper on a job today.

Doors to add, 1c: Fix the spelling opens a small window that names the jobs it changes. A row under No street number has Open the job, which lands on its address box.

Door to add, 2a: a company’s own record. The supply house window and the Customer page already list a company’s papers. Each line needs its site, and the list needs Send the NDA.

Doors to add, 3b: Every site in the filter row picks a site. A site on a line, pressed, filters to it.

Door to add, 3a: a job with no development has no site page. Its Documents tab carries the whole block: the controls, the held sends and the packet.

Doors to add, 3c: a Needs you line on the Dashboard when a send is held, an NDA waits or people owe the paper. A notice when a company signs, naming the held sends that went. A Secrecy chip in the page’s header that jumps to this block.

Doors to add, 4a: the held state at every other send. Quote requests on a bid, a supply house’s job account, a bid’s pricing package and room link, a submittal’s room link, asks to trade companies, sub papers, emailed reports, and a purchase order printed for a supply house.

Doors to add, 4b and 4c: Make the packet opens a window to look, then Download, Print and Send to the client. The copy is kept under Sent copies. The client’s page needs a sample on Settings, What customers see.
