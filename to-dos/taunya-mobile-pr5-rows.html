yours Your eight calls

  1. Customers — "Recent" means recently active (last job, payment, bid or estimate), which the app already knows. Recommended: yes. The alternative, recently viewed, means recording what each person opens, per device.
  2. Customers — build the phone view on the page, leaving Dispatch Mode's Customers tab as it is. Recommended: yes. The alternative grows the Dispatch Mode list and shows it at /customers on a phone: less to build, but it has no money, no bids and no portal door, so a phone would see a thinner customer than a desk.
  3. Customers — archived customers are never listed on a phone; a search can still find one, marked Archived. Recommended: yes.
  4. Subs — the ledger table is not drawn on a phone at all; Who's owed, opened, is the Pay view. Recommended: yes. The expand-row detail (back-charges, the story) is reached from the sheet's More….
  5. Subs — "Get all N in writing" opens the list first, then sends — it never sends from the board in one tap. Recommended: yes. These are agreements going to subs.
  6. Supply houses — the balance on the card is for office roles only (whoever sees Accounts payable today); an estimator's card stays without money. Recommended: yes.
  7. Supply houses — the notepad is the house's existing notes field, editable in place. Recommended: yes. The alternative is a dated log of call notes — who wrote what, when — which needs a new table and a migration. Say so if the history matters.
  8. People · Hours — the week grid is not drawn on a phone; the view says it opens on a wider screen, and a person's week is their own screen. Recommended: yes. This is the design rule the second pass settled for matrices.
