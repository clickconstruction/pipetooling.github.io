Drawn from the real screen 2026-09-17 · split checked on prod

Before Owner-secured debt can sit inside the red cells — today it barely does

The heat map is unpaid dollars by days past the invoice due date, per house. Since v2.2652 the Job Accounts tab bars treat job-account invoices — bought on the owner's account at the house, the owner's debt to collect on — as their own thing; the heat map does not. Checked on prod 2026-09-17: of 245 unpaid invoices, exactly one is on a job account — National Wholesale, $2,759.01, current. Every red cell on the map, Texas Plumbing Supply's $18,285 at 90+ included, is Click's own. So the question is not "which cells are wrong today" — none are — but what the map should do as job-account invoices accumulate, which the Job accounts train (v2.3423–v2.3440) makes likely.
