A text when a charge lands on your card, with a place to photograph the receipt

Diane, 2026-11-09: whenever a user gets a charge on a card assigned to them, what if we sent them a text with a place to take a photo of their receipt and upload it. Her calls the same day: every charge once it posts, no sign-in on the link, no quiet hours, a Needs you item for the office when the holder has no cell on file, and the push to Mercury's own receipt slot parked for much later (#300). The text carries its charge, so a photo taken through it lands on that charge with no guess. It writes the same ticket record as the Tally press (#295), and the office reads both the same way.
