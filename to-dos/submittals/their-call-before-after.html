What the step now says without being asked
  • Where it stands: one square a fixture, red for sent back, grey for waiting.
  • Which ones came back, the part, and the reviewer’s own words, with the door to change each.
  • Who is still owed an answer, and the two ways to record one.
  • Why the count is 13 and not 14.

Is this the best we can do?

The first draft, and what the second look changed

0approved
4sent back
9waiting
  1. The first draft was three number tiles. They look tidy and answer nothing new. She still had to go to step 3 to find the four. The list of what came back is the real content, so it moved into the step.
  2. One square a fixture, not a bar. A plain bar shows a share. Thirteen squares show the fixtures themselves, and the fourteenth, dashed, is the sink with no product. That settles 13 against 14 and 9 against 10 at a glance.
  3. The reviewer’s words are labelled. The first draft showed “TEL145” in quotes and left her to guess whose words they were. “they wrote” says it.
  4. The waiting fixtures are buttons that look like buttons, under a line that says to press them. The first draft made them plain labels with one shared Their answer button, which then had to ask which fixture.
  5. Buttons start with a verb. “Mark all 9 approved…” for “They approved the other 9…”.
  6. The step does not repeat the Rev 2 button. The first draft ended with a third copy of it. One plain line points to step 7.

Looked at and left out

  • Typing answers right in this step. Their answer already has a window that goes part by part. A second way to do the same thing would drift from it.
  • Guessing that “TEL145” is the model they want and offering to swap it in. It usually is, but the app cannot know, and a wrong swap goes to the GC.
  • Folding the waiting fixtures behind a count. Nine names fit on two lines and save a trip to step 3.

What it costs

  • The step is taller when something came back: about eleven lines here against three. It is shorter than the trip to step 3 it replaces.
  • On a version that was shared, parts are locked. There the list has no Edit, and its last line reads “Start Rev 2 in step 7 to change them.”
