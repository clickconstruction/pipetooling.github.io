Submittals tab: what to settle before Monday

The tab works when a submittal is shared from the app. The estimator emails hers and types the answers in, and on that road five things can lose work or mislead her.

I walked every step and every window on the live SpaceX bid (BP375, read only, nothing changed) and on the test bid (BP398). Each finding below opens a window. The left side is the screen as it is today. The right side is what it could be.

Where this stands, Oct 4. Nine of the twelve findings are live: 1 to 9, as v2.4477 to v2.4486. Three follow-ons shipped after them. Findings 10, 11 and 12 are not built. They wait until the estimator has used the tab for a week.

Each finding below carries its version. Its window says where the build differs from the drawing. The drawings are left as they were on Oct 3.
