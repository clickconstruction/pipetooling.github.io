If a step title jumped the way a pill does

punch list #17 · Submittals
drawn 2026-09-30 · after v2.4201 · not built

Since v2.4201 a pill on the strip opens its stage, scrolls to its controls and rings them. A step title on the road is a toggle: it folds or unfolds the step where it stands. The question: what if the title did what the pill does? Both panels are live — the road is a viewport the height of a phone's middle, so the lower steps start off screen. Tap the titles.

Try, in each panel: tap 5 · Share (a later step, folded) · tap 4 · Package (already open, on screen) · tap 7 · Resubmit (folded, off screen) · then tap the same title again.

Before today — the title is a toggle

  • Tap opens or folds, in place. Nothing moves, nothing rings. The caret says which way the next tap goes.
  • A later step opens with its controls held. The button is there, disabled, with its reason (v2.4169).
  • Only the pill jumps. Two doors, two feels: the pill takes you somewhere, the title reveals what is here.

Beforetitle = fold / unfold

Tap a title or a pill.

After the title jumps like the pill

  • Tap opens, then rings the controls. The ring lands on the button a frame after the step unfolds — the same ring the pill gives.
  • It scrolls only when it has to. If the controls are already on screen the page stays put; if they would land below the fold, it scrolls just enough. The title never yanks the page from under your finger.
  • The caret folds. A jump only opens, so folding moves to the caret as its own small button — the one thing that gets a little harder.

Aftertitle = open + ring · caret = fold

Tap a title or a pill.

What it would look like, in one breath

  • A folded step (5 · Share, 6 · Their call, 7 · Resubmit): tap the title → it unfolds, its held button rings blue for 1.6 s, and if the button sits below the bottom edge the road scrolls it into view. Same as tapping the pill, minus the long scroll from the top of the page.
  • An open step on screen (4 · Package, 8 · Procure): tap the title → nothing moves; the button rings. The ring is the only sign the tap landed. Today the same tap folds the step.
  • Folding: the caret ▴ at the end of the title becomes its own button. Tap it to fold a finished step back to its green line. It is a smaller target than the title is today.

Is this the best we can do?

  1. A plain jump would not do. Tapping "5 · Share" and having the page scroll the controls to centre — the pill's rule — moves the screen when the title was already under your finger. On a phone that reads as a lurch; on a desktop with the step centred it does nothing, so the ring looks random. The panel on the right scrolls only when the controls would be off screen.
  2. The opening was already the useful half. A title tap on a folded later step has opened it since v2.4169, and Procure never folds since v2.4201. What "After" adds is the ring and the scroll-if-needed — a confirmation, not a new ability.
  3. The cost is the fold. Today one big target does both. After, the title opens and a small caret folds. If the office folds finished steps often, that is the trade to feel before deciding.
  4. Where it would live if built. RoadSection gains an onJump beside onToggle; the tab's goToStage already opens, waits a frame and rings — it would take a scrollIfNeeded flag (scrollIntoView only when the control's rect is outside the viewport). One tab-test case per behaviour; the guide's road paragraph and the SU-12 map row follow.
