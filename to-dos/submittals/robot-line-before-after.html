Is this the best we can do?

  1. The chip carries the state, not just the word “Robot”. A stuck ask is amber and says the day it was asked. Hover adds detail. It never holds the only warning.
  2. It opens on focus and on a tap too. A phone has no hover. A tap on the chip pins the card open, and Esc or a tap elsewhere closes it.
  3. The button stays outside the card. Ask, Cancel and Take the ask back sit beside the chip, one press away.
  4. All three robot spots get the same line. The offer on a vendor PDF in step 3 and on a reviewer’s file in step 6 are the same four line box today. One shape everywhere was your earlier call, so they change together.
  5. The two cards end up the same height, so the takeoff card is no longer half empty. Nothing else on the cards changes.

Changed after your note. I had kept the two cards’ sentences, thinking a first-timer needs them on a fresh bid.
