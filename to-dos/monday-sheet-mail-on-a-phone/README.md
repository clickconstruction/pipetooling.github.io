# The Monday sheet's email stacks on a phone (#200)

## The ask

On 2026-10-27 the owner asked to put this on the punch list: *please add these two suggested tasks
to the punchlist*. It was found that day while the weekly send (v2.5883) was tested against the live
sheet. #201 is the other one.

## Found

- The dry run's HTML was rendered 390 px wide in headless Chrome. The three tiles keep one row. The
  bar chart's labels do not wrap, so the first tile holds its width. Net position is cut at the right
  edge and Cash is off the screen. People squeezes to a word or two per line.
- At 1000 px the mail matches the page.
- Some mail apps shrink a wide mail to fit the screen instead. Then the words are too small to read
  without zooming.
- The builder's own doc says the sheet is one *the owner can read on a phone or get by email*.
- The text part orders the sections differently. The HTML puts The weakest link under the tiles. The
  text part puts it last. The page puts it between the moves and the people, and stays as it is.

## The fix

1. In `mondaySheetHtml`, each tile becomes an inline block at most 312 px wide, so the row wraps on a
   narrow screen. The moves and the people get blocks at most 468 px wide. This is the fluid hybrid
   way of laying out an email.
2. Outlook on a desk gets the same blocks as table cells through `<!--[if mso]>` comments.
3. A `<style>` media query lets a block fill a phone where the mail app keeps it. Where an app drops
   it, the block stays 312 px wide and centred.
4. Each move's lever moves under its words, so the words keep the width.
5. The bar chart's amounts drop their ≈. One line under the chart says light bars still wait on
   approval. A narrow tile then fits all eight weeks.
6. `mondaySheetText` takes the HTML's order, with The weakest link after the three tiles.
7. The words and the numbers stay as they are. `buildMondaySheet` and the page do not change.

The mock-up beside this file shows today's mail and the proposal at 390 px, and the proposal at
960 px. Its numbers, names and jobs are made up.

## How to verify

- The builder's tests cover a block per tile, the Outlook comments and the text part's order.
- Render the HTML of a made-up sheet at 390 and 1000 px with chrome-headless-shell and look at both.
  The mock-up was made this way: `esbuild` bundles `mondaySheet.ts`, and a script calls
  `mondaySheetHtml` with an invented sheet.
- After the merge, run `npm run gen:edge-kernels` if the PR did not, and redeploy what
  `node scripts/check-edge-function-drift.mjs --full` names. Then run a dry run of
  `bridge-weekly-sheet` as a dev with `{ "dry": true }` and render its `html` at both widths.
- Press Email me this sheet and open the mail on a phone.
