The Monday sheet goes out by email to every dev each Monday once the switch is on. The mail sets the three tiles side by side in one table row. On a phone that row cannot shrink, so Net position is cut at the edge and Cash runs off the screen. The words and numbers stay as they are. Only the layout changes.

Nothing moves on a wide screen. The tiles sit three across and the moves and people two across, as today.

How Each tile becomes an inline block at most 312 px wide, so a row of them wraps when the screen is narrow. Outlook on a desk gets the same blocks as table cells through a conditional comment. A media query lets the blocks fill a phone where the mail app keeps it. Where an app drops it, each block stays 312 px and centred. Each move's lever moves under its words, so the words keep the width. The bar chart loses the ≈ on each amount, and one line under it says light bars still wait.
