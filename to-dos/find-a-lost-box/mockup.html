Find a lost box: Where is it?

My recommendation: one press on a box, Where is it?, that opens one sheet. The sheet says what the app knows in plain sentences, shows the newest photo, and draws the box on the view from above. Show where I am adds your own dot, a straight line and an honest distance. I found it pins the box where you stand, so the next search is shorter.

The short of it

Today

  • A box can carry a pin on the site map. The pin keeps no day and no name.
  • A box page says pinned on the map or nothing. It never says how far away.
  • Pinning is its own press, Pin on map. Nobody uses it.
  • A box that is not where the app says stays wrong until someone fixes it by hand.

My suggestion

  • The pin keeps who, when and how sure the phone was.
  • Where is it? says it in sentences, with the photo and the map.
  • Show where I am draws a straight line and says about how far.
  • I found it is one press that pins the box where the finder stands.

Your calls my pick first on each

  1. What is a crate, and who is looking for it? My pick: a cage or a box from Materials on site, looked for by the job's crew or the office. The rest hang on this one.
  2. What should the path be? My pick: the straight line drawn here first, then a way painted once to each place.
  3. How does a box get its spot? My pick: I found it, and later a one-press ask after a count, a photo and a scan.
  4. Who may be shown where a box is? My pick: whoever can read the box today. Nobody signed out.
  5. Who says a box is lost? My pick: nobody has to. This build serves the person asking.
  6. One way per place, or a map per lost box? My pick: one way per place, painted once.
  7. Try it first? My pick: yes. Pin five boxes on the test job with Use my location, some outdoors and some inside, and look at where the dots land on Map.
  8. Should a helper's search offer Paint a map? My pick: yes, when the painted way is built.
  9. Did you mean a tag on the crate? My pick: no, not now. That is hardware and a different card.

Two small calls this drawing adds. The press takes the place of the Pin on map chip, and a pin older than 30 days turns amber. Say so if either reads wrong.

Said plainly

  • The line is straight. It can cross a fence or a building, as it does above.
  • Indoors the phone is lost, and a phone knows no floor. The words and the photo do the last steps.
  • The dot moves only while the screen is on.
  • A pin is where someone said the box was, on some day. The sheet always says the day and the person.
  • None of this has been tried on a real phone. The try in call 7 is the first proof.
