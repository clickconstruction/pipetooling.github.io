#198 · a note The orange icons on the Stages row, and the Lien desk shortcut

Three row icons (Test report, the gavel, the fee) and the Lien desk shortcut are orange graphics, not words. The lens checks words only and never flags them. By the 3:1 rule for a graphic they are a shade under in light (2.94:1 on white) and fine in dark (5:1). Left alone under every option above. If the owner wants one rule for every orange on a light ground, the same word token lifts them to 5.18:1, and in dark they turn pale.

#199 · call 1 The trade tag on the portal's job band

The job band leads with the trade in its colour, as coloured words, never a pill. PLUM reads 2.68:1 on the band, ELEC and HVAC about 2:1. The app's own trade pills draw white on these same fills and fail the same way (3.15, 2.38 and 2.43:1); A's shades read with white too, so one source would lift both.
