Window as text, calendar on click

Version 3. The dates are text — the most compact thing that is still exact — with the GC's state as a small chip beside them and one quiet line of context beneath. The dates are a link: click them and a calendar modal opens showing this stage in its months, with the sub's pick, the GC's ask, today, the sub's days off, and the job's other stages as thin marks, plus every window and GC move on the side. The picture is there when you want it and never takes up a column when you don't.

The cell, every state

Line 1: the dates (a link) and the GC chip (a door). Line 2: one sentence of context. An open GC ask puts its dates and two buttons on line 2 and the GC's reason, in their words, on line 3.

  • Two months, Monday-first, always the months the window touches. Weekends are dimmed because windows and picks are counted in working days. Today is outlined.
  • The same four shapes as the track, now on real day cells where they are unambiguous: the light fill is our window, the solid bar the pick, the dashed ring the GC's ask, the hatch the sub's day off (already collected from their portal).
  • The job's other stages are thin marks under the day numbers, so Rough-in before and Trim after read as context without competing. The side panel lists them with their dates; the current one is bold.
  • Every move sits on the right — Change our window, Accept, Answer with, Take it off their portal — the same four writes as today. Nothing is done by clicking a day; the calendar is for looking, the buttons are for acting.
  • Later, if wanted: drag across days to set or move the window, and a "Behar is also on #273 Sep 8 – 9" mark from the dispatch data. Neither is needed to ship this.

What this replaces in the prototype

  • Out: the time track, its axis header, the board-wide scale, and the GC tag on the bar. In: a `WindowText` cell (two lines, the chip door) and a `StageCalendarModal` that reads the window, pick, ask, off days and sibling windows the board already has.
  • Cost: the cell is an afternoon; the calendar modal is about a day (two month grids, the side panel wired to the existing four writes). The scale kernel and track component come out of the tree.
