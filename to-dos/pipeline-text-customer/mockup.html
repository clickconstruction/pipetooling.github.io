Punch list #123 · three mock-ups for one pick · nothing built

Text the customer from the Pipeline row

Today the office can text or email a job's customer, but only inside the job window, below the activity: open the job, scroll, press Text, write, press Send text…, check, send. Five presses and a scroll, and nothing on the row says the job can message its customer at all. Each option below puts one door on the row and the card. They differ in what the door opens.

What every option shares

The door

A speech-bubble icon in the customer's orange, beside the teal ☎ call icon the row already carries, on the desktop quick-action stack (Crew & Dates cell) and on the phone card's foot. Orange is free on the stack (green, blue, teal, sky, purple are taken) and is already the customer's colour in the box.

Who sees it

The office team only (dev, master, assistant, controller, estimator), the same gate as the job window's Note · Text · Email row. The crew's Pipeline keeps the call icon and nothing more.

What the row already knows

The job's customer, their mobile and their email ride on the row today (the call icon reads the same phone), so the door needs no new read. A job with no customer has no icon.

Where the message lands

In the customer's thread in Messages, tied to the job, with the sent copy kept, exactly as the job window's box does now. Texts still wait on Send texts in Settings; until it is on, the door offers Email and says why.

1 The icon opens the job window, already on the box

The cheapest cut. The orange icon opens the job window as the name does, but scrolled to the notes box with Text already pressed and the cursor in the box. One press to writing; the window and its activity are still there above.

The dead states, the same in every option

No customer on the job

No icon. The ⋯ sheet's row reads This job has no customer yet. Add one in Edit Job.

A customer with no mobile

The icon is dim, titled No mobile on file. Email instead. A press opens the door on Email.

Send texts is off

The door opens with Text saying texts are not on yet, Email beside it live, and the switch linked for the owner or a dev.

Not the office

No icon. The crew's row keeps ☎ and the note box in the job window, as today.

Cost and the recommendation

1 · the job window at the box

S. One icon in the stack and on the card foot, one open at the box on Text argument on the job window, the scroll the note box already has. Still loads the whole window. Fallback if the budget is one PR.

2 · the box alone, over the board

M. One new small window mounting the existing job box with Text chosen, the board's read after a send, the phone sheet. One press to writing, nothing to scroll, nothing to host. The pick.

3 · the thread over the board

M–L. The Jobs page mounts the messages host the Messages and GC pages mount, plus the icon. Best context, heaviest page. Worth it when the Jobs page hosts the thread for another reason too.

The phone placement is a second pick: the foot icon beside ☎ (one press; breaks the card's one-quick-action rule, which ☎ already bent for the same person), the ⋯ sheet row (two presses, keeps the rule), or both.
