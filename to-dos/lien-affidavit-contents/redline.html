Mechanic's and materialman's lien affidavit: proposed changes

Our software prints this affidavit for each unpaid job. We compared its sworn text with Texas Property Code § 53.054 and found places where it may fall short. Eight questions follow, then the affidavit in redline, then a clean copy of the proposed text.

This was prepared by the company and its software, not by a lawyer. Every statute reference is our reading and may be wrong. Nothing in the software has changed. We will change only what you approve.

Eight questions

Each question names the paragraph it touches. The same number marks the change in the redline below. A reply by number is all we need.

  1. 1
    The months of workParagraph 5§ 53.054(a)(3)

    TodayParagraph 5 swears a date range, first day through last day. No month is named in the sworn text. A reference line above the title shows one month as a code, such as 2026-08.

    ProposedOn a job under a general contractor, paragraph 5 adds a sentence naming each month for which payment is requested. The code above the title goes.

    Is the added sentence what the statute asks of a claimant other than an original contractor?

  2. 2
    When and how each notice wentParagraph 9§ 53.054(a)(8)

    TodayParagraph 9 swears only that all statutory notices were sent in the time and manner required. It also prints on a job contracted directly with the owner, where no monthly notice is required.

    ProposedParagraph 9 lists each notice: the month it covers, the day it was sent and the method. The software holds each of these. On a job contracted with the owner the paragraph is left out.

    Is a list in this form sufficient? Should the certified mail number be in it? What should the paragraph say when a notice went out after its deadline?

  3. 3
    Three figures that can disagreeParagraph 8§ 53.054(a)(1)

    TodayParagraph 8 swears the contract amount, the amount paid and the amount unpaid. The first two come from the job's price and its payments. The third comes from the bills sent and still unpaid. When part of a job is not yet billed, contract less paid does not equal unpaid on the face of the affidavit.

    ProposedParagraph 8 swears the claim amount alone, after all just credits, offsets and payments.

    Do you want the contract and paid figures kept when they reconcile, or left out always?

  4. 4
    Addresses of the hiring party and the original contractorParagraphs 3 and 7§ 53.054(a)(4), (5)

    TodayParagraph 3 names the party we contracted with and gives no address. Paragraph 7 prints the original contractor's address when one is on file and drops it without a mark when none is.

    ProposedParagraph 3 adds the last known address. The software will not mark an affidavit ready to file while either address is missing.

    Is "last known address" the right phrase in both places?

  5. 5
    The claimant's mailing and physical addressParagraph 1§ 53.054(a)(7)

    TodayParagraph 1 says the claimant is "located at" one address, the one on the company's letterhead.

    ProposedParagraph 1 states the mailing address, and the physical address when the two differ.

    Is that wording right, and should the physical address print even when it is the same?

  6. 6
    One affidavit for several jobs at one propertyWhole affidavit

    TodayEach job gets its own affidavit. A property with five service tickets under one general contractor produces five affidavits, five notarizations, five recording fees and five copies to serve. Our § 53.056 notices already combine jobs at one property.

    ProposedNothing yet. If you approve, one affidavit would cover every unpaid job at a property under the same original contractor, with a schedule of the jobs. A sample schedule is under the redline.

    May one affidavit cover them, or must each ticket stand alone as its own contract?

  7. 7
    HomesteadsTop of page§ 53.254

    TodayThe software holds any property marked as a homestead and tells the office to call you. A property where nobody answered the question is treated as not a homestead and can reach Ready to file.

    ProposedA property with no answer is held too, until someone answers. No change to the affidavit's text until you say what a homestead affidavit needs.

    When a lien on a homestead is proper, should the affidavit carry the § 53.254(f) notice at the top, and is anything else different?

  8. 8
    What sits above the title, and what is attachedTop and foot of page§ 53.054(b)

    TodayThe company's letterhead and a reference line sit above the title. The line carries the job number, the work month as a code and the day the paper was drawn, which is often not the day it is signed.

    ProposedThe reference line goes. A return address for the recorded original is added. The job number moves to the foot. A copy of each notice is attached as an exhibit.

    Is a letterhead acceptable on a recorded instrument? Do you want the notices attached, and the contract when there is one?

What stays as it is

  • Paragraphs 2, 4 and 6, the lien claim itself, the signature block and the jurat.
  • The claim amount: what the sent bills still owe, less any correction the office wrote down with its reason.
  • The software does not mark an affidavit ready until the owner of record, the county and the legal description are on the property's record, and a § 53.056 notice is recorded on a job under a general contractor.
  • After filing, the software tracks the copy that goes to the owner and the original contractor under § 53.055.
