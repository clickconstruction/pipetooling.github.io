Mechanic's and materialman's lien affidavit: the short form

Our software prints this affidavit for each unpaid job. The owner's instruction is the minimum the law requires and nothing more. Below is what Texas Property Code § 53.054 lists, today's affidavit with what the statute does not ask for struck out, and the short form we propose. Five questions follow.

This was prepared by the company and its software, not by a lawyer. Every statute reference is our reading and may be wrong. Nothing in the software has changed. We will change only what you approve.

Also required of the paper, outside § 53.054

Read on November 10, 2026. Each is kept or already handled.

  • It must be sworn. The affidavit keeps its jurat, "sworn to and subscribed before me", with the notary's signature and seal. An acknowledgment alone would not do.
  • The clerk's format rules, Local Government Code § 191.007. A heading at the top of the first page naming the kind of paper, and a name printed under each signature. The short form keeps the title as its heading and prints the signer's name. A page that misses these pays twice the recording fee. The clerk may not refuse it, and the lien is not invalid.
  • A homestead, § 53.254(f). The affidavit must carry "NOTICE: THIS IS NOT A LIEN. THIS IS ONLY AN AFFIDAVIT CLAIMING A LIEN." at the top in at least 10-point bold. The software holds every homestead for you today, so no form prints this yet. Question 4 asks about it.
  • A copy after filing, § 53.055. The owner, and the original contractor on a job under one, get a copy within five days of filing. This is a step the software tracks. It is not text on the affidavit.

Five questions

A reply by number is all we need.

  1. 1
    Is the short form complete?§ 53.054(a)

    The table above maps each item the statute lists to a paragraph of the short form. Two details changed with it. The county under STATE OF TEXAS is now the county where the affidavit is signed, since paragraph 2 already names the property's county. A line for the notary's printed name is added.

    Is anything the law requires missing, or said in words that will not hold up?

  2. 2
    Six things we cutStruck in today's affidavit

    None of these is on the statute's list as we read it: the letterhead and the reference line above the title, the "BEFORE ME" preamble, the first and last day of work, the contract amount and the amount paid, the general sentence about notices, and "together with all interest, costs, and attorney's fees allowed by law". The jurat stays.

    Do you want any of the six back, and why?

  3. 3
    The noticesShort form ¶ 6§ 53.054(a)(8)

    The short form gives the day each notice went to the owner and the method. It leaves out the certified mail number and the copy sent to the original contractor.

    Is that enough? What should the paragraph say when a notice went out after its deadline?

  4. 4
    Homesteads§ 53.254

    The software holds any property marked as a homestead and tells the office to call you. We will also hold a property where nobody has answered the question.

    When a lien on a homestead is proper, does the § 53.254(f) notice go at the top of this form, and is anything else different?

  5. 5
    One affidavit for several jobs at one propertyFewer papers

    Today each job gets its own affidavit. A house with five service tickets under one general contractor means five affidavits, five notarizations and five recording fees.

    May one affidavit claim the total for every unpaid job at a property under the same original contractor, or must each ticket stand alone?

What the software does around the form

  • The claim amount is what the sent bills still owe, less any correction the office wrote down with its reason.
  • An affidavit is not marked ready until the owner of record, the county and the legal description are on the property's record. On a job under a general contractor a § 53.056 notice must be recorded too.
  • We will add two holds: one while the hiring party's or the original contractor's address is missing, and one while the homestead question has no answer.
  • After filing, the software tracks the copy that goes to the owner and the original contractor under § 53.055.
