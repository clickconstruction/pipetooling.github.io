The HR file's History view

A long account of a person — the owners' history with them in dated sections — read one section at a time from an index, each section naming the Timeline entries that cite it, a counsel-held section named but never shown, and filed through the seat like everything else. Made-up names and words throughout.

1 · The tab, beside the file's others

History sits with Timeline, Evidence, Narrative, By theme and Matter. The fact strip gains nothing: the document's size is not a fact about the person. The index is the left column; the reading pane holds one section.

§16 · Bidding conduct and the other companies

May–Jul 2026 · written Aug 4, 2026, extended Sep 8 and Oct 12 · section 16 of 36

In the spring the bidding seat stopped answering. Of the invitations that came to the company's address in those months, most were never opened, and the three proposals that did go out went to prospects the office had not heard of. The owners learned of the first outside company from the state's record, registered the morning of the customer's letter, and of the others from a teammate who had been asked to join one.

Added Sep 8, 2026, from the mailbox: the forwarding of a customer's email from the company account to an outside address, while still employed, fixes the earliest date the second company was in use.

Added Oct 12, 2026, from a recording: a teammate's own account of the May pitch — the out-of-town jobs "already in place" — sixteen days after the first company was registered.

2 · A held section

The app copy carries a fence where the words were. The index keeps the section so the numbering and the span stay whole; the pane says it is held and says nothing else. The seat strips a fence's body before it writes, so a copy scrubbed by hand and one that was not land the same.

3 · The seat's door

One verb pair for the three long documents. The plan reads the text as it would be written: the sections, the held ones, the ones the entries cite, the bytes and the live revision it would replace. Apply writes only when nothing moved, and archives the prior version as the trigger always has.
