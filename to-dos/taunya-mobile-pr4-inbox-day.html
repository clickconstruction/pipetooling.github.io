The dashboard's first screen really is a technician's. For an office person Clock Out and Update Focus wrap to two rows at 375 px, My Schedule always draws "No blocks scheduled" twice, and Crew Day, the Teams inbox group, Recent reports, the Billing pipeline and My Time all render when empty. The block sheet exists — on the People tab. HubPeoplePhoneBoard already opens a sheet on a block: Open the job · Note · Copy to techs · Move · Remove.
