Why a scanned code gates the search

What opens the searchWhat anyone with the address seesCall
Nothing: prts.co open to allEvery live box on every job with its place and contents. The company's whole on-site inventory, which cage holds the brass and which the copper, readable from the parking lot.No
A company-wide secret in the addressThe same inventory to anyone who has the address. A secret in a link is forwarded in a text the day it is useful.No
A code scanned from any box on the siteThe boxes on that box's job with the lines that match. The person is standing on the site with a label in front of them.Yes

What this gives up: a person on one site cannot find a part sitting on another site without signing in. The signed-in search (Go anywhere, Boxes on site) already does that across every job the person can see, one tap away. If the owner wants the whole company open with no key, the first row says what that shows.

The front door: prts.co with nothing after it
