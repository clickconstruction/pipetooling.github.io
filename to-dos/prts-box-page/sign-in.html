What happens between the frames

  1. The press writes /b/4f2a9c18 into the app's one-shot return key, the same key the Add Task shortcut uses, and goes to the sign-in page.
  2. The sign-in page is unchanged. Once signed in, the app reads the key, clears it, and lands on /b/4f2a9c18 with a session.
  3. With a session, that route does what it does today: finds the box under the person's own permissions and opens its screen on the On site tab. PR 1 makes sure a helper, a primary or a superintendent is let through instead of bounced to the Dashboard.
  4. A signed-in person who is not on the job's team sees the lines and no press, as today. Every press that writes still goes through the one gate on the five tables.

A crew member who is already signed in never sees frame 1: the scan goes straight to frame 3. The public page is for the phone with no session: a new helper, a GC's superintendent, an inspector, or the owner on a borrowed phone.
