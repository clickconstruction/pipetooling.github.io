The whole group Dashboard → Robots → three lenses, one vocabulary pass 3 · holistic

Zooming out from the Audits lens to everything the estimator meets on the way to it.

  • One count kernel. openEstimatorQuestions(rows) fed by the full open list; the panel, the Scoreboard's Your part, the Dashboard card and the group tab's badge all read it. Today three surfaces compute three ways.
  • One strip, on every lens. The Robot Board's six numbers become the group's header. The Scoreboard's Your part keeps its sentences (they are the personal reading of the same six) but drops the ones the strip already says.
  • Fewer words for the same things. The page uses audit, ruling, question, verdict, shadow, backtest, sealed, envelope, slate. The estimator-facing copy needs three: questions (the panel keeps "standing rulings" in its tooltip and the guide), audits, sealed. "Backtest R2" and "ZZ Twin" leave the rows; "re-bid" and the slate date replace them.

  • Is this the best we can do? Not quite — two things are left on purpose. Both are Your calls below, not gaps in the draft.
