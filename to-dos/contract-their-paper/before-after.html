Before The app can only file a contract as signed by the customer

A GC sends their own subcontract. Our RMP signs it; the GC never countersigns — and later their termination notice relies on that paper. Today the job window's one door files any existing contract as signed by the customer, which would be false, so the PDF is parked on Evidence and the desk keeps saying there is no agreement at all.

After A contract kind: their paper — who signed, that they rely on it, and the terms

The record says what is actually true: signed by us (who, when), not countersigned, they rely on it (where), the file. Key terms are short fields with clause references. The desk's agreement line reads it, keeps the sworn account as the backup basis, and the narrative editor can insert the terms instead of retyping them.
