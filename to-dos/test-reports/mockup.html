Test reports, inside PipeTooling

The hydrostatic and gas test report the office builds on plumbingtooling.com today, rebuilt as a job document: prefilled from the job, sent to the GC with the Stripe pay link in one click, logged on the job, and drafted automatically from the tech's clock-out report. Billing does not move.

04The automation dial — pick one to start

A · Office sendsstart here

The clock-out report drafts the Test report. Needs You lists what's ready. A person glances and clicks Send.

  • Two clicks per report instead of four surfaces
  • Every send has a name on it
  • "Bill first" catches the missing invoice

B · Auto-send when complete

Pass result + Stripe bill + GC email present → sent, no click. Activity line says so.

  • FAIL always waits for a person
  • Blocked ones stay on Needs You
  • Per-customer override

C · Tech sends from the field

The clock-out report is the form; the tech's Send goes to the GC.

  • Fastest
  • Customer-facing paper in the crew's hands
  • Best as a per-customer option later
