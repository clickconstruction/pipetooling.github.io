Legal desk · the firm window · a mock-up, not the app · 2026-10-30 · punch list #222

Firms and their coverage: see where a firm covers, extend it, hand it off county by county, and let two firms share a county

The owner, 2026-10-30, on the firm window's Replace with a new firm…: I don't totally understand "replace with a new firm". We should do a better job showing where a firm appears on the map, and if we're going to replace someone, replace them for that coverage zone. We should be able to extend coverage zones for firms, and it is okay if multiple firms have the same coverage zones.

Snell Law Firm is the firm the desk knows today. Everything else is invented: Alamo Collections Law, PLLC as the San Antonio firm, the counties each covers, every case and every dollar. The county map on this page is a schematic grid of twenty central-Texas counties, so the pictures stay small; the app draws the real Texas county map it already has. Every other shape is the real one: the firm window, the desk's header chip, the release sheet, the Map view's legend, the firm portal's Who covers what.

This page is a drawing of what we want to build, not the app. One made-up pair of firms runs through every picture. Read it top to bottom once, then answer the five questions at the end. Section 6 is the second pass, where the design argues with itself.

Why the particulars fold

The eight particulars are Click's legal entity, license, registered agent and the rest. They are the same on every firm's portal. In a window about one firm they read as that firm's, and they push the firm's own facts below the fold. Folded with their 8 of 8 filled chip and one door, they are still one press away and the window is half as tall.

A firm's coverage and a firm are one screen. The question where does Snell appear on the map is answered the moment the window opens.

The list says what the map cannot at a glance: how many cases and dollars ride on each county, and where Snell is first call or only also covers.

Everything here reads from what the desk already has: the county map component in lens mode, countiesByFirm, the cases the Map view places. Nothing new is stored for this picture.

3 Hand off counties: replace a firm for a zone, not everywhere

Hand off counties… is what Replace should have been. Pick which of Snell's counties move, pick the firm that takes them (one that exists, or a new one typed here), read what happens, press a button that says the count and the firm. The matters already with Snell stay with Snell until they close. When Snell is left with nothing and holds nothing, the same sheet offers to retire it. One database call does all of it in one transaction, so the map is never half moved.

4 Shared counties on the desk: first call, and a switch

Two firms covering one county is fine once the desk knows which one to pick by default. That is first call: one firm per county wears it, the one that has covered the county longest unless a dev says otherwise. The header chip names the first-call firm and says who else covers; the switch segment the two-county accounts already have offers the other firm; the release sheet asks the same question in full. The trap the second pass found · the bridge ends on the first click

On the live database today there is one firm and no county colored, so the desk runs on the bridge: the one firm takes everything. The first county anyone colors, for Snell or for a second firm, ends the bridge, and every account outside a colored county suddenly has no firm and a held release. Nothing on the screen says this before it happens. Section 1b puts the warning in the window before a second firm exists, with the press that fixes it and the area presets so it is three presses, not fifteen. This is PR 1, before anything else here, because it is a cliff the current app already has.

Weighed and kept out: a per-county percentage split between two firms (nobody asked, and the desk cannot explain it); letting a firm be first call in some counties and absent in its neighbor's by rule (that is just coloring); a Replace that moves live matters (consent); showing the other firm's case names on the portal's shared county (counsel's call still open).
