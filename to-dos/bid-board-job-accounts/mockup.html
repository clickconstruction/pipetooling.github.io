Job accounts on the Bid Board: the row, the strip, the lens

The estimator lives on the board. Today the job-account tools sit one click away, in Edit Bid's Job block. These four screens bring them to the board using the three devices it already has — the won row's pill line, the "Where this bid is" step strip, and the lenses — plus one email that covers several properties at once.

The proxy rule, like every step's

Done when every house that quoted the bid or expects an account is open or not needed. Next when the job exists and a house is missing — the pill names it. Untracked before the job exists. A house nobody buys from never blocks the strip.

The door

Step 11's door is the Job block ("Open the job"). Step 12's door is the Job accounts question for that job — a new door kind, accounts, beside edit · pricing · cover-letter · review. Same landing rule: open, scroll, ring the thing the step is about.

Why a lens and not a filter

Lenses on this board are work queues with their own sort and their own actions (Waiting to hear sorts by days waiting; Forecast by start). Job accounts sorts by first parts run and acts on the account, which no filter of the main list could do.

Mine and everyone's

The lens shows every estimator's won bids, with the bidder named on each row, and a Mine toggle like Waiting to hear's. The office sees the same lens; the Won pill's count line gains "4 missing job accounts".

What logging does

One tap marks each of the three jobs requested at Ferguson and writes three send-log rows, each with its bid. The lens rows turn purple in place; the tech's card on each job reads "Ferguson · requested".

When the GC differs

It does here, and the note lists each property with its own GC. The house wants the property and the buyer per account; one message with three numbered blocks is what a rep can work down a list with.

Kernel change

composeRepEmail takes one property today; it grows a properties[] form that numbers them and joins the start dates. The single-property note is the one-item case. Tests pin both.

Build order

Two client-only PRs; the RPCs already exist.

  1. The row and the lens. Chips on the won GC line (one list_bid_job_account_strip call for the page's won bids) with Mark opened and the "…" door; the Job accounts lens with the by-house groups, the per-house email (the multi-property composer), Mark opened / Not needed per row, the Mine toggle, the count on the Won pill.
  2. The strip steps. Steps 11 and 12 on "Where this bid is" with the proxy rule and the accounts door.

Decisions for the owner

Won bids with no job yet in the lens? Proposed yes, as a row whose only action is "open the job first" — otherwise a won bid can wait unseen.
One email per house across properties, even when the GCs differ? Proposed yes, numbered blocks with each GC.
Whose rows does the lens show by default? Proposed everyone's, bidder named, with a Mine toggle.
