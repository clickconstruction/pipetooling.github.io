The sub's page: a place for their statements (#371)

Card #350 gave the office three papers for a sub: a work statement, a payment record and a statement of account. This card puts the same three on the sub's own page. Every name, job and amount here is made up. The sub is Harlan Mechanical, with four sheets on three jobs and $4,100.00 still open.

One correction to card #350's drawing. Its mock-up drew the sub's page with tabs (Pay · Work · Papers to sign). The page has no tabs. It is one long page, drawn as a paper, in English or Spanish. This mock-up starts from the page as it really is.

What a sub cannot find on it
  • A paid sheet. Once a sheet is settled it leaves the page. #2033 Alder Court is only a payment line.
  • Anything older than 90 days. The payments list stops there.
  • One check as one thing. The $2,200.00 Zelle of Nov 12 shows as two lines, with nothing tying them.
  • A number to quote. Nothing on the page can be named on the phone: no WS, no PR.
  • A paper for one job. Print prints the whole page.
  • A period. No "what happened in October".
The page also says Owed to you and Balance owed to you. Card #350's papers say Still open, since much of it waits on a customer's payment or an inspection. Call 6 below asks whether the page follows the papers.

4. Got it, and Something is wrong

A sub can tell the office a payment reached them, or that a figure looks wrong. Neither is a condition of being paid. Each is recorded the way a signed work order is: the name, the time, where it came from and the copy they saw.

9. The calls

Each of these puts something in front of a sub, so each is Taunya's. The pick is what the drawings show.

  1. A switch, or one more section? Pick: the switch, Today and Statements (section 2). The other way is section 7.
  2. Does the sub's page ever give out a number? Pick: no. It shows a number the office gave. A statement of account a sub saves says Saved from your page and takes none.
  3. How far back? Pick: every sheet and every payment, with paid sheets under open ones. The other way is the last 12 months, with older ones on request.
  4. Got it and Something is wrong. Pick: both, neither a condition of being paid. Got it is a quiet chip for the office. Something is wrong flags the sheet and tells the office.
  5. Spanish. Who reads the draft words in section 6 before they go on the page? The PDFs stay in English for now.
  6. Does the page say Still open where it says Owed to you today? Pick: yes on the Statements view, so the page and the papers use one word. Today keeps its words until that is a call of its own.
  7. Who is told a paper is there? Pick: nobody, by itself. The page shows it. The office sends a paper when it wants a sub to look (card #350's Send).
  8. Confirm stays off. Not a call here: it waits on counsel's sentence, as card #350 says.
