Box photos after a combine: the files follow by themselves

When two jobs are combined, each box photo goes to the target job in the app, but its file stays in the old job's Drive folder. The dev seat's move takes the files with it since v2.6885. A combine does not yet. The owner's idea: offer it after the combine, so the person can do it then and watch, or carry on and let a nightly pass do it.

3 How it works underneath

The same three parts serve the choice in section 1 and the pick in section 2. They are the pattern counter tickets already use when a ticket changes job.

  1. The database marks the photo. When a filed box photo's job changes, a trigger stamps the row as due a move. That covers a combine, the seat's move and any door added later, with no caller to remember it.
  2. The photo sweep moves what is due. It already runs every five minutes while anything waits. Due photos become one more thing it files, twenty a run. So nothing depends on a person, a page staying open or a button.
  3. The app asks at once, for speed. Right after a combine the app makes the ask that shipped in v2.6885, about one file a second. If the page closes, the sweep finishes the rest. This needs the ask opened to the roles that may combine: dev, master technician, assistant, controller.
  4. A held file waits a day. Its reason is written on the row, and the sweep tries it again the next night.
