?The estimate's link: three ways

The app keeps only a hash of an estimate's link, so a message cannot reuse the emailed one. Each way below gets a working link into the text.

A · Resend's link

  • No new table: the path Resend link already uses.
  • The emailed link stops working, so Dana's old email opens not found.
  • Resend also emails Dana again, so it needs its own quiet path.
Making this link retires the one in Dana's email.

B · A second link for the message (recommended)

  • Each share makes its own token, kept hashed beside the email's.
  • Both links open the estimate, and the history says who shared it and when.
  • One table, one function, and the accept page reading either hash, then its redeploy.

C · Dana's customer page

  • One link for every paper Dana has.
  • The portal shows no estimates today, so an Estimates card comes first.
  • The text opens Dana's page, and Dana finds the estimate there.

?The owner's calls

  1. The estimate's link: A, B (the recommendation) or C.
  2. The words a paper puts in: the three lines above, or the team's own.
  3. Paid bills: offered as a copy (drawn), or left out.
  4. Draft estimates: left out until sent (drawn), or offered with a note.
  5. The words that call papers: bill, invoice, pay, owe and estimate, quote, price, or fewer.
