A window for every company

The owner's ask (2026-10-04), from Trade partners: "I would like to be able to click on any of the paperwork buttons and have that paperwork appear … information about the customer in one tab, a ledger of interactions in a second tab, and documents in a third tab." Made-up example: Iron Horse Fabrication, a steel company we use.

1. First idea: a modal with three tabs

Click a company or a paperwork chip and a modal opens with Info, Ledger and Documents. Documents lists the files.

2. Is this the best we can do? Not quite.

It is one window for every company, not a customer window.These chips are on Trade partners, so this company is a trade. GC mode already has a company window for customers and architects (click Cibolo anywhere). A second window for trades would split one company's record in two. Build one window that knows what each company is to us: customer, architect, trade, or more than one.
A file list hides what matters.The question is never "which PDFs do we have". It is "what is missing, what runs out soon, and what does that hold up". Documents should lead with status: insurance runs out May 5, the master agreement covers every job, one lien waiver is owed on Fair Oaks D. A missing paper gets Ask for it, which becomes a promise.
The ledger mostly exists already, in pieces.Calls and texts on each ask, promises, quotes, declines with reasons, awards, draws, pay apps sent back. Activity should merge them into one timeline, newest first, with filters (calls and notes, quotes, paperwork, money), each line linked to its job. Log a contact lives at the top.
Open where you clicked.A paperwork chip should open Documents with that paper showing. A promise opens Activity at it. The name opens About. One click from any chip to the answer.
Show the paper, not a link to it.Picking a paper shows it beside the list: the certificate's insurer, limits and dates; the master agreement's version and who signed; a statement of work's price and what they will not do. In the real build, the real file opens there.
About is the decision page.The facts that decide whether to ask or award: trades and coverage, vetting, how they answer, how often their word holds, their schedule record, what they usually exclude, and the money (jobs with us, paid, owed, held).

4. The plan

  • One company window for every company, opened from any company's name and from every paperwork chip. Customers and architects keep everything they have today, under About.
  • About: contact, coverage, trades, vetting, how they answer, word kept, schedule record, usual exclusions, and the money with us. For a customer, today's window: what they owe, how they pay, their projects.
  • Activity: one timeline merged from what the model already keeps: ask contacts, promises, quotes, declines and reasons, awards, statements of work, draws and pay apps, daily-log mentions, change orders. Filters, a link to each job, and Log a contact.
  • Documents, status first. Company papers (master agreement, insurance, W-9, the vetting form) and papers by job (statement of work, pay applications, lien waivers, quotes). Missing or running out gets Ask for it, which writes a promise. Picking one shows it beside the list. The prototype draws a made-up paper; the real build opens the file. A W-9's tax number is never shown.
  • Opened where you clicked: a paperwork chip opens Documents at that paper.
