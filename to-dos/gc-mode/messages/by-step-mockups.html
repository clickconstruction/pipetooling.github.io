Keep the board, but draw one lane at a time, Once or Per job, so four or five columns get 230 px each and a line is a small card: the whole name, whose move, the day. The per-job lane's cards carry the job on a second line. The cost is a tab press to see the other lane, and the page still scrolls sideways for a flow with six per-job steps.

The pick, and is this the best we can do?

A, with B as the List tab. A keeps the question the page was built for, who is on each step, and answers it without cutting a word, because the width of the page is the column and chips wrap. B is the better answer to the other question, where is this company, and that is what the List tab was for, so the List tab becomes B. C keeps the shape that failed and fixes it with a tab press; dropped.

  1. No initials. The avatar beside a name said the name twice; in A and B the name is the only mark. Faces return only where there is no room for a name, the phone's rail.
  2. Nothing cut. A chip and a row grow to their words. A long flow is a long page, which scrolls down, the way every list in the app does; nothing scrolls sideways.
  3. The lanes stay visible. A's Once and Per job headings keep the shape of the flow, and the per-job chips say the job, since the same company can be on three.
  4. Late reads first. A tinted chip at the front of its row, a red count under the step's name, and in B a red pill in the per-job column.
  5. The Customers board is the same A under its By step button, and its List tab the same B.
