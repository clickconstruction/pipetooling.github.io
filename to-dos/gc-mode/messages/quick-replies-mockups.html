The pick, and is this the best we can do?

C. Half the lines never fit the thread they are on, so A's groups are the big win; B's order inside them keeps the first row the lines you reach for; the filter box costs nothing until the list is long enough to need it.

Three things weighed:

  1. Tagging is work. A line with no tags reads as anyone, so an untagged list behaves as today and gets better as lines are tagged. The real build can guess a first tag from the words (quote, portal, pay application) and let the editor correct it.
  2. The strip grows taller. Whole lines on wrapping rows take more height than two scrolled rows. The groups keep the top short, and the quick replies already fold away on a note for the team; a show fewer that keeps only the step's group is one press if the height bites.
  3. The step comes from the flow. On Asked to quote reads the same flow the right panel draws, so a line tagged to a step follows the person as they move. That is the third surface the flow list would drive, with the panel and the nudges, which the plan already names as the point of keeping it in Settings.
