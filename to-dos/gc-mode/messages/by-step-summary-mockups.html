Is this the best we can do?
  • A wins on sameness. The office will read this bar on Money → Bill day every month; the same bar here, with the same legend, costs nothing to learn. The legend is the A you picked for the money bar.
  • C says the most, a dot per company with its color, and it is the one to grow into if the list gets long: forty companies is forty dots, still one glance. But its tiles are tall, and the trouble dots repeat what the pills say.
  • B is the prettiest and the thinnest. It draws the direction and nothing else. If the page ever gets a "this month" read (how many moved from quoting to building), B is where it goes.
  • In every shape the pieces press. A segment, a block or a tile jumps to its first row; a pill jumps to Follow up with that filter.
  • Not sure of: whether "over usual" belongs on the hand at all (six is most of the board, which says the usuals are tight, not that six people need a call). Drawn as a pill in orange, never red; dropped from the Follow up count.
