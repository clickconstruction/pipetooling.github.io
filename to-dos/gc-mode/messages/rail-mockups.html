The smallest: how far, what is on now, whose move. Hides the shape of the journey, which is the point of a flow, so it is the least like the panel it opens.

Is this the best we can do?

A for the stamps: the labels column is what makes two lines read as a pair, and right-hugging keeps the court pill in the eye's first position. R1 for the rail: it is the panel's own chart at a smaller size, so folding and unfolding changes nothing but size, and the orange ring with us / them answers the question the owner asks most, whose move is it, before the panel is even opened. One thing R1 cannot do that the panel can: show a loop (each draw, each ask). In the rail a loop is a mark with a small ↻ in its tip and a second ring once a round is on. I would not draw more than that at 64px.
