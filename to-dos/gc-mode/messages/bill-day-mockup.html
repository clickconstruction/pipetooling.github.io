Is this the best we can do?

  1. Across jobs with a filter, not per job. Bill day is one day for every job, and the office works it as one morning; a per-job page would be opened six times. The filter row keeps the job in view when that is the question.
  2. Grouped by state, not by trade. The page's question is "what is still missing", and the groups answer it top down: sent back, not in, waiting on us, approved. A sort by trade is the By company tab's job.
  3. Nothing new is written here. Approve and Send back are the Draws window's; Ask for it is the step's line; Nothing this period is the one new fact, and it is a recorded word, not a typed-over draw.
  4. The cut-off is one setting, days before bill day, not a day per job; a job that needs a different one is the open question, not the default.
  5. What was dropped: a calendar of every trade's promised days (the days strip says the month in one line); approving from the row (two places to approve a draw is one too many); dollar bars (the words say it).
