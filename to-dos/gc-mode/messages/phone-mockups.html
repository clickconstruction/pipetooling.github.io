How Job activity reads the lines

  1. Which lines. The messages rows tied to this Pipeline job, jobs_ledger_id = the job. One read when the row's activity opens, beside the notes, reports and events useJobThreadNotes reads now.
  2. Who sees them. Messages' own rule, the office team. Everyone else reads no rows, and the feed looks as it does today, so a tech's or an estimator's view does not change. The rows' policy already says this. Nothing new is opened.
  3. The columns. Main's MESSAGE_COLS, plus a call's length and summary once cut 5's 20261012170000_quo_messages.sql is on prod. Until then a call line reads "call" with no length, because asking for a column that is not there fails the whole read.
  4. Not a new kind in the old union. The lines come in beside JobThreadActivityItem, not inside it. buildJobActivityLines takes them as a second input and numbers them with everything else. That keeps Job Detail, Job Mode, Quickfill and Dispatch's customer summary, which share the union, exactly as they are.
  5. Live. messages is not in the realtime publication, so a text that lands while the feed is open shows on the next open. Notes stay live as today.
  6. An index. The table has none on jobs_ledger_id. One small migration adds messages (jobs_ledger_id, at) where the job is set, for when Quo and the mailbox fill the table.

  2. Who is orange. I read "the customer" as a contact on the customer's side of the job: the customer, or a person at the customer. A line with anyone else tied to the job, a supplier or a trade, shows plain with its channel word and only under All. Is that right?
  3. Lines not tied yet. v0.3 shows Dana's untied reply on both her open jobs until someone ties it. A message on main holds at most one job, and a read by jobs_ledger_id cannot see an untied line. Do I add "her lines with no job, marked not tied yet" in J2, through contact_jobs, or leave it to the customer window's work?

Three ways it could be better, and the pick

  1. a · One sheet shell, not five. The tie sheet, the ⋯ menu, the three windows, the step card and Who has it could each grow their own bottom sheet. One PhoneSheet gives them the same grab bar, safe area, focus trap, Esc and backdrop rule, and one smoke covers the nested-windows case. Better, and it costs one PR up front.
  2. b · Fold by the column, not the screen. A 760 px media query would fold the header only on a phone. The thread's measured width folds it wherever the column is narrow, as v1.32 asks, and the same width decides the rail's row. Better, and useMeasuredWidth is already on main.
  3. c · Messages as their own tab in Job activity. A Messages tab would be simpler to build and to filter. But the plan and v0.3 both mix them into one numbered feed, and a reader would lose the order of what happened. Worse, so not this.
The pick: a and b, not c. P1 to P4 make the thread whole at 375 px on main as it is. P5 and P6 follow helper 2's and helper 6's drafts as they land. J0 to J2 put the customer's calls, texts and emails into the Pipeline's Job activity in v0.3's orange, read only by the office team. Nothing is built until you say go.
