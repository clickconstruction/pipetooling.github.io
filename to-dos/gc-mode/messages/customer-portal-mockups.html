Customer's portal · mock-ups

The page a customer opens from the link in our emails and texts. Today it lists the flow's steps and nothing else (the prototype), while the real app's portal already carries bills, a pay-by-card press, the contract to sign, change orders to sign or decline, Accept the work, a schedule in words and the papers. These mock-ups put the two together and add what a customer asks us on the phone. Rule kept throughout: nothing of ours shows. No trade names, no costs, no nudges, no other customers.

5. Is this the best we can do?

  • Why B and not A. A reads fine in month one and badly in month six: eight pay applications, twelve change orders and twenty weeks of photos make one scroll nobody finishes. Tabs let the home page stay short and each tab grow. C is too small: the phone calls it saves are the ones about papers and money, which C leaves in email.
  • Your move is the whole point. Most weeks the customer owes us nothing, and the card says so in one line. The weeks they do, it is the first thing on the page with its button. The same read drives our side: the step on now with "their move" is the card.
  • Money in plain words, not a G702. Six lines a dentist reads in ten seconds. The pay application itself, with its schedule of values and the architect's certificate, opens from Papers for the customer who wants it.
  • One conversation, three doors. The portal's Messages tab is not a fourth channel. It reads and writes the same thread the office sees. A customer who writes there shows up in Follow up exactly like a text.
  • This week costs Wendi one sentence a day. The daily log already exists. A customer line and a photo mark on it are the only new asks of the field, and the Friday roll-up writes itself.
  • What I left out on purpose. No live schedule chart, no trade names, no our-costs, no document markup, no chat with the superintendent. A log-in: the link is the key, as today; a customer who wants a password gets one later.
  • What I am not sure of. Whether "Accept the work" and the warranty year belong on Home as a final Your move or on Papers. Whether the architect sees the same page or a cousin of it (the certify step is theirs). Whether photos should be opt-in per job, since some owners share the link with tenants.

Pick a shape and I add it to the prototype's portal window, with the office reads on the thread header, the step card and Follow up.
