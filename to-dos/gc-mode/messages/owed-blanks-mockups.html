The pick, and is this the best we can do?

All three together: they are one pattern. The blanks make the two facts read as typed facts, the list makes the first choice fast and ties it to the flow, and the empty strip makes marking and changing the same gesture.

Three things weighed:

  1. The day blank. A press opens a small box that takes a day in words, Monday, Oct 12, next Friday, and reads it back as a date; the prototype shows the box and the read-back. How a typed day is read is on the plan's open list already.
  2. The words on the buttons follow the blank. Send Priya the start date becomes Send Priya a pay application when the what changes, so a wrong pick is seen at once.
  3. Who may change it. Anyone on the team, as with everything in the thread (the owner, 2026-10-05: everyone sees every thread). A change writes a gray event line, Robert changed what we owe Priya: the start date, by Mon Oct 12, so the small Rosa, last Saturday stays honest and the history keeps.
