The pick, and is this the best we can do?

B1, built so a third line can be switched on later. The number-first line is how the rest of the app names a job, so the eye finds the job the same way everywhere; the day gets the whole second line, in words, with its tone, which is more than a chip's slot could give it. B2 reads the days fastest but turns every tile's identity into a caption, and a job between days has nothing to headline. B3's third line is the right fact (what we wait on, whose court) but it belongs to the flow panel today; the tile should earn its third line once the Board's and the Pipeline's records feed it, not carry made-up words until then.

Three things weighed:

  1. The second line's words come from facts, never typed. A bid's day and whether ours went; a GC project's stage and its start date; a Pipeline job's Stages word and its last bill. Each exists; the real build reads them with the contact's jobs. The exact words per stage are the owner's to confirm, and the hover card says the full sentence.
  2. Width. 240 px shows most names whole with the badge; "Fair Oaks Shops, Building D" is cut to the comma. 260 px would show it and still fit two tiles beside the label at a 700 px thread. The folded strip uses 220 px and scrolls.
  3. Four targets on a tile (star, name, ⧉, ×) against five on today's chip. The × could still move to the job's card; it stays on the tile for now so nothing the chip does today is lost, and the legend on the Their jobs card (v1.33) explains all four.
