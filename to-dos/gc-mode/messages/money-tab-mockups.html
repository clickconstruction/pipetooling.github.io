The Money tab, four ways

Elena's job, Boerne Retail Shell, month four: three pay applications paid, the fourth with the architect, two change orders signed. The six-line statement (v1.162) is correct and cold. A customer pays gladly when the page shows what the money built, that someone independent checked it, that everyone under us got paid, and that nothing is coming they did not sign. The words stay plain and true; nothing here is a trick.

4. Is this the best we can do?

  • Why not just B. B is the warmest card but it starts on the open application, the thing they owe. C's header puts the win first and the forecast second, and that order is what changes how the ask lands.
  • Why not just C. C's history is a list of lines with "certified · 6 waivers" in small print. The photos and the covers sentence are what make a paid application feel like a purchase rather than a payment. B's card does that.
  • The forecast is the risky line. "Roughly $120,000 to $140,000" comes from the schedule of values and the look-ahead, and must say so. Shown as a range with the sentence under it, it is the single most calming thing on the page. Shown as one number, it is a promise we will miss.
  • "Paid on time: 3 of 3" is earned, not given. It appears only when it is true. A customer who paid late sees nothing there, not a scold. The page praises and never nags; the nagging is ours, by text, from Follow up.
  • The thank-you is real work. "Thank you · 6 trades paid Sep 28" tells them their money went where it should within days. Few GCs say it. It costs us one line we already know.
  • Left out on purpose. Our costs and margins, trade names on the waivers list (the waiver itself carries the name when opened), retainage math (a line in the statement, not the header), interest on late payment (in the reminder, never on this page), a pay-now button on an application the architect has not certified.
  • Not sure of. Whether the percent is money or schedule (money is honest and simple; schedule percent can run ahead or behind it). Whether the photos per application are picked by Wendi or are the three most recent of those weeks. Whether a card customer should see the 3% on the header or only on the application.

Pick one and I rebuild the Money tab in the prototype to match, with Priya's month-zero version.
