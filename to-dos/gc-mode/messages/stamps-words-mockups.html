Three things weighed:

  1. The wide block. It keeps its dates and (+6): there is room, and a date is exact. Only the folded form speaks in words. If that split bothers, C makes them one.
  2. "Last Saturday" against "Saturday". Within this week the weekday alone ("on Wednesday"); the week before, "last Saturday"; from seven days, "last week", then weeks counted to three, then the date. Each is one unambiguous reading from today, never a guess.
  3. Both today. Bill's thread has both stamps from today, so each keeps its time; the newer one still reads darker. The court pill says the rest.
