The draws bar · its legend, three ways

Today the legend is a wrapped list under the bar, out of step with the segments. Three ways to make it lateral: the color and the number above, the state below, in the bar's own order.

A · Four columns in bar order

A row of columns, left to right like the bar. Each column: the swatch and the count on top, the state and its detail under. Even widths, so long labels never squeeze.

Is this the best we can do?
  • B wins on the drop-down read. The question under a segment is "which is this and how many", and B answers it without a swatch, in the segment's own column. A needs the eye to match colors across a gap; C runs out of room on thin segments.
  • B's weakness is a thin segment. Under 10% the label shortens to the state alone, and under 6% it shows the count only, with the full words on hover. The count always shows.
  • Helotes, one segment: B becomes one column, "1 · Not in · due Oct 19", which reads fine.
