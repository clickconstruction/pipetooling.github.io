4. Is this the best we can do?

  • The summary line is the page's first sentence. Four numbers in the flow's order, then what needs a hand today. It is the same read the Trade partners funnel on main gives, in words, so nothing new is computed.
  • Grouping by job is the biggest single gain. Five job names become two labels, the due day moves to the label where it belongs, and the chips go back to being company names. A row with one job keeps the label, so every row reads the same way.
  • "Over usual" is the honest middle between fine and late. Late needs a day; most steps have none. Usual is on the flow already and the By company view reads it. Here it tints the day orange and counts under the step.
  • The row's press is the step's own nudge, the same send Follow up would make: remind the late, ask for papers, open the compare. Draws keeps Bill day. Nothing sends until the send window.
  • Empty steps fold to one line each lane, with the sentence that says what lands there, so the page never looks broken on a quiet week. Closeout stays as its own line because its emptiness means something (nobody is finishing).
  • The filters become controls: a job picker, so a superintendent sees one job's trades; a seat picker, so Robert sees his. Both already exist as readings on this page.
  • Left out: a count bar per step (a funnel drawn in rows), which repeats the summary line; sorting chips by days, since late-first then name is enough.
  • Not sure of: whether the Once lane's Invited and Portal opened should fold together, as drawn, or stay apart; and whether "Remind the 1 late" belongs on the row or only on the summary line, to keep one place for sends.

Say which pieces and I build them into By step; By trade and the Customers board take the same pieces where they apply.
