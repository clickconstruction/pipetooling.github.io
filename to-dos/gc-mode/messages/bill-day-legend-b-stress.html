Legend B under stress

The worry: a thin segment has no room for its words. The rule below gives every segment a floor and drops words in steps, never the count, and anything dropped reads again on the also line. Six cases, worst first.

The rule

FloorNo segment draws thinner than 14px, even at 1%. The bar's other segments give up the difference. A 1px white seam separates neighbors, so two thin ones never read as one. Wide, 14% and upDollars in the bar. Under it, the count and the full words: Not in · due Oct 19. Middle, 7 to 14%Dollars in the bar. Under it, the count and the state alone: Sent back. Thin, under 7%Nothing in the bar. Under it, the count alone. Its state and dollars move to the also line under the legend, with its color square, and read on hover. One segmentOne column, left-aligned, the full words. No also line.

Is this good to go?
  • Nothing is ever lost. The count always sits under its segment, and whatever words a thin segment cannot hold reappear on the also line with the color square. Hover on the segment or the count says the same.
  • Case 4 is the real test and it still reads: one big number, three small counts under three slivers, and one line naming them. That is the shape of a job in its first month, when nothing is in yet.
  • The 14px floor is a lie of one or two percent. A 1% segment draws at about 2%. The hover carries the true dollars, and the section below carries the rows. Worth it so a sliver is never invisible.
  • The widths are measured, not guessed. The tiers use the segment's drawn pixels, not its percent, so the phone and the desk pick the right tier on their own.
  • Not sure of: whether a thin segment's count should sit under it (as drawn) or move to the also line too, leaving the legend row with gaps. Drawn under, so the count and the color stay together.
