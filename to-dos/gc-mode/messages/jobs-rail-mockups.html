The pick, and is this the best we can do?

A, with B's No job reachable: the lines that sit on the person alone are a filter stop too, as a last ghost chip on Greg alone · 2 that filters like the others. One click filters, as the plan has it; the row names the state; the star means one thing and is explained on hover; the rare moves (make default, remove) live in the job's card in words, which also makes them work on a phone.

Three ways it could be better, weighed:

  1. The filter and the default could be one thing. If "the job I am looking at" were also "the job new messages land on", there would be one state. It is not: the default is a fact about Greg that outlives the view, and reading Boerne's lines must not move his next text there. Two states, so two marks.
  2. C's menu is the phone-safe shape. A keeps the one-click filter and leans on hover for the star's meaning; on a phone the hover card never shows. The phone layout is on the plan's open list; when it comes, C's menu is the likely fold for narrow columns, as the tie sheet already is.
  3. The × could stay on the chip. It is one more target on a small chip, and removing a job from a person is rare and worth a named press in the card, so it goes; the card's button says what the tied lines keep.
