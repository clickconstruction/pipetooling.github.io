Proposal · GC mode and the whole app · drawn with the prototype's made-up people

Every call and text in one place

The company gets one number in Quo. Everyone answers calls and texts in Quo's phone and computer apps, as they would anyway. Each call, text, voicemail, recording, transcript and summary comes into this app, sorted by who it is from and tied to the job. Replies by text go out from the company number, right from the thread. Big files go to the job's Google Drive folder through a file agent, and the thread gets the link.

The app files itChecks the event came from Quo, matches the number to a person or company, threads it, ties it to a job, and reads it for a promised day or a plans question.
It shows upMessages, Follow up, the company's Activity, and the board's people count. A text reply goes back through Quo from the company number.

The app's part is one webhook receiver, one sender, the tables for threads and phone numbers, the matching rule, and the Messages screens. Quo keeps the phones ringing. Email can join the same threads later, from a shared Google mailbox.

6 · Is this the best we can do?

No. The draft builds a second inbox, and the office already has one.

  • Two badges asking for the same attention.

    Follow up says 5 and Messages says 3, and Greg is in both. A text nobody has answered is someone waiting on us, which is what Follow up is for. Unanswered calls and texts belong at the top of Follow up, in its count, not in a tab of their own.

  • Nothing says a message still needs an answer.

    A shared number lives or dies on that. Each conversation needs a state: needs a reply (and for how long), someone has it, or done. Today the draft only shows what was said.

  • Two people could answer the same text.

    With a shared number, Robert and Wendi both see Greg's voicemail. The thread needs "Robert has this" so the second person sees it is taken.

  • The app wrote down a promise by itself.

    The call card says "Written down from the summary". The rest of the app never applies a guess on its own; a person confirms it. The voicemail already does it right with "Write it down". The call should match.

  • One thread per person hides the jobs.

    A company quoting four of our bids would have one long thread. The job is buried in a small dropdown on each line. The thread needs job filters at the top and a visible job label on every line.

  • The project page cannot see the conversation.

    Sitting on Boerne's Trades tab, you cannot tell that Hillside left a voicemail an hour ago. Each job needs its own short list of recent calls and texts, by company.

  • "Money in messages: owner only" cannot work.

    A text is free words: the app cannot strip a price out of it. Who sees a thread has to be decided by the kind of thread and the job, not by what is typed in it.

  • Texts sent from Quo's own app must show up too.

    People will reply from their phones. The thread is only whole if those replies come back into it. Quo reports sent messages as they are delivered, so this should work; it is a thing to check before building.

9 · Big files · the file agent

Big files go to the job's Drive folder, and the thread gets a link

A plan set is too big to text and often too big to email. A helper we call the file agent saves every file it is handed to the right job's Google Drive folder. When it is sure, it files on its own. It reads the file, puts it on the open side or the team side of the folder, and hands back a link ready to send. Big files never pass through the phone line or the mailbox. They move from where they are straight into Drive.

10 · Decisions before anything is built

Seven questions for you, and three decided

  • One company number, or a number per estimator?

    The mockup has a main line and an Estimating line, both into Messages.

  • Who sees which threads?

    Drawn in the revised version: estimators and the owner see trade threads, a job's project manager and the owner see customer threads.

  • Where people reply.

    In the app, in Quo's phone app, or both. Both work if Quo's sent messages come back into the thread, which is the first thing to check.

  • Which job does a message belong to?

    A company quoting four of our bids texts once. The draft rule: a job named in the message, else the most recent open ask, else the app asks.

  • Recordings, and the notice.

    Texas needs only one side's consent, but callers from other states may need both. A short notice at the start of each call covers it.

  • Photos in the job's Drive folder too?

    Every photo is kept in the app either way. A copy would go in the job's Team only folder, where the field team already looks.

  • The Quo plan.

    Transcripts, summaries and the API are plan features. The plan sets the price per person: roughly $23 to $35 a month each, billed yearly.

Decided, Oct 5

  • The file agent files on its own when it is sure.

    Move and Undo sit on every file. Only the unsure ones wait for a person.

  • Anyone can open the plans, and the team keeps a folder of its own.

    Each job's Plans folder opens for anyone with the link, trades and us alike. Team only holds quotes, our bid, contracts and photos.

  • No files@ address for now.

    The team forwards to bids@, the mailbox quotes and plans already come to.
