The schedule as a Gantt

Five pictures of one schedule: the office's chart, one activity opened, the weekly update, what the customer gets, and what one trade gets. All five are drawn from the same dates. Made-up data: Fair Oaks Shops, Building D, today Fri Oct 2. The plan is in GANTT_PLAN.md and every feature is numbered in GANTT_FEATURES.md.

1

The office's chart

One screen answers: where are we, what is late, what moves the finish, and who do I call. The red outline is the chain of work with no spare days. The thin grey line under a bar is where it sat in the plan at Start. Striped bars are held by a submittal or a question. Weekends are shaded so a date reads at a glance. Every day is a working day.
