2 · Is this the best we can do?

No. It shows the days, but not what went into them.

  • The squares are empty.

    Fourteen companies were invited on Sep 19. Quotes came in on five different days, four of them on Oct 1. Three questions were asked. None of that shows, so the strip says when, not what. A square should carry what happened that day.

  • Weekends weigh the same as work days.

    Six calendar days are left, but only five working days, counting today. Full-size Saturday and Sunday squares make the time look longer than it is.

  • The future has only two dates in it.

    Questions close and the bid is due. Nothing says when the quotes we still need should come in. Our levelling needs them before the last day, so the calendar should show a day we want them by.

  • Days before the bid set and after the bid look like part of it.

    Mon Sep 14 to Thu Sep 17 and Fri Oct 9 to Sun Oct 11 fill out the weeks. They should be faint, so the eye reads only the bid's own days.

  • Building is five months long.

    About 160 day squares will not fit. The same idea has to work in weeks there.

4 · Asked again: is this the best we can do?

Closer. Three more changes made it in, and one thing stays as a question.

  • The count sits beside the calendar.

    "Quotes in 9 of 14 wanted" makes the dots add up to something. Without it, dots on squares are decoration.

  • Labels only for dates that matter.

    An early pass labelled every square with an event. Only the bid set, an addendum, today and the deadlines get words. The rest is in the hover, so labels never collide.

  • The same strip for the other stages.

    Buying out has a few weeks, so it keeps day squares: from our contract to the start date, with the papers coming in as marks. Building switches to a square a week with a space between months, sampled below.

  • Still a question: a promised day per company.

    When a company says "by Monday", the prototype can already write it down. Those days could sit on the future squares as hollow dots. No company on Boerne has given one yet, so it is not drawn here.

5 · What I need from you

Three choices before I build it

  • Narrow weekends?

    Saturday and Sunday at half width, so the working days read first. Or every day the same size.

  • A "quotes wanted by" day?

    The day we want every quote in. My draft puts it on the day questions close, three days before the bid. It would also go to the trades in their invitations and portals.

  • Building in weeks?

    One square a week with a space between months, milestones marked. Or keep the line there and use day squares only while bidding and buying out.
