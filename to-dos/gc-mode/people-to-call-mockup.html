3. Is this the best we can do? Not quite.

Count people, not lines.The eight lines are five trade companies. One call to Greg at Hillside covers his late quote and Addendum 1. The card lists each person once, with every reason under their name.
The architect and the customer are people too.Marsh & Vale owe us two answers (roof curbs for 5 days, the 400 amp service for 2). After our bid goes out, the customer's answer is a call too. They belong in the count, tagged.
Our own moves are not calls.A statement of work we drafted and never sent, or pre-bid minutes to send, is our job. The ring's card already lists our steps. The pill counts only people we are waiting on.
Say each thing once.The price chip repeats the ring and the "with 4 holes" line. A plans chip that reads "Permit set" with nothing to do is noise. Both go. Who has not opened the newest set shows as a reason in the card.
The pill carries the urgency.Red when anyone's day has passed, amber when it is today, grey while everyone still has time: "6 to call · 2 late". When nobody owes us anything, the column says so quietly.
Act from the card, like the ring, only more.Hover opens it, a click pins it, Escape closes it, and a tap opens it on a phone. Each person has Call and Follow up, the Building lane's draft. Work the list · 6 opens the call sheet for just this job.
