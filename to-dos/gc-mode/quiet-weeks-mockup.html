1 · What goes wrong today

Three problems in your screenshot

  • Labels collide.

    "Pricing set" under Thu Oct 1 and "Today" under Fri Oct 2 print on top of each other. A wide label also pushes its own square sideways, so the amber Oct 19 square hides under Oct 20.

  • Empty weeks take the room.

    Oct 5 to 18 has nothing in it, but its fourteen squares are half the strip. The two weeks that matter are squeezed to the ends.

  • A square is only a number.

    Nothing says which day is a Monday, so "19" and "22" read as numbers, not as "the Monday questions close" and "the Thursday the bid is due".

3 · Is this the best we can do?

Closer, but the days still do not read

  • The weekday is still missing.

    A letter over each square (M T W T F S S) makes "22" read as Thursday the 22nd at a glance.

  • "Week of Sep 28" hides where the month turns.

    "Sep 28 – Oct 4" says both ends, so Oct 1 is not a mystery "1".

  • The labels need their own lane.

    Each label sits under its day without moving any square. Two labels that would touch take two lines.

  • A quiet rectangle should say what kind of quiet.

    Ahead: "5 working days". Gone by: "nothing came in". When the job has no last day: "No start date", dashed red.

  • Considered and left out: one rectangle for two quiet weeks.

    One rectangle a week keeps the weeks countable, which is the point of the calendar.
