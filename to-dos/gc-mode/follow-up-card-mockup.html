2 · What repeats

Four kinds of repeat

  • The promise, four times.

    The section says their day passed. The sentence says it. The red chip says it. The last call ends "Quote by Sep 30." The chip alone says the day and how late, and it is the one that changes color.

  • The section, again in the card.

    "Never opened it" heads the section, and the card's first line says "has not opened it". The card only needs what the section does not say: when we asked.

  • The bid date on every card.

    "Our bid is due Thu Oct 8" is on all three Boerne cards. It belongs with the job's name, in the chip.

  • Three ways to log a call.

    Call and Follow up open the sheet, which writes down what they said and the day they gave. Log a contact does it a third way, on the card itself.

What changed

  • One status, in the chip.

    The sentence under the name is gone. The chip says the promised day and how late, or when we asked.

  • The last call says what they said.

    "Quote by Sep 30" comes off the last line when the chip already shows that day. The full story keeps every promise.

  • The bid date sits with the job.

    "Boerne Retail Shell · Sitework · bid Thu Oct 8".

  • Log a contact leaves this card.

    Call and Follow up already log it. The Trades tab and the company window keep it.

The buttons are left as they are in the build. They are drawn on their own row here only to fit this column.

4 · Is this the best we can do?

Two more repeats came out on the second look

  • The paragraph at the top repeats the sections and adds a third count.

    It says "3 to call now" right under "Work the list · 5". The sections already count their cards, so the paragraph keeps one sentence and drops its count.

  • "No contact logged on this ask yet" says too much.

    It becomes "No call or message logged yet."

  • Kept as they are, on purpose.

    Will not do it and Cannot do it stay two buttons: you asked for each to ask why. Who else? stays. The card's sentence still feeds the sheet, the board's people count and Trade partners, so it stays in the data and only leaves this card.

  • Not fixed here: three counts of people.

    "Work the list · 5" counts people across every job. The paragraph's "3 to call now" counts companies with a quote overdue or unopened. The board row's "6 to call" counts everyone on one job, the architect included. The paragraph's count goes. Making the other two one number is the Board lane's to settle.
