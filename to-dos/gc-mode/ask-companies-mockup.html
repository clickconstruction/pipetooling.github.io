Is this the best we can do? What the second look changed

  1. The preview is the real email, not a summary. The first sketch listed "the terms" in a box of its own. The invitation already says the day, the plans and the scope, so the right side shows that email and the box went away.
  2. One preview, a tab per company. Two full emails stacked would bury the button. A Spanish company's tab shows it in Spanish, as it will go.
  3. What helps you choose sits on each company: how they answer when asked, the drive, their language. A company not vetted yet says so.
  4. Too-far companies are offered, not ticked. That folds "Ask anyway" into the same window.
  5. The foot says what the press does: how many emails, and whether the trade reaches the two quotes we want.
  6. One way to ask. The "+ Ask on …" chips and the assistants' Ask action open this window too, with that company ticked.
  7. Left out on purpose: a typed note added to the invitation. It needs a new field on the invitation, which moves the shared model. Worth a yes from the owner first.
