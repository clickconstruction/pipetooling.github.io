2. First idea: a Send button that goes to Contracts

Put Send it on the row. It closes the window and opens the project's Contracts tab, where the master agreement is sent today.

3. Is this the best we can do? Not quite.

Send where you are.Leaving the window to send loses your place, and a master agreement belongs to no single job, so there is no project to land on. The window already shows the paper. The send belongs beside it.
Show what they will get.The email, in their language, with the paper and the link to sign in their portal. Nobody sends blind, and you can add a line of your own.
Name the button for what happens.Not sent: Send to sign. Sent and waiting: Remind them. Insurance and a W-9: Ask for it. A lien waiver: Ask for the waiver. One button per row, the next step only.
Asking has to send something.Today's Ask for it only writes a promise. Every ask now sends an email and shows in their portal under Needs you. The promise stays: Follow up chases it after its day.
Give it a day.Every send carries a Sign by or Send by day, a week out unless you pick another. Signing keeps it. A passed day shows on Follow up.
Say what already happened."First sent Sep 30, 2 days ago" sits above the button, so nobody sends twice in a day. After sending, the row reads "reminded today · sign by Fri Oct 9".

4. Revised: the row's button opens the send beside the list

Press Remind them on the master agreement. The right side changes from the paper to the send: who it goes to, the day, a line of your own, and the email exactly as Bluebonnet will get it, with the agreement attached. Cancel goes back to the paper. Dashed outlines mark what is new.

7. The plan

  • Kernel (gcPaperSend.ts, tested): each paper's next step (the button's words, first send or reminder, sign by or send by) and the email it sends, in the company's language.
  • One action, sendPaper: the first master agreement or statement of work goes out as today; then it writes the promise with its day and keeps the send on the company, so the row, Activity and their portal can say it.
  • The send view in the Documents tab: the row's button opens it beside the list; Cancel goes back to the paper; Send closes it and shows the paper again with the new line.
  • Their messages: each send shows in the trade's inbox (one add-only line in the Portal lane's message list, told to that lane).
  • Replaces today's silent Ask for it. Golden: one new step at the end.
