# G-37: pull work earlier, on a press

## What it is, and what it is not

Today a bar only ever moves later. `pushAfter` (`gcBuildingSchedule.ts`) pushes what waits on a
moved bar out, and it never pulls anything in. A finish is recorded three ways: the walk's
**It finished today**, a trade's 100% report (`withReportedActuals` sets `actualFinish`), and an
inspection's pass or an added activity's **Mark it done today**. None of them moves a date. So a
trade that finishes a week early gives the job nothing. The week sits as spare days nobody sees.

G-37 is one press that catches the plan up with the work that finished early and brings in what was
waiting right behind it. The owner's call: it happens on a press, never by itself. Like every move,
it is saved with a reason and a sentence (call 2), it can be undone, and the trades whose dates came
in are told through **Tell the trades** (call 4).

## The rule

**What finished early.** An activity whose finish day is before its planned finish. The finish day
is its recorded actual finish (the walk, the editor, a trade's 100% report), an inspection's pass
day, or an added activity's done day. A line reported at 100% with no day recorded counts as
finished today. A finish the walk already answered with **Keep the dates** is left out.

**What comes in.** The work right behind it: an activity that waits on it and starts within
`RIGHT_BEHIND_DAYS` = 2 days of the day after it finishes, plus any gap on the wait (G-35). Two days
covers a weekend in a schedule drawn in weekdays, which is how the made-up job was drawn. A drafted
schedule has no room at all (all 18 waits on the Helotes draft are tight). Each one comes in by the
days the work before it gave back, **never more**. What is right behind those comes in by what they
gave back, and so on down the line.

**Never before**: tomorrow (`PULL_SOONEST_DAYS` = 1, since today is too late to tell a trade), its
own *Not before* day (G-36), or the day after a delivery, a decision, a permit or the utility it waits
on is expected (G-73 to G-75).

**What stays, and is said.** Work that has started or is done. Work held by a submittal or an RFI
(it would only be late on paper). An inspection that failed and waits on its re-inspection (the city
set that day). Work drawn with more than 2 days of room before it (someone set that room by hand).
Work whose other waits still finish later ("It still waits on Top out, which finishes Fri Oct 9").
Work the office unticks because the trade cannot come sooner, and what waits on it. The milestones,
the baseline and the contract's finish do not move.

**Why only the days given back, not every day of room.** I tried "as early as the waits allow" on
the made-up job first. It would bring Plumbing trim in from Nov 30 to Oct 14, because its 47 days of
room were drawn by hand for work that is not on this schedule. A pull gives back what the early finish
freed and nothing it did not.

## The made-up job, run through the rule

Fair Oaks D, today Fri Oct 2. The fixture has no early finish, so nothing shows until one is made.

- **Summit reports TPO membrane at 100%** (7 days early). Both bars right behind it are held:
  Sheet metal and flashing by submittal 07 62 00-01, Rooftop units by RFI-003 with the architect.
  Nothing can come in, and the line says what to chase.
- **Cool Breeze reports Ductwork at 100%** (7 days early). The rough-in inspection still waits on
  Top out, which finishes Fri Oct 9. Nothing comes in. The walk and the opened activity say so; the
  line over the chart stays quiet, since there is nothing to press or chase.
- **Our crew then reports Top out at 100%** (7 days early). Now the rough-in inspection can move from
  Oct 12 to Oct 13 to Mon Oct 5 to Tue Oct 6, 7 days sooner. Trim keeps its dates: it was drawn with
  47 days of room before it. The job still finishes Tue Dec 8.

The second case is why the offer is one per project, not one per finished bar. Ductwork alone or Top
out alone moves nothing. Together they move the inspection. One press catches both plans up.

## The screens

### 1. A line over the chart (the Schedule tab, a job being built)

Under the walk line, green, only when there is something to press or something holding the days.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ (walked) Walked today by Robert. 3 kept as drawn, 1 moved.              [Update the week · 9] │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ (finished early) Top out finished Fri Oct 2, 7 days early. Ductwork finished Fri Oct 2,    │
│ 7 days early. 1 activity right after them can start 7 days sooner. The job still finishes  │
│ Tue Dec 8.                                                           [Pull the work earlier] │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

When the work right behind is held there is no button. The line names what to chase:

```
│ (finished early) TPO membrane finished Fri Oct 2, 7 days early. The work right after it is    │
│ held, so nothing can start sooner yet. Sheet metal and flashing waits on submittal            │
│ 07 62 00-01. Rooftop units waits on RFI-003, with the architect.                              │
```

When tomorrow already ate some of the days (the finish was recorded on Monday, walked Friday):
"2 of those days are gone already. Each day it waits costs one more."

A finish that brings the job in: "The job finishes Fri Dec 4, 4 days sooner."

### 2. The window every pull goes through: *Pull the work earlier*

```
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Pull the work earlier                                              │
│ Work finished early. What was right behind it can start sooner.    │
│ ┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐ │
│ │ FINISHED EARLY                                                │ │
│ │ Top out · Our own crew          planned Oct 9, done Oct 2  (7 days early) │
│ │ Ductwork · Cool Breeze Mech.    planned Oct 9, done Oct 2  (7 days early) │
│ │ CAN START SOONER                                              │ │
│ │ [x] Rough-in inspection · The city                            │ │
│ │     Oct 12 to Oct 13  →  Mon Oct 5 to Tue Oct 6   (7 days sooner) │
│ │ KEEPS ITS DATES                                               │ │
│ │ Trim · Our own crew: It was drawn with 47 days of room before it. │
│ │ The job still finishes Tue Dec 8.                             │ │
│ └───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘ │
│ WHY IT MOVED                                                       │
│ (Weather) (The trade before) … (Something else) ((Finished early)) │
│ WHAT HAPPENED, IN YOUR WORDS                                       │
│ ┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐ │
│ │ Top out finished Fri Oct 2, 7 days early. Ductwork finished   │ │
│ │ Fri Oct 2, 7 days early.                                      │ │
│ └───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘ │
│ Untick one a trade cannot start sooner. It keeps its dates.        │
│ Saved as one move by Robert, today.          [Cancel] [Pull 1 earlier] │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Finished early** is preset as the reason, and the sentence is filled in. Both can be changed.
  Both are required, like every move.
- Each activity that comes in has a tick. Unticking one recomputes the list: it moves to *Keeps its
  dates* ("Left out. It keeps its dates."), and what waited only on it stays too.
- A pull bound by something other than the early finish says so on its row: "Tomorrow is the
  soonest it can start." · "It cannot start before Mon Oct 5." · "It waits on Rooftop units,
  expected Tue Oct 20."
- **Pull 1 earlier** is disabled with nothing ticked.

### 3. The walk (*Update the week*)

**Right after *It finished today*.** The walk's own way of recording a finish. When it makes an
early finish, a green box opens under the actual-dates row, in the bar's panel:

```
  Really: Sep 14 to Oct 2, 7 days before the planned finish.
  ┌ It finished 7 days early. 1 activity right after it can start sooner. [Pull the work earlier…] ┐
```

With nothing to pull it says why, with no button: "Nothing right after it can start sooner yet. It
still waits on Top out, which finishes Fri Oct 9." Saving the pull from here marks this bar
*moved* in the walk, with the pull as its move.

**A *Finished early* item at the top of the list,** for finishes recorded outside the walk (a
trade's report in its portal). It is listed when the walk opens, above *The week*:

```
FINISHED EARLY
  Work that finished early
  1 can start sooner
THE WEEK · 0 of 8
  Roofing · TPO membrane
  …
```

Its panel reads the same sentences as the line over the chart, then asks:

```
  Pull the work after it earlier?
  [Pull them earlier…]  [Keep the dates]  [Skip for now]
```

**Keep the dates** records the finished lines in the walk's *kept*, so the offer stops for them.
That is the one way to say no and have it kept. Outside the walk, the line over the chart stays
until the work is pulled, kept in a walk, or tomorrow catches up with the days. The count on
**Update the week · N** includes this item.

### 4. The opened activity (the bar's menu)

Pressing a bar opens it in the editor. For a line that finished early:

```
It finished Fri Oct 2, 7 days early. 1 activity right after it can start sooner. [Pull the work earlier…]
```

For a line that can come in: "It can start Mon Oct 5, 7 days sooner. The work before it finished
early." with the same button. Both open the same window.

### 5. The chart

Each activity that can come in gets a green dashed outline at its sooner days, the way a drag shows
amber ghosts on what it pushes. Its hover card gains a row: **Could start** "Mon Oct 5, 7 days
sooner. The work before it finished early." After the pull, the bar's own note reads "7 days sooner
than at Start", which `barNote` already says.

### 6. After the press

- **Changes to the schedule**, a move like any other, with **Undo** and **Redo**:
  ```
  Fri Oct 2 · Robert  (Finished early)                                   [Undo]
  Plumbing · Top out and 1 more finished early.
  "Top out finished Fri Oct 2, 7 days early. Ductwork finished Fri Oct 2, 7 days early."
  Rough-in inspection was pulled earlier with them.
  ```
  One finished line reads "Roofing · TPO membrane finished Fri Oct 2, 7 days early." Several pulled
  read "3 after it were pulled earlier." A finish that moved adds the existing "The finish moved 4
  days sooner, to Dec 4."
- **Tell the trades** counts the companies whose dates came in and never the company that finished
  (its work is done). The message is the existing one, neutral and true: "Sheet metal and flashing:
  Oct 5 to Oct 14, not Oct 12 to Oct 21. Why: finished early: TPO membrane finished Fri Oct 2, 7 days
  early. In your portal, tell us the dates work, or give us another day." A trade that cannot come
  sooner answers **I need another day**, which lands on Follow up as it does today (call 5).
- **The customer's What changed this week**: "Rough-in is 7 days sooner than planned. The finish
  holds." No *because* clause: the good news explains itself, and no company is named.
- **Days lost, by cause**: a pull is laid at a trade's door as days back ("7 days back" on the work).
- **The log**: "Robert pulled 1 activity earlier on Fair Oaks D. Top out finished Fri Oct 2, 7 days
  early. Ductwork finished Fri Oct 2, 7 days early."

## Where it sits

The Schedule tab of a job being built: the line under the walk line, the window over the tab, the
walk's item and box, the editor's line, the chart's ghosts. Nothing on the board row, the ring card,
Needs you or the dashboard (those move other lanes' snapshots). No tour stop: the made-up data has no
early finish to anchor one on.

## The kernel: `src/lib/gcMode/gcPullEarlier.ts` (pure, tested, out of the barrel)

```ts
export const RIGHT_BEHIND_DAYS = 2
export const PULL_SOONEST_DAYS = 1

export interface PullFinished { lineId; name; trade; company; planned: Span; finishedOn: string; early: number }
export interface PullLine { lineId; name; trade; company; from: Span; to: Span; days: number; limit: string | null }
export interface PullStay { lineId; name; trade; company; why: string; held: boolean }
export interface PullOffer {
  finished: PullFinished[]
  pulls: PullLine[]
  stays: PullStay[]
  /** The whole schedule as it would stand: the finished lines caught up, the pulls in. */
  activities: ScheduleActivity[]
  finishFrom: string; finishTo: string; finishDays: number
  /** Days tomorrow already cost: the work could have come in this many more. */
  lostDays: number
  /** The sentences the line, the walk and the window read. Each passes plainWordsFailures. */
  words: { finished: string[]; pulls: string; held: string[]; finish: string; lost: string | null }
  /** The explanation the window starts with: the finished sentences. */
  note: string
  /** Something to press (pulls), or something to chase (a held stay). */
  show: 'pull' | 'chase' | 'quiet'
}

/** The project's one offer: every early finish, what comes in, what stays. Null: nothing finished early. */
planPull(state: GcState, project: GcProject, leaveOut?: string[]): PullOffer | null
/** The holds the reducer can read: an open RFI, a submittal not approved, a wait that is late. The same three the tab's holdsOf reads. */
pullHolds(state, project): Map<string, string>
/** The move the press saves: the first finished line, every other change in `pushed`, `pull.finished` set. */
pullMove(schedule, offer, why, today): ScheduleMove
/** The chart's ghosts: each pull's sooner days and its hover words. */
pullGhosts(offer): Map<string, { start: string; finish: string; words: string }>
/** One line for the editor and the walk's box, about one activity. Null: the offer says nothing about it. */
pullWordsFor(offer, lineId): string | null
```

Inputs: the state (today, the trades' reported percent through `scheduleItems`), the project (its
schedule, walks, waits, submittals, RFIs). Outputs: the offer above. Nothing is changed.

## The action

```ts
/** Work finished early: its plan catches up, and what was right behind it comes in (G-37). On a press, never by itself. */
| { type: 'pullScheduleEarlier'; projectId: string; leaveOut: string[]; why: { reason: ScheduleMoveReason; note: string; by: string } }
```

The reducer recomputes `planPull(state, project, leaveOut)`, refuses an empty pull or a missing
reason and sentence (`moveWhyProblem`), keeps the plan at Start (`withBaselineKept`), applies
`offer.activities`, records `pullMove(…)` newest first, and logs it. Undo and Redo need nothing new:
the move's line and `pushed` carry every date it changed.

Type additions (`gcTypes.ts`, additions only): `'early'` at the end of `ScheduleMoveReason`;
`pull?: { finished: string[] }` on `ScheduleMove` (the lines that finished early, so Tell the trades
skips them and the history says "finished early"); the action at the end of `GcAction`.

## Defaults the owner has not confirmed

- `RIGHT_BEHIND_DAYS` = 2: further than that after the work before it, the room was drawn on purpose.
- `PULL_SOONEST_DAYS` = 1: tomorrow is the soonest a pulled activity can start.
- A pull's days are laid at a trade's door in Days lost.
- The customer reads a pull with no *because* clause.

## What I am leaving out, and why

- **Needs you on the dashboard** ("Work finished early on Fair Oaks D"). A new line there moves the
  dashboard's tests and the board's counts. It waits for the owner's word.
- **A walkthrough stop.** The made-up data has no early finish to anchor one on.
- **Pulling on pace** (a trade running ahead). A pace is a guess; the owner said *finishes*.
- **A drag earlier bringing its followers.** A bar dragged earlier still says "Nothing after it
  moves." G-37 is about a finish, and the office can drag the next bar too.
- **Asking the trade before the bar moves.** The bar moves on the press and Tell the trades asks.
  That is call 4's loop, and the trade's **I need another day** already answers it.
- **Catching up a finished line's plan on its own**, when nothing comes in. It would move dates
  nobody waits on and add moves nobody asked for. The press does it when it matters.

## Is this the best we can do?

Three ways it could be better, one for each job of the chart:

1. **See: show the sooner days on the chart before anyone presses.** A green ghost at each
   activity's sooner days, and *Could start* on its hover card. **I pick it.** The line over the
   chart says it in words. The chart should say it in its own picture, the way a drag shows its
   ghosts. It is one optional prop on `GcGantt` and costs nothing else. It is in the screens above.
2. **Chase: say what holds the early days.** When the work right behind is held, the line names the
   hold with no button, instead of saying nothing. **I pick it.** It is the only case the made-up
   job makes (TPO membrane's followers are held by a submittal and an RFI). An early finish wasted on
   a late submittal is exactly what someone should chase today. It is in the screens above.
3. **Tell: a pulled trade reads an ask, not a notice.** "Can you start sooner on Fair Oaks D?"
   instead of "Your dates moved". **Not now.** The existing message is already true ("Why: finished
   early: …"), and its two answers fit an ask. A second message kind is a second Spanish list for
   the native speaker (call 9). It belongs with the real emails through Resend.
