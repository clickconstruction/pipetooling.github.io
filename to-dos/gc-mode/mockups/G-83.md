## 1. Where a place comes from

**A plain word on the bar**, `ScheduleActivity.place?: string`: *Roof*, *Inside*, *Site*,
*Level 2*, *Suite 101*. The office types it, 40 characters at most. Unset means no place yet.

**A guess, where one can be made**, in this order:

1. **The name.** *Rooftop units* and *Roof curbs* are on the roof. *Site lighting*, *Paving* and
   *Sidewalks and curbs* are the site. *Storefront* is the outside walls. *Framing, level 2* is
   *Level 2*, and *Suite 101 ceilings* is *Suite 101*.
2. **The trade**, when the name says nothing. Roofing is the roof. Sitework and Landscaping are the
   site. Glass and storefront is the outside walls.
3. **The stage** (`lineStage`), when neither does. Framing, rough-in, close-in, finishes, trim and
   closeout are inside. Site prep and site finish are the site.

Foundations, underground, the slab, the frame, and dry-in by any other trade get no guess. They
are the whole building at once.

**Never invented silently.** A guess is shown as a guess, with where it came from. It counts for
nothing until the office keeps it. With no place kept, nothing is flagged anywhere. That is the
made-up job today, so no other lane's pinned list moves.

## 2. What "too many" means: one named rule

`TRADES_IN_ONE_PLACE = 3`, said wherever the flag is:

> *Our rule: 3 trades or more in one place on the same day is too many.*

- **Counted by the day, flagged by the week.** A week is flagged when some day in it has 3 trades
  in one place. Two trades a week apart in the same place are no crowd.
- **On site by the plan's own dates**, as G-84 counts them. A bar not done runs from its start to
  its finish, so a late bar does not fill every week ahead.
- **A trade** is one trade on the job. Our own crew counts. Inspections and the job's own bars do
  not, and a bar that is done is not there.
- **From today on.** A past week is history.
- **The people come with it, never as a second rule.** Each trade's number for the week is G-84's:
  its own count (G-142), else the log's last, else 3. They are added up and said beside the trades.

On the made-up job, once the guesses are kept: **Inside has 3 trades at once from Fri Oct 2 to
Fri Oct 9.** They are Pecan Valley Electric with 2, our own crew with 3 and Cool Breeze Mechanical
with 3, about 8 people a day. The roof's 2, Oct 12 to Oct 21, is under the rule.

The number is the owner's call. At 3, the made-up job's rough-in weeks are flagged. At 4, nothing
is flagged until a fourth trade joins them, such as the steel crew if Erection is put inside.

## 8. Left out, and why

- **A limit for each place**, such as the roof taking 2. One named rule comes first. A limit per
  place is a field on the job and a second action, the owner's call once the rule is in use.
- **Grouping the chart by place.** That is option 3 below.
- **The trade's portal** saying who else is in the place. The flag is the office's, by the lead's
  word.
- **The paper (G-21).** Its head prints facts. A crowded week is a warning, and it lives in the
  chart's lane, not on a bar.
- **The job's own bars**, such as the customer's own work inside. They take no place yet, since
  they have no trade to count.
- **A place's name in Spanish.** It is the office's word, so a Spanish message carries it as typed.
