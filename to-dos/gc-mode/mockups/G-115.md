# G-115: By company as a call list

## The job

The office presses **By company** on a job's chart because it is about to pick up the phone. Today that
grouping only reorders the bars: the company with the most late work comes first. Nothing says who
to ask for, what to say, or who else holds the work. Their dates may have gone out with no answer,
and the chart never shows that. The insurance or a waiver they owe sits on another tab. G-115 makes
By company the list to work the phone from: one row per person whose answer moves the chart, every
reason under their name, and the Follow up tab's own **Call** and **Follow up** on each row.

## Where it sits

- **Schedule tab → the chart's toolbar → By company.** The list opens under the toolbar, above the
  chart's rows, in both **Chart** and **List**. By trade and By stage do not show it.
- Only on a job being built. While the schedule is drawn, nothing is late yet, so By company stays
  a grouping.
- **Call** and **Follow up** open the Follow up sheet, the one the board row's Who to call card and
  the Follow up tab open. Here it walks this list.
- The opened activity, the editor above the chart, gets the bar's company with **Call** and
  **Follow up** at its foot (pick 1 below). That is picture 2 of `gantt-mockup.html`, never built.

## Who is on it, and why

One row per person whose answer moves this chart. A row needs one reason that is theirs.

- **A hired trade**, for its bars: an inspection that failed on its work (the failure names the
  trade), a bar late, due today or behind (`ganttBars`' standing), new dates told and not
  answered, a first day within 14 days that nobody confirmed (`startsToPromise`), its own submittal
  holding a bar, its supplier's delivery holding a bar (the waits that hold, `waitHolds`' rule).
- **The architect**, for a submittal or an RFI with them that holds a bar.
- **The customer**, for a decision they owe that holds a bar.
- **Then everything else they owe on this job**, merged under their name: `projectPeople`'s reasons
  (insurance, a waiver, a statement of work, a late bill, a change order to sign, an ask for another
  day). One call covers it all. It is the same rule as the Who to call card.
- **Not on it:** the city (a failed inspection goes to the trade whose work failed), the utility
  and the permit office (no phone on record), our own crew, the job's own bars, finished trades.
  A hold that waits on us is an aside under the trade, never a row.
- **Order:** red, then amber, then grey. Inside a tone, the most reasons first, then the trades in
  the chart's trade order, then the customer and the architect. The chart's group order stays as it
  is, the most late bars first.
- **Tones** follow the tabs they come from: an RFI's tone is the RFIs tab's (`neededTone`), and a
  submittal's uses the same days.

## The kernel

`src/lib/gcMode/gcCallList.ts`, pure, out of the barrel, with `gcCallList.test.ts`.

```ts
/** A reason on the call list: Follow up's reason, plus the bar it is about. */
export interface CallReason extends PersonReason {
  kind: 'failed' | 'late' | 'due' | 'behind' | 'dates' | 'start' | 'submittal' | 'delivery' | 'rfi' | 'decision' | 'aside' | 'followUp'
  lineId?: string
}
export interface CallPerson extends ProjectPerson { reasons: CallReason[] }

/** Everyone whose answer moves this chart, worst first, with everything they owe on this job. */
export function callList(state: GcState, project: GcProject): { people: CallPerson[]; count: number; late: number; tone: PeopleTone | null }

/** The same people for the Follow up sheet: this job's Follow up items, then one item per schedule reason, in en and es. */
export function callListFollowPeople(state: GcState, project: GcProject): FollowPerson[]

/** One bar's company and its word on the bar's dates, for the opened activity (pick 1). Null: no company to call. */
export function barCaller(state: GcState, project: GcProject, lineId: string): { person: CallPerson; word: string | null } | null

/** What a saved call does past logging it (pick 2): the dates answer, a delivery's new day, a promise. */
export function callListCallActions(person: FollowPerson, items: FollowItem[], dates: 'none' | 'work' | 'another', day: string | null, said: string, by: string): GcAction[]
```

## The actions

No new action type, so the golden test's list does not change. Each answer reuses one that exists:

- A saved call is logged on the company (or on the architect's or customer's record) by the sheet,
  as today (`logPartnerContact`). The list's last line shows it.
- **They work** → `tradeAnswerDates` with `ok: true`, the same answer the portal gives. The line
  clears, and the move's row reads *Summit Roofing: the dates work.*
- **They need another day** with the day → `tradeAnswerDates` with `ok: false` and the day. The line
  becomes Follow up's own *Asked for … after we moved it* until the bar moves again.
- A day on **their submittal** → `recordPromise` kind `submittals`. Building's rule keeps it when they send it.
- A day on **a start** → `recordPromise` kind `start`. It is kept when the daily log has their crew.
- A day on **a delivery** or **a decision** → `setScheduleWaitStep` step `expected`, with *Carla said
  on a call* as who said so. A day before the work starts lifts the hold.
- A day on late work under way, a failed inspection, an RFI or the architect's submittal: logged
  with the call only. See *Left out*.

## Left out, and why

- **The board row's count, Follow up's badge and Needs you** do not count the schedule's reasons.
  Counting them moves the golden walk's people counts at every step with a building job. That is
  the owner's call: option 3 below.
- **A day given on late work under way** is logged with the call, not kept as their word on the bar.
  That is G-117's new day, Helper 4's row. One record should serve both, so when it lands, the call
  form can write it too. I will tell the lead before building if G-117's shape is known.
- **The Follow up draft's nudge for several things** ends *Could you send them this week?*. That reads
  oddly for late work. *A full note* reads right. Changing the draft's words moves Building's tests.
- **The walkthrough** gets no stop: the list only shows on By company.
- **The utility and the permit office** have no phone on record, so they get an aside, not a row.

## Is this the best we can do?

Three ways it could be better, one for each job the chart has:

1. **See: the line and the bar open each other.** A line about a bar is a button. It opens that bar
   in the editor above the chart and scrolls there. The opened bar shows its company, its word on its
   dates, and **Call** and **Follow up**. That is the mock-up's picture 2, which promised *chase the
   company* from the opened activity and never got it. **Picked:** it costs one optional prop and a
   strip, and a caller can see what they are talking about without hunting for the bar.
2. **Chase: a call's answer lands where it belongs.** Without it, a call is logged and the list says
   the same thing tomorrow, which breaks Follow up's rule that the list stays honest. With it, *they
   work* answers the dates the way the portal does, and a day on a delivery moves the delivery. A day
   on a submittal or a start becomes their word, which Follow up already chases and Building already
   keeps. **Picked:** every answer reuses an action that exists, so the golden test does not move.
3. **Tell and Chase everywhere: the schedule's reasons on the board row, Follow up and Needs you.**
   One count everywhere is what the owner asked for on 2026-10-04 (*make them match*). Today the
   board row would say 2 to call while this list says 6. **Not picked now:** it moves the golden
   walk's people counts, so it needs the owner's OK. I recommend asking him. The list's kernel is
   built so `projectPeople` could merge it in one line.

What the second look already changed from the first sketch:

- **Held work goes to whoever holds it.** A submittal with the architect is a call to the architect,
  not to the trade. The trade's row keeps it as an aside, so the caller can answer if asked.
- **Everything they owe is under their name.** A call about a late bar that skips their lapsed
  insurance means a second call tomorrow.
- **A list above the chart, not buttons in its group rows.** The 360 px name column has no room
  for a name, two buttons and the reasons. A phone's 168 px has less. The bars below still carry
  their own late and held notes.

## Built (2026-10-06): what changed from this plan

- **Any hold on a bar makes a row for whoever owes it** (the lead's word, for G-77). `HOLD_OWNERS`
  goes by the hold's kind. A kind it does not know is the trade's own, worded as the chart words it
  (*Test and balance waits on current insurance, theirs ran out Sep 15.*). G-77's paperwork hold
  needs no change here. The list reads the chart's own holds (`holdsOf`), plus a late wait the chart
  keeps under a submittal or an RFI on the same bar.
- **The customer is not on the fixture's list.** The restroom tile decision does not hold Trim yet:
  a wait holds only when it comes after the work starts, as on the chart. So the list has five people,
  two late, not six and three.
- **A company that asked for another day is on the list for it**, with the bar, not only merged in.
- **G-117, merged before this pushed.** Each open late notice (`openLateNotices`) is a line of its own
  under the company, in the office card's words: *Summit Roofing says TPO membrane will finish Wed
  Oct 14, not Fri Oct 9: materials.* It puts a company on the list by itself. Follow up's copy of the
  same notice, merged in from `projectPeople`, is said once.
- **G-77, merged before this pushed.** Its paperwork hold folds a hold already on the bar into one
  pill (*current insurance and submittal 28 31 11-01*). So a submittal, an RFI and a wait reach their
  owner from their own records, and paperwork names only the trade's papers (*Fire alarm waits on
  current insurance.*).
- **Messages name a line with its trade**: *your structural steel erection*, *your roofing TPO
  membrane*. A line's name keeps its capitals only when it is an acronym.
- **The opened bar's strip** shows for a hired trade's bar with work left. Its Follow up opens the
  sheet on the call list; a company with nothing on the list joins it with that bar.
