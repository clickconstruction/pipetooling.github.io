# G-146: to call means the whole call list

## What it is

The counts (round three) put a company's own schedule reasons on the board row's pill, Follow up's
badge and Needs you. Those are a paper a bar waits on, its dates, the log, its crew and a crowded
place. They left out the call list's bar reasons, the ones about how the work runs: a bar late, a
bar due today, a bar behind, a bar held, a failed inspection. So on Fair Oaks D the board row says
**2 to call**, while By company (G-115) lists **5**.

G-146 counts those too. Then *to call* means the whole call list, plus what Follow up already had.
The pill, the badge and Needs you agree with By company.

## Which bar reasons count

Each comes from the call list's own read (`gcCallList.ts`), with its own words:

| The call list's line | Its code on the count | Tone | Who it is on |
|---|---|---|---|
| An inspection failed on their work | `failed` | red | each trade whose work failed |
| A bar past its finish | `overdue` | red | the trade doing the bar |
| A bar due today, not done | `dueToday` | amber | the trade doing the bar |
| A bar behind the plan | `behind` | amber | the trade doing the bar |
| A bar held by what someone owes | `held` | the hold's own | whoever owes it: the trade's submittal, the architect's RFI or submittal, a delivery's company, the customer's decision |

Left as they are:

- **An aside never counts.** An aside is a hold that waits on us, the city or the utility, said
  under the trade so the caller knows. The call list does not count it either.
- **G-77's paperwork holds are not counted again.** They are the counts' *not ready* reason
  already, one per company, so the call list's held lines for the same papers are skipped.
- **The codes are new**, so nothing clashes. `overdue` is not G-117's `late`, which is a trade's
  own word that it will be late.

**How they fold.** Once per company, as the counts do. Every reason goes under the person who
already has a row, and one call covers all of them. A company only on the call list gets a row
of its own. The architect and the customer are people too: an RFI the architect owes, holding a
bar, is a reason under Marsh & Vale.

## The words: a call about the work is not a paper owed

**The person's reasons in two short groups**, in the pill's card, Follow up's *By people* and By
company. They all share `PeopleRows`:

```
Pecan Valley Electric · Electrical                                   [Call] [Follow up]
  ON THE SCHEDULE
  ● Electrical service inspection failed Mon Sep 28. The city sees it again today.
  ● Panels and feeders is due today and 80% done.
  ● Lighting is behind: 40% done against 48% in the plan. It is due Fri Oct 23.
  ● Site lighting and Fire alarm wait on current insurance. Site lighting starts Mon Oct 19.
  OWED TO US
  ● Their insurance ran out Tue Sep 15. Nothing they do for us is covered. They are at work
    on Panels and feeders, and Lighting.
  ● The unconditional waiver on draw 1 has not come.
```

- **On the schedule** holds every reason that comes from the chart: these five, the counts'
  reasons (not ready, dates, a push back, the log, the crew, a crowded place), a late notice and
  another day asked for.
- **Owed to us** holds the papers, the answers and the money: a quote, the plans, a statement of
  work, a contract, a change order, a bill, a waiver, insurance, a W-9, a day given, the
  architect's questions.
- A person with reasons of one kind shows one group. Each group is worst first, and the dot keeps
  its tone.

**Needs you's phrase** names the work for a bar reason. It is chosen by the person's worst reason,
as now:

| Code | Phrase |
|---|---|
| `failed` | *Pecan Valley Electric's work failed an inspection* |
| `overdue` | *Summit Roofing is late on TPO membrane* |
| `dueToday` | *Iron Horse Fabrication is due today on Erection* |
| `behind` | *Summit Roofing is behind on TPO membrane* |
| `held` | *Cool Breeze Mechanical holds up Rooftop units* |

**The message drafted on Follow up** asks about the work in the call list's own words. For Summit
it is about *your roofing TPO membrane on Fair Oaks Shops, Building D*. It says *It is 50% done, and
our plan had 100% by today*, then asks *When will it be done?* It does not read as a paper we are
waiting for.

## The press

The same as for any reason: **Call** and **Follow up** on the person.

**The sheet gets an item for each bar reason**, from the call list's own `words` (G-115's
`scheduleItem`). The board row's sheet and Follow up's *Work the list* then cover every reason the
count counts.

This also fills a gap the counts left. `projectFollowPeople` built a trade's items from Follow up's
known asks and the plans, so a company there only for its dates or its crew had no item, and the
sheet left it out. The board row's sheet becomes the call list's people with their items, then
Follow up's other people on the job, such as Cibolo.

## The kernel and the files

- **`gcCallList.ts`, its own commit first, tests untouched:** `callList` splits into `callRows`,
  the call list's own lines before Follow up's merge, and `callList`, which is `callRows` plus the
  merge it does today. The counts read `callRows`, so the people kernel never calls itself.
  `scheduleItem` is exported for the sheet.
- **`gcCounts.ts`:** `barReasons(state, project)` reads `callRows` on a job being built. It keeps
  the five kinds, leaves out asides and G-77's paperwork, and recodes them. It is kept per state and
  job, like the rest. `CALL_LIST_SAYS` gains the five codes, so By company's merge says each once.
  `boardFollowPeople(state, project)` is the sheet's list.
- **`gcProjectPeople.ts`, an addition:** one loop adds `barReasons` under their people, the
  architect and the customer included. `allFollowPeople` reads `boardFollowPeople`.
- **`gcNeedsYou.ts`, an addition:** the five phrases.
- **`GcPeoplePill.tsx`, the one change to shared rendering:** `PeopleRows` shows the two groups.
  Which group a code goes in is `reasonGroup(code)` in `gcCounts.ts`, tested.
- **`GcMode.tsx`:** the board row's sheet reads `boardFollowPeople`.

## Left out, and why

- **By company itself.** It is the call list. Nothing about it changes but the merge.
- **A bar on track, or done.** Nobody needs a call about it.
- **Our own crew's bars.** They are ours, with no company to call. The call list leaves them out
  too.
