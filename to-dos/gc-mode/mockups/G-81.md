# G-81: a what-if copy of the schedule

## What it is

G-80 answers "if this one bar slips 5 days". The office's real questions are bigger. What if
the roof slips a week and we bring the HVAC crew in early? What if we take the city's later
inspection day? Today the only way to try that is to move the real bars. Each move asks why,
lands in the history, and waits for Tell the trades. Then the office moves them back.

G-81 is a copy of the whole schedule to try moves on. It sits beside the real one, is never told
to anyone, and is then **kept**, as real moves with their reasons, or **thrown away**.

## Where the copy lives

`GcProject.whatIf?: ScheduleWhatIf`, beside `project.schedule`, never inside it:

```ts
export interface ScheduleWhatIf {
  /** The copy: the real schedule when it was made, with moves of its own. Never walked, told, sent or baselined. */
  schedule: ProjectSchedule
  /** Each activity's planned dates and waits when the copy was made. Keep is safe only while the real schedule still has them. */
  base: Record<string, { start: string; finish: string; after: string[]; lag?: Record<string, number>; notBefore?: string; mustFinishBy?: string }>
  on: string
  by: string
}
```

The copy starts as `{ ...schedule, moves: [], walks: [] }`. It has the same activities, milestones,
baseline and look-ahead marks, and an empty history of its own. Everything outside the Schedule tab
reads `project.schedule` and never sees the copy. That covers the trade's and the customer's
portals, the architect's view, Tell the trades, Follow up, the call list, Needs you, the board row,
the ring card, the Friday report and the billing forecast's own months. One copy per job: a second
*Try a what-if* while one is open opens the one there is.

## What the office can do in the copy

Every move the real schedule allows, through the same windows:

- drag a bar or an end, or change it in the opened activity (dates, waits, the gap on a wait, *Not
  before*, *Must finish by*);
- draw or take off a wait on the chart;
- **Pull the work earlier** (G-37), when something finished early;
- Undo and Redo on the copy's own history.

**Why it moved is optional** in the copy. The window says *Try it* where it says *Save the move*.
With a reason and a sentence, the move keeps them. Without, it is kept as *no reason yet*, and Keep
asks for one.

Nothing else works in the copy. The walk, actual dates, inspections, milestones, waits, added
activities, baselines, Tell the trades and the customer's send all stay on the real schedule. The
cards for them are hidden while the copy is shown.

## The screens

### 1. The way in: the chart's toolbar

```
[Chart|List] [Days|Weeks|Months] [By trade|By stage|By company]  Today  Fold all  ⇢ links  Print or PDF  [What if…]
```

**What if…** makes the copy and shows it. With a copy open it reads **What if · 2**, the moves
tried, and opens it. Inside the copy it reads **See the real schedule**. The copy stays open while
the office looks at the real one.

### 2. The line over the chart, in the copy

It takes the walk line's and the pull line's place. Violet, so it never reads as the real schedule:

```
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ (what if)  A copy of the schedule to try moves on. Nothing here reaches the trades or the     │
│ customer. 2 moves tried. 5 bars differ from the real schedule. The job finishes Fri Dec 11,   │
│ 3 days later than the real one. The bills: $22,661 of the Oct 25 bill moves to Nov 25.         │
│                                   [Keep the 2 moves…]  [Throw it away]  [See the real schedule] │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

With nothing tried yet: "A copy of the schedule to try moves on. Nothing here reaches the trades or
the customer. Move a bar to try something." Keep is disabled.

### 3. The chart, in the copy

The copy's bars, drawn as always: the red chain, spare days, holds, pills, the green pull ghosts. The
copy's measures sit over it. Under each bar that differs, **the real schedule as a ghost**: a grey
dashed outline at its real dates, the way a drag shows its amber ghosts. Its hover card gains a row,
**Real**, "Oct 12 to Oct 21". A ghost is a picture, not a second set of bars, so the chart reads as
one schedule with the difference visible. A toggle would make the office flip and compare from
memory.

### 4. Why it moved, in the copy

The same window, titled *Try a move on …*, with *Try it* for *Save the move*. Under the reason
chips: "In the what-if, a reason is optional. Keep asks for one." *Try it* saves with or without one.

### 5. Changes to the schedule, in the copy

The copy's own moves, newest first, with Undo and Redo, titled *Tried in the what-if (2)*. A move
with no reason yet reads "No reason yet. Keep asks for one." There is no Tell the trades and no
"The trades have not been told." Nothing in the copy is for them.

### 6. Keep

```
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Keep the what-if                                                   │
│ These moves go on the real schedule, oldest first, each as a move  │
│ with its reason. The trades are told when you press Tell the trades.│
│ ┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐ │
│ │ 1  Roofing · TPO membrane moved from Sep 21 to Oct 9, to       │ │
│ │    Sep 21 to Oct 16. 2 after it moved out.                     │ │
│ │    (Weather) "Rain is forecast all next week."                 │ │
│ │ 2  HVAC · Test and balance moved from Nov 30 to Dec 4, to      │ │
│ │    Nov 30 to Dec 9. Final inspection moved out with it.        │ │
│ │    No reason yet: (Weather) (The trade before) (Crew) …        │ │
│ │    [What happened, in your words                         ]     │ │
│ └───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘ │
│ The real schedule then finishes Fri Dec 11, 3 days later.          │
│                                  [Cancel]  [Keep the 2 moves]       │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- Each move without a reason asks for one, in the same chips and box as Why it moved. Keep waits
  until every move has a reason and a sentence: "Give each move a reason and a sentence."
- **Keep the 2 moves** puts them on the real schedule as real moves. Each has its reason and words,
  by the person who kept them, today, marked *Tried in a what-if first* on its row. The real
  activities take the copy's planned dates and waits. The real schedule's own actual dates, reports
  and walks stay as they are. The copy is then gone.
- So everything that reads moves reads them. Tell the trades counts their companies. The customer's
  What changed has their sentences. Days lost lays them at their doors. The billing forecast's row
  says what they moved, and Undo works on the last one.
- **Keep is refused if the real schedule moved since the copy was made**, say someone walked it and
  moved a bar. Replaying on top would quietly overwrite a move someone else made and explained, the
  same reason Undo stops when a later move stands. The window then says "The real schedule changed
  since this copy was made: Roofing · TPO membrane moved. Throw this copy away and make a new one."
  A trade's report or an actual date does not count as a change: neither moves a planned date.

### 7. Throw it away

An inline yes: "Throw away the copy and its 2 moves? The real schedule stays as it is. [Throw it
away] [Cancel]". The copy goes and nothing else changes. One line goes in the activity log, "Robert
threw away a what-if: 2 moves tried."

## The kernel: `src/lib/gcMode/gcWhatIf.ts` (pure, tested, out of the barrel)

```ts
/** The copy made from the real schedule (`startWhatIf`). */
whatIfCopy(project: GcProject, by: string, today: string): ScheduleWhatIf | null
/** The project with the copy as its schedule: what the tab, the chart, the editor and the pull read in the copy. Null: no copy. */
whatIfProject(project: GcProject): GcProject | null
/** What changed on the real schedule since the copy was made (planned dates and waits only). Empty: Keep is safe. */
whatIfBaseChanges(project: GcProject): { lineId: string; name: string }[]
/** The copy against the real schedule, for the line over the chart and Keep. */
whatIfDiff(state: GcState, project: GcProject): {
  moves: number; noWhy: number
  bars: { lineId: string; real: Span; copy: Span }[]
  finishReal: string; finishCopy: string; finishDays: number
  billing: ForecastShift[]        // G-97's forecastShifts: the copy's dates against the real ones
  words: string[]                 // the line's sentences, each passing plainWordsFailures
}
/** The schedule with an allowed action applied to the copy, never the real one. Null: refused. */
applyInWhatIf(state, project, action, by): ScheduleWhatIf | null
/** Keep: the real schedule with the copy's standing moves on it as real moves. Or why it cannot. */
keepWhatIf(state, project, whys, by): { schedule: ProjectSchedule } | { problem: string }
```

## The actions (additions at the end of `GcAction`)

```ts
| { type: 'startWhatIf'; projectId: string; by: string }
/** A move tried in the copy: setScheduleActivity, pullScheduleEarlier, undoScheduleMove or redoScheduleMove. Any other is refused. */
| { type: 'inWhatIf'; projectId: string; action: GcAction }
| { type: 'keepWhatIf'; projectId: string; by: string; whys: Record<string, { reason: ScheduleMoveReason; note: string }> }
| { type: 'throwAwayWhatIf'; projectId: string; by: string }
```

`inWhatIf` runs the inner action through the reducer on a state whose project has the copy as its
schedule. It keeps only the schedule that comes out, as the copy. The real schedule, the log and
the rest of the state are untouched. A move with no explanation is given a stand-in so it is kept on
the copy's history, marked `noWhy`. That is one optional field on `ScheduleMove`, never on a real
move. The kept moves carry `fromWhatIf` (the day the copy was made) for their row's words. All four
actions go in the golden test's `all` list, each with `used.add(…)` naming `gcWhatIf.test.ts`. No
fixture data and no new step, so the golden test does not move.

## The tests

`gcWhatIf.test.ts`, played from the made-up data:

1. *Try a what-if* copies the schedule: same activities, an empty history, the base recorded. The
   real schedule is the same object as before.
2. A move in the copy pushes what waits on it, in the copy only. The real schedule, the log, Tell the
   trades, the customer's What changed, Follow up and Needs you read exactly as before.
3. A move with no reason is kept on the copy's history as `noWhy`. One with a reason keeps it.
4. A pull (G-37) and Undo and Redo work in the copy. Every other action type inside `inWhatIf` is
   refused, state unchanged.
5. Keep refuses while a move has no reason, and when the real schedule moved since the copy. It does
   not refuse for a trade's report or an actual date.
6. Keep puts the moves on the real schedule, oldest first: real ids, their reasons, `fromWhatIf`, the
   copy's planned dates, the real actual dates kept. Tell the trades lists their companies, the
   customer's What changed reads them, Days lost counts them, and Undo works on the last one.
7. Throw away removes the copy and leaves the real schedule as it was.
8. The line's sentences, the TPO week and Test and balance case: "2 moves tried. 5 bars differ from
   the real schedule. The job finishes Fri Dec 11, 3 days later than the real one. The bills:
   $22,661 of the Oct 25 bill moves to Nov 25." Each passes `plainWordsFailures`.

`GcWhatIf.render.test.tsx`: the toolbar's way in, a move tried with no reason (*Try it*), Keep
asking for the missing reason, throw away's yes, and the cards hidden in the copy.

## What I am leaving out, and why

- **More than one copy** (plan A beside plan B). One copy keeps the model to one question at a time.
  Two copies means naming them and comparing three schedules on one chart.
- **Keeping some of the moves.** Later moves are tried on top of earlier ones, so keeping a part
  means replaying a schedule nobody looked at. Undo in the copy takes a move out first.
- **Showing the copy to the customer or a trade** ("if we add a crew, we finish Dec 4"). The lead's
  rule for this round: nothing in the copy reaches them. Keep is the way out.
- **The copy in the walk, actuals, waits and milestones.** Those record what happened, not what we
  plan, so they belong to the real schedule.
- **A copy across reloads.** Like everything in the prototype it lives in the session store.

## Is this the best we can do?

Three ways it could be better, one for each job:

1. **See: the copy's money.** The line says what the copy does to the bills, using G-97's forecast
   on the copy's dates against the real ones. **I pick it.** A slip that holds the finish can still
   move $22,661 into a later bill, and that is often the question behind the what-if. It is one call
   to the forecast that already exists.
2. **Tell: Keep ends on Tell the trades.** After Keep, the line over the chart says "2 moves kept.
   Summit Roofing and Cool Breeze Mechanical have not been told." with **Tell the trades** right
   there. **I pick it.** A kept what-if usually moves several companies at once, and call 4 already
   sends them as one message each. It is the existing window, opened from one more place.
3. **Chase: the copy names who it would need to call.** Each bar the copy moves that belongs to a
   trade lists its company: "This would move Summit Roofing and Cool Breeze Mechanical." **Not
   now.** The Keep window already shows each move's work, and the call list (G-115) reads real moves
   once kept. A second list of people inside a copy would chase nobody until Keep.
