Helper 4, branch `spike/g140` (from `origin/spike/gc-mode` at c89929638), 2026-10-06. The mockup
and the plan come first, for the lead's go.

**The row** (`GANTT_FEATURES.md`, raised by G-97): *The cash forecast follows the bars too: The
next 6 weeks counts each bill as the schedule has it, and the trades' draws follow the same bars.*
It serves See and Tell. The owner's word, 2026-10-06: yes, knowing it moves Fair Oaks D's Oct 25
by about $270,000.

**What is there today.** **The next 6 weeks** on the Money tab is `cashAhead`. Of what is not on
the books yet it counts two things per job, both from the work reported **today**:
- **In:** the draft bill, on the day that customer usually pays.
- **Out:** each trade's next draw, for work reported and not drawn, less retainage, paid 10 days
  after the bill day.

So a bar that moves moves nothing here. On Fair Oaks D the expected bill is the draft's $98,566,
while the schedule (G-97) has $368,744 by Oct 25.

**The golden test does not read `cashAhead`.** Its readings are `stageProgress`, the board's lists,
the customers' summaries and the promises. The only readers are the Money tab, Owner Billing's
tests (`gcOwnerBilling.test.ts`) and G-97's "untouched" test. So this row re-pins nothing: it
builds through to the screen.

## 1. One rule each side, and both sides move together

- **Our bills (in):** G-97's forecast. Each month's bill, `billingForecast`, which is Owner
  Billing's own `ownerPayApp` run on the job at that day's percents, counts on the bill day plus
  that customer's usual days to pay. A job with no schedule keeps today's rule: its draft. So
  does a job whose customer has never paid, which has no day, as now.
- **The trades' draws (out):** each package's draw at each of our bill days, from the percents its
  bars reach by then (`forecastPct`, G-97's rule). It goes through **the trade's own pay
  application**, `payApplication` with `drawMoney` from `gcBuilding.ts`, the same math the
  portal's pay application uses. That is each line's percent this period past what was drawn
  before, less the trade's retainage. Each draw counts as made before the next month. It is paid
  10 days after the bill day, `PAY_WITHIN_DAYS`, as today. A package with no bars, on a job with
  no schedule, keeps today's rule.
- **Nothing else moves.** What is on the books stays as it is: bills sent, draws asked or
  approved, late money, retainage with no day.

**At reported percents it is today's rule, number for number.** With every bar held at the percent
it has reported, `cashAhead(state, { bars: 'reported' })`, the in side is each job's draft and the
out side is each trade's next draw. Through the pay application rule that is Summit Roofing
$54,000, Cool Breeze Mechanical $9,720 and Hill Country Interiors $14,688, as today. **Owner
Billing's tests that pin today's numbers keep every assertion word for word**, with one change:
their calls pass `{ bars: 'reported' }`. That is the proof the lead asked for.

## 4. The kernel

`src/lib/gcMode/gcCashForecast.ts` is new, pure and tested. `gcOwnerBillingAhead.ts` calls it in
place of its two inline blocks.

| Function | In | Out |
|---|---|---|
| `expectedBills(state, project, bars)` | a job, `'schedule'` or `'reported'` | the in moves: each forecast month's bill on its paid day, or the draft |
| `expectedDraws(state, project, bars)` | a job | the out moves: each package's draw at each bill day, through `payApplication` and `drawMoney` |
| `cashFollowsBars(state)` | the state | `{ outMore, inMoreLater, inMoreInWeeks }`: the change lines, from both runs |

In `gcOwnerBillingAhead.ts` (Owner Billing's file, changed with the owner's yes):
- `cashMoves(state, opts)` and `cashAhead(state, opts)` take `bars?: 'schedule' | 'reported'`,
  with `'schedule'` the default.
- `CashMove` gains `billOn?`, the bill day an expected move is for.
- `nextBills` becomes the first bill day's bills as the schedule has them.

In G-97's `gcBillingForecast.ts`: an exported `drawsAt` helper is shared with the trade side, so
"the percents a package's lines reach by a day" is written once.

## 6. Left out, and why

- **Pay-when-paid.** The trades are paid 10 days after the bill day whether or not the customer has
  paid, as today. A trade paid only when we are paid would change the out side's days. It is a
  term in each statement of work, and the fixture carries none.
- **Retainage growth.** The retainage the customer holds rises with each forecast bill, and ours
  on the trades with each draw. Both still show as *no day yet* at today's amounts. They come
  with the final bills.
- **Our own crew's cost.** It is not in the cash weeks today, and payroll is the Pipeline's.
