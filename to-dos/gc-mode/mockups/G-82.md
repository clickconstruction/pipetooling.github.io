# G-82: how to get days back

## The job

On a job past its contract, someone asks: *what can we do about it?* The chart shows the red chain,
the work with 5 or fewer spare days, but not which of it could come in. G-82 lists the offers, each
with the days it would give back and what that does to the contract (G-98's lines). Pressing one
opens a window that shows the move in full, and saving it is one move like any other.

Two levers, the two a superintendent reaches for:

- **Side by side**: the next trade starts before the one ahead of it finishes, in another part of the
  building. Fast-tracking.
- **A second crew**: the same work, done by two crews in fewer days. Crashing.

## Three things found in the model first

1. **There are no link kinds.** A wait is finish-to-start with a gap of days (G-35 built the gap,
   not "starts with it"). A side-by-side start is a gap below zero: *Sheet metal and flashing starts
   3 days before TPO membrane finishes* is a gap of −3. Today the floor is zero in four places:
   `lagOf`, the reducer's `setScheduleActivity`, `planMove`, and the editor's box (`min={0}`). Lifting
   it is four one-line changes, and the chart's label reads *−3* where it reads *+3* now. No fixture
   has a negative gap, so the golden test does not move.
2. **A moved bar never pulls what is behind it.** Moving a bar earlier leaves the work after it where
   it was, so no finish ever comes in. G-37 solved this for work that finished early: the work right
   behind comes in by the days given back, held work keeps its dates, never before tomorrow. G-82
   needs that same cascade from a different seed. **I propose lifting G-37's cascade into a function
   both read** (`pullBehind`, in `gcPullEarlier.ts`). It is a refactor with its 30 tests unchanged.
   The other way is a copy of about 60 lines, which I would rather not.
3. **Undo refuses a move whose links changed** (`undoMove`: the old links are not kept). A
   side-by-side move changes one gap, so the move keeps the gap it replaced (`recovery.gapWas`), and
   Undo and Redo put it back. That is one branch each in `undoMove` and `redoMove`.

## The lead's questions, answered

- **Which pairs can run side by side.** The kernel reads one wait, *B waits on A*:
  - both on the red chain;
  - A is not done, and B has not started;
  - **different trades** (two `packageId`s), and neither is an inspection or the job's own bar
    (cure time cannot overlap, and an inspection needs the work finished);
  - **the wait has no gap now**, since a gap is cure or lead time and is kept;
  - **nothing holds B**: no submittal, RFI, late wait or papers (the chart's holds, G-77 included),
    and no late notice or other day its trade asked for;
  - A has at least 2 days left to share.

  The overlap is `OVERLAP_DAYS` = 3, or fewer when A or B is shorter: B moves whole, 3 days sooner,
  and its wait on A becomes −3.
- **What a bigger crew means.** A bar has dates and a reported percent, no crew. So the rule is a
  plain one, named and on the surface: `SECOND_CREW_SHARE` = 2/3, *A second crew does the days left
  in two thirds the time.* Two thirds, not half, because two crews get in each other's way. It applies
  to a trade's line or our own crew's stage, with at least `CREW_MIN_DAYS` = 3 days left, not an
  inspection, not the job's own bar.
- **Where the list sits.** Under the measures, full width, only on a job past its contract (G-98's
  `late` > 0). The best offer's worth joins G-98's lines in the Projected finish measure.
- **What a press does.** It is an offer, like G-37's pull. *Look at it* opens a window with the bar's
  dates before and after, the side-by-side wait in words, what comes in behind it, what keeps its
  dates and why, the finish before and after, and its worth against the contract. The reason is
  preset to a new one, **Getting days back** (`ScheduleMoveReason` `recovery`), with the sentence
  filled in and changeable. *Save the move* saves one move. Undo and Redo work as on any move.
- **What it never does.** Nothing moves by itself. The list is read-only until a press. The trades hear
  only through Tell the trades: the company whose bar moved and each company pulled in.

## The kernel

`src/lib/gcMode/gcRecovery.ts`, pure, out of the barrel, with `gcRecovery.test.ts`.

```ts
export const OVERLAP_DAYS = 3
export const SECOND_CREW_SHARE = 2 / 3
export const CREW_MIN_DAYS = 3

export interface RecoveryOffer {
  key: string                       // 'side:<B>:<A>' or 'crew:<B>': what the reducer re-plans by
  how: 'side' | 'crew'
  lineId: string                    // the bar that changes
  after?: string                    // side by side: the bar it now overlaps
  from: Span; to: Span
  gapWas?: number; gap?: number     // side by side: 0 → −3
  pulls: PullLine[]                 // what comes in behind it (G-37's shape)
  stays: PullStay[]                 // what keeps its dates, and why
  finishFrom: string; finishTo: string      // the projected finish, before and after
  daysBack: number
  who: { company: string; name: string; phone: string }[]   // who has to agree
  words: { title: string; detail: string; worth: string }
  note: string                      // the move's sentence, prefilled
}
/** On a job past its contract: every offer that brings the projected finish in, most days back first. */
export function recoveryOffers(state: GcState, project: GcProject, holds: Map<string, GanttHold>): RecoveryOffer[]
/** The move one offer saves: its bar's own dates, the pulls in `pushed`, `recovery` with the gap it changed. */
export function recoveryMove(schedule: ProjectSchedule, offer: RecoveryOffer, why: MoveWhy, today: string): ScheduleMove
```

Days back are read on the **projected finish** (`projectedFinish` on the schedule as it would
stand), not the plan's last bar. So an offer that only moves the plan, while the work's pace sets the
finish, is not offered.

## The action

`recoverScheduleDays` `{ projectId, key, why }`: a new type, last in the reducer. It re-plans the
offer by its key from the state (a stale press does nothing), then saves one move: new dates, the gap,
the pulls, `ScheduleMove.recovery`. It goes in the golden test's `all` list with a
`used.add('recoverScheduleDays')` naming `gcRecovery.test.ts`. The reason `recovery` is added to
`MOVE_REASONS` (*Getting days back*), `CAUSE_OF` (its own door: *N days back from work we sped up*,
the way G-37's *Finished early* has its own line), and `CUSTOMER_WHY` (*a faster plan for the
work*): "Closeout is 1 day sooner than planned, because of a faster plan for the work."

## Left out, and why

- **What a second crew costs.** The trade's price, overtime, a change order: not modeled. The offer
  names who has to agree, and that conversation is where the money is.
- **Two offers at once.** Each stands alone, so days back do not add up. Save one and the list reads
  the job again.
- **Starts with it, finishes with it.** G-35's other link kinds: a negative gap covers the
  side-by-side case, and the rest stay unbuilt.
- **Recovery before the job is late.** The lead's word was a late job. See option 1.

## Is this the best we can do?

1. **See: offer it before the contract is missed.** When the finish has no days to spare, the same
   list reads *If anything slips, these give days back.* That turns a remedy into a margin. **Not
   picked:** the lead placed it on a late job. It is one condition to widen if the owner wants it.
2. **Chase: who has to agree, on the offer.** A second crew is the trade's to bring, and side by side
   needs both trades. The offer and the window name each company and person, with *Call Andre* and
   *Follow up* through G-115's sheet: one item, *the days back on Test and balance*, and the call's
   words logged. **Picked:** saving the move before anyone asked the trade writes a plan nobody
   agreed to. The press stays the office's, after the call.
3. **Tell: the trade being overlapped hears it too.** Tell the trades tells companies whose dates
   moved. Summit's dates do not move when Sheet metal starts beside it, but Summit should know. **Not
   picked:** it changes who `companiesToTell` reads for every move. Better as its own row.

## Built (2026-10-06): what changed from this plan

- **The refactor went first, alone** (`pullBehind`, commit c41a20700): G-37's 30 tests, its 9 render
  tests and the golden test passed unchanged, and the typecheck was clean, before anything else.
- **The cascade reads the dates as drawn**, with the compressed bar's new span as its seed, the way
  `planPull` seeds it. My first cut seeded a schedule that already had the new dates, and nothing
  behind came in.
- **The gap's words in the editor** are `sideBySideWords`, the offer's own: *starts 3 days before Sheet
  metal and flashing finishes*. The box goes down to the wait's own length, never before it starts.
  The editor's warning that a wait finishes after this starts is quiet for a wait it overlaps on
  purpose.
- **Holds** come from `chartHolds` (Helper 5's), the same map the chart draws.
- **Undo after a side-by-side move** puts back the exact activities, pinned by a test that compares
  the whole list with the job before the press.
- **A second crew is offered once per bar**, found in the live check: after saving the crew on Test
  and balance, the list offered another on the same bar. A bar with a standing second-crew move now
  gets none, and Undo brings the offer back.
