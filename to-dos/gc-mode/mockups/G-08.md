# G-08: spare days as a faint tail, on request

Helper 2, branch `spike/g08`, 2026-10-06. The row in `GANTT_FEATURES.md`: *Spare days shown per
activity, and as a faint tail after the bar on request* (See, Phase 1). It stands **Part**: the
number is in the pill ("37 spare days") and on the hover card ("Spare: 37 days before it moves the
finish"); no tail is drawn. The mockup and the plan, for the lead's go before any code. **Built 2026-10-06**
on the lead's go with the hover row; `GANTT_PLAN.md` → *Phase 1, spare days as a tail, as built*
says how it stands.

## What it is for

The pill says how many spare days a bar has, but not where they run to. A superintendent asking
"can Top out slip a week?" has to count days off the axis. A tail out to the last day the bar can
finish answers that at a glance, and the bars with tails that end on the same day read as one
chain: on Fair Oaks D, six of the fourteen tails stop at Sun Dec 6, the day before the final
inspection.

It is a request, off until pressed, because 14 tails on a chart already busy with links, holds and
notes would be noise on most days.

## Where it sits

- **The chart's toolbar, beside Hide the links**: a quiet button **Show spare days**, which reads
  **Hide spare days** while on, `aria-pressed` like its neighbor. Its title: *A faint tail after each
  bar, out to the last day it can finish before the job finishes later. The work that sets the
  finish has none.*
- **Off when the chart opens.** Held the way Hide the links is held: for as long as the chart is
  open. A reload or another tab starts it off again, as it does Hide the links.
- In both views, like Hide the links. On *List* it draws nothing; it only sets what the paper prints.

(`▁▁▏` is the tail and its end mark; the words on the right are this sketch's, not the chart's.)

- **Who gets one**: every bar that is not done and is not on the red chain. The red chain is the
  bars with 5 or fewer spare days (`TIGHT_SPARE_DAYS`, the red edge); they never get a tail, so the
  red stays the one thing that says "this sets the finish". A late bar, a held bar and an
  inspection with spare days get one: the room is still the news.
- **How far**: the bar's own spare days, `GanttBar.spare`, the read the chart already makes from the
  schedule's float. Nothing is worked out anew.
- **How it looks**: 4 px tall, the chart's pale blue (`--bg-blue-200`) at the bar's lower edge, a
  1.5 px end mark in the chart's blue, both at low opacity, behind the links and the notes. The
  notes beside a bar stay where they are: they sit at the bar's middle, the tail at its foot. A
  change order's tail and a trade's new day (G-76, G-117) are full-height outlines; the spare tail
  runs under them.
- **The legend** gains one entry while it is on: *a bar's spare days, how long it can slip before
  the job finishes later* (the words of the tab's own *Spare days are how long an activity can slip
  before the job finishes later*).

## On the paper

With the tails on, our team's copy prints them the same way: a thin pale bar at each bar's lower
edge out to its last day, the end mark, the key entry *its spare days, how long it can slip before
the job finishes later*, and one more sentence in the window and the head: *Each bar's spare days
show as a faint tail.* In the look-ahead a tail runs to the page's edge and stops there. **The
customer's copies never print one**: a customer never sees spare days.

## The rule, once

One read, used by the chart and the paper, added to `gcGantt.ts` beside the spare days it reads:

```ts
/** A bar's spare days as a tail (G-08): from the day after its finish to the last day it can finish before the job finishes later. Null on the red chain, when done, or with none. */
export function spareTail(b: GanttBar): { from: string; to: string; days: number } | null
```

## Left out, and why

- **Remembering the toggle across a reload.** Hide the links is not remembered either; both would
  change together, and that is a call for the chart, not this row.
- **A one-company filter** (G-13's other half). By company does that job.
- **The List view** gets no tails. It has no time axis to run them on; its pill already says the days.
- **The room each bar had at Start** (a second mark where its spare ran out in the plan at Start): the
  owner's call, since it needs the float worked out over the baseline's dates.
- **Spare days counted to the contract's day** instead of the plan's last day: the owner's call, since
  it changes what spare days mean on the pill, the filter, the red chain and the walk.

## Is this the best we can do?

Three ways to make it better, by the chart's jobs:

1. **See: the day on the hover card.** The tail shows where the room runs out; the hover card
   says only "37 days". A new row, *Can finish by Sun Nov 15*, under *Spare*, gives the exact day
   without counting ticks. It shows on every bar with spare days, toggle or not, the red chain
   included, where the day matters most (Trim: *Can finish by Sun Dec 6*). **Picked**: one row, an
   addition, and the question a superintendent actually asks.
2. **Chase: the room it had at Start.** A second faint mark where the spare ran out in the plan at
   Start would show which bars have been eating their room, before they turn red. **Not picked**:
   it needs the float worked out over the baseline's dates, a new read the row was told to keep out
   of; worth its own row if the owner wants it.
3. **See: count to the contract's day, not the plan's last day.** Today the spare days run to the
   day before the final inspection. When the contract's finish is later than the plan's, the job has
   more room than the tails show. **Not picked**: that changes what "spare days" means everywhere
   (the pill, the filter, the red chain, the walk), which is the owner's call, not a display choice.
