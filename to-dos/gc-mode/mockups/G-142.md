# G-142: a trade's own crew count, from its portal

Helper 4, branch `spike/g142` (from `origin/spike/gc-mode` at efc5d92ff), 2026-10-06. The mockup
and the plan come first, for the lead's go.

**The row.** It is new, the *C* set aside on G-118's list and now Helper 4's in round three. It has
no number yet, so I propose **G-142**, the next free one: no branch or commit holds it. *A trade
says from its portal how many it will have on site each coming week; the superintendent's morning
list reads it beside the daily log's count.* It serves See and Chase.

**What is there today.**
- **No trade reports a crew count.** The only count is the daily log's workers by trade.
- **The morning list (G-118)** therefore says *Last on the log Thu Oct 1 with 4.* and nothing about
  what the trade meant to send. A short crew is not news. It only shows up as a smaller number.
- **The portal's look-ahead**, *Your next three weeks* (`GcPortalLookAhead.tsx`), is where a trade
  already speaks every week. It marks each activity done or not, and it lists this week and the
  next two, each with the trade's own activities.

## 1. The trade's side: *Your next three weeks*

Under each coming week that has work, a line per trade. Here is Summit Roofing on Fair Oaks D,
today, Fri Oct 2:

```
This week · Mon Sep 28
TPO membrane                         … (the marks, as today)
People a day on site:  [ 4 ]  [ Tell Click ]

Next week · Mon Oct 5
TPO membrane · Roof curbs            …
People a day on site:  [   ]  [ Tell Click ]

Week of Mon Oct 12
Sheet metal and flashing             …
People a day on site:  [   ]  [ Tell Click ]
```

**After sending:** `(You said 4 a day.)  [ Change it ]`. A trade that sends nobody for a week
says 0: `(You said nobody this week.)  [ Change it ]`.

- **Which weeks.** This week and the next two: the look-ahead's own weeks. Last week's block, kept
  while marks are owed, takes no count, because the log has that week.
- **Per trade.** A company with two trades on one job gives a count for each, the way the daily
  log counts them. The trade's name goes before the line only then: *Roofing: people a day on
  site*.
- **The rule.** A whole number from 0 to 50. Anything else leaves *Tell Click* off, and the line
  says *A whole number, 0 to 50.*
- **In Spanish** (G-119), *Personas al día en la obra*, *Avisar a Click*, *Dijo 4 al día.* and
  *Dijo que nadie esta semana.* `PORTAL_SPANISH.md` is redrawn by its script.

so nothing G-118 pins moves.

## 3. One record and one action

```ts
/** A trade's own word on how many a day it will have on site in a week (G-142). Kept newest first; the newest for a trade and week is the one that counts. */
export interface CrewCount {
  packageId: string
  partnerId: string
  /** The Monday of the week it is for. */
  weekOf: string
  /** About how many people a day. 0: nobody that week. */
  count: number
  /** The day they said it. */
  on: string
}

GcProject.crewCounts?: CrewCount[]          // unset until the first one: the golden state does not move

| { type: 'tradeSetCrewCount'; projectId: string; partnerId: string; packageId: string; weekOf: string; count: number }
```

The reducer case goes at the end. It refuses:
- a job not being built;
- a trade the company was not awarded;
- a week outside this week and the next two, or not a Monday;
- a count that is not a whole number from 0 to 50;
- a count the same as the newest for that trade and week.

It logs *Summit Roofing says 4 a day on Roofing at Fair Oaks Shops, Building D, the week of Sep 28.*

## Is this the best we can do?

1. **Chase: ask for it.** A to-do on the trade's home from Friday on, for next week's count when
   it has work and has not said: *Tell Click how many people a day you will have on site next week
   at Fair Oaks Shops, Building D.*
   **Not now.** It adds a to-do for trades on the made-up data today, which is a Friday. That
   changes the pinned to-dos in other lanes' portal tests. That is a changed sentence, the lead's
   call. If you want it, it is one loop in `portalTodos` and those tests' new lines.
2. **See: a cut is news before the day.** When a trade lowers its count mid-week, the morning list
   says so: *They said 3 a day this week, down from 4 on Wed Sep 30.* A crew pulled to another job
   is the call to make before anyone is short on site.
   **Picked.** The record keeps every count anyway, and it is one clause in `crewLogWords`.
3. **Tell: the office records what a trade says on a call.** A field on the morning list, *They
   told me: [ n ]*, would write the same record, marked as from the office. Trades often say it on
   the phone.
   **Not now.** It is a second action and a second door to the same number. Worth the lead's word
   first.
