# G-13: one company's work on the chart

## What it is, and what it is not

- **What it is:** a sixth control in the chart's *Show only* row, *One company*. Pick a company and
  the chart shows only its activities. The five pills then count that company's work, so the row
  reads as their summary: how much of theirs is tight, late, held, coming up or moved.
- **Why By company is not enough:** By company groups every company and folds none of them. On
  the phone with one trade, the office still scrolls past every other group to find theirs. The
  pills keep counting the whole job, so *Late or behind 6* does not say how many are theirs. A
  printed page for that call carries everyone.
- **What it is not:** not a new view, and not a change to what a customer or a trade sees. The
  customer's copy ignores the filters today and still does. The export is always the whole schedule
  (G-136) and still is. The trade's portal shows its own bars already (`portalSchedule.ts`).

## The screen

The *Show only* row today, on Fair Oaks D, a job being built. These numbers are only for the
picture; the build reads them from the fixture.

```
Show only  (5 or fewer spare days 3) (Late or behind 6) (Held 2) (Next 3 weeks 9) (Moved since Start 4)  [Every company ▾]
```

The new control is the last one in the row: a pill-shaped picker, quiet while it says
*Every company*. Its list is the job's companies in By company's order, the most late work first:

```
Every company
Pecan Valley Electric · 2 late
Iron Horse Fabrication · 1 late
Summit Roofing
The city
…
```

A company with nothing late shows its name alone. *The city* is the inspections, as By company names
them.

With Pecan Valley Electric picked, the picker turns blue like a pill that is on. The pills count
their work, and the line after them says what is shown:

```
Show only  (5 or fewer spare days 1) (Late or behind 2) (Held 1) (Next 3 weeks 3) (Moved since Start 0)  [Pecan Valley Electric ▾]
           Showing 5 of 38 · Show all
```

- The pills still combine with it. *Late or behind* plus Pecan Valley Electric shows their 2 late
  activities.
- A pill whose count is 0 for that company is dimmed and cannot be pressed, as a 0 is today.
- *Show all* clears the company and the pills together. Picking *Every company* clears only the
  company.
- **The picker's title** (its hover words): *Show one company's work, and count only theirs.*
- **Grouped by company**, picking one leaves one group, open. The call list under By company does
  not change. It lists people, not bars.
- **The links**: a link shows only when both its ends are shown, as with the pills today. A company's
  bar that waits on another company's work shows no line to it.
- **Printing** (G-21, *Our team's copy*): the page's words gain a sentence after the count,
  *Only Pecan Valley Electric's work shows.* The customer's copy is unchanged. It still says *The
  filters and folds do not change it.*
- **A phone**: the row wraps as it does today. The picker is a native select, so the phone's own
  list opens.

## Where it sits

The Schedule tab's chart, in Chart and in List, on a job being built and on a first draft. The
same row the five pills are in (`GcGantt.tsx`, *Show only*). The tour's stop *The chart*
(`gcTour.ts`, anchored on `gc-gantt-toolbar`) already says *The pills count what is late, held or
tight, and filter the chart.* One sentence goes after it: *Pick one company to see only their
work.* If a test pins that body word for word, the sentence is left out and said here.

## The kernel (on main, `src/lib/gc/schedule/gantt.ts`)

`gantt.ts` lives on main since the schedule's PR 1a, so the kernel change is a main PR. The spike
reads it after it merges, and the control is drawn on the spike's chart. PR 7 lifts that chart to
main word for word, control and all.

```ts
export interface GanttFilters {
  critical: boolean
  late: boolean
  held: boolean
  soon: boolean
  moved: boolean
  /** One company's work only (G-13), by the name By company groups it under. Absent: every company. */
  company?: string
}

/** The five pills: every filter but the company. */
export type GanttToggle = Exclude<keyof GanttFilters, 'company'>

/** The companies on the chart for the picker, in By company's order: the most late work first. */
export function ganttCompanies(bars: GanttBar[]): { company: string; bars: number; late: number }[]

/** How many bars each pill would show, within one company when one is picked. */
export function ganttCounts(bars: GanttBar[], company?: string): Record<GanttToggle, number>

/** The bars that pass every filter turned on, the company first. None on: all of them. */
export function ganttFilter(bars: GanttBar[], filters: GanttFilters): GanttBar[]
```

- `company` is optional, so `NO_FILTERS` does not change, nor does any test that builds filters
  from it. A company picked that has no bars left shows none, which is the honest answer. The picker
  never offers one, since it lists only companies with bars.
- `FILTER_TEST` becomes `Record<GanttToggle, …>`. `ganttCompanies` reads `ganttGroups(bars,
  'company')`, so the order and the late count are By company's own and are never worked out twice.
- `ganttPrint.ts`: `showsWords` reads the five toggles only for *Filters on* and adds the company's
  sentence. `filterNames` becomes `Record<GanttToggle, string>`. Nothing else changes.

## The action

None. A filter is the person's view of the chart, held while the chart is open, as the pills are. No
reducer action, no table, no migration.

## Defaults the owner has not confirmed

- The pills count within the picked company. The other reading, pills always counting the whole job,
  hides the answer to "how much of theirs is late".

  as a company.

## What I am leaving out, and why

- **More than one company at once.** Not asked, and By company does a comparison better.
- **Remembering the company** after the chart closes or in the page's address. The pills are not
  remembered either. See option 2.
- **The call list narrowing to the company.** It lists people and their reasons, not bars, and
  G-115 owns it.

## Is this the best we can do?

Thought through for the chart's three jobs: See, Tell, Chase.

1. **From the call list to their bars (Chase).** Each person in By company's call list gets a quiet
   *Their work* link that picks their company in the chart. On the phone with Marcus, one press shows
   what he owns. *Picked*: it is one link on rows that exist, it serves the reason the row was
   asked for, and it needs nothing new in the kernel.
2. **A link that opens the chart on one company (Tell).** For example, the Company window's
   *Their schedule* opens the job's chart filtered to them. *Not picked now*: the chart has no
   address of its own yet on main, so it waits for PR 7.
3. **What they wait on, faded (See).** With a company picked, the bars theirs wait on and the bars
   waiting on theirs show faded, with their links. One trade's work alone hides why it cannot start.
   *Not picked*: it changes what a filter means everywhere else, and the trade's portal already
   says who comes before and after them.
