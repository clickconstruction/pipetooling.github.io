The address on file

A customer whose record carries this address (customers.contact_info.email), or a person at one (customer_contact_persons.email). The press names the sender as that person.

A bid or a job that names them

A bid whose contact is this address or this name (bids.gc_contact_email, gc_contact_name), a job whose customer email is this address (jobs_ledger.customer_email). The press names them as the bid's customer and ties the thread to the bid, so it leaves no job.

The company's address

A customer whose address shares the sender's own domain (never gmail and the like, companyDomain). The press names the sender as a new person at that customer; the contact keeps the sender's name, the way Who is this? keeps a name today.

The name, and the number

A customer or a person at one whose name holds the sender's name, from the email's From line or this is Dale in a text; a customer with this number on file, as today.
