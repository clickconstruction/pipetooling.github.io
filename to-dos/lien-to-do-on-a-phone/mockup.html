The Lien desk's To do on a phone: before and after

This is the screen The Board › opens from the Dashboard. Every job, name, count, date and amount here is made up. Nothing is copied from the live app.

What it does well. It opens cleanly: four tiles, the bins folded away, and one list by the day each paper is due, with the money on every day.

What I would change. The rows. Give every row one press at the right, one size, as you asked for on Needs you. A computer's rows already work that way. Put the second way out under the words. Say No day set once.

What you pick. Whether to build the rows this way, and whether three long presses may take shorter words.

After recommended

  • 1The press is a different width on every row. A phone sizes each press to its words, from 69 px for Note it to 241 px for Answer the homestead question. The eye has to find it again on each row.
  • 2The press has a line to itself. It sits under the words with the paper's chip and its dots. That line is why a row is 111 to 171 px tall.
  • 3On a row with two presses, the main one jumps to the left. Not lienable… takes the right side, where the main press is on every other row. The main press drops to a line of its own at the left.
  • 4Row presses are 40 px tall. The app's own rule for a phone is 44.
  • 5No day set is a heading twice. Once in the middle of the days and once at the end. The list is two pools drawn one after the other, and each pool ends with its rows that have no day.
  • 6The first tile's words leave one word alone on a line. And its Show them › is at the foot of the tile while the next tile's is in the top corner.
  • 7The same row many times. Every filed lien is its own row with the same words and the same press. This is the owner's run, punch list #362, and is not redrawn here.
