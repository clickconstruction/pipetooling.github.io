The flow of a lien paper, on a phone: out of it and back in

Every job, name, count, date and amount here is made up. Nothing is copied from the live app.

Why it is hard. The flow is a wide picture, two columns of eight bins with an arrow across. A phone is one narrow column. Today the bins stack into about 2,050 px, and the work a bin leads to is far below it.

What I recommend. Draw the flow as counts, not chips, so both columns fit on one screen. Give it a switch of its own beside the list by day. When you press a stage, the flow shrinks to one line pinned at the top. That line says where you are and takes you back in one press.

What you pick. This, or the strip drawn at the end, and whether To do opens on the flow or on the list by day.

Out of the flow, and back in

Three screens in the order a person meets them. The pinned line is the flow in small: eight squares for the eight stages of that column, with their counts, and the one you are in lit.

  • 1The flow is counts. Each stage is one press, 44 px tall, with its name and its count. A job's number is no use as a 22 px chip on a phone, since the card that names the job needs a pointer. The numbers come back as rows, one press later.
  • 2Out. Press a stage and its papers are the screen. The flow becomes the pinned line. Press another square on that line to move to the stage before or after without going back.
  • 3Further out. Press a row and the job has the screen, as it does today. The pinned line stays, in its short form, and names the stage you came from.
  • ↩Back in. ‹ The flow on the pinned line, or the phone's own Back, returns one step at a time. The flow opens where you left it, with your stage still lit.

The other idea: the flow as a strip over the list

The list by day stays the screen. The flow is a row of stages pinned above it that slides sideways. Press a stage and the list shows only its papers. Press it again and the whole list is back. You never leave, so there is nothing to come back to. It gives up the picture: about three stages show at a time, and the two columns and the arrow between them are gone.

2 the strip · dark

A third idea, the flow as a tab of its own beside To do, was not drawn. Five tabs do not fit a phone's width at a size a thumb can press.
