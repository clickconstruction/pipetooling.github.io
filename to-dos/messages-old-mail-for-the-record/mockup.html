Old mail lands for the record, and a thread can be closed in plain sight

A batch of old email was tagged onto jobs so anyone could catch up. Messages read each old mail as a message nobody had answered. Five spots below, each as it is now beside my suggestion.

My recommendation
  1. A mail more than 14 days old when it lands is filed for the record. It shows in the thread and on the job. It opens nothing and is nobody's move.
  2. The age decides. A person overrules in one press. There is no tick before a tag.
  3. No reply needed sits in plain sight on the thread, on New's card and on the Sort desk. The thread shows its state.
  4. A fold lists what was done with no reply this week, each row with Reopen. No seventh lane.
  5. Keep the words the app has: Done for the state, No reply needed for the press. Do not add Closed.

The threads there now

Four threads from the batch are open and stale. They are the top rows of New. Open each one, press ⋯, press No reply needed. That is eight presses and no code. Two more from the batch hold mail from the last week. Those are live.

What I would not build

  • A seventh lane. A lane with a count reads as work to do.
  • A third word such as Closed, beside Done and No reply needed.
  • A tick before every tag. Most tags are today's mail.
  • A sweep that closes old open threads by age. Seven open threads are 15 to 30 days old and real.
  • A press that closes many rows at once on New. It would close those seven.
  • A script or a seat verb for four rows.
