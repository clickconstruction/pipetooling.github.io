What moves

  1. The pay code moves into the box with the amount and the day. A letter with two or more bills keeps its codes on page 2, above the bills.
  2. The statement of account, the notice history and the enclosures move to page 2.
  3. The signature drops the phone and the email. The letterhead at the top already shows both.
  4. One phrase changes. “for the work below” becomes “for the work on the statement of account behind this letter”. No other word changes.
