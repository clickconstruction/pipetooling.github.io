Room for a crowded end on the phone's Where everyone stands band

The Bid Board's Health section hangs one name tag per person under the band, on two rows. On a phone the band is 343 px wide (a 375 screen) or 288 px (320). When four names fall in one quarter of it, two rows cannot hold their tags: a tag lands on its neighbour and hides the name. v2.5980 kept every tag inside the band; the crowded end is what is left. My recommendation is A, a third row: every name stays whole, the band grows about 24 px only on a phone that needs it, and desktop does not change.

Made-up names and rates.
