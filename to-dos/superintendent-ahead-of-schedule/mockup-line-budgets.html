Line budgets: before and after

Every line the customer sees carries, inside, what we think it will cost us: labor as hours and dollars at the cost rate, materials as dollars. Nobody types it: the workbench's number rides along when the line was priced there, the company's split stands in when the line was typed, and the owner tunes the guesses in a pass he makes when he wants. One control runs through all of it: two sliders, labor and materials, with the margin as what is left. The sheet and the Settings card below are live; drag them. Made-up names and figures; nothing here reaches a customer's paper.

The one setting. Two sliders per stage kind and a rate. The materials slider can only reach what labor left, so the two never sum past 100; the margin turns red under the target. Each row reads itself out in dollars and hours on a line of the size you pick. The learning line sits on the row it belongs to, with one press to take it. Save names how many rows changed; Back to what you saved is the undo. The cost rate here is the one the tuning sheet's hours use.

The rule

  • Nobody types a budget. A line priced on the workbench keeps the hours and parts it was priced from; a typed line gets the company split, marked assumed; only the owner's drag writes ✎.
  • The job's budget is the sum of its lines. At award the set is frozen as the snapshot; a change order adds lines with their own budgets; Refresh writes a dated sibling.
  • Internal roles see the quiet line; the portal, the proposal, the pay app and the GC packet never draw it. The lens's What customers see check proves it.

What is new

  • Per line: labor_hours, labor_cost, materials_cost, budget_source (workbench · assumed · tuned), tuned_by, tuned_at on the Stage Plan row, or a job_line_budgets table beside it. One additive migration.
  • The quiet line and the Tune door on ① Line Items and the bid's Pricing tab; the tuning sheet with the two sliders, the brush and Undo; the Needs you review card; the Settings card with the sliders and the rate; the learning line.

Decisions (yours)

  • Which rate? The bid's cost rate per line where it has one; else the Settings rate, burdened.
  • Who sees dollars? Drawn: the office and the estimator; the superintendent reads hours per stage and no money.
  • Does the learning line move the default by itself after N tunes, or only with your press? Drawn: your press.
