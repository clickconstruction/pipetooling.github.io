How it is scored
Baseline: the award snapshot's hours, plus change orders. Refresh from bid never moves the score.
Percent: the pay app the GC certified. The crew's own percent is provisional and shown grey.
Hours: field clock hours on the job. Sub labor sheets have no clock hours, so a sub-performed stage [counts on the sub's hours | is left out].
Days: the planned finish from the rough schedule against the finish at the progress pace so far.
A commercial job with no baseline is not scored and says so. Residential and service jobs are never scored.
Every certified job feeds Book vs jobs, so the next baseline is sharper.
