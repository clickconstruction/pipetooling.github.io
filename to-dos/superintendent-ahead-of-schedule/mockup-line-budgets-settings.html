What changed from the first draw. Four pairs of boxes became two sliders per stage kind, labor and materials, with the margin read out under them instead of implied. The materials slider can only reach what labor left, so the two never sum past 100. A dashed mark on the read-out bar is the company's target margin, and the margin turns red under it. Each row reads itself out in dollars and hours on a line of the size you pick. The learning line sits on the row it belongs to, with one press to take it. Save names how many rows changed; Back to what you saved is the undo.

The rules the bar keeps

  • Labor and materials never cross and never sum past 100; the margin is the remainder and can reach zero, drawn red when under the target.
  • Typing a number moves the thumb; dragging the thumb writes the number. One source.
  • The example line is only a reading; nothing on a job changes until Save, and Save changes only lines still assumed, never a workbench line or one you tuned.

What is new against the first draw

  • The dual-thumb bar per stage kind, the target-margin mark, the dollars-and-hours read-out on a chosen line size, the learning line as a press on its row, the roster's rate as a press.
  • Same columns underneath: a split per kind and one cost rate.
