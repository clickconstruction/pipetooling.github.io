#234 PR 1: a number at award, every time, with its source

When a project is awarded, the app writes one hours baseline, frozen, and says where it came from. Three sources, tried in order: the bid's Labor rows, the labor book's assumed hours, a typed figure. The office sees the pick and can change it in the same window. Afterward the Costs tab, the Pipeline's burning card and the superintendent's line all read that one row and its source. Made-up names and figures. Project here is #238's kind: a service call never sees any of this.

The deck line catches the projects awarded before this shipped, newest first, with the two numbers side by side and a one-line read of which is better. The row is today's job_budgets with three more columns: the source, the award flag, the freeze stamp.

The rule

  • A project leaves award with exactly one baseline row marked at_award. Sources in order: the bid's Labor rows when usable (the Labor head's bar already says usable or not, v2.3598), else the book's assumed hours, else typed.
  • The office can pick a different source in the award window; the press names the number and the source.
  • Refresh from bid writes a dated sibling; the award row never changes. Change orders add to it.
  • A book baseline draws the line grey and is skipped by the score; a bid or typed baseline scores.

What is new

  • Three columns on job_budgets: source_ref, at_award, frozen_by (and kind gains book). One additive migration.
  • The award window's block, the strip's four states, the Quickfill deck line, the gate on #238's jobKind.

Decisions (yours)

  • Does a book baseline score, or only bid and typed? Drawn here: only bid and typed.
  • Can Not now leave award with nothing? Drawn here: no, the book's number is written and marked assumed.
  • Who may type a baseline? Recommended: the office set and the estimator on his own bid.
