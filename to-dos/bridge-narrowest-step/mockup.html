The question this is really asking

The owner said what he wants the Bridge for, on 2026-11-11:

"the goal of my recent changes were to get the Bridge more useful so I can narrow in on the scaling function as it is the limiting factor"

He has not said what the scaling function is. My reading: a company moves only as many dollars a week as its narrowest step. To grow, widen that step. Widening any other step only makes a pile. If that is what he means, the suggestion is the page saying it. If he means something else, this drawing is the place to say so.

  • A pile is not a jam. Eight weeks of sold work in front of the field is a backlog. It is a jam only if it is growing.
  • Market and Sales can never be lit today. They hold no pile, so the weeks rule skips them. When too little is sold, the page cannot say so in red.
  • This only became possible this week. Until punch list #318, Market read every dollar that came in and was far the widest step. Now all five are on one scale, so the narrowest one means something.

What could be wrong with it

  • A pace is what happened, not what a step could do. Build built $44k a week because it had the work. Starve it and it will read narrow too. That is why each row also says which way its pile is going: a narrow step with a shrinking pile is starved, not slow.
  • Two steps can be close. Here Sales and Collect are $1k apart. The tile names both, one for growth and one for cash, and the page keeps its rule of a close second.
  • The week's call changes with it. The card would propose Sales' moves: call the askers past their usual answer, mark the old bids. In a week cash is under the floor, cash still comes first, as today.
  • Friday's question and the weekly email follow the rule. Both read the same kernels, so they would light the same step.
