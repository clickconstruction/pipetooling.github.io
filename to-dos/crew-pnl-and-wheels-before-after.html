Wheels PR 3 Employees are bare wage on Crew P&L; subs are market price

Since v2.2735 Review prices each person's vehicle deal per field hour — last 30 days that was Fuel & gas $3,215 + Company trucks $3,623 on $185,659 gross, about $6.80 per field hour across ~1,000 field hours. Crew P&L never got it: an employee's Labor Cost is wage alone, while a sub's is what they invoiced, truck included. Comparing a company driver's $/hr to a sub's $/hr on the same table is comparing two different things.

The $50 default A literal in the code, typed once, never revisited

DEFAULT_SUB_LABOR_EQUIVALENT_RATE = 50 is what an employee hour is worth when Crew P&L compares it to a sub's market hour. It was typed on 2026-08-02. The field crew's real loaded hour is knowable from the same period Review already computes; the box should show it and let the owner take it, not guess.
