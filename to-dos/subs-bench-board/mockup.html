Subs: the board by trade, built for the moment a set of plans lands

The owner's pick, 2026-10-23, in his words:

I like all the features of A and C, but B makes the most sense for me, just because we are looking for specific kinds of subs at any given time. And often we will get a set of plans and then have the need to contact that particular sub as swiftly as we can. Would you be able to help me? Refine B.

So the shape is B, one column a trade, and the reason is the moment of use: plans land, the office knows which trades the job needs, and it wants the right subs of each trade on the phone at once. The refined B below starts from that moment. A's strip and path and C's record and priority are folded into it: the stages in sight on the board, the press on each card that moves a company, the record and the office's priority as the rank's reason and the column's default order, and the hand rank by drag over it, as Hiring's #1 is.

B The board with nothing picked: every trade, the panel's order the pick

With no project picked the board is Hiring's: one column a trade the bench buys out, in the panel's order (short of quotes, then short on the bench, then the book's trades nobody does). Each column's head carries the bench's need and Call the top 3 →. Cards are ranked by the column's order, with a drag pinning one by hand; each card's chips say why it sits there. ? Decisions for the owner

  1. The For picker. Drawn: the GC projects at bidding, whose trades are their packages; or trades ticked by hand. A Trades-mode bid carries no divisions the app knows, so it is not offered.
  2. Call the top N. Drawn as three. It could follow the ask: the quotes still wanted plus one.
  3. When one does not answer. Drawn: the call is filed, a callback is set for 3 pm today, the run moves on, and a one-press text is offered only when they said we may text. The hour and the text's words are yours.
  4. The ask on landing. Drawn: To the bench ✓ during a run ticks Ask them to quote this job now, the ask Trade partners makes. The invitation email goes out as asks do today (a dev sends it, or the ask carries the note).
  5. Where a call is logged. Drawn: only on the deck (Call now → on a card is a run of one). The other way is Didn't answer · Answered on the card too, with no lock and no clock.
  6. The rank. Drawn: the column's default order is Call first, then the record, then never called, then coldest, and a drag pins a card over it. The other way is Hiring's, every card hand-ranked from day one.
  7. Priority's steps and 🔥 warmth. Drawn: Call first · Normal · Later, and warmth retires on Subs.
  8. Where the Board sits. Drawn as a sub-tab beside Follow Up, with the Prospect List kept. The other way is the Board in the list's place on Subs.
  9. PR 1 first. Drawn: A's cut (the strip, the list by trade, the picker, the press per row) ships first, no migration; the columns, the run, the drag and the ask tick follow.
