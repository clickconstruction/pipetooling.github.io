What changes where. A new board under Subs, Hiring's components reshaped (a 300 px column, a sortable card, the drag kernel's dense ranks). The ranks need a home: a sub holds one row and can name two trades, so the rank is per trade, a new table prospect_trade_ranks (prospect, trade, rank) with a migration. The four presses write the deck's own comments, so Activity counts the calls; nothing on the board takes the calling lock or runs the timer, so 14m on calls stays the deck's. Call now on the list and the deck's queue orders stay as they are; the rank becomes a fourth order, By rank.

Is this the best we can do? Hiring ranks ten people a role by hand because each hire is a judgment of a person. Here it is seven companies a trade and the judgment is do they answer, will they quote, which the calls themselves say (C). The deck is the calling machine: the lock so two callers never dial the same company, the clock, the callbacks, the script. A board beside it is a second view of one queue, and a caller working 104 numbers does not drag cards. The board's two real gains, the trade in view and a press on every row, A gives the list without a table or a drag. So B is drawn for the owner's eye, and held.

C Who answers, who first: the record and the priority, on the row and the card the pick, second

On top of A, two more things a row and the card say. Answers is what the app already knows about a company, in the bench's own words: answers · hit or miss · often silent · new to us, with the facts under it (answered 2 of 3 calls, replied by text Wed, asked us to call back Fri). Priority is the office's word, three steps: Call first · Normal · Later, set on the card or the row. The deck gains the order By priority: Call first, then the biggest gap on the bench, then who answers, then never called, then coldest. The record follows the company onto the bench, where it stands in for new to us until the first ask.

What the record reads. Calls and answers from the lane's own call log (every Didn't Answer and Answered the deck filed), a callback they asked for (Set callback), a text or email back on the thread Messages ties to the prospect, and the day of the last touch. The words are the bench's (answers at three of four, hit or miss under that, often silent under two of five, new to us under two calls), so a company reads the same on both pages. Computed on load, never stored, so it can never go stale.

What changes where. One column, prospects.priority, a migration. The deck's order toggle gains By priority and By need folds it in. The card's head wears the record chip and the three-step picker. Call now and the outcome presses are unchanged. Activity gains nothing per user; the record is per company. ? Decisions for the owner

  1. The stage names. Drawn: To call · Called · Talked · On the bench · Quoted. The last could read Working with us and count awards instead of quotes.
  2. Where the strip sits. Drawn on the Prospect List and over Follow Up's panel both. The list alone saves Follow Up 57 px.
  3. The list's groups. Drawn by trade, in the panel's order, each group headed by the bench's line. The other way is by stage, Hiring's, with the trade as a chip on the row.
  4. Priority's steps. Drawn as three words, Call first · Normal · Later. One star on or off is simpler and loses Later.
  5. The old 🔥 warmth. It climbs by one on every Answered and is hand-set in Edit prospect. With C it should retire on Subs, or on every lane.
  6. A way back. No screen takes a company off the bench today. A company declined on Trade partners could get Send back to Prospects, the door a lost GC project already has, so the two lists never hold one company by accident.
  7. B at all. Drawn and held. Say the word if the drag is wanted, and it is one PR after A and C, with its migration.
