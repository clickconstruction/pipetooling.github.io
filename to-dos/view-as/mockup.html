Gear menu → View as… · to-do mock-up · 2026-09-18

View as: see the app as a role or a person

The owner's ask: "as a dev I want to be able to click on a button and view things as a certain class of user or a certain user." Imitate already mints a real session as another person. This adds the door, a sample account per role, and a landing that keeps the page you were on.

1 · The door: gear menu → View as…

Dev only. Sits with Punch list and Release notes, from any page. The two Imitate buttons on People and the person desk stay where they are.

2 · The panel: roles on the left, people on the right

Each role is backed by a sample account — a real user, hidden from every roster — and shows that account's switches as chips so you know what you are about to see. Picking a role imitates its sample; picking a person imitates them (never a dev). Either way you land on this page.

You land on /prospects?tab=team as that account, or wherever the app sends that role instead. Your own session is stashed; Exit brings you back here.

Why a real account and not a role switch in the client: the rows come from row security on the signed-in user. A pretend role would still fetch as you and show a helper the Office Manager column they cannot actually see.

3 · Landed: the same page, the amber header names who you are, Exit goes back

The chrome turns amber as it does for Imitate today. The Exit control says who you are viewing as and where Exit returns you — the page you left, not the dashboard.

What the sample can do: anything its role and switches allow — Talked today, a note, Advance. Its writes carry its own name. Training mode (read_only) is there if a look-only sample is wanted.

4 · Settings → Active accounts: a Sample accounts heading

Like Digital twins: their own heading, switches set here like anyone's, and a Create the missing samples button for a dev that calls create-user once per role without one. Samples never appear on People → Users, the crew pickers, the Person rail, the review deck or in notifications.
