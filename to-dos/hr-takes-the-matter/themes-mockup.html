Themes on the record

A theme is a plain-words name for a pattern the office wants to find again: Dishonesty, Disrespectful conduct, Interference with the business. A theme is marked on things that already exist on the record, an HR entry, a piece of evidence, a matter's document or event, and the review reads by theme instead of by date. Names and events below are made up.

1 · The company's themes

Settings → People → Themes, a dev-only card. A theme has a name the office says, one line on what counts, and optional words for counsel. A theme is retired, never deleted, so old marks keep their meaning.

4 · By theme: the investigation

A view beside Timeline, Evidence and Narrative, and the place a large situation is worked. Pick one theme and the page holds only its items, oldest first, each with how it proves the theme. The pick sits in the address (?tab=hr&person=…&theme=money-without-say-so), so a link opens the same isolated view. Press any item and it opens in a window over the list: the whole entry or memo, its files, every theme on it, who marked it and why, and Previous and Next through the theme. Close and you are where you were.

5 · Where the firm sees it

On a matter, the firm's portal reads the themes on the Evidence tab and the Timeline, grouped and counted, with counsel's words beside the office's. The firm can add its own words to a theme; it cannot mark or unmark.
