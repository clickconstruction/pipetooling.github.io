Not built, by design

  • A % on a repeating card. The tick stays; a ring on Grease the skid steer every Sunday would mean nothing.
  • A photo kept only in the app. Drive holds it; the app keeps the row and the thumbnail until the sweep has filed it, as every other photo.
  • The email to anyone outside the company, or about people a non-office role cannot see.
  • Two numbers on one card. Lines make the %, or the hand makes it, never both.
  • A reason typed on an office card. A card that does not ask for a photo closes on its last tick or its Done with no question.
  • A 0 chip. An untouched card is an empty ring; Reopen on the thread is the way back.

The second look, 2026-11-06

Taunya asked whether this is the best we can do. Five things changed from the first draft:

  • Done stayed two taps, never three. The first sheet had 100 as a chip beside 25, 50 and 75, so finishing a plain card was ring, chip, Set. Now the sheet carries one green Done under the chips.
  • The photo ask became the card's own switch. Requiring a photo or a reason on every finish would have asked an office card like Check the AAA policy to explain itself. The switch turns on by itself when lines are written, since lines mean work someone can see.
  • A % got words. What is left? on the sheet, one optional line, so 75 means something on the thread and in the email.
  • The office's Dashboard got the people. Goal 1 can be read two ways; stop 3b draws the second reading for Taunya to keep or drop.
  • The email leads with what matters. A count line up top and a Stuck group per person, Review's own age rule, so the Monday read starts with who needs a nudge.
