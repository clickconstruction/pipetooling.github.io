A job's kind: service call or project

One kind per job, read from the job's own facts by one kernel (a bid or a GC project behind it, Stage Plan rows, an HCP number) and settled by one press when the facts are thin. Shown as a chip, filtered on the board, backfilled on Quickfill. Made-up names and figures.

1 · The New Job form: one press, defaulted

New Job✕
Defaulted from the signals. A job made from a bid reads Project, from bid B917; one made from an HCP import reads Service call, from HCP 9057; one with neither shows no default and the control waits for the press. The explicit press wins over the signals from then on.
New Job✕
A T&M commercial job is the case the signals cannot settle. The control says why and what each kind means in one sentence each.

The kernel

  • jobKind(job): the explicit kind if set; else bid_id or project_id → project; else Stage Plan rows → project; else an HCP number alone → service call; else not set.
  • One source. #234's baseline, #237's checklists, the Pipeline's board, pay apps and the reports each read it; none re-derives it from a column of its own.

What is new

  • jobs_ledger.kind, nullable, service · project. One additive migration.
  • The form's control with its default and reason; the chip and the board filter; the deck line; the guide and the Glossary's Job kind.

Later, not here

  • A warranty call on a finished project links back to its project: a parent link, not a kind.
  • Reports that split service revenue from construction revenue read the kind instead of the HCP-number heuristic: each its own PR.
