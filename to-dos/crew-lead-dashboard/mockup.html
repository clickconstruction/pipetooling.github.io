What moves
One hero card and one press, and both change four times a day. Asks are held to the hours he can answer them: the report at wrap up, everything else off the clock.
Behind a door
Whatever is not this hour's. More opens the other three faces and the full page.
Removed
The report as a separate window. It is the page itself for the last hour of the day.
What it risks
The page moves under him, so a press is not where it was this morning. His clock is written for him, so the hour is guessed from the schedule and it will guess wrong on a short day.
Cost: large. A day rule for a person who never presses the clock, asks held by hour, the report built into the page. Eight PRs or more, and the report form is its own design.
Is this the best we can do?

Build B, starting with A's cuts, and borrow one rule from C

A is worth doing whatever is picked, and its PRs are B's first PRs. B is the only shape that answers all three questions: the tools sit together under the job's name, the story is three sentences he can take in, and every ask is on one list with one count. C is the better phone in the end, but it moves the page under a man who barely presses it today. Take C's one safe rule now: the bar's single press follows his day.

Decide by the day, not by the person. A day with one job gets this page. A day with three stops keeps On my way and the route it has now.
