Weak spot in AWhy it mattersWhat B does instead
1 · Fix the race and stopA race fixed in the loader can come back from the next loader. The screen itself still trusts whatever it is handed.Two guards. The loader keeps a request counter and drops a late answer. The deck never draws a card whose kind is not the lane's, and says Loading Subs… while the first load runs. A leak then shows as a quiet line, never as another lane's card.
2 · Escape does nothing#5774 makes the window fit. An untouched window still needs a press on Cancel to leave; the house rule since v2.5530 is that Escape and the backdrop close a window while nothing changed.New Prospect and Edit prospect close on Escape and the backdrop until a field changes. Then they ask, as the big windows do.
3 · A second form under the listThe row stays in the list while its copy sits in the form at the foot. The caller reads the same job twice and the box grows past the card on a phone.Edit in place: the row becomes the form where it sits, the other rows stay put, Save changes puts the row back and re-sorts. One copy of the job on the screen.
4 · A third warning, a third ruleThe bench double, the customer double and now a deck double, each its own words and its own door. A fourth home would be a fourth rule.One lookup, one sentence, one door. Typing a name or a phone checks the four places a company can already live and says it in one shape: <Company> is already <where>. <One fact>. <One door>. The two warnings that exist become cases of it.
5 · Hide the clockHiding is right for this call and wrong for my day. A caller who opens an empty lane at 8:00 and a full one at 8:05 did start the day at 8:00.Drawn in A already: the card's clock starts with the card; the day's clock runs from the first lane opened. The Activity report's Time column reads the card clocks only.

What A got right and B keeps: one spot, one patch; nothing new on the card's shape; the warning's door is the same Open it the bench already uses; Save stays allowed on a double; no new tab, no new table.

What B says no to: blocking a double (two branches of one company, or a GC who is both a customer and a lead, are real); a Remove on open rows (an edit covers a mistake, and a removed job loses its story); a Notes field on the sub form (the comments box is the notes, and it logs who wrote them).
