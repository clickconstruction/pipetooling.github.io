Where it stands

Built and live, v2.6817 to v2.7121: the reader and the backfill, Bill lines, What we buy, Price over time, What to stock, Codes with no part. Not built: PR 2b, the price offered from a bill, and the joins of stage 5.

The ten calls, each with its pick

  • Does a bill read by itself?Yes. Every bill with a PDF is read after Save, with no press
  • Does a bill change the book's price by itself?No, for the first month. The price is offered with the bill beside it
  • The readerThe model reader behind the five checks. A parser only if a house needs one
  • Who matches codes to partsWhoever keeps the Parts Book, from the list sorted by dollars
  • How far back the backfill goesEvery bill on file, newest first
  • Freight, fuel and feesCounted as spend under Charges, never as a part
  • Who sees the viewsThe owner, a dev, the office roles and an estimator. Half built: the office sees them and an estimator does not
  • Ship tickets, quotes and acknowledgementsBills and credits only through stage 4
  • Ask the houses for a file of linesNot now
  • Who fixes the bills that wait for a personThe office, from the Bill lines window
