What this can and cannot say. A bill says what was bought, when, from whom and for what. It does not say what was installed, what sat on a truck, what moved between jobs or what walked away: that is the On site ledger's Used press, not a bill. So this list answers what do we keep going back for, not how many did the jobs use.
Big-box card purchases are not here. 118 card charges at hardware stores in these 90 days have no lines on any paper. Those are the small, frequent buys this list most wants. A photo of the receipt at the counter (punch list #295) is the door for them.
