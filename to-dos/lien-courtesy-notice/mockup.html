It always says

  • The notice we mailed on this day stands; this is a reminder.
  • One number: the notice's claim, or what is still owed on it.
  • The lien's day, from the live affidavit step only.
  • Where to pay, in one line, and that we cannot take a joint check.
  • A person's name and number, and that a wrong bill should be answered.

It never says

  • "Notice of intent to lien" as if it were a statutory step. Texas has none.
  • Interest, fees, theft of service, or "you are in default".
  • "The law makes you pay us", or a joint check.
  • Anything about the GC's other jobs.
  • A lien day the gate refused. The message is held instead.

What a supply house's letter does that ours copies

  • Short, one page, one number, one day.
  • Signed by a person with a phone number.
  • Arrives by a channel the reader checks today.
  • Says plainly that paying ends it.
