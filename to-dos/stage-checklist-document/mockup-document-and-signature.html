The company's standard lists are made here too: author one on any job and press Use as the company's list. Settings → Jobs & billing → Stage checklists becomes a read-only card that says which job each list came from.
One door. The office authors and reads in the same place. A stage with a list shows it as a document: filed with its record number, time and Drive copy, or open with what is left. A stage with no list offers to make one, item by item, each with its two toggles, and to keep it as the company's list for that kind. Every answer carries its stamp; a signed one carries the mark. Amend on a filed document starts a new version and never edits the old.

The document

  • One record per job stage: stage_checklist_records, the hours statement's shape: a snapshot of the questions as they stood (the words never change under an answer), the record id CL-<job>-<n>, a state (open · filed · amended), filed_at, the PDF's Drive id.
  • It files itself when the last row is answered, stamped by the last answer's time, and a PDF lands in the job's Drive folder the way a checklist photo does. No press to forget.
  • Filed means immutable. Adding a check or taking an answer back starts version n+1; version n stays readable and printable.

The stamp and the signature

  • The stamp exists today: stage_checklist_answers keeps who and when on every press. The sheet, the read-out and the paper now show it.
  • The signature follows lienNoticeSignature.ts: a mark typed or drawn (SignatureTypeOrDrawInput), adopted with the press, under the signer's sign-in, bound by a hash of the answer as given. Columns on the answer row: the mode, the PNG path, the printed name, the hash, the time.
  • A signature item is not answered until it is signed; Not here signs too, since it is the claim an inspector would test.
  • Signed rows lock. Take it back writes a new answer row with a reason; the voided signature stays.

Is that the best we can do?

  • A number with a unit. "Did the test hold?" is weaker than "Test pressure · 60 psi · 15 min". A third toggle, asks for a number, makes the record what an inspector reads. Recommended for PR 2.
  • A countersign. Some items may want the superintendent's signature under the crew's. Recommended as a later toggle, not now.
  • Who reads it. The filed PDF can ride the GC portal and the pay application packet. Later.
  • No signal. The mark is a small PNG; it rides the outbox the way report photos do, so a signature on one bar of signal still lands.

Decisions (yours)

  • Files itself, or a press? Recommended: itself, at the last answer.
  • Not here on a signature item: sign or not? Recommended: sign.
  • Who may mark an item as needing a signature? Recommended: whoever may add it (office, superintendent on his jobs).
  • Retire the Settings card, or keep it as a read-only mirror? Recommended: a mirror for one release, then gone.
  • Add a number-with-unit item kind now or later?
