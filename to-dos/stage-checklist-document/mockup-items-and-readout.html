The office on a phone is the same section, narrow: ready stages fold to one line, the open stage shows its rows, and Add a check is a 44 px press that opens the same add box the superintendent has.

What exists and is reused

  • stage_checklist_templates (the company's questions per kind) and stage_checklist_answers (pick, note, who, when, photo), the kernel stageChecklist.ts with the strict rule, the sheet, the strip, the Pipeline's ☐ mark, the photo road to Drive, report photos answering a question.
  • The Documents tab already holds paper sections (pay applications, lien paper, folders, photos, test reports); the checklist read-out is one more.
  • Who may answer is can_report_stage_progress(job_id): the office, and the superintendent and crew on their own jobs.

What is new

  • Items on the job. Recommended shape: question rows with a job_id + fixture_id scope beside the company's null-scoped rows, so answers, the kernel and the sheet change nothing but the grouping. Columns: the words, asks for a photo, added by, added at, retired at.
  • Who adds: the office set and the superintendent on his jobs. The technician answers and notes; he does not add. Who takes off: the adder or the office; retired keeps its answer.
  • The sheet's second group, Added for this job, and the superintendent's Add a check at its foot.
  • The read-out on Edit Job → Documents, desk and phone, with inline add, take off and Print.
  • The line's proof reads this same count: "checklist 3 of 6 ready".

Decisions (yours)

  • Does a job item count toward ready? Recommended yes, same strict rule, or adding one is decoration. It means an item added after a stage read ready flips it back, which is the point.
  • Should the technician add items too? The mock-up says no: he notes, the superintendent and office add.
  • A push when an item is added to a stage someone is clocked in on, or trust the strip?
  • Print: in PR 1, or later? It is what makes "Documents" the right home.
  • Reuse an item across jobs ("make this a company question") from the read-out, or keep the editor the only door?
