Print labels: tape made the obvious choice

The owner, 2026-11-10, with the Brother PT-D610BT and tape in hand: should the new view make label printing more apparent and easy to find. The app already prints a strip per box for that printer. What is hard to find is the choice itself, and the three things to do the first time.

Drawn 2026-11-10. Every job, stencil and code is made up. Green dashes mark what the after adds. The strips are drawn at 3 px a millimetre: 24 mm tape, 72 mm long.

!The owner's calls

  1. Which tape width is in the box? The app lays out 18 mm and 24 mm. Another width needs a layout added.
  2. Does the test strip print the first ticked box, or a sample that belongs to no box? My pick: the first ticked box, so the test is a real label and no tape is wasted.
  3. Should the app remember which boxes already have a label? My pick: not yet. None and Every box cover a new cage for now.
  4. Is "Brother tape" the right name, or "Tape label"? My pick: Brother tape. It is the word on the printer.
