Print labels: a Which job? window that says what you are about to print

The owner's ask of 2026-11-16: he does not like how the window looks when someone presses Print labels, and it should be much more informative. Today it is a list of job names with a count. The drawing below keeps the one question and makes each job answer four more: what is on it, what is new, how much tape it takes, and whether a run was left half done.

Every job, street, name and count on this page is made up. The window is drawn 640 wide, its size in the app. Nothing here is built.

What it does not say
  • What the 91 are. Cages or boxes, which names, on which level.
  • What is new. Six boxes were added this week. They are the ones that need a label, and nothing points at them.
  • What it prints on. Tape or paper is asked one window later. A browser that cannot reach the printer says so one window later too.
  • What it costs. 91 strips is close to half a cassette of tape. Nobody knows before the run starts.
  • What was left half done. A run that stopped this morning is remembered by the computer, and shows only after the right job is picked.
  • Which boxes already carry a code. A box made from a blank sticker needs no label.
  • Where the other press is. A box that is not in the app yet takes a blank sticker, and this window never says so.

? Your calls

Five things to say yes or no to. My pick is under each.

1. Three doors on a job, or one.The whole card opens every box, as today. The doors add the rest of a stopped run and the new boxes.Pick: three doors. They are the two things people come back for.
2. Say which boxes already wear a sticker.It needs one more read of each job's stickers when the window opens. Without it the chip is left out and nothing else changes.Pick: yes. A box made from a blank sticker already has its code on it.
3. "Added this week", or a true "not printed yet".The app keeps no record of which labels were printed. Added this week is free. Not printed yet needs the app to mark a box when its label goes out, which is a new column and its own change.Pick: added this week now. The printed mark as its own step, if you want it.
4. The printer's line in this window.Where the browser already has leave to use the P-touch, the window can connect quietly and say what tape is in it before a job is picked.Pick: yes. A wrong tape is found before the pick and not after.
5. The same for Print blank stickers.Its Which job? window is a twin of this one. Its cards would say how many stickers are still blank and the next free name.Pick: yes, as the next small change after this one.

Size. The window as drawn is one small to medium change in the app alone: no database change. Call 2 adds a small read. The printed mark of call 3 is a separate change with a migration.
