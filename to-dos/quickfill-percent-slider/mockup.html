What the assistant actually knows

The suggestion is a floor. Rough ticked says the job is at least 40% done. The crew report says they are two days into Top Out, so the honest number is somewhere between 40 and 75. On today's card that is mental arithmetic: 40 plus about half of 35.

The bar already holds the answer. The stage blocks are sized by price. A knob dragged to the middle of Top Out reads 57. Nobody needs the arithmetic.

Two numbers the assistant jumps to most: the end of a stage, and the paid share. Both are on the card already, as a tick and a figure. They should be one press.

Is B the best we can do? Four things it gets right, two it leaves out. Right: the facts and the answer are one picture; the two numbers an assistant jumps to are one press; the move line and the button name the number before anything is written; nothing new is written on the server, the save is the same note and the same column. Left out, on purpose: a note box beside the slider, since the move line is the note the save writes, and snapping the knob to a stage end as it passes, since a knob that jumps fights the hand. The chips are the snap.
