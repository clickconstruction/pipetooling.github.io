A navy, orange and yellow skin: before and after

The owner, 2026-11-10, after picking the navy plate for the box page: "I think navy and the safety orange and yellow we use is a good combo. Perhaps we should skin more of the app?" This page draws two ways to do it on five screens, holds both to six rules, and says what each costs.

Drawn 2026-11-10 from screenshots of main at 375 and 1280, light and dark: the sign-in page, the Dashboard, Jobs, Customers, the Tally's On site tab and the bill link's sample. ?Is this the best we can do?

A is the pick. The brand goes in the frame, where nothing is read as a state. The page inside the frame keeps the colour language the crew already knows: blue is a press, amber is late, red is wrong, green is done.

Keep one thing from B: the plate, for a page about one thing. The box page proved it. A cage, a bill, an estimate, a job and a customer each have a name worth a plate. A list, a board and the Dashboard do not. There the plate is a tall title that pushes the work down.

B's orange press loses. Orange already asks for attention as a warning. White words fail on it and navy words pass by a hair. It touches 830 files by hand. And the app already has its one orange press: Clock In, the first press of the day. That is the right amount of orange.

Yellow is the weakest of the three as paint. It cannot be a word, cannot hold white words, and is amber's neighbour everywhere a state is read. It is safe in exactly two places, and it is already in both: inside the app's own tile, and on the bill link's Pay now under navy words.

Thought about and not drawn: the portal's paper inside the app. Cream ground, copper accents, navy words. It lost before a drawing. The app's pages are dense tables tuned on grey under white cards, and every one of about 250 tokens would need a new pair on cream.

What a drawing cannot settle. Whether a navy bar reads in full sun on a phone. Whether the yellow tile or the hand feels like Home. Whether Dark needs any change at all. Each is cheap to try live, and A is cheap to take back.

↓The order, in small PRs

  1. Signed-out pages take the plate, one page a PR. The bill link, then the estimate link, then the sign-in page's name panel. The box page already has it. These are light, few, and the owner has already picked the look.
  2. The bar and the tab bar go navy. The four chrome tokens, light and dark, and the bar's words to white. One PR, one walk on a phone and at a desk, in both themes.
  3. The marker and the mark. The orange bar under the page you are on, the Home press, the light edge on the mode tiles.
  4. What the walk finds. The menus, the search overlay, the health strip, training mode and the imitate bar, each checked on a phone.
  5. Page heads, only for a page about one thing, and only after a week of living with 1 to 4. The cage's screen on the Tally first, since its public twin already wears the plate. Each page its own PR.
  6. Not planned: an orange press.
