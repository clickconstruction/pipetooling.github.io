Has the press count moved since A shipped?

Not yet, and it cannot have. A went live on Saturday 2026-11-14, and the tabs have been counted since. The two days since are a weekend.

  • Tab presses: about 100, every one by the dev account: the build sessions checking their own work, and the dev himself. No office role and no master tech has pressed a tab.
  • A master tech: opened the tabbed window once on Saturday morning, from the Dashboard, and pressed nothing counted. Three opens in the week, all from the Dashboard.
  • The superintendent: the same as the nineteen days before. He opens the plain window and presses Arrived, Leaving and Leave report.
  • What the count cannot say: it does not record the screen size. An open from the Dashboard is very likely a phone. An open from the Jobs page is very likely a desk.

What building C costs, and what a person would lose

  • Size L, as the card says. A new JobWindowPhone, a kernel that picks the tabs, the sections and the bar by role, and a new Today's work section. With tests, a guide and a walk as each role, that is four or five PRs.
  • Today's work needs reads the crew roles do not have cleanly today. The plain window's Plans section failed to load on the live walk. That is fixed first, and it is worth fixing even if C is dropped.
  • Two windows to keep in step. Every later change to the job window is then made twice, once for the desk and once for the phone window. That cost does not end when C ships.
  • A master tech loses one tap. Edit, Bill, Costs and History move behind More ▾, and Status ▾ moves into the ⋯ sheet. Nothing is removed.
  • The crew lose what was never theirs. Job accounts, the bill date, the Status rail. The superintendent keeps Parts cost under a fold.
  • The office loses nothing. Their window stays as A and B left it.

The pick

C is two jobs of different worth, and the walk says to split them.

  • Build the crew half now (pairs 2 and 3), sized M. It needs no new window and no role kernel. It gives the plain window B's order and sections, which the tabbed window already has. The people it serves are the ones who press this window on a phone every working day.
  • Leave the crew lead's tabs for later (pair 1). Three opens a week and no tab pressed is not yet a reason to shape tabs by role. A month of tab counts will say whether it is.
