4. A sheet two subs share

It is counted once, on nobody's row, until someone says whose it is. The window says what each choice does to each sub's Balance due before anything is saved. There is no press that links one sheet to both subs. Which split is right is the owner's call (the card, question 1). The drawing shows the pick: a sheet each, by line.

Why the row is the pick

The owner asked to dig in from the page. The row opens where the figure is, with the rest of the roster still in sight, and Documents already opens this way. The desk is the better home on a phone, so the phone's card opens a sheet that is the same block. Both draw one component from one kernel, so the pick can change without new numbers.
