Punch list #326 · Settings → What customers see · every name, address and figure on this page is made up.

My suggestion, and what is built. One new group on the tab, Lien letters. It says the order first, one line a stage. Under it sits a card for every letter, in the order it goes, each drawn by the code that sends the real one. Press a card and it opens large, with Open the PDF on a paper.

Nothing on the tab said which letter comes first, or that the demand letter is not a step of the notices.

Built: the order, over the cards

  1. Step 1 · Notice of claimCertified mail to the owner and the builder, for each unpaid month. Due by the 15th of the third month after the work. On a house, the second month.
  2. Step 2 · Courtesy noticeA reminder by email and text. It goes on days 1 to 5 of the month the lien is due.
  3. Step 3 · Attorney’s letterA second letter to the owner, 10 to 14 days after the notice, when nobody has paid or answered.
  4. Step 4 · Lien affidavitFiled with the county clerk by the 15th of the fourth month after the last month worked. On a house, the third month.
  5. Step 5 · ReleaseSent once the claim is paid. A filed affidavit also gets a release of record.
  6. Beside the order · Final demand letterOptional. A last demand with a pay by date, before an affidavit.
  7. Beside the order · Retainage noticeOnly for money the builder holds back. Due 30 days after our contract ends.
