3 An error line with its door

The newer surfaces already read Could not read the papers · the reason · Retry. The door is one more word after the reason, What this means. A press opens the same card in place: what happened, what was saved, what to do, and the guide for that family of error. The guide opens in a new tab, as § Rules does, so the error and the form stay where they are. On the desktop frame the error is a phone with no signal, inline on the Bill tab. On the phone frame it is the server's own words on a failed save, as a toast whose action is the door.
