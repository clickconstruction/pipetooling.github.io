Tag a teammate in a job note

Taunya's ask of 2026-11-15: type @name in a job note, have it reach that person, and show a red count above Jobs like the one above Messages. This page draws the note box with the name list, the place the tag lands, the count, and the same on a phone. The purple outline marks what I would build.

The short of it 1 · The note box 2 · Where it lands 3 · The other places 4 · What clears the count 5 · On a phone 6 · The calls

Today

A job note reaches nobody. It sits in the job's list until someone happens to open that row. Typing @dana does nothing: it is seven letters in a sentence.

What I would build

Pick a name from a list under the box. The tag lands in a Tagged job notes section at the top of the Pipeline, counted above Jobs, with one line on the Dashboard for people who have no Jobs link. You answer right there and the answer goes on the job.

What I would leave out first

Subs, email, tags in reports, estimates and bids, tagging a whole group, and any reminder for a tag nobody answers. Each can follow once the first version has been used for a few weeks.

Why a pick and not the typed word
Two people can share a first name. The list shows the full name and what the person does, so the right Chris is one tap.

The team already types @ to mean "at". Notes on prod read like arrive @ 8:45 and materials @ $300. If the app guessed from the words, those would look for a teammate named 845.

A pick can be checked. The list offers only people who can open this job, so nobody is tagged on a job they cannot see.

No, not by itself. A task has a due day, a tick and a history of its own. A tag would become a second copy of the note that someone must tick as well as answer. If a tag does need to be a job with a date, the tagged person can still make a task from it by hand.

4 · What clears the count the life of one tag

Taunya's words were "so a user knows what they need to respond to". So the count is what still waits on an answer, and reading alone does not clear it. The Messages count already works this way.

Waiting · counted
From the post until the tagged person acts. Shows in their section, above Jobs and on the Dashboard line. The sender's note says waiting on Dana.
Opened · still counted
Opening the row or the job changes nothing. A tag read on a phone in a truck is still owed an answer at the desk.
Answered · cleared
The tagged person posts any note on that job, from the section or from the row. The sender gets a push and sees answered under You asked.
Done, no reply · cleared
One press, no note. The sender sees Dana marked it done. For a tag that only needed reading.
Nobody answers
It stays, and its age turns amber after two working days on both screens. The first version sends no reminder and never expires a tag by itself.

6 · The calls eight, each with my pick

CallMy pickThe other way
1 · Where it landsTagged job notes at the top of the Pipeline with the count above Jobs, and one line on the Dashboard's Needs you card.A lane in Messages, or a task on the checklist. Section 3 says why not.
2 · What clears itAn answer on the job, or Done, no reply. Opening does not.Opening clears it, like an unread mail. Simpler, but a tag read in passing is then lost.
3 · Nobody answersIt stays and turns amber after two working days. No reminder, no expiry.A second push after two days, or the tag falls off after two weeks.
4 · Picking the nameA list under the box on @ or the @ button, the job's crew first. Only a picked name is a tag.The app reads @word from the text. Fails on two people named Chris and on "arrive @ 8:45".
5 · Who can be taggedAnyone with a login who can open that job: the office and the job's crew. Not subs, not people who left, and not an estimator, who cannot open a job today.Subs too. They see only their own jobs through their own pages, so it needs its own design.
6 · A pushYes, one push per tag, under the notice switches a person already has. No email.The count only. Cheaper, but the crew is not looking at the header all day.
7 · Telling the senderA push on the answer and the You asked fold. No count for the sender.The answer tags the sender back, which they must then clear. Two people end up clearing each other.
8 · Other note boxesThe Pipeline row first, then the job window, Job Mode and Quickfill, which share one box. Reports, estimates and bids later.Every box at once. A bigger first PR with more to walk.
