Punch list #209 · after the folders press · 2026-10-29

Where files go: the office sets the root and the name of a job's folder, on Documents

Today a job's Drive folder is a link someone pastes into Edit job, one job at a time, and the root the app files under is a secret a developer sets on the functions. Now that the app makes the folders, the office should own the two things that matter and see the rest. My recommendation: a section on Documents called Where files go with three cards, the root with a live write check, the name as a pattern of chips with a real example, and the tree with one choice, plus the two presses. The five folder names inside stay fixed, since the sweeps file by them.

Three cards, left to right: where, what it is called, what is inside. The root's Check runs the same write test the photo sweep runs, and says the same words. The batch button names its count; the jobs it cannot do are listed, not stopped on.

What the office gains: moving the root or changing the name needs nobody technical and no deploy, a pasted folder the app cannot write to is caught the moment it is pasted, and every job gets the same tree.

What the office loses: nothing it has today. A change applies to folders made from then on; existing jobs keep their links, and no folder is moved or renamed by the app.
