Is this the best I can do? — five calls before any code

  1. The letter as one node or two. Drawn: one square node whose date is the reply-by day, its words carrying sent Sep 14. The other way is two nodes, sent and reply by, which is truer to the rail but spends a column on a date that is never a deadline.
  2. The whose-move word: under every node, or only the live ones. Drawn: under every step that is not done, missed or blocked. The quieter way is the word only on the next step and in the Waiting-on line, leaving the rail as it is.
  3. Waiting on as a second line, or folded into Next on the path. Drawn: a second line. Folding it (File the affidavit by Oct 15 · waiting on the GC's reply first) keeps one line but makes it longer than a phone likes.
  4. Where the overdue letter points. Drawn: the Next line names the affidavit with the letter as its reason. The other reading is that an unanswered letter's next step is counsel, not the filing — that is your call and counsel's.
  5. The Timeline tab's column. Add Waiting on beside Next on the grid, or leave the grid as it is and keep the word on the job.
