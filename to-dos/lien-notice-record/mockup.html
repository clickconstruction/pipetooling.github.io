Lien affidavit: the notice record and copies of what was sent

Punch list #322. Every name, number and amount here is made up. The pages are drawn by the app’s own print code, at Letter size.

The ask: when a lien affidavit prints, add a page that says what was sent and its certified mail details, so an attorney or a teammate can be sure of the paper. After the first draw the owner asked for the month table to come off and for copies of the notices to print in its place.

My suggestionThe hard case: two notices, one late, one copy with no mail number

Behind the help sheet and in front of the paper: one page of the record, then a copy of each notice. The record page says who each copy went to, the day, the method and the mail number. It reads sentences 5 and 6 against the record. It lists what the record is missing. None of these pages is filed with the clerk or kept in the job’s saved copy.

NowWhat prints today for the same job

The help sheet says Ask first and names the months. The paper’s sentence 6 has two days. Nothing lays out what went, to whom, by what number.

My suggestionThe clean case: one notice, both copies on time, both numbers

The same page says plainly that the record backs the paper and that nothing is missing.

My suggestionWith the help sheet in front, and the batch print

With the record behind it, the help sheet keeps its mark and points at the record, so a sheet with Ask first fits one page again. The Lien desk’s batch press prints one block for each paper, several to a page, in front of the whole stack. Copies of the notices are a box there that starts unticked, because a batch has many notices behind it.

What was decided, and what is not built

  • Its own page, in front of the paper. Striped at the top and marked “do not file”, like the help sheet, so it is pulled before the clerk.
  • Its own tick box. “Print the notice record” sits under “Print the help sheet first”. Both start ticked.
  • No month table. The first draw listed each month with its last day to mail. The owner asked not to see it. The Ask first line still names the months that are late.
  • The delivery record leads each copy. A big check on its left, the days since it went in large type, what went, and a line for each envelope. The owner asked for this after the second draw.
  • A copy is drawn from the record. It is the notice’s form with the words the app kept when it was recorded. It does not show the invoices that rode behind the notice.
  • Not built: printing the scanned file from Drive, or the exact page kept in Documents. A print cannot pull those in without a new server function. The page says where they are.
  • Not built: the address on each envelope. The record does not keep it, and the page says so.
