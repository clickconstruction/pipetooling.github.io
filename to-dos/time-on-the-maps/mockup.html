The calls

What this mock-up picks

The press

Time, with a clock, to the right of Satellite and Streets. On, it fills dark and reads the date: Time · Mar 2019. On a phone it reads the date alone.

The row

One line under the map, oldest on the left, Today held at the right end. It slides sideways. Thirty dates stay one line.

Time frames the spot

Time zooms to the open dot on today's photograph, with Today lit. A date swaps the photograph and keeps the framing, so then and now can be flipped.

Back to today

Today shows today's photograph of the same spot. Time again, Satellite or Streets leaves Time, and the map fits its dots again.

Leaving

Time lives in the map on screen and nowhere else. Another tab, a closed window or Hide map ends it. Only the dates found are kept, a week.

What stays in the past

Dots, counts, lines and the office stay as they are. The row says The dots are today's.

One or the other

The archive is Esri's. The Google map comes down while a date shows, and comes back on Today.

One piece

A frame around the two pin canvases carries the switch, the press and the row. Seven cards stop writing it themselves.

Weighed: a slider

Dates are uneven and few, and each stop is a fresh picture. Jobs already has a slider, for another clock. Not picked.

Weighed: then beside now

Google beside Esri breaks Google's rule. Esri beside Esri is allowed and worth a later card.

Weighed: one date for the whole map

A date means one spot. Across fifty miles each tile has its own. Not picked.

The maps here are drawings. Google's and Esri's own pictures cannot be shown in a mock-up.
