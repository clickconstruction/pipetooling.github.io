Where it is still weak

The tab is sorted by builder, and the work is sorted by job. Both documents end at the same place: what a sub owes you on one job. A job's own row, with its sheet, its secrecy paper and each trade's standing, would be the truer home later.

Each builder still mixes setting it up with using it. Setting up is rare and suits a dev. Using it is weekly and is office work. Splitting the two is what lets the office in.

The check list is ticked by hand. Reading the certificate and ticking what it finds would be a later step. The list is what makes that possible.

Opened means their page or the link was opened. It does not say the right person read it.

The send is an email only. A text with the same link would be a small step after.

The amounts and the words are placeholders. I cannot tell you what to require. Your insurance agent and your attorney set them.

A field is plain words. It has no bold of its own and no picture. A logo at the top would be one more field to add.

A finished paper for a GC is not drawn. What goes to a GC here is the builder, a thing to walk. A job's secrecy paper would go the same way once the card's own train makes one.

What is already there

The Sub NDA's flow is used three times as it is: as the page a dev types on, behind the link and on a GC's page. Its rules and its tests come along unchanged. For insurance, the app already asks a trade for its certificate, takes it on the trade's page, lets the office mark it good and watches the day it runs out. Steps 3 to 6 add what the certificate must say in front of that, and the check list behind it.

For the build

Shared parts, each a kernel with its tests. A field, which is a text box drawn as the document's own words and keeps only what differs from its base. The read mark, a revision with who read it and when. The send, a recipient's states and the batch's count, its ready list and its held list with reasons.

Step 1. documentsPageTab.ts gains builder, shown to a dev only through shownDocumentsPageTab. A registry lists the builders. PaperFlow, paperBuilder.ts and the fixture are renamed to the document builder and reused. The route in App.tsx is removed. No migration. One release note and fragment, with the Documents architecture doc, the glossary and the access doc amended.

Step 2. A migration alone, pushed before any client reads it: document_builders, a row a builder, with its kind (walked or filled), a draft, a published copy, a revision, who published and when, and who read which revision. Read and written by a dev only. It ends with the four fence calls.

Step 3. document_builder_sheets, a builder and a project, with the job's blanks and its own fields as a draft and a published copy, and the usual's revision it was published on. A pure kernel lays the standing sheet over a job and names what is missing. The blanks start from the job: gc_projects.property_owner_customer_id and the project's address. Your name comes from the company's own settings, as the portal's letterhead does. The press that publishes your usual is a batch: it says its count, acts on the jobs that are ready and names each it held. Printing goes through printRoot.ts.

Step 4. An edge function answers a published sheet for a link's token, and refuses one that is off. A public page outside the app's frame, light, with no login. Only the token's hash is kept. Step 8 reuses the function and the page's frame.

Step 5. document_builder_sends: the builder or the sheet, who it went to, the contact, the job it is about, the day it went, the day and the revision they opened. The email is the insurance ask paperEmail.ts already writes, with the link. The trade portal's answer gains each job's published sheet, and PAPER_WORDS.coi its line and press in both languages. A send makes a page for someone who never had one, never turns one back on, and writes a sent copy.

Step 6. A certificate's row in person_contract_documents gains the project it is for and the asks it was checked against. A kernel turns a published sheet into its asks, and says a certificate is outdated when they differ. companyPapers.ts reads a company's certificate for a job. The start gate keeps reading the company's own certificate until this lands.

Steps 7 to 10. Kernels for what differs between the draft and the published copy, page by page. Step 9 adds only the customer portal's block, placed by job, to step 5's send. Step 10 is a new kind on submit-portal-request that writes the picks and nothing else.
