Stickers before boxes

My recommendation: make the sticker its own thing. It is a printed code and a short number that point at a box, or at nothing yet. The office prints a run of 200. On site, a scan of a blank sticker asks one question: an old box or a new one. The next unused sticker is the next box, with no office step. A scan later opens the box, and the box can be renamed there. Every label printed so far keeps working.

The short of it

Now

  • A box is typed into the app first. Its label is printed from that record.
  • A label's code is cut from the box's own row. No box, no label.
  • Nothing renames a box. A wrong name means retire it, add another and print a new label.
  • A scan is the phone's own camera, one box at a time.

My suggestion

  • A sticker is printed first. It is blank until someone scans it.
  • The first scan asks one thing: an old box or a new one.
  • The sticker never carries the name, so a rename needs no new sticker.
  • A walk screen keeps the camera open for a hundred in a row.

Build the first three steps, then walk the site with a roll.

Also possible · a sheet of 30
Sheet 1 of 14. Weatherproof labels, one inch tall.

Fourteen pages print in a minute, where 400 strips of tape take about half an hour. It needs a laser printer and one alignment test.

Why a plain number. One count for the whole company means a leftover sticker works on any site and no two runs collide. A site prefix, such as JR-0147, is the other way. The code in the square cannot be guessed, which matters because a box's page opens with no sign-in.

6 · Twenty in a row the fifth step of the plan

The spot is what happens between one sticker and the next.

Now, and after the first three steps
Around every sticker
  1. Open the phone's camera.
  2. Point it at the square and tap the link.
  3. A new browser tab opens the app.
  4. Say what the box is and save.
  5. Go back to the camera.

It works on any phone with nothing to install. It leaves a hundred tabs open.

Fine for one box. Slow for a hundred, and it needs signal for every page.

Your calls seven, each with my pick first · all seven taken as recommended on 2026-11-12

1 · What a sticker is

Recommended: its own code that points at a box.

Also possible: 200 empty boxes made at print time. An old box could then never take a new sticker.

2 · The number

Recommended: one company-wide number, 0147.

Also possible: a site prefix, JR-0147. A leftover then looks wrong on another site.

3 · The paper

Recommended: Brother tape now, and the sheet of 30 if a run is too slow.

Also possible: the sheet of 30 first.

4 · The boxes the app already knows

Recommended: any blank sticker, matched on the phone. Old labels keep working.

Also possible: a named label for each known box, found by hand in a pile of a hundred.

5 · The walk

Recommended: the camera in the app, with a press that needs no scan.

Also possible: the phone's own camera only.

6 · Who prints a run

Recommended: the office, a primary and a superintendent.

Also possible: anyone on the job. Placing a sticker is open to the whole team either way.

7 · A new box's name

Recommended: the sticker's number until someone types one.

Also possible: ask for a name every time.
