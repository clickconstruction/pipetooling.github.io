2 What each piece reads

Nothing here invents a number. Every row is a read the app already has — except the payout tile and the pending-deposit read, which are the two new pieces named in the plan.

Carried from last week

Bills whose expected day fell in last week's window with no payment landed since, promises now past, and returned checks (list_ar_return_cases). The one-week rule is new; its parts exist.

Came in

Recorded payments with paid_on this week: a Stripe payment, a check deposit that posted at Mercury, a wire. Doors to the job.

Checks in hand

Recorded payments with no bank link whose type reads as a check — typed in, not yet driven to the bank. Door: the AR deposits window.

Pending at the bank

Mercury rows still pending — deposited, not yet posted. A failed one goes red here with the bank's reason and a door to its return case. (New read: today no screen shows pending money in.)

Their word · they say it's sent

Promises due this week and open paid claims (“check mailed Thursday”), with the open money they cover and who heard them.

By their usual pace

Bills whose expected day falls this week by the payer's own pay speed — folded, counted, and labeled a guess so nobody reads it as a fact.

Stripe + the payout tile

Open Stripe invoices, and the next payout with the day it lands (new edge function, get-stripe-payouts). The payout is not added to either lane — it is money from the left lane reaching the bank.

Who sees it: the same roles as the cards above it — no technician, estimator or sub ever does. Dollars: the strip's totals must foot to the windows its doors open; any gap is a bug in the strip, not the windows.
