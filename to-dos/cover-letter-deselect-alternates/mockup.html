Deselect alternates on the letter itself

Wendi: “i want the ability to deselect alternates from the cover page in click tooling.” On a bid with no versions, a price offered to the GC rides the letter as an alternate, and the studio’s only advice is “manage offers on the Pricing tab”. Every number and name below is made up.

Her seat She grooms the letter with the preview beside her. The alternates she wants gone are named in Step 1 and printed in the preview — neither spot can remove them.
The job behind the ask Offers pile up: ＋ Add price offers the new price by default, so a copy made to compare rides the letter. Twin alternate lines in a contract document read as carelessness.
The whole office Split-bid packets and group alternates already have Step 1 checkboxes. The deselect writes the one offered flag the room, the Approval PDF and the Pricing card all read.

5 What one deselect changes — and the owner’s calls

The deselect writes the one offered flag, so every reader follows by itself. Nothing is stored twice; a sent bid is no special case (the Pricing tab’s toggle already changes a live room today, and the stamped send record never recomputes).

The letter + print + Google Docs copythe Alternates block drops the line; the last one gone returns the plain letter
The bid roomthe signable page loses that option / add-on
The Approval PDFits letter page plans through the same kernel
Pricing tab + Version pickerthe card’s caption flips to “not offered”; the badge reads “not in letter”
  1. Both doors, or just the panel? Default: both — PR 1 is the discoverable, symmetric fix; PR 2 is the ask as spoken.
  2. What a deselect means. Default: stop offering (the shared flag). Rejected: a letter-only hide — the letter and the Pricing card would disagree.
  3. Do unticked prices stay listed? Default: yes, as “not offered” rows — one click to re-offer, junk stays visible.
  4. ＋ Add price keeps “Offer it right away” checked? Default: keep — that door exists to offer; a wrong offer is now one click to take back.
  5. The same-amount nudge. Default: include in PR 1 — it names the exact mistake on Wendi’s screen.
