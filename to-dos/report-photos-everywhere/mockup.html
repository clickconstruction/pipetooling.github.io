Is this the best we can do?

The self-review in full is on the card. The short of it:

  • One block, one strip. Nothing new is designed; the per-job form's photo block becomes a hook and the card's strip is mounted where the words are. A second photo design would be the mistake.
  • The Tally pattern kept whole. The bytes go up as soon as the shot is shrunk, Save only ties them, Drive is the home and the bucket a waiting room, the sweep is one function, the card says where each photo is in words.
  • A weak signal is the real case. Section 6 is in the train, not a leftover, because the tech on a bad site is the person this is for. The shrunk copy is what waits, so ten shots are 3 MB on the phone.
  • Two shapes rejected. Routing every door through the per-job form, which is a decomposition card of its own; and a photo as a template field type, since every report type benefits and the table is keyed to the report.
  • Four calls for the owner, each made as recommended unless the owner says otherwise: no photo on a project or a bid report yet; no taking back a saved photo on the Edit form; attach the photos to the email rather than link them; the queue built last as its own PR.
