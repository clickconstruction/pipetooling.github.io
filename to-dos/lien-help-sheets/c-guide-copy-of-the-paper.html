A guide copy of the paper itself

The paper prints twice. The first copy is a guide: the blanks are filled in as they should read, numbered marks sit beside them, and the notes run down the side. The second copy is clean, and that is the one to sign and file.

Good because the reader sees the answer on the paper, exactly where the pen goes. The risk is that someone signs or files the guide copy, so it carries a band across the top and bottom saying it is not the paper.

The same sheet for the other two papers

PaperWhat changes on the sheet
§ 53.056 noticeNo notary and no blanks to write in. The steps are: sign, put the invoice behind it, mail one copy to the owner and one to the original contractor by certified mail, then type both tracking numbers in the app. The date on the sheet is the mail-by day. The stop block keeps the public owner and adds a missing owner address.
§ 53.057 retainage noticeThe same as the § 53.056 notice, with the day our contract ended in the facts strip and counsel's cover letter named as page 1 of the owner's copy.

What goes in each blank

1 The county you sign in. Here Birch. Not the property's county.

2 Pat Sample signs in front of the notary.

3 The same county as mark 1.

4 The day it is signed.

5 The notary signs and prints a name.

6 The seal, in ink that shows on a copy.

Read these two lines

7 A person or a company. Never ATTN, never a city, never someone who lives in the house.

8 Every month has a notice mailed on time, to the owner and to the contractor.

If either is wrong, call the office. Do not sign. A signed paper is never fixed by hand.
