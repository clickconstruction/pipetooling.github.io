The lien help sheets: changes 5 to 11, one at a time

Changes 1 to 3 are built. Each change below shows a piece of a help sheet as it prints today on the left, and the same piece with that change on the right. Each can be built without the others. Change 4, a dotted circle for the notary’s seal on the paper itself, is not drawn here.

Change 5 · small

Two halves, by who does it

Two different people use this page. The signer and the notary need the top for five minutes. The office needs the rest for a week. Today both lists run down the page in one voice. Side by side, each person finds their half at a glance.

Built today

Four things to write. Nothing else is written on the paper.

  1. The signature line. Pat Sample signs in front of the notary, and not before.
  2. The day, the month and the year. The day it is signed.
  3. The notary’s signature and printed name. Both lines.
  4. The notary’s seal. In ink, dark enough to show on a copy.

After it is signed

  1. File the original with the Alder County Clerk. That is the property’s county. The clerk charges a fee for each paper.
  2. Write the clerk’s recording number and the day filed on the stamped copy.
  3. The same day, open the job on the Lien desk. Press Open the affidavit tab ›, then Record filing…, and type both.
  4. Mail a copy by certified mail to the owner and to the original contractor. The app shows the last day to mail.
  5. Press Record service… and type the tracking numbers.
