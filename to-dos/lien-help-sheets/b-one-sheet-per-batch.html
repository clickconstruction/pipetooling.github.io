One sheet for the whole batch

One page prints in front of a batch of affidavits. The top half is the same for every batch: the blanks and the notary's checklist. The bottom half is a row for each paper, with where it is filed, who gets a copy, and boxes to tick as each step is done.

Good when one person signs a stack in one sitting, as the office did on its first run. A paper the app would stop is listed with the reason and is not printed behind the sheet.

Each paper has six blanks

  1. COUNTY OF, at the top: where you sign. Today: Birch. Not the property's county.
  2. Signature: Pat Sample, in front of the notary.
  3. COUNTY OF, notary's block: the same county.
  4. Day, month, year: the day it is signed.
  5. Notary: signature and printed name.
  6. Seal: in ink that shows on a copy.

Call the office first

  • The owner lives in the house.
  • The owner is a city, county, school or the State.
  • A month has no notice mailed on time.
  • Anything printed on a paper is wrong.

A signed paper is never fixed by hand. Print it again.

After the notary, for each paper

  1. File the original with the county clerk named on its row. Write the recording number on the stamped copy.
  2. The same day, open the job on the Lien desk. Press Open the affidavit tab ›, then Record filing….
  3. Mail a copy by certified mail to the owner and to the original contractor. The app shows the last day to mail.
  4. Press Record service › and type the tracking numbers. Tick the row.
