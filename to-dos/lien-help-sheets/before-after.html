Do not sign. Call the office first if any of these is true.

  • The owner lives in this house. That is a homestead, and it goes to the attorney.
  • The owner is a city, a county, a school or the State. A lien does not attach there.
  • Line 4 names an office or a job title, such as ATTN, where an owner's name should be.
  • A month on line 5 has no notice on line 6 mailed on time, or the contractor got no copy.
  • Your stack holds more than one affidavit for this same property.
  • Any word, name, address or amount on the paper is wrong.

A signed and sealed paper is never fixed by hand. Fix it in the app, print it again, and sign the new one.

The blanks. Nothing else is written on the paper.

  1. COUNTY OF, at the top. The notary writes the county you are standing in when you sign. Here that is Birch. It is not the property's county.
  2. The signature line. Pat Sample signs, in front of the notary and not before.
  3. COUNTY OF, in the notary's block. The same county as blank 1: Birch.
  4. The day, the month and the year. The day it is signed.
  5. The notary's signature and printed name. Both lines.
  6. The notary's seal. In ink, dark enough to show on a copy. A paper with no seal goes back.

After it is signed

  1. File the original with the Alder County Clerk. That is the property's county. The clerk charges a fee for each paper.
  2. Write the clerk's recording number here:   and the day filed:  
  3. The same day, open the job on the Lien desk. Press Open the affidavit tab ›, then Record filing…, and type both.
  4. Mail a copy by certified mail to each address below. The app shows the last day to mail.
  5. Press Record service › and type the two tracking numbers.
  6. Keep the clerk's stamped copy with the job.
