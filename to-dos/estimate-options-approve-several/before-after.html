Estimate options: approve more than one — before and after

Customer acceptance page (/estimate/accept/:token) · Estimates → draft editor → the option cards · the Customer acceptance record

What changes

Today every option on an estimate is an alternative: the customer page draws radio cards, the Approve button names one option, and acceptance freezes that one option's lines. After: each option is offered either as one of the choices (radio, exactly one, the ★ pre-selected — today's behavior) or as an add-on (checkbox, any number, none pre-ticked). The customer page shows two groups, Choose one and Add to it; the total card and the Approve button sum whatever is ticked; acceptance freezes every accepted option's lines, in offered order, into the same fields every downstream reader already uses. An estimate whose options are all add-ons has no Choose one group — it is Taunya's "5 separated" case, and at least one tick is required.

Assumptions the drawing made

  • The customer page is drawn at its 560px document width with the v2.2772 total card under the title; the estimate header, terms, signature block and footer are unchanged and omitted.
  • Add-on cards use a square check where choice cards use the round radio, in the same orange; the blue Add-on badge is the only new color and it is a saturated status color, not a neutral.
  • The line-items section under the picker shows the accepted options as groups with a small heading each; the frozen line_items_snapshot itself stays a flat list in offered order (the accepted document, the PDF and the job created from it keep reading a flat list).
  • The office builder's Offered as control is a two-way segmented switch under the option name field; the ★ Recommended button applies to choice cards only, and an all-add-on estimate keeps the star on one card so the pre-accept forecast has a number to mirror.
  • The Approve label pattern is Approve "Replace 50-gal" + 2 add-ons — $5,150.00; with no choice group it is Approve 3 options — $4,340.00. Both are placeholders for the CX wording the owner approves.
  • Board 3's five scopes are invented to match the shape of Taunya's request (five separate priced scopes on one estimate); the real customer's scopes are not on record.
