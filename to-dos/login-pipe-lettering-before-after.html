The owner, 2026-09-18: "I think it would be really cool if we made clickplumbing dot com have letters that look like pipes." Every option below keeps the same photo, tiles and card; only the title changes. Options A–D draw the word from one pipe alphabet — twelve glyphs of straight runs and 90° elbows, three strokes per glyph (shadow · tube · highlight) so it reads as a round tube, not a flat outline. Option E is CSS only, no SVG.

A Copper tube S · SVG component

Type L copper: warm gradient, a bright specular line along the top of every run, a dark drop shadow onto the photo. The i-dot and the period are valve caps. Hover the word and water runs through it (the dashed inner stroke animates) — off by default on phones.

/signin · 1180 pxoption A
  • Why copper: it is the pipe a plumber pictures; the orange also echoes the Plumbing tile's wrench.
  • Cost: one presentational component, PipeWordmark, taking the word and a palette; the h1 keeps the text for screen readers and the link stays the whole word.

B White PVC with purple-primer joints S · same component, palette 2

Schedule 40 look: white tube, a cool grey shadow, and a purple band at every elbow where the primer would show. Cleaner against the photo's greys and yellows; the most legible of the five at phone size.

/signin · 1180 pxoption B
  • Trade-off: reads "plumbing" only to plumbers; to a homeowner it is white tubing. Copper says plumber to everyone.

C Copper runs, brass fittings, red handwheels S+ · component with fittings layer

The same copper, but every elbow wears a brass fitting (a slightly fatter gold sleeve) and the i-dot and period become red gate-valve handwheels. The most "plumbing" of the set, and the busiest — best at desktop, simplified at phone width (fittings drop, handwheels stay).

/signin · 1180 pxoption C
  • Cost: the fittings are one more pass over the same glyph corners; ~40 lines on top of A.

D Only "Click" in pipe, "Plumbing.com" stays Playfair XS

The brand word gets the treatment, the rest stays the serif people know. Smallest change, fastest read, and it keeps the long tail of the URL crisp at every size.

/signin · 1180 pxoption D

E Copper-plated Playfair — CSS only, no SVG XS · 6 lines of CSS

Not pipe, but metal: the current font with a copper gradient clipped to the letters and a hard bevel shadow. Zero new markup; the h1 stays an h1. The honest cheap option if the pipe alphabet feels like too much.
