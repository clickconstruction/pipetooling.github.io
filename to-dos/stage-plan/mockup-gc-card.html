The GC's Stages card, kept simple

A GC needs three things from this card: what's done, what we're on, and when the next one is. Stages happen in order, one at a time, so the card is a numbered sequence with three states. Nobody's name, no "offered", no chips to decode.

One stage at a time

Stages run in order. A stage can't start until the one before it passes, so the card never shows two live stages. Done, now, next, later.

Three states, no chips

A green check for done, a filled dot for now, a hollow dot for what's coming. The state is the shape of the row, not a label to read.

Our crew, always

Nothing on the card says who does the work or that it was offered to anyone. Days come from the sub's pick, progress from their percent; the GC sees a crew and a schedule.

One place to ask

The next stage carries the only link. Asking about the stage under way or one two steps out makes no sense to the GC, and it made the old card busy.

What changes. The office and sub sides stay as built; only the GC's card and the data behind it change.
  • The stage kernel sends the stages in sequence order with one of three states, no name, and the percent for the live stage only.
  • The customer portal replaces the Stages card with the sequence: a headline line, then done / now / next / later rows.
  • The ask control mounts on the next stage only; "Re-scheduling" becomes "new dates coming" on that row.
  • The sample GC fixture carries these four stages so the office preview and the guide screenshots come from it. No migration, no prod test data.
  • The "show a GC the stages you plan" guide is rewritten to match.
