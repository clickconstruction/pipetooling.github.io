The two shapes One portal per job, against one link per company

JobTread gives a sub one portal per job, five tabs, with the GC's whole crew on the schedule. Ours gives a company one link for everything it has with us, opening on where it is, and in detail only its own work. The shape stays. Each borrow below fits inside it.

Row 2 A thread with a reply box, and a word to every trade on a job

JobTread's sub writes to the GC from the Dashboard and reads an inbox per job, where the GC's Prioritization went to every trade at once. Ours lists the emails we sent, read only. The company answers by email or text, and the office reads it on Messages. The customer's page already has the thread with its box, since v2.5331.

Row 5 The map and the weather

JobTread draws the address on a map and the week's weather. Ours prints the address as one line. The app draws Google Maps elsewhere, and nothing in the repo reads a forecast.

Recommendation drawn: build the Directions link and the job head first (S each), then the thread with its box (M) and the word to every trade (S), then the files list with its free press (S) and the stage fold (S); leave the map canvas, and bring the forecast to the look-ahead only if the owner wants it there.
