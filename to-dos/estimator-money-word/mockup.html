The estimator's word on money

Theo bid Dana Whitfield's repipe and still talks to her more than anyone. When she tells him the check goes out the 25th, or I paid it Tuesday, Theo today phones Rosa and Rosa marks it. The app gives Theo the jobs born of his bids, with their money, in the places he already works: the Bid Board row, a lens beside Job accounts, the thread in Messages and a card on his phone. What Dana says about paying, a day or that she already did, is Theo's own word and goes straight on the job as one record. Paid stays the bank's word: her word that she paid meets the deposit when it lands, so Rosa's allocation is one press, and a word nothing lands behind comes back to Theo, not to Rosa.

Second pass — is this the best we can do?

  1. The scope was estimator-shaped; it is job-shaped now. jobs born of my bids, for the estimator role → the job's people: the office, the job's account manager (jobs_ledger.account_manager_user_id, the field primary_scope already runs on) and the bid's estimator or account manager. Opening a job from a bid copies the estimator in as the account manager, so the field means what it says. One predicate serves the primary role's mirror bug too (they see their jobs but no promise chip, and may mark any job paid).
  2. Two sheets were one too many. Write down their word · Tell the office they paid → one press, one sheet with a switch, one record: a day they'll pay, or that they say they paid, both They said in the trail, both voided by Never said that.
  3. The claim does work for the office instead of making work. a Needs you line at Rosa on every claim, with a confirm box → the claim shows up where Rosa already is, the bank deposit's allocation, as the suggested job; only a stale claim surfaces, and to Theo.
  4. The thread is the first door, not the sixth. The Messages bridge moves up to PR 3 and gains the They say they paid reading, so the common case needs no new screen.
  5. The pay page is still the real tool. Send the pay page on the line and {bill} filled for these jobs: what gets money in.
  6. Left out on purpose. No RLS change on jobs_ledger; no estimator Mark paid; no new table (the claim is a job_payment_promises kind with a method column); the Bid Board's hidden J#### chip stays hidden, the money line is his door.
