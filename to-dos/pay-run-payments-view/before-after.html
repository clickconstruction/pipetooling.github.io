Pay run · a Payments view — before and after

People → Pay → Payroll (the Pay run ledger) · drawn from the real screen 2026-09-17 · 337 payments to 21 people since Mar 9 · for the owner's read

What changes

Today the ledger is one row per pay stub (a person's week), and a stub's payments live inside it — you see Paid to date and Balance, not the payments themselves. The after adds a third pill beside Pay run · Balances: Payments, one row per payment made — when, to whom, for which week, how much, the memo, who recorded it — with every column header a sort, a window (30 d · 90 d · this year · all), the same name box, a total for what is visible, and a by week paid band mode like the ledger's. Nothing else on the tab changes; a payment row's Stub link opens the same pay stub the ledger's View does.

Assumptions the drawing made

  • Rows read pay_stub_payments joined to pay_stubs (person, period). The eight rows are real — the newest eight in prod on 2026-09-17; amounts, memos and dates are as recorded. The Recorded by column is drawn as one name because every payment in the sample was recorded by the same account.
  • The Method chip is derived from the memo's first word today (Cash App, Mercury, otherwise none) — there is no method column on the table. Question 1: derive it, or add a real method field on Record payment (Cash App · Mercury · check · client-direct · other) so the chip is always right and can be filtered? Deriving is one kernel and zero migrations; a field is one migration and a Record-payment change.
  • Sorting is by the header clicked — Paid on (default, newest first), Person, Period, Amount, Memo, Recorded — click again to flip; the arrow on the active header says which. The choice is remembered on the device like the tab's other prefs.
  • By week paid bands use the same tinted band row the ledger draws for pay periods, keyed on the Monday of the paid-on week, with the week's count and sum. By person is drawn as a third mode with a subtotal row per person. Question 2: are three modes (flat · by week paid · by person) worth it, or is flat + sort enough for day one?
  • The window defaults to 90 d so the page opens fast (a few dozen rows), and All loads every payment (337 today) in one query. The name box also matches memo text, so typing D-P7PR finds a Cash App transfer.
  • The total line is over the visible rows only, like the ledger's tiles; a by person subtotal is that person's visible payments, not their balance — balances stay on the Balances view.
  • No new writes: Record payment stays on the ledger row (and in the stub view); a payment's delete stays where it is today. Question 3: should Record payment also live on this view (a "+ Record a payment" that asks for the person and week first), or is the ledger the only place money gets recorded?
  • Matched: the page's tab pills and the Pay run · Balances toggle, the Open · Paid · All chips and the blue Draft Payroll button, the name box and its hint, the ledger table's 0.72 rem headers and tabular cells with small cents, the week band row (v2.3318), and the light tokens from src/index.css. Build with the CSS variables, never these hexes.
