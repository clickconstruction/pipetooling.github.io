The menu, open on each line

The same list every time. Only the mark changes: a dot where one person is picked, a tick where several can be.

To. One address, as the send takes today. Picking another swaps it.

Cc. Tick as many as ten. Whoever is on To is greyed. A typed address gets its own row.

Reply to. Office people only. Each row says what picking it does.

The two extra RMC rows are examples. The real list reads the contact people already kept on the customer record, with their "gets bill copies" mark. Teammate addresses here are samples.
