GC Review, built for the person who actually drives it

mock-up · 2026-09-27

names from the app · amounts illustrative

What we learned

The weekly round was designed around the account man opening the app, emailing his GCs from his own inbox and marking each one. In practice only the assistant works this screen. When the knowledge is his, she phones him and types the answer in. So the screen hands work to someone who never picks it up.

The assistant — at the keyboard

Checks the bills, sends the statements, records what was said. She is the operator for every GC.

The account man — on the phone

Knows the GC: when they will pay, what they are disputing. He is the source, not a user.

What the record needs

Both names. Whose word it is and who entered it — so his read is not filed as hers.

6 The order to build it

#ShipsSizeTouches
1The worklist in place of the rounds panel — every GC over the line, three steps, grouped by account man; each step opens the window that exists todayS–MScreen only. No database change.
2Whose word — the mark keeps the source beside who entered it; every pill, tally and email reads itMOne additive migration, the mark form, six displays, the round email
3The call sheet — inline answers for one account man’s GCs, one save, printableMScreen, plus a batched save
4Check & send from the row, with “Replies go to”S–MDraft Message, the send function
5Broken promises — a passed pay-by date with money still open turns red and sorts firstSOne tested rule, the worklist, the header pill
6Prompts re-aimed — the Pipeline card, the Dashboard row and the morning email go to the office; the old per-sender pieces retireMTwo cards, one email, one database function, the help guide
7Ask without a call (optional) — text the account man a link; he taps answers on his phone, no sign-in; she approves themLA new tokened page. Only if the call is a cost, not the point.

Yours to decide

1 · Where do a GC’s replies go?Proposed: the account man, with the assistant copied.
2 · Who answers for Wednesday?Proposed: the assistant for the round being done; the account man for the relationship and for his word being current.
3 · May she record “no change”?Faster on a quiet week, but it is how a record goes stale. Proposed: allowed once, then a fresh sentence is required.
4 · Is the phone call a cost or the point?If it is a cost, step 7 removes most of it. If the call is how you keep account men engaged, skip step 7.
