The six calls, each built to its pick

  • Does a typed name tag by itselfNo. A tag is a tap or Enter only.

  • Where a tag landsThe Checklist: a count and Tagged task notes on Today. The Needs you line counts job and task tags together.
  • A plain note with no tagIt still tells nobody.
  • Bid and estimate notesAfter tasks, as their own small train.
