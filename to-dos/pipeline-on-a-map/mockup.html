Pipeline on a Map

The Bid Board's map, moved to the top of the Pipeline, plus one thing the bid map does not have: a slider that rewinds the map to any day since the Pipeline started recording moves.

What we keep from the Bids map, and why it works

The Bid Board card (v2.3162 → v2.3251) is productive for four reasons that carry straight over:

  • It is a second view of the list, never a filter on it. Pins follow the search and the trade pill; chips toggle pins only. On the Pipeline the pins will follow the search box, the GC / development / account-man filters and hidden groups.
  • A pin lands you on the row. Click a pin, the row lights below and the popup carries Open, Edit, Directions. The Pipeline already has a row-jump mechanism (the job-number jump chip) to reuse.
  • The empty land became numbers. The rail beside the map (v2.3206) turns distance into three buckets with counts and dollars, and lists what is due nearest first.
  • Everything underneath is shared. The two canvases (Leaflet and Google), the office anchor with 25 / 50 mile rings, the geocode cache hook, clustering, Fit all, Hide map, the phone bar. None of that is rewritten; the new card is a kernel plus a component that composes them.

What is different about jobs

Sections and colors

Pipeline sections, not bid sections: Waiting Working Ready to bill Billed Paid. A job in collections keeps its Billed color and wears a red ring, the way an overdue bid wears one on the Bid Board. Paid starts off (723 pins would bury the 112 live ones), exactly as Lost starts off on the bid map.

The popup

Number and name, customer or GC (whoever the bills go to), status with the current stage and % done, address with miles from the office, and what is owed on a Billed job. Buttons: Open job (the job window), Edit (the window's Edit tab), Directions.

The rail

Same three distance buckets, but the second number is dollars still to collect and the sub-line is N to ask for (Ready to bill + Billed). The list under it is Ask for money · nearest first: Ready-to-bill and Billed jobs, longest-waiting first, then nearest. Tapping a row selects the pin and lights the Pipeline row.

Coverage today

All 829 jobs have an address. 542 are already in the geocode cache; the other 287 place themselves through the existing geocode function the first time the card opens (twenty per request, painting as each batch returns). No "no map location" sheet is needed; a geocode miss just lists the job with a link to its row.

The addition: scroll back in time

The precedent is the As of slider on Job Summary → Timeline (v2.2807): a slider walks back a day at a time, Play walks forward, jump chips for common distances, and a strip that says what changed since. The map takes the same control and the same words, so nobody learns a second grammar.

What "as of a day" means for a pin

  • Its color is the status the job had on that day, from the recorded moves: the last move at or before the day wins. Before a job's first recorded move it takes that move's from status. A job created after the day is not drawn.
  • The chips count that day's world. Waiting 41 · Working 28 · … as they stood then, so the legend doubles as the day's Pipeline headline.
  • The rail follows. Dollars to collect and "to ask for" are computed from the day's statuses and the bills that had been sent by then.
  • The "since then" strip counts moves after the day: started, billed, paid, collections. It is the Timeline strip, reworded for jobs.
  • Play walks forward one day per tick until today; any drag, chip or pin click pauses it. The section tour from the bid map (v2.3208) is not ported; the time walk is this card's Play.

Loading it without slowing the Pipeline

  • Nothing extra loads until As of is switched on. Then one light read of every job (id, numbers, name, address, status, created_at, GC, revenue) and the full move table, both paged past the 1,000-row cap, kept for the session in the same style as the day-ledger cache (jobDayLedgerSessionCache).
  • The Pipeline's own list is scoped (paid jobs load on demand), so the rewound map draws from this history set, not from the board's rows. Today's view still draws from the board's filtered rows so it stays a second view of the list.
  • Coordinates come from the same geocode hook; a rewound day only adds addresses the cache has not seen.

Decisions for you

Shown or collapsed by default on the Pipeline? The bid map shows by default and remembers Hide per device. The Pipeline is open all day in the office. Recommend: shown, remembered per device, same as Bids.
Which sections start on? Waiting, Working, Ready to bill, Billed on; Paid off. Recommend as listed. A rewound day still shows a now-paid job in the color it had then, because the chips govern the day's status, not today's.
Dollars on the rail. "To collect" is billed minus paid plus ready-to-bill exposure, the Pipeline's own numbers. The Pipeline is office-only by RLS, so this stays inside the owner-only line already drawn.
Play speed. The Timeline walks a day per tick. Recommend: one day per 150 ms, so six months plays in about 30 seconds.
Rail list. "Ask for money" (Ready to bill + Billed, longest first) is my pick for the list under the buckets. Alternatives: "Started this week", or "Working past billable progress" from the money strip.

Not in this train

  • The bid map's section tour (Play through the sections) — Play means time here.
  • Pin size by revenue, area select for site walks, and the office Map page's polygon tools.
  • History before Feb 22, 2026.
