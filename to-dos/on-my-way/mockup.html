7 Is this the best we can do? The second pass

The first draft was shape D as drawn. Read back against the owner's three words, deeply, holistically and modify, seven things changed.

  1. The minutes were typed. Now the app guesses them from the phone and the job's point and the tech only corrects. The straight line rounds up, so the guess errs early. A routed guess is one gate change away.
  2. I'm here always texted. Now the arrival stamp happens on every press, and the text is a choice, since the stamp is the data and the text is a courtesy.
  3. The notes box stayed. Now the app writes the note, which is truer than what a tech types in a truck and reads right on the office's strip.
  4. The window was ignored. Now a guessed arrival past the block's end gets one quiet line that points at Running late, which already slides the board.
  5. Every advance became a drive. Now the same-site rule and Start here instead keep a text from going out for a drive that is not one.
  6. The lines were found by their words. Now a column names which line each press sends, the editor shows it, and the migration seeds both.
  7. The stamps were the press's alone. Now they are named as what unblocks Travel on Days, the office's On the way / On site line and the Day view's gaps.
