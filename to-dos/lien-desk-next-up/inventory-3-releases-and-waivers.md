## Easy to miss

- **Three shortcuts skip the Unconditional confirm.** The "Are you sure you meant to choose Unconditional?" dialog fires only from the step 2 toggle (LienReleaseModal.tsx:1200-1205). It does not fire for "Waive the $X already paid instead ›" or "Waive the $X paid ›" (1030-1035), for "Issue unconditional" from the strip or the Dashboard queue (preset form), or when a settled bill picks unconditional by itself (414).
- **The 7-day check wait is applied in only some places.** The Bill tab, View bill and GC Review hold the unconditional for 7 days after a check (lib/jobs/checkClearing.ts:13, 31-42; lienWaiverCell.ts:88). The Bill Customer strip's "Issue unconditional" and the Dashboard queue count a release as cleared as soon as applied payments reach its amount (lienReleaseTracking.ts:64-80). Cards, transfers and cash never wait.
- **The Dashboard queue always opens Unconditional · progress** and its chip is hard-coded "Conditional · progress" (DashboardLienReleaseQueueModal.tsx:304, 372). The strip maps a conditional final to unconditional final (BillCustomerLienReleaseStrip.tsx:217).
- **A queue row disappears as soon as any live unconditional row exists** on or after the conditional, including an autosaved draft (lienReleaseTracking.ts:113-130 has no status filter). Releases with no bill snapshot never enter the queue (216-218).
- **No paper without the record.** Print, Download PDF, Mark issued, Send it to his desk and sign-now all mint the row first and lock every field. After that the only way to change it is Cancel request (while asked) or void and start again.
- **Every void is two clicks except one.** "Discard this draft" inside the already-waived warning voids and closes in one click (LienReleaseModal.tsx:1182).
- **Draft behaviour.** Nothing is created until the first real edit. The window resumes the newest draft, or an awaiting-signature row so the request stays cancelable. Signed or issued rows never resume; they live under "Already on this job".
- **An opener's preset form beats the bill's pick.** "+ New release" from the strip always starts on Conditional · progress with no bill hint.
- **Signing rules.** The row must be awaiting signature to be signed, so sign-now on a paper-issued waiver first flips it to awaiting (LienReleaseModal.tsx:769-789). A typed signature is refused whenever the signer is not the signed-in user, on every door. The signer picker locks once asked. The ink is trimmed before storage so it prints the same size. Mid-signature holds the app's auto-reload (LienWaiverSignOnPage.tsx:52). The pad keeps strokes when the phone turns.
- **Recipient differs by door.** The window and the leader's seat send to the GC's billing email (job customer email when no GC). The inbox lane's "Email to customer" always uses the job's customer email (LienSignatureInboxSection.tsx:86-91).
- **"Mark sent without emailing" exists only in the inbox lane**, and that lane shows only to the person who requested the signature. A leader who signs with sign-now in the window never passes through it.
- **The strip's "View" shows the page without the signature**; View in the window, Documents, the queue and the job's Lien paper show the stored ink. The strip's Void also does not stamp `voided_by`.
- **The Pipeline blue box counts drafts**, though its tooltip says "issued" (useStagesRowFlags.ts:122).
- **Email details.** Only a signed, non-voided release can be emailed. The stored signed PDF is preferred and rebuilt with ink if missing. The subject is `Lien waiver for Bill N · <job#> <job name>` only when exactly one bill is picked, else `Release of lien — <project>`. A Settings template override replaces the built-in wording.
- **A blank title never blocks signing; any other blank printed detail does.** An empty amount blocks too.
- **Escape does not close the Release of Lien window or its pad.** The queue, the leader's seat and the sub dialog do close on Escape.
- **Sub waiver drift from its guide.** The dialog sends directly; the guide (content/help/send-a-lien-waiver.md:15) says it opens the normal Send for signature email. The settle guess is 5 days here against 7 on the GC side. The link to the sheet is only the `sheet:<id>` text in the document's note.
- **The older guide is stale against the code.** content/help/give-a-customer-a-lien-release.md still describes three forms and buttons "✍ Request signature" and "Print for signature"; the window now has four forms and the six-step labels above.
- **The window's header comment mentions copy-for-email**, but the window has no copy button. Only the Bids → Lien Release tab copies to the clipboard.
