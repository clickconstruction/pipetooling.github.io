## Easy to miss

**Props and what the opener controls**
- `initialTab` lands the window on `demand`, `notice`, `affidavit` or `release_record` (LienInstrumentsModal.tsx:123, 230). The Lien desk uses `notice` and `affidavit`; a Documents row uses whichever tab holds the paper.
- `invoice` preselects that one bill when it still has money open. Otherwise every unpaid billed line starts selected (LienInstrumentsModal.tsx:224-229).
- `noticeMonths` (from the Lien desk item) replaces "the last work month" as the notice's `months_covered`, in the reference strip and in the by-hand prefill (LienFilingTabs.tsx:209, 411, 796).
- `signerNameFallback` is the job master's name. It signs the letter and is the contact person / claimant person on the notice, affidavit and release. Nothing in the window lets the user change it.
- `onRecorded` fires after every record, void or filing change. On the Pipeline it refreshes the amber box on the lien icon and marks the Lien desk item sent (JobsStagesTab.tsx:4578-4583). On the Documents tab it reloads the Lien paper list.

**Prefills and what is remembered**
- Every open resets: tab, record method to Certified mail, sent-on to today, tracking empty, both exhibit switches on, email strip closed (LienInstrumentsModal.tsx:213-243).
- Changing tab closes any open record step, drops a pending "Confirm void" and refills County from the property record (LienFilingTabs.tsx:140-144).
- The letter re-prefills whenever bills, payer, history or property data change, but keeps the user's deadline, payment-method line, corrected address and switches. The lien and interest switches fall back to the default when their basis changes (LienInstrumentsModal.tsx:492-534).
- Defaults in the letter: deadline 10 business days out, suit line on, theft-of-services off, notary block off (src/lib/jobsDocuments/demandLetter.ts:860-871).
- The theft-of-services switch is forced off as soon as any payment exists on the job (LienInstrumentsModal.tsx:573-579).
- The email To box keeps a typed address when the strip is closed and reopened in the same sitting (LienInstrumentsModal.tsx:1430).
- Steps / Windows is one choice per browser, shared with the Lien desk and the History tab strip.
- The phone steps strip always opens folded. The supply-houses card always starts folded in this window, even when houses are owed.
- By-hand record prefills the claim from the job's open balance and the months from the desk months or the last work month (LienNoticeByHandPane.tsx:74-75).

**Automatic behaviour with no button**
- For each Stripe-hosted bill the window fetches the invoice number, lines and due date the customer actually saw. Only `dev` can read in Stripe test mode; everyone else reads live (LienInstrumentsModal.tsx:337-366). This is the only role check inside the window.
- The letterhead is re-read from company settings on every open (LienInstrumentsModal.tsx:165-176).
- The packet is built twice so the letter's Enclosures line carries real page counts; the recorded `exhibits` carry them too (LienInstrumentsModal.tsx:540-569, 667-677).
- Emailing the demand letter records the send in the same press. There is no separate record step, and the email id becomes the tracking string.
- The notice gets a homestead statement automatically when the property record calls for one (LienFilingTabs.tsx:159). The affidavit swears the notices paragraph automatically when we are the original contractor or a notice is on file (LienFilingTabs.tsx:183).
- Affidavit serve-by is computed from the filed day on Record (LienFilingTabs.tsx:441).
- Recording a demand letter inserts with the new columns first and falls back to the old shape if `exhibits` / `debtor_party` are rejected (LienInstrumentsModal.tsx:693-706).
- The window draws nothing at all until the letter prefill exists (LienInstrumentsModal.tsx:793).

**Things that only appear in certain states**
- "Release of record" tab: only with a live affidavit that has a filed date. If that affidavit is later voided, a Documents row can still open the window on `release_record`; no tab is highlighted and the body reads "No recorded lien on this job…".
- "Record filing…" disappears once any live affidavit record exists. "Record service…" shows only until served. To redo either, the record must be voided first.
- Notice tab on a job with no GC: one sentence, no document, no buttons, no preview. The Filings list still shows.
- Mechanic's lien tab with a shut gate: checklist only. The text says each item "links back" to its fix, but no links are drawn.
- "tracking owed" editor: only on a notice or retainage filing whose certified or courier send has no number.
- "§ 53.057 retainage notice" rows can appear in the Filings list (View, Void, saved copy, tracking), but this window cannot create one.
- Different-payer bill chips are dimmed and carry a payer suffix only when the job's bills went to more than one payer.
- "Copy for an email" hides when every supply house is paid.
- Phone: no Cancel; the email strip replaces the foot buttons; every record step is a full-page sheet except Release of record, which stays in place.

**Not wired in this window (present in the shared components, so easy to assume)**
- "set the date ›" on a dashed timeline step: the window passes no `onDoor`.
- "They told us…" on the supply-houses card: the window passes no `word`.
- Claim correction (LienClaimBox): Lien desk only. In this window the notice always claims the job's open balance; only the by-hand path lets the printed claim differ.
- Nothing on the notice, affidavit or release documents is editable here. Work start and end on the affidavit are both the last work date (LienFilingTabs.tsx:174-175).

**Suspected existing defect, from reading only (not run)**
- The "Saved copy" link and note typed on a record step may not be saved. `insertFiling` and `recordNoticeSends` read `docUrl` / `docNote` inside `useCallback`s whose dependency lists omit them (LienFilingTabs.tsx:329-355, 385-430). Typing in those boxes does not rebuild the callback, so the affidavit filing and the release record likely write an empty saved copy; the notice record does too unless a send row is edited afterwards. The render tests only check that the boxes exist. Adding the link later from the Filings list ("link the saved copy") uses a plain function and does not have this problem. Worth confirming before the redesign treats the record-step boxes as working.

**Other notes for the merge**
- Two print mechanisms exist: the demand letter's "Print packet" opens a merged PDF in a new tab; "Print" / "Print for notarization" on the other tabs open an HTML print window. "View" on a recorded demand letter reopens the letter HTML only, without exhibits.
- Print, View and Print packet each toast "Popup blocked…" when the browser refuses the new window.
- The file-a-lien guide still describes a header reading "⏱ Notice by … / File by …". The code now draws the timeline strip instead; no such header line exists in LienInstrumentsModal.tsx.
- No component in the window checks a role. Access comes from who can see the openers and from RLS; both guides list `dev, master_technician, assistant, controller`.
