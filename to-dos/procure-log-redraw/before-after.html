Is this the best we can do?

Asked twice, and the answer was no both times. The second draft fixed the first. Then I looked at the second at full size with a busy log in it, and it failed in a new way. The third draft is what I would build.

What the third look changed

  1. Parts became orders. The second draft said "Order by 10/27 · tomorrow · released 10/22 · needed 12/08" on six rows running, each wrapped to two lines. Parts that share a deadline are now one order that says it once. After ordering, parts that share a PO are one line.
  2. The four dots became a calendar. Fifteen rows with the same first dot lit said nothing. A week line shows what dots could not: how near each deadline is, and how far past its needed date a late order runs.
  3. Sent back says what they wrote and offers Pick another product. Before, it only said Rejected.
  4. I was wrong about the missing lead times. I called them unimportant until the GC approves. They are what lets the log say when the GC must answer. The line now gives that reason, and a waiting fixture shows its answer-by date.
  5. One row shape, one column of tick boxes. Waiting mixed folded fixtures with loose single lines at two indents.
  6. Changed is no longer amber dots. That repeated the mistake of finding 5. The count is on the Send update button.
  7. One grey sentence went. It repeated the line of missing facts beside it.

What the second look changed

  1. A bar by share became four fixed steps. With 44 of 47 waiting, the bar was one grey block and a sliver, and a step at zero vanished.
  2. A track on every line became a track on started lines. Forty-four empty tracks said nothing.
  3. House and Lead left the rows when every row says the same.
  4. Sent back came out on top. It is the only thing you can act on today.
  5. Waiting folds by fixture, not by house. With one house, one fold hid everything.
  6. On site folds away. Done work should not lead the page.

Where it is still short

  • The calendar is proven on made-up dates only. BP375 has no stage dates and no lead times, so today its log has nothing to draw. The first job with dates will test it.
  • Grouping by order-by date is my reading of how an order gets placed. If Wendi orders by fixture, or all at once, the groups should follow her.
  • The dates editor is today's, restyled and nothing more.
  • The phone cards are not redrawn here. An order would be a card and the calendar would drop to words.

Your calls

  • Orders by order-by date, then by PO once placed. Is that how the office thinks of an order?
  • Waiting by fixture, or by house as it is today?
  • Set… in place of Tick the 43, and Mark ordered… on an order. Both are quicker, and both change a flow Wendi knows. The tick boxes stay either way.

How it would be built

  1. The top of the card: one card, the four steps, the Next line, the buttons, the line of missing facts with its reason.
  2. Orders: the groups by order-by date and by PO, sent back with their note, waiting by fixture, in a tested kernel first.
  3. The calendar: the week line, today, the needed dates, the marks.
  4. The phone cards.

Four small PRs. The GC's printed sheet and the update text do not change.
