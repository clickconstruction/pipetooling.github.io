Quickfill as a round

Twenty-seven sections open at once is the audit's worst screen: 28,692 px, 30 inputs that zoom the page, a jump strip 4,069 px wide. The round already exists in the data — every section carries an org-wide Mark with a history that snapshots its count — so the phone page becomes a list of those marks measured against each section's own rhythm, one section per screen, and the mark button moves to the thumb and carries "next section" with it. Nothing here is built. Five questions at the bottom are yours.

read first What the code says that the audit did not

The mark is org-wide, and it already carries "looked". quickfill_section_marks (one row per section: when, by whom) and quickfill_section_mark_events (every mark, with the section's outstanding count at that moment). "Marked 9:17 AM by Taunya" is that row. The round's Mark looked is the same mark — it does not need a new table, and it marks for everyone, as today.
Most counts are only known while a section's body is on screen. A collapsed section never mounts, so its "12 open" goes silent; four counts survive (missing job info, complete-no-bill, unreachable prospects, dispatch inbox) because the page computes them. A row that needs every count live would have to mount all 27 bodies hidden — the very thing the phone cannot afford. So a row shows the live count where the page has it, otherwise the count at the last mark, labelled as such; opening the section shows the live one.
One-at-a-time already exists. The Dashboard's Needs You card has a Walk the list mode — "4 of 20", Skip, Next — saved per person per device. The section screen copies that shape. And every section has a one-line banner question the office wrote ("Which houses are past due?"); on the phone it becomes the screen's prompt under the title.
The 4,069 px "table" is the strip. The phone jump strip is ~25 chips that do not wrap, each with its stamp; the list replaces it, and the desktop's chip dock never renders on phones anyway. The real tables inside sections (Accounts payable, Banking, Billed awaiting) still scroll sideways in PR 3 — rows-not-tables is PR 5.
