The second pass: is this the best we can do?

  • Cut: a deposit picker in the close box. The first idea. Once the case notices on evidence, a picker is a slower spelling of the same press, and it still makes a person connect the dots the app already holds. Settled another way… stays for the truly other: paid by card, cash, settled inside a write-down.
  • The rule never fires early. A deposit bigger than the bounce is not evidence — the applications are. A partial make-good (money on the job, but less than the check) keeps the case open, and the stake box keeps the arithmetic, as today.
  • No migration, no new store. close_ar_return_case has taken replaced + the deposit's id since v2.4325; the UI only ever offered it off the exact match. One kernel rule beside arReplacementFor, tested the same way; the pane and the apply flow each draw it once.
  • The watch sentence tells the truth. It becomes: When Southern Post's money lands — this amount alone, or inside a bigger payment on the job — it shows here.
  • What stays the same: the exact-match banner and its one-press close, Take it off the job, They said…, the fee press, Reopen.

Your call Two things only the owner can decide

  • The fee never blocks the close. Drawn so — the read-back says the fee is undecided and lets the close go. Want a hard stop instead, so no make-good close ships while the $30 is undecided?
  • How loud the apply moment is. Drawn as one card after the apply toast, two presses. If that is too loud for every apply that happens to touch a case's job, it can live only in the case pane (panel 1) and the apply stays silent.
