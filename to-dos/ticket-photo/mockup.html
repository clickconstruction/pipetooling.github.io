A photo of the supply house ticket, taken at the counter

Two papers leave a counter. A card receipt is the only list of what was bought, and no invoice ever follows it. An account ticket is followed days later by the bill in the mail. Neither reaches the app from the tech today. In the last 90 days the company cards were swiped about 176 times at material houses, and 2 of those charges have a receipt behind them. The office sorts each charge to a job from the clock and the schedule, without seeing what was bought.

The shape drawn here: one Photo of a ticket press on Job Parts Tally, pressed before the tech leaves the counter. The tech names the job in one tap. The photo goes to the job's folder. The app ties it to the card charge by the minute of the swipe, often within the hour, or to the PO code and later the bill. A dashed copper outline marks what is new on a screen that exists today.

6 · Later, not in the first build: the app reads the ticket

The bill reader behind Add invoice costs about a cent a read. Pointed at a tech's ticket it fills the house, the total, the day and the PO code, so the tech types nothing and the match is exact. Its lines can then be offered to the job's Materials on site as a delivery. This waits for the owner's word, since a field press would spend a read.

The calls that are the owner's

  1. Not for one job. Does such a ticket go to the Office job by itself, or wait for the office to place it, as frame L draws?
  2. The matches that make themselves. The only swipe on that person's card in the half hour before the photo. A typed total equal to the charge within a day. Are both sure enough to tie the photo to the charge with no press? The sort to a job takes the sorter's tap either way.
  3. Who may see a ticket. The tech who took it, the office, and whoever can open the job's Team only folder. Anyone else, or fewer?
  4. Any receipt, or material only. The press takes whatever is photographed: fuel, a meal, a tool. Is that wanted, or should the words say supply house only?
  5. The second door. The Job Mode card's Ticket line beside Photos. In the first build, or after the Tally press has been used for a while?
  6. A ticket that never meets a charge. The tech paid cash, or with their own money. Paid another way… in frame L records which. Does the office then read it as money owed to the tech?
