A photo of the supply house ticket, taken at the counter

Two papers leave a counter. A card receipt is the only list of what was bought, and no invoice ever follows it. An account ticket is followed days later by the bill in the mail. Neither reaches the app from the tech today. In the last 90 days the company cards were swiped about 176 times at material houses, and 2 of those charges have a receipt behind them. The office sorts each charge to a job from the clock and the schedule, without seeing what was bought.

The shape drawn here: one Photo of a ticket press on Job Parts Tally, pressed before the tech leaves the counter. The tech names the job in one tap. The photo goes to the job's folder. The app ties it to the card charge when the bank settles the charge, or to the PO code and later the bill. A dashed copper outline marks what is new on a screen that exists today.

6 · Later, not in the first build: the app reads the ticket

The bill reader behind Add invoice costs about a cent a read. Pointed at a tech's ticket it fills the house, the total, the day and the PO code, so the tech types nothing and the match is exact. Its lines can then be offered to the job's Materials on site as a delivery. This waits for the owner's word, since a field press would spend a read.

The calls that are the owner's

  1. Shop or truck stock. Does a ticket for no one job go to the Office job, or wait for the office to place it?
  2. The match that makes itself. Same card holder, same amount to the cent, within a day of the swipe. Is that sure enough to tie the photo to the charge with no press? The sort to a job still takes the sorter's tap either way.
  3. Who may see a ticket. The tech who took it, the office, and the job's team through the job's folder. Anyone else?
  4. The total. Asked as an optional field, as drawn, or left out until the app can read it?
  5. The second door. Frame H puts a Ticket line on the Job Mode card beside Photos. In the first build, or after the Tally press has been used for a while?
  6. A ticket that never meets a charge. The tech paid cash, or with their own money. After how many days does the ticket stop waiting, and does the office then see it as money owed to the tech?
