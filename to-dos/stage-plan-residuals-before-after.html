Item 3 Plain (—) rows on the final draw

A line can be Order (a numbered stage → a draw), Any (bills whenever), or — (plain: just a line item). The plan reads a plain row as later · with the final draw — but when the last Order stage's Bill it fires, the invoice holds only that stage; the office ticks the plain rows into it by hand, or forgets. J892 has no plain row today, so one is drawn in: a $2,400 water heater.

Item 4 Who says a stage is done

Today a row is done when the sheet reads 100% or the inspection was called (open question 2, built as proposed). The alternative is an office-marked done: one tick per row, with who and when. The two are not exclusive — the tick could sit beside the automatic reading as an override.
