Recommended: the short Dashboard direction B

One list of what waits on him alone. One line for his day. One door to everything else. The office sees the same list from its side, so it can prepare each paper and remind him.

Dashboard view

One setting per person. He can change it, and so can the office from his desk on People. A leader who wants everything keeps Full.

ShortWhat waits on you, your day, one doorFullEvery section, as today

Alternative: fold what is there direction A

Keep today's Dashboard and give the leader the folds the office roles already have. Needs you shows his lines first. Everything else shuts to one line each.

Alternative: bring it to him direction C

He does not open a Dashboard at all. When the office has a paper ready, a text takes him straight to the signing screen. One text a day at most, at an hour he picks.

Calls only the owner can make

  1. Which lines stay his alone? The rules name five: approve a notice, sign a notice, approve a GC's run, sign a waiver, review the line budgets.
  2. May he sign several notices with one press after reading the list, or one paper at a time?
  3. Who owns what leaves his screen: money over 30 days, hours to approve, bank label approvals?
  4. Does his day belong on the page, or is the list of what waits on him the whole page?
  5. Which four tabs sit at the bottom of his phone?
  6. Should a text reach him when a paper is ready, and at what hour?
  7. Is Short the default for every leader, or set person by person?
