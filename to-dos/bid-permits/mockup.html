Is this the best we can do?

No, on three counts. Each comes from the test, not from taste.

  1. It leads with the part that is empty. One GC name in 27 has a permit under it. The feed names one applicant, often a person, and Texas gives a general contractor no license number to match on.
  2. It asks the match question without evidence. Same company? on two similar names is a guess. This name pulled the permit on the site you are bidding to them is a reason, and five sites in eleven have one.
  3. It is only there when the bid is open. A permit changes what a person does mostly after the bid is sent: the permit issues, or another plumber's permit lands. Nobody opens a sent bid to look.

So the same parts are turned around: the site leads, the question rides on the site, the GC's year says how much it knows, and news comes to the board.
