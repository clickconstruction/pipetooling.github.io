PR 2 The Job acct chip is the act

  • Recording stays one tap. On a past-due row whose job has an open account (the “we forgot to mark it” case), + Job acct flags the invoice in one press — recording the truth is never blocked, and the teal lines re-sum everywhere at once. Tap Job acct ✓ to take it off. A current row instead opens the move workflow (PR 3 below).
  • No account → the form’s own words. The tap unfolds the invoice form’s amber band under the row, verbatim, with its doors: Mark opened… (the shared sheet — saved as open, the account is on record from then on) or Flag anyway (the roster’s bought, no account row keeps telling the truth).
  • The flag is per property. A split invoice shows no chip (the form’s own sentence as the tooltip); a row with no job reads Pick a job › and opens the invoice form (PR 4) — the modal never forks the allocation flow.

Try it in the Bluebonnet · 60–90 panel below: BS-19344 is past due with no account on record — its + Job acct unfolds the amber band (Mark opened… / Flag anyway); flag it and watch the cell’s teal line above change. BS-19210’s Job acct ✓ taps off.

PR 3 The move window: a charge moves only while it is current

  • The rule, as the owner set it. A charge can be moved onto the job account only while the invoice is Current — once it is past due, the window is closed. Each invoice has its own clock (its due date); the teal strip counts the still-movable dollars and names the soonest window to close.
  • The move is a workflow, not a flag. Pressing + Job acct on a current charge opens the move modal: either tick “I called the house — this charge was moved onto the job account” and press Mark it moved ✓ (dead until the box is ticked), or send the request by email from the app — the drafted email to the house’s job-accounts rep is previewed, and a sent ask turns the row amber: asked 10/20 · follow up ☎.
  • Worked as a list, by house. Work the list › groups every still-movable charge by house with the rep’s phone on the group line, and ✉ Email the list › asks for the whole group in one send. The footer counts to ask · asked · dollars; past-due rows stay honest (“past its window — flag it only if it is already on the account”).

Try it: press Work the list › in the teal strip above — on BS-21004, send the email (the row turns asked · follow up), reopen it and tick the call box to mark it moved; on CP-8903 tick the box straight away. The strip reaches ✓.

Calls The owner’s decisions (defaults recommended)

  • 1 · Which cells click? Default: every money cell — buckets, Credits, Owed, the footer row. Alternative: bucket cells only.
  • 2 · What can PR 1 do? Default: read-only + View + Open house; edit / due date / payment doors are PR 2. Alternative: ship both at once.
  • 3 · The phone bars? Default: nothing now — a bar already opens the house’s phone screen. Alternative: a segment tap opens this modal as a sheet.
  • 4 · Job names in the modal? Default: yes, one allocations fetch on open (PR 2’s chips need them anyway). Alternative: chip only, zero extra queries.
  • 5 · Flag with no account on record? Default: Flag anyway stays — the invoice form’s checkbox ticks regardless, and the roster’s amber evidence row carries the truth. Alternative: the band offers only Mark opened….
  • 6 · Per-row taps or a batch? Default: per-row — a cell holds a handful and an open account makes each tap one press. Alternative: tick-boxes + “Flag N as job account”.
  • 7 · The email. Default: sent from the app’s mailbox to the house’s job-accounts rep, reply-to the sender — one invoice from the move modal, or the house’s whole list from the group line. Alternative: a mailto draft the office sends from their own mail.
  • 8 · Where does the nudge live? Default: the strip on this screen only. Alternative: a Dashboard / Quickfill line when a window is about to close (“$2,210 still movable, soonest closes Friday”).
  • 9 · When does an unanswered ask escalate? Default: the app’s waiting convention — amber at 3 days, red at 7, red asks lead the worked list. Alternative: no aging; the office just sees “asked”.
