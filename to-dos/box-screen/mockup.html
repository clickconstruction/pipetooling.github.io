Where this changes what the Take from a box build does

Nothing here undoes that build. Its press, its run, its photos and its takes stand as drawn. Four things shift around them.

  • The Tally's title row folds too. That build folds the tabs and the ticket press. This look also folds ← Dashboard and Job Parts Tally while a box is open. If its code already does, nothing changes.
  • The chips and the four line presses are regrouped. That build leaves them as they are today. Same presses and same sheets, in new places.
  • Photos and takes become one strip under the tiles. That build draws a box's photos where the dashed square is, and its last takes as their own card.
  • Used stays, as that build decided. When Take replaces it, an open part shows three presses in one row.

Limits of this look

Not read, or not verified

  • No crew member's screen, and no crew member asked. The screen was walked as the dev. Sizing by use rests on the ledger's counts, which describe one site walked mostly by one person.
  • The closed part is a bet. Can't find was pressed once when it was in plain sight. Nobody knows whether a tap more will cost reports, or whether a clearer screen will bring more.
  • A real mixed box was not walked. The test job holds one box with one part and two empty ones. G12 is drawn from the code and made-up parts. Five kinds and the four tiles fit one screen as drawn, with 6 px to spare and every part closed. An open part, or a sixth kind, pushes the tiles down.
  • The second and third phones are drawings. Their press counts and where the part starts are measured on the drawings, not on a build.
  • The other build is a design, not code. Where its code differs, such as the title row or an empty box's Take press, the drawing shifts with it.
  • The camera. A headless browser cannot open one. Photo and Take are read from the code and the design.
  • Two sheets were not pictured. Where is it? and Move box did not open in the walk's script. Both were read in the code.
  • Unsorted. Its parts carry Put in…, which is the whole point of that screen. There the part should open by itself. It was not drawn.
  • Not in this look. Damaged and Back to the supply house sit under Move to…, where a person with a cracked fitting may not look.
  • Widths. Today's screen was walked at 390 by 844 and 430 by 932 in light, and at 390 in dark. Not at 320 wide, and not with large text.
