Privacy

In effect from November 26, 2026.

ClickTooling is the work tool at clicktooling.com. It is run by Click Construction, LLC, called "we" on this page. This page says what ClickTooling collects, what we use it for, who else sees it, and what you can ask us to do.

Who this page is for

  • People with a ClickTooling account: our staff and the subcontractors we work with.
  • People who open a link we send: a customer, a builder or anyone else reading an estimate, a bill, a contract or a schedule.

Accounts are made by our office. Nobody can sign up alone.

What we collect

  • Account details. Your name, email address, phone number and role.
  • Work records. What you and your team put into ClickTooling: jobs, bids, hours, clock-ins and clock-outs, notes, messages, photos and files.
  • Location. Where your device is when you clock in or use a map, and only when your device allows it. You can turn this off in your device's settings.
  • Customer and job details. Names, job addresses, contact details, signatures, and what was billed and paid.
  • Sign-in and device records. When you signed in, your IP address and the kind of browser you used.

Card payments are taken by our payment company on its own pages. ClickTooling does not see or keep a card number.

Signing in with Google

You can sign in to your ClickTooling account with Google. When you do, Google tells us four things:

  • your name,
  • your email address,
  • your profile picture,
  • the id Google gives your account.

We use these for one thing: to sign you in to the ClickTooling account that already exists under that email address. Signing in with Google does not let us read your mail, your calendar, your contacts or your files.

We do not sell what Google tells us. We do not use it for advertising. We do not use it to train AI models. We share it with nobody except the companies that run sign-in for us, named below.

Our use of what we receive from Google follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use rules.

To stop using Google with ClickTooling, ask our office to take Google off your login. You can also remove ClickTooling in your Google Account, under Security, then your connections to third-party apps.

What we use it for

  • To run the work: schedule it, price it, bill it, pay for it and keep its records.
  • To sign you in and keep your account safe.
  • To send you notices about your work by email, text or a notice on your phone.
  • To keep the records the law asks a business to keep, such as pay and lien records.

ClickTooling shows no ads and carries no advertising trackers.

Who else sees it

We do not sell personal information. We share it only in these ways:

  • Companies that run parts of ClickTooling for us. Supabase keeps the database and runs sign-in. GitHub hosts the site. Google provides sign-in, mail, file storage and maps. Stripe takes payments. Other companies send our email, texts and letters, and read documents such as bills and plans for us. Each one handles the information only to do that job.
  • People on the same work. Your team sees the work records your role lets them see. A customer or a builder sees what we send them.
  • When the law requires it, such as a court order.

What stays in your browser

ClickTooling keeps your sign-in and a copy of the app in your browser's storage. That is how you stay signed in and how the app opens with a weak signal. Signing out or clearing the site's data removes them.

How long we keep it

We keep account details while the account is open. We keep work records for as long as the business and the law need them, which can be years after a job ends.

What you can ask for

  • A copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • A correction of something that is wrong.
  • A deletion. We will delete what we are free to delete, and tell you what we must keep and why.

Write to the address at the bottom of this page. We answer within 30 days.

How we protect it

Information travels encrypted between your device and ClickTooling. What each account can see is set by its role. No system is perfectly safe, so tell us right away if you think your account was used by someone else.

Children

ClickTooling is a work tool for adults. It is not for anyone under 18.

Changes to this page

When this page changes, the date at the top changes with it. A large change is also told to account holders.
