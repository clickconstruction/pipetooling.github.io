## Why it matters
Five mistakes, each one a lesson in this course on its worst day. Each is drawn beside the right way. If you can name the five, the course has done its job.

## Must know
- A jumper left off. The meter went back with no jumper, and the ground path has a gap. Lesson 8.
- {{figure:plumber-mistakes|A jumper left off, a plastic section, a cord with no GFCI, a cover off live, a drilled cable. Each beside the right way.}}
- A metal to plastic change with no word to the electrician. The ground stops at the plastic and nobody knows. Lesson 23.
- A tool on a cord on a wet floor with no GFCI. The person is the only path left. Lesson 22.
- A cover off with the breaker on. Live terminals at hand height on a water heater. Lesson 24.
- A drilled cable. A hole or a screw through a cable in a stud, and an arc behind the drywall. Lesson 28.
- The right way for each is one step. The jumper back on, the call, the GFCI, the breaker off and tested dead, the look first.
- Every one of the five is a plumber's mistake, not an electrician's. They happen on our jobs with our hands.
- None of the five needs a licence to avoid. They need the one step, every time.
- When one of the five has already happened, the fix is a call. Not a quiet repair.

## Traps
- A small job is not a safe job. Four of the five happen on a one-hour call.
- A mistake nobody saw is not a mistake that went away. The gap in the ground is still there next year.
- Knowing the five is not the same as doing the step. The step is the point.

## Numbers
- **the jumper** goes back on
- **the plastic section** gets a word to the electrician
- **the GFCI** on every cord
- **the breaker** off and tested dead before the cover
- **the cable** behind the drywall, looked for before the hole

## Check yourself
1. The meter went back in with no jumper. What is the right way?
- [ ] Leave it, the rod is enough
- [x] The jumper goes back on, and the electrician hears if it is missing
- [ ] Wrap the meter in tape
- [ ] Turn the water off
> right: The jumper is the path round the gap. It goes back.
> wrong: A jumper left off is a gap in the ground. It goes back on, or the electrician is called.

2. A metal section is being replaced with plastic. What is the one step?
- [ ] Use a longer section
- [x] A word to the electrician before the change
- [ ] Add a second clamp
- [ ] Nothing, plastic is safe
> right: The electrician decides what replaces the lost ground path.
> wrong: Plastic breaks the ground path. The electrician hears before the change.

3. A cover is coming off a water heater. What is the one step before it?
- [ ] The water off
- [ ] A glove on
- [x] The breaker off and the circuit tested dead
- [ ] The thermostat down
> right: Off and tested dead, every time, under the company's rule.
> wrong: No cover comes off live. The breaker goes off and the tester proves it.

4. One of the five has already happened on a job. What is the fix?
- [ ] A quiet repair
- [ ] Nothing, if nobody was hurt
- [x] A call
- [ ] A note in the truck
> right: A mistake near electrical is a call, not a quiet repair.
> wrong: When one of the five has happened, the fix is a call to the electrician or the office.
