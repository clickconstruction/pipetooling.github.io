## Why it matters
When a plumber's work needs a circuit off, someone opens the breaker, locks it and tags it. The plumber's part is to check the lock, test the circuit dead, and never touch anyone else's lock.

## Must know
- Lockout is a lock on the breaker so it cannot be turned back on. Tagout is a tag that says who locked it and why.
- {{figure:lockout-tag|The breaker is off, locked and tagged with a name. The plumber tests the circuit dead. Only the name on the tag takes the lock off.}}
- The electrician or the owner opens the breaker, locks it and tags it. That person is the one who takes the lock off.
- The plumber checks the lock is on and the tag has a name. Then the circuit is tested dead at the work.
- A plumber never cuts, removes or works around another person's lock. Not even to go home.
- A plumber working under a lock puts their own lock on too. A hasp, a clip with holes for several locks, holds more than one.
- The lock comes off only when the person who put it on takes it off. If they have gone, the office finds them.
- A tag alone is not a lock. A tag can be ignored by a stranger. A lock cannot.
- The water heater's breaker on a long job gets a lock. Nobody can turn it back on with the covers off.
- On the company's own jobs the owner says who locks and who unlocks. That is the company's rule.

## Traps
- A tag with no lock is a request, not a guarantee. Someone can flip the breaker past it.
- Tape over a breaker is not a lock. It is a mistake waiting for a hand.
- A lock on the panel door is not a lock on the breaker. The breaker itself takes the lock.

## Numbers
- **the one who locks it** is the one who unlocks it
- **a tag** says who and why
- **test dead** after the lock

- [x] Test the circuit dead at the work

> right: The lock is step one. The tester at the work is step two.

3. Is tape over a breaker a lockout?

- [ ] Yes, if the tag is on the door
> right: Tape stops nobody. A lock does.
> wrong: Tape, labels and door locks are not lockout. The breaker itself takes a lock.

4. Another trade's lock is on the breaker you need, and they have left. What now?
- [ ] Cut the lock
- [ ] Flip the breaker and relock it
- [x] Leave it, and the office finds them
- [ ] Wait until dark and cut it
> right: Nobody removes another person's lock. The office reaches them.
> wrong: A lock is never cut or worked around. The person who put it on takes it off.
