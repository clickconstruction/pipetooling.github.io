## Why it matters
A switch is the simplest break in the loop. It breaks one wire, the hot, and leaves the other side live. Know which side is which before you trust a dark light.

## Must know
- A switch breaks the hot wire only. The neutral runs on past it.
- {{figure:switch-and-light|The hot runs to the switch, then up the switch leg to the light, and back on the neutral. The box stays live on the line side when the switch is off.}}
- The wire from the panel to the switch is the line side. It is live whenever the breaker is on.
- The wire from the switch to the light is the switch leg. It is live only when the switch is on.
- Off at the switch leaves the box live on the line side. A dark light proves nothing.
- A three-way is two switches on one light. Either one changes it, so neither one is a true off.
- A dimmer is a switch that lets part of the current through. It is still live on its line side.
- LED does the same light for a tenth of the watts. An old 60 W bulb is a 9 W LED.
- A light in a wet place, a shower or outdoors, has a rating for it. The fixture says so.
- A ceiling fan on a switch has a motor. It can start when someone else flips the switch.

## Traps
- A switch off is not the circuit off. The breaker is the only off, and it is tested after.
- A three-way switch has no up and down. Up can be off and down can be on.
- A dark bulb can be a dead bulb. The socket can still be live.

## Numbers
- **the hot** is the wire it breaks
- **2** switches in a three-way
- **line side live** with the switch off
- **60 W** an old bulb, 9 W the LED that matches it

## Check yourself
1. Which wire does a switch break?
- [x] The hot
- [ ] The neutral
- [ ] The ground
- [ ] All three
> right: Only the hot. The neutral runs past the switch.
> wrong: A switch breaks the hot only. The neutral and the ground are not switched.

2. With a switch off, which side of it is still live?
- [ ] The switch leg
- [x] The line side
- [ ] Neither side
- [ ] The light
> right: The line side comes from the panel and stays live.
> wrong: The line side is live whenever the breaker is on. Off only kills the switch leg.

3. In a three-way, how many switches control the light?
- [ ] One
- [x] Two
- [ ] Three
- [ ] One switch and one dimmer
> right: Two switches, either one changes the light.
> wrong: A three-way is two switches on one light. The name counts the wires, not the switches.

4. Which LED matches an old 60 W bulb?
- [ ] 60 W
- [ ] 30 W
- [x] 9 W
- [ ] 1 W
> right: About a tenth of the watts for the same light.
> wrong: A 9 W LED gives the light of a 60 W bulb.
