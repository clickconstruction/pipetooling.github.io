## Why it matters
Two pumps start on their own with nobody in the room. One sits in a pit of water and the other sits down a well. Both are a plumbing job with a motor in it.

## Must know
- A sump pump is a 120 V motor in a pit. A float switch, a ball that rises with the water, starts it.
- {{figure:pumps-start-alone|The sump starts when the float lifts. The well starts when the pressure drops. Neither waits for a person.}}
- The sump plugs into its own outlet near the pit. The outlet is a GFCI in most code cycles, and the lessons give the company's rule.
- A sump can start while a hand is in the pit. The plug is pulled before the pit is touched.
- A well pump is often a 240 V motor down the well, on its own two-pole breaker.
- A pressure switch reads the tank's pressure and starts the well pump when it drops to its low setting. Often 30 psi on and 50 psi off.
- A control box on the wall, holding the motor's starting parts, runs some well pumps. It is live, and it is the electrician's.
- The well pump's wire runs down the well with the pipe. Pulling the pipe pulls the wire.
- Both pumps start by themselves. A pump that is quiet is a pump that is about to run.
- A pump that hums and does not pump is a motor that is stuck or a float that is stuck. The breaker goes off before anyone looks.

## Traps
- A quiet pump is not an off pump. The float or the pressure switch can start it any second.
- A well's control box is not a box of plain wiring. Nothing in it is the plumber's.
- A sump's cord is often wet. A GFCI outlet is the only thing between that cord and a person.

## Numbers
- **120 V** the sump
- **240 V** the well, often
- **starts by itself** with nobody there

## Check yourself
1. What starts a sump pump?
- [ ] A wall switch
- [x] The float lifting
- [ ] The pressure switch
- [ ] The dishwasher
> right: The float rises with the water and closes the switch.
> wrong: A sump starts on its float. Nobody has to be there.

2. What starts a well pump?
- [ ] The float
- [ ] A timer
- [x] The tank pressure dropping to the switch's low setting
- [ ] The water heater
> right: The pressure switch closes when the tank drops to its low number.
> wrong: A well pump starts on pressure. When the tank drops to about 30 psi, it runs.

3. Before a hand goes into a sump pit, what happens?
- [ ] The float is tied up
- [x] The plug is pulled
- [ ] The switch is turned off
- [ ] The water is bailed out
> right: Pulling the plug is the only off a sump has.
> wrong: A sump has no switch. The plug comes out before a hand goes in.

4. Whose is the well's control box?
- [ ] The plumber's
- [ ] The homeowner's
- [x] The electrician's
- [ ] The utility's
> right: It is a live control box, and nothing in it is the plumber's.
> wrong: The control box is live and it is the electrician's.
