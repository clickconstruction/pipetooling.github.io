## Why it matters
One box takes the three wires and splits them into every circuit in the house. The one big breaker at its top is the nearest thing to an off switch the house has. It still leaves part of the box live.

## Must know
- The main panel is one box. The three service wires land in it and every circuit leaves from it.
- The main breaker sits at the top. It shuts off everything below it in the panel.
- The lugs above the main breaker are where the service wires land. They are live with the main off.
- Two bus bars run down the panel. The hots land on them, and every branch breaker clips onto them.
- A branch breaker takes power from a bus bar and feeds one circuit. Its number is its amps.
- A neutral bar holds every white wire. A ground bar holds every bare or green wire.
- A subpanel is a second box fed by one breaker in the main panel. That breaker is its real off switch.
- A panel's cover is screwed on for a reason. With it off, the bus bars are bare and live.
- The space in front of a panel stays clear. Nothing stored there, nothing leaning on it.

## Traps
- The main off is not the panel dead. The lugs above it stay live until the utility pulls the meter.
- A subpanel's own main breaker does not kill the feed to the subpanel. The breaker in the main panel does.
- A panel in a bathroom or a clothes closet is a code problem. The inspector will see it.

## Numbers
- **1** main breaker
- **100 to 200 A** a common house
- **the lugs above the main** stay live with the main off
- **a subpanel's real off** is its breaker in the main panel
- **36 in** clear in front of a panel

## Check yourself
1. What does the main breaker shut off?
- [x] Everything below it in the panel
- [ ] The meter
- [ ] The wires from the pole
- [ ] The whole house, lugs included
> right: Below it, every circuit goes dead. Above it, the lugs stay live.
> wrong: The main kills what is below it. The lugs above it and the meter stay live.

2. What is a subpanel's real off switch?
- [ ] Its own cover
- [ ] The meter
- [x] The breaker that feeds it in the main panel
- [ ] The nearest light switch
> right: One breaker in the main panel feeds the subpanel. That is where it goes off.
> wrong: A subpanel is fed by one breaker in the main panel, and that breaker is its off.

3. What do the two bus bars carry?
- [ ] The neutral and the ground
- [x] The two hots
- [ ] Water and gas
- [ ] The telephone and the cable
> right: Each hot lands on a bus bar and the breakers clip onto them.
> wrong: The bus bars are the two hots. Neutral and ground have bars of their own.

4. How much clear space stays in front of a panel?
- [ ] 12 in
- [ ] 24 in
- [x] 36 in
- [ ] None, a shelf can sit there
> right: Three feet clear in front, so a person can open it and step back.
> wrong: The code asks for 36 in clear in front of a panel. Nothing stored there.
