## Why it matters
A whole second world of wiring runs at 24 V. Thermostats, doorbells, sprinkler controllers and the gas water heater's valve. It cannot hurt you. The box that makes it can.

## Must know
- Low voltage is 24 V or less. It comes from a transformer that steps 120 V down.
- {{figure:low-voltage-world|A transformer turns 120 V into 24 V for the thermostat, the doorbell and the gas valve. The 24 V side is safe. The 120 V side of the transformer is not.}}
- The 24 V side will not hurt you. It is the thermostat, the doorbell, the sprinkler controller and the gas valve.
- The 120 V side of the transformer is a normal live box. It is wherever the transformer is mounted.
- A gas water heater's valve runs on 24 V from its own small transformer or control board.
- A standing-pilot gas heater uses a thermocouple. It makes its own tiny current from the pilot's heat and needs no wire from the house.
- A power-vent gas heater is different. Its blower is 120 V on a cord, and lesson 15's rule holds for it.
- Low voltage wire is thin and often in a bundle. Cutting it stops a thermostat or a sprinkler, not a person.
- A doorbell transformer is often in the attic, the basement or on the panel. Its 120 V side is live.
- A smart water shutoff runs on low voltage or a plug. Its valve is the plumber's and its wiring is not.

## Traps
- 24 V is not a reason to touch the transformer. The other side of it is 120 V.
- A thermostat wire that sparks is a short on the 24 V side. It blows a small fuse, not a person.
- Low voltage is not no voltage. A sprinkler controller's valves still open on it.

## Numbers
- **24 V** the usual low voltage
- **a transformer** makes it from 120 V
- **thermostat, doorbell, sprinkler, gas valve** run on it
- **the transformer's box** is 120 V
- **the thermocouple** makes its own

## Check yourself
1. What makes 24 V in a house?
- [ ] The meter
- [x] A transformer, from 120 V
- [ ] The ground rod
- [ ] A battery in the thermostat
> right: A transformer steps 120 V down to 24 V.
> wrong: Low voltage comes from a transformer. Its other side is 120 V.

2. Which side of a doorbell transformer is live at 120 V?
- [ ] The bell wires
- [x] The box it is mounted on, where the 120 V feeds it
- [ ] Neither side
- [ ] The button at the door
> right: The 24 V side is safe. The feed side is a normal live box.
> wrong: The transformer's feed side is 120 V. The bell wires are the safe side.

3. What does a thermocouple do?
- [ ] Steps 120 V down to 24 V
- [x] Makes its own tiny current from the pilot's heat
- [ ] Starts the power-vent blower
- [ ] Resets the high-limit
> right: The pilot's heat makes the current that holds the gas valve open.
> wrong: A thermocouple makes its own current from heat. It needs no wire from the house.

4. Which of these runs on low voltage?
- [ ] The dishwasher
- [x] The thermostat
- [ ] The water heater's element
- [ ] The sump pump
> right: The thermostat is 24 V from a transformer.
> wrong: The thermostat is low voltage. The dishwasher, the element and the sump are 120 V or 240 V.
