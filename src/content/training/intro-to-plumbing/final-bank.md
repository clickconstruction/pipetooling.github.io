## Questions
1. Which system has no pump and runs downhill?
- [ ] The supply
- [x] The drain
- [ ] The hot water line
- [ ] The manifold
> right: The drain carries used water out by gravity.
> wrong: The drain runs by gravity. The supply is the one under pressure.

2. What keeps sewer gas out of the house at every fixture?
- [ ] The vent
- [ ] The angle stop
- [x] The water held in the trap
- [ ] The air gap
> right: The trap's water is the seal.
> wrong: The plug of water in the trap under the fixture is the seal against sewer gas.

3. Which pipe is big and sloped, the supply or the drain?
- [ ] The supply
- [x] The drain
- [ ] Both
- [ ] Neither
> right: Drain pipe is big and runs at a slope. Supply pipe is small and strong.
> wrong: The drain is the big sloped one. The supply is small and under pressure.

4. What does the meter at the curb do?
- [x] Counts every gallon that enters the house
- [ ] Lowers the pressure
- [ ] Heats the water
- [ ] Stops backflow
> right: The meter counts the water. The valve beside it is the city's.
> wrong: The meter counts gallons. It does not change pressure or stop anything.

5. What stops every faucet, toilet and the water heater at once?
- [ ] An angle stop
- [ ] The T&P valve
- [x] The main shutoff
- [ ] The vacuum breaker
> right: One valve at the house stops everything.
> wrong: The main shutoff on the service line stops the whole house.

6. What range of pressure feels good at the shower and is kind to the pipes?
- [ ] 10 to 20 psi
- [x] 40 to 60 psi
- [ ] 80 to 100 psi
- [ ] 120 psi and up
> right: 40 to 60 psi is the comfortable range.
> wrong: The comfortable range is 40 to 60 psi. Under 40 is weak and over 80 needs a PRV.

7. What sets the house side of a PRV?
- [ ] The meter
- [x] The spring on top
- [ ] The water heater
- [ ] The size of the pipe
> right: Turning the spring's screw changes the house pressure.
> wrong: The spring on the PRV sets the pressure it lets through.

8. About how much pressure is lost going up one floor?
- [ ] None
- [x] About 4 psi
- [ ] About 20 psi
- [ ] About 40 psi
> right: Every 2.31 ft costs 1 psi, and a floor is about 10 ft.
> wrong: A floor is about 10 ft, and 2.31 ft costs 1 psi, so about 4 psi.

9. What does the dip tube in a water heater do?
- [x] Carries cold water to the bottom of the tank
- [ ] Carries hot water to the top
- [ ] Drains the tank
- [ ] Vents the burner
> right: Cold goes in at the top and the dip tube takes it to the bottom to be heated.
> wrong: The dip tube drops the cold water to the bottom of the tank.

10. What temperature should a water heater be set to?
- [ ] 100°F
- [x] 120°F
- [ ] 150°F
- [ ] 180°F
> right: 120°F is hot enough and does not scald.
> wrong: The set point is 120°F. Hotter scalds and wastes fuel.

11. What happens to water when it is heated?
- [ ] It shrinks
- [x] It grows and needs room
- [ ] It gets lighter and leaves the tank
- [ ] Nothing
> right: Heated water takes more room. That is why a closed system needs an expansion tank.
> wrong: Water grows when heated. An expansion tank gives it room in a closed system.

12. What is the common size of a branch line to a fixture?
- [x] 1/2 in
- [ ] 3/4 in
- [ ] 1 in
- [ ] 2 in
> right: Branches and manifold lines are 1/2 in.
> wrong: The branch to a fixture is 1/2 in. The trunk is 3/4 in.

13. Which copper type is used for underground service lines?
- [ ] Type M
- [ ] Type L
- [x] Type K
- [ ] Type P
> right: Type K is the thick one and goes underground.
> wrong: Underground lines use the thick-walled type K.

14. What must both faces of a copper joint be before soldering?
- [ ] Wet
- [ ] Painted
- [x] Cleaned bright and brushed with flux
- [ ] Heated red
> right: Clean bright metal and flux let the solder flow.
> wrong: Both faces are cleaned bright and fluxed. Dirt or water in the joint means a leak.

15. Which pipe can turn a corner with no fitting at all?
- [ ] Copper
- [x] PEX
- [ ] CPVC
- [ ] Cast iron
> right: PEX bends.
> wrong: PEX is the one that bends around a corner. The others need an elbow.

16. What does the ring in a crimp joint do?
- [x] Squeezes the pipe down onto the barbed fitting
- [ ] Glues the pipe to the fitting
- [ ] Expands the pipe
- [ ] Marks the hot line
> right: The ring is crimped down over the pipe and holds it on the barbs.
> wrong: A crimp ring squeezes PEX onto the barbed fitting. It is the expansion joint that stretches.

17. Why push a glued joint in with a quarter turn?
- [ ] To tighten the threads
- [x] To spread the cement with no dry line
- [ ] To line up the markings
- [ ] It is not needed
> right: The turn spreads the cement all the way round.
> wrong: The quarter turn spreads the cement so no dry line is left to weep.

18. Which valve must be all the way open or all the way shut?
- [ ] The ball valve
- [x] The gate valve
- [ ] The check valve
- [ ] The angle stop
> right: A gate valve half open wears and sticks.
> wrong: The gate valve is for on or off only. Half open, it wears out.

19. What stops house water from flowing back to the street?
- [ ] The meter
- [ ] The PRV
- [x] A check valve on the service line
- [ ] The main shutoff
> right: The check valve's flap shuts when flow reverses.
> wrong: A check valve on the service line lets water in and shuts against it going back.

20. Which fixture takes only cold water?
- [ ] The sink
- [x] The toilet
- [ ] The tub
- [ ] The shower
> right: A toilet has one supply and one angle stop.
> wrong: The toilet is cold only. The others take hot and cold.

21. What can a hose left in a pool do without a vacuum breaker?
- [ ] Nothing
- [ ] Raise the house pressure
- [x] Pull pool water into the drinking lines when pressure drops
- [ ] Freeze the bib
> right: A drop in pressure sucks the pool back up the hose. The breaker stops it.
> wrong: Without a breaker, a pressure drop pulls the pool water back into the house lines.

22. Which fitting joins two pipes of the same size end to end and never comes apart?
- [x] A coupling
- [ ] A union
- [ ] A tee
- [ ] A cap
> right: A coupling is permanent. The union is the one that opens.
> wrong: The coupling joins for good. A union joins and comes apart.

23. Which fitting changes a big pipe to a small one?
- [ ] An adapter
- [ ] A coupling
- [x] A reducer
- [ ] A 45
> right: The reducer steps the size down.
> wrong: A reducer joins a big pipe to a small one. An adapter changes the kind of joint.

24. Where does the water heater's cold supply line connect?
- [x] At the top of the tank
- [ ] At the bottom of the tank
- [ ] At the T&P valve
- [ ] At the burner
> right: Cold connects at the top and the dip tube carries it down.
> wrong: The cold line enters at the top. The dip tube takes the water to the bottom.
