## Why it matters
The turn the water makes picks the fitting. A drop can be short. A horizontal turn must be long. And every fitting faces downstream, or it is a clog in waiting.

## Must know
- A combo is a wye and an eighth bend in one fitting. It joins a branch and turns it into the run in one piece.
- {{figure:sweeps-and-direction|A long sweep turns a horizontal run. A combo joins and turns in one fitting. Every fitting points the way the water goes.}}
- A long sweep 90 turns a horizontal drain. Its wide curve keeps the water and the solids together.
- Two 45s do the same job as a long sweep, with a short straight piece between them.
- A short sweep is for a drop, where the water turns down into a vertical pipe.
- Where a stack lands on the building drain, the turn is a long sweep or two 45s. The water is moving fast.
- Every drain fitting points downstream. A combo or a wye is set so the branch enters with the flow.
- A street fitting has one end that fits inside another fitting. It saves a stub where room is tight.
- A fitting with the wrong sweep is a clog waiting, and a cable that cannot pass.

## Traps
- A quarter bend on a horizontal run is a wall for solids. Horizontal turns take a long sweep.
- A combo is not a wye with a 90 glued on. The eighth bend is what makes the merge gentle.
- A fitting can be the right shape and still face the wrong way. Check the direction before the glue.

## Numbers
- **long sweep** on every horizontal turn
- **two 45s** do the same
- **a combo** is a wye and an eighth bend
- **short sweep** only into a drop

## Check yourself
1. What is a combo?
- [ ] Two 45s glued together
- [x] A wye and an eighth bend in one fitting
- [ ] A tee with a cleanout
- [ ] A long sweep with a branch
> right: The wye joins the branch and the eighth bend turns it, in one piece.
> wrong: A combo is one fitting made of a wye and an eighth bend.

2. What turns a horizontal drain around a corner?
- [ ] A short sweep
- [ ] A sanitary tee
- [x] A long sweep, or two 45s
- [ ] A coupling
> right: The long curve keeps the water and the solids together.
> wrong: Horizontal turns take a long sweep or two 45s. A short turn stalls solids.

3. Where is a short sweep allowed?
- [x] Where the water drops into a vertical pipe
- [ ] On any horizontal turn
- [ ] Only on supply lines
- [ ] Nowhere
> right: Falling water does not need the long curve. A drop can turn short.
> wrong: A short sweep only goes where the water drops into a vertical pipe.

4. Which way does a drain fitting's branch point?
- [ ] Toward the fixture
- [x] Downstream, with the flow
- [ ] Up
- [ ] It does not matter
> right: The branch enters with the flow. Against it, the fitting catches solids.
> wrong: Every drain fitting faces downstream. The branch joins with the flow.
