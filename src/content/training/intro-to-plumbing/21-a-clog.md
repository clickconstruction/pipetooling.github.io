## Why it matters
Water seeks the easiest way out. When the drain is blocked, that way is the lowest open fixture. Reading where the water shows up tells you where the clog is.

## Must know
- When the drain is blocked, water backs up and comes out at the lowest open fixture.
- {{figure:clog-strip|A clog backs water up to the lowest open fixture. Water seeks the easiest way out.}}
- A clog below two fixtures shows at both. A tub fills when the toilet is flushed because the tub is lower.
- A clog in one fixture's trap stops only that fixture. A clog in the building drain backs up the whole house.
- The lowest fixture, a tub, a shower or a floor drain, is the first to show a main line clog.
- The cleanout is the door in. A cable goes through the cleanout and follows the pipe to the clog.
- Chemicals sit on the clog and burn the pipe and the plumber. A cable or a plunger clears it.
- A plunger pushes and pulls water, not air. Cover the overflow and keep water over the cup.
- A clog that comes back is a belly, a root or a broken pipe. A camera finds which.

## Traps
- Flushing again does not clear a clog. It adds a gallon and a half to the floor.
- A tub that fills when the toilet flushes is a main line clog, not a tub clog.
- A slow drain is a clog forming. Clear it now, before it is a flood.

## Numbers
- **the lowest fixture** shows it first
- **the cleanout** is the door in
- **a cable** clears it, never chemicals
- **a camera** finds a clog that comes back

## Check yourself
1. Where does a main line clog show first?
- [ ] The highest fixture
- [x] The lowest fixture
- [ ] The water heater
- [ ] The roof vent
> right: Backed-up water finds the lowest open drain.
> wrong: Water backs up to the lowest fixture first, a tub or a floor drain.

2. The tub fills when the toilet is flushed. Where is the clog?
- [ ] In the tub's trap
- [ ] In the toilet's trap
- [x] Below both, in the main line
- [ ] In the vent
> right: The toilet's water had nowhere to go but up the tub's drain. The block is below both.
> wrong: Water from one fixture showing at another means the clog is below both of them.

3. Where does a cable go in?
- [ ] The vent on the roof
- [x] The cleanout
- [ ] The water meter
- [ ] The trap's plug
> right: The cleanout is the capped door made for the cable.
> wrong: The cable goes in at the cleanout and follows the pipe.

4. Why not pour chemicals on a clog?
- [ ] They are too slow
- [x] They sit on the clog and burn the pipe and the plumber
- [ ] They only work on hair
- [ ] The code requires a permit
> right: The chemical stays where the clog is. The pipe and the next hand in it take the burn.
> wrong: Chemicals do not clear the clog. They sit on it and burn what they touch.
