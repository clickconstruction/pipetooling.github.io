## Why it matters
The inspector reads a gauge or a water level, not your word. The tests are simple, and a system that passes them is a system that will not leak inside a wall.

## Must know
- The inspector reads a gauge or a water level, never your word.
- {{figure:inspector-tests|The drains hold a 10-foot head of water for 15 minutes. The supply holds pressure. The inspector reads the gauge.}}
- The drain and vent system is filled with water to **10 ft** above the highest joint. It must hold **15 min** with no drop.
- Or the drains are tested with air at **5 psi** for 15 min. The gauge must not move.
- The supply is tested at the working pressure, or with air at **50 psi** for 15 min.
- Test plugs and caps close every opening. A test ball in the cleanout holds the water in the stack.
- At the final, every fixture runs, every trap holds, and the T&P drain line runs where it should.
- The inspector also looks. Slope, hangers, cleanouts, the vent through the roof, fittings facing the right way.
- A failed test is a leak somewhere. Find it, fix it and call again. Nothing closes until it passes.

## Traps
- A water test is not a pressure test. The 10 ft head is under 5 psi, but it finds the leaks a drain will see.
- A cap left on after the test is a plug in the line. Count your test caps.
- Passing the test is not passing the inspection. The inspector still walks the slope and the fittings.

## Numbers
- **10 ft** of water on the drains for 15 min
- **5 psi** air on the drains instead
- **50 psi** air on the supply
- **every trap** holds at the final

## Check yourself
1. How is the drain and vent system water tested?
- [ ] Run every faucet for a minute
- [x] Filled to a 10 ft head and held 15 min with no drop
- [ ] Pressurised to 50 psi
- [ ] Smelled for gas
> right: Ten feet of water above the top joint, fifteen minutes, no drop.
> wrong: The drains are filled to a 10 ft head for 15 min. A drop is a leak.

2. What air pressure tests the drains?
- [ ] 1 psi
- [x] 5 psi
- [ ] 50 psi
- [ ] 100 psi
> right: Five psi for fifteen minutes, and the gauge must not move.
> wrong: Drains are air tested at 5 psi. The supply takes 50.

3. What air pressure tests the supply?
- [ ] 5 psi
- [ ] 20 psi
- [x] 50 psi
- [ ] 200 psi
> right: Fifty psi for fifteen minutes, or the working pressure with water.
> wrong: The supply is air tested at 50 psi. Five psi is the drains.

4. What happens when a test fails?
- [ ] The inspector passes it anyway
- [x] Find the leak, fix it and call again
- [ ] Add more water
- [ ] Close the walls and try at trim
> right: A failed test is a leak. Nothing closes until it holds.
> wrong: A failed test means a leak to find and fix. The walls stay open until it passes.
