## Why it matters
Every fitting does one thing to the water. Once you know the eight shapes, you can read a supply run on sight and name what is missing.

## Must know
- A **90** turns the water a quarter turn, a corner.
- A **45** turns the water an eighth of a turn. Two 45s make a gentler corner than one 90.
- {{figure:supply-fittings|Each fitting does one thing to the water. It turns it, splits it, joins it, shrinks it, ends it or changes its material.}}
- A **tee** splits the water into two lines, or joins two lines into one.
- A **coupling** joins two pipes of the same size end to end.
- A **union** joins two pipes and comes apart again with a nut. It is the one fitting you can open without cutting.
- A **reducer** changes the size, from a big pipe to a small one.
- A **cap** ends a line. A plug does the same inside a threaded fitting.
- An **adapter** changes the joint, from glue or solder to a thread, or from one material to another.
- A street fitting has one end that fits inside another fitting instead of over a pipe. It saves a short stub.

## Traps
- A coupling cannot be taken apart. If a part will ever need to come out, put a union beside it.
- A tee lying on its side sends water sideways. Its branch must point where the branch line goes.
- A reducer is not a bushing. A bushing sits inside a fitting to shrink the hole, a reducer joins two pipe sizes.

## Numbers
- **90** a quarter turn
- **45** an eighth of a turn
- **tee** splits the line
- **union** the one that comes apart

## Check yourself
1. Which fitting can you open without cutting the pipe?
- [ ] A coupling
- [x] A union
- [ ] A reducer
- [ ] A cap
> right: The union's nut comes apart. Everything else is cut out.
> wrong: Only a union opens. A coupling is permanent.

2. What does a tee do?
- [ ] Turns the water a corner
- [x] Splits one line into two, or joins two into one
- [ ] Ends the line
- [ ] Changes the size
> right: The branch leaves or joins at the tee.
> wrong: A tee splits or joins. The 90 and the 45 turn.

3. How much does a 45 turn the water?
- [ ] A quarter turn
- [x] An eighth of a turn
- [ ] A half turn
- [ ] It does not turn
> right: Two 45s make the same corner as one 90, more gently.
> wrong: A 45 is an eighth of a turn. The 90 is the quarter.

4. What is an adapter for?
- [ ] Shrinking the pipe
- [ ] Ending the line
- [x] Changing the kind of joint, such as glue to thread
- [ ] Turning a corner
> right: It joins one kind of end to another.
> wrong: An adapter changes the joint, from a glued or soldered end to a thread or another material.
