## Why it matters
Three valves do three jobs. One stops water fast. One stops it slowly and sticks. One lets water go one way only. Knowing which is which saves a wrong turn.

## Must know
- A ball valve has a ball with a hole through it. A quarter turn opens or shuts it.
- {{figure:ball-gate-check|A ball valve turns a quarter. A gate valve winds a gate down. A check valve swings shut when flow turns around.}}
- A ball valve's handle tells you its state. Along the pipe is open. Across the pipe is shut.
- A gate valve winds a wedge down across the pipe. It takes **many turns** and must go all the way open or shut.
- A gate valve left half open wears out and sticks. It is for on or off, never for throttling.
- A check valve has a flap that swings open with the flow and shuts when the flow turns around. Water goes **one way**.
- A check valve on the service line stops house water flowing back to the street.
- A PRV is a valve too. Its spring is set to about **50 to 60 psi** for a house.
- New work uses ball valves for shutoffs. Gate valves are what you find in older houses.

## Traps
- A gate valve handle that spins and spins may have lost its gate. The valve looks shut and is open.
- A ball valve handle across the pipe is shut even if water is still draining past it.
- A check valve does not stop water. It only stops it going backwards.

## Numbers
- **a quarter turn** the ball valve
- **many turns** the gate valve
- **one way** the check valve
- **50 to 60 psi** a PRV's usual setting

## Check yourself
1. How do you know a ball valve is open?
- [x] The handle runs along the pipe
- [ ] The handle runs across the pipe
- [ ] It makes a sound
- [ ] You count the turns
> right: Along is open. Across is shut.
> wrong: A ball valve's handle lines up with the pipe when open and crosses it when shut.

2. Why should a gate valve never be left half open?
- [ ] It leaks at the stem
- [x] The half-open wedge wears and the valve sticks
- [ ] It raises the pressure
- [ ] It is against the code
> right: The gate is for all the way open or all the way shut. In between it wears out.
> wrong: A gate valve throttling flow wears its wedge and then sticks. Open it or shut it fully.

3. What does a check valve do?
- [ ] Stops all flow
- [ ] Lowers the pressure
- [x] Lets water flow one way and shuts against the other
- [ ] Counts the gallons
> right: The flap opens with the flow and shuts when it reverses.
> wrong: A check valve only stops backwards flow. Forward flow passes.

4. Which valve do new shutoffs use?
- [ ] Gate valves
- [x] Ball valves
- [ ] Check valves
- [ ] PRVs
> right: A ball valve shuts fast with a quarter turn and does not stick.
> wrong: New work uses ball valves. Gate valves are the older kind.
