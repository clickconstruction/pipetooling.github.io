## Why it matters
Water grows when it freezes. A pipe full of ice has nowhere to put that growth, so it splits. The flood comes later, when the ice melts and the split opens.

## Must know
- Water grows when it freezes, about **9%**. A pipe full of ice splits.
- {{figure:freeze-strip|Ice grows along the pipe and splits it. The leak shows when it thaws.}}
- The split does not leak while it is frozen. The flood comes with the thaw.
- Pipes in outside walls, attics, crawl spaces and garages freeze first. So does a hose bib with a hose left on.
- Water freezes at **32°F**. A night at **20°F** or below is the one to worry about, and wind makes it worse.
- Moving water freezes slower. A drip at the farthest faucet keeps the line moving through the night.
- Open the cabinet doors under a sink on an outside wall. The room's heat reaches the pipe.
- A frozen pipe is thawed slowly with warm air, never a torch. Open the faucet first so the melt can move.
- Insulation slows freezing but does not stop it. Heat tape stops it, where it is plugged in.

## Traps
- The leak shows when the pipe thaws, not when it froze. A dry house at 20°F is not safe yet.
- A torch on a frozen pipe boils the water inside and bursts it. Warm air, slowly.
- A faucet that gives nothing on a cold morning is frozen somewhere. Shut the main before it thaws, then find it.

## Numbers
- **32°F** water freezes
- **9%** bigger as ice
- **20°F** the night to worry about
- **a drip** keeps it moving

## Check yourself
1. Why does a frozen pipe split?
- [ ] The cold makes the metal brittle
- [x] Ice takes more room than water, about 9% more
- [ ] The pressure from the street rises
- [ ] The water boils
> right: The ice has to go somewhere, and the pipe wall is what gives.
> wrong: Ice is about 9% bigger than the water it came from. That growth splits the pipe.

2. When does a frozen pipe leak?
- [ ] The moment it freezes
- [x] When it thaws
- [ ] Only in the spring
- [ ] Never, it just stops
> right: The ice plugs its own split. The melt opens it.
> wrong: A frozen pipe leaks when it thaws. Shut the main before then.

3. What keeps a line from freezing on a cold night?
- [ ] Shutting the main
- [x] A drip at the farthest faucet
- [ ] Turning the water heater up
- [ ] Closing the cabinet doors
> right: Moving water freezes slower. The drip keeps the whole line moving.
> wrong: A drip at the farthest faucet keeps the water moving all night.

4. How is a frozen pipe thawed?
- [ ] With a torch
- [x] Slowly with warm air, with the faucet open
- [ ] By pouring boiling water on it
- [ ] By waiting for spring
> right: Warm air thaws it without boiling the water inside. The open faucet gives the melt somewhere to go.
> wrong: Never a torch. Warm air, slowly, with the faucet open.
