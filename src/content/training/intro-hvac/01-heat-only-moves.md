## Why it matters
An air conditioner does not make cold. It picks up the heat inside a house and carries it outside. Once you see that heat is only being moved, every part of the system has a job you can name.

## Must know
- HVAC is short for heating, ventilation and air conditioning. It is everything that heats, cools and moves the air in a building.
- Cold is not a thing. Cold is what is left when heat has been taken away.
- Heat moves by itself from a warm thing to a cooler thing. It never goes the other way without help.
- An air conditioner is that help. It moves heat from the cooler house to the hotter yard, which takes a machine.
- {{figure:heat-moves|The air conditioner picks up heat inside and drops it outside. Nothing makes cold. The heat is only moved.}}
- Most houses have a split system. One unit sits outside and one sits inside.
- Refrigerant is the fluid that carries the heat. It runs in a sealed loop of copper pipe between the two units.
- A fan in the indoor unit pushes house air through ducts, the big tubes that carry air to every room.
- The cold part inside pulls water out of the air. That water leaves by a drain.
- For heating, a furnace burns gas or runs electric heat. A heat pump moves heat inward instead.
- The thermostat is the control on the wall. It reads the room and calls for heating or cooling.

## Traps
- An air conditioner does not add cold air to a house. It takes heat out of the air already there.
- The outdoor unit is not pulling in fresh air. No air passes between outside and inside through it.
- A system that cools poorly is not out of cold. It has stopped moving heat somewhere along the way.

## Numbers
- **0** cold is made, heat is only moved
- **2** units in a split system, one outside and one inside
- **1** sealed loop of refrigerant joins them

## Check yourself
1. What does an air conditioner do with the heat in a house?
- [ ] Destroys it
- [x] Carries it outside
- [ ] Turns it into cold air
- [ ] Stores it in the ducts
> right: The heat is picked up inside and dropped outside. It is only moved.
> wrong: Heat is never destroyed or turned into cold. The system carries it outside.

2. Which way does heat move when nothing helps it?
- [x] From the warm thing to the cooler thing
- [ ] From the cooler thing to the warm thing
- [ ] Upward only
- [ ] It does not move
> right: Heat runs from warm to cool by itself. Moving it the other way takes a machine.
> wrong: Left alone, heat goes from warm to cool. The air conditioner is the help that sends it the other way.

3. What carries the heat between the indoor unit and the outdoor unit?
- [ ] The house air
- [ ] The water in the drain
- [x] The refrigerant in its sealed loop
- [ ] The thermostat wire
> right: Refrigerant picks the heat up inside and gives it up outside.
> wrong: No air or water passes between the two units. The refrigerant in the copper loop carries the heat.

4. What is a split system?
- [ ] One unit in a window
- [x] One unit outside and one inside, joined by a loop
- [ ] Two furnaces in one house
- [ ] A system with two thermostats
> right: The outside unit and the inside unit are two halves of one system.
> wrong: Split means the system is in two units, one outside and one inside.
