## Why it matters
Most no-cooling calls are not a broken machine. They are a filter nobody changed or a small wire somebody cut. Both are things a crew can spot in a minute, and both are things a crew can cause.

## Must know
- The filter sits in the return, ahead of the blower and the coil. All the house's air passes through it.
- An arrow on the filter's frame shows which way the air goes. The arrow points toward the unit.
- A clogged filter cuts the airflow. In cooling, the coil gets too cold and ices over.
- In heating, low airflow lets the furnace overheat. A safety switch then shuts the burners off.
- A filter is changed on the maker's schedule, often every one to three months.
- The thermostat runs on low voltage, **24 V**. A transformer in the indoor unit makes it from house power.
- A transformer is a small part that turns one voltage into another.
- A thin cable of coloured wires joins the thermostat to the unit. Each wire is one call.
- {{figure:filter-and-stat|The filter's arrow points toward the unit. The thermostat's thin cable carries each call on its own wire.}}
- R is the power. Y calls for cooling, G for the fan and W for heat. C is the way back.
- The float switch from lesson 9 is wired into that cable. When it trips, the call never reaches the outdoor unit.

## Traps
- A filter in backwards still fits. The arrow is the only thing that says it is wrong.
- The thermostat cable is not phone wire to cut out of the way. Cut it and the system is dead or shorted.
- 24 V will not hurt you. The same unit holds house power a few inches away.

## Numbers
- **24 V** runs the thermostat and its cable
- **5** common wires, R, Y, G, W and C
- **1 to 3 months** is a common time between filter changes

## Check yourself
1. Which way does the arrow on a filter point?
- [x] Toward the unit, the way the air goes
- [ ] Toward the room
- [ ] Up
- [ ] It does not matter
> right: The arrow follows the air, and the air goes toward the unit.
> wrong: The arrow shows the airflow. It points toward the unit.

2. What does a clogged filter do in cooling?
- [ ] Makes the air colder in the rooms
- [x] Cuts the airflow until the coil ices over
- [ ] Raises the refrigerant
- [ ] Nothing for months
> right: Too little air leaves the coil too cold, and ice grows on it.
> wrong: Low airflow lets the coil get too cold. It ices over and cooling stops.

3. What voltage does the thermostat run on?
- [ ] 240 V
- [ ] 120 V
- [x] 24 V
- [ ] None
> right: A transformer in the indoor unit makes 24 V for the thermostat and its cable.
> wrong: The thermostat is low voltage, 24 V, made by a transformer in the unit.

4. Which wire calls for cooling?
- [ ] R
- [ ] W
- [ ] G
- [x] Y
> right: Y calls for cooling. W calls for heat, G for the fan, and R is the power.
> wrong: Y is the cooling call. R is the power, G the fan and W the heat.
