## Questions
1. What is cold?
- [ ] A thing the air conditioner makes
- [x] What is left when heat has been taken away
- [ ] A kind of refrigerant
- [ ] Air from outside
> right: Cold is not a thing. It is less heat.
> wrong: Nothing makes cold. Cold is what is left when heat has been taken away.

2. What does the thermostat on the wall do?
- [ ] Cools the air itself
- [x] Reads the room and calls for heating or cooling
- [ ] Pumps the refrigerant
- [ ] Drains the water
> right: The thermostat is the control. It reads the room and calls for heating or cooling.
> wrong: The thermostat only reads the room and calls. The units do the work.

3. In what order does the refrigerant pass the four parts of the loop?
- [ ] Evaporator, condenser, compressor, metering device
- [x] Compressor, condenser, metering device, evaporator
- [ ] Condenser, compressor, evaporator, metering device
- [ ] Metering device, compressor, evaporator, condenser
> right: Compressor, condenser, metering device, evaporator, and back to the compressor.
> wrong: The order never changes. Compressor, condenser, metering device, evaporator.

4. What is a coil?
- [ ] A spring in the compressor
- [x] A long tube bent back and forth through thin metal fins
- [ ] A roll of thermostat wire
- [ ] The fan's motor
> right: Air blown across the tube and its fins trades heat with the refrigerant inside.
> wrong: A coil is a long tube bent back and forth through thin metal fins.

5. Which coil soaks up heat from the house air?
- [ ] The condenser
- [x] The evaporator
- [ ] The compressor
- [ ] The metering device
> right: The evaporator is the indoor coil. The cold refrigerant in it soaks up the house's heat.
> wrong: The evaporator, the indoor coil, soaks heat up. The condenser gives it away outside.

6. Under less pressure, what happens to a liquid's boiling temperature?
- [x] It boils cooler
- [ ] It boils hotter
- [ ] It stops boiling
- [ ] Nothing changes
> right: Less pressure, a cooler boil. That is how refrigerant boils cold in the indoor coil.
> wrong: Lower pressure lowers the boiling temperature. Water and refrigerant both follow that rule.

7. What tells a technician the temperature that goes with a pressure reading?
- [ ] The thermostat
- [ ] The filter
- [x] The chart for that refrigerant
- [ ] The breaker's number
> right: A gauge reads the pressure and the chart gives the boiling temperature for that refrigerant.
> wrong: Each refrigerant has its own chart. It gives one boiling temperature for each pressure.

8. What is the outdoor unit called?
- [ ] The air handler
- [ ] The evaporator
- [x] The condensing unit
- [ ] The furnace
> right: The condensing unit holds the compressor, the condenser coil and a fan.
> wrong: The outdoor unit is the condensing unit. The air handler is indoors.

9. How is a dirty outdoor coil cleaned?
- [ ] With a pressure washer, power on
- [x] Rinsed gently with a hose, with the power off
- [ ] With a wire brush
- [ ] It is never cleaned
> right: A gentle rinse with the power off clears the grass, lint and dirt.
> wrong: The coil is rinsed gently with a hose and the power off. Its thin fins bend under force.

10. What is an indoor unit called when the house has electric heat or a heat pump?
- [ ] A condenser
- [x] An air handler
- [ ] A line set
- [ ] A disconnect
> right: With electric heat or a heat pump, the box that holds the coil and the blower is an air handler.
> wrong: It is an air handler. With gas heat the coil sits on a furnace instead.

11. About how much cooler does the air leave the indoor coil?
- [ ] 2 to 3°F
- [x] 15 to 20°F
- [ ] 40 to 50°F
- [ ] It leaves warmer
> right: The air leaves the coil about 15 to 20°F cooler than it went in.
> wrong: A working coil cools the air by about 15 to 20°F.

12. What keeps dust off the indoor coil?
- [ ] The blower
- [ ] The pan
- [x] The filter ahead of it
- [ ] The thermostat
> right: The filter catches the dust. A coil caked with dust cannot pick up heat.
> wrong: A filter sits ahead of the coil to keep dust off it.

13. What does the small line of the line set carry?
- [ ] Cool vapor back to the compressor
- [x] Warm liquid refrigerant to the indoor coil
- [ ] Water to the drain
- [ ] Power to the blower
> right: The small bare line is the liquid line. It carries warm liquid to the indoor coil.
> wrong: The small line is the liquid line. The big wrapped one carries cool vapor back.

14. Why does refrigerant copper come capped?
- [ ] To keep the price up
- [x] To keep damp air out, since water ruins a compressor
- [ ] To hold the foam on
- [ ] So it cannot be bent
> right: The line stays clean and dry until it is joined. Damp air brings in water.
> wrong: An open line takes in damp air. Water in the loop ruins a compressor.

15. What do the locking caps on the outdoor unit's ports do?
- [ ] Hold the pressure in
- [x] Keep the refrigerant away from children
- [ ] Mark the unit as new
- [ ] Stop the fan
> right: The caps lock so a child cannot open a port and breathe the refrigerant.
> wrong: The locking caps are there to keep children out of the refrigerant.

16. What does the label on an A2L line say?
- [ ] Hot water
- [ ] Do not paint
- [x] Risk of Fire, Flammable Refrigerant
- [ ] Thermostat wire
> right: An A2L refrigerant can burn, and its line is labelled so.
> wrong: The label warns of fire. An A2L refrigerant can burn.

17. Who may buy refrigerant?
- [ ] Anyone
- [ ] Any plumber
- [x] Only a carded technician
- [ ] Only the homeowner
> right: The sale is restricted to technicians who hold the card.
> wrong: Refrigerant is sold only to a carded technician.

18. What does the trap on a condensate drain do?
- [ ] Catches refrigerant
- [x] Lets water out while the blower pulls on the pan
- [ ] Filters the air
- [ ] Stops the float
> right: The blower's pull would hold the water in the pan. The trap lets it leave.
> wrong: The trap is there for the blower's pull. With it the water can leave the pan.

19. What is an air gap at the end of a condensate drain?
- [ ] A leak in the pipe
- [x] The open air between the end of the pipe and the drain under it
- [ ] A hole in the duct
- [ ] The space around the outdoor unit
> right: Sewer water cannot climb back across open air.
> wrong: The air gap is the open air between the pipe's end and the drain below it.

20. What does water in the second pan mean?
- [ ] The system is working well
- [ ] The roof is leaking
- [x] The first drain is blocked
- [ ] The refrigerant is low
> right: A dry second pan is the normal state. Water in it means the first drain is blocked.
> wrong: The second pan stays dry while the first drain works. Water in it means a blocked drain.

21. What does the reversing valve in a heat pump do?
- [ ] Reverses the fan
- [x] Swaps the two coils' jobs
- [ ] Reverses the power
- [ ] Drains the pan
> right: It sends the hot refrigerant indoors instead of outdoors, so the coils trade jobs.
> wrong: The reversing valve swaps the coils' jobs. That turns the loop round.

22. What does the thermostat call the heat strips?
- [ ] Cooling
- [ ] Fan only
- [x] Auxiliary or emergency heat
- [ ] Defrost
> right: The thermostat shows the heat strips as auxiliary or emergency heat.
> wrong: Heat strips show on the thermostat as auxiliary or emergency heat.

23. What does the blower do in a furnace?
- [ ] Pulls the flame's gas up the flue
- [x] Pushes house air across the outside of the heat exchanger
- [ ] Lights the burners
- [ ] Opens the gas valve
> right: The blower moves the house's air past the heat exchanger and on to the rooms.
> wrong: The blower is the house air's fan. The inducer is the one that pulls the flame's gas.

24. What proves that the inducer is pulling?
- [ ] The flame sensor
- [ ] The thermostat
- [x] A pressure switch
- [ ] The filter
> right: The pressure switch proves the inducer is pulling before anything lights.
> wrong: A pressure switch proves the inducer. The flame sensor proves the flame.

25. Which of these does not need a sediment trap?
- [ ] A furnace
- [ ] A water heater
- [x] A clothes dryer
- [ ] Both a furnace and a water heater
> right: A range, a clothes dryer and an outdoor grill do not need one.
> wrong: A furnace and a water heater each get a sediment trap. A clothes dryer does not need one.

26. How long does a house's gas test hold on a gauge, at the least?
- [ ] 1 minute
- [x] 10 minutes
- [ ] 2 hours
- [ ] A full day
> right: A house's new gas pipe is tested with air for at least 10 minutes.
> wrong: The least a house's gas test holds is 10 minutes on a gauge.

27. How much room does the code count as enough for each 1,000 Btu an hour of burner?
- [ ] 5 cubic feet
- [x] 50 cubic feet
- [ ] 500 cubic feet
- [ ] Any room will do
> right: Fifty cubic feet of room for each 1,000 Btu an hour.
> wrong: The code counts a room as big enough at 50 cubic feet for each 1,000 Btu an hour.

28. What does a vent connector join?
- [ ] The gas line to the burner
- [x] The appliance to the vent
- [ ] The thermostat to the unit
- [ ] The two ducts
> right: The vent connector is the pipe from the appliance to the vent.
> wrong: A vent connector is the pipe from the appliance up to the vent.

29. Where does the code want a carbon monoxide alarm placed?
- [ ] Inside the furnace closet
- [ ] In the attic
- [x] Outside each sleeping area, near the bedrooms
- [ ] At the gas meter
> right: The alarm goes outside each sleeping area, near the bedrooms.
> wrong: A CO alarm goes outside each sleeping area, near the bedrooms, where it will wake people.

30. What is the plenum?
- [ ] The filter's frame
- [x] The box on the unit where the ducts begin
- [ ] The pan under the coil
- [ ] The flue's cap
> right: The plenum is the box on the unit where the ducts begin.
> wrong: The ducts begin at the plenum, the box on the unit.

31. What makes the thermostat's 24 V?
- [ ] A battery in the wall
- [ ] The outdoor unit's capacitor
- [x] A transformer in the indoor unit
- [ ] The gas valve
> right: A transformer in the indoor unit makes 24 V from house power.
> wrong: The low voltage comes from a transformer in the indoor unit.

32. What does an evaporative cooler need to do its work?
- [ ] A gas line
- [ ] A refrigerant loop
- [x] A water line
- [ ] A flue
> right: It cools by wetting a pad and blowing air through it, so it needs a water line.
> wrong: An evaporative cooler wets a pad to cool the air. It needs a water line.

33. What grows in a condensate drain all summer?
- [ ] Rust
- [x] Slime
- [ ] Ice
- [ ] Roots
> right: Slime grows in the drain and collects in the trap and at any flat spot.
> wrong: Slime is what blocks a condensate drain. It collects in the trap.

34. What does ice on the coil do to the air?
- [ ] Cools it more
- [x] Blocks it, so the coil gets colder still
- [ ] Cleans it
- [ ] Nothing
> right: The ice blocks the air, and with less air the coil gets colder still.
> wrong: Ice blocks the air. A frozen coil never fixes itself while it runs.

35. What does a technician hold at the draft hood to test the draft?
- [ ] A wet rag
- [ ] A thermometer
- [x] Smoke
- [ ] A magnet
> right: Smoke at the hood should be pulled in, a few minutes after the burner lights.
> wrong: The draft is tested with smoke at the hood. The smoke should be pulled in.

36. What is added to natural gas as a warning?
- [ ] A colour
- [x] A smell, like rotten eggs
- [ ] A sound
- [ ] Water
> right: The smell is added on purpose. It is the warning.
> wrong: Natural gas has a smell added to it, like rotten eggs, so a leak gives a warning.

37. What does refrigerant break down into in a fire?
- [ ] Water
- [ ] Carbon monoxide only
- [x] Hydrogen fluoride, a poison gas
- [ ] Nothing
> right: In a fire or on a very hot surface, refrigerant breaks down into hydrogen fluoride.
> wrong: Heat breaks refrigerant down into hydrogen fluoride, a poison gas.

38. Who takes a lock off a disconnect?
- [ ] Anyone who needs the power
- [ ] The homeowner
- [x] The one who put it on
- [ ] The newest person on the crew
> right: The one who puts a lock on is the one who takes it off.
> wrong: Only the person who put the lock on takes it off.

39. How big is the level work space at an attic unit?
- [ ] 12 in by 12 in
- [x] 30 in by 30 in
- [ ] 6 ft by 6 ft
- [ ] The code sets no size
> right: At the unit there is a level work space 30 in by 30 in.
> wrong: The code gives the unit a level work space of 30 in by 30 in.

40. Where is the switch for the attic's light?
- [ ] At the unit
- [ ] In the kitchen
- [x] At the attic opening
- [ ] On the roof
> right: The light's switch is at the opening, so nobody crosses a dark attic to find it.
> wrong: The code puts the light's switch at the attic opening.

41. Which class of licence covers a system of any size?
- [x] Class A
- [ ] Class B
- [ ] Class C
- [ ] Every class
> right: A Class A licence covers a system of any size. Class B stops at 25 tons of cooling.
> wrong: Class A covers any size. Class B covers cooling up to 25 tons.

42. Who stays with a worker under 18 on a job?
- [ ] Nobody
- [ ] Any adult
- [x] The contractor or a certified technician, at all times
- [ ] The homeowner
> right: A worker under 18 is with the contractor or a certified technician at all times on a job.
> wrong: Under 18, a worker is never alone. The contractor or a certified technician is there at all times.

43. When is the HVAC rough inspected?
- [x] Before the walls close
- [ ] After the paint
- [ ] At start-up
- [ ] A year later
> right: The rough is inspected before the walls close, while a mistake is still easy to reach.
> wrong: The rough is inspected before the drywall. After it, a mistake is a hole in a wall.

44. What is emptied out of the loop at start-up before the refrigerant goes in?
- [ ] Oil
- [x] Air and water
- [ ] Dust from the ducts
- [ ] Nothing
> right: The loop is tested for leaks and emptied of air and water first.
> wrong: Air and water are emptied out of the loop before the refrigerant goes in.
