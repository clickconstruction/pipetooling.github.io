## Why it matters
The mechanical inspector reads the same things on every house, and most of them are in this course. A crew that knows the list builds to it the first time. A plumber's part of the job can fail a mechanical inspection too.

## Must know
- The mechanical inspector reads the HVAC work against the code and the maker's sheet.
- The condensate drain is on the list. Its size, its slope, its trap and where it ends.
- Over a ceiling, the second defence is on the list. The second pan and its drain, or the float switch.
- The attic is on the list. The opening, the walkway, the work space, the light and the outlet.
- {{figure:inspector-mechanical|The inspector reads the drain and its second defence. Then the gas, the closet's air, the vent and the attic.}}
- The gas is on the list. The shutoff within reach, the sediment trap and the connector.
- The closet's two air openings are on the list. So is the room an appliance sits in.
- The vent is on the list. Its slope, its joints and what else shares it.
- The line set is on the list where it shows. Its foam, its steel plates and its locking caps.
- A change of unit needs a permit in most cities. The office pulls it before the work starts.
- A failed item is fixed and inspected again. Nothing is covered until it passes.

## Traps
- A drain that carries water today can still fail. The inspector reads the slope and the trap, not the drip.
- The plumber's gas line and vent are on the mechanical list too. One inspector reads another trade's work.
- A unit that cools is not a unit that passed. The list is about what goes wrong in year five.

## Numbers
- **4** things on a drain, its size, slope, trap and end
- **2** air openings in a furnace closet
- **0** work covered before it passes

## Check yourself
1. What does the inspector read on a condensate drain?
- [ ] Only that water comes out
- [x] Its size, its slope, its trap and where it ends
- [ ] Its colour
- [ ] Nothing, it is not inspected
> right: Four things, and a drain that drips today can still fail on any of them.
> wrong: The inspector reads the drain's size, slope, trap and where it ends.

2. What is on the list for a unit over a ceiling?
- [ ] A bigger filter
- [x] The second defence, a second pan and drain or a float switch
- [ ] A louder alarm
- [ ] A second thermostat
> right: Where an overflow could damage the building, the second defence is inspected.
> wrong: Over a ceiling the inspector looks for the second pan and its drain, or the float switch.

3. Which of the plumber's work is on the mechanical inspector's list?
- [ ] The kitchen faucet
- [ ] The water meter
- [x] The gas line and the vent
- [ ] The garden tap
> right: The shutoff, the sediment trap, the connector and the vent are all read by the mechanical inspector.
> wrong: The gas line and the vent are on the mechanical list, though a plumber piped them.

4. An item fails inspection. What happens next?
- [ ] The wall is closed and it is fixed later
- [x] It is fixed and inspected again before anything is covered
- [ ] The inspector fixes it
- [ ] Nothing
> right: Fixed, inspected again, and only then covered.
> wrong: A failed item is fixed and inspected again. Nothing is covered until it passes.
