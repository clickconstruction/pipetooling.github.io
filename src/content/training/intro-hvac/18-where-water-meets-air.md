## Why it matters
Some machines belong to both trades at once. A plumber sets them, and they hold a coil, a fan or a sealed refrigerant loop. On these the two crews have to know where one trade's work stops.

## Must know
- A boiler heats water, not air. A pump sends the hot water to radiators or to a coil in an air handler.
- Texas law sets boiler work apart from air conditioning work. It has rules and inspections of its own.
- A hot water coil in an air handler heats the house's air. Its water comes from a boiler or a water heater.
- A heat pump water heater is a tank with a small cooling loop on top. It moves heat from the room's air into the water.
- {{figure:water-meets-air|A heat pump water heater pulls heat out of the room's air and puts it in the water. It makes condensate and needs a drain.}}
- A heat pump water heater cools and dries the room it sits in. It needs the air space its maker's sheet calls for.
- It makes condensate like any cold coil. It needs a drain, piped by lesson 8's rules.
- Its refrigerant loop is sealed at the factory. Opening that loop takes the card, like any other.
- A humidifier on a furnace has a small water line and a drain. Both are plumbing on an air machine.
- An evaporative cooler cools by wetting a pad and blowing air through it. It needs a water line too.
- The company's rule is simple. Water in pipes is plumbing. Air in ducts is HVAC. A sealed loop is a carded technician's.

## Traps
- A heat pump water heater in a small closet starves for air. It needs the space on its maker's sheet.
- A water heater that drips from its top may be making condensate. It is not always a leak.
- The sealed loop on a water heater is still a refrigerant loop. A plumbing licence does not open it.

## Numbers
- **3** kinds of work on one machine, the water, the air and the sealed loop
- **1** drain for the condensate a heat pump water heater makes
- **0** sealed loops opened without the card

## Check yourself
1. What does a heat pump water heater take its heat from?

- [ ] The drain
> right: Its small loop moves heat from the room's air into the water.
> wrong: A heat pump water heater pulls heat out of the room's air. That is why it cools the room.

2. Why does a heat pump water heater need a drain?
- [ ] To flush the tank daily
- [x] Its cold coil makes condensate
- [ ] To vent the flame
- [ ] It does not need one
> right: A cold coil pulls water out of the air. That water needs somewhere to go.
> wrong: Like any cold coil it makes condensate, so it needs a drain.

3. Who may open the sealed loop on a heat pump water heater?

- [ ] The homeowner
- [x] A carded technician
- [ ] Nobody, ever
> right: It is a refrigerant loop, and opening it takes the card.
> wrong: A sealed loop is a refrigerant loop. Only a carded technician opens it.
