## Why it matters
A saw, a drill or a shovel can open a refrigerant line in a second. What comes out is under pressure, freezing cold and heavier than air. In the new systems it can also burn. The crew's part is to get clear, not to stop it.

## Must know
- A cut line hisses and sprays an oily mist. Frost forms at the cut.
- {{figure:cut-line-set-strip|A cut line sprays cold refrigerant under pressure. It sinks and fills low places. Step back, air the space, and call.}}
- The liquid boils at about **60°F below zero**. On skin or in an eye it causes frostbite in a moment.
- The vapor is heavier than air. It sinks and collects in a crawl space, a pit or a closed closet.
- Enough of it pushes the air out of a low space. A person there can pass out with no warning.
- The line is under pressure, and the hiss can go on for minutes.
- The new A2L refrigerants can burn. Their safety sheet warns of flammable gas, so no torch and no spark nearby.
- In a fire or on a very hot surface, refrigerant breaks down into hydrogen fluoride, a poison gas.
- The move is to step back and get out of any low or closed space. Open doors to air it.
- Then the call goes to the lead and to a carded technician. A cut line is their repair, and the loss is theirs to record.

## Traps
- A thumb over the hole is a frostbitten thumb. Nobody stops a leak by hand.
- No smell does not mean no gas. Refrigerant has almost no smell and gives no warning in a low space.
- A torch near a leaking line is two dangers at once. Fire with the new kind, and poison gas with any kind.

## Numbers
- **60°F below zero** is about where the liquid boils
- **heavier than air** so it sinks and fills low spaces
- **0** torches or sparks near a leaking line

3. Why is a torch kept away from a leaking line?

> right: A2L refrigerants can light, and heat breaks refrigerant down into hydrogen fluoride.
> wrong: Flame near refrigerant means fire with the A2L kind and poison gas with any kind.

4. A drill has opened a line set in a crawl space. What is the move?
- [ ] Hold a rag on it
- [ ] Finish the hole first
- [x] Get out of the crawl space, air it, and call the lead and a carded technician

> right: Out of the low space first, since the vapor sinks and pushes the air out.
> wrong: Leave the low space at once and air it. The repair is a carded technician's.
