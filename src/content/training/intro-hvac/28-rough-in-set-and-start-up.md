## Why it matters
An HVAC crew comes to a new house three times, like the plumbers. Knowing which visit you are on tells you what is being built and what can still be changed. What goes in the walls at the first visit stays there for the life of the house.

## Must know
- An HVAC crew visits a new house three times. Rough-in, set and start-up.
- Rough-in comes before the drywall. The ducts go in, with a boot at each room where a register will sit.
- At rough-in the line set is run and capped, and the thermostat cable is pulled.
- The condensate drain, the flue and the gas stub go in at rough-in too. So does the attic walkway.
- The rough is inspected before the walls close. After the drywall, a mistake is a hole in a wall.
- {{figure:rough-set-start|Rough-in puts the ducts, the lines and the drain in the walls. Set brings the units. Start-up proves the system.}}
- Set comes after the paint. The indoor unit, the outdoor unit, the registers and the thermostat go in.
- Start-up is the last visit, and a carded technician runs it.
- At start-up the loop is tested for leaks and emptied of air and water before the refrigerant goes in.
- Then the airflow is checked, and the drain and the float switch are tested with water.
- A start-up that is skipped shows up later. It shows as a compressor that dies young.

## Traps
- Rough-in is not rough work. A flat drain or a kinked line hidden in a wall is there for good.
- Setting the units is not finishing the job. A system is not done until its start-up is on paper.
- An open line set at rough-in is taking in damp air. It stays capped until the day it is joined.

## Numbers
- **3** visits, rough-in, set and start-up
- **before** the drywall is when the rough is inspected
- **last** comes start-up, run by a carded technician

## Check yourself
1. At which visit do the ducts and the line set go in?
- [x] Rough-in, before the drywall
- [ ] Set
- [ ] Start-up
- [ ] The final inspection
> right: Rough-in puts the ducts, the line set, the drain and the cable in while the walls are open.
> wrong: The ducts and the line set go in at rough-in, before the drywall.

2. Why does the line set stay capped at rough-in?
- [ ] To hold the foam on
- [x] An open line takes in damp air
- [ ] To mark which house it is
- [ ] It does not need a cap
> right: Damp air brings water into the loop, and water ruins a compressor.
> wrong: The cap keeps damp air out until the line is joined.

3. Who runs the start-up?
- [ ] The painter
- [ ] The newest helper alone
- [x] A carded technician
- [ ] The homeowner
> right: Start-up works on the refrigerant loop, so it is a carded technician's visit.
> wrong: Start-up is on the refrigerant loop. A carded technician runs it.

4. What is tested with water at start-up?
- [ ] The gas line
- [x] The drain and the float switch
- [ ] The thermostat cable
- [ ] The outdoor fan
> right: Water poured in the pan proves the drain runs and the float switch stops the unit.
> wrong: The drain and the float switch are proved with water before the house needs them.
