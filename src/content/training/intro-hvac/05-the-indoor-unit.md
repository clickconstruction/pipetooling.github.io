## Why it matters
The indoor unit is where the house's air meets the cold coil. It sits in an attic, a closet or a garage. Air, refrigerant, power and water all pass through this one box.

## Must know
- The indoor unit holds the evaporator coil and a blower, the fan that moves the house's air.
- With electric heat or a heat pump the box is called an air handler. With gas heat the coil sits on a furnace.
- The blower pulls air from the house, pushes it through the coil and sends it back out to the rooms.
- The air leaves the coil about **15 to 20°F** cooler than it went in.
- The coil is colder than the air, so water forms on it, like a cold glass in summer.
- That water is condensate. It drips into a pan under the coil and leaves by a drain.
- {{figure:indoor-unit|The blower pushes house air across the cold coil. The air leaves cooler. The water that forms drips to the pan and the drain.}}
- Cooling is sized in tons. One ton moves **12,000 Btu** of heat an hour. A Btu is a small measure of heat.
- Each ton needs about **400 cubic feet** of air a minute across the coil.
- A filter ahead of the coil keeps dust off it. A coil caked with dust cannot pick up heat.

## Traps
- A ton is not the unit's weight. It is how much heat the system moves in an hour.
- A bigger unit is not a better one. One too big cools fast, stops early and leaves the house damp.
- Water at the indoor unit is not a leak in the roof. Look at the pan and the drain first.

## Numbers
- **12,000 Btu** of heat an hour is one ton
- **about 400 cubic feet** of air a minute for each ton
- **15 to 20°F** cooler is how the air leaves the coil

## Check yourself
1. What does the blower do?
- [ ] Squeezes the refrigerant
- [x] Moves the house's air across the coil and out to the rooms
- [ ] Pumps the water out
- [ ] Cools the outdoor unit
> right: The blower is the fan for the house's air. It pulls it in and pushes it across the coil.
> wrong: The blower moves air, not refrigerant or water. It sends house air across the coil.

2. Why does water form on the indoor coil?
- [ ] The coil leaks
- [x] The coil is colder than the air, like a cold glass in summer
- [ ] The refrigerant turns to water
- [ ] The roof drips on it
> right: Warm damp air gives up its water on a cold surface. That water is condensate.
> wrong: The coil is cold and the air is damp. Water forms on it the way it does on a cold glass.

3. How much heat does one ton of cooling move in an hour?
- [ ] 400 Btu
- [ ] 2,000 Btu
- [x] 12,000 Btu
- [ ] 240 Btu
> right: One ton is 12,000 Btu an hour.
> wrong: A ton is 12,000 Btu of heat an hour. The 400 is the cubic feet of air a minute for each ton.

4. What happens when a unit is too big for the house?
- [ ] It saves power
- [ ] It dries the air better
- [x] It cools fast, stops early and leaves the house damp
- [ ] It needs no filter
> right: It reaches the set temperature before it has run long enough to pull the water out.
> wrong: An oversized unit runs short cycles. The air cools before the damp is taken out of it.
