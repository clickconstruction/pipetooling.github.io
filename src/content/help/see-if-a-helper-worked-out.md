When the office is trying out a new helper and puts them on your job, the app asks you one thing at the end of the day: **would you take them again?** Your answer is how the office decides who gets hired — so it is asked by name, the same day, and it takes ten seconds.

## Who gets asked

Whoever could run the job that day: a master, or a sub or helper the office has marked as able to run a job. The app works it out from the schedule and the clock — you were on the helper's job that day, listed or clocked in. Nobody has to assign you, and another helper who still needs supervision is never asked.

## The card

Once the helper clocks out, the card is at the top of your Dashboard:

:::example Bryan worked with you today at J258 · Oak St
**Take Bryan again?**

{{button:outline|Yes}} {{button:outline|No}} {{button:outline|Not sure}}

*A word for the office (optional)* — "careful, a bit slow"

{{button:gray|Skip}} {{button:blue|Save}}
:::

- Pick one of the three, add a word if you have one, and press {{button:blue|Save}}.
- {{button:gray|Skip}} puts the card away without an answer. It will not come back for that day.
- If your phone gets the app's notifications, you also get one the moment the helper clocks out — *Bryan clocked out of Oak St — take Bryan again?* — and tapping it opens the card.
- If you clock in and out yourself, the card also comes up right after **your** clock-out, before the crew cards.

## Changing your mind

Today's answer stays on your Dashboard as one line — *You said yes · "careful, a bit slow"* — with {{button:gray|Change}} beside it. You can change it until the day is over. A card you have not answered waits for you through the next morning, then leaves.

## Who sees it

The office, on the helper's Try-out card, **with your name on it** — *Mike yes, Jake no*. This is not the anonymous crew rating: the point is which leaders want which helpers. The helper does not see it.
