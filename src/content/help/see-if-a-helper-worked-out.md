The office puts a trial helper on your job. At the end of the day the app asks you one thing.

The question is **would you take them again?** Your answer is how the office decides who gets hired. So it is asked by name, the same day. It takes ten seconds.

## Who gets asked

You are asked when you could run the job that day. That means a master, or a sub or helper the office has marked as able to run a job. The app works it out from the schedule and the clock. You were on the helper's job that day, listed or clocked in. Nobody has to assign you. Another helper who still needs supervision is never asked.

## The card

The helper clocks out. Then the card is at the top of your Dashboard.

:::example Bryan worked with you today at J258 · Oak St
**Take Bryan again?**

{{button:outline|Yes}} {{button:outline|No}} {{button:outline|Not sure}}

*A word for the office (optional)* — "careful, a bit slow"

{{button:gray|Skip}} {{button:blue|Save}}
:::

- You pick one of the three. You add a word if you have one. You press {{button:blue|Save}}.
- You press {{button:gray|Skip}} to put the card away without an answer. It will not come back for that day.
- Your phone may get the app's notifications. Then you also get one the moment the helper clocks out. It reads *Bryan clocked out of Oak St — take Bryan again?* You tap it and the card opens.
- You may clock in and out yourself. Then the card also comes up right after **your** clock-out, before the crew cards.

## Changing your mind

Today's answer stays on your Dashboard as one line. It reads *You said yes · "careful, a bit slow"* with {{button:gray|Change}} beside it. You can change it until the day is over. A card you have not answered waits for you through the next morning. Then it leaves.

## Who sees it

The office sees it on the helper's Try-out card, **with your name on it**. It reads *Mike yes, Jake no*. This is not the anonymous crew rating. The point is which leaders want which helpers. The helper does not see it.
