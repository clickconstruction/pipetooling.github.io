The **My Schedule** card on your Dashboard now has a {{button:outline-blue|+ Add job}} button. Tap it to search **any active job** — Waiting, Working, Ready to Bill, or Billed — by number, name, address, or customer, and put it on your own schedule.

## Add a job

1. Tap {{button:outline-blue|+ Add job}} and search. Each result shows the job's number, name, address, customer, and stage.
2. Pick the job, choose **Today**, **Tomorrow**, or any date, and set the time window. If it overlaps something you already have, an amber warning tells you — you can still save.
3. Leave **Add me to the job crew** checked (it's what keeps the job visible to you everywhere else in the app), and tap {{button:green|Add to my schedule}}.

The new card carries an **added by you** tag — those are fully yours: retime them, move them, or remove them.

## Move my day around

The ✎ button next to {{button:outline-blue|+ Add job}} opens **My day** — every visit on your schedule in one list:

- **−30m / +30m** nudge a visit earlier or later; the date and time boxes set anything precisely; overlapping visits get an amber outline.
- **Every visit is movable — even ones dispatch set for you.** Moving a dispatch-set visit isn't sneaky though: it posts a note to the job's thread and the office Schedule shows an amber {{chip:yellow|moved by tech · was 12:00 PM–2:00 PM}} tag, so everyone stays honest.
- Only visits **you added** show the 🗑 remove button. A dispatch-set visit can be moved but never removed from the field — that's a call to dispatch.

## Request a job

Searched and it isn't there? Tap **Request a job** at the bottom of the search. Enter the job name (required), address, customer name / phone / email, the GC if there is one, and the line items of what you'll do.

:::example What happens when you hit Request & schedule
The job is created in **Waiting** for real — it's on your schedule, on the office Schedule page, and on Jobs → Pipeline immediately — and Dispatch gets a **Review field-requested job** inbox item to price it and link the real customer.
:::
