The **My Schedule** card on your Dashboard has a {{button:outline-blue|+ Add job}} button. Tap it to put any active job on your own schedule.

Active means Waiting, Working, Ready to Bill or Billed. You can search by number, name, address or customer.

## Add a job

1. Tap {{button:outline-blue|+ Add job}} and search. Each result shows the job's number, name, address, customer and stage.
2. Pick the job. Choose **Today**, **Tomorrow** or any date, and set the time window. If it overlaps something you already have, an amber warning tells you. You can still save.
3. Leave **Add me to the job crew** checked. That is what keeps the job visible to you everywhere else in the app. Tap {{button:green|Add to my schedule}}.

The new card carries an **added by you** tag. Those cards are fully yours. You can retime them, move them or remove them.

## Move my day around

The ✎ button next to {{button:outline-blue|+ Add job}} opens **My day**. It lists every visit on your schedule in one place:

- **−30m / +30m** nudge a visit earlier or later. The date and time boxes set anything precisely. Overlapping visits get an amber outline.
- **Every visit is movable, even ones dispatch set for you.** Moving a dispatch-set visit is not sneaky, though. It posts a note to the job's thread. The office Schedule shows an amber {{chip:yellow|moved by tech · was 12:00 PM–2:00 PM}} tag, so everyone stays honest.
- Only visits **you added** show the 🗑 remove button. A dispatch-set visit can be moved but never removed from the field. That is a call to dispatch.

## Request a job

Searched and it is not there? Tap **Request a job** at the bottom of the search. Enter the job name, which is required. Add the address and the customer's name, phone and email. Add the GC if there is one. A GC is the general contractor. Then add the line items of what you will do.

:::example What happens when you hit Request & schedule
The job is created in **Waiting** for real — it's on your schedule, on the office Schedule page, and on Jobs → Pipeline immediately — and Dispatch gets a **Review field-requested job** inbox item to price it and link the real customer.
:::
