A trade on a job we are building asks to be paid with a pay application. You approve it, pay it and record its waiver in the Draws window.

Each trade with a signed statement of work has a card there. Only devs see the window for now.

## Open the Draws

1. Press {{button:outline|GC}} on the Bids page to open [GC projects](/gc).
2. Press {{button:outline|Draws}} on the card of a job we are building.

Each trade's card shows its contract, what is billed and what is paid. It also shows the retainage we hold and what is left to bill. Each line's bar shows what they reported and what we billed. **To pay** at the top lists the approved draws and the day each one is due.

## Record a pay application that came by email

1. Press {{button:outline|A pay application came by email}} on the trade's card.
2. Each line starts at what they last reported. Change a percent to match their form.
3. Tick **It asks for materials stored on site** when it does. Type the dollars on each line.
4. Pick the day it runs to. Type who signed it and their title.
5. Paste its Drive link. A note shows when our office may not be able to open it.
6. Press {{button:blue|Record it}}.

The window says what it asks for. That is the work this period and the stored materials, less the retainage we hold. One pay application waits on us at a time.

## Approve it or send it back

:::example A pay application waiting on us
Draw 2 {{chip:yellow|waiting on us}} {{button:blue|Approve}} {{button:outline|Approve less}} {{button:outline|Send back}}
:::

- Press {{button:blue|Approve}} to approve it as it is.
- Press {{button:outline|Approve less}} when a line is not as far along as they say. Pick the percent we approve on that line. Say why, then press the button with what we approve.
- Press {{button:outline|Send back}} when it needs fixing. Pick the percent we see on each line we doubt. Say what is not right, then press {{button:blue|Send it back}}. The trade reads it in its portal.

Approve waits on the company's papers. A red line says which one is missing, like insurance that ran out. A pay application we send back keeps its number. Their fixed one comes in with the same number.

## Mark it paid

1. Press {{button:blue|Mark paid}} when the payment goes out. We pay an approved draw within 10 days.
2. Their unconditional waiver comes after we pay. Press {{button:outline|Their unconditional waiver came in}} when it does.

## Charge a trade back

Charge a trade for work we did or fixed for them.

1. Press {{button:outline|Charge them}} on the trade's card.
2. Type the amount and what it is for. Add a photo's link if you have one.
3. Press {{button:blue|Send the charge}}.

They have 5 days to agree or dispute it. Keep or drop a disputed charge, and say why. Press {{button:blue|Take it off draw 2}} to take an agreed charge off a draw we approved. The button names the draw.

## Send a change to the trade

A change order the customer signed goes to the trade it belongs to. Press {{button:blue|Send the change to Iron Horse Fabrication}} on the change. The button names the company. They sign it in their portal, and it becomes a line of their statement of work.

If they sign on paper or by email instead, press {{button:outline|They signed it}} on the change. Add the file and its Drive link if you have them. Then press {{button:blue|Record their signature}}. The change becomes a line of their statement of work.

## Email the trade

Tick **Email the trade about what I press here** at the top of the window. The tick starts off.

With the tick on, the trade gets an email when you mark a draw paid or approve one for less. They also get one when you charge them, keep or drop a charge, or take one off a draw. A change you send them goes by email too.

With the tick off, no email goes. Tell them yourself. When an email does not go, the press is still saved and the window says why. [Email a trade partner](/help/email-a-trade-partner-from-gc-mode) says who at the company gets it.
