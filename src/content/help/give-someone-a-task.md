The ＋ Task button in the top bar opens the **New task** window from anywhere in the app. Type what needs doing, pick who does it and send it.

The **＋ Task button** is the checkmark icon. The window reads from the top down: the task, who it is for, when, and where. A green sentence at the bottom says what will happen. The blue button under the sentence names who gets the task, like {{button:blue|Send to Theo}}. The task lands on each person's **Checklist → Today** list on its day. On a phone a second {{button:blue|Send}} appears at the top once you start typing. Tapping outside the window leaves it open. So a stray tap never loses your draft. Use the × in the top corner to close it.

## Pick the people

The row called **For** shows faces. **Me** comes first. The people you gave tasks to last come next.

- **A face**: tap it and the task is theirs. Tap it again to take them off. Tap more faces to give the task to several people.
- **Someone else**: opens the full list under the row, with a search box. Tick a name and that person joins the faces.
- **A crew**: every team from **People → Teams** shows as a face with its member count. Tap it to pick the whole crew at once. Tap again to take them off.
- **＋ New crew / ✎ Edit**: these sit under the full list. Build or change a crew right there. Name it and tick who's in it. Crews save to People → Teams. So the same crews appear on the roadmap dialogs too.

The window opens with **Me** picked. Pick someone else and you drop off, so the task is theirs alone. Tap **Me** again to share it.

:::example The Monday flurry
"Clean the shop bays" → tap **Farm crew** → Send. Four people get the task in two taps, and tomorrow the crew is still there for the next one.
:::

## Say where it is

The row called **Where** holds the place. Use it when the work is at a spot, like a pump house or a gate. Leave the row alone for a task with no place.

- **A place used before**: every place a task already has shows as a chip. Tap a chip and the task has that place. Tap the chip again to take the place off. Slide the row sideways to see more places.
- **＋ New**: opens **Where is it?**, a view from above with a ring in the middle.

In **Where is it?** you set the place one of two ways.

1. Stand on the spot and tap {{button:outline|Use where I am}}. The map moves to you and says how sure your phone is.
2. Drag the map until the ring is on the spot. Do this at a desk, or when your phone is not sure.

Type a name, like *Pump house*. Then tap {{button:blue|Use this place}}. The name becomes a chip for the next task.

The app asks your phone where it is only when you tap **Use where I am**. That one reading becomes the task's place. The app does not follow you after that.

:::example A place in two taps
"Grease the pump bearings" → tap {{chip:blue|Pump house}} → Send. The person sees the place on the task, with directions. [Find where a task is](/help/check-off-my-daily-tasks#find-where-a-task-is) shows their side.
:::

## Show what the spot looks like

At the end of the **Where** row sits {{button:outline|Photo}}. Tap it. On a phone the camera opens. Take a picture of the spot, like the valve or the gate.

The person sees your photo on the task, beside the place. A place says where. A photo says what to look for. The two together need no other words.

- The button shows your picture once it is in. Tap the button again to take the photo off.
- A task needs neither a place nor a photo. Leave both alone and the task is made as before.
- This photo is yours, the giver's. It does not count as the photo a task can ask for at the finish.

## When it happens

The **When** bar has four choices. **Today** is lit when the window opens. **Tomorrow** is one tap. **Pick a day** opens a date box. **Repeats** makes it weekly-on-days or "N days after it's done". A repeating task keeps its tick. A one-off gets the % ring, so the person can say how far along it is.

A one-off stays on the list until it is done. [Schedule a task for later or make it repeat](/help/schedule-a-task-or-make-it-repeat) has the rest.

## Add the rare things

The row called **Add** holds what most tasks do not need. Each one is a button with its name. Tap one and it opens under the row. A thing you have set turns blue and shows its value.

- **Remind** sets a nudge at a time of day.
- **Tell someone else** adds one more person who hears when the task is done.
- **Link** adds a link to a page of the app.
- **Due by** sets the day after which the task is late.
- **Just for the day** makes the task leave the list when its day ends.

The bell beside Send is **Tell me**. It is lit, so you hear when the task is done. Tap the bell to turn that off. To see or edit everything you've handed out, open **Checklist → Manage**.

## Say what done looks like

Tap **+ Done when…** in the **Add** row. Type one line a row. Each line is one thing that has to be true. The card shows each line as a box the person ticks. The card reads its percent from the ticks. Two of three ticked reads 67%. Leave the box empty and the person sets the percent by hand.

Beside it sits **Photo at the finish**. It turns on by itself once you write a line. Tap it on a plain task too when you want a photo before the card closes. The green sentence above Send says what the card will carry, the lines and the photo.

:::example Lines on a card
Put the plumbing tools in a neat home

{{chip:gray|Done when…}} Pipe is racked by size

{{chip:gray|Done when…}} Fittings are in labeled bins

{{chip:gray|Done when…}} The cart is clear and the floor swept
:::

You can change the lines later. Open **Checklist → Manage**, then **Edit** on the task. A line you take off keeps the ticks already on it. The Roadmap's **Add task** takes the same lines. Every card the roadmap makes from that task carries them.

## Send a job as a task

Tap the purple arrow on a job. It sits on the Jobs pipeline row, on the phone card's **Send as task**, and on the Job Detail header. The arrow opens the same dialog with the job on a bar above the text box. The bar reads {{chip:purple|1016 PLUM · Mission faucet}} with the address and customer under it. Type only the task. On every task list the job then shows as a chip in front of it. The chip reads {{chip:purple|1016 · Mission faucet ↗}} and opens the job. **open ↗** on the bar shows the job behind the dialog without losing your draft. **×** turns it into an ordinary task with no job attached.

## Send a message thread as a task

Some email is a chore, not a conversation. A supply house asks for our W-9. A builder asks for a certificate of insurance. Open the thread on [Messages](/messages). Press the **⋯** in its header, then **Send as task**. The same dialog opens with the thread on the bar. The bar reads {{chip:purple|Email · Dale Ferris · W-9 needed}} with the company under it. Type only the task and pick who does it. On their list the task shows the chip in front of it. The chip opens the thread at that message.

:::example A supply house asks for our W-9
Press **⋯** on the thread, then **Send as task**. Type "Send our W-9 and COI". Tap **Rosa**. Press **Send to Rosa**. Rosa's Today list reads {{chip:purple|Email · Hill Country Supply ↗}} Send our W-9 and COI.
:::
