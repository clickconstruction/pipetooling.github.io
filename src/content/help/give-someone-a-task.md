The ＋ Task button in the top bar opens the Add-task dialog from anywhere in the app. Type what needs doing, pick who does it and send it.

The **＋ Task button** is the checkmark icon. Tap {{button:blue|Send}}. The task lands on each person's **Checklist → Today** list on its day. As soon as you start typing, a {{button:blue|Send}} button also appears at the top of the dialog. It is the same as the one at the bottom, just closer to your thumb. Tapping outside the dialog leaves it open. So a stray tap never loses your draft. Use the × in the top corner or **Cancel** to close it.

## Pick the people

- **Search**: start typing in the people box and tick names.
- **Recent**: your last few assignees appear as one-tap links above the search.
- **Crew chips**: every team from **People → Teams** shows as a chip with its member count. Tap a chip to tick the whole crew at once. Tap again to untick them. Untick one person by hand and the chip goes dashed. Your manual picks always win.
- **＋ New crew / ✎ Edit**: build or change a crew right here. Name it and tick who's in it. Crews save to People → Teams. So the same chips appear on the roadmap dialogs too.

:::example The Monday flurry
"Clean the shop bays" → tap **Farm crew** → Send. Four people get the task in two taps, and tomorrow the crew chip is still there for the next one.
:::

## Say where it is

Under the people sits a row called **Place**. Use it when the work is at a spot, like a pump house or a gate. Leave the row alone for a task with no place.

- **A place used before**: every place a task already has shows as a chip. Tap a chip and the task has that place. Tap the chip again to take the place off.
- **＋ New place**: opens **Where is it?**, a view from above with a ring in the middle.

In **Where is it?** you set the place one of two ways.

1. Stand on the spot and tap {{button:outline|Use where I am}}. The map moves to you and says how sure your phone is.
2. Drag the map until the ring is on the spot. Do this at a desk, or when your phone is not sure.

Type a name, like *Pump house*. Then tap {{button:blue|Use this place}}. The name becomes a chip for the next task.

The app asks your phone where it is only when you tap **Use where I am**. That one reading becomes the task's place. The app does not follow you after that.

:::example A place in two taps
"Grease the pump bearings" → tap {{chip:blue|Pump house}} → Send. The person sees the place on the task, with directions. [Find where a task is](/help/check-off-my-daily-tasks#find-where-a-task-is) shows their side.
:::

## Show what the spot looks like

Under **Place** sits **Photo of the spot**. Tap {{button:outline|＋ Add a photo}}. On a phone the camera opens. Take a picture of the spot, like the valve or the gate.

The person sees your photo on the task, beside the place. A place says where. A photo says what to look for. The two together need no other words.

- **Take another** swaps the picture. **Remove the photo** takes it off.
- A task needs neither a place nor a photo. Leave both alone and the task is made as before.
- This photo is yours, the giver's. It does not count as the photo a task can ask for at the finish.

## When it happens

**Today** is the default. **On a date** schedules it. **Repeats** makes it weekly-on-days or "N days after it's done". *Stays on the list until done* keeps an unfinished task visible past its day. A repeating task keeps its tick. A one-off gets the % ring, so the person can say how far along it is.

Reminders, notify-on-complete, and links to app pages live in the same dialog. Set them and Send. To see or edit everything you've handed out, open **Checklist → Manage**.

## Say what done looks like

Open **Done when…** under Links. The closed row says what it is for. Type one line a row. Each line is one thing that has to be true. The card shows each line as a box the person ticks. The card reads its percent from the ticks. Two of three ticked reads 67%. Leave the box empty and the person sets the percent by hand.

Beside the row sits **Asks for a photo at the finish**. It turns on by itself once you write a line. Tick it on a plain task too when you want a photo before the card closes. The green sentence under When says what the card will carry, the lines and the photo.

:::example Lines on a card
Put the plumbing tools in a neat home

{{chip:gray|Done when…}} Pipe is racked by size

{{chip:gray|Done when…}} Fittings are in labeled bins

{{chip:gray|Done when…}} The cart is clear and the floor swept
:::

You can change the lines later. Open **Checklist → Manage**, then **Edit** on the task. A line you take off keeps the ticks already on it. The Roadmap's **Add task** takes the same lines. Every card the roadmap makes from that task carries them.

## Send a job as a task

Tap the purple arrow on a job. It sits on the Jobs pipeline row, on the phone card's **Send as task**, and on the Job Detail header. The arrow opens the same dialog with the job on a bar above the text box. The bar reads {{chip:purple|1016 PLUM · Mission faucet}} with the address and customer under it. Type only the task. On every task list the job then shows as a chip in front of it. The chip reads {{chip:purple|1016 · Mission faucet ↗}} and opens the job. **open ↗** on the bar shows the job behind the dialog without losing your draft. **×** turns it into an ordinary task with no job attached.

## Send a message thread as a task

Some email is a chore, not a conversation. A supply house asks for our W-9. A builder asks for a certificate of insurance. Open the thread on [Messages](/messages). Press the **⋯** in its header, then **Send as task**. The same dialog opens with the thread on the bar. The bar reads {{chip:purple|Email · Dale Ferris · W-9 needed}} with the company under it. Type only the task and pick who does it. On their list the task shows the chip in front of it. The chip opens the thread at that message.

:::example A supply house asks for our W-9
Press **⋯** on the thread, then **Send as task**. Type "Send our W-9 and COI". Tap **Rosa**. Press **Send**. Rosa's Today list reads {{chip:purple|Email · Hill Country Supply ↗}} Send our W-9 and COI.
:::
