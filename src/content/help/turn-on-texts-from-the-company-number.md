The office can text a person from their thread once texts are on. The owner or a dev turns them on in Settings.

## Turn it on

1. Open [Phone and texts](/settings?tab=settings-phone-texts) in Settings.
2. **Send texts** is the first card. Press its {{button:outline|Off}} switch.
3. Press {{button:blue|Turn it on}}.

The switch now reads {{button:green|On}}. A text from a thread's send window now goes from the company number.

Nothing else on the page is needed first. The seats and the inboxes only add names and a second number. While texts are off, the wait line under **Text** in a thread links to this page.

## What goes while it is off

Two texts go even when the switch is off. Each one answers a text the person sent first.

- When someone sends HELP or INFO, the app answers with our name, the company number and our email.
- When someone sends REVOKE or OPT OUT, the app stops that number and confirms it once.

The carriers expect both answers. [Know what a text from the company number says](/help/know-what-a-text-from-the-company-number-says) shows their words.

## Turn it off

Press the {{button:green|On}} switch. A text in the send window then waits, and its Send stays off. Email still goes.
