When nobody picks up a call to the company number, the app can text the caller back. The owner or a dev turns it on in Settings and writes the three lines.

## Turn it on

1. Open [Phone and texts](/settings?tab=settings-phone-texts) in Settings.
2. Find the **Missed calls** card. The card sits under **Office hours**.
3. Press its {{button:outline|Off}} switch.
4. Press {{button:blue|Turn it on}}.

The switch now reads {{button:green|On}}. The next missed call gets a text within a minute.

:::example The card, with the switch on

**Missed calls** {{button:green|On}}

When nobody picks up, the app texts the caller back from the company number. One text a number every three hours.

**In office hours.** Sorry we missed your call. This is Click Plumbing. Text us here and we answer shortly.

**After hours.** Sorry we missed your call. The office opens at 7 AM. Text us here and we answer then.

**With a voicemail.** We got your voicemail and will call you back.

:::

## Write the three lines

The card holds three lines. The app picks one for each call.

- **In office hours** goes when the call came while the office is open.
- **After hours** goes when the call came while the office is closed. The hours are the **Office hours** card's.
- **With a voicemail** goes when the caller left a voicemail.

Type each line in its box. Press {{button:blue|Save the lines}}. Press {{button:outline|Put them back}} to drop your edits.

A first text to a number carries the opt-out line after your words, as every first text does.

## What holds a text back

The text goes through every gate the office's texts have. Nothing goes when one says no.

- **Send texts** is off. Turn it on at the top of the page.
- The number sent STOP.
- The caller is a prospect who has not said we may text. Ask on the call, then set **May we text?** on their card.
- The number is one of our own.
- We texted that number in the last three hours.
- Texts are paused for the phone balance, or the carriers' daily cap is reached.

## Read the last texts back

**Last texts back** lists the last ten missed calls. Each row says when they called, who, and what the app did.

:::example Three rows

today · 4:12 PM · **(512) 555-0187** · Sent, in office hours.

today · 2:05 PM · **Ridgeway Builders** · Not sent. We texted this number at 1:40 PM.

today · 11:31 AM · **(512) 555-0101** · Not sent. They sent STOP.

:::

## What the office sees

The thread shows the missed call. Under the call sits the app's text, named **The app texted back**. The row still waits in Follow up. Nothing is cleared, and nobody is taken off it.

[Turn on texts from the company number](/help/turn-on-texts-from-the-company-number) covers the Send texts switch.
