Some checklist questions need your signature. The person who wrote the question asked for one. A signed answer carries more weight than a stamp. The office, the GC and an inspector can read it. This guide says how you sign, what locks after, and how an answer is taken back.

## Which questions need a signature

The open question wears a chip that reads *signature*. On the job's Documents tab the row reads *needs a signature* until it is signed. The question is not answered until you sign. Not here needs a signature too. Not here is the claim an inspector would test.

## Sign your answer

1. Open the stage's checklist in Job Mode or on the job window. The guide [fill a stage checklist on the phone](/help/fill-a-stage-checklist-on-the-phone) has the steps.
2. Press {{chip:green|Yes}}, {{chip:red|No}} or {{chip:gray|Not here}} on the open question. The press saves nothing yet. A sheet opens on top.
3. Read the words at the top. They say what you are signing, where it goes and who reads it.
4. Check your name. The box holds the name on your profile. You can change it.
5. Press {{button:outline|Type}} to sign with your name, or {{button:outline|Draw}} to sign with your finger. Draw in the white box. Press **Clear** to start over.
6. Type a note if you want to say more. A No needs a note that says why.
7. Tick *I did this check myself and the answer is true.*
8. Press {{button:green|Sign and save}}.

The sheet closes. The row reads your answer with your name, the time and *signed*. Your mark sits under the row. The list moves to the next question by itself.

:::example What the sheet says
You are signing **Yes** to “Did the final water test hold?”. It goes on this job's Trim Set record with your name and the time. The office, the GC and an inspector can read it.
:::

## When the phone has no signal

Press {{button:green|Sign and save}} as usual. The signed answer waits on the phone. The line at the top of the screen says it is waiting. It sends by itself when the signal is back. The signed time is the time you pressed. The guide [keep working when the signal drops](/help/keep-working-when-the-signal-drops) says how the line works.

## A signed row is locked

A signed answer cannot be changed. Tap the row and it opens with no presses. It shows your pick, your mark and *Signed by Luis Ortega · Nov 1 · 9:14 am*. A signed question that asks for a photo still takes the photo. Press {{button:outline|Take the photo}} under the mark.

## Take an answer back

A signed answer can be wrong. You signed the wrong row. The unit changed after you signed. Take it back and answer again.

1. Tap the signed row to open it.
2. Press **Take it back**.
3. Type why the answer is taken back.
4. Press {{button:outline|Take it back}}. Press **Keep it** to change your mind.

The question opens again with its three presses. The old answer stays under the question as a line that reads *Taken back*. The line keeps the old pick, the old mark, who took it back, when and why. Nothing is erased. Anyone who can answer the question can take an answer back.

A filed stage opens again when an answer is taken back. The filed version stays as a record. The guide [read a filed stage checklist](/help/read-a-filed-stage-checklist) says how a record works.

## What the office reads

On the job's Documents tab a signed row wears a green *signed* tag. The mark sits beside the name and the time. A row with a pick but no signature reads like *Yes, not signed yet*. An answer taken back reads under its question as a *Taken back* line with the old mark kept.

## Mark a question as needing a signature

The person who adds a question decides which ones need a signature. Tick **Needs a signature** beside **Asks for a photo** on the add box. The tick is on the add box on the job's Documents tab. Settings, under Jobs, bills and liens, only shows *needs a signature* beside such a question. Use it where a wrong answer costs a wall or an inspection. The guide [add a check to a job's stage checklist](/help/add-a-check-to-a-jobs-stage-checklist) has the add box's steps.
