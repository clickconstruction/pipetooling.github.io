The new phone layout gives the page more of your phone. The layout is a trial, and you switch it on yourself.

The bars at the top and the foot of the screen get smaller. No press is taken away. Each one is still on the screen, and this guide says where.

## Turn it on

1. Open the {{icon:gear}} gear menu on your phone.
2. Tap **New phone layout**. The row sits under **Show**.
3. The page turns at once.

Tap the row again to go back to the layout you had.

The switch belongs to the phone you tick it on. Your other phone keeps its own choice. The row shows on a phone only. Nothing changes on a computer.

Leaders and devs see the row for now. A phone with the layout on shows the row to anyone who signs in on it. So anyone on that phone can switch it off.

## The top bar

The top bar is one row. The bar says the name of the page you are on.

These stay in the bar:

- The menu, at the left, for the roles that have one.
- The house that opens the Dashboard. The house leaves the bar when a **Dashboard** tab is at the foot.
- **Search**.
- The green phone that calls the office, in Job Mode.
- The {{icon:gear}} gear.

On the Bids page the **Bid** press stays in the bar too.

## The rows that moved into the gear menu

Every other press of the old bar is a named row at the top of the gear menu. The rows keep the order the icons had. Each row opens what its icon opened.

- **Tell the dev**
- **Task Dispatch**
- **Ask estimating**
- **Task**
- **Customers**
- **People**

A partner finds **Statement** there too. You see only the rows your role had as icons.

:::example Where a helper's presses are
Old bar: Dashboard, Search, Tell the dev, the green phone, Task Dispatch, Ask estimating, Task, the gear.
New bar: the page's name, Search, the green phone, the gear. The Dashboard is the first tab at the foot.
Gear menu, at the top: Tell the dev, Task Dispatch, Ask estimating, Task.
:::

## The day's presses in Job Mode

The day's presses share one row above the tabs. **Turnaway** and **Leave report** are small presses at the left. The main press takes the rest of the row. The main press is the one that reads **Clock In** or **On my way**.

**% done** and **Clock out** join the row when you are clocked in on a job. On a small phone a label of two words folds onto two lines.

## The tabs

The tabs keep their words and their order. The tabs stand a little shorter.

## The Home Screen offer

The offer to add an **Add Task** icon no longer sits above every page. The offer is one quiet row at the top of the Dashboard. **Add** and **Dismiss** do what they did. An offer you dismissed stays dismissed.

## Needs you

On your Dashboard, **Needs you** is one line. The line says how many things wait on you. The line names the first one.

:::example the Needs you line
*Needs you · 8 · Sign 7 lien notices before the run* **Inbox ›**
:::

Tap the line to open the **Inbox** tab. The whole list is there.

In the **Inbox** tab the list opens on **Walk the list**. You see one thing at a time, with its press. Tap **Skip for now** to see the next one.

Tap **Cards** to see every line at once. The phone keeps your pick.

You get the one line when **Inbox** is a tab at the foot of your phone. Job Mode and Dispatch Mode both have that tab. In Job Mode the line shows only when a line is yours.

With no **Inbox** tab, the list stays on your Dashboard. The list opens on **Walk the list** there too.

The count on the line is your own lines. Lines that wait on other people are in the list, under their name.

Nothing else on your Dashboard moved. **The office's round** is where it was, under the line.

The guide [work the Needs you list on the dashboard](/help/needs-you-card) says what each line means.

## Tell us how it goes

This layout is a trial. Open the gear menu and tap **Tell the dev** to say what works and what does not. The guide [tell the dev what is wrong with a page](/help/tell-the-dev-about-a-page) says how.
