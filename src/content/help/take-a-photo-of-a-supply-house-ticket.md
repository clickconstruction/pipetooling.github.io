A ticket is the paper the supply house hands you at the counter. A receipt from any store counts too. You take its photo in **Job Parts Tally** before you leave the counter. The office then sees what was bought when it sorts the charge to a job.

The photo is filed in the job's Drive folder, in **Team only**, in a folder named **Tickets**. A ticket shows prices, so the customer's picture folder never gets one.

## Take the photo at the counter

1. Open **Job Parts Tally**. The press is under the tabs, on every tab.
2. Press {{button:blue|Photo of a ticket}}. The phone's camera opens.
3. Take the photo. Hold the phone over the whole ticket so the small print reads.
4. A sheet comes up and asks **Which job is this ticket for?**
5. Check the job that is picked. Press the save button at the bottom.

The save button names the job, like **Save the ticket to JP1042**. Read it before you press.

You type nothing. The bank knows the minute your card was run. The app uses that minute to find the charge for you.

## From the Job Mode card

The Job Mode card has a **Ticket** line under **Photos**. Press it and the camera opens. That job is picked first on the sheet.

## Pick the job

The sheet offers the jobs it thinks are likely, in this order.

- A PO code the office made for you today. The sheet shows the code and the supply house.
- The job you are clocked in on now.
- The other jobs on your day.

The first one is picked for you already. Tap another job to pick that one as well. Tap a picked job to take the pick off.

Is the job missing from the sheet? Press **Another job…** and search for it.

Was the ticket for two jobs? Tap both. The office splits the charge between them.

Was the ticket for stock, the shop or a tool? Press **Not for one job**. The office places it.

Do you not know the job yet? Press **Pick the job later**. The photo is saved, and you name the job afterwards.

## A long ticket, or a blurry one

- Press **+ Another page** to take the next page of the same ticket.
- Press **Retake** to take the last page again.

## Add the total or a note

You do not have to. Press **+ total** to type the total printed on the ticket. Press **+ note** to tell the office something about it.

The total helps when you ran your card twice at one counter. The app then tells the two charges apart.

## A receipt the store sent you

Some stores send the receipt by email. Save it to your phone first.

1. Under the blue button, press **or choose a photo or a PDF you already have**.
2. Pick the file.
3. Pick the job and save, as above.

## With no signal

Take the photo as usual and save it. The ticket stays on your phone and sends by itself when the signal is back.

**My tickets** counts the tickets still on the phone. The bar at the top of the app has a button to try now.

Keep the paper until the ticket has sent. A phone that is lost or wiped before then loses the photo.

## See your tickets

1. In **Job Parts Tally**, open the **Transactions** tab.
2. Find **My tickets**, above your purchases.

Each ticket shows its job, when you took it, and where it stands.

- **Waiting for its charge or its bill** means nothing has met the ticket yet.
- **On account, waiting for the bill** means you bought on a PO code. The bill comes later by mail.
- **Matched to** a store and an amount means the app found the charge.

Sometimes one charge fits loosely, like a ticket you took hours after the swipe. The row then asks a question, like **is it $228.58 at Ridgeway Supply?** Press {{button:blue|It is}} or {{button:outline|It is not}}.

## Fix a ticket

Open the ticket from **My tickets**.

- Press {{button:outline|Change the job}} when you picked the wrong job.
- Press {{button:outline|Add a page}} when you missed a page.
- Press {{button:outline|Remove this ticket}} when you took it by mistake, or took it twice.

A removed ticket leaves your list. The photo is kept.

Once the office has used a ticket, you can no longer change its job or remove it. The window says so. Ask the office to change it.

## A receipt link from the office

The office can send you a link for one charge on your card. You need no sign-in to open it.

1. Open the link. The page shows the store, the amount, the day and whose card.
2. Press **Take a photo of the receipt**. Hold the phone over the whole receipt.
3. Wait for **Got it**. The receipt is then on that charge.

The page may ask **Which job was it for?** Tap the job, or skip it. The office sorts the charge either way.

- Press **Retake** to take the photo again.
- Press **+ Add a page** for a long receipt.
- Press **or choose a photo or a PDF you already have** for a receipt the store sent you.

Stay on the page until the photo sends. The page has no sign-in, so it cannot keep a photo for later.

With no signal the photo waits on the page. Press **Try again** when the signal is back.

The link opens for 14 days. Once the link has closed, take the photo in **Job Parts Tally** as above.

## What the office does with it

The office sorts card charges on the **Team** view, as [sort the team's card purchases from the office](/help/sort-the-teams-card-purchases#a-ticket-on-the-charge) explains. Your ticket's job is the office's first choice for that charge. Your own purchase wears a green **Ticket** chip. Your ticket's job comes first when you sort the purchase yourself. Read [sort my card purchases to jobs](/help/sort-card-purchases-to-jobs) for that.
