A trade can tell us from its portal that its work will be late. The schedule shows each notice on a card. You take the new day as a move, or you push back. Nothing on the chart moves until you do.

Only devs can answer a notice for now.

## Find the notices

1. Open the project's schedule from [GC projects](/gc).
2. Find **Trades say they will be late** above **Changes to the schedule**. The card shows only on a job being built.

Each notice says what the trade asked for and why. It shows the trade's own words. It also says what taking it would do to the work after it.

## Take the new day

1. Press {{button:blue|Take Wed Oct 14}}. The button names the day the trade asked for.
2. **Why it moved** opens with the trade's day, reason and words.
3. Change the reason or the words if you like.
4. Press **Save the move**.

The move names the notice. The notice then leaves the card.

Someone may answer the notice before you save. Then Why it moved says so, and the notice leaves the card. Close the window.

## Push back

1. Press **Push back**.
2. Say what you need from them, in a sentence.
3. Press **Send it to** and the company's name.

Your words go to the trade's portal. The bar keeps its day. The card shows the notice as pushed back until they answer.

**Call** under a notice dials the company.

## Check the look-ahead marks

Each trade marks its work in the look-ahead as done or not done. Our superintendent checks each mark on the job. Only a checked mark counts.

1. Find **To verify** under the chart.
2. Press **Right, done** or **Right, not done** when the trade's mark is right.
3. Pick why and press **Not done** when the work is not done after all.
4. Press **It is done** when the trade said not done but the work is done.

Our own crew's work this week shows here too. Press **Done** or **Not done**. Our own mark counts as checked.

An inspection due this week shows here too. Press **It passed today**, or **It failed** to say what failed. See [record an inspection on the schedule](/help/record-an-inspection-on-the-schedule).
