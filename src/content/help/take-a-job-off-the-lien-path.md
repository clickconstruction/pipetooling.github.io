Some money has no lien behind it. A lien is for labor done on the property or materials furnished to it. A charge for anything else is collected another way. This guide shows how to take one job off the lien path, and how to put it back.

## When to use it

Use it when one job should get no lien papers. A trip charge to pick up a check is one case. No work was done on the property for that money.

The app does not judge the bill. You read the bill, and you say why. Ask the attorney when you are not sure.

Two other cases have their own tools.

- A job you have given up on is marked **Uncollectible**. Read [mark a bill uncollectible](/help/mark-a-bill-uncollectible).
- A city, a county or a school is public property. Its window takes every job at the address. Read [public property](/help/read-the-board-on-the-lien-desk#public-property).

## Take a job off the path

:::steps

1. Open the job's Lien window. Under the steps, press {{button:outline|Not lienable…}}.
2. Read **What this job billed**. Each line is the bill's own words, with its money.
3. Read **Its lien papers**. The line says what already went out on the job.
4. Type the reason under **Why**. The reason is kept on the job, with your name and the day.
5. Tick **Also put it on the attorney's list** when the attorney should collect it.
6. Press the blue button. It names the job, like {{button:blue|Take 977 off the lien path}}.

:::

The same press stands on the Lien desk. A lien affidavit that a fact holds has a quiet {{button:outline|Not lienable…}} under its row.

The blue button stays grey until a reason is typed.

## What the press does

The job leaves the Lien desk. It leaves the Board, the lists, the run and the counts. It leaves the Pipeline's lien dates and the lien calendar too.

Nothing is filed, voided or deleted. The job's lien papers stay as they are.

Nobody is reminded of the job's lien dates after this. The dates still run. A lien right can be lost while a job is off the path.

The job's activity gets a line. The line says who took the job off and why.

A job can go on the attorney's list only once it is billed. The window says so before you press. The job still leaves the lien path.

## Find the jobs that are off the path

The Board has a bin under the affidavits, {{chip:gray|Off the lien path}}. It opens folded to one line, with the number of jobs and the money they owe. Press {{button:outline|Show ▾}} on that line to open it.

Each row gives the job and what it still owes. The row names the GC, who took the job off, and the day. The row says whether the job is with the attorney. The reason sits on its own line.

A job that owes nothing and has no lien paper is not listed.

The job's own Lien window still opens. An amber note at its top says the job is off the lien path. The note gives the day and the reason.

## Put a job back

:::steps

1. Find {{chip:gray|Off the lien path}} on the Board. Press {{button:outline|Show ▾}} to open it.
2. Find the job. Press {{button:outline|Put it back…}} on its row.
3. Read why it came off, who took it off, and the day.
4. Type why it goes back.
5. Press the blue button, like {{button:blue|Put 977 back on the lien path}}.

:::

The job is on the Lien desk again, with its papers where they stood. Check each lien date first. The dates did not stop while the job was off.

The job stays on the attorney's list when it is on it.

The Lien window's amber note has the same {{button:outline|Put it back…}}.
