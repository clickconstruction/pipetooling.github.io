Some work on a job is no trade's line. Mobilizing, cure time and the customer's own work get bars of their own on the schedule. The schedule also keeps the dates the job must meet.

The office and estimators can open the schedule. Superintendents get it later.

## Add the job's own work

1. Open [GC projects](/gc).
2. Press {{button:outline|Schedule}} on the project's card.
3. Under the chart, press **Add an activity**.
4. Name it, like Slab cure.
5. Pick whose it is under **Whose**.
6. Set the day it starts and the day it finishes.
7. Tick what it waits on under **It waits on**.
8. Tick what waits on it under **It holds up**.
9. Press {{button:blue|Put it on the schedule}}.

Work that waits on it moves out if it has to. Nobody reports this work, so the office marks it done.

## Mark it done

Press its bar on the chart. The bar's form opens below the chart. Press **Mark it done today**. To take the mark back, press **Not done after all**.

## Take it off the schedule

Press its bar, then press **Take it off the schedule**. Whatever waited on it stops waiting. Only the job's own work comes off this way.

## Set the dates to meet

The **Milestones** card lists the dates the job must meet. Each milestone has a day and the trade it belongs to.

1. Type a new milestone's name.
2. Pick its day.
3. Pick its trade, or leave it as the job's own.
4. Press **Add milestone**.

To move one, change its day and press **Save** beside it. Press **Take off** to remove one.
