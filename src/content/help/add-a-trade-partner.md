A trade partner is a company we hire to do one trade on a GC project. Add each one on Trade partners, so we can ask it for a quote.

Only a dev sees Trade partners while it is built.

## Find the trade

1. Open [GC projects](/gc).
2. Press {{button:outline|Trade partners}} at the top of the board.
3. Find the card for the trade, such as Sitework. Press its pill on the strip to jump there.

Every trade on our projects has a card. A trade with no company yet says so in red.

## Add a company

1. On the trade's card, press {{button:outline|Add a company}}.
2. Type the company name. The name is the only box you must fill.
3. Type the person to call, their phone and their email.
4. Type their address with the street and the town.
5. Type how many miles they will drive to a job. Leave the box empty when they go anywhere.
6. Press {{button:blue|Add to Sitework}}. The button names the trade of the card you are on.

The company shows on the card at once.

## Have we worked with them before?

Tick **We have worked with them** only when we have. Leave the box empty for a company new to us.

A company new to us can still send a quote. Nothing is awarded to them until a dev approves them. A company new to us waits at the top of Trade partners under **New to us**. [Approve a company that is new to us](/help/approve-a-company-new-to-us) says what to do next.

## The address and the drive

We work out the drive to each job from the town in the address.

- A green chip such as {{chip:green|in Boerne}} means we found the town.
- An amber chip that says {{chip:yellow|no drive yet}} means we could not find the town. We keep the address as you typed it.

A job farther than their miles shows in amber on their line.

## Change where a company drives from

1. Find the company on its trade's card.
2. Press the address under its name. The line says *address not set* when there is none.
3. Change the address or the miles.
4. Press {{button:blue|Save}}.

Press Escape to leave the address as it was.

## Read a company's line

- **When we ask** says how often they send a quote when we ask. *answers* is good, and *often silent* means they rarely reply.
- **Asked right now** lists each open job we asked them about. A red chip means the day they promised has passed.
