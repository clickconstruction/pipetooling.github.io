Need the current Bank Deposit Details, I-9, or Certificate of Insurance? They live in one place, always the latest copy.

## Company papers in Drive

The top of **Documents → Company** is **Company papers**. It lists the company folder in Drive, newest change first. Each line shows the file's name, the day it last changed and its size. Press a name and the file opens in Drive. Press {{button:outline|Open the folder}} to see the folder itself.

The office sets the folder once on **Where files go**. Until then the tab says *No company folder yet* and links to that tab.

{{button:outline|COI 2026.pdf}} 2026-03-02 · 80 KB
:::

### Upload a file

The office presses {{button:blue|Upload a file}} and picks a file on this computer. The file lands in the company folder and the list reads again. A file over 10 MB is held with its reason. Put that one in the folder in Drive instead. An estimator reads the list and opens the folder, and does not see the press.

## Where to find them

Open **Documents → Company**, next to Where files go at the end of the tab row. Or open **Settings → Your account → Company documents**. It is the same list in both places. Each document is a {{button:outline|📄 Name}} button. Click it and the current copy opens in a new tab. The grey date next to each shows when it was last updated, so you know how fresh it is.

{{button:outline|📄 Bank Deposit Details}} updated Jul 28

{{button:outline|📄 Certificate of Insurance}} updated Jun 12

{{button:outline|📄 W-9}} updated Jan 5
:::

## Keeping them current (the owner and devs)

On **Documents → Company**, the owner and devs see a {{icon:gear}} gear at the top right of the tab. It opens the manager. There you change a link when a new version exists, and the "updated" date refreshes automatically. You can rename, reorder with ↑ ↓, delete, or add a new one. A name plus a link is all it takes. Everyone in the office sees the change immediately. There is nothing to send around.
