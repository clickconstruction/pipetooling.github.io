A company quoting a trade asks about the plans. The office records the question, sends it to the architect and records the answer. The next set of plans carries the answer.

Questions close three days before our bid is due. Questions never open on a bid we lost. The office and estimators see the window.

## Record a question

1. Press {{button:outline|GC}} on the Bids page to open [GC projects](/gc). Press {{button:outline|Questions about the plans}} on the project's card.
2. Press {{button:outline|A question came in by phone or email}}.
3. Pick the trade, type the company that asked and the sheets it is about.
4. Type the question in their words and press {{button:blue|Add the question}}.

## Send it to the architect

1. Press {{button:outline|Email it to the architect}} on the question.
2. The email goes to the architect's address on the customer record. Replies come to the project manager.
3. Press {{button:outline|It went to them another way}} when you asked by phone or in a meeting.

## Record the answer

1. Type the architect's answer under the question, as they gave it.
2. Press {{button:blue|Record the answer}}.

The answer shows under **Answered** with the words the next set's note will carry. Open **A new set of plans came in**. Each answer not in a set yet is ticked to ride in the note.

## Email the answer to the companies

A dev also sees each company on the question's trade under the answer, ticked.

1. Untick any company that should not hear it.
2. Press {{button:blue|Send the answer to 2 companies}}. The number counts the companies ticked.

One press records the answer and emails it to them. The company that asked from its portal reads **asked it**.

Only companies still asked on the trade hear it. A company that passed hears nothing. Once the job is ours, only the company we award it hears it. A question about the job as a whole is not emailed.

An answered question says who it went to. Press {{button:outline|Send it to Sample Electric Co.}} for a company that has not had it. A company never gets the same answer twice.

Everyone else records the answer the same way. Emails to the companies go out once the portal opens. [Email a trade partner from GC mode](/help/email-a-trade-partner-from-gc-mode) shows what the email says.
