A sub's business can sign papers for all of its workers. Enter the business once, and each worker shows under it on People → Contracts.

## Why a sub needs a company

In the app a sub is a person. A business signs the subcontractor agreement and the W-9, not one worker. Without a company, those papers hang on one worker and the next worker shows none. With a company, People → Contracts draws the business once and puts its workers under it.

:::example What the office sees
**ZZ Sample Mechanical LLC** {{chip:gray|Trade company}}
*Workers Pat Sample, Owner · Lee Sample*
:::

## Enter a sub's company

1. Open **People** and press the **Contracts** tab.
2. Press the sub's row to open it.
3. Press the value beside **Their company**. A sub with none on file reads *None on file*.
4. Type the company's name. The box offers the trade companies we already have as you type.
5. Press {{button:blue|Keep}}.

The sub's row moves under the company's row. The company's row names every worker it holds.

:::tryit
Many subs already go by their business name. Press {{button:outline|Same as their name}} to keep their own name as the company in one press.
:::

## What the line under the box means

- *A trade company we have* means Keep links the sub to that company. Nothing new is made.
- *A new trade company* means Keep makes the company, with its trade set to Plumbing, and links the sub.
- *Keep takes them off the company* shows when you empty the box.

## Say what they are at the company

Once a sub has a company, a second line shows. Press the value beside **What they are there** and type a word like *Owner*. A worker with a title leads the company's line.

## Move a sub or take them off

- To move a sub, press **Their company** and type the other company's name.
- To take a sub off, empty the box and press {{button:blue|Keep}}.

The sub's own papers stay on the sub's row either way. Papers already filed under a person do not move to the company.

## Send the company its papers

The subcontractor agreement and the W-9 belong to the business. Those two papers show once, under the company's row, and not on a worker's row.

1. Press {{button:blue|Send for signature}} under the company's row.
2. Pick the **Paper**. A paper the company has signed shows as signed already and cannot be picked.
3. Check **Who signs**. The window opens on the worker with a title, with their email from the roster.
4. Press {{button:blue|Send email}}.

The email goes to the signer and to anyone else at the company who gets its contracts. The paper says who signs and the company they sign for.

## Enter a paper signed on paper

1. Press {{button:outline|Enter from paper}} under the company's row.
2. Pick the **Paper** and **Who signed**.
3. Set the **Day they signed**. Paste a **Link to the scan** if you have one.
4. Press {{button:blue|Enter as signed}}.

The paper shows as {{chip:green|Signed}} under the company. No email goes out.

## Find a worker by the company

Type part of the company's name in the search box on People → Contracts. Every worker of that company shows.

## Who can do this

The leader, a dev, an assistant and the controller enter a sub's company and send its papers. Anyone else sees the tab as before, with no company rows.

:::fold A paper says who the worker signs for
A paper a worker signs carries the line *signing for* and the company's name. The app takes the name from **Their company** when the paper is made and each time it is sent. A signed paper never changes.
:::
