A note on a task used to reach nobody. Now you can tag a teammate in a task note. The note reaches that person, and it waits there until they answer.

Tagging in a job note works the same way. That guide is [tag a teammate in a job note](/help/tag-a-teammate-in-a-job-note).

## Tag someone in a task note

You tag from the note box on a task.

1. Open the task. On Today, press its card.
2. Look above the note box. The task's own people are offered there, after the word **Tag**.
3. Press a name. The name is written into your words and shaded.
4. Type your note.
5. Press {{button:blue|Post}}.

:::example The names above a task's note box
Tag: {{chip:blue|@Dana Whitlock}} gave this task

Which bay first? @Dana Whitlock
:::

To tag someone who is not offered, type **@** and a few letters. A row of names opens. Press the name you mean.

The **@** button beside the box opens the same row.

A tag is the name you pressed. A name you only typed is plain words. So a note like "@will call" tags nobody named Will.

Press Backspace right after a shaded name to take the tag off.

## Tag from History or a Roadmap task

The note box on **History** has the same **@** button. So does the note box on a Roadmap task's card.

On History, **Reopen** uses the same box. A name you tagged in the reason is tagged.

## Who you can tag

You can tag only people who can already open the task.

The task's own people come first. The task's own people are the person who gave it and the people who have it. The office comes after them.

The row does not offer a sub or an estimator. The row does not offer someone who has left.

A tag never gives a new person the task. To bring a teammate in, give them the task.

If you see no **@** button, the task is not yours and you are not the office.

## What the note shows after you post

The name in your note becomes a chip. After the note you read who it waits on.

:::example The words after a tagged task note
{{chip:blue|@Dana Whitlock}} waiting on Dana

{{chip:blue|@Dana Whitlock}} waiting on Dana · 4d

{{chip:blue|@Dana Whitlock}} answered by Dana
:::

The waiting words turn amber after two working days. Nothing reminds the person again.

## When someone tags you

A red number shows on **Checklist** in the header. The number counts the task notes that tagged you and still wait.

Open the Checklist. The **Tagged task notes** section is at the top of Today. The oldest tag is on top.

The Dashboard helps too. The **Needs you** card has a **Tagged notes** line. Press **Open** and one list opens with your job tags and your task tags.

In Task Mode the section at the top of Today holds your job tags as well.

## Answer a tag

Each tagged note has its own box.

1. Type your answer in the box under the note.
2. Press {{button:blue|Post}}.

Your answer goes on that task's notes. The tag clears, and the person who asked sees it under **You asked**.

Any note you type on that task answers the tag. The note can be on any day of a task that repeats.

Marking the task done does not answer a tag. Type a few words first, then press **✓ Post & complete**.

## Say no reply is needed

Some tags only need reading. Press {{button:outline|No reply needed}} on the note. The tag clears and no note is added.

## See what you asked

Under the tagged notes is **You asked**. **You asked** lists every task note where you tagged someone.

- **no answer yet** means the person has not answered.
- **answered** shows their answer.
- **no reply needed** means they read it and pressed that.
- **has left** means the person no longer works here. Ask someone else.

A new answer counts in the red number until you have seen it. Seeing it in the list clears it.

## See who a task waits on from Review

On **Review**, a task with an open tag shows who it waits on. The words sit beside the task's note count.

## What does not answer a tag

A tick answers nothing. A line such as stuck, moved or handed on answers nothing. Only a typed note does.

## When a task is handed on

The tag goes with the task. If you gave the task away, you still see the tagged note in your list. Press **No reply needed**, or tell the new person.
