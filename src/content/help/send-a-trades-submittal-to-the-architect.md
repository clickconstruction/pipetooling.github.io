A trade sends product data, shop drawings or samples for the architect to approve before its work. You keep each one in the trade's register, send it to the architect and record the answer.

Each submittal holds the work it covers on the schedule until it is approved. Only devs see the window for now.

## Open the register

1. Press {{button:outline|GC}} on the Bids page to open [GC projects](/gc).
2. Press {{button:outline|Submittals}} on the card of a job we are building.

Each awarded trade has a card. The chips count what waits on the trades, on us and on the architect.

## Add a submittal

1. Press {{button:outline|Add a submittal}} on the trade's card.
2. Type what it covers and pick the kind.
3. Type the spec section, like 23 81 19. The box suggests the sections on the trade's own lines.
4. Tick the work it holds on the schedule.
5. Set the days from approval to on site.
6. Press {{button:blue|Add submittal}}.

The register numbers it by its section, like 23 81 19-01. A submittal with no section gets a plain count.

A submittal is needed by the first start of the work it holds, less those days. When none of that work is on the schedule, give it a day under **Needed by**.

## Record what came by email

1. Press {{button:outline|It came by email}} on the submittal.
2. Type the file's name and paste its Drive link.
3. Press {{button:blue|Record it}}.

A warning shows when only some people can open the link. The architect may not be one of them.

## Send it to the architect

1. Press {{button:blue|Send to Marsh & Vale Architects}}. The button names the job's architect.
2. The email carries the file's link and the day we need the answer. Replies come to the project manager.

Press {{button:outline|We sent it another way}} when it went by hand or in a meeting. A submittal that came with no Drive link can only be sent that way.

## Record the answer

1. Press {{button:outline|Approved}}, {{button:outline|Approved as noted}} or {{button:outline|Revise and resubmit}}.
2. Type the architect's note. A revise needs what to change.
3. Press {{button:blue|Record it}}.

A revise sends it back to the trade for another round. Once a submittal is approved, the work it holds can start.
