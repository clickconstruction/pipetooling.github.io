Every bid tab has a **Plans** button beside the bid's name. You press it and type a word or a question. The plans answer at once.

## What the panel holds

The panel reads two things. The first is the words on the sheets. The robot writes them when it reads the set on a round, within minutes of the plans being filed. The second is the robot's read of the set. That read has the sheet index, every schedule as a table, and the general notes with a flag on each. A flag is one word, like exclusion or coordination.

## Find a word

1. Press {{button:blue|Plans}} on any bid tab.
2. Type a word in the box, like *floor drain* or *P-401*. The answer card says which sheets hold it. The hits list below it shows each sheet and the line it found.
3. Press {{button:gray|Open sheet}} on a hit to see that page.

## Ask a question

You can type a whole question, like *is there a grease interceptor on this job*. The panel takes the words that matter and looks in three places. The sheets' text, the schedules and the notes. The card answers in one or two sentences. It names the sheets. It quotes the schedule row or the note it found. When no sheet mentions the words, it says so.

The card never gives a count of the robot's own. It says what the plans say and where. Counts come after you send.

## When the set has no text

A scanned set has no words to search. The card says so. The robot's read still answers from the schedules and the notes. You open the sheet to read it yourself.

## When no robot has read the set

A bid the robots skip, or a bid filed a minute ago, has no page text yet. The panel reads the set in your browser once and saves the words for everyone. It says how many sheets it is reading. A large set takes about a minute.

## The sheet index, the schedules and the notes

Under the hits are three folds. **Sheet index** lists every sheet by number and title. **Schedules** shows each schedule as a table. **Notes with flags** lists the general notes the robot flagged. An exclusion flag means the note may belong on the letter as an exclusion. You read the note and decide.

## Ask the robot to look

When the plans' own answer is not enough, you press {{button:blue|Ask the robot to look}} under the card. Your question goes to the robots' rounds. The robot reads the sheets and answers within a few minutes. The answer names the sheets it read. It lands in the thread under the box, where the whole office can see it. Your Dashboard says when it is there.

The robot looks at a bid only after it has sealed its own number on that bid. Until then the card says so. The plans still answer at once. Most bids seal within an hour of filing.

Before you send the bid, the robot never gives a count or a price of its own. Ask how many floor drains and it names the sheets and tells you to count them there. Counts come after you send.

When the answer says the plans do not say, you press {{button:gray|Make this an RFI}}. That files the question on the RFI tab with the sheet the robot cited. You send it to the GC from there. The GC is the general contractor.
