A job's whole trail on **Jobs → Pipeline** — every note and report plus schedule, clock, status, and billing events — opens as **one view** wherever you open it from: the floating window, the panel under a row, and that panel's full-screen mode all show the same compact numbered feed.

## Open it

Three ways in, same content:

1. Click the {{button:outline-blue|2 Reports}} button in a job's **Job** column (the count varies), **or**
2. On wide screens, click the small expand arrows in the top-right corner of the row's **Job activity box**, **or**
3. Click a row's **▶ notes toggle** to open the panel under the row — its ⛶ button takes it full screen.

The job's identity sits at the top — the {{chip:blue|Job: 4821}} number badge, service type, job name, and address. Close the floating window with the **✕**, the **Esc** key, or the dark backdrop; **Esc** also exits full screen.

## How the feed reads

- **One line per item**, in columns: circled number · time · who · what. The time is just the clock ("9:45a") because the **day separator** above carries the date and how long ago it was ("Wed, Aug 12 · 3d ago").
- **Every line has a circled number**, 1 is the oldest — notes, reports, schedule, clock, status, and billing lines alike — so "check line 7" points at one thing. The numbers don't shift when you filter.
- **Messages stand out.** Notes and reports — a person talking — sit on a subtle highlight band; system lines (clock, schedule, crew, status) stay quiet and gray between them, so scanning the feed reads like a conversation with the bookkeeping in the background.
- **Click a line to open it in place.** In the compact panel under a row, a report folds to its title (`Status Report · ▸ 3 answers`) until you click it; in the **floating window and full-screen mode, reports show all their answers open by default** — there's room, and the reports are usually why you went big (click a line to tuck one away). Clock and schedule notes show expanded everywhere. The opened text is selectable for copying, and clicking it won't fold the line — click the line's top row to fold it back.
- **Schedule lines name their scheduler** in the who column — the people *assigned to go* are part of the line itself, same as the green **Next** strip.
- **Filter pills** — All, Notes, Reports, Status, Billing, Crew — narrow the feed; each shows its count.
- The people on the job and the **% complete** share the top line, with the manage-people button for editors; the green **Next** appointment stays pinned above the feed.
- The note bar stays at the bottom: type and press Enter to post. Editors also get {{button:outline-blue|Set % complete}} — pick the percent, add a note (required below 100%), and both land on the job.

:::example A day in the feed
**Wed, Aug 12 · 3d ago** — ⑦ `9:45a` **Abraham** — Leaving job · ⑩ `9:57a` **Abraham** — Status Report ▸ 3 answers · ⑪ `9:57a` Abraham — Working → Ready to Bill
:::

On a phone the same view fills the screen edge to edge, and each item stays a **single flowing line** — number, time, name, then the text — wrapping neatly past the number column only when you open it.
