A job's whole trail on **Jobs → Pipeline** — every note and report plus schedule, clock, status, and billing events — opens as **one view** wherever you open it from: the floating window, the panel under a row, and that panel's full-screen mode all show the same compact numbered feed.

## Open it

Three ways in, same content:

1. Click the {{button:outline-blue|2 Reports}} button in a job's **Job** column (the count varies), **or**
2. On wide screens, click the small expand arrows in the top-right corner of the row's **Job activity box**, **or**
3. Click a row's **▶ notes toggle** to open the panel under the row — its ⛶ button takes it full screen.

The job's identity sits at the top — the {{chip:blue|Job: 4821}} number badge, service type, job name, and address. Close the floating window with the **✕**, the **Esc** key, or the dark backdrop; **Esc** also exits full screen.

## How the feed reads

- **One line per item**, in columns: circled number · time · kind · who · what. The time is just the clock ("9:45a") because the **day separator** above carries the date and how long ago it was ("Wed, Aug 12 · 3d ago").
- **Notes and reports keep their circled numbers** (1 is the oldest) — the same numbers the row's activity box shows, so "check note 3" means the same thing everywhere. Schedule, clock, status, and billing lines sit between them with their colored tags, unnumbered.
- **Click a line to open it in place.** A report folds to its title (`Status Report · ▸ 3 answers`) until you click it; clock and schedule notes fold the same way. Click again to fold it back.
- **Filter pills** — All, Notes, Reports, Status, Billing, Crew — narrow the feed; each shows its count, and the numbers don't shift when you filter.
- The people on the job and the **% complete** share the top line, with the manage-people button for editors; the green **Next** appointment stays pinned above the feed. Planner roles also get {{button:green|Schedule}} and {{button:outline-blue|Week dispatch}} next to the pills.
- The note bar stays at the bottom: type and press Enter to post. Editors also get {{button:outline-blue|Set % complete}} — pick the percent, add a note (required below 100%), and both land on the job.

:::example A day in the feed
**Wed, Aug 12 · 3d ago** — ③ `9:45a` **Abraham** | Leaving job · ④ `9:57a` **REPORT Abraham** | Status Report ▸ 3 answers · `9:57a` **STATUS Abraham** | Working → Ready to Bill
:::

On a phone the same view fills the screen edge to edge, with each line's details stacked under its time and name.
