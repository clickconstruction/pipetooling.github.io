Training is a card at the bottom of your Dashboard, under My Time. In Job Mode it sits under My Schedule. It lists the courses your role must take. Each course is a set of short lessons you read at your own pace. A phone or a desk both work. Some courses are open to everyone. Those sit under an **Available** line, with no colour and no clock.

## Start a course

1. Open your [Dashboard](/dashboard) and scroll to the **Training** card.
2. Read the line at the top. It says how you stand in one colour. Red means a course is not done. Amber means a pass has lapsed or is about to. Green means you are caught up.
3. Press {{button:blue|Start}} on a course. The course opens on its first lesson.

The first course is the OSHA 30 refresher. It is thirty lessons of about five minutes each, plus a final check. It is a refresher, not an OSHA card. The card comes from the 30-hour course. The second is Intro to plumbing. It shows how water comes into a house and how it leaves, with a drawing in every lesson. The third is Intro to electrical. It shows how a house's electrical system works and where plumbing work touches it. It teaches understanding, not doing.

## Read a lesson

A lesson has five parts. **Why it matters** says why in a line or two. **Must know** lists the facts, with the numbers in bold. **Traps** lists the mistakes the real quizzes catch. **Numbers to remember** repeats the numbers as chips. **Check yourself** asks four questions. Some facts come with a drawing under the sentence. The line under the drawing says what it shows.

The list of lessons sits on the left. On a phone, press **Lessons** to open the list. You can read the lessons in any order. A tick shows each lesson you have done.

## Answer the checks

Pick one answer for each of the four questions. A right pick turns green and says why. A wrong pick turns red and says why. Press {{button:outline|Try again}} to answer that question again. The answers are in a new order.

The lesson is done when all four are right. The app records it. Press {{button:blue|Next lesson ›}} to go on, or stop and come back later. The course opens where you left off.

## Take the final check

**Final check** opens at the foot of the lesson list once every lesson is done. It asks twenty questions from every part of the course. Answer them all, then press {{button:blue|Hand it in}}.

Fourteen right passes. A pass is recorded with the day and your score. Your Training card turns green and says when the course is due again. If you miss, the page shows your score. Press {{button:outline|Try again}} for twenty new questions.

## When a course comes due

A pass is good for a set time, usually a year. Thirty days before it runs out, your card turns amber and says the day. Press {{button:blue|Take it again}} to pass the course again. A fresh pass starts the clock again.

The office can see where everyone stands. A red or amber course shows beside your name on the People page, the same way an expiring paper does. A lapsed course never stops you clocking in.

## See the due day as a strip

Press {{button:outline|Due strip}} at the top of the Training card. Each course then draws a thin line from the day you passed to the day the pass runs out. The last thirty days are shaded. A blue tick shows today. The version you passed is named under the start. A chip beside the course name says where you stand. **Due soon** means today is inside the shaded part. **Lapsed** means the day has passed. **Older version** means a dev asked everyone to take the course again after your pass. The line then ends at that day. The switch stays on for you on this phone or desk. Press it again to go back to the words.

On Settings → Training the same switch turns the counts into chips. Press {{chip:yellow|due soon 1}} to see who is due soon. Press it again to see everyone. Each name gets its chip and its own line.

## Reference

A dev turns each course on and sets who must take it and how long a pass is good for. That is done in Settings. A dev can also open a course to everyone. If your Training card says a course is not on yet, the office has not turned it on.
