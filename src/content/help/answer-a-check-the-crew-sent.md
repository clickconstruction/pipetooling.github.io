A crew member on a stage checklist can send a check they cannot answer to Dispatch. The check lands in the Dispatch inbox with a *Stage check* tag. The row carries the job, the stage and the check. Press the row to read what the crew sent and answer them.

## Find it

The check sits in the Dispatch inbox. That inbox shows in Dispatch Mode under Inbox, on the Dashboard's Teams Inbox, in Quickfill and under Checklist Review. The row reads the stage, the check and why the crew sent it.

:::example The row
{{chip:yellow|Stage check}} Trim Set · "Is the pan drain run to the hub?" · need a part: the caps are not on the truck · JP1088 ZZ TEST Riders Walk

From Luis Ortega · 12 min · Ref: JP1088 · ZZ TEST Riders Walk · 12925 FM 20, Kingsbury

{{button:outline|Open the check}} {{button:outline|Open the job}}
:::

A **Can't do it** check sits at the top of the list. A red line over it says how long the crew has waited. The check stays off the customer waiting banner. That banner is a customer's.

## Read it from the job

You can see a check with Dispatch without the inbox. On the Pipeline the stage's name wears a small flag in amber. Hold the pointer on the name to read with Dispatch. On the job's Documents tab the check's row wears the chip with the age and the reason. Dispatch's last reply sits under it. Under Questions on this job the request wears the Stage check tag.

:::example The Documents row
☐ open · 1. Is the pan drain run to the hub?

{{chip:yellow|With Dispatch · 8 min · need a part}}

Dispatch: "Hub pours Thursday. Press Not here for now." · Jo · 2 min ago
:::

## Open the window

Press the row. A window opens with everything the crew saw when they sent it.

:::example What the window shows
**JP1088 · ZZ TEST Riders Walk** {{button:outline|Open the job ›}}

12925 FM 20, Kingsbury · Trim Set · 2 of 5 answered · 3 left

**Is the pan drain run to the hub?** {{chip:gray|P-201}}

the company's question · not answered yet

**Luis Ortega** · Mon 9:31 AM · sent from the sheet
The caps for the 4 inch line are not on the truck. Two needed, units 7 and 8.
:::

The card reads the job, the address, the stage and its count. Under the card sit the check's words and the pick so far. The crew's words come first in the thread. Each reply from Dispatch follows them.

## Reply to the crew

Type in the box under the thread. Press {{button:blue|Reply}}. The crew member gets a push on their phone. The reply also sits under the check's row on their sheet until they answer it. Say what they should do next.

:::example A reply
Hub pours Thursday. Press Not here for now and answer it again Friday.
:::

## Act on the check

Press {{button:outline|Open the check}} to open the stage's sheet with that row open. There you answer it, add a note, skip it on this job or take it off. You act under your own name. The guide [fill a stage checklist on the phone](/help/fill-a-stage-checklist-on-the-phone) has the sheet's steps.

Press {{button:outline|Open the job ›}} to see the whole job in its window.

## When it closes

The request closes itself once the check is settled. An answer on the sheet, a skip on this job or a take off each closes it. The closed row says how.

:::example The closed row
Closed · Luis Ortega · Mon 2:10 PM

"Answered on the sheet by Luis Ortega · Yes"
:::

Nobody needs to close by hand what the row already settled. You can still close it yourself with words. Type the reply and press {{button:outline|Reply and close}}. The crew member hears it as a push. The person who sent the check never closes their own. The sender's window says to answer the check on the sheet instead.

## Lower the priority

A **Can't do it** check leads the inbox. When the crew is no longer stopped, press {{button:outline|Lower priority}} in the window. The press asks why in one tap. The row drops to its place in the list, oldest first.

## Leave it for now

Press {{button:outline|Not now}} to close the window. The check stays in the inbox as it was.
