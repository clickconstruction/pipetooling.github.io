If a company vehicle is assigned to you, a **My Vehicle** card shows on your Dashboard just below My Time. It's how you keep the office in the loop without a phone call.

## Sending an odometer reading

Type the number off your dash into **Odometer today** and tap {{button:blue|Send reading}} — done. When the card shows a {{chip:yellow|reading requested}} chip, the office hasn't had a number in a while; sending one clears it.

:::example What the card shows
**2019 Ram ProMaster** · {{chip:gray|121,480 mi · today}} {{chip:green|Oil OK · next 123,000}}
:::

The oil chip tracks your vehicle's oil-change schedule from the readings you send — another reason to keep them coming.

## Reporting a problem

Tap {{button:outline|Report problem}}, describe what's wrong in your own words, and pick how serious it is:

- {{chip:gray|Monitor}} — worth knowing, not urgent
- {{chip:yellow|Needs service}} — book a shop visit
- {{chip:red|Urgent}} — deal with it now

{{button:blue|Send to the office}} files it on the vehicle's record — the office sees it immediately on the fleet board, and it stays open until someone resolves it. The card shows your vehicle's open problem count so you know it was received.
