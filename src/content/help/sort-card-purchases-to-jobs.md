Every purchase on your linked company card shows up in **Job Parts Tally → Transactions**, waiting to be sorted to the job (or jobs) the material was for. Open the tally from the {{icon:gear}} **gear menu → Job Parts Tally** (or the blue wrench square on your dashboard). On your phone, each purchase is a card — store name, amount, and day — and sorting them takes a tap or two.

## Sorting one purchase

1. Tap {{button:blue|Sort to job}} on any purchase.
2. The sort screen shows the purchase up top and **your jobs from that day** — everything you clocked on or were scheduled for, from the day before through the day after.
3. Tap the job the material was for, then tap the green confirm button. Done — the next unsorted purchase slides in automatically.

:::example Buying at the supply house on Saturday?
The jobs you worked Friday–Sunday are already on screen — no searching.
:::

## Splitting across two or more jobs

Tap a **second job** (or a third) and the screen becomes a split:

- It starts **even** — a $90.42 run across two jobs shows $45.21 each.
- Type an exact amount on any line and the other line adjusts itself so the total always matches the purchase.
- Tap {{button:green|Save split}} when the numbers look right.

## Going through the whole list

Tap {{button:blue|Sort N purchases}} at the top to walk your unsorted purchases one at a time. **Skip** anything you're not sure about, use **‹ Back** to revisit, and **Undo** right after a save if you picked the wrong job.

## When the job isn't on your list

Tap **Another job…** to open the full assign window and search every job — for material bought days ahead, or for someone else's job.

## Memos

Tap **+ memo** on any card to jot what the purchase was for ("PRV + fittings for Beck"). The office sees your memo next to the transaction.

## Already-sorted purchases

Sorted cards show their job in green — tap the green label to review or change the assignment.
