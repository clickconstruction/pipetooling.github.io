See every GC job that is ours on one screen. It shows what the customers paid us, what we paid the trades and who owes us.

The owner, the leaders and the controller see it.

## Open it

1. Press {{button:outline|GC}} on the Bids page to open [GC projects](/gc).
2. Press {{chip:blue|Money}} above the projects.

## What it shows

- **The top line** says where we stand across our jobs. It counts what customers paid us less what we paid the trades.
- **Who owes us** lists each bill with money still open. A late bill comes first. A bill waiting on the architect comes last.
- **Bill day** shows what each job would ask on the next bill day.
- **The next weeks** show what comes in and goes out each week.
- **Each job** shows its price, what we billed, what the customer owes and where we stand.
- **What each job makes us** shows our fee, what buying out saved and the change orders. **Our own work** shows what our general conditions really cost against their budget.
- **Interest on late bills** shows each job that charges interest. The card shows what has built up, what we billed, what they paid and what is left to bill. [Set a job's rate](/help/bill-the-customer-on-a-gc-job#interest-on-late-bills) in its Bill the customer.
- **The late finish** shows each job's finish against the contract's substantial completion. With [a late fee typed](/help/bill-the-customer-on-a-gc-job#the-late-fee), it shows what the late days cost. A job with no schedule says so in one line.

## What our general conditions really cost

General conditions are the superintendent's time, the trailer, temporary power and the dumpsters. Name the Pipeline job they are spent on, and Money counts what they really cost.

1. Open **Our number** on the job's card.
2. Press {{button:outline|Name the Pipeline job}} under the three boxes.
3. Find the job by its number, name or address, and press {{button:outline|Use this job}}.

A job our own crew already uses shows {{chip:gray|On Plumbing already}} and cannot be picked. One job counts once.

- While the job is being built, they count at their budget. Once the spend passes the budget, the overrun counts that day.
- Once the job closes, they count at what they cost. A saving counts then too.
- **Earned so far** counts them as real money. It is what we billed for them less what they cost so far.

Labor costs show only to people who can see pay. Anyone else sees general conditions at their budget, with a line saying why.

## What comes later

Some parts wait on work still being built. Each one says so in one line and shows no number.

- Paid in counts once customers pay our bills in the app.
- Paid out counts once the trades draw in the app.
- Until the trades report their work, a bill carries only the signed change orders.
- What we bill month by month comes once each job's schedule is in the app.
