The material location report is one paper that shows what is in every cage and box across a development. The report reads the same ledger the crew writes on the phone and the office reads on Jobs → Materials. You print it from the Development page and save it as a PDF.

## Open the report

1. Open the development. The guide [see everything on a development in one place](/help/see-everything-on-a-development) shows the doors to the page.
2. Find the **Jobs & bids** look.
3. Press {{button:outline|Print the material location report}} in the look's head.

A window opens and reads every job on the site. The paper draws once the read is in. The press is there for the office roles, the same people who open a job's materials.

## Read the paper

The head names the site, its customer and its address. At the right it shows the day the report was printed and who printed it. Under the head one line counts the jobs, the places, the cages and boxes, and the lines on hand.

Each job with material on site gets a section. Under the job, each place on the site is a heading. Under a place, each cage or box is a block with its stencil and its kind, such as *A1 · cage*. The block carries the box's short code, the same one its label has. The block lists every part on hand with its count and its unit. Each line also shows the day something was last recorded on it. An empty box reads *Empty*, so you know the box exists and holds nothing.

After the places, **Unsorted** lists the delivered parts nobody has put away yet. The job's boxes with no place yet come last under **No place yet**.

The paper closes with **Nothing on site**. One line names each job with no material on site. A job with no cages or boxes says so. A job whose boxes are all empty names them with their places.

## Switch the grouping

The window's head has two chips, **By job** and **By location**. By job is the paper above. Press **By location** to read the same boxes by place instead.

By location gives each place one section across the jobs. Two jobs that each name a place Arena share one Arena section. Under the place, each job is a heading with its cages and boxes. Unsorted and No place yet get sections of their own at the end, with the job named on each box.

The app remembers your last pick on this device. The counts and the lines are the same both ways. Only the order changes.

## Print it and save a PDF

1. Press {{button:blue|Print}} in the window's head.
2. In the print window, pick **Save as PDF** as the printer.
3. Press Save.

The paper prints on Letter sheets. A cage or box and its lines stay together on one page. The foot of every page names the ledger the paper reads, the day and who printed it. The report is a plain print and files no copy.

## When the paper says nothing is on site

A development with no cage or box at all prints the head and one paragraph. The paragraph says a job's Materials page is where a cage or box is added. The guide [see what is on every job site and what walked away](/help/see-what-is-on-every-job-site-and-what-walked-away) shows how to add one. The crew's phone is where parts are counted into a box.
