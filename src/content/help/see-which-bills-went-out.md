Bills sent is one list of every bill that left the company. Each line says who got the bill and the minute it went.

You do nothing to fill it. A line appears when someone sends a bill from the app.

## Open the list

1. Open [Bills sent](/bills-sent).
2. Save the page as a bookmark so you can come back to it.

On a phone you can add the page to your home screen. The list opens to the newest day.

You can also reach it from [Documents](/documents?tab=sent). Press **Sent copies**, then {{chip:green|Bills}}, then the **Bills sent** link.

## Read a line

Each line is one send. The line shows these facts.

- The time it went. All times are Central.
- The bill's number, the job and who the bill is for.
- The amount.
- The address it went to. A copy address follows the words *copy to*.
- How it went, and who pressed send.

Press **Copy as sent** to open the email as the customer read it. The bill is attached to that copy.

Press the green job name to open the job.

## A bill sent more than once

Every send is its own line. A second send reads *Email, sent again*.

A second send also says which send it is and the day of the first one. The words read like *2nd send. First sent Tue, Nov 3.*

## Read a day

The days run newest first. Each day has a band with a count and a total.

The total counts each bill once, on the day it first went. A resend is listed and not added again.

Two tiles at the top give the same count for today and for this week. The week starts on Sunday.

## Statements and other papers

A statement to a general contractor is one line, marked {{chip:yellow|Statement}}. The line shows the statement's total and how many jobs the statement lists.

A statement lists bills that already went. A statement's total is never added to the day.

A printed pay sheet reads *Printed*. The app cannot know who was handed the paper, and the line says so.

A pay application saved as a file reads *Saved as a file*. The app cannot know where the file went.

## Find one bill

Type in the box at the top. You can type a job, a customer, an address or a bill number.

Press {{chip:gray|Email}}, {{chip:gray|Printed}} or {{chip:gray|Statement}} to see one kind of send. Press {{chip:gray|All}} to see every send again.

## What the list does not hold yet

The list starts on Oct 5, 2026. The app began keeping a copy of each send that day.

A bill entered from another program has no line here yet. A bill handed over on site has no line either.

A bill that was made and never sent is not on the list yet. Ask the office when a bill you expect is missing.

To find other papers we sent, see [find a copy of what we sent someone](/help/find-a-copy-of-what-we-sent).
