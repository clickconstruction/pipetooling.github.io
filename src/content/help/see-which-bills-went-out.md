Bills sent is one list of every bill that left the company. Each line says who got the bill and the minute it went.

You do nothing to fill it. A line appears when someone sends a bill from the app.

## Open the list

1. Open [Bills sent](/bills-sent).
2. Save the page as a bookmark so you can come back to it.

On a phone you can add the page to your home screen. The list opens to the newest day.

You can also reach it from the [Pipeline](/jobs?tab=stages). Find the **Billed Awaiting Payment** header. Press {{button:outline|Bills sent}} at the end of its row of buttons.

There is a third way in from [Documents](/documents?tab=sent). Press **Sent copies**, then {{chip:green|Bills}}, then the **Bills sent** link.

## Put a button on your Dashboard

1. Turn on Pin Mode. See [turn on Pin Mode to pin pages](/help/pin-mode).
2. Open [Bills sent](/bills-sent).
3. Press {{button:outline|Pin}} in the footer at the bottom of the page.

A **Bills sent** button now sits in the pinned row on your Dashboard. One tap opens the list.

The owner and developers can pin the page for someone else. Press the small button beside **Pin** in the same footer. Pick the person, then press **Pin for**.

## On a phone

The list is the first thing on the screen. Each send is a card with three or four short rows.

- The first row has the time, how the bill went, who sent it and the amount.
- The next row names the job and the customer.
- The last rows hold the address, then whether the bill arrived.

Tap anywhere on a card to open that bill's record. The record has **Copy as sent** and **Print this record** as full buttons.

The chips sit in one row. Slide the row sideways to reach the rest.

The yellow box is one line on a phone. Tap the line, or the yellow tile above, to open the box.

**Print** and **Download CSV** are under the list.

## Read a line

Each line is one send. The line shows these facts.

- The time it went. All times are Central.
- The bill's number, the job and who the bill is for.
- The amount.
- The address it went to. A copy address follows the words *copy to*.
- How it went, and who pressed send.

Press **Copy as sent** to open the email as the customer read it. The bill is attached to that copy.

Press the green job name to open the job.

## Did the email arrive

An email line says what the mail service last reported.

- *Delivered* and a time means their mail server took the email.
- *Opened* and a time means someone opened the email after it was delivered.
- *Bounced. It did not arrive.* means their mail server refused the email. The line has a red border.
- *Sent. Their mail server has not answered yet.* means the answer is still to come.

Delivered does not mean someone read the email. Opened is the stronger word.

A red tile at the top reads **Did not arrive** with a count. The tile counts sends that did not arrive on a bill that is still open.

Press {{chip:gray|Did not arrive}} to see only those sends. Fix the address on the bill and send the bill again.

A line whose bill was paid says *Paid* and the day.

## A bill sent more than once

Every send is its own line. A second send reads *Email, sent again*.

A second send also says which send it is and the day of the first one. The words read like *2nd send. First sent Tue, Nov 3.*

## See every send of one bill

Press the bill's name on a line. The name is underlined.

The bill's record opens. The record lists every send of that bill, newest first.

Each send shows the day and time, who sent it, who it went to and whether it arrived. Press **Copy as sent** on a send to open that email.

Press {{button:outline|Print this record}} to print the record as one page. The page is for your file or for counsel.

The address of the record is a link you can save or paste. Press **← All bills sent** to go back to the list.

## Read a day

The days run newest first. Each day has a band with a count and a total.

The total counts each bill once, on the day it first went. A resend is listed and not added again.

Two tiles at the top give the same count for today and for this week. The week starts on Sunday.

## Statements and other papers

A statement to a general contractor is one line, marked {{chip:yellow|Statement}}. The line shows the statement's total and how many jobs the statement lists.

A statement lists bills that already went. A statement's total is never added to the day.

A printed pay sheet reads *Printed*. The app cannot know who was handed the paper, and the line says so.

A pay application saved as a file reads *Saved as a file*. The app cannot know where the file went.

## Find one bill

Type in the box at the top. You can type a job, a customer, an address or a bill number.

Press a chip to see one kind of send. The chips are {{chip:gray|Email}}, {{chip:gray|Text}}, {{chip:gray|Printed}}, {{chip:gray|Statement}}, {{chip:gray|Housecall Pro}} and {{chip:gray|Recorded by hand}}. Press {{chip:gray|All}} to see every send again.

## Print the list or save it as a spreadsheet

Narrow the list first if you want only part of it. Use the search box or a chip.

Press {{button:outline|Print}} to print what the list shows. The page keeps the days, the totals and each line's words.

Press {{button:outline|Download CSV}} to save what the list shows as a spreadsheet file. Each send is one row.

The file has a column that says whether a row is in its day's total. Add only those rows to get the same totals as the screen.

The bills with no send on record come along in both. Those bills are last in the file and first on the printed page.

## Bills with no send on record

A yellow box sits above the days when a bill was made and never sent from the app. The box reads like *3 bills are billed with no send on record*.

Each row names the job, the customer and the amount. The row also says when the bill was made and that the bill is not paid.

The app cannot see a bill handed over on site. The app cannot see a bill sent from a person's own mailbox either. Ask the office how a bill in the box went out.

Press **Show all** to see every open bill in the box. Press **Show them** to see the bills that were paid with no send.

Press {{chip:yellow|No send on record}} to see the box alone. The yellow tile at the top counts the open bills in the box.

## Say how a bill went

Do this when you know how a bill in the yellow box went out.

1. Press **Say how it went…** on the bill's row.
2. Press the chip that says how the bill went. The pressed chip turns dark.
3. Type who the bill went to. Add an address if you have one.
4. Pick the day the bill went.
5. Press {{button:blue|Record how it went}}.

The bill leaves the yellow box. The bill now has a line on the day you picked.

The line says *Recorded by hand*, with your name and the day you recorded it. The line has no copy, because the app sent nothing.

A record made by hand cannot be changed once saved. Check the day and the name before you press.

No chip may fit how the bill went. Then add a way to the list. See [Change the list of ways](#change-the-list-of-ways).

## Change the list of ways

The chips under *How the bill went* are a list your office keeps. Everyone who opens Bills sent picks from the same list.

To add a way:

1. Press **＋ Add a way**.
2. Type the new way in a few words.
3. Press {{button:blue|Add to the list}}.

The new way shows as a chip. The app picks the new chip for you.

To change the list, press **Edit list**. A window opens with every way on the list.

- Press **Rename** to change the words of a way. Then press {{button:blue|Save the name}}.
- Press ▲ or ▼ to move a way up or down.
- Press **Take off** to take a way off the form.
- Press **Show** beside *Taken off* to see the ways taken off. Press **Bring back** to return one.

Each change saves when you press it. Press {{button:blue|Done}} to close the window.

A bill already recorded keeps the words it was recorded with. A new name changes the chip and no older line.

The list holds 12 ways at most. The list always keeps one way.

Every way counts under the **Recorded by hand** chip, whatever its words.

You may see no **＋ Add a way** chip. Then the list did not load. Open the page again.

## Sends that kept no copy

Some lines have no **Copy as sent**. Each one says what the app does not have.

- An email sent before Oct 5, 2026 shows its time. The line reads *The address was not kept.*
- A bill entered from Housecall Pro shows *Day only*. The app has the day, with no address, no sender and no copy.
- A text with the pay link shows the phone number and who sent the text. The line says *Delivered* when the text arrived.

## What the list cannot know

A bill handed over on site leaves no record. A bill emailed from a person's own mailbox leaves none either.

Those bills show in the yellow box until someone says how the bill went.

To find other papers we sent, see [find a copy of what we sent someone](/help/find-a-copy-of-what-we-sent).
