A machine template is a kind of machine at a size. Small excavator, Medium excavator, Skid steer and Directional drill are templates. Each one carries two things. The gauges the crew reads on the check sheet. The questions the crew answers on the walk. A machine on the fleet board picks a template and reads these.

The card sits at Settings → Jobs & billing → **Machine templates**. It ships with nine templates filled in. Change the words to match the machines in the yard.

## Pick a list

The tabs across the top pick a list. **Every machine's walk** holds the questions every machine gets. **The leaving check** holds the three questions asked at the end of the day. The tabs after those are the templates. {{button:outline|+ New template}} starts one.

## Edit a template

Give it a name, a kind and a size. Size can be small, medium, large or one size. The name is what the office sees when it adds a machine.

Under **Gauges** each gauge says what it holds and where to read it on the machine. Pick its look. A tank fills in quarters. A sight glass has a line the oil sits on. A dipstick has ADD and FULL. A dial has a red zone and a green zone. The small drawing shows what the crew will see. {{button:outline|+ Tank}}, {{button:outline|+ Sight glass}}, {{button:outline|+ Dipstick}} and {{button:outline|+ Dial}} add one. {{button:outline|Remove}} takes one off.

Under **This kind's own questions** add the questions only this kind gets. Write each as a statement the crew checks, like Bucket pins, teeth and the thumb tight. Tick **a No holds it** on a question that should stop the machine. The arrows set the order. {{button:outline|Take off}} removes one.

{{button:blue|Save Small excavator}} writes the template and its questions. {{button:outline|Take it off the list}} retires a template. Machines that picked it keep working.

## The questions every machine gets

Open **Every machine's walk**. These are asked first on every morning walk. Tracks, hoses, lights, the seat belt, the fire extinguisher, grease and the air filter come filled in. {{button:outline|Add one}} adds a question. {{button:blue|Save 7 questions}} writes the list.

## The leaving check

Open **The leaving check**. It is short on purpose. Nothing new broken, parked and locked, cab cleaned out. The gauges, the hours and where the machine is tonight are asked after these on their own.

## A machine's own gauges

A machine can keep a gauge list of its own. Open the fleet board, tap {{button:outline|Gauges}} on the machine and change the list there. {{button:outline|Use the template's}} goes back to the template. See [manage company vehicles and track their odometers](/help/manage-company-vehicles#machines-on-the-board).
