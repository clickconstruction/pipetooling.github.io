When we still have to do something for someone, mark it in their thread. Follow up keeps it on the list until a team note marks it done.

The office team and estimators can use it.

## Mark what we owe

1. Open the person's thread from Follow up.
2. Press {{button:outline|We owe them…}} under their name.
3. Pick what we owe from the list. The steps of ours in their flow come first, then the usual asks.
4. Or type your own words, and press {{button:outline|Use these words}}.
5. Press the day blank to pick a day. The day is optional.
6. Press {{button:blue|Mark it}}.

A gray line in the thread says who marked it. The person shows under **We owe them** on [Follow up](/gc?view=followUp). It stays our move until it is done.

## Change what we owe or its day

1. Press {{button:outline|Change what we owe}} under their name.
2. Press what we owe, or the day, and pick again.

Each change adds a gray line to the thread. Press {{button:outline|Clear the day}} to keep it with no day.

A day you type that is not a date is kept as words, like *after the walk*. Words never bring the row back by themselves.

## Mark it done

1. Press {{button:outline|Mark it done, with a team note}}.
2. The reply box turns to a team note. Say how it got done, or why nothing is owed.
3. Press {{button:blue|Done, with this note}}.

Changed your mind? Press {{button:outline|Not done yet}} above the box.

The note stays in the thread. The person leaves **We owe them**. The move goes back to whoever wrote last.

## Put it off

Press {{button:outline|Put it off}}. The person leaves Follow up until Monday morning. What we owe stays marked.
