The collections law firm gets one private link, no sign-in, that opens every account a dev has marked attorney-ready — as the same five-section packet the Legal desk shows — and nothing else.

## Create and send the link

1. On **Jobs → Pipeline → Collections**, open {{button:outline|⚖ Legal}}. The header shows {{button:outline|🌐 Firm's link}} once a firm is set up on Settings → Jobs & billing → Collections law firm.
2. {{button:blue|Create the firm's link}} mints it. {{button:blue|Copy link}} puts it on your clipboard; send it however you like. {{button:outline|Preview ↗}} opens what the firm sees without counting as their visit.
3. **Rotate** mints a new link and kills the old one. **Turn off** is the kill switch: the firm gets "This link is no longer active" until you create a new one.

## What the firm sees

- Every matter marked attorney-ready, largest first, with its stage (new · demand sent · suit filed · judgment).
- The same five tabs as the desk: **Account · Paper · Their word · Evidence · Fees & steps** — built from the same records by the same rules, so the firm and the office never disagree.
- On **Paper**, *Where each job stands* is the job's lien timeline — the same rail the Lien desk draws, every Chapter 53 step dated from the job's hours and filings, today marked, one *Next on the path* line — and *The paper that went out* lists each envelope once: the day, by the run or by hand, the method and tracking, the claim as printed, every job's share when one paper covered several, the months as printed (*as information* where a month's window had already closed), and the saved copy.
- Only the entries you marked **to counsel**. Held entries never leave the office: the portal function applies your decisions before anything is sent.
- Signed agreements as PDF links (good for an hour, minted fresh on every open), the exhibits list, and {{button:outline|⎙ Print packet}} — the browser's print-to-PDF is the packet.
- **Click's particulars for filing**: legal entity, license, registered agent, custodian of records, affiant, office phone and email, the W-9 note — set them once on Settings → Jobs & billing under the firm.

:::example Pull back and the link
Pulling an account back on the desk removes it from the portal on the firm's next open. Turning the link off hides everything at once; the matters and their record stay exactly as they were.
:::

## The lien grid

A third panel beside **Matters** and **Notifications**: every billed job with money open and a lien month, one row each in counsel's twelve columns — the same rows the office's Lien desk Timeline tab holds, read live. Pick a GC, switch **Something due** to **All** for the whole book, and {{button:blue|⎙ Print the grid}} hands over the same letter-landscape page the desk prints. A red **?** is a fact the office has not entered yet (the payment bond, paid out to the GC, the 10 % reserved, the owner's contract completion).

## What the firm does on the portal

On a matter's **Fees & steps** tab the firm can:

- **Add a fee or cost** — an amount and a note. It rolls into the matter's total demand and shows on the desk's Fees & steps.
- **Record a step** — demand sent on firm letterhead · suit filed · judgment entered · settled — with the court, cause number or terms. The step moves the account's stage on your Pipeline row chip; *settled* closes the matter.
- **Record a payment received** — money the firm holds. You apply it on the job with {{button:outline|Mark Paid}} from the Pipeline row, then press {{button:outline|Mark applied}} on the desk: the recovery and the firm's contingency (as a legal cost on the matter) are recorded.
- **Ask the office** — a question you answer from the desk; the answer shows on their portal.
- **Answer the office** — your asks sit at the top of their Fees & steps under *From the office*: a question gets an answer box; a sign-off on one job (the owner wants to pay Click direct while the GC is silent) gets {{button:blue|Signed off}} / {{button:outline|Not yet}} with a note. Their answer lands on your Needs You card.

Each act lands on the Dashboard as **"The law firm has N things for you"** (office roles) and clears from the desk's Fees & steps tab when you answer, apply or acknowledge it. The firm never marks anything paid, edits a job, or emails the customer through Click.
