Your record is a small card on your Dashboard. It lists up to three things this week that only you can put right.

The card is on the Job Mode view too. When your record is clean, the **Your record** card is not there at all.

## The three things

- **You're still on the clock from a past day.** You clocked in and never clocked out. So the day cannot be approved. You tap {{button:blue|Fix day}}. It opens that day in My Time. You set the clock-out and save.
- **A job you worked has no % complete.** Nobody has said how far along it is. So the office is guessing. You tap {{button:blue|Set %}}. It opens the job's progress. You pick the number that is true and save.
- **No report on a job you worked.** You clocked hours there this week. There is no field report from you. You tap {{button:blue|Report}}. It opens the report for that job.

:::example A Friday card
**Your record** · this week · 2 things only you can fix
You're still on the clock from Thursday — Clocked in at 878 Take 5 Seguin, never clocked out. {{button:blue|Fix day}}
1007 SpaceX has no % complete — You clocked 24h there this week. {{button:blue|Set %}}
:::

## What it isn't

There are no dollars on it and no scores. It is your own hours, your jobs and your reports. It covers the current pay week, Sunday to Saturday. Hours waiting on the office's approval are not your item. Clocking out is all you have to do.
