**Your record** is a small card on your Dashboard — on the Job Mode view too — that lists up to three things this week that only you can put right. When your record is clean, the card isn't there at all.

## The three things

- **You're still on the clock from a past day.** You clocked in and never clocked out, so the day can't be approved. {{button:blue|Fix day}} opens that day in My Time — set the clock-out and save.
- **A job you worked has no % complete.** Nobody has said how far along it is, so the office is guessing. {{button:blue|Set %}} opens the job's progress — pick the number that's true and save.
- **No report on a job you worked.** You clocked hours there this week and there's no field report from you. {{button:blue|Report}} opens the report for that job.

:::example A Friday card
**Your record** · this week · 2 things only you can fix
You're still on the clock from Thursday — Clocked in at 878 Take 5 Seguin, never clocked out. {{button:blue|Fix day}}
1007 SpaceX has no % complete — You clocked 24h there this week. {{button:blue|Set %}}
:::

## What it isn't

There are no dollars on it and no scores. It's your own hours, your jobs and your reports, for the current pay week (Sunday to Saturday). Hours waiting on the office's approval aren't your item — clocking out is all you have to do.
