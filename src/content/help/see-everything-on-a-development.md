A development is one site with many bids and jobs on it. The Development page shows the whole site on one screen, in five looks.

You put bids and jobs into a development first. The guide [group bids and jobs into a development](/help/group-jobs-into-a-development) shows how. Then the page gathers them.

## Open the page

1. Open a bid or a job that is in a development.
2. Find the house line near the top. It names the development with a small house.
3. Press the development's name.

The page opens with the site's name, its customer and its GC at the top. Under them you see who is on site and five counts.

## Read the five counts

The counts are Waiting, Bids, Jobs, Bid undecided and Open on bills. Each one is a rule over the site's records.

Bid undecided counts the bids sent or still being worked. An archived bid sits apart and is not counted. The Bids count names how many are archived.

1. Rest your pointer on a count.
2. A card drops under it with the records behind the number. Each record is a door.
3. Press the count to keep the card open. Press Escape to close it.

On a phone, tap the count. The card opens as a sheet under the counts.

## Jump between the five looks

A strip under the counts names five looks: History, Pieces, Money, Plans and Questions. Each look shows its count and what in it waits on you.

1. Press a look's name to jump to it.
2. Press the chevron on a look's card to fold it. The page remembers your folds on this device.

## Follow the site in History

History is one dated list, newest first. It holds what the records did and what people wrote. A send, a stage move, a bill or a payment draws a line. So does a note on a job or a bid, a plan set filed, and the customer's contact log.

1. Press a chip under the search box to narrow the list. Press {{chip:blue|Plans}} to see every plan set filed, newest first.
2. Type a name, a number or a word into the search box, marked with a spyglass, to find a line.
3. Press {{button:outline|Mark as looked}} when you have read the new lines. The list draws a line where you stopped.

A long line shows its first words and a **more** press. Press it and the whole line opens in a window.

## Keep what happened

An email came in, a call was made, someone walked the site. History keeps those too, at the time they happened.

1. Press {{button:outline|Write a line}} at the top of History. Type what happened in the box.
2. Pick what it is about under About. The site is picked by default. Press the box and type a number or a name to find a job, a bid or the customer. The pieces written about last sit near the top.
3. Set when it happened. The box starts at now. Set it back to the email's own time.
4. Pick the source, like {{chip:blue|Email}} or {{chip:blue|Call}}.
5. Press {{button:blue|Keep it}}.

The line reads at the top at once. A kept line never changes. To correct one, keep a second line that says so.


Notes a record carries with no date sit under the chips in a block called On record. A bid's notes, a job's collections note and the customer's terms live there.

## Pair each bid with the job it became

Pieces lists each bid beside the job it became. A gap in the row means the pair is not on record yet.

1. Press {{button:outline|Pair bids with jobs}} above the table.
2. In a gap, press the likely pair the page names, or press {{button:outline|Pick the job}} to choose one.
3. A window shows the pair before the page keeps it. Press the same words in the window to confirm.
4. Press {{button:outline|New job from bid}} when the job does not exist yet.

The window shows each fact now and after. The ones that change are marked. The rest read the same.

Press {{button:outline|Unpair}} on a paired row to take a pair apart. A window shows what changes first. The message after it offers {{button:outline|Put it back}}.

Press {{button:outline|Mark the 2 paired bids won}} above the table to set won on paired bids with no outcome. A window shows each bid as the Bid Board will read it. The message after it offers {{button:outline|Undo}}.

The paid jobs fold under the table with their sum. Press **Show them** to see them.

## See what is at the address but not in the development

A line above the looks counts the bids and jobs at the site's address that are not in it yet. Rest on a chip in that line to see them.

Press {{button:blue|Put the N in}} to put them all in at once. A window shows the counts now and after, and the pieces that go in. Press the same words in the window to confirm.

Press {{button:outline|Show the N here}} to draw them in the Pieces table first. Edit Bid and Edit Job still put one record in at a time.

## Read the money

Money shows the site's progress as one bar, then every job by stage with what is billed, paid and left. Press a column head to sort a section. Press the band's note about jobs with no percent to sort by percent and light them.

## Check the plans and the questions

Plans lists every plan set, newest first, with the pieces on it. A chip counts the pieces without plans. Press it to see them. Ask the estimators about them from the card. The card keeps the question in view while it waits, and shows the answer when it comes.

Questions lists the open questions on the site's pieces and how long each has waited. With none open, the card shows the kinds a site waits on.

## What an estimator sees

An estimator opens the same page. It shows the pieces, the history, the plans and the questions. It shows no money. The Money look is one line that says the office reads it.

A job's number opens nothing for an estimator. The job's stage, crew, plans and history are on this page.

## Ask a question

1. Press {{button:blue|Ask a question}} at the top of the page.
2. Pick who answers. The estimators take a bid, its plans or its price. Dispatch takes a crew or a day on site.
3. Pick what it is about. The site is picked by default. You can pick a job or a bid.
4. Type the question and press {{button:blue|Ask the estimators}} or {{button:blue|Ask Dispatch}}.

The question lands in their inbox with the site named on it. The answer comes back here, under Questions.

## Ask from a job or a bid

You can also ask from a job's window or a bid's window.

1. Open the job or the bid.
2. Find Questions on this job or Questions on this bid.
3. Press {{button:blue|Ask the estimators}} or {{button:blue|Ask Dispatch}}.
4. Type the question and press the button with the same name.

The sheet already names the job or the bid. The question lands in their inbox with that record named on it. The answer comes back on the window and on this page, under Questions.

The window lists every question asked about the record. The open ones come first with how long each has waited. The answered ones follow with the answer's words.

A question asked on a bid also shows on the job that bid became. The job's list marks it asked on the bid.
