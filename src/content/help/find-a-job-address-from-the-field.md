Someone says "head over to 878" and you need the address. Tap the {{icon:search}} **search icon** in the top strip of your Dashboard, type the job number, part of the name or part of the street, and tap the job. A small card shows what you need — nothing else, and nothing changes on the job.

## Where it is

- On a phone, the search icon sits in the top strip next to the Dashboard icon.
- On a wider screen it sits at the right end of the header.
- It is there on every page, not just the Dashboard. Farm Mode hides it, like everything else.

## What the card shows

:::example Job 878
**J878 · Example Plaza** · Plumbing
1200 Example Rd, Kyle TX
{{button:outline|Directions}} {{button:outline|Call office}} {{button:outline|Close}}
_Read only. To start work on it, use the Clock In button._
:::

- **Directions** opens the address in your maps app.
- **Call office** dials the office.
- **Close** puts it away. Nothing you did here is recorded on the job.

## What it is not

- It does **not** clock you in. The Clock In button at the top of your Dashboard is still the only way to start time on a job — this card is for reading an address, not for punching.
- It only finds **jobs**. Bids, estimates and customers stay with the office.
- It shows no money. The office's job window, with billing and costs, is not part of this.

## If the job isn't there

Type fewer letters — two are enough to start. If it still doesn't appear, the job may not be on the ledger yet: tap **Call office** from any other card, or use the header's **dispatch** button to ask.
