Someone says "head over to 878" and you need the address. The search in the top strip of your Dashboard finds the job and shows where it is.

Tap the {{icon:search}} **search icon** in the top strip of your Dashboard. Type the job number, part of the name or part of the street. Tap the job. A small card shows what you need and nothing else. Nothing changes on the job.

## Where it is

- On a phone, the search icon sits in the top strip next to the Dashboard icon.
- On a wider screen it sits at the right end of the header.
- It is there on every page, not just the Dashboard. Farm Mode hides it, like everything else.

## What the card shows

:::example Job 878
**J878 · Example Plaza** · Plumbing
1200 Example Rd, Kyle TX
{{button:outline|Directions}} {{button:outline|Call office}} {{button:outline|Close}}
_Read only. To start work on it, use the Clock In button._
:::

- **Directions** opens the address in your maps app.
- **Call office** dials the office.
- **Close** puts it away. Nothing you did here is recorded on the job.

## What it is not

- It does **not** clock you in. The Clock In button at the top of your Dashboard is still the only way to start time. This card is for reading an address, not for punching.
- It only finds **jobs**. Bids, estimates and customers stay with the office.
- It shows no money. The office's job window, with billing and costs, is not part of this.

## If the job isn't there

Type fewer letters. Two are enough to start. If it still doesn't appear, the job may not be on the ledger yet, the app's list of jobs. Tap **Call office** from any other card, or use the header's **dispatch** button to ask.
