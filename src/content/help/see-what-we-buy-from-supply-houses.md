Every supply house bill is read line by line. **What we buy** adds those lines up. The page shows which parts the company buys, how often, how many and for how much.

Open **Materials**, then {{button:outline|What we buy}} in the Purchase group.

## Read the page

Four figures sit at the top.

- **Spent on parts** is the spend in the months you picked, and how many bills it is on.
- **Charges** is freight, fuel and fees. A charge is never counted as a part.
- **Distinct parts** is how many rows the table has. The line under it says how many have no part yet.
- **Bills not counted** is how many bills wait for a person. Each of those bills has a reason on [Supply houses](/materials?tab=supply-houses).

Each row under the figures is one thing the company buys.

- **Bought** is how many bills carry it.
- **Quantity** is how many, in the unit the bills print.
- **Spend** is what it cost in the months you picked.
- **By month** is a small bar for each month. Hold the pointer on a bar to read its month and its dollars.
- **Houses** lists where it was bought, the most spent first.
- **Last paid** is the price for one on the newest bill.

## Narrow it down

:::steps

1. Pick a house under **House** to see one house alone.

2. Pick a job under **Job** to see one job alone. A bill split across jobs gives each job its share.

3. Set **From** and **To** to change the months. The page opens on the last six.

4. Press {{button:outline|Spend}}, {{button:outline|Quantity}} or {{button:outline|How often}} to sort.

:::

Press *Clear the filters* at the foot of the page to go back to every house and every job.

## A row with no part yet

A house prints its own code for a part on every line. The parts book learns a house's code once. Until then the row is counted by the house and its code, and it reads *no part yet*.

The same part bought at two houses is two rows until both codes are matched. Teach a code under **Their numbers** on the part. See [keep a supply house's number on a part](/help/keep-a-supply-houses-number-on-a-part).

## What the page cannot say

Only bills whose lines add up are counted. A quote, an acknowledgement and a ship ticket are never counted.

A bill says what was bought. A bill does not say what was installed or what is on a truck.

Card purchases at big-box stores are not here. A card purchase has no lines on any paper.
