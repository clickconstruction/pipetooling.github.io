A set of plans never comes alone. A spec book, a schedule, the owner's task list or a soil report comes with it. Each one lands beside the set it goes with, on the job's **Documents** tab under **Plans**.

Keeping a paper does three things at once. The paper lands under its set. The site's History gets one line for that day. The question the paper raised can go to the people who answer it.

## Read the papers on a job

Open the job and press the **Documents** tab. Under **Plans**, the **Papers with the plans** block lists every paper on the job's part.

Papers sit under the set each one goes with. The set the job builds from says **building from**. A paper on no set reads under **On the part itself**.

Each line names the paper's kind, who sent it and the day it came. Press {{button:outline|Open ↗}} to open it in Drive.

An amber chip on a paper means it asked us something and the answer has not come. A green chip means the answer came. Press the chip to read the question.

**Owed to us** lists the papers we are still waiting on.

## Keep a paper

1. Press {{button:outline|Keep a paper}}.
2. Pick the **Kind**, such as *Spec* or *Task list*.
3. Type the paper's **Name**. Use the name on its cover.
4. Pick the set it **Goes with**. The set the job builds from is first.
5. Pick who it is **From**.
6. Pick the day it **Came**.
7. Type a **Memo**. Say what it means for us, in a line.
8. Paste the Drive link under **Link**. You can leave it empty and add it later.
9. Press {{button:blue|Keep the paper}}.

The form shows the History line before you press. The line starts with *Kept*, names the paper and the set, and ends with your memo.

When a new copy of a paper comes, pick the old one under **Replaces**. The old one folds away and the History line says what it replaced.

## Hand on the question it raised

Some papers ask something of us. The owner's task list asks for a count. A spec asks which finish the overhead piping gets.

1. Tick **Asks us**.
2. Pick **Who answers**. Pick *The estimators* for a price or a count. Pick *Dispatch* for a date or a crew.
3. Type **The ask** in a line.
4. Press {{button:blue|Keep the paper}}.

The question lands in that lane's inbox the same way an **Ask the estimators** press does. The paper shows the amber chip until the answer comes. The answer then reads on the paper's line and on the site's History.

:::example A spec that asks a question

Kept spec Finishes sheet, the owner, on Addendum 1: paint colours by room · asked the estimators: which finish on the overhead piping?

:::

## Record a paper we are owed

Some papers never come. The owner promised a soil report and the crew is still waiting.

1. Press {{button:outline|We are owed a paper}}.
2. Pick the **Kind** and type the **Name**.
3. Pick the day you **Asked on**.
4. Pick **Who answers** and type **Who owes it**, such as *The owner owes the soil report*.
5. Press {{button:blue|Keep it as owed}}.

The paper reads under **Owed to us** in amber. When the paper arrives, press {{button:outline|It came}} on its line, paste the link, and press {{button:outline|It came}} again. The paper moves under its set and the History gets a second line.

## Keep a paper straight from the mail

A paper often arrives as an attachment. Open the mail in **My Mail**. When the mail is tagged to a job, a press offers to keep it as a paper on that job.

Press it. The form opens with the kind, the name, the sender and the day filled from the mail. Check them. Pick the set it **Goes with**. Press {{button:blue|Keep the paper}}.

The mail's files follow the paper. The files land in the paper's own folder under the job's **Plans** folder in Drive. The line under the mail says how many landed.

The line turns amber when a file stayed behind, and it names the file with its reason. A file too big for us to keep stays in Gmail. Press {{button:outline|File 1 file again}} when a file did not land. The file goes into the same paper's folder.

A mail that is not on the job yet cannot bring its files. The form says so. Press {{button:outline|Put it on the job}} first, then keep the paper.

A job inside a development may not have named its part yet. The form then asks which part of the site the paper is for. A job outside any development gets its own part made for it.

## See the papers on the whole site

On the development page, press {{chip:blue|Plans}}. Each part's row now has a **Papers** column. The count opens the list of papers under each set. An amber chip counts the open asks. A second one counts the papers owed.

Every paper also wrote its line on the site's **History** the day it came. Read the History to see the plans and the papers in one stream.

## Move a paper to another part

A paper kept on the wrong part can move. On the development page, press {{chip:blue|Plans}} and open the part's papers. Press **Move to…** on the paper. Pick the part it belongs on, then press {{button:blue|Move it}}. The paper lands on that part itself, since the set it went with stays behind. The site's History gets a line that says where it moved from.

:::tryit

A change order was kept on the Bunker, and it is about the Lodge. Open the Bunker's papers, press **Move to…** on it, pick Lodge and press {{button:blue|Move it}}. It now reads under the Lodge.

:::

## Take a paper off

Press **×** on a paper, then {{button:outline|Remove}}. The press is on the job's Papers section and on the site's Plans look. Removing a paper never deletes the file in Drive, and its History line stays.

See [keep the plans on a job](/help/keep-the-plans-on-a-job) for the sets themselves.
