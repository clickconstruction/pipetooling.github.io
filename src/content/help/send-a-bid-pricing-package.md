**Share with a teammate** bundles everything the field needs to look at a bid into one email or text. It goes to someone on your team, never to the GC.

The bundle holds the job address, plans links and the external pricing table. Sharing never marks the bid sent. It never counts toward the board or the Followup lenses. The Cover Letter's **Mark sent** and the bid room do that.

## Open it

On Bids → Pricing with a bid open, you tap {{button:green|Share with a teammate}}. A window opens with the same title. A one-line reminder says it shares with a teammate and sends nothing to the GC. The window shows exactly what the recipient will get:

- **Job address** is the bid's address with an {{button:gray|Open in Maps}} button. The recipient's copy gets the same thing. Tapping the address in the email opens it straight in Google Maps. So does the Map link in a text.
- **Job plans** and **CountTooling Plans** are the links on the bid. Each opens right from the window.
- **Pricing preview** shows the four columns the recipient will see: Fixture or Tie-in, Count, Sale Price and Revenue. Hidden rows stay hidden. The total still matches the Pricing tab.

No address on the bid? The package still sends without one. If the crew will need directions, you use **Edit bid** in that section to add it first.

## Send it

You pick a recipient. Leaders appear as one-tap chips. Then you choose:

- {{button:blue|Send for me}} emails the package now from ClickTooling and logs the send.
- {{button:gray|Send via my mail}} opens your own mail app with the text version. The table is copied to your clipboard to paste in.
- {{button:gray|Copy for text}} copies a short summary for a text, in Messages or WhatsApp. It includes the address and map link.

:::example Getting a leader to a new job
Wendi opens BP376's pricing, taps {{button:green|Share with a teammate}}, taps the {{chip:blue|Malachi}} chip, and {{button:blue|Send for me}}. Malachi's email has the address at the top — one tap and Google Maps is routing him to the site.
:::

## Send both prices

Viewing a price that is not the {{chip:green|★ base}}? Then tapping {{button:green|Share with a teammate}} first asks **Send which price?** You can pick the customer's ★ price, the one you're viewing, or **Both**. With Both, the package carries the customer's price first and the one you're viewing under it. Each has its own heading and total. All three ways to send carry the same pair: {{button:gray|Copy for text}}, {{button:gray|Send via my mail}} and {{button:blue|Send for me}}. The caption at the top of the window reads "★ Value Engineered + Written to Plan". So you can see both are in.

:::example Texting Malachi both numbers
Grace is viewing *Written to Plan* on BP385; the customer's price is ★ *Value Engineered*. She taps {{button:green|Share with a teammate}}, picks **Both**, then {{button:gray|Copy for text}} — one text to Malachi, both totals in it.
:::

Viewing the ★ price itself? There is nothing to pair it with. You switch to the other price first, then share. **Print** and **Export** still take one price at a time.

## Send a supply house the fixture list

Need a supply house to price the job? A supply house is the store that sells us the parts. Don't send them the package. It has your sale prices in it. Instead, you open the {{button:green|▾}} menu beside **Share with a teammate** and pick **Supply house list**. It copies only fixture names and counts. There are no prices, no totals and no links. It is ready to paste into a text or email to the parts house.

A prepare screen opens first, so you're never copying blind:

- **Scope it to the vendor**: one tap on {{chip:blue|Whole job}}, {{chip:blue|Pipe &amp; fittings}}, or {{chip:blue|Fixtures &amp; equipment}} ticks whole sections. You fine-tune with the checkbox on any section or row. Scoping applies to that copy only. The bid never changes.
- **Fix codes on the spot**: names the ledger doesn't recognize sit at the top. Each has a section picker and {{button:green|Pin it}}. The ledger is the app's list of fixture names and their codes. A pin codes that name on every bid, forever.
- **See the paste**: the preview pane shows the exact text, updating as you toggle. {{button:green|Copy}} puts precisely that on your clipboard.

{{gif:supply-house-list-prepare.gif|Scope with one tap — the preview updates live and Copy puts exactly that on the clipboard}}

The list arrives grouped by Division 22 spec section, in spec-book order. Division 22 is the plumbing part of the spec book. Examples are *22 11 16 Domestic Water Piping* and *22 42 13 Water Closets &amp; Urinals*. So the counter can work it section by section. Anything still uncoded rides in a "No code yet" tail. The copy never blocks on an incomplete ledger.

:::example Sending just the pipe to the supply house
Wendi opens BP339, picks **Supply house list**, taps {{chip:blue|Pipe &amp; fittings}} — the preview drops the fixtures and the gas tail, showing 37 items of pipe and fittings — and hits {{button:green|Copy 37 items}}. The supply house gets exactly the scope they price, nothing else.
:::

## Teach the ledger the missing codes

The toast may say some names have no code. A toast is the small message that pops up for a moment. Then you open the same {{button:green|▾}} menu and pick **Division 22 codes**. The audit shows every fixture name you've ever counted, run through the ledger. Uncoded names are on top, sorted by how many bids they appear on:

- You pick a section for a name and tap {{button:green|Pin it}}. That name is coded everywhere, instantly: past bids and every future one.
- Some things aren't Division 22 buyout items at all, like DEMO or safety line items. You tap {{button:gray|No code}} and they stop counting as gaps.
- The coverage bar shows how close the ledger is to knowing everything. If you pin the top few names, it jumps.

:::example Clearing the gas rows
Wendi copies BP339's fixtures and the toast says 36 names have no code. She opens **Division 22 codes**, sees "11/2IN 90 GAS" at the top (17 bids), picks the section the specs use for gas piping, and pins it. Every gas fitting on every bid now files under that section — one pin, done forever.
:::

It works as soon as the bid has Counts, even before you've set up a price book or labor.

:::example Getting cost pricing before the bid is priced
Wendi finishes the takeoff on BP375, opens the {{button:green|▾}} menu, taps **Supply house list**, and pastes the list into a text to her Ferguson rep. The rep prices from names and counts — Wendi's numbers stay in the building.
:::

## When the prices come back

The ways to handle replies have their own guide: *get supply house prices on a bid*. One is pasting a vendor's text into **Plug in a quote**. The others are the {{chip:blue|Quotes (1)}} chip and comparing houses part by part. Sending a quote link is covered in *send a supply house a quote link*.
