Once we win a GC job, the customer signs our contract. You send it from the customer's window with the contract file. The price it goes with is kept with it.

Sending our contract shows to a dev while it is built.

## Open the customer's window

- Press the customer's name on the board. Their window opens.
- Or press {{button:blue|Send to sign}} on the contract row of Get started. The window opens at that job's contract.

**About** lists their jobs with us. **Documents** has our contract on each job we won.

## Send it to sign

1. Press {{button:blue|Send to sign}} on the job's row.
2. Check the price under **The price**. It is our number today.
3. Pick the contract PDF under **The file**. Check that it says the same price.
4. Pick the day under **Sign by**. A week out is picked first.
5. Type a line of your own under **Your line** if you want one.
6. Press {{button:blue|Send to sign}}.

The row then reads {{chip:yellow|waiting on their signature}}. Get started shows the day it went.

:::example Our contract once it went
**Our contract with them** {{chip:yellow|waiting on their signature}} Sent today. Sign by Fri Oct 16.
:::

## Remind them

Press {{button:blue|Remind them}} on the row. A reminder keeps the price and the file that went before. Pick another PDF only if the file changed.

The row reads late once the sign by day passes.

## When our price changes

The row says **Our price changed after we sent it.** Press {{button:blue|Send the new price}}. Pick a new PDF that has the new price. The new one takes the place of the old one.

## Signed on paper

Press **Mark it signed** on Get started instead. [Get a GC job ready to start](/help/get-a-gc-job-ready-to-start) shows where it is.

## What the customer gets

No email goes yet. Their portal cannot take a signature yet. The send is kept with its price and its file for when it can.
