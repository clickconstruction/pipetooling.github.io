When the app says No connection, your phone or computer could not reach the server. Nothing you pressed was saved, and what you typed is still on the screen.

## What happened

The app sends every save and every read to the server over the internet. When the signal drops, the request never arrives. The app can tell this apart from every other failure, so it says so in plain words.

## What was saved

Nothing from that press. The words you typed are still in the form. Nothing was half saved, so you do not need to check the record.

Clock In, Clock Out, a job note and a field report work another way. With no signal the phone keeps them and sends them when the signal is back. Read [keep working when the signal drops](/help/keep-working-when-the-signal-drops).

## What to do

1. Check the signal. On a phone, step outside or turn Wi-Fi off and on.
2. Press {{button:outline|Retry}} beside the line.
3. Some screens try again on their own when the signal comes back. The line then reads *Back online*.

## When it keeps happening

Tell the office which page you were on and what you pressed. If everyone sees it at once, the server may be down. A dev checks that.
