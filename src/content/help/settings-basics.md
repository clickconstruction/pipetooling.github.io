Settings is where you manage your own profile and notifications. On a desktop, its pages sit in a list down the left side.

The list is grouped. **You** holds Your account and Your dashboard. **Company** holds settings named after the page they affect, for office roles. Then come **System** and **Help**. A search box sits on top. The last tabs you opened show as chips. A row of doors sits just under the search, on every tab. Doors are shortcut buttons. **Job Parts Tally** is there for everyone. **View as…** is there for devs. **Punch list** is there for devs and leaders. On a phone, the same list is a dropdown.

The page opens on the tab you used last. Which tabs you see depends on your role. Every user gets the You group, plus **Activity logs**, **Guides** and **Release notes**. The list says when more tabs are for masters and devs.

Devs also see **Dev doors** in the System group. The card there is called **Only devs and the controller see these**. The card lists every page, window and switch only a dev or the controller can open today. Each row is a link that opens the door. Beside each link, a chip says who may open it. The card changes no gate.

## Your profile

Under **Your account → My Profile**, you can edit your details:

:::example My Profile
Name: `Mike Thompson` &nbsp;·&nbsp; Email: `mike@example.com` &nbsp;·&nbsp; Phone: `(555) 123-4567`

{{button:blue|Save}}
:::

On a sub or helper account, the Name field is locked. The note reads "Name is managed by admins. Contact a master or dev to change it."

## Your bio

Techs and office staff see **Your bio** under **Your account**. The office writes it. A customer gets it with your first On my way text to them, and never again. Each line has a switch. **Say it** keeps the line in. **Don’t say this** takes it out. Ask the office to change the words.

## My way

Office staff see **My way** under **Your account**. It covers two moments: a booking's first visit, and an estimate. For each, pick **Ask me first** or **Send it and show me the receipt**. With Ask me first, the Tell the customer sheet opens and waits for you. With the other, the text or email goes the moment it can, and the receipt shows what went. See [schedule a job and tell the customer](/help/schedule-a-job-and-tell-the-customer).

## Push notifications

Push notifications are how the app reaches you: dispatch alerts, report notifications, reminders.

:::example Your account → Push Notifications
{{button:blue|Enable push notifications}} &nbsp;→&nbsp; {{chip:green|Enabled}} &nbsp;→&nbsp; {{button:outline|Test notification}}
:::

1. Tap {{button:blue|Enable push notifications}}.
2. When it shows {{chip:green|Enabled}}, you send a **Test notification**. You should see "If you see this, push notifications are working!"

Recent notifications you've received are listed under the **Activity logs** tab, in the System group, as *Most recent push notifications*. So you can catch up on anything you dismissed. Devs also see "Most recent emails sent" there, 25 at a time. That list shows every email the app has sent, with delivery status. In that list, rows from a recurring stream are clickable. Recurring streams are digests, Paid in Full, Billed reports and the like. You tap one and land on that stream's card in **Emails & reports**. The card is highlighted, so you can see who's subscribed.

On a phone, you add the app to your Home Screen from the browser's Share menu. Then the app and its notifications behave like an app installed from the app store.

### When you are pushed

Under the test button is **Push me**. Pick **Push me any time**, or **Push me only at these hours**.

:::example Push me
◉ Push me only at these hours
Days {{chip:blue|Mon}} {{chip:blue|Tue}} {{chip:blue|Wed}} {{chip:blue|Thu}} {{chip:blue|Fri}} {{chip:gray|Sat}} {{chip:gray|Sun}}
From 06:00 AM &nbsp; To 08:00 PM
Pushed Mon to Fri, 6:00 AM to 8:00 PM.
{{button:blue|Save my hours}} {{button:outline|Put them back}}
:::

1. Tap **Push me only at these hours**. The app saves every day, 6:00 AM to 8:00 PM, at once.
2. Tap a day to turn it off. A day that is off gets no pushes.
3. Set **From** and **To**. To must come after From.
4. Tap {{button:blue|Save my hours}}. The line under the days reads your hours back.

Tap **Push me any time** to clear your hours. Nothing holds a push yet. From the next update, a push outside your hours waits for you. A customer's reply still goes to the office at once.

## Pinned pages

Pages you pin appear in a quick bar for fast navigation. Pins sync across your devices. You manage your pins from Settings, where you can remove or clear them. The pin footer at the bottom of pages only shows while **Pin Mode** is on. Pin Mode is in the gear menu. See the "How do I turn on Pin Mode to pin pages?" guide.

**Pin a specific bid**: you open a bid on any Bids tab, say BP352 on Pricing. The {{button:outline|Pin bid}} button at the bottom of the page pins *that bid's tab*. The Dashboard chip reads like ***BP352 · pricing***. The chip takes you straight back to the exact bid. You can pin as many bids as you like. You unpin from the same button, or from Settings, when you're done with one.
