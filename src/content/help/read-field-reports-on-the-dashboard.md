The Dashboard's **Recent Reports** section is an inbox for the crew's latest field reports. A blue {{chip:blue|N new}} badge on the header counts what you haven't seen yet — and it's the same on every device, so reading on your phone clears it on the office computer too.

## The three states

- **New** — blue dot, bold job name. You haven't opened it.
- **Opened** — tap a report to expand it (the answers show right in place, with {{button:outline|Job detail}} and {{button:outline|Map}} buttons). When you collapse it, it stays in the list, dimmed with a green ✓ — nothing disappears until you say so.
- **Done** — tap {{button:outline-blue|✓ Done}} on an expanded report to clear it from the dashboard on all your devices. It's not deleted: **View all →** still shows every report ever filed.

## Handy extras

- {{button:outline|Mark all opened}} in the header quiets the badge in one tap.
- **Mark unread** on an expanded report puts the blue dot back (and undoes Done).
- If you've opened reports in earlier sessions, a **Show N opened reports** link at the bottom reveals them.

:::example a normal morning
Three new reports overnight. You tap each one, skim the answers, hit ✓ Done on the two that need nothing, and leave the third dimmed in the list as a reminder to call the customer. The badge reads 0 — on your phone too.
:::
