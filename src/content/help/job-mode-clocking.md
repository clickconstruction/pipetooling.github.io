Job Mode turns the Dashboard into one big card built for working in the field. It shows your current job, your next job, and big buttons for what you do on site.

## Is Job Mode on?

- **Subs and helpers:** yes, already. Job Mode is on the first time you open the app on any phone. Nothing to set up.
- **Leaders and superintendents:** the Dashboard shows a one-time card, **Working in the field? Turn on Job Mode.** You tap {{button:green|Turn on Job Mode}} and you are in. {{button:outline|Not now}} hides the card on that phone.
- **Anyone who can file field reports:** you tap the {{icon:gear}} **gear menu** in the top-right of the header. You toggle **Job Mode** on or off.

Turning it **off** is remembered on that phone even if your role has it on by default. The same gear-menu row turns it back on. With Job Mode on, the Dashboard shows the Job Mode card first. You can always tap {{button:outline|Show full dashboard}} underneath it. Then you see everything else for the rest of the visit. The next time you open the app you are back on the card.

Job Mode, Dispatch Mode and Task Mode are [one at a time](/help/task-mode#one-mode-at-a-time). Turning Job Mode on takes you out of the other two. Turning it off puts you back in Dispatch Mode if you had that on.

:::example A new phone
Your helper gets a replacement phone, signs in, and lands straight on the Job Mode card with {{button:green|Clock In}} at the bottom of the screen — no gear menu, no guide. If they'd rather see the whole Dashboard, one gear-menu tap turns Job Mode off for that phone.
:::

The header also changes. The three task buttons become a **Contact:** row. It has a green phone button that calls the office. Then {{button:blue|dispatch}}, {{button:purple|estimating}}, and {{button:blue|teammate}} are spelled out. On a narrow phone screen they stay as the compact icon buttons, with the phone button on the left. The purple one is **Ask estimating**. It sends a question to the estimators. You are not opening anyone's inbox.

## The row under the header

A row of squares sits under the header. Each square has its name on it.

The two squares on the left are for your job. Those two squares change with your day.

- **Before a job, and on the way:** {{button:blue|Directions}} opens the route in your phone's maps. {{button:blue|Job details}} opens the job's window.
- **On site:** {{button:blue|Photo}} opens the job's photos, so you can add one. {{button:blue|Job details}} stays where it was.
- **No job left today:** **This week** shows your hours so far. **Tomorrow** shows how many jobs you have. You tap **Tomorrow** to open your schedule.

The two squares on the right never move.

- {{button:outline|Paint a map}} starts a map of the way in. See [Paint a map](/help/paint-a-map#start-a-map).
- {{button:outline|My spend}} opens your card charges. A number on its corner counts the charges with no job yet.

Some sites keep their materials in boxes. On one of those you also see **On site** while you are there. The number on the square counts the boxes on the site. You tap **On site** to open [the boxes](/help/keep-track-of-the-material-in-the-cages-and-boxes-on-site).

On a phone the day's presses sit in a bar at the bottom of the screen, above the tab bar. The card scrolls above it. The bar stays in reach whatever the day's list holds. The bar slides away while you type.

- You tap {{button:blue|Leave report}} to file a field report on the current job. See the Reports guide.
- The green button changes with your day: {{button:green|Clock In}} with no schedule, else {{button:green|On my way}}. While you drive it reads {{button:blue|I'm here}}. When only your current job is left, it turns into {{button:red|Wrap Up Day}} to clock you out.
- Over those two sit three smaller buttons. You tap {{button:outline-amber|Turnaway}} when you arrive and cannot do the work. See the Turnaways guide.
- You tap {{button:outline|% done}} to move the job's percent. The stepper is the same one My Schedule's {{button:outline|Update % done}} opens. The button shows for subs, helpers and superintendents while a job is clocked in.
- {{button:outline|Clock out}} is for lunch, a parts run, or leaving early. See Clocking out below.
- On a computer or a tablet the same buttons sit inside the card, under the day's list.
- Under the card, each stage with a checklist is a press. A job whose stages have no questions shows no checklist rows there. The job window still lists them.

## Your day on the card

Under the job header, the card lists **every job on today's schedule**, in order:

- ✓ green check means you have already clocked time there today.
- A green ring marks the job you are on **now**.
- An amber **still open** flag marks a job you drove past that is still owed a visit. Maybe the customer was not home. Maybe the site was not ready.
- A hollow dot means coming up later.

**Tap any open job to jump straight to it.** You do not need to take them in order. You get the On my way sheet, already pointed at the job you tapped. Jobs you skip are not lost. They stay flagged. {{button:green|On my way}} offers them once the rest of your day is done.

On a slow morning the card draws the day your phone last read. A line above it reads *Your day as read at 7:52 AM · reading again…*. Every press on the card waits until the live read lands. See [know what to do when the app is slow](/help/know-what-to-do-when-the-app-is-slow#your-day-on-the-phone).

While you are clocked into a job, the card keeps going below the buttons:

- **Customer** shows the customer's name with tap-to-call phone and tap-to-email links. So reaching them is one tap.
- You tap {{button:outline|Job detail}}. The job's window opens right over the card. On a phone the window carries a bar at the bottom. See [the job window on a phone](#the-job-window-on-a-phone).
- A **Street View photo** of the job address. You tap it to open Street View in Google Maps.
- **Job updates** is the same updates thread as Job Detail. You read what the office posted. You add your own note without leaving the card. The pills over it show only the kinds of lines you can read. A helper sees All, Notes, Status and Crew.

## The job window on a phone

You tap {{button:outline|Job detail}} on the card, or a job on My Schedule. The job's window opens. On a phone a bar sits at the bottom of the window. The bar stays put while the window scrolls. So the press you need is always under your thumb.

- {{button:green|Arrived}} posts an arrived note on the job's thread. Once you press it or clock in on the job, the button reads {{button:outline-amber|Leaving}} instead.
- {{button:outline-amber|Leaving}} posts a leaving note. Then the On my way sheet opens for your next job. One press clocks the drive and texts the customer. With no next job on your schedule, Update Focus opens instead, so you can pick any job. The button reads Arrived again for when you come back.
- {{button:blue|Leave report}} opens the phone's camera, then the field report form for this job. Each shot goes with the report into the customer's Drive folder. See [take photos for a report](/help/take-photos-for-a-report) and [file and review field reports](/help/reports).
- {{button:outline|Note}} opens a note box at the bottom of the screen, with the keyboard under it. You type the note. You press {{button:blue|Save}}. The note lands on the job's thread and the box closes. The bar is put away while the box is up. Type **@** in the box to [tag a teammate](/help/tag-a-teammate-in-a-job-note#tag-from-the-job-s-own-window).

Above the bar the window reads from the top down. The job's name comes first, with its status beside it. The job's number and the customer sit on a quiet line under the name. At the right, **⋯** opens a short list of doors, like **Share the job**. **✕** under it closes the window.

Then the window runs in sections.

- **Where** shows the street photo with the address on it. You tap the address to open it in Maps. Painted maps sit under the photo, then **＋ Paint a map**.
- **Photos** is a row of pictures you flick sideways. **Add a photo** is the first tile. It opens the camera.
- **Who** names the customer. The phone number is written out. You tap the number to call. The square beside the number copies it. A subcontractor sees who runs the job for us instead, with that person's number. The customer's number is not shown to a subcontractor.
- **Today's work** shows the stage the job is on. A bar under it shows how far the job has come. Each stage with a checklist is a press. **Plans** and **Materials on site** are folds. Each fold says what it holds beside its name. A job with no stages shows the two folds alone.
- **What's happening** shows the block you opened the job from, with its hours on their own line. **Last note** shows the newest note or report, who wrote it and when. A subcontractor also gets **Your pay**. **Your pay** shows what is open on their own work on the job. The agreed price and what has been paid sit under it. **Open your portal** shows the rest.
- **More on this job** holds the rest in folds. **Notes and reports** holds every note and the note box. A superintendent also gets **Parts cost**. You tap a fold to open it. The phone remembers which folds you left open.

In the **Materials on site** fold, {{button:blue|Open Materials on site}} takes you to the job's containers. See [keep track of the material on site](/help/keep-track-of-the-material-in-the-cages-and-boxes-on-site).

Masters and primaries open the tabbed window instead. On a phone the tabbed window's bar carries {{button:green|Arrived}} or {{button:outline-amber|Leaving}} when you are on the job's crew. {{button:outline|Note}} on that bar opens the same note box. See [the bar at the bottom](/help/job-window-tabs#on-a-phone-the-bar-at-the-bottom).

On a computer neither window has a bar. Arrived, Leaving and Post note sit under the note box as before.

## Training under the card

Under My Schedule, a Training card lists the courses your role must take. It is the same card the full Dashboard shows. One press opens the course. See [take a training course](/help/take-a-training-course).

## The short Job Mode page

The office can set a superintendent's **Dashboard view** to **Short**. With Job Mode on, the Dashboard is then about one screen.

- The row of squares sits at the top, the same row as on the long page. See [The row under the header](#the-row-under-the-header).
- On this page **This week** opens the full Dashboard at **My Time**, where your hours are.
- The job card's top stays as it is. Your next stop, today's stops and the buttons at the bottom do not move.
- **Is it as told?** sits above **Open this job** on a day there is something to look at. See [say whether a site is as we were told](/help/say-whether-a-site-is-as-we-were-told).
- **Open this job** opens the rest of the card in place. You get the customer, photos, the ticket, checklists, materials on site and job updates.
- Tap **This job** to fold it away again.
- The pace line is the **Job pace** card's first line. Two thin bars sit under it. The blue bar is the work done. The amber bar is the hours in. Tap the line for the pace card. **Fold the pace card** closes it.
- **Waiting on you** lists what only you can fix. The list shows only when something waits.
- **Tomorrow** says how many stops you have and when the first is. Tap it to open the **Schedule** tab.
- **Everything else** opens the full Dashboard for this visit. The second line of the row says how many courses you still have to do.

Press **Back to the short page** at the top to return. The **Dashboard** tab at the bottom returns too.

Nothing is removed from the app. Each thing is one press from the short page.

To turn the short page on or off, see [see only what waits on me on the dashboard](/help/use-the-short-dashboard#turn-it-on-or-off).

## Clocking in and out

{{gif:job-mode-clocking.gif|Starting the day: the Ready to start card, the green button, and the clocked-in card}}

- You tap {{button:green|Clock In}} to start your day. If you have a schedule, the card offers your scheduled job. Otherwise you can pick a job manually.
- **Nothing listed?** The sheet says so and stays that way. It reads **Nothing on your schedule today, and no jobs assigned to you**. It has dispatch's number to tap and a pointer to the search box. You call, or you type the job's name or number.

:::example Day one, no schedule yet
Your new helper taps {{button:green|Clock In}}. No jobs appear, so the sheet says **Nothing on your schedule today, and no jobs assigned to you — call dispatch at 512 360 0599**. One tap dials the office; dispatch adds the block, and the job shows up as a pick.
:::
- Switching jobs from the full Dashboard uses {{button:blue|Update Focus}}. The job you are clocked into is listed first with a {{chip:green|You are here}} tag. You tap any other job to switch. The same tag shows on the clock-out review.
- Moving on? You tap {{button:green|On my way}}. The sheet shows **where you can go**. Your suggested next job is preselected. You can pick any open job instead. The app writes the note for you. Or you choose **Done for the day** at the bottom. See the next two sections.
- Your location is captured when you punch. Your hours flow to the office automatically for approval.

### When you have no signal

The punch may not reach the server in a crawlspace, a basement, or a dead zone. The phone keeps it with the moment you pressed. The sheet closes as it always does. You do not need to press again. The line at the top of the screen shows your press waiting.

The phone sends the press on its own when the signal returns. Then the line turns green until you press {{button:green|Got it}}. Your time counts from the moment you pressed. The card draws your day as it was last read until the press lands. Then the card reads your day again. A punch that reaches the office late is filed like typed hours. Read [keep working when the signal drops](/help/keep-working-when-the-signal-drops) for the line, the list behind it and what needs a signal.

:::example Clocking in from a basement

You tap {{button:green|Clock In}} in the basement and the sheet closes. The line at the top reads **Clock in from 7:58 AM** · waiting to send. You go to work. An hour later you climb the stairs. The line turns green: **Clock in from 7:58 AM sent ✓**. You tap {{button:green|Got it}}. Your time counts from 7:58.

:::

A job note and a field report wait on the phone the same way. A report's photos go with it. A Turnaway report needs a signal, since it also raises a dispatch alert.

A screen that only shows you things works another way. The Job Mode card draws your day as it was last read. A stamp above the day says when. Your **Schedule** tab, **My requests**, **Customers** and your **My crew** list show a No connection line with {{button:outline|Retry}}. Each one reads again by itself the moment the signal returns. A message may say something else, for example that you do not have permission. Then Retry will not appear, because trying again would not change the answer.

## Tell the customer you are on the way

{{button:green|On my way}} does two things in one press. It clocks you in on the job as on the way. It texts the customer the office's line.

1. Press {{button:green|On my way}}. The sheet opens on your next job.
2. Read the orange box. That is the text the customer gets.
3. Check the minutes. The app guesses them from your phone to the job. Press − or + to change them by five, or type a number.
4. Read **What happens**. It says what the press will do.
5. Press {{button:green|On my way, text Dana}}.

:::example The text as it goes
Dana, this is Luis with Click Plumbing. I am on my way, about 20 minutes out.
:::

The clock-in happens first. If the text cannot go, the sheet says why under **What happens**. The button then reads **On my way** alone. You are clocked in either way.

- **Clock in, no text** clocks you in and sends nothing.
- **Start here instead** is for when you are already at the door. It clocks you in on site with no drive.
- When the guess lands past the customer's window, a line says so. **Running late** under **Message the customer** moves the board.

Your phone's location is only a guess. Change the minutes when you know better.

## Send your bio the first time

The office writes a short bio about you. A customer gets it the first time you press {{button:green|On my way}} to them. It goes as a second text, right after your line. The same customer never gets it again.

1. Read the bio under the orange box. The line above it says the customer hears from you for the first time.
2. Press **Edit bio** to pick the lines for this customer. Tick a line on or off. Press {{button:blue|Use these}}.
3. Press **Not this time** to send the line alone.
4. Press {{button:green|On my way, text Dana}}. The line goes first, then your bio.

Your usual picks are in Settings under **Your bio**. A change on the sheet is for this customer only. See [set up my profile and notifications](/help/settings-basics#your-bio).

## Say you are here

While you drive, the green button becomes {{button:blue|I'm here}}. The header reads **On the way** with the minutes so far.

1. Press {{button:blue|I'm here}} at the door. The app marks you on site right away.
2. When the customer can be texted, the sheet shows the office's I'm here line.
3. Press {{button:blue|I'm here, text Dana}}, or **I'm here, no text**.

The header then reads **On site since** with your drive time. The green button goes back to {{button:green|On my way}} for the next job.

:::example A customer who is not home
A property manager across town asked to know when you arrive. You press {{button:blue|I'm here}}, read the line, and press {{button:blue|I'm here, text Priya}}. She gets the text and calls the tenant.
:::

## Clocking out

Three ways, depending on your day:

- **End of the day.** When your current job is the last one open, the green button becomes {{button:red|Wrap Up Day}}. You tap it and you get the usual clock-out review, below.
- **From the On my way sheet.** You pick **Done for the day** at the bottom of it.
- **Any other time.** For lunch, a parts run, or leaving early, you tap {{button:outline|Clock out}} on the bar. On a computer the same press is a small underlined link under the card's buttons. Your remaining jobs stay flagged on the card for when you are back.

### What the clock-out review asks, and why

All three doors run the same short stack. Most days you see one screen. The others only appear when there is something to catch.

1. **Assign your spending before you clock out** appears only sometimes. You see it when you have card charges from today with no job on them. Pick the job for each. Your recent jobs are offered first. Or **skip** and sort them later in Job Parts Tally. Why now? The charge is easiest to place while you still remember which house you were at.
2. **Clock out** always appears. *What did you work on?* is filled from the reports you filed today, one line a report. Edit it if you like. With no report today, you type the note. The note is required. The job list confirms where the time goes. The job you are clocked into is first with a {{chip:green|You are here}} tag. So the usual answer is just to leave it. Why? The note is what the office reads when they approve your hours.
3. ***Missing reports from today (click to make report)*** appears only when a job on today's schedule has no report yet. It shows one red button per job. Tap one to file the report right there. Or finish clocking out and file it later with **Job Report** on your Dashboard. Why? A report filed the same day beats a phone call tomorrow.
4. **Team feedback** is a short card about how your teammates did. It comes on a cadence the office sets, days or weeks apart, not daily. {{button:blue|Start}} answers it now. {{button:outline|Not now · remind me in N days}} puts it off. Left alone, it closes itself in 30 seconds and comes back when it is due again.

Then {{button:red|Complete clock out}} ends the session. With no signal the window closes the same way. A small message reads *Kept on the phone. It goes out when the signal is back.* Your time ends at the moment you pressed. The press goes out when the signal returns. See [When you have no signal](#when-you-have-no-signal).

:::example A normal Tuesday
Tap {{button:outline|Clock out}} on the bar → *Clock out* → the note already reads your report's words → the job already shows {{chip:green|You are here}} → {{button:red|Complete clock out}}. One screen, five seconds.
:::

Salaried teammates do not clock out manually. Their hours are handled automatically. So these buttons do not appear.

The details section also shows for your **Ready to start** job before you clock in. That is the customer contact, Street View, and updates. You review the visit before you head out.

Below the card, **My Schedule** shows your upcoming visits. It is the same section as the full Dashboard. So you can see what is next without leaving Job Mode. {{button:outline|Show full dashboard}} sits just under it.

## The Job Mode tab bar

With Job Mode on, a tab bar pins to the bottom of the screen. Everything on it is **yours only**:

- **Dashboard** is the Job Mode card you know.
- **Schedule** is a two-week strip plus your own day agenda. It lists your visits with times, customer, and address. You tap one to open the job.
- **Inbox** starts with any notification banners meant for you, like stale tally transactions. Then come your **My Inbox** tasks. Then comes **My requests**. That is everything you have sent to Dispatch. A red phone tap, a red photos tap, or a note to Dispatch all land there. They split into **Waiting on Dispatch** and **Answered**. An answered one shows the office's note as {{chip:green|Office answered: "Added — it's 555-0100"}}. You also get a push, **Dispatch answered**, the moment they close it.
- **Customers** is just the customers whose jobs have been on your schedule. You tap one for its full interaction summary.

## Good habits

- Clock into the job you are physically working on. Switching is one tap. Accurate time keeps everyone's numbers right.
- Leave a report before you head out. It takes under a minute and saves phone calls later.

## Extra work the customer asked for

You may have switched **Make change order from the field** on in Settings. Then a **Make change order** press sits under the day's list on the card. The press opens the write-up already on the job you are clocked into. See *write up a change order from the field*.

## The customer signs on your phone

You are clocked in on a job that has no signed agreement yet. The customer is standing there. Under the day's list there is one more press: {{button:outline|✍ Hand the phone to the customer to sign}}.

1. Tap it. The app opens this job's agreement on your phone. It is the same page the office sends by link. It shows the work, the price and payment line and the terms. It shows a note that says you are present.
2. Hand the phone over. They read it. They type or draw their name. They tick **I agree to sign electronically** and press **Sign agreement**.
3. Take the phone back. The job now reads {{chip:green|✍ Signed}} everywhere. Their signed copy goes to the email on the job.

Nothing is emailed until they sign. If the office already sent this customer an agreement, the same one opens. If there is none yet, the app makes it from the job's own facts. So there is nothing to type. The line shows for the master, primary, superintendent and estimator roles. It disappears once the job is signed.

## Read your project's line as a superintendent

A superintendent on a project sees one more card on Job Mode, above the job's details. The card is named **Job pace**. The card compares two things on the job. **Hours used** is the time the crew has clocked, measured against the job's hours budget. **Work done** is the percent you set. The two bars sit one over the other on one scale. The striped end of the hours bar is the gap between them.

- *On track.* means the hours and the work are moving together. You do not need to do anything.
- *Hours are ahead of the work.* means the job is using hours faster than it is getting done. The card says the gap in hours. If more work is done than the percent says, press {{button:blue|Set the %}}. If not, press **My finish date** and say when you will finish.
- *Is 40% still right?* shows when the crew has put in hours since the percent was last set. Press {{button:blue|Set the %}} to change the number. Press {{button:outline|Still 40%}} to say the same number again today. That press files a status report.
- *The work is ahead of the hours.* means the job is under its hours so far.
- The card never shows money. When the money is ahead of the work, one grey line says the office watches it.
- When you are clocked in with no job linked, the card shows your next stop. A line reads *Next stop* with the job's number and name.
- Press **What is this?** to read what the card compares and why. The fold opens by itself the first time you see the card.

Under the bars, the job's stages run top to bottom. A stage with a tick is done. The stage with a filled circle is the one in hand. Work on the bid that is not a stage shows as one row, **Other work**. The last row is the finish day.

- When the card is on track, {{button:outline|Set the %}} sits on the stage in hand. The window takes a number for each stage.
- A stage with checklist questions says how many are answered under its name. Press the stage to open its checklist.
- When the job is behind, a row under **Finish** reads **At this pace** with the day this pace lands on. Press {{button:outline|Open the checklist}} to answer the stage's open questions.
- Press **My finish date** to say when you think the job finishes. Pick a day and say what is in the way. Your day shows on the card as **You said**. It does not change the planned finish or your score.
- Two days before that day the card asks *Still finishing?* with the day. Press **Yes**, or press **Pick a day** to change it.
- Press **Ask a master** to send a question to the master on the job. The percent, the hours and the finish day go with it. The card then says who your question waits on. See [tag a teammate](/help/tag-a-teammate-in-a-job-note) for where the answer shows.
- Press **All projects** to open the Projects board, with every working project on it.
- Press **How this is figured** for the rest. It holds the strip with the days the crew worked and where the budget came from. It also holds the office's sign-off and your score this month.
- Inside it, tap a day on the strip to read who clocked and for how long. The day also shows the report.
- When the office set expected hours for the job, the same fold reads them with 🎯. The line says who set them, when, from what, and why.

A job with fewer than two stages has no list. It shows one **Work** bar and the finish day in words.

The office reads the same numbers on its [Projects board](/help/see-where-every-project-stands-and-set-its-expected-hours). The two can never disagree, because they read one number.
