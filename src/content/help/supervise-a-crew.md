If you can run a job on your own — every master, and any helper or sub the office has marked that way — then on any day you are on a job with other people, you are that job-day's supervisor. Nothing is assigned: it is read from the schedule and the clock. Your Dashboard shows a **My crew** section for the week with what that means.

## Reports owed

One line per job-day you supervised that has no report yet — *J258 · Oak St · Tue*, with the crew you had that day underneath. Press {{button:blue|Write it}} and the report form opens for that job. Today's line reads *no report yet* and turns red from the next day on. When every job-day has a report, the card says so and how many were filed.

## My crew's hours

The clock sessions of everyone who worked a job-day you supervised, by person, newest day first: the job, the hours, *in* while they are still clocked in, a tick once the office has approved. There is no approve button here on purpose — approval stays with the office and the pay-approved masters. You see your crew; you do not sign for their pay.

:::example A week with one thing owed
**Reports owed** 1 owed
J258 · Oak St · Tue &nbsp;*with Bryan Ortiz, Sam Reyes* &nbsp;{{button:blue|Write it}}
**My crew's hours** read-only
Bryan Ortiz **16.50h** · Tue J258 · Oak St 8.00h · in · Mon J258 · Oak St 8.50h ✓
:::

## Who sees this

Anyone who supervised a job-day that week. A helper or sub who still needs supervision sees nothing, and so does a master with no job-day. Step weeks with ◀ ▶; {{button:outline|This week}} brings you back.

## Rate my crew, once a month

At the top of the section, a **Rate my crew** line counts the people you supervised on two or more days this month. Press {{button:blue|Rate my crew}} and the deck deals one card per person: the three sliders — Ability, Drive, Integrity — with a box under each for a word on why, then {{button:blue|Save · next}}, or **Skip**. It is by name, one rating per person per month, and you can open it again to change one. Your ratings sit beside the office's on Hiring → Review with a {{chip:green|supervisor}} chip, so the people who hire see what the people who supervise saw.
