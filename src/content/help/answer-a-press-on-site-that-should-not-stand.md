A press on site is never deleted. When one should not stand, a dev answers it. The answer says what the press was, and the press stays in the list for good.

## Pick the presses

Open a site on the Materials desk and press **By person**. The guide [see what is on every job site](/help/see-what-is-on-every-job-site-and-what-walked-away#see-what-one-person-did-on-a-site) says how to read the list.

Tick the box at the start of each press to answer. Then press **Answer 2 presses…** under the list. The button counts what you ticked.

A box moved, retired or renamed has no tick box. Those are changed back on the box itself.

## Choose an answer

The window offers one answer for each press. Change it when it does not fit.

:::cards

:::card Take it back
The press did not happen, or it should not stand. Every number then reads as if nobody made it.
:::

:::card Mark it as not allowed
The press happened and was not allowed. The numbers keep it. The mark is the record.
:::

:::card Ask for a second count
Use this for a count you doubt. A task opens for the person you pick to count the box again.
:::

:::

Take a press back only when it did not happen. A real take that you take back makes the app say the material is still there.

## Read the numbers first

A panel in the window says what the numbers would read. Each line shows the part, the box, the number now and the number after.

Some presses move no number by themselves. A later count already reset the line. The window says so under that press.

## Say why and press

Type the reason under **Why**. One reason goes on every press in the window.

The last button names each act with its count, like *Take back 1, mark 2*. Press it.

A press that the app refuses stays ticked. A note says which one and why.

## Read an answer later

An answered press wears a chip. It reads *taken back by* or *marked not allowed by* with a name. The reason sits beside it.

A press taken back is struck through. It is in no count and no total.

The line under **pieces taken or used** counts the pieces marked not allowed.

## Change your mind

Press **Let it stand…** on a press taken back. Type why and press **Let the press stand**. The press goes back into every number.

Press **Take the mark off…** on a marked press. Type why and press **Take the mark off**. No number changes.

The first answer stays on record as withdrawn. You can give the press a new answer after that.
