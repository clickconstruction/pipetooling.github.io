A stage checklist files itself as a record when the last question is answered. The record gets a number, like *CL-1042-1*. A PDF of it lands in the job's Drive folder. The filed list cannot change. The office can amend it, and the stage opens again as a new version. This guide says what you see and what you can press.

## When the checklist files

The crew answers the last question on the sheet. The record files with that answer. The time on the record is that answer's time. Nothing to press.

A question that needs a signature counts only once it is signed. A question that asks for a photo counts only once the photo is taken. A question skipped on this job does not count. Only then does the stage file.

## What the crew sees

The sheet turns to paper. The top line reads the record number, the version, the time and the counts, like *Record CL-1042-1 · v1 · Oct 15 · 4:02 pm · 4 answers, 1 signed*. Every row reads its answer with the name and the time. A signed row shows the mark. The foot says *Trim Set now reads ready on the Pipeline.* A row that reads No is named there too.

Nothing on the paper can be pressed. Press {{button:outline|Close}} to leave it.

The moment it files, the paper's first line reads *Filed as record CL-1042-1 with the last answer.*

## What the office sees

Open the job window and press **Documents**. Under **Stage checklists** a filed stage wears a green chip, like *Filed · CL-1042-1*. The line under the name reads the record's version, time and counts. The line ends with where the PDF is.

The PDF takes a few minutes. The line reads *PDF on its way to the job's Drive folder* until then. Once it is there, press **Open in Drive** to read it. The PDF goes in the same folder as the job's photos. Each version is its own file.

A stage with more than one version lists them on a line that starts *Versions:*. An amended version says who amended it, when and why.

## Amend a filed checklist

A filed list is wrong. The roofer came back. A check was answered on the wrong unit. The office amends it.

1. Find the stage under **Stage checklists** on the Documents tab.
2. Press **Amend**.
3. Type why.
4. Press {{button:outline|Amend as a new version}}. Press **Keep it filed** to change your mind.

The stage opens again. Its chip reads *Amending v1 · 4 of 4*. The crew's sheet opens again with *Amending v1* under the count. The filed version stays as it was. When the list is complete again, it files as version 2 with a new record number.

The superintendent and the estimator can amend on their own jobs too.

## When a filed stage opens by itself

Some presses open a filed stage without Amend. Adding a check for the job. Skipping a company question. Taking a signed answer back. A new question on the company's list. Each one makes the list incomplete, so the filed version reads amended and the stage opens again. The next completion files the next version.

## Print the checklists

Press {{button:outline|Print the checklists}} beside the **Stage checklists** heading. A print window opens with every stage on one paper. A filed stage prints from its record. An open stage prints as it stands, with *open* where there is no answer yet. A signed row prints the signer's name in the signature face. The ink itself is on the PDF in Drive.

This is the paper an inspector or the GC reads. The filed PDF is the same paper. A copy of each print is kept under **Sent from this job** on the Documents tab.
