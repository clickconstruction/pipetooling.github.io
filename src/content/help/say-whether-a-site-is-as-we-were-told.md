The app asks **Is it as told?** when you reach a stage of a job. The card lists what must be ready on the site before we start. You look at the site and say whether each thing is true.

The office wrote the list. Most of it came from the customer's own word. You are the first of ours to see the site.

## Find the card

1. Open the Dashboard in Job Mode.
2. Find the card of your job.
3. Look under the job's name, above the maps and the photos.

The card reads **Is it as told?** and names the stage, like Rough In.

On the short page the card sits above **Open this job**. You do not open the job to find it.

The card shows only when there is something to look at. The first day we planned for the stage is the first day it shows. A stage with no planned days is not asked about.

## When everything is as told

1. Read each line on the card.
2. Look at each thing on the site.
3. Press {{button:green|Yes, all as told}}.

Each line is now marked as seen by us, with your name and today's date. The office has no stronger yes than one of ours seeing it.

## When something is not as told

1. Press {{button:outline|No}} on each line that is not true. The line turns red.
2. Press **No** again to take one back.
3. Press {{button:red|Tell Dispatch: 1 thing not as told}}. The press counts your lines.

Dispatch gets one line at the top of its inbox. The line names the stage, the thing, and who told us the thing was ready. The office sees the stage turn red.

Every line you left alone is marked as seen by us.

## After you answer

The card says what was kept. You read *Saved: all as told.* or *Dispatch has it: 1 thing not as told.*

Nothing stops you. Keep working either way.

The first of ours to answer has answered for the whole crew.

A **Yes, all as told** after someone else has answered is not kept. The app says who answered first.

A **No** is kept even then. Your card may have been open before the other person answered. Dispatch still gets your line.

## When the card comes back

A line with a **No** can come back on a later day.

The office calls the customer about it. The customer says the thing is ready now. The card then asks about that line again.

Look at the thing and answer the same way. You are the first of ours to see it since the **No**.

## With no signal

The card needs a signal to load and to send. With no signal the card does not show. The card comes back by itself when the phone finds a signal.

A card that is already up stays up when the signal drops. Your **No** presses stay on it.

A send with no signal says the answer was not kept. Press the send again when the signal is back.

## When the send is refused

The office can change the list while your card is open. Your send is then refused, and the app says why.

The card reads the list again by itself. Look at the lines again and press the send.

The card goes away when there is nothing left to ask.
