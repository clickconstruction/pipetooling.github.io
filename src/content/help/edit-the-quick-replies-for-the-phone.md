The office picks the quick replies a tech gets on the phone. Mark a line for the phone, give it a short name, set the order and write the Spanish.

## Put a line on the phone

1. Open the quick replies window. [Edit the quick replies](/help/edit-the-quick-replies#open-the-window) shows where it is.
2. Press a line for customers. It opens in place.
3. Tick **On the tech’s phone**.
4. Write **Its short name on the phone** if you want one. Leave it empty and the button reads the line’s first sentence.
5. Press {{button:blue|Save}}.

The line goes last on the phone. Only the team’s lines for customers go on the phone. A line you keep for yourself stays off it.

A line on the phone shows {{chip:blue|☎ phone}} in the list.

## Set the phone’s order

1. Press the **Phone** tab. It shows once a line is on the phone.
2. Press **↑** or **↓** to move a line one place.
3. Press **off** to take a line off the phone. It stays on the list.

The phone shows the lines for the job’s step first. The rest keep this order. Six lines reach the phone, and **Something else** is always last. A line past six waits until one above it comes off.

## Write the Spanish

1. Press a line for customers.
2. Write the same line in Spanish in the **Spanish** box.
3. Press {{button:blue|Save}}.

The app keeps the Spanish beside the English for customers who read Spanish. A line for customers with no Spanish shows {{chip:yellow|no es}} in the list. A line with its Spanish shows {{chip:gray|es}}.

## Put blanks in a line

A blank is a word in braces, like `{first}`. The app fills it when someone presses the line.

1. Press a line to open it.
2. Put the cursor where the blank goes.
3. Press a chip under the words, like **{first}**.

The preview under the chips shows the line as it would go. A blank the app cannot fill stays lit.

The reply box fills their first name, your first name and the job’s name. It leaves the rest lit. Fill each lit blank in the box, then send. **{found}** and **{thing}** are always yours to fill.

:::example A line with a blank to fill
Dana, here is what we found: {found}. Luis will send you the estimate today.
:::

Every change to the Spanish or the phone goes in the line’s history, with your name and today.
