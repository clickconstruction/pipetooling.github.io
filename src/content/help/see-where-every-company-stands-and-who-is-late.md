By step shows every trade partner and customer on the step of the flow they are on. The late ones come first, in red. One look tells you who needs a call today.

## Open By step

1. Open [Trade partners](/gc?view=partners).
2. Press {{button:outline|By step}} above the list.
3. Press {{button:outline|List}} to go back to the list.

The steps come in the order of the flow. The first group is the steps a company goes through once. The second group repeats for each job.

## Read the tiles

The tiles at the top add up the whole board. Each tile counts companies, so a company asked on two jobs counts once.

- **Getting set up**, **Quoting** and **Building** each show how many companies are in that part of the flow.
- Each company is a dot on its tile. A gray dot is fine. An orange dot is over the usual time, or a paper ran out. A red dot is late.
- **Off the flow** counts the companies that passed, went elsewhere or were let go.
- **Needs a hand today** counts the late ones and the lapsed ones. The late and the lapsed are the calls to make today.

Over usual is only a watch. Over usual never counts toward **Needs a hand today**, and it never reaches Follow up.

1. Press a tile to jump to its first step.
2. Press a dot to open that company.
3. Press **Needs a hand today** to open Follow up.

## Read a step

Each step has a row. The counts for the step sit under the step's name. You see how many are on it, how many are late and how many are lapsed. You also see the step's usual time.

Each company on the step is a chip with its day.

- {{chip:red|late 2 days}} is past the day they gave us.
- {{chip:gray|due Thu}} has its day still ahead. A day ahead is on time, however long the step has taken.
- {{chip:yellow|4 d over usual}} has no day, and the step has taken longer than usual.
- {{chip:yellow|insurance expired}} finished the step once, and a paper has run out since.
- {{chip:blue|waiting on Jonah}} waits on someone else's step, so there is nothing to ask them.

Asked to quote shows one chip for each job. The chips of a job sit together under the job's name. The job's due day shows once when they share it.

Steps nobody is on fold into one line. The line says what lands there next.

## Act from a row

1. Press a company's chip to open its window on the Flow tab.
2. Press the button at the right of a row to do the step's own task.

The button changes with the step.

- {{button:outline|Ask the 4 for papers}} opens the first company's papers with the ask ready.
- {{button:blue|Remind the 1 late}} goes to the late ones first.
- {{button:outline|Open the compare}} opens the job's card, where Compare quotes is.
- {{button:outline|Bill day}} opens Money for that job.
- {{button:outline|Invite a company}} opens the list, where you add a company.

Nothing goes to a company until you press Send.

## Narrow the board

1. Pick a job in the list that starts on All jobs. Every row and tile keeps that job only.
2. Pick a seat in the list that starts on Everyone's seat. You see the steps in that person's seat, and every step that is theirs to move.
3. Pick All jobs and Everyone's seat again to see everyone.

## See one row for each company

1. Press {{button:outline|By company}} at the top.
2. Each company has one row, so its name shows once.

- The dots show the steps a company goes through once. A green dot is done. An orange ring is the step on now. A filled orange dot has lapsed.
- **On now** shows the step, whose move it is and how long it has taken. Over usual shows in orange.
- **Per job** has a line for each job with its step and its day. A job that ended says declined or not carried.
- **Portal** says opened when the company has opened its portal, and never when it has not.

Press {{button:outline|Open}} on a row to open the company's window on the Flow tab.

## See them by trade

1. Press {{button:outline|By trade}} at the top.
2. Each trade has a row, A to Z. The counts for the trade sit under its name.
3. Each company shows the step it is on, its job and its day.
4. Press a company to open its window on the Flow tab.

The late ones come first, in red. The companies off the flow come last. A company with two trades on its record shows under each trade. A company with no trade sits under **No trade yet**.

## See the customers the same way

1. Open [Customers](/gc?view=customers).
2. Press {{button:outline|By step}} or {{button:outline|By company}}.

The steps follow the customer's flow. The tiles read **Setting up**, **Bidding** and **Building**. A change order counts under Building.

In By company, the **Portal** column says when the customer last opened their page. The column reads today, 3 d ago or never. Opens by our own office never count.

## See who is off the flow

The last row is **Off the flow**. The row is folded at first.

1. Press **show** at the end of the row.
2. Each company shows the day it went off the flow. Point at a company to read why.
3. Press **fold** to fold the row again.
