The timeline shows one customer's whole story on one page. Every job is a colored rail from its first record to its final payment. Office work sits on the left and field work sits on the right.

## Open the timeline

1. On **Jobs → Pipeline**, click the customer's name on any row.
2. The customer's window opens. Press {{button:blue|Timeline}} at the top.
3. The window remembers your pick on this device. Next time it opens on the timeline.

Press {{button:outline|Profile}} to go back to the customer's contact details and job list.

There are three more ways in:

- Click the GC's name under a job on the Pipeline. The GC's timeline opens with every job they are the GC on.
- Type a customer's name in the Pipeline search. A chip like {{chip:blue|Ridgeway Builders · timeline ›}} appears above the board. Press it.
- On a customer's own page, press {{button:outline-blue|Timeline}} beside {{button:outline|✎ Edit customer}}.

Press {{button:outline-blue|Copy link}} in the timeline to copy a link to it. The link opens the Pipeline with this timeline on top.

## Read the bar at the top

The bar stays in place while you scroll. It shows four numbers.

- **Owes us** is every open bill. It names the oldest bill and any date they promised to pay.
- **Not yet billed** is work on working jobs that has no bill yet.
- **Unpaid hours** is crew time on jobs that are not paid in full.
- **Unpaid materials** is the supply-house tickets on those same jobs.

Scroll down and the bar shrinks to one line. A second line then shows what stood on the day under the bar. Scroll back a year to see what they owed us then.

## Read a job's rail

Each job has a rail of its own color. Today is at the top, so every rail runs up toward today.

- A ring marks the day the job was first on record.
- A dashed rail means the job is waiting to start.
- A solid rail means the crew is working.
- A hollow rail means the job is billed and we are waiting on their money.
- A red hollow rail means the job is in Collections.
- A filled circle with a check marks the day the job was paid in full.
- A rail that reaches the top under an arrow is still open.

A busy customer can have more open jobs than there is room for. The four jobs that owe the most get colored rails. The rest share one grey rail.

## Read the cards

Cards on the left are what happened with the customer. They are bills, payments, promises, statements, lien notices and office notes. Cards on the right are what we put in. They are crew hours, field notes, reports, tests and supply-house tickets.

- A payment says how many days after the bill it came. It also says when it was deposited and who paid.
- A job's crew days fold into one card until the office does something on that job. Press **show the days ›** to see each day and who worked it.
- When someone writes the same note on many jobs, the notes fold into one card. So do many bills or many new jobs on one day.
- The space between two days grows with the time that passed. A long stretch with nothing open folds to one quiet line.

Press a job's name on any card to open that job.

## Focus on one job

Press a job's chip in the bar, like {{chip:gray|944 · Clinic add-on}}. Every other job fades. Press the chip again to see every job.

## Show only the money or only the field

Press {{button:outline|Money}} to see only bills, payments, promises and notices. Press {{button:outline|Field}} to see only the field side. Press {{button:outline|Everything}} to see it all again.

## A GC's timeline

A GC's timeline holds every job where they are the GC. It also holds every job they pay for. When the owner of the house pays, each card says who pays.

## On a phone

On a phone the timeline is one column. Each day shows its office cards first and its field cards under them.
