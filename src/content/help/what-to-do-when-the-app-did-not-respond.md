A few error lines mean the app could not finish through no fault of yours. This guide says what each one means, what was saved and what to do.

## It did not respond in time

The line says the action *did not respond within* some seconds. The server was too slow, so the app stopped waiting. A save may still have landed after the app gave up. Look at the record before you save again. Refresh the app if it stays slow. Tell a dev if it lasts more than a few minutes.

## The link points to something that does not exist

The line reads *This link points to something that doesn't exist any more.* The record was deleted, merged or renamed. Nothing was saved. Find the record by its name or number instead. Tell whoever sent the link that it is dead.

## The app was renewing your sign-in

The line reads *The app was busy renewing your sign-in.* Another tab of the app was renewing it at that moment. Nothing was lost. Press {{button:outline|Retry}}. If it happens twice, close the other tabs of the app.

## Something failed with no better word

Sometimes the line has only the words the app got back. Try once more. If it fails again, tell a dev the words and what you pressed.
