ClickTooling is one system for the whole shop. You win work, run it and settle it in one place.

You win work with **Bids** and **Estimates**. You run it with **Projects** with workflow steps, the **Jobs** pipeline, and **Schedule & Dispatch**. You settle it with billing, payments, payroll and banking.

**People** come in nine roles. **Leaders**, the master plumbers, run the work and the office. Office staff work across the whole company. Those are **Assistants** and **Controllers**. Controllers also handle payroll. Field roles see only work assigned to them. Those are **Subs**, the subcontractors, and **Helpers**. **Estimators** live in Bids and Materials. **Primaries** are customer-side principals with billing visibility. **Superintendents** supervise their assigned projects. **Devs** administer everything.

## The basic model

1. The company has **Customers**. One company, one book. Nobody owns a customer.
2. Customers can have **Projects**.
3. Leaders assign People to **Project Steps**.
4. When People complete Steps, the office is updated.

Day-to-day service work runs through **Jobs**. Each job belongs to a customer and moves through a simple pipeline. You'll see these status chips all over the app:

:::example The job pipeline
{{chip:gray|Waiting}} → {{chip:blue|Working}} → {{chip:yellow|Ready to bill}} → {{chip:red|Billed}} → {{chip:green|Paid}}
:::

**New jobs start in Working.** That is true whether you add them with New Job or they come out of an accepted estimate. **Waiting** is the parking stage. You send a job back to it when it isn't live yet.

A job collects everything about the work in one place: schedule blocks, clock time, reports, materials and billing.

## One company

- Every office role sees every customer, project, job, bid and estimate. That means leaders, assistants and controllers. There is nothing to adopt or share. A new office hire sees the whole company on day one.
- Superintendents see the projects they are assigned to. Primaries see their customer's money. Field roles see their own jobs.

## Subcontractors

- Only see a workflow step when it is assigned to them.
- Can Start and Complete their steps, and **accept or decline work orders** offered on a step.
- See **their own pay**: their sub sheets and a money view on the Dashboard. They never see Notes for Office or anyone else's financials.
- Cannot add, edit, delete, or assign steps.

A Leader or Assistant can select **Notify** on a step. That step then shows up in their Subscribed Steps on the Dashboard.

## Finding your way around

The header is your map. On a phone, most of these live behind the {{icon:gear}} gear menu in the top-right.

:::example Header icons (top-right)
{{icon:gear}} **Gear menu** — Job Mode toggle, Dark Mode toggle, Calendar, Help (this page), Settings, Sign out
:::

- **Dashboard**: your day at a glance. Assigned jobs, clock, inboxes, and billing queues for office roles.
- **Jobs**: the job ledger and its Pipeline board. Jobs open in two modals that link to each other. **Job Detail** is the read-only view with the activity history. It has an Edit affordance. **Edit Job** is the form. Its {{button:outline-blue|Job Detail}} button at the top closes the form and opens the detail view. Edit Job has no Save button. Changes save automatically as you make them. See the "know when Edit Job saves my changes" guide.
- **Schedule**: who is on which job, each day.
- **Bids / Estimates**: winning the work before it becomes a job.
- **Settings**: your profile, notifications, and role-specific configuration.
- **Help**, this page: guides for every part of the app. Each guide has a feedback box at the bottom that goes straight to the devs.

## Our mission

ClickTooling is designed to decrease the actions and thinking it takes to engage and win work. It is for Plumbers, Electricians and HVAC techs. It also reduces the communication risk of completing that work with Assistants, Teammates, Subs and Customers. The mission is to reduce uncertainty so better and faster decisions can be made.
