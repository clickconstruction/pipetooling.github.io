A trip is one going out to a site away from home and coming back. You keep it on the site's Development page, apart from the hours on its jobs.

On a far site, the hours on a job do not say where the crew was. Some hours are at the site. Some are a truck on the road. Some are a person at home loading the trailer. A trip sorts them.

A trip never changes the clock. It changes no bill and no cost. It is a reading laid over the hours you already have.

## Find the Trips look

1. Open the site's Development page.
2. Find **Trips** at the end of the strip of looks.
3. Press **Trips** to jump to it.

The look shows on a site that has a trip. It also shows on a site 150 miles or more from the shop.

A nearer site with no trip has no look. Its page has one door instead. Find **Trips** in the line under the address and press *none yet · add one*.

## Add a trip

1. Press {{button:blue|+ Trip}}.
2. Answer **Who went?** Pick each person from **+ a person**.
3. Press **+ someone not in the app** for a sub or a visitor. Type the name and press **Add**.
4. Answer **Which days?** Pick the first day and the last day.
5. Answer **What for?** in a few words.
6. Press {{button:blue|Save the trip}}.

One person and one day is a whole trip. Everything else can wait.

## Paint the days

Every day you pick starts as At the site. A tap changes one day.

{{chip:green|At the site}} {{chip:blue|On the road}} {{chip:yellow|Getting ready}}

1. Tap a day once to make it On the road.
2. Tap it again to make it Getting ready.
3. Tap it a third time to turn it off. A day off is a day nobody was on the trip.
4. Tap it once more to bring it back to At the site.

Getting ready is a day at home for the trip, such as loading the trailer. To count one, move the first day back and paint the new day.

## Give one person their own days

Everyone on a trip shares its days at first. One person can join late or drive home early.

1. Add two or more people.
2. Press **Give one person their own days ›**.
3. Tap the days in that person's row.

Press **Same days as the trip** to put the person back on the trip's days.

## Fill in the rest when you know it

Press *More · all of it can wait* on the sheet. Nothing under it is needed to save.

- **Who asked for it?** Press **Us** or **Them**. For Them, type who asked and how.
- **Work goes on.** Pick the jobs the trip's work belongs to. A trip can sit on one job or several.
- **Billing.** Press **Inside the price**, **Extra** or **Ours**. The choice bills nothing by itself.
- **How it went.** Type a sentence once the trip is back.

## Read the band

The band at the top answers one question. Of all the hours on this site, how many were at the site?

:::cards

:::card At the site
Hours on a day painted At the site, with a punch at the site.
:::

:::card On the road
Hours on a day painted On the road. A punch somewhere else on an At the site day counts here too.
:::

:::card Getting ready
Hours on a day painted Getting ready.
:::

:::card No place on the punch
Hours from a row that carries no place. An imported row or a scheduled row has none. The row says the day was paid. It does not say where.
:::

:::card At home for the site
Hours on the site's jobs outside every trip the person was on.
:::

:::

The five figures always add up to the hours on the site. No hour is counted twice.

A row with no place is never added to At the site. That keeps the At the site figure honest.

## Read where a trip stands

Nobody types where a trip stands. The page reads it from the days and today.

- **Planned** means the first day is still ahead.
- **Out now** runs from the first day through the last day.
- **Back** means the last day has passed.

The strip under the band shows the year's trips against time. A planned trip is drawn faint. Press a bar to open its trip.

## Answer Looks like a trip

Sometimes people punch in at the site on days no trip covers. The look then says **Looks like a trip** above the trips. The line names who punched in, on how many days, and when.

The app never makes a trip by itself. You answer each line once.

1. Press {{button:blue|Make it a trip}} to open a new trip.
2. Check the people and the days. Each day they punched in at the site is painted At the site.
3. Change what you need and press {{button:blue|Save the trip}}.

Nothing is saved until you press **Save the trip**. Close the sheet to leave everything as before.

Press {{button:outline|Not a trip}} when those days were not a trip. The page remembers your answer and does not offer those days again. A message offers **Undo** for one minute.

The look shows three of these lines at most. A quiet line under them counts the rest.

No line shows while the clock is being read. No line shows on a site whose address has no point on the map.

## Open a trip

1. Press a trip's row.
2. Read the left side for what was typed. It shows the days, who went, who asked and how it went.
3. Read the right side for what was counted from the clock.

A trip with no punch at the site says so in amber. The trip still stands as painted.

## Read What shows it

An open trip says **What shows it** under the counted hours. Each chip is a count over the trip's own days.

{{chip:green|8 punches at the site}} {{chip:green|4 On my way and I'm here stamps}} {{chip:gray|3 lines in History}}

- The first chip counts clock rows by the trip's people, on their trip days, at the site.
- The second chip counts the **On my way** and **I'm here** presses on those same days.
- The third chip counts the History lines on the trip's days. The trip's own two lines are left out.

A green chip means the clock saw something. A grey chip with a zero means nothing was found.

A day a person was off is not their trip day. A punch on that day shows nothing for the trip.

The counts change nothing. They only say what stands behind the painted days.

## Change or delete a trip

1. Open the trip.
2. Press {{button:outline|Change}}, or tap one of its days.
3. Change what you need and press {{button:blue|Save the trip}}.

To delete, press **Delete this trip** on the open trip. The page asks once, by the trip's days. Press {{button:red|Delete this trip}} to confirm.

A message then offers **Undo** for one minute. Deleting a trip changes no clock row, no bill and no cost.

## See a trip in History

The History look shows a line the day a trip leaves. It shows a second line the day the trip is back. The lines come from the trip's days, so they move when the days change.

## Who can do this

The office, the leaders and the controller read and change every trip. The related guide is [see everything on a development in one place](/help/see-everything-on-a-development).
