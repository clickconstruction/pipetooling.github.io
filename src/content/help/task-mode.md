Task Mode strips the app down to one thing: your checklist. The mode is for anyone whose whole day is tasks.

Think shop clean-up, truck checks and the morning feed run. That person does not need Jobs, Schedule, or anything else in the way.

Task Mode was called Farm Mode until November 2026. The name changed and nothing else did. A device that had the mode on still has it on.

While Task Mode is on:

- The way out is always on screen. A borrowed tablet never looks like a broken app. Every role but the estimator sees [the row at the top](/help/job-mode-clocking#the-row-at-the-top), with **Task Mode** in blue. An estimator sees a green strip that says *☑ Task Mode · Exit*.
- The header shows a single **Checklist** link. Every other page redirects back to the checklist. The Roadmap is included.
- The checklist keeps two views on one joined bar. **Today** is your assigned tasks for the day. **History** is what you have completed.
- The top of Today shows the date and one line that says where you stand. Your first open task waits in a card marked **Next**, with {{button:blue|Mark done}} on it.
- When one of your open tasks has a place, Today opens on a map called **Where my tasks are**. [See all my tasks on a map](/help/check-off-my-daily-tasks#see-all-my-tasks-on-a-map) shows how to use it.
- Everything else comes back the moment you turn the mode off. The office tabs like Review and Manage come back. So do the Roadmap page and the `/` people finder.

## Turning Task Mode on

Tap **Task Mode** on [the row at the top](/help/job-mode-clocking#the-row-at-the-top) of the Dashboard. The app jumps to your checklist.

An estimator's first page is Bids, so an estimator has no row. An estimator does it this way:

1. Tap the {{icon:gear}} **gear menu** in the top-right of the header.
2. Tap **Task Mode**. A green check appears and the app jumps to the checklist.

The gear menu of every other role does not list **Task Mode**.

## Turning it off

Tap **Dashboard** or **Job Mode** on the row at the top. The app opens the page that was tapped. On **Dashboard**, Dispatch Mode is on again if you had Dispatch Mode on before.

An estimator has no row. An estimator taps {{button:outline|Exit}} on the green **Task Mode** strip at the top of the page. The {{icon:gear}} gear menu's **Task Mode** does the same. The full app comes back as the estimator left it.

## One mode at a time

Job Mode, Dispatch Mode and Task Mode each change the whole app. So only one of them is on at a time.

- The row at the top shows the mode you are in, in blue. The row never shows two.
- Tap another part of the row and the app switches to it.
- **Dispatch Mode** is a tick in the {{icon:gear}} gear menu. Tap **Dashboard** on the row and Dispatch Mode is back, if you had Dispatch Mode on.
- An estimator's gear menu ticks the mode the estimator is in. That menu never ticks two.

**Pin bar**, **Tell the dev** and **Dark Mode** are not part of this. Those three sit under **Show** in the gear menu. Each of those goes on or off by itself.

## Good to know

- The mode is **per device and per account**. Turning it on at the shop tablet does not change your phone.
- Task Mode only changes what this device shows. Your permissions stay the same. The office still sees your completed tasks in Review.
- Anyone can use it, whatever their role.
- The **＋ Task** button in the header and the home-screen **/task** shortcut keep working inside the mode. So does {{button:outline|＋ Add a task for me}} under today's tasks. Adding a task never needs the full app.
