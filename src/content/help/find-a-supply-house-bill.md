One box searches every supply house bill on file. Type what you know about the bill, and the bills that hold it are listed.

## Search every bill

:::steps

1. Open [Supply houses](/materials?tab=supply-houses) in Materials.
2. Type in the box named **Search every bill**, above the list of houses.
3. Read the bills under the box. The newest is first.

:::

Type any of these:

- An invoice number. Spaces and dashes do not matter.
- A supply house's name, or part of it.
- A PO number or its words.
- An amount, such as **1,085.58**.
- A day, such as **nov 16** or **11/16**.
- A month, such as **october**, or a month and a year.
- A part printed on the bill, such as **ball valve**.
- A supply house's part code, or the maker's number.
- The job the bill is on, by its name or its number.
- Any other word printed on the bill, such as a street in the ship-to.

Type two words and a bill must hold both. A search starts at two letters.

## Search from anywhere

The search at the top of the app finds bills too. Type an invoice number or a part there. Bills are listed under the jobs and bids, each marked **Bill**. It lists the first five. Press one to open it.

## Read what it found

Each bill shows its number, its supply house, its day and its amount. Under that are the jobs the bill is on, and its PO. A bill split between jobs shows each job's share.

A bill found by a part says **On the paper** and shows the line that matched. Your words are marked in it.

:::example Find who sold us a part
Type **ball valve oct**. The list shows each October bill with a ball valve on it. Each one names its supply house, its job and the line on the paper.
:::

A bill with no PDF reads {{chip:yellow|no PDF}}. A bill that is paid reads **paid**.

The count beside the box says how many bills were found. The search shows the first 60. Add a word to narrow it.

## Open a bill or share it

Press a bill's number. The bill opens over the page, with the bills you found beside the paper. Press **Next** or the right arrow key to read the next one. See [search and share a job's supply house invoices](/help/search-and-share-a-jobs-supply-house-invoices#flip-through-the-invoices).

Press the link icon at the left of a bill to copy a link to it. A note on the screen reads **Link copied to clipboard**.

## What it can and cannot find

The search is exact. A bill is found when your words are on it.

A part is found on the bills whose lines the app has read. That is about nine bills in ten. A bill with no PDF is found by its number, its house, its PO, its amount or its day.

The app also keeps every word printed on a bill, such as the ship-to address and who ordered. Type one of those words to find the bill. The list then shows the words around the one you typed. A scanned bill has no words to keep. The bills already on file get their words over a few hours, by themselves.

When nothing matches, the list says so. Press **Clear the search** to start again.
