When a move changes a trade's days, the trade needs its new dates. Tell the trades sends each company one email with its old and new days and why. The company answers from its portal.

Only devs can tell the trades for now. Nothing goes to a real trade without the owner's yes.

## Tell the trades

1. Open the project's schedule from [GC projects](/gc).
2. Find **Changes to the schedule** under the chart. The **Tell the trades** button there counts the companies not told yet.
3. Press it. The window shows each company and the email it gets.
4. Press a company to read its email.
5. Press {{button:blue|Tell 2 companies}}. The button names the company when there is only one.

Each company gets one email with its portal link. The window then says who was told. A company that was not told is named with why.

Emails go in English for now. Spanish comes once a native speaker reads it.

## After a what-if

After you keep a what-if, a violet line over the chart names the companies not told. Press **Tell the trades** on that line. See [try moves on a copy of the schedule](/help/try-moves-on-a-copy-of-the-schedule).

## See who was told

Each move in **Changes to the schedule** says who was told and what they answered. A move nobody was told of says **The trades have not been told.**

A company answers in its portal. The company can say the dates work, or ask for another day.

## When a company was not told

A company with no email on file is not told. Add an email to the company's record. Then press **Tell the trades** again. Only that company gets an email.

The email may go out while the record of it does not save. Press **Tell the trades** again. Nothing is sent twice.

## When you undo a told move

Undo puts the bar back. The company still has the dates you sent. The move then says **Told, then undone** with a **Call** link. Call the company and give them the dates.
