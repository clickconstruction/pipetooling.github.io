You can text the customer from Job Mode in two presses. You pick a line the office wrote, and the app fills it in for this job.

## Open it

1. Clock in on the job in Job Mode.
2. Under the big buttons, press **Message the customer**.

An orange dot on **Message the customer** means the customer wrote back. Their answer shows at the top when you open it.

If you do not see **Message the customer**, clock in on the job first. If a text says texts are off, ask the office to turn them on.

## Pick a line

The sheet lists the lines the office put on the phone. Each one shows its name and where its words come from.

- **On my way** takes the minutes you type. The big green button sends the same line and guesses the minutes for you. See [clock in and out with Job Mode](/help/job-mode-clocking#tell-the-customer-you-are-on-the-way).
- **Running late** takes the new window from your schedule.
- Their first name, your name and the job's address are filled for you.

Press a line to see it before it goes.

## Check it and send it

1. Read the line in the orange box. That is what the customer gets.
2. Type anything the app cannot know, like the minutes out. {{button:outline|Send to Dana}} waits until you do.
3. Press **Send to** and their name.

:::example A line filled for this job
Dana, this is Luis with Click Plumbing. I am on my way, about 15 minutes out.
:::

Their answer comes back to you. The office sees it too.

## See what they said

**Job updates** on the Job Mode card lists the texts and emails with the customer. They are orange, beside the notes. Press {{button:outline|Customer}} to see only those.

The job's **Job detail** window shows them the same way. You see the lines of a job you worked this week or last. A new text from the customer shows as it lands.

## Say something else

The phone has no box for your own words. When none of the lines fit, press **Something else**.

1. Write what the customer should hear.
2. Press **Send to the office**.

The note goes on the job for the office. The customer never sees it. The office sends them what they should hear.

## What stays off the phone

Lines about money stay off the phone, since Job Mode shows you no prices. Lines with a link to the customer's page stay off too. The office sends those.
