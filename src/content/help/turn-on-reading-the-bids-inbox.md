The app can read new email in the bids inbox and put each email on its sender's thread in Messages. The owner or a dev turns it on.

## Turn it on

1. Open [Phone and texts](/settings?tab=settings-phone-texts) in Settings.
2. Under **Read bids@**, press the {{button:outline|Off}} switch.
3. Press {{button:blue|Turn it on}}.

The switch now reads {{button:green|On}}. The first read comes within a minute. After that the app reads the inbox every minute.

An email that Gmail first put in Spam lands too, once it reaches the Inbox. Moving it there is enough.

Each email it reads lands on its sender's thread. In Gmail, that email gets the label **In ClickTooling**.

An invitation to bid lands as **Bid it?** in New, held by the estimator. An email whose subject names a bid we are pricing lands on that bid's thread. The guide [sort new messages before anyone answers them](/help/sort-new-messages#bid-it) shows the card.

## A teammate's email

An email from someone on the team lands too. It goes on their own thread, under the **Our team** pill on Messages. It shows in New or Needs a reply like their texts do.

A teammate who emails a customer can copy the inbox. That email then lands on the customer's thread as ours, with the teammate's name. The office sees it without asking.

:::example A tech writes from the job
Rosa emails Elena about Thursday and copies team@. Elena's thread shows *Rosa Diaz to Elena Marchetti · copied to team@*.
:::

## An email sent from the inbox

The app reads what each inbox sends too. Answer a customer from bids@ in Apple Mail or in Gmail. The answer lands on the customer's thread as ours within a minute. The line carries the name the inbox signs with. Their move becomes the next one, and Went out lists the email.

The name on the line is the name your mail app puts on the From. Set it to your own name and the line is yours.

An email sent from the inbox to a teammate only stays in Gmail with no label. An email the app sent from the inbox is already on the thread. It gets the label and nothing more.

:::example An estimator answers from the inbox
Wendi answers Rusty from bids@ in Apple Mail. A minute later his thread shows *Wendi Doll to Rusty Cottar · sent from bids@*. The thread reads *Their move*.
:::

## What it never does

- The app never deletes, moves or archives an email.
- The app never marks an email read.
- A newsletter stays in Gmail with no label. The app knows one by its headers, or by the unsubscribe line at its foot.
- A draft stays in Gmail with no label. So does an email sent only to the team.

## A sender you marked a notice

The office can mark a sender **A notice, not a person** on the sort card in New. The guide [sort new messages before anyone answers them](/help/sort-new-messages#a-notice-not-a-person) shows the press. From then on the app leaves that address out. Its email stays in Gmail with no label, like a newsletter. Marking the company's domain leaves out every address there.

Press **Not a notice after all** on the thread to take them off the list. Their next email lands again.

The app writes down each mail it sets aside, a marked sender's or one that says unsubscribe at its foot. New shows the count in a fold with **Put back** on each. The guide [sort new messages before anyone answers them](/help/sort-new-messages#notices-set-aside) shows it.

## See how it is doing

The line under the switch says when the app last read the inbox, such as *Last read today at 7:12 PM. Nothing wrong.*

When a read stops on a problem, the line shows the problem's own words. The next read starts where the last good one stopped.

## The team inbox

The same switch reads the office's own inbox too, once a dev has entered its address. Its email lands on each sender's thread, as the bids inbox's does. The first read starts with new email only.

A second line under the switch then says how the team inbox is doing, such as *The team box: Last read today at 7:12 PM. Nothing wrong.*

A problem with one inbox never stops the other.

## Turn it off

Press the {{button:green|On}} switch. Email to the bids inbox then stays in Gmail only.
