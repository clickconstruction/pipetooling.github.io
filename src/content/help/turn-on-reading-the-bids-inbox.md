The app can read new email in the bids inbox and put each email on its sender's thread in Messages. The owner or a dev turns it on.

## Turn it on

1. Open [Phone and texts](/settings?tab=settings-phone-texts) in Settings.
2. Under **Read bids@**, press the {{button:outline|Off}} switch.
3. Press {{button:blue|Turn it on}}.

The switch now reads {{button:green|On}}. The first read comes within 3 minutes. After that the app reads the inbox every 3 minutes.

Each email it reads lands on its sender's thread. In Gmail, that email gets the label **In ClickTooling**.

## What it never does

- The app never deletes, moves or archives an email.
- The app never marks an email read.
- Newsletters and notices stay in Gmail with no label.

## See how it is doing

The line under the switch says when the app last read the inbox, such as *Last read today at 7:12 PM. Nothing wrong.*

When a read stops on a problem, the line shows the problem's own words. The next read starts where the last good one stopped.

## Turn it off

Press the {{button:green|On}} switch. Email to the bids inbox then stays in Gmail only.
