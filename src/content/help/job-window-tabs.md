A job now opens as one window with six tabs. No more separate Job Detail and Edit Job modals bounce you between each other.

The window is **one window with six tabs**. On a computer the first line holds the job's number, its name and its status. A quiet line under it reads the development, the customer, the old numbers and the kind. The tabs run in a strip under that line. One **✕** at the top right closes the whole thing. **Escape** closes it too. The {{button:outline|?}} beside the ✕ opens a card that says what each tab holds. The card ends with a link to this guide. On a phone the window fills the screen. The job's name and its status come first. A quiet line under them reads the number, the trade, the customer and the kind. The **⋯** and the **✕** stand at the right, one above the other. The seven tabs run on one line under the name and scroll sideways. As you read, the name, the tabs and the ✕ scroll away. Scroll back up and the tabs come back at the top with the **✕** at their right.

On a computer, **every tab** shows the same action band under the tab bar. The band holds the trade chip and the action icons. Each icon has its name under it. The icons are share, **Add job account**, **Email the paid-in-full report** and calendar. Then come send as task, 📝 **Make change order** and ⚙. The 📝 shows only for people who switched *Make change order from the field* on in Settings. The header also holds the **Street View photo** with the 📍 map link. The icons work from any tab. You open the job calendar while billing. You share the job while editing. The address stays one glance away. So you always know which house you are on. The **Add job account** storefront icon turns **teal** once a job-account packet has gone out for this job. You hover it to see who got it and when. You click it for the history or to resend. See [add a job account at a supply house](?g=add-a-job-account-at-a-supply-house).

On a phone there are no icons. Every door is a row of the **⋯** sheet by the job's name. **Share the job** and **Open the calendar** come first. Then **Add job account**, **Money story** and **Email the paid-in-full report**. Then **Send as a task** and **Make change order**. **What this window holds** is the last row. You see only the rows your role has. The last row opens the card that says what each tab holds.

## On a phone: the bar at the bottom

On a phone the window carries a bar under the body. The bar stays put while you scroll. The bar reads {{button:outline|Status ▾}} {{button:blue|Ready to bill}} {{button:outline|Note}}. The middle button is **the job's next move**. The job's Pipeline row flags the same move. {{button:red|Set % done}} shows when a bill went out with no progress recorded. {{button:green|Bill it}} shows when a draw is ready. A draw is one bill for part of the job. **Send bill…** shows when a draft is waiting. Otherwise the button reads **Move to Working**, **Ready to bill** or **Mark paid**. You tap **Status ▾**. The Edit tab's status rail opens in a sheet. You tap the next stage. A move that needs a reason, a bill line or a payment asks for it there. **Note** opens a note box at the bottom of the screen, with the keyboard under it. You type the note and press {{button:blue|Save}}. The bar is put away while the note box is up, so no other button sits under your thumb. Type **@** in the box to [tag a teammate](/help/tag-a-teammate-in-a-job-note#tag-from-the-job-s-own-window).

The status shows as a chip beside the job's name. The Street View photo shows on every tab, with the address on it. On the other tabs it is the only thing above the tab's own content. **Paint a map** sits centred under it on the Job tab. On a computer it is a small press under the photo. The photos are a banner you flick sideways, on the Job tab. **Add a photo** is the first tile. Then every photo follows, newest first, with its day. Tap a photo to open it.

## On a phone: the Job tab in sections

The Job tab reads in six sections, each with a title. **Where** holds the Street View photo, the address and the painted maps. **Photos** holds the banner. The count and **open all** sit at the right of its title. **Who** holds the customer. The number is a press that calls. **Text** and **Copy** sit beside it. The email address is a press that emails from the office. **Copy** sits beside it. Then come three presses: **Portal**, **Files** and **Photos**. A grey one says *set* when the link is missing. Tap it to add the link.

**What's happening** is three rows. **Next on site** reads the day, then the hours on their own line, then who is on it. It ends in **Open the calendar**. **Waiting on** says who the job waits on and for what, such as *The office, to bill it*. When the move is ours, the words are blue. Tap them to make the move. When the move is the customer's, the row ends in our act, such as **Mark paid**. **Hours** is the hours target. It ends in **Set the hours**.

**The thread** is three rows from the Where it stands card. **Last word** says whose word came last and when. It ends in **Reply**. **They promised** holds a day the customer gave. It ends in **Write it down**, or **Change it** once one is written. **We owe them** holds what we still owe them. It ends in **Mark it**, or **Change it**. Under the rows sit the newest two messages with the customer. Then comes **Open the thread**.

**More on this job** holds the rest in folds. The folds are **Questions on this job**, **Activity** and **Money**. Then come **Plans and papers**, **Work and time** and **Their journey**. Tap a fold to open it. The phone remembers which folds you opened. **Money** starts with two lines. One ends in the next money move, such as **Bill it**. The other ends in **Add job account**. **Arrived** and **Leaving** appear only for people on the job's crew. On a phone the one you are due sits on the bar before **Note**. The bar then reads {{button:outline|Status ▾}} {{button:blue|Ready to bill}} {{button:outline-amber|Leaving}} {{button:outline|Note}}. Leaving posts the note and opens the On my way sheet for your next job. With no next job it opens Update Focus. See [the job window on a phone](/help/job-mode-clocking#the-job-window-on-a-phone).

## The six tabs

- {{chip:blue|Job}} is the read view. It shows the photo and address, the customer and contacts, and the **Job accounts** line. That line reads *Ferguson ✓ · Reece none yet*. You tap a chip for what to say at the counter or to ask the office. See *open a job account before buying parts*. The tab also shows the numbered activity feed, the work and bill dates, and a compact **Costs** card. The card has one line each for team labor, sub labor, parts, and margin. You tap it to open the Costs tab. This is where "open job detail" lands.
- {{chip:blue|Edit}} is the job itself: numbers, name, address, service type, and the people-and-customer rows below. The row's ✎ Edit button lands here. So does the ⚙ on the Job tab.
- {{chip:blue|Bill}} is money **in**. On a computer the **Line Items** sit right at the top. On a phone the money card comes first. Then come **Make a bill**, the bills and the payments. The line items come last. On a phone each line item has a **⋯** at its right. It holds **Move up**, **Move down** and **Remove this line**. They are the job's scope and Job Total. A price box reads its cents small, and the Job Total does too. Click into a box and it is the plain digits again. The badge at a line's left sets its stage kind. Tap it to flip between In order and Any time. See *split a job into stages and bill stage by stage*. Then comes **② Bills and payments**. Its money card says what is done, paid, billed and left. It gives each line a row with where its money stands. **Make a bill** follows while money is left. Then come the bills, each with the payments that paid it. A saved payment that landed on the wrong job has {{button:outline|Move to job…}}. You pick the right job. You read both jobs' paid and open before and after. You say why. The payment moves with its date, amount and bank link. A payment a sent bill already counted asks you to unlink it from that bill first. Both jobs keep a grey trace line under the table. A payment moved onto a job and later removed gets a second line under its moved line, like *$1.00 removed · Robert*. A supply house **job account** may be on file for the job. Then a teal note sits just above Invoices. It says who holds it and when the packet went out. It flags any unpaid supplier invoices on the account. See *mark an invoice as on a job account*.
- {{chip:blue|Costs}} is money **out**, told straight. Four numbers come first. They are **true margin at completion**, **spent so far**, **earned so far** and **time left**. True margin at completion is the share of the price the job should keep after every cost. Earned so far is % done × the price. Then comes the baseline strip. On a project with line budgets, **Where we are** comes next. It reads spent of the whole budget, a Labor bar and a Materials bar, and one row per line. See *see what a line should cost and tune it*. Then comes one chart. Owners, controllers and master techs see the chart. See *read the cost and value timeline on a job*. Then **Where the money went** shows each source's share. It sits above the parts accordions. Those are supply house invoices, card charges, parts from tally, and other job charges with **+ Add other charge**. An other charge's amount reads as money, with commas and smaller cents. Click into the box and it is the plain digits again. A charge whose description starts with **ESTIMATE** wears an *Estimate* chip and shows the words after it. Click into that box and the whole description is there to change. Each charge has a date on its left, the day the charge applies to. A new row gets today's date, and you can clear it. **+ memo** under a charge opens a line for the basis of the number. Say where it came from and what would replace it, so the description stays short. The printed Costs packet shows the date and the memo too. **Card charges** counts what the job cost, the same way Job Summary does. An Internal Transfer is not a cost. A card charge that is also on a supply-house invoice is counted once, under the invoice. Those lines still show in the list. Each has a grey note saying why it is not in the total. **Fuel gets its own line**. It sits on the Job tab's Costs card under Parts. In **Where the money went** it is ⛽ **Fuel & gas** beside **Other card charges**. The total does not change. The fuel is just no longer hidden inside Parts. A card charge counts as fuel when its accounting label is in the Fuel & gas tag. With no label, it counts when the bank filed it under fuel. Each one carries a ⛽ marker in the card charges list. Any other tag the office marks *show as a cost line* gets a line the same way. The daily spend and the Cost Timeline sit behind ***Show the timeline · daily spend***. Everything that used to sit at the bottom of Bill lives here now. Owners, controllers and master techs also see {{button:gray|Print}} on the tab's first line. It prints the whole tab as four Letter pages, with the date and your name on each sheet. On a phone the chart sits behind **Show the chart**. Tap it and the chart draws. **Where the money went** is rows on a phone. Each row has the source at the left and the cost at the right. A bar under the row shows its share. A row that opens something says so, such as *show below*. Tap the row and that card opens below.

- {{chip:blue|History}} is the day grid. It has one row per day worked, coloured by how many people were on site. It is the same view Projects → Job History shows. Now it is there for every job, project or not.

- {{chip:blue|Documents}} is the job's paperwork. It lists the job's pay applications, each with its number, its period and the payment due. You press **Open the file** to see the file that was sent. You press **Open** to change the application. See *fill out an AIA G702-G703*. Under them come the job's **Bills**. You press a bill's name to open it, or **PDF** to get the invoice in a new tab. Then comes the job's **Contract**. A signed contract opens the Contract window. Then come the job's **Test reports**. A sent report opens the PDF the GC received. A draft opens the Test report window. Then comes the job's **Lien paper**. That is its lien notices, demand letters and releases of lien. A release opens the page as it was signed. A notice or a letter opens the Lien window. Then come the job's **Stage checklists**. On a phone each stage is one row with its standing, such as **Ready, 4 of 4** or **Filed**. Tap the stage to open it. They read every answer with the person's name and the time. A finished list files itself as a record with a number. **Print the checklists** prints every stage on one paper. See *add a check to a job's stage checklist* and *read a filed stage checklist*. The tab ends with the job's folders. On a Pipeline row, the folder icon under **Edit** opens this tab. Hold the Command key and press it to open the job's folder in Drive.

## Add a file to a job's folder

Open the job's **Documents** tab and find **Job folders**. Each folder has a small {{button:outline|Add a file}} press beside its name.

1. Press **Add a file** on the folder you want. Your computer's file picker opens.
2. Pick the file. The tab shows its name and size, and says where it goes.
3. Press {{button:blue|Add it to Plans}}. The press names the folder you chose.

The app puts the file in that folder in Drive. A line then says it is there, with **Open in Drive**. Press **Add another** to put in one more.

Job folder is the job's own folder. Plans is for the drawings and anything trades should see. Pictures is Customer Pictures, with the job's photos.

On a phone each folder row ends in **Add ▾**. It opens a sheet of three. **Take a photo** opens the camera. The shot goes into that folder by itself. It is named by the day, who took it and the folder. **Choose a file** opens the phone's own picker. **Open in Google Drive** opens the folder.

A file over 10 MB is too big for the press. Put it in the folder in Drive instead. A training account cannot add a file.

## Where is the team labor number?

Owners, controllers and master techs see **Team labor** as the first line of the Job tab's Costs card. It is also the first row of the cost block on the **Costs** tab. It shows the total, then *8.0 h · Malachi* or *277.5 h · 7 people* under it. You tap it for the per-person split. That row is the same number the Cost Timeline's 👷 markers add up to. It is the same one Job Summary's **Labor** column shows. So the three always agree. A salaried day counts as that day's hours in the person's set week, on the job they clocked to. Hourly people count their recorded session hours. Other roles see just the Parts line on the card. They see the block as **Parts Cost** on the Costs tab, without the row. The dollars come from wages.

## Who the job still owes for sub labor

The **Costs** tab shows **Subs on this job** under the cost block. It lists each person the job's sub sheets are owed to. Each person's line shows their sheets and what is still open. A green check says the lines add up.

A teammate with a sheet of their own is listed too, with a {{chip:purple|Team · sub labor}} chip. **Open the sheet ›** opens that sheet on Jobs → Subs → Pay. **What we owe ‹name› ›** opens that person's row on People → Subs. An amber line warns when two sheets of the job carry the same line and amount.

Devs, master techs, assistants and controllers see the block. A job with no sub sheets shows nothing here.

## The History tab

The job may still owe money. Or a lien paper or demand letter may be out. Then **History** shows the job's **lien timeline** above the grid. A lien is a legal claim on the property for unpaid work. The timeline lists every deadline in order and whose move it is. It shows the demand letter with its reply-by day, and a *Waiting on* line. It is the same strip the Lien window's header draws. On this tab it is the calendar. The verdict sits first in a tinted band. Every window is drawn, the closed ones too. See *send lien notices from the Lien desk* → *Where a job stands*. Under it, **Days on the job** is a calendar. One line counts the days worked, first day to last. It also gives the most people on one day, and the hours. Under the line, **Who** lists each person with their days. Tap a name and only their days stay lit. Tap it again to see everyone. Then comes one month grid for every month the job had work. On a sub job each month also carries its § 53.056 notice line. The § 53.056 notice is the Texas form we send the owner and the GC for each unpaid month. It uses the lien timeline's own words and colors. The number in a day is how many people clocked in. Weekends sit back. Today has a blue ring. A day still clocked in has an amber corner. Tap a day to see who was there, their hours and what the day cost. Nothing on this tab edits anything. The calendar shows the whole job by default. The {{button:outline|Whole job}} menu on the card's top line narrows it to the last 90 or 365 days. Pick dates… in that menu shows From and To pickers. When the range holds no day worked, the card says so and offers Whole job. On a phone the months stack one under the other.

## The Edit tab reads as rows

The middle of the Edit tab is a compact list. Its rows are **Account man, Team, Customer, Phone, Email, GC/Builder, Date met, Folders, Project, Plans, Bid, Development**. GC means the general contractor. Each row shows the current value at a glance, with a *—* where nothing is set. You tap a row or its ✎ to open the familiar editor for just that field. You tap again to fold it away. On a phone the rows read under five titles. They are **The people**, **The folders**, **The customer**, **The GC and the site** and **The paper**. Each row is 44 px tall and a chevron sits at its right. The status rail is not on the Edit tab on a phone. The bar's **Status ▾** is the same rail. The **Folders** and **Plans** rows keep their Drive links clickable right on the row. So opening the customer's files never requires expanding anything.

## Things worth knowing

- **Switching tabs never loses work.** You type half a job name. You hop to Bill to check the remaining amount. You come back. Your keystrokes are still there. Autosave keeps running throughout. See *know when Edit Job saves my changes*.
- Edits you make on the Edit or Bill tab show up on the Job tab right away. It refreshes itself after each save.
- {{button:red|Delete}} lives at the bottom of the **Edit** tab only. Its confirm rounds team labor to a readable figure, such as "≈ 22.8 hrs". It points a dev to **Settings → Data & recovery → Recently deleted** for the 90-day restore.
- Creating a **new** job still uses the plain New Job form. A job with nothing to read or bill yet does not need tabs. If you have typed anything, Cancel, Escape or clicking outside asks **Discard this job?** first. An untouched form just closes. Escape closes only the window on top. So a New Job opened from a bid never takes the bid window with it.

:::example A billing round-trip
Open the job → **Bill** tab → **Make a bill** → **Bill part of it** → drag the slider to 80% → **Make a bill** → hop to **Job** to confirm the billed bar moved. One window the whole time.
:::

:::example Checking margin before you bill
Open the job → glance at the **Costs** card on the Job tab → tap it → the **Costs** tab shows the Cost Timeline and every bucket → back to **Bill** to write the draw.

Field roles, Sub and Helper, keep the simple read-only Job Detail view they have always had. On a phone that view reads in sections, with the job's name first. It carries its own bar with Arrived or Leaving, Leave report and Note. See [the job window on a phone](/help/job-mode-clocking#the-job-window-on-a-phone).
