The My Bids card on the Dashboard lists the bids where you are the estimator or the account manager. You open the ones you are working on. You hide the ones you do not need to see.

## How the bids are grouped

The card groups your bids by the state they are in. The words are the same ones the Bid Board uses.

- **Unsent** holds the bids still to price and send. This group starts open.
- **Not yet won or lost** holds the bids you sent that have no answer yet.
- **Won** holds the bids the customer said yes to.
- **Started or Complete** holds the bids whose work is under way or done.
- **Hidden** holds the bids you hid.

Each heading shows how many bids it holds. Press a heading to open or fold its group. A lost bid is not on the card.

## What a bid shows

Each bid is one box.

- The name is on top.
- Under the name you see when the bid is due. A sent bid shows when it was sent.
- {{chip:blue|Estimator}} and {{chip:purple|Account Manager}} say what you are on the bid.

The edge of the box has a color. Red means the due date has passed. Red also means a bid has gone 14 days with no due date. Amber means the bid is due within three days.

## Open a bid

Press the bid. The bid's window opens over the Dashboard. The window shows the bid's dates, contact and notes. On a phone the bid's name has the top line with the ✕ beside it. **Copy for text** and **Edit bid** sit on the line under the name.

Under **Open in Bids**, press the tab you want to work in. You land on that tab with the bid picked.

## Hide a bid

On a phone, swipe the bid to the left. A strip under the bid reads **Hide**. Keep going until the strip turns blue, then let go. A shorter swipe springs back and hides nothing.

On a computer, press {{button:outline|Hide}} on the bid.

The bid moves to **Hidden** at the bottom of the card. A note at the bottom of the screen says which bid you hid. Press **Undo** on the note within ten seconds to put the bid back.

Hiding changes nothing on the bid. Nobody else sees a difference.

A hidden bid follows you. Hide it on your phone and it is hidden on the computer too. If a hide cannot be saved, the bid stays hidden on that device and a note says why.

## Show a hidden bid again

Open **Hidden** at the bottom of the card.

On a phone, swipe the bid to the right. A strip under the bid reads **Show again**. Keep going until the strip turns blue, then let go. A shorter swipe springs back and shows nothing.

Start the swipe on the bid, away from the left edge of the screen. A swipe from that edge is your phone's own Back.

On a computer, press {{button:outline|Show again}} on the bid.

The bid goes back to its group. A note at the bottom of the screen says which bid came back. Press **Undo** on the note within ten seconds to hide it again.

A hidden bid comes back by itself when someone writes a note on it. The bid stays in its group until you press {{button:outline-amber|Mark read}}.

## How the card shows you the swipe

On a phone, the first bid on the card moves by itself the first time you scroll down to it. The bid slides to the left, shows **Hide** for a second and slides back. Nothing is hidden. The card is showing you that a bid swipes.

Touch the screen and the bid goes straight back. A press still opens the bid.

The card does this once a day at most and three times at most. It stops for good the first time you swipe a bid yourself. It follows you, so a new phone does not start over.

The first bid in **Hidden** does the same to the right, to show the way back.

If your phone is set to reduce motion, no bid moves. The card writes *Swipe a bid left to hide it.* under the group's heading for that visit.

## If you swipe the wrong way

Left hides a bid. Right shows a hidden bid again. Swipe the other way and nothing happens to the bid. The bid slides a little by itself and shows its strip. The strip is on the side the bid really goes.

## Unread updates

An amber box under a bid means someone else wrote a note on the bid or on its customer. Read the note, then press {{button:outline-amber|Mark read}}.

A folded group still shows any bid that has unread updates. The group's heading says how many.

## See every bid

Press **Bid Board ›** at the bottom of the card. The Bid Board has every bid, with the lost ones too. See [read the bid board](/help/read-the-bid-board).
