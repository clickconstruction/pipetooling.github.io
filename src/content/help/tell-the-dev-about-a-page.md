The wrench sends a note to the dev from the page you are on. Use it when a page confuses you. Use it when something goes wrong. Use it when you want the app to do something new.

## Find the wrench

At a desk the wrench floats at the bottom left of every page. It says **Tell the dev** until you have opened it once.

On a phone the wrench is in the header at the top of the page. Nothing floats on a phone, so no button is covered.

A small phone has no room for the wrench in the header. There it is **Tell the dev**, the first row under the gear at the top right. It opens the same box.

A window on a phone has its own wrench in its title row. Use that one when the window is what confuses you.

## From an error

A red error line has a **Tell the dev** button on it. Press it to send the error with one more press. You do not need to type what went wrong.

An error that only says the signal dropped has no button. See [A lost signal is not a fault](#a-lost-signal-is-not-a-fault).

## Write the note

1. Press the wrench.
2. Pick a start.
3. Finish the sentence. One sentence is plenty.
4. Press {{button:blue|Send to the dev}}.

There are three starts.

- **I want…** is for something new you would like.
- **This page is confusing because…** is for a page you could not follow.
- **Something went wrong when I…** is for a fault.

The last two can be sent with no words. The dev still sees which page you were on.

## What goes with your note

The box lists what it sends under **Goes with your note**. You see the list before you press.

:::example what the box lists
**Page** Jobs · Stages

**Window open** None

**From** Dana Whitfield, Assistant

**Screen** Desk, 1440 wide

**Last error line** None in the last 5 minutes
:::

The note carries the page, the open window, your name and role, and your screen. It carries the app version and the last error line you saw. It never carries a picture of your screen.

## Who reads it

The owner and the dev read every note. Your name is on it, so they can answer you.

## Read the answer

The wrench shows a green dot when a note of yours has an answer. Press the wrench. The answer is under **Your notes**, below the box.

On a small phone the gear shows the dot. Press the gear, then press **Tell the dev**.

Each note there says where it stands. **Sent** means it is waiting to be read. **Answered** means the dev wrote back.

## A lost signal is not a fault

Your phone keeps a press made with no signal. It sends the press when the signal is back. You do not need to tell the dev about that. See [keep working when the signal drops](/help/keep-working-when-the-signal-drops).

## Hide the wrench

Fix Mode shows or hides the wrench. It starts on.

1. Press the gear at the top right.
2. Press **Fix Mode**.

Press it again to bring the wrench back. The choice is kept on this device. On a phone with the dock, press **More** and find Fix Mode under the modes.
