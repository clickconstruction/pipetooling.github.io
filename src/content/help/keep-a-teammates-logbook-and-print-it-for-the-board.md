A teammate's logbook shows the licenses they hold and how close they are to the next one. The logbook also prints the record of hours a licensing board asks for.

## Open a logbook

There are two ways in.

- On **People**, open **Licenses**. Press a teammate's row to open it, then press **Open logbook**.
- On a teammate's desk, find the **Licenses** row and press **Logbook**.

The logbook opens for the same people who can see the Licenses tab.

## Working toward

Each trade a teammate holds a license in gets a card. The card names the next license and how far along they are.

:::example A plumbing card
**Working toward journeyman plumber**
1,381.7 of 8,000 hours. Next mark 1,500 h. 118.3 hours away.
:::

Plumbing and electrical count hours. HVAC counts months. A month counts when it holds 80 or more HVAC hours.

The card also names who signs for the trade. The signer is a current teammate whose supervising license is on the Licenses tab. A teammate who has left never signs.

## How an hour counts

An hour counts toward the trade of the job it was clocked on. The job's service type decides the trade.

Some hours count toward no license:

- Time on estimating.
- Time on the office job.
- Time on a job with no trade.

Those hours stay in the log, so the totals still match payroll. The boards count only trade work.

## Hours that count toward no license

A yellow card appears when a teammate works a trade they hold no license in.

:::example The yellow card
**1,462.4 hours of plumbing work count toward no license.**
:::

The board counts hours worked while the teammate is registered. Press {{button:outline|Add a license}} to put in the registration. The hours count from that day.

## An expired registration

A red line on a card means the license on file shows as expired. Check the board's own record first. Often the license was renewed and only the date on file is old. Fix it with **Edit** on the license, as the next section shows.

## Edit or add a license

Each license under **Licenses held** has an **Edit** button. Press {{button:outline|Add a license}} for a new one.

1. Pick the **Trade** and the **Level**. For a notary, pick **Other** and type the name.
2. Type the **Number** and set **Expires**.
3. Check the line at the bottom. That line shows how the license will be saved.
4. Press {{button:blue|Save the license}}, or {{button:blue|Add the license}} for a new one.

The logbook reads the license again as soon as it is saved.

:::example Checking the state's record
**BLOEMER, PAIGE**, A/C Technician REG. Expires Dec 29, 2026. {{chip:green|Matches}}
:::

For an electrical or HVAC license, press {{button:outline|Check TDLR}}. The window shows the state's record for that number. When the state's date differs, press {{button:outline|Use this date}}. Then save.

Plumbing licenses live with TSBPE. The window links to its search. That search asks you to prove you are not a robot, so a person looks it up.

## Set the goal and the hours before the clock

Each goal card starts from the ladder's rule. For a plumber apprentice that is journeyman plumber, 8,000 hours.

The office can change it. Press {{button:outline|Edit the goal}} on the card. On the Licenses tab, the **Goals** block under a teammate's licenses has the same press.

1. Pick **Working toward**. The list holds the licenses above the one held. The first choice keeps the ladder's rule.
2. Type the **Hours the board asks for**. The seeded number fills in when the board has one.
3. Type the **Hours before the clock started**. These are hours worked before this app, here or at another shop.
4. Say **Where they came from**. Name the shop and where the signed form is.
5. Check the line at the bottom. Then press {{button:blue|Save the goal}}.

:::example The line at the bottom
Saves as working toward journeyman plumber, 7,000 hours, with 1,200 hours before the clock.
:::

The card now says **Includes 1,200 hours before the clock started** and names the source. The bar, Your week and the Monday email count them from then on.

A journeyman electrician's card says the next rule is not seeded yet. Press {{button:outline|Set the goal}} beside it. Pick master electrician and type the board's hours, and the card appears.

HVAC counts months. The same window asks for months instead of hours.

## Show a teammate their logbook

The office can let a teammate see their own logbook. A strip under the logbook's name says whether they can.

:::example The strip
Jordan cannot see this logbook. Show it, and Jordan finds My logbook in the app's menu. {{button:outline|Show Jordan this logbook}}
:::

1. Check the licenses first. A stale date shows the teammate a wrong count.
2. Press {{button:outline|Show Jordan this logbook}}.
3. The strip turns green. It names the day and who pressed.

The teammate now finds **My logbook** in the gear menu. On a phone in Dispatch Mode it sits in the **More** sheet. They see their own hours and licenses and can print the summary sheet. They cannot edit a license or see anyone else's logbook.

Press {{button:outline|Stop showing}} to take it back. On the Licenses tab, a row says **Jordan sees it** beside **Open logbook**.

The guide [see my own logbook](/help/see-my-own-logbook) shows the teammate's side.

## Print for the board

Two buttons sit at the top of the logbook.

- {{button:outline|Print summary sheet}} prints the totals by trade and year. The sheet ends with a signature block for each trade the teammate has hours in.
- {{button:blue|Print full log}} prints every week, every job and its hours.

A window shows the page as it prints. Press {{button:blue|Print the summary sheet}} or {{button:blue|Print the full log}} to send it to the printer.

The summary sheet goes behind the board's own form. For plumbing that is the TSBPE Employer Certification Form. For electrical and HVAC it is the TDLR Experience Verification Form. The board accepts only its own signed original.

For a spreadsheet of the same hours, use the hours log. The guide [export a license hours log](/help/license-hours-log) shows how.
