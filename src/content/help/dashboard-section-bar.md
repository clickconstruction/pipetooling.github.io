On desktop, a floating bar sits at the bottom of the Dashboard and the Quickfill page. It holds one chip per major section.

:::example the section bar
{{chip:gray|Notifications}} {{chip:blue|ClockedIn}} {{chip:gray|My Inbox}} {{chip:gray|Teams Inbox}} {{chip:gray|My Schedule}} {{chip:gray|Bids}} {{chip:gray|Reports}} {{chip:gray|Assigned Jobs}} {{chip:gray|Billing}} {{chip:gray|Projects}} {{chip:gray|Me}}
:::

- The ringed chip tracks where you are. It updates as you scroll.
- Click any chip to jump straight to that section.
- You only see chips for sections your role actually has. Sections that hide when empty drop their chip too.
- On pages with many sections, like Quickfill, the bar scrolls itself. Your current chip stays centered. It stops once the end of the list is in view.

The bar stays out of the way on phones and tablets. {{icon:help|gear}} It's desktop-only, wider than 640px. It never covers a modal.

Want it gone for a while? Click the **>** at the bar's right edge. The bar collapses to a single small button tucked into the bottom-right corner. Click **<** there to bring the bar back. The choice sticks on your device.

## What the chips point at

| Chip | Section |
| --- | --- |
| Notifications | Financial cards and alerts at the top |
| ClockedIn | Team clock activity strip |
| Teams Inbox | The **Teams Inbox** card. Help feedback, Dispatch and Estimator inboxes grouped together |
| My Inbox | My Inbox card. Due Today, Overdue and Recently Completed Tasks |
| My Schedule | My Schedule card. Today and tomorrow blocks |
| Bids | My Bids |
| Reports | Recent Reports |
| Assigned Jobs | Assigned Jobs card. It starts collapsed. Tap the ▶ title to expand. Your choice is remembered |
| Billing | Billing Pipeline card. It sits below Assigned Jobs |
| Projects | The **Projects** card. Assigned Steps and Subscribed Steps grouped together |
| Me | My Time |
