A punch list is what is left to fix on a trade's work near the end of a job. You keep each trade's list in the Punch list window, check each item, and then accept the work.

Each trade we hired with a signed statement of work has its own list. Only devs see the window for now.

## Open the Punch list

1. Press {{button:outline|GC}} on the Bids page to open [GC projects](/gc).
2. Press {{button:outline|Punch list}} on the card of a job we are building.

The same lists also sit under each trade on [Closeout](/help/close-out-a-trade-and-close-a-gc-job).

## Add an item

1. Press {{button:outline|Add an item}} on the trade's list.
2. Type what is left to fix. Add where it is, like a room or a grid line.
3. Paste a photo's Drive link if you have one.
4. Press {{button:blue|Add to the punch list}}.

You can add to a list until we accept the trade's work. After that, anything wrong is under their warranty.

## When the trade says it is fixed

The trade marks an item fixed in their portal once their portal takes it. Until then they tell us by phone or text.

Press {{button:outline|They say it is fixed}} on the item when they do. The item then waits on our check.

## Check it on the job

Walk the item, then press one of these:

- Press {{button:blue|Checked, it is fixed}} when it is done right.
- Press {{button:outline|Not fixed}} when it is not. Type what is still wrong, then press {{button:outline|Send it back}}.

An item sent back is open again. The list counts how many times it went back.

## Take off an item added by mistake

Press {{button:outline|Take it off}} on the item, then press it again to say yes. The item leaves the list but stays in the record.

Only an item nobody has worked on comes off. Once it was marked fixed or sent back, it stays on the list.

## Accept the work

{{button:blue|Accept the work}} on Closeout waits until every item on the trade's list is checked fixed. An item taken off holds nothing up.
