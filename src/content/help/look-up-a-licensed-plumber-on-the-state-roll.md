The Everyone view lists every licensed plumber the app keeps for our counties. Press a name to see that person's sheet with our notes.

The list comes from the state plumbing board's public files. The app reads them every Monday morning. The list has no phone, street address or email. The state does not publish those.

## Who sees it

You see it when you hold the Hiring switch. It sits on the State roll tab of [Prospects](/prospects?tab=roll&roll=everyone).

## Open it

Go to [Prospects](/prospects?tab=roll). Press **State roll** in the top row. Then press **Everyone**.

## Find a person

Type in the box that says **Search a name or a license number**. The list narrows as you type.

You can type a first name, a last name or part of a license number. Two words find people who match both.

The chips narrow the list too.

- **Current** shows licenses that are in force. The list opens on it.
- **Lapsed** shows licenses that have ended. Press **Current** again to see both.
- **Master**, **Journeyman** and **Tradesman** pick the ranks. A responsible master counts as a master.
- **Medical gas** shows people with that endorsement.
- **Has our notes** shows people the office wrote a note on.

The count on a chip is for the whole roll. The line under the chips says how many match.

:::example One row
**Pat Sample** · Journeyman **J-00000**

Since **2014** · Expires **Mar 31, 2027** · **Bexar** {{chip:green|Current}}

{{chip:blue|Medical gas}} {{chip:gray|2 notes}}
:::

The list shows fifty people at a time. Press **Show 50 more** at the bottom to see the next ones.

## Read a person's sheet

Press a row to open the sheet. The top lists what the state says. That is the license, its number, its dates, the county and the endorsements.

**What changed, as we saw it** lists what each Monday read found. That can be a renewal, a lapse or a move to another county. The state keeps no history. So the list starts on the day of our first read.

## Add a note

Press **Add a note** on the sheet. Type what the office knows about the person. Then press **Keep the note**.

Everyone who holds the Hiring switch reads the note. Nobody outside the company sees it. Your name and the time go with it.

A note stays when the person drops off the list and comes back. A note also follows a journeyman who makes master. The sheet marks a note written on their other license.

Press **Delete** to take back a note you wrote. Only you can delete your own note.

## Leave off a person who asks

Use **Leave off** when a person asks not to be listed. Press **Leave off** at the bottom of the sheet.

A window asks first. You can type how they asked. Then press the button that names them, such as **Leave Pat off**.

The person leaves the list at once. No Monday read brings them back. Their line of history goes. Our notes stay.

A license that is one of ours cannot be left off. Our own still checks it against the state.

## Put a person back

A message with **Put back** shows right after you leave a person off. Press it to undo.

Later, go to the bottom of **Everyone**. Press **Show them** under **Left off at their asking**. Press **Put back on the list** beside the person. They come back with the next Monday read.

## What comes next

This view does not say who to call. A list of who to call comes later. To see when the state's files were read, see [the state roll of licensed plumbers](/help/see-the-state-roll-of-licensed-plumbers).
