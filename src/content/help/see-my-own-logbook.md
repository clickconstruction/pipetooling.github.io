Your logbook shows the licenses you hold and how close you are to the next one. It counts your approved hours, week by week.

## Open it

The office turns your logbook on for you. Until then it stays closed.

Once it is on, press the gear at the top of the app. Press {{button:outline|My logbook}}.

On a phone in Dispatch Mode, press **More** on the bottom bar instead. **My logbook** sits above Help.

If the row is not there, ask the office to show you your logbook.

## What it shows

Each trade you hold a license in gets a card. The card names the next license and how far along you are.

:::example A plumbing card
**Working toward journeyman plumber**
1,382 of 8,000 hours. Next mark 1,500 h. 118 hours away.
:::

The card also names who signs your hours for the board.

Hours you worked before the clock started count too, once the office puts them in. The card then says **Includes 1,200 hours before the clock started** and names where they came from.

Below the cards is the log. Each week lists the jobs you worked and the hours on each.

## How an hour counts

An hour counts toward the trade of the job it was clocked on. Only approved hours count. Time on estimating or the office job counts toward no license.

## Something looks wrong

Ask the office. They fix the hours and the licenses, and your logbook follows.

## Print it

Press {{button:outline|Print summary sheet}} for the totals by trade and year. Take it to the board with the board's own signed form.
