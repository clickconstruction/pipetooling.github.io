Most bids live on an ITB portal — PlanHub, BuildingConnected, Procore — and when a project is out to multiple GCs there's often a page on more than one. Every bid now has a spot for those links.

## Adding links

Open the bid's **Edit** tab and find **ITB & submission links** (between People and Money):

1. Click {{button:gray|+ Add ITB link}} for a new row.
2. Paste the portal URL — the row labels itself from the address (*PlanHub*, *BuildingConnected*, or the site's name).
3. Add as many rows as the bid needs; the **×** removes one. Save as usual.

:::example Bidding one project to two GCs
One GC invited you through PlanHub, another through BuildingConnected — add both links. The preview shows two chips, one per portal, so whoever picks up the bid can jump straight to either page.
:::

## Where they show up

On the bid's preview (the **Bid** tab, or the preview from the Bid Board and search), each link appears as a chip in the **Files & links** row next to Drive folder, Plans, Takeoff, and Submission. A bid with no ITB links shows a quiet "ITB — none" chip so you can spot the gap.
