Most bids live on an ITB portal, such as PlanHub, BuildingConnected or Procore. Every bid has a spot for those links.

An ITB is an invitation to bid, and the portal is the website that holds it. A GC is a general contractor. When a project is out to several GCs, there is often a page on more than one portal.

## Adding links

Open the bid's **Edit** tab and find **ITB & submission links**. It sits between People and Money:

1. Click {{button:gray|+ Add ITB link}} for a new row.
2. Paste the portal URL. The row labels itself from the address: *PlanHub*, *BuildingConnected*, or the site's name.
3. Add as many rows as the bid needs. The **×** removes one. On a saved bid the rows save as you go. On a New Bid they go in with {{button:blue|Create bid}}.

:::example Bidding one project to two GCs
One GC invited you through PlanHub, another through BuildingConnected — add both links. The preview shows two chips, one per portal, so whoever picks up the bid can jump straight to either page.
:::

## Where they show up

Open the bid's preview on the **Bid** tab, or from the Bid Board and search. Each link appears as a chip in the **Files & links** row next to Drive folder, Plans, Takeoff, and Submission. A bid with no ITB links shows a quiet *ITB — none* chip so you can spot the gap.
