Every purchase from Mercury, the company bank, arrives with the debit card that made it. One place names those cards and says whose they are.

You open Banking → Sorting → {{button:outline|Nicknames ▾}} → **Debit cards**. That page names the cards, says whose they are, and keeps the purchases flowing to the right person. The Sorting tab loads every transaction in your window. Its "Without person" and "Not split to jobs" counts cover the whole list, not just the first page.

## One row per card

:::example Debit cards (19)
cc3…783 · 👤 Malachi — Nickname **Malachi 6783** · Card is **Person's card** · Person **Malachi** {{button:blue|Link}}
8f9…6e4 · 🏢 company card — Nickname **r-GPS** · Card is **Company card** · Person: *Not a person's card.*
bb2…40f · nobody yet — Nickname **Jonathan 4692** · Card is **Person's card** · Person **— Nobody yet —**
:::

- **Nickname** is what the card is called everywhere in Banking. Save with Enter or the button. Clear removes it.
- **Card is** has two choices. Pick {{chip:blue|Person's card}} for a card someone carries. Pick {{chip:gray|Company card}} for management tools such as GPS trackers, charging and app subscriptions. Company-card purchases are never counted as anyone's fuel. The role is stored with the nickname, so name the card first.
- **Person** is who carries the card. Pick the person and press {{button:blue|Link}}. The person is filled in on the card's past purchases right away. The toast says how many. Every new purchase gets the person as it syncs. Choose *Nobody yet* and Link to remove the link. Purchases already attributed keep their person.

The ***Nobody yet (N)*** filter at the top shows only the person's cards with no person. That is the list worth clearing.

## Why it matters

A purchase's person is what carries a fill-up to People → Vehicles → Wheels. A fill-up on no job then goes to that person's vehicle line on People → Review. Wheels lists the cards whose fuel has nobody on it. Each name is a door straight to its row here.
