Every Mercury purchase arrives with the debit card that made it. One place names those cards, says whose they are, and keeps the purchases flowing to the right person: Banking → Sorting → {{button:outline|Nicknames ▾}} → **Debit cards**.

## One row per card

:::example Debit cards (19)
cc3…783 · 👤 Malachi — Nickname **Malachi 6783** · Card is **Person's card** · Person **Malachi** {{button:blue|Link}}
8f9…6e4 · 🏢 company card — Nickname **r-GPS** · Card is **Company card** · Person: *Not a person's card.*
bb2…40f · nobody yet — Nickname **Jonathan 4692** · Card is **Person's card** · Person **— Nobody yet —**
:::

- **Nickname** — what the card is called everywhere in Banking. Save with Enter or the button; Clear removes it.
- **Card is** — {{chip:blue|Person's card}} for a card someone carries, {{chip:gray|Company card}} for management tools such as GPS trackers, charging and app subscriptions. Company-card purchases are never counted as anyone's fuel. The role is stored with the nickname, so name the card first.
- **Person** — pick who carries the card and press {{button:blue|Link}}. The person is filled in on the card's past purchases right away (the toast says how many) and on every new one as it syncs. Choose *Nobody yet* and Link to remove the link; purchases already attributed keep their person.

The **Nobody yet (N)** filter at the top shows only the person's cards with no person, which is the list worth clearing.

## Why it matters

A purchase's person is what carries a fill-up to People → Vehicles → Wheels and, from there, to that person's line on People → Review. Wheels lists the cards whose fuel has nobody on it; each name is a door straight to its row here.
