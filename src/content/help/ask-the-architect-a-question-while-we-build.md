A question about the plans while we build is an RFI. You record it, send it to the architect and record the answer.

The company we awarded a trade can also ask from its portal. Its question lands here with its name, ready to send on or answer.

Each RFI holds the work it is about. The answer is needed 3 days before that work starts. Only devs see the window for now.

## Open the RFIs

1. Press {{button:outline|GC}} on the Bids page to open [GC projects](/gc).
2. Press {{button:outline|RFIs}} on the card of a job we are building.

The chips count what waits on us, what is with the architect and what is answered. A red chip counts the answers due now.

## Ask a question

1. Press {{button:blue|Ask a question}}.
2. Type the question and the sheets it is about, like A-501, M-101.
3. Under **About**, pick the trade it is about, or our own work.
4. Under **Asked by**, pick our superintendent or the company we awarded that trade.
5. Tick the work it holds. The lines drawn on those sheets are ticked for you.
6. Press {{button:blue|Add the question}}.

Each job numbers its RFIs RFI-001, RFI-002 and on. Change the days before the work when you need the answer sooner.

## Send it to the architect

1. Press {{button:blue|Send to Marsh & Vale Architects}}. The button names the job's architect.
2. The email carries the question, its sheets, the work it holds and the day we need the answer. Replies come to the project manager.

Press {{button:outline|We sent it another way}} when it went by phone or in a meeting.

## Record the answer

1. Press {{button:outline|Record their answer}}. For a question we answer ourselves, press {{button:outline|Answer it ourselves}}.
2. Type the answer.
3. Pick what it changes. Press {{button:outline|No change}}, {{button:outline|Changes the plans}} or {{button:outline|Adds cost or days}}.
4. For a cost, type our cost and the days it adds.
5. Press {{button:blue|Save the answer}}.

Once the RFI is answered, the work it held can start.

## Start a change order

An answer that adds cost can start a change order for the customer. Only the money team sees the button.

1. Press {{button:blue|Start a change order}} on the answered RFI.
2. The draft takes the answer's words and our cost plus the job's fee.
3. The Change orders window opens on the job. [Send it to the customer](/help/change-our-contract-with-the-customer#send-it-to-the-customer) from there.

An answer that adds days only starts no change order. Ask for the days on the schedule instead.
