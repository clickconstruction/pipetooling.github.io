A tech's bio is the short resume a customer gets with the tech's first On my way text. You write it on the person's desk. The tech reads it in Settings and can turn a line off.

## Open the bio

1. Open **People**, then **Users**.
2. Find the tech. Press **Open desk**.
3. Scroll to the bio section. Its title is the tech's first name and the word bio.

## Write the lines

1. Press {{button:outline|+ Add a line}}.
2. Press a prompt, or write your own sentence.
3. Write the same line in Spanish when you can.
4. Press {{button:blue|Add the line}}.

Write each line the way a customer reads it. Use the tech's name, or he or she. The app opens the bio with the words **A little about** and the tech's first name.

:::example Luis's bio as a customer gets it
A little about Luis: he has been a plumber for twelve years and holds a Texas journeyman license. Water heaters and repipes are his specialty. Off the clock he coaches Little League in Seguin.
:::

The six prompts are a resume's shape. Years in the trade. License. Best at. Family. Off the clock. Hometown. Each one starts the sentence for you.

## Keep it short

The whole bio holds 320 characters. That is two text messages. The count under the box says what is left. A line that goes over is refused.

## Change or remove a line

1. Press **Edit** on the line.
2. Change the words or the Spanish.
3. Press {{button:blue|Save}}.

Press **Remove** to take a line out. The app asks first.

## Spanish

A customer who reads Spanish gets the Spanish lines only. A line with no Spanish shows **No Spanish yet**. Add the Spanish when you can.

## When the tech turns a line off

A tech can turn off any line in Settings. The line shows crossed out with the tech's name and **turned this off**. It still counts toward the 320. Change the words or remove the line if they ask.

## When the bio goes

The bio goes as a second text the first time a tech presses On my way to a customer. It never goes to that customer again. A different tech gets their own first time. See [clock in and out with Job Mode](/help/job-mode-clocking).
