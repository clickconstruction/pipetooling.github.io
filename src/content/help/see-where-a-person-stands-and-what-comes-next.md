Every trade partner, customer and architect has a flow. The flow lists the steps we take with them, from the first ask to the end of a job. The app moves each person along it from facts it already keeps.

Nobody marks a step done by hand. A signed paper, a quote that came in, or a paid draw finishes a step on its own.

## Open someone's flow

1. Open the company's window or the customer's window.
2. Press the **Flow** tab.

The flow is in lanes. **Once** holds the steps a person does one time, like their W-9. **Per job** holds the steps for one job. Pick the job in the **Which job** box at the top of **Per job**.

## Read the steps

:::example A trade partner's flow
{{chip:green|✓ Invited}} done

{{chip:red|! Papers in}} lapsed, insurance expired

{{chip:yellow|↻ Asked to quote}} on now, their move, late 2 days
:::

A green step is done. A yellow step is on now. A red step was done and then ran out, like insurance past its last day. A step marked ↻ repeats, once for each ask, draw or pay application.

Under each step you see its papers and its day. A check means the paper is on file. An arrow means it went and we wait on them. A step on now also says whose move it is. While we owe them something, it is our move.

## Open a step's card

Press a step. Its card opens beside the flow. On a phone, the card slides up from the bottom.

The card says where the step stands, what we need, and what comes next. Its buttons act on that one step:

- {{button:outline|+ a note}} writes a note for the team on the step. The note also shows in their thread.
- {{button:outline|Skip for Tom}} skips the step for this person. Say why in the box that opens.
- {{button:outline|Put it back}} puts a skipped step back on their flow.
- {{button:outline|Hold until Mon}} keeps the step's reminder quiet until Monday.
- {{button:outline|Remind now}} writes down that you reminded them. Their **Activity** tab opens, so you can log the call.

A paper's button opens the window's **Documents** tab. The paper is sent from there, as always.

## See the flow beside their thread

Their flow also sits at the right side of their thread in [Messages](/help/find-every-message-with-a-person-in-one-place). It starts as a narrow rail with one mark for each step.

- Press a mark to open that step's card.
- Press {{button:outline|‹}} at the top of the rail to open the whole flow.
- Press {{button:outline|fold ›}} to shrink the flow back to the rail.
- Press {{button:outline|‹}} under the × at the top right to hide the side. Press {{button:outline|›}} there to show it again.

A supply house or a candidate has no flow. Their side starts hidden. Open it and it says so.

On a phone, the rail is a row above the messages, so the messages keep the whole width. Swipe the row sideways to see every step. Press {{button:outline|›}} at its end to open the whole flow. A step's card opens from the bottom and stays above the keyboard while you type a note.

## See their page

Press **See their page** under the flow. Their portal opens in a new tab, as they see it. Your visit does not count as theirs.

## Take someone off the flow

Press **Take off the flow** under the flow, and say why. No reminder goes to them while they are off. To bring them back, open any step's card and press the button that puts them back on.
