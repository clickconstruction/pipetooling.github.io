When a certified bill on a GC job is past the day it was due, send the customer a reminder. The reminder asks them to pay by a day you pick.

A reminder is our ask, not their promise. The day the bill was due stays the same. The owner, the leaders and the controller see the window.

## Send a reminder

1. Press {{button:outline|GC}} on the Bids page to open [GC projects](/gc). Press {{button:outline|Bill the customer}} on the job's card.
2. Find the late bill under **Sent**.
3. Press {{button:outline|Remind them to pay}}.
4. Pick the **Pay by** day. The day starts five days out.
5. Type a line of your own in **Your line in it** if you want one.
6. Read the email as the customer will get it.
7. Press {{button:blue|Send the reminder}}.

The email comes from Click Construction. The email says what is still open and the day to pay by. The email asks them to reply with the day they will pay. A customer with a portal link gets the link too. The project manager gets their replies.

## After it goes

The bill says when you reminded them and the pay-by day. A note goes on the customer in the Payment Chase queue too.

When the email does not go, the bill says so. The window says why. Fix that, then remind them again.

{{button:outline|Remind them to pay}} shows only on a bill that is certified, open and past the day it was due. When the customer pays, [record what they paid](/help/record-what-a-gc-customer-paid).
