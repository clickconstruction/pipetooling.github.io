A GC customer's window shows what they owe us across all their jobs. It also lists each job's bills and change orders.

The money team sees this part of the window. Today the customer's window opens for a dev.

## Open the customer's window

Press the customer's name on the board. Their window opens on About.

## Read what they owe

The top of About shows their totals.

- **Priced, waiting on them** is our price on the jobs we still bid to them.
- **Under contract** is the price on the jobs they gave us.
- **They owe us now** is what our sent bills ask that they have not paid. The line under it says how much the architect certified.
- **They are holding** is the retainage they keep until the end.
- **Interest on late bills** shows on a job that charges interest. It says what was paid and what built up.
- **They picked us** counts the jobs they gave us out of the ones they decided.

Each job reads its own retainage and days to pay. So each job's numbers match Bill the customer.

## See each job's bills

1. Press **Documents**.
2. Each job lists our contract first. Its pay applications, late bills, interest bills and change orders come after.
3. Press {{button:outline|Open the bill}} on a bill to open Bill the customer. Remind them about a late bill from there.
4. Press {{button:outline|Open the change orders}} to see that job's change orders.

Press {{button:outline|Open the contract}} on our contract to open the file we sent. Only a dev opens the file today.
