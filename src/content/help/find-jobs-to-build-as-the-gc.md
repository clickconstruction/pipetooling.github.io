**Prospects → Jobs to GC** is the calling deck for owners, developers and architects. Each has a job we could build as the GC. You call them, log how it ended, and press {{button:purple|Asked us ✓}} when they ask us to bid.

The deck works like the customer deck. The queue, the timer, callbacks and the Activity report are the same. Only the facts on the card and the last press change.

## Add a lead

Press {{button:blue|New Prospect}} and pick **A job to GC** at the top. Type the company, the contact and the phone. Then say who they are:

- **The owner**, **A developer**, **An architect**, **An owner's rep** or **Another general contractor**.
- **Where we found them**: PlanHub, a permit, a referral, the architect, or anything you type.

The card shows who they are and the source under the name. Under that sits the **Jobs** box.

## The jobs

A lead can have several jobs over the years. Each one is a row in the Jobs box on the card. Press **+ Add a job**. Type the job, the address and the size. Then say when bids are due and where the plans are. The row shows the bid day and how many days are left.

- **Edit** turns the row into the form, filled in. Change the bid day or the size and press **Save changes**.
- **Lost** marks a job another builder got. It asks who won.
- **Drop** marks a job that is not happening. **Reopen** brings either back.
- **Remove** takes a lost or dropped job off the card.

The queue has a third order on Jobs to GC, **Soonest bid**. It puts the lead whose job bids soonest first, then never called, then coldest.

:::example A developer from PlanHub
You type Hill Country Developers, A developer, PlanHub. Then a job: retail shell on FM 1431, 12,000 sq ft, bids due Nov 3. The row reads "Nov 3 · bids due · 19 days".
:::

The strip over the deck, **GC work in flight**, counts the Project Board: Bidding, Buying out, Building and Lost.

## Make the call

The card at the top is who you are calling. Press the {{button:blue|📞 phone}} button to dial. Then log how it ended:

- {{button:amber|Didn't Answer}} or {{button:green|Answered}} logs the call with your note attached.
- {{button:outline|Can't reach}} drops a dead number from the queue.
- {{button:outline|Not a fit}} is for a job we would not build.
- {{button:purple|Asked us ✓}} is for an owner who asked us to bid.

## Asked us

{{button:purple|Asked us ✓}} opens **Add customer**, filled from the card and already linked. Press {{button:blue|Save}} to make the customer. Then **New project** opens on GC projects with the job filled in. It carries the name, the address, the size, who we work for and the bid day. Read [start a GC project](/help/start-a-gc-project) for the rest of that window.

The job with the soonest bid day is the one handed on, and it reads **asked us** on the card. The prospect leaves the queue and sits under **Asked us** on the Prospect List. A lead with another open job stays in the queue for it.

## A lead who is already a customer

New Prospect checks the name and the phone against this lane, the other lanes, the bench and the customers. One line says where the company already lives. For a customer, press **Link this lead to them**. Then Asked us ✓ uses that record and makes no second one. For a company already on a lane, press **Open it** to go to its card. Press {{button:blue|Save anyway}} only for a second branch of the same company.

## A lost bid comes back

On GC projects, a lost project's card has **Send the owner back to Prospects**. It makes a lead here with the job marked lost and who won in the note. The next call is on the deck.

## Keep calling

Edit who they are and the source with {{button:outline|Edit prospect}}. Keep the bid days current in the Jobs box. Read [work the prospect calling queue](/help/work-the-prospect-calling-queue) for the queue, callbacks and the timer.
