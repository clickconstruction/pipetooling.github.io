If you work with the company as a partner, your Dashboard has a **Your ledger** card — your running balance and everything behind it, week by week.

## Reading the card

The card opens on the **current week**: your balance so far, with the hours that have been approved priced at your deal rates. Hours still waiting on office approval show as a line with no dollars yet — they post when approved.

Use the **‹ ›** arrows to step back through past weeks. Each closed week shows:

1. Labor lines — your hours at the rate they were priced that week (rate changes never reach back into old weeks).
2. Anything added — profit shares from your jobs, credits.
3. Anything deducted — back-charges, utility overage.
4. **Paid out** lines — money you were actually paid.
5. The week's **opening and closing balance** — every closing balance opens the next week, so the chain is the ledger.

:::example A typical closed week
Field labor 22.5 h × $50, estimating 18.0 h × $35, a profit share from a finished job, one back-charge, and Friday's payout — ending at the week's closing balance.
:::

## Acknowledging a statement

When the office closes a week, the statement waits for your sign-off. The current week's card reminds you when last week is unacknowledged — step one **‹** back and tap {{button:blue|Acknowledge statement}}. That timestamps your side of the record; the office sees it immediately. Use {{button:outline|Print / save PDF}} for a paper copy of any closed week.

## What feeds the numbers

Only **approved** hours post. Jobs appear in your profit shares only after the office confirms you did the majority of the work on them. If a week looks short, check whether hours are still pending approval before anything else.
