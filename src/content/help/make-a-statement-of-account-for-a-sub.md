A statement of account is one paper for one sub. It lists their work, what was paid and what is still open. You make it as a PDF and hand it to them.

A work statement is the same kind of paper for one sheet. Both read the sheets and payments the Pay view already shows.

## Open the papers window

The press sits where the sub's row already is.

- On [Jobs → Subs → Pay](/jobs?tab=subs&view=pay), press {{button:outline|Statement of account…}} beside the person's name.
- On [People → Subs](/people?tab=subs), open what we owe a person. Then press {{button:outline|Statement of account…}} at the top of the block.
- For one sheet, press **⋯** on its row in the Pay view. Then press **Work statement…**.
- On People → Subs, open a sheet in the block. Then press {{button:outline|Work statement…}} under it.

The window opens on the paper you asked for. The chips at the top switch to another paper for the same person.

**Work statement…** takes the place of **Print** on a sheet that is one person's. A sheet two people share keeps **Print**, and so does a crew sheet.

## Pick the period

A statement of account covers a period. Four chips set it.

- {{chip:gray|To date}} covers everything up to today. The window opens on this chip.
- {{chip:gray|This month}} starts on the first of the month and ends today.
- {{chip:gray|This year}} starts on January 1 and ends today.
- {{chip:gray|Pick dates}} shows two date boxes.

**What it will show** counts the work statements, the payments and the back-charges in the period. The last figure is what is still open.

A work statement has no period. It always shows the whole sheet.

## Read the paper

The preview under the chips is the paper itself. The PDF shows the same words and the same figures.

A statement of account has four parts.

- **Still open** is boxed at the top. It is what the sub has not been paid yet on the closing day.
- **Brought forward** lists work from before the period that was still open. **Work in this period** lists the new work.
- **Last payments** lists each payment in the period with its day and how it was sent.
- **How it adds up** is one line. It starts with what was open and ends on what is still open.

A work statement shows one sheet. It lists each line and what was agreed, then each back-charge in red. It lists each payment, then **Paid so far** and **Still open on this statement**.

A part payment reads the same on both papers. The sheet's row shows what was paid and what is left.

A memo the office hid from the sub is left off. The amounts always show.

## Print it or save it

Press {{button:outline|Print}} to open the PDF in a new tab, ready to print. Press {{button:outline|Download the PDF}} to save the file. The line under the buttons shows the file's name.

Each print and each download files a copy. You find it on [Documents → Sent copies](/documents?tab=sent), under **Statements and reports**. The copy is filed on the sub and on each job.

This window does not send the paper. Give the sub the printed page, or attach the file to your own message.

## What the papers do not have yet

- **No number.** Each paper says the day and time it was prepared. Numbers come in a later change.
- **Draft sentences.** A few sentences are boxed and marked §. They are draft wording until our lawyer has read them.
- **No payment record.** A paper for one payment comes in a later change.

## When the papers do not add up

The window checks the papers against each other before it draws one. The work statements must leave open what the statement of account says.

A set that does not agree is not drawn. A red box lists what is off, and **Print** and **Download the PDF** go grey.

Open the sheet the box names on [Jobs → Subs → Pay](/jobs?tab=subs&view=pay) and check its payments. Then open the papers again.
