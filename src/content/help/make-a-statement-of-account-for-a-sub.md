A statement of account is one paper for one sub. The paper lists their work, what was paid and what is still open. You make it as a PDF and hand it to them.

A work statement is the same kind of paper for one sheet. A payment record is the paper for one payment. All three read the sheets and payments the Pay view already shows.

## Open the papers window

The press sits where the sub's row already is.

- On [Jobs → Subs → Pay](/jobs?tab=subs&view=pay), press {{button:outline|Statement of account…}} beside the person's name.
- On [People → Subs](/people?tab=subs), open what we owe a person. Then press {{button:outline|Statement of account…}} at the top of the block.
- For one sheet, press **⋯** on its row in the Pay view. Then press **Work statement…**.
- On People → Subs, open a sheet in the block. Then press {{button:outline|Work statement…}} under it.
- For one payment, open the sheet's row in the Pay view. Then press **Payment record…** beside the payment.
- Right after you record a payment, press {{button:blue|Payment record…}} in the window that follows.

The window opens on the paper you asked for. The chips at the top switch to another paper for the same person.

**Work statement…** takes the place of **Print** on a sheet that is one person's. A sheet two people share keeps **Print**, and so does a crew sheet.

## Pick the period

A statement of account covers a period. Five chips set it.

- {{chip:gray|Since the last statement}} starts the day after the last statement ended. The window opens on this chip once a statement has been made for the sub.
- {{chip:gray|To date}} covers everything up to today. The first statement for a sub opens on this chip.
- {{chip:gray|This month}} starts on the first of the month and ends today.
- {{chip:gray|This year}} starts on January 1 and ends today.
- {{chip:gray|Pick dates}} shows two date boxes.

**What it will show** counts the work statements, the payments and the back-charges in the period. The last figure is what is still open.

A work statement has no period. A work statement always shows the whole sheet.

## Read the paper

The preview under the chips is the paper itself. The PDF shows the same words and the same figures.

A statement of account has four parts.

- **Still open** is boxed at the top. The figure is what the sub has not been paid yet on the closing day.
- **Brought forward** lists work from before the period that was still open. **Work in this period** lists the new work.
- **Last payments** lists each payment in the period with its day and how it was sent.
- **How it adds up** is one line. The line starts with what was open and ends on what is still open.

A work statement shows one sheet. The paper lists each line and what was agreed, then each back-charge in red. Under those come each payment, then **Paid so far** and **Still open on this statement**.

A payment record shows one payment. It can be one check that paid several sheets. The top says who was paid, the day, how it was sent and the amount.

- **Applied to** has a row for each sheet the payment went to. Each row shows what was open before, this payment and what is open after.
- A row reads **Part payment** or **Paid in full**.
- **After this payment** lists every sheet still open and the total.
- The office's note to the sub prints under it. A hidden memo is left off.

Money paid from a sub's credit has no payment record. No money left the bank for it.

A part payment reads the same on all three papers. The sheet's row shows what was paid and what is left.

A memo the office hid from the sub is left off. The amounts always show.

## Print it or save it

Press {{button:outline|Print}} to open the PDF in a new tab, ready to print. Press {{button:outline|Download the PDF}} to save the file. The file is named for the paper's number.

Each print and each download files a copy. You find it on [Documents → Sent copies](/documents?tab=sent), under **Statements and reports**. The copy is filed on the sub and on each job.

This window does not send the paper. Give the sub the printed page, or attach the file to your own message.

## How the papers are numbered

A paper takes its number when you make it. Printing and downloading both count as making it. The preview shows no number until then.

- **A work statement** is numbered for its job, like *WS-2041-1*. The sheet keeps that number for good.
- **A statement of account** takes the next number each time, like *SA-0007*. Every one you make is a new paper.
- **A payment record** carries its payment's own number, like *PR-0231*. A payment recorded before those numbers reads *Not numbered*.

No number is ever given out twice.

**A reprint keeps the number.** Print the same work statement again and nothing changes.

**A change makes a revision.** A back-charge or a payment after the first copy makes revision 2 under the same number. The foot of the new copy says which copy it replaces. The copy that went out before stays under Sent copies.

Sometimes a paper cannot be numbered. Then it is not made, and a red line says why. An account in training cannot make a paper.

## What the papers do not have yet

- **Draft sentences.** A few sentences are boxed and marked §. The boxed sentences are draft wording until our lawyer has read them.
- **Sending.** The window does not send a paper yet. That comes in a later change.

## When the papers do not add up

The window checks the papers against each other before it draws one. The work statements must leave open what the statement of account says.

A set that does not agree is not drawn. A red box lists what is off, and **Print** and **Download the PDF** go grey.

Open the sheet the box names on [Jobs → Subs → Pay](/jobs?tab=subs&view=pay) and check its payments. Then open the papers again.
