A statement of account is one paper for one sub. The paper lists their work, what was paid and what is still open. You make it as a PDF, then hand it to them or email it.

A work statement is the same kind of paper for one sheet. A payment record is the paper for one payment. All three read the sheets and payments the Pay view already shows.

## Open the papers window

The press sits where the sub's row already is.

- On [Jobs → Subs → Pay](/jobs?tab=subs&view=pay), press {{button:outline|Statement of account…}} beside the person's name.
- On [People → Subs](/people?tab=subs), open what we owe a person. Then press {{button:outline|Statement of account…}} at the top of the block.
- For one sheet, press **⋯** on its row in the Pay view. Then press **Work statement…**.
- On People → Subs, open a sheet in the block. Then press {{button:outline|Work statement…}} under it.
- For one payment, open the sheet's row in the Pay view. Then press **Payment record…** beside the payment.
- Right after you record a payment, press {{button:blue|Send the payment record…}} in the window that follows.
- On a phone, press a sheet's row in the Pay view. Then press **Work statement…** or **Statement of account…**.

The window opens on the paper you asked for. The chips at the top switch to another paper for the same person. On a phone, the chips are how you reach a payment record.

**Work statement…** takes the place of **Print** on a sheet that is one person's. A sheet two people share keeps **Print**, and so does a crew sheet.

The paper presses are for a dev, a master technician, an assistant and a controller. A superintendent sees **Print** on every sheet.

## Pick the period

A statement of account covers a period. Five chips set it.

- {{chip:gray|Since the last statement}} starts the day after the last statement ended. The window opens on this chip once a statement has been made for the sub. A statement made today does not move this period. It still starts after the last statement from before today.
- {{chip:gray|To date}} covers everything up to today. The first statement for a sub opens on this chip.
- {{chip:gray|This month}} starts on the first of the month and ends today.
- {{chip:gray|This year}} starts on January 1 and ends today.
- {{chip:gray|Pick dates}} shows two date boxes.

**What it will show** counts the work statements, the payments and the back-charges in the period. The last figure is what is still open.

### A payment typed late

A period is cut by the date on the payment. Sometimes a payment is typed after a statement was made, with a date inside that statement.

{{chip:gray|Since the last statement}} carries that payment into the new statement. The payment is listed under its own date, with the words **recorded after the last statement**. A line under **How it adds up** says so too.

The new statement then opens on the figure the last one closed on. A back-charge or a sheet typed late is carried the same way.

The other four chips do not carry. They list a payment only when its date is in the period.

A work statement has no period. A work statement always shows the whole sheet.

## Read the paper

The preview under the chips is the paper itself. The PDF shows the same words and the same figures.

A statement of account has four parts.

- **Still open** is boxed at the top. The figure is what the sub has not been paid yet on the closing day.
- **Brought forward** lists work from before the period that was still open. **Work in this period** lists the new work.
- **Last payments** lists each payment in the period with its day and how it was sent.
- **How it adds up** is one line. The line starts with what was open and ends on what is still open.

A work statement shows one sheet. The paper lists each line and what was agreed, then each back-charge in red. Under those come each payment, then **Paid so far** and **Still open on this statement**.

A payment record shows one payment. It can be one check that paid several sheets. The top says who was paid, the day, how it was sent and the amount.

- **Applied to** has a row for each sheet the payment went to. Each row shows what was open before, this payment and what is open after.
- A row reads **Part payment** or **Paid in full**.
- **After this payment** lists every sheet still open and the total.
- The office's note to the sub prints under it. A hidden memo is left off.
- **Received** says whether the sub pressed **Got it** on their page, and on which day. A payment nobody marked reads *Not marked yet*.

Money paid from a sub's credit has no payment record. No money left the bank for it.

A part payment reads the same on all three papers. The sheet's row shows what was paid and what is left.

A memo the office hid from the sub is left off. The amounts always show.

## Print it or save it

Press {{button:outline|Print}} to open the PDF in a new tab, ready to print. Press {{button:outline|Download the PDF}} to save the file. The file is named for the paper's number.

After a download, a green line names the file. The line goes when you pick another paper or open the send step.

Each print and each download files a copy. You find it on [Documents → Sent copies](/documents?tab=sent), under **Statements and reports**. The copy is filed on the sub and on each job.

## Send it by email

The blue press at the bottom of the window reads **Send to** and the sub's name. Press it, and the window turns to the send step.

The send step shows everything before anything goes.

- **To** is the sub's name and the email on their roster row. The paper goes to that address and no other.
- **They will get** is the message as the sub reads it, with each file under it.
- **When you press Send** lists what the press does.

Then press the blue press under them. The press reads {{button:blue|Send 3 papers by email}} when three files go. The count is always in front of you.

1. Each paper takes its number.
2. One email goes to the sub with every PDF attached.
3. A copy of each paper is filed on the sub and on each job.

A sheet's first work statement takes its number on this press. The subject of the email names that number, and so does the file.

A green line says how many papers went, and to which address. The press turns into {{button:blue|Done}}. Press {{button:outline|Back to the paper}} to print the paper as well. The green line goes when you leave the send step.

The copies are on [Documents → Sent copies](/documents?tab=sent). Each paper has a copy of its own, with the message the sub got.

### Send a payment record right after a payment

Press {{button:blue|Send the payment record…}} in the window that follows [Record payment](/help/pay-a-sub-across-sheets-or-keep-a-credit). The papers window opens on the send step, with that payment's record ready to go.

### Attach more papers to a statement of account

A statement of account can carry other papers in the same email. Two boxes under **Attach** set them, before you press **Send to**.

- **Each open work statement** is ticked to begin with. Each sheet that is still open rides along as its own PDF.
- **Payment records in the period** adds the record of each payment the statement lists.

The statement names each work statement by its number, so the sub can match the files.

One email carries 12 papers. Past 12, the oldest ride first. A yellow line names the papers left for the next email.

Once the first email went, press {{button:blue|Open the next email, 3 papers}} to send the papers that were left. The press says how many papers that email carries. The next email holds only those papers, so the statement is not sent twice.

The same press sits in the yellow line before you send. There it opens the next email and sends nothing.

### When the sub has no email

A sub with no email on their roster row cannot be sent a paper. The press goes grey, and a yellow line says so.

Add their email right there. On the **To** line, press the underlined words **no email on the roster**. Type their email, then press {{button:blue|Keep}}. The address is saved on their roster row, and the send press comes back.

Some subs sign in with an email. A line under **To** then shows that address. The press beside it puts the address on their roster row.

A wrong address is put right the same way. Press the address on the **To** line and type the right one.

You can also press {{button:outline|Back to the paper}}, then print or download the paper for them.

### Who can send

A dev, a master technician, an assistant or a controller can send a paper. An account in training cannot send.

### When the email does not go

A red line says why, and nothing is filed. Press the send press again once the reason is fixed.

A statement of account takes a new number each time you make it. One that was numbered for a failed send keeps that number, and the red line names it.

## How the papers are numbered

A paper takes its number when you make it. Printing, downloading and sending each count as making it. The preview shows no number until then.

- **A work statement** is numbered for its job, like *WS-2041-1*. The sheet keeps that number for good.
- **A statement of account** takes the next number each time, like *SA-0007*. Every one you make is a new paper.
- **A payment record** carries its payment's own number, like *PR-0231*. A payment recorded before those numbers reads *Not numbered*.

No number is ever given out twice.

**A reprint keeps the number.** Print the same work statement again and nothing changes.

**A change makes a revision.** A back-charge or a payment after the first copy makes revision 2 under the same number. The foot of the new copy says which copy it replaces. The copy that went out before stays under Sent copies.

Sometimes a paper cannot be numbered. A paper with no number is not made, and a red line says why. An account in training cannot make a paper.

## What the papers do not have yet

- **Draft sentences.** A few sentences are boxed and marked §. The boxed sentences are draft wording until our lawyer has read them.
- **A texted link.** The window sends by email only. A text with a link to the paper is not built yet.

## When the papers do not add up

The window checks the papers against each other before it draws one. The work statements must leave open what the statement of account says.

A set that does not agree is not drawn. A red box lists what is off, and **Print** and **Download the PDF** go grey.

Open the sheet the box names on [Jobs → Subs → Pay](/jobs?tab=subs&view=pay) and check its payments. Then open the papers again.
