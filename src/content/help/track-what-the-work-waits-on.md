Some work waits on things outside the trades. A delivery, the customer's decision, a permit and the utility's work can each hold it. Put them on the schedule, and the chart holds the work until they are in.

The office and estimators can open the schedule. Superintendents get it later.

## Add what the work waits on

1. Open [GC projects](/gc).
2. Press {{button:outline|Schedule}} on the project's card.
3. On the **What the work waits on** card, press **Add one**.
4. Pick what kind it is, like **A delivery** or **A permit**.
5. Name it, and pick whose work it is for.
6. Type who you wait on. Left blank, the usual one is kept.
7. Pick the day it is expected.
8. Tick the work that cannot start without it.
9. Press {{button:blue|Put it on the schedule}}.

## Keep it up to date

The card shows where each one stands and its next step. Press the next step's button on the day it happens, like **Shipped today**. Press **A new expected day** when the day changes. Say who gave the new day, then press **Save the day**.

## Take it off

Press **Take it off** when it no longer holds the work.
