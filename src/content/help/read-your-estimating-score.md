The Bid Board shows your own estimating score. It counts the bids you estimated or manage. The owner reads everyone's on the Scores tab.

## Find your row

Open **Bids** and press **Bid Board**. Your row sits above the bids. It shows only when you have a bid in the last 8 weeks.

:::example Your row before a bid has scored
Your score **2 of 5** of your priced bids can be scored · 3 need their Labor tab finished
:::

:::example Your row once a bid has scored
Your score **$42,300** won above the hurdle on 2 bids · 1 job closed and moved it by +$2,300
:::

## What counts

A bid counts when it is awarded. It counts its priced profit, the price less the cost on the Pricing tab. It counts only when that profit on each labor hour clears the hurdle. A bid awarded under the hurdle counts zero.

When the job is billed, the real profit replaces the estimate. Your number moves by the difference on that day.

## Open the sheet

Press your row. The sheet opens with the hurdle as one figure. Under it are your bids, grouped by what happened to them.

:::cards

:::card Finish these

Priced bids that cannot be scored yet. Each says why. Press {{button:outline|Open Labor}} to fill the hours.

:::

:::card Won above the hurdle

Awarded bids that count, with the profit and the dollars an hour.

:::

:::card Closed

Bids whose job was billed, with what the job moved.

:::

:::card Out

Bids sent and waiting on an answer, with their days out.

:::

:::

## What a closed job did

When a job is billed, its bid's row on the Bid Board carries a chip. {{chip:green|Closed +$2,300}} means the job made that much more than the bid was priced to make. {{chip:yellow|Closed −$1,100}} means it made less. Hover the chip to read both numbers. The chip shows for 8 weeks after the job closes.

## Why a bid cannot be scored

A bid needs hours on the Labor tab and a cost on every priced row. See [read the team scores and set the hurdle](/help/read-the-team-scores#a-bid-says-so-before-it-goes-out).
