The Lien desk can tell someone where our liens stand in two taps. The message says who we are about to lien, for how much, and by when. Send it as a text, or email it to someone on the team.

## Open Share

Open the [Lien desk](/jobs?tab=stages&liendesk=1). Press {{button:outline|Share}} at the end of its title line. It sits in the same place on every tab. On a phone or a narrow screen, it shows only its square with an arrow.

## Pick what to send

**What to send** starts on **Everything on the desk**. Open it to pick one GC instead. Each GC in the menu shows its jobs and the money owed on them.

The message counts every notice that has not gone in the mail. It names what waits for approval, then each GC with its jobs and its money. It ends with what is left to do and any liens to file.

:::example the top of the message for the whole desk
Liens, Thu Oct 1, 2:14 PM
We are about to send lien notices on 23 jobs.
$173,597 is owed on them.
The first must be mailed by Oct 15, in 14 days.
:::

For one GC, the message lists each job with its months, its money and where it stands. Jobs with no property kind set get one line that counts them. Those jobs' dates could come a month sooner.

The menu also offers **Jobs where a supply house is also owed** when there are any. That message lists each job that still owes a supply house. Each line says what is owed to us and what is owed to the houses. It names the house whose own notice comes first. Read more in [see which supply houses are owed on a lien job](/help/see-which-supply-houses-are-owed-on-a-lien-job).

## Send it as a text

Press {{button:blue|Send…}} and the share sheet opens. Pick Messages, Mail or any app, then pick the person. The message goes from your own phone or your own inbox.

A computer with no share sheet shows {{button:blue|Copy the text}} instead. It copies the message. Paste it into a text, an email or a chat. Where the share sheet works, {{button:outline|Copy}} sits beside it.

The link at the end opens the Lien desk. The person signs in first, as themselves. The link carries no names and no money.

## Email it to a teammate

Press {{button:outline|Email a teammate…}} and tap the people to send it to. Only people who use the Lien desk are on the list. When a notice waits for approval, the leader starts picked.

Type a line under **A note on top** if you like. The subject is filled in, and you can change it. The email shows below, the way it will arrive. Press **Preview the whole email** to see all of it.

Press {{button:outline|Email me a test}} to send one to yourself first. Then press the blue button. It names who gets the email, like {{button:blue|Send to Malachi}}.

The email comes from ClickTooling, and replies come to you. A leader reads **Waiting for your approval** over the notices that wait on him. Mail to anyone outside the company goes from your own inbox. Use **Send…** for that.

## Counsel

Counsel already sees every lien on the firm's portal, and it is always up to date. When the firm's link exists, the panel shows **Copy the firm's link**. Send them that link rather than a message that will go out of date.

## Good to know

- The message is a snapshot. It says the time the numbers were read.
- Close Share and open it again to read the numbers fresh.
- One email goes to five people at most.
- A training account can copy the message but cannot send the email.
- The whole flow of the desk is in [send lien notices from the Lien desk](/help/send-lien-notices-from-the-lien-desk).
