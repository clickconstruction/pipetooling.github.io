Each person who answers the company number has a seat in Quo. Match each seat to its person here, so texts and calls carry their name.

The office team can read the seats. Only the owner or a dev can change them.

## Open the seats

1. Go to [Settings](/settings?tab=settings-phone-texts).
2. Press **Phone and texts**.
3. **Company phone seats** is under Who gets a new message.

## Add a seat

1. Press **Add a seat**.
2. Type the seat's Quo user id. Quo's user ids start with US.
3. Pick who answers on it.
4. Press {{button:blue|Save the seats}}.

Each seat is one Quo user id. Each person has one seat.

## What a seat does

- A text from the reply box goes from the sender's own seat.
- A call or a text that comes in names the seat it rang.
- With no seats, texts go from the company number with no one's seat.

## Change or remove a seat

Change the id or the person, then press {{button:blue|Save the seats}}. Press **×** to remove a seat. Press **Put them back** to undo what you have not saved.
