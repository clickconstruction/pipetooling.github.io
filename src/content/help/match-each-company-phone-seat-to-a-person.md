Each person who answers the company number has a seat in Quo. Match each seat to its person here, so texts and calls carry their name.

The office team can read the seats. Only the owner or a dev can change them.

## Open the seats

1. Go to [Settings](/settings?tab=settings-phone-texts).
2. Press **Phone and texts**.
3. **Company phone seats** is under Quo connection.

## Add a seat

1. Press **Add a seat**.
2. Pick the seat's person in Quo under **Quo member**.
3. Pick who answers on it.
4. Press {{button:blue|Save the seats}}.

The list of Quo members comes from Quo. It is there once the [Quo connection](/help/connect-the-app-to-quo) has its key. The member's Quo user id shows small under the pick.

Each seat is one Quo member. Each person has one seat.

## Type the id by hand

Without a key, the card shows a **Quo user id** box instead of the list. Type the seat's Quo user id there. Quo's user ids start with US.

The same box shows when Quo did not answer. The card says so under the seats.

With the list up, pick **Type it by hand** for a seat Quo does not list. Press **Pick from Quo** to go back to the list.

## What a seat does

- A text from the reply box goes from the sender's own seat.
- A call or a text that comes in names the seat it rang.
- With no seats, texts go from the company number with no one's seat.

## One seat for the whole office

One Quo login can be signed in on several phones. Pick **Shared by the office** as who answers on that seat.

- A call it takes reads *the office answered*. The thread does not say who picked up.
- A text from the reply box still carries your own name. It goes out from the shared seat when you have no seat of your own.
- A text typed in Quo's own app reads *the office*. Text from the app instead, so the line carries your name.

## Change or remove a seat

Change the member or the person, then press {{button:blue|Save the seats}}. Press **×** to remove a seat. Press **Put them back** to undo what you have not saved.
