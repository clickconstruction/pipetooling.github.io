A what-if copy lets you try moves on a schedule without changing it. Nobody else sees your copy. Keep the moves you like, or throw the copy away.

Only devs can make a copy for now. Each person has their own copy of each schedule.

## Make a copy

1. Open the project's schedule from [GC projects](/gc).
2. Press **What if…** at the end of the chart's buttons.

The chart now shows the copy. A violet line over the chart says so. A grey outline shows the real dates under each bar you move.

## Try moves

Drag a bar, drag an end, or link two bars, as you do on the real schedule. You can also change a bar in its form.

The **Why it moved** window opens. In the copy, a reason is optional. Press {{button:blue|Try it}} to put the move on the copy. Keep asks for any reason you left out.

**Pull the work earlier** and **Days back** work in the copy too. Both windows fill in the reason already.

To take a try back, press **Undo** under **Tried in the what-if**.

## What the copy leaves out

The copy only tries moves. The walk, the real days and inspections stay on the real schedule. So do waits, places and the dates to meet. **Print or PDF** and **Export** show only on the real schedule.

## Keep the moves

1. Press the keep button on the violet line. The button counts the moves, like **Keep the 2 moves…**.
2. Give a reason and a sentence to each move that has none.
3. Press {{button:blue|Keep the 2 moves}}.

The moves go on the real schedule, oldest first. Each one says **Tried in a what-if first.** in **Changes to the schedule**. Undo takes them off one at a time.

## When the real schedule moved

Someone may move a bar on the real schedule while your copy is open. Keep then names the bars that moved. Press **Throw it away** and make a new copy.

Someone may also save just before you press Keep. The window then reads the schedule again and stays open. Press the keep button again to keep your moves.

## Throw the copy away

Press **Throw it away** on the violet line. Press it again to be sure. The real schedule stays as it is.

## Come back to it later

Press **See the real schedule**. The copy stays open for you. Press **What if** with its count to go back to it.

The money team also sees what the copy does to the bills. To make moves for real, see [move a bar on the schedule and say why](/help/move-a-bar-on-the-schedule-and-say-why).
