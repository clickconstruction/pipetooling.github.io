Every stage with a checklist reads on the Pipeline row. One icon under **Edit** shows how far the job's checklists are. Press it and the checklists open beside the row. You answer, add a check or skip a question from the board.

## Read the icon

The icon sits in the row's icon strip under **Edit**, after the plans icon. The icon's color says where the job stands.

- Amber with a count, like **3/6**, means a stage has a question left. The count is what is left to answer across the open stages.
- Green means every stage with a list is filled.
- Grey means the stages take a checklist but have no questions yet.

A job with no Rough In, Top Out or Trim Set has no icon. A service call is one such job.

Rest on the icon to read each stage. The words say *Trim Set 3 of 6* for a stage with a question left and *Rough In filled* for a filled one.

The amber stage name under the bar is a press too. The name opens the same sheet.

## Open the checklists

1. Find the job on the Pipeline.
2. Press the checklist icon under **Edit**.

The sheet opens beside the row. The top line counts the stages, like *1 of 3 stages ready*. Each stage reads with its name and a chip. The chip says *Not ready · 3 of 6*, *Ready · 4 of 4* or *No questions yet*.

A ready stage folds to its chip. Press the stage's name to open its rows. Press it again to fold them.

:::example What the sheet shows for one job
**Rough In** {{chip:green|Ready · 4 of 4}}

**Top Out** {{chip:green|Ready · 3 of 3}}

**Trim Set** {{chip:yellow|Not ready · 3 of 6}}
:::

## Answer from the board

A stage with a question left shows its rows. The next open question is the big row marked **Next**.

1. Press {{button:outline|Yes}}, {{button:outline|No}} or {{button:outline|Not here}}.
2. Press **Add a note** to say more, if you want.

Each press saves by itself, under your name. The stage's chip moves at once. The list moves to the next question on its own.

To change an answer, press the answered row. The row opens again with its three presses.

## Add a check, skip a question, take one off

The sheet carries the same presses as the job window. Press {{button:outline|+ Add a check for Trim Set}} to add a check for this job alone. Press **Skip on this job** on a company question that does not apply here. Press **Take off** on a check you added. Each one works as the guide [add a check to a job's stage checklist](/help/add-a-check-to-a-jobs-stage-checklist) says.

Who sees these presses follows the same rule as the job window. The office sees them on every job. A superintendent sees them on their own jobs. An estimator sees them on the jobs they priced. The crew answers and leaves notes.

## Open the job for the rest

Press {{button:blue|Open on the job}} at the foot of the sheet. The job window opens on its Documents tab. There you read the skipped questions, put one back, or add a job's check to the company's list.

## Show only the jobs with a question left

The search bar shows {{chip:gray|Checklist left · 4}} when a job on the board has a question left. The number counts the jobs.

1. Press {{chip:gray|Checklist left · 4}} in the search bar.
2. Read the board. Every section shows only the jobs with a question left, in the usual order.
3. Press {{chip:yellow|Checklist left · 4 ×}} to show every job again.

The chip is not there when no job has a question left.

## On a phone

A phone card has no icon strip. Open the card's **⋯** sheet and press **Checklists**. The count sits beside the word, like *3 of 6*. The checklists open as a sheet from the bottom of the screen.

## What the row shows after

The row's icon catches up when the sheet closes. A stage you filled turns the amber mark under the bar off. Once every stage is filled, the icon turns green.
