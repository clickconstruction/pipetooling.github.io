Settings has a lot of tabs, and the thing you want is sometimes three clicks deep. The **search bar at the top of Settings** takes you straight there.

## How it works

Start typing what you're looking for — the name of the setting, or just a plain word for it:

:::example Searching "vacation"
{{icon:help}} **Personal time off** (vacation) &nbsp;&nbsp;·&nbsp;&nbsp; in Your account →
:::

Each result tells you where it lives before you commit. Pick one (tap it, or ↑/↓ then **Enter**) and the app opens that tab and scrolls to the section. **Esc** clears the box.

Plain words work: "vacation" finds Personal time off, "trash" finds Recently deleted, "password" finds My profile, "what's new" finds Release notes.

You'll only ever see results for tabs your role can open — the list is different for a tech than for the office.
