The Deadlines tab on the Lien desk shows when each lien deadline falls. Pills at the top sort the jobs by month. Each job is counted once, at its next date.

## Open Deadlines

Open the Lien desk from the orange gavel on the Pipeline. Press the {{button:blue|Deadlines}} tab, beside Do now. Every billed job with money still owed is on it.

## Read the pills

The pills sit at the top of the tab. Each one shows how many jobs it holds and the money owed on them.

- {{chip:gray|All}} shows every job.
- {{chip:red|Overdue}} holds the jobs whose notice or lien window has closed. Nothing can be filed on them, but the money is still owed.
- {{chip:yellow|This month}} holds the jobs whose next date falls this month.
- {{chip:blue|Next month}} holds the jobs whose next date falls next month.
- {{chip:gray|Later}} holds every job after that.

A job's next date is the notice it still owes. A job with no notice owed counts at its lien date. So each job sits in one pill only.

Press a pill to see only that month. Press it again to see every month.

## Read a month

With **All** picked, each month is a section with a bar on top. The bar says the date, the days left and what falls due. **Overdue** starts folded at the top. Press its bar to open it.

An overdue job is listed with its property when that property has a date still ahead. It sits under the property's address, greyed, after the jobs that are still on the clock. Its amount reads *still owed*. So one property reads in one place. The Overdue bar says how many of its jobs are listed that way. The Overdue count and its dollars do not change. Press the {{chip:red|Overdue}} pill to see every overdue job in one list.

:::example the bar on top of this month
This month by Oct 15, in 14 days. 24 notices across 9 GCs.
:::

Inside a month, each job sits under its GC. The GC row says how many notices to send. Jobs with no GC sit together under one heading. A notice the desk printed but has not recorded reads differently. The bar counts it as *in the mail*. The GC row says *record the mailing* when every notice it owes is printed.

## Draft the notices

When a month's notices can go out, its bar shows a button like {{button:amber|Draft the 24 ›}}. Press it to open the Notices tab on those jobs. The desk lists a notice once its date is 30 days away or less. A notice already printed is not drafted again. The bar offers {{button:amber|Record the mailing · 10 ›}} for those instead. It opens the run on its recording step, where the certified numbers go in.

## Use the date row

The row above the jobs shows each deadline date. The small number beside a date counts the jobs due on it. The dark line down the board is today.

Type in the search box at the left of that row. A job number, a name, a customer, a GC or an address all work. The pills then count only the jobs that match.

## Set a property kind

A property with no kind is read as a house. Its lien date could be a month later. The bar of pills then shows a button like {{button:outline-amber|6 kinds not set ›}}. Press it to set each kind, biggest balance first.

Office users on a computer can press it. Everyone else sees the count.

## Read a mark

Rest the pointer on any mark and a card opens under it. The card says what the mark is, its date and how many days away that is. It says the money owed on the job. It ends on a button for the next move, such as {{button:amber|Draft the notice ›}} or {{button:amber|Record the mailing ›}}.

Marks that touch share one card, with a column for each mark in the same order. Rest on a GC row's flag to see every job behind its count. Each job shows its month and its money. The card's button acts on exactly those jobs. A card wider than the board scrolls sideways.

Press Esc to close a card, or move the pointer away. Pressing a mark still does what it did before.

## Read the key

The key runs along the bottom of the Deadlines tab. Press a mark to read what it means. Press it again or press Esc to close it. Three marks tell a month's notice apart. A hollow flag is a notice still owed. An amber circle with an envelope is a notice printed and in the mail, its tracking number still owed. A green check is a notice on file. The envelope sits right after the check in the key. Press the envelope on a row to open Record the mailing.

## On a phone

A phone shows the same pills with their counts only, and the months without the time line. Press the magnifier to search. Each job shows its dates under its name.

## Good to know

- Press a job's row to open its Lien window.
- Press the job's number to open the job itself. That window has its history, its bills and **Edit**. Close it and you are back on the desk.
- Office users can press a pay dot to record when they said they will pay.
- A storefront on a row means a supply house is still owed on that job. Press the storefront button beside the search box to see only those jobs. See [see which supply houses are owed on a lien job](/help/see-which-supply-houses-are-owed-on-a-lien-job).
- The whole flow of the desk is in [send lien notices from the Lien desk](/help/send-lien-notices-from-the-lien-desk).
