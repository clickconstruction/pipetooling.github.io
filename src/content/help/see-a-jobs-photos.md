A job's photos live in the customer's Google Drive folder. The job window shows them under the address. Nothing is copied out of Drive. The app reads the folder each time you open the window.

## Open the job window

Open a job from the Pipeline, the Dashboard or a schedule card. Under the address you see **Photos** and the count of pictures in the folder. Three small pictures sit under it, the newest first. A fourth tile says how many more there are.

## See every photo

Press **Photos** with its count, or any tile. The viewer opens with every picture in the folder. The day sits on each one. Press a picture to see it full size. Press {{button:outline|Open in Drive}} to see it in Google Drive. Press {{button:outline|Back to the photos}} to return to the grid.

## The Documents tab

The job window's Documents tab has a Photos line too. It says how many photos are in the folder and the day of the newest. Press the line to open the same viewer. Every photo across every job is on [Documents → Photos](/help/find-every-photo-that-was-uploaded).

## Where the photos come from

The app reads two places in the job's Drive folder. The first is the Customer Pictures folder. The second is Photos under Team only. A photo taken in the app saves to that folder. A text's photo saves there when the office turns that on for the job. A checklist photo saves there too.

A photo just taken shows under the strip as on its way to the folder. It appears among the pictures once the sweep files it. That takes a few minutes.

## A job with no folder

A job with no Drive folder link says so under the address. The office presses {{button:outline|Set the folder}} and pastes the folder's link. The office can also press **Make the job's Drive folders** on the Documents tab. Anyone else asks the office.
