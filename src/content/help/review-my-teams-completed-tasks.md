When someone finishes a checklist task you assigned, it lands in Checklist review. You sign it off there, or send it back with a comment.

Tasks that notify you on completion land there too. **Checklist review** is a section of the Teams Inbox. You'll find it on the **Dashboard**, in **Dispatch Mode → Inbox**, and on **Checklist → Review**. It only appears when there's something waiting.

:::example A completed task waiting on you
Install Cassie's golf cart charger
Michael A · done yesterday 4:41 PM · "Mounted and tested"
:::

## The three tiles up top

**To sign off**, **Outstanding** and **Missed this week** are tappable. Each shows its number. The number is grey with a green ✓ caption when it's zero. Each has a caption that answers *who / how old*, like *one-offs · 7 people · oldest 118 days*. You tap **To sign off** to open the sign-off list. You tap **Outstanding** to jump to the by-person board. You tap **Missed this week** to flip that board to the Missed view. The tile for the view you're on stays outlined in blue. So the tiles double as a range switcher.

## Signing off

If the work is good, you tap the green {{button:green|✓ Dismiss}} rail on the row's left. It is the same one-tap pattern as the Dispatch inbox. The card is stamped signed off and leaves the queue. The worker sees "signed off" on their card.

You tap the row itself to expand it first when you want the full story. It shows every completion, reopen, sign-off, and note, with who and when. {{button:outline|Dismiss}} works from there too.

## Sending it back

If it's not done right, you type a comment. The comment is required, so the person always knows why. Then you tap {{button:amber|Reopen with comment}}.

- The task goes back to open on their **Today** list. It has an amber "Reopened by you" strip carrying your comment.
- They get a notification with your reason.
- When they complete it again, it returns here for another look.

## Good to know

- **Rearrange someone's list by feel**: you press and hold any card on the by-person board. About a third of a second does it, with mouse or finger. The card lifts to you, and you drag it above or below the others. A quick tap still opens the card. Scrolling never picks one up. The order saves per person. It is the same order their Today list follows.
- You review tasks you created or are set to be notified about. Devs see everything. Your own completions never queue to you.
- Quiet items age out on their own after 7 days. The queue doesn't pile up if you skip a few.
- Anyone who can see a task can add notes on it. So the conversation can continue right on the card.

## Goals moved to the Roadmap page

The **Goals** strip now lives at the top of the **Roadmap** page, where the plan is. You reach it from the header, under **Roadmap**. The strip holds each roadmap's stage progress, unlock banners, 🔔 Remind, ⇅ Reorder, and the stage ledger. Review keeps what it is for: what the team finished and what is still outstanding. Roadmap-born tasks still carry their purple {{chip:purple|⛰ goal}} chip here. You tap it to see where the task fits. **Open full roadmap →** jumps to the plan. See *plan the roadmap and see how each goal is going*.
