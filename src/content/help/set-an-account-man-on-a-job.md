Every job can name an Account Man. That is the team member who owns the customer relationship.

The Account Man carries a communication level. It tells everyone else how careful to be.

## Set it on Edit Job

1. Open the job and click {{button:outline|Edit}}.
2. Find the **Account man** row right below Job Address. Tap it to open the picker. **Who** lists only people already on the job's **Team**. If it is empty, add them on the Team row first.
3. Pick the **Relationship**:
   - **Primary communicator** is the default point of contact. It shows quietly on the job.
   - **Preferred communicator** means you route conversations through them when you can. It shows as an amber chip.
   - **Only communicator** means nobody but the Account Man talks to this customer. It shows loud and red.

The Account Man may later be removed from the job's team, from any screen. Then the assignment clears automatically. So it can never point at someone who is not on the job.

## Where it shows

On **Jobs → Pipeline** the Account Man appears in the Job column under the customer and GC lines. GC means the general contractor. The Account Man is marked with the person icon. On the **Job Detail** window it sits under the title.

:::example the three looks
{{chip:gray|Malachi}} — primary, quiet · {{chip:yellow|Malachi · preferred}} — amber chip · {{chip:red|Malachi · only}} — red chip
:::

**Only communicator** also draws a thin red stripe across the top and bottom of the job's card or column. It draws the same stripe around the Job Detail window. So the crew can tell at a glance: route every customer conversation through the Account Man.
