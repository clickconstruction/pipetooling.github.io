A customer whose job we build sees that job on its own page in their portal. The page says where the job stands, in plain words.

## What the customer sees

The page has four tabs:

- **Home** says what we need from them first, what happened this week and who to call.
- **Money** shows how far along the job is, each pay application and the statement.
- **Schedule** shows the job's dates and why a date moved.
- **Papers** lists what they signed, what waits on them and what comes later.

Nothing of ours shows there. They never see a trade's name, our costs or an office note.

## Change our words

Every sentence of ours on the page is in **Settings → What customers see**, under **Customer page**.

1. Type words you see on the page in **Find a sentence**.
2. Press {{button:gray|Edit}} on the sentence.
3. Change the English and the Spanish.
4. Press {{button:blue|Save}}.

The change reads on every customer's page. Words in braces are blanks the page fills in, like a day or a name. Keep each blank as it is, in both languages. The sentence shows who changed it and when, and how we first wrote it.

A training account sees the sentences and the switches but changes nothing.

## The three switches on each job

**Each job** lists every job on a customer's portal. Each job has three switches.

- **Photos** links this week's line to the job's photos.
- **Next bill** shows the size of the next bill once the page can work it out.
- **Spanish** offers the page in Spanish once Spanish is on for every portal.

A switch saves the moment you tick it. Each one starts off.
