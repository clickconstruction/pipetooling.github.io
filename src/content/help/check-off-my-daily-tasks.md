The Checklist → Today tab is your task list for the day. Each task is a card.

Tap the big checkbox to mark it done. Use notes to talk about the work right on the card. Anyone who can see the task can join in.

:::example A task card
{{button:outline|☐}} Install sheet metal across the front of the office

{{button:outline|💬 Notes 2}}
:::

## Completing a task

Tap the checkbox. That's it. There is no save button. Want to say something about how it went, like "used the scrap ply, looks clean"? Tap **＋ Add a note** and post it. Once a task has notes, the button becomes **💬 Notes**. Open it to read the thread or add another.

Tapping the checkbox again reopens the task. Every completion and reopen is remembered on the card's history, with who and when.

## Say how far along it is

A card that happens once shows a ring where the box was. An empty ring means nothing has moved yet. Tap the ring to say where it stands. Pick **25**, **50** or **75**. Type what is left if you want. Tap the blue **Set** press. The ring fills to your number. The card's notes keep each move with your words.

:::example The sheet
Where does it stand?

{{button:outline|25}} {{button:outline|50}} {{button:blue|75}}

What is left? Puddy and the glue, Thursday

{{button:blue|Set 75%}}

{{button:green|✓ Done}}
:::

Tap **✓ Done** on the same sheet when the work is finished. It takes the same path as the big checkbox. Tap a green ring to reopen the card. A card that repeats keeps its box. A card with lines reads its ring from the ticks.

## Tick the lines

Some cards list what done looks like. The lines sit under the title as boxes. The next one wears an amber ring. Tap a box when that line is true. Your name and the time appear under it. The card reads its percent from the ticks. Two of three ticked reads 67%.

:::example A card with lines
{{button:outline|☐}} Put the plumbing tools in a neat home

{{chip:gray|1 of 3 done when…}}

{{button:green|✓}} Pipe is racked by size

{{button:outline|☐}} Fittings are in labeled bins

{{button:outline|☐}} The cart is clear and the floor swept
:::

Tap the last box and the card is done. It takes the same path as the big checkbox. Tap a ticked box to untick it. Every tick and untick is a line on the card's notes. On a computer the card shows a chip like {{chip:gray|2 of 3 done when…}}. Tap the chip to open the lines. A card that comes back starts its lines over. The notes keep who ticked what.

## Find where a task is

Some tasks have a place, like *Pump house*. The place's name sits under the task with a blue dot. A task with no place has no dot, and nothing else about it changes.

Tap the task to open it. Under its details you see a small picture of the spot from above. Tap the picture to open the map full size, where you can drag and zoom.

The person who gave the task may have added a photo of the spot. The photo sits beside the picture from above. Tap the photo to see it full size.

- {{button:blue|Directions}} opens your phone's maps app at the spot. Off a road, the maps app gets you to the nearest road. The picture does the rest.
- {{button:outline|How far am I?}} asks your phone where it is and answers, like *250 ft north-east of you*. The answer also says how sure your phone is.

The app asks your phone where it is only when you tap **How far am I?**. Your location stays on your phone. The app does not save it or send it.

The line under the buttons says who set the place and when. A machine moves and a gate gets rebuilt. If the place looks wrong, say so in a note.

With no signal the picture may not load. The task says so. **How far am I?** still works, since your phone finds itself without a signal.

## See all my tasks on a map

When an open task of yours has a place, the Today tab opens on a map. The map is called **Where my tasks are**. It sits above the list, in Task Mode too.

- Each place is a dot with a number. The same number sits beside the place's name on the task below.
- A red dot means a task there is overdue.
- Tasks at one place share a dot. The dot shows the first number and a plus, like *2+*.
- Tap a dot to see its tasks under the map. Tap {{button:outline|Show the task}} and the list scrolls to that task and opens it. {{button:blue|Directions}} takes you to the place.
- Tasks with no place are not on the map. A line under the map counts them, and they are still in the list.

Tap {{button:outline|Show where I am}} to add a dark dot for you. Each task's line then says how far away its place is, like *250 ft north-east*. Your location stays on your phone.

**Satellite** shows the ground from above. **Streets** shows roads. Tap **Hide map** to fold the map away. Your phone remembers, and **Show map** brings it back.

## Notes are a conversation

Tap the **💬 Notes** button to open the card's thread. The blue badge counts the notes. You'll see every note anyone has left, plus the card's history, in order. The history shows completed, reopened and signed off. Type in the box and hit **Post** to add yours.

## When a card comes back

If whoever assigned the task sends it back, the card shows an amber strip:

:::example A reopened card
Reopened by Robert · 9:05 AM
"Breaker keeps tripping, check the wire gauge before re-mounting."
:::

Fix it up, reply on the thread if useful, and check it off again.

## After you complete

Your finished task may show **waiting on review**. That just means the person who assigned it hasn't looked yet. When they sign off it reads **signed off**. Nothing more is needed from you.

## Outstanding: overdue work that still needs doing

Below your Today list you may see a red-framed **Outstanding** section. It holds one-off tasks whose day came and went without getting done. Each row shows how overdue it is. It reads *due Thu, Jul 30 · 20 days ago*. Check it off right there when you knock it out. Or tap the row to read its notes first. Daily and weekly tasks never pile up here. Only work that's still genuinely waiting does.

Nothing escalates on its own. An overdue one-off doesn't page anyone or move up someone else's list. What the office does see is its age. On Checklist → Manage the open one-offs sort oldest first. A chip turns {{chip:yellow|amber}} after a week and {{chip:red|red}} after a month. A **🔔 Remind** from whoever assigned it is a person deciding to nudge you, not a timer.

## Looking back: the History tab

On your phone, **Checklist → History** shows your days newest-first. Each day says how many you finished. Every task carries a chip. {{chip:green|✓ 7:58 AM}} means done. {{chip:yellow|✓ by Maria}} means someone covered it. The other chips are {{chip:red|✗ Missed}} and {{chip:yellow|Reopened}}. The strip up top shows your week percentage and streak. Tap any task to read its whole story and add a note.

On a computer, History is two lists. **Repeating** tasks keep the day-by-day grid. Each row starts the day the task was created. It reads *since 6/14* under the title, with a blue tick on its first day. So there are no empty boxes for days a task didn't exist yet. **One-off tasks** sit below as a simple ledger. A row reads {{chip:green|✓ done}} with created → done dates, {{chip:red|✗ missed}}, or open.

## Sending a task back yourself

Assigned to a task someone else marked done, but it isn't? On **History**, tap the row. Write a note saying why. The note is required. Then tap {{button:amber|Reopen}}. Whoever completed it gets notified with your reason. The task returns to **Today → Outstanding** until it's actually finished.

## ⏳ Waiting For

Roadmap tasks that are yours but not yet ready show in **Waiting For**. They are grayed, with no checkbox. On Checklist → **Today** it's its own section. On the Dashboard it's a quiet folded strip at the foot of the **My Inbox** card. The strip shows the count plus a peek at the first blocker. Tap to unfold. They're grouped under the step that's holding them. Each group names that task once, with who's on it and the stage. Your waiting tasks are listed beneath in the order they'll unlock. A small *then* marks the ones queued behind another. Only the first three groups show. Tap **…and N more steps ahead** for the rest. The moment a step ahead is done, your task moves onto your list on its own.
