The **Checklist → Today** tab is your task list for the day. Each task is a card: tap the big checkbox to mark it done, and use notes to talk about the work right on the card — anyone who can see the task can join in.

:::example A task card
{{button:outline|☐}} Install sheet metal across the front of the office

{{button:outline|💬 Notes 2}} {{button:outline|＋ Add note}}
:::

## Completing a task

Tap the checkbox. That's it — no save button. If you want to say something about how it went ("used the scrap ply, looks clean"), tap the big **＋ Add note** button and post it; the note travels with the task.

Tapping the checkbox again reopens the task. Every completion and reopen is remembered on the card's history, with who and when.

## Notes are a conversation

Tap the **💬 Notes** button (the blue badge counts them) to open the card's thread. You'll see every note anyone has left, plus the card's history — completed, reopened, signed off — in order. Type in the box and hit **Post** to add yours.

## When a card comes back

If whoever assigned the task sends it back, the card shows an amber strip:

:::example A reopened card
Reopened by Robert · 9:05 AM
"Breaker keeps tripping, check the wire gauge before re-mounting."
:::

Fix it up, reply on the thread if useful, and check it off again.

## After you complete

Your finished task may show **waiting on review** — that just means the person who assigned it hasn't looked yet. When they sign off it reads **signed off**, and nothing more is needed from you.

## Looking back: the History tab

On your phone, **Checklist → History** shows your days newest-first — each day says how many you finished, and every task carries a chip: {{chip:green|✓ 7:58 AM}} done, {{chip:yellow|✓ by Maria}} someone covered it, {{chip:red|✗ Missed}}, or {{chip:yellow|Reopened}}. The strip up top shows your week percentage and streak. Tap any task to read its whole story and add a note.
