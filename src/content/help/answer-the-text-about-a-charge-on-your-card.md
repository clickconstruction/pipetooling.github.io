When a charge posts to your company card, the company number may text you. The text has a link, and the link takes a photo of the receipt. You need no sign-in.

## What the text says

The text names the store and the amount. A text reads like *$228.58 at Ridgeway Supply posted to your card today. Snap the receipt here:* and then the link.

Your first text from the company number also says our name. The first text ends with a line about how to stop the texts.

A charge posts some hours after you pay. The text often comes that evening or the next morning.

## Send the receipt

1. Tap the link in the text. A page opens on that charge.
2. Check the store and the amount.
3. Press **Take a photo of the receipt**. Hold the phone over the whole receipt.
4. Wait for **Got it**. The receipt is then on that charge.

The page may ask **Which job was it for?** Tap the job, or skip it.

[Take a photo of a supply house ticket](/help/take-a-photo-of-a-supply-house-ticket#a-receipt-link-from-the-office) explains the page's other presses.

## When one text names two charges

Charges that post within one hour share one text. The page then shows each charge with its own press. Take one photo for each charge.

## When you get a second text

A charge with no receipt gets one reminder. The reminder comes in the evening. Its link opens the same charge.

You get no more texts about that charge after the reminder. The office may ask you for the receipt.

## When you already took the photo

You took the photo in **Job Parts Tally** at the counter? Then no text comes for that charge. Your ticket is already on the charge.

## When the link has closed

A link opens for 14 days. After that the page reads *This link has closed*. Take the photo in **Job Parts Tally** instead.

## Stop the texts, or get them back

Reply STOP to stop every text from the company number. The office then asks you for receipts another way.

Reply START to get the texts back.

## When no text comes

The texts go to the cell number the office has for you. No text means the office may have no number, or an old one. Tell the office your cell number.

The owner can also have these texts turned off. The office then sends you a link by hand, or you take the photo in **Job Parts Tally**.
