The Timeline forecasts your finish date from how fast work actually gets done. Estimates make that forecast honest: without them, "drill a well" and "buy a hose clamp" each count as one task. With them, the Timeline counts **days of work** — and the 🎯 flag lands where the math really points.

## Set an estimate

Open any task's card (tap it on the Plan, Map, or Timeline) and find **Estimate**:

:::example The stepper
⏱ Estimate &nbsp; {{button:outline|−}} **2.5 days** {{button:outline|＋}} · half-day steps · clear
:::

- **＋ / −** move in half-day steps; **clear** removes the estimate.
- A task with no estimate counts as an **average task** — the roadmap's mean estimate, shown as *≈ 2d*. You never have to estimate everything: sizing just your big rocks already improves the forecast.
- Editors only — same rule as editing the task.

## What changes on the Timeline

- **Slot widths scale with effort.** A 5-day task draws five times wider than a 1-day task — the chart shows where the real work is.
- **The pace strip counts days**: *"≈ 212d of work left · 5.5 days/week done · 🎯 ≈ Jun '27."* Stage rows and wave headers carry their day sums too.
- **Nothing is scheduled.** The columns are still your dependency arrows and the dates are still derived from your observed pace — estimates are a task's *size*, never its calendar.

The what-if dial keeps working — it just asks "what if we finished N days of work per week" instead of N tasks.
