The Timeline forecasts your finish date from how fast work actually gets done. Estimates make that forecast honest.

Without estimates, "drill a well" and "buy a hose clamp" each count as one task. With them, the Timeline counts **days of work**. The 🎯 flag then lands where the math really points.

## Set an estimate

Open any task's card and find **Estimate**. You can tap the task on the Plan, Map, or Timeline.

:::example The stepper
⏱ Estimate &nbsp; {{button:outline|−}} **2.5 days** {{button:outline|＋}} · half-day steps · clear
:::

- **＋ / −** move in half-day steps. **clear** removes the estimate.
- A task with no estimate counts as an **average task**. That is the roadmap's mean estimate, shown as *≈ 2d*. You never have to estimate everything. Sizing just your big rocks already improves the forecast.
- Editors only. It is the same rule as editing the task.

## What changes on the Timeline

- **Slot widths scale with effort.** A 5-day task draws five times wider than a 1-day task. The chart shows where the real work is.
- **The pace strip counts days.** It reads *≈ 212d of work left · 5.5 days/week done · 🎯 ≈ Jun '27*. Stage rows and wave headers carry their day sums too.
- **Nothing is scheduled.** The columns are still your dependency arrows. The dates are still derived from your observed pace. Estimates are a task's size, never its calendar.

The what-if dial keeps working. It just asks "what if we finished N days of work per week" instead of N tasks.

## Drag to resize (desktop)

On the Timeline, expand a stage and hover any open task's bar. A blue handle appears on its right edge. **Drag it** to size the task. The bar widens with a live *2d → 4.5d* readout. It snaps to half-days. The 🎯 flag re-derives as you pull. Release to save. Press **Esc** to cancel. Nothing gets scheduled. You are sizing the work. The drag writes the same estimate the card's stepper does. On phones, use the stepper.
