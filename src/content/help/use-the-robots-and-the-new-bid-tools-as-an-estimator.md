Six new tools landed on your bids and your Messages page this week. None of them need you to run anything. Here is what each one does, in one line, and the button to press. Two minutes to read.

## The ring on the Bid Board

The robot icon is a ring of seven segments. It fills as the robot works your bid.

**Try it.** Hover for the step. Tap for the sheet.

:::example If you want the details
The sheet draws the same seven steps as a bar, with the last word and the Mac it came from. Press {{button:blue|Read the run}} to see every signal the robot sent. Press {{button:gray|Leave this one to me}} to take the bid off the robots within five minutes. A ring that goes quiet for ten minutes turns amber.
:::

## The Plans button

Every bid tab has {{button:gray|Plans}} beside the bid's name. Type a word. The plans answer at once.

**Try it.** Type "water heater". Press **Open sheet**.

:::example If you want the details
It says which sheets hold the words, what the schedule row says and what the note says. Under the box sit the sheet index, every schedule as a table, and the general notes. The robot writes the words when it reads the set, within minutes of filing. It never gives a count of its own before you send. Read [search the plans on a bid](/help/search-the-plans-on-a-bid).
:::

## Ask the robot to look

When the plans' own answer is not enough, press {{button:purple|Ask the robot to look}}. It answers in minutes and names the sheets.

**Try it.** Ask about a note the schedule does not explain.

:::example If you want the details
The robot reads on its next round. The thread stays on the bid for the office. Your Dashboard says when the answer is there. The robot looks only after it sealed its own number, so your question never reaches it early. When the answer says the plans do not say, press {{button:gray|Make this an RFI}}. The question lands on the RFI tab with the sheet the robot cited. Read [ask the architect about the plans](/help/ask-the-architect-about-the-plans).
:::

## Needs You on your Dashboard

When a robot cannot open a bid's plans, your Needs You card names the bid.

**Try it.** Press it. Share the folder or attach the PDF.

:::example If you want the details
The robot goes again on the next round. The same card says when a robot's answer to your question is there. Progress itself never lands on the Dashboard. The bid's own ring and sheet carry that.
:::

## The robot's number at send

The moment you send, the robot's sealed number opens beside yours.

**Try it.** Send a bid. Read the six biggest differences.

:::example If you want the details
One tap per difference tells the robot what you know. Nothing you see there changes what you sent. Read [review the robot's number when you send](/help/review-the-robots-number-when-you-send) and [audit a robot bid and teach it what you know](/help/audit-a-robot-bid).
:::

## Answer all on Messages

Needs a reply has {{button:blue|Answer all}}. It walks the people waiting, one at a time, with nothing else on the screen.

**Try it.** Press it after lunch. **Send text** or **Send email** moves on.

:::example If you want the details
Each card shows the person, their last words, their job and the reply box with their step's quick replies first. Skip for now puts a person after the rest. The end offers the people who went quiet on us, with the reminder already written. Read [answer everyone waiting on us from one page](/help/answer-everyone-waiting-on-us-from-one-page).
:::

## A day with the robots

1. Morning. You file the plans on a bid. Within minutes the Plans button answers questions about the set.
2. Mid-morning. The ring on the Bid Board starts to fill. The robot is reading the sheets.
3. Lunch. The ring is full. The robot's number is sealed. You never saw it, and that is the point.
4. Afternoon. You press Ask the robot to look about a note the schedule skips. Your Dashboard says when the answer is there.
5. Send. The robot's number opens beside yours. You read the six biggest differences and send.

## Also new on Messages

- {{button:blue|Nudge}} on Waiting on them. The reminder is already written. You press Send. Read [see who is waiting on us and what we owe them](/help/see-who-is-waiting-on-us-and-what-we-owe-them).
- Search under the pills. Type two letters. Every message that holds them, newest first.
- The week in four numbers under the search box. Answered in an hour, the longest wait, texts not delivered, estimates sent and opened.
- A sent text says delivered. One that did not reach them reads not delivered in red, with **Call them**.
- The thread shows when a customer opened, accepted or declined an estimate.
- {{button:blue|Start an estimate}} and {{button:blue|Book a visit}} on a lead's thread. Either makes them a customer on the spot. Read [send an estimate and turn it into a job](/help/send-an-estimate-and-turn-it-into-a-job).
- Call in the reply box. Write what they said and press {{button:blue|Save the call}}. Add the day they gave.
- The Show switch. Mine narrows every list to the people you hold.

## Not yours, on purpose

The Robots Console and the Queue are the owner's pages. The rounds run from the owner's Mac every five minutes. You never start a robot. The Robot Board and the Scoreboard are yours to read.
