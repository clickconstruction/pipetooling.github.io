This guide lists the tools you have as an estimator after this week's updates. It covers the robots on your bids, the plans, your Dashboard and the Messages page. Each part links to the guide that goes deeper.

## What the robots do for you now

The robots run on their own. A Mac in the office that stays on makes a round every five minutes. You do not start them and you do not watch them.

A round picks one bid to shadow. A shadow is the robot's own blind bid beside yours. It reads the plan set itself, counts, prices and seals its number before you send. Read [let the robots shadow a bid](/help/let-the-robots-shadow-a-bid) for how a bid gets on the list.

The same Mac holds the pricing robot. Press **Price it with the robot** under a bid's supply house prices. A round reads the quotes and hands back its picks. Read [get supply house prices on a bid](/help/get-supply-house-prices-on-a-bid).

## The ring on the Bid Board

The robot icon on a bid is now a ring of seven segments, one per step of the robot's work. It fills as the robot goes. The segment it is on fills by the sheets it has read. Hover and it says the step in words, with when the robot last spoke.

Tap the icon and the sheet draws the same seven steps as a bar. It shows the last word and the Mac it came from. Press {{button:blue|Read the run}} to see every signal the robot sent on this bid.

Press {{button:gray|Leave this one to me}} to take the bid off the robots. The robot lets go within five minutes.

A robot that goes quiet on a bid for ten minutes turns the ring amber. The Robot Board row says the same thing.

## Plans on every bid tab

Every bid tab has a {{button:gray|Plans}} button beside the bid's name. Press it and type a word or a question. The plans answer at once. They say which sheets hold the words, what the schedule row says and what the note says. Press **Open sheet** to see the page.

Under the box sit the sheet index, every schedule as a table, and the general notes. The words come from the plan set itself. The robot writes them when it reads the set on a round, within minutes of the plans being filed. A set no robot has read is read once in your browser and saved for everyone.

The panel says what the plans say and where. It never gives a count of the robot's own before you send. Read [search the plans on a bid](/help/search-the-plans-on-a-bid).

## Ask the robot to look

When the plans' own answer is not enough, press {{button:purple|Ask the robot to look}} under the card. The robot reads the sheets on its next round. It answers within a few minutes and names the sheets it read. The thread stays on the bid for the office. Your Dashboard says when the answer is there.

The robot looks at a bid only after it has sealed its own number on it. So your question never reaches it before its number exists. Before you send, it never gives a count or a price of its own.

When the answer says the plans do not say, press {{button:gray|Make this an RFI}}. The question lands on the RFI tab with the sheet the robot cited. Read [ask the architect about the plans](/help/ask-the-architect-about-the-plans).

## Your Dashboard

Your Needs You card names a bid whose plans the robot cannot open. Press it to open the bid's needs sheet. Share the folder or attach the PDF there. The robot goes again on the next round.

The same card says when a robot's answer to your question is there.

Progress itself never lands on the Dashboard. The bid's own icon and sheet carry that.

## When you send

The robot's sealed number opens beside yours the moment you send. Read [review the robot's number when you send](/help/review-the-robots-number-when-you-send). To teach it what you know, read [audit a robot bid and teach it what you know](/help/audit-a-robot-bid). To see the whole scoreboard, read [see how close the robots are to our numbers](/help/see-how-close-the-robots-are).

## On Messages

You have the Messages page in the header, with the office. These parts are new this week.

A search box sits under the kind pills. Type two letters or more. The lists give way to the messages that hold those words, newest first. Press a row to open the thread at that line.

A strip under the search box shows the week in four numbers. Answered within an hour, the longest wait right now, texts not delivered, and the estimates sent, opened and accepted. Each number opens the rows behind it.

Needs a reply has {{button:blue|Answer all}} on its header. It walks the people waiting one at a time. Read [answer everyone waiting on us from one page](/help/answer-everyone-waiting-on-us-from-one-page).

Waiting on them lists every person who went quiet after our send, two days old or more. Press {{button:blue|Nudge}} and the reminder is already written. Read it and press Send. Read [see who is waiting on us and what we owe them](/help/see-who-is-waiting-on-us-and-what-we-owe-them).

Went out holds the emails we sent beside the texts. A sent text reads delivered once the phone company answers. One that did not reach them reads not delivered in red, with **Call them** on the row.

The thread shows when a customer opened, accepted or declined an estimate, in order with the texts. A text that carried the estimate link has **Open estimate** under its words.

A lead's thread has {{button:blue|Book a visit}} and {{button:blue|Start an estimate}}. Either one makes them a customer on the spot. Their thread comes with them. Read [send an estimate and turn it into a job](/help/send-an-estimate-and-turn-it-into-a-job).

The reply box has a fourth kind, Call. After you phone someone, write what they said and press {{button:blue|Save the call}}. Add the day they gave and the thread reads Their move until that day.

A Show switch sits beside the pills. Mine narrows every list to the people you hold. Everyone shows them all.

Under Went out, open Everyone we have talked with. It lists every person with a message, back to the first, with its own search.

## What you do not see

The Robots Console and the Queue are the owner's pages. The rounds start from the owner's Mac, not from yours. The Robot Board mirror and the Scoreboard are yours to read.

:::example What a day looks like
You file the plans on a bid in the morning. Within minutes the Plans button answers questions about the set. By lunch the ring on the Bid Board is full and the robot's number is sealed. You press Ask the robot to look about a note the schedule does not explain. Your Dashboard says the answer is there before you send.
:::
