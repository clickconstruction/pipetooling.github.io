A trip is one going out to a site away from home and coming back. The app keeps your days on the trip apart from the hours on its jobs.

You can start a trip from your phone when you clock in at a far site. Your hours are the same either way.

## Answer the question at clock in

Job Mode asks one question after you clock in on a job at a far site.

:::example The question on your phone
**PLUM 2101 · Lift station**
Heron Point · 1,180 miles from the shop

This site is a trip from home. Is this a trip?

{{button:green|Start a trip}}

{{button:outline|Join Luis’s trip, out since Mon}}

Just clock in

*The office can change it later. Your hours are the same either way.*
:::

The question comes after your clock in has gone through. You are clocked in whatever you answer.

1. Press {{button:green|Start a trip}} when you are out on a trip and nobody has started one.
2. Press the **Join** press when a crewmate already started the trip. The press names the crewmate and the day they left.
3. Press **Just clock in** when this is not a trip.

The phone asks on a site far from the shop. Far is 150 miles or more unless the office has set another distance. The phone also asks on a site that has had a trip before.

**Just clock in** is remembered on your phone for that job until tomorrow. Closing the question counts as **Just clock in**.

The office can change or delete your trip later. See [keep a trip to a site away from home](/help/keep-a-trip-to-a-far-site).

## Read the banner while you are out

While your trip is open, the job's card shows a green banner under the job's name.

:::example The banner on the job's card
**Trip 5 · day 3 at Heron Point**
with Priya · started Mon Oct 26

{{button:outline|Heading home}} &nbsp; {{button:outline|End the trip}}

*Heading home marks the drive as On the road.*
:::

The banner shows the trip's number. The office uses the same number for the trip.

The banner counts your own days on the trip. The banner names who is on the trip with you.

The banner also shows before you clock in, on the job that is ready to start.

Each day you clock in on a job at that site, the app adds the day to your trip. You press nothing for that.

A day you drive in reads {{chip:blue|On the road}}. The day turns to {{chip:green|At the site}} when you press {{button:blue|I'm here}}.

A day you clock in at the site with no drive reads At the site from the start.

A day the office marked {{chip:yellow|Getting ready}} stays as marked when you clock in.

## Press Heading home

1. Find the banner on the job's card.
2. Press {{button:outline|Heading home}} on the day you drive home.

Today is then marked On the road. The banner says *Today is marked On the road.*

Heading home does not clock you out. Clock out the way you always do.

## End the trip

1. Press {{button:outline|End the trip}} on the banner.
2. Read your days on the sheet. The sheet counts your days at the site and your days on the road.
3. Type a sentence under *How did it go? (you can skip this)* if you want to.
4. Press {{button:green|End the trip}}.

Press {{button:outline|Not yet, still out}} to keep the trip open.

Ending a trip changes no hours. The office can still change the trip afterwards.

The app does not remind you to end a trip when you are back at the shop. Use the banner on a job at that site.

## Your hours are the same either way

A trip is a reading laid over the clock. Starting, joining or ending a trip changes no clock row. A trip changes no pay and no bill.

A press that fails says why on the screen. You stay clocked in.

## Who can do this

Anyone clocked in on a job in Job Mode gets the question and the banner. A training account can read them. The app refuses a training account's presses and says so.
