Some jobs ask you to sign papers first. An NDA or site rules are common ones. The job stays shut to you until each paper is signed. Everyone signs, the office too.

## What a locked job looks like

A locked job shows its number and a lock. It says how many papers wait, such as *2 papers to sign before this job opens*. You do not see its name, its address or its customer yet.

You meet it in a few places.

- Your day in Job Mode lists it with the lock.
- Your schedule and your calendar show its number with *Papers to sign first*.
- The clock in list shows its number with the lock.
- A **Papers to sign** line shows under **Needs you**.

## Read and sign

1. Press the locked job. A card opens named **Before you open** with the job's number.
2. The card lists each paper and says which are signed.
3. Press the blue button. It names the paper, such as {{button:blue|Read and sign: Site rules}}.
4. Read the paper. Sign at the bottom and press the button there.
5. Press **Back to the job**.

The card comes back and says what is left. Sign the next paper the same way.

## When nothing is left

The card says *Nothing is left to sign*. Press {{button:green|Open the job}}. The job opens as it always does. It stays open to you.

## Clocking in

You cannot clock in to a job while you owe it a paper. Pressing the job opens the same card. No clock starts. Sign the papers, then clock in.

## If the card says your login is not tied to your file

The card can say *Your login is not tied to your file yet*. Ask the office to link it on **People**. Then open the job again and sign.

## A paper added later

The office can add a paper to a job you already work on. The job locks again for you until you sign the new paper. The papers you signed before stay signed.
