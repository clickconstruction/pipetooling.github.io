A call to make is a task in Follow up. It sits under We owe them with the day and the words to say. Press Call to dial. The task closes by itself when the call lands in the thread.

## Add a call

After a text or an email goes to a customer, the receipt shows the call and what to say.

1. Read **What to say**.
2. Press **Add to my calls**. The task is set for tomorrow.

On Messages, a text you sent a customer today shows a quiet line under it. Press **Add to my calls** there for the same task.

## Make the call

1. Open Follow up and find **We owe them**.
2. Find the row. It reads **Call Dana about the estimate** with its day.
3. Press **What to say** to read the script.
4. Press {{button:green|Call}}.

:::example A call row
**Dana Whitfield** {{chip:blue|call · Sat Oct 18}}
Call Dana about the estimate, added by Rosa 2 hours ago
{{button:green|Call}} {{button:gray|I called}} {{button:gray|Tomorrow}} {{button:gray|Skip}}
:::

When a call made in Quo ends, Quo writes it in the customer's thread. The task closes on its own.

## Call in Quo or from your phone

Quo is the app the company number runs on. A call made in Quo shows the company number and lands in the thread.

On a phone, the first press of {{button:green|Call}} asks which way to call. The choice is yours each time.

- {{button:outline|Call in Quo}} opens the Quo app and dials from the company number. Quo writes the call in the thread, and the task closes on its own.
- {{button:outline|Call from my phone}} dials from your own number. Quo does not see the call. Press {{button:gray|I called}} after.

The app keeps your pick on that phone. The next press dials at once. The button reads {{button:green|Call in Quo}} when that is what it will do. Press the small {{button:green|▾}} beside it to pick the other way.

You see the choice only when you have a seat on the Company phone seats card. With no seat, Call dials from your phone as before.

On a computer, Call opens the app the computer uses for calls. To call through Quo there, set Quo as the default for calls once. On a Mac, open FaceTime, then its Settings, and pick Quo under Default for calls. On Windows, open Settings, Apps, Default apps, choose by link type, and pick Quo for TEL. The {{button:green|▾}} beside Call shows these steps too.

The same choice sits on every Call button in the app.

## Close or move it by hand

- **I called**. Closes the task and writes a note in the thread.
- **Tomorrow**. Moves the task one day.
- **Skip**. Closes the task with no note. A gray line says so.

A late call turns red. One due today turns amber.

## Write the words to say

The office writes the script on the step in Settings → Flows. Open the step and press **Call script**. Write it in English and in Spanish. The blanks fill in for each customer. See [set up the steps a person goes through](/help/set-up-the-steps-a-person-goes-through#write-the-call-script).

Untick **Suggest a call after this goes** to keep the receipt from asking.
