A template is the shape of a job's schedule, kept to start the next job like it. Each line keeps its days and what it waits on. A template keeps no dates, companies, percents or moves.

Only devs can open the schedule for now. The rest of the office gets the Schedule window later.

## Draw a first draft from a template

1. Open [GC projects](/gc).
2. Press {{button:outline|Schedule}} on the card of a job with no schedule yet.
3. Pick the day work starts.
4. Pick one under **Start from a template**.
5. Read the line under it. The line says how many of this job's lines the template covers and how many weeks the template makes.
6. Press {{button:blue|Draw a first draft}}.

The lines the template covers run as they ran on its job. The other lines take the usual stage days. The schedule says which template it was drawn from.

## Save a job's schedule as a template

1. Open the **Schedule** of a job being built.
2. Find the **Templates** card under the chart.
3. Keep the job's name, or type another.
4. Press {{button:blue|Save as a template}}.

Every template shows the job it came from and the jobs drawn from it. Press **Rename** to give it a new name. Press **Set it aside** to stop offering it to new jobs. The jobs drawn from it keep what they drew. Press **Bring it back** to offer it again.
