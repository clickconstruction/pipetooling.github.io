A template is the shape of a job's schedule, kept to start the next job like it. A template made on the Templates page is phases and rows with no dates. Each row keeps who does it, its days and what it waits on. A template keeps no dates, companies, percents or moves.

## Draw a first draft from a template

1. Open [GC projects](/gc).
2. Press {{button:outline|Schedule}} on the card of a job with no schedule yet.
3. Pick the day work starts.
4. Pick one under **Start from a template**.
5. Read the lines under it. They say what will land on this job, the weeks it makes, and which trades have no company yet.
6. Press {{button:blue|Draw a first draft}}.

Every row of the template lands from the start day. A trade's row lands on the job's line of the same name. A row the scope book lacks lands as the job's own work. A row for a trade the job has no package for lands with *No company yet*. The job's own lines in no row take the usual stage days. The schedule says which template it was drawn from.

## Read the schedule by phase

1. Open the **Schedule** of the job.
2. Press {{button:outline|By phase}} over the chart.

Each phase is a row that reads its start, its end and its days from its rows. Press {{button:outline|List}} to read the rows with their days and spare days. *No company yet* under a row opens Trade partners at that trade.

## Save a job's schedule as a template

1. Open the **Schedule** of a job being built.
2. Find the **Templates** card under the chart.
3. Keep the job's name, or type another.
4. Press {{button:blue|Save as a template}}.

The template keeps the schedule as phases and rows. The **Templates** pill on GC projects lists every template and opens it in the editor. Rename, Set it aside and a new version live there. See [build a schedule template](/help/build-a-schedule-template).
