When every line of a GC job is billed, the customer walks the job and accepts it. Then our final pay application asks for the retainage they hold.

Closeout shows in the Bill the customer window, below the bills under **Sent**. Closeout has six steps in order, and each step says who moves it. A done step shows {{chip:green|done}}. The step up next shows {{chip:yellow|next}}. The owner, the leaders and the controller see the window.

## The six steps

1. **Every line billed.** Our pay applications have billed the whole price.
2. **Every trade's final pay application.** Each trade asks for its own retainage with its conditional waiver on final payment.
3. **The customer accepts the work.** The customer walks the job and accepts it.
4. **Our final pay application.** Our final asks for what the customer holds.
5. **The architect certifies it.** Record the certificate the way you do for any bill.
6. **The customer pays it.** Then our unconditional waiver on final payment goes.

The trades step names each trade still to send its final, and what stands in that trade's way. Our final waits for every trade's final, since the trades' retainage is paid from ours.

## Record that they accepted the work

1. Press {{button:outline|GC}} on the Bids page to open [GC projects](/gc). Press {{button:outline|Bill the customer}} on the job's card.
2. Find **Closeout** below the bills under **Sent**.
3. Pick the day they accepted it. The day cannot be one still to come.
4. Type who walked the job and accepted it.
5. Type a note if you want one.
6. Press {{button:blue|Accept the work}}.

{{button:blue|Accept the work}} shows once every line is billed. The step then says the day they accepted it. A job is accepted once.

## Send our final pay application

{{button:blue|Send the final pay application}} shows once every trade has sent its final and the customer has accepted the work.

1. Tick **Email it to the customer and the architect now** to email them the form. The tick starts off.
2. Press {{button:blue|Send the final pay application}}.

The final holds nothing back and asks for the rest of the price. With the tick on, the customer gets it with the form. The architect gets the same form and an ask to certify it. After the final, the window bills nothing more.

When the architect certifies it, [record the certificate](/help/bill-the-customer-on-a-gc-job#record-the-certificate).

## Our waivers on final payment

Our conditional waiver on final payment goes with our final pay application.

1. Find our final pay application under **Sent**.
2. Press {{button:outline|Make our conditional waiver}}. The waiver window opens on the final payment form.
3. Check the form, sign it and send it the way you send any waiver.

When the customer pays the final, [send our unconditional waiver](/help/record-what-a-gc-customer-paid#send-our-unconditional-waiver) on the final payment form.
