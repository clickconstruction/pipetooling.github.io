PipeTooling is the record of who works here. CountTooling and TakeoffTooling each keep their own accounts. The Sites view sets the two beside each person, so you can see who has a seat where.

## Open the Sites view

1. Open **People**, then **Users**.
2. Press {{button:outline|Sites}} beside Contact, Account and Pay.

The view is for a developer. It shows on a computer and not on a phone.

## Read a row

Each row has two cells. One is for CountTooling and one is for TakeoffTooling.

- {{chip:gray|no seat}} means the site has no account for them.
- {{chip:green|active}} means they can sign in there.
- {{chip:yellow|retired}} means the site has turned their seat off.

A cell can say more after that.

- A role, such as **admin**, is the role they hold on that site.
- **twin** marks a robot's seat.
- A time, such as **3d ago**, is their last sign-in there. TakeoffTooling sends it. CountTooling does not yet.
- **by address** means the match was made by email address. Their row holds no id for that site yet.
- **address differs** means the site holds another address for them. Hover it to read that address.
- **link gone** means their row points at an account the site no longer has.

## Read the line above the list

The line above the list has one sentence for each site. It counts the accounts on that site that match nobody here. Those are worth a look. Someone may have left, or an address may have changed.

:::example When a site cannot be read
The line says **TakeoffTooling could not be read** and gives the reason. The other site still shows. Press **Read again** to try once more.
:::

## Change a seat

The Sites view reads only. Nothing you press here changes an account.

Open the person's desk to give them a CountTooling seat. Archiving a person retires their CountTooling seat too. The guide [archive user accounts](/help/archive-user-accounts) walks through that.
