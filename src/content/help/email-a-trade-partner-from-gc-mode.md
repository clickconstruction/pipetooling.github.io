GC mode sends our emails to a trade partner company for us. Each email carries the company's portal link and reaches the right people there.

Only a dev's press sends one while GC mode is built. The questions window sends an answer to the companies on its trade. [Ask the architect about the plans](/help/ask-the-architect-about-the-plans#email-the-answer-to-the-companies) shows how. The Ask window and a new set of plans send theirs too. So do the Draws and Closeout windows, with their email tick. [Close out a trade](/help/close-out-a-trade-and-close-a-gc-job#pay-their-retainage) shows the tick.

## What each email says

Every email has the same frame around its own words.

- It greets the people it goes to by first name.
- It says what this email is about, such as the trade we want priced.
- It has an {{button:blue|Open your portal}} button with the company's link.
- It ends with the project manager's name. Their replies go to the project manager.

It comes from Click Construction. A message about the company itself, such as the master agreement, is signed by whoever sent it.

## Who at the company gets it

Each email is one kind. The kinds are quotes, the job, contracts, and pay and papers.

The company picks who gets each kind in its portal. The main contact gets every kind until the company changes that. [Look up a trade partner](/help/look-up-a-trade-partner) shows who gets what.

Plans and answers go to the people who quote while we bid. Once the job is ours, they go to the people on the job.

Someone with no email is left out. When nobody left has an email, nothing goes out. The screen then tells you to call them.

## The link in the email

Every email carries the same link to the company's portal. A company with no link gets one with its first email.

[Share a trade partner its portal](/help/share-a-trade-partner-its-portal) says how to make a new link or turn it off.

## Each message goes once

A second press on the same message sends nothing. The screen says it went before.

A reminder is a new message, so it goes out.

## See what was sent

The company's portal lists every email we sent it under **Messages from Click**, newest first. Press **Open it as the office** on the company's portal block to read them.

Each email is also kept as a sent copy. The kept copy never shows the company's link, so nobody can open the portal from it.

## When an email does not go out

The screen says why in a short line. These are the usual ones.

- The company has no ask on that project. Ask it to quote first.
- No one there has an email for that kind. Call them.
- The email service refused it. Try again in a minute.
