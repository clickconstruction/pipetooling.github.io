A teammate who left and comes back keeps the same account. Their hours, logbook, HR file and job history stay with them. Bring back is one window on their desk. It restores the login and the roster row together and keeps the first stint beside the new one.

Never hire a returner as a new person. A new account starts with no hours, no logbook and no HR file. Pay is keyed by name, so the new pay row lands on the old one.

## On their last day

1. Open their desk. Press the header's **⋯** menu, then **End employment…**.
2. Work the rows. Hours you typed by hand wait on a second office person. Have them approve the last day first.
3. Set the **End date** to their last working day. Keep **Archive after** ticked.
4. Press {{button:red|End employment}} and confirm.

Their login is banned and they leave every roster and picker. Their open roadmap tasks move to the Plan view's Needs a person lane. A person with no login is archived the same way.

## While they are away

Their desk stays. Open People → Users, press {{button:outline|Archived}}, and find their name. The desk reads *Archived* with the dates of the stint that ended. A license that expires meanwhile still shows on Licenses. Bring back marks it red on the day back.

You can leave them one screen to find when they sign in. Press {{button:outline|Leave a note…}} on the Status row. See [leave a note for a teammate who is away](/help/leave-a-note-for-a-teammate-who-is-away). Bring back takes the note down.

## On the day they are back

1. Open People → Users and press {{button:outline|Archived}}. Press {{button:blue|Bring back…}} on their row. The desk's Status row and its **⋯** menu offer the same press. If their Away note counted down to today, the Dashboard's Needs you card lists them with {{button:blue|Bring back…}} too.
2. Set **Back on** to their first day back. The line under it says how long they were away and the first stint's dates.
3. Work the rows. Each one is something time away made stale.
4. Tick **Give back their open roadmap tasks** only when you want the old tasks back. The box starts ticked within 90 days of leaving. After that it starts unticked.
5. Leave **Start in training mode** unticked unless they need it. A returner knows the app.
6. Press {{button:blue|Bring back}} and confirm.

:::cards

:::card Sign-in email
Press {{button:blue|Still theirs}} when the address is still theirs. A dev changes it on Access & account.
:::

:::card Wage
Press {{button:outline|Keep $24.00}} or {{button:blue|Change…}} and type the new wage. This row cannot be left open.
:::

:::card Team, Licenses, Paperwork
Each takes {{button:outline|Leave open…}} with a reason. A license that expired while they were away reads red.
:::

:::

Both halves come back in one press. The end date clears and the start date becomes the day back. A new stint opens beside the old one. One line lands on the HR file. The desk's Employment row then lists every stint with the time away between. Tenure counts from the day back.

:::example The HR line
"Back on 2029-11-05 for Corinne Vela, after 3 years away. Left open on purpose: licenses (renewal in the mail)."
:::

## Ended by mistake

Under the date, press **Undo the end instead**. The end date clears and the old stint reopens. The login and the roster row come back. Use it when the end was pressed on the wrong person or the wrong day.

## Hire by mistake

Someone may press + Hire for a returner. The form checks the name and the email against everyone who left. A match stops the form and offers {{button:blue|Bring back Corinne}}. That press opens the desk with the Bring back window up. See *hire someone*.
