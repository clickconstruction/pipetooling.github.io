You can text or email a job's customer right from the job. The message lands in their thread in Messages, tied to the job.

## Find the box

1. Open the job from [Jobs](/jobs). The job window opens on its **Job** tab.
2. Scroll to the notes under the job's activity.
3. Over the box is a row of three: {{button:outline|Note}}, {{button:outline|Text}} and {{button:outline|Email}}.

The row shows for the office team only. The crew's view of the job keeps the note box as it was.

## Write to the customer

1. Press {{button:outline|Text}} or {{button:outline|Email}}. The box turns orange, since orange is the customer's.
2. Write the message, or press a line in **Quick replies** to fill the box.
3. Press {{button:blue|Send text…}} or {{button:blue|Send email…}}.
4. Check the message in the window that opens. It shows what the customer sees.
5. Press **Send as email** to send it.

**Tie to** under the box names the job. A message from the job window always lands on that job.

Texts wait until **Send texts** is switched on in Settings. Until then **Text** says so in place of the box. Press {{button:outline|Email}} to send it as an email instead. The owner or a dev turns texts on. For them, the line links to the switch. [Turn on texts from the company number](/help/turn-on-texts-from-the-company-number) shows how.

An email goes under the plumbing company's letterhead. Its copy is kept in Documents.

## Add a note for the crew

Press {{button:outline|Note}} to go back to the note box. A note stays on the job for the crew and the office. The customer never sees it.

## When the row cannot write to them

- *This job has no customer yet.* Add one in **Edit Job**, then come back.
- A customer with no mobile number cannot get a text. A customer with no email address cannot get an email. The window says which is missing.

## More on the box

The box is the same one as in Messages. [Reply to a person from their thread](/help/reply-to-a-person-from-their-thread#use-a-quick-reply) shows the quick replies. [Send a text or an email](/help/reply-to-a-person-from-their-thread#send-a-text-or-an-email) shows the window before it goes.
