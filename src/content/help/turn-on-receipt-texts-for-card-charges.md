The app can text a person when a charge posts to their company card. The text carries a link, and the link takes a photo of the receipt. The owner or a dev turns the texts on in Settings.

## Before you turn it on

Two things must be true for a text to go.

- **Send texts** is on. [Turn on texts from the company number](/help/turn-on-texts-from-the-company-number) shows how.
- The card is tied to a person, and that person has a cell number on file.

A charge that cannot text says why on the Team queue. [Sort the team's card purchases from the office](/help/sort-the-teams-card-purchases#what-a-line-says-about-the-text) shows those lines.

## Try it on your own card first

1. Open [Phone and texts](/settings?tab=settings-phone-texts) in Settings.
2. Find the card **Text a receipt link when a card charge posts**.
3. Under **Who gets these texts**, pick **Only me, to try it first**.
4. Press the {{button:outline|Off}} switch.
5. Press {{button:blue|Turn it on for me}}.

The switch now reads {{button:green|On}}. The next charge that posts to your card texts you. No one else gets a text.

## Turn it on for everyone

1. Press the {{button:green|On}} switch to turn the trial off.
2. Under **Who gets these texts**, pick **Everyone with a card**.
3. Press the {{button:outline|Off}} switch.
4. Press {{button:blue|Turn it on}}.

The card then says who gets the texts and since when. A charge that posted before that time gets no text. Turning the switch on never texts about old charges.

## What a person gets

Each person gets one text when a charge posts. Charges that post within one hour share one text and one link.

A charge with no receipt gets one reminder. The reminder goes in the evening, at least twelve hours after the first text.

A charge that already has its ticket gets no text. Money back to the card gets no text.

## Turn it off

Press the {{button:green|On}} switch. No more receipt texts go. A link that was already sent still opens until its last day.

The card shows an amber line when **Send texts** is off. Receipt texts wait until **Send texts** is on again.
