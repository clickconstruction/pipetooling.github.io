A job photo is a picture with a few words, on the job and in Drive. The office opens it from the Pipeline row. The field opens it from the Job Mode card. The same window adds a photo and shows every photo on the job.

## Open the job's photos from the Pipeline

1. Open Jobs and find the row.
2. Press the camera icon under {{button:outline|Edit}}. The icon sits right after the Job folder icon.
3. The **Job photos** window opens on what is there.

The icon is amber when the job has a Drive folder. The icon is grey when the job has none. A grey icon still opens the window. The first photo makes the folder.

Hold ⌘ on a Mac, or Ctrl on Windows, and press the icon. The photo folder opens in Drive instead.

On a phone, open the card's ⋯ menu and press **Photos**.

## Open the job's photos from the Job Mode card

1. Open the Dashboard with Job Mode on.
2. Under the stage checklists, find the **Photos** line. The number after it is how many photos the job has.
3. Press the line. The same window opens as a full sheet.

A job with no photo yet says **add the first** on that line.

In the window, press {{button:outline|Take a photo}}. The phone's camera opens. The words box comes up after the shot.

The job window's **Documents** tab has the same door. Its **Photos** line opens the window, and {{button:outline|Add a photo}} sits beside it.

## Add a photo with its words

1. Press {{button:outline|Add a photo}}. The tile sits first in the grid.
2. Pick one photo or several from this computer.
3. Each photo shows as a tile at the top while it shrinks and sends.
4. Under each tile, type what the photo shows in the box **What does this show?**
5. On a job with stages, tap the stage the photo shows.
6. Press {{button:blue|Add 2 photos to the job}}. The number is how many are ready.

The words are optional. Type them anyway. The words are how you find the photo later.

Several photos at once? Type the words under the first one. Then press **Use these words for the rest**. Every photo with no words takes them.

The press waits while a photo is still sending. A photo that did not send says why under its tile and offers {{button:outline|Retry}}.

Close the window without pressing Add and the new photos are let go. Nothing lands on the job.

## Where a photo lands

Each photo goes to the job's Customer Pictures folder in Drive within five minutes. The file is named by the day, who took it and the first words. The tile reads *on its way to Drive* until then.

A job with no Drive folder still takes a photo. The first photo makes the folder under the customer.

## Browse the job's photos

The grid shows every photo on the job, newest first, by day. The words sit under each photo. A photo that came with a report, a Tally box or a text shows where it came from.

A file someone put in the folder by hand shows too. It reads *Added in Drive* with its file name.

Type in the box **Find a photo on this job** to narrow the grid. The search reads the words, the person and the stage. Two letters start it.

When photos came through more than one door, chips sit over the grid. Press a chip to see one kind. The chips carry counts.

## Open one photo

1. Press a tile. The photo opens at full size with its words as the title.
2. Read who took it, when, the stage and where it is in Drive.
3. Press {{button:outline|Open in Drive}}, **Open the job** or **Only Carla's photos**.
4. Press **Next** to see the next photo.

## Change the words later

1. Open the photo.
2. Press **Edit the words**.
3. Type the new words and press {{button:blue|Save the words}}.

You may change the words on your own photos. The office may change the words on any photo. The Drive file takes the new words on the sweep's next pass.

## A photo or a report?

Use {{button:blue|Leave report}} when the office should hear it now. A report pushes, emails and moves the thread. See [take photos for a report](/help/take-photos-for-a-report).

Use **Photos** when the picture should be findable later. A job photo files no report and pushes nobody. One line lands on the job's activity, so the office sees what was added.

Every photo across every job is on [Documents → Photos](/help/find-every-photo-that-was-uploaded). The job window's strip shows the folder too. See [see a job's photos](/help/see-a-jobs-photos).
