Your week is one card on your home. It shows one number the office picked for you and how far it moved this week.

The number is yours alone. It is never compared to anyone else.

## Where it is

The card sits on your home under the job card. It names the week, Sunday to Saturday.

If you have no card, the office has not picked your signal yet. Ask them.

## The three signals

The office picks one of three.

**License hours** is for an apprentice. The big number is your approved hours toward the next license. Hours waiting for approval show hatched beside them. A short bar runs to the next 500 hour mark.

**Jobs without a callback** is for a tech. It is a rolling count, like 29 of your last 30 jobs. One callback costs one job. It never wipes out a year.

**Crew hours against target** is for a lead. It counts the crew's hours on the finished jobs that carry a target. A job that finished with no target is named as not counted.

:::example A tech's card
**Jobs without a callback**

29 of your last 30 jobs

▲ 5 jobs this week, none came back
:::

## The week's move

The green pill says what moved this week. On a quiet week it turns grey and says **Same as last week**.

The dots under the number are your last eight weeks. On the crew card a dashed line marks the target.

## A callback

A callback counts only after the office confirms it. The office reads the return visit first. Then it presses **It was a callback** or **Not a callback**.

Everyone clocked on the first job shares the callback, not the lead alone.

## After your last clock-out of the week

Your last clock-out of the week brings up one sheet. It says when you clocked out and your hours this week. Under that it says your signal and how far it moved. The customer's words of the week sit there too.

Press {{button:blue|Have a good weekend}} to close it.

The app knows it is your last clock-out when nothing is on your schedule for the rest of the week. With no schedule at all, it is Friday's clock-out. On a day the crew review deck deals, the sheet stays away.

## The Monday email

On Monday morning the same card comes to your email, for last week. It goes only to people with a signal, and only once a dev turns the Monday email on.

## The customer's words

When a customer gives your job four or five stars this week, their words sit on the card. See [see what a customer said about my work](/help/see-what-a-customer-said-about-my-work).
