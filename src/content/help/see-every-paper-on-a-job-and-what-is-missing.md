Papers on this job shows every paper for each company on a job. A dot for each paper says whether it is in. The companies with something missing come first.

## Open the papers of a job

1. Open [GC projects](/gc).
2. Find the job's card in the list below the board.
3. Press {{button:outline|Papers}} on the card.

## Read the grid

Each company on the job has a row. The trade partners come first, then the customer, then the architect. Each paper has a column, in the order of the flow.

- A green dot is done. The day it came sits under the dot.
- A white ring is waiting on them. A paper with a day ahead shows that day.
- A gray dot is none yet. The paper comes with the next step.
- An orange dot is late, or runs out soon.
- A red dot has lapsed. The paper ran out.
- A dashed ring is not yet on this job.

## Show only what needs you

The counts in the title add up the dots.

1. Press a count, such as {{chip:red|1 lapsed}}. Only the companies with a lapsed paper stay.
2. Press the same count again to see everyone.

## Read the strip before bid day

On a job we are bidding, a strip sits above the grid until bid day. The strip names the insurance that ran out and the late quotes. The strip also names a compare that has sat longer than usual, and the quotes still due.

Each line has its own button. The strip goes once our bid is sent.

## Act on a paper

1. Press a dot to open the company at that paper.
2. A paper on file opens as it is.
3. A paper they owe opens with the ask ready.
4. A paper we write opens with its send ready.

Nothing goes to a company until you press Send.

## See the list

1. Press {{button:outline|List}} at the top of the window.
2. Each company shows the papers it has reached.
3. Each paper shows its state, the step it finishes and where the person is on that step.
4. Press {{button:outline|Grid}} to go back to the grid.
