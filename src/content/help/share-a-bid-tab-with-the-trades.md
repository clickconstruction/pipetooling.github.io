A bid tab shows each company that quoted a trade how the quotes came in, low to high. Its own row is marked. It is our thanks for bidding, and a company that sees where it stood answers the next time we ask.

Only a dev sees bid tabs while they are built.

## Open the bid tabs

1. Open [GC projects](/gc).
2. Find the project's card.
3. Press **Bid tabs**. The number counts the trades with a tab.

A trade has a tab once two companies quoted it.

## Wait for our bid

Bid tabs open once our bid is in. Before then a tab would show one company another company's price while it can still change its own.

Press **We sent our bid** on the project's head when our price goes to the customer. See [set our number and send our bid](/help/set-our-number-and-send-our-bid).

## Share a tab

1. Find the trade's table.
2. Press {{button:blue|Share with the 2 who quoted}}.
3. The trade now shows {{chip:green|shared Oct 8}}.

Each company sees its own row marked in its portal. The other rows read *Another company*.

## Show the names

Tick **Show company names to each other** to let each company see every name. Untick it to hide them again.

## See who opened it

The **Their copy** column says {{chip:green|opened}} once a company opened its tab. Until then it says {{chip:yellow|not opened yet}}.
