Each trade partner has its own window. The window shows how the company answers, who there gets our emails, and the work it does with us. The company's portal link is there too.

Only a dev sees the window while it is built.

## Open a company's window

Press a company's name wherever it shows. The name works on Trade partners, on Follow up and on a project's trades.

Press Escape or {{button:outline|×}} to close the window.

## Read the top of the window

The top line names the company with its chips.

- A chip such as {{chip:green|answers}} says how often they send a quote when we ask.
- A company new to us says {{chip:yellow|not vetted yet}} until a dev decides.

Under the name are its trades, the person to call, the phone and the email. A company with no email says {{chip:red|no email on file}}.

## Set the language

Pick English or Español in the **Language** box. The company's portal opens in that language. Our emails to them go out in it too.

Set it when a company asks for Spanish on a call.

## Read About

About lists what helps you decide whether to ask or award them.

- **Quotes** counts how many asks they answered with a quote.
- **Vetting** says who approved them, the day and any limit.
- **Drives from** gives their address and how far they go.
- **Insurance** shows when their insurance runs out, once we have the day.

## See who gets our emails

**Who gets our emails** lists the main contact first. Each chip names a kind of email they get, such as {{chip:gray|Pay and papers}}.

The company names other people in its own portal, like a bookkeeper for pay. You see them here once they do.

## See their work and the times they passed

The **Their work with us** list shows each job we awarded the company. **Times they passed** lists each job they said no to, with the reason we wrote down.

Press a job's name to go to its card.

## Share their portal

The **Their portal** section at the bottom shows the company's portal link and whether they opened it.

1. Press {{button:blue|Make the link}} if they have none yet.
2. Copy the link and send it with the ask to quote.

A new link stops the old one from working. Turning the link off asks once more first. See [share a trade partner its portal](/help/share-a-trade-partner-its-portal).
