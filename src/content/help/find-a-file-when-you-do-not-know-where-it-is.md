Every job's files live in its Drive folder. When you know a file exists but not which job holds it, search by its words.

## Search by the words in the file

Open **Documents** and press **Search**. Under the job and bid search sits **Find a file**.

Type two or more words from the file. Its name counts, and so do the words inside it. Drive reads the words inside PDFs and the scans it has read. A scanned permit is found by the words on its page.

The results come a moment after you stop typing. Each file sits under the job or bid whose folder holds it. The line under the file names the folder, the day and the size.

Press the file's name to open it in Drive. Press **Open the job** to open the job window.

Under many files a line shows where your words appear. The words are in bold. A Google Doc or a PDF with real text shows the line. A scanned paper says *Matched inside the scan*. A photo or a very big file shows no line.

:::example Two words find a scanned letter

**Find a file** &nbsp; {{chip:gray|rock clause}}

*2 files say “rock clause”.*

**Job 1187 · Maple Court townhomes** &nbsp; Open the job

Rock clause letter.pdf &nbsp; *Plans · Oct 14 · 410 KB*

Permit 2026-0412.pdf &nbsp; *the job's folder · Oct 14 · 2.1 MB*

:::

## What the search reads

Our own index answers first. The index holds the words inside every file the app has read. A Google Doc, a PDF with real text and a text file are read. A scanned paper is read with OCR once the office has it set up. OCR is text recognition, the computer reading the words on a picture of a page. A very long PDF is read from its first page until its time runs out. A PDF over 12 MB is not read. Drive's own search runs behind the index and may still find those words. The count line says *from our index* when the index answered.

Once the office sets up search by meaning, the index also finds a file by what it is about. A letter where the GC agreed to extra rock is found even when it never says *agreed*. Such a hit carries a small *by meaning* tag and shows the opening of the part that matched.

Drive's own search runs behind our index every time. The index's hits show first, usually within a second. Drive's finds that the index did not name follow a moment later. When the index has nothing, Drive's finds are the whole list and the count line says *from Drive*. Drive reads the words inside PDFs and the scans it has read. When the index found files by meaning alone, Drive's finds come first.

To put a job's files in the index, open the job window and press **Documents**. Under **In the folders**, press **Index this job's files**. The press reads the files in rounds and says how many are left. A file it could not read is listed with the reason. New files are read on their own every ten minutes after that. A file moved in from another folder is read too, whatever date it carries.

## When nothing comes back

The list says *Nothing in the job folders says* your words. Try fewer words, or a different one. A photo straight from a phone has no words for Drive to read. A file put in a folder a moment ago may take Drive a minute to read.

## What the search covers

The search looks through the PipeTooling Jobs shared drive, where every job's folder lives. A file outside it is not found here. Search in Drive itself for those.

A file under no job shows under **Not under a job**, with the folders it sits in. Move it into the job's folder in Drive and it shows under the job next time.
