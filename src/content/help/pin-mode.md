Pinning puts your favorite pages on the Dashboard as one-tap shortcuts. You turn on Pin Mode to show the pin footer on each page.

You can also pin a specific bid's tab. The pin controls live in a small footer at the bottom of each pinnable page. That footer only shows while **Pin Mode** is on. So the app stays clean for everyone who never pins.

The Dashboard itself cannot be pinned. It is where pins land. With Pin Mode on, the Dashboard shows a one-line note in place of the footer. Open the page you want as a shortcut and pin it from there.

## Turn it on

1. Open the {{icon:gear}} gear menu in the header.
2. Tap **Pin Mode**. It sits with the other modes: Job Mode, Dispatch Mode and Farm Mode.
3. The pin footer appears at the bottom of pinnable pages. {{button:outline|Pin}} pins the page you are on. On Bids with a bid open, {{button:outline|Pin bid}} pins that bid's tab.

Turn it off the same way when you are done arranging shortcuts. The footer disappears, and everything you pinned stays.

:::example What stays when Pin Mode is off
Your pinned pages keep working: the Dashboard quick bar still shows them, tapping them still jumps straight there, and Settings still manages (removes/clears) them. Pin Mode only controls whether the pin **footer** shows on pages.
:::

Pin Mode works inside Dispatch Mode too. The footer shows on the dispatch Dashboard, Schedule, Inbox, Customers and PO pages. PO means purchase order. Those pins read like **Dispatch Schedule** on the quick bar.

The toggle is per device. Turning it on at the office does not show the footer on your phone.
