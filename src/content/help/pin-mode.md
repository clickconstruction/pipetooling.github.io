Pinning puts your favorite pages on the Dashboard as one-tap shortcuts. You turn on the pin bar to pin a page.

You can also pin a specific bid's tab. The pin bar is a small bar at the bottom of each pinnable page. The bar holds the pin controls. The bar only shows while **Pin bar** is ticked in the gear menu. So the app stays clean for everyone who never pins.

The Dashboard itself cannot be pinned. The Dashboard is where pins land. With the pin bar on, the Dashboard shows a one-line note in place of the bar. Open the page you want as a shortcut and pin it from there.

## Turn it on

1. Open the {{icon:gear}} gear menu in the header.
2. Tap **Pin bar**. The row sits under **Show**.
3. The pin bar appears at the bottom of pinnable pages. {{button:outline|Pin}} pins the page you are on. On Bids with a bid open, {{button:outline|Pin bid}} pins that bid's tab.

Turn it off the same way when you are done arranging shortcuts. The bar disappears, and everything you pinned stays.

On a phone with the dock, press **More** and find **Pin bar** under **Show**.

The row was called Pin Mode before. The switch is the same one. Your choice is kept.

:::example What stays when the pin bar is off
Your pinned pages keep working: the Dashboard quick bar still shows them, tapping them still jumps straight there, and Settings still manages (removes/clears) them. The switch only controls whether the pin **bar** shows on pages.
:::

The pin bar works inside Dispatch Mode too. The bar shows on the dispatch Dashboard, Schedule, Inbox, Customers and PO pages. PO means purchase order. Those pins read like **Dispatch Schedule** on the quick bar.

The switch is per device. Turning it on at the office does not show the bar on your phone.
