A trade's line can run in parts, like the east half and then the west half. Split its bar into parts on the schedule. Each part gets its own bar and its own dates.

The office and estimators can open the schedule. Superintendents get it later.

## Split a line

1. Open [GC projects](/gc).
2. Press {{button:outline|Schedule}} on the project's card.
3. Press **Open all**, then press the line's bar.
4. Under the bar's form, press **Split into parts…**.
5. Name each part and give it its days.
6. Press **+ Another part** for one more.
7. Press {{button:blue|Split it}}.

The first part starts with the line, and the last part ends with it. Each part's share of the work comes from its days. The line keeps its dates and its percent.

## Change a part's dates

Press **Change a part's dates…**. Pick the part and give it new days. Press {{button:blue|Save, and say why}}. The **Why it moved** window opens, as it does for any move.

## Make it one bar again

Press **Make it one bar**, then press it again to be sure. The parts go, and the line keeps its dates and its percent.
