Our number is the price we give the customer for a GC project. That price is the trades we carry, plus our general conditions, contingency and fee. You set those three on the project's card, then mark the bid sent, won or lost.

Only a dev sees this while it is built.

## Open our number

1. Open [GC projects](/gc).
2. Find the project's card under the board.
3. Press **Our number**. Press **Hide our number** to close it.

Only the owner, the leaders and the controller see our number. Everyone else sees the trades alone. The board shows them **Trades so far**, not **Price so far**.

## Read the price

The strip shows the trades we carry, then general conditions, contingency, fee and the price to the customer.

- The price is red while a trade has no number.
- The price turns green once every trade has a number and every line has a cost.
- {{chip:red|Concrete: no number}} marks a hole in our number.
- {{chip:yellow|Sitework: our budget, no quote}} marks a trade we carry on our own budget.

A plus sign and a question mark mean a carried quote still has a line with no cost. Put a cost on it in [Compare quotes](/help/compare-quotes-and-carry-one).

## Set general conditions, contingency and fee

1. Type general conditions in dollars.
2. Type contingency and fee as a percent. Each one goes from 0 to 100.
3. Press Enter, or click outside the box. The number saves and the price moves.

The table under the strip lists each trade. The table shows where the trade's number comes from, our budget for it and the number we carry.

## Mark our bid sent

Press {{button:outline|We sent our bid}} on the project's head once the customer has our price. The button turns into {{chip:green|our bid went in Oct 8}}. The day is the company's day.

## We won it or we lost it

1. Press {{button:blue|We won this. Start buyout}}.
2. The card asks once more, because a win cannot be undone here.
3. Press **Yes, we won it**. The project moves to Buying out.

If we lost it, press {{button:outline|We lost this}}. Pick why we lost it. Add who won it and a note if you know. Then press **Mark it lost**. The project moves to the board's Lost section.

The customer came back? Press **Bring it back** on the lost project. The project goes back under Bidding to the customer, as it stood.
