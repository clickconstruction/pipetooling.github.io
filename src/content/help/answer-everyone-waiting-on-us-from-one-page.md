Messages is the office's one page for everyone who writes to us. It shows who is waiting on us and what we owe them.

The office team and estimators see **Messages** in the header. Press it to open the page.

## The page

The page is Follow up for every kind of person. It has the same three lists.

- **Needs you** holds notes whose day has come, and customers who wrote on their page.
- **Needs a reply** holds everyone who wrote or called and has no answer yet.
- **We owe them** holds what we still have to do for someone, with its day.

[See who is waiting on us and what we owe them](/help/see-who-is-waiting-on-us-and-what-we-owe-them) explains each list and its buttons. They work the same way here.

## Show one kind of person

The pills above the lists pick who to show.

- Press {{button:outline|Customers}} to see only the customers. The owner of a GC project is a customer too.
- Press {{button:outline|Trade partners}}, {{button:outline|Architects}}, {{button:outline|Leads}} or {{button:outline|Subs}} to see one of those.
- Press {{button:outline|All}} to see everyone again.

Each list counts only the people under the pill you picked. A number nobody has named shows under **All** alone.

The page's address keeps the pill. A link to [Messages for customers](/messages?kind=customer) opens with **Customers** picked.

## Supply houses and candidates

Supply houses and people we may hire never count on Follow up. Press {{button:outline|Supply houses and candidates}} to see them.

This pill shows a list of those people in place of the three lists. The person with the newest message is on top. Type in the search box to find a name, a number or any words.

## Open a thread

Press a row to open the person's thread over the page. The thread works as [Find every message with a person in one place](/help/find-every-message-with-a-person-in-one-place) shows.

Press Escape or {{button:outline|×}} to close the thread. The pill you picked stays.

## Open a customer's messages from their name

A speech bubble sits beside a customer's name on Customers, on their page, on Jobs and on Estimates. Press it to open their thread here.

- The first time, the app starts their thread with the number and the email on file.
- In a job's window, the window closes first.
- Pressing the name itself still does what it did before.

Only the office team and estimators see the speech bubble.

## From the Dashboard

The Needs you card has a **Customers** line when customers wait on us. It counts customers who need a reply, what we owe them and senders to sort. Press {{button:outline|Messages}} on it to open this page with **Customers** picked.
