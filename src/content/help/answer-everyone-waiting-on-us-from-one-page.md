Messages is the office's one page for everyone who writes to us. It shows who is waiting on us and what we owe them.

The office team and estimators see **Messages** in the header. Its count is everyone waiting on us. Press it to open the page.

On a phone with the dock at the bottom, press **More** and then **Messages**.

## The page

The page is Follow up for every kind of person. It has the same four lists.

- **New** holds what came in and still needs sorting. [Sort new messages before anyone answers them](/help/sort-new-messages) shows how.
- **Needs you** holds notes whose day has come, and customers who wrote on their page.
- **Needs a reply** holds everyone who wrote or called and has no answer yet.
- **We owe them** holds what we still have to do for someone, with its day.

[See who is waiting on us and what we owe them](/help/see-who-is-waiting-on-us-and-what-we-owe-them) explains each list and its buttons. They work the same way here.

Under the lists, **Went out** shows every text and email we sent in the last 7 days, newest first. The rows sit under the day each one went: **Today**, **Yesterday**, then the date. Each row names the person, the words and who pressed the send. A send to someone nobody has named says how it went. An email reads **An address nobody has named**. A text reads **A number nobody has named**. An estimate email's row says **opened** or **accepted** once the customer did that. Press {{button:outline|Texts}} or {{button:outline|Emails}} to see one kind, and {{button:outline|Both}} for all. Press a row to open the thread and read the text where it landed. The lists above hold what came in, so this is where you check that a send went. Once Quo answers, the row says **delivered** beside its time. A text that did not reach them reads **not delivered** in red, with {{button:blue|Call them}} on the row.

## Finish switching on

The owner and devs see one more line at the top of the page. It reads **Finish switching Messages on**, with how many of the eight switches are on.

1. Press the line. The card lists each switch with its state.
2. Press the door beside a switch that is off. It opens that setting.
3. Press {{button:outline|Later}} to fold the card for a week on this device.

The card goes on its own once all eight are on. The switches themselves are set on [Phone and texts](/settings?tab=settings-phone-texts). The reminders' door {{button:outline|Offer them}} opens the card **Reminders the app offers** there. See [offer the app's reminders](/help/offer-the-apps-reminders).

## The bar at the top

One bar sits at the top of the page. It stays there while you scroll. It holds the title, {{button:outline|All kinds}}, **Mine** and **Everyone** and the search glass. Beside them sit the lanes with their counts, {{button:outline|7 days}} and {{button:outline|?}}.

Press {{button:outline|?}} to read what each lane holds. A card opens under the bar with one line per lane. Press {{button:outline|Close}} to put it away.

Each lane's heading is a band in the lane's color. The count sits in a pill beside the name.

On a phone the bar is two short lines, and the lanes sit under them on their own line.

## Show one kind of person

Press {{button:outline|All kinds}} in the bar. A list of kinds opens.

- Press {{button:outline|Customers}} to see only the customers. The owner of a GC project is a customer too.
- Press {{button:outline|Trade partners}}, {{button:outline|Architects}}, {{button:outline|Leads}} or {{button:outline|Subs}} to see one of those.
- Press {{button:outline|All}} to see everyone again.

The button then reads the kind you picked. Each list counts only the people under that kind. A number nobody has named shows under **All** alone.

The page's address keeps the kind. A link to [Messages for customers](/messages?kind=customer) opens with **Customers** picked.

**Mine** and **Everyone** sit beside the picker. Press {{button:outline|Mine}} to see only the people you hold, in every list. Press {{button:outline|Everyone}} to see them all again. This browser keeps your choice. Waiting on them and Went out always show everyone, since nobody holds a send.

## See how the week went

Press {{button:outline|7 days}} in the bar. **The last 7 days** opens under the bar. It stays open on this browser until you press {{button:outline|7 days}} again. It is five numbers over the last seven days, today included. Each number opens the rows behind it. A tile with nothing behind it reads *none*.

- **Answered within an hour** is the share of people we answered inside an hour. A person who writes twice before we reply counts once. A reminder the app sent does not count as an answer. Neither does a note, since they never see one. The line under it compares last week. Press it to open Needs a reply.
- **Longest wait right now** names the person who has waited longest for a reply, and who has it. Press it to open their thread.
- **Texts not delivered** counts the texts of ours that never reached a phone this week. Press it to open Went out on its texts. The rows in red are the ones.
- **Estimates** counts the estimates sent this week, how many the customer opened and how many they accepted. The line under it says how many were opened and then went quiet. Press it to open Waiting on them.
- **Ratings** counts the rating asks sent this week, how many the customer answered and the score of 5. The line under it counts answers of 2 or less. Press it to open the newest answer. [Edit the quick replies](/help/edit-the-quick-replies) says how an ask goes out.

Under **Mine** the strip still shows the office's numbers, not only yours. It says so.

## Jump to a lane

The bar names each lane with its count. The lanes are **New**, **Needs a reply**, **We owe them**, **Waiting on them** and **Went out**. **Needs you** joins them while it has rows. The counts are the same numbers the lane headings show.

The lane you are reading is lit in its color.

Press a lane on the bar to go to it. On a phone the lanes are one line of names and counts under the bar. A press there opens the folded lists first, then goes to the lane.

## Answer everyone, one at a time

When someone waits in **Needs a reply**, its header has {{button:blue|Answer all}}. Press it to answer them one at a time, with nothing else on the screen. It works like Sort all.

Each card shows one person. You see their name, who has them, their last words and their job. Under that is the reply box, with the quick replies for their step first. Write your answer and press {{button:blue|Send text…}} or {{button:blue|Send email…}}. Read it in the send window and press Send. The next person comes up.

- Press {{button:outline|No reply needed}} when their line needs no answer. The next person comes up.
- Press {{button:outline|Skip for now}} to put them after the rest. They come back once the others are done.
- Press {{button:blue|Call}} to dial them.
- Press {{button:outline|Open the thread}} to leave the desk at their line.

A person with no number and no email on file cannot be answered from the desk. The card says so and offers their thread. When someone else answers a person while their card is up, the desk says so and offers {{button:blue|Next}}.

The end says what went. When people went quiet on us after our last word, it says how many and offers {{button:outline|Nudge the first}}. That opens the first one with the reminder written. Under **Mine** the desk walks only the people you hold.

## Supply houses and candidates

Supply houses and people we may hire never count on Follow up. Press {{button:outline|Supply houses and candidates}} to see them.

This pill shows a list of those people in place of the three lists. The person with the newest message is on top. Type in the search box to find a name, a number or any words.

## See who went quiet on us

**Waiting on them** sits between the lists and Went out. It holds every person whose newest line is ours, two days old or more. The one quiet longest is first. Each row shows **Their move** with the days, our last words and the day they went. Press {{button:outline|Nudge}} to open the thread with the reminder for their step already written. The send window is open too. Read it and press {{button:blue|Send as text}}. The words are the ones the morning run would send, so the team's quick reply for the step comes first. A person on no step gets a plain check in. Press the name to open the thread as it is. Press {{button:blue|Call}} to dial. A reply from them clears the row.

When our last text never reached their phone, the row turns red and says {{chip:red|⚠ not sent}}. They never read it, so a nudge would land on the same dead number. Press {{button:blue|Call them}} to dial. Press {{button:outline|Send it as an email}} to open the thread with the same reminder written as an email. The send window is open too.

## Find anyone we have talked with

Under **Went out**, press {{button:outline|Show everyone}}. The list shows every person with a message, newest conversation first, back to the first message. The pill you picked narrows it, like the lists above. Type in the search box to find a name, a number or any words in their messages. Press a row to open the thread. Press {{button:outline|Hide everyone}} to fold it away.

## Search every message

Press the search glass in the bar. The search box opens under the bar. Type two letters or more. The lists give way to **Lines that match**, the messages that hold those words, newest first. Each row names the person, whose line it is and the words around the match. Press a row to open the thread at that line. Press the glass again to close the box and see the lists again. The page's address keeps the words, so a reload keeps the search and a link can carry it.

On a phone, three empty lists fold to one line that shows each count at zero. Then Went out sits near the top. Press the line to open the lists. Any count opens them on its own.

## Open a thread

Press a row to open the person's thread over the page. The thread works as [Find every message with a person in one place](/help/find-every-message-with-a-person-in-one-place) shows.

Press Escape or {{button:outline|×}} to close the thread. The pill you picked stays.

## Open a customer's messages from their name

A speech bubble sits beside a customer's name on Customers, on their page, on Jobs and on Estimates. Press it to open their thread here.

- The first time, the app starts their thread with the number and the email on file.
- In a job's window, the window closes first.
- Pressing the name itself still does what it did before.

Only the office team and estimators see the speech bubble.

## From the Dashboard

The Needs you card has a **Customers** line when customers wait on us. It counts customers who need a reply, what we owe them and customers in **New** to sort. Press {{button:outline|Messages}} on it to open this page with **Customers** picked.
