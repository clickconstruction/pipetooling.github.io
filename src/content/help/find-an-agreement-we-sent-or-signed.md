The Agreements tab lists every agreement in one place. It holds job contracts, warranties, a GC's own paper, staff and sub agreements, partner agreements and sub work orders. It also holds a paper sent to a customer or a supply house.

The newest is on top. A press opens the window each agreement already has. A paper that waits on a signature can be sent again from its own line. A new paper can be sent from here too.

## Open the list

1. Open [Documents](/documents?tab=agreements).
2. Press the **Agreements** tab.

The list sits under one band per month, each with its count.

## Read a line

Each line is one agreement. It starts with a tag that says its kind.

- **Contract** is our contract on a job. A signed paper the office filed is also a Contract.
- **Warranty** is the warranty sent in place of a contract.
- **Their paper** is a GC's own form that we signed.
- **Staff** and **Sub** are a person's agreement from People.
- **Partner** is a partner's agreement.
- **Work order** is a sub work order on a job.
- **Owner contract**, **Statement of work** and **Trade company** are a GC project's papers.
- **Customer** and **Supply house** are a paper with an outside company.
- **Not on a list** is a paper sent to someone you typed in.

A paper with an outside company is not always an agreement. A second tag then says what it is. It reads **W-9**, **Insurance certificate** or **License**.

After the tag comes the paper's name. Under it you see who it is with, then the job as a green link.

A paper with a company also says who signs. It reads *Lena Park signs, Branch manager*. Once signed it reads *signed by Lena Park, Branch manager*.

Sometimes a paper names one company and its line names another. Adding someone to a list under a second company does this. The line then also reads *signing for Haddad Engineering*, the company the paper names.

On the right is where it stands, in the words its own window uses. Then comes the day it was signed, sent or ended.

:::example Three lines of the list
{{chip:blue|Contract}} **Residential plumbing contract**, with Harlan Pierce, on J2418. {{chip:yellow|sent ×2 · opened 3×}} Sent Nov 16.

{{chip:gray|Work order}} **WO-2391-01**, with Ortega Mechanical, on J2391. {{chip:green|✍ Signed}} Signed Nov 14.

{{chip:yellow|Staff}} **Employee handbook**, with Priya Nair. {{chip:green|signed ✓}} Signed Oct 20.
:::

## Find one

Type in **Find an agreement**. You can type a customer, a job number, a person, a company or the paper's name.

Every word you type must match. Type `cedar wo` to find the work orders on a job named Cedar.

## Find one from Search

The **Search** tab finds agreements too. Type in its box and look under **Agreements**, below Jobs.

Search looks at every agreement, voided ones included. Each line still says where it stands.

## Pick a kind

Press a chip in the first row. **All** shows every kind. The others are **Job contracts**, **Warranties**, **Staff and subs**, **Outside companies**, **Partners**, **Work orders** and **GC projects**.

## See what is waiting

Press **Waiting on a signature** in the second row. The number on that chip is how many are out and not signed.

This is the list to chase. A staff, sub or partner paper that waits has two presses on its line.

:::example A line that waits
{{chip:yellow|Staff}} **Employee handbook**, with Priya Nair. {{chip:yellow|never opened}} Sent Nov 3. {{button:outline|Send again}} {{button:outline|Copy the signing link}}
:::

## Send a waiting paper again

1. Press **Send again** on the paper's line.
2. Check the address under **Signer email**. The address is filled in from the person's record.
3. Press **Send the email again**.

The person gets an email with a new signing link. The link sent before stops working.

## Copy a signing link to text

Use this when the person reads texts and not email.

1. Press **Copy the signing link** on the paper's line.
2. Read the note. The note says the link sent before stops working.
3. Press **Make and copy the link**.
4. Paste the link into a text to the person.

Nothing is emailed. The link works for 14 days.

## Let the app remind them

The app can remind a person who has not signed a staff or sub paper. The owner turns this on in Settings.

1. Open **Settings** and pick **People & teams**.
2. Find **Paper reminders by email**.
3. Press **Turn reminders on**, then confirm.

From then on, a paper you send by email is reminded by itself. The person gets up to 3 reminders, 3 days apart. Papers already out are not reminded.

Each reminder carries a new signing link. The link before it stops working. The email tells the person so.

A waiting paper's line shows the count and the next day.

:::example A waiting paper with reminders on
{{chip:yellow|sent}} 1 of 3 reminders sent · next Nov 23 {{button:outline|Turn reminders off}}
:::

## Stop the reminders for one paper

1. Find the paper under **Waiting on a signature**.
2. Press **Turn reminders off** on its line.

The line then reads *Reminders off*. Press **Turn reminders on** to start them again. The next reminder goes 3 days later.

**Copy the signing link** also stops that paper's reminders. You have the link, so the chase is yours.

An outside company's paper is never reminded by the app. A person who left the team is not reminded.

## Chase a job contract

Press the name of a contract that waits. The Contract window opens.

Press **Resend email** there to send it again. **Void & redo** starts it over. **File the signed copy** files a paper they signed by hand.

## Send a paper to sign

1. Press **Send a paper**, beside the find box.
2. Pick the paper. The list is the Contract Book.
3. Pick who it is with. People who have signed it are not listed.
4. Check **Signer name** and the address under **Signer email**.
5. Press **Send email**.

Nothing is saved until you press **Send email**. The new line then shows at the top of the list.

If the window cannot send, it says why right above **Send email**.

A person who already has the paper out gets the same copy again. The window says so.

## Send a paper to an outside company

The second window is **Who is it with?** It lists your team and your subs first.

Type a name to find a customer or a supply house. Press **Supply houses** to list every one.

- A person on file at a company signs for that company. Pick them and their name fills in.
- A company picked alone has no signer yet. You name the signer in the next window.
- **Someone not in any list** is the last row. You type their name, their email and their company.

If the customers cannot be read, the window says so. Your team and the supply houses still show. When it offers **Retry**, press it to read them again.

The next window shows what the paper will say. It reads *Lena Park, Branch manager, signing for Tallgrass Supply Co.*

One tick keeps a new signer with their company. The next paper then offers them by name.

The app tells you when it already keeps an address you type. The paper then goes to that record.

**Send email** waits for that check. If the app keeps the address, the window names who holds it. Press **Send email** again to send the paper to them.

Someone on your roster is not sent a paper this way. Close the window and pick them from the list.

The new line sits under **Outside companies**. It can be sent again or copied as a link from its line.

**A company may already have the paper out.** The app checks when you pick the company. A window then says who has the paper and the day it went. It offers two presses.

- **Send it to Lena Park again** sends the same copy to the same person. They get a new signing link. The link sent before stops working.
- **Send a second copy to someone else** makes a new copy for another person there. The first copy stays as it is, with its own link. Each person signs their own copy.

Each copy is its own line under **Outside companies**.

## Add someone to a list

A paper sent to someone you typed in reads **Not on a list**. Its line carries **Add to a list**, signed or not.

1. Press **Add to a list**.
2. Pick what they are. They can be a new customer, a new supply house or vendor, or someone at a company you already have.
3. Check the company's name, or find the company they are at.
4. Press the blue button. It names what it will do.

Their paper comes with them. Nothing is copied.

A new customer opens the same form you use to add any customer. Save it and they are added.

If adding them fails, the window says why and stays open. Press the blue button again. The second press goes on from where the first one stopped, so nothing is made twice.

The line then reads **Customer** or **Supply house**. A signed paper's record carries the same press.

A job's contract is not sent here. Send it from its job.

## Take a paper back

A paper sent to an outside company by mistake can be taken back. Its line carries **Take back** until the paper is signed.

1. Find the paper under **Outside companies**.
2. Press **Take back** on its line.
3. Read the window. It names the paper and who holds its signing link.
4. Press **Take back the paper**.

The paper is deleted and its line leaves the list. The signing link stops working. Nobody is emailed.

Someone who opens the old link reads that it no longer works. The page tells them to ask the office for a new link.

:::example A waiting paper with an outside company
{{chip:yellow|never opened}} Sent Nov 18 {{button:outline|Send again}} {{button:outline|Copy the signing link}} {{button:outline|Take back}}
:::

A signed paper has no **Take back**. It is the record of what was agreed, so it is kept.

They may sign while the window is open. The paper is then kept, and the app tells you so.

Taking a paper back cannot be undone. To send the paper again, press **Send a paper** and start over.

The leader and a dev have **Take back**. They are the two who can delete a paper anywhere in the app. Anyone else in the office asks one of them.

The email that went out stays under Sent copies. The record that they opened the paper goes with it.

A teammate's or a sub's paper is not taken back here. It stays on People, under Contracts.

## Send a paper that was never sent

A paper can be made for an outside company and never sent. The list keeps it under **Not sent**.

The **Not sent** chip is in the second row. It shows only while such a paper exists. The number on it is how many there are.

1. Press **Not sent**.
2. Press **Send** on the paper's line. The Send window opens.
3. Fill in **Signer name** and **Signer email**.
4. Press **Send email**.

The line then reads *never opened*. It moves under **Waiting on a signature**.

:::example A paper that was never sent
{{chip:blue|Supply house}} {{chip:gray|W-9}} **W-9**, with Tallgrass Supply Co. {{chip:gray|not sent}} Made Nov 21. {{button:outline|Send}} {{button:outline|Take back}}
:::

Maybe the paper should not exist. The leader or a dev presses **Take back** on its line. Nobody holds a link to it, so nothing stops working.

Someone you typed in has no page of their own. This list is where their papers are found.

## Write or change a paper

Press **Contract library**, beside **Send a paper**. The library opens over the list.

The library is the same one People has under Contracts. Write a paper there, change one, or build a packet.

Press **Send to…** on a paper to pick the person right away.

## See a voided one

The list opens on **Sent and signed**. A voided contract is not in it.

Press **Voided or ended** to see them. That chip also holds a declined or cancelled work order and an offer that ran out. An agreement that a newer one replaced is there too.

## Open an agreement

Press the name on a line.

- A signed contract, warranty or their paper opens the Contract window on that record.
- A contract that waits on a signature opens the Contract window too.
- A voided contract opens as a preview in its own window.
- A signed staff, sub or partner agreement opens its signed record.
- One that is still waiting opens People, on Contracts, at that paper. The person's papers are open and that paper's line is marked. Press **Resend** on the line to send it again.
- A work order opens the work order window.
- A GC project's paper opens the GC page.

Press the green job name to open the job.

## Get a PDF of a signed paper

1. Press the name of a signed staff, sub or partner agreement.
2. Press **Open the PDF** on its signed record.

The PDF holds the words as they were signed and the signature. A paper signed before this was added gets its PDF the first time you open it.

A form opens its own PDF from the same record.

## The signer gets a copy

A person who signs an agreement you emailed gets their signed copy by email. The copy is a PDF.

The copy goes to the address the paper was sent to. A paper signed in person, with no address, emails nobody.

An hours statement, a W-9 and a certificate are never emailed.

## One paper over several jobs

A signed paper filed for several jobs is one line. It names the first job and says how many more it covers.

You can still find it by any of those jobs. Type one of their numbers in the box.

## A paper a job requires

A job can require papers before it opens, such as an NDA or site rules. Each person's copy is a line here once it goes out to sign. It shows as **Staff** or **Sub**, with the paper's own name and the job beside it.

Type the job's number to see every paper signed for that job. The job's own **Papers before this job opens** section shows who has not signed yet.

## See a company's papers on its own page

A supply house and a customer each list the papers that are with them.

- For a supply house, open [Materials](/materials?tab=supply-houses) and press **Edit** on its row. The list is under **Papers**.
- For a customer, open their page. The list is in the **Agreements** card, under **Papers with this customer**.

The list holds every kind of paper. A W-9 and an insurance certificate are there with the agreements.

Each line says who signs and where the paper stands. A paper that was made and never sent reads *not sent*.

:::example A supply house's papers
{{chip:blue|Agreement}} **Mutual secrecy agreement**, signed by Lena Park, Branch manager. {{chip:green|signed ✓}} Signed Nov 20.

{{chip:blue|W-9}} **W-9**. {{chip:gray|not sent}} Made Nov 21.
:::

Press the name of a signed paper to open its signed record. A paper that is not signed has no press.

Nothing is sent from these lists. To send a paper, press **Send a paper** on the Agreements tab.

## What is not on this tab

- A draft. A draft stays in the window that made it until it is sent.
- A job with no contract. That list is the Contract sweep, under Tools on the [Pipeline](/jobs?tab=stages).
- An accepted estimate. It is on the [Estimates](/documents?tab=estimates) tab.
- A teammate's or a sub's W-9, insurance certificate or licence. A person's papers stay on People. An outside company's are on this tab, and on that company's own page.
- A paper made for a teammate or a sub and never sent. It stays on People until it is sent.
- A lien waiver. It is under its job on the [Jobs](/documents?tab=jobs) tab.

## What an estimator sees

An estimator has the tab to read.

- The chips are **All**, **Outside companies** and **Work orders**.
- The list holds papers with a customer, a supply house or a trade company. A W-9 and a certificate are among them. Sub work orders are on the list too.
- A paper that was made and never sent reads *not sent*.
- A staff paper, a sub's own paper and a job's contract are not on it.
- A paper sent to someone on no list is not on it until the office adds them to a list.

An estimator has no **Send a paper** and no **Contract library**. Nothing is sent or taken back from an estimator's line.

### Open a signed paper

Press the name of a signed paper. Its signed record opens right there.

:::example An estimator's signed line
{{chip:blue|Supply house}} **Subcontractor Agreement**. With Tallgrass Supply Co., signed by Lena Park, Owner. {{chip:green|signed ✓}}
:::

The signed record lists the **Form answers** and draws the **Signature**. A sensitive answer, like a tax number, reads *in the PDF only*. Press **Open signed PDF** to read the whole form. Each open is logged.

A paper that is not signed yet has nothing to open. Its name is plain words.

A supply house's page and a customer's page open the same record.

### A training account

A training account reads the lines and opens nothing. The form answers, the signature and the signed PDF stay closed to a training account.

### Who says who someone is

Someone on no list can hold a paper. Only the office says who they are. That is the leader, an assistant, the controller or a dev.

An estimator who tries in Messages reads *They hold a paper, so only the office says who they are.* Ask the office to name them. An estimator can still name someone who holds no paper.

## Who can see the tab

The office can. That is the leader, the assistants, the controller and a dev.

An estimator can read it. The office sends, and an estimator does not.

Other roles do not see the tab. Nobody reads a paper here that they could not already open.

The Contract library keeps the rules it has on People. The leader and a dev can delete there. The controller and a dev see its **Forms** tab.

A GC project's papers follow GC mode. An owner contract shows to a dev. A statement of work shows to the leader, the controller and a dev.
