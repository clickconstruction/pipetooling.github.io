Every job's files live in one Drive folder under the jobs root. On the Documents page you set where that root is and how a job's folder is named.

## Open the tab

Open **Documents** and press **Where files go**. The tab is for the office. An estimator does not see it.

Three cards sit left to right. The first is the jobs root. The second is how a job's folder is named. The third is what is inside. On a phone the cards stack.

## Move the jobs root

The first card names the root folder with its path and the count of job folders inside. Under the name a green line says the app can write there. An amber line means it cannot, and says what to do.

To move the root, paste a Drive folder link into the field and press {{button:outline|Check}}. The app looks at the folder and answers in the same words. If the app can write there, press {{button:blue|Move the jobs root here}} and then {{button:blue|Move the root}}. New job folders are made under the new root from then on. The folders that exist stay where they are.

If the app cannot write in the folder, the amber line names the account to share it with. Share the folder with that account as an editor and press Check again.

## Name a job's folder

The second card shows the name as a row of pieces. As it ships the name is the job number and then the job name. Under the row, *Reads* shows the name a real job would get.

Press a dashed piece under **Add:** to put it at the end of the name. Press a dark piece to take it out. A name keeps at least one piece. To change the order, take a piece out and add it back. Press {{button:blue|Save the name}} when the example reads right, or **Undo** to go back.

The same row sits under *A bid's folder:* for the folder a bid gets when its plans come in.

A new name applies to folders made from then on. Nothing that exists is renamed.

## Choose when the folders are made

The third card draws the tree inside a job's folder. The five names inside stay fixed, because the photo and plan sweeps file by them.

Under the tree, pick one. **Make all of it when the job is made** makes every folder at once. **Make each folder when something first lands in it** makes the job's folder alone. Customer Pictures is made the first time a photo lands, and the photo files there. The choice saves when you press it.

A bid's folder is named by the bid pattern the same way. A bid that already has a folder keeps it.

## Make the folders for every job without one

Under the cards, {{button:blue|Make the folders for 14 jobs without one}} names how many live jobs have no folder. Press it once. The app makes each job's folder under the root and writes the links on the job. A job whose link is not a folder is held with the reason and a door to the job. A job that fails stays listed with its reason. The rest go through.

## Check a job's folder

Press **Check a job's folder** and type a job number or name. Press the job. The app looks at the folder on the job and answers in the same words as the root card.

Press **Check 41 pasted folders** to look at every live job's pasted folder in one run. After the run an amber line counts the jobs whose folder the app cannot write to. Press the line to read each one with its reason.
