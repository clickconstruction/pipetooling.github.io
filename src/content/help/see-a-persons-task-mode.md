You can open a window that shows a person's Task Mode screen. It draws their day the way their phone draws it. You stay signed in as yourself, and nothing in the window changes their tasks.

## Open the window

Go to **Checklist → Review** and find **Outstanding by person**. Press a person's card to open it. Under their tasks you see two presses.

:::example An open card
{{button:outline|＋ Add task for Dana}} {{button:outline|See Dana's Task Mode}}
:::

Press {{button:outline|See Dana's Task Mode}}. The window opens on that person's day.

The owner and a dev see this press. Other office roles see the board without it.

## What the window shows

At a desk the window draws a phone. On a phone the window fills the screen. Either way you see what the person sees.

:::cards

:::card Their day
The date and the green sentence of where the day stands. Then **Next**, **Then** and **Done today**.
:::

:::card Older tasks
**Still open** lists the older one-time tasks. A late task leads the list.
:::

:::card Waiting For
Roadmap steps that wait on an earlier step. They show under the older tasks.
:::

:::

## Coming up

Beside their screen the window lists **Coming up**. These are the person's open tasks for the next seven days.

:::example Coming up
**Tomorrow, Wed, Nov 18**
Check the shop compressor oil {{chip:gray|repeats}}
**Thu, Nov 19**
Meet the inspector at the duplex
1 repeating task again
:::

A repeating task is named on the first day it comes up. After that the day only counts it.

A task is not on a phone before its day. So the person does not see this list yet.

On a phone, press {{button:outline|Coming up}} in the bar under the amber line. Press {{button:outline|Their screen}} to go back.

A week with nothing dated says so. It offers {{button:outline|＋ Add task for Dana}}, which opens the New task window for that person.

## Reading it again

The top of the window says when it read the day. The window does not move after that. Press {{button:outline|Read again}} to read the day again.

## It is a look

An amber line under the title says **A look**. Every press on their screen is drawn, and none of them works. **Mark done** does nothing here. The note box takes no typing.

A press that only shows more still works. You can press a task's title to read its notes and see its photos. You can press **Show 5 more** to open the rest of the older tasks.

To act for the person, close the window and use the card on Review. That card still has mark done, **Forward** and **＋ Add task for**. [Give someone a task](/help/give-someone-a-task) covers adding one.

## What the window does not show

Some things are kept on the person's own phone. No other screen can read them. The window lists them beside the phone.

- Whether their phone has Task Mode switched on. The window always draws Task Mode.
- A task they lifted with **Do first**. The window shows the list's own order.
- How far each task is from them. The window shows the place with no distance.

The window also leaves out **Tagged notes**. Those are read only for the person who is signed in.

## This is not View as

**View as** in Settings signs you in as the person, and the app records it. Every page then works as them. This window is smaller than that. It reads their tasks and draws one screen.

[Use Task Mode to see only my checklist](/help/task-mode) explains the screen itself.
