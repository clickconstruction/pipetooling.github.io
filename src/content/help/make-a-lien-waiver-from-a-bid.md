A GC sometimes asks for a lien waiver while the work is still a bid. The **Lien Release** tab on Bids makes one from the bid. It uses the same four Texas forms as the job's Release of Lien window. A waiver on a billed job belongs in that window instead. That path is in [send a GC our lien waiver](/help/send-a-gc-our-lien-waiver).

## Open the tab

1. Go to [Bids](/bids) and click the **Lien Release** tab.
2. Pick the bid. Search by number, name, customer or GC.
3. The boxes fill from the bid. The amount is the bid's number. The payor is the bid's GC.

## Pick the form

Two switches pick the form.

- **Conditional** or **Unconditional**. Conditional means the check has not cleared yet. Unconditional means the money has settled.
- **Progress** or **Final**. Progress means more payments will follow. Final means the last payment.

The grey line under the switches names the Texas form. The line under it says when that form is right.

Clicking **Unconditional** asks first. A window says an unconditional waiver gives up all your rights. Most GCs will accept a conditional one, even when they ask for the other. {{button:blue|Stay conditional}} changes nothing. {{button:red|Acknowledge and choose Unconditional}} switches the form.

:::example The four forms
{{chip:yellow|Conditional · progress}} covers a payment on the way, with more to follow.
{{chip:yellow|Conditional · final}} covers the last payment, before its check clears.
{{chip:green|Unconditional · progress}} covers a payment that has settled.
{{chip:green|Unconditional · final}} says the job is paid in full. It releases everything.
:::

## Check the boxes

Every box is yours to change. A bid has no bills yet, so you type what the waiver covers.

- **Check from** names who pays. It starts as the bid's GC.
- **Amount** starts as the bid's number. Type what this release covers instead.
- **Project** starts as the bid's name and address.
- **Progress payments through** is the last day the waiver covers. A final form has no such box.
- **Signed by** names the leader who signs on the line. Type their title too.

The page beside the boxes redraws as you type. What you see is what prints.

## Send it out

Three buttons sit under the boxes.

- {{button:blue|Copy for email}} copies the document to paste into an email.
- {{button:outline|Print}} opens the letter for a printer.
- {{button:outline|Download PDF}} saves the same letter as a PDF.

## An unconditional waits for a leader

An unconditional waiver gives up rights. So the buttons above hold when one is picked. An amber note takes their place. It says the waiver only goes out over a leader's signature. Ask your leader. A leader or the controller sees the buttons and sends it. On a billed job the leader signs it in the [Release of Lien window](/help/send-a-gc-our-lien-waiver) instead.
