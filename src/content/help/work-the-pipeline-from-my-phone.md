On a phone the Pipeline opens on the list, one stage at a time, and every job is two lines. The desktop board is exactly as before.

## The stage chips

Under **Reports | Pipeline | Billing** a row of chips stays pinned as you scroll: {{chip:gray|Waiting 20}} {{chip:blue|Working 39}} {{chip:gray|Ready 0}} {{chip:gray|Billed 71}} {{chip:red|Coll. 7}}. Tap one and that stage's jobs show — only that stage. The app remembers which stage you left open on this phone.

Under the chips, three filters: **All**, **Needs me** (only the rows with a red or amber chip — the ones that want something from you) and **Today** (the rows with a block on the schedule today). A line under them says the stage's money, for example *Working · $393.4k · $42,334 capable of billing*.

## A job is two lines and one chip

:::example A row
**1040** Iannotti PRV · Sal Iannotti
NEXT Thu Sep 24 1–4 PM · Abraham · ✍ Signed · estimate #146 {{chip:gray|›}}
:::

The first line is the job and the customer. The second is the next scheduled block and who is on it (or who was last on site), then one fact — the signed agreement, when the bill is due, or how long the job has been open. On the right sits **at most one chip**, the first of these that applies:

- {{chip:red|no bid value}} — no line items on the job. Tap to add them.
- {{chip:red|set % done}} — a bill went out with no progress recorded.
- {{chip:red|quiet 15 d}} / {{chip:yellow|quiet 6 d}} — nothing has happened on the job past the stage's follow-up threshold.
- {{chip:yellow|12 d past expected}} — a Billed job is past the day its customer usually pays.
- {{chip:yellow|no contract}} — no agreement on file and none marked as not needed.
- {{chip:green|draw 2 ready}} — a stage draw can be billed now.
- {{chip:green|$4,200 done, not billed}} — finished work on no bill yet.

**Tap the chip** and it opens the fix — the line items, the job's Notes, the contract door. **Tap the row** to open the job window. **Press and hold** a row for the ⋯ sheet (Edit, Partial invoice, Send back, and the rest).

## Moving a job on: swipe right

No row carries a live status button. **Swipe a row to the right** and the stage's next move slides out — {{button:green|Ready to Bill ✓}} on a Working job, {{button:green|Move to Working ✓}} on a Waiting one, {{button:green|Bill Customer ✓}}, {{button:green|Mark Paid ✓}}. Let go and it **always asks first**:

:::example Ready to bill
**Ready to Bill · 1040 Iannotti PRV**
Working → Ready to bill · $600 capable · ✍ Signed · estimate #146 · Abraham's block Thu stays on the schedule
☑ I have reported all the Job Parts I've used
☐ The customer knows the work is done and is satisfied
{{button:blue|Confirm}} {{button:outline|Not yet}}
:::

The questions are the same ones the desktop asks. The line under the title is new: the money the move touches, the agreement on file, and the block it leaves on the schedule — so nothing moves by surprise. Bill Customer and Mark Paid open the same windows as on the desktop.

## The map and the money

They are still there, under the list: **Overview · map · money tiles · today's opportunities — Show ▾** at the bottom. Open it once and it stays open on this phone until you hide it. Typing in the search box shows every stage's matches with the stage names, as it always has.
