A job can require papers. An NDA, site rules or a GC's own form are common ones. Everyone signs each paper, the office too. You set them on the job's **Documents** tab.

## What locks

A person who owes a paper meets a prompt when they open the job. The prompt shows the job's number and the papers. It shows nothing else of the job until they sign. Nobody can clock in to the job before signing.

For the crew and subs the database holds the lock too. A helper, a sub, a superintendent or an estimator who owes a paper cannot read the job at all. That covers its notes, photos, plans, files and texts. Their lists show the job by its number and a lock. They get no push about that job until they sign.

Your own lists keep showing the job. So do a primary's. For the office and a primary the prompt at the job is the lock.

One place the app cannot close is Google Drive. A person the job's Drive folder is shared with can still read it there.

## Put a paper on a job

1. Open the job and go to **Documents**.
2. Find **Papers before this job opens**.
3. Press {{button:outline|Add a paper}}. The list shows the papers in the Contract Book.
4. Press each paper the job needs. It joins the list as *not saved yet*.
5. Press the blue button. It names its count, such as {{button:blue|Require 2 papers}}.

The line under the button counts the people who can open the job today. That is the job's crew, its subs and the office. You are one of them.

:::example A paper that cannot be picked
A paper with no words is grey in the list. Write it in the Contract Book first. A third-party PDF becomes a form in the Form Studio.
:::

## When the paper is the GC's own form

A GC may hand you a form of their own. Their PDF becomes a form in the Form Studio. Once the form is in the Contract Book, it shows in the **Add a paper** list.

1. Press {{button:outline|Add a paper}}.
2. Find the form's line.
3. Tick **This is the GC's own form** beside it. The form joins the list as *not saved yet*, with its tick on.
4. Press the blue button, such as {{button:blue|Require 1 paper}}.

The form's row then reads *The GC's own form*. The GC's page says the subs sign the GC's own form. It shows how many people have signed and each sub business with its day.

The tick stays on the form's row. Tick it or clear it there at any time. It is saved at once.

The tick changes what the GC's page says and never the form. Nobody signs again.

Only a form takes the tick. A paper built from settings has none. A paper in prose has none.

## Set a job's settings for a paper

Some papers are built from settings. Such a paper changes with the job. The list says *Built from settings* beside its name.

1. Press the paper in the **Add a paper** list. The settings page opens.
2. Read the notice and tick its box.
3. Pick the basis. That is the level the whole paper should express.
4. Read the note on the real site.
5. Go through the topics, one page each. Drag a topic's slider to move it off the basis.
6. On **Review**, type the words that are for this job only.
7. Press {{button:blue|Require this paper}}.

Each topic's page says what puts a person in breach. It says what the site must have too. A level the site cannot enforce protects nothing.

The button stays grey while a blank is empty. A line under it names the blank. Press that line to go to the blank.

The paper joins the job at once. It does not wait for the blue count button.

:::example A paper no attorney has read
A real job takes such a paper only after an attorney has read it. The page says so at the top and holds the last press. A ZZ Test job takes any version.
:::

## Change a job's settings

Press **Settings** on the paper's row. One page shows every topic's slider, the basis and the words typed for this job.

The button says what it will do before you press it.

- While nobody has signed, the change is saved in place.
- After someone has signed, the change makes a new version. Everyone signs the new version again. The job stays closed to each person until then.

The change can make the paper stricter or looser. Moving the basis alone asks nobody to sign again.

## Sign your own

Saving locks the job for you too. The prompt takes the place of the job at once. Press {{button:blue|Read and sign: Site rules}} with the paper's own name on it. Sign, then press **Back to the job**.

The prompt has one more press for the office. **This job's papers and who has signed** opens this same section from the prompt. Use it to take off a paper you added by mistake. You do not have to sign it first.

A **Papers to sign** line under **Needs you** also leads to the prompt.

## See who has signed

The table under the papers lists each person against each paper.

- {{chip:green|signed ✓}} shows with the day it was signed.
- {{chip:yellow|not yet}} means that person still owes that paper.

The line beside the heading counts it up, such as *3 of 8 have signed*.

## What the GC sees

The GC on the job reads about a paper built from settings on their own page. They see the level, each topic's choice in plain words, and how many people have signed.

- They see each sub business with the day it finished signing. They see no worker's name unless you set it.
- A sub's business is the company on their file. A sub with no company shows under their own name.
- After you change the settings, their count starts again, as yours does.
- **Download a sample** gives them the job's paper with made-up names and SAMPLE across each page.

You choose how much of that they see. Open **Settings** on the paper's row and find **What the GC sees**.

- **How many have signed** shows the count alone.
- **How many, and each sub business with its day** is how every paper starts.
- **How many, each sub business, and every signer by name** adds each person who has signed.

Then press {{button:blue|Save what the GC sees}}. This changes their page and never the paper. Nobody signs again. A new version of the paper keeps your choice.

A paper written in prose does not show there. The GC's own form shows there once you tick it. What their page looks like is in [see what a customer sees on their portal](/help/see-what-a-customer-sees-on-their-portal#a-paper-a-job-requires).

## When the GC proposes a stricter setting

The GC can propose a stricter setting from their page. It changes nothing until you take it.

The office hears of it on the Needs you card. The line reads *A GC proposed stricter settings*. {{button:outline|Open the job}} opens the job on its Documents tab. Nobody gets a push or an email.

A proposal shows under the paper's row. It names who proposed and the day. Each topic it moves has a line, such as *Ask first to None*.

1. Read each line.
2. Press {{button:blue|Take it}} to use the stricter settings.
3. To say no, press {{button:outline|Decline…}} and type why. {{button:blue|Decline the proposal}} stays grey until you type a reason.

The GC reads your reason on their page.

The blue button says who signs again before you press it.

- While nobody has signed, the settings are saved in place.
- After someone has signed, the take makes a new version. Everyone signs the new version again.

A GC can only make a topic stricter. One proposal waits on a paper at a time. A newer one takes its place.

Your own change of the settings sets a waiting proposal aside. Taking the paper off does the same.

## Text someone their signing link

1. Find the person's row in the table.
2. Press **Copy the signing link**. With two papers owed it says **Copy 2 signing links**.
3. Paste it into a text to them.

Each paper has its own link. A new link replaces the last one for that paper.

A row can say *No file is linked to this login*. That person cannot sign yet. Link their login to their file on **People**. Then come back and copy their link.

## A sub with no login

A sub with no login signs on their own page. The page shows a card for the job, such as *Work on J2391*. The card lists the job's papers and which are signed. The sub presses {{button:blue|Read and sign: Site rules}} with the paper's own name on it. After signing the sub presses **Back to your page**.

Until every paper is signed the sub's page holds the job back. The page shows the job's number and *Papers to sign first*. The page does not show the address, the scope, the plans link or any note. The sub's money stays on the page.

The sub cannot accept that job's work order before signing. The sub cannot pick its days or report on it either.

You can still text the sub a link. Find the sub's row in the table and press **Copy the signing link**.

## Take a paper off

Press **Take off** on the paper's row and confirm. Nobody is asked to sign it for this job any more. Every signature already on file is kept.

## Change a paper's words

The job keeps the words the paper had on the day you required it. Editing the Contract Book later does not change them. To use new words, take the paper off and add it again. Everyone then signs the new words.

A paper built from settings is different. Its words are read from the Contract Book each time someone opens it to sign. Each signed copy keeps the words that person signed.

## What the job's head shows

A job that requires papers shows a chip in its head, such as {{chip:yellow|2 papers to sign}}.
