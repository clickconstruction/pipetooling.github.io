A check on a stage checklist is a question the office wrote for the stage. Sometimes you cannot answer it. The part is not on the truck. The check is wrong for this job. The office has to decide. Send the check to Dispatch from its row. Dispatch reads the job, the stage and the check with your words. The answer comes back on the row.

## Open the check

In Job Mode, scroll to the job's details. Under **Stage checklists**, press the stage you are working. The sheet keeps one question open at a time. Tap a row to open it.

## Send it

Press **Send to Dispatch** under the open question. The press sits beside **Add a note**. A second sheet opens on top.

The top of the sheet shows what Dispatch will read. You do not type any of it. The card has the job's number and name, the address, the stage and the count. The check sits under the card with its details. If you already picked an answer or wrote a note, that is there too.

Under **Why**, press one chip. Pick one before you send.

:::cards

:::card Can't do it
The work cannot be done right now. Something on the job stops you. Dispatch sees this one first. It leads their inbox as a stopped crew.
:::

:::card Not on this job
The check does not apply here. Dispatch can skip it on this job.
:::

:::card Need a part
Something is missing. Say what it is in the box.
:::

:::card Need a decision
The office has to choose. Say what the choice is.
:::

:::card Something else
None of the chips fit. The box is required for this one.
:::

:::

Type what Dispatch should know in the box. Keep it short. The box is optional for every chip but **Something else**.

Press {{button:blue|Send to Dispatch}}. Dispatch gets a push on their phones. The check lands in their inbox with the job, the stage and the check on it.

## What you see after

The row now wears a chip like *With Dispatch · 2 min · need a part*. The chip stays while Dispatch has the check. The row still takes an answer. Dispatch may tell you to press **Not here**. You can do that any time.

One check goes to Dispatch once. If someone already sent it, the row wears the chip and the press is gone. The sheet says *Already with Dispatch* if two phones send at the same moment.

The office sees it too. On the Pipeline the stage's name wears a small flag instead of the box. The job's Documents tab shows the same chip on the check's row. The job's questions list marks it as a stage check.

## Read the reply

When Dispatch replies, your phone gets a push. The reply also sits under the row on the sheet.

:::example Under the row
{{chip:yellow|With Dispatch · 14 min · need a part}}

*Dispatch: "Hub pours Thursday. Press Not here for now." · Jo · 2 min ago*
:::

The request closes itself once the check is settled. Pick **Yes**, **No** or **Not here** and the chip goes. A skip on this job or a take off closes it too. The closed request says how. For example *Answered on the sheet by Luis Ortega · Yes*. Nobody has to close it by hand.

## What Dispatch does

Dispatch reads the check in their inbox with a *Stage check* tag. A **Can't do it** check sits at the top. A red line over it says how long it has waited. Dispatch presses **Open the job** to see the job. Dispatch presses **Open the check** to open the same sheet with your row open. There Dispatch answers it, adds a note, skips it on this job or takes it off. The guide [fill a stage checklist on the phone](/help/fill-a-stage-checklist-on-the-phone) has the sheet's steps.

Dispatch presses the row to open a window. The window shows the same card you saw and your words. There Dispatch presses **Reply**, or **Reply and close** to end the request with their words. **Lower priority** takes a **Can't do it** check off the top of their list. **Not now** closes the window and leaves the check as it was. The guide [answer a check the crew sent](/help/answer-a-check-the-crew-sent) has their side.
