The search box on **Jobs → Pipeline** filters every section of the board at once — counts, totals, and prints all follow what you type.

## What the search matches

Out of the box, typing matches the job's own fields:

- **Job number** — HCP or Click number
- **Job name**
- **Job address**
- **GC/Builder name** (see the GC guide)

:::example Jump straight to a job number
The **#** chip inside the search bar is a faster path when you already know the number — type the digits and press Enter to jump straight to that job's row. On a keyboard, just press **n** anywhere on the board (while nothing else is focused) and the field opens ready to type.
:::

## Optional: also search schedule and clock notes

Searching dispatch-schedule notes and clock-session notes (plus the people and dates on them) is available as an opt-in, because it is much heavier — the app has to pull schedule and clock records for every job on the board while you type.

1. Click the {{button:outline|⋯}} tools button at the right end of the search bar.
2. Turn on **Schedule &amp; time in search**.

While it's on, the search placeholder reads "Search HCP, name, address, schedule notes, or clock notes", and results can include jobs matched only by a schedule block or clock session. The setting is remembered per device, so turn it off again from the same menu when you're done.

## Optional: Mobile cards — a phone-friendly board

The same {{button:outline|⋯}} menu has a **Mobile cards** toggle. When it's on, every section renders as full-width cards instead of the wide desktop table — no sideways scrolling on a phone. Each card reads in three zones: the job number chip and name on one line with the section's stage button (like **Ready to Bill**) pinned top-right, then the customer and address together on one tappable line; a tinted **money strip** — the one place money appears — with the % done editor, bid, progress bar, the Billed / Paid / Left line, and (on billed jobs) the invoice email status with its **Resend** button; then an action row with **Reports**, the job/bill/hours chips, the phone icon, and the card's own {{button:outline|⋯}} button. The card ends with the green **Next** appointment chip and the latest activity on a single line — tap the card and the message thread opens right below them. The {{button:outline|⋯}} button slides up a sheet headed by the job and its **crew**, with **View job**, **Edit job**, **Activity and notes**, **Calendar**, **Assign work**, **Send to Dispatch**, **Send as task**, and the rest of the desktop row's tools (Plumbing Tooling, AIA, hazmat, lien, collections — whichever apply to that section). It's remembered per device, works with Edit mode's EDIT tabs, and flipping it off brings the classic table straight back.
