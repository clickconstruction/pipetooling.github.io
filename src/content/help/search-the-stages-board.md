The search box on Jobs → Pipeline filters every section of the board at once. Counts, totals and prints all follow what you type.

## What the search matches

Out of the box, typing matches the job's own fields:

- **Job number**, the HCP or Click number
- **Job name**
- **Job address**
- **GC/Builder name**, the general contractor. See the GC guide.

:::example Jump straight to a job number
The **#** chip inside the search bar is a faster path when you already know the number — type the digits and press Enter to jump straight to that job's row. On a keyboard, just press **n** anywhere on the board (while nothing else is focused) and the field opens ready to type.
:::

## Optional: also search schedule and clock notes

You can also search dispatch-schedule notes and clock-session notes, plus the people and dates on them. This is an opt-in, because it is much heavier. The app has to pull schedule and clock records for every job on the board while you type.

1. You click the {{button:outline|⋯}} tools button at the right end of the search bar.
2. You turn on **Schedule & time in search**.

While it is on, the search placeholder reads *Search HCP, name, address, schedule notes, or clock notes*. Results can include jobs matched only by a schedule block or clock session. The setting is remembered per device. You turn it off again from the same menu when you are done.

## Optional: Mobile cards — a phone-friendly board

The same {{button:outline|⋯}} menu has a **Mobile cards** toggle. When it is on, every section renders as full-width cards instead of the wide desktop table. There is no sideways scrolling on a phone. Each card reads in three zones. The first zone has the job number chip and name on one line. The section's stage button, like **Ready to Bill**, is pinned top-right. Then the customer and address sit together on one tappable line. The second zone is a tinted **money strip**, the one place money appears. It holds the % done editor, bid, progress bar and the Billed / Paid / Left line. On billed jobs it also holds the invoice email status with its **Resend** button. The third zone is an action row. It holds **Reports**, the job, bill and hours chips, the phone icon, and the card's own {{button:outline|⋯}} button. The card ends with the green **Next** appointment chip and the latest activity on a single line. You tap the card and the message thread opens right below them. A tap inside the thread leaves it open, even full screen. You tap the card above it to fold it again. The {{button:outline|⋯}} button slides up a sheet headed by the job and its **crew**. The sheet offers **View job**, **Edit job**, **Activity and notes** and **Calendar**. It also offers **Assign work**, **Send to Dispatch** and **Send as task**. It also offers the rest of the desktop row's tools. Those are Test report, AIA, hazmat, lien and collections, whichever apply to that section. The toggle is remembered per device. It works with Edit mode's EDIT tabs. Flipping it off brings the classic table straight back.
