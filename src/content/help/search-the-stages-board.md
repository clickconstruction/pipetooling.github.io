The search box on **Jobs → Stages** filters every section of the board at once — counts, totals, and prints all follow what you type.

## What the search matches

Out of the box, typing matches the job's own fields:

- **Job number** — HCP or Click number
- **Job name**
- **Job address**
- **GC/Builder name** (see the GC guide)

:::example Jump straight to a job number
The **#** chip next to the search is a faster path when you already know the number — type the digits and press Enter to jump straight to that job's row.
:::

## Optional: also search schedule and clock notes

Searching dispatch-schedule notes and clock-session notes (plus the people and dates on them) is available as an opt-in, because it is much heavier — the app has to pull schedule and clock records for every job on the board while you type.

1. Click the {{button:outline|⋯}} tools button to the right of the search.
2. Turn on **Schedule &amp; time in search**.

While it's on, the search placeholder reads "Search HCP, name, address, schedule notes, or clock notes", and results can include jobs matched only by a schedule block or clock session. The setting is remembered per device, so turn it off again from the same menu when you're done.
