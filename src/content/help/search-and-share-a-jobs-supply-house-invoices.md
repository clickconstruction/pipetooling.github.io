A job's supply house invoices sit together on its Job Summary. You can open one, search a long list, and copy a link to an invoice for a teammate.

## Open the list

:::steps

1. Open [Job Summary](/jobs?tab=job-summary) and press the job's row.
2. Open **Parts Cost**.
3. Open **Invoices from Supply Houses**.

:::

Each row shows the supply house, the invoice number, the date and the amount on this job. The cents are drawn smaller than the dollars. A date from this year shows no year, and an older date keeps its year.

## Open an invoice

Press the invoice number. The bill's PDF opens in a new tab.

A bill with no PDF reads {{chip:yellow|no PDF}} beside its number. Its number is plain text, since there is nothing to open. To add the PDF, see [record a supply-house bill from its PDF](/help/record-a-supply-house-bill-from-its-pdf).

## Copy a link to an invoice

Press the link icon left of the invoice number. A note at the bottom of the screen reads **Link copied to clipboard**. Paste the link into a text or an email to a teammate.

The link opens for the office alone. That is a dev, a leader, an assistant or the controller, signed in. Someone who is signed out is asked to sign in first. Anyone else reads **This file is not one you can open.**

Only the office sees the link icon. A bill with no PDF has no icon, since the link would have nothing to open.

## Search a long list

A list of eight or more invoices has a search box above it. Type any of these:

- A supply house's name, or part of it.
- An invoice number. Spaces and dashes do not matter.
- A day, such as **nov 16** or **11/16**.
- A month, such as **october**.
- An amount, such as **1,085.58**.

Type two words and a row must match both. The count beside the box says how many invoices are left. It also says what they add up to on this job.

:::example Find one house's October bills
Type **october** in the box. Then press the supply house's chip under it. The list shows that house's October invoices, with their count and their total beside the box.
:::

## See one supply house

Under the box is a chip for each supply house, with its number of invoices. Press a chip to see that house alone. Press **All** to see every house again.

When nothing matches, the list says so. Press **Clear the search** to see every invoice again.
