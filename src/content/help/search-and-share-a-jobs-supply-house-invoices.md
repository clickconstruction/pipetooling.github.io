A job's supply house invoices sit together on its Job Summary. You can flip through them without leaving the page, search a long list, and copy a link to an invoice for a teammate.

## Open the list

:::steps

1. Open [Job Summary](/jobs?tab=job-summary) and press the job's row.
2. Open **Parts Cost**.
3. Open **Invoices from Supply Houses**.

:::

Each row shows the supply house, the invoice number, the date and the amount on this job. The cents are drawn smaller than the dollars. A date from this year shows no year, and an older date keeps its year.

## Flip through the invoices

Press an invoice number. The bill opens over the page, with the job's list beside the paper.

- Press {{button:outline|Next ›}} or {{button:outline|‹ Previous}} to move one bill at a time.
- The right and left arrow keys do the same.
- Press any bill in the list to jump to it.
- Press **Esc** or the ✕ to close.

The bill's facts sit beside the paper. They show the supply house, the invoice number, the date, the bill total and what is on this job. They also show how many lines the app read from the paper, and whether the bill is paid.

One PDF sometimes holds several bills. The paper then shows this bill's pages alone. A line under it says which pages they are. Press **See the whole file** to read the rest.

Press {{button:outline|Open in Drive ↗}} to open the PDF in a tab of its own.

On a phone the bill fills the screen. **Previous** and **Next** sit at the bottom, and the facts sit under the paper.

:::example Check a week of bills
Type **nov** in the search box. Press the first invoice number. Then press the right arrow key to read each November bill in turn. The count at the top says where you are, such as 3 of 9.
:::

## When there is no paper

A bill with no PDF reads {{chip:yellow|no PDF}} beside its number. Open it and the facts still show. Press {{button:outline|Open the bill}} to add the PDF on the bill itself. See [record a supply-house bill from its PDF](/help/record-a-supply-house-bill-from-its-pdf).

Sometimes the app cannot open a file. The bill then says why. A file kept outside the bills folder is one reason. Press {{button:outline|Open in Drive ↗}} and Drive may still open it for you.

## Copy a link to an invoice

Press the link icon left of the invoice number. A note on the screen reads **Link copied to clipboard**. Paste the link into a text or an email to a teammate. The same icon sits at the top of an open bill.

The link opens that bill in the app, with the same job's list beside it. It opens for the office alone. That is a dev, a leader, an assistant or the controller, signed in. Someone who is signed out is asked to sign in first. Anyone else reads **This file is not one you can open.**

Only the office sees the link icon and the bill in place. Anyone else who sees the list opens a bill's PDF in a new tab.

## Search a long list

A list of eight or more invoices has a search box above it. Type any of these:

- A supply house's name, or part of it.
- An invoice number. Spaces and dashes do not matter.
- A day, such as **nov 16** or **11/16**.
- A month, such as **october**.
- An amount, such as **1,085.58**.

Type two words and a row must match both. The count beside the box says how many invoices are left. It also says what they add up to on this job.

The same box sits above the list in an open bill. **Previous** and **Next** follow the search, so you flip through what it left.

## See one supply house

Under the box is a chip for each supply house, with its number of invoices. Press a chip to see that house alone. Press **All** to see every house again.

When nothing matches, the list says so. Press **Clear the search** to see every invoice again.
