Each GC project card shows how its stage is going, under the project's name. It says whether the job is on track and the one thing to do next.

The strip shows once the job's ring on the board has read the job. Today the office sees it on jobs we are bidding. A dev sees it in every stage.

## Read the strip

- The chip says **On track**, **Watch** or **Behind**. The words beside it say why. The block takes the chip's colour.
- The numbers sit under the chip. They say how far the stage has come.
- **The clock** shows each day of the stage. Each week runs Sunday to Saturday. Its deadlines sit under their days.
- Under **The trades**, each trade is a row. Its left edge is green when done, amber when it needs a look, and red when late. Violet is our own crew.
- The row says who is asked, where the trade stands and the one thing to press. Its scope lines, its statement of work and its asks sit under it.
- Press the arrow at the end of a row to fold it. Press it again to open it.
- Press **Key** on The clock or The trades to see what its colours mean. Press **Hide the key** to put it away.

The rows about money show only to the money team.

## Do the next step

Press the **Next** button on the strip. It opens the part of the card or the window for that step.

A step you cannot open shows its words with no button. Ask a dev to open it for you.

## Set the start date

A job we are buying out with no start date asks for one on the strip.

1. Pick the day under **Set a start date**.
2. Press {{button:outline|Save}}.

The day also checks off its line on Get started. Only a dev sets it today.
