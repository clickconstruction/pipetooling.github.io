While we bid a GC job, a rough schedule says how many weeks it takes to build. Draw it from the stages of the job. Our number shows the weeks beside the price.

Only devs can open the schedule for now. The rest of the office gets the Schedule window later.

## Draw the rough

1. Open [GC projects](/gc).
2. Press {{button:outline|Schedule}} on the card of a job we are bidding.
3. Pick the day work would start under **If work starts**.
4. Pick a template under **Start from a template** if one fits. The line under it says how much it covers.
5. Press {{button:blue|Draw a rough schedule}}.

The card shows one row per stage, with its days, its dates and its bar. The weeks to build sit at the top. Nothing on the rough goes to the trades or the customer.

## Change a stage's days

Type a stage's own days in its box. Press {{button:blue|Redraw}}. The weeks follow the new draw.

## When our bid goes in

Press **We sent our bid** on the project's card. The rough keeps its weeks as they went with the bid. The rough's boxes and **Redraw** go away.

If the card says the weeks were not kept, open the schedule. Press **Keep the weeks as sent**. The weeks are kept with the day our bid went.

## When we win

The first draft starts from the rough. The first draft takes the rough's start day and its stage days. The card says how the first draft compares to the weeks we bid.
