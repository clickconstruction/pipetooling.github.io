Some GCs cost more to work for. The super changes the order of work after we mobilize. Pay apps come back twice. The office waits a week for an answer. Before, a master asked the estimator to add money to the price with no reason attached. Now the GC is rated once, on its record, in sentences. Every new bid to them reads the rating.

## Where the rating lives

Open the customer's page and find the card **How they are to work with**. It sits under the money strip, beside **Pays in kind**. A commercial customer has the card. So does any customer with a bid or a GC job.

The card shows one scale at a time. Each scale has rungs. A rung is a number of points and one sentence. Pick the sentence that fits them.

## Rate a GC

:::steps

1. Open the customer's page.
2. Under **Field and superintendent**, pick the sentence that fits how the site goes.
3. Under **Their office**, pick the sentence that fits how the paperwork goes.
4. Under **What happened**, write the job and the day in a sentence. The rating needs a reason.
5. Press {{button:blue|Save 3 points}}. The press names the points the picks add up to.

:::

Who may rate is set on the Bid Board's price list. See [set what difficulty costs](/help/set-what-difficulty-costs). The list starts with the office and the masters. An estimator reads the card and cannot save until the list names the estimators.

**Paying** is not picked. The app reads it from the GC's bills. See the next section.

## Paying is read from the bills

Under **Paying** the card says what it found. Paid in 52 days, the median of 7 bills. The GC pace here. How many bills in the last year went past their pay calendar. The rule sits under it. A point when the median is past 45 days, or two bills in a year are late. Fewer than three bills reads no point, and the line says so. The numbers are the Bid Board's price list's.

The point counts in the total. When you save, it goes into the rating with its words. When the bills later read a different point, an amber line says so and {{button:blue|Save 4 points}} wakes. Save again to read it in.

Press {{button:outline|Set by hand…}} when the bills do not tell it. Pick the points, write why, and press {{button:outline|Use this}}. The rating carries your reason. Press **Read from the bills again** to take it back.

## Rate from a bid

A master rates from the Bid Board, on a phone or at a desk. The sheet shows what the points do to that bid before you save.

:::steps

1. Open Bids and find the bid.
2. Press {{chip:yellow|Rated 3 points}} beside the GC. For a GC with no rating, open the row and press **Rate them**.
3. Pick the sentence that fits on each scale. **Paying** is locked. It is read from their bills.
4. Under **What happened**, write the job and the day in a sentence.
5. Read the box above the buttons. It says what your points add to this bid.
6. Press {{button:blue|Save 4 points}}.

:::

:::example The box before Save
**4 points · about +$5,150 in cost**
48 field hours, 12 office hours and the wait on their pay. About +$7,357 on the price at 30%.
Bids like this: won 2 of 3 to them, at 28 to 32%.
:::

The dollars are this bid's own. They come from its field hours, its labor rate and its price. A bid with no hours yet says it is not costed. The last line counts the decided bids to this GC for the same kind of work.

The save is the same rating as the card's. The newest rating stands. Press **Open their record** to see the earlier ratings or to set Paying by hand.

## The ask at the end of a job

When a job with a GC moves to **Ready to Bill**, the master on the job is asked once. The job's window shows a line at the top.

:::example The line in the job's window
This job is done. How was Seguin Ridge Builders to work with? {{button:blue|Rate them}} {{button:outline|Not now}}
:::

Press {{button:blue|Rate them}} to open the sheet. It names the job. Say what happened on it and save. Press {{button:outline|Not now}} to put the ask away. That job will not ask again.

The Bid Board shows the same asks in one place. Press **Rate GCs after a job** on the tools row. The press only shows when a job is waiting. One answer settles every job with that GC.

A GC that someone rated after the job was done is not asked about.

## What the points do

The points add up at the foot of the card. {{chip:yellow|Rated 3 points}} shows beside the GC's name on the Bid Board and on the Workbench. Hover the chip to read the sentences behind the points. On the Bid Board, press it to rate from that bid. On the Workbench, press it to open the card.

What a point costs is the Bid Board's price list. When the Pricing tab first costs a bid to them, the points become cost lines on the Labor tab. See [set what difficulty costs](/help/set-what-difficulty-costs).

## Rate again

The newest rating stands. Every earlier one is kept. Open **Earlier ratings** at the foot of the card to read them. To rate again, change a pick, write what happened and save. The press stays grey until a pick changes.

## When the criteria change

A dev can publish a new version of the scales. Your rating keeps its own version and its points. The card carries each pick to the new version where the sentence still exists. A sentence the dev removed leaves that scale unrated. The card says so in an amber line. Pick again and save to rate under the new version.

## A GC with no rating

The Bid Board shows **Rate them** beside a GC with no rating, on an opened row. Press it to rate from that bid. The chip reads **Not rated yet** on the card until someone saves.
