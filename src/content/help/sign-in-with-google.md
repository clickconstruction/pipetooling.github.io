You can sign in with your Google account in place of your password. It opens the same account, with the same role and the same work.

## Sign in

1. Open the sign-in page.
2. Press {{button:outline|Continue with Google}}.
3. Pick your Google account.

Google sends you back signed in. You land on the page you asked for.

## Which Google account works

Google must sign you in with the same email address the office has for you here. That is the address you type on the sign-in page today.

A Google account on another address opens nothing. The page says **No account uses that Google address**. Use the right Google account, or ask the office to check the address on your account.

:::example Nobody gets an account by pressing the button
Continue with Google signs in a person who already has an account. It never makes a new one. The office adds people.
:::

## Your password still works

Signing in with Google changes nothing about your password. You can use either one on any day. The emailed sign-in link still works too. The guide [sign in when my password won't work](/help/sign-in-when-my-password-wont-work) walks through that.

## When the button is not there

The office can turn Google sign-in off for everyone. The button then leaves the sign-in page. Sign in with your email and password.
