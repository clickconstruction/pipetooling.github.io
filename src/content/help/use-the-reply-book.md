The **Reply book** is one place for the wording the team sends more than once. Everyone who opens the Bid Board reads it, copies from it and adds to it.

You open it from Bids → Bid Board → {{button:outline|Reply book}}. A reply might be a decline for a project too far from the office. A reply might also be a follow-up a week after a bid went out. Or a reply might ask for the plumbing sheets.

## Copy a reply

You open the Reply book, find the reply, and press {{button:blue|Copy}}. You paste it where you are writing: your email, a bid note, or a message to the GC. The GC is the general contractor. Then you change what this bid needs, like the hours or the project name.

**The copy is signed with your name.** The wording in the book has no name on it. When a reply ends with *Thank you,* the name under it is whoever pressed Copy. The card shows that name in a yellow chip before you copy. Nobody sends a letter signed by someone else.

To find a reply, you type in the search. The search reads what the reply is for, the wording, and who posted it. Or you tap a kind:

{{chip:gray|All 4}} {{chip:gray|Declining 2}} {{chip:gray|Following up 1}} {{chip:gray|Asking for something 1}} {{chip:gray|After a decision 0}} {{chip:gray|Other 0}}

## Add a reply

You press {{button:blue|+ Add a reply}}.

- **What is it for?** A line someone else will recognize in a list, like *Declining: too far from the office*.
- **Kind**: which chip it sits under.
- **The wording**: you paste it as you sent it. If the wording ends with a closing and your name, the form says what it will leave off. The form's note reads like *Left off when it is saved: "Thank you, Wendi"*.
- **End with "Thank you," and the name of whoever copies it**: you leave it ticked for a letter. You untick it for wording that is not a letter, or that has a closing of its own to keep.

Then you press {{button:blue|Post to the book}}. The reply is in the book for everyone at once.

:::example share the decline you just sent
You turned down a project four hours from the office and the wording was right. Open **Reply book**, press **+ Add a reply**, call it *Declining: too far from the office*, paste the email, and post it. The next estimator who has to decline for distance copies yours, changes the hours, and sends it under their own name.
:::

## Change or delete a reply

{{button:outline|Edit}} and {{button:outline|Delete}} show on the replies **you** posted. Your replies carry a blue edge and read *You*. A dev sees them on every reply. Delete asks first. A deleted reply is gone for everyone and cannot be brought back.

If someone else's reply needs a fix, you ask them or a dev. A reply whose author no longer has an account stays in the book under their name. A dev can change it.

## On a phone

The Reply book button on the board is its icon, a speech bubble, beside the Customer review icon. In the window, **Copy** is the wide button under each reply.
