The **Reply book** (Bids → Bid Board → {{button:outline|Reply book}}) is one place for the wording the team sends more than once: a decline for a project too far from the office, a follow-up a week after a bid went out, a request for the plumbing sheets. Everyone who opens the Bid Board reads it, copies from it and adds to it.

## Copy a reply

Open the Reply book, find the reply, and press {{button:blue|Copy}}. Paste it where you are writing — your email, a bid note, a message to the GC — and change what this bid needs (the hours, the project name).

**The copy is signed with your name.** The wording in the book has no name on it. When a reply ends with *Thank you,* the name under it is whoever pressed Copy, shown on the card in a yellow chip before you copy. Nobody sends a letter signed by someone else.

To find a reply, type in the search — it reads what the reply is for, the wording, and who posted it — or tap a kind:

{{chip:gray|All 4}} {{chip:gray|Declining 2}} {{chip:gray|Following up 1}} {{chip:gray|Asking for something 1}} {{chip:gray|After a decision 0}} {{chip:gray|Other 0}}

## Add a reply

Press {{button:blue|+ Add a reply}}.

- **What is it for?** — a line someone else will recognize in a list: *Declining: too far from the office*.
- **Kind** — which chip it sits under.
- **The wording** — paste it as you sent it. If it ends with a closing and your name, the form says what it will leave off (*Left off when it is saved: "Thank you, Wendi"*).
- **End with "Thank you," and the name of whoever copies it** — leave it ticked for a letter. Untick it for wording that is not a letter, or that has a closing of its own to keep.

Then {{button:blue|Post to the book}}. It is in the book for everyone at once.

:::example share the decline you just sent
You turned down a project four hours from the office and the wording was right. Open **Reply book**, press **+ Add a reply**, call it *Declining: too far from the office*, paste the email, and post it. The next estimator who has to decline for distance copies yours, changes the hours, and sends it under their own name.
:::

## Change or delete a reply

{{button:outline|Edit}} and {{button:outline|Delete}} show on the replies **you** posted — they carry a blue edge and read *You* — and on every reply for a dev. Delete asks first; a deleted reply is gone for everyone and cannot be brought back.

If someone else's reply needs a fix, ask them or a dev. A reply whose author no longer has an account stays in the book under their name; a dev can change it.

## On a phone

The Reply book button on the board is its icon (a speech bubble), beside the Customer review icon. In the window, **Copy** is the wide button under each reply.
