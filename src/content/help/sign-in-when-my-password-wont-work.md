If your password isn't working, the sign-in page can email you a one-time sign-in link instead. You need no password to use it.

## Get the email link

1. On the sign-in page, enter your **email** and try your password.
2. After **two failed tries** with the same email, a blue box appears: *"Trouble signing in? We can email you a one-time sign-in link instead."*
3. Press {{button:outline-blue|Email me a sign-in link}}.
4. Open your email and tap the link. You'll land in the app already signed in.

:::example Open the link on the device you want to use
The link signs in whichever browser opens it. If you want to be signed in on your phone, open the email **on your phone** — not at a desk computer.
:::

## You land on the page you asked for

Did a link or a scanned code send you to the sign-in page? The app keeps the page you asked for. Sign in with your password or with the emailed link. The app then opens that page. A job, a guide and the code on a lien help sheet all open this way.

The app keeps the page for one hour. Past the hour, scan the code or open the link again.

Did you open the app with no page in mind? Then you land on your usual first page.

## If the email doesn't arrive

- Check your spam folder.
- The **Resend link** button unlocks after a one-minute wait. Email limits mean back-to-back sends don't go through.
- If it says *"No account found for that email"*, the email you typed isn't on an account. Double-check the spelling, or contact the office to get set up.

## Afterward

The link signs you in once. Your password is unchanged. If you've forgotten it for good, contact the office and they'll help you set a new one.
