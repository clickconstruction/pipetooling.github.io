A **team lead** is someone who sees and approves their members' hours from **Dashboard → My Team**. Any account can be a leader or a member, and a member can be on more than one leader's team.

## Where to manage them

The same manager appears in two places — use whichever is closer:

- **People → Users** → the {{button:outline-blue|Team leads}} button beside the search box (opens a window).
- **People → Teams** tab.

You'll see one card per leader, like {{chip:blue|Maria Lopez}} **· 3 members**. Click a card to expand or collapse it, and use the search box to find a leader *or* a member by name.

## Add and remove members

- {{button:blue|+ New leader}} starts a new team: pick the leader, pick their first member, and press **Create**.
- Inside a card, **+ Add member** links another person to that leader. The picker skips people already on the team.
- The **×** next to a member removes that link (you'll be asked to confirm). Removing a link never deletes anyone's hours — it only stops the leader from seeing them.

:::example Someone left the company?
Members whose accounts were archived show muted with an **(archived)** tag and a **Remove stale link** button, so it's easy to tidy up old teams.
:::

## Full vs Strip

Each member row has a **Full / Strip** switch that controls what the leader sees for that member on their Dashboard:

- **Full** — the whole My Team section: hours, approvals, details.
- **Strip** — just the clock strip (who's clocked in), nothing else.

Only a developer can change this switch; everyone else can see the current setting.
