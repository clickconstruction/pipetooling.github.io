Utilization shows the hours each person said they can work against the hours the clock recorded. It says who is over their hours, who is light, and when the crew itself is full.

## Where it is

Open **People**, stay on the **People** group and press {{chip:blue|Utilization}}. You see the tab if you can read other people's clock sessions. That is a dev, a controller, an assistant or a leader with pay approval.

## Read the lines first

The box at the top says the week in words. One line gives the field crew's percent of its hours last week. One line names the people over their hours three weeks running. One line names the people under 60 percent four weeks running. One line counts the people who have not drawn their week yet.

The tiles under the lines count the field crew. **Over their hours** and **Light** count last week. **Days over 8 h** counts the whole window. **Not asked yet** counts the people the board still reads at an assumed 40 hours.

## Read a row

The field crew and the office are two lists. Each row is one person.

- **Can work** is the hours they can work in a week. Amber *assumed, not asked* means nobody has drawn their week yet, so the board reads 40.
- The bars are the last eight pay weeks. A pay week runs Sunday to Saturday. The dashed line is 100 percent. Red is over, green is full, blue is steady, amber is light.
- **Last week** reads worked hours against the hours they could work, with the percent under it.
- **Days over 8 h** counts the days past eight hours in the window.
- **Outside the drawn hours** counts the hours the clock ran outside the week they drew. It reads *not drawn* until someone draws their week.
- **Read** is the verdict. *Over three weeks running* is the sign to add people. *Light four weeks running* is the sign to use them more, or to sell more.

Press {{button:outline|Weeks}} on a row to open every week with its hours, its days off and its long days. Press {{button:outline|Set hours}} or {{button:outline|Change}} to draw the week for that person.

## Draw a week for someone

The window shows Sunday to Saturday as columns and 5 AM to 8 PM as rows. Press and sweep to paint the hours the person can work. Sweep over blue to erase. A day's name turns the whole day on or off. The chips under the grid open two time boxes for an exact start and end.

Pick which Sunday the drawing holds from, this week or next. The weeks before keep the answer that held then. A first answer reads the weeks before it too. Press {{button:blue|Save 40 h a week}} and the row reads the new hours.

People can draw their own week from **My Time** on their dashboard. The office's drawing and theirs land in the same place.

## Watch-outs

- The board counts recorded time. That is every closed session approved or waiting, plus the one on the clock right now.
- Salaried people stay on the schedule's hours and out of the counts. They join the counts only when pay lets them record hours and they punched.
- Office people clock unevenly. A light office run is never a verdict.
- Company holidays have no record in the app, so a holiday week reads light for everyone.
- Recorded days off come out of a person's week at that day's drawn length.
- Someone who left inside the window keeps the weeks they worked, marked *Left the roster*.
