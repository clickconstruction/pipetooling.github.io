A leader who writes up a change in the field snaps photos into app storage. You move those photos to the customer's Google Drive from Quickfill.

A leader writes up a change order from the field with **Write up a change**. Any photos they snap land in app storage. They cannot reach Google Drive from a job site. All customer photos belong in Google Drive long-term. So the photos wait for you on Quickfill until you move them over.

## Work the section

1. Open **Quickfill** and find **Field photos → Drive**. Each card is one write-up that still has photos in app storage. The cards run oldest first. Each card has a link to the estimate.
2. Click each photo to open it full size, and save it. Put the photos in the **customer's Google Drive folder**. That is the same Customer Pictures folder the job uses.
3. Paste the Drive folder link into the card. Press {{button:green|Replace photos with this link}}.

:::example What that does
The estimate now shows **"Field photos — moved to Google Drive"** linking to the folder, the photos are deleted from app storage, and the card disappears. The handover records who moved them and when.
:::

## Good to know

- The card links straight to the estimate. You can check the write-up while you file the photos.
- If the customer does not have a Drive folder yet, create it first. This flow is what keeps every photo findable in one place.
- The section shows a green check when nothing is waiting.
