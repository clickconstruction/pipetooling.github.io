When a master writes up a change order from the field with **Quick Estimate**, any photos they snap land in app storage — they can't do Google Drive from a job site. All customer photos belong in Google Drive long-term, so the photos wait for you on Quickfill until you move them over.

## Work the section

1. Open **Quickfill** and find **Field photos → Drive**. Each card is one write-up that still has photos in app storage, oldest first, with a link to the estimate.
2. Click each photo to open it full size, and save it. Put the photos in the **customer's Google Drive folder** (the same Customer Pictures folder the job uses).
3. Paste the Drive folder link into the card and press {{button:green|Replace photos with this link}}.

:::example What that does
The estimate now shows **"Field photos — moved to Google Drive"** linking to the folder, the photos are deleted from app storage, and the card disappears. The handover records who moved them and when.
:::

## Good to know

- The card links straight to the estimate, so you can check the write-up while you file the photos.
- If the customer doesn't have a Drive folder yet, create it first — this flow is what keeps every photo findable in one place.
- The section shows a green check when nothing is waiting.
