Every guide in Help has a share link that looks right when you text it or paste it into a chat: the guide's question as the title, its first line underneath, and the ClickTooling card as the picture.

## Copy the link

1. Open the guide in **Help**.
2. Under its title press {{button:outline|Copy share link}}. The link is copied — it looks like `clicktooling.com/g/split-a-job-into-stages/`.
3. Paste it into a text, an email, or a chat. Whoever taps it lands on that guide after signing in.

:::example What the person receiving it sees
A card with the ClickTooling mark, **How do I split a job into stages and bill stage by stage?**, the guide's first sentence, and *clicktooling.com* — not a bare grey link.
:::

## Good to know

- The link works for anyone with an account; the guide itself still opens inside the app, so they sign in first if they aren't already.
- If you copied the address bar instead (`/help?g=…`), that works too, but it previews as a plain ClickTooling card rather than the guide's own title. Use the share link when the preview matters.
- Phones cache previews for a while. If a link you shared earlier still shows the old look, share the `/g/…` link instead.
