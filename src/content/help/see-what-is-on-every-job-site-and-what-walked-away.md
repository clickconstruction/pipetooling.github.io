The Materials tab on Jobs is the office side of the cages and boxes on a big site. It reads the same ledger the crew writes on the phone. You see what came in, what is on site, what was used and what is missing, job by job.

## Open the desk

Open **Jobs** and press **Materials**. The desk lists every job with a container and sorts the ones losing money to the top. Press a job to open its page.

The Pipeline's tools menu has a **Materials desk** row with the count of jobs that lost something this week. A job's window has a **Materials** tab with the same facts and **Open the materials page**. A bid's Procure step has the same door once the bid is a job. The line under its four steps reads **Materials on site**.

The Needs you card on the Dashboard carries two lines for the office. One names the money of material that walked away this week across every job. The other counts the boxes not counted in 14 days. Press **Open the desk** on a line to see the jobs. When one job holds every row, the press reads **Open the job** and opens that job's page.

## Read the row

Under the job's name one row reads the material's path left to right. It reads {{chip:blue|1,250 came in}} → {{chip:green|1,105 on site}} → {{chip:gray|120 used}} → {{chip:yellow|25 missing}}. Each figure is in its own colour. An amber badge counts what waits for a hand. A red badge is the money that walked away this week. Press a figure to open its section.

A very large job can pass 5,000 rows in its ledger. Then History and the boxes show the newest 5,000 rows. The totals come from the database, and a line under the job's name says so.

## On site

The boxes sit grouped by their place on the site. **All**, **Due a count** and **Can't find** narrow them. **List** and **Map** at the row's end switch between the boxes and the site map with their pins.

Press {{button:outline|Add a container}} to add a cage or box with its stencil and its place. On a line, **Correct…** sets the count the crew should have found. It writes a count row in your name.

## Missing

The table holds one row per part. It reads what was bought and what was added on site. Then what was used, returned, taken or damaged, and what the crew could not find. Then what is still on site and what walked away. Below it, **Reported this week** lists each short count and each Can't find with the person and the day.

The money uses the parts book's price. Beside each figure a small chip reads {{chip:gray|parts book · checked Oct 3}}, the day someone last checked that price. The chip turns amber when the check is older than 90 days. Then the price typed off the invoice that landed the part stands in, and the chip reads {{chip:gray|invoice price}}. A part typed in by name on site has no page in the book, so it keeps the invoice price. A part with no price at all shows its count alone.

## Land a delivery

Open **Deliveries**. Press **Open the job's Drive folder** and drop the invoice there. The app reads it from the folder.

A job with no folder yet shows {{button:blue|Make the job’s Drive folders}}. Press it once. The app makes the job's folder under Jobs with Customer Pictures, Plans and Team only inside. The office sees it, and a box photo from the phone files there by itself. On the desk, **Make folders for 12 jobs** does the same for every live job without one. A job whose link is not a folder is held with the reason. A job with a folder but no pictures or plans link has the same press on its window's Documents tab. It fills in what is missing.

To land it now, press **Paste the text instead** and paste the invoice's lines. Press {{button:blue|Read the lines}} and look them over. Press {{button:blue|Add 12 lines to Unsorted as a delivery}}. The parts wait in Unsorted for the crew to put away.

## Print a label

Press **Label** on a box to print that box alone, or **Print labels** for every box on the job. A label carries the stencil, the job, the place, a small code and ruled lines to write on. The crew's camera opens the box from the code.

## Print a manifest

A manifest is the paper that hangs on a box and says what is in it. Press **Print the manifest** on a job's page. The window opens on every box. Press a stencil to leave that box out. **None** clears them and **Every box** picks them all again. The press names its count, such as {{button:blue|Print 2 manifests}}.

Each box prints on its own Letter page in large plain type. The page carries the stencil, the job and the site. It carries the same code the label has, so the crew's camera opens the box from the manifest. The page shows the day the manifest was printed. The page also shows the day something was last recorded in the box, and by whom. Under the dates it lists what is in the box with each number. Ruled lines at the foot take what is added on site by hand. Each line has room for the item, the quantity, who added it and the day.

Laminate the page and hang it on the box in a document holder. The manifest is a plain print and files no copy. When someone writes a line on it, press the same thing in the app so the count stays true.

For every cage and box across a development, print the [material location report](/help/print-the-material-location-report) from the Development page.

## Bring back a retired box

A job's container list ends with a fold named Retired. Open it to see every cage or box retired on the job, with the day it left. Press **Bring back G4** and the box is live again. Its QR label works as before, and nothing in its history changes.

The press is withheld when a live box on the job already carries that stencil. The fold says which one, so no job carries two G4s. Retire the live one first, or give it another stencil.

## Find a box from anywhere

Open the search box at the top of the app and type a stencil, a part or a place. **Boxes on site** lists the box with its job. Press it to open the job's materials page, or press **Open on the phone screen** for the box itself.
