The Materials tab on Jobs is the office side of the cages and boxes on a big site. It reads the same ledger the crew writes on the phone. You see what came in, what is on site, what was used and what is missing, job by job.

## Open the desk

Open **Jobs** and press **Materials**. The desk lists every job with a container and sorts the ones losing money to the top. Press a job to open its page.

The Pipeline's tools menu has a **Materials desk** row with the count of jobs that lost something this week. A job's window has a **Materials** tab with the same facts and **Open the materials page**.

## Read the row

Under the job's name one row reads the material's path left to right. It reads {{chip:blue|1,250 came in}} → {{chip:green|1,105 on site}} → {{chip:gray|120 used}} → {{chip:yellow|25 missing}}. Each figure is in its own colour. An amber badge counts what waits for a hand. A red badge is the money that walked away this week. Press a figure to open its section.

## On site

The boxes sit grouped by their place on the site. **All**, **Due a count** and **Can't find** narrow them. **List** and **Map** at the row's end switch between the boxes and the site map with their pins.

Press {{button:outline|Add a container}} to add a cage or box with its stencil and its place. On a line, **Correct…** sets the count the crew should have found. It writes a count row in your name.

## Missing

The table holds one row per part. It reads what was bought and what was added on site. Then what was used, returned, taken or damaged, and what the crew could not find. Then what is still on site and what walked away. Below it, **Reported this week** lists each short count and each Can't find with the person and the day.

The money uses the price typed off the invoice that landed the part. A part with no price shows its count alone.

## Land a delivery

Open **Deliveries**. Press **Open the job's Drive folder** and drop the invoice there. The app reads it from the folder.

A job with no folder yet shows {{button:blue|Make the job’s Drive folders}}. Press it once. The app makes the job's folder under Jobs with Customer Pictures, Plans and Team only inside. The office sees it, and a box photo from the phone files there by itself. On the desk, **Make folders for 12 jobs** does the same for every live job without one. A job whose link is not a folder is held with the reason. A job with a folder but no pictures or plans link has the same press on its window's Documents tab. It fills in what is missing.

To land it now, press **Paste the text instead** and paste the invoice's lines. Press {{button:blue|Read the lines}} and look them over. Press {{button:blue|Add 12 lines to Unsorted as a delivery}}. The parts wait in Unsorted for the crew to put away.

## Print a label

Press **Label** on a box to print that box alone, or **Print labels** for every box on the job. A label carries the stencil, the job, the place, a small code and ruled lines to write on. The crew's camera opens the box from the code.

## Find a box from anywhere

Open the search box at the top of the app and type a stencil, a part or a place. **Boxes on site** lists the box with its job. Press it to open the job's materials page, or press **Open on the phone screen** for the box itself.
