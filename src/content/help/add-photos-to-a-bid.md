A bid can hold photos. A site walk's pictures sit on the bid and in the bid's Drive folder. The Bid Board shows a photos icon on every bid.

## Find the photos icon

1. Open Bids. The Bid Board opens.
2. Look in the **Links** column. The camera is the first icon on every row.

A grey camera means the bid has no photos yet. A blue camera means the bid has photos. The number beside it is how many.

Every icon in the column keeps its own place. The camera comes first, then the folder, the plans, CountTooling and the bid submission. A link the bid does not have shows grey. See [see what files a bid has](/help/see-what-files-a-bid-has).

On a phone the camera sits on the bid's card, under the name.

## Add photos at a desk

1. Press the camera. The **Files** window opens with the bid's photos at the top.
2. Drag photos onto the photos. They turn blue and read **Drop photos here**. Or press {{button:blue|Pick files}} and choose them.
3. Each photo shows as a tile while it is made smaller and sent.
4. Type a name in the box **What is it?** The name is optional.
5. Read the line under the tiles. It says where the photos will go.
6. Press the blue button. It says what it will do, with its count.

:::example The first photos on a bid
{{button:blue|Make the folder and add 3 photos}}
:::

The first time on a bid, the button makes the bid's photo folder. After that it reads {{button:blue|Add 3 photos}}.

The button waits while a photo is still sending. A photo that did not send says why and offers **Retry**.

Close the window without pressing the button and the new photos are let go.

## Add photos on a phone

1. Press the camera on the bid's card.
2. Press {{button:blue|Take a photo}}. The phone's camera opens.
3. Take the photo. It shows as a tile in the window.
4. Press **Take another** for the next one.
5. Press the blue button to add them.

Press {{button:outline|Pick from this phone}} to choose photos you already took.

## With no signal

A site walk often has no signal. You can still take the bid's photos.

1. Open the bid's photos before you lose the signal, if you can. The Bid Board must already be on the screen.
2. Press {{button:blue|Take a photo}} and take the photo.
3. The tile reads **Kept on this phone.** The photo is safe on the phone.
4. Type a name if you want one.
5. Press the blue button. It reads {{button:blue|Add 1 photo}}, with its count.

:::example A photo taken with no signal
{{chip:yellow|Kept on this phone.}}

{{button:blue|Add 1 photo}}
:::

The window then says how many photos wait. It reads **1 photo waits on this phone.** The line at the top of the screen counts them too. It reads **Bid photos**, then the time, then **waiting to send**.

The photos go to the bid by themselves when the signal is back. You do not take them again. Each one is sent once.

Press the blue button before you close the window. A kept photo you did not add is let go, as any new photo is.

The window may open with no signal. It then says it could not load, and it still offers {{button:blue|Take a photo}}.

A waiting photo stays on the phone if you close the app. It is sent the next time the app is open with a signal. Keep the app open until the line at the top reads **sent**.

Do not clear the browser's data while a photo waits. That removes the photo. Read [keep working when the signal drops](/help/keep-working-when-the-signal-drops) for the line at the top of the screen.

## What you can add

Photos only, for now. A video or another file in the pick is left out. The window says which one and why.

Each photo is made smaller before it goes up. A site photo stays easy to read.

## Where the photos go

The photos go to the bid's project folder in Drive. Inside it they sit under **Team only**, in **Photos**. A picture saved from a text onto the bid lands in the same place.

A bid with no project folder gets one. The app makes it in the company's shared Drive and fills in the bid's folder link.

A photo is in the app the moment you add it. It reaches Drive within five minutes.

## When a photo is not in Drive yet

A tile that reads **Not in Drive yet** is still on its way. Most of the time it files within five minutes.

Sometimes the bid's folder is one the app cannot save into. A folder in a personal Drive is the usual cause. The window then says the photos are safe in the app, and why they wait.

Do what the window says. Move the folder into the company's shared Drive, or share it with the address the window names. The photos file themselves after that. The app tries again every hour.

## See the bid's photos

The **Photos** row shows every photo on the bid, newest first. Under each one is its name, who added it and the day.

A photo someone put in the Drive folder by hand shows too. It takes its file name.

A picture the app filed from a text or a report shows by its caption, with who sent it.

Press a photo to see it large. Press **‹** or **›** for the one before or after. Press {{button:outline|Open in Drive ↗}} to open the file in Drive.

Press {{button:outline|Open in Drive ↗}} over the grid to open the whole photo folder.

## Who can add photos

Anyone who can open the bid can see its photos and add one. A primary sees the photos on their own bids.

A training account can look at a bid's photos. It cannot add one.

## After the bid is won

Nothing moves. The bid's folder becomes the job's folder, and the photos stay in **Team only**, in **Photos**.

A job has photos of its own too. See [add a photo to a job](/help/add-a-photo-to-a-job).
