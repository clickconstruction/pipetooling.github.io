A task card takes photos of the work. A photo is offered each time you set a percent. Some cards ask for a photo at the finish. Every photo files in Drive under the Tasks folder.

## A photo at a percent

Tap the ring on the card. Pick **25**, **50** or **75**. Tap {{button:outline|📷 A photo of where it stands}}. On a phone the camera opens. On a computer the file picker opens. The photo shrinks on your device. Tap the blue **Set** press. The photo sends first and the percent follows. You can skip the photo. The press never waits for it.

## The finishing photo

Some cards ask for a photo at the finish. The person who wrote the card set that. The finish opens a sheet. It opens when you tick the last line. It also opens when you tap **✓ Done** on the percent sheet. Tap {{button:outline|📷 The finishing photo}} and take it. Then tap {{button:green|Done · 100%}}.

The press reads **Photo first** until a photo is in. No camera at hand? Type why in the **No photo, here is why** line. The press frees. Your reason lands on the card's notes. A card that does not ask for a photo closes without the sheet.

## Where the photos go

The card shows its photos as a strip under the lines. The strip says *on its way* until the photo is in Drive. Then it says *in Drive*. A photo that could not file says *not in Drive yet*. It tries again on the next pass.

The photos land in the Tasks folder in Drive. Each card has a folder of its own. It is named by the day and the title. A roadmap card's folder sits under its roadmap and stage. Each photo is named by the day, who took it and what it shows. The office sets the Tasks folder on Documents → Where files go. Until it is set, photos wait with the reason on the card.

## Good to know

- A photo on a card with no signal does not send yet. Take it again once the signal is back.
- Review's sign-off row shows the finishing photo. The office signs off on what it can see.
