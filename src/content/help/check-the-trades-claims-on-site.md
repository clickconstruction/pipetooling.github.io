Before bill day, each trade claims how far along its work is. The superintendent walks the job and taps what they see on each line. The office reads the taps before it approves a draw.

Claims to check is on the daily log. Only a dev sees it until Building opens to the superintendent.

## Open the claims

1. Press {{button:outline|GC}} on the Bids page to open [GC projects](/gc).
2. Press {{button:outline|Daily log}} on the card of a job we are building.
3. **Claims to check** sits above the day's log on the checking days.

The checking days run from the cut-off to two days before bill day. On other days the card says when the list comes. Press {{button:outline|Show the list now}} to walk early.

## Tap what you see

- The list goes in walking order: the site, the yard, then each level.
- Each line shows the claim, the last draw and the schedule.
- Tap the percent you see. The first button is the claim, and the next ones are less.
- Press {{button:outline|…}} for another percent. Type it and press **Keep**.
- For stored material, tap **yes** or **no** for whether it is on site.

## Under the claim, say why

A tap under the claim needs a photo or a note. Paste the photo's Drive link, or type what is not there. Then press {{button:blue|Keep it}}. The office sends the draw back with those words.

The card never shows a dollar. You see percents and photos, and the money is the office's. A tap never approves a draw. The office approves each one in the Draws window. See [get the trades' draws in](/help/get-the-trades-draws-in-and-checked-before-bill-day).
