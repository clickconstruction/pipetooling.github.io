New messages that need sorting wait at the top of Follow up. Sort each one so the right person answers it on the right job.

## What waits in New

**New** is the first list on [Messages](/messages) and on Follow up. A person waits there for one of four reasons.

- **Bid it?** means an email to bids@ is an invitation to bid, an addendum, a solicitation or a pre-bid notice.
- **Who is this?** means a number or an address nobody has named wrote to us.
- **Which job?** means someone on two or more active jobs wrote. Their line went to their starred job on its own.
- **Who has it?** means someone wrote and nobody is on the conversation yet.

A person in **New** is not in **Needs a reply** too. Once sorted, they move to **Needs a reply** until someone answers.

The count on **Messages** in the header is everyone waiting on us. A person in **New** counts there once.

## Sort one

Press the person's row in **New**, or the blue question beside their name. Their thread opens with the sort card at the top. The card's heading says who it asks about. An email reads *Who is Dale Ferris, dale@ridgeway.example?*. A text with no name reads *Who is 512-555-0142?*.

{{chip:blue|Who is 512-555-0142?}} {{button:outline|A new lead on Prospects}} {{button:outline|One of our own team}} {{button:outline|Someone else}}
:::

## Bid it?

A general contractor's bid platform or a public agency sends us an invitation to bid. The app reads it as one. The row's purple question is **Bid it?** and the estimator holds it. The card's heading names the job and who it is from. It reads *Bid it? Oak Hill Clinic, Boerne, from Ridgeway Builders*. The line under it says what the email read. It reads *Invitation to bid, due Thu Nov 12 at 2:00 PM, on BuildingConnected*.

:::example The Bid it? card
{{chip:purple|Bid it?}} Bid: Oak Hill Clinic, Boerne &nbsp; {{button:outline|Start a bid on Ridgeway Builders}} {{button:outline|Not for us}} {{button:outline|Keep it with Dale}}
:::

- The **Bid** box holds the bid's name. It starts with what the email said. Fix it if the subject read wrong.
- Press **Start a bid on** a customer under **A customer we know**. The app makes the bid on them with the name, the platform's link and the due day. The thread lands on the bid. The Bid Board opens on it.
- Is the contractor new? Type the company in the **Customer** box. Press **Make the customer and start the bid**. The company becomes the customer. The sender stays the person on the thread.
- Type why under **Not for us, because**, then press **Not for us**. The thread keeps the reason in a grey line. The row closes. Their next invitation shows again.
- Press **Keep it with** the sender to leave the bid for later. The row then asks who they are, or who has it.

The bid is a Plumbing bid with the rule's estimator on it. Open it to add the address, the plans and the rest. An addendum to a bid we are pricing reads the same way. Press **Keep it** and tie the line to the bid with **Which job?** when it comes up.

## Who is this?

The card lists who the sender may be. **A customer we know** comes first, on a text and on an email alike. Each customer says why they match. The reason is the address or number on their record, a bid or job that names them, or their name. The company's email address counts too.

- Press a customer to name the sender as them. The thread stays with them. A bid or job that names them takes the thread.
- Press a trade partner the same way on an email to bids@.
- Press the new lead to keep them as a lead until they book. Their thread then offers **Book a visit** and **Start an estimate**. Either makes them a customer, and their thread comes along.
- Press **One of our own team** when the number is a teammate's. The card lists the roster. Press their name.
- Press **Someone else** to find them yourself in **Who is this?**.

**A new customer** is one press. The **Customer** box starts with the sender's name. Press **Make the customer** to add them and move the thread to them. Is the sender someone at a company? Type the company's name in the box instead. The company becomes the customer. The sender stays the person on the thread. An email to bids@ makes a commercial customer. A text to the team number makes a residential one.

:::example A new customer from an email to bids@
Customer: Ridgeway Builders &nbsp; {{button:outline|Make the customer}} &nbsp; Dale stays the person on the thread.
:::

**A new trade partner**, **A supply house** and **A candidate, to the hiring board** are one press each too. Press the row and its box opens. The company's name or the house's starts as the address spells it. Fix it if they go by another name. A trade partner asks for the trade they do. A candidate's box starts with the sender's name. Press the button under the box. It makes the record and names the sender.

:::example A new trade partner from an email to bids@
Company: Ridgeway Builders &nbsp; Trade: Plumbing &nbsp; {{button:outline|Make the trade partner}}
:::

The blue **Who is this?** sits at the top right of the card. It opens the search for anyone, the same door as **Someone else**.

A teammate's number goes on their People record. Next time the app knows them without asking. Their thread sits under **Our team** on Messages.

A trade partner made here waits for approval under Trade partners, like any company added there. A supply house's lines stay off Follow up. A candidate's do too.

## A notice, not a person

Some mail to bids@ is not a person writing to us. A newsletter, a supply house's specials or a platform's own notice lands as **Who is this?** when its headers do not mark it. The card's last block is **A notice, not a person**. It has one press, or two.

- Press **Mark** and the address **a notice** to close the row. The app leaves that address out from now on.
- Press **And everything from** the company's domain to leave out every address there. The app offers this only for a company's own domain, never for a shared mail service like gmail.com.

:::example The block under the people, for a newsletter
{{button:outline|Mark news@alamosurety.example a notice}} {{button:outline|And everything from alamosurety.example}}
:::

The thread goes to Done with a grey line, *Marked a notice*. Nothing in Gmail changes. Search still finds the thread, and the **Who is this?** pill on Messages still lists it. Their next email stays in Gmail with no label.

Pressed it by mistake? Open the thread. The strip at the top reads *Marked a notice*. Press **Not a notice after all**. The address comes off the list and the row is back in New.

An invitation to bid from a general contractor is not a notice. The contractor is a customer. Its row asks [Bid it?](#bid-it) instead.

## Which job?

The card lists the person's active jobs. Their starred job is picked.

- Press **Keep it on** and the job's number when the starred job is right.
- Pick another job, then press **Move it to** and its number.

Either press marks the line as tied by a person, so it leaves **New**.

## Who has it?

The card lists the office team. The person the rule picks is first. Press a name to give them the conversation. Press your own name to take it.

The rule is [Who gets a new message](/help/choose-who-gets-a-new-message). It picks whoever answered them last, else the seat for their kind.

## Sort a backlog

When two or more people wait in **New**, its header has {{button:blue|Sort all}}. Press it to sort them one at a time. Needs a reply has the same kind of desk, [Answer all](/help/answer-everyone-waiting-on-us-from-one-page#answer-everyone-one-at-a-time).

:::example The desk, one person at a time
New · 3 to sort · 1 of 3 &nbsp; {{chip:blue|Who has it?}} &nbsp; {{button:outline|Skip for now}}
:::

The desk shows one person's card. It says how the line came in, who sent it and what they wrote. The sort card sits under it, with the same presses it has above the thread.

- Press a choice on the sort card. The next person comes up.
- Press **Skip for now** to come back to someone after the rest.
- Press **Someone else** on **Who is this?** to close the desk. Their thread opens at the line.

The top of the desk counts who is left. When nobody is left, it says **All sorted.** Press **Back to Follow up** to close it.
