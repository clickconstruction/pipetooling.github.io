New messages that need sorting wait at the top of Follow up. Sort each one so the right person answers it on the right job.

## What waits in New

**New** is the first list on [Messages](/messages) and on Follow up. A person waits there for one of three reasons.

- **Who is this?** means a number or an address nobody has named wrote to us.
- **Which job?** means someone on two or more active jobs wrote. Their line went to their starred job on its own.
- **Who has it?** means someone wrote and nobody is on the conversation yet.

A person in **New** is not in **Needs a reply** too. Once sorted, they move to **Needs a reply** until someone answers.

The count on **Messages** in the header is everyone waiting on us. A person in **New** counts there once.

## Sort one

Press the person's row in **New**. Their thread opens with the sort card at the top.

:::example The sort card for a number nobody has named
{{chip:blue|Who is this?}} {{button:outline|A new lead on Prospects}} {{button:outline|One of our own team}} {{button:outline|Someone else}}
:::

## Who is this?

The card lists who the sender may be. A text to the team number guesses a customer we know first, then a new lead. An email to bids@ guesses a trade partner first.

- Press a customer or a trade partner to name the number as them. The thread stays with them.
- Press the new lead to keep them as a lead until they book.
- Press **One of our own team** when the number is a teammate's. The card lists the roster. Press their name.
- Press **Someone else** to find them yourself in **Who is this?**.

Some choices wait for a record of their own. The card says where to make it.

A teammate's number goes on their People record. Next time the app knows them without asking. Their thread sits under **Our team** on Messages.

- A new customer waits until you add them on [Customers](/customers).
- A new trade partner waits until you add the company under Trade partners in GC mode.
- A supply house waits until you add it on Materials.

## Which job?

The card lists the person's active jobs. Their starred job is picked.

- Press **Keep it on** and the job's number when the starred job is right.
- Pick another job, then press **Move it to** and its number.

Either press marks the line as tied by a person, so it leaves **New**.

## Who has it?

The card lists the office team. The person the rule picks is first. Press a name to give them the conversation. Press your own name to take it.

The rule is [Who gets a new message](/help/choose-who-gets-a-new-message). It picks whoever answered them last, else the seat for their kind.

## Sort a backlog

When two or more people wait in **New**, its header has {{button:blue|Sort all}}. Press it to sort them one at a time. Needs a reply has the same kind of desk, [Answer all](/help/answer-everyone-waiting-on-us-from-one-page#answer-everyone-one-at-a-time).

:::example The desk, one person at a time
New · 3 to sort · 1 of 3 &nbsp; {{chip:blue|Who has it?}} &nbsp; {{button:outline|Skip for now}}
:::

The desk shows one person's card. It says how the line came in, who sent it and what they wrote. The sort card sits under it, with the same presses it has above the thread.

- Press a choice on the sort card. The next person comes up.
- Press **Skip for now** to come back to someone after the rest.
- Press **Someone else** on **Who is this?** to close the desk. Their thread opens at the line.

The top of the desk counts who is left. When nobody is left, it says **All sorted.** Press **Back to Follow up** to close it.
