The Follow up sheet walks you through everyone we wait on, one person at a time. The sheet drafts a polite message and keeps a log of each call.

The office and estimators can use it. A text or an email goes from your own phone or your own mail. A dev can also send an email from Click.

## Open the sheet

There are two ways in.

- In [Follow up](/gc), press {{button:outline|By people}}, then {{button:blue|Work the list}}. The sheet holds everyone on **We are waiting on them**.
- On a board row, hover over the count of people to call. Press {{button:blue|Work the list}} on the card that opens. The sheet holds that job's people.

A dev can also open it from a job's schedule. Group the chart by company, then press {{button:blue|Work the list}} above the call list.

Each person has a {{button:outline|Follow up}} button too. That button opens the sheet at that person.

Follow up also ends with **More to follow up on**. That list holds everyone we wait on for something other than a quote. Papers, plans not opened, the schedule, the architect and customers show there.

## Send a message from your phone or mail

The people are down the left side. What each person owes us is ticked.

1. Untick anything you do not want to ask about.
2. Pick {{button:outline|Text}} or {{button:outline|Email}}.
3. Pick {{button:outline|Quick nudge}} or {{button:outline|A full note}}.
4. Read the draft. You can change any word in it.
5. Press {{button:blue|Draft text}} or {{button:blue|Draft email}}. Your own Messages or mail opens with the draft filled in.
6. Send it from there.
7. Come back and press {{button:blue|I sent it}}.

The message goes in the log with your name and the date. A quote's ask gets the line. Anything else goes on the company's own log. The sheet then moves on to the next person.

An email goes to whoever at the company gets that kind of email. A waiver may go to their bookkeeper, for example. The sheet says who and why above the subject.

Text is off when we have no phone number for them. Email is off when we have no email address.

## Send it from Click

A dev can send the email from Click Construction instead of from their own mail. It goes to a trade partner only.

1. Under **From**, press {{button:outline|Click Construction}}. Email is picked for you.
2. Read the words in the box. The email adds the greeting, their portal link and the sign-off.
3. Press {{button:outline|Send a test to me}}. A copy marked [TEST] comes to your own email. The sheet says who the real one goes to.
4. Press {{button:blue|Send email}}.

The email goes to everyone at the company who gets those kinds of email. Each ask and the company log it as a nudge from Click. The same email sends only once a day. A company that writes in Spanish waits until Spanish emails are on.

## Log a call

1. Press {{button:outline|Call}} beside their number. Your phone dials them.
2. The sheet asks what they said. Type it in.
3. Did they give a day? Pick it under **They gave a day**.
4. Press {{button:blue|Save the call}}.

With no number on file, press {{button:outline|Log a call}} instead. You can log a call you made some other way.

A day for a quote is kept on the ask. A day for a paper is kept as their promise. Insurance, a W-9, a scope of work and a waiver each work this way. When the day passes, Follow up shows the promise as late.

A day for the schedule only goes in the log. Move the bar on the schedule yourself.

## A customer or the architect

A customer or the architect gets a text, an email and a call the same way. The line goes in their own log, under the job it was about. A day they give is only logged.
