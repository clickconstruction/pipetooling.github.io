The robot that shadowed your bid sealed its own number before yours existed. You record the bid as sent with its value, and the envelope opens for you.

A shadow is the robot's own blind bid on your job. The score is taken at that moment, while the bid is still in your head.

## Better: open it before you send

You price the bid first. Then a card sits on the **Cover Letter** between the proposed amount and Mark sent. The card is **Your best effort**. You tap {{button:blue|Record best effort · open the robot's envelope}} and two things happen. The letter's amount goes on the record as the number you would send right now. The robot's sealed number opens beside it. The bid can still change.

- Your best effort is what the robot is scored against from then on. A bid can offer an alternate, an extra option priced on its own. See [offer an alternate on a bid](?g=offer-an-alternate-on-a-bid). On such a bid, the number recorded is the whole. That is the proposed amount plus what each offered alternate adds. The robot priced the alternate too. You cannot record it again once you have seen the envelope. The bid can still change, and the bid note will say by how much.
- Recording also marks the bid reviewed, so the flow strip's Review step fills in.
- The robot may still be estimating. Then the card records your number anyway. It says the envelope will open here when the robot locks. Sending is never blocked.

:::example Why this moment
After pricing you have just touched every row. That is when the robot's questions get their best answers, and when a missed roof drain can still go into the bid instead of only into the robot's lessons.
:::

## When it opens at send

1. With no best effort on record, you confirm the send the way you always do. You tap **Mark sent today** on the Cover Letter. Or you set the sent date on the Edit tab with the three acknowledgments. There is nothing new to click.
2. If a robot sealed a number on this bid, **The robot's envelope** opens on top. It shows {{chip:blue|Robot locked}} beside {{chip:blue|Ours · just now}} and the delta, the gap between the two. The delta is green when the robot landed within 8%.
3. It opens for the bid's estimator, because the number it scored against is yours. Someone else marking the bid sent gets the {{chip:blue|Robot bid}} line on their Dashboard instead.

:::example Why it can't change your number
Your value and sent date are already saved when the envelope opens. That is the same instant the score is computed and stamped on both ledgers — so nothing you see here can move the score, and nothing you saw before could anchor your bid.
:::

## Three minutes, one tap each

- **Where the delta lives** sorts the gap into missed, added, counts, priced differently and everything else. It sums back to the headline.
- **The biggest differences** shows six rows, the largest dollars first. Each takes one tap. You tap {{button:outline|✗ Robot's wrong}} and the robot learns. You tap {{button:outline|📋 Ours is off}} when our record needs a fix. You tap {{button:outline|✓ Both fine}} for a judgment call. The first two open a short note you can edit before posting.
- **The robot asked** lists its questions. Each comes with the two to four answers it offered as buttons. {{button:gray|Something else…}} gives you a box.
- You tap {{button:blue|Finish audit}} to close it. You tap {{button:gray|Later}} to leave everything exactly as it was. The audit then waits on the Audits lens like before. {{button:gray|Full audit}} opens the whole card there.

## Changing the bid after you looked

The robot may have caught something. You raise or lower the bid afterwards. The score stays as it was taken. A note lands on the bid: *Bid value revised after seeing the robot's number: $148,200 → $151,400 (+$3,200)*. That is the robot earning its keep, on the record.

## Later, from the Robot Board

Every bid with a robot run sits on **Bids → 🤖 Robots → Robot Board**. It sits in the same section it has on the Bid Board. A row whose audit is still waiting carries {{button:outline-blue|Review now}}. That opens the same envelope. It serves a run you skipped at send. It also serves an older backtest, a robot run on a bid already decided.
