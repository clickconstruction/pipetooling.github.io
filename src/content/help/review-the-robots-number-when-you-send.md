The robot that shadowed your bid sealed its own number before yours existed. The moment you record the bid as sent **with its value**, the score is taken and the envelope opens for you — while the bid is still in your head.

## Better: open it before you send

On the **Cover Letter**, after pricing, a card sits between the proposed amount and Mark sent: **Your best effort**. Tap {{button:blue|Record best effort · open the robot's envelope}} and two things happen: the letter's amount goes on the record as the number you would send right now, and the robot's sealed number opens beside it — while the bid can still change.

- Your best effort is what the robot is scored against from then on. It cannot be re-recorded once you have seen the envelope; the bid can still change, and the bid note will say by how much.
- Recording also marks the bid reviewed, so the flow strip's Review step fills in.
- If the robot is still estimating, the card records your number anyway and says the envelope will open here when the robot locks. Sending is never blocked.

:::example Why this moment
After pricing you have just touched every row. That is when the robot's questions get their best answers, and when a missed roof drain can still go into the bid instead of only into the robot's lessons.
:::

## When it opens at send

1. With no best effort on record, confirm the send the way you always do: **Mark sent today** on the Cover Letter, or the sent date on the Edit tab with the three acknowledgments. Nothing new to click.
2. If a robot sealed a number on this bid, **The robot's envelope** opens on top: {{chip:blue|Robot locked}} beside {{chip:blue|Ours · just now}} and the delta, green when the robot landed within 8%.
3. It opens for the bid's estimator (the number it scored against is yours). Someone else marking the bid sent gets the {{chip:blue|Robot bid}} line on their Dashboard instead.

:::example Why it can't change your number
Your value and sent date are already saved when the envelope opens. That is the same instant the score is computed and stamped on both ledgers — so nothing you see here can move the score, and nothing you saw before could anchor your bid.
:::

## Three minutes, one tap each

- **Where the delta lives** — missed, added, counts, priced differently, everything else. It sums back to the headline.
- **The biggest differences** — six rows, the largest dollars first. Each takes one tap: {{button:outline|✗ Robot's wrong}} (it learns), {{button:outline|📋 Ours is off}} (our record needs a fix), {{button:outline|✓ Both fine}} (a judgment call). The first two open a short note you can edit before posting.
- **The robot asked** — its questions, with the two-to-four answers it offered as buttons; {{button:gray|Something else…}} gives you a box.
- {{button:blue|Finish audit}} closes it; {{button:gray|Later}} leaves everything exactly as it was, and the audit waits on the Audits lens like before. {{button:gray|Full audit}} opens the whole card there.

## Changing the bid after you looked

If the robot caught something and you raise or lower the bid afterwards, the score stays as it was taken and a note lands on the bid: *Bid value revised after seeing the robot's number: $148,200 → $151,400 (+$3,200)*. That is the robot earning its keep, on the record.

## Later, from the Robot Board

Every bid with a robot run sits on **Bids → 🤖 Robots → Robot Board**, in the same section it has on the Bid Board. A row whose audit is still waiting carries {{button:outline-blue|Review now}} — the same envelope, for a run you skipped at send or an older backtest.
