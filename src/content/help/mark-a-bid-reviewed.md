The one sentence: **before a letter goes out, someone looks the bid over and presses Mark reviewed — the app records who, when, and their notes, and the flow strip's Review step turns green.**

## Where the button is

Open the bid's flow strip — on the Bid Board by clicking the row, or above the bid's title on **Counts, Takeoffs, Labor, Pricing** or **Cover Letter** — and press {{button:blue|Mark reviewed}} at the strip's right end. You can also click the **Review** step itself.

## What it asks

One box: *Any notes for the file? (optional)*. Leave it empty or write what the next person should know, then {{button:blue|Mark reviewed}}. {{button:outline|Cancel}} records nothing.

## What it records

- **Who and when** — under the Review step the strip reads *by Wendi · Sep 9*. Hover the step for the notes.
- **A bid note** — the same words land in the bid's notes timeline as *Reviewed by Wendi. Notes: …*, so anyone reading the bid's story sees it. It is a plain note, not a contact, so it does not move the bid's **Last Contact**.

:::example a second look before the letter
The estimator prices the bid and asks the leader to check it. The leader opens Pricing, reads the Workbench, presses **Mark reviewed** and types *trim margin is thin — hold at 38%*. The strip now shows Review done and rings **Cover letter**; the estimator sees the note in the tooltip and in the bid's notes.
:::

## Reviewing again

Press the button again to re-review: the stamp moves to the new person and time, and the new notes replace the old ones on the strip. The earlier review stays in the bid's notes timeline.

## Who can

Anyone who can edit the bid. Robot estimators cannot mark a bid reviewed — the database refuses it — because reviewing is a person's call, like sending and marking won or lost.
