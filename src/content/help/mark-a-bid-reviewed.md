You look a bid over before its letter goes out, then you press Mark reviewed. The app records who, when and their notes, and the Review step turns green.

## Where the button is

You open the bid's flow strip. On the Bid Board, you click the row. On **Counts, Takeoffs, Labor, Pricing** or **Cover Letter**, the strip sits above the bid's title. You press {{button:blue|Mark reviewed}} at the strip's right end. You can also click the **Review** step itself.

## What it asks

One box: *Any notes for the file? (optional)*. You leave it empty or write what the next person should know. Then you press {{button:blue|Mark reviewed}}. {{button:outline|Cancel}} records nothing.

## What it records

- **Who and when**. Under the Review step the strip reads *by Wendi · Sep 9*. You hover the step for the notes.
- **A bid note**. The same words land in the bid's notes timeline as *Reviewed by Wendi. Notes: …*. Anyone reading the bid's story sees it. It is a plain note, not a contact. So it does not move the bid's **Last Contact**.

:::example a second look before the letter
The estimator prices the bid and asks the leader to check it. The leader opens Pricing, reads the Workbench, presses **Mark reviewed** and types *trim margin is thin — hold at 38%*. The strip now shows Review done and rings **Cover letter**; the estimator sees the note in the tooltip and in the bid's notes.
:::

## Reviewing again

You press the button again to review again. The stamp moves to the new person and time. The new notes replace the old ones on the strip. The earlier review stays in the bid's notes timeline.

## Who can

Anyone who can edit the bid. Robot estimators cannot mark a bid reviewed. The database refuses it. Reviewing is a person's call, like sending and marking won or lost.
