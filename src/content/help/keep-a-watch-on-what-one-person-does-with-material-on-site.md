A watch follows what one person does with the material on a site. The person keeps their access and sees nothing different. Only a dev can turn a watch on or read one.

## Turn the watch on

Open People and press the person's row. Find **Watch on site** under Access & account.

Type the reason under **Why**. The person never sees it.

Leave **Tell me when they press** ticked to get a push on your phone.

Press **Turn on the watch**. The row then says when the watch began and who set it.

## What the app tells you

The app sends three kinds of push. None goes to the person watched.

- One when they make their first press of the day.
- One for a press outside the process. Several at once come as one push.
- One in the evening with the day's count.

The app checks every five minutes, so a push can trail the press by a few minutes.

Press the push to open the site's materials. Then press **By person**.

## Read what they do

Open a site on the Materials desk and press **By person**. Pick the person under **Person**.

A chip beside the name reads *Watched since* with the day.

A red chip marks a press outside the process. The watched person may take, use and return material with a note. They may move parts and move a box.

These are the office's to do, so each one reads in red.

- A count.
- A part added.
- A box retired, brought back, renamed or changed.
- A price or a join taken off.
- A take or a use with no note.

Press **Outside the process only** to read those rows alone. The last figure over the list counts them.

## See the boxes they opened

The app keeps the boxes a watched person opens on a screen. A line over the list names the boxes they opened and pressed nothing on.

Press a box's name to open it. A count of that box is the only way to know what left it.

The phone reports each open. A person who takes material without opening the box in the app leaves no line.

## Stop the watch

Go back to **Watch on site** on the person's row. Type why under **Why it stops** and press **Stop watching**.

The watch stays on record as stopped. You can turn a new one on later.

## Answer what you find

To take a press back or mark it, see [answer a press on site that should not stand](/help/answer-a-press-on-site-that-should-not-stand).
