Tasks born from a roadmap carry a purple {{chip:purple|⛰ Farm 1}} chip next to their title on **Checklist → Review → Outstanding by person**. Tap the chip to see where that task sits in the bigger picture — without leaving the page.

## What the window shows

:::example Where this task fits
⛰ Farm 1 · **7.1** · does our carpentry tools have a neat home?
THE WHOLE ROAD — 24 stages, 1 done · ▼ pin on stage 7
STAGE 7 · do we have all the tools easily accessible? · 0 of 10 done
:::

- **The task's number** — `7.1` means stage 7, first task in it. Same numbering as everywhere else in the roadmap.
- **The whole road** — one segment per stage: green is done, amber-ringed is in progress, gray is locked, and the blue ring with the ▼ pin is this task's stage.
- **The stage up close** — every task in the stage in order, done ones struck through, this task highlighted. You can see at a glance who else's work sits beside it.
- **What it unlocks** — the stages that open up when this one finishes, and (for a locked stage) what it's still waiting on.

## Jumping to the full picture

Tap {{button:outline|Open full roadmap →}} at the bottom to land on **Checklist → Roadmap** with that roadmap selected. Close with the ✕, the {{button:outline|Close}} button, Esc, or a tap outside.
