Tasks born from a roadmap carry a purple stage chip under their title. You tap the chip to see where that task sits in the bigger picture.

The chip looks like {{chip:purple|make efficent water and feeding}}. It sits under the title on your **Today** cards. It also sits next to the title on **Checklist → Review → Outstanding by person**. It names the **stage** the task belongs to. The full *roadmap · stage* path sits in the tooltip. The window opens without leaving the page.

## What the window shows

:::example Where this task fits
⛰ Farm 1 · **7.1** · does our carpentry tools have a neat home?
THE WHOLE ROAD — 24 stages, 1 done · ▼ pin on stage 7
STAGE 7 · do we have all the tools easily accessible? · 0 of 10 done
:::

- **The task's number** reads `7.1`. That means stage 7, first task in it. It is the same numbering as everywhere else in the roadmap.
- **The whole road** shows one segment per stage. Green is done. Amber-ringed is in progress. Gray is locked. The blue ring with the ▼ pin is this task's stage.
- **The stage up close** lists every task in the stage in order. Done ones are struck through. This task is highlighted. You can see at a glance who else has work beside it.
- **What it unlocks** lists the stages that open up when this one finishes. For a locked stage, it says what the stage is still waiting on.

## Jumping to the full picture

You tap {{button:outline|Open full roadmap →}} at the bottom. You land on the **Roadmap** page with that roadmap selected. You close with the ✕, the {{button:outline|Close}} button, Esc, or a tap outside.

## Work on a task from here

Every task row in the stage box is a button. You tap one and its **task card** opens over the window. It is the same card the roadmap uses, so there is nothing new to learn. On the card you can:

- **✎** renames the task.
- **☆ / ★** pins it to the top of Next up.
- **＋ Assign someone** assigns a person. You tap a name chip to unassign.
- You add a **note** that follows the task onto the assignee's Today list.
- {{chip:green|○ Mark done}} is new on the card. It marks the roadmap task complete. ***✓ done · reopen*** undoes it. It writes the same field the Map's checkbox does. So the segmented bar, the Goals ledger and the Timeline all agree instantly.

You close the card and the window behind it is already refreshed. The stage's *1 of 3 done* count, the bar and the names are all current. Editing is for roadmap editors. Those are dev, leader, assistant-like and primary. Everyone else opens the card read-only and can still add a note.
