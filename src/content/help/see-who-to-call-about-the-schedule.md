On a job being built, the schedule lists everyone whose answer moves the chart. Every reason to call them sits under their name.

The office and estimators can open the schedule. Superintendents get it later.

## Open the call list

1. Open [GC projects](/gc).
2. Find the project's card.
3. Press {{button:outline|Schedule}}.
4. Press **By company** over the chart.

The list sits under the chart's buttons. The list's first line says how many people to call, and how many are late. A job that is not being built has no list.

## Call someone

Press **Call** beside a name. Your phone or your computer starts the call. One call covers every reason under that name.

## Open a bar from the list

Some reasons are about a bar on the chart. Press one of those lines. The bar opens below the chart.

## Show one company's work

Press **Their work** beside a name. The chart then shows only that company's bars. Pick **Every company** in the **One company** list to see every bar again.

## Call from a bar

Press a bar on the chart. The bar's card names the company doing the work. Press the card's call button, like **Call Andre**, to reach them.

## Fold the list

Press **Hide** to fold the list to its first line. Press **Show** to open it again.
