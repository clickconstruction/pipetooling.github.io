Quickfill has a station called Physical inbox. It is the tray on the office desk, drawn as a deck of papers. The count is the paper nobody has decided on yet.

## The two-minute rule

Pick up a paper. If it takes two minutes, do it now and never type it. A toll bill you can pay is done. A card you can drop in a truck is done.

Type the rest once. The deck keeps the day the paper came, so its age counts from then.

## Adding a paper

Type what the paper is in the box at the top. Press Enter or {{button:outline|Add}}. The paper lands on the deck dated today.

Some papers sat in the tray before anyone typed them. Set **Came** to the day the paper arrived. Its age then counts from that day.

:::example A paper on the deck
New insurance cards, 6 trucks · {{chip:yellow|5 d · Oct 19}} · Marisol · {{button:outline|Done}} {{button:outline|Trash}} {{button:outline|File it ▾}} {{button:blue|Task}} {{button:outline|Ask ▾}}
:::

The chip shows the age. It turns amber after two days and red after a week.

## Deciding a paper

Every paper leaves the deck on one press. Pick the press that says what happens to it.

- {{button:outline|Done}} means you handled it.
- {{button:outline|Trash}} means it goes in the bin.
- {{button:outline|File it ▾}} asks where. Press a place or type one. The paper is filed there.
- {{button:blue|Task}} opens the Task window with the paper's words filled in. Pick an owner and a day. The paper leaves once the task is saved.
- {{button:outline|Ask ▾}} sends the paper to dispatch or to estimating as a request. The paper leaves once the request is sent.
- {{button:outline|Waiting for ▾}} asks whom and the day to check. The paper moves to the Waiting for fold.
- {{button:outline|Someday}} parks the paper in the Someday fold.
- {{button:outline|Comes back}} is for a kind of paper that arrives again and again. It opens the Task window set to repeat. Pick the days and an owner. The paper leaves once the task is saved.

A pressed paper turns into a green line that says what happened. Press {{button:outline|Undo}} on the line to put it back.

## The two folds

Under the deck sit two folds. They hold papers that have been decided but are not finished. Neither counts as undecided.

**Waiting for** lists each paper with whom it waits for and the day to check. A paper past its check day reads in red at the top. Press {{button:outline|Done}} when it came back. Press {{button:outline|Still here}} to put it back on the deck.

**Someday** lists the parked papers. Read it once a month. Press {{button:outline|Done}}, {{button:outline|Trash}} or {{button:outline|Back on the deck}}.

## The tray's routines

Under the folds sits a short list called The tray's routines. It shows each repeating task made with Comes back. Each row says how often it repeats, who has it, and its next day.

Press **Edit in Checklist ›** to change a day or an owner. The routines live in the Checklist like any other task.

## The mark

The station's ✓ Mark records the count as it is. An empty deck marks as zero. The mark also writes a short note on its own. The note says how many papers wait, the oldest age, and what was decided that day.

:::example A mark's note
2 undecided · oldest 4 d · 1 task · 1 filed
:::

## What belongs here

Only paper that needs a decision. A kind of paper that comes every month belongs in a repeating task, not on the deck. Press Comes back on it once. See [run the office day with Quickfill](/help/quickfill) for the whole page.
