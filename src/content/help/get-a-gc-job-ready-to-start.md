A won GC job needs a few things in before work starts. Get started lists them all in one window. Start stays shut until nothing is missing.

Get started shows to a dev while it is built.

## Open Get started

1. Open [GC projects](/gc).
2. Find the won job on the list.
3. Press **Get started** on its card.

The bar at the top counts the steps done. It reads **Ready to start. Nothing is missing.** once every step is in.

## With the customer

- **Our contract with the customer is signed.** Press {{button:blue|Send to sign}} to send it from the customer's window. Press {{button:outline|Mark it signed}} once they sign it on paper.
- **The permit is in hand.** Press {{button:outline|Mark it done}} once you have it.
- **A start date is set.** Pick the day work starts beside it.

A step you marked shows {{button:outline|Undo}} in case it was a mistake.

[Send our contract to a GC customer to sign](/help/send-our-contract-to-a-gc-customer) shows the send.

## The schedule

This step is done once the schedule has work drawn on it. Press {{button:outline|Draw it on Schedule}} to draw it.

## The trades

Each trade has five steps in the order they happen. The **Next** column says the one thing to do next.

1. **Awarded.** Press {{button:blue|Award}} to open Compare quotes.
2. **Master agreement.** Press {{button:blue|Send the master agreement}}. The company's window opens at that paper.
3. **Insurance.** Press {{button:blue|Ask for their insurance}}.
4. **W-9.** Press {{button:blue|Ask for their W-9}}.
5. **Statement of work.** Press {{button:blue|Send the statement of work}}. The window steps aside to the trade's card, where you send it.

A trade reads {{chip:green|ready}} once all five are in. [Send a trade its papers](/help/send-a-trade-its-papers) shows how each paper goes.

## Our own crew

A trade we do ourselves has one step. Our own bid for it must be priced. Press **Price our own bid** to open it on Bids.

## Start the job

Press {{button:blue|Start the project and tell the 2 on the job}}. The job moves to building today.

Each company awarded a trade gets an email that the job started. The window then says who was told. A company the email missed is named with the reason. Press {{button:outline|Send again}} to try it again.

:::example Started, with every company told
**Started Oct 10. Work begins Mon Oct 19.** Told Lonestar Earthworks, Hill Country Concrete.
:::

## Start anyway

Sometimes the job has to start before everything is in.

1. Type the reason under **Why start before everything is in?**
2. Press {{button:outline|Start anyway}}.

The job starts today. The window keeps who started it and why. What was missing stays listed under **Still owed**. The steps stay open until it is all in.

## When an email does not go

A company that reads Spanish gets no email yet. Our Spanish emails wait for a native speaker to read them. Call the company to tell it the job started.
