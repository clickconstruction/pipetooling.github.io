The Crew Day card on your Dashboard can email itself to you at the end of the day. It is the same rollup the card shows.

The email carries who worked which jobs, hours, field-report excerpts and flags. It is grouped by job, so it reads like a site diary.

## Set it up

1. On the Crew Day card, tap the {{button:outline|✉}} button.
2. Pick a recipient. That is yourself, or another office teammate. This email is office-only. Superintendents and field roles can't receive it. They use the Crew Day card on their Dashboard instead.
3. Choose **Send now**, or **Schedule…** with a date and time. Evening works best, since the email covers the day it's sent. Check **Repeat weekly** to make it standing.

:::example A standing weekday digest
Schedule Mon 6:00 PM with Repeat weekly, then do the same for Tue–Fri — five weekly chains make a weekday digest. Cancel any pending send to stop that day's chain.
:::

## Good to know

- {{button:outline|Preview}} shows today's email in a new tab. {{button:outline|Email me a test}} sends a [TEST] copy to your own address.
- The email is rebuilt **fresh at send time**. A 6:00 PM send shows the day as of 6:00 PM, including anyone still on the clock.
- Your pending sends are listed in the email dialog with a **Cancel** on each. They also show under {{icon:gear}} **Settings → Your account → My email schedule**.
- Like the card, the email shows **hours only**. It never shows wages or billing amounts.

If you can open the Day book, the email shows one more thing. Each office person's row carries their line of outcomes for that day. It reads like *billed 3 · 4 deposits · 2 contracts sent*. It is the same line the Dashboard's Crew Day shows.
