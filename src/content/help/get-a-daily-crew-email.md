The **Crew Day** card on your Dashboard can email itself to you at the end of the day — the same rollup of who worked which jobs, hours, field-report excerpts, and flags, grouped by job so it reads like a site diary.

## Set it up

1. On the Crew Day card, tap the {{button:outline|✉}} button.
2. Pick a recipient — yourself, or another office teammate. This email is office-only: superintendents and field roles can't receive it — they use the Crew Day card on their Dashboard instead.
3. Choose **Send now**, or **Schedule…** with a date and time — evening works best, since the email covers the day it's sent. Check **Repeat weekly** to make it standing.

:::example A standing weekday digest
Schedule Mon 6:00 PM with Repeat weekly, then do the same for Tue–Fri — five weekly chains make a weekday digest. Cancel any pending send to stop that day's chain.
:::

## Good to know

- {{button:outline|Preview}} shows today's email in a new tab; {{button:outline|Email me a test}} sends a [TEST] copy to your own address.
- The email is rebuilt **fresh at send time** — a 6:00 PM send shows the day as of 6:00 PM, including anyone still on the clock.
- Your pending sends are listed in the modal with a **Cancel** on each, and also under {{icon:gear}} **Settings → Your account → My email schedule**.
- Like the card, the email shows **hours only** — never wages or billing amounts.
