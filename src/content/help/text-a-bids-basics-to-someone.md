Open any bid's preview — from the Bid Board's eye icon, a **BP-number** link, or global search — and use {{button:gray|Copy for text}} in the header. It puts the bid's basics on your clipboard, ready to paste into a text message:

:::example What lands on your clipboard
ALSATIAN
CASTROVILLE TX
Bid due: 8/27/2026
:::

- One line each for **project name**, **address**, and **bid due date** — anything the bid doesn't have yet is skipped, so you never paste a stray dash.
- You'll see a **"Copied for text"** toast when it's on the clipboard. Paste it anywhere: iMessage, WhatsApp, email.

## While you're in the preview

The preview also shows the bid's key facts up top — due date (with a countdown), bid value, GC/Builder, and the estimator — plus one-tap chips for the Drive folder, plans, and takeoff links.
