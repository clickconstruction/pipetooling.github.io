The **⚡ Next up** panel is a short list of the roadmap tasks worth doing next, and *why*. It just points, and never re-orders your roadmap.

The **Plan** view is where the **Roadmap** page opens. You open the page from the header, under **Roadmap**. You tap **Map** or **Timeline** to switch views. The app remembers your pick on that device. The Plan view starts with the small Next up panel. The stage numbers and the full lists below it stay exactly as you arranged them.

## Two lanes

- **Ready to go** holds open tasks that already have a name on them. Someone can start these today.
- **Needs a person** holds open tasks nobody owns yet. You tap one and press {{button:outline|Assign →}} on its card to hand it out. It moves up to the first lane as soon as it has a person.

Each lane shows at most five tasks. It never shows more than two from the same stage. So one big stage can't crowd everything else out.

## Why this order

Only tasks in **unlocked** stages are eligible. The arrows on the Map decide what's possible, not what's first. Eligible tasks are then ordered by these rules:

1. **Closes a stage** means the stage has two or fewer tasks left, fewest first. Its chip reads {{chip:yellow|closes stage 6 · 2 left}}. Finishing a stage is what unlocks the arrows behind it.
2. **Unlocks the most** counts how much work waits behind the stage's arrows. Its chip reads {{chip:yellow|feeds Foundry · 4 stages wait}}.
3. **Your stage order** is the numbers you set with {{button:outline|Order stages}}. Its chip reads {{chip:yellow|your #2}}.
4. Task order within the stage.

Every row wears the reasons it was picked as small amber chips. You tap **why this order?** in the panel header for the rule in a sentence. There are no hidden weights. If two tasks tie on the first rule, the second rule decides, and so on.

:::example Reading a pick
**6.2** · *install easy-to-open gate on river frontage* — {{chip:yellow|closes stage 6 · 2 left}} {{chip:yellow|feeds Full Use of Land · 1 stage wait}}
Stage 6 has two tasks left, and finishing it is what moves "Full Use of Land". That's why it's at the top, even though stage 1 is numbered first.
:::

## Pin a task — "this one, now"

You open any task's card and tap the **★** next to its title. Only editors can pin. A pinned task jumps to the **top of its lane** on the shortlist. It sits above everything the rules would pick, wearing a {{chip:yellow|★ pinned}} chip. It shows a small ★ on its Plan row and inside its Map cluster. You can pin several, and they keep the order you pinned them in. You tap ★ again to unpin. Pinning never changes stage numbers, arrows or anyone's lists. It only changes what the shortlist points at first.

## The same picks, elsewhere

- **On the Map**, tasks on the shortlist wear a small ⚡ after their title. A stage with picks shows a {{chip:yellow|⚡ 2 next up}} chip in its header. So a collapsed cluster still tells you there's something worth doing inside.
- **On the Dashboard**, a card can show when a roadmap has three or more unowned tasks in open stages. It says *Farm 1 · 84 roadmap tasks need a person*, with the top pick. You tap {{button:outline|Open Plan →}} to land on that roadmap's Plan, with the panel at the top. The card is the owner's. It does not appear on other dashboards, even for people who can open the Roadmap page.

## What it doesn't do

It never re-numbers stages and never hides anything. It never moves a locked stage's tasks onto the list. When you'd rather work in your own order, you scroll past it. The **Now**, **Up next** and **Goals** lists are unchanged.
