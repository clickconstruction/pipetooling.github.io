The **Bids → Cover Letter** tab now has two layouts, switched by the {{chip:gray|Old}} / {{chip:gray|New}} pills at the top of the selected bid.

- {{chip:gray|Old}} — the classic single-column layout, exactly as it's always been. This is the default.
- {{chip:blue|New}} — the **studio**: your controls sit in two numbered steps on the left (**Scope & pricing**, **Letter content**), and the letter itself stays on screen at the right the whole time, repainting live as you type. The **Copy & open in Google Docs**, **Print**, and paste-the-link buttons sit right under the letter.

## Nothing to lose

Both layouts are the same data underneath — an inclusion typed in New is there when you flip to Old, and vice versa. Your choice sticks on this device only, so trying New never changes anyone else's screen.

:::example Why two layouts?
The studio is being refined in the open. Use whichever gets today's letter out faster, and tell the office what the new one should do better — Old stays untouched until New has earned its place.
:::
