Leave report opens the phone's camera first. Each shot goes with the report into the customer's Drive folder, small, and sits on the stage it shows.

## Take the first photo

1. Press {{button:blue|Leave report}} on the bar.
2. The phone's own camera opens. Take the shot.
3. The report form opens with the shot as a tile at the top.

The line over the tile says what the phone did. It reads *New photo · 4.2 MB → 310 KB · 1600 px*. The full size shot never leaves the phone. Drive gets the small copy.

Nothing to photograph? Press Cancel on the camera. The form opens with {{button:outline|Take a photo}} as the first tile. Press it any time.

## Say which stage it shows

On a job with stages, a row of chips opens under a new tile. The row reads **This photo shows**. Tap the stage. The photo then sits on that stage's row, where you set its number. A job with one stage skips the chips.

Every stage row has a camera press. Tap it to take a photo of that stage. The photo lands on that row already.

## Answer a checklist question with the photo

Some stage checklist questions ask for a photo. Under a new tile, below the stage chips, a line reads **Or it answers a checklist question**. One chip sits there for each such question. A chip reads *Rough In · Did the water test hold?*

Tap the chip. The photo sits on that stage's row. The tile reads *Answers: Did the water test hold?* under it. When you save, the photo becomes that question's answer. The checklist shows it with *Taken on a field report.*

The question still needs your pick. Open the checklist and press {{chip:green|Yes}} or {{chip:red|No}}. Then the question counts as answered. The guide [fill a stage checklist on the phone](/help/fill-a-stage-checklist-on-the-phone) has the rest.

Tap a stage chip instead to let the question go. The photo then shows the stage only.

## Take more, or take one back

- {{button:outline|Take another}} opens the camera again.
- **Remove** under a tile drops a shot before you save.
- A shot that did not send shows why, with {{button:outline|Retry}}. A shot that never sends does not ride with the report.

## Save

Save sits at the bottom and names what it writes. It reads {{button:blue|Save · job 36% · 2 photos}}. A Note or a Walk files no percent, so its button reads {{button:blue|Save report · 1 photo}}. While a photo is still on its way, the button reads *Sending 1 photo…* and waits. Press Save once. The report and its photos go together.

## With no signal

A shot taken with no signal is kept on the phone. Its tile reads *Kept on the phone. It goes with the report.* Save never waits for it. Press Save as usual. The report and its photos wait in the queue together. The line at the top reads *Field report from 7:58 AM*, then *waiting to send* and *1 photo with it*.

When the signal is back the phone sends the photos first, then the report. The report card then shows them as usual. A photo that fails for another reason still shows why, with {{button:outline|Retry}}.

## Where the photos go

Within a few minutes the office's sweep copies each photo into the job's Photos folder under the customer's Drive folder. The file is named by the day, by you and by the stage. It reads *2026-10-29 Luis Ortega · Rough In.jpg*. A photo that answers a checklist question adds the question, as in *2026-10-31 Luis Ortega · Rough In · Did the water test hold.jpg*. The copy in the app is then deleted.

The report card shows the photos. One in Drive reads *in Drive* with a link. One still waiting reads *not in Drive yet*.

:::fold A job with no Drive folder yet
The photo stays in the app and the report card reads *not in Drive yet* with the reason. The office makes the folder from the job's window. The next sweep files the photo.
:::

## At a desk, or from the Reports tab

The New report form takes photos too. Open it from Jobs → Reports → {{button:blue|New report}}. The Stages tab's row report and the Dashboard's Job Report square open it too. Pick the job. An {{button:outline|Add a photo}} tile sits under the report type.

1. Press {{button:outline|Add a photo}}. At a desk the file picker opens. On a phone the camera opens.
2. The shot comes back as a tile. The line over it says what the app did to the size.
3. On a job with stages, tap the stage it shows. Each stage row has its own camera press too.
4. Press {{button:blue|Save report · 1 photo}}.

Copy to Text names the photos at the end, as in *Photos: 1 photo on Rough In*.

:::fold A report on a project or a bid
The form shows no photo tile. A line reads *Photos go on a job's report. A report on a project or a bid takes none yet.* Pick the job instead when the work has one.
:::

## A Turnaway's photo

The Turnaway form takes a photo too. Press {{button:amber|Turnaway}} on the bar and pick the reason. A camera tile sits under the reasons. Take the shot of the locked gate or the empty lot. The press reads {{button:amber|File Turnaway · 1 photo}} and waits while the shot is on its way. The photo is named by the reason in Drive. Dispatch sees *1 photo on the report* on its line.

## Add a photo to a saved report

Open Settings → **My Reports** and press {{button:blue|Edit}} on the report. The report's photos show under the fields. On a job's report an {{button:outline|Add a photo}} tile sits under them. Add the shot and press {{button:blue|Save · 1 photo added}}.

A photo already on the report stays. Take one back in the job's Drive folder.

## The size the office picks

The office picks the size once in Settings → Jobs & billing → **Photos**. Small is 1200 px, Medium 1600 px and Large 2400 px on the long edge. Medium is the default. It reads fine on a desktop and goes up on one bar of signal. The app refuses any photo over 2 MB.

:::example A rough in, two shots
You press Leave report. The camera opens. You shoot the drain lines in bath 2. The tile says *4.2 MB → 310 KB*. You tap **Rough In** under it. You press Take another and shoot the water heater. You tap **Water heater**. You move Rough In to 45 and the water heater to Done. Save reads *Save · job 56% · 2 photos*. Four minutes later both files are in the customer's Photos folder.
:::
