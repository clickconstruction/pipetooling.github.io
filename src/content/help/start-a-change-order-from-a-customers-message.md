A customer often asks for a change in a text or an email. You can start the change order right from that message.

Anyone in the office can start one. The money team prices it and sends it to the customer to sign.

## Start one

1. Open the customer's thread. [Find every message with a person in one place](/help/find-every-message-with-a-person-in-one-place) shows how.
2. Press the message that asks for the change.
3. Press {{button:outline|Start a change order}}.
4. Check **What changes**. It starts with the first line of the message. Finish it in your own words.
5. Pick **Why it changes**.
6. Press {{button:blue|Save the draft}}.

Before you save, the window shows the number the change order takes and its job. The customer who wrote the message signs it.

## When it cannot start one

The message must be tied to a job we won. The words under the button say what is missing.

- *Tie the message to a job first.* [Tie a message to a job](/help/find-every-message-with-a-person-in-one-place#tie-a-message-to-a-job) shows how.
- *A change order needs a job we won.* The job was lost, or it is still bidding.

Only a customer's message can start one. A trade partner's thread has no such button, and neither has an architect's.

A message on a Pipeline job goes on the customer's estimate instead. [Put a customer's message on their estimate](/help/put-a-customers-message-on-their-estimate) shows how.

## After you save it

The message shows a {{chip:blue|became CO 2}} chip in the thread. The money team finds the draft in **Change orders** on the job. A line there leads back to the message. The money team prices it and sends it, as [Change our contract with the customer](/help/change-our-contract-with-the-customer#price-a-draft-from-a-message) shows.
