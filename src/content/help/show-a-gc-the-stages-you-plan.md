A GC's portal shows bills and payments. On the jobs you choose, it can also show the **stages** you plan — the window, then who is coming and when, then how far along they are. Nothing shows until you turn it on for the job and offer a stage.

## Turn it on for the job

1. Open **Edit Job → Customer → GC/Builder**. Under the GC picker, tick **Share stage dates with this GC**. It is off on every job by default.
2. Tick **Offer the next stage on its own when one passes inspection** if you want the next stage to appear without asking. Otherwise the dispatch inbox asks you each time.

The status also reads in the customer portal globe's gear.

## Offer a stage

1. On **Jobs → Subs → Work**, give the stage a window (see *set a window for a sub's stage*).
2. Beside the dates press the {{chip:blue|Offer to GC ›}} chip and confirm. The chip turns to {{chip:blue|On Summit's portal ›}}; click it (or the dates) to open the calendar, where **Take it off their portal** takes it back.

:::example What the GC reads
**Rough-in** · Between Sep 8 – Sep 19 · *Planned* — then, once the sub signs and picks, **Behar · Sep 9 – Sep 10** · *Scheduled* — then *50% along*, *Inspection next*, *Passed*. First name only; never the price, the paperwork or a phone number.
:::

## The rare bundle

When several stages fall on the same dates, press **Offer several together…** at the foot of the job, tick two or more, and {{button:blue|Offer 2 together}}. The GC sees one card with one window. Withdrawing any of them withdraws the bundle.

## After inspection

When you move a sheet to Post-inspection on its story, the next stage with a window is either offered on its own (the second switch) or a dispatch line asks *"a stage passed — offer Top-out to Summit General?"*

## When the GC asks

Under an offered stage the GC can press **Ask for other dates** — two days and a why. It lands in the dispatch inbox (*Summit General asks for Top-out Sep 22 → Oct 2 on #1004*) and on the stage row on **Jobs → Subs → Work**: *GC asked Sep 22 – Oct 2 · "framing slipped"*. Press {{button:blue|Accept}} to make that the window, or **Answer with…** to propose your own dates with a why the GC reads. If the sub had already picked days that no longer fit, their portal asks them to confirm new dates inside the new window, and the GC sees *Re-scheduling* until they do.
