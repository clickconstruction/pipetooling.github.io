Sometimes you worked and the clock has no record of it. The app would not clock you in, or you forgot. You can tell the office yourself. You do not need to text anyone your hours.

## Where it is

- In **Job Mode**, under the big card: *Worked a day the clock missed? Tell the office*.
- On the full Dashboard, at the bottom of **My Time**: {{button:outline|The clock missed a day…}}

## Fill it in

:::example It did not clock me in
**Which day** — Fri, Sep 25
**Started** 10:00 AM · **Stopped** 9:00 PM
**Which job** — the job you worked
**What happened** — The app would not let me clock in
:::

- **Which day** goes back to the start of last week. For anything older, call the office.
- **Started** and **Stopped** are the times you worked. A shift that ran past midnight is fine. Put the time you stopped, and the form reads it as the next day.
- **Which job** lists the jobs on your schedule that day and the ones you clocked on lately. If yours is not there, leave it. The office will set it.
- **What happened** is one sentence. The office reads it before they approve.

Under the times, the form says what the clock already has for that day. Report only the part it missed. Hours on top of time you already clocked are refused.

## What happens next

Press {{button:green|Send to the office}}. The hours go to the office with a pencil on them: {{chip:blue|✎ typed by you, 5 days late}}. The pencil tells whoever approves that the hours were typed, not clocked. **They count once someone approves them.** You cannot approve your own hours. Nobody can approve their own.

If you are still working right now, clock in instead. The form will not take a stop time that has not happened yet.
