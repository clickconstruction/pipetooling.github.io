The office can ask you to look at a customer's photos before there is a job. You say whether it can be priced from the photos.

## Open the look

The ask comes as a task on your [Checklist](/checklist). The task reads *Can you price this from the photos?*

Press the task's link. The link opens the customer's First look.

You see the photos, the video and what the customer wrote. Press a photo to open it full size. The video plays on the page.

## Send your answer

1. Press **I can price it from this** or **Needs a visit to price**.
2. Write a note for the office. Say what you see, what it likely needs and what to bring.
3. Press {{button:blue|Send my answer}}.

The office reads your answer on the customer's thread. Then mark the task done on your Checklist.

## When the job is booked

Once a job is made, the same photos show on the job's Job Mode card. Look for **What the customer sent** above the checklists.
