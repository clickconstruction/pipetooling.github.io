Dashboard job cards (Ready to Bill, Assigned Jobs, Superintendent Jobs) show quick icons for the customer's phone and the job's photos folder. When one is **missing**, the icon turns **red** — and tapping it asks Dispatch to add it, without you leaving the Dashboard.

## The red phone

A blue phone means the job has a customer number on file: tap it to call (with the mis-tap guard and **Log call** notes). A **red phone** means there's no number yet — tap it and a note goes straight to the Dispatch inbox:

:::example What Dispatch receives
Add a customer phone number for HCP 846 - David and Diana Uhl
:::

You'll see a confirmation toast. If someone already asked, tapping again just tells you the note is already in — no duplicates. Need the number urgently? Call Dispatch instead.

## The red photos icon

Works the same way: a blue photos icon opens the job's Customer Pictures folder, and a red one means no folder is linked — tap it to ask Dispatch to set one up.

If the job turns out to already have a folder, tapping tells you so instead of sending a note:

:::example What you'll see
{{chip:blue|This job already has a Customer Pictures link — no need to ask Dispatch.}}
:::

That happens when your screen is showing a stale copy of the job. Nothing is sent, and any earlier request for that job is cleared automatically.

## Hearing back

You don't have to call to find out. When Dispatch adds the number or the folder, your request closes on its own and you get a push:

:::example What you'll see
{{chip:green|Dispatch answered}} Handled: Add a customer phone number for HCP 846 - David and Diana Uhl — Customer phone number added
:::

The same push arrives when Dispatch closes any request of yours by hand, with the note they wrote you. Every request you've sent — waiting or answered — is listed under **My requests** on the Job Mode **Inbox** tab, and the answers also appear under Settings → Notifications.

## Related

- Dispatch sees these as ordinary open requests in the Dispatch inbox. Saving the phone number or the photos link on the job closes the matching request automatically; anything else they close with a note.
- Requests for a job that already has a photos folder close themselves — Dispatch no longer has to hunt for them.
