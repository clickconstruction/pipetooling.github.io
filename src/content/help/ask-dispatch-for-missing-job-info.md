Dashboard job cards show quick icons for the customer's phone and the job's photos folder. When one is missing, the icon turns red and a tap asks Dispatch for it.

Those cards are Ready to Bill, Assigned Jobs, and Superintendent Jobs. You tap the red icon and it asks Dispatch to add what is missing. You never leave the Dashboard.

## The red phone

A blue phone means the job has a customer number on file. Tap it to call. The mis-tap guard and **Log call** notes are there too. A **red phone** means there's no number yet. Tap it and a note goes straight to the Dispatch inbox:

:::example What Dispatch receives
Add a customer phone number for HCP 846 - David and Diana Uhl
:::

You'll see a confirmation toast. If someone already asked, tapping again just tells you the note is already in. There are no duplicates. Need the number urgently? Call Dispatch instead.

## The red photos icon

It works the same way. A blue photos icon opens the job's Customer Pictures folder. A red one means no folder is linked. Tap it to ask Dispatch to set one up.

If the job turns out to already have a folder, tapping tells you so instead of sending a note:

:::example What you'll see
{{chip:blue|This job already has a Customer Pictures link — no need to ask Dispatch.}}
:::

That happens when your screen is showing a stale copy of the job. Nothing is sent, and any earlier request for that job is cleared automatically.

## Hearing back

You don't have to call to find out. When Dispatch adds the number or the folder, your request closes on its own. You get a push:

:::example What you'll see
{{chip:green|Dispatch answered}} Handled: Add a customer phone number for HCP 846 - David and Diana Uhl — Customer phone number added
:::

The same push arrives when Dispatch closes any request of yours by hand. It carries the note they wrote you. Every request you've sent, waiting or answered, is listed under **My requests** on the Job Mode **Inbox** tab. The answers also appear under Settings → Notifications.

## Related

- Dispatch sees these as ordinary open requests in the Dispatch inbox. Saving the phone number or the photos link on the job closes the matching request automatically. Anything else they close with a note.
- A job may already have a photos folder or a phone number. Requests for it close themselves the next time the Dispatch inbox loads. A note says so. Dispatch no longer has to hunt for them.
