Dashboard job cards (Ready to Bill, Assigned Jobs, Superintendent Jobs) show quick icons for the customer's phone and the job's photos folder. When one is **missing**, the icon turns **red** — and tapping it asks Dispatch to add it, without you leaving the Dashboard.

## The red phone

A blue phone means the job has a customer number on file: tap it to call (with the mis-tap guard and **Log call** notes). A **red phone** means there's no number yet — tap it and a note goes straight to the Dispatch inbox:

:::example What Dispatch receives
Add a customer phone number for HCP 846 - David and Diana Uhl
:::

You'll see a confirmation toast. If someone already asked, tapping again just tells you the note is already in — no duplicates. Need the number urgently? Call Dispatch instead.

## The red photos icon

Works the same way: a blue photos icon opens the job's Customer Pictures folder, and a red one means no folder is linked — tap it to ask Dispatch to set one up.

## Related

- Dispatch sees these as ordinary open requests in the Dispatch inbox and clears them once the info is added.
