A trade partner can answer us from its portal. This guide shows where each thing it sends lands for the office.

Only a dev makes portal links while the portal is built. [Share a trade partner its portal](/help/share-a-trade-partner-its-portal) says how.

## A day for its quote

The company can say when its quote will come. The day lands on [Follow up](/help/follow-up-on-a-quote-from-a-trade-partner) as a chip on its card.

:::example A day the company gave in its portal
{{chip:blue|promised by Mon Oct 12}}
:::

Its story gets a line that says the company gave the day in its portal. When that day passes with no quote, the card moves to **Late on their word**.

## A quote

The company can send its quote with a tick for each scope line. It can add how long the number holds and alternates. It can also add its own schedule of values and what its quote leaves out.

The quote shows on the project's trade at once. Press {{button:blue|Compare quotes}} to read it beside the others. [Compare quotes and carry one](/help/compare-quotes-and-carry-one) says how.

A quote can be changed. Each new one is kept, and the newest counts.

## Lines you could not read

You may mark a line of a quote as not clear. The company then sees the line in its portal and answers it. Its quote comes back with that line in its number or left out.

## A pass

The company can pass on an ask before it sends a quote. The ask then reads passed, and the card leaves Follow up. The portal does not ask why, so call them if you want to know.

## A question about the plans

The company can ask about the plans until questions close. The question shows in the questions window with the company's name. Answer it the way you answer any other. [Ask the architect about the plans](/help/ask-the-architect-about-the-plans) says how.

## Who gets our emails

The company can add people and tick the kinds of email each one gets. Every kind always keeps someone. The people show in the company's window. [Look up a trade partner](/help/look-up-a-trade-partner) says where.

## A charge's answer, or a change it asks for

On a job of ours, the company can press **Agree** on a charge from us. It can also press **Dispute it** and say why. It can press **Ask for a change** to the work it signed for. Each one is saved with the job. No office screen shows them yet.

## Its report, or a change it signed

On a job we are building, the company picks each line's percent done. The Draws window shows it as reported. It signs a change we sent it, and the change becomes a line of its statement of work. [Pay a trade's draw](/help/pay-a-trades-draw) says what comes next.

## Its files

The company can pick a file with its quote, a change it asks for, or a submittal it owes. It sends a PDF or a photo of 10 MB at most.

Each file goes into the job's folder in Drive. A submittal's file goes in **Submittals**. Any other goes in **Team only**, under **From trades** and the company's name.

The quote, the change and the submittal each show the file's link. A file over 10 MB still comes by email, or as a Drive link the company pastes.
