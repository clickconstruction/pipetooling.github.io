A company we have not worked with before can send a quote right away. Someone in the office approves the company before we award it any work.

The office and estimators see Trade partners.

## Find the companies waiting

1. Open [GC projects](/gc).
2. Press {{button:outline|Trade partners}} at the top of the board.
3. Look at the box at the top called **New to us**. Each company waiting for our answer is in it.

The box is gone when no company is waiting.

:::example A company waiting for our answer
{{chip:yellow|not vetted yet}} {{button:blue|Approve}} {{button:outline|Approve up to this}} {{button:outline|Decline}}
:::

## Read their form

Each company sends us a short form about itself. The box shows what they wrote.

- **License** is their license number.
- **Insurance** is their insurance company and how much it covers.
- **Years in business** is how long they have worked.
- **References** are people we can call about them.
- **Jobs like ours** are jobs they did that look like this one.

A company whose form has not come in says so. You can still decide when you know enough about them.

## Approve or decline

1. Type a note in the box if you want one. The note is kept with your answer.
2. Press the button for your answer.

Here is what each button does.

- {{button:blue|Approve}} lets us award them work of any size.
- {{button:outline|Approve up to this}} sets the most we award them on one job. Type the amount in the box beside the button first.
- {{button:outline|Decline}} means we do not award them work. Write why in the note.

The company leaves the box once you answer. Your name and the day are kept with the answer.

## See the answer later

Find the company on its trade's card. A chip beside its name shows a limit or a decline, such as {{chip:blue|approved up to $150,000}}. Point at the chip to see who answered, the day and the note.

A company approved with no limit has no chip. Neither does a company we already knew.

## Add a company that is new to us

Leave **We have worked with them** empty when you add the company. [Add a trade partner](/help/add-a-trade-partner) shows each step.
