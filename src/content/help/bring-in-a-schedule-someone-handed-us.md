Sometimes the customer or the architect hands us their own schedule. Bring it in, and the job's schedule starts from their dates. The file is read on your screen and never kept.

Only devs can open the schedule for now. The rest of the office gets the Schedule window later.

## Start the schedule from their file

1. Open [GC projects](/gc).
2. Press {{button:outline|Schedule}} on the card of a job with no schedule yet.
3. Press **Bring in their schedule** under the first draft.
4. Pick who handed it under **Who handed it**.
5. Press **Choose a file** and pick their file. Ask for it saved as XML from Project or Primavera, or as a spreadsheet.
6. Read what it holds. Each of their activities shows where it goes and why. The ones with no place come first.
7. Pick a place for any row that needs one. Pick **Not ours** for work that is not ours.
8. Press {{button:blue|Make the schedule from it}}.

Our lines not in their file are drawn as the first draft draws them. The schedule's log names the file and who handed it.

## Take their schedule in place of a draft

A draft nobody walked or moved can still give way to their file. Press **Bring in their schedule instead** on the schedule, and follow the same steps. The draft's changes are lost.

## Take only their dates to meet

On a job being built, their file can bring in only their dates to meet.

1. Open the job's **Schedule**.
2. Press **Bring in their dates…** on the **Milestones** card.
3. Pick who handed it, then press **Choose a file**.
4. Each of their dates shows beside ours, with the difference in days.
5. Tick the dates to take. Pick which of ours each one takes the place of, or keep it a new date.
6. Press the blue **Take** button. The button names how many dates you ticked.

Nothing else on the schedule moves, and nothing is sent.
