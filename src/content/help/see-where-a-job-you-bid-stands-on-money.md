You bid the job and you still talk to the customer. The won bid now shows you where its job stands on money, right under the bid.

## Open the bid

1. Open [Bids](/bids) and find the bid you won.
2. Press {{button:outline|✎}} to open Edit Bid.
3. Look under the Job block. The money line sits under the job accounts.

:::example The money line
{{chip:green|1032}} Billed Sep 30 · **$4,200 open** · They said Oct 25 · Dana, by text, heard by Theo
:::

## Read the line

The line names the job and its stage. Then it says what is still open on its bills.

- **not billed yet** means the office has not sent a bill.
- **$4,200 open** is what the customer still owes after every payment.
- **paid** means the bills are settled.

After the money comes the customer's last word on paying. **They said Oct 25** is a day the customer named. When that day has gone by with money still open, the line turns red and counts the days past. **no word yet** means nobody has written one down.

## Send the pay page

A bill sent through Stripe has a pay page. Press {{button:outline|Send the pay page}} on the line. A text to the customer opens with the pay page filled in. If the customer has no mobile number, an email opens instead. Read it over and press Send.

## Reach the customer

The speech bubble on the line opens the customer's door. Pick **Call**, **Text**, **Email** or **Open their thread**. The door shows the customer's last lines first, so you know where the conversation stands.

## Who sees the line

The line shows for the job's people. That is the office, the job's account manager, and the estimator or account manager of the bid. When you open a job from your won bid, the app makes you its account manager. The office can change that on the job.

## Write down their word

Press {{button:outline|Write down their word}} on the line. A sheet opens with a switch at the top.

- **They'll pay by…** is for a day the customer named. Pick the day, who said it and how, then press {{button:green|Save promise}}.
- **They say they paid…** is for a customer who says the money is on its way. Pick how they paid, the day they name and who said it. Leave the amount blank when they paid all of it. Then press {{button:green|Write it down}}.

The word shows on the line and in the job's activity. The job stays billed until the money lands. The office pairs the word with the deposit. A wrong entry comes off with **Never said that** under the sheet.

Read [text a customer from the job](/help/text-a-customer-from-the-job) for the box the door opens.
