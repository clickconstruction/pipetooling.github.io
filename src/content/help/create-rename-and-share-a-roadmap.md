The roadmap picker sits at the top of the Roadmap page. It switches roadmaps, and its bottom section holds the management actions.

You open the page from the header. Tap **Roadmap** there.

## Create a roadmap

Open the picker and choose **＋ New roadmap…**. Name it. It becomes your selected roadmap, with you as an editor.

## Rename a roadmap

Open the picker and choose **✎ Rename roadmap…**. The dialog comes pre-filled with the current name. Only editors can rename. Every reference picks up the new name immediately. That includes the Review tab's Goals strip, the task cards and the picker itself.

## Share it

Press {{button:outline|Members}} next to the picker. **Editors** can change the graph and manage members. **Viewers** see the roadmap and complete tasks assigned to them. People whose accounts were archived show with an *(archived)* tag. You can remove them any time.
