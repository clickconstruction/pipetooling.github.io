The roadmap picker at the top of **Checklist → Roadmap** does more than switch roadmaps — its bottom section holds the management actions.

## Create a roadmap

Open the picker and choose **＋ New roadmap…**. Name it and it becomes your selected roadmap, with you as an editor.

## Rename a roadmap

Open the picker and choose **✎ Rename roadmap…** — the dialog comes pre-filled with the current name. Editors only; every reference (the Review tab's Goals strip, task cards, the picker itself) picks up the new name immediately.

## Share it

Press {{button:outline|Members}} next to the picker. **Editors** can change the graph and manage members; **viewers** see the roadmap and complete tasks assigned to them. People whose accounts were archived show with an *(archived)* tag — remove them any time.
