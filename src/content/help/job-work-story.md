When a customer asks **"what did you do on the job?"**, the answer is one tap away. On Jobs → Pipeline, every row's crew column shows a small clock chip with the job's man-hours ({{icon:help|gear}} e.g. **⏱ 36h 42m**). Tap it and the job's **work story** opens.

## What you see

Every clock session on the job, grouped by day, oldest first — who worked, when they punched in and out, how long, and **what they did**. The header totals it up: hours worked, people, days.

- A session with no description shows **+ add** — tap it, type what was done, and **Save** (or press Enter). The description saves straight onto the clock session, so it shows everywhere sessions do.
- An existing description has an **edit** link right beside it.

## Overlay the schedule

Turn on **Overlay schedule** in the header and the dispatch calendar's blocks for this job appear inside each day, tinted blue with a 📅 — what was *planned* next to what was *worked*. Schedule descriptions are editable the same way.

:::example Answering the customer
Mrs. Tison calls asking what the crew did last week. Open her job's work story: "Fri, Aug 15 — Michael A: Gas line pressure test — passed · Bryan: Water heater install + strapping." Read it to her, or send it.
:::

## Hand it over

- {{button:blue|Copy work summary}} puts a dated, per-person summary of every described session on your clipboard — paste it into a text or email.
- {{button:outline|Print}} opens a clean printable page (always light, like every printed document) with the full story and totals.

Who can edit: description edits follow the same permissions as editing clock sessions and dispatch blocks — office roles with pay access for sessions, dispatch editors for blocks. Anyone who can see the Pipeline can open and read the story.
