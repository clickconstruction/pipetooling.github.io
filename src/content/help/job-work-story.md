A customer asks what you did on the job. The answer is one tap away.

On Jobs → Pipeline, every row's crew column shows a small clock chip. The chip holds the job's man-hours, the hours worked by every person added up. It looks like {{icon:help|gear}} **⏱ 36h 42m**. You tap it and the job's **work story** opens.

## What you see

You see every clock session on the job, grouped by day, oldest first. Each session says who worked, when they punched in and out, and how long. It also says **what they did**. The header adds it up: hours worked, people, days.

- A session with no description shows **+ add**. You tap it, type what was done, and tap **Save**. Enter saves too. The description saves straight onto the clock session. So it shows everywhere sessions do.
- An existing description has an **edit** link right beside it.

## Overlay the schedule

You turn on **Overlay schedule** in the header. The dispatch calendar's blocks for this job then appear inside each day. They are tinted blue with a 📅. So you see what was planned next to what was worked. You edit schedule descriptions the same way.

:::example Answering the customer
Mrs. Tison calls asking what the crew did last week. Open her job's work story: "Fri, Aug 15 — Michael A: Gas line pressure test — passed · Bryan: Water heater install + strapping." Read it to her, or send it.
:::

## Hand it over

- You tap {{button:blue|Copy work summary}}. It puts a dated, per-person summary of every described session on your clipboard. You paste it into a text or email.
- You tap {{button:outline|Print}}. It opens a clean printable page with the full story and totals. The page is always light, like every printed document.

Who can edit: description edits follow the same permissions as editing clock sessions and dispatch blocks. Office roles with pay access edit sessions. Dispatch editors edit blocks. Anyone who can see the Pipeline can open and read the story.
