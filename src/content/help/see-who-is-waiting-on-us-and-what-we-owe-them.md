Follow up shows the people waiting on us for an answer. The same tab shows what we still owe each of them.

The office team and estimators can use it. Open [Follow up](/gc?view=followUp) on GC projects. The number on its button counts the people waiting on us. Each person counts once, even with rows in two lists.

The Dashboard's **Needs you** card shows the same count on a **GC projects** line, like *2 people are waiting on us*. Press {{button:outline|Follow up}} on that line to come straight here.

## The lists

The tab has three lists at the top.

- **Needs you** holds notes whose day has come, and customers who wrote on their page.
- **Needs a reply** holds everyone who wrote or called and has no answer yet.
- **We owe them** holds what we still have to do for someone, with its day.

Below them, **We are waiting on them** lists the quotes and papers we wait on from others. [Follow up on a quote from a trade partner](/help/follow-up-on-a-quote-from-a-trade-partner) covers that list.

Press anywhere on a row to open the person's thread at their message. The buttons on a row each do their own thing.

## Needs a reply

The person waiting longest is at the top. The {{chip:yellow|Our move · 2 days}} chip says how long they have waited. The row shows their last message.

- Press {{button:blue|Reply}} to open their thread and answer. A number we do not know shows {{button:blue|Who is this?}} instead.
- Press {{button:outline|No reply needed}} when nothing is owed. The row leaves Follow up.
- Press {{button:outline|Remind me Monday}} to hide the row until Monday morning.

A missed call or a voicemail also shows {{button:outline|Call back in Quo}}. The button stays off until the phone link is on. Call from your own phone until then.

## When they gave a day or asked about the plans

A message may give a day, like a quote by Wednesday. The row then shows a button like {{button:outline|A day was heard: Wed Oct 7 · say what it is}}. Press it to open the message and write the day down.

A question about the plans shows {{button:outline|Ask the architect}}. The button shows only when the message is on a GC job. Press it to ask the job's architect.

- The question goes on the job, under **Questions about the plans**, with who asked.
- The message is marked as asked, and the button goes away.
- The email goes to the job's architect. A line in the person's thread says it was sent.

Sometimes the email cannot go. The words on screen then say why. The question still waits on the job. Send it from there, as [Ask the architect about the plans](/help/ask-the-architect-about-the-plans) shows.

## Who has it

Each row has a pill that says who has the conversation.

- {{button:outline|Nobody has it · Take it}} means nobody has claimed it. Whoever answers first takes it.
- {{button:outline|You have this}} means it is yours. The team leaves it to you.
- {{button:outline|Rosa has this}} means a teammate has it.

Press the pill to change who has it.

1. Press {{button:outline|I'll take it}} to make it yours.
2. Or press a teammate's name to hand it to them. Each name says how many waiting rows they hold.
3. Or press {{button:outline|Nobody}} to put it back for whoever answers first.

The person's thread records each change and who made it.

## Needs you

A team note can carry a day to come back. On that morning the person shows in **Needs you** with the note.

- Press {{button:blue|Open the thread}} to read the whole conversation.
- Press {{button:outline|Done with it}} once it is dealt with. The day clears, and the note stays in the thread.
- Press {{button:outline|Another day}} to bring it back on the next working day.

A customer who wrote on their page shows here too, with {{chip:purple|portal · wrote in the portal}}. Answer them by text or email, like any other message.

A controller or a dev also sees the contract clause changes waiting on their approval. [Change a contract clause](/help/change-a-contract-clause#approve-or-decline-a-change) shows how to approve or decline one.

## We owe them

Each row says what we owe and by when, like {{chip:blue|we owe the start date · by Mon Oct 5}}. The chip turns red once that day has passed. The line under the name says who marked it and when.

Press the chip to open their thread at the reply box. What we owe stays our move until a note or a message marks it done.
