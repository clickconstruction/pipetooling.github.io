An email in your own Gmail can go on a job in Messages. Forward it to our team or bids inbox. The email then lands on the thread of the person who wrote it.

## Before you start

The app reads both inboxes only while **Read bids@** is on. The owner or a dev turns it on in [Phone and texts](/settings?tab=settings-phone-texts). The guide [turn on reading the bids inbox](/help/turn-on-reading-the-bids-inbox) shows the switch.

Forward from your work email, the address you sign in with. The app takes a forward only from someone on the team.

## Forward an email you got

1. Open the email in Gmail.
2. Press **Forward**.
3. In **To**, type the inbox that person writes to, team@clickplumbing.com or bids@clickplumbing.com. Replies from their thread then go from that inbox. The guide [set up the two inboxes](/help/set-up-the-two-inboxes#who-each-inbox-is-for) says who each one is for.
4. Above the email, write a note for the team if you like.
5. Press **Send**.

About a minute later, the email shows in [Messages](/messages) on the thread of the person who wrote it. Your own thread does not get a copy. The email keeps the time they sent it. Files on the email come with it, each up to 25 MB.

Your note lands on the same thread as a team note. Only the team sees a note.

A writer the app does not know yet shows in New as **Who is this?** The guide [sort new messages before anyone answers them](/help/sort-new-messages) shows what to press.

:::example Dale's thread, a minute after Marisol forwards his email
**Email** · Dale Okafor · forwarded to bids@ by Marisol Vega &nbsp; {{chip:gray|JP1077}}

**Re: JP1077 water heater**

The owner wants the 50 gallon, not the 40. Can you price the difference?

**Note** · Marisol Vega · For the job. He called about this too.
:::

## Which job it lands on

The app reads the subject the writer gave the email. Gmail shows that subject inside your forward, on its **Subject:** line. The subject line of your forward itself is not read.

- A job's number in that subject ties the email to that job.
- The number reads as the app shows it. A bid's is like *BP523*, a GC project's like *#42* and a Pipeline job's like *JP1077*.
- A job's whole name ties it too, when the writer is on that job.
- With neither, the email lands on the job with the writer's star.
- With no star, the email sits on the person with no job.

:::example Where three forwarded emails land
*Re: JP1077 water heater* lands on JP1077. *Oak Hill Clinic plumbing*, from the builder on that bid, lands on the bid. *Quick question* lands on the job with their star, or on no job.
:::

## Put it on the right job

On the thread, press {{chip:gray|no job}} on the email and pick the job. On a phone, press and hold the email instead.

Did it land on the wrong job? Open the email and press **change** beside **Tied to**.

The guide [find every message with a person in one place](/help/find-every-message-with-a-person-in-one-place#tie-a-message-to-a-job) shows every way to tie a message.

## An email you send from your own mailbox

The app never sees an email you send from your own Gmail. Two ways bring it to the thread.

- Copy team@ or bids@ when you write. Your email lands on the thread of the person you wrote to, under your name. A job's number in your subject ties it to that job.
- Or forward it from your **Sent** folder, the same way as above. The forward lands on the thread of the person you wrote to.

Before an email goes from Messages, its window names the inbox to copy.

Put the job's number in the subject of each email you start. Replies to that email keep the number. A forward of a reply then ties to that job on its own.

## What it does not do

- An email you never forward or copy stays in your Gmail only.
- Nothing changes in your own Gmail.
- A forward from your personal address lands as if that address wrote to us.
- A forward of an email between teammates lands nowhere.
- Each forward lands one more copy. Forward an email once. Skip one that our inbox got already.
