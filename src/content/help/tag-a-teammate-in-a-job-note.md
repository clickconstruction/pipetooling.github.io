A job note used to sit on the job until someone opened that row. Now you can tag a teammate in a note. The note reaches that person, and it waits there until they answer.

## Tag someone in a note

You tag from a job's note box. These steps use the box on a Pipeline row.

1. On the job's row, press {{button:blue|+ Add}}.
2. Type your note. Type **@** where the name goes. A row of names opens at the box.
3. Keep typing a few letters. The row gets shorter with each letter. The job's crew is first.
4. Press the name. You can also use the up and down arrow keys and Enter.
5. Press {{button:blue|Post}}.

The **@** button in the box opens the same row.

Each name is a big press, with what the person does under it. On a phone the keyboard stays up when you press one.

The row stays up when you type a space. Type "@chris b" and every Chris whose last name starts with B is still offered.

:::example A note with one teammate tagged
Will tag: {{chip:blue|@Dana Whitlock}}

Did the check for the change order come in yet? @Dana Whitlock
:::

A tag is the name you pressed. A name you only typed is plain words. So a note like "arrive @ 8:45" tags nobody. A note like "@will call" tags nobody named Will.

The tagged name is shaded in your words while you write. The name also shows above the box, after **Will tag**.

## Take a tag off before you post

Press Backspace right after the shaded name. The whole name comes off in one press.

You can also press the × on the name above the box.

If you change a letter inside a shaded name, the tag comes off. Typing the name back does not tag. Press the name in the row again.

## Who you can tag

The list offers the office and the crew of that job. The list does not offer a sub or an estimator. The list does not offer someone who has left.

If no name shows for the letters you typed, that person cannot open this job's notes.

## What the note shows after you post

The name in your note becomes a chip. After the note you read who it waits on.

:::example The words after a tagged note
{{chip:blue|@Dana Whitlock}} waiting on Dana

{{chip:blue|@Dana Whitlock}} waiting on Dana · 4d

{{chip:blue|@Dana Whitlock}} answered by Dana
:::

The waiting words turn amber after two working days. Nothing reminds the person again. Ask them yourself if it is urgent.

## When someone tags you

A red number shows above **Jobs** in the header. The number counts the notes that tagged you and still wait.

Press **Jobs**. The **Tagged job notes** section is at the top of the Pipeline. The oldest tag is on top.

On a phone the number also rides on the menu button.

Your phone also gets a notice, if phone notices are switched on in Settings. A notice outside your **Push me** hours is held until you are back.

If you have no Pipeline, open the Dashboard. The **Needs you** card has a **Tagged notes** line. Press **Open** and one list opens. The list holds your job tags and your task tags.

Tagging in a task note is its own guide: [tag a teammate in a task note](/help/tag-a-teammate-in-a-task-note).

## Answer a tag

Each tagged note has its own box.

1. Type your answer in the box under the note.
2. Press {{button:blue|Post}}.

Your answer goes on that job's notes. The tag clears, and the person who asked is told.

You can also answer from the job's own row. Any note you type on that job clears your tag there.

Opening the job does not clear a tag. Reading it does not clear it. Only an answer does, or the press below.

## Say no reply is needed

Some tags only need reading. Press {{button:outline|No reply needed}} on the note. The tag clears and no note is added. The person who asked sees that you pressed it.

## Ask a question back

Your answer can tag the person who asked. The name of the person who asked is offered above the answer box, after **Tag**. Press it, type your question and post. Now your note waits on them.

## See what you asked

Under the tagged notes is **You asked**. **You asked** lists every note where you tagged someone.

- **no answer yet** means the person has not answered.
- **answered** shows their answer.
- **no reply needed** means they read it and pressed that.

A new answer counts in the red number above **Jobs** until you have seen it. Seeing it in the list clears it. You press nothing.

## Fold the section

Press **Fold** on the section's top line to shut it. Press **Open** to bring it back. With more than three tags waiting, press **Show the rest** to see them all.

## Tag from the job's own window

The note box in the job's window has the same **@** button and the same row of names. So does the box in Job Mode. A note you type there answers a tag the same way.

A note typed with no signal is kept on your phone. The tag goes with the note when the note is sent.

## What does not clear a tag

A stamp such as Arrived clears nothing. A % complete line clears nothing.
