The short Dashboard is a shorter home page for a leader or a superintendent. The page shows only what waits on you, then your day, then one door to everything else.

The full Dashboard holds every section the company has. The short one holds a list you can finish. The office keeps working the rest, and the page says how much that is.

## What is on it

- **Waiting on you** lists the things only you can do. Each row has one button that says the act.
- Lien notices to sign come first when a deadline is close. Press {{button:green|Open 650 to sign}} to open that notice.
- A run the office prepared for a GC has {{button:blue|Decide}}. GC means the general contractor.
- A lien waiver that needs your name has {{button:amber|Sign it}}.
- A job note that tagged you has {{button:amber|Open}}. A teammate is waiting on your answer.
- The weekly look at the line budgets has {{button:outline|Review}}.
- **My Schedule** shows your day under the list.
- One line says how many other things the office is handling.
- **Everything else** opens the full Dashboard.

The rows are the same lines the [Needs you card](/help/needs-you-card) shows. A press here opens the same screen.

## When nothing waits

The list says **Nothing waiting on you.** The office puts a paper there when it needs your signature or your word.

The page reads the Lien desk before it says that. While it reads, the list says **Reading what waits on you…** If the read fails, the page says so. Press **Open the Lien desk** to check the desk yourself.

## Everything else

Press **Everything else** to open the full Dashboard for this visit. On a phone each section is folded to one line. Tap a line to open it.

Press **Back to the short Dashboard** at the top to return. The next time you open the app you are on the short page again.

Some of your own lines may sit behind the door. When they do, the door says how many.

## The Dashboard tab at the bottom

On a phone, the **Dashboard** tab in the bottom bar opens the short page. The tab does so with Dispatch Mode on too.

The tab is also the way home. After **Everything else**, press **Dashboard** once to return to the short page.

## For a superintendent

A superintendent can be on **Short** too. The office sets it, or you set it yourself in **Settings**.

With Job Mode off you open onto this page. **Waiting on you** lists a job note that tagged you. The lien papers are the leader's, so they never show on your list.

**Clock In** sits behind **Everything else**. The door's second line says so.

With Job Mode on you get the short Job Mode page. The short page keeps your job card on top and folds the rest. See [the short Job Mode page](/help/job-mode-clocking#the-short-job-mode-page).

## Turn it on or off

:::steps

Open **Settings**, then **Your dashboard**.

Find **Dashboard view** at the top.

Press **Short** or **Full**.

:::

The office can set it for a leader or a superintendent. Open the person's desk on **People**. Under **Dashboard view** press **Short** or **Full**. The person sees the change the next time the Dashboard opens.

**Full** is the Dashboard as it always was. Nobody is on **Short** until someone presses it.
