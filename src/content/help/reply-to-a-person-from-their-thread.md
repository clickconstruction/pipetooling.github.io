The reply box sits at the foot of every thread in Messages. Write a text, an email or a team note to the person there.

## Open the reply box

1. Go to [GC projects](/gc) and press {{button:outline|Messages}}.
2. Press a person's row. Their thread opens as a window.
3. The reply box is at the bottom of the window.

To find a person first, see [find every message with a person in one place](/help/find-every-message-with-a-person-in-one-place).

## Choose what to write

The row above the box has three kinds. The one you chose has an orange ring.

- {{button:outline|Text}} goes to their phone from the company number.
- {{button:outline|Email}} goes to their inbox under our letterhead.
- {{button:outline|Note}} stays with the team. They never see it.

The box starts as a text when their number can take one. Otherwise it starts as an email.

While the box is empty, a grey line in it says who the message is from. It always names the company, never you.

## Use a quick reply

Quick replies are lines the office types every day. They sit above the box for a text or an email.

1. Press **Quick replies** above the box. The lines open, and this computer keeps your choice.
2. Press a line. It fills the box. Nothing sends.
3. Change the words if you like, then send as usual.

The lines for the step they are on come first, then the rest for their kind of person. The lines for anyone come last. In each group the lines you use most come first. A press counts, so the order follows the office.

While you type, the lines fold away. They come back when the box is empty.

## Find a reply

Press **⤢ Find** in the quick replies. A window opens with their last message on top. It also says where they are in the flow, and what finishes that step. Press **the step’s card ›** to open the step itself.

Every line shows with room to read. Type in the narrow box to find a line by its words. Press a line to fill the box.

## Add or change a quick reply

Press **Edit** in the quick replies. The quick replies window opens. There you add a line, change one, or take one off. The guide [edit the quick replies](/help/edit-the-quick-replies) walks through it.

When the narrow box finds no line, a button offers to add your words as a quick reply. The window opens with your words in a new line.

## Tie it to a job

**Tie to** sits under the box on the left. It says which job the message lands on.

- In a thread narrowed to one job, it starts on that job.
- Otherwise it starts on the job with their star.
- Press it to choose another job, or **On the person, no job**.

## Send a text or an email

1. Write the message.
2. Press {{button:blue|Send text…}} or {{button:blue|Send email…}}.
3. A window shows the message the way they will see it. Nothing goes yet.
4. Press {{button:blue|Send as email}} to send it.

Press {{button:outline|Change it}} to go back to the box. The window has an **Email** tab and a **Text** tab. Press either one to see the message the other way.

### An email

The window shows the email the way their inbox opens it.

- The subject comes from the job and your first line. Press **change** beside it to type your own for this email only.
- The greeting uses the name they go by.
- Your name, your title and your cell sign it.
- The letterhead is the company the job is for.

Their answer comes to your own inbox. Bring anything that matters back to the thread. A copy of every email is kept in Documents.

### After this sends

Under the message, **After this sends** says whose move it is once it goes.

- Press **Their move** when this message leaves nothing on us.
- Press **Our move** when something is still on us.

When we owe them something, **Their move** marks it done by this message. **Our move** keeps it open.

When nothing is owed yet, **Our move** opens two blanks. Write what we still owe and by when. The thread keeps it, and Follow up lists it under We owe them.

### A text

Texts wait until the phone is switched on. Until then the **Text** tab shows the text, and its Send stays off. Send it as an email instead.

Once the phone is on, the window shows their phone with our last texts to them. A first text carries the opt-out line the carriers require. So does a text after 30 quiet days. The line shows as its own grey bubble, so it never reads as your words.

After a quiet spell, press **Leave it off this time** to hold the line back on that one text.

If they texted STOP, the box says so. The app will not text that number.

## Send a file with a message

1. Write the message as a text or an email.
2. Press {{button:outline|Attach}}. A list of the job's files opens.
3. Press a file to add it. Or press **A file from this computer** or **A photo**.

Each file shows under the box as a chip. Press its **×** to take it off. A file goes on the message once.

Press **Preview** beside a file to read its facts first. Then press **Open in a tab** to see it, or **Attach it** to add it.

The send window shows how each file goes:

- A small file goes with the email as the file.
- A plan set always goes as our link.
- A file over 10 MB goes as our link.
- Files over 20 MB together go as links, the largest first.
- A text cannot carry a file, so each goes as our link.

When they have a portal and the file has a page there, our link opens their portal at that page. Otherwise it opens the one file. We see when a link is opened, never who.

A file from this computer goes into the job's Team only folder before the email leaves. A photo goes into the job's photos. The copy of the email in Documents keeps its files.

## Add a note for the team

1. Press {{button:outline|Note}}. The box turns yellow.
2. Write the note.
3. Press {{button:blue|Add the note}}.

The note goes straight into the thread. Only the team sees it.

Press **↩ Bring it back on a day…** before you add the note. The row comes back to Follow up that morning. Pick a day, or type one. Press **No day ×** to take the day off.

## Mark what we owe done with a note

The strip above the box may say what we owe them. Its **Mark it done, with a team note** turns the box into that note.

1. Write how it got done, or why nothing is owed.
2. Press {{button:blue|Done, with this note}}.

Press **Not done yet** to go back to the box as it was.
