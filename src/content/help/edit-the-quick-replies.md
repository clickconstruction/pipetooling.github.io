The quick replies window holds every line the office types every day. Change a line, add one, take one off the list, or read who changed it.

## Open the window

1. Open a person's thread in [Messages](/messages). A trade partner's thread is in [GC projects](/gc) under {{button:outline|Messages}}.
2. Choose **Text** or **Email** in the reply box.
3. Press **Quick replies** above the box, then press **Edit**.

The **Find a reply** window has the same door. Press **Edit quick replies** beside its narrow box.

## Read the list

The tabs cut the list. **All** holds the team's lines and yours. **Trade partners**, **Customers**, **Architects** and **Anyone** hold the lines for each kind of person. **Mine** holds the lines only you see. **Removed** shows once a line is taken off.

The lines sit in groups by who they are for, then by step. In each group the lines used most come first.

- **Used** says how often the office pressed the line, and how often you did.
- **Added** says who wrote the line and when.
- **Last change** says who changed it last and when.

Type in the search box to find a line by its words.

## Change a line

1. Press a line. It opens in place.
2. Change the words, who it is for, or its step.
3. Press {{button:blue|Save}}.

Saving keeps the old words in the history, with your name and today. The history under the line lists every change, newest first. It also says who used the line and when it was last used.

## Add a line

1. Press {{button:blue|+ Add a line}}.
2. Write the line.
3. Choose who it is for, and its step if it has one. The app guesses both from the words until you choose.
4. Tick **Just for me** to keep the line on your own strip.
5. Press {{button:blue|Add the line}}.

When a search in the quick replies finds no line, a button offers to add your words. This window opens with your words in the new line.

## Remove a line or bring it back

1. Press the line, then press **Remove this line…**.
2. Press **Remove it** to take it off the list. Press **Keep it** to leave it.

A removed line leaves the strip and the list. It stays in the history.

To bring it back, open the **Removed** tab and press the line. Then press {{button:blue|Bring it back}}.
