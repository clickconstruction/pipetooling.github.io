The app can push a reply to the phone of whoever has the conversation. It works for a customer, a lead or a sub. The owner or a dev turns it on.

## Turn it on

1. Open [Phone and texts](/settings?tab=settings-phone-texts) in Settings.
2. Under **Reply push**, press the {{button:outline|Off}} switch.
3. Press {{button:blue|Turn it on}}.

The switch now reads {{button:green|On}}. The line under it says *Pushes go out within a minute.*

## Who gets the push

- Whoever has the conversation gets it right away. [Who gets a new message](/help/choose-who-gets-a-new-message) says how the app picks them.
- The office hears too when nobody on the team writes back during its wait. The wait starts at 15 minutes. [Change the wait](#change-the-wait) says how to set it.
- Whoever has it hears once more at 50 minutes when still nobody has answered. The push says the hour is close. It never comes after hours.
- After the office closes, the office hears first. The reply waits for the morning. [Set the office hours](#set-the-office-hours) says when that is. [Open around the clock](#open-around-the-clock) says how to have no after hours at all.
- A reply to one of the Lien desk's letters goes to the office alone.

A person gets the push only on a phone where they turned on push notifications. [Settings basics](/help/settings-basics#push-notifications) shows how.

## Change the wait

1. Under **Reply push**, find **The office hears after**.
2. Type the minutes. 0 means the office hears at once, with whoever has it.
3. Press {{button:blue|Save the wait}}.

The line under the number says what it means. The wait can be 0 to 240 minutes.

## Set the office hours

1. Under **Office hours**, press each day the office is in.
2. Pick the open time and the close time.
3. Press {{button:blue|Save the hours}}.

The line under the times reads them back, like *Mon to Fri, 7:30 AM to 5:00 PM*. After hours the office hears first. The reply waits for the morning. The app sends no answer of its own after hours.

One more line reads the tech's hour, like *A tech who texts a customer keeps the replies until 5:00 PM*. A tech who texts a customer from a job keeps that customer's replies until then. A dev sets the hour.

Only the owner or a dev can change the hours. Everyone else reads the lines.

## Open around the clock

When the office answers at every hour, press the switch instead of picking days and times.

1. Under **Office hours**, press the {{button:outline|Off}} switch next to *The office is open around the clock*.
2. Press {{button:blue|Save the hours}}.

The switch now reads {{button:green|On}}. The days and times fold away. The line reads *Open around the clock*. Whoever has a reply hears at once. The office hears after the wait, at 3 AM as at 3 PM. No reply waits for the morning. The tech's hour does not change.

The app keeps your days and times under the switch. Press the {{button:green|On}} switch and save to use them again.

## Turn it off

Press the {{button:green|On}} switch. The line under it says *Off: nobody is pushed when a customer answers.* Replies still land in Messages as before.
