The app can push a reply to the phone of whoever has the conversation. It works for a customer, a lead or a sub. The owner or a dev turns it on.

## Turn it on

1. Open [Phone and texts](/settings?tab=settings-phone-texts) in Settings.
2. Under **Reply push**, press the {{button:outline|Off}} switch.
3. Press {{button:blue|Turn it on}}.

The switch now reads {{button:green|On}}. The line under it says *Pushes go out within a minute.*

## Who gets the push

- Whoever has the conversation gets it right away. [Who gets a new message](/help/choose-who-gets-a-new-message) says how the app picks them.
- The office hears too when nobody on the team writes back during its wait. The wait starts at 15 minutes.
- After the office closes, the office hears first. The reply waits for the morning.
- A reply to one of the Lien desk's letters goes to the office alone.

A person gets the push only on a phone where they turned on push notifications. [Settings basics](/help/settings-basics#push-notifications) shows how.

## Turn it off

Press the {{button:green|On}} switch. The line under it says *Off: nobody is pushed when a customer answers.* Replies still land in Messages as before.
