When you clock out after a day on bids, the app lists the bids you worked on. You do not type them. You check the list, fix it if it is wrong, and clock out.

The list shows for anyone whose day held a bid. An estimator sees it every day.

## What the sheet shows

Press {{button:red|Clock Out}} on the Dashboard. The **Clock out** sheet opens.

:::cards

:::card Your day
A strip from your clock in to now. Each bid has one colour. Grey is time the app did not see.
:::

:::card Bids you worked on today
The bids you changed today. Each row has a ticked box in the bid's colour.
:::

:::card Opened, nothing changed
The bids you had open and did not change. Each row has an empty box.
:::

:::

Every row reads the same way. First the size, then the tab. {{chip:gray|3 hours · Takeoffs and Pricing}}

The sizes are rough. The sizes are rounded, and nobody is billed by a size.

## Fix the list

:::steps

1. To take a bid off, press its ticked box. The row stays, struck through. Press the box again to put it back.
2. To add a bid you only opened, press its empty box under **Opened, nothing changed**.
3. To add a bid the app missed, press it under **Add a bid the app missed**. Those are the bids on your board.
4. For any other bid, use **Search to choose other job or bid** lower on the sheet. A bid you pick there joins the list.

:::

## Clock out

With one bid on the list, {{button:red|Complete clock out}} is ready at once. The button says how many bids it keeps.

**Anything else?** is for work the app cannot see, such as a call or a site walk. You can leave it empty.

The box may open on the plan you typed at clock in. A line under the box says **From your plan at clock in. Change it or leave it.** Words left in the box are kept under the bids in the note the office reads.

:::example A day the app saw nothing
The sheet says **The app saw no bid today.** Add the bids you worked on, or type what you did under **What did you work on?** Then clock out.
:::

## What the app can see

The app sees a bid you change. The app also sees a bid you have open on the Bids page while you press, type or scroll.

The app does not see a takeoff in CountTooling, a call, or plans on paper. Add those bids yourself.

## Who sees your list

The office reads your list beside your hours, where your note has always been. The time behind each size is read by you, the owner and a dev.
