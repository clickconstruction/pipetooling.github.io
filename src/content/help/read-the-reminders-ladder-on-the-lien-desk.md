The Lien desk's Do now draws a Reminders strip above the rows. The strip has four steps, and every sent notice stands on one of them. This guide shows what each step means and why some rows go pale when you press a step.

## Press a step

Press a step on the Reminders strip. The rows on that step stay bright. Every other row fades to a third. A line under the strip reads *Showing 3 · Letter two* with the count of bright rows. The line says the rest are faded, not held. Press {{button:outline|Show all 15}} to bring every row back. A second press on the same step does the same.

A faded row is not blocked. The row's button still works. The row is waiting on its own press, not on the step you pressed.

## The four steps

:::cards

:::card 1 Reminder due

The packet went and nothing came back. From day 7 the desk offers {{button:blue|Send a courtesy notice}}. One press sends an email and a text in plain words. The office presses it. A notice before day 7 waits here and is not counted. The step reads *· 2 before day 7* beside its count.

:::

:::card 2 Reminder sent

A courtesy notice went. The desk waits a week for an answer. After a week with nothing, another may go. From day 10 the row leads with {{button:blue|Send letter two}} instead.

:::

:::card 3 Letter two

Letter two is counsel's certified second letter. The desk offers it from day 10 and calls it overdue from day 14. A notice stands here when letter two is due and the courtesy is held. A notice also stands here while a letter two is drafting, awaiting the leader, in the run or sent. The leader presses {{button:blue|Send letter two}}.

:::

:::card 4 Closed

The bill is paid. Or the GC authorized direct pay in writing. Or the owner called, or they answered, or the affidavit is filed. Or no reminder may go at all. Nothing is left to press. The notice reads under Sent.

:::

:::

## Why two rows at the same day get different buttons

Two notices can both be at day 21 and both overdue for letter two. One row offers {{button:blue|Send a courtesy notice}} and the other offers {{button:blue|Send letter two}}. The difference is whether a courtesy notice may go.

:::fold When the courtesy is held

A reminder may never name a lien day the law would not allow. So the desk holds the courtesy in four cases. The property is a homestead. The job has no approved work month yet. The filing window closed. The affidavit is due within 7 days, so you file it instead. Counsel's letter still goes, so the row offers letter two. The card on the row's dots names the reason.

:::

## See the names behind a count

Press a step on the Reminders strip. The notices on that step with no row on the list read under the strip. Each notice has {{button:outline|Open}}, which opens the notice under Sent. The step's rule reads there too, in one line. Read [send a courtesy notice before a lien](/help/send-a-courtesy-notice-before-a-lien) for the courtesy itself.
