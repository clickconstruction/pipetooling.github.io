When the work on a GC job changes, write a change order and send it to the customer to sign. A signed one changes their price.

A change order is for a job we won. Each one gets the next number on the job. Once it goes to the customer, it keeps what it said. Only a dev sees the window while it is built.

## Write one

1. Press {{button:outline|GC}} on the Bids page to open [GC projects](/gc). Press {{button:outline|Change orders}} on the job's card.
2. Press {{button:blue|New change order}}.
3. Type what is changing, with the sheet if there is one.
4. Pick the reason. Pick whose work it is, or **Our own work**.
5. Type the days it adds to the job.
6. Press **Added work** or **Credit, work coming out**. Type what it costs us.
7. The price starts at the cost plus our fee. Type over it to change it.
8. Press {{button:blue|Save the draft}}.

A draft can be deleted. Press {{button:outline|Delete the draft}} under it.

## Send it to the customer

1. Press {{button:blue|Send for signature}} on the draft.
2. Copy the words the window shows. Send them from your own email with the change order.

The app does not email change orders yet. The change order now waits on the customer.

## Record their answer

1. Pick the day they answered.
2. Press {{button:blue|They signed}} or {{button:outline|They declined}}.

A signed change order adds its price and its days. Pick how much of its work is done. Its line on the bill follows that.
