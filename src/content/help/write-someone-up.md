You saw something on a job that HR should know about. You can write it up on your phone while it is fresh.

Your Dashboard has one press for it, {{button:outline|Write someone up}}. The press sits right under the row of squares at the top. It opens a sheet and saves nothing by itself.

## Pick the person

The sheet opens on a list of people.

1. Look under **With you today**. These are the people on your job today.
2. Look under **With you this week** for someone from an earlier day.
3. Type a name in **Search everyone** to find anyone else.
4. Press the person's name.

You are never on your own list.

## Say what happened

The sheet fills in three things for you. They are who it is about, when it happened and which job. Press the day, the time or the job to change one.

1. Pick a kind under **What kind?**. The last kind is **Good work**, for something done well.
2. Type what you saw under **What did you see?**. Give the times.
3. Or press {{button:outline|Talk it through}} and say it. The phone types your words. No sound is kept.
4. Press a name under **Who saw it?** for each person who saw it too. **I did** is already on.
5. Answer whether you have talked to the person about it.

:::example Words that hold up
"The trench by the east wall was open at lunch with no cones or tape. I found it at 12:20 pm. I told Dana at 12:30 pm and Dana taped it off."
:::

Write what you saw and heard. Leave out what you think it means. *Late three mornings this week* holds up later. *Lazy* does not.

The button at the bottom says what is still missing. When nothing is missing it reads {{button:blue|Read it back}}.

## Read it back and send it

**Read it back** shows the whole write-up, one fact a line, as HR will get it.

1. Read every line.
2. Press {{button:outline|Fix it}} to change something.
3. Press {{button:blue|Send write-up to HR}} to send it.

Nothing is saved until you press **Send write-up to HR**. A stray press outside the sheet closes it and keeps your words. The press on your Dashboard then says one is not finished.

## What happens after

HR reads your write-up before it goes on the person's file. Nobody else on the crew can see it. The app tells the person nothing.

The sheet shows **Your write-ups** with where each one stands.

:::example Where a write-up stands
{{chip:yellow|Waiting on HR}} means HR has not read it yet.
{{chip:green|On file}} means it is on the person's record.
{{chip:gray|Set aside by HR}} means HR closed it, with the reason under it.
{{chip:gray|Taken back}} means you took it back.
:::

You see only your own write-ups. You never see the rest of a person's file.

## Add to it or take it back

Open **Write someone up**, then press **Your write-ups** at the bottom of the list.

- **Add to it** writes more about the same thing. HR gets it as a second note beside the first.
- **Take it back** works while a write-up still waits on HR. You give a reason, and HR can read both.

A write-up is never changed after it is sent. Adding to it is how you correct one.
