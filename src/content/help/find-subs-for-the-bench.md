**Prospects → Subs** is the calling deck for trade companies we have not hired yet. A sub is a company that does a trade we buy out on a GC project. You call them, log how it ended, and press {{button:purple|Add to the bench ✓}} when they will quote for us.

The deck works like the customer deck. The queue, the timer, callbacks and the Activity report are the same. Only the facts on the card and the last press change.

On the Board tab, the {{button:outline|?}} at the end of the For row opens a card that says what each part of the board holds.

## Add a sub

Press {{button:blue|New Prospect}} and pick **A sub** at the top. Type the person you reach first, with their title and cell. For the company, open their Google Maps profile and press Share. Paste what it copies into **Paste from Google Maps**. The company, the address and the link fill in. Add their license. Then the sub's own facts:

- **Trades**: the trades they do, with a comma between two. Use the bench's own names, like Concrete or Fire sprinkler.
- **Goes up to**: how many miles their crews drive. Leave it blank if they did not say.
- **Language**: English or Spanish. The bench sends them papers in it.
- **Where we found them**: a GC's sub list, PlanHub, a permit, a referral, or anything you type.

Save puts them in the Subs queue. The card shows the trades as chips, with the range, the language and the source under the name.

:::example A company from a GC's sub list
You type Bexar Fire Protection, Fire sprinkler and HVAC, 40 miles, English. The card reads "goes up to 40 mi · English · from A GC's sub list". The queue shows them as {{chip:green|never called}}.
:::

## Make the call

The card at the top is who you are calling. Press the {{button:blue|📞 phone}} button to dial. Then log how it ended:

- {{button:amber|Didn't Answer}} or {{button:green|Answered}} logs the call with your note attached.
- {{button:outline|Can't reach}} drops a dead number from the queue.
- {{button:outline|Not a fit}} is for a company we would not hire.
- {{button:purple|Add to the bench ✓}} is for a company that will quote for us.

## Add to the bench

{{button:purple|Add to the bench ✓}} opens **Add a company**, the same window Trade partners uses. It is filled from the card, with the person's cell, the license and the Google Maps link. Check the trades and the miles, then press {{button:blue|Save to the bench}}.

The company lands on **GC projects → Trade partners** under each of its trades. It can quote at once. An award waits until someone approves it there. The prospect leaves the queue and sits under **On the bench** on the Prospect List.

Tick **We have worked with them before** only when we have. A company we know skips the vetting step on the bench.

When a project is picked in **For**, the window offers one more tick. It reads *Ask them to quote Oak Run Clinic · Fire sprinkler now*. Leave it ticked and the company is asked on that job as it lands. It is the same ask Trade partners makes. The invitation goes out as asks do today. The card then reads *asked* under the trade on Trade partners.

## The path to the bench

A strip sits over the Subs lane. It reads {{chip:gray|To call}} → {{chip:gray|Called}} → {{chip:gray|Talked}} → {{chip:purple|On the bench}} → {{chip:purple|Quoted}}, each with its count. Nothing is typed to move a company. {{button:amber|Didn't Answer}} moves it to Called. {{button:green|Answered}} moves it to Talked. {{button:purple|Add to the bench ✓}} puts it on the bench. Their first quote on the bench moves it to Quoted. The last two boxes open Trade partners. Can't reach and Not a fit are folded under the list.

The **Prospect List** on Subs is grouped by trade, in the same order as the needs panel. Each group names the bench's state beside it. Each row shows the trades, a stage chip and the last touch. Press {{button:blue|Call now →}} on a row to open the deck on it. A row we have talked to also offers {{button:purple|Add to the bench ✓}}. The bench is one press from the list. Press {{button:outline|Call for Concrete →}} on a group to work that trade alone on the deck.

:::example A list after a week of calls
The strip reads To call 96, Called 5, Talked 3, On the bench 2, Quoted 1. The Concrete group reads 1 on the bench, wants 2 more. Ridgeway Concrete shows {{chip:blue|Talked}} and the two presses. Kendall Concrete shows {{chip:green|To call}} and Call now →.
:::

## When a set of plans lands

Pick the project in **For** over the list. The picker lists the projects out for a bid, with their bid day and how many trades they buy out. Its trades' groups come first, marked with a star, and the rest fold. Press {{button:blue|Call for Fire sprinkler →}} on a lit group. The deck opens on that trade with the project carried along. Pick **every trade** to clear it.

## The Board

**Prospects → Subs → Board** is one column a trade, in the same order as the needs panel. Each column's head says how the bench stands on that trade. Each card has a number, the company, a chip for how they answer, and three presses. The chip reads {{chip:green|answers}}, {{chip:yellow|hit or miss}}, {{chip:gray|new to us}} or {{chip:red|often silent}}. The words are the bench's own, judged on our calls instead of asks. A column puts who answers first, then never called, then coldest. {{chip:green|call next}} marks the first card nobody has called. {{chip:blue|also in: Sitework}} means the company sits in that column too.

Pick the project in **For** over the board. Its trades' columns come forward with the project's ask on the head. The rest fold behind {{button:outline|14 trades folded · Press to show all}}.

## Call the top three when plans land

Press {{button:blue|Call the top 3 →}} on a column. Follow Up opens as a run. A run is the column's three best cards in order, fixed when you press. The head reads *Run · Fire sprinkler · for Oak Run Clinic · 1 of 3*. The rail reads **The run**. Nothing reshuffles under you. {{button:blue|Call now →}} on one card is a run of one. The deck keeps its lock, its clock and its outcomes.

:::steps

1. Press {{button:blue|📞 phone}} and make the call. The call script names the project.
2. Press {{button:amber|Didn't Answer}} when nobody picks up. The call is logged. A callback is set for 3 pm today. After mid afternoon it is set for 10 am tomorrow.
3. Press {{button:outline|Text them}} to send one text in the office's words. The press reads **Ask first** until they have said we may text.
4. Press {{button:blue|Next in the run →}}. The next card comes up.
5. Press {{button:green|Answered}} when they pick up. Press {{button:purple|Add to the bench ✓}} when they will quote. Add a company opens with one tick, **Ask them to quote the job now**. Leave it ticked and press {{button:blue|Save to the bench and ask}}. The company lands on the bench and is asked on the job in one press.
6. Press {{button:blue|Finish the run →}} on the last card. The deck shows what the run did. Press {{button:blue|Back to the board →}}.

:::

:::example A run of three
The head reads Run · Concrete · for Oak Run Clinic · 1 of 3. The first company does not answer. The line under the outcomes reads callback set today 3 pm and Ask first. The second answers and lands on the bench. The receipt reads 1 will quote and 1 to call back today 3 pm.
:::

{{button:outline|To the bench ✓}} on a card opens Add a company filled from it. {{button:outline|Pass}} takes a company we would not hire out of the queue, after one question. **By rank** on the deck's order uses the column's order on the whole lane.

## Pin a card and set a priority

Drag a card by its handle to pin it at the top of its column. Pinned cards keep the order you dropped them in. They sit over the flow and show {{chip:yellow|pinned}} with an amber edge. Press the chip to let the card flow by its record again. A pin is per trade. A sub pinned for Fire sprinkler keeps its place in Sitework.

Press the priority on a card or on the deck's card. It reads **Call first**, **Normal** or **Later**. Call first puts a company over who answers, with {{chip:yellow|★ Call first}} on the card. Later puts it at the foot. The flame for warmth is gone on Subs. The priority stands in its place.

On Trade partners, a company from this lane reads *from Prospects* under its name. The line says who landed it, the day and how many calls they answered. It stays until their first quote.

## What GC mode needs

The panel over the deck is **What GC mode needs right now**. It reads one row a trade, worst first.

- **Short of quotes**: a project asked for quotes and fewer than two came in. The row names the project, the quotes in and the due day. Press the project to open its card.
- **On the bench**: trades a company does. The row says how many do it, how many answer, and how many more the bench wants. The bench wants three a trade.
- **Nobody does these yet**: the scope book's trades with no company. Each one is a chip.

Every row ends with how many subs wait here to call. Three doors follow, in order:

1. Press {{button:blue|Ask them →}} when a bench company is not asked yet. It opens Trade partners at that trade. Ask them there.
2. Press {{button:outline|Call for Concrete}} when subs wait here. The deck shows that trade alone. Press **All subs** to widen the queue again.
3. Press {{button:outline|Add a sub}} when nobody is left to call. New Prospect opens as a sub with the trade filled in.

The blue door is the best next press. A chip under **Nobody does these yet** opens Add a sub on that trade.

:::example A lane with one ask out
The row reads Concrete, then the project, 1 of 2 quotes, due Oct 24, tomorrow. Under it: 1 on the bench, everyone asked, 0 to call here. Add a sub is blue. Fourteen chips sit under the row, Sitework to Electrical.
:::

The queue has a third order on Subs, **By need**. It puts the trade with the biggest gap first, then never called, then coldest.

On Trade partners, each "Find 2 more" line has {{button:outline|Find on Prospects →}}. It opens this lane on that trade. Edit a prospect's trades on {{button:outline|Edit prospect}} if the first call changed what they do.

## The call script

{{button:outline|Call script}} sits beside the phone number on the card. It is the same script Trade partners has, with your name in it. Open it in a new tab to keep it beside the app while you call.

## Keep it tidy

A sub stays a prospect until the bench has them. Do not type the same company twice. New Prospect checks the name and the phone against this lane, the other lanes, the bench and the customers. One line says where the company already lives and what happened last. Press **Open it** to go to that record instead of saving a second one. Press {{button:blue|Save anyway}} only for a second branch of the same company. Read [work the prospect calling queue](/help/work-the-prospect-calling-queue) for the queue, callbacks and the timer.
