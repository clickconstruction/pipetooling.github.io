On a phone, the Customers page is a search box. Type, and the matches come up as rows you can call or email from.

Leave the box empty and you get the customers who were active lately.

## Search

The box is the first thing on the page. It stays at the top while you scroll. Type any of these:

- a **name**. *lenox* finds *Lenox Builders* first, then anyone with Lenox in the name.
- an **address**. *12 Lenox* finds the customer at 12 Lenox Ct.
- a **phone number**, with or without the dashes.
- an **email**, or the **account person's** name.
- a **job**. Its number, its name or its address.

Customers and jobs come back in two groups. Each customer row says where they are and how many jobs are open. It also says what they owe and when they were last active.

You tap {{button:outline|☎}} to call the customer. You tap {{button:outline|✉}} to start an email. Neither opens the customer first. Tap the name to open the customer's page. Tap a job to open the job window.

:::example Someone calls about "the Lenox job"
Type **lenox**. *Lenox Builders* and the customer on Lenox Ct come up, and under **Jobs**, *J273 · Dudley (Lennox)*. Tap the job.
:::

An archived customer is never listed. A search still finds one, marked **Archived**.

## Before you type

Three chips choose the list:

{{chip:blue|Recent}} {{chip:gray|Owes 38}} {{chip:gray|Everyone A–Z · 431}}

- **Recent** shows the 25 customers most recently active. Active means a job, a payment, a bid or an estimate.
- **Owes** shows everyone with an open balance, the most owed first. Logins that do not see money do not get this chip.
- **Everyone A–Z** shows every customer, fifty at a time. {{button:outline|Show 50 more}} brings the next fifty.

{{button:outline|+}} beside the search box adds a customer.

## What stays on a computer

Some things are only on the page at full width. The totals at the top and the type and lifetime-value sorts stay there. So do **Show similar** for duplicates, and classifying and linking old jobs. See *find duplicate customers* and *classify customers by type*.
