On a phone, the **Customers** page is a search box. Type and the matches come up as rows you can call or email from; leave it empty and you get the customers who were active lately.

## Search

The box is the first thing on the page and stays at the top while you scroll. Type any of these:

- a **name** — *lenox* finds *Lenox Builders* first, then anyone with Lenox in the name
- an **address** — *12 Lenox* finds the customer at 12 Lenox Ct
- a **phone number**, with or without the dashes
- an **email**, or the **account person's** name
- a **job** — its number, its name or its address

Customers and jobs come back in two groups. Each customer row says where they are, how many jobs are open, what they owe and when they were last active.

{{button:outline|☎}} calls the customer and {{button:outline|✉}} starts an email, without opening them first. Tap the name to open the customer's page. Tap a job to open the job window.

:::example Someone calls about "the Lenox job"
Type **lenox**. *Lenox Builders* and the customer on Lenox Ct come up, and under **Jobs**, *J273 · Dudley (Lennox)*. Tap the job.
:::

An archived customer is never listed — but a search still finds one, marked **Archived**.

## Before you type

Three chips choose the list:

{{chip:blue|Recent}} {{chip:gray|Owes 38}} {{chip:gray|Everyone A–Z · 431}}

- **Recent** — the 25 customers most recently active: a job, a payment, a bid or an estimate.
- **Owes** — everyone with an open balance, the most owed first. Logins that do not see money do not get this chip.
- **Everyone A–Z** — every customer, fifty at a time. {{button:outline|Show 50 more}} brings the next fifty.

{{button:outline|+}} beside the search box adds a customer.

## What stays on a computer

The totals at the top, the type and lifetime-value sorts, **Show similar** for duplicates, classifying and linking old jobs are on the page at full width. See *find duplicate customers* and *classify customers by type*.
