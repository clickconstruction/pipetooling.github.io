You invite a new hire by email and they choose their own password. Or you set a password now and hand it over.

Both start at People → Users → {{button:blue|+ Hire}}. See *hire someone*. One form makes the login, the roster row, pay and the workday in one go.

## Email them a link

1. Open People → Users and press {{button:blue|+ Hire}}.
2. Type their **name**, pick their **kind** and type their **email**.
3. Leave **Send the invite** ticked. Under *How they sign in*, keep **Email them a link**.
4. Tick ***Start in training mode (read-only)*** if they should look around before they can change anything.
5. Press **Hire** at the foot of the form. The button shows their name.

The email says who they are joining as in plain words: *"You've been invited to join … as a Helper"*. Its link opens a **Welcome** page where they set a password once.

:::example Which role?
**Helper** or **Subcontractor** for field crew. They see their own day and their own pay, nothing else. **Estimator**, **Assistant**, **Controller**, **Primary** and **Superintendent** are the office and supervising roles. **Leader** sees every office route. **Dev** is administration. If you are unsure, start low. A dev can raise a role later from the person's desk.
:::

## Set a password now

Use this when someone will not click an email link. On the same form, pick **Set a password now** under *How they sign in*. Type the password in **Password** and again in **Type it again**. No email goes out. You give them the password in person or by phone.

## Training mode from the first minute

Ticking ***Start in training mode (read-only)*** flags the account before they ever sign in. They see everything their role can see. Every save is blocked. Clocking in and out still works, so their hours reach payroll. Switch it off from their row on People → Users → Account when they are ready. The person's desk has the same switch on its *Training mode* row. See *put someone in read-only training mode*.

## Attach a login to someone already on the roster

A person added without an email has no login. The person's row on People → Users says {{chip:gray|no login}}. The person still holds hours, pay, paperwork and a portal.

1. Open their desk. The ⋯ menu on their row has **Attach a login**, or scroll to *Access & account*.
2. On the **Login** row press {{button:blue|Attach a login…}}.
3. Type their email and press {{button:blue|Attach and send the sign-in email}}.

The login lands on the same person. The person's hours, Charged as and pay stay where they are. The email carries a link that signs them in, and they choose a password from it.

Only a dev can attach a login. An invite to that address from the Hire form is refused, and the refusal points you here. Nobody is made twice.

## Changing a role later

Open their desk. The **Role** menu on the *Access & account* section asks you to confirm before it saves. **Cancel** leaves the role exactly as it was.

## Take a login away

A dev can remove a person's login and keep the person. Open their desk and find the **Sign-in** row under *Access & account*. Press {{button:outline|Remove the login…}} and confirm.

The sign-in, any password and their push devices go. The person, their hours, Charged as, pay, paperwork and portal stay. The row then reads {{chip:gray|no login}}, and **Attach a login** brings a sign-in back on the same person.

Nobody can remove their own login. A sample account or a digital twin keeps its login and retires from its own door.

## Good to know

- The invite link is for setting a password once. If they open it again while already signed in, the page says **You're already set up** and offers Sign in. It never resets a password.
- If a link expired, open their desk and press {{button:outline|Send sign-in email}}. It sends a fresh link that signs them in.
- People → Users → **Add to roster** with *Also attach a login* ticked is the other door. It makes the person, then attaches the login and sends the sign-in email. The role comes from the roster kind. It does not offer training mode.
- The invitation wording, including the company name at the top, is editable at Settings → **Templates** → *Invitation Email*.
