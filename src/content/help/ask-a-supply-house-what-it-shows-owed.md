One sheet lists every job with a balance at one supply house. It writes the message that asks the house what it shows. Then you type the answers down the list and save them together.

## Open the sheet

Open [Materials, Held for suppliers](/materials?tab=job-accounts). Press **Ask a house…** above the list. The sheet opens on the house that is owed the most.

Press another house at the top to switch. The number beside each house counts its jobs.

## Read the list

Each job with a balance at that house has one row.

- **Job** shows the job, its address and what the customer still owes you. A job the customer paid in full says so in red.
- **Our books** shows what your books say the house is owed.
- **Its own notice** shows when the house can send its lien notice. It is our estimate until the house tells you.

The jobs with the soonest date come first.

## Ask the house

The sheet names who to ask at the house. Press {{button:outline|Email the ask}} to open the message in your own mail. Press {{button:outline|Copy the ask}} to paste it somewhere else. A phone button shows when that person has a number.

The app sends nothing by itself. You send the message.

:::example the start of the message
Can you tell us what you show on these jobs? For each one we need two things.
1. What you show as still owed.
2. The day your lien notice goes out, if one is set.
:::

The message then lists each job by letter. Each line gives the address, what your books show and the invoice numbers.

Press **Read the message** to see all of it first.

## Type the answers

When the house answers, type its figure under **Their balance**. Pick the day under **Their notice goes out**. Fill in only what the house told you.

Type the person's name beside **Who said it**. Press {{button:blue|Save}}. With more than one answer typed, the button reads **Save N answers**. Every answer you typed is saved at once.

A row you left blank is not changed.

## When the house shows nothing owed

Type 0 under **Their balance**. The row then says your books still show money owed. That means an invoice was paid and never marked. Mark it paid under Supply houses.

## Where the answers show

- On the job's row in Held for suppliers.
- On the job's card on the Lien desk.
- In the paragraph the Lien desk copies for an email.

## Good to know

- A click outside the sheet does not close it once you have typed something. Press **Close without saving** to leave.
- A house with no billing contact on file says so. Add one under Supply houses.
- To change one answer later, press **Change what they told us…** on that house in the job's row.
- The rest of the tab is in [see which paid jobs still owe my supply houses](/help/see-which-paid-jobs-still-owe-supply-houses).
