Compare quotes puts each trade partner's quote side by side, line by line. You put a cost on work a quote leaves out, so the totals compare fairly. Then you carry one quote as the trade's number in our price.

Only a dev sees Compare quotes while it is built.

## Open Compare quotes

1. Open [GC projects](/gc).
2. Find the project's card and its trade.
3. Press {{button:blue|Compare quotes (2)}}. The number counts the quotes in.

The button shows once a quote is in.

The price card opens it too. Press the price on the project's row. Then press **Compare quotes** under the trade. The button reads **Set a cost** while the quote we carry has a line with no cost.

## Read the side by side

Each company has a column. Each line of the trade's scope has a row.

- A tick that says *in their price* means the line is in their number.
- {{chip:red|left out}} means their quote does not cover the line.
- {{chip:yellow|not clear}} means their quote does not say.

The box at the top says what the quotes really cost. The box names the lowest once every line has a cost.

## Put a cost on what a quote leaves out

1. Find a line marked left out or not clear.
2. Type our cost to cover it in the box under it.
3. Press Enter, or click outside the box. The cost saves.

The **All in** row adds the costs to their quote. A quote with a line that still has no cost shows {{chip:yellow|needs a cost}}. That quote's number also shows a plus sign and a question mark.

## Read their exclusions

Some companies list what their quote leaves out, such as dewatering. Each one gets its own row.

- {{chip:red|excluded}} means they leave it out. Type a cost to cover it, the same way as a line.
- A tick that says *included* means they said it is in their price.
- {{chip:yellow|not said}} means their quote does not say. Call them and ask.

## Take an alternate

An alternate is another way to do the work, at a different price. Press {{button:outline|Take it}} to count it in their All in number. Press {{button:outline|Put it back}} to leave it out again.

## Carry a quote

1. Press {{button:blue|Carry this number}} under the quote you pick.
2. The trade's number in our price is now that quote's All in number.

The button then reads **Carrying. Stop**. Press it to carry nothing.

To carry our own budget for the trade instead, press **Carry our budget of $60,000 instead**. The button shows the trade's own budget.

The board and the price card mark the trade with a plus sign and a question mark. The mark stays until every line of the carried quote has a cost.

## What comes next

Awarding a trade comes in a later step. So do asking a company to confirm its number, and typing in a quote that came by email.
