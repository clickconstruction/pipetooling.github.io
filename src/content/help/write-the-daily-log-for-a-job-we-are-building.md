Our superintendent writes one daily log for each working day on a GC job we are building. The log keeps the weather, the crews on site, the work done and what held work up.

The log starts once work starts on the job. Only a dev sees the Daily log while GC mode is built.

## Write today's log

1. Press {{button:outline|GC}} on the Bids page to open [GC projects](/gc).
2. Press {{button:outline|Daily log}} on the job's card.
3. Pick the weather. Type the high and the low.
4. Tick **Work stopped for the weather** if it did.
5. Type how many workers each trade had on site. Type 0 for a trade that was not there.
6. Type what got done.
7. Press {{button:outline|Add a delay}} for each thing that held work up. Pick whose work it held up and why. Type what happened.
8. Type the inspections and visitors.
9. Press {{button:blue|Save the log}}.

Today's log starts from the day before. The crews and the weather are filled in already. You change what is different today.

## Catch up a missed day

The button on the job's card counts the working days in the last week with no log. The button reads like {{button:outline|Daily log · 2 missed}}.

1. Press the button for the day at the top of the window, like {{button:outline|Write Wed Sep 30}}.
2. Fill in the log the same way as today's.
3. Press {{button:blue|Save the log}}.

The log keeps the day you wrote it. A log written after its day says so.

## Change a day's log

1. Press the day under **This week**.
2. Press {{button:outline|Change it}}.
3. Change what you need. Press {{button:blue|Save the log}}.

Saving a day again replaces its log. A day never has two logs. Press {{button:outline|Keep it as it was}} to leave it alone.

## Which trades show

Our own crew shows on every log. A trade we hire shows once its statement of work, the work we agreed it will do, is signed.

## Our own crew's count

Our own crew's count can come from the Pipeline. Once [Draws names our crew's Pipeline job](/help/link-our-own-crew-to-its-pipeline-job), the log reads who clocked in on that job each day.

- A day with clock-ins shows the count with **clocked in**. Nobody types over it. The count changes only in the Pipeline.
- A day with no clock-ins keeps the box. Type the count if the crew was there.
