One property can hold many jobs. The Lien desk lists papers and jobs one by one, so a property with seven jobs is seven rows. The property card shows them together. Open it when an envelope or a list shows fewer jobs than you expected at an address.

## Open the property card

Go to the Lien desk and press **By job**. On a computer that tab reads **Job**. A job on a property with more than one job has a blue line under its GC. The line reads like *412 Ridgeline Dr, Hollow Creek · 7 jobs here ›*. Press it.

The card opens over the desk. It reads nothing new if By job was already open.

A job on a property with one job has no blue line. Its row already says all there is.

The notices' run opens the card too. In the run, press **Envelopes and copies**. An envelope's card ends with a line about its property when other jobs are there. Press **See the property ›** on that line. The card opens over the run.

:::example The top of the card
**412 Ridgeline Dr, Hollow Creek**

Owner of record: Avery and Jordan Pell · residential · GC: Marlow Builders

**7 jobs** · $15,870 open

4 notices sent Oct 14 · 2 on Sep 22 · 1 to print and mail

6 lien affidavits ready to file
:::

## Read the lines that count

Two lines under the owner count the whole property. Read them first. The notices have one line. The liens have the other. The two are never added together.

The notices line:

- *4 notices sent Oct 14* means four jobs had their notice mailed that day.
- *2 on Sep 22* means two more went on that day.
- *1 to print and mail* means one notice is approved and goes in the next run.

The liens line:

- *6 lien affidavits ready to file* means six jobs have an approved lien, ready for the county.

So an envelope of notices for this owner holds one job today. The other six went in earlier envelopes. The six liens are not notices, and the notices' run does not hold them.

## Read one job's row

Each job has one row. The row has the job, its months, its notice, its affidavit and what it still owes.

The **Notice** words say where that job's notice stands.

- *Notice sent Oct 14* is green. The notice went that day.
- *Approved · to print* and *Approved · to mail* are blue. The notice is going now.
- *Awaiting Master approval*, *To send for Master approval*, *Needs the owner* and *Held* are amber. The notice waits.
- *No notice needed · contracted with the owner* is grey. A job with no GC needs no notice.
- *No notice on record* means the desk holds no notice for that job.

A small line under the words gives one more fact. It can be the last day, the day it was printed, or an earlier notice that went.

The **Affidavit** words say the same for the lien affidavit. They use the Board's bin names. *Filed* with a day means it is at the county.

The row you came from is shaded.

## Open a paper from the card

Press a job's notice words to open that notice. Press its affidavit words to open that affidavit. Press the job's number to open the job itself. The card closes and the desk shows what you picked.

## The same owner at another property

A line at the foot can read *Same owner also at 88 Fenwick Ln, Hollow Creek · 1 job ›*. It shows when the same owner, at the same mailing address, owns another property on the desk. Press it to see that property.

The line can also read *a second record for this address*. That means two property records hold the same address. The jobs are split between them. Tell the office lead, so the two records can be made one.

## Group By job by property

By job has a picker named **Group**, beside **GC** and **Show**. Pick *By property*.

The rows now stand under a head for each property with more than one job. A head names the address and the owner. Then it counts the whole property, the same way the card does. Press the address to open the card.

The properties with one job come next, under **One job each**. Jobs with no property record come last, under **No property record**.

A head always counts the whole property. If **GC** or **Show** hides some of its jobs, the head ends with how many are shown.

To see what one GC has at each property, pick the GC first, then group.

Pick *None* to go back to the plain list.

## A job with no property record

A By job row can read *No property record* in amber. That job is on no property card. Press **Set the property ›** beside it to link the job to its property.

## Good to know

- The card changes nothing. It has no send, print or save.
- A property is a property record, not a typed address. Two jobs are together only when they share the record.
- The card counts every job in By job, not only the ones a filter left on screen.
- For what each bin name means, see [the Board](/help/read-the-board-on-the-lien-desk).
- For the run and its envelopes, see [the Lien desk](/help/send-lien-notices-from-the-lien-desk).
