The app keeps a copy each time we send someone something. The copy is the page as it went out, not a fresh one drawn from today's numbers.

Printing counts as sending. You do nothing extra. The copy is filed when you print or send.

## Find a copy

1. Open the job.
2. Press the **Documents** tab.
3. Scroll to **Sent from this job**.

The newest send is on top. Each line says what went, how, when, to whom and by whom.

## Open a copy

Press the name on a line. The copy opens in its own window, as it was that day.

Press {{button:outline|Print}} in that window to print the same page again. Printing from there does not file another copy.

An email may list attachments after its line. Press one to open it.

## Read a line

- **Printed** means someone printed it here.
- **Handed over**, **Mailed** and **Emailed** come from how the send was recorded.
- **Printed 3 times** means the same page was printed three times. The line shows the last time.
- **The copy was not kept** means the send happened but the copy could not be saved. The line is still the record that it went.

## What files a copy today

Copies are kept from the day each kind of paper was added. Nothing older is listed here.

These keep a copy today.

- Every email the app sends to a customer, a GC, a supplier, a sub or a law firm.
- The owner's records packet on the Lien desk, when printed and when recorded as sent. See [give an owner our records](/help/give-an-owner-our-records-for-their-property).
- A printed lien paper. That is a notice packet, a notice, an affidavit, a release of record or a release of lien.
- The grid and the legal packet printed for your attorney.
- A printed contract, work order, sub sheet or purchase order.
- A printed bid cover letter, schedule of values or procurement update.
- A printed GC statement, checks applied sheet or pay code sheet.

The other sections of the tab still list each bill, contract and lien paper.

Some papers cover no single job. A statement to a GC and a price request to a supply house are two. They are kept, and a place to browse them is coming to the Documents page.

## What is not kept

Our own working sheets and reports are not sends, so they are not kept here. Pay stubs stay with payroll.

## Who can see the copies

The office can. That is the leader, the assistants and the controller.

:::example Did Umar Khan get the October figure
Taunya opens job 273 and presses Documents. Under Sent from this job she sees Records for 9703 Lenox Hill, handed over on Oct 6. She presses the name. The window shows $30,387.00 owed, the figure he was given that day.
:::
