Your world is Bids: from the board through takeoff and pricing to a submitted proposal. You don't manage crews or company money.

## Your day

1. **Bid Board** — every bid by stage, with due dates. See [bid due date and time](?g=bid-due-date-time).

:::example A bid's life on the board
{{chip:gray|Unsent}} → {{chip:yellow|Pending}} → {{chip:green|Won}} or {{chip:red|Lost}} — and Won work moves to {{chip:blue|Started}} once the office runs it
:::
2. **Takeoff** — count fixtures and tie-ins off the plans (or [import them from CountTooling](?g=import-a-takeoff-from-counttooling)), then pick the bid's materials model — [By Stage or Combined](?g=choose-by-stage-or-combined-for-materials).
3. **Pricing and Labor** — the books turn those counts into materials, hours, and a price. Versions let you price the same project more than one way; Labor lists the fixtures counted on the version you're on, and shows a short loading card while a bid opens — never an empty list for a bid that has fixtures.
4. **Cover Letter and Submission** — build the proposal and send it. If you're bidding one project to several GCs, see [bid one project to multiple GCs](?g=bid-one-project-to-multiple-gcs). Want the GC to sign online? The **Bid room** panel starts with {{button:blue|Get the link}} — it publishes the room and copies the link so you can send it from your own email; **Send to GC** is there when you'd rather the app email it ([send a bid for signature](?g=send-a-bid-for-signature)).
5. **Followup** — the **Call queue** is where sent bids get chased; the board goes quiet on purpose once a letter is out ([follow up with builders](?g=follow-up-with-builders)).

## Your other tabs

- **Materials** — the price book and assemblies behind your numbers; [find assemblies that include a part](?g=find-assemblies-that-include-a-part). You also see **PO Builder** and **Purchase Orders** (line-item material lists); the office-only **PO Generator** is where counter PO codes come from ([raise a purchase order](?g=raise-a-purchase-order)).
- **Map** — where your bids are, colored by outcome.
- **Prospects** — leads coming in.
- **Calendar**, **Checklist**, **Job Parts Tally**.

## When a bid wins

Wherever you mark a bid Won — Edit Bid, the board's GC pill, Followup — {{button:green|Open the job}} is right there, and you can use it: New Job opens filled from the bid, even though you don't have the Jobs page ([turn a won bid into a job](?g=turn-a-won-bid-into-a-job)). Signed estimates and bid rooms email the office too — [estimate accepted notifications](?g=estimate-accepted-notifications). Running the job — dispatch, billing — is the office's; when you open a job from Estimates or Customers you get the read-only Job Detail pane (notes and reports, no Edit tab). If the job needs a price change, that comes back to you as a change order — [write a change order and send it for signature](?g=write-a-change-order); the whole estimate loop, new → sent → signed → job, is in [send an estimate and turn it into a job](?g=send-an-estimate-and-turn-it-into-a-job).

## Service-type limits

If your account is restricted to certain service types, pickers only show matching work. That's deliberate — ask the office to widen it if something you need is missing.
