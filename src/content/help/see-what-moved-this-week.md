Jobs move through the Pipeline all week. Stage moves this week shows a whole week of those moves in one place.

Jobs go into Working, up to Ready to Bill, out as Billed, and home as Paid. **Stage moves this week** is the Pipeline menu item. The emailed copy is still called the Weekly movement report. The report shows which jobs entered each stage, on what day, and who moved them.

## Open it

You go to **Jobs → Pipeline**. You open the hamburger menu at the left of the stage strip. The strip reads Waiting, Working and so on. The **Pipeline** group at the top holds {{button:outline|Stage moves this week}}.

## Reading the report

The week runs Monday to Sunday, Central time. You use the **‹ ›** arrows to step between weeks. Each section lists the jobs that **entered** that stage during the week:

:::example A week at a glance
**Moved to Ready to Bill** · 2 jobs · $535
943 · Phinisse — Wed — Abraham — $250
**Moved to Billed** · 3 jobs · $19,800
880 · Reliant Health — Thu — Taunya — $4,800
:::

- Every row shows the **job**, its **address** in small underneath, the **weekday**, **who moved it**, and the job's value. Some moves are made by the app itself. A Stripe payment marking a job paid is one. Those show as **Automatic**.
- **Sent back** gets its own section at the bottom. It lists every backward move with its from → to arrow. Those are the ones worth a second look.
- A job that moved twice in one week shows both moves, but section totals count each job once.

## Print it

You press {{button:outline|🖨 Print}} for a one-page report of the week. You choose *Save as PDF* in the print window to download a copy.

## Email it weekly

Office roles see an **Email this report** box in the modal. You pick a teammate and a first send date. The date defaults to next Monday 7:00 AM Central. You tick **Repeat weekly** and press {{button:blue|Schedule}}. Each send covers the **previous complete week**. A Monday morning email reports last Monday to Sunday. The email is rebuilt fresh at send time. Your pending sends list right there, each with a **Cancel**. Cancelling ends a weekly repeat. Recipients see theirs on **Settings → Your account → My email schedule**.
