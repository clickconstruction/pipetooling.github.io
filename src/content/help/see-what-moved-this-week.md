Jobs move through the Pipeline all week — into Working, up to Ready to Bill, out as Billed, home as Paid. **Weekly movement** shows a whole week of those moves in one place: which jobs entered each stage, on what day, and who moved them.

## Open it

On **Jobs → Pipeline**, open the hamburger menu at the left of the stage strip (Waiting → Working → …) — the **Pipeline** group at the top holds {{button:outline|Weekly movement}}.

## Reading the report

The week runs Monday–Sunday (Central); use the **‹ ›** arrows to step between weeks. Each section lists the jobs that **entered** that stage during the week:

:::example A week at a glance
**Moved to Ready to Bill** · 2 jobs · $535
943 · Phinisse — Wed — Abraham — $250
**Moved to Billed** · 3 jobs · $19,800
880 · Reliant Health — Thu — Taunya — $4,800
:::

- Every row shows the **job**, its **address** in small underneath, the **weekday**, **who moved it**, and the job's value. Moves made by the app itself (a Stripe payment marking a job paid, for example) show as **Automatic**.
- **Sent back** gets its own section at the bottom — every backward move with its from → to arrow, because those are the ones worth a second look.
- A job that moved twice in one week shows both moves, but section totals count each job once.

## Print it

{{button:outline|🖨 Print}} makes a one-page report of the week — choose *Save as PDF* in the print window to download a copy.
