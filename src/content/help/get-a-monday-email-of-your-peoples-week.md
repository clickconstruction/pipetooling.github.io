Get one email each week about the people you pick. It says what each person moved on their task cards and jobs during the week just finished.

## Where to start it

Open **Checklist → Review** and press {{button:outline|✉ Email me their week}}. The people on the board are already picked.

On the **Dashboard**, the **Your people** card has {{button:outline|✉ Get this every Monday}}. Its people are picked the same way.

## Pick the people, the days and the time

:::steps

1. Tick each person you want in the email. You can pick up to 50.
2. Pick the days it goes. Monday is picked for you.
3. Set the time in Central time. It starts at 6:00 AM.
4. Press {{button:blue|Start it}}.

:::

The first email comes on the next day you picked. After that it comes every week.

## What the email says

Each person gets their own part. The headline counts the people, the cards done, the cards moved and the cards stuck.

**Cards.** Each card that finished or moved, with the percent it went from and to. The Done when… lines ticked and the photos taken ride along. The last words typed about what is left sit at the end.

**Roadmap.** The roadmap tasks among those cards, with the stage and the roadmap they belong to.

**Jobs.** The jobs the person clocked on, with the job's percent, their days on it and their reports.

**Stuck.** Any open card with no move for a month.

**Open Review** at the bottom lands on **Checklist → Review**.

## Check it before it goes

Press {{button:outline|See the email as it would go now}} to read it on the sheet. Press {{button:outline|Email me a test}} to get a copy marked TEST at your own address.

## Change it or stop it

Open the sheet again. Your people, days and time are shown as they stand. Change them and press {{button:blue|Save the change}}. Untick every day and press {{button:red|Stop it}} to end it.

**Settings → My email schedule** shows the next send beside your other emails. The office sees every scheduled send under **Settings → Email streams**.
