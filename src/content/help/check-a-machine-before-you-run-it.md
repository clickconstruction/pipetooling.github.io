A machine gets a walk around before it runs. The walk takes about two minutes. It finds a cracked hose or a loose pin before the machine is in the trench. At the end of the day a short leaving check says how you left it.

## Open the check

The Job Mode card on your Dashboard lists the machines on your job under the stage checklists. Each one reads **Not checked today** until someone walks it. Tap the line to open the check.

Every machine also has a label with a QR code on it. Scan it with your phone's camera. The check opens on that machine. If the machine is not on a job yet, the check asks which job it is on first.

## Walk around

The walk asks one question at a time. Tap {{button:green|Yes}}, {{button:red|No}} or {{button:outline|Not here}}. The next question opens on its own.

A No keeps its row open. Say what is wrong in a few words. Tap {{button:outline|Take the photo}} to add a picture of it. Some questions hold the machine. The row says **a No holds the machine** under those. A No on one of them means the machine does not run until Dispatch says so.

## Read the gauges

Each gauge is drawn the way it looks on the machine. A tank fills in quarters. A sight glass has a line the oil should sit on. A dipstick has ADD and FULL. A dial has a red zone and a green zone. Under each gauge the words say where to read it on the machine.

On a phone, tap a gauge and it opens big. Drag the fill to where it sits. Tap where it sits if that is easier. The words under the gauge say what you set, like **about ⅜** or **on the line**. A guess is fine.

A low reading asks for a photo. Tap {{button:outline|Low · add the photo}} and take one of the gauge. The photo is the proof behind the reading.

## Hours and photos

Type the number on the hour meter into **Hours on the dash**. The line under it shows the last reading and the change. A number lower than last time turns amber. Read it again if that happens.

Take the five photos. The hour meter, the front, one side, the back and anything wrong. Every photo files into the machine's own Drive folder within a few minutes.

## Sign

The sign press reads **E-12 is safe to run** once the walk is done. Tap it and you are done. If a holding question got a No, the press reads **Finish the walk, E-12 stays held**. Tapping it tells Dispatch. The machine stays held until Dispatch says run it anyway or a new walk signs it safe.

Tap **Finish later, keep what I did** to leave and come back. Every press saves by itself.

## The leaving check

Once the walk is signed, the machine's line on the card grows a second line. It reads **Leaving E-12?**. Tap it at the end of the day. The leaving check is short. It asks three things, the gauges and the hours. Then it asks **Where is it tonight?**. Tap {{button:outline|On the job}}, {{button:outline|Back to the yard}} or {{button:outline|Going to another job}}. The answer moves the machine on the fleet board.

The label works for the leaving check too. Scan it after the morning walk is signed and the leaving check opens.

## A rented machine

A rental shows who it is from and when it is due back at the top of the check. The photos you take are the evidence if the rental company claims damage. Take all five at pickup and all five at return.
