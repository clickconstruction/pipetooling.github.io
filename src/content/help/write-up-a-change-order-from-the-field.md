You're on a job and the customer says "while you're here…" — the scope just grew. **Quick Estimate** lets you write it up in about three minutes, right from your phone, and hand it to Dispatch to finish. You answer one question per screen, skip anything you don't know, and the office takes it from there.

## Turn the button on (once)

1. Go to {{icon:gear}} **Settings → Dashboard & alerts** and check {{chip:yellow|Quick Estimate button}}. It's off by default.
2. A purple {{button:purple|Quick Estimate}} button appears at the top of your Dashboard.

## Write up extra work on your job (a change order)

1. Tap {{button:purple|Quick Estimate}}. It opens on **your schedule** — today's and tomorrow's jobs.
2. Tap the job you're on. That's it — the write-up becomes a **change order** and the customer fills in automatically. (Different job? Use the search. No job? Tap **Skip**.)
3. **What's the extra work?** Talk or type — tap the mic on your keyboard and just describe it. Snap photos with the **＋** square; photos count as answers.
4. **About how much?** Type a ballpark, tap a quick number ($250 / $500 / $1,000 / $2,500), or **Skip — let the office price it**. Your ballpark is a note to the office, never a price the customer sees.
5. Check the ✓/— summary, add a note for dispatch if you want, and hit {{button:amber|Send to Dispatch}}.

:::example What happens next
Dispatch gets an inbox note with your summary and an **Open the draft** button. The office prices real cost lines, finishes the paperwork, and sends it to the customer. **You'll get an email the moment the customer accepts** — that's your green light for the extra work.
:::

## New work for someone else (an estimate)

Someone flags you down who isn't your current job? On the first screen tap **New work for someone else →**, then pick a customer, type just a name ("Mike down the road" works), or skip it — the office will find or create the customer later. The rest of the flow is the same.

## Good to know

- The ✕ **saves and closes** — never discards. Your half-finished write-up sits in Estimates → Unsent until you or the office picks it up.
- After you send, the draft shows a {{chip:yellow|With Dispatch}} chip in Estimates so nobody double-works it.
- Send needs at least a description **or** a photo — everything else is skippable.
