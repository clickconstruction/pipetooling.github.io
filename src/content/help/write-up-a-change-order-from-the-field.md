You're on a job and the customer says "while you're here…" — the scope just grew. **Write up a change** lets you write it up in about three minutes, right from your phone, and hand it to Dispatch to finish. You answer one question per screen, skip anything you don't know, and the office takes it from there.

## Turn it on (once)

1. Go to {{icon:gear}} **Settings → Dashboard & alerts** and check {{chip:yellow|Write up a change from the field}}. It's off by default.
2. Every job in your Dashboard's **My Schedule** now carries a paper-and-pencil square beside **Leave Report**, and a small **Write up a change on another job, or new work** link sits under the list.
3. The same paper-and-pencil sits in the **Job window**'s icon row (any tab, before ⚙), and in **Job Mode** a **Write up a change** link sits under the card's buttons for the job you're clocked into. Every door opens the write-up already on that job.

:::

## Write up extra work on your job (a change order)

1. On the job you're standing on, tap the paper-and-pencil square. The wizard opens already on that job — the customer fills in automatically — and asks **What's the change?** A **‹ Back** button on every screen lets you step backwards any time — nothing you typed is lost.
2. Not on your day? Tap the link under the list, pick **Change order**, and answer **Which job is it on?** — your schedule's jobs are right there; use the search for a different one, or **Skip**.
3. **What's the change?** The same fields the office's change order has: **Description of change** (talk or type — tap the mic on your keyboard), plus optional **Reason**, **Schedule impact**, and **Answer needed by**. Snap photos with the **＋** square; photos count as answers.
4. **About how much?** Type a ballpark, tap a quick number ($250 / $500 / $1,000 / $2,500), or **Skip — let the office price it**. Your ballpark is a note to the office, never a price the customer sees.
5. Check the ✓/— summary, add a note for dispatch if you want, and hit {{button:amber|Send to Dispatch}}.

:::example What happens next
Dispatch gets an inbox note with your summary and an **Open the draft** button. The office prices real cost lines, finishes the paperwork, and sends it to the customer. **You'll get an email the moment the customer accepts** — that's your green light for the extra work.
:::

## New work for someone else (an estimate)

Someone flags you down who isn't your current job? Tap the link under My Schedule, pick **Estimate** on the first screen, then pick a customer, type just a name ("Mike down the road" works), or skip it — the office will find or create the customer later. The rest of the flow is the same.

## Good to know

- The ✕ **saves and closes** — never discards. Next time you open **Write up a change** it asks first: **Pick it back up** (your words, photos and job come back), **Start fresh** (the old one stays in Estimates → Unsent), or **Leave it for the office** (it stops asking about that one).
- After you send, the draft shows a {{chip:yellow|With Dispatch}} chip in Estimates so nobody double-works it.
- Send needs at least a description **or** a photo — everything else is skippable.
- If you already sent a write-up for the same job today, the **Ready to send** screen says so, with the time — send again only if it's different work; otherwise the office already has it.
