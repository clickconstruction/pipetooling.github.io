Every photo taken in the app shows on one list, newest first. Each row says which job it is on, who took it and whether it is in Google Drive yet.

## Open the list

Open **Documents** from the gear menu. Press the **Photos** tab. The newest photos are at the top, grouped by day.

## Read a row

Each row shows a small picture, its caption, the job and the customer. Under them you see where the photo came from and who took it. A chip names the source. It reads **Report**, **Tally box**, **Checklist**, **Text** or **Email**.

On the right the row says where the photo is.

- **In Drive** means the photo is in the job's Drive folder. Press {{button:outline-blue|Open ↗}} to see it there.
- **On its way to Drive** means the app has the photo and files it within five minutes.
- **Not in Drive yet** means the app could not file it. The reason is under it.
- **Still on a phone** means the crew kept the shot for want of signal.

## Find one photo

Type in the box at the top. You can type a job, a customer, a person or a stage. The list narrows as you type.

Press a source chip to see one kind of photo. Press **In Drive** or **Not in Drive yet** to see photos by where they are.

:::tryit
Press **Not in Drive yet**. Every photo the app could not file is on this list with its reason. A job with no Drive folder is the usual reason. Press **Make the job's Drive folders** to fix it.
:::

## See a photo

Press the small picture. The viewer opens with the photo at full size. Its facts sit under it. Press {{button:outline|Next ›}} to move to the next photo on the list.

The viewer has doors too. Press {{button:blue|Open in Drive}} to see the file there. Press **Open the report**, **Open the box** or **Open the thread** to see what the photo came with. Press **Open the job** for the job window. Press **Only Mateo's photos** to see one person's photos alone.

## Photos put in Drive by hand

This list shows photos taken in the app. A photo someone put in a Drive folder by hand is not here. Open the job and look under its address. See [see a job's photos](/help/see-a-jobs-photos).
