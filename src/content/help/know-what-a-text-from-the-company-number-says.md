Every text we send goes from the company number. The person sees our name, and their reply comes back to their thread.

## The first text to a number

The first text we send a number ends with a line of its own. It reads *Click Construction. Reply STOP to opt out.* The line goes in the same text, after a blank line.

The line comes back after 30 days with no text from us. You can leave it off once when only those quiet days put it there. You can never leave it off a first text.

## When they answer STOP or HELP

When someone sends STOP, the phone service stops texts to that number. The app stops texting that number too, and their thread says so. START brings the texts back.

When someone sends HELP, the app answers. The answer gives our name, the company number and our email.

A longer text that may ask us to stop does not stop anything. It leaves a note in their thread for someone to read.

## See it before it goes

The texts sit on What customers see, one to a customer and one to a trade partner. [See what customers see](/help/see-what-customers-see) shows how to open them.

Texts start once the owner turns on **Send texts** in Settings. [Turn on texts from the company number](/help/turn-on-texts-from-the-company-number) shows how. Until then this guide is for devs.
