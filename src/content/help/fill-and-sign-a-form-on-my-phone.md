Some paperwork is a form to fill in rather than text to read. The IRS W-9 is the common one.

When the office sends you one, the link opens the **actual form** with boxes to type into. The boxes sit exactly where the answers go. What you type goes onto that form and nowhere else.

## Open it

Open it from the email with {{button:blue|Read and sign}}. Or open your portal page and find it under **Your paperwork** with {{button:blue|Sign now}}. No account or password is needed.

## Fill it in

- **On a computer or tablet**, type straight into the boxes on the page.
- **On a phone**, the page is small. So a panel under it shows the box you are on, large. It has a plain-language label and a help line. Tap a box on the form, or use {{button:outline|Next ›}} to move through them in order. Pinch to zoom the page any time.
- Boxes marked **required** must be filled. Where the form offers choices, pick one and the others clear. The tax classification on a W-9 is one such choice.
- Tap **Español** at the top right to see the labels and help in Spanish. The form itself stays as the agency printed it.

:::example Your Social Security or Employer ID number
A W-9 needs one or the other, not both. Type the digits and the dashes are added for you. As soon as you leave the box the number shows as dots, and it is kept **only inside the signed form** — nowhere else in the app.
:::

**Show rarely-needed lines** shows lines most people leave blank. **Hide rarely-needed lines** tucks them away again. Exempt-payee codes and account numbers are examples. Open it only if the office asked you to.

## Sign and submit

Under the page, type your full legal name or draw your signature. Tick **I agree to sign electronically**. The line above it says a typed or drawn signature counts like ink. It also says you can ask the office for paper instead. **How electronic signing works ▸** opens the detail, in your language. Tick the certification too. Then press {{button:green|Sign and submit}}. Your signature and today's date land on the form's signature line. If a required box is empty or a number is the wrong length, the box is outlined. The panel tells you what to fix.

## Afterward

The signed form appears on your portal page under **Your paperwork on file**. No builder holds up a check over missing paperwork. You never have to send it again. If your name, business, or address changes, tell the office. They will send a fresh form.

## A shaded part of the form

Some forms have a part the office fills after you. On the I-9, that is Section 2. That part is shaded and labelled **This part is completed by the office**. That part has no boxes for you. The panel under the page never stops on it, and **Next ›** goes straight past it.
