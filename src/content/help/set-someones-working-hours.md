Working hours are the start and the end of each day of a person's week. A dev or the controller sets them on the person's sheet.

## Where they are

Open the person's sheet from their name. Find **Pay & schedule**, then the line **Working hours**.

Days that share their hours read on one line. A week of five mornings reads *Mon to Fri*, then *6:00 AM to 12:00 PM*, then *6 h a day*.

:::example A week with a half day on Wednesday
**Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri** · 7:00 AM to 3:00 PM · 8 h a day

**Wed** · 7:00 AM to 11:00 AM · 4 h

**Sat, Sun** · Off

**Week** · five days · 36 h
:::

## Set them the first time

1. Press {{button:blue|Set working hours}}.
2. For a person on a set week, pick the time each day starts. The end is worked out from their set week.
3. Pick {{button:outline|This week}} or {{button:outline|Next week}}.
4. Press {{button:blue|Set these hours}}.

For a person paid by the hour, the line opens on Monday to Friday. Pick the start and the end, then press {{button:blue|Keep}}.

## Change a line

1. Press the underlined hours on a line.
2. Pick a new start and a new end. Times go in 15-minute steps.
3. Read the sentence under the times. That sentence says what the days and the week come to.
4. Press {{button:blue|Keep}}.

Every day on the line changes together.

## Give one day its own hours

1. Press the hours on the line that holds the day.
2. Press {{button:outline|Give one day its own hours}}, then the day.
3. Press {{button:outline|Half day}}, or pick the start and the end yourself.
4. Press {{button:blue|Keep}}.

A day on its own line has three more presses.

- {{button:outline|Same as the other days}} gives the day their hours again.
- {{button:outline|Add a break}} splits the day in two. The break is not counted in the day's hours.
- {{button:outline|Make it a day off}} takes the day's hours away.

## When pay moves

For a person on a set week, pay follows the hours.

- A change of times alone moves no pay. The line says *Each day keeps its length, so pay does not move*.
- A change to a day's length moves pay. The change starts this week, and you say why before you keep it.
- The reason goes on the pay log.

A person paid by the hour is paid the hours they log. Their working hours change no pay.

## Watch-outs

- Hours hold from a Sunday. Days already worked this week keep the time on them.
- The newest save holds. Hours you save for this week replace hours saved earlier for next week.
- {{button:outline|Earlier hours}} shows what the hours were, who set them and why.
- An owner reads the hours and cannot change them. The office reads them on People, **Utilization**.
- The person reads their own hours on their dashboard, in **My Time**.
