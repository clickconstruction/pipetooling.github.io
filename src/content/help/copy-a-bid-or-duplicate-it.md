You can copy a bid so you do not type it again. The copy is a new bid with its own number. The first bid stays as it is.

## Open the Copy Bid window

1. Open the bid's **Edit Bid** window.
2. Click the small trade chip at the top, for example {{chip:blue|Plumbing}}. The **Copy Bid** window opens.

The window lists this bid's trade first. Under it are the other trades.

## Duplicate the bid in the same trade

Press {{button:blue|Duplicate this Plumbing bid}}. The copy opens with *(copy)* after the project name, so you can tell the two apart.

## Copy the bid into another trade

Find the trade and press {{button:blue|Copy to new Electrical bid}}. The copy opens in that trade with the same project name. That trade may already have a bid for this customer and project. Then the window shows **Open** with its bid number instead. Press it to go to that bid.

## What a copy takes

- The customer, the project name, the address, the links and the due date.
- The counts.
- The takeoff: every part line, its Sold in rule and its stage boxes.
- The labor rows and the travel numbers.
- This bid's own prices. The copy gets its own copy of each one, so a change on one bid never moves the other.
- The costs you applied from a supply house quote, when the copy stays in the same trade. A copy into another trade leaves them, because the quote was for the first trade.

A copy does not take the sent date, the outcome or the cover letter's history. It is a fresh, unsent bid.

## A bid with versions

A bid with versions copies the version you are on. The copy is one plain bid with that version's counts, takeoff and prices. Pick the version first, then make the copy.

:::example Copying the Value Engineered version
BP398 has two versions, *To Plans* and *PEX in lieu of copper*. You are on *PEX in lieu of copper* and press Duplicate. The copy has the eight fixtures of that version, not sixteen.
:::
