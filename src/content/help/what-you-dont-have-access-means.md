When the app says you do not have access, the server refused the read or the save for your role. The record is there, but it is not yours to read or change.

## Why it happens

Each role sees the parts of the app its work needs. A helper does not see the company's money. An estimator does not edit payroll. The server checks every read and every save against your role. It refuses the ones your role does not cover.

## What was saved

Nothing. Nothing of yours was changed either.

## Who to ask

- For a job, a bill or a customer, ask the office.
- For your hours or your pay, ask your leader.
- For a page you think your role should open, ask a dev. A dev can change what a role sees.

A page your role cannot open at all sends you to the Dashboard. The same rule turns you back before the page loads.
