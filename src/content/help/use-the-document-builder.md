The Document builder is a tab on Documents that holds builders. A builder makes one kind of paper from a few settings.

Only a dev sees the tab for now. The first builder is the Sub NDA. That paper is a job's secrecy paper for the people you bring onto the job.

The second builder is the insurance sheet for a job's subs. See [set the insurance a job's subs must carry](/help/set-the-insurance-a-jobs-subs-must-carry).

Every word in the builder is a placeholder. No attorney has read any of it. The Sub NDA is written for a made-up job.

## Open a builder

1. Open [Documents](/documents?tab=builder).
2. Press the **Document builder** tab. You find it last in the row.
3. Find the builder's row. The chips on the row say where the builder stands.
4. Press {{button:blue|Open}}.

The builder takes the whole tab. Press {{button:outline-blue|‹ Document builder}} to go back to the list.

## Load a builder's first draft

A builder's words are kept in the database. A builder with no words yet reads {{chip:gray|Not loaded yet}}.

1. Press {{button:blue|Load the first draft}} on its row.
2. Wait for the row to read {{chip:gray|Draft, not published}}.
3. Press {{button:blue|Open}}.

The first draft is placeholder words, for you to change. Nobody outside sees a draft.

## Read where a builder stands

- {{chip:gray|Draft, not published}} means nobody outside has seen it.
- {{chip:green|Published, revision 2}} names the copy everyone outside sees.
- {{chip:yellow|Changes not published}} means the draft has moved on from that copy.
- {{chip:yellow|Not read by an attorney}} means nobody has marked this revision as read. A new revision is unread again.

## Change the Sub NDA's words

The Sub NDA opens on two sides. The right side is what a GC sees. The left side is the map and what changed.

1. Press any words on the right side and type over them.
2. Wait for the bar to read *Draft saved*.

The bar reads *Draft not saved* when a save fails. Type again to send the draft again.

- **Add a line** adds a line at the end of a list.
- The small **×** beside a line takes it off.
- A breach line that a stricter level keeps is one rule. Change it on the level that brings it.

Nobody outside sees a draft. No clause wording is typed here. A clause stays grey bars.

## Read the map

The map has a row for each topic and a column for each level.

1. Press a cell on the map.
2. Read that topic at that level on the right side.

A changed cell is marked. On a phone a cell is a small square. You do not type in the map.

Under the map, each changed page has two presses.

- {{button:outline-blue|Open it}} shows the page.
- {{button:outline-blue|Back to revision 2}} puts the page back as it was published.

## Publish a revision

1. Read the list of what changed.
2. Press the blue button on the bar. It names the revision, like {{button:blue|Publish revision 3}}.

A list **Before this can be published** shows while something is missing. Fix each line and the button wakes.

Press **Marks are on** to read the flow as a GC does. Press it again to type.

To mark a revision as read, press **Mark it read** on the bar. Type the attorney's name and press the blue button. A new revision is unread again.

## When someone else changed it

Two people can have one builder open. A tab left open for days counts too.

Your typing is saved only when nobody saved a newer draft since your page read it. If someone did, a yellow panel says so. It names what changed, like **Your usual sheet changed somewhere else**.

What you typed stays on the page. Nothing is saved and nothing is dropped until you press one of the two.

- {{button:blue|Load the newer one}} takes their draft. What you typed here goes.
- {{button:outline-blue|Save mine over it}} saves what you typed. Their draft goes.

A revision someone published stays published either way. The publish button waits until you choose.

## Share the Sub NDA by link

The link is a second way in, for someone with no page of their own. Anyone who holds it walks the published revision. They only look. Nothing they pick is saved or sent.

Find **This builder's link** on the left side, under the list of what changed. Publish a revision first. A link opens only what is published.

1. Press {{button:outline|Switch the link on}}.
2. Press {{button:outline|Copy the link}}.
3. Send the link to the person yourself. The app sends nothing.

The card says the day the link was made. It also says how many times someone outside opened it.

- {{button:outline-blue|Make a new link}} stops the old link. Anyone you gave it to needs the new one.
- {{button:outline-blue|Switch the link off}} closes the builder to links. Switching it on again makes a new link.

The page the link opens is named *Secrecy on your jobs*. It shows the eleven steps with no field to type in. Its last step has no blue button.

A draft never shows on the link. A revision you publish later shows on the same link.

## Send the Sub NDA to a GC

A GC here is a customer who is the GC on a job with you. The card **Send it to a GC** lists each one. The card sits on the left side, under **This builder's link**.

1. Publish the builder.
2. Type a name in **Find a GC** when the list is long.
3. Press {{button:outline-blue|Send it to them}} on the GC's row.
4. Pick who at the GC gets the email.
5. Pick what it is about. *All their jobs with you* is the choice it starts on.
6. Read the note and change it if you want.
7. Press the blue button. The button names the person, like {{button:blue|Send to Dana}}.

The GC gets an email with a link to their own page. The builder is on that page. One about a job sits under that job's name. One about all their jobs sits at the top.

A GC can look only. The GC walks the builder and reads each level. Nothing they pick comes back to you.

- {{chip:gray|No page yet}} means the GC never had a page. Sending to that GC alone makes one.
- {{chip:yellow|Their page is off}} means someone turned the page off. A send never turns a page back on.
- A revision no attorney has read goes only after you tick **Send it anyway**.

One button under the list sends to every GC whose page is on. The button asks first and says how many. Each of those sends is about all their jobs.

## Read where a GC's send stands

- {{chip:gray|Sent Nov 20 to Dana Whitlock}} names the day and the person.
- {{chip:yellow|Not opened yet}} means they have not opened this revision.
- {{chip:green|Opened Nov 21}} names the day they opened it.
- {{chip:gray|All their jobs}} or a job's name says what the send is about.
- {{chip:yellow|Changed since they opened it}} means you published a newer revision since.

- {{button:outline-blue|Send it again}} sends a reminder to the same person.
- {{button:outline-blue|Tell them it changed}} tells them of the new revision.
- {{button:outline-blue|Take it off their page}} ends the send. The builder leaves their page.

Each send is kept under [Sent copies](/documents?tab=sent) on Documents.

## Walk the Sub NDA

The Sub NDA is a flow of eleven steps in one frame. The bar at the top has one dash for each step. Press a dash to open that step.

1. Read *Before you start*.
2. Tick **I have read this.** No later step opens until you do.
3. Press {{button:blue|Next: The basis}}.
4. Press the blue button on each page to move on. The button names the page it opens.

There are four levels: Standard, Elevated, Aggressive and Strict. Each level sits beside a kind of place. The line under a level's name says which.

## Pick the basis

The basis is the level the whole paper starts on. Press a level's row to pick it. You can also drag the slider under the list.

Four facts above the slider change as you move it. Two say how long secrecy lasts and how fast files come back. Two say how fast a leak is reported and how much the site must have.

*Off* on this slider means the job needs no secrecy paper. The blue button then goes straight to *Review*.

## Set one topic

Steps 4 to 10 are one page for each topic. The slider at the bottom sets that topic alone.

- **What puts a person in breach** lists the lines for the picked level. The lines the level brings are on top. The lines it keeps from the level under it sit in grey under *Also applies*.
- **What the site must have** lists what the level asks of the site. Pick a lower level when the site does not have these.
- **The core clause** is drawn as grey bars. No clause wording is in the builder.

A ring on the slider marks the basis. Press the stop named *Basis* to put the page back on it.

Three topics are in every paper and have no *Off*. The three are how long secrecy lasts, files at the end and reporting a leak.

## Read the review

The last step lists every topic with a small track. A dot marks the topic's level and a tick marks the basis. Press a row to open that topic.

*What this site must have* lists what the paper asks of the site as it now stands. Hold that list against the real site.

The button on the review requires nothing of anyone yet. What you pick on the sliders is not saved.
