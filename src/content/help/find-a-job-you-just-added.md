Just entered a job and can't spot it on the board? The Recently added view lists the last 100 jobs added to the app, newest first.

The Pipeline's sections order by job number and hide Paid jobs by default. So a fresh entry isn't always where you expect. The Recently added view includes every status.

## Open the view

1. On **Jobs → Pipeline**, open the ☰ menu at the left end of the stage bar. That is the row that starts with Waiting. Click **Recently added**. Its icon is a clock with an arrow going back.
2. The sections are replaced by one flat list. Each row shows the job number, the name and the job's address. It shows the job's GC or Builder when it has one. GC means the general contractor. A status chip shows where the job currently sits. The row also says when it was added: {{chip:gray|today 12:08 PM}} for today, the date for older jobs.
3. Click any row to open the job's detail window. Everything about the job works from there as usual: status moves, billing, notes.

## Go back

Tap {{chip:blue|Back to board}} at the right end of the stage bar to return to the normal sections.

:::example A link you can share
`/jobs?view=recent` opens the Pipeline already in this view — handy as a bookmark if checking new entries is part of your routine.
:::
