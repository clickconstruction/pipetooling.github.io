A trade partner can ask for a change from its portal. Make it a change order to the customer, or turn it down with why.

A trade partner asks when it finds something no one could see. A trade partner also asks when the customer or the plans change. Each ask waits on the job's change orders until you answer it. Only devs see the asks while GC mode is built.

## Find the asks

1. Press {{button:outline|GC}} on the Bids page to open [GC projects](/gc). Press {{button:outline|Change orders}} on the job's card.
2. A job with asks says {{chip:red|1 asked by the trades}} at the top.

Each ask shows the company, its trade, the day it asked and what it asks. The ask says why and the days it adds.

## Make it a change order

1. Press {{button:blue|Make a change order}} on the ask.
2. Check the words. The words start as the company's own.
3. The company's ask is our cost. The days start as the company's days.
4. The price starts at the cost plus our fee. Type over it to change it.
5. Press {{button:blue|Save the draft}}.

The draft is on the trade's work, for the reason the company gave. Send it to the customer as any change order. See [change our contract with the customer](/help/change-our-contract-with-the-customer).

Delete the draft and the ask goes back on the list.

## Turn it down

1. Press {{button:outline|Turn down}} on the ask.
2. Type why, in words the company reads.
3. Press {{button:blue|Turn it down}}.

The company gets an email with your reason. The company's portal says it too.

## What the company hears

The company gets an email at each step. One goes when you turn its ask down. One goes when its change order goes to the customer, with its part. One goes when the customer says no.

An email that did not go shows a button on the change order. Press {{button:outline|Tell Tri-County Site}} to send it. The same button shows when the customer said no in their own portal.

The company only ever sees our cost. The company never sees the customer's price.
