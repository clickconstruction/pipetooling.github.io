You see everything: customers, projects, jobs, money, people, and payroll. This page is the map, plus what each role below you sees.

This page helps when you're setting someone up or answering "where do I click?"

## The four surfaces you'll live in

:::example Where a dollar lives at each stage
{{button:blue|Bids}} price the work → {{button:blue|Projects}} run the work → {{button:blue|Jobs}} bill the work → {{button:blue|People}} pay the people
:::

1. **Jobs → Pipeline** is the busiest board. The board shows every job by billing state, from Ready to Bill through Collections.
2. **Projects** holds long-running work as step chains. Attention pills mark whatever is stuck. See [project status at a glance](?g=see-project-status-at-a-glance).
3. **Bids** is estimating, from takeoff to submitted proposal.
4. **People** holds accounts, hours, payroll, contracts, and [your subs](?g=review-your-subs).

## Running subs

Offer a sub a step for an agreed amount. The sub accepts from their phone. Approving the walk, your check of the finished work, releases the money into Sub Labor. See [pay a sub per step](?g=pay-a-sub-per-step). What you still owe everyone is in [sub labor outstanding](?g=sub-labor-outstanding).

## Working in the field yourself

The first time you open the Dashboard it offers **Working in the field? Turn on Job Mode.** Tap {{button:green|Turn on Job Mode}} for the one-card field view. The view shows the current job, today's stops, Clock In, Leave Report and Next Job. {{button:outline|Not now}} keeps the full Dashboard. Either way, the {{icon:gear}} gear menu toggles Job Mode later. See [clocking in and out with Job Mode](?g=job-mode-clocking). Subs and helpers get Job Mode on by default.

## What each role sees below you

| Role | Their world | Not visible to them |
|---|---|---|
| **Controller** | Everything an assistant sees, plus payroll and money detail | Nothing |
| **Assistant** | Dispatch, billing, jobs, projects, people | Payroll detail |
| **Superintendent** | Projects, jobs, dispatch, reports, sub sheets | Payroll, company totals, People page |
| **Estimator** | Bids, materials, map, prospects | Jobs, dispatch, crews, money |
| **Primary** | Their own jobs, estimates, bids, billing | Crews, payroll, other customers |
| **Sub / Helper** | Only steps assigned to them, their own schedule and pay | Everything else |

In **Settings → Guides**, the "Viewing guides for" chips flip the guide list to any of these roles. The chips are the fastest way to see what a new hire will actually read.

## Setting someone up

Add the account in Settings → People & teams, and pick the role. For subs and helpers, you can restrict them to service types. Some subs get paid per step. For them, link their roster row to their login, so their work orders and money reach them.
