You see everything: customers, projects, jobs, money and people. This page is the map, plus what each role below you sees.

This page helps when you're setting someone up or answering "where do I click?"

## The four surfaces you'll live in

:::example Where a dollar lives at each stage
{{button:blue|Bids}} price the work → {{button:blue|Projects}} run the work → {{button:blue|Jobs}} bill the work → {{button:blue|People}} pay the people
:::

1. **Jobs → Pipeline** is the busiest board. The board shows every job by billing state, from Waiting through Paid in Full. Collections shows only while a job is in it.
2. **Projects** holds long-running work as step chains. Attention pills mark whatever is stuck. See [project status at a glance](/help/see-project-status-at-a-glance).
3. **Bids** is estimating, from takeoff to submitted proposal.
4. **People** holds accounts, contracts, and [your subs](/help/review-your-subs). The Hours and Payroll tabs show once a dev adds you to the leaders approved for pay.

## Running subs

Offer a sub a step for an agreed amount. The sub accepts from their phone. Once the step is complete or approved, press {{button:blue|Settle → release $X}} on it and confirm. Settling makes the sub's sheet for that money in Sub Labor. See [pay a sub per step](/help/pay-a-sub-per-step). What you still owe each sub is in [sub labor outstanding](/help/sub-labor-outstanding).

## Working in the field yourself

The first time a leader opens the Dashboard, it offers **Working in the field? Turn on Job Mode.** Tap {{button:green|Turn on Job Mode}} for the one-card field view. The view shows the current job, today's stops, Clock In, Leave Report and Next Job. {{button:outline|Not now}} keeps the full Dashboard. Either way, the {{icon:gear}} gear menu toggles Job Mode later. See [clocking in and out with Job Mode](/help/job-mode-clocking). Subs and helpers get Job Mode on by default.

## What each role sees below you

| Role | Their world | Not visible to them |
|---|---|---|
| **Controller** | Everything an assistant sees, plus payroll, Banking and money detail | — |
| **Assistant** | Dispatch, billing, jobs, projects, people | Payroll detail, Banking |
| **Superintendent** | Projects, jobs, dispatch, reports, sub sheets | Payroll, company totals, People page |
| **Estimator** | Bids, materials, map, and prospects when their account has it on | Jobs, dispatch, crews, money |
| **Primary** | Their own jobs, estimates, bids, billing | The Pipeline, crews, payroll, other customers |
| **Sub / Helper** | Only steps assigned to them, their own schedule and pay | Everything else |

In **Settings → Guides**, the "Viewing guides for" chips flip the guide list to any of these roles. The chips are the fastest way to see what a new hire will actually read.

## Setting someone up

Press {{button:blue|+ Hire}} on People → Users, and pick their **Kind**. The Kind decides the role their login gets. Your hire puts them on the roster. A dev then sends the invite that makes their login. See [hire someone](/help/hire-someone).

A dev can limit a sub or helper to some trades, from the Role row on their desk. Some subs get paid per step. Link their roster row to their login, so their work orders and money reach them. Open the row's ⋯ menu and press **Link account**. A dev, or whoever made the row, can do that.
