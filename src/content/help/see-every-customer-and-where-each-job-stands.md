Customers on GC projects opens on a list. Each customer has a card with their jobs, where each job stands, and the next press.

## Open the list

1. Open [GC projects](/gc).
2. Press {{button:outline|Customers}} at the top of the board.
3. The list is the first view. Press {{button:outline|By step}} or {{button:outline|By company}} to see them another way.

The customers who need a hand come first. A customer off the flow sits at the foot in grey.

## Read a card

The band at the top names the customer and counts their jobs. A chip says how they stand: late, lapsed, waiting on someone else, or all quiet. A late customer's card has a red edge.

Under the band you see the person to call, their phone and email, and the address. At the right you see whether the master agreement is signed, and when they last opened their portal.

Each job is a row. You see its stage on the Project Board, the step it is on with its day, and the next press.

:::example A job's row
{{chip:green|Building}} Pay application · waiting on someone else {{button:outline|Bill day}}
:::

## Act from a card

1. Press {{button:outline|Open}} on the band to open the customer's window on its Flow tab.
2. Press a job's name to go to its card on the Project Board.
3. Press the button at the right of a job's row to do the step's own task.

- {{button:blue|Remind Priya}} opens the customer's window at the step's paper with its send ready.
- {{button:outline|Bill day}} opens Money for that job.
- {{button:outline|Open the card}} shows when the step is ours to move.

Nothing goes to a customer until you press Send.
