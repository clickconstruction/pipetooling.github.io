Dispatch Mode is a phone-first shell for whoever's running the day — the office-side companion to Job Mode. Turn it on and a tab bar pins to the bottom of every screen: **Dashboard · Schedule · Inbox · Customers** (plus **PO** if you turn it on).

:::example The pinned tab bar
{{button:blue|Dashboard}} {{button:outline|Schedule}} {{button:outline|Inbox}} {{chip:red|4}} {{button:outline|Customers}} {{button:outline|PO}}

The red badge on Inbox counts **open** dispatch and estimator requests — closed ones don't count, whether or not anyone has dismissed them yet.
:::

## Turning Dispatch Mode on

1. Tap the {{icon:gear}} **gear menu** in the top-right of the header.
2. Toggle **Dispatch Mode**.

It's per-person and per-device, so switching it on for yourself on your phone changes nothing for anyone else (or for you on your desktop).

## The tabs

- **Dashboard** — your clock in/out control up top, then **My Schedule** (your day at a glance), the **financial cards** your role sees on the main Dashboard, and **Job Pipeline**: every open job grouped under Waiting, Working, Ready to bill, Billed awaiting payment, and Collections — each card shows the customer, address, and the assigned crew (on the right on wider screens). Tap a job to open its Job Detail.
- **Schedule** — a two-week strip up top and the selected day's agenda below: every person's blocks with time, duration, job, customer, address, and who's assigned. Each row is two buttons: **tap the time on the left to edit the block** — the Edit schedule block modal opens with the slider, start/end times, and note, and linked crew blocks move together (an overlap with anyone's other blocks stops the save) — and **tap the job info to open the job** as before. That modal also **moves the block to another day**: a **Move day** row sits just under the name, with a chip for yesterday, the block's own day, and the next two days — so back-dating yesterday's visit or pushing a job to tomorrow is one tap. Need a different day than those four? Tap the small **calendar button** beside them and pick any date. The date at the top follows your pick, a banner spells out the day it's moving to, and the blue button changes to **{{button:blue|Move and save}}** so a day change is never a surprise. The new day is checked for clashes the same way the times are — if that person is already booked then, the save stops. One exception: a **crew block that spans several days** can't be moved from here (moving one of its days would drag the rest along); change those from the linked crew instead. The modal's bottom-left {{button:outline|Remove}} button takes the block **off the schedule entirely** — a confirmation asks first, and only that one block is removed (a linked crew-mate's block stays put). A **person chip row** above the agenda isolates whoever you pick — tap one or more names, or **Everyone** to clear. The top-left corner shows the visible month with **‹ ›** arrows that slide the strip across months, each week row is labeled with its week number (w30, w31…), and **Today** brings you back. The blue **+** button opens **Assign work**: pick a job, tap a day, then tap people. The date row opens on the day you were just viewing (weekday letters above the numbers), and after you hit Schedule the page jumps to that day so the new block is right in front of you. A **search box** above the people list finds teams and people by name — matching a team name shows the whole crew. Each team has its **own color** with a header band showing how many people are in it; each person shows a ribbon of their day so you can see who's free at a glance, and tapping a team's header band selects the whole crew — the band turns blue with **✓ crew selected** so there's no doubt. The app suggests times when **everyone selected is free** ("1:00–4:00 · all free"); pick one (or set a custom window — overlaps get an amber warning, but you can still schedule through them), add job instructions, choose whether the crew's blocks are **linked**, and hit Schedule. Need a brand-new job? **Create new job** in the picker opens the New Job form and drops you straight back into the flow with it selected. And **tap a person's name** (or long-press their row) to open **Manage day** right on top of the sheet: their full day for the selected date with a small timeline of their blocks, **‹ ›** arrows to step across days, and **✎ edit** and **🗑 remove** on every block. Edit changes the times, note, or day — for a linked crew block you choose whether the change applies to the **whole crew** or **just that person** (which unlinks them); remove takes off only that person's block, and crew-mates keep theirs. Anything you change refreshes the availability ribbons the moment you're back, and a **+ Select for this assignment** button at the bottom adds them to the crew you're building. Long-press a **crew header** still peeks at the whole crew's day without losing your place.
- **Inbox** — an **Open Gmail** link up top (jumps to your email inbox in a new tab), then your Dashboard's **notification banners** (stale tally transactions, lost bids needing a reason, unallocated bank payments, and the rest) up top, then **My Inbox**, the **Dispatch inbox**, and the **Estimator inbox** stacked in one scroll, working exactly like their Dashboard versions. The tab's red badge counts **open** dispatch + estimator requests only; **Dismiss** on a closed row tidies your own list and never changes the badge. When you **Add & Close** a request, the person who sent it gets a push with your note ("Dispatch answered: Handled: … — your note") and sees it under **My requests** in their Job Mode Inbox — so write the note to them. Adding a note to a closed row reopens it and tells them that too.
- **Customers** — a searchable customer list; each row shows how many jobs that customer has had and when they were last worked (or "in Nd" when a visit is scheduled). Tapping a customer opens their summary: the customer's name as the title, with tappable contact rows underneath — the address opens the map, the email starts an email, and the phone number starts a call. A **billed summary** line shows the money still outstanding across their jobs; tap it to expand which jobs carry the balance (paid-in-full jobs compress to a count). Below that, every interaction across all their jobs — notes, reports, schedule, clock sessions, and system events — with the same All / Notes / Reports / Status / Billing / Crew filter buttons as Job Detail (each button shows how many entries it holds; empty categories fade out and aren't tappable), and each line tagged with its job number and address.
- **PO** *(optional — assistants get it by default; anyone can flip it under the {{icon:gear}} gear menu → **PO tab** while Dispatch Mode is on)* — mint a material PO code in three taps: pick the job (today's schedule is offered first), who the material is for (that job's crew floats to the top), optionally a supply house (your last one is pre-picked) and a note, then **Generate PO**. The code shows huge — read it across the counter — with **Copy** and **Text to {{chip:gray|tech}}** buttons, and a **Recent POs** list below so you can re-read a code or check one was already made. Same numbering and ledger as Materials → PO Generator. Long lists stay tidy with an **Other** bucket: **hold** any person or supply house and **slide to confirm** — it tucks under the **Other (N)** entry at the end of that list, for the whole company. Tap **Other** to pick from the tucked-away options, or hold one and slide to bring it back. When you pick from Other, the row shows just that name with an orange ring — tap it again to see the full list. People working the picked job today always stay in the main list, and nothing is ever deleted — moving back is the undo.

## Turning it off

Same gear menu, tap **Dispatch Mode** again — the tab bar disappears and the app is exactly as before.
