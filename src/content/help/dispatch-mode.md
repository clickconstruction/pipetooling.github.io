Dispatch Mode is a phone-first shell for whoever's running the day — the office-side companion to Job Mode. Turn it on and a tab bar pins to the bottom of every screen: **Dashboard · Schedule · Inbox · Customers** (plus **PO** if you turn it on).

## Turning Dispatch Mode on

1. Tap the {{icon:gear}} **gear menu** in the top-right of the header.
2. Toggle **Dispatch Mode**.

It's per-person and per-device, so switching it on for yourself on your phone changes nothing for anyone else (or for you on your desktop).

## The tabs

- **Dashboard** — your clock in/out control up top, then **My Schedule** (your day at a glance), the **financial cards** your role sees on the main Dashboard, and **Job Pipeline**: every open job grouped under Waiting, Working, Ready to bill, Billed awaiting payment, and Collections — each card shows the customer, address, and the assigned crew (on the right on wider screens). Tap a job to open its Job Detail.
- **Schedule** — a two-week strip up top and the selected day's agenda below: every person's blocks with time, duration, job, customer, address, and who's assigned. Each row is two buttons: **tap the time on the left to edit the block** — the Edit schedule block modal opens with the slider, start/end times, and note, and linked crew blocks move together (an overlap with anyone's other blocks stops the save) — and **tap the job info to open the job** as before. That modal also **moves the block to another day**: a **Move day** row sits just under the name, with a chip for yesterday, the block's own day, and the next two days — so back-dating yesterday's visit or pushing a job to tomorrow is one tap. Need a different day than those four? Tap the small **calendar button** beside them and pick any date. The date at the top follows your pick, a banner spells out the day it's moving to, and the blue button changes to **{{button:primary|Move and save}}** so a day change is never a surprise. The new day is checked for clashes the same way the times are — if that person is already booked then, the save stops. One exception: a **crew block that spans several days** can't be moved from here (moving one of its days would drag the rest along); change those from the linked crew instead. A **person chip row** above the agenda isolates whoever you pick — tap one or more names, or **Everyone** to clear. The top-left corner shows the visible month with **‹ ›** arrows that slide the strip across months, each week row is labeled with its week number (w30, w31…), and **Today** brings you back. The blue **+** button opens **Assign work**: pick a job, tap a day, then tap people — each person shows a ribbon of their day so you can see who's free at a glance, and tapping a swim-lane name selects the whole crew. The app suggests times when **everyone selected is free** ("1:00–4:00 · all free"); pick one (or set a custom window — overlaps get an amber warning, but you can still schedule through them), add job instructions, choose whether the crew's blocks are **linked**, and hit Schedule. Need a brand-new job? **Create new job** in the picker opens the New Job form and drops you straight back into the flow with it selected. And **long-press any person or crew name** to peek at their full day (times, jobs, customers, addresses) without losing your place — the tip next to the title reminds you.
- **Inbox** — an **Open Gmail** link up top (jumps to your email inbox in a new tab), then your Dashboard's **notification banners** (stale tally transactions, lost bids needing a reason, unallocated bank payments, and the rest) up top, then **My Inbox**, the **Dispatch inbox**, and the **Estimator inbox** stacked in one scroll, working exactly like their Dashboard versions. The tab's red badge counts open dispatch + estimator requests.
- **Customers** — a searchable customer list; each row shows how many jobs that customer has had and when they were last worked (or "in Nd" when a visit is scheduled). Tapping a customer opens their summary: the customer's name as the title, with tappable contact rows underneath — the address opens the map, the email starts an email, and the phone number starts a call. A **billed summary** line shows the money still outstanding across their jobs; tap it to expand which jobs carry the balance (paid-in-full jobs compress to a count). Below that, every interaction across all their jobs — notes, reports, schedule, clock sessions, and system events — with the same All / Notes / Reports / Status / Billing / Crew filter buttons as Job Detail, and each line tagged with its job number and address.
- **PO** *(optional — assistants get it by default; anyone can flip it under the {{icon:gear}} gear menu → **PO tab** while Dispatch Mode is on)* — mint a material PO code in three taps: pick the job (today's schedule is offered first), who the material is for (that job's crew floats to the top), optionally a supply house (your last one is pre-picked) and a note, then **Generate PO**. The code shows huge — read it across the counter — with **Copy** and **Text to {{chip:gray|tech}}** buttons, and a **Recent POs** list below so you can re-read a code or check one was already made. Same numbering and ledger as Materials → PO Generator. Long lists stay tidy with an **Other** bucket: **hold** any person or supply house and **slide to confirm** — it tucks under the **Other (N)** entry at the end of that list, for the whole company. Tap **Other** to pick from the tucked-away options, or hold one and slide to bring it back. When you pick from Other, the row shows just that name with an orange ring — tap it again to see the full list. People working the picked job today always stay in the main list, and nothing is ever deleted — moving back is the undo.

## Turning it off

Same gear menu, tap **Dispatch Mode** again — the tab bar disappears and the app is exactly as before.
