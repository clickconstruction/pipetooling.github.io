Dispatch Mode is a phone-first shell for whoever's running the day. It is the office-side companion to Job Mode.

Turn it on and a tab bar pins to the bottom of every screen. The tabs are **Dashboard**, **Schedule**, **Inbox** and **Customers**. **PO** joins them if you turn it on. PO means purchase order. On a phone, an **assistant's** bar is her own four. Those are **Jobs**, **Schedule**, **PO** and **Inbox**, with **More** as the fifth.

The red badge on Inbox counts **open** dispatch and estimator requests — closed ones don't count, whether or not anyone has dismissed them yet.

**Jobs** opens the Pipeline; **PO** is the three-tap code for the counter. To change a slot, tap **More**, tap that slot in **The dock** row at the top, then tap the page you want in its place — Quickfill, Supply houses, Subs · Pay, People · Hours, Customers, anything you can open — and it stays that way on that phone (**press and hold** the slot on the bar does the same). **Reset to the role's four** under the row puts it back. **More** is the phone's one menu: a *Suggested for you* row from where you spend your time, every page, the mode switches, and Help / Settings / Sign out. While this bar is up the {{icon:gear}} gear and the ☰ menu step aside — everything in them is under More. Your desktop keeps its menus.
:::

## Turning Dispatch Mode on

1. Tap the {{icon:gear}} **gear menu** in the top-right of the header.
2. Toggle **Dispatch Mode**.

It's per-person and per-device. Switching it on for yourself on your phone changes nothing for anyone else. It changes nothing for you on your desktop either.

## The tabs

- **Dashboard** starts with your clock in/out control up top. Then comes **My Schedule**, your day at a glance. Then come the **financial cards** your role sees on the main Dashboard. Then comes **Job Pipeline**, every open job grouped by stage. The groups are Waiting, Working, Ready to bill, Billed awaiting payment and Collections. Each card shows the customer, the address and the assigned crew. The crew sits on the right on wider screens. Tap a job to open its Job Detail.
- **Schedule** shows a two-week strip up top and the selected day's agenda below. The agenda lists every person's blocks with time, duration, job, customer, address and who's assigned. Each row is two buttons. **Tap the time on the left to edit the block.** The Edit schedule block modal opens with the slider, start and end times, and note. Linked crew blocks move together. An overlap with anyone's other blocks stops the save. **Tap the job info for the block's sheet.** The sheet holds {{button:outline|Open the job}}, {{button:outline|Add a note}}, {{button:outline|Move or reassign}} and {{button:red|Remove from the schedule}}. **Move or reassign** picks the day and the person in one step. It is the way to hand a block to someone else. Someone who cannot edit the schedule still opens the job with that tap. The edit modal also **moves the block to another day**. A **Move day** row sits just under the name. It has a chip for yesterday, the block's own day and the next two days. So back-dating yesterday's visit or pushing a job to tomorrow is one tap. Need a different day than those four? Tap the small **calendar button** beside them and pick any date. The date at the top follows your pick. A banner spells out the day it's moving to. The blue button changes to **{{button:blue|Move and save}}**. So a day change is never a surprise. The new day is checked for clashes the same way the times are. If that person is already booked then, the save stops. One exception: a **crew block that spans several days** can't be moved from here. Moving one of its days would drag the rest along. Change those from the linked crew instead. The modal's bottom-left {{button:outline|Remove}} button takes the block **off the schedule entirely**. A confirmation asks first. Only that one block is removed. A linked crew-mate's block stays put. A **person chip row** above the agenda isolates whoever you pick. Tap one or more names, or **Everyone** to clear. The top-left corner shows the visible month with **‹ ›** arrows. The arrows slide the strip across months. Each week row is labeled with its week number, like w30 or w31. **Today** brings you back. The blue **+** button opens **Assign work**. Pick a job, tap a day, then tap people. The date row opens on the day you were just viewing, with weekday letters above the numbers. After you hit Schedule the page jumps to that day. So the new block is right in front of you. A **search box** above the people list finds teams and people by name. Matching a team name shows the whole crew. Each team has its **own color**, with a header band showing how many people are in it. Each person shows a ribbon of their day. Every busy bar reads the job number, the customer's name and the job's town. The customer's name is its first two words. A bar reads like *J568 · Diamondback Homes · Neeses*. Hovering one shows the job name, customer and address. So you can see who's free at a glance. Tapping a team's header band selects the whole crew. The band turns blue with **✓ crew selected**, so there's no doubt. The app suggests times when **everyone selected is free**. A suggestion reads like *1:00–4:00 · all free*. Pick one, or set a custom window. Overlaps get an amber warning, but you can still schedule through them. Add job instructions. Choose whether the crew's blocks are **linked**. Then hit Schedule. Need a brand-new job? **Create new job** in the picker opens the New Job form. It drops you straight back into the flow with the new job selected. **Tap a person's name**, or long-press their row, to open **Manage day** right on top of the sheet. Manage day shows their full day for the selected date with a small timeline of their blocks. It has **‹ ›** arrows to step across days, and **✎ edit** and **🗑 remove** on every block. Edit changes the times, note or day. For a linked crew block you choose whether the change applies to the **whole crew** or **just that person**. Choosing just that person unlinks them. Remove takes off only that person's block. Crew-mates keep theirs. Anything you change refreshes the availability ribbons the moment you're back. A **+ Select for this assignment** button at the bottom adds them to the crew you're building. Long-press a **crew header** still peeks at the whole crew's day without losing your place.
- **Inbox** starts with an **Open Gmail** link up top. It jumps to your email inbox in a new tab. Then come your Dashboard's **notification banners**. Those are stale tally transactions, lost bids needing a reason, unallocated bank payments and the rest. Then **My Inbox**, the **Dispatch inbox** and the **Estimator inbox** stack in one scroll. They work exactly like their Dashboard versions. The tab's red badge counts **open** dispatch and estimator requests only. **Dismiss** on a closed row tidies your own list and never changes the badge. Open requests list **oldest first**. Each carries an age chip. It reads {{chip:gray|today}} or {{chip:gray|2 days waiting}} while it's fresh. It reads {{chip:yellow|4 days waiting}} from day 3 and {{chip:red|12 days waiting}} from day 7. So the request that has waited longest is at the top and reads red. It never sits gray under this morning's. Once the oldest open request is 3+ days old, your Dashboard's **Needs You** card adds a **Dispatch inbox** item. That item says how old it is and jumps you here. When you **Add & Close** a request, the person who sent it gets a push with your note. The push reads *Dispatch answered: Handled: … — your note*. They also see it under **My requests** in their Job Mode Inbox. So write the note to them. Adding a note to a closed row reopens it and tells them that too. A row that reads *Open the job for B398 · … — won with …* is a won bid handed to you. Its {{button:outline|Open the job}} opens New Job filled in from the bid. The row closes itself when you press **Create Job**. See [turn a won bid into a job](?g=turn-a-won-bid-into-a-job). On a phone, an office login sees a different layout. Office logins are assistant, controller, primary and estimator. The tab leads with **Needs you** one card at a time. **My Inbox** and the **Team inboxes** fold to one line each. The lines read like *My Inbox · due today 2 · overdue 1* and *Team inboxes · Dispatch 5 open · oldest 4 d*. Tap {{button:outline|Open ▾}} to read one in place. The phone remembers which you left open. **Open Gmail** sits at the foot of the page.
- **Customers** is a searchable customer list. Each row shows how many jobs that customer has had and when they were last worked. It reads "in Nd" when a visit is scheduled. Tapping a customer opens their summary. The customer's name is the title, with tappable contact rows underneath. The address opens the map. The email starts an email. The phone number starts a call. A **billed summary** line shows the money still outstanding across their jobs. Tap it to expand which jobs carry the balance. Paid-in-full jobs compress to a count. Below that sits every interaction across all their jobs. That means notes, reports, schedule, clock sessions and system events. The same filter buttons as Job Detail sit above them: All, Notes, Reports, Status, Billing and Crew. Each button shows how many entries it holds. Empty categories fade out and aren't tappable. Each line is tagged with its job number and address.
- **PO** is optional. Assistants, controllers and leaders get it by default. Anyone can flip it under the {{icon:gear}} gear menu → **PO tab** while Dispatch Mode is on. It mints a material PO code in three taps. Pick the job. Today's schedule is offered first. Pick who the material is for. That job's crew floats to the top. Optionally pick a supply house. Your last one is pre-picked. Optionally add **what they said they need**, in their words, like *40ft of 3/4 PEX*. Then tap **Generate PO**. The code shows huge. Read it across the counter. What they said sits under it. If you skipped that step, the question **What did they say they need?** sits there instead. So you can ask and {{button:blue|Write it down}} while they are standing in front of you. **Copy** and **Text to {{chip:gray|tech}}** buttons carry the code. A **Recent POs** list below lets you re-read a code or check one was already made. A code with nothing written down reads *add what it was for…* there. So the claim can land later. It uses the same numbering and ledger as Materials → PO Generator. Pick a job and a supply house. Then a line under the chips says whether the job has a **job account** at that house. It is amber with **Call**, **Mark opened…** and **Send the packet** when it has none. It is teal with the reference once it does. See [open a job account before buying parts](?g=open-a-job-account-before-buying-parts). The line shows again under the big code. Long lists stay tidy with an **Other** bucket. **Hold** any person or supply house and **slide to confirm**. It tucks under the *Other (N)* entry at the end of that list, for the whole company. Tap **Other** to pick from the tucked-away options. Or hold one and slide to bring it back. When you pick from Other, the row shows just that name with an orange ring. Tap it again to see the full list. People working the picked job today always stay in the main list. Nothing is ever deleted. Moving back is the undo.

## Turning it off

Open the same gear menu and tap **Dispatch Mode** again. The tab bar disappears and the app is exactly as before. On the assistant's phone bar the gear is under **More → Modes**.
