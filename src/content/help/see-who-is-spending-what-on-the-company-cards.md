People → Spending shows how much each person spent on the company cards in a period. It also shows how much of it is on a job.

## Pick a period

Open [People → Spending](/people?tab=spending). Press a period at the top. The page opens on {{button:dark|This month}}. Press {{button:outline|Custom}} to pick your own first and last day.

A custom range can be up to one year long. A longer range keeps its last 366 days.

## Read the totals

Five boxes sit above the table.

- **Card spend** is every card charge in the period. A refund comes off.
- **⛽ Fuel** is the part that was fuel. It follows the same rule as the fuel line on a job.
- **On jobs** is the part someone put on a job.
- **Not on a job yet** is the part nobody has put on a job. The box turns amber when there is any.
- **Not tied to anyone** is the part with no person and no card holder.

Money moved between our own accounts is not spend, so it is left out.

## Read the table

Each person has one row. **Company cards** and **Not tied to anyone** come last. **Everyone** is the total.

A charge belongs to the person Banking puts on it. With nobody on it, it belongs to the person who holds the card. A company card charge with nobody on it counts under **Company cards**.

A charge on a supply house invoice counts as on a job. The job counts it through the invoice.

Money put on the **Office** job is not job cost. It shows in its own **Office** column.

## Open a person

Press the arrow beside a name. The row opens to the person's jobs. Press a job to open its Job window.

Under the jobs you see the person's supply house invoices and the charges not on a job yet. Press {{button:outline|See the charges}} to list them.

On a phone, each person is a card. Press {{button:outline|Show the details}} on the card. The card opens to the jobs and the charges not on a job yet.

A line under the person says when a charge counts only because the card is theirs. Put a person on it in Banking to change that.

## Put a charge on a job

1. Press {{button:outline|See the charges}} under a person. On a phone, open the person's card.
2. Press {{button:outline|Put on a job}} beside a charge.
3. Pick the job in the window that opens. It is the same window as [Team purchases](/help/sort-the-teams-card-purchases).
4. Save. The page loads again and the charge moves to **On jobs**.

Some charges have no button. A line says who can put them on a job instead. A charge bought before sorting began has no button either.

## Charges marked as payroll

Some card charges are marked as payroll in Job Parts Tally. Only people with payroll access see them. They show in a **Payroll** column.

Everyone else does not see those charges. Their totals are lower by that amount. A line at the top of the page says so.

:::example One person's month
Malachi spent $4,218 on his card in September. $1,106 of it was fuel. $3,540 is on jobs. $678 is not on a job yet, from seven charges.
:::
