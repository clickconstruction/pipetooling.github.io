You have a client-side view: your jobs, their money, and the estimates and bids attached to them. You don't manage crews or see other customers' work.

## Where things are

- **Dashboard** is today at a glance: what's scheduled, what's ready to bill, and recent field reports.
- **Jobs** shows the field reports from your jobs. **Newest**, **By job** and **By person** sort them. Under By job, {{button:outline|Edit job}} opens that job's window. The window holds its line items, bills, payments and reports.

:::example Where a job stands
**1482 · 210 Oak Bend Dr** — Water heater replacement

The stepper on the job window's Edit tab shows its billing state: {{chip:gray|Waiting}} {{chip:yellow|Working}} {{chip:purple|Ready to bill}} {{chip:blue|Billed}} {{chip:green|Paid}}
:::
- **Estimates** holds proposals out for acceptance, and what's been accepted.
- **Bids** is the Bid Board for your trade, plus RFIs, change orders and lien releases. An RFI, a request for information, is a written question about the plans. A change order is a priced change to the agreed work. A lien release is the paper that gives up our lien rights for the work it covers. Pricing, cover letters, followup and the estimating workbench are office-only, so they stay out of view.
- **Calendar** and **Job Parts Tally** are for the schedule and parts logging. Calendar is in the {{icon:gear}} gear menu. Job Parts Tally opens from Settings.

## Billing

Your Dashboard's **Ready to Bill** card lists your jobs that are finished and waiting to be billed. The card shows only when there are some. Open a job and bill it from the window's **Bill** tab. See [bill a customer and get paid](/help/ready-to-bill-pipeline). You can also split a large job so you bill it in pieces.

## Reports

Field reports with photos land on the job, and on your Jobs page. [Reports](/help/reports) covers reading them.

## What you don't see

The Pipeline board, payroll, crew costs, sub pay, and company-wide totals are office-only. If a number you expect is missing, that's usually why. Ask the office.
