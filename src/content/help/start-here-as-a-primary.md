You have a client-side view: your jobs, their money, and the estimates and bids attached to them. You don't manage crews or see other customers' work.

## Where things are

- **Dashboard** is today at a glance: what's scheduled, what's ready to bill, and recent activity.
- **Jobs** is the job list, with billing state on every row. Open a job for its full detail: line items, invoices, payments and reports.

:::example A row in your job list
**1482 · 210 Oak Bend Dr** — Water heater replacement {{chip:blue|Billed}} `$3,850`

The chip is the billing state: {{chip:gray|Waiting}} {{chip:yellow|Working}} {{chip:purple|Ready to Bill}} {{chip:blue|Billed}} {{chip:green|Paid}}
:::
- **Estimates** holds proposals out for acceptance, and what's been accepted.
- **Bids** is the Bid Board for your trade, plus RFIs, change orders and lien releases. An RFI, a request for information, is a written question about the plans. A change order is a priced change to the agreed work. A lien release is the paper that gives up a claim on the property. Pricing, cover letters, followup and the estimating workbench are office-only. They stay out of view.
- **Calendar** and **Job Parts Tally** are for the schedule and parts logging.

## Billing

**Ready to Bill** is the queue of work finished and waiting to be invoiced. See [the ready-to-bill pipeline](?g=ready-to-bill-pipeline). From a job you can [bill a customer and get paid](?g=ready-to-bill-pipeline). You can also split a large job so you bill it in pieces.

## Reports

Field reports with photos land on the job. [Reports](?g=reports) covers reading them and emailing them out.

## What you don't see

Payroll, crew costs, sub pay, and company-wide totals are office-only. If a number you expect is missing, that's usually why. Ask the office.
