With no signal the phone keeps your press and sends it when the signal is back. One line at the top of every screen tells you where each press stands.

## What waits on the phone

Four presses can wait on the phone. Each one is kept with the moment you pressed it.

- Clock In and Clock Out, from the Dashboard or Job Mode.
- A job note from Job Mode.
- A field report from {{button:green|Leave Report}}.

When you press one with no signal, the window closes as it always does. A small message reads *Kept on the phone. It goes out when the signal is back.* The clock shows you clocked in from the moment you pressed. You can go to work.

## What needs a signal

Some presses never wait on the phone. They fail as they do today, and you press them again when the signal is back.

- A payment, a bill or a card split.
- A text or an email to a customer.
- A signature.
- A field report with photos. The report stays open with its photos. Press {{button:outline|Retry}} when the signal is back.
- A Turnaway report, because it also raises a dispatch alert.

## The line at the top

The line at the top of the screen is the queue's face. The line is gone when nothing waits.

:::example While a press waits

**Clock in from 7:58 AM** · waiting to send · trying again in 12 s · {{button:outline|Try now}}

:::

The line is amber while a press waits. The phone tries again on its own, after 5, 10, 20 and 30 seconds, and then every 30 seconds. It also tries the moment the signal returns. You do not need to do anything. {{button:outline|Try now}} sends at once if you want to.

With no signal the line reads *No signal. 2 waiting to send ›*. Tap the words to see every press that waits.

:::example Once a press lands

**Clock in from 7:58 AM sent ✓** {{button:green|Got it}}

:::

The line turns green when a press lands. It stays green until you press {{button:green|Got it}}. When several land together it reads *3 sent ✓ · see them*.

:::example When the office refuses one

**Clock in from 7:58 AM could not be sent.** The office closed that session at 8:05 AM. {{button:outline|Fix}}

:::

The line turns red only when a press needs you. That happens when the office refuses it, or when a press has waited since yesterday. The line says why. {{button:outline|Fix}} opens the place to sort it out. The other presses still go.

## The list behind the line

Tap the words on the line to open the list. The list has three parts.

- **Needs you** holds a press the office refused, with the reason, {{button:outline|Fix}}, {{button:outline|Send now}} and {{button:outline|Drop it}}.
- **Waiting** holds each press that waits, with the time you pressed it and the last try.
- **Sent today** holds each press that landed, with the time you pressed it and the time it landed.

Drop it removes a press you no longer want sent. Nothing is sent twice. Every press carries its own id, and the office counts a repeat once.

## A clock press that lands late

A clock press that reaches the office late is filed like a late entry. The hours before it landed show as typed by you. They wait for approval like any typed hours. The session carries a note with the time you pressed and the time it reached the office. Read [report a day the clock missed](/help/report-a-day-the-clock-missed) for how typed hours are approved.

## Today's schedule with no signal

My Schedule and Job Mode show your day as it was last read. A stamp at the top says when. Read [know what to do when the app is slow](/help/know-what-to-do-when-the-app-is-slow) for the kept page and the slow line.

## For the office

A press that waited is filed at the moment it was pressed. A late clock in or clock out shows on Hours as typed by the person. The session carries the sent late note. Approve it as you would any typed hours.
