You bid the property, you know the GC, and you already asked Ferguson and Reece for prices on it. When the bid is won, you are the natural person to tell the house "we got 4114 Pond Hill — open the account." The tools for that are on the job the win moment creates, and they are yours as the estimator even though the rest of the job is not.

## Win, open the job, answer the question

On the bid's **Job** block (Edit Bid, the Waiting-to-hear strip, the per-GC panel), {{button:green|Open the job}} is unchanged. Once the job saves, the app asks **Job accounts for J1018?** — the same question the office gets:

- The houses that **quoted this bid** come first and start picked, with a *· quoted* mark. Every other house that expects a job account follows.
- {{button:blue|Send to Dispatch}} files the ask with the office today, so the account is open before the first parts run.
- **Ask the rep by email…** is your lane (below).
- **None needed** and **Later** are one tap.

## Ask the rep by email

Pick the house. The sheet shows who it goes to — the house's {{chip:green|Job accounts rep}} — and what it will say, all from the bid: the property address, our company, the GC and the contact you submitted to, the start date. The owner of record is soft here: if the office does not have it yet, the line reads *to follow from our office*, with **Send to Dispatch — find the owner** right there.

{{button:blue|Email from my inbox}} opens the draft in your own mail client; {{button:outline|Copy for email}} puts the addressed text on your clipboard. Then one honest tap: {{button:blue|Sent — log it}} marks the house **requested** on the job and remembers the send; **Didn't send it** backs out.

:::example What Curly gets
**Subject: Job account — 4114 Pond Hill Rd, Building #2, San Antonio, TX 78231 (Click Plumbing and Electrical)**
Curly — we won this one. Please open a job account for Click Plumbing and Electrical at the property below; first parts run is around Sep 22. Property: 4114 Pond Hill Rd … General contractor: H & I Construction — Marco Reyes, 210-555-0170 … Owner of record: to follow from our office. Reply here or call the office at (512) 360-0599. — Tristen
:::

No rep with an email on file? The sheet says so and points you to **Materials → Supply houses → the house → Contacts** (role *Job accounts*), which you can edit.

## See it on the bid, mark it opened

Under the Job block the **Job accounts** line reads the state per house — {{chip:green|Ferguson ✓}} {{chip:purple|Reece · requested}} {{chip:gray|Moore · none yet · quoted}} — and **Job accounts…** reopens the question whenever you want to come back to it. When the rep says yes, {{button:green|Mark opened…}} takes the reference and how it was opened; the tech's card reads ✓ at the counter from then on.

What you cannot see stays the same: invoices, balances and the Job Accounts money view are the office's. See [open a job account before buying parts](?g=open-a-job-account-before-buying-parts) for the field's and the office's side.
