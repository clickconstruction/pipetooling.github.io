You bid the property, and you know the GC. You already asked Ferguson and Reece for prices on it.

GC means the general contractor. When the bid is won, you are the natural person to tell the house. You say "we got 4114 Pond Hill, open the account". The tools for that are on the job the win moment creates. They are yours as the estimator, even though the rest of the job is not.

## Win, open the job, answer the question

On the bid's **Job** block, {{button:green|Open the job}} is unchanged. The Job block is on Edit Bid, the Waiting-to-hear strip, and the per-GC panel. Once the job saves, the app asks **Job accounts for J1018?** It is the same question the office gets:

- The houses that **quoted this bid** come first and start picked, with a *· quoted* mark. Every other house that expects a job account follows.
- {{button:blue|Send to Dispatch}} files the ask with the office today. So the account is open before the first parts run.
- **Ask the rep by email…** is your lane, below.
- **None needed** and **Later** are one tap.

## Ask the rep by email

You pick the house. The sheet shows who it goes to, the house's {{chip:green|Job accounts rep}}. It shows what it will say, all from the bid. That is the property address, our company, the GC and the contact you submitted to, and the start date. The owner of record is soft here. If the office does not have it yet, the line reads *to follow from our office*. ***Send to Dispatch — find the owner*** sits right there.

{{button:blue|Email from my inbox}} opens the draft in your own mail client. {{button:outline|Copy for email}} puts the addressed text on your clipboard. Then one honest tap. {{button:blue|Sent — log it}} marks the house **requested** on the job and remembers the send. **Didn't send it** backs out.

:::example What Curly gets
**Subject: Job account — 4114 Pond Hill Rd, Building #2, San Antonio, TX 78231 (Click Plumbing and Electrical)**
Curly — we won this one. Please open a job account for Click Plumbing and Electrical at the property below; first parts run is around Sep 22. Property: 4114 Pond Hill Rd … General contractor: H & I Construction — Marco Reyes, 210-555-0170 … Owner of record: to follow from our office. Reply here or call the office at (512) 360-0599. — Tristen
:::

No rep with an email on file? The sheet says so. It points you to **Materials → Supply houses → the house → Contacts**, role *Job accounts*. You can edit that.

## See it on the bid, mark it opened

Under the Job block, the **Job accounts** line reads the state per house. It shows {{chip:green|Ferguson ✓}} {{chip:purple|Reece · requested}} {{chip:gray|Moore · none yet · quoted}}. **Job accounts…** reopens the question whenever you want to come back to it. When the rep says yes, you **tap the house's chip**. It opens {{button:green|Mark opened…}} for that house. You enter how it was opened, the reference, and a note. The tech's card reads ✓ at the counter from then on.

**From the Bid Board too.** On **Bids → Bid Board**, under **Won** and **Started or Complete**, the same chips sit under the GC's line. They read {{chip:green|Ferguson ✓}} {{chip:purple|Moore Supply · asked}} {{chip:gray|Reece · quoted}}. You read them in one pass for every won bid. So you need not open each bid to see where its accounts stand. A chip that is not open opens **Mark opened** for that house. The **…** beside them reopens the Job accounts question. A won bid with no job yet reads *after the job is opened*. Each section's header counts the bids with a house still missing. So the pile is visible from the top. A bid moves from Won to Started the moment its job is opened. So the rows with chips are mostly there.

## Work the pile from Followup

**Bids → Followup → Job accounts** is the same list as one page. It shows every won job still missing an account, soonest first parts run first. It is grouped by house, with the rep named. One row reads {{chip:gray|Ferguson}} *none yet · quoted this bid*. The next reads {{chip:purple|Ferguson · asked}} *Sep 12 · Curly opens them*. Each row has {{button:outline|Mark opened…}} and **Not needed**. A won bid with no job yet reads **Open the job first**. **Only my bids** keeps it to the bids you estimate or manage. On the Bid Board, the *N missing job accounts* note in the Won and Started headers opens it.

From a house's band, {{button:blue|Ask Curly for both}} drafts one email for the ticked properties. Each property is a numbered block with its own address, GC and owner line. The start weeks are joined. You send it from your inbox, or you copy it. Then {{button:green|Sent — log both}} marks every ticked job **requested** at that house. Rows already asked about sit out of the email until the house answers. Bids with no job sit out until the job exists.

:::example One note to Curly for two properties
**Subject: Job accounts — 2 properties (Click Plumbing and Electrical)**
Curly — we won these two. Please open a job account for Click Plumbing and Electrical at each property below; first parts runs are the week of Sep 21. 1. 4114 Pond Hill Rd, Building #2 … General contractor: H & I Construction … 2. 118 Vaughn Ln, Buda … General contractor: Dudley Mason … Reply here or call the office at (512) 360-0599. — Tristen
:::

What you cannot see stays the same. Invoices, balances and the Held for suppliers money view are the office's. See [open a job account before buying parts](?g=open-a-job-account-before-buying-parts) for the field's and the office's side.

## On the bid's step strip

You open a won bid's row on the Bid Board. Its **Where this bid is** strip runs past *Sent* into a **Won** lane. **11 Job opened** turns done once the win moment has created the job. **12 Job accounts** turns done once every house is {{chip:green|open}} or marked not needed. That is every house that quoted the bid or expects an account. While a house is still missing, step 12 rings as *next* with the name under it, like *Reece missing*. Clicking it opens the same Job accounts question the row's **…** chip opens. Before the job exists, step 12 reads as untracked. The account keys on the job.

## Catching a house up

On the house's card under Materials → Supply houses, the **Job accounts** roster shows every job with an account. It also shows every amber *bought, no account* job. There are two shortcuts for the pile. {{button:green|Mark all N opened…}} at the top writes one open row per amber job. Each gets the same how, rep and note. You add each house reference from the row's **Edit** when you have it. {{button:green|Flag n invoices}} on an open row flags every invoice allocated to that job alone as on the job account. The aging table then reads it as owner-secured from then on.
