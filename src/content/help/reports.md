Field reports turn what happened on site into something the office can act on. Techs file them in under a minute, and the office reviews them in one place.

The office can also subscribe to the report types they care about.

## Filing a report (field side)

You tap {{button:blue|Leave Report}} on the Job Mode card, or during clock-out. On a phone the camera opens first, and each shot goes with the report into the customer's Drive folder. See [take photos for a report](/help/take-photos-for-a-report). The **Reporting on** card pre-fills the job from your last report. It says so under the name. You tap {{button:outline|Change}} to search for a different job, project, or bid instead. You choose a report type, fill in the fields, and submit. Your location is attached automatically.

On a phone the report form opens **full screen** with {{button:blue|Save report}} pinned at the bottom. There is no scrolling to find it. If you close the form with something typed, it asks before discarding your entries. Switching report types keeps what you've typed. You can jump from Status to Note and back without losing anything. Only the fields of the type you save are submitted.

Say your scheduled time on a job ended today and you haven't filed a report in the last 12 hours. Then the Dashboard nudges you. A yellow ⚠ badge appears over that job's {{button:blue|Leave Report}} button. My Schedule shows the same note under **Today**: *"You haven't filed a report yet. File one."* Filing a report clears both.

Report type: &nbsp;{{button:outline-blue|Status}} &nbsp; {{button:outline|Walk}} &nbsp; {{button:outline|Note}}

How complete is the job? `100` %

{{button:outline|Cancel}} &nbsp; {{button:blue|Save report}}

The most common type is the **Status Report**. It is a general progress update with "How complete is the job?" as a percentage. That percentage becomes the job's **% done** everywhere. The Jobs Pipeline progress bar and "% done" box update the moment the report saves. So do the dashboard cards and the job's activity feed. The feed gets a *N% complete — from field report* note. Reporting **100%** on a Working job also triggers the prompt below. That is how finished work flows straight into billing. See the billing guide.

The slider opens on the job's **current %**. The line under it says where that number came from. It reads *Currently 30% — move to update · crew report Aug 27*, or *· set by office*. If you leave it where it is, the report files that same number. The job's % doesn't move. If you drag it or tap a quick pick, the job follows. The line changes to *Was 30%*.

### Talk it through

You can say the report instead of typing it. The button {{button:outline-blue|Talk it through}} sits over **Report type**.

1. Press {{button:outline-blue|Talk it through}}. The button turns red and reads *Listening*.
2. Say what was done, which lines you worked on, the parts you used and the next step.
3. Stop talking, or press the button again. The button also stops on its own after a few seconds of quiet.
4. Read the draft. Fix anything it got wrong.
5. Press {{button:blue|Save report}}.

When the owner has set a model key, the app drafts the report from your words. **Notes** gets three short lines. The three lines say what was done, the parts and the next step. Each line you named gets its number, and the first one opens. Notes shows *Drafted from your voice note* in blue until you type in it. Under the fields, **What you said** holds your words and the lines the draft set. Each line shows the words it came from. Check the numbers against your words before you save.

Without a model key, your words go into Notes as you said them. You set the lines as usual. The button says which one happened.

The words go under anything already in Notes. Nothing is saved until you press Save report. The button listens in your phone's own language, so Spanish works too. A draft from Spanish is written in English, and **Lo que dijiste** keeps your own words.

Some phones have no speech for the app. Then the button reads *Use the microphone on your keyboard*. Tap Notes, then the microphone key on the keyboard, and talk. The keyboard's microphone works with no signal too. With no signal the button reads *No signal* and says the same. A microphone that is off for the app says so, with the way to allow it.

### On a job with more than one stage

Many jobs have more than one priced line on the Bill tab. Those lines are often the stages, like **Rough In**, **Top Out** and **Trim Set**. On such a job the percent question becomes a list of the job's stages. The question reads **How far along is each stage?**. Every stage is in view with its own number. Move the stages you worked on. The rest stay as they are. The first stage not yet done is open when the form opens. Tap another stage to open it.

The open stage takes a precise number three ways:

1. Tap a quick pick: {{button:outline|25}} {{button:outline|50}} {{button:outline|75}} or {{button:outline|Done ✓}}.
2. Tap {{button:outline|−5}} or {{button:outline|+5}} to move the number by five.
3. Tap the number itself. The keypad opens. Type the number and press Done on the keypad.

A stage you moved shows the old number and the new one. A stage nobody has reported reads *not started*. A stage whose draw is already paid reads {{chip:green|✓ Paid}} and cannot be moved. A stage set as **In order** on the Bill tab carries its number. Any other line carries a diamond.

The app works out the job's percent from the stages. Each stage counts by its share of the job. The **Job** line under the list shows the job's new percent. Tap **how?** to see each stage's share and points. No prices are shown. The job's percent is what the report saves as *How complete is the job?*. So the Pipeline, the Dashboard cards and the activity feed read the same number as before. The report also lists each stage you moved under **Stage progress**. The office reads it on the report card.

{{button:outline-blue|Set the whole-job % instead}} brings back the plain slider. Use it when you would rather call the whole job. Jobs with one priced line never see the list. They report with one percent for the job. That percent opens on the job's number, not on 0. The line under it says so. Leave it, and the report files the same number. The **Additional report** window on a job's reports list asks the same way.

:::example Halfway through rough-in
Job: Underground ✓ Paid · Rough In 35% · Top Out not started · Trim Set not started · Water heater not started. You tap **Rough In**, then **+5** twice. Rough In reads 35% → 45%. The Job line reads 32% → 36%. You tap the water heater's line and **Done ✓**. The Job line reads 32% → 56%. Save. The office sees Rough In at 45% on the Bill tab and the job at 56% everywhere else. The report card reads *Rough In 35% → 45% · Water heater — → 100% · job 32% → 56%*.
:::

## From a job's window

The office can file a report from the job's window too. Open the job from the Pipeline. In the activity list's filter row, press {{button:outline-blue|＋ Report}}. It sits right after the **Reports** pill. The same report form opens for that job. When you save, the report shows at the top of the feed. The **Reports** pill counts it. The job's percent follows the report.

## Reviewing reports (office side)

Reports live at **Jobs → Reports**, at `/jobs?tab=reports`. The page opens on **Newest**, a feed of the latest reports. Each card shows what kind, which job, who wrote it, and the first lines. You tap a card, or **Read report ›**, to read it in full. The {{chip:blue|By job}} and {{chip:gray|By person}} chips group the same reports. Inside an open job you'll find labeled buttons: {{button:outline|Files}} {{button:outline|Pictures}} {{button:outline|Edit job}} {{button:outline|Preview}}. A button shown dashed means that link isn't set up yet. Search matches job, number, or person from any view. A card or row with photos carries a badge like 📷 2. An open group's row shows the photos under the words.

Your own reports are available under **My Reports**. You can edit a report within the edit window, which is two days by default.

## Getting notified

Don't poll the Reports tab. Subscribe instead. In **Settings → Your dashboard → Report notifications**, you check the types you want and press {{button:blue|Save report notification preferences}}. You'll get a push the moment one is filed. Enable push notifications first. See Settings Basics.

## Special reports

Some reports are filed by dedicated buttons rather than the generic picker. For example, **Turnaway** reports come from {{button:amber|Turnaway — not ready / not home}} on the Job Mode card. A turnaway is a visit where the work could not be done. Those reports also alert dispatch for a trip charge. They still appear in the Reports tab like any other report.
