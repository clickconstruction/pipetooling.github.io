A trade partner owes us three papers before they work for us. They are our master agreement, a W-9 and an insurance certificate. You send for each one from the company's window.

Sending papers shows to a dev while it is built.

## Open their papers

1. Open [GC projects](/gc).
2. Press the company's name. Its window opens.
3. Press the **Documents** tab.

The tab counts the papers still to get. Each paper's row says where it stands. A missing paper has its next step on its row.

## Send a paper

1. Press the button on the paper's row. It reads {{button:blue|Ask for it}}, {{button:blue|Send to sign}} or {{button:blue|Remind them}}.
2. Pick the day under **Sign by** or **Send by**. A week out is picked first.
3. Type a line of your own under **Your line** if you want one.
4. Read the email the company gets. It shows under your line.
5. Press the send button, such as {{button:blue|Send the ask}}.

The line at the top of the tab says who it went to. Follow up chases the day you picked.

:::example The W-9 row once its ask went
**W-9** {{chip:red|none on file}} Asked today. Send by Thu Oct 15.
:::

## The W-9

The W-9 goes out to sign. Its email has a link to fill it in and sign it. The tax number never shows to us.

## The master agreement

Our master services agreement is not written yet. Its row reads **Waiting on the agreement** until the Contract Book has it.

## Their insurance

1. Press {{button:blue|Ask for it}} on the insurance row. The email asks them to reply with their certificate.
2. Save the certificate they send in Drive.
3. Press {{button:outline|Record their insurance}} on the same row.
4. Pick the day it runs out under **Runs out**.
5. Paste its link under **Link to it**.
6. Press {{button:blue|File the certificate}}.

The row then says the day it is good to. Filing it keeps their promise to send it.

## When an email does not go

The send stays on record, so Follow up still chases it. The words at the top say why the email did not go. Press {{button:blue|Remind them}} to try again.

A company that reads Spanish gets no email yet. Our Spanish emails wait for a native speaker to read them.
