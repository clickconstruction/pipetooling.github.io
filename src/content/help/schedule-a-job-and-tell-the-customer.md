When you book a job's first visit, the app asks before it tells the customer. You see the text, change any word, and press Send or Skip. Nothing goes on its own.

## Book the first visit

1. Open the job, or the Dispatch hub.
2. Press {{button:blue|Add job to schedule}}. Pick the day, the window and who is going.
3. Press {{button:blue|Save}}.

The **Tell the customer** sheet opens. It only opens on a job's first visit. Later visits are yours to text from the customer's thread.

## Read the text, then send it

The sheet shows the job and the visit at the top. Under **What goes**, the text is ticked with the number it goes to.

1. Read the text in the orange box. The app fills it from the block: the day, the window and the tech.
2. Change any word you want. A blank in braces holds the send until you fill it.
3. Read **What happens**. It says the text goes from the company number and the answer comes to the office.

Press **Preview** beside the text to see it as it lands on their phone. The sheet stays open under it.
4. Press {{button:green|Send text to Dana}}.

:::example The text as it goes
Dana, this is Rosa with Click Plumbing. We have you booked for Friday, October 23, 2026, 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM. Luis will text you when on the way.
:::

Press **Skip** to send nothing. The visit is still saved.

The words come from the office's Booked line in the quick replies. With no Booked line yet, the app uses its own words. See [edit the quick replies](/help/edit-the-quick-replies).

## Make this the office's line

Changed a word for the better? Press **Make this the office's line** under the box. The words come back with their blanks, like {first} and {day}. Look them over, then press **Save as the office's line**. The step's line is now this for everyone. The send is not changed.

## When the customer asked for email

Some customers ask to hear from us by email. The sheet says so under **What happens**. The text can still go. A call may be kinder. The office sets how a customer hears from us on their thread. See [see who is waiting on us and what we owe them](/help/see-who-is-waiting-on-us-and-what-we-owe-them#in-their-thread).

## When a visit moves

Move a first visit to another day or window, and the Rescheduled text waits on the Dashboard under Today's sends. Press it there, or put it away. See [work Today's sends](/help/work-todays-sends).

## Send it in the morning instead

Booked a visit late in the day? Press **Send in the morning** beside Skip. The text waits, and the app sends it at 9 AM.

1. Read and change the text as usual.
2. Press **Send in the morning**. The sheet says which day it goes.
3. Press **Done**.

Until then the text sits on the Dashboard under Today's sends. You can change it there, or take it back. See [work Today's sends](/help/work-todays-sends).

## The receipt, and the call

After the send, the sheet shows what went and when. Under it, **Call Dana to talk it through?** with the words to say.

Under the words, the sheet says whose script it is. Press **Change the script in Settings** to write the office's own, in English and Spanish.

1. Read **What to say**. It names the day and the window.
2. Press {{button:blue|Call}} to dial. On a desk, the number shows and Quo places the call.
3. Press **Done** when you are finished.

The call lands in the customer's thread when it ends. You do not need to log it.

Not calling right now? Press **Add to my calls**. The call becomes a task for tomorrow in Follow up under We owe them. See [call a customer from a task](/help/call-a-customer-from-a-task).

## When no text can go

The sheet says why under **What happens**, and the button reads **Close**.

- The job has no customer yet. Add one on the job first.
- The customer has no phone number on file. Add one on the customer.
- The customer sent STOP. Call instead.
- Texts are off. The owner or a dev turns them on in Settings → Phone and texts.
