The app has two inboxes. One is for our own customers and one is for the GC side. Type each inbox's address and number here.

The office team can read the inboxes. Only the owner or a dev can change them.

The app starts using these once the two inboxes are switched on. Until then, nothing changes when you save.

## Open the inboxes

1. Go to [Settings](/settings?tab=settings-phone-texts).
2. Press **Phone and texts**.
3. **Inboxes** is under Company phone seats.

## Who each inbox is for

- The **Team inbox** is for customers, leads, subs and candidates.
- The **Bids inbox** is for trade partners, architects and supply houses.

A reply goes from the inbox the person last wrote to.

## Fill in an inbox

1. Type its **Email address**. This is where the team@ or office@ answer goes.
2. Type its **Phone** with all ten digits. The app keeps it the way the company number reads.
3. Type its **Quo number id**. Quo's number ids start with PN.
4. Press {{button:blue|Save the inboxes}}.

A phone and its Quo number id go together. Fill in both or neither.

The two inboxes cannot share an address, a phone or a Quo number id.

## Leave a field empty

An empty field keeps what the app uses today.

- In the Team inbox, an empty field keeps today's company number.
- In the Bids inbox, an empty field keeps today's bids@.

## Change your mind

Press **Put them back** to undo what you have not saved.
