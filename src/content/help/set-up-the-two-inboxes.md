The app has two inboxes. One is for our own customers and one is for the GC side. Give each inbox its address and its number here.

The office team can read the inboxes. Only the owner or a dev can change them.

The app starts using these once the two inboxes are switched on. Until then, nothing changes when you save.

## Open the inboxes

1. Go to [Settings](/settings?tab=settings-phone-texts).
2. Press **Phone and texts**.
3. **Inboxes** is under Company phone seats.

## Who each inbox is for

- The **Team inbox** is for customers, leads, subs and candidates.
- The **Bids inbox** is for trade partners, architects and supply houses.

A reply goes from the inbox the person last wrote to.

## Fill in an inbox

1. Type its **Email address**. This is where the team@ or office@ answer goes.
2. Pick its number under **Quo number**. The pick fills the phone and the Quo number id together.
3. Press {{button:blue|Save the inboxes}}.

The list of numbers comes from Quo. It is there once the [Quo connection](/help/connect-the-app-to-quo) has its key. The number's Quo number id shows small under the pick.

The two inboxes cannot share an address or a number.

## Type the number by hand

Without a key, the card shows a **Phone** box and a **Quo number id** box instead of the list.

1. Type the **Phone** with all ten digits. The app keeps it the way the company number reads.
2. Type the **Quo number id**. Quo's number ids start with PN.

A phone and its Quo number id go together. Fill in both or neither.

The same boxes show when Quo did not answer. The card says so under the inboxes.

With the list up, pick **Type it by hand** for a number Quo does not list. Press **Pick from Quo** to go back to the list.

## Leave a field empty

An empty field keeps what the app uses today.

- In the Team inbox, an empty field keeps today's company number.
- In the Bids inbox, an empty field keeps today's bids@.

## Change your mind

Press **Put them back** to undo what you have not saved.
