After a job, ask the customer how we did. The ask is a text with one link. The link opens a page with five stars. The answer comes to the office first, and a happy customer is asked to put it on Google.

## Send the ask

1. Open the customer's thread in Messages.
2. Pick a quick reply that holds **{rate}**, or put **{rate}** in your own words.
3. Wait for the link to fill in. Then press {{button:blue|Send}}.

:::example The text Dana gets
Dana, thank you for your business. Would you tell us how we did? It takes one tap: clicktooling.com/rate?t=…
:::

A second ask about the same job reuses the same link while it is open. The guide [edit the quick replies](/help/edit-the-quick-replies) says how to put **{rate}** in a line.

## When the app asks for you

The morning after a job's last bill is paid, the app prepares the ask. It waits on the Dashboard under **Today's sends**. A person reads it and presses {{button:blue|Send}}. The link is made when it goes.

:::example The morning's ask
Dana, thank you for paying for the water heater. Would you tell us how we did? It takes one tap: clicktooling.com/rate?t=…
:::

The app never asks a customer who is waiting on an answer from us. Answer them first. The ask comes back the next morning.

To ask any day, open the customer's thread and find the **Review asked** step. Press **Remind now** to put it on the next morning. Press the thread's **Nudge** to text it now.

The words live on the step. Open [the Review asked step](/settings?tab=settings-flows&kind=residential&step=review) in Settings to change them. The same place sets the step to Off, Offers or Sends.

## What the customer sees

The page names the company and the job. The customer taps one to five stars. One tap saves the answer. They can tap again to change it, and add a sentence after.

- At five stars, the page asks them to put it on Google. The button opens our Google page, and their words are copied for the paste.
- Below five stars, the page asks what we could have done better.
- At three stars or fewer, the page says someone from the office will call.

The Google line shows to every customer who answers. Google's rules forbid asking only the happy ones. While the company's Google link is empty, the page never mentions Google. The link's place is below.

## Where the answer lands

The customer also finds the ask on their portal. Their **Your jobs** card links to the review page until they answer.

The answer lands in the customer's thread in Messages as a line from the app. Their words land as a second line.

:::example A good answer in the thread
⭐ Dana left 5 stars on job 1312.
:::

:::example A low answer in the thread
⚠ Dana left 2 stars on job 1312.
:::

A low answer needs a call. Read what they said, and call them the same day.

## Where the office reads it

- **The job window.** The Job tab shows the job's review: the stars, their words, and whether they pressed the Google button.
- **Needs you.** A customer who left three stars or fewer shows on the Dashboard until someone calls. Press it to open their thread.
- **The Customer Hub.** The timeline has a card on the day they answered.
- **What customers see.** The Reviews block counts this month and lists the newest answers.

After the call, open the job and press **I called** on its review line. Write what was said, if you like, and press **Save the call**. The line leaves Needs you.

## Set the Google link

The owner or a dev sets the company's Google review link once. In Google Business Profile, open **Read reviews**, then **Get more reviews**, and copy the link. Paste it in the Reviews block on [What customers see](/settings?tab=settings-what-customers-see) and press **Save the link**.

## See the page yourself

Open [What customers see](/settings?tab=settings-what-customers-see) and find the homeowner strip. It shows the page three ways: before an answer, after five stars, and after two stars. Nothing pressed there is saved.
