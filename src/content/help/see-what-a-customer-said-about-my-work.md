When a customer gives a job four or five stars, every tech who clocked onto that job sees it. It lands on your home the same day.

## Where it shows

A green strip sits at the top of your home. It names the customer by first name and the stars they gave.

:::example The strip
**Dana gave you five stars** ★★★★★

“Marcus explained everything before he started and cleaned up after.”

Water heater at 1208 Mesquite Ln · Thursday {{button:outline|Got it}}
:::

Their words sit under the name, if they wrote any. The job and the day sit under the words.

## Got it

Press {{button:outline|Got it}} once you have read it. The strip then shows the next one, if a second customer wrote too.

The words are not gone. The office keeps every review on the job.

## Who sees it

Everyone with a clock session on the job sees the same strip. The lead and the helper both get the credit.

A review with no job reaches nobody. It stays with the office.

## Low stars

Three stars or fewer never show on your home. The office reads those and talks them through with you in person.

## The Google ask

The customer's five stars are the signal. A customer who gave five stars is asked to post them on Google too. See [ask a customer for a review](/help/ask-a-customer-for-a-review) for how the office sets that up.
