A customer often asks for more work in a text or an email. You can put that message on their estimate as a line.

Anyone in the office can do it. The line goes on at $0. The money team prices it and sends it.

## Put a message on the estimate

1. Open the customer's thread. [Find every message with a person in one place](/help/find-every-message-with-a-person-in-one-place) shows how.
2. Press the message that asks for the work.
3. Press {{button:outline|Line on the estimate}}.
4. Give the line a name in **Line item**. Leave it blank for the money team to name.
5. Check **Description**. It starts with the first sentence of the message.
6. Press {{button:blue|Save the line}}.

Before you save, the window shows the line's number and its estimate. The customer who wrote the message approves it.

## Which estimate it goes on

The line goes on the job's open estimate. That is a draft that already holds a message from this job. When the job has none, the line starts a new estimate for the job.

A sent or signed estimate never takes a new line. Work the customer asks for after that goes on a new estimate.

## When it cannot take a line

The message must be tied to one of the customer's Pipeline jobs. The words under the button say what is missing.

- *Tie the message to a job first.* [Tie a message to a job](/help/find-every-message-with-a-person-in-one-place#tie-a-message-to-a-job) shows how.
- *The job is billed already.* Start the work as a new job.
- *The estimate has options.* Add the line on the estimate itself.
- *The estimate changed since you opened it.* Press the message again and start over.

A message on a GC job starts a change order instead. [Start a change order from a customer's message](/help/start-a-change-order-from-a-customers-message) shows how.

## After you save it

The message shows a {{chip:blue|a line on estimate 1044}} chip in the thread. The money team finds the line on the estimate in [Estimates](/estimates). They price it and send it, as [Send an estimate and turn it into a job](/help/send-an-estimate-and-turn-it-into-a-job) shows.
