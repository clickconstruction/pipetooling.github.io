Your Dashboard has a **Your money** card showing everything the office owes you — no phone call needed.

## The balance

The big number is your total open balance across every sub sheet. It updates the moment the office records a payment.

## Committed work

Work orders you've been offered or that you've accepted show here with their agreed amounts — money that becomes a sheet once the step is done and approved.

- {{chip:yellow|offered}} — the office proposed this amount for a step
- {{chip:green|accepted}} — you're on the hook; do the work

## Your sheets

Each sheet shows what it's worth and what's open. Tap one to see the full history — payments in, backcharges out, dated.

:::example Reading a sheet
"706 · 1875 Co Rd 777 Devine — $4,400.00 open" expands to: Total $6,400 · paid $2,000, with the $2,000 draw dated Jul 28.
:::

If a number looks wrong, contact the office — they see the same sheet in Jobs → Sub Labor.
