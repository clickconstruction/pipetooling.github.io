Your Dashboard has a **Your money** card. The card shows everything the office owes you, with no phone call needed.

## New offers

The office may offer you a work order. The offer appears at the top of the card. The offer shows the step, the project, the amount and any retainage, and the proposed dates. Retainage is a share of the amount the office holds back for a while.

- {{button:green|Accept}} means you're on. If the step had no dates yet, the dates you accepted become its schedule.
- {{button:red|Decline…}} asks for a quick reason, like busy or price, or whatever it is. Then the office knows what to fix.

The office is notified the moment you answer.

## The balance

The big number is your total open balance across every sub sheet. The number updates the moment the office records a payment. A sheet is the office's pay record for one piece of your work.

## Committed work

Work orders you've been offered or have accepted show here, with their agreed amounts. An agreed amount becomes a sheet once the step is done and approved.

- {{chip:yellow|offered}} means the office proposed this amount for a step.
- {{chip:green|accepted}} means you're on the hook. Do the work.

## Your sheets

Each sheet shows what it's worth and what's open. You tap a sheet to see the full history. The history lists payments in and backcharges out, each with its date. A backcharge is a cost charged back to you.

:::example Reading a sheet
"706 · 1875 Co Rd 777 Devine — $4,400.00 open" expands to: Total $6,400 · paid $2,000, with the $2,000 draw dated Jul 28.
:::

If a number looks wrong, you contact the office. The office sees the same sheet in Jobs → Subs → Pay.
