Your Dashboard has a **Your money** card. It shows everything the office owes you, with no phone call needed.

## New offers

The office may offer you a work order. It appears at the top of the card. It shows the step, the project, the amount and any retainage, and the proposed dates. Retainage is money held back until the work is done.

- {{button:green|Accept}} means you're on. If the step had no dates yet, the dates you accepted become its schedule.
- {{button:red|Decline…}} asks for a quick reason, like busy or price, or whatever it is. Then the office knows what to fix.

The office is notified the moment you answer.

## The balance

The big number is your total open balance across every sub sheet. It updates the moment the office records a payment. A sheet is the office's pay record for one piece of your work.

## Committed work

Work orders you've been offered or have accepted show here, with their agreed amounts. That money becomes a sheet once the step is done and approved.

- {{chip:yellow|offered}} means the office proposed this amount for a step.
- {{chip:green|accepted}} means you're on the hook. Do the work.

## Your sheets

Each sheet shows what it's worth and what's open. You tap one to see the full history. It lists payments in and backcharges out, each with its date. A backcharge is a cost charged back to you.

:::example Reading a sheet
"706 · 1875 Co Rd 777 Devine — $4,400.00 open" expands to: Total $6,400 · paid $2,000, with the $2,000 draw dated Jul 28.
:::

If a number looks wrong, you contact the office. They see the same sheet in Jobs → Subs → Pay.
