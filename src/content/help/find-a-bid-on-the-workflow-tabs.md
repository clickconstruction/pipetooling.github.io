The one sentence: **when no bid is selected on a workflow tab, the bid list is sorted — bid number first by default — and the buttons next to the search bar switch the order.**

## Pick the order

1. Open any bid workflow page — **Counts, Takeoffs, Labor, Pricing, Cover Letter, RFI, Change Order,** or **Lien Release** — without a bid selected.
2. Next to the search bar, pick a view:
   - {{button:blue|Bid # ↓}} — highest bid number first. The default, best for "I know it's B3-something".
   - {{button:outline|Due date}} — soonest due first; bids with no due date drop to the bottom.
   - {{button:outline|Sent}} — most recently sent first; unsent bids last.
   - {{button:outline|Value}} — largest bid value first; unpriced bids last.
3. Your choice sticks: every workflow tab uses it, and it's still set next time you sign in on the same device.

:::example Looking for last week's sends?
Tap **Sent** — the bids you sent most recently rise to the top, and everything still waiting to go out sits together at the bottom.
:::

## Narrow it down

- Type in the search bar to filter by **bid #, project name, or GC/Builder** — the matches keep the order you picked.
- On Counts, Takeoffs, Labor, Pricing, and Cover Letter, {{button:outline|Only my bids}} keeps just the bids where you're the estimator or account manager.
