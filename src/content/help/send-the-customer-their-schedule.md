When a customer asks where their job stands, send them their schedule as a dated letter. The letter says the finish, each stage, the dates to meet and what changed. The letter never names a trade or a dollar.

Only devs can open the schedule for now. The rest of the office gets the Schedule window later.

## Read the letter

1. Open [GC projects](/gc).
2. Press {{button:outline|Schedule}} on the card of a job being built.
3. Find the letter's card under **Milestones**. The card's title names the customer.
4. Press {{button:outline|Read the letter}}. The card shows the letter as it would go today, and who gets it.

The letter also says what we need from them, like a change order to sign.

## Send a test to yourself first

Press {{button:outline|Send a test to me}}. The same email goes to your own address only. The test's subject starts with TEST.

The card keeps the letter as not emailed yet. When you send it for real, that same letter goes.

## Send it

Press the blue button that names their contact, like {{button:blue|Send to Elena}}. The letter goes to the contact's email on the customer. A reply goes to the job's project manager.

Each send shows under the card with its day and who sent it. A letter that did not go says not emailed yet. Press the button again to send it.

If the card says there is no email address on file, add the contact's email on the customer. Then send it again.

## Print it or save a PDF

Press {{button:outline|Print the letter}}. The letter opens as a page to print or save as a PDF. A copy of what you printed is kept in Documents, under the customer.
