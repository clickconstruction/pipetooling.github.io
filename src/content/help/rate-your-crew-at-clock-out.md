Every couple of weeks, right after you clock out, the app deals you a short deck: one card for each person you worked with this cycle, then one for your lead, then a card for anything you want to say. It takes about two minutes, and only the office ever sees it.

## The cards

Each card is one person: their name, how many days you worked together, and which jobs. Under that are three sliders.

- **Ability** — talent and problem-solving.
- **Drive** — work ethic and motivation.
- **Integrity** — trustworthiness and goodness of heart.

Slide each one from 0 to 100, or leave it unrated. A small box under every slider takes a note if you want to say why. Then {{button:blue|Save … next}} moves to the next person. {{button:outline|Skip}} moves on without saving anything about them.

:::example Rating your lead
Your lead gets the same card as everyone else. Rate them the same way. The office sees an average of what the crew said, never who said it.
:::

## Your words

The last card has four boxes: something we should fix or improve, safety or tools, training you want, and anything at all. Fill any of them or none, then {{button:green|Send}}.

## Who sees what

- Your ratings are **anonymous**. Nobody but the developer can see which rating came from you, and the person you rated never can.
- The office sees each person's crew average only after at least two people have rated them.
- Your notes and open words are read by the office; they carry your name only for the developer.

## If now is a bad time

**Not now** on the first screen snoozes the deck for a week. Closing it skips this cycle. If you do nothing for 30 seconds it closes on its own. Some Dashboards also have a **Rate your crew** button so you can open the deck whenever you like.
