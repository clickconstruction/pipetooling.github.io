Tapping a task on the Roadmap — a row in **Plan** or a task inside a Map cluster — opens its **task card**. On a phone it slides up as a bottom sheet; on a computer it's a centered card. Everything on it saves as you go: there is no Save button.

## Put names on the task

1. Under **Assigned**, tap {{chip:gray|＋ Assign someone}}.
2. Tap names — each tap assigns (or unassigns) **immediately**. People already working this roadmap are listed first under *On this roadmap*; search finds everyone else.
3. Tap {{button:outline|Done}} to fold the picker away.

To take someone off later, tap the **✕** on their chip. When the stage unlocks, assigned tasks land on each person's **Today** list automatically.

## Talk about the work

The **Activity** section is the task's conversation — notes as message bubbles, status changes (completed, reopened, signed off) as quiet one-liners. Type in the bar at the bottom and hit the send button; your note follows the task onto the assignee's checklist card, so the field sees it too.

:::example What the office sees vs. the field
A note posted from the roadmap card ("flag the wet section, we'll culvert later") shows up on the same task in the assignee's **Checklist → Today** — one conversation, two doors.
:::

## Rename a task

Tap the **✎** next to the title, type, and press **Enter** (Esc cancels). Editors only.

The card's stage crumb wears the stage's **number badge**, so "the gate task on stage 4" is easy to find from either view.
