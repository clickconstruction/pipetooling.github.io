My Time on your dashboard is one picture of your time. The picture shows how much you have added up and which days it came from.

## Read this week

Open your dashboard and find **My Time**. In Job Mode the same picture is a card under Your record.

- The big figure is your time this week. **Today** sits beside it.
- Under the figure are seven columns, Sunday to Saturday. Each one shows that day's hours and minutes.
- Today has a box around it. A day still to come is faint and empty.
- While you are clocked in, a line reads *On the clock since 8:00 AM*, with your own start time.

A pay week runs Sunday to Saturday.

## Look at an earlier week

The lower row is your last eight weeks. Each column shows that week's time, with its Sunday under it.

Press a week and the seven columns change to that week. The arrows beside the week's name step one week at a time. Press {{button:outline|Back to this week}} to return.

## When a day is marked

A day with an amber mark is a day nobody clocked out of. The clock stopped itself at midnight, so the time on it is too long.

- If you clock in and out yourself, press the day and fix its end time.
- If you are salaried, ask the office to fix it.

## Watch-outs

- The card counts time. The card does not compare your time with a number of hours.
- Press a day of this week to see its sessions. A day the office has marked correct does not open.
- Only you and the office see your time. Nobody sees a wage here.
