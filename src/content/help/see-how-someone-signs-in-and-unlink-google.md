A person can sign in with a password, with an emailed link, or with Google. The Ways in row on a person's desk shows which of these their login has. You can take Google off a login from the same row.

## Read the row

1. Open the person's desk. Go to **Access & account**.
2. Find the **Ways in** row.

- **Password** reads {{chip:green|set}} or {{chip:yellow|not set}}. A person with no password signs in with an emailed link or with Google.
- **Google** reads {{chip:green|linked}} or **not linked**. A linked account shows its email address and when it was last used.
- {{chip:yellow|address differs}} means the Google account is on another address than the one the roster has. Check that it is the right person.

## Take Google off a login

1. Press {{button:outline|Unlink Google}}.
2. Read the question and press **Unlink Google** again.

The person's password still works. The emailed sign-in link still works. Nothing of the person's changes. The person is signed out everywhere and signs in again.

:::example When Google is the only way in
The desk refuses and says **Google is their only way in. Set a password first.** Press **Set password…** on the Sign-in row, then unlink.
:::

## When you change someone's email

Changing a person's sign-in email takes their Google link off too. The old Google account stops opening the login. The person can link Google again by signing in with a Google account on the new address.
