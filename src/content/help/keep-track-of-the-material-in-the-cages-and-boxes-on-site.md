On a big job the material lives in stencilled cages and boxes left on the site. The On site tab on the Tally keeps what is in each one. The office then knows what is there, what was used and what walked away.

## Open the tab

Open **Tally** and tap **On site**. Pick the job at the top. On your Dashboard, the Job Mode card has **Materials on site**. Tap it and the tab opens on the job you are clocked into.

The row under the job reads your day left to right: {{chip:blue|Unsorted 2}} → {{chip:gray|Used 6}} → {{chip:green|Count 25}}. A dot marks the one that needs you now.

## Put away a delivery

A delivery the office lands waits in **Unsorted**. Tap it, then tap {{button:outline-blue|Put in…}} on each line and pick the cage or box it goes in. The parts move and Unsorted empties.

## Use a part, move it or say you can't find it

Tap a box to open it. Each line has three presses.

- {{button:outline|Used}} takes the number you used out of the box.
- {{button:outline|Move to…}} puts some in another box, back to the supply house, to the truck or marks them damaged.
- {{button:outline-amber|Can't find}} says the box should have it and it is not there. The office sees it that day.

Every press is one row in the ledger. A wrong press gets a new press. Nothing is edited.

## Count a box

A box is due a count after two weeks. Tap {{button:blue|Count this box}} and walk the lines. Tap **Right** when the number matches, type the number when it does not, or **Skip** a line.

The count becomes the truth for that box. A short count is what the office reads as missing.

## Add a container or a part

Tap {{button:outline|Add a container}} to add a cage or box with its stencil and its place on the site. On a box, tap **Add a part** for a part that came without a delivery.

## Take a photo

On a box, tap {{button:outline|Photo}}. For ten minutes after a count or a put away it reads **Photo after Count**. Shoot and tap Use Photo. The phone shrinks the picture and files it in the job's Drive folder by itself.

With no signal the photo stays on the phone. Tap **Send now** when you have signal again, or **Share instead** to send it another way.

## Find a box on the site

Tap **Map** to see the pinned boxes on the site. On a box, tap **Mark on the map** and stand where it is. Tap **Move this box** when it moves to another place.

## Scan a label

A printed label carries a code. Point your camera at it and the box opens. The office prints labels from Jobs → Materials.
