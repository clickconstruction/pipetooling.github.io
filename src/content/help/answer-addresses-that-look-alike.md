You tell the app which addresses on file are one place written two ways. Each pair takes one answer.

Addresses stay as they were typed. So one place can be on file under two spellings. The app finds the pairs that look alike and asks you about each.

## Open the list

1. Open **Jobs → Pipeline**.
2. Press the tools button, the three dots at the right of the toolbar.
3. Press **Addresses that look alike…**, under **Combine / Separate…**.

A count on the tools button says how many pairs wait. It goes away when none do.

## Answer a pair

The pairs come in three groups. Each tab shows how many wait.

- **Written alike, same spot on the map.** A typo, a unit or a missing space. These are the likeliest.
- **Written alike, the map says apart.** Worth a look. The map may have one wrong.
- **Different words, same spot.** Neighbours, or one place written two ways.

Each row shows both addresses and what is at each one. The words that differ are marked.

- {{button:blue|Same place}} records that the two are one place.
- {{button:gray|Not the same}} records that they are not. The pair is not asked again.

:::example A typo
One row reads "115 E Sample St" beside "115 E Sampel St", both at the same spot. Press **Same place**. The row moves down and reads who answered and when.
:::

An answer changes no job's address. It only records the match.

## Take an answer back

An answered row shows {{button:gray|Undo}}. Press it and the pair waits for an answer again.

## Addresses with no street number

The last tab is **No street number**. Words alone cannot match these addresses, so they are listed for you to see.

To write a new job's address the way it is on file, see [use an address the company already has](/help/use-an-address-already-on-file).
