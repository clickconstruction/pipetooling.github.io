A contract we send a trade partner is made of clauses. Anyone in the office can change one. A controller or a dev approves the change before it goes out.

## Open the clauses

1. Open [Contracts and clauses](/settings?tab=settings-clauses) in Settings.
2. Find the paper's card. It shows the paper's version and its clauses.

The clauses for everyone come first, under **Everyone**. A clause for one trade sits on that trade's exhibit. Each clause shows who it applies to and its own version.

## Change a clause

1. Press {{button:outline|Edit}} on the clause.
2. Change the words under **English**.
3. Write them under **Spanish** too, when you can.
4. Under **Applies to**, pick **Everyone** or the trades it covers.
5. Say why under **Why**.
6. Press {{button:blue|Send for approval}}.

The change waits under the clause until a controller or a dev approves it. It shows on their Needs you card.

A clause for a trade covers only the trades you pick. A clause for Electrical is not on the low voltage exhibit unless you pick both.

:::example A change waiting on approval
**Rosa proposed a change** Oct 12, 10:00 AM. The insurer asked for it.
Insurance: general liability of $2,000,000 per occurrence.
{{chip:yellow|Waiting on your approval}}
:::

## Add or retire a clause

To add a clause, press {{button:outline|+ a clause}} on the paper's card. It goes after the last clause.

To take a clause off the next paper, press {{button:outline|Retire}} on it. A retired clause is never deleted. Signed copies keep it.

## Approve or decline a change

This part is for a controller or a dev.

1. Press {{button:outline|Review}} on the **Contracts** line of your Needs you card. It opens the paper's card.
2. Read the change under the clause.
3. Press {{button:blue|Approve}} to send it out. The paper's version goes up by one.
4. To say no, press {{button:outline|Decline}} and say why. The person who made it reads your why on the clause's history.

Approve is refused when the clause changed after the change was made. Decline it and ask for it again.

Your own changes go out as soon as you save them. They show on the clause's history too. See [work the Needs you list on the dashboard](/help/needs-you-card) for the card itself.

## See a paper as a company gets it

1. Press {{button:outline|Preview per trade}} on the paper's card.
2. Pick the company under **As it would go to**.
3. Pick **English** or **Spanish**.

The preview shows the clauses for everyone. Then it shows the exhibit for that company's trades, then the version.

## What a signed copy keeps

A signed copy keeps the words and the version it was signed with. A later change never reaches it. Nor does a retire. The next paper sent carries the new words.

## Build a paper from clauses

1. Under **Build a paper from clauses**, pick a document from the Contract library.
2. Press {{button:blue|Write its first clause}}.

Its first clause goes through approval like any other. Fillable forms such as the W-9 are not offered.
