Supply houses setting up a **job account** ask for the same packet every time. The share button packages it all into one email that goes out **from your own inbox**.

A job account is an account the house opens for one property. The packet holds the property's name, address, and phone numbers. The packet also holds the owner's info. The info names the homeowner, or for commercial work the building owner **and their company name**. The email can go from ClickTooling instead, if you prefer.

## Where it is

Open any job's **Job Detail** and look at the icon row in the header. The small **storefront icon** sits next to the share button. The icon's name is "Share with supply house". Office roles only.

## Filling the packet

The icon opens a window with three sections:

- **Property**: name, address, and site phone from the job.
- **General contractor**: filled automatically when the job has a GC. The GC is *not* the owner. The GC is shown so the supply house knows who's buying.
- **Property owner**: what the supply house actually needs. The section takes the homeowner, or the building owner's **company**. The section also takes the owner's **mailing address**, because that's what lien notices run on. A lien is a legal claim on a property for an unpaid bill. On a homeowner job with no GC this fills in from the customer. On a GC job it starts **blank**. Get the owner from the GC and type it in. {{button:blue|Email from my inbox}} stays off until the owner is entered. The footer names exactly what's missing.

Owner info is **remembered for the job**. Enter it once, and every resend fills it in, even from another desk.

Don't know the owner? Hit ***Send to Dispatch — find the owner***. The errand lands in the Dispatch inbox. The errand comes with a one-click **Open Share with supply house** button that brings them right back to this screen. Once they fill the owner and send, the dispatch request closes itself. The window shows {{chip:blue|Dispatch is on it}} while the request is open, so nobody files it twice.

**Optional but worth doing**: tap the supply house chips under **Send to** *before* hitting the dispatch button. Your pick rides along with the errand. The inbox request names the supply house, as in *"…send the job account to Ferguson — Central desk"*. When Dispatch opens this screen to finish, those chips are already selected. Skip it and the flow works exactly as before. Dispatch just chooses the supply house themselves.

## Sending

Pick who gets it from your **supply house contacts**. Tap the chips to select one or more. {{button:outline|+ Add contact}} takes a label and an email, like *"Ferguson — Central desk"*, and remembers the contact for next time. The list is shared with the whole office.

Three ways to get the packet out:

- {{button:blue|Email from my inbox}} is the main path. The button opens a ready-to-send draft in your own email app. The draft is addressed to the contacts you picked, with the subject and packet filled in. You press send there. So the email comes **from your address**, and replies land straight in your inbox.
- {{button:outline|Copy for email}} copies the addressed packet: the To line, the subject and the body. You can paste it into any compose window. Handy when you write email in the browser.
- **Other → Send from app** is the original path. ClickTooling delivers *"Job account setup — 964 · Pondhill demo"* with the packet as a clean table. Replies still go to your email.

The app can't see your inbox. So the first two paths finish with one honest tap. After the draft opens, or the packet copies, the window waits for you. Send the email, then hit {{button:blue|Sent — log it}} so the share is remembered. {{button:outline|Didn't send it}} backs out without recording anything.

## Keeping track

Every send is remembered. If a job was shared before, the window shows a blue ***Already shared with … · date*** pill. Click it to see every previous send, with the exact time, contact, and who sent it. Sends from someone’s own inbox are tagged "from their inbox". **Materials → Supply Houses** has a collapsible **Job accounts** section. The section lists every job with an account, the contacts it went to, and the last send. The section has a search box too. Click a job there to open its Job Detail.

The packet is one way to get an account opened. A phone call to the house's job-accounts rep, the person there who opens them, is the other. Either way, once the house says yes, record the account with **Mark opened**. The button is on the house's roster on **Materials → Supply houses**. The same button is on the tech's ask in the Dispatch inbox. Then the job's **Job accounts** line reads ✓ at the counter. See [open a job account before buying parts](?g=open-a-job-account-before-buying-parts).

Once the account is open and invoices start arriving, tick **On Ferguson's job account** as you enter them. If such a bill goes unpaid, the house bills the property owner, not you. The tick keeps your payables picture honest about that. Payables are the bills you owe. See [mark an invoice as on a job account](?g=mark-an-invoice-on-a-job-account).
