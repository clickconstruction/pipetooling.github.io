On **Bids → Bid Board**, your name gets a highlight box wherever you're a bid's **Estimator or Account Man** — so your bids jump out while you scan. You pick what that box looks like.

## Open the wheel

At the right end of the board's jump row — the same line as {{chip:gray|Health}} — there's a small **color wheel**. Click it to open **"Your name on the board."**

## What you can set

- **Box color** — eight quick swatches, or **Custom…** for any color.
- **Text color** — **Auto** picks black or white so the name stays readable on your box color; or force White, Black, or a custom color.
- **☀ Light mode and 🌙 Dark mode separately** — the app switches themes with the time of day, and a box that pops at noon can vanish at night. The preview chip shows your name exactly as each theme will render it.

Every click saves immediately — no Save button — and your colors follow your account to any device. **Reset this mode to default** puts one theme back to the standard look.

:::example Why dark mode gets its own pick
The board's old highlight was a near-black box — fine by day, invisible on the evening theme. Now the default flips with the theme, and your own picks can differ per theme too.
:::

Your choice only changes how **you** see your own name; everyone else keeps their own colors.
