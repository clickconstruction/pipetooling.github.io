On the Bid Board, your name gets a highlight box wherever you are a bid's Estimator or Account Man. You pick what that box looks like, so your bids jump out while you scan.

Open **Bids → Bid Board** to see it. The box marks every bid where you are the **Estimator or Account Man**.

## Open the wheel

Look at the right end of the board's jump row. That is the same line as {{chip:gray|Health}}. There is a small **color wheel** there. Click it to open **"Your name on the board."**

## What you can set

- **Box color**: eight quick swatches, or **Custom…** for any color.
- **Text color**: **Auto** picks black or white so the name stays readable on your box color. Or force White, Black, or a custom color.
- **☀ Light mode and 🌙 Dark mode separately**: the app switches themes with the time of day. A box that pops at noon can vanish at night. The preview chip shows your name exactly as each theme will render it.

Every click saves immediately. There is no Save button. Your colors follow your account to any device. **Reset this mode to default** puts one theme back to the standard look.

:::example Why dark mode gets its own pick
The board's old highlight was a near-black box — fine by day, invisible on the evening theme. Now the default flips with the theme, and your own picks can differ per theme too.
:::

Your choice only changes how **you** see your own name. Everyone else keeps their own colors.
