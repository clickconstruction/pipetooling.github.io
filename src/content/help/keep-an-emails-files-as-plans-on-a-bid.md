An addendum or a revised spec often comes by email. One press files the email's files with the bid's plans in Drive. The same press adds them to the bid as a set of plans.

The office team and the estimators have the press.

## Before you start

The email must be on the bid. The guide [save an email to a job from your own mail](/help/save-an-email-to-a-job-from-your-own-mail) shows one way to put it there.

The email must carry a file. An email with no file has no press. That email is already on the bid's history.

A logo in the sender's signature does not count as a file. Nor does the small picture Gmail adds for a Google Drive link. An email that carries only those has no press. Beside a real file each one is listed with its tick off.

## Keep the files

1. Open Messages and open the person's thread.
2. Press the email to open it.
3. Press {{button:outline|Keep its files on the bid…}}. The press sits beside {{button:outline|Open the bid}}.
4. Look at the ticks beside the files. Untick a file you do not want.
5. Read the name, the date and the memo. Change what is wrong.
6. Press {{button:blue|Keep 2 files as a set on B482}}. The button counts the files it will file.

The form fills itself in from the email. The name comes from the subject. The date is the day the email came. The memo says who sent it.

The files go into the bid's Drive folder, under Plans. A bid with no Drive folder gets one made.

A green line says what landed. Press **Open the folder** on that line to see the files in Drive.

## Add to a set that is already there

A later email may bring one more sheet of the same addendum.

1. Press {{button:outline|Keep its files on the bid…}} on the later email.
2. Pick the set under Put them in.
3. Press {{button:blue|Add 1 file to Addendum 2}}.

The file joins that set's folder. No second set is made.

The list shows a set only when its link is a Drive folder. A set whose link is one file cannot take more files.

A reply in the same conversation carries the same subject. The form picks the first email's set for you.

## When a file stays behind

A big file may have stayed in Gmail. The form names that file and files the rest.

A file may fail to land. The line after the press names it with the reason.

1. Read the reason on the line.
2. Press {{button:outline|File 1 file again}}.

The second try goes into the same set.

## Know what is kept

An email whose files are kept shows {{chip:green|Kept as Addendum 2 on B482}} in place of the press. Press **Open the set** beside the chip to open its folder.

On the bid, open Plans and look under Plan sets. A set that came this way shows {{chip:blue|From a mail}}. Press **The mail** on that row to open the email in its thread.

Remove the set on the bid and the email offers the press again.

## From the bid

You can start from the bid when you do not know whose thread holds the email.

1. Open the bid and open Plans.
2. Under Plan sets, press {{button:outline|+ From a mail on this bid}}.
3. Find the email in the list. The list shows only email that carries a file.
4. Press {{button:outline|Keep its files on the bid…}} on that row.

An email whose files are kept shows its green chip in the list.

## From the Development page

A bid in a development shows its email on the site's History.

1. Open the development and look at History.
2. Find the email's line. A line with files to keep shows **Keep its 2 files…**.
3. Press it. The line opens in a window with the press under the words.

The line says *kept on the bid* once the files are in a set. The new set draws a line of its own, under {{chip:blue|Plans}}.

## From My email

My email shows your own Inbox and Sent. An email there that is on a bid shows the same press over its words.

## What the press leaves alone

The press does not change which set the robot reads. Press {{button:outline|Robot reads this set}} under Plan sets to change that.

The press does not change the bid's price or its Priced on set.
