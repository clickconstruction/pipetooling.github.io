Every city's permit site is listed under Permit Portals on Jobs → Inspections. The shared sign-in is stored right on the card.

There is no more hunting through notes for the password.

## Finding the right portal

You type the city into the search box. The matching portal card surfaces. One portal can serve many cities. The cities appear as tags on the card. So searching the city always beats remembering which site covers it.

:::example Finding the Buda portal
Type `buda` in the search box. The card whose city tags include **Buda** is the only one left, with the matching tag highlighted.
:::

On the card:

- {{button:blue|Open portal ↗}} opens the permit site in your browser.
- {{button:outline|Copy user}} and {{button:outline|Copy password}} put the sign-in on your clipboard.
- {{button:outline|Show}} reveals the password if you would rather read it.

The fastest phone flow is this. Copy the password, then open the portal and paste.

## From a scheduled inspection

Rows in **Upcoming** and in the day popup on the calendar show the same buttons. They show when the inspection's address contains a city one of the portals serves. So from any scheduled inspection you can jump straight into the right portal to check or edit it.

## Managing portals

{{button:outline|Edit Portals}} manages the list. Each portal has a label, a URL, the cities it serves, the shared sign-in and a free-text note. The cities are comma-separated. They power both the search and the inspection matching.

Sign-in details are visible to everyone who can open the Inspections tab.
