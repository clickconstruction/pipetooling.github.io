A stage checklist is a short list of questions for a stage of a job. There is one for Rough In, one for Top Out and one for Trim Set. The office writes the questions. You answer them on the phone while you are on the stage.

## Open the checklist

In Job Mode, scroll to the job's details. Under **Stage checklists**, press the stage you are working. The same list sits under the address on the job window. A stage with no questions is not listed. The checklists start the day the office types the questions.

## Answer each question

The sheet keeps one question open at a time. The open one is marked **Next**. A line under the title counts the answers, like *2 of 5 answered · 3 left*.

Each open question has three presses. Press {{chip:green|Yes}}, {{chip:red|No}} or {{chip:gray|Not here}}. Not here means the thing is not on this job. Every press saves by itself. The sheet moves to the next question on its own. You can close the sheet and come back.

Every answer stamps its row with your name and the time. The office reads the stamp on the job's Documents tab.

Press **Add a note** under the open question when there is something to say. The note saves when you leave the box.

To change an answer, tap its row. The row opens again with its three presses. Press **Close this one** when you are done.

## Questions that need a signature

Some questions wear a chip that reads *signature*. Yes, No or Not here opens a sheet where you sign your answer. The question is not answered until you sign. Not here needs a signature too. A signed row locks. The guide [sign a checklist answer](/help/sign-a-checklist-answer) has the steps, and how an answer is taken back.

## Checks added for this job

Some questions sit under **Added for this job**. The office, the superintendent or the estimator added them for this job alone. The row says who added it, when and why. Answer them like any other question. They count toward ready the same way.

A check can name a plan sheet. The chip on its row reads like *📐 P-201 detail 4 ↗*. Press the chip to open the job's Plans folder.

## Questions skipped on this job

A company question may not apply on your job. The office, the superintendent or the estimator can skip it with a reason. The question then sits under **Skipped on this job** with who skipped it, when and why. A skipped question needs no answer and does not hold the stage.

## Send a check you cannot answer to Dispatch

Sometimes you cannot answer a check. The part is missing. The check is wrong for this job. Press **Send to Dispatch** under the open question. A sheet opens on top with the job, the stage and the check already filled in. Pick why and send. The row then reads *With Dispatch* until Dispatch answers. Dispatch's reply sits under the row. The request closes itself once you answer the check. The guide [send a check to Dispatch](/help/send-a-check-to-dispatch) has the steps.

## Take the photo a question asks for

Some questions ask for a photo. Press {{button:outline|Take the photo}} under the question. The camera opens. The photo is shrunk on the phone and sent to the customer's Google Drive folder. The sheet says when it is in the folder. Not here needs no photo.

If the phone has no signal, the photo is not taken. Press again when there is one.

A photo from a field report can answer the question too. Under a new photo on the report, tap the chip with the question's words. The sheet then shows that photo with *Taken on a field report.* The guide [take photos for a report](/help/take-photos-for-a-report#answer-a-checklist-question-with-the-photo) has the steps.

## When the stage reads ready

The bottom of the sheet counts what is left. A stage reads **not ready** on the Pipeline until every question is answered. A question that asks for a photo needs its photo too. Once every question is answered, press {{button:green|Done}}.

The answers never change the stage's percent or its bill. A test report does not count as a checklist.
