A stage checklist is a short list of questions for a stage of a job. There is one for Rough In, one for Top Out and one for Trim Set. The office writes the questions. You answer them on the phone while you are on the stage.

## Open the checklist

In Job Mode, scroll to the job's details. Under **Stage checklists**, press the stage you are working. The same list sits under the address on the job window. A stage with no questions is not listed. The checklists start the day the office types the questions.

## Answer each question

Each question has three presses. Press {{chip:green|Yes}}, {{chip:red|No}} or {{chip:gray|Not here}}. Not here means the thing is not on this job. Every press saves by itself. You can close the sheet and come back.

Type a note under a question when there is something to say. The note saves when you leave the box.

## Take the photo a question asks for

Some questions ask for a photo. Press {{button:outline|Take the photo}} under the question. The camera opens. The photo is shrunk on the phone and sent to the customer's Google Drive folder. The sheet says when it is in the folder. Not here needs no photo.

If the phone has no signal, the photo is not taken. Press again when there is one.

## When the stage reads ready

The bottom of the sheet counts the answers. A stage reads **not ready** on the Pipeline until every question is answered. A question that asks for a photo needs its photo too. Once every question is answered, press {{button:green|Done}}.

The answers never change the stage's percent or its bill. A test report does not count as a checklist.
