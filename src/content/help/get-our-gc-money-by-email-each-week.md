Get an email about our GC jobs on the days you pick. The email says who owes us, what is late and what went last week.

The email is for the owner, the leaders and the controller. You can set it up for yourself or for a teammate.

## Set it up

1. Press {{button:outline|GC}} on the Bids page to open [GC projects](/gc).
2. Press {{button:outline|Money}}.
3. Find **The Monday money email** below the money.
4. Pick the person in **Who gets it**.
5. Press each day the email should go. Monday starts picked.
6. Set the time in **At, Central time**. The time starts at 7:00 AM.
7. Press {{button:blue|Save}}.

The email goes at that time on each day you picked, every week.

## What the email says

- The first line says what customers owe us on our GC jobs, and how much of that is late.
- **Late** lists each bill past its day. The day is the customer's promise, or the day we expected.
- **Waiting on the architect** lists the bills the architect has not certified yet.
- **Coming in** lists the rest, each with its day.
- **Last week** says which pay applications went out and which ones the architect certified.
- {{button:blue|Open Money}} opens Money in GC projects.

The days match Who owes us on Money. See [the money on our GC jobs](/help/see-the-money-on-our-gc-jobs).

## Look at the email first

Press {{button:outline|See the email as it would go now}} to read the email here. Press {{button:outline|Email me a test}} to get a copy marked TEST. Only you get the test.

## Change the email or stop it

Pick the person in **Who gets it**. The days and time the person gets the email on now show. Change them and press {{button:blue|Save}}.

To stop the email, press {{button:outline|Stop it}} on the person's line. The person who set the email up can stop it. So can the person who gets it.
