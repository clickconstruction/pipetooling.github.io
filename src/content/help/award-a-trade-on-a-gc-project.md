Once we win a GC job, each trade goes to one company. You award the trade to that company's quote. The app then drafts the trade's statement of work from the quote.

Award shows to a dev while it is built.

## Award a trade

1. Open [GC projects](/gc).
2. Find the project's card. The project must be won.
3. Press {{button:blue|Compare quotes (2)}} under the trade.
4. Under the quote you pick, choose who decided in the **Estimator** box.
5. Press {{button:blue|Award and draft the statement of work}}.

The quote then reads {{chip:green|Awarded}}. A trade is awarded once.

While we still bid, Compare quotes offers **Carry this number** instead. See [compare quotes and carry one](/help/compare-quotes-and-carry-one).

## When Award is off

The app checks the company before it awards. The words under the button say what stops it.

- A company new to us is approved first. See [approve a company new to us](/help/approve-a-company-new-to-us).
- A company we declined is not awarded.
- A company approved up to an amount cannot take a bigger award.

The app measures the award by the quote's **All in** number.

## Read the statement of work

The statement of work shows under the trade on the project's card.

- The chip says where it stands. It reads {{chip:gray|Statement of work drafted}} first.
- **Price** is the quote's All in number.
- **Retainage** is what we hold back from each draw.
- **Based on** names the set of plans it follows.
- Our schedule of values splits the price over the trade's scope lines.
- Theirs shows the lines they sent with their quote.
- **What they will not do** lists the exclusions in their quote.

## Send it to sign

Press {{button:blue|Send to their portal to sign}}. The chip then reads {{chip:yellow|Statement of work waiting on their signature}}.

The company signs it in their portal. The portal's sign screen comes in a later step.
