A stage checklist is a short list of questions the crew answers on the phone for one stage. The company's questions are written once on Settings. You can add a check for one job alone. The crew sees it on that job's sheet the next time the sheet opens.

## Who adds a check

The office adds a check on any job. A superintendent adds one on their own jobs. An estimator adds one on the jobs they priced. The crew answers and leaves notes. The crew does not add.

## Add a check from the Pipeline

The Pipeline row has a checklist icon under **Edit**. Press it and the job's checklists open beside the row. Each stage shows its rows with the same presses as the sheet. The guide [see every checklist on the Pipeline](/help/see-every-checklist-on-the-pipeline) walks through it.

## Add a check from the sheet

A superintendent or an estimator adds from the sheet. The office can too, once the stage has a question.

1. Open the job. Under the address, press the stage under **Stage checklists**. On your own jobs you see every stage that takes a checklist, even one with no questions yet.
2. Scroll to **Added for this job**. Press {{button:outline|+ Add a check for Trim Set}}.
3. Type the check as a question. Write it so Yes, No or Not here answers it.
4. Type the plan sheet it comes from, if it has one. Type why the check is here, if you want to say.
5. Tick **Asks for a photo** when the crew must take a photo with the answer.
6. Tick **Needs a signature** when the crew must sign the answer. Use it where a wrong answer costs a wall or an inspection.
7. Press {{button:blue|Add the check}}.

The sheet counts it at once. The toast says the new count, like *Added. Trim Set is now 3 of 6.*

The box stays open with its boxes empty, so you can type the next check. Press {{button:outline|Done adding}} when the list is in.

:::example Checks a superintendent might add
1. Are the carriers in units 7 and 8 set?
2. Is the water heater pan drain run to the hub?
:::

## Add a check from the Documents tab

The office adds from here on any stage, even one with no questions yet. A superintendent and an estimator have no Documents tab.

Open the job window and press **Documents**. Scroll to **Stage checklists**. Each stage reads with its standing, like *Not ready · 3 of 6*. Press {{button:outline|+ Add a check for Trim Set}} under the stage. The same box opens.

## Open the plan sheet a check names

A check that names a plan sheet wears a chip on its row, like *📐 P-201 detail 4 ↗*. Press the chip to open the job's Plans folder. The folder opens, and the chip's words say which sheet to look for. A job with no Plans link shows the words alone.

## Put the checks in order

The checks under **Added for this job** go in the order you put them. A new check goes last.

On the Documents tab, press {{button:outline|▲}} or {{button:outline|▼}} on a check. On the sheet, tap the check to open it, then press the same arrows. The company's questions keep the order set on Settings.

## Skip a company question on this job

Every job is a little different, so a company question may not apply on this one. Anyone who can add a check on the job can skip it.

1. Open the question on the sheet, or find it on the Documents tab.
2. Press **Skip on this job**.
3. Type why it is not on this job.
4. Press {{button:outline|Skip on this job}}. Press **Keep it** to change your mind.

The question leaves this stage's list and its count, on the Pipeline too. The line named **Skipped on this job** now shows it, with who skipped it, when and why. The company's list stays whole, and every other job still asks it.

To undo a skip, press **Put it back** on that line. An answer the question already had reads again.

## Add a check to the company's list

The office can make one job's check a question for every job.

1. Find the check on the Documents tab, under **Added for this job**.
2. Press **Add to the company's list**.
3. Read what it will do. Press {{button:outline|Add to every Trim Set}}. Press **Keep it on this job** to change your mind.

Every Trim Set in the company asks it from now on. A stage that read ready on another job reads not ready until the crew answers it. This job keeps its answer. The plan sheet and the why are left off, since they were written for this job.

## What the crew sees

The check sits under **Added for this job** on the crew's sheet. The row says who added it, when and why. The crew answers it like any other question. A check added for one job counts toward ready like the company's questions. A stage that read ready reads not ready again on the Pipeline until the new check is answered.

## Take a check off

Only the person who added a check, or the office, can take it off.

1. Open the check on the sheet, or find it on the Documents tab.
2. Press **Take off**.
3. Press {{button:outline|Take it off}} to confirm. Press **Keep it** to change your mind.

The crew stops seeing it. The Documents tab stops listing it too. The app keeps any answer it already has.

## Read the answers

Every answer stamps its row with the person's name and the time. On the Documents tab each row reads the pick, the name, the time, the note and where the photo is. A row that reads *open* is not answered yet. A No or a Not here is an answer. Open is what holds a stage.

A check that needs a signature reads *needs a signature* until the crew signs it. A signed row reads *signed* with the mark. The guide [sign a checklist answer](/help/sign-a-checklist-answer) says what the crew sees and how an answer is taken back.

Once every question is answered, the stage files itself as a record with a number. Its chip reads like *Filed · CL-1042-1*. A check added to a filed stage opens it again as a new version. The guide [read a filed stage checklist](/help/read-a-filed-stage-checklist) has the record, Amend and Print the checklists.
