A stage checklist is a short list of questions the crew answers on the phone for one stage. The company's questions are written once on Settings. You can add a check for one job alone. The crew sees it on that job's sheet the next time the sheet opens.

## Who adds a check

The office adds a check on any job. A superintendent adds one on their own jobs. The crew answers and leaves notes. The crew does not add.

## Add a check from the sheet

A superintendent adds from the sheet. The office can too, once the stage has a question.

1. Open the job. Under the address, press the stage under **Stage checklists**. A superintendent sees every stage that takes a checklist, even one with no questions yet.
2. Scroll to **Added for this job**. Press {{button:outline|+ Add a check for Trim Set}}.
3. Type the check as a question. Write it so Yes, No or Not here answers it.
4. Tick **Asks for a photo** when the crew must take a photo with the answer.
5. Press {{button:blue|Add the check}}.

The sheet counts it at once. The toast says the new count, like *Added. Trim Set is now 3 of 6.*

:::example Checks a superintendent might add
1. Are the carriers in units 7 and 8 set?
2. Is the water heater pan drain run to the hub?
:::

## Add a check from the Documents tab

The office adds from here on any stage, even one with no questions yet. A superintendent has no Documents tab.

Open the job window and press **Documents**. Scroll to **Stage checklists**. Each stage reads with its standing, like *Not ready · 3 of 6*. Press {{button:outline|+ Add a check for Trim Set}} under the stage. The same box opens.

## What the crew sees

The check sits under **Added for this job** on the crew's sheet. Until it is answered, the row says who added it and when. The crew answers it like any other question. A check added for one job counts toward ready like the company's questions. A stage that read ready reads not ready again on the Pipeline until the new check is answered.

## Take a check off

Only the person who added a check, or the office, can take it off.

1. Open the check on the sheet, or find it on the Documents tab.
2. Press **Take off**.
3. Press {{button:outline|Take it off}} to confirm. Press **Keep it** to change your mind.

The crew stops seeing it. The Documents tab stops listing it too. The app keeps any answer it already has.

## Read the answers

Every answer stamps its row with the person's name and the time. On the Documents tab each row reads the pick, the name, the time, the note and where the photo is. A row that reads *open* is not answered yet. A No or a Not here is an answer. Open is what holds a stage.
