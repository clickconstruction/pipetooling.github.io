## What this surface is

> **Anchors since v2.1613:** schedule blocks anchor to **exactly one of a job or a bid** (`job_schedule_blocks.job_id` is nullable; `bid_id` + one-anchor CHECK). Hub plumbing carries an opaque **anchor id** — the job uuid, or `bid:<uuid>` — produced by `scheduleBlockAnchorId()` and decoded by `scheduleBlockAnchorFromId()` (both in [`jobScheduleBlocks.ts`](../src/lib/jobScheduleBlocks.ts)); `hubJobTitleById` / `hubJobAddressById` carry `bid:`-keyed entries so every id-keyed lookup just works. The linked-group helpers `updateJobScheduleBlockGroup` / `fetchJobScheduleBlockGroupLegs` are **group-keyed** (no job argument); the `move_job_schedule_block_group` RPC takes a nullable `p_job_id` (NULL = group only) and the drag kernel still passes `block.job_id`. Job-only affordances (Job Detail, job week `?jobId=`, team fetch, week summaries) skip bid anchors. **Identity line (v2.2901, Tier-2 #22):** every block title comes from the pure `scheduleBlockTitle()` kernel ([`scheduleBlockTitle.ts`](../src/lib/scheduleBlockTitle.ts)) — `J<hcp or click number> · name` for jobs, `Bid visit · B408 · Project` for bids; `formatScheduleDispatchHubJobTitle` is a positional wrapper over it. `hubJobTitleById` carries the `bid:` entries for every schedulable bid plus `hubScheduledBidTitleById` (Phase C, `fetchBidTitlesForScheduleBlocks`) for bids that still have a block this week after leaving the schedulable list. `HubPeopleBlockCard` tints bid blocks violet with a `bid` chip and opens the bid from both card buttons; the Jobs panel renders `bidRows` (`blocksToBidWeekMatrixRows`) under the job rows. **Opening a bid block (v2.2861, J18-F1):** `openJobWeekGrid` runs the anchor through `scheduleBlockTarget()` ([`scheduleBlockTarget.ts`](../src/lib/scheduleBlockTarget.ts), pure) and sends a `bid` to the Bids page's Edit Bid deep link (`bidOpenPath` → `/bids?bidId=<uuid>&openBidEdit=1`) instead of writing `?jobId=bid:<uuid>`; the router does the same for any old link still open.

The Schedule Dispatch hub is the week-grid scheduling surface at `/schedule-dispatch`. It is **already split into the playbook's two layers**, but both halves are individually oversized and hot:

## The shared substrate

There is **no per-record selection pointer** here (no `setSharedBid` analog — nothing like `?id=` selects a block/job across tabs; the closest thing, `?jobId=`, *leaves* this surface entirely). What the tabs share instead:

1. **The week-scoped data engine** (`loadHub` + its 22 state atoms, 18 memos and satellites, dossier above). People and Jobs are two projections of the same `hubWeekBlocks`/`hubJobs`; the Day tab re-fetches independently and pings back via `onDayScheduleChanged`. This engine is the page's `useBidPricingEngine` equivalent, and **building the `useScheduleDispatchHubData` seam is the unlock** for every Page-side hook — built v2.4863.
2. **The interaction-mode state machine** (`cardPlacementMode` ∥ `linkedCopyMode` ∥ `hubAssignJobPlacement` ∥ `hubMultiCellAdd*` ∥ `plusMenuBlockId`) — mutually exclusive, page-owned, URL-armed (`?placeJob=`), rendered by the Hub purely as props. It must remain a single owner.
3. **The URL router** (`week`/`day`/`hubTab`/`placeJob`/`focusPerson`) — permanent parent resident.

Consequence for extraction: Hub-file splits are prop-preserving file moves (cheap); Page-file splits are hook extractions where the parent destructures the hook result so downstream references don't change.

## Preserve-quirks list (odd but load-bearing)

1. **`jobId = ''` vestigial dual-mode** — dead branches everywhere; keep verbatim (dossier above). The add-block modal's edit mode and its `onRemove` are dormant on this page for the same reason.
2. **Quiet vs loud loads** — `loadHub({quiet:true})` never sets `hubLoading`; the `hubLoadSeqRef` monotonic guard means an older loud load finishing late cannot clear a newer load's spinner. Every schedule-mutation refresh is quiet; job saves reached through the Job Form / Job Detail contexts (`onCreatedJobId`, `onSaved`, `onEditJobSaved`) reload loud with `loadHub()`.
3. **`hubUserTimeOffPrimedRef`** — skips the standalone `user_time_off` refetch whenever `(weekStart, weekEnd, rosterKey)` still equals what `loadHub` last seeded (the ref is never cleared, so every same-key rerun skips, not just one); a week or roster change refetches. The key is the board's (the roster without the archived) since v2.4869 — see #23.
4. **Two `HubPeoplePanel` mounts** in the Hub shell (embed vs people tab) with hand-duplicated prop spreads (75 / 76 props; only `weekNav` differs).
5. **`closeJobDetail` effect dependency** — must depend on the stable callback, not the `jobDetailModal` context object (context identity changes on open → the modal used to self-close instantly).
6. **Missing-note indicator + card note colors gate on `work_date < scheduleTodayYmd`** — past days never light up red (`effectiveNoteRequirement`); `missingNoteCount` also returns 0 for past focus days.
7. **Multi-cell add uses a fixed 08:00–16:00 window** and a fresh `shared_block_group_id` per inserted block (not one group across the selection); overlapping cells are skipped, not errored; the cells are worked one at a time in selection order. Pinned by `multiCellAdd.test.ts`.
8. **Linked copies must land on the source block's `work_date`** — enforced in cell-pick (`linkedWrongDay` graying) AND in `onCardPlacementPickCell`; lane/person applies and Copy to techs always use `source.work_date`.
9. **Assign-picker stacking** — Job Detail (and Edit Job from it) stack **above** the picker; closing them lands back on the picker (comment at `onOpenJobDetail`, 2925).
10. **Per-device prefs are split across owners** — `personSort` (`pipetooling_dispatch_people_sort_v1`, default `lanes`) lives in the panel; `hideWeekend` (default **hidden**), `highlightLinkedGroups` and `phonePeopleViewPref` live in the page.
11. **Wages are keyed by trimmed person NAME** (`people_pay_config.person_name` matched against `fetchUserNamesForIds`), not user id, and the match is **case-sensitive** — unlike `payConfigLookupKey`, which the bid board's labor cost lowercases; 'Unknown' names are excluded; missing wage renders `$0` with the explanatory tooltip. Pinned by `hubWages.test.ts`.
12. **`hubMergedRows` sort:** `totalBlocks` desc, then `hcp_number` **descending** with `numeric: true`; the Click number names a job but does not rank it, so a job with no HCP number sorts after the ones that have one. Pinned by `buildHubMergedRows`' tests.
13. **Drag disabled ≠ hidden** — the red drag strip stays visible and clicking it toasts `SCHEDULE_DISPATCH_DRAG_DISABLED_READONLY_MESSAGE`; drag is also disabled during multi-cell add and linked-copy modes.
14. **Escape handling is per-mode effects on `window`** (placement/assign 1066, linked copy 1105, multi-cell 1114, delete-confirm 1086 each have their own listener with different guards, e.g. delete ignores Esc while busy).
15. **Tomorrow variant week-nav escapes the embed** — `shiftWeek`/`goThisWeek`/`openJobWeekGrid` `navigate()` to `/schedule-dispatch?…` instead of mutating local state.
16. **`move_job_schedule_block_group` has NO date predicate** — it moves every leg of a `shared_block_group_id` group (optionally job-filtered) whatever `work_date` each sits on. Since "Add person to crew" (v2.1371) writes one leg per distinct job/date/window, **multi-day groups are routine and moving one day of one collapses the whole group onto a single date**. `saveEditedScheduleBlockTimes` guards this (refuses a day move when the fetched legs span `>1` `work_date`, v2.1377), but **`moveScheduleDispatchBlockTo` still calls the RPC unguarded** — and it now backs three entry points (drag via `executeScheduleDispatchBlockReassign`, tap-to-move placement, the Move sheet). Proper fix is a date-scoped `move_job_schedule_block_group_day(job, group, from_date, to_date)` RPC that every path shares; until then do not add new callers.
17. **Two derivations of "phone board active"** — the page's `phoneBoardActive` (1032: `!isTomorrow && hubTab === 'people' && phonePeopleView === 'board'`) hides the banners; the panel's (`onPhonePeopleViewChange != null && phonePeopleView === 'board'`) swaps board for grid. They agree only because the tomorrow variant passes `undefined` for both phone props — keep it that way.
18. **Lateness refreshes on every quiet reload** — `refreshHubLateness` depends on `hubWeekBlocks` + `hubJobTitleById`, so each mutation also re-fetches the week's clock-ins; a data hook must keep that trigger.
19. **NCNS order** — the incident RPC runs while the day's blocks still exist (with zero clock sessions it only accepts "scheduled, no clock time"); clearing blocks first would make it refuse. Pinned by `recordNcns.test.ts`. The hub's call differs from the My Time day editor's (`lib/myTimeNcns.ts`) in two ways, both kept: it sends the details untrimmed, and it treats no row back as a refusal where the day editor treats it as done.
20. **Day-first redirect is one-shot** (`hubMobileDayFirstAppliedRef`, mount-only) and skipped when the URL names `hubTab` or `placeJob`.
21. **A linked copy groups a solo source before it checks the overlap** — `insertScheduleDispatchCopiedLeg` calls `ensureSharedBlockGroupForRow` first, so a copy refused for overlap (or for a failed day read) leaves the source with a group of one. Nothing shows it: the card's linked float and accent both need `linkPeerCount > 1`. Pinned by `scheduleDispatchMirrorInsert.test.ts`; reordering the two is a behavior change, its own PR.

## The schedule ledger (v2.3726)

Every add, move, reassign and remove on `job_schedule_blocks` writes one `schedule_block_events` row (`change`, the old and new window + assignee, the actor at trigger time) through the SECURITY DEFINER trigger function `job_schedule_blocks_to_ledger()`, fired by three triggers (insert · update of `work_date`, `time_start`, `time_end`, `assignee_user_id` · delete). Nothing on the hub changes; the ledger is what People → Day book reads for *Updated the schedule · N people · N blocks · Thu–Fri* (`docs/recent-features/v2.3726.md`). A write with no `auth.uid()` (a backfill, a service-role function) lands with a NULL actor and is counted on the Day book's day header as a system row, never credited to a person.
