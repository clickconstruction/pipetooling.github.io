# v2.8493 — Bills sent: a day names the bank deposits that are on no bill yet (2026-11-24)

The line v2.8453 left as a decision (punch list #422, problem 2 of the live test of 2026-11-24). No migration, no edge function.

## The decision

A day's figure in By day paid stays the payments on a job. Bank deposits that are on no bill yet are named beside it and never added.

## What was wrong

For one Monday the Dashboard's Money in bar showed seven payments and By day paid showed five. The other two were bank deposits Money in marks *Landed · not applied yet*. v2.8453 said why in the words under the month. The day itself still gave no sign of them.

## What changed

- **One rule, lifted.** `src/lib/moneyInDeposits.ts` now holds what `moneyInWeek.ts` and `moneyInWeekLoad.ts` each held a part of: which bank row is a customer paying us (`isCustomerDeposit`, `moneyInDepositsFromRows`), which typed-in payment carries each deposit (`matchMoneyInDeposits`), and which deposit is on no bill yet (`depositOnNoBill`, `depositsOnNoBillByDay`). `buildMoneyInWeek` calls it for its matches and its *Landed · not applied yet* tag. `moneyInWeek.ts` re-exports the names it used to hold, so no caller changed.
- **One set of reads.** `src/lib/moneyInDepositsLoad.ts` holds the reads the Dashboard's loader made inline: pending deposits, posted deposits through Accounts Receivable's own list, the typed-in payments no deposit carries, the open return cases. The loader calls them inside its own soft wrapper as before.
- **The line.** `billsPaidNoBillLine` (`billsPaidDays.ts`) answers the line under a picked day: *2 deposits landed that are on no bill yet.*, their total through `Money` with small cents, *Not added to the day.*, and **Open deposits**. The count is a marked value, drawn apart by `ValueSentence`. A day with none has no line.
- **The read.** `readDepositsOnNoBill` (`billsPaidIo.ts`) is a second read beside the payments. It reads from the month's first day, or from where Money in starts when that is earlier, so a day both screens show is decided over the same deposits.
- **The foot** now reads *Until then the Dashboard's Money in counts it. This page names it under its day and does not add it.* The guide's sentences of v2.8453 are amended the same way.

## What a deposit on no bill is

A posted customer deposit, by the org's Accounts Receivable filter, that did not come back, is not closed out, still holds money not applied, and that no typed-in payment carries. Its amount is the money not applied, not the whole deposit.

A deposit a typed-in payment carries is left out. That payment is already on a job and in a day's figure, so naming its deposit would say the money twice.

Money in sometimes names such a deposit by the one open bill it can only be. That is a guess and puts the deposit on no bill, so By day paid counts it too. Money in then tags it by its kind and says *not applied yet* on the line under the name.

## Never added

The day's figure, the day's cell, the three tiles, the print's totals and the CSV's *In the day total* read the payment lines alone, as before. No code path hands them a deposit. The print's foot still says a deposit not yet on a bill is not shown, which stays true of the print.

## The chips

The line shows under **All** alone. A chip narrows a day to one way of paying, and the way is written on the payment a deposit does not have yet. Under **Stripe** the line would name money that is never Stripe's. Tested in the kernel and on the screen.

## The press

**Open deposits** goes to `/accounts-receivable`, the door Money in's deposit chips use. It is drawn only for a role `canRoleSeeArBankUnallocatedOrgNudge` lets in, the gate that page itself reads. Today that is all four roles that open Bills sent, so the gate turns nobody away yet. A role it would turn away still reads the line.

## A read that fails

The deposits never fail the page. `readDepositsOnNoBill` answers *unread* when the posted deposits or the typed-in payments do not read, and the line then says *The bank deposits did not read. A deposit that is on no bill yet is not shown.* It is never drawn as none. Both reads must answer, since a count from the deposits alone could name one whose money is already in the day. The pending deposits and the open return cases only sharpen the match, so each fails soft. No line is drawn while the read is on its way. **Retry** on the payments asks the bank again too.

## Limits

- The count is as of now. A deposit that landed on a day and was put on a bill later is no longer on the line for that day.
- The typed-in payments are read through `list_unlinked_payments_for_bank_payments`, which answers the last 180 days. For a day older than about 150 days, a deposit a typed-in payment carries can read as on no bill. Accounts Receivable lists the same deposit as not applied.
- Money in reads deposits from about two weeks back. For an older day By day paid matches over more deposits than Money in ever read, so the two are only compared on days both show.
