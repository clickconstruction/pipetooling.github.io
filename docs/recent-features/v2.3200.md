# v2.3200 — Bid flow: the office poster drawn on every bid (2026-09-09)

Client only. Grace brought in the paper poster from the office wall — "When an estimate hits!!!!" — whose red-pen list is the estimator's twelve steps: load in Drive, send RFQ, load in clicktooling, count, import, takeoffs from RFQ and loaded data, price, review, generate cover letter and save as PDF, put a copy in Drive and link it, send and follow up, mark sent. This PR draws that list on each bid, derived from what the bid already carries. Design canvases: the first mockup (artifact `80cc3c18…`) and the six board placements (artifact `ba3fae36…`); the owner picked **C on the board, F on the workflow tabs, E when a row is opened**.

**Twelve becomes ten.** Two pairs read one signal each and are merged: *Count* + *Import* are both the bid's count rows (the app cannot see CountTooling itself), and *Send* + *Mark sent* are both the sent date (the one sent rule, v2.2937). The steps: Plans in Drive · Send RFQ · Plans in Tooling · Count & import · Takeoffs · Price · Review · Cover letter · PDF filed · Sent, in the poster's own order — Intake appears twice because the poster puts the RFQ between the two plans-loading steps.

- [`src/lib/bids/bidFlow.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/bidFlow.ts) — the one kernel (12 tests). `deriveBidFlow(bid, facts)` → ten steps with state `done | next | todo | untracked | loading`, each carrying its `proxy` (what the app read) and its `door`. Proxies: `drive_link`/`plans_link` · a `bid_rfqs` row · `count_tooling_plans_link`/`count_tooling_link` · `bids_count_rows` · `bids_takeoff_rough_part_lines` · `bid_value > 0` or `bid_pricing_assignments` · `reviewed_at` (optional key; absent → **untracked**, the honest state until PR 2) · a `bid_proposal_rooms` row or the sent date · `bid_submission_link` · `bid_date_sent`. Rules: **derive, never mint**; "sent" is `bids.bid_date_sent` and is never re-derived; **decided bids go quiet** (no "next" ring, per the due-cell rule); **"next" is the first gap after the furthest finished step**, so a bid sent before the RFQ desk existed is not nagged about the RFQ. `bidFlowSummary` is the one sentence every surface prints ("7 of 9 done · next: Cover letter"); `bidFlowSegments` rolls the steps into the eight phase runs the hairline draws.
- [`src/hooks/useBidFlowFacts.ts`](../../src/hooks/useBidFlowFacts.ts) — the five existence facts the bid row lacks, loaded **once per list** through `fetchAllRowsChunkedIn` (`select('bid_id')` on the five tables), never per row. Facts default to `null` (→ loading), and a failed load leaves the steps in `loading` rather than pretending "not done".
- [`src/components/bids/BidFlowStrip.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/BidFlowStrip.tsx) — three sizes of the same flow. **`hairline`** (option C): a 3px bar split by phase under the Bid Board's five jump icons, green done / blue next / grey not yet / dashed outline untracked, `role="img"` with the summary as its name. **`full`** (options E and F): phase labels, numbered nodes (✓ when done, a ring on next, dashed when untracked), step labels, and the summary line; every step with a door is a button. **`compact`** (twelve dots) is kept in the file for the record — the owner rejected it on the board because it displaced the buttons.
- [`BidsBidBoardTab`](../../src/components/bids/BidsBidBoardTab.tsx): the jump-icon cluster is unchanged except that the five icons now sit in a column with the hairline beneath them (desktop rows and phone cards share the cluster, so both get it; the 375px e2e pin stays green). `renderBidBoardExpandedContent` renders the full strip above the Project / GC / Address line; doors route through the existing `onOpenBidTab` / `onEditBid`, and `canSeePricingTabs` still hides Pricing and Cover Letter doors for superintendents.
- Workflow tabs — [`BidsCountsTab`](../../src/components/bids/BidsCountsTab.tsx), [`BidsTakeoffTab`](../../src/components/bids/BidsTakeoffTab.tsx), [`BidsLaborTab`](../../src/components/bids/BidsLaborTab.tsx), [`BidsPricingTab`](../../src/components/bids/BidsPricingTab.tsx), [`BidsCoverLetterTab`](../../src/components/bids/BidsCoverLetterTab.tsx): the full strip above the selected-bid header (Pricing's doors open Edit Bid and the Counts/Takeoffs/Labor tabs; the other tabs render it read-only for now).

**Not in this PR.** The Mark reviewed stamp (`bids.reviewed_at / reviewed_by / review_note` + a twin guard) is PR 2, v2.3201 — until it lands the Review step reads *not tracked in the app* and the count says "of 9". Submittals have no home in the app and are not a step. The five facts reads should fold into the board's own load or one aggregate before the strip reaches a heavier surface. Doors on Counts / Takeoffs / Labor / Cover Letter's strips.
