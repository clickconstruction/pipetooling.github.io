## Why

PR 2 showed the agreement; a thin scope or a missing amount still meant leaving the sweep for the full Contract modal. The mock-up's rule: the fix is typing where the hole is.

## What shipped

- **The edit fields** in [`JobsContractSweepModal`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsContractSweepModal.tsx): **Scope** (a textarea, one line per item) and **Amount** (decimal; blank = time and materials) between the To/Terms rows and the document. They start from the job's draft when it has one, else the prefill (accepted-estimate lines or fixtures, the accepted total or revenue). The iframe redraws from the typed values on every keystroke.
- **Autosave** (600 ms after the first real edit) through the new kernel [`jobContractDraftWrite.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobContractDraftWrite.ts) (+2 tests on the payload builder): `buildJobContractDraftPayload` (fields, the chosen terms, the recipient — the Contract modal's payload shape) and `saveJobContractDraft` (a draft row updates in place, its terms untouched; no row inserts a fresh draft; a **sent** row is locked and comes back as-is). The pane says *Saving… · Saved to the job's draft · Save failed*, or *Locked — sent; Void & redo in the full editor*.
- **Readiness follows the edit**: saved edits are kept per job (`overrides`) so the row's chips and the To send / Needs a look split recompute from what was typed — *Scope is just the name* becomes *Ready*, the footer sentence becomes *Emails may@corewellpartners.com · then J778*, and Send & next enables.
- **Send flushes first**: Send / Send & next / Send ours instead write any unsaved edit before calling the send, so the minted contract is what the pane showed. Closing the full editor re-reads the draft and the fields.
