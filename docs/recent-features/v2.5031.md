# v2.5031 — Liens: a property kind not set dates as residential, the earlier date, everywhere (2026-10-09)

Client, plus migration `20261010001000_lien_blank_kind_residential.sql`. The push rides GC MODE's 09:12 UTC batch if this merges first, or Punchlist pushes it after that batch. The sample fixtures change too, so nine portal functions want a redeploy; see Deploy. The owner's call of **2026-10-09**, lien punch item K, sent by Punchlist: a blank property kind reads residential, the earlier date, on every screen.

The earlier date is the safe one: a house read as commercial loses its lien a month late. The runway and the Calendar already read an unset kind as residential (*Property kind is not set, so the earlier (residential) date is shown*). Every other reader ran the commercial clock, because each one gave the earlier month only to `residential`. On one job, the Calendar and the Lien desk could show dates a month apart.

## What changed

- **The kernel** (`src/lib/jobs/lienDeadlines.ts`): `lienCommercialClock` is true for `non_residential` only. `noticeDeadlineForMonth` and `filingDeadlineForMonth` give everything else the 2nd and 3rd month. Every caller that passes the kind follows:
  - the Lien window's clock;
  - the desk's Timeline tab and month grid;
  - the timeline book;
  - the GC run's dates;
  - the Forecast kernel;
  - § Rules;
  - the supply houses' windows;
  - the last-work-day preview;
  - the legal packet and paper.
- **SQL**: `lien_notice_deadline` and `lien_filing_deadline` get the same rule. The desk's queue dates come from them. Docs: `docs/migrations/20261010001000_lien_blank_kind_residential.md`.
- **Readers with their own copy of the rule:**
  - the demand letter's `lienFilingDeadlineForMonth`;
  - the Forecast panel's 3rd/4th-month words;
  - the Lien window's *15th of the 2nd month* line, which adds *(kind not set, so the residential date)*;
  - the legal grid's kind cell, which reads *Residential* with its not-entered mark;
  - the timeline book's column, *Res?* instead of *Com?*.
- **The run's letter**: `affidavitMonthWordFor(propertyKind)` replaces the tone-keyed `affidavitMonthWord`. An unset kind still gets the commercial letter's wording, but it names the residential, third month, the date every screen shows. The desk, the run and the GC on-notice preview, which now receives the property kind, all use it.
- **The "set the kind" warning stays everywhere** and now says which dates are shown:
  - the timeline's note (`LIEN_KIND_UNKNOWN_WORDS`), the desk's Timeline tab and gate 3 (title, clock words and rule line);
  - the Forecast chip;
  - the legal grid and packet (`PROPERTY_KIND_UNKNOWN_WORDS`);
  - the book's lede.
- **Sample fixtures** (`_shared/customerSampleFixtures.ts`): the sample used `property_kind: 'commercial'`. The database never holds that value (its CHECK allows `''`, `residential` and `non_residential`), and under the new rule it would read residential. The sample now uses `non_residential` and dates on the same rule.
- **The guides** say one rule: *see when a customer will pay*, *send lien notices from the lien desk*, *understand how liens work and which lien tool to use*, and *Texas lien rules the app follows* (whose *What you do* now says a commercial pick moves the dates a month later). *Read the Pipeline money view* and *see what is due on the lien calendar* already said it.
