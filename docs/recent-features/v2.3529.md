# v2.3529 — Money with no bill, PR 2: the reason-coded close-out (2026-09-16)

Client + one migration. Second and last PR of `to-dos/money-with-no-bill/` (PR 1, the tip strip, was v2.3496 + v2.3499); item 9 of the owner's ready-to-build train.

## Why

A deposit that is not a customer's payment — bank interest, a vendor's refund, an owner putting money in — has no bill and no job, so it sat in Accounts Receivable's To match forever and nagged on four surfaces. The only exits were wrong: apply it to a bill (there is none) or Mark returned (a claim the money bounced). PR 1 took the tip case off this door on purpose; this PR adds the honest exit for the rest and names the reason on the row instead of hiding it.

## What shipped

- **Schema** — `mercury_transaction_ar_closed` + `set_mercury_transaction_ar_closed` ([migration doc](../migrations/20260916230000_ar_deposit_close_out.md)); the list and count readers of To match exclude closed-out deposits unless *All* is on. The RPC refuses a returned deposit and any deposit with money already applied to a job — that money is a customer's, and its leftover is the tip strip's.
- **Kernel** — `src/lib/jobs/arCloseOut.ts`: `buildArCloseOutOffer` (offer only while nothing is applied, not returned, not already closed, money left), `suggestArCloseReason` (reads *interest* / *refund* / *owner* in the bank's counterparty, note and memo), `arCloseOutReady`, `describeArCloseOut`, `AR_CLOSE_REASONS` (bank interest · vendor refund · owner deposit · something else + note). Nine tests.
- **The strip** — `src/components/jobs/ar/ArCloseOut.tsx` above Allocations in `BankPaymentsModal.tsx`, mutually exclusive with the tip strip by rule. Reason select (pre-picked from the suggestion), a note box for *Something else*, **Close out $312.48**, then a confirm step. A missing RPC answers *This is not live in the database yet*.
- **The record** — `ArDepositHeader` shows *Closed out · Vendor refund · Sep 16, 2026* with a **Reopen** link; the row wears a muted *closed out* chip (`arDepositRowState` gains the `closed` state, below `returned`); the footer's waiting sentence gains *or close it out with a reason* (`arApplySentence`, `closeOutOffered`).
- **Guide** — `match-bank-deposits-to-bills.md` gains *If it isn't a customer's payment at all*.

## Decisions taken (owner's open item 3)

The reason list is **bank interest · vendor refund · owner deposit · something else (note required)** — the three cases named in the to-do plus one escape hatch that forces words. No accounting label is required before a close-out: Banking's label is a separate act with its own rule (v2.3514 only labels *applied* deposits), and holding the AR exit hostage to it would put the nag back. Both are easy to change: the list is one constant plus the CHECK constraint.

## Verify

Open Accounts Receivable, pick an untouched deposit — the strip reads *Not a customer's payment?* with the reason pre-picked when the memo says *refund* or *interest*. Close it out: the toast says it left To match, the row is gone from the pile, it shows with a *closed out* chip under *All*, and the header carries the reason and date with Reopen. Reopen puts it back. On a deposit with a bill already applied the strip does not appear (the tip strip does when money is left).
