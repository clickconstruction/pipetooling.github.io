# v2.3472 — Field report emails and the digest on My email schedule (2026-09-15)

One migration (`20260915170000_email_schedule_report_email_stream.sql`, [`docs/migrations/`](../migrations/20260915170000_email_schedule_report_email_stream.md)) + client; no edge function. PR 1 of 2 from the owner's ask — *offer some people every report submitted on every job* — which turned out to exist already as **Report email recipients** (v2.746: `report_email_subscriptions`, all authors or named authors, auto-sent by `send-report-email` at filing) but was invisible on Settings, and the recurring digest was only ever a week-grid chip. PR 2 adds the team-lead scope and a door on Jobs → Reports.

## What shipped

- **`get_my_email_schedule()`** gains `report_emails` — every subscription addressed to the caller, by user id or by an outside address equal to the account email — and the `weekly` digest rows gain `crew_filter`. **`get_global_email_schedule()`** gains `report_email_subscriptions` (recipient name or address, label, enabled / auto-send / all-authors, author names). Both bodies are the live definitions with only the additions.
- **Kernel** [`emailScheduleWeek.ts`](../../src/lib/emailSchedule/emailScheduleWeek.ts): `reportEmails` on the normalized subscriptions (enabled first, blank names dropped), `describeReportEmailSubscription` ("every report anyone files" · "reports from Darren and Paige" · "reports from A, B, C and 2 more" · "sent on demand only" · "paused"), `describeDigestScope` ("jobs yesterday · my team · with costs"), `describeDays`, `describeDigestSchedule` ("Daily recap — Mon–Fri · 7:00 AM · jobs yesterday · my team"). The week-grid chip's detail now carries the scope. Tests.
- **Settings → My email schedule** ([`SettingsMyEmailScheduleSection.tsx`](../../src/components/settings/SettingsMyEmailScheduleSection.tsx)): two new standing rows at the top of *My email subscriptions* — **Job report digest** (one phrase per schedule; managed from Jobs → Reports) and **Field reports** (whose reports; a paused-only setup reads *paused — …* instead of *not subscribed*; managed from the Dashboard's Recent Reports mail button). The list's intro reads "every standing email stream".
- **Settings → Email streams** (dev, [`SettingsEmailStreamsSection.tsx`](../../src/components/settings/SettingsEmailStreamsSection.tsx)): a **Field report emails** card after the digests — one chip per recipient with *outside* / *all reports* or *from N people* (hover for the names) / *on demand* / *paused* tags, and × does the modal's Remove.
- **Guides**: [`see-your-email-schedule.md`](../../src/content/help/see-your-email-schedule.md) (the two rows, the chip's scope), [`email-reports-to-people.md`](../../src/content/help/email-reports-to-people.md) (where recipients see it). [`REPORT_SUBSCRIPTIONS.md`](../REPORT_SUBSCRIPTIONS.md) gains the `report_email` inventory row and a paragraph on how the two report streams relate.
