# v2.6416 — The Development page on a phone: the strip holds all six looks in its one row again (2026-11-03)

A fix to the Gray Fox spike's phone strip (v2.6377, `to-dos/developments/SPIKE_GRAYFOX.md`; the owner on the first cut, *I don't like how this looks on mobile*). v2.6377 drew the phone strip as a grid of `repeat(5, minmax(0, 1fr))` beside five looks. v2.6371 (punch list #247) then added **Photos** as a sixth look to `ORDER`, and the phone nav drew six cells in five columns: Photos alone on a second row, the strip 104 px tall instead of 53, which is the ragged strip the owner turned down. The phone nav's label also still read *Five looks at the site*. No migration, no edge function.

## Change

- **The row counts no columns.** `src/index.css`: `.developmentPhoneStrip` is `display: flex` and each `.developmentPhoneLook` is `flex: 1 1 auto; min-width: 0`, so a cell starts as wide as its name or figure and the cells share what is left. A look added to `ORDER` stays in the row. On a screen too narrow for every name, the names give way with the ellipsis they already carried. Measured on Gray Fox as the dev with Playwright: one row, 53 px tall, no name or figure cut, the page exactly as wide as the screen, at 375, 360 and 320 wide.
- **The strip names itself from its list.** `DevelopmentJumpStrip.tsx`: `LOOKS_NAME` counts `ORDER` into words (*Six looks*), and the desk's cap, the desk's nav and the phone's nav all read it, so the phone's label says *Six looks at the site* and none of the three can lag the list again. The desk draws the same words it drew.
- `lookFigure.ts`'s note counts six cells.
