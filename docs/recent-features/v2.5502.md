# v2.5502 — Messages: leads, supply houses and candidates show their names (2026-10-13)

Residential **R0, PR 4: the fetchers** (`RESIDENTIAL_BUILD_PLAN.md` on branch spike/gc-mode). R0's SQL (#5593,
`20261013180000`) gave `contacts` the four id columns, R0's loaders (#5570) read their records' names, and the types
(#5657, then the tidy #5658) carry them. But no fetcher selected the columns or read the records, so a lead, a supply
house, a rep at one and a candidate had no record at runtime. This was the hand-off's "fetchers' new columns after the
push", never built until now. No migration, no edge function.

- **`contacts/load.ts`**: `ContactRow` picks the four ids (`prospect_id`, `supply_house_id`, `supply_house_contact_id`,
  `team_prospect_id`) with the rest. `NewRecordColumns` and its optional reads are gone.
- **`messages/messagesIo.ts`**: `CONTACT_COLS` selects the four. `loadRecords`, the Messages view's read, now exported for
  its test, reads the four record tables by the ids that point at them:
  - `prospects(id, contact_name, company_name)`.
  - `supply_houses(id, name)`, for a counter's own house and each rep's.
  - `supply_house_contacts(id, supply_house_id, name)`, read beside the other people in the first, parallel step.
  - `team_prospects(id, name)`.
- **`followUp/followUpIo.ts`**: `CONTACT_COLUMNS` selects the four and embeds only `prospect:prospects(id,
  contact_name, company_name)`. Follow up never holds a supply house or a candidate (`OFF_FOLLOW_UP`), so their
  records are not read there (the lead's call). `recordsOfContacts` gathers the prospects.
- **Who reads what (the lead's call, the plan's § 7 call 11)**: `prospects` is readable only by
  `user_has_prospects_staff_access()`, the dev, the owner and the assistants. For an estimator or a controller the read
  comes back empty, or the embed null, with no error. A lead then shows the name `name_contact` stored on the contact,
  per `Contact.name`'s rule. Whether estimators and controllers should read Prospects is the owner's call, a policy
  migration of its own.
- **Fixtures**: the six helpers that build a `contacts` row (`spineRows.ts`, the followUp tests and smokes,
  `useFollowUpQueue`'s smoke) carry the four as null.
- **Tests**:
  - `messagesIo.test.ts`: the four tables read by their ids, a rep's house beside a counter's, once each. A customer
    alone reads none of them.
  - `followUpIo.test.ts`: the four ids and the `prospect` embed are selected, and the other three records are not.
    `recordsOfContacts` gathers a prospect and skips a null embed.
  - `FollowUpPanel.render.test.tsx`: a lead named from Prospects, and one whose prospect was not read keeping their
    stored name.
  - Dropping the embed, or a rep's house, each fails a test on purpose.
