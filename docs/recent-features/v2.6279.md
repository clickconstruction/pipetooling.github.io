- **`follow`**, the cron's action in [`supabase/functions/gmail-mine/index.ts`](../../supabase/functions/gmail-mine/index.ts): a `POST` with `X-Cron-Secret` equal to `CRON_SECRET` (as `gmail-count` checks it) and `{ action: 'follow' }`, which a signed-in caller may not send (`parseMyMailRequest` refuses it, 400) and which the secret's door alone runs. It reads, as each person whose `users.my_mail_on` is on and who is a real account on the office team (`followPeople`: the roles of `gc_office_team()`; never a sample, a twin, the walk tester, an archived or a training account), their `email_tag_threads` rows with `coalesce(last_read_at, tagged_at)` in the last 60 days, the oldest read first and a row whose last read failed after the rest, at most `FOLLOW_ROWS_PER_RUN` (100) a run, with one Google token per person per run. Each row: `threads.get` with the metadata headers, the lines that hold its mail (by `rfc_message_id`, else by `gmail_id` in this person's box, as `list` finds them), then `planFollow`. A mail no line holds is read whole and lands through **`landMail`**, factored out of `tag` so one function knows how a mail becomes a line: the same party placing, files, `.eml`, job columns, `mailbox` `mine` and `mail_user_id`, behind the same `Message-ID` fence. A box's line on no job that nobody tied or untied by hand is tied through `tie_message` as the service role and stamped `tied_by` the row's `tagged_by`; every line a person placed stays where it is. What landed or was tied gets the Gmail label. Then `last_read_at` is now and `last_error` null, or `last_error` the reason and the next row still runs; a person whose token fails has their rows' `last_error` written and is skipped for the run. One log line a run, `gmail_mine_follow` with rows, people, lines, tied and failed; the answer is `{ ok, rows, lines, tied, failed }`.
