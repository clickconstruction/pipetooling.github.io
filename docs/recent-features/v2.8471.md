# v2.8471 — The app never reloads itself over words someone typed, and a page that lost a window mends itself (2026-11-24)

Punch list #85. The third thing the live look of 2026-11-24 found and left (`docs/recent-features/v2.8377.md`, *What differed*: *the next press does not ask the network*). Diane said yes on 2026-11-24 to the page reloading itself once the signal is back, and set the rule it is built on: *a user will never feel like they are going to lose text they typed, especially for a report or a message to a customer. It should stay until they delete it or they press send.* Client only: no migration and no edge function.

## What was wrong

A window whose code did not come closes alone and says *The job could not open. Check your signal, then press it again.* (v2.8377). `lazyWindow` asks for the code again on a later open. In Chromium that ask never reaches the network.

A bare harness on 2026-11-24 (Chromium 149, six cases) says why:

| What failed | The same address again | A new address (`?retry=1`) |
|---|---|---|
| The file itself | fails at once, no request | loads |
| A file it imports, lost through `modulepreload` or through the import | fails at once, no request | fails: the lost file is still at its own address |

Vite preloads every file a window needs before it imports the window, so with no signal each of them is lost with it. No address can load the window again in that page. Only a new page can.

So the second press started the recovery reload (*Updating app…*), and a third press opened the job. Inside a minute of a recovery reload the second press only said the line again, with the network whole.

The reload that second press started waited for a save on its way out, and for nothing else. A message typed on the page behind the window went with it.

## The rule: nothing typed is lost to a reload the app starts

`src/lib/typedText.ts`. `pageHoldsTypedText()` is true while either of these is on the page:

1. **A box made for free words with words in it**: a `textarea`, or an element edited in place (`contenteditable`). Whoever put the words there. A draft the app brought back is still a draft.
2. **A one-line field the person typed into, while it still has words.** `watchTypedText()`, started in `main.tsx`, hears every `input` event in the capture phase and remembers the field. A field the app filled by itself, a date or a saved amount, does not count: a reload brings it back.

Not counted: a search box (by its type, its role, or a name that starts *Search*, *Find* or *Filter*), since its words are typed again in a second and holding for them would hold every list; a field that is read-only or disabled; spaces alone. A field that has left the page is forgotten. A page that cannot be read counts as holding words.

So the words hold until they are cleared, or until they are sent and their box empties or leaves. Nothing counts presses or time.

Two reloads read it.

**The recovery reload** (`chunkLoadRecovery.ts`). `startChunkRecoveryReload` starts nothing while the page holds typed words, with its guard left unclaimed, as it does offline. The caller's fallback shows: for a window, its line; for a route, *This page could not load…* with **Reload**, which is the person's own press. Words typed after the reload was started, while it waits for a save, hold it too (`decideChunkReloadWait`), past the half minute the wait otherwise ends at, until they are sent or cleared.

**The cure**, below.

## The cure: the page mends itself before the next press

`src/lib/windowCure.ts` decides, `src/hooks/useWindowCure.ts` watches, mounted once for everyone signed in (in `JobDetailModalProvider`, beside the warm-up).

`lazyWindow` keeps every window whose last load failed (`lostWindowCount`, `subscribeLostWindows`), a warm-up's failure too, until its code lands. The watcher looks when a window is lost, when the browser says the signal is back (`online`), when the tab is looked at again, and after every hold. `decideWindowCure` answers:

| The page | The answer |
|---|---|
| no window lost, or inside a frame | nothing |
| the cure was tried inside the last ten minutes | nothing |
| the browser says offline | nothing: its `online` event wakes the cure |
| **words typed on the page** | wait, look again in 15 seconds, for as long as they stay |
| unsaved work held, a write in flight, a cursor in a field, a window open | wait, look again in 15 seconds |
| inside a minute of a recovery reload | wait for the minute |
| otherwise | ask the network |

The ask is `probeForSignal`, the one a customer's **Try again** sends. With no answer it asks again a minute later. With an answer it asks the question above a second time, since the ask can take five seconds and someone may have typed in them. Then it writes down the try and asks for the lost windows again (`rewarmLostWindows`).

- **Safari and Firefox** fetch the code again. The window is loaded and nothing else happens.
- **Chromium** fails the ask at once. That failure takes the road every lost chunk takes: Vite's `vite:preloadError`, `main.tsx`'s handler, the one guarded recovery reload. The page comes back whole, and the next press opens the window.

The cure adds no reload of its own. It only asks sooner than the person's next press would have.

**Once in ten minutes.** A window whose file is gone from the server fails again after the reload. Without the gap the page would reload every minute for as long as it sat quiet, and each reload empties the caches.

## The line over typed words

A press on a lost window while typed words are on the page, with the browser saying the network is there, cannot open it and will not reload. The line says what frees it (`windowLostLine`), for fourteen seconds:

> The job could not open. The app must reload first, and it will not reload over what you typed. Send or clear it, then press again.

Offline the line is the signal one, as before: the signal is the first thing to mend, and no reload would come anyway. Soon after the words are sent or cleared the cure runs, and the press opens the window.

## Seen in the built app

On 2026-11-24 the app was built (`npm run build`, its `tsc -b` included) and its `dist` served locally. A headless Chromium 149 at 1,280 by 800 and 390 by 844, signed in with a session from the dev-login account, with the build's own service worker in control. On Jobs → Pipeline the search named a ZZ test customer, and its chip opens the customer's window. That window's file was taken out of the precache and the browser set offline, so a press on the chip said *The customer could not open. Check your signal, then press it again.* Read only: nothing was written.

**A quiet page.** The network was let back and nobody pressed. One ask left (`/favicon.svg?signal=…`), and the page reloaded by itself: `/?nocache=…`, then Jobs → Pipeline again. The next press on the chip opened the customer's window, with one request for its file.

**Words on the page.** The same loss, then a note box on the page (put there by the test) with *We can start Monday. Call me first.* typed into it by the keyboard, and the cursor moved off it. The network was let back.

- For 40 seconds nothing reloaded and no ask left. The words stayed.
- A press on the chip: the same document, the words still in their box, and the amber line *The customer could not open. The app must reload first, and it will not reload over what you typed. Send or clear it, then press again.*, at the foot on the phone, 358 wide.
- The words were cleared. Inside 40 seconds one ask left and the page reloaded by itself. The next press opened the window.

Both widths gave the same result. Nothing was wider than the screen.

## Not proved

- Safari and Firefox, where the ask for a lost window should load it with no reload.
- A real report or message box in the app: the note box was the test's own. The rule reads any `textarea`, and `typedText.test.ts` holds that.

## What is left

- **The update's own reload does not read typed words yet.** `autoReload.ts` holds for a cursor in a field, an open window, unsaved work a component holds and a write in flight. Words typed and left in a field with no hold are not among them, at its idle moment. `pageHoldsTypedText` is ready for it. It is its own change. Mended in v2.8499: typed words hold the update reload at every moment and at its second look.
- **A press a person makes is theirs.** **Reload** on a page that could not load, the update pill's **Reload** and the gear menu's **Hard Reload** reload over typed words, as a browser's refresh does. None warns. Since v2.8499 the pill's **Reload** warns first and shows each place.
- **A browser that wrongly says offline** gets no cure until it says online: the recovery reload the cure leans on does not start offline.
- **Safari and Firefox were not looked at.** That they fetch a failed import again is how they are known to behave, not something seen here.
