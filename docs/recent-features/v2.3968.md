# v2.3968 — Bid room: the proposal the GC signs shows the same Terms and Exclusions as the printed letter (2026-09-28)

Client only; no migration, no edge function.

## Why

The cover letter's Terms and Exclusions reached two readers with different fallbacks. The Cover Letter tab resolves each as *the bid's own entry, else the org default, else `''`* and hands that to the letter builders, which print the built-in `DEFAULT_EXCLUSIONS` / `DEFAULT_TERMS_AND_WARRANTY` when the text trims to nothing. The same `''` went to both `BidRoomPanel` mounts; `buildBidRoomRevisionPayload` trims it into the published revision and the public room page hides an empty block. With no org default saved and nothing typed for the bid, the GC signed a proposal online that showed no Terms and no Exclusions, while the printed letter showed the built-in wording.

## What shipped

- **[`src/lib/bidDocuments/coverLetterWording.ts`](../../src/lib/bidDocuments/coverLetterWording.ts)** (pure) — `effectiveCoverLetterWording({ perBid, orgDefault, builtIn })`: the bid's own entry when it has one, else the org default, and the built-in whenever the text so picked is blank or whitespace only. That is the letter's rule as a function, so a box the estimator emptied resolves to the **built-in** wording, not the org default — the entry exists, the org default is never read, and the letter swaps blank text for the built-in.
- **`BidsCoverLetterTab.tsx`** — both `BidRoomPanel` mounts (the plain bid and the per-GC packet) are handed `roomExclusions` / `roomTerms` from the kernel. What the letter builders receive and what the two textareas show are unchanged.
- **Tests** — `coverLetterWording.test.ts` (13): the six rules, then seven states of the tab run through `buildCoverLetterText` and `buildCoverLetterHtml` to prove the kernel names the wording the letter prints and no other. `BidsCoverLetterTab.room.render.test.tsx` (3), the tab's first render test: with `BidRoomPanel` replaced by a stand-in that prints its props, nothing typed gives the room the built-in wording, typed wording arrives as typed, an emptied box gives the built-in. Two of the three fail against the old wiring.
- **The map** ([`BIDS_DOCUMENT_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md`](../BIDS_DOCUMENT_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md)) names the third, bid-room variant of the wording, lists the kernel as extracted, and says the tab now has one render test.

## Knock-on effects

- **The room-link email now states validity in more cases.** `supabase/functions/_shared/bidRoomLinkEmail.ts` reads a day count out of the revision's terms (`validityDaysFromTerms`); the built-in terms say "thirty (30) days", so a room published from a bid whose terms used to go out blank now sends "Pricing is good for 30 days." The function is not changed and needs no deploy.
- **Published revisions are frozen.** A room published before this release keeps its empty blocks until someone presses **Publish update**; nothing is backfilled.
