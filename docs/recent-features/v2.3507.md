# v2.3507 — What customers see: open to the office, with teaching on every step (2026-09-16)

PR 7 of the train in `to-dos/what-customers-see-journeys/README.md`, shipped second because it is the one change that makes the rest worth building. Client only; no migration, no edge function.

## Why

The tab was gated to the dev role, so the people who send these pages every day had never seen them from the customer's side — which is how a plain-text estimate email went unnoticed for months (v2.2747 → v2.2758). Decision taken as proposed: dev, master and the assistant-like roles see it; training-mode users too, because sample mode is an anon-reachable fixture and nothing on the tab writes.

## What shipped

- **`canSeeWhatCustomersSee(role)`** in [`settingsGroups.ts`](../../src/lib/settingsGroups.ts): dev · master_technician · assistant-like (assistant, controller). One gate for the tab list and the render in `Settings.tsx`. Subs, estimators, primaries and superintendents still do not see it.
- **Teaching on every step** — [`customerJourneys.ts`](../../src/lib/customerJourneys.ts) gains two required fields per step: `customerCan` (what the person can do on that surface, in the office's words) and `guide` (the help slug that covers sending it). The journeys test asserts every guide exists on disk.
- **Every card opens** ([`SettingsWhatCustomersSeeTab.tsx`](../../src/components/settings/SettingsWhatCustomersSeeTab.tsx)): a *Next release* or *sent by another system* card was inert; it now expands to its note plus the teaching lines. The expanded view shows **What sends it · When · What they can do there** and a **How to send it →** door to `/help?g=<guide>`. Rendered steps keep their frame beneath.
- Help guide `see-what-customers-see.md`: roles widened, a *Learn a step* section.
- `docs/ACCESS_CONTROL.md` → Settings (selected): the row.

## Verified

Typecheck, lint, build; journeys (5), settings-groups (8), registry (8), sample-email, help-guide and release-note tests. Live as Robert (dev): every card opens, the teaching lines and the guide door render on a rendered step and on a Next release step; the tab is listed for the assistant role in `getZonedSettingsGroups` (test), and the E2E settings spec's marker (`Sample data:`) is unchanged.
