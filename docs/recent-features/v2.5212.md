# v2.5212 — GC mode: a ring on each board row shows how far the job has come (2026-10-10)

The Board's B2b-vi-c, the last of B2b-vi's three cuts (`to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/board-b2b-vi.md` on branch `spike/gc-mode`, call L). The lead approved it, and gc 2 co-signed with three conditions, all written in. No migration, no deploy.

- **The ring** (`GcProgressRing.tsx`): the design spike's component, word for word but its import. It sits at the head of each Project Board row, By stage and By customer, lost and closed rows too. On a phone it stands over the days block in that column, so the name keeps its width (checked at 375 px on the dev server). It reads `stageProgress` (B2b-vi-a):
  - Bidding counts the steps before our bid can go in.
  - Buying out counts Get started.
  - Building is the percent of the work reported.
  Hover, a press or the keyboard opens the card: each type's done of total, what is left, and *Also*. A full ring draws a hollow check.
- **Who sees which ring** (`ringStages.ts`, call L): a stage's ring draws only for a reader every source it counts lets in.
  - A GC table lets a role in through `GC_TABLE_DOORS`, by its door or its wider `reads` (gc 2's condition 1).
  - What is not a GC table is in `GC_OTHER_SOURCES` with its readers: the papers' function, the change orders' office view, our crew's clock-ins and percent (condition 2). The test fails on a source in neither map.
  - The schedule's sources are every Schedule-lane table, since `loadSchedule` reads them all.
  - Today the office team and the money team see the bidding ring; a dev sees all three. The schedule's PR 10, the award door and Building's door widen it with no edit here.
  - A row whose stage the reader may not see keeps an empty first cell, so the rows line up.
- **What it reads** (`boardLayers.ts`, `useRingLayers.ts`): the board with what each stage counts laid over it, in the Schedule window's order. That is the logs with our crew's clock-ins, the change orders' office half, the draws and the trades' side of the changes, and the submittals; then the trades' asks, the punch lists, the schedules and our crew's percent.
  - The hook reads only the layers the reader's stages count, after the board paints (gc 2's condition 3: the reads are driven by `ringStages`).
  - A layer is set only once its read succeeds. `ringReady` keeps a stage out until every layer it counts was read. Until then its ring is an empty track that says *Reading what the ring counts.*, or that a read failed. A missing layer never reads as nothing.
  - The page names the one state `ringState`. B2b-vii's How the stage is going reads the same value.
  - So the board now reads the punch lists and the submittals once, up front, for the jobs being built, for readers who see the building ring: a dev today, Building's door's readers later. The windows still read their own on open. `GcProjects.render.test.tsx` mocks the hook to null in one line, so Building's tests of those lazy window reads keep their meaning; `useRingLayers.render.test.tsx` covers the hook (a dev reads each layer, then building is ready; an estimator reads none; a failed read leaves building waiting).
- **Counts, never money** (the lead's call, on the owner's rule): the ring's count, headline and building items carry no dollars (`progress.money.test.ts`). The bidding card keeps the kernel's words, whose trade lines name the quoted numbers the same reader sees on the row's price card.
