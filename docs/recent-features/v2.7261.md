# v2.7261 — Lien desk: a started paper stays on the desk, whatever its day (2026-11-14)

Punch list #354 ([`to-dos/lien-desk-started-papers-stay.md`](../../to-dos/lien-desk-started-papers-stay.md)). Client only: no migration, no edge function, and no kernel that is copied to the edge functions changed.

## Before

`useLienDeskData` asked the three readers (`list_lien_notice_months`, `list_lien_affidavit_windows`, `list_lien_retainage_windows`) for `LIEN_DESK_LEAD_DAYS` (30), and the affidavit and retainage queues were built from those rows alone. A paper someone had drafted, sent for approval, approved or held dropped off every list while its day was further out. On 2026-11-14 the dev seat drafted three affidavits for a Dec 15 day (v2.7247); each showed in its job's Lien window and nowhere on the desk. The owner chose this fix over widening the window for every job, which would roughly double the To do count and move the lien calendar's draft band, the supplier dates and the timeline book, which read the same constant.

## What changed

- **[`src/lib/jobs/lienDeskStarted.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDeskStarted.ts)**, a pure kernel. `LIEN_DESK_STARTED_READ_DAYS` (180) is how far the desk's read now asks each reader. `lienDeskItemStarted` is the rule for a started paper: not voided, not sent, not missed (the notice queue's `itemIsLive`). `lienDeskKeepRows(rows, items, kind, todayYmd)` keeps an affidavit or retainage row whose day is inside the lead, has no day (a retainage clock not started), or whose job holds a started item of that kind. `lienDeskKeepNoticeRows` keeps a notice month inside the lead, or one a started notice on the job names, so a draft for September never brings October's window onto To draft. Tests in `lienDeskStarted.test.ts`: a row inside the lead stays, one outside goes, one outside on a job with a drafted, held, approved or awaiting paper stays, one whose paper was sent, missed or voided goes, a paper of another kind keeps nothing, and a far notice month stays only when the live notice names it.
- **`src/hooks/useLienDeskData.ts`** asks the readers for `LIEN_DESK_STARTED_READ_DAYS` and passes each reader's rows through the kernel with the items it already reads, before the jobs join and before any queue is built. Light mode (the Dashboard's counts) goes through the same lines, so a started paper counts there too: it is work in hand.
- The guide *send lien notices from the Lien desk* says under the Monthly notices and Lien affidavits tabs that a started paper stays listed, whatever its day.

## Not changed

- No database function: the readers already take any `p_within_days`.
- `LIEN_DESK_LEAD_DAYS` stays 30 and every other reader of it (the lien calendar's draft band, the supplier dates, the timeline book, the GC picker's line) reads as before.
- A started paper further out lands under **Coming up** on To do by its day, as any row more than seven days out does (`groupOf` in `lienNextUp.ts`); one waiting on the leader goes to his group.
