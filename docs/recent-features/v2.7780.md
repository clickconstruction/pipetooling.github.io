# v2.7780 — Lien desk: the owner-records door is a row of the call window, and the title bar is one line (2026-11-18)

The owner's ask of 2026-11-18, with a screenshot of the title bar and red boxes around ☎ and *An owner asked for records ›*: *"I think an owner asked for his records is so infrequent that we can combine these two buttons where 'an owner asked for his records' can sit inside the first press of the call button. It would clean up the header well."* Two shapes were drawn: a row in the window ☎ opens, or a two-row menu under ☎. His pick: *"Build A"*, the row, so a call stays one press. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## Before

- On a computer the title bar was two lines on every view. The second held *An owner asked for records ›* alone at the right, under **Share** (v2.7311). On By paper it also held the paper tabs and the piles.
- Punch list #390's second part would have moved the door up beside § Rules where the title line had room, 1,038 px of window at its longest.

## What changed

- **The door is the last row of the window ☎ opens.** `LienCallScriptModal` takes `onOwnerRecords` and draws *Asking what we billed and what was paid?* with the press *An owner asked for records ›* (`CALL_SCRIPT_RECORDS_ASK`, `CALL_SCRIPT_RECORDS_DOOR` in `lienCallScript.ts`). The press closes the call window and opens the records window. A desk handed no records window draws no row.
- **With a paper open on By paper, it opens on that job.** `callScriptRecordsJob` in `lienCallScript.ts` answers the job of the paper in the pane (a notice, an affidavit or a retainage notice), `onOpenOwnerRecords` takes it, and `LienDeskPage` hands it to `LienOwnerRecordsModal` as `initialJobId`, the entrance the Dashboard's line already used. To do, By date and By job have no pane and the desk still holds a notice there that nobody chose, so those views hand none and the window starts at the owner picker, as the title bar's door did.
- **The title bar is one line off By paper.** `lienDeskTitleBarLayout` has no `recordsDoor` and its answer no `door`. `lienDeskTitleLineFit` answers `all` or `none`: the four presses end the title line or they are the second line. `LienDeskTitleLineParts` has no `door` or `toolsGap`.
- **The longest title line needs 829 px of window**, 209 less than with the door on it. `LIEN_DESK_TITLE_LINE_PARTS.tools` is 231 and `LIEN_DESK_PILE_LINE_PARTS.right` 406.6.
- **☎'s hover words name both**: *An owner is calling, or asked for our records.* Its name for a screen reader is unchanged.
- **A phone is unchanged.** The door is still a row of More. The call window carries the row there too.

## What it does not do

- A records request often comes by email, and ☎ is a phone. The guide, the hover words and the search's *owner records* word carry the new place. The press's face is still the glyph alone.
- Punch list #390's second part is replaced by this. Its first part, the Public jobs box folded, is not built.
