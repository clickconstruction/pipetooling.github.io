# v2.7807 — The pace card: a place to keep a finish date (2026-11-18)

Punch list #396, PR 3a of the train. One migration, `20261118110000_job_finish_calls.sql`. No client change and no edge function.

## Why

The owner asked for a press on the superintendent's pace card where he reports when he thinks the job will finish. That word needs a place of its own: the planned finish is the office's, frozen at award, and the day *at this pace* is worked out from hours. This PR is the ground. The press, **My finish date**, follows as its own PR once the table is on prod.

## What landed

- **`job_finish_calls`**: one row a saying, with the job, who said it, the day, an optional line on what is in the way, and the planned finish as it stood then. Never changed and never deleted, so the newest row a person has on a job is his word today and the rest is the history.
- **Who.** A person writes his own row on a job he can read, and reads his own rows. Anyone who can read the job reads what was said on it. Both rules ask `jobs_ledger` under the caller's own row-level security.
- **It moves nothing.** No function reads it into the planned finish, the pace line or the score.
- The four fences stand on the table.

`docs/migrations/20261118110000_job_finish_calls.md` has the columns and the rules.

## Why a table and not a job note

A finish date posted as a note would need no migration. But the card has to show his date beside the plan's, the Projects board has to show it to the office, and a one-tap *Yes, still Friday* would fill a job's feed with notes. A day that is drawn and compared wants a column.
