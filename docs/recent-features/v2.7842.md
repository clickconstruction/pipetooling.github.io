# v2.7842 — Materials on site: a watch tells you (2026-11-18)

The eighth and last PR of the train on seeing what one person does with the material on a site. One migration, `20261118200000_person_watch_notices.sql`, one new edge function, `watch-notify-dispatch`, and the words on the Person desk's control.

## What landed

- **`watch-notify-dispatch`**, a cron function, every five minutes. For each live watch with someone on its list it reads what the watched person did since the watch began (three days back at most) and sends what is new. See [`EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`](../EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md#watch-notify-dispatch).
- **Three notices, never one a press** ([`_shared/watchNotice.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/watchNotice.ts), pure, tested through the app's door `src/lib/people/watchNotice.ts`): the first act of the day; each act outside the process, more than two in one run going as one push; the day's count after 6 p.m. company time.
- **Once only.** `person_watch_notices` takes a notice's key before its push goes, unique a watch, so two runs never send one twice.
- **The control** now reads **Tell me when they press** and says what is sent.

## Deploy order

1. `supabase functions deploy watch-notify-dispatch`
2. `supabase db push` for `20261118200000_person_watch_notices.sql`, which schedules the job.

The function reads `person_watches` and `job_container_changes` (v2.7825), so those are on prod first.

## The limits

- A push can trail a press by up to five minutes.
- A person outside their Push me hours is not pushed. The notice is still marked sent, and the held row is in the notification history.
- The list is the dev who set the watch. Adding other people to it is not on a screen yet.
- There is no Needs you line on the Dashboard. The push and the By person look carry it.
- The function could not be type-checked on this machine, which has no Deno. CI links it (`check:edge-bundles`), and its first run on prod is read from its logs.

## Tests

`watchNotice.test.ts`: what is outside the process, an act as one line, the first act once, each outside act once, several as one push with every act settled, an act that landed late, the evening line once and never on a day with no act.
