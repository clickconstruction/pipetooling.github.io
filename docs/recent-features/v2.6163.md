# v2.6163 — The GC customer's Money tab as one bar, a prototype to compare (punch list #217, outside-2) (2026-10-30)

Punch list #217, *Show it, don't say it*: the outside scan's second finding (`.claude/sessions/scans/show-not-tell/findings/outside.md` → outside-2). `/portal?t=sample-owner` → Money heads *Getting started · $0 of $187,000 so far · [empty bar] · ✓ The sum is your contract. Nothing else* over a statement asking $45,000, and after a payment *34% built · $63,580 of $187,000 invested*, where *built* is the paid share and the bar draws one part of a sum with three, so the owner cannot tell whether the $45,000 is inside the $187,000 or on top of it. On the mock-up Taunya wrote of outside-1 and outside-2: *I really like the idea of this. Could I please see this side-by-side of the before and after as a live prototype? You may need to use test data.* This is that prototype, a draft PR with no auto-merge.

## What changed

- **The kernel** (`src/lib/portal/moneyBar.ts`, new, with `moneyBar.test.ts`): `moneyBar(lang, input, words, lines)` over the same `MoneyStoryIn` the story reads: the revised sum as a length in parts — paid, with the architect, certified and due, the next pay application as our guess (`nextGuess`: the middle of its range, or its about), and to finish — each a fraction with its legend words; *34% paid* (`paidPct`), *Nothing paid yet* in month zero, the on-time count, and *Pay application 3 is our guess from the schedule: about $38,000, around Nov 25.* `moneyStory()` carries it as `bar`. The words live in the kernel's own bilingual table (`barWord`, `MONEY_BAR_WORD_KEYS`, held to the plain-words rules by its test), not in `pageWords.ts`: that table is the one the office edits on Settings → Customer page and a migration seeds (`customer_page_words`, `customerPageWords.test.ts`), and a prototype ships no migration; the build step moves them there.
- **The picture** (`src/components/portal/PortalMoneyBar.tsx`): the bar in the portal's light paper colours — paid green, with the architect pale amber, certified and due amber, the guess hatched, the rest the hairline grey — the legend under it, the on-time count and the guess, and the statement's six rows as the bar's legend. The geometry is the Legal desk's bars' (`lib/legal/moneyBars.ts`: fractions of a width, a hatch for a figure not on record); its segment kinds are the Legal desk's own, so the portal's bar has its five and not those.
- **The page** (`PortalGcPage.tsx`): `usePublicShapeView('outside-2', token)` — `?shape=1` draws the bar in the head's place; `?sbs=1` on a sample page (`t=sample…`) or for a signed-in dev draws today's head over it in the shared frame. The sample owner's page is month zero (no pay application yet), where the bar is one grey length; so on the sample's side-by-side a second frame draws today's words and the bar three months in, on made-up figures (a paid application of $63,580, $45,000 with the architect, the next one sized at $38,000), named as made up on both captions. A real visitor's page is unchanged.
- **The shared side-by-side** (punch list #217's helper): `src/components/prototypes/SideBySide.tsx` and `src/lib/prototypes/shapeView.ts` are the tally session's copy as pushed (branch `claude/217-tally-day-strip-6400df`), byte for byte; `src/lib/prototypes/publicShapeView.ts` (+ test) and `hooks/usePublicShapeView.ts` are the public pages' rule; `withSideBySide(url)` adds the flag. The **Side by side** press sits beside every page on Settings → What customers see (`SettingsWhatCustomersSeeTab`, `stepFrame.ts`). The proto-portal-dates session's branch was not pushed when this was written, so these files are this branch's copy under the agreed names; the outside-1 PR carries the same files byte for byte, so whichever lands second changes nothing in them.
- **Guides**: `see-what-a-customer-sees-on-their-portal.md` names the bar; `see-what-customers-see.md` → Open a step large names the press.

## How to see it

- A real owner's portal, as a dev: `?sbs=1` on its address; `?shape=1` draws the bar alone.

## Not done, and why

- **The next pay application** is sized on the page only when the payload carries it, and `customer-portal` does not send one yet (`customerPageModel` passes `next: null`: *the page does not size the next bill yet*). On a live portal the bar has no hatched guess until the function sends the forecast's next bill; the build step adds `next` to `PortalGcPage` from `billingForecast.ts` and a `customer-portal` deploy. The sample's three-months-in frame shows the guess on made-up figures.
- The finding's **"work in place belongs on the Schedule tab's figure"** is the Schedule tab's own change and is not here.
- The office's preview of the page (Settings → Customer page) draws today's head; the flag reads the portal's own address.
