# v2.7989 — Lien desk: Months on this job is one card, the ledger (2026-11-19)

Punch list #361. On 2026-11-15 the owner said the **Months on this job** section of a notice looked wrong and asked for a refresh worked out as a spike. He saw the before and after and asked for the card, with direction B, the ledger, as the one to build. Grace's go for the build is of 2026-11-19. Client only: no migration, no edge function, no change to a kernel.

The spike's prototype, its note and its shots were not on any branch when the build started, so this is B built from the card's seven points, laid over the section as it stood at v2.7741.

## What read wrong

The section was five boxes with four type systems: a bordered bar for the last day of work, the grid with a tinted column down its right side, the claim indented behind a vertical rule beside a column of notes, a retainage line, and the bills under dashed rules. The claim figure was drawn twice, at the foot of the notice's column and again in the claim box one row lower, and the bills' **Owed** column lined up with neither. A paragraph over the grid explained the grid on every notice. On a phone the grid slid sideways inside its frame, so the tick was off the screen.

## What changed

- **One card, one rhythm.** [`LienDeskMonths.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskMonths.tsx) draws every part as a row on the card's hairline, inset by the same half rem, so the rows share a left edge and a right edge: the last day of work, the lead line, the grid, the line that names the missed months, the money, the quiet rows. The layout is in [`src/index.css`](../../src/index.css) (`.lienLedger*`) and not inline, because an inline style beats the container rule that stacks a narrow card (the lesson of v2.7446).
- **The last day of work is the first row.** [`LienLastWorkDayLine.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienLastWorkDayLine.tsx) takes `flat`: no border, background or padding of its own. The caption, the day, the source chip, the reason and **Change ›** are as they were. Edit Job's lien row and the Lien window keep the box.
- **One lead line.** *Tick a month to put it on this notice.* stands where the paragraph was. *A check is a paper that already names the month.* follows only when an earlier paper is on the job. A notice that already went has no column to tick, so it reads the second sentence alone.
- **The grid.** Still a table, so every row shares the head's column widths. Each paper and this notice keep to their words. The month and its window share the rest, and where the card has room for a closed month on one line the window takes all of it. A window is one line where it fits: a chip only where the state changes (*mail by*, *closed*, *not noted*, *skipped*, *not a work month yet*), the rest faint words beside it. The Windows view keeps its bar of days gone. A closed month is one line, as v2.7430 made it.
- **Red means late.** A month whose window closed with nothing sent keeps its red tint and gains a red bar at its left edge (`data-missed` on the row). Nothing else on the card is red.
- **This notice stays quiet.** Its head is tinted under a blue edge and reads the name alone. A cell is tinted only when it is ticked, so a tick changes what it changes. The column no longer runs tinted from head to foot.
- **The money is one block.** [`LienClaimBox.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienClaimBox.tsx)'s read view is the total line: *Claim amount on the notice* with *Still unpaid on this job* and **Correct the claim ›** at the left, the figure at the card's right edge, under the ticks that make it. Under the figure is the foot of the column, *This notice names Aug, Sep · 2 months*, which `LienDeskMonths` hands in through `claimNode(foot)`. The `tfoot` row of v2.7430 and its second total are gone: the total is still at the foot of its column, and it is said once. The bills (`LienClaimBills`) are the lines under it, on the card's hairline in place of dashed rules, their three money columns one width so every figure shares the total's right edge. **Billed**, **Paid** and **Owed** are said over the first bill and kept for a screen reader on the rest. *Not billed yet* with **Bill it from here ›** is the last line.
- **The quiet rows close the card**, in this order: the retainage line with **Set on the job ›**, the by-hand line with **Record it…**, the several-months note, the information note, the late note, and the affidavit's state when a caller passes one. Their words are unchanged.
- **A phone.** Under 560 px of card each row is its own grid: the month over its window, an earlier paper as a mark with its letter (*A ✓*, *A ✓ as information*) under them, and the tick in a column of its own at the right edge, wide enough for a thumb. The papers' heads become lines over the rows. The bills keep **Owed** alone. The total line keeps its figure at the right, with the months it names on a line under it.
- **The empty card** says *No months on this job yet.* through `EmptyState`.
- **Help.** [`send-lien-notices-from-the-lien-desk.md`](../../src/content/help/send-lien-notices-from-the-lien-desk.md): the notice column's bullet and the paragraph under the bullets say where the claim sits now and add the phone's layout. The guide said **Preview ›** sat at the foot of the column, which the notice pane never handed; that sentence is gone.

## Not changed

- No words a first-timer reads, beyond the lead line. The by-hand line keeps its sentence as it was.
- Every hook: `data-lien-grid-row`, `data-lien-claim-bills`, `data-lien-desk-by-hand`, `data-lien-desk-note-missed`, `data-lien-claim-box`, `data-lien-months-missed`, `data-lien-months-late`, `data-lien-grid-paper`, `data-lien-grid-foot` (now on the line under the figure). `data-lien-grid-total` went with the second total.
- Every door: the tick, **Change ›**, **Correct the claim ›**, **Record it…**, **Set on the job ›**, **Note it as missed**, **Bill it from here ›**. The claim's editor and the last day's editor are the boxes they were.
- The grid's kernel (`lienMonthGrid.ts`), the claim's kernel and the bills' kernel.

## One call that went up

The card, written before v2.7430, puts the claim figure faint in the head of the notice's column. v2.7430, the office's pick of the same day, took the figure out of the head and put the total at the foot. The two disagree, so the choice went to the lead session as a question. This build keeps the head as the name alone and makes the total line the foot of the column.
