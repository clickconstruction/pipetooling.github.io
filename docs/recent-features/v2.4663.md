# v2.4663 — Submittals: a draft you have not shared is "Not sent yet", not "Waiting on the GC" (2026-10-06)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Punch list #89, item 1 (`to-dos/submittals-second-look.md`): on BP398, Rev 4 was a draft nobody had shared, and the log's first step read *Waiting on the GC · 11*, its orders section *Waiting on their answer · not ordered until they approve*, and every fixture offered **Their answer…**. Someone reading that waits for an answer that cannot come.

## What changed

- `procurementSteps(rows, draftRev)` in `lib/submittals/procurementBoard.ts`: while no line waits on the GC and some are not on the submittal (and not hand lines), the first step is labelled `NOT_SENT_LABEL` (*Not sent yet*) with *Share Rev 4 first* under it (a line sent back keeps the note; when lines the GC holds from an earlier revision stand beside new unsent ones — the live BP398, 8 Rev 3 lines and 3 new — the step keeps its name and says *3 not sent yet* under it). `procurementNextLine(rows, asOf, draftRev)` adds *Share Rev 4 first. 11 parts have not been sent.* after the GC's own sentences. `stepOnlyWords` takes the step's own label. `draftRev` is the newest revision's number while it is an unshared draft, null once shared — with null nothing is "unsent", so a shared revision's hand lines and no-product rows read as before.
- `orderSections(rows, asOf, changed, draftRev)` in `procurementOrders.ts`: a sixth section kind, `not_sent` (*Not sent to the GC yet · 2 fixtures · share Rev 4 to get an answer*), with the same fixture groups as *Waiting on their answer* but `answerItemId: null` and `right: ''` — no door, no *answer by*, and `groupDateWords` says nothing on the phone card.
- `SubmittalProcurementPanel` takes `draftRev` and hands it to the three kernels; `onCounts` carries `gcLabel` so the strip's Procure pill reads *11 not sent yet* rather than *11 waiting on the GC*. `BidsSubmittalsTab` passes `selectedRev.rev_number` while the revision's status is `draft`.
- The guide *build a submittal package* says what the step and the section read on a draft.
