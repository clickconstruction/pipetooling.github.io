# v2.7804 — Trips: see a site's deleted trips and put one back (2026-11-18)

## Why

PR 2 of 2, the screen. The owner asked whether anyone can see deleted trips and whether a delete can be undone, and said *"Yes, build it"* to keeping them and putting them back. PR 1 (v2.7800) laid the ground: a deleted trip is kept in the deleted-records archive for 90 days, and `trip_deleted_list` reads a site's own for the trips office. This PR is the fold that shows them and the press that puts one back.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/trips/tripDeleted.ts`**, the kernel, pure. `parseDeletedTrips` reads the function's reply into trips ready for their own save, passing over anything that is not a trip with an id. `deletedTripLine` says one in words: *Trip 3 · Nov 9 to 11*, what for, who went, *Deleted Nov 18 by Dana*, *kept until Feb 16*, and *A job it named is gone since.* when one is.
- **`readDeletedTrips`** in `tripsIo.ts`, and **`useSiteTrips`**: the site's deleted trips are read beside its trips, for the office door. The hook hands back `deleted`, `deletedKnown` and `deletedFailed`. A delete reads them again. A trip the site holds again, as after an Undo, is not also listed.
- **The fold** on `DevelopmentTripsLook`, under the rows, for a reader who can write: **Deleted trips · 2**, closed until pressed. Open, it says how long a trip is kept, then each trip with **Put back**. A read that failed says so in the fold's place.
- **Put back** in `DevelopmentTripsCard` is the hook's own `save` on the listed trip, the door the Undo of a delete uses. The toast says *Trip 3 is back.* A save that is refused says its reason.
- **The Trips look shows on a site whose only trip was deleted** (`tripsLookShows` is handed the kept and the deleted together). Without that, a near site's one deleted trip would have had no screen to come back from. The page's door to a first trip waits for the deleted read, so it never shows and then gives way to the look.
- **The delete's question** adds *After that it waits under Deleted trips for 90 days.* after *You can undo it for a minute.* On a trip that holds a line it now says *Undo or Put back brings the trip back, but the line is no longer tied to it.*
- **Docs**: the guide *keep a trip to a site away from home* gains *Put a deleted trip back*. `docs/GLOSSARY.md` names the fold and the press.

## Order

It calls `trip_deleted_list`, so it merged after PR 1's migration was pushed. Until then the read fails, the fold is not drawn, and a quiet line says the deleted trips did not read.

## Decisions

- **One more small read on the Development page, for the office.** The deleted trips are read with the trips, not only once the look shows. A site near the shop whose only trip was deleted shows no look until that read says a deleted trip is there.
- **No confirm on Put back.** It destroys nothing, and the trip can be deleted again.
- **No "delete forever" press.** The archive's daily purge takes a deleted trip after 90 days, and the fold says the day.
- **The fold is closed until pressed**, so a site's old mistakes do not sit above its next trip.

## Left as it is

- A trip's line on a job is not tied again when the trip is put back. The delete's question and the guide say so.
- A trip held by a job with no development has no fold, since no screen shows a job's own trips. A dev can restore it from Recently deleted.
- The dev's Recently deleted names a trip's bundle by the start of its id.
