# v2.8639 — Accounts Receivable: a check that comes back knows its lien releases (2026-11-26)

Piece 6 of punch list #435 (`to-dos/lien-payoff/README.md`), built before piece 5 on the answer of 2026-11-26. The card's guard: *a check comes back after the release is filed*. Until now the returned-check case (punch list #76) knew the jobs a check paid and nothing of the liens filed on them, and a saved release kept its **Record the county’s stamp ›** press while the bank said its check had come back. Client only. No migration, no edge function, and nothing here writes.

## What it does

**In the case** (Accounts Receivable → *Came back* → a case). Under what the check costs, a box named **Liens on this money** lists every live filed lien on the check's jobs. Jobs with no filed lien draw nothing, which is most cases. Each lien is in one of three cases:

| Case | Read from | The chip | It says |
|---|---|---|---|
| None printed | no release saved, or one saved with no printed copy | *On record*, or *Release held* | *No release has been made. The lien stays on record until a payment holds.* or *A release was saved Nov 25 and never printed. Do not print it until a payment holds.* |
| Printed | a printed copy of a release on the job since the lien was filed, the county's day not on the release | *Do not record* | *A release was printed Nov 25 by Alex Rivera. Do not record it. Find the paper and keep it back from the county.* |
| Recorded | the release carries the county's day | *Released, money came back* | *The county recorded the release Dec 3 as 2026-0431955. The app cannot undo that. Ask counsel what to do.* |

One line over the liens sums them: *1 release is recorded on check 1184. Ask counsel.*, *2 releases are printed on check 1184. Do not record them.*, or *No release has left the office. 2 filed liens stay on record.* The box is red when a release has left and amber when none has. Each lien has **Open the job ›**.

**The check's mark.** A check typed once in Payoffs (piece 3) is one payment a bill, each with the check's number and day, and none is linked to the bank's deposit. The case's own match takes a hand-typed payment only when it is for the whole check, so a payoff check spread over three bills came back *on no job*. The box now reads the mark: the unlinked check payments that carry the deposit's own check number, typed within sixty days of it, that add up to no more than the check. Their jobs join the case's jobs. Such a lien adds a red line, *Check 1184 still counts as paid on this job. Take it off on the job’s Bill tab.*, because the case's **Take it off** does not reach a payment it does not know.

**On the Lien desk.** While the bank says a job's last payment came back:

- a saved release in Payoffs reads *The release was made Nov 24. Do not record it.*, with **Open the bills ›** in place of **Record the county’s stamp ›**;
- a recorded one reads *Released, money came back. The county recorded it Nov 26 as 2026-0431955.*, in red, with *The county’s record stands. Ask counsel.*, and its place stays in the open list and out of the *Released* fold;
- the place's card turns its *Recorded at the county* stop red (*Do not record. The check came back.*), sends no copies, and its line names how many releases are already recorded;
- the desk's Payoffs line reads *1 release rests on a check that came back.*;
- the Release of record tab shows the same words in red over the county's stamp. The stamp's two values stay open: a stamp the clerk already gave is a fact to keep.

## How it is built

- [`payoffReturned.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/payoffReturned.ts) (new, 23 tests): `payoffReturnedView` gives the three cases and the line, `returnedReleaseCase` picks the case, `paymentsOnReturnedCheck` reads the check's mark, `returnedCaseJobs` names each job once (it still counts the check, it was typed there by hand, or the check came off), and `releaseOnReturnedCheck` is the desk's one flag. It imports nothing of `lienPayoff.ts` but types, so that file can read the flag.
- [`payoffReturnedIo.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/payoffReturnedIo.ts) (new): up to five small reads when a case opens, the bank's row of the deposit, the marked payments, the filings on the jobs, the jobs' names and the printed copies. A case on no job stops after two reads and jobs with no filed lien stop after three.
- [`useReturnedCaseLiens`](../../src/hooks/useReturnedCaseLiens.ts) (new, 4 tests): read once a case, again when its jobs change as after a take off, and never for a case that is not a check that came back.
- `ArReturnCasePane` takes `liens` and draws the box (5 new render tests). `BankPaymentsModal` gains three lines: the import, the hook and the prop.
- `lienPayoffStep` sets `moneyBack` on a saved or recorded release whose last payment the bank sent back, `lienPayoffStrip` counts it, `lienPayoffCard` reads it, and `LienPayoffs` keeps such a place open (5 kernel tests, 2 card tests, 2 render tests).
- `releaseMoneyBackWords` in `lienReleaseOfRecord.ts` gives the tab its line (2 kernel tests, 2 render tests).

**Printed** is read from the copy a print files: `sent_documents`, kind `lien_release_of_record`, by job. A copy names its job and not its lien, so a print on a job counts for each of that job's liens the county has not released, from the day the lien was filed. When the copies cannot be read, a saved release reads as printed and the box says so.

## Where it stops short of the card

1. **No press opens a matter for counsel.** The card's guard says one press does. The app has no door from a job to a matter, and what counsel can do is the card's question 8, not answered. The recorded case says *Ask counsel what to do.* and offers **Open the job ›**.
2. **The desk forgets once the payment is off the job.** Payoffs and the tab read the bank's answer off the job's last payment. After **Take it off**, that payment is gone, and the desk falls back to the red line it had, *is still open on this job’s bills*. The case in Accounts Receivable is what remembers, for as long as it is open. A mark kept on the release row would outlast both, and is a write: put to the person as a question, not built.
3. **The case's Take it off does not reach a payment found by the mark.** That is the database's function (`take_returned_check_off_jobs`) and its match (`list_ar_return_cases`), which take a hand-typed payment only for the whole check. The box names each such job instead. Mending it is a migration, so it is the database lane's.
4. **A downloaded PDF is not a print.** Only **Print for notarization** files a copy. A release saved and sent out as a PDF reads *Release held*.
5. **Only a check that came back.** A check the bank could not read and a typed check that was never deposited did not come back: each is deposited again, and their cases draw no box. A card dispute draws none either.
6. **A print cannot be tied to one of two liens on a job.** Both read *Do not record*. That is the cautious reading.
