# v2.6103 — Legal desk: How much? adds only the retainage no sent bill carries (punch list #213, PR 2) (2026-10-29)

Punch list #213 (`to-dos/legal-desk-two-readings.md`), finding 1. A read of a live account on the Legal desk on 2026-10-29 found *How much?* too high by the job's retainage: the bill had gone out for the whole amount, the GC had paid it short by the retainage, and the desk read *Open bills $X inside it, plus retainage held $Y* with the retainage counted in both.

## Why

Since v2.5849 (punch list #171, step 2) the claim is the open bills plus every job's `jobs_ledger.lien_retainage_held`. That is right when the bills went out net of retainage, as the AIA window's bill does (`current_payment_due`): the retainage then sits on no bill. On a bill sent for the whole amount the open remainder after the GC's short payment is the retainage, and the lien details hold the same figure, so the claim carried it twice and *What's left for us?* worked the firm's cut from the inflated figure.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/legal/legalClaimRetainage.ts`** (new, pure): `claimRetainage(job, applications)` says what part of a job's held retainage the sent bills already carry, and `claimRetainageForAccount` sums the account. A bill tied to a pay application (`job_pay_applications.invoice_id`) carries that application's period retainage when its amount is above the application's payment due by that much (a bill sent for the whole amount); a bill that equals the payment due carries none; between, the difference. A later application whose retainage to date dropped is the release, and its sent bill carries the drop. The release bill (`jobs_ledger_invoices.retainage_release`), once sent, carries the figure up to its amount. A bill the GC paid short by exactly what is still held carries it — a sent bill with money on it whose open remainder (`legalJobMoney`'s `openByInvoice`) equals the figure to the cent, or several whose remainders add up to it; this is the live account's shape, found on the dev server after the first cut: no pay application, a hand-made bill for the whole draw paid 90%, the typed figure its remainder. A bill tied to an application or marked as the release is read by its own rule, never twice. A draft bill carries nothing, so nothing leaves the claim early; an application with no bill, or whose bill the job no longer lists, carries nothing. What the bills carry never exceeds the figure held. What it cannot tell: a hand-made bill for the whole amount on a job with no pay application while the GC has paid nothing on it, or paid it short by some other figure — the typed figure is added there, and the sentence names it.
- **`legalPacket.ts`**: `LegalPacketInput.payApplications` (optional; an older caller adds every held dollar as before); `LegalWorth` gains `retainageOnBills` and `retainageAdded`, and `claim = balance + retainageAdded`. `useLegalPacketData.ts` reads the jobs' live applications (`job_pay_applications`: the number, the retainage to date, the payment due, the bill) as one more source.
- **`deskQuestions.ts`** `howMuch`: *Open bills $X inside it, plus retainage $Z that no bill asks for yet.* — and, when part is on a bill, *Another $W of the retainage held is already on a bill.*; when every dollar is on a bill the balance leads with no chip and *The retainage held $Y is already inside the open bills.* The office print reads the same words.
- **Tests**: `legalClaimRetainage.test.ts` (17 cases: nothing held, the typed figure, the release bill sent, paid, drafted, short and over, a net bill, a whole-amount bill paid short, a bill between and above, two periods, the final application's release through a sent and a draft bill, no bill, a bill the job no longer lists, the cap at the figure held, strings and cents; the short-paid rule on the live account's shape, a bill short by another figure, a bill nobody paid on, money not read, two draws adding up, a tied or release bill never read twice, the rule reading what the others left; the account sum); `legalPacket.test.ts` (the whole-amount bill paid short reads the balance as the claim, the net bill adds the retainage, a bill short by another figure adds it as before, the live account's three bills); `deskQuestions.test.ts` (the three sentences and their plain words).
- **Docs**: the Glossary's Legal desk entry and punch list #171's status say so in place; the guide *review a collections account before it goes to your attorney* (the How much? bullet).

No migration, no edge function.
