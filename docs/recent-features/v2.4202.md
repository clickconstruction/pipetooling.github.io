# v2.4202 — Pricing solves the base; Labor splits materials too (2026-09-30)

Alternates round two, PR 6 (after [v2.4199](v2.4199.md)). No migration, no edge function. Round one gave the Workbench a second scoreboard line but the solver still priced every row, so "get the base to $50,000" meant hand-holding the alternate's rows; Labor's card split hours but said *materials split the same way on Takeoffs*.

## What changed

- **The kernel** — [`workbenchSolveScope.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/workbenchSolveScope.ts): `WorkbenchSolveScope` (`'base' | 'whole' | { alternate }`), `scopeWorkbenchRows(rows, scope, tags, overhead)` — the rows in scope and the overhead they carry pro rata by fixture cost — `effectiveSolveScope` (no alternates → whole; a vanished alternate → base), `solveScopeLabel`. Tests beside it.
- **The Workbench** ([`BidsPricingTab`](../../src/components/bids/BidsPricingTab.tsx)): `wbSolveScope` (default `'base'`); `runWorkbenchSolve` hands the solver the scoped rows and overhead, so a margin solve means the scope's margin and the slider syncs to the scope's cost after a target solve; `workbenchScopeCost()` is the target's floor (*Target must beat our cost*). Inside the open solver ring, before Margin, a **Solve for** row (`data-testid="workbench-solve-scope"`) of pills — Base · + <alternate> · Whole bid — shown only when the bid has an alternate with rows. Rows outside the scope are not in the solve at all, so they keep their prices; the strip's second line moves as you drag.
- **The Labor card** ([`BidsLaborNewView`](../../src/components/bids/BidsLaborNewView.tsx)): the engine's cost-estimate load keeps each count row's takeoff materials (`costEstimateFixtureMaterials`, rough model — the same number the Pricing load keeps per row) and [`BidsLaborTab`](../../src/components/bids/BidsLaborTab.tsx) hands it down as `materialsByCountRowId`; the card sums it by the Count Sheet's scope (`sumByAlternate`) into a **Materials** row and ends with **Direct cost** per column (labor at the rate + the column's hour-share of driving and the other direct costs + its materials). Without takeoff lines (or on the PO model) the old footnote stays.
