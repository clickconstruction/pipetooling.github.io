# v2.6516 — Settings → Jobs & billing under six headings, the forms folded, a jump strip (punch list #261, PR 1) (2026-11-04)

Grace, 2026-11-04: *there is so much going on in that settings category we could potentially clean it up a little better.* The card `to-dos/settings-jobs-billing-tidy/` (PR #7035) inventoried the tab's 28 cards in the order drawn, who sees each and what it sets, and read the usage ledger: 29 minutes on the tab in 60 days by one person, and no count for any card, because no Settings card wrote a press. This PR builds the card's recommended defaults.

## What changed

- **The kernel** (`src/lib/settings/jobsBillingGroups.ts`): the six headings in order with a short name for the strip and a line each; every card's slug, anchor (`settings-jobs-<slug>`; the law firms keep `settings-legal-firm`, which the Legal desk has linked since v2.4711), title, line, who sees it (`dev`, `dev-master`, `dev-master-office`, `money-team`) and whether it folds (call 1: a switch stays open, a form folds — 7 open, 21 folded). `jobsBillingGroupsFor(role)` is what the page draws, `jobsBillingAnchorToTab()` what the deep links register, `jobsBillingCardIsTarget` what opens a card, `jobsBillingActiveGroup` what the strip lights. The child anchor `#who-counts-as-what` maps to the rate card.
- **The page**: `Settings.tsx` builds one element per card (the dev-only cards for a dev alone) and hands them to `SettingsJobsBillingGroups`, which draws the strip (`stagesStageBar` classes as Messages reuses them, one wrapping line under 760 px, `--settings-jobs-jump-offset` for the headings' and cards' `scroll-margin-top`), the headings and every card in a `SettingsFoldCard`: the header row with the ▶, the name and the line, the body under it when open; a switch card gets the anchor shell alone. `SettingsJobsTab.tsx` now exports `settingsJobsDevCards`, the company owner account and the labor rate by slug plus the re-assign confirm window apart; the four `…SectionOpen` states `useSettingsJobsAdmin` returns are no longer read.
- **The cards fold once**: the seventeen that folded themselves (Uncollectible, the company owner, the labor rate, the trip charges, the rate card, the markup, the city lines, the county suggestions, developments, Job Book, test reports, the three footers and the memo, the company block, Docsmit, HCP reconcile) lost their own ▶ and outer box, and the six that loaded on their toggle load on mount, which the wrapper does only when open; the four that stood open (the sub portal pay schedule, the law firms, the office county, the stage checklists) lost their box and title, which the fold header now carries.
- **A folded card opens for its link** (`src/hooks/useSettingsCardOpen.ts`, `src/lib/settingsTarget.ts`): the card starts open when the address's hash is its anchor or a child anchor; `pollScrollToSettingsAnchor` announces the target before it looks for the element, so the deep-link effect and a search pick open the card while the tab is mounted; `?focus=issuer.…` opens the company block whatever its fold. `resolveSettingsDeepLink` registers every card and heading anchor from the kernel and sends `#who-counts-as-what` to the tab as its own anchor.
- **Every door lands on its card**: the Settings search entries that named the tab gain their `anchorId` and the cards with no entry get one (the door to nothing, *Dispatch groups*, goes); the five dev doors on the tab point at their card and gate on the kernel's row; Contracts & terms' two *edit* doors, What the team sees' GC notices door and the Development page's Edit land on theirs.
- **The folds count**: each fold header carries `data-navtrack="settings-jobs" data-navbuttons` and the card's slug as `data-nav`, recorded as the viewer, so Settings → Usage reads *Jobs & billing card* with `#rates`, `#stage-checklists` and the like.
- **Tests**: `jobsBillingGroups.test.ts` (28 cards, 6 headings, the fold rule, each role's view, the anchor map, the target rule, the lit heading), `SettingsJobsBillingGroups.render.test.tsx` (every anchor lands and opens its card, the child anchor, the forced open, the counted press, each role's headings and strip), `settingsDeepLink.test.ts` (the anchors and the child), the render smokes of the converted cards drive the wrapper's header or render the card open.
- **Docs and guides**: `docs/SETTINGS_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md` → 3. Jobs & billing in the new order; `docs/PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`'s Settings line; the Settings guide gains *Jobs & billing*; the four guides that said *scroll to* or *expand* say *press*, with the heading.
