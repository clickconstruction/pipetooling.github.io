# v2.5021 — Stages: the last stage's bill takes the discount with it (2026-10-09)

Client only. No migration, no edge function. This builds Stage Plan item 3 on the owner's call of 2026-10-09: **Bill it** on the last Order stage sweeps the remaining plain rows into that final invoice. Since v2.3696 the only plain rows left are the discounts, which `syncDiscountRows` keeps off every stage.

## What the sweep is, and is not

A discount's dollars already ride every draw that bills its basis rows (v2.3252). `lineDollars` counts a discount row as 0 and every work row net of its share, and each bill prints its share as a labeled negative line. So the sweep moves no money.

What stayed wrong was the row itself. The discount never landed on a bill, so the plan read it *not a stage · no draw* after the job was billed out. The sweep links it to the final draw's invoice. The plan then reads it *ready to bill*, then *billed* and *paid* with that bill.

A linked discount row adds nothing to the bill. The bill's lines skip `line_kind = 'discount'` both in `stripeInvoiceItemsFromFixtures` and in the client mirror `invoiceScopedFixtures`. Its amount is the selection's net, as before.

## The change

- **`finalDrawDiscountSweep`** (`src/lib/jobs/stagePlanForm.ts`) returns the discount rows to link when Bill it runs on a row.
  - The row must be an Order stage, not yet billed, with no other Order stage still open. That makes it the final draw.
  - Each unbilled discount whose basis is fully billed once this bill goes out is swept. A discount whose basis still holds an unbilled row, an Any-time line say, keeps its row open, because a share of it still rides that later bill.
- **`fixtureSequencePositions`** (`src/lib/jobs/jobSegmentsCoverage.ts`) gives those rows' saved positions, whatever their dollars. It follows the same position rule as `selectedSegmentSequencePositions`.
- **`useJobFormInvoiceActions`**:
  - `billStageRow` passes the sweep to `createInvoiceFromSegmentIds(selection, alsoLink)`. The link write covers the stage and the discounts, and the form mirrors both.
  - The toast adds *· the discount closes on this final bill*.
- *split a job into stages*: the discount bullet says Bill it on the last in-order stage puts the discount on that final bill.
