# v2.5391 — Messages: who answers a customer's reply, behind the scenes (2026-10-12)

Messages, residential R5, the who-answers kernel (`RESIDENTIAL_BUILD_PLAN.md` on `spike/gc-mode`, R5 and the owner's decision 4 in § 7). It is lifted from the residential prototype v0.8: screen 2's sheet and preview, *Who gets the reply*, and the pill `Open · Luis until 5 PM`. It adds one new pure file, `src/lib/messages/hasRule.ts`, with its test. No screen, table or migration.

- **The rule** (`whoHasReply(input, now)`): a reply goes to the tech who last texted the person from a job, until the tech's hold ends. Then an open office has it: `whoGetsIt`'s pick, or whoever answers first. While the office is closed nobody has it. The row waits on Needs a reply, and `until` is the next opening. The tech's hold wins over the office's hours, so a tech who texts before 7:30 AM or on a Saturday hears the answer. It returns `{ has, userId, name, until, reason, tech, autoAnswer, write, now }`. `write` uses `contacts`' own columns: `has_user_id`, `has_since`, `has_how = 'rule'`, and R5's `has_until`, which arrives in R5's SQL PR.
- **The hold** (`techHoldUntil`): it runs to 5 PM Chicago on the day of the text. A text sent after 5 PM, on an evening call, holds until midnight that day. The caller can pass its own `hasUntil`.
- **The office's hours** (`app_settings.messages_office_hours_v1`, read by `parseOfficeHours`): Monday to Friday, 7:30 AM to 5 PM, the tech until 5 PM, and no after-hours number by default. A field it cannot read takes its default. The setting arrives in R5's SQL PR. Holidays are not known, so a closed weekday reads as open until the owner asks for them.
- **After hours** (`autoAnswerDue`, `afterHoursAnswer`): the app answers once a night. A night runs from the office's last close to its next opening, so a weekend gets one answer. The caller passes `lastAutoAnswerAt`. When the owner sets an emergency number, the text ends with a leak line. The number is written 512-555-0144, because parentheses fail the plain-words test.
- **The hand-off** (`handOffDue`, `handOff`, `handOffEvent`): when the rule's hold ends, the event line says where it landed. With the office open it reads *Passed from Luis to Rosa.* With the office closed it reads *Passed from Luis to the office. It opens tomorrow at 7:30 AM.*
- **The words** (`hasPill`, `hasHover`): *Open · Luis until 5 PM*, *Open · Rosa*, *Open · the office*, *Open · waits for Mon 7:30 AM*. The closed pill's hover names the answer that went: *The app told Dana we open Monday at 7:30 AM.* Every sentence passes `plainWordsFailures`.
- **Tests** (`hasRule.test.ts`, 27, Chicago clock, Dana, Luis and Rosa): a 9:04 AM reply to Luis's 9:02 text goes to Luis until 5 PM. At 5:10 PM and at 9 PM it goes to nobody, and the app answers once that night. A Saturday reply waits for Monday. A customer with no tech text goes to the office. One test crosses the fall clock change on Nov 1. The tests pass under UTC and Tokyo time too. On purpose, an office open at 5 PM sharp failed two tests. So did an answer to every reply after hours.
