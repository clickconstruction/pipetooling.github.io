# v2.5200 — GC mode: a trade partner sees its own schedule in its portal (2026-10-10)

The schedule's PR 14b (`to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/schedule-pr14b.md`, on branch `spike/gc-mode`, with amendment 1, co-signed by gc 3): the trade's chart in its portal (G-110). No migration. **`gc-trade-portal` redeploys after it merges**; the page reads an answer without `schedules` as no chart, so either order works.

- **The copy of the kernels** (calls 1 and 2): an edge function cannot import `src/lib` as it stands, so `scripts/edge-kernels.mjs` (`npm run gen:edge-kernels`) copies into `supabase/functions/_shared/gcKernels/` the files the value imports of `portalSchedule`, `gcProjectFromRows`, `boardStateFromRows`, `withDraws` and `withScheduleRows` reach: 30 files in `src`'s layout, `.ts` added to each relative import, an import of types alone made `import type` and pointed back at `src` when outside the copy. It refuses a package import, a dynamic or side-effect import, a browser global and an import it cannot find, by file. `check:timezone` allows the copy of `src/utils/dateUtils.ts` as it allows the file; the other 29 are checked as they are in `src`. `src/lib/gc/edgeKernels.test.ts` fails when the copy differs from a regenerate, or its importers' list from the functions whose bundles reach it (15b's `customer-portal` joins in its own PR).
- **The read** (calls 3 to 5): `portalScheduleJobs` keeps the jobs being built with a trade awarded to one of the company's invites. `readScheduleRows` reads only those jobs' trades, their awarded invites and those companies' names, the awarded trades' statements of work with their draws and reports, and the bars with their links and baselines, paged past 1,000 rows. `_shared/gcTradePortalSchedule.ts` works out `portalSchedule`'s answer, sent as `schedules` beside the slice. A chart that fails to read answers `{}` and leaves the slice whole.
- **What leaves** (call 6): the company's own bars and those right before and after them, each by its company's name, dates, percent and days slipped. A neighbour's percent is its own report, never our send-back's `we_see` (`withoutOthersSendBacks`, gc 3's amendment). Our own crew as a neighbour shows its name and dates, no percent (call 7).
- **The page** (calls 8 and 9): `GcTradePortalSchedule.tsx`, the prototype's `GcPortalSchedule` drawn from the answer, first on the job's page under its name (gc 3's pick), in the company's language, with an `ownRow` slot under each own bar for the Portal's late door. The sample job has a made-up chart (`gcTradePortalSampleSchedules`).
- **Proof and boot** (call 10): on the local edge runtime v1.70.0, with only `supabase/functions` mounted, the function answers the sample with its chart and a proof function runs the copy's kernels on a two-trade job. Cold boot to a first answer went from about 1.16s to 1.32s, and a warm answer stayed about 2ms, so the chart stays in `gc-trade-portal`.
- **Tests**: `edgeKernels.test.ts`; `tradePortalSchedule.test.ts` (the jobs, the chart on a hand-built job, our crew as a neighbour, the never-sees test with a third company, every dollar, notes, contacts, a neighbour's send-back and draw amounts, and the same answer through `src`'s own builders); `GcTradePortal.schedule.render.test.tsx`; the answer reader's. Four planted bugs (a neighbour reading our send-back, a buyout job charted, `src` changed without a regenerate, the chart not drawn) each fail a test.
- **Docs**: `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`'s `gc-trade-portal` (the response, the sample, the copy, the status); `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`'s portal paragraph; `GLOSSARY.md`'s *Trade partner company*; the guide *share a trade partner its portal*.
