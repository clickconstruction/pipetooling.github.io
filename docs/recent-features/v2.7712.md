# v2.7712 — Lien desk: an approved notice that waits on a signature has its own bin, Approved · to sign (2026-11-17)

Punch list #383, option B, PR 2 of 3. PR 1 (v2.7708) put every press on its bin's title line. This one makes a bin's count and its press the same number. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## Before

- An approved notice the leader had not signed stood in *Approved · to print*, with the notices that could print. On the owner's screenshot the notices to print read 14 on the third tile, 8 on the bin and 1 on the press.
- One line under the bin's name said why: *7 wait on the leader's signature*, with *See them ›* and the office's *Remind*.

## What changed

- **A new stage, `to_sign`**, in `LienBoardNoticeStage`, between `awaiting` and `to_print`: the bin *Approved · to sign*. `noticeStageOf` places an approved, unprinted notice there while `lienItemUnsigned` says its signature does not stand. The list's pile is still *Approved · to print*: the bin is the Board's.
- **Its chip is violet**, as every paper that waits on the leader is, and opens the notice itself, where it is signed. The run holds an unsigned notice, so the run is no longer its door.
- **Two stages left where it had one**: *the leader signs it*, then the print. So an unsigned notice close to its day now reads as at risk a working day sooner, and the day tile's red line counts it.
- **The third tile counts `to_print` and `in_mail`**, the two bins under it. It counted every approved notice by pile, the unsigned ones too. They are the second tile's alone now.
- **The bin's press** (`boardBinPress` in `LienDesk.tsx`): the office's reminder, the same press as To do's waiting rows carry, as an outline. On a master's desk it is *Sign N notices ›* (`lienBoardSignDoor`), filled, the door to the approved notices on By paper.
- **The notices' print has no line.** `lienBoardNoticePrint` no longer words what waits on a signature or takes the leader's name, and `LienBoard`'s `prints.remind` is gone. The pair beside the find box still says *7 wait*.
- **A GC's run row on the list** stands in *Approved · to sign* while every one of its notices waits on a signature, and in *Approved · to print* once one can print (`lienBoardRowBin`).
- **The second tile's pick** lights the new bin with the two awaiting ones (`lienBoardLeaderPick`).
- An arrow runs into the new bin and out of it, since the flow's arrows are drawn from `NOTICE_FLOW`.

## Files

- `src/lib/jobs/lienBoard.ts`: the stage, its words, `LIEN_BOARD_SIGN_STEP`, `noticeStageOf`, the third tile, `lienBoardRowBin`, `lienBoardLeaderPick`.
- `src/lib/jobs/lienBoardPrints.ts`: `lienBoardSignDoor`; the notice side's `left` is null.
- `src/components/jobs/LienBoard.tsx`, `LienDesk.tsx`: the bin's press, `prints.remind` removed.
- Guides: `read-the-board-on-the-lien-desk`, `send-lien-notices-from-the-lien-desk`.
