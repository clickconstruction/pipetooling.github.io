# v2.7470 — Lien desk: a print at the foot of each Board column (2026-11-16)

Punch list #362. The owner, 2026-11-16, on To do: *"Do you think on the to do page we do a good enough job offering the user the tools to print batches?"* Three drawings later, on a print under each Board column, notices left and lien affidavits right: *"I like how the band separated notices on the left and liens on the right like it would match the flowchart"* and *"Yes, build this"*.

## What was wrong

- Notices printed by GC from To do: a GC's row opened the run narrowed to that GC. The whole run opened only from the Board's third tile or the title bar.
- No row said how many could print now. On the walk 7 of 15 approved notices still lacked the leader's signature.
- The affidavits' press was a door to By paper's list, where the real button stood.

## What changed

- **Kernel** `src/lib/jobs/lienBoardPrints.ts`: `lienBoardNoticePrint` and `lienBoardAffidavitPrint` turn the desk's own counts into a press, the line for what it leaves out with its door, the affidavits' *Prints with …* line, and a short form. The counts are the ones the run's door and By paper's print bar use (`counts.ready`, the unsigned among them, `counts.printed`, the ready retainage notices; `lienAffidavitPrintPlan`'s `ready`, `gated` and `held`), so the three cannot disagree. Words through `countedLabel`. A press is dead only when nothing is ready.
- **The notices' press**: *Print and mail 8 notices* opens the whole run (`openWholeRun`: no GC, every approved and printed notice). *7 wait on Malachi's signature · See them ›* opens By paper's notices at the approved pile, where each row says it is unsigned. With every approved notice printed the press reads *Enter mail numbers · 4* and opens the run on the mail numbers, as the run's own door does.
- **The affidavits' press**: *Print 10 for notarization* calls the batch print itself (`printReadyAffidavits`) from To do, with the pages the office ticked on By paper's bar (the choices are kept on the computer). It carries the print's own name, not *Print 10 affidavits* as drawn: one act, one name (Taunya's walk of 2026-11-15, v2.7398). *2 held on a property fact · See them ›* opens the affidavits list narrowed to the held papers. *Prints with the help sheet and the notice records · Change ›* opens the list at Ready to file, where the three ticks and the signing county are. That is where the print's options live.
- **`LienBoard`** takes them as `prints` and draws each at its column's foot, under a rule. It is not a stage, so no arrow leads to it. On a phone the two stand stacked above the folded bins. The leader's own bins (*Only my bins*) draw none.
- **`LienDoNowBar`** takes the pair in short form (*Print and mail 8 notices · 7 wait*, *Print 10 for notarization · 2 held*) and shows it while pinned, the notices' first.
- A find typed on To do narrows the affidavits' print as it does on By paper, and the press then says *shown*.

## What was kept, and what goes next

- **Kept**: the Board's third tile and its line, since the words PR (#8355) is renaming them. The folded row *N affidavits approved and ready to file* keeps its door to the list (v2.7456). By paper's own print bar is unchanged.
- **Not in the drawing, not built**: chips by GC, ticks on To do's rows, one print job for both kinds.
- **Next, agreed in direction by the owner the same day**: the title bar's run door is retired once this is live, with a door to the run kept on By date and By job; and the run window is redrawn with D (one count said one way, three steps, one primary press). Both are on the card.
