# v2.7563 — Forward hands on the same task from My Inbox, and tells who takes it (2026-11-17)

The follow-up v2.7548 named. Punch list #369's step C7 moved the Checklist's two Forward windows onto `task_hand_on`. The third Forward, on the My Inbox card, kept the old flow.

## Why

My Inbox's `saveFwd` inserted a new one-time task for the other person, then deleted the first day row. The row's thread went with it by the foreign key. The copy carried the title, the date and five notify and reminder fields, and none of the place, the Done when… lines, the photos, the links or the due day. So the same task forwarded from the Checklist kept its history, and forwarded from the Dashboard lost it.

The card draws on the Dashboard, in Job Mode, in Dispatch Mode and on Quickfill, so all four had the old flow.

## What changed

- **Forward is the same act everywhere.** `DashboardMyInboxCard`'s `saveFwd` calls `handOnTask` (`task_hand_on`), as Today's and Review's do. Every task in the card is the viewer's own, so no one is named as who it comes from.
- **One window, in its own file**: `TaskForwardWindow` (`src/components/checklist/`). It shows the task's words and the line *The task goes as it is, with its notes, place and photos.* It takes no new title.
- **The press names who takes it**: **Forward to Hal** (`forwardPressLabel`). It is dead until a person is picked, and the list opens on *Pick a person*. The old list had no such row, so the first name looked picked while nothing was.
- **The person forwarding is not in the list** (`forwardTakers`). The function refuses a hand-on to oneself.
- **A refusal stays in the window in its words**, such as *This task belongs to a roadmap. It is handed on from the Roadmap.* The old flow wrote its error on the card behind the window.
- A task that repeats is forwarded for that day alone, as on the Checklist. The old flow turned that day into a new one-time task.
- **Whoever takes it gets a push, from all three Forwards** (the owner's call, 2026-11-17). `tellForwardTaker` in `taskDoerActsIo.ts` sends Hand it on's own words, *Theo handed you "Sort the fittings wall".*, through `tellTaskGiver`. It is called once the hand-on has landed and never holds the window. Today's and Review's `saveFwd` in `Checklist.tsx` call it too, and all three windows' sentence now ends *Whoever takes it gets a message.* Forward told nobody until now, on any screen.

## How it is built

- `FORWARD_KEEPS_WORDS`, `FORWARD_TITLE`, `FORWARD_PICK_LABEL`, `forwardTakers` and `forwardPressLabel` in `src/lib/tasks/taskDoerActs.ts`. `Checklist.tsx` reads the sentence from there.
- No database change. `task_hand_on` has been on prod since 2026-11-17 (`20261117120000_task_doer_acts.md`).

## Not in this PR

- **The Checklist's two Forward windows are still drawn in the page.** They make the same call and can take `TaskForwardWindow` in a later step. Review's needs one more value, whose task it is.
- **Forward tells the taker alone.** The doer's **Hand it on** also tells whoever gave the task. From Review, the person the task leaves is not told either.
