# v2.4915 — GC mode: the rules for changing our contract with the customer (2026-10-08)

Owner Billing's O3 in the GC mode real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/OWNER_BILLING_REAL_BUILD.md`; its plan `to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/owner-billing-o3.md`, both on branch `spike/gc-mode`). The migration is `supabase/migrations/20261008110000_gc_change_order_rpcs.sql`, documented in `docs/migrations/20261008110000_gc_change_order_rpcs.md`. The mockup's SQL is used byte for byte.

- **Four RPCs on O1's `gc_change_orders`:**
  - `gc_draft_change_order` (the next number under the project's lock; not while bidding or lost);
  - `gc_draft_time_extension` (G-141: no price, its days from this job's moves, not undone, not asked for twice);
  - `gc_send_change_order`;
  - `gc_answer_change_order` (signed or declined, by the office or later from the customer's portal).
- **A trigger, `gc_change_orders_keep_what_went`:** a change order steps only forward. Once sent it keeps its words, money, days and trade, and once answered its answer stays. Only a draft deletes, while a project's cascade still goes through.
- **`src/lib/gc/changeOrderRows.ts`:** a change order's row read back as the kernels' `ChangeOrder`.
- **`ChangeOrder`'s money fields:** `ChangeOrder` gains `price` and `pctDone`, written again with the lift tools (`owner-billing-o3.lift.json`, on the union of the earlier lifts' fields). Four direct tests' change orders gain the two fields, and no expectation moves: Building's, the Portal's, the schedule's moves, and O2a's.
- **The window comes next.** The Change orders window, its io and its guide are O3-ui, after this migration's types.
