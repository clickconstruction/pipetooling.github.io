# v2.8282 — Materials on a phone: Track opens On site, one Print press, and Add a part on the desk (2026-11-22)

The owner's approved phone design for the Materials page's Track → On site screen. No migration. A desktop draws what it drew.

## What was wrong

On a phone the strip drew three group names and, under them, the open group's tabs. Track holds one tab, so a person pressed **Track** and then pressed **On site**. Track's row also carried Scan a label, Print labels and Print future stickers as worded pills, and the third ran off the screen. On a job's page the four figures and their arrows wrapped to two rows, four more worded presses took two rows over the boxes, and the reserved stickers took five.

## What changed

- **Track opens On site at once.** In the strip's row of names a group with exactly one tab is opened by its name and draws no tab row (`groupOnlyTab`, `oneTabGroupLabel`). Plan and Purchase hold several tabs and are as they were.
- **Scan and print are two icons.** While Track is open, two 40 px squares sit at the right end of the names: **Scan a label**, then **Print** (`materialsPhonePresses`, drawn with `MaterialsPressGlyph` at 18 px). A role with no press on On site's row has none.
- **A phone always draws the row of names.** `stripFit` takes `rows`, set by the page from `useNarrowViewport640`, so the icons are there even for a role whose strip would fit one row.
- **The Print sheet** (`lib/materials/printSheet.ts`, `MaterialsPrintSheet.tsx` on the On site tab's own `Sheet`, through a portal on the body). With a job open it is titled *Print for job 224* with the job's name under it, and its rows are **Labels for its boxes**, **The manifest**, each reserved run as its line prints it, **Future stickers** and **Print for another job**. The labels and the manifest are dead with *This job has no box yet.* on a job with no box. A role that may not print a run sees the runs and Future stickers dead with `STICKER_MINT_WHO`. With no job open it is titled *Print* and holds the strip's own print presses. Every row opens a window that was there before.
- **Who draws the sheet.** The page holds one flag. With a job open the job's section (`JobView`) draws the sheet, since the boxes, the reserved runs and the windows are read there (`MaterialsPrintSheetDoor`). With none the page draws it.
- **Four tiles** (`FlowTiles` in `FlowRow.tsx`, `flowTileWord`): the figures in four equal columns, the number over its word, no arrows. A tile keeps its colour, its badge at the top corner, its outline when open, its `title` and its press.
- **One row under the tiles**: List / By part / Map as one joined control while On site is open, and **+ Container** (`aria-label` *Add a container*, the same test id) on the right while the list is the view.
- **The job's print presses leave the phone.** Print labels, Print future stickers and Print the manifest are in the sheet.
- **Reserved stickers are one line** (`reservedShortWords`: *58 stickers reserved, G93 to G150*) that opens a sheet with the full sentence, **These are on boxes**, **Runs** and the presses that print them.

## Add a part on the desk

A master technician counts as office, so the app sends him to this desk on his phone. The desk had no way to put a part into a box, and a crew lead hit that dead end on 2026-11-22. The Edit window's Contents tab said *Add a part on the box itself* and offered no press.

- **Add a part on every live box's card**, at every width (`materials-box-add-part`, `aria-label` *Add a part to G100*). It opens the sheet the Tally's On site tab opens. `AddPartSheet` and its `Stepper` moved out of `TallyOnSiteTab.tsx` into `tally/AddPartSheet.tsx` as written, and the Tally imports them back.
- **The same row the Tally writes** (`lib/materials/addPart.ts`, `addPartMovement`): `delivered` into the box, from nowhere, note *added on site*, signed by the signed-in person, through `onSiteStore.addMovement`. A write that throws shows its reason through `formatErrorMessage` and leaves the sheet open. The parts book search is `addPartIo.ts` (`searchPartsBook`), the Tally's query with no service type.
- **The Contents tab's empty state is a press.** `BoxTabs` takes `onAddPart`. With it the tab reads *Nothing in G100 yet. Add a part puts the first one in.*, and the press closes the Edit window and opens the sheet on that box. With no handler the words are as they were.
- **A new box stays in sight.** After **Add <name>** the filter goes back to All and the new box's card (`data-box-card`) is scrolled into view. The toast still reads *<name> added.*

## What did not change

- A desktop, and Jobs → Materials at every width: that mount has no strip and so no Print sheet, and it keeps its presses.
- `FlowRow`, `ReservedPrintPresses` and the Tally's On site tab.
- Every window, word constant and test id the windows own.
