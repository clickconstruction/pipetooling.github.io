# v2.1918 — Bid Board Health Old/New views: the Estimating Pulse (2026-08-20)

The Health section at the bottom of Bid Board gets the Old/New pills
(localStorage `bid_board_health_view_v1`, default Old). Old is the classic
weekly pivot + band legend + sliders + Scoreboard tables, byte-identical. New
is the **Estimating Pulse** — the same dataset rendered once, in three layers,
with every number drilling down to its bids:

1. **The pulse** — five stat cards (Sent, Last 4 weeks, Won rate by count,
   Won rate by $, Still waiting) over a stacked weekly bar chart (last 26
   weeks, oldest → newest, √-scaled heights). Each bar splits by the bids'
   *current* outcome (won incl. started / waiting / lost); clicking a bar
   opens that week's bid list (reuses `BidBoardWeeklySentCellModal`).
   Axis: `W##` per bar + month labels only at month changes.
2. **Where everyone stands** — the five interpretation bands drawn once as a
   shared field; per-person markers (full-name pills below the bar, staggered
   when close) on their combined record across both roles, plus a dark ALL
   company marker. Dashed = under 5 decided bids.
3. **People** — one card per person; Estimator and Account Man rows on the
   same card (header/sparkline dedupe dual-role bids), collapsed single-line
   role stats with clickable W / L / ⏳ chips and won % by count and by $.

New kernel `src/lib/bids/estimatingPulse.ts` (+9 tests): outcome classify,
window weeks incl. empty ones, stats, person rows with per-role tallies and
union dedupe, band stagger, money formatting. Outcome semantics identical to
the Scoreboard. The dev-only labor table still renders under both views.

Guides: new `read-estimating-health.md`; `read-the-bid-board.md` due-date
bullet updated for the v2.1914 quiet-once-sent rule.
