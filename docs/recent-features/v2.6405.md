# v2.6405 — The Pipeline search says when your filters hide a match (2026-11-02)

The owner's yes, after the search-by-development work could not explain their report (`docs/recent-features/v2.6354.md` → *Correction*). Hide groups and the GC, development, Account Man, contract and job kind filters run before the search (`stagesBoardLists`), so a match behind one never reached the board. With a development's group hidden, typing its name read *No match anywhere — Paid in Full included* and every section read *No jobs in … match*, with the jobs sitting behind the hide and only the *Hiding 1 group* chip to say so. Client only: no migration, no function change.

- **The kernel `src/lib/jobs/stagesSearchHeldBack.ts`.** `countStagesSearchHeldBack` runs the board's own builder over the rows the filters took off and counts the distinct jobs it would draw, so a row the board never draws (a billing-only job with no bill) is never promised and a job on two lists counts once. `stagesSearchHeldBackLines` words it: *2 matches are hidden by your filters.* with **Show them**, then *Showing 2 matches your filters hide.* with **Hide them**. `StagesSearchHeldBackHint.tsx` draws the lines under the paid hint, on the desk and the phone.
- **The press sets the filters aside for this search only.** `stagesSearchShowsHeldBack` makes `stagesBoardLists` read every job instead of `stagesJobsUnderFilters`, the memo the filter chain moved into. Clearing the search resets it. The saved hides and the filter chips are never written, so a person who searched for one job gets their board back with no presses. The judgment taken here: a hide is a saved preference, and "show me this one job" should not undo it. **Show everything** in the window stays the way to drop them for good.
- **The words that were false.** `stagesPaidSearchHint` takes `heldBackCount` and says *No match anywhere* only when nothing sits behind the filters either; the paid hint and *Checking Paid in Full…* are unchanged. `stagesGroupEmptyWords` ends a section's sentence *with your filters on* when a filter is on, so a section cannot claim no job matches. While the press is on, the sections read as they do with no filter.
- **A dev's ZZ test jobs.** They are held back earlier, by the jobs cache, so `countStagesZzHeldBack` counts the lookup's ids that are ZZ jobs (`zzTestJobIds`, new on `useJobsListCache`). The line reads *1 match is a ZZ test job.* with **Open Hide groups**, which opens the window with the switch; flipping it moves the strip, Quickfill and the Dashboard too, so the press does not flip it. Only a dev whose switch hides them has a count: `zzTestJobsSwitchLine` is null for every other role, and those roles never hear of a ZZ job.
- **Guides.** *search jobs on the Pipeline board* gains *When a filter hides a match*; *hide groups of jobs from the Pipeline board* and *group bids and jobs into a development* point at it.
- **Tests.** `stagesSearchHeldBack.test.ts` (held back in any stage, not the ones on the board, the lookup's ids, a billing-only job with and without a bill, one job on two lists, the ZZ count, every line's words, plain words); `stagesPaidSearchHint.test.ts` +1; `stagesGroupEmptyWords.test.ts` (the pin that search words ignore filters now reads the new sentence); `JobsListCacheContext`'s render test reads the ids. `JobsStagesTab.heldBackSearch.render.test.tsx` walks the wiring: five of its nine cases failed on main before the change, and the true no-match, the no-filter search, a dev showing ZZ jobs and an assistant passed on both sides.
- **Live, on the dev server against prod as the dev.** The one real development hidden on a test origin, its name typed. On main: *No match anywhere — Paid in Full included*. Here: *24 matches are hidden by your filters.*, the same 24 its rows hold on prod, and no false line. **Show them** drew 24 rows and named the 14 paid; **Hide them** took them off; clearing the search left the saved hide byte for byte and retyping started behind the filters again; a name nothing matches still read *No match anywhere*. A ZZ development's name read *1 match is a ZZ test job.* and the press opened *Hide groups from the board* at its row. At phone width the line sits inside the screen under the paid hint.
- **Not changed.** Search still works inside the filters until the press, as the guide has always said. A lookup that fails (`fetchLeanJobSearchIds` answering `ok: false`) still falls through to *No match anywhere*, which it cannot know; that is a separate fix.
