# v2.8140 — Before we start: a line of time with no day to draw says so (2026-11-20)

Punch list #382, from the audit of PR 11 (v2.8111). Client only: `DevelopmentScheduleCard.tsx` and two lines of words in `timeLine.ts`. No migration, no edge function.

## The fault: a press that did nothing

`timeLine` answers null when no stage ahead has a day to draw: no days of ours, no day of theirs, so no day to decide either. That is a site as the office first sets it up, with the lists written and nothing planned yet.

On such a site **Show it on a line of time** turned to **Hide the line of time** and the screen stayed as it was. Nothing said why.

## The fix

The Schedule look draws an `EmptyState` there, from `TIME_LINE_WORDS.nothing`: *No stage here has a day to draw yet. Plan our days on a stage below, and its row shows here.* **Plan our days** is the press each such stage's own card already carries, right under it.

## Checked

A render test on a site with two stages and no day: the press opens the words and no line, and Hide puts them away. A kernel test holds the words. The line's other tests pass unchanged.
