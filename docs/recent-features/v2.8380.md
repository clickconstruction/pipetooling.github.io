# v2.8380 — State roll: a place for our notes on a licensed plumber, and Leave off (2026-11-23)

Punch list #411, step three of the train in [`to-dos/state-roll/`](../../to-dos/state-roll/README.md), its first PR. One migration, [`20261123185500_license_roll_notes`](../migrations/20261123185500_license_roll_notes.md). No screen changes.

## Why

The roll has been kept since 2026-11-21, about 6,500 rows for five counties, and no screen shows an outside person. Step three, approved by the owner on 2026-11-23, is the Everyone view and each person's sheet, with our notes and a Leave off press. The notes need a table, and Leave off needs a way to write to `license_roll_left_off`, which step one made with no way to add to it.

## What changed

- **`license_roll_notes`**: our notes, kept by the license's number and by name and ZIP, so a note outlives the roll row. Stamped with its author and time by a trigger. The Hiring switch holders read and write; the author alone deletes; nobody edits.
- **`license_roll_leave_off()`**: writes the left-off row and takes the person's rows off at once, by number and by name and ZIP. It refuses a person who holds one of our own licenses. Their line of history goes with the rows. Our notes stay.
- **`license_roll_left_off`** gains `shown_as` and `noted_by_name`, and a delete for the same people: Put back on the list. The next pull keeps the person again.

## Verified

- A new SQL bed, `supabase/tests/license_roll_notes/` (`npm run test:pg:license-roll-notes`), on the whole schema in CI. Every name and number in it is made up.
  - A holder writes a note, and the author, name and time are the server's. A blank note and an edit are refused.
  - Another holder reads it and cannot delete it. The author can.
  - A person without the switch and a subcontractor read nothing and write nothing. A training account reads and writes nothing.
  - Leave off refuses one of our own. On another person it takes both of their rows off by name and ZIP, keeps the left-off row with who and why, drops the history and keeps the note.
  - Put back is refused to a person without the switch and to a training account, and works for a holder. `anon` cannot run the press.
