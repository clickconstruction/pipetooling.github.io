# v2.4135 — Contracts & terms: the website's Terms of Service joins the index (2026-09-29)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. The owner, 2026-09-29, after a read of every contract text: *add the housecall pro terms to the contracts catalog.*

## Why

The footer of clickplumbing.com links "Terms of Service" to `/terms`, a stub that redirects (meta refresh, JS and canonical) to a page Housecall Pro hosts, `https://pro.housecallpro.com/ClickPlumbing/712596/terms`. The page is not in the site's sitemap, and nothing in the app knew it existed — yet it says it applies to every invoice, proposal and contract, and the app's Estimate Terms and Conditions (a Settings text) is that document plus clause 11 (the biohazard fee) and a different phone number. A customer can meet it before anything else, so it belongs first on Settings → Contracts & terms.

## What shipped

- **[`websiteTerms.ts`](../../src/lib/contracts/websiteTerms.ts)** — the URL, the host, the day the copy was taken (`2026-09-29`), the wording as copied (clauses 1–10 and the signature block, verbatim), and `copiedLabel(ymd)` → *copied Sep 29, 2026*. The app cannot read the page at run time (another origin, no API); when it changes, a dev pastes the new wording here and moves the date.
- **[`customerContractCatalog.ts`](../../src/lib/contracts/customerContractCatalog.ts)** — a new area `website` (label *Website*), a new audience `public` (*Anyone on the website*), a new source kind `external` (`url`, `hostedBy`, `text`, `copiedYmd`) resolving to the new status `external` (*Hosted elsewhere*, dated by the copy), a new edit door `external` → the action `link` (*Open the live page*, plus the note saying where it is really edited), the count `external` (*N hosted elsewhere*), and the entry `website-terms` first in the catalog: group *notice* (nothing is signed), no sample surface, no route.
- **[`contractsIndex.ts`](../../src/lib/contracts/contractsIndex.ts)** — `website` first in `CONTRACT_AREA_ORDER` with its hint; the `external` lens last.
- **[`SettingsContractsTab`](../../src/components/settings/SettingsContractsTab.tsx)** — the *Hosted elsewhere* chip, the `link` action as an `<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">` pill (`contract-live-page-<key>`) with its note under the facts. Compare, Show all, the find box and Mark reviewed work on the card as on any other; there is no *Read it as the customer sees it* (no sample surface) and no history (the copy is dated by its own day).
- **Guide** `see-every-contract-side-by-side` — seven sections, the new chip, a section *The website's terms*; **`docs/GLOSSARY.md`** → *Contracts & terms* amended.

## Decided here

- **A dated copy, not a live read.** The page is on another origin with no API; an edge function could fetch it, but a copy the office can Compare against the Estimate Terms today is the value, and the date on the card says how fresh it is. A live check is a follow-up if the page turns out to change often.
- **A notice, not a signed contract.** Nobody signs the website's terms; they bind by their own words. The card carries the *Notice* chip like the lien waivers and the invoice footers.
- **Its own area, first.** A customer reads the website before an estimate exists; folding it into *Estimates* would hide that the app's terms are a fork of it.
