# v2.3180 — Job Summary table: cents in small type (2026-09-09)

Client only — no migration, no edge function. Owner, the morning after v2.3177 put small cents on the tiles: "I would also like the small decimals to be a part of this table." Same treatment, same component, now on every money cell of the Jobs → Job Summary ledger table.

## What shipped

- **[`SignedAmountSmallCents`](../../src/components/AmountSmallCents.tsx)** (new export beside `AmountSmallCents`): a true minus sign before the `$` for a loss, an em dash for `null` / `undefined`, the cents at `0.7em`. Extracted from the tiles' `JobSummaryTileMoney` (v2.3177), which now delegates to it, so the tiles, the table rows, the footer and the group rows share one definition. 3 render smokes (`AmountSmallCents.render.test.tsx`).
- **[`JobsJobSummaryTab`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsJobSummaryTab.tsx)** main row: Revenue, Labor, Subs, Parts, Gross, Overhead and True profit cells render `<SignedAmountSmallCents>`; the per-tag cost lines under Parts ("⛽ fuel & gas $601.58") use `<AmountSmallCents>`. The `—` / `…` (ledger loading) / role-gated branches are unchanged, as are the `title` tooltips (plain strings). The totals footer's seven money cells match.
- **[`JobSummaryCutByPanel`](../../src/components/jobs/JobSummaryCutByPanel.tsx)** `JobSummaryGroupRow`: the subtotal row's money cells switch from the rounded `money()` (no cents) to `<SignedAmountSmallCents>` so a group row lines up with the job rows under it. The bar chart's labels and `<title>` keep the rounded form.
- Not touched: the expanded row's drill-down tables, the CSV export and the print builder, the Days / Timeline / other views.
