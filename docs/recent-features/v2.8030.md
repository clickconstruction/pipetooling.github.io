# v2.8030 — Accounts Receivable: the client's role lists match the database's four roles (2026-11-20)

A tidy-up after punch list #400, PR 5 (v2.7931, `20261119104000_primary_money_desks.sql`). That migration took `primary` out of twenty-two functions and two read policies and changed no client code, because no screen of a primary's reaches them. The client's role lists still named the role. Found by the docs catch-up pass of 2026-11-20. No migration, no edge function, nothing to deploy.

## Before

- `canRoleUseArBankCount` (`src/hooks/useArBankUnallocatedCount.ts`) let a primary ask `count_mercury_transactions_for_bank_payments`, which refuses the role.
- `canRoleApplyBankPayments`, a private function of `BankPaymentsModal.tsx`, named primary for `canApply`. That gate arms the cases read (`useArReturnCases`: `list_ar_return_cases`, `list_ar_deposit_trails`), the close-out, Book it as Income, the returned tick and every case handler. Each of those refuses a primary.
- `canRecordArPayments` (`src/lib/jobs/stagesRoleGates.ts`) named primary on the Pipeline's Accounts Receivable button, with a comment that a primary "can use the AR door". The window behind it lists deposits through `list_mercury_transactions_for_bank_payments`, which refuses the role.
- `canOpenAr` in `usePipelineMoneyOpportunities.ts` named primary and armed the same count on Quickfill.
- The button's disabled words and the window's role banner read *dev, leader, assistant, and primary* and left the controller out.

## What changed

- **One list, four roles**: dev, master_technician, assistant, controller. `canRoleUseArBankCount`, `canRecordArPayments` and `canOpenAr` drop the role.
- **The window reads the tested gate.** `BankPaymentsModal` takes `canRecordArPayments` from the stages gates, and its private copy is gone. This is row B-8 of `docs/AR_PAYMENT_MODALS_ARCHITECTURE.md`.
- **The words name the controller**: `accountsReceivableButtonName` and the window's banner.
- **The guide** *match bank deposits to the bills they pay* leaves `primary` out of its roles. Its screen turns the role away.
- **Tests**: the gate matrix (`stagesRoleGates.test.ts`) pins Accounts Receivable to the office pool and the expected-pay chips to the office and a primary. `useArBankUnallocatedCount.gates.test.ts` (new) pins the three count gates for every role.

## What it leaves alone, on purpose

- `canSeeBilledExpectedPay`, in both its copies. The Bill tab of a primary's own job draws its expected-pay dates, its pay calendar and its returned-checks warning from the four reads v2.7931 left open: `get_billed_customer_pay_speeds`, `list_job_promised_pay_dates`, `can_read_payment_promises()` and `list_ar_returned_check_payers`.
- `canUnlinkMercuryPayment` on the job form. The paid verbs answer a primary on their own job (v2.5655).
- `canEditJobPctComplete` and the other gates that name the role for a call that still answers it.
- The tip strip in the bank payments window has no client gate, as before.

## Why no primary screen loses anything

- A primary's Jobs page is the Reports tab alone (`primaryTabs` in `src/pages/Jobs.tsx`), so the Pipeline's button and the window never draw for them.
- `/accounts-receivable`, `/quickfill` and `/people` are not on `PRIMARY_PATHS`.
- The Needs you sources that open these windows (`arBank`, `bankReturned`, `gcReview`) were the office's already.
