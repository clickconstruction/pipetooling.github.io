# v2.6982 — Lien affidavit: Record service takes each copy's certified mail number and a photo of its receipt (2026-11-11)

Punch list #314, change 5, the form half. The owner, 2026-11-11: *it might make sense to offer the user a QR code where if they scan it brings them to that part of the app to enter a number and upload their receipt of the certified mail.* The card split that into two pieces: the form first, then the code on the sheet. This is the form. The code is the next PR.

## What changed

- **Each mailed copy has a place for its proof.** **Record service…** took one thing, the day served. It now also draws one row per copy, *Copy to the owner* and, on a job under a GC, *Copy to the original contractor*, each with a **Certified mail number** box and a **Take a photo of the receipt** press. On a phone the step is the record sheet it already was, with a numeric keypad on the number; on a computer it is the amber box in the tab.
- **Neither holds the day.** `serviceSends` (`src/lib/jobs/lienServiceProof.ts`) writes a row for a copy only when it has a number or a photo. The day recorded alone writes `sends: []` and clears the serve-by watch as before.
- **The proof can come later.** A served affidavit's row under *Filings on this job* shows each copy's number and a *receipt ›* link, and a dotted door, *add the mail receipts* or *mail receipts*, reopens the step on what the filing holds (`serviceCopyDraft`). The press then reads **Save the mail receipts**.
- **Where it is kept.** `job_lien_filings.sends`, the shape a notice's sends already use (`recipient`, `method: 'certified_mail'`, `tracking`, `sent_on`), with one more key, `receipt_url`. `parseLienSends` carries it. No migration.
- **The photo.** `LienReceiptPhotoPress` shrinks the shot on the device (`shrinkPhoto`, as a box photo) and puts it in the job's Drive folder under Team only › Sent by us through `putJobFolderFile(jobId, 'sent', …)` the moment it is taken, so a failure shows on the press and not after the record is saved. The file is named for what it is: *Lien affidavit, Job 77, mail receipt, owner, 2026-11-18.jpg* (`lienReceiptPhotoName`).
- **The clerk's stamped page.** **Record filing…** has the same press, *Take a photo of the stamped page*. Its link fills the filing's **Saved copy** with the note *the clerk's stamped page*. It rides on **Already filed — record it…** too, which draws the same box.
- **The help sheet's step.** `lienHelpAfterSteps` now says what the form asks: set the day, type each certified mail number, take a photo of each receipt. v2.6961 had taken the tracking number out of that step because the form had no place for it.

## A hole closed on the way

**A filed affidavit lost its Record service… press whenever a hold was closed.** The press sat inside the branch that draws the paper, so one closed gate (a missing county, a GC with no address, and from 2026-11-17 an unanswered homestead question) left only *Clear the ✗ items above*, on a computer and on a phone, while the paper's five days ran. The homestead hold (PR 7667) would have closed on 11 of the 12 papers filed on 2026-11-16. The step is now offered in front of the gate list as well, the way **Already filed — record it…** is for the filing, with one line under it (`heldServiceWords`): the paper is on file, mail its copies by the serve-by day, the items above hold a new paper only.

## Two things kept as they were, on purpose

- **A sworn paper redraws as it was sworn.** `buildLienAffidavitBlocks` closes its page with a delivery box when a caller hands it sends. Until now an affidavit row never had any. **View** on a recorded affidavit (`LienFilingTabs.viewFiling`) and the stop packet's *As it went out* page (`filingSnapshotPage`) now hand it none, so recording the receipts changes no page.
- **The filing keeps its own line on the customer's timeline.** A notice is its sends, so its sends stand in for it. An affidavit is filed with the clerk and its copies are mailed after: `buildCustomerTimeline` keeps *Lien affidavit recorded* and adds *Lien affidavit copy mailed* for each copy.

## Is this the best we can do?

Asked of the form as the owner asks it of everything on this card.

- **The number is typed, not scanned.** A phone's browser reads the USPS barcode unreliably, and a wrong number is worse than a typed one. The photo is the proof; the number is what a person searches by.
- **One method.** The form does not ask how the copy went. The help sheet says certified mail and the box is labelled for it. A copy that went another way has no number to type, and its row is not written.
- **Not done here:** the code on the cover sheet and the batch sheet that opens this step on a phone (the next PR), and the same press on a notice's **Save & record sends…**, which already takes a number per recipient and could take a receipt photo the same way.
