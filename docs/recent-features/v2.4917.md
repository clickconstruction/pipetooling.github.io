# v2.4917 — GC mode: ask trade partners for a quote, for a dev (2026-10-08)

The Board's B4-a, second half (`to-dos/gc-mode/BOARD_REAL_BUILD.md`, on branch `spike/gc-mode`): the Ask window on real data at `/gc`, dev only, over B4-a's kernels (v2.4906) and the Portal's `inviteMessage` (P1b-i, v2.4905). Guide: `ask-trade-partners-for-a-quote`.

- **`GcAskCompanies.tsx`** is ported from the spike's window.
  - The left side is `askChoices`: the companies in range come first and start ticked, then those too far or declined. Each shows its drive, its answer record, *not vetted yet*, *no email on file* and its language.
  - The head shows the trade's standing, the day quotes are wanted, the plans and the scope line count.
  - The right side is the invitation from `inviteMessage` over a draft ask (invited today, nothing written), in the company's language. It is marked *not sent yet*, and a line stands where the portal link will go.
  - The foot gives `askStanding`'s sentence, then says nothing is emailed yet.
- **Ask N companies** calls `askGcCompanies` in `gcIo.ts`. That runs `gc_invite_companies`, then writes one `gc_company_contacts` line per new ask (how 'note', `ASK_NOT_SENT_NOTE`) saying its email waits. This is the lead's "shown and logged" until P3's `gc-trade-email` sends it.
  - `boardRows.ts` leaves an ask's 'note' lines out of `Invite.contacts`, so the note never reads as a call or a chase.
- **Where it opens:**
  - **Ask for quotes** on each trade of a project's card (`GcTradeAsks`), while a company in the trade is not asked.
  - On Trade partners: a need line's **Ask the N we have not asked**, or **Ask anyway** when nobody in range is left.
  - The assistants' ask items.
- **Languages:** the window reads each company's language from the board's rows (`gc_companies.lang`), since the kernels' company carries none yet.
- **Waits:** sending the email (P3), and the map in the Bid Board's card.
