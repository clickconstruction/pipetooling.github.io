# v2.4382 — Procurement log: one calm line per part (2026-10-02)

Procure layout train, change 2 of 4 (Grace, 2026-10-02; boards *Procure 1 · By tag* on canvas https://claude.ai/artifact/JtoZEiwKP7zbABzXEy7hH8). On BP375 the log was 13 columns × 47 lines with about 220 empty boxes on a draft: *Not shared* on every line, long catalog names wrapping to four lines, the house and stage buried in a grey sub-line, order-only trim mixed in with the GC's parts, and headings that said only *5 lines*.

## What changed (`SubmittalProcurementPanel`, `procurementLog.ts`, `procurementLogIo.ts`)
- **Columns: tick · Part · Qty · House · Stage · Lead · Status · ×.** Part shows the tag (unless under its heading), the maker and model bold (`splitPartLabel`) and the catalog words clamped to one line, then a quiet line for *in place of the priced …* (`pricedLabel`), *order only* (outside By tag) and *counted with*. House, Stage and Lead are their own cells; a missing house reads *no house* in amber.
- **One Status** (`lineStatus`): *✓ On site 09/29*, *Ordered · arrives 10/20*, *Arrives 10/20, 14 d late* (red), *Order by 10/06*, *Released, not ordered*, *Sent back by the GC*, *Waiting on the GC*, or nothing on a draft; the sub line carries ordered date, needed date, PO and the note. It is a button (`.procure-status`, `aria-expanded`): a tap opens a `procurement-editor` row under the line with the same Ordered / PO / Expected / Delivered / Note boxes (`dateCell`, unchanged behaviour) and *The GC and the job* facts (the call, released, needed, float). The printed sheet, the update snapshot and the CSV keep their columns.
- **By tag** (`procurementSections`): the GC's parts first, the order-only ones after a *Ordered, not on the GC's copy · N parts* divider (`orderOnlyFrom`), in grey (opacity .72, `data-order-only`); the heading reads `tagRollUp` — *WC 1&2 × 10 · 5 parts · 1 on site* — from the takeoff's fixture name and count (`fixture`, `fixtureCount`, read for every row in `procurementItemsFrom`), and a tag whose GC parts include two carriers shows a *Two carriers* chip.
- **A draft said once** (`logIsDraft`): *This version is a draft, so nothing is released yet. The GC releases each part when they approve it.*
- **The house opens the row**: the House cell is a button (as the Part cell since v2.4354) that opens the row's Edit window on that part, its house box ready.
- Width: the table's minimum drops from 1,140 px to 760 px; the Part column shrinks (tag and model may wrap) so the log fits at 1024 without sideways scrolling.
