# v2.4191 — Takeoffs and Labor: the base and what the alternate adds (2026-09-29)

The alternates train, PR 3 of 5 (after [v2.4188](v2.4188.md), which brought the alternate groups onto the Count Sheet). No migration, no edge function. The owner's pick for these two tabs: the rows stay where they are with a mark, and only the totals split — no separate sections.

## What changed

- **One kernel for every tab** — [`alternateScope.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/alternateScope.ts): `partitionByAlternate(rows, tags)` (the base, then each alternate alphabetical, first spelling kept), `sumByAlternate(rows, tags, value)` (base / each alternate / total, or null on a bid without an alternate that holds a row), and `laborHoursByAlternate({ laborRows, countRows, alternateTags })`. Membership is the Count Sheet's rule (`isAlternateRow`).
- **Takeoffs → the Sheet view's rail** ([`TakeoffCostRailView`](../../src/components/bids/TakeoffCostRailView.tsx), prop `alternateTags`): under *Materials on this bid*, an amber block reads *Base $5,076.00 · + Break room $1,152.50 · With the alternate $6,228.50* — each fixture's costed total (its lines × count plus its slice of the sticks, `coverage.perFixture`) summed by scope, so the three add up to the number Pricing reads. The per-fixture unit-cost list and every fixture label on the Combined sheet (all three renderers in [`BidsTakeoffTab`](../../src/components/bids/BidsTakeoffTab.tsx)) wear the **ALT** mark on an alternate's row.
- **Labor → a card above the bottom line** ([`BidsLaborNewView`](../../src/components/bids/BidsLaborNewView.tsx), props `countRows` + `alternateTags` from the tab): *With and without the alternate* — Field hours, Labor at the rate and Driving (which follows hours) in columns for the base, each alternate and the whole. Labor rows are keyed by fixture name and carry the summed count (v2.4188), so a fixture that sits in the base and in an alternate shares one row; its hours split in the ratio of the scoped counts, which is exact because hours are linear in the count for `each` and `per_100ft` rows. Fixed hours, tasks and sub lines stay in the base.

## Decided here

- **Materials are not on Labor's card.** The Labor tab reads the takeoff's stage totals, not its per-row coverage, so the card says where the materials split lives (Takeoffs → What Pricing sees) instead of loading the takeoff a second time. If the owner wants it here, the engine can expose the coverage to both tabs.
- **The One-at-a-time view is untouched** — it walks one fixture; the mark would say nothing the sheet's row does not.
