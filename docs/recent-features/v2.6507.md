# v2.6507 — A band on each New row on Messages says which desk finishes it, Dispatch or Estimating (punch list #258, PR 1) (2026-11-04)

Punch list #258 (`to-dos/messages-desk-band/README.md`, PR #7022), built on the card's recommended defaults. Grace, 2026-11-04, looking at New on `/messages` at desktop width: the lane should visually sort what needs Dispatch to complete from what needs Estimating, as a banner on the left hand side re-using the colors and icons of the header's two presses. No migration, no edge function.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/messages/rowDesk.ts`** (new, pure, tested): `rowDesk(row)` reads the desk off the facts a New row already carries and guesses nothing from the words. *Bid it?* is Estimating's whatever the sender (an invitation, an addendum, a solicitation, a pre-bid notice, each with its own why). *Who is this?*, one of our own team, a supply house and a candidate are nobody's. A trade partner or an architect is Estimating's, as the seat rule already holds them. A customer, a lead or a sub is placed by the jobs they are on: any Pipeline job is Dispatch's, only bids Estimating's, only GC projects nobody's here; with no job under way the box says it, bids@ Estimating's and the team line Dispatch's. `deskRead` gives the why and `deskTitle` the hover, *Estimating · An invitation to bid.*
- **`src/lib/messages/deskMarks.ts`** (new): the two desks' word, fill, ink and icon path as one record. `Layout.tsx`'s four inbox presses (the words pair and the icon pair for Task Dispatch and Ask estimating) draw from it instead of four literals, and `deskMarks.test.ts` refuses a violet literal or either path written back into the header, so the header and the band cannot drift. The fills stay the header's literals, not theme tokens, so a desk reads the same in both themes; `readablePairs.test.ts` holds white on each at 4.5:1 in both.
- **`src/components/followUp/DeskBand.tsx`** (new) over `FollowUpSections.tsx`'s `Row`, which takes a `band`: a strip the row's full height on its left edge, 22 px at a desk and 18 on a phone (`useIsMobile`), the row's own corner radius, the icon 14 or 12 px white at its middle, `role="img"` with the hover as its label, the row's padding moved right by the band's width. `NewRowView` passes `deskRead(row)`; a row the rule cannot place draws as before. The band is not a press: a press on it opens the thread, as a press anywhere on the row does.
- **Guide** `sort-new-messages` → *The band on the left*; `docs/MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md` → Follow up's New sentence; `docs/GLOSSARY.md`'s New entry.

## Decisions

- **A band, not a chip.** A chip beside the sort's question would be a second pill on the name line, which reads as a second press and wraps on a phone at three things. The band sits where the eye scans a list and costs the name line nothing.
- **The rule reads facts, never words.** A customer who texts the team line to ask for a quote gets a Dispatch band, since nothing on the row says quote; the office reads the words and hands it on, as today. The hover names the fact that placed the row.
- **Nothing for a row it cannot place.** The blue question already says *sort first*; a grey band would say it twice. Once the sender is named, the kind places the row and the band appears, by the same rule.
- **New only.** Needs a reply's rows are the same `Row` with the same facts, so the band is one line there, but it waits on the owner's look at New with it (call 3 on the card). The thread's folded sort card is left for the same PR.
