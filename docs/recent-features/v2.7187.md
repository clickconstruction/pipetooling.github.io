# v2.7187 — The Lien window's header names the step an affidavit is on (2026-11-13)

Punch list #335, row 2, on v2.7184. A billed row read *Draft the affidavit*, *Approve the affidavit* or the fact that holds the paper (*Answer the homestead question*), and the Lien window it opened read *File the affidavit — 54 days* at every one of those steps. The window builds its path from the job's filings and passed no word on an affidavit that is not filed. v2.7077 closed the same gap for a notice. Client only. No printed paper changes: these are the header's words, not the affidavit's.

## What changed

- **`lienTimeline.ts`**: new `LienTimelineAffidavitState` (`needs_property`, `to_draft`, `awaiting`, `ready`, `held`, or `''` for a reader that cannot know). `LienTimelineInput.affidavit` takes `state`, `missingKeys` (the closed gates by key) and `holdUntil`. When the affidavit is the next paper, *Next on the path* opens on the words the act table gives the row (`lienActFor`):
  - *Draft the affidavit — 54 days.* · waiting on *the affidavit to be drafted*
  - *Approve the affidavit — 54 days.* · *the leader's approval of the affidavit*
  - *Answer the homestead question, then file the affidavit — 54 days.* · *a yes or a no on the homestead question*; likewise *Find the owner*, *Add the legal description*, *Add the GC's address*, and *Fix the property record* with the missing facts listed when there are several
  - *Homestead, ask the attorney — 54 days.* · *the attorney to be asked about a homestead lien*
  - *Public property, no lien — ask the attorney about the bond.* with no day and no countdown
  - *Held — the affidavit re-asks Oct 5; file by Nov 16.* · *the hold to lift*
  - `ready` and `''` read *File the affidavit* as before.
  The affidavit's step on the strip says its place too (*54 days · to draft*, *· awaiting approval*, *· ready to file*, *· held*). `LienTimelineNext.affidavitState` carries the step for the window's door.
- **`lienTimelineDesk.ts`**: new `lienWindowDeskAffidavit(entry)` reads the job's affidavit entry (`LienAffidavitEntry.pile`, its closed gates, the item's `hold_until`) into `{ state, missing, holdUntil }`; null with no entry or one that is filed or missed. `buildLienTimelineFromWindow` takes it as `deskAffidavit`. `buildLienTimelineFromDesk` hands the kernel the entry's pile and gate keys, so the Lien desk's Timeline tab and its affidavit pane's strip name the step as well.
- **`lienWindowNextStep.ts`**: an affidavit to draft, waiting on approval or on hold is worked on the desk's Affidavits list, so that step's button reads *Open it on the Lien desk* (door `{ to: 'desk', kind: 'affidavit' }`). One held by a fact, or ready, keeps *Go to the affidavit*: the window's tab lists the facts with their doors.
- **`LienInstrumentsModal`**: new prop `deskAffidavit`, read by value into the timeline's memo. `onOpenLienDesk` may now be called with `'affidavit'`.
- **Openers**: `JobsStagesTab` and `LienDeskPage` pass the job's affidavit entry. `JobDocumentsLienPaper` holds no desk queue and passes nothing.
- **Tests**: `lienTimelineDesk.test.ts` (each pile's sentence, *Waiting on* and strip words; six facts; public property; a hold with and without a day; no entry; a notice still owed comes first and a filed lien moves on to service; the desk's own adapter), `lienWindowNextStep.test.ts` (the door per step), `lienOneReading.test.ts` (the header joins the affidavit table: its first word is the row's, and it counts the row's days), `LienInstrumentsModal.render.test.tsx` (the header with and without the opener's word, and the button's door).
- **Guides** *send lien notices from the Lien desk* and *file a lien and never miss its deadlines*. Glossary *Lien window*; `LIEN_DESK_ARCHITECTURE.md`.

## Not done, and why

- **The button opens the Affidavits list, not the job's row in it.** The desk selects a notice from its address and has no such read for an affidavit; `LienDesk.tsx`'s Affidavits pane was in another session's hands the day this shipped. Listed on the card.
- **A paper waiting on someone else's approval** reads *Waiting on Robert* on the row and *Approve the affidavit* in the header, whose second line says *the leader's approval*. A notice reads the same two ways (v2.7077).
- **A held affidavit** reads *Review the hold* on the row and *Held* in the header, the act and the state, as a held notice does (row 3 on the card).
- **The window opened from a job's Documents tab** and the firm's page pass no desk word, so their header reads *File the affidavit* as before.
