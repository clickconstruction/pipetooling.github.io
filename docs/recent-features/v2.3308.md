## Why

Customers who pay by ACH, wire or a mailed check had nothing on their statement but the card button; the office answered "where do I send it?" from a PDF on someone's desktop. The remittance details are the same numbers printed on every paper check the company writes, so showing them to customers is the exposure already carried — what needed care was **where they live** (a public repo is not it) and **the sentence under them** (the fraud that actually costs contractors money is a scammer emailing a customer *different* details in the company's name).
