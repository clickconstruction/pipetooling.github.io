# v2.4249 — Lien desk: “Someone’s calling” finds any job on the desk, and has a practice call (2026-09-30)

Taunya (assistant), 2026-09-30, with a screenshot of the box saying *No sent notice matches “lenn”*: *"I need to be able to search by job or address or owner or gc, and would like the option to click on a sample one to explore how this page works."* Punch list #47 (the owner is calling). Mock-up: [`to-dos/owner-is-calling/search-and-practice.html`](../../to-dos/owner-is-calling/search-and-practice.html); built as drawn on Grace's word.

Why she found nothing, three reasons at once:

1. The box (v2.3854) matched **sent notices only**. 9703 Lenox Hl (job 858) was in *To draft*; 20 jobs were waiting on the Notices tab and few letters had gone out. No spelling would have found it.
2. It matched **exact substrings**. "lenn" is not inside "Lenox".
3. An **empty box** showed a caption and nothing to press, so the call sheet could only be seen when a real owner called about a real letter.

## What changed

- **The search covers every job the desk holds.** [`lienCallerMatch.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienCallerMatch.ts): `matchCaller` is replaced by `callerIndex(input, fmt)` (built once per desk load) and `findOnDesk(query, index)`. `CallerMatchInput` gains `deskJobs: DeskJobRef[]` — one row per job from the Notices, Affidavits and Retainage entries (`{ jobId, tab, pile, openBalance, deadline }`), built in `LienDeskModal`'s `callerInput` memo. Nothing new is loaded.
- **Two groups.** A job with a sent notice is a `CallerOwnerHit` (*Letter sent*, opens the call sheet, unchanged). A job with nothing mailed is a `CallerJobHit` (*No letter mailed yet*: the job label, its pile, `GC … · $… · notice by …`; opens the job). One row per job: the letter wins, and a job on two tabs is listed under the first.
- **Open the job ›** — `openDeskJob` in `LienDeskModal`: switches to the job's tab, lifts the pile filter (and the Calendar's *Draft the N* narrowing) and selects the job. `doorPickedJobId` keeps the list's selection effect from snapping back to `initialJobId` on that one list change.
- **A GC's name** heads the results with how many of its jobs are on the desk and the line *A GC's own call goes to the master, not to the call sheet*. Its jobs follow in the two groups (the GC's name was always in each job's words).
- **A slip is forgiven.** When the query finds nothing, each word that is nowhere on the desk loses letters from its end down to `CALLER_TRIM_MIN` (3) until it is somewhere; `DeskFind.trimmed` / `used` drive the line *Nothing has “9703 lenoz” in it. Showing “9703 leno”.* Words that are each on the desk but never on one job still find nothing.
- **A job number typed whole comes first**; otherwise sent letters are newest first and unsent jobs soonest date first. Each group shows `CALLER_MATCH_MAX` (8) and counts the rest.
- **The box before a word is typed** ([`LienCallerDoor.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienCallerDoor.tsx)): *Try* chips from `callerTryWords` (a job number, a street, an owner, a GC — cut from jobs on the desk, from different jobs where possible, each checked to find something), *Letters out now* from `lettersOutNow` (sent, the job still has money open, newest five), and the practice row. The prompt reads *Find*; the blue button keeps its name. Matched letters are marked.
- **Practice call.** `practiceCallFacts` builds a made-up letter dated from today (Pat Sample, Sample Builders, *000 · Practice job*, $4,250.00, mailed eight days ago for the two months before). `LienOwnerCallDialog` takes `practice`: an amber banner (*Practice call · a made-up letter · nothing is saved*, *Start over ↺*), no Save — *Done practicing* closes it. The desk mounts it from `practiceCallOpen` with no item, so `noteOwnerCall` cannot run.
- **Not on the desk** reads *No job on the Lien desk matches “…”. The desk holds jobs with an unpaid balance and a lien deadline. For any other job, search the Pipeline.*

- **A pick clears the words**, so the box is empty for the next caller; closing it with × or Escape keeps them.
