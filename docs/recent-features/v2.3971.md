# v2.3971 — GC statements: the Pipeline cards are for the whole office (2026-09-27)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Step 6a of punch list **#49** (`to-dos/gc-review-one-operator/`). Step 6b — the Dashboard row and the morning email, which read a database function — follows.

## What shipped

- **`worklistCards(worklist)`** (`lib/jobs/gcWorklist.ts`, 2 tests) — the two Pipeline cards off the week's list: `held` (bills still to check) and `ready` (checked, statement not out, with `late` = how many broke a promise). Both count every GC over the line. A word taken early still leaves the statement to send — the old round called that GC "contacted" and dropped it from both cards.
- **`JobsStagesTab`** and **`usePipelineMoneyOpportunities`** (Quickfill) build the cards from `buildGcWorklist` + `worklistCards` in place of `summarizeStatementRound(items, authUserId)`.
- **`PipelineMoneyOpportunities`** — 📬 reads "Statements to send — N GCs, $X · checked and ready · grouped by the account man to ask", door **Open the list →**; 🔏's door reads **Check in GC Review →**.
- **Retired:** the round overlay in `JobsGcReviewModal` (Start round, Sent it ✓, Skip, Round done) with `roundOpen`, `roundFocusGcId`, `roundSentFormOpen`, `emailFromRoundGcId`; and from `gcStatementRounds.ts`: `buildStatementRound`, `summarizeStatementRound`, `statementRoundCards`, `StatementRoundItem` / `StatementRoundState` / `StatementRoundSummary`. The kernel keeps the mark's own rules. 2,487 → 2,328 lines in the modal.
- **`?round=1[&gc=<id>]`** — the modal's `startInRound` / `startInRoundGcId` props are one `focusGcId`: GC Review opens on the list, and with a GC named, on its account man's call sheet. The `gcRoundStart` and `gcRoundCertify` moves both open the list.
- Draft Message's auto-mark reads the worklist row (over the line, not yet marked sent).
- **Skip** went with the overlay; a GC skipped before this still reads "skipped this week" with **undo**.
- **Still per-sender until 6b:** the Dashboard's "N GCs are waiting on your statement" row and the morning round email (`get_statement_round_for_user`).
