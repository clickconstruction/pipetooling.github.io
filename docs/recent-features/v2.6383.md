# v2.6383 — Lien desk: the owner's call closes the courtesy notice after letter two too (2026-11-02)

PR 4 of the sweep after Taunya's report on job 890 (v2.6376, v2.6380, v2.6382). No migration, no function.

- **The cause.** `letterTwoStatus` (`lienLetterTwo.ts`) returns `sent` or `in_flight` as soon as a letter-two item exists, before it reads the owner's call, and `courtesyStatus` (`lienCourtesyNotice.ts`) closed the courtesy only on `lt.state === 'owner_called'`. So after letter two went out, an owner's call recorded on the first packet closed nothing: the courtesy stayed `due`, `lienNextUp` kept the amber *Send a courtesy notice* row (which the AR window's chip reads too) and the sent footer kept its door, re-asking every seven days.
- **The fix.** `LetterTwoStatus` carries `ownerCalledAt` whatever its state, and `courtesyStatus` closes on it (*owner called Oct 28*) as it closes on `owner_called`. While letter two is in flight or went out, the status carries `letterTwoWords` and `courtesyFooterSentence` ends with them (*…your number. Letter two sent Oct 24 · unresponsive.*) in place of *Letter two is counsel's certified second letter, from day 10*, which read as if it were still to come.
- **Left on the card (#249):** a Messages reply still closes nothing (no caller passes `repliedAt`), and letter two's day 0 never reads the Docsmit piece's delivery day (no caller passes `mailClocks`).
- Tests: `lienLetterTwo.test.ts` (the call rides on a `sent` status), `lienCourtesyNotice.test.ts` (closed by the call after letter two; the footer names letter two as it stands, before and after a courtesy went).
