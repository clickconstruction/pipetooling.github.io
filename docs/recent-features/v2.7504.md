# v2.7504 — Lien desk: a place to say a job is not lienable (2026-11-16)

The owner's ask of 2026-11-16, reading the Lien desk's To do on a job at a homestead: *"how should we use the lien desk todo as a state of things and carry it through the process?"* The job's one bill was three trips to pick up checks that were not there. That money is not for labor or materials on the property, so most likely no lien secures it, and the desk had no way to say so. Of four changes drawn he picked this one: a job can leave the lien path with a reason, a name and a day. This is PR 1 of the train, the database half. No screen changes.

## Why a stored answer on the job

A job left the Lien desk three ways until now. It was paid. It was marked Uncollectible, which writes the money off. Or its whole property was answered public (v2.7436), which is about the land and takes every job at the address. A job that should be collected another way had none of these: it stayed in the bins, and on the day this was read its monthly notice stood approved, one signature from the mail.

## What ships

Migration `20261117080000_job_lien_path_answer.sql` ([its page](../migrations/20261117080000_job_lien_path_answer.md)):

- **Four columns on the job** (`jobs_ledger`): `lien_path_answer` (NULL or `'off_path'`), `lien_path_answered_at`, `lien_path_answered_by` and `lien_path_reason`.
- **One function**, `set_job_lien_path_answer`, for the lien office (dev, master technician, assistant, controller). It takes a job off the path or puts it back, asks a reason both ways, and writes a `lien_path_change` line on the job's activity each time the answer changes. On the way off it can also put the job on the attorney's list through the Collections flag that already exists, and it names what that flag refused.

Who decided, when and why stay on the job after it is put back, as who put it back.

Nothing is filed, voided or deleted. The lien papers already made stay as they are, so a job put back finds them where they stood. They will stop counting once the desk reads the answer, which is the client work that follows.

## Not in this train

- A homestead's own questions (whether the owner's contract with the contractor was signed before the work and recorded, and what counsel said). They were drawn the same day and not picked.
- Any judgment by the app of what a bill is for. A person reads the bill and says why.

It runs against the real `set_job_collections_flag`, lifted out of its migration.
