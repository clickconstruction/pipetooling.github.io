# v2.8590 — Accounts Receivable opens with nothing picked (2026-11-25)

Punch list #437, from the walk of v2.8552 on 2026-11-25. The owner's answer of the same day: asked *"Do all the picks on round 2, rows 1 to 22, stand?"*, *"Yes, all picks"*, where row 15 read *"#437: Accounts Receivable's window opens with nothing picked"*, pick **Yes**. Client only. No migration, no edge function.

**Seen.** On opening Accounts Receivable → **To match**, the window picked the first deposit in the list by itself. Its pane filled and **Apply** was live, on a real row, with no press made. v2.8552 stopped that after a case is closed and left the first open as it was.

**The cause.** In `BankPaymentsModal.tsx` three places fall back to the first row when nothing is picked: the list's read, the filter's effect, and the re-select after a Returned tick. `holdNothingRef`, the switch v2.8552 added, held the first two off only after a close that left no case.

**The mend.**

- The switch is set when the window opens (`holdNothingRef.current = open`), so no fall back picks a row until one is pressed. Pressing a row clears it, as before.
- The Returned tick's re-select asks the switch too. It was the one fall back v2.8552 left out.
- The pane reads *Select a bank transaction.*, the footer's sentence is empty and no bill is offered until a row is pressed.

**What did not change.**

- A link that names a deposit (`?check=<id>`) still opens on it.
- After an Apply, the window still moves to the next deposit, and after a case closes it still lands as v2.8552 and v2.8584 left it.
- The *To match · N* count, which reads the list.
- A phone, whose pane starts hidden.
- A window closed and opened again on the same page keeps the row someone picked before. That row was chosen by a person.

**Tests.** A new render smoke, `BankPaymentsModal.opensEmpty.render.test.tsx`: the open reads *Select a bank transaction.* with no bill and no sentence, a pressed row fills the pane, the filter to All and back picks nothing, and a Returned tick taken off a row picks nothing. The first and third fail on main, and the fourth fails without the tick's guard. Sixteen older tests in seven files read the pane on open: each now presses its deposit first, through one helper, `src/test/pickArDeposit.ts`.

**Not walked.** The recipe is read only: open Accounts Receivable → **To match** as the dev, read the pane and **Apply**, press one row, press nothing in it.
