# v2.6871 — Materials: on a narrow window the tab groups fold to their name (punch list #304) (2026-11-10)

The owner, 2026-11-10, with the Materials page on a laptop and Track dropped to a second row under Plan and Purchase: *what if on viewports where the entire header fully expanded can't be seen, instead of wrapping, Plan and Purchase collapse to behind their button, and clicking on one expands that section?* Then: *build it tastefully and test along the way.*

## What changed

- **`lib/materials/materialsStripFit.ts`** (new, pure): `stripFit` takes each group's width drawn open and drawn folded, the room between groups and the row's width, and answers which groups stay open. Everything that fits stays open. When the row is short the group just pressed open comes first, then the group holding the open tab, then the rest in the strip's order; each stays while the row still fits and the others fold. When even one open group beside the folded names is too wide, the shape is `rows`: the names take a row and the open group's tabs sit under them. A width of zero (not measured, a test's DOM) reads as everything fits. `foldedGroupLabel` is what a name says to a screen reader.
- **`MaterialsGroupStrip`**: measures itself (a `ResizeObserver` on the row, and a hidden copy of every group drawn open and folded) and draws from `stripFit`. A folded group is one press with its name, its badge, and the open tab's name when it holds it; `aria-expanded` is on it. Pressing it sets which group is open, moves the keyboard to that group's first tab, and never calls `onTab`. A tab pressed, or the row's width changing, clears it. The `phone` prop is gone: the phone's row of names is the `rows` shape, reached by measuring, and a name there opens its group where it used to open the group's first tab. `measured` hands the widths in for a test.
- **The presses** (the owner, the same day, on the phone's row: *move Print labels to the right of Scan a label and move both to be right aligned*): `materialsPressesFor` answers Scan a label, then Print labels, and the strip pushes the pair to the right end of On site's row. On a wide window that is the right end of the strip, since Track is the last group and takes the rest of the row.
- **`Materials.tsx`**: no longer asks `useMatchMedia('(max-width: 640px)')` for the strip.

## The calls made

- **As many as fit, never one at a time.** A 1,280 window on On site keeps Plan open beside Track and folds Purchase alone. Folding all but one would hide tabs there is room for.
- **The open tab's group can fold.** Pressing Purchase on a window too short for Purchase and Track together folds Track to *Track · On site*, in blue, so where the page is stays on screen.
- **No fixed width.** The estimator's strip is shorter than the office's and folds later; a badge on Track makes it wider and it folds sooner.
