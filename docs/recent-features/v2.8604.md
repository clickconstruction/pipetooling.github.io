# v2.8604 — Dashboard: the first bid shows you that it swipes (2026-11-25)

Punch list #426, the sign. The person who runs the session tried the swipe on their own phone on 2026-11-25 and asked: *I think we need to make it clear to our user that they have the option to swipe on my bids. Maybe we should make it reactive in some way so a user knows that when they scroll down to my bids, they can swipe to hide?* Their pick from the mock-up (`to-dos/my-bids-refresh/swipe-sign-and-back.html`) was *A and no press*: way A, the first row shows its strip. Client only. No migration, no edge function.

## What changed

- **The first row of each card shows its strip by itself.** `SwipeAwayRow` takes `sign`. The row that has it watches itself (`IntersectionObserver`). Once it has stood in full view for half a second (`SWIPE_SIGN_STILL_MS`), with no finger down and the page still, it slides out far enough to read its strip's word, holds and slides back. It is the slide v2.8603 added for a wrong-way swipe: 64 px for **Hide**, 108 px for **Mark opened**, always short of the 112 px mark. Only a finger passes the mark, so the sign can never put a row away.
- **A scroll or a finger on its way down starts the wait over**, and a touch stops a row that is showing its strip. A press on the row still opens it.
- **How often** (`src/lib/swipeSign.ts`): once a day at most, three times at most, and never again once the person makes that swipe themselves. A press on the strip or the desk's word press does not count as the swipe. The day is the company's (`todayYmdInAppTz`).
- **One sign for the gesture, not one a card.** My Bids and Recent Reports read the same count at the moment of asking, so whichever card comes into view first shows it and the other stays still that day.
- **The way back has its own count.** The first row of **Hidden** and of **Opened** shows its strip to the right when the group is opened and the row is in view.
- **Where it is kept.** `pipetooling.swipeSign.<user id>` in the device's storage, mirrored to `user_dismissals` through `dismissalStore.ts`, as a dismissed alert is. A new device is seeded at sign-in. No migration: the table takes any key and each person writes their own rows.
- **View as writes nothing**, since the person viewed has not seen it. A card still shows the sign once only while it stays on the page.
- **Reduced motion.** No row moves. The card draws the gesture's words under the group's heading for that visit (*Swipe a bid left to hide it.*, *Swipe a report left to mark it opened.*), and the visit counts the same.
- **Phones only.** A desk has no swipe, and no row there carries the sign.
- The foot's line stays where it was, as the standing words.

## Which row

| Card | The way out | The way back |
|---|---|---|
| My Bids | The first bid of the first group that draws one | The first bid in **Hidden** |
| Recent Reports | The first report in the list | The first report in **Opened** |

The row that carries the sign has `data-swipe-away-sign`.

## Known edge
