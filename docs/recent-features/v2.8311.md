# v2.8311 — Painted maps: the sample of a shared map sits on dry ground (2026-11-22)

A loose end of punch list [#387](../../to-dos/painted-maps-on-google.md), from the card's *What is left*. Client only: no migration and no function.

## What was there

`/way/sample`, which Settings → What customers see opens, draws a made-up site from `src/lib/paintedMap/paintedWaySample.ts`. Its point was 39.5, -98.35, chosen as *the middle of the lower 48, nobody's place*. That point is the water of Waconda Lake, a reservoir in Kansas. The step 4 walk of 2026-11-21 saw it on Google's road map and left it. On the page's **Streets** view the way in for deliveries crossed open water, with its truck gate and its laydown yard in the lake.

Checked again on 2026-11-22 before anything was changed: OpenStreetMap names the ground at that point *Waconda Lake, natural = water*, and Google's road map and its photograph both draw it as water.

## What changed

- **The point.** The sample's truck gate is at 39.4822, -98.3632, two kilometres south-west of where it was. Every corner and every place is still so many metres east and north of the gate, so the drawing is the same shape and the same size.
- **The ground.** Scrub beside a gravel road inside Glen Elder Wildlife Area, which the Kansas Department of Wildlife & Parks keeps. It is public ground, with no home, business or building near. The sample's whole footprint, with 20 m of margin, is inside the area's boundary as OpenStreetMap maps it, 250 m from the nearest edge.
- **The gate is on the road.** The words on the sample say *Start at the truck gate on the county road*. The gate now sits on the gravel road, and the way leads off it north and then east.
- **A test holds it there.** `paintedWaySample.test.ts` keeps every corner and every place inside the box of ground that was checked, and more than a kilometre from the old point. A sample moved later fails it until the new ground has been looked at.

The two wide starts that share the old numbers, `TASK_PLACE_WIDE_START` and the painting canvas's `WIDE_START`, are left alone. Each is only the middle of a first view to drag away from, when a map opens with nothing on it, and neither draws a site there.

## Seen
