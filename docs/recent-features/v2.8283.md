# v2.8283 — Materials: a job with many box photos no longer crashes the phone that took them (2026-11-22)

A crew lead's iPhone could not open the office's Materials page for one job (`/materials?tab=on-site&job=…`). Safari said *This webpage was reloaded because a problem occurred* and reloaded, again and again. The job has 92 boxes and 91 box photos, and that phone took the photos. No migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

- `photoBlobs.ts` kept every shot a phone took in IndexedDB (`pt.onSite.photos`) and nothing ever deleted one: `deletePhotoBlob` had no caller. v2.7819 left this written under *Not done*.
- `boxPhotoSource` answered `'phone'` first whenever the phone held the bytes, and `readBoxPhotoBytes` handed back an object URL of the whole 1,200 by 1,600 picture for a thumbnail too. Every other phone drew Drive's small thumbnail.
- `BoxPhotos` read every photo when it mounted, and the page mounts every box card at once. The taker's phone decoded about ninety whole pictures for 44 px squares and ran out of memory.
- The logs agreed: another phone that opened the page asked `job-photos` for 90 thumbnails, and the affected phone's reloads asked for none.

## What changed

- **A filed shot is read from the job's folder and leaves the phone** ([`boxPhotoSource.ts`](../../src/lib/materials/boxPhotoSource.ts), pure). `boxPhotoIsFiled` is `status === 'filed'` with a Drive id, and for such a row the source is `'drive'` even where the phone still holds bytes. `readBoxPhotoBytes` deletes the phone's copy on the way, not waited on. A row that is `on-phone`, `sending` or `shared` still reads the phone first and its copy is never deleted: those bytes may be the only copy, and **Send now** and **Share instead** need them.
- **Old copies go without a card being drawn** (`dropFiledPhotoBlobs` in `photoBlobs.ts`, called by `loadJob` in `onSiteStore.ts` after every read of a job, not waited on). It lists the ids the phone holds and deletes only those `phoneCopiesToDrop` matches to a filed row with a Drive id, in one transaction over one connection. An id no row of that job names is left alone.
- **A thumbnail is never the shot whole** (`thumbOfPhoto` and the pure `thumbSize` in `photoShrink.ts`). Where the phone's bytes, or the bucket's, are the source of a thumbnail, the square draws a small JPEG: the short side at twice the square's CSS size, so 88 by 117 for a 44 px square. The copies are made one at a time and each decoded shot is closed at once. The bytes handed back beside the URL are still the shot whole. When the browser cannot make the small copy the URL is null and the square stays empty, with the bytes still there for the two presses. `BoxPhotoViewer` still opens the whole picture, one at a time.
- **A square reads its picture only near the screen** (`BoxPhotos` in `PhotoPress.tsx`, no caller changed). One `IntersectionObserver` a strip with a margin of 600 px above and below; with no observer, as in the test DOM, every square reads at once. A row the sweep files is read again and its old picture stays up until the new one lands.

## The trade

On the phone that took it, a filed photo no longer draws with no signal: the phone's copy is gone and Drive cannot be reached. The square is the dashed one with its words, as on every other phone. The same holds for the minutes after the sweep files a shot and before Drive has made its thumbnail, and for a taker `job-photos` refuses (its gate is `can_report_stage_progress`, v2.7819's first *Not done*).

## Checked

- `boxPhotoSource.test.ts` holds the source rule and which copies may go. `photoShrink.test.ts` holds the thumbnail's size, `photoThumb.test.ts` that one copy is made at a time and the decoded shot is closed, `boxPhotoBytesIo.test.ts` that a filed shot reads Drive and deletes the copy while a waiting shot keeps its bytes whole beside a small picture. `PhotoPress.render.test.tsx` holds the read near the screen, the re-read of a shot the sweep filed, and **Send now** for a shot whose small copy could not be made.
- In a browser at 375 px on the job itself, as the dev, looking only (Chromium on a dev server, production data). All 91 of the job's photo rows are filed with a Drive id. This browser's own store was seeded with a 1,200 by 1,600 JPEG of 1.14 MB under each of the 91 ids, and one more under an id no row has. After a reload: the 91 were gone from the store by the time the boxes drew and the one stayed; at the top of the page 2 of the 90 squares had read their picture and 88 had not, the nearest of those 1,679 px down; each scroll read the squares it brought near and asked `job-photos` once a square (3 squares, 3 asks); after a pass down the page 83 squares held a picture, 72 at 300 by 400 and 11 at 400 by 300, none wider than 400 px, and the 7 a fast scroll had jumped past read when scrolled to. A press on a filed thumbnail opened the picture whole at 1,200 by 1,600.
- The same browser, `readBoxPhotoBytes` asked directly for the seeded id as a shot that waits (`on-phone`, `sending`, `shared`): the thumbnail was 88 by 117 and 3.8 KB for a 44 px square, 144 by 192 for 72 px and 420 by 560 for a take's tile, the bytes beside it were the whole 1,139,793, `full` was 1,200 by 1,600, twelve asked together all came back small, and the copy was still in the store afterwards.

## Not done

- `photoBlobs.ts` still opens a connection a call. The other stores in the app do the same, and Safari is known to drop a connection kept open while a page sleeps. The reads are now a few at a time, and the sweep uses one connection.
- The bytes of a shot marked `shared` stay on the phone. Nothing files such a shot, so nothing says its copy may go.
- Not tried on the affected iPhone itself.
