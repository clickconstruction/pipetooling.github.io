# v2.7078 — Materials: what a site's material is worth, and a name tied to the parts book (2026-11-12)

Punch list #330, PR 4 of 5. The app priced what went missing and never what is on hand. On the first site read about one piece in twenty had a price, so a bare total would have priced a sliver and read as the whole. This PR adds the figure with its cover, and the press that raises the cover.

## What changed

- **The rules** (`src/lib/materials/siteWorth.ts`), pure, 6 tests: `rowWorth` prices a parts-book row through the one rule every on-site money figure reads (`priceLookup`: a fresh book price, the invoice price past 90 days, a stale book price flagged) across all its lines, and a typed row line by line from the price the office read off its invoice; `siteWorth` adds what is known, counts the lines with a price against the lines on hand, counts the stale ones and lists the rows with an unpriced line, most pieces first; `worthWords` never drops the cover (*$740.00 known* over *3 of 6 lines have a price · 1 on a price older than 90 days*, or *No price yet*).
- **The figure**: a ninth tile, **Worth**, for the roles that read the desk's money. A press narrows the list to the rows with a line that has no price. A priced row carries its money and where the price came from, amber when the book's check is older than 90 days.
- **This is a parts-book part**: on an open row with no book part, for the roles that may answer Same part?. An inline picker searches the parts book by words (`bookSearchIo.ts`: every word in the name, any order; the search opens on the row's own words with the inch marks set aside). A pick writes one `same` tie between the book part and the row's name in `on_site_part_ties`, the table PR 3 made. `tieCanon` already keys such a group on the book part, so the row takes the book's name (`sitePartRows`' `bookNames`) and price, and the store reads that part's prices (`loadBookPrices`).
- **Names joined here** lists a book tie like any other, with **Undo** (`joinedTies` now reads through the groups, since a tied book part may have no line of its own on the site).

No migration: the tie is a row of PR 3's table.

## What it does not do

- **A price typed off an invoice still lives on the device that read it** (#209's open gap). Storing it on the delivered row would raise the cover at once; that is #209's PR.
- **A tie is the site's own.** The job page's Missing table still prices each spelling as before.
