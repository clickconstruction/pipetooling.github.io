# v2.7544 — Checklist: move a task to a day you can do it (2026-11-16)

Punch list #369, step C3 of the Task Mode train (tool 1), stacked on C2 (v2.7543). It calls `task_move_day` from C1's migration.

## Why

Read through the dev seat on 2026-11-16, 12 of the 17 open one-time tasks were more than 80 days old and no due day had ever been changed. Only the giver or the office could move a task, from Manage, so the person holding it had two choices: do it, or leave it.

## What changed

- **⋯ More**, a quiet pill on a card of Today and at the top of an open Still open row (`useTaskDoerActs`, `TASK_MORE_LABEL`). It opens *More for this task*, which lists what this task allows (`taskMoreChoices`). A task with nothing to offer draws no More. This step puts one row in it, and the next steps add theirs.
- **Not today** (`TaskMoveDaySheet`), for an open one-time task: one joined bar in the New task window's shape, **Tomorrow · Monday · Pick a day** (`moveDayPicks`). The date box opens only for Pick a day. A green line says what the press will do, *It moves to Fri, Nov 20. Imani is told.*, and the press names the day. Until a day the database will take is picked, the press is dead and says why in the database's own words (`moveDayRule`).
- **The due day moves and nothing else** (`moveTaskDay` → `task_move_day`). The task keeps its place in the list, its age and its thread. Its row then reads *due Fri, Nov 20* in grey, and sinks under the tasks that are late or older, since the list is ordered by due day.
- **The giver is told** by a push through the function the completion push uses (`tellTaskGiver`, `moveDayPush`): *Theo moved "Give every tool a home" to Fri, Nov 20.* A task the person gave themself tells nobody.
- **A row says how often it was moved**: *moved once*, *moved twice*, *moved 3 times* (`movedTimesWords`), the mock-up's answer to a task pushed forever.
- **The threads word the tools' lines** (`doerLineWords`): *is stuck*, *closed it as no longer applying*, *handed it to*, *put a map on it*. The shared thread leaves the `moved` line out, since its due-day line already says the change with both days.

## How it is built

- `taskDoerActs.ts` gains the target a tool acts on, More's rows, the day arithmetic and the words. `taskDoerActsIo.ts` gains `moveTaskDay` and `tellTaskGiver`.
- `useTaskDoerActs` is one hook for the page: a More press per task and one set of sheets. The card knows none of it. It sits above the tab's loading return, with the tab's other hooks.
- `ChecklistOutstandingSection` takes `extrasFor`, drawn at the top of a row's open panel.
- Today reads the givers' names beside the names of the people who hear.
