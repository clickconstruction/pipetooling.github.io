# v2.3417 — Pipeline: stages recognized from the line items (2026-09-14)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Second PR of the *Where the Job Is* train (owner-approved brief, artifact *Where the Job Is*, 2026-09-14; PR 1 was v2.3416, the legend).

## Why

The v2.3198 stage bar fires only on a job with an Order line item, and the only door to Order is the selector on Bill → ① Line Items. The 2026-09-14 census of production: 0 Working jobs with an Order row (3 Order rows in the whole ledger), while seven jobs were split exactly *Rough In · Top Out · Trim Set* at 40 / 40 / 20 by the Multiple Segment Generator's preset — four of them Working — every row kind `any` from the v2.3083 backfill. The plumbing vocabulary in ascending order *is* a stage plan; the app should read it instead of asking.

## The rule — [`stageRecognition.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/stageRecognition.ts) (+11 tests)

- `stageRank(name)`: Ground / Pre-pour / Slab → 0 · Rough → 1 · Top Out / Stack Out → 2 · Trim / Finish → 3 · Final / Punch → 4; a "Stage 2 —" prefix is dropped first; systems (Gas line, Sewer & water), fees, change orders and the migrated *Job total* rank nothing.
- `recognizeStages(fixtures)`: **no explicit Order row** (the office declared a plan — leave it) · at least **two priced rows of the `any` default** carrying a rank · ranks **never go backwards** in sequence order (two Rough In rows, one per floor, are fine; Trim before Rough is not a plan). Unranked rows stay Any; a deliberate plain row (kind null) and an unpriced row are never recognized. Reasons come back for a tooltip or a test: `explicit` · `too-few` · `out-of-order`.
- `applyRecognizedStages(fixtures)` re-kinds the recognized ids to `order` and returns the same array when nothing changed.

## Where it lands

- **The Pipeline row** — [`pipelineStageBar.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/pipelineStageBar.ts) (+1 test): `stageBarAvailable` is true for an explicit Order row *or* a recognized plan; `buildPipelineStageBar` re-kinds the recognized rows before `buildStagePlan`, so the chips, segments and caption come out exactly as they would for an Order job. `PipelineStageBar.recognized` says which it was. Every consumer (both tables, the card list) is unchanged.
- **The crew's report picker** — [`stageProgressReport.ts`](../../src/lib/reports/stageProgressReport.ts) (+2 tests): `stageProgressRowsFromRpc` applies the same rule to the `list_job_stage_progress` rows (weight > 0 is the priced test), so `stageModeAvailable`, `stagePickerNumber` and `defaultStageToReport` see numbered stages on a recognized job. The RPC and `record_stage_progress` are unchanged — they never keyed on the kind.
- **Not the Bill tab.** Owner decision 2026-09-14 ("row only, for now"): `stagePlanForm.ts` and `_shared/stagePlan.ts` are untouched, so a recognized plan draws and reports as stages but bills exactly as before — a draw does not wait on the stage above it until the office sets Order.

## Guides

*bill a customer and get paid* (the stage-bar paragraph), *reports* (the stage list), *split a job into stages and bill stage by stage* (the new second paragraph: what recognition does and does not do).
