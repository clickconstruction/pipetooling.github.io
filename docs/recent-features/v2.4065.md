# v2.4065 — The full-screen button on the Lien desk, Put a GC on notice and Accounts Receivable (2026-09-28)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. After [v2.4045](v2.4045.md) (the toggle in the shared shell) and [v2.4049](v2.4049.md) (the sweep's three columns). The owner, 2026-09-28, from the captures on the mock-up canvas (artifact `9Cp63b55vq9db8Yka2UsnA`): *build it on all three.*

## Why

These three draw their own frames — `min(1140px, calc(100vw - 2rem))` on the two lien windows, `maxWidth: 980` on Accounts Receivable — so the shell's `fullScreenKey` could not reach them. The toggle's state, memory and button come out of the shell into one module any dialog can use.

## What shipped

- **[`ModalFullScreenToggle.tsx`](../../src/components/ModalFullScreenToggle.tsx)** — `useModalFullScreen(key)` (the remembered choice, false on a phone whatever was kept, `showToggle`) and `ModalFullScreenButton` (the chrome, `aria-pressed`, the name as tooltip). `ResponsiveModalShell` now uses both; its behavior and render cases are unchanged.
- **Lien desk** (`lien-desk`) — the button sits left of the absolute × (`right: 3.1rem`); full screen the panel is `100vw` by `calc(100dvh - var(--app-bottom-chrome))`, no radius, and its `auto 1fr auto` rows give the job list and the notice the whole height.
- **Put a GC on notice** (`gc-on-notice`) — the × moves into a small flex group with the button; the panel sizes as the desk's does.
- **Accounts Receivable** (`accounts-receivable`) — the button sits between ⋯ and Close; full screen the overlay drops its padding and ends above the app's bottom bar (`bottom: var(--app-bottom-chrome)`, it used to run under the z-1000 bar), and the panel takes the whole overlay with no max width.
- **Guides** — `send-lien-notices-from-the-lien-desk` (covers both lien windows) and `match-bank-deposits-to-bills` each gain *Give it the whole screen.*
- Three render cases, one per dialog: the button reads *Full screen*, a press sizes the panel to the screen and reads *Back to a window*, a second press restores it; no key is left in storage.

## Decided here

- **Full screen here is the same window with more room** — none of the three changes its columns the way the sweep's third column did. The captures on the canvas showed that is already worth it for the Lien desk's gates and the GC window's jobs table; a redrawn layout is a separate ask.
- **Each key is its own memory.** The Lien desk full screen does not make Put a GC on notice full screen.

## Verified

- In the browser (Playwright against this worktree's dev server, the dev login on production data, read only, 1440 × 900): each dialog's real button was pressed. Window → full screen → window measured 1140 × 808 at (150, 16) → 1440 × 840 at (0, 0) → 1140 × 808 on the Lien desk and on Put a GC on notice (RMC- Dudley Mason), and 980 wide → 1440 × 840 → 980 wide on Accounts Receivable; the radius went 10px / 8px → 0 → back; the button read *Full screen* → *Back to a window* → *Full screen*; no key was left in storage after the second press. The 840 is the screen less the app's 60 px bottom bar.
