# v2.6224 — The morning run fills {amount} and {bill} from the job's newest open bill (punch list #124, the last PR) (2026-10-31)

Punch list #124's open piece, left by v2.6079 (*Filling `{amount}` and `{bill}` in the run means reading each customer's newest bill the way the box does; that is its own PR*). The morning run (`src/lib/flows/nudgeDispatch.ts` → `planNudgeRun`, the edge function `messages-nudge-dispatch`) filled `{first}`, `{job}` and `{link}` and kept back or held a line with any other blank. Now it fills the bill's three blanks the way the reply box does at a press, through the box's own kernel and no second copy. No migration. This PR closes the card: `to-dos/pipeline-stage-texts/` is deleted.

## What changed

- **`planNudgeRun`** takes `papers`, each residential customer's bills as the reply box offers them (`messages/papers.ts`'s `billPapers`, by contact), and fills `amount`, `bill` and `number` from `defaultPaperFills(papers, step.jobId, jobOnly)`: the step's job's newest open bill and no other job's, as a job's box speaks of its own bills; with no job on the step, their newest open bill anywhere, as a thread's box does. `{amount}` is what is left on the bill after the payment rule, formatted as the box formats it (`billMoney`: `$640.50`, `$1,200`); `{bill}` is the bill's own pay page (`payLinkUrl(invoice.id)`, only for a bill with a Stripe id and a pay-link id, the rule `theyOweUs.ts` and `billLink.ts` already apply); `{number}` is the job's number. A person with no entry, or no open bill on the job, keeps the blanks and the v2.6079 hold with its words (*the line still has {amount} and {bill}*).
- **`nudgeRunInputs.ts`**: `NudgeRunRows.bills` (`RunBillRows`: the residential customers' `jobs_ledger` rows with `customer_id`, `jobs_ledger_invoices` and `jobs_ledger_payments`, the columns `messages/papersIo.ts` selects), `billPapersByCustomer` folds them to each customer's papers with that customer's own jobs and payments only, and `nudgeRunReads` hands a residential contact theirs by the flow rows' `card.customer_id`, as the box reads them by the customer.
- **The edge function** `messages-nudge-dispatch` reads the three tables for the residential contacts' customer ids, a hundred at a time, and passes them as `bills`; nothing else of its own changed. The kernel copies are regenerated (`npm run gen:edge-kernels`): `nudgeDispatch.ts`, `nudgeRunInputs.ts` and six files new to the copy, `messages/papers.ts`, `followUp/theyOweUs.ts`, `billing/payLink.ts`, `contacts/kernel.ts`, `customerSimilarity.ts` and `identityKey.ts`. **Redeploy required**; `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` says so and names the reads.
- **Tests**: `nudgeDispatch.test.ts` (Dana's Paid reminder the morning after her pay-by day: the fill with cents and the pay page; whole dollars and `{number}`; the hold for no bill read and for a bill paid, on Sends and on Offers; a bill outside Stripe fills the amount and leaves `{bill}`; another job's bill is never spoken of; the app's own Paid words and the person's reason first); `nudgeRunInputs.test.ts` (`billPapersByCustomer` keeps each customer's payments to their own bills; `nudgeRunReads` hands a residential contact their papers and a trade partner none).
- **Guides**: *work Today's sends* → **When a text is held** says the app fills `{amount}` and `{bill}` from the job's newest open bill, so the hold shows for a customer with no open bill or a bill with no pay page; *offer the app's reminders* says the Paid reminder fills both, so the office may move Paid from Offers to Sends.

## Decisions

- **The box's kernel, not a second read.** The run calls `billPapers` and `defaultPaperFills`, the same two functions `ReplyBox` runs through `replyFills` at a press, so the morning's figure and link are the ones the office would see in the thread.
- **A bill outside Stripe leaves `{bill}`**, as the box does: the brief allowed the sent copy's door instead, but the box has no such fill (it leaves the blank and the office presses Attach), and the run says the same thing with the same words. The amount still fills.
- **The office moves Paid to Sends; this PR does not flip prod's switch.** Read on 2026-10-31 as the dev: `messages_nudge_app_sends_v1` is `{"residential": {"paid": "offer", "review": "offer", "approved": "offer"}}`.

## What prod holds, and what this PR does not fix

Read on 2026-10-31 as the dev: `contacts` has **no row with `kind = 'residential'`**, `nudge_offers` has never held an Approved, Paid or Review asked row, and the 2026-10-30 run's summary is *No text went. Customers wait until the run can read their page.* (`prepared: 0`, `customersHeld: 16`). Two things stand between this fill and a live Paid reminder, and both are outside this PR:

1. **No contact is a residential customer yet**, so the run has nobody whose flow is the residential one.
2. **The residential flow's loader is not wired.** `residentialStampsFor` (`flows/residentialLoad.ts`, v2.5395, moved out of `load.ts` in v2.5490) has no caller outside its tests: `flowStampsFor` gives a `residential` person empty lanes, in the app's step card (`useFlowPerson` → `personFlowFrom`) and in the run alike, so a residential customer's Paid step is never due. Wiring it means reading each residential customer's Pipeline rows (`jobs_ledger`, blocks, clock-ins, reports, estimates, status events, bills, payments, texts, skips, pay speeds and promises) in `flow_rows_for` or beside it, in the client and the run, and is a PR of its own.

Once both land, the first morning fills the Paid line as the tests draw it, and a step on Sends texts it at 9 AM.
