# v2.5758 — Legal: the county map zooms, and shows today’s cases while you color (2026-10-23)

Punch list #144, PR 1b: the owner settled the mock-up (`to-dos/legal-firms-by-county/mockup.html`, 2026-10-22 23:05) after PR 1 (v2.5756) was queued, with four additions to the Settings picker. Built on main after v2.5756; no migration.

- **Zoom and pan** (`src/lib/legal/countyMapView.ts`, pure, tested): one SVG whose view box moves while the 254 paths are drawn once. The wheel zooms at the cursor (`exp(deltaY · 0.0015)`), a pinch at the midpoint, **+** and **−** step by 1.4× about the middle, a drag pans, **Central Texas** (the service area, −99.3…−96.9 by 28.95…31.05, the view it opens on) and **All of Texas** reset. The view never shows more than the state plus a margin nor less than a thirtieth of it, and its middle stays inside the world. County names draw once the view is a third of the state or less; a colored county's name from 70%. A press that travels more than 3 px is a drag, and the click that ends it is swallowed, the guard lifting on the next tick, so a cancelled drag never eats a later click.
- **Today's cases as dots** (`src/lib/legal/firmMap.ts`, pure, tested; `src/hooks/useLegalFirmMapCases.ts` gathers): the desk's own Collections accounts (the billed jobs in Collections grouped by payer as `groupCollectionsByPayer` does, plus those given up on), placed at the first job's geocode from the shared `address_geocodes` cache, the county from the property record (`customer_addresses.county` by the job's `customer_address_id`) else point-in-county over the county file (`countyAt.ts`, ray casting, tested against the real file with the live addresses). The venue follows the job, not the customer. Each dot is 6–12 px on the page by balance whatever the zoom (×0.75 on the picker, where they are for reference), colored by stand: amber *needs a dev's eyes* (no matter, or review / pulled), green *with the firm*, gray *given up on* (the job flag), red when not with the firm and in a county no firm covers. A closed matter is off the map.
- **The contents card** (`LegalCountyContentsCard.tsx`) under the firm cards, with Save counties / Undo changes moved above it as the mock-up has them: the county under the pointer, its firm or *no firm covers it*, its cases with where each stands, jobs, balance and **Open on the desk** (`/jobs?tab=stages&legal=<payer key>`). A dot click holds its case on the card, marked, until another dot is clicked; hovering moves the card only while nothing is held. A dot click never colors a county; a county click still colors and the card shows that county.
- The kernel is built once for PR 3's Map lens: `legendCounts` (cases and dollars per firm, the uncovered last) and `uncoveredWords` (the flag above the lens) ship here unread.

Guide `set-which-law-firm-covers-each-county` gains *Move around the map* and *See where today's cases sit*. The render smoke covers the dots, the card, the hold and the + button.
