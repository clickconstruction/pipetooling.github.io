# v2.6964 — The Bridge keeps what the owner sets: the floor, the typed cash and the accounts left out (punch list #303) (2026-11-11)

One of the four items the Bridge's train left open (`to-dos/bridge-one-story/README.md`), on the owner's word of 2026-11-11: build it first. One migration. No client change, no edge function.

## What was wrong

Behind **Money** on `/bridge` the page saves three `app_settings` rows with an upsert: the cash floor (`bridge_cash_floor_usd_v1`), the typed cash on hand (`bridge_cash_on_hand_v1`) and the bank accounts left out of cash on hand (`bridge_cash_accounts_out_v1`, v2.6838). No key-scoped policy named them, so only the baseline's *Devs can manage app settings* let the write through. The owner's role has opened the page since v2.6763 and was refused on all three. A switch on an account held for the visit and was lost on the next one, so the weekly email never left that account out. The floor did not move at all.

Read on prod the same day through the dev seat: the floor has no row, so nobody has ever saved it and the page reads the $5,000 it ships with. One account, a dev's, has opened the page. The weekly email is on and its first send is Monday 2026-11-16, to the owner and the devs, with a press that leads to these saves.

## What changed

- **The migration** (`20261112013000_bridge_cash_keys_owner.sql`, `docs/migrations/`): `master_or_dev_insert_bridge_cash_keys` and `master_or_dev_update_bridge_cash_keys` on `app_settings`, the three keys and `is_master_or_dev()`. An upsert needs both, and the floor has no row to update. No delete policy: the page never deletes them. `bridge_target_usd_v1` is left out, since the page stopped writing it.
- Nothing on the page changes. Its saves, its words and the guide's *The choice is saved, so it stands until you change it* were already written for a save that passes.

## Verify

Run on a local Postgres 15 with the baseline's `app_settings`, its two general policies, the read-only fences and the real `is_dev()` and `is_master_or_dev()`, the migration applied twice. Each of five accounts made the page's own upsert on five keys, before and after:

- **Before**: the dev passes on every key; the owner, a read-only owner, the controller and the assistant are refused on every key.
- **After**: the owner passes on the three keys, on the insert path (the floor with no row) and the update path, and is still refused on `bridge_target_usd_v1` and on a key the migration does not name. A read-only owner, the controller and the assistant are refused as before. The dev is unchanged.

After the push, as the owner's role on `/bridge`: behind **Money**, type a **Floor** and press **Set**. The toast reads *Cash floor set*. Clear one account's box under **The bank's accounts**, leave the page and come back: the row still reads *Left out*.

## Deploy

`supabase db push` after the merge. The old client is unaffected: it makes the same writes and they now pass.
