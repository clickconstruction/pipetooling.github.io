# v2.6489 — The Scoreboard reads every crew row, On its way counts a job photo, and an On paper send leaves its thread line (2026-11-04)

Three fixes read off main. The Development page's Scoreboard (v2.6407, v2.6457) read a job's crew rows with one unpaged `.limit(5000)` per job, which PostgREST's `max_rows` (1,000, `supabase/config.toml`) cuts with no error, the incident class v2.6463 and v2.6465 swept: a job past 1,000 person-days undercounted Hours, Days, Labor, Cost and Profit, its recorded-hours read paged with no order, and the man-hours fallback was unpaged too. The job-photos function's `waiting` count, behind the strip's and the Documents tab's *N on its way*, counted the checklist's and the boxes' bucket rows and never the Job photos window's (v2.6365) or a report's, so a photo just added read as nowhere until the sweep filed it. And the On paper send of an agreement (`share-job-contract` `send_to_sign`) wrote no line on the customer's thread, where the link send and the signature each write one.

## What changed

- `src/lib/developments/fetchSiteLabor.ts`: the crew read per job pages through `fetchAllRows` in `work_date` then `person_name` order (the table's key); the recorded-hours read keeps its day chunks and gains the same order (one row per person and day on both relations); `readManHours` pages `get_man_hours_by_job` in `job_id` then `person_name` order (the RPC groups by the pair).
- `supabase/functions/job-photos/index.ts`: `waitingCount` adds a head count of `job_photo_notes` (added, in the bucket, not yet filed) and of `report_photos` (saved on a report, in the bucket, not yet filed), each the photo-save cron's own clause. **Redeploy required.**
- `supabase/functions/share-job-contract/index.ts`: `send_to_sign` writes the customer's thread line through `_shared/jobContractThreadLine.ts` after the email went and the `sent` event is logged, with the row's kind, so a warranty reads *The warranty went to Dana to initial and sign: …* and a contract *The contract went to Dana to sign: …*. **Redeploy required.**
- `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`: the job-photos `waiting` sentence and status, and a v2.6489 paragraph under share-job-contract.

## Tests

Both cases fail against the unpaged reads.
- `src/lib/photos/jobPhotosWaiting.test.ts`: reads the function's source and holds `waitingCount` to one `head: true` count per table with each table's clause, four tables added.
- `src/lib/jobs/contractPaperThreadLine.test.ts`: reads the two senders' sources; the On paper branch calls the shared writer with `event: 'sent'`, the row's kind, its heading and the send's time, after the email and the event, and the link send writes the same line.
