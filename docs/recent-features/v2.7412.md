# v2.7412 — Tagged job notes: tag and answer from the job window (2026-11-15)

Punch list #363, PR 6 of six: the second note box. One migration, `20261116190000_job_note_tags_from_the_job_window.sql` (its doc is in `docs/migrations/`), and the client that sends through it.

## Why this was not the small PR the card planned

The job window's box does not insert a note. It posts through the outbox (`pressThroughOutbox`, kind `job-note`, landed by `land_field_press`), so a note typed with no signal is kept on the phone. The tags had to ride that press.

## What changed

- **The migration**: `land_field_press` writes the tags of a typed note, and `job_note_tags_write` takes a transaction-local marker in place of its two-minute rule, since a kept note lands with the time it was pressed.
- **`JobNotePayload`** (`src/lib/offline/outbox.ts`) carries `typed` and `tagged`, and `rpcPayload` sends both. An absent `typed` is sent as false.
- **`useJobThreadNotesForModal.ts`**: `submitNoteWithBody` takes the same `{ tagged, typed }` as the Pipeline's hook, and `submitNote` takes the picked ids. When a typed note lands, the count reads again and `notify-note-tag` is called. The two lines the job window composes itself, the % line and the kept summary, say `typed: false`.
- **`JobThreadNotesPanel.tsx`**: with `tagJobId` and `onSubmitTagged` it draws the @ button, the list above the box, the picked names, and `TaggedNoteBody` on a posted note. `DetailJobModal`, `JobModeDetailsSection` and `QuickfillCompleteNoBillSection` pass both. An estimate's notes pass neither and are as they were.
- **The guide** no longer says the job window is left out.

## One thing to know

A note kept on the phone and sent more than fifteen minutes later carries its tag, and the tag counts above Jobs at once. Its phone notice is not sent: `notify-note-tag` pushes only for a note that is new, and a kept note carries the time it was pressed.
