# v2.7894 — Painted maps: the Saved band's Text it from my phone… opens Messages at once and no longer says it did not (2026-11-19)

Punch list #394, found on the walk of step 3 and fixed on the owner's word (*Yes, fix it*).

## Why

The Saved band (#392, v2.7866) carries its own **Text it from my phone…**, and it is the first one a painter meets after a save. It was built beside the Share sheet's press while that press was changing, so it kept the older order: make the link, then open Messages.

The walk on an iPhone simulator after v2.7877 showed two things on the band.

- Messages opened, and the band then read *The link is ready. Press Open Messages.* with the press renamed **Open Messages**. The band waits 1.5 seconds to see the page left, and the simulator took longer than that to switch apps. This is the false note v2.7863 took out of the sheet.
- The link came back fast enough that Safari asked nothing. On a slow signal the same press hands the address over after a wait, and Safari then asks *Open this page in “Messages”?* first.

## What changed

- **The band asks the server once whether a link can be made** (`desk.can`, nothing written), where its press is offered. Only on a yes does the press hand the phone its text before the link is back.
- **On a yes the phone picks the link's code** and the press sends the ask for the link, then opens Messages, both inside the press. The same doors as the sheet: `pickPaintedMapLinkCode` and `registerPaintedMapLink`.
- **A text around a link that was not made is called out.** A link refused, lost, or made under another code: the band says the reason and *The link in that text will not open. Do not send it.*
- **The band listens for the page being left** (`onPageLeft`) for a minute after a hand-off, and takes back *The link is ready. Press Open Messages.* and *This phone would not open Messages…*. A reason that is not about Messages stays.
- **Without the yes the band works as before**: the link first, then Messages, with **Open Messages** as the second press. That path now makes its link through the same ask, so `mintPaintedMapLink` is gone again. The band was its one caller.

No function deploy and no migration. `desk.can` and a code from the phone are live since v2.7877.
