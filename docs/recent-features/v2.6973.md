# v2.6973 — The Bridge's pipe stands up, and Market reads at what it is worth (punch list #318, PR 1) (2026-11-11)

PR 1 of six for *Market and Sales on the won rates* (`to-dos/market-sales-won-rates/README.md`): the owner's rules of 2026-11-11, built on the branch `spike/bridge-scaling`, walked by him and kept. Client only. No migration, no edge function.

## What changed

- **The pipe stands up** (`src/lib/bridge/flowStripDown.ts`, `BridgeFlowStripDown.tsx`): the same five sections `buildFlowStrip` makes, drawn top to bottom, each step's words beside its section. The width is still the dollars a week, sized as pipe is. The work waiting in front of a step is a bar under its words, as long as its weeks, in place of the block on top. A press is the whole row and opens the step's desk. `?pipe=across` draws the pipe lying down, as it was.
- **Where it sits** (`BridgeWeek.tsx`, `stripSide`): at a desk the pipe is a column beside the week's call, in a usual week and in a week cash leads. On a phone it keeps its place: under the tiles, or under the card in a week cash leads.
- ***How the week runs* is off the first screen** (`BridgeWeek.tsx`): the side panel a desk showed until a first call existed. The owner could not read it and said to take it off. With the pipe beside the card it had no room either. `WEEK_HOW` stays in `weekCall.ts`, which is copied to the edge functions.
- **Estimates through their won rate** (`estimateWins.ts`, `loadEstimateWins.ts`): won is approved; lost is declined, not chosen, or 90 days with no answer. *Not chosen* is an estimate with no answer whose customer approved another one for the same address after this one was sent. The rate is won dollars over won and lost dollars. *Sold a week* is the estimates sent in the page's eight weeks, a week, times the rate. Change orders and bid proposals are left out.
- **Bids through theirs** (`bidWins.ts`, `loadBidWins.ts`): a bid is won when it is marked won, or marked started or complete, and lost when it is marked lost. A job made from a bid decides nothing. A bid with no mark is part lost by its age: nothing under 90 days, half from 90, 95% from 180. Each bid is read at the rate for bids its size (under $10k, $10k to $50k, $50k to $500k, over $500k); a size with nothing won or lost reads at the rate of all sizes together. A won bid with no value takes its job's revenue. A bid never sent and a ZZ test bid are in no count.
- **Market is worth** (`buildMarketWorth`, `withMarketWorth`): what the bids win a week plus what the estimates sell a week. The Market row reads *worth $X a week* with one short line for each part, the pipe is sized again against that amount, and the row's words for a screen reader say the same amount. One press under the pipe, *How Market's figure is counted*, opens every sentence behind it. Until both reads are in, and when either fails, the row reads the dollars bid, as before, under the owner's word *bid*.
- **The left-out list is not shipped.** The loaders take a list of what the Bridge leaves out (`leftOut.ts`: the rule alone, by development, customer or address) and the page hands them an empty one. Nothing is left out yet.

## Not in this PR

- **The page still tells two stories about Market.** Behind **The flow**, the Market and Sales cards, the *short of the field* badge, the slowest-step reading, the dial and the weekly email count the old way (`stageFlow.ts`, `dial.ts`, `demandPace.ts`). PR 4 moves the page and PR 5 the email.
- **The first screen's budget.** #303 held a phone's first screen to 150 words and 24 numbers, measured with the pipe drawn across. The standing pipe prints a row for each step: on the test's usual week it is 65 words and 12 numbers against 21 and 6, which puts that screen at 161 words and 27 numbers. `stripDownWords` lists what it prints so the count can be held once the owner says which gives: the budget or the rows.
- The header's *reads the last 8 weeks* line and the list of what the Bridge leaves out, both walked on the spike, are not part of card #318.
