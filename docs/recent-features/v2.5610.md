# v2.5610 — Training: an offered course, and Intro to plumbing's Start here and Water in (2026-10-18)

Punch list #119, PR 1 of six (the plan and the outline at `to-dos/intro-plumbing/README.md`, 2026-10-17; the owner: build it, checking the work along the way). The second course rides the Training mechanism (#111) and its drawings (#116) and needed one thing from them: a way to open a course to a role without requiring it. This PR is that state, the drawing language for pipes, and the first eleven lessons as the proof. Migration `20261018010000_training_offered.sql` (`docs/migrations/20261018010000_training_offered.md`); no edge function.

- **The offered state.** `training_courses.offered boolean` (default false) and the seed row `intro-to-plumbing`, off, required of helpers, offered, good for 120 months. `trainingState.ts`: `TrainingPolicy.offered`, `offeredCourses(policies, passes, role)` (on, offered, not required of the role, with the newest pass and no clock; a null role sees no offers, as it sees no standings) and `offeredPress` (Start, Continue, Read it again). `trainingIo.ts` reads the catalog with `select *` so a client ahead of the push reads the column as false, and `loadTrainingTakenCount` counts distinct passers for the office. `training_state_for`, `training_standings`, the People rail and the Users row are untouched: an offered course has no colour anywhere.
- **The card** (`DashboardTrainingSection.tsx`): an **Available** line under the coloured rows, one row per offered course with its size from `course.md` and one quiet press; the verdict line counts required courses only, and a viewer with nothing required and one offer sees the card with the Available row alone (`data-training-card="none"`). A role the course is required of sees it as a standing, never under Available.
- **Settings → Training** (`SettingsTrainingTab.tsx`): **Also open to · Everyone** on each course, saving `offered`, with *N took it* while the course is on.
- **The drawing language** (`src/components/training/figures/plumbingParts.tsx`, its tints and widths in `plumbingTokens.ts`): `Pipe` (two lines, the bore in a tint: cold `--bg-blue-200`, hot `--bg-red-100`, waste `--bg-amber-100`, air the surface; supply narrow, drain wide), `Flow` (the arrow on the centreline), `Seal` and `Hatch` (the trap's seal), `Valve` (ball, gate, check, PRV, angle stop, with the water drawn through), `Hub`, `Sink`, `Person`, `Label`, `Panel`. A figure composes them in thirty lines; a change to how a pipe looks is one edit.
- **Eleven figures**, one per lesson: `two-systems`, `meter-and-main-shutoff`, `pressure-and-prv`, `water-heater-cutaway`, `trunk-vs-manifold`, `copper-sweat-joint`, `pex-joints`, `solvent-joint`, `ball-gate-check`, `fixture-valves`, `supply-fittings`. Each shot at 375, 768 and 1280 px in light and dark before the merge, as #116 did.
- **The course** (`src/content/training/intro-to-plumbing/`): `course.md` (*An introduction, not a licence*; 20 drawn, 14 pass; 11 lessons, 70 minutes for now), lessons 01 (Start here: Two systems, one house) and 02 to 11 (Water in: the meter and the main shutoff; pressure and the PRV; the water heater; hot and cold around the house; copper; PEX; CPVC and the glued joint; ball, gate and check valves; valves at the fixture and the hose; supply fittings), and a bank of twenty-four. Every `from:` names the outline's lesson until the owner's corrections replace it. Numbers cite the International Plumbing Code where it gives one and are the owner's to correct.
- **Tests**: `trainingState.test.ts` (an offered course is a standing for its required role and an offer for every other, never both; the press), the card's smoke (the Available row, no verdict, the press by record; a helper never sees Available), the Settings smoke (the chip saves `offered`; *3 took it*), `figures.test.ts` and `TrainingFigure.render.test.tsx` (the eleven render named by their captions), `courseContent.test.ts` (the eleven lessons and the bank hold to the shape and the plain words), the bed `supabase/tests/training/20_scenario.sql` section 9.
- **Docs**: the guide `take-a-training-course` (the Available row), `docs/twins/APP_DIRECTORY.md` (the course's path), `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`, `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` and `GLOSSARY.md` (the offered state, the second course).
