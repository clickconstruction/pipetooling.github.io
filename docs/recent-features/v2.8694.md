# v2.8694 — A job's Documents tab: Secrecy on this site (2026-11-27)

Punch list #439, piece 4, its second door (4c). Client only: no migration, no edge function. It stands on piece 4b's block (v2.8693) and on the ground of piece 4a, the migration `20261127052337_site_secrecy.sql`. Until that file is pushed the block draws nothing.

## What was there

Piece 4b put **Secrecy on this site** on a development's page. A job that is in no development had no door: its site is its address, and no screen named an address as a site. A job in a development said nothing on its own tab about the secrecy its development carries.

## What changed

- **A lone job's tab is its door.** A job with an address and no development draws **Secrecy on this site** on its Documents tab, under **Papers before this job opens**. Off, its lead reads *Turn it on and every job at this address stays shut to our own people until they sign one paper.* The panel, the count before the last press, the on state and **Turn it off…** are piece 4b's, unchanged.
- **An address is one site with its other spellings.** The block finds the site by the address's own key and by every key a **Same place** answer joins to it, the rule the database follows when it puts the paper on the jobs.
- **A job in a development reads, and presses nothing.** While its development has secrecy on, the tab shows the on state with *This job's development has secrecy on. Turn it off on the development's page.* While it is off, the tab says nothing: the door is the development's page.
- **The job's papers read again.** Turning secrecy on or off raises a key that **Papers before this job opens** listens to, so the site's paper shows on the job, or leaves it, without a reload.
- **A job with no address and no development** has no site, and draws no block.

## How it is built

- `src/lib/addresses/siteSecrecy.ts`: `jobSecrecyDoor(job)` answers the site and the mode, `door` or `reads`, or null. `secrecyLead(site)` picks the lead. Two new sentences in `SITE_SECRECY_WORDS`: `leadAddress` and `onDevelopment`.
- `src/components/jobs/SiteSecrecy.tsx` takes `mode`, `boxed` and `onChanged`. In `reads` it draws the on state alone, offers no press, and says nothing of a site that is off or a read that failed.
- `src/lib/addresses/siteSecrecyIo.ts`: `readLive` reads an address's site by `siteKeysToRead`, over the answers `readAnswers` hands back. Without the answers the address's own spelling still reads.
- `src/components/jobs/JobWindowDocumentsTab.tsx` mounts the block and raises `reloadKey` on `JobDocumentsRequiredPapers`.

## Design calls

Each is open to a flip.

- **A job in a development has no press on its own tab.** One door a site: a press here would turn secrecy on or off for every job of the development from inside one of them.
- **A job in a development whose secrecy is off says nothing here.** Most developments never turn it on, and the tab is long already.
- **The block has no box on the tab.** The tab's sections carry none, and the development page's box would read as a window inside the window.
- **A read that fails says nothing on a job in a development.** The tab's own papers block already says when the job's papers cannot be read.
