# v2.4760 — Pipeline on a phone: a bill's card says when it was billed, not when it was last sent (2026-10-07)

Client only. Punch list #93 B (the Collections look of 2026-10-06).

## What was wrong

The phone card's second line — *billed 5 weeks ago* — came from `deriveStagesBillingActivityDetail(job)`: the job's latest billing event (invoice sent, invoice billed, payment recorded). The desktop row's dates block reads the bill's own reference day (`billedReferenceYmd`). On a bill resent after a returned check the two disagreed: *Billed May 15 · 144d ago* on the computer, *billed 5 weeks ago* on the phone.

## What changed

- New kernel [`phoneBillWords.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/phoneBillWords.ts): on a Billed or Collections bill row the words read `billedReferenceYmd` (the billed day, else the hand-set estimate) — *billed 5 months ago*; a working job's progress bill, a Collections shell with no line, or a row with neither day keep the latest event (*paid today*, *billed 9 weeks ago*), the pre-change words verbatim.
- `JobsStagesTab.tsx` `phoneNextInput` calls it for `billDisplay`; `stripBillParts` / `stripDistancePhrase` leave the tab's imports.

## Tests

- `phoneBillWords.test.ts` (4): the resend case, the Collections row (billed day first, estimate as the fallback), no day on the row, the working job and the shell.
