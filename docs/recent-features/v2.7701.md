# v2.7701 — Trips: start a trip from the phone, join one, head home and end it (2026-11-17)

Punch list #379, PR 7b of the train: the phone. Client only, on Job Mode's card. No migration and no edge function in this PR.

**Push first.** This client calls `trip_phone_state` and `trip_phone`, which only the migration `20261118030000_trips_phone.sql` (PR 7a) creates. That migration is pushed before this reaches the live app. Ahead of the push the phone is harmless: the state read fails, a failed read is a null state, and nothing is asked or shown.

## The promise, and how the code holds it

The phone says *Your hours are the same either way.* No trip act may block, delay, change or fail a clock in or a clock out.

- **The question needs a clock session that already exists.** `DashboardJobModeCard` hands `JobModeTrip` the open session's id only when it holds an open session with a job, the session has landed (not a press still waiting on the phone), and the day on screen is the live read. `tripPhoneView` asks nothing without it (`clockedIn`), and the quiet paint and the distance read wait for it too. So the question comes after the clock in went through, whichever press made it: **On my way**, **Start here**, the manual clock in, or a press that waited with no signal.
- **Before a clock in the phone only reads.** On the job the card says is ready to start, `JobModeTrip` is mounted with no session: one `trip_phone_state` read, for the banner alone. It is a sibling of the clock presses and nothing they do waits on it.
- **Nothing in the clock's path was changed.** `OnMyWaySheet`, `ImHereSheet`, `ClockInOutButton`, the bridge and the outbox are untouched. The card's diff is one import and one mount, eight lines in all.
- **No trip read or act is awaited by anything else.** `useTripPhone` never throws from a read: offline, a refusal or a missing function is a null state. The quiet day paint swallows its failure after one console warning. An act the person pressed says its reason where it was pressed, and the person stays clocked in.
- **Clock out is not involved at all.** **Heading home** paints a day. It does not clock out.

## What landed

- **The question** (`components/trips/JobModeTrip.tsx`, a `ResponsiveModalShell` in the On my way family's styles). Its title is the job as the card names it. Under it: the site and the miles from the shop (*Heron Point · 1,180 miles from the shop*, the miles left out when they were not read), *This site is a trip from home. Is this a trip?*, **Start a trip**, one **Join** press for each of up to two crewmates' open trips, newest first (*Join Luis’s trip, out since Mon*), **Just clock in**, and *The office can change it later. Your hours are the same either way.*
- **When it asks** (`tripPhoneView` in the new `lib/trips/tripPhone.ts`, pure, with tests). The person is clocked in on the job, the state answered `ok` with a development, the person has no open trip there, **Just clock in** was not said for this job today on this device, and the site is a trip from home: it is 150 miles or more from the shop (`siteIsFar(milesFromShop(shop, site))`, the job's own address), or it has had a trip (`has_trips`), or a crewmate's trip is open there (`open`).
- **Just clock in** is kept in `localStorage` under one key (`trips_phone_just_clock_in_v1`, job id to day), read and written inside try/catch, and yesterday's answers are dropped on the next write. A device that cannot remember holds the answer for the screen. Closing the sheet (the ✕, Escape, the backdrop) is **Just clock in**.
- **The banner** on the card, under the header and above the day's list and the clock presses: **Trip · day 3 at Heron Point** over *with Priya · started Mon Oct 26*. It shows on the job the person is clocked in on, and on the job that is ready to start, as the mock-up draws it over **Clock in**. The day number is the person's own days before today, and today (`tripDayNumber`), so the third morning reads *day 3* before and after the clock in. *with* is left out when alone. The mock-up's hours figure is left out: the phone reads no hours here.
- **The trip's number.** `trip_phone_state` also returns `trip_number` on the caller's trip and on each open one once `20261118040000_trip_numbers.sql` is pushed. `parseTripPhoneState` reads it as a positive whole number or null, and a reply without the field is null, never an error, so the phone works before and after that push. With a number the banner's title reads *Trip 5 · day 3 at Heron Point* (`tripBannerTitle`) and the End the trip sheet opens on *Trip 5 to Heron Point* (`tripToSiteWords`). With none they read *Trip · day 3 at Heron Point* and *Trip to Heron Point*. The **Join** press never says a number: a crewmate knows the person.
- **Heading home** sends `trip_phone` `day` with `road`. Once today is On the road the press gives way to *Today is marked On the road.*
- **End the trip** opens a sheet: the trip's span, the person's own days in words (*8 days at the site · 2 on the road*, `ownDaysWords`), the box *How did it go? (you can skip this)*, **Not yet, still out** and **End the trip**. It sends `end` with the outcome when something was typed. After an end the question does not come straight back: the device remembers **Just clock in** for the job until tomorrow.
- **A day spent driving reads On the road.** The kind a trip act paints comes from the open clock session's two stamps, in one pure function (`tripKindOfSession`): `road` while the session is an **On my way** drive that has not arrived (`travel_started_at` set, `arrived_at` not), else `site`. **Start a trip** and **Join** send that kind. The card already holds the stamps on its open session (`travelFacts`, read by its own reload through `loadTravelFacts`), so no read was added: it hands them to `JobModeTrip`, and hands nothing while it is still reading them, when the hook waits or keeps what it last knew of that session.
- **The quiet rule that grows a trip a day at a time** (`tripQuietPaint`, pure, with tests). A person with an open trip who is clocked in on a job of its site has today painted the session's kind (`trip_phone` `day`), once when the clock session opens. It skips a day that is that kind already. It never turns At the site into On the road: a person who worked at the site and then drives to the motel had a site day. It does turn On the road into At the site: a morning drive, then work. It leaves a Getting ready day alone: a person at home loading the trailer clocks in on the site's job on a day the office painted, and only **Heading home** or the office changes that day. No question and no toast.
- **Arrival.** When the same open session arrives (**I'm here**, so `arrived_at` appears on the card's next read of the session, which that press already triggers) and today is On the road, today is painted At the site, quietly, once (a Getting ready day is skipped here too). No listener and no read was added for it. An arrival the card only sees after a reload is painted on that read.
- **Heading home is the person's own word.** After it, nothing quiet paints for the rest of that clock session: the hook holds the session, and the device keeps it too (`trips_phone_headed_home_v1`, session id to day), so a reload of the card does not paint the day At the site again. A new clock session at the site later the same day may paint At the site again, by the rule above. On another device the same session would paint again: the database keeps no word of who painted a day.
- **The database's words.** `trip_phone` refuses in plain words with `42501` or `22023`. `formatErrorMessage` reads a `42501` as the house's *You don't have access to this record.*, which would lose them. So `sendTripPhoneAct` throws the function's own refusal as its words alone, as `photoSavesIo.ts` does, and the screen says *Training mode is read-only. No trip was changed.* Anything else keeps its class.

## Reads the phone gained

None for a session with no job, for a bid, or for a day with no schedule.

1. `trip_phone_state(job)`, once for the job in the card's header: the job ready to start, and once more when a clock session opens on a job. The card's own reloads do not repeat it.
2. Only for somebody clocked in, when the state says `ok` with a development, no trip of the person's, no trip on the site, no crewmate out and no **Just clock in**: the shop's point (`resolveOfficeAnchor`) and the point of the job's address (`useAddressGeocodeCoords`: the shared geocode cache, and the geocoder for an address it has not seen).
3. After **Start a trip** or **Join**: the state once more. **Heading home**, **End the trip** and the quiet paint change the held state in place and read nothing. The session's two stamps are the card's own, read by the reload it already made.

A read that fails is not drawn on `OfflineRetryPanel`. The trip is an offer laid over the card, the card's own read already carries the panel, and a person with no signal is asked nothing.

## Not built

No location logic was added. **End the trip** on the banner is the door, and the banner shows only on a job of the trip's site. A trip nobody ends stops being offered three days after its last painted day (the migration's rule).
- **Add a photo to the trip** on the mock-up's second phone.
- **Hours on the banner** (*31 h so far*).
- **A job on no site.** The state says so and nothing is asked. The database refuses a start there in words.
