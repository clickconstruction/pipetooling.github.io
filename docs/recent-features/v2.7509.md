# v2.7509 — Lien desk: retainage a GC holds that nobody typed is listed (2026-11-16)

Fix A of the retainage clock check. The owner asked how the desk clocks and reminds on retainage, job by job. The read found the clock sound and its input missing: the § 53.057 clock runs only from `jobs_ledger.lien_retainage_held`, a figure typed by hand in a folded row of Edit Job. A saved pay application mirrors into it, and production has none on a real job. So a GC that pays each bill ten percent short holds retainage the desk never hears of. Such a job gets no clock, no row and no *Of which, unpaid retainage* line on its monthly notice. The owner picked this fix first: find the retainage.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/ar/retainageSuggested.ts`** (new, pure). `retainageShareHeld` reads a bill whose payments come to exactly 90% or 95% of it, within a cent, as 10% or 5% held. `retainageSuggestion` adds what such bills left behind and names the percent when they agree. `noteSaysRetainage` hears *retainage*, *retention* and *retained* in a note, and not *retainer* or *retaining*. `retainageUnrecordedRows` keeps the jobs with no answer (no typed figure, where a typed 0 is an answer, and no pay application) that carry either sign, largest figure first, notes alone after.
- **`src/lib/ar/retainageHeldIo.ts`**: `loadRetainageUnrecorded` reads the open jobs under a GC with no figure (not paid, not written off), their live pay applications, bills, payments and the notes that name retainage. Four tables, no RPC, RLS as the viewer. Payments are paired to bills by the Bill tab's own rule (`attributeJobPayments`, through `assembleRetainageHeld`). `recordRetainageHeld` writes the one column the lien details write.
- **`LienRetainageUnrecorded.tsx`** (new): the section at the top of By paper → Retainage notices, *Looks held · not on the job · N*, drawn only when a job is in this state and hidden while a pile or the find box narrows the list. A row reads *$2,460.00 looks held · 3 bills paid at 90% · a note of Sep 8 says retainage*, or *a note of Sep 21 says retainage · no figure yet*. The office's presses: *Record $2,460.00 on the job* and *No retainage on this job*, each behind a window whose button names the act, and *Type another figure ›*, which opens Edit Job on the row. A viewer outside the office sees the rows and the door to the job (a master is office on this desk, so he has the presses). After a write the section reads again and the desk re-reads, so the job appears under *Clock not started*.
- **`LienDesk.tsx`**: two lines, the import and the section above the piles.
- **Edit Job → *Our contract on this job*** (`JobFormLienContractRow.tsx`, `useJobRetainage.ts`): on a job with no answer the row offers the same figure, *The payments on this job look like {GC} holds back $2,460.00: 3 bills paid at 90%.*, with *Use $2,460.00*.

## What it does not do

- It does not tell anyone outside the Retainage notices tab. A row on To do and a count on the Board are the next PR.
- It reads round shares only. A GC that holds 5% of the running total on a later check is caught on its first short pay and not its second; the office types the full figure.
- It reads nothing into bills written net of retainage, where every bill is paid in full and the last ten percent is never billed.
- It does not ask for the day our contract ended. *Clock not started* still speaks on this tab only.
