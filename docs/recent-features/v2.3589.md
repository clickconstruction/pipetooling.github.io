# v2.3589 — Stripe pay links stay live: the nightly link refresh (2026-09-18)

New edge function `refresh-stripe-invoice-links` (**deploy before the push**) and migration `20260918172652_stripe_invoice_links_nightly_cron` (the cron). PR 1 of two; PR 2 refreshes the link on read in the portal and the Bill tab.

## Why

Taunya, 2026-09-18: Stripe bills expire after 30 days, the app still shows the links, and customers cannot use them. Stripe's rule ([hosted invoice page](https://docs.stripe.com/invoicing/hosted-invoice-page), [link expiration](https://support.stripe.com/questions/invoice-link-expiration)): a hosted-invoice link expires **30 days after the invoice's due date** (30 days after finalizing with no due date; never more than 120 days). The customer then sees "this link has expired — contact the business"; Stripe's email-recovery page only appears for invoices Stripe itself emailed, and `create-stripe-invoice` finalizes without emailing. Retrieving the invoice through the API returns a fresh link, good for at least ten days.

The app stored `hosted_invoice_url` once, in `create-stripe-invoice`, and every reader handed that copy out: the portal's Pay (`customer-portal` selects the column), the Bill tab's **Text · Copy link · Email** cluster (`StripeInvoiceSharePanel`), View bill (`HostedStripeBillPanel`), Collect payment, Documents, the Legal desk. Nothing refreshed it — `get-stripe-invoice-details` backfills memo and footer only, `stripe-webhook` syncs status only. Counted 2026-09-18: 62 open Stripe bills, 8 billed more than 60 days ago and 10 more than 45.

## The change

- **`refresh-stripe-invoice-links`** — every open Stripe bill (`status = 'billed'`, a `stripe_invoice_id`) is retrieved from Stripe in its own mode and, when the link differs, `hosted_invoice_url` is replaced. Status is not written (the webhook owns it); a Stripe status the row does not carry is counted and listed. `dry_run` and `invoice_ids` in the body; `X-Cron-Secret` auth. Full contract in `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`.
- **The cron** — `stripe-invoice-links-nightly` at 08:45 UTC, the `owner-confirm-nightly` shape (`docs/migrations/20260918172652_stripe_invoice_links_nightly_cron.md`).
- **The pure part** — [`_shared/stripeInvoiceLinkRefresh.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/stripeInvoiceLinkRefresh.ts): mode grouping (NULL = live), the per-row decision (renewed / unchanged / no link), status drift, the summary line; 5 tests from `src/lib/billing/stripeInvoiceLinkRefresh.test.ts`.
- Docs: `BILLING_FLOWS.md` (the post-create panel line), the guide *ready-to-bill-pipeline* (the copy-link sentence), `config.toml` (`verify_jwt = false`).

Not changed on purpose: due-date policy (`days_until_due` from the office's date) — pushing due dates out to keep links alive would distort aging and the lien clocks.

## Verified

- Deploy: `supabase functions deploy refresh-stripe-invoice-links`, then a dry run (`curl … -H 'X-Cron-Secret: …' -d '{"dry_run":true}'`) — the summary line and the renewed count read against the 62 open bills; then `supabase db push`.
