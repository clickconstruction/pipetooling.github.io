# v2.8055 — Job updates: the Customer pill stays on the row with the others on a phone (2026-11-20)

A follow-up to [v2.8042](v2.8042.md). With **Reports** and **Billing** gone from a field hand's row, four pills fit on one row of a phone and **Customer** still sat alone on a second. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## The measure

On the Job Mode card at 390 px the row is 314 px wide. All, Notes 4, Status 2 and Crew 14 ended at 281 px, and Customer needs 84 px and a 4 px gap: 369 px, 55 px too long. A plain no-wrap row would have left Customer as two faint letters at the edge.

## The rule

`activityPillsOnOneRow({ narrow, withCustomer })` in [`phonePress.ts`](../../src/lib/phonePress.ts): on a phone, the row that carries the Customer pill is one row. It is drawn in [`ScrollableTabStrip`](../../src/components/ScrollableTabStrip.tsx), the strip the Bids header uses, so a row too long for the card slides sideways with a fade at the edge that holds more, and the chosen pill is kept in view.

`ONE_ROW_PILLS` holds the trims that let the five stand whole at 390 px:

- A drawn pill's sides come in from 0.75rem to 0.5rem, the desk chip's. Its height stays 32 px.
- A dim word (a pill with nothing under it) takes 0.25rem a side. It has no outline to keep clear of.
- The gap between pills is 0.125rem, and between a word and its count 0.25rem.
- The fade is drawn once more than 6 px hides. A dim word's side padding and border hide first, and nothing is lost with them.

After: the five end at 311 px of 314 px. A count that grows a digit hides only the last word's padding. Past that, and on a 375 px phone, the row slides.

Every other row wraps as it did: a desk, and a phone row without Customer (the office's job window, with its ＋ Report and percent line).

## What changed

- [`phonePress.ts`](../../src/lib/phonePress.ts): `activityPillsOnOneRow` and `ONE_ROW_PILLS`.
- [`ScrollableTabStrip.tsx`](../../src/components/ScrollableTabStrip.tsx): three props, each defaulting to what the Bids header had. `align` (`start` keeps a short row at the left edge), `role` (the pills are a `tablist`) and `fadeSlack`. The chosen tab is now found by reading each tab's key, not by a selector built from it.
- [`JobThreadNotesPanel.tsx`](../../src/components/JobThreadNotesPanel.tsx): the pills are drawn once and set in the strip or in the wrapping row.

## Walked

On the dev server against the live data, as a helper clocked in on a job (the app's own View as), every write refused at the network layer:

- 390 px, before: All, Notes 4, Status 2, Crew 14 on one row, Customer on a second.
- 390 px, after: all five on one row, 311 px of 314 px, nothing hidden.
- 375 px, after: one row, 12 px hidden, Customer cut at its last letter with the fade over it. The row slides.
- The office's job window at 390 px: All, Notes 13, Reports 1, ＋ Report, Status 3, Billing 4, Crew 7 on two rows with the percent line, as before.
