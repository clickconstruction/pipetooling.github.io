# v2.7792 — Painted maps: a job can keep a list of places of its own (2026-11-18)

Punch list #393, reading C, the database half. The owner chose a list of its own on 2026-11-18, apart from Materials on site's places, and liked the mock-up, `to-dos/painted-map-pins-on-the-job.html`. No screen changes.

## Why

A place lives on one painted map. A point every map of a job should show, such as the gate, the meter or an open trench, had to be set again on each map, and a job with no map had nowhere to keep one.

## What ships

Migration `20261118090000_painted_map_job_list.sql` ([its page](../migrations/20261118090000_painted_map_job_list.md)):

- `painted_maps.job_list`, a yes or no that starts at no. A map marked yes is its job's own list of places.

The list is a painted map that holds places only. So a place on it has everything a place already has: its kind and mark, its photo, who may read and change it, and the map's own link, picture and printed page. No new table and no new fence.

## What the table does not hold

A job is held to no count of lists. A job that goes sets its maps' job to null, and two jobs can be combined, so a rule of one a job would refuse those. The app makes one and reads every one a job has as one list.

Nobody new reads or writes anything. The four policies on `painted_maps` do not read the column.

## The bed

`supabase/tests/painted_maps/80_job_list.sql`, a seventh scenario in the painted maps bed: a helper on the crew starts the list and sets a place, a second helper adds to it and can neither rename, unmark nor throw it away, a second list on the same job is kept, a helper on no crew starts and reads none, the office throws one away, and a job that goes leaves its list standing with its places.
