Punch list #194's sweep, the second of the two mechanical sweeps its card named ([`to-dos/experiences-lens/`](../../to-dos/experiences-lens/README.md)). v2.6062 gave the dead presses the lens saw `src/lib/deadPress.ts`'s pair: `DEAD_PRESS_FILL` (`--bg-200`) under `DEAD_PRESS_TEXT` (`--text-700`), 8.33:1 in light and 5.13:1 in dark. The rest still dimmed to a literal grey under white words, 2.54:1 on `#9ca3af` and 1.47:1 on `#d1d5db` in both themes. They sat in states the sample world does not draw: a Save while it saves, a Delete before its name is typed, an Add with nothing picked.

## What changed

- **A codemod**, `scripts/codemods/dead-press-sweep.mjs`, parsed with the TypeScript compiler. In a control's style object (a `<button>`'s `style`, an object with a `cursor`, or one a button helper returns), a `background` ternary with a `'#9ca3af'` or `'#d1d5db'` branch takes `DEAD_PRESS_FILL` there, and the object's `color` follows the same condition to `DEAD_PRESS_TEXT`. A static grey takes the pair when a `not-allowed` cursor or a plain `disabled` says it is dead. It is re-runnable: on a rebase conflict, run it on the new main.
- **The run**: 149 dead or busy presses in 94 files. Two more it lists were fixed by hand: `estSendButton` in `estimatesPageStyles.ts`, whose words come from the spread of `estPrimaryButton`, and the paid-job email window's send, which passes its grey to `headerBtn`. Two `primary || disabled` guards in the takeoff rails lost their dead half.
- **Busy presses with inherited words** take the pair too: the bid's distance and travel lookups and a Settings catalog's remove-unused press, whose `#d1d5db` was a light slab under the dark theme's light words.
- **CI holds it**: `npm run check:dead-press` runs the codemod with `--check` and fails on a dead or busy press drawn on a literal grey.
- **Left as they were**, the greys that fill no control: 4 text colours (the report window's job links while they load, the salaried workdays a person cannot pick, the Estimates catalog's hint), 8 borders, status dots, chart and map palettes, the select's separators and the time-range slider's ticks.

**The count**: v2.6062's fragment said 27 more; its own grep (`'#9ca3af' : '#2563eb'`) counted 19, and that sentence now says so. The sweep found 151 in all, reading both orders of the ternary, every live fill, and `#d1d5db` as well as `#9ca3af`.
