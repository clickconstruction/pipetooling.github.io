# v2.6065 — Where everyone stands makes room for a crowded end on a phone (2026-10-29)

Punch list #203, the pick the owner took on 2026-10-29 from `to-dos/phone-band-crowded-end/mockup.html`: option A, a third row on a narrow band, with the caption below the last row. Fix, no migration, no edge function. v2.5980 kept the band's name tags inside the band on a phone and left a crowded end for this.

## Cause

The Bid Board's Health section ("2 · Field band" in `BidBoardEstimatingPulseSection.tsx`) hangs one name tag per person under the five-zone strip, on two rows with 10 and 34 px stems. `buildPulseBandItems` sent a tag to the long-stem row when it was within 12 points of the one before, or (since v2.5980) when the two would touch at the band's measured width. On a desk-wide band 12 points is wider than a tag, so two rows hold everyone. On a phone the band is 343 px wide (a 375 screen) or 288 px (320), a tag is 40–65 px, and 12 points is 41 or 35 px: when four names fall in one quarter of the band, the third and fourth tags land on the first two and the names underneath go dark. The caption (*0% – 100% Won rate…*) was pulled up 3.4rem, so it ran beneath the first row of tags, on desktop too.

## Fix

- **A third row, taken only when needed.** `buildPulseBandItems` keeps today's rule as the floor: a tag within 12 points of the one before, or touching it, goes to the long-stem row when the one before sits on the short one. Then, with the band's measured width, it checks the row it was given against every tag already on it (`pulseBandSpansTouch`, the 4 px gap counted). A tag that would land on one takes the first of the three rows where it touches nothing, the third (a 58 px stem, `PULSE_BAND_STEM_PX`) last; when none is clear, the row whose last tag ends first. Without a width (the first paint, jsdom) nothing changes. `PulseBandItem.row` is now `PulseBandRow`, `0 | 1 | 2`, and `pulseBandRowCount(items)` reports the rows the tags took.
- **The band grows only with the third row.** The section keeps `4.6rem` under the band for two rows and adds the third row's 24 px (`calc(4.6rem + 24px)`) only when `pulseBandRowCount` says three were used, so a crowded phone board's band is about 24 px taller and every other board is as tall as before.
- **The caption reads below the last row, on every width.** Its `-3.4rem` pull-up is gone; it follows the band's bottom margin, about 14 px under the last row of tags.
- **Desktop draws as today.** At 1,180 px (a 1,280 screen) 12 points is 142 px, wider than any tag, so the floor alone places every row and no tag moves.
- **Guide** *read the Estimating Health section* → *Where everyone stands* says a crowded end on a phone hangs its names on a third row.
