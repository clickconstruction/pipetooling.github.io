## Two clocks

- **As of** (v2.3398) rewinds the pins to the status each job had on a day, back to February 22, 2026.
- **Time** shows a photograph of one site from an earlier day, back to about 2014.

The two are not linked. Most photographs are older than As of's first day, so a link would have nothing to join. Each moves its own thing, and the Time row says so: beside a date it reads *The pins are today's.*, or *The pins are as of Mon Oct 19.* when As of is rewound (`jobsMapTimePinsWords` in `src/lib/jobs/jobsMapAsOf.ts`, tested).
