# v2.8486 — Send a paper: a copy out with a person named under a company is found (2026-11-24)

Punch list #403, two of the six small faults its walks of 2026-11-24 found on prod, both in Documents → Agreements’ **Send a paper**. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was there

- **The look before a second copy** (the owner’s third decision of 2026-11-22, v2.8348) read one contact for the record picked: a company’s own contact, the one with no name. **Add to a list** puts someone who was on no list under a customer or a supply house by name (`name_contact` with a name), and their contact then points at the company and carries their name, with their papers still on it. On prod both waiting papers of the ZZ test customer sat on contacts of that shape. How those two came to it was not traced. Picking the customer found nothing, so a second send would have made a second copy with no question asked.
- **Escape** closed none of the three windows the press draws itself. The walk noted it on the window that says a paper is already out, whose × worked.

## What changed

- **Which contacts are looked at** is `partyContactsToLook` (`partyPaperOut.ts`, pure): a company’s own contact first, then each named one that points at it. A person on file keeps their one contact. `findPartyContacts` (`paperPartyIo.ts`) replaces `findPartyContact` and returns them all, oldest first.
- **Which copies are out** is still `partyPapersOut`, which now takes one contact or several. The window, its words and its two presses are unchanged. **Send it to them again** opens the copy where it sits, on the named person’s contact, so no second row is written. **Send a second copy to someone else** files the new copy under the company’s own contact, as before.
- **Escape** closes the paper list, **Who is it with?** and the already-out window, through one listener in `AgreementsSendPaper.tsx` that asks the modal stack whether it is on top. It waits while a pick is being read. The Send window is left alone: it holds an address and a message someone typed.
- **Help**: *find an agreement we sent or signed* says what the check covers and that Escape closes the window.

## What it does not change

A person on file at a company (a customer’s contact person, a supply house’s rep) keeps their own contact, and the company’s look does not read it. Picking the company for someone else while a person on file has the paper out still asks nothing: the office picked someone else on purpose, and the person’s own row asks when they are picked again.

## Tests

- `partyPaperOut.test.ts`: the contacts looked at for a company and for a person, and the copies out over several contacts, still by paper and by state.
- `AgreementsSendPaper.render.test.tsx`: a company whose only copy out is on a named person’s contact shows the window, and Send again opens that copy with nothing written. A company with a named person and no copy out opens the Send window as before. Escape closes each of the three windows with nothing written, and leaves the Send window open.
