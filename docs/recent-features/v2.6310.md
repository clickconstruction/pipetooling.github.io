# v2.6310 — Sent copies: a heading per day, the job each copy is for, and a coloured kind tag (2026-11-01)

The Documents page design spike of 2026-11-01, candidate 4, four rounds with the owner on the Sent copies tab. Client only, no migration and no function.

- **Round 1, the three.** The row shape (`SentCopyRows.tsx`, shared with the job window's *Sent from this job*): the title on its own line, the grey facts under it in one order (how · when · to · by, `sentCopyWords`), attachments as chips. Day headings (`lib/sent/sentCopyDays.ts`, `sentCopyDayGroups` + `sentCopyTimeOnly`, the rule Photos' `dayGroups` uses: *Today · Sun, Nov 1*, *Yesterday*, then the day, *No date* for a time that does not parse); under a heading a row carries the time alone (`when` prop; the job window keeps date and time). A failed read: `readSentCopies` in `sentCopiesIo.ts` throws its reason and the tab draws `OfflineRetryPanel` (`recoveryFailureFromError`, idempotent), where before `loadSentCopies` swallowed every error and a lost signal read as *No copy is on file yet*; `loadSentCopies` stays as the quiet wrapper (no caller left in `src`). Empty states through `EmptyState` and `emptyWords` (the filter kind with **Clear the search** / **Clear the filters**).
- **Round 2, the owner's refinements.** *Let the user know what job it is for*: `job_ids` and `bid_id` join `SENT_COLS_BASE`, `parseSentCopy` reads them (`SentCopy.jobIds?`, `bidId?`, absent on a row read without them), `loadSentCopyAbout` reads the jobs' and bids' labels once per page, best effort, and `sentCopyAbout` (`lib/sent/sentCopyAbout.ts`) draws each as a green link, *J1076 · Sneegas Post Test* to `/jobs?edit=`, *B244 · <project>* to `/bids?bidId=`, the id alone when the label did not read. *What text goes with it*: a tag before the title with the kind in words (`sentKindWords`: `invoice_email` → *Invoice email*, acronyms GC/PDF/AIA/RFQ/COI/HR upper) and the subject in quotes when it says more than the title (`sentCopySubjectLine`). *The days blend in*: the day band is bold on a tinted bar with a blue left edge and a count (*Thu, Oct 29 · 4 copies*), sticky at the top while its rows scroll.
- **Rounds 3 and 4, colour.** The tag alone first, then the filter chips to match: `SENT_GROUP_COLORS` / `sentKindTag` in `sentCopies.ts` by `sentKindGroup` — bills green (money in), lien and legal red, contracts and estimates blue, bids and suppliers violet (the estimator's), statements and reports amber, other grey, theme tokens so the pairs hold in dark; `kindChipStyle` in `DocumentsSentLedger.tsx` gives each filter chip the same pair, the pressed one a border in its text colour, All plain.
- **Tests.** `sentCopyDays.test.ts`, `sentCopyAbout.test.ts` (keys off the row, labels, the id fallback, the subject rule, the kind words, one colour per group), `DocumentsSentLedger.render.test.tsx` (day headings with counts, the time alone, the job link, the subject, the kind before the title, Clear the search, a failed read as `role="alert"` with Retry; its auth mock carries `useOptionalAuth` for the retry panel's error door).
- **Seen, not touched.** The reply box files emails to our own staff (test subjects on the list), while `SENT_COPIES.md` says mail to our own staff is not filed; the Other chip is a catch-all.
