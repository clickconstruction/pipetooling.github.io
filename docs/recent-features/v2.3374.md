# v2.3374 — Payment promises are filed under the payer (2026-09-13)

Who pays follow-up. Migration `supabase/migrations/20260913150812_promise_payer_follows_bill_to_rule.sql` (fragment in `docs/migrations/`). Apply with `supabase db push` after merge; no edge function, no client change required first.

## The gap

Since v2.3345/v2.3346 the party that pays a bill is resolved by `effectiveInvoiceParty` / `payerCustomerId` (`supabase/functions/_shared/billToParty.ts`, re-exported to `src/lib/jobs/billToParty.ts`), and every client reader of promises keys on the payer — `stageRowPayerCustomerId` on the Stages board, `billedPaymentForecast.ts`, the statement RPC's CASE in `20260911223500`. The server **writers** in `20260911051414` (the `job_promised_pay_dates` trigger, the backfill, `add_job_payment_promise`) still snapshotted the payer as `COALESCE(j.gc_customer_id, j.customer_id)`. On a job with `bill_to_party = 'customer'` that names a GC (Done Right Foundation repair jobs — the homeowner pays), a promise was attributed to the GC's customer id, so the GC's reliability record and pay-speed spread absorbed the homeowner's promise.

## What changed

- **SQL helper `job_bill_payer_customer_id(bill_to_party, customer_id, gc_customer_id)`** — pure, IMMUTABLE; the statement RPC's CASE for a job with no invoice pick in play: GC-as-customer pays by definition; `gc` with a GC set → the GC; `customer` / `split` / `gc`-without-a-GC → the job customer (a job-level promise has no invoice, so `split` falls to the customer).
- **`job_promised_pay_dates_log_promise()` and `add_job_payment_promise(...)`** re-created with the helper on the payer line; everything else verbatim (gates, the `app.promise_event_written` flag, the latest-row upsert).
- **Re-snapshot**: one idempotent `UPDATE` limited to office-sourced rows whose job bills the customer with a distinct GC while the row sits on the GC. Dry run (read-only, 2026-09-13): prod held **1** promise row, already correct — zero rows change today; the statement covers promises recorded between merge and push. Portal self-promises keep the customer who actually promised.
- **Kernel mirror** — [`jobPromisePayerCustomerId`](../../src/lib/jobs/billToParty.ts) (`payerCustomerId(job, effectiveInvoiceParty(job, null))`) with one test per CASE branch in `billToParty.test.ts`, so a drift on either side fails a test.

## Decided and left alone

`add_customer_payment_promise` (`20260911052931`) still accepts a job where the caller's customer id is either party. The portal narrows the job list first (`owedJobIdsForViewer` in `submit-portal-request`), and that gate knows invoice-level picks on split jobs — a job-level rule inside the RPC would wrongly refuse a GC promising on a split job whose invoices bill the GC.

## Guides

None — no user-facing flow changes; the promise chips, records and forecast read the same fields.
