# v2.6983 — The Bridge's Monday email reads Market at what it is worth (punch list #318, PR 5) (2026-11-11)

PR 5 of six for *Market and Sales on the won rates* (`to-dos/market-sales-won-rates/README.md`), item 5 under *Not built*. The weekly email is the page's first screen, built on the edge by the page's own kernels. Since PR 4 those kernels take the Market amount when they are handed one; the email handed them none, so its pipe still read the dollars that came in under *asked*. No migration. One edge function changes and is owed a redeploy.

## What changed

- **The reads** (`src/lib/bridge/sheetReads.ts`): `loadMondaySheetReads` also reads every bid and every estimate ever sent, through the page's loaders (`loadBidWins.ts`, `loadEstimateWins.ts`), each a soft read. One that fails is named in what was not read (*the bids sent*, *the estimates sent*) and the mail still goes. ZZ test bids stay out. The list of what the Bridge leaves out is not shipped, so both loaders take an empty one, as on the page.
- **The mail** (`sheetBuild.ts`): the two won rates and the Market amount are built as the page builds them (`buildBidWins`, `buildEstimateWins`, `buildMarketWorth`) and handed to the five steps through `marketWorthInput`, which the page now uses too. Like the page, the amount waits for both reads. With either missing, Market reads the dollars that came in.
- **Its words** (`weekMail.ts`): Market on the pipe reads *$X worth*. One line under the pipe says what the amount is made of: *What Market is worth: bids $A at N% won. Estimates $B at M% won.* (`weekMailMarketLine`, `WeekMail.marketParts`), in the html and the text. While Market reads the dollars that came in there is no such line.
- **The copy of the kernels**: `bidWins.ts`, `estimateWins.ts`, `loadBidWins.ts`, `loadEstimateWins.ts` and `leftOut.ts` join it by import; `scripts/edge-kernels.mjs` needed no new entry.
- **The function** (`supabase/functions/bridge-weekly-sheet/index.ts`): its header says what it reads now. Its code is unchanged: the same two calls.
- **The catalog's sample** (`weekMailSample.ts`): Settings → Team emails shows Market at its worth with the line under the pipe, on made-up numbers.

## Dry runs, before any redeploy

- **The new kernels, run locally** on the page's reads as a dev, through a client that refuses every request that is not a read: nothing unread, nothing refused. The mail's five steps read *Market: … worth* and the line *What Market is worth: bids … at 7% won. Estimates … at 48% won.* The amount is the one on the page's pipe.
- **The deployed function's own dry run** (`{ "dry": true }`, as a dev), the same hour: *Market: … asked*, more than six times the amount above. The switch is on, the mail goes to two people, and it has never been sent.
- The other four steps, the tiles, the call and its moves read the same in both.
