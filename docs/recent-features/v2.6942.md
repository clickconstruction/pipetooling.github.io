# v2.6942 — Cards and footers: the designs as kernels (punch list #313, PR 2) (2026-11-11)

Punch list #313 (`to-dos/cards-and-footers/README.md`). PR 2 of the plan: the designs as pure kernels, with no screen. The spike's `kit.js` fitted every number to the designer's flat files; this is the same code in TypeScript under `src/lib/brandKit/`, in the same three layers.

- **`layouts.ts`**: `BRAND` (the orange, the typeface, the two words of the wordmark, the mark's size), `LAYOUTS` and `LOOKS`. The designer's three layouts, `footer01`, `cardBack1` and `cardFront1`, are kept as measured. The owner's five picks of 2026-11-11 are small patches on copies: `footerReworked` (a title and a phone, the stack centred between the pipes), `cardBackReworked` (even steps, one left edge) and `cardFrontReworked`, all on one orange, plus `cardFrontPipes`, the drawn front that stands by if the pipe photo cannot be had clean. `CHOSEN_LOOK` is `LOOKS.reworked`.
- **`draw.ts`**: `drawKit(layout, person, opts)` returns one SVG string. One part per thing drawn: `pipePath` (straights joined by a bend at each inner point), the logo (the mark as a picture, CLICK and Plumbing as type), the photo in its ring (`placePhoto` never leaves a gap), the icons, and `placeRows`, the stack of words, where a missing fact closes the gap. `fitSize` shrinks a long line, with the real font's measure handed in by the caller and a per-letter guess otherwise. `printPage` puts one side on a 4.25 × 2.75 inch page with crop marks; `sheetOfTen` lays ten on a letter page.
- **`person.ts`**: `kitPersonFromUser` (the name on the card or the account name, the title, the email, the phone as the mobile, the company's office line), `readCrop`, `cardHold` (no name, no email or no photo holds a person; a missing mobile or title does not) and `kitFileSlug`.
- **`assets.ts`** and the files it names: `public/brand/click-mark-on-dark.png` (the wrench and hand, cut out with a clear ground), `public/brand/card-front-photo.jpg` (the designer's card front at print size, the pipe photo with the designer's own logo still in it), and Lexend in two weights, `public/fonts/Lexend-Light.woff2` and `Lexend-Bold.woff2`, taken from the `@fontsource/lexend` package 5.3.0 with its SIL Open Font License beside them, as Great Vibes already sits there.
- **Tests** (`draw.test.ts`, 32): the three pipe paths as fitted, the designer's baselines, the chosen look's steps, that a person with no title and no phone gets the designer's footer exactly, that four rows stay clear of the pipe under them, the fit of a long name, the ring's cover, the print pages, the facts and that every file the kit names is on disk.

No screen reads any of this yet. The release note is an `infra` note for devs.
