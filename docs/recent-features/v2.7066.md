# v2.7066 — Checklist Review: a map of where the open tasks are (2026-11-12)

Punch list #328, PR 7 of 8. v2.7062 gave each person a map of their own tasks. This is the same map for whoever hands the tasks out.

## What changed

- **Where the open tasks are** (`src/components/checklist/TaskPlacesTeamMapCard.tsx`), on the Review tab, right above *Outstanding by person*. It reads the board's own rows, so it follows the board's range: One-offs, Missed, Next day, Next week.
- **A colour a person** (`taskPlacePersonColor`), given by the person's place in the board's order, so a person keeps a colour while the board stands. Only people with a place on some task get a chip.
- **A press on a name** shows that person's tasks alone. A second press, or **Everyone**, brings the rest back.
- **A dot several people share** is navy and wears its count (`taskPlaceTeamDots`). A press on a dot names the place, lists who has what there, and offers **Directions**.
- **Tasks with no place** are counted in a line under the map. With no place on any open task there is no map.
- **Hide map** folds it and the device remembers, on its own key, apart from the person's own map.
- The same canvas, the same view from above and the same sizes as v2.7062.

## What it is not

It shows where tasks are. It never shows where a person is: the card imports no reading of a phone and asks for none, and a test holds that.

## Also

A task's title on either map's bar is now plain words (`taskTitlePlain`). A task made from a job carries the job as a chip inside its title, and the bar drew the chip's raw form.

- `taskPlace.test.ts` (+4, 35): the team's dots, the count line, the colours, the plain title.
