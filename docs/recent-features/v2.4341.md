# v2.4341 — Edit Job → Bill: easier to tap on a phone or an iPad (2026-10-01)

No migration, no edge function. From the live walk of the Bill tab redesign ([v2.4307](v2.4307.md), [v2.4309](v2.4309.md), [v2.4317](v2.4317.md), [v2.4329](v2.4329.md)) on clicktooling.com at desktop (1280), iPad (820, and 744 with touch) and phone (375 with touch), read-only on jobs 1059, 892, 922 and ATI Schertz, plus GC Review and both stages guides. Everything shipped read and worked at every size; four touch gaps were found and are fixed here.

## What changed

- **The money card's tick boxes** ([`JobFormMoneyCard.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormMoneyCard.tsx)): a pickable line's box now sits in a `label.tick`. On a coarse pointer the label carries a 44 px `::after` ring around the 22 px box, so a thumb that lands beside the box still picks the line, and nothing on the line moves (the line was already 44 px tall). A line that cannot be picked keeps its plain dot. +1 render test.
- **The payment line's ⋯** (`.jobPaymentLineMore`, from [v2.4293](v2.4293.md)): 44 × 44 on a coarse pointer, like the bill's own ⋯ (`.jobInvoiceMore`); it was 28 × 26. The narrower words cell broke an age like *61 d* into *61* / *d*, so `.jobPaymentLineMain .mut` (the · *N d* tail) no longer wraps inside itself.
- **① Line Items on a phone** (`.jobLineItems--stacked`, [v2.4312](v2.4312.md)): on a coarse pointer every box in the stacked lines (name, count, price, scope note, a discount's boxes) and the typed Job Total box (`.jobLineTotalInput`) take 16 px text, so an iPhone no longer zooms the page in when one is tapped. The stacked count and price boxes are sized in `ch`, so they grow with the text (measured at 375: count 35 px, price 107 px, nothing clipped). The iPad's fixed-width boxes keep 14 px.
- **The two switch examples in the stages guides** (*split a job into stages*, *schedule a sub across every surface*): the In order / Any time pair now sits on its own line under the line's name, as ① Line Items draws it. At 375 px the pair used to split, with *Any time* alone on the next line.

## Verified

Money card, ① Line Items, guide content, plain-words, share-card and illustration suites green. Headless Chrome against the dev server at 375 × 812 and 820 × 1180, both with touch (`pointer: coarse`): a tap 9 px beside the box lands on `label.tick` and picks the line (**Bill the 1 picked · $15,098** on job 892), a second tap unpicks it; the payment ⋯ measures 44 × 44 and *Loberg · 61 d* wraps as one piece on ATI Schertz; the phone's ① boxes read 16 px with nothing clipped; no element crosses the job window's edge. Not changed: the bar's blocks stay 20 px tall on touch (the tick boxes are the thumb's way to pick), and GC Review's job table on a phone still scrolls sideways to reach the waivers column.
