# v2.2931 — Their part, their percent (2026-09-06)

PR 5 of the three-party scheduling train (prototype: artifact 3a540149). Stacked on v2.2930. The owner's rules that shaped the card: a sub who picks 50% must not be able to hit a green "done" button by mistake, so there is one control (the chips) and one button whose meaning follows the pick; the buttons fill the card width so thumbs find them; nothing is sent until "Send to office".

## Data

- Migration [`20260906070000_sheet_progress.sql`](../migrations/20260906070000_sheet_progress.md): `people_labor_jobs.progress_pct / progress_note / progress_at / progress_source`.
- `submit-sub-portal` gains **`progress`** (`laborJobId`, `pct` ∈ {0,25,50,75} and/or `note`): assignee check, sheet must be working; writes the columns; mirrors `pct` onto a step-anchored sheet's `project_workflow_steps.percent_complete`; inserts `job_activity_events` (`sub_progress`, summary "Behar Kraja · 50% along on their part — “…”", detail `{ laborJobId, pct, note, source, address }`) on the sheet's Pipeline job; a **note** also drops dispatch note `sub_note` — a bare percent never does. `mark_work_done` now stamps `progress_pct = 100`.
- `sub-portal`: sheets carry `progress: { pct, note, on } | null` (`buildSubSheets`); the client payload parses it (`parseProgress`); the demo fixture shows 50% and 100%.
- `src/lib/jobActivityEvent.ts`: new event type `sub_progress` (tag "Sub progress", teal, status bucket).

## Portal (SheetCard)

"How far along is your part?" — five 48 px buttons in one row (100 ✓ outlined green, filled green when chosen; others teal when chosen), a progress bar, the note box, and the sentence: under 100 "Anything under 100% just keeps us posted — we keep waiting on you. **100% is what tells us to call it in.**" (with "last reported Sep 1"), at 100 "100% means finished. We call it in for inspection and the card moves to Pre-inspection." No button at rest; a percent change or a typed note shows teal **Send to office · N% along, still working** / **Send note to office** (→ `progress`); 100 shows green **✓ Send to office · work is done** → the existing confirm (the note carries into it; "Not yet" drops the pick back). Strings en / es in `subPortalI18n.ts`.
