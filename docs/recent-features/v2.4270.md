# v2.4270 — Lien calendar: the visual pass — two inks, one baseline, rows that breathe (2026-09-30)

No migration, no edge function. Grace, on the live v2.4265 board the same afternoon: *"looking at it I think we can still do better. How could we make this visually better?"* — then *"make the change"* on Draft 3 of the mock-up (artifact `JD7N5fPjMPGurmEdXmDd5B`, the board under the key strip). Nothing new to know; everything easier to see.

## What changed

- **Two inks for two deadlines** ([`LienDeskCalendarTab.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskCalendarTab.tsx)): the one `FLAG[tone]` map, which coloured every flag by urgency alone, is gone. `noticeInk(tone)` is amber (`#d97706`), `lienInk(tone)` is slate (`var(--text-700)`) — the density strip's own two colours — and both go red inside a week, green when done or filed. The **column headers** take the column's ink (amber where only notices land, slate where a lien does; muted when past), and a **GC row's folded flag** is now one flag per kind per date — the notices' hollow amber flag and the liens' slate flag side by side, each with its own count — instead of one flag whose count was whichever kind came first.
- **One baseline per row** (`LINE_Y`, `FLAG_H`): the track is a 1 px line; every pole ends on it, the pay dot and the dashed dot are centred on it, the run and the bracket sit just above it, and the work tick is a short 8 px mark standing on it. The stand-in's words shrink to *Aug · no hours* in small amber (`workMarkFor`'s label; the creation-day sentence stays in the hover and the key) — on the live board the bold *no hours (created)* on most of RMC's rows had been louder than the deadlines.
- **Rows breathe**: 48 px rows, alternate rows in `var(--bg-subtle)` instead of a hairline under each, dashed column rules, a 2 px top rule and a larger name on the GC row, medium-weight money on job rows (bold stays on the GC total).
- Kernel: only the tick's label and the lien entry's key words changed (`lienCalendar.ts`); tests follow. The key strip, the wash, the words and the doors are as v2.4265 left them.

## Not done

- The guide *send lien notices from the Lien desk* still says the hollow tick "says *no hours (created)*"; the tick now says *no hours*. The guide is on `LEGACY_PLAIN_WORDS_GUIDES` and the plain-words gate (v2.4233) fails CI on any edit until the whole 9,700-word guide is rewritten to the rules — that rewrite is the sweep's (batches 3–7), which should take the one-phrase change with it.
