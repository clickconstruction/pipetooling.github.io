# v2.7946 — Lien desk: each envelope of the copies to mail has its own divider page (2026-11-19)

## Why

The owner, 2026-11-19, after printing a run of 22 envelopes from *Filed liens: copies to mail*: "to avoid the possible situation where someone might get confused and put the wrong thing in the wrong envelope", put in front of each envelope what the other printouts have, "a drawn copy" of the envelope with "how the name and address would go", with its # on it so it is "easy to navigate back to", and the words "do not mail this page just the contents behind it up to the next cover page". He named the cost himself: 22 more pages for 22 envelopes.

The lien notice run has had this page since v2.4971 and calls it a divider (`runDividerHtml`). The copies' stack had none: 66 pages of letters and stamped copies behind one mail sheet, split by counting.

## What changed

- **A divider in front of each envelope** (`drawLienEnvelopeDivider`, words from `lienServeDivider` and `LIEN_SERVE_DIVIDER_WORDS`): a black band along the top edge so it shows in the stack, the envelope's number large with *#N on the mail sheet*, whose copy it is, the job and its mail-by day, a red box that says **Do not mail this page** and *Mail only the 3 pages behind it. They stop at the next divider, envelope N+1.*, the envelope's face, three things to tick, and *Pull this page out before you seal the envelope.*
- **The face** carries the return address (`lienServeReturnAddress`: the letterhead's company and address lines, never its phone or email), the certified mail line, and the name and address as the mail sheet's line has them, the street over the city, state and ZIP.
- **The number is the sheet's.** Each divider is drawn from the mail sheet's own line for that envelope, in the stack's order. If the sheet does not list exactly the envelopes behind it, no divider is drawn at all: a divider with another envelope's address would be worse than none.
- **The kept file is unchanged.** The PDF kept on each job is still its letters and stamped copies alone, what went in the envelopes. The dividers are in the printed stack only (`LienServePdfBuilt.dividers` counts them).
- The mail sheet has a step for it, *Each envelope has a divider page in front of its pages, with the same number as this list. Do not mail the divider.*, and its foot reads *The first envelope's divider is the next page*.
- `npm run lien:packet` counts each lien's dividers in its page numbers and names them.

A run of 22 envelopes of three pages is now 22 pages longer.

## Tests

`lienServePackets.test.ts`: the divider's words for a middle, a last and a single envelope, and the return address with a phone and an email dropped. `lienServePdf.test.ts`: the stack's order (sheet, then divider, letter and stamped pages for each envelope), each divider drawn from its own sheet line, the kept files with no divider in them, and no divider when the sheet and the stack disagree. `lienPacketPreview.test.ts`: the page spans with dividers. Checked by eye on a rendered sheet and dividers with made-up names.
