# v2.8036 — View as on a phone: Exit is a thin bar across the top (2026-11-20)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. View as is dev-only, so only a dev ever sees this.

The owner walked the app on an iPhone on 2026-11-20 while viewing as a crew member and marked the top of the screen: *"when impersonating someone, I think that the 'Exit (User)' at the top could be much more compact and take up less space while still being a clickable bar across the top"*.

## What was wrong

Since v2.7782 the way back from View as has its own amber line above the header on a phone (`ViewAsExitStrip`). The line held a button drawn as a box: `IMPERSONATION_CHROME_BUTTON_STYLE` inline (padding `0.35rem 0.75rem`, a 1 px amber border, a 4 px radius) inside a strip with 6 px above it and the nav's 8 px gutters each side. Measured at 375 × 812 on the Sample leader's Dashboard: the strip was 40 px tall, the button 34 px tall and 359 px wide. So the press stopped 8 px short of each edge and the line cost the page 40 px.

## What shipped

- **The line is the press.** `.appViewAsStripExit` (`src/index.css`) is now a flat band from edge to edge: no border, no radius, no gutter, no gap above the header. One line of words at 13 px on an 18 px line with 3 px above and below. `ViewAsExitStrip` drops the inline box style, so the class alone draws it.
- **24 px tall, and why.** A line of words plus a little padding lands between 20 and 24 px. 24 px is the smallest press WCAG 2.2 allows (success criterion 2.5.8, 24 by 24), and the band is the whole width of the screen, so the target is 375 × 24 on a 375 px phone. That is under the app's usual 44 px press height on purpose: the owner asked for the thinnest bar that is still a bar. Going to 20 px would save 4 px more and fall under that floor.
- **Still plainly an imitation.** The band is `var(--bg-amber-200)` under `var(--text-amber-900)` words, a shade darker than the amber header under it (`var(--bg-amber-100)`), so it reads as its own bar in light and in dark. The header keeps its amber tint and its amber rule.
- **The status bar.** The strip still pays `env(safe-area-inset-top)` on itself and still sets `--app-nav-safe-top: 0px` on the chrome after it. The band starts under the status bar and the Dynamic Island, never in them: a tap on the status bar is the phone's own scroll to the top, and it must not leave View as. The 6 px floor of padding above the old button is gone (`max(6px, …)` is now the inset alone), so with no notch the band is the first thing on the screen.
- **Unchanged:** the words (*Exit (Sample leader)*, from `impersonationExitDisplayLabel` with `compact`), the `title`, the accessible name (`IMPERSONATION_EXIT_ARIA_LABEL`), `handleBackToMyAccount`, the placement rule (`impersonationExitPlacement`, 640 px and under), and the desk's button in the header row, which keeps `IMPERSONATION_CHROME_BUTTON_STYLE`. `<main>`'s padding is untouched.
- The help guide *see the app as a role or as a person* says what the bar is on a phone.

## Tests

- `Layout.viewAsExit.render.test.tsx`, the phone case: the press is a real `button` with `type="button"`, it carries the class `appViewAsStripExit` and **no inline style**, and it is the strip's only child.
- Broken on purpose: with `style={IMPERSONATION_CHROME_BUTTON_STYLE}` put back on the strip's button the phone case fails on the inline style, and passes again with the line removed.

## Walked live

Local dev server, signed in as the dev, Settings → View as… → Sample leader. Job Mode and Task Mode were switched on this browser only (both live in `localStorage`); nothing was saved to the database while viewing as the sample.

| Check (375 × 812, Dashboard) | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Strip's height | 40 px | 24 px |
| The press | 359 × 34 px, 8 px short of each edge | 375 × 24 px, edge to edge |
| Header row's top | 40 px under the strip's top | 24 px under it |
| Page width | 375 px | 375 px |

Also checked after the change:

- The first and the last pixel of the band both land on the button (`elementFromPoint`), and a real press at the far left end returned to the dev account on `/settings`, the page left, with the stash cleared and no strip.
- The gear menu opens under the header as before. Task Mode's header (three presses) and Job Mode both lay out under the band; the header row is 65 px tall in all three, as it is with nobody viewed as.
- 320 px wide: band 320 × 24, page 320 px.
- Dark theme: band `#57430e` under `#fcd34d` words, header `#45390f`.
- Long names: a made-up 63-character label wraps to two lines (42 px) and an 86-character one with no spaces to three (60 px), both inside the screen.
- Keyboard: Tab lands on the band after the control before it, `:focus-visible` holds, and the 2 px ring is drawn inside the band (`outline-offset: -2px`), where the screen's edges cannot cut it off.
- The header search bar covers the header's box only; the band stays on top and pressable.
- A status-bar inset stood in by hand (59 px of padding forced on the strip, the Dynamic Island's inset): the pale amber fills the status bar's area, the band sits under it, and a point inside the inset is not the button.
- At 1280 px there is no strip and the header row's button is as before (30.7 px tall, its box style intact).

## Not checked

- Safari on a real iPhone, and the Home Screen app there. `env(safe-area-inset-top)` reads 0 in desktop Chrome, so the band's place under the Dynamic Island was seen with a stood-in inset, not on a phone.
- Whether a thumb finds a 24 px band under the Dynamic Island as easily as the old 34 px button. It is the owner's call on the phone; the height is one number in `.appViewAsStripExit` (`min-height` and the 3 px padding).

## Left alone

- The control's accessible name (*Back to your original signed-in account*) does not contain its visible words (*Exit (…)*), which WCAG 2.5.3 asks for. It was so before this change, in the strip and in the desk's header button alike.
