# v2.8150 — Document builder: the Sub NDA has a link (2026-11-20)

Punch list #404, step 8 of ten. No migration, and no function changed or deployed: `document-builder-link` has answered a published builder since v2.8008, and no screen asked it to until now.

## What a person can do

A dev opens Documents → Document builder → Sub NDA. On the left, under the map and the list of what changed, a card reads **This builder's link**.

- Before the first publish it says *Publish a revision first. A link then opens it.* and reads no link.
- Once a revision is published: **Switch the link on**, **Copy the link**, **Make a new link** (the old one stops, and the page asks first) and **Switch the link off**. It says the day the link was made and how many times someone outside opened it.

Anyone who holds the link opens `/d/<code>` with no sign-in and walks the published revision: the disclaimer and its box, the basis, the site, a page a topic with its slider, and the review. The page is headed *Secrecy on your jobs* with the revision beside it. It is a look only:

- No word on it is a field.
- The last step has no press. Where the editor's flow reads *Confirm and require this paper*, the page has **Back** alone and the line *That is the whole paper. Press Back to look at a page again.*
- What a person picks on a slider lives in the page and goes nowhere.
- Under the flow: *This page is a look at the paper. Nothing you pick here is saved or sent.*

A draft never shows. A revision published later shows on the same link.

## How it is built

- `src/lib/documentBuilder/linkPage.ts` (new, pure): `walkedLinkPage` turns the function's `{ kind: 'builder', builder, revision, contents }` into a paper to walk, with the name someone outside reads (*Secrecy on your jobs* for `sub-nda`: *Sub NDA* is the office's word). Only the paper's shape is checked (`parsePaperDef`), so a published paper still opens after a rule is tightened. Contents that are not a paper to walk answer null, and the page then says *Nothing is published on this link yet.*, as before. `walkedLinkCardWords` writes the card's words.
- `DocumentBuilderFlow.tsx` takes `look`: with it the last step draws no press and says the line it is handed. The flow already draws words alone when it is handed no `edit`.
- `src/pages/BuilderLink.tsx` draws the flow for a builder's link, pinned light as the page is, in a frame that ends at the bottom of the screen.
- `WalkedBuilder.tsx` draws `BuilderLinkCard` with the builder's key and no sheet, above the `share` slot the send takes in step 9. `linkIo` is handed in by a smoke.
