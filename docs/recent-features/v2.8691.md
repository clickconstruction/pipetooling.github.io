# v2.8691 — Agreements: a paper's line says when it prints its site's address (2026-11-27)

Punch list #439, piece 3. Client only: no migration, no edge function. The follow-up v2.8676 named as found and not built, asked for on 2026-11-27. Its ground on the Agreements view is the migration `20261127050501_agreements_on_file_site_print.sql`, a PR of its own, which this change does not wait for.

## What was there

Since v2.8676 the office can tick **Print the site's address on the paper**, and the signer then reads the address before signing. A paper that names a site reads *for the site at …* on a company's own list and on Documents → Agreements. No list said which papers that are out show their signer the address.

## What changed

- **The line.** A paper whose box was ticked reads *for the site at 118 Old Quarry Trl, Sampleton, TX, printed on the paper*. A paper whose box was left off reads as before.
- **A company's own list**, on a supply house and on a customer, says it now. That list reads the paper's row, which has kept the box since v2.8676.
- **Documents → Agreements** says it once `20261127050501_agreements_on_file_site_print.sql` is pushed. The list asks the view for `site_prints_address`. A view that does not have the column yet refuses that read, and the list reads again without it and says nothing about a print. One refused read a page, then the older columns alone until the page is loaded again.

## How it is built

- `src/lib/addresses/paperSite.ts`: `paperSiteNote` takes `site_prints_address` and adds `SITE_PRINTED_WORDS` after the address, only for `true` itself. A row read without the column says nothing about a print.
- `src/lib/documents/agreementsLedger.ts`: `AGREEMENTS_SELECT` asks for the column, `AGREEMENTS_SELECT_BEFORE_PRINT` is what an older view is asked, and `agreementsViewLacksPrint` knows that one refusal (42703 naming the column) from every other.
- `src/lib/documents/agreementsLedgerIo.ts`: `readAgreements` reads again without the column on that refusal, with the same filters, and remembers it for the page.
- `src/lib/contracts/partyPapersIo.ts` reads the column with a company's papers. `PartyPaperRow` and `AgreementRow` carry it, optional.

## Design calls

- **The words are the box's own, in the past: *printed on the paper*.** They read the same on a paper that waits and on one that is signed. Open to a flip to words about the signer, such as *the signer reads this address*.
- **Only where a line already says its site.** The sections **Papers for this site** on a job and on a development do not say a site on each line, so they do not say this either.
- **Soft on either side of the push**, by asking and reading again, so this PR owes nothing on prod and no order.
