# v2.5645 — Messages: an estimate's link speaks of the job's own, copies an approved one, and the line goes in at once (2026-10-19)

A review pass over punch list #127 after it shipped (v2.5641 to v2.5644) found three things in the reply box's `{estimate}` flow; this fixes them. No migration; no edge function.

- **The job's own estimate** (`messages/papers.ts`'s `defaultEstimatePaper`): a line that holds `{estimate}` pressed in a job's box took the customer's newest sent estimate, which, since a sent estimate has no job until it is approved, was always another project's. It now follows `defaultPaperFills`' rule for `{bill}`: a job's box speaks of its own estimate (the one waiting on an answer first, else the approved one that made the job) and never another job's, leaving the blank when the job has none; a thread speaks of the tied job's, else their newest sent one. A pick in the `/` list still wins.
- **An approved estimate is a copy** (`PAPER_LINES.approvedEstimate`, `slash.ts`'s `paperTeamLine`): picked from `/estimate`, it went in with the words *Open it to choose the option you want*, which the customer did already. It now takes the app's copy words, and the team's `{estimate}` line is not used for it, as a paid bill takes the copy words rather than the team's bill line.
- **At once, and nothing lost** (`ReplyBox`'s `withLink`): the line waited in a closure until `share_estimate_link` answered, then replaced the box with the text as it was at the press, so anything typed meanwhile was lost and a strip press after a `/` pick overwrote it. The line now goes in at once with its blank, the send row reads *Making the estimate's link…* (`ESTIMATE_LINK_MAKING`) and the link fills the blank when it is back, through the box's current text. A later press supersedes a share still out, so no line takes another's link; a failed share toasts and leaves the blank, as before. The note under the box is drawn while the box holds the link, not until the send.
- **Tests**: `papers.test.ts` (the job's own estimate, a thread's, none), `slash.test.ts` (no team line for an approved estimate), `ReplyBox.papers.render.test.tsx` (a thread's list and pick, the approved copy, the job's own, none for a job without one, the held share with typing kept and the note gone with the link, the refusal, a bill makes none).
- **Docs**: the guide *reply to a person from their thread*'s estimate paragraph.
