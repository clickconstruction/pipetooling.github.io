# v2.5745 — Job Summary: the job you search for always shows (2026-10-22)

Client only — no migration, no edge function. The owner, 2026-10-22, with a screenshot of Jobs → Job Summary: search `891`, Show on Finished (100%), the tiles all zero, the line *1 job matches but isn't finished yet — show all statuses.* and the floor box still reading *476 shown · 398 older imported jobs … hidden*: "on this page, when I look up a job by its number nothing shows up, can you take a look?"

## Why nothing showed

- `filterAndSortJobSummaryRows` ANDed the search with the Show, Worked in and Budget chips, so a job still in progress asked for by number lost to the Finished chip; the v2.3178 hint named it and drew nothing.
- The HCP # floor is applied in `fetchJobsLedgerStagesPrimary` before enrichment, so a job at or below 500 was never in the list the search read.
- The floor line counted `jobSummaryLedgerJobs.length`, the whole floored list, whatever the search said.

## What shipped

- **The search wins** ([`jobSummaryLedgerView.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobSummaryLedgerView.ts)): while the box holds something, the rows it matches are the rows — the Show, Worked in and Budget chips step aside, and each row is drawn with its real status and dates (a job still in progress shows earned revenue; a job worked outside the window shows no hours and no overhead for it). `jobSummaryRowsHiddenByStatus` is 0 while searching, so the empty state's v2.3178 hint is the no-search case only. Compare to and the Months, Cycle and Scatter views draw the same rows; the tiles are unchanged code and sum what is drawn.
- **The line under the controls** — new kernel [`jobSummarySearchWins.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobSummarySearchWins.ts), `jobSummarySearchNote`, drawn by the tab as `data-testid="job-summary-search-note"` while the box holds something in a rows view and the ledger is ready: *1 job matches your search. Job 891 is still in progress, so the Show chip stepped aside.* · *Job 827 was not worked in this window, so the Worked in chip stepped aside.* · *Job 432 sits under the HCP # 500 floor, so the floor stepped aside.* · counts per chip when several match, closing with *The chips and the floor step aside while you search.* · *No job matches zzz. Check the number, or clear the search box to see the list again.* Every sentence it can write passes `plainWordsFailures` (the test holds them). The old empty branch renders nothing while a search is on.
- **The floor steps aside for an exact job number** (`jobSummaryUnderFloorMatches`; [`useJobSummaryData`](../../src/hooks/useJobSummaryData.ts) takes `search`, declared above the hook in `Jobs.tsx`): [`fetchJobsLedgerWithDetailsForStages`](../../src/lib/fetchJobsLedgerWithDetailsForStages.ts) hands back the rows the floor dropped, shaped but unenriched (`flooredJobs`, from the primary query's `flooredRows`); an exact HCP or Click number lifts its row through the slim materials pass (`attachJobSummaryMaterials`, the body of the Job Summary slim enrich, now exported) and the row joins `jobSummaryLedgerJobs`, so the P&L memo, the card-charge batch and the money-story door all see it. A name or address match under the floor is counted (*2 more older jobs under the HCP # 500 floor match by name or address. Press show all under the table to see them.*), never lifted: `useJobsMercuryAllocations` refetches every allocation when the listed ids change, so a keystroke must not lift dozens of legacy rows. The lifted copies reset on every ledger reload.
- **The floor line** (`jobSummaryFloorFooter` takes `search`): *1 job shown for the search · 398 older imported jobs (HCP # 500 and below) hidden by the default HCP # 500 floor*, and *1 job shown for the search, 1 from under the floor · 397 older imported jobs …* when a lifted row is drawn.
