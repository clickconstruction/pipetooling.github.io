# v2.6520 — The property lookup no longer fills a street the map cannot find (2026-11-04)

Two edge functions change (**redeploy after merge**): **`property-lookup`** and **`owner-confirm-nightly`**. `geocode-one`, `geocode-address-batch` and `court-precinct-nightly` import the changed `_shared/googleGeocode.ts` (additive fields only; redeploy optional). No migration. Found on the ZZ walk for [v2.6497](v2.6497.md): a made-up `123 ZZ Test Ln, Taylor, TX 76574` typed on a ZZ Test customer's property sheet came back **lien-ready** — Williamson, Prop ID R015989, owner company `313 MAIN INC`, mailing `1924 NEWNING AVE, AUSTIN, TX 78704`, legal `S4790 - Taylor City Of` — because Google answered the street it could not find with a pin for Taylor itself (`partial_match: true`, `location_type: APPROXIMATE`, no `street_number`), and the roll named the parcel under that pin. A typo in a real address would have put a stranger's name on lien papers.

## What changed

- **Two gates, one kernel** — [`supabase/functions/_shared/parcelPinMatch.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/parcelPinMatch.ts), client re-export [`src/lib/customers/parcelPinMatch.ts`](../../src/lib/customers/parcelPinMatch.ts), 15 tests beside it:
  - `geocodePinHold(typed, precision)` holds a pin that is a partial match, an area (`APPROXIMATE`), the middle of an area (`GEOMETRIC_CENTER` without a street-level type), a street the map has at a number it lacks (`route`, no `street_number`), or another number on the street. Rooftop and range-interpolated pins at the typed number pass; so does a premise's centre.
  - `parcelSitusHold(typed, parcel)` holds a parcel whose own address on the roll (its situs) names another lot: a different house number, or a street whose core (no number, unit, directional or suffix word) is not the typed one. A blank situs — common on rural parcels — says nothing either way; the pin gate carries those.
  - `pinHoldWords(hold, city)` says the hold the same way on the record panel and in the nightly's summary.
- **One ladder for both functions** — [`_shared/parcelPinLookup.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/parcelPinLookup.ts), 9 tests with recorded answers: **Google first** (it alone says how well it placed the address), then the `address_geocodes` cache, then the US Census; the parcel under the pin; the county rung when the roll has nothing. A held pin is not asked of the roll and not written to the cache; a held parcel comes back as no parcel. Every outside call is injected, so the tests drive the whole ladder with no network.
- **`property-lookup`** returns `pin_hold: { kind, detail }` with `parcel: null` and the geocoder's county. **The record panel** ([`CustomerPropertyRecordPanel.tsx`](../../src/components/customers/CustomerPropertyRecordPanel.tsx)) draws the hold line in the amber box instead of *No parcel under the map pin*, keeps *County still resolved*, and leaves the CAD paste box as the way through; the client ([`propertyLookupClient.ts`](../../src/lib/customers/propertyLookupClient.ts)) reads a payload without `pin_hold` (an older function) as before.
- **`owner-confirm-nightly`** skips a held property, writes nothing for it, counts it as `held` in the summary line and the JSON, and names the reason in `details`.
- `_shared/googleGeocode.ts` returns `locationType`, `partialMatch`, `types` and `streetNumber` beside `lat`, `lng` and `county`; the Map's callers ignore them.
- The lien guide says what a street the map cannot find reads.

## Decisions

- **Google before the cache.** The cache row holds a point and nothing about how it was placed, so a cached city-centre pin would have kept the bug for any address the Map page had geocoded first. One geocode per lookup is the price; the office looks a property up a few times a day and the nightly caps at 40.
- **A corner lot can be held wrongly.** A parcel whose situs is on the cross street fails the situs gate; the line names the roll's address, and the paste box fills the record. Chosen over a wrong owner on lien papers.
- **No session card** for this branch: the base repo's `.claude/sessions/active/` is closed to a worktree session (a hook), and the claim is on GitHub.
- **What the old rule may have filled wrongly** cannot be told from the database alone — the cache has no precision and the record keeps no situs. Read on the dev server on 2026-11-04: of 513 `customer_addresses` rows, **39 carry a parcel the pin rule filled** (none from the paste box), 37 of them confirmed by a person's press and **2 still unconfirmed**; 1 row was looked up and got no parcel. Telling a wrong fill apart needs a re-geocode per row against the new gates, a later card; the two unconfirmed rows are the ones to eye first.
