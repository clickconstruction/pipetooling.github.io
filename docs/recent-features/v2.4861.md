# v2.4861 — Recently deleted: a removed bid keeps its quoted costs, splits and submittal ticks (2026-10-07)

Punch list #73, PR 0c ([`to-dos/bid-history`](../../to-dos/bid-history/README.md)): the last of the three "stop the losses at the source" fixes that needs no owner answer. Three bid tables sat outside the deleted-records archive: `bid_count_row_custom_costs`, `bid_takeoff_stage_splits` and `bid_submittal_takeoff_choices`. Removing a count row or a bid took them with no snapshot, and *Recently deleted* put the bundle back without them. Migration `20261008070000_bid_archive_three_tables.sql` ([doc](../migrations/20261008070000_bid_archive_three_tables.md)); no edge function.

## The change

- **The archive trigger on the three tables**, grouped under `bid_id` like `bid_count_row_custom_prices` and `bids_takeoff_rough_part_lines`. The restore needs nothing new: count rows and part lines go back first by FK depth, and the submittal ticks (no `id` column) insert through the same path.
- **The labels** ([`deletedRecordContents.ts`](../../src/lib/deletedRecordContents.ts)): *quoted costs*, *takeoff stage splits*, *submittal picks*; a removed quoted cost reads "quoted cost $3,700.00 · Ferguson".

The custom-costs FK the to-do also listed shipped in v2.4413.

## Tests

`deletedRecordContents.test.ts` (the three labels, the quoted-cost line). The migration ran on a scratch Postgres 15 with stub tables: applied twice, a count row and then the bid removed, every row archived under the bid, and the live `restore_deleted_records` put all of it back.
