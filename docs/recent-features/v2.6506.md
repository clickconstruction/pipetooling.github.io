# v2.6506 — Hand a conversation to someone by typing their name, with the office and estimators first (2026-11-04)

Grace's ask, 2026-11-04, with a screenshot of the who-has-it pill's menu on a New row at desktop width: "when a user opens this modal I would like for them to be able to start typing to search for a specific person, I would also like to see Assistants and Estimators at the top". The menu listed fifteen names in one alphabetical run, among them *Sample assistant*, *Sample controller*, *Sample estimator*, *Sample leader*, *Twin Estimator 1*, *Twin Estimator 2* and *Twin Pricer 1*. One PR, no migration, nothing to deploy.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/messages/handToOrder.ts`** (tested): the menu's order and search. `orderHandTo` puts the team in three groups, each by name: **Office and estimators** (assistant, controller, estimator), **Owner and devs** (master_technician, dev), **Everyone else** (any other role the read lets through; empty today, since `followUpTeamOf` keeps the GC office team alone). `handToMatches` and `filterHandTo` are one typed run against the name, case-blind, any part of it; `nextLit` is the arrows' walk, wrapping at both ends.
- **`WhoHasItPill.tsx`**: a search box at the top of the menu (*Type a name*, `aria-label` *Find a teammate*), focused on open unless the screen is a phone's (`useNarrowViewport640`), where the keyboard would jump over the list; typing narrows the groups and lights the run with the Jobs page's amber mark (`splitTextForQueryHighlight`); ↓ and ↑ move the lit row (`data-lit`, `aria-activedescendant`), Enter hands it to the lit one, Escape closes; the mouse lights a row on hover. The groups sit under thin headings with a rule between them (`role="group"`). *I'll take it* and *Nobody* draw only while nothing is typed, so Enter alone takes it; a run that lands on nobody draws `emptyWords({ kind: 'filter' })` with **Clear the search**. The list scrolls inside the menu past 70 vh. The menu hangs from the pill's right edge as before on a desktop row, and from its left when the right would not fit (`fromLeft`, read on open): at 375 px the pill wraps to the row's left and the right-hung menu ran 67 px off the screen, the walk's finding.
- **`loadGcTeam`** (`src/lib/gc/gcIo.ts`) now reads `is_digital_twin = false`, `is_sample = false` and `is_walk_tester = false`, as `followUpThreadIo.loadTeam` and `newLaneCardIo` always did: the Messages page's rows read the team through it, which is why the samples and the twins were on the page's menu and not on the thread header's. The read also feeds GC projects' pickers (who checks a set, the award), which lose the samples and twins too, the house rule for every picker. `walkTesterSweep.test.ts` lists the file.
- **Guides**: *answer everyone waiting on us from one page* gains **Hand a conversation to someone**; *choose who gets a new message* links to it. `MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md` and the Glossary's **Who has it** carry one clause each.

## Decisions

- **By role, never by load.** Grace asked for the assistants and estimators at the top; the person holding the most waiting is not promoted, since the point of the order is that the eye lands on the same people every time.
- **One group for the office and the estimators**, not two: Grace named them together, and on a team of four office people and two estimators two headings would be more rule than list.
- **The search is one run, not every-word**: a first name is what is typed; `tunnel marcos` has no place here.
- **A dev viewing as a sample** sees the real team and takes it as the sample through *I'll take it*; the samples are never a target, so the read needs no exception for them.
