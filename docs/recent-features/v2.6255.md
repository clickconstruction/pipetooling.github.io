# v2.6255 — The desk's New report form takes photos, on the one photo hook (punch list #229, PR 1 of six) (2026-11-01)

Punch list #229, PR 1. The owner's ask of 2026-11-01: *give user the ability to leave photos on their reports just like we have photos in the job tally on site section*. The per-job Leave report form took photos since v2.6083; the desk's New report form (`NewReportModal`: Jobs → Reports → **New report**, the Stages tab's row report, the Dashboard's Job Report square) took none. No migration, no edge function.

## What changed

- **`hooks/useReportPhotos.ts`** (new): the photo state the camera-first report built in `AdditionalReportModal` (the hidden file input, the tiles, the shrink in a worker, the send to the `report-photos` bucket with its row, the stage chips, the checklist question chips, Retry, Remove, the discard on a close without saving, the attach at Save with the checklist answers) lifted into one hook any report form mounts with a job id. It reads the office's size and the live questions once per open, reads whether the job has a Drive folder when the host has not, turns itself off on a null job id, and drops its tiles when the host's job changes under it, since a sent shot sits under its job's path. The host renders `<input {...photos.inputProps} />` once and draws `ReportPhotoTiles` from `photos.tiles`.
- **`AdditionalReportModal`** mounts the hook and keeps its behaviour: camera first on a phone for the field roles, the Add a photo tile at a desk, the stage rows' cameras, Save's words, the attach, the answers, Not now's discard. Its five camera tests and five form tests are unchanged and green.
- **`NewReportModal`** mounts the same block under **Report type** when the subject is a ledger job: the file picker at a desk, the camera on a phone for the field roles (`canLeaveJobFieldReport`), the stage chips and each stage row's camera on a job with lines, *New photo · 4.2 MB → 310 KB · 1600 px · goes to the job's Drive folder* over the tiles. Save reads *Save report · 2 photos* and waits while a shot is on its way (*Sending 1 photo…*); the photos' clause joins **Stage progress** (*1 photo on Rough In*); the save ties each sent shot to the report with its stage and caption and writes the checklist answers; a report with photos is never kept on the phone, as on the per-job form (the queue is this card's last PR). Cancel with a sent shot asks *Discard this report?* and Discard throws the shot away. Copy to Text ends with *Photos: 1 photo on Rough In*. A report on a project or a bid shows no tile and one quiet line, *Photos go on a job's report. A report on a project or a bid takes none yet.* (`REPORT_PHOTO_WORDS.onJobOnly`).
- **Guides**: *take photos for a report* gains **At a desk, or from the Reports tab**; *file and review field reports* says the New report form takes photos on a job. The Glossary's **Report photos** entry names the hook and the desk form.
- **Test**: `NewReportModal.photos.render.test.tsx` (new, 3): the tile, the shot, the stage chip, Save's words, the attach; a bid's report with no tile and the line; the discard on Cancel.

## Decisions

- **A hook, not a component.** The tiles are drawn in two places on the camera-first form (the top block and each stage row's foot), and Save, Copy to Text and the discard guard all read the shots, so the state is what is shared; the drawing stays `ReportPhotoTiles` and `StageRowPhotos`.
- **The desk form keeps its own camera rule.** It never opens the camera on open: the office picks a file, and a tech who reaches it on a phone with Job Mode off presses the tile. The per-job form's open-on-open stays its own.
- **No photo on a project or a bid.** `report_photos.job_id` is NOT NULL to `jobs_ledger`, the gate reads the job from the bucket path and the sweep files into the job's folder; widening that is the card's owner call 1, a migration of its own.
