# v2.7121 — Parts Book: Codes with no part, the houses' codes on the bills matched to parts (2026-11-13)

Punch list #298 PR 2a (`to-dos/supply-bill-lines/README.md`, frames D and E of its mock-up), over the crosswalk punch list #308 built ([v2.6901](v2.6901.md)) and the views ([v2.7114](v2.7114.md)). The key between a bill's line and a part is the house's own code. The crosswalk held it and the matcher read it, but nothing showed the office the codes that were missing. This is that list.

## What a person sees

**Codes with no part**, a press on the Parts Book beside *House numbers*, for the office roles.

- **The line at the top**: *1,968 codes with no part · $… behind them · the first 40 are 35% of it* (`codesStandingWords`).
- **A row per house and code**, the most dollars first: the line as printed, the house and its code, the manufacturer's number when the paper prints one, how often it was bought, the quantity, the spend.
- **The book offers**: the one part that fits and how it was found (*by its manufacturer's number*, *by the same name*, *by its words*), or the parts to pick from when two or more fit, or *no part fits yet*.
- **The presses on a row**: **This is it**, *Pick a part* (a search of the book by its words), *New part in the book* (the Add Part form opens with the line's words as the name), *Not a part* (a freight or a fee that prints a code) and *Later* (set aside until the window is opened again).
- **Match N offered codes** takes every sure offer. A code that is refused is named with its reason and the rest go on (`batchOutcomeWords`). *N left for a person* says why the others were left.
- **Nothing waiting on a part.** is the good empty state.

## How it is built

- **`src/lib/materials/lines/codesWithNoPart.ts`** (pure, `codesWithNoPart.test.ts`, 7 tests): `codesWithNoPart` gathers the purchase lines by house and folded code, leaves out a charge, a line with no code, a line with its own part and a code already on the crosswalk, and asks `matchRow` (`partMatch.ts`, the one matcher every door uses) for each row's offer. `offerIsSure` is a manufacturer's number or the same name; an offer by words alone waits for a look. `partsMatching` is the picker.
- **`codesWithNoPartIo.ts`**: the reads are the purchase view's (`purchaseLinesIo.ts`, now reading the manufacturer's number) and the parts catalog. The one write is `keepHouseCode`, with `taught_from` `bill` and the newest line as `taught_line_id`.
- **`CodesWithNoPartWindow.tsx`**: the window. `MaterialsPartsBookTab.tsx` draws its press when the page hands it `openAddPartNamed`, which `Materials.tsx` does for the office roles.

No migration and no function: the table, its policy and its unique key came with #308.

## Why teaching once is enough

Nothing writes a part onto a bill's line. What we buy, Price over time and What to stock each resolve a line through the crosswalk as they read (`lineIdentity`). So a code taught here regroups every past line and every future one with no backfill, and taking a number off under *Their numbers* undoes it.

## Choices made on the card's defaults

- **Who matches** (call 4): whoever keeps the Parts Book, and here that is the office roles, since the list reads the bills' lines. An estimator may write the crosswalk but cannot read the bills, so the press is not drawn for them.
- **The unit factor stays 1** on a code taught here. The mock-up drew it in the confirm. A house that sells a stick against a book priced a foot is rare and is set under *Their numbers*.
- **The book's price is not moved.** That is PR 2b, held to an offer for its first month.

## Is this the best we can do?

It turns the hour the measure asked for into presses, and the first forty rows are a third of the money. It is weaker than it could be in three ways.

- **Most rows have no offer.** On the live data 97 of 1,968 codes had a sure one. The book's parts are named by hand and the houses' lines are abbreviations, so words rarely agree. The manufacturer's number is the bridge, and it is on 57 percent of lines but in few of the book's part names. A column for it on the part would lift that.
- **A new part takes two presses.** The form opens on the line's words; the row then offers the new part by its name. One press that made the part and kept the code would be better, and needs the Add Part form to hand back what it made.
- **Later is forgotten when the window closes.** That is on purpose for now: a code set aside for good is *Not a part*.
