## Why

Owner, 2026-09-13: "I would like for GCs (not customers) to see jobs their customers need to pay us … if a bill is assigned to a customer and they look at their portal, they should not see bills their GCs need to pay" — then, on the plan: "we should be able to set it on a case-by-case basis." Since v2.3346 the statement counted only what the viewer owed but listed every other bill on their jobs under *On your jobs, billed to someone else*, symmetrically, and the `customer-portal` payload carried the other party's invoice amounts whether or not the page rendered them (`openBillJobIds` + an unfiltered invoice read). One code path (`billedToFor`) produced both directions, so they could not be changed independently.

## What shipped

- **Kernel [`supabase/functions/_shared/billVisibility.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/billVisibility.ts)** (re-exported to [`src/lib/jobs/billVisibility.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/billVisibility.ts); 17 tests): `shownToPartyFor(job, invoice)` — an invoice answers with its stamp and nothing else; a billed job's invoice-less shell remainder follows `jobs_ledger.show_bills_to_other_party` relative to the party the job rule bills; `statementRoleFor(job, invoice, viewer)` → `owed` (the payer's row is the viewer) · `shared` (the stamp names the party that is the viewer) · `hidden`; `otherPartyOf`, `defaultShowOtherParty`, `parseShownToParty`; client-only `shownToChipText`.
- **Schema**: `jobs_ledger_invoices.shown_to_party` (NULL · `customer` · `gc`) and `jobs_ledger.show_bills_to_other_party`. Every existing bill is NULL, so day one shows nothing that was not shown before v2.3346.
- **[`portalMergedBills.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/portalMergedBills.ts)**: `buildPortalBills` returns owed rows only (`billedTo` is gone from `PortalBillOut`); new `buildPortalSharedBills` — the stamped bills for this viewer with the payer's name, open amount, billed amount, received so far, billed date, and `viewerRole` (`gc` when the viewer is the job's GC and not its customer), oldest billed first, shells last. +5 tests.
- **`customer-portal`**: selects the two columns, emits `bills` (owed) and `sharedBills`, logs `shared_bill_count`. Everything else on the viewer's jobs is filtered before the payload — nothing the page hides can be read from the response. `submit-portal-request` is unchanged (its promise scope was already owed-only).
- **Client**: [`portalPayload.ts`](../../src/lib/portal/portalPayload.ts) parses `sharedBills` (tolerant of its absence; `PortalBill.billedTo` removed); [`PortalSharedBillsCard`](../../src/components/portal/PortalSharedBillsCard.tsx) under the ledger, one wording per viewer role — *Your customers' open bills* (summary: owners · open · oldest; rows: owner name, street and job, "Billed Aug 3 · 41 days ago · $2,000.00 received", open amount "of $6,420.00"; foot: total, "not in your balance") and *On your job, billed to your builder* ("Shown for your records · not yours to pay"); prints as its own page. [`CustomerPortal.tsx`](../../src/pages/CustomerPortal.tsx) drops the billed-elsewhere block. +2 render cases, +1 parser case.
