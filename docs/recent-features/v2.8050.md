# v2.8050 — Job Mode: Materials on site sits beside Job calendar, with a count when the site has boxes (2026-11-20)

The owner's phone walk of 2026-11-20, on the Job Mode card: *I don't like how this materials on site button looks, I don't think it's a good part of the user flow, and for 99% of jobs there are no materials left on site.* He asked for a whole look at where the door belongs, was shown three ways on a mock-up ([`to-dos/materials-on-site-door/mockup.html`](../../to-dos/materials-on-site-door/mockup.html), punch list #407) and picked the second: the door stays on every job, as a proper press beside **Job calendar**. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What changed

- **One row of presses under the address.** `DashboardJobModeCard.tsx` draws **Job calendar** and **Materials on site** as two pills in one row (`job-mode-job-doors`). Job calendar was a text link and Materials on site an underlined link alone between the day's stops and the pace card. The middle link is gone.
- **The count rides on the press.** A job whose site holds live cages, boxes or loose piles shows their number on the press (`job-mode-materials-count`), and the press's label for a screen reader says what it counts: *Materials on site, 14 cages and boxes on this site*. A site with none shows the bare label.
- **It counts the site, never the one job.** On the day of the walk every real box stood on one site, and all but two of them under a sister job nobody was scheduled on. A count of the scheduled job alone would have said two. `fetchSiteBoxCount` ([`siteBoxCountIo.ts`](../../src/lib/materials/siteBoxCountIo.ts)) reads the job's development, the development's jobs, then a head count of `job_containers` that are live and not Unsorted, in runs of 80 job ids. All three reads run under the reader's own RLS, so the number is what that person can open.
- **The number is kept on the phone.** `useSiteBoxCount` (since v2.8103 `useSiteDoorFacts`, [`src/hooks/useSiteDoorFacts.ts`](../../src/hooks/useSiteDoorFacts.ts)) draws the kept count on the first frame, reads once when the card names a job, and reads again when the page comes back to the front with a count five minutes old. A read that fails keeps the last count and says nothing. The words, the address and the kept counts' text are the pure [`siteBoxCount.ts`](../../src/lib/materials/siteBoxCount.ts).
- **The press opens the job the card names.** That is the clocked-in job or the next stop, through `siteBoxDoorHref`. Before, the link carried a job only while clocked in, and otherwise opened whichever job the tally listed first.
- **No job, no press.** A card that names no job, a day with no schedule among them, draws nothing about materials. The link used to stand there too. A scanned label, Job detail's **Materials on site** fold and Settings' **Job Parts Tally** still open the tab.
- **The short page** keeps its copy inside **Open this job**, now with the count.

## What was weighed and left

- **Say what is there, and nothing otherwise** (the mock-up's Way A): a row only on a site with boxes or with stickers waiting. It was the recommendation, on the strength of the owner's own 99%. He picked the press that is always there.
- **Off the card** (Way C): the label and Job detail as the only doors.
- **Which boxes are due a count.** A head count cannot say it. It needs the ledger or a small database function, so the press carries the one number.

## Verify

1. On a phone, in Job Mode, on a job whose site holds boxes: **Materials on site** stands beside **Job calendar** with a number. Press it. The On site tab opens on that job.
2. On a job with nothing on site: the same two presses, no number.
3. On a day with no schedule and no clock: the card reads *No schedule for today* and shows no Materials press.
4. Turn the signal off and open the Dashboard again on the first job: the number is still on the press.

Walked on the dev server at phone width on 2026-11-20 with a made-up stop on the ZZ test job (three live boxes): the count read *3*, the press landed on `/tally?tab=on-site&job=<that job>`, and nothing ran off the side.
