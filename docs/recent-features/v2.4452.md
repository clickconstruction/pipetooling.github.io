# v2.4452 — Procurement log: an answer recorded in the evening reads that day (2026-10-02)

Seen live on BP398 at about 19:30 CDT on 2026-10-02: an answer recorded then read *released 10/03* on the procurement log. Noted on the way in [v2.4450](v2.4450.md) and filed as its own fix. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

`rowFrom` in [`procurementLog.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/procurementLog.ts) cut the call's day out of `decision.at` with `.slice(0, 10)`. `decision.at` is `reviewed_at`, a `timestamptz` the database hands back in UTC, so after 7 pm CDT (6 pm CST) the slice is the next day. Every reader of the row took the day from there: the line's *released 10/03*, *Released 10/03*, *Approved 10/03* (`submittalWord`), the printed sheet's *Approved Oct 3* (`gcSubmittalWord`) and *returned Oct 3*, an update's diff and snapshot, the CSV, the GC's copy (`gcProcurementRows`) and the room's procurement card.

Both ways an answer is stamped "now" hit it: the review room (`submit-submittal-review`, the server's clock) and an answer the office enters for today (`enteredDecisionAt` → `now.toISOString()`). An answer entered for an earlier day is stamped noon UTC on purpose and already read right.

## What changed

- **The kernel.** `rowFrom` reads the call's day once, as `calledOn`, with `calendarYmdInAppTzFromIso(decision.at)` from [`dateUtils.ts`](../../src/utils/dateUtils.ts) (`APP_CALENDAR_TZ`), for both `releasedOn` and `submittalAt`. `ProcurementDecision.at` now says it is an instant. Nothing else in the kernel cut a day from an instant: the stage dates (`job_stage_windows.window_start`) and ordered / expected / delivered are `date` columns, calendar days already, and stay as they were.
- **An update's date.** The same slice cut a day out of `bid_procurement_updates.sent_at`, also a `timestamptz`. In [`SubmittalProcurementPanel`](../../src/components/bids/SubmittalProcurementPanel.tsx): *last update 10/03*, *Since …* in the send window, the *Updates sent* list, and an update reopened from it (the sheet's date and *changes since*). In [`SubmittalRoom.tsx`](../../src/pages/SubmittalRoom.tsx): the card's *Updated 10/03 by …*. Each reads `calendarYmdInAppTzFromIso(sentAt)` now.
- **The server is unchanged.** `get-submittal-room` ([`submittalRoomPayload.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/submittalRoomPayload.ts)) passes `reviewed_at` and `sent_at` through as instants, and the room page reads them with the same kernel. No function bundles `procurementLog.ts`, so nothing is redeployed.

## Not changed

- An update already sent keeps the days it was sent with. Its snapshot holds `releasedOn` as a day, and *Open* reprints it as it went out. The next update's diff asks whether a row is released, not on which day, so no row reads as changed.
- The room's own decision chips (`roomShortDate`) print the call in the viewer's device zone: Central for a Central GC.
- `summarizeSubmittalNudge` (the Needs You lead-time card) takes today as the UTC day of `now`. That is a "today" bug, not an instant's day, and is left for its own fix.

## Docs

`docs/TIME_AND_ZONES.md` names `calendarYmdInAppTzFromIso` as the web helper for an instant's day (and `todayYmdInAppTz(new Date(iso))` on the server); `enteredDecisionAt`'s comment no longer mentions the log's date slice.

## Tests

`procurementLog.test.ts` (+4):

- an approval at `2026-10-03T00:30:00Z` reads released and approved `2026-10-02`: *Released 10/02*, *released 10/02* under the line, *Approved 10/02*, *Approved Oct 2*;
- a call sent back, in the shape PostgREST returns (`+00:00`), reads *Sent back 10/02*, and a winter call at `2026-12-02T00:30:00Z` reads Dec 1;
- an answer entered for an earlier day (noon UTC) reads its own day;
- the GC's copy, the snapshot, the diff and the printed sheet carry the Central day, with no *Oct 3* on the sheet.

The evening cases fail on main's kernel.
