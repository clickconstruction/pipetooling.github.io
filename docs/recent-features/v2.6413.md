## Decisions

- **A copy, not a link.** The firm's portal and the printed packet read `legal_matter_documents`, so the signed PDF is copied into the matter's own folder rather than pointed at in the forms bucket: the matter holds its evidence whole, and the statement's `matter_document_id` says where its copy went. The forms bucket keeps the original the gate opens.
- **The exhibit is its own letter, under D.** Counsel's memo keeps labor out of the lien run's copies; the packet letters the statements beside the field evidence, never among the lien paper, and the firm's section D holds them.
- **A gap job asks no one.** The window picks from the people who clocked on the job; a job with no clock has no one to pick, and a statement there would be the worker's own declaration. The band says that instead of offering a press that could not pick.
- **Drive's *Sent by us* waits.** `drive-link`'s `put` has no caller yet; the card keeps it as the later S.
