# v2.5885 — Billed Awaiting Payment reads the lean (2026-10-26)

PR 2 of punch list #180 (`to-dos/quickfill-read-state/`). The station listed every open bill by customer with the days since it was billed, 50 to 78 of them over 117 marks and never zero, and asked on its banner whether anyone had leaned on them this week; no row said, so the person held that in their head and rebuilt it from sixty rows each morning.

- **The lean is the Payment Chase touch** the app already keeps (`list_payment_chase_touches`, 180 days, office roles) and the promised pay dates (`list_job_promised_pay_dates`). `lib/quickfill/leanRead.ts` folds the customer groups the station already builds (`buildBilledByCustomerBreakdown`) into three: **needs** (the oldest bill `LEAN_PAST_DUE_DAYS` = 30 days or older, no touch inside the owner's days, no promise standing), **waiting** (a touch inside the days, or a promise standing by `BROKEN_PROMISE_GRACE_DAYS`) and **current**. Each row says why (*no lean in 12d*, *never leaned on*, *promise broken*, *leaned on 1d ago*, *promise standing*, *18 days until past due*) and its last touch through `arLastTouchLine`, the AR view's own words.
- **The owner's number** is `app_settings.quickfill_lean_days_v1` (`parseLeanDays`: 1 to 60, default `LEAN_DAYS_DEFAULT` = 7), changed by a dev from the station's stance line.
- **The station** (`BilledAwaitingPaymentSection`, now taking `lastMarkedAt` from the page): the count line reads *N need a lean · N past 60 days · bills · open · customers* with **Call mode →** (`?chase=1`); the three folds, Need a lean open by default, each a table with a **Last lean** column; a needing or waiting row expands to **Log a lean**: *Couldn't reach* (a `cant_reach` touch with the default snooze) and *Note…* (a `note` touch), both through `addPaymentChaseTouch`, the one chase write path, then the touches re-read; a promise or a longer call goes to call mode. *Since the last look* counts customers whose oldest bill crossed past due since the mark and customers leaned on since (`leanSince`). The metric is the needs count; `leanNextLine` names the first (*Lean on Ridgeway Builders · $41,200 · 64 days · no lean in 12d*); `leanMarkNote` is the note the Mark saves.
- `billedAwaitingNextLine` in `nextLine.ts` is no longer read by the station; its test stands.

Tests: `leanRead.test.ts` (the number, the folds and their order, the why words, a broken promise, a longer number, the counts, the Next line, the since line, the note) and `BilledAwaitingPaymentSection.render.test.tsx` (the folds and the metric over mocked billed rows; a lean logged from the row through the chase RPC and the re-read; a promise keeping a customer waiting).
