# v2.8263 — An empty box asks about a take in a quiet link (punch list #398, the walk of 2026-11-21) (2026-11-21)

The walk of 2026-11-21 saw the sticky blue **Take from ZZ2** on a box with nothing in it and asked whether it belongs there. The owner picked the answer the same day: a quiet link. Client only: no migration and no function. Card: [`to-dos/box-screen/README.md`](../../to-dos/box-screen/README.md).

## Why the press was not a slip

A take on a box with no line is a drawn path. `TakeScreen` reads *Nothing is listed in ZZ2.* and offers **Something not on the list…**, and a line that reads none can still be tapped, the extra recorded as found. A list can be behind its box, so the door has to stay. What was wrong was its weight: the one blue press on the screen asked for a take of nothing, on a box whose next press is **Add a part**.

## What changed

- **`takeDoor(lines)`** in [`takeRun.ts`](../../src/lib/materials/takeRun.ts) says how a box's screen offers a take: `press` while any line has a part on hand, `quiet` when none does. That covers a box with no line and a box whose every line reads none.
- **On `quiet`** the sticky bar is not drawn. In its place, at the foot of the screen and not pinned, is a link, **Took something out of ZZ2? Say what ›** (`takeQuietLabel`), over the bar's own line, *The camera opens for a photo of ZZ2 as found.* It is 48 px tall, the screen's bar for a press. It calls the same `startTake` the press does.
- **Unsorted and a box with a run left open** draw neither, as before.

Read on the dev server on the test job, looking only, nothing pressed: at 375 × 812 the empty box shows the link, 285 × 48, centred, with no blue bar, and the page is 375 wide. At 320 × 640 the link is 272 × 48 and the page 320 wide. The box with a part in it shows **Take from ZZ1**, sticky, as it did.

## What I would not claim

The link was not pressed on the live app, since a press opens the phone's camera and keeps a run on the phone. The render test presses it: the camera opens once and the run's screen reads *Nothing is listed in A2.* with **Something not on the list…**.
