# v2.8385 — Messages: a day they named goes where that kind of date lives (punch list #421, PR 4 of 5) (2026-11-23)

Punch list #421 (`to-dos/messages-heard-day.md`), the write-throughs' hands. It stacks on PR 3 (v2.8384), which calls `write_down_heard` (PR 2, v2.8383), so it waits on that push. No migration, no edge function, nothing in the kernels copy.

## What changed

- **The read** (`messages/promiseWritesIo.ts` → `loadPromiseAt`): what the October kernel `promiseWriteThrough` reads, for one written line. That is the line's job by name and by kind from its key (`jobRefOf`: a bid's `project_name`, a GC job's `projects.name`, a Pipeline job's `job_name`), the company's open ask on that GC job for a quote (`gc_trade_packages` then `gc_invites` still invited or opened), and the person's standing holds on GC jobs (`nudge_holds` not let go, the latest a step and job). Each read is soft. A failed read leaves its piece empty, and the kernel says what was skipped.
- **The writers** (`supabasePromiseWriters`): a quote through the Board's `logGcAskContact`, a start, a delivery, insurance or a W-9 through `recordGcTradePromise` (`gc_record_promise`), a customer's answer as a `bids_submission_entries` note with the follow-up day, and a hold through `nudge_hold_until` on the GC job. A payment never comes here, since it is written from #129's sheet.
- **The words** (`promiseWrites.ts` → `throughWords`): after *Written down. Their quote by Wed Nov 25.* come where each write went (*It went on their ask on the Board.*, *It went on the Board's promises.*, *It is the bid's follow-up day.*, *The flow's reminder waits until Wed Nov 25.*) and every skip. A refusal is said alone in its own words as an error. A line on a Pipeline job skips the flow's hold with *The flow keeps reminders on a GC job only. The day stays on their line.* (`PIPELINE_HOLD_WORDS`, a new `line.pipelineJob`), never as a line on no job.
- **The press** (`useFollowUpThreadSlots` → `writeHeard`): after `writeDownHeard`, a kind runs `writeHeardThrough` on the thread io with the line, the person, the day and who pressed, and its words are the toast. Just talk writes nothing else. `writeHeardThrough` never rejects: the line's day already stands.
- **Tests**: `promiseWrites.test.ts` (the words after, nothing made, a refusal, a Pipeline job's hold); `promiseWritesIo.test.ts`, new (the read for a quote on a GC job in the app's zone, a hold kind's standing holds, a failed read, a Pipeline job; a quote onto its ask, a start onto the Board's promises, a refusal, no ask open); `useFollowUpThreadSlots.render.test.tsx` (the press hands the write-throughs the line, the person and the words, and shows theirs; just talk sends nothing through).
- **Docs**: the guide *reply to a person from their thread*; `MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md`; the Glossary; the card.

## Defaults for the owner's read

- **The write-throughs follow the lead's map of 2026-10-12** in `promiseWrites.ts`, unchanged. Holds are set only for a day after today, never earlier than one already standing.
- **A Pipeline job gets no flow hold.** The holds the kernel names are the GC flow's steps.
- **A write-through that fails leaves the line's day written.** Nothing rolls back, and the words say what to do by hand.
