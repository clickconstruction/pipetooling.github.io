# v2.3969 — Bids: the ring a link leaves on its row is one piece (2026-09-27)

Client only; no migration, no edge function, no screen change. Punch list **#46** row 5 (`to-dos/decomposition-queue.md`): the first piece of the Bids map's step 7 (`docs/BIDS_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md` → Recommended extraction order — "one `useDeepLinkHighlight`").

## What is new

**`src/hooks/useDeepLinkHighlight.ts`** (6-case test, fake timers) — when a link lands on a bid's row or a builder's card, the row is ringed for two and a half seconds and then let go. The hook holds the ringed id, a count of landings (so landing on the same row twice still scrolls and expands), the clock, and the cleanup when the page is left. `flash(id)` rings a row; a ring already showing is replaced and its clock restarted.

`src/pages/Bids.tsx` carried this three times — the Bid Board's applier, and the By builder lens's two doors (a status-lens row's jump, a bid's deep link) — as two pairs of states, two timeout refs and one unmount effect.
