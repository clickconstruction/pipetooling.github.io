# v2.7891 — Trips: split a trip in two (2026-11-18)

## Why

One of the parts the trip record's card (#379) owed, drawn on its mock-up as a press on the open trip. A trip is one going out and coming back. The phone grows a trip a day at a time, and a crew that goes home for three days and comes back presses **Start a trip** onto the trip it already had. A trip typed from a ledger can have the same fault. The fix was two edits by hand: cut the trip down, then type the second part again.

## What changed

- **Split in two** on an open trip, beside **Change**, for a reader who can change trips. A trip of one day does not show it.
- **The window** (`TripSplitWindow`): pick the day the second trip starts. Every day but the first is offered with who was on it, and a day that follows days at home says so. The day after the longest stretch at home is picked already. Both trips are drawn before the press, and the press is **Split into two trips**.
- **One write**, `trip_split` (`20261119010000_trip_split.sql`, `docs/migrations/20261119010000_trip_split.md`): the trip keeps its number and the days before the day, and a new trip takes the rest with the next number. It writes through `trip_save` twice inside one call, so both land or neither does.
- **The kernel**, `src/lib/trips/tripSplit.ts`: the days, the day offered first, both halves as the database will make them, and every word.

## Decisions

- **The first part keeps the number.** A bill or a text that cites *Trip 3* still means the trip that began on that day.
- **A trip on a bill keeps its line, and the new trip starts with no billing said.** The line's amount may cover both parts. Nothing should read *Extra, not billed yet* until a person says so.
- **A person with days on both sides is on both trips.** Nobody's day is dropped or moved.
- **The phone's ended stamp.** The new trip takes the stamp the trip had. The first is ended once its last day is behind today, so the phone stops offering it.
- **No undo, and it says so.** An undo is a join, which is its own piece of work. The window draws both trips before the press, and says how to take a split back by hand.
