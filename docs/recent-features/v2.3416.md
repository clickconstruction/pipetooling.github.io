# v2.3416 — Pipeline: the Progress & payment legend adds up (2026-09-14)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. First PR of the *Where the Job Is* train (owner-approved brief, 2026-09-14: "I love this, build it all") — the bug fix that ships before the redesign of the cell.

## Why

The owner's screenshot of a Working row (80% of $40,000 done, $13,412 paid, $11,770 billed) read **17% Unbilled · $18,588**. The 17% was the amber slice (done − paid − billed = $6,818); the $18,588 was `model.unbilled` = done − paid, which still held the $11,770 already billed. The percent of one thing sat beside the dollars of another, and the rows never summed to the bid.

## What shipped

- **Kernel** [`stagesMoneyBar.ts`](../../src/lib/stagesMoneyBar.ts) (+2 tests): `doneNotBilled` (done − paid − billed-unpaid, floored at 0) and `notDone` (total − value created). `unbilled` is unchanged — the Dashboard totals and the customer profile still read it as "not yet paid for".
- **The cell** [`StagesProgressPaymentCell`](../../src/components/jobs/StagesProgressPaymentCell.tsx) (+2 render cases): the amber row is **Done, not billed** and prints `doneNotBilled`; a fourth row, **Not done**, names the empty track with its share; the bar tooltip and the compact card line use the same figures. The row's percents are each slice's share of the bid, so the four percents and the four dollar figures both sum to the bid.
- Help guide *bill a customer and get paid* (`ready-to-bill-pipeline.md`): the paragraph on reading the bar.

## Not in this PR

The rest of the brief — stages recognized from the line names, the crew's position as the live stage, the money channel under the segments, the words line — follows in its own PRs.
