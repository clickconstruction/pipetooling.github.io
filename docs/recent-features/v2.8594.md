## What was wrong

`readLienBook` in `legal-portal` read `job_lien_filings` with `kind` in *affidavit* and *release_of_record* alone. The app's own book has read *notice_53_056* too since v2.7485 (`useLienTimelineBook.ts`), and `lienTimelineMonthsWithNoticeRecords` (punch list #415, v2.8535) makes a month a live record names read as sent, on the day its packet left.

A notice the desk's run sent reaches the firm anyway, through its desk paper. A notice **kept by hand** (v2.8554) has no desk paper. The months reader drops a noticed month a week after its day. From then the firm's grid had no month for that job and no day a notice went, over a notice on file.

## What changed

- `_shared/legalLienBookShape.ts`: `LIEN_BOOK_FILING_KINDS` names the three kinds the firm's book reads, and the function's read uses it.
- `LIEN_BOOK_FILING_COLUMNS` gains `sends`. It is read for one thing and cut before it travels (`counselSends`). On a recorded notice each send becomes `recipient`, `method` and `sent_on`, and a send with no day is dropped. On every other kind `sends` travels as `null`, as nothing of it travelled before.
- **Nothing else of a send travels:** no mail number, no name, no address, no email. `docs/DOCSMIT_LIEN_RUN_PLAN.md` says counsel's portal leaves out tracking, and that still holds.
- The page needs no change. `assembleLienBookInput` and `buildLienTimelineBook` are the app's own and already read a notice's months and its earliest send day.
