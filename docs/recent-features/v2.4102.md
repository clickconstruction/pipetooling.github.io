# v2.4102 — Bids: the Edit Bid controller is its own piece (2026-09-28)

Client only; no migration, no edge function, no screen change. Punch list **#51** (`to-dos/bids-edit-controller.md`), PR 4b — the second half of moving the Edit Bid controller out of `src/pages/Bids.tsx` (3,259 → 2,795 lines).

## What is new

**`src/hooks/useBidEditController.ts`** — what was left of the page's region R14, moved verbatim: the Bid window's four doors (New Bid, from a project, with a customer; Edit) and `closeBidForm`; the lost-reason save from the lost summary; the trade switch (`useBidTradeSwitch`, v2.4074, now called from here); the notes written after a save; the payload builders; `autosaveBid` on `useJobFormAutosaveSlice` with its visibility flush and the close guard (`requestCloseBidForm` / `closeBidFormWithoutSaving`); `saveBid`, `saveBidAndOpenCounts`; `deleteBid`. It takes the window state (`useBidWindowState`, v2.4086) as one input, plus the form, the sent-date checklist, the loader, the robot layer's two ledger calls, and two page callbacks.

**`src/hooks/useBidEditController.render.test.tsx`** (6 cases) — composed with the real window state, form and checklist hooks: Edit and New Bid open the window with the right values, close writes nothing, delete writes nothing until the project name matches and then deletes that bid once and closes, a refused delete stays open, and the lost summary's write (trimmed reason, blank → null, a zero-row answer says so).

## What stays in the page

`handleLastContactClick`, `syncFreshBidIntoSelections` and `openCountsForBid` (the selections and the Counts tab are the page's; the last two are handed in), `saveBidSubmissionQuickAdd` and `openGcBuilderOrCustomerModal` — they sat in R14's lines but are not Edit Bid. The controller is called where `openNewBid` stood, so every hook and effect keeps its order. `useBidsDeepLinks`, called above it, now gets its two doors as arrow wrappers (`() => openNewBid()`), read only inside its effects.
