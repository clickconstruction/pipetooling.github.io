# v2.5332 — Follow up: see who needs a reminder on their steps (2026-10-12)

Messages, cut 4, wave 2 piece 2: Follow up's **Nudges from the flow**, the prototype v1.178's section of that name, in helper 2's `nudges` slot on `FollowUpPanel` (v2.5289). It is drawn after We owe them and counts on no badge. Its guide section is in `see-who-is-waiting-on-us-and-what-we-owe-them`.

- **The rows** are `nudgeRows` (`src/lib/flows/nudges.ts`) over everyone on the three flows, one row a person, worst first. The panel hands its portal skip to the slot's `build`, so a paper the customer's page already shows is not nudged twice.
- **The reads** (`src/lib/flows/flowNudgesIo.ts`, `loadNudgeBatch`) are each kind's flow (`loadFlowDef`) and rows (`loadFlowRowsKind`, `flow_rows_kind`). Then four office reads, as the lead set on 2026-10-12:
  - the standing holds (`nudge_holds`);
  - the reminders sent in the last 120 days (`nudge_sent`);
  - the team's notes on a step (`messages` with a `step_key`);
  - the thread's lines in the last 120 days.

  Each person also gets their own open `contact_owed` rows, and a customer gets their page's read-back from cut 8 (`loadCustomerPortalReadBack`). Each person is built by helper 4's `personFlowFrom`. A read that fails gives an empty list, so one table behind cannot hide every reminder.
- **The names** follow the boards' rule (`nudgeNamesOf`). The press uses the person's first name, or the company when nobody is named: *Text Kerr Electric*, never *Text Kerr*.
- **The hook** (`src/hooks/useFlowNudges.ts`) reads only while Follow up is on screen. Its presses act on the steps the row's one message names (`namedSteps`):
  - **Hold until Mon** holds each until next Monday (`nudge_hold_until`);
  - **Remind now** records each with the channel the flow asked for and its round (`nudge_record_sent`, which lets a standing hold go since 20261013020000).

  Then everyone is read again, and the page's toast says what happened, or the press's own words when it was refused.
- **The section** (`src/components/followUp/NudgesFromTheFlow.tsx`) draws each row's name, the steps its message names, *Our move* with the channel, and why. Its presses are the nudge's own (*Text Greg*, *Call*, *Email*), which opens the thread until the send window lands, plus *Hold until Mon* and *Remind now*. A row's buttons wait while its press runs. It says *Reading the flows…* while it reads, and why when it could not read.
