# v2.7861 — Trips: combining two jobs keeps a trip's job (2026-11-18)

## Why

One of the four parts the trip record's card (#379) still owed, and the second of the follow-ups the owner chose on 2026-11-18. A trip names the jobs its work goes on. The combine deletes the source job after moving its rows, and the trip's link was not among the rows it moved, so the link went with the job. The trip then named no job, or one job fewer, and nobody was told.

Prod held no trip link on the day this was written (read 2026-11-18): trips are two days old. The fix lands before the first one is lost.

## What changed

One migration, `20261118213000_combine_carries_trip_jobs.sql` (`docs/migrations/20261118213000_combine_carries_trip_jobs.md`).

- **`migrate_job_ledger_costs_and_delete` restated** from its newest body with one block added before the source job is deleted: each trip that names the source job is given a link to the target, a trip that already names the target keeps its one link, and the source's links are removed.
- The file was generated from the newest body by a script. A diff of the two bodies shows the added block and nothing else.

## Decisions

- **A trip the source job holds by itself is not moved.** That is a trip on a job with no development (`trips.job_id`). No screen makes one and none exists. Moving it needs a number in the target's own run, since a trip's number is never changed by a statement. It still goes with its job and is kept in the deleted-records archive.
- **The line needed nothing.** A trip's line moves with the job's lines and keeps its trip.

## Docs

`docs/BILLING_FLOWS.md` names this file as the combine's newest body. `docs/GLOSSARY.md` says the combine carries a trip's job links. The trip record's card (#379) takes this part off its owed list.

## Tests

`supabase/tests/combine_rows/20_scenario.sql`: two trips on the bed's development, one naming the source job alone and one naming both jobs. After the second combine the first names the target, the second keeps one link, both trips are still there, and no link was kept as a deleted trip's.
