# v2.5015 — New Job and New Bid: the Deposit required? nudge reaches the GC (2026-10-09)

Client only. No migration, no edge function. This builds the owner's call of 2026-10-09 on *Uncollectible: nudge the GC when the GC was the one who never paid?*. The owner said yes.

## The gap

The owner's call 4 of 2026-10-07 built the *Deposit required?* nudge (punch list #94, v2.4795). `useCustomerTermsWarning` counted the bills the office gave up on where the picked id was the job's `customer_id`. On a GC-pays job the debtor is the GC, so the nudge reached no one there. Prod's two GC marks of 2026-10-07 had these shapes:

- **J1002** (Terrell): no customer on the job, and billed Heron Construction Group.
- **881** (Dudley Mason): the customer on the job, and billed RMC- Dudley Mason.

Both have `bill_to_party` = `gc`. A read-only look on 2026-10-09 found five given-up jobs on prod: these two and three that billed their customer.

## The change

- **`useCustomerTermsWarning`**: the given-up read is now `customer_id = X`, or `gc_customer_id = X` with `bill_to_party = gc`.
  - A GC the job billed owns those bills.
  - A job the GC was only on, which billed its customer, leaves the GC out.
  - A *split* job leaves the GC out too, because each bill picks its own payer.
  - The customer branch is as it was, so 881 nudges both its customer and RMC- Dudley Mason.
- **The forms** needed no change, because both already pass the GC:
  - New Job passes `termsPayerId`: the GC on a GC-pays job, and the GC when no customer is picked (J1002's shape).
  - New Bid's main customer is the GC.
- *mark a bill uncollectible*: the nudge's bullet says a GC counts too when the job billed the GC.
