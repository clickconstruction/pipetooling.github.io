# v2.5107 — Job Parts Tally: undo a sort on the team's card charges (2026-10-09)

Punch list #72, PR 3, its first half: undo per line (`to-dos/tally-transactions-refresh.md`). Client only. No migration, no edge function. The pay bar, PR 3's other half, waits on the owner (below).

## What shipped

- **The kernel, `src/lib/tally/tallyUndoLine.ts`.** It decides which sorted charge can be undone and builds the write. A charge qualifies when it went to jobs and nothing else, has a holder, and (on a day card's line) `viewerCanSort` is true. A charge matched to invoices keeps its invoices window. A charge marked payroll is left alone because unmarking takes payroll access. `tallyUndoRpcArgs` calls `replace_mercury_job_splits_for_linked_card_as_staff` with `p_rows: []`. The write's current body is the baseline's: with an empty array it skips the sum check, deletes the charge's splits and inserts none. So the charge is unsorted again and returns to the queue on the next read. `tallyUndoToast` gives the words: *1 charge is back to sort.*, *2 of 3 are back to sort. The rest could not be undone.*, *Nothing was undone. Try again.*
- **Three doors in `TallyTeamQueue`**, all through one `undoLines`:
  - **The message after Sort the day** is now an action toast with **Undo** (10 seconds). It undoes the charges that press wrote. This is the door for a fully sorted day, whose card leaves the queue on reload.
  - **A card's sorted line** (`TallyTeamDayCard`) gets an **Undo** link, labelled with the store and amount for a screen reader.
  - **A row of Sorted.** `TeamPurchasesSortedList` takes an optional `onUndo` and `undoBusyIds`. The Dashboard and Quickfill window passes neither, so it does not change.
- Each Undo reads *Undoing…* while it writes. The queue and the Dashboard count reload after any charge comes back.
- **Guide**: *sort the team's card purchases from the office* gains **Undo a sort in Job Parts Tally**. GLOSSARY (Team purchases), JOB_TALLY_ARCHITECTURE (the Team view's kernels) and PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION (`/tally`) are amended in place.

## To see it

As the dev on the dev server, open `/dev-login?as=1&to=/tally?tab=transactions`. On Team, sort a day, then press **Undo** on the message. The charges are back on the card. Sort one charge of a two-charge day, and its line under the card offers **Undo**. On **Sorted**, a charge that went to a job offers **Undo** and one matched to invoices does not. These are production charges. Sort and undo only a charge whose job you know, and leave it sorted after.
