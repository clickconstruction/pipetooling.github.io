# v2.7676 — Trips: the look says when punches look like a trip, and what shows a trip (2026-11-17)

Punch list #379, PR 5 of nine: **Looks like a trip** and **What shows it**. Client only. It reads and writes `trip_dismissals`, one of the four tables of v2.7655 (`20261117234500_trips.sql`), so that migration is pushed before this reaches the live app. No new migration, no edge function.

## What landed

- **Looks like a trip.** The finder of PR 1 (`findTripsNotMade`) is on the screen. `DevelopmentTripsCard` turns each person's day against the site's point (`placesByPersonDay`, already read for the hours) into the days they punched in at the site (`sitePunchDays` in `tripHourRows.ts`), and `buildTripOffers` in the new `lib/trips/tripOffers.ts` passes over the days a kept trip covers and the days somebody said were Not a trip. What is left is drawn above the rows as a dashed amber band, oldest first: *Omar punched in at Heron Point on 4 days, Sep 8 to 11. No trip covers those days.* The days are person-days. Past three offers the look shows three and one quiet line that counts the rest.
- **Make it a trip.** Opens the same sheet on a new trip filled from the punches (`tripRecordFromOffer`): each person as the record keeps them, each punch day painted At the site, a gap left off. Nothing is saved until **Save the trip**. Closing the sheet keeps nothing and the offer stays.
- **Not a trip.** One `trip_dismissals` row a person, from their first punch day in the run to their last (`dismissalRowsForOffer`), `dismissed_by` the signed-in user, stamped in `insertTripDismissals`. The toast says what was remembered and offers **Undo** for a minute, which deletes the rows it made. A failure names its reason.
- **Who an offer names.** A roster person by the roster. A login with no roster row by `users.name`, read once for those logins (`readUserNames`), and *Someone* until it is read.
- **When no offer shows.** While the hours are not read, on a site whose address has no point, and to a reader who cannot write. The offers themselves need only the roster, the punches, the point and the Not a trip rows, so the look's one line can count them before the hours are in.
- **The one line.** The look's line when folded and its chip on the strip say the offers in amber after a trip that is out: *1 out now · 2 look like trips* (`tripsLookWords`, `tripsStripWords`). The card tells the page how many it holds through one callback, so the model stays in the card.
- **What shows it.** On an open trip, under the counted table: chips counted by `tripEvidence` in the new `lib/trips/tripEvidence.ts`, over the trip's own person-days. *N punches at the site* counts the clock rows on the site's jobs, by a person on the trip, on one of their days, whose place is the site. *N On my way and I’m here stamps* counts `travel_started_at` and `arrived_at` on those people's rows on those days, each stamp one (two columns added to `PUNCH_COLUMNS`). *N lines in History* counts the page's History lines on a day anyone was on the trip, the trip's own two lines left out; the page hands the card each line's day. A count above zero on the first two is green, a zero reads plainly in grey. A punch on a person's day off shows nothing for the trip.

## Reads the page gained

When the look shows: one read of the site's `trip_dismissals`, and, only when a punch on the site's jobs is by a login with no roster row, one read of those logins' names.

## Not in this PR

- **Photos** in What shows it. The page's photo census counts by job and by week, not by day, and the photo rows at hand are one page of sixty. A count by day needs the census to keep one.
- **Card charges**, which are dollars and belong to a later PR.
- A trip held by a job alone reads no Not a trip rows yet: the read is by the development.
