# v2.4018 — Day book: its week leaves the address bar with it (2026-09-28)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Found on the live pass of 2026-09-28: after a look at Bids → Day book, every other Bids tab carried `dayb_from` / `dayb_to` in its URL. People → Day book did the same.

## What was wrong

The Day book writes its range, person and view into the URL so a manager can send a link to one person's week (`lib/people/dayBookDoor.ts`). Nothing took them out again. On any other tab they were inert — the door only reads them on its own tab — but a link copied from the Bid Board or from People → Users carried a week, and sometimes a person's id, that it had nothing to do with.

They were also, by accident, how the Day book remembered its place: come back to the tab and the leftover params reopened the same week. Taking them out alone would have lost that.

## What changed

- **`dropDayBookDoorParams(params)`** in `dayBookDoor.ts` (3 tests) — the four params the door writes, removed.
- **Bids `selectBidsTab`** and **People `goToPeopleTab`** drop them when the tab clicked is not the Day book. The Bids router's bounce drops them too, so a Day book link someone may not open lands on the board without its week.
- **`PeopleDayBookTab` takes `memory`** — a ref the host page owns. The view writes its range, person and view there as they change and opens on them when the URL names no week. A link's own params still win; a remembered person is not opened for a viewer who may not pick one. The memory lasts as long as the page does, which is how long the leftover params lasted.

## What a person sees

On Bids and on People, the address bar of every tab but the Day book is free of `dayb_…`. Coming back to the Day book opens the week, person and view it was left on, as before.

## Verified

- Live on a local dev server, Bids and People: open the Day book on 9/14–9/20, click two other tabs (clean URLs), click Day book (9/14–9/20 again).
- `Bids.render.test.tsx` — three new cases (leave, come back, the refused link); `PeopleDayBookTab.render.test.tsx` — three (the remembered week, the viewer who may not pick, the view telling the page where it is).
- eslint; typecheck; `npm test` in full.
