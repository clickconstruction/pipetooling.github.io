# v2.6668 — The Your week sheet's render test pins the Saturday-evening edge (2026-11-08)

A test change, no screen changes. `src/components/techWeek/YourWeekSheet.render.test.tsx` (punch list #255, v2.6606) built its fixture's `work_date` and `finished_ymd` from the UTC day while the sheet reads the company calendar's day (`todayYmdInAppTz`) and its Sunday–Saturday pay week (`payWeekContaining`). From 6 PM to midnight in Chicago the UTC day runs one ahead, and on a Saturday evening that put the test's hours in next Sunday's pay week; it failed the merge queue on PR #7256 at 2026-11-08 00:11 UTC and failed the same on plain `main`. #7268 (v2.6651's types PR) made the fixture read `todayYmdInAppTz()` the same night.

## What changed

- A new case pins the clock to 2026-11-08 00:30 UTC (6:30 PM Saturday in Chicago, the next pay week by the UTC day) with `vi.useFakeTimers({ toFake: ['Date'] })` and holds the clock-out line *Clocked out at 5:31 PM. 8.5 hours this week.* and *Have a good weekend*; the timers are restored after every case. Without it the fixture fix is proved only when CI happens to run in that window.

## Read closer

- The sheet and its kernels (`src/lib/techWeek/`) read the company calendar's day everywhere; a search found no other UTC-day read there, so the bug was the test's alone.

## Verify

The four cases pass at 00:43 UTC, the failing hour, under `TZ=America/Chicago` and `TZ=UTC`.
