# v2.8485 — People → Contracts: a company’s row offers what it says, and its presses fit a thumb (2026-11-24)

Punch list #403, three of the six small faults its walks of 2026-11-24 found on prod, all on a sub’s company’s row on People → Contracts (step 6). Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was there

- Under a company with nothing sent the row read *Send for signature sends it the subcontractor agreement or the W-9.* The window offers every paper the Contract Book keeps for subs (`audience` *sub*), under the Book’s own names. On prod that list held no paper called a subcontractor agreement.
- The send window opened the signer’s address from the roster alone (`workerEmail`). A sub hired with no login has no roster address. The company’s first send keeps the address the office typed, on the company’s own person row (`gc_company_people`), and the next send still opened on an empty box.
- **Send for signature** and **Enter from paper** under the row stood 36 px tall at every width.

## What changed

- **The words** (`COMPANY_PAPER_WORDS.none`, `subCompanyPapers.ts`): *Nothing sent to this company yet. Send for signature offers the Contract Book’s papers for subs.* No paper is named, so the row holds whatever the Book keeps.
- **The address** is `signerEmail` (`subCompanyPapers.ts`, pure): the roster’s, else the one the company keeps for that name, whatever its capitals. `loadCompanyKeptPeople` (`subCompanyPapersIo.ts`) reads the company’s people once when the send window opens. The box shows the worked-out address until the office types in it. What the office types stays, an emptied box included. Picking a worker again reads their address again. A read that fails leaves the box as the roster filled it.
- **The presses** take their height from `companyPressHeight` (`subCompanyPapers.ts`): 44 px at 640 px and under, the desk’s 36 px above it.
- **Help**: *put a sub under their company* says what the paper list holds and where the address comes from.
