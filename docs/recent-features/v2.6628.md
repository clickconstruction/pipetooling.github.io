# v2.6628 — A task card photo taken with no signal waits on the phone (2026-11-07)

Punch list #273's first follow-up, the piece v2.6602 named: *a shot taken with no signal does not wait on the phone yet (the outbox's pattern, v2.6370); the tile says the phone has no signal and the person takes it again*. Now it waits, the way a job photo from the Job photos window does.

## What changed

- **A seventh press kind, `task-photo`** (`lib/offline/outbox.ts`: `OutboxKind`, `OUTBOX_KIND_WORDS` *Task photo*, `TaskPhotoPayload` with one `KeptTaskPhoto`, the `task_photos` row's facts as `sendTaskPhoto` writes them: the kind, the percent, the Done when… line it proves and its words, who, when, the sizes). `KeptShot` is the base every kept photo shares now (`KeptReportPhoto` extends it); `keptPhotoCount` and the bar's *1 photo with it* count the new kind. `parseOutboxPress` and the schema are unchanged.
- **The send** (`keptPhotos.ts` → `sendKeptPhotos`): a task photo press's shot goes to the card's own io, `sendTaskPhoto` with the kept id (`SendKeptTaskPhoto`); the row is an upsert on `id` now, so a resend lands on the same row. `sendTaskPhoto` throws `PhotoSendError` with the failure's class, as the job photo's send does, so the sheet can tell a lost signal from a refusal.
- **The landing** (`landFieldPress.ts` → `landTaskPhoto`): never the RPC. The replay sent the row; landing writes a sent finishing shot onto the tick of the line it proves (`attachPhotoToTick`, the same update twice is the same row); a progress shot, or a finish on no line, lands with nothing more. `RPC_KIND` names the kind for the record.
- **After landing** (`afterLanding.ts`): the bytes go and the card's strips hear `task-photo-added` (`announceTaskPhotos`, the event name moved from `useTaskPhotos` to `lib/taskPhotos.ts` so the kernel can fire it).
- **The sheets** (`useTaskCardSheets.ts`): a send lost to the signal keeps the shrunk bytes under the shot's id (`keptPhotos.keep`), queues the press through `pressThroughOutbox` with `forceQueue` (about: the card's title; door: `/checklist`), and comes back `shotKept`; the percent or the finish is then tried as today. `TaskShot` carries its `id` from the pick (`useTaskShot`), and the host's `kept` flag makes the tile read *Kept on the phone · It goes to the card when the signal is back* with no presses, while a second Set or Done passes no shot. `onSet` and `onDone` resolve `SheetPressOutcome` (`lib/taskSheetPress.ts`: `{ ok, shotKept }`; a bare boolean still reads as `ok`). A refusal that is not the signal, a phone with nowhere to keep the bytes, or an impersonation keeps nothing and shows the reason as before.
- **Not queued, by design:** the percent set and the finish. Both are writes with no per-press id, and the card's 100 takes the completion kernel's path (the notice, the Review queue, the next repeat); with no signal they fail with their reason, the sheet stays open, and the person presses again when the signal is back. The kept photo is not sent twice.
- The guide *add a photo to a task card* gains *With no signal*; *keep working when the signal drops* lists the job photos and the task card's photo among what waits; the Glossary's outbox entry counts six.
