# v2.6223 — Honest and fast, PR 4: Quickfill's deck kept, and the Roller reads the station on screen (2026-10-31)

Punch list #183 ([`to-dos/honest-and-fast/README.md`](../../to-dos/honest-and-fast/README.md)), the fourth of five PRs. During a database freeze Quickfill's Roller drew the station chips over nothing, since the deck waits on the marks and the mark history, and the phone's round drew a blank under the nav; a desk open on the Roller ran every station's page-level read whatever station was on screen (902 reads an open, the slowest 7.9 s). This PR takes the deck through PR 2's `useKeptRead` (v2.6031) so a slow morning draws the round as the device last read it under the stamp *Counts from 7:52 AM · counting again…*, holds Done and Worked it on a kept station, and makes the Roller and the round read only the station on screen. No migration, no edge function.

## What changed

- **The kept deck** [`src/lib/quickfill/keptDeck.ts`](../../src/lib/quickfill/keptDeck.ts) (13 tests): what the Roller and the phone's round keep is one row per station in the round, the view model the deck draws, never the raw tables: the label, the count with where it came from (live this visit, *at last look* from the history, or none), the Next line of a station that counted, the last mark and the last five marks' times (`buildQuickfillKeptRows`, `keptMarkedAtsBySection`). The key is `quickfillKeptKey(userId)` → `u1:quickfill:deck`, one scope. The round's state is **not** kept: `quickfillKeptDeckRows(kept, now)` hands the kept marks back to the round's own kernel (`roundRows`) at the time the deck is drawn, so a station marked at 7:50 reads as due again by evening rather than as fresh all day, and the deck re-sorts. `quickfillKeptCountLine` reads *3 open at 7:52 AM* for a station that counted at the kept read and nothing for the history's count, whose row already says *at last look*. `quickfillDeckLoading` is the hook's `loading`: the marks, the 60-day history, the layout settings and the on-screen station's count all landed, nothing else, since the other stations' counts on the deck are the history's. `quickfillKeptRowIsValid` voids a stored deck off the shape. `quickfillStationReadsOn` is the read diet below.
- **The stamp's words** `lib/keptRead.ts`: `QUICKFILL_KEPT_LEAD` (*Counts from*) and `QUICKFILL_COUNTING_AGAIN_WORDS` (*counting again…*), and `keptReadStampWords` / `KeptReadStamp` take `again`, the page's own word for the read in flight; a failure still reads *could not read again · Retry*.
- **The held press** `lib/quickfill/markOutcome.ts`: `markPresses(count, noun, loading, held)`: held, Done and Worked it keep the words they would have (*Worked it · 3 left*) but wait with the hint *reading again…*; still counting wins the words when both hold. `QuickfillMarkPresses` draws a held press dead (`deadPress.ts`'s pair, `keptPress`, `data-held`, the title *reading again…*) rather than dim; Not sure stays lit.
- **The screen, the list and the Board**: `QuickfillRoundScreen` takes `kept` (the stamp first, `data-kept-read` on the head and the bar so the count line mutes as `keptCount`) and `held`; `QuickfillRoundList` takes `kept` (the stamp over the head, each row's count muted); `QuickfillBoard` takes `kept` (the dialog's counts muted). The station's own body is not under the attribute: its rows are its own live read.
- **The page** [`Quickfill.tsx`](../../src/pages/Quickfill.tsx): the view, the location and the viewport are read at the top of the page, so `stationReadsOn(id)` (the URL's station and the view) can gate the station hooks before they run. On the Roller and the round (`deckKey` is null on the Page and in Focus, where every station draws its own body) the page calls `useKeptRead` with the rows to keep and `quickfillDeckLoading`; while kept rows show, `deckRows` replaces `roundRowsValue` everywhere the Roller and the round read it, `rollerActive` / `roundActive` and the first-station navigation no longer wait for the layout or the history, `stationOnDeck` resolves the URL's station against the kept deck, and `roundCountParts` reads the kept row. Both deck reads now say when they fail (`sectionMarksLoaded` beside `roundEventsLoaded`, `marksReadError` / `eventsReadError` through `recoveryFailureFromError` with the fallback *Could not read the round.*): a kept deck stays up with the stamp's Retry (`deckReloadNonce` re-runs both reads), nothing kept draws `OfflineRetryPanel` (`idempotent`, so a lost signal reads again by itself) where the close sheet goes, and the close sheet itself waits for both reads so a failed history never reads as *Round done ✓*.
- **The read diet** (the card's argument *The fix belongs in the reads*): the six heavy page-level hooks, Missing job info, Property kinds, Percent complete (the jobs cache and their own chunked reads), Messages (its queue), Field photos and Complete no bill, each take `enabled` while `fetchEnabled` stays the role's answer, so a station hidden by role stays hidden and a station off screen on the Roller reads nothing and reports loading. Not gated: Unreachable Prospects, whose count decides whether the station exists on the deck; Unpriced Fixtures and the stale-tally count, one read each; and the dispatch inbox, which feeds two stations and the trip-charge door.
- **Guides**: *run the office day with Quickfill* gains *When the app is slow*; *know what to do when the app is slow* gains *The office's round*.
- **Docs**: `GLOSSARY.md` (The kept read), `QUICKFILL_ARCHITECTURE.md` (the kept deck and the read diet under Views), the to-do's status.
