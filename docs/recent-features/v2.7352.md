# v2.7352 — Banking: a bank line says when it paid a sub, and can be matched from there (2026-11-15)

Punch list #347, the first of the two *Not built* lines this session took. Taunya, 2026-11-15, of the four lines left after hand placement went to the sub labor spike: "Build all four". v2.7239 matched a payment to its bank line from the Subs Ledger. This is the same match from the bank line's side, over the same table and the same two functions.

## What it is

- **A matched line says so.** On Banking → Ledger, under the payee: *paid Calloway Tile · sending 12 · 2 sheets* in green, with **undo**. Undo takes the match off every payment on the line and clears their ticks.
- **An open line is offered.** A bank line nothing has taken gets a picker, **Match to a sub payment…**, when a sub payment fits: the same amount to the cent, within three days either side, the closest day first, a payee that reads like the sub breaking a tie. A lone payment is named by its day, its sub, its job and its amount. A sending is named by its number and how many sheets it paid.
- **A press matches.** Choosing a payment calls `match_sub_payment`, which links every line of the payment's sending and ticks them. Nothing is matched by itself.
- **The same rules on both sides.** A sending is one unit worth what was sent, its lines and the credit kept with them. A bank line a payment took is offered to nobody else. A row that is money in, a duplicate, failed or cancelled is offered nothing, and one that was matched before it became any of those still says what it paid.
- **A transfer between the company's own accounts is offered nothing, on either side.** The walk below found the matcher offering them. `bankLinesFromRows` now leaves out Mercury's `internalTransfer` kind, and the Subs Ledger's bank read asks for `kind` so its side drops them too.

## Code

- `src/lib/subWorkOrders/bankLineSubPayments.ts`, new, pure. `bankSideSheets` turns the read into the ledger's own sheets, so the lines and the units come from `buildLedgerLines` and `matchUnits`. `bankSideRead` gives each bank row on screen what it paid or what it could be matched to. `subPaymentCandidates` is the mirror of `bankCandidates`, and a test holds that a line is offered a payment exactly when the ledger offers that payment the line.
- `src/hooks/useBankLineSubPayments.ts`, new. It reads the payments above zero, their sheets, the people on them, the sending numbers and the credit kept, once when the table opens. The matches and both presses are `useSubPaymentBank`, unchanged.
- `src/components/banking/BankLineSubPayment.tsx`, new: the green line with undo, or the picker.
- `BankingMercuryTable` takes `subPaymentMatch`, and `Banking.tsx` sets it on the Ledger tab's table alone.
- `subPaymentBankMatch.ts` (`BankRow.kind`, `OWN_ACCOUNT_TRANSFER_KIND`) and `useSubPaymentBank.ts` (`kind` in the bank read): the own-account rule, three lines between them.
- No migration, no edge function. No edit to the Subs Ledger's screen, the payment window or the sending code.

## Who can use it

Banking's Ledger tab opens for the controller and the developer, and both can read and tick sub payments. Every read fails soft: a login that cannot read the payments sees the bank as it always was.

- Render tests (7) on the real table: off by default, a matched line and its undo, the picker and its press, both column layouts, a refusal's reason, and that neither press opens the row.
- Walked on the dev build against the live database, read only, 2026-11-15. Banking's Ledger drew its 16,000 rows as before and the sub payments read in the same pass. 103 bank lines were offered a payment at first, every offered amount the line's own: 28 payments out, 48 card charges and 27 transfers between the company's own accounts. With those transfers left out, 76 lines are offered across 53 payments. 35 of the card charges are to a pay-a-person app, which is how some subs are paid, and 13 are store charges that share an amount and a week with a payment. Nothing was matched yet on prod, so no line read *paid*.
- **Not walked: the press.** `match_sub_payment` has not run on prod from either side. A test payment on the Claude Test Sub's ZZ sheet was refused to this session as a prod write, so the press, the green line and undo are proved by the render tests and the migration's own bed alone.

## Not in this PR

A store charge that happens to share an amount and a week with a sub payment is still offered: nothing in a bank row says it is not a payment to a person. The Sorting tab's table, which other roles open, reads no sub payments. A shared bank line (two old payments typed one per sheet that add up to one bank line) can be matched by the database and is shown here once it is, but neither side offers it: the matcher offers an amount that is the line's own.
