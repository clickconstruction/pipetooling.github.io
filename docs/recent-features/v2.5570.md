# v2.5570 — Messages: a tall card above the lines no longer hides the reply box (2026-10-15)

The owner's report of 2026-10-15, on the thread of his own test number after naming it: the **Who has it?** card listed nine teammates as full-height rows, and the reply box sat under the window's edge with no way to scroll to it. `ThreadWindow` draws what a cut puts in the `aboveLines` slot (R7's sort card, cut 2's Who has it) as a plain flex child between the header and the lines' scroller, so a tall card took its full height, the lines shrank to nothing, and the box overflowed the window's `overflow: hidden`. Measured in the preview at a 768 px viewport before the fix: the box's foot at 736 px with its textarea clipped; after: the card's wrapper 302 px tall scrolling 636 px of content, the lines 112 px, the box whole inside the window. No migration, no edge function.

- **`ThreadWindow.tsx`**: the slot's output sits in a wrapper, `data-thread-above`, with `flex: 0 1 auto`, `minHeight: 0` and `overflowY: auto`, drawn only when a cut fills the slot; `data-thread-lines` keeps `minHeight: 7rem`. In the column's flex layout the wrapper is the one child that can shrink, so a card taller than the room scrolls inside itself while the lines keep a few rows and the box at the foot stays put. A short card is unchanged.
- **Tests**: `ThreadWindow.above.render.test.tsx` (new, 2: the wrapper's styles and place before the lines with the box after; no wrapper when no cut draws above).
- **Docs**: `MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md`'s thread-window paragraph.

Left as it was: the card's own rows (`NewLaneCard`'s `Option`), which could be denser; with nine teammates and a 92 vh window the card now scrolls rather than crowds.
