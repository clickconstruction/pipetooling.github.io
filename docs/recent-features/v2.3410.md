# v2.3410 — Lien desk, the run: one packet, one tracking form, every approved notice recorded with its months (2026-09-14)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Second PR of the train in [`to-dos/lien-desk/`](../../to-dos/lien-desk/README.md) (PR 1 v2.3405 the desk). Stacked on `claude/lien-desk-1`.

## Why

The office sends notices in batches, and recording them one job at a time in the Lien window is the step that gets skipped — which is how a sent notice ends up with no `months_covered` and the Dashboard keeps nagging. The plan's refinement: the send is a run, not a row.

## What shipped

- **Kernel [`lienDeskRun.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDeskRun.ts)** (+4 tests): `buildLienDeskRun` turns every *Ready to send* entry into a `RunNotice` — the draft's fields (or the job's, when the draft has none), the months it names, the amount, the reference strip, the cover note when the draft asked for it, and the two statutory recipients (the owner of record from the property record or the job override, the original contractor from the GC customer with its address and email) each with a method (certified mail by default) and a tracking field; `runNoticeProblems` (nobody to send to, no mailing address, email with no address on file); `runCoverSheetBlocks` (a numbered line per envelope with a tracking blank), `runCoverNoteBlocks` (the note as its own short page, signed), `runNoticeBlocks` (the statutory form with *Copy for: Owner of record / Original contractor* on the strip), `runPacketHtml` (one print document with page breaks), `runFilingPayload` (the `job_lien_filings` insert naming every month, both sends).
- **[`lienDeskRunIo.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDeskRunIo.ts)**: `recordLienDeskRun` — per notice, the courtesy emails go through `send-lien-filing-email` (the resend id becomes the tracking), the filing row is inserted, the desk item is marked sent; one failure never stops the others, and the caller gets both lists.
- **[`LienDeskRunModal`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskRunModal.tsx)** (+2 render smokes): a row per recipient (name and address, the method select — email only where an address is on file — the tracking input), the envelope count, **Print the packet**, **Record the run ▸** (blocked while a recipient is marked in red; rows that failed stay listed with the reason).
- **The desk**: **Send the run · N** in the header whenever approved notices are waiting (office roles), and on a ready item's footer beside *Just this one, from the Lien window ›*.
- Guide *send lien notices from the Lien desk* → "Sending" rewritten for the run.
