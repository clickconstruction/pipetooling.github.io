# v2.8018 — Before we start: the GC's page can show what we need, and take their answer (2026-11-19)

Punch list #382, PR 9 of eleven: the GC's page. It is the first thing of this train a GC can see, and it is off on every job until the office turns it on. No migration: the tables of v2.7997 already hold a yes from the page (`source: 'portal'`), the job's switch and the photo bucket. Two edge functions change and are deployed from main: `customer-portal` and `submit-portal-request`.

## What it takes to show

Three things, all on: the job shares its stage dates with its GC (the switch that exists today), the job's own switch **shows** under Before we start, and each bullet's eye. A bullet on them starts shown, and a bullet of ours is never shown whatever its row says.

- The job's switch is a value in place under the section's heading, for the office on a job with a GC: *The GC's page **does not show** what we need from them before each stage.* It is `start_check_settings.shows_on_gc_page` on the job's row.
- With it on and the sharing switch off, the line adds: *It shows nothing until Share stage dates is on, under the GC on the job's form.*
- The eye is a chip on a bullet's ⋯: **Shows on the GC's page** / **Hidden from the GC's page**.

## What the GC sees

One card a stage, `PortalStartChecks`, under *Where the job is*: **Before we start (stage)**, the bar for what we need from them only, then:

- **Still needed from you · N**: each open bullet its own white box with a large tick box that turns green. Nothing is ticked for them. **All of these are ready** ticks them all. One press sends: **Yes, N are ready**.
- **I am adding a photo** goes with a yes. A bullet that needs one says so, and one whose word we already have reads *You told us. A photo would settle it.* and has no box to tick again.
- **Not ready yet? It will be ready (day)** sends a day by itself. It is kept beside the days they gave before.
- **Already settled · N** below a rule, small and grey, with how we know: *you told us Nov 14*, *your photo, Nov 16*, *we saw it on site*.
- After our Go with nothing at all open: **Crew confirmed for (our first day).**

The page never shows a bullet of ours, a name of ours, a note, a trip, a cost, a day they gave before or the days they moved (the owner's call 6, on its default). It stays light when the app is dark.

## What their press writes

`submit-portal-request` takes two new kinds, both in `_shared/startChecksPortal.ts`:

- `start_check_answer`: each ready bullet becomes a `start_check_marks` row, a yes on their word in the customer's name, *on their page*, today. With a photo it is a yes with their photo. A day becomes a `start_check_their_days` row.
- `start_check_photo`: one upload slot in `start-check-photos` under the job's folder. The page shrinks the photo, uploads it straight to the bucket, and names the path in its answer. A photo counts only once its bytes are there.

Every answer is held against what the page shows at that moment (`gcPageAnswerProblem`), so a stale page cannot tick a bullet that is hidden, of ours, taken off or already settled. A link must be the job's GC's, and the job must have both switches on. It writes no line to Dispatch, no decision and no window. The page has no person's name, so the yes carries the customer's.

On the office's side the row reads as any yes does: who, how and the day, each changeable in place. The ask's text adds *Or tap to answer on your page.* once a job's two switches are on.

## How it is built

- `src/lib/startChecks/gcPage.ts`: `gcPageStages`, the words, `parseGcPageAnswer`, `gcPageAnswerProblem` and the photo path's rule. The edge reads it from its copy (`scripts/edge-kernels.mjs`, and `submit-portal-request` joins `IMPORTERS`).
- `supabase/functions/_shared/startChecksPortal.ts`: `loadGcPageJobs`, `gcPagePhotoSign`, `gcPageAnswer`, as the service role.
- `customer-portal` adds `startChecks[]` to its answer, and `portalPayload.ts` parses it.
- Limits a job an hour: 120 yeses, 10 days, 40 photo slots.
