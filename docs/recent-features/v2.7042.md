The two lines are two features on one spot: the pay runway's deadline row (v2.4205) and the Accounts Receivable lien chip hung under it (v2.5491), which was written to stand alone and so carried its own day and count. On the live board 20 of the 28 second lines repeated the row's day and count, and 13 were that exact pair. The card `to-dos/lien-row-says-it-once/README.md` holds the case table, the second pass on every live row and the train. No migration, no edge function.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/jobs/lienArChip.ts`**: `LienArChip` gains `lead` (the words with no last day and no count after them) and `action` (the Do now move the chip stands for, null for paper that is out). The chip's own words are unchanged for a notice. On an affidavit's row three wordings are fixed: *Affidavit to draft* (it read *Affidavit · Notice to draft*), *Approve the affidavit* (it read *Affidavit · Approve the notice*) and *Affidavit · Waiting on Robert* (the name sat behind the row's lead-in and read *the leader*).
- **`src/lib/jobs/billedDatesLedger.ts`**: `LedgerLienState` takes the chip's `lead`, `dueOn`, `paper` and `action`. `stateUnder` decides the line under a lien row: left out when the row asks to file and the desk's move is filing; the `lead` alone when the chip names the row's own day, or is about the row's own paper (the clock gives that paper's day, so a chip that names a month already closed on the same notice no longer puts a second day under the first); the words as written for another paper, such as a retainage notice or service. The asking lien row reads **File the affidavit** (it read *File the lien*), the Lien window's and the desk's word. Under the rule, *Notice first* and *File the lien first* become **Money lands too late · Nd short**, and *Ask for a date* drops the count the *Expected* row already shows.
- **`JobsStagesTab.tsx`**: the renderer hands the chip's parts to the ledger with its words.
- **Tests**: `billedDatesLedger.test.ts` (the line under the row in five states, the new words), `lienArChip.test.ts` (the affidavit's words, `lead` and `action`), the render test's verdict, and the two Bridge tests that build a chip by hand.
- **Guide** *read the Pipeline money view*: the deadline row and the bold line say the new words. Glossary *Lien desk*: the ledger's sentence.
