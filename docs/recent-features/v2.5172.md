# v2.5172 — GC mode: a trade partner sends its files from its portal (2026-10-10)

P5a-1 of the trade partner portal's real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/PORTAL_REAL_BUILD.md`; plan `to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/portal-p5a.md`, both on branch `spike/gc-mode`). It reads P5a-m's `gc_trade_files` and the verbs' file links (`20261010100000`, v2.5170).

- **The kind `file`** (`submit-gc-trade-portal`, `_shared/gcTradeFile.ts`):
  - It takes `for` (`submittal`, `change`, `quote`), its record, a name and the bytes as base64.
  - The type is read from the first bytes: PDF, JPEG, PNG, HEIC or HEIF, else `fileType` (400). The size is 10 MB at most, else `fileTooBig` (400). Each company may send 20 files an hour.
  - The company's claim is checked as its next kind's verb would check it.
  - The job's folder is `gc_projects.drive_folder_url`, else `noJobFolder` (409). A submittal's file goes to **Submittals**, any other to **Team only → From trades → <company>**.
  - The upload uses the service account the intake already uses, so there is no new secret. The name never meets another file's.
  - A `gc_trade_files` row keeps the link. The answer is `{ id, name, url }`.
- **`uploadBytes`** in `_shared/driveUpload.ts`: one multipart upload with no reuse by name. `uploadFromUrl` keeps its reuse and calls it for its upload half, so `drive-intake`, `file-submittal-package` and `gc-drive-access` behave as before.
- **The links**: `ask_change` takes `fileUrl` (P5a-m's `p_file_url`), and the quote takes `file`. Both are https or none, and each is left out when there is none.
- **The pickers** (`GcTradePortalFile.tsx`):
  - They sit on a submittal round, a change it asks for, and the quote form.
  - A file over 10 MB is said before anything is sent.
  - `usePress().runWithFile` sends `file` first, then the kind with its link. A refused upload says why and sends nothing else, so what was typed stays.
  - The press gains `upload`, and `submitTradePortal` now returns the function's `value`.
  - The two "email it to" lines are gone with their helpers and words: the quote's (`replyByEmailWords`) and the change's (`changeFileByEmailWords`).
- **Words**: `errFileType`, `errFileTooBig`, `errNoJobFolder`, `subPickFile`, in English and Spanish. The change and quote pickers use the existing `crFile`, `ownQuoteTitle` and `ownQuoteHelp`.
- **Tests**:
  - `gcTradeFile.test.ts`: the type by first bytes, the cap, the shapes, the names, the folders.
  - `gcTradeSubmit.test.ts`: the kind, the keys, `fileUrl` and the quote's `file`.
  - `GcTradePortal.files.render.test.tsx`: each picker's two posts, a refused upload keeping what was typed, too big said first, the preview.
- **Docs**: `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`, `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`, `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`, `DRIVE_INTAKE_SETUP.md`. Guides: `share-a-trade-partner-its-portal`, `see-what-a-trade-partner-sends-from-its-portal` and `send-a-trades-submittal-to-the-architect`.
- **Deploy** (the lead), checked transitively, six functions:
  - `submit-gc-trade-portal`;
  - `drive-intake`, `file-submittal-package` and `gc-drive-access`, through `_shared/driveUpload.ts`;
  - `gc-trade-email` and `send-contract-for-signature`, through `_shared/gcTradeSubmit.ts`.
