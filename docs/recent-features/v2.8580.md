# v2.8580 — Lien desk: an empty Months card names every way a job gets a month (2026-11-25)

Punch list #415 (`to-dos/lien-ledger-leftovers.md`), a fault a docs pass read on 2026-11-25 in the words v2.8535 shipped. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

On a notice whose job has no month, a reader who cannot set the last day of work read *Approved hours, a sent bill or a last day of work gives the job its first month.* That is three ways. The same file's head names a fourth: the creation month.

## Which was true

The brief said to read first whether the fallback still stands. It does.

- All four lien readers still give a job with no hand day, no approved hours and no dated unpaid bill one month, the month the job was created (`lien_fallback_month`, v2.3747, kept through `20261118230000_lien_closed_days.sql`). The row carries `month_source` *job_created* and the grid's `fromCreation`.
- So the head's comment was right and the words were short by one.
- That also explains the empty card. The notice reader lists a month while its window is open and for seven days after. Past that it keeps a month only when nobody noted the miss and its last day fell on or after 2026-09-14. An older creation month is dropped, and a job that takes one can still show no month.

## What changed

- [`lienMonthGrid.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienMonthGrid.ts): `LIEN_MONTHS_EMPTY.told` now reads *Approved hours, a sent bill or a last day of work gives the job its first month. A job with none of those takes the month it was created. A month whose window closed long ago is not listed here.* The comment over it says why.
- `lienMonthGrid.test.ts` and `LienDeskMonths.render.test.tsx` hold the new words, and the first holds them to the plain-words rules.

## Not changed

- The press shape, *No months on this job yet. Set the last day of work › gives the job its first month.* It lists nothing, so it left nothing out.
- The readers, the fallback and a card with months.
- The guide. It says the card says what gives the job a month, and names no list.

## Seen, and not seen

- **Tests:** the two files above, 16 tests.
- **Not seen live.** A notice whose job has no month is rare, and v2.8535's card had no live case either.
