# v2.8232 — Messages on a phone: the page fits the screen (punch lists #324 and #348, the walk of 2026-11-21) (2026-11-21)

The owner's main session walked Messages in the iOS Simulator's Safari on 2026-11-21, looking only. It saw two things: the list page (`/messages`) was wider than the phone, and in a thread with the keyboard up the window's header left the top of the screen. This PR fixes the first. The second is written up at the end, with what it needs. Client only: no migration and no function.

## The page was 1,014 px wide at 375

Punch list #324 had noted it on 2026-11-12 from a headless browser: *about 1,000 px wide*. It reproduces in any browser at phone width, not in Safari alone.

**The cause.** The page is a grid, and so are a lane's list and a day's rows inside *Went out*. None named its column. Such a grid has one column as wide as its widest child wants to be. A *Went out* row ends in when the line went, and for a mail sent from a mailbox that is the hour, who wrote to whom and from which box: about a hundred letters, held to one line, 660 px. One such row made its day 990 px wide, the day made the lane 990, and the lane made the page 990. Everything else on the page, the top bar, the row of counts, the queues, is a child of that same column, so all of it ran off the right edge.

**The fix**, all in [`src/lib/messages/pageFit.ts`](../../src/lib/messages/pageFit.ts):

- **`ONE_COLUMN`**, `minmax(0, 1fr)`: a column that takes the room it is given. The page, both lanes' lists and a day's rows take it.
- **A *Went out* row wraps.** The name with its words keeps 9 rem and takes what is left; when the row's last line does not fit beside it, that line drops under it, at the right, and may break. A short *9:58 AM · Sam* still sits beside the name at 375 px.
- **A *Waiting on them* row wraps.** Its right side is a chip over three presses, 264 px that cannot give, which left the name almost no room on a phone. The name keeps 11 rem, so on a phone the chip and the presses sit under it.
- **A name with no space in it breaks** inside its column: an email address standing in for a name nobody has typed yet. At 320 px such a name stood out of its row in both lanes and on a follow-up row (`FollowUpSections.tsx`).

No overflow is hidden.

**Read after the change**, on the dev server against live data, reads only:

- **375 × 812**: the page is 375 px wide and no box inside it runs past its parent. The same with the legend open, the search open, the week strip open and on **My email**.
- **320 × 640**: 320 px wide, the same.
- **Desk, 1,400 × 900**: of 144 boxes read, 139 are where they were. The other five are the *Went out* names' columns, which are wider and hold the same words at the same place. At 1,024 px the old page's column was 46 px wider than the room it had; it fits now.
- **iOS Simulator** (iPhone 17, iOS 26.2, Safari): the top bar, the counts and the cards under *New* and *Waiting on them* are inside the screen's edges.

## The thread window's header and the keyboard: not in this PR

Tried, walked once in the Simulator on a ZZ Test person's thread, nothing typed and nothing sent, and taken back out. What was learned:

1. **Following the part of the page that shows works for the header.** With the window's backdrop at `visibleBand`'s top and height on a phone, as `PhoneSheet` does since v2.7188, the header stayed on screen with the keyboard up: the way back, the name and ⋯.
2. **But the field being typed in was then cut off.** On an iPhone 17 the keyboard leaves about 375 px. The header is 149, the flow strip under it 42, the lines keep a floor of `max(7rem, 33%)`, 112 px there, and the reply box with its *Send …* row is about 135 as the Simulator drew it. That is about 438 px in 375, and the window cuts what is over, which is the foot of the box. Today the header leaves and the box stays, which is the better of the two faults.
3. **Dropping the lines' floor while the keyboard is up fits an iPhone 17 in Text.** Measured in a browser at 402 × 375: header 149, strip 42, lines 87, box 97. It does not fit Email, whose box is taller, or a small phone, where the keyboard leaves under 300 px.

So the header staying is more than a small fix. It needs a rule for what gives way when the room is short: the box whole first, then the header's first row, then the header's other rows and the flow strip, and the lines last. And it needs a walk on a simulator and on a small phone, in Text and in Email. The patch that was tried is kept with the session's notes, and the card says the same.
