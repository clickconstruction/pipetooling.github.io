# v2.7097 — Bid photos: a photo taken with no signal waits on the phone (punch list #306, PR 8) (2026-11-12)

The card's PR 8. Until this PR a bid photo taken with no signal failed with **Retry** and was not kept, and a bid's photos opened with no signal showed only the read that failed, with no camera. A site walk is where the signal is worst. No migration and no edge function change.

## What changed

- **A ninth press kind, `bid-photos`** (`lib/offline/outbox.ts`: `OutboxKind`, `OUTBOX_KIND_WORDS` *Bid photos*, `BidPhotosPayload` `{ bidId, photos }` with `KeptBidPhoto`, a kept shot's facts plus the name the person gave it). The kinds whose press carries photos are one list now, `PHOTO_CARRYING_KINDS`, which `keptPhotoCount`, `keptPhotosOf` and `withPhoto` read, so the next kind is one entry and not four edits. `parseOutboxPress` and the schema are unchanged.
- **The send** (`keptPhotos.ts` → `sendKeptPhotos`): a bid photos press's shots go to `sendBidPhoto`, the window's own send, under the kept id. The bucket write is an upsert on the same path and the row is an upsert on `id`, so a send whose answer was lost lands on the same object and the same row.
- **The landing** (`landFieldPress.ts` → `landBidPhotos`, over `landBidPhotoRows` in `bidPhotosIo.ts`): never the RPC. It is the write `addBidPhotos` makes online, the name and `added_at`, only on a row with no `added_at` yet. A second landing changes nothing, so the photo sweep never sees a photo added twice. The result carries `bid_id`.
- **After landing** (`afterLanding.ts`): the kept bytes go. A bid photo writes no thread line. The Bid Board hears `OUTBOX_LANDED_EVENT` and reads its photo counts again (`useBidBoardScope`); an open window for that bid reads again (`landedBidPhotos`).
- **The window** (`useBidPhotosPanel.tsx`, rules in `bidPhotoWindow.ts`):
  - The shot engine's `sendErrorName` is `PhotoSendError`, so a send lost to the signal keeps the shot (*Kept on this phone.*, no Retry). A view-as session, whose queue is off, keeps nothing and fails with Retry as before.
  - A kept shot counts as ready (`shotIsReady`), so the press is live: *Add 1 photo*. A line under the tiles says what the press does by its own name (`keptNextWords`).
  - With any shot kept the press goes through `pressThroughOutbox` with `forceQueue` (`queuedBidPhotos`; about: the bid's label; door: the Bid Board). The queued tiles leave the form and a failed tile stays with its Retry.
  - The window counts the bid's photos still on the phone from the queue itself (`photosOnThisPhone`): *1 photo waits on this phone. It goes to the bid when the signal is back.*
  - A read that failed for want of signal still offers **Take a photo** and **Pick from this phone** under the Retry panel. A refusal does not.
  - A narrow screen is called *this phone* and any other *this device* (`keptPlace`).
- Guides: *add photos to a bid* gains **With no signal**; *keep working when the signal drops* lists bid photos. Glossary: the waiting bar's kinds and the Bid photos entry. `docs/BIDS_SYSTEM.md`: the Links column's photos sentence.

## One difference from the card's brief

The brief left a desk's pick or drop out: *only a phone's camera is kept, as for a job*. The engine keeps any shot whose send is lost to the signal, on any screen, and the Job photos window does the same today. Turning that off for a wide screen would take code to make a tablet on a site walk lose its photos, so a desk's pick is kept too, and the words say *this device* there.

## Is this the best we can do?

No, in three places, and each is named so it is not found later by a person on a site.

- **From the shutter to the press, the photo is only a tile.** The bytes are on the phone from the moment the shot is made smaller, but nothing points at them until the blue button is pressed. A tab the phone ends between the photo and the press loses the photo, and so does closing the window, which lets a new photo go by design, online too. The line under the tile names the press for that reason. The better build keeps a draft of the press with the first kept shot and offers it back when the window next opens. That is a change to the queue every kind shares, and it is not this PR.
- **A waiting photo is sent only while the app is open.** The app has no background sync. A phone that is locked or a tab that is closed sends nothing until the app is opened again with a signal. Then it sends by itself.
- **A send the server refuses waits like a lost signal.** `sendKeptPhotos` cannot tell a refusal from a lost signal for any kind, so a bid deleted while its photo waited is tried again until the next morning, and then the bar turns red with **Fix**. The photo is not lost, and it is slow to say so.

What was weighed and kept: the window says what waits in its own body, since on a phone the window covers the line at the top of the screen. The press keeps its own name, *Add 1 photo*, with no signal: it is the same press, and its line says what happens.

## Walked

A local copy at 375 wide as the dev login, headless Chrome, on the ZZ Test bid. Every write to the database was refused in the browser; the bid photo writes were answered by the walk itself, so nothing reached the bid.

- Network cut in the browser: a photo, *Kept on this phone.*, the press, *1 photo waits on this phone.*; the press and the bytes read back out of the phone's own storage.
- The window closed and opened with no signal: the read failed, **Take a photo** was there, a second photo was kept.
- The tab closed and a new one opened with the writes still failing: *2 waiting to send · 2 photos with them*, both still on the phone.
- The browser saying online while the send fails at the fetch: a third photo was kept the same way.
- The writes let through: three uploads, three rows, three landings, one each, the bar *3 sent ✓*, the bytes gone from the phone, and nothing more in the eight seconds after.

## What only a real phone can prove

- That the phone's own camera hands the photo over with no signal, on iOS Safari and on Android Chrome, and that the installed app does the same.
- That the phone keeps the photo through a lock, a night and a restart. iOS may clear a site's storage after seven days without a visit when the app is not on the home screen. The app sweeps kept bytes after seven days too.
- That a full phone says so. A phone with no room refuses the bytes, and the tile then fails with Retry and is not kept.
- That a real upload on one bar of signal finishes, and that the photo sweep files the photo into Drive after it.
- That the Bid Board is still on the screen to press. The board does not open from nothing with no signal.
