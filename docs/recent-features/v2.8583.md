# v2.8583 — Materials on site on a phone: four small mends to a box's screen (2026-11-25)

Punch list #398, from three sittings in an iPhone simulator on 2026-11-25, in Safari at 402 by 874 points, on the ZZ Test Walk Project's boxes. Four of the brief's part A, the client alone. No migration, no edge function. Two more items of the brief were not built, and why is at the foot.

## 1. A filed photo whose file is gone said nothing

**Seen.** Box ZZ2 holds a photo that was filed and whose file is now in the folder's trash. It drew as an empty dashed frame with no reason.

**The cause.** In `PhotoPress.tsx` the amber reason drew only while a photo was not yet filed, and the frame's own words sat in a `title`, which a phone never shows.

**The mend.** `boxPhotoReasonWords` in `src/lib/materials/boxPhotoSource.ts` is the one rule for the amber line. A shot that still waits says its reason, as before. A frame whose picture did not come says why too, a filed one among them: the sweep's own words when it left some, and the frame's words when it left none. A filed photo whose picture draws has no line, and nothing is said while a picture is still being read.

## 2. The Add a part sheet did not follow the keyboard

**Seen.** With the number pad up, Safari's address pill sat on **Add 1 and another part** and the keyboard's bar covered **Pick a different part**.

**The cause.** `OnSiteSheet.tsx` pinned its scrim to the whole page (`inset: 0`), which an iPhone does not shrink for the keyboard.

**The mend.** On a phone the scrim is the part of the page that shows (`onSiteSheetScrim` in `src/lib/materials/onSiteSheetScrim.ts`, over `visibleBand(useVisualViewportBox())`), as `PhoneSheet`'s backdrop is. The sheet keeps its 90 percent of that band, so its foot and its footer's presses end at the top of the keys. Every sheet of the On site tab draws through this one, so each mends: the quantity sheets, More, Move, Where is it?, the sticker's sheet and the print sheet. At a desk, and where the browser cannot say what shows, the scrim keeps the page box.

## 3. Back from a box landed at the top of the list

**The mend.** `TallyOnSiteTab.tsx` remembers the box a person was in. When the view returns to the list it finds that box's row by the mark the row already carries (`data-box-card`) and brings it to the middle of the screen. A box the list no longer draws, one retired or filtered out, moves nothing. A box opened from the Materials desk still goes back there.

## 4. A box opened scrolled down

**Seen.** Opened from a list that was scrolled, the box's screen opened scrolled down by the same amount.

**The mend.** `OnSiteBoxScreen.tsx` brings its own top into view when it opens with its top above the screen. A box that opens with its top in sight is left where it is. The screen's top clears the status bar where the app is on the Home Screen.

## Not built, and why

- **The Count chip counts an empty box** (the brief's item 2), and **one added part marks the whole box counted** (its call 1, answered *the add counts its own line*). Neither is the client alone. What makes a box due a count is ruled in the database too: `on_site_door_facts` counts the boxes due for the Job Mode card's line from the newest `counted` row of each box, and `on_site_job_summary` hands the office's summary each box's last count the same way. Changing the rule in the client alone would make the On site list say one number and the Job Mode card another. Both went back to the managing session as one small database lane.
- **Rows show through under the pinned Take bar** (item 5): the brief marks it *to weigh*, and nothing is out of reach.
- Call 2, the four tiles and the Take bar, waits on a mock-up. Call 3 was never asked.
