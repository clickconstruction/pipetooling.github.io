# v2.3421 — Pipeline: the *Set stages* door (2026-09-14)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Fifth and last PR of the *Where the Job Is* train (v2.3416 legend · v2.3417 recognition · v2.3418 crew feed · v2.3419 the cell).

## Why

Recognition (v2.3417) reads *Rough In · Top Out · Trim Set*; a job named *Phase A / Phase B* or *Beginning of Job / Final* draws as plain lines. The office needed one door from the row to the place stages are set, without knowing the Bill tab has a selector.

## What shipped

- **Kernel** [`progressPaymentCell.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/progressPaymentCell.ts): `ProgressPaymentView.offerSetStages` — true in `lines` mode with two or more priced lines and the job not paid in full; never on stages, a single line, no bid value, or a paid job (+1 test, one extended).
- **The cell** [`StagesProgressPaymentCell`](../../src/components/jobs/StagesProgressPaymentCell.tsx): `onSetStagesClick`; when the view offers it, a quiet dotted-underline **Set stages** link under the words (`data-set-stages-door`). Both tables and the card list pass the row's `openEdit(job, { fixturesSectionHighlight: true })` — the same door the red *no bid value* box uses, which lands on Bill → ① Line Items with the section lit.
- Guides: *split a job into stages and bill stage by stage* (where the link lives), *bill a customer and get paid* (one sentence under the bar's paragraph).

## Not built (the brief's leftovers, none owner-blocking)

A job-form question on New Job ("Stages? In order · Any time · Not now") and a *Rough / Top Out / Trim* preset from the row — the generator preset already produces recognized stages, so the door plus recognition covers the backlog the brief measured.

## Verified

Kernel + render suites, drift tests, typecheck, lint, theme check.
