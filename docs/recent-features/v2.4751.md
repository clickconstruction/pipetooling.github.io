# v2.4751 — Legal portal: Homestead on one line (2026-10-07)

Client only. The owner (2026-10-06), on a screenshot of the live grid after [v2.4749](v2.4749.md): *I would like no homestead to be smaller text so it fits on one line.* The same screenshot showed *No GC · with the owner · 0 jobs · $0* in the rail under Something due.

## What changed

- `LegalPortalLienGrid`: the homestead line under *Residential* is 10.5 px and never wraps.
- `legalLienGridGcs`: an entry with nothing in view is left off, the *No GC* one included. Before, *No GC* was listed whenever the whole book held such a job, so under Something due it could read *0 jobs · $0*; and a chosen GC was kept at *0 jobs* so the choice could be undone. The owner (2026-10-06, on the live grid): *when there is no GC jobs in the grid, hide this; when there is one I want it to show.*
- `LegalPortalLienGrid`: a choice that left the rail falls back to *All GCs*, so switching to Something due shows every due job instead of *Nothing on the grid for …*.

## Tests

`legalLienGridView.test.ts`: the No GC entry present while its job is due and absent once it is not. `LegalPortalLienGrid.render.test.tsx`: a chosen GC that empties falls back to All GCs.
