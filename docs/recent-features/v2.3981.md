## Why the loader whole, and not the kernel first

The map asks for a pure kernel — fetched rows in, the panel's rows out. The loader does not read and then shape: it reads, shapes, reads what the shaping named (the ledger jobs, their invoices and card charges, the lifetime hours), and shapes again, four times over. Cutting a kernel out of that in one step is a rewrite with nothing under it. So the loader moves first, verbatim, and gets tests that pin the money; the kernel is cut next, between the fetch waves, with those tests to say nothing moved.

## The move

- **`src/lib/people/loadReviewPersonData.ts`** — the body of `loadReviewDataCore`, verbatim, one indent level out. What it took from the component by closure is its input: the person, the period's two ends, the paid-in-full switch (the tab still resolves `onlyPaidJobs ?? reviewOnlyPaidInFull`), the pay config and the two rosters. It returns the rows, the two headline sums, hours, reports, tasks and the contributors by job; it throws when a read fails.
- **`src/lib/people/reviewPersonTypes.ts`** — `ReviewLaborJob`, `ReviewCrewJob`, `ReviewReport`, `ReviewTask`, `ReviewLaborContributor`, which were declared inside the component.
- **`PeopleReviewTab.tsx`** — `loadReviewDataCore` resets the panel, awaits the read, checks its stale-response guard and commits. The guard and the error surface are where they were. 3,337 → 2,584 lines.

## The tests — the loader's first (16, over a mocked client)

The mock applies each query's own filters to one set of tables, so the period read and the lifetime read of a table see different rows.

- **What it asks for** — the person's period and the two lifetime windows; sheets by person id (from the roster, else the pay config) and tasks by user id, none without a user; the ledger for the jobs the person touched, through the paid-only RPC when told to.
- **A hand-built week** — the crew day (hours by the day's split, the office half left out, parts as tally + invoices + billed materials + card, value created at the job's 40%, labor on the job from every crew day and the other sheet, the row's share and the person's share of 18 lifetime hours); the sheet (a job cost with its drive, a billed job earned in full, never a share of revenue); the headline sums; who put labor on each job, the biggest first; hours, reports inside the company-calendar period, tasks with an undated one last; no office job set; paid in full only; an Internal Transfer on the card.
- **A failed read** throws, naming the load; a failed status read alone carries on with nothing read as finished.

One thing the tests wrote down that the map had not: the contributors by job are built from every sheet and crew day in the lookback, company-wide, whoever the panel is for.
