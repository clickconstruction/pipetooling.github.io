# v2.6934 — Bill Customer asks how a bill is paid when the customer has two in kind arrangements (punch list #301) (2026-11-11)

v2.6848 gave Bill Customer one question for a job off the deal: in kind, or with money. The question required exactly one open deal (`billStanding.arrangementIds.length === 1`). A customer with two open deals got no question, so the window opened as an ordinary money window, and a bill sent through Stripe is one the deals' lines pass over. A docs catch-up pass on 2026-11-11 found it.

## What changed

- **The kernel**, `billQuestionState` (`src/lib/inKind/inKindChip.ts`): asked for any job off the deal, one open deal or several. It answers whether the window holds, whether an In kind answer records here and on which deal. With one deal nothing changes. With several, *In kind* holds the window and names no deal, and only *With money* frees it.
- **The question**, `InKindBillQuestion`: with several deals the first choice reads **In kind, on an arrangement**. Picked, it shows a note and the press **Open the arrangements**, a link to the customer's page that closes the window behind the person. That is the door `JobInKindStrip` already uses for two deals.
- **The window**, `SendRecordInvoiceModal`: `inKindQuestion`, `inKindUnanswered` and `answeredInKind` read the kernel. The hold reaches every press through `outsideReady`: Stripe, the physical invoice and the HouseCall Pro record. With several deals an answer no longer moves the channel, since nothing is recorded here.

## The choice

The brief offered two paths: ask which deal in the window, or send the person to the customer's page as the strip does. This is the second. A picker needs each deal's name in a read the window does not make, and a wrong pick puts a bill on the wrong deal's ledger. The link cannot bill wrongly: until the person says *With money*, or puts the job on a deal and comes back to the on-the-deal path, no press is live.

Not walked live: no customer has two open arrangements today.
