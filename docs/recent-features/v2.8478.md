# v2.8478 — Lien: the header’s button opens the desk on the job’s affidavit (2026-11-24)

Punch list #335, *Also seen* (`to-dos/lien-row-and-window-still-part.md`): the Lien window's header button for an affidavit to draft, approve or review opened the Lien desk's Affidavits list, not the job's row in it. The note also said the Accounts Receivable chip's door landed the same way, and that the desk had no read of `&job=` for an affidavit. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was found

Reproduced on made-up rows before the change (`LienDeskPage.affidavitDoors.render.test.tsx`), with two jobs on the Affidavits list and the second job's Lien window open.

- **The desk's read is already on `main`.** `LienDesk.tsx` hands a job named on the Affidavits list to that list's selection (v2.7180), and `lib/lien/lienDeskAim.ts` (v2.7744) reads `tab=affidavits&job=` as an aim: the job's row opens, or a line says the job is not on the list and offers where it is. The card's note predates both.
- **The Accounts Receivable chip's door is already right.** `lienArChipHref` (`lienArChip.ts`) writes `/lien-desk?tab=affidavits&job=<id>` for an affidavit, and the smoke that opens the page on that address lands on the job. Nothing changed there.
- **The header's button was the fault, in two places.** Both openers of the window sent the job for a notice only: `navigate(lienDeskHref({ kind, job: kind === 'notice' ? jobId : null }))` in `JobsStagesTab.tsx` (the Pipeline) and `LienDeskPage.tsx`. An affidavit's and a retainage notice's button reached the desk with no job, and the list opened on its own first row.
- **A second fault under it, on the Lien desk page only.** With the job in the address the smoke still opened the first row. The desk re-aims when the job a door names changes (`initialJobId` and `aimKey`). A window opened from a job on Monthly notices sends the same job to another list, so nothing re-aimed.

## What changed

- `src/lib/lien/lienWindowDeskDoor.ts`: `lienWindowDeskDoorHref(jobId, kind)` is the desk's address for one job's paper, with the job on every list. Both openers call it.
- `lienDeskReaimKey(kind, jobId, aimKey)` in `lienDeskAim.ts` is what tells the desk a door named something new: the job, the paper list it is named on, and the press count. `LienDesk.tsx` keys its re-aim on it. The list counts only for a job on a paper list, so the desk's own view presses, which carry no job off Monthly notices, re-aim nothing.
- Not changed: the desk's lists, the note for a job off its list, the chip's door, any paper.
