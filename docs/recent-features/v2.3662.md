# v2.3662 — Lien desk: the draft footer says the one next step, with one primary (2026-09-21)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Fourth of the pane clean-ups (v2.3657 gates · v2.3658 owner envelope · v2.3661 months and history).

## Why

The owner, on the bottom bar: *"I also don't like this bottom section… take a look at it, make some suggestions, make a mock-up, ask ourselves is this the best we can do, and then build and commit it."* The review in the session:

1. Three unrelated things in one band — who gets mail, the cover-note option, a status sentence — then buttons; nothing said which line mattered.
2. The status sentence repeated the screen: the job's name is the pane title, and *Blocked until the owner…* is gate 1.
3. Two filled buttons of equal weight competed (*The leader said to send it*, *Approve*), and when blocked both simply went dim with no reason beside them.
4. **Skip these months…** — the one irreversible action on the desk — was first in reading order, styled like *Save draft*.

*Is this the best we can do?* changed the mock-up in three ways: a disabled primary teaches nothing, so blocked, the primary becomes the way to the blocking gate; Skip stays one click away and always visible, but as a sentence that states its cost; and the bar stays role-aware — the office's primary is *Send for approval*, the leader's is *Approve*, and *The leader said to send it…* is a plain alternative, never a second fill.

## What shipped

[`LienDeskModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskModal.tsx), the draft states only (`needs_owner`, `to_draft`, a `missed` entry with months still due) — the awaiting / ready / held / sent footers were already one sentence and their buttons:

- **Envelope line** (`data-lien-desk-send-line`): *✉ Certified mail to **owner** and **GC*** · *Courtesy PDF to …* · **Include the cover note** — routine paper, not a claim of default.
- **Next step** (`data-lien-desk-next`, `data-blocked`): a headline — *Fix gate N · <label> — then this can go* from the first `blocker` in `buildLienDeskGates`, or *Next: the leader approves it* / *straight into the run* / *it parks under the hold rule* / *you can approve this now* — over the why (the months, then the existing routing sentence; for a leader, *Approving puts it in the run — <ask reason>*). One primary: **Go to gate N ▴** (scrolls the pane to the gates) when blocked, else **Send for approval ▸** / **Put it in the run ▸** / **Approve ▸**. *Save draft* and *The leader said to send it…* are plain; the latter is hidden while blocked.
- **Skip** — *Not sending for July 2026? **Skip this month and give up the lien right…** It stays on the record under Earlier months.* Opens the same reason box; office roles only, and only with a month ticked.
- `.lienFoot*` in [`index.css`](../../src/index.css).
