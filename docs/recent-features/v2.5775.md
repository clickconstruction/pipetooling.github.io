# v2.5775 — Quickfill: Fill them in takes the vehicles missing a record (2026-10-23)

Punch list #145, PR 3 of 5. The second kind on the Fill them in deck (v2.5774): the rows behind *N vehicles are missing insurance, registration or service*, which until now was a door to People → Vehicles.

- **The kind** ([`fillInVehicles.ts`](../../src/lib/needsYou/fillInVehicles.ts), [its test](../../src/lib/needsYou/fillInVehicles.test.ts)): one row per vehicle from `vehicleRecordGaps` (the line's own kernel), most missing first, titled *2017 Ram 2500 · Luis drives it · no service on file*; what the app knows (who drives it, on a plan with no cost or off a plan, which records it does have); a decision with a block per missing part; `vehicleDecisionReady` (a cost above zero in any unit through `weeklyInsuranceCostFromInput`, a plan when off one, a service type and day, miles that parse); `vehicleWrites`, the Vehicles tab's own rows: the weekly costs on `vehicles`, a `vehicle_insurance_periods` row when the truck was off a plan, a `vehicle_service_events` row and, with miles, the `vehicle_odometer_entries` reading the tab also writes; `vehicleTrailWords` (*insurance $51.92/wk on Progressive fleet · registration $1.54/wk · oil change Oct 9 · People → Vehicles has it*).
- **The fields** ([`VehicleFields.tsx`](../../src/components/needsYou/VehicleFields.tsx)): the insurance block (the plan and *On the plan since* only when off a plan; the cost with a / wk · / mo · / yr picker), the registration block (the cost with the same picker, yearly by default, since the state bills by the year), the service block (what was done from `SERVICE_TYPE_LABELS`, the date, the odometer, a note). A vehicle off a plan with no plan to pick is told to make one on People → Vehicles first.
- **The io** ([`fillInVehiclesIo.ts`](../../src/lib/needsYou/fillInVehiclesIo.ts)): the gaps from `loadVehicleRecordGaps`, the plans from `vehicle_insurance_plans`; `save` returns the ids it made and the costs it set, `undo` deletes those rows and zeroes those costs.
- **The shell**: `FILL_IN_KINDS_BUILT` gains `vehicle`, so the vehicles line's button reads **Fill them in** and opens the deck on the trucks; [`useFillInRows`](../../src/hooks/useFillInRows.ts) reads both kinds in parallel under their lines' gates, each failing soft on its own, and a restore puts a row back in its kind's order (`compareLostBidRows`, `compareVehicleRows`); the deck's end counts *N trucks have a record*; a save or an Undo re-reads the vehicles line through `useVehicleRecordGapsNudge`'s `reload`.
- **Tests**: the kernel; [`NeedsYouFillInDeck.vehicles.render.test.tsx`](../../src/components/needsYou/NeedsYouFillInDeck.vehicles.render.test.tsx) (only the missing blocks draw, Save waits for the plan and both costs, the save carries the tab's writes, the next row's block is its own, a service row is ready as it opens, bad miles hold it, Undo hands the ids back).
- **Guide**: *work the Needs you list on the dashboard* § Fill them in names the vehicles.
- **Docs**: `QUICKFILL_ARCHITECTURE.md`, the to-do's status.

## Is this the best we can do?

- Nothing is asked that the truck already has: a service-only row shows one block.
- The writes are the tab's, row for row, so the fleet total, the pay stubs and Review read the same numbers whether the record came from the deck or the tab.
- Not pressed live: the three trucks on prod's line are real, and a made-up service record is not a test. The save path ran through the injected io; the deck was looked at live on the real rows as the dev.
