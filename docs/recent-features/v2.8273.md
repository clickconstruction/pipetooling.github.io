# v2.8273 — Sub papers: confirming a work statement is built, and stays switched off (2026-11-21)

Punch list #350, the card's PR 7, built on the owner's word of 2026-11-21 (*Please do*). No migration: `sub_paper_acks` has held `kind = 'confirmed'` and its `revision`, `amount` and `said_from` columns since `20261117040000_sub_papers.sql`. One edge function changes, `sub-portal`.

**Nothing a sub or the office sees changes.** `SUB_PAPER_CONFIRM_LIVE` in `supabase/functions/_shared/subPapers.ts` is `false`. The client and `sub-portal` both read that one line, so turning it on is a merge, a client deploy and a redeploy of `sub-portal`.

## Why it is off

What a confirm means is counsel's: *By confirming, [sub] agrees that the statement is its bill to [company] for this work* is sentence 2 of the card's six, drafted as a placeholder and printed marked §. No word from counsel is in hand. The press shows that sentence as the paper prints it, marked while `SUB_PAPER_COUNSEL_PENDING` is true, and no other word on the page says what a confirm means. The owner's call 3 picked *a press, and never a condition of being paid*, and the panel says so: *You are paid the same whether or not you confirm.*

## What a sub can confirm

A work statement **the office made, as it stands**: the sheet has a live paper in the register, and today's figures carry that paper's fingerprint (`asIssued`, the rule the page already uses for *Changed since the office's copy*). The page never makes or numbers a paper, so a sheet with no copy, or one that changed since its copy, offers no press and is refused by the function with words that say which.

## The record

`planPortalAnswer` (`_shared/subPortalPapers.ts`) takes a third action, `confirm`, with the sheet's id as `key`. While the switch is off it answers `400 Unknown action.`, as for any action it does not know. On, it plans one row of `sub_paper_acks`: `kind = 'confirmed'`, the person, the sheet, the live paper's id, the revision the sub saw and what the statement comes to. `sub-portal` adds the name and the address the press came from, as it does for the other two answers, and replies `{ ok, already, confirmed: { <sheet id>: { revision, amount, at, from } } }`. A statement is confirmed once per revision: a second press writes nothing and reads the first back. The office looking at a sub's page is refused, as for the other answers.

## The rule

`portalConfirms(acks)` reads a sheet's confirm by one rule for both sides: the newest `confirmed` row that names its sheet and a revision. `portalPaperSheets` puts it on the sheet for the sub's page, and the office's papers window reads the same rows (`SUB_PAPER_ACK_COLUMNS_FOR_PAPERS`) onto the sheet's register row. `buildWorkStatement` already printed the box: *<sub> confirmed revision 2 of this statement, at $4,100.00, on Nov 14, 2026, from <address>.* and, when the revision or the figure has moved, *It has changed since, and this revision has not been confirmed.* The confirm is not one of the paper's figures, so it does not move the fingerprint. Reading is not behind the switch: a confirm on record prints whatever the switch says.

## The press

`SubPortalStatementsView` draws **Confirm** on a confirmable row when `confirmLive` (the switch by default) is on. One tap opens one question: the statement's number and what it comes to, counsel's sentence, the line about pay, **Yes, I confirm it** and **Not yet**. Afterwards the row wears *Confirmed <date>*. A row confirmed at an earlier revision or figure reads *You confirmed an earlier copy on <date>. This one has changed since.* and offers the press again. The strings are in English and Spanish (`subPortalI18n.ts`). Counsel's sentence shows in English, as the paper prints it.

## Deploy

Redeploy `sub-portal` after the merge. With the switch off it behaves as before: it reads three more columns of `sub_paper_acks` and refuses `confirm`.

## Before it is turned on

Counsel's words for sentence 2, in English and in Spanish if the page is to say it in both. A unique index on a confirm per paper and revision, if two presses at one moment must not both be kept: today the second is a second row and the reader takes the newest. A walk on the test sub's page.
