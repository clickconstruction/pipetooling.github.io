# v2.5055 — GC projects: answer a trade partner’s ask for a change (2026-10-09)

Owner Billing's O3b in the GC mode real build: the office's answer to a change a trade asked for from its portal (the Portal's P4, `to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/portal-p4.md` decision 10, and the spike mockup `owner-billing-o3b.md` on branch `spike/gc-mode`, its SQL as built). Guides: [`answer-a-trade-partners-change-request`](../../src/content/help/answer-a-trade-partners-change-request.md), and [`change-our-contract-with-the-customer`](../../src/content/help/change-our-contract-with-the-customer.md) points to it. Migration [`20261010014000_gc_change_request_answers`](../migrations/20261010014000_gc_change_request_answers.md).

- **The two functions**, both `SECURITY INVOKER` and refusing in words before they read the ask (a training account, a digital twin, anyone but the money team, then anyone but a dev while P4a's table is dev only; the lead's calls 3 and 4):
  - `gc_draft_change_order_from_request` drafts through O3's `gc_draft_change_order` on the ask's own trade and reason, at the words, cost, price and days the office confirmed, and links the ask to it;
  - `gc_turn_down_change_request` needs the reason the company reads.
  An ask is answered once. Deleting its draft frees it (P4a's `ON DELETE SET NULL`); a change order that went keeps its link.
- **One mapper** (the lead's call 2, Helper 13's nod): `changeRequestFromRow` is exported from `tradePortalState.ts` and reads the company from the row, so the portal's slice and the office's table rows map through one function. `withChangeRequests` lays the asks over the board beside `withChangeOrders`.
- **The window** (`GcChangeOrders.tsx`): the open asks above the change orders with *N asked by the trades*, each the prototype's `ChangeRequestRow` (**Make a change order**, prefilled with the ask's words, its amount as our cost and its days, the price at `changeOrderPrice` until typed over; **Turn down** with *Why, for <company>*). A change order made of an ask says *Asked for by <company> on <date>: <amount>.*, and its draft's delete says the ask goes back on the list.
- **The trade's emails** (P4b-iii's `changeAskEmail`, kind `changeAsk`, through `gc-trade-email`, once a stage by `<request id>:<stage>`; the lead's call 1): `down` goes with Turn it down, `sent` with Send for signature, `no` with They declined. **Tell <company>** shows on the change order while its `sent` or `no` has not gone, which covers a decline pressed in the customer's portal and an email that did not go. `/gc` reads the asks and the keys that went (`gc_trade_messages`) beside the change orders.
- **Tests:**
  - `changeOrderRows.test.ts`: one request from the table's row and the slice's, another company's row, the answers, the overlay and the open asks;
  - `GcChangeOrders.render.test.tsx`: the ask's row, Make a change order at the price shown or typed, Turn down's reason, who asked, Tell only while its email has not gone;
  - `80_change_requests.sql` in the Owner Billing SQL bed (38 checks, first run on PGlite over main's GC chain).
