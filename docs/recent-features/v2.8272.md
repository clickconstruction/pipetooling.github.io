# v2.8272 — In kind: the Pipeline's map, a development's Money and the aging chart leave a bill an arrangement pays out (punch list #301, the screens traced, PR 2) (2026-11-21)

PR 1 (v2.8266) built the two screens on the customer. This PR builds the last three the trace of 2026-11-21 found: the Pipeline's map, a development's Money look, and the aging chart with the printed Billed awaiting payment report. Each ran its own clock over a bill an in kind deal pays, so each needed its own small design. No screen traced is left.

## What changed

- **The Pipeline's map** (`src/lib/jobs/jobsMap.ts`, `jobsMapRail.ts`). `jobsMapJobs` marks a job on a deal `inKind`. Its `owedDollars` is 0, its `billedAgeDays` is null and what is open sits in `inKindDollars`. The pin stays where it is, in its bucket's count. `jobsMapPinAsks` leaves it off the ask list, the status line reads *Billed · In kind* and the pin's money line reads *$1,700 scheduled in kind*. The rail counts those jobs in `inKind` and says one line under the list (`jobsMapInKindLine`): *$13,858 on 8 jobs is scheduled in kind, not money to collect*.
- **The rewound map** (`jobsMapAsOf.ts`, `loadJobsMapHistory.ts`). The history read selects `in_kind_arrangement_id` and the deal's `created_at`, as `in_kind_since_ymd`. `jobsMapJobsAsOf` reads a job as in kind on that day and every day after. Before it the bill was money, and it ages as it did.
- **A development's Money look** (`src/lib/developments/siteMoney.ts`). `fetchDevelopmentPage` selects the column. `datesFor` gives a job on a deal no expected day and no due day (`MoneyDates.inKind`), so `moneyPastDays` is 0: no *Ask for a date*, no place in a section's or the band's *past due*. The Dates cell reads *Scheduled in kind*. The lien runway stays. The band counts those bills in `inKind` and says one line (`moneyInKindLine`).
- **The aging chart** (`billedAgingChart.ts`). A row a deal pays is not plotted and is in none of the four figures. `stats.inKind` counts it and the chart's foot says *… is scheduled in kind, not plotted*.
- **The printed report** (`jobsDocuments/billedAwaitingPaymentReport.ts`). The rows go through `splitInKind` first. A deal's bill is in no customer's list, no subtotal and not in the grand total, and one line under the total says *… is scheduled in kind, not money due*, the words the emailed report has used since v2.8219.

## Decisions taken with the build

- **The pin stays.** The job is real and it is where it is. What the map got wrong was its money and its place on a list of people to ask.
- **A ready-to-bill job on a deal is set apart too.** A job is on a deal or off it, whole. Its draft bill will be scheduled the day it goes, so it is not money to collect today either.
- **The deal's own day, for the rewound map.** The ledger keeps which deal pays a job, not the day the job went on it. The day the deal was recorded is the nearest true day. A job added to an old deal later reads as in kind a little early when the map is rewound.
- **A job on a deal whose row cannot be read is in kind from the first day of history**, since the column alone is the truth today.
- **The lien runway stays on the Money look.** v2.6834 decided that a credit does not move a deadline.
- **Jobs on the map, bills on the others.** The rail's line counts jobs, since a pin is a job. The chart, the print and the Money look count bills, with the helper's own words.
