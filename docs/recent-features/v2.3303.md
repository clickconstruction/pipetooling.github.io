## Why

The tech already says it at clock-out: "100 · Hydrostatic test passed." on a job named "Johnson Pretest". Everything the office typed into plumbingtooling.com was already on the job or in that sentence. The trigger turns the sentence into the draft; the office's part shrinks to a glance and Send.

## What shipped

- Trigger `reports_draft_test_report` → `draft_test_report_from_field_report()` — the SQL twin of the kernel's `inferTestReportDraft` (same regexes, tested from `src/lib/jobs/testReport.test.ts`): type from the job name, verdict from the wording (never a FAIL from "no leaks"), one open draft per job and type, `source_report_id` links the draft to the field report, `created_by` is the tech, the date is the report's civil date. Only Status Report and Job Complete templates count; Notes, EOD and Walk reports never draft. Errors are warnings — a field report cannot fail because of this.
- The Needs You line from PR 3 now has something to count without anyone opening the modal; the card opens the first draft, prefilled.
