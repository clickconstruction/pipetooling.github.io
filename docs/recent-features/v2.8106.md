# v2.8106 — Sub papers: a second statement the same day keeps a period that reads forward (2026-11-20)

Client only: no migration, no edge function. Punch list #350, found in the audit of the card and fixed on the owner's word (*Yes, fix it*).

## Before

*Since the last statement* starts the day after the last statement ended, and ends today. A statement of account made to date, or for a period that closes today, ended today. Print, Download and Send each make a statement. So Download to check a paper, close the window and open it again to Send, and the window opened on a period that started tomorrow: the paper's head and the email read *Nov 21, 2026 to Nov 20, 2026*, every sheet stood under *Brought forward*, and *How it adds up* began *Open on Nov 21*. The figures were right. The period was not a period.

Walked on the dev server on the test sub, with one statement of today fed on the register's read: the window's line read *Nov 21, 2026 to Nov 20, 2026. The last statement, SA-0001, ended Nov 20, 2026.*

## What changed

- `subPaperRegister` (`src/lib/subWorkOrders/subPaperRegister.ts`) takes today. A statement that ended today is set apart as `statementToday`, and `lastStatement` is the last one that ended before today. The day is not over, so nothing can start after it yet. Tomorrow the same statement is the last statement, as before.
- The window (`SubPapersWindowConnected.tsx`) hands today in. The second paper of a day opens on the days the first one covered, and a payment typed between the two is on it.
- The line under the period says which: *SA-0004 was made today. The statement before it, SA-0003, ended Nov 10, 2026.* When today's was their first: *SA-0001 was made today and is their first, so this one runs to date as well.*
- The view holds one more guard for any caller: a period never starts after it ends. A last statement that ended today or later leaves the statement to date.

## What it does not change

A payment typed on a later day, and dated on or before the last statement's closing day, is still in no statement's *Last payments*: the next period starts after its date. Its amount is in the next statement's opening figure. That is the other half of the audit's finding and waits on the owner's word about what a period is cut by.

## Tests

- `subPaperRegister.test.ts` (+1): two statements that ended today are set apart, the last statement is the one from before, the next day they are the last statement, and a first statement made today leaves none.
- `SubPapersWindow.render.test.tsx` (+3): opened again the day a statement was made, the paper's period starts after the statement before it and never tomorrow; a first statement made today leaves the next one to date and says so; the view alone, handed a last statement that ended today, draws the statement to date.

## Docs

The guide `make-a-statement-of-account-for-a-sub` says a statement made today does not move the period.
