# v2.7714 — Lien desk: the Board marks the bins with something to do now, and folds the empty ones (2026-11-17)

Punch list #383, option B, PR 3 of 3: the quiet parts of the drawing the owner picked. PR 1 (v2.7708) put every press on its bin's title line and PR 2 (v2.7712) gave signing its own bin. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## Before

- The busiest bin had a blue border. It marked the most papers, not the most urgent, and it stood beside blue presses that mean *act here*.
- An empty bin was as tall as a bin with one row of chips, and said *none*.
- **Held** and **Missed** stood beside a column's flow at 0.55 of its width, so every bin of that column was a third narrower while one job was held.
- The affidavits' *To send for Master approval* bin had no press. The batch send stood on By paper alone.

## What changed

- **A blue left edge on a bin with something to do now** (`data-act`): its own press is live and is the viewer's act. The office's reminder is `quiet`, a nudge to someone else, so its bin has none. A dead press has none.
- **No bin is lit as the busiest.** `LienBoardBin.lit`, `LienBoard.lit` and `lienBoardLitBin` are gone from `lienBoard.ts`.
- **An empty bin folds to one line** (`data-folded`): its name and a zero, dashed, with no row under them. A bin with a press never folds.
- **Held and Missed stand on the heading line** (`data-lien-board-heading`, `Bin`'s `inline`): the name, the count and the chips in one row at the column's right, each still a press that narrows the list. The flow has the column's full width.
- ***Send N for approval ›*** on the affidavits' *To send for Master approval* (`lienBoardSendDoor`, counted from the desk's send plan, the papers someone has read): the door to the affidavits list at that pile, where the batch send stands with what it holds.
