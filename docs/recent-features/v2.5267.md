# v2.5267 — Messages: a place to keep the flows and what the office does on them (2026-10-12)

Messages wave 1, cluster 4, cut 4's SQL, as a draft for the lead. Migration `20261012120000_messages_flows.sql`, after the spine's `20261012010000_messages_spine.sql` (helper 1, v2.5258). Not pushed to the database: the lead pushes it after merge (`supabase db push`). No screen, no edge function.

- **Tables**: `flows`, `flow_steps` (every `FlowStep` field as a column: `phase`, `loop_ends`, `skip_one_job`, `later_en`, `later_es`, the nudge columns, `owed_words`), `flow_step_history`, `flow_removed_steps`, `flow_fact_wishes`, `contact_step_skips`, `nudge_holds`, `nudge_sent`. A step's team note is a line of the thread: `messages.step_id`, and a signed-in user may insert a team line (a note or an event) as themselves and no other line, by the same policy cut 2 writes. A person's rows name their job the spine's way (`bid_id`, `project_id`, `jobs_ledger_id`, at most one). State is never stored.
- **The code lists in SQL**: `flow_fact_ids()` and `flow_paper_ids()` hold a step's facts and papers to `facts.ts`; `src/lib/flows/flowsSql.test.ts` fails CI when the migration and the code disagree (the lists, the CHECKs' words, the seed rows).
- **The seed**: `flows_seed()` writes `SEED_FLOWS` for a kind with no flow yet, so a second run adds nothing and a removed step stays removed.
- **The gaps**: `gc_trade_portal_links.first_opened_at`, `gc_invites.lost_on`, `gc_projects.won_on`, `gc_owner_pay_apps.paid_on`, nullable, written by nothing yet.
- **Access**: the office team (`gc_office_team()`), one policy a table, the three fences; who and when are the table's (defaults and a trigger for undo, let go and put back). `contacts.off_*` become updatable by the team.
- **A fold keeps a person's place**: `contacts_fold_flows(p_from, p_into)`, which the spine's `contacts_fold` runs before the folded contact goes, moves their notes, skips, holds and sends onto the contact that stays (a skip or a hold both had keeps the survivor's). `contacts_fold` itself is never redefined.
- **`quote_declined`** (v2.5279) is in `flow_fact_ids()`, and the seed's trade ask is `quote_in` or `quote_declined`.
- **The presses** (`SECURITY INVOKER`): `flow_add_note`, `flow_skip_step`, `flow_unskip_step`, `nudge_hold_until`, `nudge_let_go`, `nudge_record_sent`, `contact_set_off`, `contact_put_back_on`, the job through the spine's `contact_job_check`.
- **`flow_rows(contact)`** (`SECURITY DEFINER`, the office team only): what `load.ts` reads for one person, days and marks only, with a trade partner's page as the office's preview link (`/t/<token>?preview=1`).
- **Bed**: `messages-flows` (`scripts/pgtest-messages-flows.sh`), in `scripts/sql-beds.txt`.
