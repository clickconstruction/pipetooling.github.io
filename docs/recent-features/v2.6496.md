# v2.6496 — Assign work finds a job by its number again on the phone (2026-11-04)

Fix, no migration, no edge function. Taunya, 2026-11-04 at 6:39 AM, with a phone screenshot: on clicktooling.com she opened the *Add job to schedule* sheet, typed `1007`, and the list read *No jobs match.* "Searching by job numbers isn't populating anything in the search menu. It used to work easily on my phone while driving and adding jobs."

## Cause

The sheet is `ScheduleDispatchAssignJobPickerModal`, drawn by seven callers. Hers is the Quick Assign sheet (`src/components/dispatchMode/QuickAssignSheet.tsx`, Dispatch mode's **Assign work**), the one caller that passes no subtitle, as in her screenshot. Its search box filtered the ledger rows in a memo of its own, written in v2.1519, that matched `hcp_number`, the name, the address and the customer, and never `click_number`. A job made in the app carries a Click number and no HCP number (the HCP numbers came with the HousecallPro import), so every newer job read *No jobs match.* when its number was typed in the search box — which is why it "used to work": the jobs she added last year all had HCP numbers. The `#` box beside the search goes through `findJobsByNumber`, which has matched both numbers since v2.1375, so the number mode worked all along. The other six callers read the effective number or the identity line (`filterHubJobPickerRows` for the hub, `subLaborAssignPickerRows` for the three Jobs pickers, the `scheduleBlockTitle` match in Quickfill and on the Person desk), so the Schedule hub's own Add job to schedule found a Click-only job by its `J1007 · …` line.

Ruled out: the ledger read is paged (`fetchAllRows`, v2.5571) and holds every job that is not billing-only, and the number mode passes its digits through unchanged.

## Fix

- **One predicate for the search box**, `jobPickerSearchMatches` in `src/lib/scheduleDispatch/hubJobPicker.ts`: the HCP number, the Click number, the identity line, the name, the address, the customer. `filterHubJobPickerRows` (the hub) reads it too, so the hub matches a Click number itself and not only through its identity line.
- **The Quick Assign sheet's rows come from the kernel**: `filterQuickAssignPickerJobs(jobs, search, numberQuery, limit = 60)` keeps what the sheet's memo had — the number box's exact-then-prefix order, the search's newest-first order, the cap of 60, finished jobs last — and the sheet only maps the rows to the picker.

## Tests

The number box's order, the cap and finished-last are pinned too.
- `QuickAssignSheet.render.test.tsx` (new, 3): the sheet with a Click-only job and an HCP job on the ledger; `1007` and `473` in the search box each list their job and `5555` reads *No jobs match.*; the `#` box lists both kinds.
