# v2.6817 — Every supply house bill's lines will be rows: the tables, the check and the wider reader (2026-11-09)

Punch list #298 PR 1 (`to-dos/supply-bill-lines/README.md`). A bill was recorded with its total, its job and a link to its PDF; the lines on the paper were rows nowhere, so nobody could ask what the company buys, how often or at what price. This PR lays the ground and shows nothing on a screen.

- **Migration `20261109170000_supply_paper_lines.sql`** (`docs/migrations/20261109170000_supply_paper_lines.md`): `supply_papers` (one row per paper read: the house, the kind, the number, date and order number, the file and pages, the bill's row when it is one, the printed money, the reader and the check state `adds_up` · `off` · `accepted`), `supply_paper_lines` (one row per printed line: the house's part code, the manufacturer's number, the description as printed, the unit, the three quantities, the unit price to four places with its pricing unit, the extension in cents, the kind of line `part` · `charge` · `component`, and the parts-book part once matched) and the view `supply_purchase_lines`, the one definition of a purchase: invoices and credits whose check passed, components left out, a credit signed negative. Office roles read, the service role writes, the four fences close it.
- **The check kernel** `src/lib/materials/lines/paperChecks.ts`: `checkPaper` holds a paper to five sums (line arithmetic with the pricing-unit divisor, lines to subtotal, subtotal with tax and freight to total, total to the bill's row, every house code found in the PDF's text layer) and answers the state, the problems with their words and the row's one line; `paperFromBillRead` turns the reader's answer into the paper it checks. Tests in `paperChecks.test.ts`, including the made-up bill set.
- **The reader widened** (`supabase/functions/_shared/billRead.ts`): `BILL_SCHEMA` asks for `line_kind`, `house_code`, `mfr_number`, `unit`, `qty_ordered` / `qty_shipped` / `qty_backordered` and `pricing_unit` on every line, `order_number` and `freight` on the paper, and reads `ship_ticket`, `acknowledgement`, `quote` and `bid` as kinds of their own; `readBillAnswer` keeps the price whole in `unitPrice` beside `unitPriceCents`, and an older answer still reads. The Add invoice window is unchanged (`billMatch` treats every kind but a credit as an invoice).

Next: PR 2 reads the bills already on file once the owner shares the bills folder with the app's account (they are private files in a My Drive folder today), PR 2a the crosswalk to the parts book, PR 3 the *What we buy* page.
