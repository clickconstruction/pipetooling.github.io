# v2.3575 — Job Summary: the earned-revenue kernel on the Jobs view (2026-09-17)

Item 8's remainder in `to-dos/job-summary-follow-ups.md` — "one shared earned-revenue kernel for Job Summary, Crew P&L and the board" — drawn in `job-summary-follow-ups-earned-revenue.html`. This PR is the Job Summary half; Crew P&L still credits gross bill by hours (its own conventions, noted in the to-do).

## The rule

Until now an in-progress row's Revenue was contract × % complete, whatever share of its hours fell inside the *Worked in* window — so a 90-day view compared 90 days of labor and overhead with value the job earned over its whole life, and a long job's true margin read too high. Now the row reads the Bridge's own kernel ([`earnedRevenue.ts`](../../src/lib/bridge/earnedRevenue.ts), v2.2677) over the window: each approved field hour earns contract ÷ expected total hours (hours to date ÷ % complete), so the window's hours earn the window's share. `earnedRevenueInWindow` is the new one-job form of that kernel; [`jobSummaryLedgerView.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobSummaryLedgerView.ts) reads it when the day ledger is loaded, with lifetime hours = the window's hours + `priorHoursByJob`.

What does not move: a job with every hour inside the window (contract × % exactly), finished jobs (the contract, the owner's 2026-09-03 pick), a job with no hours at all, and every row before the ledger loads. A job with no % is still assumed half done and flagged. The mock-up's J963 — $58,400 at 45%, 148.5 of 262 hours in the window — drops from $26,280 to $14,895.34, and its true margin from 53% to 17%; that arithmetic is the test.

The row keeps `earnedLifetimeUsd` (contract × %) and `earnedHours` (in window / lifetime / expected). The **Ahead** view subtracts the lifetime figure from the contract for remaining value, so its numbers do not change.

## On screen

The Revenue tile's sub-line reads *N in-progress earned by hours worked*; the column header's tooltip and each earned cell's tooltip carry the arithmetic (*× 148.5 of 262.0 h in the window* when they differ); the **Revenue\*** asterisk lands on a one-line rule under the table. Guides *read true profit on Job Summary*, *see profit by month on Job Summary* and *find the big jobs with thin margins* say the rule.
