# v2.4847 — Bill tab: a check the app counts can be applied to the Stripe bill after the fact (2026-10-07)

Client plus the `record-stripe-invoice-out-of-band-payment` function (**redeploy after merge**, `--no-verify-jwt`); no migration. Grace, on job 825's Bill tab (Michael A, Malachi; Michael Palmer, 121 North Calvin Barrett) beside the Stripe pay page still asking $3,850: *"Before I send the invoice to Palmer, the $3500 that was paid via check needs to be applied to correct the Stripe invoice."* The second bug of the Jobs Stages bugs session.

## What was wrong

- The $3,850 Stripe bill went out Sep 2. A $3,500 check from Palmer Custom Homes landed Sep 14 and was recorded on the job (a bank deposit matched in Accounts Receivable, or a payment typed before the bill was the Stripe one). The app counted it: the bill read **$350 open · $350 short**, the Pipeline card 91% paid. Stripe was never told — no credit note, the invoice `open` for $3,850 — so the customer's pay link and PDF asked for the whole amount.
- v2.3695 named this gap (*Accounts Receivable's partial deposit allocation on a Stripe bill still says to fix Stripe by hand*) and the row had no door: Undo part payment needs a credit note the row never had; Record payment would write a second $3,500.

## What changed

- **`record-stripe-invoice-out-of-band-payment`** gains `payment_id`: the row supplies the amount, date, type and reference; the function refuses a row with a credit note already, on another job, pinned to another bill, with no received date, or at/over Stripe's `amount_remaining` (*Mark the bill paid instead* — a whole-bill close is Mark Paid's job and would otherwise double the row through the webhook). Under the balance it writes the v2.3695 credit note and **updates** the row — `invoice_id` the bill, `stripe_credit_note_id` the note — leaving the row out of the "already applied" sum, and voids the note if the update fails. Answers `applied_existing: true`.
- **The payment line's ⋯** ([`JobFormPaymentLine`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormPaymentLine.tsx)) under a Stripe bill offers **Apply it to the Stripe bill** (*the pay link then asks for the rest*) when [`applyToStripeOffered`](../../src/lib/jobs/applyPaymentToStripeBill.ts) says so: the bill Billed and not paid in Stripe, the row saved and whole, no credit note, its amount under the bill, and the row pinned to this bill or counted here by the oldest-first rule. The ③ table passes the door through.
- **The customer's word for the type.** A bank row's Mercury kind (`checkDeposit`, `incomingDomesticWire`) and the office's spellings become *Check · Wire · ACH · Card · Cash* on the credit line (`customerPaymentTypeWord`, mirrored in the function), and a bank row's reference — the bank's id — never goes on it; the first live window read *checkDeposit received Sep 14* before this.
- **[`ApplyPaymentToStripeBillModal`](../../src/components/jobs/ApplyPaymentToStripeBillModal.tsx)**: *Stripe puts a credit line on the $3,850.00 bill: "Check #1042 received Sep 14 · $3,500.00". The pay link and the invoice PDF then ask for $350.00. Nothing moves in the bank, and the payment stays as it is here. Undo part payment is the way back.* The button reads **Apply · $350.00 stays due**; on success the job is re-read and the toast says the pay link now asks for the rest. What stays due is the app's own open figure after attribution (`attributeJobPayments`), so the words match the bill row above.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **The same credit note, not a new kind.** The customer reads the same line a part payment recorded through the Stripe window gets; Undo part payment already reverses it; the webhook's `credit_note.created` only syncs status.
- **The row is pinned.** Attribution counted it toward this bill; once Stripe holds it the link is a fact, and the remove and move rules read `stripe_credit_note_id` as Stripe's hold (v2.3784).
- **Not done:** Accounts Receivable's partial allocation on an open Stripe bill still records the row without telling Stripe. The same call with `payment_id` after the allocation RPC would close that gap at the source — its own PR.
