# v2.2089 — Roadmap Timeline calendar header replaces the pace slider (2026-08-22)

The owner didn't understand the Timeline's "at N tasks/week" what-if slider (v2.1979), so it's gone. In its place, a calendar band at the top of **Checklist → Roadmap → Timeline**:

- **New kernel** `src/lib/roadmapCalendar.ts` (+ tests):
  - `observedPace(tasks, now)` — the projection's input is now the *real* completion rate: completions in the last 28 days ÷ 4 weeks (`basis: 'recent'`), falling back to the all-time average since the first completion (`'allTime'`), `null` before any completion. No more user-invented number; nothing stored (the old `roadmap_timeline_pace_v1` localStorage key is orphaned).
  - `calendarBand(projection, now)` — month-strip geometry as lane fractions: months from the current month through the goal month (≥3, ≤12), a today fraction, a today→goal runway, intermediate wave markers (indices 1..len−2, collision-filtered at 0.035), and the goal flag. A goal beyond the 12-month horizon is `clamped`: pinned at 0.98 with its true `≈ Mon 'YY` label, plus `horizonEnd` so the view can compute the pace that *would* finish inside the horizon.
- **View** (`ChecklistRoadmapTimelineView.tsx`): slider row → calendar band (months with alternating `--bg-slate-tint`, amber today tick, `--bg-blue-tint`/`--border-blue` runway, blue wave dots labeled via `waveName`, 🎯 flag with side-flipped label past 82%; clamped goals render a ⟶ run-off instead of a flag). Caption: `🎯 goal ≈ Oct · at your recent pace — 7 tasks/week over the last 4 weeks · 88 tasks left`, plus `· N/week would finish by <horizon>` when clamped, or a "complete tasks to project a finish date" hint when no pace exists. The purple per-wave ≈dates left the wave header (the calendar owns dates); header shrank 40→26px.
- `paceProjection`'s zero-guard clamp loosened 0.5→0.1 tasks/week so honest slow paces aren't inflated.
- Help guide `read-the-roadmap-timeline.md`: "The pace slider" section rewritten as "The calendar".
