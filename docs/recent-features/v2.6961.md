# v2.6961 — Lien help sheets: a picture of the paper, papers grouped by county clerk, who to ask, and lines to write on (2026-11-11)

Punch list #314, changes 6 to 11 of the best-we-can-do pass. The owner saw each drawn beside what printed (`to-dos/lien-help-sheets/changes-5-to-11.html`) and said: *Regarding six through eleven, I like them all, please make those changes. Regarding who to call, they should call the leader or the dev.* Change 8 shipped with change 2 (v2.6957). Change 5 is not in this: the owner turned it into a different idea, a code that opens the app's record step with a photo of the receipt, which is being thought through on the card.

## What changed

- **6 · A picture of the paper.** `lienHelpPictureHtml` (`src/lib/jobs/lienHelpSheet.ts`) draws a small affidavit with a numbered spot where each thing is written, beside the list on the cover sheet and the batch sheet. The numbers are the list's own: six with blank counties, four with them printed.
- **7 · Clerk order.** `lienAffidavitClerkOrder` (`lienAffidavitBatch.ts`) sorts a batch by the county it is filed in, each clerk's papers in the desk's order. `LienDesk.tsx` prints in that order, so the stack and the sheet's rows agree. The batch sheet draws a row for each clerk above its papers (`lienHelpClerkGroupWords`: *ALDER COUNTY CLERK · 6 papers*), and the *File with the clerk of* column is gone.
- **9 · Who to ask.** `lienAskFor` (`src/lib/jobs/lienAskPeople.ts`) names the job's lien leader (its master) and the dev, each with a phone when the app holds one; `useLienAskPeople` (`components/jobs/lienAskPeopleChoice.ts`) reads the people once a visit. The line ends the reader's check on the cover sheet. A batch says it once when every paper names the same people, and on each row when they differ. Nobody known prints nothing.
- **10 · Lines to write on, and the clerk's facts.** `lienHelpAfterSteps` leaves a line for the recording number, the day filed and the mail-by day; the batch sheet has a *Recording no. · day filed* column. A clerk's address, fee and hours print only when the office typed them: **County clerks, for the lien help sheets** in Settings' company block (`countyClerksText` on the issuer, one clerk per line, `County | where to file | fee | hours`), read by `lienClerkFor` (`src/lib/jobs/lienCountyClerks.ts`). No migration: the issuer is a JSON value in `app_settings`. The app never guesses a clerk.
- **11 · The stripe, the watermark, the code.** Every help sheet has a striped top edge. The guide copy carries *GUIDE COPY* across its paper. `lienHelpGuideCode` (`src/lib/jobs/lienHelpGuideCode.ts`) draws a QR code to the lien guide's share page with the edge functions' own encoder, synchronously and with no network; it sits in the band's right corner, where it adds no height.

## Two fixes found on the way

- **The fills now print.** The sheets' black tags (*ASK FIRST*, *YOUR CHECK*, *STOP*) are white words on a black fill. A browser's print window leaves background fills off unless its own box is ticked, which would have printed white words on white. `.lien-help` now sets `print-color-adjust: exact`.
- **The Record service step said to type tracking numbers.** The form takes one thing, the day served. The step now says to set the day the copies were mailed and to keep the receipts with the job.

## Proof

Headless Chrome on the kernel's own output with made-up facts: the fullest cover sheet (six blanks, a question, a clerk line, who to ask) is one page with the paper on the next; a batch of 12 across three clerks prints its sheet on two pages and then 12 papers in clerk order; the guide copy is one page. The first try put the code in the foot and pushed the foot to a second page, which is why it sits in the band.
