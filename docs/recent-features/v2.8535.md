# v2.8535 — Lien desk: an empty Months card says what gives the job a month (2026-11-24)

Loose end 2 of punch list #415, the Lien desk's leftovers from the ledger (#361). Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

On a notice whose job has no month, Months on this job read *No months on this job yet.* and stopped. The repo's rule is that an empty list says what fills it and offers the press. The card's own comment said the next step was the row above, the last day of work, but the card did not say so to the person reading it.

## What changed

- [`lienMonthGrid.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienMonthGrid.ts): `LIEN_MONTHS_EMPTY` holds the words. A month comes from approved hours, a sent bill, or the last day of work. The last day of work is the one a person at the desk can set there and then, so it is the press.
- [`LienDeskMonths.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskMonths.tsx): the empty card draws *No months on this job yet. Set the last day of work › gives the job its first month.* through `EmptyState`. A reader with no press reads *Approved hours, a sent bill or a last day of work gives the job its first month.*
- [`LienDeskNoticePane.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskNoticePane.tsx): the press opens the window the path's LAST WORK stop opens (`LienLastWorkDayWindow`), for the same people (`lienLastWorkDoorOffered`).
- The guide [send lien notices from the Lien desk](/help/send-lien-notices-from-the-lien-desk) has the line.

## Not changed

The row above the empty words, with **Set by hand**. A card with months.

## Verified

- `lienMonthGrid.test.ts` holds the words to the plain-words rules. `LienDeskMonths.render.test.tsx` holds both shapes and that the press calls its door.
- Measured on a harness of the real pane, on made-up rows: the press is 44 px tall on the narrow card, at 380 px and at 730 px, as every press on that card is.
- `npm run typecheck` passed on the three #415 builds together, and this one's Lien tests pass alone. The full suite is left to CI. Not seen live: a notice whose job has no month is rare.
