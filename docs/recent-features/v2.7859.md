# v2.7859 — Dashboard: a Paint a map press that is always there (2026-11-18)

Punch list #392, steps 1 and 2, on the owner's word of 2026-11-18 (*Yes, build it*). A walk as a leader on a phone the same day found the flow's worst snag: with nothing on the schedule, nothing on the Dashboard said map, with Job Mode on or off. The only door with no job open was the header's search, and a person had to know to type *paint*.

## What changed

- **The kernel**, `src/lib/paintedMap/paintedMapPress.ts`. `paintPressLanding` says which job the press lands on: the job the person is clocked onto, else the first of today's stops that has not ended, else the last stop once the day is over, else the person picks. A clock on the office, on a bid or on a billing-only job is not a job. `paintPressLine` is the second line, `paintPressHome` what the start sheet opens on, `paintPressShape` which of the three shapes a row draws.
- **The reads**, `paintedMapPressIo.ts`: today's stops and the open clock, the same two rows Job Mode's card reads, and the clocked job by itself when it is on no stop today.
- **The press**, `PaintAMapPress.tsx`, on the Dashboard's top row (`DashboardPinnedQuickRow`):
  - Job Mode: wide, beside the Tally square, in the slot the Job Report button left empty (#189). Its second line reads *On J2417 · Cedar Hollow 12* or *You pick the job it is for*. It reads the day when drawn and again when the clock changes.
  - The office's phone: a third quiet link beside *Job report* and *Tally*.
  - A desk, and a leader's phone: a square, 72 wide, after Job Report. A master is not a phone office role, so a leader on a phone has the square row.
  - The square and the link read the day on the press, so the Dashboard pays nothing to draw them. The press waits at most 2.5 seconds. A slow or failed read opens the sheet with no job set.
- **The start sheet** (`PaintAMapStartSheet.tsx`): a job the press picked can be changed. `canChangeJob` on the map's home draws **Change the job** under *For*, over the sheet's own job search, with **Keep this job** to close it. A sheet opened from a job's own address is as it was.
- **The clock bridge** gains `useSessionChanges`, a listener that works with no provider above it, since the row is drawn in smokes with no bridge.
- A press records `paint-a-map` with the shape it was pressed in.

## Not in this change

- The two short pages (#384), the short Dashboard and Job Mode's short page, do not draw the top row. The press is behind **Everything else** there. Whether a short page gets the press is the owner's call, written on the card.
- The picker of today's and recent jobs, a map on no job retired from the doors, **Move it to another job…** and the Saved band are the card's steps 3 and 4.
- The header's search still lists matching jobs above **Paint a map** for *paint*. That order is the search's own for every word, so it was left alone.
