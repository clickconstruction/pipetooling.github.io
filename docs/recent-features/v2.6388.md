# v2.6388 — Job Summary: a billed job under 100 % earns by its %, the Jobs tile says what the rows are, and a crew report's % reads as the crew's (2026-11-02)

The owner, 2026-11-02, on Jobs → Job Summary searched to **J804**: *I think the margin is wrong or the header is generally too confusing.* The row read Revenue **$32,600 earned**, Gross $12,642.88 · 39 %, True profit $10,460.38 · 32 %, Burn **94 % · 40 %**, Proj. margin **−$22,749.06 · −70 %**, % **40 % set by office**; the tiles read *Jobs 1 · finished (100%) · worked since january 1*; the note under the controls read *Job 804 is still in progress, so the Show chip stepped aside*. Every number was traced to its rows through the dev seat (read only): $32,600 contract, status `billed`, two paid draws and two billed ($32,600 invoiced, not paid in full), the Oct 30 crew report at 40 %, 31 approved sessions = 139.70 h over 20 field days, labor $3,122.12 · subs $5,600 · parts $11,235 (supply invoices $9,406.20 + card charges $1,828.80), a `job_budgets` row from the bid with nothing in it (so the assumption: $32,600 × 65 % = $21,190; $19,957.12 ÷ $21,190 = 94.2 %). Three things were wrong by the app's own rules; the rest is the rule or the layout, put to the owner in the session.

## The three fixes

- **Two "finished" rules on one row.** `enrichJobSummaryRows` decides `finished` from the resolved % (the v2.2840 gate: 40 → not finished → earned revenue), then hands the Bridge's `earnedRevenueInWindow` the job's `status`, and that kernel's own rule reads `ready_to_bill` / `billed` / `paid` as done — expected hours = lifetime hours, so every hour earned the whole contract. J804 read $32,600 "earned" while the cell's hover said *contract × 40 %* and `earnedLifetimeUsd` (the Ahead view) already said $13,040; its Burn and Proj. margin ran on the 40 %. Now [`earnedRevenue.ts`](../../src/lib/bridge/earnedRevenue.ts) takes an optional `finished` on `earnedRevenueInWindow` and `expectedHoursForJob` (set, it decides; omitted, the status decides as before — the Bridge, Build desk and People → Review are untouched), and [`jobSummaryLedgerView.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobSummaryLedgerView.ts) passes the row's own `finished`. J804 at 40 %: Revenue **$13,040 earned**, Gross −$6,917.12 · −53 %, True profit −$9,099.63 · −70 %, $/hr $93 — the same −70 % its Proj. margin already said. At 100 % by the crew it reads the contract and `done`, as before.
- **The Jobs tile read the chips the search had set aside.** The tile's second line was the Show chip's label and the Worked in chip's title, lowercased whole (*finished (100%) · worked since january 1*), drawn while the search had made both chips step aside (v2.5745) and the note under the controls said the opposite. `jobSummaryJobsTileWords` in [`jobSummarySearchWins.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobSummarySearchWins.ts) reads *matching your search* while the box holds something, else the two chips with only their first letter lowered (*finished (100%) · worked since January 1*).
- **Every crew report read *set by office*.** v2.3372's rule — the newest % wins, a hand-set at or after the report makes the report stale — is read off `list_latest_report_completion_pct`'s `manual_at`, the newest `job_pct_events` row with source `manual`. The report's own mirror (`propagateReportPctToJob` → `set_job_pct_from_field`) updates `jobs_ledger.pct_complete` a fraction of a second after the report, and the trigger stamps that row `manual` because the tech is signed in (the fragment's *service rows are report-derived* holds only for a service-role writer) — so every crew report that moves the job's % was followed by a "hand-set" of its own number and read as stale; a report that repeats the current % writes no mirror and was fine. J804: the report, then its mirror a third of a second later → stale → the % fell through to `pct_complete` with source `office`, and the date for the badge was dropped. `reportPctIsCurrent` ([`jobSummaryPercentComplete.ts`](../../src/lib/jobSummaryPercentComplete.ts)) now treats a hand-set within `REPORT_PCT_MIRROR_GRACE_MS` (two minutes) of the report as the report's own mirror, so the report stands and the badge reads *crew report Oct 30*; a hand-set later than that still wins. The value was never wrong (the mirror copies the report's number); the words and the date were. Readers: Job Summary's % map and date map, Quickfill's *Complete, no Total Bill*, the GC crew rows.

The edge functions' copy of the kernel (`supabase/functions/_shared/gcKernels/lib/bridge/earnedRevenue.ts`, `npm run gen:edge-kernels`) was regenerated with it; no function passes the new option, so nothing behaves differently there and no deploy is owed.

## What did not change

The % column's order (paid invoices covering the contract → crew report → office %), the Show chip's `finished`, the day-share overhead, the Burn arithmetic, the Bridge's own earned revenue (status still decides there), and the data on J804 — the 40 % is the Oct 30 line report's Σ weight × stage % with Top Out and Trim Set never reported (`stageEffectivePct` reads an unreported line as 0), which took the job from the office's 100 % of Oct 12 to 40; whether a billed-to-the-contract job should count as finished, and whether an unreported line should read 0 on a job already at 100 %, are the owner's calls and were put to him with the header redesign.
