# v2.6809 — The lien affidavit you print carries the dates of the work and the contractor's address (2026-11-09)

Reported by the office on 2026-11-09 with two screenshots one press apart: on the Lien desk (All filings → Affidavits) a job's affidavit read *during the period from August 1, 2026 through August 31, 2026* and named the original contractor with a street address; after **Open the affidavit tab ›** the job's Lien window drew the same affidavit on its Mechanic's lien tab with *from — through —* and the contractor's name alone. The window is where the paper is printed for the notary, and the job's lien right ended seven days out.

## Cause

Two surfaces filled one paper from different inputs. The desk's pane fills `LienAffidavitFields` through `buildLienAffidavitFieldsForJob` (`src/lib/jobs/lienNoticeDraft.ts`); the window's tab (`LienFilingTabs`) filled the same fields by hand.

- **Paragraph 5.** The desk hands the kernel the RPC's last month, which is the job's creation month when it has no approved hours (v2.3747), and the span kernel swears that month first day to last (v2.6061). The window computed its span from `useForecastWorkMonths`, which leaves a job with no sessions out, so `lienWorkSpan` got no day and no month and the paper printed its blanks. The strip above the paper read *Last work Aug 2026 · dated from the job's creation* all the while: `buildLienTimelineFromWindow` had the creation-month fallback and the paper did not.
- **Paragraph 7.** The tab set `originalContractorAddress: ''`. The window already loads the GC's row (`payerRows`, for the demand letter's debtor block) and never handed its address to the tab.

Neither came from the desk's move to a page (v2.6777, v2.6780, v2.6781): the tab has filled its own fields since it was written, and v2.6061 gave it a span with no month to fall back on. The prod read (dev seat, read-only) showed the reported job and four siblings on the same GC with no `first_work_date`, no `last_work_date` and no `lien_last_work_on`, and the GC's address on file.

## What changed

- **`lienWindowLastMonth`** (`src/lib/jobs/lienTimelineDesk.ts`, pure): the last month as the window knows it and what named it, lifted out of `buildLienTimelineFromWindow`, which now calls it. The forecast's last month with a session, the later month of a day set by hand, and for a job with no sessions the ledger's last day, else its creation month. No change to the strip.
- **`buildLienWindowAffidavitFields`** (`src/lib/jobs/lienWindowAffidavit.ts`, pure, 8 tests): the window gives what it knows (the job row, the forecast's months, the GC's row) and `buildLienAffidavitFieldsForJob` fills the paper. `LienAffidavitJobFacts` takes `firstMonth`, the first month with a session, for the start while the job has no first approved day (the window's rule since v2.6061).
- **`LienFilingTabs`** fills `affidavitFields` through that kernel. Its `workSpan` prop is gone: it takes `workDays` (the window's fresh last-day read, so a day set by hand still reaches the paper the moment it is saved), `workMonths` and `originalContractorAddress`. Given none of them it reads the job row alone and still falls to the creation month.
- **`LienInstrumentsModal`** hands the tab its months and the GC's address from `payerRows`.

The statutory wording of the affidavit is untouched (`buildLienAffidavitBlocks`).

## Decisions

- **Prefill, not blank.** The blank in the window was not a legal-care choice. v2.6061 decided that a job with no approved hours swears the whole month the desk knows, that the office narrows it by setting the last day by hand, and that the window's tab prints the same two dates as the desk; the window's own strip already dated the job from its creation. The window was missing the fallback.
- **One recipe.** The tab no longer has a field list of its own, so a field the desk learns reaches the printed paper.

## Still different, not fixed here

- **The sworn amount.** The desk swears what the sent bills still owe (the RPC's `open_balance`, v2.4969) less a claim set by hand (v2.3682); the window swears revenue less payments and does not read `job_lien_claim_corrections`. Equal on every job in the affidavit window today (prod holds no claim correction), but a correction would reach the desk's paper and not the printed one.
- **A job contracted with the owner.** The desk's draft leaves the original contractor's name blank (it reads the GC's row and there is none); the window names our own company.
