## What changed

- **The column**: `job_containers.description`, nullable, at most 80 characters, never an empty string. It holds a person's own words and nothing else. The app's line is written on the screen from the box's parts and never stored, so it cannot go stale.
- **The kernel** `supabase/functions/_shared/boxLine.ts`, re-exported and extended by `src/lib/materials/boxLine.ts` (the pattern `boxPage.ts` set, so the scanned page's function and the screens read one rule):
  - `boxLineFromParts`: one kind of part reads its name and count (*Kohler toilets · 24*); two read both names; more read how many, the two the box holds most of, and how many more (*7 parts: 2 inch 90, 1-1/2 coupling and 5 more*); nothing on hand is no line.
  - `boxLine(said, parts)`: a person's words when there are any, else the app's, with who wrote it (`person`, `app`, `none`).
  - `boxLineShort` and `boxNameWithLine`, for a chip: *R76 · Kohler toilets*.
  - On the client: `boxLineVerdict` (Say it better's press: the words cleaned, null when cleared, a quiet no when nothing changed, a refusal past 80 with the count), `lineFieldValue`, `nameWordsNote` and `addedLineVerdict` (words typed into a name).
- **The box's screen** (the phone, `TallyOnSiteTab.tsx`): the line under the name, marked *from its parts* when the app wrote it, with **Say it better** (**Say what is in it** on a box with no line). The sheet takes a person's words; the press reads **Save the line**, or **Use the app’s line** when the words were cleared. The write is the store's `sayBoxLine`, kept and sent later with no signal like every other on-site press. Under a person's line: *In a person’s words. The parts under it still count.*
- **Both Add screens and the sticker sheet**: a **What is in it** field, never asked for. Words typed after a name stand in it until the field is touched (`R90 TOILETS` → the name R90, *Toilets* in the field, and the say line reads *“Toilets” moved to What is in it.*). PR 1 parked those words on the where line; `whereWithWords` and its note are gone.
- **Edit** (`editBox.ts`, `EditBoxForm.tsx`): **What is in it** holds the same line, its placeholder the app's own line. A rename that carries words puts them there unless the field was typed in the same window, which wins.
- **The lists**: each row of the phone's list reads the line under the name, and drops the *n parts* the app's line already says. The phone's search lists a box by a person's words (`searchContainers`), and the sticker sheet's match list does too.
- **The office card** (`JobsMaterialsTab.tsx`): the line under the card's heading, marked *from its parts* or *in a person’s words*. The box's name opens Edit, which holds the line.
- **Go anywhere** (`boxSearch.ts`): a box answers to a person's words, and its row reads the short line first.
- **Print labels** (`LabelsPrintModal.tsx`): a chip reads the name and the short line. The printed label still carries the name alone (v2.7195).
- **The scanned page** (`_shared/boxPage.ts`, `box-read`, `BoxPublicPage.tsx`): `box.line` on the plate under the name, and on each box a search lists; the search matches a person's words. An answer with no `line`, from the function as deployed before, is read as the app's line from the lines it carries, so the page reads right before the redeploy.

## Decisions

- **Which two parts a mixed box names**: the two it holds most of, a tie in name order. The mock-up drew two names without saying which. The most on hand says the most about the box, and the tie rule keeps the line from flickering.
- **The app's line never runs past 80 characters either.** A long line names one part, and a long name is cut with an ellipsis before its count is.
- **One column, as carded.** The mock-up drew *Said by Priya, Nov 20* under a person's line. The card's migration is the one column, so who and when are not kept and the screen says *In a person’s words*. See Left.
- **The sticker sheet's new box** is made by `place_sticker` in the database. Its line is sent as the box's own press right after the database answers (`sayBoxLine`), so `place_sticker` is not restated a third time while the stickers work is active on it. With no signal between the two the press is kept and sent later, so the words are not lost.
- **The line field once touched is the person's own**, cleared included: the name's words no longer write over it, and the say line then reads that they are not part of the name.
- **Nothing said sends no column** on an add, so a box added with no line is the row it always was.
