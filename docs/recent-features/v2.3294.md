# v2.3294 — Bids → Labor: the crew rate, the overhead fact, estimator time retired, and the bottom line (PR 4a of the Labor refresh) (2026-09-11)

Client only. The first half of rung 4 in [`to-dos/bids-labor-refresh/`](../../to-dos/bids-labor-refresh/README.md) (the second half, one direct-costs list with a kind chip, is PR 4b).

## The crew rate — never blank

- [`crewRate.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/crewRate.ts) (+6 tests): `crewRateFromLedgerDays` averages the wage-priced field hours of the job day ledger over the last 90 days (Σ `fieldLaborUsd` ÷ Σ `fieldHours`) and applies the burden factor (`app_settings.labor_burden_factor_v1`, seeded 1.20 by v2.3291); lens A (`poolUsd` ÷ field hours) rides along as `overheadPerFieldHour`. `effectiveLaborRate` says what a bid is costed at: its own `labor_rate` when set, else the company rate, else nothing.
- [`useBidCrewRate`](../../src/hooks/useBidCrewRate.ts) reads the SAME cached ledger the job's Burn section loads (`loadJobDayLedgerCached`, the office pool's first day → today), so it costs nothing extra after a job page; fail-soft to null.
- The New view's **Crew rate** card: *company $35.76/h = $29.80 avg recorded field wage (90 d, 3,120 h) × 1.20 burden · this bid: company rate · save it on the bid · override…*. **Save it on the bid** writes the company rate into the tab's rate box (autosaved to `cost_estimates.labor_rate`) so Pricing and the documents read the same number; a typed rate shows as *$35.00/h override · use company rate*. The Labor $ tile, completeness ("rate set") and the strip read the effective rate. The Old view is unchanged (its rate box is the override).

## Facts beside the rate, not in the cost

- **Overhead / field hour** tile (lens A over the same 90 days) — *shown, not added to the direct cost*, the way the job's Burn treats overhead.
- **Bid labor recorded** — the hours clocked on this bid (People → Bids, `teamLaborDataForBids`, now passed to the Labor tab) with the people and dollars: *12.5 h · $446 · in the overhead pool, not in this bid's cost*.
- **Estimator time retired.** [`computeBidCostBreakdown`](../../src/lib/bids/bidTotalCostBreakdown.ts) still reports `estimatorCost` and `teamLaborCost` but no longer adds either to `laborCostWithDriving` / `directCost` / `totalCost`. So the Pricing Workbench, the Pricing print/CSV, the Labor page print and the approval PDF all drop the invented estimator dollars (every bid carried $10 × count rows by default). The Labor page and the PDF no longer print an Estimator line; the PDF's page-1 margin moves accordingly. The Old view's *Estimators Time* box (flat / per-count inputs) is replaced by a read-only **Bid labor recorded** line; the `estimator_cost_*` columns stay and the autosave still writes them, unread.

## The bottom line

Under the grid: **Direct cost of this bid** — labor · materials (takeoff) · driving · travel · other direct → the total, the margin at the bid value (amber under 20 %), *open Pricing →*. The same kernel Pricing and the documents read, at the effective rate; the direct-cost tables come live from the tab so the line moves as the amber sections are edited. This is the number the job's Budget card will snapshot on the win (`to-dos/burn-against-the-bid/`).
