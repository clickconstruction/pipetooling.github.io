# v2.4042 — Pipeline: the schedule strip keeps next week clear of this week (2026-09-28)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. The owner's report from the Pipeline: "the 2nd Monday overlaps with the 2nd Tuesday."

## What was wrong

The two-week strip (`renderStagesScheduleStripCells`, v2.3752) laid its cells on one grid of fixed 10 px tracks and drew the week break as a 3 px `margin-left` on the second Monday. The track did not grow with the margin, so the Monday cell ran 2 px into Tuesday's track and its letter was squeezed to 7 px against the T beside it — measured on the live board before the fix (cells `128.6–138.6` and `136.6–146.6`).

## What changed

- **`src/lib/jobs/stagesScheduleStrip.ts`** — `stripWeeks(cells)` groups the strip's cells by week (splitting at the cell the kernel already marks `weekStart`), so the row draws each week as its own run.
- **`src/components/jobs/jobsStagesRowShared.tsx`** — both rows (the cells and the letters) render two `.stagesStripWeek` groups; the `isWeekStart` class and the `--strip-n` variable are gone.
- **`src/index.css`** — `.stagesStripCells` / `.stagesStripLetters` are a flex row with a 3 px gap (the week break); each `.stagesStripWeek` is a column-flow grid of `--strip-cell` tracks with the 1 px gap. The margin rule is deleted. A booked weekend still adds its cell to its own week; the strip is 111 px wide for ten cells (two 54 px weeks and the 3 px break), 2 px wider than before.

## Tests

`stagesScheduleStrip.test.ts` — `stripWeeks` splits at the second Monday with a booked Saturday kept on its week; an empty strip gives no weeks. The Pipeline table and phone-row smokes cover the render.
