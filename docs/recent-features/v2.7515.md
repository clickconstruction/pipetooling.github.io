## plan_lien_service → apply_lien_service

§ 53.055 gives five days from filing to mail a copy to the owner, and to the original contractor on a job under one. The pair records that it was done, as **Record service…** on the job's Lien window and the desk's **Mail the copies** window do:

- One update of the job's live filed affidavit: `served_at` and `sends`, as the dev under RLS.
- `sends` comes from the form's own kernel (`lienServiceProof.ts` → `serviceSends`): a send for each copy that has a certified mail number or a receipt. The day alone is a valid record.
- The form opens on what the filing holds, so a number or a photo saved earlier is kept unless the call replaces it.
- How many copies: the filing's stored `isSub` first, then whether the job sits under a GC. That is the Mail the copies window's rule.

A receipt photo is a staged upload, as a filing's scan is. Every staged file is checked before anything is written. The day and the numbers are written first, then each receipt goes into the job's Drive folder under Team only › Sent by us, under the photo press's own name, and its link is set on its copy's send. A receipt that does not land is named and the day stands.

It refuses a job with no filed affidavit, a day ahead of today or before the filing, a contractor's copy on a job contracted with the owner, and a service that changes nothing. The plan says when the copies went after the serve-by day, when the day on record changes, and when both copies carry one number.

One service or at most 20 a plan, each with its own hash. One an apply.

## read_lien_filings

A read. The newest 200 filed affidavits, each with what it still lacks (`recording_number`, `saved_copy`, `service`, `proof`) and where its serve-by day stands (*3 days left to mail the copies*). `show: 'left'` is the default. It reads as whoever asks, under their own RLS.
