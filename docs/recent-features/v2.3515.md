# v2.3515 — The demand letter counts a single-bill job's unlinked payment, and the § 31.04 gate reads every payment on the job (2026-09-16)

Two fixes found on the live check of v2.3498. Client only; no migration, no edge function, no schema.

## The letter contradicted its own exhibit

**Job 102 (Samantha Coyle)**: one $5,355 bill, one $3,000 check recorded on the job with no `invoice_id`. After v2.3498 the enclosed invoice (Exhibit A) correctly read *Paid Feb 26, 2026 − $3,000.00 · Balance due $2,355.00* — while the letter around it said **"Nothing has been paid"**, claimed **$5,355.00**, and the window header read *$5,355.00 open*. A legal instrument asserting something its own attachment disproves.

Cause: `sumApplied` in [`demandLetter.ts`](../../src/lib/jobsDocuments/demandLetter.ts) counted only payments whose `invoice_id` matched. On a multi-bill job that is a defensible refusal to guess (`to-dos/unlinked-payments-on-multi-bill-jobs.md`). On a job with **exactly one** sent bill there is nothing to guess — the payment can only be paying that bill.

**What changed**: `sumApplied` became the exported kernel **`paymentsAppliedToInvoice(job, invoiceId)`**. A payment linked to this bill always counts; one linked to another bill never does; an unlinked payment counts only when the job has exactly one bill in `billed`/`paid` status (a `ready_to_bill` draft was never in the customer's hands, so it does not make the job multi-bill). Three readers now share it, which is the "one kernel" the to-do asked for on the demand side:

- the per-invoice `paid` / `balance` on the statement of account (`buildDemandStatement`);
- `paymentsReceived` / `outstanding` on the letter, the Re line, the email subject and the header (`buildDemandLetterPrefill`);
- **which bills a letter covers** — `demandableInvoices` in [`LienInstrumentsModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienInstrumentsModal.tsx) had its own copy of the linked-only map and now calls the kernel, so a single-bill job whose unlinked payment settles the bill in full no longer offers that bill for demand.

The single-bill rule is deliberately the whole of it. Multi-bill jobs (258, 273, 251, 473) are untouched: their letters still claim only linked money, and the to-do's option list stands for the owner. The to-do's *option 2* is now half-taken — its single-bill half — and says so.

## The § 31.04 gate keyed on the wrong number

The owner's rule (2026-09-02) is that a theft-of-services report is off the table once the client has paid **anything on the job**. The Lien window decided that from `fields.paymentsReceived` — the letter's own claim sum, which counts only what the covered bills attribute. So on **job 258** ($8,000 paid on the earlier bill; the letter covers the later $9,800 bill) and on job 102 ($3,000 unlinked, before this PR) the line read **"available — no payments made on this job"** with the checkbox enabled. The toggle is off by default, so a letter carried it only if someone ticked it — but the label invited exactly that.

**What changed**: new kernel **`jobHasAnyPayment(job)`** — any positive `jobs_ledger_payments` row on the job, linked to any bill or to none. Both the disabled checkbox and the clamp effect (which un-ticks it if it was on) read that, not the claim sum. The letter's arithmetic is unchanged by this half: job 258 still claims $9,800 in full, and the gate now correctly says not applicable beside it.

## Verified

- Kernel tests in [`demandLetter.test.ts`](../../src/lib/jobsDocuments/demandLetter.test.ts): single-bill unlinked counts; a draft does not make the job multi-bill; multi-bill counts only this bill's linked payments (the job-258 shape); several unlinked plus one linked sum; `jobHasAnyPayment` on linked-elsewhere, unlinked, none and zero rows; and two prefill cases — job 102 reads `3000.00` paid / `2355.00` outstanding with the sentence *"$3,000.00 has been paid and $2,355.00 remains."*, job 258 still reads `0.00` / `9800.00`.
- Render smokes in [`LienInstrumentsModal.render.test.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienInstrumentsModal.render.test.tsx): a payment on another bill → *not applicable* while the letter still says *Nothing has been paid*; a single-bill unlinked payment → the difference in the sentence and the gate closed; no payments → *available*.
- Live on prod data (dev login, local build): job 102's window header, claim, statement, letter sentence and Exhibit A all agree at **$3,000 paid / $2,355 due**, § 31.04 *not applicable*; job 258 unchanged at $9,800 with § 31.04 now *not applicable*.
