# v2.7768 — Trips: a trip's line opens its trip, and a trip says what happens to its line (2026-11-18)

## Why

Punch list #391, PR 4 of 4, the door back. PR 3 (v2.7763) put an Extra trip on a job as one line and gave the trip a door to that line. This PR gives the line its door to the trip, and says what happens to the line where a trip is deleted or its billing is changed.

## What changed

- **The door back.** A line that came from a trip shows *Open Trip 14 ›* under it in the Job window's ① Line Items (`JobFormFixturesSection`, a `tripDoors` map by line id). The form reads which of the job's lines name a trip off the job it loaded, then reads those trips' numbers and sites in one small read (`useLineTrips`, `readLineTrips`, `lineTripDoors` in `lib/trips/tripBillLine.ts`). The press closes the Job window as any close does, with its saves, and then opens the trip on its site's page (`tripHref`).
  - A reader the trips are closed to gets no door. So does a line whose trip is kept on a job with no development, since there is no Trips look to open it in.
  - A line whose trip was deleted has lost its mark, so it has no door and reads as any line.
  - The job's lines now carry `trip_id` (`JOBS_LEDGER_FIXTURES_EMBED`, added with the types after PR 2's push). The form reads it and never writes it: the save names its own columns, and `trip_id` is not one of them.
- **The words on a trip's delete.** The question of **Delete this trip** on a trip that holds a line adds: *Its line stays on J2101's lines, with its words and its price. Undo puts the trip back, but the line is no longer tied to it.* (`tripDeleteLineWords`). The database clears the mark when the trip goes, and the Undo saves the trip again without it.
- **The note in the sheet.** On a trip that holds a line, moving **Billing** off Extra is allowed (the card's default 5). The sheet then says: *This trip is on J2101's lines. Changing the billing leaves the line where it is.* (`tripSheetLineNote`, `TripSheet`'s `lineStaysNote`).

## Left as it is

After an Undo of a deleted trip, the trip is back and reads *Extra · not billed yet*, and its old line is an ordinary line with no door. Putting the trip on a bill again would add a second line. The delete's question says so before the trip goes.

- `JobFormModal.tripDoor.render.test.tsx`: the form reads the trip, draws the door, and the press closes the window and opens the trip. A job with no trip's line reads no trip.
- `TripSheet.lineNote.render.test.tsx` and `DevelopmentTripsCard.billLine.render.test.tsx`: the sheet's note once the billing is off Extra, and the delete's question with and without a line.
