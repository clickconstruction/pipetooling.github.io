# v2.6090 — A job's Drive photos in the app (punch list #164, PR 5) (2026-10-29)

Punch list #164, the last of five. The owner's call of 2026-10-24, *for 164 it should always be the google drive*: the app lists and shows the customer's Drive folder and never keeps a copy. The card is retired with this PR; `to-dos/README.md` carries the closing line.

## What changed

- **The function `job-photos`** (`docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`): for anyone signed in who may stamp the job's stages (`can_report_stage_progress`, checked through the caller's own client), `list` reads the job's two photo folders with the service account, Customer Pictures (`job_pictures_link`) and Team only › Photos under the job's folder (`google_drive_link`), pictures only, newest first, up to 500, with `folder.kind` (`pictures`, `photos`, `none`) and `waiting`, the job's checklist and box photos still in a bucket; `thumb` streams Drive's thumbnail for one file fetched with our token, and `full` the file's bytes up to 25 MB, both only for a file whose parent is one of the two folders. Nothing is made and nothing is kept. `verify_jwt = false` in `config.toml`; the JWT is checked in-body.
- **The strip** (`JobPhotosStrip.tsx` over `lib/photos/jobPhotos.ts`, tested) under the job window's address, above the stage checklists: *Photos · 15* as the door, three thumbnails (`JobPhotoThumb`, object URLs revoked on unmount) and the *+ N* tile, the from-line, and the count on its way. A job with neither link says *No Drive folder is linked to this job yet.* with **Set the folder** for the roles that edit the job's links (the window's own add-link editor) and *Ask the office…* for the rest, without a call; an empty folder says what fills it; a failed read says why with **Try again** and never hides the window.
- **The viewer** (`JobPhotosViewer.tsx`): the grid, 24 at a time with **Show N more**, the day on each tile, one picture at full size with its day and folder, **Open in Drive**, and **Open the folder in Drive** on the grid.
- **The guide** *see a job's photos*; `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` → job-photos and the TOC; the Glossary's *Stage checklist* gains the gallery line.

## Decisions

- **Both folders, one list.** The sweep lands a Pipeline job's photos in Customer Pictures when that link is a folder and in Team only › Photos otherwise (`photoLanding`), and older jobs hold photos in either, so the strip reads both and sorts by Drive's time.
- **Thumbnails stream through the function.** Drive's `thumbnailLink` needs a credentialed request on a folder that is not public, so the function fetches it with the service account and passes the bytes through; the client keeps an object URL only while the tile is on screen.
- **The job window alone.** Job Mode keeps its press bar and details; the tech who took the photos has no need to see them there (#189's own call).
