## The crew's card

`JobModeArrivalCheck` at the head of the job's details in Job Mode, above the painted maps. It draws nothing unless there is something to ask.

- **Is it as told?** with *Look before you unload. These must be true before Rough In.* The list is every bullet on them that none of ours has seen, whether or not anyone has said yes to it.
- Each bullet has one press, **No**. Pressed, it is red and the line is red. Pressed again, it is taken back.
- The one send reads **Yes, all as told**, green, or **Tell Dispatch: 2 things not as told**, red, with its count.
- After the answer the card says what it kept: *Saved: all as told.* or *Dispatch has it: 1 thing not as told.*, with *Keep working either way.*
- A stage is asked about once, from the first of our days for it. A stage with no days of ours is not asked about.
- A read that fails asks nothing and shows nothing. It reads again by itself when the phone finds its signal or the page comes back to the front.

## What an answer does

- **Yes** turns each bullet into a yes we saw, on arrival, in that person's name.
- **No** takes their word on the bullet back and leaves it open. The office's stage turns red: *Luis got there Nov 20 and it was not as told: The slab is poured.* The bullet's row reads *Luis said No on site, Nov 20. It was not as told.*
- On that red line the office is offered **Add to the company's list for Rough In**, once, for a bullet that is not on it.

## Dispatch's line

A No sends one request down the stage check's road (punch list #245), at high priority, so it leads the inbox under the red wait line and is never drawn as a waiting customer.

- It has no checklist question behind it. Its row and its window carry **Open the list** where a check has **Open the check**: the job window on its Documents tab (`openJobDetail({ initialTab: 'documents' })`).
- The window draws the stage, the bullet and *Ray told us it was ready* from the request's own title.
- Nothing closes it by itself. The window says *Close it once the site is as told, or the list has changed.*, and the person who sent it may close it too.

## Built on defaults, for the owner to flip

Each was an open call on the parked draft.

- It is a standing card in Job Mode, not a question at the clock-in. Job Mode does not know which stage a person is on, so the database picks the stage whose days have come.
- A stage with no days of ours is not asked about.
- The bullet's press is the word **No**, as in the mock-up.
- A failed read is silent and tries again by itself.
