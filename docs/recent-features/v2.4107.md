# v2.4107 — Submittals from the takeoff: tick the fixtures, and Rev 1 is built from the parts already under them (2026-09-29)

**The confusion** (Stephen on BP375 SPACEX BA-02N, 2026-09-29): Takeoffs shows 26 fixtures, 22 costed with parts from a house; Pricing shows 7 of 10 done; Submittals says *0 tags on the schedule · 0 picked lines* and offers only "type or paste the fixture schedule". Submittals sourced rows from two places — `bid_specified_products` (the pasted schedule) and `bid_quote_lines` (the picks on the compare) — and a bid priced from a takeoff has neither, even though the takeoff already names every product. Mock-up (with the pick list, after "is this the best we can do?"): artifact `9vdsYGRj3e9LBQZgWCfmw1`.

**What shipped**

- **Stage 1 is "Where the rows come from"**: three source cards (the takeoff · Pricing picks · the plans' schedule), each with its count and its door; the takeoff's is primary when the bid has no picks and no schedule. The header's source line adds *N fixtures on the takeoff* and, with no other source, links *choose from the takeoff*. The strip's Next line and the Build Rev 1 card offer **Choose from the takeoff** first on such a bid (`submittalJourney.ts` action `choose_from_takeoff`; stage 1 reads done once a revision exists on a bid with takeoff fixtures).
- **The pick list** ([`SubmittalTakeoffPicker.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/SubmittalTakeoffPicker.tsx)): one row per takeoff fixture, in three groups — *Fixtures & equipment* (ticked), *No part on the takeoff yet* (unticked; a ticked one lands Missing to type with Edit), *Pipe, sawcutting and allowances* (unticked; tick one if a GC asks). Under each: the product (the fixture-typed part under it, else the priciest line; a bundle by its template name), the count, and the house behind the part's catalog price. **Tick all with a product**, a live bar (*11 rows will go on Rev 1 · 9 with a product, 2 to type · 15 left out*), and Build / Add. Kernel [`takeoffCandidates.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/takeoffCandidates.ts): `tagsFromFixtureName` reads the tags the way fixtures are named (*WC 1&2* → WC-1, WC-2; *DWH1 & ET* → DWH-1; *LAV2* → LAV-2); `productLineOf`, `takeoffCandidates`, `candidateCounts` / `candidateBar`, `candidateToItemInsert`. Reads in [`takeoffCandidatesIo.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/takeoffCandidatesIo.ts) (`bids_count_rows` — the bid's selected version's rows win over the base rows of the same fixture — `bids_takeoff_rough_part_lines`, `material_parts` + `part_types`, `material_templates`, `material_part_prices` + `supply_houses`).
- **Ticks are remembered**: `bid_submittal_takeoff_choices (bid_id, count_row_id, ticked)` — migration `20260929015024_submittal_takeoff_choices.sql` ([doc](../migrations/20260929015024_submittal_takeoff_choices.md)); upserted on every Build / Add and on ×. Each item carries `bid_submittal_items.source_count_row_id` (ON DELETE SET NULL) so the picker sets aside what is already on the revision.
- **A new status, `proposed`** (blue chip *Proposed*): what we intend to install when there is no schedule to compare against. Threaded through `productStatus.ts`, `ProductStatusChip`, the tiles and header line (`submittalRevision.ts` — *N proposed*), the package cover, the room payload (`RoomRowKind 'proposed'`, ordered right after *differs*, counted open until decided; why sentence *What we intend to install; the plans' schedule was not on the bid to compare against.*) and `SubmittalRoom.tsx` (decided like a differing row). **`get-submittal-room` is redeployed** with the payload change.
- **On a draft**: `+ Add from the takeoff…` beside *+ Add a row by hand*; `×` on every row (confirm dialog) deletes it and, for a takeoff row, writes `ticked = false` on its choice.
- Walkthrough: stage 1's stop reads *Where the rows come from* and a *From the takeoff* stop follows it (15 stops). Guide *build a submittal package* — "Before you build" is now the three sources, the takeoff first.

**Verify**: `npx vitest run src/lib/submittals src/components/bids/BidsSubmittalsTab src/components/bids/SubmittalTakeoffPicker` (the tab test walks the whole flow on a bid with a takeoff and no other source). Live: BP375 → Submittals → stage 1's takeoff card reads *26 fixtures, 22 with a part*.
