# v2.6353 — Rest on a job number on the Development page and a card shows the job (2026-11-02)

The owner, 2026-11-02: *hover over the blue job buttons like "JP1091" and have a modal float off my cursor with the job's full name, address and details*. Every blue job number that opens a job on the Development page is now one component, `JobChip`, which looks like the `LinkButton` it replaces and still opens the job on a press: the Jobs & bids table, the Money look's rows, the Plans look's job chips by part and on no part, a History line and its window, a question's About, and the rows of the count tiles' and the *not in it* chips' cards. Rest the pointer on it and, after a beat, a card floats off the pointer with the job's full name, its address and its details; it follows the pointer along the chip and leaves with it. Client only: no migration, no function change, and nothing is fetched on a hover, since the page's read already carries every column the card names.

- **The kernel `src/lib/developments/jobHoverCard.ts`.** `buildJobHoverCard(job, inputs)` is the card from one `MemberJob` row: the number (the page's `jobLabel`), the full name (or *No name on the job yet*), the address (or none), then the lines in a fixed order: Customer, Stage, Progress (the percent, not on a paid job), Crew (the team members' names; *not scheduled* on a waiting job with none), Last work, and for a reader who sees money Value, Paid, Open on bill and Last bill, then From bid (when the bid is on the site), Made, Old numbers (the HCP and Click numbers a combine folded in, v2.6226). `crewRead` names the jobs whose crew the page read: a job at the address, not in the development, has none read, so its card says nothing about a crew rather than *not scheduled*. `jobHoverCardLookup(jobs, inputs)` is the page's answer to a chip by id, each card built once. `placeHoverCard(point, card, window)` puts the card a gap to the lower right of the pointer, flips it left or up when an edge is near, and clamps it inside the window; `placeHoverCardUnder(chipBox, …)` does the same under the chip for a focus or a hold.
- **The chip `src/components/developments/JobChip.tsx`.** The press is the `LinkButton`'s look (`linkButtonStyle`, now shared from `developmentPageBits.tsx`). Hover reads pointer events and ignores a finger: 150 ms before the card shows, so a pointer crossing the chip draws nothing; `pointermove` re-places it through one `requestAnimationFrame` at a time with no React render; leave hides it at once. The card is `role="tooltip"`, `aria-describedby` on the chip, drawn through a portal at the body at `position: fixed` (a table's scroll box never clips it), `pointer-events: none` (it never stands between the pointer and the press), z 770 (above the dock's 700 and the History line window's 760; the press closes it before any window opens), the `developmentCountCard` shell for its tokens in both themes.
- **Keyboard.** Focus shows the card under the chip, and a press's own focus does not. A scroll re-places a focus card instead of closing it, because a Tab onto a chip past the window's edge scrolls just after the focus event: the first cut closed every card on any scroll, so the card flashed and went on exactly those chips (found with a real Tab in headless Chrome; jsdom never scrolls). A pointer's card and a held card still close on a scroll. Blur, Escape and a click elsewhere close any of them.
- **The phone's door** is the app's own hold, `useLongPress` (the hook the Pipeline's phone row holds with): 450 ms, a finger that travels past 10 px is a scroll, and the card shows under the chip as the finger lifts, with *Tap elsewhere to close. Tap the number to open the job.* A plain tap still opens the job. Only a finger counts as a hold, so a slow mouse press still opens the job and a right press at a desk keeps its menu; the focus a browser gives a tapped press just after the finger lifts draws no card.
- **The provider.** `JobCardsProvider` wraps the page's root with `jobHoverCardLookup` over the rows the page read: the jobs shown and the ones at the address (their chips sit in the *not in it* cards). A chip with no provider, or a job the page does not hold, is the plain press it was. The swaps are small in each file; `DevelopmentPartsCard.tsx` is touched only in its `carriers` helper and one import.
- **Left as they were, on purpose.** Blue job words that are not presses (the pair strip's suggestion, a confirm window's preview), the pair mode's picker rows (a press there pairs, it does not open the job) and bid numbers (the ask was the job buttons).
