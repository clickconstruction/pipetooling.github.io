# v2.6353 — Rest on a job number on the Development page and a card shows the job (2026-11-02)

The owner, 2026-11-02: *hover over the blue job buttons like "JP1091" and have a modal float off my cursor with the job's full name, address and details*. Every blue job number on the Development page — the Jobs & bids table, the Money look's rows, the Plans look's job chips by part and on no part, a History line's About, a question's About — is now one component, `JobChip`, which looks like the `LinkButton` it replaces and still opens the job on a press. Rest the pointer on it and, after a beat, a card floats off the pointer with the job's full name, its address and its details; it follows the pointer along the chip and leaves with it. Client only: no migration, no function change, and nothing is fetched on a hover — the page's read already carries every column the card names.

- **The kernel `src/lib/developments/jobHoverCard.ts`.** `buildJobHoverCard(job, inputs)` is the card from one `MemberJob` row: the number (the page's `jobLabel`), the full name (or *No name on the job yet*), the address (or none), then the lines in a fixed order — Customer, Stage, Progress (the percent, not on a paid job), Crew (the team members' names; *not scheduled* on a waiting job with none), Last work, and for a reader who sees money Value, Paid, Open on bill and Last bill, then From bid (when the bid is on the site), Made, Old numbers (the HCP and Click numbers a combine folded in, v2.6226). `jobHoverCardLookup(jobs, inputs)` is the page's answer to a chip by id, each card built once. `placeHoverCard(point, card, window)` puts the card a gap to the lower right of the pointer, flips it left or up when an edge is near, and clamps it inside the window; `placeHoverCardUnder(chipBox, …)` does the same under the chip for a focus or a hold.
- **The chip `src/components/developments/JobChip.tsx`.** The press is the `LinkButton`'s look (`linkButtonStyle`, now shared from `developmentPageBits.tsx`). Hover: 150 ms before the card shows, so a pointer crossing the chip draws nothing; `mousemove` re-places it through one `requestAnimationFrame` at a time with no React render; leave hides it at once. The card is `role="tooltip"`, `aria-describedby` on the chip, drawn through a portal at the body at `position: fixed` (a table's scroll box never clips it), `pointer-events: none` (it never stands between the pointer and the press), z 760 (above the dock's 700; the press closes it before any window opens), the `developmentCountCard` shell for its tokens in both themes. Keyboard focus shows it under the chip (a mouse press's focus does not: `pointerDown` is read first); blur, Escape, a click elsewhere and a page scroll close it. **The phone's door**: press and hold half a second and the card shows under the chip with *Tap elsewhere to close. Tap the number to open the job.*; the hold's own tap is swallowed once (and a new touch forgets it, so a hold whose tap the browser never sends cannot eat the next one), a tap elsewhere closes, a plain tap still opens the job; the browser's callout and context menu are kept off while a finger is on the chip, and a touch's emulated mouse events within 700 ms are ignored.
- **The provider.** `JobCardsProvider` wraps the page's root with `jobHoverCardLookup` over the rows the page read (`shown.jobs`, `data.names`, `data.teamMembers`, `shown.bids`, the page's labels, `labelJobsLedgerStatusForDashboard`, `money`, `shortDay`, `moneyShown`); a chip with no provider, or a job the page does not hold, is the plain press it was. The chip swaps are one line each: `DevelopmentPieces.tsx` (the Job cell), `DevelopmentMoney.tsx` (the table and the phone's cards), `DevelopmentQuestionsLook.tsx` (an About that is a job), `DevelopmentFeed.tsx` (a line about a job), and the `carriers` helper of `DevelopmentPartsCard.tsx` (the jobs by part and on no part) — the only lines touched in that file, which the papers train (#6762, #6793, #6810) owns.
- **Left as they were, on purpose.** The rows inside the count tiles' cards and the *not in it* chips' cards are already inside a floating card and name the job beside its number; a second card over the first would be clutter. Bid numbers keep the plain press: the ask was the job buttons.
- **Guide** *see everything on a development in one place* → *See a job's details without opening it*.
