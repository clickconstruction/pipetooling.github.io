## What shipped

- **`src/lib/jobs/jobFormPaymentActions.ts`**, built on the tested predicates in `jobFormPaymentPredicates`:
  - `mergePaymentRowUpdate` — an edit to a line; a line on a Stripe bill or matched to a bank deposit keeps its amount, its date and both links.
  - `paymentRowsAfterRemove` — the list after a line leaves the form; a linked line stays, an emptied list is one fresh line.
  - `planPaymentRemoveRequest` — what Remove gets: the confirm, or a refusal (`mercury-linked` · `stripe-bill` · `invoice-linked`) with `paymentRemoveRefusalWords`. A saved line on a plain invoice opens the confirm; an unsaved one is refused.
  - `paymentRemoveWritesNow` — whether confirming writes at once through the RPC. The form had this rule twice (the confirm's copy and the handler).
  - `removePaymentReply` — how `remove_jobs_ledger_payment_and_reconcile` answered: a refusal, a removal with a caveat, or done. The form parsed it three times.
- **The shell** asks the kernel in `updatePaymentRow`, `removePaymentRow`, `requestRemovePaymentRow`, `confirmRemovePaymentRow`, `finishRecordPaymentOnBill` and `executeUnlinkMercuryFromBankRow`; each keeps its calls, toasts and refetch. `JobFormModal.tsx` is 3,902 → 3,849 lines. Behavior-neutral.
- **Tests** — `jobFormPaymentActions.test.ts` (17).
- The map's Stage-A table, the shell-logic row and move 8 say what is done and what is left.
