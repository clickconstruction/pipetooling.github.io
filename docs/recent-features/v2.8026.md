# v2.8026 — Lien desk: Record it… on a notice's months opens its step every time (2026-11-20)

The read-only walk of v2.7989 (punch list #361) on the live desk found that **A paper that went out by hand? Record it…**, the press near the end of **Months on this job**, opened nothing on some notices. Grace's pick of 2026-11-20, given the choice between opening the step there too and leaving the press out: open the step there too. Client only: no migration, no edge function, no kernel.

## What was wrong

The press sets `byHandOpen`, and the step it opens (`byHandPane`, the already-mailed step `LienNoticeAlreadyWent`) draws in the footer's place. Three things kept it from showing:

- **Sent, and Missed with no month due.** Only the draft, awaiting, ready, printed and held footers draw `byHandPane`. The sent footer (`LienDeskSentFooter`) and the missed notice's closed-window line never did, so on those two piles the press did nothing. It has been so since the months card got the press (v2.3770 put the step on four footers and the press on every pile).
- **A computer.** The footer has been the pane's last section since v2.7422. The step opened there, below the paper's pages, so the press on the months card looked dead unless the reader scrolled down to the footer.
- **A phone.** The footer folds behind its bar (`footFolded`). The step's page is drawn inside the footer, so with the footer folded nothing showed until the bar was pressed.

## What changed

- [`LienDesk.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDesk.tsx): on the sent pile the footer is `byHandPane` while it is open, and the sent footer otherwise; the phone's fold bar names the mail proof only while the sent footer is the one drawn. On the missed pile with nothing due, `byHandPane ??` stands before the closed-window line, as the other footers do.
- One effect on `byHandOpen`: opening the step unfolds a phone's footer (`setFootFolded(false)`), and on a computer takes the pane to its last section through `goToSection('sign')`, the same landing the blue head's press makes. It runs for every way the step opens, the footer's own *Already mailed? Record it…* included.
- The step first shows a short box while it reads the property's papers, then grows. A pane cannot scroll past its end, so a landing made on the short box left the grown step under the window's bottom edge (its top at 890 px of a 900 px window, measured). The landing is made again each time the footer grows, for four seconds (a `ResizeObserver` on the footer's home), through a ref that holds the latest `goToSection`, so a later landing glides from where the pane is.
- The step itself, its checks and its writes are unchanged. On a sent or missed notice it touches no desk item, since `byHandPane` already handed the step no item when the notice's item is sent or missed.

## Not changed

- The press's words, the months card and the footers' own doors.
- [`send-lien-notices-from-the-lien-desk.md`](../../src/content/help/send-lien-notices-from-the-lien-desk.md) gains one line: the months card has the same door, and it opens the step on every notice.

## Measured

On the dev server against the live data, every write refused at the network layer, the months card's press was made on three notices: one in Sent, one in Missed with nothing due, and a draft. At 1,440 px on all three, and at 1,068 px on the sent one, the step's top was inside the window (654 px down on the sent and missed notices, 479 on the draft), with its Back and its Apply press on the screen. Before the fix the sent and missed notices opened nothing, and on the others the step opened in the footer below the paper's pages, out of sight. At 375 px the press opened the folded footer and the step's page showed, on the draft and on the sent notice. Nothing was pressed inside the step.

## Tests

`LienDesk.render.test.tsx` (+4, 234 in all): on a sent notice the months card's press opens the step where the sent footer stood and **Back** brings the footer back; on a missed notice with nothing due it opens the step where the closed-window line stood; on a computer the press scrolls the pane, with the step inside the pane's last section; on a phone with the footer folded the press opens the footer and the step's sheet shows. The four fail on the desk as it was before this change.
