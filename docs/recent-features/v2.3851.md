# v2.3851 — Get contracts signed: the stage counts sit in two rows, never more (2026-09-26)

The owner's ask 2026-09-26: the stage-count strip on the Pipeline's *Get contracts signed* card ([`PipelineContractCoverageCard.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/PipelineContractCoverageCard.tsx), segmented text row since v2.3845) should re-size into rows to cut the card's height — then, on seeing the free-wrapping cut, "no more than two lines". Client only, one component and its help sentence.

## What was tall

The strip was an `inline-flex` with `flex-wrap` and no flex basis: its flex-basis was its one-line width and its min-width its longest item, so on anything under ~1200px the flex row could not shrink it and dropped the whole strip under the headline as a full-width row, then the *Start the sweep* button under that — three rows where the text alone needs one.

## The change

- The strip is a **grid of max-content columns**: one row up to three stages, **two rows from four** — `⌈n/2⌉` columns, the first row the fuller (5 stages → `19 waiting · 24 working · 1 ready to bill` over `50 billed · 7 collections`). `contractStageColumns(n)` in [`jobContractNudge.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobContractNudge.ts) is the rule. Its width is its own, so it sits beside the three-line headline at every width; on a phone the button wraps under it, the strip still two rows.
- The `·` separators are gone — a second row would start with one, and the aligned columns segment the counts. Each count is still the same button with the same tooltip and `onStageGap`.
- Two cuts that did not survive the pane: a free-wrapping flex row with `max-width: max-content` (one to three rows by width — the owner wanted a hard two) and an `auto-fit` grid under the same cap (a grid's max-content width counts one column, so it collapsed to five rows). The component's comment records both.
- Help: *get a job contract signed* says the counts sit in two rows beside the headline and reads the chip the way the card prints it (`15 working`, `✓ ready to bill`).

## Verified

`PipelineOverview.render` smoke; `contractStageColumns` (1 → 1, 3 → 3, 4 → 2, 5 → 3, 6 → 3); typecheck; lint. Live on dev against prod at 1280, 900 and the phone preset: two rows beside the headline, the card the headline's height; on the phone the button under a two-row strip.
