# v2.8454 — Lien desk: a missed notice keeps its late send the day after its last window closes (2026-11-24)

Loose end 3 of punch list #415, the Lien desk's leftovers from the ledger (#361). Client only: no migration, no edge function. No rule about a lien right is new here. The rule is v2.4708's: a sub job whose every § 53.056 window closed with nothing sent can still send a late notice while the affidavit's own day is ahead.

## Confirmed, with the inputs

Two readers on one pane said whether a late notice could still go.

- The path (`buildLienTimeline`, Next on the path). It dates the affidavit from the affidavit windows' list when the list holds the job, else from the last month of work.
- The desk's `lateUntil`, which the months card, the gates' fold and the footer read. It looked the job up in the windows' list and nowhere else.

The desk keeps a windows row only within thirty days of its day, or on a job with a started affidavit paper (`lienDeskKeepRows`, v2.7261). The last notice day is a 15th and the affidavit's day is the next month's 15th, each moved to Monday off a weekend. So the day after the last window closes, the affidavit's day is 31 or 32 days off whenever its 15th falls on a weekend. On those days the list had no row.

The smallest case, on made-up rows: a commercial sub job last worked in January 2027, nothing sent, no affidavit drafted. The notice day is Thursday Apr 15, 2027. May 15 is a Saturday, so the affidavit's day is Monday May 17. On Friday Apr 16 it is 31 days off, and the pane read:

- the path: *Send the Jan notice late, then file the affidavit — 31 days*
- Months on this job: *lien right gone · the money still rides*
- the footer's head: *Window closed · Nothing to sign*, with no send
- the gates: folded to *Window closed Apr 15*

On Saturday Apr 17 the day was 30 off, the list held the job, and the card read *a late notice can still go · the affidavit by May 17* again.

A sweep of every work month from 2025 to 2030 on the three clocks, every day from the notice day to two days past the affidavit's (7,005 days), found the two apart on 69 days, never more than two running, always the same way round. The days ahead were 2027-04-16, 2027-07-16, 2027-12-16 and 17. It was never seen live: no notice was in the late window on such a day.

## Which is right

The path. The affidavit's last day does not depend on a list that looks thirty days ahead. v2.4708 says the late notice can go while that day is ahead, and the billed row, the Deadlines board and the Dashboard's card already said so on those days.

## What changed

- [`lienTimeline.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienTimeline.ts): the timeline answers `affidavitDay`, the day its path counts to while nothing is filed. It is the windows' day when they list the job, else the last month's own filing day, and empty once an affidavit is filed.
- [`lienPaneSections.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienPaneSections.ts): `lienLateNoticeUntil` is the desk's late-window rule as a kernel. The rule is word for word the one `LienDesk.tsx` held inline: no month still due, no month a notice names, a closed month to claim, no affidavit filed, the day not passed. Only the day's source moved.
- [`LienDesk.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDesk.tsx): the timeline is built above the late window, and `lateUntil` is `lienLateNoticeUntil` over its `affidavitDay`. The windows' own word that an affidavit is filed is kept beside the path's.

## Not changed

The rule. The path, the billed row, the Deadlines board and the Dashboard. A job the windows list reads the same day as before, since the path takes the list's day first. A missed notice whose affidavit day has passed still reads that the lien right is gone. A job with an older notice on record is not a late notice, as before.
