The change order's and the $0 total's ask stay as they were, since the rail's decision on silent $0 sends holds.
- **The sheet's estimate moment** (`TellCustomerSheet.tsx`, `tellCustomer.ts`, `tellCustomerIo.ts`): the customer alone is read (`loadCustomer`, no job), the office's team line on the **Estimate sent** step (`stepLine`, step `estimate`) else the app's own, filled with `{first}`, `{me}`, `{job}` (the estimate's title when the job has no name) and `{amount}` (the total, `formatCurrency`); **`{link}` stays in braces in the box** and never holds the press (`blanksLeftIn` with the moment), since the link exists only once the email went. *What goes*: the email ticked with its address and *The estimate with its link to approve, as the page previews it*; the text ticked with the number when it can go. The presses: **Skip**, **Email only**, **Send both to Dana**; with the text held (no number, STOP, texts off), the hold row says why and the primary reads **Send email to Dana**. The press runs the page's email send first; on its answer the text goes with the link filled (`fillLine(text, { link })`), tied to the estimate's job when it has one, else on the person; a text that fails leaves the email standing and says so; no link back means no text and a toast. The receipt names the email (`emailSentLine`), the text and the script for the estimate (`DEFAULT_SCRIPTS.estimate`).
- **The customer's side**: the homeowner journey gains **Estimate text** after the estimate email, with its sample (`text-estimate`, the accept link filled), the registry row, the real-customer answer; the guide *send an estimate and turn it into a job* § 2 rewritten for the sheet.
