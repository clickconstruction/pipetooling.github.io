# v2.8341 — Lien desk on a phone: every To do row has one button at the right, the same size (punch list #428) (2026-11-23)

The owner asked to see the Lien desk's To do on a phone, the screen the Dashboard's **The Board ›** opens, and whether it should be better. A look by hand found the screen opens cleanly and its rows are uneven. He had set a rule for the Dashboard's Needs you rows the same day: on the left a person sees what to do, on the right what to press, and the presses are one size. His go, 2026-11-23: *Awesome, I like it, let's do it. Please build everything we talked about today.* This PR is the rows and the tiles. Client only: no migration and no function. Card: [`to-dos/lien-to-do-on-a-phone/README.md`](../../to-dos/lien-to-do-on-a-phone/README.md), with its mock-up.

A computer's rows and tiles are as they were. Every change here is behind `isMobile`.

## What a phone's row was, and is

| Piece | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| The press | As wide as its words, 69 to 241 px, on a line of its own under the row's words, 40 px tall | One column at the right, 116 px wide and 44 px tall on every row, 100 px wide at 360 px and under. Longer words wrap inside it |
| A second press (*Not lienable…*, *Take back…*, a row's other doors) | A second button beside the first. It took the right side and the main press wrapped to a line of its own at the left | A quiet press under the words, in the link colour, 32 px tall. The press column holds one press on every row |
| What the job owes | Beside the job's name | On the words' last line, at the right, after the paper's chip and the dots |
| A run's list of jobs | Between the row's words and its press | The row's last line, across both columns, so an open list has the row's whole width |
| *No day set* | A heading after each pool's days, so twice in a list with such a row in both, under one key | One group, the list's last |

## The three shorter presses

A press in a 116 px column holds two lines of words. Three presses ran to three: *Answer the homestead question*, *Open the property record* and *Fix the property record*. On a phone they read *Answer it*, *Open the record* and *Fix the record*. The row's line above each already names the question or the record, and the press keeps its whole words as its name for a screen reader and as its title. Each is matched whole, so *Open the property record at the owner*, a door's words on the Board, is not touched. Every other press keeps its words and wraps.

## The Board's tiles on a phone

- **The first tile's label is two lines.** It read *by Dec 15 · 9 to file · 8 to send* in one run, which wrapped with one word alone on its last line. The day is the label and its parts are a muted line under it (`data-lien-board-tile-parts`).
- **A tile's door ends the line it acts on.** The second tile's *Show them ›* stood at the tile's top right while the first tile's stood at the foot, after its red line. On a phone the second tile reads *7 to sign · Show them ›* and a third tile that is a press reads *1 to print · 6 to mail · Open the run ›*. The door keeps `data-lien-board-tile-door` and gains `data-place="line"`.

What a tile counts, and what its press opens, did not change.

## How

- [`src/lib/jobs/lienPhonePress.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienPhonePress.ts) is the kernel: the column's two widths, the 44 px and 32 px heights, `lienPhoneRowColumns()` and `lienPhonePressWords()`. The widths are mirrored by `.lienPhoneRow` in `src/index.css`, since an inline style cannot ask the screen's width, and the kernel's test reads the stylesheet to hold the two together.
- [`src/components/jobs/LienDeskNextUp.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskNextUp.tsx): the phone row is a grid of two columns, `data-layout="columns"` where it was `lines`. The words are `data-lien-next-up-words`, their last line `data-lien-next-up-meta`, the quiet presses `data-lien-next-up-quiet`. Every hook the row had is kept. The press on the waiting rows' head, *Remind … of N papers*, is a head's press and is as it was.
- [`src/lib/jobs/lienPhoneRows.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienPhoneRows.ts): `lienPhoneGroups` pools the `day:none` groups into one and puts it last.
- [`src/lib/jobs/lienBoardPhoneTile.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienBoardPhoneTile.ts) splits the day's label, and `Tile` in `LienBoard.tsx` places the door.
