# v2.2123 — Split hygiene: clones keep the source scenario's name; no ☆ on unpriced scenarios (2026-08-23)

F4 of the "Send or Compare" train (decision D4). Two small truths:

- **Migration `20260823030710_create_bid_version_clone_keeps_source_name.sql`** — `create_bid_version` (used by the first split and every "Another bid to send…") clones the chosen pricing under the **source scenario's name** (`COALESCE(NULLIF(source.name,''), p_name)`) instead of the new version's name. A version is a bid; a scenario is a price point — they shouldn't share a word (8 bids in prod had both a version and a scenario called "Value Engineered"). The RPC also stamps the new version's ★ (`bid_versions.starred_price_book_version_id`, v2.2117) to the clone. `CREATE OR REPLACE`, same signature, `lock_timeout 3s`.
- `BidVersionPicker`: the split / new-version modal's checkbox reads "Start its prices from this bid's ★ — **WENDI** stays named WENDI" (new optional prop `pricingSourceNames`, fed from `Bids.tsx` with bid pricings + templates).
- `BidsPricingTab` Workbench: scenario cards with **no prices yet** hide "☆ Make customer-facing…" (both card variants — `!isCustomerFacing && !unpriced`). The existing "Copy prices from …" affordance on the viewed empty scenario (v2.2013) is the path forward.

Out of scope, on purpose: the **Old** grid's price-book dropdown still clones a template into the bid as a scenario (named after the template) and makes it the working pricing — that is Old's "pick a price book" workflow, and it's where BP329's Default / Joseph / RSmeans / Trace scenarios came from. The Workbench never auto-creates scenarios.

Verified live on BP329 (unpriced cards show no ☆; "Another bid to send…" modal names the source scenario). Help: `price-a-bid-with-the-workbench.md`.
