# v2.7569 — Sub portal: the groundwork for a Statements view on a sub's page (2026-11-17)

Punch list #371, step 1 of four: the function. The owner answered the card's seven calls on 2026-11-17, each with the mock-up's pick, so the build could start. Nothing on the sub's page reads the new block yet, and `sub-portal` is deployed only on a go that names it.

## Why

Card #350 gave the office three papers for a sub: a work statement per sheet, a payment record per sending and a statement of account. A sub could not find them on their own page. The page's function handed over open sheets only and 90 days of payment lines, with no method, no number and nothing tying one check's lines together.

## What changed

- **A `papers` block on the read.** `sub-portal` hands back every sheet that is the sub's alone, paid ones too, as the papers' kernel reads a sheet (`PaperSheet`), with the letterhead from Settings, *Still open* to date, the answers already given and how many shared sheets were left out. The shaping is `supabase/functions/_shared/subPortalPapers.ts`, pure.
- **The same sheets the office builds.** `portalPaperSheets` builds from the function's rows what `paperSheetsFor` builds from the Pay view's. `subPortalPapers.test.ts` puts the same rows through both and expects one set of papers, to the fingerprint.
- **Whose a sheet is.** `sheetParties`, `teamSheetRule`, `rosterSub` and `laborJobPersonMatch` moved under `supabase/functions/_shared/`, so the function reads the office's own rule. Their old paths re-export them and no screen's import changed. The name key is restated there as `sheetNameKey`, held to `normalizePersonNameKey` by a test, so the kernels' copy for the other functions did not change.
- **A number is read, never given** (call 2). The block reads a sheet's live number and revision from `sub_papers` and says whether today's figures are the ones that copy was issued with (`numbers[sheet].asIssued`, by the register's own fingerprint, now in `_shared/subPaperMarks.ts`). Nothing in the function registers a paper or calls the mint. A voided paper gives the sheet no number.
- **Checked first.** The three papers are built and checked against each other before anything is handed over. A set that does not add up comes back as `{ ok: false, officePhone }`, with no figure.
- **A memo the office hid never leaves the function.** The amount always shows.
- **Fail-soft.** The block's reads are its own. A refused read leaves `papers: null` and the rest of the page exactly as it was. Each read is paged, so a long history does not stop at the API's row cap.
- **Two answers, on a POST.** `action: 'got_it'` on a payment and `action: 'something_wrong'` on a statement or a payment, with the link's token in the body. Each writes one row of `sub_paper_acks`: the person, the sheet, sending or payment, the figure they saw, their name and the address it came from, and the office's paper and revision where there is one. A second *Got it* on one payment writes nothing and reads the first day back. A note is 1,000 characters at most, and a page sends ten a day at most.
- **The office cannot answer for a sub.** A signed-in teammate or a preview of the page is refused. So is a sample page, an unknown link and any other action: Confirm is not built here.
- **Migration `20261117150000_sub_paper_ack_targets.sql`.** An answer names its person and its sheet, sending or lone payment, and no longer needs a paper in the register. One unique index per target keeps a payment marked received once.

## The design point the card left open

An answer used to hang on a paper in the register, and a payment or a sheet the office has not papered has no row there. The two ways were the page registering its own copy, or the answer kept on the thing itself. The first was dropped: a work statement registered from the page would hold the place the office's first number goes, which is the page giving out a number.

## Not in this step

- The switch, the list, the PDFs and the words in both languages are step 2. The owner reads the Spanish words before they ship (call 5).
- The two presses, their one-question sheets and the office's chip and flag are step 3.
- An earlier copy of a changed statement is not handed over. A memo hidden after that copy was made would still be in it, so it needs a rule of its own.
- Confirm stays off. It waits on counsel.

## Deploy

1. Merge. The client does not change what a sub or the office sees.
2. `supabase db push` for `20261117150000_sub_paper_ack_targets.sql`.
3. `supabase functions deploy sub-portal`, only on a go that names it. Before the push, an answer would be refused by the table and the read would show no answers. The figures do not depend on it.
