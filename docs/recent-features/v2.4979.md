## What moved

**`src/components/schedule/ScheduleDispatchModeBanners.tsx`** (new, 348 lines) holds 272 lines of JSX from `ScheduleDispatchHubPage.tsx`, verbatim, as two components:

- **`ScheduleDispatchModeBanners`**: the three strips above the board, each hidden while the phone board is up:
  - moving or copying a block, with the move's day chips
  - the two-stage linked copy
  - placing a job from `?placeJob=`
- **`ScheduleDispatchMultiCellBar`**: the floating Choose job for multi-cell add bar.

The page mounts each one where its JSX was: the strips above the `DndContext`, and the bar after the share window. So the page's markup keeps its order.

The page still owns every mode and every handler, and the components only draw them and call back. Their props are what the moved JSX read:

- the mode states and `phoneBoardActive`
- the source block, the visible days and the title lookup
- the page's own callbacks
- the three state setters the Cancel and Done buttons call, typed to take only `null`

`ScheduleDispatchHubPage.tsx` goes from 2,184 to 1,935 lines, and `buildMoveDayChips` left its imports.

## Tests

`ScheduleDispatchModeBanners.render.test.tsx` (new, 10), on the real components:

- nothing draws with no mode on
- every strip hides while the phone board is up
- moving:
  - the strip names the block and its window
  - there is a chip per day, with the block's own day disabled
  - a chip moves the block to that day
- moving a block that is off the board reads "this block" and has no chips
- copying:
  - a linked copy names its day and a solo copy does not
  - Cancel clears the placement and the + menu
- linked copy, step 1: Next waits for a block and then goes to step 2, and Cancel ends it
- linked copy, step 2:
  - it counts the copies (one copy, three copies) and says when it is applying
  - Back goes to step 1 and Done ends it
- placing a job names it, and Cancel hands back to the page
- the bar shows only while cells are being picked and the picker is shut, waits for a cell, counts the cells and opens the picker

Mutations fail them:

- the linked-copy strip shown on the phone board
- linked and solo swapped
- a chip that moves to the wrong day
- a Cancel that leaves the + menu open
- a Next that never waits
- a Done that goes back instead
- copy and copies swapped
- a bar that ignores the open picker
- a bar that counts nothing
