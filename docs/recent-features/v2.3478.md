# v2.3478 — Job window → Bill → Invoices: one list, every bill, three lines each (2026-09-15)

Client only — no RPC, no migration. The Invoices table under ② on the Bill tab was a four-column `<table>` (Status / Date / Amount / Actions) that hid paid bills, truncated the payer chip to "→ R…" in a 15 % column, labelled statement-sharing with a bare 👁, wrapped six equal-weight actions across three lines, repeated the memo/footer boilerplate under every row, and — because it carried `minWidth: 480` — scrolled sideways inside the 346 px phone sheet with the Actions column off-screen. Mock-up: <https://claude.ai/artifact/A8LFiqPAd6jmpbvLSeEdbx>.

## What changed

- **Every bill is listed** — drafts, open, and paid (`status = paid` was filtered out before), sorted draft → open → paid, oldest first within a state. A **sum line** under the list reads *paid · open · = billed* (plus *to bill* for drafts and *+ $X on no bill* for unapplied payments) and uses the tiles' math (payments, not invoice status), so the list and the Paid / Billed tiles above it agree.
- **One row grammar** — three lines per bill, at every width:
  1. chip · amount · actions
  2. *sent Sep 4 to RMC-Dudley Mason* (+ the Stage-Plan draw label / *auto remainder* / *👁 shown to …* as a muted tail)
  3. *$9,800 open · 21 d past expected · They said Sep 19* — a paid row reads *$8,000 paid · Jun 4 · 22 days*, a draft *$17,800 to bill*
- **The action follows the money** — draft: Send bill… · Bill to ▾; open Stripe bill: the labelled **Text · Copy link · Email** cluster (new `labeledCluster` variant of `StripeInvoiceSharePanel`; Email dimmed with the "add a customer email" hint as its title instead of a line of prose) + View; paid: View. Everything rare or destructive moved under a per-row **⋯** menu: Add discount (line item on a draft / credit-note write-down on a sent bill), ⚡ Make Stripe bill, See in Pipeline, Show memo & footer (folded by default; a hand-sent bill's note is the line that varies), the *Who else sees this bill* radio group (was the 👁 chip menu), Delete draft, Send back (last, red). Every handler is unchanged.
- **Expected pay on the bill it's about** — the Stages card's `ExpectedPayModel` (customer/company median or the customer's promise) now renders on each open bill via the new `useJobBilledExpectedPay` hook (same role gate, same two RPCs, fail-soft).
- **Container query, not viewport** — `.jobInvoiceLedger` in `index.css` is an inline-size container; below 420 px the chase cluster / Send bill leave the head line and become a full-width 44 px row after the words, View and ⋯ grow to 44 px, the header hides, the sum line left-aligns. The Job window is capped at 560 px on every screen and is a full-bleed sheet at ≤ 640 px, so the list only ever has ~530 px or ~346 px — the viewport can't tell you which.
- **Honest about stubs** — a row stamped paid with no payment behind it (4 in prod, all Feb–Mar 2026, $37k — pre-billing-model) reads *marked paid · no payment on record* and counts for nothing in the sum. J258's $8,900 March row is one.

## Kernel

[`src/lib/jobs/invoiceLedgerRow.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/invoiceLedgerRow.ts) — `invoiceLedgerRow()` turns an invoice + its payments + who it bills + the expected-pay model into `{ state, amount, paid, whoLine, whoNote, moneyLead, moneyDetail, moneyTone, promise }`; `compareInvoiceLedgerRows()` orders; `invoiceLedgerTotals()` sums. Customers never part-pay, so state is binary and there is no per-row bar; a short payment (never in practice) reads *$X short* rather than being a fourth state. 13 kernel tests + 7 render smokes ([`JobFormInvoiceList.render.test.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormInvoiceList.render.test.tsx), rewritten for the ⋯ menu).

Live-verified on J258 (Dudley Mason) at 560 and 375: the three rows, the ⋯ menu, the dimmed Email, the sum line matching the tiles; phone measured by computed style — 346 px ledger, 44 px chase buttons, no row wider than its container. Re-verified 2026-09-18 on production data (a dev session against prod; the Pages bundle confirmed to carry `jobInvoiceLedger`): J258's three rows in order, *24 d past expected* moving a day a day, the ⋯ menu's six items, Email dimmed; J813's *They said Sep 15* green; the 375 px sheet (346 px ledger, 44 px buttons, 2 × 2 tiles, no sideways scroll); dark theme legible. The Ready-to-Bill draft row was not exercised — no draft invoice existed in prod that day. Gotcha hit: `border: 'none'` + `borderTop` on the menu items left a 1 px top edge on every item (the React shorthand gotcha) — longhands only.
