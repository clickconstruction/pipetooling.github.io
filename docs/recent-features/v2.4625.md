# v2.4625 — Legal portal: the law firm reads its own words (2026-10-05)

Client plus one shared email builder (`_shared/legalEmails.ts`: redeploy `legal-notify-dispatch` and `submit-legal-portal` after merge so the emails carry the new words); no migration. Item 3 of punch list #85 ([`to-dos/legal-portal-firm-ready/`](../../to-dos/legal-portal-firm-ready/README.md)). Mockup: `mockup-3-firm-vocabulary.html` beside the card — five panels before and after, and every phrase in one table.

## The defect

The firm's portal spoke the office's language. The toast after any act said *Saved — the office sees it on their Needs You list*, a list the firm has never seen, and the same line followed adding a person on Notifications. The page named a hard-coded brand (*From Click*, *Click's particulars for filing*, *What Click did, in order*, *pay Click direct*), and so did the footer of every email to the firm, whatever `companyName` the sender passed. The third tab was *Their word*; its intro announced entries *the office chose to share*. The stage chip read *new* and *judgment* while the step picker said *Judgment entered*. The Fees & steps tables printed raw kinds (*fee*, *recovery applied*, *ask · question*, *billed*, *collections*). Notifications read *Tell me · Right away* and *waiting for their click*. The referred email said the office *marked … attorney-ready and released it*; the pull-back arrived as *Pulled back: X*; the digest printed the raw stage key.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **Company name or "the office"?** The portal already calls the people the firm deals with *the office* (*Ask the office*, *From the office* on the firm's packet), so that stays. Where a line names the party to the claim, it takes the payload's company name: the record of contact, a direct payment, the email footer. Nothing names a brand the payload did not send.
- **One toast for every act?** No. One line cannot be true for a fee, a step, a payment and a new person at once. Each act says what happens next, from one kernel with a test; a step names the new stage so the firm sees it took.
- **"Record of contact" or "What they said"?** *Record of contact*: it matches section C of the firm's print packet (item 1), and it covers what the office did, a visit or an unanswered email, which *What they said* would leave out.
- **Stage words in one home?** Yes, in the shared email builder, so the browser and the sender cannot drift. The office's labels stay where they are.
- **Announce held entries?** No. Item 29 shares everything by default; the intro says what the record is.
- **"Right away" was plain. Why change it?** Its label *Tell me* read as a chat prompt. *Emails: Each event / Weekly digest* names the setting and its unit. The desk keeps *right away* on its own list.
- **Rename the packet's exhibit title?** Not in the kernel: the office desk and the firm print packet (item 1) key on it. The firm's view maps it with `firmExhibitTitle`.

## Left out, and why

- The matter card's *theory* line, the Jobs table's *Basis*, the Paper tab's sworn-account column and the *Terms with Click* row are item 4 (facts, not theories).
- The owner's-answers band keeps counsel's pile letters: they are counsel's own short call, each with its statute.
- The desk keeps every label it had (`LegalDeskModal.tsx`), and Settings keeps *Click's particulars for filing* as its heading.
