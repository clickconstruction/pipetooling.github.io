## What the owner chose

The mock-up drew three places for the line: the Pulled line, the city picker and the count strip. The owner, 2026-11-18: *"Build option B."* That is the city picker.

## What changed

- **A kernel, `src/lib/research/permitQuiet.ts`.** `newestPermitDay` takes the newest of a city's submitted and issued days and never one after today. `cityQuiet` answers `fresh`, `quiet`, `ours` or `unknown` from that day, today and the city's last pull. `cityPickerWords` and `cityPickerTitle` write the picker's words.
- **The allowance is per city.** `quietAfterDays` gives a daily city 7 days and San Antonio 10. A daily city reaches 5 or 6 days back on the Monday after Thanksgiving. San Antonio's widest normal gap is 9.
- **Quiet is the city's silence, never ours.** A city reads `quiet` only when the pull ran with no error inside 48 hours. A pull that stopped, never ran or could not be read answers `ours`, and the picker shows the newest day with no *quiet*.
- **The picker.** `usePermitCityQuiet` reads the five cities one at a time after the lists are in, as the skim's nudge does. Each city is two one-row reads of `permits` through `loadNewestPermitDay` and the existing `loadLatestPull`. A city that cannot be read keeps its bare name. The reads run again after **Pull now**.
- **The look.** On a desk every row carries its day. The box is amber while the city on screen is quiet, and its hover says why. On a phone the chip names the day only for a quiet city, and widens from 150 to 260 px for it.
- **The option values are still the city names**, so `?city=` and the remembered city are unchanged.

No migration and no edge function change.
