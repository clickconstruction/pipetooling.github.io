# v2.8309 — In kind: a GC's statement and GC Review leave out a bill an arrangement pays (punch list #301, the owner's call) (2026-11-22)

The sweep of the in kind readers stopped at one call it could not make: what a GC's statement, GC Review and the customer's portal say about a bill a deal pays. The owner answered on 2026-11-22. This is the GC half. The portal follows in its own PR.

## The owner's answers

1. **A GC's statement** takes the bill off its list and its total, and says one line: how much is scheduled in kind.
2. **The portal** keeps the bill, reads it as scheduled, with no pay link and not in the amount owed. Not built here.
3. **The staff screens** follow the rule the other staff screens got: out of late counts and the call sheet, with one line.
4. **The words** an outside reader sees are *scheduled in kind*, the same words the office reads.

## What changed

- **The payload** (migration `20261123010000_gc_statement_in_kind_apart.sql`). `get_gc_statement_email_payload` keeps a bill on a job with `in_kind_arrangement_id` off `rows` and off every sum of the group and the grand total. Such bills ride beside them in `in_kind_rows`, the same shape, with `in_kind` as their count and dollars by the function's own netting.
- **The count by the payment rule** (`supabase/functions/_shared/gcStatementPayload.ts`). `setInKindApart` nets each of those bills as `applyPaymentRule` nets a money bill, sets each group's `in_kind` and the payload's, and drops a group left with no money bill. `payloadJobIds` and `attachJobTotals` reach the deal's jobs, so the rule has their totals.
- **The dispatcher and the gate.** `gc-statement-email-dispatch` and `_shared/gcStatementGateIo.ts` run `setInKindApart` after the rule. A payer with nothing but deal bills reads *nothing outstanding*, and the week's check compares the money bills alone.
- **The statement's line** (`_shared/gcStatementByProperty.ts`). `statementInKindLine` words it, *$1,200 on 2 bills is scheduled in kind, not money owed.*, and the statement prints it once under **Total owed**, in HTML and in text. Every lane calls that module, so Draft Message, Preview, Copy for email and the scheduled send read alike.
- **GC Review** (`src/lib/gcReviewRollup.ts`). `buildGcReviewRollup` leaves such a row out of its group, its subtotal, its job count and its age, and counts it in the group's `inKind` and the rollup's. The window's foot says the line. Everything that reads a group's rows follows with no change of its own: the check of the week, the worklist, the call sheet, the unpaid bills print and the money moves.
- **The whole report and the printed sheet** say the line for the sections they print (`gcGroupsInKind`), on the client and in the dispatcher.

## Decisions taken with the build

- **Set apart at the source.** The RPC and the rollup drop the bill, so a reader that knows nothing of the deal cannot show it as owed. The office's statement week reads the same payload, so its amounts, its *over 90 days* and its $10,000 line leave the bill out with no change to its own function.
- **The rows ride along, the count is not trusted from SQL.** The RPC nets a bill by its linked payments. The statement nets by the one payment rule, which also places money put on the job with no bill picked. A credit entered on a job with no bill row would make the two differ, so the dispatcher counts from the rows.
- **A cent or less open is settled.** Such a bill is left out and not counted, as `splitInKind` counts everywhere else.
- **Include Collections does not count a deal's bill** in its label, since the switch would not add it to the total.

## Not here

- **The statement week's one line.** Its amounts are right after the migration. It does not yet say how much it left out.
- **The portal.** It still shows the bill as owed with a pay link until its own PR.

## Release order

1. Merge.
2. Push the migration: `supabase db push`.
3. Deploy `gc-statement-email-dispatch` and `send-gc-statement-email`, then `npm run check:edge-drift`.

Between the merge and the push, GC Review leaves the bill out and the server still lists it, so a GC with a deal bill would read *changed since it was checked*. No GC has a bill on a deal today. Between the push and the deploy, the scheduled statement leaves the bill out with no line.

The existing parity, gate, dispatch and send tests pass unchanged.
