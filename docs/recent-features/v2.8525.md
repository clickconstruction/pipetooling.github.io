# v2.8525 — Lien desk: no release before the bank clears the check (2026-11-24)

Piece 1 of punch list #437 (`to-dos/held-check/README.md`), built on the owner's *Yes, build it* the day the plan was drawn. The owner's rule: a check counts toward a lien's release once it clears, and the office is told to release then, not before. Pieces 1 to 3 of #435 only warned while a check cleared, in amber, and offered the release anyway.

## What the bank says, and what "cleared" means

The bank's own row holds a deposited check (`mercury_transactions`, kind `checkDeposit`). Reading the live rows showed the bank does two things on two days:

- It **posts** the deposit, usually within a day or three (`posted_at`, status `sent`).
- It **names a later day**, about a week after the deposit (`raw.estimatedDeliveryDate`). No code read that day before.

Every return the returned-check work counted came back within six days of posting, which is the same week. So the plan's first draft, *cleared is the day the bank posts it*, would have offered a release days before the day the owner was told. The rule built here is the careful one: **a check has cleared when the bank has posted it and the bank's own day has come.** A posted row with no such day waits seven days from its posting (`CHECK_CLEAR_DAYS`). Whether the earlier day would do is the owner's call 1 on the card.

## The rule

[`checkCleared.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/checkCleared.ts), pure, 18 tests:

- `bankCheckAnswer` reads one bank row as *at the bank*, *late* (the bank's day passed and it is not posted), *posted* (it can still come back), *cleared*, *unread* (the deposit failed with no reason from the paying bank) or *came back* (the paying bank's reason, or a failure after posting, by `bankReturnedDeposits.ts`).
- `bankCheckForPayment` finds a recorded payment's deposit: the one Accounts Receivable linked (`mercury_transaction_id`), else the newest deposit with the same check number, for at least the payment's amount, within sixty days of its day. Numbers compare without leading zeros, so *01184* is *1184*.
- `paymentCheckAnswer` answers for a payment. Anything but a check has cleared. A check with no deposit to be found waits seven days from the day typed, as before.
- `checkAnswerWords` writes what the bank says in plain words with its values marked.

The read is [`checkClearedIo.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/checkClearedIo.ts): sixty days of check deposits and any older one a payment is linked to. The office seats read those rows under the policies they already have. No migration, and nothing new is written.

## Where it holds

- **Payoffs** (`lienPayoff.ts`): a lien whose bills read paid by a check that has not cleared stands on a new step, `bank`. Its row reads *Paid Nov 24 by check 1184.* and, in amber, *Check 1184 is at the bank. The bank says it clears Dec 2. The release waits until then.* In the press's place it reads **Waits for the bank**. A check that came back is red, with **Open the bills ›**. The place's headline and the desk's line count the waiting liens.
- **The Release of record tab** (`lienReleaseOfRecord.ts`): `releaseMoneyGate` holds the print and the save on such a check, and also while the bank is being asked and when its read failed, so a slow or a failed read never lets a release out early. **Release it anyway…** lifts it with a typed reason, saved as `fields.releasedBeforeClearedReason`. Who may do that is unchanged, and is the owner's call 4 on the card.
- **The check pane** (`lienPayoffCheck.ts`): `payoffCheckBank` matches the check typed to the bank's deposit by its number and its amount to the cent. A check the bank has not cleared makes the press dead, with the day it may be recorded. A check with no deposit is recorded as before, with a line that its release waits seven days.

## What tells the office on the day

- **The place says so.** `lienPlaceCheck` finds a deposit of the last 21 days for exactly what a place's liens' open bills come to, or that plus the money open there with no lien. The place then reads *Check 01184 for $16,455.50 is at the bank. The bank says it clears Dec 2. Nothing is recorded until then.* Once the check is recorded the bills no longer owe that sum, and the line goes.
- **The desk's line turns amber** the day it clears: *1 check has cleared for liens on record. Record it.* The place reads *The bank cleared check 01184 for $16,455.50 on Dec 2. It is ready to record.*
- **Nobody types the check.** The check pane opens on the amount, the number and the deposit day the bank holds.
