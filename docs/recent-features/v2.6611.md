# v2.6611 — A partial payment pauses the lien reminders, and the desk asks what they promised (2026-11-06)

Punch list #274, PR 3, on v2.6596's timeline. Grace's board of 2026-11-06, section 1b: a payment is an event, so it is a row (PR 2); a partial payment is an answer, so it pauses the reminders (this PR). Built on the card's two defaults, since no answer from Taunya was on record when it was picked up: the pause runs until the promised day when one is given, else a week after the payment, and the attorney's letter never goes to someone who is paying by the desk's hand. Each is one line to change; Taunya kept both on 2026-11-06 (`to-dos/owner-decisions-pending.md`).

## What changed

- **`lienCourtesyNotice.ts`**: a new state `paying` between `affidavit_week` and `sent` in `courtesyStatus`, read by `courtesyPause` from the job's payments on or after day 0 (the letter's clock) that leave money owed: the pause ends on the promised day when one falls after the payment (the job's `list_job_promised_pay_dates` date or the one on the sent item, read alike), else `COURTESY_AGAIN_DAYS` after the newest payment; on that day the clocks run again. Words *paying it down · $4,000 paid Nov 3 · $8,045 still owed*, and *· the rest promised by Nov 20* when it is. `status.paying` carries the payment, the remainder, `until`, `promisedYmd` and the promise. `courtesyIsDue` stays false and `courtesyMaySend` false while paying; `courtesyFooterSentence` gains the case. The promise on the sent item is `fields.promise` (`CourtesyPromise { at, by, ymd, amount, note }`, `parsePromiseRecord`, `withPromiseRecord`); the day also goes on the job through `addJobPaymentPromise`, so the Stages board, the forecast and the desk's pre-send hold read one promise. `addDaysYmd` moved here from the timeline (re-exported there).
- **`lienCourtesyDesk.ts`**: `courtesyByJobFrom` takes the desk's `paymentsByJob` and `promisesByJob` (both optional; the AR chips' reader passes them too) and the first packet's promise.
- **`lienReminders.ts`**: `paying` on step 2 after the closed, in-flight, held and affidavit's-week branches; `lienReminderWhy` reads *paying it down*; the ladder card's second rung reads *Paying it down*; `lienReminderBrief(step, state)` returns the payment's brief for a paying notice on step 2 (usual *Record what they promised*; instead *Send the attorney's letter · anyway, when the payment was token*); rung 2's rule names the press.
- **`lienNextUp.ts`**: a `promise` action with the button *Record what they promised* leads the sent notice's row while paying, amber until a promise is on record, under Reminders (`isLienReminderRow`). **`lienNextUpSteps.ts`**: the row stands on the REMINDERS rung; its beside-card; `lienStepBrief(on, state)` hands the picked notice's state through.
- **`lienNoticeTimeline.ts`**: facts gain `pauseUntil`, `promisedYmd`, `promisedAmount`; the last payment row's next line reads *reminders paused until Nov 10*; the letter's window reads *waits* while they pay; their promise is a row (*They promised the rest, $8,045, by this day* · *promised · in 7 days* · *on Nov 13 the desk asks again*, or *promise passed* once it is); today reads *They are paying it down. The attorney's letter waits while they pay.*
- **`LienDeskModal.tsx`**: the courtesy map and the timeline take the payments and the promises; the sent footer's sentence covers paying, a *Record what they promised…* door (amber until recorded, then *They promised again…*) opens an inline form — the day, the amount, their words — whose *Record it ▸* writes the job's promise and the notice's record (`notePromiseOnNotice` in `lienDeskIo.ts`), and *They promised: …* reads back; the attorney's letter door is plain, not amber, while paying with a title saying why. The Do now row's press opens the same form on its notice. **`LienDeskNextUp.tsx`** passes the picked notice's rung state to the brief.

## What it never does

Move the affidavit's week or the lien's day, send anything by itself, or hold a courtesy the office sends by hand. No migration: the promise's day rides the job's existing `set_job_promised_pay_date`, and the notice's record is a key on its `fields`.
