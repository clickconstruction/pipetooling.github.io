# v2.4930 — GC mode: our number and how a bid ends, for a dev (2026-10-08)

The Board's B5-c (`to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/board-b5.md` on branch `spike/gc-mode`, sections 5 and 6, calls D and W): our number on real data at `/gc`, dev only. It reads and writes `gc_project_money` and the `gc_mark_*` functions that B5-a (v2.4923, migration `20261008130000`) made. Guide: `set-our-number-and-send-our-bid`.

- **The money read moves to its own table** (the client half of B5's expand and contract).
  - `loadGcBoardRows(projects, today, { money })` reads `gc_project_money` only when `money` is set, which the page sets from `canSeeGcMoney(role)`. Anyone else skips the read, as the policy would return no row.
  - `BoardRows` gains `money` (`ProjectMoneyRow[]`) and `moneyShown`. `boardProjectFromView` takes general conditions, contingency and fee from `rows.money`, 0 when absent. Its signature is unchanged (told to the lead and Helper 5 first).
  - `GcProjectView` drops the three fields, and `projectRows` stops mapping `gc_projects`' three columns. Their row fields stay optional until B6-a drops the columns, so nothing in `src/` reads them.
- **`GcOurNumber.tsx`**, ported from the spike's `GcNumberTab`, opens from **Our number** on the project's card for the money team.
  - The strip: Trades, General conditions, Contingency N%, Fee N%, and *Price to the customer*, red while a trade has no number and green once every line has a cost (`proposalTotals`, with *+ ?*).
  - A red chip per hole and an amber chip per trade on our budget.
  - Three boxes save on blur or Enter, refusing a negative number or a percent over 100 in words. Each save upserts the whole row (`setGcProjectMoney`), then reloads the board.
  - The trades table: where each number comes from (the company we carry, our budget, our own bid, or a hole), our budget and the carried number.
  - Weeks to build waits for the schedule's kernels on main, *signed for* for Owner Billing's O3, and *Price our bid* for a write on our own bid.
- **`GcProjectOutcome.tsx`** on the project's head (call D: no money, so the office gets it when the door opens): **We sent our bid** (then *our bid went in Oct 8*), **We won this. Start buyout** with a second step, **Yes, we won it**, since a win cannot be undone on the page, and **We lost this** with the loss reasons, who won and a note. A lost bid shows its words and **Bring it back**. The writes are `markGcBidSent`, `markGcWon`, `markGcLost` and `bringGcBack`, each reloading the projects, since they write `gc_projects`. The note saves, but the board does not read `lost_note` yet.
- **Trades so far** (call W): with `moneyShown` false the board row reads *trades so far*, or *the trades alone* when nothing is missing. The price card leaves out its general conditions, contingency and fee lines and reads **Trades so far**. Today the board is dev only, so a dev always sees the price.
