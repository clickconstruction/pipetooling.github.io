# v2.6074 — Offline in the field, PR 1: the outbox kernel (punch list #163) (2026-10-29)

Punch list #163 ([`to-dos/offline-field-queue/README.md`](../../to-dos/offline-field-queue/README.md)), the first of five PRs, shipped alone. With no signal the phone only offers Retry, so the clock, a note and a field report all stop until the signal returns and the press is lost. The train keeps a short queue on the phone and shows it as a bar at the top of every screen. This PR is the queue's rules, pure and tested; nothing changes on screen.

The decisions taken on the review canvas of 2026-10-29, which the train builds to: **the clock-time rule is B** (a clock press that lands late files a typed entry through the late-entry path that already exists, with the pressed time filling the entry's times and a sent-late mark, so it waits for approval like any typed hours; the live session runs from the moment the press lands); **a press may wait one workday**, then the bar turns red with its reason and a door; **photos do not queue in this train** (that is #189's PR 7, later, on this queue); **what never queues** is a payment, a bill, a card split, a send to a customer and anything needing the server's stamp on a signature; **the bar at the top of every screen** is the queue's one face and shares #183's strip.

## What changed

- **The kernel** [`src/lib/offline/outbox.ts`](../../src/lib/offline/outbox.ts) (+42 tests). What may queue: `OUTBOX_KINDS` (clock in, clock out, a job note, a field report) with their payload shapes, and `NEVER_QUEUES` in the guide's words. A press: minted on the phone with its own id and a sequence number, stamped `pressedAt` from the phone's clock, carrying what it is about and its door, and for a clock-out the clock-in press it depends on; `parseOutboxPress` shape-checks what the store hands back and puts a press that was in flight when the page closed back to waiting. When a failed write goes in: `failureQueues` says yes for a fetch-layer failure, a timeout (the request may have landed, and the id makes the resend safe) and the browser's offline flag, and no for a refusal. The retry rule: at once, then 5, 10, 20 and 30 s, then every 30 s (`retryDelayMs`, `nextTryAt`), and `markDueNow` for Try now. The wait limit: `waitLimitAt` is six o'clock the next morning in the company calendar; past it a waiting press `needsAPerson`, as a refused one does at once. The order: `oldestWaiting` is the one whose turn it is, a clock-out never ahead of its clock-in; `nextToSend` only once its try is due, so a press behind one that is not due waits with it and one lost signal costs one request; `settleDependents` refuses a clock-out whose clock-in was refused, with `DEPENDENCY_REFUSED_MESSAGE`. The transitions: `markSending`, `markSent` (a second landing keeps the first sent time, which is how a send that lands twice counts once), `markRefused`, `markTryFailed`, `markSeen` for Got it and the opened door, `dropPress` (never a sent or an in-flight press), and `prune`, which keeps what waits or was refused, a sent press for the day it landed on and a day more until Got it, and never another person's press.
- **The words**, the owner's of 2026-10-24 and 2026-10-26 (`to-dos/honest-and-fast/README.md`, part 3): `outboxBarWords` reads red first (one press: *Clock in from 7:58 AM could not be sent.*, the reason, **Fix** with its door, or *… has waited since yesterday.* with the list as its door; several: *2 presses need you*), then the send in flight (*Sending clock in from 7:58 AM…*, pulsing), then what waits (*Clock in from 7:58 AM · waiting to send · last tried 10:14 · trying again in 12 s* and **Try now**; several: *2 waiting to send*; no countdown while the browser says offline, since the resend fires on the online event), then what landed (*Clock in from 7:58 AM sent ✓* and **Got it**; several: *3 sent ✓ · see them*). Red stays until the door is opened and is never cleared by time. `outboxHoldsEverything` says when the slow line may add *Nothing you pressed is lost.*; `waitingCountWords` is the count the slow line and *No signal.* name. `outboxDoorSections` is the list behind the bar: *Needs you*, *Waiting* and *Sent today*, each row with its press, what it is about, when it was pressed, its status and reason, Send now and Drop it. Every sentence passes `plainWordsFailures`.
