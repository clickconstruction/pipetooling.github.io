# v2.8296 — Permits: the phone on an Austin permit is kept (2026-11-22)

Two of the things punch list #307 left when it closed: *the phone is not kept*, and *a phone or web domain in Compare*. The owner's word on 2026-11-22: do them here now. This is the server half. Nothing on a screen changes until the next PR, which is armed once this migration is pushed.

## What was there

Austin's feed names the contractor's phone on 4,898 of the 4,928 permits the app holds from it. The pull kept the whole record in `permits.raw` and mapped no phone to a column, so nothing read it. #307's plan called the phone a second way to grade a name: *the phone kept, which makes the phone a second way to grade a name.*

## What changed

- **`permits.contact_phone`** holds the phone the feed lists for whoever the permit's contact names, as ten digits. The contractor's beside a contractor's name, the applicant's beside an applicant's. A number that is no phone is not kept.
- **The pull writes it** for Austin from here on. The 4,928 rows already held are filled by the migration from their own `raw`.
- **`permit_name_contact_matches`** lists every customer and name on permits that share a phone or a web domain. Ours come from the customer's own row, its people and the contacts spine. Theirs come from the permits, a Places lookup and the call list's looked-up phones.
- **A value a service holds ties nobody**: a phone or a domain under more than three names, or held for more than three customers. So does a mail service's domain, and a site that hosts everyone's page.
- **Nothing is tied by any of this.** It is evidence for a person to press on.

Counted on prod, read only: 433 of 510 customers hold a phone, 1,339 Austin names have one, and three customer and name pairs share a phone today.

## How it is built

- Two small rules say what a phone and a domain are: `permit_phone_digits` and `permit_contact_domain` in SQL, `phoneDigitsOf` and `contactDomainOf` in `supabase/functions/_shared/permitLookup.ts`. The pull and the client need the same answers as the database, so each rule has two copies, and a test and the bed ask both the same lists.
- `PermitRow.contact_phone` is optional and only Austin's mapper sets it. One request writes one set of columns, so a city's rows carry the key or none of them does.
- Deployed ahead of the migration, `permit-pull` drops the column from its upsert and carries on, as it does for `builder_key`.
