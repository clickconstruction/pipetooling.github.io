# v2.8285 — A new phone layout to try: smaller bars, and every press still on the screen (2026-11-22)

The first of two PRs behind one switch (the owner's "Build those two", 2026-11-22). This one is the switch and the bars. The second re-lays the Job Mode card. No migration, no edge function.

## The rule the whole job is held to

With the switch off, nothing changes for anyone. With it on, every press that exists today is still reachable.

## The switch

One tick, **New phone layout**, in the gear menu's **Show** group, between Tell the dev and Dark Mode.

- **Off by default, and the device's.** The stored key is `pt_new_phone_layout`, with no user id in it. A dev who ticks it keeps it while looking as another role through View as. Unticked stores nothing, so a device is as it was before the switch.
- **One rule, one hook.** `newPhoneLayoutOn({ stored, phoneWidth })` in `src/lib/phoneLayout/newPhoneLayout.ts` is true only when the tick is on AND the screen is a phone's width. The width is the app's own rule, `useNarrowViewport640`. Every screen in the trial reads it through `useNewPhoneLayout()`, so none can be on while another is off.
- **Who is offered the tick.** `NEW_PHONE_LAYOUT_TICK_ROLES` is `['dev', 'master_technician']`, the one list to widen. The tick is drawn at a phone's width only, so a gear menu at a desk is the menu it was.
- **The way off is always there.** A device that already has the tick on draws it for whoever is signed in. Without that, a dev who ticked it on a shared phone would leave the next person on a layout with no press to leave it. On the role dock, which has no gear, the row is in the More sheet's Show group.

### The Home Screen offer

`AddTaskShortcutBanner` takes `quiet`. Under the switch Layout no longer mounts the line above the header. It mounts the quiet row inside `<main>` on `/dashboard` alone: the same words, the same **Add**, the same **Dismiss** and the same remembered dismissal (`markAddTaskBannerDismissed`). It is keyed by the role, which lands after the first draw, so the offer is decided once the role is known.

### The top bar

`phoneBarPlan` in `src/lib/phoneLayout/phoneBar.ts` sorts the presses a person's row holds today into three places and drops none. Layout names the presses under each press's own gate, so the kernel never asks who the person is.

- **Stay in the bar:** the menu, Search, the green phone that calls the office, the gear, and the Bids page's **Bid** press.
- **Home** stays unless a bottom tab opens the Dashboard. That is Job Mode's tabs only. Dispatch Mode's first tab opens the Dispatch home, and the menu's own Dashboard row is left out of the phone's menu, so there home stays in the bar.
- **Everything else** is a named row at the top of the gear menu, in the row's order, opening what it opened (`PhoneBarGearRows`): Statement, Tell the dev, Task Dispatch, Ask estimating, Task, Customers, People. The three coloured presses keep their mark on its own fill beside the name.
- **The page's name** is drawn at the left in words (`phoneBarPageName`), and it is the part that shortens when the row is short of room.
- **The role dock's row** has no gear, so it keeps every press it has.

The row is 3 px, a 44 px press and 3 px, with its 1 px line: 51 px. The 3 px is `--app-nav-pad-y` set on the shell's root while the switch is on, which the nav and the search bar over it both read, so the status bar's inset on a Home Screen phone still wins as it did. The gear menu under the switch scrolls inside the screen, clear of the bars at the foot.

### The day's presses

`JobModePressBar` takes `oneRow`. The same buttons from the same kernel rows (`buildJobModePressBar`), in the same order: Turnaway, % done, Clock out, Leave report, then the day's press. The compact ones are as wide as their words. The day's press takes the rest and keeps its label and its colour in every state, dead or live.

With all five up on a 375 px phone the two-word labels fold onto two lines (Clock out, Leave report) and the day's press is 112 px, enough for *On my way* on one line. The compact presses give room a thousand times as readily as the day's press, so a label folds before the day's press shrinks. A wrapping row would never fold them, since an item moves to the next line before it shrinks, so the row is held to one line by `.jobModePressBarOneRow` in `index.css` at 350 px and wider. Under that it may wrap, the bar grows by a line and its measured height follows.

### The tabs

`DispatchModeFooter` takes `compact`. The same tabs with their words, each 52 px tall. The bar's own 60 px minimum counted the phone's inset inside it, so under the switch the tabs hold the height themselves and the bar is their 52 px, its line and the inset under them. `paddingBottom: env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` is the bar's own, as before.

## What holds "nothing changes with the switch off"

- Every new branch in `Layout.tsx`, the press bar and the tab bar reads one of three values that are false or null with the switch off: `phoneBars`, `phoneBarOneRow`, `phoneBarNow`.
- `Layout.newPhoneLayout.render.test.tsx` (50): for nine roles, a phone with the switch off draws nothing of the new layout and keeps the header's padding. At a desk the shell's markup is the same string whether the device has the tick or not, with the gear menu shut and open.
- The same file renders every role in and out of Job Mode twice, off then on, and fails when a press of the old row is in neither the bar, the gear menu's top rows nor the tabs.
- `phoneBar.test.ts` runs `phoneBarPlan` over every subset of the thirteen presses and holds that the three places add up to the row it was given.
- `JobModePressBar.oneRow.render.test.tsx` (36): for ten states of the bar, the one row draws the same buttons, words, hover words and dead ones as the two rows, and each runs the press it ran. Off, the bar is its two rows with no class on it.
- `DispatchModeFooter.compact.render.test.tsx` (6): the same elements off and on, only the sizes differ.
- `DashboardJobModeCard.newPhoneLayout.render.test.tsx` (4): the card hands the bar the switch, and at a desk the card is the same markup with the tick on or off.

The one change with the switch off: a dev or a leader on a phone sees the new unticked row in the gear menu.
