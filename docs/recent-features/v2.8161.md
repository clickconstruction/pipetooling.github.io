# v2.8161 — Before we start: a No on arrival says where it was sent from (2026-11-21)

Punch list #382, from the dev-server walk of the audit of PR 10 (v2.8110). Client only: one word line in `lib/startChecks/arrival.ts`, one optional prop on `StageCheckWindow.tsx` and its mount in `DispatchInboxSection.tsx`. No migration, no edge function.

## The fault: words that were not so

A No on arrival reaches Dispatch on the road a stage check uses, and opens the same window. The window's first line is the sender, the time and where the line came from. For every row it read *sent from the sheet*, the checklist's sheet a stage check is sent from.

A No on arrival has no checklist behind it. The crew sends it from the **Is it as told?** card in Job Mode. Dispatch read a sheet that does not exist for that line.

## The fix

`StageCheckWindow` takes `sentFromWords`. The inbox passes `NOT_AS_TOLD_WORDS.sentOnArrival`, *sent on arrival*, for a row that carries a No on arrival, beside the door's name and the foot it already passes. A stage check passes nothing and keeps *sent from the sheet*.

The window's title and the group's head still read *Something else* for such a row. That is the reason the database writes on the request, and it is left as it is.

## Checked

The render test of the window on a No on arrival holds the new words and refuses the old ones. The stage check's own window tests pass unchanged. The old words were seen on the dev server at 390 and 1280 px, light and dark, on a made-up row answered in the browser. The new words were seen the same way at 390 px light and 1280 px dark.
