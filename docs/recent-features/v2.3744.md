# v2.3744 — Lien notices on a home carry the § 53.254(g) statement (2026-09-22)

The first answer back from counsel on the *Put a GC on notice* brief (punch list #16, question 2 of `owner-decisions-pending.md`): *the § 53.081 mechanics are the same on residential work; timing differs (the 15th of the second month), which the software already tracks; no different withholding paragraph is needed. Homestead is different: for a homestead lien to be valid, § 53.254(g) still requires that the Subchapter C notice include or have attached* the statement below. The notice never printed it. Client only, no migration.

## The change

- **[`lienFilingDocuments.ts`](../../src/lib/jobsDocuments/lienFilingDocuments.ts)** — `LienNoticeFields.homesteadStatement?: boolean`; `HOMESTEAD_NOTICE_STATEMENT` holds the statute's words verbatim (a heading naming § 53.254(g), the lead sentence, the two numbered conditions) and `homesteadStatementBlocks()` renders them; `buildLienNoticeBlocks` appends them after the signature when the flag is set, so the prescribed (a-2) form above is untouched. The typed-field key type excludes the flag — it is not a value the office edits.
- **[`lienNoticeDraft.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienNoticeDraft.ts)** — `homesteadStatementApplies(property)`: true on a property flagged homestead **and on every residential property** (a homestead is always residential, the roll's flag can be stale or missing, and the statement costs nothing on a residence that is not one); false on commercial or unknown. `buildLienNoticeFieldsForJob` takes the fact; `parseLienDeskDraftFields` keeps the flag on a saved draft and drops it when absent, so older drafts read as before.
- **Every builder passes it from the saved property record** (`customer_addresses.property_kind` / `homestead`): the run ([`lienDeskRun.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDeskRun.ts)), the Lien desk's job defaults ([`LienDeskModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskModal.tsx)), the affidavit pane's notice draft, the Lien window's own notice tab ([`LienFilingTabs.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienFilingTabs.tsx)), and Put a GC on notice — both `approveAll` and the read-before-you-approve preview ([`GcOnNoticeModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/GcOnNoticeModal.tsx), [`gcNoticePreview.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/gcNoticePreview.ts)). Both copies carry it — the statute speaks of the notice, and the GC's copy is the same notice.
- Guides: *understand how liens work…*, *send lien notices from the Lien desk*, *Texas lien rules the app follows* (a **Homestead** paragraph in the affidavit section).

## Not changed, on counsel's word

The cover letter's § 53.081 paragraph stays as written for residential work.
