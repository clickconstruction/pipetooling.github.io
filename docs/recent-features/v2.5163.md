# v2.5163 — GC mode: a trade partner's pay application from its portal, behind the scenes (2026-10-10)

P5c-3c-ii of the trade partner portal's real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/PORTAL_REAL_BUILD.md`; plan `to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/portal-p5.md`, both on branch `spike/gc-mode`). Built and held: every press here waits on `WAIVER_SIGN_LIVE`. No migration.

- **The kinds** (`submit-gc-trade-portal`, `_shared/gcTradeSubmit.ts`): `pay_app` (`packageId`, `app` with its `lines` by the kernels' line ids, `toPct` and `stored`, and `periodTo`, `address`, `license`, `signedBy`, `signedTitle`) on U6a's `gc_trade_pay_app`, and `final_pay_app` (the same `app` with no lines) on U6c's `gc_trade_final_pay_app`. A pay application signs its conditional lien waiver, so both are signatures: the e-sign consent before any write, typed only, the printed name the G702's signer, and the ledger row `gc_draw` keyed by the draw the verb returns (`TradeSignature.record.id` null until then). Neither is under the hourly cap: `drawWaiting` holds one at a time.
- **The hold**: `WAIVER_KINDS` (`unconditional_waiver`, `pay_app`, `final_pay_app`), refused by `waiverHeld` as `badRequest` while `WAIVER_SIGN_LIVE` is false, the same flag the page reads.
- **The keys**: `drawWaiting`, `nothingToBill`, `finalSent` and `finalNotYet` (409), with their words in English and Spanish. The guard's waiting list is empty: every key a trade verb raises has its status and its words.
- **The window** (`GcTradePortalPayApp.tsx`): Send posts through the door's `onSend`, the submit adapter, and the window stays open with a refusal in the company's words. The sign step lays out the conditional waiver as the app's own paper: `tradePayAppWaiverPaper` fills the Release of Lien window's `conditional_progress` or `conditional_final` form on our check for what the application asks, through its period, and `GcTradeWaiverPaper.tsx` draws it (the unconditional waiver's paper since P5c-3b draws there too). Then the name and title, I read this waiver and I agree to it, and the e-sign consent, which Send waits for. The spike's tick, "I sign the conditional lien waiver for $X", is gone.
- **New words**: `errDrawWaiting`, `errNothingToBill`, `errFinalSent`, `errFinalNotYet`, `condConsentNoun`, `condFinalConsentNoun`.
- **Tests**: `gcTradeSubmit.test.ts` (both kinds, the ledger key, the shapes refused, the four keys, the hold over all three waiver kinds), `tradeWaiverPaper.test.ts` (both conditional forms), `GcTradePortal.payAppSend.render.test.tsx` (the flag turned on: the paper in the sign step, Send waiting on the consent, the body posted, a refusal keeping the window open, the final one from the closeout).
- **Docs**: `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`, `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`, `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`.
- **Deploy** (the lead): `submit-gc-trade-portal`, `gc-trade-email` and `send-contract-for-signature`, the three functions that bundle `_shared/gcTradeSubmit.ts` (the last two through `_shared/gcTradeEmail.ts`). Corrected in v2.5168.
