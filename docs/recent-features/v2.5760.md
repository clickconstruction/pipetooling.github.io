# v2.5760 — Legal Desk: write an account down in one place, and see what each bill becomes (2026-10-22)

The owner, in the Legal Desk design spike: "I do not fully understand the write down button to the applied discount modal. It seems like a big jump and it's hard for me to follow. How could I offer this in a more straightforward or seamless way?" Four jumps at once: the door sat under *Attorney fees and costs*, the words changed from *Write down* to *Apply discount*, the scope shrank from the account to one bill without saying which (the header's door picked the largest bill, the Fees tab's door the first job's), and a dialog over the desk lost its context.

- **The door**: **Write it down…** beside **⚖ Mark attorney ready…** in the header (the desk's subtitle already named the two exits); the old header button and the Fees tab's door are gone, and the checklist's *Write down* fix opens the panel.
- **The panel** (`AccountWriteDownPanel.tsx`, under the header, inside the desk): **How much will we accept?** · *They owe $2,028.04 on 2 bills.* The same control as the settlement floor — a $ box, a % box and a slider that move together in whole numbers — starting at the full balance so nothing is written down until a person moves it, with *we forgive $811.22* in red once something is. The split, one row per open bill: Job · Owes · We accept · We forgive · **Still owes after** (what the customer still owes on that bill, not the bill's new total, which includes what was already paid); two bills on one job read with their billed amount and day (*891 · $16,780.40 bill, 07/09/26*). A required note of what was agreed and with whom. **Save says what it does**: *Write down $811.22 across 2 bills*, *Nothing to write down* at the full balance.
- **The run** (`src/lib/legal/accountWriteDownRun.ts`, tested with fakes): each bill through the per-bill path `AgreedWriteDownModal` uses — `stripe-invoice-agreed-write-down` for a Stripe-hosted bill, `apply_agreed_write_down_to_billed_invoice` for the rest — one at a time; a refusal or a throw holds that bill with the reason and the rest still go (CLAUDE.md's do-what-is-ready rule). The toast: *Wrote down 2 bills.* / *Wrote down 1 bill. Held 1: job 718, Stripe refused it.* On any success the matter closes as written down, as before.
- **The math** (`src/lib/legal/accountWriteDown.ts`, tested): `accountBills` (the open billed lines as `legalJobMoney` counts them), `planAccountWriteDown` (proportional to what each bill owes, to the cent, largest remainder; each bill's new total is what was paid on it plus its share), `billRowWords`, `writeDownButtonWords`, `writeDownResultWords`.
- **Words**: the guides *review a collections account…* (the exit paragraph) and *send a customer account…* (the example, the Written down row); `GLOSSARY.md` → Legal desk.
- **Not done**: the Save run has not been pressed on a live account; its first real use is on a ZZ test customer with a Collections job. `AgreedWriteDownModal` itself is unchanged and still the job window's Apply discount.

## Is this the best we can do?

1. **One question, one place**: *How much will we accept?* for the account, where the person already is, instead of a per-bill dialog that never said which bill.
2. **The split is shown, not implied**: every bill, what it owes, what it keeps, what it forgives. *Still owes after* is the number a reader thinks in; the bill's new total stays in the write path.
3. **Say what Save does**: the amount and the bill count on the button, off until the note is in.
4. **Do what is ready**: a held bill never stops the rest, and the toast names it with the reason.
5. **No new write path**: the same function and RPC a job's Apply discount uses, so the ledger, the audit rows and the Stripe credit note are the ones already trusted.
6. **Cut**: a per-bill override of the split (the proportion is fair and visible), a confirm dialog (the button's words are the confirm), writing down a bill a customer already paid in full.
