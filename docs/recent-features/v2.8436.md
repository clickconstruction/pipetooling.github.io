# v2.8436 — Agreements: a trade company’s waiting paper opens on People (2026-11-24)

A fault punch list #403 left: on Documents → Agreements, the name of a trade company's paper that waits opened GC mode's page, also when the office had sent it from People. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was there

`agreementDoor` (`src/lib/documents/agreementsLedger.ts`) sent every waiting `company_agreement` to `/gc`, with a GC project's owner contract and statement of work. Since step 6 of #403 (v2.8381, v2.8394) a sub's company is a row on People → Contracts with its own papers and its own **Send for signature**. The office's press took them away from that row to a page that shows projects and no paper.

## What was read before the rule was chosen

- **`/gc` is not dev only.** `canOpenGcProjects` opens it to the office's four roles and estimators (`GC_OFFICE_TEAM`, `estimatorAllowedPaths`). What is a dev's there is sending a trade its emails (`canSendGcTradeEmail`). So the old press landed, on a page that could not show the paper.
- **A line does not say where its paper was sent from.** The `agreements_on_file` view's facts for a company's paper are its status, its days and its signer. `from: 'office'` on the send picks the email's sender name and is not kept on the row. So *only one sent from People* is not a rule the tab can apply without a migration.
- **People → Contracts could not land on a company's paper.** The tab keeps a company's paper off the list its landing reads (`isCompanyPaperName`), so `contractsDocLanding` fell to the signed record window, which answers an unsigned paper with an error.
- **Which companies have a row there.** A company is drawn when a living roster name is linked to it (`rosterByCompany`). Its row lists every paper of the company, whoever sent it. A company no one on the roster works at has no row.

## The rule

Every waiting trade company paper opens `/people?tab=contracts&doc=<id>`, and the landing there decides:

- The company has a row: the paper is marked under it and scrolled into view, where the send is. If the roster filter hides every worker of the company, the filter moves to Everyone, as it does for a person.
- The company has no row: the tab says *… is with <company>. No one on the People list works there, so the paper has no line on this tab. It is kept in GC mode.*
- A signed paper opens its signed record, as before.

Why this one: the four roles who get the press (`canActOnAgreements`) are the four who read the companies on People (`isOfficeRole`), so the press never lands on a page its reader cannot open. *Has a row on People* is the true form of *sent from People*: a paper sent from People always has one, and a paper a dev made in GC mode for the same company is listed on that row too. The cost is one hop for a company with no worker on the roster: its paper used to open `/gc` and now opens People, which names GC mode. Today those are a dev's GC mode papers.

An estimator's waiting line still has no press (`agreementReaderDoor`).

## What changed

- `agreementDoor`: a waiting `company_agreement` answers `people-contracts`. `agreementPeopleContractsPath` writes the address for it and for a person's paper.
- `contractsDocLanding` (`src/lib/contractsDocLink.ts`) takes the companies' papers, the companies with the names under each row, and whether the companies are read. Two new answers, `company-paper` and `company-off-roster`, with `contractsDocCompanyOffRosterWords`. It waits for the companies, so a read still out, or one that failed and is read again, never says a company has no row. A caller that hands it no company papers gets the answers it always got.
- `PeopleContractsTab`: keeps the few facts of the company papers its own read saw, hands them to the landing, and marks the paper through `SubCompanyHeadRow`'s new `markedPaperId` (the tint and outline a person's linked paper carries).
- `DocumentsAgreementsTab.tsx` is not touched: it already draws a `people-contracts` door as a link.

## A cleaner rule, and what it would take

The door could choose between People and `/gc` by itself if the view's facts carried whether the company has a worker on the roster. That is a migration on `agreements_on_file` and its doc, and it would spare the one hop above.
