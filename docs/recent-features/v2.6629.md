# v2.6629 — The signed hours statement is the paper the office printed (punch list #212) (2026-11-07)

The first residual after the five-PR train on the card (`to-dos/signed-hours-statement/README.md`), found in the 2026-11-04 review: PR 3's pdf-lib paper (`supabase/functions/_shared/hoursStatementPdf.ts`) was drawn before v2.6412 restyled the print and before v2.6446 added the charged basis, so the PDF a worker signed kept the earlier two-column facts grid, separate In and Out columns and a Rate column on every row, and a charged row — which the window's `canSign` offers for signature — signed as *Statement of hours worked* with no Charged band, disagreeing with the printed charged paper.

## What changed

- **The paper's shape lives in the kernel.** `hoursStatementUniformRate`, `hoursStatementHours2`, `HOURS_STATEMENT_NOTE_FITS` and `HOURS_STATEMENT_NOTE_FITS_WITH_RATE` moved from `buildHoursStatementHtml.ts` into `supabase/functions/_shared/hoursStatement.ts` (the no-import kernel both the app and the functions read); the HTML builder imports and re-exports them, so its readers and tests are untouched.
- **The PDF follows the print** (`hoursStatementPdf.ts`): the title by basis (`hoursStatementPaperTitle`: *Statement of hours charged* · *worked and paid* · *worked*); the facts as one band across the width in three columns (Worker · Period · Rate when uniform / Job · Customer · Approved by, the Site on its own line) between two rules; the table full width — *Day · In – Out · Hours · What they were working on · [Rate] · [Amount]*, a sheet *Day · Line · Hours · [Rate] · [Amount]* — the Rate column only when the rate changes inside the period, the Amount column with the pay or on a priced charged basis, hours to two decimals, a long note (past the kernel's fit) as its own line under its day kept with it, a week heading kept with its first row, the week's subtotals under Hours and Amount, the totals row with the days' words, the column header on every page and no row split; the **Charged** band (the kernel's lines, total words, note and left-out lines) in place of Paid on the charged basis, **Paid** as before, each a small uppercase heading under a rule; the attestation and the two signature lines as full-width bands — the worker's signature and printed name on one rule with the print's 1.6in Date column, the company's line under it from the kernel's `preparedWords` (so a charged paper's line names how it was charged) with its own Date column.
- **The form's boxes keep their keys and order** (`signature`, `printed_name`, `signed_on`, the `stated_<n>_*` cells), so `get-contract-for-signer`, the signing page, `accept-contract` and `open-contract-form-pdf` see the same form; only the rects moved with the layout.
- **The result names its shape**: `buildHoursStatementPdf` answers `title`, `facts` and `columns` beside the bytes, the schema and the page count, so a test reads the layout without extracting text from the PDF.
- **Tests** (`hoursStatementPdf.test.ts`): the columns with a changing rate (Rate drawn) and a uniform one (Rate in the band, no column), the sheet's columns, the charged paper's title and columns, the hours-only paper, and every earlier case (the schema, the stated boxes, the second page, the fill through `fillFormPdf`).
- **Docs**: `CONTRACT_FORMS.md` → *A form drawn per record* says the PDF is the print's layout; the guide *make a signed hours statement* says the worker signs the same paper the office printed; the card records the owner's walk of 2026-11-07 and that counsel's word is no longer a blocker.

## Decisions

- **One kernel for the shape, two drawers.** The HTML and the PDF cannot share a renderer (one is a browser print, one is pdf-lib), so the numbers and words that decide the shape — the uniform rate, the hours format, where a note drops to its own line — moved into the kernel both import, and each file draws only geometry. A later change to the paper's shape is made once there.
- **The dev MCP's charged paper is still open.** `get_hours_statement` and `plan_hours_statement` read the paid basis only; the charged basis needs the crew-days reads (`loadCrewSessions`, the rate card, the adjustments) ported to the seat's reader, which this PR does not do. The card names it.

## Deploy

After the merge, from the main checkout: `supabase functions deploy hours-statement-signing` (it draws the PDF). The app's print is unchanged in what it draws; the client deploy carries the moved helpers. Nothing to push.
