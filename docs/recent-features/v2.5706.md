PR 14 of punch list #139 (`to-dos/press-always-answers/README.md`), stacked on PR 13 (v2.5705); the fourth of the six additions. The estimate text sat "on the person" with *no job* and no door; its link names the estimate, and the estimate names the job once it has one. No migration; no edge function change.

- **The door on texts** (`src/lib/messages/estimateDoor.ts` `textDoorsFrom`, `estimateDoorIo.ts` `loadTextDoors`, `useEstimateDoorThreadSlots.tsx`): once per open thread, the outbound texts carrying `/estimate/accept` are read, their tokens hashed as the edge minted them (`acceptLinkTie.ts`), and `estimates` read by hash (number, doc kind) — a text whose token hashes to an estimate's draws **📄 Open estimate #166** (or *Open change order #80*) under its words, the email door's own button and press. A dead link (a resend re-mints the hash) or an unknown one draws none. The hook keeps the read keyed on the thread, as R8's estimate-line hook does.
- **The job tie** (`src/lib/estimates/estimateTextsTieIo.ts` `tieEstimateTextsToJob`, called from the estimate page's create-job success before it leaves for the job): the customer's thread, the texts carrying this estimate's live link that sit on no job, each tied through the thread's own `tie_message` to `pipeline:<job>`. Best effort — it never throws into the page, and one text that will not tie does not stop the rest. Texts the sheet sends after the job exists were already tied (v2.5594).
- **Not carried**: the tie for an estimate whose link was re-minted before the job was made (its first text's token no longer matches); the hash is the only tie the rows carry.
- **Guide**: *find every message with a person in one place* names the text's button.
- **Tests**: the pure tie (a match, a change order, a dead link, no link); the hook's smoke with a text door drawn from the open thread's read and its press landing on the estimate; the email cases as before.
