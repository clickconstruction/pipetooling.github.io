# v2.3359 — Bills also go to, PR 2: copies on Stripe bills — the Send to list on the Stripe tab, and a copy from us with the same Pay link (2026-09-12)

No migration (PR 1's `20260912012359` added `jobs_ledger_invoices.copy_emails`); edge functions `create-stripe-invoice`, `send-stripe-invoice` and `send-physical-invoice-email` changed (**redeploy all three after merge**; `_shared/resendSendEmail.ts` gained an `emailType` option, so any importer redeployed later picks it up harmlessly). Second and last PR of the train (PR 1 v2.3358). Ships the regenerated `src/types/database.ts`.

## Why

In the last 90 days every emailed bill went out through Stripe — 119 Stripe bills, 76 of them sent with *Send Email invoice*, and zero PDF invoice emails — and the Stripe tab had no copy list at all: Stripe models one email per customer and has no CC. PR 1's remembered recipients would have reached nobody on the path the office actually uses.

## What shipped

- **`create-stripe-invoice`** accepts `copy_emails[]` (≤10, validated, lowercased, deduped, never the recipient's own address) and writes them to the row (`copy_emails`, null when empty — a re-create clears a stale list). The copy list is fixed at the moment the office presses *Create Stripe invoice*, exactly as ticked.
- **`send-stripe-invoice`** — the only place a Stripe bill is emailed (`invoices.sendInvoice`) — reads the row's `copy_emails` after Stripe accepts the send and sends **one email per address** through Resend ([`_shared/stripeBillCopyEmail.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/stripeBillCopyEmail.ts), +6 tests from `src/lib/billing/`): *This is a copy of the bill Click Plumbing and Electrical sent to Josh Peterson for 390 Pecan Meadows* · amount due · due date · invoice number · job · a **Pay or view the bill** button on the same hosted URL · the PDF link · *Josh Peterson was billed directly by Stripe*. Reply-to is the sender's own address. Each copy is logged to `email_send_log` with `email_type = 'stripe_bill_copy'` (new `EMAIL_CATALOG` row). The response carries `copies_sent[]` / `copies_failed[]` (and `copies_skipped` when Resend is not configured or Stripe returned no hosted URL); a copy failure never fails the send Stripe already made.
- **`send-physical-invoice-email`** records its `additional_emails` on the row as `copy_emails` too, so the record reads the same for both channels.
- **Bill Customer**: the *Send to* block is now one renderer (`sendToBlock(channel)`) shown on **both** the Stripe and Physical tabs — the primary, the payer's contacts (flagged ones pre-ticked, PR 1), *Copy the other party*, a one-off. On the Stripe tab it says *Stripe emails that address. Everyone ticked below gets a copy from ClickTooling with the same Pay link when you press Send Email invoice*; the create payload carries the list; the post-create panel names the copies that will go out.
- **Send Email invoice** ([`StripeInvoiceSendFromStripeButton`](../../src/components/jobs/StripeInvoiceSendFromStripeButton.tsx), also on the Stages *Last activity* cell): the confirm reads the row's `copy_emails` and adds *Copies from ClickTooling, same Pay link, to …*; the success toast names the copies sent and a second toast reports any that failed.
- `JOBS_LEDGER_INVOICES_EMBED` and the Dashboard invoice loader carry `copy_emails`.
- Help: `send-a-bill-to-more-than-one-person` drops the "Stripe still one address" caveat and describes the Stripe copy; `ready-to-bill-pipeline` and `choose-who-gets-the-bill` likewise. `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` three sections; `BILLING_FLOWS.md` Stripe channel.

## Notes for the next session

- Copies go out **only** with *Send Email invoice*. A bill shared by SMS or copy-link (`StripeInvoiceSharePanel`) never emails anyone, so it copies nobody either — by design; the office sees the copy list on the panel.
- The copy email carries no portal link. The payer's contacts could get the payer's statement link (the footer resolver already exists), the other party their own; left out until someone asks.
- `copies_failed` is surfaced as a toast only; nothing on the bill row says a copy bounced. `email_send_log` has the row (Settings → Notifications → most recent emails).
