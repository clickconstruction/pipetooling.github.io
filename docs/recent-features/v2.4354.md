# v2.4354 — Procurement log: tap a line to change its house (2026-10-02)

Grace, 2026-10-02, on BP375 after the parts train (v2.4319–v2.4332): "Sometimes I will want to swap out a supply house, but the part is the same, how do I change the supply house here?" — and then: "I would like for a user to be able to click on `DWH-1 WATTS LFN36M1 … Moore Supply · Trim Set` in procure and have it open that view as a modal rather than having to scroll back and forth so much." A part's house, lead time and stage live on the submittal row (Edit → the part's line); the procurement log only showed them, eight stages down the page.

## What changed
- **A log line's item opens its row.** Every line with a submittal row behind it (a part's line, a row's single line, the tag's *as logged before its parts* line) is now a button: hover tints it and underlines the product, the quiet line under it ends in a blue **· Edit**, and the tooltip says *Open DWH-1 to change this part's house, lead time or stage*. A tap opens that row's Edit window over the log (`SubmittalItemEditDialog`, the same window as the rows table's Edit). Hand lines (typed on the log, no row) stay as they were. The GC never sees the door: it is only passed by the office tab (`onOpenItem`).
- **The window opens on the part tapped.** `SubmittalPartsEditor` takes `focusId`: that part's line is ringed blue, scrolled to the middle and its house box focused (its lead time box when the bid has no house list). Opened from the rows table, nothing is ringed. Save closes the window; the log re-reads and keeps the scroll position (walked: the line stayed at the same pixel).
- **Save no longer rewrites an unchanged tag.** The draft editor sent `tag: tagText.trim().toUpperCase()` on every Save, so opening *Kitchen sinks* to change a house renamed it *KITCHEN SINKS* — and a row without parts keeps its order dates on a log line keyed by the tag, which a silent rename cuts loose. The tag is now written only when it was edited (still in capitals).
- Plumbing: `ProcurementItemSource.itemId` / `ProcurementRow.itemId` (set by `procurementItemsFrom`, carried by `buildProcurementLog`, including the *as logged before its parts* line); the tab maps the line's `partKey` back to the part by `procure_key`.

## Tests
- `procurementLog.test.ts`: each line names its row; a hand line has none.
- `SubmittalProcurementPanel.render.test.tsx`: the door hands back the row and the part; a row line hands back no part; no `onOpenItem` = plain text.
- `SubmittalItemEditDialog.render.test.tsx`: `focusPartId` rings one part and focuses its house box; Save leaves an untouched tag out; a typed tag is saved in capitals.
- `BidsSubmittalsTab.render.test.tsx`: a Procure line opens Edit on the part, a change saves on that part, the tag is not written; the rows table's Edit rings nothing. The older Edit-saves case no longer expects the unchanged tag.
