# v2.3191 — Burn beyond the job: Job Summary's projected margin and the Pipeline's burning-jobs card (2026-09-09)

Client only — no migration. Part B of the owner-approved mock-up *Burn, Budget and Stages* (v2.3189 was the Costs tab section). The Costs tab answers the question for one job; this PR asks it of every job at once, from the aggregates Job Summary already holds.

## The kernel

[`jobSummaryBurn.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobSummaryBurn.ts) (7 tests) — `projectJobSummaryBurn` over a row's aggregates: contract, spent (team labor + subs + parts), the resolved %, finished, field days in the window, overhead share to date, the Target chip. Same rules as `buildJobBurn`: budget = contract × (1 − target) via `resolveJobBurnBudget` (35 % default), `hot` past 5 lead points, `early` under 3 field days or 10 %, `no_budget` without a contract; a finished job is `done` and its "projection" is what happened (so Proj. margin equals true profit on a finished row). Overhead only in the true-margin projection: share so far + share per field day × field days left, where days left = (100 − %) ÷ (% per field day so far). `burnProjectedMarginForSort` = true margin when the ledger is in, direct margin until then. `buildPipelineBurnAlert` collects the hot unfinished rows, sums the shortfall against the target margin (contract − budget) and keeps the worst three.

## Job Summary

- [`jobSummaryLedgerView.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobSummaryLedgerView.ts): `JobSummaryEnrichedRow.burn`, computed in `enrichJobSummaryRows` (new optional `targetMarginPct`, wired from `prefs.targetTrueMarginPct` in [`useJobSummaryView`](../../src/hooks/useJobSummaryView.ts)); sort key `projMargin`; totals gain `projectedTrueMarginUsd` (Σ over in-progress rows with a projection), `projectedRows`, `burningJobs`.
- [`JobsJobSummaryTab`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsJobSummaryTab.tsx): two columns between **True %** and **$/hr** — **Burn** (`68% · 62%`, red + bold when hot, green when ok, `done` on finished rows, `41% · early` before the projection is trustworthy) and **Proj. margin** (signed dollars with the % beside it; red + bold when negative or under the Target chip; the tooltip says "direct margin" while the ledger is still loading). Both sortable on `projMargin`. The totals row shows `N hot` and the summed projected true margin. Wage roles only (`showTeamLaborAndProfit`), like True profit. Expanded-row `colSpan` 13 → 15.

## Pipeline

- [`Jobs.tsx`](../../src/pages/Jobs.tsx): `pipelineBurnWanted` (Pipeline tab + `showJobCostBreakdownTeamLabor`) arms `pipelineBurnArmed` **2.5 s** after the board shows, and that flag joins the gates of the loads Job Summary already runs — `loadLaborJobs`, `loadTeamLaborData`, `usePartsLedgerData.isActive` — plus a new `extraReportPctJobIds` input on [`useJobSummaryData`](../../src/hooks/useJobSummaryData.ts) so the `list_latest_report_completion_pct` batch also runs for the board's open jobs off the Job Summary tab. The `pipelineBurnAlert` memo then runs `projectJobSummaryBurn` over `jobSummaryData` (which already reads the Pipeline's `jobs` list off the Job Summary tab) for waiting / working / ready-to-bill jobs — direct margin only here (no day ledger off Job Summary), field days from the team-labor breakdown's distinct work dates, % from the report batch else the job's own — and `buildPipelineBurnAlert` reduces it. Null until team labor has loaded, so the card never fires on parts alone.
- [`JobsStagesTab`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsStagesTab.tsx) → [`PipelineOverview`](../../src/components/jobs/PipelineOverview.tsx) → [`PipelineMoneyOpportunities`](../../src/components/jobs/PipelineMoneyOpportunities.tsx): a red-edged card, **🔥 N jobs burning ahead of progress — $X of margin at risk**, the worst three as "J927 Mike Holub 133% spent at 70% done · …", **Open the worst first →** (the Job window on its **Costs** tab via `tryOpenEditJob(jobId, { initialTab: 'costs' })`) and **Show all N** (Job Summary with `status: in_progress`, sorted `projMargin` ascending, via `setPrefs` + the tab param). Hidden for non-wage roles, while loading, and when nothing is hot.
