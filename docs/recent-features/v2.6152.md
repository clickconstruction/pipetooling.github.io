# v2.6152 — Training's due date as a strip, with versions handled (punch list #217, field-11, a prototype) (2026-10-30)

Punch list #217, *Show it, don't say it*: eight scans found 131 places where the app tells instead of shows, and a mock-up page draws each as a before/after pair for the owner to pick from. On 2026-10-30 she asked to see field-8 and field-11 "as prototypes, live, side by side with how each card looks now", and of field-11 she added that people take different versions of training rather than the same course over and over, so she is unsure how well it applies. This is field-11, shipped as a draft PR for her to look at; field-8 is its own PR (v2.6146). No migration, no edge function.

## The finding

The Dashboard's **Training** card read *OSHA 30 refresher · Done Nov 3, 2025 · due again Nov 3, in 4 days · Take it again* under the chip *1 course to take again*; People read *training lapsed* for someone still current; and Settings → Training's *12 current · 3 lapsed · 5 not done* looked like chips and filtered nothing. The due date was prose, the amber window (`dueSoonDays`) invisible, and `courseStanding` colours a pass amber from thirty days before the day until it is passed again, so *to take again* and *lapsed* both covered a course still in date.

## How versions are handled, in plain words

- The app has one course per slug. Every pass records the content version, the hash of the course's words on the day it was passed. **The standing never reads it**: the newest pass of the course counts, whatever its version, for `goodForMonths` from its day. A changed lesson voids nothing (Settings says so under *Everyone takes it again…*).
- The one thing that voids a pass is a dev's **Everyone takes it again** (`retakeFrom`): a pass before that day counts for nothing, and `courseStanding` turns red as if the person had never passed. Today's card then shows *Required for your role · not started* with the old pass invisible.
- So the strip draws what is true. One strip per course the role must hold, from the pass of whichever version they passed, that version named at its start (*passed Nov 3, 2025 · version 1a2b3c4d*), to the due day the policy sets. A pass the retake day voided is its own case, `superseded`: the strip runs from the old pass to a red cut at the retake day, no window, the chip *older version · take it again*, the sub line *Passed Nov 3, 2025 on an older version. Everyone takes it again from Oct 1.* On Settings the facts line names the course's current version beside the chips, so a dev can tell an older pass from it.
- **Where the strip would mislead.** If "different versions" means different courses (OSHA 10 and OSHA 30, or a refresher from another provider), the app has no rule that one course satisfies another's requirement: each slug is its own requirement, so a person who holds a different course reads *not done* on this one, on the strip as on the card today. A pass taken outside the app (a paper card) is not a pass here either. The strip cannot show an equivalence the data does not hold; that is a policy question (which courses count for which requirement), not a drawing one. And the Dashboard card names the version passed but does not say whether a newer one exists: the course text lives in the lazy `/training` chunk, which the card must not load (`courseRegistry.ts`), and the policy does not care.

## What changed

- **The rule for every prototype in this program** is the same as v2.6146's: a per-user switch on the card, remembered on the device, off by default; a dev with `?sbs=1` on the URL sees today's look and the shape together. The helper `src/components/prototypes/` (`useShapeView(key)`, `SideBySide`, `ShapeSwitch`) is byte for byte the one v2.6146 adds, so whichever PR merges second merges clean; the rhythm-strips prototype writes the same helper under the same names.
- **`trainingDueStrip.ts`** (`src/lib/training/`, new, beside `trainingState.ts`): the pure kernel. `dueStateOf` splits amber by the day (`due-soon` inside the window and not past it, `lapsed` past it) and adds `superseded` and `not-done`; `dueStripFromDates` gives the strip from its days alone (today's place as a percent of the width, where the window starts, `overrun` past the end, the chip, the two labels and the sentence); `trainingDueStrip` reads a person's passes under the policy (the counting pass's strip, the superseded case when the only passes are before the retake day, not done with none); `personDueStrips` orders worst first; `dueVerdict` is the card's line with the split (*1 course to do*, *to take again*, *lapsed*, *due soon*, *You are caught up*); `dueSplit` and `dueCounts` split the SQL twin's standings (`training_standings`, which carry no version) for Settings. Nine tests: off or not required, current, due soon and due today, lapsed, a newer version by itself, superseded and a pass after the retake day, not done, the order and the verdict, the split and the counts.
- **`TrainingDueStrip.tsx`** (`src/components/training/`, new): the picture, the lien Windows calendar's bar. A thin track from a green dot (the pass) to a diamond (the due day), the last `dueSoonDays` shaded `--bg-amber-200`, today a `--text-link` tick, red at the end once past the day; the superseded case runs to a red cut with a grey dot and no window. Under it the two labels: *passed · Nov 3, 2025 · version 1a2b3c4d* and *due Nov 3 · in 4 days* (or *was due · 27 days ago*, or *counts for nothing from Oct 1 · everyone takes it again*). `compact` draws the track alone for a list row. `DueChipSpan` is the chip. Nothing drawn with no pass.
- **`DashboardTrainingSection`**: the head carries the **Due strip** switch; on, the verdict line is `dueVerdict`'s, each required course gets its chip beside the title and the strip in place of the *Done … due again …* line (the labels say it), the size or lessons line stays for a course not done, the press is unchanged. Offered courses are untouched. `data-training-shape` is `words`, `strip` or `side-by-side`.
- **`SettingsTrainingTab`**: a **Due strips** switch under the lede (the same per-user key, so one press turns both surfaces); on, *Where everyone stands* is four chips, **current N · due soon N · lapsed N · not done N**, each a press (`aria-pressed`, dead at zero) that opens See who filtered to its people, the same chip again lifting the filter; each name gets the chip with its days and a compact strip from the standing's days; the row names the course's current version (`contentVersion` joins the lazy bundle facts). Off, the tab is as before.
- **Smokes**: four new cases in `DashboardTrainingSection.render.test.tsx` (off by default, due soon with the window and the split verdict, lapsed, superseded, the dev's side by side) and one in `SettingsTrainingTab.render.test.tsx` (the chips, the filter, the strips, the version).
- **Guide** *take a training course*: a section on the switch.
- **Docs**: the card's row in `DASHBOARD_SECTIONS_ARCHITECTURE.md`.

## How to open the side by side

On a dev server from this branch, sign in as the dev (`/dev-login?as=1&to=%2Fdashboard%3Fsbs%3D1`). The Training card draws only for a role a course is required of, so as the dev the card shows what the dev's role must hold; Settings → View as a helper shows the card as a helper sees it, with the **Due strip** switch (no side by side there, since the dev check reads the signed-in role). Settings → Training with `?sbs=1` (`/settings?tab=settings-training&sbs=1`) draws *Where everyone stands* both ways.

## What the mock-up asked for that this does not do

- **People's chip.** The finding's third door, the amber *training lapsed* chip on People for someone still current, is a words change in `trainingChip` (*training due soon* when no course is past its day). It is not behind a switch and would change the roster for the office the day it merged, so this PR leaves it; it is a one-line follow-up once she says the split reads right.
- **A strip on People.** The roster's rail reads `training_standings` through `loadTrainingChipsByUser` and draws a chip, not a row per course; the strip there is the desk's job, not this card's.
- **The version on Settings' name strips.** `training_standings` returns days and a colour, no version, so a name's strip there has no version label; the course's current version is named once on the row instead.
