# v2.8530 — Take back is now a dev’s and the controller’s (2026-11-24)

Punch list #403, the answer to the first question its first decision asked: *Who may take a paper back?* One migration, `20261124214500_take_back_dev_and_controller.sql` ([its note](../migrations/20261124214500_take_back_dev_and_controller.md)); no edge function.

## The answer

Take back was built for the leader and a dev (v2.8348, and v2.8488 for a trade company's own paper), the two roles the app lets delete a paper anywhere. The card asked whether that was right. Asked on 2026-11-24 with three choices, the answer was *Dev and controller only*: the controller gains the press and the leader loses it. An assistant still has none.

## What changed

- **The rule** `canTakeBackPapers` (`paperTakeBack.ts`) is a dev and the controller. It was `canDeleteFromContractLibrary`, the leader and a dev, and is now a rule of its own.
- **Documents → Agreements**: an outside party's line and a trade company's line that are not signed carry **Take back** for those two roles.
- **People → Contracts**: under a company's row, a paper's **Take back** follows the same rule. It followed *whoever may delete a paper here*, which is still the leader and a dev and still decides every other delete on the tab.
- **The function** `take_back_company_paper` asks `is_dev() OR is_controller()` and refuses anyone else with *Only a dev or the controller takes a paper back.* Nothing else in it moved.
- **Help**: *find an agreement we sent or signed* and *put a sub under their company* name the two roles.

## What it does not change

- **Deleting a paper** on People → Contracts and in the Contract library stays the leader's and a dev's.
- **The table's delete policy**, which answers the office's four roles. For an outside party's paper the app's rule is narrower than the database, as it was before.
- **What Take back does**, its confirm, its words and what it refuses.

## After the merge

`supabase db push` for `20261124214500`. The client ships first: until the push the controller's press on a company's paper is refused by the old door in words, and nothing is deleted. No types change, since the function's shape is the same.
