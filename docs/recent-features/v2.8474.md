## The call the card asked for, and the pick

**Where is a left-out paper remembered, and does the next run take it by itself?** The card said to ask before building. Diane said to build it, so this takes the smallest default that can never lose a paper and never send one unseen. It is one thing to flip.

| Answer | What it costs | What it risks |
|---|---|---|
| In the browser, for this sitting | No migration | Each seat sees another list, a reload forgets it, and nothing can say who or when. |
| On record, and it stays out until a person puts it back | One table | A paper sits out of every run with nobody asked. It is only a line at the foot of a list. |
| On record for one run, and the next run takes it back by itself | One table | A paper left out because the customer was paying is printed next month with nobody having looked. |
| **On record, and the next run asks again** (the pick) | One table, and one answer a paper a run | A left-out paper costs a press each run until it is settled. |

**The pick.** A leave-out is a row in `lien_run_left_out` with the run's day, the reason, who and when. In the run it was made for, the paper is left out and listed with **Put it back**. In the next run it is neither taken in nor left out: it comes first on the checklist with the old reason, and a person answers **Put it back** or **Leave out again**. Until then the run does not count it as right. To flip it: `lienLeftOutStandings` in `src/lib/jobs/lienLeftOut.ts` is the one function that decides, and its two branches are the two other answers.

It needs a migration: `20261124093000_lien_run_left_out.sql`, one table and one function, with its bed. See [`docs/migrations/20261124093000_lien_run_left_out.md`](../migrations/20261124093000_lien_run_left_out.md).

## What was built

- **The door.** One line over the list under the Board: *This run · 21 papers due by tomorrow · 6 to settle* and **Get it right ›**. With nothing to settle it reads *Every paper in the run is right* and the press is **Open the run ›**.
- **The checklist** takes the list's place under the Board while it is open, with **‹ The list** as the way back. It holds the run's head, the three steps (**Get it right**, **Print**, **Out the door**) with what waits at each, then the questions.
- **A question** is one paper that cannot print as it stands: the job, the paper's bin in the Board's words, the one thing it waits on, what the job owes, and the press that settles it. The bin, the words and the press are the paper's To do row's own (`buildLienNextUp`), so the checklist and the list say one thing and open one screen. The papers the Board marks **!** come first.
- **Right** is a paper that can print or is printed: an approved and signed notice, a printed notice, an affidavit ready to file. They sit under one fold.
- **Leave out** opens in place under the line: five reasons, a box for a few words, and **Leave it out**. *Another reason* needs words. The paper moves to **left out of this run** with who left it out, when and why, and **Put it back**.
- **Every paper right.** The checklist says so and offers the Board's two prints, which exist today.
- **A failed read** of the left-out rows shows its reason in the door's place with Retry, and no count: a count drawn without those rows could call a left-out paper right.

## Where the build left the card and the mock-up

- **The door is a line, not the first tile.** The card says the run opens from the Board's first tile. On `main` that tile's red line is a press of its own (v2.7371, the owner's pick of 2026-11-18), and a tile that is a press cannot hold a second one. The line stands right under the find box.
- **One line a paper.** The mock-up folds two notices under one question with one Leave out. A leave-out is a paper's own, with its own reason, so each paper has its line.
- **No *ask the attorney first* line.** The mock-up draws one on a ready affidavit. No fact on `main` marks a ready affidavit that way, so nothing draws it. *The attorney is asked first* is one of the reasons to leave a paper out.
- **Nothing is locked.** The mock-up locks **Print the run** until step 1 reads zero. That gate is step 6. Until it is built the Board's two prints take every ready paper, left out or not, and a left-out paper whose question is answered since says so on its line. This step changes no rule about when a paper may go.
- **Print counts affidavits until they are filed.** No print is recorded for an affidavit, so the second step reads *N affidavits for the notary* while any is ready to file.
- **The find box stays** over the checklist and does not narrow it.
