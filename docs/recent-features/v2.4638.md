# v2.4638 — Legal portal: the sample matter tells one story (2026-10-05)

One edge function (`legal-portal`, deploy after merge, for the promise rule), the shared sample fixture and the firm's page; no migration. Item 9 of punch list #85 ([`to-dos/legal-portal-firm-ready/`](../../to-dos/legal-portal-firm-ready/README.md)). Mockup: `mockup-9-sample-matter.html` beside the card, with before and after screenshots of the portal's panels (Account, Paper, Their word, Fees & steps, Lien grid), walked in the preview browser.

## The defect

`/legal?t=sample` is what the firm is shown, and it contradicted itself. The note said *start with a demand on the GC*, but the job read *original contractor · § 53.056 notice not required · contracted with the owner* (the GC was stored as the customer, not as `gc_customer_id`). The payment had no date and no check number (the fixture used `payment_date` / `reference`, not the real row's `paid_on` / `reference_number`). There was no property record, no demand letter and no lien paper, so Paper said *the affidavit window closed with nothing filed — the lien is gone* and the Lien grid had no tab. The collections note said *two promises missed* beside one promise. Click's particulars were blank.

## What changed

- [`_shared/customerSampleFixtures.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/customerSampleFixtures.ts) `sampleLegalMatter`: one story, every date `d(-n)` / `at(-n)` from today, every row with the columns `legal-portal` selects for a real matter. Sample Holdings LLC owns the building and is the job's customer; Sample Contracting is its GC (`gc_customer_id`, `gcCustomer`) and the payer, so Click is the subcontractor.
  - Work 110 to 66 days ago (four approved GPS clock sessions, two GPS field reports).
  - A $4,000 progress bill, a promise, check #2291 paid 84 days ago (kept). The final $14,400 bill, a second promise, missed (broken).
  - A property record on the payer's address list (Hays County, owner of record, legal description, parcel), and `customer_address_id` on the job for item 6.
  - A § 53.056 notice to the owner and the GC 40 days ago covering every work month in time (a filing with its sends, and the desk item that sent it, shaped as the function shapes it), with the owner's answers: still owes the GC $12,000, holds the 10% (pile A).
  - A final demand on the GC by certified mail with tracking, three enclosures and its deadline passed.
  - The firm's fee, the firm's question (seen) and the office's answer. A collections note that matches the promises.
- `sampleLegalLienBook` (new): the book's raw rows for three jobs, so the Lien grid draws. This job (both months noticed, the affidavit window open); another GC's job whose next notice is the first deadline not yet passed; an original contractor's residential repipe whose affidavit was filed two days ago and must be served, so *Something due* is never empty. Deadlines come from `sampleStatutoryFifteenth`, the client's `statutoryFifteenth` rule, held equal by a test.
- `sampleLegalPortalResponse`: the particulars filled with plainly sample values (license, registered agent, custodian, affiant, each ending "(sample)"), and `lienBook`.
- `LegalPortal.tsx`: for the sample token the page builds `sampleLegalPortalResponse(PORTAL_COMPANY, todayYmdInAppTz())` itself and makes no fetch. The function answers the sample token with the same fixture, so the two cannot differ, and the demo shows the new sample as soon as the page ships.
- `legal-portal` promise records: `billedTotal` is what was billed on the job when the promise was made ([`_shared/legalPromiseBilled.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/legalPromiseBilled.ts) `billedAtPromise`), the rule the desk's `list_payment_promise_records` RPC uses. It summed every bill on the job (and fell back to the matter's total), so a promise kept on a first draw read as broken on the firm's page once the final bill went out.

- **Integration pass (review fixes).** (a) The sample takes its company from `PORTAL_COMPANY`; the render test mocks that name (*Acme Mechanical*) so item 3's check that nothing hard-codes the brand still holds, and every sample test opens the page-built sample with no fetch. (b) `legalPacket.ts` dates a filing in *What the company did* by its filed day, else its served day, else its first send's `sent_on`, else the day its record was made (`LegalFilingLine.wentOutYmd`): a § 53.056 notice is mailed, never filed, so it used to read *—*, real notices included. (c) The GC's office is in Hays County with the property (San Marcos), not Austin. (d) A bill's date for the promise rule is `COALESCE(billed_at, created_at)`, exactly the RPC's, with no `sent_to_customer_at` fallback; `legal-portal`'s invoice select adds `created_at` (item 23's `MATTER_INVOICE_COLUMNS` needs it too). (e) The demand letter's `enclosures` are `{ label, kind, title, pages }` like real rows, and the matter carries `jobAddresses` (the building's record) and `jobOwners` (none) under item 6's keys. (f) The story's people and places have plausible fictional names: Brazos Ridge Contracting (the GC, Pat Holloway), Alvarado Holdings LLC (the owner, Jordan Reyes), 200 Creekside Pkwy, Plum Creek Dental, the Whitfield repipe, real-looking tracking and recording numbers; the particulars keep *(sample)* and the strip says *this is what the law firm sees*. The law firm itself stays *Sample & Partner, PLLC* (it is every sample email's firm). (g) The sample's Lien grid opens on *All*, so the matter's own job shows beside the due ones; a real firm's grid still opens on *Something due*.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **Will the demo show the new sample tomorrow?** Not if it waits on a redeploy. Refined: the page builds the sample from the shared fixture, with no network call.
- **Is "one promise kept" honest?** Only once the function agrees with the desk. Fixed here with the RPC's rule in a tested kernel; without it a real matter shaped like the sample would show the first promise as broken to the firm.
- **Does the story hold on every day?** The test builds the packet and the grid on eight days (month ends, a leap day, a year turn, weekends): the notice in time, the affidavit open, nothing missed.
- **Is the grid empty on its default view?** It was on the first draft. Refined with the repipe's service copy, always due.
- **Left for other items:** the payment still prints `$-4,000.00` (item 5's formatter); the grid's paid-out and 10 % columns stay "?" although the owner answered (the grid kernel does not read the owner's call); the firm's print packet on #4601 reads `contacts[].role`, which neither the sample nor the function sends (`note` is the column).
