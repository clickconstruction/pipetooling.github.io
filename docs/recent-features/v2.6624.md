# v2.6624 — Lien desk: a sent job with new months due sits in To draft, its reminders riding along (2026-11-07)

Punch list #248, face one (PR 1 of two). The owner picked rule A on 2026-11-07: a job with months due goes to To draft whatever went out before, since a month's notice is a statutory deadline and the courtesy and the attorney's letter can ride on the To draft row.

## What was wrong

`buildLienDeskQueue` decided a job's pile in this order: a live item wins; else a notice sent inside thirty days puts the job in **Sent · 30d**; else months due put it in **To draft** or **Needs the owner**; else a closed month puts it in **Missed**. Job 650 on 2026-11-02 — Aug + Sep due in 14 days, the Jul notice sent Oct 14 — sat in Sent with letter two's doors and no draft door; the path read *Draft the Aug + Sep notice — 14 days*; Do now showed letter two for 650 and no draft row; the Dashboard's draft count (`summarizeLienDeskForNeedsYou`, which reads only the drafting piles) left it out. The row would have moved to To draft on Nov 13, three days before Aug's window closed. The Oct 14 run could not have carried Aug: the months RPC looks thirty days ahead and Aug's Nov 16 was 33 days away, so every job that gets a notice near a deadline sat this way while the next month's window ran down.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/jobs/lienDesk.ts`** — the pile order is now live item → months due → sent inside thirty days → missed. `LienDeskEntry.sentItem` is the latest notice sent inside `LIEN_DESK_SENT_DAYS` on whatever pile the row sits; `item` is the live item, else the sent one on the Sent pile alone, so a drafting row's readers (the stored draft, the months named, the offer) start the new notice from the job as they always have. A Sent row is unchanged: `item` and `sentItem` are the same record. `summarizeLienDeskForNeedsYou` and `lienDeskNextDeadline` read the drafting piles, so the Dashboard's draft count and the office's next deadline follow without a change of their own.
- **`src/lib/jobs/lienNextUp.ts`** — the courtesy, promise and letter-two rows (the REMINDERS heading) are built for every entry on the Sent pile or carrying a `sentItem`, with the row's own pile as the target, before the pile switch; the `sent` case is empty. One job can now have its draft row under *Needs you now* / *Coming up* and its letter-two row under *Reminders*, and the reminder's press opens the To draft pane, where the door is.
- **`src/components/jobs/LienDeskModal.tsx`** — the Notices list row off the Sent pile says *· sent Oct 14* (`data-lien-desk-sent-words`) and wears the letter-two chip when its job has a `sentItem`. The sent notice's facts (`lt`, `first`, `courtesy`, the courtesy's facts and `openCourtesy`, `jobBalance`, counsel's sign-off, `startTwo`) moved out of the Sent footer's branch to the footer's shared scope, with `first` falling back to `selected.sentItem`; the courtesy door and the attorney's letter door (its menu included) are `courtesyDoor` / `letterTwoDoor`, drawn on the Sent footer as before and, on a drafting footer whose entry carries a `sentItem`, on a row above the draft row (`data-lien-desk-sent-doors`): *Sent Oct 14 · day 19 · the attorney’s letter overdue · the next notice drafts below* with the two doors at the right. The Sent footer's other doors (the owner's call, the GC's okay, the promise, counsel) stay on the Sent pane.
- **Guides** — [send lien notices from the Lien desk](../../src/content/help/send-lien-notices-from-the-lien-desk.md) says where a sent job with a new month due sits and what its footer carries; [read the reminders ladder on the Lien desk](../../src/content/help/read-the-reminders-ladder-on-the-lien-desk.md) says a rung's Open lands on the notice's own pile.
- **Tests** — `lienDesk.test.ts` (650's shape: To draft with `sentItem`, Needs the owner without the owner, Sent with nothing due, a live draft beside a sent notice, the sent notice aging out), `lienNextUp.test.ts` (the draft row and the letter-two row for one job, the reminder targeting To draft), `LienDeskModal.render.test.tsx` (the To draft row's sent words and chip, the footer's sent doors beside Send for approval, no Sent pile). The Docsmit test's fixture now notices every month, since its subject is the Sent pile's Deliveries filter.

## Not here

- A job with a live letter-two draft and new months due still shows the letter two's pane (one live item per job, as before); the new notice drafts once the letter two is sent or pulled back. Nobody has hit it; a card if someone does.
- The gates on a Sent row with nothing due are face two, PR 2 (v2.6625).
