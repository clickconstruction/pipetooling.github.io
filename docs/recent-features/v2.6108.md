# v2.6108 — Legal desk Evidence: the papers under headings by whose they are, the state as a chip, filters where the count was (2026-10-30)

Punch list #214 (`to-dos/legal-desk-show-not-tell/`), PR 3b of 4, on v2.6106's column. The Evidence tab's *Documents for the firm* was a wall: every row in the order it was added, each a kind · size · day line and a sentence, the sent ones repeating *Sent Oct 27 by certified mail to …* in full. A live account held 39 of them, and the firm's first three asks — the payer's notice, its reply, the waiver — sat among supplier invoices and screenshots. A lawyer reads a file by whose paper it is; the mock-up tried grouping by state first and dropped it (*final* is thirty papers from five parties and answers nothing).

## What changed

- **The kernel** ([`_shared/legalMatterDocuments.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/legalMatterDocuments.ts), pure, shared with the `legal-portal` function for the follow-up; 7 tests): `LEGAL_DOCUMENT_WHOSE` (`theirs` · `ours` · `public` · `supplier`; null is *our record*), `legalDocumentGroupWords` (the payer's name as a possessive, *Ours*, *Public record*, *The supplier's*, *Our record*), `legalDocumentWhoseGuess` — the guess for a row with no `whose`, drawn and never written back: the public record's words (city, county, permit, inspection, law…), *to <payer>* is ours, a capture, report, transcript, affidavit or mail receipt is our record, the payer's name among the title's first three words or as a possessive (or opening the *shows* line) is theirs, a sent paper is ours and a received one theirs, an invoice or receipt is the supplier's, a title that opens like a letter or a bill (or with our own name) is ours — `legalDocumentGroupOf` (a row's own `whose` wins; `guessed` says which), `groupLegalDocumentsByWhose` (the groups in reading order, newest paper first, an empty group left out), `legalDocumentStateChipWords` (*sent · certified mail* · *received* · *draft* · *final*), and the filters (`LEGAL_DOCUMENT_FILTERS`, `legalDocumentPassesFilter`, `legalDocumentFilterCounts`, `legalDocumentFilterEmptyWords`).
- **The IO** ([`legalMatterDocumentsIo.ts`](../../src/lib/legal/legalMatterDocumentsIo.ts)): `whose` on the row and the select list, on the insert when the draft names it, and `setLegalMatterDocumentWhose` for *Whose paper…*. The column is spelled on the row type until the next types chore regenerates `database.ts`; the one cast in `listLegalMatterDocuments` goes with it. `LegalDocumentDraft.whose`.
- **The panel** ([`LegalMatterDocumentsPanel.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/legal/LegalMatterDocumentsPanel.tsx), `payerName` from the Paper tab's account): the groups under headings with their counts and *N by title* when rows sit there by the guess (`data-legal-document-group`, `data-legal-document-group-guessed`); each row a state chip (`data-legal-document-state-chip`, the full send record on its hover, a draft still `data-legal-document-draft`), a *held* chip, the paper's day as a column (`data-legal-document-day`, both days on its hover), the title and the *shows* line on one line with the rest on hover (`data-legal-document-shows`), the ⋯ at the right; the row's record — kind, size, days, the send record with its proof, the hold reason, *Under <group> by its title* — leads the ⋯ menu (`data-legal-document-record`) and no longer prints under every title. **Whose paper…** on the menu opens a pick on the row (`data-legal-document-whose-form`). The add form's **Whose** select starts on the title's guess and follows the title until the person picks (`data-legal-document-whose-pick`, `data-guessed`). The filters stand where the count was (`data-legal-document-filter`: All · Sent · Received · Drafts · Held · Added this week, the week from the company's Sunday), a filter with nothing behind it dead, and an emptied filter draws `EmptyState` with *Show every paper*.
- **Guide** *review a collections account before it goes to your attorney*: the Documents section says the headings, the chips, the filters and *Whose paper…*.
- **Tests**: `legalMatterDocuments.test.ts` (+7), `LegalMatterDocumentsPanel.render.test.tsx` (+3; the count, the draft chip and the record assertions follow the new shape).

## What the mock-up asked for that this does not do

- The mock-up's chip read *sent · certified*; the row's reads *sent · certified mail*, the Timeline's words (v2.6107), so one paper reads the same on both tabs.
- The firm's portal and both prints still list the documents flat. The follow-up adds `whose` to `MATTER_DOCUMENT_COUNSEL_COLUMNS` and groups `FirmDocuments` the same way (a `legal-portal` deploy after the column is on prod), then the prints; dev-mcp's `plan_matter_document` takes `whose` then too. The Timeline's `documentChipWords` (v2.6107) says the same words as `legalDocumentStateChipWords` and folds into it in that pass.
- The punch-list card's status line rides on PR 1's branch and is updated once it is on `main`.
