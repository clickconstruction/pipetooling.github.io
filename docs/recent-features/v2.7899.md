# v2.7899 — Lien desk: the list's heading says what it counts (2026-11-18)

The owner's pick of 2026-11-18, one of the last five pieces of the Board's drawing (punch list #383, option B) that a live look found still different. He saw each redrawn on the Board as it stands and picked all five; each is its own PR. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## Before

- Three numbers stand on To do and each counts by its own rule, and nothing said so. The tab, *34 to do*, counts the rows the viewer can move (`lienNextUpCount`). The first tile counts the papers due by the next due day, and its label does say *by Monday*. The head over the list read *Needs you now · 27*: papers, the ones that need someone now with the ones that wait on the leader, while the rest wait folded under **Later**. A reader set 27 beside 34 and could not make them meet.

## What changed

- **The head names its unit and says where the rest are.** On a computer it reads *Needs you now · 27 papers*, then in grey *12 more under Later*. `LienDeskNextUp` reads both from `lienNextUpNowWords` (`lienNextUpNowWords.ts`, a file of its own): the first is the sum of the bins' own `papers`, so it still equals the heads under it added up, and the second is the Later group's own figure. With nothing under Later the grey line is not drawn.
- **Not the drawing's words.** The drawing read *26 of the 34 to do · 8 more under Later*. That cannot be said truthfully today: the tab counts rows and leaves out what waits on the leader, the head counts papers and keeps them in, and one row can stand for several papers (a GC's run row, a folded stack of affidavits). The owner saw the smaller form drawn and picked it.

## What it leaves alone

- **The tab's count and the tile's count** are unchanged. Making the tab and the head one number is a change to a counting rule, not to words, and is not made here.
- **A phone.** Its list is by the day (`lienPhoneGroups`) and has no such head.
- **The list by day** (no Board drawn) keeps its head, *Needs you now · N* and who it waits on.
