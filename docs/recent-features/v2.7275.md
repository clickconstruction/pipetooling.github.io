# v2.7275 — GC difficulty rating, part 1: the criteria a GC is rated on, versioned (2026-11-14)

Punch list #357, PR 1 of six ([`to-dos/gc-difficulty-rating/README.md`](../../to-dos/gc-difficulty-rating/README.md); the canvas is linked from the card). One migration (`20261114090000_difficulty_criteria_versions.sql`, [its fragment](../migrations/20261114090000_difficulty_criteria_versions.md)), no edge function, no kernel copied to the edge functions.

## Why

The company loses bids because a master in the field asks the estimator to add twenty or fifty thousand dollars to a price for a GC that is hard to work with, with no reason attached, and an estimator will not question a master. The owner's design of 2026-11-14: rate the GC once, in sentences a person picks from, and let a price list the office owns turn the points into cost lines on the estimate. The sentences are the dev's and versioned, so that a rating and a priced bid pin to the version they were made under and a new version never moves old points. This PR is the versioned criteria and the dev's editor. Nothing prices anything yet.

## What changed

- **[`difficulty_criteria_versions`](../migrations/20261114090000_difficulty_criteria_versions.md)**: one row per version, `body` jsonb, one draft at a time, a published row immutable under its guard, everyone signed in reads and a dev writes. Seeded with version 1: **Paying** (read from the bills, 0 to 1), **Field and superintendent** (0 to 3) and **Their office** (0 to 2), the sentences as the canvas drew them.
- **[`src/lib/bids/difficultyCriteria.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/difficultyCriteria.ts)**: the shape (`DifficultyCriteriaBody`, scales of `{key, name, scope, source, max, rungs}`), `parseDifficultyCriteriaBody` (lenient, for a half-typed draft), `difficultyCriteriaProblems` (the rules a body keeps before a publish, each a sentence), `difficultyTotalPoints` (what a GC's picks add up to under a version, with the unrated and the stale), and the carry-over rule `carryDifficultyPicks`: a pick carries to a newer version only when its scale key and its points value still exist there; a removed scale or rung leaves that scale unrated, a new scale starts unrated, nothing is recomputed from old words onto new words. `DIFFICULTY_CRITERIA_V1` is the seed, and the test reads the migration file to hold the two equal.
- **[`src/lib/bids/difficultyEditor.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/difficultyEditor.ts)**: every press on the card as a pure operation over a body (add, remove and move a scale; set its name, key, scope, source and maximum; add, remove and set a rung), `diffDifficultyCriteria` (what a draft changes against the published version, line by line, including *the 2 points rung removed. A GC on it becomes unrated here.*) and `difficultyKeyIsLocked` (a key the published version has carries ratings and cannot change).
- **[`src/lib/bids/difficultyCriteriaIo.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/difficultyCriteriaIo.ts)**: the reads and writes behind one interface, the table read by name until the types are regenerated.
- **[`src/components/bids/DifficultyScalesPicker.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/DifficultyScalesPicker.tsx)**: the scales drawn as a person meets them, one block per scale, 44 px rungs as radio rows, a bills scale with its chip and no pick. The dev's card draws it read only; PR 2 draws it on the GC's record and PR 5 on the master's phone sheet.
- **Settings → Difficulty criteria** ([`DifficultyCriteriaCard.tsx`](../../src/components/settings/DifficultyCriteriaCard.tsx), a dev's tab in the System zone): the published version's line and its scales; **Start a draft**, the editor scale by scale and rung by rung, a note for the list of versions, **Save the draft**, the problems listed over a dead publish press, the fold of changes, **Preview the GC card** (the draft drawn with sample picks), **Publish version N** after a confirm that lists every change, **Discard the draft**, **Every version**. Render smokes over a fake io.
- The group is registered in `settingsGroups.ts` (dev only), `settingsSearch.ts`, the Dev doors list and `Settings.tsx`; the guide *change the difficulty criteria a GC is rated on* (dev).

## Is this the best we can do?

- **Doubted: a bills scale with sentences.** The paying scale's points come from the price list's rule, not from a pick, so its rungs looked like dead weight. Kept them: the card and the sheet need words for what 0 and 1 mean, and the rule's numbers (45 days, two late bills) belong to the price list where the office can change them without a new criteria version. A bills scale may also carry no rungs, and then keeps its own maximum.
- **Doubted: letting the dev change a key.** A key carries ratings across versions, so a renamed key would silently drop every GC's pick on it. Changed: the key box is read only on any scale the published version has, and editable only on a scale new in the draft.
- **Doubted: a draft that is a copy of the whole published body.** The alternative was a list of edits applied at publish. Kept the copy: it is what the preview draws, it survives a page reload once saved, and the diff is computed from the two bodies, so the confirm window lists exactly what changes.
- **Left for Micah, with my pick:** where the tab sits. It is its own tab in Settings' System zone, dev only, beside Dev doors, as the canvas drew it (*Settings → Dev → Difficulty criteria*). The other home would be a dev-only card inside Bids & materials next to the Bid Board value rule. My pick is the System zone: the office never needs to see it, and the price list they do need sits on the Bid Board itself in PR 4.
- **Left for Micah, with my pick:** the paying rungs' two sentences (*They pay at the GC pace, or faster.* / *They pay slow. Read from their bills against the price list's rule.*) are mine, not from the canvas. He can reword them from the card without a release; my pick is to leave them until PR 3 shows the numbers under them.
