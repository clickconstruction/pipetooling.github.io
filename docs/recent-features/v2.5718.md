# v2.5718 — Quo connection in Settings: the key and the pickers (2026-10-21)

The owner's ask of 2026-10-21: "In the future when setting things up, I would not like to have to go through a terminal. I'd like for the key to be stored in text in my clicktooling settings." Until now Quo's API key lived only as the `QUO_API_KEY` function secret, and the two cards on Settings → Phone and texts made the owner type Quo's own ids by hand, which Quo's pages never show. One migration (`20261021020000_quo_config.sql`), one new function (`quo-config`), the two senders reading Vault first. No kernel copy change: `quo-config` imports none of `_shared/gcKernels/`.

- **The key in Vault** (`docs/migrations/20261021020000_quo_config.md`): `quo_secret_set(value, saved_by)`, `quo_secret_get()` and `quo_secret_status()`, the service role's only, in the shape of the Docsmit connection's (`20261013160000`). Who saved it is the Vault secret's description, when is its `updated_at`. `_shared/quoKey.ts`'s `loadQuoApiKey` reads Vault first, then `QUO_API_KEY`, so a key saved in Settings wins and nothing breaks while Vault holds none; `sendText` (`quoLines.ts`) and the text's pictures (`quoMedia.ts`) take it, so `quo-send` and `quo-webhook` redeploy with it. `quoGet` reads one of Quo's lists, every page (`limit` 50, `after` the `nextCursor`), with `Quo-Api-Version: 2026-03-30` and the bare key as the Authorization header, against the versioned API's `GET /phone-numbers` and `GET /users` (verified against Quo's docs; the versioned list says `phoneNumber` where v1 said `number`, and the kernel reads both).
- **`quo-config`** (`docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`): the owner's and a dev's door, Bearer JWT then the role from `users` (`master_technician` or `dev`), a real account, not read-only. `status` says whether a key is held and where from (`vault` | `env` | `none`), its last four characters when Vault holds one, when and by whom, never the key; `set` keeps the key with the caller's name, a blank forgets it, a key with a space or under sixteen characters is refused before anything is written; `directory` answers Quo's numbers and members as Quo gives them, 409 without a key, 502 with Quo's own words when it refuses.
- **The Quo connection card** (`src/components/settings/QuoConnectionCard.tsx`, owner or dev, above Company phone seats): the masked key line with when and by whom, a password field for the key, **Save the key**, what Quo answered in numbers, **Ask Quo again** when it did not. `QuoConnectionProvider` (`QuoConnectionContext.tsx`) reads the status and Quo's lists once per opening of the tab and hands them to the two cards under it; a card rendered on its own, or for the office, gets the `off` state and reads as before.
- **The pickers**: Company phone seats' Quo user id becomes **Quo member**, a picker of Quo's members (name, email; the `US…` id small under the pick), and Inboxes' phone and Quo number id become one **Quo number** pick (emoji, name, number; the `PN…` id small under it) that fills both fields together. Each has **Type it by hand** for a row the list does not hold and **Pick from Quo** to go back; a saved id Quo does not list stays typed. Without a key the cards say *Paste the Quo key above and the seats and inboxes become pickers.*; when Quo did not answer, *Quo did not answer, so type the ids by hand for now.*
- **The kernel** (`src/lib/quo/connection.ts`): the status as the function answers it, the two lists by name (a row without Quo's id left out, a member's name from the email when Quo has none, the phone as the inbox keeps it), the masked and the saved words, what stops a key, the cards' state (`loading` | `no-key` | `ready` | `read-failed`), the picker's show rule. `connectionIo.ts` calls the function with the refusal's own words as the error (`readEdgeFunctionErrorBody`).
- **The guides**: *connect the app to Quo* (new; where the key is in Quo, what the card says next), *match each company phone seat to a person* and *set up the two inboxes* (the typed-id steps are the picker steps, the typed id a section of its own). **Docs**: `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` (`quo-config`; `quo-send`'s and `quo-webhook`'s secrets name Vault first), `MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md` (the settings table and § Deploying), `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` and `GLOSSARY.md` (the card and the pickers), `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` (the Vault functions and the door).
- **Tests**: `connection.test.ts` (the kernel); `quoConfig.run.test.ts` (the handler itself over the recording fakes, `FakeDb` gaining `match`); `QuoConnectionCard.render.test.tsx`; the seats and Inboxes smokes gain the picker cases. The dev-mcp catalog is regenerated for the new function.

## Is this the best we can do?

1. **Fewer presses on a phone**: the key is one paste, and a seat or a number is one pick from a list that already carries the name, the emoji and the number.
2. **Plain words**: the three guides pass the sweep; the card's lines name the state in one sentence each.
3. **Do what is ready**: a key the list cannot explain, or a read Quo refused, never blocks the save, and the typed id stays a door.
4. **Reuse**: the Docsmit connection's Vault shape, `readEdgeFunctionErrorBody`, the seats and Inboxes cards' own io and words; the pickers live inside the cards that already own the rows.
5. **Cut**: a webhook health line on the card (the webhook is still made and rotated through Quo's API, `MESSAGES_SWITCH_ON_HANDOFF_2026-10-14.md`); a **Forget the key** button (a new paste replaces it, and the env key stays the floor).
