# v2.4082 — A check that never cleared on a Stripe bill: send the bill back and bill again (2026-09-28)

Taunya, Iannotti PRV (J1040, $600): *"The payment has been deleted through the app. I believe it needs to be fixed through Stripe's end"* — she wanted the bill gone so she could send it again, because the customer's check could not be deposited. Verified live: Sep 23 the Stripe bill went out; Sep 24 09:31 Mark Paid · Check → `record-stripe-invoice-out-of-band-payment` closed the Stripe invoice `paid` (`amount_paid` 0, no charge, `pt_paid_on` 2026-09-24) and the webhook wrote the payment row; Sep 25 09:19 Mercury synced the `checkDeposit` as `failed` ("There was an issue with this transaction"), never matched in AR; Sep 25 16:08 the payment row was deleted directly (`deleted_records_archive` has it, no `jobs_ledger_payment_events` row, no `stripe_oob_payment_reverts` row) and the payments trigger put the job back to Billed. After that every door was shut: `void-stripe-invoice-for-revert` passed its payments check and 409'd on Stripe's `paid` ("Invoice is paid or has payments in Stripe; resolve in Stripe before sending back" — her screenshot), **Undo out-of-band payment** only shows on a Paid bill and its RPC refuses a bill with no applied payments, `ensure_single_ready_to_bill_invoice_for_job` said nothing left to bill (600 − 0 − 600), and Stripe never reopens a paid invoice, so the hosted link read Paid and "Resend" would have mailed a receipt.

## The change

- **`void-stripe-invoice-for-revert`** — a Stripe invoice that is `paid` with `amount_paid` 0 and no `charge` holds only ClickTooling's own out-of-band mark (`stripeHoldsOnlyOobMark`). Instead of the 409, the function issues an out-of-band credit note for the total less any live credit notes already on the invoice (the same note Undo out-of-band payment issues, so a bill unwound there first gets no second note), memo *Payment did not clear — bill sent back in ClickTooling to be billed again*, metadata `pt_oob_revert` + `pt_send_back`; then deletes the ledger row as before. Because `stripe_oob_payment_reverts` cascades away with the invoice, the trail is a `removed` event on `jobs_ledger_payment_events` with `payment_id` null, the invoice id, the amount, the mark's `pt_paid_on` / `pt_payment_type` / `pt_reference`, reason `oob_mark_reversed: payment did not clear (Stripe in_…, credit note cn_…)` and the actor. Response gains `stripe_action: 'reverse_oob_mark'` and `stripe_credit_note_id`. A bill with real money in Stripe (`amount_paid > 0` or a charge) still 409s, now worded *This bill was paid by card or bank transfer through Stripe. Refund it in the Stripe Dashboard before sending it back.* **Redeploy required.**
- **`src/lib/jobs/stripeOobSendBack.ts`** (+9 tests): `stripeShowsOobMarkOnly`, `stripeSendBackKind` (`void_open` | `reverse_oob_mark` | null), `oobMarkPaidOnWords`, `stripeSendBackWords` (button, title, bullets, note, confirm, action for both kinds), `paymentRowOffersCheckDidNotClear`, the row's label/title/reason constants, `oobUnwindDoneWords`.
- **`voidStripeInvoiceForRevert.ts`** — `invokeVoidStripeInvoiceForRevert` returns `stripeAction` + `stripeCreditNoteId`; new **`sendBackStripeBilledLine`** runs the one path (edge function → `ensureLedgerInvoiceRemovedAfterStripeSendBack` → `syncJobToReadyToBillIfNoBilledInvoicesRemain`) that View bill's confirm and the Undo modal both call.
- **View bill (`HostedStripeBillPanel`)** — the footer button and the confirm take the kernel's words: on a bill Stripe shows paid by our mark it reads **Check didn't clear · send back…** / *Check didn't clear?* with the amount, the mark's day and the re-bill step; on an open bill it is the old Void Stripe invoice… confirm. The handler is the shared path.
- **Undo out-of-band payment (`UnwindStripeOobPaymentModal`)** — a paragraph says Stripe keeps the old invoice as paid and reversed; a checkbox *Send the bill back to Ready to Bill so it can be billed again (recommended)* is on by default (`sendBackDefault`) and the action reads **Undo and send back**; after the reverse function succeeds the modal runs `sendBackStripeBilledLine` and reports `{ sentBack, sendBackError? }` to its host (`UnwindStripeOobResult`). Takes `initialReason` and `zIndex`; the `invoice` prop narrows to `id` / `amount` / `job_id`. Three render cases.
- **Edit Job → ③ Payments received** — a locked row whose bill Stripe holds as a whole-bill mark (`stripeHoldsPaymentReason` = `paid_in_stripe`, bill still Paid) shows **Check didn't clear…** (`requestCheckDidNotClear`); `JobFormModal` opens the Undo modal on that bill with the reason *Check did not clear* prefilled and the send-back on, re-reads the job and toasts `oobUnwindDoneWords` (or the send-back error with the View bill fallback). One render case.
- Guide *bill a customer and get paid* gains **The check didn't clear?**; *match bank deposits to the bills they pay* routes the Mark Paid case there. `BILLING_FLOWS.md` send-back row and blockers, `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` both sections.

## Decisions taken

- **Stripe's record of money is the gate, not Stripe's status.** `paid` + `amount_paid` 0 + no charge is our own bookkeeping and reverses from our side with the same credit note the Unwind issues; a charge is a refund in the Dashboard.
- **Re-bill, never resend.** Stripe keeps a paid invoice paid; the only way to collect again is a new invoice with a new number, so both doors end at Ready to Bill and the guide says to press Bill Customer.
- **The trail survives the row.** The revert audit table cascades with the invoice; the payment-events row does not.
- **Open question, not chased:** how the payment row on J1040 was deleted directly — the v2.3784 lock should have hidden Remove on a Stripe-held row. Ask Taunya which control she used.
