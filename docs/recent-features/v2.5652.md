# v2.5652 — Pricing: the crew rate stands in until a bid has a labor rate of its own (2026-10-19)

Punch list #128, PR 0 of the price-breakout train (the owner's D5 of 2026-10-19; the plan and mock-ups in `to-dos/bid-price-breakout/`). The labor book fills a bid's hours the first time Labor or Takeoffs opens, but nothing filled the rate: `cost_estimates.labor_rate` stayed null, every row's labor cost read $0, the Workbench called the bid uncosted and its margin read high. The crew rate card on the Labor tab already computed the number (`crewRate.ts`: the last 90 days' field wage × the burden factor) and only showed it. No migration; no edge function.

- **The rule** (`Bids.tsx`): `effectiveLaborRate({ override: cost_estimates.labor_rate, companyRate })`, the Labor tab's own read since v2.3293, now decides the rate Pricing costs at — the bid's own when typed, else the crew rate (`useBidCrewRate`), else nothing — and the one number feeds `useBidPricingRows` (every row's cost, the package and the letter's totals) and `BidsPricingTab` (`computeBidCostBreakdown`, the strip, the print and CSV through the share hook). A typed rate changes nothing.
- **The words** (`BidsPricingTab`, the new `pricingLaborRateSource` prop): the strip's revenue hover reads *labor at the crew rate, $28.66/h, until Labor has one of its own* while the stand-in is in use. The priced-margin stamp line (*no labor rate*) is what was stamped at the last price write and restamps on the next.
- **A small one found on the way**: `commitWorkbenchTypedPrice` asked the pricing lock before checking for a draft, so clicking into a sale-price cell on a sent bid and leaving raised the *press Revise* toast with nothing to write. The draft check comes first now.
- **Tests**: `crewRate.test.ts` holds `effectiveLaborRate`; the Pricing and Labor render smokes still pass.
- **Docs**: `BIDS_SYSTEM.md`'s Pricing section (the Workbench's rate rule); the to-do's status.
