The phone cards' ⋯ sheets carry the same door as a *Checklists* line with the standing as its badge, *3 of 6*, *filled* or *no questions yet* (`JobsStagesCardList.tsx`). - **The sheet** `PipelineChecklistSheet.tsx`: a `ResponsiveModalShell` beside the row, the phone's bottom sheet. The summary line Documents draws (*1 of 3 stages ready · 2 items added on this job*), then every stage that takes a checklist with its chip (*Not ready · 3 of 6*, *Ready · 4 of 4*, *No questions yet*) and, open under it, the stage's own rows as the strip's sheet draws them: `StageChecklistSheet` gained a `frame="inline"` that renders its body and rule line without its own window and Close, so the office gets exactly the strip's rows and presses, one row open at a time with Yes · No · Not here, the stamp, Add a note, the photo where one is asked, and for whoever authors on the job + Add a check, Skip on this job, Take off and ▲ ▼. ## Decisions

- **The count is what is left, not the whole job.** The card's mockup reads *3/6* on a job whose other two stages are filled. Adding the filled stages in would read *10/13* and hide what the office looks for; the sum over the open stages reads the mockup's number with one open stage and stays honest with two. The title names every stage either way.
- **A lucide glyph in the cell, not the mockup's boxed ☐ chip.** The strip's grid gives every icon the same square cell (v2.4305); a chip two cells wide would break the rows of four. The tone and the badge carry the mockup's three states.
- **The strip's rows, lifted, not a second control.** The card asked for the three presses on each row; drawing them again would be a second answer control to keep in step. `StageChecklistSheet` frames itself inline instead, a small change to its return, so #245's press on the open row reaches the door for free.
- **The board re-reads on close, not on every press.** The board's read is chunked across every job it shows; re-reading it on each Yes would be four queries a press. The sheet's own chip moves at once; the row behind the sheet catches up when it closes.
- **The name mark stays, and becomes a press.** The owner's glance at the stage names under the bar is where he noticed the checklists; the mark now opens what it reports.
