# v2.6797 — Scan a box label with no sign-in: what is in it, the newest photo, the last count (punch list #294, PR 2) (2026-11-09)

The owner's ask of 2026-11-09 ([`to-dos/prts-box-page/README.md`](../../to-dos/prts-box-page/README.md)): someone walks up to a cage, scans the label's QR, and sees what is inside with the newest photo and the manifest, signing in only to change a count. The labels, the prts.co redirect and the arithmetic in SQL already existed; this PR adds the public read and moves the route out from behind the sign-in.

## What changed

- **`supabase/functions/box-read/index.ts`** (new, `verify_jwt = false`): `GET ?code=<eight hex>` answers the live container by its code, its lines from `on_site_job_summary` cut to the container, the day of its last count and its newest photo; `&photo=<id>` streams the photo's bytes from Drive through the service account (or from the `box-photos` bucket while the sweep has not filed it), with an hour's cache. A code no box carries counts a miss through the estimate link's guard and answers 404, 429 `locked` past 20 misses in the hour; a retired box answers 410. Each open lands a `public_page_views` row, surface `box_page`; a staff session or `?preview=1` is stamped and not counted. The pure part is `_shared/boxPage.ts`, re-exported by `src/lib/materials/boxPage.ts` with `boxPageView` and tested there. [`EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`](../EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md#box-read).
- **`/b/:code` moves out of the Layout** (`src/App.tsx`), so the sign-in gate never swallows a scan. `src/pages/BoxCode.tsx` forks on the session: signed in, the box's screen on the On site tab as before; signed out, **`src/components/materials/BoxPublicPage.tsx`** on the portal's paper: the stencil and place, the newest photo, the lines with their counts, *Last counted …*, and **Sign in to change a count**, which writes `/b/<code>` into `POST_LOGIN_REDIRECT_KEY` and goes to `/sign-in`, which lands back on the box with a session. Never the job, the customer, a price or a name. The route has its row in `customerSurfaceRegistry.ts`.
- **Docs**: `MATERIALS_ON_SITE_ARCHITECTURE.md` (the box page beside the doors), `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` (what the signed-out read answers and what it never can), `docs/twins/APP_DIRECTORY.md`, the guide *keep track of the material in the cages and boxes on site*.

## Release order

Merge, then `supabase functions deploy box-read`. Nothing in the database changes (`on_site_job_summary`, `estimate_link_miss` and `public_page_views` exist; the service role keeps its default EXECUTE on the summary). Until the deploy a signed-out scan reads the gateway's refusal on the page with Try again; a signed-in scan is unchanged.

## Test

On a ZZ test job: add a cage, print its label, take one photo on the phone and let the sweep file it. In a browser with no session open `prts.co/b/<CODE>`: the 301, then the page with the stencil, the photo, the lines and the press, nothing of the job. A wrong code: *is not a box on any job*. Press Sign in to change a count and sign in as a helper on the job's team: the On site tab opens on that cage. Retire the cage and scan again: the retired words.
