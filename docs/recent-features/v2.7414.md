# v2.7414 — Painted maps: a shared map asks who is opening it (2026-11-15)

Punch list #345, the link that asks who is looking, step C. The table is v2.7411 and the function's side is v2.7413. This is the page at `/way/<code>`.

## What changed

- **A name step first.** A reader who is not signed in sees who sent the map and the map's name, and a box: *Who is opening this map?* The map opens on **Open the map**.
- **The page says what is kept before it asks,** under the box: *Click Plumbing and Electrical keeps your name, the phone or computer you are on, and when you open this map. If you press Show where I am, they also see where you are.*
- **Asked once a phone.** The name and the device's id are kept on that phone, so the same person opening the same link again, or another map's link, is not asked twice. *Not you? Change the name* under the map forgets it and asks again.
- **A signed-in teammate types nothing.** Their session names them from their account, and that name is not kept on the phone.
- **Show where I am tells before it shares.** The line under the press reads *… will see where you are while this is on.* Once on, the box says the company sees it and how to stop. The page sends the first place at once and then about one a minute.
- **The old sentence is gone from a real link:** *Where you are stays on your phone. We never see it.* It stays on the sample page, which sends nothing and keeps nothing.
- Links already out ask too. It is one page for every link.

## How it is built

- Pure, in `paintedWay.ts` (+6 tests): `wayDeviceId`, `wayRememberedName`, `rememberWayName`, `wayScreenWords`, `wayPointIsDue`, and the words (`wayKeptWords`, `wayWillSeeWords`, `wayIsSeenWords`, `wayOpenedAsWords`).
- `paintedWayIo.open` posts `way.open` and `here` posts `way.here`. The page's old `read`, a plain `GET`, is gone from the app.
- A name typed when the signal drops is sent again by **Try again** and not asked for twice.

## Is this the best we can do

- **A name is whatever is typed.** The page refuses only an empty one. Proving a name would mean a code by text, which is a sign-in by another word.
- **The device's id lives in the browser.** A private window, or cleared site data, is a new device. The card says so.
- **No second confirm on Show where I am.** The line under the press says who will see, and the phone asks its own question next. A third question would be pressed through unread.
- **The words are the notice.** If links go to people under stricter privacy law, a lawyer should read the name step's two sentences.
