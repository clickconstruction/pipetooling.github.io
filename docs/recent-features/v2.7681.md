# v2.7681 — Supply houses: the aging table is its own file (2026-11-17)

The first half of the twelfth cut of `src/components/SupplyHousesTab.tsx`, step 12 of the *SupplyHousesTab extraction order* in `docs/MATERIALS_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md`. A pure move and one deletion: no behaviour changes.

**What moved.** `src/components/materials/SupplyHouseAgingMap.tsx` is the aging heat map: the phone's one row per house with its five-colour bar, and the desk's table whose every money cell is a door onto the bills it sums. The JSX moved as it stood, with the two style constants only it used and with `agingCellDoorProps`, the helper that makes a cell a door, which only it called. It draws only: the matrix is the tab's, a row opens its house, and a cell asks the aging windows' engine to open on it.

**One helper stopped being written twice.** `formatYmdLocal`, the date as the reader's locale writes it, went to the aging cell window in step 10 and a copy stayed in the tab for the move strip's one use. It lives once now, in the window's kernel `src/lib/materials/agingCellByJob.ts`, with two tests, and so do the words under an aging cell that say how much of it is on a job account. The window's file exports only the window.

**What was deleted.** The tab's `showTitle` prop and the title block behind it. Materials never passed the prop and Quickfill passed `false`, so the block had never drawn. Quickfill's `SupplyHousesSection` now mounts `<SupplyHousesTab />` bare.

**Size.** `SupplyHousesTab.tsx` went from 1,000 lines to 768. The map is 241 lines.

**Proof.** The tab's render test (27 cases) and the Materials page's pass unedited. They press a past-due cell and a current one on the desk table, and open a house from the phone's list.

**Docs.** The map marks this half of step 12. The read that nothing uses and the two reads with no paging are the other half, in their own change, because they change what the page asks the database for.
