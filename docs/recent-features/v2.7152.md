# v2.7152 — A card charge shows its receipt on Banking and Job Summary, and the card holder has a guide for the text (punch list #299, PR 3) (2026-11-13)

PR 3 of three on [`to-dos/ticket-text/README.md`](../../to-dos/ticket-text/README.md), *the rest*: the guide, and the charge row in Banking and in Job Summary's costs. The push beside the text (the owner's call 4) is not in it and waits on her word. It sends nothing, adds no migration and calls no new name.

## What changed

- **The receipt's state as one short form** (`src/lib/tickets/receiptState.ts`, pure): a charge with its paper says which door the paper came by (*Ticket*, or *Receipt from the link*); a charge the app texted about says when (*Texted 6:14 PM*); a charge it could not text says *No text*. The sentence behind each is the chip's title: the reminder's time, or who has no cell on file and what mends it. A charge with none of these draws nothing. The words are the Team queue's own (`receiptLineWords`), so the three screens cannot disagree.
- **Banking** (`BankingMercuryTable.tsx`, the Ledger and User Sort): the chip sits after the store's name, in both layouts of the cell.
- **Job Summary** (`JobSummaryDrilldownMercuryTable`, the six card-charge drill-downs): a **Receipt** column, drawn only when one of the charges has something to say, so an old job's list is as it was. It rides the drill-down's print and CSV as a column of its own.
- **The read** (`receiptStateIo.ts`, `useReceiptStates`): the live ticket and the request of each charge, as the viewer. A table asks about its card purchases of the last 60 days, at most the newest 600 (`receiptStateChargeIds`), 150 a read, so a ledger of fifteen thousand rows asks about a few hundred. It never throws: a read that fails leaves the table with no chips.
- **Guides**: *answer the text about a charge on your card* (new, for the holder: what the text says, the page, two charges in one text, the reminder, STOP and START, no text at all); *sort the team's card purchases from the office* (The receipt on Banking and Job Summary); *sort the bank feed* and *take a photo of a supply house ticket* link to them.

## Who sees a chip

No new permission. The read runs under `counter_tickets`' and `receipt_requests`' own policies: the office sees every charge's state, a card's holder sees their own, and anyone else reads nothing and sees no chip. Banking is the controller's and above already.

## Is this the best we can do?

For a state on a row, yes. Two limits.

- **The chip is not a door.** It says the state and its reason; the ticket's window, + Ticket and Copy the receipt link stay on the Team queue's line and the Tickets list. A chip that opened the ticket would need each table to mount the ticket window, which is more than this row needs today.
- **Sixty days.** A charge older than that shows no chip even when it has a ticket. The window keeps the read small; the Tickets list is the place for an old ticket.

## Proved, and what is not

- The state and which rows are asked about, 9 tests (`receiptState.test.ts`); the read, 4 (`receiptStateIo.test.ts`); both tables, 5 render tests (`ReceiptStateTables.render.test.tsx`): the chip beside a charge with its paper, a texted one and a held one, none on a plain charge, a check or a charge from January, the Sorting tab's combined cell, and the drill-down's column appearing only when it has something to say.
- The guide tests hold the new guide and the amended ones.
- Not seen on the live pages: no request has a text on it until the switch is pressed, so today only the green *Ticket* chips can show.
