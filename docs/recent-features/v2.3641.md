# v2.3641 — Materials: the Job Accounts tab is renamed Held for suppliers (2026-09-20)

Item 5 of `to-dos/supply-house-job-account-aging.md` — the owner's call, made 2026-09-20: *"Yes, rename it to Held for suppliers."* Client and docs only.

Since v2.3423 a **job account** is the per-property account a supply house opens for a job (the house chips, the job strip, the Dispatch errand, the PO moment, the invoice flag, the New Job question, the teal lines on the aging table). The older Materials tab of the same name (v2.2652) is a different thing: per job, what the customer paid against what is paid or owed to supply houses, headline *Held* — so someone told to open a job account landed on a cash-flow lens with no accounts in it.

- The tab button and its heading read **Held for suppliers** (`Materials.tsx`, `MaterialsJobAccountsTab.tsx`); the Pipeline Fix-ups tooltip that sends people there names it too.
- **The key does not change**: `?tab=job-accounts`, the `'job-accounts'` tab id, the component and kernel file names (`MaterialsJobAccountsTab`, `jobAccountsFlow.ts`) and every deep link (Dashboard pin, Pipeline *No job account* chip, the role redirects) are as they were. Code comments say the tab was named Job Accounts until this release.
- Three help guides, `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` (five mentions), `GLOSSARY.md` (the entry is now *Held for suppliers (Materials tab)*, with its anchor and the cross-reference from *Job account*) and `MATERIALS_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md` follow. The guide *see which paid jobs still owe supply houses* keeps "job accounts" as a search keyword and says what the tab used to be called.

The to-do's last item — the Ferguson rep's missing phone and mistyped email — is an office fix on Edit house → Contacts and moved to `owner-decisions-pending.md`; the to-do file is deleted.
