# v2.8591 — Lien desk: Print the run names a right notice that does not print (2026-11-25)

Punch list #362 (`to-dos/lien-to-do-one-run/README.md`), a fault in step 6 (v2.8536) met on the live desk on 2026-11-25, looks only. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

The card **2 · Print the run** read *Nothing waits to print*, a dead press and *No paper is right yet. Settle the 11 above, or leave each out.* The line right under it read *5 papers are right, 1 notice to print*, and the Board read **Print and mail 1 notice**.

Two counts stood side by side and counted different things:

- The checklist (`lienGetItRight.ts`) counts a notice as right from the Board's chip: *Approved · to print* with no open question.
- The press counts the notices the packet prints (`lienRunReadyNotices`). The packet is the run window's own, and the run window knows more. It leaves out a notice it holds (no mailing address, an owner nobody confirmed, a paper parted from its bills), one that waits on the leader's signature, and one whose every copy went with no paper.

`readySelection` built the reason for each stopped envelope and the hook threw it away. `lienRunPrintPress` was handed two counts and was never told a right notice had been dropped. So its words were untrue: a paper was right, and settling the eleven above would not have made that notice print.

## What changed

- `lienRunNoticesStopped` (`lienRunPacket.ts`) answers every notice the packet leaves out that has not printed, in the run's order, each with a reason and a kind. *held*: the run window's own hold in its own words, or *It waits on the leader’s signature.* *paperless*: *Its copies went by Docsmit, so no paper prints.* or *None of its copies goes on paper.*
- `lienRunPrintPress` takes their count. *No paper is right yet* is said only when no paper is right. With a right notice that does not print it reads *No paper prints now. 1 right notice does not print here. It is named below with its reason.*, then how many more wait on step 1. A live press says the same sentence under its own.
- The card (`LienGetItRight.tsx`) lists each under the press: the job, then the reason. When one of them is held, one more line says where it is settled: *The Board’s Print and mail press opens the run window, where a held notice is settled.*
- The guide *read the Board on the Lien desk* says a notice can be right and not print here, and why.

## What it does not change

- **What prints.** The packet, the stack, the stamps and the filed copies are as they were. This is words and one list.
- **The checklist's count.** The strip's *1 notice to print* and the line *N papers are right* still count such a notice, since the checklist is a kernel over the Board's chips and never reads the run's notices. The card under them now says why it does not print. Making the checklist ask about a notice the run window holds is a larger change to step 5 and is not in this.
- **Which case the live desk's notice was in** was not read: it is a real job. The card will say it.
