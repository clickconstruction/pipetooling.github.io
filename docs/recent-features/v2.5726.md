# v2.5726 — Messages: our own address or number is a person only when the walk kit's contact carries it (2026-10-22)

Punch list #142 PR 1 (`to-dos/walk-kit/README.md` decision 4, `BUILD_PLAN.md` § PR 1). The walk kit's ZZ Walk customer carries the bids box's address and the bids number, so a session can reply from bids@ to the team box and text from the bids number to the company number. Before this, `gmail-sync` left every mail from a box's own address out, and `quo-webhook` made a Who is this? row for any number nobody carried, our own included: a text between two of our own numbers made two.

- **The rule, in both readers**: an address or number of our own (both boxes' addresses, the app's sender, both boxes' numbers, read from `messages_boxes_v1` and the secrets) is taken as a person only when the walk kit's contact carries it; otherwise it is left out, as before. Narrowed to the kit's contact, not any contact: two test records on prod already carry bids@, and under the wider reading a mail from bids@ would land on one of them. With no kit the rule is dormant.
- **Kernels** (mirrored to the edge, `scripts/edge-kernels.mjs`): `src/lib/walk/kit.ts` — `WALK_KIT_KEY`, `WalkKit`, `parseWalkKit` (the `app_settings` `walk_kit_v1` row `walk_setup` will write), `channelKey` (the same key as `public.contact_channel_key`), `kitCarries`, `numberPlan`; `src/lib/messages/ownChannels.ts` — `ownChannels`, `isOwnAddress`, `isOwnNumber`. `supabase/functions/_shared/walkKit.ts` reads the row once per run.
- **gmail-sync**: `loadTeam` builds the own set from the boxes row, `EMAIL_FROM` and the reader's addresses, and `isOurs` / `isTheBox` answer false for an own address the kit carries, so `leftOut` takes the mail and `emailParty` reads it as a person writing in. `takeOne` passes such a mail over, with a log line and no label, when `email_place_address` lands it anywhere but the kit's contact.
- **quo-webhook**: `handle` skips a recipient of our own the kit does not carry (every recipient skipped answers *ignored: our own numbers*), `placeEvent` ignores a text or a call in from one (*our own number, the kit does not carry it*), and either ignores and logs a placement that is not the kit's contact. The mirrors Quo sends of a walk's texts (the delivered event on one number, the received event on the other) are the ones ignored.
- **Tests**: `kit.test.ts`, `ownChannels.test.ts`; `leftOut.test.ts` and `emailLine.test.ts` gain the three cases (from bids@ while the kit carries it: taken, in; with no kit: left out; the app's sender while the kit carries bids@: left out); `src/lib/quo/walkKitWebhook.run.test.ts` runs the webhook's handler on the fake database over the four events of a walk's text legs — two lines on the kit's contact and no contact made; with no kit, nothing placed and no contact made; a customer's number placed as always.
- **Deploy** after merge: `gmail-sync`, `quo-webhook`, then the mirror's other importers `check:edge-drift` names (the copy's index changed). Then the owner's one text, *walk check*, from the bids number to the company number in Quo's app, answers whether Quo delivers between two of its own numbers: a `message.received` ignored as our own number in `quo-webhook`'s log means it does. Before this PR that text would have made two Who is this? rows.
