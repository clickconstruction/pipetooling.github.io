# v2.8758 — The Training card's presses each name their course (2026-11-27)

Beside punch list #419. Finding 345 of the fixing thread's ledger, from the walking thread's sitting 35 of 2026-11-27 (the dev's own Dashboard, 1024 px, reads only). Client only. No migration, no edge function. Nothing owed after the merge.

**As seen.** The card *Training · at your own pace* lists its courses, each with one press. All four presses read **Start**, so a screen reader heard *Start* four times with nothing to tell them apart.

**Read.** `DashboardTrainingSection.tsx` draws one press a course, for a course to do and for one on offer under *Available*. A press's words are its act alone: Start, Continue, Take it again, Read it again. The course's title is a sibling box in the same grid, and the press had no name of its own that joined the two.

**The mend.** A kernel in `src/lib/training/trainingState.ts`, `coursePressName(press, title)`, joins the act and the course: *Start Intro to HVAC*. Both presses take it as their `aria-label`. The name begins with the words on the press, so saying what is seen still finds it. The words on the screen do not change.

**Not in this change.** The press's height. The walk read 64 by 37 px at 1024 px, a desk, and the style gives `minHeight: 36`. What it stands on a phone was not read.

**Tests.** `trainingState.test.ts`: two courses give two names, each begins with the press's own words, and a course with no title keeps the act alone. `DashboardTrainingSection.render.test.tsx`: two courses to start are found by two names though both read **Start**, and no press on the card is left with its act alone for a name; the name follows the press to Continue and Read it again; a course on offer is named too.

**To see it live.** On a Dashboard with the Training card, read each press's `aria-label`, or let a screen reader say the card. Nothing is pressed.
