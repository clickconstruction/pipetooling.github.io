# v2.4281 — Hours added in bulk: a burst of typed days shows on the Needs You card (2026-09-30)

The owner, after typed hours got their second look (#70) and the Add clock session calendar its window (v2.4271): the dashboard has a bulk *deletion* notice — add a bulk *addition* notice, so that when one person adds more than one set of hours to the history it shows up in Needs You.

## What was true

The typed-hours ledger (`clock_typed_entries`, v2.4242) records every stretch of time someone typed onto a day the clock had not recorded — who, for whom, which day, how much, when — and the hold keeps the typist from approving it. Both assumed somebody opens the approvals queue. Nothing said "Taunya just typed a week of hours for two people": a second person found out only by opening the queue that day.

## What changed

- **`list_bulk_hours_alerts()`** — migration `20260930233154_bulk_hours_alerts` ([doc](../migrations/20260930233154_bulk_hours_alerts.md)): the sibling of `list_bulk_deletion_alerts()`. A read over the ledger's `added` entries, one row per (typist, time bucket) with at least N distinct **(person, day)** pairs — days, not sessions: fixing one missed Friday is one day whatever it took; typing a week for two people is ten. Thresholds in `app_settings` (`bulk_hours_alert_*_v1`: 2 days, 60 minutes, 7 days back; the switch), read server-side so the card and its numbers cannot disagree. Answered for whoever approves hours (dev, pay access, assistants); never the caller's own typing, so a burst is never invisible to everyone. A worker's own late entries count like anyone else's typing.
- **The Needs You item** `bulk-hours` ([`dashboardNeedsYou.ts`](../../src/lib/dashboardNeedsYou.ts)) — red, kicker *Hours added in bulk*, title *Taunya typed hours onto 8 days in 25 minutes*, detail *Darren, Michael A · Sep 21–25 · 71.5 h the clock did not record, 6 days still waiting, 2 already looked at. Nothing is blocked; this is so a second person sees it the same day.* The figure is the days. Tier 5: under the attack indicators, above everything else. Words in [`bulkHoursAlert.ts`](../../src/lib/clock/bulkHoursAlert.ts). **Snooze 24h** / **Dismiss until count increases**, the deletion notice's own semantics (`bulkHoursAlertDismiss.ts`, `useBulkHoursNudge`), because a burst never drains on its own.
- **Look at them** opens the approvals queue on *Typed by hand*, narrowed to that typist (`&typist=<id>`; the queue's `startTypist` prop and a *typed by Taunya ×* chip that widens it back).
- **Settings → People & teams → Bulk hours alert (dev)** — [`BulkHoursAlertSettingsBlock`](../../src/components/settings/BulkHoursAlertSettingsBlock.tsx), the deletion block's shape: the switch, days, minutes, look-back.
- Guide *approve my team's hours* → *Hours someone typed* gained the paragraph on the card.

## Tests

`bulkHoursAlert.test.ts` (9), `dashboardNeedsYou.test.ts` (+2), the PG bed `npm run test:pg:typed-hours` (+ `40_bulk_hours_scenarios.sql`: the counts of one burst, who sees it, the typist's own hidden, a worker's own two days, the four settings).

## Known limits

Hours typed straight into the Hours grid (`people_hours` cells) carry no typist or time on the row, so a bulk backfill through the grid is invisible to this notice — the next PR stamps the grid. Fixed time buckets, not a sliding window, as the deletion alert.
