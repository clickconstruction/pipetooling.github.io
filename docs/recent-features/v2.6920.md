# v2.6920 — Price sheets on the Parts Book, and a quote that came twice (2026-11-10)

Punch list #308, PR 7 of nine. No migration.

## What changed

- **`PriceSheetDoor.tsx`** (renamed from `TakeoffPriceSheetDoor.tsx` and moved to `components/materials/`): the bid, the takeoff hooks, the supply houses and the parts are all optional now. With no bid a sheet is kept with no bid and files part prices only: no fixture is placed and no assembly is made. The door reads the supply houses itself when they are not handed in, and reads the whole parts book once, when a sheet opens.
- **The Parts Book** (`MaterialsPartsBookTab.tsx`) draws the door in place of PR 3's *Paste a price* button, which is now its second item. A file dropped or pasted on the Parts Book is read the same way.
- **All price sheets…** in the door's menu opens a window that lists every sheet (the newest hundred): the supply house, the quote's number, the bid it is for, the day it was read, how many rows are filed and how many wait, and **Open**. On Takeoffs the menu lists the bid's own sheets and offers the window past six.
- **A later copy of a quote.** When the house is sure and a sheet with the same number from that house is on file, the door measures the new rows against the earlier read (`sheetRevision` in `sheetPlan.ts`, a row known by the house's number, else by its words) and says it in one line: *Q-88213 was read on Nov 6. This copy is later: 3 prices changed, 2 rows are new, and 66 rows are the same.* The later copy is kept as its own sheet. Its unchanged rows are ready, since the earlier read taught their numbers.
- `priceSheetIo.ts`: `findSheetByNumber`, and `listSheets` with the bid's label.
- The guides `read-a-supply-houses-price-sheet` and `paste-a-price-from-a-quote-or-a-call` name the Parts Book door.

## What the card planned here and this PR leaves out

- **Read as a price sheet on a mail's file.** The office's Messages screens offer *Record this bill* to the office roles, and a price sheet needs a bid to land on and the estimator to open it. That is a design question, not a small PR. The drop on Takeoffs covers a quote saved from a mail.
- **Read this quote on a price request's row.** The request holds a link to a PDF or a Drive folder, which the browser cannot fetch; it needs the server's Drive read.

Both stay on the card.
