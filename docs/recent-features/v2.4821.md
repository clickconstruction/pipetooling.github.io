# v2.4821 — Legal portal: the firm's answers (2026-10-07)

One migration (`20261008001000_legal_firm_intake.sql`, push after merge) and two functions (`legal-portal`, `submit-legal-portal`, **deploy after the push**). PR B of 2, after [v2.4820](v2.4820.md). The owner's intake flow (2026-10-07) carried *what Click needs from the firm*: what it wants at intake, where it prefers to file, whether it e-files and serves through the constable, a review of the Texas lien rules the app follows, and who gets the link and the emails. This turns those into Start here's step 4.

## What changed

- **[`_shared/legalFirmIntake.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/legalFirmIntake.ts)** (client door `src/lib/legal/legalFirmIntake.ts`): the answers' shape (`needs`, `fileWhere`, `efile`, `constable`, `rules`, `rulesNote`), trimmed and capped, a choice that is not ours dropped, the rules note kept only with *Changes needed* (`shapeLegalFirmIntake`); the questions in the firm's words (`legalIntakeQuestions`); each answer for the office (`legalIntakeAnswerRows`); *Sent Oct 7 by Ann Sample.* in the company's day (`legalIntakeSentWords`); and the unread rule behind the desk's dot (`legalIntakeUnseen`).
- **The migration** ([page](../migrations/20261008001000_legal_firm_intake.md)): `legal_firms.intake`, `intake_sent_at`, `intake_sent_by`, `intake_seen_at`, and `legal_firm_intake_seen(p_firm_id)`, the office's one write here.
- **`submit-legal-portal`**: kind `intake` writes the firm's own row with the shaped answers and who sent them. **`legal-portal`**: the answer carries `intake` from a query of its own, left out until the migration lands, so the page hides the step rather than offer one that cannot save.
- **The portal**: Start here gains step 4, *Your answers* ([`LegalPortalIntakeForm`](../../src/components/jobs/legal/LegalPortalIntakeForm.tsx)): two text boxes, three pill choices, a note that opens with *Changes needed*, *Read them ›* to the rules sheet, *Add them on Notifications ›*, *Answered by* (a person on the firm's list, or a name typed in), and **Send to Click**. The answers on file fill the boxes; typing is kept on the browser until it sends. Until someone answers, Matters carries *Click asked five quick questions. Answer them on Start here ›*, which opens Start here at step 4. The sample shows the step and saves nothing.
- **The desk**: the firm's name on the Legal desk wears a copper dot while the answers are unread (its label adds *new answers from the firm*). The firm's window opens with *Their answers to Start here*: each question and answer, who sent them and when, *New* on fresh ones. Opening it stamps them read and reloads the desk, which takes the dot off. A firm that has not answered says so.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **On the firm's own row, not a matter.** The answers are about the firm, not an account, and a replaced firm starts blank.
- **A dot the office cannot miss, cleared by reading.** The owner pitches the firm and waits for its answers; the firm door is where he already looks for the firm.
- **Who gets the link stays on Notifications.** That page already adds a person with a confirmation email; the step points there instead of asking for names twice.
- **Not done: an email to the office when answers arrive.** The portal has no office mailbox yet; the dot carries it.
