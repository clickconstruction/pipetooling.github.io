# v2.3423 — Job accounts at the counter, PR 1: the record and the roster (2026-09-14)

Migration `20260914210000_job_supply_house_accounts.sql` (fragment in `docs/migrations/`); no edge function. First PR of the five-PR train the owner approved on 2026-09-14 from the artifact *Job Accounts at the Counter* ("I like all of this, build it"): the record and the roster · the job strip and the Dispatch errand · the PO moment · the invoice default + Needs You + Pipeline fix-up · the New Job question.

## Why

"Before ordering parts, Curly needs to be reached out to for opening of job accounts." The app could not hold that rule: it recorded *packets emailed* (`supply_house_job_accounts`, one row on prod — the Pondhill demo) rather than *accounts that exist*, Curly opens accounts on the phone so the truth lived in Taunya's head, the rep was the free text "REP FOR JOB ACCTS" on a contact label, and zero invoices carried the v2.2669 flag. The full read is in the artifact's "why the current build never became a process".

## What shipped

- **The record** — `job_supply_house_accounts`: one row per (job, supply house) once anyone asked for or opened the account; `status` requested · open · not_needed, `account_ref`, `opened_via` phone · packet · counter, `rep_contact_id`, who/when stamps (server trigger), `requested_from_counter`, `note` (the not-needed reason). RLS: readable by anyone who can read the job's operational activity (the crew on the team included — the strip in PR 2 needs it at the counter); the office writes any status, a job reader may insert a `requested` row as themselves; update/delete office only.
- **The house** — `supply_houses.job_accounts` expects · optional · none; Ferguson, Reece, Moore backfilled to expects, non-supply-house vendor kinds to none. Edit house: a **Job accounts** chip row under Kind (office only, supply houses only) with the hint per choice. Sent only when changed, so an untouched save survives the pre-push window.
- **The contact** — `supply_house_contacts.role` price_requests · job_accounts · billing + `phone`; labels containing "job acc" backfilled to the job_accounts role. The Contacts block in Edit house shows each contact's phone, the role as three chips (click to move), and takes phone + role on add. A new SELECT policy lets every authenticated user read unarchived job_accounts contacts (the field's Call button).
- **The roster** — [`SupplyHouseJobAccountsRoster`](../../src/components/materials/SupplyHouseJobAccountsRoster.tsx) in the expanded house on Supply houses → Accounts payable, above Invoices: one row per job with an account (chip open / requested / not needed, reference, when and how, invoices at this house with allocated and unpaid dollars) **plus the evidence rows** — jobs with invoices allocated at this house and no account, amber, *bought, no account*. Requested first, then bought-no-account, then open, then not needed. Header names who opens them (the job_accounts rep, phone as `tel:`), or says no rep is on file. Quiet for a `none` house with nothing recorded.
- **The sheet** — [`MarkJobAccountOpenedModal`](../../src/components/materials/MarkJobAccountOpenedModal.tsx): Mark opened (how · reference · rep · note) or Not needed (reason required); upserts on (job, house). Shared by the roster now and by the job window, the PO code and the Dispatch errand in the next PRs.
- **The send log** — `supply_house_job_accounts.supply_house_id` (the column v2.3161 deferred; backfilled from the addressed contact's house) + `account_id`. The Accounts-payable section is retitled *Job account packets sent* so it reads as the log it is.
- **Kernel** [`jobSupplyHouseAccounts.ts`](../../src/lib/materials/jobSupplyHouseAccounts.ts) (+8 tests): policies, roles, statuses and their labels; `jobAccountRepOf`; `buildHouseJobAccountRoster` / `countHouseRoster` / `houseRosterSummary`; `jobAccountsForJob` (the per-job read the strip will use).
- Types hand-extended in `src/types/database.ts` (regenerate after the push, as v2.3422 did).
