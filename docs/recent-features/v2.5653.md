# v2.5653 — Bids: the sales tax rate is saved on the bid (2026-10-19)

Punch list #128, PR 1 of the price-breakout train (the plan in `to-dos/bid-price-breakout/`). The rate was `useState('8.25')` in `Bids.tsx`, edited by the Takeoffs materials summary's Tax % box, never saved, applied to a row's takeoff material and left out of the bid's cost total (the map's "tax fallback duality"). The rate varies by city. Migration `20261020010000_bid_tax_rate.sql` (`docs/migrations/`); no edge function.

- **The column and the default**: `bids.tax_rate_pct numeric(6,3)` with a 0–100 check, null = the company default; `app_settings` `bid_tax_rate_pct_v1` seeded 8.25, set on Settings → Bid Cover Letter Defaults beside the schedule-of-values factor and labor share (`BidCoverLetterDefaultsSettingsBlock`).
- **The kernel** (`src/lib/bids/bidTaxRate.ts`): `effectiveBidTaxRate(bidValue, companyDefault)` → the bid's own or the company's with its source; `bidTaxRateToSave(text, companyDefault)` → the rate, null for blank or the company's number, undefined for garbage. `parseBidTaxRatePct` in `appSettingsKeys.ts`. Reads and the write in `bidTaxRateIo.ts` (`loadBidTaxRateDefault`, `saveBidTaxRate`; the update's payload is cast until the types regen after the push).
- **The page** (`Bids.tsx`): the default is read once; the box seeds from the selected bid's rate whenever the bid changes (`select('*')` carries the column once pushed; before the push the default stands, as it always did); the box's blur or Enter saves the bid's own rate (`onCommitTaxPercent` through `BidsTakeoffTab` to `BidsTakeoffMaterialsSummarySection`), patches the bid in the list and the shared bid, and a bad number is put back with a word.
- **The cost** (`BidsPricingTab`'s `derivePricingWorkbench`): the bid's total now carries `taxCost = materials × rate`, as every row's cost already did, so the Workbench's overhead line stops absorbing it and the strip's profit and margin stop running high by the tax.
- **Tests**: `bidTaxRate.test.ts` (the bid's own, the company's, the fallbacks, what the box's text saves); the Takeoffs and Pricing render smokes.
- **Docs**: `BIDS_SYSTEM.md`'s Takeoff tab line, the two maps' tax quirks, `docs/migrations/20261020010000_bid_tax_rate.md`.
- **The copy of the kernels**: `appSettingsKeys.ts` is one of the files copied under `_shared/gcKernels/`, so the copy regenerates here (`npm run gen:edge-kernels`) and `check:edge-drift` names the importers to redeploy after the merge; no function changes what it does.
- **Deploy**: merge, then `supabase db push` and the importers' redeploy; a types PR after the push.
