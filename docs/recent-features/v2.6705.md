## Read closer than the card

- **Copies stay words.** The demand letter has one recipient (name, email, address) and one email address on its send; it carries no CC. The strip lists the copies so the office sees them where the letter is written, and the card names the letter's own copies line as the next step.
- **Seeding is a press, not a read.** The list starts empty with *Add the people the record knows*; a read never writes. The roles the seed gives are the customer, their billing (a contact person) and owner of record; the office changes any row.
- **The firm does not see the people** in this PR: the never reasons are the office's own words (a witness, a bounce). The owner's call gives the firm the proceedings list, PR 2.
