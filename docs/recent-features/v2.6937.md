# v2.6937 — Lien affidavit: a house holds until the homestead question is answered (2026-11-11)

Punch list #305, item 3 of *The build*. `customer_addresses.homestead` is `boolean NOT NULL DEFAULT false`, so a property nobody asked about read the same as one answered no, and the desk passed it as *Not a homestead*. A lien on a homestead needs a pre-work contract signed by both spouses and recorded (§ 53.254, the app's reading; counsel has not answered), so *nobody asked* must not read as *no*.

## What changed

- **A column that records the answer.** `customer_addresses.homestead_answered_at timestamptz` (migration `20261112080000_homestead_answered.sql`): NULL until a person answers yes or no on the property record. The one property already marked homestead is stamped as answered.
- **`src/lib/customers/homesteadAnswer.ts`** (pure): `homesteadAnsweredOnRow` reads the column softly (null off a row that does not carry it), `homesteadAnswer` gives *yes · no · unanswered*, `homesteadQuestionOpen` is the hold (a residential property, not marked homestead, with no answer on record), and the words the screens share.
- **The property record asks a yes or a no.** `CustomerPropertyRecordPanel` (Edit customer's Properties, the job form's add sheet, the Lien desk's property window) draws *Is this the owner's homestead?* with **Yes** and **No** in place of the tick, and an amber line while nobody has answered. A press sets the flag and stamps the answer (`propertyDraft.ts` carries `homestead_answered_at` only off a row that has the column, or once pressed). A pasted CAD page whose exemptions line says homestead or no homestead answers it too. Edit Job's Homestead tick stamps the answer on a tick or an untick (`savePropertyHomestead`, which falls back to the plain write when the column is refused).
- **The gate.** `homesteadGate` in `src/lib/jobs/lienDeskAffidavits.ts` is the homestead slot of `affidavitGates`, one line with three answers: marked homestead (key `homestead`, the attorney's hold, unchanged), nobody answered (key `homestead_answer`), *Not a homestead*. The key says which hold it is, so what reads `homestead` (the courtesy notice's homestead reading, the share's needs) never takes an unanswered question for a homestead.
- **The fact is the desk's, not the RPC's.** `list_lien_affidavit_windows` is unchanged. `useLienDeskData` reads the property rows under the residential affidavits (`select('*')`, in light mode too, so the Dashboard's count and the desk agree) and fills `homestead_answered` with `withHomesteadAnswer`. A failed read, a job with no linked record, and a row that does not carry the column are not judged.
- **Where it speaks.** The gate list on the Affidavits pane, with **Answer it ›** (`data-lien-affidavit-homestead-door`), which opens the Lien desk's property window focused on the question; the Lien window's Mechanic's lien tab (`LienFilingTabs`), which lists the same line (`resolveLienProperty` gives `homesteadAnswered`) and draws no paper while it is closed; the hold line on a timeline stop, the list row's *missing the homestead answer*, the timeline's missing gates, and the paper preview's record blank (`lienPaperGaps` key `homestead_answer`, **Fix the property**).

## What a hold does

It changes the pile, never the paper's words. A paper not yet drafted, or drafted and not sent, sits in **Needs the property facts**. A paper already approved stays where its desk item put it, as with the other gates; its Lien window draws no paper until the question is answered. A yes moves the property to the attorney's hold; a no clears the line.

## Apply order

Client first, then `supabase db push` right after the merge. Before the push the rows carry no column, nothing is held, and a yes or no pressed on a property record cannot be saved (the write names the new column). After the push every residential property under an affidavit holds until answered.

## Not in this PR

- Edit Job's row keeps its tick: it reads a slim copy of the record through a named select, which cannot name the column before the push. It can answer yes, or no by ticking and unticking; the property record's Yes and No is the plain door.
- The share's status payload knows four needs; a job held for the question alone lists none there.
- **Print N for notarization** prints every approved short-form paper whatever its gates show, as before.
