# v2.4015 — Edit Job: New Invoice can no longer turn into Ready to Bill as you press it (2026-09-28)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Found in the live pass on punch list **#46** row 4 (2026-09-28): on job J1053, Working with $1,200 left, 5000 was typed into the New Invoice amount and the green **New Invoice** button pressed. No invoice was made; the job moved to Ready to Bill.

## Why

The break-off chip's one button does two things (`JobFormBreakOffSection`): **New Invoice** (`createInvoice`) for part of what is left, and **Ready to Bill** (`moveWorkingJobToReadyToBillFromEdit`) when the amount is the whole remainder of a Working job. Which one was chosen when the click arrived. Pressing the button takes the focus off the amount field; the field's blur cuts a typed amount back to what is left (`clampTypedBreakOffAmount`); an amount cut back to everything left is the whole remainder — so between pointer-down and click the button re-rendered as Ready to Bill, and the click ran the move. This predates the #46 decomposition.

## What shipped

- **`lib/jobs/jobFormBreakOff.ts`** — `breakOffButtonAction`, `breakOffPressStillMeansTheSame(pressed, now)` and `breakOffButtonChangedWords`.
- **`JobFormBreakOffSection`** remembers what the button said at pointer-down and runs the click only if it still says the same. When it does not, nothing runs and a note says the amount was cut back to everything left, that billing all of it moves the job, and what to press. A press that leaves the button without a click is forgotten; a keyboard press has no pointer-down and runs as before.
- **Not changed:** what either button does, the cut-back on blur, the rule that the whole remainder of a Working job is a move.
- **Tests** — `jobFormBreakOff.test.ts` (+5); `JobFormBreakOffSection.render.test.tsx` (+5): each button running when it says the same; New Invoice turning into Ready to Bill between the press and the click running neither and showing the note, and the next, deliberate press moving the job; a keyboard press; a press that left the button.
- Help: `ready-to-bill-pipeline` says which button does what and what happens when the amount is cut back.

## Verified

- The two test files (33); eslint on the touched files; a scoped typecheck. The full typecheck and suite run in CI.
- **Not verified live.** The Browser pane was hidden when this was ready to try, so a real press could not be made; a scripted attempt did not reproduce the blur. The render test drives the same sequence (pointer-down, the re-render the blur causes, click).
