# v2.7530 — Lien desk: the book, counsel's grid and the Lien window count what the bills owe (2026-11-16)

U1 of three fixes that followed a lien packet stating two amounts (v2.7517, v2.7520). The owner's words: a job with three stages was moved to collections, and what was owed did not count the stage's percent complete against the percent billed. The app totalled "owed" two ways. The desk's own RPCs have returned *billed and still open* (`lien_billed_open()`) since v2.4969; these lien screens still took the job's price less its payments, so on a job billed one stage of three they counted the stages never done or billed. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What changed

- **The book and counsel's grid.** `assembleLienBookInput` (`lienTimelineBookAssemble.ts`) set `LienBookJob.openBalance` to `revenue − payments_made`. It now takes the `open_balance` the desk's own rows carry for the job (the notice month's row, else the affidavit's), so the By job tab's *Unpaid months · $*, the book's total and the firm's portal grid (`LegalPortalLienGrid`, which assembles through the same fold) read the bills. A job on no row of either queue, whose paper is already filed or released, has no bills read there and keeps the job's balance.
- **A sent notice on the desk.** `jobBalance` in `LienDesk.tsx` is the entry's `openBalance`: *GC paid: yes / no*, the *still owed* words and placeholder, counsel's sign-off amount and the two doors read the bills, as the claim above them does. The call facts for a job with nothing drafted take the entry's figure too.
- **The Lien window.** `LienInstrumentsModal` computes `lienWindowClaim(linkedJob, null).openBalance`, the figure its papers already claimed, and hands it to the window's timeline and to the supply houses' card in place of the job's price less payments.

## Not changed

- `collectionsNoteLine` (`lienClaimCorrection.ts`), the Collections row's note when a claim was set by hand, still measures against the job's balance: the kernel is mirrored in the edge copy, and a change there is a function redeploy. No job carries a claim set by hand today.
- The notice recorded by hand (`LienNoticeByHandPane`, `lienNoticeByHand.ts`) and its dev-seat twin still offer other jobs' price less payments as their amounts.

## Verified

Two new tests in `lienTimelineBookAssemble.test.ts` (a job billed one stage of three reads its bills; the row order and the fallback), one in `LienDesk.render.test.tsx` (*GC paid: yes* with the bills paid and the price above the payments). `LegalPortalLienGrid.render.test.tsx`'s court fixture now raises the job's bills with its price. The lien, legal and Lien window suites pass. Not looked at in a browser.
