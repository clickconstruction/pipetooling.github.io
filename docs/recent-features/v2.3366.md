# v2.3366 — The Vectors row is the door: a name on the Bridge opens that person's pay week on People → Review (2026-09-12)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Step 2 of the Bridge ↔ Review fold (`to-dos/review-into-the-bridge.md`); stacked on v2.3360 (step 1, one earned convention).

## Why

The owner's plan: "Make Vectors the door. A person's row on the Bridge opens their Review panel for that pay week. Vectors is the scoreboard, Review's panel is the drilldown. No screen merges yet." With step 1 in, the two surfaces count earned the same way, so the drilldown finally explains the scoreboard's number instead of a different one.

## What shipped

- **Kernel [`src/lib/people/reviewDoor.ts`](../../src/lib/people/reviewDoor.ts)** (+3 tests): `reviewDoorHref({ person, from, to })` → `/people?tab=review&review_person=…&review_from=…&review_to=…`; `parseReviewDoor(search)` (all three required, dates `YYYY-MM-DD`, a backwards range swapped, the wrong tab or the Person desk's own bare `person=` → null); `reviewDoorPersonIndex(roster, person)` (exact, then trimmed + case-insensitive — the same match the Team Summary kernel uses between `users.name` and pay-config `person_name`).
- **[`BridgeVectorsPanel`](../../src/components/bridge/BridgeVectorsPanel.tsx)**: the name cell is a `Link` to the door for the panel's `weekStart` / `weekEnd` (title *Open <name>'s week on People → Review*); the footnote says so.
- **[`PeopleReviewTab`](../../src/components/people/PeopleReviewTab.tsx)**: reads the door once at mount — sets the period to `custom` with the two dates, then selects the person the moment the roster memo is populated (a ref guards both against re-applying, so a later period change or click is never overridden).
- **Gotcha found live**: the first cut used `person=`, which `People.tsx` deletes on arrival because it is the Person desk's own page param — the panel opened on the right range with nobody selected. Namespaced params fixed it; the kernel test pins that a bare `person=` is ignored.
- Help: `the-bridge.md` (Vectors section: a name is the door) and `read-the-team-review.md` (how you get here from the Bridge).
