# v2.7196 — Lien desk on a phone: the Do now rows are in reach (2026-11-13)

Punch list #352, PR 0 of eight. The owner used the Lien desk on his phone, recorded it and called it terrible. A read-only walk of the live desk at three phone sizes found the worst of it on **Do now**, the view the desk opens on: no row could be reached.

## What was wrong

- **The rows had no window.** The Do now view was a grid of `auto auto minmax(0, 1fr)`: the lien month, the find box, then the list in what was left. On a computer the month is one bar 165 px tall. On a phone its five zones stand one over the other, over 500 px, which is taller than the panel. The list got a window 26 px tall, below the panel's clipped bottom. The first row was 1,526 px down. Pressing a month card narrowed a list nobody could scroll to.
- **A grey band.** `LienDeskPage` sized the page from its top edge, read once when drawn and on a window resize. When the Home Screen banner above the app closed, the page moved up and kept its old height, leaving a band the banner's height above the phone dock.

## The change

- `src/lib/jobs/lienDoNowFrame.ts` (new kernel): `lienDoNowFrame(oneColumn)` gives the view's box its style and says whether the list scrolls by itself. `pinned` on a computer is the grid as it was. `one-page` on a phone is one scroller holding the month, the find box and the list. Exactly one thing scrolls in either mode, and the test holds that.
- `LienDeskNextUp` takes `scrolls` (default true). One-page, it is false and the list's own `overflow-y` is `visible`.
- `LienDesk.tsx`: the Do now box reads the kernel. `pickMonthZone` wraps `setMonthZone`; one-page, a pressed zone brings the first group of rows (or the empty line) to the top, since the cards fill the screen and the narrowed list is under them.
- `src/hooks/useTopEdge.ts` (new): the page's top edge, read when drawn, on a window resize, when anything above the page changes size (a `ResizeObserver` on everything drawn above it) and when anything above it is added or taken away (a `MutationObserver` on each box it sits in). `LienDeskPage` uses it in place of its own one-time read.

No migration, no edge function, no change to what any press writes.

## Checked

- Unit: `lienDoNowFrame.test.ts`. Render: `useTopEdge.render.test.tsx` (a banner above closes and the edge is read again), `LienDeskNextUp.render.test.tsx`, `LienDesk.render.test.tsx` (pinned on a computer, one page stacked, a pressed zone brings rows up and opening the desk does not).
- Walked on the dev server against live data, reads only, every write refused at the network. At 390 × 720: the view scrolls 8,644 px in a 343 px window, rows arrive, the Liens card brings its first group to the top, and the gap above the dock after the banner closes is the page's own 8 px. At 320 × 520: one page, no sideways slide. Deadlines and All filings still scroll in their own lists.
- At 1,400 × 900 the title bar, the month bar, the find box and the list sit at the same place and size as on the live desk, to the pixel.

## Left

The header still takes six rows on a phone and a row is still about 225 px. Those are PR 1 (one header row and **More**) and PR 3 (the two-line row) of the card.
