- Guide *send a GC our lien waiver* gains **From View bill**.

## Design calls

- **One card, the bill's paper first.** The waiver belongs to this bill; the contract and the sub work order belong to the job. Pass 1's three boxes pushed the money down and gave two equal blue buttons.
- **Say the stake.** An unpaid GC bill with no waiver says how far past due it is and that a GC often waits for the waiver — on job 251 that may be why the bill is open.
- **Fill a blank, never overwrite.** The bill's email goes on the GC's record only when the record has none.
- **Homeowner jobs show no waiver row** until one exists, as on the Bill tab.
