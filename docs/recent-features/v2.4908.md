## Tests (+10)

- `useMyTimeBoundaryGestures.render.test.tsx` — on the real split store and kernels, with window events as mouse events carrying a `pointerId` (jsdom has no `PointerEvent` or pointer capture; capture falls into the hook's catch):
  - a drag moves a boundary by the pointer's travel from where it was grabbed; a grab 10 px off the line does not jump it. Replacing the grab offset with the line's own Y fails it.
  - release ends the drag and its listeners; it snaps onto the row join within a minute of it.
  - cancel puts the split back and clears the focus.
  - a tap adds a split where it landed. A press that moves past 8 px, one on a boundary handle and one while saving add nothing.
  - Alt+click moves the focused boundary to the click.
  - the Arrow keys nudge a minute at a time.
  - Visual ↔ Form ends a drag where it got to, drops a tap and clears the focus.
  - unmounting mid-drag removes the window listeners and the body class. Dropping the cleanup's `pointermove` removal fails it.
  - nothing moves while the timeline is locked.

## Found, not fixed

- **The Arrow keys cannot move a boundary that sits on a row join.** The nudge moves it a minute, then the finalize snaps anything within `ROW_JOIN_SNAP_MS` (60 s) back onto the join, so ArrowUp and ArrowDown leave it where it was. A drag moves it fine. Only clusters of two or more rows with the same metadata have such joins. Pinned by the test; the fix ships as its own PR.
