## Read closer than the card

- **The pay app is not the sign-off.** No pay application carries a certified line percent (`job_pay_applications.lines[]` is the office's own figures, never locked; the GC owner app's `certified` is a whole-app dollar). The owner chose the office's sign-off, and `job_pct_events` records who set each percent, so the newest office set is the sign-off — the same press the office already makes on the Dashboard stepper or the Bridge prompt.
- **The sub's sheet hours** are the items' hours (count × hours per unit, or the fixed hours) at the item's or the sheet's rate; a sheet carried as a direct amount counts its dollars and no hours, so a superintendent is not charged hours a sheet never named.
- **The People page stays closed to a superintendent**; his row reaches him on his project's card, where the read is already his.
- **Days are a reading, not a rule**: the finish at this pace uses the hours used over the signed-off percent at 24 crew hours a day, weekends by the week's five-in-seven; the line on the board uses the job's own days, so the two can disagree by a day.
