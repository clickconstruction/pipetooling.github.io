# v2.6943 — Cards and footers: Your card and footer, a teammate's, and the team's (punch list #313, PR 3) (2026-11-11)

Punch list #313 (`to-dos/cards-and-footers/README.md`, way A). The owner asked for a tool that makes a business card and an email footer from a person's facts and photo, and said to build it on 2026-11-11. This is the screens: one card on Settings → Your account, under *Your signature on an email*, and two doors on People → Users. It folds the plan's PRs 3 to 6 into one. The card is no use without its presses, and CI's test step runs over half an hour a PR, so four more rounds would have kept the owner waiting most of a day.

## What a person gets

- **The photo.** *Add a photo* opens a sheet (`PhotoRingSheet.tsx`, a `PhoneSheet`: a sheet on a phone, a box at a desk). Take a photo or choose one, drag it so the face sits in the ring, slide to zoom, *Save the photo*. The photo is shrunk on the device (`shrinkPhoto`, 1600 px), kept whole in the private bucket `person-photos` under the person's own id, and placed by three numbers on their row.
- **The words.** *Name on the card* (`users.card_name`; the account name stands in when it is empty) and *Title* (`users.title`, the same field the email signature reads), saved by *Save the card*. The email, the mobile and the office line are shown and changed elsewhere.
- **The footer.** A live preview, then *Download the picture* (a PNG at 2709 × 625, the size of the designer's files) and *Copy the picture*. *Where it goes in your mail* lists the steps for Gmail.
- **The card.** The front and the back, then *Download the print file* (a PDF, front then back, each 4.25 × 2.75 inches with bleed and crop marks, drawn at 600 to the inch) and *Download a sheet of ten* (a letter page, fronts then backs, at 300 to the inch).
- **A hold.** With no photo the four presses are dead and the card says *Add a photo to make your footer and your card.* A missing mobile or title holds nothing: the line is left off.

## What the office gets

- **A teammate's card.** People → Users, a person's row menu, *Card and footer…* (`CardAndFooterWindow.tsx`). It is the same card with that teammate's facts and photo. The office may add their photo, set the name on their card and their title, and take their footer and card. This is also the first place the office can set a teammate's title: *Edit name, title & phone* saves its box to a free note, not to `users.title`.
- **The team.** *Cards and footers* on the same page (`TeamCardsWindow.tsx`; in the phone's More tools menu). It lists every login that is not archived, a sample, a twin, a walk tester or a subcontractor, with a chip: *Ready*, or *No photo*, *No email*, *No name*. A missing mobile or title holds nobody; the row says the line is left off. Everyone ready starts ticked.
- **The batch.** *Make 3 cards · 1 is held ▸* gives one print file, a front and a back for each ready person. *Download 3 footers* gives one picture each, named for its person. The words are `batchWords` and `countedLabel`'s (`teamPressWords` in `lib/brandKit/team.ts`). A held person's door opens their card. A person whose photo will not load fails by name with the reason, and the rest are still made (`batchOutcomeWords`).
- **Who.** The doors show for dev, master_technician, assistant and controller, the roles that open *Edit name, title & phone*. The database lets the same four write a teammate's row and photo folder.

## How it is built

- **`src/components/settings/YourCardCard.tsx`**: the card. It reads through an injected io and draws through an injected renderer, as `SignatureCard` reads through its io. With `userId` and `teammate` the office opens the same card for someone else; the People door is the next PR.
- **`src/lib/brandKit/cardIo.ts`**: the reads and writes. The row is read fail-soft, so before the migration is pushed the card says it arrives with the next update. A photo save uploads, points the row at the new file, and removes the old one; if the row refuses, the upload is taken back out. Every write asks for the row back, so a write the database quietly filtered is reported, not shown as saved.
- **`src/lib/brandKit/render.ts`**: from a drawing to a file. A browser draws an SVG onto a canvas as an image, and an image may not reach outside itself, so the font is written into the drawing as data and the kit's pictures are handed in as data. The PDF is made by `pdf-lib`, loaded when a PDF is asked for.
- **`src/lib/brandKit/cropMath.ts`**: a drag or a slide on the sheet turned into the crop's three numbers, never leaving a gap in the ring.
- **`src/lib/brandKit/cardWords.ts`**: the card's words, and the same words for a teammate's card.
- `drawKit` takes `width: '100%'` so a preview fills its box.
