# v2.8425 — Bills sent: both views name the same person on a bill (2026-11-23)

Found on the Bills sent walk of 2026-11-23 (punch list #422), on the printed month. No migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

By day sent names a bill by its sent copy's recipient, the one who was billed. By day paid named the job's customer. For a job a builder pays on a homeowner's behalf the two differ, so the same three bills read one name in one view and another in the other, and the guide's *who paid* was the homeowner who did not pay.

## What changed

- `readBillsPaid` already reads each bill's sent copies for its number and first day. It now reads `recipient_name` with them and answers `sentTo`, by bill id, from the first send that names one.
- `billsPaidLines` names a payment by `sentTo`, else the job's customer as before. A payment with no bill keeps the job's customer.
- The screen, the printed month and the spreadsheet's *From* column read the same line.

`billsPaidDays.test.ts`: a bill named by its sent copy, a payment with no bill, a bill with no named send.
