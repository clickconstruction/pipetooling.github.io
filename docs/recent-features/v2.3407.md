# v2.3407 — Sold in sticks, PR 2: the rounding, once per part per bid, in every price (2026-09-14)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Second of three PRs (PR 1 v2.3406 authored the rule and snapshotted it onto lines; PR 3 lists what to order). Brief: the *Sold in Sticks* artifact `0851b7b5`.

## The rule

A takeoff line's quantity is per fixture; the bid multiplies by the fixture's count; a part's real footage is the sum over every fixture that uses it. That sum is what a supply house rounds. So the rounding happens **once per part per bid**, never per line, and the extra is **spread back over the fixtures by their share of the part's footage** so Pricing's per-fixture rows still add up to the bid (`docs/TAKEOFFS_REFRESH_PLAN.md` decision 9 holds: the strip never disagrees with Pricing).

## What shipped

Words: `orderRoundingHover` (*105 ft needed on this bid → 120 ft (6 × 20 ft sticks) · +15 ft extra*), `orderRoundingTitle`.
- **The four readers, one PR** (miss one and the strip disagrees with Pricing): [`takeoffCoverage.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/takeoffCoverage.ts) (`materialsTotal` now includes the extra; `materialsBeforeRounding` and `orderRounding` exposed; each `perFixture.total` carries its `roundingExtra`), the pricing engine's two rough reads (`costEstimateMaterialTotalRoughIn` for the Old view's summary and the Labor tab; `pricingMaterialTotalRoughIn` + `fixtureMaterials` for the Workbench rows — both selects widened to `part_id, order_increment, order_increment_unit`), and the Pricing tab's version compare.
- **The strip**: an **Order rounding** tile (*+$318*, violet when non-zero) after Materials whenever any part on the bid carries a rule; its hover counts the parts and says the extra is included and spread.
- **The line**: a violet chip with the pack (*20 ft* · *100 ft coil* · *box of 10*) beside the part name on lines whose part rounds; hover reads the bid-level sentence. No per-line "ordered" number, on purpose.
