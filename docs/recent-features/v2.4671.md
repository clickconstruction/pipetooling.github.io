# v2.4671 — People: one list decides who is archived on every tab (2026-10-06)

Punch list #29 ([`to-dos/people-spine-residuals.md`](../../to-dos/people-spine-residuals.md)), item 3a: the archived folds and filters take the People spine's roster view instead of `get_archived_user_names()`. Client only. The RPC is dropped in 3b, a day after this is live; 3c regenerates the types.

## The change

- **[`archivedRosterNames`](../../src/lib/people/rosterPeople.ts)**: every name an archived `roster_people` row answers to (pay, account and roster names, trimmed). A name that a live row also answers to is left out, compared without case, so a living person never hides behind an archived namesake. The RPC knew archived accounts only and had no such guard.
- **[`People.tsx`](../../src/pages/People.tsx)**: `loadRosterPeople` sets both the pay roster's verdict and the archived names from one read. It now also runs for Contracts viewers. `loadArchivedUserNames` and its RPC call are gone from the Hours, Review and Contracts/Offsets loads.
- **[`fetchRosterPeople`](../../src/lib/people/rosterPeople.ts) retries a blip twice** (about 3 s at most). It was set never to retry while the view might not be pushed yet. Now the one read carries both answers.
- **No consumer changed.** The five take the same set as before: Offsets (the Archived users section), Contracts (the archived group), Review (pay names beside the pay roster), the Teams member filter (`teamsWithoutArchivedMembers`), and the Hours grid's rule 1 (its header amended).

## What a person sees

- **The namesake guard:** someone who shares a name with an archived person is no longer hidden as archived on any of the five.
- **Teams, the Hours grid and Review:** a roster-only person (no app login) whose roster row is archived now drops off. Offsets and Contracts already folded those through their own archived-people list.
- **When the roster read fails** after its retries, nothing folds as archived and every pay row stays for that load. That is the documented no-verdict path. It is also what a failure most often meant before: the network took out the RPC and the view together.

## Tests

`src/lib/people/rosterPeople.test.ts` covers five cases: an archived account's three names; an archived roster-only person; a living namesake kept, in any case; an unarchived twin or sample left out (the pay roster drops those); blank names. The People, roster and Hours suites pass unchanged.
