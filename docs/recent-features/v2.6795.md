## Read closer than the ask

- A `collections_note` cannot be edited in place: the RPC has no such press, so the verb says to clear the flag and set it again.
- The final bill for unbilled stages is not a new verb: `plan_bill` bills the remainder exactly as Bill Customer does, which on a job with the rough billed and paid and the top-out and trim unbilled is those stages, provided revenue is the contract total; a stated amount or a hand-picked set of lines stays a later verb (the owner's call this morning).
