# v2.5979 — Bid Costs fits on a phone (2026-10-27)

Fix, no migration, no edge function. Found on 2026-10-27 by Settings → What everyone sees (punch list #194): as the estimator, the Phone 375 frame of Bids → Bid Costs wore *Scrolls sideways*, with the document 434 px wide.

## Cause

The lens switcher at the top of `BidsBidCostsTab` (Pursuit · Cost to win · Bid vs actual · History & forecast) was a `display: flex` row with no wrap, and each chip is `white-space: nowrap`, so none of them could shrink. The four chips and their gaps need about 418 px; the content box at 375 is 343 px. *History & forecast* ran from 300 to 434 px, the document's exact width. It was the only element past the edge on all four lenses, in light and dark, as the estimator in the lens world and as the dev on real data. Every table on the tab already scrolls inside its own `overflow-x: auto` box, and the filter row under the switcher already wraps.

## Fix

- The lens row takes `flexWrap: 'wrap'`. At 375 the first three chips stay on one line and *History & forecast* drops to a second (the row goes from 26 to 58 px tall). At 768 and 1280 the row still fits on one line, so nothing moves there.

## Tests

- `BidsBidCostsTab.render.test.tsx` (+1): the lens tablist wraps and holds its four chips in order. jsdom cannot measure layout, so this pins the wrap.
- `e2e/viewport-smoke.spec.ts`: `/bids?tab=bid-costs` joins the 375 px no-sideways-overflow pages. Its marker waits for *N bids with time* so the ledger has loaded before it measures. The suite runs after each deploy as the dev test user and does not gate PRs; `docs/E2E_SMOKE.md` lists the page.

## Verified

Dev server, Settings → What everyone sees, Who: Estimator, Where: Bid Costs, Across: Widths and themes.

- Before: Phone 375 dark `scrollWidth` 434 on every lens (436 with History & forecast picked, its bold label is wider). The only unclipped element past 375 was the *History & forecast* chip.
- After: all six frames (375, 768 and 1280, light and dark) measure exactly their width. Every lens at 375 in both themes measures 375 with nothing past the edge, and the tool shows no *Scrolls sideways* badge. The lens row is 58 px at 375 and 26 px (one line) at 768 and 1280.
- The page opened alone at 375 × 812 as the dev on real data (dollars on, 85 bids with time): every lens measures 375.
