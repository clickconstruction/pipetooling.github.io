# v2.6932 — Lien affidavit: a hold while the general contractor has no address on file (2026-11-11)

Punch list #305, item 4 of *The build*. § 53.054(a)(4) and (a)(5) ask for the hiring party's and the original contractor's name and last known address, as the app reads them; counsel has not answered. On a job under a GC both are the GC, and the short form's paragraph 3 printed a blank where the address goes when the GC's customer row held none. The desk now holds that paper. On a job contracted with the owner the hiring party is the owner, whose mailing address the owner gate already needs, and the original contractor is our own company: nothing new is asked there.

## What changed

- **A fifth gate, `contractor`.** `contractorAddressGate({ isSub, hasAddress })` in `src/lib/jobs/lienDeskAffidavits.ts` is the one place for its words; `affidavitGates` lists it third, after the owner and the legal description. `LienAffidavitGateKey` names the five keys.
- **The fact is the desk's, not the RPC's.** `list_lien_affidavit_windows` is unchanged and there is no migration. `useLienDeskData` already reads each GC's customer row; it now builds the affidavit queue after that read and fills `has_contractor_address` on each row with `withContractorAddress`. A GC row that is blank, or was not returned, is no address. A row nobody read the GC for (the firm's portal, the timeline book) carries no fact and the gate does not judge.
- **Where it speaks.** The gate list on the Affidavits pane (`LienDeskAffidavitPane`), with **Add the address ›** (`data-lien-affidavit-contractor-door`) for the office roles: `LienDesk` takes `onOpenCustomerRecord`, and `LienDeskPage` opens the Edit customer window on the GC and re-reads the desk on save. The Lien window's Mechanic's lien tab (`LienFilingTabs`) lists the same line from the same function and draws no paper while it is closed; it judges only once the window has read the GC's row (`originalContractorAddressRead`), so a slow or failed read holds nothing.
- **The words around it.** The hold line on a timeline stop (`lienAffidavitFootBlockedSentence`, `lienAffidavitGateShortWords`), the list row's *missing the GC's address* (`affidavitGateMissingWord`), the timeline's missing gates, Do now's row (*Affidavit · the general contractor has no address on file*, **Add the address**, when the property record is whole) and the paper preview's blank (`lienPaperGaps` key `gc_address`, marked on the address in paragraph 3, *Add it on the customer record*).

## What a hold does

It changes the pile, never the paper's words. A paper not yet drafted, or drafted and not sent, sits in **Needs the property facts** instead of **To draft**, and its send and approve presses are dead with *Blocked until every gate above is clear.* A paper already approved stays where its desk item put it, as with the other four gates; its Lien window draws no paper until the gate clears.

## Prod, read through the dev seat on 2026-11-11

It holds nothing. `list_lien_affidavit_windows` lists 13 jobs, 11 of them under a GC, and the two GCs they name both have an address on their customer row. The other two jobs were contracted with the owner.
