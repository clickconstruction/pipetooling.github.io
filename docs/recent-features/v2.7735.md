# v2.7735 — Edit Job: the highlight rings are their own piece (2026-11-18)

Client only; no migration, no edge function, no screen change. Punch list **#46** row 4 (`to-dos/decomposition-queue.md`): the Job form's second pass, cut 5 (the map's row 14), after v2.7718, v2.7721, v2.7722 and v2.7729.

## What shipped

- **`hooks/useJobFormHighlights.ts`**, new: the five flags a deep link opens the Job form with, the element each lands on, and the eight effects that scroll to it, ring it and let the ring fade. Moved as written.
  - **Billing customer**: scrolls to the customer block. It clears once the job has a customer, and never by the clock.
  - **Line items**: scrolls to ① Line Items. Fades after 2.5 s.
  - **Payments received**: waits for the block to have a size, scrolls it to the middle, fades 4 s later. A block that never has a size is given up on after 150 looks, 15 s.
  - **Status or % done** (the GC run's chips): the same wait, fading 4.5 s later.
  - **Customer Pictures link**: scrolls to the box, focuses and selects it. Fades after 2.5 s.
- **`JobFormModal.tsx`**: five `useState`, six refs and eight effects are one call. The form drops 90 lines. The hook is called where the effects ran, so the order of the form's effects is unchanged. The form's load, reset and close still set the flags, through the setters the hook hands back; those functions are not touched.
- The two wait-until-it-has-a-size effects are still two. They differ by a ref, a fade time and a setter, and folding them was not worth a change to code that was otherwise only moved.

## How it was proven the same

- The whole document's HTML was written out for eleven ways of opening the form, each at open and at later times (39 snapshots), before and after the cut. Each snapshot also records which elements were scrolled to, in what order, and where the cursor was. All 39 are byte for byte identical.
- `JobFormModal.highlights.render.test.tsx` (10 cases, on the form itself) passes unchanged on the form before the cut. Ten wiring mutants each fail at least one case: a customer never passed or always passed, the focus field dropped or fixed, and six swaps of two names the hook hands back.

## Tests

- `useJobFormHighlights.render.test.tsx`, 14 cases on a fake clock.
- `JobFormModal.highlights.render.test.tsx`, 10 cases. Its clock is fake but keeps pace with the real one, so the form loads as usual and the fades are then walked forward.
