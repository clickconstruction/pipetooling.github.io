# v2.3269 — People → Overhead pool chart: click a bar to see the lines behind it (2026-09-11)

Client only; no migration, no new fetch. Owner ask: "click on specific bars, a part of larger bars in this chart, and see the transactions … there are some overhead parts transactions that are very large sticking above the rest." Built mockup-first (v1 → a "best we can do" review → v2), then shipped.

## What the review changed (v1 → v2)

The first cut listed a day's lines. The review asked what a list cannot answer — *is this normal?* — and the shipped version carries the judgment context on every line: how often that counterparty shows up in the window and whether this is its largest; a person's typical day and whether this is the longest; the day and the segment as "N× a typical day" (median of days with anything in them); the clicked bar stays outlined after the panel closes; the fix doors are real links.
