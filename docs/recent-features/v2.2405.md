# v2.2405 — Cross-packet price copy re-keys by fixture name; Add GC stays put (2026-08-27)

Root-caused from Wendi's BP384 ("numbers disappearing for my primary gc"):
her $264k of prices sat on the NORTHSTAR packet while PlanHub (her primary)
had none — she'd priced after a ＋ Add GC silently switched her onto the new
packet. And the rescue button made it worse: since v2.2132 every bid version
owns its OWN count rows, so "Copy X's base price" cloned children still keyed
to the SOURCE version's rows — invisible under the target's grid ("No prices
yet" straight after a copy).

- **New kernel `mapCountRowsByFixture`** (`src/lib/bids/`, 5 tests): source
  row → target row by trimmed case-insensitive fixture name; names duplicated
  on either side match nothing (never guess).
- **`copyBasePriceFromVersion`** (`BidsPricingTab.tsx`): after the clone,
  `rekeyClonedPricingToVersion` re-keys the clone's custom prices,
  assignments, and submission hides onto the target version's rows; children
  with no unique same-named row are deleted, and the toast reports
  matched/dropped counts.
- **`BidVersionPicker.submitAddGc`**: no more `onSwitch(newId)` — adding a
  GC keeps you on the packet you were on (first split selects the base,
  which is where you were standing), and the toast names whose packet you're
  still on.

Also observed while repairing BP384 (follow-ups, not in this PR):
scenario-card copy (`copyPricesIntoViewedScenario`) has the same
cross-version blindness; the scenario-delete archive captures only
`price_book_entries` (not the scenario row, custom prices, or assignments);
and the ★-delete guard let a starred scenario through earlier in the day.
