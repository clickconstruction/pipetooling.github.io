# v2.8714 — Pay calendar: the terms in days choice is hidden until a contract needs it (punch list #341) (2026-11-27)

Client only: no migration, no edge function. Punch list #341, on the answer to its call 4, quoted on the card from the managing session's record of 2026-11-25: *None does. The "terms in days" choice can be hidden until a contract needs it.*

## The change

- **The window no longer asks *How they pay* first.** `PayCalendarFields` drew a pair of presses at the top, *On a set day of the month* and *A number of days after the bill* (v2.7343, the card's PR 5). No contract here pays a number of days after the bill, so the pair is not drawn. The fields open on **Bills must be in by**.
- **Both places that share the fields read the same**: the office's Pay calendar window (`PayCalendarModal`) and the estimator's pay terms on a won bid (`DraftPayTermsPart`, `BidPayTermsToOffice`).
- **Hidden, not taken out.** Everything PR 5 built still reads, shows and saves terms in days: the column, the kernel's second shape, every line that says *Paid 45 days after the bill*, the morning run's read.
- **Terms already in days are never out of sight.** When the terms on the form are in days, a stored row or a draft's, the pair is drawn as before. It stays while the fields are open, so a switch to a set day can be switched back.
- **One switch brings it back**: `PAY_CALENDAR_DAYS_AFTER_OFFERED` in `src/lib/jobs/payCalendar.ts`, off. `showsHowTheyPay` is the rule the fields read.
- **The lines that introduce the calendar name what can be picked.** The window's first line, the estimator's, and the hover words of *Set a pay calendar…* said *a set day of the month, or a number of days after the bill*. They now say *a set day of the month* (`payTermsKindsWords`), and name both kinds again on terms that are in days.

## A call made in the build

*Hidden* is read as no control at all, with a switch in the code for the day a contract needs it. The other reading is a quiet press in the window, like *They pay a number of days after the bill instead*, which anyone could press with no change to the code. It was not built: the answer says hidden, and a press is still a choice on the page. It is one small change if Taunya wants it.

## The guide

`set-a-payers-pay-calendar`: *Fill it in* loses its first step. *A number of days after the bill* says the window does not ask for these terms today, how to get the choice back, and that a payer already on them still shows it.
