# v2.5904 — The Pipeline row, the job window and every Call button count their presses (2026-10-26)

PR 1 of punch list #190 (`to-dos/usage-paths/`), the recorder. The to-do measured sixty days of the app's own ledgers and found that the office's most common presses, the Pipeline row's own buttons and the Call button, write no row to `ui_nav_clicks`, so Settings → Usage could not see them and the next moves (the row's next-move button, the crew chip's +) could not be sized. This PR makes those presses count themselves. Nothing a person sees changes.

- **The resolver** (`src/lib/navClickTelemetry.ts`): `resolveNavClick` keeps its link rule (a root-relative `<a href>` inside `data-navtrack` chrome is the nav press it always was) and gains two. A `tel:` link inside any tracked chrome is the Call press, `control: call_pressed`, `target: #surface=<the marker>`. A labelled button counts only inside a marker that also carries `data-navbuttons`, named by `navButtonName`: `data-nav` when set, else the aria-label, title or text, cut where it starts naming a thing (` — `, ` · `, `: `, ` for `, ` at `, ` on `, `’s `, ` + `, ` (`), digits folded to `#`, months and weekdays to `date`, 48 characters at most. Then a guard: a capitalised word that is not one of the app's own (`NAV_WORDS`: verbs and nouns such as Open, Bill, Customer, Waiting, AIA) ends the name with `…`, since a customer's, a company's or a person's name is capitalised too. The press still counts under the shorter name, and a new button whose words are missing from the list counts as `Open …` until they are added or it gets a `data-nav`. So *Open job detail for Ridgeway Builders* is `#Open job detail`, *Billed Aug 12 + Ridgeway Builders’s pay speed* is `#Billed date #`, *Call customer at (830) 555-0187* is a Call press, and a bare person's name is `#…`. The older chrome (the nav, the gear menu, the banners) keeps recording links alone.
- **The markers**: `data-navtrack="pipeline-row" data-navbuttons` on the Pipeline's rows: the Waiting and Working sections' table (`JobsStagesTable.tsx`), the job rows (`StagesUnifiedJobRow.tsx`) and the bill rows (`StagesUnifiedInvoiceRow.tsx`), each with one capture listener, `recordViewerNavClick`; `data-navtrack="job-window" data-navbuttons` on the job window's header action row (`DetailJobModal.tsx`) and its phone bar (`JobWindowActionBar.tsx`), which since v2.5910 is also the crew's window's bar (Arrived or Leaving · Leave report · Note), so a tech's presses there count too. The listener records as the signed-in viewer, which the auth provider installs at sign-in beside the refused-write identity and the error-shown sink (`src/lib/navClickViewer.ts`), so no control reads auth while it renders and a render smoke that mocks the auth hook never meets it. A press it cannot read is dropped, never thrown. The buttons whose label is built around a customer carry a fixed `data-nav`: the speech bubble (`customer-messages`, `MessagesButton.tsx`), the owner's eye (`owner-sees`, `OwnerShareChip.tsx`), the development filter (`development-filter`), the customer's profile and link-or-create (`Open customer profile`, `Link or create customer`) and the portal globe (`customer-portal-link`).
- **The Call buttons**: `CallButton` (v2.5803) and `CallPhoneButton` (v2.3247) take a `site`; a press that dials writes `call_pressed{#surface=<site>}`, on the main link and on a way picked from the sheet, never on the sheet's open. The sites: `tell-customer`, `call-task`, `reply-box`, `thread`, `waiting-on-them`, `job-customer-door`, `customer-waiting`, `customer-waiting-banner`, `supply-house-ask`. The customer's pay page passes none: no user, no row. The Pipeline row's and the phone card's own `tel:` links are caught by the marker.
- **Settings → Usage**: `controlLabel` names the three controls (*Pipeline row*, *job window*, *Call*), so the control totals read them.
- **Tests**: `navClickTelemetry.test.ts` (the Call link in tracked chrome, the button inside and outside `data-navbuttons`, a wordless button, `data-nav`, and `navButtonName`'s cuts, folds, cap and guard: a company, an owner, a lone first name, a pay-date chip, a promise marked by a person, a month's letters inside *Mark* and *Money*), `navClickViewer.test.ts` (the viewer set and cleared, a row button and a Call link recorded as the viewer, nothing while signed out, no throw), `jobsStagesRowShared.customerDoor.render.test.tsx` (every button on both customer lines names itself without the customer, the GC or the development; broken on purpose by taking the bubble's `data-nav` off, and it failed), `OwnerShareChip.render.test.tsx` (the eye is `owner-sees`), `usageDashboard.test.ts` (the labels). The first CI run failed one smoke, `DispatchInboxSection.render.test.tsx`, whose auth mock had no `useOptionalAuth`, which the first cut read in the Call button at render; the viewer store replaced that read.
- **Docs**: `docs/APP_SHELL_ARCHITECTURE.md` → the markers; the to-do's *Where it stands*. No migration, no edge function, no schema change: the rows ride the table and the RPC v2.2342 built.

**Verified on the dev server** as the dev on prod data, pressing only what reads. A sweep of the Pipeline with every section open ran `navButtonName` over all 3,223 buttons in the 107 marked rows: 68 names, none of them a customer, a company or a person (the first sweep, before the guard and the `data-nav`s, found about twenty that were, which is why both exist), and the job window's header and phone bar name their presses as *Plumbing*, *Money story*, *Share job*, *Status ▾*, *Note* and the like. Then the presses: a row's Call with the dial blocked wrote `call_pressed · #surface=pipeline-row`, Assign work `pipeline-row · #Assign work`, the bubble `pipeline-row · #customer-messages`, Open job `pipeline-row · #Open job detail`, Share in the window `job-window · #Share job`, the phone bar's Note at 375 wide `job-window · #Note`, and the call task's Call on Messages `call_pressed · #surface=call-task`; every window was closed with nothing typed, posted or dialled. Frame 5 of the to-do's mock-up draws the same rows.

**What it leaves**: the phone's Pipeline cards (`StagesPhoneRow`) are not marked yet; their presses go through the ⋯ sheet, which is its own cut. The Dashboard's job rows and Job Mode's card are not marked. A Settings → Usage panel that lists the row's targets by name, and the hour of day, is the next readout the to-do names.
