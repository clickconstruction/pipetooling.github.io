# v2.8589 — Lien desk: every printed notice and affidavit keeps what it said (2026-11-25)

Punch list #362 (`to-dos/lien-to-do-one-run/README.md`), step 7, *changed since printed*: the part that needs no call and no migration. Client only: no migration, no edge function. Nothing on a screen changes.

## What it is for

The owner's run prints every paper, then mails and files them. A paper can be right at the printer and wrong a week later: a bill is corrected, an owner's name is fixed, a day of work is added. Step 7 names such a paper before it is mailed or filed. To do that the desk has to know what the paper said when it printed, and until now nothing kept that but the copy's HTML. An affidavit had no printed fact at all, so after a reload a printed affidavit still counted as to print.

## What this ships

1. **The paper's values ride with its filed copy.** Every lien print already files a sent copy (`docs/SENT_COPIES.md`). Its filing now fills `sourceSnapshot` with each paper's fields as they printed, under the key `lienPrint`: the kind, the jobs it speaks for, the desk items of a notice, and every value the paper prints as text. `sent_documents.source_snapshot` has held a pay application's figures since v2.4714, so no table changes. Five presses file it:
   - the run's one print (`useLienRunPrint`, through `lienRunReadyPacket` and `lienAffidavitPrintRun`),
   - the run window's packet, one copy and one envelope (`LienDeskRunModal`),
   - the Board's *Print N for notarization* (`lienAffidavitPrintRun`),
   - a job's Lien window, *Print for notarization* and the § 53.056 notice's envelopes (`LienFilingTabs`).
2. **The kernel that compares**, `src/lib/jobs/lienPrintSworn.ts`. `lienChangedSincePrint` sets a paper as it printed beside the paper as it would print now and answers each change with both values, in the paper's order. Money is compared to the cent whatever its commas, words whatever their spacing and case, a day or a tick as written. `lienPrintStanding` answers one of four: *never* printed, *printed* and the same today, *changed*, or *unread*.
3. **The read of each job's newest filed print**, `src/lib/jobs/lienPrintsIo.ts` over `loadSentPrintsForJobs` in `sentCopiesIo.ts`. Fifty jobs a read, the lien kinds alone, out of a printer alone. A read that fails answers `null`, never "nothing printed". A read that filled its limit says it is not whole.

## Two lists, on purpose

The snapshot keeps every value the paper prints. `LIEN_SWORN_FIELDS` names the ones a change in which counts. The first is wider than the second, so the answer to *which fields count as sworn* can grow the second list later and still read every print filed from today.

Built to the pick, which is the owner's to flip: every statement the paper makes counts. Three values are kept and not counted: the day a notice is dated, its contact person, and the county an affidavit is signed in, which is the notary's.

## What it does not do yet

- **Nothing reads it on a screen.** No count, no line and no press changed. The checklist does not yet say *changed since printed*, and a printed affidavit still counts as to print after a reload. Both wait on two calls: which fields count, and where *It stands* is kept.
- **A print before this version is `unread`.** It counts as a print with nothing to compare. The check will say it printed and not guess whether it changed.
- **A notice combined at one property is `unread` too.** It printed one claim for several jobs, so one job's paper alone is not its match. The run's one print never combines.
- **What "now" is for a notice is the caller's.** A desk item keeps the notice as drafted, and `lienNoticeParted` (v2.7517) already sets that against the bills. The compare takes whichever fields it is handed.
- **One copy printed alone counts as the notice's newest print.** If the office redraws a notice and reprints one envelope, the other recipient still holds the earlier paper, and the compare reads the newer one.
