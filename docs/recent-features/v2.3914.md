## What shipped

- **`src/hooks/useStandingDiscountOffer.ts`** — the customer's standing rate (read when the customer changes; a failed read is "no standing discount"), the waived flag (re-read per job), the offer memo over the tested `standingDiscountOffer`, `applyStandingOffer` (the discount row added through the shell's `setFixtures`, so the discount invariant still runs) and `waiveStandingOffer` (the fire-and-forget write of `jobs_ledger.standing_discount_waived_at` when a job is open). Takes `customerId`, `editing`, `fixtures`, `setFixtures`.
- **`src/hooks/useJobPropertyCandidates.ts`** — the saved addresses of the job's customer and GC for the property record's list. The link (`customerAddressId`) stays the form's — it is an identity field the autosave carries — so the hook is handed it with its setter, clears a held link when there is no customer and no GC or when a successful read proves it foreign, and hands back `setPropertyCandidates` for the fact rows that add to and patch the list. `PropertyCandidateRow` moved with it.
- **The shell** calls each hook once: the standing offer where its block was, the property candidates where the loader's effect ran, so the order of the form's effects is unchanged. `JobFormModal.tsx` is 4,754 → 4,677 lines; two `useState` and the three effects left with them. Behavior-neutral.
- **One quirk retired** (the map's #30): the property loader's effect listed `open` in its dependencies with no such local — it was the global `window.open`, a constant. The hook keys on the customer and the GC alone, which is what ran.
- **Tests** — `useJobPropertyCandidates.render.test.tsx` (5): no customer and no GC reads nothing and clears a held link; the list is both parties' addresses; a foreign link is cleared; a failed read keeps the link; the link changing alone does not re-read. `useStandingDiscountOffer.render.test.tsx` (5): no customer, the offer and Apply, a failed read, waving off on an open job, waving off on a new job and a job already waved off.
