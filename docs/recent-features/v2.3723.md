2. **The filing sheet threw itself away.** Typing the Google Doc link and clicking **Record as signed** without Enter: the field commits on blur, the sheet reflows, the click that began on the button ends on the backdrop, and [`ResponsiveModalShell`](../../src/components/ResponsiveModalShell.tsx) treated it as a backdrop click — closed, nothing filed, no toast. The function now stamps `sent_channel = 'link'` on every send ([`EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`](../EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md#send-job-contract)). Deploy: `bash scripts/deploy-functions.sh send-job-contract`.
