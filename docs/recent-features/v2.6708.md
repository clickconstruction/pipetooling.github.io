## What changed

Off Sat and Sun."), `outsideMinutes` (a session's minutes outside the day's ranges, on the company clock through `appTzWallTimeMs`). ## Read closer than the spike

- Capacity (Jobs → Job Summary, v2.2828) stays as it is: company-wide, Monday weeks, roster × 8. A later card can read the summed drawings instead.
- Long day stays 8 h (the owner's ask); the count is a fact about how the crew works, not an alarm, so the label reads *Days over 8 h*.
- Company holidays have no record in the app, so a holiday week reads light; the footer says so.
