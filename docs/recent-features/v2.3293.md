# v2.3293 — The Legal desk (PR 1 of the Legal portal train): review a Collections account the way an attorney would receive it (2026-09-11)

Client only — no migration, no edge function. First of five PRs: desk read side → the attorney-ready gate (migration) → the firm's portal read → the firm's portal write → the firm's emails. Prototype and decisions: the owner's click-through prototype (artifact 071289bc) and the memory note `legal-portal-proposal`.

## Why

Nothing packaged a non-paying account for a law firm: demand letters, lien filings, contracts, promises, chase calls, field evidence and the customer's contacts ledger all existed as rows with no per-account view, no "is this worth referring", and no place for the office to see what would leave the building. The owner's flow: any office staff move a job to Collections; a dev reviews the account on the desk and marks it attorney-ready; only then does a firm see it. This PR is the review.

## What shipped

- **Kernel [`src/lib/legal/legalPacket.ts`](../../src/lib/legal/legalPacket.ts)** (+14 tests): `payerForJob` / `groupCollectionsByPayer` (account = payer: `gc_customer_id` first, else `customer_id`, else a name-only key), `buildLegalPacket(input)` → the five sections plus:
  - **theory** — `contract` (any job signed per `buildJobContractCoverage`) · `sworn` (every job: a bill the customer received per `invoiceReachedCustomer`, field evidence with GPS, no dispute touch on record) · `lien` (a lien-ready property record and an open or filed window) · `none`;
  - **worth** — balance, the firm's cut (`LEGAL_DEFAULT_FEE` 33%, $350 filing cost until the firm row carries it), net, flags ("no money" note, dispute, broken promises, name-only payer), verdict `worth it · marginal · not worth it`; `quickNet` sorts the rail before packets load;
  - **lien clock** per job via `computeJobLienClock` from the last clock-session day (falls back to `last_work_date`); `isSub` = the payer is a GC; statuses `notice_open · affidavit_open · filed · closed · no_work`;
  - **the "what was said" timeline** — `customer_contacts` entries, promises (with outcome), chase touches, the collections note — keyed `contact:<id>` / `promise:<id>` / `call:<id>` / `note:<jobId>`, with `sharedByDefault` = on or after the first bill and `holdOverrides` (PR 2 stores them) winning both ways;
  - **gaps** — the contract gap is red only when no sworn-account basis holds either; lien windows open; payer/contact/address stops; note, demand, property, evidence, never-asked notes; a `write_down` nudge when the verdict is not worth it. `readiness` + lettered `exhibits`.
- **Loader [`useLegalPacketData`](../../src/components/jobs/legal/useLegalPacketData.ts)** — fourteen fail-soft sources (customer, contact persons, `customer_contacts`, `customer_addresses`, `job_contracts`, accepted `estimates`, `job_demand_letters`, `job_lien_filings`, the three promise/chase RPCs, `list_reports_for_job_ledger` per job, `clock_sessions`, thread notes), each named in `failed` when it can't load so the desk never shows a gap that isn't real.
- **[`LegalDeskModal`](../../src/components/jobs/legal/LegalDeskModal.tsx)** (+2 render smokes) — rail by net, worth panel, gap strip with Fix buttons, five tabs, a ↗ door on every section (Edit customer via `useEditCustomerModal`, Edit job, Contract desk, Lien instruments, Accounts Receivable, Sessions, Reports/Activity, Job thread, They said…, Call mode, the Pipeline row), **Print packet** ([`legalPacketPrint.ts`](../../src/lib/legal/legalPacketPrint.ts): cover sheet + sections, held entries omitted), **Write down…** → the existing `AgreedWriteDownModal` on the account's largest open bill line.
- **Stages tab** — the ⚖ Legal button in a tier under the Collections header (Collections managers: dev / master / assistant-like), the modal mount, `openLegalDesk` on the imperative handle; **Jobs page** — `?legal=1` / `?legal=<payer key>` deep link, same gating and wait-for-the-handle shape as `openBankPayments`, forces the Stages tab.
- Help guide `review-a-collections-account-before-it-goes-to-your-attorney`; GLOSSARY "Legal desk".
