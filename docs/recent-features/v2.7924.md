# v2.7924 — Documents: a waiting staff or sub agreement opens on its own line in People (2026-11-19)

Two faults a read-only review of the Agreements tab found on 2026-11-19. Neither had harmed data.

## 1. A waiting paper's line opened an error

### Why

On Documents → Agreements, `agreementDoor` sends a staff, sub or partner agreement that is not signed to `/people?tab=contracts&doc=<id>`: "People → Contracts on that paper, where it is sent again" (punch list #376). People → Contracts opened the signed record window on every `doc` id, because the address was first written by the signing page's thank-you for a signed paper. For a waiting paper the window answered *This document is not in signed status.* Two such lines were on prod.

### What changed

- `src/lib/contractsDocLink.ts` (new kernel) reads the id off the address (`contractsDocLinkId`) and chooses the landing once the tab has read its papers (`contractsDocLanding`):
  - a signed paper, or an id the tab does not list, opens the signed record window as before;
  - a paper that is not signed opens its person's line, with the paper's own row scrolled to and marked;
  - a paper whose person has no line on the tab says so in words (`contractsDocOffRosterWords`).
- `PeopleContractsTab` answers the link once, after its first read. It opens the person through the Agreements panel's own jump (`jumpToContractsPerson`, which also opens the Archived section and widens the filter) and marks the row: `data-contract-doc-row`, `aria-current`, a blue outline on the card or the table line. The row's own **Send** or **Resend** is the next press. The send window does not open by itself, so a stray press cannot send.
- `PersonContractSignedRecordModal`'s refusal for a paper that is not signed now reads *This paper is not signed yet, so it has no signed record.*
- `agreementDoor` is unchanged: its address was right.
- The guide *find an agreement we sent or signed* says what the press opens.

### Tests

`contractsDocLink.test.ts` holds the choice, and reads the address `agreementDoor` writes. `PeopleContractsTab.docLink.render.test.tsx` holds the wiring: the waiting paper's row is marked with Resend and no error, a signed paper still opens its signed record, and no id opens nothing.

### Checked on live data

In a dev preview signed in as a dev, both waiting lines of Documents → Agreements were pressed. Each opened People → Contracts with its paper's row marked and in view, **Resend** on it, no window and no error. One is under a living person and one under the Archived section, which opened by itself. A signed paper's address still opened its signed record. Send and Resend were not pressed.

The row is scrolled to at once, not with a glide: a glide never runs in a tab that opens in the background, and the walk's own hidden pane showed it.

## 2. The consent table's allowed list had lost a kind

### Why

`esign_consents_record_type_check` lists the kinds of record a consent row may name. `20261030030000_hours_statements` restated it from an older list, with `hours_statement` and without `gc_owner_contract`, which `20261010090000_gc_owner_contract_sends` had added. `submit-portal-request` writes a `gc_owner_contract` row when a GC-mode customer signs our contract in their portal. That write is best-effort, so the signing would have gone through and the consent words would have been lost with only a log line.

### What changed

- `20261119120000_esign_consents_owner_contract_kind.sql` restates the check with all eleven kinds in use, `NOT VALID` then `VALIDATE` ([its page](../migrations/20261119120000_esign_consents_owner_contract_kind.md)).
- `src/lib/esignConsentKinds.test.ts` (new) reads the newest restatement of the check and fails when it differs from the functions' union `EsignConsentRecordType`, or when a function writes a kind the check refuses.
- The bed `gc_owner_contract` runs the new migration again last and inserts all eleven kinds. Its second run of the contract's own migration had put the lost kind back in the bed, which is why the bed never saw the loss.

### Confirmed, not changed

- Prod's ledger holds an `hours_statement` row of 2026-11-08, which only the later list takes, and no migration after it restates the check. So the live check lacked `gc_owner_contract` until the push.
- `gc_owner_contract_sends` had 0 rows. Nothing was lost and nothing is backfilled.
- No edge function changed, so none is redeployed.

## Not done

- The other beds that run an older restatement a second time (`gc-award`, `gc-portal-p5`) still end with that older list inside the bed. They assert only the kinds of their day, so they pass.
