PR 2 of the train in `to-dos/what-customers-see-journeys/README.md`. Client + three edge functions (`get-job-contract`, `send-job-contract`, `remind-job-contracts`); no migration. **Deploy the three functions after merge**, plus the other importers of `_shared/customerSampleFixtures.ts` (`customer-portal`, `sub-portal`, `get-estimate-for-customer`, `get-bid-proposal-room`, `get-contract-for-signer`) so `check:edge-drift` stops flagging the shared edit.

## What shipped

- **One builder for the agreement emails** — [`_shared/jobContractEmail.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/jobContractEmail.ts): `buildJobContractSendEmail` and `buildJobContractReminderEmail`, the senders' own wording moved verbatim. `send-job-contract` and `remind-job-contracts` now call them (behaviour identical; +3 tests pin the subject, the greeting, the amount line, the typed message with its line breaks, the last-reminder closing). `src/lib/jobContractEmail.ts` re-exports them for the browser, the same door the estimate and bid-room emails use — so the tab renders the real email over the sample and there is no second copy to drift. *This was the decision's alternative: a sample mode on the senders was proposed, but the reminder is a cron function and the app already had the re-export pattern; it costs one deploy either way and this keeps the send paths untouched.*
- **The sample agreement** — `SAMPLE_JOB_CONTRACT` in `_shared/customerSample.ts` (Sam Sample's water heater at 100 Sample St, $1,850, four scope lines, half down, a short sample terms body) and `sampleJobContractResponse(state, company, todayYmd)` in `_shared/customerSampleFixtures.ts`, the exact shape the signing page reads. `get-job-contract` answers `sample` (out for signature) and `sample-done` (signed by Sam Sample) before touching the database: no row, no view stamp, no event.
- **`JobContractSign.tsx`** honours `sampleStateFromToken`: the Sample banner, and signing on the sample page moves straight to the signed view without calling `sign-job-contract`.
- **Four steps go live** in `customerJourneys.ts`: Agreement email (`email: 'job-contract'`), Agreement to sign (`/contract/sign?t=sample`), Reminder (`email: 'job-contract-reminder'`), Signed (`/contract/sign?t=sample-done`). `SAMPLE_EMAIL_IDS` lists every email the tab builds, so the tab stops hard-coding four.
