## What shipped

- **Our number** names the Pipeline job general conditions are spent on (`GcGeneralConditionsJob.tsx`): the Pipeline's own search, billing-only jobs left out (`searchPipelineJobs`, `search_jobs_ledger`), **Use this job**, **Change** and **Let it go**, saved by `setGcGeneralConditionsJob` through `gc_project_money`'s policy. A job our own crew holds comes last with no press (`heldBy`; its entries come with Building's U8b).
- **The read** (`ownWorkCostIo.ts`, `loadOwnWorkCosts`): `has_payroll_access()` first, then each named job the Costs tab's own way (`loadJobChargesTimelineInputs`, 4 at a time as Bid vs actual), into its spend (`ownWorkJobCost`). Without pay access nothing is read, since labor would read $0.
- **The rules** (`ownWorkCost.ts`, `generalConditionsCost`): at their budget until the spend passes it, then at the spend; at what they cost once the job closes; at their budget with no job named, without pay access, before the read and when it failed. One sentence says which (`generalConditionsWords`), on Money and on Closeout.
- **Money's margin** (`GcMoneyMargin.tsx`, `jobMargin(state, project, own)`): **Our own work**, the general conditions' over or under, in the margin; the breakdown's general conditions line; earned so far as billed for the trades and the fee, and as billed less spent for general conditions; the footnote rewritten.
- **Closeout**: the general conditions line.
- **Guide**: `see-the-money-on-our-gc-jobs` (*What our general conditions really cost*). **Docs**: `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` (Our number, Money), `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` (the **Owner Billing (Our number):** bullet: the job's name and the pay rule; and a blank line between the first door's lane bullets, the lead's ask, so two lanes' edits never touch).
