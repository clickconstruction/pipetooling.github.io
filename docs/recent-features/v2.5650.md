# v2.5650 — The office side of a paid claim: the deposit it fits, and the week with nothing landed (2026-10-19)

Punch list #129, PR 4 of 6, on PR 3a (v2.5648, the `paid_claim` kind) and PR 3b (v2.5649). A customer's word that they paid is useful at two moments: when the deposit lands, and when nothing lands. Neither makes the office a task. Migration `20261019100000_paid_claims_read.sql` (`docs/migrations/20261019100000_paid_claims_read.md`); no edge function; nothing in the kernels copy.

- **The read** (`list_paid_claims()`): every live claim for the promise readers, their jobs' for the job's people (`is_job_people`), oldest day first, each with its job (number, name, status, bid), its customer, who heard it, and `landedSince` / `landedOn`: the sum and the latest day of the job's payments on or after the day they named. A claim closes on its own when the deposit is allocated.
- **The kernel** (`lib/jobs/paidClaims.ts`): the parse, `claimOpen`, `claimStale` (open and `STALE_AFTER_DAYS` = 7 or more since the day they named), `staleClaims` and `myStaleClaims` (by who heard it), `staleClaimLine` (*J1032 Whitfield repipe · Dana said a check went out Oct 20 · nothing landed in 9 days*), `claimFitsDeposit` (open, the amount they named or the job's open money within a dollar, on or after the day they named; the customer's name in the counterparty a second sign, never a requirement) and `claimSuggestionWords` (*Theo wrote Oct 21: Dana says they paid, a check in the mail Oct 20 · “Check #1187”*).
- **The deposit it fits** (`BankPaymentsModal`, `ArPayerMatches`): the modal reads the claims once per open (`paidClaimsIo.ts`, fail-soft) and, for the selected deposit, the first claim whose job has open bills on the board and fits leads the pane under **They said they paid**: the words, *the deposit names them* when it does, and the job's open bills as the same rows the payer's section draws, so the press that allocates is the one the office already makes. Bills already on a line are left out, as the payer's are.
- **The week with nothing landed** (`useMyStaleClaims`, `dashboardNeedsYou.ts`'s `paid-claims-stale`, rank 12 beside the deposits): the Dashboard's Needs you carries *A customer said they paid, and nothing landed* with the oldest claim's line, for the person who heard it (the office, an estimator, a primary), opening the won bid's Job block for an estimator (`/bids?tab=bid-board&bidId=`) and the Pipeline for the office. The won bid's money line turns amber with *nothing landed in N days* (`bidMoneyLine.ts`'s `staleDays`, from the strip's own payments) once a week has passed; before that a claim reads quiet.
- **Tests**: `paidClaims.test.ts` (the parse, days since, stale at seven and never once something landed, the stale ones oldest first and mine, the line, the deposit a claim fits, the suggestion's words); `bidMoneyLine.test.ts` (a stale claim amber with the days, quiet before, quiet once something landed); the SQL bed `scripts/pgtest-paid-claims-read.sh` over `supabase/tests/paid_claims_read/` (who reads what, a payment before and after the day they named, a voided claim), registered in `scripts/sql-beds.txt`.
