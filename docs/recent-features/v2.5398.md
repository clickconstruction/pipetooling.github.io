# v2.5398 — Messages: the rules behind They owe us on Follow up, behind the scenes (2026-10-12)

Residential Messages R8 · 6 (`to-dos/gc-mode/messages/RESIDENTIAL_BUILD_PLAN.md` on `spike/gc-mode`, card 6 of `customer-messages-integrations-mockups.html` v0.2): the kernel for a fourth Follow up section, **They owe us**, beside We owe them. A new file, `src/lib/followUp/theyOweUs.ts`, with its test. No screen, no schema, and `followUp/queue.ts` is untouched; the section is wired in later.

- **`theyOweUs(bills, contacts, now)`**: one row per person with an open bill, carrying their worst bill and a count of the others. It writes no money rule of its own:
  - **Open** is the AR page's bill-truth kernel, `computeBillTruth`: the billed and Collections rows with money still open. A bill marked Uncollectible, one on a paid job, and one fully paid but waiting on Mark Paid are not rows.
  - **Owner Billing's bills stay out.** A bill on a GC project's billing job (`jobs_ledger.billing_only`) is reminded by `gc/ownerBillingRemind.ts`, so it never appears here.
  - **Late and the due day** come from the AR page's expected-pay chip, `billedExpectedPayModel`: the payer's own pace (the company median with no history), or their promise when they gave one. The rows run soonest due first, so the latest late bill leads. Bills with no clock come after, the longest billed first. A job balance with no bill row has no pace, as on the Stages board.
  - **The person** is the bill's payer by `listPayer`, as on the AR lists, matched to the spine's contact for that customer. A payer with no contact yet stays on the AR page alone.
  - **The last reminder** is the newest send after the first in `jobs_ledger_invoice_stripe_email_sends`, the table the Lien desk's demand letter reads as "Invoice re-sent". The first send is the bill itself.
  - **The row's line** is card 6's: *invoice 1312 · $4,850 · 20 days · last reminder Oct 15*, or *no reminder yet*. The job's number names the bill, and `· 1 more bill` follows when the person owes on more than one.
- **`billDueDay(job, inv, paySpeeds, promise, todayYmd)`** is the one step from a bill to its expected day, exported so the residential flow's loader stamps a Paid round's due day the same way. It is `billedExpectedPayModel` read against the bill's payer (`paySpeedPayer`), the GC on a GC-billed bill, as the Stages board reads it.
- **`reminderLine(row, lang)`** returns the bill's reminder as a team quick reply for customers on the **Paid** step, which is the customer's move once the bill is out. It has the blanks `{first}`, `{job}`, `{amount}` and `{link}`, in English and Spanish, and the fills from the row. A bill with a pay page links to `/pay/<bill id>`. A paper bill or a job balance gets the line that asks for their day, as Owner Billing's reminder does. The words pass `plainWordsFailures` with the blanks in and filled.
- **`theyOweUsCount(rows)`** is the badge: each person with a late bill. A bill still inside the payer's pace sits in the section without counting.
- **Tests** run over made-up bills: Dana Whitfield's 1312 at 20 days with a reminder on Oct 15, Marcus Bell's paper bill 1288 at 6 days with none, a paid job, and a GC project's open bill. They pin the rows, the order, the flag, the line, the count and both languages of both reminder lines. They also cover a settled bill, an Uncollectible one, Collections and part payment, two bills for one person, a promise, missing pay speeds, a missing contact, a job balance and the GC payer's pace. Dropping the Owner Billing filter fails 12 of the 21 tests, and running the latest due first fails 10.
