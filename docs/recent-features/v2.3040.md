# v2.3040 — Test-gap audit, pass 50: the customer profile fetch gets its first tests (2026-09-07)

Follow-up to v2.3039. Tests only; no kernel, client, migration or edge-function change.

## Why this one

`customers/fetchCustomerProfile.ts` (134 lines) is the one batched fetch behind the customer profile modal and the Customer Hub: nine reads under existing RLS — the customer, contact persons, jobs with invoices and payments, projects with their workflow steps, bids, estimates, extra addresses, the GC job count and the last GC statement — assembled into what the modal shows. Two callers, no test.

## What the tests found

Nothing wrong. Contracts now pinned that were only implied:

- Every read is keyed to the customer, with the orders the modal relies on (jobs, projects, bids and estimates newest first; contacts by name; addresses by sequence; the last statement by sent date, one row); the addresses read stays `select *` so it keeps working until the primary flag lands.
- Contacts without a name are dropped; the primary address (which mirrors the customer's own) and blank addresses are hidden, while rows that predate the primary flag are listed.
- A project's attention comes from its first workflow's steps as of today in the company calendar, is null with no steps, and is null when the attention builder throws.
- The GC chip reads a head-count (null → 0) and the latest statement's sent date (none → null).
- Only the customer and jobs reads are hard: a missing customer throws "Customer not found", a failed jobs read throws, and the side reads degrade to empty.

Covers the nine reads' filters and orders, the full assembly, the throwing builder and empty side reads, and the hard versus soft failures.
