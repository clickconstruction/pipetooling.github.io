# v2.8368 — Before we start: Is it as told? marks only the things it showed you (2026-11-23)

Punch list #382, the first of the faults the audit of PR 10 saw and left as they were, built on the owner's word of 2026-11-23. One migration and a small client change: `20261123100000_start_check_arrive_shown.sql` (`docs/migrations/20261123100000_start_check_arrive_shown.md`), `lib/startChecks/startChecksIo.ts` and `JobModeArrivalCheck.tsx`. No edge function. **Pushed only on a go that names it**, and right after the merge: the client calls the new signature.

## The gap

`start_check_arrive` marked every bullet there was to look at when the answer landed, not the bullets the card had shown. A card left open while the office added a bullet, or gave a No a new yes, marked one it never showed: **Yes, all as told** said a yes we saw on a thing nobody of ours had looked at.

## The fix

- The door takes a third argument, `p_shown`, the ids of the bullets the card showed, and marks the bullets to look at among them and no other. A bullet to look at that the card did not show is left as it is, with the office's word standing and no yes we saw over it; since someone has arrived, the read asks only about a No with a new yes (v2.8238), so that bullet is the office's to tick. A later No on such a bullet is news and is kept with its line to Dispatch, where the old door could not meet the case. A No on a bullet not shown, or a card that showed nothing of what is asked, is refused with *The list has changed. Open it again.*, and the card reads the list again as since v2.8136.
- The old two-argument function is dropped and the new one defaults `p_shown`, so an old client's call resolves and PostgREST has one function to pick. With `p_shown` null the door marks everything to look at, as before.
- The card sends `ask.bullets`' ids with every answer.

## Checked

The SQL bed `30_arrive.sql` has a sixth section, nine checks: a card that showed one of two marks one and leaves the other with no mark and nobody asked, a No on a bullet not shown is refused, an old card showing only what is seen now is refused, a card showing both keeps a No with its line to Dispatch, one function remains with three arguments and the grants. Docker does not run on this Mac, so the SQL beds workflow is the bed's first run. `JobModeArrivalCheck.render.test.tsx` reads the shown list on every send. The guide *say whether a site is as we were told* says the card marks only the lines it showed.
