# v2.7259 — The law firm's lien grid leaves off a job paid in kind, as the Lien desk does (2026-11-14)

v2.7253 took a job on an open in-kind deal off the Lien desk (`src/lib/jobs/lienInKind.ts`, the owner's rule of 2026-11-14) and named two readers it left alone. v2.7257 closed the first, Materials' held line. This closes the second: the lien grid on the firm's portal.

## Why it is the same rule, not a new one

The firm's grid is not a list of the firm's matters. `legal-portal`'s `readLienBook` sends the whole book (the owner's call of 2026-09-24, asked again 2026-10-05), and the firm's guide says its rows "are the same ones the office's Lien desk By job tab holds, read live". Since v2.7253 that was no longer true: the office's By job book dropped a job paid in kind and the firm's grid still drew its path and its lien days. A job the owner treats as paid has no place in a book of billed jobs with money open.

A matter with the firm is a separate read (the matter's jobs, bills, filings and lien timeline) and is untouched. A job on a matter that is also on an in-kind deal still shows whole on its matter. It is only off the grid.

## The change

- `shapeLienBookForCounsel` (`supabase/functions/_shared/legalLienBookShape.ts`) drops a job whose `in_kind_arrangement_id` is set from every list, at the seam the ZZ test jobs leave by: its months, affidavit windows, desk items, filings and owners, and a GC or a property only such jobs point at. The link is read for the rule and cut before the book is sent, like `customer_name`, so the firm receives no link to a deal.
- The rule is the Lien desk's own. `lib/jobs/lienInKind.ts` joins the generated edge copy (`npm run gen:edge-kernels`, off the index, read by path) and `legal-portal` joins `IMPORTERS`. Its bundle reaches two files of the copy (18 KB) and no index. `edgeUploadGraph.test.ts` said the portal's upload holds none of the copy, a guard from the day the upload went over 5 MB; it now names those two files and still holds the upload under 500 KB (it is 236 KB).
- The function's jobs read is `LIEN_BOOK_COUNSEL_SELECT.jobs`, built with `withLienInKindColumn`. The function no longer writes a column list of its own, and a test holds the select equal to the app's `LIEN_BOOK_JOB_COLUMNS`.
- `lienInKind.ts`'s list of readers, `docs/LIEN_DESK_ARCHITECTURE.md`'s v2.7253 note and `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`'s lien book note name the firm's grid. The Lien desk guide and the attorney's portal guide each say it in one sentence.

No migration. No word of a notice, an affidavit, a waiver or a release changed.

## Deploy

**`legal-portal` must be redeployed** for the grid to change: `supabase functions deploy legal-portal`. Until then the portal answers as before. The deploy needs the owner's yes and was not run by this PR.

Adding a file to the generated copy rewrites the header of its index, as every such PR does. `check:edge-drift` will name the four functions that read the index (`customer-portal`, `drive-link`, `gc-trade-portal`, `photo-save`) for that comment alone. Their behaviour is the same before and after.
