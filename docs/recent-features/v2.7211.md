# v2.7211 — Paint a map: draw the way in to a job, and mark what is where (2026-11-13)

Punch list #345, PR 2. The first screen of Paint a map: a line drawn by hand on the view from above and named places with a photo, saved on a job, and shown under the job's address. Walking a line and the link that shares a map are the PRs after this one.

## What changed

- **The line under the address** (`JobPaintedMapsStrip` in `DetailJobModal`'s top band, so on every tab): each of the job's painted maps as one line with its name and length, a development's map with *from* its development, and **＋ Paint a map**. A job with no painted map looks as it did, plus that one press.
- **The same line in three more places**: on a tech's own job card in Job Mode (`JobModeDetailsSection`, beside the address and out of the day's press bar, with the press to paint one, since the person standing on the site is the one who can mark it); on the **On my way** sheet, for the job picked, with no press to paint one and nothing at all for a job with none; and under a development's address on the Development page (`DevelopmentPaintedMapsStrip`), the site's own maps first and each lot's after with the lot's name. A map painted on the Development page is for every lot.
- **The start sheet** (`PaintAMapStartSheet`): a name with three chips, a line of words, and what the map is for. A job in a development may be painted for the job or for every lot. **Start drawing** begins with a line and **Start with a place** with a dot. Nothing is saved by the sheet.
- **The window** (`PaintedMapWindow`, a portal at `PAINTED_MAP_WINDOW_Z`): the map, its lines with an honest length and who made each, **Draw a line**, **Directions to the start**, a measured length typed in, **Move its corners**, **Remove this line**, **Rename this map**, **Throw this map away**. A new map is made by its first save, a line or a place, and not before.
- **Drawing by hand** (`PaintedMapCanvas`): a press adds a corner at the end, a drag moves a corner, a drag on the small dot in the middle of a leg makes a corner, **Undo** steps back one move at a time (`draftLine.ts`). A corner dragged near the end of another line catches it.
- **Places** (`PaintedPlacePanel`): **＋ Place** puts a blue dot in the middle of what the map is showing. The painter drags it onto the spot, names it from six chips or their own words, adds a word for whoever comes next and a photo. **Put the dot where I am** takes one reading of the phone and shows how sure it was, and the margin is kept only while the dot still sits on that reading. A place wears a number, the same on its dot and in the list. The photo is shrunk on the phone by the task photos' own rule (`useTaskShot`), goes to the private `painted-map-photos` bucket under the map's id, and is read back by a signed address. A place whose save is refused takes its photo back out of the bucket.
- **No codes on a place**: the sheet says so beside the words box. A shared map shows a place's words to whoever holds its link.
- **The magnifying glass**: on a phone, a held press that slides, or a drag on a corner, opens a round glass above the finger. It is a second small Leaflet map two zoom steps in, centred on the spot under the finger, with a cross at its centre. It draws the tiles the big map already holds, larger, so it is never blank on a weak signal, and the sharper tiles over them where the picture goes that deep. A mouse gets no glass.
- **The door** (`paintedMapDoor.ts`, `PaintedMapHost` in the Layout): any screen asks for a map to open or a new one to start, and one host answers. The line sits inside the job's window and needs no provider above it.
- **The guide** `paint-a-map`, and the glossary, project and access docs.

## Is this the best we can do

Things that changed while building, each for a reason found on the screen.

- **A dragged dot could be pulled back from under the finger.** react-leaflet moves a marker to its `position` prop whenever the prop is a new array, and swaps its icon whenever the icon is a new object. The canvas re-renders on every move of a drag, to feed the glass, so each move handed the marker a new array and a new icon, and the drag's result depended on which came last. Positions are memoized on the point and every icon is made once now. Found on the phone walk: the dot stood still while the glass moved. After the fix the dot's centre and the finger read 132, 290 and 132, 289.
- **A new map may start with a place.** The first build opened every new map on drawing, so a map of what is where had to begin with a line nobody wanted. The start sheet has two presses now, and giving up a first line or place goes back to the empty map and not out of the window.
- **The glass had no cross on the first try.** The cross and its centre dot were drawn under the small map's own layers. They sit above them now. Found on the phone walk's picture, not by a test.
- **A press held still was chosen over a second mode.** The card offered *press and hold, then slide*. The other way was a switch between adding and placing. One gesture more is cheaper than one mode more, and a quick press still adds a corner as before.
- **Catching uses the screen, not the ground.** The card said a corner catches *within a few feet*. At the zoom a route is drawn at, a few feet is three dots of the screen, which no finger can aim at. A corner now catches a target within 14 dots of the screen, and never from more than 50 ft away.
- **A line's corners can be moved only on a line drawn by hand.** A walked line carries the stretches the phone was not sure of, by position, and moving corners would shift them. Walked lines get their own fixing screen with the walking PR.
- **The Development page has no card for painted maps.** The mock-up drew a seventh card after Plans. The page's six looks are a strip of chips, a fold each and tests that count them, and the rule this card set itself was *wherever the app already shows an address*. So the maps are lines under the development's address, as on a job, and the six looks stay six.
- **A line under an address costs nothing to draw.** The first build looked the job's address up on every open of a job's window, to know where a new map would open. Found by the job window's own tests, which saw a lookup they had not asked for. The window does the lookup now, and only for a map with nothing on it yet.
- **The header's search has no Paint a map door yet.** It would start a map with no job, and nothing in the app lists such a map, so the painter could not find it again. The door comes with the Map page's layer, where every map is listed.
