# v2.5794 — Tell the customer: Preview shows the email and the text as the customer sees them (2026-10-23)

The owner, on the Tell the customer sheet during the Messages walk: "I would like to be able to hit something here that pulls up a preview in a new modal on top without closing the old modal that shows the users what the customer will see." Each What goes row gets **Preview**, and the window opens over the sheet.

- **The kernel** (`src/lib/tellCustomer/preview.ts`, its test): `textPreview` fills `{link}` with `SAMPLE_ACCEPT_LINK`, the real link's shape, and says when it did, so the window can note that the real one fills in when the email goes; any other blank stays in braces and is named with the sheet's own words. `previewKinds` offers the email only when the page handed one in and the text only when one goes. `splitLinks` cuts the words at each link so the bubble draws it as a phone would. `PREVIEW_WORDS` holds every sentence, under the plain-words rule.
- **The window** (`src/components/customers/TellCustomerPreview.tsx`, its smoke): a `ResponsiveModalShell` at z 1250 over the sheet's 1100, titled *What Dana sees*; **The email · The text** pills when both go; the email in a `data-theme="light"` box with From, Reply to, To and Subject over a sandboxed frame of the page's built HTML; the text in a phone frame, one incoming bubble under *To (737) 300-9478, from the company number.*, the link underlined, the sample note under it. Escape is taken in the capture phase and `preventDefault`ed, so the sheet's own window listener, which skips a defaulted Escape, keeps the sheet open; × and **Back to the sheet** close the window alone.
- **The sheet** (`TellCustomerSheet.tsx`): `TellEstimate.previewEmail`, the email the page built; the quiet **Preview** at the right of *Email to …* (only with a built email) and of *Text to …*; the window drawn inside the shell's children with the box's words as they are, so an edit previews at once.
- **The page** (`Estimates.tsx`): hands `customerEmailPreview` in as `previewEmail`, the same message its own Email preview tab shows, built by `buildEstimateLetterheadEmail` with the preview accept link, From through `estimateEmailFrom`. A booking's sheet has no email, so only the text previews there.
