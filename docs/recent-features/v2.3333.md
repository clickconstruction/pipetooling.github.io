# v2.3333 — Cash App reconciliation, PR 5: the memo rule for split pay (2026-09-11)

Owner (2026-09-11, after the first import): "a lot of payments were split with Less | Additional and some have memos showing how pay was split." True in the data: recorded payments carry memos like `Cashapp 500`, `CashApp in 300 and 100`, `-500 for motorcycle 1809.20 paid via cashapp`, `Paid out of 1,073.94 on cashapp made today` — the recorded amount is the report's figure, the memo names the cash that actually moved. Measured against the review lane on the dumps: 19 sends, $9,709.20, are named this way.

## What shipped

- **Migration `20260911212811_cashapp_match_rule_memo.sql`** — `cashapp_transactions.match_rule` check gains `memo`; column comment updated. Additive, idempotent.
- **Matcher rule (e) `memo`** in [`matchCashAppTransactions.ts`](../../src/lib/cashapp/matchCashAppTransactions.ts) (+2 tests): after rules a–c fail, a send matches a recorded payment for the same person within `memoWindowDays` (default 10 — memos are written when the report is paid, often days after the send) when a number in that payment's memo equals the send. `memoAmounts` reads dollar-looking numbers (`1,809.20`, `500`, `300 and 100`) and ignores Cash App ids. Each memo number backs one send, and the payment itself is **not** consumed, so `CashApp in 300 and 100` backs a $300 send and a $100 send, and the payment can still satisfy rule (b) for a third. `summarizeCashAppMatches.byRule` gains `memo`.
- The modal needs no change: `laneForMatchResult` passes the rule through and the summary chips already count by lane.

## Not done

Two memo numbers summing to one send (0 hits on the data) and payment ± the stub's Less lines (1 hit) were measured and left out.
