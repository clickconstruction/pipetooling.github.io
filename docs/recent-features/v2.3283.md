## Why

PR 1 found every promise-capture surface empty. The plan's rule: promises are captured where they already happen, not by asking the office to remember a form. Three capture points, in order of how little they cost anyone:

## What shipped

- **The customer names the date (portal).** [`PortalPromiseAsk`](../../src/components/portal/PortalPromiseAsk.tsx) sits under *Balance due* on the statement (screen only) once `promiseAskVisible` says so — money due and at least one open bill ≥ 7 days old (`PROMISE_ASK_MIN_BILL_AGE_DAYS`), so the first invoice asks for money, not a date. Three Friday chips (`promiseDateChoices`: this Friday or next when today is Friday/weekend, then +7, +21 days) or *Pick a date*, an optional note, one *Tell us*. Posts `submit-portal-request` `{ kind: 'payment_promise', date, note }`; the function validates with `promiseDateProblem` (today … +60 days), rate-limits 5 customer promises per customer per hour on `job_payment_promises`, resolves the link's job scope exactly like `customer-portal` (`openBillJobIds`), and calls the new service-role RPC `add_customer_payment_promise(customer, job_ids, date, note)` — one `source='customer'`, `channel='portal'`, `heard_by NULL` event per open-bill job the customer pays for (owner or GC), and the latest table upserted with `marked_by NULL`. No inbox row: the Billed row is where the office reads it. `customer-portal` returns `promise: { promisedYmd, source }` (latest `job_promised_pay_dates` across the open-bill jobs; `source='customer'` when the row is unmarked and its matching event is customer-sourced) so the strip reads *You told us to expect payment by Sep 12. Changed?* Sample mode (`token=sample`) shows the sent state without a network call. Shared rules live in [`_shared/portalPromise.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/portalPromise.ts), tested from `src/lib/portal/portalPromise.test.ts` (+8).
- **The chip says who.** `list_job_promised_pay_dates` (body lifted from the live 20260821110000 definition) now emits `markedByName = 'customer'` and `source` for portal self-promises (marked_by NULL + a matching customer-sourced event), so the Billed chip reads *✓ Promised Sep 12 · customer*; office marks keep the marker's name.
- **"They said…" (office).** `SetPromisedPayDateModal` gains *Who said it* (free text) and *How* (phone · text · email · in person) and writes through `add_job_payment_promise`; when PostgREST says the function is missing (`PGRST202`, the deploy window before the push) it falls back to `set_job_promised_pay_date`. The Billed row link reads **They said…** / **They said… (new date)**; the modal says a new date counts the earlier one as broken; *Clear date* keeps the promise on record and the toast says so.
- **The late-payment backfill.** `BilledPaymentConfirmationModal` takes optional `billedYmd` / `existingPromiseYmd`; when [`shouldAskPromiseBackfill`](../../src/lib/jobs/promiseBackfillPrompt.ts) says the payment is ≥ 14 days after the bill (`PROMISE_BACKFILL_MIN_DAYS_LATE`) and no promise is on the job, a *Did they promise a date for this?* block offers *No* (default), the paid-on day and the two Fridays before it (`promiseBackfillChoices`, all after the bill date), or *Another date…*. A chosen date is written after the payment succeeds via `add_job_payment_promise(…, channel 'backfill', note 'Recorded with the payment')`, best-effort — a missing RPC or a gate refusal never costs the payment. `JobsStagesTab` wires the invoice-mode opener (`billed_at` day, the row's promise) and reloads promises on success; the Dashboard and stepper openers pass nothing and never prompt. +6 kernel tests.
- Help guide `know-whether-a-customer-keeps-their-word` rewritten around the three capture points; `see-when-a-customer-will-pay` renamed the link. `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`: the portal RPC is service-role only. `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`: both function sections.
