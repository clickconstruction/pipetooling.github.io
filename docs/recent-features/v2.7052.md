# v2.7052 — Bills sent: a bill that never went out is shown, and older sends are on the list (2026-11-12)

Punch list #327, PR 2. PR 1 (v2.7049) drew the sends that kept a copy. The master's question, *did that bill go out*, has a second half: a bill that did **not** go out must be seen, and a send the app knows less about must still be on the list, saying what it lacks. Read-only on the live data, 24 open Stripe bills carry no send stamp at all.

## What changed

- **The kernel** (`src/lib/billing/billsSent.ts`, 25 tests in all). `billsSentLines` takes three more kinds of send, each a line with no copy and a note:
  - **A row of the send log** (`jobs_ledger_invoice_stripe_email_sends`) with no copy filed within five minutes of it for the same bill: an email before the copies began (*The address was not kept. The app kept no copy of a send before Oct 5, 2026.*) or one Stripe made for us (*No copy of this send was kept, so the address is not on record.*). A row whose copy is on the page is the same send and is not drawn twice. The copy list on the row shows as *copy to*.
  - **A stamp** on a bill entered from Housecall Pro, or a PDF emailed before the copies began: the day only (`dayOnly`, the line reads *Day only*), with no address, no sender and no copy. A stamp is left out when a copy of that bill is the record.
  - **A text with the pay link** (`messages`): the number, who sent it and Quo's answer (*Delivered*, *Not delivered*). Its bill is the one the link opens, by id or by the bill's short code (`payLinkRefInText` over the pay page's own `payLinkRef`).
  - Which send of a bill a line is now counts every one of these, so a text after an email reads *2nd send*.
  - A line with no copy takes its bill's number from any copy of the same bill on the page.
- **The gaps**, `billsSentGaps`: a Stripe bill, billed or paid, live, with no send stamp, and no line of any kind on the list. Open ones apart from paid ones, newest first. `billsSentGapFilter` is the search over them.
- **The reads** (`billsSentIo.ts`): four more, each best effort, capped with the page's limit. The read of other sends of the page's bills now runs only when the page is full.
- **The screen**: a yellow strip above the days, *N bills are billed with no send on record*, five rows then **Show all N**, the paid ones behind **Show them**; an amber tile with the open count; the chips **Text**, **Housecall Pro** and **No send on record** (the strip alone). A route chip hides the strip. The foot line says a send before Oct 5, 2026 has its time and no address.
- **The guide** gains *Bills with no send on record*, *Sends that kept no copy* and *What the list cannot know*.

## Not changed

- Nothing is written. No table, policy or function.
- A bill handed over on site or emailed from a person's own mailbox still leaves no record: it stays in the strip until it is paid. Recording how such a bill went is PR 6.

## Checked

Walked on the live data with every write refused: 349 lines under 101 days (181 emails, 143 Housecall Pro days, 7 texts, 17 statements), 128 lines saying the address was not kept, the strip with 24 open bills and 58 paid behind the press, nothing past the edge at 375, 768 and 1400 wide.
