## Read closer than the card

- **The gate on the author is the signed estimate pointing at the job, not the activity row**: `job_auto_created_from_estimate` is best-effort telemetry inside an exception block and may be missing; `estimates.job_ledger_id` is what the create writes and what Edit Job's *Source estimate* banner reads, so the predicate and the chip read the same fact.
- **The office's Create job on a signed estimate counts too**: the card's words were the auto-create, but the two leave the same mark and the estimator sold the job either way. A draft or a sent estimate linked by hand is not a sale, so `customer_accepted` is required.
- **An estimator still reads no `jobs_ledger` row**, so the surfaces that draw the chip are the office's and a primary's; the estimator's own view of the job stays the money line under the won bid, which now draws for a job from their estimate as well — once `list_bid_money_strip` is reached from a bid, which a job from an estimate with no bid has none of. The Money lens and the Dashboard card read `list_my_money_strip`, whose own leg is the bids a person estimated and the jobs they account-manage; a job from an estimate with no bid joins neither list. The card's follow-ups carry that.
