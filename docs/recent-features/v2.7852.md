# v2.7852 — Lien desk: the affidavits' To send bin counts what its press sends (2026-11-18)

Found on the live look at punch list #383 on 2026-11-18, and built on the owner's word the same day: *"Yes, build them"*. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## Before

- The affidavits' **To send for Master approval** bin has the press *Send N for approval ›* (v2.7714). N is the send plan's count: the papers in the pile with a row a person made, by opening and reading the paper.
- The bin counted every paper in the pile. On the live desk it read 5 beside *Send 1 for approval ›*, and nothing said why.
- That morning the owner picked the rule for a held paper (v2.7783): it stands in its bin with a mark, and *a bin counts what can move*, so the count is the press's own number.

## What changed

- **One test for the send and for the bin.** `lienAffidavitSendReady` in `lienDeskAffidavits.ts` says whether the batch send takes a paper. `lienAffidavitSendPlan` uses it, and `buildLienBoard` puts `unread` on an affidavit's chip in *To send* when it fails.
- **The bin counts what the press sends.** `lienBoardBinCount` leaves an unread paper out, as it leaves a held one out.
- **An unread paper stands after the ones the send takes**, and before any held one (`lienBoardChipRank`), with a little air before the first.
- **Its chip has a dashed edge and a ○ mark** (`data-unread`, `data-lien-board-unread-mark`). Its colour is still its day's. Its hover says *nobody has read it yet*, and its card reads *To send for Master approval · not read yet* (`lienBoardUnreadWords`, `lienBoardUnreadBinWords`).
- A press on the chip opens the paper, as before. Reading it makes its row, and the send then takes it.

## What it does not do

- The notices' *To send for Master approval* is unchanged: the Board has no batch send for notices, so nothing there disagrees.
- The tiles, a chip's stage, its stages left and the at-risk rule are unchanged. The first tile still counts every paper to send.
- A new mark was chosen without a drawing. ○ is the mark the desk already uses for a step not done yet. It is one glyph in `LienBoard.tsx` if the owner wants another.
