# v2.8645 — Settings: a kept map address that cannot be cleared says the reason that holds (2026-11-26)

Punch list #443, finding 253 of the walking thread's sitting 25 (2026-11-26), briefed by the fixing thread as `443-kept-address-with-no-number-says-too-short.md`. Client only. No migration, no edge function. Nothing owed after the merge.

**Read.** Settings → Data & recovery → **Kept map addresses (dev)**. A walk left one miss on prod whose made-up street line is 39 characters with no number in it. Under it the block read *This address is too short to tell apart from the others. It stays.* and **Clear…** was dead. The line is not short. In `src/lib/map/keptAddressClear.ts`, `keptAddressLikePattern` gives no pattern for a street line under six characters, or one with no letter, or one with no digit, and `keptAddressClearProblem` had one sentence for all three.

**The mend: the words only.** A new `keptAddressUncheckedWhy(key)` says the reason that holds, and `keptAddressClearProblem` answers with it:

- Under six characters, as before: *This address is too short to tell apart from the others. It stays.*
- No digit: *This address has no street number, so it cannot be told apart from the others. It stays.*
- No letter: *This address has no street name, so it cannot be told apart from the others. It stays.*

A short line is said to be short even when it also lacks a number, since that is the first thing wrong with it.

## What did not change

- **The rule.** A line with no number or no name matches loosely, and a loose match must never clear an address in use. Such an address still stays, its **Clear…** still dead. Whether a long line with no number may be cleared when nothing holds its words is a call, not a fix, and is not in this change.
- `keptAddressLikePattern`, the search, the confirm and what a clear deletes.
- No guide: the block is a dev's alone and no guide names it.

## Tests

- `keptAddressClear.test.ts`: each of the three reasons; a 39-character line with no number reads *no street number* through both functions; a line that can be checked has no such reason.
- `KeptMapAddressesBlock.render.test.tsx`: a long miss with no number draws the new sentence under its row with **Clear the miss** dead.
- `keptAddressClearIo.test.ts` still refuses a short address with the old words.
