# v2.6840 — The Bridge is one screen, with the rest behind four doors (punch list #303, PR 2) (2026-11-10)

PR 2 of the Bridge's refresh under one story (`to-dos/bridge-one-story/README.md`). The owner's yes to the six cuts, 2026-11-10. Client only. No migration, no edge function.

PR 1 drew the first screen above the page as it was, so the page was still 13.7 phone screens under it. This PR makes the first screen the page. At 1440 × 900 with no door open the page is 203 words and 20 numbers, against 2,597 and 414. On a phone the doors are on the first screen.

## The doors

- **The kernel** (`src/lib/bridge/doors.ts`): four doors, one open at a time, kept in the address as `?door=flow|money|people|soft` so a link can open one. `doorFromSearch` reads it, `searchAfterDoor` writes it (a press on the open door closes it, and a link that opens a door never closes it), `doorsToDraw` leaves Soft numbers out when nothing is soft. A stale `?view=monday` is dropped on the way.
- **The row** (`BridgeDoors.tsx`): the presses under the first screen, each `aria-expanded`. They left `BridgeWeek.tsx`, so the row draws even when the calls cannot be read; the first screen then says why in one line.
- **What sits where** (`Bridge.tsx`):
  - **The flow**: the rule, the five stage cards and their desks, the dial, the three resources, the link's desk, the usual fixes beside last week's call, the weekly email's switch.
  - **Money**: the cash forecast with the bank's accounts, bills and receipts, net position, money owed and its panel, the eight week chart with its two lights, the line to the guide.
  - **People**: the strip with Vectors and the day grid, always open there, and crew hours with its panel. The day grid reads its month once the door is open.
  - **Soft numbers**: the one list, and the percents prompt under it.
- *Pick another step* opens The flow. The sentence under the cash tile that names a left-out account opens Money at the bank's accounts. *Go to this week's call* on the link's desk goes back up to the card.

## The six cuts

1. **The Daily profit rate card** is gone. It showed a loss beside a headline that showed a gain. Its panel stays, as a dig-in (below).
2. **Four of the five lists.** `softNumbers.ts` and `BridgeSoftNumbers.tsx` draw one list of what makes the reading unsure, each row saying what it moves and ending in its door. The rows, their order and their doors are `buildReadingGaps`'s, so the count on the first screen is unchanged; the words are new and held to `plainWordsFailures`. *Fix the reading* and *This week* left the page (`BridgeReadingGaps.tsx`, `BridgeThisWeek.tsx` removed; `thisWeek.ts` stays, since the weekly mail's sheet and the call's doors read it). The moves on the link's desk are the call card. The usual fixes stay with the dial, and carry *This week*'s sizes (below).
3. **The truth check** opens with the net position chart's *break it down*, under the waterfall.
4. **Bills and receipts** show the next fourteen days (`moneyLists.ts`: `listWindow`, `laterPress`). One press under either list shows the later days and their dollars, and takes them away again.
5. **The owner box on every move.** The link's desk keeps what the step is, why it matters, how it is read and today's reading. The owner box on each move and the desk's record press are gone: the call is made on the first screen's card, which proposes the pressed step with a person on each move. The moves and the note stay, as dig-ins (below).
6. **The Monday sheet as a view.** The header's button, `?view=monday`, *Email me this sheet* and `BridgeMondaySheet.tsx` are gone. The weekly email's switch moved to the foot of The flow with words that no longer point at a sheet on the page (`WEEKLY_EMAIL_WORDS`; `weeklySend.ts` is copied to the edge functions and is untouched). The header chip went too: the card under it says the same thing.

## What the cuts alone showed, kept behind a press

The owner, on the six cuts, 2026-11-10: they "had the ability to give us clarity on specific things", so "find a way to add them back … behind buttons or behind views". Three of the six were already one press away (the truth check, the later bills and receipts, the soft numbers). Four things were not, and he said yes to all four from the drawing (`to-dos/bridge-one-story/dig-ins.html`). Each opens from the number it explains.

- **What is left after overhead** (`profitPanel.ts`, `BridgeProfitPanel.tsx`), behind Money: one press under the eight week chart's lights. Closed it adds five words and no number. Opened, the warning stands first while hours wait (`warn`: *Reads low for now. 151 hours still wait on approval. With them it reads about $1,462 a day.*), then what a day keeps (`left`), where a day's money goes, break-even, earned against costs by pay week, and the sources behind one more press. The reading is the old panel's, on approved hours; `warn` says what it would read with the hours waiting.
- **What a fix is worth** (`fixSizes.ts`), behind The flow: *Add a field day* and *Cut overhead* on the usual fixes carry their dollars a day, and **Bids due** under them lists the first two bids due with what a win is worth and a door to Bids. The sums are `buildThisWeek`'s, held equal by a test. The two fixes the app cannot size stay bare, and the block is absent when no bid is due.
- **Every move for a step** (`deskMoves`, `deskMovesFoot` in `weekCall.ts`), on the link's desk: one press lists every move the step has, each with today's number and *on the call* or *not on it*. It is a list to read; nothing in it records.
- **The card slides, and takes a note** (`movesShown`, `BridgeWeek.tsx`): a step's proposal carries every move it has, and the card shows three still on the call, two on a phone. Take one off and the next comes on; the one taken off stays, greyed, with its way back. *Add a note ›* opens one line, kept on the call (`bridge_calls.note`) and shown under it once the call stands.
