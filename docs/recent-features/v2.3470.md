## Why

Owner, 2026-09-14: "If I just learned a GC has been taking from Peter to pay Paul … I would like to send notices of intent to lien to all of his clients. How could I add a button to help do this in the app?" Texas has no separate notice of intent to lien — the § 53.056 notice the Lien desk already sends is that document, and its teeth are fund trapping (§ 53.081). The desk sends one month at a time, thirty days ahead; a GC in trouble needs every job with unpaid work and every unnoticed month, today, with one approval — and the owners found first.
