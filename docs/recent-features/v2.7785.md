# v2.7785 — Lien desk: a stop's window takes you to what a copy is missing (2026-11-18)

The owner's ask of 2026-11-18, on the stop window open at *§ 53.055 serve*, with a box drawn at the right of the owner's *No receipt yet* line: "an action button here where someone can be brought to where they can enter the owner of record where it opens the page scrolls to where they need to go, and for a brief moment highlights the section they need to enter". The window's *What went out* was read-only, so a reader who saw what was missing had to find the right screen alone.

**Which reading.** The card named *Owner of record* is the owner's copy of the paper (`LIEN_SEND_RECIPIENT_WORDS.owner`), and its two lines are that copy's mailing and its receipt. So the press goes to where the copy is mailed and its number and receipt are added. The other reading, that the owner is missing from the job, is one state of the same card: when the property record has no owner yet, the press reads **Enter the owner of record ›** and opens that record at the owner's line.

**The rule (`src/lib/jobs/lienMailProofDoor.ts`, pure).** One card lacks at most one thing at a time, in this order, and gets the press for it:

| The copy | The press | Where it opens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| not mailed, no owner on the property record (the owner's card only) | **Enter the owner of record ›** | the property record, at *Owner of record name* |
| not mailed, the owner has no mailing address | **Enter the owner’s mailing address ›** | the property record, at *Owner mailing address* |
| not mailed | **Mail this copy ›** | *Filed liens: copies to mail*, at the job's line for that copy |
| mailed, no mail number, and one is owed | **Enter the mail number ›** | a filed lien not yet served: the same window. Otherwise the job's Lien window |
| a number and no receipt | **Add the receipt ›** | the same two places |
| number and receipt on file, or a copy that owes none (email, by hand, a mail provider) | no press | |

`LienMailProofEnvelope` gained `wantsNumber` (`lienMailProof.ts`): a mailed copy that went by tracked post with no number typed and none coming from a provider. A receipt with no number is read as a number still owed.

**Where the Lien window lands (`lienMailProofWindowSpot`).** An affidavit's mailing and its mail number open the service step (**Record service…**, or **Save the mail receipts** once the day is on record) at that copy's fields. A notice's number lands on the paper's row under *Filings on this job*. A receipt lands on the copy's line in that row's *What went out*, with the cursor on **Add the receipt**. The desk opens the window through a new `onOpenLienProof` on `LienDesk`, which `LienDeskPage` answers with `land` on `LienInstrumentsModal`.

**The stop window steps aside.** The press closes the stop window as the place opens. *Filed liens: copies to mail* and the property record open over the desk, and the Lien window opens over it too, so closing any of them returns to the job's paper with its path, where the stop window was opened.

**Who gets it.** The office (`isLienOffice`: dev, master, assistant, controller), who do those acts. `lienMailProofDoorPress` takes one opener per place, and a place with no opener is closed: anyone else sees the card with no press, never a dead one. The copies window is offered only for a lien that is in it. In the job's own Lien window the press lands inside that window, and the owner's gap is not read there, since its service step already names a missing address under its print press.

**The light (`src/lib/landOn.ts`, `[data-landed]` in `index.css`).** One shared piece: `landOn(el)` scrolls the place to the middle of its scroller and marks it, and `landOnWhenThere(find)` waits up to eight seconds for a place that is drawn after the press. The mark is an amber tint and a ring from theme tokens, held 1.5 s and faded over 0.6 s. Under reduced motion nothing glides and nothing fades: the place is in view at once and a steady outline stands for 1.5 s. The property record's *open at the missing line* (`LienPaperPropertyWindow`) now uses it too, so every door into that window scrolls to its field and lights it, where before it only took the cursor.

**A bug the smoke caught.** `LienFilingTabs` closes any open record step when its tab or the property's county arrives. The landing ran first and its service step was closed in the same pass. The landing effect now stands after that one, and a county read late within eight seconds opens and lights the step once more.
