# v2.7283 — The job window on a phone: the Edit tab as grouped rows (2026-11-14)

Punch list #355, PR E ([`to-dos/job-window-phone.md`](../../to-dos/job-window-phone.md), pass 3; the mock-up [`to-dos/job-window-phone-tabs.html`](../../to-dos/job-window-phone-tabs.html)). Client only: no migration, no edge function, and no kernel that is copied to the edge functions changed.

## Before

Measured at 375 px, Edit ran 3.3 screens with 40 presses under 32 px: the Stages card and the status rail at desk width, then sixteen fact rows each with a 24 px pencil and nothing grouping them. Every field sat at 14 px, so an iPhone zoomed the page on each tap.

## What changed

- **`JobFormFactRow.tsx`**: on a phone (`useNarrowViewport640`) a row stands 44 px with larger words, the whole row is the press as before, and a chevron stands where the pencil was (a chevron up while the editor is open); `data-fact-row` names each row.
- **`JobFormEditFactRows.tsx`**: on a phone the rows read under five group titles, `FactGroupTitle`, in today's order so the desk's rows and the phone's are the same rows with their open state: **The people** (account man, team), **The folders**, **The customer** (customer, phone, email, bills go to, bills also go to, contract, sub work order, property record, date met), **The GC and the site** (GC/builder with its sub rows, development, project), **The paper** (plans, bid), then Charging the customer as before.
- **`JobFormStagesGroup.tsx`**: on a phone a stage row stands 44 px, the eye switch 40 × 32, and See it as the customer and Set stages on Bill → are 40 px presses.
- **`JobFormModal.tsx`**: the status rail's wrapper is `display: none` on a phone in the embedded form (the bar's Status ▾ opens the same `JobStatusStepper`); the embedded form's root carries `data-job-form-embedded`, and a style at 640 px and under sets every text field, select and textarea to 16 px, so Bill and Costs gain it too.
- `JobWindowModal.phoneSections.render.test.tsx` opens Edit at 375 px and reads the five titles, a 44 px row and the hidden rail.

## Is this the best we can do?

The groups follow today's order rather than the drawing's (which put contract and sub work order under The paper and the customer under The people): reordering would move the editors and their open-row state, and the rows are the same rows the office knows from the desk. The titles give the phone what it lacked, a place to stop the eye. What is still not done: the identity fields (C#, service type, name, kind, address) keep the desk's layout, which fits at 375 px.

## Verify

At 375 px open a job, press Edit: the stages are 44 px rows with a thumb-sized eye; no status rail; the rows read under The people, The folders, The customer, The GC and the site, The paper, each 44 px with a chevron; tap a field and the page does not zoom. At a desk nothing moves.
