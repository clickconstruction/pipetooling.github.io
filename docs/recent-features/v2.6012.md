# v2.6012 — A missed call gets a text back from the app (2026-10-28)

Punch list #195 PR 1 (`to-dos/missed-call-text/README.md`, the owner's pick B of #186's item 3, parked 2026-10-26 and given the go 2026-10-28). When nobody picks up a call to one of our numbers, `quo-webhook` texts the caller back from that number with the office's words, through every gate the app's texts already have, and the line is the app's own: it neither clears Needs a reply nor starts Waiting on them. The owner's two answers of 2026-10-28: every number that is a phone gets the text (a new customer is the point; a number that sent STOP or a prospect who said no is skipped), and the to-do's three lines ship as the defaults.

## What changed

- **Migration** [`20261028150000_missed_call_reply.sql`](../migrations/20261028150000_missed_call_reply.md): `messages.auto`; `messages_move_state` skips an auto line and a later one never hides theirs; `quo_write_line` carries the column; the `app_settings` row `messages_missed_call_reply_v1` (off as it ships, the three lines) with its UPDATE policy for `is_master_or_dev()`; the table `missed_call_replies`, one row per call keyed by the call's Quo id, with the three fence calls.
- **Kernel** [`src/lib/quo/missedReply.ts`](../../src/lib/quo/missedReply.ts), mirrored to the edge: `parseMissedReply` / `serializeMissedReply` for the row, `missedCall` for a call nobody answered, and `planMissedReply`, one answer from the call's line, the setting, the office hours (`hasRule.ts`'s `officeOpen`), Send texts, our own number, the number's STOP, a prospect's *May we text?* (`textingRule.ts`), our last text to the number (three hours, `MISSED_REPLY_HOLD_MS`) and the pause: the words to send with which line, or the hold in a word and in words (`holdWords`). `textedBackLine` words the New lane's quiet line.
- **`send.ts`**: the purpose `missed`, which the Send texts switch gates (`textsSwitch.ts`'s `switchGates`); its line reads *The app texted back · from the company number* (`sentWho`) and carries `auto: true` (`SentLine`, `QuoLine`, `rows.ts`'s columns and read), kept over Quo's delivery event (`sendOver`, `mergeSent`).
- **`quo-webhook`**: after the call's line, on `call.completed` of a call in nobody answered, while the card is on: the call is claimed in `missed_call_replies` (a repeat or Quo's retry does nothing), the gates are read (`loadSettingText`, `loadProspectTexting`, `lastOurTextAt`, `textsSwitchOn` in `_shared/quoLines.ts`) and the text goes through `sendText`, or the reason is written on the claim. A failure there is written on the claim and never fails the delivery.
- **The client reads the mark**: `ThreadMessage.auto` (`load.ts`, `MESSAGE_COLS`), `lastOut` and the thread's court skip it (*Our move* stays); Follow up reads the app's texts back to open conversations and the New lane's row says *The app texted back at 4:12 PM. The row still waits on you.* (`newLane.ts`'s `autoLine`, `FollowUpSections.tsx`); *Who has it?* is never answered by it; Waiting on them leaves auto lines out of its read; the reply push's `answeredBefore` ignores them, so the office still hears about the call (`notifyRule.ts`, `messagesReplyPush.ts`).
- **Tests**: `missedReply.test.ts` (the setting, the gates in order, the hours, plain words), `missedReply.run.test.ts` (the webhook end to end on a fake database: the text goes once, a retry sends nothing, the card off claims nothing, Send texts off writes the reason), and the `auto` mark through `send`, `rows`, `events`, `textsSwitch`, `thread`, `notifyRule` and `newLane`. The SQL beds `messages-follow-up` (the trigger's clause) and `quo-messages` (the write, the row and its policy).
- **Docs**: `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` quo-webhook; the migration doc; this fragment. PR 2 brings the card, the guide and `MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md`.

## How to verify

On a ZZ test contact whose number is a dev's own phone, with Send texts on and the setting's `on` true (a dev sets it until PR 2's card: `UPDATE app_settings SET value_text = jsonb_set(value_text::jsonb, '{on}', 'true')::text WHERE key = 'messages_missed_call_reply_v1'` from the dashboard as the owner, or the card once it ships): call the company number and let it ring out. The phone gets the in-hours line within a minute. The thread shows the missed call and under it *The app texted back · from the company number*. Needs a reply still holds the row with *Our move*, and Waiting on them does not list them. Call again within three hours: no second text, and `missed_call_replies` holds the second call with `reason` `recent-text`. Leave a voicemail on a third call after three hours: the voicemail line. Turn Send texts off and call: nothing goes, the row says `texts-off`. Nothing is pressed on a real customer.
