# v2.8414 — Search: the box at the top of the app finds supply house bills (2026-11-23)

Punch list #431, PR 7 of seven, the last. The header search found jobs, bids, estimates, customers, pages and guides. It now finds supply house bills for the roles that read them. No migration and no edge function.

## What changed

- **Bills are a kind of row** in the header search (`HeaderGlobalSearch.tsx`). The office's search asks `search_supply_bills` with the same words, read by the same kernel as the box on Materials → Supply houses (`billSearchArgs`), so a number, a house, a part, a day and a month mean the same in both.
- **Where they sit**: after the jobs, bids, estimates and customers, so each of those keeps the place it had, and before the pages and guides. The arrow keys walk them with the rest.
- **What a row shows** (`HeaderBillResultRow`): a *Bill* tag, the invoice number, the supply house, the amount with small cents, the day with no year this year, and *On the paper:* with the line or the words that found it.
- **A press opens the bill's own page**, `/files/bills/<invoice id>`, the bill viewer.
- **Five at most** (`HEADER_BILL_MAX`). The box on Materials lists sixty.
- **Who is asked for**: the four roles that read bills (`canShareSupplyInvoice`). The field lookup and the roles that get the doors alone never ask. The function runs as its caller, so it decides again.
- **A bill search that fails leaves the box as it was.** The other rows are drawn, and the failure is logged. Bills are one kind among five, and the box's own *did not read* line is kept for the kinds it always had.
- The header's result type stays as it was for the ten other pickers that share it. The header keeps a wider type of its own.

## Tests

- The header's four other suites (20 tests) and `billSearch.test.ts` (10) still pass.
