# v2.8469 — Document builder: the Sub NDA's title, job, company and party can be typed on (2026-11-24)

Punch list #416, part 3, the second of two PRs for *The rest of the Sub NDA's words as fields*. The first is v2.8460, the words the flow already drew. Client only: no migration, no edge function, and no new key in what the database holds. It is cut on top of v2.8468, the Edit / Share switch, since the card sits on the Edit side.

## What was there

A paper holds four names of its own: `title`, `job`, `company` and `protectedParty`. No step of the flow shows them. The tab's line under an open builder reads three of them, and the dev seat's paper-words verbs can load them. Nothing in the app let a person type one.

## What changed

- **Paths.** `paper.title`, `paper.job`, `paper.company` and `paper.protectedParty` (`PAPER_NAME_KEYS`, `paperEdit.ts`). `paperWordsAt` reads them and `withPaperWords` writes them.
- **A page of their own**, `paper`, named *The paper's names*. It is a page of the paper and of no step of the flow, so `paperPages` does not list it and `changedPages` puts it first when a name changed. `revertPage` puts the four back together.
- **The card.** At the foot of the Edit side, **The paper's names**: one fact a line, the label on the left and the name on the right (`ValueLines`), each name a `BuilderField` (`paperNames.ts`, `WalkedBuilder.tsx`). The field is the builder's own, with its mark and its save a moment after the typing rests, and not the app's `ChangeableValue` with **Keep** and **Cancel**: every other word on the builder is typed in place, and a name should not work another way on the same screen.
- **With the marks off** the names read as plain words, as every other word on the builder does.
- **The changed row has no press that opens nothing.** `changedRows` marks the names' row `opens: false`, and the row reads *Typed on the card below.* where **Open it** would be. **Back to revision N** is still there.
- **An empty name holds the publish.** `paperDefProblems` lists *The paper has no title.*, *The paper names no job.*, *The paper names no company.* or *The paper does not say who it protects.* A draft with an empty name still opens, since only its shape is read to open it.
- **A name is not a line.** The plain-words rules still do not hold the four, as `definition.ts` already said.
- **Docs and help**: the glossary's *Document builder* entry, and *use the document builder* under *Change the Sub NDA's words*. The glossary sentence v2.8460 wrote, that the four names are not fields, is cut down to the levels' names.

## Not changed, and why

- **A line that names the company keeps its own words.** The first draft's lines say *Acme Plumbing* in so many words, which the walk of 2026-11-23 noted. Typing the company's name on the card does not rewrite them. Doing so would mean a blank in every line, which is a new shape for a line and a call nobody has made. The card says so in its last line.
- **The levels' names** stay as they are, by #404.
- **The tab's line under the builder** still reads the title, the job and the party from the saved draft, so it follows a typed name once the save lands.
