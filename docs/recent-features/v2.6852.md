# v2.6852 — On site on the phone: find a part on any job, scan a label, and print where you stand (punch list #304) (2026-11-10)

The second half of card #304. The crew never opens the Materials page, so the phone's On site tab gets the same answers where they already are. Three things were wrong there: the search read *Find a part on this job* and stopped at that job; a job with no box read *No containers on this site yet*, as if none existed anywhere; and a box's **Print the label** and **Print the manifest** opened `/jobs?tab=materials`, which only the office may open, so a helper, a subcontractor, an estimator, a primary and a superintendent were sent away without a print.

## What changed

- **`TallyOnSiteTab`**: a row under the search with **This job · Every job** and **Scan a label**. Every job runs `findOnSite` over the desk load (`useOnSiteDeskJobs`, handed the Tally's own job list so a field role reads its jobs by name) and draws the other jobs' hits under each job's name; a press opens that job on that box. The desk loads only when it is asked for: Every job pressed, or a job with no box. A search with no hit on this job offers **Look on every job**.
- **An empty job** lists the other jobs that hold a box (`otherJobsWithBoxes`), each a press, above the old **Add a container** line.
- **Prints in place**: the two presses open `LabelsPrintModal` and `ManifestPrintModal` in the tab, on the rows it already holds, for every role. This is the card's call 3 taken as its pick: a label and a manifest show no money.
- **Scan a label** opens `ScanLabelWindow` (v2.6851) and its code goes to `/b/<code>`.
- Only jobs the Tally's list holds are offered, so a press on another job's box always lands.
