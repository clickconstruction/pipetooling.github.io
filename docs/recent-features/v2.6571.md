# v2.6571 — The stage checklist files itself as a record: the number, the paper, the PDF in Drive, Amend, Print (punch list #237, PR 3) (2026-11-06)

Punch list #237's third PR (`to-dos/stage-checklist-document/`, *A checklist is a record on the job, not a setting*). PR 1 (v2.6328) and PR 1b (v2.6347) put the checks on the job, PR 2 (v2.6563) the signature. This PR makes the finished list a document in the signed hours statement's shape, by the owner's two defaults: it files itself at the last answer, and each version keeps its own PDF.

## What changed

- **`stage_checklist_records`** (migration `20261106050000`, `docs/migrations/20261106050000_stage_checklist_records.md`): one row per job stage and version, `record_id` `CL-<job number>-<n>` counted per job, the stage, `state` (`filed` · `amended`), `filed_at` the last answer's stamp, a `snapshot` of the rows and the skipped questions as they stood, the counts, the amend stamp and reason, and the PDF's name, Drive id and standing.
- **It files itself.** `stage_checklist_record_settle(fixture)` is the SQL twin of `questionWaitsOn`: the company's live questions less the ones skipped on the stage plus the stage's own items, each answered with its pick, the signature where asked and the photo where asked unless Not here. Triggers on the answers, the questions and the skips call it after every write, so the sheet, the Pipeline door, a report photo and a signature that waited on the phone all file the record. Complete and no filed record → a new version with the next number; a filed stage made incomplete (a check added, a question skipped, an answer taken back, a company question added) → the filed record reads `amended` and the stage opens again.
- **The sheet as paper** (`FiledPaper` in `StageChecklistSheet.tsx`): while a stage's latest record is filed the sheet draws the record instead of the rows, light paper whatever the theme: *Record CL-1042-1 · v1 · Oct 15 · 4:02 pm · 4 answers, 1 signed · PDF in the job's Drive folder* with **Open in Drive**, every row a statement from the snapshot with the mark where it was signed, the company questions left off this job, the foot *Trim Set now reads ready on the Pipeline. One check reads No; the office acts on that.*, and *Filed. Nothing here changes. The office can amend it as a new version.* over Close. The record that appears with the press just made opens with *Filed as record CL-1042-1 with the last answer.* An amended stage reads open again with *Amending v1* under the count.
- **The Documents read-out** (`JobDocumentsChecklists.tsx`): a filed stage's chip reads *Filed · CL-1042-1* (green), the line under the name the record's version, time, counts and the PDF's standing with **Open in Drive ↗**, a stage with more than one version a *Versions:* line naming each with who amended it and why, and **Amend** (two presses, a reason of three characters or more, through `amend_stage_checklist_record`) for whoever authors on the job. A stage open after a filing reads *Amending v1 · 3 of 6* (amber).
- **Print the checklists** beside the section's heading: `buildChecklistPaperHtml` draws the job's stages on light Letter paper through `openHtmlPrintWindow`, a filed stage from its record and an open one as it stands with *open* where there is no answer, a signed row's name in the signature face.
- **The PDF** (`_shared/stageChecklistPdf.ts`, `photo-save`'s `sweepRecords`): the sweep draws each new record from its snapshot with pdf-lib, the drawn marks read from the checklist bucket and embedded, the typed ones in italic, and files it in the job's photo folder as *CL-1042-1 Trim Set checklist.pdf* (a free name when the folder holds one), five tries at most with the reason on the row; the cron runs while a record's PDF waits. The `photo-save` function is deployed after the push.
- The hook `useJobStageChecklists` reads the records with the rest and reads them again after every press that lands, so the sheet turns to paper and the chip reads *Filed* with the press.
- The guide *read a filed stage checklist*; lines in *fill a stage checklist on the phone*, *add a check to a job's stage checklist* and *sign a checklist answer*; `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`'s `photo-save` section; the docs; the card.

## Decisions

- **The database files, not the client.** The spike filed from the store on the phone. A client-side filing misses a report photo's answer and a signature landed from the outbox, and two readers could race for the number. The rule is written twice, in SQL and in the kernel, and the migration doc says so; a bed for the SQL side is the next session's.
- **Amend is a state, not a copy.** The card asked for "a new version n+1 with version n readable and printable": the filed row keeps its snapshot and reads `amended`, and the next completion inserts the next row. No history table.
- **The filed stage keeps its presses on Documents.** Take off, skip and the company's list still work on a filed stage; each one amends the record by itself, which is the design ("adding a check or taking an answer back starts version n+1"), so the office is never stopped by a filed list. The phone sheet, which the crew reads, has nothing to press on the paper.
- **The PDF lands in the photo folder.** The card says "the job's Drive folder by the sweep that already files checklist photos"; the photo folder is the one that sweep knows, and the job's folder tree places it under the job.
- **Nothing is backfilled at the push.** A stage already complete files on its next write, since the filing time is the last answer's stamp and a record no one pressed for should not appear with a stale time.
- **Not in this PR**: the filed No on the Pipeline's mark (the walks' wish; the mark reads ready, and the paper's foot and the read-out carry the No) and the PDF riding the GC portal and the pay application packet (*Is that the best we can do?*).
