# v2.8257 — Dev seat: a paper's words can be loaded as a draft (2026-11-21)

Taunya, 2026-11-21, asked whether a dev verb should load approved words into the app. The Sub NDA has over a hundred fields, and typing them by hand invites mistakes. `dev-mcp` server 0.125.0, one new pair: `plan_paper_words` → `apply_paper_words`. No migration and no RPC: both rows are ones the app writes under RLS, so the seat writes them as the dev. It takes a deploy of `dev-mcp`.

## Two places the words can land

- **A builder's draft** (`into: "builder"`). `document_builders.draft` is replaced whole, on the draft number the plan read. That is the tab's own save, so a draft someone saved in the tab since the plan matches nothing and nothing is written. A paper someone walks is held to its title, its levels and a line for every level of every topic, so the seat cannot load a draft the tab would refuse to draw.
- **One new Contract Book entry** (`into: "book"`). One `contract_template_documents` row in a packet named by its name or id, with the columns the Book's **Add** writes. Markdown or plain words only: the seat cannot run the app's HTML cleaner.

## What it never does

- It never publishes a builder or marks a revision as read. Those stay presses in the app.
- It never changes a Book entry that exists. Saving one in the app also opens a new version for everyone who holds that paper, and the seat does not do that on a session's say.
- It sends nothing, and assigns nobody a paper.
- **It refuses the entry name `Master Services Agreement`.** A Book entry has no draft state. GC mode sends the entry with that exact name as a company's master agreement (`GC_MASTER_AGREEMENT_TITLE`), so making one is turning a send on. The words load under another name, and the rename is a press in the Contract Book. A test holds the seat's list to the app's own constant.

## The plan and the log never repeat the words

The plan counts a draft's changed, new and removed lines and shows the first twelve, each cut at 140 characters, with what a packet already holds for a Book entry. The call log keeps where the words went and how many characters, and the plan hash. The words themselves are in neither.

## What the app can and cannot hold

Found while building, and worth knowing before the Sub NDA's words are loaded:

- **A builder holds** each level's line, its breach lines, its site lines and a blank's value, with the disclaimer, the site page and the flow's own words.
- **A builder holds no clause wording.** There is no field for an agreement's own sections: who it is between, what is secret, what follows a breach, the signatures. On the flow those are drawn as grey bars. The cards say that wording belongs in the Contract Book.
- So a paper like the Sub NDA loads in two parts: its topics and levels into the builder's draft, and its agreement text into the Book as an entry.

## Tests

`devMcpPaperWords.test.ts`, 24 tests on made-up words: the input, the refused name, the line-by-line difference, a walked paper's shape (the made-up Sub NDA passes both the seat's check and the tab's own `parsePaperDef`), the reads over a fake reader, the plan, the hash, the row a Book entry lands as against the Book's own insert, and the log. The pins of the server's version and verb counts moved across about fifty test files.

No handler-run test: the case is held by source pins here and proven on prod after the deploy, on a test target that is put back.

## Left

- **Changing a Book entry that exists.** Load under a new name, or change it in the Book.
- **Deleting a Book entry.** A press in the Book.
- **A clause-built paper** (`contract_clauses`). The seat loads a text entry, not clauses.
