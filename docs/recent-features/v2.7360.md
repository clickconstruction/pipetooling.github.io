# v2.7360 — GC difficulty rating, part 6: the site is picked on the bid, and the GC's record shows what the rating did (2026-11-15)

Punch list #357, PR 6 of six, the last of the train ([`to-dos/gc-difficulty-rating/README.md`](../../to-dos/gc-difficulty-rating/README.md)). One migration (`20261116030000_difficulty_site_scale.sql`, [its fragment](../migrations/20261116030000_difficulty_site_scale.md)), no edge function. PR 1 was v2.7275 (the criteria), PR 2 v2.7279 (the rating), PR 3 v2.7296 (Paying from the bills), PR 4 v2.7301 (the price list and the lines), PR 5 v2.7358 (the master's sheet and the ask).

## What changed

- **Criteria version 2**, published by the migration: version 1's three scales and **The site** (`scope: bid`, picked, 0 to 2). `DIFFICULTY_CRITERIA_V2` in [`difficultyCriteria.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/difficultyCriteria.ts) is the same JSON, held equal by a test that reads the migration. A GC's rating carries to the GC's scales only (`carryDifficultyPicks(…, 'gc')`): before this, every rated GC's card would have read *The site is unrated under this version*.
- **A bid's own picks** (`bid_difficulty_picks`, [`bidDifficultyPicks.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/bidDifficultyPicks.ts), `bidDifficultyPicksIo.ts`, `useBidDifficultyPicks`): one row per bid, kept apart from the pin. The Workbench prices them with the GC's rating into the same lines (`bidDifficultyScalesFor`; `difficultyLines` has read the site percent since PR 4), and the pin's `picks` keeps both. A bid to a GC nobody has rated is priced on its own picks alone (the pin's `rating_id` is null and its version is the published one).
- **On the Workbench** ([`BidSitePick.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/BidSitePick.tsx)), under the difficulty chip: *The site: not picked on this bid* · **Pick**, then *The site: 1 point · An occupied building, or no room to stage.* · **Change**. A rung saves at once and, where the Workbench may write (the customer-facing price of the bid's own GC, not sent, hours and a rate on the bid), the lines are rewritten in the same press. A sent bid keeps its pick. Nothing draws under a version with no bid scale.
- **On the master's sheet**, from a bid: **On this bid** under the GC's scales. The consequence box prices the site with the rest. A site pick saves by itself with no reason asked and leaves the GC's rating as it stands (`rateSheetSavePlan`); the rating's own rules bind only when the rating is being saved. From the ask at the end of a job there is no bid and no site.
- **Rated since this was priced** also says when the bid's own picks moved (*The site was picked since this was priced: 1 point now, unrated when priced.*) and when a bid priced on its own picks is to a GC rated since, with the same **Read the new rating in**. The GC's points are compared without the bid's own.
- **What the rating did** ([`difficultyFeedback.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/difficultyFeedback.ts), `difficultyFeedbackIo.ts`, [`GcDifficultyFeedbackCard.tsx`](../../src/components/customers/GcDifficultyFeedbackCard.tsx)), under the rating's card on the customer's page: the bids to the GC priced since it was first rated (won, lost, waiting, not sent), the difficulty cost priced into them (`bids.priced_difficulty_usd`), and for the office the jobs from those bids against the **hours priced**: the pin's field hours plus its field and site lines, never the office hours, which nobody clocks on a job. A job is judged once it is Ready to Bill or later, or as soon as it is past its hours. *Hours ran 16 hours past the hours priced on 1 of 2 jobs. Rate them again?* with a press that goes to the rating above; the jobs sit behind *Show the jobs*. An estimator reads the bids and is told the hours are the office's. A GC nobody has rated draws no card.
- **Three small things the earlier walks named**: a reason and a sentence keep the letter they were written with (`bidDifficultyRemovedWords`, `difficultyLineWords` lowered it, which turned a name into *zZ walk…*); a capped line's words say what the cap left (*10% of 480 field hours at $80, held to $1,226 by the cap*), so the Labor tab's row and its dollars agree.
- Guides *rate how a GC is to work with* (the site on the sheet, *What the rating did*) and *set what difficulty costs* (the site's card, *Pick the site on a bid*); ACCESS_CONTROL, APP_DIRECTORY and the glossary entry amended.

## Not changed

- The price list: the site's percent has been on it since PR 4 (4% a point in version 1).
- A difficulty row on a print or a CSV still reads *Other* with its note.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **Doubted: a column on the pin.** The brief suggested `bid_picks` on `bid_difficulty`. The pin is what a bid was priced with, written by the Workbench when it first costs the bid; a pick made on a phone before that would have needed a pin with no lines, and the first-write rule (no pin yet) would never have run. Changed: the bid's own picks have their own small table, the pin snapshots them with the GC's, and the two are compared to say when a pick moved.
- **Doubted: publishing from the dev's card.** The first live publish would have been a write made during a walk. Kept the migration, so the walk is a read and the kernel's constant is pinned to the same JSON by a test.
- **Doubted: version 2 as the test drew it.** `difficultyCriteria.test.ts`'s `v2()` also gave the field scale a fourth rung (*We walked off the job once.*). That is a sentence the owner has not seen. Left out: version 2 is version 1 and the site, word for word.
- **Doubted: a reason for the site.** A GC's rating needs *What happened* because the points follow the GC to every bid. A site pick stays on one bid and its sentence is the reason. Kept it without one.
- **Doubted: the hours the jobs are held against.** The brief said the field hours plus the lines' hours. Office hours are priced as hours but nobody clocks them on the job, so a job would always look inside its hours by that much. Changed: the field and site lines only.
- **Doubted: showing hours to estimators.** Clock sessions are not theirs to read, and a card that read 0 clocked hours would say every job ran inside. Changed: the hours are read for the office only, and the card says so to an estimator.
- **Left for Micah, with my pick:** the card counts a bid from the day its margin was last kept (`priced_at`), so a bid made before the rating and repriced after counts as *since rated*. My pick is to keep it, since repricing is when the lines land.
- **Left for Micah, with my pick:** whether *What the rating did* should also show for a GC with bids and no rating, as a prompt. My pick is no: the card above already says *Not rated yet*.
