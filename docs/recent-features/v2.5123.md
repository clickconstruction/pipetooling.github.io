# v2.5123 — GC projects: a customer can pay a certified bill by card in their portal (2026-10-09)

GC mode's Owner Billing O8b, on O8a's database (v2.5113, migration `20261010026000`). The owner's answer to the Stripe Pay question (2026-10-09; counsel's okay on the 3% the same day): the customer turns a certified GC bill into a card payment from their portal with a 3% credit card fee, and staff never turn one. The plan is `to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/owner-billing-o8.md` on `spike/gc-mode`. No migration. Two function deploys, and the offer stays off (`GC_CARD_BILL_ON` unset) and Stripe in test mode (`GC_CARD_BILL_STRIPE_MODE` unset) until the owner says live.

## What shipped

- **`gc-card-bill`** (new, `verify_jwt = false`), two doors:
  - the portal's, `{ token, invoiceId }`: the switch, the live link, the bill a certified bill of the link's customer's GC job, an email for Stripe's receipt; then `gc_card_bill_begin`, a card-only Stripe invoice (the bill's line, then *Credit card fee (3%)*), and `gc_card_bill_finish`. A failure after begin voids the half-made invoice and clears the pending row. Nothing is emailed.
  - the office's, `{ undo: invoiceId }`: the money team only, never a training account or a twin; Stripe's page down first (refused once Stripe shows a payment), then `gc_card_bill_undo` as the caller. O8c's **Back to a check bill** calls it.
- **`_shared/gcCardBill.ts`**: the pure half, the switch, the mode, the fee, the request, the refusals in words (O8a's own pass through, anything else does not), the Stripe lines, the due day and the portal's offers (`gcPortalCardBills`).
- **`customer-portal`**: `cardBills[]`, an `offer` on each certified bill with no Stripe page and nothing paid when the switch is on and the customer has an email, and `onCard` on each bill turned to card. The owner sample shows the offer: its certified bill (`samplePortalMoneyRows('owner')`, v2.5110's builders) is no longer on Stripe, and its `cardBills` come from `gcPortalCardBills` itself.
- **The portal** (`CustomerPortal.tsx`, `PortalCardBill.tsx`, `lib/portal/portalCardBill.ts`): **PAY BY CARD** beside the check chip with *Card adds 3%* under the date; its panel in the plan's words; **Go to the card page** opens Stripe's page in a new tab and the statement reads again on return; a refusal shows the function's words with our office's line; a bill on card says *Includes the $X card fee.*
- **`create-stripe-invoice`**: one refusal before any Stripe call, *A GC bill goes on card only from the customer's portal.*, for a row a GC pay application's certificate or an interest bill made.
- **What customers see**: the journey step `gc-portal-pay-by-card` on the owner sample. **Guide**: `see-what-a-customer-sees-on-their-portal` → *A GC bill they can pay by card*.
- **Docs**: `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` (`gc-card-bill` and its contents line, `customer-portal`'s `cardBills`, `create-stripe-invoice`'s refusal), `BILLING_FLOWS.md`, `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`, `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` (§20, Pay by card).
