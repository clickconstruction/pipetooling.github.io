## How

- **`thumbInLine(drawn)`** in [`src/lib/phonePress.ts`](../../src/lib/phonePress.ts) is the rule: a press that sits in a line of words or a bar is 44 px tall on a phone, and a negative margin over it and under it gives back what it grew by, so the line stays as tall as it was drawn. The Months card did the same in `index.css` in v2.8119.
- **The ×** is `PhoneSheet`'s own (`src/components/PhoneSheet.tsx`), so every sheet that rises from the bottom of a phone gains it: the Lien desk's More and the windows inside a Messages thread. Side margins give back its extra width, so the × is drawn where it was. At a desk the window's × is the 25 × 27 it was.
- **A job's number** is `LienJobNumber` with `phone`, which only a By date phone row passes. It takes a 44 px width as well, so every row's name now starts at the same place.
- **Print and mail** and the *Deliveries* lens are `LienPilePress` (`LienPileHeads.tsx`). On a phone the button is 44 px with no look of its own and the pill is drawn inside it at the 30 px it had. The pinned title stays 40 px, since a taller pinned title takes rows from the list, the card's own lesson. The press reaches 3 px past the title's two edges.
- **Send 1 for approval…** and **Redraw…** over the affidavits take `LIEN_PHONE_LIST_PRESS` (`lienDeskRowStyles.ts`) on a phone.
- **The find box**: the field and its clearing × are 44 px, and the line's own padding came in from 11 px to 6, so the line is the height it was.

## Read and left as they are

- **The bucket chips on By date and the pile chips on Lien affidavits slide sideways.** On By date the strip is 695 px of chips in 359; on affidavits five chips need about 860. Each is 40 px tall and the strip scrolls inside itself, so the page does not slide. Wrapping them would put two or three rows of chips over the list. Left for the owner's look.
- **The More sheet did not list *Put a GC on notice*.** That row is conditional (`gcNoticeShown` in `LienDesk.tsx`): it shows on By paper → Monthly notices, for an office seat, when a GC has unpaid work to put on notice. Read there on 2026-11-21 it is the sheet's second row. On To do, By date and the other papers it is not offered, as on a computer.
- A pile's title is a press 38 px tall inside its 40 px bar, and an open paper's section titles are the same. The walk did not name them and this PR leaves them.
