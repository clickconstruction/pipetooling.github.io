# v2.5800 — Legal desk: a Timeline tab plans what happens next on a matter (2026-10-23)

Click v Burd & Assoc. (Take 5 Oil Change, Liberty Hill; job 891): the lawyer is out of the country and the office is running the case itself, so the owner asked for "a tab that allows any user or the MCP to add a timeline where we can plan what is happening next and show it as a timeline with notes so all parties can have an idea of the plan ahead." The Fees & steps tab showed the past as tables; nothing on the desk held the plan.

- **The data** (migration `20261023195135_legal_plan_items.sql`): a planned step is one more kind of `legal_matter_entries` row, `plan` — `occurred_on` is the day it is planned for, `body` is what, `meta` carries `{ notes, done_at, done_by, source: office | firm | mcp }`. Reusing the stream means a planned step inherits the office read policy, the portal's service-role read, the shaper, undo with a reason and hold-back from counsel (`held_overrides` key `plan:<id>`). Three office RPCs, gated like `legal_add_entry`: `legal_plan_add(p_matter_id, p_on, p_body, p_notes)`, `legal_plan_update(p_entry_id, p_on, p_body, p_notes)` (NULL leaves a field), `legal_plan_mark_done(p_entry_id, p_done_on, p_note)` — marking done stamps `meta.done_at` and `done_by` and keeps the planned day, so the line can read *Done Oct 18. Planned for Oct 20.* `legal_void_entry` now undoes a planned step the office wrote too. No notification event is written for a planned step: the firm reads the line on its portal.
- **The kernel** (`src/lib/legal/legalTimeline.ts`, tested): `buildLegalTimeline` merges every entry (by `occurred_on`; a done plan by its done day) with the documents for the firm (by the day added) into one sorted line, splits it into past · today · upcoming, counts planned / overdue / due today / done / held, and words each state in plain words (*Planned. In 5 days.* · *Due today.* · *Overdue by 3 days. Not marked done.* · *Done Oct 18. Planned for Oct 20.* · *Undone: entered twice*). `planDraftProblem` holds the form to the RPC's caps (500 / 4,000 characters).
- **The desk** (`LegalDeskModal.tsx` → `LegalTimelinePanel.tsx`, render-tested): a seventh tab, **Timeline**, with a **Plan a step** form (day, what, notes; the matter is made first when the account has none), a **Today** marker, **Earlier** folded behind *Show N earlier*, and **Ahead**. On a live planned step: **Edit**, **Mark done** (a day not ahead of today and what happened), **Hold from counsel…** (the office's hold door and reason, as on Record of contact) and **Undo…** (a reason the firm reads). A document is a read-only *added* item with **Open Evidence ↗**. `?legal=<key>&legalTab=timeline` opens on it. Fees & steps is unchanged: planned steps leave its *On the matter* table and the printed packet's.
- **The firm's portal** (`LegalFirmMatterView.tsx`, `LegalPortal.tsx`): the same line read-only on the portal's paper, with the firm's own **Plan a step** form under it (`submit-legal-portal` kind `plan`, `occurredOn` may be ahead — `legalPlanDateProblem`, three years either way — `meta.source = 'firm'`, `via_portal = true`) and **Undo…** on its own planned steps; `legal-portal` drops a held planned step and counts it in `heldCount`. A firm's planned step is a plan, not an act: it does not land on the office's Needs You (`buildFirmActivity` skips `plan`).
- **The dev MCP** (`plan_matter_plan_item` → `apply_matter_plan_item`, server 0.6.0): `dev_legal_plan_write(p, p_dry_run)` wraps the three RPCs the way `dev_legal_narrative_write` wraps the narrative — the dry run names the matter, the action, the item before and after and whether the firm reads it; apply writes with `source = 'mcp'`. `{ item: { action: add | update | mark_done, matter_id | entry_id, on, body, notes, done_on, note } }`; one apply per plan as for every write verb.
- **Docs**: the guide *review a collections account before it goes to your attorney* gains *Plan what happens next*; PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION (Legal desk), GLOSSARY (*Planned step*), ACCESS_CONTROL (who plans, marks done, holds, undoes), EDGE_FUNCTIONS (dev-mcp, legal-portal, submit-legal-portal), `docs/dev-mcp/README.md`.
- **Deploy**: push the migration first, then deploy `legal-portal`, `submit-legal-portal` and `dev-mcp`. `src/types/database.ts` and the MCP catalog follow with the next `gen-types:linked` run (the write verbs POST fixed RPC names, so the MCP works before it).
