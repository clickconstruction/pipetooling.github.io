# v2.8344 — Dashboard: Recent Reports has one heading row, a foot, and an Opened group (2026-11-23)

Step 3 of punch list #426. The owner's ask of 2026-11-23: the title *becomes two lines* with *mark eight opened and view all and the mail button all there*, move some of it *to the bottom line*, and make it *super easy for someone to clear work off their list*. His picks the same day: Opened is the one put-away state, the list refills, and a press on a report's name opens the report. Client only. No migration, no edge function.

## What was there

- **A heading of two rows.** The fold, the title and its badge wanted one row. **Mark N opened**, **View all →** and a 32 px envelope wanted another. Together they did not fit under about 440 px, so the heading wrapped on every phone.
- **The newest eight, cut first.** The card read every report and kept eight before it looked at anything else. The badge could never read more than 8. A ninth new report was never shown, and was not shown after the eight were cleared either.
- **Two put-away states.** *Opened* left the list on the next load and waited behind **Show N opened reports**. *Done* left it for good. **Mark unread** was said to undo Done, but it sat inside an opened report and a done report was never drawn, so nothing reached it.
- **A blank for a cleared list.** The empty words showed only when the person could read no report at all. Eight reports all opened earlier, or all done, drew a heading over nothing.
- **One moment in two places.** The clock at the right of the name and the day under it were the same timestamp.
- **Two things on one row.** A press on the name opened the job. A press anywhere else opened the report.
- The read of the person's marks had no filter and no limit.

## What changed

- **One heading row**: the fold, **Recent Reports** and the **N new** badge.
- **A foot of two rows**, each with what it is on the left and its press on the right. The count of new reports and **Mark N opened**. Then **Email reports**, in words with its envelope, and **View all ›**.
- **One put-away state.** **Mark opened** on a report, or **Mark N opened** for every new one in the list, moves it to the **Opened** group at the card's foot at once. A toast names it with **Undo** for ten seconds. **Mark unread** in the group brings one back. **✓ Done** is gone, and a report already done reads as opened, so nothing comes back as new.
- **The list refills.** `buildRecentReportsCard` in `src/lib/dashboardRecentReports.ts` reads the reports of the last 14 days (`RECENT_REPORTS_WINDOW_DAYS`), lists the new ones eight at a time, and counts every new one for the badge. The Opened group draws the newest eight of what was put away and says when it holds more.
- **A report read in place stays put.** A press opens the report in place and marks it opened. It stays in the list, dimmed with its tick, for the session.
- **One press a report.** A press anywhere opens the report. **Job detail** is inside the opened report, for every role but a primary, as before.
- **One value for when**: *yesterday 1:02 PM* (`formatReportRowWhen`), drawn with `WorkedOutValue`.
- **A clear list says so**: *Nothing new to read. A report lands here when the crew files one.* (`RECENT_REPORTS_CLEAR_EMPTY`).
- **A bounded read.** The marks are read from the window's start. A report filed inside the window can only have been marked inside it.
- **A mark that does not save says why**, and the card reads the marks again.
- Every press on the card is 44 px tall. The rows are a divided list in place of eight boxes.

**Mark opened** is a word press on each row at every width in this step. Step 5 turns it into a swipe on a touch screen and keeps the word press at a desk.

## What it does not change

- Who sees the card and whose reports each role reads. The database decides both.
- The office phone's fold over the card. It reads the card's counts, and *listed* is now every report of the 14 days.
- Reading a report elsewhere still leaves it new here. Only this card writes the marks.
