# v2.3313 — Legal desk PR 2: attorney-ready is the release, held entries, the dev's review card (2026-09-11)

Migration `20260911195745_legal_matters.sql` ([`docs/migrations/20260911195745_legal_matters.md`](../migrations/20260911195745_legal_matters.md)); no edge function. Stacked on v2.3293 (PR 1). Second of five: desk read side → **this gate** → the firm's portal read → the firm's portal write → the firm's emails.

## Why

PR 1 let the office see what a firm would receive; nothing was stored and nothing could leave. The owner's flow needs three stored facts: which entries the office holds back, whether the office asked a dev to look, and the dev's attorney-ready mark — which is the moment an account reaches the firm.

## What shipped

- **Kernel [`src/lib/legal/legalMatters.ts`](../../src/lib/legal/legalMatters.ts)** (+9 tests): stage labels and the with-firm / closed sets, `legalRowChip` (nothing while simply under review; blue "review requested"; ⚖ once with the firm), `heldOverridesOf` / `withHoldOverride` (an override exists only where it differs from the default), `feeModelOf(firm)`, `indexMatters`, `buildLegalReview` (accounts under review, requested-first, the oldest, the account the card opens on), `releaseRecipients` (the handling person by email, until PR 5's rules).
- **Hooks** — [`useLegalMatters`](../../src/hooks/useLegalMatters.ts) (firm, matters, job links, entries; `available=false` before the migration so the desk stays read-only) and [`useLegalReviewNudge`](../../src/hooks/useLegalReviewNudge.ts) (Collections jobs grouped by payer + matters → the dev's card, refetch on focus, fail-soft).
- **Desk** — the rail groups *Needs a dev's eyes* (requested first) · *With the firm* · *Closed*; header actions by stage: **⚖ Mark attorney ready…** (dev; a sheet with the handling person, who hears about it, the red-gap and not-worth-it warnings, held count, a note → `legal_mark_attorney_ready`), **Ask a dev to review…** / **Withdraw** (office → `legal_matter_save_review`), **Pull back** (dev → `legal_pull_back`), **Write down…** (after the agreed write-down succeeds the matter closes `written_down`). Their word: a *to counsel* checkbox per entry plus *Only after the first bill* / *Share all*; overrides save to `held_overrides` and the packet re-derives. Fees & steps lists the matter's entries. The worth panel reads the firm's contingency and filing cost.
- **Pipeline rows** — the ⚖ chip beside the contract chip on the table and the phone cards (`legalMatterByJobId` on the row context).
- **Dashboard** — `legal-review` Needs You item (dev only, tier 40): "N Collections accounts await your review before an attorney sees them", who asked and how long the oldest has sat, amber when asked or over 30 days; **Review** opens `/jobs?tab=stages&legal=<payer key>`.
- **Settings → Jobs & dispatch → Collections law firm** (dev): name, handling person, email, phone, contingency %, filing cost — one active row in `legal_firms`.
- Help guide updated (the two exits, asking a dev, holding entries); GLOSSARY; ACCESS_CONTROL.
