# v2.6570 — A parcel the roll fills with the word Null is nothing under the pin (2026-11-06)

Two edge functions change (**redeploy after merge**): **`property-lookup`** and **`owner-confirm-nightly`** (both import the changed `_shared/txParcelRecord.ts`). No migration. Third of the lookup train after [v2.6520](v2.6520.md) (the pin and situs gates) and [v2.6537](v2.6537.md) (`Null` on the situs and mailing lines, Bexar's comma). Found when the two rows v2.6537 had held were run again after its deploy: today's pins for `858 FM1209, Bastrop` and `976 County Road 7718, Devine` land on parcels whose `OWNER_NAME`, `LEGAL_DESC` and `NAME_CARE` are the word `Null` with a prop id — placeholder polygons — and the parse took `Null` as the owner's name. Before v2.6520 the same answer would have filled a record's owner of record with `Null`.

## What changed

- **`rollText` on every field of the parcel record** ([`_shared/txParcelRecord.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/txParcelRecord.ts) `recordFromAttributes`): prop id, owner, care-of, legal description, county, source and tax year read `Null` as blank, as the situs and mailing lines have since v2.6537.
- **A parcel has substance when it names an owner or a legal description.** `hasSubstance` no longer counts a prop id alone, so a placeholder polygon is nothing under the pin: `parseTxParcelIdentify` takes the next result on a boundary, the county rung still answers, the record panel reads *No parcel under the map pin* and offers the paste box, and the nightly counts a miss rather than saving a blank owner.

## Testing

- `propertyRecord.test.ts` +1: a placeholder result alone parses to null; before a real result, the real one wins.
- The record, kernel and ladder suites pass; typecheck, lint, `check:edge-bundles`.

## Deploy

`supabase functions deploy property-lookup` and `supabase functions deploy owner-confirm-nightly` after the merge (`npm run check:edge-drift` before and after); then the two addresses above read *No parcel under the map pin* with their county.
