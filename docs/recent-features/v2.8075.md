# v2.8075 — Before we start on a phone: a bullet’s words get the full width (2026-11-20)

Found by the Fable 5.2 audit of punch list #382 (Before we start). A layout fix in the two bullet rows of a stage's list. No migration, no function, no words changed.

## What was wrong

The audit measured both at 390 px wide on the job window's Documents tab, light and dark:

- **A bullet of ours was squeezed into a narrow column.** `OursRow`'s line was a flex row with no `flexWrap`. Its words took what was left (`flex: 1; minWidth: 0`) after the tick box, the tag *ours, ticked by hand* (`whiteSpace: 'nowrap'`, longer still for *read from the city's permits*) and the ⋯. That left the words about 110 px: *Our material lands before day one.* wrapped onto three lines and its fact line broke as *Not marked done / yet. Mark it done*.
- **A bullet from them left its ⋯ alone on a line.** `TheirsRow`'s line did wrap (`flexWrap: 'wrap'`, words `flex: '1 1 14rem'`), but the tag and the ⋯ were two separate pieces of the line. On a phone the ⋯ dropped to a line of its own at the far left under every bullet, about 60 px of empty height a row.

## The fix

Both rows in [`StartCheckStage.tsx`](../../src/components/startChecks/StartCheckStage.tsx) now have the same three pieces on a line that wraps (`flexWrap: 'wrap'`):

1. the tick box,
2. the words with the line under them (`flex: '1 1 12rem', minWidth: 0`),
3. whose it is and the ⋯ together in one group that never wraps inside itself (`display: 'flex', gap: 10, alignItems: 'center', flex: 'none', marginLeft: 'auto'`).

Where there is room the line reads as before: box, words, tag and ⋯ on one row. Where there is not, the group drops under the words as one piece and sits at the right, so the ⋯ is never alone. `TheirsRow`'s hidden photo picker stays inside the line. No word, aria-label, data attribute or handler changed.

The new structure was laid out in Chromium on a static page carrying the rows' own inline styles, in a 330 px line (a 390 px phone less the card's sides). It was not walked signed in.

| Row | Words now | Tag and ⋯ now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ours, *ticked by hand* | 296 px, one line | one 18 px line under the words, at the right |
| ours, *read from the city's permits* | 296 px, one line | one 18 px line under the words, at the right |
| from Ray | 200 px, beside the tag | on the first line, at the right |

At 1280 px all three rows stay on one row with the tag and the ⋯ at the right edge, as before.
