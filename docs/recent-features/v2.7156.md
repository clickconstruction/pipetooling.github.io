# v2.7156 — Materials: price a site's rows one at a time (2026-11-13)

Punch list #342, PR 1 of 3. A press on **Worth** listed the rows with no price and stopped there: pricing one row was three presses and a parts-book search that found nothing for most names a crew typed. On the first site measured, 96 of 100 rows had no price. The owner picked a window that asks about one row at a time. This PR is that window with its offers, and it needs no migration.

## What changed

- **The offers** (`src/lib/materials/priceOffers.ts`, pure, 12 tests): a candidate is offered when its name holds every word of the row's name, as the screen's own same-words fold reads both with the inch mark set aside (`offerTokens`). `rowOffers` returns the parts-book parts that fit, each with the price the one rule would read for it (`pickBookPrice`), closest fit first, then what the bills paid: one offer per house and wording, the newest line, at its price for one through the bill's pricing unit. A different size is never offered, a name of one word is offered nothing, a book part with no price is not an offer, and a credit is not a price. Nothing picks for the reader: a name with a size and no material gets a list.
- **The read** (`priceOffersIo.ts`, `usePriceOffers`): the parts book's names and prices and the priced part lines of `supply_purchase_lines`, each whole and in pages, asked once on the first press of Worth by a role that may tie. The two sources fail apart, so a role that cannot read the bills still gets the book's offers and the window names the source that did not answer.
- **The window** (`SitePricingWindow.tsx` over the pure `pricingWalk.ts`, 6 tests): **Price 88 rows one at a time** on the line over the narrowed list opens it. It shows one row with its pieces and boxes, its first three offers with a dashed edge and the word *Offer*, and **more fits**. A parts-book offer is an answer: a press or its number key writes the tie that already exists (`on_site_part_ties`) and the window moves on. **Other part…** opens the parts-book picker. **Skip**, or S, moves on. The foot names the last answer with **Undo**, which deletes that tie and returns to the row. Past the last row the window says what was priced and offers the rows still open again.
- **The tile**: Worth's small line gains a count of rows, *41 rows have an offer*. An offer is never added to the dollar figure.
- **The picker** moved to its own file (`BookPartPicker.tsx`) and opens on the folded words (`bookSearchFolded`), so a crew's *tees* finds the book's *TEE*.

## What it does not do

- **A bill's price cannot be pressed.** A typed name has no price on the server until PR 2's table. The window shows the bill's line to read.
- **No price can be typed, and a row cannot be set aside** as not a part. Both are PR 2.
- **A tie still prices the site's screen alone.** The job page's Missing table reads it in PR 2.

## Checked

`priceOffers.test.ts`, `pricingWalk.test.ts` and three new cases in `SiteMaterialsView.render.test.tsx` (the window end to end with a number key, Undo, Skip and the end of a pass; a source that did not answer; a key typed in a box is not an answer). On the real site, as the dev on a local copy of the app with every write to a table refused: Worth read *48 rows have an offer* beside its cover, the press read **Price 96 rows one at a time**, and the window opened on row 1 of 96 with three numbered answers and *25 more fits*. A row further down showed a bill's lines to read and no answer. No answer was pressed, since each is a write. 1400 and 375 wide, no sideways scroll. The first look showed one house's part three times under three of its numbers, so the bills' offers now fold by house and wording.
