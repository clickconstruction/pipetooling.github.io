# v2.4729 — Lien desk: enclose a conditional release of lien with the notice (2026-10-06)

**The ask (Stephen, 2026-10-06, on top of Taunya's cover-letter request — PR 2 of 2 after [v2.4725](v2.4725.md)):** "what if we also gave our users the option to add a conditional release of lien?", with the paragraph the letter should carry: *This release is not effective today. It becomes effective only after $15,722.49 is received and the funds have cleared. Until then, the notice stands.* Mock-up https://claude.ai/artifact/EyKmS86NApfASP3Ar2TMTP (the release section), then "build it all".

**Why this shape.** A GC or owner often says "send a conditional release and we will cut the check"; the office made one by hand in the Release of Lien window and mailed it after. The window's rule is that no paper leaves without its record, so the enclosed release is a `job_lien_releases` row from the tick, issued when the run is recorded — and the Dashboard's cleared-release queue then offers the unconditional the day the money clears, the follow-through counsel's letter promises.

## Decisions

- Off until asked for: counsel's letters promise the release the day funds clear; enclosing one up front is the office's call per owner.
- A record, not just a page; the release rides on both copies, the paragraph on the owner's letter only; the form starts on the GC as the check's maker.
- **For counsel** (the amber line): the § 53.284 form names the check's maker, and counsel's third door lets the owner pay with the GC's written okay — how a release that either may pay should be worded. Put a GC on notice gets no tick yet.
