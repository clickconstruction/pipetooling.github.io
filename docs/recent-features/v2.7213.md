# v2.7213 — Lien desk: on a wide screen the tools sit on the title line (2026-11-13)

The owner sent a screenshot of `/lien-desk?tab=deadlines` and said he did not like how the header looked at his window. He picked arrangement A of three drawn beside today's.

## What was wrong

The screenshot is a window about 1,846 px wide. Measured on the live desk at that width, reads only:

- The header was 179 px in four rows before the first bar: the title line, the tool line, the pills and the date row.
- The title line used 759 px and left 999 empty. The tool line held only the five tools, 440 px, pushed right, and left 1,318 empty.
- Both lines together need about 1,300 px, so they fit on one. They sat on two because since v2.6569 the bar breaks after the title line at every width, a rule made for a 1,167 px window.

## The change

- **One line in a wide window.** In a window of 1,500 px or more the tools end the title line: *An owner asked for records*, **§ Rules**, ☎, **?** and **Share**, in that order, pushed to the right edge. On Do now and Deadlines the bar has no second line: 52 px, down from 92, and the header on Deadlines is 139 px, down from 179. On All filings the second line stays for the paper tabs, the piles and *Put a GC on notice*.
- **A rule, not a wrap.** `lienDeskTitleBarLayout` in `src/lib/jobs/lienDeskTitleBar.ts` (new kernel) says where the tools sit, whether the bar breaks and whether the second line's right span is drawn. It reads `useMatchMedia('(min-width: 1500px)')`, a viewport rule like the bar's own in `index.css`, so every view and every kind of paper answers the same way and the tools never change lines between them.
- **Why 1,500 and not 1,300.** The run button is 270 px today (*Record the mailing · 7 sealed*). At its longest (*Send the run · 24* with three counts and a long first name) it measured 463 px. `LIEN_DESK_TITLE_LINE_PARTS` holds the measured parts and a test holds the width to the longest line, so the tools cannot fall off it. The longest label was forced into the page at 1,500 px and the line held.
- **The right span is never empty.** With the tools on the title line the span would hold nothing off Notices. An empty flex item still takes a column gap, which would push Share 14 px off the right edge, so the span is drawn only with the GC door.
- **The second line keeps one height.** With the tools gone from it, the paper tabs (22 px) were the tallest thing on it for three kinds and the GC door (25 px) for Notices. The tabs take 2 px above and below in a wide window, so the bar is 92 px on all four.
- **Under 1,500 px and on a phone nothing moves.** The two lines of v2.6569 and the phone's one row with More (v2.7200) are the same code paths as before.

## What it does not change

- No button is renamed, hidden or reordered, and the keyboard reaches them in the order they are drawn: the views, the run, then the five tools.
- All filings → Affidavits still has three rows at 1,170 px, and Notices at 1,000 px. Both were so before this change. Arrangement B, one line with More, ends them and was not picked for this PR.
- The two blue buttons, the tools drawn alike, and *This month · Nov 16* written on the pill, the date row and the bar are as they were.
