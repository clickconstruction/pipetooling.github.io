# v2.7824 — Materials on site: Take from a box, with a photo before and after (2026-11-18)

Take from a box, step 3 of 4. The owner, 2026-11-18: *when someone goes to take materials out of a box they take a photo of how they found the box, they mark the materials they're taking, and they take a photo of the box after … the least amount of cognitive overhead*. He approved the mock-up after its second pass (*Build all the steps*). This step is the run on the phone.

## What the look found, and why the run is shaped this way

- **Nobody marked what they took.** The ledger held 162 counts and 3 presses of Used, one of them a test. A take was a sheet a part.
- **Most boxes hold one kind of part**: 81 of the 93 live boxes off the test job. A list of parts to tap is the wrong screen for nine boxes in ten.
- **A browser opens the camera only from a tap.** So the tap that starts the run opens the camera, and the tap that saves the take opens it again. No screen says step 1 of 3.

## What changed

- **Take from G12** stays at the bottom of a box's screen (`takeBar`, sticky above the app's own bottom bar). It mints the take's id, opens the camera for the photo as found, and opens the run. Never on Unsorted.
- **The run's screen** (`TakeScreen` in `TallyOnSiteTab.tsx`, rules in the pure [`takeRun.ts`](../../src/lib/materials/takeRun.ts)). A box with one line asks **How many are you taking?** on the number sheet's own stepper, opened on 1, with the quick numbers and **All N**. A box with more lists its lines: a tap takes one, and **−**, 2, 5, 10 and **All N** open in the row. **Something not on the list…** takes a part by name through the Add a part sheet.
- **Take 3 from G12** writes the rows and then opens the camera for the photo after. One Used row a line, each with the take's id. Where a person takes more than the app counted, or a part the list never held, a Delivered row with the note *found during a take* comes first, so a box never goes under zero and the app's number never caps a take.
- **The photos are asked for, never required.** A camera closed with no shot leaves *No photo of G12 as found* with **Take the photo**, and after the take **Take the photo** or **Finish without it**. The photo after ends the run by itself.
- **A run left half done** is kept on the phone (`takeRunMemory.ts`, this phone's storage, on every change) and offered again on the box and on the list: *You started a take from G12 at 2:14 PM* with **Finish it** and **Nothing taken**, or *You took 3 from G12* with **Take the photo** and **Finish without it**. After twelve hours it is forgotten. A phone that drops the page while its camera is open comes back to the box with the strip.
- **A photo kept with no signal sends by itself** (`keptBoxPhotosIo.ts`, `useKeptBoxPhotos` mounted in `Layout`): a pass runs when the app opens, when the signal is back, when the page comes to the front and when an on-site press lands. It finds the caller's own rows that say `on-phone`, sends the bytes this phone holds and marks the row `sending`, which is what **Send now** does. This holds for the **Photo** chip's shots too.
- **One path for a box's shot** (`boxPhotoShoot.ts`): the chip and a take's two photos shrink, name, keep and send the same way. A take's photos are named *G12 as found* and *G12 after a take*, with `kind` and `take_id` on their rows.
- **While a box is open the Tally's tabs and Photo of a ticket fold away** (`JobTally.tsx`, `useOnSiteAddress`' `openContainerId`). The box's screen starts at the top and its only camera press is its own.
- The store reads and writes the take columns (`take_id` on a ledger row, `kind` and `take_id` on a photo). The kernel `onSite.ts` is untouched, since it has a copy for the edge functions: a row's take rides as `TakeMovement` in `takeRun.ts`.
