# v2.3344 — The Bridge: Vectors — one row per person per pay week, what each moved on the company line (2026-09-11)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Dev-only page (`/bridge`). Step 2 of the "what to maximize" arc (step 1: v2.3335, the Truth check).

## Why

The owner's framing: a company is vectors that have to point one direction, and the app should show each person's push on the number the company maximizes (contribution: earned − direct cost). The Bridge had the company line; nothing on it said who moved it. Crew P&L has per-person billing by hours for a date range, People → Review has hours, but neither is keyed to the Bridge's own earned rate or to one week, and neither carries the office's and the estimator's terms.

## What shipped

- **Kernel [`src/lib/bridge/vectors.ts`](../../src/lib/bridge/vectors.ts)** (+7 tests). `buildVectors({ weekStart, weekEnd, people, wages, sessions, ratePerHourByJob, assumedHalfJobs, invoiceSends, payments, pctUpdates, fieldReports, bidsSent, bidsWon })` → one `VectorRow` per person with anything in the week, plus totals and an `unattributed` bucket:
  - **earned** = approved field hours × `ratePerHourByJob` (contract ÷ expected hours — the exact rate `earnedRevenue.ts` uses; a test proves a person's earned dollars equal the Bridge's earned dollars on their hours). Hours on a job with no rate count as `unratedHours` rather than earning $0 silently; earned on an assumed-half job accrues to `guessedEarnedUsd`.
  - **labor** = hours × the pay-config wage (field rate on jobs; office rate on the office job and bid time); `noWage` flags a person with no config (costed at $0, never hidden).
  - **contribution** = earned − field labor, null without field hours; `contributionPerHour`.
  - **% reports** = job % updates + field reports filed; **billed** = invoice sends by actor with the invoice's amount; **collected** = payments recorded by actor; **bids sent / won** credited to the bid's estimator. Events with no actor (system writes, service-role sends, a bid with no estimator) land in `unattributed`, stated under the table.
  - Order (`compareVectorRows`): field contributors by contribution, then everyone else by what they moved (billed + collected + bids won), then name.
- **Loader [`src/lib/bridge/loadBridgeVectors.ts`](../../src/lib/bridge/loadBridgeVectors.ts)** — loads once per Bridge window: `users` (archived included so past weeks keep their rows), `people` + `people_pay_config` (wage by `account_user_id`, then the trimmed-name join payroll uses), every closed `clock_sessions` row in the window with its approval state, `job_activity_events` `invoice_sent` / `payment_added` (invoice amounts fetched by id, chunked), `job_pct_events` (seed rows excluded), `reports`, `bid_versions` won by `outcome_at`, and `bids` sent by `bid_date_sent`. All whole-set reads page (`fetchAllRows` / `fetchAllRowsChunkedIn`).
- **Panel [`BridgeVectorsPanel`](../../src/components/bridge/BridgeVectorsPanel.tsx)** under the Truth check: the week's field contribution as the headline (earned − labor, people in the field, billed, collected, bids won, hours awaiting approval, and the ≈ guessed share in amber), ‹ › across the Sun–Sat pay weeks inside the window (current week reads "so far"), the table (Person · Field h + waiting · Office/bid h · Earned · Labor · Contribution · $/h · % reports · Billed · Collected · Bids sent · Bids won), a Company total row, and the attribution footnote. Contribution and Earned carry ≈ in amber when any of it rests on an assumed-half job; the cell tooltip spells out earned / labor / guessed / unrated / no-wage.
- Help guide `the-bridge.md`: new **Vectors** section.

## Live reading (2026-09-11, pay week Sep 6–12 so far)

Field contribution +$125k on 50 approved field hours — $124k of it one person's 24h on a job with no % complete (assumed half done, so its expected hours are a guess and the rate per hour is $5,150). 166h awaiting approval across nine people, most of whom therefore show "—" for the week. Billed $0 attributed (the week's $9.1k of sends were service-role writes with no actor); collected $23.4k across six payments recorded by the office. Two bids sent ($50.5k). The panel is doing its job: the number is real only once the % is set and the hours are approved.

## Notes for the next session

- **Billed attribution is thin**: `invoice_sent` events written by edge functions (service role) carry no actor. Stamping the sender (the signed-in user who pressed Send) into the event detail is a small edge-side follow-up; until then most sends land in "not on anyone's row".
- Subs on sheets have no session and no account — their labor is a job cost here, not a vector. A per-sub row would need the sheet's assigned names resolved (Crew P&L's resolver) and a sub "earned" convention; deliberately not done.
- Salary people are costed at hours × `hourly_wage` like `teamLabor.ts`; the 8/0 salary-day convention is not applied per week here.
- Step 3 of the arc is the staff-visible one-line Bridge on the Dashboard with the viewer's own row.
