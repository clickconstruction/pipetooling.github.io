# v2.3724 — Supply-house invoices: the PO codes for this job near this date (2026-09-22)

PR 3 of [`to-dos/po-generator-stated-need`](../../to-dos/po-generator-stated-need.md) (punch list #25). Client only, no migration.

## What was wrong

v2.3599 put a card under the invoice form's PO # — the job, the person, the day, and what they said they needed — when the number typed there is a five-digit code on the house's ledger. The live list said how often that is: at Reece, the house with half the payable balance, **3 of 117** invoices with a PO # carry a code; the other 114 carry what the tech said at the counter (*Auto Zone*, *ATI Schertz*, *Take 5 Seguin*, *Tuscarora*). The house prints the job name; the code is the office's key, not the counter's. So the reader that made the claim worth writing fired on one bill in forty.

## The change

- **[`supplyHouseInvoiceForm.ts`](../../src/lib/materials/supplyHouseInvoiceForm.ts)** — `PoLedgerEntry` carries `jobLedgerId`. New `poLedgerJobMatches({ hint, entries, jobIds, invoiceDateYmd, houseName, windowDays = 7 })`: once the invoice is on a job (its allocations), the codes minted for that job at this house within a week of the invoice date, nearest first (on a tie, the one minted before the trip), each with who, the day, *same day* / *2 days before* / *1 day after*, and the claim. Null while the exact-code card is showing, the ledger has not loaded, no job is picked, or there is no date. With no code in the window it returns one sentence instead of nothing: *No PO code on the ledger for this job at Reece within a week of Aug 19 — the trip was made without one, or its code sits on another job.* A split invoice reads *these jobs* and names the job on each row. `poLedgerEntryJobMismatch(hint, entries, jobIds)`: the exact card's one warning — the code on the paper was minted for a job the invoice is not on. +10 tests.
- **[`SupplyHousesTab.tsx`](../../src/components/SupplyHousesTab.tsx)** — the house's ledger query selects `job_ledger_id`; the invoice form renders the matches block (`data-invoice-po-job-matches`) under the job cards, because it needs the job, and the amber mismatch line (`data-invoice-po-job-mismatch`) inside the exact card. Nothing about saving changed.
- Guide *raise a purchase order* (the invoice paragraph); `GLOSSARY` → PO Generator ledger.
