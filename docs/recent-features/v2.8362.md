# v2.8362 — Dispatch inbox: a row's doors are thumb-sized on a phone (2026-11-23)

Punch list #414, the fourth of its four small parts, built third. The owner's word of 2026-11-23 to build the card's four app-wide things. No migration and no edge function.

## What was there

The doors of a Dispatch inbox row, from punch list #245 (**Open the job**, **Open the check**, **Open the list**, and the row's other presses: the job from a bid, the pictures link, the job account, the trip charge, the field estimate), stood in a column at the right of the row with `padding: 0.35rem 0.75rem` and no height of their own: 112 by 31 px at 390 px by the audit of punch list #382. In the window a row opens (`StageCheckWindow`), **Lower priority** was 103 by 23 px and **Open the job ›** 90 by 20 px. A thumb needs 44.

## What changed

- `DispatchInboxSection.tsx`: on a phone (`useNarrowViewport640`, the row's own line) every door in the column takes `doorPress`: 44 px tall, `flex: 1 1 10rem`, its words centred. The column becomes a wrapping row the width of the card, so two doors sit side by side when they fit (two at 160 px in a 358 px row) and stack when they do not. On a desk the column keeps its right-hand place, its `min(220px, 45%)` width and the doors' size.
- `StageCheckWindow.tsx`: on a phone (`useIsMobile`) **Lower priority** is 44 px tall with room each side, and **Open the job ›** is a 44 px inline-flex press in the card's title row. The words keep their size and colour; a desk keeps both as they were.

## Tests

- `DispatchInboxSection.phoneDoors.render.test.tsx`, new: at phone width the two doors of a No on arrival row are 44 px tall and share a wrapping row and still open the list and the job; the window's Lower priority and Open the job › are 44 px tall with the link's words unchanged; a desk keeps the column and the small doors.
- `useIsMobile` reads `window.matchMedia` only when the browser has one, as `useNarrowViewport640` does (the same guard as v2.8359's): a render test that drew the panel with no `matchMedia` stub crashed in CI's first run.
- Not looked at on a phone: the proof is the render test.
