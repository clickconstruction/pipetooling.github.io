# v2.4590 — Contracts: a second signer is named everywhere, and the page to sign by hand has their line (2026-10-05)

Punch list #64. [v2.4186](v2.4186.md) gave a job's service agreement a second signature frame. The customer's page, the stored PDF, the window's banner and its paper read both frames; every other reader of `job_contracts` still read the first frame's `signer_*` columns only. The one that mattered: the agreement downloaded to sign by hand had one signature line, so a homestead contract handed over on paper had no line for the second spouse. No table change, no migration, no change to the signing function.

## The kernel

`src/lib/jobs/jobContractSigners.ts` gains the one way a line names signers. `signedNames(row)` lists the filled frames' printed names in frame order. `joinSignerNames(names)` gives *Sam Owner* or *Sam Owner and Alex Owner*. `signerNamesLine(row)` is the words after "signed by". `framesWaitingLine(row)` gives *Sam Owner signed · waiting on Alex Owner* while one of two has signed. A one-frame row reads its printed name exactly as before.

The edge functions cannot import `src/`. So `supabase/functions/_shared/jobContractSigners.ts` is a byte-identical copy, and `jobContractSigners.sharedParity.test.ts` fails when the two files differ.

`src/lib/jobs/jobContractLifecycle.ts` gains two readers of the kernel:

- **`jobContractSignersAuditLine(row)`**: the agreement's line in a list. It reads *Signed electronically by Sam Owner and Alex Owner (typed and drawn) · … · consent recorded*. `jobContractSignatureAuditLine` stays the line under one signature block (the paper's blocks and the customer's page read it per frame), so it could not take the second name itself. One frame and every paper record read the old line unchanged.
- **`jobContractSignatureBlocks(row, opts)`**: the blocks every print draws. That is the first frame, plus a second when a second signer is named. Each block is signed with its own stamp and image, or left open. A paper record draws one *Signed on paper* block, because the signatures are on the scan.

## The readers

1. **The page to sign by hand.** The window's *Download the PDF*, and *On paper → Download & mark handed over*, now send the draft's `co_signer_name` (`ContractDraftPdfSource`). `share-job-contract`'s `draft_pdf` has read it since v2.4186, so the PDF prints two pairs of pen rules, each named.
2. **The browser print** (`buildJobContractDocumentHtml`). It takes `coSignerName` / `coSignature`, mirroring `_shared/jobContractPdf.ts`, and draws a *Second signature* block. An open block is now pen rules (*Sign — Alex Owner* · *Date*), as the stored PDF draws it. The old frame tagged *Signed electronically* with *Not yet signed* inside is gone. The readers:
   - `buildJobContractRecordHtml`, behind the rail's *Print / save as PDF* and a sent row opened from Documents or the Job window's Documents tab
   - the window's *Open full size*, on a draft or a history row
3. **The coverage chip** (`jobContractCoverage.ts`).
   - A signed contract's `signerName` is the names line, and `signerNames` lets the chip shorten each: *✍ Signed Sep 29 · S. Owner and A. Owner*.
   - A sent agreement carries `frames`, `framesDone` and `framesWaiting`. Once one of two has signed, the chip reads *Contract sent · 1 of 2 signed · 3d*: the count takes the place of the opens, since a signature was an open. Its title says who it waits on.
   - The batch reads (`useStagesRowFlags`, `useJobContractsNudge`, the legal desk's `useLegalPacketData`) select one shared list, `JOB_CONTRACT_COVERAGE_COLUMNS`, with the frame columns.
   - This reaches the Pipeline row (`contractRowChip` keeps the sent and signed words), the bill's contract strip, the Job window's Contract row, the legal desk, the firm's page and the legal packet's timeline.
4. **The window's pill** reads `signerNamesLine`: *✍ Signed Sep 29 · Sam Owner and Alex Owner*. The paper's first frame now carries that frame's own signing time, not the agreement's. A paper record no longer draws an open *signs here* frame beside its *Signed on paper* block.
5. **The audit line** in the window's History list, on Documents and on the Job window's Documents tab (v2.4496) reads `jobContractSignersAuditLine`.
6. **The customer portal's *Your agreements*** (`customer-portal`). It names both signers. A part-signed agreement says *Sam Owner signed · waiting on Alex Owner* in place of *Waiting for your signature* (`signingProgress`, optional in `portalPayload.ts`).

Found while checking the readers again, and fixed here:

- `share-job-contract`'s *Email a copy…* says *signed by Sam Owner and Alex Owner*. The share sheet's attachment line does too (`signedBy`).
- The signed rail's banner reads the kernel instead of joining names itself.
- `legal-portal` selects the frame columns, so the firm's page names both.
- The filing sheet's *Who signed* defaults to both names when the draft named a second signer.
- The covers kernel (`groupPapers`, v2.4301) reads the names line.

## A paper for several jobs

A paper filed for several jobs (v2.4301) is one typed *Who signed*, copied to every covered row, with no frames. Every job reads that typed name, unchanged. With the new default it is both names when the draft named a second signer.

## Deploy

`customer-portal`, `share-job-contract` and `legal-portal` need a redeploy after the merge. Each reads either order safely. Before the deploy the portal and the firm's page name the first signer, and the email names the first signer, as before. The draft PDF's second line needs no deploy.
