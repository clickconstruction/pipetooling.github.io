# v2.4590 — Contracts: a second signer is named in the office, and the page to sign by hand has their line (2026-10-05)

Punch list #64, PR A of two. [v2.4186](v2.4186.md) gave a job's service agreement a second signature frame. The customer's page, the stored PDF, the window's banner and its paper read both frames; every other reader of `job_contracts` still read the first frame's `signer_*` columns only. The one that mattered: the agreement downloaded to sign by hand had one signature line, so a homestead contract handed over on paper had no line for the second spouse. This PR is the app side and needs no deploy. PR B carries the customer portal, the signed copy's email and the firm's page, which are three edge functions. No table change, no migration, no change to the signing function.

## One kernel

The names live in `supabase/functions/_shared/jobContractSigners.ts`, moved there from `src/lib/jobs/`. `src/lib/jobs/jobContractSigners.ts` re-exports it, as `mcpKeyPrefixes.ts` and `customersListLcv.ts` do, so every importer and test stays put and the edge functions in PR B read the same file. New in it:

- `signedNames(row)` lists the filled frames' printed names in frame order.
- `joinSignerNames(names)` gives *Sam Owner* or *Sam Owner and Alex Owner*.
- `signerNamesLine(row)` is the words after "signed by".
- `framesWaitingLine(row)` gives *Sam Owner signed · waiting on Alex Owner* while one of two has signed.

A one-frame row reads its printed name exactly as before.

`src/lib/jobs/jobContractLifecycle.ts` gains two readers of it:

- **`jobContractSignersAuditLine(row)`**: the agreement's line in a list. It reads *Signed electronically by Sam Owner and Alex Owner (typed and drawn) · … · consent recorded*. `jobContractSignatureAuditLine` stays the line under one signature block (the paper's blocks and the customer's page read it per frame), so it could not take the second name itself. One frame and every paper record read the old line unchanged.
- **`jobContractSignatureBlocks(row, opts)`**: the blocks every print draws. That is the first frame, plus a second when a second signer is named. Each block is signed with its own stamp and image, or left open. A paper record draws one *Signed on paper* block, because the signatures are on the scan.

## The readers

1. **The page to sign by hand.** The window's *Download the PDF*, and *On paper → Download & mark handed over*, now send the draft's `co_signer_name` (`ContractDraftPdfSource`). `share-job-contract`'s `draft_pdf` has read it since v2.4186, so the PDF prints two pairs of pen rules, each named. No deploy is needed.
2. **The browser print** (`buildJobContractDocumentHtml`). It takes `coSignerName` / `coSignature`, mirroring `_shared/jobContractPdf.ts`, and draws a *Second signature* block. Its readers:
   - `buildJobContractRecordHtml`, behind the rail's *Print / save as PDF* and a sent row opened from Documents or the Job window's Documents tab
   - the window's *Open full size*, on a draft or a history row

   **An open block** is now a line labelled *Signature* (with the name when there are two) and a line labelled *Date*. The same page is the on-screen preview of an agreement going out for e-signature and a page someone may print and sign, and the builder cannot tell which. So the labels are true for both, and neither turns false once a pen signs. The paper PDF keeps its own *Sign*. The old frame tagged *Signed electronically* with *Not yet signed* inside is gone.
3. **The coverage chip** (`jobContractCoverage.ts`).
   - A signed contract's `signerName` is the names line, and `signerNames` lets the chip shorten each: *✍ Signed Sep 29 · S. Owner and A. Owner*.
   - A sent agreement carries `frames`, `framesDone` and `framesWaiting`. Once one of two has signed, the chip reads *Contract sent · 1 of 2 signed · 3d*: the count takes the place of the opens, since a signature was an open. Its title says who it waits on.
   - The batch reads (`useStagesRowFlags`, `useJobContractsNudge`, the legal desk's `useLegalPacketData`) select one shared list, `JOB_CONTRACT_COVERAGE_COLUMNS`, with the frame columns.
   - This reaches the Pipeline row (`contractRowChip` keeps the sent and signed words), the bill's contract strip, the Job window's Contract row, the legal desk and the legal packet's timeline.
4. **The window's pill** reads `signerNamesLine`: *✍ Signed Sep 29 · Sam Owner and Alex Owner*. The paper's first frame now carries that frame's own signing time, not the agreement's. A paper record no longer draws an open *signs here* frame beside its *Signed on paper* block.
5. **The audit line** in the window's History list, on Documents and on the Job window's Documents tab (v2.4496) reads `jobContractSignersAuditLine`.

Found while checking the readers again, and fixed here:

- The share sheet's attachment line says *signed by Sam Owner and Alex Owner* (`signedBy`). The email itself changes in PR B.
- The signed rail's banner reads the kernel instead of joining names itself.
- The filing sheet's *Who signed* defaults to both names when the draft named a second signer.
- The covers kernel (`groupPapers`, v2.4301) reads the names line.

## A paper for several jobs

A paper filed for several jobs (v2.4301) is one typed *Who signed*, copied to every covered row, with no frames. Every job reads that typed name, unchanged. With the new default it is both names when the draft named a second signer.
