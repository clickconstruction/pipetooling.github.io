# v2.3361 — The Costs tab, told straight (2026-09-12)

Client only. PR 1 of the Costs-tab refresh (owner, 2026-09-12: "the tab seems super chaotic… zero people who have been bidding have been adding in labor… focus on collecting labor baselines instead of just guessing"; before/after mockup artifact dcd2f7a5). Mission Hills J523 was the test job: eight blocks and three charts, every budget cell "≈ assumed", true margin 6 % in a tile's small print.

## What changed

- **Kernel** [`src/lib/jobs/jobCostsVerdict.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobCostsVerdict.ts) (tests beside it): `buildCostsVerdict` reads the burn model, the spend by component, the resolved budget and the price → true margin at completion (or direct margin when overhead is not loaded), spent (share of price, team hours, materials), earned so far = % done × PRICE, true margin so far, time left (working days at the progress pace, spend per field day), the four **sections** (spent · at completion at pace · the bid's figure or "no hours on B66"), and the **baseline** the job is producing (hours, hours per $1k, average wage, people, materials share; or, when the bid carried hours, recorded ÷ bid hours against the work done). A snapshot with nothing on it is no footing. `pctDoneWords` names the report that set the % and its age, and says when the job's own % disagrees.
- [`jobBurn.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobBurn.ts): `earnedBasis: 'price'` steps the cumulative earned line off the price; the legacy budget basis stays the default (tested).
- [`jobMoneyTable.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobMoneyTable.ts) (tests): "Where the money went" — supply invoices · team labor · card charges · sub labor · tally parts · other charges, largest first, share of the direct total, sources that failed or are loading left out and named.
- **Components.** [`JobCostsVerdict.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobCostsVerdict.tsx): the hero tile (full width, click → the by-section table), three tiles, the baseline strip (amber "No baseline… what it has taken so far" with **Count sheet on B66 →** and **Take its estimate**; green "◆ Baseline from B76" with Refresh / Clear when the bid carried hours; a job with no bid folds the old Budget card's link-a-bid doorway under **Link the bid ▾**). [`JobCostsTabCharts.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobCostsTabCharts.tsx) composes verdict → cumulative chart (`JobCostsBurnSection mode="chart"`: cost, value earned off the price, the bid figure line only when one exists, the price line) → **Show the timeline · daily spend ▾** (per-device pref) → `mode="detail"` (daily chart, burn rate, work left; "bid figure runs out" only with a real figure) + the Cost Timeline. [`JobFormPartsCostSection.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormPartsCostSection.tsx) renders the money table above its accordions; a row click opens that ledger (or Open on Jobs for team / sub). [`JobFormLaborCostPanel.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormLaborCostPanel.tsx) shrinks to the charts host; the Team / Sub Labor lines moved into the table.
- Gone from the tab: the four-row "≈ assumed" budget grid, "% of budget vs % done", "budget runs out: already over by $260", the second and third charts above the fold.

## Verified

Dev against prod on J523 as dev: header "$123,600 · 77% done per the May 15 report (120 days old) · the job says 90%"; true margin $8,001 · 6 % (at pace $104,675 direct + $10,924 overhead); spent $80,600 (65 %); earned $95,172 ($14,572 ahead on direct); time left ≈ 21.8 working days · 1.1 % a day · $944 a field day. Margin tile → 9-row build-up summing to the tile; supply row → its accordion opens; the toggle reveals daily spend, pace rows and the Cost Timeline. Baseline strip reads 959 h · 7.8 h per $1k · $34.22/h · 13 people · $46,376 materials · 38 % of price. Unit tests, lint, theme check, `tsc -b` clean.
