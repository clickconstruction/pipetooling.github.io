# v2.3992 — Contracts & terms: each card says what last went out — `contractLastSent` (2026-09-28)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Punch list **#50** ([`to-dos/contracts-and-terms`](../../to-dos/contracts-and-terms/README.md)), PR 7 of 7 by the plan's numbers, built before PR 6 because it needs no new table. After [v2.3977](v2.3977.md).

## Why

A card shows the template. The owner's question was the other one — *what are we sending out* — and the read that started this train found three places where the two differed. The answer is already in the database: every send freezes a copy. This reads the newest one.

## What shipped

- **[`contractLastSent.ts`](../../src/lib/contracts/contractLastSent.ts)** — pure. `LAST_SENT_READERS` names the six entries that keep a copy and which one; `contractLastSent(entry, text, data)` returns the status (`same` · `differs` · `only` · `never`), one sentence, the copy itself and, for the job agreement, two counts. `sameWording` ignores spacing and line ends and nothing else.
- **[`fetchContractLastSent.ts`](../../src/lib/contracts/fetchContractLastSent.ts)** — four reads, each on its own: `job_contracts` (live rows, 300 newest), the newest `bid_proposal_room_revisions`, the newest sent `estimates` row with `doc_kind = 'estimate'`, the newest `esign_consents` for a customer or a GC. A read that fails, or that the viewer's role may not make, leaves its cards without the line.
- **The tab** — a **Last sent** row on the card, amber when what went out differs or a draft is behind; **Compare what went out** gives that copy a column headed *What went out · Sent Sep 10*; the count line gains *N cards where what went out is not what the card says*.

## What each reader says

| Card | Read from | Says |
|---|---|---|
| Job service agreement — standard terms | `job_contracts` rows written from that document (or from the built-in wording, for the built-in card) | the newest one sent by us carries this wording, or older wording and of which date; how many are out for signature with older wording; how many unsent drafts are behind |
| Bid terms · Bid exclusions | the newest published revision's `payload.terms` / `payload.exclusions` | carries this wording · carries its own · **went out with none at all** |
| Terms box on each estimate | `estimates.terms_snapshot` | what the last estimate carried; there is no one wording to set it against |
| Estimate agreement checkbox | `estimates.customer_experience_sent.acceptCheckboxLabel` | carries this wording · carries its own |
| Electronic-signature consent | `esign_consents.clause_text` | set against today's text for that row's own audience, noun and language |

## Decided here

- **Paper that was filed and never sent is left out** of the job agreement's read (no `sent_at`): the row carries our terms and the customer signed their own page.
- **A bid or an estimate that carries its own wording is not a fault**, and the line says so (*A bid can carry its own.*). It still counts as differing: the office should know.
- **Change orders are left out** of the estimate read — they swap in their own sentences.

## Found on the way (not changed)

- The bid room's consent rows are filed under `record_type = 'estimate'`, so the consent read cannot say *which kind* of document the last signature was on beyond the noun the row carries.
- A draft behind its document is brought up to date when it is sent or handed over (v2.3965); the count here is the drafts that have not been touched since.
