# v2.6860 — Photos on a bid, from the Bid Board (punch list #306, PR 3) (2026-11-10)

Punch list #306's third PR, the one that answers the owner's ask of 2026-11-10: a photos icon on the Bid Board that is always in its place, grey until the bid has photos, with upload tools and the photos kept in Google Drive and managed in the app. PR 1 made the table and bucket, PR 2 the Drive leg. The owner took direction C, so the folder is made by the first photo and the press names it.

## What changed

- **The photos icon** (`BidsBidBoardTab.tsx`): a camera, first in the Links cluster on the desk's table and the phone's card, drawn on every bid. Grey with *No photos yet · Add photos* at none, blue with the count after (`photosSlot`). The counts are one paged read of `bid_photos` for the page's bids (`useBidPhotoCountsByBid`, in `useBidBoardScope`), read again after the window adds a photo. A row with no other link shows the camera where the dash stood.
- **The Photos window** (`BidPhotosWindow.tsx`): opens on what the bid has, newest first, with each photo's name, who added it and the day, and **Not in Drive yet** on one the sweep has not filed. A photo opens large with the one before and after, and **Open in Drive ↗** once it is filed.
  - **Adding**: a drop anywhere on the window or **Pick files** at a desk; **Take a photo** and **Pick from this phone** on a phone. Each photo is a tile while it shrinks and sends (`useShotTileEngine`, the job photos' engine, with the bid's io), with an optional name. A video or another file in the pick is left out and named with its reason.
  - **The press** (`addPress`): *Make the folder and add 3 photos* the first time on a bid, with the folder's path over it, and *Add 3 photos* after. Counted from the shots that are ready, dead while one is on its way.
  - **A folder the app cannot file into**: the window says the photos are safe in the app and why they wait, in `bidFolderHoldWords`' words.
  - **When the Drive function does not answer**, the rows are read straight from the table, so the photos still show, with the reason.
- **The rules** `src/lib/bids/bidPhotoWindow.ts` (18 tests) and the io `src/lib/bids/bidPhotosIo.ts`; a render test of the window (6 tests).
- **The guide** `add-photos-to-a-bid`. `docs/BIDS_SYSTEM.md`, `docs/PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`, `docs/GLOSSARY.md` and `docs/ACCESS_CONTROL.md` carry the icon and the window.

## Decisions

- **The other link icons are unchanged in this PR.** They still draw only when the link exists, so the camera does not line up down the column yet. PR 5 gives all five fixed slots.
- **A name at the pick, not a rename later.** The tile's box names the photo before it is added. Renaming and removing a photo that is already there are PR 4, behind the owner's call 6.
- **No shot is kept on the phone with no signal yet.** A send that fails keeps its tile with **Retry**. Keeping it for later is PR 7.
- **A training account** sees the photos and a dead **Add photos** with its reason.
