On BP375, folding Wendi's three Josam carrier rows into their fixtures (v2.4332 **Make it a part of…**) appended each carrier, so WC-1, WC-2 listed the takeoff's ZURN Z1201 *and* the JOSAM 12694 — two carriers that both looked like they needed ordering — and every carrier read *no stage on the takeoff* (WC, UR) or *Trim Set* (EWC), so an order-by date would come out weeks late.

## What changed
- **In place of** (`foldWrites(…, { replaceId })`, `SubmittalFoldModal`): when the row it joins has parts, the window asks what the new part takes the place of. `foldReplaceSuggestion` picks the row's own carrier when the folded row is a carrier and the row has exactly one; otherwise *Nothing. Add it as an extra part*. A replacement deletes that part, puts the folded part at its place in the order with its GC switch, and keeps the replaced name as `priced_label` (so the row reads *in place of the priced ZURN Z1201…* and asks why with Edit, as a house-file swap does). The preview names what comes off.
- **Carriers at Rough In** (`isCarrier` in `itemParts.ts`: *carrier* or *EZCARRY* in the catalog name): a folded carrier gets `stage = rough_in`; on the procurement log any part with no stage of its own that reads as a carrier is needed at Rough In (`procurementItemsFrom`), before the tag's takeoff stage.
- **Two carriers** (`doubledKinds`): a row whose GC-seen parts include two carriers reads *Two carriers. One may be extra.* in amber under its parts (`SubmittalPartsCell`). Only carriers: a fixture can rightly list two supplies or two stops.
- Guide: the *Make it a part* paragraph names **In place of**, Rough In and the two-carrier line.
