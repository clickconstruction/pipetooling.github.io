# v2.6490 — A person who left prices the same on every Job Summary (punch list #254) (2026-11-04)

A PR review on 2026-11-04 of charges by crew (v2.6441–v2.6461) found the three readers of a person's standing — the Job Summary's Charges and Premium columns, the Charges window and the charged hours statement — each read `users` as the signed-in user, and the `users` select policy (`20260905090000`) is `archived_at IS NULL OR is_dev()`. A dev set a person who left in June to *tech*; the dev's Job Summary priced their twelve days, while the controller's Job Summary for the same job showed Charges lower by those days, Premium as a dash, and the note *Bobby has no login for a Charged as. Link the People record to a login on People.*, which was false.

## What changed

- **One read for every charge reader** (`20261104133000_charge_standings.sql`, `docs/migrations/20261104133000_charge_standings.md`): `charge_standings()`, a `SECURITY DEFINER` read returning `id, name, charge_standing, archived_at` for every login and nothing wider, `search_path` pinned, gated as the Charges column's adjustments are — `is_dev() OR has_payroll_access()`: a dev, the controller, a pay-approved master — and refused with `42501` and plain words outside the gate, never answered with no rows (no rows would price every day at nothing and name everyone as counting as nobody). Archived rows come first, so a last-wins map lands on the live login when two share a name.
- **The three readers call it** (`loadChargeStandings` in `src/lib/charges/jobChargesRead.ts`): the Job Summary (`Jobs.tsx`) holds the rows as null until read, and a read that fails leaves the column unpriced with the reason in the cell's title; `loadCrewSessions`, under the Charges window and the charged hours statement, maps each session's standing from the same rows by id (`standingsById`).
- **The name rule** (`liveOverArchived` in the new `src/lib/charges/chargeStandings.ts`): `standingLookup` and `loginLookup` were last-wins over an unordered read, so an archived login could shadow a live one with the same name. Now a login still here wins over one that left by name, among the same the last read wins as before, and the People record's link still reaches the login that left. The name fallback for a People record with no `account_user_id` stays.
- **Docs**: `docs/ACCESS_CONTROL.md`'s `users.charge_standing` paragraph and a matrix row for the read; the Glossary's crew-day entry.
- **Tests** (`ledgerCharges.test.ts`): a departed person's standing prices the same for the Job Summary (by name and by the link) and the Charges window (by id) from the same rows, with no loginless note; an archived duplicate never shadows the live login in either order of the read; a mutant that drops the rule fails the test.
- **Left as they were**: Who counts as what and the person's desk read `users` for the dev's edits; a reader who is not a dev sees the logins still here on the card.

## Decisions

- **Refuse, never answer empty.** A reader outside the gate gets the reason, so the screen says why instead of pricing every day at nothing.
- **The gate is the adjustments'.** A master who is not pay-approved saw the column priced without the office's adjustments before; now the column is unpriced for them with the reason, the same set `job_charge_adjustments` admits.

## Test

`npx vitest run src/lib/charges`, `npm run typecheck`. After `supabase db push`: a controller's and a dev's Job Summary show the same Charges and Premium for a job whose crew includes an archived login with a standing, with no *has no login* note.
