# v2.4220 — The Bridge: a day cell opens its card, and says why it is red (2026-09-30)

Punch list #69, PR 3 of 4 (PR 1: v2.4217 · PR 2: v2.4219). The grid could show a red day; this PR lets it say why, in the job's terms.

- **The card** — [`BridgeVectorDaysPanel.tsx`](../../src/components/bridge/BridgeVectorDaysPanel.tsx) `CellCard`: a click on a day or period cell (a second click closes it; a new grid clears it) opens a card under the grid with the person and the period in words, the jobs at `hours × earned rate` beside the wage line (a salaried person's reads *the flat workday × wage*), the contribution with $/h, the verdict, and three doors — **Open J…** (`/jobs?tab=stages&stagesJob=<id>`, the lowest-rate job on the cell), **Set % complete** (`/jobs?tab=job-summary&job=<id>`) and **This day / period on People → Review** (the existing door, the cell's dates).
- **The verdict** — [`vectorDays.ts`](../../src/lib/bridge/vectorDays.ts) `vectorVerdict(bucket, name, wage)` (+6 tests): red leads with *Red because <job> earns $31 an hour and Tristen costs $38. That is the job's price against the hours it is taking, not how fast the day went — everyone on it reads the same.*; a no-price job *earns nothing … set the price and they count*; a priced job with no % gets *set the % and the rate firms up either way* (a job with no price is not told to set a %); green says every job cleared the wage, or *green on balance* naming the jobs that did not; an office day has no verdict.
- **The Why line** under each name: the red days and the jobs they were on — *7 red · all on 990* or *4 red · 1 on 1017, no price · 1 on 922* — from the kernel's `redByJob` (a day with two red jobs counts on both).
- **The ↻ mark** — the kernel takes `priorRatePerHourByJob`, the rates as they stood a week ago, and flags a day (`flippedDays`, folded into weeks and months) whose verdict differs under them (+3 tests). [`loadVectorDays.ts`](../../src/lib/bridge/loadVectorDays.ts) builds the prior rates from `loadEarnedJobs`' new `lifetimeHoursAsOf` (hours through the cutoff, `VECTOR_REPRICE_LOOKBACK_DAYS` = 7) and the % each job carried at the cutoff (the newest `job_pct_events` row at or before it, seed rows included; no row → the current %), through `expectedHoursForJob`. The contract price and the status are read as they stand today — a price change or a finish inside the week is not replayed. The cell shows ↻ top-right with the reason in its hover; the card says it in words; a period cell counts its flipped days.
- Guide and glossary amended.

**Live check** on a local preview against prod, September 2026: 14 red cells of 110; the Why lines read *1 red · all on 1017, no price*, *7 red · 7 on 990 · 1 on 1017, no price*; a red cell's card read *Red because 1017 Terrell- Concrete cut and pour earns nothing and Isiah costs $8 …* with its three doors; no ↻ this month.
