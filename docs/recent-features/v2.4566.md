# v2.4566 — Team purchases: go back to a sorted purchase, and tick more than one invoice (2026-10-05)

Asked by the assistant who sorts the team's card charges: "I want to be able to see recent transactions. I needed to assign more than 1 invoice to a transaction and it only let me assign the first invoice."

## What was wrong

- **Link invoices did take several invoices, and hid the fact.** The list is the search's result, so the invoice she ticked left the screen the moment she searched for the second one. The tick was still held, but nothing on the page showed it except a count. She saved with one.
- **A sorted charge had no way back.** `list_stale_unlinked_mercury_transactions_for_tally_staff` drops a charge once it has a job split or an invoice link, so the charge with one of its two invoices left the window for good.

## What changed

- **Link invoices** ([`MercuryTransactionInvoiceLinkModal`](../../src/components/MercuryTransactionInvoiceLinkModal.tsx)): the ticked invoices are held as rows (`chosen`), drawn in a **Chosen** box above the search and left out of the results below. Under them, one line from `invoiceTotalForCharge`: green *The invoices add up to the charge*, amber *$183.44 of the charge has no invoice yet* or *The invoices come to $X more than the charge*, with *$412.10 of $595.54* beside it. The button reads *Save 2 invoices*. Never a gate: a short total saves.
- **Team purchases follow-up → Sorted** ([`DashboardStaleTallyStaffFollowUpModal`](../../src/components/DashboardStaleTallyStaffFollowUpModal.tsx), [`TeamPurchasesSortedList`](../../src/components/TeamPurchasesSortedList.tsx)): a third segment beside *To sort* (was *All*) and *Stale*. One flat list, newest sort first: posted, amount, store, where it went (the job, each job of a split with its share, or each invoice with its amount), whose card, *sorted today by Taunya*. **Add invoice** (short) / **Invoices** opens Link invoices on the charge with its invoices ticked; **Change** opens the Assign window, which reads the charge's splits itself. An amber line counts the charges whose invoices add up to less than the charge, with *Show only those*.
- **Kernel** [`teamPurchasesSorted.ts`](../../src/lib/teamPurchasesSorted.ts): the row type, the two JSON parsers, `invoiceTotalForCharge` (in cents), `sortedRowIsShort`, `sortedWentToLines`, `sortedWhenWords`.
- **Migration** [`20261005181927_recently_sorted_team_purchases.sql`](../../supabase/migrations/20261005181927_recently_sorted_team_purchases.sql) ([doc](../migrations/20261005181927_recently_sorted_team_purchases.md)): the read. The window calls it caught; before the push the Sorted segment is not drawn.

## Where the build differs from the drawing

- The Sorted list is not grouped by person. "Recent" is the question, so the newest sort leads and each row names whose card it was.

## Not built

- Nothing changed on Job Parts Tally → Transactions; its refresh is punch list #72.
- The pre-existing quirk that one Escape closes both the Assign window and the invoices window under it.
