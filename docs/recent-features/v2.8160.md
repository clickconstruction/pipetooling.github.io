# v2.8160 — Dashboard: write someone up from your phone (2026-11-21)

Punch list #410, PRs 2 to 4 and 6 of the train in [`to-dos/write-someone-up/`](../../to-dos/write-someone-up/README.md), as one change: the press, the sheet and its kernel, the same press on the short pages, a send that waits for signal, and the office's card. Client only: it calls the three functions [v2.8157](./v2.8157.md) put on prod.

## Why

Taunya, 2026-11-20: writing someone up was not made easy enough for a leader or a superintendent from their Dashboard. She asked for one press, a pick of the person, and the details written on a phone and saved to HR. A leader's only door was the HR Report card, 8.7 phone screens down the full Dashboard and folded. A superintendent had none.

## What changed

- **Write someone up**, a named line under the Dashboard's row of squares, for a leader, a superintendent or a dev (`WriteSomeoneUpDoor`). The row itself is full at phone width, so the press is its own line. It opens the sheet and saves nothing.
- **The same press on the short pages.** The short Dashboard draws it after the day and above the office's line. Job Mode draws it under the job card, and Job Mode's Short view under what waits on the person. Taunya kept that pick: a press that is hard to find is the fault she named.
- **Who is it about?** `write_up_people()` answers the list. The crew on the job today come first, each with *on the clock since* or *not clocked in*. Then the people from earlier in the week, then everyone else, with a search over every name.
- **What happened.** Who, when and which job are filled in, and a press on the day, the time or the job changes it in place. Seven kinds, the last one Good work. The words are typed, or spoken through the phone's own speech, which types them. No sound is kept. Who saw it is a row of the crew on that job, with *I did* already on. One question asks whether the person has been talked to.
- **Read it back.** The record one fact a line, the words under it, and what happens next. **Send write-up to HR** is the only press that saves.
- **No signal.** The send goes through the outbox as a new kind, `person-report` (`landPersonReport`). With no signal, or a send that times out, the write-up is kept on the phone under the id the outbox minted, listed as *Waiting for signal*, and sent when the signal is back. `land_person_report` answers a second arrival of that id as the first, so it lands once. It carries the moment Send was pressed, not the moment it arrived. A refusal shows the server's own sentence and keeps the words.
- **Your write-ups.** The writer's own, each with *Waiting on HR*, *On file*, *Set aside by HR* or *Taken back*, and HR's reason when it gave one. **Add to it** sends a second report that names the first. **Take it back** closes a waiting one with a reason.
- **The office's card.** People, HR, **Pending reports** draws a write-up's kind as a chip and its facts one a line: when, the job, who wrote it with their role and how soon after, whether they were on the clock at that job, who saw it, whether the person was told. One line counts how many the writer has sent this year and how many others stand about the person. A report from before the sheet reads as it did.
- **Filed with its facts.** Both presses that file a report, the queue's and the Timeline line's, go through one call (`filePersonReport`). A write-up is filed with its facts ahead of the writer's own words, so the Timeline's entry says what the record stood on. An older report keeps the function's own preamble.
- **A line the same day.** The dev's Needs you line for field reports waited until the oldest was three days old. It now shows the day a report is sent: *A field report waits to be filed* (`HR_REPORTS_MIN_AGE_DAYS` is 0).
- **A draft is not lost.** It lives with the press, so a stray press outside the sheet closes it and keeps the words. The press then reads *1 not finished*.
- **The guide** `write-someone-up`.

## Decisions

- **The press is a line, not a square.** Measured on the live page at 375 px, the row's last square ends 8 px from the edge. A sixth square came out 44 px wide with its name wrapped.
- **The form is one screen that scrolls, with the button pinned.** In the first drawing the Send button fell below the screen. The pinned button also says what is missing, so there is no dead press with no reason.
- **The read-back is the confirm.** A small window that only asks *are you sure* reads back nothing. Here the writer reads the record itself before the one press that saves.
- **Words about what was seen.** The box asks *What did you see?* and the line under it says to leave out what it means.
- **The facts are filed by the client's words, not by a new function body.** `file_person_report` takes rewritten words already. Composing them in one kernel (`reportEntryBody`) left the function on prod untouched.
- **The old HR Report card stays for now.** It is retired in its own change once the sheet has been walked live, so a leader is never without a door.
