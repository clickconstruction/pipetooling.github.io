## The shape

A quick add is an **ordinary finished `clock_sessions` row** — `origin` stays `'user_punch'` — marked with one new nullable column, `quick_add_minutes`. So My Time, People → Hours, approval, pay, Who's where and costing need no change, and the 11 client files and the split / merge / approve RPCs that branch on `origin` never hear about it. Only the new surfaces (PRs 2–3) read the mark.

## What the build found (both changed the plan)

- **Office-only; the job pick is deferred.** A finished session on a real job flips it *waiting → working*, moves its **last work date** (lien deadlines read it), fills the customer's date met, and joins the office person to the job's crew. None of that is right for a phone call, so the RPC takes no job and pins the Office job. Pinning one later means teaching four triggers to skip quick adds — a new owner call, recorded in the to-do.
- **`NOT VALID` then `VALIDATE`.** A CHECK inside `ADD COLUMN` would scan `clock_sessions` under ACCESS EXCLUSIVE.

Also decided: a trainee (read-only) is refused, unlike their ordinary punches; a salaried person who still records hours gets the door; the owner of a quick add may fix its note or slide it but not stretch it or clear the mark — the office may, on someone else's entry only, because an assistant both uses the door and approves hours.
