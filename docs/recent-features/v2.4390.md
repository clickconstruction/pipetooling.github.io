# v2.4390 — Pipeline: a job with no price reads "no price yet" on the phone too (2026-10-02)

No migration, no edge function. Grace, 2026-10-02, after the progress-bar viewport sweep (v2.4387) pointed out the phone board's wording: *"make them match."*

## What was true

Since v2.4351 the Pipeline's money cell (table and Mobile cards) shows a job with no priced line items as a dashed track, *No price yet* and **Add the price ›**. The phone board's one chip (`pickJobNextChip` in [`jobNextLine.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobNextLine.ts)) still said *no bid value*, and so did the Follow-ups window's chip (`JobsFollowupModal`). Every Pipeline caller of `StagesProgressPaymentCell` passes a view, so the cell's classic *no bid value* button no longer draws on the Pipeline. The Bids pages keep *no bid value*: there it is a bid's own value.

## What changed

- `pickJobNextChip`: the `nobid` chip reads *no price yet*, its hover *No priced line items on the job, so nothing to bill against. Tap to add the price.* Same red tone, same `no-bid` action (Edit Job at the line items). `jobNextLine.test.ts` updated.
- `JobsFollowupModal`: the chip beside *Bid $X* reads *no price yet* when there is no bid.
- Guide *work the Pipeline from my phone* rewritten in plain words and taken off `LEGACY_PLAIN_WORDS_GUIDES` (`plainWordsLegacy.ts`). It also caught up with the code: the chip list now has the returned-check chip (v2.3806) and the lien chip (v2.4051) in their places, it says which stage swipes to which move, and the Overview fold's **Show ▾** / **Hide ▴**.
- `GLOSSARY.md` phone board: the chip order starts *no price yet*.

## Verified

`jobNextLine`, the help-guide content, plain-words and share-card suites, and the jobs component suites. Live at 390 px with Mobile cards on, production data read only: the Ready row 1023 shows *no price yet*, and no *no bid value* is left on the board.
