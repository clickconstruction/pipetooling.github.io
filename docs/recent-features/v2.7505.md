# v2.7505 — Lien desk: the ground for applying a notice that already went (the migration alone) (2026-11-16)

The owner, 2026-11-16: a lien went out on a job whose notice was part of a larger notice mailed earlier, *"that we failed to apply. I think we need a route to apply edge cases like this."* This is the record that route writes. No screen changes, and no client code writes what this adds.

## What was found

One certified notice went to an owner for every unpaid job at a property, in one claim. The desk held it on some of those jobs and not others, in two packets. On one job it was recorded with the months the paper printed, and the desk had since moved that job's unpaid money into a later month, when the last day of work was set by hand. So the desk read that month as not noticed, kept an approved notice waiting to be mailed, and offered *Print and mail* for what would have been a second notice of the same claim.

*Already mailed? Record it* could not settle it. It asks for the months as printed, and the paper did not print that month. It records a paper anew, and this paper was already on record.

## What ships

Migration `20261117090000_lien_filing_applied_from.sql` ([its page](../migrations/20261117090000_lien_filing_applied_from.md)): four columns on `job_lien_filings`.

- `applied_from`: the filing that holds the paper this row applies.
- `applied_from_label`: that paper's job number, for the row's words.
- `applied_reason`: why it stands for the months, when the desk warned.
- `applied_by_name`: who applied it.

An applied row is a notice row on the job it is applied to, with the months that job stands on and the paper's own day, mail numbers, total and saved copy. Every reader of a job's notices already reads filing rows, so the month check, the affidavit's notice paragraph and its gate count it with no change. The paper's own row is never written, and undo is the void every filing has.

## The owner's picks, for the route that follows

- **Same property only.** A paper applies to a job when it went to that job's owner of record about that property, under the same original contractor. A paper to another property is refused.
- **A month the paper does not print goes through with a reason.** The reason, the name and the day stay on the record.

## Questions for counsel

1. Does a notice that states the whole unpaid balance and lists earlier months stand as notice for a later month's work that added no new charge?
2. Does a notice sent to the owner alone hold when no copy to the original contractor is on record?
3. Can one paper about several jobs at one property stand as each job's notice?
