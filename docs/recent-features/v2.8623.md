# v2.8623 — Settings: the developer can clear an address the map could not place (2026-11-26)

Punch list #443, row 16: the ninth of the take-backs that need a migration, on the owner's answers of 2026-11-25 (*All eight*, then *Yes, all picks* to the row that made it a ninth), each push on its own yes.

## The press nothing took back

An address no map service can place, saved on a bid or a job or typed in the job form's address box, is kept in `address_geocode_misses` under the address as typed (v2.8473, punch list #408), so the screens stop asking the services for it. It goes by itself when a point is saved for the address, and it stops counting after 30 days. Nobody could delete one by hand: the table granted `SELECT, INSERT, UPDATE` and had no delete policy. The walk of #441 on 2026-11-25 left one kept miss for a made-up address.

## What changed

- **Migration `20261126024719_geocode_miss_delete_dev.sql`**: a `DELETE` policy for a developer (`address_geocode_misses_delete_dev`, `USING (public.is_dev())`) and the grant, as `bill_hand_ways_delete_dev` and `sent_documents_delete_dev` open theirs. The four fences still hold a read-only account, a twin and an archived caller back. No table is made or changed. Page: `docs/migrations/20261126024719_geocode_miss_delete_dev.md`.
- `src/lib/map/keptAddressClearIo.ts`: `searchKeptAddresses` reads the misses beside the kept addresses, each row with its `kind`; `clearKeptAddress(key, kind)` deletes from the right table under the same rule, no record anywhere holding the street line (`readKeptAddressHits`), and says so when the delete reaches no row.
- `src/lib/map/keptAddressClear.ts`: `KeptAddressKind`; the confirm and the toast say a miss when one is cleared.
- `src/components/settings/KeptMapAddressesBlock.tsx`: a miss lists with the day the services were asked and the day it stops counting by itself (`geocodeMissAsksAgainAt`), and its **Clear…** goes through the same confirm, with **Clear the miss**.
- `docs/ACCESS_CONTROL.md` and the glossary's `address_geocode_misses` entry say who may delete one.

## The walk, for the walker

1. As the dev: Settings → Data & recovery → **Kept map addresses (dev)**, type a word of the made-up street, **Find kept addresses**. The miss lists with *No map service could place it. Checked …* and the day it stops counting.
2. **Clear…** on it: the confirm *Clear this kept miss?* with **Clear the miss**. Confirm. The toast reads *The kept miss was cleared.* and the line leaves.
3. Read `address_geocode_misses` through the dev seat: the row is gone. As the sample assistant through View as, Settings has no **Data & recovery** tab, as before.
