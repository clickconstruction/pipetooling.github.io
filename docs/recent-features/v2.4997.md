# v2.4997 — GC projects: record what the customer paid on a GC bill (2026-10-08)

Owner Billing's O5c in the GC mode real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/owner-billing-o5.md` → *O5c*, on branch `spike/gc-mode`): money in on Bill the customer (v2.4995), all of it the Pipeline's own on the project's billing job. Guide: [`record-what-a-gc-customer-paid`](../../src/content/help/record-what-a-gc-customer-paid.md). No migration, no edge function, no send.

- **Read back:** O5a's mapper (`src/lib/gc/ownerBillingRows.ts`) takes the billing job's money. `billMoney` gives each bill its payments from `jobs_ledger_payments` by `invoice_id`, oldest first, and the day of the payment that closed it. `promisesOnBill` lays each live promise on every bill open when it was made (decision 8), in the app's day, the customer's own as theirs. `OwnerBilling.paid` adds them up, and an interest bill reads its paid day the same way. The kernels (`appPaid`, `appOpen`, `ownerPayDue`, `ownerLateBills`, `ownerAccount`) read it unchanged. `gcIo.ts`'s loader reads the billing job's bills, payments and waivers, and its promises through `list_job_payment_promises` (the Pipeline's reader; the table itself is dev-only).
- **The window** (`GcBillMoneyIn.tsx`, the prototype's row): on each certified bill the due chip (`payDueWords`: *promised* or *expected Oct 26*, red *late · …, 3 days ago*), what came, **Mark paid $X**, **They paid part…**, **They said when…** (the day, how they told us, who said it) and the newest promise with any missed days. Once paid, *paid $X Oct 28*, and **Make our unconditional waiver** for each payment that has none. **Where we stand** lists the late bills. A payment on the billing job that names no bill is one line, *A payment of $X on Oct 3 on the billing job names no bill.*, never laid on a pay application.
- **The presses:** `recordGcPayment` is `mark_invoice_paid` on the app's day (left out, it stamps UTC's), its `{ error }` answers in words (`paymentRefusalWords`, `src/lib/gc/moneyIn.ts`). `recordGcPromise` is `add_job_payment_promise` on the billing job. The money team passes both: `is_assistant()` has counted the controller since July, so the controller records promises too. Our unconditional waiver is the Pipeline's `LienReleaseModal` on the bill (`unconditional_progress`, or `unconditional_final` on the final pay application), whose prefill already counts the payments on it.
- **Tests:** the mapper's money (payments in order, a part payment, a write-down, promises in the app's day, a promise after the paid day, a payment naming no bill, interest bills), `moneyIn.test.ts`, and the window's render test (the due and late chips, Mark paid, both forms, the unconditional waiver, the unnamed payment).
