# v2.5677 — The move window: a charge moves onto a job account only while it is current (2026-10-20)

Migration `20261020130000_supply_house_invoice_move_requests.sql` (fragment in
`docs/migrations/`); edge function `request-job-account-move` (deploy after the push). Punch
list **#132** PR 3 of 3 (`to-dos/ap-aging-cell-invoices/`); builds on v2.5673's cell modal
and v2.5675's chips. The owner's rule and ask, 2026-10-20: *"we can only add [invoices] to
job accounts while they are in the 'current' column… a modal workflow where the person
doing it should check a box that they called [the house] and the item was moved OR … send
an email from the app requesting the move where we then follow up with a call."*

## What shipped

- **Kernels** [`supplyHouseAging.ts`](../../src/lib/supplyHouseAging.ts):
  `movableCandidates` (unpaid, positive, unflagged, due today or later — the window edge is
  the due date, tested on it), `isMovableAgingInvoice` (… on exactly one job),
  `moveStripSummary` (the money, the asks out, the soonest window). `SUPPLY_MOVE_ASK_AGE`
  (3/7 days) joins the app's one waiting convention in [`ageState.ts`](../../src/lib/ageState.ts).
- **The strip** on Accounts payable (desktop, office): the still-movable dollars and count,
  the asks awaiting the house, the soonest closing window, **Work the list ›**; a quiet ✓
  line when every current charge that belongs on an account is on one.
- **The worked list** — the cell modal's `movable` mode: every movable charge grouped by
  house in the matrix's order, the house's job-accounts rep (name · phone) on the group line,
  **✉ Email <rep> the list ›** per house, the footer counting *to ask · asked · dollars*.
- **The move modal**, opened by a movable row's **+ Job acct** (one job, still current —
  past-due rows keep PR 2's band and one-tap recording): the charge's facts and days left in
  its window; the (job, house) account state with the shared **Mark opened…** sheet; the
  attestation — *"I called <house> — this charge was moved onto the job account"* — gating
  **Mark it moved ✓** (the flag flips and the ask is confirmed; a pure phone move writes its
  own confirmed `via='phone'` row); or the previewed email and **Send the request ›**. A sent
  ask turns the row's chip *asked <date> · follow up ☎*, aged by `SUPPLY_MOVE_ASK_AGE`, with
  **Send the email again ›** inside the modal.
- **The table** `supply_house_invoice_move_requests` — one open ask per invoice (partial
  unique index), office-only RLS, a stamp trigger, the three standard write-block guards.
  Types hand-extended in `src/types/database.ts` (regen after the push). The client's asks
  read is fail-soft, so the client can deploy ahead of the push.
- **The edge function** `request-job-account-move`: one house per call, "do what is ready"
  (held reasons returned), one email grouped by job from `COMPANY_EMAIL_FROM` with the sender
  as Reply to, catalog id `job_account_move_request`, filed as a sent copy.
  `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` has the contract.
- **Guide**: *mark an invoice on a job account* gains **The move window**.
- **The sweeps the repo runs on every send**: the email body lives in
  [`_shared/jobAccountMoveEmail.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/jobAccountMoveEmail.ts)
  so Settings → What customers see renders exactly what the function sends (the v2.3512
  rule) — the house journey gains the **Move request email** step and the sample; the send is
  filed as a sent copy; the dev-mcp catalog was regenerated for the new table and function.

## Apply

`supabase db push` after the merge, then `supabase functions deploy request-job-account-move`.
The client is live first and degrades cleanly (no asks shown, the send returns the function's
error) until both land.
