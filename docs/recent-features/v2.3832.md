# v2.3832 — Dashboard: assistants see the Payroll line in the Payables list (2026-09-25)

The "suspected display gap" of [`DASHBOARD_SECTIONS_ARCHITECTURE.md`](../DASHBOARD_SECTIONS_ARCHITECTURE.md) (Financials, and extraction step 14's starting case), found by the 2026-09-25 map sweep. Client only, no migration.

## The bug

The AP drill-down in [`DashboardFinancialsSection.tsx`](../../src/components/DashboardFinancialsSection.tsx) builds its sections by key prefix — Team payroll `stub:`, Sub labor `sublabor:`, Supplies `supply:`. An assistant never gets per-person pay: the modal is handed `redactApPayrollItems(data.ap)`, and the hook builds the bucket with `buildApBucketFromAggregates` when stubs are out of reach — both emit the team's pay as **one** `payroll:aggregate` line ([`lib/dashboardFinancials.ts`](../../src/lib/dashboardFinancials.ts)). That key matched no section, so an assistant's drill-down had no Payroll rows while the card headline and the footer total (the bucket's) still counted it — the rows did not add up to the total.

## The change

- New kernel `partitionApDrillItems(items)` in [`lib/apPayrollGroups.ts`](../../src/lib/apPayrollGroups.ts) → `{ teamPayroll, subLabor, supplies }`; Team payroll takes `stub:` **and** `payroll:aggregate` (`AP_PAYROLL_AGGREGATE_KEY`). `upcoming:` stays out — it joins as its own "(estimate)" section, as before.
- The modal's AP sections read the partition. The aggregate renders as a plain row (the per-person grouping passes a single item through) with its "N open pay reports" sublabel and no payroll-ledger door (that door is `stub:`-only, and assistants lack pay access). When it is the aggregate, the section count reads *1 line*, not *1 week*.

## Verified

- `apPayrollGroups.test.ts` (+4): per-person weeks land in Team payroll; both assistant producers (the redacted bucket and the aggregates bucket) keep their one Payroll line and every counted dollar sits in a section; the upcoming estimate stays out on both paths. `dashboardFinancials.test.ts` unchanged and green.
- Typecheck, lint. Not checked signed in as an assistant (no assistant session in this environment) — the partition is pure and pinned by the tests above.
