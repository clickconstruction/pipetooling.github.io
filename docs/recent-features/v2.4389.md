# v2.4389 — Takeoffs: every bid prices its materials one way (2026-10-01)

Punch list #77, PR 1 of 3. Grace, 2026-10-01: Combined's stage boxes make By Stage redundant. The owner the same day, on the plan: "deeply check the work, I don't trust it", then build it, and ask *is this the best we can do?* #78 went first (v2.4388), so every copy of a Combined takeoff keeps its stage boxes.

## The check before the build
The plan's claims were read against the code and, where a screen could show it, against prod through the app.
- **By Stage is a second store, not a view.** Confirmed in `useBidPricingEngine`: three loads branch on the model, and the By Stage branch reads `bids_takeoff_template_mappings` and prices only from the three POs on `cost_estimates.purchase_order_id_*`. The retired switch window said it itself: *By Stage and Combined data are stored separately. Switching does not copy lines from the other mode.*
- **Nothing built since the summer reads By Stage.** Confirmed by table: the Cover Letter's stages (`materialsByStageIo`), Submittals (`takeoffCandidatesIo`), the flow strip (`useBidFlowFacts`) and the version cards (`usePricingCardsData`) read part lines only.
- **The one thing only By Stage did** is make purchase orders from a takeoff (`createPOFromTakeoff`, `addTakeoffToExistingPO`). No Combined screen does. That door closes with this PR.
- **In SQL** only `bid_estimate_breakdown` and `bid_pricing_history` read the stage POs (POs first, else the lines), and nothing reads `bids.materials_model` but the duplicate, which copies it. No view reads either.
- **Readers the plan did not list:** `useTakeoffRoughLines.ts`, `useTakeoffPartsCatalog.ts` (both gate on the model), `Bids.tsx`'s raw `materials_model === 'exact'` for the version picker, and twin-mcp's count of both takeoff tables.
- **Live, on the dev server against prod:** B403 (By Stage, no POs) read $0 materials and an 82% margin before and after. B159 reads its $14.96 of parked lines. B82 read $27,734 of materials from two POs on a $27,000 bid, and now reads no materials with *4 fixtures carry no material cost*.
- **Not recounted here:** the company-wide numbers (151 bids flagged, one made since May, five POs ever). This session had no direct database access. Two earlier sessions measured them independently (`docs/TAKEOFFS_REFRESH_PLAN.md`, the #77 plan) and agree.

## What changed
- **The pills are gone** from Takeoffs (`BidsTakeoffTab`) and Labor (`BidsLaborTab`), with their caption.
- **The *Switch materials model?* window is gone** (`Bids.tsx`), with `openMaterialsModelSwitch`, `confirmMaterialsModelSwitch` and their state in `useBidPricingEngine`. The engine no longer takes `setSharedBid`.
- **`normalizeMaterialsModel` reads every bid as `'rough'`.** A bid still flagged By Stage opens on One at a time or the Sheet with its counts, and Pricing, Labor, the cost estimate print and the Approval PDF read its part lines. The By Stage branches still compile and are unreachable. PR 2 deletes them.
- **The version picker's By-Stage caveat is gone** (`BidVersionPicker`), with its prop.
- **Guides:** *choose By Stage or Combined for a bid's materials* is deleted. *import a takeoff from CountTooling*, *raise a purchase order*, *fill a takeoff from the book*, *cost a takeoff one fixture at a time* and *create an assembly while doing a takeoff* lose their By Stage lines. *get started as an estimator* is rewritten in plain words and leaves the legacy list.

## Tests
Thirteen tests pinned By Stage's behaviour. Each now pins the new rule: a bid flagged `exact`, or with no model, reads as Combined and never reads a stage PO (`bidMaterialsIo`, `costEstimatePage`, `approvalPdf`, `usePricingCardsData`, `bidTakeoffHelpers`, and four `BidsTakeoffTab` smokes that also check there are no Materials pills). `npx vitest run src/components/bids src/lib/bids src/lib/bidDocuments src/hooks src/pages/Bids`: 354 files green.
