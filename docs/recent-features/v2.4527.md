# v2.4527 — Pipeline: the section header buttons wear the menu's icons (2026-10-04)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. v2.4524 gave the ☰ Section tools menu line icons and left the section headers' own buttons on their emoji, so one action had two marks. The owner asked for the headers to match.

## What changed

- **Eleven marks** in [`JobsStagesTab.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsStagesTab.tsx) are now `<StagesToolsMenuGlyph name=… inherit />`: *Ready to Bill notifications* (bell); on Billed Awaiting Payment *GC Review* (building, was the GC hard hat), *Accounts Receivable* (bank), *Share / Print* (share), *Chart* (bar chart), *Payment forecast* (calendar with bars), *Paid notifications* (bell); the Collections header's *Lien desk* (the orange gavel); on Paid in Full *Chart* and *Paid in Full notifications*; and the stage bar's *Back to board* pill (history).
- **`inherit`** on [`StagesToolsMenuGlyph`](../../src/components/jobs/StagesToolsMenuGlyph.tsx): beside a button's own label the icon takes the button's text color, so it follows a pressed or disabled look; in the menu it stays `var(--text-muted)`. The gavel is orange in both. The glyph wrapper is `aria-hidden` and does not shrink.
- **Not changed:** the Collections header's *⚖ Legal* button (the Legal desk is not in the menu and has no pick), the money view's *⏱ lien notices due* card, the phone ⋯ sheets, and the GC hard hat everywhere else it marks a GC.

## Tests

`JobsStagesTab.render.test.tsx` (+1): the headers' buttons draw, in board order, bell · building · bank · share · bar chart · calendar · bell · gavel · bar chart · bell, and none still carries one of the retired emoji.
