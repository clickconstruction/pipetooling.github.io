# v2.2677 — The Bridge: dev-only Chart Table on live data (2026-09-03)

The ship's-computer idea, built after two rounds of mock-ups ("The Bridge" console → "Bridge Wireframes" → "The Chart Table", owner-approved). Navigation, not instrumentation: position, speed, destination, hazards, course corrections. One clock: days. Dev-only preview at `/bridge` (route registered; the page `Navigate`s non-devs to `/dashboard` — `layoutRouteAccess` already admits dev/master/assistant to every path, so the gate is in the page).

## Client only — no migration (target lives in `app_settings` as a row, `bridge_target_usd_v1`)

- **Earned revenue kernel** [`lib/bridge/earnedRevenue.ts`](../../src/lib/bridge/earnedRevenue.ts) (+4 tests): labor-weighted percentage of completion — each approved field hour earns contract ÷ expected hours; expected hours = lifetime hours ÷ pct_complete (finished statuses = 100%; no pct → assumed 50%, reported in `assumedHalfJobs`); no contract $ → earns $0, reported in `noRevenueJobs`. The first earned-value number in the app.
- **Course model** [`lib/bridge/courseModel.ts`](../../src/lib/bridge/courseModel.ts) (+5 tests): daily earned/direct/overhead → cumulative net track, trailing-14-day speed (earned, direct, overhead, burn, climb), window contribution margin, projection at the climb rate bent by levers (`ratePerDay` from an offset, `onceUsd` at an offset), target measured from the track end, verdict (makes/misses/no-target + underwater days). `bidWinLever` sizes "win this bid" as bid_value × contribution margin over 60 days from its estimated start.
- **Loader** [`lib/bridge/loadBridgeData.ts`](../../src/lib/bridge/loadBridgeData.ts): 56 days back. Overhead/day + field labor/day + hygiene from the shared `loadOverheadPoolSnapshot` (extended with `poolUsdByDay` / `fieldLaborUsdByDay` / `fieldHoursByDay`); approved field sessions + lifetime hours per touched job; materials = Mercury allocations to non-office jobs by posted day + supply invoices × allocation pct by invoice date; sub labor = sheet items by job date; levers = Ready-to-bill revenue, collections open balance, open bids due ≤14 days; hazards = unpaid supply invoices by due date; hygiene counts via the two queue RPCs; target from `app_settings`.
- **Page** [`pages/Bridge.tsx`](../../src/pages/Bridge.tsx) + [`components/bridge/BridgeCourseChart.tsx`](../../src/components/bridge/BridgeCourseChart.tsx): Speed / Fuel (via `useDashboardFinancials`: AR, AP, not billed; cash runway explicitly "not wired" — no Mercury balance in the DB) / Engine instruments; the course chart (track solid, projection dashed, destination dotted, ▼ hazards, per-day tooltips); tickable corrections; hazards list; HULL strip (unapproved hours, unsorted transfers/cards, assumed-half jobs, no-$ jobs); destination input saved to `app_settings`; a "how the track is drawn" footer with the window totals.
- Verified live as dev on prod data. Help guide `the-bridge.md` (dev role) ships with it.

## Known limits (stated on the page)

Cash runway needs a Mercury balance sync. Hazards cover supply-house due dates only (payroll cadence, recurring overhead, jobs finishing are next). Earned revenue is labor-weighted, not cost-to-cost. The projection is a line, not a cone.
