# v2.6510 — A locked bid's letter and base price say what was sent (2026-11-04)

An estimator's report on a bid due the same day: *the robot locked my numbers and I need to return to the original price I had sent the bid at.* The bid had gone out on Oct 19, the Pricing tab read eleven thousand dollars lower the week after, and the lock chip said *pricing locked*. The lock (v2.3591) holds every write to the bid's prices, but the number itself was never held: the grid and the letter recompute from the bid's price copy, and the sent-vs-today line (v2.3484) only says both figures. She pressed Revise and chased the sent figure by hand, landing short.

## What changed

- **Kernel** [`lib/bids/lockedQuote.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/lockedQuote.ts) (5 tests): `quoteToShow({ lockState, bidValue, live })` answers the sent figure (`bids.bid_value`, stamped at send) while the lock holds and one was stamped, else the live figure; `sentValueOf` reads the column; `sentTagWords` is the ★ card's *as sent*.
- **Pricing tab** (`BidsPricingTab.tsx`): the ★ card's amount is the sent figure while locked, with *· as sent* on its footer (`PricingCardsRow`'s `sentTag`); the strip, the rows and the sent-vs-today line stay live, so *Sent Oct 19 at $X · the book prices it at $Y today* still says how far the book moved.
- **Cover Letter** (`BidsCoverLetterTab.tsx`): the headline — and so the letter's amount in words and figures, Print, Copy and the room — reads the sent figure while locked. A custom amount wins as before, an options letter keeps the lead option's ★ (the bid's value is never the letter's sum), and Revise… reads live. The tab's sent-vs-today line compares the live figure and reads *the book prices it at $Y today* while the letter says the sent one.
- Guide *price a bid with the workbench*: two sentences under the lock. `docs/BIDS_SYSTEM.md`'s lock clause.

## Not changed

The per-row prices: `price_book_entries` keeps no history, so a sent bid's rows cannot be rebuilt (v2.3484's reason). Why this bid's recomputation fell is not settled — its copy's entries carry no updated-at, and a drawer edit on a bid-owned copy is not gated by the lock (v2.3591 gates the eleven pricing writes); that gap is a card of its own.
