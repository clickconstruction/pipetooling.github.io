# v2.3322 — Legal portal PR 4: the firm's portal, write side (2026-09-11)

No migration (the v2.3313 entries table and RPCs carry it). **New edge function `submit-legal-portal`** (`verify_jwt = false`; deploy with `legal-portal`). Stacked on v2.3319 (PR 3). Fourth of five.

## Why

PR 3 let the firm read; the owner's flow has the firm adding its fees, recording what it did, reporting money it received, and asking the office — without ever marking anything paid or touching a job — and the office seeing each of those on Needs You.

## What shipped

- **[`submit-legal-portal`](../../supabase/functions/submit-legal-portal/index.ts)** — kinds `fee` · `cost` · `step` · `question` · `payment_received`; one `legal_matter_entries` row each with `via_portal = true`; a `step` also moves `legal_matters.stage` (`settled` closes). Honeypot, matter-belongs-to-firm and with-firm checks, 30 acts per firm per hour, amount and body caps. `config.toml` entry.
- **Portal page** — the four forms on Fees & steps (`FirmActs`), reload after each act, the notice line; the firm's own entries show *waiting on the office* / *seen*.
- **Kernel** `buildFirmActivity` (+2 tests): unacknowledged portal entries → counts by kind, totals, and the account the card opens on (a payment first, then a question).
- **Dashboard** — `legal-firm-activity` Needs You item (office roles, tier 20; amber when a payment or question waits): "The law firm has N things for you" → `/jobs?tab=stages&legal=<payer key>&legalTab=fees`. Hook [`useLegalFirmActivityNudge`](../../src/hooks/useLegalFirmActivityNudge.ts).
- **Desk** — Fees & steps lists the firm's entries with **Answer…** (inline; `legal_add_entry` kind `answer` + acknowledge), **Acknowledge**, and for a payment received **Mark Paid on the row ↗** then **Mark applied** (`recovery_applied` entry + the firm's contingency as a `cost` entry on the matter + acknowledge). `initialTab` / `&legalTab=fees` deep link.
- Help guides (both), EDGE_FUNCTIONS, ACCESS_CONTROL.

## Verified

Legal suites, Needs You kernel, typecheck, lint, theme. Live after deploy: the firm adds a $450 fee and asks a question → the office card counts 2, the desk answers and acknowledges → the card clears; a payment received → Mark applied writes the recovery and the cut.

## Notes for the next session

- The firm's contingency is recorded on the **matter** (a `cost` entry), not as a job cost line — the job's cost surfaces run through cost batches, and a legal-cost line there is a follow-up once a real recovery exists.
- Applying a payment is still two taps (Mark Paid on the row, Mark applied on the desk); a one-tap apply that opens Mark Paid prefilled from the entry is the next refinement.
- PR 5 adds the firm's people and their email rules; until then the handling person on the firm row is the only address.
