# v2.5034 — Robots: travel is $0.70 a mile, round trip, once per job day (2026-10-09)

Docs and a data migration, plus `twin-mcp` (**redeploy after merge**, the briefs regenerated). The owner's call of **2026-10-09**, sent by Punchlist: travel in the robot book is $0.70 a mile, round trip, once per job day. It replaces the interim 10% cap, and the travel-bands question closes.

## What changed

- **`docs/twins/PLACEMENT.md`**, the doctrine `get_placement_guide` serves. The travel bullet now says the robot rows the book's `Travel & Rentals (per mile from office)` entry once, at count = miles from the office × job days.
  - The book prices a count at $1.40, which is $0.70 both ways, so the row reads $0.70 × miles × 2 × job days.
  - Job days come from the bid's schedule. Otherwise the robot estimates them from the labor, says how, and never uses fewer than 1.
  - Worked example: 45 mi and 12 job days rows count 540 and carries **$756**. Brownsville at 293 mi carries $410.20 a job day, where the old $80 rate had put $23,464 on that ~$49k proto.
  - The lock note states the miles, the job days and the line.
  - Rentals stay a human line, and incidentals and DSC rows stay never.
  - The interim rule, the lesser of $80 × miles and 10% of the building, is gone, and so is its PENDING bands question.
- **The robot book** (migration `20261010004000_robot_book_travel_rate.sql`, its doc `docs/migrations/20261010004000_robot_book_travel_rate.md`): the 🤖 Robot Default book's travel entry goes from $80.00 to **$1.40**.
  - The name is unchanged, so past bids' rows still match.
  - Each bid's own robot copy keeps its price.
  - Punchlist pushes it.
- **`docs/twins/LEARNING_PLAN.md`**: the standing-rulings bullet now says travel bands closed on 2026-10-09, and where the rule lives.
- **`docs/twins/FEEDBACK_LOOP.md`**: the 2026-09-04 audit's list notes that travel was re-ruled.
- **`supabase/functions/twin-mcp/briefs.ts`**, regenerated with `node scripts/build-twin-mcp-briefs.mjs`.
  - The run also carries `docs/twins/APP_DIRECTORY.md`'s GC-mode lines, which had not been regenerated: the Project Board, Trade partners, Follow up, Ask for quotes, Compare quotes, Our number, Bid tabs, Change orders, and the `/t/:token` trade partner portal.
