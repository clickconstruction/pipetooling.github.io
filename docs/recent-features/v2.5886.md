# v2.5886 — Prospects: who is past their day, not how many there are (2026-10-26)

PR 3 of punch list #180 (`to-dos/quickfill-read-state/`). The station reported every active prospect as its count, 268 at every one of its 32 marks in eight weeks and 107 marks all time without a zero, and asked on its banner whether every prospect heard from us on time; nothing on it knew a prospect's day.

- **The read** (`lib/quickfill/prospectsDue.ts`): the active customer prospects (`kind = customer`, not can't reach, not a fit or converted, `isActiveProspect`) with `last_contact`, `warmth_count` and `created_at`, plus `prospect_callbacks` from the last `PROSPECT_CALLBACK_LOOKBACK_DAYS` (90). A prospect is **missed** when a promised callback is past and nothing has touched them since (a kept callback has a later `last_contact`), **past** when its last touch, or its arrival when never touched, is older than the days for its warmth, and **on time** otherwise. `prospectDueRows` sorts missed, past, on time, most over first; each row says its words (*promised Fri, Oct 23 · 3 days over · last touch 10d ago*, *never touched · added 120d ago · due every 60 days · 60 days over*).
- **The owner's days per warmth** are one `app_settings` row, `quickfill_prospect_days_v1`, JSON `{ w3, w2, w1, w0 }` with `PROSPECT_DAYS_DEFAULT` 7 · 14 · 30 · 60 (`parseProspectDays` keeps a default for a missing or out-of-range field; 4 and up follow warmth 3), changed by a dev from the station's stance line.
- **The station** (`QuickfillProspectsSection`, now taking `lastMarkedAt`): the count line *N past their day · N promised and missed · N on time of N* with **Follow Up →**; three folds, the first two open, each row with its days-over chip, its warmth, its words and **Open →** into the Prospect List; the warmth buckets and the team's 30-day chart under **On time**; *Since the last look* (`prospectsSince`: crossed past since the mark, touched since); the stance line with the four numbers. The metric is missed plus past (`prospectDueCounts.due`), the Next line `prospectsNextLine` (*Call Westlake Custom Homes · Dana Whitfield · promised Fri, Oct 23 · 3 days over*), the Mark's note `prospectsMarkNote`.

Tests: `prospectsDue.test.ts` (the setting, warmth to days, the active filter, the order, the words, a callback not yet due and one kept, the counts, the Next line, the since line, the note) and `QuickfillProspectsSection.render.test.tsx` (the folds and the metric over mocked prospects; the owner's days moving a prospect between folds).
