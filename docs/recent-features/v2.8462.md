# v2.8462 — Signing a paper: the press on the thank-you page takes a finger (2026-11-24)

A fault punch list #389's walk of a sub with no login found on prod on 2026-11-24: on the signing page's thank-you, **Back to your page** measured 40 px tall at 375 px. Client only: no migration and nothing to deploy.

## What changed

- `contractThankYouPrimaryLinkStyle` (`src/pages/ContractAccept.tsx`) is the one style of every press the thank-you can show: **Back to your page**, **Back to the job**, **Sign in**, **Complete the office section**, **Go to dashboard** and **Return to ClickTooling**. It was an inline block with 0.5rem of padding, which came to 40 px.
- It is now an inline flex box at least 44 px tall, with its words centred. Its colour, its width and its place on the page are as they were.

## Tested

- `ContractAccept.clauses.render.test.tsx`: both ways back, a sub's **Back to your page** and a signed-in person's **Back to the job**, read 44 px.
- Not measured again on the live site: that needs a paper required on a job, which shuts the job to its roster. The next walk of a job's papers measures it.
