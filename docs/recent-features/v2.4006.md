# v2.4006 — GC Review: a stage with nobody waiting reads “0 waiting” (2026-09-28)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Found on the first run of v2.3997 against the week's real data, on a Monday with every GC still at Check.

## What was wrong

- `GcStageTrack` draws a stop's count as a number and its words, and took the words from `gcStageStopLabel` with the leading number stripped. The idle label, "none waiting", has no number to strip, so the stop read "0 none waiting". The button's `aria-label` was right, and the render test only read the `aria-label`.
- The panel was as tall as the open tab. Scheduled with two sends is a third the height of This week, and the window is centred, so changing tabs moved every edge of it.

## What shipped

- `GcStageTrack.tsx` — an idle stop's words are "waiting". `gcStageStopLabel` is unchanged.
- `src/index.css` — `.gcReviewPanel` is a column with `min-height: min(85vh, 36rem)` and its body takes the slack (`flex: 1 0 auto`), so a short tab keeps the foot at the panel's bottom edge.

## Verified

- `GcStageTrack.render.test.tsx` (5, one new) reads the drawn text of a waiting, an idle and a finished stop.
- In the app (dev login, read-only) on the week's data: the stops read "14 to check", "0 waiting", "0 waiting"; the panel is 765px on This week, 697px on Temperature and 576px on Scheduled (it was about 250px), with the foot on the bottom edge in each; the track and the foot stay in place while the list scrolls.
