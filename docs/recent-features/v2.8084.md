# v2.8084 — Send for signature: a refusal shows beside the Send press (2026-11-20)

Found by the audit of punch list #403. No migration and no function. One component and its render test.

## What was wrong

The Send for signature window (`src/components/contracts/ContractSendWindow.tsx`) drew its error right under the heading, at the top of a dialog that scrolls (`maxHeight: min(92vh, 100%)`, `overflow: auto`). The **Send email** press is at the foot, below the fields and a 420px email preview.

So when the office pressed **Send email** and the window refused, the words landed 400 to 800px above what was on screen and the press looked dead. Every refusal went there: no signer name ("Enter the name of the person who signs."), a bad address ("Enter a valid email address."), an address that is someone on the roster, and a send that failed.

Confirmed on the running app at 320, 390 and 1280px wide. With the dialog showing its foot, the alert's box was at y = -522 on a 390px phone and y = -392 at 1280x900.

## What changed

The one alert moved to the foot, directly above the row that holds **Cancel** and **Send email**, and left its place under the heading. It keeps `role="alert"`, the colour `var(--text-red-700)` and its words, and takes a small top margin so it does not touch the preview's caption.

Nothing else changed: no wording, no send logic, no props. The window's three hosts only pass props, so People → Contracts, and Documents → Agreements' **Send a paper** and **Send again**, all get the fix.

## Test

`ContractSendWindow.render.test.tsx` gains *draws a refusal at the foot, after the email preview and right above the Send email press*. It empties Signer name, presses **Send email**, and holds the alert after the preview's frame and before the **Send email** button in document order, with one alert drawn. It failed before the change and passes after.
