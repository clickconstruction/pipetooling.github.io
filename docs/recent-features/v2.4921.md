# v2.4921 — GC mode: a trade partner's own window, for a dev (2026-10-08)

The Board's B3-c (`to-dos/gc-mode/BOARD_REAL_BUILD.md`, on branch `spike/gc-mode`): the company window's About on real data at `/gc`, dev only. Guide: `look-up-a-trade-partner`.

- **Lifted word for word** with `to-dos/gc-mode/scripts/board-b3-c.lift.json` (branch `spike/board-b3-c-config`). `lift-same.cjs` reads every declaration the same.
  - `src/lib/gc/companyPeople.ts`: `companyPeople`, `mailGroupName`, `mailGroupList`.
  - `partnerWork`, appended to `companyFile.ts`.
  - `followItemMailGroup` stays on the spike for B2b.
- **`GcCompanyWindow.tsx`** is ported from the spike's window, with About alone.
  - The header has the vetting and answer chips, the trades, the contact, a phone link and the email. A company with no email shows *no email on file*.
  - The **Language** pick writes `gc_companies.lang` (`setGcCompanyLanguage`).
  - About shows Quotes, Vetting, Drives from and Insurance (`insuranceRenewals`), then **Who gets our emails** (`companyPeople`).
  - A contact the kernel marks `madeUp` shows *no email on file*, never the prototype's made-up address.
  - Then come the stats and **Their work with us** when there is an award (`partnerWork`), and **Times they passed** (`partnerDeclines`).
  - Activity, Documents and Their portal wait for their kernels.
- **`gcCompanyOpener.ts`** (a context) and **`GcPartnerName.tsx`** let a company's name open the window. The name shows on Trade partners' lines and New to us rows, on a project's trade asks and on Follow up cards. Outside the page the name stays plain text.
- **`boardRows.ts`** maps `gc_company_people` (not removed) onto `Partner.people`, and `gc_companies.contact_gets` onto `Partner.contactGets`. `loadGcBoardRows` reads both. The language comes from the rows' `lang`, as the Ask window's does, so `Partner` gains no field.
