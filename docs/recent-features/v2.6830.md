# v2.6830 — Permits: skim one permit at a time, best first, with two answers (punch list #302, PR 1 and 2) (2026-11-10)

Step one of the owner's ask for Prospects → Permits: a card with everything needed to decide, and two answers. Built on today's tables, so there is no migration and nothing to deploy. The kernel and the screen ship together, since the owner asked for the whole first build live in one night.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/research/permitSkim.ts`** (new, pure, `permitSkim.test.ts`): `skimDeck` (every permit with no verdict and no plumbing permit on it, above the value floor, with no window), `skimScore` (best first: our history with the name, a builder's mark once builders exist, the declared value, the kind of work, the miles, how old it is, and the words that say why a card sits where it does), `milesFromOffice` (the straight line times the road-winding factor), `filedWords`, `skimTitle`, `skimMoney`, `skimVerdict` (Keep writes `look`, Pass writes `not_for_us`, each with words the next reader can read), `PASS_REASONS`, `skimKeyAct`, and the caught-up words.
- **`PermitSkimDeck`** (new, `src/components/prospects/`): the card, the Up next column on a desk, the two answers, the dark bar (the pass reasons and Undo), the caught-up state with **Dig in**. An answer is held `SKIM_HOLD_MS` (15 seconds) before `recordVerdict` writes it, and is written at once on the next answer, when the tab is hidden and when the deck closes; a write that fails names the permit, says why and puts the card back. The county's owner of record is asked for the top card through the cached `permit-lookup` call. On a phone the two answers stick to the foot of the screen over the app's bottom bar, and the bar sits above the card.
- **`PermitBuildingSheet`, `WhoPulledItSheet`**: an `answers` prop puts Pass and Keep at the foot when the sheet is opened from the card.
- **`ProspectsResearchTab`**: a Skim · Ledger · Map switch that opens on Skim; the window, Showing and Include houses controls show on the ledger and the map only; the Pulled line reads the pull time alone on Skim (`pulledAtWords`), since the lens counts are the window's.
- **One set of words** (`VERDICT_WORDS`, `RESEARCH_SHOWING_WORDS`, `pulledWords`): Keep, Pass, Pursue, Has a plumber. The stored verdict values are unchanged.
- Guide `research-permits-for-work-to-bid`: a Skim section, and the four words throughout.

## Walked live

2026-11-10, the dev login on localhost, desk and 375 wide, nothing written: the deck opened on 340 permits to skim (every one the feed has brought, where the 30-day ledger showed 163), best first with the why line; Keep and Undo by button; Pass, a reason and Undo by key. On the phone the Keep press first sat under the fold at 973 of 812; the sticky foot put it at 728, above the bottom bar at 752.
