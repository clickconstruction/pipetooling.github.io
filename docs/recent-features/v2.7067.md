# v2.7067 — Tasks: add a place to a task while you stand at it (2026-11-12)

Punch list #328, PR 8 of 8, the last of the train. The first pass at the design counted what people already skip in this app: *Attach this location* on 2 of 211 Task Dispatch requests, no box with a spot out of 102. The givers are four people at desks. The people standing on a task's spot are the people doing it, and until now they could not say where it was.

## What changed

- **One quiet line in an open task with no place**: *Standing at this task? Add its place* (`src/components/checklist/TaskPlaceAddHere.tsx`), on the Today tab's cards and the Outstanding rows. A closed task shows nothing of it, since a task with no place must not look unfinished, and a finished task and a task that has a place never show it.
- **The press** asks the phone where it is, once. The answer is shown before anything is kept: *Found you. Your phone is sure within 20 ft.*, a box for the name, and what will be saved in words, *This saves where you stand as the task's place. The app does not follow you after.* Then **Add this place**.
- **A reading too wide is not kept at all.** Past about 150 ft the line says how wide it was and offers **Try again**. The giver's sheet lets a wide reading be fixed by dragging a map. This press has no map, so it has no way to fix one.
- **The write** is one function, `add_task_place_from_the_spot` (`20261113010000_task_place_from_the_spot.sql`), since the person doing a task cannot edit it. It holds every rule again on the server: the office or an assignee, never a training account, a twin or an archived account, a margin within 46 m, and never over a place that is already there. A roadmap task's place lands on the roadmap task.
- **Only a missing place.** Moving a place that is wrong stays with whoever gave the task: the doer says so in a note. That was one of the card's open calls and this is its cautious answer.

## Checked on a database

The function was applied twice to a throwaway local Postgres 15 with stub tables and called as eight made-up people, 27 checks, all passing on the first run: every kind of person refused in its own words, every bad reading refused, nothing refused writing anything, a second press unable to move a place, and `anon` unable to call it.
