# v2.4451 — Bids on a phone: the title line stays inside the card (2026-10-02)

The owner, after v2.4448: "Help me deal with Phone title line: the × and Mark buttons run off the right edge on a phone."

## What was wrong
Measured headless at 375 × 812 on every bid workflow tab (the nine detail tabs and the Followup view), on BP403, BP398 and BP483, against prod:
- **The × covered the first line of the card on all nine tabs.** It floated 0.75rem in from the corner, over whatever came first: the bid's name, or the *Build* step when the full step strip was open.
- **Pricing's title could not wrap.** Its `h2Style` held the heading at full width (`flex: 0 0 auto`, carried over from the May 2026 decomposition). BP403's title row was 470 px wide in a 343 px card, BP483's 826 px. *Mark* and *For someone…* ran off the card, and the page scrolled sideways: BP403's Pricing by 144 px, 75 of them from this row.
- **Pricing's *Share with a teammate* split button** needs about 390 px and could not shrink: its group was `flex: 0 0 auto` without wrap, and *Supply house prices (RFQ)* was `nowrap`.

## What changed
- [`bidDetailCloseFloatMobileStyle`](../../src/lib/bids/bidStyles.ts) puts the phone × at the card's top-right corner (`top: 0`, `right: 0`), 2rem wide and 2.5rem tall, inside the 2rem right padding that every bid card has. Nothing in the card's flow can sit under it. One style, nine tabs.
- [`BidsPricingTab`](../../src/components/bids/BidsPricingTab.tsx): the title has no `h2Style`, so it shrinks and wraps like every other tab's. The right-hand group may shrink to the card and wrap (`flex: 0 1 auto`, `minWidth: 0`).
- [`PricingShareMenu`](../../src/components/bids/PricingShareMenu.tsx): the split button is no wider than its row (`maxWidth: 100%`), and the caret's label may wrap. On a phone both labels wrap to two lines inside the green buttons.
- `bidStyles.test.ts`: the × sits in the corner; the nine cards that float it each keep a right padding at least as wide as the ×; and no host holds the bid title at full width. Each check fails when its part of the bug is put back.
- [`scripts/bids-live-walk.mjs`](../../scripts/bids-live-walk.mjs) gains `phone <out> [bids]`: every bid tab at 375 px, what runs past the card and what sits under the ×, exit 1 when anything does ([E2E_SMOKE.md](../E2E_SMOKE.md)).

## Measured
- **Phone, after:** 30 tab and bid pairs, nothing past the card, nothing under the ×. BP403's Pricing title is 277 × 72 px (two lines) instead of 470 × 36 px.
- **Desktop unchanged:** the header geometry of six tabs on three bids at 1,400, 1,024 and 700 px is identical on main and the branch, 52 of 54 cases. One differed only because "Bids like this" had not loaded yet; reloaded, it matched. The other is the fix: BP483's Pricing at 700 px stopped scrolling sideways by 191 px.

## Not fixed here
The phone check also found pages that scroll sideways because of content lower down: Labor by 188 px (the labor book table's header), RFI and Change order by 105 px (the *Response request date* field), and Pricing by 69–198 px (the *What the bid is made of* tiles). They are not in the title line and were present before.

No migration, no edge function.
