# v2.6474 — A thread on a laptop gives its messages the room (2026-11-03)

The first of three PRs that fold a person's thread by height (the owner's pick A of three mock-ups, 2026-11-03). On a laptop window about 700 px tall, the header took 170 px, the sort card 258 px and the reply box 170 px, leaving the person's own email about 100 px. This PR folds the header; the sort card and the reply box follow.

## What changed

- `isThreadShort` and `THREAD_SHORT_QUERY` (`src/lib/messages/threadFold.ts`): a window under 820 px tall, at a desk only. A phone keeps its own fold (cut 10).
- `ThreadWindow` on a short window: *Who is this?* rides the *An address nobody has named* line as a link-blue press instead of a row of its own; the status chips, the stamps sentence (one line, cut with an ellipsis, the whole sentence in its title) and the jobs strip share one row; their numbers and addresses wait under the arrow as on a narrow column; the window is 100% of the overlay's height in place of `min(92vh, 100%)`; the lines keep `max(7rem, 33%)`.
- The open thread (`OpenThread.short`, `openThread.ts`) says the window is short, so a slot's component can fold itself.
- `JobsRail`'s strip keeps 22 px at its end for its fade, so a last tile that fits no longer looks cut off.
- Guide: *Read a thread on a short screen* in `find-every-message-with-a-person-in-one-place`.

## Decisions

- **820 px**: covers 13 and 14 inch laptops at their default size, where the window is about 700 px under the browser's own bars.
- **Height folds beside width.** A short window that is also narrow takes the narrow fold's header and the short window's rows.

## Test

`npx vitest run src/lib/messages/threadFold.test.ts src/components/gc/messages/`, `npm run typecheck`. Live at 1000×700 on a Bid it? thread: the header 103 px (was 170), the lines 222 px (was about 100).
