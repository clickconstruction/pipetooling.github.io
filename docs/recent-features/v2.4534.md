# v2.4534 — Old payments no longer pay off newer bills (2026-10-05)

Client + two edge functions (`customer-portal`, `gc-statement-email-dispatch`); no migration. The owner's catch on the Lien window for job 273: the § 53.056 notice claimed $17,585 and the demand letter beside it claimed $0.00.

## The cause

Job 273 is a $56,365 job. Three open bills add up to $17,585; four payments totalling $38,780 came in before the first of those bills was sent, none linked to a bill. The notice reads the job (`revenue − payments_made`). The demand letter reads bill by bill through `attributeJobPayments` ([`_shared/paymentAttribution.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/paymentAttribution.ts)), whose one rule since v2.3592 was *oldest bill first*: it spent the $38,780 on the three bills, so each read paid. That money had paid the $38,780 of the job the app never put on a bill.

Two more jobs had the shape (473 Mike Holub $5,723.58, 251 Michael Palmer $4,720.00) — $28,028.58 of open bills read as paid across the three. Job 102, the job the v2.3515 rule was written for, was the same thing in small: its $3,000 check paid the $3,000 of an $8,355 job that its one $5,355 bill left out, and the customer later paid that bill in full.

## The rule now

`attributeJobPayments(bills, payments, jobTotal?)`: with the job's total, unlinked money first pays the part of the job on no sent bill (`jobTotal − Σ sent bills`, reported as `offBill`); what is left walks the sent bills oldest first, as before; what no bill needs is still the `surplus`. Linked money is untouched. No total (or a blank one) is oldest-first alone. On all four jobs this leaves exactly the open bill unpaid, matching the job's balance to the cent.

## Who passes the total

- **Demand letter and Lien window** — `paymentsAppliedToInvoice` reads `job.revenue` ([`demandLetter.ts`](../../src/lib/jobsDocuments/demandLetter.ts)); the claim, the statement of account and which bills a letter can cover follow.
- **The bill's paper** — `PhysicalInvoiceDetailFromJob.jobTotal` → `filterPaymentsForPhysicalInvoiceHistory`.
- **The Bill tab** — `JobFormInvoiceList` and `splitBillsAndPayments` (also `JobFormPaymentsTable`) pass `editing.revenue`; *+ $X on no bill* counts `offBill` with the surplus.
- **Where the checks went / the bill's paid-by line** — `ChecksJobIn.revenue` ([`gcChecksApplied.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/gcChecksApplied.ts)), selected by `gcChecksAppliedIo.ts` and by `gc-statement-email-dispatch`'s `receivedFor`; `billPaidByWords` takes `total`.
- **Customer portal** — `buildPortalBills` / `buildPortalSharedBills` take `sentBills` (the billed and paid bills already read for *Your payments*) and run the rule over that whole list with the job's `revenue`. Until now the portal ran the rule over the open bills alone, so a paid bill never took its unlinked money ahead of an open one. *Your payments* carries a job's `revenue` only when the viewer pays every sent bill on it.

## Left as it was

The GC statement email's *paid by* line under each bill (`render.ts` `rowPaidBy`): its rows come from an RPC that does not carry the job's total, so it still reads oldest-first alone. Giving it the total is a database change.
