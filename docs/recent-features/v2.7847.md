# v2.7847 — Lien desk on a phone: the Board's two prints share one row on every phone (2026-11-18)

Found on the live look at punch list #383 on 2026-11-18, and built on the owner's word the same day: *"Yes, build them"*. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## Before

- A phone keeps the Board's bins folded, so its two prints stand in a row over the fold (v2.7708): *Print and mail 1 notice* and *Print 1 for notarization*.
- The row was a wrapping flex row. On a phone 375 px wide it is 335 px, and the two presses needed 164 + 165 with an 8 px gap, 337. The second fell under the first, each then the full width. From 390 px they shared the row.
- The guide said side by side, and the build had been checked at 390.

## What changed

- **The row is a grid of equal columns, a column a press** (`data-lien-board-prints` in `LienBoard.tsx`): `repeat(N, minmax(0, 1fr))`, so two prints are two halves at any width and one print is the whole row.
- **A press too narrow for its words wraps them inside itself** (`minWidth: 0`, a 1.2 line height, 6 px of side padding where it had 12). It stays 44 px tall or more.

## Files

- `src/components/jobs/LienBoard.tsx`.

## Verified

- `LienBoard.render.test.tsx`: the row is a grid of two equal columns.
- The real Board drawn in a browser over made-up jobs at 320, 360, 375 and 390 px wide, with one-figure and two-figure counts: the two prints share one row at each, 44 px tall, and the page does not scroll sideways.
- The live desk had one print that day, so it could show the row but not the pair: one press, the whole row, at the same four widths.
