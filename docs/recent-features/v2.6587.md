# v2.6587 — The Away note: a teammate who left signs in and finds the one screen the office wrote for them (punch list #256, PR 8) (2026-11-06)

Punch list #256's eighth PR, the third of the Away note's four. PR 6 (v2.6566) is the screen's kernel, PR 7 (v2.6572) and PR 7b (v2.6579) the seal that makes a sealed sign-in safe: the pre-request gate, the fence on every table and on storage, the guard in every edge function. This PR is the note itself: its table and RPC, the window the office writes it in with a live preview, the sealed sign-in that draws the one screen, and the two account functions learning about it. Taunya's call 5 of 2026-11-03: *"When she signs in on the app, it's the only page she can see."*

## What changed

- **Migration `20261106140000_away_notes.sql`** (`docs/migrations/20261106140000_away_notes.md`): `away_notes`, one row per account (the words, the day back, the where and call switches, the alert for PR 9), read by the people who may archive and written by nobody but the service role; `my_away_note()`, the one RPC the gate lets an archived caller reach, answering their first name, the note, the day they left, the office address setting and the office line, null for anyone else. The four fence calls close it.
- **Edge function `away-note`** (new; `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`): `save` upserts the row and, on an archived login, lifts the ban; `clear` deletes it and puts the ban back. The gate `archive-user` keeps, the archived guard, the kernel's rules restated in `readNote`. **`archive-user`** skips the ban when a note is on (`away_note_on` in the answer); **`restore-user`** deletes the note after the unban (`away_note_taken_down`), so Bring back and Restore both take it down.
- **The sealed sign-in.** `useAuth` reads the seal's refusal of the role read (sqlstate `PT403`, `isArchivedCallerRefusal`) as `archivedCaller` and settles instead of retrying. `App`'s `ArchivedCallerGate`, right under the toast and confirm providers, draws `pages/Away.tsx` in place of every other provider and route, so nothing under it reads as them and any path lands on the note. The page reads `my_away_note()` alone, builds `buildAwayScreen` once a minute, and draws it with `components/away/AwayScreen.tsx`; *Use my location* asks the browser only after the press and works the miles out on the phone with the office's pin; a failed read draws on `OfflineRetryPanel`; no note (a session that outlived the note or the ban) rests on *This account is resting.* with Sign out.
- **Leave a note** (`PersonDeskAwayNoteModal`): on an archived desk's Status row (*Leave a note…*, then *Edit the note…* with a blue chip, *Away note on · sign-in opens to it*) and in End employment's footer before the finish, for a dev, a controller or a pay-approved master. The note, the four switches with the office address and the line named beside them, and the preview drawn by the same `AwayScreen` with `preview`, which follows the typing; *Today · On the day* draws the day-of screen; on a phone the preview drops under the switches with a Preview link at the top. The footer says what Save does for this person (`awayNoteFootWords`); *Take the note down* confirms and closes the sign-in again.
- **Kernel** (`lib/people/awayNote.ts`): `parseMyAwayNote`, `awayOfficeFromSettings`, `awayDraftFromRow`, `awayNoteFootWords`, `awayNoteChipWords`, `AWAY_NO_NOTE_WORDS`; the reads and writes in `awayNoteIo.ts`.
- Guide *leave a note for a teammate who is away*; *bring back a teammate who left* and *archive and restore user accounts* point at it. `GLOSSARY.md` gains *Away note*. The office line is the dispatch phone (`field_dispatch_phone_v1`), the one office number the app keeps as a setting.

## Decisions

- **One writer for the row.** A note on an archived login is also an unban, and taking it down a ban; the client never touches `auth.admin`, so the row's writes go through the function and the table has no write policy at all.
- **The gate sits above the providers, not in the router.** The settings and ledger providers read on mount as the signed-in person; under the seal every one of those reads is refused. Drawing the Away page above them means nothing reads as the archived caller but the one RPC.
- **The refusal is an answer.** The role read's PT403 settles the gate at once; a retry loop or the four-second watchdog would have shown an archived teammate a null-role frame before the Away page.
- **The preview's bar stands on today** when the desk does not know the day they left; the person's own screen counts from their roster row's end date, which the RPC answers.
