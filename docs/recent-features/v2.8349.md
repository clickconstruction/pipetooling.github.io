# v2.8349 — Dashboard: swipe a bid left to hide it (2026-11-23)

Step 4 of punch list #426, the shared piece. The owner's ask of 2026-11-23: *instead of seeing this icon, I would like for the user to be able to swipe to the left or right to hide a bid*, and later the same swipe on Recent Reports, *one gesture, designed once, for both*. His pick: A, let go to put away, with Undo. Client only. No migration, no edge function.

## The gesture

Swipe a row to the left. A strip under the row names the act. Past the mark the strip turns blue, and letting go puts the row away. Short of the mark the row springs back. The card says what happened with **Undo**, and keeps what was put away in a folded group at its foot.

- **Left only.** People's phone rows already slide left to show their presses, and the Pipeline's phone rows slide right to move a job forward. Left keeps one meaning. A right swipe does nothing.
- **Scroll and Back.** The row carries `touch-action: pan-y` and the kernel decides swipe or scroll at 6 px (`classifySwipe`), so a touch that moves up or down scrolls the page. The phone's Back swipe starts at the left edge and moves right, so a left swipe never starts it.
- **Hard to do by accident.** The strip is 160 px wide and the mark is at 112 px (`SWIPE_AWAY_STRIP_WIDTH`, `SWIPE_AWAY_SETTLE_AT`), a third of a phone's width.

## How it is built

- `src/lib/swipeAway.ts`: the rule and its numbers, what the press is named, and the hint a card prints (`swipeAwayHint`).
- `src/components/shared/SwipeAwayRow.tsx`: the row. It draws itself through `children`, which gets the word press at a desk and null while the row swipes.
- `src/components/people/useSwipeRow.ts` gains one optional number, `settleAt`, handed to `swipeSettles` as the threshold that function already took. People's rows pass nothing and settle at a third of their strip, as before. `StagesPhoneRow` keeps its own swipe. No third swipe was written.
- **Which device swipes** is `handHeld()` in `src/lib/paintedMap/handHeld.ts`, the app's one reading of a coarse pointer.

## The equal for a mouse, a keyboard and a screen reader

- **A mouse cannot swipe**, so at a desk the same act is a word press on the row's right, 44 px tall.
- **A keyboard.** On a hand-held device the strip is a real button. Tab reaches it and the row slides aside to show it. Enter does the act.
- **A screen reader.** The strip's button is always in the accessibility tree, named for the act and the row: *Hide, 88 Quarry Rd Ste 100*. People's rows hide their strip from a screen reader until the row is open. These rows do not.

## My Bids

- A bid is drawn through `SwipeAwayRow` with **Hide**. On a hand-held device the bid has no button, only a small arrow that says it opens, and the card's foot reads *Swipe a bid left to hide it.*
- **Hide says what it did**: *Hidden:* and the bid's name, with **Undo** for ten seconds (`showActionToast`), by swipe or by word press.
- The hidden ids are now changed from what they are at the moment of the press, so an Undo after a second hide takes back only its own bid.

Recent Reports takes the same row in step 5.
