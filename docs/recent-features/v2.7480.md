# v2.7480 — Liens: upload the scanned PDF of a filed affidavit (2026-11-16)

Punch list #366. The owner's ask of 2026-11-16, over a screenshot of **Record the filing (after the County Clerk stamps it)** with *Take a photo of the stamped page* circled: "The option to upload a pdf from the scanned filings would be helpful". Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## Before

The stamped page reached the filing two ways: a photo taken on a phone (`LienReceiptPhotoPress`, an `image/*` input with `capture`), or a Drive link pasted by hand into *Saved copy*. An office that scans its stamped affidavits to PDF had only the second.

## What changed

- **`LienFiledScanPress.tsx`** (new): **Upload the scanned PDF**, beside the photo press on *Record filing…*, on a computer and on the phone's sheet, and so on *Already filed — record it…*, which draws the same box. The file goes up as it is, never shrunk, through `putJobFolderFile(jobId, 'sent', …)` into the job's Drive folder under Team only, Sent by us, named *Lien affidavit, Job N, the clerk's stamped affidavit, scanned, <day filed>.pdf*. Its link fills the filing's *Saved copy* with the note `LIEN_CLERK_SCAN_NOTE`, and the record carries both.
- **On a filing already recorded**: *link the saved copy* (or *change*) on an affidavit's row under *Filings on this job* now offers the same press, and the scan is written as the saved copy at once (`saveFilingScan`), with no second press.
- **`src/lib/jobs/lienScanFile.ts`** (new, pure): the kinds of file (`LIEN_SCAN_ACCEPT`, a PDF or a picture), the one reason a file is refused (`lienScanProblem`: not a scan, empty, or over the folder's 10 MB, each with what to do), the name in Drive and the words. The rule is the one a notice's mailed copy is held to on its own branch (`lienMailedCopies.ts`); the two differ in one word, the paper to scan alone, so the rule takes it as an argument and the two can share it once both are on main.
- The photo press is unchanged. A photo and a scan carry different notes, so each press shows *saved* for its own file alone.

## Not built, on purpose

One file for one lien. The owner wrote *the scanned filings*, and the office files in batches, so he is being asked whether a batch comes back as one PDF per lien or as one scan of the whole stack before anything is built for several at once.

## Verified

`lienScanFile.test.ts` (5) and `LienFilingTabs.filedScan.render.test.tsx` (5), with the Lien window's other render tests. The live desk was not walked: nothing was uploaded to a real job's folder and nothing was recorded on a real lien.
