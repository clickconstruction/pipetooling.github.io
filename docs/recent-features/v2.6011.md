# v2.6011 — The Monday sheet's email stacks on a phone (2026-10-28)

Punch list #200. The sheet's mail laid its three tiles in one table row and Five moves and People in another. A table row cannot shrink below its content, and the bar chart's labels do not wrap, so at 390 px Net position was cut at the right edge and Cash was off the screen; a mail app that shrinks a wide mail instead made the words too small to read. Since v2.5883 `bridge-weekly-sheet` mails this sheet to every dev each Monday, and the sheet was drawn to be read on a phone.

## What changed

- **The mail's HTML** ([`mondaySheet.ts`](../../src/lib/bridge/mondaySheet.ts) → `mondaySheetHtml`): the fluid hybrid layout. Each tile is an inline block at most `MONDAY_SHEET_TILE_PX` (312) wide and the moves and the people blocks at most `MONDAY_SHEET_HALF_PX` (468), so a row of them wraps on a narrow screen and sits side by side in the 960 px sheet; `col()` and `row()` build them. Outlook on a desk gets the same blocks as table cells through `<!--[if mso]>` comments. A `<style>` media query under `MONDAY_SHEET_STACK_PX` (620) lets a block fill a phone where the mail app keeps it; where an app drops it, the block stays its width and centred. The head carries a viewport meta so a phone lays the mail at its own width.
- **Each move's lever** sits under its words in the same cell, so the words keep the width; the chart's amounts drop their ≈, the chart's columns are fixed with 1 px gutters, and `BARS_WAITING_LINE` (*Light bars still wait on approval.*) prints under the chart when a week waits.
- **The text part** (`mondaySheetText`) takes the HTML's order: the three tiles, The weakest link, Five moves, People.
- **Unchanged**: every word and number, `buildMondaySheet`, the page, `bridge-sheet-email` (it sends what the page builds) and `bridge-weekly-sheet` (a redeploy carries the regenerated copy `supabase/functions/_shared/gcKernels/lib/bridge/mondaySheet.ts`).
- **Tests** ([`mondaySheet.test.ts`](../../src/lib/bridge/mondaySheet.test.ts), +3): a block per tile and two halves, Outlook's cells and table, the media query and the viewport meta, the old table cells gone, the sections' order; the lever under its words, no ≈ in the chart, the waiting line present and absent; the text part's order.
- **Looked at**: a made-up sheet's mail rendered in headless Chrome at 390 px (the tiles stack and each fills the width, all eight bars fit) and at 1000 px (three across, two across, as before).

## Deploy

The client deploys with the merge. `supabase functions deploy bridge-weekly-sheet`; `npm run check:edge-drift` names anything else behind. Then a dry run of `bridge-weekly-sheet` as a dev with `{ "dry": true }` shows the new `html`.
