# v2.3817 — Lien desk: a clearer "Put a GC on notice" list and a two-line header (2026-09-25)

The owner asked to improve the ⚠ Put a GC on notice… picker (screenshot, 2026-09-25) and to move the pile pills to the header's second line. Before/after drawn from the live desk the same day. Client only, no migration.

## What was wrong

- Long GC names squeezed the figure onto two lines (*1 job · / $38,625*), so the dollars wandered.
- Nothing said how soon, or what was stuck: Michael Holub's two jobs both lacked an owner, Southern Post was already awaiting approval.
- **Loberg Contracting sat fourth, but a run would claim nothing:** its one job's only month, June, had closed. The picker sorted by desk dollars alone.
- *most open first* read as everything open with the GC; it was only the notices due in 30 days (RMC: 6 jobs · $40,122 here, 22 jobs · $141,654 inside the run).

## The change

- **Kernel** `src/lib/jobs/lienDeskGcPicker.ts` (new): `buildLienGcPickerOptions(entries, gcsById)` replaces the modal's inline memo. Each GC (unsent desk entries only) gets jobs, open dollars, the earliest open deadline with days left, `needOwner` (the needs-owner pile or no owner), `awaiting`, `missed`, and `nothingToClaim` when no entry has an open window. For those GCs it also carries `closedMonths`: the desk's missed list, else the job's own past unnoticed months, else the saved draft's months (Loberg's June is held by a saved draft). Order: a window within `LIEN_GC_PICKER_URGENT_DAYS` (7) first, soonest first; then money; nothing-to-claim last, by money. `lienGcPickerCloseWords` gives *closes Oct 15 · 20 days*, *today*, *tomorrow*.
- **Picker** (`LienDeskModal.tsx`):
  - Header: *Put a GC on notice*, plus one line: *Notices due in the next 30 days, most money first. The run covers every unpaid job with the GC, not only these.*
  - Each row: the name on one line (ellipsis) with bold, tabular dollars in their own column; a second line with the jobs, the closing words (amber, red inside a week) and chips (*N need the owner*, *awaiting approval*, *N missed*, *rule: send*).
  - Nothing-to-claim GCs sit under a **Nothing left to claim** divider, at 65 % opacity, with *Jun window closed* and *the lien is gone · chase it in Collections*. Clicking one still opens the modal (letter two, the grid).
  - The menu is `min(420px, 100vw − 4rem)` wide and scrolls at 70vh, so it fits a 390 px phone.
- **Header**: a `flex-basis: 100%` break after § The rules. The tabs and the rules door are line one; the piles (all three kinds), Put a GC on notice, Send the run and *Sent on your word* are line two.
- **Guide**: *send lien notices from the Lien desk* → the piles sit on the header's second line; the picker's rows and *Nothing left to claim* are described.
