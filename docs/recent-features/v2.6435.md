# v2.6435 — An older set’s presses stay inside its card on a phone (2026-11-03)

Found on the live walk of v2.6356 (#6816) on 2026-11-03: on the Development page's Plans look, drawn as cards on a phone since v2.6377, the presses of an older set (*Open ↗* or *link still to come*, *Build from this set* and the × remove, `data-testid="site-older-presses"`) ran past the right edge of the part's card. Measured on a ZZ test development at 375 × 812: the card ended at x = 379, the group with *link still to come* at 395, 16 px past it, and the page scrolled sideways. The cause was the older-sets layout of v2.6355 (the owner's 2026-11-02 ask: date first, a dashed rule between sets, the presses grouped right and wrapping together): each row is a grid of `7rem minmax(0, 1fr)`, and the group was an `inline-flex` `nowrap` unit inside the words column; on a 356 px card that column is 214 px, and the group with *link still to come* is 239. With *Open ↗* the group is 192 and fit, which is why the fault showed only on a set with no link. Client only; no migration, no edge function.

- **`DevelopmentPartsCard.tsx`, the older-sets list.** The group is built once per set and placed by the screen: on the desk it sits inside the words' flex row exactly as before (`margin-left: auto`, `flex: 0 0 auto`, `white-space: nowrap`); on a phone (`useMatchMedia(DEVELOPMENT_PHONE_QUERY)`) it is the row's third grid child, `grid-column: 1 / -1` and `justify-self: end`, so it takes a line of its own across both columns at the right, with `flex-wrap: wrap`, `justify-content: flex-end`, `max-width: 100%` and no `nowrap`, so it can never leave the card: the presses wrap between themselves, never inside one (`LinkButton nowrap` holds each whole). The date still leads the row beside the name; only the presses line moves. What the owner asked for on 2026-11-02 holds on both: *Open ↗* and *Build from this set* stay together, at the right.
- **Measured after, same development, 375 × 812:** both groups end at x = 368 inside the card's 379; the group's parent is the row; no `nowrap`. On the desk, reloaded at the pane's width, the group reads as before inside the words row.
- **Tests.** `DevelopmentPartsCard.render.test.tsx`: the desk case is named as the desk's (*on the desk groups the presses at the right as one nowrap unit*) and also pins that the group is not a child of the row; a new phone case (`screenState.phone = true`) opens the fold and pins, on both rows, the group as the row's own child spanning both columns, `justify-self: end`, `flex-wrap: wrap`, `max-width: 100%`, no `white-space`, with its three presses inside and *link still to come* on the set with no link.
- **Not in this PR.** The e2e viewport page (`e2e/viewport-smoke.spec.ts`) is not given this surface: it needs a development whose part has older sets and the fold opened, and the suite is read-only against prod data that moves. A second, separate fault measured on the same walk: the Plans look's chips row (`site-plans-chips`) carries the no-part chip *every job that builds from plans names a part with a set* as one `nowrap` chip 356 px wide, which widens the look's root grid (its column is `auto`) to 356 px in a 329 px column, so every child of the look, the part cards included, ends at x = 379 on a 375 px screen and the page scrolls 3–4 px sideways; this PR leaves it, named as a follow-up. The job window's Plans section (`JobPlansSection.tsx`) lists its older sets the old way and was not touched.
