# v2.4289 — Accounts Receivable: the trail names who recorded a payment, even through Mark Paid (2026-09-30)

Punch list **#74** PR 4 of 4 (`to-dos/ar-where-the-cheque-went.md`; the card retires once the train, its migrations and the two function deploys are on prod). The second-pass mock-up had 13 of September's 38 AR payments with no name and proposed reading *the app* there. Read against prod before building: every one of the 13 was a Stripe bill marked paid by check through `record-stripe-invoice-out-of-band-payment`, which inserts with the service role, and then linked to its deposit in Accounts Receivable, an UPDATE nothing logged. They were people, not a robot. Migration `20261001153000_payment_actor_and_link.sql` (`docs/migrations/20261001153000_payment_actor_and_link.md`); the function redeploys.

## What changed

- **Migration** — `jobs_ledger_payments.created_by`, `linked_at`, `linked_by`; a `BEFORE` trigger fills `created_by` from `auth.uid()` when the writer named none and stamps the link when a deposit is attached; the history's *Payment* event takes its actor from `created_by` first; a one-time backfill from the history; `list_ar_deposit_trails` remade with `payment_type`, `reference_number`, `recorded_by_hand`, `linked_at`, `linked_by`.
- **`record-stripe-invoice-out-of-band-payment`** — the part-payment insert names its caller (`created_by: user.id`), and the full-payment path carries the caller on the invoice's metadata (`pt_recorded_by`, `_shared/pipetoolingStripeOobPaymentMetadata.ts`); **`stripe-webhook`** hands it to `mark_invoice_paid_from_stripe` as `p_recorded_by`. Both functions redeploy after the push. `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` says so under each.
- **[`src/lib/jobs/arDepositTrail.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/arDepositTrail.ts)** — a hand-recorded row is its own part: *→ #1025 Tovi Polk Repairs · recorded as Check 3463 9/28 by Taunya · linked to this deposit 9/30 by Grace* (or *linked to this deposit later* for rows from before the stamp). `AR_TRAIL_UNSIGNED` is now empty: a name not on record leaves the *by …* out. Tests updated.
- **The trail's order** (found on the live check, 2026-10-01, once `20260930230000` was on prod): each old job is followed by its own take-off, and the bank's failure sits before the first take-off that came after it, else last. Before, every take-off trailed at the end, so the Elgin cheque's two read as one list, and the Loberg $5,622.49 (taken off 9/30, stopped by the bank 10/1) read *taken off, marked returned 9/30*. *Marked returned* now goes only on a take-off that answered a return. Two tests added.
- Help guide: one sentence. `docs/BILLING_FLOWS.md` → *Payments · Shape* names the three columns.

## Decisions taken

- **Never invent an actor.** *the app* was the mock-up's call on the wrong premise; the fix is to keep the name, and to say nothing where it was never kept.
- **The link is a stamp, not a history event.** `linked_at` / `linked_by` on the row; the job history already carries the *Payment* event and this is a fact about the deposit, not the job.
- **Backfill from the history, read from the column.** The RPC still falls back to the history's actor, so nothing is lost if the backfill missed a row.
