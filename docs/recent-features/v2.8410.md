# v2.8410 — Smaller cents in People (2026-11-23)

Part 2 of the owner's ask of 2026-11-23, the cents smaller than the rest of an amount across the app: the People surfaces. The door is v2.8374's `Money` and `MoneyText`, and the sweep is v2.8376's `scripts/codemods/small-cents-sweep.mjs`, run and not edited by hand. No migration, no edge function.

## What moved

23 screen files under `src/components/people`: 80 plain amounts to `Money` and 105 pieces of words to `MoneyText`. The characters on the screen are the same. `scripts/codemods/small-cents-swept/people.txt` lists the folder, so `check:small-cents` now refuses a bare amount written there.

The Overhead tab's dollar columns are bare figures, `0.00` with the `$` in the column's heading. They took `<Money symbol="" />`. The hours beside them, also to two places, were left alone: the script holds back a figure named like hours.

## Left as it was

- The Overhead lens's two rate labels are SVG text, for the close-out's hand pass with `MoneyTspans`.
- 104 lines build an amount into a `const`, a tooltip, an HR paper's builder or a typed box. The close-out's hand pass takes the ones that reach a screen.
- One line of the pay ledger, *Paid … past net* beside *Nothing is open to move it to*. `check:empty-words` fails a bare empty sentence on a line a PR writes and only counts one on a line it left alone, so rewriting the amount there made that older sentence count as new and failed CI. The line is as `main` has it. The script now leaves such a line alone and lists it, so a later sweep does not trip the same way.

## Tests

- The 16 test files that depend on a changed file, through any chain of imports: all pass. 13 matchers took `moneyText`.
- `PeopleOffsetsTab.render.test.tsx` had its own matcher, a leaf element whose text is the amount. An amount is no longer in one leaf, so its two checks that an old figure is gone would have passed with nothing behind them. They read through `moneyText` now. That is the reason the script's `--tests` run was given every dependent test here, not only the failing ones: a check that an amount is absent does not fail when it stops looking.
- A scoped `tsc` and `eslint` over the changed files: clean.
