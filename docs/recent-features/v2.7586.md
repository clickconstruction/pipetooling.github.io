# v2.7586 — Lien desk: the title bar’s tabs and the Put a GC on notice menu moved into their own files (2026-11-17)

The fourth cut of `src/components/jobs/LienDesk.tsx`. No behaviour changes.

**Three pieces left the component.**

- `useLienDeskTitleBarFit` (`src/components/jobs/useLienDeskTitleBarFit.ts`) holds the bar's ref, the two measured fits (`titleLineFit`, `pileLineFit`) and the layout effect that reads the line the bar drew, as they stood. The desk calls it where the effect stood.
- `LienDeskGcPicker` (`src/components/jobs/LienDeskGcPicker.tsx`) holds the amber Put a GC on notice… door and the menu under it. The desk keeps whether it is open, because a phone's More sheet opens it too.
- `LienDeskViewTabs` and `LienDeskPaperTabs` (`src/components/jobs/LienDeskViewTabs.tsx`) hold the four view tabs and By paper's three. The desk keeps the tab row's scroller, since it brings the picked tab into view.

**What stayed.** The bar's own line (the way back, the title, the tools, the pile chips, the owner-records door, the legend) stays in the desk: each piece is built from the desk's state, and a component for the whole bar would only pass them through. The map's estimate of 221 lines assumed the whole bar; this cut is the parts that stand alone.

**Shared constants.** `headDoor` is `LIEN_HEAD_DOOR` in `lienDeskButtonStyles.ts`, `boxHead` is `lienBoxHeadStyle` in `lienDeskRowStyles.ts`, and `LIEN_DESK_HINT` sits beside `LIEN_DESK_NAME` in `src/lib/jobs/lienDeskTitleBar.ts`. The desk imports the first two under their old names.

**One sentence is a named constant now.** `check:empty-words` reads a moved line as a written one, and the picker's head over the GCs with nothing left to claim opens on *Nothing*. It is a section's title, not an empty list, and it reads exactly as before. It is `LIEN_GC_PICKER_NOTHING_LEFT` in `src/lib/jobs/lienDeskGcPicker.ts`.

**Size.** `LienDesk.tsx` went from 4,718 lines to 4,622, and from 97 `useState` calls to 95.

**Proof.** `LienDesk.render.test.tsx` (207 tests) and the page's two render tests pass with no edit. New: `LienDeskTitleBarParts.render.test.tsx`, eight tests. Five are on the GC picker, which no test named before: the door, one row a GC, the *Nothing left to claim* head, a row's press, and a phone's sheet.
