# v2.3680 — Lien desk: a closed window nobody has noted no longer ages off the desk (2026-09-21)

One migration, no client change beyond a comment. The durable half of the missed-window alarm ([v2.3679](./v2.3679.md)), which named a closed window the day it happened but could only do so for the seven days `list_lien_notice_months` kept the month.

## What shipped

- **Migration [`20260921193000_lien_notice_months_keep_unrecorded.sql`](../../supabase/migrations/20260921193000_lien_notice_months_keep_unrecorded.sql)** — the RPC keeps an unnoticed closed month with no `missed` desk row naming it for as long as its deadline is on or after 2026-09-14, the day the desk went live; a recorded one (a skip, or a noted miss) keeps the old seven days and then drops. Detail: [`docs/migrations/20260921193000_lien_notice_months_keep_unrecorded.md`](../migrations/20260921193000_lien_notice_months_keep_unrecorded.md).
- The kernel's `missedMonths` comment says so.

## Why the floor is the desk's birthday

Widening to "ever" would sweep in every unnoticed month on every billed sub job with money open since the company started clocking — hundreds of alarms about windows nobody could have caught with a desk that did not exist. Since 2026-09-14 the desk has been the thing responsible; that is the line.
