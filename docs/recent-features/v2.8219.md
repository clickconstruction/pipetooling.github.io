# v2.8219 — In kind: the Billed awaiting payment email and the pay reminder leave a bill an arrangement pays out (punch list #301, the sweep, PR 2) (2026-11-21)

PR 1 (v2.8218) took a bill an in kind deal pays out of the screens that count late money. Two senders on the server still read it as late: the staff's Billed awaiting payment report, and the morning run's reminder to a residential customer. Both read the same column now, through the helper PR 1 added.

## What changed

- **The Billed awaiting payment email** (`billed-report-email`). `get_billed_report_email_payload` lists every open billed row and does not carry the deal. `fetchPayload` stamps it on each row with `stampInKindDeals` (`_shared/inKindPayloadRows.ts`, the read v2.6931 made for the money emails), and `withInKindApart` (`_shared/billedReportEmail.ts`) takes those rows out of the list, the grand total and both aging chips. The RPC summed its totals over every row, so they are summed again over the rows that stay, by the RPC's own rule. The header and the text end with *$14,643 on 8 bills is scheduled in kind, not money due* (`billedReportInKindLine`, over `inKindApartWords`). A report with nothing but such bills reads *No bill is waiting on money*, not *Nothing billed awaiting payment. Nice.*
- **The residential Paid step** (`messages-nudge-dispatch`, and the step card on screen). `PIPELINE_SELECTS.jobs` (`src/lib/flows/residentialRows.ts`) selects `in_kind_arrangement_id`, and `paidRounds` (`residentialLoad.ts`) gives a bill on such a job no due day. The step's reminder goes at its day, so with no day it is never due by itself. The round stays open, waiting since the bill went, and a person's **Remind now** still works.

## Decisions taken with the build

- **A stamp in the function, not a new payload RPC**, as v2.6931 chose: one column read beside a long SQL body, no migration and no push.
- **A failed stamp stops the send.** A report that ages a credit as late is the wrong this fixes, so the read throws and the next tick tries again.
- **The round stays, with no day.** The bill is not paid, so the round does not read paid, and leaving the round out would have left the job's lane reading as if no bill had gone. No due day is the true reading of a bill on a schedule, and it is what stops the offer.
- **Her promise is no day either.** A promised pay date on a job a deal pays is a contradiction a person fixes on the job. The step follows the column.

## Not here

- **The office's statement week** (`statement-round-email-dispatch`). `get_statement_week_for_office` hands back one line per GC, already summed (`oldest_age_days`, `over_90`), with no job to stamp. Setting a deal's bill apart there is a change to that RPC, and what it should say follows the owner's call on what a GC's statement says. No job billed to a GC is on a deal today.
- **A GC's statement and the customer's portal**: the owner's call, as the card says.
- **dev-mcp's `get_customer` and `get_job`**: neither select carries the column yet.
- **The printed Billed awaiting payment report**, the email's twin on screen, still lists such a bill with its days.

## Release order

Merge, then deploy `billed-report-email` and `messages-nudge-dispatch`. No migration. The step card's change ships with the client.

## Verified

Kernel tests, three per reader (a covered bill, a payer part covered, an ordinary late bill): `src/lib/billedReportEmail.test.ts`, which also runs the stamp into the split, and `src/lib/flows/residentialLoad.test.ts`. Not walked live: neither function is deployed until the merge.
