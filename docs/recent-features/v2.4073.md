# v2.4073 — Subs on a phone: the sheet form stands above the bottom bar (2026-09-28)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Punch list **#30** (`to-dos/taunya-mobile.md`) — the first finding from Taunya's walk on her own iPhone (2026-09-28): under *Subs · Work*, "the Save button is cut off partially".

## What was wrong

`JobsSubLaborFormModal` — the Sub Labor sheet form that *Edit the sheet* / *Open the sheet ›* opens from the Work and Pay rows — is a hand-rolled fixed overlay at `zIndex: 50`. The phone dock (`DispatchModeFooter`) is fixed at 1000, so with Dispatch Mode on the bar drew over the form's last 60 px plus the home indicator. Scrolled to the end of the form, that strip is where Save sits. The emulated look of 2026-09-27 never opened this form.

## What shipped

- `DispatchModeFooter.tsx` exports `DISPATCH_MODE_FOOTER_Z_INDEX` (1000) beside its height, and uses it.
- `JobsSubLaborFormModal.tsx`: the form's overlay stands at 1100 (`SHEET_FORM_Z`, where `ResponsiveModalShell` stands by default); the add-subcontractor dialog it opens on top of itself at 1110 (`SHEET_FORM_INNER_Z`); the book-entry form at 1100, after it in the DOM as before. Nothing else about the form moved.

## Tests

`JobsSubLaborFormModal.render.test.tsx` +1: the open form's overlay is fixed and stands above `DISPATCH_MODE_FOOTER_Z_INDEX`.
