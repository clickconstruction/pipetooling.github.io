Client only; no migration, no edge function. Estimator Wendi (relayed by the owner, 2026-10-06): *I want the cover page to match our view. Two versions as base with alternate pricing for each one.* BP503 Emergency Animal Hospital Buda: To Plans $922,196.69 (excluding med gas $880,227.11) and Value Engineered $366,998.23 (excluding med gas $329,441.55). Mock-ups: this build's https://claude.ai/artifact/4kuUM2Ju6TzG51LLPipHqn (four passes) and the "Docs review and bids" session's https://claude.ai/artifact/XAKN2fbNFvKuQ64jJ7brFW (drawn in parallel, a switch *Added together / Options*); reconciled on the first.

## Why no switch

The first draft kept today's sum as a mode for two scopes of one job (Building A + Building B). Read off prod (the e2e account, read only): 24 bids have versions, 14 put two or more base versions in the letter, and all 14 are either/or — To Plans vs Value Engineered on eleven, one version per GC on three. None is two scopes added up. So the sum was never a shape anyone wanted; it is the bug, and two base versions are options always. **Separate pages** (one full letter per section) remains for a GC who wants that. Eleven of the fourteen were sent with the summed letter; their filed sent copies stay as sent, and a reprint from the tab draws options.

## What changed

- **Kernel** [`coverLetterSamePage.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/coverLetterSamePage.ts): `planSamePageLetter` plans two or more base sections as `options` (`SamePageOption`: the base and the offered prices on its own version), even with no alternates; the headline is the lead (first base in packet order), its revenue and its rows, never the sum; `alternates` keeps only the in-lieu-of sections (a version flagged Alternate, and offered prices not owned by an option). `buildOptionsBlock(plan, texts, fmt, toWords, editable, naming)` → `CoverLetterOptionsBlock`: *Option N — <customer-facing name>* (a saved label verbatim), the amount in words and figures, each alternate a `formatAlternateDeltaText` against **its own option**, numbered across the letter, a bare *Alternate N* until the estimator names it (the in-lieu-of rule); `optionsAlternateCount` so `buildAlternatesBlock` (new `startAt`) continues the count; `optionsShareFixtures`.
- **Builders** [`bidDocuments/coverLetter.ts`](../../src/lib/bidDocuments/coverLetter.ts): a last `optionsBlock` argument on `buildCoverLetterHtml` and `buildCoverLetterText`. With it the sentence reads *As per plumbing plans and specifications, we propose to do the plumbing in one of the following amounts:* (`COVER_LETTER_OPTIONS_INTRO_DEFAULT`), then one bold line per option with its alternate lines under it; Inclusions print *Option 1 — To Plans, fixtures provided and installed by us per plan:* per option unless `sharedFixtures`; the typed inclusion lines, exclusions, terms, SOV, payment schedule and closing print once. Without the block, byte-identical to before.
- **Tab** [`BidsCoverLetterTab.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/BidsCoverLetterTab.tsx): `letterOptions = samePagePlan?.options`; `headlineAmount` is the lead's ★ when options exist (Mark sent, the best effort, the payment schedule's dollars and the Bid Board's value follow, whatever `bid_board_value_rule_v1` says); the total box reads *Option 1 leads · 2 options · 2 alternates* with a line per other option; the summary line *In the letter: 2 options, pick one — …*; the Mark sent caption says Option 1's amount; the Base button's title; click-to-edit finds an option's and its alternates' keys; the bid room is handed Option 1 as the proposal, its alternates, then the other options as in-lieu alternates (their own alternates wait for the room's option picker, PR 2).
- **Approval PDF** [`approvalPdf.ts`](../../src/lib/bidDocuments/approvalPdf.ts): the letter page passes the same block and `startAt`, so it says what the tab says.
