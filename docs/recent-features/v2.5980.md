# v2.5980 — Where everyone stands keeps its names inside the band on a phone (2026-10-27)

Fix, no migration, no edge function. v2.5977 (PR #6300) cleared the Bid Board's sideways scroll at 375 px and left this band as the next risk.

## Cause

The Health section's field band ("2 · Field band" in `BidBoardEstimatingPulseSection.tsx`) hung each name tag off a wrapper at `clamp(4%, pct, 96%)`, centered with `translateX(-50%)` and `nowrap`. On a phone the band is 343 px wide (375 screen) or 288 px (320), so half a tag is 8–10% of the band, and a tag near either end hung past it. Measured as the estimator twin on Plumbing: at 375 the right-most tag ended at 374 px, 1 px inside the screen; at 320 it alone made the page 321 px. The tags at the left end started 13 px left of the band. The 4%/96% clamp also stood those stems off their person's true rate.

## Fix

- **Tags stay inside, stems stay true.** Each person is now two absolute pieces. The stem stands on the true won % (`clamp(0px, pct% - 1px, 100% - 2px)` keeps its 2 px inside the band). The tag sits in a box centered on the stem that reaches the nearer band edge: `left: 0` or `right: 0`, `min-width` twice the distance to that edge, `max-width: 100%`, `text-align: center`. A tag that fits is centered on its stem as before. One that doesn't grows the box from the edge and ends flush with the band. A label wider than the band ellipsizes. The tag's box is plain CSS 2.1 sizing, with no container queries or transforms, so every phone browser draws it the same. `pulseBandTagBox(pct)` gives the side and the box width.
- **Desktop keeps its look.** The pill keeps its inline-block line box. The label's ellipsis lives on an inner `inline-block` span with `vertical-align: top`, so the pill's baseline and its 2.7 px gap under the stem don't move. At 1,280 every tag between 4% and 96% sits within half a pixel of where it was, and every row matches. Only the tags at the band's two ends moved, about 20 px each, to sit flush with its ends over their true marks. The other option, proportional anchoring (`translateX(-pct%)` with the stem at pct% of the tag), sets every tag off-center over its stem on desktop, so it was not built.
- **The stagger knows the band's width.** `buildPulseBandItems(people, stats, { bandPx })` takes the band's width, measured on the section with `useMeasuredWidth`. A neighbor whose tag would touch the one before it also drops to the long-stem row. On a desk-wide band two tags touch only inside the old 12-point rule, so desktop rows don't change. On a phone, a tag slid in from an edge no longer lands on its neighbor. `pulseBandTagSpanPx` is the rule the CSS draws, and a test proves the two agree. `estimatePulseBandTagPx` sizes a tag from its label, within 4 px of the measured pills.
- **Tests** (`estimatingPulse.test.ts`, +6): the box per side; every tag inside the band with its stem under it, centered when there is room; the drawn box and the span rule agree at three band widths, four tag widths and every quarter point; the label estimate; the phone and desk stagger at both ends. `BidBoardEstimatingPulseSection.render.test.tsx` (new, 1) pins the wiring: each tag's box on its side with its width, its stem a sibling with the same title. It fails with the right side flipped.

## The phone smoke waits for the board to settle

`e2e/viewport-smoke.spec.ts` measured the Bid Board as soon as the words *Bid Board* showed. The room chips wait on `bid_gc_recipients`, which loads after the bids' recency reads, about 2 s after the rows. Counts and pricing land late too. A `data-board-ready` marker from the GC rows' own pulls was tried first and fired 0.3–2 s before the chip; covering every late pull would take a page-wide "settled" signal. Instead the Bid Board entry takes `settle: true`. `watchNetworkQuiet` counts the page's requests from before `goto`, leaving out sockets and event streams, and waits until none has been in flight for 1.5 s before measuring.

## Verified

Dev server, estimator twin (`/dev-login?as=twin:estimator`), Plumbing:

- **375** (phone preset), with v2.5977's chip fix applied locally: `scrollWidth` 375 = `innerWidth`, and no element passes the window outside a scroll box. Every tag sits inside the band's 343 px and every stem within 1 px of its true rate. The stem-to-pill gap is 2.73 px, as before.
- **320**: `scrollWidth` 320 = `innerWidth`, no element past the window, every tag inside 288 px.
- **1,280**: rows match the old rule, and tags sit where they were except at the two ends.
- **Smoke**: the spec passes on the Bid Board against the dev server, transpiled with esbuild because this Mac's Node 20.0 can't load Playwright's TypeScript; CI's Node 20 runs it as is. A scratch copy signed in as the estimator twin, without v2.5977's chip fix, measured both waits. The old wait measured at 1.6 s and read 375, a pass. The settled wait measured at 6.7 s and read 394, the chip's overflow. With v2.5977's fix the settled wait reads 375.
- `estimatingPulse.test.ts` (19), the render smoke and the release-note checks; eslint, the theme-token check and a narrow typecheck of the changed files and the spec.

## Left as is

- **A crowded end on a phone.** When several tags crowd one end of a 343 px band, two rows can't hold them. They overlapped before, and the end tags now sit flush with the band instead of hanging 13 px past it, so they tuck further under their neighbor. On this board, same-row overlap went from 45 to 59 px at 375 and from 71 to 89 px at 320, all of it at the crowded end. The right end came clear. A third row or initials on a narrow band would fix it, but either changes the band's height, so it waits for the owner.
- The caption under the band still runs beneath the first row of tags.
