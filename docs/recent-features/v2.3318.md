# v2.3318 — Pay run: week bands with subtotals between runs of same-week reports (2026-09-11)

Client only, no migration. Owner ask on People → Pay → Payroll → **Pay run**: "soft separation of the weeks — between the last transaction of the first week and the first transaction of the second week, some sort of separator." Three mock-ups offered (rule + air / week band with subtotal / alternating week tint), owner picked **B, the band** (artifact 8090984e).

## What shipped

- **Kernel [`payRunWeekBands.ts`](../../src/lib/payRunWeekBands.ts)** (+5 tests): `buildPayRunWeekBands(rows, fullyPaidIds)` walks the visible rows in their existing order and opens a band at the first row of every run of consecutive rows sharing `period_start`/`period_end`, accumulating `count`, `hours` (`hours_total`), `gross` (`gross_pay`) and `openCount` (rows not in the fully-paid set). `bandsByStartIndex` keys them by the row index they precede.
- **[`PeoplePayStubsTab.tsx`](../../src/components/people/PeoplePayStubsTab.tsx)**: the table body `flatMap`s each visible stub to `[band?, row]`. The band is one `colSpan={11}` cell on `var(--bg-subtle)` with a hairline above and below: the period in small caps (`ledgerPayPeriodShortLabel(start, end, false)`), the ISO week number in monospace (same midweek anchor as the Period column), then `N reports · H h · $G gross · K open`. The fully-paid set is the existing `ledgerPaidStubIds` memo, so "open" agrees with the Open / Paid / All segments and the Payment column.
- Help: `run-the-pay-week.md` gains "Reading the Pay run table".

## Decisions

- **Runs, not a re-sort.** The table's order is `created_at desc` (the parent's load), which clusters reports by the batch they were generated in. The band marks where consecutive rows change period; a catch-up report generated later for an earlier week sits where it was created and opens its own band. Re-sorting by period would have changed the order the office is used to and moved late reports out of sight.
- Bands follow the visible rows, so each segment (Open / Paid / All) and a name search band their own rows; subtotals are of what is on screen.
- Week number is always shown on the band even when the Period column's `(w#)` toggle is off — the band is where the week is named.

## Not changed

Row content, the Period column and its week-number toggle, the segments, the Total line, the upcoming-payroll link.
