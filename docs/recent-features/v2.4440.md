# v2.4440 — Held for suppliers: catch a paid job a supply house can still notice (2026-10-02)

No migration, no edge function. **The ask (Robert, 2026-10-02)**, after the live walk of [v2.4404](v2.4404.md)–[v2.4412](v2.4412.md) found 22 jobs paid in full with a supply house still owed ($24,657.84), four of them with a house's own notice window still open: "I do want your next step, help me do it in a way so it is easy to update and we get information from a supply house. Think through it and ask yourself is this the best we can do before building it." This is step 1 of 2; step 2 is the **Ask a house…** sheet.

## The gap

The Lien desk marks a job only while money is owed to us. A job whose customer has paid in full never reaches it, yet a house still owed there can send its own § 53.056 notice, and that notice lands on a customer who owes nothing. Those jobs showed only on Held for suppliers, which said what was owed and how late, and nothing about the date a house can act.

## Is this the best we can do?

The first plan was the house's date and a filter on Held for suppliers, with the Lien desk's **They told us…** on each row. It was kept, but it is one job and one house at a time, and a house's rep answers for every job in one call. So the updating moves to a per-house sheet in step 2, and this step is the seeing: the date, the risk, and the single-row form for the one-off.

## What shipped

- **The kernel** ([`heldHouseNotice.ts`](../../src/lib/materials/heldHouseNotice.ts), new, 5 tests): `customerPaidInFull` and `heldNoticeRisk` — on a paid-in-full job, the soonest house that is still owed and can still act (`lienSupplierHouseNotice`: the day the house gave when it is still ahead, else our estimate's open window), with its words (*Paid in full · Reece can send its own notice by Oct 15*). Null while the customer still owes us (the desk has that job) and when every window has closed.
- **The tab** ([`MaterialsJobAccountsTab`](../../src/components/materials/MaterialsJobAccountsTab.tsx)): the load keeps what it already read, adds `invoice_date`, each job's property kind and `job_supply_house_words`, and builds the desk's `buildLienSupplierJobs`. A risk job's row says *Reece notice by Oct 15* in red; its open statement leads with a red line; each owed house's row says *its own notice by Oct 15 · our estimate · 13 days*, *notice window closed Sep 15 · our estimate* or *its notice goes out Oct 14 · Dana said so · noted Oct 2 by Grace*, the house's balance under ours when they differ, and **They told us…** opening the desk's form (`LienSupplierWordForm`, exported from [`LienJobSuppliers.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienJobSuppliers.tsx)); a save re-reads the words alone. The filter **Paid, house can still notice** shows when there is such a job, sorted soonest first (`?filter=notice_risk`). [`Materials`](../../src/pages/Materials.tsx) passes the signed-in name.
- **The guide** [`see-which-paid-jobs-still-owe-supply-houses`](../../src/content/help/see-which-paid-jobs-still-owe-supply-houses.md) is rewritten in plain words and comes off `LEGACY_PLAIN_WORDS_GUIDES`.
