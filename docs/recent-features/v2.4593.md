# v2.4593 — Submittals: What the GC sees says what the link shows today (2026-10-05)

Punch list #62 (*See what they see*), the first fix after a re-read of Layers 1 and 2 on main. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

Both views still draw their rows through the room's own kernel (`roomRowsFrom` → `roomCounts`, `_shared/submittalRoomPayload.ts`): order-only rows drop in `gcRoomItems`, order-only parts in `roomPartsFrom`, and a fixture or part left out never becomes a row. The rows were right. The sentence about the link was not, in two cases:

- **A draft over a shared revision.** On BP398 the newest revision is a Rev 4 draft; Rev 3 was answered by email and never shared; Rev 2 was shared on Sep 15 and the room is open with Dana Whitfield named on it. The line under the rows and the window both said *The GC sees nothing until you share.* Dana's link shows Rev 2. Every resubmit draft read this way.
- **A closed room.** A shared revision read *That is what the link shows now* after *Close the room*, while the link answers only *This review is closed*.

## What changed

- **One kernel for both views** ([`seeWhatTheySee.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/seeWhatTheySee.ts)):
  - `linkShowsRevOf(revisions)` is the revision the link shows now, by `get-submittal-room`'s own rule: the newest revision with a share date. A revision answered by email and never shared has none, so the link skips it.
  - `isRoomClosed(room)` is the function's own closed test: the room's status or its close date.
  - `describeLink({ rev, linkShowsRev, roomClosed })` gives the lead, the note and the window's intro. `describeForReviewer(items, view)` adds the quoted headline and subline. *Shared* is derived (the link shows this revision), so the two cannot disagree.
- **The window** (`SeeWhatTheGcSees`) takes `link` in place of `shared` and draws `describeLink`'s intro and note.
- **The tab** computes `link` once beside `isNewest` and hands it to both.

| The revision on screen | The note |
|---|---|
| Nothing shared | *The GC sees nothing until you share.* |
| A draft over shared Rev 2 | *Until you share Rev 4, the link shows Rev 2.* |
| The shared one, room open | *That is what the link shows now.* (lead *The GC's page reads:*) |
| Room closed, this one shared | *The room is closed. Its link says only that the review is closed. Reopen it to show this again.* |
| Room closed, a draft | *… Reopen it, then share Rev 4.* |

The window's intro reads *This is the page the GC opens from your link* only while the link shows these rows, and *will open* otherwise.
