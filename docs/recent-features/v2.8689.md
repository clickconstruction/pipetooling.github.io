# v2.8689 — Ready to bill: a test job sends no notice (2026-11-26)

Punch list #443, row 7's walk. One migration that replaces one function, one sentence on one window, and no edge function. The owner's answer of 2026-11-27, asked whether to build it: *Yes, build it*. The yes covers the build. The push is owed after the merge, on its own yes, and was not done.

## What the read of row 7 found

v2.8682 made a voided test bill leave a record. Its walk voids a ZZ test job's test bill. A void that takes a job's last billed bill moves the job from Billed back to Ready to bill: `syncJobToReadyToBillIfNoBilledInvoicesRemain` calls `update_job_status`. Every move into `ready_to_bill` fires `enqueue_ready_to_bill_notification_au` on `jobs_ledger`, whose function queues one `paid_job_email_queue` row of kind `ready_to_bill`. The `paid-job-email` drain sends it within about 15 minutes, by email and by push, to the people on the office's Ready to Bill list. No step on that path left a test job out. So the walk, and every other move of a ZZ test job into Ready to bill, told real people about a job that is not real.

## The call

One question was put to the owner: build the one-line migration that leaves ZZ test jobs out of the notice? Its cost was said with it: no ZZ test job would ever send that notice, the times a person moves one to look at it included. The answer was yes.

## Why the function and not the drain

The function is the one place every move into `ready_to_bill` passes. A row never queued is never coalesced, retried or counted. A rule in the drain would wait on a deploy and leave rows in the queue that are stamped and never sent.

## What else reads that row

Read in the code, not on a database.

- **The drain** (`supabase/functions/paid-job-email/index.ts`, `runDispatch`) is the queue's only reader and its only writer after the triggers. It holds no ZZ rule of its own, so nothing there disagrees: it sends what is queued, and a ZZ test job now queues nothing.
- **Preview & test** in the same window calls the function's `preview`, `test_send` and `test_push` modes with a job's id. None reads or writes the queue, so the trigger is not on their path and each still works for a ZZ test job.
- No client code reads `paid_job_email_queue`: the generated types and the dev seat's catalog name it and nothing else does. No count, badge, Settings list, email schedule or Day book reads it. My email schedule and Email & notifications read the recipient lists in `app_settings`.
- `supabase/tests/combine_rows` inserts a queue row of the default kind by hand, to show a combine lets it go. It does not pass through this trigger.
- No walk recipe under `to-dos/` or `docs/` moves a ZZ test job to Ready to bill to see the notice. `to-dos/lien-payoff/README.md` notes that its walk's jobs stayed Working, so no notice left, which still holds.
