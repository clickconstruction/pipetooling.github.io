# v2.8081 — Developments, Schedule: one tap on the ready bar shows its list, and the list stays on the screen (2026-11-20)

Found by the Fable 5.2 audit of punch list #382 (Before we start), on the live walk of the Schedule look at phone width. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

The card for #382 says of the ready bar: *Resting the cursor on the row, or pressing it on a phone, lists what it takes to start*. Two things kept a phone from that list.

- **One tap opened the list and shut it again.** A tap on a touch screen reaches the page as three events in a row: the pointer enters, the press takes focus, then it clicks. `ReadyMarks` opened the list on enter and on focus, and its click toggled it, so the click of the same tap shut what the enter had just opened. A Playwright tap on the Development page at 390 px read the list as shown after each of three taps: no, yes, no. The render test fired a click alone, which a phone never sends by itself.
- **The open list ran off the right edge.** The list hangs from the bar (`left: 0`, `min(420px, 80vw)` wide), and the bar sits after the stage's name and days. On a 390 px screen the bar began at 179 px, so a 312 px list ended at 491 px: the last 101 px of every line were off the screen.

## The fix

`src/components/startChecks/ReadyBar.tsx`, in `ReadyMarks`:

- The list keeps the moment it opened. A click within 400 ms of that moment is the press that opened it, and leaves it open. A later click shuts it, as before. Resting a cursor and then clicking still shuts it.
- When the list opens it is measured against the page's own width (`document.documentElement.clientWidth`, since a phone's `innerWidth` reads wider while something overflows). A list that would pass the right edge is pulled left by the overflow, never past the left edge, with 8 px of room.

After the fix the same walk reads yes, no, yes for three taps, and the list sits from 70 px to 382 px on a 390 px screen.

## Tests

`src/components/developments/DevelopmentScheduleCard.render.test.tsx`, two new cases: a tap's three events leave the list open and the next press shuts it; a list measured at 179 to 491 px on a 390 px page moves 109 px left. Both fail on the old code and pass on the new.
