## Row 1 — a failed record never resends

`sendCourtesyNotice` (`lienCourtesyIo.ts`) sent the emails and texts first and wrote the record on the sent item last; when that write failed it threw, the window stayed open with its counted Send press live, and a second press sent every message again. Now the write's failure comes back on the result as `recordError` beside the record and what went, never thrown. The desk (`LienDeskModal.tsx`) closes the window as for any send, toasts `courtesyUnrecordedWords` (*1 message went: email to the GC. The record could not be written: <reason>. Write the record again. Nothing goes out a second time.*), keeps the press's record in `courtesyUnrecorded`, and the footer draws that sentence in amber with **Write the record again ›** (`data-lien-courtesy-record-again`) in place of the Send press (`data-lien-courtesy-door` is gone for that job until the record takes). The press calls the new `writeCourtesyRecord(item, record)`, the record write alone; on success the band goes and the footer reads *courtesy sent* as it always did. A reload loses the kept record (the messages went; the office reads the toast), named here so nobody looks for a table.

## Row 4 — the clock reads the delivery day

`letterTwoStatus` has taken `mailClocks` since the Docsmit train (D9) and no caller passed them, so day 0 was always `sent_at` while the guide promised the delivery day. `letterTwoMailClocksFrom(items, piecesBySource)` (`lienLetterTwo.ts`, pure, 3 tests) keys the owner's piece by the sent notice item, reading pieces under the item's own id and its filing's, the newest mailing winning; `useLienDeskData` reads the sent packets' pieces (`readMailPiecesBySource` over the item ids and filing ids) **in light mode too**, so the Dashboard's lien month card (v2.6650) and the desk count the same day, and passes the clocks to `letterTwoByJobFrom`. The courtesy's day 7 follows, since `courtesyStatus` reads letter two's clock. The GC run page (`useGcOnNoticeData`) still counts from `sent_at`; it draws no reminder.
