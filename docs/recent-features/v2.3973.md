# v2.3973 — Edit Job: autosave is its own piece (2026-09-27)

Client only; no migration, no edge function, no screen change. Punch list **#46** row 4 (`to-dos/decomposition-queue.md`): the Job form map's move 9, the save engine — the last on its list (`docs/JOB_FORM_MODAL_ARCHITECTURE.md` → *The save engine*), after its Stage A ([v2.3966](v2.3966.md)).

## What shipped

- **`src/hooks/useJobFormAutosaveEngine.ts`** — the four edit-mode autosave slices (billing, identity, materials, team), each with the mirror refs its writer reads, its record of what was last saved (`hydratedPaymentIdsRef`, the discount snapshot, the last saved pictures link, phone and bid link) and the writer itself; `rehydrateFixturesFromDb` with its re-baseline effect; `flushAllAutosaveSlices`; the two bid-outcome helpers `createJob` also uses. 15 inputs, handed in on every render; 24 things handed back.
- **Moved as written.** Five stretches of the form's text, in their order, checked against the form before the move. Two additions: the engine keeps its own mirror of the customer cache (the form's copy stays with its customer / GC pickers), and the bid-read type moved to module scope.
- **The order of effects.** The slices register in the order they had, so they keep their order among themselves, and the re-baseline effect still follows the billing slice. They now run after three of the form's effects they used to be interleaved with — the GC-pays default, the property-candidates read and the Account Man un-team. None of those reads or writes a slice's timers or baselines, and each slice's effect reads only the render it belongs to.
- **Stays in the shell:** every field; hydration, undo, and the footer's aggregate (they reach the engine through what it hands back); the close guard and `createJob`. `JobFormModal.tsx` is 3,611 → 3,372 lines. Behavior-neutral.
- **Observed, not changed:** after the line items are re-read from the database (Bill Customer wrote a discount while the form was open) the billing slice is re-baselined, but the debounce the new rows started is not cancelled — 1.2 s later the slice writes the same rows once more. It was so before the move; the test pins it.
- **Tests** — `useJobFormAutosaveEngine.render.test.tsx` (18), on a fake clock over a client that records every call: opening a job saves nothing; no job, no save; a line item saves at 1.2 s and not at 1.199; a dropped payment is deleted by the ids hydration gave; typing restarts the wait; a failed save says so, stays dirty and tries again; a flush does not wait; the identity slice's one update, its hold on a blank required field, the bid read before and after, the pictures-link auto-close; materials; team at 0.4 s; all four changing at once; a flush of all four in close-flush order; and the re-read of the line items.
- The map's save-engine section and move 9 say what was built, what stayed and why.
