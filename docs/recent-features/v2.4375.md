# v2.4375 — Approval PDF: the letter uses your company's wording (2026-10-01)

Left over from v2.4373, which made the Approval PDF's letter page the Cover Letter tab's letter: the wording still differed. `Bids.tsx` resolved the PDF's exclusions and terms as the bid's own entry, else `DEFAULT_EXCLUSIONS` / `DEFAULT_TERMS_AND_WARRANTY`, and the PDF passed no closing. The tab uses the bid's own entry, else the org default from `app_settings` (Settings → Bid Cover Letter Defaults), else `''` so the builder prints its built-in wording. The `coverLetter*ByBid` entries live only for the browser session, so nearly every PDF took the fallback. On BP385 the tab's letter lists the company exclusion *This proposal excludes water service and sewer line to street.* and the PDF's did not. The PDF also bulleted the built-in terms, because the builder bullets text it is handed but prints its own Terms as one paragraph.

## What changed
- **One rule in [`coverLetterWording.ts`](../../src/lib/bidDocuments/coverLetterWording.ts).** `letterWording(perBid, orgDefault)` is what a letter builder is handed (`perBid ?? orgDefault ?? ''`), and `effectiveCoverLetterWording` now builds on it. `coverLetterOrgDefaultsFrom(rows)` parses the three `app_settings` rows, trimmed, a blank value meaning none saved. `COVER_LETTER_ORG_DEFAULT_KEYS` names the keys.
- **The Cover Letter tab** reads its defaults through `coverLetterOrgDefaultsFrom` and hands its builders `letterWording`. Same letter as before.
- **The Approval PDF** gets the bid's own entries from `Bids.tsx` (`undefined` when nobody typed in the box), reads the three keys fresh, and hands the builder `letterWording` for exclusions and terms and the org's closing.

## Tests
- `coverLetterWording.test.ts`: `letterWording` (typed, emptied, org, none); the built-in terms print as one paragraph and handed terms get a bullet; `coverLetterOrgDefaultsFrom` trims, reads a blank or null value as none, and ignores other keys. The agreement table now hands the builders `letterWording`.
- `approvalPdf.test.ts`: with the org's exclusions and closing saved and nothing typed, the letter prints them and the built-in terms unbulleted, and never the built-in exclusions or closing; typed entries win over the org's, and the org's closing still prints. Both cases fail on the v2.4373 builder.
