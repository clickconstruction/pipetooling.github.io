# v2.7652 — Papers before a job opens: the prompt that stands in for the job until you sign (2026-11-17)

Papers before a job opens, step 3 of seven: the prompt. No migration. One edge function changes, `get-contract-signing-link-for-self`, and it must be deployed after the merge.

**The prompt is the only lock at this step.** A direct request to the database still reads the job's rows. The lock in the database is step 4, and the office's section says so in a note until then.

## What is new

- **One read for the tab**: `useJobsClosedToMe` (`src/hooks/useJobsClosedToMe.ts`) reads `jobs_closed_to_me()` when the first follower mounts, when a job's papers change in this tab, on focus, and every 90 seconds. `useMyJobPapers(job)` reads one job's prompt and is shared by every row that shows that job. `useJobsNeedingMe` follows the closed set and reads nothing while it is empty.
- **The prompt**: `JobPapersGate` draws **Before you open J…**, the papers, **Read and sign** on the first one not signed, and **Open the job** when nothing is left. `JobPapersGateModal` is the same card as a window.
- **The job window**: `JobDetailModalProvider` draws the prompt in place of `JobWindowModal` or `DetailJobModal` when the job is in the closed set. Both openers pass through that one place.
- **Job Mode's card**: the prompt takes the place of `JobModeDetailsSection`, a closed job's row in the day list shows a lock and opens the prompt, and a closed job is left out of the On my way list.
- **Clock-in**: `pickByHand`, `handleCompleteClockIn`, `applyUpdateFocusDirectImpl` and `handleUpdateFocus` all turn a closed job aside to the prompt. No clock starts.
- **Lists**: Job Mode's rail and header, `DispatchModeSchedule`, the Calendar and the clock-in picker show a closed job by its number and a lock, for every role but the office (`useJobsLockedInLists`).
- **The way back**: the prompt remembers the page and the job in session storage before it leaves for `/contract/accept`. The thank-you there offers **Back to the job**, and the provider reopens the job on arrival (`src/lib/jobs/jobPapersReturnIo.ts`).
- **Needs you**: the line **Papers to sign** (`job-papers`, source `jobPapers`, every role) over `job_papers_needing_me()`. Its press opens `JobPapersWaitingWindow`: one job's prompt, or the list of jobs by number.
- **`get-contract-signing-link-for-self`**: a row with `job_required_paper_id` skips the Dashboard reminder's switch, and its signer is matched by `people.account_user_id` alone.
- **Help guide**: `sign-the-papers-a-job-requires`, for every role. The office's guide now says what locks.

## Choices worth knowing

- **The office's lists keep the job** (the owner's call 8, "Prompt is enough"). Names are hidden in lists only for the crew and subs. The prompt at the job, the clock-in refusal and the Needs you line apply to everyone.
- **The office can manage a job's papers from the prompt.** Saving a paper locks the job for the person who saved it, at once. Without that door a paper required by mistake could only come off after signing it.
- **A read that fails closes no job.** The first read waits four seconds at most, and a failed one keeps what was read before. A lost signal must never shut a crew out of its day. The database lock of step 4 is what holds behind the prompt.
- **The section's own *sign yours* line is gone.** The prompt takes over the moment the office saves.
- **`JOB_PAPERS_LOCK_STAGE`** is now `prompt`.
