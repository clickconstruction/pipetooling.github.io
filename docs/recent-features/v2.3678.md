# v2.3678 — Quick time add, PR 6: the after-midnight rule (2026-09-21)

PR 6 of [`to-dos/quick-time-add`](../../to-dos/quick-time-add/README.md), the last of the three follow-ups queued on 2026-09-21. Client + one migration (`20260921181500_quick_add_recent_window.sql`, doc in `docs/migrations/`). Client first, push soon after.

## Why

*Today only* was found wanting when a local test run crossed midnight Central: a call that ran 11:54 pm – 12:04 am could not be added at 12:05 (its window starts yesterday), and one that ended at 11:50 pm could not be added at 12:30 either — the sentence sent both to My Time, five taps deep, for the exact case the door exists for. The to-do's *Open questions* proposed the rule; the owner queued it.

## The rule

The window must be **today on the company calendar, start and end — or have ended within the last two hours**. Never the future. In the kernel ([`quickTimeAdd.ts`](../../src/lib/clock/quickTimeAdd.ts): `QUICK_ADD_RECENT_WINDOW_MINUTES = 120`, `quickAddRefusal`) and in `add_quick_time()`, together, with the one sentence — *Quick time is for today, or the last two hours. For another day, use My Time.* — pinned by the kernel test against the newest migration.

Two things follow in the function: the row's `work_date` is the day the window **started** (a punch is dated by its clock-in), and the daily ceiling counts on that day. The sheet's *ended … ago* choices already stopped at 2 h ago, so nothing there changes.

## The sheet

The line under the composer says **last night** instead of *today* when the window started before today (`quickAddDayWord`; `quickAddEntryLine` takes the calendar as an optional argument, so its other callers and tests are unchanged).

## Docs

Guide *add a quick call or email to my hours*: the *Reach into another day* bullet now says the rule. `GLOSSARY.md`'s quick-add entry says *today, or ended in the last two hours*. The to-do's *Open questions* bullet on the half hour after midnight closes.
