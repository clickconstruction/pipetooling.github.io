# v2.7441 — Lien desk: a notice with a late month reads its open window, not window closed (2026-11-16)

Taunya, on job 804 (Summit GC, Auto Zone) in the Lien desk's Monthly notices list (2026-11-16): "on this screen, why does it say window closed?" Then: "do the fix here". Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What a person saw

804's notice waited on the leader's approval and named **Jul + Aug + Sep**. July's window closed on Oct 15. August's closed that day, Nov 16, and September's closes on Dec 15. The row's first chip read *window closed* in red, beside a second chip reading *Jul window closed · not noted*. The pane's header said the right thing: *Approve the Aug + Sep notice — today*.

The notice keeps July on purpose. A late notice still counts toward the affidavit while the affidavit's window is open (v2.4708).

## The fix

`buildLienDeskQueue` in [`src/lib/jobs/lienDesk.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDesk.ts) judged an entry by the earliest deadline among every month its notice names, closed ones included. The `earliestDeadline` field's own doc already said *earliest open deadline*. It now takes the earliest deadline among the named months that are still open. Only when every named month is closed does it fall back to the earliest closed one, so an all-late notice still reads *window closed*.

804 now reads *due today*. Its closed July keeps its own chip.

## Where else it shows

`earliestDeadline` and `daysLeft` feed every notice reader: the row's chip (`deadlineWords` in `LienDesk.tsx`), its severity, the queue's sort, a held notice's re-ask day (`holdUntilFor`), the Board's chip colour and the To do list. Each one now goes by the soonest open month. That is what those readers were written for.
