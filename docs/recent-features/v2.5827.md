# v2.5827 — Quickfill Email station, PR 4 of 5: the station reads the counted labels (2026-10-24)

Punch list #159, the owner's "build it all". The station was three free boxes and a mark, and its
history showed the same six emails retyped every morning for a week. With PR 1 to 3 (v2.5814,
v2.5820, v2.5824) the app counts the labels the owner lists; this PR is the station that reads them.

- **The model** [`lib/quickfill/emailStation.ts`](../../src/lib/quickfill/emailStation.ts) ([tested](../../src/lib/quickfill/emailStation.test.ts)):
  the boxes the viewer may see, as `email_box_can_see` decides (a shared box; their own; everyone's
  for the lead), shared first, each with who sees it (*for the office*, *shows to you and Dale*,
  *shows to Marisol and you*) and how fresh its count is; one row per label, Gmail's own count
  (`labels_state.total`), the oldest age, the threads past the limit oldest first with the day they
  arrived, the sender, the subject and a door into Gmail under the box's address (none for a lead
  looking at someone else's box), the newer ones as a number, *The newest N of M are listed* when the
  sweep stopped at its page cap; a missing label in words with a door to Gmail's labels, a label not
  counted yet, a label the count could not read; what moved since the station's last look (new,
  cleared, still here); the stance in sentences (what is counted, *Senders, subjects and dates only,
  never the words.*, who sees the viewer's rows, *It changes nothing in Gmail.*); and the note a mark
  saves (`composeLookNote` with the person's own word last). `mode: 'byHand'` with the reason while
  the switch is off, while no box the viewer can see is listed, or before the first count.
- **The reads** [`emailStationIo.ts`](../../src/lib/quickfill/emailStationIo.ts): the settings, the
  rows the policies allow, the threads open or gone in the last two weeks a page of 1,000 at a time,
  the first names of the box owners and the lead; **Read again** asks `gmail-count` for each box.
- **The station** [`QuickfillEmailSection.tsx`](../../src/components/quickfill/QuickfillEmailSection.tsx):
  the count line (*4 open · 3 past 3 days · read 4 min ago* · **Read again** · *In Messages: 5 people
  are waiting on us →*), a card per box, the since line, *A word about these (optional)*, the stance
  with **Change in Settings** (the owner's Phone and texts, or the person's Your account), and
  **Looked · N open**, which saves the composed note. By hand, today's three rows with the reason above
  them and the same refusal of an empty mark.
- **The mark note**: the station reports its composed note as its metric's `markNote`
  (`useReportQuickfillSectionMetric`'s fifth argument, v2.5825's `getMarkNote`, which the Physical inbox
  landed first), and `markSectionUpToDate` takes it when no note is passed. `email-inbox` leaves
  `QUICKFILL_NOTE_FIRST_SECTIONS` ([`round.ts`](../../src/lib/quickfill/round.ts)), the last station in it,
  so the Roller's and the phone round's foot reads **Looked · N open · next →** and saves the list.
- **Smokes** `QuickfillEmailSection.render.test.tsx`, one per state: a person with labels, a label
  that does not exist, one with none (a viewer with no box of their own; the owner's door), and by hand.
- **Guide** *Quickfill* (the Email line and the round's sentence) and *choose which mail quickfill
  counts*. Docs: `QUICKFILL_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `GLOSSARY.md` (*Mail the app counts*), the to-do.

## Deploy

Client only. The station reads PR 1's tables; until the owner's admin step and his switch, it shows
the hand-typed rows with *The app does not count this mail yet…*, so nothing changes for the office
before the first count.

## Is this the best we can do?

- The station now says the thing it exists for, by name and by age, on the day nobody looked as well
  as the day someone did (the trend is PR 5).
- The Roller's Looked saves the list without a typed note, and the person may still add a word.
- Not verified on live mail: no box is counted until the admin step. Checked by the kernel's tests and
  the smokes over made-up rows, and live in the browser pane on the by-hand mode.
