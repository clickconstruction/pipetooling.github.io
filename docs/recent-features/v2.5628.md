# v2.5628 — The `{bill}` blank: a quick reply carries the bill's pay page (2026-10-19)

Punch list #124, PR 1 of 3 (the plan and the mock-ups at `to-dos/pipeline-stage-texts/README.md`, PR #5850; the owner: build it as drawn). Tanya's message from the Pipeline, *here is a copy of your last invoice*, needs the bill in the text; by text that is a link, and the app has one: every bill billed through Stripe has its pay page, `/pay/<invoice id>` (v2.3754), which Follow up's They owe us already builds per bill. This PR makes it a blank. No migration; no edge function.

- **The blank** (`quickReplies/blanks.ts`): `BlankKey` gains `bill`, *the bill's pay page*, from the money; the editor's chips (`BLANK_CHIPS`) offer it after `{amount}`.
- **The kernel** (`lib/billing/billLink.ts`): `billedNewestFirst` (the bills still owed, newest `billed_at` first, an undated one after the dated by the day it was made) and `newestBillLink` (the first with a Stripe id and an id the pay page accepts, `isPayLinkId`, as `theyOweUs.ts` picks it, to `payLinkUrl`). `billLinkIo.ts`'s `loadNewestBillLink(jobId)` reads `jobs_ledger_invoices` for the job's billed bills; a read that fails fills nothing.
- **Where it fills**: `replyFills` takes `billLink` and gives `bill` only when it knows one, so the box's fills keep their shape; `ReplyBox` takes `billLink`; `JobCustomerBox` reads it once per job (`loadBill`, a smoke's fake) and hands it down, so the job window's box and the Pipeline's customer sheet (v2.5626) both fill it; `tellFills` takes `billLink` and `tellCustomerIo.loadJob` reads it beside the customer, so Tell the customer fills it on a booking, an estimate or a move. The thread's own foot on Messages has no one job and leaves it.
- **The hold**: a line with `{bill}` left holds the press as every unknown blank does (`sendWaits`: *Fill {bill} first. The app does not know it.*; the sheet's *Fill {bill} before it sends*), and the editor's preview says *Fill {bill} in the box before it sends.* Attach picks the bill's sent copy from the job's files, as it could.
- **Not in this PR**: the morning run's reminders send a team line as written and fill no blanks; the Paid reminder with `{first}` and `{bill}` is PR 4 of the plan, an edge change with its redeploy.
- **Tests**: `billLink.test.ts`; `filling.test.ts` (the chip, the fill with and without a bill, the preview's note and the wait); `tellCustomer.moves.test.ts` (the sheet's fill and the blank left); `blanks.test.ts` and the pinned chip list; `JobCustomerBox.render.test.tsx` (the read once per job). The reply box's eleven smokes and Tell's sixteen still pass.
