# v2.7088 — Supply houses: a house's card opens on its bills, and each bill is marked paid by itself (2026-11-12)

Punch list #334, PR 5 of the Bridge train. It touches no Bridge file, so it is cut from `main` and merges beside the stack. The owner opens a supply house "to see the invoices and to potentially mark some of them as paid". The card put the Job accounts roster first and listed the bills in a table eleven columns wide, newest first, with a Paid checkbox that read the whole card again on every tick.

## What changed

- **The rules** (`src/lib/materials/supplyBillGroups.ts`), pure, 15 tests:
  - `groupSupplyBills`: the unpaid bills in groups in the order to work them. Past due first, oldest day first, then each due day soonest first, then the bills with no due day, then the paid ones when the page shows them. Each group carries its count and its total. A credit memo is stored negative and takes its amount off its group.
  - A bill marked paid on this visit stays in its group and leaves the group's count and total, so the rows under it do not move.
  - `supplyBillSummary`: up to four numbers. Open, Past due, the next due day, and Not on a job account, which is counted only at a house that uses job accounts (`isBareOfJobAccount`).
  - `billDateWords` and `billDayName`: month and day, and a two-digit year only when it is not this year (`10/31`, `12/5/25`, *Nov 15*, *Jan 15 ’27*).
  - `dueGroupKey`: the group a link's due day lands on, the day itself or the past-due group once it has passed.
- **The card** (`src/components/materials/SupplyHouseBillsCard.tsx`, 6 render tests): the summary numbers, the groups, and two-line rows that lead with the job (the PO's words when no job is on the bill). The invoice number, the PO, the billed day, the lines check, *Look ›*, *See the bill ›* and *Receipt ›* sit on the second line. The amount, **Mark paid** / **Mark unpaid** and Edit sit at the right edge, the largest bill of a day first. It draws only: the tab holds the bills and the save.
- **The tab** (`src/components/SupplyHousesTab.tsx`):
  - Invoices lead the expanded house and say the house's pay day beside the heading. **Job accounts** folds under them, starts folded, and the fold is remembered on the device (`job-accounts-open`).
  - `toggleInvoicePaid` changes the one row in place from the row the update hands back. No second read of the card, so no *Loading…* and no lost scroll. A refusal is a toast with its reason (`formatErrorMessage`). The aging table above is read again in the background.
  - A bill marked on this visit stays listed, greyed, reading *Paid today* with **Mark unpaid**, until the house is opened again.
  - A link that names a house (`?tab=supply-houses&house=<id>`) scrolls its row into view once the card has read, and `&due=<YYYY-MM-DD>` lands on that due day's group. The Bridge's press that writes this link comes in a later PR of the train.
- The paragraph under the heading that explained the red PO Generator icon is gone. The icon keeps its own words on hover.

## What the owner decided

Asked about ticking several bills and marking them at once, he refused it: "I don't think we should mark two as paid. I think we should individually mark them as paid." So the card has no tick boxes. Paying several bills with one payment is still **Make Payment**, unchanged. The phone's house screen was already bills first and is unchanged, except that its Mark paid now also changes the row in place.
