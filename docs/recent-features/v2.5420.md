# v2.5420 — Messages: a sub’s flow on a job, from the work order to their pay, behind the scenes (2026-10-12)

R8 · 12, PR 1 of 3 of the residential build (`to-dos/gc-mode/messages/RESIDENTIAL_BUILD_PLAN.md` on `spike/gc-mode`): the sub's flow, its facts and its seed, with the migration that seeds it (`20261013200000_sub_flow`). It stacks on v2.5396 (the residential flow, which allows the `sub` kind). No screen.

**A sub's job is one labor job**, the sub sheet (`people_labor_jobs`). It carries the money, the stage and the assignee. A sub can hold several sheets on one Pipeline job, and a sheet can have none. PR 3's loader reads it that way.

**The six facts** (`src/lib/flows/facts.ts`), each on the job:

| Fact | Reads | Kept |
|---|---|---|
| `sub_asked` | `step_commitments.offered_at`, the work order on the sheet or on its job. Withdraw clears it | yes |
| `sub_said_yes` | `step_commitments.accepted_at`: their portal's sign, the app's press, or the office's Mark accepted | yes |
| `sub_declined` | `step_commitments.declined_at`, with its reason. The way out of the fork: it ends the lane | yes |
| `sub_on_site` | `people_labor_jobs.progress_at` once set. A portal progress report stands in for arriving, since no clock in is tied to a sheet, and the column holds the newest report | half; gap `on_site_at` (PR 2) |
| `sub_done` | `people_labor_jobs.stage` past `working`, dated by `stage_changed_at` at the walkthrough. It keeps only the newest move | half; gap `done_at` (PR 2) |
| `sub_paid` | `subLaborJobBalance` at 0 or less (the sheet's lines less its payments, where a negative payment is a back charge), dated by the newest `payment_date`. `paid_at` is never written on main, so it is not read | half |

**The flow** (`SEED_FLOWS.sub` in `seed.ts`): one Per job lane of five steps, Asked (ours, the work order), Said yes (theirs, a fork whose no ends the lane), On site (theirs), Done (theirs), Paid (ours, their pay). There are no reminders. Our two steps sit with Rosa until the office sets a seat.

**The migration** re-creates `flow_fact_ids()` with the six facts last and `flows_seed()` with the sub flow, then runs the seed, which on prod adds the sub flow alone. The new `sub-flow` bed reads it back step by step. `messages-flows` and `residential-flow` now count six flows, and `residential-flow` checks the seeded sub flow instead of adding one by hand.
