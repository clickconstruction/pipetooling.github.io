# v2.6977 — The Bridge's Market and Sales windows open on one screen, on the won rates (punch list #318, PR 3) (2026-11-11)

PR 3 of six for *Market and Sales on the won rates* (`to-dos/market-sales-won-rates/README.md`, drawn in its `mockup.html`, sections 1 and 2). The owner, on the five step windows drawn before and after, 2026-11-11: "Like all of your recommendations, build it all." On the three leftovers in Market and Sales: "Yes, tidy them". Client only. No migration, no edge function. Stacked on PRs 1 and 2.

## What changed

- **The frame** (`src/components/bridge/BridgeStepWindow.tsx`): one shape for a step's window. Where the step stands, in a headline and one line. What to do this week: short groups of rows, each row one thing with one press, the first three showing and the rest behind *Show all*. Everything else the window used to print whole, behind a press at the foot, one open at a time, with *How this is counted* last. The frame owns the overlay, the close press, Escape and the sizes: every press is at least 44 px tall.
- **The Market window** (`BridgeMarketDesk.tsx`, `market/marketStepWindow.ts`): opens on *Market is worth $X a week. The field builds $Y.*, with how far short or over that is, the two parts (bids and estimates, each with its won rate) and the won rate for each size of bid. *Do this week* lists up to three: the old bids to mark won or lost, a quiet GC to call, the bids with no value. At the foot: **Every source**, **The asks by week**, **What this window cannot see**, **How this is counted**.
- **The Sales window** (`BridgeSalesDesk.tsx`, `sales/salesWorth.ts`, `sales/salesWeek.ts`): opens on what is out and about what that is worth at the won rates, over what we sold a week beside what the field builds. *Do this week* lists the calls, each past its asker's usual answer, and the old bids to mark. *Why we lost* reads the last 12 months by reason, with a press to name a loss that has none. At the foot: **Every asker**, **What is in the pile**, **Every decided bid**, **How this is counted**, which holds the won rate for each size.
- **Only a mark decides** (`market/marketDesk.ts`, `sales/salesDesk.ts`): `bidWon` and `isWin` no longer count a job made from a bid. A bid that became a job with no mark is neither won nor decided in either window.
- **Plain words** (the same two kernels): a bid *has no value* where it *rode at $0*; a source that answers nothing reads *no answers* where it read *dead weight*; the footnotes say the rules without column names. A source's quiet weeks, the field's pace and the count of smaller sources are fields on the model, so nothing reads a number back out of a sentence.
- **The page** (`Bridge.tsx`): hands both windows the Market amount and the two won rates it already reads for the pipe.

## Not in this PR

- **The list behind *Every asker* and *What is in the pile* still rank each asker by their own record**, and fall back to the page's old win share for an asker with few marked bids. The window says so in its own words. PR 4 hands the Sales kernel the rate for the bid's size and the estimates' won rate.
- **A bid a job was made from still leaves the open pile** in `loadSalesDesk.ts`, marked or not, so the list and the pile do not add up to the headline. PR 4.
- **The Market and Sales cards behind The flow, the slowest-step reading and the weekly email** still count the old way. PRs 4 and 5.
- **The Build, Bill and Collect windows** were rebuilt on the frame on the spike too. They are not part of card #318 and are not here.
- **The fixtures in `marketDesk.test.ts` and `salesDesk.test.ts` still carry names from before this work.** PR 6 swaps them for made-up ones.
