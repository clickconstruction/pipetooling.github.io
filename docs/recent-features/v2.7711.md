# v2.7711 — Lien desk: the timeline's Last work agrees with Months on this job (2026-11-17)

Seen while building v2.7684: on one notice the timeline's LAST WORK stop read one month, *dated from the job's creation*, while the line in Months on this job read a day in the month before, *from the bill*. The question was why, and then the fix.

## Why they disagreed

- The months' line reads the job itself (`lienLastWorkDay`): a day set by hand, else approved hours, else the latest sent, unpaid bill, else the day the job was made.
- The desk's strip (`buildLienTimelineFromDesk`) built its months from two things merged: the months the reader returns (`list_lien_notice_months`) and every month a sent or missed notice named, so the notice stops can say what went out. It then took the **last of that whole list** as the last month of work.
- A job nobody clocked on is dated from a stand-in, and a stand-in moves. A job made in one month and billed the month before was dated from its creation month until the bill rule (v2.7224). A notice naming the creation month went out under the old rule. After the rule the reader returned the bill's month, the sent notice still named the later one, and the later one won on the strip.
- The words fell through: the paper's month has no reader row to say where it came from, so the stop said *creation*.
- It was more than words. The strip counted the affidavit's last day and the year to sue from the paper's month, a month later than the Lien window, the affidavit queue and the SQL readers counted them.

## What landed

- `lienDeskDatingMonth` (`src/lib/jobs/lienTimelineDesk.ts`) picks the month the strip dates a job from:
  - a day set by hand, from the reader's `hand` row or from the job's own column, which still counts when the reader no longer returns its month;
  - else, for a job with no approved hours, the stand-in of the job the caller hands (`lienStandInMonth`, the months' line's own rule), so the strip and the line agree by construction;
  - else, with no job handed, the last month the reader returns when every reader month is a stand-in. A month only a past paper names stays a notice stop and dates nothing;
  - any other job keeps the last of the whole list: a worked month the reader no longer returns, noticed and closed, is still a month of work.
- `LienTimelineDeskSource` gains `job` (the hand day, the creation day and the read bills). The notice pane's strip (`LienDesk.tsx`) and the affidavit pane's hand it.
- The By job book and the firm's grid hand no job: their job read (`LIEN_BOOK_JOB_COLUMNS`) is pinned to the firm's select and carries no bills. They take the reader-rows rule.

## Unchanged

- A day set by hand outranks hours approved after it and outranks a bill (the owner's reading of 2026-11-13, `lienClockMonths`). The strip follows that rule as every other reader does; it does not take the later of the two.
- No migration, no edge function. `lienBillMonths.ts` and `lienDesk.ts`, which the edge functions' copy holds, are read and not edited.
- The papers on a job whose stand-in moved are not touched. A notice that named the old month stays on record; the office settles the job's month by setting the last day of work.

## Tests

`lienTimelineDesk.test.ts` (+5): the moved stand-in with and without the job, the later of two bill months from the job, a hand day the reader no longer returns, and a worked month kept on a job with clock hours.
