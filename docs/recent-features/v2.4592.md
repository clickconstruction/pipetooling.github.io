# v2.4592 — Procurement log: a calendar beside the orders (2026-10-05)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. PR 3 of the procurement log redraw (*Procurement log: orders on a calendar*; its card was PR #4527, closed unmerged once the train shipped): section 8 of the spec and its key. PR 1 was [v2.4581](v2.4581.md), PR 2 [v2.4587](v2.4587.md). The phone cards are PR 4.

## What changed

On a wide screen's **To order** lens a calendar column sits between the quantity and the right-hand words, about a third of the table and no less than 300 px.

- **The line of weeks** starts seven days before today and ends five days after the latest date among the parts not yet on site. The head names each Monday (*11/2*), with **today** in blue in place of a Monday within three days of it, and over them each stage at the day the job needs it (*Rough In*, *Top Out*, *Trim Set*), written beside its line. A thin blue line marks today down every row and a thin grey line each stage's day.
- **One mark per order**, on the order's own line. A part inside an order carries none.

| The line | The mark |
| --- | --- |
| An order to place | A blue diamond at the order-by date, a dotted line to a dark tick at the needed date. Amber within three days, red once past. |
| An order placed | A blue bar from the ordered date to the expected date, a dotted line on to the tick. Late, the bar is blue up to the tick and red past it. |
| A fixture that waits | An open diamond at the day the GC must answer by (`approveBy`), a dotted line to the tick. Nothing with no lead time or no needed date. |
| A part sent back | None. Its note and doors use the calendar's width. |
| An order on site | None. |

- A bar that began before the line of weeks is cut square at the left edge.
- **The key** is one line under the table, in place of the paragraph: *order by · the GC must answer by · on order · past the needed date · needed on the job · today*, and **new** once an update has gone.
- **With nothing to place** the column is not drawn, the lens row adds *No dates to draw yet.*, and the line under the table reads *Each order gets a place on a calendar once its parts have a lead time and the job has stage dates.*

## Asked along the way: is this the best we can do?

Each of these is a place the first build fell short of that question, and what it became.

- **A calendar with nothing on it.** The spec draws the column when any part has "a date to place". A job with stage dates and no lead times would then show an empty calendar with three stage lines. The column now needs a real mark: an order-by date, an arrival, or a day the GC must answer by.
- **A job needed months out.** The spec left this open. The line of weeks stops at 26 weeks so this month's orders are not squeezed into a sliver; past 14 Mondays the head names every second one; the key says when the calendar stops short.
- **A label struck through.** With Rough In a few days back, today's line ran through the words *Rough In*. Each label in the head now sits on the surface, so a line passes behind it.
- **A filter that moved the weeks.** The line of weeks is built from every line, so pressing a step or a missing fact narrows the rows and leaves the calendar where it was.
- **An order of several parts.** A PO's bar runs from its first ordered date to its last arrival, and the late part decides it. A fixture's diamond is the earliest answer its parts need.
- **A mark nobody can read aloud.** Every calendar cell says its mark in words (`aria-label`, *order by 10/27, needed 12/08*), and each diamond, bar and tick has a hover line.
- **The paragraph under the table.** The spec removes it. It still explains the Status column of *By tag* and *By house*, which keep their rows, so it stays on those lenses and on a phone, and goes only where the key takes its place.
- **Two lines where one would do.** With the calendar in, a waiting fixture's *answer by* and **Their answer…** wrapped onto two lines. They hold to one.

## How it is built

- [`src/lib/submittals/procurementCalendar.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/procurementCalendar.ts), new: `calendarAxis` (the line of weeks, or null) and `groupMark` (one order's mark as plain data: `diamond`, `bar`, `lateBar`, `dotted`, `tick`, `words`). `CALENDAR_MAX_DAYS`.
- `SubmittalProcurementPanel.tsx`: `CalendarHead`, `CalendarCell`, `CalendarLines`, `Diamond`; the orders table takes a fifth column when `axis` is not null; `renderRow`'s `order` option carries the axis so a part line draws its empty cell and a sent-back line spans it.
