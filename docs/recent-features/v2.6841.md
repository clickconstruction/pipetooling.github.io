# v2.6841 — A month of in kind credit can be entered before it is due (punch list #301) (2026-11-10)

The owner, the day the first arrangement was set up: *Enter $1,000 now*. November's rent had not left the bank, so the month read *Not due yet* and had no door: the month's window opened only for a month that was due.

## What changed

- **The ledger** (`InKindLedgerWindow`): a month that is waiting, on a deal that has not ended, carries a quiet press, **Enter it early** (`monthEarlyPress`). It opens the month's window as the blue press of a due month does.
- **The month's window** (`InKindMonthWindow`): a month that is not over with nothing at the bank is *early* (`monthNotOver` in `inKindMonthChoices.ts`, kept out of the kernel the edge functions copy). The usual credit leads, since an empty bank says nothing yet (`monthChoices` takes `early`), the whole bill reads *We pay nothing this month.*, the bank strip says *Nothing has left the bank … yet*, and an amber strip says the month is not over (`monthEarlyWords`). Once the bank has a line in the month the window reads as before.
- **Last credit** on the customer's card counts from the month after this one when this month already has its line (`nextCreditMonth`). It counted the entered month a second time.
- **Help**: the guide's monthly section says how to enter a month early.

## Verified

Kernel and render tests (`inKindMonthChoices`, `inKindLedger`, the ledger and month windows). Walked on prod on the first arrangement: the press, the window with the usual credit picked and its bill shown, the month recorded, the row reading the amount, and Last credit a month later than before the fix.
