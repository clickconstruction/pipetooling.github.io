# v2.8083 — Before we start: material that is overdue no longer reads ready (2026-11-20)

Found by the Fable 5.2 audit of punch list #382, *Before we start*. No migration and no edge function.

## What was wrong

A bullet of ours can be read from the order log. The rule is *ready when every line of the stage is on site, or is ordered and lands before the first day*. `readOrderLog` in `src/lib/startChecks/startCheckReaders.ts` compared each undelivered line's expected day with day one and never looked at today.

**The scenario.** One line, ordered, expected Nov 3, never marked delivered. Day one is Nov 23 and today is Nov 20. The reader returned `ok: true` with the fact *0 of 1 line on site. The last lands Nov 3, 20 days ahead.* The bullet *Our material lands before day one* counted as a yes, the ready bar turned green for it, and the stage could read *Nothing is open. Press Go*. The material was 17 days late and nobody had it.

## The fix

Today is threaded into the reader.

- `readOrderLog(read, stageName, dayOne, today = '')`. With today known, an undelivered line whose expected day is before today is open. One such line reads *1 of 3 lines was due Nov 3 and is not on site.* More than one reads *2 of 3 lines are past due and not on site. The oldest was due Nov 3.* Both carry the order log's door and its link, like the other open cases.
- The check runs after *not ordered yet* and *no day to land yet*, and before *we have no days planned*, *lands on the first day or after it* and the ready case. An overdue line is said first even on a stage with no days of ours.
- A line expected today is not overdue yet. A line marked on site is never counted, however old its expected day.
- `readerFor(lines, reads, today = '')` hands today on. With no today, or an empty one, both read exactly as before, so a caller that has no day keeps the old result.
- `useStartCheckReads` already received `todayYmd`, the company's day, for the read itself. It now passes it to `readerFor` and names it in the memo's deps. Both screens that use the hook pass the day: the job's Documents tab and the development's Schedule.

The plural sentence names the oldest day and not a *due by* day: with two overdue lines, *due by* the oldest day would be false for the newer one.

The four files copied to the edge functions (`startChecks.ts`, `startCheckRows.ts`, `gcPage.ts`, `valuesInPlace.ts`) are untouched. `startCheckReaders.ts` is not copied.

The guide *say what must be true before a stage* gains one sentence in the order log's row of its table.

## The tests

`startCheckReaders.test.ts` gains five cases:

1. The scenario above reads not ready with the overdue sentence, alone and with two overdue lines, ahead of a line that lands after day one, and on a stage with no days.
2. A line expected tomorrow, or today, and before day one still reads ready.
3. With no today, or an empty one, the old result holds.
4. A delivered line with an old expected day is not counted as overdue.
5. `readerFor` hands today to the order log's reader, and reads as before with none.

`JobDocumentsStartChecks.render.test.tsx` gains one case for the wiring: a reads io hands back a line expected Nov 3 on a job whose today is Nov 17, and the row shows the overdue sentence and stands open.

Run both ways. Against the old reader, cases 1 and 5 failed and 2, 3 and 4 passed, as they should: those three hold what must not change. Against the new reader with the old hook, the render case failed. With the fix all 20 reader tests and all 27 render tests pass.
