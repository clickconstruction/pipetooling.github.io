# v2.7165 — Materials: old prices checked one at a time, and the end of the list (2026-11-13)

Punch list #342, PR 3 of 3, the last. PR 1 and PR 2 let a person price every row with no price. Two things were left: the list had no end to reach, and the other half of the Worth cover, *on a price older than 90 days*, was a count with no door.

## What changed

- **Three chips over the list** (`SiteMaterialsView.tsx`): a press on **Worth** shows **No price**, **An old price** and **Left out**, each with its count, and each lists its rows (`siteWorth`'s `unpriced`, `stale` and `leftOut`). The chips stay when a list is empty, so the other two are one press away.
- **The old prices' walk** (`SitePricingWindow.tsx`, `mode="old"`): under **An old price**, **Check 9 old prices one at a time** opens the same window on the rows on an old price. Each row shows the price it reads now in amber, with its source and its age. The window offers only a bill line newer than that price, never the parts book it already reads, and takes a typed price or **Not a part** as before. A parts-book row has **Open the Parts Book**, since the book's own price is checked there for every site. A newer price is one more row of the site's own prices and outranks a stale book price by the rule of v2.7159.
- **The end**: with nothing left, **No price** reads *Nothing waiting on a price on this site.* and offers the rows on an old price, or every row. **An old price** and **Left out** each say when they are empty. The window's last screen says the same for its walk (`walkEndWords` reads either walk).

No migration.

## What it does not do

- **It does not check the Parts Book's own price.** That stays the book's **Check prices**, which changes the price for every site.
- **No reminder.** A price that turns 90 days old shows in the count and nowhere else.

## Checked

Two new end-to-end cases in `SiteMaterialsView.render.test.tsx` (the three chips, an old typed price checked against a newer bill, each empty list; the list with no row left to price) and two more lines in `pricingWalk.test.ts`. On the real site, as the dev on a local copy of the app with every write to a table refused: the chips read *No price · 96*, *An old price · 3* and *Left out · 0*, each listed its rows, and the empty one said so. 1400 and 375 wide, no sideways scroll. The old prices' press was not there, as it should not be before v2.7159's table is on the database.
