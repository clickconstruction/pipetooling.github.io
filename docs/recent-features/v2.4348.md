## What was left

After PR D1 the Active Accounts window (Settings → People & teams → *Manage accounts…*, and the samples card's button) held only things that now had another home, plus *Invite via email* and *Manually add user*. Two homes for one person kept drifting: that is how the email-only edit (PR C) and the missing supervision row (PR B) happened.

## The change

- **Deleted**: `ActiveAccountsPanel.tsx`, `useActiveAccountsManagement.ts`, `ActiveAccountsModalContext.tsx` (and its provider in `App.tsx`), plus the two kernels only the window used, `lib/activeAccountsSearch.ts` and `lib/inviteUserForm.ts`, with their tests.
- **+ Hire → How they sign in** ([`HirePersonModal`](../../src/components/people/HirePersonModal.tsx), [`hireWrites`](../../src/lib/people/hireWrites.ts)): *Email them a link* (`invite-user`, the default) or *Set a password now* (`createHireLogin` → `create-user`, the window's *Manually add user*): two password boxes checked with `passwordProblem`, no email sent. `hireWrites.test.ts` +2.
- **Settings → People & teams**: the pointer keeps *Find duplicates…* and loses *Manage accounts…*; its sentence names the desk and Archived. `onActiveAccountsDataChanged` is gone from `SettingsPeopleTab`.
- **Sample accounts** ([`SampleAccountsCard`](../../src/components/settings/SampleAccountsCard.tsx)): a **Desk** button per sample (samples are hidden from People → Users, so the card is their door).
- **The desk's Extra access row** shows *Hiring columns shared with them: …* (`useHiringColumnShares` + `sharedColumnsLine`), the line the window's Edit showed.
- **Words that pointed at the window**: View as's empty state, the Users tab's link dialog, Hiring's Pass confirm and toast, Prospects' no-access note.
- **Guides**: *invite someone to sign in* (rewritten around + Hire), *hire someone*, *link an external person to an account*, *merge two user accounts into one*, *put someone in read-only training mode* (an expired link → Send sign-in email), *archive and restore user accounts* (archive from the desk), *see the app as someone else*, and **team-prospects rewritten in plain words** (off `LEGACY_PLAIN_WORDS_GUIDES`; its three Active accounts doors now name the desk). Docs: `ACCESS_CONTROL`, `EDGE_FUNCTIONS`, `GLOSSARY`, `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION`, `PROSPECTS_TABS_ARCHITECTURE`, `SETTINGS_TABS_ARCHITECTURE`, `twins/TWIN_HARNESS.md` (mint a twin from Digital twins; briefs rebuilt), `twins/missions/estimator.md`.
