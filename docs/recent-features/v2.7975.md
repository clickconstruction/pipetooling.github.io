# v2.7975 — Trips: the join window says a line keeps its name (2026-11-19)

## Why

The second live walk of **Join with the next** (v2.7963) put a trip on a bill and joined it into the trip before. The line moved to the joined trip as the rule says. Its name did not change: *Trip 12, 1 person, Nov 5 to 7* stayed on the joined Trip 11, which ran Nov 2 to 7. That name goes on the customer's bill, and nothing told the office to look at it. The owner chose a sentence in the window over renaming the line, since the name may be words the office typed for the customer.

## What changed

- **`tripJoinNotes`** in `src/lib/trips/tripJoin.ts` adds `TRIP_JOIN_LINE_NAME_NOTE` after the sentence that says where the line ends up: *The line keeps its name. Change the name on the job's Bill tab if it should show the joined trip.* It shows when exactly one of the two trips holds a line, whether the line stays or moves. A join where both hold one is still refused, and a join with no line says nothing about lines.
- **The guide** `keep-a-trip-to-a-far-site` says the same under *Billing and a trip's line*.

No migration and no new call. `trip_join` is unchanged.

## Tests

`tripJoin.test.ts` gains a case for the four line states, and the window's render test reads the sentence on a line that moves.
