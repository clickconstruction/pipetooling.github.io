# v2.6167 — New Bid lives in the app header, the way the estimator's Bid press always did (2026-10-30)

Feature, no migration, no edge function. The owner's second item of the Bids tab day: *if someone has "bid" in the header they don't see "new bid" on the bid board on mobile*, and then, on the first cut: *instead of new bid wrapping at this viewport … perhaps it should be moved to the header next to the icons as "bid" like estimators have* — and *remove this space* (the two bands around the tab rows).

## Before

`Layout.tsx` drew a blue **Bid** press in the app header for an estimator alone (`role === 'estimator' && !farmModeActive`), on every page and width, opening New Bid through `/bids?new=true`. `Bids.tsx` drew its own **New Bid**: at the right of the tab row on a wide screen (≥1151 px), and below that on a row it shared with the trades — where, with the Trades | GC switch and three trade pills ahead of it, it wrapped onto a row of its own. An estimator on a phone saw two presses a thumb apart; everyone else saw a row spent on one button.

## Now

- **`lib/headerTaskDispatchEstimatorEligible.ts`**: `showHeaderNewBidButton(role, farmModeActive)` — `canOpenBids(role) && role !== 'primary'`, off in Farm Mode: dev, master, assistant, controller, estimator and superintendent (the page's own New Bid rule, less the trades' load). `Layout.tsx` reads it where the estimator literal was; the press stays the word **Bid**.
- **`Bids.tsx`** reads the same rule (Farm Mode from `useFarmModeEnabled`, as Layout does) and draws its New Bid only on the wide header. Below it the header's press is the one New Bid; the empty lists' *New Bid* press (`onNewBid`) is untouched.
- **The space**: below the wide header the stacked strips sit `0.25rem` apart (was `0.65rem`), and the detail row's rule sits `0.75rem` above the body (was `2rem`, on every width).
- `BIDS_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md` (the header paragraph).

## Tests

- `headerTaskDispatchEstimatorEligible.test.ts` (+3): the six roles and Farm Mode; primary, the field roles and no role; Layout draws the header press on the rule alone (the literal is gone).

## Verified

Dev server from the worktree at 1,000 × 800 as the dev: the header's *Bid* and no page New Bid, the strips 4 px apart, the body 12 px under the detail rule; at 375 the same; at 1,400 the header's *Bid* and the page's New Bid at the tab row's right.
