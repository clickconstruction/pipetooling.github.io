# v2.7721 — Edit Job: the top of the Bill tab is its own piece (2026-11-17)

Client only; no migration, no edge function, no screen change. Punch list **#46** row 4 (`to-dos/decomposition-queue.md`): the Job form's second pass, cut 2 (the map's row 11), after v2.7718.

## What shipped

- **`components/jobs/JobFormBillingHeader.tsx`**, new, props only: the top of the form's Bill region. The standalone form's **Billing** title with the billing slice's autosave line beside it, the Job window's right-aligned line (no title there, the tab names the section), and on New Job the `MoneyLifecycleBar`. An open job draws no bar here, since its money is the ② money card (v2.4307).
- **`lib/jobs/jobFormBillingHeader.ts`**, new, pure:
  - `billingHeaderStatusLine` — *Saving…* or *Autosave failed — edit again to retry*, only for an open job, only while the save is in flight or failed. The form wrote this test and these words twice, once for each layout.
  - `newJobMoneyBar` — the bar's hover words, its three slices, its legend rows (each led by its own share of the job total, the Draft row only while a draft holds money) and the *Remaining to bill* row.
- **`JobFormModal.tsx`**: 102 lines of JSX are one mount of ten lines. With v2.7718 the form is 3,751 → 3,658 lines. The header reads the billing slice's status to draw it and nothing else of the autosave engine; the engine, the close guard, hydrate, reset and `createJob` are not touched.

## How it was proven the same

- The whole document's HTML was written out for ten states of the form before and after the cut and compared byte for byte: New Job empty, priced and with the slice reporting a save; an open job standalone with the slice idle, saving and failed; the Job window's Bill tab idle, saving and failed; the Job window's Edit tab. All ten are identical.
- `JobFormModal.billingHeader.render.test.tsx` (7 cases, on the form itself) passes unchanged on the form before the cut. Eight wiring mutants each fail at least one case.
