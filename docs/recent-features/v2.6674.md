## The rule

`src/lib/jobs/lienCourtesyReply.ts` (pure): `courtesyReplyContacts(party, contacts, channels)` names whose line counts — every Messages contact on the GC's customer row, and the contact whose phone or email channel carries the owner of record's number or address (`contact_channels.key`, the spine's own match; `emailChannelKey` mirrors `contact_channel_key('email', …)` beside the existing `phoneChannelKey`), so the read and the send agree on whose answer counts and nothing is made to find out. `lastCourtesyAtByJob(letterTwoByJob, items)` takes the newest courtesy off the first packet the way `courtesyByJobFrom` reads the records; a packet with no courtesy is left out, since a reply closes nothing that never went. `courtesyRepliedAt(notice, lines)` is the newest inbound line after that courtesy on any of the notice's contacts, handed back in the record's own `toISOString` form so the kernel's `repliedAt > last.at` compares like with like (PostgREST returns `at` with an offset, the record keeps a Z). `courtesyRepliedAtByJob` runs it per notice.

## The read

`src/lib/jobs/lienCourtesyReplyIo.ts`: `readCourtesyRepliesByJob(sources)` — three batched selects over the desk's sent notices that carry a courtesy, never per job: `contacts` (`id, customer_id`) by the GCs' customer ids, `contact_channels` (`contact_id, kind, key`) by the owners' keys, then inbound `messages` (`contact_id, at`, `direction = 'in'`) on those contacts since the oldest last courtesy. `readCourtesyOwnerContacts(jobs)` resolves the owners' numbers and addresses through `resolveLienProperty` for a reader without the property rows.

`useLienDeskData` runs the read in both modes — the light read too, because the Dashboard's lien month (`useLienMonth`) builds the courtesy's state from it and would otherwise show due what the desk shows closed — scoped to the notices with a courtesy (usually a handful), the owner's number from the rows already loaded on the full read and from `readCourtesyOwnerContacts` on the light one. The answer rides `LienDeskData.courtesyRepliedAtByJob`; `courtesyByJobFrom` takes it as `repliedAtByJob` and passes each job's to `courtesyStatus`. The modal, `useArLienChips` and `useLienMonth` pass it, so the footer reads *they answered Nov 1*, the Reminders strip moves the notice to Closed (`answered`), the AR chip and the lien month agree — no new words anywhere. A failed read answers nothing closed, never a dark desk.
