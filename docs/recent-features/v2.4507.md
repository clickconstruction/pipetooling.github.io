# v2.4507 — Release of Lien: Unconditional asks before it switches (2026-10-04)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. The owner's ask: a person who clicks *Unconditional* in step 2 should be stopped and told what the form gives up.

## What changed

- **The warning.** In [`LienReleaseModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienReleaseModal.tsx) step 2, the *Unconditional* button no longer switches the form on the click. While the form is conditional it opens the app-wide confirm with `UNCONDITIONAL_WAIVER_WARNING` ([`lienWaiverRelease.ts`](../../src/lib/jobsDocuments/lienWaiverRelease.ts), the owner's words): *Are you sure you meant to choose Unconditional?* · have you spoken to your master plumber · most GCs will accept a conditional waiver even when they ask for an unconditional one · signing one gives up all your rights and is usually done only at the very end of a job, with 100% in hand. **Stay conditional** (blue) changes nothing: no form change, no amount refill, no autosave. **Acknowledge and choose Unconditional** (red) runs the same `pick({ conditional: false })` the button ran before.
- **When it does not ask.** Clicking *Unconditional* while the form is already unconditional; *Progress | Final*; the form the app picks from the bill (`pickLienWaiverForBill`); a window opened on the unconditional follow-up (`initialFormType`, the Dashboard queue and *Add the unconditional*); and *Waive the paid*, which is the app's own offer for money already in hand.
- **`cancelIsSafe`** on `ConfirmDialogOptions` ([`ConfirmDialogContext.tsx`](../../src/contexts/ConfirmDialogContext.tsx)): the cancel button is the blue one and takes the focus, so Enter on a warning keeps things as they are. The button row also wraps now, for a long confirm label on a narrow window. No other caller passes the option; their look is unchanged.

## Tests

`LienReleaseModal.gcWaiver.render.test.tsx` (+1): the warning's words, nothing moved while it is open, the focus on *Stay conditional*, staying keeps the conditional page, acknowledging switches it, and neither a second *Unconditional* click nor *Final* asks again. The two cases that clicked *Unconditional* (`gcWaiver`, `amountMath`) now acknowledge first.
