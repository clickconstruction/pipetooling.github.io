# v2.8586 — Card and footer: anyone who can change a photo can remove it (2026-11-25)

Punch list #443, row 8. The owner's word of 2026-11-25, asked whether the press built for a developer on a test row should be wider: *"Wider, as fixing picked"*. The pick read: **Remove the photo** for whoever may change that photo. No migration, no edge function.

## 1. The press goes wider

**Before.** v2.8565 drew **Remove the photo…** for a developer alone, and refused any person whose name did not start with ZZ.

**Now.** The press is drawn wherever **Change the photo** is, for the same people, on any person who has a photo: a person on their own card in Settings, and the office on a teammate's card from People.

- The rule is one place, `src/lib/brandKit/photoRemove.ts`. `photoRemoveDrawn(person)` asks only whether there is a photo. `photoRemoveProblem` no longer refuses a real person. `removeTestPersonPhoto` is `removePersonPhoto`, and still reads the person again right before the write.
- The line under the press that said only a developer sees it is gone.
- The confirm stays, and its button still reads **Remove the photo**. It gained one sentence: *The team's cards leave this person out until a new photo is saved.*

**The database already allowed it.** `photo_path` and `photo_crop` are granted for update and the path may be null. The bucket's delete policy lets the person or the office remove the file (`20261111200000_person_card_facts.sql`). A write the row rules refuse comes back as words through `cardIo.removePhoto`, as before.

**A person with no photo, read before building.** Until now only a test person could lose a photo, and a test person is in no batch. The team's batch of cards already holds a person with no photo as *No photo*, with its door (`team.ts`, `cardHold`). The card and the footer draw their no-photo ring. Nothing else points at a photo.

## 2. The card read *Add a photo* while a photo loaded

**Seen**, on the live site on 2026-11-25: for about two seconds after the window opened on a person who has a photo, the card read **Add a photo** and *No photo yet*, beside the remove press. Once the picture loaded it read **Change the photo**. The fault is older than v2.8565, which made it show.

**The cause.** The press's words and the line under it asked whether the picture had loaded, not whether the person has a photo. The drawn footer and card did the same, and drew their *No photo yet* ring for that moment.

**The mend**, in `YourCardCard.tsx`:

- The press and its line ask the row (`loaded.photoPath`). A person with a photo reads **Change the photo** from the first draw.
- The drawings wait for the picture. Until it is in, their place reads the card's loading line.

**Add a photo pressed in those two seconds**, read in the code as the brief asked: the press opened the photo sheet with no picture in it, the sheet a new account gets. A photo saved from it replaced the person's photo, as **Change the photo** does, so nothing was lost but the look. The press now reads **Change the photo** in that moment and opens the same sheet.
