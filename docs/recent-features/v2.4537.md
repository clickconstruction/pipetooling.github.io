# v2.4537 — Submittals: the Edit window fits on one screen (2026-10-05)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Finding 12 of the tab review, the first of three redraws the owner picked from [`to-dos/submittals/redraws-10-11-12-before-after.html`](../../to-dos/submittals/redraws-10-11-12-before-after.html) ("Build all three now, 12 then 11 then 10"). The mock-up page rides in this PR.

## What changed

- **One line a part, under headings.** A part was three lines of unlabelled boxes (name; switch, count, house, lead; stage). It is one grid row now — Part · Each · House · Lead time · Stage · Shown to — under one line of headings. LAV-1 on the SpaceX bid (eight parts) went from 1,459 px of content in a 698 px pane to 747 px: about one screen.
- **Three groups, by who sees the part.** *The GC sees these · 3* first, in the order the submittal prints them; *Order only · 5*; *Left out*. The count that was a sentence at the top right is the group's own title. The arrows show on the GC's parts only and trade a part with the next one **in its group**, however many order-only parts are kept between them.
- **Shown to is a small menu**, where a three-button switch sat on every part. Changing it moves the part to that group. Left out is disabled on a part the log holds an order for, and says so in the choice. On an order-only fixture *GC sees it* is disabled.
- **A part the reviewer answered says so under its name**: *Rejected Oct 2 “TEL145”* (also Approved, Revise). The window used to say "1 rejected" at the bottom and leave her to remember which.
- **An empty lead time shows “add” in amber.** The placeholder was *lead: 3 wk*, an example that read like a lead time on every part.
- **The title names the row**: *Edit LAV-1*, with what it answers to beside it (*Specified: TOTO CT708UVG#01*, and *from LAV 1 assembly SPACEX* when the parts came from one). The tag box has a label.
- **The status the row has is lit.** *Proposed* was a status no button showed, so a takeoff row opened with nothing lit and could not be set back. It leads the choices on a row with nothing specified, and stays offered on a row that already is Proposed.
- **A row added by hand opens as *Add a row***, and Save waits until it has a tag or a product.
- Gone: the *Lead time* heading over a sentence saying it moved (the row's lead time is one quiet line once a part has one), and the status chip beside Cancel.
- Status, why and note sit beside the cut sheet (two columns; one on a narrow screen).

## Where the build differs from the drawing

- The window is **1,040 px** wide, not about 940: at 960 the part name had 260 px, at 1,040 it has 384.
- On a phone a part is **three short lines** (name; house and lead time; count, stage and Shown to), not two with the stage behind a tap. Every field stays visible.
- "The same parts editor is used in two windows" on the drawing was wrong: *Choose what the GC sees* has its own list. Only this window changed.
