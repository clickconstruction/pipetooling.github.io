# v2.8352 — Agreements: ready to list a paper that was made and never sent (2026-11-23)

Punch list #403, the second piece of the owner's first decision. Client only. No migration and no edge function. It stands on v2.8348, Take back.

## Why it is its own PR

The decision's pick: the Agreements tab lists every paper that is with an outside party, of any kind and any state. The list is the view `agreements_on_file`, which today answers agreements that were sent or signed. Widening it gives the tab a state it has never read, *not sent*, and kinds of paper it has never drawn. A client that does not know the state reads it as *ended* and would call an unsent paper *Voided*. So the client learns first, and the view's migration comes after it. Nothing visible changes until that migration is pushed: the view answers no such row yet.

## What the client now knows

- **A fourth state, `not_sent`.** Its chip on a line reads *not sent*, the word the party's own page uses, in the same quiet pair. Its day reads *Made Nov 21*.
- **A Not sent chip** in the state row, with its count. It is drawn only while a paper is not sent, or while it is the chip picked, so most days the row is as it was. A count that cannot be read draws no chip.
- **A second tag** on a party's paper that is not an agreement: *W-9*, *Insurance certificate*, *License*. The view will say the kind in `facts.doc_type`. A row that says none draws no second tag.
- **Send** on a line that is not sent (`agreementCanBeSent`). It opens the Send window on that paper for a first send, the same window **Send again** opens. The office names the signer and the address there.
- **Take back** on the same line, for the leader and a dev, from v2.8348. Its confirm says the paper was never sent, so nobody holds a link.
- The default chip, **Sent and signed**, still asks for those two states. A paper that is not sent is under its own chip and under the Search tab.

## Who sees what

An estimator reads these lines as they read the rest: no press. The office's four roles get **Send**. The leader and a dev get **Take back**.
