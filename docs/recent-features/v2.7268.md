# v2.7268 — Lien affidavits: the batch presses take what the find shows (2026-11-14)

Punch list #353 part 1 ([`to-dos/affidavits-picked-set-and-notice-owed.md`](../../to-dos/affidavits-picked-set-and-notice-owed.md)). Client only: no migration, no edge function, and no kernel that is copied to the edge functions changed.

## Before

The Affidavits list's two batch presses took every paper in their pile: **Print N for notarization** printed every approved short-form affidavit and **Send N for approval…** sent every draft. The find box narrowed the list on screen but not what either press took, so the office could not print just the three papers for one property (the owner, 2026-11-14).

## What changed

- **[`src/lib/jobs/lienAffidavitBatch.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienAffidavitBatch.ts)**: `LienAffidavitShown` is the set of job ids the find leaves shown, `lienAffidavitShownEntries(entries, shown)` is what a press takes (every entry with no find), and `lienAffidavitHiddenWords(verb, hidden, what)` is the line under the press: *2 more ready papers are not shown. Clear the find to print them too.* `lienAffidavitPrintWords` and `lienAffidavitSendWords` take `shown: { hiddenReady }`: the button reads *Print 3 shown for notarization* / *Send 2 shown for approval…* (the confirm label carries the verb, `countedLabel`), and `hidden` holds the line. Tests beside them.
- **`LienDesk.tsx`**: `affShown` is the visible rows' ids while a find is typed (memoised on their keys), `affBatchEntries` the entries both presses take; the print plan, the send plan, the print papers and the help sheet read them, so a paper the find hides is neither printed nor listed as held, since nobody held it. The held-only toggle (v2.7209) still reads every paper. Two spans, `data-lien-affidavit-send-all-hidden` and `data-lien-affidavit-print-all-hidden`, draw the lines. Render smokes type a find and read both presses.
- The guide *send lien notices from the Lien desk* says it under the two presses.

## Not changed

- A pile picked in the header without a find narrows the list, not the presses, as before.
- Tick boxes on each row were rejected on the card: the find box already picks a property, a GC or an owner.
