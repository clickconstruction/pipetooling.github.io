- **The update pill**: [`src/lib/phoneSheetOpen.ts`](../../src/lib/phoneSheetOpen.ts) is a small store with a subscribe, the shape of `modalStack.ts`.

  - This covers every `PhoneSheet`, not the write-up sheet alone. A window that is not a `PhoneSheet` is not changed.
- **Words**: the guides *write someone up from my dashboard* and *update the app when a new version is ready* say where the press and the pill sit. `docs/GLOSSARY.md` has the two sentences.
