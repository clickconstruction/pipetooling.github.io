## What was wrong, and what shipped

- **The phone card covered the queue on arrival.** Below 1151 px the open card is a full-screen panel, and `pickOpenAudit` opened the top card on load — so a phone landed on a card, not on the sentence, the button and the queue the mock-up draws. `pickOpenAudit` takes **`autoOpen`** (the tab passes `twoPanes`): where the card would cover the queue nothing opens until a row is tapped, a card left open by the wider layout closes, a tapped one holds (+1 test). `finishAudit` treats *Finish audit → next* on the panel as the tap that opens the next card.
- **Three of the nine "same item" pairs were wrong** (`pairAliases`, `auditCardShape.ts`): *S-1 ↔ Water Heater (DWH)* and *SCAVENGER OUTLET $250 ↔ Scavenger Unit $3,354* paired because every equipment row is ×1, and *4IN DOUBLE SANI WASTE ↔ 3" Sanitary Waste · Tee* on the word *waste*. Three rules: a count of **one** is not "the same count"; two names that state **different pipe sizes** never pair (the size read off the name — `parseRowSignature` drops it on a name it cannot place); a **one-letter tag** (S-3) needs the dollars within 10% as well, since any long name starting with that letter fits it (without this S-1 would have moved to the Scavenger Unit). The six real pairs survive — RENTALS/TRAVEL, EEW, S-3, OM-1, TD-1, RP-1 (+3 tests over the live nine).
- **19 on the button, 16 in the sheet.** Three shared questions each answer two copies, so the run has 16 steps for 19 questions. `runThroughProgress` takes the question count and the header reads **Question 1 of 16 · 19 questions** when they differ (+1 test); the sheet sums its items' copies.
- Guide *audit a robot bid* (the phone sentence). The tab smoke now taps a row before reading the card (jsdom is narrower than two panes) and asserts nothing opens before the tap.
