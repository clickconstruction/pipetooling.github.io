## The gap

Since v2.8238 a stage can be arrived at more than once: a later No is kept as an arrival of its own, and a bullet with a No is asked about again once it has a new yes. The office's screens still read the newest arrival alone. Luis said No on the inspection; Sam got there later and said No on the power. The stage read Sam's No, the power's row said so in red, and the inspection stayed open with no line under it, though Luis's No stood on it.

## The fix

- A `Stage` holds `arrivals`, every arrival oldest first, where it held the newest alone.
- `noOn(stage, bullet)` answers the arrival that said No on the bullet while no yes stands on it since, reading the arrivals newest first. `saidNo` is true when it answers one. A yes given after the No, by the office, on the GC's page or by one of ours who saw it, still clears the cross.
- The office's row, the ready bar's list and the Dispatch line's words draw that arrival's name and day, not the newest arrival's.
- The stage's sentence (*Luis got there Nov 17 and it was not as told*) still reads the newest arrival; the third fault, that it holds after a new yes, is its own change.
