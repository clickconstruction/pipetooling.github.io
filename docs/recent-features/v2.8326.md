# v2.8326 — Painted maps: the magnifying glass on Google Maps is sharp (2026-11-22)

A loose end of punch list [#387](../../to-dos/painted-maps-on-google.md), from the card's *What is left*: *The glass's photograph is soft on Google*. Client only: no migration and no function. The owner picked how on 2026-11-22, from two choices shown with pictures: **ask Google first**.

## What was there

On a phone, a press held still while placing a dot or a corner brings up a round magnifying glass above the finger. Its picture is a second small Google map, made once for the page and for each light or dark look, and kept (`paintedGoogleGlassMap.ts`). Since v2.8235 that map was moved to the big map's own zoom and blown up four times with a CSS `scale(4)`, so it never asked Google for a zoom it had no photograph for. Step 3's walk on clicktooling.com saw the result: a soft photograph, with a street name's letters filling the glass, while the lines and places drawn over it were sharp.

## The choice

- **A, picked: ask first.** Ask Google how deep its photograph goes at the spot, once a held press, with `google.maps.MaxZoomService`. Draw the glass's map that close, up to two steps, and blow it up only by what is left.
- **B: no ask.** Always go two steps closer, capped at zoom 20, where the photograph usually ends. It makes no call, but in a town it stops one step short of what Google has. Sent past its photograph with no ask, Google's map was seen to draw a blank grey glass for 45 s, so the cap is needed.

Google's pages were read on 2026-11-22: the Maximum Zoom guide, the JavaScript API's usage and billing page, the pricing list and the SKU details for Maps. None names Maximum Zoom or `MaxZoomService`, so none says an ask is billed and none says it is free. Dynamic Maps is billed by the map loaded, 10,000 free a month.

## What changed

- **The rule** (`handPlacing.ts`). `glassPictureZoom(mapZoom, depth)` is the big map's zoom plus up to `GLASS_ZOOM_IN` (2), never past the depth, and the big map's own zoom while Google has not said. `glassPictureScale(mapZoom, pictureZoom)` is 4, 2 or 1 for the zoom the glass's map is at.
- **The ask** (`askGlassDepth`, `paintedGoogleGlassMap.ts`). One `MaxZoomService` kept for the page, asked at the spot the press began on, once when the glass comes up and never as the finger moves. A failure leaves the glass as it was. Google's own script keeps the answer for the same spot, and asking makes no map.
- **Streets** is not asked. Google draws its road map at every zoom down to 22 (`GLASS_ROAD_MAP_DEPTH`), so its glass goes two steps closer at its own size.
- **The zoom is read back.** On new ground Google can turn a kept map's closer zoom down for a moment, until it knows how deep its own photograph goes there. Seen on a scratch page with Google's own script: 2 of 5 presses on new ground were refused at first, and a second ask about 30 ms later was taken every time. So the glass draws its picture for the zoom the map says it is at (`zoom_changed`), which keeps the photograph under the lines in every case, and asks again every 50 ms while the map is short of the zoom it wants, for at most two seconds.
- The one kept glass map per look is unchanged. A phone that places or draws still makes two Google maps in a visit, the map and the glass's.
