## The change

- **The call list** ([`GcCallList.tsx`](../../src/components/gc/GcCallList.tsx), G-115): grouped **By company** on a job being built, the chart draws everyone whose answer moves it, worst first, each with every reason under their name (`callList` over the chart's own holds). A line about a bar opens its card. **Their work** sets the chart's one company (G-13), shared with the **One company** picker. **Hide** folds it to its first line.
- **The rows** ([`GcPeopleRows.tsx`](../../src/components/gc/GcPeopleRows.tsx)): the prototype's `PeopleRows`, in the Board lane's file, so its Who to call pill (B2b) wraps them rather than making a second copy.
- **The opened bar's company** (`GcBarCaller`): the bar's card names the company doing it, with **Call**, while its work is not done.
- **Moved word for word** from the prototype, with one known difference held by `schedule-pr7c-ii.lift.json` on the spike: `onFollowUp` (and `onWorkList` on the list) is optional. Unset, **Call** only dials, and neither **Follow up** nor **Work the list** is drawn, until the Board lane lifts the Follow up sheet. The window passes neither.
- **A customer's phone and email** ([`boardRows.ts`](../../src/lib/gc/boardRows.ts), [`gcIo.ts`](../../src/lib/gc/gcIo.ts)): the board reads `contact_info` with the customers it names, and the mapper fills `GcCustomer.phone` and `.email` through `extractContactFromCustomer`, so **Call** reaches a customer too (7c-i stood them in as empty).
