# v2.7084 — Messages: a run that files the mail we already have onto its jobs (2026-11-13)

Punch list #332: the matching rule, the runner and the runbook, and the first read-only count. **Nothing was filed.** No migration, no function to deploy, no screen changes.

## Why

The office's mail about a job sits in thirteen separate mailboxes. v2.7032 made it safe to file old mail onto a job for the record. Grace asked for a run that does it for the jobs we have, and approved building the rule and the runner and counting first.

## What was built

- **The rule** (`src/lib/myMail/backfillMatch.ts`, pure): sure, held or left out for each conversation, with its reason. Sure when a subject names the job (the reader's own `jobInSubject`), when the words name the job's street and the other party is on the job, or when an address on a customer's record is on the mail and that customer has exactly one job and nothing else with us. Everything else is held for a person or left out.
- **The runner** (`scripts/mail-backfill.ts`, `npm run mail:backfill`): `seed`, `count`, `plan`, `apply`, `undo`, over the dev seat's existing verbs. Sheets and the ledger live outside the repo. Every tag carries `for_record`. `apply` asks for `--approved`, caps a run and never retries a filing.
- **The runbook** (`docs/MAIL_BACKFILL.md`).

## What the count taught the rule

The count ran five times on 2026-11-13, read-only, and each run changed the rule or the runner.

1. **The seat's limits.** A read of 200 wide rows and a search of thirteen mailboxes at 100 hits each both came back cut short, and a search is at most 500 characters. The runner now reads in pages by id, asks each mailbox for a few hits first and pages only the ones with more, and packs a customer's addresses to fit. A lost connection is tried again, for a read alone.
2. **The one-job rule was too generous.** The first full count called 586 conversations sure, and three jobs took 456 of them, one job 257. It counted anyone at the customer's company, and ignored a customer's bids and projects. It now needs an address on the customer's own record and a customer with no bid or project, and a job that would still take more than 40 by this rule is held whole (`holdCrowdedJobs`). That count's 529 one-job matches became 8.
3. **A hit carries only its first words.** The street search finds a street anywhere in a mail, so a hit it found now counts as naming that street (`streetJobs`). Street matches went from 34 to 65.
4. **Many jobs share one street.** Of the open jobs' street searches, 30 were for a street another open job also has. A subject's job number now wins over other jobs at the same street, and a street several jobs share is its own hold (`sameStreet`).

## The count that stands (2026-11-13)

162 searches over 13 mailboxes found 5,451 conversations. In 7 searches one mailbox had more hits than the run could read, so this is a floor.

| | Conversations |

The 93 sure conversations hold 305 mails and sit on 20 of the 120 open jobs, at most 15 on one. 59 of the 67 outside addresses on them have no contact yet.
