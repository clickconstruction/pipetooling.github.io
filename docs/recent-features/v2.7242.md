# v2.7242 — Lien desk on a phone: the Lien window and the run fill the screen (2026-11-13)

Punch list #352, PR 7 of eight, the last of the train on the owner's pick of direction A. Cut from main; it needs none of PR 4, PR 5 or PR 6.

## What was wrong

Measured on the dev server against live data, on a phone:

- **A job's Lien window** was a card inset 16 px from each edge and 58 px short of the screen. Its header (the title, the job, the next step, the last day of work, the next-step card) was 320 px and stayed put. With the papers' bar and the footer, the paper showed through 168 px at 390 × 720, and through nothing at 320 × 520.
- **The run window** was inset the same way. Its six steps took 175 px in two rows of three, so the list of envelopes had 266 px at 390 × 720 and 69 at 320 × 520. The run is used at the post office counter, on a phone: its tracking-number boxes were 27 px tall with 13 px words, and an iPhone zooms the whole page when a field under 16 px takes the focus.

## The change

- **Both windows fill a phone's screen.** No inset and no rounded corners.
- **The Lien window's header folds.** `src/lib/jobs/lienPhoneWindows.ts` (new kernel), `lienWindowHeadFolded`: once the paper is 48 px down the header keeps its title line alone, and it returns at the paper's top. It folds only when the paper would still scroll with the header gone, so a short paper cannot flap between the two. Every tab's body is a scroller that is a child of the card, so one `onScrollCapture` on the card hears them all; `lienWindowScrollIsPaper` drops the papers' bar sliding sideways and the supply-house list inside the header. The stylesheet hides the header's other children; nothing unmounts.
- **A press on the title line** (an arrow beside § Rules, 44 px) folds the header and brings it back by hand, and holds that against the scroll.
- **A small phone opens folded.** `lienWindowHeadStartsFolded` is read after every draw until the reader presses the arrow, since the header grows as the job's facts arrive: a header that leaves the paper under 140 px starts folded.
- **The run's six steps are one line.** `lienRunJourneyLine`: the step the run stands on, the first not done, with its count and what blocks it (*Step 2 of 6 · Signed*, then *0 of 7* and who it waits on), amber when blocked. A press unfolds the six.
- **The run's presses and fields.** On a phone every press in the run is at least 40 px tall, and its fields are 40 px with 16 px words.

No migration, no edge function, no change to what a paper says, what any press writes or who may press it.
