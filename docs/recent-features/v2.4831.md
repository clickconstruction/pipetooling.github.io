# v2.4831 — GC mode: the tables for billing the customer (2026-10-07)

Owner Billing's O1 in the GC mode real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/OWNER_BILLING_REAL_BUILD.md`, on branch `spike/gc-mode`). The migration is `supabase/migrations/20261008010000_gc_owner_billing_tables.sql`, documented in `docs/migrations/20261008010000_gc_owner_billing_tables.md`.

- **Seven tables.** `gc_owner_contract_lines` holds the customer's price as signed, by line. `gc_change_orders` holds changes to our contract with the customer. `gc_owner_pay_apps` and `gc_owner_pay_app_lines` hold our pay applications as each went, before the fee is spread. Then `gc_owner_pay_reminders`, `gc_owner_interest_bills` and `gc_owner_acceptances`.
- **Eight columns on `gc_projects`.** The day our contract with the customer was signed, the retainage percent and its step, interest a month, the late fee a day, the days to pay, and `billing_job_id`, the Pipeline job that will carry the bills (decision 1, used from O4a).
- **`gc_sign_owner_contract`.** It writes the signed day and the price by line in one transaction, and the Board's Get started calls it. Signing again moves only the day. A null day clears both, refused once a pay application has gone out.
- **Append only, by privileges.** That covers pay applications, their lines, reminders and interest bills, with two doors: the architect's certificate and a reminder's email. The trade keys are checked at commit, so a billed or signed trade can't be deleted while a whole project still cascades.
- **The schedule's moves key.** `gc_schedule_moves.change_order_id` gains its foreign key, agreed with the schedule lane.
- **Dev only while it is built.** One policy a table for `(SELECT public.is_dev())`, `anon` revoked, and the three house sweeps. Nothing reads or writes them yet. The types come in the PR after the push.
