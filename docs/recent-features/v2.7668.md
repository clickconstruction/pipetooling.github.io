# v2.7668 — Tell the dev: a went wrong note reaches the dev at once (2026-11-17)

Punch list #380, PR 5 of six: the client's half of the push. The start a person picks is also how fast the note should travel. A fault is worth an interruption. A wish and a confusing page are worth a careful read, later.

## What changed

- **The client** calls `notify-help-feedback` for a `broken` note alone, after the row is saved, fire and forget (`sendDevNote`). `want` and `confusing` call nothing.
- **The function's half is v2.7679**, sent and deployed first: it words a page note's push around its page, opens the inbox, and answers a `want` or `confusing` row with no push.
- **Hours are honoured**: a dev outside their Push me hours gets no push, and the Dashboard's *While you were off* line names it.
