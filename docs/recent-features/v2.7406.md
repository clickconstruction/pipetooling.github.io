## What changed

- **The migration** `20261116153000_place_sticker_range.sql` ([`docs/migrations/20261116153000_place_sticker_range.md`](../migrations/20261116153000_place_sticker_range.md)): `place_sticker_range(p_job_id, p_names text[], p_kind, p_place_id, p_where_note)`, `SECURITY INVOKER`, a caller of `place_sticker` once a sticker. For each name: no live box on the site and a blank sticker makes the box on the job with the kind, the place and the where line (`made`); a live box of the name takes its blank sticker (`took`); a name whose sticker is on a box is left (`already`); a name no sticker was printed with makes nothing (`no_sticker`); a name whose writes are refused is undone in its own subtransaction and answered with the reason (`held`) while the rest go on.
- **`src/lib/materials/stickersOnBoxes.ts`**, pure: `onBoxesAsk` (the Names field, through `stickerNames.ts`'s parser, one name alone being one name), `onBoxesPlan` and `onBoxesPlanLines` (what the press will do, counted from the site's boxes and blank named stickers), `onBoxesPressLabel` (*Put 73 stickers on their boxes*), `onBoxesPrefill` (a run's blank names as one typed range, or nothing when one range cannot say them), `onBoxesRequest`, `placeStickerRangeArgs`, `onBoxesAnswerOf`, `onBoxesOutcome` (a sentence a result, the held names apart with their reasons). **`stickersOnBoxesIo.ts`**: `placeStickerRange`.
- **`StickersOnBoxes.tsx`**: one form in two frames, a window on the office page and a sheet on the phone. Names, *How they sit*, the place, the where line, the plan in lines, one press, then the answer.
- **`StickerRunsFold.tsx`**: a named run with stickers still blank has **These are on boxes**, which opens the form on the run's blank names. **`JobsMaterialsTab.tsx`** mounts the form and reads the job, the fold and the names beside the boxes again after. **`TallyOnSiteTab.tsx`**: the same press under the boxes while the job has stickers not yet on a box.
- CI's `sticker-names` bed gains `supabase/tests/sticker_names/30_place_range.sql` and applies the new file a second time.
- Both site guides, the map and `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`. The Glossary's clause came with the types PR, since a queued PR edited the same line.

## The picks

- **The database decides, the form only counts.** The plan under the field is read from the site as the form opened; the function reads again as it writes, and its answer is what the form says after. A form whose read failed counts nothing ahead and still sends the names.
- **A name with no sticker makes nothing.** The press places stickers; a box with no sticker is Add a container's. This keeps it clear of the 200 empty boxes at print time the card rejected.
- **A box already on the site keeps its kind and its place.** The kind and the place in the form are for the boxes it makes.
- **Anyone who may touch the job** may press it, as for one scan: it is `place_sticker`'s own gate, once a sticker.
- **It needs signal**, as a scan's placing does. Only the database can say which stickers are still blank.
