# v2.4989 — Dispatch: one strip at a time, gaps a to c in the mode rule fixed (2026-10-08)

Client only. No migration, no edge function. This fixes three of the six gaps that `useScheduleDispatchHubModes` pinned when it took over the hub's modes (v2.4986), as item 6 of `to-dos/dispatch-residuals.md`. Each fix is one row of the rule in `src/lib/scheduleDispatch/hubModes.ts`, and no hook or page code changed.

## What a user saw, and sees now

- **a. `urlArm`.** Following a `?placeJob=` link while copying jobs linked showed two strips at once: the placing strip and the linked-copy strip.
  - The arm now ends linked copy and the job picker too.
  - So Back or Forward landing on a placing link shows the placing strip, not the picker over it.
- **b. `openAddBlock`.** Opening the add-block window left linked copy on, and its strip came back when the window shut. It also left `?placeJob=` in the URL until the window shut.
  - The window now ends linked copy.
  - It also takes the param off at once. `closeAdd`'s own strip then finds nothing to strip.
- **c. `tabAway`.** The Jobs or Day tab left linked copy and the picker on, so the linked-copy strip stayed on a tab where its blocks cannot be picked.
  - The tab now ends both.

Gaps d to f (`weekNav`, `openCellPicker`, `escapePlacement`) are the owner's calls, and they stay as they were.
