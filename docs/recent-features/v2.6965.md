# v2.6965 — The Bridge's profit after overhead counts the hours still waiting, as the headline does (punch list #303) (2026-11-11)

One of the four items the Bridge's train left open (`to-dos/bridge-one-story/README.md`, drawn in `open-items.html`), on the owner's word of 2026-11-11. Client only. No migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

The eight week chart behind **Money** reads recorded time: every closed session, the ones waiting on approval too, marked ≈. The breakdown under it, **What is left after overhead** (v2.6840), read the loader's day maps, which count approved hours only. So the two disagreed whenever hours waited, and the breakdown had to open on *Reads low for now*. Its *With them it reads about* figure added what the waiting hours would earn and left out their wages, so it read high.

v2.6840 left this open because it expected a change to `loadBridgeData.ts`, which is copied to the edge functions. The loader does not need to change: the page already holds every recorded session of the last thirteen pay weeks for the headline.

## What changed

- **The kernel** (`src/lib/bridge/waitingDays.ts`):
  - `buildWaitingDays` takes the sessions, wages and job rates the headline reads and returns what the waiting field hours earn and cost by work day, as the difference between the grid's recorded and approved readings (`buildVectorDayCells`). So a waiting hour is priced at its job's rate and its person's field wage, and a salaried day is charged once.
  - `buildRecordedReading` adds those days to the loader's approved maps, measures the trailing rates and the margin again (`buildCourseModel`, the page's own settings), and adds to the window's totals.
  - **Nothing moves when no hours wait.** The base is the approved figures the panel has always read; only the waiting part is added. Waiting office and bid hours are left out: they earn nothing here and their wages sit in overhead, which the breakdown reads at its 90-day rate.
- **The panel's kernel** (`profitPanel.ts`): given `waiting`, the panel is `reading: 'recorded'`. `left.approx` is true when waiting hours move the day's figure. `warn` reads *Counts 93 hours that still wait on approval. On approved hours alone it reads $670 a day.*, the second sentence only when the two figures differ. The first two source rows say *recorded field hours* and *counted, and can change*. A loss is no longer explained by approvals, since every recorded hour is in.
- **The drawing** (`BridgeProfitPanel.tsx`): on recorded time the figure leads, with ≈, and the amber line sits under it. On approved time the warning still stands first.
- **The page** (`Bridge.tsx`): `waitingDays` from the weeks inputs; the panel is built on recorded time once they are read. While they are still being read the press waits with the chart above it, so the figures never switch under a reader. If they cannot be read, the panel is the approved reading with its warning, as before.
- **The fallback's guess, put right**: on approved time *With them it reads about* now adds what an approved field hour made, earned less wages (`fieldMadePerHour`, renamed from `fieldEarnedPerHour`).

## Not in this PR

- The window's other readings that sit on approved hours (the net position's profit on paper, the usual fixes' sizes, the cash forecast's payroll estimate) are unchanged.
- The weekly email does not carry the breakdown, so `bridge-weekly-sheet` is untouched.

While hours wait on approval the big number carries ≈ and the amber line under it reads *Counts N hours that still wait on approval*, with what approved hours alone read.
