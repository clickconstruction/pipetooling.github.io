# v2.8347 — Job Mode: My Time is the same picture of your time (2026-11-23)

The second and last PR for punch list #427, after the picture on the full Dashboard (v2.8338). Client only. No migration and no function.

## Why

Job Mode shows no My Time section, so since v2.6709 a card under Your record held the hours block: *Salaried: your week is 40 h*, *16 of 40 h so far*, a percent, and eight bars in four colours. v2.8338 took that block out of the full Dashboard's My Time on the owner's word that a person wants to see the time they are adding up, not a grade. A crew member who lives in Job Mode still met the old block.

## What ships

- **`DashboardMyTimeCard.tsx`**, a card titled **My Time** at the anchor the top row's **This week** square scrolls to (`dash-my-hours`). It draws `DashboardMyTimePicture` and, for an hourly person, `MyTimeCanWorkLine`. A salaried person is asked nothing.
- **`myTimeWeeksIo.ts`**: the card's own read of the person's sessions for the eight weeks, four columns, built by `buildMyTimeWeeks`. `myTimeThisWeekHours` is the week's hours to a tenth, which the card hands the top row through `onWeek`, so the week is still read once. A read that fails hands on no number and draws no card.
- The card reads again when the clock changes, on the signal the clock button already sends, as the block did since v2.8113.
- **`DashboardMyHoursBlock.tsx` is gone**, with its render test. `UtilizationWeekBars` stays for People, Utilization.

A day is not a press in Job Mode, as it was not before: the day editor opens from My Time on the full Dashboard.

## One difference in the square

The old block handed the top row no number for a person with no answer and nothing on the clock, so the square read **My hours** with a clock icon. The card hands on 0 for any week it read with nothing on it, so the square reads *0 h*. It is the true figure, and the picture under it says the same.

## Tests

`DashboardMyTimeCard.render.test.tsx`: the picture under its title at the anchor, an hourly person asked and a salaried person not, the week handed on matching the figure and the square's own words, zero for an empty week, a failed read, and the read made again when the clock changes. That last case fails without the subscription.
