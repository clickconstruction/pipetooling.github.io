# v2.2583 — AR Customers view: fixed day/money mini-columns replace the pace sentence (2026-09-01)

Owner report (follow-up to v2.2581): the right-hand stats block — "88d waiting /
no history — vs company ~6d · $487.50 on 1 job" — was ~330px wide, mostly from
the pace sentence. Chosen from four mocked proposals ("proposal C").

`DashboardArCustomersView.customerRow` right block is now two fixed grid
columns (desktop 58px + 88px, mobile 52px + 80px; grid template updated on the
row container):

- **Days column**: `169d` in the tone color, with `35d avg` beneath —
  `ownMedianDays ?? baselineDays` (the customer's 12-month median, else the
  company baseline). Undated rows show `—` / `undated`. Hover title carries the
  full old sentence ("usually pays in ~35d (their own median)" / "no payment
  history, company average ~6d").
- **Money column**: open dollars rounded to whole dollars
  (`Math.round(...).toLocaleString('en-US')`), with `N jobs` beneath. Hover
  title has the exact `formatCurrency` amount.
- The words "waiting", "usually", and "no history — vs company" leave the row;
  fixed widths make days and dollars align vertically down the list. Freed
  width flows to the flexing bill-bars column.

Also added (owner follow-up): a **color legend** at the right end of the
toolbar row — four `BILL_TONE` dots labeled "on pace / past their avg /
2× their avg / no bill date", with a hover title noting colors read against
each customer's own average pay speed. Wraps under the sort pills on mobile.

Help guide `drill-into-the-dashboard-money-cards.md` example updated to the new
row shape. No schema/kernel changes (`arCustomerRollup` untouched).
