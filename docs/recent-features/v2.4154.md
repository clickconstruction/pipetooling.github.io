# v2.4154 — The Contract window, PR 1 of 5: the signing rail (2026-09-29)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. The owner, 2026-09-29, on a screenshot of the Contract window: *I don't love this modal when it comes to signing contracts, it is not the best we can do. It seems there are multiple paths to sign.* Two design passes (the paper as the form; one question, then one button), then *please build all of it* — five PRs, sequential, each off fresh main: **1 the rail** (this) · 2 the paper as the form · 3 one window across states · 4 a second signer · 5 Job Mode's one tap.

## Why

Seven doors with equal weight across the header and footer — File a signed contract, Preview as customer, Download PDF, Not needed…, Sign in person, Copy link, Send by email — five of them ways to a signature, two exits, one preview; the deciding question asked last, after the form had asked for an email, a mobile and copy recipients the picked way might not need; Send by email enabled with the email blank. The Contract sweep had already solved this for the backlog (v2.3644): one row of ways, pre-picked, one button that follows the pick, a sentence that says what it does.

## What shipped

- **[`contractWindowWays.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/contractWindowWays.ts)** (pure, 5 tests) — `windowWaysPlan({ emailOk, phoneOk, gcJob, gcName })`: *Send a link* (greyed with `LINK_NEEDS` when there is neither an email nor a mobile), *Sign here, now*, *On paper*; a builder's job (`jobTakesTheirSubcontract`, the sweep's rule) leads with *File their subcontract* and demotes ours behind *Send ours anyway*; the default is a link when an email or a mobile is on the job, else paper. `effectiveWindowWay` falls back when the pick cannot run. `windowWaySentence` and `windowWayButton` say what the button does — to whom, from which address, with what reminders — and grey it when it cannot. `windowStatusPill` is the title bar's pill in the chip's words.
- **[`JobContractSigningRail.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobContractSigningRail.tsx)** — presentational: the ways as radio rows (`contract-way-<way>`), the pane for the pick (`contract-pane-<way>`: Signer; a link's Email, Mobile + *Text it too*, Copies, Message, Reminders; Sign here's optional copy address; On paper's *Download to print* / *Email the PDF*), the button (`contract-way-go`) and sentence (`contract-way-sentence`, with *Copy the link* inline), the exits (`contract-exit-file`, `contract-exit-not-needed`), the Not needed panel under them.
- **[`JobContractModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobContractModal.tsx)** — two columns (one under 820 px): the agreement's fields on the left, the rail on the right; the footer and the header door are gone; the pill rides as `headerAction`; the window can go full screen (`fullScreenKey="job-contract"`). The **Standard terms** row names the document with its clause count and date, *Read the wording* opens it in place, *Edit the wording* keeps its reach line; the select returns only with two or more customer documents. New acts: **Send a link** emails (and opens a text when *Text it too* is on), or texts alone when only a mobile is known; **Download & mark handed over** builds the PDF and records the hand-off (`markJobContractHanded`, the sweep's v2.3629 rule, after `handoffBlocker`); **Email the PDF** goes through `share-job-contract` `send_to_sign`. While it is out the rail reads **Nudge** (Resend / Send by email, Text the link, Copy link), **Sign here** (Open the signing page on this device), **Change it** (Edit & re-send while unopened, Void & redo); handed over on paper: **It's back** (File the signed copy), **Change it**. The plain *Download the PDF* stays as a line under the fields and still records nothing. Every test id the reopen test reads is kept.
- **Guide** `get-a-job-contract-signed` → *Send a contract* and *While it's out* rewritten around the rail; **`docs/GLOSSARY.md`** gains *Contract window (Jobs)*.

## Decided here

- **The default is a link when the job has an email.** The sweep defaults a homeowner to the PDF to sign by hand, because that is what has produced signatures in the backlog; the window is where the office is talking to one customer, and the mockup the owner approved picked the link. If a month of use says otherwise, one line in `windowWaysPlan` moves it.
- **Download means the same thing here as on the sweep.** The rail's *On paper* records the hand-off; the small *Download the PDF* under the fields keeps the old behaviour (nothing written) and says so.
- **Not a rewrite of the fields.** The paper-as-form (PR 2) replaces the left column; this PR only moves *Who signs* and *Sending* into the rail's panes so the two changes do not fight over one file.
