## Kept on purpose

- **The cover letter guide's slug.** Its title changed when the studio became the only layout (v2.1895), and its filename did not. A rename would break every shared `/g/` and `/help?g=` link to it, and guides have no alias. So the slug stays.
- **"The bid room signs Option 1 and offers the other options in place of it."** A reader found that on a bid with versions, the room may offer the bases summed as one price (`BidsCoverLetterTab.tsx`, `bidRoomPayload.ts`). Punchlist has Helper 11 verifying it with a two-version test and a read of live rooms. The sentence stays until that settles.
