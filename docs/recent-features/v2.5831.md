# v2.5831 — Phone: edit a customer and see each property’s note from the summary sheet (2026-10-24)

Taunya, an estimator, from her phone: "I can't edit customers on my phone or see additional notes on addresses like door codes." The sheet she was on is the Customer summary (`src/components/dispatchMode/CustomerSummaryModal.tsx`), which the phone dock's **Customers** slot and Job Mode's Customers tab open over the list in `DispatchModeCustomers.tsx`. Since v2.898 (2026-07-22) it replaced the jump to the Customers page there, and nothing on the phone led back to `/customers`, so the edit flow estimators may use (`/customers/:id` → ✎ Edit customer → `EditCustomerForm`, RLS in `docs/ACCESS_CONTROL.md` → Estimator Customers) was unreachable from the dock. The door codes are `customer_addresses.note`, one per property, typed in Edit customer's Properties; the sheet read only `customers.address`, and the customer's page showed the note beside the extra addresses but never beside the primary.

- **Edit customer** on the sheet, beside Close, for the roles whose RLS lets them update customers (dev, master, assistant, controller, estimator; never a sub, a helper, a primary or a superintendent). It opens the app-wide editor through `useEditCustomerModal().openEditCustomerModal(customerId, { onSaved })`; a save re-reads the header and the properties without reloading the feed. A delete or a merge that removes this customer closes the sheet.
- **Open record** to `/customers/:id`, for the roles `isPathAllowedForRole(role, '/customers')` admits; it closes the sheet on the way.
- **Every property under the header**: the sheet reads `customer_addresses` (id, address, note, is_primary, sequence_order) in the same load as the header and draws the primary first, then by sequence order, each a Maps link with its note in full beneath, never clipped. A property with no note reads *No note*. A viewer whose role cannot read `customer_addresses` (a primary or a sub on Job Mode's Customers tab) sees the customer's own address as the one property, as before.
- **Kernel** `src/lib/customers/customerSummaryProperties.ts` (tests beside it): the ordering, the blank-row drop, the stand-in for a missing or unreadable list, the *note or No note* words, and `canEditCustomerFromSummary(role)`.
- **The customer's page** (`CustomerDetail.tsx`): the primary address shows its note in the faint parentheses the extras always had; `fetchCustomerProfile` carries it as `primaryAddressNote`.
- **Words**: the guides *dispatch mode* (the Customers bullet) and *find a customer from my phone* (a *From the dock* section); the stale header comment on `DispatchModeCustomers.tsx` (it said a tap opened the Customers page); `docs/PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` § 1a, `docs/GLOSSARY.md` → Dispatch Mode and `docs/twins/APP_DIRECTORY.md` name the sheet.
- **Tests**: the kernel's; `fetchCustomerProfile.test.ts` for the primary note; the new `CustomerSummaryModal.render.test.tsx` (an estimator's Edit customer opens the editor on this customer and Open record points at `/customers/c1`; the properties in order with the note and *No note*; a sub gets neither door and the one address).
- No migration, no RLS change.

## Verify

On a phone as an estimator: More → Customers → a customer with a property note. The sheet shows Edit customer and Open record beside Close, every property with its note under the address. Press Edit customer, change a note under Properties, save: the sheet shows the new words. Open record lands on the customer's page, where the main address shows the same note in parentheses. As a sub on Job Mode → Customers: no Edit customer, no Open record, the one address.
