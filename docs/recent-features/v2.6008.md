# v2.6008 — Bridge dial: a day more than a year out shows its year (2026-10-28)

Punch list #201. The dial's pile line (*The pile shrinks … a week and is gone by <day>.*) and its field line (*It runs out of work by <day>.*) labelled the day with a month and a day alone. On 2026-10-27 the live sheet read *gone by Jun 4* for a day in June 2028, and on 2026-10-26 *gone by Oct 15* for one about two years out; a reader takes either as the next one. Since v2.5883 the line also goes out in the Monday email.

## What changed

- **Kernel** ([`dial.ts`](../../src/lib/bridge/dial.ts)): `farDayLabel(ymd, todayYmd, dayLabel)` appends the year (`Jun 4, 2028`) when the day is more than `FAR_DAY_DAYS` (335, about eleven months) past today, and otherwise returns `dayLabel(ymd)` as before. `buildDialReading`'s `dateAfterWeeks` goes through it, so both sentences gain the year with no change to their callers (`src/pages/Bridge.tsx`, `src/lib/bridge/sheetBuild.ts`, which keep passing `md`). `dayLabel` itself is unchanged for its other uses.
- **The other `dayLabel` users** in `src/lib/bridge/` were read for a day that can fall a year out: a call's day and the last run's day are in the past, the carrying day and a payer's expected day sit inside the cash window, a bid's due day is weeks away. None can, so none takes the rule.
- **Edge copy** `supabase/functions/_shared/gcKernels/lib/bridge/dial.ts` regenerated (`npm run gen:edge-kernels`).
- **Tests** ([`dial.test.ts`](../../src/lib/bridge/dial.test.ts), +3): `farDayLabel` at ten weeks, across New Year, at the 335-day edge and two years out; the pile line and the field line each with a near day and a far one, through a month-and-day label.

## Deploy

The client deploys with the merge. `supabase functions deploy bridge-weekly-sheet` so the Monday email reads the same line; `npm run check:edge-drift` names anything else behind.
