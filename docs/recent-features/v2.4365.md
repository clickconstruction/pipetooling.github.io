# v2.4365 — Expected dates follow whoever the bill went to (2026-10-01)

No migration; one edge function to redeploy (`payment-forecast-email-dispatch`, after the v2.4362 push). PR 2 of 3 of Grace's pay-speed-by-payer plan (mock-up https://claude.ai/artifact/9C4fxShqPJKnRWVP543RuE, board *Job 1009's dates*). [v2.4362](v2.4362.md) keyed the pay-speed medians and receipts on the payer; this makes every Expected date look them up the same way.

## What changed

- **Kernel** `paySpeedPayer(job, inv)` in [`billToParty.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/billToParty.ts): `{ id, name }` of whoever the bill went to (`effectiveInvoiceParty` + `payerCustomerId`; the GC's name from `job.gcCustomer`), null id for a bill typed to someone else.
- [`billedExpectedPay.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/billedExpectedPay.ts): `ExpectedPayRowInput.customerId` is the payer and `payerName` names it in the hover (*Billed Sep 3 + RMC- Dudley Mason's median pay speed (~35 days over 4 payments…)*; *… Southern Post Construction has too little payment history* on the company fallback).
- Readers: the Pipeline's lien runway, dates block and its *Pays in* line (`JobsStagesTab`: `lienRunwayFor`, `billedLienRunwayRenderer`), the phone row (`phoneNextInput`), the Job window's Invoices list (`useJobBilledExpectedPay`, now handed the payer fields), the payment forecast (`billedPaymentForecast`: estimate, name and segment). The promise records already keyed on the payer (v2.3346), so the *Pays in* line's two halves now describe one party.
- Edge: [`_shared/paymentForecastCore.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/paymentForecastCore.ts) `rowPaySpeedKey` (the payload's `payer_id`, else `customer_id` on an older payload) and `payer_name`.
- Guide *read the pipeline money view*: Expected is the bill date plus the payer's usual pace.
