# v2.3360 — People → Review adopts the Bridge's earned convention: one way of counting a person's revenue (2026-09-12)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Step 1 of the owner's plan for the Bridge ↔ Review fold (2026-09-11): "pick one earned convention — the Bridge's, since it's the one proven against net position; Review's Team Summary adopts it." The math audit of 2026-09-03 had left this as the open "earned-revenue decision" (finding 3: four revenue models across Review, Job Summary, Crew P&L, the Bridge).

## What changed, in the two places Review differed

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| A job with no % complete | value created = contract × 100% | contract × 50%, marked *(assumed)* |
| A finished job (ready to bill / billed / paid) | contract × whatever % is on it (100% if none) | contract × 100%, always |
| A person's share of a job | their labor $ in the period ÷ every contributor's lifetime labor $ (sub sheets included) | their crew clock hours in the period ÷ the job's lifetime crew clock hours |
| Sub labor sheets | earned a share of revenue by cost | a job cost only (no clock hours → no share) |

Both halves are `earnedRevenue.ts`'s rule (expected hours = lifetime ÷ pct; an hour earns contract ÷ expected hours), so hours × rate = value created × share exactly — a person's earned on Review's Team Summary is their earned on the Bridge's Vectors row for the same hours. `reviewEarned.test.ts` proves it against `buildEarnedRevenue`.

Tests: two rewritten (hours share, wage-independence; assumed-half + finished), three sheet-revenue tests now assert no share, one new.
