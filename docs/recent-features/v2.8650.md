# v2.8650 — Lien desk: a payoff card names no clearing day before the money is recorded (2026-11-26)

A fix on punch list #435's payoff card (piece 4, v2.8632), from the first walk of its looking-only recipe on 2026-11-26. Client only. No migration and no edge function.

## What was wrong

One open place in Payoffs drew a value line *Check clears* with a day about five months back. Every lien there stood on **Record the payment ›**, and the card's second stop read *After the money is recorded.* So the line read as a check on its way that clears on a day long past.

`lienPayoffCard` took the clearing day from every row's `step.check`, whichever step the row stood on. A lien still on the money carries the bank's answer on its job's last payment, which may be an old part payment by check. That check is not the one the card waits for. The label read *Check clears*, not *Check cleared*, because the label follows the second stop, which was *later*.

## The mend

The clearing day is read from the liens past the money step alone: waiting for the bank, release owed, at the county, or released. A place whose liens all still owe has no clearing day, and `LienPayoffs` draws no line for it. A place with one lien paid and one still owing shows the paid lien's check, as before.

## Checked

- `lienPayoffCard.test.ts`, 2 new tests: no day while every lien waits on the money, with an old cleared check and with an old check the bank still holds; and the day of the paid lien's check where another lien still owes. The first failed before the mend with the old check's day.
- The Payoffs kernel and render tests pass.
- Not seen live after the mend.
