# v2.8155 — Document builder: Publish to N jobs goes to the jobs it says, or publishes nothing (2026-11-21)

Punch list #404. Taunya asked for it on 2026-11-21, after v2.8153 left it open. No migration and no function.

## What was wrong

v2.8153 made the usual's publish read each job at the press, so a teammate's change to a job was kept and the job held. But the press's count, and the rows that say which jobs get the change, were still made from what the page knew when it loaded. A press that read *Publish to 3 jobs* could publish the usual and go to two. The outcome said so afterwards, which is too late for a press that says what it will do: the usual was already out as a new revision.

## The fix

The jobs are read **before** anything is published, and the plan made from that read is held against the plan the press showed.

- `readSheetsNow` (`sheetsIo.ts`) reads every job's sheet. `usualPlanOfSheets` makes the plan from them and from what the page holds beyond them. `sameJobsGetIt` (`sheet.ts`) says whether the same jobs get the change as the press showed. It holds the jobs themselves, not their count, so one job becoming ready as another is held does not pass.
- **The same jobs:** the usual is published, then each job, from the sheets just read. Nothing is read twice. Each job's write still names the draft's number, so a change that lands in between is refused and named, as before.
- **Not the same jobs:** nothing is published. The page takes the jobs as just read, the rows and the press are drawn again from them, and a line says *Nothing was published yet*, with the reason and *Press it again*. The next press does what it now says.
- **The jobs do not read:** nothing is published, and the reason is shown. v2.8153 published the usual first and then said it had gone to no job.

`publishUsualToJobs` no longer reads: it takes the sheets handed in and answers the outcome.

The page takes a job's sheet as read only where it holds no words of its own for it: none typed and waiting on a save, none unsaved, none waiting on a choice, and none typed since the read began.

## What it does not do

It does not read the jobs while the page sits open, so the count can still be out of date until the press. The press is where that is caught, before any write. A teammate's change in the moment between the read and the usual's own publish is still possible: the job's write is then refused and named, and the usual is out. That window is one request long.

## Tests

- `FilledBuilder.movedOn.render.test.tsx`: the page loads, a teammate's draft arrives, and the first press publishes nothing, says why, and redraws the press as *Publish your usual*. The second press publishes. On v2.8153 the first press published the usual. Two more: the same jobs go through on the first press with the jobs read once, and jobs that do not read publish nothing.
- `sheetsIo.test.ts`: the plan read at the press against the plan shown, and the batch from sheets handed in.
- `sheet.test.ts`: `sameJobsGetIt`. `sheetScreen.test.ts`: the words, as plain words.
