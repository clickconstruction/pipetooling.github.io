# v2.5317 — Contracts and clauses: the approvers get a push and an email (2026-10-12)

Messages cut 4c's edge piece (`BUILD_PLAN.md` → Cut 4c, Edge), built by helper 6 for the GC Review lead. A change to a contract clause from anyone but a controller or a dev waits as a proposal (cut 4c, `20261012150000_contract_clauses`). Until now nobody heard of it until they opened their Needs you.

- **`messages-notify`** ([`EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`](../EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md#messages-notify)). The app calls it right after the RPC: a client call, not a row trigger (the lead's call, below).
  - **On a proposal**: the approvers get a push on every device and an email with the change. The approvers are role `dev` or `controller` (`gc_clause_approve_team()`), `REAL_ACCOUNT`, not archived.
  - **On a decision**: the editor gets an email. Approved, it is the clause's next version and the next paper sent carries it. Declined, it carries the why.
  - **Security**: the caller names only rows. The function refuses anyone but the proposer or the decider, and only within 15 minutes of the change. It writes every word itself.
  - **Batching**: the changes one save sends are one push and one email per approver, so the New paper window's clauses never buzz once per clause.
  - Each person reached gets a `notification_history` row per channel.
- **The words**: [`_shared/clauseNotify.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/clauseNotify.ts), re-exported at `src/lib/contracts/clauseNotify.ts`.
  - The push body is the change's Needs you title and the clause's first words.
  - The link is the paper's card, `/settings?tab=settings-clauses#settings-clause-paper-<paper>`.
  - The function keeps its own copy of the clause numbering and the paper's name. `clauseNotify.test.ts` holds both to `assemble.ts`'s `clauseLabel`, `approval.ts`'s `proposalTitle` and `clauseRows.ts`'s `paperFromRow` and `clausePaperHref`. Reversing the exhibits' order on purpose failed it.
- **The app**:
  - `clausesIo.ts` gains `tellClauseApprovers` and `tellClauseEditor`. Both are best effort: null on any failure, and the change still waits on the Needs you.
  - The four call sites call them after their RPC and toast from what the call reached: `ClauseEditWindow`, `ClauseDeclineWindow`, `ClauseNeedRows`, and `SettingsClausesTab`'s approve.
  - `CLAUSE_NOTIFY_LIVE` is now true.
  - `proposedToast` takes `ClauseReach` (`reachOfTold`) and says *with a push and an email*, *by email* or *with a push* as reached. With nobody reached it keeps *It waits for … to approve it*.
  - `approvedToast` and `declinedToast` say *an email* where they said *a text*.
  - The decline window's line reads *Rosa gets your why by email. It stays on the clause's history.*
- **The Needs you line states the rule** (the lead's call, option b): *Robert and Maria get a push and an email.* That is true whether or not one call went. It replaces *A push and an email went to …*, which no row could know.
- **Not a trigger** (the lead's call):
  - Every staff notifier here is client-invoked and checks the caller against the row. `pg_net` is used only from `pg_cron` with the vault's secret.
  - A trigger would fire once per proposal row.
  - The client call needs no migration, and lets the toast say what went.
  - The cost: a dropped call loses the buzz, while the change still waits on the Needs you.
- **Follow-up, named**: the editor's text by phone on a decision comes with Quo (cut 5). The New paper window (#5476) gets one `tellClauseApprovers` call for its proposed clauses once this lands.
- **Registrations**:
  - Every recipient is our own staff (approvers and editors). So no sent copy, no `EMAIL_CATALOG` row, and none of the four registrations an outside email needs.
  - `CUSTOMER_SURFACES` gets one staff `sender` entry with its exempt reason, and `sentCopiesEmailCoverage.test.ts`'s `NOT_OUTSIDE` gets the function with its reason.
  - `supabase/config.toml` gets `verify_jwt = false`.
  - `dev-mcp/catalog.ts` is regenerated to list the function.
- **Deploy**: `bash scripts/deploy-functions.sh messages-notify dev-mcp` after merge (the lead).
