# v2.4460 — Pipeline: a bill marked billed in the evening reads that day (2026-10-02)

One of the instant-day fixes that follow [v2.4452](v2.4452.md); [v2.4459](v2.4459.md) named this family and left it for its own sweep. No migration. [`_shared/paymentForecastCore.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/paymentForecastCore.ts) is bundled by `payment-forecast-email-dispatch` and, through `_shared/moneyWaitingCore.ts`, by `money-waiting-email-dispatch`; both are redeployed after merge.

## What was wrong

The Pipeline's billed money cut a day out of `jobs_ledger_invoices.billed_at`, a `timestamptz`, by its first ten characters, so a bill marked billed after 7 pm CDT (6 pm CST) read as the next day:

- **`billedReferenceYmd`** in [`billedExpectedPay.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/billedExpectedPay.ts), and its server twin in `paymentForecastCore.ts`, whose header said so (*slices the RAW billed_at ISO (UTC date)*). Every expected-pay date counts from it: the chip on Pipeline rows and phone cards, the job window's Bill tab (`useJobBilledExpectedPay`), the Billed line of a row's dates block (`buildBilledDatesLedger`, `datesBlockBilledYmd`), Payment forecast and Money waiting (their modals and their two emails), the chase queue's lateness and the Bridge's receipts.
- **`billedRowReferenceYmd`** in [`invoiceBilling.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/invoiceBilling.ts): the date and age on the printed Billed awaiting payment report, the aging chart and the GC review. The board's own age in the same file (`stageRowBilledAgeReference`) has read the day in Central since v2.2130, so the two could disagree by a day.
- **The BILL line** ([`stagesJobReferenceDates.ts`](../../src/lib/stagesJobReferenceDates.ts)): `collectBillingCandidates` put `sent_to_customer_at` and `billed_at` through `timestampOrDateToYmd`, whose `trimYmd` keeps the first ten characters of any timestamp. That is the BILL line on Pipeline rows and cards, the Job Detail middle row and the Job Summary header.
- **The chase queue** ([`paymentChase.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/paymentChase.ts)): its `ymdFromIso` helper, a regex slice a `_at.slice` grep misses, gave the card's *billed Oct 3*, the base of a *net 30* promise, and the day of the last call (`createdAt` from `list_payment_chase_touches`), which starts a can't-reach snooze and the quiet spell. The dispute card's *flagged Oct 3* sliced the same `createdAt`.
- **The bill row's *sent Oct 3*** (`stagesBillRowLine`, from `StagesUnifiedInvoiceRow` and `JobsStagesCardList`), and the bill day the Record payment window measures a promise backfill from (`billedYmd`, from `JobsStagesTab` and `JobFormModal`). That window's *Sent:* line sliced `sent_to_customer_at` the same way.

## What changed

Each reads the instant's day in `APP_CALENDAR_TZ`: `calendarYmdInAppTzFromIso(iso)` from [`dateUtils.ts`](../../src/utils/dateUtils.ts) on the client, `todayYmdInAppTz(new Date(iso))` from [`_shared/appTimeZone.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/appTimeZone.ts) on the server, guarded because it throws on an Invalid Date. An unreadable `billed_at` falls back to the estimated bill date, like a missing one. `paymentChase.ts` drops `ymdFromIso` and takes a bill's day from `billedReferenceYmd`, the day its lateness already counted from. `timestampOrDateToYmd` still reads `paid_on`, a `date`; its comment now says it is for dates.

The [Bridge](../../src/pages/Bridge.tsx) passed its bill day (`dateYmd` from `dashboardFinancials`, already a day) as `billedAtIso`. Read as an instant, a bare day is midnight UTC, the evening before in Central, so it would have moved every receipt a day early. It now passes the day as `estBillYmd`, which the kernel keeps as a day. Every other caller passes `inv.billed_at`.

The SQL side already reads Central: the pay-speed RPCs (`pay_speed_samples`, `get_billed_customer_pay_speeds`, `get_pay_speed_transactions`) take `(billed_at AT TIME ZONE 'America/Chicago')::date`, and both email payloads pass `billed_at` whole with `today` in Chicago. The sample under Settings → What the team sees stamps its bills at noon UTC, so its `ref_date` reads the same day either way; it now uses the helper too.

## Not changed

- A bill's age on the board counts to today's UTC date (`calendarDaysSinceDateUtc`'s `now`), so after 7 pm Central every age reads a day older. That is a "today" bug, not an instant's day, and is left for its own fix.
- The job window's Bill tab reads its own sent days (the Invoices list, a payment's bill line, the Stripe panel) and the Job Summary's Cycle view reads its own bill day. Those are their own fix.

## Tests

- `billedExpectedPay.test.ts` (+4): `2026-10-03T00:30:00Z` (7:30 pm CDT, also as `+00:00`) reads Oct 2, `2026-12-02T00:30:00Z` (6:30 pm CST) reads Dec 1 and noon UTC reads its own day; the server twin reads the same days, and an unreadable `billed_at` falls back to the estimated date; an evening bill's expected date counts from Oct 2; the Money waiting email's wait for it is 10 days on Oct 12.
- `invoiceBilling.test.ts` (+1): the printed date reads *Oct 2, 2026*, 10 days, matching the board's age reference.
- `stagesJobReferenceDates.test.ts` (+1): the BILL line reads an evening bill or send as its own day.
- `paymentChase.test.ts` (+1): the card's billed day, a *net 30* promise from it, and a can't-reach snooze from an evening call.

All seven fail on main's kernels. Fixtures that stamped `billed_at` at midnight UTC (`gcReviewRollup.test.ts`) or passed a bare day (`stagesJobReferenceDates.test.ts`) move to midday and keep their expectations.

## Checked against prod

Read only, from the worktree dev server: 16 of the 530 billed or paid bills were marked billed in a Central evening, none of them among the 83 open bills, and 3 were sent in an evening, none open. So no open bill's chip, chase card or printed line moves today. One of 668 jobs has an evening bill as its latest billing day, and its BILL line now reads Dec 9, 2025 where main read Dec 10. None of the 17 chase calls logged in the last 180 days was made in a Central evening.
