Docs and help content only. One new guide, one pointer line in an existing guide, two to-dos updated, both to-do views regenerated. No source, no migration, no edge function.

## Why

The owner: *"We have recorded the important rules of liens in texas. I would like to expose them somewhere in the app for user Tanya to see the code."* The rules had one home, the table in `to-dos/texas-lien-rules-in-the-app.md`, which the office never opens. The app's surfaces quote section numbers inline (the demand letter's basis strings, the Lien desk's gate labels), but nowhere listed every rule the app applies with its cite and what the app does about it.

This is PR 1 of that to-do: the guide. PR 2, the *§ The rules* doors on the Lien desk header and the Lien window, waits for the open Lien-window PR #3272 so the two do not collide.
