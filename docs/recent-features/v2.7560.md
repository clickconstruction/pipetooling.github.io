# v2.7560 — Plans by date: short names on the chart, and the list leads with the drawings (2026-11-16)

Punch list #367, the polish after the train (v2.7494, v2.7496, v2.7499, v2.7500, v2.7501). It comes from the first look at the view on a real site in production, read-only, after the last deploy: a site with several parts, dozens of drawing sets, a long clock and a long History. The made-up rows the train was tested with had short names and a short log, and both hid what real data does. No migration.

## What the real site showed

- **Long names.** Real set names ran 32 to 65 characters, most ending in the date the axis already shows. On a phone the set a part builds from was wider than the chart, so its name was not drawn at all. On a desktop the names that did draw were 200 to 400 px bands of text.
- **Names stacked between lanes.** A name drawn under one lane and a name drawn over the next could stand 16 px apart at the same spot, so it was not plain which lane each belonged to.
- **A drowned list.** The owner's hand chart listed *every issue* under it. On the real site most of the list's lines were kept History emails, the drawing issues were buried among them, and the print would have run several pages instead of one.

## What changed

- `plansByDateChartName` draws a set's name short: the date the mark stands on comes off, in any of the ways names write it (*… - 2026-09-14*, *(2026-06-23)*, *(2025-11-03, ours)* reads *(ours)*, *… 2025-11-20*), a date that differs from the mark's stays, and a name longer than 32 characters (24 on a phone) is cut at the last whole word that fits, never on a separator or an abbreviation's stop. The card, the list, the hover line and the print's list keep the whole name. On the real site a desktop now draws more names, each short and beside its own mark, and a phone names every part's current set.
- `placePlansByDateLabels` takes the names already drawn under the lane before (`takenAbove`), so the gap between two lanes holds one row of names. A name that would stand on one goes below its own mark, or into the hover and the list.
- `plansByDateListRows` opens the list on the drawings and the bids (with the schedule's two lines and today), and **Also show the N crew and History lines** adds the rest; **Show only the drawings and the bids** goes back. A site with no drawing or bid line lists everything. The list folds only past 60 lines now, to its newest 40, so a list a few lines over forty is not cut for a handful. The print lists what the screen lists, and says so under the list when it leaves the log out (`PLANS_BY_DATE_PRINT_LIST_NOTE`), so the real site's print is one page again.

## Retired

The punch-list card `to-dos/plans-by-date/` (#367) is deleted: the train shipped, the migration is pushed, and the live page was looked at. The release notes and these fragments carry the record.

## The look itself

Read-only, in a new tab, on the owner's own sign-in: the By date view on the real site, a set's card (its memo, what it replaces, who added it, **Open ↗**, **Set the sheet count**, **See it in By part**, **Ask the estimators**), a bid's card with **Set the schedule**, the schedule window opened and cancelled, Escape closing a card, a clean console. The Plans view was put back to By part, removing its saved preference as it was. No schedule, sheet count or other row was saved. The first load served the build before the last deploy from the service worker's cache; the next load had it.

## Verify after deploy

Open a development with long set names and press **By date**. Each current set's name is drawn short and bold, on a desktop and a phone, and no two names stack between lanes. The list opens on the drawings and the bids, and the press adds the rest. The print is one landscape page.
