# v2.6232 — A residential customer's steps read from their job (2026-10-31)

Fix, no migration, one edge function to redeploy (`messages-nudge-dispatch`). The gap v2.6224's fragment names: the residential flow's loader, `residentialStampsFor` in `src/lib/flows/residentialLoad.ts` (v2.5395, moved out of `load.ts` in v2.5490), had no caller outside its tests. `flowStampsFor` has no residential branch and gave a `residential` person empty lanes, and `personFlowFrom` called it for every kind but candidate and sub, so the step card (`useFlowPerson` → `readFlowPerson` → `personFlowFrom`) and the morning run (`nudgeRunInputs.ts`'s `nudgeRunReads`) both read a residential customer as having done nothing, and the residential Approved, Paid and Review asked reminders (on Offers on prod) were never due.

## Where the Pipeline rows come from

**Beside `flow_rows`, not in it.** `flow_rows_for` is documented and granted as days and marks, never an amount or a price, and the loader reads bills and payments through the Stages tab's own rules (`billTruth`, `billedExpectedPay`, `billToParty`, `paymentAttribution`). So the client and the run select the Pipeline tables themselves, as `flowPersonIo.ts` already does for a candidate's board row and a sub's sheets. No migration, so nothing to order against the client deploy.

- **`src/lib/flows/residentialRows.ts`** (new, pure): `PIPELINE_SELECTS` names the columns each read selects, the loader's row types and `job_name` for the card; `PipelineRows` is what was read, one customer's or everyone's; `residentialRowsOf(rows, who)` cuts one contact's `ResidentialRows` out of it: the jobs they are the customer on (`contacts.customer_id`) and the ones the office tied to them (`contact_jobs.jobs_ledger_id`), everything on those jobs, their own texts out, `flow_rows`' skips mapped onto the Pipeline job, and the two RPCs parsed by the loader's own parsers (`parsePaySpeedsRpc`, `parsePromisedPayDatesRpc`). Every list is theirs alone, so another customer's payment never pays their bill. `pipelineJobNames` gives `PersonFlow.jobs` each job's name, which fills `{job}`.
- **`load.ts`**: `FlowRows.residential?: ResidentialRows | null`, a type import only, so the bills' rules stay out of `load.ts` (the v2.5490 rule).
- **`person.ts`**: `personFlowFrom` picks `residentialStampsFor(rows.residential, now)` for the `residential` kind when the rows are there, and `flowStampsFor` (empty lanes) as before when they are not, so a board that reads residential contacts set-wise through `flow_rows_kind` still reads. `jobs` names a residential customer's Pipeline jobs.
- **`flowPersonIo.ts`** (the step card): the contact select gains `customer_id`; a residential contact's read goes on to `residentialOf`: their jobs by customer id, their ties, their texts and the two RPCs at once, then the seven job tables by job id. The RPCs read soft (a role the gate refuses reads no pace, and the bill's due day stays unset); a table the reader may not read fails with its reason through `check`.
- **`nudgeRunInputs.ts`** (the run): `RunContactRow.customer_id`, `NudgeRunRows.pipeline?: PipelineRows`; `nudgeRunReads` takes a residential contact's customer id from the contact when the card names none (`flow_rows_for` puts `customer_id` on the card for `customer` and `arch` only), and spreads `residentialRowsOf`'s cut onto their flow rows before `personFlowFrom`.
- **`supabase/functions/messages-nudge-dispatch/index.ts`**: the contact select gains `customer_id`; `readPipeline` reads everyone's rows at once, a hundred ids a chunk and paged past 1,000, only when a residential contact exists (prod has none today, so the morning reads nothing new), and calls the two RPCs as the service role (both grant it); a refusal is logged and leaves the pace null. `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` → messages-nudge-dispatch carries the note.
- **The kernel copy** (`npm run gen:edge-kernels`) gains nine files: `flows/residentialLoad.ts`, `flows/residentialRows.ts`, `jobReportsTimeline.ts`, `jobs/paymentAttribution.ts`, `jobs/jobFormatting.ts`, `personNameKey.ts`, `reportSignatureField.ts`, `reportTemplateFieldDisplay.ts` and `subLaborOutstanding.ts`; the four `_shared` kernels the loader reaches (`billTruth`, `billToParty`, `peopleLaborJobItemLineCost`, `billedExpectedPay`'s pace) were already pointed at or copied. `deno check` of the function passes. Every importer whose bundle reaches `person.ts` shows on `check:edge-drift` until redeployed.
