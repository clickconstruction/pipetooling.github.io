# v2.7634 — Prospects: the page's data is one piece the tabs read through (2026-11-17)

Step 10 of the order in `docs/PROSPECTS_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md`: the data seam, the step the map called its highest risk. No JSX moved. No behaviour changes.

**`useProspectsData`** (`src/components/prospects/useProspectsData.ts`, 300 lines) holds what every tab reads:

- the deck (`followUpProspects`) with the pointer to the card that is up, and the effect that follows `?prospect_id=`;
- the list (`prospectListProspects`) and the last-call map;
- the Subs lane's two more caches (every row for the path strip, the whole queue before the trade filter);
- the call-history sets that travel with them;
- the two loaders, with the newest-load-only guard and the calling-order ref they read;
- the calling order itself: its state, the remembered order read on entry, and the switch;
- `updateUrlProspectId` and `markCalledLocally`.

The code is the page's, cut by script. The page calls the hook, destructures it under the names its handlers already used, and still patches the caches itself through the setters the hook hands back. The three tab-keyed effects that decide *when* a loader runs stay in the page.

**`useSubsBoardData`** (100 lines) holds what the Subs Board reads beside the lane's rows (the pins by trade, who wrote back, the upcoming callbacks) and its pin writes. The data hook takes its two loaders as parameters.

**One kernel: `orderSubsDeck`** in `src/lib/prospects/subsDeckOrder.ts`, the map's Stage-A row 8. It is what the deck loader did to the Subs queue between two awaits: a run's ids in their order, else the trade filter, By need, or By rank with the named trade's pins. The loader now reads the pins before it calls the kernel, when the order is By rank and there is no run: the same read, at the same condition.

**One circle, and how it is cut.** The data hook needs the jobs hook's `mergeJobs`, and the jobs hook needs the card the data hook finds. The page hands the data hook an arrow that calls `mergeJobs`; a loader only runs after the render that made both.

**The calling-order switch** returns whether it switched, and the page folds the phone's order sheet when it did. The sheet's flag is the page's.

**Size.** `Prospects.tsx` went from 3,358 lines to 3,092: seventeen pieces of state, two refs, two effects and ten functions fewer.
