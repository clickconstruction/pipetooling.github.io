## The window: Take this job off the lien path?

`LienJobPathWindow`, one window per job. It reads the job itself (`readLienJobPath`), so it opens the same from the Lien desk and from the job's own Lien window, and it shows the way off or the way back by where the job stands.

- **What this job billed**: the bill's own lines (`jobs_ledger_fixtures`), each with its money. One line of small type says what a lien is for, as the app reads it, and to ask the attorney when it is not clear. **The app judges nothing.** A person reads the bill and says why.
- **Its lien papers**: what already went out, in the public window's own sentences (`lienPublicWhatWentOut`).
- **Why · kept on the job**: required. The press is grey until a reason is typed.
- **Also put it on the attorney's list**: a tick, off by default. A job the Collections flag will hold (not billed yet) is named before the press, and it still leaves the lien path. A job already on the list has no tick and says since when.
- **What this press does**: five lines, said before the press. The job leaves the desk; nothing is filed, voided or deleted; nobody is reminded of its lien dates and they still run; the list, by the tick; the name, the day and the reason are kept.
- The press names the act and the job: *Take 977 off the lien path*.

The toast says what happened, and what the attorney's list answered when it was asked. A refusal says the job is still on the lien path, with the function's own reason, and the window stays.

**Put this job back on the lien path?** is the same window on a job that is off: why it came off, who took it off and the day, a required reason for the way back, and four lines that say what the press does, the second being *Its lien dates did not stop while it was off. Check each one first.*
