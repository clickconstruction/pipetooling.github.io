# v2.5593 — Schedule: booking a job’s first visit asks before it texts the customer, and shows the receipt with the call to make (2026-10-16)

PR 2 of Tell the customer (punch list #115, `to-dos/tell-the-customer/README.md`; the plan and mock-up #5797, the build plan and the autonomy pass #5798). Grace's principle: nothing reaches a customer without a person pressing it, or seeing it the moment after. This PR carries the principle on the schedule: when the office saves a job's first visit, a sheet opens before anything goes, and a receipt follows the send with the call to make. No migration; no edge function change in behaviour (`blanks.ts` is in the edge's kernel copy, so every importer of the copy redeploys).

- **The sheet** (`src/components/customers/TellCustomerSheet.tsx` over the pure `src/lib/tellCustomer/tellCustomer.ts` and `tellCustomerIo.ts`): the job and the visit at the top; under *What goes*, the text ticked with the number it goes to (the customer's own thread through `contact_for_customer`, the office's door, and the oldest phone on it) and the words in a box the office may change (the reply box's grammar: the office writes free text; a blank left in braces holds the press and says which); *Email* unticked with *No email goes for a booking*; *What happens* rows; **Skip** and **Send text to Dana**. The words are the office's team line for customers on the Booked step (`stepLine`), in the customer's language, else the app's own (`DEFAULT_LINES`), filled by `tellFills` from the block: their first name, ours, the job, its address, the day (*Friday, October 23, 2026*), the window (*10:00 AM to 12:00 PM*) and the tech, a new blank **`{tech}`** in `quickReplies/blanks.ts` (*the tech coming, from the block*; the editor's chip row shows it). The send is the reply box's own path (`supabaseReplyIo().sendText`, `quo-send` tied to the pipeline job), so the opt-out line, STOP, the pause and the switch all hold as they do from the thread.
- **The receipt**, the same window's second view: the words as they went, *Text · from the company number · 2:41 PM · in Dana's thread*, then *Call Dana to talk it through?* with the script filled for her (`DEFAULT_SCRIPTS`, the office's own per step comes with the Flows editor in PR 5), **Call (830) 555-0121** as a `tel:` link (`telHrefFor`; one tap on a phone, the number on a desk, Quo places the call and the webhook writes it into the thread) and **Done**. *Add to my calls* comes with the call task in PR 5.
- **The holds** (`tellHold`, in order): not the office team, no customer on the job, texts off, no number on file, STOP; each said in its row with the button reading **Close**. A tech never opens the thread.
- **Where it opens** (`src/components/jobs/ScheduleJobModal.tsx`): after a successful save by an office member, when the job has no other block than the group just saved (`isFirstVisit`), on Add job to schedule from the job and from the Dispatch hub's picker. The modal ignores Escape while the sheet is open. Later visits, Quickfill's rows and Dispatch mode's quick assign do not open it; a moved first visit (the Rescheduled line, `DEFAULT_LINES.rescheduled`) is drawn in the kernel and waits for the block editor's hook in the next PR.
- **The customer's side**: the homeowner journey gains **Booked text** (`customerJourneys.ts`, rendered as the customer text sample) and `quo-send`'s registry row names it; the guide *schedule a job and tell the customer*.
- **Tests**: `tellCustomer.test.ts` (the fills, the line and its fallback, the blanks left, the holds in order, the rows, the receipt's line and script, the words plain); `TellCustomerSheet.render.test.tsx` (the fill, an edit, the send and the receipt with the dial; Skip; a blank holds; the app's own words; each hold; a tech). `blanks.test.ts`, the registry and journey tests, `edgeKernels.test.ts` regenerated.

**After the merge**: `npm run check:edge-drift` names every importer of the kernel copy; redeploy them. **Not walked live before the merge**: a send texts a real customer; the walk is on a test job whose customer's number is the owner's own.
