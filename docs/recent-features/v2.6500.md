# v2.6500 — Waiting on them clears when they accept the estimate, when you log their call, or when you press Heard back (2026-11-04)

Taunya's report of 2026-11-03 (`/messages?kind=customer`, Follow up, Customers, Everyone · 16): under **Waiting on them · 2** sat Hadlow Construction Group and Evan Jarrow, each *Their move · 14 d* with our Oct 20 estimate text as the last word and only **Nudge** and **☎ Call** on the row. Evan had approved his estimate through our link; Taunya had confirmed Hadlow's by text from her own phone. Neither row could be cleared. One cause for each: the lane's read (`waitingOnThemIo.ts`) loaded only `messages` with `direction in ('in','out')` and a text, email or call channel, so the **event** line `accept-estimate` writes into the thread (`estimate:<id>:accepted`, v2.5705) was never seen and Evan's row stayed after he said yes; and a text or call made from a personal phone never reaches the thread, and the row had no press to say the office heard back. No migration; no edge function change.

## What changed

- **Their word is more than a line in** (`src/lib/messages/waitingOnThem.ts`): `theirWord(line)` is the one rule. A line in is theirs. An event line is theirs when its key is `estimate:<id>:accepted` or `estimate:<id>:declined` (`estimateAnswerOf`; an `opened` key, a rating line, a take or the customer's say count for nothing and never restart the wait) or when its body is a Heard back line (`heardBackOf`). A call line of ours is theirs when it is one the office logged with what they said — `log_call`'s shape, `callLog.ts`'s new `isLoggedCall` beside `callLineBody` — since it carries their words; a call Quo recorded stays ours. The kernel keeps each person's newest line that is theirs or ours and draws the row only when it is ours and two days old, so an answer before our text does not clear it and a text after their answer starts the wait again. `WaitLine.direction` gains `'event'` and `key` (the `quo_id`).
- **The read** (`waitingOnThemIo.ts`): a second read after the first, the thread's event lines of the thirty days whose key is `estimate:*` or whose body has the shape of a Heard back line (`ANSWER_EVENTS_FILTER`, one `or`), at most 500, mapped with `direction 'event'`; the kernel decides which count.
- **Heard back ▾** on every row (`components/messages/WaitingOnThem.tsx`, `HeardBackMenu`): **By phone**, **By text from my phone**, **In person**, **No answer needed** (`HEARD_ROWS`), a small menu under the press closed by a tap outside or Escape (`useCloseOnOutsideClick`), on the failed-text row too. A pick clears the row at once (the count with it) and shows the toast *Heard back from Evan Jarrow by phone. The line lands on the thread in ten seconds.* with **Undo** (`showActionToast`, `HEARD_UNDO_MS`). Undo brings the row back and writes nothing, *Undone. Evan Jarrow is back in Waiting on them.* When the ten seconds pass, `heardBack` writes the gray line — `follow_up_event`, the writer the thread's ⋯ menu uses for the customer's say (v2.5597), as the signed-in person named by `follow_up_user_name`: *Taunya heard back by phone on Nov 3.*, *… by text on their own phone on …*, *… in person on …*, *Taunya said on Nov 3 that no answer is needed.* (`heardBackBody`; the day from `formatDenverCalendarDayShort`). The write is deferred rather than undone because a signed-in client may insert a note or an event and delete nothing (`messages_client_writes_own_team_lines`, the grants of `20261012160000`); leaving or closing the page writes a pending one at once (`pagehide` and the unmount), so a pressed Heard back is never lost. A refused write puts the row back and says why (`pressProblem`). The thread's live read draws the line as any event line (`EventLine`), and the next read of the lane keeps the row away by the same rule.
- **A logged call clears the row**: decided here, since the call box saves nothing without their words (`callLogProblem`) and a call where nobody answered is Quo's own line, not a log. The day they gave (`message_promises` kind `said`) is the thread chip's business and holds nothing in the lane; the flow's reminders carry a day that passes.
- **The words**: the lane's `?` sentence (`WAITING_WORDS.note`, read by `laneLegend.ts`) ends *A reply from them clears the row. So does their answer on an estimate, a logged call with their words, or Heard back.*; the guide *answer everyone waiting on us from one page* § See who went quiet on us gains the paragraph on the three ways and Heard back with Undo. `docs/MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md` and `GLOSSARY.md` amend their Waiting on them sentence.
