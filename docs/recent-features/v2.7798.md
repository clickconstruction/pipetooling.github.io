# v2.7798 — Lien desk: a filed lien's copies read Print, then Enter mail numbers (2026-11-18)

The owner's word of 2026-11-18, the day *Mail this copy ›* shipped (v2.7785): "mail this copy is confusing because someone will see it after having already mailed a copy and not know if it goes to a printout or if it is where they record their receipt of the mail." He asked for a look through the Lien desk, then said yes to renaming by stage.

**What the look found.** One step, a filed lien's copies under § 53.055, went by three verbs and no door said which half it opened. *Mail* stood on the Board's bin (*Mail 12 liens' copies ›*), the To do row (*Mail the copies*) and the stop window's card (*Mail this copy ›*). *Record the service* stood on the band of the job's path and on the affidavit pane's footer. *Enter mail numbers* stood on the copies window's second half and on the printed mail sheet, which sent the office to a press that existed for notices only.

**The rule (`src/lib/jobs/lienServeStage.ts`, pure).** The step has two stages and one name each, the notices' own pair (`lienRunWords.ts`): **Print the copies to mail** until the copies are printed, then **Enter mail numbers**. A lien's copies count as printed once a print of them is filed on its job, or once a mail number or a receipt is on its filing. A lien the app did not draw has no copy to print, so it reads **Enter mail numbers** from the start.

Numbers: the copies window at the job's line, the cursor in its number |
| The stop window's card (v2.7785) | **Print the copies to mail ›** | **Enter the mail number ›** | the same |

**How the desk knows.** Every print was already filed against its filing (`sent_documents`, kind `lien_affidavit_copy`). `useLienDeskData` now asks once which unserved liens have such a row (`lienServePrintsIo.ts`, `data.servePrintedFilingIds`), fail soft. The copies window tells the desk what it just sent to the printer (`onPrinted`), so the doors change at once. The Lien window's press does the same (`onServePrinted`), and its opener reads again a moment later.

**Also.** The To do row's step card lists the two halves by these names. The printed mail sheet's line now names where the press is. The copies window lands on a half or on one copy's line (`LienServeLanding`).
