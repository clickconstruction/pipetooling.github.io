# v2.5834 — The Bridge: the Bill card counts finished work, not the whole contract (2026-10-24)

The second fix from the owner's question about the Bill card on 2026-10-24 (the first is [v2.5833](v2.5833.md), the out-rate). He asked whether the 15 "finished, not yet billed" jobs were commercial jobs billed on the GC's portal and never recorded here. They were recorded; the number was wrong for another reason.

## What was wrong

`Bridge.tsx` fed the Bill stage `fin.data.unbilled`, the Dashboard's **Not billed** bucket (`buildUnbilledBucket`, `dashboardFinancials.ts`): every `working` or `ready_to_bill` job's contract less payments less bills in `billed`. The percent done never entered the amount. Read on prod on 2026-10-24: **$260,255 on 15 jobs**, 14 of them `working`. SpaceX BA-02N ($249,716, 50 % done, $103,386 billed) alone put $146k on the pile where its finished-and-unbilled share was $21k; Mission Hills ($123,600, 90 % done, 90 % billed) put $12k where it was $0. The percent-times-contract reading over the ten jobs with a percent was **$39,102**; the five with no percent held $14,009 at most. A test job ("GC test project, delete me", not ZZ-named) carried $5,464 of it.

## The rule

`src/lib/bridge/billPile.ts` — `buildBillPile({ jobs, points, todayYmd })`, pure:

- a **working** job with a percent: `max(0, pct × contract − covered)`, where *covered* is the larger of the bills with a billed day and the job's payments (`coveredUsd`: a paid bill is one sum of money; money paid on no bill still covers the work — job 363 was 100 % done with $31,400 paid and no bill, and must not wait on Bill). The percent is the row's own `pct_complete` (the office's word, however old), else the newest field report's answer on or before today from Build's points (`effectiveBillPct`; Heron's row says 40 % while a June report said 100 % — the row stands);
- a **ready-to-bill** job: the whole remainder `max(0, contract − covered)`, whatever its percent — the office said the work is done;
- a working job with **neither**: half the contract less what is covered, apart as the **guess** (`BILL_GUESS_PCT = 50`), drawn grey;
- a job with **no contract price**: counted (`noPriceJobs`), nothing on either pile.

`billStageInput(pile, in, out)` hands `stageFlow` the known pile with `guessUsd` / `guessJobs`. `stageFlow.ts` gains `BillStageInput` (the guess fields optional) and draws the Bill card like Build: the sub lines *on N jobs that have a %* and *grey: M jobs have no %*, the grey share of the bar, a weeks range when the guess is over zero, **· set the %s** on the link or pair badge, and the thin-reading gate (`BUILD_GUESS_GATE`, now any stage whose guess is over a third of its pile).

The loader `src/lib/bridge/loadBillPile.ts` reads the working and ready-to-bill jobs with their payments (billing-only and the office job left out, as on Build; ZZ test jobs left out for everyone but a dev who shows them, as on the Dashboard — `loadZzTestJobIds`) and every bill on them with a `billed_at` (billed or paid; a hand-marked bill counts). The page composes it with Build's percent points once both are in.

## What reads the pile

Everything on the page that said "finished, not yet billed" now reads the known pile: the Bill card, the dial's per-job unit and the reflex lines, the calls' *Bill the finished work* number, This week's move, the Money owed panel (its source line now says what it reads), the Truth check's *Finished, unbilled* row and the header's money line. `linkDesk.ts`'s *How the Bridge reads it* says the rule. The Dashboard's own Not billed card is unchanged: it answers "what is left to bill on these jobs", a different question.
