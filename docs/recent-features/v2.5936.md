# v2.5936 — Every door to the Legal desk opens its page (2026-10-26)

Punch list #182, PR 2 of 3 ([`to-dos/desks-as-pages/`](../../to-dos/desks-as-pages/README.md)). After [v2.5894](v2.5894.md), which made the Legal desk a page at `/legal-desk` and moved no door. This PR moves every door and takes the window out of the Pipeline.

## What shipped

- **The doors** (`JobsStagesTab.tsx`): the ☰ Section tools row `legal-desk`, the Collections header's ⚖ Legal and the Lien desk's `onOpenLegalDesk` call `navigate(LEGAL_DESK_PATH)`; the stage bar's scales are a react-router `Link` to it (`StagesLegalDeskShortcut` takes `to` in place of `onOpen`; `DeskShortcut` draws a link for a desk that is a page and a button for one drawn as a window, so the Lien desk's gavel is unchanged). A link means a ⌘-click or a middle click opens the desk in a new tab beside the board, and the address shows on hover. The header button keeps its button and its gate on the header's cached count, since a link cannot be disabled. `.stagesDeskShortcut` gains `box-sizing: border-box` and `text-decoration: none`, so the link draws the same 2rem square as the button.
- **The window leaves the Pipeline** (`JobsStagesTab.tsx`, 4,845 lines): the `legalDesk` state, the effect that fetched the non-paid scopes when the desk opened, `openLegalDesk` on the tab's handle (no caller since v2.5894's redirect), the 32-line `<LegalDeskModal>` mount with its sixteen callbacks, and the `LegalDeskModal` and `PORTAL_COMPANY` imports. The tab keeps `useLegalMatters` for the rows' ⚖ chips, the scales' count and the Lien desk's sign-off. The money surfaces' quirk 4 loses its Legal desk exception: the page reads every Collections row, never the board's filtered ones.
- **The Dashboard** (`lib/needsYouDoors.ts`): `legal-review` writes `legalDeskHref({ account })` (`/legal-desk?account=…`) and `legal-firm-activity` adds `tab: 'fees_steps'` (`…&tab=firm`), on both hosts. The old hrefs still redirect; the new ones skip the hop through the Pipeline.
- **An account under Given up on opens on the page** (`LegalDeskModal.tsx`): v2.5894's page wrote the pressed account into the address, and the desk read the address back through a lookup in the open accounts alone, so a given-up account (punch list #94) missed, the selection went back to the first open account, and the address followed it within 150 ms (measured on the dev server). The lookup now knows both lists, and a selection is kept while it is in either. The window had the same lookup but read its address once, so it only lost a given-up selection when the rows reloaded. `LegalDeskPage.render.test.tsx` holds it, and fails on the old lookup.
- **The Pipeline row door lights the row** (`JobsStagesTab.tsx`'s `focusJobOnBoard`): it opened the section the job's status names, so a Collections job, `billed` by status, opened Billed Awaiting Payment while its row drew in Collections, still folded, and nothing lit (found on the dev server from the desk's Account tab). The window called the same function, so the desk's door never worked for its own jobs. It now opens the section the row draws in, `stagesSectionKeyForJobRow`, the job-number jump's kernel, which also sends a paid job to Paid in Full. A render case holds it and fails on the old mapping.
- **Words**: the guide *review a collections account before it goes to your attorney* (Open the desk: any of the three doors opens the page; ← Pipeline and Back; ⌘ or Ctrl on the scales; the window's full screen sentences go), *see which supply houses are owed on a lien job* (its link to the desk), `GLOSSARY.md` → Legal desk, `docs/twins/APP_DIRECTORY.md`, `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` → Legal desk, `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`, `JOBS_STAGES_TAB_ARCHITECTURE.md` (the handle, the scope effects, cluster J, the stage bar, the mount row, the tests, quirk 4), `JOBS_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md` (the handle row), `LIEN_DESK_ARCHITECTURE.md` (the Legal desk door).

## Decided here

- **Only the scales become a link.** The ☰ row is a menu item and the header's ⚖ Legal is disabled while Collections reads 0; both navigate on a press. The scales are drawn only while Collections has rows, so a link there never points at an empty desk.
- **The Lien desk's door opens the desk on every account**, as it did; picking the account from the affidavit's job is its own small change.
- **The window frame stays in `LegalDeskModal.tsx` for one more PR.** Nothing mounts it now; PR 3 deletes it with its full-screen button and its two click-outside cases, and draws the phone's rail as a list.
