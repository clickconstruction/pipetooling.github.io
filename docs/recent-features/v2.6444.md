# v2.6444 — The missed-call test no longer fails a change in the minute before midnight (2026-11-03)

Test-only fix. No migration, no edge function, no client change.

## What failed

The merge queue's build of #6857 (v2.6387) left the queue on `failed_checks` at about 06:05Z on 2026-11-03 (run
41585375946). Two cases of `src/lib/quo/missedReply.run.test.ts` failed: *writes the missed call, claims it, texts
Dana the in-hours line as the app* and *texts back on call.missed alone*, each receiving the after-hours line. The test
drives `quo-webhook`'s real handler, which picks the line from the time now (`new Date()`, then `officeOpen` in
`lib/messages/hasRule.ts`), and its fixture's office hours run every day from `00:00` to `23:59` with the close
exclusive. The file ran at 05:59:45Z, 11:59:45 PM Central, inside that one closed minute. Any other minute passes,
which is why the queue's earlier builds that evening were green.

## The fix

The test pins the clock, Date alone (`vi.useFakeTimers({ toFake: ['Date'] })`), to `FRIDAY_AFTERNOON`, 4:11:30 PM
Central on Fri Oct 23 2026, the instant its events already carry as `createdAt`, and restores the real clock after
each case. The signature stamp and the *a text of ours in the last three hours* case read the same pinned clock, so
they agree as before. The fixture's hours are unchanged: the test still drives the hours path, not the *always open*
switch.

## Proof

Replayed with a scratch vitest setup file that fakes Date before the file runs: unchanged, the file fails the same two
cases at 23:59:30 and 23:59:45 Central and passes at 00:00:30; fixed, it passes at all three and at noon, and on the
real clock.

## The rest of the suite

The two other tests whose hours close at 23:59 (`missedReply.test.ts`, `pushHours.test.ts`) hand the kernel fixed
instants and cannot flake this way. Of the handler-driven `*.run.test.ts` files, only one other loads `quo-webhook`
(`walkKitWebhook.run.test.ts`), and it never reaches the missed-call text back where hours are read; no other function
they load reads office hours or the hour of day.
