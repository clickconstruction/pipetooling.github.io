# v2.5533 — Lien desk: the run records what is ready, and a printed notice is not held for its signature (2026-10-14)

Taunya's morning of 2026-10-14: *Record the mailing · 19 notices*, every envelope red — 13 *Held · Unsigned* (notices printed and mailed on October 8, before the signing train of October 9 gave the app a signature to hold for) and 9 *No mailing address, and the claim is $0* — and the one button dead under *Something is marked in red · Fix the recipients marked in red before recording*, while its own label promised *Record 17 mailed · 2 stay in the pile*. Since v2.5086 a held notice blocked the record for the whole run, "as a missing address always has". The owner's ask: keep the app intuitive and swift so the team keeps moving. Two rules fix it, the first now a working convention in `CLAUDE.md`: **do what is ready, never all or nothing** — a batch action acts on what is clean, lists what it held with the reason and its door, and its button says exactly what it will do, dead only when nothing is ready; and **a notice already printed is not held for its signature** — the paper went out as it was, the hold would only stop the record. No migration, no edge function.

- **`lienDeskRun.ts`**: `runRecordSplit(notices, problems?)` returns `RunRecordSplit` — `mailed` (records now), `held` (a notice with a problem in red: an owner nobody confirmed, no address, unsigned before printing — it waits whatever its numbers say), `unnumbered` (clean, no number while others have theirs, v2.4119's rule unchanged), `waiting` (unnumbered then held), `partial`. With no number anywhere the clean notices still record whole. `runRecordWords(split)` writes the foot and the button: *9 notices are held · They stay in the pile with the reason in red. The 13 ready ones record now. · Record 13 · 9 held stay in the pile ▸*; with a number split too, *1 held · 1 with no number yet* and *Record 17 mailed · 2 stay in the pile ▸*; when every notice is held, *Nothing is ready to record · Sign or fix what is marked in red, then record.* and the button waits; null when everything records, so the foot keeps its own words. `runNoticeProblems` skips `RUN_UNSIGNED_PROBLEM` once `printedAt` is set.
- **`lienRunPaper.ts`** `runEnvelopeHold`: the unsigned hold skips a printed notice the same way; an envelope with no address or a $0 claim is held as before.
- **`LienDeskRunModal.tsx`**: `blocked` is gone. The split takes `problems`; `nothingReady` (no notice records) is the only thing that disables Record; the record sends `split.mailed`, and the held notices stay in the window with their rows, their reasons and their doors, as the unnumbered ones always did. A printed, unsigned envelope's row carries an amber *In the mail unsigned · it records as it went. If Malachi signed the paper, record that first.* (`run-unsigned-<n>`) in place of the red hold, and *Leader here, sign ▸* / *He signed the paper ▸* now show whenever a notice inside is unsigned, held or not.
- **Tests**: `lienDeskRun.test.ts` (+1: the three piles, the words in every case, the whole-run case, nothing ready, the numbers' own split as before; the printed notice is not a problem); `lienRunPaper.test.ts` (the printed notice is not held); `LienDeskRunModal.render.test.tsx` (the no-address case now reads *Nothing is ready to record*; +2: two notices, one held — the button's label and state, the record carries only the ready one, the held one keeps its row and the window stays open; a printed unsigned notice — no hold, the amber line, both doors, Record live and the record sent).
- **Guide** *send lien notices from the Lien desk*: the foot's sentence, step 3 and *What the run holds back* say a held envelope never stops the rest and a printed notice records as it went. **`CLAUDE.md`** → Working conventions gains the rule; `docs/LIEN_DESK_ARCHITECTURE.md` carries the note.

What does not change: what else is held and why, the printing (a held envelope still prints nothing), the signature rule before printing (an unsigned notice still cannot reach the stack), and the desk's *Only you can sign* row for the leader. After a printed notice records unsigned, the run's doors go with it: a pen signature on the paper is recorded before the record, or not at all.
