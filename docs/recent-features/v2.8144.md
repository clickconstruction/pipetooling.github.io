# v2.8144 — Document builder: the Sub NDA's map reads on a phone (2026-11-20)

Punch list #404, left by the review of 2026-11-20 under *Against the mock-up*. No migration and no function.

## What was wrong

The map on the Sub NDA's editor is a row a topic and a column a level. Its grid was one inline style at every width: a label column and four equal columns, each headed by the level's name in small capitals.

Measured at 375 px on the made-up paper: each level column was 58 px wide and the head *Aggressive* needed 70, so it ran over *Strict* beside it. A cell was a 58 px bar stretched to its row's height, where the mock-up draws small squares.

## The fix

The grid's columns, a head's contents and a cell's shape moved from inline styles to `index.css`, where a width can change them. `WalkedBuilder.tsx` hands the stylesheet two values: `--map-levels` on the grid and `--tone` on a cell.

At 720 px and under:

- The columns are `minmax(0, 1fr)` for the topic and 44 px a level.
- A head shows the level's bars (`LevelBars`, the slider's own) and hides its name. The name stays as the head's `title`, and every cell's label still says its level.
- A cell is a 44 px square holding a block of the level's colour. A changed cell keeps its amber tint and border.

Above 720 px nothing changed: the names, the words in a cell and the stripe of the level's colour along its top.

Measured at 375 px after: four heads of 44 px with nothing wider than its column, 28 cells of 44 by 44, a topic column of 129 px where it was 99, and no sideways scroll.

## One call made here

The mock-up's squares are 40 by 36 px. They are 44 by 44 here, because the review's own list also says a press on a phone should be 44 px tall, and the row had the room.

## Tests

`WalkedBuilder.render.test.tsx`: every head carries its level's name, its words and its four bars, a cell hands the stylesheet its colour, and the phone rules are read out of `index.css`.

## Not changed

The open cell has no outline yet, and the card of changed pages has no counts and no legend. Both stay on the card's list.
