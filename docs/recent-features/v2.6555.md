# v2.6555 — The tables for machines on the fleet board and the pre-use check, pushed before any screen reads them (2026-11-06)

Punch list #270, the equipment pre-use check, after the owner's spike (2026-11-05): the whole flow was built against a store on the phone first, the owner played it at three widths, and the design settled: machines on the fleet board with gauges drawn the way they look on the machine, a morning walk and a short leaving check, photos filing into a machine's own Drive folder, a rental known by its agreement, and machines attached to a job's day when the job goes on someone's schedule. He then asked for templates by kind and size (small, medium and large excavators, skid steers), so the schema carries templates from the start. This PR is the migration alone, so the push can land in a quiet moment with no client waiting on it.

## What changed

- `supabase/migrations/20261106030000_machines.sql` (`docs/migrations/20261106030000_machines.md`): `machine_templates` with the gauges as a `LevelDef` list and nine seeded by fixed id; `machine_check_questions` for every machine, a template or one machine, with `holds_machine` and `asked_on`; the machine columns on `vehicles` (`kind`, `machine_kind`, `unit_label`, `meter`, `code`, `template_id`, `levels`, the four `rental_*`, `google_drive_link`); `vehicle_possessions.job_id` with one holder per row; `machine_checks`; `machine_check_photos` and the `machine-check-photos` bucket; `machine_assignments`, one per machine, job and day; the `machine_held` kind named on `dispatch_requests.pending_action` with a partial index; the photo-save cron rewritten with a sixth clause; the three fences.
- A deviation from the design card: the checks are their own table rather than columns on `vehicle_checkins`, since a truck's check-in is a different shape (answers and an odometer entry) and a machine's carries a kind, a job, a verdict, gauge readings and where it was left.

## Test

`npm run check:migration-drift` after the push; the SQL bed in CI applies the file against a clean Postgres and a second time for idempotence. No client change, so no render test; the typecheck and lint ran on the note alone.
