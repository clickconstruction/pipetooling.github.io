# v2.3760 — Lien desk: letter two, the second owner letter from a sent notice (2026-09-23)

Punch list **#33** PR 2 of 3 — section B of [`to-dos/gc-failure-playbook/mockup.html`](../../to-dos/gc-failure-playbook/mockup.html). Client-only; no migration (both new facts live in the desk item's `fields` jsonb). PR 1 (the § 53.057 retainage notice) is v2.3753; PR 3 (the owner's call, the piles, the grid) follows.

## Why

Counsel's memo of 2026-09-22: the second owner letter goes **10–14 days after the first packet** when the GC has neither paid nor authorized a direct payment — the *paid-out* letter (replaces the next-draw pitch with the § 53.101 reservation, the § 53.105 failure-to-reserve lien, the affidavit date, and three questions the owner is asked to answer by phone) or the *unresponsive* letter — on the same § 53.056 form, to the same two recipients, the GC copied by the same traceable mail. The desk's Sent pile was one muted line with no buttons and nothing counting the days.

## What changed

- **Kernel [`lienLetterTwo.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienLetterTwo.ts)** — `letterTwoStatus({ items, openBalance, todayYmd, ownerCalledAt })`: the first packet is the latest sent notice item that is not itself a letter two; the state is `paid` (the job's balance is 0 — the job's, not the desk row's), `gc_authorized` (the first packet's `gcAuthorizedDirectPay`), `owner_called` (PR 3's fact, already read), `in_flight` (a letter-two draft on the desk), `sent`, else `waiting` / `due` from day 10 / `overdue` from day 14 (`LETTER_TWO_FROM_DAY`, `LETTER_TWO_BY_DAY`); `letterTwoByJobFrom` builds it for every job (both hooks), `summarizeLetterTwo` for the Dashboard. [`gcOnNotice.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/gcOnNotice.ts): `paidOutOwnerLetter` (counsel's words, the same fills) and `letterTwoTemplate(kind)`.
- **Draft fields** ([`lienNoticeDraft.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienNoticeDraft.ts)) — `letterTwo: { kind, afterItemId, afterSentAt }` on the second item, `gcAuthorizedDirectPay: { at, name, note }` on the first packet's item; both parsed and kept. [`lienDeskIo.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDeskIo.ts): `startLetterTwo` (a fresh draft on the job with the first packet's months, a fresh claim, the chosen letter and the mark — the office reads it on the paper and sends it for approval like any notice; the run records it as a second `notice_53_056` filing, and the month history already dedupes by month) and `noteGcAuthorizedDirectPay`.
- **The desk** ([`LienDeskModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskModal.tsx)) — the Sent footer becomes *Sent Sep 2 · Day 12 · GC paid: no · GC authorized direct pay: no · Letter two: …* with **The GC authorized direct pay…** (a note: where the okay is and when) and **Send letter two ▸** (a menu of the two letters, amber once due); the Sent row wears the clock chip (amber from day 10, red from 14, green once sent or okayed); a letter-two draft wears *letter two · paid-out* in the list and *letter two · paid-out · after the Sep 2 packet* on the pane heading. `LienDeskData.letterTwoByJob`; `LienDeskNeedsYou.office.letterTwo`.
- **The Dashboard** ([`dashboardNeedsYou.ts`](../../src/lib/dashboardNeedsYou.ts)) — the lien deadline card gains a secondary line *N sent notices at day 10+ — letter two (M past day 14) ›*, or its own amber/red card when nothing else is due. **The GC run** ([`GcOnNoticeModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/GcOnNoticeModal.tsx)) — step 2's claims table gains a *Letter two* column with the same words.
- Guide *send lien notices from the Lien desk* gains the *Letter two* section; `docs/GLOSSARY.md` → Lien desk. The to-do folder stays (PR 3 pending).
