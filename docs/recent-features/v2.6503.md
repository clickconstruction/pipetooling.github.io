# v2.6503 — The sign-in page carries the ClickTooling tagline under the pipe title (2026-11-04)

The sign-in page named the company (the ClickPlumbing.com pipe wordmark, v2.3583) and said nothing about what the app does for the people signing in. Grace picked the tagline *Changes are constant. ClickTooling keeps you paid.* on 2026-11-04, in the two-beat shape of GitHub's *Change is constant, GitHub keeps you ahead*: a truth every contractor nods at, then the promise. Five placements were rendered on the real page (under the title in two weights, a frosted band under the trade tiles, the head of the white card, a last line under the Tooling family) and the owner's side chose the line under the title.

## What changed

- **`src/components/LandingTagline.tsx`**: the two beats as named constants and one `<p>` with a span each.
- **`AuthPublicLandingLayout`** takes a `heroTagline` slot drawn under the `h1`, inside `.letter-header`; the estimate, contract and bid-room pages that share the layout pass nothing and look as before.
- **`SignIn.tsx`** passes `<LandingTagline />`.
- **`authPublicLanding.css`**: Playfair Display 1.5rem, the first beat at 78 % white, the second at weight 600 and `white-space: nowrap`; under 480 px the size drops to 1.15rem and the first beat becomes its own line, so the card stays on a phone's first screen (checked at 375 and 1100).

## Why the title spot

It is the first thing read after the name, it leaves the sign-in card clean, and the wordmark's photo backdrop already carries white serif text. The card-head placement made the box taller on every phone; the band read as a fourth trade tile; the foot line fell below the fold.
