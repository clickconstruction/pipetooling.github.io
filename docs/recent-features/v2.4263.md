# v2.4263 — The Hours grid stops before a typed 0 writes over clocked hours; the dev switch (2026-09-30)

Punch list #70, PR 5 — the last of the train. Two holes the ledger could not see, and the switch that turns the rule on.

## What changed

- **The grid stop.** `saveHours` on People → Hours writes `people_hours` — the pay-totals table — directly, with no session and no approval, whenever the typed number does not open the draft editor: a **0**, or a roster name with no login. A 0 on a day with approved clock sessions took those hours out of pay while the sessions stayed approved. Now [`PeopleHoursGrid`](../../src/components/people/PeopleHoursGrid.tsx) asks the page for the cell's approved clock hours (`approvedClockHoursForCell`, from the same session→cell join the pending badge uses) and, when a typed 0 would cover any, calls `onTypedOverClockHours` instead of writing: *Paige · Thu 9/24 has 6.50 h approved from the clock. A 0 typed here would take those hours out of pay without changing the clock. Open the day and change the sessions instead — that is recorded, and someone else looks at it.* **Open the day** opens the clock-day editor. Both blur paths (the desktop cell and the phone day sheet). A 0 on a day with no clock hours writes as it always did — that is how a name with no login gets its hours.
- **The dev switch** — [`TypedHoursSwitchBlock`](../../src/components/settings/TypedHoursSwitchBlock.tsx) on Settings → People & teams: *Typed hours: a second person approves* — **On for everyone** / **Test accounts only** / **Off**, one `app_settings` row (`typed_hours_second_look_v1`, [`typedHoursSwitch.ts`](../../src/lib/clock/typedHoursSwitch.ts)) that `typed_hours_rule_applies()` reads. Turning it on asks first. The ledger, the pencil and the Needs You card run whatever it says.
- **`quickfill/HoursSection.tsx` deleted.** Nothing mounted it (its section was replaced by `QuickfillPeopleHoursNewSection`), and it carried its own direct `people_hours` write and a tenth `approveClockSessions` call the stamp would never have reached.

## Tests

`typedHoursSwitch.test.ts` (3), `PeopleHoursGrid.render.test.tsx` (+2: a 0 over clock hours is a stop, a 0 elsewhere still writes).

## Walked live (2026-09-30, prod)

Settings showed the switch reading **Test accounts only** from the database. On the Hours grid, a 0 typed into Abraham's Monday (8.00 h approved from the clock) raised the stop — *Abraham · Mon, Sep 28 has 8.00 h approved from the clock…* — and **Cancel** wrote nothing: the cell still read 8:00:00 and no `people_hours` request went out.

## What the owner does next

Set the switch to **On for everyone** once the office has read the guide (*approve my team's hours* → *Hours someone typed*). From then on a person who types hours for someone and approves alone has to find a second person — the Needs You card tells that person.
