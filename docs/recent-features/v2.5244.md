# v2.5244 — The Bridge: the field’s pace read by hours as well as by percent, and the thin reading does not take the link (2026-10-11)

Follow-up A of the weakest-link train (`to-dos/bridge-weakest-link-follow-ups.md`, calculations 1, 2 and 7, bug 1). Dev only. No migration.

**What was wrong.** Build's pace came only from the percents (`buildPace.ts`: percent deltas × contract). With 14 of 27 open jobs carrying no percent, built-per-week read about $24k against the $34k the field earned by its hours, so Build read 15–17 weeks and took the link from Bill and Collect on sparsity, not on the field. Bill's in-rate was `totals.earnedUsd`, approved sessions only, which with 585h waiting read low and made the unbilled pile look slower-growing than it was. The dial's "Of the field's $X a week" and the reflex presses inherited both. The Talent card read "0 billing" when the window's invoice sends carried no actor.

- **The pace by hours** (`src/lib/bridge/fieldPace.ts`, `fieldPaceByHours`): earned per week on recorded time over the window, from the sessions the top line's weeks grid already loads (`weeksInputs.sessions` × `ratePerHourByJob`): approved or pending, field sessions only, with the hours per week and the approved-only figure beside it.
- **The honest pace** (`stageFlow.ts`, `withFieldPaces`): Build's `outPerWeekUsd` becomes the larger of by-percent and by-hours, with both kept in `paces`. The card's second flow line reads "the field builds $34k a week by hours" and a third names the other, "by the percents $24k a week"; within a twentieth of each other (`PACE_AGREE_MARGIN`) only one line shows. `fieldPerWeekUsd`, the dial's marks, the dial's per-field-hour unit and the reflex sizing all read the honest pace; the dial's unit and the reflexes divide by recorded hours when the pace is recorded.
- **The gate** (`BUILD_GUESS_GATE` = ⅓): when Build is the longest reading with more than a third of its pile a guess, it is nominated only if it leads the next stage by the hold margin (15 %). Otherwise the next stage is the link, and Build's badge reads "reads 15.0 weeks on a thin % · set the %s". When Build is the link with any grey, its badge ends "· set the %s".
- **Bill's in-rate**: `finishedPerWeek` is the recorded pace when the weeks grid has loaded, else the approved figure as before.
- **Talent** (`resources.ts`, `billsWithoutSender`): with zero named billers and bills sent, the gauge reads "1 billing, by the bills"; the throttle rule (one biller or fewer) is unchanged.
- **The page** (`Bridge.tsx`): the weeks-grid block moved above the stage wiring so the stage input can read it; everything else in place.
- **Tests**: `fieldPace.test.ts` (2), `stageFlow.test.ts` (+2: the two paces on the card; the gate, the clear overtake, the known pile), `resources.test.ts` (+1).
