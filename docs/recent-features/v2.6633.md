# v2.6633 — The Legal desk’s Signed statements line says who can read them (punch list #212, residual B) (2026-11-07)

The second residual after the five-PR train on the card (`to-dos/signed-hours-statement/README.md`), found in the 2026-11-04 review: `LegalEvidenceStatementsBand` drew *Nothing signed here yet* for a role that cannot read statements even when signed ones exist, because `hours_statements`' policy (`can_touch_hours_statement`, a dev or the controller since v2.6450) answers those roles no rows and the band read an empty list as an empty job.

## What changed

- **`legalStatementsWords(statements, { canRead })`** (`src/lib/legal/legalPacket.ts`): with `canRead` false and no rows the line is `LEGAL_STATEMENTS_CANNOT_READ_WORDS` — *Only a dev or the controller can read the signed statements here.* — never *Nothing signed here yet*; with rows it reads the counts as for anyone; a reader who may read keeps both words as before.
- **The band** passes its `canAsk` (`canMakeHoursStatement`, the same line the policy holds) as `canRead`; no new prop. A gap job's words are unchanged.
- **Tests**: `legalPacket.test.ts` (the three cases), `LegalEvidenceStatementsBand.render.test.tsx` (the gate words for a reader who cannot read, the empty words for one who can).
- **Docs**: the guide *review a collections account before it goes to your attorney* and `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` say what the other roles now read.

## Decisions

- **The gate is named where the empty state was.** The band could have hidden the line for those roles, but the Evidence tab lists the line under every job so an office reader knows the paper exists and whom to ask; the words say who reads it, as the section's *Controllers and devs only* tag does.
