# v2.4802 — Map: pins by status, chips as the key and the switches, Cluster (2026-10-07)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. PR 2b of the Map page refresh ([`to-dos/map-page-refresh/`](../../to-dos/map-page-refresh/README.md)), after [v2.4796](v2.4796.md); the owner's *build all of it step by step*.

## What changed

- **[`mapPageSections.ts`](../../src/lib/map/mapPageSections.ts)** (pure): `mapPageJobSection` (`jobs_ledger.status` → the Pipeline section, `readyToBill` for `ready_to_bill`, null off the pipeline); `mapPageBidDueTone` (the Bid Board's due ring, `bidBoardDueCellParts`: overdue / soon for an unsent bid with a due date); `mapPageLegendCounts` (placed records per job section, per bid stage, estimates, and the Collections count beside Billed); `MAP_PAGE_DEFAULT_JOB_SECTIONS` (Paid off); the cluster ring priority (Collections red, then overdue, then due soon); the per-device Cluster preference. The section colors and labels are `jobsMap.ts`'s own, re-exported.
- **[`useMapPageData`](../../src/hooks/useMapPageData.ts)** selects `collections_at` on jobs and `bid_due_date` on bids and carries `jobSection`, `inCollections` and `bidDueTone` on the entity.
- **[`mapPagePins.ts`](../../src/lib/map/mapPagePins.ts)**: `mapPagePinColors` — a job is its section's color with the Collections ring, a bid its stage's color (its outcome for the focused GC in builder focus) with the due ring, an estimate violet (`MAP_PAGE_ESTIMATE_COLOR`), a record with no section grey.
- **[`mapLayerFilter.ts`](../../src/lib/map/mapLayerFilter.ts)**: the filter is now `{ jobSections, bidStages, showEst }` — the Jobs / Bids layer switches are gone; a record with no section shows while any chip of its kind is on. `DEFAULT_MAP_BID_STAGES` is every stage but Lost (was Won and Started only, v2.837).
- **The page**: `SectionChip` replaces the layer pills, the bid stage chips and the legend overlay — one row of job sections, one of bid stages, Estimates — each the key (the dot is the pin's color; Billed's dot wears the Collections ring, Unsent's the due ring) and the switch, with its count. **Cluster** beside Fit all (`Clustered ✓` while on, remembered per device under `pipetooling_map_page_clustered`), on the canvas's own clustering with the ring priority; the canvases take a `clusterNoun` so a disc's title reads *12 records here*.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **A color means one thing across the app.** The Pipeline's dots, its map, the Bid Board's map and this page now agree, so nobody learns a second key.
- **The chips count what the map can show**, not what is on: a chip turned off still says how many it hides, which is the reason to turn it on.
- **Not done:** places (one pin per address) and the rail (PR 3); the address sheet (PR 4); the Precincts chip (PR 5).
