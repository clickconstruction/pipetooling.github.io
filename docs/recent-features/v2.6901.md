# v2.6901 — A part keeps each supply house's own number for it (2026-11-10)

Punch list #308, PR 2 of nine. A supply house prints its own number for a part on every quote and bill. The parts book had no place for it, so the only key was the name. This PR adds the place, the block that edits it, and a one-time offer of the numbers people already typed into names and notes. It is the crosswalk #298 designed as its stage 2a; #298's list of codes with no part builds on this table.

## What changed

- **Migration `20261111020000_supply_part_codes.sql`** ([doc](../migrations/20261111020000_supply_part_codes.md)): the table `supply_part_codes` and the function `may_edit_parts_book()`. Push right after the merge.
- **`src/lib/materials/houseCodes.ts`**:
  - pure: `toHouseCodes` (the rows as `matchRow` reads them), `partNumberRows` (a part's rows: the houses that price it, then any other house with a number), `numberProblem` (why a typed number cannot be kept, naming the part that has it), `houseNumberOffers` (the numbers found in names and notes, with the held ones and their reasons), `isNotLiveYet`.
  - reads and writes: `loadHouseCodes`, `keepHouseCode`, `removeHouseCode`. A read against a database that has no table yet answers `live: false`, so the client can merge before the push.
- **`src/components/materials/PartHouseNumbers.tsx`**, *Their numbers*: a row per house with its numbers as chips, a field and **Keep**, and *Another house…*. Drawn under the prices in the Parts Book (`PartPricesManager`) and in Takeoffs (`TakeoffPartPricesModal`). It draws nothing while the table is not live.
- **`src/components/materials/HouseNumbersWindow.tsx`**, opened by **House numbers** on the Parts Book: the count on file by house, and *Found in names and notes*. A number at the head of a name, or a note that is one number, is offered when one house alone prices the part. **Keep N numbers** keeps the ready ones one by one, names any that fail with the reason, and goes on. *Not a house's number* takes a row out. A number whose house cannot be told is held with why.
- The guide `keep-a-supply-houses-number-on-a-part` is new.

## Decisions

- **One number, one thing, at one house.** Unique on the house and the folded number, so `77120-B` and `77120B` cannot mean two parts. A part may carry several numbers.
- **A catch-all number is a row too**, marked *not a part*, so a house's code for anything it does not stock is never matched.
- **`unit_factor`** says how many of the book's units one of the house's prices covers: 100 for a house that prices by the hundred against a book that prices each. The price sheet reader writes it when it asks.
- **The Add Part form's price rows are left alone.** Their keyboard order is a tested contract, and a new part made from a sheet will get its number from the sheet.
