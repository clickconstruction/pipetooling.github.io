# v2.6092 — Phone first: clock out in one press (punch list #189, PR 8) (2026-10-29)

Punch list #189's PR 8, the last of the train's one-thumb pass: decision 7 of 2026-10-29, the clock-out note filled from the day's reports, editable. The usual press is one, *Complete clock out*; the office reads the same words it read on the report. With it, two of the card's follow-ups: the Job Mode card not refreshing after its own clock-out, and the Job Report button in Job Mode on a tablet or a computer that opened nothing.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/clock/clockOutNote.ts`** (tested): `clockOutNoteFromReports` writes one line a report in the order filed — the job's label, the stage moves (*Rough In 35% → 50% · job 32% → 38%*) or the percent (*45% done*), and what got done (the Status report's words, else the first words of any other field, cut on a word at 140 characters); a report of another kind names it (*· walk*, *· turnaway*); a signature or a bare number is never the words; a line said twice is said once; `clockOutDayReports` keeps the session's day in the app's calendar. The review's title is `CLOCK_OUT_REVIEW_TITLE`, *Clock out*; the line under a filled note is `CLOCK_OUT_NOTE_FILLED_LINE`.
- **`ClockInOutButton`**: when the review opens it reads the tech's reports of the session's day (`reports` with the job's number and name and the template's name), and when the day has any, fills the note with them and says so under the box; the tech edits it or leaves it. With none, the note is the session's own words as before (the clock-in plan, or the On my way line). The title reads **Clock out**.
- **The card refreshes after its own press**: the bridge (`UpdateFocusOpenerBridgeContext`) gains `subscribeSessionChanges` / `notifySessionChanged`; `ClockInOutButton` notifies whenever the open session changes under it (a clock-in, a job change, a clock-out), and `DashboardJobModeCard` reloads on the signal. The live run of 2026-10-27 found the card and the bar keeping the clocked-in state after Wrap Up Day until a reload.
- **`Dashboard`**: `hideJobReport` on the pinned row in Job Mode at every width (a phone only since v2.5905). The row's Job Report opened nothing in Job Mode, whose report window is the card's own Leave Report.
- **Guide** *clock in and out with Job Mode*: the review's name and the filled note.

## Decisions

- **The reports alone write the note, not the day's jobs too.** The card's *Is this the best we can do?*: the office already knows the jobs from the clock sessions; the reports carry what a tech would type.
- **A tech who typed nothing on the report still gets a line**: *PLUM 512 · Whitfield water heater: 45% done*, since the number is what the office checks the hours against.
- **The note is filled once, when the review opens**, and only while the box still holds what the review put there; a tech who started typing is never overwritten by a late read.

## The live run

On the phone preset as the dev with Job Mode on, on the ZZ test jobs 1088 and 1085 (two blocks put on the day for the run, taken off after): Start here instead clocked in on 1088, Leave report filed a Status report (Rough In to 50, the job to 17%), Clock out on the bar opened the tally gate, then the review as *Clock out* with the note *1088 · ZZ TEST Riders Walk: Rough In — → 50% · job 0% → 17%. ZZ live check #189, will be deleted* and the line under it; Complete clock out ended the session, which kept that note, and the card read *Ready to start · PLUM 1085* with no reload. The report, the session, the blocks, the fixtures added for the run and the crew row the blocks' trigger made were deleted afterwards; the job's percent was set back to none. The Leaving stamp note and the percent's thread note stay on 1088, as the card's first live run left its own.
