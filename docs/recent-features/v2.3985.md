## What shipped

- **Migration `20260928045522_gc_word_asks.sql`** — `gc_word_asks`, `gc_word_ask_answers`, `mint_gc_word_ask()`, `revoke_gc_word_ask()`, and `'link'` on the mark's `word_heard_via`. Detail and how it was tested: `docs/migrations/20260928045522_gc_word_asks.md`.
- **`supabase/functions/_shared/gcWordAsk.ts`** (import-free; 13 tests through `src/lib/gcWordAsk.ts`): `wordAskLinkProblem` / `wordAskLinkMessage` (a dead link reads the same whether revoked or never made), `wordAskPageGcs`, `validateWordAskAnswers` (a started row needs a temperature and a sentence; "no change" only where there is a word to repeat; nothing about a GC the link does not name), `wordAskUrl`, `wordAskTextMessage`, `wordAskEmail`. `isNoChangeNote` / `noChangeNote` moved here from `gcCallSheet.ts`, which re-exports them.
- **Edge function `gc-word-ask`** — GET the page by token, POST his answers, POST `mode: 'email'` (office JWT) to email him the link. `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` → gc-word-ask.
- **`/ask?t=…`** — `src/pages/GcWordAsk.tsx`, a public page built for a phone, pinned light: each GC with what it owes, the promise, the last word; four temperature buttons, a sentence, a pay date, "no change" where allowed; a sticky **Send N answers to Taunya**. It sends only the rows he finished and names the first one he did not (`src/lib/gcWordAskPage.ts`, 7 tests). An answer the office has read shows as read and cannot be changed.
- **GC Review**:
  - **Ask by link** on each account man's group, never on your own accounts (`GcWordAskDialog`): make the link, **Copy a text for Malachi**, **Copy link**, **Email it to Malachi**, **new link**, **turn the link off**, and where the link stands ("asked Tue · emailed · not opened yet"). Nothing is sent until she texts or emails it.
  - **"Malachi answered 3 — review"** opens the call sheet on his answers (`initialDrafts`), source = him, heard by **His link**. Saving writes the marks through `mergeRoundMarkWrite` as ever — `acted_by` = her, `word_from` = him, `word_heard_via = 'link'` — then marks those answers `accepted`. A row she clears stays waiting.
  - `src/lib/jobs/gcWordAskState.ts` (7 tests): `wordAskStage`, `wordAskStatusLine`, `liveAskByOwner`, `pendingAnswersByOwner` (a rotated link's answers are kept), `gcIdsToAskAbout`, `callSheetDraftsFromAnswers`. IO: `src/lib/gcWordAskIo.ts`.

- Registered: `EMAIL_CATALOG` (`gc_word_ask`, 42 rows), `customerSurfaceRegistry` (`/ask` and the sender, both staff-exempt), `config.toml`.
- The real page rendered with sample data is saved beside the to-do: `to-dos/gc-review-one-operator/ask-by-link-page.html`.
