# v2.2116 — Partner ledger card readability pass (2026-08-22)

Owner asked for "any more suggestions that would affect the user's experience" on the partner ledger after v2.2111/v2.2112 and approved all six. Everything is client-only; the journal/chain math is untouched.

1. **Opening speaks in words** — `Week opened $368.86 · you owe Click` (unsigned + `balanceWords`) instead of `−$368.86`, so both ends of the card use the closing's language. Print keeps signed opening/closing (already consistent there).
2. **Hours to the hundredth** — tier lines `Labor · 40.55 h × $37.50`, live so-far lines, farm line; journal labor label `Labor — 12.86 h (week of …)` (also the office Ledger tab). Hours are priced from exact clock time, so 1-decimal hours made `h × rate` miss the amount by dollars.
3. **Card and Full ledger agree on hours** — new `tierHoursTotal(stub)` in [`partnerWeeks.ts`](../../src/lib/partnerLedger/partnerWeeks.ts); `partnerStubsToJournal` feeds Σ tier hours (what the card shows) as the journal stub's `hours_total` when tiers exist. Bryan 08-09: card said 12.9 h (tier 12.86), Full ledger 12.8 h (`hours_total` 12.85, float-rounded) — both now 12.86. `JournalRow` gains optional `hours` on labor rows.
4. **Full ledger on a phone** — `useIsMobile()` (≤640px) switches the 4-column table to a stacked list: `Aug 15` + posting on the left, `+$450.10` over `bal −$1,008.13` on the right, so the running balance never leaves the screen. Wide screens keep the table, with short dates (`shortDate`, year only when ≠ current) and `postingLabel` (`Labor · 12.86 h` — the date column already says the week).
5. **Long labels** — a pasted chat transcript used as a back-charge description rendered six lines on a phone. Labels > `LONG_LABEL_CHARS` (80) clamp to two lines (`-webkit-line-clamp`) with a `more ▾ / less ▴` toggle keyed `${weekStart}:${i}`. Office side: [`PersonOffsetFormModal`](../../src/components/pay/PersonOffsetFormModal.tsx) gets a hint under Description — it is the line's label on the person's statement/ledger; keep it short, back-story belongs in a ledger note.
6. **Week nav on one row** — `justify-content: space-between`, title `flex: 1` (was wrap + `minWidth: 11rem`, which stacked into three rows at phone width); `Week of May 3` via `weekOfLabel`; print header uses `weekRangeLabel` (`May 3 – May 9, 2026`).

New kernel [`partnerLedgerFormat.ts`](../../src/lib/partnerLedger/partnerLedgerFormat.ts) (+6 tests): `balanceWords`, `shortDate`, `weekOfLabel`, `weekRangeLabel`, `isLongLabel`, `postingLabel`. `partnerWeeks.test.ts` +1 (tier hours) and label expectations moved to two decimals. Help guide `see-your-partner-ledger` updated. Caveat noted in the suggestion write-up: the phone findings were taken through the Partnerships partner-view lens (three cards deep); Bryan's real Dashboard is wider, but the table/nav structure was the same.
