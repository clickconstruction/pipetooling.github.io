# v2.3839 — Job windows: Escape never closes the job under a smaller window (2026-09-25)

The Esc-gate gaps of [`JOBS_MODALS_ARCHITECTURE.md`](../JOBS_MODALS_ARCHITECTURE.md) (quirk 34) and [`JOB_FORM_MODAL_ARCHITECTURE.md`](../JOB_FORM_MODAL_ARCHITECTURE.md) (quirk 29), found by the 2026-09-25 map sweep and widened by the triage. Client only, no migration.

## The bug

Job Detail (v2.1104) and Edit Job (v2.1100) close on Escape through a window listener that is removed while a stacked layer is open — each layer's open flag is listed in `detailEscBlocked` / `escCloseBlocked`. Layers added since were never listed, so Escape under them closed the modal beneath (in the tabbed Job window, the whole window, after the autosave flush), and whatever was half-typed in the layer was gone:

- **Job Detail** ([`DetailJobModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/DetailJobModal.tsx)): the *Add Customer Files / Photos link* dialog, the supply-house packet (`SupplyHouseShareModal`), and the job-account sheets of [`JobAccountsStrip`](../../src/components/jobs/JobAccountsStrip.tsx) (their state lived inside the strip, invisible to the host).
- **Edit Job** ([`JobFormModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormModal.tsx)): the *Remove payment?* and *Unlink and remove?* confirms, *Record payment* (`BilledPaymentConfirmationModal`), *Undo part payment*, *Move to job…*, delete + migrate, *Pick the winning GC*, customer terms, the Stages drawer (its own Escape closed it **and** the form), and the dialogs owned by children — `JobFormInvoiceList`'s delete-draft / send-back / convert and `JobFormPeoplePicker`'s *Add people*.

## The change

- `detailEscBlocked` adds `addLinkTarget`, `supplyHouseShareOpen` and `jobAccountSheetOpen`; `JobAccountsStrip` reports its sheet through a new optional `onSheetOpenChange`. Escape inside the add-link field closes just the dialog (`preventDefault`, which the shell's listener honors), matching the calendar and paid-email layers.
- `escCloseBlocked` adds the twelve Edit Job layers above. `JobFormInvoiceList` and `JobFormPeoplePicker` report their dialogs through a new optional `onOverlayOpenChange` (the `JobFormSourceEstimateBanner` pattern, false again on unmount). The gate moves below the payment/delete state it now reads.
- The layers without an Escape of their own still close by their buttons — Escape does nothing while they are up, rather than closing the job.

## Verified

- New `DetailJobModalEscLayers.render.test.tsx`: with the add-link dialog open, Escape keeps Job Detail; Escape in the field closes just the dialog; the next Escape closes Job Detail. Fails on the previous `DetailJobModal`.
- New `JobFormPeoplePicker.render.test.tsx`: the Add people dialog reports open → closed → open, and closed on unmount.
- The 22 existing `DetailJobModal*` / `JobForm*` / `JobAccountsStrip*` test files pass. No Edit Job render harness exists, so its gate list is code-reviewed, not rendered. Typecheck, lint.
