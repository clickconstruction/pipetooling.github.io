# v2.4130 — Pipeline Billed rows: the line under the bar tells the bill's story (2026-09-29)

Owner, on a Billed row of Jobs → Pipeline: "the box on the right is scrunched and the three boxes on the left are hard to understand." The 140 px action column carried nine things, three of them facts about the bill (*Open 3 weeks*, the *21d past expected* pill, *They said…*); the amber line under the bar joined two facts that mean nothing on a finished job (*On the sheet, no clock-ins · 100% Sep 15*), coloured amber by the money clause. Mock-up: artifact `EaARfQMBzU9yV5e2RE2xyw`. The owner added: the expectation itself is the click, to save the space of a separate link.

## What changed

- **Kernel** [`lib/jobs/billedWordsLine.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/billedWordsLine.ts) — `buildBilledWordsLine({ row, data, todayYmd, promise, inCollections })` → `{ billed, expect, action, tone, title, full }`. *Billed Sep 15* · then the estimate (*expect ~Oct 6* / *21 d past expected*, from `billedExpectedPayModel` **without** the promise) · then the promise beside it, never in place of it (*they said Oct 3*, *they said Oct 3 · 4 d past it*). `action` is `they-said` or `new-date`. Tone: red in Collections, amber when the estimate or the promise is past, plain otherwise. 8 tests.
- **Component** [`BilledWordsLine.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/BilledWordsLine.tsx) — the sentence with the expectation as a dotted-underline button (`stopPropagation`, tooltip = the math + "click to record …"); `billedWordsOverride(line, onExpect)` packs it as a `WordsOverride` (`node`, `tone`, `title`). 4 render tests.
- **`StagesStageBar` / `StagesProgressPaymentCell`** take `wordsOverride`: the bar's words div draws the node, wears its tone and its `full` sentence as the tooltip; the crew clause is untouched for Working rows.
- **`JobsStagesUnifiedTable`**: `billedExpectedPayChip` is replaced by `billedBillLine?: (row) => BilledRowBillLine | null` (`{ words, extras }`). The job row, the invoice row and the invoice-bearing card compute `billLine` once, pass `words` into the cell and draw `extras` under it (after the lien runway): the bank-returned ⚠ button, a shell row's *No bill line* / *In Collections N days* pill (with its own *They said…* link, since a shell has no expectation to click), and the reliability line. Nothing of it is in the action column any more.
- **`JobsStagesTab`**: `billedExpectedPayChipRenderer` → `billedBillLineRenderer`; `showTimeOpen: false` for the Billed and Collections section props (table and cards), so *Open 3 weeks* is gone there. [`BilledExpectedPayChip.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/BilledExpectedPayChip.tsx) is deleted; `billedExpectedPayModel` still feeds the chase, the forecast, the runway and the Bridge.
- **Kept at 140 px**: the action column is one `<colgroup>` for every section, and Ready to Bill's *Bill Customer* needs the width; the column no longer wraps because only buttons remain in it.
- **Guides**: *see when a customer will pay* (Reading the line, the click, the promise wording), *know whether a customer keeps their word*, *run your GC statement round* — every "chip" and "They said… link" sentence now describes the line. `docs/JOBS_STAGES_TAB_ARCHITECTURE.md` row H names the new renderer.
