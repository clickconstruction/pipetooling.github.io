# v2.4465 — Legal: an evening call or bill keeps its day, and stays with the office (2026-10-02)

One of the instant-day fixes that follow [v2.4452](v2.4452.md). No migration. `legal-portal` is redeployed after merge.

## What was wrong

The legal packet decides what goes to the collections firm by day: an entry dated before the account's first bill is held unless the office shares it (*Held entries never leave*). Both sides of that rule cut days out of `timestamptz` columns by their first ten characters, so anything stamped after 7 pm CDT (6 pm CST) read as the next day:

- **The function** ([`legal-portal`](../../supabase/functions/legal-portal/index.ts)) applies the rule under the service role before anything leaves. Its `ymd` helper sliced the first bill's `billed_at` (else `sent_to_customer_at`), a contact's `customer_contacts.contact_date` (a `timestamptz` despite its name), a promise's and a call's `created_at`, and the collections note's `collections_at`. It also sliced the matter's `released_at`.
- **The packet kernel** ([`legalPacket.ts`](../../src/lib/legal/legalPacket.ts)) recomputes the rule on the office's Legal desk and on the firm's page. Its `ymdOfIso` sliced the same instants, and `billed_at`, `sent_to_customer_at`, `agreed_write_down_at` and a signed agreement's day on the ledger and the steps. It also sliced `collections_at` behind the account's age (`jobAgingYmd`) and review days (`groupCollectionsByPayer`).
- **The desk's loaders**: [`useLegalPacketData`](../../src/components/jobs/legal/useLegalPacketData.ts) sliced `contact_date`, and the Dashboard's legal review nudge ([`useLegalReviewNudge`](../../src/hooks/useLegalReviewNudge.ts)) sliced `collections_at`.

Because both sides sliced alike they agreed with each other, but a call or promise logged the evening before the first bill read as the bill's own day and went to the firm by default.

## What changed

Every instant reads its day in `APP_CALENDAR_TZ`: `todayYmdInAppTz(new Date(iso))` in the function, `calendarYmdInAppTzFromIso(iso)` on the client, on both sides of the rule at once. The `date` columns keep their slice: `paid_on`, `sent_on`, `last_bill_date`, `last_work_date`, a filing's `filed_at` and `served_at`, and a demand letter's `sent_at`, all `date`s whatever their names. Both helpers now say they are for date columns.

## Tests

- `legalPacket.test.ts` (+2): a call and a promise logged at `2026-04-17T00:30:00Z` (7:30 pm CDT on Apr 16) against a first bill at noon on Apr 17 read Apr 16 and are held by default, while the same call at noon UTC reads Apr 17 and goes. An evening `billed_at` dates the first bill, the ledger and the aging on its Central day, and a collections flag at `2026-12-02T00:30:00Z` (6:30 pm CST) reads Dec 1 for the review days and the note.
- New `useLegalPacketData.render.test.tsx`: a contact at 7:30 pm CDT the evening before the first bill reaches the packet dated that evening, and held.

All fail on main.

## Checked against prod

Read only, from the worktree dev server: 8 Collections jobs in 6 accounts, and 3 legal matters, all still in review, so nothing has gone to a firm. One timeline entry was logged in a Central evening and moves a day earlier, and no entry flips between held and shared.
