# v2.4866 — Records for an owner: two fixes from the first walk (2026-10-07)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Punch list #86 ([`to-dos/owner-records-portal/`](../../to-dos/owner-records-portal/README.md)) asked for one end-to-end walk on a ZZ TEST request before the first real owner. This is that walk and the two things it found.

## The walk

Run 2026-10-07 on localhost against prod, as the card's *How to verify* says. The ZZ jobs the card named (J1050–J1053) had been folded into the sink by the ZZ sweep (v2.4157), so the walk used the two ZZ jobs left: **J1064** *ZZ TEST sink* (customer *ZZ TEST Owner On Notice*, 1 Test Street) and **J904** *ZZ TEST held check A* (customer *ZZ TEST Claude Billing*, no address). Each got one `missed` notice row (`noteLienWindowMissed`) so the records window's picker offered it, and both rows were voided after the walk.

All eight steps passed:

1. Offer it with a second name: check 1 reads *Offered on their portal Oct 7, 2026. Waiting for them to sign.*
2. On the portal, initials are refused with *Type your name as the county lists it: ZZ TEST Owner On Notice. Every letter must match.* The owner's name typed `zz  test-owner on notice` matches, and the card thanks them.
3. The first `esign_consents` row of type `lien_owner_record_request` is on prod (migration `20261006040000` works), with `file.request.how = 'portal'` and `file.acknowledgment.printedName`.
4. The Dashboard's Needs you reads *zz test-owner on notice signed for the records on 1 Test Street, Austin, TX*. **Open their request** opens the Lien desk and the window on that property.
5. Check 4 reads *Signed on their portal Oct 7, 2026 by zz test-owner on notice, typed.* with no Change.
6. **Record it as sent** with *On their portal* files an `owner_records_packet` copy with a PDF `copy_path`, `how: 'link'`, and `file.sent.how = 'portal'`. The Needs you line goes.
7. The portal reads *The office sent your records on Oct 7, 2026.* **Download the records** serves one 7 KB PDF as an attachment: the cover note and the statement.
8. On J904, signing as the second name with a drawn signature matches, keeps the ink at `<request id>/acknowledgment-signature-<uuid>.png`, and check 4 reads *drawn*.

Left on prod as test residue: two sent requests, the second customer's portal link, two consent rows, two packet PDFs and one ink PNG.

## What changed

- **No address.** J904 has no address on the job. The portal's acknowledgment read *I am an owner of the property at .*, because `customer-portal` sends the request's blank `property_address`, and the window's printed copy read *the property at this property*, its fallback. [`ownerRecordsDocs.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/ownerRecordsDocs.ts) now has `OWNER_RECORDS_NO_ADDRESS` (*this property*), which the window's fallback uses. The acknowledgment's first line reads *I am an owner of this property.*, and the cover note says *our records on this property*, for a blank address and for the fallback alike. The packet's title and file name already read *this property*.
- **Check 1 keeps no Change on a portal signing.** [`LienOwnerRecordsModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienOwnerRecordsModal.tsx) hid Change on check 4 once the owner signed on the portal (v2.4650), but check 1 still offered it. Pressing it would let the office rewrite a request whose words say *Their signing is the request.* Check 1 now shows no button when `file.request.how` is `portal`.
