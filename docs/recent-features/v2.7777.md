# v2.7777 — Painted maps: a place keeps its mark in a picture and on paper (2026-11-18)

Punch list #393, reading B, the copies. The marks themselves are v2.7776. A copy handed over from the Share sheet drew every place as a plain numbered dot, whatever its kind.

## What changed

- **The picture.** A place with a kind is drawn as its mark: the disc, the kind's drawing and the place's number on its shoulder. The key under the map draws the same mark beside each place's words, so the key reads against the map. `pictureKindMark` in `paintedMapPicture.ts` says what to draw and where. `paintedMapPictureIo.ts` strokes the drawing's path on the canvas.
- **The printed page.** A place with a kind has its mark in the list, from the same markup the map's own dots use (`placeMarkHtml`). The number's dark disc is marked to print as drawn. Without that a browser drops backgrounds and the white figures vanish on white paper.
- **A file for another map.** A `.kml` or a `.gpx` draws no mark, so the kind is said in the place's name where the name does not already say it: *7. Hazard: Open trench*, and *1. Truck gate* as before (`placeSaysWords`).
- A plain place, and a place whose kind this app does not know, are drawn as the numbered ring they always were, in all three.

## Checked

- `paintedMapPicture.test.ts`: where the drawing and the number land for a dot on the map and for a key row, a hazard's warning, and null for a plain place and an unknown kind.
- `paintedMapPrint.test.ts`: a hazard's row carries its drawing in red and its number, a plain place and an unknown kind keep the ring, and the page asks for its colours to print.
- `paintedMapFiles.test.ts`: the kind in a place's name in both files, never said twice, and the file still well-formed.
- `PaintedMapCopies.render.test.tsx`: a place's kind reaches the picture, the page and the file from the sheet.
- Looked at in a browser on a scratch page: the real picture, drawn over made-up places of every kind, and the printed page beside it. Every mark and its number read, the hazard is red, and a plain place and an unknown kind are numbered rings.
- Not walked: a page sent to a real printer, so the number's dark disc on paper is not seen yet.
