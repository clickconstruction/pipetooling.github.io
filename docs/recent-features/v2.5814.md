# v2.5814 — Quickfill Email station, PR 1 of 5: the counted boxes, the label facts and their tables (2026-10-24)

The owner's yes on punch list #159 (`to-dos/quickfill-email-station/`), his words on all four Quickfill
proposals at once: "I like your suggestions, build it all." The station today is three free boxes and
a mark, and its history showed the same six emails retyped every morning for a week. The build is
option B over the delegation the bids@ reader already holds: the station reads Gmail's own labels per
box and per person, with the metadata-only scope (senders, subjects, dates and ages; Google refuses
the words). This PR is the kernels and the migration; nothing shows yet.

- **The kernels** (`src/lib/emailIn/`, each with its test):
  - [`countedBoxes.ts`](../../src/lib/emailIn/countedBoxes.ts): the boxes the sweep counts from
    `email_box_watch` rows (on, an address, one per address, the shared boxes first, then the people),
    the labels cleaned (`cleanLabels`: trimmed, once each case-blind, the Inbox under one name, the
    default three when empty), and `findLabel` (the Inbox by its system id, else the exact name, else
    case-blind, else null for the station to say so). Not `readerBoxes.ts`, which is the taker's list.
  - [`labelSweep.ts`](../../src/lib/emailIn/labelSweep.ts): the pure part of one sweep. `threadSeen`
    reads a thread's metadata into its row (the first message's date, sender and subject, the last's
    date). `planSweep` says which listed threads to fetch, which open rows are gone (only on a complete
    listing; a label cut short at `SWEEP_PAGE_CAP` says nothing about the rest) and which are still
    there. `ageDays` is whole app-calendar days. `dayRow` is the trend's row for the sweep's day.
  - [`labelFacts.ts`](../../src/lib/emailIn/labelFacts.ts): what the station says. `labelFacts` is one
    label's count, oldest age, the threads past the limit oldest first (with the day they arrived, who
    wrote, the subject and a door into Gmail under the box's own address, `gmailThreadHref` with
    `authuser=`), the newer ones as a number, and what moved since the last look (`newSince`,
    `clearedSince`, `stillSince`; zeros when nobody has looked). `composeLookNote` writes the mark's note
    in the history's shape, one line per box, the moves, then the person's own sentence, capped at the
    mark's 10,000 characters. `missingLabelWords` for a label the box does not have.
- **The migration** `20261024160000_email_box_count.sql`
  ([its page](../migrations/20261024160000_email_box_count.md)): `email_box_watch` (the app's own list
  of what it reads, with the sweep's health per box and a guard trigger so a person may change their own
  labels and switch but only the owner or a dev changes whose box a row is), `email_box_threads` (one row
  per thread seen, never a body), `email_box_days` (the trend); `email_box_can_see` (the office team for
  a shared box; the person and the lead for a person's; nobody else); the settings `email_count_on_v1`
  (off), `email_count_lead_v1` (empty), `email_count_limit_days_v1` (3); the cron `gmail-count` every 15
  minutes while the switch is on; the three fences.
- **Docs**: `docs/ACCESS_CONTROL.md` (who sees whose inbox facts), `docs/MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md`
  (Email in: the counter beside the taker), the to-do's status.

## Deploy

`supabase db push` from the main checkout after the merge; then the types regen in its own PR
(`npm run gen:types`, `src/types/database.ts`). The function the cron names lands in PR 2; the switch
stays off until the Google admin step (the `gmail.metadata` scope on the delegation row) is done.

## Is this the best we can do?

- The sweep's and the station's rules are pure and tested before any screen or function reads them,
  so PR 2 and PR 4 are wiring.
- The lead is one setting, not a role: the owner names one person and can change their mind in a
  press, and the policy reads the same setting the card writes.
- Not built here, by design: the sweep (PR 2), the cards (PR 3), the station (PR 4), the trend body in
  the history window (PR 5).
