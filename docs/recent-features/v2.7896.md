# v2.7896 — Lien desk: the Board's tiles say where their press goes (2026-11-18)

The owner's pick of 2026-11-18, one of the last five pieces of the Board's drawing (punch list #383, option B) that a live look found still different. He saw each redrawn on the Board as it stands and picked all five; each is its own PR. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## Before

- The second tile, *waiting on the leader*, has been a press since v2.7448: it narrows the list under the Board to the papers that wait on him. The third, *to print and mail*, has opened the run since v2.7367. Neither said so. A tile read as a figure, and its only sign of a press was a blue border under the pointer, which a phone never shows.

## What changed

- **Words only, on the two tiles that are presses.** `Tile` in `LienBoard.tsx` writes the door's words at the tile's top right, in the link colour: *Show them ›* on the second tile and *Open the run ›* on the third. The words sit inside the tile's own button, so the whole tile is still the press and no press was added.
- **The second tile says the way back.** While the list is narrowed to the leader's papers the tile reads *Show every row ›*, the words of the press on the line under the bins, and carries `aria-pressed`. A pressed bin or the red line's pick leaves it at *Show them ›*.
- **The kernel is a file of its own.** `lienBoardTileDoor.ts`: `lienBoardTileDoorWords` names the `leader` and `run` doors and answers null for a tile that only reads and for the `risk` door, which is the red line's own. `lienBoardTilePicked` tells the leader's pick from a pressed bin by its name, *waiting on Sam*, which the tile and the pick both take from the same leader's name.

## What it leaves alone

- **A tile that only reads has no words**: the first and fourth tiles, a second tile with nothing waiting, a third with nothing to print or mail.
- **A phone's third tile.** Where the notices' print stands in the row under the tiles the third tile only reads (v2.7708), so it gets no words there. The second tile reads the same on a phone.
- **The doors themselves**, and who may open the run, are as they were.

## Tests

- `lienBoardTileDoor.test.ts`: the two doors' words, null for a reading tile and for the red line's door, the way back in the pick line's own words, and the pick told apart from a pressed bin and from the red line's pick.
- `LienBoard.tileDoors.render.test.tsx`: the words on the two tiles and on no other, a press on the words opens the tile's door, no words with no handler or nothing waiting, *Show every row ›* with `aria-pressed` under the leader's pick, and a phone's third tile left alone.

## Verified

- On the dev server against the live desk in headless Chrome, a computer (1440 px) and a phone (390 px), light and dark: the words sit clear of the figure and the label on both tiles.
