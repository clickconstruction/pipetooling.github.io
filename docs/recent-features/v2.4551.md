# v2.4551 — Dashboard on a phone: windows answer your taps when My Inbox is empty (2026-10-05)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Wendi (estimator) sent a screen recording: quick clock opened on her iPhone, and the length cells, the kind buttons and the text box each flashed under her finger and did nothing. She had not been able to add quick time since the week before.

## Why

[`PhoneFold`](../../src/components/dashboard/PhoneFold.tsx) (v2.3882) drew two different trees: the children bare when `fold` was false, and `section > div > children` when it was true. A change of `fold` therefore moved the children in the tree, and React mounted them again.

On the Dashboard the My Inbox fold is `fold={phoneOffice && myInboxDockVisible}` (v2.3883), and `myInboxDockVisible` is reported by the card inside it (`DashboardMyInboxCard` → `onVisibleChange`). The card counts itself visible while its own read is running (`outstandingLoading` starts true). For a phone office role with an empty inbox that made a loop:

1. The card mounts, is loading, reports visible. The fold turns on. The card moves, so it mounts again.
2. Its read comes back empty. It reports not visible. The fold turns off. The card moves, so it mounts again, loading.
3. Back to 1, one round trip per turn: about two a second, each with its own read against the database, for as long as the Dashboard was open.

Nobody with something in My Inbox saw it, and no desktop did (`phoneOffice` is false there). On an iPhone the page that never settled is what killed the taps: the touch highlighted its button, and the click never arrived. Chromium delivered the clicks through the same loop, which is why the sheet passed every desktop check at phone width.

## What changed

- **One shape for both states.** `PhoneFold` always renders `section > div > children`. Not folding, both wrappers are `display: contents` and carry no `data-phone-fold`, id or `hidden`, so a desktop and a field role lay out as before. Folding, it is the tree it was. `fold` can now change without the children mounting again, which is what the component's own comment promised.
- The same holds for the other fold whose switch a section drives (`dash-reports`, on `showRecent`) and for a `phoneOffice` that settles late (the role loading, the width changing).

## How it was found

Signed in as Wendi through View as in the iOS Simulator (iPhone 17 Pro, iOS 26.4, Safari): quick clock opened and 20 and ＋5 did nothing; the same taps worked as the dev account. A MutationObserver on her Dashboard in a desktop browser showed the My Inbox section swapped nine times in four seconds. With the change, the same account in the same simulator: 20 lights four cells and the line reads the time window. Nothing was added to her hours.

## Tests

`PhoneFold.render.test.tsx` (4, one new): a child that counts its mounts is mounted once across fold on → off → on. It counted 3 before the change.

- As a phone office role with an empty My Inbox, at phone width: the Dashboard's DOM is still (0 nodes added or removed in 5 s; it was 12 and 18 in 3 s), and My Inbox is not drawn.
