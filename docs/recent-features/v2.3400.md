# v2.3400 — Payment forecast: work months under each row, the lien clock per month (2026-09-14)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Mock-ups (Draft A → critique → Refined B, computed from real clock sessions) at the Claude artifact `ac19bb72-c80d-4ccf-9e9c-c1085c47df5d`.

## Why

Owner, 2026-09-14: "enable a drop down for each row [of the Payment forecast] showing the weeks and days people worked. I think this is the first step in arming the app to handle notices at deadlines after certain months." Texas Property Code ch. 53 (post HB 2237) counts every deadline from the **month labor was furnished**, never the invoice date: a sub job needs one § 53.056 notice per unpaid month (15th of the 3rd month after; 2nd month residential) and one affidavit from the last month worked (§ 53.052). The Lien window and the Dashboard watches key everything on the job's last work month, so on a multi-month sub job the earlier months' notice windows close silently. Live example the day this shipped: J650 ATI Schertz, a sub job with $33,500 unpaid and work in June through September — June's notice was due September 15; the app said December 15.

## What shipped

- **Kernel [`forecastWorkMonths.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/forecastWorkMonths.ts)** (+11 tests): `buildJobWorkMonths` / `buildWorkMonthsByJob` group a job's clock sessions (rejected and revoked left out) into chronological months and Monday weeks — people (deduped display names), hours from clock-in/out, **pending hours** from unapproved sessions carried separately, day counts, each week's `daysSince` today, each month's share of the job's hours; on sub jobs each month's `notice` (`noticeDeadlineForMonth` from the lien kernel, states `sent` / `closed` / `due` ≤ 7d / `closing` ≤ 14d / `open`); the job's `affidavitDue` from the last month; `nearestOpenNotice` (the row chip), `summarizeNoticeMonths` (the line above the buckets), `noticeStateText`.
- **Hook [`useForecastWorkMonths`](../../src/hooks/useForecastWorkMonths.ts)**: while the forecast is open, one `clock_sessions` fetch for the open-bill jobs (chunked, `withSupabaseRetry`), the crew's names, `customer_addresses.property_kind` for the clock, and the live `job_lien_filings` notices so a named month reads *sent*. Empty on error — the modal simply shows no chevrons.
- **[`ForecastWorkMonthsPanel`](../../src/components/jobs/ForecastWorkMonthsPanel.tsx)**: the month lines — week bars (height = hours, crew count above, hatched when sessions await approval, hover for names and "(98d ago)"), *5 people · 82.7 h · 5 days · 24% of hours · 8 h pending*, and on sub jobs the notice chip, *by Sep 15*, and **Send notice…**; folds to *+ N earlier months* past six; a role line that teaches the rule once (*Sub job · each unpaid month needs its own notice · affidavit for all of it by Jan 15, 2027 (4th month after Sep 2026, the last month worked)* / *Direct with owner · no monthly notice · one affidavit by …*), the *property kind unknown* amber chip when no property record is linked (commercial dates shown; residential would be a month earlier), and on a commercial direct job the prompt *if Southern Post is a GC and someone else owns the site, set the GC on the job*. `ForecastNoticeChip` is the collapsed row's chip.
- **[`BilledPaymentForecastModal`](../../src/components/jobs/BilledPaymentForecastModal.tsx)** (+4 render cases): rows are now a chevron beside the jump-to-bill button (the chevron only where the job has sessions; *· 4 mo* faint on the row); the chip on sub rows with a month due or closing; the amber line above the buckets — *1 work month on 1 sub job has a lien notice closing within 14 days · $33,500 open · 650 · ATI Schertz Jun 2026 work, notice due tomorrow*; the footer says *loading the months worked…* until the fetch lands. New props `workMonths` and `onOpenLienNotice`.
- **[`LienInstrumentsModal`](../../src/components/jobs/LienInstrumentsModal.tsx)** gains `initialTab`; the Stages tab's Send notice… door closes the forecast and opens the window on the § 53.056 tab for that job.
- Help guide *see when a customer will pay*: "The months people worked, under a row"; *file a lien and never miss its deadlines* points at it.

## Not in this PR

The Lien window's notice tab still records only the last work month in `months_covered`, and the Dashboard watch still iterates jobs, not months — the kernel here is what both can reuse next. Which dollars belong to which month is not known; hours share is shown as a labelled proxy.

## Verified

Kernel suite (11), forecast render smokes (10, 4 new), release-note and guide drift tests, typecheck, lint, theme check; live on the dev server against prod data: J650 opens to four months with June's notice *due tomorrow · by Sep 15*, the chip on the collapsed row, the line above the buckets, Send notice… landing on the § 53.056 tab.
