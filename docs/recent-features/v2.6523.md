# v2.6523 — Bring back a teammate who left, in one window (punch list #256, PR 3) (2026-11-04)

Punch list #256's third PR (`to-dos/bring-back-a-teammate/`). The walk of 2026-11-04 on the card showed the way back as it was: two Restores on two lists, the end date left set, nothing asking for the day back, the wage or the team, and a worker with no login left active after End employment. This PR is the Bring back window the card drew, on the owner's answered calls. PR 1 (v2.6480) is the kernel, PR 2 (v2.6481) the stints and `restore-user`'s roadmap flag.

## What changed

- **The window** `src/components/personDesk/PersonDeskBringBackModal.tsx`, the mirror of End employment: *Back on* first with the away line (`awayLine`), *Ended by mistake? Undo the end instead* under it, the kernel's rows (`buildBringBackChecklist`) with their presses — *Still theirs* on the sign-in, *Keep $24.00* or *Change…* with an inline wage on the wage row, *Pick a team…*, *Update…* and *Assign packet* as links, *Leave open…* with a reason on the rows that allow it — the roadmap box (`roadmapBoxWords`, ticked by default only within `ROADMAP_RETURN_DAYS`), *Start in training mode* (off by default, `RETURNER_TRAINING_MODE_DEFAULT`), *Note to HR file* for a dev, the footer's words (`bringBackFooterWords`) and the button (`bringBackButtonLabel`). A day back before the day they left blocks the press with `bringBackDateProblem`'s words.
- **The writes** are the kernel's ordered plan, `planBringBackWrites`: `restore-user` with `restore_roadmap` first (so the parked rows are dropped or kept before the roster row flips), then **one** `UPDATE people` clearing `archived_at` and `end_date` and setting `start_date` to the day back, so `people_stints_follow_dates` opens a new stint beside the old one (the review note on the card: two statements would reopen the old stint and then move it), then training mode when the box differs from the account, the wage when Change… was pressed, the HR line (`bringBackHrLine`). A step that fails stops the run, and the toast names the step and what was already done. `planUndoEndWrites` is the undo: the login with its roadmap rows, the roster row with the end cleared and the start kept, `undoEndHrLine`.
- **The reads** `src/lib/people/bringBackFacts.ts`: the account row, the roster row's pair, `employment_stints` (the first stint's start for the away line), the pay row, `person_licenses`, the documents signed on or before the end date and the packets assigned after it, open vehicle and housing possessions, the Hours-tab team, and the parked roadmap count (a dev's table; null for anyone else, and the box then shows without a number).
- **Where it opens**: the desk's Status row (*Bring back…* where Restore sat, `PersonDeskAccessSection`) and the header's **⋯** menu, on an archived desk whose roster row has an end date, for the people who may archive (`canArchiveAccount`); People → Users → Archived, on both the *Logins* and the *Roster rows* lists, where a row with an end date offers *Bring back…* and opens the desk with the window already up (`PersonDeskOpenArgs.flow`). A login archived with no end date keeps Restore.
- **The Team row** replaces the mock-up's Leader row (`BringBackFacts.needsTeam`, `team`; `kindNeedsTeam`). The app records no leader for a person: hours are approved by any office role, and Supervision is a flag. It does keep the Hours tab's teams, and a returner's member row outlives the archive, so the row reads *On Crew A* or offers *Pick a team…*; it can be left open with a reason, since a team is optional.
- **End employment archives the account by the row's id** (`archiveAccount` sends `user_id`, as the desk's Archive… has since v2.6519). The walk's follow-on 1: the modal archived the account only when the row carried an email, so a #260 worker with none stayed active in every list after their last day, in the Helper group and under Archived at once.
- **Guides**: `bring-back-a-teammate` (new): the last day, while they are away, the day back, ended by mistake; `archive-user-accounts` and `open-a-persons-desk` point at it.

## Decisions

- **The desk carries the dates.** `usePersonDesk`'s `PERSON_SELECT` reads `start_date, end_date` too (`PersonKeyPersonRow` takes them as optional), so the body decides whether to offer Bring back without another read.
- **The roster rows list reads `end_date`** (`usePeopleRoster`'s archived select), so a roster-only person who left gets the same press.
- **Training mode is written only when it differs** from the account, so a controller bringing back an account whose flag already matches makes no privileged write.
- **The wage's Change… upserts `hourly_wage` alone**, keyed by the pay name, leaving the salaried flags as they were.
