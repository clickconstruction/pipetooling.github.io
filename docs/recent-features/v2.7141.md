# v2.7141 — The Bridge's Monday email leaves out what the Bridge leaves out (2026-11-13)

A fix on the Bridge train's ground (card #334, `to-dos/bridge-pulse/`), found by the docs pass of 2026-11-13 (#7936). v2.7095 shipped the list of what the Bridge leaves out and said, under *Left, on purpose*, that the weekly email does not read it. It gave no reason. So while the owner's list named anything, the email counted jobs, bids and estimates the page did not, and the two could say different numbers on the same morning. The email's first real send is Monday 2026-11-16.

## What was wrong

- `loadMondaySheetReads` (`sheetReads.ts`) handed `loadBidsSent` and `loadEstimatesSent` an empty list (`leftOut: []`), where the page hands them the saved rules.
- Its seven job-keyed reads took the ZZ test jobs alone: the Bridge data, the financials, the people, the eight weeks, the build pace, the Bill pile, and the asks and the bids. The page hands each of those the ZZ jobs joined to the jobs the list names (`readZzTestJobIds` in `Bridge.tsx`), and hands the financials hook the same ids. The Collect desk's reads follow the financials on both.
- Nothing but the page read `app_settings` `bridge_left_out_v1`.

## What changed

- **The email reads the list first** (`sheetReads.ts`): `loadLeftOutScope` reads the saved row through the page's own parser (`parseLeftOut`) and then the jobs the rules name (`loadLeftOutJobIds`, the page's read). Those ids join the ZZ jobs for every job-keyed read. The bids and the estimates sent take the rules themselves with the ZZ jobs alone, which is how the page calls them, so a ZZ bid is judged by the ZZ rule and a left-out bid by the list's.
- **One set, made in one place** (`withLeftOutJobIds`, `leftOut.ts`): the page's own union moved into the kernel, and the page calls it. With nothing left out it returns the ZZ set itself, so a read under an empty list is the read there was before.
- **The list alone** (`loadLeftOutRules`, `leftOutIo.ts`): the email draws no picker, so it does not read every development and customer by name as `loadLeftOut` does for the page. Both read the row through one function.
- **The email says it** (`weekMailLeftOutLine`, `weekMail.ts`): under its name, in the page's sentence (`leftOutWords`): *Leaves out Oak Hollow and Ridgeway Builders.* With nothing left out the email says nothing of it, and its text and HTML are the ones there were. The page always shows its line because the line carries **Change ›**. A mail has no such press.
- **A list that cannot be read** leaves nothing out, as on the page, and the mail's last line names it: *Not read when this was built: the list of what the Bridge leaves out.* The page says nothing there. A mail cannot be read again, so it says so. When the list is read and its jobs are not, the rules still reach the bids and the estimates, as on the page, and the same gap is named.

No row, an empty row and broken text are an empty list. A saved empty list is too.
