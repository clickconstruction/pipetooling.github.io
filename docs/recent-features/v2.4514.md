# v2.4514 — Overhead: Man hours has a picture (2026-10-04)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. PR 2 of 3 on the Man hours card (v2.4513 was the numbers and the table). A table answers "how many"; the owner's question is which way the office is going against the field, and that is a shape.

## What changed

- **Two charts on one set of columns**, in [`ManHoursChart.tsx`](../../src/components/people/ManHoursChart.tsx), between the card's header and its table. Hours are stacked bars, bottom to top: field, office, bids, not on a job (hatched grey). Under them the office share is its own line, 0 to 50% (0 to 100% once any period passes half). Two charts and one scale each, never two scales on one chart.
- **A headline** above the picture reads the newest finished period against the one before it: *September 2026: 1,435 hours. Office share 25%, down from 28% the month before.* With nothing finished yet (the Year zoom in the clock's first year) it reads the open period and compares nothing.
- **Hover or focus a column** for its numbers: each side, the total, the office share, and the hours waiting for approval when there are any. The box stands beside the column, not on it. Every column also carries the same numbers as its accessible name.
- **The open period** is drawn faded, and the legend says so. The first point of the share line and the last finished one carry their number; the rest show on hover.
- **Colors** are three new tokens in `index.css`, one set per theme: `--man-hours-field`, `--man-hours-office`, `--man-hours-bids`. Each set was checked against the card's ground for color-blind separation and 3:1 contrast. Bids are orange here: the tab's blue bid labor beside its purple office labor fails that check (deutan ΔE 1.3), which matters once the two are stacked in one bar.
- **The kernel**, [`manHoursChart.ts`](../../src/lib/manHours/manHoursChart.ts): `manHoursAxis` (a round top in at most five steps), `buildManHoursBars`, `buildManHoursShareLine`, `pickManHoursHeadline` and `manHoursHeadlineWords`. The component only draws what these return. `manHoursPeriodShortLabel` names a period on the axis.
- On a phone the picture keeps a 620 px floor and scrolls sideways inside the card, like the table.
