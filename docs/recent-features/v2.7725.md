# v2.7725 — Bill Customer: the Invoice Modifications cards leave the window (2026-11-17)

Client only; no migration, no edge function, no screen change. The first cut on `src/components/jobs/SendRecordInvoiceModal.tsx` (the Bill Customer window), from its map `docs/SEND_RECORD_INVOICE_MODAL_ARCHITECTURE.md`. The file is not a row of punch list #46's ten; the map is its tracker.

## Why this cut

The window had three async submit paths (Stripe, physical, HouseCall Pro) that send real bills, and the map's order opens with kernels that edit those paths' own lines. The largest region that is JSX alone, with no submit path, no effect and no state of its own, was the gray **Invoice Modifications (optional)** card: the Line item override, Memo and Footer disclosures, written out once for the Stripe bill and once for the physical invoice.

## What shipped

- **`BillCustomerStripeModifications.tsx`** (388 lines) and **`BillCustomerPhysicalModifications.tsx`** (360) — the two cards, their markup the window's own, moved by script. Each takes a typed prop bundle: the window's state and setters under the window's own names, because the line is shared by both channels, the memo is written by the other tabs' preset rows, and the two shared disclosures stay open from tab to tab. Only two one-line derivations moved in (`activeStripeFooterPreset`, `physicalFooterActiveId`), each computed from a prop.
- **`BillCustomerMemoPresetRow.tsx`** — the memo presets' row of presses, a module component of the window until now, moved verbatim (the map's order step 2). The window still mounts it once, in HouseCall Pro's memo.
- **`billCustomerStyles.ts`** — the eight field styles and the line placeholder, one home read by the window and by the pieces cut out of it.
- **Stage A, three small functions to their lib homes with tests:** `billCustomerLineOnBillSummaryLine` and its 48-character max in `lib/billCustomerMemoPresets.ts`, `defaultStripeLineDescriptionFromJob` in `lib/stripeInvoiceLineDescription.ts`, `stripeInvoiceFooterSummaryLine` in `lib/stripeInvoiceFooter.ts`.
- **`SendRecordInvoiceModal.tsx`** — mounts the two cards. 3,522 lines (was 4,265): 743 out.

No submit path, effect, state, RPC or edge call was touched. `supabase/` is untouched.
