# v2.3368 — Your record: a teammate's three fixes, in their own units, with no money on the screen (2026-09-12)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. The teammate half of the Bridge ↔ Review fold (`to-dos/review-into-the-bridge.md`), built to the owner's decision of 2026-09-11: "I don't want to expose these numbers to teammates … I want to keep them focused." Mock-up T1 (items, present only when needed) from *Step Three, Redrawn* (artifact 9c72e792).

## Why

The Bridge's earned number rests on three inputs only the person who worked can supply: a % complete on the job (the kernel assumes half done without one and marks the figure ≈), closed sessions (a clock left running can't be approved), and a field report. The office's Needs You list already nags the office; nothing nagged the field, and the field never sees Needs You (`DashboardPinnedQuickRow` gates it to office roles, and Job Mode replaces the Dashboard with one card). This card puts those three things in front of the person, in their words, with the door beside each.

## What shipped

- **Kernel [`src/lib/dashboardYourRecord.ts`](../../src/lib/dashboardYourRecord.ts)** (+4 tests): `buildYourRecordItems({ todayYmd, weekStart, sessions, jobsById, reportedJobIds, officeJobId })` → up to `YOUR_RECORD_MAX_ITEMS` (3) items in priority order — `clock-open` (a session with no clock-out whose work date is before today, oldest first), `no-pct` (open jobs with closed sessions this pay week and no % > 0, most hours first; finished jobs, the office job and last week skipped), `no-report` (the same jobs with no `reports` row by this person this week). `yourRecordItemCopy` gives the row's title / detail / verb ("You're still on the clock from Thursday" · "1007 SpaceX has no % complete" · "No report on 878 Take 5 Seguin").
- **[`DashboardYourRecordCard`](../../src/components/dashboard/DashboardYourRecordCard.tsx)**: loads the viewer's own sessions for the Sun–Sat pay week plus any open session (RLS-scoped to `user_id`), the touched jobs, and their own reports; renders nothing when clean; refreshes on window focus and after each door saves. It **owns its three doors**: `FieldPctUpdateModal` (the field's existing % modal, `set_job_pct_from_field`), `AdditionalReportModal` (leave a report on the job), `DashboardMyTimeDayEditorModal` on that date (subject = self) — so the same card works on the Job Mode early return, where the Dashboard's tail modals are not mounted.
- **[`Dashboard.tsx`](../../src/pages/Dashboard.tsx)**: mounted under `DashboardJobModeCard` on the Job Mode view and directly above My Schedule on the full Dashboard, for every signed-in role (it is empty for anyone who didn't clock, so the office sees it only when their own record slips).
- Help guide `keep-your-record-straight.md` (roles: all).

## Verified

Kernel tests; typecheck · lint (0 errors) · theme check; dev server: the card renders the live items for a field account (see the PR for the impersonation used) and each door opens its modal; hidden for a clean record.

## Notes for the next session

- The Needs You builder was left alone on purpose — this card is the field's list, and the office's items (hours awaiting approval, finished jobs with no invoice) already exist there.
- "No report" counts any `reports` row by the person on the job this week, whatever the template; if a job type has no report template the row will keep showing — the office can decide whether that job class should be exempt (a `service_type` skip list would be one line in the kernel).
