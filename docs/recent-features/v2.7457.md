# v2.7457 — Lien desk: an affidavit that waits on its notice has a bin of its own (2026-11-16)

The owner's ask of 2026-11-16 (*"work on the affidavits in the wrong bin"*), after a read-only walk of the desk found the bin **Needs the property facts** holding affidavits whose property record was whole. The owner picked choice B of three drawn: a bin of its own, over one bin with a wider name and over keeping the affidavit off its column until its notice is on record.

## What was wrong

- The affidavit's `notice` gate is `noticed` from `list_lien_affidavit_windows`: a `job_lien_filings` row of kind `notice_53_056`, not voided. That row is written when the run records the mailing. So *on record* means mailed and recorded, and the gate is right to stay closed for a notice that is approved and still in the run.
- The words were written for one case, the late notice (v2.4708). Every affidavit whose one closed gate was the notice read *Send the § 53.056 notice first* under the tag *No notice on record*, with the press *Send the notice first*. On the day of the walk every such notice was already approved and standing in the run.
- The row's press opened Monthly notices on the **Missed** pile (`pile: 'missed'`, fixed), where a job with an approved notice is not listed.
- The pile was `needs_property`, named for a fact these papers did not lack, and the Board's crossing arrow from Sent landed on a bin that also held papers missing a property fact.

## The bin and the pile

- `LienAffidavitPile` gains `needs_notice`, *Waiting on its notice* (`LIEN_AFFIDAVIT_NEEDS_NOTICE_LABEL`). `affidavitWaitsOnNoticeAlone(gates)` is true when the notice is the one closed gate, and a paper not yet sent then stands in the new pile (`unsentPile`). A paper that lacks a fact and a notice stays in `needs_property`: the fact is the affidavit's own work, and the notice has its own chip and row. A sent, approved, held, filed or missed paper keeps its pile as before.
- The Board: `LienBoardAffidavitStage` gains `needs_notice`, second in the flow (`needs_property → needs_notice → to_send → …`). The crossing arrow runs from `notice:sent` to `affidavit:needs_notice`. The stages a late paper still has to pass start with *Mail its notice and record the mailing*, then the affidavit's three, so the count the at-risk rule races is four, as it was.
- A pair: an affidavit in the new bin and its job's notice chip share `pairKey` `notice:<jobId>` (`LienBoardPair.reason: 'notice'`), so pointing at one lights both. The pair's door is the notice's own. A job with no notice on the desk has no pair.
- The chip's press: the notice, or the run on that envelope when the notice is approved or printed.
- **By paper → Lien affidavits** lists the pile under the same name, from `LIEN_AFFIDAVIT_PILES`, with its chip and its gate strip (`LIEN_GATE_STRIP_PILES`).
- The tiles' counts, the at-risk count, the phone's folded count and the leader's Board do not change: the chips are the same chips in two bins.
- Readers of the pile that treat it as *a paper a gate holds* take the new pile with the old: the timeline's affidavit state (`affidavitStateOf` maps it to `needs_property`, read by its closed gate), the share's *to file* set, the redraw's live piles, the pane's draft footer. The lien month's rhythm words gain *wait on a notice*.

## The words

New pure kernel `lienAffidavitNoticeStanding.ts`:

- `lienAffidavitNoticeStanding(affidavit, notice)` reads where the notice stands from the notice's own pile on the desk: `not_needed`, `on_record`, `late` (nothing started, no month open), `needs_owner`, `to_send`, `awaiting`, `held`, `approved`, `printed`, `unrecorded` (marked sent with no mailing on record).
- `lienAffidavitNoticeWords(standing, { leader })` holds every string a screen says for it. `late` keeps the old words to the letter.
- `lienAffidavitNoticePile(notice)` is the notices pile a press opens.

Where they show:

- **To do row**: *Waiting on its notice · Its § 53.056 notice is approved and not mailed yet.* with the tag *Notice approved · not mailed yet* (`lienAffidavitNoticeTag` takes the standing). The press is the notice row's own: `BUTTON.send` to the run on the GC for an approved notice, `BUTTON.record_mailing` to the run for a printed one, *Open the notice* on the notice's own pile for the earlier stages, and *Send the notice first* on Missed for the late notice alone.
- **The chip's card** reads the row's detail, and its other-paper line already said which bin the notice is in.
- **The affidavit pane**: the gate row shows the standing as its fact and ends in *Open the notice ›* (`lienGateRow`), the band's press reads *Mail the notice first ›* for an approved or printed notice (`lienNextAct`, the standing passed through the timeline's affidavit input by the desk's builder), the hold line and the gates head say *§ 53.056 notice not mailed yet* (`lienDeskGates`, `lienAffidavitPaneSections`).
