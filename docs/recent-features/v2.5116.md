# v2.5116 — The Pipeline leaves out ZZ test jobs (2026-10-09)

Punch list #61, PR 1 of 3 (`to-dos/zz-test-jobs-hide.md`, planned in #5217; Todd's go 2026-10-09). The client only: no migration, no edge function. The owner, 2026-09-29: "the primary goal is to not let other non devs see them on jobs stages in particular." The gate was met on 2026-10-09: five ZZ jobs were on `jobs_ledger`, one 10 days old, past the sweep's week.

## What shipped

- **The kernel, `src/lib/jobs/zzTestJobVisibility.ts`.** The rule stays `isZzTestJob`: the job's own name or its customer's name starts with ZZ.
  - `hidesZzTestJobs(role)` is true for every role but dev, including a role still loading.
  - `withoutZzTestJobs(rows)` hands back the same array when nothing is dropped.
  - `zzTestJobIds` names the ZZ ids among the rows.
  - `withoutZzTestJobMoney({ jobs, invoices, payments }, extraIds)` drops a ZZ job together with its bills and payments. Dropping the job alone would leave its bills for `computeBillTruth` to file under `excludedOwed`.
- **The jobs cache (`JobsListCacheContext.tsx`).** State still keeps every row the reads return. The rule runs where the cache hands rows out: the context's `jobs` (a memo over state) and `runFetchJobs`' result.
  - So every way rows get in passes it: the board remembered in IndexedDB that paints before the fetch, each scope merge, the full fetch and the enrichment patch.
  - A dev whose role lands after the first read still gets the rows.
  - Everything that reads the cache loses the ZZ rows in this PR:
    - `/accounts-receivable`;
    - the Dashboard's Needs you → **Match deposits** window (`DashboardArDepositsModal`), whose bill targets no longer offer a ZZ job;
    - Quickfill's *Complete, no bill*, *No customer* and *Property kinds* sections;
    - the job window's list of jobs (`JobDetailModalContext`).
- **The strip and Quickfill's money (`fetchStagesHeaderStats.ts`).** `LEAN_STATS_JOB_COLUMNS` gains `job_name, customer_name`, and a third argument `{ excludeZzTestJobs }` is added.
  - When set, a fifth read lists every ZZ job the caller can see by name on the server: `ZZ_TEST_JOBS_OR_FILTER`, re-checked by the kernel.
  - That read takes no customer filter, like the invoice and payment reads it cleans (review on #5226). Under a filter, collected-by-day still reads every customer's payments, so it must lose every customer's ZZ ones. The paid subtraction counts only the filtered customer's paid ZZ jobs, as the head-count does.
  - Those ids and the active rows' own ZZ names drop the jobs, their bills (the unlinked-money rows included) and their payments before assembly and bill truth.
  - A paid ZZ job, which the active read never ships, comes off the paid head-count, and its payments leave collected-by-day. That is the name filter the plan recommended.
  - Three callers pass `hidesZzTestJobs(role)`:
    - the cache, for the Pipeline strip, whose 60-second freshness key now carries the choice;
    - `BilledAwaitingPaymentSection`;
    - `usePipelineMoneyOpportunities`, whose only host is Quickfill's Jobs cleanup.
- **A dev sees no change.** PR 2 gives a dev the switch in Hide groups….
- **The Dashboard's money does not move in this PR.** Its Billed pin (`useBilledTotal`), AR / Unbilled card (`useDashboardFinancials`) and Ready to bill / Billed lists (`useDashboardBillingInvoices`) read `jobs_ledger` themselves. Until PR 2, a non-dev sees the Pipeline strip and Quickfill read lower than those cards by exactly the ZZ jobs' money. No real job's figure changes anywhere.
- **Docs.** BILLING_FLOWS (One bill truth) and GLOSSARY (ZZ convention) are amended in place, and the card's status says PR 1 shipped.

## Proof

- **`zzTestJobVisibility.test.ts`, 8 tests:**
  - the role matrix, including a null role;
  - a ZZ job by name and by a customer name with leading spaces;
  - the same array back when nothing is dropped;
  - the money drop, with and without an extra id;
  - `computeBillTruth` over the result has no orphan, where dropping the job alone leaves one.
- **`fetchStagesHeaderStats.test.ts`, 3 new tests (12 in all).** The scenario has made-up jobs: a real billed A, a billed $2,200 ZZ job by name, a ready-to-bill ZZ job by customer, and a paid ZZ job outside the active read.
  - Without the option, no ZZ read is sent. Billed reads $2,400, the paid count 7 and collected $350.
  - With it, billed reads $300 with `excludedOwed` at 0, the lean billed rows are A alone, Ready to Bill reads 0, the paid count 6 and collected $200.
  - The ZZ read's select, order and range are pinned.
  - Under customer c1's filter, the ZZ read carries no filter. The paid count loses c1's paid ZZ job and not c2's, and c2's ZZ payment still leaves collected-by-day.
- **`JobsListCacheContext.render.test.tsx`, new, 4 tests on the real provider.**
  - An assistant's board and fetch result are without the two ZZ jobs, and the stats are asked to leave them out.
  - The dev gets all three, and the stats are asked to keep them.
  - A role that lands late: while it is null the board hides the ZZ jobs; once it lands as dev, all three show with no second read.
  - An assistant's remembered board, painted while the fetch is still out, comes without the ZZ job.
- The related suites pass (`src/lib/jobs`, `src/lib/billing`, `src/contexts`, `src/components/quickfill`, `src/hooks`, the Pipeline render smoke: 517 files, 4,938 tests). So do eslint, the theme-token check and the full typecheck.

## To see it

On the dev server, sign in as an assistant (`/dev-login?as=<assistant>&to=/jobs?tab=stages`). The Pipeline lists no ZZ job, and its sections and strip count without them. As the dev, every ZZ job is there as before. Quickfill's Billed awaiting payment lists no ZZ job for the assistant.
