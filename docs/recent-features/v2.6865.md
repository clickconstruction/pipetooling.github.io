# v2.6865 — Labels: tape prints straight to the Brother P-touch over USB (punch list #304) (2026-11-10)

The owner bought the Brother PT-D610BT and could not print to it from his Mac: the labels window sent tape to the browser's print window, and macOS asked for a driver Brother does not make (the printer speaks only its own raster language, `CMD:PT-CBP`, and is not AirPrint over USB). Card #294 had assumed it installs as an ordinary printer. Asked how he wanted to get past it, he picked *print straight over USB*. A scratch page proved Chrome can drive the printer over WebUSB on his machine, and he then printed from the real window: *it seems to be working*. He asked that the window say which browsers it works in.

## What changed

- **`lib/materials/ptouchRaster.ts`** (new, pure): the printer's commands as bytes (initialise, status request, raster mode, print information, margin, auto cut, feed and cut after, one raster line, print), `ptouchLabelJob` (one label as one job), `rasterLinesFromRgba` (a drawn strip to 16-byte lines, bottom row at pin 0, centred on the 128-dot head), `inkWidth` (the strip trimmed to what is drawn), `parsePtouchStatus` (the 32-byte reply: tape width, errors, phase), `labelPrintMs`, and the words the window says.
- **`lib/materials/ptouchUsb.ts`** (new): the WebUSB side. `ptouchUsbAvailable` asks the browser whether it reaches USB at all, never its name. `connectPtouch` reuses a printer the person already allowed (`getDevices`) or asks for one, claims the printer interface and reads its status. `print` sends one label per job: before each it initialises and asks for status, so nothing is sent while a label prints, then waits the label's own time.
- **`lib/materials/tapeStrip.ts`** + **`tapeStripCanvas.ts`** + **`TapeStripBitmap`** (new): the strip drawn at the printer's dots. The QR's squares come from `qrcode.react`'s own path, at 5 dots a square on 24 mm tape: 125 of the 128 dots.
- **`LabelsPrintModal`**: with Brother tape picked in a browser that reaches USB, the press is **Connect the P-touch** until a printer is connected. Then a line names the printer and its tape, the width chips go (the printer says its width), and **Print 1 test strip** and **Print N tape labels** send the strips, with *Printing 2 of 5…* and what went if it stops. **Use the print window instead** keeps the old route one press away, for Windows with Brother's driver.
- **A browser with no USB reach** (Safari, Firefox): the Brother tape choice carries a **Needs Chrome or Edge** chip before it is picked. Picked, a notice says to open the page in Chrome or Edge and why, with **Copy the link to this page**.
- **`tapeSetupSteps`**: the first-time steps for the direct route name no driver. The print window's steps say Brother's driver is for Windows and that there is none for a Mac, where v2.6853 said to add it.

## What the printer taught

- A label sent while another prints comes out blank. Each label waits for the one before it.
- One job holding several pages printed the first and left the printer deaf until it was switched off and on. Each label is its own job.
- The printer did not report *printing completed* for these jobs, so the wait is by the label's length, cut short when it does report.
- The cutter sits about an inch past the head, so every cut strip carries about an inch of blank lead-in. Chaining strips to save tape is not built.

## Checked

Unit tests for the bytes, the status reply, the raster mapping and the strip's layout; two render smokes for the window (a browser with USB, mocked, and one without). The owner printed from the real window on his PT-D610BT with 24 mm tape, from a local build in Chrome on macOS. Not checked: Edge, Brave (built on Chrome's engine; the window offers Connect the P-touch wherever the browser reaches USB), Windows by either route, and 18 mm tape.
