## What the GC reads now

The flat table (address · bill sent · amount owed, one row per bill) printed 628 Terrell Rd seven times, 9703 Lenox Hl under four spellings, and *nothing applied yet* on 16 of 19 rows. The statement for one GC is now:

- **The total first** — *Owed now $84,601.00 · 20 open bills at 6 properties.* When anything is paid: *$103,701.00 billed, $22,600.00 paid so far.*
- **One block per property** — the street once, the city and the count beside it, the property's subtotal on the right. Properties read A→Z as before (v2.1434); inside one, the oldest bill first.
- **One line per bill** — *Job 868 · Service Visit* · *Aug 18* · *$2,650.00*. The day is short inside the statement's own year and carries the year otherwise.
- **The job's name only when it says something** — a repeat of the GC's or the customer's own name is left off; an address and *(HCP 867)* tacked onto the end of a name are cut (`statementJobName`).
- **A payment under the bill it is recorded against** — *$5,000.00 paid by #4417 on Sep 8, of $14,800.00 billed*. A bill with nothing paid is one line; *nothing applied yet* is gone.
- **The account card** — the shared *Your account, any time* card bill emails carry (`_shared/portalAccountCard.ts`): the QR code, the address in words, *Pay online and see every open bill and payment, with no login.* The link keeps its `?src=gc-statement` tag.

## What counts as one property

Jobs linked to the same property record (`jobs_ledger.customer_address_id`), and jobs whose cleaned address matches (`normalizeAddressKey` — the portal's rule: commas, ZIP and state variants merge; suites never do). A job with no address stands alone under its name. The heading is the spelling that names a city, the most used among those.

Live on the Dudley Mason statement, the four Lenox spellings came out as one block of seven bills: those jobs are linked to one property record. *Lenox Hill* and *Lenox Hl* on two unlinked jobs would stay two blocks — the grouping is never fuzzy.

The dispatcher reads `customer_address_id` for the payload's jobs itself (`loadPropertyIdByJob`, chunked); the RPC is unchanged. A failed read groups by address alone and still sends.

## Decided here

- **Owed is the board's figure, and the payment line names only payments recorded against that bill.** Before, the amount counted linked payments while the v2.4100 line walked unlinked money oldest-bill-first (`paymentAttribution.ts`) — so job 273, with $38,780.00 on the job and on no bill, read *paid in full* beside $13,420.00 owed. One line can no longer say both. No dollar figure on the statement changed.
- **The office is told instead.** Draft Message shows *Paid on the job, not on a bill: Job 273 $38,780.00. The statement shows that job's bills as owed in full. If the money was for these bills, match it in Edit Job → Payments first.* (`gcStatementUnmatchedWords`). Whether the board and the statement should count that money the way the portal does is on `to-dos/owner-decisions-pending.md`.
- **When the payment rows do not add up to what is paid, the line names none** — *$5,000.00 paid so far, of $14,800.00 billed* — the portal's rule (v2.2320).
- **The QR code rides only where a file can.** A scheduled send and a Draft Message send attach it inline (`cid:portal-qr`); Preview draws it as a data URL; **Copy for email** pastes the card with the address and no code, because a picture pasted into a personal mail program may not arrive.
- **Deploy order is safe either way.** The client sends `email_html` (card, no code) and, beside it, `email_html_qr` + `portal_url`. A function from before this PR ignores the new fields and sends the first; the new one attaches the code and sends the second, or falls back when the address is not one of ours or is too long for a code. The response says `qr_attached`.
