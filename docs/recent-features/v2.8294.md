# v2.8294 — Customer pages on a phone: the signing form and the small presses are sized for a thumb (2026-11-22)

The pages a customer opens from a link, at a phone's width. The owner asked whether more pages should be made phone friendly, said look at the customer's pages, and said build what the look found. No migration, no edge function. Sizes only, at every width: no words changed and nothing moved to another place, with one exception named below.

## What the look found

Eight pages were opened through their sample links at 393 px: the estimate, the account page, pay, the agreement, the rating, the map, **Show us the problem** and the terms. None is wider than the phone. They are built for a phone and most of each one reads well. What was small:

| Page | What was small |
|---|---|
| The agreement, and every form that signs | The button that signs stood 34 px and as wide as its words. The two boxes a signer must tick were the browser's own 13 px squares. **Type** and **Draw** stood 32 px |
| The agreement | The office number under the button was words. A signer with a question copied it by hand |
| The account page | The pay-by days stood 28 px and **Tell us** 31. In the two ask forms the boxes stood 29 px with 13 px words, **Send request** and **Request a bid** stood 32 px, and *use a different number* stood 15 |
| Show us the problem | The phone number on top and the language press were one line of words each, about 20 px |
| The rating | The two links at its foot, the same |
| The estimate | **What's included** stood 18 px |

## What changed

- **The signing form** (`ContractAcceptSignatureForm`, shared by the customer's agreement, a sub's work order, the lien waivers and a GC's forms):
  - The button that signs is as wide as the name box, to 400 px, and 48 px tall.
  - Each box to tick is 22 px in a row 44 px tall, and the whole row is the press.
  - **Both boxes start at the form's left edge, one over the other.** Each row was centred on its own words, so a short line's box sat 30 px right of a long line's. This is the one thing that moved.
  - **Type** and **Draw** are 44 px tall and **Clear** is 40.
  - *How electronic signing works* has 12 px of press above and below its words.
- **The agreement's office number dials.** It is a link through `telHrefFor`, with the same room for a thumb.
- **The account page**: the pay-by days and **Pick a date** are 40 px, the note and **Tell us** 44. In the ask forms the boxes are 44 px with 16 px words, **Send request** and **Request a bid** are 44 px, and *use a different number*, *Use … instead* and *Show all N properties* are 44. **Print all** is 40, *Rate us with one tap* 44, and the payments search 44 with 16 px words.
- **16 px words in every box a customer types in on that page.** A phone zooms the whole page when a box with smaller words is pressed.
- **Show us the problem**: the number on top and the language press are 44 px each, one under the other with no shared spot. The number at the foot has room above and below.
- **The rating**: both links at the foot have room above and below.
- **The estimate**: **What's included** is 43 px tall.

## How it is built

- `src/components/contracts/signatureFormStyles.ts` holds the sizes: `SIGN_PRESS_MIN_HEIGHT`, `SIGN_TICK_ROW`, `SIGN_TICK_BOX`, and `INLINE_THUMB_PAD` for a link inside a sentence.
- `INLINE_THUMB_PAD` is 12 px of padding above and below with the same taken back as margin. A link ignores the margin and its line never moved. A button is a box on its line, and the margin is what keeps the line where it was.

## What the look changed

Seen on the dev server through the sample links, one page at a time, at 393 × 852, with the old code photographed from the same server.

1. **The line with *How electronic signing works* was pushed 24 px down.** The first build gave the press padding and no margin. It has both now.
2. **The two boxes to tick did not line up.** Larger boxes made it plain. Both rows start at the left edge.
3. **Show us lost its tight top.** The first build made the two presses 44 px by growing their rows, and the title fell 40 px. The presses now reach past their rows, and the title is 13 px lower than it was.
4. **The account page's ask forms were missed on the first look** and found by measuring every press on the page.
5. **The estimate's tags stay as they were.** At 11 px, *Recommended* pushed an option's name from two lines to three. The change was taken back.
6. **What's included keeps its card's height.** The first build left 12 px more under it.

`innerWidth` and `scrollWidth` were equal on every page at 393 px, and on the estimate, the agreement and the account page at 375 px. **What's included** was pressed and opened its list. Nothing that sends was pressed.
