# v2.6431 — Hours charged statement, PR 1: the rate the company charges for an hour, and the kernel's charged basis (punch list #251) (2026-11-03)

The owner, 2026-11-02, after seeing the Signed hours statement (#212) print on a real job: a second version of the same paper that says how much the customer is charged for the hours, not how much the worker was paid, at the company's rate of $285 an hour, set job by job in the window where the paper is made, and shown to everyone in the office where the paid paper is for a dev and the controller alone. The card `to-dos/hours-charged-statement.md` holds the ask, the map and the seven decisions. This first PR gives the rate a home and teaches the kernel the second basis; the window, the print's Charged band, the row's basis and the gate by basis are PR 2, which waits for the restyle (#6892) and the signing (#6893) to land so the charged paper copies one layout and widens one gate.

## What changed

- **The rate has two homes** (migration `20261103040000_hourly_charge_rate.sql`): the company's rate in `app_settings.hourly_charge_rate_v1`, seeded $285.00, and a job's own rate in `jobs_ledger.hourly_charge_rate`, nullable, over zero when set, null until PR 2's window and Edit Job write it. `src/lib/appSettingsKeys.ts` names the key, the default and `parseHourlyChargeRate` (a number over zero to the cent, else $285.00).
- **Settings → Jobs & dispatch → Rate charged for an hour** (`HourlyChargeRateSettingsBlock.tsx`, dev, self-contained like the trip charge amounts): one dollars-an-hour field and **Save the rate**; the words say the wage the worker is paid is never this number.
- **The kernel's `basis`** (`supabase/functions/_shared/hoursStatement.ts`, re-exported by `src/lib/people/hoursStatement.ts`): `basis: 'paid' | 'charged'` and `chargeRate` on the input, missing = paid, so every caller today builds the same paper. On `charged` the pay switch is ignored, every counted hour of a clock, schedule or sheet row is priced at the rate, the `pay` section is null, and a **`charged`** section carries one line per week or sheet (*Week of May 31 · 31.5 h at $285.00 = $8,977.50*), the total (*48 h at $285.00 an hour = $13,680.00*), the note that nothing on the paper is what the worker was paid, and `leftOut`, the sub's flat lines with no hours named and left off. The attestation drops the paid clause; `preparedWords` gives the company's line under the signature, with the rate on the charged basis, for the print to add who prepared it. With no rate given the paper carries hours alone and the section says so.
- **Tests.** `hoursStatement.test.ts` +6 (the charged week, a pending session uncounted, the flat sheet line left off and named, no rate given, a salaried person's days priced, no basis = the paid paper); `appSettingsKeys.hourlyChargeRate.test.ts`; `HourlyChargeRateSettingsBlock.render.test.tsx` (the fallback on open, the saved rate shown, Save writing one row under its key).
- **Docs.** The migration page; the card reads PR 1 built, PR 2 next.

## Decisions

- **The kernel is given the rate; it reads nothing.** The reads (PR 2) pick the job's rate, else the company's, and say which; the kernel prices and words.
- **A missing basis is the paid paper.** Nothing printed or saved before this PR changes shape.
- **The print, the window and the gate wait.** The charged paper copies the restyled print and widens the narrowed gate; building them against the old shapes would change two shapes instead of one.

## Deploy

After the merge, from the main checkout: `supabase db push` (one settings row, one nullable column), then `npm run gen-types:linked` as its own `chore(types)` PR. No edge function. The Settings card reads the row with $285.00 as the fallback, so the client may deploy first or last.
