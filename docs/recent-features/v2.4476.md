# v2.4476 — Robot runs, bank checks and trials: an evening stamp keeps its day (2026-10-03)

The last of the instant-day fixes that follow [v2.4452](v2.4452.md): what a fresh grep for file-local day helpers found after the surface PRs. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

These read an instant's day as its UTC date, so anything stamped after 7 pm CDT (6 pm CST) read as the next day:

- **The robot console** ([`twinConsoleDisplay.ts`](../../src/lib/twinConsoleDisplay.ts), `relativeTimeFrom`): past two weeks it printed `iso.slice(0, 10)`. That is a run's start on the twin runs ledger ([`TwinRunsLedger.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/TwinRunsLedger.tsx)), a twin seat's *used* day ([`DigitalTwinsPanel.tsx`](../../src/components/settings/DigitalTwinsPanel.tsx)) and a dev key's *issued* day ([`DevMcpKeysCard.tsx`](../../src/components/settings/DevMcpKeysCard.tsx)). The run's hover printed `started_at`'s first sixteen characters, a UTC wall clock.
- **The Mercury check** in Banking ([`BankingMercuryReconciliationTab.tsx`](../../src/components/banking/BankingMercuryReconciliationTab.tsx)): a missing transaction's day from Mercury's `postedAt`. The rest of Banking already reads it in Central ([v2.4463](v2.4463.md)).
- **A hiring trial's Keep trying** ([`trialTally.ts`](../../src/lib/hiring/trialTally.ts)): *Keep trying — Maria Sep 21* from `team_prospects.trial_deferred_at`, through `new Date(…).toISOString().slice(0, 10)`.

## What changed

Each reads `calendarYmdInAppTzFromIso(iso)` from [`dateUtils.ts`](../../src/utils/dateUtils.ts); the run's hover reads `toDatetimeLocal`, the wall clock in `APP_CALENDAR_TZ`.

## Not changed

- The manual accounts list's oldest and newest postings slice rows that the manual import stamps at noon on the typed day ([v2.4463](v2.4463.md)).
- The audit queue and the audit stamp slice a stamp only when it does not parse.
- The local day helpers left in `_shared/gcStatementByProperty.ts`, `_shared/billPaidBy.ts`, `_shared/gcChecksApplied.ts` and `stagesScheduleStrip.ts` read `date` columns or typed text.

## Tests

- `twinConsoleDisplay.test.ts` (+1): a run at `2026-08-02T00:30:00Z` (7:30 pm CDT on Aug 1) reads *2026-08-01* four weeks on; noon UTC reads its own day. Its fixture at midnight UTC pinned the UTC reading; it moves to midday and keeps its expectation.
- `trialTally.test.ts` (+1): Keep trying pressed at 7:30 pm CDT on Sep 20 reads *Sep 20*.

Both fail on main. The Mercury check's day and the run's hover change one expression each.

## Checked against prod

Read only, from the worktree dev server: 81 of the 250 robot runs older than two weeks started in a Central evening, so they read a day earlier on the ledger. The one trial deferral was pressed in daytime. The Mercury check reads Mercury live, so its sample was not counted.
