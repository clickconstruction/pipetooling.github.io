# v2.8343 — Bills sent: By day paid, what came in on any given day (2026-11-23)

Punch list #422, the third of three PRs. The owner's ask of 2026-11-23: the leader would like to know how much money is coming in on any given day from Stripe, perhaps as a view he switches to. The card and the mock-up he approved are in `to-dos/bills-sent-who-paid/`. No migration, no edge function.

## What is new

- **A switch at the top of Bills sent**: **By day sent**, the list as it was, and **By day paid**, at `/bills-sent?view=paid`.
- **A month of days.** A day money came is green and shows its figure and how many payments. Today has a ring. A day that has not come is dashed and cannot be pressed. ‹ and › step a month, and the newest month is this one.
- **A picked day** shows its facts one a line, then its payments: the bill, the job and who paid, the amount, the way as a pill, the time for a Stripe payment, the day the bill went and how long the money took.
- **One deposit that paid several bills** is drawn once, with its bills under it.
- **Chips narrow the month** to one way of paying: Stripe always, then each other way some payment that month came by. The month, the tiles and the day all follow the chip.
- **Tiles**: Today, This week and the month. A fourth, *Next Stripe payout to the bank*, shows what Stripe sends next and the day it arrives.
- **An empty day says so** and offers the next press: every way that day when the chip hid some, else today.
- **Paid this week**, a green tile on the list, counts the week's money and opens the view.
- **Print** prints the month a day at a time. **Download CSV** saves one row for each payment.
- The day, the month and the chip ride in the address, so a day can be saved or sent.

## What a day's figure is

From what the app keeps, and said in the view's foot and the guide:

- A payment sits on its `paid_on`: for a Stripe payment the day the customer paid, for a matched deposit the day it posted at the bank, for a payment typed by hand the day the person named.
- The amount is what the payment took off its bill. Stripe's fee is not taken off, because the app does not keep it.
- A refund made in Stripe is not seen. A credit in kind is listed and never added. A payment on a test-mode bill is left out.
- The payout tile is what Stripe sends to the bank after its fee. It is drawn beside the days and added to none.

The payout day by day, with the payments inside each payout, would need a new read of Stripe's payout history. It is not built.

## How it is built

- `src/lib/billing/billsPaidDays.ts`, new and pure: `billsPaidLines`, the month's grid (`billsPaidMonthCells`), a day's deposit groups (`billsPaidDayGroups`), the chips (`billsPaidHowOf`, `billsPaidChips`), the tiles, the empty day's words, the spreadsheet and the print. A payment's way is worded by `billPaymentWayWords`, the job window's own words.
- `src/lib/billing/billsPaidIo.ts`, new: `readBillsPaid` reads `jobs_ledger_payments` by `paid_on` for a span, which the page's four roles read under the table's own policy, then the bills, the jobs, and each bill's sent copies for its number and the day it first went. This month is read back to the Sunday that began the week, so the week's tile is whole. `readStripePayout` asks `get-stripe-payouts`, the edge function the Dashboard's Money in bar asks, whose own gate is the same four roles.
- `src/components/billing/BillsPaidByDay.tsx`, new. Its values are the app's own working out, so none is a press: a day's facts are `ValueLines`, and every amount goes through `AmountSmallCents`.
- `BillsSentLedger.tsx` draws the switch and does not read the list while the second view shows. The looks the two views share moved to `components/billing/billsSentLook.ts`.
- The print is a reading of the payment rows, not a send: it has its row in `NOT_A_SEND` (`sentCopiesCoverage.test.ts`).

## Not built

- A red word on a disputed card payment. The case is on record in `ar_stripe_cases`; the view does not read it yet.
- A week's total at the end of each row of the month.
