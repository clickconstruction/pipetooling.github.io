# v2.7397 — GC rating and Scores: the fixes from the viewport walk (2026-11-15)

Punch lists #357 and #360. Taunya asked for everything the two trains built to be walked at every width before they close. This is what that walk found and fixed. It also carries the live-walk records of #357 PR 5 and PR 6, #360 PR 5 and the residual, in their own fragments.

## The walk

On clicktooling.com as the dev, on the ZZ test GC, bid and job. Each surface was loaded fresh at 375, 768, 1024, 1280 and 1600 px and measured: sideways scroll, anything past the edge, clipped words, presses under 44 px on a phone, type under 11.5 px, and the contrast of every word in light and in dark. Then each was pictured at 375 and 1280 px.

The surfaces: the GC's card *How they are to work with* and *What the rating did*; the Workbench's *Can't be scored* line, difficulty strip and site pick; the Bid Board's row and phone card with their chips; the master's sheet; *Rate a GC after a job* and its list; the ask in the job's window; Pricing rules with *The score's target*; People → Scores.

Nothing scrolled sideways, ran past an edge or clipped at any width, and no word of these surfaces failed contrast in dark. The sheets sit in the screen with their foot presses in view at every width.

## What was fixed

- **Small presses on a phone.** The quiet text presses were 15 to 20 px tall: *Pick*, *Change*, *Done* and *Clear the pick* under the strip, *Open Labor* on the *Can't be scored* line, *Open the GC's rating* and *Read the new rating in* in the strip, *Open their record* on the sheet, *Open Pricing rules* on the Scores tab. Each now has a 45 px hit area and draws where it drew (`display: inline-block` with padding the margin takes back). *Rate them* and *Not now* in the job's window went from 36 to 44 px, and *Open Labor* on the estimator's score sheet from 36 to 44.
- **The paying line's order** (`difficultyLines.ts`). When the cap bit, its words were added at the end of the line, after where the point came from, so a point set by hand read *…their bills are on another system, held to $113 by the cap: They pay slow.* and one read from bills read *Read from their bills., held to…*. Where the point came from now closes the line, after the cap's words. A bid keeps the words it was priced with, so an old line reads the old way until it is read in again.
- **The stale line on a narrow Workbench** (`BidDifficultyStrip.tsx`). *Read the new rating in* broke into three lines beside its sentence. It now stays whole and drops under the sentence.
- **A reason set by hand on the master's sheet** (`difficultyRateSheet.ts`) ends with a full stop before *Not yours to pick.*
- **The two cards' heads** on the customer's page: the lead under the title was pushed in 8 px when it wrapped on a phone. It now lines up with the title.

## Seen and left

- **Pricing rules on a phone** is 272 px wide in a 375 px screen, its number boxes are 34 px tall and its close press takes a row to itself. It is the bids area's shared window shell (`ModalShell`), and the office's screen more than a phone's. Left; it wants its own look.
- **A floating figure on the Bid Board** draws over Pricing rules on a phone and covers part of *Save as version*. It is not this train's and sits above the window. For whoever owns it.
- **The Bid Board's chips** (*Rated N points*, *Can't be scored*) are 20 to 23 px tall as presses on the phone card. So is every other press on that card (18 to 28 px). Left to the card's own convention.
- **The Scores tab's window presses** (*Last 4 weeks* …) and *Open the N jobs* are 28 px on a phone. PR 1's; left, with this note.
- **Muted words on the page's grey ground** read 4.3 to 1 in light: the Scores tab's lead and its *Read on the server* line. It is the app's muted token on the page ground, not this tab's choice.
- **Shrinking a desktop window to phone width without a reload** leaves the header's desktop row and a sideways scroll. The app's header, on every page.
- **The pane's phone emulation** reports a wider window than it draws, so the app's scroll lock pads the page by 103 px behind an open sheet. A real phone reports the same width both ways. The pictures were taken with that pad set to nothing.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **Doubted: 44 px everywhere.** A text press that is 44 px tall in a sentence pushes the lines apart. Kept the look and grew the hit area instead; two presses a line apart can now overlap by a few pixels, which is the trade.
- **Doubted: rewriting the kept words on old bids.** A line's words are part of what the bid was priced with. Left them; the next read-in writes the new order.
- **Doubted: fixing the shared window shell here.** It carries every Bids window. A change to it is a sweep of its own, cut from fresh main.
- **Not walked at every width:** the estimator's score row and sheet, which need a real estimator's account. It was read at 390 and 1280 px in v2.7369's walk and its one finding is fixed here.
