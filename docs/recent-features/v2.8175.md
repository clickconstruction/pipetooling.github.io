# v2.8175 — Documents: an estimator reads the Agreements tab (2026-11-21)

Punch list #403, step 4, the last PR of three: the Agreements tab for an estimator, the owner's call 8. A kernel, the tab and its read. No migration and no edge function.

## What changed

- **`canSeeAgreements` takes the estimator.** The Documents page draws the **Agreements** tab for them, and the Search tab's Agreements section too.
- **An old link stops landing them on Estimates.** `/documents?tab=agreements` used to show an estimator the Estimates tab, since the tab was not theirs. It now opens the tab.
- **The list is what the database answers them.** The read is the same view, `agreements_on_file`, under each table's own row security: an outside company's agreements and sub work orders. No policy changed.
- **The chips are theirs.** *All*, *Outside companies* and *Work orders*. The chips for kinds an estimator cannot read are left out: job contracts, warranties, staff and subs, partners and GC projects.
- **Nothing to act on.** No **Send a paper**, no **Contract library**, and no press on a line. A paper's name and its job are plain words. A waiting line carries no **Send again**, no **Copy the signing link** and no **Add to a list**.

## Where a trade company's agreement sits for an estimator

The office finds a trade company's agreement under **Staff and subs** or **GC projects**. An estimator has neither chip, and an outside company is a customer, a supply house or a trade company. So their **Outside companies** chip asks for all four kinds: `customer_agreement`, `supply_house_agreement`, `trade_agreement` and `company_agreement` (`ESTIMATOR_GROUP_KINDS`). The mock-up draws it that way.

A party on no list is closed to an estimator by the read policy, so that kind is asked of no chip of theirs.

## Who acts

`canActOnAgreements` is the four roles that write a paper: a dev, a leader, an assistant and the controller. The tab reads it for every press it draws. Sending is also held on the server, where `send-contract-for-signature` has named the four roles since v2.8101.

`agreementsReader` says how a role holds the tab, *office* or *estimator*, and the read carries it so a chip asks for that reader's kinds.

## Words an estimator reads

- Under the list: *This list is for reading. A signed paper, a W-9 or a certificate opens from the company's own page.* The office's line about the Contract sweep and Estimates is not drawn for them.
- With nothing on file: *No agreements yet. The office sends a paper to an outside company from this tab.* It has no press, since an estimator sends nothing.
- A search or a chip that matches nothing reads as it does for the office, with **Clear the search** or **Clear the filters**.

## What is still not on the tab, for anyone

The tab lists agreements that were sent. A W-9, a certificate and a paper that was made and never sent are on the company's own page since v2.8162, and not here. Whether the tab should list them is asked of the owner.
