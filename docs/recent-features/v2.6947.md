# v2.6947 — Your card and footer: every phone number one way (punch list #313) (2026-11-11)

Punch list #313 (`to-dos/cards-and-footers/README.md`). The owner, looking at his own card on the day it went live, with a screenshot of *617 939 6295* over *(512) 360-0599*:

> visually I would like all numbers to either be ### ### #### or (###) ###-#### right now there is a mismatch

## What went wrong

The card printed the mobile exactly as the account holds it, and accounts hold whatever was typed: *617 939 6295*, *6179396295*, *(830) 743-2797*. The office line comes from the company's constant, already *(512) 360-0599*. The two sat side by side in two shapes.

## What changed

- **`kitPhone`** in `src/lib/brandKit/person.ts`: a number of ten digits, or eleven with a leading 1, prints as *(512) 555-0142*. It is `formatChasePhone`, the rule Call mode already uses, not a second one. A number that is not ten digits prints as typed. `kitPersonFromUser` runs the mobile and the office line through it, so the card, the footer, the team's batch and the card's own *Mobile* and *Office* rows all read the same.
- **The pick** is *(###) ###-####*: the office line and most accounts are already written that way, so fewer cards change.
- **The account is not touched.** Only what is drawn.
- **`to-dos/cards-and-footers/make.mjs`** prints numbers by the same rule, so a file made by the command matches one made in the app.

## Tests

`draw.test.ts`: six ways of typing one number all print as *(617) 939-6295*; a mobile and an office line typed two ways come out alike; a short or foreign number is left as typed; an empty one stays empty.
