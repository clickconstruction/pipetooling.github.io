# v2.5403 — Messages: a line on an estimate from a customer's message, behind the scenes (2026-10-12)

Residential R8 · 8, the kernel: `src/lib/estimates/lineFromMessage.ts`, pure and tested
(`to-dos/gc-mode/messages/RESIDENTIAL_BUILD_PLAN.md` → R8; card 8 of `customer-messages-integrations-mockups.html` v0.2,
both on branch spike/gc-mode). It mirrors cut 4b's change order from a message (`changeOrders/fromMessage.ts`) with the
estimate's form. No migration, no RPC, no edge function and no screen.

- `lineDraftFromMessage(message, contact, jobs, estimates)`: the Estimates form's starting line, on the Pipeline job the
  message is tied to (`jobs_ledger`), with the customer who wrote as the signer and the message kept as the source. The
  line is the estimate's own `EstimateLineItemNormalized`: the message's first sentence as the description, cut at a
  word past 160 characters, quantity 1, $0 and no line name, for the office to fill in. It joins the job's open estimate
  (a draft estimate, the newest when there are two, taking the place of a blank or placeholder last line) or starts a
  new one. A sent, accepted, declined or superseded estimate and a change-order document are never joined. Refused in
  words: someone who is not a customer, a message with no job, a job that is not theirs, and a job that is billed or
  paid. A job that is ready to bill still takes a line.
- `whoMayDraft`: the GC office team. `whoMaySend`: the GC money team (`gc/access.ts`), as with the change order from a
  message. An ordinary estimate's send is wider today: `/estimates` is open to primary and superintendent too. Widening
  this one is the single `LINE_SEND_TEAM` line.
- `becameChip`: *became line 2 on estimate 1044, $0 until priced*, then the price; *became line 1 on a new estimate*
  before the new one has its number. `draftedWords` for the office. `pricedWords` for the line back to the customer once
  it has a price.

The name: card 8 used the word for one of an estimate's options, a priced extra the customer ticks (GLOSSARY →
*Estimate options*). This is a plain line in `line_items_snapshot`, so the lead named it a line from a message.

It is a new estimate, never a change order: on main a change order is the GC customer's paper with its own signing, and
new work after the first estimate is accepted is a second estimate the customer approves the same way.
