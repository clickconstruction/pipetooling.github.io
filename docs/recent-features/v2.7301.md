## What changed

- **The price list** ([`src/lib/bids/difficultyPrice.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/difficultyPrice.ts), `difficulty_price_versions`): what a point costs, versioned like the criteria. Each field point a percent of the bid's field hours at its labor rate; each office point so many office hours at the office rate; each site point (PR 6) a percent of field hours; the paying point a rate a month on the price for the days the GC's median runs past the threshold; all lines together at most a percent of the bid's own cost; the Paying rule's numbers (the days, the late bills in a year, the bills a read needs), which `readDifficultyPaying` now takes from it; and who may rate a GC (`raters`: the office, the estimators, the masters — `difficultyCanRate` replaces the card's and the Bid Board's `isOfficeRole`). Version 1 is the canvas's numbers. **Bid Board → Pricing rules** ([`BidDifficultyPriceModal.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/BidDifficultyPriceModal.tsx)): the owner, the controller and a dev edit the numbers and tick the raters; the try-it line reads the kernel on the canvas's example as they type; *Save as version N* writes the draft and publishes it in one press; *Every version* lists each in sentences. Estimators read it.
- **The lines** ([`difficultyLines.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/difficultyLines.ts)): a standing rating, the price list and the bid's field hours, labor rate, own cost and price → one line per rated scale with its hours, rate, amount, the rung's sentence and the arithmetic in words; a scale the list has no rule for, or that prices to nothing, is named and adds nothing; the cap shrinks every line by the same share. A by-hand paying point carries one threshold's worth of days.
- **On the bid** ([`bidDifficulty.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/bidDifficulty.ts), [`useBidDifficulty.ts`](../../src/hooks/useBidDifficulty.ts), `bid_difficulty`): when the Workbench first costs a bid to a rated GC under a published price list (the numbers settled, a labor rate and field hours on the bid, the customer-facing price of its own GC on screen, not sent), the hook writes the lines as rows of `cost_estimate_other_rows` — one per line, the note *Difficulty · Their office 1 · 12 office hours at $80*, the dollars split by stage as the bid's field hours are — and pins what it wrote: the rating, the two versions, the picks, the lines, the rows' ids. The cost kernel, the margin, the pricing history, the budgets and *Bids like this* count them as any direct cost. The Labor tab draws the pinned rows with the chip **difficulty**, read only, no ×.
- **The strip** ([`BidDifficultyStrip.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/BidDifficultyStrip.tsx)), under the priced-margin line: *Difficulty · 4 points · +$6,420 in cost* and *about +$9,170 on the price at 30%*; the fold lists each line, the lines taken off (struck through, with who and why), who rated and who priced under which price version, the rating's note and the door to the GC's card. **Take a line off this bid…**: the line and a reason, the press named for the line; the row is deleted and the pin records it. A GC rated again since the bid was priced: *Rated higher since this was priced: 5 points now, 3 when priced, by Taunya on 2026-11-20.* (or lower, or again) with **Read the new rating in**, which rewrites the rows from the new rating; a sent bid keeps its lines.
- **The stamp**: `stamp_bid_priced_margin` takes `p_difficulty_usd` and writes `bids.priced_difficulty_usd`; the priced-margin line reads *$960 of it difficulty*.
- Guide *set what difficulty costs*; the rating guide says the price list sets who may rate and what a point costs.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **Doubted: a sixth direct-cost kind.** The plan said rows "of a new kind". The five kinds are five tables unioned by hand in the view, the pricing-history SQL (pinned to the kernel by a test), the budgets, the bid-changes list, the primary-scope copy and the deleted-records archive, and the kernel's breakdown has a fixed field per kind. A sixth table would have spread one change across all of them. Kept: the lines are Other rows the pin names by id, so every reader counts them unchanged, and the Labor tab and the Workbench tell them apart by the pin. The cost is that a row's kind chip says *difficulty* only where the pin is read; a print or a CSV reads *Other* with the note *Difficulty · …*.
- **Doubted: writing on open.** The lines are written when the Workbench first costs the bid, which is a read. The alternative, a press, would have put the owner's intent (every new bid to a rated GC carries the cost) behind an estimator's choice. Kept the write, narrowed: a labor rate and field hours on the bid, the customer-facing price of its own GC on screen, not sent, once per bid per open (a refused write is not retried every render), and the strip says it wrote and what.
- **Doubted: what the paying point carries.** "On the price" is the canvas's words, but the price is what the lines feed. Kept: the revenue over saved prices when the bid has one, else the bid's own cost, and the pin records which (`carried_usd`). A by-hand point has no median, so it carries one threshold's worth of days (45), named in the line's words. The median comes out of the stored reason's words (*Paid in 52 days*), which are the kernel's own.
- **Doubted: the stage split.** A difficulty line is dollars by stage like any direct cost. Kept the bid's own field-hours shares (all rough-in with no hours), so the lines follow the work.
- **Left for Micah, with my pick:** whether a bid rated *lower* since priced should offer the press too. The owner's line was *rated higher*; the strip says lower and reworded as well, with the same press, since a lower rating is the GC's record too. My pick is to keep it.
- **Left for Micah, with my pick:** the difficulty row on a print or a CSV reads as *Other* with its note. My pick is to leave it until someone asks; the note says what it is.
