# v2.6923 — The pricing twin leaves a quote as a price sheet (2026-11-10)

Punch list #308, PR 9 of nine: the AI's door. The estimator asked for a tool for the AI to enter pricing from a price sheet without harming the parts book. The pricing twin already reads a bid's quote PDFs. It now leaves each one as a price sheet draft, and the same cards a dropped sheet gets decide what is filed.

## What changed

- **Migration `20261111140000_price_sheet_robot.sql`** ([doc](../migrations/20261111140000_price_sheet_robot.md)): `supply_papers.robot_request_id`, the pricing request a twin read the sheet for. Push after the merge, before `twin-mcp` is deployed.
- **`twin-mcp` v1.12.0, verb `put_price_sheet`** (pricing twin only, `PRICER_VERBS` in `_shared/twinSeatGate.ts`): the house's quote row by row as printed, under its fixture headings, with the house's own item numbers and each row's own price. Service-role writes of one `supply_papers` row, its groups and its lines, held to a request the twin has claimed. One sheet per house per request. `replace: true` rewrites the twin's own sheet and is refused once a person has filed a row or placed a fixture. Deploy after the migration is pushed.
- **`supabase/functions/_shared/priceSheetRows.ts`**: `shapeTwinSheet` checks and shapes what the twin sent (a description on every row, no price of 0, `C` and `M` as per hundred and per thousand, charges marked, at most 800 rows) and `twinSheetSaved` writes its reply. Unit tested from `src/lib/materials/priceSheet/priceSheetRows.test.ts`.
- **The twin's guide** (`docs/twins/pricer.md`, bundled into `twin-mcp/briefs.ts`), its kickoff template and the kickoff text the app builds name the verb and its rule: a draft of what the paper prints, never a part, never a filed price.
- **The app**: `readerWords` (`sheetPlan.ts`) says *read by the pricing twin* on the panel's header and in the list of all sheets, and the chip on Takeoffs ends with *twin*.
- **Guide** `read-a-supply-houses-price-sheet`: a section on a sheet the twin read.

## Why a draft

The twin decides nothing about the parts book. Matching a row to a part, the house question, a price that moved more than a quarter and a part that is new all stay on the estimator's cards, where the same kernel (`partMatch.ts`) runs for every door. A twin cannot call a filing function: `price_sheet_gate` refuses it.
