# v2.3661 — Lien desk: months say what their deadline means, and a skipped month stays on the record (2026-09-21)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Third of the pane clean-ups after v2.3657 (gates) and v2.3658 (owner envelope).

## Why

The owner, on the Months row (*Jul · 5.2 h · by Oct 15*): *"I don't totally understand this section, why the 'by'… can we present this better so it is more clear to the user its significance."* And on the footer's **Skip these months…**: *"since there is an option to skip these months, we should also present the user with something that shows them any months in the past that have been skipped — maybe bars or dots, and when they click on them it opens a modal with more detail."*

"by Oct 15" is the statute's deadline — the § 53.056 notice for July's work must be mailed by the 15th of the third month after, or July's lien right is gone — and every work month has its own. The chip never said so. And a skip, the one irreversible decision on the desk, left no trace once its row aged off the list.

## What shipped

- **Kernel** — [`lienMonthHistory.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienMonthHistory.ts): `buildLienMonthHistory(jobId, items, months)` → one entry per settled month: `sent` (a sent desk item, or the RPC's `noticed`), `skipped` (a `missed` item **with** a `skipReason`), `missed` (a closed window, or a `missed` item with no reason — the desk's mark, not a person's decision). An item wins over the RPC's flags; a send is final; other jobs, affidavit items, voided and live items are ignored. `lienMonthOutcomeMeaning` is the dialog's closing sentence. 5 tests. No new read — the desk already loads every item.
- **[`LienDeskMonths.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskMonths.tsx)** — *Months this notice covers* with the one-line why; a tickable card per open month (name, approved hours + crew — the evidence that used to hide in a tooltip — and *Mail by <date> · N days left*, red inside a week); the claim beside them; a note naming the earliest deadline when several are ticked; and **Earlier months**, a row of dots (green sent · hollow amber skipped · red missed). A dot opens a small dialog: when, by whom, the typed reason, the deadline and hours when known, and what it meant for the lien right. A month is shown once — a card if it is open or named by this item, otherwise a dot. The dialog is portalled because the card is a size container (which would capture a `position: fixed` child).
- **Who skipped** — `skipLienDeskItem` takes `userName` and stores `fields.skippedBy = { name, at }`; `parseLienDeskDraftFields` reads it. No schema change (`fields` is jsonb). Older skips show the date from `updated_at` and no name.
- `.lienMonths*` in [`index.css`](../../src/index.css): its own container; under 520px the claim drops beneath the cards.
- Guide *send lien notices from the Lien desk* → the month cards, and what a skip leaves behind.

## Limits

History is what the desk can see: every sent or skipped **item** however old, plus the months the RPC still returns. A window that closed with nothing on record more than a week ago has no row anywhere, so it has no dot.
