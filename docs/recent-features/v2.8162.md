# v2.8162 — Papers: a supply house and a customer each list their own papers (2026-11-21)

Punch list #403, step 4, the second PR of three: the party's own page. Screens, a kernel and its reads. No migration and no edge function.

## What changed

- **A supply house's window has a Papers section.** Under the house's contacts, it lists every paper that is with the house or one of its reps.
- **The Customer page's Agreements card lists the customer's own papers**, under *Papers with this customer*, beside the job contracts it has always shown. The card now also shows for a customer who has one of these papers and no job contract.
- **An estimator gets the same list.** The supply house's window draws the section for them. On the Customer page, where an estimator has no Agreements card, the papers get a card of the same name. It shows only when there is a paper.
- **A signed paper's name opens its signed record**, the window Documents → Agreements opens. A paper that is not signed has no press. Nothing on these lists sends, changes or removes a paper.

## Which papers are a party's

A paper is with a party when its `party_contact_id` names one of the party's contacts:

- for a customer, the customer's own contact or the contact of a person on file at that customer;
- for a supply house, the house's own contact or the contact of one of its reps, an archived rep among them.

The rule is `contactIsOfParty` in `src/lib/contracts/partyPapers.ts`, pure, with its tests. The reads are `partyPapersIo.ts`: the people at the party, then the contacts, then the papers, each under its own table's row security. The contact ids are asked in chunks and paged, so nothing is cut at 1,000 rows. A party with no contact yet has no paper, and the read makes none.

## What a line says

One paper a line: a tag with its kind (*Agreement*, *W-9*, *Insurance certificate*, *License*), its name, who signs, where it stands and its day.

- A paper that waits reads *never opened* or *viewed, not signed*, and a signed one *signed ✓*. These are the Agreements tab's own words, so a line here and its line there agree.
- The day and the count are drawn as values, apart from the words (values in place).
- A read that fails says so on `OfflineRetryPanel`: a lost signal offers Retry and reads again by itself, and a refusal shows its reason. It never reads as an empty list.
- An empty list in the supply house's window names where a paper is sent from. The office gets the press to Documents → Agreements. An estimator reads *The office sends one from Documents.* and gets no press.

## Two things this list shows that no screen showed before

The Agreements tab reads the view `agreements_on_file`, which lists agreements that were sent. This list reads the papers' own table, so it also shows:

- **A W-9, a certificate or a license that is with an outside company.** The owner's call 9 put these on the company's own page.
- **A paper that was made and never sent**, such as the row a send leaves behind when it fails. It reads *not sent*, with the day it was made and no press.

Both are the build's reading, and both are asked of the owner in the PR. A party on no list has no page, so its W-9 and its unsent paper are still on no screen.

## The signed record, for a reader it was not built for

An estimator's role is refused two reads the signed record makes: the form's layout (`contract_form_templates`) and the signature's image (the `contract-signer-signatures` bucket). The window used to wait on both without end.

- The signature now reads *The signature's image did not open. It is kept for the office.*
- A form whose layout did not read says so, and that the PDF holds its answers.
- The line under a form's answers adds that an estimator opens an outside company's PDF, which has been true since v2.8108.

Whether an estimator should read the layout and the image is a policy change, so it is a migration and the owner's call.

## Who sees what

| Role | Supply house's window | Customer page |
|---|---|---|
| Dev, leader, assistant, controller | Papers section, every paper | Agreements card, with *Papers with this customer* |
| Estimator | Papers section, what the database answers them | A card named Agreements, when there is a paper |
| Primary, superintendent | No section, and nothing is read | No card |

No policy changed. Every row comes back under the reader's own row security.
