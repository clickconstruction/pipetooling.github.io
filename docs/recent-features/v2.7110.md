# v2.7110 — Supply houses: one list of the bills whose lines need a person (2026-11-12)

Punch list #298 PR 3 (`to-dos/supply-bill-lines/README.md`, frame F of its mock-up), over the sweep ([v2.6882](v2.6882.md)) and the checks ([v2.7103](v2.7103.md)). The sweep reads every bill with a PDF by itself. What it could not give lines to, and what does not add up, waited where nobody saw it: a held bill reads *No lines yet* on its own row, on a house's card, and nowhere else. The measure counted 49 such bills. This is the screen that shows them.

## What a person sees

- **One line under Accounts payable** on Materials → Supply houses: *Lines read on 496 of 543 bills · 475 add up · 47 need a person · 19 with no PDF* (`standingWords`). Its press names its count, **Show the 47** (`showHeldLabel`).
- **The window *Bill lines*** (`src/components/materials/BillLinesHeld.tsx`): the counts as chips, then each reason a bill waits with how many bills and what opens it. A reason opens to its bills, newest first: the house, the number, the date, the amount and what was found (the gap in words, or the numbers the file does hold).
- **Each bill has its door.** **Look** opens the Look window ([v2.6819](v2.6819.md)) on a paper whose total is not the amount typed, or whose lines do not add up. **Open the bill** closes the window, opens the bill's house and its own Edit window, where the link, the number and the amount are. *Open in Drive* is beside it when the bill has a link.
- **A reason with nothing to do has no press**: a statement, a read that failed and is tried again, a read the model refused or cut.

## How it is built

- **`src/lib/materials/lines/heldBills.ts`** (pure, `heldBills.test.ts`): `heldBills` takes every bill, the papers on bills, the holds and the houses' names, and answers where the set stands (`LinesStanding`) and the groups. A paper wins over a hold an older run left. An off paper is *the total is not the amount typed* when its note is the check's own sentence for that (`noteIsRowAmount`, held to `checkPaper`'s words by a test), else *the lines do not add up*. A hold's sentence is the sweep's own (`HOLD_WORDS`). A link that is no Drive file is held as that before the sweep says so.
- **`heldBillsIo.ts`**: four reads under the office's SELECT policies, each paged with `fetchAllRows`. A read that fails throws, and the line draws the failure on `OfflineRetryPanel`, so a lost signal offers Retry and a refusal shows its reason.
- **`SupplyHousesTab.tsx`** lends the component its `paperIo`, a function that opens a bill by its house and its id (`openBillFromLines`), our copy of a paper's PDF, and a key it bumps after a bill or a paper is saved. Nothing else in the tab changed.
- **The Look window's price box** is wider. A price with four decimals was clipped, the leftover the card carried from PR 1b's walk.

## Decisions

- **No press reads a bill.** The mock-up drew *Read 287 bills* for the backfill. The backfill ran by cron on 2026-11-10 and the sweep reads a new bill within minutes, so the press would be dead nearly always. The window says how many wait to be read and that the app reads them by itself.
- **A bill with no PDF is listed and not counted as needing a person.** Some bills never had a paper. The count a person is asked to work is the off bills and the held bills that have a door.
- **Reasons are stacked blocks, not a table**, so the window reads at phone width with no sideways scroll. The bills under a reason are a table that scrolls inside its own box.

## Is this the best we can do?

For seeing them, yes: every bill that waits is one press away, with its reason and a door that lands on the field to fix. Two things would make it better and are not built.

- **A held bill's door opens the whole Edit window.** A press that fixed the link or the number in place, with the file's own numbers offered as choices, would be faster for the 19 whose number is not in their file. The note already lists those numbers.
- **The window does not say what a fix did.** After a link is fixed the bill moves to *to read* and leaves the list within ten minutes. A line that says *3 fixed today, waiting to be read* would close the loop.

## Walked

Look opened on an off paper and Escape came back to the window. Open the bill closed the window and opened *Edit invoice* with its link in the field. At 375 wide the page did not scroll sideways.
