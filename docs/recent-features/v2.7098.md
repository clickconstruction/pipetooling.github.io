# v2.7098 — Corrections as links: the Timeline's chips, Correct this entry… and the flag on a cited section (2026-11-12)

Punch list #315, upgrade 1, PR 2. Builds on v2.6969's `person_file_amendments`.

## What changed

- **The Timeline** (`PersonFilePanel.tsx` → `amendBits`): a line that a later entry or record corrected wears *Corrected Nov 3, 2026 ›* in amber (*Completed … ›* in grey when it was added to), a press that opens the newer line, lit. The newer line reads *Corrects the entry of Oct 28, 2026 ›* and opens the older one. A withdrawn link is not drawn.
- **Correct this entry…** on an entry's line, *Correct this record…* on a record's: the composer opens with that line picked (*This entry corrects the entry of …*, the first words beside it), the pick switched between *corrects* and *completes* or left out with ✕, more than one line picked in turn. **Add entry** writes the entry as before, then one link a pick (`addAmendments`); a refused link leaves the entry on the line and says why.
- **Withdraw**: ✕ beside the link on the newer line asks why and stamps the row (`withdrawAmendment`); the press is dead until a reason is typed.
- **The narrative's Jump to**: a section cited by an entry corrected since shows its count in amber, its label reads *cited by 2 entries, 1 of them corrected since*, and the strip marks that entry (`citedCorrectedWords` in `hrDocCitations.ts`).
- **The read** (`src/lib/people/personAmendmentsIo.ts`): the links are a source of their own, so a read that fails takes only the chips out and every entry still draws.
- **Help**: `keep-private-employee-files` gains *Correct an entry*.
