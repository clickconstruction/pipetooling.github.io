# v2.4444 — Four more windows sit below the iPhone clock (2026-10-02)

No migration, no edge function. Left open by [v2.4397](v2.4397.md) (*Not done*: "the same shape on other surfaces … they need an audit of their own"); Robert, 2026-10-02: "help me fix the three things you noticed but left alone". This is the second.

## The audit

Every `position: fixed` layer that covers the screen, read with the TypeScript compiler (object literals with `inset: 0`, or `top: 0` and a bottom), on main 7fc87944d: **571** layers. **139** already leave room for the status bar (`env(safe-area-inset-top)` or `--app-top-chrome` in the layer's own style). Of the **432** that do not, 14 are bare click-catchers behind a menu.

The windows built the Lien desk's way (a layer from the top of the screen whose panel is the full height, or within 2rem of it) are the certain ones, and this release fixes them:

| Window | What sat under the clock |
|---|---|
| [`PersonDeskDrawer`](../../src/components/personDesk/PersonDeskDrawer.tsx) | the panel is `height: 100%`: **Close** at 16–36 px |
| [`ClockedInMapModal`](../../src/components/map/ClockedInMapModal.tsx) | a phone's sheet is `height: 100%` with no padding: its title row at 0–61 px |
| [`MissedClockInModal`](../../src/components/clock/MissedClockInModal.tsx) | a tall card reached 12 px from the top (`max-height: calc(100vh - 1.5rem …)`) |
| [`BankPaymentsModal`](../../src/components/jobs/BankPaymentsModal.tsx) | at full screen (an iPad) the padding was 0 and the card `height: 100%` |

Each layer pads its top by `var(--app-top-chrome, 0px)` (v2.4397), so the dim still covers the status bar and the panel starts below it; the two cards with a `max-height` take it off. One `padding` string per layer, never `padding` beside `paddingTop`.

## Verified

Dev server at 390 × 844, production data, read only, with the variable set to 59 px by hand and then cleared:

- Person desk (`?person=u:<id>`): panel 59–784 (was 0–784), **Close** at 75 (was 16). A hit test at y = 30 lands on the backdrop.
- Clocked-in map: sheet 59–784 (was 0–784), × at 67.
- With the variable at 0 both are where they were.
- The other two by their render tests (the padding on the layer).

## Not done — the larger class

The rest of the 432 are centred cards, and whether one reaches the status bar depends on how tall its card may grow. On a 844 px screen with a 59 px status bar, a centred card touches it once it is taller than about 86% of the screen:

- about **70** cards may grow to 88–94% of the screen (52 with a plain `88vh`, `90vh`, `92vh` or `94vh`; 18 behind a `min()` whose other limit is taller than a phone): up to 25 px of their top can sit under the clock when the content fills them;
- **6** may grow to within 2–3rem of the full height (`calc(100vh - 2rem)`, `calc(100vh - 3rem)`), as the Lien desk did;
- about **95** stop at 86% or less, or are capped shorter than a phone (`85vh` 45, `80vh` 20, the `min(…, 640px)` kind), and are clear;
- **185** set no height on their first child (most are short confirm windows; some set it deeper), and were not read one by one;
- **59** are not centred at all (sheets from the bottom, drawers, top-anchored cards with their own padding) and need a look each.

That is a sweep of its own, by script, with a CI check behind it so a new window cannot bring the fault back — and it wants one look on a real iPhone first, to see how many of the 88–94% cards really fill.
