# v2.4334 — Release of Lien: the amount shows its commas as you type (2026-10-01)

No migration, no edge function. Grace (2026-10-01), on step 3 of the six-step window ([v2.4314](v2.4314.md)): have the commas appear in the Amount box on their own, intuitively, without getting in the way of entering values.

## What changed

- **Kernel** [`moneyTyping.ts`](../../src/lib/moneyTyping.ts) (16 tests): `moneyTypingSanitize` keeps digits and the first dot (two decimals at most, leading zeros dropped, one zero kept before a dot) and counts the kept characters before the cursor; `moneyTypingGroup` puts commas in the whole-dollar part; `moneyTypingCaret` puts the cursor back after the same count of kept characters; `moneyTypingEdit` is the three in one; `moneyTypingDeleteAcrossComma` makes Backspace just after a comma (Delete just before one) remove the digit beside it instead of a comma that would only come back; `moneyTypingSettle` shows cents on leaving ("9000" → "9000.00", "" stays "").
- **The box** [`MoneyTypingInput`](../../src/components/MoneyTypingInput.tsx) (6 render tests): a string in and out, always plain ("17777.51") — the waiver's fields, autosave, math and documents see exactly what they saw before; only the screen has commas. The cursor is restored in a layout effect after the parent re-renders; an edit that keeps nothing new (a letter, a second dot) rewrites the text in place with the cursor where it was. Settling to cents happens only after typing in the box, so tabbing through a saved draft never re-saves it. `inputMode="decimal"`.
- **The window** ([`LienReleaseModal`](../../src/components/jobs/LienReleaseModal.tsx)) step 3 uses it; tabular figures. The window's render tests now read the box as "17,777.51".
- Guide *send a GC our lien waiver* (*Why the amount is less than the bill*) says so.

## Why not the existing money box

`MoneyDecimalAmountInput` (payment lines, fixtures, segments, rough parts) shows commas only once you leave the box and carries a number. Grace asked for commas that appear as you type; the new box is for that, and the other boxes are unchanged.
