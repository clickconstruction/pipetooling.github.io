# v2.8042 — Job updates: the Reports and Billing pills show only to people who can read them (2026-11-20)

The owner's phone walk of 2026-11-20, on the Job Mode card as a field hand: *some users don't have access to billing and reports for jobs, as a result I would like to not show them in this title*. The row of filter pills over a job's updates drew all six for every viewer, so a helper saw **Reports** and **Billing** as two dim words that could never hold a line for them, a third of the row. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## The rule

A pill is drawn when this viewer reads that kind of line on this job. **All**, **Notes**, **Status**, **Crew** and **Customer** are unchanged. **Reports** and **Billing** ask:

- **The office** (dev, owner, assistant, controller, `isOfficeRole`, the client's copy of `is_office_staff()`): both, on every job, with no read. The office passes the money half of `can_read_job_activity(job, true)` and the first leg of `list_reports_for_job_ledger` everywhere.
- **A primary**: both are asked of the database, once per job. Billing is `can_read_job_activity(job, true)`, narrowed on 2026-11-19 to the job's people. Reports is `primary_can_read_job_reports(job)`, the account manager or the listed superintendent.
- **A superintendent**: Reports is asked, `superintendent_report_job_anchor_allowed(job)`. Billing is never theirs: the money half's role list leaves them out.
- **A helper, a subcontractor, an estimator**: neither on the role. A helper and a subcontractor read their own reports only, an estimator none, and none of them the money half.

One exception keeps the row honest: a gated pill is also drawn when the feed the database handed over holds such a line. A helper who left a report on the job gets **Reports 1**. So a pill is never hidden over a line the person can read under **All**, and a person with access and nothing yet keeps the dim pill as before.

A chosen filter whose pill is not drawn reads as **All**.

## What changed

- [`jobActivityFilter.ts`](../../src/lib/jobActivityFilter.ts): `visibleActivityFilters(filters, access, counts)` and `resolveActivityFilter(selected, visible)`, with `ActivityFilterAccess`.
- [`jobActivityChipAccess.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobActivityChipAccess.ts), new: `activityFilterAccessForRole`, `activityChipQuestions` (which gate a role asks, by name) and `settleActivityFilterAccess`. No role list of its own: the office predicate, and the database's gates for the two roles whose answer turns on the job.
- [`useJobActivityFilterAccess.ts`](../../src/hooks/useJobActivityFilterAccess.ts), new: asks the questions once per person, role and job, and keeps a whole answer for the visit. A question that fails opens nothing and is asked again on the next open.
- The three screens that draw the pills read the one kernel: [`JobThreadNotesPanel.tsx`](../../src/components/JobThreadNotesPanel.tsx) (the Job Mode card's Job updates, the job window, Quickfill), [`JobActivityView.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobActivityView.tsx) (Job activity on the Pipeline, compact, floating and full screen) and [`CustomerSummaryModal.tsx`](../../src/components/dispatchMode/CustomerSummaryModal.tsx) (a customer's summary in Dispatch, where many jobs feed one list, so the role alone answers and the count does the rest).
- **＋ Report** on the job window rode on the Reports pill. A helper can leave a report there, so the door stays in the pill's place when the pill is not drawn.

## Checked: are the lines themselves held, or only the pills?

The lines are held by the database, and were before this change. The pills decide nothing about what is read.

- `list_job_activity_events` and the table's own select policy both ask `can_read_job_activity(job, financial)` per row, so a reader without the money half is handed no `financial` row.
- On the live trail every row of a billing kind is `financial` (read 2026-11-20: 23 kinds, 2,786 rows, none with `financial = false`).
- Read through the dev seat as a helper on a job of theirs that carries 10 money lines and another person's report: no event of a billing kind, no report, and `can_read_job_activity(job, true)` answers false.
- By design, three billing kinds can be written without the money mark, so the crew reads them: the hazmat notice emailed to the customer, and a hazmat fee's edit or delete that moved no dollar. None is on the trail today. One landing would draw the helper a **Billing** pill with its count, which is right: the line is theirs to read.
- A customer's word on paying (`payment_promise`) rides its own read, the promise readers and the job's people. A field hand is in neither.
