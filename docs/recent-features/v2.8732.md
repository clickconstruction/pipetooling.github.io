# v2.8732 — Permits: a builder's sheet says who to ask for (punch list #405) (2026-11-27)

The loose end the card names under *Left to do and to walk*: [`to-dos/permits-market-page/README.md`](../../to-dos/permits-market-page/README.md), *The Ask for block is not on the builder's sheet*. Client only, no migration. It owes nothing on prod.

## Why

Step three (v2.8118, v2.8121) drew what a one-time lookup found about a company on its row of **Call this week** alone. A builder's sheet is where a person reads one company whole, from Builders or from a name on Market, and it showed the Google press and nothing of the lookup. So a fact already looked up, and perhaps already checked, was not where the company was being read.

## What landed

- **The Ask for block on the builder's sheet** (`BuilderSheet` in `PermitBuilders.tsx`), under the header and the whole width of the sheet. It is the call list's own component, `PermitAskFor`, with nothing redrawn: the kind of company, the office phone, a person and title, each with its page and day, grey until a person checks it.
- **The sheet reads its one name's facts as it opens** (`loadBuilderLookupOf` in `permitMarketIo.ts`): the rows of `builder_lookups` under the name's key, then the presses on those rows alone. It folds them with the call list's kernel (`foldLookups`), so a later lookup replaces an earlier one and the newest press stands, as on the list.
- **Checked, Wrong, Not right and Put back** are kept through the same write (`recordLookupCheck`), with the same toast. The sheet then reads its facts again. Opened from Market, the call list behind it reads its lookups again too, so the two never disagree.
- **Who checked a fact** is named from the people the sheet already knows (`ground.people`), and *you* for the reader's own press.
- **Until the read lands the sheet shows Google the builder alone**, as it did, so it never says *Nobody looked this name up yet.* before it knows. A read that fails says *What was looked up could not be read.* A name nobody looked up says so. Before the tables are on a database the name reads as not looked up.
- The Google press moved from the header into the block, which draws it for a name with no lookup and for one with a fact missing. A name with all three facts shows no Google press, as its row on the list shows none.
- Guide: *research permits for work to bid* → Check who to ask for.

## What it does not do

- It looks nobody up. The lookups stay at San Antonio's first 25 names until the owner says otherwise.
- It changes no order, tag or count on any list.
