# v2.7478 — Lien desk: a retainage notice tells the leader (2026-11-16)

The owner, 2026-11-16, on the follow-up v2.7445 named: *build the retainage notice's message*. One edge function (`notify-lien-approval`, redeploy) and the client. No migration.

## What was wrong

A § 53.057 retainage notice goes through the same draft, approve and send steps as a monthly notice, and it had no message at any of them.

1. **The send told nobody.** The retainage pane's **Ask <leader> to approve ▸** wrote the item and said *Sent for approval.* It never called `notify-lien-approval`, so the leader learned of the paper by opening the desk, while the footer over the press read *One line on <leader>'s phone.*
2. **Remind left it out.** `lienReminderPlan` held every retainage row with the reason *is a retainage notice, which has no message yet*, and the desk built no item for one.
3. **The function refused the kind.** Since v2.7445 the plain body answered 400 *Not a lien notice* for anything but `notice_53_056`, so the wording of a monthly notice could not be put on another paper.

## What changed

- **`notify-lien-approval`**: the plain body (`item_id`, optional `due_ymd` and `remind`) takes a `retainage_53_057` item under the notice's gate: the office, not archived, the item awaiting approval and not voided. The words are `lienRetainageApprovalPush` in `_shared/lienApprovalPush.ts`: *Approve a retainage notice* over the job, the retainage and who holds it, and the mail-by day; a reminder's email says so and names who pressed. Its own tag, its own `notification_history` type (`lien_retainage_approval_ask`) and its own door, `/lien-desk?tab=retainage&job=…`. An affidavit sent through the plain body is still refused.
- **The retainage pane** (`LienDeskRetainagePane`): the send computes its outcome once. A paper that lands in *Awaiting Master approval* tells the leader through `tellLeaderOfLienApproval` with the day the window closes, and the toast is `lienNoticeToldWords`: which way it reached the leader, or in amber that it did not go, with the reason. A paper the standing rule approved or held tells nobody and says which it was; the held one used to read *Sent for approval.*
- **Remind** (`lienLeaderReminder.ts`, `LienDesk.tsx`): a retainage notice on the desk is one of the notices, one message each, sent with its own item and its own day. `LIEN_REMINDER_HOLDS.retainage` is gone; a retainage row with no item stays out as any paper with none does.
- **The press on the list by bin** (`lienNextUpByBin.ts`): a retainage notice has no bin on the Board, so its row sits under *Other lien papers*, and only a bin's head carried **Remind**. When no bin holds a waiting paper, that head carries the press now, so a retainage notice waiting alone can be reminded of. One press still reminds of every paper that waits.
- **The address opens the paper**: `/lien-desk?tab=retainage&job=<id>` now opens the Retainage list on that job, as the affidavit's address has since v2.7180. Before, it opened the list on its first row.
- **A desk ahead of the function**: `lienTellFailureReason` reads the old function's *Not a lien notice* as *The part of the app that tells the leader is waiting for its update.*
- **While you were off**: a retainage message held outside the leader's Push me hours is named *retainage notice* (`whatWaited.ts`).
