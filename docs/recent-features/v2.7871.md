# v2.7871 — Lien desk: a stop's window shows the contractor's copy on every screen (2026-11-18)

A gap named in v2.7785's fragment and picked up at the owner's word the same day. Three screens open a stop's window: the desk's notices pane, its affidavit pane and the job's Lien window. Each built the paper's proof by hand, and only the notices pane said whether a filed lien owes the original contractor a copy.

**What a reader saw.** On a job under a GC, the affidavit pane's and the Lien window's stop window drew the owner's card alone until the contractor's copy had a record, and counted the proof one short: *Mail proof: 1 of 2 on file* where the notices pane read *1 of 3*. A contractor's copy still to mail had no card there, so it had no press either (`lienMailProofDoor.ts`).

**The fix (`src/lib/jobs/lienStopProof.ts`, pure).** One reading, `lienStopProof(filing, { isSub, mailedCopies })`, called by all three. Whether the contractor gets a copy is the filing's own answer, and the job's when the paper keeps none (`lienServeHasContractor`), the rule *Filed liens: copies to mail* and the Lien window's Filings list already read. The notices pane read the job alone until now, so a lien drawn before the job's GC changed follows its paper there too.

**Also.** The affidavit pane hands the reading the notice's mailed files off its desk item, so a notice's stop opened from that pane lists each file, as it does from the notices pane. The Lien window's stop window still lists the saved copy alone: it does not hold the job's mailed files where the stop's paper is built.
