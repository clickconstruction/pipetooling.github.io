Found live on 2026-10-26: Southern Post's $13,680 check bounced off #878 on Sep 23 (the case of v2.4325's own doc comment), and the replacement arrived Oct 26 inside a $34,825 ACH covering other bills too. Taunya applied $34,200 of it to #878 at 9:51 AM, and the case still read *Waiting for the new check*, ten days past the promise, with a watch line that could never fire: the exact pick (`arReplacementFor`) needs a same-payer deposit of **the exact amount to the cent, completely untouched**, and the `replaced` close that names the new deposit was only reachable through it. ## The rule

`arCombinedReplacementFor(view, deposits, trailRowsByDeposit)` (`src/lib/jobs/arReturnCase.ts`) fires on **evidence, not size** — a big deposit alone proves nothing, payers send big checks monthly:

- a *bank* or *hand* case still waiting for the new check (`next.kind === 'new_check'`);
- a same-payer deposit (`_ar_payer_key`) posted on or after the bounce, not itself returned;
- whose **live** payments on the check's own jobs (the bills it paid + the job it came off) reach the check's amount.

A partial make-good stays below the amount and keeps the case open, the stake box keeping the arithmetic. Oldest posted wins; the exact match, when one exists, wins over this. `arMadeGoodAfterApply(views, deposit, appliedByJob)` is the same read at the apply, over this apply's dollars alone.

## The surfacings

- **The case notices** (`ArReturnCasePane`): the exact-match banner's green box, loosened only by the rule — *The new check looks like it came inside this payment. SOUTHERN POST CO · $34,825 · Oct 26. $34,200 of it went on #878 Take 5- Seguin today, by Taunya. That covers the $13,680 that bounced.* **This payment made it good** → a read-back (what closes, nothing moves on the job, whether the $30 fee is on — it says, it never blocks) → **Close the case** runs `close_ar_return_case` with reason **`replaced`** and `p_replaced_by` the payment (the RPC took both since v2.4325; the UI never offered them off the exact match). The stale *Waiting for the new check* words and the watch line step aside while the banner is up; They said… too.
- **Caught at the apply** (`BankPaymentsModal.submitApply`): when the apply's own allocations cover a waiting same-payer check, a card offers *This payment covers Southern Post's $13,680 check that came back* — one press closes the case, **Leave it open** dismisses. The modal keeps each deposit's raw trail rows (`trailRowsById`, from the `list_ar_deposit_trails` read it already makes) so the pane's rule reads them with no new RPC; a waiting case also turns on the hidden All-rows fetch, because a fully-applied payment has left To match.
- **The watch tells the truth** (bank and hand cases): *When Southern Post's money lands, it shows here to pick. The new check can come alone or inside a bigger payment on the job.* A rejected check keeps the exact-pick sentence — it is deposited again as itself.

No migration, no edge function. Guide *match bank deposits to bills* gains the bullet **The new check comes inside a bigger payment**; the Glossary's *Returned deposit* entry carries the close.
