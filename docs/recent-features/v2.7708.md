# v2.7708 — Lien desk: every press on the Board stands beside the name of the bin it empties (2026-11-17)

Punch list #383, option B, PR 1 of 3. The owner boxed the band between the Board's four tiles and its two columns of bins: *"I don't super love this section. It seems to draw away from the flow chart. And I can't think of any situation where someone would click only my bins instead of every bin."* Three whole-page options were drawn and measured (`to-dos/lien-desk-to-do-layout/`). His pick: *"Build B but with the action button in the same line as the title of the card it is in instead of at the bottom."* Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## Before

- Two rows stood between the tiles and the chart, 126 px of a 900 px screen. The first was the sentence *Every bin.* with the switch **Only my bins** a page width away. The second was the two batch prints, one over each column, 561 px apart at 1,440 wide.
- **Only my bins** showed a dev two empty bins. It was a master's default view, and it hid the approved notices that wait on his signature, so his Board could read *Nothing waits on you* under a tile that read *7 to sign*.
- The third tile only read while the notices' print stood under it.
- The one press that already stood in a bin, *Mail N liens' copies ›*, stood under the bin's chips.

## What changed

- **Nothing stands between the tiles and the bins.** The first bin's top edge moved from 404 px to 279 px down the window, measured on the real Board with made-up jobs.
- **A press stands on its bin's title line**, right of the bin's name and count. `Bin` in `LienBoard.tsx` draws a `LienBoardBinPress`: filled blue for the bin's main act, an outline for a door to a window, grey when dead. Where a narrow column has no room, the press wraps under the name and the name is never cut.
- **The two prints moved into their bins.** `LienBoardPrintSide` gained `bin`: *Print and mail N notices* stands on **Approved · to print**, *Print N for notarization* on **Ready to file**, and once every notice has printed the notices' press stands on **Approved · to mail** as *Enter mail numbers · N*. What a print leaves out is a line under the bin's name, over the chips, with the same doors: *See them ›*, *Remind … ›*, *Change ›*.
- **Approved · to mail has the run's door while notices still wait to print** (`lienBoardMailNumbersPress`): *Enter mail numbers · N*, an outline, opening the same run.
- **The leader's own view is retired.** `boardMine`, its switch and the `leader` prop are gone. Every viewer reads every bin. `lienBoardLeaderBinPress` words his press, *Approve N affidavits*, for the title line of his **Awaiting Master approval** bin of affidavits. It counts the jobs he leads, so a dev who leads none sees no press. A late affidavit stays out, and one line under the bin's name says so.
- **A master's line carries no reminder.** He now reads the bin where the office's *Remind* stands, and the notice is his own to sign.
- **The third tile opens the run again** on a computer.
- **A phone** keeps its bins folded, so the two prints stand side by side over the fold in their short words. An opened bin draws a print's lines without the press a second time.

## What it does not do

- The notices to print still read three numbers: 14 on the third tile, 8 on the bin, 1 on the press. The line under the bin's name says why, *7 wait on the leader's signature*. PR 2 gives signing its own bin, so the bin's count and its press are one number.
- The busiest bin still has the blue border. PR 3 makes the blue edge mean a bin with something to do now, folds the empty bins and the Public jobs box, and moves Held and Missed to the heading line.
