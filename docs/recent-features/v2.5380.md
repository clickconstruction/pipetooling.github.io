# v2.5380 — Messages: turn on reading the bids inbox from Settings (2026-10-12)

Cut 6's follow-up (the lead, after cut 2's PR D, v2.5311): the switch for the `gmail-sync` reader (v2.5354) on
Settings → Phone and texts, so the owner turns the reader on after the live email test. No migration and no edge
function: the switch's row and its UPDATE policy came with v2.5350 (`20261013080000_messages_email_in.sql`). Guide
`turn-on-reading-the-bids-inbox`.

- **Read bids@** (`EmailReaderSettingsCard.tsx`, under **Who gets a new message**): for the owner and a dev
  (`canSwitchReader`, the client's copy of `master_or_dev_update_gmail_sync_on`'s `is_master_or_dev()`), the rest of
  the office sees no card. A switch (`role="switch"`) sets `app_settings.gmail_sync_on_v1` to `'true'` or `'false'`
  (`emailReaderIo.ts`). Turning it on asks first (`READER_CONFIRM`), since it starts reading the live mailbox; turning it
  off does not. A refused change keeps the switch and says why (`READER_NOT_SAVED`).
- **How it is doing** (`readerHealthWords` in `emailReader.ts`): from `gmail_sync_state`'s `last_ok_at` and `last_error`,
  *Last read today at 7:12 PM. Nothing wrong.*, the error's own words after *The last run stopped:*, or *Not read yet.*
  The time is Chicago's, as a thread line's (`lineWhen`).
- `readerIsOn` reads only `'true'`, the word the cron's statement posts on. The key is `APP_SETTINGS_KEY_GMAIL_SYNC_ON_V1`.
  `gmail_sync_state` is read through `as never` until the generated types carry it.
- **Tests**: `emailReader.test.ts` and `EmailReaderSettingsCard.render.test.tsx` over a fake io.
