# v2.8131 — Document builder: a reminder goes to the person the sheet was sent to, and says who (2026-11-20)

Punch list #404, left by the review of 2026-11-20. No migration. One function changed: `document-builder-send`, which takes a deploy.

## What was wrong

On a published sheet's **Send the sheet to this job's trades**, a row that was sent to carries a second press: **Send it again**, or **Tell them it changed**. It found who to email by the name on the send, `party.contacts.find((c) => c.name === send.contactName)`, and fell back to the trade's first contact when nobody carried the name. Two people at one trade can share a name, so the reminder could reach the other one. A person who had left the record was swapped for the first contact with no word. Nothing on the row said who the press would email.

## The fix

The send's own row already holds the address it went to (`document_builder_sends.contact_email`, written by the function since step 5). An address is what a contact is known by here: the trade's main contact is two columns on `gc_companies` with no id of its own, the box's **To** list is keyed by address, and the function finds its person by address. So no column was added.

- `sendIo.ts` reads `contact_email` onto `SendRecord.contactEmail`.
- `resendTo(party)` in `send.ts` answers who the second press goes to: the contact whose address is the one on the row. It answers nobody when that address is off the record, with the line that says the way out. A row that kept no address goes by name only when one person alone carries it. Its sentences mark their values with `val`.
- `BuilderSendList.tsx` sends to that person, draws the line under the two presses with `ValueSentence` (*It goes to Ana Pruitt at office@cedarfork.test.*) and ties it to the press with `aria-describedby`. With nobody to go to the press is dead and the line is drawn as a warning. **Take it off their page** stays live, since that is the way out.
- `document-builder-send` holds the same rule for a caller that does not: `resendGoesToWhoItWentTo` (`_shared/documentBuilderSend.ts`). A send with `why` of `again` or `changed` whose address is not the live row's is refused, `notWhoItWentTo` (409), before any page is made or email goes. The live row is now read before the email for that. The answer's `send` gains `contactEmail`.

## Deploy

The client works against the function as deployed today: it reads the row itself and sends the right address. The deploy adds the refusal for an older tab that still picks by name. `supabase functions deploy document-builder-send` from `origin/main`, on Taunya's go by the function's name.

## Tests

- `send.test.ts`: two people of one name, and the press goes to the one the row names. The address alone for a person with no name. Nobody once that person is off the record, namesake or not. A row with no address goes by name for one person and to nobody for two or none. Every line passes the plain-words rules.
- `sendIo.test.ts`: the row's address is kept on the record, and `resendGoesToWhoItWentTo` for a reminder, a first send, no live row and a row with no address.
- `BuilderSendList.render.test.tsx`: the press sends to the namesake the row names and the line says who. With that person gone the press is dead, the line says so, nothing is sent and **Take it off their page** is live.

## Not changed

To send a reminder to someone else, take the send off their page and send again. Changing who a live send goes to in place is not built.
