# v2.7081 — Bids: the pricing email names its price column Sale Price, as the window does (2026-11-12)

Found while making the cents smaller in v2.7065, and put to Grace as a question: the window's preview headed the price column "Sale Price" and the email **Send for me** sends headed it "Unit price". Her answer, 2026-11-12: *Sale Price.*

## What changed

The pricing table has two copies of its builder, kept in sync by hand: `src/lib/buildBidPricingPackageHtml.ts` draws the window's preview and the table **Send via my mail** copies, and `supabase/functions/_shared/bidPricingPackage.ts` draws the email the edge function `send-bid-pricing-package` sends. The two had drifted on one word.

The edge copy now says **Sale Price** in both places it named the column:

- the HTML table's header cell, and
- the header line of the plain-text part of the same email.

"Sale Price" and "Unit price" are the same length, so the plain-text table's columns stay where they were.

## Left alone

Every amount, the count, the row order and the cents in smaller type (v2.7065). The text from **Copy for text** has no column names. The Pricing tab, print and CSV already said Sale Price.

## How it was checked

`buildBidPricingPackageHtml.test.ts`, three new cases that hold the two copies together from now on:

- the edge copy's table says Sale Price and never Unit price,
- the edge copy's table equals the app's table character for character on the same rows, and
- the edge copy's plain-text body equals the app's.

The second and third are stronger than the word alone. A later change made to one copy and not the other now fails CI.

## Is this the best we can do?

For the word, yes. The better end is one builder with a generated edge copy, so there is nothing to keep in sync by hand. The repo already does that for other kernels: `npm run gen:edge-kernels` writes `supabase/functions/_shared/gcKernels/` from `src`. Moving this builder there is its own change and was not asked for. The two equality tests are the fence until then.

## Deploy

`supabase functions deploy send-bid-pricing-package` from `main` after the merge. Until then the sent email keeps "Unit price". No migration.
