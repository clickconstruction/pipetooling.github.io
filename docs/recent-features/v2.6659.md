# v2.6659 — The bio line in Messages wears a small bio mark (2026-11-07)

The follow-on the tech's bio left open (punch list #114, v2.5590's fragment; the card for this piece is punch list #282, `to-dos/thread-bio-mark/README.md`, written and built the same morning the walk closed #114). The bio goes as the second text after a tech's first On my way to a customer, and in the thread it read as a plain outgoing text from the tech: a four-sentence paragraph with nothing saying the app sent it. The row has carried the tech it introduced since v2.5590 (`messages.introduces_user_id`); the thread now reads it and draws it.

## What changed

- `ThreadMessage` carries `introducesUserId`; `messagesIo`'s column list reads `introduces_user_id`, and `load.ts` maps it. A read that leaves the column out draws nothing, as with `delivery`.
- `bioMark` (`src/lib/messages/thread.ts`): the word *bio* and the hover words, naming the tech from the thread's roster names when it knows them, *the tech's bio* otherwise; null on every other line.
- `ThreadLine` draws the mark in the meta row beside who, a small blue-bordered chip (`data-thread-bio=<user id>`), on the bio line only. The On my way line before it is untouched.

## Why

The office answers threads, not the tech. A paragraph about the tech in the tech's own voice, with no mark, invites a reply to it, a second intro, or a question about why he wrote his resume to a customer. The mark answers all three at a glance, and the hover says whose bio it is when the thread's who is a first name the office shares.

## Not done

The thread's search and the Follow up rows do not read the mark; nothing filters by it. The quo-send ordering gap v2.6640 named (it sends through Quo before it writes the thread, so a failed write reports a sent text as not gone) is still open and is not this card's.
