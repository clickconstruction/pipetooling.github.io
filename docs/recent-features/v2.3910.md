# v2.3910 — Bids: the Bid Date Sent rules have tests (2026-09-27)

Client only; no migration, no edge function, no screen change. Punch list **#46** row 5 (`to-dos/decomposition-queue.md`): the Bids map's step 2, the attestation rules (`docs/BIDS_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md` → Recommended extraction order). Stage A — pure logic to `src/lib` with tests, nothing moved.

## What is new

`src/lib/bids/bidDateSentAttestation.ts` (34 tests) — the rules that decide whether `bids.bid_date_sent` and its eight attestation stamps are written. They had no test before:

- **`bidDateSentAttestationMerge`** — the columns a save spreads over the bid payload: all eight nulls when the date is cleared, the confirmed stamps when the date changed and the checklist was confirmed for that exact date, nothing otherwise.
- **`bidDateSentAttestationSaveError`** — the sentence that refuses a save when the date differs from the saved one and nobody confirmed it. A test walks every combination and pins that a save that passes with a changed date always carries stamps.
- **`bidDateSentAttestationPromptDate`** — when the checklist opens (not while it is open, not for an empty field, the saved date, or a date already confirmed).
- **`bidDateSentInputDropsPending`** — typing in the field: a confirmed checklist is dropped when the field is emptied, goes back to the saved date, or moves to a third date.
- **`buildBidDateSentAttestationPayload`** — the eight stamps; a line ticked earlier keeps its own time.
- **`BID_DATE_SENT_ATTESTATION_NULLS`** — moved from the page's module scope, keyed off `BidDateSentAttestationPayload`.

## What changed in `src/pages/Bids.tsx`

`getBidDateSentAttestationPayloadMerge`, `validateBidDateSentAttestationForSave`, `promptBidDateSentAttestationIfNeeded`, `handleBidDateSentInputChange` and `confirmBidSentAttestationModal` keep their names and their callers and read the kernel. 5,049 → 5,000 lines. Behavior-preserving.

## Left, on purpose
