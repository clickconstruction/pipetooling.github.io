# v2.3261 — Overhead pool and day ledger read recorded time (2026-09-11)

Client only; no migration. Owner decision after the overhead-allocation train (v2.3258–v2.3260): "switch to all recorded time but let's not include rejected time."

## The rule

`isRecordedClockSession(s)` in [`lib/overheadDailyLabor.ts`](../../src/lib/overheadDailyLabor.ts): a session counts once it is **closed** (`clocked_out_at`) and **not rejected or revoked** — approved or still awaiting approval. Revoked is excluded with rejected: it is an approval withdrawn, and v2.3179's `people_hours_recorded` view excludes it the same way. Payroll (`people_hours`, pay stubs, Draft Payroll, the Moneyfill close, the unallocated-time queue) does not read this and stays approved-only.

## What changed

- **Builders** `buildOverheadDailyLabor` (office + bid pool labor) and `buildOtherJobsLaborByDay` (field hours and labor $) use the rule instead of `approved_at != null`. Every pool reader flips with them: People → Overhead's day table, 90-day averages, the three lenses and their modal series, the pool trend card, the people table, the Review tab's 90-day Methods table, the Dashboard overhead card (`overheadPoolSnapshot`), and the Job Summary day ledger (Hours · days, overhead allocation, Days, Timeline worked spans, Capacity, Burn).
- **`overheadPoolSnapshot`**: the bid-and-job overlap count uses the rule; `pendingFieldHours` keeps its meaning (closed, awaiting approval) but is now informational.
- **`loadJobDayLedger`**: prior-hours query no longer requires `approved_at`; the pending count stays (the chip's copy changed).
- **Copy**: Job Summary's pending chip — "⏳ N sessions await approval · H h counted as recorded time — reject in People → Hours to remove"; Field hours tile "recorded, in the window"; the math footnote; Days / Timeline / Capacity labels; People → Overhead's Pending approvals card (hint + rule), the unpriced-hours rule, three breakdown-modal captions, the office-job explainer, the lens modal (denominator rule, the "approve pending time" levers became "clock every field hour", the what-ifs now describe what a rejection would do), the people table's empty state.
- **Tests**: `overheadDailyLabor.test.ts` — rejected and revoked skip, a closed pending session counts, an open session doesn't; `loadJobDayLedger.test.ts` — the prior-hours query shape.
