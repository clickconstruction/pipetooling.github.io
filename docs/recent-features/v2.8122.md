# v2.8122 — Job pace: the card shows your next stop when you are clocked in with no job (2026-11-20)

Punch list #406, from its walk as a real superintendent. Client only. No migration and no edge function.

## What the walk found

The owner asked for the Job pace card to be walked as the company's superintendent, on his phone. Through View as at a phone's width, his Job Mode page showed no Job pace card at all. He was clocked in with no job linked, and the page read *Clocked in · No job or bid linked yet* over his five stops.

The card was drawn only for the job the Job Mode card shows details for: the job he is clocked on, or his next job before he clocks in. A clock with no job has neither. So the card went away for the whole of that clock, with his first stop still ahead of him. The owner's word: "Yes, build it".

## What landed

- **The rule** is `paceNextStopJobId` in `src/lib/jobMode/paceNextStop.ts`, pure and tested. When the Job Mode card has no job of its own and the day still has a stop not yet visited, the pace card draws for that stop. That covers a clock with no job linked and a clock on a bid.
- **The card says whose pace it is.** `SuperintendentCard` takes `nextStop`, and the face reads one line under its name, *Next stop: PLUM 1044 · Maple Court*. On the short page the folded row's second line says the same, then *Tap for the pace card.*
- **Only the pace card draws.** The job's details stay off until he is on the job, as before.
- **It never stands in for a job he is on.** While the job he is clocked on is still loading, nothing draws, and that job's own card follows.
- **A job whose papers still close it to him** draws nothing, as on the rest of the card.
- `DashboardJobModeCard` asks the rule where it used to draw nothing. Other roles are unchanged: the pace card is the superintendent's alone.
- The guide `job-mode-clocking` gains one line.

## Checked

- `paceNextStop.test.ts`: the next stop, a job of its own, a job still loading, no stop left, closed papers, and every other role.
- `DashboardJobModeCard.paceNextStop.render.test.tsx`: clocked in with no job linked, the pace card draws for the next stop and names it, with no job details. Clocked onto the job, the job's own card with no *Next stop* line. Another role sees none.
- `SuperintendentCard.render.test.tsx`: the line on the face and on the folded row, and no line on a job's own card.
- Walked read-only as the same superintendent at a phone's width, on the dev server against live data, while he was still clocked in with no job linked. His page read **Job pace**, *Next stop* with his first stop's number and name, then the card's reading for that job. Nothing ran off the side. Nothing that writes was pressed.
