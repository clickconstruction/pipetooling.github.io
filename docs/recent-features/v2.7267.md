# v2.7267 — Materials on site: a delivery refused part way is safe to send again (2026-11-14)

Client only. No migration, no edge function. From the read of Materials on site on 2026-11-14 (the briefing session's note): `onSiteWritesIo` lands a press's rows one at a time with no transaction, so a delivery refused part way left its first lines landed, and the Deliveries panel's second press minted fresh ids and landed those lines again. v2.7250's fragment named it under *Not done*.

## The pick

Keep the press's ids across the resend, and answer a delivery line by line. The alternative, one RPC that lands the lines in a transaction, goes against the map's rule that there is no RPC for a press and the tables' own rules judge every row; it is the road not taken. With ids kept, the tables already do the work: an insert that meets its own row is a landing (`insertAlreadyLanded`, v2.7173), so the same rows sent again count once whatever happened to the first answer.

## What changed

- **`onSiteWrites.ts`**: `OnSiteLanding` (`rows`, `landed` ids, `refused` with each row's reason and code), `landedWhole`, and `onSiteLandingWords`, the queue's sentence for a press not all landed (*2 of 3 landed. 1 was refused: …*, the reason once when every refused row gives the same).
- **`onSiteWritesIo.ts`**: `landOnSiteWrites(raw, { rowsStandAlone })` returns the landing. Without the word a press stops at its first refusal as before (a new box, then the part put in it). With it, a refused row is kept with its reason and the rows after it still go; a lost signal still stops the press, so the rest wait for the next try under the same ids.
- **`outbox.ts`**: `OnSitePressPayload.rowsStandAlone`, and `OutboxRefusal.rows` for the screen that pressed (the kept copy holds the message and the code alone). **`landFieldPress.ts`**: `landOnSitePress` passes the word and turns a landing with a refused row into a refusal that names what landed.
- **`onSiteSend.ts`**: `OnSitePressArgs.rowsStandAlone`; a refused outcome carries `rows`. **`onSiteStore.ts`**: `commitAnswered` takes the word; the store's *did not save* toast is for a press refused whole, since a line-by-line answer is said by the screen that has the lines' names. `addDelivery` sends its lines standing alone and takes each line's own `id`.
- **`JobsMaterialsTab.tsx`**: each line on the table carries the id its row lands under, minted when the lines are read (and on *Add it as a line*), so an edit keeps it and a line sent again is the same row. After a line-by-line answer the landed lines leave the table with their prices kept, each refused line stays with *Did not save: reason* under its name, and a red note says what landed and what the next press sends (`deliveryPartWords`). The *landed before* note and its window skip the rows this press's own landing put on the job.
- **`deliveryWords.ts`**: `deliveryPartWords` and `refusedLineWords`. **`manifest.ts`**: `landableLines` keeps the line's own type, so the id rides through.
- The map's *The writes and their rules* sentence on a refused press, and the office bullet, are amended. The site guide's *Land a delivery* says what happens to a line that could not be saved.

## Tests

`onSiteWritesIo.test.ts`: a refused row kept and the rows after it sent; the same press again with the bad row mended lands every row once; a lost signal still stops the press; without the word the first refusal still stops it. `onSiteStore.write.test.ts`: a delivery refused part way lands the lines before and after, the answer names them, the store says nothing, and the same press again lands every line once (six inserts, three rows). `landFieldPress.test.ts`: a landing with a refused row is a refusal with `rows`. `onSiteWrites.test.ts` and `deliveryWords.test.ts`: every sentence. `JobsMaterialsTab.deliveries.render.test.tsx`: the panel keeps the refused line with its reason, drops the landed ones, asks nothing about the rows it landed, and the second press sends the mended line alone under the id it had.

## Not walked

The new words draw only when the office refuses a line, which a ZZ Test job on prod does not do on its own, and the review's rule is that nothing is written to prod. The stand-in tables prove the line-by-line answer and the resend end to end.

## Left

- A delivery that waits in the outbox and is refused part way on the replay is listed with the queue's sentence; the lines' names are not on that list, since the panel is gone by then.
- The guard still reads the invoice's number. A delivery landed with no number is not known again.
