# v2.5085 — GC mode: record inspections and the job's own work on the schedule (2026-10-09)

PR 9a of the schedule's real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/schedule-pr9.md` on branch `spike/gc-mode`, the first of PR 9's four cuts). A dev's until the schedule's PR 10. No migration and no edge function: every write is PR 5's and 6b's.

## The change

- **An inspection** ([`GcScheduleCards.tsx`](../../src/components/gc/GcScheduleCards.tsx), `GcInspectionCheck`): an inspection not passed yet, on a job being built, gets **It passed today** and **It failed** at the foot of its form (the form's new `check` slot). A pass is a record (`passScheduleInspection`); a date to meet of the same name is met that day. A failure takes what failed, whose work it was and a re-inspection day after today, and is a plan write (`failScheduleInspection`) with the version read and the reducer's line in the log.
- **The known difference: a failed inspection pushes with main's `pushAfter`** ([`scheduleWindow.ts`](../../src/lib/gc/schedule/scheduleWindow.ts), `failInspectionPress`). What waits on the inspection moves out, down the line, each gap kept and done work left alone, as a move pushes. The prototype's reducer pushes with New project's `pushSchedule`, over the whole plan, a wait's gap left out and done work moved: on a real job it would undo days got back and move finished bars. The lead's pick; the spike's reducer follows in a later follow-up.
- **The job's own work** (G-38): **Add an activity** under the chart (`GcAddOwnWork`) puts a bar that is no trade's line on the chart, with what it waits on and what it holds up, and what that pushes (`ownWorkPress`, the reducer's `addScheduleActivity`; a plan write through `addScheduleActivity`). Its form's buttons (`GcOwnWorkButtons`, the new `extra` slot) mark it done today or not done after all (a record, `setOwnWorkDone`) and take it off (a plan write, `removeScheduleActivity`).
- **The dates to meet** (`GcMilestones`): add, move and take off a milestone with its day and its trade, each a record (`setScheduleMilestone`, `removeScheduleMilestone`). Their dates from a file (G-145) stay PR 12's.
- **A plan write someone else beat** shows 8a's refusal (`GcScheduleRefusal`), keeps what the person typed, and reads the schedule again. Every press shows its own failure in its words.
- Ported from the prototype's `GcBuildingSchedule.tsx` (`InspectionCheck`, `InspectionFailForm`, `AddActivityCard`, `MilestonesCard`, `MilestoneLine`, `TradePick` and the opened activity's own buttons), their words kept: each press is a callback where the prototype dispatched to its reducer.
