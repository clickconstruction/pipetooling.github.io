# v2.6120 — Green words read in dark mode across the app (2026-10-30)

Punch list #194's sweep, the first of the two mechanical sweeps its card named ([`to-dos/experiences-lens/`](../../to-dos/experiences-lens/README.md)). `#15803d` is green-700's light value. Written as a literal text colour it stays dark in dark mode: 2.93:1 on the surface `#1f2937`, 2.85:1 on `--bg-green-tint`, 2.06:1 on `--bg-muted` and 1.82:1 on `--bg-green-100`. v2.6075 and v2.6116 moved the green words the lens saw. 54 more lines on main still wrote the literal as a text colour, most in states the lens's sample data does not draw: a profit, a check that passed, a count that is clear.

## What changed

- **The codemod's text map** (`scripts/theme-tokenize.mjs`) maps `'#15803d'` to `var(--text-green-700)`, so CI's `--check` refuses the literal as a `color:` from now on. The token is the same `#15803d` in light, so light mode is unchanged, and `#4ade80` in dark (8.42:1 on the surface).
- **The run**: 52 rewrites in 36 files, both sides of a same-line ternary included. Three were lines that already read `var(--text-green-700, #15803d)`, whose fallback the run turned into the token itself; they read `var(--text-green-700)` now.
- **By hand**, the five the codemod cannot reach: the nested ternaries in `PlugInQuotesModal.tsx`, `JobsWeeklyMoneyModal.tsx` and `BidsPricingTab.tsx` (the robot chip's green tone, over several lines), the link check's message colour in `DocumentsAddDriveLinkModal.tsx`, and the Lien desk calendar's count beside a green flag (`GREEN_WORDS` beside `GREEN_INK` in `LienDeskCalendarTab.tsx`).
- **The fills keep `#15803d`** under white words, 5.02:1 in both themes, where white on `#4ade80` would read 1.74:1: Call, the action-phase press, the thread notes' press, the Lien desk's ✓ circles and the rest. So do the borders, chart bars, swatches and map markers.
- **The pin**: `readablePairs.test.ts` holds the token at 4.5:1 in both themes on `--bg-green-tint`, `--bg-subtle` and `--bg-muted`, the grounds the sweep put words on (on the surface since v2.6075, on `--bg-green-100` since v2.6116). It walks `src/` for `'#15803d'` written as a `color:`, with one helper shared with v2.6116's walk for `#0f7a52`.

## Left as it was

- **The training figures' SVG inks**: `plumbingTokens.ts`'s `RIGHT` (`#15803d`) and `WRONG` (`#b91c1c`) and the figures' `<text fill="#15803d">` labels. An SVG `fill` is not a `color:`, so neither the codemod nor the lens reads it, and the figures' red needs the same pass. v2.6133 and v2.6135 moved them onto the theme's tokens, and v2.6137 taught the lens to read SVG text.
- **Bridge's sales bars**: their dollar labels draw in `var(--surface)` on the literal fills, so in dark they read 2.93:1 on the green. The desk opens only on a press, so the lens's frames never drew it. v2.6133 gave each segment's dollars an ink that reads on its fill (`SALES_BAR_INKS`).
- **Light mode on `--bg-page`**: green-700 reads 4.47:1 there, on the crew P&L's zebra rows. The sweep leaves light mode as it was, and that is above the lens's 3:1.

On main they read 2.93:1, and the lens never saw it: `auditFrame` skipped text of one character, so a lone `0` or `✓` was not measured until v2.6137.
