# v2.7222 — Lien desk on a phone: rows by the day they are due, with the money (2026-11-13)

Punch list #352, PR 3 of eight, on the owner's pick of direction A, the one list. It builds on PR 0, PR 1 and PR 2.

## What was wrong

A row of the first view on a phone was a card about 225 px tall: a chip and a date, the job, four named step tiles, a line of words and a full-width button. Three fit on a screen with nothing else on it. A row carried no amount, though the affidavit list did, and that list is the one the owner's recording went to. The rows sat in *Needs you now* and *Coming up*, which split at seven days and say nothing of which day.

## The change

- **Groups by the day.** `src/lib/jobs/lienPhoneRows.ts` (new kernel), `lienPhoneGroups`: the two day-driven groups are pooled and split by the day each row is due, soonest first, at the place *Needs you now* stood. A head reads *Due Mon Nov 16 · 22*, then how long is left, then the money. It is red inside three days or late, amber inside a week. Rows with no day of their own come last under *No day set*. The groups that say whose move it is (only you can sign, the pen and code group, only you can approve, the reminders, waiting on the leader) pass through with their place and words, and so do a pressed step's two groups: who must act is not a date.
- **The money.** `lienPhoneAmounts` reads each row's open balance from the entry of its own kind (the notice's, the affidavit's, the retainage's), which is the figure each of those lists already draws. A head sums its jobs once, so a job with a notice and an affidavit due the same day is one debt. A job with nothing open draws no amount. A run row stands for many jobs and has none.
- **The row.** Three lines, `data-layout="lines"`: the job (still the door to the job) and what it owes; where the paper stands, with the row's own day only under a head that is not a day; then the paper's chip, the four dots and the press. The dots are `LienStepMark` with `compact`, so the fraction gives its room to the press; a tap opens the step card as a sheet, as a tap on the tiles did. The press keeps its exact words (*File the affidavit*) and is as wide as they are, 40 px tall. With no press for this viewer, `lienPhoneStepWords` says the step and whose it is beside the dots (*Approve · leader*).
- Group heads stick to the top of the list while their rows scroll under them.

The four named tiles of v2.4881 are gone from the list. They were there because a phone has no hover; the dots open the same card on a tap, and the tile that mattered, the one saying whose step it is, is now the press itself or the words beside the dots.

The card drew a row of 69 px in two lines with a short press (*File*). The built row is three lines and 112 px, because the press keeps its own name and the line that says why a row is held is kept whole. The chips over the list wait for PR 5, where they take the place of the view tabs: here they would have been a third way to narrow one list.

No migration, no edge function, no change to what any press writes or who may press it.
