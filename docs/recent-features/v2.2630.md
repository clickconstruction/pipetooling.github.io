# v2.2630 — RFQ Phase 1b: plug in quotes + compare (2026-09-01)

Phase 1b of `docs/SUPPLY_HOUSE_RFQ_PLAN.md` — the screens over the v2.2629
store. Cost-side surfaces, gated `canPackageAndSendBidPricing` like the rest
of the share group (matching the store's RLS roles).

- **`PlugInQuotesModal`** (Share ▾ → "Plug in a quote", nested under
  Supply house list): header picks the supply house + rep + good-until +
  freight; left pane takes the raw pasted reply and "Match to fixtures" runs
  `parseVendorReply` — one editable line per proposed match (✓ exact /
  ? confirm, matched-from provenance, can't-supply, outlier flag), fuzzy
  synonym hits fan across sibling fixtures (WC-1…4 for "wc carriers") for
  the human to trim; unmatched chatter lands in an amber strip. Lines can
  also be added by hand (fixture SearchableSelect at `MODAL_Z + 10` +
  `fillViewportHeight`). Save inserts `bid_quotes` (+`raw_paste`
  provenance, `source` pasted/typed) + `bid_quote_lines`, upserts the
  name-keyed `supply_house_fixture_prices` memory, then opens compare.
- **`QuoteCompareModal`** (the "Quotes (n)" chip that appears beside Share
  once a quote exists, count via head-count query refreshed on save):
  renders `buildQuoteComparison` — rows grouped by Division 22 section
  (rules fetched once, `classifySpecSection`), one column per house
  (latest quote wins), best live price starred, expired quotes gray +
  line-through and excluded from best/star, per-house coverage chips with
  apples-to-apples common-line totals, Last-quoted column from the price
  memory. Tapping a price toggles `bid_quote_lines.picked` (one house per
  part) and the picked total recomputes at CURRENT counts.
- Live-tested on BP339: 10-line paste → 7 exact + fuzzy fan-out + 1
  unassigned; save → compare → pick ($580 = 5 × $116) → expiry graying
  verified by backdating `valid_until`.
