# v2.7079 — Materials: By part on the printed report and on a job (2026-11-12)

Punch list #330, PR 5 of 5, the last. The site's screen reads every part across a site (v2.7074), and this PR takes that reading to the three other places a person looks: the paper, one job's page and the search box at the top of the app.

## What changed

- **The paper, by part** (`siteMaterialsReport.ts`: `reportPartRows`, `partRowsWords`; `SiteMaterialsReportModal.tsx`): a third chip, **By part**, beside By job and By location. One line per part across the site's jobs with its total, its unit when every line names the same one, the other ways it was written, and the cages and boxes that hold it with each count and place. It is the same fold as the screen's: the same words by themselves (`rollKey`), plus the names a person joined on the site's screen, read through `fetchPartTies` when the window is given the site's id. A refused read leaves the same-words rows. The device remembers the pick, as it did the other two.
- **The print press on the site's screen**: **Print the material location report** links to the site's Development page with `?report=materials`, and the page opens the window by itself for the office door. The paper keeps the page's own job labels this way. It replaces *Open the Development page*; closing the window leaves you on that page.
- **One job, by part** (`JobsMaterialsTab.tsx`): **By part** beside List and Map on a job's On site section, the job's parts as the site's screen rolls them up. A job on a site reads the names joined on that site and carries a line, **Open the whole site**. A job on no site reads its own.
- **Go anywhere** (`siteDoors` in `boxSearch.ts`, `HeaderGlobalSearch.tsx`): a site that holds boxes is a row in *Boxes on site*, **Juniper Ridge · the whole site**, to the roles that get the site's screen. Two small reads for the sites, made once the box is open.

No migration.

## What it does not do

A job on no site has no screen to answer **Same part?** on. Its By part folds the same words alone until someone asks for the ask there.

## Checked

Read only on the live app with every write refused: the press on the site's screen opened the report on the Development page by itself; By part listed 99 parts and the same piece total the screen and the by-job paper read, on 6 pages where by job takes about 10; 375 wide with no sideways scroll; the job's By part listed the same rows once the boxes were read, and its line opened the site; the site's name in the search box gave the door and the door opened the screen. The first look at the job's By part caught it saying *No part is on hand* while the boxes were still loading; it now says the boxes are being read.
