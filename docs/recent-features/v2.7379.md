# v2.7379 — Box labels: what is inside a box needs a sign-in (2026-11-15)

Taunya's question of 2026-11-15, as a subcontractor: what would she not want the GC, the people who own the building or another trade to see from a scan of a box label, and should everyone sign in before seeing what is in a box. She was shown today's page beside two narrower ones and answered with a picture of her own, which is what was built. No migration. One edge function changed: `box-read`, deployed after the merge. The link `prts.co/b/<code>` and the code are untouched, so every label already printed opens as before.

## Why

From v2.6797 a signed-out scan showed the place, the newest photo, every part with its count, how fresh the count was, what ran out or could not be found, and (v2.6799) a search over every box on that site. Read as someone who does not work for the company, that is a list of what is worth taking and where it stands. It needed no visit to the site: a photo of the label opens the page from anywhere, for as long as the box is live. The counts, the shortage notes and the count dates are also the company's own business if a pay application or a backcharge is argued.

## What a signed-out scan shows now

- The label's address and *Not signed in* across the top.
- One navy plate: *Box on this site*, the name as the label prints it, the code, and *Counted and photographed by its crew.*
- A card: *This material belongs to* Click Plumbing and Electrical, with the letterhead's line, **Ask for a bid** and the office's number as a press that calls.
- Three rows: **Text the office about box G81** (*Moved, damaged or in the way.*), **Work with us** (*Plumbers and electricians wanted.*) and **Sign in to see what is inside** (*On Click's crew? Once on this phone.*).

No part, count, photo, place, one-line contents, count verdict or search. Signed in, nothing changes: the route opens the box on the On site tab under its own gate and never asks the function.

## The gate is in the function

- **`supabase/functions/box-read/index.ts`** selects `id, label, kind, retired_at, code` from `job_containers` and nothing else. It no longer calls `on_site_job_summary`, reads `job_box_photos` or `job_places`, or streams from Drive or the bucket. A request with `&find=` or `&photo=` is answered **401** `{ error: "sign_in" }` before the database client is made, whatever the code is, so the refusal says nothing about the code.
- **`_shared/boxPage.ts`** (pure, shared with the client): the payload is `{ ok, box: { code, stencil, kind, stickerNumber? }, office }`. `boxPagePlate` cuts the name to what the label prints, so `G12 COPPER 90` answers `G12`: a name from before the name rule can say what the box holds. `boxPageAsksInside` names the two refused doors. The lines, the gone lines, the photo address, the search and its parser are removed, with their tests.
- The view ledger and the guess guard are as they were: an open still lands one `public_page_views` row, and a wrong code still counts a miss.
- A blank sticker, a lost one and a retired box answer what they answered. A retired box's name is cut the same way.

## The page

- **`BoxPublicPage.tsx`** is redrawn to Taunya's picture. The three texts are `sms:` links to the one number the function answers, each opening with its own words and the box that was scanned (`boxPageBidWords`, `boxPageWorkWords`, `boxPageTextWords`), so the office can tell which site a lead was standing on. Nothing signed out writes to the app.
- **`BoxScan.tsx`** (bare `prts.co`): the *Find a part on that site* press for a phone that scanned a box that day is gone with the search, and the page keeps nothing on the phone (`boxPageMemory.ts` holds the typed code's dash and no more).
- The guide's Scan a label section, the function's section in `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`, and the sentences in `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`, `MATERIALS_ON_SITE_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `GLOSSARY.md`, `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` and the app directory say the page as it is now. Card #294 has the picks.

## The picks

- **Her picture, not one of the three shown.** The mock-up drew today's page, a page with the name and the office's presses only, and one with part names and no counts. Her picture is the first of the narrower two with her own words, **Ask for a bid** and **Work with us**.
- **Ask for a bid and Work with us are texts**, because the app has no public page for either and the picture draws a message icon on the first. They go to the number Text the office uses, the team line the Messages page reads (v2.6910).
- **The number on the page is the team line**, the one the function has answered since v2.6910. Her picture shows the letterhead's number, whose texts reach nobody in the app. One number serves the call and the three texts.
- **No signed-in answer from the function.** A full answer for any valid session would have opened every box to every account by its code, past the job-team gate the app holds. Someone signed in reads the box in the app.

## The order

The client ships first: the new page reads an answer from the function before this version as the sealed page, the lines dropped unread. `box-read` is deployed right after the merge, and until then the old function still answers the contents to anyone who asks it directly. A phone holding a build from before this version reads the new function's answer as *did not open* with Try again until it reloads.

## Who this costs

Read from the live accounts on 2026-11-15: 9 of the 10 live helpers have signed in, as have both superintendents and both master technicians. 16 of the 19 live subcontractor rows have never signed in. A sub's person with no login sees no contents until the office gets them online.
