## What was wrong

`apply_hr_entry`, `apply_person_doc` and `apply_person_report` refuse a plan that was kept for a yes and then dropped or gone stale. The refusal says why, in the words `keptPlanOffWords` builds (`supabase/functions/_shared/devMcpKeptPlans.ts`, v2.8102). It set the reason into two sentences that each assumed how the reason ended:

- **Dropped** put its own full stop after the reason. A person who ended the reason with one read it back with two: *… dropped by Dev One: It failed the list check.. A dropped plan is never applied.*
- **Stale** put none. The stale check's reasons end in a full stop today, so it read right. A reason without one would have run into the next sentence: *… went stale: The narrative was saved since Plan again, …*

## What changed

A new `keptPlanWhyTail` ends the reason once. It takes any full stops and spaces off the end of the reason and puts one full stop back, keeps a reason's own question mark or exclamation mark, and answers a bare full stop when there is no reason. Both sentences use it.

The words are otherwise the same. The two cases the old test held read as they did.
