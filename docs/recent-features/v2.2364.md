# v2.2364 — The margin slider works on bids with no-cost rows (2026-08-26)

Estimator report (Wendi): on a 39-row bid where 23 hand-priced rows carried
no Takeoffs cost ($26k revenue), the Workbench margin slider was unusable —
every solve priced fixtures below their own cost (MS-1 $590.85 → $120,
−392%) and the slider was dead from 20–60% (every position landed the same
total, pinned at the 20%-of-basis safety floor).

Root cause: `solveWorkbenchPrices` targeted a **whole-bid blended margin**
and subtracted `uncostedFixedRevenue` from the target for BOTH solve modes.
When hand-set no-cost revenue exceeds the blended target, `needed` goes
negative and every costed row floors.

- **Solver** (`src/lib/bids/pricingWorkbenchSolver.ts`): margin solves no
  longer subtract `uncostedFixedRevenue` — a target margin now applies to
  the rows being priced (costed rows, overhead pro-rata) and hand-set
  no-cost revenue stacks on top; the whole-bid blended margin lands at or
  above the slider. Target-total solves are unchanged (the typed number is
  still the whole-bid total, uncosted revenue counts toward it — BP298
  regression stays covered). Tests: stacking semantics, never-below-cost on
  the Wendi shape, slider monotonicity.
- **`BidsPricingTab.tsx`**: new landing chip under the solver strip after a
  margin solve — "56% on 12 costed rows → bid lands at $48,506 · 80%
  blended" (revenue/blended read live from preview totals; chip clears on
  apply/discard/scenario switch/hand-edit via `wbSolveLanding`). Target-total
  solves now sync the slider to the **costed-rows** margin, not blended.
  No-cost banner reworded and calmed (surface, not amber): "…keeps the
  prices you set and stacks on top of the solve." Slider title/aria updated.
- Help guide `price-a-bid-with-the-workbench.md` updated to match.

Old-view `applyMarginPricing` (per-row margins) never had the bug.
