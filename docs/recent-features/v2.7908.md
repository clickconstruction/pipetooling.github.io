# v2.7908 — Lien desk: the red line on the first tile says the way back (2026-11-19)

The owner's yes of 2026-11-19 to a leftover of the Board's last five pieces (v2.7893, v2.7896 to v2.7899). Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## Before

- The second tile reads *Show every row ›* while the list under the Board is narrowed to the leader's papers (v2.7896). The first tile's red line, a press since v2.7371 and for late papers since v2.7897, kept *Show them ›* in that state. A second press did put the list back, and the line's words still said the opposite. The two could not be made alike until both PRs were on main, since each drew the same `Tile`.

## What changed

- **The line says the way back.** While the list is narrowed to the papers a red line counts, the line ends in *Show every row ›*, the words of the press on the line under the bins, and its button carries `aria-pressed`. Pressed again, the list is whole and the line reads *Show them ›*.
- **Both lines.** It holds for *N are past their day* and for *N will not make Monday as they stand*.
- **How the Board knows.** Each line's pick names the line it came from: `lienBoardRiskPick` sets `line: 'risk'` and `lienBoardLatePick` sets `line: 'late'` on `LienBoardPick`. `lienBoardDayLinePicked` (`lienBoardDayLine.ts`) matches that to the tile's `lineDestination`, so a pressed bin and the leader's pick never read as the line's. `lienBoardDayLineDoorWords` words the door.

## What it leaves alone

- **Which papers each line shows**, and the line under the bins, are unchanged.
- **The second tile** still tells its pick by its name (`lienBoardTilePicked`).

## Tests

- `lienBoardDayLine.test.ts`: each pick names its line and a bin's and the leader's name none, the door's two wordings against the pick line's own *Show every row*, on for each line under its own pick, off with no pick, a pressed bin, the leader's pick and the other line's pick.
- `LienBoard.dayLine.render.test.tsx`: the late line reads *Show every row ›* with `aria-pressed` under its pick and *Show them ›* again under a pressed bin, and the at-risk line does the same under its own.

## Verified

- On the dev server against the live desk in headless Chrome, a computer (1440 px) and a phone (390 px), light and dark: the line pressed reads *Show every row ›*, pressed again *Show them ›*. Look only: the one press used changes the view.
