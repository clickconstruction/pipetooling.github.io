# v2.5405 — Dispatch: what slides when a tech is running late, behind the scenes (2026-10-12)

Residential customers in Messages, R8 · 3 (dispatch writes back), lifted from the Messages integrations mockups v0.2, card 3 (`to-dos/gc-mode/messages/customer-messages-integrations-mockups.html` on branch `spike/gc-mode`; the plan is `RESIDENTIAL_BUILD_PLAN.md` → *R8* beside it). A pure kernel with its test. No screen, no migration, no edge function; the board's components and `self_move_schedule_block` are untouched, and `dispatch_move_reason` stays R8's SQL.

- **`src/lib/scheduleDispatch/whatSlides.ts`** (beside the hub's other kernels):
  - `whatSlides(blocks, move, nowIso)`: a tech's blocks for the day (`job_schedule_blocks` read as minutes on one `work_date`, each with its job and customer) and the new window a Running late text gave one of them. The stops after it in the day's order each start no earlier than the one before ends, keeping their length, until the first gap that absorbs the delay (`holds`). A block on another day stays out. A slide that would end after 11:59 PM sets `pastDayEnd`, since a block cannot cross midnight. A linked crew block's slide carries `unlinks`. The reason reads *said to Dana, 9:02 AM*.
  - `moveAsk(read, tech)`: the one strip on the send. *Move the board too?*, then *Job 1312 moves from 8:00 AM to 9:30 AM.* and *Luis’s next stop slides 30 minutes.* (or every stop's slide, or *Nothing else on Luis’s day moves.*), and *Luis leaves the crew block.* when the move takes the tech out of one. **Move it** and **Leave the board**; past midnight only **Leave the board**. The card's *1312 · 8:00 → 9:30* is written as sentences, since the · fails `plainWordsFailures`.
  - `boardWrite(read, tech)`: one `self_move_schedule_block` row per block in the RPC's own generated `Args` shape, the last stop first. That RPC moves one block per call and only the tech's own. It also posts its own job thread note on a block dispatch made and takes the tech out of a linked crew block; those stay the record. So the job's event line (*Running late. Moved from 8:00 AM to 9:30 AM, said to Dana, 9:02 AM.*) and the dispatcher's note (a `dispatch_requests` title and summary) are words for R8's SQL to carry with the reason, never a second insert. Null past midnight.
- **Tests:** `whatSlides.test.ts` over Luis's morning (made-up rows: the sewer line at 8:00, the water heater at 9:30 that slides 30 minutes, the church at noon whose gap takes the rest, and a Tuesday block that stays out), a chain of two slides, a move with nothing after it, a slide past midnight, the words through `plainWordsFailures` and the write's shape. Two breaks on purpose each failed a test: the same-day filter and the last-stop-first order.
