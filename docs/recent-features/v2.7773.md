# v2.7773 — Painted maps: ＋ Place on Look it over (2026-11-18)

Punch list #393, reading A. The owner asked on 2026-11-17 to *set pins Like points of interest beyond just having a map*, with a screenshot of a walk's **Look it over**. A painted map already held places, set from three doors: the start sheet, the walk's red bar and the map's own row. Look it over, the screen in the screenshot, had none.

## What changed

- **Look it over** has **＋ Place**, between the walk's lines and **Save**. It opens the place panel that every other door opens (`PaintedPlacePanel`), over the lines that are still waiting on the phone.
- The blue dot starts in the middle of what the map shows, as the map's own **＋ Place** does. The phone is not read for it. **Put the dot where I am** on the panel reads it once.
- **Add this place** saves the place at once, the way the walk's own **＋ Place** does, and makes the map if this was its first save. Look it over comes back with the place numbered beside the press and on the map with the lines. **Save the map** then reads **Save the line**, since the map exists.
- **Cancel** goes back to Look it over with nothing sent.
- A Look it over with no line on it (the phone did not move far enough) offers the press too. A place added there opens the map itself, since nothing is left to look over.

## Words

- Under the press: *Mark a gate, a meter or where to park. A place is saved when you add it, so it stays if you throw the walk away.* (`WALK_REVIEW_PLACE_WORDS`)
- **Throw the walk away?** said *Nothing was sent anywhere, so nothing is left behind* on every map. That was already untrue after a place set during a walk, and this press makes it common. On a saved map it now reads *The lines you painted go from this phone. What is already saved on the map stays.* A map that was never saved keeps the old sentence (`walkThrowAwayWords`).
- The small print under **Save** said *Nothing has left this phone yet* on every map. On a saved map it now opens *What you painted has not left this phone yet* (`walkNothingLeftWords`). The walk on the local server showed the old sentence under a place that had just saved.

## Not changed

- A place still has no kind and no mark of its own. That is #393's reading B.
- A place is not held on the phone until Save. Its photo would have to be kept there too, and the walk's own **＋ Place** already saves at once.
