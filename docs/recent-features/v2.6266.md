# v2.6266 — My email: the kernels, the contract and the migration (2026-11-01)

Punch list #230 PR 1 (`to-dos/messages-my-email/README.md`, the plan card on PR #6681): the ground for **Messages → My email**, a tab that reads a person's own @clickplumbing.com Inbox and Sent live and puts a whole Gmail conversation on a job or a bid in one press. The owner's calls of 2026-11-01 stand as built here: a read-through that keeps nothing until a tag; the switch *Show my mail on the My email tab* on by default for every office member, off by the person's own hand on Settings → Your account; the conversation as the unit of a tag, later mail following by itself (PR 4); the Legal desk pulling Evidence (PR 5), a tag never writing to a matter; the delegation the bids@ reader holds as the key, the function reading only the signed-in person's own `users.email`. Nothing shows yet: the edge function `gmail-mine` is PR 2 and the tab PR 3.

- **The contract**, [`src/lib/myMail/contract.ts`](../../src/lib/myMail/contract.ts): the request and answer types `gmail-mine` and the tab both import (`MyMailRequest` over `list`, `open`, `tag` and `untag`; `MyMailRow`, `MyMailListOk`, `MyMailOpenOk`, `MyMailTagOk`, `MyMailUntagOk`; `MyMailRefusal` with `MY_MAIL_REFUSAL_WORDS`, the office's sentence for each of the eight reasons, and `myMailRefusal`), `MY_MAIL_FUNCTION`, `MY_MAIL_PAGE` (50), `MY_MAIL_GMAIL_LABEL` (*Tagged in ClickTooling*) and `MY_MAIL_NO_SUBJECT`. No imports, so the edge copy can hold it as it stands.
- **The kernels**, each pure with its test on made-up mail, re-exported by [`index.ts`](../../src/lib/myMail/index.ts):
  - [`rows.ts`](../../src/lib/myMail/rows.ts): a Gmail message to the tab's row over the bids@ reader's own parser (`readGmailMessage`): out when its From is the person's own address, case-blind; the other party; *(no subject)*; Gmail's snippet as text; the time; the files the reader keeps (`fileKeep`); and the fold by the reader's rules (`leftOut`'s list and machine headers, the body's foot, a no-reply sender, Gmail's other tabs, then the office's *A notice, not a person* list) read past the reader's Inbox gate, since Sent and archived mail are read too. A copy of our own from a box or the app folds as a notice; the person's own mail never folds. `readMyMail` hands the function the row, the parsed email and the Message-ID (`rfcMessageId`, passed through) from one parse; `MY_MAIL_METADATA_HEADERS` names the headers a metadata read must ask for.
  - [`mailGuess.ts`](../../src/lib/myMail/mailGuess.ts): `guessJob(row, facts)` in the card's order, a tagged conversation first (no guess), then the subject through `jobInSubject` (*the subject names JP1077*, or the job's name when the name stood in it), then the other party's one starred, else one open job (*Dale Okafor is on this job*), then a customer's domain with one open job, never an open mail service's (`OPEN_MAIL_DOMAINS`, *ridgeway.example is Ridgeway Builders*). Two candidates at a step name none and the next step is tried.
  - [`tagPlan.ts`](../../src/lib/myMail/tagPlan.ts): `planTag(thread, person, job, known)`, what a tag writes mail by mail. A mail a line already holds by its Message-ID is tied when it sits on the person (or is the person's own line from an earlier tag, the known line's `mine`), never written twice, and an office tie on another job is left where it is; any other is written with its party and way from `emailParty`, the person, the team and the boxes as ours: a mail in on its sender, a mail out to a customer on the customer as ours under whoever sent it, a note to self on the person's own thread coming in. Drafts, Spam, Trash and Chats are never written, nor a copy from a box or the app, nor one Message-ID twice. Each written line carries its files by the 25 MB rule, its file hosts' links, `<gmailId>.eml`, `mailbox` mine and the person as `mail_user_id`; the plan carries the `email_tag_threads` row and the Gmail label.
  - [`view.ts`](../../src/lib/myMail/view.ts): `viewRows`, `rowsForView` and `foldedCount` for To tag (hides the tagged, the folded and a line the boxes already tied), Tagged and All (the folded behind one line, a tagged mail never folded), newest first; `MY_MAIL_WORDS`, the card's line, the fold line and each view's empty words in `emptyWords`' kinds, every sentence held to plain words.
