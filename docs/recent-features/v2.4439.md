# v2.4439 — Submittals: each part is GC sees it, Order only or Left out (2026-10-02)

PR 4 of the train ([v2.4435](v2.4435.md), [v2.4436](v2.4436.md), [v2.4438](v2.4438.md)). The owner, on the mock-up: "we need the option to also decide which of the sub parts as well". Mock-up: https://claude.ai/artifact/VHfgwjigciY5BjkGKmtYiv. No migration (`left_out_line_ids` came with `20261002193129`); no edge function.

## What was there

A part had a *GC sees it* checkbox (off = order only) and, on a draft, a × that took it off the row. Two controls for one question, and the × had a trap: a draft whose row lacks a part the takeoff has reads as *behind its takeoff*, so **Refresh from the takeoff…** offered to put the part straight back.

## What changed

- **Three buttons on each part** ([`SubmittalPartsEditor`](../../src/components/bids/SubmittalPartsEditor.tsx)): *GC sees it · Order only · Left out* (`partPickOf`, `withPartPick` in [`itemParts.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/itemParts.ts)). A left-out part stays in the window, greyed, with its house, lead time and stage hidden, until Save; any other button brings it back. A part typed in the window and never saved simply goes. On a shared revision the three hold, as the checkbox did. Under an order-only fixture *GC sees it* is held: every part it keeps is order only.
- **Save takes a left-out part off the row** (`diffPartDrafts` reads `keptPartDrafts`), and the row's roll-up is read from what is left. Every part left out holds Save and points at the row's ×: that is leaving the fixture out.
- **The bid remembers it.** `leftOutPieceKeys` names the takeoff identity of each part left out (its assembly item, else its takeoff line — `partPieceKey`); `rememberLeftOutLines` adds them to `bid_submittal_takeoff_choices.left_out_line_ids`. `takeoffCandidates` then leaves those lines out of the fixture's `pieces` (kept aside as `leftOutPieces`), so the next build, *Refresh from the takeoff…* and its *behind the takeoff* count no longer read them. A part typed by hand has nothing to remember.
- **An ordered part cannot be left out.** Opening the window on a draft row reads the log by the row's tag (`loadPartOrderWords`); a part whose procure key holds an order has *Left out* struck through and says *Ordered 09/23, on site 09/29*.
