## The gap

The desk's **🌐 Firm's link** card could create, copy, preview, rotate and turn off the link, but not send it. The office pasted a bare link into its own mail, so nothing told the firm what the portal is, that it should add its people for emails, or that the link is a key to keep inside the firm, and nothing was filed. On the portal, `recipient_add` answered `confirmationSent: false` when the confirmation email did not go, and the page ignored it and said *Saved — the office sees it on their Needs You list*. The confirm page's expired text said *ask someone at the firm to add you again*, which the portal refuses while the first row lives.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **Should sending the link also put the address on the email list?** No. The two guards stay: nothing reaches an address until a person at the firm adds it and clicks the confirmation. The welcome email asks them to do that first.
- **One address, or several?** The address on file is often a shared inbox and the attorney is often the reader. The card takes both, at most three, in one email with the rest on Cc, so the firm sees who else got it.
- **Who is it from?** The company, signed by whoever pressed send, reply-to them. A firm that replies reaches a person.
- **Does it need a stop link?** No. One email, sent once by a person; the footer says nothing else follows unless the firm adds the address.
- **What after a Rotate?** The emailed link dies, so the *Sent to* line starts over for the new link and the toast says to send it.
- **Where does *Sent to … on …* live?** In the filed copy (`sent_documents`), not a new column: one record of what went out, and the office can open the copy from Documents once step 4 of the sent-copies plan lands.
- **Is a passing red line enough on the portal?** For the meeting, yes: it replaces a *Saved* that was untrue. A line that stays against the person until an email gets through is item 26.
- Refined after the first draw: *1 account is on it now* read badly and became *One account is waiting for you there now*.
