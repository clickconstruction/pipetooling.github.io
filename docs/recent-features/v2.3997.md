# v2.3997 — GC Review shows where you are (2026-09-28)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Owner ask: GC Review is "very long with a lot of scrolling" — show a user what stage they are at. Built from a before / after mock-up the owner approved ("build it all I love it"), with its three defaults: bills closed until a row is opened, no track under By Development, the stage filter cleared on close.

## What was wrong

- Each GC was drawn three times — a worklist row, a Temperature board row, a statement card with its job table — in one scrolling panel, with the round-email box and the scheduled sends between them.
- The only week-level indicator, the amber strip, scrolled away, and nothing said which step held the work.
- A GC's state used two vocabularies: step pills on the worklist row, Sent / Certified pills on its card further down.

## What shipped

- **`src/lib/jobs/gcReviewStages.ts`** (pure). A worklist row already has one `next` step, so a GC is at exactly one stage.
  - `gcRowStage(row)` — `next`, or `done`.
  - `buildGcStageTrack(worklist)` → three stops (`waiting`, `waitingTotal`, `done` / `of`, `complete`), `here` (the first stop with a GC waiting, `done` when the week is finished) and `finished` (`done`, `skipped`, `of`). The word is owed only over the line; a skipped GC is owed only the steps it already did; a GC whose bills changed after sign-off is back at Check.
  - `gcWorklistAtStage(worklist, stage)` — the groups with only the rows at that stage, groups with none dropped. A broken promise stays first, then the earliest stage, then the largest balance; the worklist it is given is not reordered.
  - `gcStageStopLabel`, `GC_STAGE_WAITING_ON` — the words.
- **`GcStageTrack.tsx`** — the track. A stop is a button (`aria-pressed`): it sets the stage, and the stop already picked clears it.
- **`GcReviewRow.tsx`** — one line of the list: chevron, name and balance, the steps, the action, and the detail drawn only while open. A click on the row opens it unless it landed on a control or inside the detail.
- **`GcWorklistPanel.tsx`** — rows are `GcReviewRow`s. The three pills became one line of dots (`Step`), each still the door it was: Check / Re-check, Send (locked until checked), Word (open before the statement goes out), and Sent / Word opening What went out. The next step is also the row's one button — *Check bills*, *Send*, *Get the word*, *Re-check*. **change account man**, **or mark sent** and **undo** moved into the opened row. A row says what the statement on screen owes (`statementByGc`), so it agrees with the bills under it when Include Collections is ticked. Call sheet, Ask by link and the answers-to-review button are as they were.
- **`JobsGcReviewModal.tsx`** — the panel is a title block, a sticky block (tabs with Share all / Print all, then the track), the open tab's body and a sticky foot (Include Collections, **Total outstanding**).
  - The statement card's chips, actions and table are `groupDetail(g)`, drawn inside an opened row. The card's name and stats line are the row's.
  - Groups the week asks nothing of — a GC that owes only in Collections, the no-GC bucket — are rows under **Nothing to check this week**. Under By Development every group is such a row and there is no track.
  - Tabs: **This week** (or **Developments**), **Temperature** when the board has rows, **Scheduled** when there is a pending send or the week's-list email applies. The scheduled box is full width and says so when nothing is scheduled.
  - `stage` and `tab` reset when the window closes; open rows are kept.
  - The amber strip, the subtitle and the grand-total line are gone: the track, the dialog's label and the foot say the same.
- **`src/index.css`** — `.gcReview*`, `.gcStage*`, `.gcStep*`, `.gcWorklist*`. The panel is the `gc-review` container: under 560px the stops drop to a number, a GC's steps move under its name and the detail takes the row's full width.
- Help guides `run-your-gc-statement-round` (new section *Where the week stands*; *The week's list* rewritten) and `track-a-general-contractor-on-a-job`; `GC_REVIEW_MODAL_ARCHITECTURE.md` and the glossary's GC Review entries.

## Not changed

- What each step opens, what a mark writes, certification, the two rollups, Draft Message, Share all, the call sheet, ask by link.
- The kernel's own row order (`buildGcWorklist`), which the call sheet reads.
