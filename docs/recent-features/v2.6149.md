# v2.6149 — The portal's pay-by promise drawn on the bill's row (punch list #217, outside-12, a prototype) (2026-10-30)

Punch list #217, *Show it, don't say it*: eight scans found 131 places where the app tells instead of shows, and a mock-up page draws each as a before/after pair for the owner to pick from. Of outside-12, the customer portal's pay-by promise, the owner wrote on 2026-10-30: "I really like the idea of this. I just want to see it side by side in a prototype before I approve it." This is that prototype, a draft PR with no auto-merge, viewable beside today's page on the portal's own sample. No migration. One field on `customer-portal`.

## The finding

Once a bill is a week old, the statement asks for a day, and once a day is on record it says *You told us to expect payment by Nov 6, 2026. Changed?* with three Fridays, *Pick a date* and *Tell us*, then *One tap, no sign-in. We'll hold off on reminders until then.* A promise about a process the customer cannot see, kept by a person reading a list: the day moves the bill's expected day on the office's forecast and puts a *promised* pill on the chase queue, and nothing on the page shows what happens on the day or after it.

## What changed

- **`portalPromiseStrip.ts`** (`src/lib/portal/`, new): the pure kernel from a bill, the promise and today to the strip. `promiseStrip` gives the marks left to right with where each sits — *billed* Oct 27, *today*, *your day* Fri Nov 6, *we call* Nov 13, if not in — and the strip in three short sentences for the hover and the screen reader; `promiseCallYmd` is the office's rule, the promise plus `BROKEN_PROMISE_GRACE_DAYS` (seven days, `paymentChaseTouches.ts`), the day a promise still unpaid re-enters the Payment Chase queue; `promiseChangeChips` is the page's three Fridays less the day already promised. Eight tests.
- **`customer-portal`**: the payload's `promise` carries `callYmd`, the promise plus the same constant from the kernel copy, so the page draws the office's day and not its own idea of it. A page on a function deployed before it works the same day out from the same constant (`portalPayload.ts` reads `callYmd` as null when absent), and so does the sample: its fixture leaves `callYmd` out, because an import of the kernel copy from `customerSampleFixtures.ts` would pull the copy into the bundle of every function that serves a sample page (`edgeKernels.test.ts` names the importers exactly).
- **`PortalPromiseStrip`** (`src/components/portal/`, new): the strip under a bill's row on the portal's paper — a hairline axis, a filled dot for the billed day, a thin line for today, a copper dot for the customer's day, a diamond for the call day, the words over and under the marks — and, under the first owed bill's strip only, the *Changed?* chips, *Pick a date* and *Tell us*, which send the way the statement's ask sends. Screen only: the printed statement asks for money, not a date.
- **`CustomerPortal.tsx`**: the ask and the ledger are one section (`PortalMoneySection`) drawn in one of three ways from the page's own switch (`portalShapeMode`, `src/lib/portal/portalShape.ts`): as today; with the strip (`?shape=1`), where a day on record draws on each owed bill's row and the ask at the top is gone until a day exists; or both one above the other (`?sbs=1`), captioned **Today** and **With the strip**, on the sample token or for a signed-in dev. The day on record is lifted to the statement, so a *Tell us* from the ask or the chips redraws every strip on the new day with its call day worked out here. `PortalPromiseAsk` sends through the shared `postPortalPromise` (`portalPromiseIo.ts`) and tells the page the day went through.
- **`PortalSideBySide`** (`src/components/portal/`, new): the two captioned blocks on the portal's paper, the same names as the office pages' `SideBySide` helper, which reads theme tokens the pinned-light portals do not.
- **Settings → What customers see**: the Portal step's expanded view has a **Side by side** press beside *Open in new tab*, opening the sample with `?sbs=1`.
- **Smoke** `CustomerPortal.promiseStrip.render.test.tsx`: today's sentence and no strip without the flag; the strip with its three days and the chips under `?shape=1`; a day moved from the chips on the sample, sending nothing; both looks under `?sbs=1` on the sample; today's look alone under `?sbs=1` on a real token with nobody signed in.
- **Guide** *know whether a customer keeps their word*: the portal bullet names the prototype and the Side by side press.
- **Docs**: `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` → customer-portal, the Their Word line carries `callYmd`.

## Decisions

- **The call day is seven days after the promise, not three.** The mock-up drew *we call Nov 9* under a Nov 6 promise by hand. The office's rule is `BROKEN_PROMISE_GRACE_DAYS` = 7 (`paymentChaseTouches.ts`): a promise unpaid seven days past its date re-enters the chase queue. The strip draws the rule, so the sample reads *we call Nov 13* under a Nov 6 day. The number to show is the owner's call; the kernel reads one constant.
- **One ask per account.** The promise is one day across the open bills, so the chips sit under the first owed bill's strip only; the other bills carry the strip without them.
- **The first ask stays as today.** Before a day is on record the top strip asks *Can't pay today? Tell us when to expect it.* as before; the finding is about the page after *Tell us*.

## Seeing it beside today's look

A dev server from this branch, then `/portal?t=sample&sbs=1`: the statement draws **Today** (the sentence and the chips at the top) above **With the strip** (the strip on each bill's row). `/portal?t=sample&shape=1` draws the strip alone. In Settings → What customers see, the Portal step's **Side by side** press opens the first. The sample's day is a week out and its oldest bill forty days old, so the ask is on.

## Deploy

`customer-portal` changes (the `callYmd` field) and is not deployed by the merge. Until the deploy the page works the call day out from the same constant, so the prototype reads the same either way.
