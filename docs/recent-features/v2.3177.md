# v2.3177 — Job Summary tiles: small cents, centered (2026-09-09)

Client only. Owner's ask, right after v2.3176 landed: "In other parts of the app, we take all the numbers past the decimal place and we make them smaller on the screen. I think we should do that to this tab too. Center these in the card."

## What shipped

- **[`JobSummaryTileMoney`](../../src/components/jobs/JobSummaryLedgerToolbar.tsx)** (exported from the toolbar, +1 smoke): the tile headline as `AmountSmallCents` (v2.2246's pattern — the whole amount in the text, the `.NN` at `0.7em`), a true minus for a negative figure, `—` for null. Used by the **Revenue**, **Gross profit**, **Overhead charged**, **True profit** and **Per field hour** tiles, which used to round to whole dollars (`formatUsdNoCents`) — Per field hour already carried cents at full size. The **Jobs** / **Field hours** tiles are counts and unchanged; the ▲/▼ delta lines and the hygiene chips keep the rounded `money()` form because they are secondary text.
- **Tiles centered**: `textAlign: 'center'` on the tile, so the eyebrow label, the figure, the sub-line and the delta line all sit on the card's axis.

## Verify

Jobs → Job Summary → the strip: `$96,420.18` with a small `.18`; a loss reads `−$1,234.50`; everything centered in each card.
