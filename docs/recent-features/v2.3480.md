Owner decisions 2026-09-15: a subscription may name **individuals or team leads**; **office-managed** (no self-service); **one email per report** (the digest already covers "everything, once a day").

## What shipped

- **Schema**: `report_email_subscription_team_leads` (subscription × leader; RLS = report-email managers; both fence appliers). `list_report_email_team_leads()` — the picker's leaders with member counts, gated on `can_manage_report_email_subscriptions()` (a controller may manage report emails but `can_manage_team_leader_assignments()` is dev/master/assistant only). Both schedule RPCs carry `team_leads` names beside `authors`.
- **The rule** ([`reportEmailSubscriptions.ts`](../../src/lib/reportEmailSubscriptions.ts) `subscriptionMatchesReport`, tests): all authors · the author is named · the author is, or is led by, a named team lead. Team membership is `team_leader_assignments` **read at send time**, so a new hire on Todd's team is covered the day they join. `scopeSummary` names teams as "Ann's team"; `validateSubscriptionDraft` accepts a lead alone; load/save reconcile the sidecar.
- **Edge** [`send-report-email`](../../supabase/functions/send-report-email/index.ts): `auto` adds the team-scoped subscription ids (the author's leaders ∪ the author as a lead); `manual` expands the author set with each lead and their members. Dispatch-log idempotency unchanged.
- **Modal** ([`ReportEmailSettingsModal.tsx`](../../src/components/dashboard/ReportEmailSettingsModal.tsx)): *Only from selected people or teams* shows a **People** picker and a **Team leads — their whole team, kept current** picker ("Todd — leads 4 people"); an empty leaders list says where to set one up. Pre-migration RPC or a non-manager reads as no leads, never an error.
- **Door**: Jobs → Reports gains **Report email recipients** beside Recurring Email Reports (phone: **Recipients**), same gate ([`JobsReportsTab.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsReportsTab.tsx)); the Dashboard mail button stays.
- **Settings**: the Field reports row reads "reports from Darren and Paige, and everyone Todd leads" ([`emailScheduleWeek.ts`](../../src/lib/emailSchedule/emailScheduleWeek.ts), tests); the dev Email streams chip tags "from 2 people · 1 team" (hover for names).
- **Guides**: [`email-reports-to-people.md`](../../src/content/help/email-reports-to-people.md) (two doors, the team-lead option, a team example), [`see-your-email-schedule.md`](../../src/content/help/see-your-email-schedule.md). Docs: [`EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`](../EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md) → send-report-email, [`REPORT_SUBSCRIPTIONS.md`](../REPORT_SUBSCRIPTIONS.md). Hand-added types for the table and RPC (gen-types chore after the push).

## Verified

Kernel suites, typecheck, lint, the changelog drift tests (the edge function was not Deno-checked locally — no Deno on this machine). Live on a dev server against the pre-migration DB: the door opens the modal from Jobs → Reports with the existing recipient, *Only from selected people or teams* shows the People picker and the "no team leads yet" hint. The first live pass caught a deploy-order break — the loader threw on the missing sidecar table — so `loadReportEmailSubscriptions` now reads a missing `report_email_subscription_team_leads` as no leads, and `reconcileSubscriptionTeamLeads` only errors (with a plain message) when leads were actually picked before the push. Apply order: `supabase db push` (this migration, after v2.3472's) **then** `supabase functions deploy send-report-email`; the old function ignores the new table until redeployed, and the new client saves lead rows only once the table exists.
