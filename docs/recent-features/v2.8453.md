# v2.8453 — Bills sent: By day paid says what a day's figure leaves out (2026-11-24)

The cheap half of one fault of By day paid found on the live test of 2026-11-24 (punch list #422, problem 2). No migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

For Monday, Nov 23 the Dashboard's Money in bar showed seven payments and By day paid showed five. The five agreed to the dollar. The other two were bank deposits Money in marks *Landed · not applied yet*. By day paid reads payment rows, and a deposit has none until someone puts it on a bill or a job. Neither the page nor its print said so. The foot named Stripe's fee and refunds only.

## What changed

- `BILLS_PAID_LEFT_OUT`, a sentence pair in the kernel: *A bank deposit counts once it is put on a bill or a job. Until then the Dashboard's Money in shows it and this page does not.* The words under the month carry it.
- The printed month's foot says *A bank deposit not yet put on a bill or a job is not shown.*
- The guide's own sentence on this is in the guide's change of the same day.

## Left for the owner

The words say why the two differ. They do not close the gap. Two bigger answers are open:

- a quiet line under a day, such as *2 deposits landed that are on no bill yet*, with the press to the deposits;
- one kernel for *what came in on a day* that Money in and By day paid both read, so the two cannot differ without saying so.

The first needs the bank's deposits read into this view. The second is a rework of two screens' reads. Neither is built.
