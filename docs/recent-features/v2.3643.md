# v2.3643 — Dev MCP keys move to Settings → Your account (2026-09-20)

The owner, hours after v2.3640 put the card under Digital twins: "Perhaps we should move the dev mcp keys into the settings, away from the digital twins area." Right — Settings asks *is this about ME or the COMPANY?* first ([`settingsGroups.ts`](../../src/lib/settingsGroups.ts)), a key is issued by a dev **for themself** (RLS: `user_id = auth.uid()`), and the Digital twins tab is the robots' fleet admin. Nobody had issued a key yet, so nothing to migrate.

## The change

- [`Settings.tsx`](../../src/pages/Settings.tsx): `DevMcpKeysCard` renders at the end of **Your account** for devs, and no longer under `settings-digital-twins`. The tab is everyone's; the card is `myRole === 'dev'`.
- Deep link `/settings#settings-dev-mcp-keys` (`SETTINGS_HASH_ANCHOR_TO_TAB`, +1 test). No Settings-search entry: that index is filtered per tab, not per entry, and the tab is visible to every role.
- The card drops the twins' violet chrome for Your account's (blue actions, local styles instead of `twinConsoleStyles`).
- Every sentence that named the old place: the `dev-mcp` function's missing-key message (redeploy), the help guide (its button tokens go blue too), `docs/dev-mcp/README.md`, `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`, `GLOSSARY.md`, the kernel and card headers.

## Verify

Settings → Your account as a dev: the card is the last section; `/settings#settings-dev-mcp-keys` lands on it; Settings → System → Digital twins no longer carries it. As a non-dev the account tab is unchanged.
