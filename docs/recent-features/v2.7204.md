# v2.7204 — A test of Materials on site no longer fails by chance (2026-11-13)

Test only. `src/lib/materials/onSiteStore.write.test.ts` failed now and then on a loaded machine. Nothing in the app changed.

## What happened

An unrelated PR left the merge queue on `failed_checks` on 2026-11-13. Two cases of *an on-site press through the store* had failed, out of 4,713 test files:

- *with a signal it is on the screen at once and at the office, and nothing waits*: `tables.job_material_movements` was still undefined.
- *with no signal it is kept, said once, and still on the screen after a fresh read*: an insert was already on the list.

The same file failed on `main` alone, about 1 run in 4, when run beside the rest of `src/lib/materials`, `src/lib/billing` and four jobs render tests. Alone it passed every time.

## Why

The file's `settle()` waited a fixed 30 ms, six 5 ms timers, for a send to finish. A send loads its lander the first time it runs: `landFieldPress` imports `../materials/onSiteWritesIo`. How long that takes is the machine's to say. On a busy runner it passed 30 ms. The first case then read the table before the write, and the write landed during the second case.

Proved before the change: a scratch copy of the file that made that one import take 80 ms failed with exactly those two errors, every run.

## The change

- `settle()` now waits on the sender's own word, `onSiteSender().inHand()`, that no press is still on its way (`vi.waitFor`, up to 10 s), and only then gives what follows a send its few turns. Those are the word to the person, the read after a refusal and the store's answer to the outbox, and none of them loads anything.
- `bench()` takes the sender from the same fresh modules as the store.
- `afterEach` settles too, so a case that failed part way cannot land its press in the next case.

`settle()` keeps its name and takes no argument, so the cases the two open PRs on this file add keep working and gain the same wait.

## Verified

- The scratch copy with the slow import passes at 80 ms and at 900 ms. It is not committed.
- The command that failed about 1 run in 4 passed 15 runs in a row on the fixed file.
- No other test under `src/lib/materials` or `src/lib/offline` waits on a fixed timer.
