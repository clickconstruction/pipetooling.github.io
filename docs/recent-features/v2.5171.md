# v2.5171 — GC mode: the customer signs our contract in their portal (2026-10-10)

The Board's B6-d-iii-a, the plan `to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/board-b6d.md` on branch `spike/gc-mode` ("As built: B6-d-iii in two cuts"). It follows Owner Billing's O7c pattern line for line, and Owner Billing (gc 5) read it back with four changes. It signs on B6-d-i's `gc_customer_sign_owner_contract` (v2.5160) and the sends B6-d-ii makes (v2.5167). No migration. Deploys after merge: `customer-portal` and `submit-portal-request`.

- **`customer-portal`** (`_shared/gcPortal.ts`): a won job carries `contract` while the newest send is unsigned and the job is going.
  - **What it carries:** the price as one number, never by line; every term we bill by (retainage and its step, days to pay after the architect certifies, late interest, late finish), said as sentences (`gcContractTermsLines`); the sign-by day; and a one-hour link to the file.
  - **A changed price:** `priceChanged` when `gc_owner_contract_worth_ok` says the send's price is no longer whole, so no sign form shows.
  - **Signed there:** who signed and when, with "Read what you signed".
  - **Signed on paper:** shows nothing.
  - A job with only a contract to sign shows.
- **`submit-portal-request` kind `gc_owner_contract_sign`**, in order:
  1. refuses a link for a general contractor;
  2. `gcPortalOwns`;
  3. the e-sign consent, required;
  4. the file's SHA-256 against the send's, else "This contract changed after we sent it.";
  5. the drawn signature stored, and taken back on a refusal;
  6. `gc_customer_sign_owner_contract` as the service role;
  7. the e-sign ledger row (`gc_owner_contract`) at the time it returns.

  Refusals are in the customer's words (`gcContractSignWords`). No inbox row and no email.
- **`PortalGcJobs`**: **Your contract · to sign** leads the job, then `ContractAcceptSignatureForm` with the consent's audience `customer`.
- **What customers see**: the sample portal shows a contract to sign, and the journey step `gc-portal-sign-contract` is on both portal routes.
- **Proof:**
  - `gcPortal.test.ts`: the contract on a job, every term, the price warning, the sign request's parse, the refusal words and the fingerprint;
  - `PortalGcJobs.render.test.tsx`: the sign posts the send, the name and the consent; signed, with the link; and the price warning;
  - the bed `gc-owner-contract`'s new check, "the service role signs and checks the price" (69 checks).
