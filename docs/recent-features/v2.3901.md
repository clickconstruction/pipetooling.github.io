# v2.3901 — Put a GC on notice: the jobs re-read after you mark one right (2026-09-27)

Found on the write half of punch list #43's live pass — the half v2.3819 could not take, for want of a job on the test GC. Client only, no migration.

## What was wrong

- **The band never re-read.** The run window's doors passed `onSaved: () => refetchLienDesk()`, which re-reads the Lien desk's data; the run's jobs are `useGcOnNoticeData`'s, inside the modal. A job moved from Waiting to Working through its chip stayed under Waiting, chip and counts unchanged, until the run was reopened.
- **% done told nobody.** `commitPctComplete` saves outside the autosave slices and never called `onSaved`, so no opener heard of a percent typed in the Job window.
- **A part-done line read "done · not billed".** The pour gives a line part of the done money (Top Out $720 of $800 at 80 %), and the state `done` spoke for the whole line.
- **Names cut to three letters.** Name, price and state shared one row of a 26 % column: "Rou…", "Top …".
- **On a phone the card was wider than its box.** The table's layout was automatic on a phone, so a long job name (no wrap) widened it; the open figure, the bid and the line prices sat off the right edge behind a sideways scroll.

## The change

- **[`GcOnNoticeModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/GcOnNoticeModal.tsx)** — a `rereadKey` prop; a change re-reads the run. **[`JobsStagesTab.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsStagesTab.tsx)** bumps it from both doors' saved callbacks, beside the desk's re-read.
- **[`JobFormModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormModal.tsx)** — `commitPctComplete` calls `onSaved` after a saved change, as every other save does.
- **[`gcNoticeJobsBand.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/gcNoticeJobsBand.ts)** — `gcNoticeBandLinePartWords` (pure, tested): a part-paid line lists its money; a part-done line reads its dollars.
- **[`GcNoticeJobsBand.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/GcNoticeJobsBand.tsx)** — the state under the name; the table's layout fixed at every width.
