# v2.6794 — The dev seat bills a job: plan_bill → apply_bill (punch list #293, PR 3) (2026-11-09)

PR 3 of three on [v2.6790](./v2.6790.md)'s road, after [v2.6793](./v2.6793.md): the bill, the owner's third verb ("send bills"). No migration.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/tellCustomer/billedLine.ts`** (new): the Billed line's words lifted out of `tellCustomer.ts` (`DEFAULT_LINES.billed` is now that object) with `billedText`, so the kernel copy holds the sheet's own line without the sheet's imports. `scripts/edge-kernels.mjs` mirrors it with `stripeInvoiceShareCopy.ts`, `billing/billTextLink.ts` (and `portalShortOrigin.ts` under it), `billing/newestBill.ts` and `billing/openLineAllocation.ts`.
- **`supabase/functions/_shared/devMcpBill.ts`** (new, pure): `billInput` shapes `bill = { job_id, stripe_mode, due_date?, memo?, send? }` or `{ action: 'send', invoice_id, send }` (the mode required, the due date today when left out, the sends `true` or a number or address as a string); `readBillWorld` names the reads (the job, its lines and payments, the customer, the contact and its numbers and texts, the texts switch, the office's Billed lines, the sender); `remainderCents` works the amount out as `ensure_single_ready_to_bill_invoice_for_job` sizes the primary row; `fullyInvoiced` is the modal's promotion rule; `numberOrder` and `pickNumber` are the sheet's To chip rule, `pickEmail` is `billEmailChannel`'s; `billedLineFor`, `signatureFor` and the words; `billDryRun` is the plan with its refusals; `billAppliedWords` the reply.
- **`dev-mcp` 0.53.0** (numbered behind v2.6792's 0.50.0 and v2.6804's 0.51.0, which merged first): `plan_bill` / `apply_bill` join `WRITE_VERBS` with `rpc: null` (sixty-seven write verbs). The apply runs the modal's doors in order as the dev — the ensure RPC (refused when the amount is not the plan's), `create-stripe-invoice` with the modal's own body, the row read back and `bill_pay_code` when the code is missing, `update_job_status` to Billed when fully invoiced, `contact_for_customer` when the customer has no contact, `quo-send` with the words and the real link, `messages-email` with the draft and signature — each outcome in the reply, a refused send leaving the bill standing.
- **Tests**: `src/lib/devMcp/devMcpBill.test.ts` — the shape, the reads pinned, the arithmetic, the number order held to `compose.numberOrder`, the email pick to `billEmailChannel`, the signature to `signatureLines`, the words to `tellWords` over the sheet's line, the Spanish line, the office line, the refusals, the hash, the words after, and the server's doors in the modal's order.
- **Docs**: `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` → dev-mcp, `docs/dev-mcp/README.md`, `to-dos/mcp-servers.md`, the card.

## Read closer than the design

- The office's Billed line wins over the app's when one is on the step and visible to the dev (the team's or the dev's own); when several are, the oldest is taken — the sheet orders by use, which the seat does not read.
- The plan's text carries the link as `my.clickplumbing.com/pay/<ten letters>`; the apply puts the real link in its place, so the plan's hash holds across the bill's birth.
- A job's `allow_rebill` is not taken: the verb bills a Ready to Bill job only, and the paid-job guard in `create-stripe-invoice` cannot fire on one.
