# v2.8551 — Bill Customer: a bill due today is made due today (2026-11-24)

Punch list #343, the first of the three faults its live walk of 2026-11-24 found. One edge function, `create-stripe-invoice`, and one line of the window. No migration.

**After the merge:** `bash scripts/deploy-functions.sh create-stripe-invoice` from `main`, then `npm run check:edge-drift`. Until the deploy the function makes bills as it did, so the client may go first.

## What was wrong

The walk pressed **Make the bill and email** at 7:24 PM Central on a bill the window showed as due that day. The made bill read due the day after.

Read in the code, it was not the evening. `create-stripe-invoice` sent Stripe a count of days (`days_until_due`), worked out from noon UTC of the due day and never under 1, and Stripe adds the count to the minute the bill is made:

- **A bill due today came out due tomorrow at any hour.** The count for today is 0 or less, and the floor made it 1.
- **A bill made between midnight and 6 AM Central came out a day late** whatever its due day, because the count was rounded up from noon UTC.
- A later due day made in working hours came out right.

The window's face was right throughout. So were the email's face and the preview, which read the day the window holds.

## What changed

**The function sends the due day itself.** `supabase/functions/_shared/stripeBillDueDate.ts`, new and pure, with the clock handed in. `stripeBillDue(dueYmd, nowMs)` gives what `stripe.invoices.create` takes:

1. `due_date` at 23:59:59 UTC of the due day, when that is at least ten minutes ahead. In Central that is about 6 PM on the due day. It reads as the due day in the company's zone, in UTC and in every US zone, so no page can print it a day off.
2. Else `due_date` at the last second of the due day in the company's zone, when that is ahead. That is an evening press of a bill due today, the walk's own case.
3. Else the day is over, or within ten minutes of over: `days_until_due: 1`, as before. Stripe takes no due day in the past. A recorded bill turned into a Stripe bill after its billed day lands here, and reads as it did.

**A refusal never stops a bill.** The `due_date` param had never been sent by this function, and this repo has no Stripe to try it on. If Stripe refuses it (`isStripeDueDateRefusal`, by the error's `param` or its words), the function makes the bill on a count of civil days in the company's zone, never under 1 (`stripeBillDueFallbackDays`), and logs the refusal. At worst a bill is made as it was before.

**Which day the customer is told.** Each reader of the made bill prints Stripe's instant in the company's zone: the bill email and each copy (`formatDueDate`), the made-bill panel (`formatStripeDueDateChicago`), the split window and `stripeUnixToAppYmd`. All of them now give the day the window showed. Stripe's own page and PDF print the same instant by Stripe's rules, which this repo cannot read. Rule 1 is there for that: its instant is the due day in UTC too.

**The window's first day is the company's today.** `todayIsoDate` in `SendRecordInvoiceModal.tsx` read the browser's own date. It reads `todayYmdInAppTz()` now (`docs/TIME_AND_ZONES.md`), for the sent day and the due day alike.
