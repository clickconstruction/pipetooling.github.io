# v2.4228 — Pricing: the Workbench walkthrough in plain words (2026-09-30)

Punch list #58, item 1 (the row opened 2026-09-29 for what the Submittals plain-words train, v2.4123–v2.4126, did not reach). No migration, no edge function. The Workbench's *Take the tour* walked five stops written in the old voice: 30-word sentences glued with dashes and parentheses, and trade words (packet, price option, base, margin, preview, solver) never explained.

## What changed

- **The five stops** in [`workbenchHelp.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/workbenchHelp.ts) are rewritten by the rules the Submittals tour set (`to-dos/plain-words-elsewhere.md` holds them): one idea per sentence and no sentence over 20 words; *you* + a verb and the control's exact name (*＋ Add GC*, *☆ make base*, *Offer it to … as an alternate*, *Apply*, *Discard*, *Solver ›*, *‹*, *Price unpriced only*, *📌*); a trade word explained beside itself the first time; no dashes, semicolons, parentheses or `·` lists inside a sentence. The anchors and their order are unchanged, so nothing on the tab moves. Two facts the old words carried were dropped on purpose: hand-set prices on no-cost rows stacking on top of a solve, and the solver's open state being remembered — both are the page's own behaviour, not something the first-timer has to do.
- **The rules test** — `submittalTour.test.ts`'s cases copied over `WORKBENCH_TOUR_STEPS` in [`workbenchHelp.test.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/workbenchHelp.test.ts): the sentence rules, a verb in every stop, one exact control name per stop, and the six trade words each explained where they first appear. A stop that fails is rewritten, never exempted.
- The to-do's status: item 1 done; the guide *build a submittal package* (item 2) and the convention call (item 3) are what is left.
