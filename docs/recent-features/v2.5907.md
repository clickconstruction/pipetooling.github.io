# v2.5907 — Quickfill: Today's lines opens on the rows to fill in (2026-10-26)

D's PR 1 of punch list #178 (`to-dos/needs-me-rail/`). The owner asked on 2026-10-26 which Needs you lines are not in Quickfill for assistants, and whether pulling the fill-outs into Quickfill would get better compliance. A live read of the stations' Mark history windows as him, read-only, answered it: the stations marked every workday are the ones whose body is the work, a tick list, a ledger, the tray's deck, while Today's lines, a list of doors to other pages, had 7 marks in 35 days with something open at every one and none since it moved to first in the round ([v2.5843](v2.5843.md)). The Fill them in deck ([v2.5774](v2.5774.md)) held 205 rows behind a chip nobody pressed. So the deck becomes the station's body.

## What changed

- **The opening rule** is a kernel, `needsYouStationOpening({ rows, loading, role })` in `lib/needsYou/fillIn.ts`: the deck whenever it holds rows that are the viewer's own lines, and while it is still reading them for a viewer whose lines they would be, so the station never opens on the card and then jumps; the card once the read ends with nothing to fill. Whose rows they are is `fillInKindIsViewers`, each kind's line's actor from [v2.5843](v2.5843.md)'s `needsYouActor.ts`: every kind built so far is the assistant's, so the owner and the controller open on the card with their own lines first and the deck one chip away. The first live look found that gate: as the dev, the first draft opened the station on the office's 206 rows above the owner's own 10 lines. The stored Cards / Walk choice governs the card after the deck, never the opening: the deck is the body, not a preference.
- **The card** (`DashboardNeedsYouCard`) takes `fillIn.opensFirst` from its host. With it the card opens on the deck, draws the viewer's lines the deck cannot fill under it with the folds (`fillInOtherLinesHeading`: *Your other lines · 4*), and keeps Cards and Walk the list behind their chips, the **Fill them in · N rows** chip the way back. A viewer who presses anything keeps that for the visit (`touched`); the deck closes by itself only when its first read ends with no rows (`hadRows`), so the deck's end card with the station's Mark still shows after the last save. The Dashboard host passes no `fillIn` and is unchanged.
- **The station** (`QuickfillNeedsYouSection`) computes the opening from `useFillInRows`' rows and loading, passes `opensFirst`, and reports the deck's first row's sentence as its Next line (`todaysLinesNextLine(yours, firstRow)`), so the Roller's card and the round's list say the first press.
