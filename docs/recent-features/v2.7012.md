# v2.7012 — Pipeline: the hazmat fee button shows on the three stages before the bill goes out (2026-11-12)

The owner's call (2026-11-12): the orange biohazard button belongs on **Waiting**, **Working** and **Ready to Bill** rows, and not on **Billed Awaiting Payment** or **Collections**. It opened the Hazmat Fee wizard on every row of the board, including rows whose bill the customer already holds, where a new fee can only wait for a bill that may never be sent.

**What changed**

- `src/lib/jobs/hazmatFeeStages.ts` (new kernel, `stageOffersHazmatFee`): the one list of stages that offer the press. Waiting, Working and Ready to Bill answer yes. Billed, Collections, the Uncollectible band and Paid in Full answer no.
- The three row layouts take a new `offerHazmatFee` prop and draw the press only when it is true **and** the role may create a fee (`canCreateHazmatFee`, unchanged): the Waiting / Working desk rows (`JobsStagesTable`), the billing desk rows (`StagesUnifiedJobRow`), and the phone's ⋯ sheets (`JobsStagesCardList`, both lists). A section that says nothing offers none, so a new section cannot grow the button by accident.
- `JobsStagesTab` sets the flag from the kernel inside `stagesSectionActionProps`, the object the board's sections and the follow-up deck's rows both spread, so the deck's billed and Collections rows lose the press with the board's. Paid in Full keeps its props at its own site and reads the kernel there.

**What a person does when an exposure is found after the job is billed:** press **Send back** on the billed row (from Collections, **Send back to Billed** first), which returns the job to Ready to Bill, then press the biohazard button there. The fee joins the open bill and goes out as its own line on the next send. The guide *charge a hazmat fee* has the steps under *When the job is already billed*.

**Left alone on purpose:** the riders strip in Edit Job (`JobFormHazmatRidersStrip`) and the notice presses in Bill Customer (`SendRecordInvoiceModal`) are untouched, so a fee already on a billed job keeps its notice, its PDF, its email and its Edit / Void / Delete locks exactly as before. The role gate, the wizard and the green box on a job with a live fee are unchanged on the three stages that still show the button.

**Is this the best we can do?** For the ask, yes: one kernel holds the rule, every layout reads it, and the default is *no button*. Two things it costs, both named in the guide: a billed row no longer wears the green box that marked a job with a live fee (the red rider line in Edit Job still says so), and a late exposure takes one Send back before the fee. One gap it closes on the way: on the phone, a **Ready to Bill** job's ⋯ sheet had never listed *Hazmat fee* (only Waiting and Working did), which would have left *Send back, then the button* with no button on a phone. The billing sheet (`JobsStagesUnifiedCardList`) now lists it under the same two conditions, so the three stages offer the press on every layout.

**Tests:** `hazmatFeeStages.test.ts` (the list), and two cases in `JobsStagesTab.render.test.tsx` that draw one made-up job per stage on the real board: the desk rows carry the press on Waiting, Working and Ready to Bill and not on Billed, Collections, Uncollectible or Paid in Full, and the phone sheet lists *Hazmat fee* on Waiting, Working and Ready to Bill cards and not on the other four. Both fail when the kernel is widened to Billed.

No migration, no edge function deploy.
