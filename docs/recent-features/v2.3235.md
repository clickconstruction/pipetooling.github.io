# v2.3235 — Job window History on a phone: one row per day worked instead of the 36 px-a-day grid (2026-09-10)

Client only; no migration.

## Why

The job window's History tab is the Projects day grid in single-job mode: 36 px per calendar day, 181 days by default, opened at its right edge. On a 359 px pane that is ten days per screen and eighteen swipes end to end, almost all of them empty for one job, and a finger on it drags sideways while nothing scrolls up or down — it read as the window being stuck (Taunya, 2026-09-10; the ✕ was v2.3231).

## What shipped

- **Kernel** `src/lib/jobs/jobHistoryDayList.ts`: `buildJobHistoryDayList(rows, { jobId, todayYmd, startYmd, endYmd })` turns the same approved clock-session rows the grid aggregates into one row per day worked, newest first — distinct people in first-seen order, `open` when today has a session not clocked out, `gapBefore` (days with no work down to the next older row), `quietAfter` on the newest row when the job has gone quiet — plus `daysWorked`, `maxPeople`, and the distinct people. `jobHistoryDayListSummary` ("6 days worked · 3 people at most"), `jobHistoryGapLabel`. Tested on a six-day fixture with other-job and out-of-range rows, an open session today vs. an unclosed old one, a one-day job (180 quiet days), and an empty range.
- **Component** `JobHistoryDayList`: summary line, then rows — weekday + date (· today), the names (from `useUserDisplayNames`, else "N people"), *still clocked in* in amber, the people-count square in `peopleCountColor`'s blue scale — with dashed one-line gaps between runs and a "quiet since" line above the newest row. Tap → the same day detail the grid's numbered cell opens (`onDayCellClick(bar, ymd)`).
- **Tab** (`ProjectsJobHistoryTab`): keeps the raw session rows alongside the bars; under 640 px in single-job mode (`useNarrowViewport640`) renders the list instead of `ProjectsJobHistoryTimeline` and hides the Expanded / Compact toggle. The range pickers and the 90d / 365d chips stay. Projects → Job History and every desktop view are unchanged.
- Help guide `job-window-tabs.md` History section notes the phone list.

## Differences from the mockup

- No hours per row: the rows the tab fetches carry `clocked_out_at` but no clock-in, so hours are not derivable here. The day detail (tap) has them. Adding a minutes column to `fetchProjectsJobHistoryClockSessions` would allow it; not done blind.
- The From / To pickers were not collapsed to one line; they already stack acceptably on a phone.

## Notes for the next session

- The Projects page's multi-job history on a phone still draws the grid. A per-job list there is a different question (many jobs, lanes) and was deliberately left alone.
