# v2.6034 — Billed: the expected-pay model reads the Collect desk's pace order (2026-10-28)

Punch list #177's last piece (`to-dos/collect-desk-weakest-link.md`, PR F, decision 1). [v2.5857](v2.5857.md) moved the Bridge's receipts band to the pile's paces and left `billedExpectedPayModel` on the company median, so every other surface that reads it (the Pipeline's dates block and chips, the payment forecast, call mode, the AR window, the Customer hub, the Follow-up flow, the Job window's bills) still expected a first-job GC's pay application a week after it was marked billed, and read it late from then on. This moves the model.

## One rule, not two copies

- **`src/lib/jobs/payPace.ts`** (new, pure): the pace rule the pile wrote in [v2.5848](v2.5848.md), lifted out of `collectPile.ts` so both read one function. `paceFor` — the payer's own median when it has `minSamples`, else the GC pace here when the payer is a GC and `gcPaceForNewGc`, else the segment median (residential or commercial), else the company's. `readsAsGc` — decision 1: a GC by record (some job names it as its GC), by a pay application on the job, or by a commercial payer's bill marked billed by hand. `gcPaceHere` — the median of the GC payers' own medians, with how many it rests on. `PACE_RULES_DEFAULT` carries the pile's defaults; the rules are inputs, never constants. `PAY_SPEED_MIN_SAMPLES` lives here now (re-exported from `billedExpectedPay.ts`, so nothing else moved). `paceContextFor` works the facts and the GC pace out once for a screen's bills; `paceInputFor` hands one bill its input; `sentFromHereOf` reads a bill's send stamp (absent on a lean row = unknown, never "by hand").
- **`collectPile.ts`**: `CollectRules` is `PaceRules` plus the two day rules; `collectPaceFor` and `gcPaceHere` are the shared functions under the pile's names; `buildCollectPile` reads each bill through `readsAsGc` and `paceFor`. Nothing it returns changed.
- **`billedExpectedPay.ts`**: `billedExpectedPayModel(input, data, today, promise, pace?)` — the fifth argument is `ExpectedPayPaceInput` (`isGc`, `gcPace`, `gcPacePayers`, `rules`); a caller that cannot say passes nothing and the payer reads as one that is not a GC, by the default rule (own → segment → company). `source` gains `'gc' | 'segment'`; the model gains `samples`, `payers` and `paceWords` — *pays in ~35d*, *the GC pace ~35d over 3 payers*, *commercial pace ~12d*, *company avg* — the label's tail, and the hover names which pace it read and over how many payments or payers.
- **`payPaceIo.ts`** (new): `loadPaceFacts` — the two reads decision 1 needs (`jobs_ledger.gc_customer_id`, live `job_pay_applications`), for the jobs named or for every job. The Collect desk's loader (`loadCollectDesk.ts`) reads through it now, so the desk and the board read the same facts.

## The consumers

- **The Stages board** (`JobsStagesTab.tsx`): `useBilledMoneyData` loads the facts once (fail-soft); `billedPaceContext` rests the GC pace here on the GC payers among the board's open Billed and Collections bills, filed under whoever owes them (`listPayer`), the way the desk rests it on the GC payers in its pile — the first cut rested it on every billed job's payers, paid bills included, and read 19 days over 10 GCs against the desk's 31 over 9. The lien runway, the phone next line, the dates ledger (`billedDatesLedger.ts`, a `pace` input), the payment forecast (`buildBilledPaymentForecast`, sixth argument; `BilledPaymentForecastModal` shows `paceWords` with the hover) and call mode (`buildPaymentChaseQueue`, seventh argument; the customer's speed line borrows the bills' pace) all read it.
- **The Bridge** (`sheetBuild.ts`): the fallback before the pile is in reads the new order; its words are the pile receipt's (*the GC pace here ~31d*, *commercial pace ~13d*) and the basis of a borrowed pace is `company`, as `collectReceiptFor` has it. The pile path is unchanged.
- **The Collect desk's footnote** (`collectWords.ts`) no longer says the forecast reads the company median; it says the board's chips and the forecast read the same paces.
- **Pass nothing, read the segment**: the Job window's bills list (`useJobBilledExpectedPay`), the Follow-up flow (`theyOweUs.ts`), the residential flow (`residentialLoad.ts`) and the AR window read own → segment → company. They have no pile or board to rest a GC pace on, and a read on every screen was not worth it; a new GC's bill there reads the commercial pace, with the hover saying so. The pay-speeds breakdown's two sentences on the company median say the borrowed order now.
- **The forecast email** (`supabase/functions/_shared/paymentForecastCore.ts`, a hand-written port, not generated) keeps today's order, own → company: its payload carries no GC facts, and adding them is an RPC change. The email can read a first-job GC a few weeks earlier than the app does; the to-do carries it.
