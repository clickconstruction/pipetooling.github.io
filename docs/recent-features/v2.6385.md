# v2.6385 — Today's sends: Stop reminding and Hold until find a homeowner's step (2026-11-02)

Taunya, 2026-11-02, relayed by the owner: "Under Needs Me, Today's sends: this customer has already been texted but it
won't let me click Stop reminding on this job. When I click it I get the error at the top." The toast read *Failed to
stop the reminders: stepUnknown* on a homeowner's moved-visit text (a `booked` offer the schedule's move made, v2.5597).
PR 1 of three on that report; migration `20261102193000_flow_step_check_residential.sql`
(`docs/migrations/20261102193000_flow_step_check_residential.md`), pushed after the merge. No edge function.

## The cause

The offer's contact is kind `customer`. Every customer's own contact is: `contact_find_or_make` writes `customer` for
every customer, and `contacts_kind_known` has no `residential` value, so prod has no contact of kind `residential` at
all (v2.6232's fragment says as much). The steps the app prepares for a homeowner are the residential flow's all the
same — the moved visit's Rescheduled text on `booked`, the Tell the customer sheet's, the Pipeline's forward moves —
and `flow_step_check` joined `flows.kind = contacts.kind` alone, so for a homeowner it looked for `booked` on the GC
customer flow and refused every press on a residential step: Stop reminding on this job (`flow_skip_step`), Hold until…
(`nudge_hold_until`), a step note, and the Send press's own record (`nudge_offer_sent` → `nudge_record_sent`). The
window showed the same mismatch quietly: the row read *(830) 555-0121 · booked* (the number redacted here), the number for a contact with no name
of its own and the raw key for a step the `customer` flow does not have.

## What shipped

- **`flow_step_check`** reads the person's flow as the app does: their kind's, and the residential flow too for a
  `customer` contact whose customers row says `residential` (through the contact's `customer_id` or the named person's
  `customer_contact_persons` row, as `flow_rows_for` resolves them), as `SECURITY DEFINER` so the answer does not
  depend on which customers the caller may read (an assistant sees her masters' alone, a controller none). The
  refusal keeps its `stepUnknown` code and names the step in `DETAIL`. The flows bed holds it: a residential customer's contact skips Booked; Portal opened on them,
  and Booked on a commercial customer's contact, are still refused.
- **The window reads the customer beside the contact** (`offersIo.ts`'s `loadOpen`: `customers(name, customer_type)`):
  the step's name comes from the flow `contactFlowKindOf` picks (new in `flows/facts.ts`, the client's reading of the
  same rule), and a contact with no name of its own is named from the customers row — *Dana Whitfield · Booked*.
- **A `stepUnknown` that still comes back** — a step the office took off the flow after the text was prepared — is
  said in words (`offers.ts`'s `offerRefusalWords`, on every press of the window): *The step Booked is not on Dana
  Whitfield's flow, so there is nothing to stop or hold. Put this text away with Not this one.*
- **Guide** *work Today's sends* says what to press when the app says the step is not on the flow. Docs:
  `GLOSSARY.md` → Today's sends, `MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md` (the presses), the flows migration's doc.

## Tests

## Not in this PR

The wider finding stays open for the owner: with no `residential` contact kind, the step card, Follow up and the
morning run (which reads contacts of every flow kind but `customer`) all read a homeowner as a GC customer, so the
residential flow's own reminders never run for a real homeowner. PR 2 holds an offer whose text already went; PR 3
makes a moved visit's text expire with its visit.
