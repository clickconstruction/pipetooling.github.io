## What a GC sees

Under the block that says what a job's paper asks, a press reads **Propose a stricter setting**. It opens a panel:

- The list's notice with its box, where the list has one.
- Each topic on the office's own slider. Every stop looser than the job's is dead and says *You can only make this stricter*. A list with no levels shows each topic as a list of its choices from the job's own up.
- The plain words of the stop under each slider.
- **Your name**, and **Send the proposal**. The line above the press says what is missing: a stricter topic, the tick, the name.

After the send the block reads *Your proposal of (day) is with our office. Nothing changes on the paper until our office takes it.* Later it reads that the office took it, or declined it with the office's reason, or that it was set aside because the paper changed.

## What the office sees

A proposal that waits shows under its paper's row in **Papers before this job opens**:

- Who proposed and the day, from the name the GC typed.
- Each topic it moves, from the job's choice to the stricter one, with that choice's plain words.
- **Take it**. With signers it reads *Take it, and ask 2 people to sign again*. A confirm says what it does first.
- **Decline…**, which asks for a reason the GC reads.

A take saves through the same door as the office's own change. It is saved in place while nobody has signed, and it is a new version that everyone signs again once somebody has.

## The rules, and where each is held

The database holds every one (`job_paper_propose`, `job_paper_proposal_decide`):

- Only the customer who is the job's GC proposes, through their own portal link.
- No topic may loosen, and one must tighten.
- A typed name, and the notice's tick where the list has a notice.
- Six proposals an hour from one customer.
- One proposal waits on a paper. A newer one, the office's change of settings and the paper coming off each overtake it.
- Only the office answers, and only a proposal still waiting.

`jobPaperProposal.ts` says the same rules before a press, for both screens.

## What never reaches the GC's page

The portal function trims the settings list (`gcProposeDef`): names, plain words, the levels and the notice. What puts a person in breach, what the site must have, the blanks, the site note and the Spanish stay behind. A settings list holds no clause wording at all.
