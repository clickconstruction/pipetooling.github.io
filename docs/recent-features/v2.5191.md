# v2.5191 — GC mode: what lets a trade partner send its own papers from its portal, behind the scenes (2026-10-10)

P5b-m of the trade partner portal's real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/PORTAL_REAL_BUILD.md`; plan `to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/portal-p5b.md`, both on branch `spike/gc-mode`). Migration `20261010140000_gc_portal_p5b_papers` (doc `docs/migrations/20261010140000_gc_portal_p5b_papers.md`); the lead pushes it after the merge. No client or function change.

- **`gc_trade_files` takes a certificate**, which is on no job: purpose `coi`, and a project on every file but a certificate (`gc_trade_files_job_or_company`). Both CHECKs are added `NOT VALID`, then validated.
- **Three verbs, the service role's alone**, each taking the link's company first:
  - `gc_trade_coi` files the company's insurance certificate from its own upload, as a signed `coi` paper with its day and link, and ties the upload to it. The keep trigger keeps the insurance promise. A day not after today, or more than three years out, is refused.
  - `gc_trade_vetting_form` writes the vetting form of a company still new to us, and a second send replaces the first.
  - `gc_trade_paper_open` opens the company's master agreement the office sent, or its W-9, to sign on `/contract/accept`. A W-9 with none is copied from the Contract Book's W-9 for subs. It stores the hash of the token the function mints, and the newest link wins.
- **Eight new refusal keys** (`coiDayNeeded`, `coiPast`, `coiTooFar`, `certNeeded`, `vetDecided`, `formIncomplete`, `msaNotSent`, `noW9Form`) wait in `gcTradeSubmit.test.ts`'s `WAITING` as `'P5b'`.
- **The bed** `gc-portal-p5b` (`npm run test:pg:gc-portal-p5b`, in `scripts/sql-beds.txt`) passed locally on Docker, applied twice, and caught six mutants. `gc-portal-p5a` and `gc-papers` pass with it.
- **Docs**: the migration's doc and `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`.
