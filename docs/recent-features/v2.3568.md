# v2.3568 — Supply houses: job-account invoices marked in the aging heat map (2026-09-17)

Item 1 of `to-dos/supply-house-job-account-aging.md`, decided by the owner 2026-09-17 as **B — shade** (the before / after in `supply-house-job-account-aging-before-after.html`): job-account invoices stay in the cells, with their own "of which on a job account" line. Item 3's remembered toggles ride along.

## The reading

The heat map on **Materials → Supply houses → Accounts payable** is unpaid dollars by days past due, per house. Since v2.2652 the Job Accounts tab bars treat job-account invoices (bought on the owner's account at the house; the owner's debt to collect on) as their own thing; the heat map did not. On 2026-09-17 prod had 245 unpaid invoices and exactly one on a job account — National Wholesale, $2,759.01, current — so nothing was wrong that day; the map would have started lying the first time a job-account invoice aged.

Option A (exclude them, a column beside the map) was drawn as the recommendation; the owner chose B: the cell stays the house's total, because to the house an aged job-account invoice is past due, and the teal line says how much of it is an owner's.

## The change

- [`supplyHouseAging.ts`](../../src/lib/supplyHouseAging.ts): `buildSupplyHouseAgingMatrix` takes `on_job_account` on the invoice rows and carries, per house, `jobAccount` (per bucket) and `jobAccountTotal`, and on the matrix `jobAccountTotals`, `jobAccountGrandTotal` and `jobAccountInvoiceCount`. Every existing number is unchanged — the buckets, the totals, the 60+ count, the credits. Two tests.
- [`SupplyHousesTab.tsx`](../../src/components/SupplyHousesTab.tsx): the summary query reads `on_job_account`; under each aging cell and the Owed cell, `$X job acct` in the Job acct chip's teal when the share is nonzero; a **Mark job-account invoices** checkbox on the bar (shown only while a job-account invoice is unpaid) hides the lines; the "N houses 60+ past due" sentence and the totals row do not move. The phone layout is unchanged.
- The three toggles on the bar — *Show paid invoices*, *Show last payment*, *Mark job-account invoices* — are remembered per device (`localStorage`, `supplyHouses.accountsPayable.*`, fail-soft).

## Verified on the dev server (prod data)

National Wholesale's *Current* cell reads $4,298.65 with *$2,759.01 job acct* under it, and its Owed cell carries the same line; no other cell changed; *6 houses 60+ past due* unchanged. Unchecking the toggle removes both lines; a reload keeps the toggle unchecked; re-checking brings them back.
