## What changed from the prototype, and why

1. **The portal's write row showed for no one.** It tested `c.state === 'open'`, a state no contact has. It reads the open state now, and a portal write sits in Needs you only, once (the lead).
2. **The badge counted Needs a reply only.** It counts each person once across Needs you, Needs a reply and We owe them, so Priya in all three counts one (the lead).
3. **Needs a reply sorted by the newest line of any kind**, so a team note could move a row. It sorts by their newest message, as the move line counts it. The demo's order is the same.
4. **A snooze had no day and nothing woke it.** It keeps the coming Monday (`snoozed_until`) and wakes that morning. A press on a Monday means the Monday after.
5. **Another day added three days on Saturday**, landing on a Tuesday. It is the next working day now.
6. **The day reader hardcoded 2026 and 2027**, and read *monster* as Monday and *octopus 5* as Oct 5. It reads today's year, rolling to the next once the date has passed, and only real day and month words. *Next Monday* said on a Friday is the coming Monday, and *the Monday after next* the one after (the lead). It also reads *in 3 days*, *next week*, *Oct 16, 2027*, *10/16/27* and *the 7th*, the way a voicemail says a day.
7. **A day was a label, re-read by a regex.** It is a date when the words read as one, and the words when they do not. It is never refused: `by_words` sits beside `by_day`, and a row with words reads *no day* (the lead). A note with only words for a day never comes back, and says so when it goes. The send window's blank skipped the reader. Every day goes through it now.
8. **The what list keyed its words by the prototype's step ids.** A real step is a `flow_steps` row, so the step carries its words (`FlowStep.owedWords`, seeded with the prototype's seven in `flows/seed.ts`), with *the* + its name as the fallback. A number nobody knows has no flow of ours, so *the who is this?* is gone.
9. **Eight promise kinds** stand, and `message_promises.kind` takes all eight (the lead). *A start or delivery* writes a start, and *insurance or W-9* writes insurance. The written line and the pill name the one written.
10. **The running promise was a string kept on the contact for good.** It is read now: the newest written promise whose day is today or later.
11. **The heard day arrives read** (cut 5's summaries). This cut parses no transcript.
12. **The plans question** keeps its states here: offered, asked, answered (the prototype's *mine*). It lives in `message_promises` with the kind `question` (the lead).
13. **Done with it** keeps the note and stamps `back_done_at` on it (the lead).
14. **A trade partner with no company** read *undefined's quote*. Their first name names it, *their* for a number nobody named, and a quote with no trade drops *their trade*.
15. **Plain words.** Sentences with a semicolon or parentheses are split, and the tests hold every sentence to `plainWords.ts`. The prototype's written line ran to 22 words on a long job name, so it is two sentences: where, then what and by when.
16. **Times are ISO and days are civil dates** in the app's zone. The prototype read the browser's clock.
17. **Who has it and the state's moves** come with this cut (the lead). A conversation the rule gives out reads like any other on its pill, with no *by the rule* (the plan's audit, v1.116); `has_how` still keeps `rule`.

18. **One row per person, never one per job** (the lead, 2026-10-12). A person's newest note with a day stands for them, as the prototype's one `c.back` did, and nothing older comes back once it is done. A customer has one portal row, led by their write when they wrote, with every paper waiting on them after it, the oldest first: a change order on each of two jobs is one row. We owe them keeps one row per person. The kinds stay separate rows in Needs you (a note, the portal, a clause), as the prototype draws Priya, and the badge counts her once.
19. **A paper is never shown twice** (the lead with helper 5, 2026-10-12). An open change order nudges like any their-move step, so the nudges come as a builder the queue calls with `onPortal`, in the shape `nudgeRows`' `skip` takes (the person, the paper's kind, the open round's key, which for a change order is its row id, as `PaperView.id` is). A paper a portal row shows stays out of its nudge row before the row's line is written, and the portal row wins. The queue hands back the same `onPortal` for the step card, so the card says so too.

Two smaller changes. A note brought back past its morning reads *back since Thu Oct 1*, where the prototype said *back today* for every day. We owe them sorts the soonest day first, with a late row flagged.
