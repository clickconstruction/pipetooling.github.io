# v2.3895 — Phone fixes: past-due chips, customer rows, the move sheet’s days (2026-09-27)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Four things a look at an iPhone 15 (393 × 852) found in punch list #30's work — the first time any of it was seen on a phone. The look ran on a local server through the dev sign-in as each sample login, read-only.

## What was wrong, and what shipped

- **Supply house, a past-due invoice** — the *12 d past due* chip sat after the invoice's title and was clipped to *12 d p…* under a long one. `SupplyHousePhoneScreen.tsx`: the chip leads the second line and never shrinks.
- **Customers, a row's second line** — the address, the jobs, the balance and the activity were one line, so a long address pushed the rest off the end. `customerPhoneSearch.ts` gains `phoneCustomerLines` (the place; the standing) and the row draws them on two lines.
- **Subs → Pay, a sheet row's reason** — cut to one line (*nothing signed · $40…*). It may take two.
- **Dispatch Mode's Schedule, Move or reassign** — the move sheet was handed both visible weeks, and fourteen day chips overlapped at phone width. It gets the selected day's own week; any other day is *Or pick a date*.
