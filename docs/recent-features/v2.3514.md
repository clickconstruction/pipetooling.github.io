# v2.3514 — Deposits applied in Accounts Receivable count as Income (2026-09-16)

Migration `20260916150000_ar_applied_deposits_income.sql` ([`docs/migrations/20260916150000_ar_applied_deposits_income.md`](../migrations/20260916150000_ar_applied_deposits_income.md)); no edge function. Mock-up with the version-1 critique: *Applied Means Income*.

## Why

The app keeps revenue in two books that never spoke. On the **job side**, applying a bank deposit in Accounts Receivable writes a payment row pointing at the transaction — Job Summary, Crew P&L and the job's Paid status read that. On the **bank side**, every Mercury transaction carries one accounting label, and the P&L and Visuals count a deposit as income only when that label is *Income*. Nothing linked the two: the AR modal never read or wrote the label, and neither did the allocation or tip RPCs.

Labels came from 50 rules, every one *counterparty contains \<customer name\>*, which create a suggestion someone approves on Banking → Accounting (the server-side auto-approve switch from v2.2889 is still off), or from a hand pass on Drag Sort — which assistants cannot open since v2.3305. So a customer who pays once is never caught by a rule, and the person who matched the deposit cannot label it.

Read-only from prod on 2026-09-16, deposits posted in the last twelve months:

| Label | Applied in AR | Not applied |
|---|---|---|
| Income | 101 · $251,690 | 294 · $1,400,579 |
| Unlabelled | **24 · $171,710** | 52 · $107,266 |
| Something else | 1 · $4,890 | 1,127 |

Twenty-two of the 24 had no rule at all (TF Harper $81,917, Burd $15,102, Summit, Loberg, Elaine Giesber). The app already knew all 24 were customer payments. The one applied deposit filed under something else is a $4,890 City of Seguin deposit on job 908 labelled *Taxes and Licenses* — most likely a permit refund — which is why the rule must never overwrite a label a person set.

## What shipped

**The rule lives in the database and fires on the payment write, whoever writes it.**

- **Org switch** `app_settings.ar_applied_deposits_count_as_income`, seeded `false`; dev or master flips it (the same shape as the auto-approve switch).
- **Trigger `jobs_ledger_payments_ar_income_label`** (AFTER INSERT OR UPDATE OF `mercury_transaction_id` OR DELETE): the first payment recorded against a deposit labels it *Income* through `ar_label_deposit_income(...)` — **only when the deposit carries no label at all**. Every writer is covered at once: the allocation RPC, the tip RPC (v2.3496), the HouseCall Pro backfill, a recorded payment linked later, Edit Job's autosave. The body swallows its own errors with a WARNING so a label problem can never abort a payment.
- **Provenance sidecar `mercury_transaction_ar_income_labels`** — one row per deposit AR labelled, with the payment, bill and job that earned it. It is what lets the label be withdrawn: `ar_unlabel_deposit_income(...)` clears an AR-set label only once **no payment on the deposit remains** and only while it still reads Income; a label a person changed is left alone and the stale claim is dropped.
- **Backfill `backfill_ar_applied_income_labels(p_dry_run)`** (dev/master): the dry run counts what would be labelled; the real run labels every applied, unlabelled deposit once, through the same writer.

**The screens only report** — nothing on the deposit rows, no new control that writes a label from AR (assistants stay off Banking):

- **Accounts Receivable** ([`BankPaymentsModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/BankPaymentsModal.tsx)): the footer sentence carries the act — *Applies $5,574.60 to 804 · Summit GC and books it as Income. The bill is settled.* (several lines: *… Books the deposit as Income.*; a recorded-payment link the same). When the deposit already carries some other label, a quiet amber line under the header reads *Labelled Taxes and Licenses in Banking, not Income. Apply leaves that alone.* and the footer ends *Stays Taxes and Licenses in Banking.* Already-Income deposits and a switch that is off say nothing. The success toast gains four words: *Applied $5,574.60 · booked as Income*.
- **Banking → Accounting** ([`BankingMercuryAccountingTab.tsx`](../../src/components/banking/BankingMercuryAccountingTab.tsx)): a second org switch beside the first — *Deposits applied in Accounts Receivable count as Income*. While off, dev/master see the dry-run line (*24 deposits are applied in Accounts Receivable and still unlabelled · $171,709.63. Turning this on labels them Income, and every one applied after.*); flipping it on runs the backfill and the toast reports the count. Before the migration is pushed the switch shows *(not live yet)* and is disabled.
- Kernel [`src/lib/jobs/arBankLabel.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/arBankLabel.ts) (+9 tests) decides every word from the switch and the deposit's label; [`arApplySentence.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/arApplySentence.ts) gains `booksIncome` / `bankLabelStays` (+6 tests). Render smoke [`BankPaymentsModal.arIncome.render.test.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/BankPaymentsModal.arIncome.render.test.tsx) pins the note and both footer forms.

## What was deliberately not done

- **No chip on the deposit rows.** Once the rule runs, 125 of 126 applied deposits read Income; a chip on every row would bury the one that deviates.
- **No built-in row in the rules table.** Every row there has criteria a person wrote and can edit or delete; this one has neither. A switch is the honest shape.
- **No "not income" picker in AR.** Banking is controller and above by owner decision (v2.3305). The rule never overwrites a hand-set label, so the permit-refund case is safe by construction and no side door is needed.
- **The 294 Income deposits nobody matched in AR are untouched** — mostly Stripe and HouseCall Pro payouts covering several bills, so *not applied* is not a clean signal there.
- **The 36 pending internal-transfer suggestions are untouched** — they wait on the first switch, which is the owner's call.
- **The ledger tooltip ("by Accounts Receivable · Sep 15 · Invoice #2 on 804")** from the mock-up is deferred: both ledgers build the label cell's title from the label's description only, and the Accounting tab is already known to time out on load, so a per-row sidecar read there is its own PR.

## Deploy

Client first (the switch renders disabled until the row exists), then `supabase db push`, then `npm run gen-types:linked` (the table and the RPC are hand-carried in `src/types/database.ts` until then, optionals written as `p_dry_run?: boolean` so the regen is a no-op at call sites). The switch ships **off**; turning it on labels the 24 and is a one-click owner decision on Banking → Accounting.
