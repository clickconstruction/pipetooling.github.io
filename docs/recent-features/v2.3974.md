## The move

The Review tab worked out its overhead rates in a 237-line effect inside the component, with the twelve-field state beside it.

- **`src/lib/people/loadReviewOverheadRates.ts`** — the scan, verbatim: the 90-day company-calendar window, the office-or-bid and field session reads, office parts with Internal Transfers out, invoices a day wide with Stripe test mode out, the person links, the pay config's two rates, and the three rates from the shared kernels. It returns the twelve figures, returns null at the two points the effect checked its cancelled flag, and throws when a read fails. `EMPTY_REVIEW_OVERHEAD_RATES` is the all-null object the tab started with and reset to.
- **`src/hooks/useReviewOverheadRates.ts`** — `useReviewOverheadRates(isDev, authUserId)`: the state and the effect's shell. Loads once per signed-in dev, `loading` while it runs, the silent all-null reset on a failure, nothing taken from a scan that was cancelled.
- **`PeopleReviewTab.tsx`** — one line where the effect was, so the effects run in the order they did; the state declaration and eleven imports are gone. Every reader of `reviewOverheadRates` is as it was. 3,610 → 3,337 lines.

One difference, in the hook's favour: the effect wrote its result in the same tick as its last cancelled check; the hook checks once more after the loader resolves, so a scan that finishes in the instant after an unmount is dropped instead of written.

## The tests — the scan's first

- **`loadReviewOverheadRates.test.ts`** (11, over a mocked client) — the window and what each read asks for, with and without an office job; a hand-built window worked into the twelve figures (office 2 h and a bid 1 h at the office rate, a field job 4 h at the field rate, $60 of parts, $1,200 invoiced: pool $120, rates 30 · 0.1 · 1); an empty window as zeros with no rate; the two cancel points; a failed read throws.
- **`useReviewOverheadRates.render.test.tsx`** (6) — no load for a non-dev or an unknown user; loading then the rates; the all-null reset; cancelled on unmount; a user change loads again and ignores the first scan; a cancelled scan changes nothing.
