# v2.3704 — Dashboard: one lien card that leads with the next deadline (2026-09-22)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Mock-up: the Claude artifact *One Lien Card* (desktop, tablet and phone boards, drawn to the real card's wrapping rules). Approved as drawn.

## Why

The owner, on the v2.3679 missed-window card: *"I don't love how this looks on the dashboard, I think it does a poor job of communicating. Instead of showing what was lost, perhaps we could show upcoming."* The first redraw led with "this week" and, on the day's real numbers, read *No lien windows close this week · $0* — the July notices were 23 days out. A card that leads with an empty bucket most days is a bad card; the second pass leads with the next deadline whatever it is.

## What shipped

- **Kernel** ([`lienDesk.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDesk.ts)) — `office.next` (`lienDeskNextDeadline`): the drafting-pile jobs whose earliest window is the soonest one — the date, how many, the dollars, their GC ids, how many are on the desk to draft and how many still wait on the owner of record. The hook fills `gcNames`.
- **The card** ([`dashboardNeedsYou.ts`](../../src/lib/dashboardNeedsYou.ts)) — `lien-notice-draft` is now the one lien card: *Next lien deadline: Oct 15 · in 23 days* (grey beyond 14 days, amber inside, red inside 7 as *4 lien windows close in 5 days · Oct 15* with *Mail by Oct 15 or the lien right on that work is gone*); one detail line — *10 notices to 4 GCs — RMC- Dudley Mason, Knight Contracting and 2 more. 9 on the desk to draft, 1 waiting on the owner of record.* — capped at two GC names; the figure is that deadline's dollars; the missed count is the card's secondary line (*5 windows closed with nothing recorded · $51,780 · note them ›*), which both Dashboard surfaces route to the desk's Missed lens. `lien-window-missed` survives only for the case with nothing upcoming, as a quiet grey card at tier 40.
- Guide *send lien notices from the Lien desk*: the card's words.
