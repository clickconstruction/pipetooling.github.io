# v2.7249 — Materials on site: a stand-in is never a price, and two fittings never share a row (2026-11-14)

Punch list #344, PR 7, on the owner's word to build the rest of the train. From the review of Materials → On site: *the price and the fold*. Both were shown by running the kernels.

## The price

`pickBookPrice` refused only a price under zero. A `material_part_prices` row at 0 won a same-day tie as the lower price and read as known and fresh, so its part added nothing to a site's worth or to what walked away, and was never asked for a price. A row at the book's stand-in, `PLACEHOLDER_PRICE` (999,999, `materialPriceIndex.ts`), priced each piece at that.

`isRealPrice` (`bookPrice.ts`) is the app's own rule for the book: above zero and under the stand-in. `pickBookPrice` skips a row that fails it and `invoicePick` refuses one, so the part reads its next source (the invoice price, a site's answer through `sitePriceLookup`, an older real book row) or *no price*, which the desk says in words since #8088. A site's answer typed by a person is not touched.

## The fold

`sameWordsKey` (`siteRollup.ts`) sorts a name's words so `PVC 2" 90` and `2 inch 90 pvc` are one row. Its guard for two sizes of one fitting only held with the `x` right between two numbers (`3x4`). With a word between them the sort swapped the sizes:

- `1/2 pex x 3/4 mip adapter` and `3/4 pex x 1/2 mip adapter` were one key, as were `3/4" MIP x 1/2" FIP` and `1/2" MIP x 3/4" FIP`.
- `4" to 2" reducer` and `2" to 4" reducer` were one key.
- The punctuation sweep took the point of a decimal: `.5" coupling` was `5" coupling`, and `1.5` was `1 5`.

Now a name that holds `x`, `by` or `to` as a word is folded word for word, in the order typed, and never sorted. A point that leads or sits inside a number stays, `0.5` reads as `.5`, and a point after a letter or at the end of a word is still punctuation.

**The cost, chosen**: the same two-ended fitting typed in another word order (`adapter 1/2 pex x 3/4 mip`) is now two rows, which **Same part** joins in one press. Two different fittings in one row had no press that split them. A name with no such word folds as before, `wye 3x4 pvc` and `PVC 3 x 4 wyes` included.

**Saved answers**: a row's key is stored in `on_site_part_ties` and `on_site_prices`, so a changed fold could strand an answer saved under the old key of such a name. Both tables were read through the dev seat before this was written and held no row.

Client only.
