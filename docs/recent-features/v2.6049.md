# v2.6049 — Materials on site: a box photo that cannot be filed says why (2026-10-29)

Punch list #209, the first fix after the four PRs went live. The end-to-end check of v2.6045 on the owner's test job 972 sent a shot to the bucket and the sweep could not file it: the job has no Drive folder. The sweep reported that only in its own response, the row stayed at *sending*, and the box read "on its way to the folder" with nothing to say why.

## What changed

- **`photo-save`**: the box pass reads every waiting row, with or without a reason; a job with no Drive folder writes `fail_why = That job has no Drive folder yet.` on the row and the sweep keeps trying each run, so setting the folder on the job is all it takes; a filed row clears `fail_why`.
- **Migration `20261029160000_photo_save_cron_any_waiting.sql`** (`docs/migrations/20261029160000_photo_save_cron_any_waiting.md`): the cron calls while any box photo waits, not only the ones without a reason.
- **The screens**: `PhotoNote` carries `failWhy`; `BoxPhotos` draws it in amber under the status on the phone's box screen and the office panel.
- Docs: `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` → photo-save, the release note and this fragment.

## Deploy

`supabase functions deploy photo-save`, then `supabase db push` (the cron). Within five minutes a waiting photo on a job without a folder reads its reason.
