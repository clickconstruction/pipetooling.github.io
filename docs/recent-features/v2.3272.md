# v2.3272 — A standing discount per customer: the rate on the card, the decision on the job (2026-09-11)

Migration [`20260911030000_standing_discount.sql`](../migrations/20260911030000_standing_discount.md). Discount tools round two, PR 3 of 4 (mockups + review: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/f56651a5-0d7b-49c3-bdc0-6bfca39ec454).

## Why

Repeat customers and GCs with a negotiated rate were re-typed on every job. The first draft inserted the row on every new job by itself; the review threw that out — a job priced from a bid that already took the cut would be cut twice with nobody asking, and "applied at creation only" is a rule nobody remembers. Now it is an offer, and a person always taps it.

## What shipped

- **Customers → Edit customer** gains **Standing discount**: a `%` box and the reason chips (Negotiated · Referral · Repeat customer · Goodwill · Price match), with the rule spelled out under it. Saved as `standing_discount_pct` / `standing_discount_reason`.
- **The offer chip** (`standingDiscountOffer`, shared kernel): while the customer has a rate, the job has no discount row of any kind, and the job wasn't waved off, the Bill tab shows *Done Right Foundation gets 5% (repeat customer, set on their card) — −$120.00 here* with **Apply** and *not on this job*. Apply adds the ordinary row (`applyStandingDiscount`; the lone empty placeholder of a new job is reused) — the trail event follows from the normal save. *Not on this job* sets `jobs_ledger.standing_discount_waived_at` (local-only on a job not yet created).
- **Bill Customer** shows the same offer as the first line of the discount strip — **Use it** fills *Take off* with the rate, picks the reason, and applies it to the whole job.
- Everything reads the columns loosely: before the push the customer select errors and the app treats that as "no standing discount".
- Help: *give a customer a discount* → "A standing discount".
- Tests: 3 kernel cases (loose read / offer rules / apply + placeholder reuse), the Bill tab chip and the strip's offer line render smokes.
