# v2.7502 — Lien desk: Sign it again on an approved notice (2026-11-16)

The office looked at an approved notice in *Approved · to print* whose signature box showed a drawn mark, and wanted the leader's electronic signature (his name in the cursive face) on it instead. Nothing on the desk could do that: a signed, approved notice had no press that took a signature off. *Not me* / *Not what I signed* shows only to the leader, and only for a code or pen signature he has not confirmed. *Pull back to draft* and *Not what I said* clear the approval too. The owner's word on the proposed press: *"Yes, build it"*.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/jobs/lienSignAgain.ts`** (new kernel, pure): `signAgainOffered` says when the press is drawn (the lien office or a leader; the notice approved, not printed, not set aside, and signed as it stands), `canClearSignatureRow` is the row rule the write filters on, `signAgainConfirmWords` / `signAgainDoneWords` / `LIEN_SIGN_AGAIN_GONE` are every word a person reads, and `signatureClearedRecord` / `fieldsWithSignatureCleared` / `signatureClearedOf` / `signatureClearedWords` are the line the notice keeps.
- **`clearLienSignatureToSignAgain`** in `src/lib/jobs/lienDeskSignIo.ts`: reads the row, then writes `LIEN_DESK_SIGNATURE_CLEAR` and the line, and nothing else. The write is filtered on what it read (`status = approved`, the same `signed_at`, `printed_at` null, `voided_at` null), so a notice that printed, went, was set aside or was signed afresh in the meantime is left alone and the person is told. The stored drawing or signed page is not deleted; the line names its path.
- **`LienDesk.tsx`**: the link *Sign it again…* (`data-lien-desk-sign-again`) in the Ready footer's row, on a computer and on a phone (one footer serves both). The confirm goes through `confirmDialog` with *Clear the signature* as its button. After the clear the desk reads again, the notice is unsigned, and the footer's existing rules draw the signing line: the leader's box with *Sign ▸* on his own screen, his line with the code on another screen (`readyBoxed`). While the notice waits, a line under the paper (`data-lien-desk-signature-cleared`) says who cleared the last signature.

## The record

`fields.signatureCleared` holds the latest clear: `at`, `by`, `byName`, and the signature that came off (`signedAt`, `signedBy`, `signerName`, `mode`, `storagePath`). It is written in the same update that clears the signature, so it never turns a standing signature stale: the row is unsigned when it lands, and the next signature's hash covers it. Like `runTakenBack`, a later draft save does not carry it forward.

## No migration

`job_lien_desk_items_guard()` rules on who may place a signature and who may confirm one; a write that nulls the signature columns passes it, as a draft save and a pull-back already do. The table's update policy admits a dev, an assistant or controller (`is_assistant()`) and a master technician.

## Left out, on purpose

- **The leader's own unconfirmed code or pen signature**: his footer already offers *Not me* / *Not what I signed*, which clears it, so the link is not drawn beside it. The office still gets the link on such a notice.
- **A printed notice**: no link. *Back to Approved · to print* comes first.
- **The retainage notice's footer**: its Ready footer has no signing line, so a cleared retainage notice could only be signed in the run window. The press waits until that footer can sign.
- **The run window's row**: the window is being rebuilt in PR #8382; the same kernel can serve a press there once it lands.

## Tests

`lienSignAgain.test.ts` (when the press is offered, the row rule, the record, every sentence, and that each passes the plain-words rules), `lienDeskSignIo.signAgain.test.ts` (the patch is the ten columns and `fields` alone, the filters, no storage delete, a row that moved is refused), and four cases in `LienDesk.render.test.tsx` (the office's press, confirm, Cancel and cleared footer; the leader's; a refused clear; no press on a printed or unsigned notice or beside *Not me*).
