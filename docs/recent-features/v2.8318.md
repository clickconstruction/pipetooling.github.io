# v2.8318 — Painted maps: the magnifying glass on Google Maps is sharper (2026-11-22)

Punch list [#387](../../to-dos/painted-maps-on-google.md), the fault seen on step 3's walk of 2026-11-22: the photograph in the glass was soft. Client only: no migration and no function.

## The fault

The glass rides above a finger that is placing a dot or a corner. Its picture is a second small Google map, kept one for each light or dark look. It was held at the big map's zoom and drawn four times the size with CSS, so at the zoom a line is drawn at the ground blurred.

## What changed

- **The ask.** The glass's map is asked for the glass's own view, two zoom steps in from the big map (`glassView`). It is the same map as before: no map is made sooner, later or more often.
- **What Google gives.** Google's map of photographs gives a zoom no deeper than its photograph goes at the spot. The glass reads back the zoom its map took and draws the picture larger only by the steps Google held back (`glassPictureScale` in `handPlacing.ts`): by one where Google gave the view, which is sharp, by two one step short, and by four where the photograph ends at the big map's zoom, which is what every glass drew before.
- **Asking again.** On ground it has not drawn before, Google learns the depth a moment after it is asked. It then draws at that depth and goes on saying the zoom it was asked for until it is asked again. So while the finger rests the glass asks again: after 150 ms, 600 ms and 1.5 s (`GLASS_ASK_AGAIN_MS`), and when the map's pictures have come in, twice at the most for a spot. It also listens for the map's own word that its zoom changed. An ask Google has already answered moves nothing and fetches nothing.
- **Streets.** The road map is asked the same way, so its street names are drawn at their own size in the glass, not four times the size.

## What Google charges

Nothing more, by its pricing pages as read on 2026-11-22. `MaxZoomService`, which the card named, is not used: the pages list no price for it and do not say it is free, and the map answers the same question by itself. The glass makes the one map it made before, and Google's price is for a map load. The pages name no charge for moving a map that is already made, and the glass moved its map on every slide before this change too. A closer view does fetch more picture tiles while a finger slides.

## What Google does past its photograph's depth

Looked at on a scratch page with Google's own script and no key, which draws Google's tiles with a watermark and bills nothing:

- Asked for zoom 22 in the middle of a city, the map took 21. Asked for 21 on open range, it took 20. It never drew an empty tile or a *no imagery* tile.
- It held its zoom through a slide of a few metres and through one of 300 metres.
- Ten asks in a row for a zoom deeper than the photograph, on ground it had already capped, made no request to Google at all.
- On ground it had not drawn before, it sometimes took the zoom asked for, drew tiles one step shallower, and still said the deeper zoom. The next ask put that right, and the map then said its zoom had changed. This is why the glass asks again.

## Checked

- `handPlacing.test.ts`: the scale for a view given, one step short, two steps short and held back further, and never smaller than one.
- `PaintedMapGoogleCanvas.render.test.tsx`, with the glass's map stood in and capped at a depth a case sets: the ask is the big map's zoom plus two; the picture is drawn by one, two and four as the depth falls; a change of zoom Google says later is followed; a spot held back is asked again and sharpens; a map that says the zoom it was asked for on new ground is asked again by the clock and when its pictures come in; the asking stops after three for a rest of the finger and when the glass comes down.
- Seen on a scratch page that mounts the real canvas with no key, at 393 px, before and after on the same crosswalk: the same ground on the same pixels under the same line, the stripes' edges crisper after, the glass's tiles at zoom 21 where they were 19. On open range with the finger resting, the glass went from a scale of one to two 600 ms in, when Google said it had taken zoom 20.

Not seen: the glass on the live site under the deploy's key, and on a real phone. Google's script with no key covers the middle of every map with a notice, which was pressed away for the pictures.
