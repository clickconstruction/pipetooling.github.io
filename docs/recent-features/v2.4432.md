# v2.4432 — Submittals: entering a reviewer's call says that nobody is emailed (2026-10-02)

No migration, no edge function, no behavior change: words only. Wendi, 2026-10-02, on BP375's row editor with *a reviewer not on the room…* picked and the email box empty: "it is not clear that it is just for a record and will not contact anyone, I am scared to add their email here".

## What entering a call does (read from the code before writing the words)

`saveItem` → `roomAndReviewerFor` in [`BidsSubmittalsTab`](../../src/components/bids/BidsSubmittalsTab.tsx): a room row if the bid has none, a `bid_submittal_people` row for a new reviewer (`how: 'named'`), the call on the row or its parts, one system line on the thread and one `decided` event. Nothing is sent.

- No trigger sits on any `bid_submittal_*` table, and no SQL function that reads the people table posts anywhere.
- One edge function emails a person on the room: `send-submittal-reply-email`, called only by `replyToRoom` when the office answers a question that person asked in the room. A person named for an entered call has asked nothing.
- `get-submittal-room` never selects `email`, so the GC's room shows the reviewer's name and never the address.
- The address is the person's key on the room (`NOT NULL`, unique per room on `lower(email)`): the room matches a later visitor to the same person by it, which is why the box stays required.

## What changed

- [`SubmittalReviewerPicker`](../../src/components/bids/SubmittalReviewerPicker.tsx), shared by the row editor and *They approved all of it…*: a quiet line under the picker (`data-testid="reviewer-no-contact"`), *Only for the record. Nobody is emailed or contacted.* With *a reviewer not on the room…* picked it goes on: *The email stays in the office. It tells the room who they are if they open it later.* The box's hint reads *their email*.
- [`SubmittalItemEditDialog`](../../src/components/bids/SubmittalItemEditDialog.tsx): the line beside *Enter a call from their PDF or email* opens with *This only records their call. Nobody is emailed.*
- Guide *build a submittal package*: the same sentences where it tells you to type a name and email, and *Nobody is emailed.* on the approve-all paragraph.
