# v2.8496 — On a phone, the presses inside a bid's window are easier to tap (2026-11-24)

The follow-up to v2.8464 (punch list #426, `to-dos/my-bids-refresh/README.md`, *The walk in the lens frame*). The live look at v2.8464 measured three more presses under 44 px inside the Bid window. Reading the window found three more of the same two kinds, which the lens frame's invented bid does not draw. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was measured

The Bid window, opened from My Bids inside the lens frame as the estimator, at 430 by 932. *Before* is clicktooling.com on v2.8483. *After* is this branch on a local server. Sizes in px, wide by tall:

| Press | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An *Open in Bids* tab, **Bid Board** | 76 by 26.5 | 76 by 44 |
| **Map** | 27 by 16 | 44 by 44 |
| The GC's name, *Sample Contracting* | 144 by 23 | 144 by 45 |
| **Show all fields** | 84 by 19 | 84 by 45 |
| A *Files & links* chip | not drawn by the frame's bid | 44 tall by the style |
| The development's line | not drawn by the frame's bid | 44 tall by the style |

After the change the GC's line was still 22.8 px tall with *no contact on file* straight under it, and the page had no sideways scroll.

## What changed

All of it is in `BidPreviewModal.tsx` and `src/lib/bids/bidWindowPhonePress.ts`, at a phone's width (`useNarrowViewport640`, 640 px and under). A desk gets nothing: each function answers one frozen empty object there.

- **The chips** (`bidWindowChipPress`). An *Open in Bids* tab and a *Files & links* chip take a 44 px floor. Their rows grow with them. A chip with no link (*Plans — none*) is not a press and takes the same floor, so a row of both stays even.
- **The presses in a line of words** (`bidWindowLinePress`). **Map**, the development's line, the GC's name and **Show all fields** take padding above and below that brings them to 44 px, and a margin that gives the same back, so the line stays where it was drawn. **Map** takes the same to each side, since it is 27 px wide. The numbers are `thumbInLine`'s from `phonePress.ts`.
- **Why padding and not `thumbInLine`'s minimum height.** A GC's name can wrap to two lines, which is already 45 px. A minimum height would add nothing to it while the margins still took 21 px back, and the second line would sit on the contact under it. Padding grows with the lines. It also works on **Map**, an anchor in a line, which takes no minimum height.
- **`BID_WINDOW_LINE_DRAWN`** holds what each press measured, rounded down, so a press comes out at 44 px or a little over and never under.
- The development's line was drawn with `marginTop: 4`. It is now the shorthand `margin: '4px 0 0'`, so the phone's margin replaces it whole and React never sees a shorthand beside its own longhand.

## Left as found

- **Further down the same window**, in the pieces it shares with other screens: **Ask the estimators** and **Ask Dispatch** are 30 px tall, the note filters (**All**, **Bid**, **Customer**, **Reports**) 25.5, and **+ bid note** and **+ customer note** 21 to 25.5.
- The Bid window's title is squeezed beside its three top presses at 430 px, as v2.8464 said.
- Two presses in a line 20 px apart cannot both be 44 px tall without overlapping. **Map** and the development's line under it share a band about 24 px tall, and the development's line takes a touch there.

- `BidPreviewModal.phonePress.render.test.tsx` (+3, 5 in all), on a bid with an address, a development, a GC and one file link: the chips, the four presses in a line, and a desk.
