## What it caught, fixed here

- **"sent September 4"** — the statement took `sent_to_customer_at` (the delivery / re-send stamp) before `billed_at` (the day the bill went out, August 18). Now `billed_at` first; the ch. 28 interest date moves with it (September 23, not October 10).
- **No due date** — the row's `estimated_bill_date` was null though the Stripe bill said September 5. `DemandInvoiceSource.stripe.dueYmd` (from `get-stripe-invoice-details` `due_date`, company calendar) fills it.
- **"Invoice #0"** on the notice's enclosure — the primary bill has `sequence_order` 0 and the physical-invoice document renders `#0`. `fallbackInvoiceNumber()` reads the job number for sequence 0 (both the statement and the enclosure title); the enclosure's date is the bill date, not the delivery date.
- *"This letter is Click Plumbing and Electrical final formal demand"* → *… Electrical's …*.
