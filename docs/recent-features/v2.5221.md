# v2.5221 — The Bridge: This week, the moves that change the two lines, up top (2026-10-10)

PR 5 of the Bridge's redraw (plan and mock-up at https://claude.ai/artifact/2GciUJPCjaTysjpTEULWyJ). Client only, dev only. No migration, no edge function.

- **What was wrong**: the page's most useful panel, *What would change it*, sat at the bottom of the page under the bills and receipts lists, and it knew nothing of the late invoices the forecast leans on (v2.5219) or the jobs with no price that make the day grid red.
- **The kernel** (`src/lib/bridge/thisWeek.ts`, `buildThisWeek`): the moves in order — bill the finished work (the unbilled total and job count, the door to Stages), chase the late invoices (count and dollars of receipts whose `basis` is `late`, the door to Billing), approve the pending hours (with the weekly payroll they hold down, the door to the approvals queue), price the worked jobs with no contract (named, first three), set % complete on the jobs assumed half done (the earned dollars on the guess), one more field day a week, cut overhead 10% (the door to People → Overhead), and up to three bids due — each a `Move` with a title, its why, the lever text and tone, and its door.
- **The page** (`Bridge.tsx`): `BridgeThisWeek.tsx` sits beside *Fix the reading* in one row under the top line; the old panel is gone from the bottom grid, which keeps *Bills coming due* and *Receipts expected*.
- **Tests**: `thisWeek.test.ts` (3: the empty case, every move with its words, lever and door on 2026-10-10's numbers, a bid with no margin) and `BridgeThisWeek.render.test.tsx` (2).
- **Docs**: guide *read the Bridge* gains "This week" and loses the old panel's example; the Glossary's Bridge entry.
