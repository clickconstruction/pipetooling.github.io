# v2.3849 — Lien letters and notices name the job's trade (2026-09-26)

The owner, 2026-09-26, reading the retainage cover letter: *"does the 'plumbing' switch based on the service type like electrical and hvac?"* It did not. Client only, no migration.

## What was wrong

Counsel's four § 53.056 letters and the paid-out letter (`gcOnNotice.ts`) said *"We are the plumbing contractor…"*, *"We did the plumbing on your home…"*, *"We installed the plumbing…"*; the retainage letter (`lienNoticeDraft.ts`) said *"We are the plumbing contractor…"*; the form's *Type of labor or materials* defaulted to *"Plumbing labor and materials"* and the affidavit's work description fell back to the same. Counsel wrote "plumbing" because Click is a plumber, not as a choice against the job's trade. Every job has a service type (`jobs_ledger.service_type_id` → `service_types.name`: Plumbing, Electrical, …) — the lien paper never read it. The form's line was the one thing the office could retype per job (the guide even said "an electrical job says so here"); the letter beside it still said plumbing.

## The change

- **`lienTradeWords(serviceTypeName)`** (`src/lib/jobs/lienTradeWords.ts`, pure): `contractor` ("plumbing contractor" / "electrical contractor" / "HVAC contractor"), `work` ("the plumbing" / "the electrical work"), `installed` ("installed the plumbing" / "did the electrical work" — counsel's residential verb stays on a plumbing job) and `laborMaterials` ("Electrical labor and materials"). An acronym stays upper-case; blank or missing reads as plumbing.
- **Three new fills** on `COVER_LETTER_FILLS` — `{{trade_contractor}}`, `{{trade_work}}`, `{{trade_installed}}` — in the five letters' openings; `fillCoverLetter` resolves them from `fills.trade` (the service type name). A run's letter is written once and stored per item with its fills unresolved, so one run across a plumbing job and an electrical job prints each job's own trade. Letters already stored on items keep their literal words.
- **`LienNoticeJobFacts.serviceTypeName`** and **`LienAffidavitJobFacts.serviceTypeName`** drive the form's default type of labor and the affidavit's work-description fallback; **`lienRetainageCoverLetter`** takes `trade`.
- **`LienDeskJob.service_type`** — `LIEN_DESK_JOB_COLUMNS` embeds `service_type:service_types(name)`; the desk, the GC run (which reads the same columns) and the retainage pane pass it at every form and letter call (`lienDeskRun`, `gcNoticePreview`, `LienDeskModal`, `LienDeskAffidavitPane`, `LienDeskRetainagePane`, `GcOnNoticeModal`).
- **Guide**: *send lien notices from the Lien desk* — the form's default follows the service type; the letters name the trade; the trade fills listed.
