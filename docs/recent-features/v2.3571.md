# v2.3571 — The `tel:` sweep: one phone reading for every hand-rolled link (2026-09-17)

The "shared call button sweep" residual from `to-dos/customer-waiting-residuals.md` (v2.3247 made `CallPhoneButton` + `phoneContact.ts` the first shared `tel:` affordance; ~35 hand-rolled links with five sanitizers remained). A mechanical sweep, cut from fresh `main` and merged alone per `CLAUDE.md`.

## The change

- [`phoneContact.ts`](../../src/lib/phoneContact.ts) gains `telHrefFor(raw)`: `phoneContact(raw)?.telHref` when the number is dialable (US ten digits → `tel:+1…`, international as typed), else `tel:` + the typed digits and `+` — what the swept links produced before, so no link goes blank. Two tests.
- [`scripts/codemods/tel-href-sweep.mjs`](../../scripts/codemods/tel-href-sweep.mjs) rewrites every `href={\`tel:${…}\`}` (JSX) and `href="tel:${…}"` (the HTML builders) to `telHrefFor(<the phone expression>)`, unwrapping the five sanitizers — `.replace(/[^+\d]/g, '')`, `.replace(/[^\d+]/g, '')`, `.replace(/[^0-9+]/g, '')`, `encodeURIComponent(…)`, `esc(…)` — and adds the import. Idempotent; `--dry` lists the sites. The rebase rule applies: re-run it, never hand-resolve its diff.
- **34 links in 25 files**: Customers, CustomerDetail, Prospects (×4), Workflow, the Customer profile and contacts, Team prospects, People (Users rows, App activity, Settings → People ×2), Supply house directory (×2) and job-accounts roster, Jobs (the Stages row and card list, Payment chase), Quickfill (Can't reach ×2, Vehicle odometers), the stale-tally follow-up, five Bids surfaces (Call queue, Builder review ×2, Waiting to hear, Why we lost, the builder call session), and the two printed sheets (`builderCallSheet.ts`, `followupSheet.ts`).
- Skipped on purpose: the four links that read `fieldDispatchPhone`'s `telHref` (already one reading, `+1…` digits), `CallPhoneButton` and the kernel itself.

The anchors' text and styles are untouched: the number still displays as typed. What changed is only the `href` — a US number now dials `tel:+15125550142` where it dialed `tel:5125550142`, `tel:(512) 555-0142` or `tel:512%20555-0142` depending on the file. Four test expectations that pinned the old hrefs — three on the printed sheets, one on the Quickfill odometer smoke — were updated to the `+1` form.

## Verified

Kernel and sheet-builder tests; the full suite. On the dev server the Supply house directory's call link and a Customers row link both read `tel:+1…`.
