# v2.7532 — Collections: Move to Collections shows the money, and what is owed reads the bills (2026-11-16)

U2 and U3 of the fixes that followed a lien packet stating two amounts (v2.7517, v2.7520, v2.7530), in one change because they share one kernel. The owner's words: a job with three stages was moved to collections, and collections did not count the percent complete on a stage against the percent billed. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

- A job's price (`jobs_ledger.revenue`) can sit above its sent bills: one stage billed of three, the rest never done. Percent complete (`pct_complete`) never entered any owed figure, and no window set it beside percent billed.
- The Collect desk and the Pipeline's totals read the bills. Three windows still took the job's price less its payments: the Mark Uncollectible confirm (*$X open.*), the Dashboard's plain job card (*Remaining:*), and, upstream of both, Move to Collections, which showed no money at all. So the moment a job was flagged, nobody was told that most of what its price said was owed had never been billed.

## What changed

- **One split.** `src/lib/jobs/jobOwedSplit.ts`: `jobOwedSplit(job)` returns what the sent bills still owe (`lienBilledOpen`, the payment rule every bill reader shares), the part of the price on no sent bill (`lienUnbilled`), percent billed (the sent bills over the price) and percent complete. `jobOwedLines` says each in one sentence, and the last two only when something is unbilled.
- **Move to Collections** (`StagesCollectionsConfirmModal`) shows the split before the press (`data-stages-collections-owed`): *$2,030 billed and still open.* and, when part of the job is on no bill, an amber note: how much of the job's price is not billed, *The job is 30% complete and 41% billed.*, and what to do: Collections chases what is billed, and if the rest will not be done the job total is corrected on the job. *Send back to Billed* shows no money, as before.
- **Mark Uncollectible** (`StagesUncollectibleConfirmModal`) names what the sent bills owe, with the unbilled part apart, in place of the price less payments.
- **The Dashboard's job card.** A job with two or more billed bills gets a plain card and then a card per bill. `dashboardJobCardMoney` (`dashboardBillingInvoiceUnits.ts`) sums what those bills still owe and the card reads *Billed and still open: $2,030.00 · $25,150.00 of the job is on no open bill*. A job with no billed bill, billed as one shell, keeps *Remaining:*: its price less payments is what is owed. The Ready to Bill section's *Remaining:* is unbilled work and is unchanged.

## Not changed

- Record payment's default amount on a job still offers the price less payments: a payment may rightly be more than what is billed.
- The Dashboard's *Not billed* total still reads Working and Ready to Bill jobs only. Adding a Billed job's unbilled remainder changes a headline total and is left for its own change.
- The Collections row's note for a claim set by hand (`collectionsNoteLine`, mirrored in the edge copy), the owner-share chip and the in-kind windows.
- No stage is billed by percent: an AIA pay application is still the only bill by percent complete.
