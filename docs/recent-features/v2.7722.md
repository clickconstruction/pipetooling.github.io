# v2.7722 — Edit Job: the Stripe memo read is its own piece (2026-11-17)

Client only; no migration, no edge function, no screen change. Punch list **#46** row 4 (`to-dos/decomposition-queue.md`): the Job form's second pass, cut 3 (the map's row 12), after v2.7718 and v2.7721.

## What shipped

- **`lib/jobs/stripeMemoBackfill.ts`**, new, pure. The form wrote one test three times: *a bill Stripe hosts, with a pay link, whose memo or footer has not been read back*.
  - `stripeBillLacksMemoOrFooter` — that test, whatever the bill's status.
  - `invoiceNeedsStripeMemoBackfill` and `stripeMemoBackfillTargets` — the same, for sent bills only. This is what the read on open asks about.
  - `stripeMemoBackfillKey` — the job and the sorted ids of the bills that wait, null when none does. The read is keyed on it, so a job read again with the same bills waiting does not ask Stripe again.
- **`hooks/useStripeMemoBackfill.ts`**, new: the form's first effect and its key memo, moved as written. For each waiting bill it calls `get-stripe-invoice-details`, one after the other, then reads the job again and shows it. It is called where the effect always ran, so the order of the form's effects is unchanged.
- **`JobFormModal.tsx`**: the memo and the effect are one call, and the bill view's close loop reads `stripeBillLacksMemoOrFooter`. 56 lines out.

## What the tests pinned, unchanged

- The bill view's close loop has never tested the bill's status; the read on open does. The kernel keeps the two apart.
- A bill whose memo never comes is asked about once per open, not on every re-read.
- A form closed in the middle of the read stops: no further call, no re-read, nothing set.

## Tests

- `stripeMemoBackfill.test.ts`, 14 cases.
- `useStripeMemoBackfill.render.test.tsx`, 9 cases: the calls in order, the body and the token, a dev's own Stripe mode, no token, the same key not asking twice, a job the re-read cannot find, a close mid-read.
- `JobFormModal.stripeMemo.render.test.tsx`, 6 cases on the form itself: the read on open and what it shows, and the bill view's close loop at one, two and three tries. It passes unchanged on the form before the cut, and six wiring mutants each fail at least one case.
