# v2.7367 — Lien desk: To do scrolls as one page, and the find box stays with you (2026-11-15)

Client only. Punch list #362, step 1 of B's three. Taunya's go of 2026-11-15, on the mock-up redrawn with the Board in place: build all of B. The Board (v2.7332) took the top of To do and is the most important pane; the list under it was pinned in what was left.

## What was wrong

`lienDoNowFrame` kept the frame `pinned` on a computer: the Board, the find box, then the list as its own scroller in the remainder. Measured on prod data, 2026-11-15: at 1440 x 900 the frame is 751 px, the Board 614 and the find box 47, so the list had 91 px, about two rows. At 1024 x 768 the Board is 683 px in a 615 px frame, and the list started below the bottom of the screen. The frame went to one page only where the Board's columns stack, under 760 px.

## What landed

- `src/lib/jobs/lienDoNowFrame.ts`: `lienDoNowFrame(oneColumn, room)` takes the measured heights. `lienDoNowListRoom` is the frame less the Board and the bar; under `LIEN_DO_NOW_MIN_LIST_PX` (320, about five rows) a computer is `one-page` too, and with nothing measured it stays `pinned`. `lienDoNowBarStuck` says when the bar has reached the top of the scroller, and `lienDoNowListTop` where the page stands with the Board just out of view.
- `src/components/jobs/useLienDoNowRoom.ts`: the frame's, the Board's and the bar's heights, read in a layout effect and again from a `ResizeObserver` on the frame and the Board. The mode does not move what is measured, so a reading never flips it back.
- `src/lib/jobs/lienDoNowBar.ts`: `lienDoNowBarParts(board)`, the Board's three counts in the tiles' own words with the tiles' doors (`lienBoardDots`), the money and a zero left out.
- `src/components/jobs/LienDoNowBar.tsx` wraps the find box (`data-lien-do-now-bar`, `data-pins`, `data-stuck`). Where the frame is one page on a computer it is `position: sticky` at the top of the frame. Stuck, it draws the counts beside the find (a count with a door is a press, `data-lien-do-now-bar-part`), or what a pressed bin narrowed the list to with *Show every row* (`lienBoardPickWords`), and *↑ The Board*, which scrolls the frame to its top. Not stuck, and wherever the Board stays put, it is the find box alone.
- `LienDesk.tsx`: `doNowRoom` into `lienDoNowFrame`; `doNowBarPins` (one page on the wide desk; a phone and the stacked desk keep the find box in the page); `openBoardTile`, the tiles' press, shared with the bar; a pressed bin scrolls the frame to `lienDoNowListTop` where the bar pins, and brings the first group into view as before where it does not (a phone, the stacked desk).
- The Board guide gains *Scroll down to the list*; the architecture map's note on the frame says the rule.

## The one thing "is this the best we can do" changed

The first cut made every computer one page. On a screen tall enough to hold the Board and a working list together (measured at 1440 x 1500: a 1,351 px frame, a 691 px list) that would have taken away the one arrangement where a pressed bin narrows the list in sight of the picture. So the room decides: under 320 px for the list the page scrolls as one, and over it the Board stays put.

A pressed bin first brought the bar itself into view. Deep in a long list the bar is already at the top, so nothing moved and the narrowed list began wherever the reader stood. The page now goes to the Board's bottom edge, so the list starts right under the bar from anywhere.
