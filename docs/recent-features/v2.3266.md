# v2.3266 — Job Summary hygiene chip: overhead landed on jobs the HCP # floor hides (2026-09-10)

Client only; no migration.

## Why

Verifying the Overhead column (2026-09-10, 12-month window, the app default 30 days · 20% carry · 14-day idle cap): an independent re-computation of the spread over the ledger tied to the dials strip to the cent (pool $115,601 = by hours $89,725 + carry $22,431 + in flight $3,445), and every visible row matched — but the *Overhead charged* tile read $100,316 while the strip's by hours + carry summed to $112,156. The $11,840 difference had landed on eight jobs under the default HCP # floor of 500 (J234–J473, real 2026 field hours on legacy-numbered jobs). The pool rightly spreads over their hours; the tile rightly sums shown rows; nothing on the page said so.

## What shipped

- `jobSummaryHygiene(ledger, settings, listedJobIds?)` gains `offListUsd` / `offListJobs`: the day-share landed on jobs whose id is not in the list the page enriches (the HCP # floor drops them server- and client-side before enrichment; a job deleted since its sessions would land here too). Without a list both read 0. Test: no list → 0; every charged job listed → 0; one charged job dropped → exactly its share, counted once, reconciliation unchanged; the smoothed settings agree.
- `useJobSummaryView` passes the listed job ids (a memoized `Set` over the rows input).
- `JobSummaryLedgerToolbar`: after the *in flight* chip, a muted chip — *$11,840 of overhead landed on 8 jobs this list leaves out (HCP # floor) — charged, not shown* — under the Day-share method only (the lenses do not use the allocation), behind `showMoney`. The title explains the floor and points at the control under the table.
- Help guide `read-true-profit-on-job-summary.md`: the chips sentence names the new chip.

## Not changed

The allocation, every row's Overhead, the tile's definition (shown rows), and the dials strip. Whether the floor should also drop those hours from the spread is an owner call the chip makes visible rather than takes.

## Verified

Typecheck · lint · `npm test` · build; live as dev on the 12-month window: the chip reads $11,840 on 8 jobs; dropping the floor to −1 (show all) makes it disappear and the tile rise to the strip's total.
