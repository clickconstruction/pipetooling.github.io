# v2.6263 — Edit report shows the photos and takes more (punch list #229, PR 4 of six) (2026-11-01)

Punch list #229, PR 4, on PR 1's hook (v2.6255). The author may edit a report within the edit window (Settings → My Reports → **Edit**), and the form showed the fields and nothing else: no photo seen, added or taken back. The card's owner call 2 taken as recommended: more photos, and a saved photo stays. No migration, no edge function.

## What changed

- **`ReportEditModal`**: under the fields, `ReportPhotosStrip` draws the report's photos as the card does (the thumbnail of one waiting, the Drive door of one filed), with a foot line on a job's report, *A photo already on the report stays. Take one back in the job's Drive folder.* (`REPORT_PHOTO_WORDS.savedStays`). Below it, on a job's report, `useReportPhotos` with no stages: the Add a photo tile (the camera on a phone for the field roles), the tiles, *New photo · 4.0 MB → 293 KB · 1600 px*. Save reads what it adds (`reportEditSaveWords`: *Save · 1 photo added*, *Sending 1 photo…* and held while a shot is on its way) and, after the fields save, ties each sent shot to the report captioned by the first long field's first words. Cancel, × and the backdrop drop a sent shot the report does not hold yet. A report on a project or a bid shows its photos and no tile.
- **`ReportForEdit.job_ledger_id`**: Settings → My Reports passes the row's job, so the form knows whether to offer the tile. The Dashboard's Recent reports mounts the form but no longer opens it (its state is set only to null), so nothing there changes.
- **`ReportPhotosStrip.foot`**: a node drawn under the tiles when there are any.
- **Guides**: *take photos for a report* gains **Add a photo to a saved report**. The Glossary's **Report photos** entry gains its line in the card's closing docs PR, since five PRs amending one line would conflict in turn.
- **Tests**: `ReportEditModal.photos.render.test.tsx` (new, 2); `reportPhotos.test.ts` (+1).

## Decisions

- **No taking back on the form.** The delete policy on `report_photos` allows only an unsaved row, and a filed photo lives in Drive with the bucket copy gone; a policy and a sweep that delete a filed photo are the card's owner call 2, a migration and an edge change if the owner wants them.
- **The Stage progress line is not rewritten.** The edit form writes the template's fields only, as it did before this PR, so a photo added here is counted on the card by the strip and not in the line's words.
