# v2.5526 — Job Mode: a helper on the crew leaves a note for the office (2026-10-14)

A finding from the prod field walk after the residential build, by Customer communication helper 10, at cc's ask. The Sample helper, on the ZZ job's crew and clocked in, pressed **Message the customer**, then **Something else**, and got *You don't have permission to leave the note for the office*. The helper also could not read the Sample primary's note in Job updates.

- **Why**: the field branch of `jobs_ledger_thread_notes`' insert and select policies checked the crew with an `EXISTS` on `jobs_ledger_team_members` under the caller's RLS. That table's SELECT policy leaves helpers out, so only a dispatch block on the job let a helper through.
- **Migration `20261014220000_helper_on_job_thread_notes.sql`**:
  - `helper_on_job(job)`, SECURITY DEFINER with `row_security = off`: on the job's crew, or a schedule block on it this week or last (Chicago, Monday to Sunday). Revoked from anon.
  - Both policies are recreated from `20260907050000` with the crew `EXISTS` replaced by `helper_on_job`. The branch's own any-day dispatch check stays, so nobody loses access. The office branch and the restrictive fences are untouched.
- **SQL bed** `helper-on-job` (`scripts/pgtest-helper-on-job.sh`, `supabase/tests/helper_on_job/`): a crew-only helper writes and reads the note, a helper on no job does neither, a sub dispatched three weeks ago and taken off the crew keeps reading, the office reads both, and anon may not call the function.
- **Docs**: `docs/migrations/20261014220000_helper_on_job_thread_notes.md`, and a line in `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`'s helpers section.
- **Push**: the lead pushes the migration after the PR lands. No client change.
