# v2.6368 — Job photos for the field: the Job Mode card's Photos line, the job window's door and the thread line (punch list #244, PR 4) (2026-11-02)

Punch list #244's fourth PR, on PR 3's window (v2.6365). Field roles rarely see the Pipeline, so the card's call 2 put their door on the Job Mode card; call 5 put one quiet line on the job's thread.

## What changed

- **The Job Mode card** (`components/jobmode/JobModePhotosLine.tsx`, mounted by `JobModeDetailsSection` under the stage checklists): one line, **Photos · 14 ▸**, from one head count of `uploaded_photos` for the job (`countJobUploadedPhotos`), **Photos · add the first ▸** with none, *Photos* alone while unread; a press opens the Job photos window as a full sheet with the camera first for the field roles; the count reads again when the window closes. The card hands the job's number and name when it has them (the clocked-in job); the next job's card hands the id and address, and the window reads the job's folder links itself (`loadJobPhotoLinks`, a new `links` read on the window's io) when a door hands none.
- **The job window's Documents tab** (`JobDocumentsPhotos.tsx`): the Photos count line is now the door to the Job photos window (it was the strip's viewer), **Add a photo** sits beside it, and a job with no folder still offers it, since the first photo makes the folder. The strip under the address keeps its own viewer.
- **The thread line**: after the press the window writes one line on the job's thread through `postJobThreadNoteBody`, *Added 2 photos: Cracked flange under the second lav; Meter location* (`threadLineWords`: the count, then the photos with words, named by their caption), best-effort, as the author; no push, no email.
- The guide *add a photo to a job and find it later* gains the Job Mode card and the Documents tab doors and the thread line; the Glossary's *Job photos* and `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`'s Documents tab line are amended.
