# v2.7825 — Materials on site: the app keeps who changed a box (2026-11-18)

The second PR of the train on seeing what one person does with the material on a site. Two migrations, `20261118140000_on_site_box_changes.sql` and `20261118150000_person_watches.sql`. No client change and no edge function.

## Why

The By person look (v2.7818) reads every press by who made it, but three kinds of change left no person behind: a box retired or brought back, a box's kind, where line or words edited in place, and a part tie or a site price deleted. The office also asked to follow one person's presses closely while that person keeps their access. Both need ground in the database before a screen can read them.

## What landed

- **`job_container_changes`**: a trigger on `job_containers` writes one insert-only row for each of a retire, a bring-back, and a change to the kind, the where line or the words, with the old value, the new one, who and when.
- **`on_site_removed_answers`**: a trigger on `on_site_part_ties` and `on_site_prices` keeps a deleted row as it stood, with who took it off. A row that goes with its job or development is not kept.
- **`person_watches`**: a watch a dev turns on for one person: why, who to tell, who set it, who lifted it. One live watch a person, lifted and never deleted. Devs alone read it.
- **`watched_box_opens`** and **`on_site_note_box_open`**: the box screens will call the function for everyone, and it writes a row only for a watched caller, at most once a box each ten minutes. It answers nothing either way, and a refusal by the training-mode fence is swallowed, so the screen cannot tell a watched person from anyone else.
- Nothing changes what anyone may do, and no client reads any of it yet.

## The limits, said plainly

- A box opened is reported by the phone's own screen. A person who takes material without opening the box in the app leaves no row; only a count shows that.
- The watch is a mark and a list of who to tell. Telling them, and the chip on the By person look, are the next PRs.

## Checks

No SQL bed in this PR: the two files add tables, triggers and one function, and change no existing rule. Each was desk-checked against the tables it reads, and the push is followed by a read of the new objects on prod.
