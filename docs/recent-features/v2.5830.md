# v2.5830 — Quickfill: Focus no longer reloads its folded sections over and over (2026-10-24)

A fix to the Focus view ([v2.5778](v2.5778.md), punch list #147 PR 1). Measured on 2026-10-24 on plain main 3e7104ff6, a local Vite on prod signed in as the dev: one tab on `/quickfill?view=focus` made **1,765 remote requests in 3 s** after it settled (13,450 in a 10 s window), about 45 % `clock_sessions`, then `salary_work_schedule_templates`, `supply_houses`, `service_types`, `supply_house_job_accounts`, `supply_house_invoices`, `users`. Left open, the tab logged `ERR_INSUFFICIENT_RESOURCES` and lost its auth. Page read 118 and Roller 96 in the same window, so the loop was Focus's alone. Found by the Texts station's session (punch list #157) while it checked its PR live.

## The loop

The fold meant to keep a station's body mounted, hidden, so a reporter inside it kept its count. It drew the body in another place in the tree: the open station's body was a fragment at the station div's second child, the fold's a `<div>` there, so React unmounted the body and mounted a new one on every fold. A body that loads its own count (Supply Houses, People Hours, Unassigned field time and the clock strip and salary prefetch they mount; map quirk 18) then loaded again and reported *loading*, which reopened it (`focusBucket` never folds on a count that is not in), which remounted it again, until the load came back 0 and folded it, which remounted it… Each turn is one full read of the station. A render smoke of the old wrapper with one such body counted **10,481 mounts and 10,481 loads in 50 ms**.

## The fix, two layers

- **The fold keeps the body** (`src/pages/Quickfill.tsx`, `QuickfillSectionWrapper`): both drawings put the body in one place, `stationBody(folded)`, the station div's second child holding the banner, the children and the foot mark button; the fold hides that div. A fold now changes the station's first line and the body's `display`, nothing else. The body's DOM gains that one plain div in Page view too, with no style, so nothing moves.
- **The kernel holds the last count** (`src/lib/quickfill/views.ts`): `FocusInput.lastCount` and `keepFocusCounts(prev, metrics)`, the last count each station reported this visit with nothing loading. `focusBucket` folds on it while the station reloads or its reporter is gone; the page holds it in `focusLastCountsRef`. Alone it bounds the old wrapper to one remount per fold (2 mounts in the same smoke, not 10,481); with the first layer it also keeps a folded station from opening and closing while it checks again.
- **Tests**: `views.test.ts` (a zero station stays folded when its reporter unmounts and when it loads again; work coming in opens it; `keepFocusCounts` takes only settled counts and hands back the same map when nothing changed), and the new `src/pages/Quickfill.focusFold.render.test.tsx` (the page's wrapper exported for it, the kernel's bucket, a body that loads its own count: folded at zero with one mount and one load, Open now shows the same body without a second load, a station with work stays open).
- **Docs**: `docs/QUICKFILL_ARCHITECTURE.md`'s Focus dossier (the derived bucket, the wrapper's fold), the wrapper's dossier and quirk 18 amended. The guide's words already said a folded section opens in place; they are now true, so the guide is unchanged.

## Verified live

The fixed build on a local Vite against prod, as the dev, a 1400 × 900 viewport, 3 s windows after 15 s to settle, nothing pressed: **Focus 0** (twice, the first 3 s of its load read 339, so the counter counts), **Page 0**, **Roller 0**. Focus read *16 groups with work · 9 at zero · 3 doors* and held its 12 folded lines, each with its body mounted and hidden. No console errors.

## Verify

Quickfill as the dev on a desk, `?view=focus`. Wait for the headline under the search, then watch the network panel for a few seconds: nothing should keep loading. The sections at zero stay folded. **Open now** shows a section's body at once, as it was. Never leave a tab on an older build's Focus view, and never press Mark on prod while checking.
