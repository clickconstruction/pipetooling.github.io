# v2.5694 — Supply Houses: an aging cell's bills sit under their job, with its job account in words (2026-10-20)

The owner, opening Moore Supply's 90+ cell: *"When I open the boxes for supply houses to see what is
and what is not on a job account, I do not like how this looks."* Every row wore the same dashed
**+ Job acct** chip, standing for six different states with none of them in words; a job account
belongs to a property, but the list was by invoice, so one job's state repeated on every row; and
long job names wrapped to four lines. Two layouts were mocked up on the cell's real rows; the owner
picked **A · By job**. Builds on v2.5673 (the cell modal), v2.5675 (the chip as an act) and v2.5677
(the move window).

## What shipped

- **Kernel** [`agingCellByJob.ts`](../../src/lib/materials/agingCellByJob.ts) (tested):
  `groupAgingCellByJob` groups a cell's rows per (job, house) for invoices on exactly one job,
  ordered by the dollars not on a job account, largest first; split invoices, invoices on no job
  and credit memos are groups of their own. Each job's state — `all_on`, `open`, `requested`,
  `not_needed`, `none`, `loading` — comes from its rows and its `job_supply_house_accounts` row.
  `agingCellAccountSplit` sums on vs not (credits in neither), `agingCellJobStateWords` and
  `agingCellJobsLine` say it.
- **The window** ([`SupplyHousesTab.tsx`](../../src/components/SupplyHousesTab.tsx)): the table
  gives way to classed grid rows (`.aging-win-*` in `src/index.css`, one phone breakpoint):
  - **The strip** (every cell but credits and the movable list): *$X on job accounts · n of N ·
    the owners' debt* beside *$Y not on one · m of N · Click's debt*, a two-part bar, and the
    jobs line (*"7 jobs. None has a job account at Moore Supply yet."*).
  - **A heading per job**: number and name, *n invoices · $total* (with *k of n on it* when it
    mixes), the state pill in words, and its press — **Mark opened…** for none/asked (the shared
    v2.3423 sheet; from a heading it only records the account, the move modal's copy still flags
    its invoice on an open save), and a quiet **Flag anyway…** for none/asked/not needed when a
    past-due bill is unflagged. That keeps to-do #132's call 5 (a past-due bill may be flagged with
    no account on record, never a block) without a band on every row: the press unfolds **Mark it
    on** on that job's past-due rows, **Done** folds it. Call 6 (one press per row, no batch) holds
    too.
  - **A row per bill**: invoice # (mono), *PO · date*, the age (*116 d* chip past due, *due M/D*
    while current), the amount, the row's state — **On it ✓** (press to unflag), **Mark it on**
    (open account, or Flag anyway unfolded; past due), **Move it…** (still movable: the PR 3 move modal; the *movable
    until* chip folds into its title, the date already sits in the age column) or the asked
    chip — and ↗ to the invoice.
  - The every-house cell and the movable list keep their house lines (rep, **✉ Email … the
    list ›**); the footer sticks flush (`bottom: -1.5rem` + the negative margin), so no row
    shows through under it.
- **Retired**: the per-row amber band (its two presses moved to the job heading), the footer's
  job-acct line (the strip says it), and `agingCellChipState` / `agingCellBandWords` with their
  tests.
- **Token**: `--bg-teal-tint` (light `#ccfbf1`, dark `#11312e`) for the *on* pill.
- **Guides**: [mark an invoice as on a job account](../../src/content/help/mark-an-invoice-on-a-job-account.md)
  gains *Work a cell by job*; [mark supply house bills paid](../../src/content/help/mark-supply-house-bills-paid.md)
  redraws *See the bills inside an aging cell*.

Verified on the local build against prod data as the sample controller (Reece 1–30 mixed,
Moore 90+, Reece credits, the movable list, phone width, both themes); no write was pressed.
