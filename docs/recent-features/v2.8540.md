# v2.8540 — Bills sent: a dev who shows ZZ test jobs can say how a test bill went (2026-11-24)

A fix for what the live walk of punch list #340 found on 2026-11-24. No migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

v2.8445 took every bill on a ZZ test job out of the yellow box of bills with no send on record, so a test bill is in no total.
**Say how it went…** is offered only on a row of that box, and `ByHandForm` takes a gap and nothing else.
So no door opened the form on a test bill. The card's walk writes a record that cannot be changed, and it could only have been made on a customer's bill.

## What changed

- `src/lib/billing/billsSent.ts`: `billsSentGaps` is unchanged in what it answers. Its row builder is now `gapOf`, and `unsentNewestFirst` reads the bills with no line once, each marked test or not.
- `billsSentTestGaps(input, lines)`, new and pure: the bills on a ZZ test job with no send on record, paid or not, the newest first. `billsSentTestGapHeading` and `BILLS_SENT_TEST_GAPS_NOTE` are its words.
- `src/components/billing/BillsSentLedger.tsx` reads the dev's switch for ZZ test jobs (`useZzTestJobsHidden`, the Pipeline's **ZZ test jobs** line under Hide groups…, punch list #61). For a dev who shows them, the page draws the test bills in a box of their own under the yellow box: *1 test bill has no send on record*, then *You see these because you show ZZ test jobs. They are in no total.*
- Each row is the yellow box's own row, with **Say how it went…**. The other bills the form offers to tick are the same customer's test bills alone, so a press on a test bill never records a real one, and a real bill's form never offers a test bill.
- The box follows the search box and shows under **All** and **No send on record**, as the yellow box does.

## What did not change

- The yellow box, its count, the **Not paid** tile, the print and the spreadsheet: a test bill is in none of them, for a dev as for anyone.
- Every role but a dev, and a dev who hides ZZ test jobs, sees no box.
- A record made on a test bill is a `sent_documents` row like any other and cannot be changed. Its line on the list reads *A test bill. It is in no total.* (v2.8445). It is on the list of presses nothing takes back (punch list #443).

## Tests

- `billsSent.test.ts`: the test bills are read apart, a test bill with a line is no gap of either kind, and the heading's words.
- `BillsSentLedger.render.test.tsx`: the office sees no box whatever the browser's switch says; a dev who hides ZZ test jobs sees none; a dev who shows them sees the box with its words, no yellow box and no tile for it, and the form one press away.
