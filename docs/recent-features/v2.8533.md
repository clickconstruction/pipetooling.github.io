## What the office sees

On the one page of settings for a paper a job already requires, under the blanks, a control named **What the GC sees**:

- **How many have signed.**
- **How many, and each sub business with its day.** Every paper starts here, which is what every job showed before.
- **How many, each sub business, and every signer by name.**

The page's one press saves it. With nothing else changed it reads **Save what the GC sees**, over *This marks the GC's page, never the paper. Nobody signs again.* With the basis moved too it reads **Save the basis and what the GC sees**. With a change of settings it rides along, and after a signature it lands on the new version.

## What the GC sees

- **Under the count alone**, the block shows the count and its meter, and no business line.
- **Under every signer by name**, a list headed **Who has signed** sits under the businesses. Each person who has signed has a line with their day, and a sub's line names their company.
- Nothing else on the block changes.

## Choices worth knowing

- **Settled 4 of the owner's five calls, 2026-11-19**: the count and the businesses, and a job can be set to the count only or to every signer by name.
- **It is on the row, not the job.** The setting belongs to the one paper built from settings. A job with two such papers sets each.
- **It is not in the first walk.** A paper is required on the count and the businesses, and the control shows from the next visit. Requiring a paper did not need another question.
- **It is no part of the paper.** It changes in place whoever has signed. A new version of the paper keeps it, by a trigger, so no function that makes a row can drop it.
- **Every signer is everyone who has signed**: the office, the crew and the subs. A person who has not signed is not named.
- **What the setting hides never leaves the database.** The function withholds it, and the shared file drops it again.
- **The row keeps who set it and when.** Nothing shows that yet.
