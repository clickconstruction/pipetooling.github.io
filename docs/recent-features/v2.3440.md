# v2.3440 — Job accounts at the counter, PR 5: the New Job question and same-property reuse (2026-09-14)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Fifth and last of the train (v2.3423 · v2.3424 · v2.3426 · v2.3430).

## Why

The queue should be mostly empty by the time a tech is at the counter. The contract sweep proved the pattern: one question the moment a hand-made job saves. And houses key the account on the property while the app keys it on the job, so a second phase at the same address should not cost a second call to Curly.

## What shipped

- **[`JobAccountsAfterCreatePrompt`](../../src/components/jobs/JobAccountsAfterCreatePrompt.tsx)** — mounted once by `JobFormModalProvider`, chained after `JobContractAfterCreatePrompt` for the same job (the contract prompt's close hands the id on). Office roles only; closes itself when the job has no house left to ask (no expecting house without an account). Reads the job, its strip (`list_job_account_strip`), and up to 12 other jobs whose address starts with the same street line (their strips too).
  - **Same property** (teal band): one chip per house where a same-address job has an *open* account — *Same Ferguson account · JA-4114* — on by default. Saving upserts an **open** row for the new job with the reference, how it was opened and the rep copied, note *Same property as 964 · Pondhill demo* (`ignoreDuplicates`, so an existing row is never overwritten).
  - **Ask Dispatch to open**: the expecting houses as pick chips (a carried-over house is greyed). Saving files the one errand through `submitOpenJobAccountRequest` (`requested` rows + the `open_job_account` card, note *New job — open before the first parts run*).
  - **None needed** writes a `not_needed` row per house with the note *New job — no job account needed* so nothing signals; **Later** just closes.
- **Kernel** [`jobAccountsNewJob.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobAccountsNewJob.ts) (+6 tests): `samePropertyOffers` (exact normalized address, else the street line — the lien desk's `normalizeAddressForMatch`), `buildNewJobAccountsPlan` (reuse never doubles as ask; reuse with no offer falls back to ask; default skip), the notes.
- Guide: *open a job account before buying parts* gains "When the job is made".
