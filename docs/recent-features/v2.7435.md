# v2.7435 — Dashboard: the lien notices that wait on a leader are a card of their own (2026-11-16)

Taunya's ask of 2026-11-16, after she looked at what a leader sees on the Dashboard when lien notices wait on him: *"I need to improve how it looks for a leader on their dashboard when they need to approve liens."* The lien desk session drew it twice and she approved the second drawing (*"Yes, build it"*). Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What a leader sees

The row *Approve N lien notices the office drafted* is gone. In its place is a card, **Lien notices · your signature**:

- **The title is the deadline.** *Sign 1 lien notice today* when a window closes today. *Sign 2 lien notices by Thursday, Nov 19* (or *by tomorrow*) when the soonest window is within 7 days. Otherwise *3 lien notices wait on your signature*.
- **One line under it**: how many more and by when, the money and the jobs, and the wait. *2 more by Dec 15 · $6,529 on 3 jobs · they have waited on you since Oct 7, 40 days*. Sent on different days, the wait reads *the oldest has waited on you 40 days*. On a phone the wait takes its own line.
- **Up to three notices, the most pressing first.** Each row has the job's number and name, the claim, the GC and its chips: *August closes today*, *August closes tomorrow*, *4 days*, *July closed Oct 15, not sent* for a month of that notice whose window closed with no notice, and *nothing claimed, ask the office* on a $0 claim. A row with no chip that names a month says the notice's months (*Jul + Aug + Sep*).
- **The presses.** The first row has **Open 891 to sign ▸** in the green of the notice's own Sign and approve. On a computer the others have **Open 804 ›**. On a phone only the first row has a button, 44 px tall, and every other row is the press itself. Each opens the Lien desk at that notice on the Awaiting approval pile (`lienDeskHref({ kind: 'notice', job, pile: 'awaiting' })`), which on a phone is the one-screen approve.
- **Past three**: one line, *6 more wait on you ›*, opens the Awaiting approval pile.
- **Where it sits.** While a window is within 7 days or has closed with nothing sent, the card is red and first in Needs you, ahead of every other line. Otherwise it is blue and sits where the row sat.

## The three faults in the old row

1. **The money was short.** The row read *$2,976 open* over three notices whose jobs owed $2,976, $3,553 and $0. `leader.dollars` adds each awaiting entry's `openBalance`. The Dashboard reads the desk in light mode, and light mode skipped the read of the bills (`billed_open`) that gives a job with no month row its money, so two of the three read $0 there and their own figure on the open desk. `useLienDeskData` now makes that read in light mode for the notices that wait on the leader, and no other. The card's claim is the notice pane's own: `correctedClaim` over the entry's open balance and a claim set by hand. The figure is the rows' whole dollars summed, so it is the number a person gets by adding the rows.
2. **A raw date a month past.** *The earliest window closes 2026-10-15.* The card writes days in words (*Oct 15*, *Thursday, Nov 19*), and a window that closed is a red chip on the notice it belongs to.
3. **No word on the wait.** The card reads the day each notice was sent for approval (`submitted_at`, the list row's *awaiting approval · Oct 7*).

## Three calls made in the kernel

`src/lib/jobs/lienLeaderSignCard.ts` (`buildLienLeaderSignCard`, pure, 15 tests) holds them:

- **Which notices, and who.** Every notice on the desk's Awaiting approval pile that is not in a run the office prepared for one GC (`lienDeskEntryInRun`, the same test `lienDeskBatches` makes, held equal by a test). That is the whole company's pile, not the leader's own jobs, shown to the seats the old row was shown to: master technician and dev.
- **A notice whose only window already closed** is never called *today*, since nothing on it can still be made today. It counts under *wait on your signature*, its row says which month closed and when, and it turns the card red.
- **A notice the desk holds no window for.** A job the month reader (`list_lien_notice_months`) returns no row for has no day to print: its row says the notice's months and no day, and the line counts it as *with no open window*. On 2026-11-16 that was two of the three notices waiting.

## How it is wired

- `useNeedsYouInputs` builds the card from the desk data it already loads and hands it to `buildNeedsYouItems` as `lienLeaderSign`.
- `lienLeaderSignItem` (`dashboardNeedsYou.ts`) makes the `lien-notice-approve` line: the key, its rank, its actor and its door are unchanged. The line carries the card (`lienSign`), the first notice's address (`openHref`) and, while red, `first`, which `rankNeedsYouItems` sorts ahead of every tier.
- `DashboardNeedsYouCard`'s Cards view draws `LienLeaderSignCardView` for a line with `lienSign`. A press calls the host's `onAction` with the line and that notice's `openHref`, and `needsYouDoor` opens it, so the Dashboard and Quickfill both work with no new prop.
- **Walk the list** shows the same title and line as one card, and its press (*Open 891 to sign*) opens the first notice. **Skip** there is the only snooze a Needs you line has, and it works on this line as on any other. The line has no dismiss, as before.
- Styles are `.lienLeaderCard` in `src/index.css`. The red and amber tints are the Lien desk chips' tokens.

## Not changed

- The rows `lien-notice-batch` and `lien-notice-sign`, and the office's Lien desk strip.
- No text or reminder goes to the leader. That is a separate change with a scheduled job.
