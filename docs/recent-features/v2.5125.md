# v2.5125 — GC projects: the office's side of Pay by card, and the owner's switch in Settings (2026-10-09)

GC mode's Owner Billing O8c, on O8a (v2.5113) and O8b (v2.5123). Migration `20261010042000_gc_card_bill_switch` (doc: `docs/migrations/20261010042000_gc_card_bill_switch.md`). Three function deploys: `gc-card-bill`, `customer-portal`, `gc-customer-email`. The plan is `to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/owner-billing-o8.md` on `spike/gc-mode`.

## What shipped

- **The switch is an `app_settings` row, the owner's own press** (the lead's call). `gc_card_bill_on_v1` = 'false' from the migration; the owner (master_technician) and dev flip it through a key-scoped UPDATE policy, everyone signed in reads it. **It replaces O8b's env value `GC_CARD_BILL_ON`**, which can be unset after the deploys. Stripe's test or live stays the env value `GC_CARD_BILL_STRIPE_MODE`. `gc-card-bill`, `customer-portal` and `gc-customer-email` read the row as the service role (`GC_CARD_BILL_SETTING_KEY`, `gcCardBillOn`: only 'true' is on, a missing row is off).
- **Settings → Jobs & billing → Let GC customers pay a certified bill by card** (`GcCardBillSettingsBlock`, `src/lib/gc/cardBillSetting.ts`), for dev and the owner.
- **The mapper reads a card bill at its base** (`ownerBillingRows.ts`): `loadGcBillingJobMoney` reads each bill's card page and the card rows; `billMoney` lays a card payment on the pay application only up to the certified amount (the rest is the fee); `billCard` gives each sent one its `card` (on card or taken back). Money, Bill the customer, Closeout, interest and the reminder's open amount all read what was certified.
- **Bill the customer** (`GcBillCard.tsx`, words in `ownerBillingCard.ts`): a sent bill on card says *They chose card in their portal on Oct 10. Stripe asks $X with the $Y card fee.* with **Their card page** and **Back to a check bill** (a confirm in words, then `gc-card-bill`'s undo door through `takeBillOffCard`); paid by card says the day and the fee; taken back says so; with the switch on, a certified bill not on card says they can choose card. The part payment press hides on a bill on card, since Stripe records the payment. Record the certificate's hint, with the switch on: *Recording it makes their bill. They pay it by check, or by card in their portal with a 3% fee.*
- **The emails**: a reminder on a bill on card says *You chose to pay it by card. With the 3% card fee, the card page asks $Z.* and *Pay it here:* with the bill's pay link, and no longer asks for their day (`payReminderMail`). `gc-customer-email`'s portal line, for a certified email or a reminder on a bill that can still turn with the switch on, reads *Or pay it by card in your portal:* and adds *Paying by card adds a 3% card fee of $X.* (`gcEmailCardFee`, `buildGcCustomerEmail`'s `cardFee`). The interest bill is as it was.
- **Guides**: `record-what-a-gc-customer-paid` (*A bill they pay by card*, *When they want to pay by check after all*); the portal guide says where the owner turns it on. **Docs**: `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`, `BILLING_FLOWS.md`, `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`, `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`, `GLOSSARY.md` (*Card bill · card fee*).
