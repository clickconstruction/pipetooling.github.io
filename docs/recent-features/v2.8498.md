# v2.8498 — Legal desk: the retainage door opens Retainage notices (2026-11-24)

Seen and left by v2.8478 (`docs/recent-features/v2.8478.md`, *Seen and left*), picked up the same day when asked for. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## The fault

The Legal desk's Paper tab draws a door to each job's next lien step at the verdict band's right (`legalPaperNextDoor.ts`, v2.6104). A § 53.056 notice and the § 53.057 retainage notice are worked on the Lien desk, so both doors said `to: 'desk'` and nothing more. The page (`LegalDeskPage.tsx`) sent every desk door to `lienDeskHref({ kind: 'notice', job })`, so *Draft the retainage notice…* opened **Monthly notices** on the job. A job waiting on its retainage notice is on the Retainage notices list. The Lien desk then said the job was not on that list and offered Retainage notices (`lienDeskAim.ts`), so the press landed on a line and a second press, not on another job's paper.

Reproduced before the change on a made-up job (`LegalDeskPage.lienDoors.render.test.tsx`): the retainage door's address read as Monthly notices on the job. The other doors already read right.

## What changed

- **A desk door names its list.** `LegalPaperNextDoor`'s desk shape carries `kind: 'notice' | 'retainage'`. A notice, on time or late, is `notice`. The retainage notice is `retainage`.
- **One place a door becomes an address.** `legalPaperNextDoorHref(jobId, door)` in the same kernel: a desk door through `lienWindowDeskDoorHref` (v2.8478, the helper the Lien window's own button reads), a window door through `lienWindowHref`, and no door to the job's Lien window as before. The page calls it and holds no address logic of its own.
- **Every other door was checked and is unchanged.** A notice opens Monthly notices on the job. The affidavit, the mail numbers and the release open the job's Lien window on their tab. A press with no door opens the job's Lien window. The addresses are word for word what they were.
- No label changed, and no paper prints any of this.
