## Why both came out fully controlled

The page's `load()` blanks the whole editor, and a press on Save draft runs it. A piece that owned its own state or its own read would lose it there. So three things stay in the page until the draft's engine is a hook:

- Check link's answer, which before this change outlived a Save draft, and still does.
- The read of who can be told, made once per visit.
- The read of the projects, made once per visit. A section that made it would show an empty Project box after every save.

## What did not move

The fieldset held two branches for a sent estimate (the document as sent, and *No supporting document for this quote.*). It sits inside the block that only a draft draws, so neither could ever show. They are gone, not moved.
