# v2.6478 — On a wide laptop, the sort card sits beside the messages (2026-11-03)

The owner's B of the three mock-ups (2026-11-03), on top of A (v2.6474, v2.6476, v2.6477). A laptop is wide and short, so once the thread's column has the width, the sort card leaves the top of the lines for a column at their right and shows whole. At 1000×700 on a Bid it? thread the lines go from 455 px to 508 px tall (614 px wide), and the whole card is in view without *Other ways*.

## What changed

- `isThreadBeside` and `THREAD_BESIDE_PX` (`src/lib/messages/threadFold.ts`): a short window whose thread column is 900 px or wider. An unmeasured column reads narrow.
- `ThreadWindow`: the lines, the strip over the box and the box sit in one body (`data-thread-body`, `under` or `beside`). Beside, what the cuts draw above the lines (the sort card, a lead's two doors, the notice strip) goes in `aside.thread-beside` at the body's right instead of `data-thread-above`. The body keeps its place in the tree either way, so a draft in the box lives through a resize across the line.
- `.thread-beside` (`src/index.css`): `min(360px, 38%)` wide, its own scroll, and `display: none` when `:empty`, so a thread whose cuts draw nothing there gives the lines the full width.
- The open thread says `beside` (`OpenThread.beside`); `NewLaneSlot` does not fold the card there.
- `ThreadWindow.side.render.test.tsx` reads the side's neighbour as the body's column now.
- Guides: a line under *Read a thread on a short screen* in `find-every-message-with-a-person-in-one-place`, and a sentence under *Sort one* in `sort-new-messages`.

## Decisions

- **900 px of column**: a 1000 px window with *Their flow* folded clears it; with the flow open the column is narrower and A's one-row card stays, so the two columns never squeeze the lines under about 600 px.
- **Under the header, not beside it.** The header keeps the window's width, so its two rows (v2.6474) hold; only the body splits.
