## Decisions

- To stays one address — the send takes one `to_email`; several would change the function and the scheduled lane. Extras go on Cc.
- The sender's copy is held, not removable: the rule in `_shared/gcStatementReplyTo.ts` always copies her when replies go to someone else, and the line shows it rather than hiding it.
- Sample and twin logins were already off the roster (`fetchActiveUsers`); the *test* pill in the screenshot is a real office login named "test", which stays until the office archives it.
