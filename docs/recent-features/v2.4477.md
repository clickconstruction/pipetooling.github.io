# v2.4477 — Submittals: a resubmit keeps the rows nobody has answered (2026-10-03)

Finding 1 of the owner's tab review before the estimator's first Monday ([`to-dos/submittals/tab-review-before-after.html`](../../to-dos/submittals/tab-review-before-after.html), https://claude.ai/artifact/SJ3skkPrsSDEugjmXe4h2k; the owner: "Build 1, 3 and 4 first…"). Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

Seen on BP375 (SpaceX), read only, 2026-10-03: Rev 1 is a draft the estimator sent by email, with structura's answers typed in — 4 rows sent back, 10 with no answer (one of them with no product), none approved. Step 7 offered *Rev 2 from the 4 rows sent back*, and its confirm read *Only the rows the reviewer marked Revise or Reject on Rev 1 carry into the new draft — 4 rows. The rest stand as approved on Rev 1 and stay on the procurement log.*

Nothing was approved. `newRevision(true)` kept `itemsSentBack(previous)` alone, so the ten unanswered rows would have stayed on Rev 1, which then reads superseded. `rowsThatStand` lists only approved rows of an earlier revision, so their 44 lines would have left the procurement log as well. [v2.4449](v2.4449.md) named this under *Not done here*.

## What changed

- **The kernel** ([`reviewDecisions.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/reviewDecisions.ts)): `resubmitSplit(rows)` → sent back · no answer · approved. Only an approved row stays behind. `resubmitLabel(next, sentBack, noAnswer)` is the button; `resubmitConfirm(rev, counts)` is the question, one sentence pair per kind, and the confirm button says how many rows the draft will hold. `DecisionSummary.noAnswer` counts the rows with no call whatever their status (`open` keeps its meaning: the differing rows a reviewer is asked about).
- **The tab** ([`BidsSubmittalsTab`](../../src/components/bids/BidsSubmittalsTab.tsx)): `newRevision(true)` carries sent back + no answer (order-only rows carry as before). Step 7's line and button read the kernel.
- **The strip** ([`submittalJourney.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/submittalJourney.ts)): `decisions.noAnswer`; with rows still waiting, the Next line says they go on the new version too, and its button carries the same label.
- A part approved on a row that goes on keeps its approval, marked carried, as before (`carryPartInsert(…, keepApproval)`), so it stays released on the log.

With no unanswered row the button, the line and the rows built are what they were; the confirm's words changed for every resubmit.
