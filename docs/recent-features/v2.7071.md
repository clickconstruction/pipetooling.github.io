# v2.7071 — Lien cover letters: the bullets are indented and the letter fits one page (2026-11-12)

Two asks from the owner on the first export of the lien letters from the live site (punch list #326's group): *can you help me indent these bullet points?* and *can you help me make the first two pages one page?* Both are about counsel's cover letter, the first page in the owner's envelope. No word of any letter changed.

## The bullets

A cover letter lists the ways a claim ends as paragraphs that open with `• `. They were drawn flush with the body, so the list did not stand apart and a second line ran back under the mark.

`filingBulletBody` (`lienFilingDocuments.ts`) knows such a paragraph. It is now drawn indented with the mark hanging in front of the words:

- **Screen and print** (`filingDocHtml`): the words 2.6em in on every line, the mark in its own 1.1em box in front of the first.
- **PDF** (`drawFilingDocPdf`): the mark 6 mm in, the words 10 mm in and wrapped to the narrower width.
- **Plain text** (`filingDocText`) keeps the line as written.

It applies to every paper this builder draws, so the older letter templates and a draft's own bullets get it too. The numbered points in a letter (*1. You may withhold…*) are plain paragraphs and stay at the margin: the owner asked for the bullets.

## One page

The letter ran a few lines long, and the signature travels with the paragraph above it (v2.6810), so the *Enclosed* line and the signature moved whole to a second sheet.

- **The PDF** (the emailed copy, the copy a mail provider prints, the sample in Settings): `filingDocPdfBlob` takes `fit: 'one-page'`. A paper that runs past one page is drawn again down `FILING_FIT_ONE_PAGE_STEPS` and the first drawing that fits is kept: 11.5 point on 5.6 mm lines, then 11, 10.5 and 10 point. A letter that fits as it is, and one no step brings to one page, are drawn as before. Only the body's paragraphs take the step. `buildRunNoticePdf` asks for it on the owner's cover letter and on no other paper.
- **The packet printed in house** (`runCopyPages` → `runPagesHtml`): a cover letter's page carries `RUN_COVER_LETTER_CLASS`. In print it sits at `FILING_PRINT_TIGHT_LEADING` (1.45, the short affidavit's setting and the PDF's own leading) with its paragraphs a little closer. The screen keeps the paper's leading. Print cannot measure itself, so this is one fixed setting, not a ladder.

## Where it does not reach

- **A letter with the envelope window block** (a copy mailed through Docsmit) starts lower on the sheet. With an ordinary builder name every letter but the commercial one fits, at 10 to 10.5 point. The commercial letter, and four of the five letters with the 66-character name, still take two pages and are drawn at the usual setting.
- **The in-house print on a real job was not measured.** Real names wrap differently from the sample's. The fixed setting leaves about an inch spare on the sample.
- **10 point is the floor**, and it is this change's own choice. A higher floor is one number in `FILING_FIT_ONE_PAGE_STEPS`.
