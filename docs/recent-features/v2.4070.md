# v2.4070 — Cover Letter: the schedule of values can be your own lines (2026-09-28)

The **My lines** shape from the "one schedule, two shapes" mock-up (https://claude.ai/artifact/2tLGjhg8hy8eFC4JeGmApB), on top of [v2.4075](./v2.4075.md) (the split) and [v2.4066](./v2.4066.md) (the pill). Client only; the table and the `bids.sov_shape` column came with v2.4075's migration.

## The kernel — `sovLines.ts`

- `SovLine` (`id`, `sortOrder`, `label`, `value`, `labor` or null, `note`, the `stage` it was seeded from). `seedLinesFromStages(letter, split?)`: the three stage lines with the split's labor and notes when there is one.
- `lineSplit(line, ruleLaborPct)`: the typed labor clamped to the value, else the company share; material is the rest. `sovLinesTotals`, `reconcileLines(lines, contract)` (`total`, `gap` = contract − lines, `balanced`), `scaleLinesToContract` (every line by the same factor, largest-remainder cents, a typed labor scales with its line; null when the lines are all zero), `parsePastedLineNames` (one per row; a leading number, bullet or dash dropped).
- `buildSovLinesSectionLines(lines, { split, ruleLaborPct, totalOnly })`: `Label — $value` (+ `(labor … · material …)`), a note indented under its line, the lines' Total. `buildSovLinesSheetHtml`: the attached sheet in the pay-application form (#, description of work, labor / material when split, scheduled value, notes), "for progress billing only", and a footer sentence when the lines miss the contract. 7 tests.

## The editor — `CoverLetterSovLinesEditor.tsx`

Its own component (render smoke, 2 cases): label / labor (when split; placeholder = the company share) / material / value boxes committing on blur or Enter, ▲ ▼ ×, a note per line, the totals row, the amber gap bar with **Scale every line to the contract** (green "Lines add to the contract" when balanced), **+ Add line**, **Paste the GC's line names…** (an inline textarea; values blank), **Seed again from the stages** (the confirm dialog names the three lines; disabled until the takeoff has loaded). Writes go straight to `bid_sov_lines`; the parent re-reads through `onChanged`.

## The tab, the letter, the PDF

- `BidsCoverLetterTab.tsx`: a By stage / My lines radiogroup in the switches row (`bids.sov_shape`, `setSovShapeOnBid`); the lines are read whenever the schedule is on (`loadSovLines`, keyed `sovLinesBidId`) so a switch is instant and nothing is lost either way; the first switch to My lines seeds the three stages as they stand (`sovSeeds` from the preview + the split). With the shape on My lines the editor card renders the component and **Print the schedule** prints the lines sheet (`buildSovLinesSheetHtml`); the label reads *my lines*.
- `CoverLetterScheduleOfValues.lines` (`{ lines, ruleLaborPct, split }`) — `scheduleOfValuesSectionFor` renders the lines section instead of the stages. The lines are as typed for every letter in a packet (a bundled section's amount does not rescale them; the bar in the editor is the check).
- The Approval PDF reads `sov_shape` in the same fresh read and loads the lines (`loadSovLines`, `sovLineFromRow`) and the company share.

## Decided here

- **Nothing is lost on a shape switch.** The rows stay while the shape is By stage; only *Seed again* replaces them, after asking.
- **A hand-added line with no labor takes the company share**, marked "(rule)" on the sheet and by its placeholder in the editor.
- **The lines sheet is the pay-app form**, not the two-page stage print — a GC handing over a form wants their line names, which *Paste* gives them.
