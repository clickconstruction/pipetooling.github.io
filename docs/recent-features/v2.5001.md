# v2.5001 — GC projects: the contract's days to pay set when a first bill is due (2026-10-08)

Owner Billing's O5d in the GC mode real build, the lead's call on 2026-10-08 after O5b. A bill's due day came only from the customer's own pay history or their promise. A customer who had never paid us therefore never had a late bill, and **Remind them to pay** (v2.5000) never showed for them. `gc_projects.owner_pay_days` (O1, decision 7: the contract's days to pay after the certificate) was read with the terms but never used, and nothing wrote it. Guide: [`bill-the-customer-on-a-gc-job`](../../src/content/help/bill-the-customer-on-a-gc-job.md) → *The days they have to pay*. No migration, no edge function.

- **The kernel** (`ownerBilling.ts`):
  - `ownerExpectPaidOn` takes the customer's usual days to pay first, then the contract's (`GcProject.ownerPayDays`), counted from the certificate's day. A promise still wins in `ownerPayDue`, and with neither the day stays null.
  - Everything that reads the day follows: the due chip and late, `payReminderStep`, *We expect it by* in the certified bill's email, the late lists, and interest's start (`ownerInterestFrom`).
  - `cashForecast.ts` keeps its own rule, the customer's days only. That goes to O6's read-back.
- **The mapper** (`billCustomer.ts`): `billingStateForAll` lays `ownerPayDays` from the terms row on the project.
- **The window** (`GcBillCustomer.tsx`): **Days to pay** in the terms beside the retainage.
  - The line reads *They pay within 30 days of the certificate, by the contract.*, or *Type the contract's days to pay so a first bill can go late.*
  - **Change the days to pay** takes whole days from 0 to 365, or blank, and **Save the days to pay** writes `gc_projects.owner_pay_days` (`setOwnerPayDays`).
  - The Owner Billing door's trigger already keeps the column to the money team.
- **Tests:**
  - `contractPayDays.test.ts`: history first, the contract's days, neither, a promise first, a first-time customer's bill late with the reminder offered, and the words;
  - `billCustomer.test.ts`: the mapper;
  - the window's render test: the line both ways, whole days only, saving and clearing.
- **Also:** one sentence in the reminder guide no longer opens on a bare pronoun.
