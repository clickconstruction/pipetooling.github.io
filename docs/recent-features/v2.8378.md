# v2.8378 — Outside papers: only the office says who a paper holder is (2026-11-23)

Punch list #403, the owner's second decision, the first of two PRs: the database. One migration, `20261123184149_estimator_reach.sql` ([its page](../migrations/20261123184149_estimator_reach.md)). No edge function and no screen. The screens and the signed form's gate are v2.8379.

## The decision

The card's pick: a signed line opens its record from the tab. An estimator reads the form's layout and the signature's image for an outside company's paper. Naming a party who holds papers needs one of the four roles. A training account reads the line and is refused the signed file. The owner answered "Yes, all four" on 2026-11-22, and on 2026-11-23 said to build it with a training account reading the line only.

## What changed

- A guard on `contacts` (`paper_holder_named_by_office`): a contact that holds a paper is named by `is_office_staff()` alone. It answers `name_contact`, and a statement of the caller's own, with `officeNamesPaperHolder`.
- `contacts_fold_papers` refuses the same caller when the contact folded holds a paper and is not an outside company's.
- `contract_form_templates_estimator_outside`: an estimator who is not a twin and not in training reads the layout of a form an outside company's paper is filled on.
- `contract_signer_signatures_estimator_outside` on `storage.objects`: the same estimator reads the signature image of a paper `estimator_reads_paper` says yes to.
- `contact_holds_paper`, a definer nobody signed in may call.

## What the card had wrong

- The card says an estimator names a party through `name_contact`. The `contacts` table itself is the office team's to write, so one `UPDATE` did the same with no function. The guard is on the table for that reason.
- The never-run draft refused the fold only for a contact on no list. A contact on the roster or the hiring board that holds a paper could still be folded into a customer's. The helper now refuses any contact that is not an outside company's.
- The draft's guard fired only when a contact's kind changed, so a lead put on Prospects passed. It now reads the record columns too.

## Defaults this was built on

- A training account reads the line only. So both new read rules leave a training estimator out. Their read of the paper's own row is unchanged.
- The refusal's words are *They hold a paper, so only the office says who they are.* They are the session's, in the app's plain words.

## Not done here

- No screen's code changes. Once the migration is pushed, the signed record an estimator already opens from a company's own page lists its form answers and draws its signature. Until v2.8379 Messages' **Who is this?** shows the database's own words on a refusal.
- No walk on prod. The bed `paper_party` holds every case, and the statements were played on a local Postgres with stub tables before the push.
- The migration is pushed by the parent session after the merge.
