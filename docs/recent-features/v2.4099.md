# v2.4099 — Payment promises in the job's activity feed — "They said" as an event, read-side (2026-09-28)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. PR A of punch list #55 (`to-dos/lien-calendar/README.md`). The owner asked, designing the Lien calendar's pay-dot editor: *when a person adds a note like this, does it also add that note to their activity section and job stages?* It did not — a promise was its own record (`job_payment_promises`, `add_job_payment_promise`) and nowhere else: the Pipeline row's Activity box and the Job window's feed read `jobs_ledger_thread_notes`, reports, schedule blocks, clock sessions and `list_job_activity_events`, none of which a promise touched.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/jobActivityEvent.ts`** — one more `JobActivityEventType`, `payment_promise`, in the render registry as *They said* (money green, the billing bucket, financial). The feed already has one render branch for every event type, so nothing else draws.
- **`src/lib/jobs/promiseActivityEvents.ts`** (new, 5 tests) — `paymentPromisesToActivityEvents(promises)` maps the live promises on a job into `event` items: `dedupeKey` `ev:promise:<id>`, `occurredAt` = when it was recorded, `actorName` = who on our side heard it (null, so *System*, when the customer named the date themselves), and the summary *Pay by Nov 20, 2026 — Umar Khan · by phone · heard by Taunya · “check goes out with the draw”* (`promiseSaidLine` supplies the middle; the year stays because a promise is read months later). Promises with no usable timestamp are dropped rather than sorted to 1970.
- **`src/hooks/useJobThreadNotes.ts`** — a sixth source beside notes, reports, blocks, clock and events: `list_job_payment_promises` (RLS-scoped, live rows only — the RPC excludes `voided_at`), filtered to the job with `promisesOnJob`, appended to the event items before the merge. Fail-soft: an error or a role that cannot read promises leaves the feed as it was.

**Read-side by choice.** The alternative — posting a stamp note when a promise is saved — duplicates the record and leaves a stale note when a promise is voided. Reading the promises means every promise ever recorded shows, and *they never said that* takes the line off the feed with the record.

## Verify

Open a billed job's Activity (the Pipeline row's box or the Job window): each promise on record reads as a *They said* line at the moment it was recorded, between the notes around it. Record a new one from the row's *They said…* chip — it appears on the next load. Void it — it leaves. A GC's word from GC Review shows the same way on each of its jobs.
