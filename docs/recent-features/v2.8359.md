# v2.8359 — On a phone, a toast shows at the foot of the screen (2026-11-23)

Punch list #414, the second of its four small parts, built first. The owner's word of 2026-11-23 to build the card's four app-wide things. No migration and no edge function.

## What was there

The corner toast (`Toast.tsx`, placement `corner`) sat top-right at every width, under the app header. At 390 px the audit of punch list #382 found it over the heading of the card at the top of **Is it as told?** for as long as the *not kept* words showed. Every screen with a card at the top of a phone had the same overlap, since the toast's place is the app's.

## What changed

- On a phone (`useIsMobile`, the 640 px line the rest of the app draws) the corner toast draws at the foot of the screen, centred, never wider than the screen less a 1rem gutter each side, and slides up as it comes.
- It stands 12 px above the bottom chrome: `max(var(--app-bottom-chrome), env(safe-area-inset-bottom))` for the tab bar while one is up (its height already holds the home indicator's inset) or the inset alone, plus `var(--job-mode-press-bar, 0px)` for the Job Mode press bar pinned on the tab bar. So it covers neither bar on a crew's screen, and on a screen with no tab bar it sits on the home indicator's inset.
- What else sits at the foot was checked: the outbox bar is the app-health strip at the top of the page, in flow, so the foot is clear of it; the section dock draws nothing at phone width; the Job Mode press bar publishes its height and the toast reads it.
- On a desktop nothing moves. An anchored toast (one with a `clientX`/`clientY`) and a centre toast (`placement: 'center'`) keep their own places on a phone as before.

## Tests

- `Toast.render.test.tsx`, new, through `ToastProvider`: a desktop keeps top-right; a phone draws the toast at the foot with the chrome variables in its `bottom`, centred, above the tab bar's z-index; the foot toast still carries its press and dismisses; an anchored toast and a centre toast keep their places on a phone.
- `useIsMobile` reads `window.matchMedia` only when the browser has one, as `useNarrowViewport640` does: the toast now calls the hook, and a render test that showed a toast with no `matchMedia` stub (`useSupplyHousePayments.render.test.tsx`) crashed in CI's first run.
- Not looked at on a phone: the proof is the render test and the chrome variables the bottom chrome library already publishes (v2.8035).
