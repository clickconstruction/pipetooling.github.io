# v2.5963 — What everyone sees: every screen, as every role, side by side (2026-10-26)

Punch list #194, PR 3 ([`to-dos/experiences-lens/`](../../to-dos/experiences-lens/README.md); the sealed frame is v2.5960). The tab that names the frames.

- **The tab** ([`SettingsWhatEveryoneSeesTab.tsx`](../../src/components/settings/SettingsWhatEveryoneSeesTab.tsx)), dev only (`settings-what-everyone-sees`, beside What customers see and What the team sees), lays out three things.
  - **The knobs:** Who (the nine roles, in the app's own words from `HUMAN_ROLE_LABELS`), Where, State, Across, Width and Theme. Where lists every page, window and Settings card the role can open, through the app's own gates (`doorsFor(plainViewer(role))`). The knob that varies and the ones it replaces are disabled.
  - **The frames:** iframes of the real app named `pipetooling-lens:{as, mood, theme}`. Each is drawn at its real width (375, 768, 1280) and scaled into its place. Each shows its ask count and its gaps, read from `window.__lens.asks`, and *What the world answered* breaks them down and names each gap. Each has Reload and Open alone ↗, which opens a sealed tab under a name of its own.
  - **The limit:** at most six frames are live at once, one on a phone (`useIsMobile`). A waiting frame is a short card with Draw it, which swaps it in for the oldest.
- **The kernel** ([`lensTab.ts`](../../src/lib/lens/lensTab.ts), 5 tests) sets the frames for each Across:
  - Across widths and themes gives six frames.
  - Across roles gives nine. A role that cannot open the chosen screen shows its dashboard, and the frame says so.
  - Across states gives seven.

  `askSummary` counts who answered.
- **Registered** in four places: `settingsGroups.ts` (and its tab-order pin), `Settings.tsx`, `settingsSearch.ts` and `devDoors.ts`, plus `e2e/settings-tabs.spec.ts`.
- **Words**: the guide [*see every screen as every role*](../../src/content/help/see-every-screen-as-every-role.md); the Glossary's *The lens's sealed frame*; `ACCESS_CONTROL`'s Settings row and sealed-frame note; `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION`'s Settings line.
