# v2.5956 — Evening times land on their own day across the app (2026-10-26)

The sweep v2.5940 and v2.5954 left: 49 places read a stored timestamp's day by its first ten characters (`x_at.slice(0, 10)`), the UTC date — tomorrow for anything stamped after 7 PM Chicago (5 PM in winter). Each was traced to its column's SQL type and its writers before deciding.

**The finding that set the rule.** Where the app stores a chosen *day* in a `timestamptz`, it anchors it at noon — pay payments at local noon (`useRecordPayStubPayment`, `RecordSplitPaymentModal`, the Cash App match), a backdated bill at noon Chicago (`create_billed_shell_invoice`: `p_billed_on + 12 hours AT TIME ZONE 'America/Chicago'`), and the database reads `billed_at`'s day the same way (`billed_at AT TIME ZONE 'America/Chicago'`). No writer of these columns stores UTC midnight. So `calendarYmdInAppTzFromIso` is right for every `timestamptz` here: a noon-anchored day reads the same, and a real evening instant stops reading as tomorrow.

**30 fixed** — `timestamptz` instants, now dated with `calendarYmdInAppTzFromIso` (the edge function with `todayYmdInAppTz(new Date(iso))`):
- Collect on the Bridge: `collectMoves`, `collectPayers`, `collectScorecard` (×2), `collectWords` (×2) — the chase touch's `created_at`, the same bug as v2.5940's lean read.
- Pay: `personMoneyLedger` (×7), `payRunPayments`, `PeoplePayLedgerView`, `cashAppReconcileInputs`, `partnerLedgerJournal`, `partnerPayReportHtml` — `pay_stub_payments` / `pay_stubs.paid_at`.
- `SupplyHousesTab` (×2, a move ask's `requested_at`), `gcIo` (×2, `saved_at`), `jobFollowupStore` (a job's `created_at`), `ownerRecords` (`billed_at`), `paidClaims` (*wrote Oct 21*), `CourtAreasPanel` (*drawn by the office*), `loadDemandPace` (an estimate's `created_at`, and its `ymdOf` helper for `sent_at` / `acceptor_consented_at`, which sliced the same way under another name).
- `customer-portal`: an owner-records packet's *sent* day; a bare `YYYY-MM-DD` stays as it is, since the `fields.sent.at` writer was not found.

**19 waived** with `// tz-ok: <why>` — the read is right as written:
- `date` columns: `person_contract_documents.signed_at` (the contract form office half, the lien waiver send, the work-order MSA ×3, People contracts, the paperwork rollup), `job_demand_letters.sent_at` and `job_lien_filings.filed_at` / `served_at` (the Liens window, the billed lien clocks, the lien timeline ×5 — so the lien dates were never off).
- `ClockSessionEditSplitModal` (×2): a `datetime-local` value, already the wall-clock day.
- `subPaymentMoveRemove`: the slice is a fallback only when the value does not parse.
- `lienLastWorkDay` (×2): sliced a day already converted; the redundant slice is gone.

**Tests**: four cases pinned at 8:30 PM Chicago (01:30 UTC the next day) — `paidClaims`, `ownerRecords`, `personMoneyLedger` (`paidTotalInRange`), `collectWords` — each fails on the old code. Two fixtures corrected: `CourtAreasPanel.render.test.tsx` asserted the UTC day of a 10 PM Chicago drawing (now Oct 6, the drawing's day), and `ownerRecordsPdf.test.ts` fed a bare date where production passes `billed_at` (now noon Chicago, as the shell writer stores it). They pass under `TZ=UTC` and `TZ=America/Chicago`.
