# v2.4340 — Lien desk Calendar: overdue, this month, next month and later (2026-10-01)

**The ask (Grace, 2026-10-01).** "On the lien desk, the header takes up a lot of space before a user can browse through the calendar … I want to be able to see what is overdue, due this month, due in next month, etc." Plan, then *is this the best we can do?*, then a mock-up, then the same question. Canvas: https://claude.ai/artifact/4gmBXmdWX7cTGESbfw8NQv (row 1 the board as it was, measured; row 2 the first pass with its faults numbered; row 3 the approved desktop and phone). Grace: "I like this, please help me build it". The five calls went as suggested: All is the default view, Overdue folded at the top, each job counted once at its next date, the key one line at the bottom, and a new short guide now.

**What was wrong.** At 1280 × 800 the first GC row sat 354 px below the card's top: the summary line and search, the to-do band, the key, the density bars and the dates came first (at 1000 × 600, one row fit). The to-do counted a job once per deadline, so *By Oct 15: $184k* and *By Nov 16: $201k* together came to more than the $249k on the whole board. Nothing said what was overdue; the dead liens sat in a folded group at the very bottom.

## What shipped

- **The buckets** ([`lienCalendarBuckets.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienCalendarBuckets.ts), new, kernel-tested): `lienNextDate` is a job's next date on the lien clock (the § 53.056 notice it still owes, the earliest month since v2.4308, else its § 53.052 lien date; none once a window closed or the lien is filed). `lienBucketOf` puts it in **Overdue** (the runway is closed), **This month**, **Next month** or **Later** by the calendar month of that date; a filed lien waits in Later. `buildLienCalendarBoard` returns all four with their jobs, their groups (`buildLienCalendar`, by GC, then direct, then lien gone), the money, the pill's date, one line of facts (*by Oct 15 · in 14 days · 24 notices across 9 GCs*; *Dec 15 and after · 8 liens to file*; *18 jobs · nothing left to file · money still owed*), the sentence for an empty month (*Nothing else falls due in October.*), the stretch of the axis it covers and **Draft the N** — only for notices inside the desk's 30-day lead, since the Notices tab lists nothing further out. Under a bar with one date, a GC row says only *send 9 notices*. `lienNextDateCounts` counts the jobs per 15th; `lienPhoneLine` is a phone row's line. The real board on Oct 1 reads Overdue 18 · $43,021, This month 24 · $184,271, Next month 14 · $16,547, Later 8 · $4,689, and the four add up to the 64 jobs and $248,528.
- **The tab** ([`LienDeskCalendarTab`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskCalendarTab.tsx)): one toolbar of pills (All, Overdue, This month, Next month, Later, each with its count and money; the date and then the money drop away on a narrow desk through a container query) and the **N kinds not set ›** door to the kinds sheet (plain words where the pen does not write). A pill shows its bucket alone and shades its stretch of the axis; a second tap goes back to All, which folds Overdue again. Under All, each bucket with a job is a section with a bar that sticks under the date row: the fold, the title, the facts, **Draft the N ›** and the money. Overdue's rows sit straight under its bar, and its hover says who chases the money. The **date row** carries the search over the names column, each 15th with how many jobs fall due on it (amber for notices, slate where a lien does) and today; on a narrow axis the dates read as months alone. The **key** is one line at the bottom with its panel above it. The to-do, the density strip, the summary line and the footer sentence are gone, and the dead rows no longer repeat *nothing left to file after this day* (the bar says it once). The phone keeps the pills (search behind ⌕) and each bucket's rows as two lines, the dates under the name.
- **Measured on the walk**: at 1280 × 800 the first section bar is 143 px below the card's top and the first GC row 221 px (was 354), eight job rows in view; at 1000 × 600 three rows fit where one did. Walked by eye at 1000, 768, 375 and 320 (dark mode at 1000) and by measurement at 1440 and 1280; no sideways scroll at any width.
- **Kernel moves**: `lienCalendarTodo`, `lienCalendarDensity` and their types left `lienCalendar.ts`; the key's lien entry no longer mentions the density strip.
- **Guide** [`see-what-is-due-on-the-lien-calendar`](../../src/content/help/see-what-is-due-on-the-lien-calendar.md), new, in plain words. The desk's own guide is on the plain-words legacy list, so its *Start on the Calendar* section still describes the to-do and the density strip until that guide is rewritten whole.

## Fixed on the walk

- The selected bucket's band lay over the rows and faded their flags; it blends now (`multiply` in light, a soft `screen` in dark), so every mark keeps its ink.
- A GC row's words were cut off at 1000 px (*send 9 notices by Oct 15 · 14*); they are shorter under a one-date bar and end in an ellipsis with the whole words on hover.
- The dates on the date row ran together at 768 px (*Aug 17Sep 15*); a narrow axis shows the month alone.

## Left as it was

- At about 1000 px wide the desk's title bar still wraps **Share** to a second line. The tab row alone is 440 px, so hiding the button's word did not fit it; that is the title bar's own fix, not this tab's.
