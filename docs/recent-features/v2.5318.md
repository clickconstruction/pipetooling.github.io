# v2.5318 — Messages: How it is going reads the time each step took (2026-10-12)

Messages, cut 4: what Settings → Flows' How it is going reads (`src/lib/flows/going.ts`), from where each person stands on their flow. `going.ts` takes the times a company went through a step (`StepSpan`) and its runs through the per-job lane (`FunnelRun`), and nothing produced them. `src/lib/flows/goingInputs.ts` reads both from the same readings the boards read (`readFlow` over `flow_rows_kind`), so the two pages agree and no table is added. No screen, no SQL.

- **`goingInputs(flow, people)`** returns `{ spans, runs }` for everyone of a kind.
- **A step's time** runs from the day it came on to the day it was done. It came on the day the nearest step before it in its lane's chain finished, whatever that step's state: `readFlow`'s rule for `since`, kept for a done step. A lane's first step came on with its own first fact. A step on now runs from its `since` and a lapsed one from the day it lapsed, neither with an end yet. A loop's rounds are each a time through the step. An aside step, a change order, runs off the chain from its own stamps.
- **What sticks**: a step every fact finishes keeps the fact that turned true last. A fork ends on either, so it keeps none.
- **A run** through a per-job lane begins at the ask, the first round opened or the first stamp. It keeps every fact that turned true, once per round, and ends when a fork ended its lane or the person is off the flow. A job whose lane has not started has none.
