# v2.7583 — Lien desk: the Retainage notices list moved into its own file (2026-11-17)

The second cut of `src/components/jobs/LienDesk.tsx`, after the By date view (v2.7571). No behaviour changes.

**The list.** By paper → Retainage notices' list is the component `LienDeskRetainageList` in `src/components/jobs/LienDeskRetainageList.tsx`: the retainage nobody recorded above the piles, each pile under its sticky title, one row a job, and the three empty states. The JSX is the old block. The desk still calls `useLienPileHeads` for it and hands the result in, so the lit title is kept while the list is off screen, as before.

**The rule.** What the list shows was six inline lines; it is now the kernel `lienRetainageListView` in `src/lib/jobs/lienDeskRetainage.ts`: the entries in the piles' order, narrowed by the pressed pile and then by the find box, the row that is open (the picked job, else the first row where the list and the pane stand side by side, else none) and the piles to draw. Nine unit tests.

**Shared row styles.** `chip` and `severityColors` were private to the desk and every list uses them, so they are `lienRowChipStyle` and `lienSeverityColors` in `src/components/jobs/lienDeskRowStyles.ts`. The desk imports them under their old names. The Affidavit and notice lists will read the same two when they move.

**What stayed.** The pick (`retSelectedJobId`), the pressed pile, the ringed unrecorded job and the footer slot stay in the desk: To do's rows, By job's rows and the title bar's chips all set them. The pane was already its own component and its mount is unchanged. So this cut is smaller than the map's estimate of 115 lines: `LienDesk.tsx` went from 5,103 lines to 5,052.

**Proof.** `LienDesk.render.test.tsx` (207 tests, seven of them opening Retainage) and the page's two render tests pass with no edit. New: `LienDeskRetainageList.render.test.tsx` (five tests) and the nine kernel tests.
