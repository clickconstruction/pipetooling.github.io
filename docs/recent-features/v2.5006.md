# v2.5006 — Billed money: a payment with no bill picked counts oldest bill first on the board, GC Review and the statement (2026-10-09)

Client, plus `gc-statement-email-dispatch` (**redeploy after merge**). No migration. The owner's call of **2026-10-09**, on the decisions list: *a payment put on a job with no bill picked is applied oldest bill first everywhere*.

The Billed board, GC Review and the GC statement now follow the rule that the portal, the bill paper, the demand letter and the Bill tab already followed. The rule is `attributeJobPayments` in `_shared/paymentAttribution.ts`:

- Linked money is its bill's, in full.
- A job's unlinked money first pays the part of the job on no sent bill, given the job's total (v2.4534, the owner's call of 2026-10-05).
- Then it pays the sent bills oldest first.
- What is left over is a surplus on no bill.

## Job 273, read on prod (read-only)

- **The job:** a $56,365 job with three billed bills of $13,420, $665 and $3,500, $17,585 in all.
- **The money:** four payments on the job with no bill picked, **$39,680** in all.
- **Under the rule:** $38,780 pays the part of the job on no bill and the $900 left pays the oldest bill. The bills owe **$12,520, $665 and $3,500 — $16,685**, which is the job's revenue less everything paid.
- **Before this change:** the board, GC Review and the statement said **$17,585**.
- **The decisions row was out of date.** It read "$38,780 on no bill; the portal says its three bills are paid". That predated v2.4534, which made the portal's figure $16,685.

Four jobs on prod carry unlinked money and an open bill. Only 273's open bills change:

- 473 and 251 also have unlinked money, but the rule gives all of it to their off-bill work and their paid bills.
- 672's job is marked paid, so its bill is in no owed total.

## What changed

- **One helper for the board's rows.** `src/lib/jobs/billApplied.ts` (`billAppliedOnJob`) runs the rule with the job's revenue. `invoiceBilling.sumInvoiceAppliedFromJobPayments` and `jobsStagesBoard`'s `billedStageRowRemainingAmount` read it. That moves every billed row and everything summed from them: Who owes what, the aging chart, the forecast, call mode, the money card, the progress bar's Billed slice and the billed print.
- **Bill truth follows the rule** (`_shared/billTruth.ts`, "the ONE kernel behind every Owed figure").
  - The new `appliedByInvoiceUnderRule` runs the rule per job for jobs that carry unlinked money. Every other job reads exactly as `appliedByInvoiceId` did.
  - `computeBillTruth` uses it, and `computeBillTruthFromJobs` gives each payment its job. Payments carry an optional `job_id`, and bills an optional `sequence_order` and `billed_at`.
  - So the board's header, strip and Ready-to-ask tile agree with its rows again.
- **The lean header read loads what the rule needs** (`fetchStagesHeaderStats`). It used to read linked payments, payments from the last 30 days, and open bills only. Now it also reads:
  - the unlinked payments of every job with a billed bill;
  - the paid bills of each job that has unlinked money, because the rule walks every sent bill.

  Payments are deduped by id, so the 30-day *collected* window does not count a payment twice.
- **GC Review** (`gcReviewRollup`): a bill's `billPayments` are the rule's slices. Job 273's first bill names the **$900 share** of check #1951, not the whole check. `unmatchedOnJob` still totals the job's unlinked money for Draft Message.
- **The statement.** The client builder reads the review rows. The scheduled dispatch's `applyPaymentRule` (`render.ts`) re-nets the RPC's linked-only `remaining`:
  - It uses `attributeJobPayments` over each row's `job_bills`, `job_payments` and job total, then sums the subtotals and the grand total again.
  - A row whose job total was not read keeps the RPC figure. Without the total, money that paid work on no bill would land on the bills.
  - The line under each bill names the rule's slices.
- **Draft Message's note is information now:** *Paid on the job with no bill picked: Job 273 $39,680.00. The statement counts it as the portal does, first for the work on no bill, then for the oldest bills. To put it on a different bill, pick the bill in Edit Job → Payments.* The guide *track a general contractor on a job* says the same.
