# v2.6380 — Lien desk: a sent notice keeps the job's money (2026-11-02)

The second fix from Taunya's report on job 890 (v2.6376 was the GC). On the live desk as the dev, 890's row read **$0**, the pane's claim **$0**, gate 5 **Nothing billed · Bill the work first** with *The sent bills are paid. What is left on the job is not billed yet*, and the bills card under it read bill #890-2607150932 · Billed $285 · Paid $0 · **Owed $285**, then *Not billed yet · $285 of the job's $285*. No migration, no function.

- **The cause.** The same as v2.6376's: an entry's `openBalance` was the first month row's `open_balance` (`lien_billed_open()` in the RPC), and a job the RPC no longer lists has no row, so the entry read 0. The pane then took 0 as the billed-open figure, found the job's $285 "unbilled" (`lienUnbilled` is revenue less payments less billed-open) and drew the fifth gate.
- **The fix.** `useLienDeskData`, on the open desk (full mode), reads `jobs_ledger_invoices` and `jobs_ledger_payments` for just the jobs that are on the desk with no month row and computes `lienBilledOpen` for each — the client twin of the RPC's rule (every sent bill net of what the payment rule says it was paid) — and hands it to `buildLienDeskQueue` as the job row's `billed_open`; the kernel falls back to it when there is no month row, and a month row's figure still wins. `status` joins the desk's `jobs_ledger` columns, since a job billed as one shell is read by it. A failed read leaves the RPC's figure. The light read (the Dashboard's counts) skips it: the sent pile is not in those counts.
- **What 890 now shows.** Row $285; gate 5 gone (the sent bill owes money); *Claim amount on the notice* $285 and *Still unpaid on this job* $285; the *Not billed yet* line gone; the envelope and the letter-two words carry $285 as they already did from the job's balance.
- **Tests.** `lienDesk.test.ts`: `billed_open` is read with no row, is 0 without it, and loses to a month row; `useLienDeskData.zz.render.test.tsx`: the full read over a sent-only job with one billed bill of $285 gives the entry $285 and leaves a listed job's RPC figure alone.
