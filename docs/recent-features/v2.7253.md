# v2.7253 — Lien desk: a job paid in kind is treated as paid and is off the desk (2026-11-14)

The owner, looking at the By date tab: jobs he is paid for in kind (a fixed credit each month against finished work) stood on the Lien desk with lien deadlines. Each was correctly on its in-kind deal; no lien code read the mark. His rule: "The lien desk should treat a job paid in kind as though it was paid and it should not be on the lien desk."

## The rule

A job whose `jobs_ledger.in_kind_arrangement_id` is set is off the Lien desk, the way a job marked Uncollectible is (v2.4788). Ending a deal clears the link on its jobs (the trigger `in_kind_arrangements_end_releases_jobs`, migration `20261110071633`), so a set link means an open deal and a job comes back by itself on the desk's next read.

The rule is one pure kernel, [`src/lib/jobs/lienInKind.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienInKind.ts): `jobOffLienDeskInKind`, `lienInKindJobIds`, `lienJobIdsOffDesk` (the ids a desk read drops after its jobs join, the hidden ZZ test jobs with them), `withLienInKindColumn` (a read names the link through the kernel) and the Lien window's note.

## Every reader calls it

| Reader | What it feeds | Where the job leaves |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `useLienDeskData` | To do, By paper's three lists, the piles and their counts, the money, the notice run and the affidavit print run, the lien month, Share's payload; in light mode the Dashboard's lien cards, the Pipeline's gavel count and money card, the AR chips | the seam the ZZ test jobs leave by: the due months, the desk items, the affidavit and retainage windows and the jobs, before any queue is built |
| `lienCalendarRows.ts` | By date (`buildLienCalendarRows`) and the lien clocks behind the Pipeline's Billed and Collections rows (`billedLienClockJobsOf`) | beside the Uncollectible skip: no clock is read, so no row and no lien date |
| `useLienTimelineBook` | By job | the same seam, its own read |
| `useGcOnNoticeData` | Put a GC on notice | the same seam, its own read |
| `useLienWatchNudge` | the Dashboard's lien watch | filtered on its own read of the billed jobs |

No migration and no edge function: the three `list_lien_*` RPCs still list such a job, and the client drops it. `send-lien-desk-summary` only renders the payload the desk builds, so the shared summary follows with no redeploy.

## The job's own Lien window

It still opens from the job, whole. `lienInKindWindowNote` puts one line under its header (`data-lien-window-in-kind`): the job is paid in kind, it is off the desk while the deal is open, nobody is reminded of its lien dates there, the dates still run, and every paper still drafts from the window. No paper on file is hidden or changed, and no word of a notice, an affidavit, a waiver or a release changed.

## The trade-off, said once

The statutory lien days run whether or not the deal is honoured. Off the desk, nothing reminds anyone of them. That is the owner's decision (2026-11-14); the guide says it in plain words under *A job paid in kind is off the desk*.

## Not in this change

- **The firm's portal** (`legal-portal`) reads the two lien RPCs with its own column list and would still draw such a job's path if the job were on a matter. Changing it is an edge deploy of its own.
- **Materials → Job accounts**: the held-materials line reads the two RPCs directly and can still name a lien day on such a job.
- **A paper in flight when the job joins a deal** (a draft, a notice printed and not yet recorded) leaves the desk's lists with its job. It is still on the job's Lien window.
