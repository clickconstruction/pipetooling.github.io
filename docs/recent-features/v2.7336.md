# v2.7336 — Pipeline: Collections reads whole in the stage bar, and the desk buttons stand off its edge (2026-11-15)

Taunya, with two screenshots of the stage bar on Jobs → Pipeline (2026-11-15): the word Collections was cut where the Lien desk's gavel began, and the Legal desk's scales sat flush against the bar's rounded right border. She does not like any part of a word cut, and wanted the buttons spaced. Client only, CSS only: no migration, no edge function.

## The cause

Every cell of the bar was `flex: 1 1 0`: an even share of the row whatever it held (Billed Awaiting Payment 1.6 shares). The last stage's cell, `.stagesStageSegTail`, took the same one share but holds the stage and the desks' two 32 px squares. Its stage was the only part allowed to shrink (`flex: 0 1 auto; min-width: 0`), and the name clips (`overflow: hidden`), so under about 1,300 px the name gave up the width the squares needed. The cell had no padding of its own, so once the name was shrinking the scales ended on the bar's border. The count line does not clip; it ran on under the gavel, which paints over it.

## The change (`src/index.css`, scoped to `[data-stages-stage-bar]`)

- A stage, and the last stage's cell, is `flex: 1 1 auto`: as wide as its words (the cell: its words and its squares) plus an even share of the spare room. No name is cut while the bar has room for all of them, and past that every cell gives way in proportion, as before.
- The last stage's cell has `gap: 0.75rem` and `padding-right: 0.75rem` (a stage's own padding): the squares stand 12 px apart and at least 12 px off the bar's edge. Under the 800 px container step, where the arrows already go, both are `0.5rem`.
- Only the Pipeline's bar carries `data-stages-stage-bar`. The Messages, Settings and Development strips wear the same classes over their own cells and keep their even shares.

No other stage's name is cut at any of these widths, before or after, and the page's own sideways overflow is what it was (50 px at 1,024 and 306 px at 768, before and after, none of it the bar's). The stage and its squares are still centered together in the last cell, so the room to the edge grows with the window; 12 px (8 px under the step) is its floor. The gavel's badge, at two digits or three, clears the scales by 4.2 px (it overlapped them by 2.2 px before), and a badge on the scales clears the bar's edge.

The cells are no longer equal: at 1,280 they are 176, 177, 202, 282 and 296 px (were 209, 209, 209, 320 and 185: an even share each, plus a stage's own padding, which the last cell did not have). The `isWide` share on Billed Awaiting Payment no longer does anything in this bar, since the cell's own words set its width.

A phone draws the stage chips, not this bar (`phoneBoard`), so nothing changes there: read at 375 px after a reload, no bar in the page. Dark and light draw the same widths; the change touches no colour.
