# v2.8134 — Permits, Market: the count of companies that build says it covers every city (2026-11-20)

Punch list #405 ([the card](../../to-dos/permits-market-page/README.md)), the first of three lines its audit of 2026-11-20 left under *Seen while building, not done*: **one count says *here* and means every city.**

## What was wrong

Market is drawn a city at a time. Three things on it counted a company's permits in every city the pull reads, and each said *here*:

- the third tile, *companies we bid pulled a permit here*,
- the table's column, *Permits here, 12 mo*,
- the table's first chip, *Builds here*.

So San Antonio's page and Seguin's page showed the same total under a word that read as that city alone.

## Which was fixed, the count or the word

**The word. The count stands.**

The card settles what is counted. Its *How each number is counted* says a company's permits are *commercial permits in twelve months, in all five cities, under that key or under a name a person tied to the customer*. The first build wrote the same in its fragment ([v2.7978](./v2.7978.md)). The table is a list of the companies we priced for, and the question it answers is whether estimating hours go to companies that build at all. A company that builds in Austin and not in San Antonio still builds.

A count for the page's own city was not what the card asked for, so none was added. The small words under a company already say where its permits are, a city at a time, each a press.

## What the office sees

- The tile reads **companies we bid pulled a permit in the cities we read**.

- The chip reads **Builds**.
- No number moved.

The heading, *Do the companies we bid build here?*, is the card's name for the part and stays. The line under it already said *in the cities we read*.
