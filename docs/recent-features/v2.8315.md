# v2.8315 — Lien grid: the owner's answers fill their two columns, and a GC reads by name (punch list #85, a review residual) (2026-11-22)

Punch list #85 (`to-dos/legal-portal-firm-ready/README.md`), one of the residuals the October reviews left: *a GC who is not the payer reads GC on the portal; the grid's Paid out to GC and 10 % reserved stay ? after the owner answers.* Two faults, one function to redeploy, `legal-portal`. No migration.

## What was found

**The two columns.**

- The answers are kept on the sent notice's record: `job_lien_desk_items.fields.ownerCall` (`src/lib/jobs/lienOwnerCall.ts`), typed in *The owner called* on the Lien desk. The toast on saving says *the affidavit pile and the grid read it*.
- The grid never read it. `lienGridRows` (`src/lib/jobs/lienTimelineBook.ts`) wrote `paidOut: ''` and `reserved: ''` for every job, so both columns were blank on the office's own print too, not only on the portal. The print's footnote said the office fills them by hand.
- On the portal a second thing stood in the way. The book's desk items travel without `fields` by design (`_shared/legalLienBookShape.ts`, item 2: nothing anyone said), so the page could not have read the call even if the kernel had looked.
- On prod on 2026-11-22: 49 live § 53.056 notice items, none with an owner's call recorded. The fault was waiting for the first call.

**The GC's name.**

- `legal-portal` reads `customers` for the matters' payers and looked each job's GC up among those rows. A job whose GC is another customer found none, `gcCustomer.name` was null, and `payerForJob` (`src/lib/legal/legalPacket.ts`) wrote the word *GC* as the account's name.
- On prod on 2026-11-22: 28 jobs on the seven matters, 24 with a GC, none whose GC is not the matter's payer. Latent as well.

## What changed

- **One rule for the two columns** ([`_shared/lienOwnerFacts.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/lienOwnerFacts.ts), no imports): `lienOwnerFactsFromCall` reads a stored call as four values, `{ paidOut, owesAmount, reserved, releasedOn }`. *Paid out* is the other side of the call's *do you still owe the GC anything*. `lienOwnerFactsByJob` takes each job's newest call off its live notice items, the desk's own pick (`ownerCallByJobFrom`) without needing the notice's draft block. `lienOwnerFactsWords` writes the cells in the playbook grid's words: *yes* or *no · $12,000 owed*, and *yes · held*, *released Sep 3* or *never*. No answer is a blank.
- **The book carries them.** `buildLienTimelineBook` puts `ownerFacts` on each row: from `input.ownerFactsByJob` when the reader hands it, else off `input.items`. `lienGridRows` words them, so `lienGridHtml` (the print), the Lien desk's grid and the firm's grid all fill from one place. The print's footnote now says the two columns fill from the owner's call.
- **`legal-portal`** reads the live notice items once more with `LIEN_BOOK_OWNER_CALL_SELECT` (`job_id, kind, voided_at, fields`). The rows never leave the function. `shapeLienBookForCounsel` folds the kept jobs' calls to `lienBook.ownerFacts` and cuts each to its four keys. A ZZ test job, a job paid in kind and a job off the lien path lose their facts with the job. A failed read leaves the columns blank and the book still goes.
- **The page**: `parseLienBookRaw` reads `ownerFacts` when the payload has the key, and `assembleLienBookInput` hands it on. The `?` under the two columns now says *The owner's answer is not on record yet* on hover.
- **The GC's name** ([`_shared/legalGcName.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/legalGcName.ts)): `gcIdsToRead(jobs, payers)` names the jobs' GCs the payers' rows do not hold, one read takes their `id, name`, and `gcCustomerFor` picks the name from either list.
- **The sample** reads its book's facts off the sample matter's own notice item (`withSampleOwnerFacts`), in the function's sample branch and on the page, which builds the sample itself. The fixture file is untouched, so no other function that carries it drifts.

## What travels to the firm that did not before

For each job on the grid whose owner's call is recorded: whether the owner has paid the GC out, the dollars it says it still owes, what became of the 10 %, and the day it was released. Nothing else off the call: not the note, not who took it or when, not the original contract's completion, not that the owner asked to pay direct. `legalLienBookShape.test.ts` holds that with a call full of private words. For a job on a matter the firm already read the whole call in words under the notice on Paper. The grid is the whole book, so for the other jobs these four values are new. They are the columns counsel's memo asked for, and the grid already carried the bond and the contract's completion from the same calls.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **Why not send the notice items' `fields` and let the page read the call?** `fields` holds the draft, the cover letter, the skip reason and the GC's word. Item 2's rule is that a select widened later cannot leak by itself, so the function folds the call and sends four values.
- **Why not show *inside the hold* on a released 10 %, as the playbook grid does?** That needs the original contract's completion date, a fifth value. The firm reads it on Paper for a job it holds.
- **An answer of *unknown*** reads as a blank, the same as no call. The grid cannot tell *asked and not answered* from *not asked*. The matter's Paper tab can.
