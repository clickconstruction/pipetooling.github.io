# v2.4552 — Submittals: a long part name no longer runs over the answer beside it (2026-10-05)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. A defect in the rows table redrawn in [v2.4542](v2.4542.md), found while taking screenshots of the SpaceX bid in a 1,000 px window: *ELKAY LZSTL8WSLK* and *TSL.MON.B.38.2.PS1.BK* printed over *No answer yet*. A wide window hid it — the redraw was checked at 1,400 px and at phone width, and nothing between.

## What was wrong

- A part's maker and model was held to one line (`white-space: nowrap`). In a narrow product column it ran out of its grid cell and over the answer in the next one.
- At that width the answer column kept its 12rem and the product column was left with about 90 px.

## What changed

- **The maker and model wraps inside its column at every width** (`SubmittalRowProducts`): it can break anywhere, and the quiet catalog words beside it give up their room first.
- **A table narrower than 900 px stacks each answer under its part** and drops the *Their answer* heading, as the phone cards do. The rule measures the table itself (a container query on `.sub-rows-wrap`), not the screen, so it holds whatever sits beside the table. At 1,000 px the part's line went from about 90 px to 280.
- A wide table is as it was: answers in their own column, lined up under the heading.

## Tests and checks

`SubmittalRowsTable.render.test.tsx` (+1: the maker and model is not held to one line). Checked in the app on BP375, read only: at 1,000 px no part name overlaps an answer (33 part lines, 0 overlaps, measured); at 1,400 px the answers still line up under *Their answer*.
