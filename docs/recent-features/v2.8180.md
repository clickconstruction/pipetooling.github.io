# v2.8180 — Document builder: the list of jobs under your usual sheet says how many are on it (2026-11-21)

Punch list #404. Found on the walk of v2.8153 and v2.8155 on the live site on 2026-11-21, and Taunya asked for the fix the same day. No migration and no function.

## What was wrong

On **The standing sheet** of *Insurance requirements for subs*, the card that lists the jobs has two headings. With a change to the usual waiting it reads *Where this change goes*. With none it read *Every job is on your usual*, whatever the rows under it said.

On the walk the usual was published while a job had changes of its own waiting. That job was held, as it should be, so its sheet stayed on the older revision. The card then read *Every job is on your usual* over a row that read *Held. It has changes of its own waiting.* It read the same over a job with no sheet at all.

A second case had no word on the screen. A held job whose own changes are taken back is clean again but still on the older revision. Its row read *Revision 1* in green, the same as a job that is up to date.

## The fix

A job is **on the usual** when it has a published sheet and the usual was not published again since (`jobIsOnUsual` in `src/lib/documentBuilder/sheetScreen.ts`). Changes of its own that wait do not take it off, since subs do not see them yet.

- `usualJobsHead` words the heading. With no change waiting it reads *Every job is on your usual* only when every job is, *1 of 2 jobs is on your usual* when some are, and *No job is on your usual yet* when none is.
- `usualPlanChip` takes a fourth value, `behind`. With no change waiting, a clean job on an older revision reads *Revision 1, behind your usual* in amber. With a change waiting it reads *Gets it, as revision 2* as before.
- The fine print under the rows now reads *A job that is held or behind gets your usual when you publish that job.*

`FilledBuilder.tsx` draws the heading and the chip from those two. Nothing about what is published, to whom, or when has changed.

## Tests

- `sheetScreen.test.ts`: which jobs count as on the usual, the heading's five readings, the behind chip, and the new words held to the plain-words rules.
- `FilledBuilder.render.test.tsx`: every job on the usual reads *Every job*, and a usual at revision 2 over one job at revision 1 reads *1 of 2 jobs is on your usual* with the behind chip on that job's row.

## Docs

The guide *set the insurance a job's subs must carry* gains two lines under **Publish your usual**.
