# v2.5396 — Messages: the residential customer flow, from the booking to the review ask (2026-10-12)

Residential R4 · 1 of 4 (`to-dos/gc-mode/messages/RESIDENTIAL_BUILD_PLAN.md` on `spike/gc-mode`), by Customer communication helper 3, directed by customer-comunication. It stacks on R0 (#5565), which adds the `residential` and `sub` kinds. The flow is the customer prototype's screen 4 (`customer-messages-prototype.html` v0.8, Settings → Flows). Migration `20261014050000_residential_flow`, renumbered from `20261013170000` while it waited; no edge change and no screen.

- **Eleven facts** (`src/lib/flows/facts.ts`), each read from what main keeps. Nothing new is stored. Each `reads` says what helper 1's loader (R4 · 2, #5560) reads.
  - `booked`: the first `job_schedule_blocks` row on the job.
  - `arrived`: the first `clock_sessions.clocked_in_at` on it that is not revoked or rejected.
  - `found`: the first `reports` row on it whose answers have words. A percent alone is not a finding.
  - `estimate_sent`: the newest `estimates.sent_at`, an estimate rather than a change order.
  - `estimate_approved`: `acceptor_consented_at` once the status is `customer_accepted`, which accept-estimate writes.
  - `working`: the first `job_status_events` move to Working, else the job's status past Waiting with no day.
  - `done`: the job's status at Ready to Bill or past, dated by the first move there after the last move back to Working.
  - `billed`: `jobs_ledger_invoices.billed_at`, else `sent_to_customer_at`, per bill.
  - `paid`: a sent bill covered by `attributeJobPayments`, its own payments and then the job's unlinked money, else its `paid` status. The day is the newest `paid_on` it took.
  - `on_way` and `review_asked` are kept by half. A sent text keeps no line of its own, so the loader's word rules on an out text stand in (`isOnOurWayText`, `isReviewAskText`). Their gap, `messages.quick_reply_id`, goes in R3's SQL.
  - Six of the facts sit in a fifth picker group, **On the job**: booked through found, working and done.
- **Two papers**, the estimate and the bill. Each finishes its step when it goes (`UNSIGNED` in `nudges.ts`), as our bid does. The yes and the payment are steps of their own.
- **The portal's words** (`PAPER_WORDS`, `src/lib/portal/pageWords.ts`) gain the estimate and the bill in English and Spanish, since the table holds every paper.
- **The flow** (`SEED_FLOWS.residential`, `src/lib/flows/seed.ts`): one Per job lane.
  - The steps, in order: Booked, On the way, Arrived, What we found, Estimate sent, Approved, Working, Done, Billed, Paid and Review asked.
  - The step ids are the prototype's: `booked`, `onway`, `arrived`, `found`, `estimate`, `approved`, `working`, `done`, `billed`, `paid`, `review`. A quick reply ties to a step by its id.
  - Approved and Paid are the customer's move. Every other step is ours, with its We owe them words.
  - Billed and Paid loop once per bill (`each bill`, `rounds`), so a job with two bills reads each one.
  - The usual days are a first guess, until a year of done steps gives the middle.
- **The reminders, all off.**
  - Approved: the estimate reminder, a text from the app after one quiet day.
  - Paid: the bill reminder, a text from the app at the bill's pay by day.
  - Estimate sent and Billed carry none, because `nudgeDue` never fires on our move.
  - The review ask waits for R4 · 4's stage send after paid.
  - Nothing sends until the owner turns a step on (`AppSends`, decision 6 of the plan).
  - The loader stamps each bill's pay by day from the expected pay model. With no day, the reminder stays quiet.
- **The SQL**: the migration does three things.
  - It widens `flows_kind_known` to `residential` and `sub`.
  - It adds the eleven facts and the two papers to `flow_fact_ids()` and `flow_paper_ids()`.
  - It seeds the residential flow through `flows_seed()`. On prod, every other kind already has its flow, so the run adds the residential flow alone.
  - `FLOW_KINDS` gains both kinds to match. `flowsSql.test.ts` now reads the kinds from the newest migration that defines `flows_kind_known`.
- **Tests**:
