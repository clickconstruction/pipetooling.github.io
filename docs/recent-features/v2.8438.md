# v2.8438 — Customers: removing a contact asks first (2026-11-24)

A small fault the #416 walk found and left (`to-dos/document-builder-whats-left.md`, walk 1i, while a test contact was cleared off a customer): **Remove** on a customer's contact asked nothing first. Diane's call of 2026-11-24: it asks. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## The cause

`CustomerContactsSection` (Edit customer → Contacts) ran its delete of the `customer_contact_persons` row straight from the press. One press on a small red word took a person off the customer, and with them their copy of every bill.

Reproduced before the change: the new render test, run against the old component, found no confirm window after **Remove**.

## What changed

- `removeContactConfirm` in `src/lib/customers/removeContactConfirm.ts` writes the question: *Remove Dana Whitlock from this customer's contacts? A dev can put them back for 90 days, from Recently deleted in Settings.* A contact who gets a copy of every bill adds *They stop getting a copy of every bill.* The press reads **Remove Dana Whitlock** in red, and Cancel reads **Keep them**.
- The section asks through the shared `useConfirmDialog` before the delete. **Keep them**, Escape or a press outside writes nothing and leaves the contact's sheet open.
- The question names the contact by the name the list holds, so a name typed over in the open sheet does not change who it says is going.
- A removal that fails now words its reason through `formatErrorMessage`.
- Help: *send a bill to more than one person* says the press asks and where a removed contact comes back from.

## Can it be undone

Read in the migrations, not run: `customer_contact_persons` has been in the deleted-records archive since `20260716230000_deleted_records_archive_tier2.sql`, grouped by its customer, the archive is purged after 90 days (`20260716120000_deleted_records_archive.sql`), and `list_deleted_records` lists a group by its key whether or not its parent was deleted. So the words say a dev can put the contact back, and do not say it is gone for good. No contact was removed and restored on the live site for this change; the dev seat that could have read the archive row of the walk's removed test contact timed out.

## Tests

- `removeContactConfirm.test.ts`: the words, the press and Cancel for a plain contact and for one who gets every bill; a row with no name reads *this contact*; the words pass the plain-words rules.
- `CustomerContactsSection.render.test.tsx`: **Remove** opens the window and deletes nothing; **Keep them** closes it with nothing written and the sheet still open; the press that names the contact deletes the row and the list is read again.
