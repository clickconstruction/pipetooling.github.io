# v2.7327 — The Lien desk's draft step goes: the paper saves itself (2026-11-15)

Client only (one edge-function file changes words; see below). PR B of the owner's call of 2026-11-15 (PR A is v2.7324, one press per footer): the office never "drafts" a lien paper — the paper is drawn from the job and the record when the pane opens — so the draft step goes. The row in `job_lien_desk_items` still reads `drafted` until it moves on; `approval_mode`, the signature payload, the piles' keys (`to_draft`) and `saveLienDeskDraft` are unchanged.

## What changed

- **Autosave** (`LienDesk.tsx`, the notice pane; `LienDeskAffidavitPane.tsx`): `draftTouch` counts the changes — a month ticked, the cover box, a wording or pay-line edit committed on the paper or sent from the preview window, a claim correction saved or cleared, the affidavit's months set by hand — and an effect writes the row through `ensureDraft(true)` `LIEN_DRAFT_AUTOSAVE_MS` (800 ms, `lienDeskIo.ts`) after the last one, then re-reads the desk. Never on open (the count starts at 0 and resets on every job change), never when nothing changed, only for the office on a row that is still `drafted` (or none). The state line shows *Saving…* then *✓ Saved* for 2.5 s (`data-lien-desk-saved`, `data-lien-affidavit-saved`); a failure shows `formatErrorMessage` in a toast. The forward presses keep saving first: `run` clears the pending wait, and `ensureDraft` awaits a save the autosave has in flight, then updates the row it made (`draftIdRef` holds the id until the desk re-reads), so no second row is ever made. The enclosed release already saved on its own toggle.
- **Save draft is gone** from the notice footer (`lienFooter`'s `links` carry `by_hand` alone). The preview window keeps its own *Save draft*, which saves at once; the desk would have saved a moment later anyway.
- **To send**: `LIEN_DESK_PILES`, `LIEN_AFFIDAVIT_PILES` and `LIEN_RETAINAGE_PILES` label `to_draft` *To send*; the empty pile reads *Nothing is left to send.* The ladders (`lienNextUpSteps.ts`) step *Open the notice* / *Open the affidavit*, their briefs say the paper is drawn from the record and name *He already okayed it ▾* as the other move; the To do rows (`lienNextUp.ts`) read *Notice to send*, *Affidavit to send*, *Retainage notice to send*, their presses *Open the notice* / *Open the affidavit*. `lienAffidavitRedraw` and the affidavit pane say *back in To send*. `lienAffidavitBatch`'s foot reads *N in To send were never opened. Open each from its own paper.* — the batch's rule (a row only when opened and changed, or approved) is unchanged and now written in the guide.
- **The dev seat**: `supabase/functions/_shared/devMcpAffidavitDraft.ts` says *To send* in its refusal, its dry-run `desk.pile` and its `next` line. **dev-mcp needs a redeploy for the words** (`supabase functions deploy dev-mcp`); nothing else in it changed.
- Guides *send lien notices from the Lien desk*, *file a lien and never miss its deadlines*, *read the reminders ladder on the Lien desk*; `docs/GLOSSARY.md`'s pile list.

## Not changed (follow-ups)

- The lower-case *to draft* on the Pipeline's Lien desk door (`lienDeskMoneyCard.ts`), the month-rhythm bar (`lienMonthRhythm.ts`) and the Dashboard's lien card (`dashboardNeedsYou.ts`) still say *to draft*; the calendar's *Draft the N* press too.
