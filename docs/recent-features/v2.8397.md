# v2.8397 — The confirm window’s buttons are easier to tap on a phone (2026-11-23)

A small fault the #389 walk found and left (`to-dos/sub-nda-builder/README.md`): the shared confirm window drew its two presses 35.5 px tall at both widths, under the 44 px a thumb needs. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## The cause

`ConfirmDialogProvider` (`src/contexts/ConfirmDialogContext.tsx`), behind `useConfirmDialog` (160 files) and `usePromptDialog` (19), sized Cancel and the act by their padding and words alone, `0.5rem 1rem` at 0.875rem, with no floor at a phone's width.

## What changed

- `confirmPressSize` in `src/lib/confirmDialogPress.ts`: at 640 px and under, `min-height: 44px` with `box-sizing: border-box`; at a desk, the same padding and no floor.
- The provider reads `useNarrowViewport640` and spreads it into both presses. The colours, the words and every caller are unchanged. At a phone each window grows by the presses' 8.5 px and nothing else moves; the presses already sit on their own line.
- Not changed: the older `ConfirmDialog` component (14 files), a separate call.

## Tests

- `confirmDialogPress.test.ts`: 44 px at a phone, the desk padding with no floor.
- `ConfirmDialogContext.render.test.tsx`: at a phone width both presses carry `min-height: 44px`; at a desk width neither does and the padding is the same.
- Harness (not committed): the window at 390 px measures 44 px presses in Chromium and WebKit, light and dark; at 1,280 px the presses have no floor.
