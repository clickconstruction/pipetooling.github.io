# v2.5032 — AIA pay applications: each one shows the bill it became, and what was paid (2026-10-09)

Migration `20261010002000`. No edge function. This builds the owner's call of 2026-10-09 on `to-dos/aia-pay-application-follow-ups.md` item 2: the office picks the bill an application became. The app does not match it by itself. An amount-and-date match only pre-fills the pick. The train's mock-up drew the line in its section C: *Paid $13,588.20 · Aug 22* under each application, and *bill not yet tied* on a dotted edge. The mock-up artifact could not be read from this session, so this follows the card's description of section C. PUNCHLIST chose that Bill Customer ties a bill on its send, and left creating a bill from an application out.

## The change

- **The column** (`20261010002000`, `docs/migrations/20261010002000_job_pay_applications_invoice.md`):
  - `job_pay_applications.invoice_id` references `jobs_ledger_invoices`, and a deleted bill unties it.
  - A unique index on live rows means one bill belongs to at most one live application.
  - The stamp trigger is restated with two additions. An update that moves only `invoice_id` keeps the saved stamps, so a tie is not a save. A bill must be on the application's job, and a restore whose bill was taken comes back untied.
- **The rules** (`src/lib/aiaPayApplicationBill.ts`, new, pure):
  - `aiaPaidLine` reads a tied bill the way the job window's bills do. It uses `attributeJobPayments` (linked payments, then unlinked money oldest bill first) with `invoiceLedgerState` and the newest payment's day:
    - *Paid $13,588.20 · Aug 22*
    - *Paid $5,000.00 of $13,588.20 · Aug 10*
    - *Billed $13,588.20 · nothing paid yet*
    - *$13,588.20 marked paid · no payment on record*
    - *Bill $13,588.20 not sent yet*
    - *Bill not yet tied*
    - *The tied bill is not on this job*
  - `aiaBillOptions` offers the job's bills, oldest first, leaving out a bill another live application holds.
  - `suggestAiaBill` pre-fills the bill whose amount is the payment due, to the cent. When several match, it takes the one sent nearest the application's date, else its period. It offers none when two are as near.
  - `suggestAiaApplication` does the reverse for Bill Customer: a live, untied application whose payment due is the bill's amount, the one dated nearest today.
  - `canTiePayApplication` matches the table's policies: dev, master, assistant, controller.
- **The reads and the write** (`aiaPayApplicationsIo.ts`):
  - The newest column set adds `invoice_id`, and the read goes again without it on a database that lacks it.
  - The deleted list now falls back the same way. Before, it came back empty on any error.
  - A save reads the bill back with the row and never writes it.
  - `tiePayApplicationBill(appId, invoiceId | null)` writes the bill alone. It throws `PayApplicationBillTaken` on the index, `PayApplicationTieNotReady` before the push, and the trigger's own words for a bill on another job.
  - `SavedPayApplication.invoiceId` is absent when the column was not read, and the window then offers no tie.
- **The history** (`AiaG702G703History.tsx`): each live line gains the bill line under its saved and workbook lines.
  - Untied, it reads *Bill not yet tied* in a dashed box with **Tie a bill…**. Tied, it reads the paid line with **change**.
  - The pick lists *#2 · $13,588.20 · sent Aug 2*, starts on the match, and says *This bill is the payment due, to the cent.*
  - **Tie it** or **Untie it** writes the bill, reloads the applications and says so.
  - A role that cannot write the table reads the line and gets no pick.
- **Bill Customer** (`SendRecordInvoiceModal.tsx`): on a job with saved applications that can still be tied, and only for the roles that can write them, one line reads *This bill is pay application [No. 3 · $2,630.00 due · period to Sep 30 ▾]*.
  - The match pre-picks it, the office may change it or choose **none**, and *Its payment due is this amount, to the cent.* marks a pre-pick.
  - After any of the three sends succeeds (Stripe, physical, HouseCall Pro), the app ties that application to the bill it just sent.
  - A tie that fails says *The bill went. Tying it to pay application 3 did not: …* and never undoes the send.
  - Before the push nothing is read, because no application carries the column, so the line does not show.

## Left

- Creating a bill from an application (a draft at its payment due, then Bill Customer on it) is out. It reaches into the draft and remainder planning, and PUNCHLIST kept it out tonight.
- The primary role can open the AIA window and send bills, but the table's policies keep it from tying. It reads the line and gets no pick.
- The job window's money card counts only payments linked to a bill for its *paid* day. The bills list and this line use the shared attribution, so on a job with unlinked money the money card's day can differ.
