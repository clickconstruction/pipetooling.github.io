# v2.6816 — In kind: a payment that pays a bill down but is not money (2026-11-09)

A customer who is also the company's landlord is settling $22,643.46 of finished work across fifteen jobs by taking $1,000 a month off the rent the company owes him. No cash moves. The office (2026-11-09) wants it on the books honestly: the jobs' revenue stays whole (not a discount, not a write-down), each month's credit pays the bills down, and nothing claims cash arrived. Before this, a hand-recorded payment took one of Cash, Check, Wire, ACH, Card (external), Other — and `moneyInWeek.ts` counted every non-check hand-typed payment as *Landed* money in the bank and listed it, from `list_unlinked_payments_for_bank_payments`, as a payment waiting on a deposit. Recording the credit as Other would have shown $1,000 of cash each month that never arrived, for ever.

## What shipped

- **One predicate, shared.** `supabase/functions/_shared/inKindPayment.ts`: `IN_KIND_PAYMENT_TYPE = 'In kind'`, `isInKindPaymentType()` (the word *in kind* / *in-kind* / *inkind*, anywhere in `payment_type`, never the *kind* inside another word) and `IN_KIND_CUSTOMER_WORD = 'Credit'`. The app's kernel `src/lib/jobs/inKindPayment.ts` adds `PAYMENT_KIND_CHOICES` (Cash, Check, Wire, ACH, Card (external), In kind, Other), the memo words (`IN_KIND_MEMO_LABEL` *What was given instead of money*, the placeholder, `IN_KIND_MEMO_REQUIRED`), `inKindMemoMissing()` and `inKindWindowNote()`. `instrumentWord()` learns `'in kind'`, so the Bill tab's chip reads *In kind · typed by hand* (or *· recorded in Stripe*) and the legal packet's line reads *in kind*.
- **Offered wherever a kind is picked.** `BilledPaymentConfirmationModal` draws its select from `PAYMENT_KIND_CHOICES`; with In kind picked the memo label becomes *What was given instead of money*, a note under the kind says what it counts as and what Stripe will do, and Confirm refuses a blank memo. `JobFormPaymentLine`'s free-text Type box gets a `<datalist>` of the same kinds; an in-kind row's Memo box carries the same label and an amber border until the memo is typed (asked for, not enforced — the line's save path is unchanged).
- **Pays the bill down as any payment does.** Nothing changed: `mark_invoice_paid` / `mark_job_paid` take `p_payment_type` as free text, `payments_made` is trigger-maintained, the job moves to Paid at zero.
- **Never money.** `buildMoneyInWeek` drops in-kind rows from `payments` and `unlinked` before it reads a dollar, so one is never *Landed*, *At the bank* or *In the drawer* (test: a $1,000 in-kind row beside a $1,000 posted deposit — the deposit shows *not applied yet*, the row shows nowhere). `list_unlinked_payments_for_bank_payments` (migration `20261109180000_unlinked_payments_skip_in_kind.sql`, in place, same shape) leaves such rows out, and `BankPaymentsModal` filters the rows it reads too, so nothing depends on the push landing first. `isCheckPayment` never matches it, so neither the clearing wait nor the held Stripe mark sees it.
- **A Stripe bill: the existing two paths, decided safe.** Under the open balance, `record-stripe-invoice-out-of-band-payment` writes a Stripe credit note and the ledger row itself — a credit note is exactly a non-cash reduction of what is due, and the row carries the kind. Its line now reads *Credit applied Mar 3 · $1,000.00* (never *received*), mirrored in `stripeCreditLineText` and `customerPaymentTypeWord` on the client. At the balance, `invoices.pay({ paid_out_of_band: true })` with the kind and memo on the metadata; the webhook's `mark_invoice_paid_from_stripe` writes the row with `payment_type = 'In kind'` and the memo as its note (not *Stripe*), so the money-in week never reads it as a Stripe payment. Neither path moves money or sends a receipt, so neither is refused.
- **Customer-facing words.** `portalPaymentMethodLabel` (portal statement, printed invoice's payment history) and `_shared/billPaidBy.ts`'s `paymentKind` → `'in-kind'` → `checkLabel` *Credit* (GC statement email, GC Review) read *Credit*. The paid-job email is internal and names no kind.
- **Help:** `record-payments-so-they-count.md` (new section *A payment that is not money*), `ready-to-bill-pipeline.md` (the Billed → Paid paragraph names the kind).

## Owed after the merge

- `supabase db push` — the RPC. The client holds without it.
- `supabase functions deploy record-stripe-invoice-out-of-band-payment` — the credit line's words on a Stripe part payment (*Credit applied* instead of *Payment received*). `customer-portal` and `gc-statement-email-dispatch` bundle `_shared/billPaidBy.ts`; until redeployed a statement reads the old generic *Payment* for an in-kind row, never a cash word.

## How to record the rent credit once it is live

Each month, on the oldest job still open: Edit Job → Bill → the open bill's **Record payment** (or **+ Record a cash or check payment** under ③ on a job with no bill) → amount $1,000.00 (or what is left on the bill), the month's date, Payment type **In kind**, memo *Rent credit, March 2026* → Confirm. When a bill has less than $1,000 open, record what is open on it and the rest on the next job. On a Stripe bill the window does the Stripe side itself; on a plain bill it is a ledger row. Nothing lands on the Dashboard's money-in week or Accounts Receivable's deposit list, the job moves to Paid at zero, and the customer's statement reads *Credit on Mar 3*.
