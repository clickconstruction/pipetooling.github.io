# v2.6391 — A stage nobody has reported counts from the job's record, not as 0 (2026-11-02)

Job 804, found while tracing its Job Summary margin (v2.6388, PR #6858): the office had billed the job in full and set it to 100% on Oct 12; on Oct 30 a crew member's line report moved Rough In to 100, and the stage-weighted sum read the two stages nobody had ever reported (Top Out and Trim Set, billed but not paid) as 0, so the job fell to 40 and the report's *Stage progress* field read *Rough In — → 100% · job 100% → 40%*. The owner was shown the rule question with the default "seed never-reported lines from the job's recorded %" and this PR builds it. No migration; no edge function.

## The rule

A line nobody has reported counts, in the job's sum, as its weight's share of **what the job's recorded percent leaves beyond the lines that have a number and the paid draws**, spread evenly by weight over the lines with none, clamped to 0..100 and left unrounded so the lines add up to the record (`unreportedLineSeed` in [`lib/reports/lineProgressReport.ts`](../../src/lib/reports/lineProgressReport.ts)). A job at 100 with nothing reported reads every line as done; a job at 60 reads them at 60; only the lines the crew moves change the sum, so a move on one line changes the job by that line's share alone and `after` never falls below `before` because of lines nobody touched.

Why the residual and not "every unreported line reads the job's %": the common case after a job's first line report. Rough In (40% of the job) marked done writes the job to 40; the next report would seed Top Out and Trim Set at 40 too and count Rough In twice — Top Out to 100 would read 88, not 80. The residual seeds them at 0 there (40 − 40 leaves nothing), and at 100 on 804 (100 − 0 over the whole weight). A record the reported lines cannot carry (the office typed 100 while Rough In sits at 35) clamps the seed at done; one the lines already pass seeds 0, and the lines' own truth stands.

What stays as it was: `stageEffectivePct` (paid draw = 100, else the crew's number, else 0 — the record), the row's *not started* words and its control opening on 0, `movedLines` (a pick is a move when it differs from the record or the 0 that stands for none; a pick put back is no pick), `defaultOpenLine`, the voice draft's per-line numbers, and every call that passes no recorded percent.

## What shipped

- **Kernel**: `unreportedLineSeed(rows, recordedPct)`; `linePct(row, picks, seedPct?)` reads the seed only for a line with no pick and no record; `jobPercentFromLines(rows, picks, recordedPct?)` and `howRows(rows, picks, recordedPct?)` take the record and read through the seed; `jobLine` and `lineProgressFieldValue` already took it and now pass it to the sum, so the modals' *Stage progress* line is right with no change of theirs; `pickOf` is the one home of "a pick put back is no pick"; **how?** stands a seeded line *counts as 60%* (`LINE_COUNTS_AS`) so the tech can see where the job's points sit.
- **Wiring**: `useJobLineProgress.derivedPct` reads through the record it already fetched; `ReportLinesProgressField` passes `recordedPct` to **how?**; `NewReportModal` and the My Schedule stepper (`FieldPctUpdateModal`) compute the filed number over the same record the list and the field read (the New report window's fallback seed, the stepper's fresh `base`), so the filed percent and the *job X% → Y%* clause always agree; `AdditionalReportModal` reads the hook's number, which is the same record.
- **Tests**: `lineProgressReport.test.ts` (+7 cases: the seed in every shape, 804's — the job at 100 stays at 100 and the old call still reads 40 —, a job at 60 with one line to 50 reads 56, the fresh job's Top Out to 100 reads 80 never 88, a pick put back keeps the seed and a pick on the seed is still named and stamped, the clamps, **how?**'s standings and points; two expectations moved from the old reading: the mock-up's job at 46 with Rough In to 50 now reads 51, not 38), `useJobLineProgress.render.test.tsx` (46 untouched and 65 after two picks, over the record), `ReportLinesProgressField.render.test.tsx` (+1: 804 on the field — *Job 100% → 100%*, the rows *not started*, **how?** *counts as 100%*).
- **Docs**: guide `reports` (the stage section: the rule in plain words and a second example on 804's shape); `GLOSSARY.md` → Report by line; this fragment.
