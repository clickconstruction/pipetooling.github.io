# v2.3503 — Supply house credits, step 4: the form (2026-09-16)

Client only, **lands after the v2.3502 migration is pushed**. Last of the four PRs in [`to-dos/supply-house-credits/README.md`](../../to-dos/supply-house-credits/README.md); the to-do can be deleted once this is live-tested.

Taunya's ask, in the owner's words: *"Tanya would like to be able to enter negative amounts into supply houses for refunds."* She had tried a Reece return of `−888.10` against purchase order `Return`, with the credit memo scanned on Drive, and the form refused it.

## The design decision

The amount box **does not** learn to accept a minus sign. The form asks what the paper is, and the sign is derived from the answer. A form that simply allowed negatives could not tell a credit memo from a mistyped invoice, and a stray minus would move a job's cost by twice the amount with nothing on screen to catch it.

## Kernel — [`supplyHouseInvoiceForm.ts`](../../src/lib/materials/supplyHouseInvoiceForm.ts) (+20 tests, 37 total)

- `signedAmountForSave(kind, typed)` — **the only place the sign is applied.** Takes the magnitude of whatever was typed, so `-888.10` on an invoice saves as `888.10` and `888.10` on a credit saves as `-888.10`. Returns null for a zero credit.
- `typedAmountFromStored(amount)` — the box always shows a positive magnitude, whichever kind the row is.
- `amountProblem(kind, typed)` — replaces the old inline *Amount must be a non-negative number*; explains that Credit, not a minus sign, is what takes money off.
- `documentWords(kind)` — every label that names the document: *Invoice #* / *Credit #*, *Paying it* / *Applying it*, *Not paid yet* / *Open — still on the account*, *Paid on* / *Applied on*, *Save invoice* / *Save credit*, and the `$` / `− $` adornment. The invoice branch delegates to the existing `invoiceSaveLabel`, pinned by a test.
- `documentKindFromRow(row)` — reads `document_kind`, falling back to the sign for a row written before the column existed.
- `creditEffectSentence(...)` — *"Takes $888.10 off what we owe Reece, and $888.10 off J878's parts cost."* With no job: *"No job gets money back — add one above if these parts went back from a job."*

## Form — [`SupplyHousesTab.tsx`](../../src/components/SupplyHousesTab.tsx)

A new first section **What the paper is** above *From the invoice*, holding a two-way Invoice / Credit control. Every label follows it, the amount adornment becomes `− $`, and the effect sentence renders above the pinned footer whenever Credit is picked with an amount. The invoice list and the phone list mark a credit in green with a **credit** tag.

`document_kind` was hand-added to [`database.ts`](../../src/types/database.ts) (the lien-desk precedent) — **regenerate with `npm run gen-types:linked` after the push.**

## Owner decisions taken as recommended (flag if Robert disagrees)

1. **An open credit gets its own column on the aging table**, rather than netting into the buckets. A credit has a size but not an age; netting is what makes the sixty-day number lie.
2. **"Paid" on a credit reads as applied**, defaults to open, and is labelled that way.
3. **A credit may be saved with no job.** The form says plainly that no job gets money back.
4. **Anyone who may enter an invoice may enter a credit.** Narrowing it would put Taunya through someone else to do her own job.
