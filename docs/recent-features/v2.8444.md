# v2.8444 — What customers see: the Sub NDA sent to a GC, as a sample email and a sample page (2026-11-24)

Punch list #416, part 3, the build *The GC send on Settings → What customers see*, which v2.8151 left. Client only: no migration, and no edge function changed, so nothing to deploy.

## What was missing

The Document builder's send to a GC (v2.8151) writes an email and puts a block on the GC's own page. Neither was on Settings → What customers see. The registry (`customerSurfaceRegistry.ts`) placed the sender `document-builder-send` on the subcontractor's two steps only, so the GC's half of it had no place.

## What changed

- **Two steps on the general contractor strip** (`customerJourneys.ts`), after *Portal as GC*: *Secrecy on their jobs: the email* and *Secrecy on their jobs: on their portal*.
- **The email** is `buildSampleBuilderSendEmail` (`customerSampleEmails.ts`): the function's own words (`builderSendEmail`, the first send, about all their jobs, on the note a send starts with) in the frame the function sends it in (`buildGcTradeEmail`), to the sample GC, with the one press to the sample GC's page. It goes out under the company's name, as the function sends it.
- **The page** is the sample GC's portal, `/portal?t=sample-gc`, with one builder at its top. `CustomerPortal.tsx` lays it over the function's answer for that sample alone (`withSampleBuilders`, `lib/documentBuilder/gcSendSample.ts`). The made-up answer goes through the page's own parse (`customerBuildersFrom`), so the block, its *New* chip and the flow it opens are the page's own. Its words are the placeholder first draft.
- **Why in the browser and not in the function's sample branch.** `customer-portal` answers the sample from `_shared/customerSampleFixtures.ts`. Putting the builder there would take a deploy of that function, and the placeholder paper lives under `src/`. The link page's sample (`/d/sample`, v2.8008) is made in the browser the same way. An answer that already carries a builder is left as it reads, so a sample from the function would win.
- **A sample tells nobody.** `PortalBuilders` already stays quiet on a sample token, so opening the sample builder sends no opened mark.
- **The registry**: `/portal` and `/p/:slug` gain the page step, and `document-builder-send` gains the email step. **A person's journey** reads both as *Read on Documents → Document builder → Sub NDA, under Send it to a GC*: the send's own card is where a real send stands.
- **Docs and help**: the glossary's *Document builder* entry and the project doc's builder paragraph each gain the sentence, and *use the document builder* says where to see both before sending.
