# v2.2967 — One company, Phase 1: ownership and adoption go inert at the helpers (2026-09-06)

Will, 2026-09-06: "Instead of jobs being owned by people and assistants being adopted by specific masters, tear down these walls … it is just simply one company." Decisions the same day: the company owner id is a **settings row, never a hand-picked person**; the `pay_approved_masters` grant **stays** (pay is too dangerous to open by role); **superintendents and primaries stay walled**; the role name "master" becomes **"leader"** in copy only (Phase 3). Plan: [`docs/ONE_COMPANY_PLAN.md`](../ONE_COMPANY_PLAN.md). One migration (`20260906190000_one_company_owners_inert.sql` — **`supabase db push` after merge**) and three edge redeploys.

## What shipped

- **Migration** ([doc](../migrations/20260906190000_one_company_owners_inert.md)): `company_owner_user_id()` + the seeded `app_settings.company_owner_user_id` row; `master_adopted_current_user` / `assistants_share_master` / `can_see_sharing_master` / `master_shared_current_user` answer `is_office_staff()`; the six project helpers and `user_can_access_estimate` grant office roles outright while keeping the superintendent (assigned-only, v2.2836), primary, owner and assignee branches byte-for-byte; the three job/project/customer/GC owner-equality trigger functions are no-ops; `create_job_from_estimate` (both overloads) and `apply_estimate_to_job` lose their owner guards, and new jobs from an accepted estimate are stamped with the company owner account.
- **Edge**: `create-stripe-invoice`, `preview-stripe-invoice`, `update-collect-payment-stripe-customer-email` no longer refuse "Customer does not belong to this job master" — with the DB invariant gone, a cross-account customer link must still bill. Redeployed with this PR.
- **Docs**: `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` gains "One company (v2.2967)"; the plan doc records the four decisions.

## What did not change

Field, primary, superintendent and customer-side scoping; `pay_approved_masters`; the `master_user_id` columns (still written, read for access only by the ~23 inline policies Phase 2 sweeps); the client's owner pickers (Phase 3).
