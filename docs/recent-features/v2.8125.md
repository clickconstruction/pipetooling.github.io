# v2.8125 — Lien desk: a missed notice with nothing due shows no empty tick column (2026-11-21)

Found on the live walk of 2026-11-21 that checked the fixes of the Fable 5.2 audit of punch list #361 (the ledger, v2.7989). Client only.

## What was wrong

v2.8064 took the *This notice* column off a notice that already went, because nothing on it could be ticked. The same column stayed on a missed notice with no month still due. There, every month is closed and none is ticked, and there is no draft, so the column was a row of empty locked boxes under *This notice* for a notice nobody is drafting. On the live desk, job 868's missed notice drew two of them, one for July and one for August. v2.8064 had already stopped the lead line asking for a tick there. The boxes stayed.

## What changed

[`LienDeskMonths.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskMonths.tsx) draws the column of this notice only where it says something: where a month can be ticked, or where one is ticked already. The head, the cells, the lead line's first sentence, the foot under the claim and the table's `data-this` all follow that one test (`thisShown`).

- A missed notice with nothing due and nothing ticked has no column. On a phone its rows take the full width, as a sent notice's do since v2.8064.
- While the affidavit's window lets a closed month go late (v2.4708), the months can be ticked, so the column stays.
- A notice past editing keeps its column, since its ticked boxes show the months that notice carries.
- A draft is unchanged.
