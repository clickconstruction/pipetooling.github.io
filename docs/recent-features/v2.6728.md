# v2.6728 — Materials on site: the Needs you line for stale containers and missing money (2026-11-08)

Punch list #209 (`to-dos/materials-on-site/README.md`), the first of the card's leftovers and the one its `next` named first. The design put two facts on the Needs you card for the office — containers not counted in 14 days, and the money that walked away this week — and the four PRs of 2026-10-29 left them off. No migration, no edge function.

## What changed

- **The kernel** `src/lib/needsYou/materialsOnSite.ts` (5 tests): `materialsNeeds(jobs)` takes each job's facts from `jobMaterialsSummary` (the boxes due, the week's short lines, their money) and sums them across every job with a box, naming the one job when a single job holds every box due or every shortfall, so the door can land on it; `materialsNeedsHref` is the desk, or that job's page.
- **The hook** `src/hooks/useMaterialsOnSiteNeeds.ts` (4 tests) reads the on-site store's desk load (`loadDesk`), the same cache Jobs → Materials, the Job window's tab and the ⋯ menu's count read, so the card's figures are the desk's by construction; it is null until the desk has loaded and on a refused read, re-reads on every store write (a press on the phone or the office page moves the line), and drops ZZ test jobs by id when the viewer hides them.
- **Two lines** in `dashboardNeedsYou.ts`, gated by the new source `materialsOnSite` (the office set, both hosts; no Quickfill station carries it), actor the assistant, doors in `needsYouDoors.ts`:
  - `materials-missing-week` (rank 40, amber, kicker *Materials on site*): *$1,240 of material walked away this week*, or *3 lines of material came up short this week* when nothing short has a price; the detail names the lines and the jobs, that a line is a count under what the ledger expected or a Can't find press, and whose price the money is. **Open the desk**, or **Open the job** with one job.
  - `materials-count-due` (rank 60, gray): *12 boxes on 3 jobs not counted in 14 days*, *A box on one job has not been counted in 14 days*; the detail says a crew counts from the phone's On site tab and the office can press Correct…
- **Docs**: `DASHBOARD_SECTIONS_ARCHITECTURE.md`'s Needs You table and key count; `MATERIALS_ON_SITE_ARCHITECTURE.md` (The screens → the doors, The gaps); the guide *see what is on every job site and what walked away*; the card's status, next, ver, As built, The plan and Left list; the release note and this fragment.

## Read closer than the card

- **One source, two lines.** The card said *the Needs you line carries two facts*; they are two keys so each takes its own tier — the money sits with billing accuracy (40), a box due a count is hygiene (60) — and each opens on the job that holds it when one does.
- **The desk's cost is the line's.** `loadDesk` reads every job with a live container whole (to the store's 5,000-row cap). On prod that is two jobs and a few hundred rows; the summary RPC the card lists is the answer when a job outgrows it, and the line would read it through the same store.
- **A yard on a paid job reads loud.** JP972 holds the company's own cages (the map's reading of 2026-11-02), most never counted, so the office's first read of the line will name most of them; the gray tier and *Open the job* are right for that. A home for a yard that is not a job stays on the map's gaps.
