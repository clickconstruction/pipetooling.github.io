# v2.6162 — A progress bill's lines beside the contract's, a prototype to compare (punch list #217, outside-1) (2026-10-30)

Punch list #217, *Show it, don't say it*: the outside scan's first finding (`.claude/sessions/scans/show-not-tell/findings/outside.md` → outside-1). A progress bill prints the job's fixture lines scaled down by a rule, one of each at a figure nobody priced: the contract said 24 water closets at $1,180, the bill printed one at $5,862.40, and nothing on the paper said the lines were this bill's share. On the mock-up Taunya wrote of outside-1 and outside-2: *I really like the idea of this. Could I please see this side-by-side of the before and after as a live prototype? You may need to use test data.* This is that prototype, a draft PR with no auto-merge.

## What changed

- **The kernel** (`src/lib/physicalInvoiceShare.ts`, new, with `physicalInvoiceShare.test.ts`): `progressBillShare(contractLines, scaledLines)` pairs the bill's scaled lines with the contract lines they came from, in order by their words, and says the share once (`pct`, a whole percent, never under 1); `shareHeadline` is *This bill: $20,266, 32% of the $63,332 contract.*, `contractLineWords` is *24 × $1,180 = $28,320*, `shareColumnHeads` are *In the contract* and *This bill · 32%*, and `stripeShareTail` is the Stripe line's tail for the build step, *· 32% of $28,320*.
- **The presentation** (`resolvePhysicalInvoiceLinePresentation`) returns `share` when it scaled and null when the lines match the bill, so `breakdownMatches` keeps its meaning; `PhysicalInvoiceDocument.share` carries it.
- **The office's preview** (`PhysicalInvoicePreview`, from Bill Customer's Physical tab): `shape` draws the Services table as the headline, each line with the contract line beside this bill's share, and the two sums under a rule; `sideBySide` draws today's table over it in the shared frame. `SendRecordInvoiceModal` reads the page's `?shape=1` / `?sbs=1` (`usePublicShapeView('outside-1')`; the side-by-side for a dev). With no flag the preview is as it was.
- **The email body** (`buildPhysicalInvoiceEmailBodies(doc, { shape })`): the same table in the email's layout, text and HTML; `buildPhysicalInvoiceEmailSideBySideHtml` is today's body over the shape's, one light document.
- **Sample data**: a new paper, `progress-bill` (`paperSamples.ts`), *A progress bill, part of the job* on the GC journey of Settings → What customers see: the sample GC job's three lines (24 × $1,180, 18 × $854, 2 × $9,820, $63,332) billed $20,266, 32%. Its **Side by side** press (the tab's `?sbs=1`) frames today's email over the shape's.
- **The shared side-by-side** (punch list #217's helper): `src/components/prototypes/SideBySide.tsx` and `src/lib/prototypes/shapeView.ts` are the tally session's copy as pushed (branch `claude/217-tally-day-strip-6400df`), byte for byte; `src/lib/prototypes/publicShapeView.ts` (+ test) and `hooks/usePublicShapeView.ts` are the public pages' rule — a real visitor sees no change, the shape draws on `?shape=1`, and the side-by-side on `?sbs=1` only on a sample page (`t=sample…`) or for a signed-in dev; `withSideBySide(url)` adds the flag. The **Side by side** press sits beside every page on What customers see (`SettingsWhatCustomersSeeTab`, `stepFrame.ts`), where each page's own prototypes answer it. The proto-portal-dates session's branch was not pushed when this was written, so these files are this branch's copy under the agreed names; the outside-2 PR carries the same files byte for byte, so whichever lands second changes nothing in them.
- **Guides**: `ready-to-bill-pipeline.md` names the shape under Physical invoice; `see-what-customers-see.md` → Open a step large names the press.

## How to see it

- Settings → What customers see → the GC strip → **A progress bill** → **Side by side**; or `/settings?tab=what-customers-see&sbs=1`.
- Bill Customer on a job billed in parts, with `?sbs=1` on the page's address as a dev (today's table over the shape), or `?shape=1` (the shape alone).

## Not done, and why

- **The Stripe line** (`supabase/functions/_shared/stripeInvoiceItemsFromFixtures.ts`): in the proration branch each item's description becomes `fixtureStripeDescription(row) + stripeShareTail(pct, lineExtendedCents(row) / 100)`, clamped to `STRIPE_INVOICE_LINE_DESCRIPTION_MAX`, with `pct` from the target over `sumRaw`. It is the build step's change and a `create-stripe-invoice` deploy; a prototype deploys nothing, so the function is untouched.
- **The PDF** (`physicalInvoicePdf.ts`) keeps today's four columns; the build step draws the three.
- **The portal's bill page**: the customer's portal has no page that draws a bill's lines — its Pay press opens Stripe's hosted invoice, and the GC customer's pay application opens the G702, which already draws scheduled value beside this period. So the side-by-side lives on the paper sample and the office's preview.
