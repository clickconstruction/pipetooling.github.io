# v2.7172 — Subs pay: each sheet says when it was paid (2026-11-13)

Punch list #347, PR 1. The owner, on **Jobs → Subs → Pay** with the **Paid** chip on: "On this screen, I want to be able to see the dates payments were made." The card (`to-dos/subs-payments-ledger/`, PR #7980) goes on to a ledger by date; this is its first step and stands alone.

## What was there

The Pay view is one row per sheet: Agreed, Paid, Due, Where it stands, Pay when. A paid sheet read *Paid · balance is $0*. A payment's date sat only in the opened row's Payments table, one sheet at a time. A phone showed no payment date anywhere.

## What changed

- `src/lib/subWorkOrders/sheetPaymentDates.ts`, new, pure. `sheetPaymentDates(payments)` reads a sheet's payments (amounts above zero; a back-charge is not a payment) into their days, oldest first. A payment's day is its **Date sent** (`payment_date`). A row with none takes the day it was recorded, cut in the app's timezone (`calendarYmdInAppTzFromIso(created_at)`), flagged `recorded`. `sheetPaidLine` gives the row's line: each day for one or two payments (`Oct 21 · Nov 10`), the count and the newest day for more (`3 payments · last Nov 12`). `sheetPaidInFullWords` gives a settled sheet's words (`paid in full Nov 12`, or `paid in full · recorded Aug 14`). A day in another year carries its year.
- `JobsSubLaborTab`: the desk row draws the line under the Paid amount (`data-sub-pay-dates`), and a sheet whose balance is exactly zero reads *paid in full …* in Pay when in place of *balance is $0*. An overpaid sheet keeps *over by $…*. The phone row's line gets the same words, and the phone sheet's subtitle carries the same dates in brackets after the paid amount.
- Nothing is fetched: `useSubLaborLedger` already hangs every payment, with `payment_date` and `created_at`, on its sheet. No migration.
- Guides `sub-labor-outstanding` and `work-the-subs-tab-on-a-phone` say where the dates are.

## Walked

On the worktree's dev build against prod, read only (headless Chromium, every write but the nav-click log refused by the route guard, none attempted):

- **Paid chip, desk:** 81 sheets, 81 date lines. 80 read *paid in full …*; the other is over-paid and keeps its own words. No row reads *balance is $0*.
- **62 of the 81 read *recorded …*.** Those payments have no Date sent: the field arrived 2026-08-14 (`20260814060000_labor_payment_date.sql`) and older rows were never back-filled. The ledger card's call 1 is about these.
- **Phone, 390 px:** 33 rows in the first sub's group, each *paid in full …*, no sideways scroll. The sheet's window reads the amount paid with its day in brackets.
- No page error at either width.
