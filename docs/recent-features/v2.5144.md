# v2.5144 — A returned check fee comes back to its case when its bill goes (2026-10-10)

Punch list #105, gap 1 (`to-dos/bill-riders-survive-delete-and-split.md`). Migration [`20261010062000_case_fee_back_on_bill_delete.sql`](../migrations/20261010062000_case_fee_back_on_bill_delete.md), pushed after the merge. No edge function.

## Why

`add_ar_return_case_fee` (v2.5033) writes the $30 on the case, on the bill's `fee_lines` and on the job's revenue. Deleting the bill (send back, Delete on a Ready to Bill line, Split's void) took the bill's entry with the row and nulled the case's `fee_invoice_id`, but kept `fee_added_at`. So the case still said *The $30 fee is on its bill* and refused a second press, while the next rewrite of the job's total dropped the $30. The office could not put it back.

## What shipped

- **The migration** (details in its doc):
  - adds `mercury_transaction_ar_returned.fee_came_off_at`;
  - adds a `BEFORE DELETE` trigger on `jobs_ledger_invoices`, `jobs_ledger_invoices_give_case_fee_back`. A bill whose `fee_lines` names a case gives the fee back to that case: its fee columns are cleared and the stamp is set, so the press runs again. The trigger also writes a `returned_check_fee_off` line in the job's history and takes the entries' amounts off the job's revenue, as the next rewrite would;
  - restates `list_ar_return_case_fees` to return the stamp;
  - gives back any fee that already left with a bill. None on production.
- **The case pane** (`arCaseFeeOffer`, `ArReturnCasePane`). A case whose fee came off shows *The $30 fee came off with its bill on Oct 10. Add it again.* above **Add the $30 fee to bill N**. When only Stripe bills are left, it shows the first sentence beside the reason there is no press. Once the fee is on again, the case reads as any fee that is on. `listArReturnCaseFees` reads the new column when it is there.
- **The help guide** *match bank deposits to bills* gains three sentences on the fee coming off with its bill.

## Proof

- **The revenue riders bed** (`scripts/pgtest-revenue-riders.sh`, Postgres 15 in Docker): 32 checks, 14 of them new. They cover the backfill, the delete, the revenue at once and after a rewrite, the history line, a case id that is not a uuid, and the whole job going. Two SQL mutants each failed it.
- `arReturnCaseFee.test.ts` (1 new) and `ArReturnCasePane.render.test.tsx` (1 new), both red on `main`. A fee that came off is offered again with its note, a Stripe-only case keeps the note beside its reason, and a fee on again reads as added.
- The returned-check suites, eslint, the theme check, the guide checks and the full typecheck pass.
- **Production, read-only, 2026-10-10 05:34 UTC:** no case has a fee on and no bill carries a case entry, so the backfill changes nothing.

## Left on purpose

- Split (#5274, v2.5140) carries a bill's fee lines onto its parts. With this trigger, a returned check fee carried that way would also go back to its case. Split should refuse such a bill, as it refuses a GC card fee. That is a follow-up once both are on `main`. No production bill carries a case entry today.
- A dev restore of a deleted bill brings its entry back, but the case has been cleared. Fix such a case by hand if it ever happens.

## To see it

After the push, on a ZZ TEST job with a check that came back:
1. Add the $30 fee from the case.
2. Take the payment off and send the bill back.
3. The case reads *The $30 fee came off with its bill on …* and offers the press again, and the job's total is $30 lower.
