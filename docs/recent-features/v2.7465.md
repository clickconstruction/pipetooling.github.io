# v2.7465 — Lien desk: the title is the first tab (2026-11-16)

The owner's question of 2026-11-16, over a screenshot of the title and the tabs: "since the title Lien desk and "to-do" access the same page should we combine this title together?" He was shown two drawings and picked the first: "A, title is the tab". Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What changed

On a computer the title bar has no title of its own. The first segment of the tab row carries the page's name, the count and the way home in one press: *⏱ Lien desk · 32 to do*.

- **The words are a kernel.** `lienDeskFirstTab({ isMobile, count })` in `src/lib/jobs/lienDeskTitleBar.ts` answers `{ words, titled }`: *Lien desk · 32 to do* and `titled` on a computer, the name alone while the desk's book is not in (`count: null`), *To do · 32* and not `titled` on a phone. Nothing waiting reads *0 to do*: the figure keeps its place, and it is the number the phone's tab shows. `LIEN_DESK_NAME` is the heading's word and the tab's.
- **The tab** (`LienDesk.tsx`, the `data-lien-desk-kinds` tablist): a `titled` first tab is 0.9rem at weight 700 beside the other three's 0.78rem at 600, on their own 1.17rem line, so the row is still 24.72 px, the height of the run's door and § Rules (`headDoor`). It is filled blue when To do is the view. On the other views it is unfilled in `var(--text-base)`, the heading's own colour, so the page keeps its name. The clock mark sits in an `aria-hidden` span: the tab's name is *Lien desk · 32 to do*. It carries `data-lien-desk-title` beside `data-lien-desk-view="next"`, and the desk's long hint (`LIEN_DESK_HINT`) after *To do, at the Board.*
- **The press is the title's**: `setKind('next')` and `boardBring`, so it opens To do with the Board in view from any view, and on To do it brings the Board back up. Until now the first tab's press was `setKind('next')` alone and only the title brought the Board.
- **The heading stays** for a screen reader and the page's outline: `<h2 class="lienDeskOutlineHeading" data-lien-desk-heading="outline">Lien desk</h2>`, clipped and out of the flow (`.lienDeskTitleBar > .lienDeskOutlineHeading` in `index.css`), so it takes no column gap, with no press inside it. The tablist's roles are unchanged.
- **A phone is as it was**: the `h2` with its `lienDeskTitleDoor` press on its own row beside More, and four tabs across the width whose first reads *To do · 32*. Four tabs across 375 px cannot hold the long segment. A one-column desk with a paper open hides its tab rows (`data-lien-pane-open`), so it draws the heading row too; in the app that is a phone, since the page asks for the stacked layout on a phone alone.

## The way back's margin, found on the way

The way back (`BACK_DOOR` in `LienDeskPage.tsx`) keeps 8 px clear of what follows by a margin of its own, over the bar's 14.4 px gap. `readLienDeskTitleLine` read the button's box alone, so the line it reckoned was 8 px short of the one drawn. In an 8 px band of windows the kernel said the four presses fit, the bar drew them on the title line, and the browser wrapped them to a line by themselves: three lines, at 1,210 px with that day's run. The reader now counts the first child's side margins with it (`back` 97.5 where it read 89.5), and `LIEN_DESK_TITLE_LINE_PARTS.back` is 98. The band was there before this change, 22 px wider up the scale.
