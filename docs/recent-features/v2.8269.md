# v2.8269 — Sub papers: a payment typed late is carried to the next statement of account (2026-11-21)

Punch list #350. The owner's call on how a statement's period is cut, asked under *Audited 2026-11-20* and answered on 2026-11-21: **a period is cut by the date on the payment, and a late one is carried.** No migration. The papers' kernel is shared with three edge functions, which is why they read as behind until redeployed (see *Deploy*).

## The fault

A payment typed on a later day and dated on or before the last statement's closing day was listed on no statement. The statement before was made without it, and the next one started after its date. Its amount was still in the next statement's opening figure. On made-up rows: one statement closes on $750, a $200 check dated inside it is typed two days later, and the next statement opens on $550 with no payment listed.

## The rule

`StatementRange` takes `lateAfter`, the moment the statement before this period was made (an ISO instant). `buildStatementOfAccount` (`supabase/functions/_shared/subPapers.ts`) then carries into the period:

- **a money line** (a payment or a back-charge) dated before `from` and typed after `lateAfter` (`PaperMoneyLine.at`, the row's `created_at`). It is not part of the opening figure. It counts in the period's payments or back-charges, and a payment is listed under **Last payments** with its own date and `late: true`.
- **a sheet** dated before `from` and typed after `lateAfter` (`PaperSheet.at`, new, optional). It is new work in this period, not brought forward, with every line it has. The owner's call named payments. A sheet typed late broke the opening figure the same way, so it follows the same rule.

So the statement opens on what the last one closed on: $750, a $200 payment listed, $550 at the close. `late` on the paper counts what was carried, and `statementLateWords` says it: *1 payment dated before this period was recorded after the last statement was made, so it is counted in this one.* The HTML page and the PDF print that line under **How it adds up**, and *recorded after the last statement* under the payment's day.

The period's own words do not move: it still reads *Nov 16, 2026 to Nov 20, 2026*. A line with no typed time, or a `lateAfter` that cannot be read, carries nothing.

## Who hands it in

Only **Since the last statement** in the office's papers window. `subPaperRegister` answers `lastStatement.madeAt` (the register row's `issued_at`), and the window's range for that chip carries it as `lateAfter`. A statement made today is still set apart (v2.8106), so a second statement the same day covers the same period and carries the same lines. The next day the first of them is the last statement, and a line typed after it that same day is carried then.

**This month**, **This year**, **To date** and **Pick dates** hand in nothing and are cut by the date alone, as before. So is the sub's own page: its sheets do not carry `at`.

## Not built here

The dev seat's `since_last` (`_shared/subStatementMake.ts`) still cuts by the date alone, and still refuses the day a statement was made where the window runs from the statement before. Bringing it to the window's rule changes `sub-statement` and the seat's words, and is its own change.

## Deploy

`_shared/subPapers.ts` and `_shared/subPapersPdf.ts` are bundled by `sub-statement`, `sub-portal` and `send-sub-paper-email`. None hands in `lateAfter`, so none behaves differently. Redeploy `sub-statement` and `sub-portal` after the merge so `npm run check:edge-drift` reads clean. `send-sub-paper-email` imports types only.
