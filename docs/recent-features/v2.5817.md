# v2.5817 — Quickfill: the Mark history window learns to read a habit, the maths first (2026-10-24)

Punch list #156, the owner's *"build it all"* on the proposal in [`to-dos/quickfill-mark-history/`](../../to-dos/quickfill-mark-history/README.md) (PR #6094): a station's Mark history window redrawn as the checklist History tab reads a habit. This is PR A of the train, the kernel alone; the window follows.

- **`src/lib/quickfill/markHistory.ts`**, pure, over the station's `quickfill_section_mark_events` rows (`MarkHistoryEvent`: the mark, who, the open count, the note):
  - `markHistoryWeeks(events, todayYmd, { weeks, usual, thirdTint })` — eight rows of seven cells ending on the week that holds today, oldest first. A day with a mark is `marked` with its last mark's count, who and time (`marks` says how many landed). A workday the station's rhythm expected a look on, with no mark, is a `gap`. Everything else is `off`: weekends, the days before the station's first mark ever, the days after today, and the days the rhythm did not expect. The rhythm is `markHistoryRhythmWorkdays`, the strip's idea (`sectionRhythmDays`) in the grid's unit: the median workday gap between the last five marked days, rounded down so a missed day never hides itself by slowing the rhythm, at least one, and one under three marked days. A workday is due when the last marked day before it is that many workdays back or more, and stays due until a mark lands. Days are the company calendar (`calendarYmdInAppTzFromIso`).
  - `markHistoryTint(count, usual, thirdTint)` — `none` for nothing open, `some` for something, `tick` for a station with no count, and `many` only when asked and the count is above the window's usual (`markHistoryUsual`, the median of the counted marks).
  - `markHistoryTiles(weeks, todayYmd)` — the ledger's three: *This week* (looks against the days expected), *Streak* (due days in a row with a look, walking back; an unfinished today never breaks it), *Missed* (due days with no look, today left out) over the window's due days.
  - `markHistoryHabit(events)` — who marks it, most first; each mark's hour in the company's time zone (`markHistoryHour`), the median and the middle half for the band; two sentences in plain words: *Dana marked it 36 times and Marisol once. The usual time is about 9:30 AM.* and *Nothing open at 31 of 37 looks. Lately about 1 open at a look, down from about 4.* (the first half of the window against the second; nothing is said when both halves round to none), *Something was open at every one of the 38 looks.* on a station that is never at zero, or *This station has no count. A mark is a look.*
  - `markHistoryRows(events, todayYmd)` — the last seven days and the rest, newest first; `markHistoryHasNotes(events)` — the Note column is drawn only when a mark carries one; `markHistoryCellTitle(cell)` — *Tue, Oct 20 · 9:30 AM · Dana · 3 open*.
  - **The boxes band**: `MARK_HISTORY_BOX_SOURCES` names the three stations whose boxes write a row a tick and where (Office Arriving and Assistant Dailys in their daily-checks tables with the labels in `app_settings`, Difficult people with its items table; Office Leaving keeps a done map and has no history); `parseMarkHistoryBoxItems` reads the labels as `app_settings` stores them; `markHistoryBoxes(items, ticks, weeks, boxWeeks, firstTickByItem)` — one row a box over the last four weeks' due workdays, a cell on when a tick row exists for that box and day, keyed on `work_date` as the page wrote it, the boxes ticked on fewer than half the days named under the band (*Left unticked most days: Trash out when leaving, 18 of 20.*). Each box counts from its first tick on record, as the checklist grid starts a task at its birthday: the labels carry no creation day, and removing a box deletes every tick it had, so a reworded box comes back with a new id and no past. The days before draw no box and do not count (a row's `since`, a cell's `before`, *2 boxes count from their first tick on Fri, Oct 23.*); a box with no tick on record is measured over every day.
- **Tests** (`markHistory.test.ts`, 24): the eight weeks and their month labels, a gap, a weekend mark, two marks a day, the days before a station's birth, a station with no count, a weekly station's due days, the third tint, the tiles and the streak's today rule, the habit's sentences, a falling count and a station that is never at zero, the rows' split, the note rule, the cell's words, the boxes band, a box counted from its first tick, and the band's empty states.
