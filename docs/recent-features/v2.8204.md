# v2.8204 — The job window has a Map tab (2026-11-21)

Punch list #413, step 8, the last of the card's screens. Taunya's first picture of the idea on 2026-11-21 was this tab: *perhaps a job in the modal for jobs should have an additional map tab*.

## Why

The card put this tab last, to be built once jobs held enough places to need a map of them. Taunya's word was to build the whole card. By this step a job can hold painted maps of the way in, a list of places with photos, buried lines with their lengths, and the places an earlier job kept at the same address. Each has its own window. Nothing showed them together.

## What ships

**Map**, the eighth tab of the office's job window, after Materials (`JobWindowMapTab`).

- **What it draws** (`jobMapParts` in `paintedJobMap.ts`): every line of the job's painted maps and its development's, the places of the job's own list with their letters, and the places an earlier job kept at the same customer address, each in a dashed ring.
- **A buried line** is drawn in the color a locator paints for what is buried.
- **A press on a dot** opens that place's list on the place, and counts an open.
- **Under the map**, one line: *2 painted maps · 4 places on this job · 3 kept at this address*.
- **A job with nothing on it** says so and names the press that fills it, **Photo of a spot**, and the map opens on the job's address.
- **Read only.** A place is set from the presses under the address, which sit over every tab.

**A place set on one painted map**, such as a gate on the way in, is not drawn as a dot here. Its number is that map's own and would be taken for another map's. Its map's line is drawn.

## What it costs

Nothing on Google. The tab draws on the old map, Leaflet with Esri's photograph, as the Map page and Materials on site do. The tab's code and the map are a chunk of their own, asked for on the press, so a job window that never opens the tab loads none of it.

## Who sees it

The roles that get the tabbed window: dev, master technician, assistant, controller and primary. The field roles' plain window has no tabs. It draws the same places as rows with photos under the address (v2.8200).
