# v2.8323 — Papers: the record of who opened a signed form outlives its paper (2026-11-22)

Punch list #403, the owner's fourth decision. A migration and its bed. No screen, no client change and no edge function.

## The decision

The card asked whether the open log should outlive its paper, and picked that it does. On 2026-11-22 the owner was asked "Take the card's pick on all four of #403's decisions?" and answered "Yes, all four".

## What changed

- **A deleted paper leaves its lines.** `contract_form_pdf_opens` was tied to its paper with `ON DELETE CASCADE`. It is `ON DELETE SET NULL` now, and the link may be empty.
- **A line names its paper by itself.** Two new columns, `paper_name` and `paper_with`, are filled by a trigger when the line is written: the paper's name, and the person, the party or the trade company it was with. The lines already kept are filled once.
- **`open-contract-form-pdf` is unchanged.** It writes the paper's id and who opened it, as before.

## What stays as it was

- Who reads the log: a dev and the controller.
- Who may delete a paper, and that no screen deletes an outside party's paper yet. That press is the first decision, and is its own PR.
- An open cannot be written for a paper that is not there.

## Proof

The bed `form-pdf-opens-outlive-paper` (`supabase/tests/form_pdf_opens_outlive_paper/20_scenario.sql`), on the whole schema in CI. It writes opens of three kinds of signed paper, deletes two papers as a dev through row security, and checks the lines stay with their words, that a later rename changes no line, and who reads the log. Docker is not available on the machine that wrote this, so CI's run is the bed's proof.
