# v2.4902 — Accounts Receivable: a check that was never deposited becomes a case (2026-10-08)

Punch list #76, piece 3 (`to-dos/ar-returned-checks.md`). Migration `20261008100000_ar_unbanked_check_cases.sql` ([doc](../migrations/20261008100000_ar_unbanked_check_cases.md)); **`ar-returned-checks`** redeploys (it runs the new sweep and tells the office). Piece 4, the customer-release queue waiting for a check, had already shipped as punch list #83 row 5 (v2.4564); the card now says so.

## Why

A payment typed in as a check reads paid on its job the moment it is saved. The returned-check train (v2.4313–v2.4333) opens a case only for a check the bank holds: one it sent back, or one Mercury could not take in. A check that was never deposited leaves nothing at the bank, so nothing said anything. On 2026-10-08 eight check payments typed in since Jul 1 had no deposit after ten days, every job reading paid. The migration doc names them. Six are $250 pretest and post checks from Aug 19–21, five of them billed to Done Right Foundation. Done Right Foundation's $250 check deposits in that stretch are every one applied to other jobs. So these six were either never deposited, or deposited together with no link. #819 also carries a second $250 payment the same day on a $250 job.

## The change

- **The case** (migration): `ar_unbanked_check_cases`, one per payment. There is no Mercury row, so no foreign key; the job, amount and day ride on the case, so it outlives a payment taken off its job.
- **The sweep**: `open_ar_unbanked_check_cases(p_days 10, p_floor 2026-07-01, p_quiet)`. A check-typed payment with no deposit link, on a job, typed in at least ten days ago and on or after the floor, opens a case. The exception: an unused check deposit of at least its amount posted since three days before it was typed in. It may be this check, waiting in To match. The floor is there because before spring 2026 the office did not link checks to deposits. Two triggers on `jobs_ledger_payments` close a case the moment its payment gets a deposit (deposited) or comes off its job (taken_off). The office closes one as paid another way or not coming; only those reopen.
- **The clock**: `AR_UNBANKED_CHECK_DAYS = 10` in `_shared/bankReturnedDeposits.ts`, mirrored by the SQL default (`bankReturnedDeposits.test.ts` keeps them equal). It is its own constant on purpose. `CHECK_CLEAR_DAYS` (7) counts a deposited check clearing; this counts a check that never got deposited.
- **One list**: `list_ar_return_cases` adds these as rows with source `unbanked`, the case's own id in `mercury_transaction_id`, the payment in `recorded_payment`, the GC as the payer when the GC pays. Its columns are unchanged, so Accounts Receivable, the Dashboard and Pipeline nudge and the notifier read them with no type change.
- **The pane** (`arReturnCase.ts`): the chip *never deposited*. The story reads *$250 was recorded as paid on #066. No deposit is linked to it.* · *10 days on, no deposit had come in for it.* The stake reads *#066 reads paid in full. The money is not in the bank.* The next step reads *Find the check and deposit it. If it went in with other checks, link this payment to that deposit.* It has the rejected check's two buttons, *It was paid another way* and *Take the $250 off #066*. There is no *Use it as the new check* (`arReplacementFor` refuses it). The footer says how it closes. More → close goes to `close_ar_unbanked_check_case`.
- **The notice** (`ar-returned-checks`, `_shared/arReturnCaseNotify.ts`): *A check was never deposited · Done Right Foundation · $250*, with *No deposit has come in for … check.* and plain sentences. An unbanked case claims its own `notified_at` in place of the deposit ledger.
- **The Dashboard**: *…'s $250 check was never deposited*, or *8 checks were never deposited ($2,885)* when every open case is one.
