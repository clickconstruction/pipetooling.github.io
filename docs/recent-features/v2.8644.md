# v2.8644 — Lien window: deleting a test notice record takes its Activity line too (2026-11-26)

Punch list #443, row 1, the Activity line: one of the take-backs that need a migration, on the owner's answer of 2026-11-25 (*All eight*), each push on its own yes. v2.8567 built the delete of the record alone and said why the line stayed.

## What was left

A § 53.056 notice recorded by hand is one `job_lien_filings` row with `by_hand` set. Its insert trigger, `job_lien_filings_to_activity_ins`, writes one `job_activity_events` line of type `lien_notice_sent` whose `detail` names the record as `source_id`. The Lien window's **Delete…** of v2.8567 was a direct delete under the table's dev policy. The line stayed, because `job_activity_events` has a read policy and no other: only `SECURITY DEFINER` code writes it. Every word on the screen said *Its line on the job's Activity stays.* Three records deleted that way on the ZZ Test job of #415's walks left three lines that name nothing (card #443, *What sits on prod*).

## Who sees it

## The window's count and the function's

The window counts a line as a stray when the job it is open on holds no record with that id. The function looks for the record on every job. So the function may clear fewer lines than the window counted, and the toast says the count the function answered, or *No Activity line was cleared.*
