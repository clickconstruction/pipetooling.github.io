# v2.8192 — Document builder: the job list leaves out lost and closed jobs, and one press goes to awarded trades (2026-11-21)

Punch list #404. The review of 2026-11-20 listed both: *the job list is every `gc_projects` row, a bid pursuit and a lost job among them*, and *the one press counts a trade that was asked and not awarded*. It asked Taunya which trades the one press should reach. Her answers on 2026-11-21: the list keeps a job that is being bid and leaves out a lost or closed one, and a trade that was asked and not awarded is sent to one at a time only. No migration and no function.

## The job list

`readGcJobs` still reads every GC project, now with whether each is over (`gcJobOver`: lost when `lost_on` is set, else closed when the stage is `closed` or `closed_on` is set). The screen lists what `listedJobs` keeps:

- A job that is being bid, bought out or built.
- A job that is over, only when its sheet was published. It stays so its sheet can be read, its link switched off and its sends taken off a trade's page. Its line in the **Job** list ends in *lost* or *closed* (`jobPickLabel`).

A job that is over and has no published sheet is not listed, and no sheet can be started for it.

## The usual's publish

`usualPublishPlan` has two more reasons to hold a job, `lost` and `closed`, checked before the job's own changes. The row reads *Held. This job was lost.* or *Held. This job is closed.* in grey. The button's count leaves the job out, and the publish that reads the jobs again plans from the same list.

## The one press

`SendParty.alone` marks a trade that was asked for a price and not awarded (`readSheetParties`). `sendBatch` leaves it out of the ready set and lists it as *Held: … was asked and not awarded. Send to them alone.* The press on its own row is unchanged. On a job still being bid no trade is awarded, so the one press reads *Nothing to send* and each row still sends.

## The printed sheet's copy

The page now reads a project's billing job with the job (`GcJob.billingJobId`), so a printed sheet is filed under it when there is one. v2.8190 did the same for the sent email.
