# v2.7013 — Pipeline: *Pays in 3–67d* on a billed row opens Pay speeds on that payer (2026-11-12)

The owner's ask (2026-11-12): where a person can see *Pays in 3–67d* under a billed row's Expected date, pressing it should open a pay speeds window, much like the one **Payment forecast → Pay speeds** opens.

**What changed**

- **The line is a press.** `BilledReliabilityLine` takes `onOpenPaySpeeds` and `payerName`. With them, the *Pays in …* words are a real `button` (`.billedPaysInPress` in `src/index.css`). The owner's call on seeing it (2026-11-12): the underline alone says press. So the words keep the grey of the line they sit in (`color: inherit`, the line's `--text-muted`, which has a light and a dark value), with no ring around them: a dotted underline at rest, solid under the pointer, solid and thicker under the keyboard's focus. Its name says what it opens: *Pays in 3–67d. Open pay speeds for <payer>*. The promise record and returned-check words after it (*keeps 3 of 7 · slips ~9d*) stay plain, since the window does not hold them. A line with no *Pays in* has nothing to open and stays plain.
- **The same window, opened on one payer.** No second window was built. `PaySpeedsBreakdownModal` takes `focusPayer` (and `focusRows`). With it the window leads with that payer's card: their name and segment, their open dollars, the range in the row's own words (`formatPaysIn`, so the two can never disagree), one sentence on what the two numbers are, **Owes now — by job**, and **Paid before — each payment, days from bill to money**, every payment listed with no click. A bill or a payment opens its job, as in the list. Under the card, **All customers** (a button with `aria-expanded`, `.paySpeedsAllPress`) opens the whole window: the tiles, Data health, Money waiting and By customer, unchanged. Without `focusPayer` the window is exactly what the forecast opened before.
- **The kernel.** `src/lib/jobs/paySpeedsPayerFocus.ts`: `buildPaySpeedsPayerFocus` (the card's facts from the pay speeds the board already holds, zero extra reads) and `payerRangeSentence`. The sentence is honest about what the range is: from four payments up it is the middle half (*The middle half of their last 9 measured payments came 3 to 67 days after the bill. The middle one took 21 days.*), with two or three it is every payment, with one it is that payment, and with none it says the expected day borrows a pace.
- **The wiring.** `JobsStagesTab` holds the pressed payer and hands the press to the dates block's line; `StagesBilledMoneyModals` (the billed-money windows' host) mounts the window with the same rows, role gates and doors it hands the forecast's copy. The payer's open bills are looked for in the Collections rows too, so a chased payer's card still says what they owe.

**Where the line is used, and what each place does:** `BilledReliabilityLine` has one caller, the dates block's renderer in `JobsStagesTab`, which three layouts draw: the desk's billed and Collections rows (job rows and bill rows) and the Mobile cards. The press works in all three. The phone's two-line rows never drew the dates block, so they have no line and no press; that is unchanged.

**Is this the best we can do?** The task named two shapes: open on the one payer, or open the whole window scrolled to them. This is the first, and it took no rework of the window: the payer's card reuses the panel the By customer list already unfolds, and the whole window sits one press under it. A person who pressed one customer's line gets that customer's record with no scrolling and no second click, and the 3–67 is explained where it is shown, which the list never did. Two limits, left as they are: the payments the pay-speed lookup returns are capped at the newest twelve per payer, so the card says *their last 12*; and at phone width with Mobile cards off the press is a line of 11px words, as small a target as the dates block's other doors.

**Tests:** `paySpeedsPayerFocus.test.ts` (the sentence for 1, 2–3 and 4+ payments and none; the card's facts); `BilledReliabilityLine.render.test.tsx` (a real button with its name, the row's click not set off, the plain cases, and the class in `index.css` saying press with the underline alone, no colour or ring in any state); six cases in `PaySpeedsBreakdownModal.render.test.tsx` (the payer's card, All customers opening and closing the whole window, a payment opening its job, a payer with nothing measured, the wider rows, and the forecast's whole window untouched); three in `StagesBilledMoneyModals.render.test.tsx` (rows, gates and doors); and `JobsStagesTab.paysInPress.render.test.tsx`, which presses the line on the real board and finds the window open on that row's payer, and on the other row's payer for the other row. All names and numbers are made up.

Guides: *see when a customer will pay* gains **Open their pay speeds from the line**; *see which customers pay slowly* says the window also opens on one customer. `GLOSSARY.md`'s dates-block entry names the press.

No migration, no edge function deploy.
