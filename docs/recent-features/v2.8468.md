# v2.8468 — Document builder: the Sub NDA's left side has an Edit and a Share view (2026-11-24)

Punch list #416, part 3, the build *The Sub NDA's Edit / Share switch*, which #404 lists under *Against the mock-up*. Client only: no migration, no edge function. It is cut on top of v2.8456, the card of changed pages, since both change the editor's left column.

## What was there

The Sub NDA opened on two sides: the map and the changed pages on the left, what a GC sees on the right. Steps 8 and 9 then put the builder's link and the send to GCs under the map, so the left column ran four cards long and the send sat far below the fold.

## What changed

- **The switch.** At the top of the left column, two presses: **Edit** and **Share** (`BUILDER_SIDES`, `lib/documentBuilder/builderSide.ts`). The pressed one is blue under white words, and a line beside the switch says what the side on show holds.
- **Edit** shows the map and the card of changed pages. **Share** shows **This builder's link** and, under it, whatever the tab hands in as `share`, which is **Send it to a GC**. What a GC sees stays on the right under both.
- **Both sides stay drawn**, and one is hidden. A note half typed in a send's box, an open **What it is about** choice and the find box's words are all still there after a look at the map. The link and the GCs are read once, when the builder opens, as before.
- **The side is in the address**: `?tab=builder&builder=sub-nda&side=share`. A reload and the browser's Back land on the side the person was on. Edit carries no key, so the address a builder opens on has one form. Going back to the list drops the key (`builderSideFromSearch`, `withBuilderSide`).
- **On a phone** the Share side stands alone: `index.css` hides what a GC sees while Share is on, at 720 px and under, as the mock-up does. Edit stacks the map over the flow there, as before.
- `WalkedBuilder` takes `side` and `onSide`. With neither it opens on Edit and keeps the side itself, which is how its smokes draw it.
- **Docs and help**: the glossary's *Document builder* entry, and three sentences of *use the document builder* that said where the link and the send are.

## Against the mock-up

- The drawing's Share side has a third card, *What a GC can do*, with *Look only* and *Look, then send their picks*. That is step 10, which waits on Taunya's call, and is not built.
- The drawing's Edit side has a third card, *The levels and the other pages*. It is not built: the step bar opens those pages, and the levels' names are not fields.
- The drawing puts the line about the revision beside the switch. The built bar keeps that line under itself, and the switch's own line says what the side holds.
