# v2.3485 — Submittals stage 4a-i: the review room, read-only, and Share (2026-09-16)

Client + one migration (`20260916015805_bid_submittal_rooms.sql`, [`docs/migrations/`](../migrations/20260916015805_bid_submittal_rooms.md)) + two new edge functions (`get-submittal-room`, `open-submittal-pdf`, [`EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`](../EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md)). Stage 4's first PR ([`to-dos/submittals/README.md`](../../to-dos/submittals/README.md) → PR 4a-i, decisions 8–12 of 2026-09-15): the door out of the building. The reviewer is usually the customer's architect or designer, met in the GC's email chain after the win; the review is a **room** of its own, never the customer portal; nothing about money reaches it.

## What shipped

- **Schema**: `bid_submittal_rooms` (one per bid: the durable token, `open · closed`, shared/closed stamps), `bid_submittal_people` (one per identified or named person: name · email · role `architect · owners_rep · designer · builder · other` · `may_decide` · a personal token · `how` `named · identified · forwarded` · opened stamps · `closed_at`; UNIQUE (room, lower(email))), `bid_submittal_events` (room · person · revision · `view · identified · decided · reply · file_dropped · shared · closed`), `bid_submittal_items.reviewed_by_person_id`. Helper `can_access_submittal_room(id)`; RLS for the pricing sharers on bids they can price; outsiders only through the functions (service role, token-validated — the Bid Room precedent); all three fence appliers.
- **Share** ([`SubmittalShareModal`](../../src/components/bids/SubmittalShareModal.tsx), beside New revision on the newest revision): the first Share mints the room (48-hex token, `newRoomToken`) and copies `/submittal?t=…` to the clipboard; optional pre-named people (name · email · role · watching), each with a personal token and `how='named'`, upserted on (room, email); *Before it goes*: Done with this file for every untrimmed vendor file with pages on rows (the tab's `trimFile`, now snapshot-driven so several files trim in a row) and a package rebuild; the revision → `shared` with `shared_at/by`, earlier shared revisions → `superseded`; a `shared` event. No email leaves the app.
- **The tab** ([`BidsSubmittalsTab`](../../src/components/bids/BidsSubmittalsTab.tsx)): the room line above the revision chips (`describeRoomLine`: *Room link · shared Sep 16 · opened 9×* + Copy link + Close the room / Reopen), the people under it (`describeHow` + `describeTrail`: *identified via the room link · opened Sep 17 · 3× · decided 22*; the **deciding / watching** switch on `may_decide`; Personal link; × closes a person's link), and *+ N opens by people who did not say who they were* (`anonymousOpens`).
- **`get-submittal-room`** (GET `?t=`; room token or personal token): the bid label, the company from the test-report settings, the person when the token was personal, every shared revision newest first with its rows in the customer's words (`_shared/submittalRoomPayload.ts`: `roomRowsFrom` → *matches · differs · not_quoted · added*, `whySentence`, `roomCounts`, `roomHeadline` / `roomSubline`); a `view` event unless staff or `?preview=1` (`publicViewDecision`); a personal token's view bumps the person's `open_count`; closed → 410 with the closed payload; nothing shared → 404 `empty`.
- **`open-submittal-pdf`** (GET `?t=&r=`): a 300-second signed link to a shared revision's `package_path`, as a redirect, with a download name — the only customer-facing way to the file.
- **The room** ([`SubmittalRoom.tsx`](../../src/pages/SubmittalRoom.tsx) at `/submittal?t=`, light-pinned, phone first): the letterhead, *Product review · project*, the revision strip, the headline (*3 rows need a call*) with *Just looking? Fine — to decide or ask, we'll ask who you are*, the differing rows first as cards (*The plans · Proposed · Why*, the performance-change line, any decision with its name), added and to-follow rows, the matching rows folded under *Show the N rows as the plans specify*, *N decided · N to go* with **Download Rev N PDF**, the footer line. Approve / Revise / Reject open the identify sheet, which in this release reads *deciding comes in the next release — reply to the email that brought you here*. Closed, dead-link and nothing-shared states. Route registered beside `/bid-room`; a Playwright Tier-1 smoke (`e2e/submittal-room.spec.ts`).
- **Guides**: `build-a-submittal-package.md` gains "Share it — the review room"; `see-what-customers-see.md` names the room.
