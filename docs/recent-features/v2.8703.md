# v2.8703 — Recently deleted: a test job's entry no longer says its Drive folder was left behind (2026-11-27)

Punch list #443, row 18. Finding 292 of the fixing thread's ledger, from the walking thread's sitting 32 (2026-11-27, reads only). Client only. No migration, no edge function. Nothing owed after the merge.

**What was seen.** Settings → Data & recovery → Recently deleted. The entry of a ZZ test job that a developer deleted an hour before read *This test job kept a Drive folder. Deleting the job did not remove it.* That delete had answered that the job's folder went to Drive's trash (v2.8681). So three entries carried the line where two were expected, and one of the three said a thing that was not so.

**Read.** `deletedBundleFolderOffered` (`src/lib/jobs/deletedJobFolderOffer.ts`) draws the line for a developer on a deleted job whose kept copy is ZZ-named and holds a Drive link. The kept copy is the job as it stood at the delete, so it holds the link whether the folder was left, sent to the trash at the delete, or sent later with this very press. Only Drive knows which. The line's words said the folder was left.

**The mend.** The words, and nothing else. The line now reads *This test job had a Drive folder. Press Open it in Drive to see whether it is still there.* The rule for where the line is drawn does not change, and neither does the press, the link, the confirm or the function. On a folder already in Drive's trash the press answers *Its Drive folder is already in Drive's trash.*, as before.

**Not done.** The entry does not learn that its folder went. That would need the delete to write the outcome somewhere the entry can read, which is a migration or a function. The count of entries that carry the line is as it was.

**The guide.** `recover-a-deleted-job` quotes the new words, and says a folder already in the trash stays there.

**Tests.** The kernel's test holds the new words and that they never say *kept*, *left* or *did not remove*. The line's render test reads them on the card.

**Not built beside it: finding 293.** The entry's presses are small: **Send its Drive folder to the trash…** 15 px tall, **Open it in Drive ↗** 18, **Preview restore** and **Restore** 31, at a desk and at 375 px alike. A developer alone sees the block. It is listed for Taunya to weigh.

**To see it live.** A read: as the developer, Settings → Data & recovery → Recently deleted, a ZZ test job's entry. The line reads as above. Nothing is pressed.
