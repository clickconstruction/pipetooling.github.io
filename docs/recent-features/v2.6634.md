## Decisions

- **A press, not an automatic copy at signing.** `accept-contract` is the public endpoint the worker's phone hits and has no Drive door; the office's own press through the signed-in client reuses the Add a file door as it stands, and the section shows at a glance which statements are in the folder.
- **Sent by us, not a new folder.** The card's call 6 and the Documents tab agree: what the office sent lives there.
