# v2.3784 — A bounced check on a Stripe bill can be taken off the job (2026-09-23)

Taunya, Take 5 – Seguin (JP878): *"I need this recorded payment removed so I can generate the lien notice."* A $13,680 check deposit from the GC was matched to the job's first Stripe bill in Accounts Receivable on Sep 21 (`jobs_ledger_payments` row, Mercury-linked, no credit note); on Sep 23 Mercury synced the deposit as `status = failed`, `reasonForFailure = "Insufficient funds"`. The Pipeline row still read 35% Paid $13,680 · Left on Job $24,945 and every door refused: **Unlink and remove** (`remove_jobs_ledger_payment_and_reconcile`) rejected any payment on a Stripe-hosted bill, **Undo part payment** needs a `stripe_credit_note_id` the row never carried (the deposit was matched in AR, not recorded through Stripe), and **Unwind** needs a bill Stripe marks paid. The Stripe invoice was still `open` for its full $15,200 — nothing there to reverse, only the ledger row to delete.

Same day, same report: jobs 963 and 978 "Nothing left to bill" — that is v2.3775 (merged 14:30 CDT); her screenshots at 13:33 and 14:39 predate the deploy that carried it. Nothing more to build there.

## The change

- **Migration `20260924020000_remove_bounced_payment_on_stripe_bill.sql`** — `remove_jobs_ledger_payment_and_reconcile` refuses a Stripe-hosted bill only while Stripe holds a record of the payment: a credit note on the row (*press Undo part payment on the row instead*) or `stripe_invoice_status = 'paid'` (*unwind the out-of-band payment on the bill first*). Otherwise the row deletes as on any other bill, with the same reconcile (payments_made, the invoice's paid/billed, paid → billed). Every removal writes a `removed` event to `jobs_ledger_payment_events` (the kind check gains `'removed'`; the client's move trace reads only `moved`) with the row's snapshot, the actor and a reason — `bank_failed: Insufficient funds`, `unlinked_stripe_bill_unrecorded`, or `unlinked`. When the Mercury deposit behind the row is `failed`, the call also upserts `mercury_transaction_ar_returned` so the freed money never re-enters To match. The reply carries `bank_failed`, `bank_reason`, `marked_returned`.
- **`jobFormPaymentPredicates.ts`** (+8 tests): `stripeHoldsPaymentReason` (`credit_note` | `paid_in_stripe` | null) is the one rule; `mercuryUnlinkBlockedByStripeHostedInvoice` reads it; `unlinkLeavesStripeBillUntouched` is the confirm's sentence; `mercuryDepositFailed` / `mercuryDepositFailedWords` the row's chip; `unlinkedPaymentToastText` the toast from the RPC's reply.
- **Edit Job → ③ Payments received** (`JobFormPaymentsTable`): the Mercury lookup reads `status` and `raw->>reasonForFailure` with the posting date; a locked row whose deposit failed wears ⚠ *Returned by the bank · Insufficient funds*; **Unlink and remove** shows on a Stripe bill the moment Stripe holds nothing, with a title that says the pay link stays as it is. The confirm (`JobFormModal`) adds the same sentence; the pre-check toast is the RPC's own words; the success toast says the deposit was marked returned when it was.
- Guide *match bank deposits to the bills they pay* gains **If a check bounces after you matched it**.

## Decisions taken

- **Stripe's record is the gate, not the bill's channel.** The old rule guarded against a ledger delete leaving Stripe showing money it still counted. That is exactly the credit-note and paid-out-of-band cases, and each has its own door; a deposit matched in AR to an open Stripe bill has no Stripe side.
- **The bank's word marks the deposit returned; a person still presses the button.** Mercury's `failed` is read, never acted on by itself — the office decides to take the payment off the job, and the app does the AR bookkeeping that follows.
- **Not touched:** the § 53.056 notice itself. JP878 carries no `gc_customer_id` (its customer is Southern Post Construction, commercial); the Lien desk lists sub jobs by GC, so the notice still needs the GC set on the job — that is Taunya's call, noted in the session's report.
