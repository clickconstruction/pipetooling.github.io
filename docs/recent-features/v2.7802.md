# v2.7802 — Time, Satellite and Streets on four more maps (2026-11-18)

Punch list #395, step 2 of 7. The four maps that drew dots on the shared canvases, and nothing else, move into `PinsMapFrame` (v2.7794).

## What changed

- **Dashboard → Your jobs on a map**, **Bids → Bid Board → Bids on a map**, **Prospects → Permits → On a map** and **Find it on the map** (the hiring window) each gain the Satellite and Streets switch and the **Time** press.
- **Each opens on Streets**, as it always has. Satellite is Google's photograph with street names, or Esri's over the Leaflet fall back.
- **Time** frames the open pin, or the only pin. Its row says *The pins are today's.* beside a date. The home map has one pin and says nothing about pins.
- **What rides through.** Clusters, the office and its rings, and the Bid Board's home fit and row-hover pulse all draw on the archive view, since every one has a Leaflet twin.
- **No popup in Time.** The frame hands the canvas no popup while Time is on (v2.7794), so a pin's popup never covers the photograph on these cards. It is back when Time is left.

## What each card lost

Each card held the same block: the provider rule, `googleFailed`, two lazy imports and a ternary between the canvases. The frame holds it once.

- `DashboardJobsMapCard` now builds its canvas pins and popup itself. Its two adapters are gone: `DashboardJobsMapGoogleCanvas.tsx` is deleted and `DashboardJobsMapCanvas.tsx` is `DashboardJobsMapPopupBody.tsx`, the one thing left in it.
- `BidBoardMapCard`, `ResearchMapCard` and `HomeMapWindow` hand the frame what they handed the canvases.
- A card with no pin to draw still draws its own empty box and words, and mounts no frame.
