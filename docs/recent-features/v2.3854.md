# v2.3854 — The owner is calling: the door and the conversation cards (2026-09-26)

To-do **#47** (`to-dos/owner-is-calling/`, mock-ups `mockup.html` and `conversation.html`). The owner, 2026-09-26: *"I need something in the app that an assistant can read off based on what the customer says … and record the situation the customer is in when the customer calls us asking about the letter that was just sent"*; then, of the first draft, *"it is not intuitive for a user to see the questions and click through"*; then *"Build it — the door and the conversation cards as one PR."* Client only, no migration: the new facts ride on `fields.ownerCall` of the first packet's desk item, where v2.3767's call already lives.

## What was missing

The owner of record is a name on the property, not a customer — an owner saying "I got a letter about 9703 Lenox Hill" had to be found by someone who knew to open the Lien desk, switch to Sent and pick the job. Counsel's rules for what may be said to an owner (memo of 2026-09-22) lived in the letters and the memo, never in front of the person on the phone. `LienOwnerCallDialog` took three bare answers and a note, and not *when* the 10% was released (which decides § 53.105), whether the owner wants to pay us directly (counsel's sign-off, #41), whether the GC is answering them, or what the office told them.

## The change

- **`lienCallerMatch.ts`** (pure) — `sentNoticesForCalls` (one per job: the first packet, where the call is recorded; the letter's facts via `callLetterFactsFor` — instrument, which of counsel's letters by property kind or letter two's kind, mailed, amount, months, GC, signer, the § 53.052 date by property kind) and `matchCaller` (every word of the query against the owner's name and company, the mailing address, the job's address, number and name, the GC; owners newest-packet first, capped at 8; a GC's name adds a signpost hit). Over `LienDeskData` — nothing loaded.
- **`lienOwnerCallScript.ts`** (pure) — the conversation: `CallLetterFacts`, the six openings in the owner's words (`CALL_OPENINGS`), `callCard` (the line to say, its cites, the replies in the owner's words, some with inputs — the amount, the release date, the completion date or "still going"), `callReply` / `callJump` / `callBack`, `callSentence` (the record read back), `callPile`, `callToOwnerCall` (what Save writes), `holdingWords`, `NEVER_SAY`. The words are counsel's, for the phone: the opener (*you did nothing wrong by paying the GC; you didn't hire us; this is not a lawsuit*), § 53.084(a) when they paid in full, the § 53.081 hold (or § 53.081(c) and the affidavit copy on a retainage packet) when they still owe, the 10% asked as *did that stay with you or go to the GC?*, § 53.105 only when it applies (released inside the hold, or never held — `releasedAgainstHold`), the affidavit date and the release the same day, a direct payment as the owner's decision with counsel's sign-off first, the call back.
- **`lienOwnerCall.ts`** — `OwnerCall` gains `releasedOn`, `wantsToPayUs`, `gcSilentToThem`, `callbackWanted`, `told` (tolerant parse; old calls read as before); `releasedAgainstHold`; `ownerCallWords` and the grid's *10% held* column say the release date, *inside the hold*, and *wants to pay us → counsel*.
- **`LienOwnerCallDialog.tsx`** — rewritten as the cards: the holding line, the chips, the Say block, the replies (inputs open under a reply that needs them, then *Next ›*), the sentence and pile, the Never strip on the last card, Back / Cancel / Save. Same props but `facts` in place of the three names.
- **`LienCallerDoor.tsx`** — *☎ Someone's calling ›* on the desk header (the office set): the box, the hits, the GC signpost. **`LienDeskModal`** opens the same dialog from the door's pick (`callerJobId`) or the Sent footer, building the facts with `callLetterFactsFor` and the signer's phone.
- Guide *send lien notices from the Lien desk* → *The owner's call and the three piles* rewritten for the door and the cards; `docs/GLOSSARY.md` → Lien desk; to-do #47 marked built.
