## What changed

[v2.4590](v2.4590.md) and [v2.4596](v2.4596.md) taught every reader of a job's service agreement to name both signers. One writer was left. The `job_contracts` trigger writes the job's activity line when an agreement is signed, and it named the first frame's printed name only. Two people who signed online read *Contract signed by Sam Owner*.

`job_contracts_to_activity()` now joins the filled frames' printed names with *and*, as `signerNamesLine` does:

- **The first frame** counts when it has a printed name.
- **The second frame** counts when the row names a second signer and the frame has a stamp and a printed name.

The line reads *Contract signed by Sam Owner and Alex Owner — Service agreement — $1,200.00*. One signer, no printed name (*the customer*) and a paper record (*Signed contract on file (paper)*) read as before.

Lines already written keep their words. None needs rewriting: on 2026-10-07 no agreement on prod had two frames signed online.
