# v2.7905 — Dashboard: a primary's My Bids opens folded on a phone too (2026-11-19)

The owner's ask of 2026-11-19, with a picture of the Dashboard's **My Bids (24)** card standing open: *for a primary, on their dashboard, the my bids section should be collapsed by default.* Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## Before

- The card has folded for a primary since v2.494, but only on a computer. `DashboardMyBidsSection` drew two headings: wider than 640 px a ▶/▼ press that folds the list, and at 640 px or under a plain heading with the list always open (`isMobile || myBidsSectionExpanded`). The owner's picture is the narrow one: no ▶, 24 rows, most of them old unsent bids, and everything under the card far down the page.
- Folded on a computer, the card hid **New Bid** with the list.

## What changed

- **One rule for how the card opens.** `myBidsStartsOpen(role)` in `dashboardMyBids.ts`: folded for a primary, open for every other role, and open for a role that has not arrived yet. The section's first state and its one-shot effect for a role that hydrates late both read it.
- **One heading at every width.** The ▶/▼ press with its count is the heading on a phone as well, so a primary's card is folded there too. The `isMobile` prop is gone from the section.
- **New Bid stays in view.** It sits beside the heading whether the list is folded or open.
- **The count stays truthful.** Folded, the heading reads *My Bids (24)* once the bids have loaded and plain *My Bids* while they load (its label *My Bids, loading*), as it did open.

## Choices

- **No memory of the press.** The card kept none before and keeps none now: a primary who opens it finds it folded on the next load, as on a computer since v2.494. The sibling cards that remember (`collapseStorageKey`) do it per device, and a remembered open would put the long list back for the person the ask is about.
- **Other roles.** Their card opens as it did at every width. Two things reach them because one heading is simpler than two: on a phone the heading is now a press, so they can fold a list they could not fold before, and on a computer **New Bid** no longer hides when they fold it.

## What it leaves alone

- Who has the card (dev, master technician, assistant-like, estimator, primary), what it loads and when it hides itself with nothing to show.
- The office phone's own folds (`PhoneFold`), which never wrapped this card.

## Tests

- `dashboardMyBids.test.ts`: `myBidsStartsOpen` for a primary, for the other four roles and for a role not yet known.
- `DashboardMyBidsSection.render.test.tsx` (the section's first smoke): a primary's card folded with its count and **New Bid**, open on one press and folded on the next; an estimator's, an assistant's, a master technician's and a dev's open as before; the folded heading with no count while the bids load; a role that arrives late as primary.

## Verified

- On the dev server against live data, at 1280 px and at a phone's 375 px, signed in as the dev: the card opens as before with ▼, one press folds it from 435 px to 61 px with *My Bids (5)* and **New Bid** in view, and a second press opens it. Look only: no press that saves.
- View as the sample primary: its Dashboard draws with no My Bids card, because the sample account is on no bid and the card hides when it has nothing to show. A primary's folded first look is held by the render smoke, not by this look.
