## What changed

- **Database.** `job_baselines` (one per job: bid, kept_at / kept_by / kept_on, price, recorded team hours and wages, people, materials, generated hours-per-$1k and average wage, grade `per_thousand` | `fixture`) and `job_baseline_rows` (recorded hours allocated across the bid's count rows by its predicted hours where any, else by count). `keep_job_baseline` (internal keeper), `keep_job_baseline_now` (the Costs tab's door, wage roles), the `jobs_ledger_keep_baseline_on_billing` trigger (status → billed / paid), `job_baseline_rates()` (hours only, estimators included). Back-filled every billed / paid job with recorded hours.
- **Hooks.** [`useJobBaseline`](../../src/hooks/useJobBaseline.ts) (the row + fixture rows + `keepNow`), [`useJobBaselineRates`](../../src/hooks/useJobBaselineRates.ts).
- **Kernel** [`src/lib/bids/bidBaselineRates.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/bidBaselineRates.ts) (tests): `baselineReading` — the median hours per $1k over usable baselines (price > 0, ≥ 8 recorded hours), the middle half, and the hours that implies for this bid's value; `BASELINE_MIN_SAMPLE` = 3.
- **Costs tab** ([`JobCostsVerdict.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobCostsVerdict.tsx)): the strip says "Baseline kept Sep 12 on billing · per $1k" or offers **Keep as baseline** (mid-job, wage roles) / **Keep again ↻**; a fixture-grade baseline folds open its rows. - **Bids → Labor** ([`BidsLaborNewView.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/BidsLaborNewView.tsx), [`BidsLaborTab.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/BidsLaborTab.tsx)): the **Jobs baseline** tile — "7.8 h per $1k (6.5–10.2 middle half) · 42 jobs · this bid ≈ 960 h".
