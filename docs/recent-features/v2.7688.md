# v2.7688 — Lien mail receipts: add a photo or a PDF, and it is kept the moment you add it (2026-11-17)

The owner, 2026-11-17, asked whether uploading copies of the mail had got easier and whether a copy is easy to find afterwards. The look found five gaps, he said yes to all of them over before and after mock-ups, and this is the first of three changes: the receipt press itself, and the filed liens window. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was there

- The receipt press took a picture alone (`accept="image/*"` with `capture`), so a receipt scanned to PDF at the office could not go up, and on a computer the picker offered pictures only.
- The press read *Take a photo of the receipt*, then *Take it again* and *Photo saved ›*. A wrong photo could only be taken again.
- In **Filed liens: copies to mail** the photo went to Drive at once, but its link lived in the window's own state until **Record**. A window closed first lost the link while the file stayed in Drive.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/jobs/lienReceiptFile.ts`** (new, pure): a receipt is a photo or a PDF (`LIEN_RECEIPT_ACCEPT`, `lienReceiptProblem`, the rule a scan of the paper is held to), named as a receipt photo is named with the file's own extension (`lienReceiptFileName`), and every word the press says. It also holds the merge a kept proof makes, `lienSendsWithProof`: one copy's number and receipt into the filing's `sends`, the copy's method and day kept, every other copy untouched, and a row left with nothing dropped.
- **`LienReceiptPhotoPress.tsx`**: the press reads **Add the receipt** wherever it stands. On a computer it opens the picker for a PDF or a picture. On a phone it opens a sheet (`PhoneSheet`) with **Take a photo** and **Choose a photo or a PDF**. A photo is shrunk and its barcode read, as before. A PDF goes up as it is and is not read, and the press says so. A saved receipt shows **Open**, **Replace** and **Take off**, which asks first and leaves the file in Drive. The clerk's stamped page stays a photo, since its scan has a press of its own.
- **`lienReceiptKeepIo.ts`** (new): `keepLienCopyProof` reads the filing's `sends` fresh and writes them with the one copy changed. Only `sends` is written. The day served is still **Record**'s, and the serve-by watch still clears on the day alone.
- **`LienServeWindow.tsx`**: a receipt added, a receipt taken off, and a number whose box is left are kept at once. A line under the envelope reads *Kept on this lien.*, or says why it was not kept and that **Record** still saves it. A green line over the list says what is kept and what **Record** does. The desk reads again once, when the window closes after anything was kept. The envelope's presses moved under its number, so they wrap on a narrow window.
- **`LienFilingTabs.tsx`**: **Record service…** and a notice's **Save & record sends…** hand the press a way to forget a receipt, so **Take off** shows there too.

## Left as it was

`lienServiceProof.ts` is untouched: the edge functions hold a copy of it, and a change there reads as drift on every function that imports the copy. The new words live in the client's own file.
