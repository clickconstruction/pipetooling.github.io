# v2.8139 — Document builder: a new line on the insurance sheet never takes a removed line's place (2026-11-20)

Punch list #404, one of the faults the review of 2026-11-20 found and did not fix. No migration and no function.

## What was wrong

Every line on the sheet has a key, and a job keeps its own changes against that key: `cover.c6.limit` for an amount it changed, `c6` in the list of lines it leaves off. A new line's key counted the lines (`freshKey`, `sheet.ts`): with five coverage lines the next was `c6`.

Remove `c6` from the usual and add another line, and the count was five again, so the new line was `c6` too. A job that had changed the old line's amount now showed that amount on the new line. A job that had left the old line off now left the new one off. Nothing on the screen said so, since the job's old change had been out of sight from the moment its line was removed.

## The fix

**A key is never given out twice.** A new line's key is its prefix and letters of its own, made as a row's id is made (`mintKey`: `crypto.randomUUID`, or sixteen random bytes on a page with no https). It cannot match a key whose line was removed last week, on this page or on a teammate's. The keys already on a sheet, `cgl` or `o2`, stay as they are. This holds for the usual's coverage lines, its opening lines, and a line added on one job.

Two things were weighed and not done. A count kept on the sheet would be exact, but it is part of the sheet's contents: adding a line and removing it again would leave the usual reading the same and still waiting to be published, and a publish makes the agent's check unread. A count worked out from the keys in use cannot see a key that was removed.

**A job's change to a line that is gone is dropped at the job's next publish.** The card's rule for a field is that a job keeps only what differs, and the list of what differs already left such a change out, *since there is nothing left for it to differ from*. `pruneJobSheet` now lets go of a change to a path the usual no longer has, and of a line left off that the usual no longer asks for. It runs where it already ran: when a job's sheet is published, and when the usual's publish goes to a job. Until then the change stays in the job's draft, out of sight, and it can no longer land anywhere. The page reads the same before and after, which the test holds.

The other choice, showing it as a change to a line that was removed, would list a row with no usual to go back to and no line to open.

## What prod holds

Read through the dev seat on 2026-11-20, reads only. `document_builders` holds the usual at revision 1, its draft the same as its published copy: three opening lines `o1` to `o3` and five coverage lines `cgl`, `auto`, `wc`, `el`, `umb`. `document_builder_sheets` holds one row, a test project's sheet at revision 1, with no changed field, no line left off and no added line. **No live sheet holds a reused key, and no job holds a change against a line that is gone.** Nothing needs mending.

## Tests

`sheet.test.ts`, *a line's key is never given out twice*, four tests. The first removes a line and adds a line with one job that changed the old line's amount and one that left it off, and fails on the old code. The tests that pinned `c6`, `o4` and `x1` hand in a key maker that counts.

## Not walked on prod

Adding or removing a line on the usual saves the usual's draft, and the dev server writes to prod, where the usual is to be left exactly as it is. The change is in the pure kernel and its tests hold it.
