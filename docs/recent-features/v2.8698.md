# v2.8698 — Jobs: the Billing tab is retired, and its links open the Pipeline (2026-11-27)

Punch list #190, decision 5 (`to-dos/usage-paths/README.md`). Taunya answered *Retire it* on 2026-11-25 in an earlier managing session. The PR was opened held, so she could say it again with what goes with the tab in front of her.

**Why.** The card's 60-day read found 0 office minutes on Jobs → Billing while 98 bills were made on the Pipeline. Read again on 2026-11-27 over 90 days: one office person opened the tab once, for under a minute. Every other minute on it was the dev seat's.

**What the tab held, and where each thing lives now.**

| The tab showed | Where it lives now |
|---|---|
| **New Job** | the Pipeline's **New Job**; the Dashboard's **Job** quick button now opens it there |
| A job's line items, as **Specific Work** and **Other job charges** | the job's window, on the **Bill** tab |
| **Total Bill** with *paid*, *billed open* and *unbilled* | the Pipeline row's Progress & payment bar, and Job Summary |
| The crew on the job | the Pipeline row's crew chip |
| The stage chip and the stage filter | the Pipeline is the stages |
| The **EDIT** rail | the Pipeline row's Edit door |
| The footer's count, Total Bill and paid | each stage's header on the Pipeline, and Job Summary |

**What goes with the tab.** Two things had no other home, and they leave with it:

1. **The search by a line item's words** over every open job (*water heater* found every job that billed one). The Pipeline's search reads the number, the name, the address, the GC and the development. The Dashboard's Assigned Jobs box still reads line items, for the jobs assigned to the person.
2. **The Needs labor flag and filter**: the red icon on a numbered job with no Team hours and no Sub Labor book. No other screen flags it. Job Summary shows each job's labor, so the gap can be read there a job at a time.

The HCP sort's saved direction (`jobs_billing_sort_asc_<uid>` in the browser) is no longer read.

**Old links.** `?tab=billing`, and its older name `?tab=ledger`, land on the Pipeline with every other parameter kept (`retiredJobsTab` in `lib/jobs/jobsPageTabs.ts`). Billing's says so once: *The Billing tab is gone. Bills are made here on the Pipeline.* The landing sits under the page's role gates, so a superintendent or a primary is walked to Reports as before and is never told of a Pipeline they do not have. A pin or a remembered search row that names the old tab opens the Pipeline the same way.

**The doors that pointed at it.**

- The Jobs page's row of tabs: Billing is gone. Its thin bar now sits after Pipeline, before whichever tab the role has next.
- The Dashboard's **Job** quick button: `/jobs?tab=stages&newJob=true`.
- The header's search: the Billing row is gone, and its words (*billing*, *invoice*, *bill a customer*) find the Pipeline.
- Banking's Stripe invoices panel, when empty: **Ready to bill** on the Pipeline.
- Quickfill's **Complete, no Total Bill**: the link reads **Pipeline**.
- The Bridge: Bill's desk opens each leaking job's own window (a job in nobody's queue is on no stage's list), its ready jobs and its foot link open the Pipeline's Ready to bill stage, Build's **Bill it** opens the job, and the three **Open Billing** doors on the week, Owed and Net cash read **Open Billed** and open the Billed stage.

**Guides.** *audit job bills on the Billing tab* is retired; its old address answers as *bill a customer and get paid* (`helpGuideFormerSlugs.ts`), which gains **Where the Billing tab went**. That guide's **Go there** now lands on the Pipeline. Three other guides lose their mention of the tab.

**Code.** `JobsBillingTab.tsx` and its render test are deleted. `lib/jobs/billingTab.ts` keeps `billingJobMatchesSearch` alone, which the Dashboard reads. `Jobs.tsx` no longer builds the two labor sets the flag read, and its labor loaders no longer fire for the tab.

**Owed after the merge.** Two generated copies under `supabase/functions/` change by one line each, so two deploys are owed, and neither changes what anyone sees until it goes, since the old link lands on the Pipeline:

- `twin-mcp`: the twins' directory (`briefs.ts`, from `docs/twins/APP_DIRECTORY.md`).
- `bridge-weekly-sheet`: the edge copy of `lib/bridge/thisWeek.ts`, whose *Chase the late invoices* door changed. `npm run check:edge-drift` says if another function reaches the copy.

No migration.
