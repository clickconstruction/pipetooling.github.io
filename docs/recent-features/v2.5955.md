# v2.5955 — Money in: this week, and what last week still owes (2026-10-26)

Punch list #191, PR 3 of 3 (`to-dos/money-in-this-week/`; the planned PRs 3 and 4, the bar and its
pen, shipped as one): drawn from the to-do's mock-up, on the reads of v2.5937 and the kernel of v2.5926.

- **`DashboardMoneyInStrip.tsx`**, mounted by `DashboardFinancialsSection` after its grid with the
  AR and Collections items it already holds, so both Financials homes (the Dashboard and Dispatch
  Mode home) carry it under the cards' own gate (`showFinancials`: dev, master, assistant,
  controller). `MoneyInStripView` is the presentational half, smoke-tested on its own.
- **Closed by default** (the owner's call on the first drawing): one line, *Came in* · *Still
  expected* · *Last week still out* in an amber pill, a red *N RETURNED* pill only when a check came
  back. The open/closed choice is per device (`localStorage` `dashboard-money-in-open`).
- **Open, the week line** (the owner's call on the lane drawing): a header with the week and the
  read time; the dashed drawer shelf (checks typed in, not yet at the bank, oldest first, with the
  count of older *never deposited* checks); the amber *Last week* column; Monday to Friday and a
  quiet *Sat · Sun* cell, today marked with a blue top rule; each column's head carrying
  *✓ landed* and expected sums; every payment a chip with its kind as a small uppercase tag, the
  payer, the amount (*covers* when it is the open money a word or claim covers), one sub line, a
  *was Mon* tag when it slid, and a door line. Pace chips are dashed. On a phone the days stack
  and only the days with money (and today) show.
- **Doors**: a bill's chip opens the job (Job Detail), a deposit, drawer check or returned check
  opens `/accounts-receivable`, the payout opens Banking only for `canAccessBanking` roles — for an
  assistant the door is left off, never drawn dead.
- **The pen** (the owner's third call): a *Their word* or *Pace · a guess* chip on a job opens the
  Payment Chase's own sheet, *Write down their word* (`SetPromisedPayDateModal`), from its body or
  its *New date ›* link, with two new optional props — `quickDays` (the next three bank days and
  next Monday, `penQuickDays`) and `contextLine` (*Now a guess from their usual pace: Tue Oct 27.*,
  `penContextLine`). The save is the sheet's own `add_job_payment_promise`, append-only, so slips
  and misses keep counting; the bar re-reads and the moved chip carries *was Tue, by pace* for the
  visit. A bank, drawer, claim, landed or Stripe chip takes no pen. The sheet's four other callers
  pass neither prop and are unchanged (their smokes pass).
- **Fail-soft**: the key line under the days names any source that could not be read; the rest
  stays live.
- Guide *see what money is coming in this week*; `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` §8, `GLOSSARY.md`
  (*Money in*), `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` (Dashboard table), `DASHBOARD_SECTIONS_ARCHITECTURE.md`.
