# v2.8033 — Checklist: less empty space around the tabs on a phone (2026-11-20)

The owner walked the app on an iPhone on 2026-11-20 and circled two bands of nothing on the Checklist: one between the header's line and the tab row (Today, History, Review, Manage, with the page title at its right), one between that row's underline and the tab's first content. CSS only: no component, no kernel, no migration.

## Where the bands came from

Measured on the dev server at 375 px, the same on all four tabs:

- **Above the row, 18 px** from the header's line to the filled tab: 8 px of `.appMain`'s top padding (shared by every page), 4 px of the Checklist's own top padding (`0.25rem` on the page's wrapper in `Checklist.tsx`), and 6 px of transparent border that `pageTabStyle` puts on a tab so the press area is taller than the pill.
- **Below the row, 24 px**: `.checklist-tab-bar`'s own `margin-bottom: 1.5rem`.

Neither shared value is at fault. `pageTabStyle` and `.appMain` are untouched, so Jobs, People and the other pages that use them draw as before.

## What changed

[`src/index.css`](../../src/index.css), one `@media (max-width: 640px)` block after the `.checklist-tab-title` rule (640 px is the app's phone breakpoint, the one that sets `--app-main-pad`):

- `.checklist-tab-bar` takes `margin-top: -0.25rem`, which cancels the page's own top padding, and `margin-bottom: 0.5rem`.
- `.appMain > div:first-child .checklist-tab-bar` takes `margin-top: calc(-0.25rem - 6px)`: when the page's wrapper is the first thing in `<main>`, the tabs' 6 px of transparent press area tucks under `.appMain`'s top padding. When a banner stands first (the assistant's *ready to bill* banner is a `<button>`, so the wrapper is no longer the first child), the row keeps the smaller pull and its press area stays clear of the banner.

The pull is on the bar, not on the page's wrapper, because Task Mode draws no tab bar: its view of the page keeps the wrapper's padding and is untouched.

## Measured

At 375 px on the dev server against the live data, read-only:

| | before | after |
| --- | --- | --- |
| header's line → filled tab | 18 px | 8 px |
| row's underline → first content, Review / History / Manage | 24 px | 8 px |
| row's underline → first content, Today | 24 px | 9.6 px (the day header's own margin) |

The tab's press box starts 2 px below the header, so it overlaps nothing. With a stand-in banner put first in `<main>`, the press box starts exactly at the banner's foot. At 641 px and at 1,400 px both margins read as before (0 and 24 px; 18 px from the header to the filled tab). With Task Mode switched on, the `Task Mode · Exit` strip, the day bar and Today drew as before, with no tab bar in the page.

## Tests

None added: the change is two margins in a media query, which no unit test reads. The Checklist's five render smokes still mount the page.
