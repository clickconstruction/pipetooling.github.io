U3b-ii of the Building lane's real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/building-u3b.md`, on branch `spike/gc-mode`): the punch list's window over U3b-i's presses (`supabase/migrations/20261010049000_gc_punch_writes.sql`), ported from the prototype's `GcBuildingPunchList`. A dev's while Building is built. No migration.

- **The Punch list window** (`src/components/gc/GcPunchWindow.tsx`), from **Punch list** after **Daily log** on the card of a job being built, at `punch=<projectId>`, behind `canUseGcBuilding` alone: at Building's door it goes to the schedule's team, while Closeout stays the money team's.
- **One trade's list** (`src/components/gc/GcPunchList.tsx`), for each trade we hired with a signed statement of work:
  - **Add an item** with where it is and a photo's Drive link (`gc_add_punch_item`), while the job is being built and the work is not accepted;
  - **They say it is fixed** on the trade's word (`gc_punch_fixed_in`);
  - **Take it off** on an item nobody worked on, after asking in the window's own words, kept in the record (`gc_remove_punch_item`);
  - **Checked, it is fixed**, or **Not fixed** with what is still wrong and **Send it back** (`gc_check_punch_item`).
- **On Closeout**, the same list sits under each trade's six steps, so Accept the work's hold and its cause are side by side.
- **The read** (`src/lib/gc/punchRows.ts`, `punchIo.ts`): an item taken off is left out (`.is('removed_at', null)`, and `withPunch` drops one), `punchCanAdd` says when a list takes a new item as the press does, and `punchIo.ts` gains the four presses. Each press reads the list again.
- **The daily log's window** (`GcDailyLog.tsx`) reads a cleared High or Low as blank, never 0, so **Save** waits; the press refuses one missing since U3b-i.
