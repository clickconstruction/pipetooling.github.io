# v2.7715 — Lien desk on a phone: a sent notice's folded bar shows its mail proof (2026-11-17)

Two findings from the live walk of the mail proof change (v2.7688, v2.7691, v2.7695), fixed on the owner's yes. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was there

- On a phone an open notice's footer folds behind one bar, and a tall footer starts folded (`lienPhoneFootStartsFolded`). **What went out** made the sent footer taller, so on the live site a sent notice opened with the bar reading *Show the next step* and nothing naming the mail proof behind it.
- In the run window on a phone **Add what was mailed** drew 40 px tall, not the 44 px v2.7695 wrote. The window's own floor for every button (`.lienRunWindow button { min-height: 40px }`, a class and an element) outranks a bare class.

## What changed

- `lienPhoneFootFold(folded, proof)` in `src/lib/jobs/lienPhonePane.ts` takes the sent paper's proof (`LienPhoneFootProof`: the block's own chip words and whether it is whole) and returns it as `chip` while folded. Open, it returns none, because the block is on the screen under the bar.
- `LienDesk.tsx` reads the sent notice's proof once where it builds the sent footer (`lienMailProof` over the sent filing and the item's mailed copies) and draws the chip inside the bar (`data-lien-desk-foot-fold-proof`, `owed` or `complete`, the block's own amber and green tokens). A notice in any other pile, an affidavit and a retainage notice pass none.
- The bar itself is 44 px tall, up from 40, since it is a press.
- `src/index.css`: the phone rules for the run's file presses name the element (`button.lienRunCheckCopyAdd`, `button.lienRunCheckCopyOff`), so they outrank the window's floor.

## Tests

- `lienPhonePane.test.ts`: the chip rides the folded bar only.
- `LienDesk.render.test.tsx`: a sent notice at phone width, the bar with and without the chip as the footer folds and opens.
- `LienRunChecklist.steps.render.test.tsx`: the stylesheet's rule names the element and comes after the window's floor.
