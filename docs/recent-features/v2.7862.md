# v2.7862 — Discounts: a job billed from the GC desk takes none (2026-11-18)

## Why

A finding from Put on a bill (v2.7769), left with no card, and the third of the follow-ups the owner chose on 2026-11-18. `apply_job_discount` adds a discount line and writes the job's total again from its lines and its riders. A GC project's billing job is priced by its contract (`gc_owner_billing_revenue`), so the first discount on one would have moved its total off the contract figure until the next Owner Billing write.

No GC project had a billing job on prod when this was written (read 2026-11-18). The fix lands before the first one exists. The rule is the one the owner gave for a trip's line there: the price changes only by a signed change order, so the press is refused and points to Change orders.

## What changed

- **`apply_job_discount` refuses a GC billing job** (`20261118223000_discount_refuses_gc_billing_job.sql`, `docs/migrations/20261118223000_discount_refuses_gc_billing_job.md`). One check, after the caller's own and before anything is written. The function was restated from its newest body by a script, and a diff shows the check alone.
- **Bill Customer does not offer the press there.** `SendRecordInvoiceModal` reads once, when it opens, whether its job is a GC project's billing job (`readTripBillGcJobs`, the read the trip's window makes). `BillCustomerDiscountStrip` takes `heldWords` and draws them in the place of **− Add discount**.
- **One sentence, in both places.** `lib/jobs/gcBillingJobDiscount.ts` holds the words, and its test reads the migration and holds them to the function's refusal word for word.
- A read that fails or is closed leaves the press, and the function's refusal then says the same sentence on Apply.

## Decisions

- **Refuse, with the next place named.** No press to Change orders in this window: Bill Customer sits deep in the Jobs page, and the sentence says where to go.
- **Every role.** The check is about the job, not the caller.

## A finding, not fixed here

The Job window's own **− Add discount**, and any edit to a line there, does not call this function. The form's billing save writes the lines and the total itself (`writeBillingSlice`). On a GC billing job that save would write the total from the lines as well. Nothing in the Job window knows a job is a GC project's billing job. It could not be checked live, since no such job exists yet, and it is left for a card of its own before the first one is made.

## Docs

The guide *give a customer a discount* says a job billed from Owner Billing takes no discount line. `docs/GLOSSARY.md` and `docs/ACCESS_CONTROL.md` say the press is not offered and the function refuses.
