# v2.5243 — The Bridge: the people tables in two groups, and the day grid read three ways (link PR 12) (2026-10-11)

PR 12 of the weakest-link build, the last of the train (`to-dos/bridge-weakest-link/README.md`; the to-do is retired with it). Dev only. No migration.

- **The groups** (`src/lib/bridge/peopleGroups.ts`, `groupPeopleRows`): Vectors' rows split by role — Field (master technician, helpers, subcontractor, superintendent, primary) and Office & estimators (assistant, controller, dev, estimator); a row with no known role is field when it has field hours. Each group sorts by contribution, then by hours waiting, then by name, and carries a subtotal (`PeopleSubtotal`: hours, waiting, earned, labor, contribution and its per-hour, reports, billed, collected, bids sent and won) and a one-line note ("7 people · 80h approved, 269h waiting · all of the contribution"; "4 people, dev counts as office · 59h waiting · all of the billing, collecting and bids").
- **The table** (`BridgeVectorsPanel.tsx`): a small-caps head row per group, the rows, a subtotal row drawn like the Company row; Company stays at the bottom.
- **The reading** (`vectorReading.ts`, `VECTOR_READINGS`, `vectorReadingValue`; `BridgeVectorDaysPanel.tsx`): a Revenue · Profit · Cost group beside the Days / Weeks / Months and Recorded / Approved controls. Profit is the grid as built, untouched. Under revenue a cell shows the bucket's earned dollars in a blue tint; under cost its wages (field plus office labor) in an amber tint; a zero reads "0"; pending days stay dashed. The week and total columns, the Field crew row and the subtitle follow the reading. The page keeps the reading in state (`daysReading`), profit by default.
