# v2.7420 — Jobs → Subs: a sheet paid past its total becomes a credit (sub labor train) (2026-11-15)

Punch list #347's *Not built* list (the owner, 2026-11-13: "overpayment -> credit"). Since v2.7235 a payment typed past what its sheets need keeps the extra as the sub's credit. A sheet over-paid before then still read *Over*, with the extra on a payment row and counted against nothing. Taunya walked the repair in the design spike on 2026-11-15 and said to ship the train.

## What landed

- `src/lib/subWorkOrders/subSendingRepair.ts` (+ test, 3): `overToCreditPlan(sheet, payments, sub)` picks the newest payment that can carry the overage. That is money that left the bank, with no credit on it yet, and larger than the overage so the row never drops to zero. The plan carries its three sentences and its press, *Keep $50.00 as their credit*. It refuses a sheet that is not over, a sheet with no one roster person, and a sheet where no single payment is large enough, each with its reason.
- `src/components/jobs/SubSendingRepairWindows.tsx` (+ render test, 5): `OverToCreditWindow` shows the plan and the sum, *on the sheet + credit = the payment, as it was*, then makes one call. A function that is not on the database yet reads *not switched on yet. Nothing was changed.*
- `JobsSubLaborTab.tsx`: *Keep the $… over as credit…* on the ⋯ menu of a sheet whose balance is below zero.
- Migration `20261116194500_sub_overpayment_to_credit.sql`: `convert_sub_overpayment_to_credit(p_person_id, p_payment_id, p_amount)`. See `docs/migrations/20261116194500_sub_overpayment_to_credit.md`.
- Guides `pay-a-sub-across-sheets-or-keep-a-credit` (a new section) and `sub-labor-outstanding` (the menu's line). Card #347's *Not built* list loses the item.

## The bank tick and the bank match

The amount edit fires `clear_sub_payment_check_on_change`, which clears a tick and deletes the payment's bank match. The spike's draft of the function put the tick back and lost the match. This one reads both before the edit and puts both back as they were made. `match_sub_payment` counts a row's amount plus the credit kept with it against the bank line, so the line holds the figure it held.
