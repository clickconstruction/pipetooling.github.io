# v2.4570 — Legal desk: Write down opens on the account's largest open bill (2026-10-05)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Row 23 of the lien defects card (punch list #83, [`to-dos/lien-defects.md`](../../to-dos/lien-defects.md)).

## The defect

The guides say **Write down…** on the Legal desk opens the agreed write-down on the account's largest open bill line. The header's button called `openWriteDown(null)`, which fell to the account's first job and that job's primary line. On an account of several jobs that was the largest line only by luck, and when the first job had no billed line the desk said *No billed line to write down on this job* though another job had one.

## The fix

- [`legalPacket.ts`](../../src/lib/legal/legalPacket.ts): each `LegalJobLine` carries `primaryInvoiceOpen` beside `primaryInvoiceId`; `legalLargestOpenLine(jobs)` returns the job and line with the most still open.
- [`LegalDeskModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/legal/LegalDeskModal.tsx): with no job named, `openWriteDown` opens on that line. A door that names a job (a gap's *Write down*, the fees section) is unchanged.

## Tests

`legalPacket.test.ts`: the open amount on the line, the largest across an account, a job with no billed line skipped, and none at all.
