# v2.7155 — Tasks: the window stays on screen when the keyboard comes up on an iPhone (2026-11-13)

Punch list #331, the close-out. The card's last open line was "a walk on a real phone, to see the bar hold above the keyboard, and the second Send at the top comes off if it does". The walk was done in the iOS Simulator's Safari (iPhone 17 Pro, iOS 26.4) with the on-screen keyboard up, on **New task**, **Edit task** and the roadmap's **Add task**. It found a failure, this fixes it, and the second Send comes off. The owner then said *Yes, retire it*, so this change also deletes the card (`to-dos/new-task-window/`, its mock-up in git history at this commit) and this fragment carries its record.

## What the walk found

- **The words box: held.** The card shrank to the room the keyboard left and the bar sat above the keyboard, as v2.7085 meant.
- **A field low in the window: failed.** With Done when… (or a link, or any field that sits where the keyboard will be) Safari does more than shrink the visual viewport. It slides it down the page (`visualViewport.offsetTop` 253 px on the 17 Pro) to show the field. The card is `position: fixed` at the top of the page, so the whole card went off the top of the screen: the title, the × and the field being typed in. Only the bar stayed in sight. The second Send at the top went with the head, so it was no fallback in the one place the bar's design failed.
- **Safari's page zoom: failed a second way.** The aA menu at 125% reports `visualViewport.scale` 1.25 with the same slide. A first fix that stood down for any scale above 1, taking it for a pinch, left the failure in place there.

## What changed

- `src/lib/tasks/taskWindowFrame.ts`, new, pure. `taskWindowPhoneFrame({ height, offsetTop })` gives the card's `top` (the slide, never below 0) and its `maxHeight` (the room, less the safe area). The scale is not read: both numbers are the page's own px at any zoom. `scrollTopToReveal` gives the scroll position that brings a field inside a scrolling box, moving it no further than it must, a field taller than the room from its top.
- `src/hooks/useVisualViewportBox.ts`, new. Like `useVisualViewportHeight`, and it also hears the visual viewport's `scroll`, which is how an iPhone says the page slid. It hands back the same object when nothing moved.
- `TaskWindowShell` (the frame of all three windows) reads the hook, puts `marginTop: frame.top` on the phone card and, in a layout effect on each change, scrolls the focused field back into the form with `scrollTopToReveal`. The desk card is as it was.
- `ChecklistAddModal`: the head's Send (v2.2740) is removed. `PhoneSheet` and the dispatch job picker still use `useVisualViewportHeight` and are not touched.

## What it does not prove

- **A simulator is not a phone.** Its Safari and keyboard are the real ones, but a real iPhone's keyboard height, its predictive bar and a third-party keyboard were not seen.
- **One screen size.** A small iPhone simulator could not be driven (its access was not granted), so 125% page zoom on the 17 Pro stood in: 322 by 302 px of room, smaller than an iPhone SE leaves.
- **iOS only.** Android Chrome resizes the page for the keyboard and should need none of this, but it was not walked.
- **Sideways with the keyboard up** iOS leaves about a finger's height of page. No window fits there, before or after.
- **Where is it?** (`TaskPlaceSheet`) is its own sheet and still slides: with the keyboard on the place's name, its title and Cancel leave the top. The name and **Use this place** stay in view. Listed below.
