# v2.8416 — Bills sent: the printed month has its calendar, and the month buttons say where they go (2026-11-23)

Punch list #422, after the three PRs that built it. Two asks from the owner on 2026-11-23, once he had printed By day paid himself and stepped through its months: the printout should carry the calendar the screen draws, and the small ‹ button beside the month's name could be better. No migration, no edge function.

## What was there

- **The print was a list alone.** `billsPaidMonthHtml` wrote the month's title, its count and total, then each day newest first. The month of days, which is the view's whole point on the screen, was not on the paper.
- **The month steps were two bare glyphs.** A 30 px box holding a thin `‹`, and a `›` after the month's name. Neither said where it went. The `›` sat right after the name, so it moved each time the name changed width, and a second press landed on nothing.
- **A day's band could be left alone at the foot of a page**, with its payments on the next one.

## What changed

- **The printed page opens with the month's grid**: a week a row from Sunday, as on the screen. A day money came is boxed in a dark rule with its figure to the cent and its count (*3 paid*). A day with nothing is its number alone. A day after the print is dashed and pale.
- **The figure on paper is whole**, where the screen shortens it (*$96.1k*): the grid and the day under it say one number, and a printed cell has the room.
- **The dark rule carries the mark, not the fill.** A browser leaves a background off the paper unless the person ticks *Background graphics*, so the green tint is asked for (`print-color-adjust: exact`) and never relied on.
- **A chip narrows the grid with the list**, since both are drawn from the lines the chip left. A month with nothing prints its one sentence and no grid.
- **Each month step names the month it opens**: `‹ Oct` and `Dec ›`. A step that leaves the year on the screen says so, `‹ Dec 2025`. The words come from `billsPaidStepLabel`, new and pure, beside `billsPaidShiftMonth`.
- **Each step keeps its own edge of the card.** The header is three columns, the step back at the left, the month's name and total in the middle, the step on at the right. Nothing moves as the name changes width. This month still has no step on, and its place stays empty.
- **The steps look like the page's other presses**: the look of Print and Download CSV at a desk, the phone's full button on a phone, 44 px tall.
- **A day's band stays with its first payment** on the printed page (`break-after: avoid`).

## How it is built

- `src/lib/billing/billsPaidDays.ts`: `billsPaidMonthGridHtml` draws the grid from `billsPaidMonthCells`, the screen's own kernel, with the day the page was printed as today. `billsPaidMonthHtml` puts it between the summary line and the days. `billsPaidStepLabel(month, by)` is the step's words.
- `src/components/billing/BillsPaidByDay.tsx`: the month card's header, and the two steps' test ids `bills-paid-step-back` and `bills-paid-step-on`.
- The guide `see-which-bills-went-out` says both under *Move between months*.

## Tests

- `billsPaidDays.test.ts`: the step's words, across New Year both ways. The printed grid sits between the summary and the days, runs five weeks for November 2026 filled out to Saturday, holds a paid day's figure to the cent, a blank day's number, and a day that has not come, and is absent from an empty month.
- `BillsPaidByDay.render.test.tsx`: an older month offers both steps by name, this month offers the step back alone.

## Looked at

With invented rows, not on prod: the printed page under print media, and the month card at a desk and at 375 px, light and dark, with a step across New Year. The live screen was not walked.
