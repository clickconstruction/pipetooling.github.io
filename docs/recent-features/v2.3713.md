# v2.3713 — Contract sweep: the save word sits with what you type, and the tabs never clip (2026-09-22)

The second pass of the day on [`to-dos/contract-sweep-refresh`](../../to-dos/contract-sweep-refresh/README.md) (#28), from a live look at the pane after v2.3707 with the owner's standing question, *is this the best we can do?* Client only.

## What was wrong

- *Edits save to the job's draft as you type* was rendered inside the **Amount** row — the one field v2.3707 made read-only. It described the scope box above it and the payment line below it, from the one place it could not be true.
- The list's tab row was `overflow-x: auto` with no-wrap tabs. With four tabs (once the Drive pass has found something) in the desktop column's 300–380 px, **All** was cut off with no sign it was there; on a phone the row scrolled sideways, as the to-do had decided, but on the desktop it just clipped.

## The change

[`JobsContractSweepModal`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsContractSweepModal.tsx):

- The save word (`sweep-save-state`) moves onto the **This job** heading, beside the label, and its idle text reads *scope and payment save to the job's draft as you type*. *Saving… · Saved to the job's draft · Save failed · Locked — sent* are unchanged. The Amount row is now the number, its source and its door — nothing else.
- The tab row wraps (`flexWrap: 'wrap'`; each tab keeps `flex: 1 0 auto`, so two rows fill the width). Nothing scrolls sideways, nothing clips.

## Verified

- The sweep's render smokes (12); eslint; `npm run typecheck`.
- Live on the worktree's dev server against prod data (nothing sent): the This job heading carries the save word, the Amount row does not; the four tabs read in full at 800 px and at 375 px.
