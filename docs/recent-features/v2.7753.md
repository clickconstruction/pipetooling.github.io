# v2.7753 — Three trims: empty checklists, your own reports, and supply house chips (2026-11-18)

Punch list #384 (Job Mode, short), PR 3 of 3: the trims everyone gets. Client only. No migration and no edge function.

The look behind #384 found three things on a field person's page that say nothing or say it too often. Each is small and stands alone.

## Empty stage checklists draw nothing in Job Mode

A superintendent or an estimator who authors on a job sees a stage that has no question yet, so the first check has a door (punch list #237). On Job Mode's card that put a row per stage reading *No questions yet. Add a check for this job.* on every job.

- `JobStageChecklistStrip` takes `hideWhileEmpty`. With it, the strip draws nothing while every listed stage has no question to answer (`stripHiddenWhileEmpty` in `lib/jobs/stageChecklistItems.ts`). One stage with a question brings every row back.
- `JobModeDetailsSection` passes it. The job window (`DetailJobModal`) and the crew's Today's work do not, so the empty stage is still listed there and the first check keeps its door, one press from the card's rail.

## Recent Reports stops counting your own reports as new

The count reads `report_reads`. Nothing writes a row when a person saves their own report, so each one counted as new, and the section opens itself when anything is new.

- `readIdsWithOwnReports` in `lib/dashboardRecentReports.ts` adds the viewer's own reports to the read ids, from `created_by_user_id`, which `list_reports_with_job_info` already returns. `DashboardRecentReportsSection` reads through it.
- Nothing is written. The viewer's own report reads as opened: it is behind *Show N opened reports* with the rest, and one marked done stays done.

## Supply house chips fold to one line

Every My Schedule card drew one dashed chip per supply house that expects a job account. On a job with none opened, three chips said *none yet* three times, on every stop.

- `JobAccountsStrip` takes `foldNone`. While two or more houses all read none yet (`stripFoldsToOneLine`), the chips fold to one press, *none yet at 3 houses* (`stripFoldedWords`). A press opens the chips as they were.
- A house that is open, asked or not needed keeps every chip, and so does a single house.
- `DashboardMyScheduleSection` passes it. Assigned Jobs, Dispatch Mode's schedule and the job window are untouched.
