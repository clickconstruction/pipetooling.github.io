# v2.7269 — Lien desk: a job with a recorded notice that still owes one says why, with the doors that fit (2026-11-14)

Punch list #353 part 2 ([`to-dos/affidavits-picked-set-and-notice-owed.md`](../../to-dos/affidavits-picked-set-and-notice-owed.md)), the owner's three doors of 2026-11-14. Client and the dev seat: `_shared/devMcpAffidavitDraft.ts` and the copied kernel `_shared/gcKernels/lib/jobs/lienNoticeOwed.ts` change, so `dev-mcp` is redeployed after the merge. ## Before

A § 53.056 notice recorded by hand for three jobs at one property named April to August. Each job's last work month was September, from a last day set by hand, and no notice named it, so each sat in To draft with *Notice to draft* and no reason, two days from the window. The office, who had just recorded the mailing and its receipt, expected the row to clear. The affidavit pane had the same gap: its gate asks for *a* notice on record, so a draft went through while September's own notice was still due.

## What changed

- **[`src/lib/jobs/lienNoticeOwed.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienNoticeOwed.ts)**, a pure kernel. `lienNoticeOwedWords(dueMonths, namedMonths)` is the row's sentence (*September 2026 has no notice. The one on record names April, June, July and August 2026.*), null with no notice on record. `LIEN_NOTICE_OWED_DOORS` are the two doors' words. `lienLastMonthNoticeDue` says whether the last work month's own notice is still due on a sub job (no live notice names it, its window open), and `lienAffidavitNoticeDueLine` is the pane's line. `lienNoticeToExtend` picks the newest live notice the first door corrects, `lienNoticeMonthsAfter` its months with the owed ones added, `lienNoticeOwedConfirmWords` the confirm window (*Add September 2026 to the record*). Tests beside it.
- **`lienNextUp.ts`**: `LienNextUpInput.noticeMonthsByJob` (the months the live notices name, from the desk's `filingsByJob`); a To draft row with a notice on record takes the sentence and `doors`: *The paper named September 2026 too* → `{ open: 'notice_record_months', jobId, months }` and *The last day of work is wrong* → `{ open: 'lien_window', tab: 'notice', openLastWork: true }`. A viewer with no press gets the words and no doors.
- **`LienDeskNextUp.tsx`** draws the doors beside the button (`data-lien-next-up-door`), and a phone's job screen (`lienPhoneJobScreen.ts` `moreDoors`, `LienDeskJobScreen` `onMoreDoor`) lists them above the foot.
- **`LienDesk.tsx`**: the first door asks (`confirmDialog`) and writes through [`lienNoticeOwedIo.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienNoticeOwedIo.ts) `addMonthsToLienNotice`, then reads again; the second opens the Lien window through the page's `onOpenCalendarLastWork` (the *Worked later?* door's own), so the day is ready to change.
- **`LienDeskAffidavitPane.tsx`** draws `data-lien-affidavit-notice-due` above the send presses: *September 2026's notice is still due, by Nov 16. The paper will not name that month until the notice is recorded.* A line, not a gate.
- The dev seat's `plan_affidavit_draft` reply carries `notice_still_due` with the same sentence.
- Guides: *send lien notices from the Lien desk* (the row and its doors), *file a lien and never miss its deadlines* (the pane's line).

## Not changed

- The desk's rule stands: a month with no notice and days left is To draft. Only the words and the doors are new.
- The first door writes nothing until the office confirms, and never touches the notice's sends, claim or saved copy.
