# v2.5124 — The Lien desk leaves out ZZ test jobs (2026-10-09)

Punch list #61, PR 3 of 3 (`to-dos/zz-test-jobs-hide.md`), built once the lien lane was quiet (no lien PR open; #5187 and #5192 had landed). No migration. One edge function's source changed, with nothing it sends (below). It is built on 2a's rule (`hidesZzTestJobs` with the dev's switch). Most lien reads here already join the jobs, and the joined rows carry the job's and the customer's names. The two that read only job ids use the shared ids (`loadZzTestJobIds`).

## What shipped

- **The Lien desk** (`useLienDeskData`, new option `hideZzTestJobs`). The desk now reads its four lists (the due months, the desk items, and the affidavit and retainage windows), joins the jobs, and drops ZZ jobs from all four and from the jobs before any queue is built. That covers the leader's and the office's piles, the batches, letter two, the missed lines and the claim corrections.
  - The Pipeline passes the option, and so do the desk's light reads on the Dashboard's pinned row and Quickfill's Needs you.
- **The timeline book** (`useLienTimelineBook`, a new fourth argument, passed by `LienDeskModal`). `LIEN_BOOK_JOB_COLUMNS` gains `customer_name`, which is also optional on `LienBookRawJob`, so the portal's rows stand. ZZ jobs leave the months, the affidavit windows and the jobs right after the join. The desk items handed in are already without them.
- **GC on notice** (`useGcOnNoticeData`, a new third argument, passed by `GcOnNoticeModal`). A ZZ job under the GC leaves the run right after the jobs are read: its months, desk items, line items, bills and payments, and its band. Each list is filtered into a new array at the read, and the bands are built from the kept jobs. A GC whose own name starts with ZZ is a test run: every job leaves, real-looking names too, as on a ZZ customer's page, and their lists are not read. The GC's name is read beside the months; a failed name read falls back to the per-job rule.
- **The lien releases owed nudge** (`useLienReleasesOwedNudge`, a new second argument). It moved here from 2b to stay off the lien lane. Owed is worked out once (`owedLienReleasesByJob`, now exported); once the owed jobs are read, a ZZ job leaves that map, and the count and dollars are summed from what is kept (`summarizeLienUnconditionalOwed`, split out of `computeLienUnconditionalOwed`).
- **The other lien nudges on the same cards** (review on #5250), so a card never reads "Lien desk 0" beside a ZZ job's line. The Dashboard's pinned row and Quickfill pass the option to all four.
  - **The lien watch** (`useLienWatchNudge`): its billed-jobs read adds `job_name, customer_name`, and ZZ jobs leave before the bills are read.
  - **Demand deadlines** (`useDemandDeadlinesNudge`) and **owner records signed** (`useOwnerRecordsSignedNudge`, by the request's seed job): the letters and requests carry only job ids, so they drop by the shared ids. Both warn about real money or a real owner waiting, so a failed id read counts every row.
- **legal-portal** (edge source only). `readLienBook`'s job select adds `customer_name`, so it is the same list as `LIEN_BOOK_JOB_COLUMNS`. The column is cut from each job before the book is shaped, so the firm's payload is unchanged. The firm's book applies no ZZ rule.
- **Docs**: GLOSSARY (ZZ convention), EDGE_FUNCTIONS (legal-portal) and the card are amended in place. The card moves to *close*, for a live look before it retires.

## Proof

- `useLienDeskData.zz.render.test.tsx` (2 tests): ZZ jobs, by their own name or their customer's, leave all four lists and the jobs; without the option every job reads.
- `useGcOnNoticeData.zz.render.test.tsx` (4 tests): a ZZ job leaves the run with its bills; a ZZ GC's run drops every job and reads none of their lists; without the option the run reads every job.
- `useLienReleasesOwedNudge.zz.render.test.tsx` (2 tests, with the release kernels stubbed): the ZZ job's release leaves the count ($500 instead of $2,700) and the queue, and owed is worked out once.
- `useLienWatchNudge.zz.render.test.tsx` (2), `useDemandDeadlinesNudge.zz.render.test.tsx` (3) and `useOwnerRecordsSignedNudge.zz.render.test.tsx` (3), with their kernels stubbed: a ZZ job's row leaves; a failed id read keeps every row; without the option nothing leaves and no ids are read.
- The timeline book has no test of its own. It applies the same drop as the desk, with the same rule over its joined rows.
- The related suites pass, along with eslint, the theme-token check and the full typecheck.
