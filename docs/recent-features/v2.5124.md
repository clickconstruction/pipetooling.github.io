# v2.5124 — The Lien desk leaves out ZZ test jobs (2026-10-09)

Punch list #61, PR 3 of 3 (`to-dos/zz-test-jobs-hide.md`), built once the lien lane was quiet (no lien PR open; #5187 and #5192 had landed). Client only. No migration, no edge function. It is built on 2a's rule (`hidesZzTestJobs` with the dev's switch). Every lien read here already joins the jobs, and the joined rows carry the job's and the customer's names, so no shared id read is needed.

## What shipped

- **The Lien desk** (`useLienDeskData`, new option `hideZzTestJobs`). The desk now reads its four lists (the due months, the desk items, and the affidavit and retainage windows), joins the jobs, and drops ZZ jobs from all four and from the jobs before any queue is built. That covers the leader's and the office's piles, the batches, letter two, the missed lines and the claim corrections.
  - The Pipeline passes the option, and so do the desk's light reads on the Dashboard's pinned row and Quickfill's Needs you.
- **The timeline book** (`useLienTimelineBook`, a new fourth argument, passed by `LienDeskModal`). `LIEN_BOOK_JOB_COLUMNS` gains `customer_name`, which is also optional on `LienBookRawJob`, so the portal's rows stand. ZZ jobs leave the months, the affidavit windows and the jobs right after the join. The desk items handed in are already without them.
- **GC on notice** (`useGcOnNoticeData`, a new third argument, passed by `GcOnNoticeModal`). A ZZ job under the GC leaves the run, in place right after the jobs are read: its months, desk items, line items, bills and payments, and its band.
- **The lien releases owed nudge** (`useLienReleasesOwedNudge`, a new second argument). It moved here from 2b to stay off the lien lane. Once the owed jobs are read, a release on a ZZ job leaves, and the count, dollars and queue are made again. The Dashboard's pinned row and Quickfill pass the option.
- **Docs**: GLOSSARY (ZZ convention) and the card are amended in place. The card moves to *close*, for a live look before it retires.

## Proof

- `useLienDeskData.zz.render.test.tsx` (2 tests): ZZ jobs, by their own name or their customer's, leave all four lists and the jobs; without the option every job reads.
- `useGcOnNoticeData.zz.render.test.tsx` (2 tests): a ZZ job leaves the run with its bills; without the option the run reads every job.
- `useLienReleasesOwedNudge.zz.render.test.tsx` (2 tests, with the release kernels stubbed): the ZZ job's release leaves the count ($500 instead of $2,700) and the queue.
- The timeline book has no test of its own. It applies the same drop as the desk, with the same rule over its joined rows.
- The related suites pass, along with eslint, the theme-token check and the full typecheck.

## To see it

On the dev server, sign in as an assistant and open the Lien desk from the Pipeline. No ZZ job is in any pile, the timeline book or a GC's run. The Needs you lien card counts none. As the dev, showing test jobs in Hide groups… brings them back on that device.
