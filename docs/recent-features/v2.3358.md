# v2.3358 — Bills also go to, PR 1: remembered copy recipients on the job, the customer's contacts, and Bill Customer (2026-09-12)

Migration `20260912012359_bills_also_go_to.sql` ([`docs/migrations/20260912012359_bills_also_go_to.md`](../migrations/20260912012359_bills_also_go_to.md)); no edge function. First of two PRs for Taunya's ask (2026-09-11, from the job window's Edit tab): *"Option to add a 2nd email to send bill to (DRF)."* PR 2 carries the copies onto the Stripe path. Mock-up + the three options: artifact `6fab2ec9`; the owner picked A (a row on Edit Job, remembered on the customer).

## Why

Who pays (v2.3345–v2.3353) settled **who is billed** — "DRF" turned out to be Done Right Foundation, the GC on a homeowner pretest, and the owner marked them the payer. What was still missing was **who else gets a copy**, remembered. Bill Customer had a *Send to* list since v2.940 (the customer's contacts + a one-off), and v2.3345 added a *Copy <the other party>* tick, but every choice was per bill: the office re-ticked the AP clerk on every send, and nothing on Edit Job said a second person existed. And in the last 90 days every emailed bill went through Stripe (119 Stripe, 0 PDF emails), where no copy list exists at all — that half is PR 2.

## What shipped

- **Schema**: `customer_contact_persons.gets_bill_copies` (a contact copied on every bill sent to their customer), `jobs_ledger.bill_copy_other_party` (the party *not* billed — the GC on a customer-pays job, the customer on a GC-pays job — gets a copy of every bill), `jobs_ledger_invoices.copy_emails` (the copy list fixed when a bill goes out; PR 2's consumer, unused by this PR's client). Additive; existing RLS.
- **Kernel** [`src/lib/jobs/billCopyRecipients.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/billCopyRecipients.ts) (+11 tests): `defaultCopyContactIds` (flagged contacts with an address), `defaultCopyOtherParty` (job flag + the party has an email), `buildCopyEmails` — the one builder both channels call: lowercased, deduped, never the primary, the customer's people never ride a typed bill-to recipient (tenant), capped at `BILL_COPY_MAX` = 10 (the physical-invoice function's limit) — `billsAlsoGoToSummary` (the collapsed row wording), `parseCopyEmails`.
- **Edit Job → Edit tab**: a **Bills also go to** fact row under *Bills go to* (shown once the job has a customer). Collapsed it names the people (*DRF · Done Right Foundation (GC)*) or *nobody else*. Opened, [`JobFormBillCopyRecipientsControl`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormBillCopyRecipientsControl.tsx) lists the **payer's** contacts (the GC's people when the GC pays, else the customer's) each with a tick that writes `gets_bill_copies` straight away (optimistic, reverts on refusal), the other party as a one-tick copy (none on a split job — each draft picks there; hidden when the GC is the customer row), and **+ Add a person** (name + email → a new contact of the payer, already ticked). `useBillCopyContacts` owns the fetch/writes so the fact-rows file stays a list of rows.
- **The job flag rides the identity autosave slice** (`bcp` in the slice JSON, `bill_copy_other_party` in the PATCH and the New Job insert) and the undo snapshot — same mechanics as *Bills go to*.
- **Edit customer → Contacts**: the sheet gains **Gets a copy of every bill** (disabled until the contact has an email; saved false when the address is blank); the row wears a green *gets every bill* chip.
- **Bill Customer** ([`SendRecordInvoiceModal`](../../src/components/jobs/SendRecordInvoiceModal.tsx)): on open, the payer's flagged contacts start ticked and *Copy <the other party>* follows the job flag; the physical-invoice `additional_emails` now come from `buildCopyEmails` (same rules as before, one home). The Stripe tab is unchanged in this PR — copies there are PR 2.
- Help: new guide **send a bill to more than one person** (`send-a-bill-to-more-than-one-person`); `ready-to-bill-pipeline` routes its "more than one person" section there; `choose-who-gets-the-bill` notes the copy tick is remembered. `GLOSSARY.md` "Bills also go to"; `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` and `BILLING_FLOWS.md` one line each.
