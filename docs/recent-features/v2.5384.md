# v2.5384 — Lien desk: the rules for a run that mails through the mail service, and its Developer switches, behind the scenes (Docsmit train, PR 5a) (2026-10-13)

PR 5 of the Docsmit train (`docs/DOCSMIT_LIEN_RUN_PLAN.md`, *The run window*) splits in two: the pure side here, the window (the button, the price step, the review modal, the send) once the run train's cards, foot and checklist have landed on `LienDeskRunModal.tsx`. Nothing on screen; both files sit in `src/lib/mail/`.

**`runDocsmit.ts`** — `docsmitCandidates(envelopes)`: the certified envelopes as the provider's party (`mailParty`, v2.5375) with their problems, the piece's kind from the instrument, the one job inside or none, the item ids, and the window lines (v2.5382). `docsmitRunDoor(settings, candidates)`: hidden while paused, dim with nothing certified or nothing clean, *Mail through Docsmit · N envelopes* otherwise, with the why. `docsmitPrepareDraft(candidate, pages, { receipt, filingIds })`: the short code, the envelope by pages (`mailEnvelopeFor`), the send type (D1), and the `piece` exactly as `mail-send-docsmit`'s `prepare` takes it. `docsmitCoverWindow`: the owner's letter's window and code as `CoverPageInput.window` takes them. `docsmitPriceWords(prepared, balance, warn)`: *2 envelopes · $22.25 · balance $120.00*, with *$17.75 left after, add credit soon* under the warning and *Docsmit credit is short by $7.25* when the balance will not cover it. `docsmitSendOutcome(answer, prepared)`: which envelopes mailed, which stay, and one line for the foot (*1 envelope mailed through Docsmit. Docsmit credit is short by $7.25 at envelope 2; nothing more was sent. The rest stay on paper.*). `docsmitSendRecord(pieceId, tracking)`: the `via` / `piece_id` the filing's `sends[]` keeps. `docsmitSendByStrip(windowLastYmd, settings, now)`: *Send to Docsmit by 2026-10-14 at 3:00 pm*, or the late line. Six tests.

**`docsmitSettings.ts`** — `DocsmitSettings` with every default (`DOCSMIT_SETTINGS_DEFAULT`: Tracksmit, not paused, the run opens on Docsmit, the electronic return receipt, no plain copy, cutoff 15:00, one business day of buffer, warn under $25, poll hourly, no callbacks, no site matching), the `app_settings` keys (`docsmit_*`), `docsmitSettingsFrom(rows)` (a missing, blank or malformed row keeps its default; a clock is normalised; numbers are clamped) and `docsmitSettingRows(settings)` for the Developer block to write. Two tests, one of them the round trip.
