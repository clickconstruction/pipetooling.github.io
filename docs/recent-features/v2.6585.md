# v2.6585 — Any job can carry a target: the Target line on the job window (2026-11-06)

Punch list #255 (`to-dos/technician-progress-signals/`), the second of the three builds Micah named first: job targets. The lead's signal on the Your week card, the crew's hours against the target, counts only finished jobs that carry one, and the card's own read of the data said *many jobs carry no target*: the expected hours of v2.6321 are set on the project board alone, and a service job had no door. No migration: `job_expected_hours` and its two functions (v2.6321) take any job the setter sees.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/jobs/jobTarget.ts`** (+ 4 tests): `jobTargetWords(target, budgetHours, budgetSource)`, the line's words: *Target 96 h* with `targetWords`' who, when, from what and why; *Target 110 h from the labor budget* naming the budget's source (from the bid, typed by the office); *No target yet* with what the target is for. `targetDraftProblem(hours, why)`, the editor's check (hours above zero, a reason of 3 to 240 characters, as `set_job_expected_hours` requires), and `parseHours`.
- **`src/lib/techWeek/crewTarget.ts`** (+ 7 tests), the lead's signal's kernel for the card to come: `targetOf` (the open expected hours first, else a labor budget above zero), `crewAgainstTarget(jobs, week)` (finished in the pay week with a target: the crew's hours, the target, the count, how many finished with none), `crewTargetWords` (*112 h for a 124 h target*, *✓ 12 hours under on 3 jobs*, *8 hours over on 3 jobs*, *On the target over 2 jobs*, *No finished job with a target this week*, and the note *One more job finished with no target, so it is not counted.*), `lastWeeks` and `crewTargetWeeks` for the eight-week dots.
- **`JobTargetLine`** (`src/components/jobs/JobTargetLine.tsx`, + 4 render tests) on the job window's Job tab above the review line, for every job: the line, **Set a target** / **Change the target** (hours and why, `useExpectedHours.setTarget`) and **Back to the budget** (`clearTarget`) for the leader and the office (`canSetExpectedHours`, never a training account); a crew lead reads it. The budget's hours come from the job's `job_budgets` row under the viewer's own policy.
- **Guide**: *see where every project stands and set its expected hours* gains *Set a target on any job*.

## Decisions

- **The labor budget is the fallback target, named as such.** A bid's hours are the number the crew was sold on; the office overrides it with a reason, as on the board. The signal reads the same `targetOf` on every surface.
- **Typed hours, no slider.** The board's slider snaps to a project's crew days; a service job has no pace to snap to, so the window takes the number typed.
- **Under the target is up.** The pill reads ✓ and *under*, never a green arrow beside a smaller number (the mock-up's second pass).
