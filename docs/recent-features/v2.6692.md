# v2.6692 — The Pipeline's phone rows show the customer's newest line (punch list #125, follow-up to PR 2) (2026-11-08)

Punch list #125 (`to-dos/pipeline-customer-conversation/README.md`): the live walk of 2026-11-08 at 375 px found no card on the phone board drawing the teaser v2.6689 (#7297) promised. The cause was in the render, not the read or the kernel: the phone board (`isMobile` under 641 px with Mobile cards on, the width-gated default under 560 px) has `JobsStagesCardList` render `StagesPhoneRow` — two lines and one chip, punch list #30 — and `cardActivityTeaser` sat only on the fuller card the list draws when `phoneRows` is unset, so the teaser showed on Mobile cards above 640 px and never on a phone. No migration, no edge function.

## What changed

- **The kernel** `src/lib/jobs/jobActivityBoxFeed.ts` (+2 tests): `phoneRowCustomerTeaser(stat, lines, waiting)` is `activityBoxTeaser`'s pick when, and only when, it is the customer's line (`JobActivityBoxLineTeaser`, its `line` present). A note or a report that is newer gives null, so the phone row keeps #30's two lines; the fuller card's teaser is unchanged and still reads all three kinds.
- **The phone row** `StagesPhoneRow.tsx`: an optional `teaser` node under the second line. The row's own tap stays the job's; the teaser's maker marks its press `data-phone-row-chip`, the mark the row already honours for the development door and the chip.
- **The card list** `JobsStagesCardList.tsx` (+2 render tests): `activityTeaserWords` is the words both teasers share (who in the deep orange when from the customer, the channel word, how long ago, the body, *Nobody has answered.* while the dot is on), lifted out of `cardActivityTeaser`; `phoneRowTeaser(ctx, job)` draws them on the phone row through the kernel's pick, on the orange tint with the bar when from them, as a press that opens the customer's window (`ctx.onOpenCustomerDoor`) in both phone branches (the Working-style list and the billed rows). The render test pins the line, the waiting words, the press reaching the sheet and not the job, and the two-line row while a note is newer.
- **The guide** `text-a-customer-from-the-job.md`: the phone paragraph now describes the row's third line and says a note or a report does not earn it there.

## Why the customer's line alone

#30 built the phone row as two lines and one chip so a crew lead reads the board on the truck without scrolling through notes; the fuller card's teaser of the newest note or report was the thing it dropped on purpose. The customer's line is different: it is the one thing on the row the office has not read yet, and v2.6689 promised it on the phone. So the row gains it, and only it, and only while it is the newest thing on the job. Whether the notes should come back as a third line on the phone is the owner's call and stays on #125.

## How to verify

On the live board on a phone (or Chrome's device toolbar at 375 wide), a job whose customer's text is newer than its last note shows the orange line under the row's two: *Dana · text · 1d ago — Can you do Thursday?*, and *Nobody has answered.* while the dot is on. Press it: the speech bubble's window opens. Press the row's name: the job opens. A job whose newest thing is a note keeps two lines.
