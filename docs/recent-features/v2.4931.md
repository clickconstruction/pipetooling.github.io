# v2.4931 — GC mode: bid tabs for the trades, for a dev (2026-10-08)

The Board's B5-d (`to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/board-b5.md` on branch `spike/gc-mode`, section 7): bid tabs on real data at `/gc`, dev only. It reads and writes the `gc_bid_tabs` and `gc_bid_tab_views` that B5-a (v2.4923, migration `20261008130000`) made. Guide: `share-a-bid-tab-with-the-trades`.

- **`boardRows.ts`** maps `gc_bid_tabs` (`BidTabShareRow`) and `gc_bid_tab_views` (`BidTabViewRow`) onto `TradePackage.bidTab`: the day, whether names show, and `seenBy` (the companies that opened it). `loadGcBoardRows` reads both for the projects' trades.
- **`GcBidTabs.tsx`** is ported from the spike's `GcBidTabs.tsx`.
  - The office's read, one card per trade with two quotes (`packageHasTab`): rank, company, quote, over the low, and *Their copy* (*not sent*, *opened* or *not opened yet*).
  - **Share with the N who quoted** waits for `bidTabsOpen` (our bid in, or past bidding). **Show company names to each other** changes the names. Both go through `shareGcBidTab`, an upsert that keeps the first day, then reload the board.
  - The line under each table says what every company reads beside it, by the project's stage. A lost bid says the customer did not pick us, never the price or who won.
  - `BidTabTable`, the company's own read in the portal's language, is exported word for word for the Portal's P5e (agreed with Helper 3: one copy).
  - Left out: *See it as them* (the Portal's preview) and *awarded by* (main's trade has no `awardedBy` yet).
- **The card**: **Bid tabs (N)** on the project's head, counting the trades with a tab, opens the panel for a dev. When the bid is not in yet, the panel points to **We sent our bid** on the head (B5-c) rather than repeating it.
- **Tests:** a mapper case (tab, names, seen by); `GcBidTabs.render.test.tsx` (waits for our bid, shares low to high without names, a shared tab with its day, who opened it and the names tick, a company's own read); a page test opening and hiding the tabs.
- **Door 2's seam** (Helper 6): the two tables stay dev only until door 2's migration gives them the office team policy. Without it an estimator reads no rows, so every tab reads *not shared yet*.
