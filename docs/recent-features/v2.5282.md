# v2.5282 — Messages: the database for quick replies (2026-10-12)

Messages, cut 3's SQL (migration `20261012190000_quick_replies`, after the spine's `20261012010000`): quick replies, the lines the office types every day, on helper 3's kernel (`src/lib/quickReplies/kernel.ts`), whose types these columns hold. No screen, no edge function.

- **`quick_replies`** (words, who it is for, its flow step as a plain `step_key` with no foreign key, team or mine, who added it and when, when removed), **`quick_reply_versions`** (every change with who and when: added, words, tags, removed, restored; an identity id keeps a save's words before its tags) and **`quick_reply_uses`** (each press, by whom, in whose thread). The office team's, a line marked mine its owner's alone; read only for `authenticated`, with the three fences.
- **`add_quick_reply`, `edit_quick_reply`, `remove_quick_reply`, `restore_quick_reply` and `press_quick_reply`** make every change and write its version, so the history holds every change. `SECURITY DEFINER`, each refusing in words someone signed out, a training account, a twin and anyone off the office team; an edit writes one or two versions, words and tags apart, and refuses "Nothing changed."
- **`contacts_fold_quick_replies`** moves a folded contact's presses for the spine's `contacts_fold`; outside a trigger, a call from someone signed in off the office team, or a twin, moves nothing.
- **`quick_replies_seed`** adds the prototype's nineteen lines once: the team's sixteen as the app's when the migration runs, the three the prototype's viewer keeps when a person is named. A second run adds nothing, and a line the team removed or reworded stays as they left it.
- **`users.title`**, nullable and 80 characters at most, for the signature under an email (`compose.ts`'s `signatureLines`).
- **The bed** (`scripts/pgtest-quick-replies.sh`, in `scripts/sql-beds.txt`): the seed once, words then tags as two versions with who, a press read by the team, remove and bring back, each refusal in words, the title, and the fold. Not pushed: `supabase db push` waits for `main`.
