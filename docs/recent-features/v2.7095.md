# v2.7095 — The Bridge says what it leaves out, and you can change the list (punch list #334, PR 2) (2026-11-12)

PR 2 of the Bridge train (card #334, `to-dos/bridge-pulse/`), the bottom of stack A, on PR 1. The owner asked for one development to leave every count on the page, then for the page to say so and let a person choose, for any development, any customer or any address. The rule (`isLeftOut`) and the loaders' `leftOut` option shipped with the won rates (v2.6977) and were handed an empty list. This PR ships the list: its read, its save, the line that says it and the picker that changes it.

## What changed

- **The line under the page's name** (`BridgeLeftOut.tsx`): *Leaves out Oak Hollow and Ridgeway Builders.* or *Nothing is left out.*, with **Change ›**. Behind the press: one sentence that says how many jobs the list names today, each rule as a chip with **Put back**, and a picker. Pick **Development**, **Customer** or **Address**, type a few letters, press **Leave out …** with the name. An address is its words, five letters or more: **Leave out this address**. On a phone every press and the field are 44 px tall.
- **The list** (`leftOut.ts`, `leftOutIo.ts`): one `app_settings` row, `bridge_left_out_v1`, a JSON list of `{ kind, key, label }`. `parseLeftOut` drops what is not a rule and keeps a rule named twice once; no row, an empty row and broken text all read as never saved, which is an empty list. A change is followed at once and saved behind it; a save that is refused says why and holds for the visit.
- **Where a left-out job leaves** (`Bridge.tsx`, `useDashboardFinancials.ts`): `loadLeftOutJobIds` reads every job and keeps the ids a rule names (its development, its customer, the customer as its general contractor, the words of its address). Those ids join the ZZ test job ids every Bridge read already takes, and the financials hook takes them as a fifth argument. So a left-out job leaves exactly where a test job does: the tiles, the pipe, every step window, the Bill pile, the people tables, Money. The bank's cash and the overhead pool are not a job's and stay whole. The won rates take the rules themselves, as they have since v2.6977.
- **The page reads once**: the list is read before the page's data, so nothing is read with the jobs in and then again without them. Each lazy read's key carries the list (`leftOutKey`), so a window opened under one list is read again under another.
- **The owner may save it** (`20261113050000_bridge_left_out_key_owner.sql`): insert and update on that one key through `is_master_or_dev()`, the shape of the cash keys' policies (v2.6964). A dev alone could write the row before.

## Three things settled against the spike's draft

- **No development is named in the code.** The draft's default list named one. The default is now an empty list, and `defaultLeftOut` is gone. The list the owner set is already the saved row, so the page reads the same on the day this ships.
- **ZZ test names stay off the picker** for a viewer who leaves ZZ test jobs out (`leftOutPickerNames`, by `isZzTestName`): their jobs have left the page by the ZZ rule already. A dev who shows ZZ jobs is offered every name. A ZZ rule that is already on the saved list still shows as a chip, with its **Put back**.
- **A dev who shows ZZ bids keeps them in the won rate when the list names something else.** In the bids' loader a set of ids stands for the viewer's ZZ switch, so handing it the left-out jobs' ids would have dropped every ZZ bid. The bids' read takes the switch alone (`readZzSwitchIds`); a bid on something left out leaves by the list's own rules.

## Left, on purpose

- For a dev who shows ZZ test jobs while the list names anything, the other reads still take one set of ids, and they read a set as *leave the ZZ jobs out*: a job or a bid with a ZZ name leaves the pipe, the step windows and the people tables. Nobody else is affected, and a dev who hides ZZ jobs (the default) sees no difference. Separating the two meanings is a change to every loader's options, not this PR's.
- The weekly email does not read the list.

## Verify

Walked on a dev server as a dev on production data, in headless Chrome, at 375 × 812, 768 × 1024 and 1400 × 900, light and dark at 375 and 1400. Every write to the database was refused; the only requests refused were the app's own last-seen stamp and its nav-click log. Nothing that records was pressed: no **Leave out …**, no **Put back**, no **Leave out this address**.

- The line read *Leaves out* and the two names on the saved list at every width, and **Change ›** opened the list with two chips and *Today that is N jobs.* It did not say the list was unsaved.
- With ZZ test jobs hidden, typing *zz* offered no development and no customer.
- **Address**: the button was dead on a short entry and live on a long one. It was not pressed.
- The page never scrolls sideways, open or folded. On the phone every press and the field are 44 px tall.
- The pipe and the tiles drew under the line. No page error at any width.

Not walked: a save, as the owner or as a dev, because it records. The owner's save needs the migration pushed.
