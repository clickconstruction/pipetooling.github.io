# v2.8441 — Punch list: your own tick is cleared after 12 quiet hours (2026-11-24)

Punch list #418's one open decision, answered. No migration.

## Why

v2.8226 made a tick a claim that goes quiet after 12 hours. One reading of Taunya's two asks of 2026-11-21 was left open: once a claim is quiet, does the claimer's own tick stay or go? It was built to stay, marked as gone quiet with **Still on it**, and she was asked on 2026-11-22. Her answer came on 2026-11-24, relayed by Diane: *"Wipe it"*.

## What changed

- **`OWN_TICK_AFTER_QUIET` is `'wiped'`** (`src/lib/todos/punchListClaims.ts`). A person's own quiet tick is no tick: their box is clear and **Working on** does not count it.
- **Their own page deletes the row**, with no press, the next time it draws the board on the built site (`usePunchListTicks`). A live claim is never deleted, and neither is anyone else's row.
- **The card says why, for that visit.** *Your tick was cleared after 12 quiet hours. Tick the card again if you are still on it.* The hook remembers the rows it wiped until the page is left, so the line outlives the row. The next visit says nothing: the row was the only record.
- **A tick puts it back.** Ticking the card again is a new claim, fresh from that moment, and the line goes.
- **Everyone else is unchanged until then.** They read *Free to pick up. ZZ Sam had it and went quiet 2 h ago.* while the row exists. Once its claimer has opened the board the row is gone and the card says nothing.

## Following the one word through

The hand-over said the change was one word. Three things followed from it.

1. **A page left open must not wipe a newer claim.** The page reads the table on opening and when its tab comes back to the front, and its clock moves once a minute. A tab left in view at a desk can hold a row that is 12 hours old while the person ticked the card again on their phone an hour ago. With `'stays'` that was a stale line. With `'wiped'` it would have been a deleted claim. So the delete of a quiet row names the time this page read on it (`ticked_at` no newer than that), and asks for the rows it deleted. When it deleted none, the page reads the table again and the card says what is true.
2. **Still on it has nowhere to stand.** It was offered only on the claimer's own quiet line, and that line no longer exists. No card offers it now. The press, its words and its tests stay for the other reading. Whether a fresh claim should offer it before the 12 hours are up is a new call, named on the card.
3. **Training mode.** The page already held its own deletes on a dev server and under View as. It now holds them in training mode too (`users.read_only`), where the database would refuse the delete anyway. In all three the look shows what the person will see, the cleared line included, and the row stays.

A refused delete is asked once a visit, as before. A row with no time on it is hidden and never deleted.

## A call made here

**The tick does not go silently.** The smallest change was no word at all: a box that was ticked last night is empty this morning. That reads as a tick that failed to save, and her first ask was a tick kept between sessions. One line for one visit is the least that says what happened and why. It needs no table change. Taking it out is one branch of `claimLines` and the hook's `wipedRows`.

## What is unchanged

- The 12 hours, and what counts as an update: the tick and the card file's last change on `main`, as the build read it. A card whose file changed since the last deploy is judged by the old time until the page loads the new build.
- A second person may tick a quiet card, two people may hold one card, and a move to another section ends every claim.
- Who may read and write `punch_list_ticks`.

## Both readings stay tested

- `punchListClaims.test.ts`: the default is `'wiped'`, with the cleared line, the list of rows to delete, and that a fresh tick, another person's row and a card kept fresh by a file change are never in it. The `'stays'` reading is named and held as before.
- `usePunchListTicks.render.test.tsx`: a quiet own tick is deleted and a live one is not; nothing is deleted where the page may not clear; a refused delete is asked once; a claim renewed on another device is left and the page reads again; the clock passing the twelfth hour wipes with no read. The hook takes the reading as an argument so **Still on it** is still driven end to end.
- `PunchList.ticks.render.test.tsx`: the page draws the clear box, the count, the cleared line and no **Still on it**, in both views, and a tick puts the claim back. The quiet line with **Still on it** is drawn by `PunchListClaim` alone.
