# v2.8660 — Agreements: a paper sent to an outside company can name the site it is for (2026-11-26)

Punch list #439, piece 3 of seven, its first client part (3a). Client only: no migration, no edge function. It writes the four `site_` columns of `person_contract_documents` that `20261126070436_paper_names_a_site.sql` made (v2.8642, pushed 2026-11-26).

## What was there

**Send a paper** on Documents → Agreements sends a Contract Book paper to a customer, a supply house, a trade company or someone on no list (punch list #403). The paper's row had nowhere to say which place it was for. A company held each paper once: a second send found the first copy and reused it.

## What changed

- **The site's line.** The email window of an outside party's new paper reads *This paper names no site.* with **Name a site…**. Named, it reads *For the site at …* with **Change the site…** and **Take the site off**.
- **Which site is it for?** The window that opens over the email window finds an address on file, the same list the job form's box reads, each row with its jobs, its bids and its development. **It is not for a site** takes a site off. A site is always an address a job or a bid already has: nothing typed here becomes a new one.
- **One paper a site.** The copy a send reuses is one for the same site. A paper for another site, or for none, is left as it is, and the send writes a row of its own.
- **A copy already out for the site** is offered again in the window from v2.8325, which now says the site. **A signature for the site** stops the send with the reason and its day: *… signed … for this site on Nov 12. One signature covers a site.*
- **Two spellings of one place are one site**, by the answers kept in **Addresses that look alike** (`samePlaceKeys`). If the answers cannot be read, the same spelling is still the same site.
- **Written at Send email**, with the row, and never before. Closing any window leaves nothing behind. Escape closes the site's window alone, so an address and a message typed in the email window stay.

## How it is built

- `src/lib/addresses/paperSite.ts` (pure). `PaperSite` is an address under its cleaned key, or a development. `paperSiteColumns` is what a new row writes: a key with the address as written, or a development alone, and never the print. `rowIsForSite` and `rowsForSite` are the one rule for *the same site*. `paperSiteOfEntry` refuses a key over the row's 300 characters and cuts the address to its 500.
- `PaperSitePicker.tsx`: the window. It reads `readAddresses` and draws `onFileMatches`, eight rows at most. A read that fails draws `OfflineRetryPanel`.
- `AgreementsSendPaper.tsx`: `nameSite` looks at what the company holds for the picked site before it is taken. `preparePartyPaper` picks the copy to reuse from `rowsForSite` and hands `materializeQuickSendRow` the site. The first look for a copy that is out counts only papers that name no site.
- `ContractSendWindow.tsx` takes `siteSlot` and draws it under who the paper is with. `agreementsSendIo.ts` reads `site_development_id` and `site_address_key` with each paper. `QuickSendPersonRow` carries both, optional.

## Design calls

- **In the email window, not a step of its own.** Most papers are for no site. A fourth window on every send would cost each of them a press. The site sits where the office reads the send once more before it goes.
- **An address in a development is kept as the address.** The row's checks let the address as written sit only beside a key, and the print needs it. The lists of the next parts read a development's jobs from the key.
- **Outside companies only.** A teammate's own paper for a site is piece 4's gate across a site's jobs.
- **No print box yet.** Nothing of a site reaches a signer until `get-contract-for-signer` is changed. The box ships with that change, so no box is ticked that prints nothing.
- **A signature stops the send, with no way round.** The card's settled call 10 is one signature per site. Open to a flip if a second signer at the same company must sign for the same site.
- **A copy sent again keeps its own site.** The line is not drawn on *Send it to … again*.
