# v2.6722 — The seat reads a matter's text document a section at a time (punch list #288, PR 3) (2026-11-08)

Punch list #288 (`to-dos/matter-documents-counsel-only.md`), PR 3 of three, stacked on v2.6720. PR 1 gave a matter a text document and the search a section on its hits; the seat could still read the words only whole, through `read_rows`. Migration `20261108233000_matter_document_read_by_section.sql` ([`docs/migrations/`](../migrations/20261108233000_matter_document_read_by_section.md)); dev-mcp 0.44.0, deployed after the push and after the catalog is regenerated from v2.6716's.

## What changed

- **`get_matter_document(matter_id, doc_id, section?)`** on dev-mcp (`_shared/devMcpMatterDocRead.ts`): `doc_id` is the Evidence row; the reply is the section index or one section with its neighbours, and `read_next` names the next section, the paper itself and where the words are changed (the Evidence tab; the seat has no section write on a matter document yet).
- **`dev_matter_doc_read`**: devs only; the Evidence row on the matter, its words from `documents` through `text_document_id`; a file alone is refused with what it is; a counsel-only paper is refused to the seat (`request_is_dev_seat()`) with the count, and read, marked, by a dev signed in.
- **`hr_doc_slice(text, section?, who?)`**: `dev_doc_read`'s slice lifted whole, so the HR file's documents and the matter's read by one grammar; `dev_doc_read` and `dev_doc_history` move off the `person_documents` view onto `documents`. `dev_person_doc_write` stays on the view: #286 and #287 replace it this week, the later stamp carrying both.
- **The seat's words**: `search_case`'s `matter_document` hit with a section points at `get_matter_document`; `get_case_brief`'s notes read and `read_next`, `get_person_file`'s documents note and `search_case`'s `document` hit name `documents`.
- **Docs**: `docs/dev-mcp/README.md`, `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` → dev-mcp, the migration page, the card.
