# v2.6027 — Retainage held: a release paid by a recorded payment clears the line (2026-10-28)

Punch list #165's verify walk (the second half, in [v2.6021's fragment](./v2.6021.md)) found the gap: the release bill on ZZ job 1064 was recorded as sent and then paid by a check typed into the Bill tab, the Bill tab read it *Paid $50.00*, and the Retainage held line still read *release billed · awaiting payment*. A payment recorded against a bill leaves `jobs_ledger_invoices.status` at `billed`; the app reads a bill paid by the money on it (`invoiceLedgerState`, the Bill tab's rule), and the kernel read only the status.

## What changed

- **`lib/ar/retainageHeld.ts`**: `RetainageBill` gains `paid` — what the job's payments cover on the bill — and `releaseState` reads the bill through `invoiceLedgerState(status, amount, paid)`: a draft is `drafted`, an open bill `billed`, a bill whose payments cover it `paid`, whatever its status column says. A draft stays a draft whatever was paid on it.
- **`lib/ar/retainageHeldRows.ts`**: `assembleRetainageHeld` takes the job's payments (`RetainagePaymentRow`: `invoice_id`, `amount`, `paid_on`) and folds each bill's `paid` with the shared `attributeJobPayments` — linked money in full, unlinked money first to the part of the job on no sent bill (the job's `revenue`), then oldest bill first — the rule the Bill tab and the AIA history's paid line use.
- **`lib/ar/retainageHeldIo.ts`**: `readPayments` (`jobs_ledger_payments`, in chunks of 200) joins the reads for AR's band and for one job; `JOB_COLS` adds `revenue`. The hooks pass the payments through.
- **Tests**: `retainageHeld.test.ts` (+2: a billed release bill covered by its payments reads paid and clears, a part payment stays billed; the final application's bill the same, a draft never), `retainageHeldRows.test.ts` (+2: the fold of linked and unlinked money, and the off-bill share of an unlinked payment).
