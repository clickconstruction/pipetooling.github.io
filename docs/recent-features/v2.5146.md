# v2.5146 — GC mode: send the customer the Friday report, for a dev (2026-10-10)

U7c of the Building lane's real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/building-u7.md`, on branch `spike/gc-mode`): the weekly report window, ported from the prototype's `GcBuildingWeekly.tsx`, over U7a's `weeklyReport` and U7b's `weekly` kind on `gc-customer-email`. A dev's while Building is built. No migration.

- **The card** on the Daily log window (`GcWeeklyReportCard` in `src/components/gc/GcWeeklyReport.tsx`): this week's report, ready from Friday once the week has a log, or sent, or a draft to look at early.
- **The window** (`GcWeeklyReportWindow`): what goes in, *Name the companies*, from me or Click Construction, short or full, a line of my own, who it goes to and the architect to copy, the text to edit, and the report as the customer reads it.
  - **From me**: the link opens the sender's own mail filled in, and the report is kept as from me.
  - **From Click Construction**: the report is kept as a `gc_weekly_reports` row, then sent through `gc-customer-email` kind `weekly`. A refused or failed send goes again on the same row (`weeklyReportRetryRow`), so one email never leaves two rows.
  - **Email me a test**: the same, with `test`, to the sender only.
  - Escape closes the report before the Daily log window.
- **The read** (`src/lib/gc/weeklyReportRows.ts`, `weeklyReportsIo.ts`): the job's reports, a company send counting only once its email went (`weeklyReportWent`), laid over the board (`withWeeklyReports`). The page also reads the job's submittals and schedule, and for the money team its change orders and the customer's bills, so the late finish reads as on Bill the customer.
- **The email's answer** (`customerEmail.ts`) keeps `copied` and `test`, and the window says a refusal in its own words (`weeklyReportRefusal`).
- **Docs**: the guide *send the customer the Friday report* (dev), which the Friday report's journey step now names; `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`, `GLOSSARY.md` (*Friday report*), `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` (the gate, and the row deciding who may send, with the door's open question).
- **Tests**: `GcWeeklyReport.render.test.tsx` (9), `weeklyReportRows.test.ts` (5), the email answer's case, and a page case.
- **Not done**: the reports in the customer's portal (the schedule's PR 15).
