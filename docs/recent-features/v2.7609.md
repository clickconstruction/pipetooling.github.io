# v2.7609 — Sub portal: Got it on a payment, and Something is wrong on a statement or payment (2026-11-17)

Punch list #371, step 3 of four: the two presses, the record, and the office's chip and flag. It stands on step 2's Statements view (v2.7597). It carries a small migration and a change to `sub-portal` that is deployed only on a go that names it.

## What changed

### On the sub's page

- **Got it** sits on a payment that is not marked. One tap opens one question under the row: *Did this payment reach you?*, the amount, how it was sent and the day, and what a yes means. **Yes, I got it** marks it received. **Not yet** sends nothing. The row then reads *Received* with the day, and the payment record's own Received box is filled.
- **Something is wrong** sits under every work statement and every payment. It opens a note form about that row, with the office's phone beside it. The press is dead until there are words. After it goes, the row says it was sent and that nothing on the statement changes.
- **A note the sub sent before** reads on its row with its day.
- **The answer goes to the page's own function** as a POST with the link in the body (`answerFromPage` in `SubPortal.tsx`). The sample and demo pages send nothing. A refusal reads in the page's language. Two of the function's refusals have words of the page's own: the office looking at a sub's page, and a note past ten in a day.
- **Both languages.** 20 new strings, and the guide sheet's entry says what the two presses do.

### In the office

- **A grey chip on the payment's line** on Jobs → Subs → Pay: *Received Nov 2 · from their page*. A payment nobody marked says nothing. The mock-up drew *Not marked received* on those lines too. It was left off: most payments predate the page, and a chip on every one of them would say nothing.
- **A red flag** on the sub's group (*⚑ 1 note from their page*) and on the sheet (*⚑ Something is wrong*). The opened sheet reads the note under *From the sub's page*. A note about a payment shows on each sheet that payment paid on.
- **Mark answered** puts the flag down. The note stays on the sheet with who answered it and when. The card did not draw this press. Without it a flag could never be cleared.
- **Kernel and hook:** `src/lib/subWorkOrders/subPaperAnswers.ts` and `src/hooks/useSubPaperAnswers.ts`. Only the office roles who see sub pay can read the table, so anyone else gets no chip and no flag.

### The function

- **A note tells the office** (the owner's call 4). After a `something_wrong` answer is saved, `sub-portal` drops a line in the dispatch inbox and calls the push, as `submit-sub-portal` does for a sub's other notes. `got_it` tells nobody. A note that cannot be dropped is logged, and the answer is still kept.

### The migration

- `20261117190000_sub_paper_ack_answered.sql`: `answered_at`, `answered_by` and `answered_by_name` on `sub_paper_acks`, and `answer_sub_paper_ack(p_ack_id)`, a definer function for the office roles. A note is answered once.

## Deploy

1. Merge. The chip and the flag read rows the live function already writes.
2. `supabase db push` for the one migration, right after the merge. Before it, the notes show with no **Mark answered** press.
3. `supabase functions deploy sub-portal`, only on a go that names it. Until then a note is kept and flagged on the Pay view, and nothing lands in the dispatch inbox.
