# v2.7965 — My Inbox: Forward on an overdue task is the office's, as it is on a task due today (2026-11-19)

Client only: no migration and no function. Found while writing the card's Forward render test (#8937).

## The gap

`docs/ACCESS_CONTROL.md` has held **My inbox → Forward** as office work since v2.6758 (`isOfficeRole`, a dev's alone before). The My Inbox card has two Forward presses. Due Today's **fwd** read the rule. Overdue's arrow read nothing: it was drawn for every role, and had been since before the card was cut out of the Dashboard, through the time Forward was a dev's alone.

Since v2.7563 the press calls `task_hand_on`, which lets a person hand on a task of their own. So Overdue's arrow worked for a field role. It was the doer's **Hand it on** without its two courtesies: no reason could be given, and whoever gave the task was not told (`tellForwardTaker` tells the taker alone).

## The fix

Overdue's `actions` in `DashboardMyInboxCard` are drawn only when `canForward` is true, the value Due Today's **fwd** and Recently completed already read. The card draws on the Dashboard, in Job Mode, in Dispatch Mode and in Quickfill, so the rule holds in all four.

The database is unchanged. `task_hand_on` still lets a person hand on their own task: that is what **Hand it on** calls, from **⋯ More** on the task in the Checklist.

## The guide

`dashboard-my-inbox.md`: the step for a task you cannot do names both presses as the office's, and sends everyone else to **Hand it on** in *check off my daily tasks*. Two older sentences that spoke of an arrow on Due Today and of forward buttons for every reader are put right.

## Tests

`DashboardMyInboxCard.forward.render.test.tsx` gains a fourth case: a helper with one task due today and one overdue sees both tasks and no Forward on either. With the gate taken off locally the case fails; the card was then restored. The three cases of #8937 still pass, Overdue's hand-on among them, as an office role.

## Not checked

- Whether field people used Overdue's arrow in production. The old copy-and-delete left no line, and since v2.7563 a `handed` line with no reason reads the same from Forward and from Hand it on.
- The card was not walked on the live site as a field role.
