# v2.7856 — Lien desk: Back from the clerk records every filed lien in one window (2026-11-18)

The owner's ask of 2026-11-18: *"she would also like a place one click away from the Lien to do page to add the document number the recorders office gave her back"*. He picked option B of the look, its own short window, and said how it saves: each lien is recorded as its number is typed.

**What the look found.** A filed lien was recorded in one place, the job's Lien window: the chip in *Ready to file*, **Open the affidavit tab ›**, **Record filing…**, the number, **Record filing**. Four presses a lien and two windows deep. Of eleven liens filed on one day, ten were typed by the dev seat and one by an assistant the long way.

**The door.** *Back from the clerk? · Enter document numbers · 3 ›*, a line under the print on the Board's *Ready to file* bin (`LienBoardBinPress.under`, new: lines a press adds under a print that takes its bin's title line). With no print on the bin, the door is the bin's own press. The copies window has the same door at its top while liens wait. Both are drawn for the office alone.

**The window (`LienFilingBatchWindow.tsx`, `src/lib/jobs/lienFilingBatch.ts`).** Every lien standing in *Ready to file* (`lienFilingTargets`), the soonest day first, one line each: the job, its county clerk, its file-by day and a box. **Filed on** is one day for every lien recorded in the sitting.

- **Typed.** Enter, or leaving the box, on a number that passes its check records that lien at once. Enter then moves to the next lien. A number another lien holds, on record or on another line of the stack, is held (`lienFilingCheck` over `lienDocCheck`). A number that is not the county's shape is asked about, and **Record it anyway** records it.
- **From the clerk's files.** **Choose the stamped liens** takes several files. Each goes to the one lien whose job number is in its name (`lienFilingFilesPlan`), and a long number in the name is typed in (`lienDocNumberFromName`). A number that came from a name waits for a look at the stamp: Enter on it records it, and the counted press **Record N filings** records every one that waits, keeping going past one that fails. A file that names no lien, two liens, or a lien that has a file is left out with its reason.
- **After.** A recorded line turns green with the day and the mail-by day, and keeps **Open its Lien window ›**, where a filing recorded by mistake is voided. **Print the copies to mail ›** opens the copies window.

**The write (`lienFilingBatchIo.ts`).** A read that no live filed affidavit is on the job, then the Lien window's own row (`lienFilingPayload`: kind `affidavit`, the sworn amount, the paper as the desk draws it through `lienDeskAffidavitFieldsFor`, the county, the number, the day and `serveDueForFiling`), as the person signed in. Then the desk item is marked (`syncLienDeskAfterRecord`), and the stamped file, when one was chosen, is saved as the filing's saved copy (`saveLienScans`). A file that did not save is said on the line, with the lien still on record. No database change and no new policy.

**A lien the desk cannot draw** has no box. Its line sends it to its own Lien window.
