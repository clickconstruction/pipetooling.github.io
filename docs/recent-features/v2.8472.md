# v2.8472 — Lien desk: a held affidavit row opens on the gate that holds it (2026-11-24)

The one piece punch list #364 left after the gate marks (v2.7335). Diane said on 2026-11-24 to build it. No migration, no edge function.

## What changed

A press on a Lien affidavits list row whose gates hold now opens the pane with the first open gate on the screen. The gate's row is lit for a moment, and the keyboard is on it, so the next press opens the record that clears it. A row with every gate clear opens as it did.

- **The rule** is `src/lib/jobs/lienGateLanding.ts`, pure. `lienGateLanding(rows)` picks the first gate not clear, in the order the pane lists them, which is the order of the row's marks. `lienGateLandingScrollTop(view)` answers how far the pane scrolls.
- **The wiring** is `src/components/jobs/useLienGateLanding.ts`. The list row's press says it is a row (`onPick(jobId, 'row')`), the desk keeps one ask per press (`useLienGateAsk`), and the pane answers it once (`useLienGateLanding`).
- **The light** is the app's one landing light, `[data-landed]`. `src/lib/landOn.ts` now gives it alone as `lightLanded`, for a place its own scroller already brought into view.

## Where the code had moved since the card was written

The card named `scrollPaneTo` in `LienDesk.tsx` and the row's press there. Both had moved.

- The list is `LienDeskAffidavitList.tsx` now, and its row calls `onPick`.
- The affidavit pane is its own scrolling box with its own pinned heads (v2.7320). `scrollPaneTo` reads the notice pane's box, and a plain `scrollIntoView` lands a row under the stacked heads and can move the page around the desk.
- So the pane does its own landing, with the heads' own numbers (`lienPaneHeadOffsets`, `lienPaneSectionScrollTop`).

## The calls, each with the default taken

1. **Which gate is first.** The pane's own order, `entry.gates`: owner, legal description, the GC's address, the § 53.056 notice, homestead. The marks are drawn in that order, and a test holds the two together.
2. **Which rows.** Any row with a gate not clear, in any pile. The marks are drawn in the early piles only, but a later row with an open gate still reads *missing …*, so its press lands too.
3. **What in view means.** A gate already clear of the pinned heads moves nothing. Otherwise the gates section lands under its own head, as a press on the head lands it, so the gate reads with its neighbours. A section taller than the pane lands the row itself.
4. **Focus.** The keyboard goes to the gate's row, on a phone and on a computer, as the card asked. A row with no door is a disabled button, so it is brought into view and lit only. A mouse press shows no focus ring. A keyboard press does.
5. **The mark.** The amber landing light, a second and a half and then a fade. It is the one the Serve window and the property window already use.
6. **Less motion.** Under `prefers-reduced-motion` the pane jumps with no glide, and the light stands still for a second and a half.
7. **What is not changed.** A single mark is not a press, since the row is the button. A pane opened any other way lands nowhere: the Board, By job, a link with a job in it, the desk's first row at open, a job named in a batch's failure line.

## The pane settles after it opens

A landing measured at mount came up short by that much. While the light holds, a `ResizeObserver` on the pane's sections places the row again.
