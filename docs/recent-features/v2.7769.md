# v2.7769 — Trips: a job billed from the GC desk sends an Extra trip to a change order (2026-11-18)

## Why

Punch list #391, after its four PRs. The card's default 7 asked what happens on a job whose total is not its lines. PR 2 (v2.7756) built the card's own fallback, a refusal in the function, while the question was with the owner. His answer came on 2026-11-18: *refuse it*. The reason he agreed to: on a project we build as the GC the customer's price is the contract, and it grows only by a signed change order. An Extra trip there is in substance a change order, so its amount needs somewhere to go.

PR 3 (v2.7763) showed the function's refusal in the window only after the job was picked and an amount typed, in the function's words, with no next press. This change puts the reason and the door where the job is chosen.

## What changed

- **The window no longer offers a GC billing job.** `TripBillLineWindow` reads which of its jobs are a GC project's billing job (`readTripBillGcJobs`: `gc_projects` by `billing_job_id`, one small read when the window opens). `tripBillJobChoices` leaves those out of the pick and hands them back as `held`, each with its project and its reason (`tripBillHeldWords`).
  - A trip on two jobs, one of them the GC's: the other is picked and the line goes there as before.
  - A trip whose only job is the GC's: nothing can be picked, the button is dead, and the foot says *No job here can take a trip's line.*
- **The reason and the next press**, under Job: *J2140 is billed from Owner Billing on the GC desk. Its price grows only by a signed change order. Put this trip's amount on a change order there.*
- **The door.** **Open Change orders** closes the window and opens that project's Change orders on the GC desk. Nothing is added.
  - A project's change orders already open by an address, `/gc?changes=<project id>`: the project card's own **Change orders** door sets the same parameter. `changeOrdersHref` and `changeOrdersFromSearch` in `src/lib/gc/changeOrdersLink.ts` now name it, and `GcProjects.tsx` reads it through the helper. They sit in their own file because `links.ts` is copied into the edge functions' kernels, and this change must not owe a redeploy.
  - Change orders are the GC money team's (dev, master_technician, controller: `canSeeGcMoney`). An assistant may press Put on a bill but cannot open them, so an assistant is told *A leader or the controller writes the change order in Change orders on the GC desk.*
- **No migration.** The function's refusal from PR 2 stays the backstop, in one place, with its two assertions in the trips bed. When the read of `gc_projects` fails, every job is offered and that refusal stops the press, as before.
- The guide gains three lines under *Put a trip on a bill*. `docs/GLOSSARY.md` and `docs/ACCESS_CONTROL.md` name the door and who gets it. The card records the owner's answer.

## A finding about the discount press, not fixed here

`apply_job_discount` (Bill Customer, **Add discount**) has no case for a GC billing job. On that job it rewrites `jobs_ledger.revenue` from the lines plus the riders, where `gc_owner_billing_revenue` keeps it at the contract, the interest billed and the riders. A discount added to a GC billing job would move its total off the contract figure until the next Owner Billing write lays it again. That is the discount press's own edge. It was found while building this card and is left for a card of its own.
