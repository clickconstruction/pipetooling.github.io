## The change

| Function | Kind | Found by |
|---|---|---|
| `send-estimate-to-customer` | `estimate`, `estimate_resent` | the estimate's job, customer and bid |
| `send-job-contract` | `job_contract` | the job |
| `remind-job-contracts` | `job_contract_reminder` | the job |
| `sign-job-contract` | `job_contract_signed_copy` (with the PDF), `job_contract_next_signer` | the job |

The four that call `sendEmailViaResend` pass `file`; the two with their own Resend call invoke `fileSentEmailBestEffort` after a successful send. A signing email is a link to a page; the page's own content stays where it was (the contract's `body_html` and `fields` by revision, the signed PDF), and the email is what is new. Notices to our own staff are not filed.
