Drawn first, side by side with the live step, in [`to-dos/submittals/their-call-before-after.html`](../../to-dos/submittals/their-call-before-after.html) (added here); the owner: "i like it, build it".

## What it replaced

One dense sentence (*Their call: 4 with a part rejected · by structura · 4 entered by Wendi · 9 still open*), a button phrased as a statement (*They approved the other 9…*) and a folded line called *Thread*. The step said how many came back, not which, in whose words, or what to do. To find the four she scrolled back to step 3 and read fourteen rows.

## What step 6 says now

- **One square a fixture** (`theirCallBoard`): green approved, red sent back, grey waiting, dashed for a fixture with no product yet; each square's tooltip names its fixture. The counts sit under them — *0 approved · 4 sent back · 9 waiting · 1 no product yet* — and one line says who answered: *structura answered. Wendi typed the answers in on Oct 2.* (`whoAnsweredLine`; on the review link it reads *Dana Whitfield answered on the review link, last on Oct 9.*).
- **What came back**: each fixture sent back, the parts the reviewer marked, the answer and their words (*they wrote “TEL145”*). On a draft **Edit** beside it opens the row's window, where the part is marked too (v2.4537). The list ends by pointing at step 7; on a shared version, where parts are locked, it says so instead of offering Edit.
- **Who is still owed an answer**: each waiting fixture is a button that opens *Their answer* for it; **Mark all 9 approved…** is the one-entry door (the window it opens is unchanged). More than fourteen fold behind *and N more*.
- **A fixture with no product** is set apart with *Add the product*: there is nothing on it for the reviewer to answer.
- **Quiet at the foot**: *Messages and history* (the thread, renamed for what it holds), *Drop a reviewer's file*, *Copy their decisions as text*. The reviewer's own files and what the robot read in them are as they were.
- Nothing waiting and everything approved reads *All 13 approved by structura on Oct 9. Nothing is left to do here. Order them in step 8.*

## 9 against 10

The step said *9 still open* while the Next line at the top said *10 rows still have no answer*: the tenth is the sink with no product. `summarizeDecisions` now counts `noProduct`, and the Next line says it apart — *9 rows still have no answer. 1 row has no product yet.* All ten still go on the next version's draft.

## Where the build differs from the drawing

- The foot keeps the names the guide already uses — *Drop a reviewer's file*, *Copy their decisions as text* — where the drawing renamed them.
- *Messages and history*, not *History*: the thread also holds the reviewers' questions and the reply box.
