# v2.4733 — Lien desk: the notice pane keeps its place as you scroll (2026-10-06)

**The ask (Taunya, 2026-10-06, a screenshot of 891's pane):** "as I scroll through this section of the page, I can get lost in where I am, to the left of this in that column we do a good job showing the user what is above them and below them. could I build the same here?" Three lenses, mock-up https://claude.ai/artifact/T9jiFQXSGQAwu4huutisSk (the pane at three depths), the *is this the best we can do?* pass (the heads are the titles, not a second set), "build it".

**The gap.** Every card in the pane had a title and every title scrolled away with its card; the pinned strip repeated the verdict and the months but said nothing about *where* anything was. The Notices list already answered the question with stacked pile heads (v2.4651).

## What shipped

- **The kernel** ([`lienPaneSections.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienPaneSections.ts)): `lienPaneSections` — the five heads in pane order with the fact a glance wants (*Where this notice is · Draft the Jul + Aug notice — 9 days*, *The four gates · ✓ Ready to go out · All 4 clear*, *Supply houses · 1 house owed · $6,258 · from our books · Oct 6* (only when the job bought from a house), *Months on this job · Jul + Aug · $27,199*, *In the envelope · 3 pages · owner + GC · courtesy PDF to the GC*); `lienPaneHeadOffsets` (the list's `top: i·30`, `bottom: (n−1−i)·30`, with the pinned strip's height on top); `lienPaneSectionScrollTop`; `litLienPaneSection` (the list's relight rule).
- **The head** ([`LienPaneHead`](../../src/components/jobs/LienPaneHead.tsx)): the list's `.lienPileHead` reused, full width of the pane, the fact in words at the right with its muted tail cut first, the blue bar on the lit one, a button that scrolls its section up under it.
- **The pane** ([`LienDeskModal`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskModal.tsx), region E): each section is a `data-lien-pane-body` wrapper with its head as a sibling before it (sticky stacking needs the pane itself as the scrolling box); `relightPane` on scroll; `goToSection` on press; `stripRef` + a `ResizeObserver` measure the pinned strip so the heads stack beneath it.
- **The cards gave up their titles**: `LienDeskGates`, `LienJobSuppliersCard` and `LienDeskMonths` take `headless` — the gates' verdict row, the houses' *Supply houses on this job · from our books · Oct 6* line and the months' bold title are now the heads' words (the months card keeps its helper sentence; a folded houses card keeps its row, since the fold lives there).
- Guide [`send-lien-notices-from-the-lien-desk`](../../src/content/help/send-lien-notices-from-the-lien-desk.md) (*Reading the notice before it goes*), GLOSSARY, the Lien desk map.

## Decisions

- The heads are the titles, not a sticky echo of them — nothing reads twice. Same CSS as the list, so the two columns share one grammar. Under the strip, not instead of it: the strip is the job's line, the heads are the pane's map.
- Rejected: a table of contents at the top (scrolls away; her problem was mid-scroll); a progress rail in the margin (needs a legend, fights the gate numbers and the paper's edges).
- Phones pay what the list pays: five rows at most when every section is passed. The Affidavit and Retainage panes can take the wrapper later.
