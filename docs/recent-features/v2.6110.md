# v2.6110 — Other job charges read as money: commas, smaller cents, an Estimate chip (2026-10-29)

Punch list #215, PR 1 of 3 (`to-dos/other-job-charges-date-memo/`). No migration. The owner's ask: the Other job charges rows on the Costs tab showed the amount as it was typed (`39796.7`), with no thousands and a cent missing, and the `ESTIMATE —` descriptions written by cost batches ran off the end of their box.

## What changed

- **`OtherChargeAmountInput`** ([`src/components/jobs/OtherChargeInputs.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/OtherChargeInputs.tsx)) replaces the `type="number"` box on the Costs tab's Other job charges row. Blurred, it reads as money — `39,796` with `.70` drawn at 0.78em — through an overlay over one real `<input>`, so the box keeps its label (`Other charge amount`), its tab stop and its click target. Focused, it is the plain digits (`39796.70`) with the caret; commas are allowed while typing and stripped. It commits on every keystroke, as the number box did, so the accordion total and the autosave follow the typing.
- **`EstimateChip`** (same file): a description that starts with `ESTIMATE` in any case (the cost-batch contract in `docs/COST_BATCHES.md`, read the way the function reads it) wears an amber *Estimate* chip and its box shows the words after the prefix. While the box has focus the whole description, prefix included, is editable; the prefix stays in the data and nothing that reads the text changes.
- **`MoneyWithSmallCents` / `MoneyStringWithSmallCents`** ([`src/components/jobs/MoneySmallCents.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/MoneySmallCents.tsx)): the cents drawn smaller for a number or for an already-formatted total. `MaterialsCostAccordionRow` draws every cost accordion's total through it (Team labor, Supply house invoices, Card charges, Parts from tally, Other job charges, on both the Costs tab and the Job tab); anything that is not money (`—`, `…`) passes through. The Job tab's read-only Other job charges list uses the chip and the small cents too.
- **Kernel** [`src/lib/jobs/otherChargeRowDisplay.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/otherChargeRowDisplay.ts): `splitMoneyForDisplay`, `splitMoneyString`, `splitEstimateDescription`, `moneyDraftForEditing`; unit tests beside it. Render tests in `JobFormPartsCostSection.render.test.tsx` cover blur/focus, the keystroke commit and the chip.

## Not in this PR

The date and the memo line the to-do draws (`charged_on`, `memo` on `jobs_ledger_materials`, the two-line row, the Costs packet, the batch function's optional fields) are PR 2; the materials save keeping a row's id instead of deleting and re-inserting every row is PR 3.

## Verify

Any job → Costs → Other job charges: type `39796.7` in an amount and tab out — it reads `39,796` with `.70` smaller; click back in — `39796.70` with the caret; the accordion header's total has small cents. A row whose description starts with `ESTIMATE —` shows the chip and the text after the dash; click into the box and the whole description is there. Job tab → Costs card → Other job charges: the same chip and cents. `npm test -- otherChargeRowDisplay JobFormPartsCostSection`.
