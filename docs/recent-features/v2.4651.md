# v2.4651 — Records for an owner: send it on their portal, and they download it (2026-10-06)

Client only plus a changed edge function (`customer-portal`); no migration. PRs 2 and 4 of punch list #86 ([`to-dos/owner-records-portal/`](../../to-dos/owner-records-portal/README.md)), shape B, stacked on v2.4650. With this the shape is complete.

## What changed

- **A fifth way to send.** [`ownerRecords.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/ownerRecords.ts): `OwnerRecordsSentHow` gains `portal` (*On their portal*); `ownerRecordsSentHows(file)` lists it only once the records were offered on the portal. The window's **Record it as sent** with that way files the packet as a **PDF** (`ownerPacketPdfBlob`), not the printed HTML, with `how: 'link'` on the sent copy.
- **The owner's Download.** `customer-portal` now sends the latest offered request whether sent or not; one sent *On their portal* carries `sent { on, downloadUrl }`, the PDF copy from `sent_documents` as a signed URL good for an hour. [`PortalOwnerRecordsCard.tsx`](../../src/components/portal/PortalOwnerRecordsCard.tsx) then reads *The office sent your records on <day>* with **Download the records**; while the copy is still being kept it says to come back in a minute. A request sent any other way shows nothing on the portal: the owner has the paper.
