# v2.6512 — The Settings search's rows read on a narrow pane (2026-11-04)

Grace, 2026-11-04, with a screenshot of the Settings search at about 520 px: typing *merge* listed *Merge users* with *users* wrapped under *Merge*, and *How fast a change ships* broken one word a line, its trail *in How fast a change ships →* drawn over the first word and *(merge)* under the stack. "In the settings search this does not look very good."

**Cause (one sentence):** the row was `display: flex; justify-content: space-between` with the trail `white-space: nowrap; flex-shrink: 0` and the title a default flex child (`flex: 0 1 auto`), so once the title's full width plus the trail's did not fit, the title shrank to its longest word and stacked, and a trail wider than what was left ran back over it.

## What changed

- **`SettingsSearchBar.tsx`**: the row is `flex-wrap: wrap` with a column gap; the title is `flex: 1 1 auto; min-width: 0` with no `width`, so it grows to the room it needs, wraps as a sentence when the row is narrower than the sentence, and pushes the trail to the row's right end when both fit; the trail is `flex: 0 0 auto; white-space: nowrap`, so it either fits after the title or wraps whole to its own line under it, never over it; a keyword hit's word is its own last line (`flex-basis: 100%`). The three parts carry `data-settings-search-title` / `-trail` / `-keyword` for the tests. The keyboard path (↓ ↑ Enter Escape, `aria-activedescendant`) and the lit row are as they were.
- **`settingsSearchTrail`** (`src/lib/settingsSearch.ts`): a door that is a whole tab (its label is the tab's own name, no anchor and no guide: *How fast a change ships*, *Training*, *Flows*, *Dev doors*, *Release notes*, *What customers see* and its two siblings) reads **a tab →** instead of repeating its title; a card or a guide inside a tab still reads **in <tab> →**. Four unit cases.
- **Tests**: `SettingsSearchBar.render.test.tsx` (the two *merge* rows and their trails, the keyword last, the row's and the parts' flex shape, ↓ + Enter, a press); `e2e/settings-tabs.spec.ts` gains *the finder's rows read at phone width*, which at 375, 520 and 1,280 types *merge* and *e*, counts each title's lines from its client rects and checks the trail's box never meets the title's, never runs past the row, and the keyword is the last line (run today against the dev server; the e2e suite is not a PR gate).
