# v2.8264 — Document builder: check a trade's certificate against a job's sheet (2026-11-21)

Punch list #404, step 6, the screens. The database half is v2.8253 (migration `20261122021800`), pushed before this merged. Taunya's call of 2026-11-21: one certificate for each job. Only a dev sees the tab. No function changed and nothing takes a deploy.

## What a dev can do

On Documents → Document builder → Insurance requirements for subs, with a job picked, the card **Certificates for this job** sits under the send:

- **One row a trade** on the job, saying where its certificate stands for this job: *No certificate yet*, *Certificate came in Nov 18*, *Certificate good to Mar 3*, *The sheet changed since their certificate*, *Their certificate ran out Mar 3*, or *Sent back Nov 20, 2 things missing* with the lines listed.
- **Check it against the sheet** on a certificate that waits for a look, and **Check it again** on one the sheet moved past. The check has a link to their file, one line to tick for each thing the published sheet asks, and **Good until**.
- **Mark it good** once every line is ticked and the day has not passed.
- **Send it back, naming N things missing** with at least one line ticked and one left, so nothing goes back unread. The trade is emailed the unticked lines.

## The rules

`src/lib/documentBuilder/certificates.ts`, pure:

- `certificateStanding` reads a company's certificates for one job. Two kinds count: one checked for that job, and one waiting for a look with no job on it yet. A new upload comes ahead of an older good one.
- A certificate is **outdated** when `asksOutdated` finds the published sheet no longer reads the lines it was checked against: a name, the place, the holder or an amount. The sheet's other words never outdate one. This is the card's own line to verify, and a test holds it.
- A certificate sent back reads as sent back until the company sends a newer file over it.
- `certificateCheck` says what the two presses may do, and words them with their counts.

`certificatesIo.ts` reads `person_contract_documents` and calls the two functions. A send-back is the record first, then the email (`gc-trade-email`, kind `coi`). An email that does not go leaves the record, and the card says the email did not go and why.

## Left

- **The trade's own page** does not say its certificate was sent back or that the requirements changed. That is `gc-trade-portal`.
- **A trade's upload** still files one waiting certificate for a company, with no job on it. The check gives it its job. A trade on two jobs sends a second certificate for the second.
- **GC mode's start gate** still reads the company's own certificate. A certificate marked good here is filed for the company too, so the gate reads it.
- **A certificate the office filed by hand** for a company, with no job on it, is not offered for the check.
