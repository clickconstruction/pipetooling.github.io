# v2.7238 — Lien desk on a phone: an open notice or affidavit has the screen (2026-11-13)

Punch list #352, PR 6 of eight, on the owner's pick of direction A, the one list. Cut from main; it needs neither PR 4 nor PR 5 and merges cleanly with both.

## What was wrong

Measured at 390 × 720 on the dev server against live data, an open notice on a phone:

- **The header stayed whole over the paper**: the way back, the title, and both rows of tabs, 173 px.
- **Five section titles stayed pinned** in the pane, 150 px, cut off at the right edge.
- **The footer took 130 to 357 px.** On a sent notice the paper showed through 58 px of a 579 px panel. At 320 × 520 it showed through 22.
- **The pane slid sideways**: 573 to 583 px of content in 374. Its one grid column sized to the widest thing in it, a page of the paper.
- **An open affidavit had no way back to its list.** Neither did an open retainage notice. The only way out was another tab.

## The change

- **A one-row header.** `src/lib/jobs/lienPhonePane.ts` (new kernel), `lienPhonePaneOpen`, says which paper is the whole desk. While one is, the title bar is marked `data-lien-pane-open`, the stylesheet hides both rows of tabs, and the page's door gives its place to the way back to that paper's list: *← Notices* as before, and now *← Affidavits* and *← Retainage*, 44 px tall. The notice pane's own *Back to the list* button, a second way back for a notice only, is gone.
- **One pinned title.** `LienPaneHead` takes `phone`; `lienPaneHeadOffsets`, `lienPaneSectionScrollTop` and `litLienPaneSection` in `lienPaneSections.ts` pin one 40 px title under the strip, the section being read, where a computer stacks all five at both ends.
- **No sideways slide.** A phone's pane holds its column to its own width, and the stylesheet does the same for each section's body and for the affidavit's and the retainage notice's panes. A page of the paper, which keeps its printed layout, slides inside its own frame when it is wider than the phone; so does the months grid.
- **A footer that folds.** A 40 px bar over the footer reads *Hide the next step*; pressed, the footer is hidden and the bar reads *Show the next step*. The footer stays mounted, so what was typed in it is kept. A footer that would leave the paper under 140 px starts folded (`lienPhoneFootStartsFolded`), which today is a sent notice. Every press in the footer is at least 40 px tall. The leader's one-screen approval (v2.4881) has no bar: its footer is the screen.

No migration, no edge function, no change to what a paper says, what any press writes or who may press it.
