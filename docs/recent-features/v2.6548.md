# v2.6548 — A matter’s Record what happened and its document form carry the theme strip, and the marks read on its Timeline and Evidence (2026-11-05)

Punch list #269, PR T2b. The HR file's composers took the theme picker in v2.6546; a matter's did not, and the analysis for counsel is written on the matter. No migration.

## What changed

- `LegalEventDraft.marks` (`MarkDraft`, optional): the composer in `LegalTimelinePanel` draws `ThemeMarkPicker` on an event when `acts.themes` is given, and hands the draft's marks to `addEvent`; the Legal desk's `eventAdd` reads the entry id from `legal_event_add` (through `legalRpcData`) and writes the marks after the row with `addMarks`, naming them in the toast.
- `LegalMatterDocumentsPanel`: a pending paper carries `marks`; the picker sits under its row when `themes` is given, and `save` writes the marks after `addLegalMatterDocument` answers with the id (a refusal says the paper is on the matter and the themes are not).
- The chips: `themeChips` on a Timeline row (an event, a step or a document) and a row of chips under a document's title on Evidence, read-only, the why and the grade on the tip, a proposed mark dashed.
- `LegalDesk` loads the catalog and the matter's marks (`loadThemes({ matterId })`) for the mark-ready hand (a dev) and passes them through the acts and the packet tab's `DocumentsPanel`; the office reads none, so its composers draw no picker.
- `legalThemesIo.authUserIdNow` reads the signed-in user's id for `marked_by` where no hook has it.
