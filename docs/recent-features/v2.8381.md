# v2.8381 — People → Contracts: a sub sits under their company (2026-11-23)

Punch list #403, step 6, PR 1 of 3: the company rows and entering a company. The owner approved the screens on 2026-11-23 and said to build on the defaults. Client only. It calls the ground v2.8313 made (`20261123020000_sub_company_ground.sql`): `person_trade_companies`, `person_trade_company_set` and `person_trade_company_enter`. No migration and no edge function.

## What was there

A sub was a person and nothing more. A business's agreement and W-9 hung on one worker, a second worker of the same business showed none, and the company's name was nowhere. The ground to link a person to a trade company had been live since v2.8313, and no screen read or wrote it.

## What changed

- **A company row.** On People → Contracts a sub with a company sits under a row for the company: its name, the tag *Trade company*, and *Workers Pat Sample, Owner · Lee Sample*, each name a value. The company takes its first worker's place in the list. A worker with a title leads (`rosterByCompany`, `src/lib/people/subCompanies.ts`). Staff, and a sub with no company, draw as before.
- **Their company, a value in place.** On a sub's opened row, *Their company* is a `ChangeableValue`. The box offers the trade companies we have as it is typed, takes a new name, and has **Same as their name**, which keeps the sub's own name in one press. Emptying it takes them off. The line under the box says what Keep will do: *A trade company we have*, *A new trade company. Its trade is Plumbing.*, or *Keep takes them off the company.* A name we have, in any case, links that company through `person_trade_company_set`. A new name goes through `person_trade_company_enter`, which finds or makes it with the trade Plumbing, since `gc_companies` asks for one trade at least (`companyKeepPlan`). Refusals: *Type the company's name.* and *A company's name is 200 letters at most.*
- **What they are there.** Once linked, a second value keeps the title, *Owner* or the like.
- **The find box** finds a worker by every word of the company's name (`companyNameHolds`).
- **Who.** The links are read and the control drawn for the office's four roles alone (`isOfficeRole`), as the table's policy allows. Anyone else reads no link and sees the tab as before. A name two roster people share finds no one person, so its row says the company cannot be kept on it.
- **`ChangeableValue` gains two props**, additive: `suggestions`, a text field's offers as a `datalist`, and `fills`, presses in the open field that keep a value at once through the same check.

## Defaults for the owner's read

- Papers already filed under a person stay where they are. No data is moved.
- The office's four roles do the pressing.
- An estimator's chip is unchanged.
- A company made from People takes the trade Plumbing.
- The control sits on the sub's opened row, not on the closed row, so the list stays as quiet as before.
