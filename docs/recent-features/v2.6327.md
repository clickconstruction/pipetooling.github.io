# v2.6327 — Honest and fast, PR 5: Messages keeps who was waiting (2026-11-02)

Punch list #183, the last of its five PRs; the card's folder goes with it and the record is here, in the four fragments before it (v2.6009, v2.6031, v2.6059, v2.6223) and in the closed line in `to-dos/README.md`. During a database freeze `/messages` drew the top bar with blank counts over *Loading who is waiting on us…*, and a read that failed printed its problem in the card, so the office's morning stopped there. This PR takes the queue through PR 2's `useKeptRead` (v2.6031): a slow morning, or a lost signal, draws who was waiting as the device last read it under the stamp *Who was waiting at 7:52 AM · reading again…*, with the bar's counts from the same read, muted, and every press that writes on a row held. No migration, no edge function.

## What changed

- **The kept queue** [`src/lib/messages/keptQueue.ts`](../../src/lib/messages/keptQueue.ts) (12 tests): one record under one key per person, `u1:messages:queue`, scope `queue`. What is kept is the queue as drawn, never the loader's tables: the four lanes' rows and the calls as `followUpQueue` built them, the badge's count, the people those rows name (with who has each) and nobody else, the lines they quote with their typed fields alone, and the notices folded under New. The kind pills and Show narrow it at draw time, as they do the live queue, so one kept read serves every pill. The clauses and the nudges are GC projects' and are never kept. `messagesKeptRowIsValid` voids a stored record off the shape; `unpackMessagesKeptQueue` rebuilds the portal question from the portal rows.
- **The day words read against the clock** `keptLanesToday`: a read kept last evening is what a slow morning shows, so a New row's *today at 4:12 PM* reads *yesterday*, a note's *back today* reads *back since Fri Oct 2*, what we owe and a call read late or today by the day they are drawn, as the deck's round re-reads its marks in PR 4. `backRowPill` and `owedLate` move out of their builders in `followUp/queue.ts` so the two share one rule; `callRows` already was one. A portal paper's *2 days* stays as read.
- **The panel** [`FollowUpPanel.tsx`](../../src/components/followUp/FollowUpPanel.tsx): `keptRead` (the page's; GC projects' tab leaves it out and keeps nothing) calls `useKeptRead` with the live queue as one record and `q.loading`, so the write waits for the read's edge. While kept rows show, the lanes, the count the page hears, the people, the lines and the fold are the kept record's, the root carries `data-kept-read`, the stamp (`MESSAGES_KEPT_LEAD` in `keptRead.ts`) sits first, `onKept` tells the page, and Sort all's and Answer all's desks wait for the live read. A failed read over a kept queue keeps it with the stamp's Retry; a failure's reason still shows under it, except a lost signal's, where the stamp and the strip say it. With nothing kept `OfflineRetryPanel` names the failure by its class (`idempotent`), with its own read words. Either way a lost signal reads again by itself on the browser's `online` event.
- **The held presses** [`FollowUpSections.tsx`](../../src/components/followUp/FollowUpSections.tsx) and [`WhoHasItPill.tsx`](../../src/components/followUp/WhoHasItPill.tsx): `held` makes Take it and a hand-off, No reply needed, Remind me Monday, Done with it, Another day, I called, Tomorrow, Skip, Put back, Book a visit, Start an estimate, the plans question, Sort all and Answer all dead with the hover *reading again…*. A row still opens its thread, Reply and Open the thread still open it, Call still dials, and What to say still opens. The pill mutes under `keptPress` but keeps its pointer events (`pointerEvents: 'auto'` over the class's `none`): with them off, a press on Take it fell through to the row and opened the thread.
- **The bar and the page** [`MessagesTopBar.tsx`](../../src/components/messages/MessagesTopBar.tsx), [`MessagesJumpStrip.tsx`](../../src/components/messages/MessagesJumpStrip.tsx), [`Messages.tsx`](../../src/pages/Messages.tsx): `kept` carries `data-kept-read` on the bar and the strip, and Show's counts, each lane's count and the lanes' bands take `keptCount`. The page holds `keptAt` from `onKept`. `queuesFolded` (`queuesFold.ts`) is the phone's fold as a kernel: a kept read never folds, whatever its count, since the stamp must show and *Nothing waiting.* is a claim a kept read cannot make.
- **Waiting on them and Went out** [`WaitingOnThem.tsx`](../../src/components/messages/WaitingOnThem.tsx), [`WentOutToday.tsx`](../../src/components/messages/WentOutToday.tsx): not kept, since each reads its own lines. An unread or failed read is now no count at all: the band and the bar read *…* until a read lands, where a slow morning's band read 0 and a failed read told the bar 0.
- **The read's failure by class** [`useFollowUpQueue.ts`](../../src/hooks/useFollowUpQueue.ts): `readFailure` (`recoveryFailureFromError`, `QUEUE_READ_FAILED_WORDS`) beside `problem`, null once a read lands, never set by a refused press. A lost signal on a read says `QUEUE_READ_OFFLINE_WORDS`, never the write's *nothing was saved*; a press that fails keeps its own sentence. `setAside` hands out one empty list, so the queue's record is not rebuilt each render.

## Two gaps closed in the shape, found by the review of this PR

- **A lost signal could beat the store** [`useKeptRead.ts`](../../src/hooks/useKeptRead.ts): a fetch that fails at once can answer before IndexedDB does, and the hook dropped the kept read as if the live read had landed, so a lost signal drew the failure panel over a device that held the queue. A store read now survives a failed live read and comes up with `failed` set. Every page on the hook gains this; a landed read still drops it.
- **Overlapping reads could strand the kept queue** `useFollowUpQueue`: a focus or a new line starts a read while another is out. When the first failed, `loading` went false, and when the second landed there was no edge for the hook, so the page stayed on the kept queue with *could not read again* over a live read in hand. A failed read now ends `loading` only when no other read is out; a landing ends it at once.
