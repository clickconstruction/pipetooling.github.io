The owner, 2026-11-16: "One name for the run stage, and one count of what waits on the leader." - `LienBoardNoticeStage` gains `to_print`. The `ready` pile maps to it and `printed` stays on `in_mail`, so the flow is needs_owner, to_send, awaiting, to_print, in_mail, sent. One more arrow joins them.

## One count of what waits on the leader

Two things wait on him, and the desk already had a word for each on his own list (*Only you can approve*, *Only you can sign*):

- **to approve**: a notice or an affidavit the office sent for approval (the awaiting bins).
- **to sign**: an approved notice with no signature on it as it stands. It was approved by a standing rule or on his word, or it changed after he signed. The run holds it.

`lienItemUnsigned` in `lienNoticeSignature.ts` is the one rule for the second. The Board marks the chip (`unsigned`), To do's row and the title bar read the same function, so the three cannot count differently.

- The Board's second tile shows the sum, *8 waiting on Sam*, with *1 to approve · 7 to sign* under it (`lienBoardLeaderWaits`, `lienLeaderWaitWords`). A part at zero is left out.
- Its press, and the same count on the pinned find box, narrows To do to those papers (`{ kind: 'leader' }`, `lienBoardLeaderPick`): the rows awaiting approval, the approved rows with no signature, and a GC's row that holds one. The line reads *Showing 8 rows waiting on Sam.* Until now the press lit the awaiting bins alone.
- The Dashboard's Needs you line reads the tile, so its number is the sum too.
- The title bar's small words say the second part in the same word, *7 for Sam to sign*, and so does the run window's foot.
- **Remind** is unchanged: it messages the leader about the papers to approve, since only they have a message. Its head now says so, *Waiting on Sam to approve*.

## Left as they were

- *In the mail · tracking owed* on the AR chip and the job's timeline, the footer chip's *Printed · in the mail* and By date's *In the mail* mark. They name the printed step from outside the desk and say *in the mail* where the pile says *to mail*. This is the next place the words disagree.
