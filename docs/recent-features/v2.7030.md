# v2.7030 — Messages: a fold shows what was done with no reply this week (2026-11-12)

Punch list #324, PR 4 of 5. No migration, no function to deploy. Reads `messages.history`, so it follows v2.7026's migration onto prod.

## Why

Every lane on Messages means something waits. A conversation closed with **No reply needed** left them all, and the only way back was to search *Everyone we have talked with*. The dev asked on 2026-11-12 whether Messages should show what was closed, as New folds the notices it set aside.

## What changed

- **The fold** (`components/followUp/DoneFold.tsx`), under Follow up's lanes on `/messages` and on GC projects' Follow up: *3 done with no reply this week*, shut until pressed. It draws nothing when there is nothing to list, and never over a kept queue.
- **Two kinds of row, one per person** (`followUp/doneFold.ts`): a person a teammate closed with the press, from the grey line v2.7029 writes (*No reply needed · Rosa, on Tuesday*), and a person whose old mail was filed for the record and asked nobody (*Old mail filed on Monday · 6 mails*). When both hold for one person, the newer fact shows. Newest first.
- **Only people whose conversation is Done today** (`doneFoldIo.ts`): two reads, joined to `contacts` on `state = 'done'`, seven days back. A person someone has since reopened, or who wrote again, is not listed.
- **Reopen** is `set_state`, which also takes the mark off filed mail. The name opens the thread.
- **Its read is the panel's** (`FollowUpIo.loadDoneFold`): once on mount and again whenever the queue reads, so a close pressed anywhere on the page is in it. A read that fails leaves the fold as it was: the lanes never wait on it.

## Decided against

A seventh segment on the jump strip. The strip is tight on a phone already, and a lane with a count reads as work to do.

## How it was checked

- `doneFold.test.ts`: the title, each kind of row, one row a person with the newer fact, newest first, a grey line that is not the press's own skipped, plain words.
- `FollowUpPanel.render.test.tsx`: the fold with both kinds of row, the name opening the thread, Reopen calling `setState(id, 'waiting')` and saying so; no fold when nothing was done.

## Not verified

- The two reads have not run against prod. They use an inner join on `contacts` with a filter on the joined state, the shape `followUpIo.ts`'s queue read already uses, and a `LIKE` on the grey line's body.
- A filed mail on a customer's thread that is Done by our reply also lists as *Old mail filed*. That is true and may read as noise once people tag their own old mail. Watch it.
