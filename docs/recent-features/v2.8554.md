# v2.8554 — Lien desk: the path keeps a notice recorded by hand (2026-11-24)

A second finding of punch list #415, the Lien desk's leftovers from the ledger (#361), fixed on the owner's yes of 2026-11-24. Client only: no migration, no edge function. Walk 7 of that card recorded a notice by hand on a ZZ Test job and then read *§ 53.056 notice · no work month yet* on the job's path.

## What was wrong

The desk's path took its notice months from two places (`lienTimelineMonthsFromDesk`): the month reader's rows, and the desk papers that read sent or missed. It never read the notices on record. The Lien window's path always has (`buildLienTimelineFromWindow`), so the two could disagree.

- A notice recorded by hand on a job with no live paper on the desk leaves no paper that says sent. `recordLienNoticeByHand` marks a live paper sent when there is one, and `syncLienDeskAfterRecord` finds none to move.
- The reader (`list_lien_notice_months`) returns a noticed month only until its notice day is a week past.
- From that day the path had no month for the job. Its § 53.056 stop read *no work month yet*, and its affidavit stop read *opens when the notice is mailed*, over a notice on record.
- A record that named more months than the job's sent paper carried showed only the paper's months.

It was a wrong reading and not a wrong date. The next step, the notice gate and every deadline read right throughout.

## Confirmed, with the inputs

A sub job on a house, dated by a last day of work set by hand in September 2026. A notice naming September is on record by hand, sent November 12. No paper is on the desk. September's notice day is November 16.

| Read on | The reader returns September | § 53.056 stop, before | § 53.056 stop, now |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nov 20 | yes, noticed | *§ 53.056 · Sep · sent* | *§ 53.056 · Sep · sent Nov 12* |
| Nov 24 | no, a week past | *§ 53.056 notice · no work month yet* | *§ 53.056 · Sep · sent Nov 12* |

On prod on 2026-11-24, read through the dev seat, counts only: 21 live notice records and 17 sent notice papers. Two billed sub jobs had a live notice on record, no sent paper and no row from the reader. Three more had a record that named more months than their sent paper. None was looked at on the live desk.

## What changed

- [`lienTimelineDesk.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienTimelineDesk.ts): `lienTimelineMonthsWithNoticeRecords` takes the desk's months and the job's live § 53.056 records. A month a record names reads sent, on the day its packet left (`earliestSendDay`, else the day it was recorded). A month a paper already reads sent keeps the paper's day. One the reader alone calls noticed takes the record's day, so the stop reads the same on both sides of that week. A month a paper calls missed or skipped reads sent once a live notice names it: the record is the fact.
- `buildLienTimelineFromDesk` hands its notice stops those months. What dates the job (`lienDeskDatingMonth`) still reads the desk's own months, so LAST WORK, the affidavit's last day and the year to sue count from the month they did.
- [`lienTimelineBook.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienTimelineBook.ts): a row's months are read the same way, so the Deadlines list and the attorney's lien grid agree with the path.

## Not changed

- The month reader and every SQL function. The Lien window's path. Any paper, any deadline, any gate.
- A voided record, which names nothing.
- The notice list's rows and the Months on this job card, which read the reader and the desk papers as before.
