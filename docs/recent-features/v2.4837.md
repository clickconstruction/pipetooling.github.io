# v2.4837 — GC mode: the schedule's chart shows one company's work (2026-10-07)

G-13's kernel (`to-dos/gc-mode/GANTT_FEATURES.md` G-13, its plan `to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/G-13.md`, both on branch `spike/gc-mode`): the chart's one-company filter in `src/lib/gc/schedule/gantt.ts`, additive, no migration. The control is drawn on the spike's chart (`GcGantt.tsx`), and the schedule's PR 7 lifts that chart to main with it.

- `ganttCompanyBars(bars, company?)`: one company's bars, by the name By company groups them under (the company hired, *Our own crew*, *The city* for inspections). No company: every bar.
- `ganttFilter(bars, filters, company?)` and `ganttCounts(bars, company?)` take the company as an optional third and second argument. The five pills (`GanttFilters`, `NO_FILTERS`) do not change, so every caller and every moved test reads as before.
- `ganttCompanies(bars)`: the picker's list, read from `ganttGroups(bars, 'company')`, so its order (the most late work first) and its late counts are By company's own.
- `ganttPrint.ts`: `GanttPrintInput.company?`. Our team's copy prints only that company's bars, its count sentence says *It shows 5 of 30 activities*, and a sentence follows: *Only Pecan Valley Electric's work shows.* The customer's copies draw from their own picture and never read it.
- Tests in main's own direct files (`gantt.direct.test.ts`, the new `ganttPrint.direct.test.ts`) on Fair Oaks D, so the files moved word for word from the spike stay as they were.
- Two defaults are on the owner's list: the pills count within the picked company, and *The city* is in the list.
