# v2.8682 — Bill Customer: a voided test bill leaves a record a developer can read (2026-11-26)

Punch list #443, row 7. One migration (a new table), one edge function changed, and a read-only line on one screen. The owner's answer, relayed 2026-11-27: *A, test bills only*. The push and the function's deploy are owed after the merge, each on its own yes. Neither was done.

## The press nothing took back

**Void invoice**, from **Void Stripe invoice…** on Bill Customer, calls `void-stripe-invoice-for-revert`. The function voids the invoice in Stripe and deletes the bill's `jobs_ledger_invoices` row. The rows of its Stripe emails (`jobs_ledger_invoice_stripe_email_sends`) go with it by their foreign key. Stripe keeps the invoice as void, and nothing in the app is left to read as one thing.

## The call

The card's first idea was a voided state on the bill's own row. About 250 files and many SQL functions read `jobs_ledger_invoices`, and each would have to leave voided rows out. Three ways were put to the owner, who picked the first: the function writes a record of the void to a new small table before it deletes, for a ZZ test job's bill alone, read by a dev alone, and no reader of bills changes.

## What was found first

- **The function's three deletes.** A sub's Collect Payment send-back of a bill that is not Stripe's; a bill on the Stripe channel with no Stripe id; and the Stripe path, after the invoice is voided, its draft deleted, or our own paid-by-check mark reversed. Each deletes as the service role.
- **What goes with the row.** By `ON DELETE CASCADE`: `jobs_ledger_invoice_stripe_email_sends`, `stripe_oob_payment_reverts`, `invoice_payer_acceptances`, `gc_owner_card_bills` and `invoice_due_days`. Only the first is a send. The ten other columns found that point at a bill are `ON DELETE SET NULL`, and none of them is a send (read in the migrations, not on a database).
- **What names a bill with no key.** `sent_documents` by `source_table` and `source_id`: the sent copy stays, and Bills sent already marks it *This bill is no longer on the job*. `email_send_log` by a copy's `resend_email_id`: stays. A text with the pay link names the bill inside its words: stays in Messages. The pay link is the bill's own id or its `pay_code` column, so it goes with the row.
- **Deleted records already keeps a copy** of the bill's row and of each Stripe email row (`zzz_archive_on_delete`). Each is a row of its own under a different group key, `deleted_by` is null because the service role deletes, what was done in Stripe is not kept, and the archive is purged after 90 days. So it can put a row back and cannot say who voided what.

## What changed

- **Migration `20261127024428_voided_test_bills.sql`**: `public.voided_test_bills`, one row a voided test bill. `invoice_id` (unique, no foreign key), `job_id` (the record goes with its job), `bill` (the whole row as JSON), `sends` (a JSON list), `stripe_invoice_id`, `stripe_action`, `voided_by`, `voided_by_name`, `voided_at`. A dev reads it. Nobody signed in writes it. The four fence calls close the file. Its page: [`docs/migrations/20261127024428_voided_test_bills.md`](../migrations/20261127024428_voided_test_bills.md).
- **`supabase/functions/_shared/voidedTestBill.ts`**, new and pure: `voidedTestBillKept` (a ZZ test job by `isZzTestJob`, and a job that could not be read keeps none), `voidedTestBillSends` and `voidedTestBillRecord`. Imported straight by the function, not through the mirrored kernels.
- **`void-stripe-invoice-for-revert`**: once the refusals that come before the Stripe step are passed (not billed, a payment on the bill, a mode mismatch), it reads the job's two names. For a ZZ test job it reads the bill's whole row, its Stripe email rows, the sent copies that name it and the caller's name, before the Stripe step. Right before each of the three deletes it writes the record with what it did on Stripe's side, as an upsert that passes over a duplicate, so a second try keeps the first record. Every success answer gains `kept_record`.
- **The record never stops a void.** Each read and the insert are caught. A table that is not there, a refused read or a row the table will not take is logged with `console.error`, and the delete runs as it did before.
- **`sends` holds two kinds.** `stripe_email` for a row of the send log, which goes with the bill. `sent_copy` for a `sent_documents` row that names the bill, which stays: who it went to, how and when are kept so the record reads whole. A text with the pay link is not copied.
- **`src/lib/billing/voidedTestBills.ts`**, the screen's rule: a dev on a ZZ test job (`voidedTestBillsDrawn`), a row read into its values (`parseVoidedTestBill`), and its sends as lines (`voidedTestBillSendLines`). A Stripe email and the copy filed within five minutes of it are one send, as on Bills sent, and a copy to the copy list is a line of its own.
- **`src/lib/billing/voidedTestBillsIo.ts`**: one read of one job's records, newest first. It answers an empty list when it has no rows to give, which covers a table that is not on the database yet.
- **`src/components/jobs/BillCustomerVoidedTestBills.tsx`**: the folded line *Voided test bills · N* at the foot of Bill Customer. Open, each record reads as values in place: the bill's number when a send's title carries one, its amount through `Money`, the day and time it was voided, who voided it, what was done in Stripe, and under *Its sends* one line a send with how it went, to whom and when. Every value is worked out. The only press is **Show** and **Hide**.

## Why the foot of Bill Customer

A void closes the window and puts the job back at Ready to Bill, so the bill's own View bill is gone with it. Bill Customer on the same job is the next screen a person opens, to make the bill again, and it is where the void was pressed. Bills sent has the dev's box of test bills, and that page is a list of sends across every job: a record of one job's voided bills reads better on the job. The line is not tied to the Pipeline's **ZZ test jobs** switch, since the dev is already on a test job.

## Who sees it

A dev, on a job whose name or stored customer name starts with ZZ. The component reads the signed-in role, so View as a sample account is that account and draws nothing. For every other role, and for a dev on a real job, the component asks the database nothing.
