# v2.3167 — The estimator's Supply houses tab, and one write predicate for the directory (2026-09-08)

PR 2 of six from [`to-dos/supply-house-directory/`](../../to-dos/supply-house-directory/README.md). Migration `20260908184533_can_manage_supply_house_directory.sql` — **push after the client deploys** (the client change is safe on the old policies; the migration only tightens who writes).

## What shipped

- **Estimators get Materials → Supply houses.** [`materialsTabsFor('estimator')`](../../src/lib/materials/materialsTabs.ts) now includes `supply-houses` and `supplyHousesPaneFor('estimator')` is `directory`, so the pill appears (left of the divider, where the office lanes sit) and `?tab=supply-houses` no longer bounces to Parts Book. The tab body is the new [`MaterialsSupplyHouseDirectoryTab`](../../src/components/materials/MaterialsSupplyHouseDirectoryTab.tsx): the v2.3166 Directory pane with `audience="estimator"` (supply houses only — insurers and the office's ledger buckets are filtered out, and the Kind column is not drawn) plus the shared house editor. **No accounts payable, no invoice, aging or balance** renders on the estimator's tab — the render smoke asserts the heading and the "Show paid invoices" toggle are absent, and `?tab=job-accounts` / `?tab=po-generator` still redirect.
- **One spelling.** The tab pill reads **Supply houses** for every role (was "Supply Houses" for the office; the to-do's open question, decided as sentence case like the app's other tabs).
- **`public.can_manage_supply_house_directory()`** — SECURITY DEFINER predicate: dev, master_technician, assistant, controller, estimator. The three write policies on `supply_houses` (`sup_supply_houses_insert/update/delete`) and the three on `supply_house_contacts` (`*_office`) now call it instead of listing roles inline. Two side effects worth knowing: **controller gains** house writes (the baseline arrays never included it, and the 2026-07-14 controller sweep did not touch this table — controllers could edit reps since v2.2648 but not the house), and **primary / superintendent lose** house writes they only ever reached through the mislabelled Parts Book modal (PR 3 retires that door). SELECT policies are unchanged: every Materials reader still reads houses and reps.

## Verified

As the estimator twin (`/dev-login?as=twin:estimator`) on the dev port: the Supply houses pill is present, Job Accounts and PO Generator are not; the tab shows **Add supply house**, no "Accounts payable", no dollar figure; `?tab=job-accounts` and `?tab=po-generator` land on Parts Book.
