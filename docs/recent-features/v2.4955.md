# v2.4955 — Lien window tests: the § Rules case no longer times out under load (2026-10-08)

Test only; no migration, no edge function, no screen change.

## The flake

`§ Rules names this job with its notice and lien dates from its last work day (v2.4829)` in `src/components/jobs/LienInstrumentsModal.render.test.tsx` timed out at the 5 s default three times on 2026-10-08 in full `npm test` runs on a loaded Mac (load averages 11–37), and passed alone in about 750 ms.

Measured alone with a lap timer, the case spent about 1,000 ms of its 1,470 ms on its own first line, `await import('./LienRulesModal')`. The rules window is the lazy chunk that carries every help guide through `helpGuideRegistry` (342 markdown files, ~1.7 MB; v2.4695 made it lazy so the app's main chunk stays under the service worker's precache cap). In a render test that import is Vite transforming every guide, and it ran *inside* the case's 5 s clock. Under a full run, with every worker transforming at once, that one import outlasts the clock. Nothing the mocked data load produces was missing: the strip's job, its two dates and the lit month all come synchronously from the job prop.

## The fix

- The file now holds a static side-effect `import './LienRulesModal'` beside the modal's import. The chunk loads with the file in the collect phase, which no case or hook timeout measures, and the door's `React.lazy` resolves from the module cache. Each case still goes through the door's lazy path and its Suspense retry.
- The case settles on `[data-lien-window-timeline]`, the marker the window's load paints and the sibling cases await, before it presses the door. It then `findBy`s the window and the job strip as before. No `waitFor` or test timeout widened.

Alone the case went from about 1,450 ms to about 450 ms, and the file's collect phase carries the chunk instead.

`LienRulesDoor.render.test.tsx` loads the same chunk in a `beforeAll`, whose 10 s hook clock is the same kind of budget; it has not flaked, and was left as is.

## Verified

- The file alone, 21 passed.
- `npm test` twice on the loaded machine.
