# v2.8101 — Papers: the server checks who sends a paper for signature (2026-11-20)

Found by the audit of punch list #403. One edge function and its shared rules. No migration and no screen.

## What changed

`send-contract-for-signature`'s person path writes with the service role, which no row policy holds. It now says who its caller may be before it reads the paper, as `complete-contract-form-office` and `contract-form-paper-entry` do:

- one of the four roles `person_contract_documents`' own write policy takes: a dev, a leader, an assistant or the controller;
- never a training account and never a digital twin, as the company branch (`trade_email`) already refuses them;
- an archived account first, as before. That check moved up beside the new one.

A caller who is refused gets a 403 in words, *Only the office sends a paper for signature.* or *A training account cannot send a paper for signature.*, with `code: 'forbidden'`. Nothing about the paper is read for them.

The rule is `signingCallerRefusal` in `supabase/functions/_shared/contractSigningSend.ts`, beside the request's and the document's refusals. The company branch keeps its own gate. Everything after the gate is as it was, and the pin test still holds the person path's bytes.

## Who this touches

Nobody the screens let send. Every send press is drawn for the four roles already: People → Contracts, Documents → Agreements' **Send a paper**, **Send again** and **Copy the signing link**, a sub's papers window, a job's papers before it opens, a lien waiver and a signed hours statement. The dev seat sends as the dev.

## Tests

`src/lib/contractSigningSendCaller.test.ts`: the four roles pass, every other role and a caller with no account row are refused, a training account and a twin are refused whatever their role, and the handler asks the gate before it reads the paper, after the archived check, with the caller read by the service role. All fourteen failed before the change.

## Deploy

**Redeploy required** after the merge: `supabase functions deploy send-contract-for-signature`. Until then prod runs the function as it was, and `npm run check:edge-drift` names it as behind.
