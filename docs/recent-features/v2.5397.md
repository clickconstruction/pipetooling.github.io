# v2.5397 — Archiving a login bans its sign-in (2026-10-12)

Archiving a login, and merging a duplicate login away, were meant to ban its sign-in. The ban never landed: the three edge functions asked the sign-in service (GoTrue) for `banned_until`, a field its admin update does not take, and it dropped the field without an error. They now send the field it takes. This is a behavior change, approved by the owner on 2026-10-12. It also clears the three functions' `deno check` errors. No migration and no screen.

- **The fault**: `archive-user` and `merge-users` called `auth.admin.updateUserById(id, { banned_until: '9999-12-31T23:59:59.999Z' })`, and `restore-user` called it with `banned_until: null`. supabase-js's `AdminUserAttributes` has no `banned_until` (TS2353), and auth-js sends the object as the body unchanged. GoTrue's `PUT /admin/users/{id}` reads the body into `AdminUserParams`, which has `ban_duration` and no `banned_until`, through a plain `json.Unmarshal` that drops unknown keys. Every field it does act on is guarded by "if present", so the call changed nothing and answered 200. `banError` was null and each function reported success. A read-only check on production, run with the owner's go, confirmed it.
- **The fix**: `archive-user` and `merge-users` send `ban_duration: '876000h'`, a Go duration of 100 years (`time.ParseDuration` reads hours, and its ceiling is about 292 years). GoTrue's `User.Ban` stores `now + duration` in `auth.users.banned_until`. `restore-user` sends `ban_duration: 'none'`, which clears it. `restore-user` had nothing to lift before, since nothing was ever banned.
- **What changes for people**: an archived or merged-away login can no longer sign in, by password or by an emailed link. GoTrue refuses its refresh token, so an open session ends at its next token refresh. `login-as-user` can no longer open a session as one either, since GoTrue refuses the link at verify. Restoring lifts the ban. The guides already say archiving and merging ban the sign-in (`archive-user-accounts`, `merge-user-accounts`), so they stay as they are.
- **Types**: `_shared/ctBridge.ts`'s `forwardCtSeatState` takes its own narrow `SupabaseAdminLike`, and the real client did not fit it (TS2589, TS2345). Both callers hand it `adminClient as unknown as Parameters<typeof forwardCtSeatState>[0]`, as `_shared/devMcpComposites.ts` does for its kernels. `ctBridge.ts` is untouched, so its other importers do not drift. The outer `catch` of `archive-user` and `restore-user` reads `(error as Error).message` (TS18046), as `merge-users` and `invite-user` do. Their `try` throws only `Error`s: `req.json()`'s `SyntaxError`, supabase-js's network `TypeError` and auth-js's UUID check.
- **Checked**:
  - `deno check --no-lock --node-modules-dir=none` (deno 2.9.6, TypeScript 6.0.3): 9 errors on `main` (`archive-user` 4, `merge-users` 1, `restore-user` 4), 0 here.
  - TypeScript's `transpileModule` output of main's and this `index.ts` differs only in the ban: the constant and the `ban_duration` line in `archive-user` and `merge-users`, the `ban_duration: 'none'` line in `restore-user`.
  - GoTrue's own source (`supabase/auth`): `AdminUserParams` and `adminUserUpdate` in `internal/api/admin.go`, `retrieveRequestParams` in `internal/api/helpers.go`, `User.Ban` in `internal/models/user.go`, and the ban checks at sign-in, verify and refresh.
- **Deploy**: redeploy `restore-user` first, then `archive-user` and `merge-users`, so a login the new code bans can always be restored. The ban works from that deploy on.
