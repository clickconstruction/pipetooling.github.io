# v2.7948 — Your hours and Utilization read a salaried person’s own week (2026-11-19)

The last reader of the per-person week. The owner asked on 2026-11-19: the pay and the job costs read the new week, but a salaried person's own dashboard and the office's board still measured every salaried person against 40 hours.

## Why

`src/lib/people/utilization.ts` held a salaried person to `scheduleHoursByUser`, which nothing filled, so every salaried row read 40. My Time's *Your hours* drew *Salaried: the schedule is 40 h a week* from a fallback written as 40. A day off took a fifth of the week. A salaried person who clocks real hours, with no week drawn, read as *assumed, not asked* at 40 and counted under *Not asked yet*.

## What ships

**The kernel.** `UtilizationPerson` carries `salariedWeek`, the set week from pay, and `scheduleHoursByUser` is gone. A person's hours are their answer when they have one. With none, a salaried person is held to their set week, source `schedule`, and anyone else to the assumed 40. That covers a salaried person read as hourly because they record real hours and punched. A recorded day off in a set week takes that day's hours, so a Friday off on a short Friday takes 4 and a Saturday off takes nothing. `CanWork` carries the week as `salariedWeek` for the words.

**The board.** `buildPayFlagsIndex` carries `salariedWeek` off `list_people_pay_flags`, which has returned the week since v2.7840. `fetchUtilizationBoard` hands it to each salaried person. People → Utilization shows *36 h · set week · 8 h Mon to Thu, 4 h Fri* where it showed *40 h · the schedule*.

**Your hours.** The dashboard's salary probe, `self_salary_clock_state`, returns the caller's own week (migration [`20261119160000_self_salary_clock_state_week`](../migrations/20261119160000_self_salary_clock_state_week.md)). A helper cannot read their own pay row, and the probe already runs once on the dashboard, so the week rides on it. The Dashboard keeps the week beside the salary flag and hands it to both blocks, the Job Mode card and the one inside My Time. The block reads *Salaried: your week is 36 h.* over *8 h Mon to Thu, 4 h Fri*. With nothing recorded and no answer the board leaves the person out, and the block still reads their own week rather than 40.

## Order

Either half alone reads as before. Until the migration is pushed, the probe's row has no week and the block reads the default week, 40. The old client ignores the new column. The board's half needs no push.

## Not in this

- A salaried person whose workday schedule makes 8-hour sessions on a 6-hour week reads over their week. The workday schedule's day length is still the fixed 8 hours.
- *Your hours* reads a salaried person as salaried even when they record real hours, as before. The board reads them as hourly once they punch.
