## Is this the best we can do?

1. **Fewer presses on a phone**: one press on the thread or the row, and Add Job opens filled in; before, Jobs → Add Job and three fields typed from memory.
2. **Plain words**: every sentence of `LEAD_DOOR_WORDS` runs through `plainWordsFailures`; the guide line passes the sweep.
3. **Do what is ready**: the customer is made and the thread re-pointed first, so a cancelled Add Job or a closed estimate page still leaves a true customer with their words; the fragment says so.
4. **Reuse**: `name_contact` keeps the thread; Add Job's own setters take the prefill; the estimate row is the page's own shape; the row's acts ride `needsReply` as every act does.
5. **Cut**: *Add to Prospects* (a kernel with no caller) stays unbuilt; the mock-up's *kept as a lead* chip is not an app phrase (the sub-line reads **Lead:** *name*). Converting on **Start an estimate** before the estimate is saved can leave a customer with no estimate if the page is closed; accepted, since a customer record with their words is still true.
