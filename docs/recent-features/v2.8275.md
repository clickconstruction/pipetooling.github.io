# v2.8275 — Papers before a job opens: the first press on a job with no papers takes a finger (2026-11-21)

Punch list #389, step 4, the second pass of its live test. v2.8229 made the presses on the way to a job's settings 44 px tall. Once it was live, the same presses were measured again on prod, and a job with no paper yet showed one that the first test never met. No migration and nothing to deploy.

## Measured again on the live build

On prod, a `ZZ Test` paper built from settings on the `ZZ TEST sink` job, with placeholder words and real presses.

- **v2.8229 holds.** At 1100 px and at 375 px, **Settings**, **Take off**, **Add a paper** and **Copy the signing link** each measured 44 px tall, a row of the list of papers 44 px, and **Close the list** 44 px.
- **Next stayed.** The box was ticked with a real press, and a second press at the same spot landed on **Next**.
- **The note press worked.** *Type … first.* measured 44 px tall. A press on it put the cursor in the blank, in view above the foot.

## What was still small, and is fixed here

- **The empty section's Add a paper.** A job with no paper draws one sentence, and **Add a paper** inside it is the only way in. It was 21 px tall on a desktop and 40 px on a phone. The first test ran on a job that already had a paper, where the press is a button. `EmptyState` takes `finger`, which makes its press 44 px tall with its words still on the sentence's line. Only this section passes it. Every other empty state is as it was.
- **The door on the Papers to sign window.** *This job's papers and who has signed* was 28 px tall. v2.8229 left it as the gate's own. It is the way to a paper's Settings once the job has shut, so it is 44 px tall now (`JobPapersGateModal.tsx`).
- **Take off alone on a line.** At phone width a paper's row wrapped **Take off** to a line of its own, on the left. Settings and Take off now wrap together, on the right (`JobDocumentsRequiredPapers.tsx`).
- **Require this paper moved.** On the review, the note under the button is a press 44 px tall while a blank is empty and one line of words once it is typed, so the button moved 25 px as the first letter went in. For a host's flow the words keep the press's room (`DocumentBuilderFlow.tsx`). The Document builder passes no host, so its review is as it was.

## Still small, and left

- **A dash of the step bar** is 38 px wide at phone width with seven steps. It is 44 px tall. Back and Next do the same job at full size.
- **The confirm window's two buttons** are 36 px tall. That window is shared by the whole app.
