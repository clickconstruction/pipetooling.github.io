# v2.5890 — The Collect card carries its desk door (2026-10-26)

The owner went looking for the Collect desk (punch list #177, v2.5848–v2.5858) on 2026-10-26 and could not find it: its only door was on the link desk (`Bridge.tsx`, v2.5853), which draws for the picked stage — and Build was the lit link, so the desk sat two presses deep behind a card press nothing advertises. The owner's call: a persistent door on the card itself, and long term a desk behind every stage card the same way.

## What shipped

- **`BridgeStages.tsx`**: a card can carry a **desk door** — a bordered full-width button at the card's foot (`StageDeskDoor`, `desks` prop keyed by stage id, `bridge-stage-desk-<id>` testid), a sibling of the pick button and the badge like v2.5867's split, so the card's three verbs stay apart: card body picks the stage for the dial, the dotted badge opens the order, the bordered footer opens the stage's desk. A stage with no desk draws no door — a dead door would lie.
- **`Bridge.tsx`**: passes `desks={{ collect: { label: 'Break it down', onPress } }}` while the financials are loaded — the same gate the link desk's door used. That contextual door on the Collect link desk stays; the words stay the page's own (*Break it down*).
- The pattern is the long-term shape: each future desk (Build, Bill, Market, Sales) lands by adding its entry to `desks`, and the cards never rearrange. The mock-up of the five-door strip was rendered and kept with the session.

## Tests

`BridgeStages.render.test.tsx` gains a case: the Collect card's door renders with its words, pressing it opens the desk without picking the card, and a stage given no desk draws no door.

## Verify

Open `/bridge` with Build as the link: the Collect card ends in **Break it down ›**. Press it — the Collect desk opens at once, and the dial above still reads Build. Press the card body instead: Collect is picked and the link desk below changes, as before.
