# v2.5578 — Prospects: a refreshed lane shows its own deck (2026-10-16)

Punch list #113, spot 1 (the live walk of 2026-10-16 and its mock-up, `to-dos/prospect-lanes-fixes/`). A full load of `/prospects?tab=follow-up&kind=gc-leads` showed the Customers deck under the Jobs to GC tab: a real customer prospect wearing **Asked us ✓**, the rail counting the customer queue. Seen three times in the walk, once while the lane's own lead sat on its list. The cause: the deck's `kind` state started as `customer` and the first load fired before the link's `?kind=` was read, so two loads raced and whichever answered last painted the deck. A tab press inside the page was fine; a refresh, a bookmark or a link from the Calendar was not.

- **`src/lib/prospects/laneLoad.ts`** (new, pure; 4 tests): `kindFromSearch` (the `?tab=subs` / `?tab=gc-leads` doors first, then `?kind=`, else the customer deck), `makeLatestOnly` (numbers each load; only the newest load's answer counts), `cardOnLane` (a card is drawn only when it is the lane's kind) and `laneLoadingWords` (*Loading Subs…*).
- **`Prospects.tsx`**: `kind` starts from `kindFromSearch(location.search)`, so the customer deck is never asked for on a lane's URL; `loadFollowUpProspects` takes a number from `makeLatestOnly` and returns after any await when a newer load has started, so a late answer never paints; the deck draws a card only when `cardOnLane` says it is the lane's, and says *Loading Jobs to GC…* otherwise. The guard means any future leak can only ever show as that line, never as another lane's card and press.
- **Docs**: the Prospects map's kind note.

Verified by the kernel tests, typecheck and lint; the live walk follows with the train's (a full load of each lane, with and without rows, and a kind-less `?prospect_id=` link).
