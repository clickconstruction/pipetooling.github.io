# v2.4159 — Job Mode: hand the phone to the customer to sign (2026-09-29)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. The Contract window train, **PR 5 of 5** (built out of order: it touches only the Job Mode card, while PRs 2–4 wait behind PR 1, [v2.4154](v2.4154.md), on `JobContractModal`). The second design pass put it plainly: *a tech at the kitchen table was an afterthought — Sign here, now belongs on the phone that is already at the job, in Job Mode, as one tap.*

## What shipped

- **[`jobContractInPerson.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobContractInPerson.ts)** (pure helpers, 3 tests) — `canHandThePhone(role)` (dev · master · primary · superintendent · estimator: the roles that speak for the company on site; not subs, helpers or the office), `inPersonSigningUrl` (adds `inperson=1`, whatever the link carries), `handPhoneState(rows)` (signed hides the door; a live row is reused; voided rows do not count), `handPhoneDoorText`. One async door, `openInPersonSigning({ jobId, authUserId, origin })`: loads the job (`fetchJobWithDetailsById`), reuses the live agreement — a draft takes the Book's current wording first (`refreshJobContractDraftTerms`), a sent one keeps its link — or mints a draft from the job's own facts (`buildJobContractPrefill` with the accepted estimate's lines and total when there is one, the newest customer Book document), then asks `send-job-contract` for the link only (`mode: 'link'`, nothing emailed) and returns it with the flag.
- **[`DashboardJobModeCard`](../../src/components/jobMode/DashboardJobModeCard.tsx)** — under the action row, on the clocked-in job (`leaveReportTarget`), a quiet link **✍ Hand the phone to the customer to sign** (`job-mode-hand-phone`): one read of `job_contracts` for that job decides whether it shows; a tap reads *Opening the agreement…* and then the phone navigates to the signing page in person, the same page the Contract window's *Sign here, now* opens. The customer reads, types or draws their name, ticks the consent; the page emails their copy if an address is on the job.
- **Guide** `job-mode-clocking` gains a section, *The customer signs on your phone*; **`docs/GLOSSARY.md`** → *Job Mode*; `docs/DASHBOARD_SECTIONS_ARCHITECTURE.md` → row 0. The contract guide's cross-reference from *Sign here, now* lands with PR 2, which rewrites that section after PR 1.

## Decided here

- **The clocked-in job only.** The customer is in front of the technician when they are clocked in; the *Ready to start* job is not yet.
- **Reuse, never a second agreement.** A sent agreement opens on its own link, so the office's copy and the customer's bookmark stay one; a draft goes out with the Book's current wording, the sweep's v2.3965 rule.
- **Same page, same flag.** No new signing surface: `?inperson=1` on the customer's page, which already says *Signing in person with a Click Plumbing team member present* and records `signer_mode = 'in_person'`.
