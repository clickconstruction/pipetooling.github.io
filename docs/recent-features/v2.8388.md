# v2.8388 — Agreements: an estimator opens a signed paper from its line (2026-11-23)

Punch list #403, the owner's second decision, the second of two PRs: the screens and the signed file's gate. No migration. Two edge functions change and each owes a redeploy: `open-contract-form-pdf` and `person-paper-pdf`. It stands on v2.8378, whose migration (`20261123184149_estimator_reach.sql`) is pushed first.

## The decision

The card's pick: a signed line opens its record from the tab. An estimator reads the form's layout and the signature's image for an outside company's paper. Naming a party who holds papers needs one of the four roles. A training account reads the line and is refused the signed file. A trade company's chip stays where it is until step 6. The owner answered "Yes, all four" on 2026-11-22, and on 2026-11-23 said to build it with a training account reading the line only.

## What changed

- **Documents → Agreements, as an estimator.** A signed line's name is a press that opens the **Signed contract** window (`agreementReaderDoor` in `lib/documents/agreementsLedger.ts`). A paper that waits, a paper never sent, a work order and a job stay plain words, and no line carries an act. The foot reads *Press a signed paper’s name to open its record. A paper that is not signed yet has nothing to open.*
- **The Signed contract window.** It lists **Form answers** and draws the **Signature** for an estimator on an outside company's paper, since both reads now answer (v2.8378). A sensitive answer still reads *in the PDF only*. The words for a read that did not come back moved to `lib/contracts/signedRecordWords.ts`, and the signature's no longer say it is kept for the office.
- **Messages → Who is this?.** A contact that holds a paper is refused for an estimator. The window says *They hold a paper, so only the office says who they are. Ask a leader, an assistant or the controller to name them.* (`lib/contacts/paperHolderNaming.ts`, read by `messagesIo.nameContact` and `nameAsLead`). A contact that holds no paper is named as before. The database holds the rule, and nobody signed in may ask it beforehand, so the window presses and reads the answer.
- **A training estimator.** Their lines open nothing, on the tab and on a company's own page (`canOpenSignedRecord` in `lib/contracts/partyPapers.ts`). The tab's foot reads *This list is for reading. A training account opens no signed paper.* The two functions refuse them the signed file (`formPdfOpener`, `paperPdfOpener`), each reading `users.read_only` with the caller's role.

## Defaults this was built on

Each is the session's, built so the owner can read it and change it.

- **(a) A training account sees the line only.** No form answers, no signature image, no PDF. So a training estimator's signed line has no press at all, on the tab and on a supply house's and a customer's own page, where it has opened the record since v2.8162. The row itself is still theirs to read.
- **(a, its edge) The refusal is the estimator's.** A dev, the controller and a pay-approved master open a signed form in training as before, and so do the four roles on a paper's PDF. The decision is about an estimator's reach, and the card does not say every training account.
- **(b) The words.** The two refusals are *They hold a paper, so only the office says who they are. Ask a leader, an assistant or the controller to name them.* and *A training account does not open a signed form.* The foot is *Press a signed paper’s name to open its record. A paper that is not signed yet has nothing to open.*
- **(c) `person-paper-pdf` carries the same training refusal.** A paper of plain words has a signed file too, and that function may also make and keep a PDF on the press, which is a write a training account should not start.
- **The refusal comes after the press.** The mock-up drew the sentence under **This is who it is**. The window cannot know before the press, because telling an estimator which unknown contacts hold a paper would say what they may not read.
