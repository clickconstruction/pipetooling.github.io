# v2.7884 — Trips: combining two jobs keeps a trip the job held (2026-11-18)

## Why

The owner's ask on 2026-11-18, after v2.7861: *a trip a job holds by itself is not carried by the combine*. A trip is kept on a development, or on a job that has no development. The combine deletes the source job after moving its rows. A trip the source job held by itself was not among the rows it moved, so it went with the job. It was kept in the deleted-records archive, which only a dev reads.

No screen makes such a trip and prod held none on the day this was written. Only the dev seat files one. The fix lands before the first one is lost.

## What changed

One migration, `20261118233000_combine_carries_job_held_trip.sql` (`docs/migrations/20261118233000_combine_carries_job_held_trip.md`).

- **`migrate_job_ledger_costs_and_delete` restated** from its newest body with one block added: each trip the source job holds by itself goes to the kept job's home. That is the kept job's development when it is in one, with the kept job named as the job the trip's work goes on. It is the kept job itself when it is in none.
- **`trips_take_number` restated.** A trip's number stood through every update. It still stands while the trip's home stands. A trip that moves home keeps its number when the new home never handed that number out, and takes the new home's next number otherwise.
- The combine's file was generated from the newest body by a script. A diff of the two bodies shows the variable, the block and one shortened comment.

## Decisions

- **Keep the number when it can be kept.** A bill may cite *Trip 2*. A kept job with no trips of its own has handed out no number, so the trip that moves in stays *Trip 2*.
- **Never hand a moving trip a number the new home handed out before.** That number may belong to a deleted trip, and **Put back** gives a deleted trip its number again.
- **A trip moved onto a job in a development becomes the development's trip.** A trip belongs to the site when there is one. The kept job is named as the job its work goes on, so the Job window still lists it.
- **A line keeps its words.** A line named after a trip is tied by the trip's id. Its name is not rewritten when the trip takes another number.

## Docs

`docs/BILLING_FLOWS.md` names this file as the combine's newest body. `docs/GLOSSARY.md` and `docs/ACCESS_CONTROL.md` say what a trip's number does when the trip moves home. `docs/migrations/20261118040000_trip_numbers.md` points here. The trip record's card (#379) says the combine carries both.
