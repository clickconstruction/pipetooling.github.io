# v2.7031 — Pay calendar: a bill not yet sent says its cutoff (2026-11-12)

Punch list #323, PR 3 of seven, stacked on v2.7028. For a payer on a Pay calendar, a bill in one day past the cutoff waits a whole month, and nothing on a bill that had not gone out said so. This is the part of the card that moves money.

## What changed

- **The kernel** (`src/lib/jobs/payCalendar.ts`): `payCutoffLine(todayYmd, cal)` gives the two sentences (*Send by Nov 30 to be due Dec 10.* and *After that, Jan 10.*), whether the cutoff is inside `PAY_CUTOFF_SOON_DAYS` (7), and the reason for the hover. `sendTodayCutoffWords` says it for the moment of sending. `nextCutoffChip` names the nearest cutoff among several and how many bills it holds.
- **A Ready to Bill row**: `PayCutoffLine` in the Progress & payment cell, blue while the cutoff is far and amber in its last week. Its first sentence is a press that opens the payer's Pay calendar. It rides the row's existing render prop (`billedLienRunway`), which returned nothing for a Ready to Bill row before, so the desk table, the unified rows and the Mobile cards all draw it with no new prop.
- **The Ready to Bill header**: *Next cutoff Nov 25 · 1 bill*, from the unfiltered board, amber in the last week.
- **Bill Customer** (`SendRecordInvoiceModal`): `BillCustomerPayCutoffLine` under the returned-checks line reads the payer's calendar by itself and says *Sent today, this bill is due Dec 10. Their cutoff is Nov 30. After that, Jan 10.* Never a gate. Fail-soft: no line on a read that fails.
- **Guides**: *The cutoff, before a bill goes out* in *set a payer's pay calendar*, and a paragraph in *bill a customer and get paid*.

## Not changed

- A payer with no calendar: no line, no chip.
- Nothing is held or reordered. The cutoff only says what waiting costs.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **The chip is words, not a filter.** The card planned a chip that filters Ready to Bill to the bills on that cutoff. Ready to Bill held one row on the day this was built, so a filter would have been a control with nothing to do. If the section grows, the chip becomes a press.
- **The phone's two-line rows do not carry the cutoff.** The Mobile cards do. The two-line rows get it with the Working row's line in PR 5.
- **The cutoff is the contract's day, with no margin.** A bill sent on the last day may not be accepted that day. The amber week asks early, and PR 4's accepted day will show whether late-month bills slip.
- **It counts from today.** A bill dated today and sent tomorrow reads tomorrow's line tomorrow; the line is only as good as the day the bill really goes in.
