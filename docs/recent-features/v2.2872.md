# v2.2872 — Work orders: the small chips elsewhere read the rail's words (2026-09-05)

PR 5, the last of the work-orders one-row-spine train (the `to-dos/work-orders-one-row-spine.md` entry is deleted with this PR, per the to-dos rule; the sweep record keeps the pointer). Everything that mentions a sub work order outside the two tabs now uses the vocabulary the rail established (v2.2866) — one drawing, one set of words.

## What changed

- **The sheet's Work order box** ([`SubSheetWorkOrderPanel.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/SubSheetWorkOrderPanel.tsx)) draws [`SheetRail`](../../src/components/jobs/SheetRail.tsx) from `buildSheetRail` (coverage from the sheet's own order via `buildJobWorkOrderCoverage`, the sheet's stage and money) instead of the v2.2786 segmented bar. `sheetWorkOrderRail` and `SubWorkOrderRailSegment` are removed from `subWorkOrder.ts` (+ their tests) — the rail has one home.
- **Chip words** (`workOrderChipLabel` / `workOrderChipTitle` in [`workOrderCoverage.ts`](../../src/lib/subWorkOrders/workOrderCoverage.ts), used by `JobWorkOrderChip` on the job window's fact row and View bill): *No agreement* · *Drafted · no price yet* / *Drafted* · *Sent · awaiting signature* / *Offer expired* · *✍ Signed* · *Declined*. The strip's sentence reads "No agreement — nothing signed for a sub on this job" / "Drafted for ‹sub› — no price yet"; its buttons follow the board's Next column: **Price…** (unpriced draft), **Send…** (priced draft), View, View record, Re-offer….
- **Person Desk → Work orders** ([`PersonDeskWorkOrdersSection.tsx`](../../src/components/personDesk/sections/PersonDeskWorkOrdersSection.tsx)): *Drafted · no price yet* / *Drafted*, *Sent Sep 4 · awaiting signature*, *Signed Sep 5*.
- **Needs You** (`work-orders-unpriced` in [`dashboardNeedsYou.ts`](../../src/lib/dashboardNeedsYou.ts)): the detail ends "Next: price it and send." — the rail's next-action words.
- Guides: `assemble-a-sub-work-order` (job-window chips and buttons), `send-a-sub-a-work-order-from-a-sheet` (the box's rail).
