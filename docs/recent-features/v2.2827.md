# v2.2827 — The anonymous crew lane in Prospects → Team → Review → Reflect (2026-09-05)

Second PR of the three-bars arc (v2.2824 shipped the clock-out deck). The office now sees what the crew said, averaged, beside its own reviews.

- **Reflect card header** gains a muted `Crew a · d · i (n crew)` span after the office `Avg …` — the newest month from `crew_review_aggregates()` for that subject (`latestCrewLane` in [`crewReview.ts`](../../src/lib/people/crewReview.ts)). The RPC already applies the 2-rater rule for staff and never returns names or notes; the tooltip says so.
- **Composite toggle** (dev, in the Leaderboard tab's gear editor next to the weights): **Crew lane counts as a reviewer** persists `app_settings.team_review_composite_include_crew_v1` = `'true'|'false'` (`APP_SETTINGS_KEY_TEAM_REVIEW_INCLUDE_CREW`). When on, `crewPseudoReviews()` turns each aggregate month into one review row by pseudo-reviewer `crew` (rounded averages, no notes), and `compositeReviews` (office rows + those) feeds `compositeScore`, the chart's composite series, and `buildRoleLeaderboards`. The pseudo-reviewer has no calibration baseline, so it enters raw — the same treatment as any reviewer with fewer than three subjects. Off by default; nothing the office relies on moves until a dev flips it.
- **Loader**: `fetchCrewReviewAggregates()` is additive — a failed RPC (migration not yet applied) hides the lane instead of breaking the section. Office `team_member_reviews` reads stay `source = 'office'`.
- Not in this PR: crew points on the per-person ratings chart; a crew column in the Dimension view. Both are follow-ups if the lane earns its place.
- Docs: guide `track-and-rank-prospective-hires` (Reflect paragraph), `PROSPECTS_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md`.
