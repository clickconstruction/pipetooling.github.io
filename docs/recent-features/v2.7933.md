# v2.7933 — Painted maps: the press opens on your next job (2026-11-19)

Punch list #392, call 16. The owner's pick, asked after v2.7920: *Open on tomorrow's*.

## What changed

- **The guess leads with the next job** (`pickerSections` in `src/lib/paintedMap/paintedMapJobPicker.ts`). With no job told and none on today's schedule, the start sheet opens on the first job under **Coming up** when its day is inside a week (`PICKER_NEXT_DAYS`, the days said as *tomorrow* or a weekday). The line under *For* says *The next job on your schedule, tomorrow.* (`pickerGuessWords`). With no such job the guess is what it was: the last job the person was at in the last two weeks. With neither, the sheet asks.
- **The first stop of the day.** The schedule's read now carries `time_start`. `pickerPlan` keeps each job ahead at its soonest day and start, and **Coming up** lists the jobs of one day in order of start. So with two jobs tomorrow the sheet opens on the one the crew reaches first.
- The office job and a job the person may not paint on are never the next job, since neither is under Coming up. A job on today's schedule still means the person is asked.
- Job Mode's wide press says *On your next or last job, or a pick* when the day names no job.
- No new read and no migration. The guide `paint-a-map` and the Glossary's line are amended.

## Why inside a week and not two

Coming up looks two weeks out, and the field's schedule on the live rows runs days ahead. A job ten days off is a plan, and the job the person stood on yesterday is then the likelier one. Inside a week the day reads as *tomorrow* or a weekday.
