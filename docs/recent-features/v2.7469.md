PR 6 of the supply house payments train. The owner's call 4 of 2026-11-16: Mark paid stays one press, and the payment it makes waits on a finish-later list. Without a list, later never comes.

## What changes

- **A paid row whose payment has no method says so**: *paid 11/14 · how it was paid is missing*, with **Finish…** beside it. A bill paid before payments were rows reads as it always did.
- **Finish…** opens **How $412.80 was sent**: the method chips (opening on how the house was paid last), that method's number, **Date sent** and **Receipt link**. The amount is the payment's and is not changed there. **Save how it was sent** is dead with its reason until a method is picked.
- **The list.** Over the aging table, Accounts payable draws one line while anything waits: **3 payments need how they were paid**, with their total. Pressed, it lists each (the house, the bill or how many bills, *marked paid* or *recorded* and the day, the amount), the longest wait first. It is not drawn at zero.
- **Needs you** counts the same payments under **Supply houses**: *3 supply house payments need how they were paid*, gray, rank 60, for the office. **Finish them** lands on `/materials?tab=supply-houses&list=finish` with the list open.
- **Edit how it was sent** in a payment's window opens the same editor for a payment that already has a method.
- A payment recorded with **Say later** waits on the list too. A payment from before never does, nor one that sent nothing (a credit memo that covered a bill whole).
