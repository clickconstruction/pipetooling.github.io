# v2.6270 — Documents → Photos: every photo uploaded, newest first (punch list #232, PR 2) (2026-11-01)

Punch list #232's second PR, on the owner's go of 2026-11-01. The owner's ask: *a view that shows me all the photos that have been uploaded, sorted by most recent to least recent and where they are saved and who uploaded the photo*. PR 1 (v2.6264) made the `uploaded_photos` view and the kernel; this PR is the tab.

## What changed

- **Documents → Photos** (`components/documents/DocumentsPhotosTab.tsx`, `?tab=photos`, between Sent and Where files go, for everyone who opens the page): a search box (*Find a photo: a job, a customer, a person, a stage*), the source chips (All · Reports · Tally boxes · Checklists, and Texts & email for the office and the estimator) and the where chips (Everywhere · In Drive · Not in Drive yet with its count, drawn amber while it is more than zero), all asked of the server through the view; the rows by day (*Today · Sun, Nov 1*, *Yesterday*, the day), newest first, a hundred at a time with **Show more**; each row its thumbnail (`UploadedPhotoTile`: a signed URL on the bucket while the shot waits, `job-photos`' thumbnail once filed, our kept copy for a text's picture), the caption, the job line as a press to the job window, the source chip as a press to what the photo came with (the report's card through `list_reports_for_job_ledger`, the Tally's On site tab on that box, the Messages thread at that line), the person, the time and the sizes, and the Drive line in the row's own words with its press (*In Drive · Open ↗*, *On its way to Drive*, *Not in Drive yet · That job has no Drive folder yet · Make the job's Drive folders*, *Still on Jordan's phone*, *Our copy, not in Drive · the thread*). The foot says a photo put in a Drive folder by hand is in the job's own strip. A read that fails draws `OfflineRetryPanel` with Retry; an empty list says what fills it, or that nothing matches the filters.
- **The viewer** (`UploadedPhotoViewer.tsx`, a `ResponsiveModalShell`): the picture at full size, streamed and never kept, Previous and Next through the list as shown, the facts (the photo and its sizes, the job, who and when, where), and the presses **Open in Drive**, the source's door, **Open the job** and **Only Mateo's photos**, which narrows the list to that person.
- **The guide** *find every photo that was uploaded*; `documentsPageTab.ts` reads `photos`; the Documents page section of `docs/PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`, `docs/DOCUMENTS_PAGE_ARCHITECTURE.md`, the Glossary's *Report photos* entry and `docs/twins/APP_DIRECTORY.md` → /documents name the tab.

## Decisions

- **One tab for everyone who opens the page**; the view's policies scope the rows, so a primary sees their own jobs' photos and no text pictures, and the chip they cannot use is not drawn.
- **The report's card opens from the job's own list** (`list_reports_for_job_ledger`, found by id), the RPC the job window already reads, rather than a new read of `reports`: the same gate, the same shape, no new function.
- **The checklist source's door is the job**: a checklist answer has no card of its own; the job window's stage checklists are where it reads.
- **The waiting count is one head read** of the view with `drive_file_id is null`, refreshed after each list read, so the chip's number is true to what the list would show.
