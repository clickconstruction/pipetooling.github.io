# v2.5007 — Sub sheets: a backcharge stays on the sheet it was raised on (2026-10-09)

Client only. No migration, no edge function. This builds the owner's call of 2026-10-09, from the decisions walk: a backcharge is pinned to the sheet it was raised on. Since v2.3562 a backcharge moved exactly like a payment, through Jobs → Subs → Pay's Move… and the Remove dialog's *Wrong job → Move it instead*.

## The guard

`src/lib/jobs/subPaymentMoveRemove.ts` gains `subPaymentMoveRefusal(payment)`. It returns the one line `SUB_BACKCHARGE_STAYS`, *A backcharge stays on the sheet it was raised on.*, for a backcharge (by its negative amount or its `isBackcharge` flag), and null for a payment. Every way into Move asks it:

- **The sheet** (`JobsSubLaborFormModal.tsx`): Move… on a backcharge row, or *Move to another sheet…* in the phone's ⋯ menu, shows the line on that row (`role="status"`) and opens nothing. A payment's Move… opens the dialog as before.
- **The dialogs** (`SubLaborPaymentMoveRemoveModals.tsx`):
  - `openMove` refuses a backcharge.
  - A backcharge's Remove dialog shows the line where a payment's offers *Wrong job → Move it instead*.
  - Remove stays a backcharge's way off its sheet, with its 30-day Undo.

## Help
