# v2.2318 — Portal statement groups bills and payments by job (2026-08-25)

Owner ask on top of v2.2313's payment rows: group the statement by job, and
put Total paid "in its own box, to the right". Approved before/after mockup:
claude.ai/code/artifact/9da611f5-ebc6-42a4-bd49-8b439c291f67.

## Kernel

- New `src/lib/portal/portalJobGroups.ts` (+13 tests):
  `groupPortalBillsByJob` regroups the flat newest-first bill list into
  per-job groups — key is `jobNumber`, falling back to label+address+name for
  unnumbered bills. Groups sort by newest bill (undated floats first, same
  convention as the edge sort); bills stay newest-first inside a group;
  payments merge across the job's bills oldest-first. Totals:
  `balance` = Σ open amounts, `totalPaid` = Σ cached per-bill aggregates
  (job-remainder rows carry aggregate-only totals — recap still works with
  zero payment rows), `billedToDate` = balance + totalPaid.
  `portalPaymentMethodLabel`: blank/`other` → "Payment" on customer paper
  (invoice kernel still prints raw types — candidate follow-up).
  `portalBillBilledAmount` = amount + totalPaid (the original billed figure).
  `showRecap` = payments landed OR multi-bill job (a lone unpaid bill gets no
  redundant box).

## Statement rendering (`CustomerPortal.tsx`)

- Ledger becomes job sections: NOTE_BAND header band (trade tag · number ·
  street, city/owner quiet on the right, As GC chip carried over) → bill rows
  (date + aging, quiet "billed $X" when payments exist, amount due, PAY
  ONLINE / check chip) → dashed "Payments received" sub-ledger (credits with
  a minus sign) → right-aligned bordered recap box (max 320px): Billed to
  date / Paid to date (PAPER_GREEN) / ink rule / **Balance on this job** —
  deliberately the same totals grammar as the v2.2313 invoice payment box.
- Header column renamed Amount → "Amount due"; a hidden pay-button twin in
  the header grid keeps the label over the money column.
- Grand double-rule Total due unchanged; edge function and payload untouched
  (payload already carried jobNumber, per-bill payments, and totals).
