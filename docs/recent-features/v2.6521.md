# v2.6521 — The who-has-it pill names the holder, you included (2026-11-04)

Grace's note of 2026-11-04, with a screenshot of the thread header's pill reading *You have this* beside the R face: "I think a user should see their name instead of you have this." The pill (`WhoHasItPill`, `followUp/queue.ts`'s `hasPillWords`) said *You have this* to the holder and *Rosa has this* to everyone else, so the same row read two ways and a screenshot could not say who held it. No migration; nothing to deploy.

## What changed

- `hasPillWords` names the holder whoever is looking: *Robert has this*. The one case kept is a profile with no name, whose `me` is the word *You*, which still reads *You have this* rather than *You has this*. The toast after the picker (`hasToast`, *You have Greg.*) is unchanged: it answers the person who just pressed.
- The guide *see who is waiting on us and what we owe them* reads *Robert has this* names who holds it; `docs/MESSAGES_PLAN.md` and the pill's header comment amended.
