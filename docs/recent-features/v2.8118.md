## Why

The call list's *Ask for* block has stood empty since [v2.7993](./v2.7993.md). On 2026-11-20 the owner agreed to a one-time lookup of the next 25 San Antonio companies on the list: what kind of company each is, an office phone, and one person and title to ask for. He makes the calls himself, so a wrong fact costs him a real call. A trial of twenty lookups got two inferences wrong. So each fact keeps the page it came from and the day, and reads as *looked up, not checked* until a person says it held.

That needs somewhere to keep a fact apart from the office's own word, and somewhere to keep the check.

## What changed

- **`builder_lookups`**, append-only: one row a fact. The builder's key, which fact (the kind, the phone, the person), its value, a detail (the title, or a few words on the kind), the page, the day, the account that wrote it, and that an agent looked. A fact with no page is refused. The one exception is a kind that could not be told.
- **`builder_lookup_checks`**, append-only: one row a press on a fact. Checked, wrong, or back, with who and when. The newest press is the fact's state.
- **Who writes.** A fact: a dev account only, under its own name. A press: anyone who reads permits, as oneself. Both are read by the same people who read permits.
- **Apart from the office's word.** Nothing here touches a builder's kind, its mark or a call-list answer.

## What the owner approved

The drawing shows the block in each state, the two tables, the write path and what a fact may do to the list. His answer on 2026-11-20: build it as drawn. That includes the one choice it put to him: a looked-up kind tags a name at once, and moves it in the order only after a person checks it.

## Decisions

- **One row a fact, not one row a name.** Each fact has its own page and is checked by a different act. The phone is checked by calling it and the person by asking for them.
- **Presses in a table of their own.** A check is a person's word and a fact is an agent's reading. Keeping them apart means a fact is never edited, and a slip is undone with one more row.
- **The app's Google Places answers (`permit_lookups`) are not the store.** That table keeps one answer a name, writes over it each month, has no page per fact and no check, and its match can be loose. The run may read it as one source, on an exact match only.
- **No function and no dev-seat verb.** The run is one session signed in as the dev. The row rule holds the write to a dev account.
