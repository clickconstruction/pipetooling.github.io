# v2.7729 — Edit Job: what a row press leaves behind has its own tests (2026-11-17)

Client only; no migration, no edge function, no screen change. Punch list **#46** row 4 (`to-dos/decomposition-queue.md`): the Job form's second pass, cut 4 (the map's row 13), after v2.7718, v2.7721 and v2.7722.

## What shipped

Four reducers in **`lib/jobs/jobFormRows.ts`**, each lifted out of a setter in `JobFormModal.tsx`. The handlers, their names and the setters stay in the form; each now hands its list to a rule.

- `materialRowsAfterRemove` — an *Other job charges* row. With more than one row the pressed row goes. The last row is cleared in place and keeps its id.
- `fixtureRowsAfterRemove` — a line item. The last line is never removed and never cleared.
- `fixtureRowsWithDiscountRow` — *− Add discount*. A lone blank placeholder (no name, no price, not a discount) is replaced by the discount.
- `fixtureRowsWithGeneratedLines` — the segment generator's *Add to job*. A lone row nobody touched is replaced by the lines.

The form drops 13 lines. The value is the tests: these lists are what the billing and materials autosave carry, and none of the four rules had one.

## What the tests pinned, unchanged

- The map's quirk #17 in full: a charge clears, a line refuses, and the payments list (already `paymentRowsAfterRemove`) gets a fresh row.
- The two adds test for a blank placeholder differently. A lone line with a count of 3 and nothing else is kept by the generator and replaced by a discount.
- A lone blank discount row has no content of its own, so the generator replaces it.

## Tests

- `jobFormRows.test.ts`, 23 new cases (33 in the file).
- `JobFormModal.rowRules.render.test.tsx`, 7 cases on the form itself, the generator a stand-in with one press. It passes unchanged on the form before the cut. Six wiring mutants each fail a case. A seventh, removing the last charge where the rule clears it, changes nothing a person can see, and is pinned by the unit test.
