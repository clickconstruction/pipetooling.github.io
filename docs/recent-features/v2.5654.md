# v2.5654 — Bids: a count row's kind, tags and split, the kernels and the columns (2026-10-19)

Punch list #128, PR 2 of the price-breakout train (the plan in `to-dos/bid-price-breakout/`; the owner walked the spike on 2026-10-19 and said build it all). The ground under PR 3, which draws it: nothing changes on a screen here. Migration `20261020020000_bid_count_row_marks.sql` (`docs/migrations/`); no edge function.

- **The columns** (`bids_count_rows`): `kind` (fixture · pipe · fitting · equipment · allowance, checked; null = the app's guess), `tags text[]` (the estimator's own words, never on paper), `split_material_cents` (the row's material as sold, typed for the whole row), `split_markup_pct` (the row's own markup, 60 = +60%) and `split_taxable` (null = taxed when the row has material). Per bid version like the row; `clone_count_rows_to_bid_version` (its 20261014210000 body, `source_shares` kept) and `duplicate_bid_to_service_type` (its 20261002130000 body) are redefined with the five columns in the copy, so a new version and a bid copied to another trade both carry them (`bidCopySql.test.ts` caught the first draft built from an older body).
- **The kind** (`src/lib/bids/rowKinds.ts`): `guessRowKind` reads equipment (rental, lift, shoring…) and allowances out of the name and the rest as `compositionKind` does; `rowKindOf(name, saved)` is the saved kind when known, else the guess, saying which; `cleanTag` lowercases and caps a tag at 40 letters. `pricingComposition.ts` gains the `equipment` and `allowance` buckets (`CompositionBucketKind`), a row's saved `kind` winning over the guess, and `buildTagComposition` (each tag's rows, revenue, cost, margin, share and the weighted material markup; a row with two tags counts in both, `rowsWithManyTags` says so; the untagged under `NO_TAG`).
- **The split** (`rowPriceSplit.ts`): a fixture's price is Material (typed, else the takeoff × 1 + the markup, the row's own markup over the bid's) · Sales tax (the bid's rate on the material while the row is taxable) · Labor (what is left, never typed, so a row always adds up); an Equipment row is all equipment and never a part of a fixture — the owner's call. `sumRowSplits` and `parseSplitDollars`.
- **The card's arithmetic** (`priceBreakdown.ts`): `buildPriceBreakdown` — Material · Sales tax · Equipment · Labor · the small costs (driving, travel, permits, subs) in three columns, Cost · Price · Profit, the Price column the rows' splits when every row has one, the tax we pay on what we buy and the price carrying it on the material as sold, labor's cost hours × the rate (the crew rate standing in since v2.5652) or blank with no hours (*Labor and margin*), `weightedMarkup` across the rows; `priceBreakdownNote` for the line under the card.
- **The reads and the write** (`rowMarksIo.ts`): `loadRowMarks(bidId, versionId)` → the marks by row id, fail-soft before the push (`isNotThereYetError`); `saveRowMarks(rowId, marks)` one row at a time; `rowMarksFromDbRow` / `rowMarksToDbPatch` pure. The payload is cast until the types regen.
- **Tests**: `rowKinds.test.ts`, `rowPriceSplit.test.ts`, `priceBreakdown.test.ts`, `rowMarksIo.test.ts`, `pricingComposition.tags.test.ts` (27 cases: the guesses, a saved kind, the split's four rules and the add-up, the breakdown's columns adding to the strip, the no-hours state, the tag sums and the two-tag row).
- **Docs**: `BIDS_SYSTEM.md`'s count-row columns, `docs/migrations/20261020020000_bid_count_row_marks.md`.
- **Deploy**: merge, then `supabase db push`; the types regen rides with PR 1's.
