# v2.8737 — Money in: a check at the bank says the day the bank says it clears (punch list #437, piece 2) (2026-11-27)

Piece 2 of [`to-dos/held-check/README.md`](../../to-dos/held-check/README.md), *The bank's own day*: the app reads the day the bank expects a pending check, and its check number, and Money in shows them. Client only, no migration and no function. It owes nothing on prod.

## Why

The Dashboard's Money in line drew a pending deposit *At the bank* on a guessed posting day, two bank days after the deposit, with *posts ~Tue*. The bank's own row says more for a check: the day it names for the check to clear, and the number it read off the check. Piece 1 (v2.8525) already read both for the Lien desk. Money in read neither.

## What landed

- **`bankCheckFacts`** beside the bank kernel (`src/lib/jobs/checkCleared.ts`): what the bank itself says of a check, read off its row. The day it posted, the day it names, the number it read, and its words when the deposit failed. `bankCheckFromRow` now reads through it, so the Lien desk and Money in can never differ.
- **The read asks for two more values** (`readPendingDepositRows` in `moneyInDepositsLoad.ts`): `raw.checkNumber` and `raw.estimatedDeliveryDate`, the paths `checkClearedIo.ts` already reads off the same rows of `mercury_transactions`. A read refused with them is asked once more without them, so a seat that may not read them still gets its deposits and its guess.
- **The chip's line says the bank's day** (`pendingDepositDay` and `pendingDepositWords` in `moneyInWeek.ts`), four ways:
  - the bank names no day: *posts ~Tue*, as before.
  - the bank's day is no later than the guess: it takes the guess's place, on the chip and in its words. *the bank says it clears Mon*.
  - the bank's day is after the posting: *posts ~Tue · the bank says it clears Dec 2*.
  - the bank's day has passed and the deposit is still pending: the chip sits on today and reads *the bank named Fri and has not posted it*.
- **The check's number** is the one typed on its payment, else the one the bank read, without its leading zeros. A pending check nobody typed a payment for reads *check #3310* where it read *check deposit*.
- Guide: *see what money is coming in this week* → What the tags mean.

## What differs from the card's words, and why

The card says Money in shows the bank's day *in place of its guess*. The two are different days. The guess is when the deposit posts, and a posted deposit reads *Landed* on this line that day. The bank's day is when the check clears, about a week after the deposit by the card's own read of the bank's rows.

So a chip moved to the bank's day would, most weeks, fall past Sunday and leave this week's line, and a check at the bank would show nowhere on Money in until it posted. This build keeps every chip where it was and adds the bank's day to its line. The bank's day takes the guess's place only when it is no later than the guess.

**Whether the chip itself moves to the bank's day is the owner's to say.** The card already holds pieces 3 to 7 for a redraw on the bank's named day, and that redraw is where the answer belongs. The change would be one line in `pendingDepositDay`.

## What it does not do

- A posted deposit still reads *Landed* on the day it posted. Counting a check on the bank's named day is piece 3.
- The totals on the closed line are worked out as before, from the same chips on the same days, except a check whose bank day is earlier than the guess, and one past its bank day.
- No held check, no badge, no count: pieces 3 to 7.

## Proved, and what is not

- `bankCheckFacts.test.ts` (6): a pending row, a posted row, a failed row, a row with none of the bank's columns, the whole row reading through it, and the two column names.
- `moneyInWeek.test.ts` (7 new): each of the four endings on a built week, a day the bank wrote that is no day, the check's number from the bank and from a typed payment.
- `moneyInDeposits.test.ts` (1 new, 1 amended) and `moneyInDepositsLoad.test.ts` (3 new): the two values carried, the columns asked for, the second ask without them, and the reason when the deposits cannot be read.
- **Nothing was written on prod and no live site was opened.** Every check, payer and day in the tests is made up.
- Not seen: the line on the live Dashboard, which needs a real check pending at the bank. The chip is drawn by the strip as it was. Only its line of words changed, and the kernel tests read those words.
- Not read: what `raw.estimatedDeliveryDate` holds on a pending deposit that is not a check. The kernel takes a day only when it is a real day on or after the deposit.
