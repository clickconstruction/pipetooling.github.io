# v2.5218 — The Bridge reads cash on hand from the bank (2026-10-10)

PR 2 of the Bridge's redraw (plan and mock-up at https://claude.ai/artifact/2GciUJPCjaTysjpTEULWyJ). Client only, dev only. No migration; no new edge function — the page calls Banking's `get-mercury-account-balances` (controller and above), which a dev may call.

- **What was wrong**: the net-position line and the cash forecast started from a figure someone typed into the page and rolled forward through the ledger's bank flows. On 2026-10-10 it was 37 days old (typed Sep 3, rolled to $32.1k), and the Data gaps strip was the only place that said so.
- **The read** (`src/lib/bridge/mercuryCash.ts`): `loadMercuryCash` invokes the function and `parseMercuryCash` turns its body into cash on hand (the total current balance, or the accounts summed) with each account; `mercuryAccountsLine` names the accounts with money and counts the empty ones.
- **The page** (`Bridge.tsx`): reads the bank when it loads and on **Refresh**; `cashToday` is the bank's figure when it answers, else the typed figure rolled forward as before. The cash row reads *Cash on hand $X · the bank's balance at 6:19 PM · <accounts>* with **Read again**; the typed box and **Set** show only when the bank could not be read, with the reason beside them. The cash light (v2.5216) says *Cash on hand is today's bank balance* and never turns amber for a stale typed figure when synced; the Data gaps strip drops its typed line.
- **The drift line**: when a typed figure exists and rolling it forward lands $1,000 or more from the bank, the row says so — *the figure typed Sep 3 rolled through the ledger would read $32.1k: $31.7k of flows since are not in the app*. That is a Fix-the-reading item for PR 4.
- **The live finding, 2026-10-10 18:19 UTC-5**: Mercury's nine accounts hold $390.31 in total (one checking at $368, one at $22, seven at $0). The typed figure rolled to $32.1k. With the bank's number the forecast's lowest point reads $21.3k under the $5k floor for 8 days. Whether Mercury is the whole bank is the owner's to say; the page now reads what the bank says.
- **Tests**: `mercuryCash.test.ts` (2: the parse, the summed fallback, the function's error, the accounts line with empties counted) and a synced case in `headline.test.ts`.
- **Docs**: guide *read the Bridge* — "First, type cash on hand" becomes "Cash on hand comes from the bank"; `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` names the Bridge as a caller; the Glossary's Bridge entry.
