# v2.8577 — Pipeline: the dot that says a customer is waiting now lights (2026-11-25)

Punch list #125. Found by a read of the code on 2026-11-25, not on the live site. No migration, no edge function.

**The fault.** On the Pipeline, a job whose customer wrote and nobody answered should light a dot on its row's speech bubble (v2.5632), draw the bar in the row's activity box and say *Nobody has answered.* None of the three could draw on a real row, from the day each shipped.

**The cause.** Two names for one job.

- The board reads the waiting customers' lines once (`loadBoardDotRows`) through the mapping Follow up uses. That mapping writes a line's job as a job key: `pipeline:<id>`, `bid:<id>` or `project:<id>` (`jobKeyOfRow`, `src/lib/messages/jobKeys.ts`).
- `boardDotsByJob` keys its map by that job key, and `useBoardDots` handed the map on as `dotByJobId`.
- Every reader asks with the row's bare ledger id, `boardDotByJobId.get(job.id)`: the two customer lines in `jobsStagesRowShared.tsx`, the two teasers in `JobsStagesCardList.tsx`, and `JobsStagesActivityBox.tsx`.
- `pipeline:<id>` never equals `<id>`, so every look-up missed.

**Why no test caught it.** The kernel's test fed lines whose job was a bare word, and the rows' tests seeded the map with `job.id`. No test crossed the real mapping and a real row.

**The mend**, in one place and not five:

- `pipelineDotsByJobId` in `src/lib/messages/boardDots.ts` turns each key back with `jobRefOf` and keeps the Pipeline's under their bare id. A dot on a bid's or a project's key is left out, and so is a key that is not a job key.
- `useBoardDots` hands that map on. Its `dotByJobId` now says in its comment which form the key is, and so does `boardDotsByJob`.

The row's other map, `customerLinesByJobId`, comes from a database function asked by ledger id and was never part of this.

A row with `bid_id` or `project_id` set finds none, and neither does a key that is not a job key.
- `useBoardDots.pipelineRow.render.test.tsx`, new: the hook reads through the real loader over a fake client that answers `contacts` and `messages` rows as the database does, and a real Pipeline customer line is drawn from the hook's own map. The dot lights on the tied job with its hover, stays dark on another job, and stays dark for a line tied to a bid.
- `useBoardDots.render.test.tsx` and `useBoardDots.live.render.test.tsx`: their lines now carry a job key, as a real line does.
- With the mend taken out, three of these fail.
