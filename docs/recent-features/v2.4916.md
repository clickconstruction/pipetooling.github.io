# v2.4916 — GC mode: a trade partner’s portal link reads its own asks (2026-10-08)

P1b-ii, its first half, of the trade partner portal's real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/PORTAL_REAL_BUILD.md` on branch `spike/gc-mode`): the read behind the portal page. No database, no screen.

- **`supabase/functions/gc-trade-portal`** (new, `verify_jwt = false`): `GET ?t=<token>[&preview=1]`.
  - It resolves a company's link with the service role, raw token then hash, the sub portal's way.
  - It reads the company's rows, each query held to that company: the company and its people still on its emails, its invites, quotes, quote-day lines, promises, the sets it was sent, the asked projects with their trades, scope lines, known exclusions, plan sets and items, the questions it may read, our people on each project and the emails we sent it.
  - It returns `{ today, slice }` through `_shared/gcTradePortalSlice.ts`, which copies only the fields named in `TRADE_PORTAL_FIELDS`.
  - Errors are keys (`linkOff`, `badRequest`, `failed`). Each load writes `public_page_views` (surface `gc_trade_portal`), stamped outside, staff or preview. Its section is in `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`.
- **`_shared/gcTradePortalSample.ts`** (new): what the sample token answers. It is one made-up company, Sample Electric Co., with one ask bidding and one it passed on, a new set that changed its trade, a quote day it gave, its own question and an answer sent to it, a bookkeeper on its pay emails, our project manager, and the invitation we sent it. The rows are dated from today and go through the same slice builder.
- `supabase/functions/dev-mcp/catalog.ts` lists the new function.
- Tests: `tradePortalSample.test.ts` checks that the sample uses only the slice's fields, stays current, and reads through the mapper and the kernels as one ask bidding and one passed.
