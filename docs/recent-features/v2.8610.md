# v2.8610 — Clock out lists the bids you worked on today (2026-11-25)

Punch list #445, `to-dos/clock-out-bids-of-the-day/`, the third PR of the build and the one the owner asked for: *when a user goes to clock out, we can list the jobs that they worked on that day, but just by reading the app*. Client only. No migration, no edge function. It reads `get_my_bid_day` and writes `save_my_bid_day`, both from v2.8605.

**Before.** The **Clock out** sheet asked *What did you work on?* and **Complete clock out** was dead until the person typed. One estimator's note had a median of one word.

**Now.** For anyone whose day held a bid, and for an estimator every day, the sheet opens with the day read from the app (`ClockOutBidDay.tsx`, the pick of `mockup.html`):

- **Your day**: a strip from the first clock in to now, one colour a bid, and *Grey is time the app did not see.*
- **Bids you worked on today**: the bids the person changed. Each row has a tick in the bid's colour. A press takes the bid off and leaves the row struck through. Another puts it back.
- **Opened, nothing changed**: a bid that was in front a minute or more with nothing of the person's changed. Its empty box ticks it onto the list.
- **Add a bid the app missed**: the bids on the person's Working board, one press each. A bid picked in the sheet's own **Search to choose other job or bid** joins the list too.
- Every row reads the size, then the tab: *3 hours · Takeoffs and Pricing*. A size is rounded to the minute under ten minutes, to five under an hour and to the half hour above.
- With a bid on the list the note is **Anything else?** and may stay empty. The press reads *Complete clock out · 4 bids*.
- On a day the app saw nothing the sheet says so and asks for the note as before.

**What the press keeps.** The clock session's note is the list, one line a kept bid, then what the person typed, so the office reads it where it reads the note today. The list is also kept in `bid_day_entries`, each bid kept or taken off, so a second clock out the same day remembers a tick. That save is best effort and never stands between a person and their clock out.

**How a row is read** (`src/lib/clock/bidDay.ts`, pure):

- *Worked* is a bid the person changed, by the bid history ledger, a note or a price request. The app's own writes, such as the Labor tab's sync on open, do not count as a change.
- A size is the beats the bid collected. Where no beat covers a change, a lone change is a minute, a bucket inside a run of changes is five, and each end of a run is half.
- The tab is where the time went, from the beats, then from the tables changed.

**When the read fails** (no signal, a slow server) the sheet shows no list and works as it did.

**Not in this PR.** Time on the Bid Board names no bid. A salaried person has no clock out, so the My Time card will hold their list. A takeoff in CountTooling is still unseen until a person adds the bid.

**Tests.** `bidDay.test.ts` (23), `ClockInOutButton.bidDay.render.test.tsx` (3: the list and its ticks, the empty day, no list for a helper). The five older clock button tests pass unchanged. Guide: `clock-out-with-the-bids-of-the-day`.
