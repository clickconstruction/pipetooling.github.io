# v2.5413 — Messages: the step a customer is on, with its presses, in one line above the reply box, behind the scenes (2026-10-12)

R4 · 3 of the residential build (`to-dos/gc-mode/messages/RESIDENTIAL_BUILD_PLAN.md` on `spike/gc-mode`, integration 5 in `customer-messages-integrations-mockups.html`): the strip above the box, as a kernel. `stepStrip(person, stepId, jobId, now, opts)` in `src/lib/flows/card.ts` folds the step card to one line. No migration, no edge function, no screen. It stacks on v2.5395 (the residential loader) and reads the residential flow.

**The strip** (`StepStrip`) comes in parts the screen draws side by side, each in plain words: the step's name ("Approved"), whose move ("Dana's move", "Our move", "Waiting on Jonah"), the days ("2 days", or "Late 3 days"), and the presses. The strip is null when the person is off the flow, or when the step is not on now. Each press is the card's `CardPress` with two more fields: what it opens (`dispatch`, `send_estimate`, `send_bill`, `record_payment`, `reply_box`) and what it writes (`StripWrite`), for the screen that does it. The kernel calls nothing.

**A residential step's presses** key on the step they are on now:
- **Approved** (the estimate went, waiting on their yes):
  - **Approve for them** writes what accept-estimate writes: `estimates` from `sent` to `customer_accepted`, `acceptor_consented_at` and `acceptor_printed_name`. The printed name is the office user's, with "by phone" on the audit row's note. There is no IP, browser or signature. `how: 'office'` rides on the write until the edge PR lets accept-estimate take it.
  - **Move the day** opens dispatch.
  - **Send it again** opens the estimate's send.
  - **They declined** writes accept-estimate's decline path, from the office.
- **On the way, Arrived and Working**: Move the day.
- **Paid** (a bill out): **Send the bill again**, and **Mark paid by check** on the oldest bill still out. The second opens the record payment window, which writes through `mark_invoice_paid` with the type 'check' after it asks for the amount, the day and the check number.
- **Review asked** (after Paid): **Ask for a review** fills the reply box, but only while the app's own review send is off.

The labels use their first name ("Approve for Dana", "Dana declined"), never a pronoun. The hover cards are a new `STRIP_TIPS` beside `CARD_TIPS`.

**Every other step**, every GC step among them, folds the card's own presses that move the step, each once: Send it, Ask for it, Remind them, File one we were handed, the rounds' Remind, Ask again and Ask, Remind now, and Send it now. The card's housekeeping stays on the card: Hold until Mon, see it, a note, Skip, See their page and Open.

**In `card.ts` besides**: the status sentence's whose-move reading moved into `moveOnNow`, which the strip shares. The card reads as before.
