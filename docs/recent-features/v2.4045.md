# v2.4045 — Contract sweep: a full-screen button in the title bar (2026-09-28)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. PR 1 of 2: the toggle in the shared shell and the sweep opting in; PR 2 lays the sweep out for the room (the list, the agreement and its paper side by side).

## Why

The owner, 2026-09-28, with a screenshot of the sweep: *a button towards the top that the user can toggle to make it jump between full screen and be a modal.* The sweep is the widest dialog in the app (1100 px) and still scrolls: the agreement's preview sits under the form.

## What shipped

- **[`modalFullScreen.ts`](../../src/lib/modalFullScreen.ts)** (pure, 4 tests): the storage key per modal (`modal_full_screen_<key>`), `readModalFullScreen` / `writeModalFullScreen` (the window state removes the key; a blocked or full store reads false and swallows the write), and the button's two names — *Full screen* in a window, *Back to a window* while full screen. The choice is per modal and per device, not per user: it is a layout preference for a screen, and a shared office machine keeps it for everyone.
- **[`ResponsiveModalShell`](../../src/components/ResponsiveModalShell.tsx)** — a `fullScreenKey` prop. Given, the title bar carries the toggle before `headerAction`, in the ⋯ menu's chrome (`aria-pressed`, the name as its tooltip); the panel takes `.respModalPanelFull` (`100dvh`, no radius, width `100%`) — the sheet phones already get, on a desktop by choice. Phones (≤640 px) do not show the button. `children` may be a function of `{ fullScreen }`, so a modal can lay itself out for the room (PR 2 uses it). One render case: the toggle flips the panel's class and the button's name, remembers the choice, and a shell without the key shows no button.
- **[`ModalFullScreenIcon`](../../src/components/icons/ModalFullScreenIcon.tsx)** — two diagonal arrows, out to the corners in a window and in from them while full screen; `currentColor`.
- **The Contract sweep** opts in with `fullScreenKey="contract-sweep"`. Its layout is unchanged in this PR: full screen is the same two columns with more width.

## Decided here

- **Escape still closes**, as on every modal. The toggle is a layout, not a mode, so leaving full screen is the button's job and nothing else's.
- **Not in the ⋯ menu.** A toggle whose state you cannot see costs two clicks each way; a button shows the state and names the other.
- **The button is the shell's**, not the sweep's: the Lien desk, Put a GC on notice, Accounts Receivable and Balances can take it with one prop when they want it.
