# v2.8512 — Maps: a new address is placed by Google first, with no wait (2026-11-24)

Punch list #436, option 3, on Diane's go of 2026-11-24. The card is `to-dos/openstreetmap-refuses-the-geocode-functions.md`. It holds the read behind this: what the functions sent, what OpenStreetMap's usage policy asks, and the four options.

## Why

OpenStreetMap's address lookup, Nominatim, answered `geocode-address-batch` and `geocode-one` with HTTP 403 on every call. The same request from a Mac, with the function's own `User-Agent`, was answered 200. So the refusal is on where the functions call from, or on the weeks of repeated queries they sent from there before #408, and it cannot be mended from our side. Every address with no saved point went on to Google and the Census anyway. Each one cost a refused request first, and each one after the first in a call waited 1.1 seconds for it.

## What changed

- **The order**: both functions ask the Google Geocoding API first, then the US Census geocoder, and nobody else. The OpenStreetMap request, its `User-Agent` and the 1.1 second wait are gone from [`geocode-address-batch`](../../supabase/functions/geocode-address-batch/index.ts) and [`geocode-one`](../../supabase/functions/geocode-one/index.ts).
- **The rule for a kept miss is the same**: Google and the Census each answered and neither found the address. `isSettledGeocodeMiss` in [`_shared/geocodeMissMemory.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/geocodeMissMemory.ts) takes those two alone now.
- **The words of a miss**: a fresh miss reads *No match from Google; no match from US Census*. It no longer leads with *OpenStreetMap (Nominatim) HTTP 403*. A miss kept before this keeps its old words until it is asked again.
- **No Google key set**: the Census alone is asked, and the failure's `detail` ends *Google Geocoding is not configured*. `geocode-one` answers `not_found`, or `census_upstream` when the Census did not answer. It answered `upstream` for OpenStreetMap's refusal before.
- **`geocode-one`'s `source`** is `google` or `census`. The job form's *On the map* line loses *pinned from the street map*, which no answer can carry now ([`addressPinWords.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/addressPinWords.ts)).
- **`refusePointOutsideUs`** in [`_shared/usPointBox.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/usPointBox.ts) names Google or the Census alone.

## What did not change

- **The maps.** The street map drawn when Google's map cannot load comes from OpenStreetMap's tile server, a different service from the lookup. It is not refused and nothing here touches it. The pins on it are the saved points.
- **The saved points and the remembered misses.** No row is rewritten.
- **Google's count.** With OpenStreetMap refusing, every new address already went to Google. That is about three a working day, under 100 a month, against 10,000 free.

## Given up

A second fallback for the lookup. With Google down or over its count, the Census alone places a new address, and it misses streets newer than its files. OpenStreetMap was not a working fallback either, since it refused every call. To bring it back as a last ask, after Google and the Census, is the card's option 3b, and it needs the block lifted first.

## Still open on the card

- Google's Geocoding policy page lets a point be kept for 30 days, and `address_geocodes` keeps points without end. That stood before this and is its own call.
- Whether to write to OpenStreetMap at all. Not needed now that the functions do not call it.

## Verified

- `src/lib/map/geocodeAddressBatch.run.test.ts` (17) and `src/lib/map/geocodeOne.run.test.ts` (16): each function run whole against a fake database and a fake network. Google is asked first and the Census second. An address Google places never reaches the Census. An address Google misses and the Census places is saved. Both at *not found* keeps the miss. A request to OpenStreetMap is an unexpected fetch in both files, so a case that reached it would fail. Two new addresses in one call run with no pause.
- `src/lib/map/geocodeMissMemory.test.ts`, `src/lib/jobs/addressPinWords.test.ts`, `src/components/jobs/JobFormAddressPin.render.test.tsx`.
- **Not verified in this PR**: the live check. It needs this merged and both functions deployed.

## After the merge

`bash scripts/deploy-functions.sh geocode-address-batch`, then `bash scripts/deploy-functions.sh geocode-one`. Either order with the Pages deploy: the client reads `source` only for the pin line's words, and an answer it does not know reads *pinned earlier*.

`property-lookup`, `owner-confirm-nightly`, `court-precinct-nightly` and `dev-mcp` import the shared files this touches. For them the change is comments and a narrower type, so `check:edge-drift` may name them and a redeploy is optional.

The live check: change a ZZ test job's address to a made-up street address and open the Pipeline. The function log shows one `geocode-address-batch` call and no *OpenStreetMap gave no plain answer* line, and the miss's words name Google and the Census alone.
