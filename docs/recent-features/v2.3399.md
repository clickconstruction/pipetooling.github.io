# v2.3399 — Pipeline on a map, PR 4: crews on the day (2026-09-14)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. The optional fourth PR of the train in [`to-dos/pipeline-on-a-map/`](../../to-dos/pipeline-on-a-map/README.md) — the owner said "build it" after the first three were live. With this the to-do is shipped.

## What shipped

- **Kernel [`jobsMapCrewDay.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobsMapCrewDay.ts)** (+4 tests): a day's clock sessions folded into people per job (`summarizeCrewSessions`; a person on two jobs counts once overall; sessions with no job or no person are ignored), `crewOnPin`, the rail line (`Crews today: 5 people on 4 jobs` · `Crews on Mon Jun 15: …` · `nobody clocked in on these jobs`, counted over the pins the map is showing), the popup line, and the per-device preference (off unless turned on).
- **Loader [`loadJobsMapCrewDay.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/loadJobsMapCrewDay.ts)**: one paged read of `clock_sessions` for the day (`work_date`, with a job, revoked and rejected left out), cached per day for the session.
- **The card**: a **Crews** chip beside As of; on, it reads the shown day — today, or the As-of day when rewound — and a pin with a crew wears the **violet ring** (`#8b5cf6`, apart from every section color and the Collections red; a cluster wears it when any member does, ahead of the red); the popup and the phone bar carry *2 people clocked in here today / that day*; the rail carries the line under the buckets.

## Not changed

The Collections ring still shows on a pin without a crew; with both, the crew ring wins while the layer is on — the layer was asked for — and the popup's Collections chip still says so.
