# v2.5028 — Lien desk: Sent on your word also lists what a standing rule sent (2026-10-09)

Client only. No migration, no function. The owner's call of **2026-10-09** (lien desk punch item G, sent by Punchlist): build the FYI list the standing rule promises.

The rule *Send notices without asking* (`customers.lien_notice_policy = 'send'`) said in its hint (`lienDesk.ts`, `LIEN_NOTICE_POLICIES`) that the leader sees each send *in your FYI list*. The guide's example said the same: *He still sees every send in his list.* No list showed them. The title bar's **Sent on your word** named only the notices the office sent on his spoken word (`approval_mode = 'word'`).

## What changed

- **`lienFyiStrip`** (`src/lib/jobs/lienWord.ts`) words the list:
  - The word sends come first: *Sent on your word: 650, 878*.
  - Then each GC's rule sends, marked as that GC's rule: *by Loberg Contracting’s rule: 702, 715*.
  - With no word sends, the rule part leads: *Sent by Loberg Contracting’s rule: 702*.
  - `wordRecordPreview`'s strip line goes through it, so the record's preview and the bar cannot drift.
- **The desk's title bar** (`LienDeskModal`, the leader only) lists every notice in Ready to send, In the mail or Sent whose `approval_mode` is `word` or `rule`. Before, it listed `word` only, and only in Ready to send or Sent. The hover says both kinds are there.
- **In the mail counts.** The printed pile (*In the mail · tracking owed*, v2.4119) came after the list (v2.3405) and was never added to it. A notice therefore left the leader's list from the day its packet printed until its mailing was recorded. On prod today, every word and rule notice sits in that pile, so the bar showed nothing at all.
- **The guide's example** (`send-lien-notices-from-the-lien-desk`) says where the list is and what a rule send reads. The hint is now true as written, so it is unchanged.
