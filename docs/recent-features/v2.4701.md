# v2.4701 — Legal portal: each exhibit says where it really is (2026-10-06)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. A follow-up to punch list #85 (`to-dos/legal-portal-firm-ready/`), found when the train was checked against the 2026-10-05 review: item 1 gave the firm its own print packet, but the portal still promised documents that packet does not carry.

## What was wrong

The firm's Exhibits list said *in the printed packet* on every exhibit but a signed agreement with a PDF, and the Evidence tab said *Individual reports and sessions come with the printed packet*. The firm packet (`legalFirmPacketPrint.ts`) prints the statement of account, the property record and the record of contact in full, but lists the demand letters by date and tracking only, counts the field reports and clock sessions, and links a lien paper only where a saved copy exists. An attorney who printed it would not find the reports or the letters.

## What changed

- `firmExhibitWhere(title, { agreementPdfs, paperCopies })` in [`legalFirmWords.ts`](../../src/lib/legal/legalFirmWords.ts) words the right-hand cell: *in full in the printed packet* (statement, property record, record of contact) · *listed in the printed packet · ask the office for the letter* (demand letters) · *counted in the printed packet · ask the office for the reports* (field reports) · *copies on the Paper tab* when an envelope has a saved copy, else *listed … ask the office for copies* · *ask the office for a copy* for a signed agreement with no stored PDF.
- [`LegalFirmMatterView.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/legal/LegalFirmMatterView.tsx): the Exhibits cell reads it (signed agreements with a PDF stay links); the Evidence note reads *The printed packet counts the reports and sessions. Ask the office for the reports themselves.*
- Guide `share-your-attorney-their-portal`: the exhibits sentence says the same.

The real fix, the documents themselves both ways, is item 12 of #85, shaped after the firm meeting.
