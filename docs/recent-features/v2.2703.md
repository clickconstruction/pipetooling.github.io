# v2.2703 — Civil dates: "today" is the Central calendar day everywhere (2026-09-03)

## Why

The app's calendar is `America/Chicago`, but a dozen places derived "today" from the UTC clock (`new Date().toISOString().slice(0, 10)` in the client and Edge, `CURRENT_DATE` in Postgres, whose session zone is UTC). Every evening after 7 PM Central (6 PM in winter) those wrote **tomorrow's date** into `date` columns and documents: contract `signed_at`, `paid_on` from Mark Paid, the turnaway trip-charge bill date, the AIA application date, lien filing dates, RFQ "today", partnership entry dates. Two estimate endpoints also expired an estimate at 7 PM Central on its last valid day instead of midnight.

The 2026-09-03 timezone sweep (this session's assessment) classified these; this PR fixes them and adds the guard rail.

## What

- **One helper per runtime**: `todayYmdInAppTz(now?)` in `src/utils/dateUtils.ts` and `supabase/functions/_shared/appTimeZone.ts` (dependency-free; parity pinned by `src/lib/appTimeZoneSharedParity.test.ts`), plus `startOfYmdInAppTzMs` / `endOfYmdInAppTzMs` (DST-aware) for "valid through end of day" math, and `ymdAddDays` on the Edge side. `todayYmdChicago` and `todayPlainDateInAppTz` now delegate to it.
- **SQL twin**: migration `20260903190000_app_today_civil_dates.sql` adds `public.app_today()` and re-creates `create_turnaway_trip_charge`, `mark_job_paid`, `create_billed_shell_invoice` from their live definitions with only `CURRENT_DATE → public.app_today()` changed.
- **Sites moved** (data dates only; filename stamps are exempt): client — Lien filing tabs / instruments modal / prefill / waiver release, AIA G702 application date, Robot queue + reference grade, Quote compare + RFQ desk "today", RFQ urgency end-of-day, License hours CSV "generated on", Partnership timeline default date, ledger bill-date nudge base, People Review report window (company calendar instead of the browser's). Edge — `accept-contract` (`signed_at`), `submit-sub-portal` (offer expiry), `sub-portal` (window), `get-estimate-for-customer` + `accept-estimate` (valid-until through end of the Central day), `twin-mcp` (bid due date).
- **Guard rail**: `npm run check:timezone` now also fails on the UTC-today pattern, on `<ymd> + 'T23:59:59Z'`, and on `CURRENT_DATE` in migrations newer than this one. Waive a deliberate case with `// tz-ok: <why>` on the line; download-filename stamps are skipped automatically. It found two sites the manual grep missed.
