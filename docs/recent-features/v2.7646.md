# v2.7646 — Papers before a job opens: the office picks a job's papers and sees who has signed (2026-11-17)

Papers before a job opens, step 2 of seven: the office's section. It calls the functions of `20261117220000_job_required_papers.sql` (v2.7641), so that migration is pushed before this merges. No migration and no edge function change here.

**The job does not lock at this step.** The section says so in a note until the prompt (step 3) and the lock in the database (step 4) land.

## What is new

- **Papers before this job opens**, on the job window's Documents tab after the Contract section, for the office roles. `JobDocumentsRequiredPapers.tsx` over the kernel `src/lib/jobs/jobPapers.ts` and the io `src/lib/jobs/jobPapersIo.ts`.
- **Add a paper** lists the Contract Book, one entry per named paper, without the customer's contracts and the flows' own papers. A paper with no words is grey and says why.
- **The counted save**: *Require 2 papers* from `countedLabel`. The line under it counts the people the roster read hands back, the person saving included.
- **Sign your own**: after a save, a line asks whoever saved to sign theirs, with **Read and sign** on the first paper they owe. The signing page opens in a new tab and the section reads again on the way back.
- **The roster**: each person against each paper, *signed ✓* with its day or *not yet*. **Copy the signing link** makes the person's copy of each paper they owe (`job_paper_start`), mints each link (`send-contract-for-signature`, link mode) and puts them on the clipboard. A paper whose link fails is named with its reason and the rest still copy.
- **Take off** on a saved paper, behind a confirm that names the act. Signatures are kept.
- **A chip in the job window's head**, *2 papers to sign*, for the office (`JobPapersChip`).
- **Help guide**: `require-papers-before-a-job-opens`.

## Choices worth knowing

- **`JOB_PAPERS_LOCK_STAGE`** in the kernel says how much of the lock is live: `none` now, `prompt` at step 3, `database` at step 4. The note in the section and the line under the save button read it, so no screen claims a lock that is not there.
- **One link per paper.** A person who owes two papers gets two links in one copy, each under its paper's name.
- **A login tied to no roster person has no link.** The row says so and points at People. On 2026-11-17 a handful of real logins were in that state.
- **The section reads again when the tab comes back into view**, so a paper signed in another tab shows without a reload.
