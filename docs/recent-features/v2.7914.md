# v2.7914 — A primary's report email follows their own jobs (2026-11-19)

The follow-up v2.7906 named. That change holds a primary to their own jobs' reports wherever the database knows who is asking (the owner's word of 2026-11-19). Three senders do not ask as anyone: they read with the service role, so no policy reaches them. One migration, pushed after the merge and before five edge functions are deployed.

## Before

- `send-report-email` (both modes), the digest (`_shared/recurringJobReportCore.ts`, read by `recurring-job-report-dispatch`, `-preview` and `-test-send`) and the push (`send-report-notification`) sent a recipient every report in the stream's scope, whatever the recipient's role.
- Nothing leaked on 2026-11-19: prod had one field-report subscription and two digest recipients, all the same master, and a primary cannot subscribe themselves (`can_manage_report_email_subscriptions`). A primary the office subscribed later would have been sent every report.

## What changed

- **The rule about a named user, in one body.** `primary_user_can_read_report(user, job, project, bid, author)` (`20261119060000_primary_reports_named_user.sql`, service role only) holds the rule. v2.7906's `primary_can_read_report` and `primary_can_read_job_reports` become one line each, the same body asked about `auth.uid()`, so the app's read and the mail's cannot part. The account manager test and the bid test of parts 1 to 3 are written for the caller, so the body restates those two comparisons; the migration says why, and the bed holds them together.
- **What the senders ask.** `reports_open_to_user(user, report ids)` answers the ids that user reads: every id unless they are a primary. `reports_open_to_address(address, report ids)` is the same for an outside address, which is narrowed only when it is a primary's own account address, the match My email subscriptions already makes. Both are service role only.
- **One helper for the three senders.** `_shared/primaryReportScope.ts`: `reportsOpenToRecipient`, and its two shapes `rowsOpenToRecipient` (one recipient, many reports) and `recipientsWhoReadReport` (one report, many recipients, the shape of `recipientsNotShut`).
- **Field report emails.** `auto` asks before it reads the photos and leaves a subscriber out (`outOfScope`). `manual` leaves the reports out and counts them (`skippedOutOfScope`). A report left out is never written to `report_email_dispatch_log`.
- **The digest.** The bundle is one recipient's, so the core keeps the reports that recipient reads. A job that had only other reports in the window drops out. The preview and the test send show the same.
- **The digest's crew time.** Under each job the digest lists the crew's hours, their clock notes and, when the office ticked it, what the hours cost. `report_jobs_open_to_user(user, job ids)` (the same migration, service role only) answers the jobs whose crew time a recipient may be sent: every job unless they are a primary, and then the jobs whose reports they read, as account manager or listed superintendent. The core keeps the clock sessions on those jobs (`rowsOnJobsOpenToUser`). A job a primary only wrote a report on keeps that report in their copy, with no crew time under it.
- **The push.** A primary holds a `user_report_notification_preferences` row only as a leftover from a role change, since the policy lets dev, master, assistant and controller keep one. They can neither see nor remove it, and the push names the author and the job. So the push takes the same filter.
- **When the question fails.** The helper does not guess the rule. It reads who the primaries are. A recipient who is not one is sent everything, as before. A primary, or anyone when that read fails too, is sent nothing, and one line is logged. A held report is not logged as sent, so the next send picks it up.
- **The editor and the guide.** **Send now** says how many reports it left out and why (`sendNowWords`, `emailReportPeople.ts`). *Email reports to owners and managers* gains **What a primary is sent**.

## What it leaves alone

- Every recipient who is not a primary, and every outside address that is not a primary's own.
- **Send now**'s cap: it takes the 50 newest reports of 14 days before the rule, so a primary may be sent fewer than 50.

## The calls made, for the owner

1. A role that is not primary is not narrowed by the mailers, whatever it reads in the app. The office picks who a stream goes to.
2. A primary's own account address typed as an outside address counts as that primary.
3. The push is filtered although no primary can keep a preference on purpose.
4. The digest's crew time goes with the jobs whose reports a primary reads, the account manager's and the listed superintendent's. The owner's sentence names reports, not hours, so this is a call. The narrower choice is the account manager alone, one line in `report_jobs_open_to_user`.
