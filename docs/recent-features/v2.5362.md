# v2.5362 — Messages: press and hold a message to tie it to a job on a phone (2026-10-12)

Cut 10's P3 for the Messages build (GC Reviews helper 7, directed by the lead session GC Review): screen 1b of `to-dos/gc-mode/messages/phone-mockups.html` on `spike/gc-mode`, the prototype's `tieMenu`. Stacked on P2, v2.5361, whose `PhoneSheet` it opens in.

- **`src/lib/messages/tieSheet.ts`** (new): `tieSheetRows(rail, tiles, defaultJobId, lineJobId)` lists the person's jobs, the starred one first and then the rail's order. The line's own job is marked *here*, and comes first when the rail does not hold it. A job with no tile is left out, and `untie` is true for a tied line.
- **`ThreadLine.tsx`** takes `onHold`, and only a phone's thread passes it. `useLongPress` (450 ms) fires as the finger lifts, so the sheet never opens under the finger, and the lift's click opens nothing else.
  - The line darkens once the hold is long enough (`data-holding`). A finger that travels past `useLongPress`'s 10 px is a scroll and clears it.
  - A holding line keeps the phone's own text menu shut.
- **`ThreadWindow.tsx`**: *Tie this message to* is a `PhoneSheet` with the rows, *Another job…* (main's job picker), *Untie it* for a tied line (it says the message stays on the person), and Cancel.
  - A pick writes through main's own tie.
  - On a phone, *no job* opens the same sheet. At a desk, *no job* opens the picker and a hold does nothing, as before.
- **Guide**: `find-every-message-with-a-person-in-one-place`'s *Tie a message to a job* says how to hold a message on a phone.
- **Tests**: `tieSheet.test.ts` covers the order, the marks and the untie flag. `ThreadWindow.hold.render.test.tsx` covers:
  - the darkening, the sheet on the lift, and a tie and an untie;
  - *no job* and *Another job…*;
  - a short press opening the line, and a scroll opening nothing;
  - the desk unchanged.

  jsdom has no `PointerEvent`, so the test stands one in, as `HubPeopleDayCell.gestures` does.
