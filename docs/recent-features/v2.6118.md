# v2.6118 — The job window's lien papers name the trade as the kind of work, not the job's name (2026-10-30)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. v2.4719 found the Lien desk's affidavit swearing *the kind of work … was: Bruce Hall* (job 838, named for its customer) and moved the desk's draft to the trade (`lienTradeWords(serviceTypeName).laborMaterials`); its fragment says the Lien window's affidavit was fixed alike, but the job window's own Mechanic's lien tab kept `workDescription: job.job_name || 'Plumbing labor and materials'`, and its § 53.056 notice kept a static *Plumbing labor and materials*. Found 2026-10-29 while fixing the affidavit's period (v2.6061), left as its own fix, done here.

## What changed

- **`LienFilingTabs.tsx`** reads `lienTradeWords(job.serviceType?.name).laborMaterials` once (`laborMaterials`) and puts it in both papers: the affidavit's `workDescription` (¶4) and the notice's `laborMaterialsType` (*Type of labor or materials provided*). A row with no trade reads plumbing, as the kernel always has.
- **`LienInstrumentsModal.tsx`** hands the tab a row whose `serviceType` comes from the board row, else from the full job it reads on open (`fetchJobWithDetailsById`, whose select embeds `service_types(name)`): the board's rows carry no `serviceType`, so without this the tab would have named plumbing on every job.
- **Removed** `fetchLienClockColumns` and `LienClockColumns` from `useLienDeskData.ts`: no caller since the timeline book's one select (v2.3789); the desk reads the clock columns in `LIEN_DESK_JOB_COLUMNS`.
