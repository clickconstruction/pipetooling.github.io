## What moved

**`src/hooks/useTeamSummaryData.ts`** (new, 320 lines) takes region C's state and everything that writes it out of `PeopleReviewTab.tsx`, verbatim:

- the rows, their loading and error, and the request-id ref
- the `reviewOverheadRatesRef` mirror the popup reads its rates through
- the auto-refresh effect: the cache dropped on any change, the empty-roster reset, the waits for a pay config and a whole custom range, the drilldown deferral, and the 200 ms wait
- `loadTeamSummaryData`, the cache key and `openTeamSummaryWindow`, both the inline load and the Open in new window popup

The page's five bridge refs and `teamSummaryDrainTick` go in as they are. So do `categoryTags`, `getReviewDateRange` and `getReviewPeriodLabel`, which the tab keeps for its other readers. Every input is passed by its own name, and the tab destructures the same four names (`teamSummaryRows`, `teamSummaryLoading`, `teamSummaryError`, `openTeamSummaryWindow`), so nothing below the call changed.

Only one moved line changed: the rates ref's comment opened "Same idiom", pointing at the roster mirror it no longer sits under.

`PeopleReviewTab.tsx` goes from 856 to 637 lines.

The hook's effect now runs before the tab's office-like and prior-period effects, where it ran after them. The three share no state or ref, so the order cannot be seen.

## Tests

`useTeamSummaryData.render.test.tsx` (new, 12), on the hook over a recording union loader with fake timers:

- it waits 200 ms, loads the roster's rows once, and stamps the popup's cache with the key from before the load
- a change inside the 200 ms starts the wait again: one load, with the latest inputs
- any change drops the popup's cache at once
- an older load that lands late is dropped
- a failed load shows its message and stops loading
- an empty roster clears the rows, the error and the pending flag
- nothing loads for a non-dev, with no pay config, or with half a custom range
- an open drilldown holds the refresh until the drain tick
- the Hours drilldown opens again 50 ms after the rows land
- Open in new window:
  - it reuses the inline rows, with no second load and no loading toast
  - without a cache it loads afresh and reads the rates as they are when the rows land
  - with no roster it warns, and a blocked popup says so

Mutations fail them:

- the newest-answer guard removed (1 test)
- the cache kept on a change (3)
- the drilldown deferral removed (1)
- the rates read from the click-time closure (1)
- no 200 ms wait (2)
- no Hours re-open (1)
- the cache stamped with another key (4)
