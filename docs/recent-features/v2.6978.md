# v2.6978 — Permits: a San Antonio permit's owner read at the permit's point (2026-11-11)

Punch list #320, PR 1. Since v2.6912 four counties are asked for the parcel under the permit's point when the street address finds none. Bexar, the first county built and the home market, was still asked by the street address alone.

## What was measured (counts only, 2026-11-11)

San Antonio's feed, the 75-day window, building permits, one row per address, through the lookup's own code against Bexar County's live layer.

- 240 addresses that have a point. Commercial: the address finds a parcel 31 times of 82, the point 81 of 82. Houses: 49 of 158 against 158 of 158.
- Of the 159 the address missed, the parcel under the point is the same number and street written another way 18 times (LANE for LN, "N 1604 W" for "N LOOP 1604 W", a direction on one side only), the same street with another number 84 times, another street 57 times.
- Where both find a parcel they are the same one 75 times of 80. In Austin, where the permit's own parcel id settles it, the city's point sat on the named lot 576 times of 577.
- Through the new kernel, 300 addresses with or without a point: commercial cards with an owner go from 50 of 101 to 95, houses from 52 of 199 to 193. 11 of the 12 left have no point.

Not checked against anything independent: that the parcel under a San Antonio point is the permit's own lot. Bexar's permits carry no parcel id. The card says the parcel was read at the point and names the county's address for it.

## What changed

- **Kernel**: `parcelFactsFromCountyAtPoint` in `_shared/permitLookup.ts` (the first parcel with an owner, `matched_by: 'point'`). In `_shared/permitParcels.ts`, `pointQueryOf(outFields, point)` is the point question for any layer by its columns (`pointQuery` calls it), `pointQuestion` is the words a kept `query` holds for it, and `askedWithoutPoint` says a kept row was asked before the point could be.
- **`permit-lookup`**, Bexar's branch: the street address as before, then the point when the address finds no parcel. With no usable address the point alone is asked. The kept `query` holds both questions joined by ` | `.
- **Kept answers**: a kept "no parcel" from before, with no point question, is asked again once when the permit has a point. A kept failure is left to its month.
- **Words**: none added. `lotWords` and `parcelPointWords` already say *read at the permit's point* and name the county's address.
- The card `to-dos/permits-lookup-as-a-whole.md` (#320) is new: every city measured, and the train this PR opens.

Tests: `permitLookup.test.ts` (the parcel at the point, the address answer left unmarked), `permitParcels.test.ts` (the point question by columns, the kept-answer rule). Every name and number is made up.

Deploy: `permit-lookup` after the merge, from `main`. `permit-pull` bundles the two shared files too and runs the same code. No migration.
