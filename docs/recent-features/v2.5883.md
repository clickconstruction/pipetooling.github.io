# v2.5883 — The Monday sheet mails itself every Monday (2026-10-26)

PR 4 of the Bridge train and the second of the owner's two asks of 2026-10-26 (v2.5879 the landing, v2.5880 the loaders take the client, v2.5881 the sheet as one kernel): the weekly send v2.5224 left unbuilt. One new edge function, `bridge-weekly-sheet`, deployed after the merge; two migrations.

- **The function** (`supabase/functions/bridge-weekly-sheet/index.ts`; `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` → bridge-weekly-sheet): on Monday at 6 AM Central it builds the sheet the page shows — `buildSheetFromReads` over `loadMondaySheetReads` from the copy of the kernels, as the service role — and mails it through Resend to every dev with a real account, each to their own address (`weeklySendRecipients`: dev, not archived, not a sample, a twin or the walk tester, an address, each once), never to anyone else, as `bridge_weekly_sheet` (`EMAIL_CATALOG`, 56 entries). The bank is read from Mercury with the API key (`_shared/mercuryAccounts.ts`, the read `get-mercury-account-balances` makes, shared now); ZZ test jobs stay out. `{ "dry": true }` from a dev or the cron secret builds the sheet and answers the recipients, the subject, the text and the html, what could not be read and each read's error, sending and writing nothing: the way to see the mail before turning the switch on. The run keeps its record in `app_settings` `bridge_weekly_sheet_last_run_v1` and sends nothing twice on one day.
- **The switch** (`src/lib/bridge/weeklySend.ts`, `weeklySendIo.ts`, `BridgeWeeklySend.tsx`): under the sheet on `/bridge?view=monday`, **Weekly email**, On after a confirm, Off at once, the line under it saying what happens and when the sheet last went and to how many people. `app_settings` `bridge_weekly_sheet_on_v1`, `'false'` as it ships, so nothing mails until a dev turns it on; only a dev may change it. The kernel holds the tick (`weeklySendTickNow`: a Monday, 6 AM Central, whichever of the two UTC ticks that is), the run's parse and the words.
- **Migrations**: `20261026120000_bridge_weekly_sheet.sql` (the two rows, the dev-only policy, the cron `bridge-weekly-sheet` at `0 11,12 * * 1` posting only while the switch is on) and `20261026121000_bridge_sheet_rpcs_service_role.sql` (five RPCs the sheet reads let the service role through their gate, bodies verbatim: the pay speeds, the promises, the chase touches, the tally parts, the unattributed count; without it the service role's sheet would read every bill at the default pace and lose the promises, the touches and the office parts). `count_unlinked_mercury_transactions_for_tally` stays per caller and reads as "not read".
- **Tests**: `weeklySend.test.ts` (the tick in summer and winter, the switch, the run's parse, the once-a-day rule, the recipients, the words), `BridgeWeeklySend.render.test.tsx` (on, off, the confirm, off without one), `edgeKernels.test.ts` names the function as an importer, `emailCatalog.test.ts` counts 56. Guide *read the Bridge* gains "The weekly email". `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`, the page map's §21, the Glossary and `docs/twins/APP_DIRECTORY.md` say the sheet mails itself.
- **Deploy** (after the merge, in this order): `supabase functions deploy bridge-weekly-sheet --no-verify-jwt` and `get-mercury-account-balances` (the shared read), the copy's index importers `check:edge-drift` names, then `supabase db push` for the two migrations, then the dry run as a dev. The switch stays off until the owner turns it on.

## Verify

As a dev, POST `{ "dry": true }` to `/functions/v1/bridge-weekly-sheet` with your session: `to` lists every dev's address and nobody else's, `subject` reads the three leads, `html` is the sheet, `gaps` is empty. On the Bridge's Monday sheet, **Weekly email** reads Off with *Not sent yet.*; turn it on: the confirm, then On. The next Monday at 6 AM the sheet is in each dev's inbox and the line reads *Last sent Nov 2 to N people.*
