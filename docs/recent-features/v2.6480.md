# v2.6480 — The rules for bringing back a teammate who left (punch list #256, PR 1) (2026-11-03)

Punch list #256's first PR. The owner, 2026-11-03: a technician finished their last day and plans to come back in three years. End employment closes a person out well, but the way back was a dev's undo: Restore brings back the login only, the one start and end date pair gets written over, old roadmap tasks come back by themselves, and + Hire does not see archived people. The card (`to-dos/bring-back-a-teammate/`) draws the Bring back window and the owner answered its calls the same day. This PR is the kernel; PR 2 is the stints table and `restore-user`'s roadmap flag, PR 3 the window, PR 4 Hire's check, PR 5 the tenure readers.

## What changed

- **The kernel** `src/lib/people/bringBack.ts` (tested in `bringBack.test.ts`):
  - `buildBringBackChecklist(facts)`: the returner's rows in the window's order. The login and roster row (informational), the sign-in email (`confirm_sign_in`: anyone confirms it is still theirs; Change… and the email stay a dev's), the wage (`confirm_wage`), the leader (`assign_leader`, suggesting the former leader by name only while they are still active), licenses (red when one lapsed before the day back), paperwork (signed in the old stint does not count), vehicle and housing (optional). Sign-in, wage and leader cannot be left open; licenses and paperwork can, with a reason.
  - `awayWords` / `awayLine`: the largest whole calendar unit (*3 years*, *11 months*, *2 weeks*), and *Away 3 years · first stint Mar 4, 2024 to Nov 3, 2026*.
  - `roadmapBoxDefault` / `roadmapBoxWords`: the old-roadmap-tasks box starts ticked only within `ROADMAP_RETURN_DAYS` (90).
  - `bringBackHrLine` / `undoEndHrLine`: the dated HR-file lines, the mirror of `endEmploymentHrLine`.
  - `findArchivedMatch` / `archivedMatchWords`: + Hire's check against archived people by name (spaces and case aside) or email, a match on both first, then the latest leaver; and the amber line's words.
  - `bringBackButtonLabel`: *Bring back · 5 open*, then *Bring back*.
- **`lifecycleChecklist.ts`**: `applyChecklistResolutions` and `checklistSummary` are generic over the row shape, so End employment's helpers resolve and count the returner's rows too. No caller changes.

## Decisions

- **The owner's calls, as constants and rules**: tenure counts from the day back (PR 5; nothing here reads stints), `ROADMAP_RETURN_DAYS = 90`, the wage row is never leaveable, `RETURNER_TRAINING_MODE_DEFAULT = false`.
- **Rows of their own type, not new kinds on `LifecycleItem`.** Start employment's kinds and actions stay as they are; the window in PR 3 renders `BringBackItem` beside them through the shared, now generic, helpers.
- **The sign-in row is confirmable by anyone who may bring back.** A dev-only row would stop a controller or a pay-approved leader from finishing; changing the email or sending the sign-in email stays a dev's, as on the Access section.
