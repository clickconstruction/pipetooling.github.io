# v2.6275 — My email: the gmail-mine function (2026-11-01)

Punch list #230 PR 2 (`to-dos/messages-my-email/README.md`), on PR 1's kernels and migration (v2.6266): the edge function **`gmail-mine`**, which the My email tab (PR 3) calls to read the signed-in person's own Inbox and Sent live and to put a whole Gmail conversation on a job or a bid. It reads Gmail as that person under the delegation the bids@ reader already holds (`gmail.modify`), as their own `users.email` and no other address, and keeps nothing until a tag; the owner's calls of 2026-11-01 hold as PR 1 built them. Nothing shows yet, and the function deploys after PR 1's migration is pushed. Its section is [`docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` → gmail-mine](../EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md#gmail-mine).

- **The function**, [`supabase/functions/gmail-mine/index.ts`](../../supabase/functions/gmail-mine/index.ts): one POST of the contract's `MyMailRequest`, every answer the contract's with the CORS headers. The caller is gated as `messages-email` gates one (signed in; never a sample, a twin or the walk tester; `gc_office_team()` asked as them; `users.my_mail_on` on), and a training account lists and opens but never tags. An address Google's token exchange refuses (a 400, 401 or 403, `unauthorized_client`, `invalid_grant`) answers `offWorkspace` with Google's reason; any other Google failure `google` (502); a step the database refuses mid-way 500.
  - `list`: one page of 50 under a fenced search (Inbox or Sent, never Drafts, Spam, Trash or Chats, never a hidden label, the person's own search as one group, Tagged and To tag narrowed by the tag's label), each mail read whole into `readMyMail`'s row, then `tagged` from `email_tag_threads`, `inMessages` from the lines holding its `Message-ID`, and the guess from the subject's numbers, the party's one contact's star or one open job, and a customer's domain with one open Pipeline job; `viewRows` answers the view's rows and its fold count.
  - `open`: the mail whole with its addresses, words, HTML and files, and its conversation as rows.
  - `tag`: `planTag` over the conversation, then a held line tied through `tie_message` as the caller, any other mail placed, its files copied under `mine/<user>/<gmail id>/`, its line written through `email_write_line` (`mailbox` `mine`, `mail_user_id`, the job, the invitation `takeOne` reads), its raw mail kept as `mine/<line id>.eml`; then the `email_tag_threads` row and the Gmail label *Tagged in ClickTooling*, and `unknownSender` when a new contact was made. A second tag writes nothing twice; a tag onto another job moves the person's own lines and the row.
  - `untag`: the person's own lines, files and raw copies go, a box's line is untied only when the tag tied it, the row goes last and the label comes off. `keptForMatter` stays `false` until PR 5.
- **Its pure half**, [`src/lib/myMail/mineFunction.ts`](../../src/lib/myMail/mineFunction.ts) with its test: the request parser, the search, the refusal statuses and words, the HTML part, the open-job rules (a bid with no outcome, a GC project neither closed nor lost, a Pipeline job not paid), the guess's facts, the line's row, the bucket paths and the untag's plan. `mailGuess.ts` exports its chip helper as `subjectJobChip` so a tagged row's chip and a guess's read one way.
- **The Gmail calls** added to [`_shared/gmail.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/gmail.ts): `gmailMessagePage` (a page of a search with its size), `gmailMessageRaw` (`format=raw`), `gmailThreadFull`, `gmailThreadHeaders` (`format=metadata` with the headers named) and `gmailRemoveLabel`.
- **The copy**: `scripts/edge-kernels.mjs` gains the six `lib/myMail/` entries (`emptyWords.ts` comes in under `view.ts`) and `gmail-mine` joins `IMPORTERS`, so every mirrored kernel's header changes and `check:edge-drift` names each importer; `supabase/functions/_shared/gcKernels/` is regenerated. The dev-mcp catalog is regenerated for the new function.
- **Tests**: `mineFunction.test.ts` (the kernel), and `gmailMine.run.test.ts`, the handler run under vitest on `gmailMineFakes.ts`: the real token code signs with a key made for the run against a fake Google behind `fetch`, and a fake database tells the caller's client from the service role's. It covers the refused sample, twin and walk tester, the switch off, the dev's address answering `offWorkspace` with only that address asked for, a training account, a page with a tagged row, a held row, three guesses and a folded newsletter, the search sent, an open, a three-mail tag with one file, a second tag, a tag onto another job, an untag, and a line the database will not let go.
- **Docs**: `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` (the section, its TOC line, the copy's paragraph), `MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md` (Email in, and the edge's list), `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` (who the function lets in, in the My email paragraph).
