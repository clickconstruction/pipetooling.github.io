# v2.8181 — Permits: the warm, cold or new name word counts the same bids as Market (2026-11-21)

Punch list #405 ([the card](../../to-dos/permits-market-page/README.md)). Seen while fixing the call list's count in v2.8138 and left on the card: **the skim card's *warm · cold · new name* word still counts every bid ever made.** Taunya said to build it on 2026-11-21.

## What was wrong

Under who pulled a permit sits one word from our bids with that name: **warm**, **cold**, **seen** or **new name**. It reads `research_contact_history`, which kept its own count: every bid ever made under the name, a digital twin's practice bids among them.

Builders and Market count by one rule since v2.8138 and v2.8147. So one name could read *Never bid* on Market's call list and **cold** on the skim card.

## The fix

`research_contact_history` counts by the same rule, through the same two functions `research_builders` calls ([the migration's page](../migrations/20261121152000_research_contact_history_our_bids.md)).

- **A bid counts** when it is no practice bid and was made within two years, by the day on the company calendar.
- **It counts under** its customer's name and the old builders list's name on it. Never under a ZZ name or a bid platform's bucket.

The function returns the same columns, so no read in the client changed. Two things in the client did:

- **The words for new name.** The hover read *no bid to this name in our history*, which is no longer what the count says. It reads *no bid to this name in the last two years* (`hintFor` in `permitResearch.ts`).
- **The test that holds the rule together.** `ourBids.test.ts` already held `research_builders` to the kernel. It now holds `research_contact_history` too, so a later migration that rewrites the function with its own count fails CI.

## What the office sees

- On **Skim**, the ledger and a permit's sheet, the word counts the bids of two years with no practice bid.
- A name with only old bids or practice bids reads **new name**.
- A name with three bids and no win reads **seen** when fewer than three of them still count.
- No name that reads **warm** today loses the word. A read of prod before the push, counts only, said so.
- **On the deck** is as it was.

The guide says the word counts the same bids as Market does.

## The order

One PR: the migration, the words and the test. The client calls nothing new, so no deploy waits on the push. For the minutes between the merge and the push the hover says *two years* over the older count.

## The bed

`permit_hint_decks` gained the rule's cases with made-up names. Its runner re-applied the one migration that wrote the function. It now re-applies both in order, since the first alone would put the old body back and the bed would test that one.
