## Before

The short form names the months the job's recorded § 53.056 notices name, cut to the job's own work span. On a job with no clock hours and a last day set by hand, a notice recorded for several jobs at a property that names earlier months leaves the paper blank, and since v2.7266 says why. Counsel's word on those jobs was that they may rely on the notice because its total included them; the app could not express that. The owner picked door 1: a person picks the months and types counsel's reason, as the claim set by hand works.

## What changed

- **`job_lien_affidavit_months`**: one row per job, `months text[]` as `YYYY-MM`, a non-blank `reason`, who and when; RLS for the desk's roles as `job_lien_claim_corrections`, and the four house fences.
- **[`src/lib/jobs/lienAffidavitMonthsByHand.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienAffidavitMonthsByHand.ts)**, a pure kernel: the row's shape, the months cleaned, a typed month three ways (`2026-09`, `9/2026`, `Sep 2026`), the editor's choices with where each month comes from (worked, on the notice, both, added by hand), the save press's words, the pane's line (*Months set by hand: April and August 2026 · Robert · Nov 14 · reason*) and the help sheet's check row. [`lienAffidavitMonthsByHandIo.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienAffidavitMonthsByHandIo.ts) reads, saves and clears.
- **`lienAffidavitSwornMonths`** takes `monthsByHand`: when any, the paper swears them in place of the record's, so `buildLienAffidavitFieldsForJob` prints them and the notice check reads each against the record (a month with no notice still raises the attorney line).
- **The desk** reads the rows in full mode (`useLienDeskData` → `affidavitMonthsByJob`) and hands them to the pane, the paper preview behind a To do row, the stop's paper and the batch help sheet (`checked.monthsByHand`, a check row *Months set by hand.* with the reason). The pane draws [`LienAffidavitMonthsByHandEditor`](../../src/components/jobs/LienAffidavitMonthsByHandEditor.tsx) under *Months the affidavit claims*: **Set the months by hand…**, the ticks, *Another month*, counsel's reason, **Set N months by hand**, then **Change…** and **Clear**. The office presses.
- **The Lien window** reads the job's row beside the claim set by hand and names it under the gates; its paper prints the months.
- **The dev seat's `plan_affidavit_draft`** reads the row and replies `months_set_by_hand`.
- The guide *file a lien and never miss its deadlines* says how; `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` names the table.

## Not changed
