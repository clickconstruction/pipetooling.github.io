# v2.8404 — Smaller cents in Banking and Pay (2026-11-23)

Part 2 of the owner's ask of 2026-11-23, the cents smaller than the rest of an amount across the app: the Banking and Pay surfaces. The door is v2.8374's `Money` and `MoneyText`, and the sweep is v2.8376's `scripts/codemods/small-cents-sweep.mjs`, run and not edited by hand. No migration, no edge function.

## What moved

27 screen files under `src/components/banking` and `src/components/pay`: 35 plain amounts to `Money` and 65 pieces of words to `MoneyText`. The characters on the screen are the same. `scripts/codemods/small-cents-swept/banking-pay.txt` lists the two folders, so `check:small-cents` now refuses a bare amount written there.

Most of Banking draws its amounts through `formatUsd` and its signed kin, which the script read from their own bodies and wrapped in `MoneyText`. `RecordPayStubPaymentModal` carries its own `formatCurrency` that writes the `$`; the script read that body too and wrapped it, rather than trusting the name.

- Strings built into a `const` or handed to a prop: the Draft Payroll window's summary line and its bars, a transaction's *Amount* fact, the ledger's job count line, the forecast's allowance line. The close-out's hand pass takes these.
- Hours drawn to two places, and the boxes a person types an amount in.
