# v2.3369 — Sent bids get their labor hours from the book (2026-09-12)

PR 3 of the Costs-tab refresh (owner: "zero people who have been bidding have been adding in labor… collect labor baselines instead of guessing"). Migration [`20260912053412_labor_rows_from_book_on_send.md`](../migrations/20260912053412_labor_rows_from_book_on_send.md).

## What changed

The Labor tab has always minted a bid's labor rows from its count sheet with the book's hours — but only when someone opened the tab, and 187 sent bids in 180 days had 57 count sheets, 36 sets of rows and 7 with hours. `mint_labor_rows_from_book(bid)` does that mint in SQL, with the same rules (the bid's book, else the first by name for its trade; hours by fixture-type name, alias, then `fixture_labor_defaults`; a zero row keeps no source and waits in the New view's queue; a row a person touched is never overwritten). The `bids_mint_labor_on_send` trigger runs it the first time `bid_date_sent` is set; the migration back-filled every sent bid with a count sheet. No client change: the Labor tab, Bid vs actual, and the job budget snapshot read the rows they would have made.

## What this does not do

It does not invent hours for fixtures the book has never seen — those rows read *none* and sit in the Labor tab's queue for a person. It does not touch bids without a count sheet (130 of 187); the Jobs baseline tile (v2.3367) is the book's answer for those.
