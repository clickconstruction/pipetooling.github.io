# v2.8313 — Papers: the ground for a sub's company, with its workers under it (2026-11-22)

Punch list #403, step 5, the ground. A migration and its bed. No screen, no client change and no edge function. The owner settled calls 3 and 7 on 2026-11-19: a sub's business is a company record of its own with its workers under it, and that record is the trade company the app already keeps.

## What changed

- **A roster person can work for a trade company.** A new table, `person_trade_companies`, holds one row a person: the company, and what they are there. A sub and each of their workers point at the same company.
- **Two doors for the office.** `person_trade_company_set` links a person to a company the app has, moves them, or takes them off. `person_trade_company_enter` finds a company by its name or makes it, and links the person in the same press. A press repeated after a failure makes no second company.
- **The office makes a company's paper.** `gc_company_paper` asked for a dev. It asks for the office's four roles now: a dev, a leader, an assistant and the controller.
- **A person's paper says who they sign for.** `signing_for` is filled from the link when the paper is made, and again each time it is sent. An unsent paper follows the link. A paper that is out keeps what it said until it is sent again. A signed paper never changes.

## Who can do what they could not before

- A leader, an assistant and the controller can make a trade company's copy of a Contract Book entry. Only a dev could.
- The four roles can say which company a roster person works for. Nobody could.
- Nobody else gains a read or a write. An estimator is refused both doors and `gc_company_paper`, and reads no row of the new table.

## What stays as it was

- No read policy on an older table changes. An estimator reads a trade company's own paper, as since v2.7977, and no person's paper, whether or not it names a company.
- Sending a company's paper by email is still a dev's. That gate is in the send function, and its PR is next.
- `gc_send_paper` and `gc_record_company_coi` are still a dev's.

## Why the link is a table

The roster's read policies are wide, so a column on `people` would have shown a superintendent and a banking reader something new. The roster's update policy would also have let a row's owner write it. A table with one policy does neither.

## Why triggers keep `signing_for`

A person's copy of a job's paper is made unsent, and can be signed from a prompt or from the sub's own page with no send between. Only the database sees every one of those writes, so the freeze is there and not in the send function.

## Proof

- The bed `sub-company-ground` (`supabase/tests/sub_company_ground/20_scenario.sql`), on the whole schema in CI. It runs as each role through row security: who links and who is refused, who reads the link, every case of `signing_for`, who makes a company's paper, the one-press door, and that each role reads the papers it read before.
- The bed `gc_papers` changes one line, the words an estimator is refused with.
- Docker is not available on the machine that wrote this, so CI's run of the bed is its proof.
