comment promised R7's count. Its press opens `/messages?kind=customer`, where the Customers pill hides those senders.

  (`followUpNewLane(input, rows.jobStates, nowIso)`). So a person waiting to be sorted leaves Needs a reply here

  people of the line's own pill, `CUSTOMERS_PILL` through `kindPills.inPill`, so the line, the New the press lands on,
  and Sort all there say one number. A sender nobody has named waits in New under All and is never counted on the
  Customers line.
