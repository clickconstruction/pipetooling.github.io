# v2.3950 — GC Review: one list for the week, worked by whoever is at the keyboard (2026-09-27)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Step 1 of punch list **#49** (`to-dos/gc-review-one-operator/`): the owner found that only assistants drive GC Review — the round handed each GC to an account man who does not open the app.

## What shipped

- **`src/lib/jobs/gcWorklist.ts`** (pure, 18 tests):
  - `buildGcWorklist({ groups, certsByGc, marks, senders, accountMen, lastSentByGcId, weekStartYmd })` → groups of rows and `counts { gcs, checked, sent, words, done }`. Every real GC with money outstanding is a row — the same set the week strip always counted — not only the GCs assigned to the signed-in user. Groups: one per account man (largest total first), then `unassigned`, then `under_line` (under `GC_ROUND_THRESHOLD`, where the word is optional). Rows with a step left sort first.
  - `worklistNextStep` — check, then send, then the word; a word taken early never skips the statement; a skipped GC asks for nothing.
  - `markCarriesWord` — a `contacted` mark, or any mark with a temperature.
  - `mergeRoundMarkWrite(existing, incoming)` — `gc_statement_round_marks` is one row per GC per week, and the week now has two things to record. A word over a sent mark stays `sent` with the statement's channel and `acted_at`; a send over a word keeps the temperature, the sentence and the pay date; notes from both are kept, the word first, and the app's own `APP_SEND_NOTE` never crowds out a sentence. Before this, Draft Message's auto-mark overwrote a "spoke with them" mark and its temperature.
  - `worklistGroupTitle` — "Ask Malachi", "Your accounts", "No account man yet", "Under $10,000".
- **`src/components/jobs/GcWorklistPanel.tsx`** (presentational, render smoke 4) — the rows and their three steps. Check opens `GcReviewCertifyModal`, Send opens Draft Message, Word and "or mark sent" open the mark dialog (`markSentDefaultAction` picks which side it opens on). Send is inert until the group is certified.
- **`JobsGcReviewModal.tsx`** — the Weekly statement rounds rows, the per-sender tally and the panel's Start round button are replaced by the panel; the round-email block keeps its own box under it. `markRound` writes through `mergeRoundMarkWrite`. The week strip reads the worklist's counts and adds words in. 2,467 → 2,312 lines.
- **Retired:** `GcSenderRoundCard.tsx` ("see a round as its sender sees it"), `senderRoundQueue`, `latestGcStatementMarkBy`, `roundStartTotal` — nothing opens the card once the chips are gone. The v2.2792 rule it enforced (never mark as someone else) stands: `acted_by` is still the person signed in; step 2 adds whose word it is beside it.
- **Still there, until step 6:** the round overlay (opened by `?round=1` from the Dashboard row and the Pipeline card, for GCs where the signed-in user is the account man) and the per-sender round email.
- `upsertGcStatementRoundMark` takes an optional `acted_at`.
- Help guide `run-your-gc-statement-round` rewritten around the list; the GC Review map, the playbook row and the to-do carry the rest.
