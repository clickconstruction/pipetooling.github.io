# v2.7476 — Lien desk: print every filed lien's copies in one press, and enter their mail numbers in one place (2026-11-16)

Punch list #366, steps 3 and 4 of 4, as one window. Step 1 (v2.7472) drew the cover letter and the COPY page and printed them from one job's Lien window. The office files liens together, twelve on the day this was asked, so the copies go out together too. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What changed

- **`LienServeWindow.tsx`** (new): *Filed liens: copies to mail*. Opened from To do: **Record service** on a serve row opens it when two or more filed liens owe their copies (`actOnNextUp`), and one lien alone keeps its own Lien window. The row's own words and the Board's bin are step 2.
- **The top half prints.** `src/lib/jobs/lienServePackets.ts` (new, pure): `lienServeJobsFromDesk` reads the desk's own data (no read of its own) for every filed, unserved, live affidavit and where its envelopes go today; `lienServePlan` sorts them by their serve-by day into what prints and what is held, with every reason; `lienServePlanWords` words the press through `batchWords` (*Print 11 liens' copies · 1 held ▸*, dead only when nothing is ready); `lienServeBatchPrint` draws one document, the mail sheet first and then each envelope's letter and copy, and hands back each job's own papers, which are filed on the job as `lien_affidavit_copy`. The mail sheet is a desk paper and is not filed.
- **The clock in words.** `lienServeClock`: *3 days left*, *due today*, *2 days late*. A late copy is still mailed and still recorded; the desk says the day it was due and nothing about what the law makes of it.
- **The bottom half records.** `lienServeNumbers.ts` (new, pure) and `lienServeNumbersIo.ts`: the day the copies were mailed, then a row per envelope for its certified mail number and a receipt photo. Enter moves to the next envelope, as a scanner ends its number. Each ticked lien is written through `serviceSends`, the same two columns **Record service…** writes, each its own write; the batch keeps going past one that fails and lists it with its reason (`batchOutcomeWords`).
- **Mailed is said, never assumed.** A lien is recorded only when its *Mailed* box is ticked. Typing a number or taking a photo ticks it. A lien that went out with no number yet is ticked by hand and takes its number later from its Filings list.

## Why one PR for two steps

The card drew the batch print and the mail numbers as two steps. They are one window: the stack prints at the top and its numbers go in below, and a door that opened a window which could print but not record would have been half a tool for the days between two merges.
