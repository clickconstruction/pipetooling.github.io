- **`supabase/functions/_shared/devMcpJobs.ts`**: `jobUpdateInput` shapes `update = { job_id, set?, status?, reason? }` — `set` over the Edit Job form's own columns (`UPDATE_COLUMNS`: the identity slice of `buildEditJobIdentityUpdatePayload` plus `revenue`, `pct_complete` and `kind`), each checked as the form checks it (`checkColumn`: the address Title-Cased, the price to cents, the percent 0–100, an id a uuid naming its finder, a link https, an email an email, a required column never cleared, an unknown column refused by name with the list); `readJobUpdateWorld` reads the job with every column and each row a new id names; `statusSteps` walks the Pipeline's statuses one neighbour at a time, forward and back, as `update_job_status` allows; `jobUpdateDryRun` shows every change before → after with the no-ops dropped, refuses a plan that changes nothing, names the step that deletes Ready to Bill lines and the one that is the Billed move alone, and commits to the before values, the status and `updated_at`; `jobUpdateAppliedWords` says what landed, what RLS held, where the steps stopped and the next press.
- **`dev-mcp` 0.52.0** (numbered behind v2.6792's 0.50.0 and v2.6804's 0.51.0, which merged first): `plan_job_update` / `apply_job_update` join `WRITE_VERBS` with `rpc: null` (sixty-five write verbs). - **Tests**: `devMcpJobs.test.ts` — the columns held to the form's payload and the statuses to the table's CHECK, the shape's refusals, the reads pinned, the steps, the plan's words, the hash's sensitivity to a changed row, the words after, the server's two calls.

## Read closer than the design

- The identity slice refuses to save a blank name, address or service type (`identitySliceReadyToSave`); the verb refuses the same three cleared or blank, and lets every other text column be cleared with `null`.
- `customer_phone` typed blank clears the column, as the form's `|| null` does; `hcp_number` and `click_number` keep a blank as the table's default.
