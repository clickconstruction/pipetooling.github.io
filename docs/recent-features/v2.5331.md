- **Whose thread** (`supabase/functions/_shared/gcPortalThread.ts`): the page's contact, the lead's rule for the customer's page: the customer's own row in `contacts` (customer_id set, name null), else the first person at that customer in the seed's order (`pageContactOf`, `findPageContact`).
- **The read** (`customer-portal`'s `thread`, `loadPortalThread`), the lead's rule: a line is theirs to see only when it went between us and them by text, email or their page (`direction` in or out, `channel` text, email or portal) and, when it is tied to a job, the job is a project, bid or Pipeline job of theirs. A team note or event, a call, a voicemail and a missed call never leave, nor `who_user_id`, a text's subject, `optout_line`, `back_*` or `step_key`. Of ours only the writer's first name passes. The newest 100, oldest first. A read that fails answers `thread: null`.
- **The write** (`submit-portal-request`'s `portal_message`, `writePortalLine`): after the link check, the service role writes their line on that contact, `channel` portal and `direction` in, tied to the job of the page they wrote on when it is a GC project of theirs. A customer with no contact yet gets their own row, as the seed makes it for a customer with nobody named, and a race between two first lines reads the winner's row. Twenty lines an hour at most. No inbox row and no email: the thread is where it lands, and the office's own reads of the thread take it from there.
- **The tab** (`PortalGcMessages.tsx`, `src/lib/portal/portalThread.ts`): the page's own words (`messagesSame`, `you`, `writeToUs`, `send`, `repliesHours`, the doors), each line with who, its day in Chicago and how it came (here, by text, by email), a long one cut at a word, then the box. *For something urgent on site, call* names the superintendent, else the project manager, else our company. The sample says thank you and keeps the line on the page; the office's preview (8d-ii) sends nothing and says so.
