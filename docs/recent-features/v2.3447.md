# v2.3447 — Owner of record: the Fix-ups list looks every GC job up on the appraisal roll; Use / Use all found confirm the owner with provenance (2026-09-15)

Migration `20260914260000_owner_confirm_list.sql` ([`docs/migrations/20260914260000_owner_confirm_list.md`](../migrations/20260914260000_owner_confirm_list.md)); no edge function. PR 1 of the owner-of-record train ([`to-dos/owner-of-record-prompts/`](../../to-dos/owner-of-record-prompts/README.md) — the second-pass mock-up and the five decisions, all taken as recommended by the owner on 2026-09-14). **Deploy order: merge → `supabase db push` → regenerate `src/types/database.ts`** (two columns and one RPC were hand-added).

## Why

Owner, 2026-09-14: "To send those 9 notices I would first have to have the right property records and names of owners saved for all those locations." Every notice due on the Lien desk that night sat in *Needs the owner* — not refused, never asked. The first draft asked the office to paste a county page per property; then the app's own parcel lookup (`property-lookup`, v2.3004) was run on the ten live addresses in the pile and answered ten of ten with the owner, mailing address and legal description — including a public owner (City of Round Rock: a lien does not attach, the remedy is a bond claim), a landlord (the tenant is the customer) and a REIT care-of address a paste would have hidden. So the office never types an owner: the app looks every GC job up and a person presses **Use**.

## What shipped

- **Migration**: `customer_addresses.owner_confirmed_at / owner_confirmed_by` (backfilled for hand-typed owners) and **`list_jobs_owner_to_confirm()`** — every GC job (GC set, or a builder in the customer row with no GC) in waiting · working · ready_to_bill · billed with approved hours whose record has no *confirmed* owner; `has_owner` is the desk's expression, `first_deadline` the § 53.056 date for the earliest approved month. Decisions 1 and 4: the count needs approved hours; the window is every GC job with hours, not the desk's 30 days.
- **Kernel [`ownerConfirm.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/ownerConfirm.ts)** (+14 tests): `parseOwnerToConfirmRows`, `ownerKind` (public · company · individual — a city, county, ISD, the State, a water district or authority, a university; `USA Properties LLC` stays a company), `isLandlord` (a customer row that is not the builder and does not match the roll's owner), `readsAs` (the chips: `public owner — bond claim, not a lien` red · `landlord · <customer> is the tenant` amber · `likely homestead` red from `homesteadHint` · `mail elsewhere` grey), `eligibleForUseAll` (everything but a public owner), `groupByProperty` (by `addressStreetKey`, jobs per property, sorted by the earliest first deadline; a first month whose window has passed reads *March's window closed · later months live*), `fixupCount`, `builderName`, `shortDay`.
- **Write helper [`ownerConfirmWrite.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/ownerConfirmWrite.ts)** (+5 tests) — **`confirmOwnerForProperty({ address, jobs, source, userId })`**, the one write PRs 2 and 3 reuse: a job with a linked `customer_addresses` row gets its owner fields filled from the roll in fill-blanks mode (a typed value survives — Use confirms it) plus the parcel provenance columns and the confirmed stamp; a job with no row gets one inserted on its customer, or the GC when there is no customer row (the v2.3401 path, never primary), and `jobs_ledger.customer_address_id` set on every job at the property with that home. `planOwnerConfirmWrites` is the pure branch logic; `source` is `{ kind: 'proposal' }` (the lookup) or `{ kind: 'record' }` (a pasted draft).
- **`buildPipelineFixups`** gains **`owner-confirm`** — *Owner of record to confirm · N*, amber (+1 test). `JobsStagesTab` loads the rows through [`useOwnerConfirmRows`](../../src/hooks/useOwnerConfirmRows.ts) beside the other fixup counts and opens the list on the chip.
- **[`OwnerConfirmListModal`](../../src/components/jobs/OwnerConfirmListModal.tsx)** (+2 render cases): *Owner of record · N jobs on M properties*; on open it runs `lookupPropertyRecord` per property, sequentially, with *Looking up 12 of 31…* (results cached for the session). Each row: property · jobs · builder · county · the first-notice chip; the roll's owner and mailing address, the reads-as chips, the district · tax year provenance and the CAD link (`this parcel on Bexar CAD ↗`), the one-line homestead / public-property note, **Use** and *Not right…*. A miss reads *No parcel under the pin — paste the CAD page…* and unfolds `CustomerPropertyRecordPanel` (the same paste box, reused) with *Save the owner on <street>*. **Use all N found** in the footer takes every found row except a public owner (its own Use still works). A saved row turns green and stays for the sitting; the footer reads *k of N confirmed · f found and waiting on you · m need a paste*; the chip's count refreshes after every save.
- Guides: *file a lien and never miss its deadlines* → "The app finds the owner; you confirm it"; *read the Pipeline money view* names the chip. `GLOSSARY.md`: *owner of record (from the roll)*.
