# v2.8565 — Card and footer: the developer can remove a test person’s photo (2026-11-24)

Punch list #443, row 8: the third of the take-backs the owner approved on 2026-11-25 (*Yes, the six small ones (Recommended)*): one small PR each, invisible to every non-dev, refusing any row that is not a test row or that a real record depends on. No migration, no edge function, nothing owed after the merge.

## The press nothing took back

**Save the photo**, on *Your card and footer* in Settings and on **Card and footer…** from People → Users, puts a photo and its crop on the person's account and one file in the `person-photos` bucket. A photo could be changed and never taken off, so a made-up photo a walk saved on a test person stood for good.

## What changed

- `src/lib/brandKit/photoRemove.ts`, the rule. `photoRemoveDrawn(role, person)` is true for a dev on a person who has a photo. `photoRemoveProblem(person)` refuses, in a sentence, a person who is not a test person (`isZzTestPerson`: the account's name or the name on the card starts with ZZ) and a person with no photo. `removeTestPersonPhoto(io, userId)` reads the person again, runs the rule on that read, then asks the card's io to write.
- `src/lib/brandKit/cardIo.ts`, `CardIo.removePhoto(userId, path)`: sets `photo_path` and `photo_crop` to null on the row while it still names that photo, then removes the file. A row that is refused, or that names another photo by then, throws and the file is left. A file the bucket would not remove is answered as `fileGone: false`, and the card says so.
- `src/components/settings/YourCardCard.tsx`: a dev reads **Remove the photo…** under **Change the photo**. On a real person the press is dead and the line under it says why. On a test person it asks in a confirm whose button reads **Remove the photo**, and the card then reads as one with no photo.
- The guide *make my email footer and business card* and the glossary's entry say so.

## Why no migration

Read in `20261111200000_person_card_facts.sql` and the baseline: the two columns are granted for update, the path's check allows null, the crop has no check, a dev may update any `users` row, and the bucket's delete policy lets the person or the office remove a file under a person's folder.

## Who sees it, and what widening would take

A dev, on a test person. The press reads the role the card is handed, which is the signed-in person's, so View as a sample account draws nothing.

The `fixing` thread asked whether this should be for whoever may change the photo. That is not answered, so it is built narrow. Widening it needs no database change, since the database already lets a person clear their own photo and the office a teammate's. It would be the same press with the two gates taken off, in `photoRemoveDrawn` and `photoRemoveProblem`, and the guide's line reworded.
