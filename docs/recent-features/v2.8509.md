# v2.8509 — Lien desk: a gate a tap lands on no longer keeps a blue ring in Safari (2026-11-24)

A mend to v2.8472 (punch list #364), found on the live walk Diane asked for on 2026-11-24. No migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

A press on a held row of the Lien affidavits list lands on the row's first open gate and puts the keyboard on it.
In Safari the first such press after a page load drew the keyboard's ring on the gate and underlined its words.
Both stayed after the amber landing light had gone, until the next press somewhere else.
Later presses on the same page showed no ring.
Chromium never showed it, which is why the harness walk for v2.8472 did not.

The cause is Safari's rule for `:focus-visible`: a focus a script sets can match it whatever made the press.
The page's readout in the simulator's Safari said `:focus-visible` true on the gate after a tap, on the first press only.

## What changed

- The list row's press says whether the keyboard made it: a click with no press count is Enter or the space bar (`lienGatePressByKeyboard` in `src/lib/jobs/lienGateLanding.ts`).
- A gate landed on by a tap or a mouse press still takes the keyboard, and wears `data-quiet-focus` while it holds it (`landOnLienGate` in `src/components/jobs/useLienGateLanding.ts`).
- `index.css` keeps the ring and the underline off a quietly focused gate. Under reduced motion the landing light is an outline, so it keeps its amber.
- The mark comes off when the gate loses the keyboard, when a key is pressed on it, and with the landing's undo. So the ring is there for anyone who goes on by keyboard.
- A press the keyboard made is not changed: the gate shows the ring.

## The call taken

A tap could have left the keyboard where it was. It still moves, because a screen reader's press reads as a tap and its reader wants to land on the gate. Only the drawing is quiet.
