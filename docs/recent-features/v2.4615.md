# v2.4615 — Legal portal: the firm prints its own referral packet (2026-10-05)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Item 1 of punch list #85 ([`to-dos/legal-portal-firm-ready/`](../../to-dos/legal-portal-firm-ready/README.md)), the evening before the portal is shown to a collections law firm. Mockup: `mockup-1-print-packet.html` beside the card, both panes drawn from the sample matter.

## The defect

The portal's **Print packet** opened `buildLegalPacketPrintHtml`, the office desk's own working copy. The firm's PDF therefore opened with *Before release · N things an attorney will ask for first* and the gap list's advice, a worth line (*After 33% and $350 costs · not worth it*), the theory and its basis, *nothing held back* (the function hard-codes the held count to zero, so it was never true when the office had held entries), and *No attorney fees or costs yet — a firm has not been assigned* even when the firm had entered fees. The firm's own entries did not print at all.

## What changed

- **A firm builder**, [`legalFirmPacketPrint.ts`](../../src/lib/legal/legalFirmPacketPrint.ts) `buildFirmPacketPrintHtml(packet, { preparedOn, companyName, firm, matter, particulars })`. The desk keeps the office builder.
- **The cover**: the company, *Referral packet · prepared … for <firm> · <handling person>*, the matter's stage in the firm's words (`firmStageWords`: *referred · no step recorded yet*, *demand sent*, *suit filed*, *judgment entered*, *settled*, *pulled back by the office*), the referral date, the payer with address, emails and phones, the job count and the first bill, then *Balance owed · Fees and costs to date · Demand as of today*. The office's note to the firm follows.
- **Exhibits** as a table: letter, title, item count, and where the exhibit sits in the packet (*A · Statement of account*, *B · Agreements*, …).
- **Five lettered sections** — A Account (contacts with roles, the jobs, the statement of account with a running balance and a *Balance owed* foot, the property record), B Paper (agreements, where each job stands under Chapter 53, final demand letters, notices and filings), C Record of contact, D Field evidence, E Fees, costs and steps (the firm's fees with a total, every entry on the matter with who recorded it, what the company did before referral).
- **Each job's record as facts** (`firmJobRecordWords`): *signed agreement 2026-04-22 by Pat Sample*, or *bill sent, crew on site with GPS, no dispute logged*, or *on file: … · missing: …*. No theory and no basis line.
- **The firm's fees** (`firmFeeEntries`) leave out the contingency row the office books when it applies a recovery: that is the firm's share of money received, not a cost the debtor owes.
- **The footer**: the company's particulars for filing (legal entity, license, registered agent, custodian of records, affiant, office, W-9), and a line saying the packet is the company's business records as of the day. `@page` margin boxes print *Page N of M* and the matter on every sheet where the browser supports them.
- **Money**: `formatLegalMoney(-4000)` reads `−$4,000.00`, the sign before the dollar; it read `$-4,000.00` everywhere the legal surfaces drew a payment. (Item 5's formatter line, done here because the statement needed it.)
- The portal's blocked-pop-up message is the page's own notice, not a raw `alert()`.

## Is this the best we can do?

Three questions asked while drawing it. Should the demand figure be on the firm's paper at all? Yes: the firm writes the demand letter, and it should start from the figure the office showed. Should dates read *June 3, 2026*? No: every table on the portal and the desk reads `2026-06-03`, and a packet that switches reads as two documents. Should the exhibits link to files? Not yet: the files are item 12, and the index is built so a file column can be added without re-lettering.
