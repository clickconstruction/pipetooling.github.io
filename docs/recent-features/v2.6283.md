# v2.6283 — The law firm's weekly digest carries every office act (2026-11-01)

Punch list #233, PR 4 ([`to-dos/legal-portal-notifications-show/`](../../to-dos/legal-portal-notifications-show/README.md)), the owner's call 3 answered *Yes, build PR 4* the same day, after the read that answered his question *would the weekly digest cover everything that changed?* with no. **One migration** ([`20261101161800_legal_digest_every_office_act`](../migrations/20261101161800_legal_digest_every_office_act.md), not pushed here) and **one function to redeploy first** (`legal-notify-dispatch`). Stacked on [v2.6281](v2.6281.md).

## The two gaps

- **Reach.** The digest reads `legal_notification_queue`, and only seven acts wrote a row: the triggers ignored the office's own steps, planned steps, what-happened lines, offers and statements, and nothing watched `legal_matter_documents`. A stage change showed only sideways, as the stage word on the heading.
- **The stamp.** A row was marked digested once the people due that weekday were sent, anyone on another weekday counting as served (the dispatcher's own comment called it an approximation), so with Owen on Monday and Priya on Wednesday, Priya's digest held Monday to Wednesday only.

## What changed

- **Every office act queues a row, while the firm has the matter** (the migration): the entries trigger grows by `step` (a step that only echoes a release or a pull-back is left to those rows), `plan` (*· planned for Nov 4*), `event`, `offer` (*made by the office* / *received by the office*, with the amount) and `statement` (its title); two new triggers on `legal_matter_documents` queue `paper` on an insert that is not held or voided, and `released` when a held paper's reason is cleared. All seven queue only while `released_at` is set (a pull-back clears it) and the matter is open, or closed in the same transaction as the closing step, which is the set the portal shows the firm. Every row carries its source's id (`entryId`, `documentId`).
- **All of them ride the digest only**: `LEGAL_NOW_TRIGGERS` grows and `legalTriggerSendsNow` names the six that send on their own, so the now lane stamps the rest with nobody to send to and the digest lists them under their matter with `DIGEST_LABEL`'s words (*Step by the office*, *Planned step by the office*, *What happened, recorded by the office*, *Offer*, *Statement by the office*, *Paper added*, *Paper released to you*). `buildLegalNowEmail` gains a default branch for them, so no caller is ever handed another kind's words.
- **Per-person digest window**: `legalDigestSince` (`_shared/legalNotifyLedger.ts`) reads back to the person's own `last_digest_at`, else their `confirmed_at` (never replayed to whoever joins later, the v2.3325 rule), and never more than `LEGAL_DIGEST_MAX_DAYS` (8) back, so a person moved onto the digest is not sent a season. The dispatcher's digest query reads `created_at > since` and `<= readAt` instead of `digested_at IS NULL`, and `readAt` becomes the person's `last_digest_at`, so a line queued while a digest sends lands in the next one, never in neither or both; `digested_at` is still stamped as housekeeping for the open-rows index.
- **A line's source is read when the digest is built** (`legalDigestSourceIds`, `legalDigestShows`): the dispatcher reads the entries, papers and matters the week's lines came from and leaves out an entry the office undid, a planned step, event, offer or statement now held under `held_overrides`, and a paper now voided or held, as the portal leaves them out. A read that fails holds that person's digest for the next tick rather than send it unchecked.
- **The sample inbox** (v2.6280) gains a step and a paper among its week, dim under *Each event* and lines under *Weekly digest*.
- Docs: the guide *manage who at the law firm gets emails* (the two bullets, the per-person sentence), `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` → `legal-notify-dispatch`, the migration page.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **Digest only, never now.** The owner's rule since v2.4649 stands: six costs entered in a row must not be six emails. A firm that wants a step the moment it lands can ask; the switch is one line in `SENDS_NOW`.
- **A matter closed** rides the step entry the close writes (*Closed as uncollectible*, *Settled*), so no separate close trigger: one line, not two.
- **Holds are read when the digest is built, not only at insert.** The first cut checked a hold only when the row was queued; the office holds a planned step, an event, an offer, a statement or a paper with a press after the save, so a held item's title would have reached the digest. The digest now reads each source as it stands.
- **Eight days, not forever.** A person moved from *Each event* to the digest reads one week, not every row since they confirmed.
- **Not done**: a `digested_to` ledger per row like `sent_to`. The per-person window needs no column and no new fence, and the open-rows index keeps its stamp.
- **Left for the owner**: the four kinds that email now (a note, an ask, an answer, a payment applied) keep their old gate, a firm on the matter. So a note on a matter pulled back or taken in house still emails the firm that had it, though the portal shows that firm nothing written after the pull-back. Narrowing them to the same gate is one line in this function, and a product call: whether a pulled-back firm should still hear a payment applied for its contingency.

## Integration pass (before merge, 2026-11-01)

A re-read of the first cut against the portal's own reads (`legal-portal`: live matters by stage and `closed_at`, a pulled-back matter's read-only view of fees, costs, questions and answers from before the pull-back, entries by `held_overrides`, papers by `held_reason`) found two ways the digest would have told the firm what the portal hides. A matter pulled back keeps its `firm_id`, and so does one taken in house after that, so the office's later steps, plans, offers, statements and papers on it would have queued to the old firm: the seven new kinds now need `released_at`. And holds are a press after the save, so the insert-time check almost never caught one: the dispatcher now reads each line's source when the digest is built. The same pass bounded the digest window at `readAt`, which the first cut left open between the read and the stamp.
