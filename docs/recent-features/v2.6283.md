# v2.6283 — The law firm's weekly digest carries every office act (2026-11-01)

Stacked on [v2.6281](v2.6281.md).

## The two gaps

- **Reach.** The digest reads `legal_notification_queue`, and only seven acts wrote a row: the triggers ignored the office's own steps, planned steps, what-happened lines, offers and statements, and nothing watched `legal_matter_documents`. A stage change showed only sideways, as the stage word on the heading.
- **The stamp.** A row was marked digested once the people due that weekday were sent, anyone on another weekday counting as served (the dispatcher's own comment called it an approximation), so with Owen on Monday and Priya on Wednesday, Priya's digest held Monday to Wednesday only.

## What changed

- **Every office act queues a row** (the migration): the entries trigger grows by `step` (a step that only echoes a release or a pull-back is left to those rows), `plan` (*· planned for Nov 4*), `event`, `offer` (*made by the office* / *received by the office*, with the amount) and `statement` (its title), skipping one held back under `held_overrides` at insert; two new triggers on `legal_matter_documents` queue `paper` on an insert that is not held or voided, and `released` when a held paper's reason is cleared.
- **All of them ride the digest only**: `LEGAL_NOW_TRIGGERS` grows and `legalTriggerSendsNow` names the six that send on their own, so the now lane stamps the rest with nobody to send to and the digest lists them under their matter with `DIGEST_LABEL`'s words (*Step by the office*, *Planned step by the office*, *What happened, recorded by the office*, *Offer*, *Statement by the office*, *Paper added*, *Paper released to you*). `buildLegalNowEmail` gains a default branch for them, so no caller is ever handed another kind's words.
- **Per-person digest window**: `legalDigestSince` (`_shared/legalNotifyLedger.ts`) reads back to the person's own `last_digest_at`, else their `confirmed_at` (never replayed to whoever joins later, the v2.3325 rule), and never more than `LEGAL_DIGEST_MAX_DAYS` (8) back, so a person moved onto the digest is not sent a season. The dispatcher's digest query reads `created_at > since` instead of `digested_at IS NULL`; `digested_at` is still stamped as housekeeping for the open-rows index.
- **The sample inbox** (v2.6280) gains a step and a paper among its week, dim under *Each event* and lines under *Weekly digest*.
- Docs: the guide *manage who at the law firm gets emails* (the two bullets, the per-person sentence), `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` → `legal-notify-dispatch`, the migration page.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **Digest only, never now.** The owner's rule since v2.4649 stands: six costs entered in a row must not be six emails. A firm that wants a step the moment it lands can ask; the switch is one line in `SENDS_NOW`.
- **A matter closed** rides the step entry the close writes (*Closed as uncollectible*, *Settled*), so no separate close trigger: one line, not two.
- **Holds at insert only.** A plan, event, offer or statement held after it was queued still reaches the digest; the portal's read keeps it home. A hold is rare after the fact, and the row's body is the office's own line, never the reason.
- **Eight days, not forever.** A person moved from *Each event* to the digest reads one week, not every row since they confirmed.
- **Not done**: a `digested_to` ledger per row like `sent_to`. The per-person window needs no column and no new fence, and the open-rows index keeps its stamp.
