# v2.7843 — The sub's page says when the customer paid (2026-11-18)

Punch list #388, step 2 of 3 (`to-dos/sub-page-customer-paid/README.md`). The client, the `sub-portal` function's source and the docs. No migration. The function is deployed only on a go that names it.

## Why

A sub's sheet waits at **Waiting on customer** until the customer pays. The page tells the sub that the customer's payment is the last thing between them and their money. It then said nothing when the office recorded that payment. The owner asked for it on 2026-11-17 and took the card's five picks the same day: yes, with the day and no amount (call 1), for the one bill the sheet is tied to (call 2), and with the office's row flagging a paid sheet that has no pay day after two days (call 5).

## What landed

- **The rule**, pure, in `supabase/functions/_shared/subPortalCustomerPaid.ts`: the day is given only for a sheet at `customer_pay` with a tie, whose bill's status is `paid`, with at least one dated payment on it. The day is the latest of those payments' days. A part payment leaves the bill `billed`, so it says nothing.
- **The function** reads the waiting sheets' ties, those bills and their payments, and sends `customerPaidOn` on the sheet. No amount and no payer. Every read is fail-soft: a refused or failed read sends nothing and the page reads as it did.
- **The page.** The chip reads *Customer paid* with the day, in green. The sentence reads *The customer paid on Nov 12. Your payment is next. We will give it a pay run day.* On a held sheet the last sentence is left off and the hold's reason sits right under it, after *On hold:*. A queued sheet reads *Queued for* its day, as before. English and Spanish, in `subPortalI18n.ts`.
- **The sub's own guide** (*How pay works*) gains one line on the third step: when the customer pays, the card says so, with the day.
- **The office's row** on Jobs → Subs → Pay says what the sub reads: *Sub sees: Customer paid Nov 12*, in grey. Past two days with no payable-after date it turns amber and reads *Customer paid Nov 9 · not queued, 3 days*. A held sheet stays grey and reads *on hold*. The day comes from the same rule the page reads, on the same rows (`loadCustomerPaidDays`).
- **The pick window's words** now say what the pick does: the sub's page will say the customer paid when this bill is paid in full.
- **The row names its bill by itself.** Step 1's walk on prod showed *Rides on a bill*: the Pay view held the job's name and not its bills. The ledger now reads the bills of the tied sheets' jobs (`loadDrawBillsByJob`), so the row reads *Rides on bill 1 of 2* whatever jobs the page has loaded.

## What the page never does

- It never shows the customer's amount and never names who pays.
- It never says paid for a sheet with no tie, or for a bill paid in part.
- It never gives a pay date. *We will give it a pay run day* promises a day, not which one.

## The Spanish

The owner read every new string side by side with the English on 2026-11-18, before this merged: *Reads right*.

## Order of shipping

The client and the function are independent. A client ahead of the function gets no `customerPaidOn` and reads as before. A function ahead of the client sends a field the old page ignores. The window's new words are true once both are out.
