# v2.6378 — The Development page’s table and History read shorter: archived bids fold, like lines fold, a shadow bid wears its chip (2026-11-02)

The Gray Fox design spike (`to-dos/developments/SPIKE_GRAYFOX.md`), PR 2 of four, on v2.6377: candidates C, D and E of the notes. On Gray Fox the Jobs & bids table listed 22 rows of which ten were archived bids, History drew the Nov 1 mailbox merge as twelve lines of *filed a set*, and a robot estimator's shadow bid read as a fourth estimator on the site. No migration, no edge function.

## Change

- **Archived bids fold (C).** `src/lib/developments/pieces.ts`: a `PieceRow` carries `archived` (a bid-only row whose status is archived; a bid that became a job is never archived here) and `rank` (0 a bid alone, 1 a bid with its job, 2 a job alone), and the sort is done last, archived before done, the waiting rows first and oldest on top, then rank, then activity. `liveRows` and `archivedFoldWords` (*10 archived bids · $2,503.68 bid*) feed `DevelopmentPieces.tsx`, which draws the **Archived** fold (`pieces-archived-fold`, *Show them* / *Fold them*) above Done. The page's strip count and the card's fold line count the live rows and name the archived (*12 working or waiting · 10 archived · 2 paid*).
- **Like lines fold (D).** `src/lib/developments/historyFolds.ts`: `foldSameDayRuns` folds a run of `FOLD_RUN_MIN` (3) or more neighbouring lines that share the day (`calendarYmdInAppTzFromIso`), kind, verb, record and person into one line, id `fold:<first>`, whose detail reads *N sets this day* or, when the details differ, *N sets this day:* and one detail a line; the folded lines ride on it as `folded`. Questions, answers, entries and a line that waits never fold. `DevelopmentPage.tsx` folds `feedShown` unless the search box has words, so a search sees every line, and `DevelopmentFeed.tsx` draws a folded detail `pre-line`.
- **The shadow bid (E).** `src/lib/developments/overview.ts`: `shadowBidIds(bids, twinUserIds)` and `withoutShadowBids(members, twinUserIds)` over the `twinUserIds` v2.6377 put on the page's data; the page counts, draws the head's people and the Money look's band from the members without them, and `DevelopmentPieces.tsx` draws the row with a *shadow* chip (`chip-shadow`). On Gray Fox Estimating reads Robert, Trace, Wendi, and BP557 sits in the table marked.
