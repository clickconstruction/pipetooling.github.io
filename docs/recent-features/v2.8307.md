# v2.8307 — Messages: Never said that keeps a day another job still has promised (punch list #129, the leftovers) (2026-11-22)

Punch list #129, the one limit v2.8271's fragment named under *One day, one thread*. A customer has one thread for every job of theirs, and a line's mark (`message_promises`, a payment by a day) carries no job. So **Never said that** on one job's promise took the day off every line of the thread, the line that marked another job's promise for the same day among them. The thread then read *Their move · 5 d* and that other job's **They promised** row *Nothing promised yet*, while its promise still stood on the Pipeline. No migration, no edge function.

## What changed

- **The rule** (`wordFromLine.ts`): `dayStillPromised(lines, ymd)` is true while any live promise on any of the customer's jobs names the day. It reads the money lines' `words`, every live word of each job, and leaves out a paid claim, which names the day they say they paid. `dayKeptWords(ymd)` is what the office reads: *Their line still shows Fri Oct 9. Another promise of theirs names that day.*
- **The slot** (`useFollowUpThreadSlots`): after **Never said that**, and only when a line of the thread carries the day, `markLine` reads the customer's jobs again (`loadCustomerMoneyLines`, the read the press already makes). The read comes after the void, so the word taken off is not in it. If the day still stands, no mark is taken off and the line above is said. Otherwise every line that carries the day loses it, as before.
- **A read that fails** leaves the marks and says *The word is off the job. Their line still shows the day.* with the reason, the words v2.8271 already says for a mark that would not come off.
- **Tests**: `wordFromLine.test.ts` (another job's promise for the day, another day, a paid claim, no words, the plain words); `useFollowUpThreadSlots.render.test.tsx` (the day kept and said with two jobs, and a failed read leaving the marks).
- **Docs**: the guide *reply to a person from their thread*, `MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md`'s slots, the Glossary's R3 line.

## Read closer than the call

- **A mark still carries no job.** The job's **They promised** row reads the promises on the customer's thread (`threadSummaryIo.ts`, by the customer's contacts), so with two jobs each job's row names the other's day. That is how the row has read since v2.5973. This PR stops the wrong removal and does not give the mark a job, which would take a column on `message_promises`.
- **When the last promise for the day goes**, every line that carries the day loses it, whichever job each was written for.
- **The same job twice.** Two live promises on one job for the same day behave the same way: taking one off keeps the day.
