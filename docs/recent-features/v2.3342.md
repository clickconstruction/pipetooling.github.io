# v2.3342 — Bid Costs refresh PR 3: the Bid vs actual lens (2026-09-11)

Client only. Mockup C of the 2026-09-11 Bid Costs review, stacked on PR 2 (v2.3341). The first surface that reads the Burn train's `job_budgets` snapshots back against recorded hours.

## What changed

- **Kernel** [`src/lib/bids/bidVsActual.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/bidVsActual.ts) (tests beside it): `buildBidVsActualRows` joins linked jobs × budget snapshot × recorded hours × pursuit cost into one row per job (largest price first) and `readBidVsActual` words each: **not costed** (no predicted hours, no direct cost), **hours missing** (materials predicted, no count sheet), **check the count sheet** (predicted hours over `HOURS_PER_THOUSAND_OUTLIER` = 15 h per $1k of price — B75 Lagan Casita predicts 603 h on $11,920), else recorded ÷ predicted against the work done (`% done` when known, the whole prediction when not): **over** (> 110 % of expected), **on the book**, **under**. `bidVsActualTiles` counts linked / with predicted hours / rate set / recorded vs predicted where any / not costed / over / outliers.
- **Hook** [`src/hooks/useBidVsActual.ts`](../../src/hooks/useBidVsActual.ts): `jobs_ledger` (bid_id not null) → `job_budgets` by job, `get_man_hours_by_job`, and a minimal `bids` row for any linked bid the tab's list lacks; fail-soft per source; loads only while the lens is open.
- **Tab** [`BidsBidCostsTab.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/BidsBidCostsTab.tsx): third lens with four tiles and the table (Job ← Bid · cost / time to bid · bid value · predicted h · recorded h · predicted direct $ (dollars roles) · done · read). Job label opens the job window (`/jobs?jobDetail=<id>`); **Cost it →** on a not-costed row calls the new `onCostIt` prop — [`Bids.tsx`](../../src/pages/Bids.tsx) selects the bid and switches to the Labor tab. Empty state and the first tile link to Settings → Data → Link jobs to their bids.

## Verified

Dev against prod as dev: 15 linked jobs; 4 with predicted hours, 1 with a rate set; 1,789 h recorded; B75 reads "check the count sheet · 603 h on $11,920"; SPACEX reads "hours missing · materials only"; J775 reads "140% of hours · over the book"; Cost it → on SPACEX selected BP375 and landed on the Labor tab. Unit tests, lint, theme check, `tsc -b` clean.

## Residue

- The "won bids never costed" count is the honest headline: 11 of 15 linked bids have no count sheet. The lens is the nudge; costing them is the estimators' work.
- Cost to win **by GC** ratios stay thin until a quarter of clocked bids has decided (bid clock began 2026-03-20).
