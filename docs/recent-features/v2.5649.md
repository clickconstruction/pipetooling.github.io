# v2.5649 — The word from the thread: Write down their word under a customer's line (2026-10-19)

Punch list #129, PR 3b of 6, on PR 3a (v2.5648, the two kinds of word). Most of an estimator's words from a customer arrive in Messages, in a text or a Quo-recorded call. Cut 2 planned an offer under a heard day (`write_down` on `message_promises`, the kernels in `messages/promises.ts` and `promiseWrites.ts`), but no screen ever drew it: a voicemail's *Keep the day* writes an offered row and nothing reads it back. This PR draws the simpler door: the sheet itself, under the line. Migration `20261019090000_customer_money_strip.sql` (`docs/migrations/20261019090000_customer_money_strip.md`); no edge function; nothing in the kernels copy.

- **The read** (`list_customer_money_strip(p_customer_id)`): the customer's jobs the caller is a person of (`is_job_people`), newest first, each in the bid strip's entry shape (the job with its customer columns, its bills, its payments, its live words of both kinds), so `parseBidMoneyStrip` and `bidMoneyLine` serve it as they serve the won bid's line (`BidMoneyStripEntry.bidId` is null for a job with no bid).
- **The kernel** (`lib/messages/wordFromLine.ts`): `lineTakesWord` (a customer's own call, voicemail, missed call, text or email; never ours, a note or another kind of person), `wordChannel` (a text by text, an email by email, every call by phone), `wordLanding` (the line's own job first, `messages.jobs_ledger_id`, even with nothing open; else the one job with money open; else a pick among those with money open; else *They have no bill open on a job of yours…*, or *None of their jobs is yours to write on*), `jobLabelOf`.
- **The slot** (`useFollowUpThreadSlots`' `underLine`, the office team's thread window on Messages and GC projects): **Write down their word** under the line; a press reads the customer's lines (`wordFromLineIo.ts`) and opens `SetPromisedPayDateModal` on the landing job with `initialSaidBy` the contact's name and `initialChannel` the line's, or draws *Which job is it about?* with one button a job and *Never mind*. A save reads the thread again. The call ask under our own text is unchanged.
