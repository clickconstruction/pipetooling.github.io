## What changed

- **The print box folds.** On a phone the box keeps its status line, the count and the held papers' amber press, and one bar under it folds the rest away: the print press, the three chips, the county, the sheet flags and the lines under them. The bar is 44 px, reads **Show the print for the notary** or **Hide the print for the notary** (`lienPhonePrintBoxFold`, `src/lib/jobs/lienPhonePane.ts`), and opens folded, since printing a stack for the notary is a computer's work. What it folds stays mounted and is hidden by `index.css`, as the open paper's footer is, so a tick or a typed county is kept through a fold.
- **A thumb's square inside an open paper.** The three panes share `useLienPaneHeads`, and on a phone it now starts the job window's press pad on the pane (`watchSmallPresses`, v2.7309): every button, link, role button and summary under 40 px is marked with `data-press-pad` and its two custom properties, and `index.css` draws the pad as a `::after` over the press, scoped to `[data-lien-pane-phone]`. Nothing moves and nothing is seen. The watch follows the pane's element, since the notice pane mounts when a paper opens, long after the desk's first render.
- **The By job grid's controls.** `LienDeskTimelineTab` takes `phone` from the desk. On a phone the two pickers are 44 px tall at 16 px words, so an iPhone does not zoom the page on focus, and *Print the grid* is 44 px. The bar no longer pins over the rows there: a thumb-tall row makes a pinned header taller, the train's own lesson, so it scrolls away with the list.
- The guide *send lien notices from the Lien desk* says each of the three.

## A computer is unchanged, and why

Every branch is behind the desk's own phone flag. The fold bar is drawn only when `isMobile`, and its rule in `index.css` selects that bar alone. The pad's rules select under `[data-lien-pane-phone]`, an attribute a pane carries only on a phone, and the watch does not start off a phone. The grid's `phone` is false on a computer, which leaves each style object as it was. The tests assert the computer's side of each: no bar, no pad, and the pickers' and the press's old sizes with the pinned bar.

Read on the live desk at 1,400 × 900 before the change and again after the deploy, on 2026-11-15, and the two reads match to the pixel: the print box 319 × 177 with its four children at their places and no bar, no padded press anywhere with a paper open beside the list, and the By job bar 1,352 × 43, still pinned, with pickers 23 px tall and *Print the grid* 115 × 25.

## What the pad cannot reach

A tick box and a native picker cannot carry a `::after`, so those inside an open paper stay the size they are drawn. A press with a press or a field inside it, and one that clips its overflow, are left alone, as in the job window. Two neighbouring pads can overlap, and the later one in the page takes the overlap.
