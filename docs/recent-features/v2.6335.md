# v2.6335 — The superintendent's phone reads the project's line: the same strip as the Projects board (punch list #242, PR 4) (2026-11-02)

Punch list #242, PR 4 of five. The field side of the line: the superintendent's phone draws the same `PaceStrip` from the same read (`list_project_board`, v2.6352) and the same `projectPace` as the office's Projects board, so the office and the field read one number. Client only, no migration, no function. It lands after v2.6352 because a superintendent cannot read the crew's hours, the wages or the ledgers any other way: the first cut of this card read the Costs tab's hooks and, measured on a sample superintendent, drew nothing.

## What landed

- **`src/components/jobMode/SuperintendentCard.tsx`** — for a superintendent whose current or next job on Job Mode is a project (`isWorkingProject`: a stage In order, set or recognized, or $25,000 and up), a card above the job's details: *J1044 · The Lodge · 260 h budget ≈ assumed · 170 h in*, the headline in the tone's colour, the pace strip with the real days and the stages, named under it (a tap on a day opens `DayDetailsModal` with `hoursOnly`: who, the hours and the report, no dollar and no purchases, since the read carries none; the strip's legend carries the money as a percent), the planned finish when one exists, the office's target line (🎯 *Expected 200 h, set by Robert on Nov 1 (was 260 h): …*), the money line only when it leads, the proof, and the one move with **Set the %** (the Dashboard's `FieldPctUpdateModal`, through `setPctJob`) and **Open the checklist** (the next stage with a question left, `StageChecklistSheet` over `useJobStageChecklists`, the same read the details' strip makes). Loads the job, its crew hours, its report points and its spent percent with `loadProjectBoardRead([jobId])`, again when the job's updates tick; a failed read draws `OfflineRetryPanel` with its reason, and a job the read does not return draws nothing. The target comes from `useExpectedHours`; the proof's checklist count is the stage checklists of v2.6077 (#164), with the items a job adds of #237, through the same `useJobStageChecklists` the details' strip reads. A job that is not a project draws nothing.
- **`DashboardJobModeCard`** mounts it beside `JobModeDetailsSection` when the role is `superintendent`; nothing changes for any other role.
- **Guide** *clock in and out with Job Mode* gains *Read your project's line as a superintendent*; `APP_DIRECTORY.md` (`/dashboard`), `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`, the Glossary's *Job Mode*.

## Decisions

- **One read, one assembly**: the card calls `list_project_board` and `projectPace` exactly as the board does; nothing on the phone computes its own number.
- **The presses show with the move**, as the spike drew them: a card on pace has nothing to press, and the stage checklists' strip under the details already lists every stage.
- **The hover cards stay off a touch screen, the board's strip included**: `PaceStrip` asks `(hover: hover)`. Without it a tap opens the day even under a stage's end bar (the screenshot at phone width found the bar taking the tap), a hover card never sticks, and the legend offers no hover. The phone card draws the stages' names under the strip instead, up to four of them, so a stage reads with no press at all. The mark and the chip now sit over the end bars, so *money ahead* reads whole, and a job with no hours in no longer reads *0 d at 24 h?*.
