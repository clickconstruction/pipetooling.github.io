# v2.6543 — An HR file can carry a matter: the analysis for counsel on a person, with the narrative, the documents and the timeline (2026-11-05)

Punch list #269, PR 3a. The owner's case folder for a former teammate holds a claims matrix for counsel beside the facts and the exhibits. The HR file is the right record for the facts; the analysis is not an HR entry (the convention says write nothing you would not want read back in a dispute), and the Legal desk's matter has exactly its shape — a narrative for the firm, documents with a hash and a state, a timeline of planned steps and events, a firm to release to — but stands on a Collections payer and its jobs, so a person had no home there. Migration `20261105220000_legal_person_matter.sql` ([`docs/migrations/`](../migrations/20261105220000_legal_person_matter.md)): push right after the merge.

## What changed

- The schema: `legal_matters.kind` (`account` | `person`) and `person_id`, one matter a person (`p:<person_id>` as the payer key), `legal_matter_is_person` and five restrictive `_person_dev_only` policies so a person matter's rows are a dev's on every table that hangs on a matter, and `legal_person_matter_open` (devs only) to open one or find the one that stands.
- `src/lib/people/personMatter.ts`: the words — `personMatterStageWords` (the desk's stage label, *Closed · reason* first), `personMatterChipWords` (*Matter · Under review*), `personMatterStandingWords` (*narrative written · 3 documents · 2 steps and events*), the empty state's two lines and *Open a matter…*. `personMatterIo.ts`: `loadPersonMatter` (the row by `person_id`, its entries, its live documents; a column not there yet reads as no matter) and `openPersonMatter`.
- `src/components/people/personFile/PersonMatterPanel.tsx`, the HR file's **Matter** view (a tab beside Timeline, Evidence and Narrative, drawn for a dev): the stage chip and the standing line, then Narrative · Evidence · Timeline. The pieces are the desk's own — `LegalNarrativeEditor` over `legal_set_narrative`, `LegalMatterDocumentsPanel` on the same bucket and rows, `LegalTimelinePanel` over `legal_plan_add`, `legal_event_add`, `legal_plan_update`, `legal_plan_mark_done` and `legal_void_entry` — so a document put on a person matter is a document the firm can be given, and a planned step reads the same way on both desks. The account's tabs are not drawn. With no matter, the empty state says what a matter holds and offers *Open a matter…*.
- The guide *keep private employee files* gains *Open a matter on a person*; `HR_FILES.md` → The tab, `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` → Legal desk and the Glossary's Legal desk entry carry the kind.
