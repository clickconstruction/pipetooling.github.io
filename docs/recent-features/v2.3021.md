# v2.3021 — Test-gap audit, pass 32: the Stages header-stats fetch gets its first tests (2026-09-07)

Follow-up to v2.3020. Tests only; no kernel, client, migration or edge-function change.

## Why this one

`jobs/fetchStagesHeaderStats.ts` (172 lines) is the spine read under the Pipeline strip, the Dashboard AR card, the Billed pin and Quickfill's chase queue: four bounded, paged reads — the active-cohort jobs, a paid head-count, the open invoices, and the payments that are invoice-linked or inside the trailing collected window — fed to the header-stats and bill-truth kernels. Five callers; the kernels were tested, the fetch was not.

## What the tests found

Nothing wrong. Contracts now pinned that were only implied:

- The job read takes waiting / working / ready-to-bill / billed plus null status, selects only the lean columns, and is paged and id-ordered; paid arrives as a head-count only, so paid jobs' rows never ship.
- The invoice read takes ready-to-bill and billed only; the payment read takes rows that are invoice-linked or paid on or after the first day of the trailing 30-day window, so the collected-by-day series never depends on paid jobs' rows.
- A customer filter narrows the two job reads and nothing else; the invoice and payment rows for other customers are dropped in assembly.
- The result carries the head-count as `paid.count`, collected-by-day from the raw payments, the bill truth on both the stats and the result, and the lean billed rows (the billed job with its invoice merged) for the chase queue; the bill-truth shadow is reported for the strip. A null head-count reads as zero; a failed read reports the message or "Could not load board stats".

## What shipped

Covers the collected-window start, the four reads' filters and paging, the customer filter's reach, the result shape, the null head-count, and the two failure messages.
