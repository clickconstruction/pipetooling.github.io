# v2.8671 — A person's desk: a developer sees how they sign in, and can take Google off their login (2026-11-26)

Punch list #447, the desk's *Ways in* row (piece 5), built the simple way: no token hook and no per-person switch. One migration, one edge function changed, and the row. **Google should be switched on for the project only after this is pushed and deployed.**

## What was there

v2.8670 put *Continue with Google* on the sign-in page, hidden until the provider is on. Nothing in the app could read a login's identities or take one away. A Google link is held by Google's own id, so after a dev changed a person's address the old Google account would have gone on opening the login.

## What changed

- **Migration `20261126200600_dev_login_ways.sql`**: `dev_login_ways(p_user_id)` reads a login's ways in for a dev, and `dev_unlink_google(p_user_id)` takes the Google one off, ends the login's sessions, and refuses a login whose only way in is Google. Details in `docs/migrations/`.
- **The desk's *Ways in* row** (`PersonDeskWaysInRow.tsx`), a dev's alone, on anyone with a login: *Password · set* or *not set*, and *Google · linked* with the Google account's address and when it was last used, or *not linked*. *address differs* marks a Google account on another address than the roster's. **Unlink Google** asks first, then says *Google is off their login*.
- **`change-user-email`** calls `dev_unlink_google` after it moves the address, fail-soft, and answers with `google_unlinked`.
- The words are a kernel, `src/lib/people/loginWays.ts`.
