# v2.7781 — The header stays on one line on every phone (2026-11-18)

The owner's ask of 2026-11-18, with a screenshot of the header on a 375 px phone: the menu, home and search on a first line, and the wrench, the three coloured presses, the people press and the gear on a second.

> at any time the header is going to wrap to two lines I want to move this wrench into the gear menu on the left I never want the header to be two lines
>
> if a phone is ever so small it needs to wrap further please move the people into the gear menu as well

## Why it wrapped

- `.appNav` carried `flex-wrap: wrap` at 640 px and under. A row longer than the window fell to a second line.
- As the dev the row needs 365 px: 114 for the menu, home and search, 243 for the right end and 8 between. A 375 px phone gives it 359, since the nav pads 8 px a side. It wrapped at 320, 360 and 375 and fitted from 381 up.

## What changed

- The header never wraps. The rule at 640 px is now `flex-wrap: nowrap`.
- When the row has no room, presses leave it one at a time and become rows of the gear menu, in one order: the wrench (**Tell the dev**), then the people press (**People**), then the customers press (**Customers**). A press leaves only while the row would not fit without it leaving, and comes back as soon as the window has room.
- The row is measured, not ruled by width, because what it holds differs by role and by mode: Job Mode adds the call press, a partner has the statement mark, View as adds the way back. The shape is the Lien desk title bar's (`lienDeskTitleBar.ts`): a pure kernel that answers from measured widths, and a reader that measures the DOM.
  - `src/lib/navRowFit.ts`: `navRowFit(width, parts)` answers `{ inMenu, tight }`, and `readNavRow` reads the row. The read does not depend on what the row shows now, so the row cannot flap: a press in the menu counts at the width it had on the row, the gaps count at their plain size, and the way back counts at its full width.
  - `src/hooks/useNavRowFit.ts` measures in a layout effect, so a row that does not fit is never painted on two lines. It measures again on a resize, when a part of the row changes size, when a press arrives, when the content key changes, and on the settle schedule `useNavFitCollapse` keeps for a tab that never renders.
  - `src/components/layout/NavRowMenuRows.tsx` draws the gear rows. **Tell the dev** is the gear menu's first row and asks for the box before the menu closes, since the box reads the page inside the press. **People** and **Customers** sit above Materials with the other page rows. The gear wears the wrench's green answer dot while the wrench is one of its rows.
- A row still too long with every press in the menu closes its gaps, 8 px to 2. The way back from View as, the one part with words, shortens with an ellipsis. Nothing falls to a second line.

## Which menu

The owner wrote "the gear menu on the left". In his screenshot the gear is at the far right and the menu at the left is the hamburger. The gear menu was picked, for three reasons:

- He named the gear menu twice, the second time with no side.
- The gear menu is already where a phone's header icons go when they leave the row: Materials, Checklist and Map are gear rows on a phone.
- The switch for the wrench, **Fix Mode**, is a gear row. The hamburger holds page links only.

"On the left" reads as where the wrench sat: the leftmost press of that line.

## The customers press

The owner named two presses. The customers press is a third step, added because his rule needs it: an assistant or a controller with Dispatch Mode off has a customers press on the row too, and by the measured parts that row on a 320 px phone is still 18 px too long with the wrench and the people press gone. It leaves last, and only then. The dev and a leader never see this step, since their customers door on a phone is already the hamburger's.

## Who sees what

Nobody loses a door and nobody gains one. Each gear row shows only for a person whose press left the row, under the gate the press had.

- The wrench: anyone signed in with Fix Mode on. With the phone dock up there is no gear, so the wrench stays on the row, which has room for it.
- The people press: every role but a subcontractor, a helper, a primary, a superintendent and an estimator, outside Task Mode and the dock.
- The customers press on a phone: the same roles plus the estimator, less the dev and the leader.

## Checked

In headless Chrome against the dev server on prod data, at 320, 360, 375, 390, 430 and 1,440 px, light and dark:

- As the dev: one line at every width, no sideways scroll. At 320 the wrench and the people press are gear rows; at 360 and 375 the wrench alone; at 390, 430 and 1,440 nothing moves. Before the change the row was two lines at 320, 360 and 375.
- **Tell the dev** from the gear opens the same sheet and the menu closes. **People** from the gear opens the People page. Turning the phone to 760 px wide brings both presses back, and turning it back moves them again.
- Through View as, read only: the leader and the assistant in both themes, and the controller, the estimator, the primary, the superintendent, the subcontractor and the helper in light. One line at every width for each, no sideways scroll, nothing past the window's edge. The estimator's **Customers** row opens Customers.
