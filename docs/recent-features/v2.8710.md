# v2.8710 — A development's page: the proof of who signed, to print for the client (2026-11-27)

Punch list #439, piece 5, its first part (5a). Client only: no migration, no edge function. It stands on piece 4b's branch for one read, the site's secrecy, and reads softly without it.

## What was there

The card's ask is that the client be shown who signed. A development's page listed the site's papers (**Papers for this site**, v2.8669), each with its company and where it stands. Nothing put them in a form to hand a client.

## What changed

- **The block.** Under the lines of **Papers for this site** sits **The proof for** the client, by name when the page knows one. One line counts the companies that signed and the ones asked, such as *2 companies signed. 1 asked, not yet signed.* Two papers of one company count once. Then the press **Make the packet**.
- **The packet.** The press opens the packet as it prints, light whatever the theme: the client, the site's name, the count, then **Signed** with each company, who signed, the paper and the day, the oldest first. **Asked, not yet signed** follows with each company that has the paper out. A paper made and never sent is on neither list, and a paper a newer one replaced is left out.
- **Our own people.** While secrecy is on for the site the packet reads *18 of 21 have signed. A job at this site stays shut to a person until they sign. Workers are counted, not named.* With secrecy off it says nothing of them. A count that cannot be read is said: *Their count could not be read just now.*
- **Print** opens the packet in a print window as a page of its own, and keeps a copy under Documents → Sent copies, filed as `site_proof_packet` with the contracts: the packet goes to the client, and a print that goes to someone is a send (`sentCopiesCoverage.test.ts`). A blocked print window is said, with what to do. **Close** or Escape closes the packet.
- **The foot** says the day and *This is the list. The signed copies are not attached.*
- **Who.** The roles that read the site's papers: the office and an estimator. Looking at the packet writes nothing. A print writes its copy's row and file, by the path every filed print uses.

## What it reads

Before piece 4a's migration is pushed the table is not there, and the packet says nothing of our own people. ## Design calls

Each is open to a flip.

- **The block sits inside Papers for this site**, as the mock-up draws it, and so is drawn only when a company has signed or been asked.
- **A company is counted once**, however many papers it signed. Each paper is still its own line on the packet.
- **The asked are on the packet.** The mock-up lists them, and a client who reads only the signed would take the list for whole.
- **A paper never sent is not on the packet.** Nobody was asked.
- **An estimator can make the packet.** It reads what an estimator already reads. Sending it is part 5b's, and the office's.
- **The packet names no job and no address.** It names the client and the site's name.
- **The foot says the copies are not attached**, since the mock-up's packet carries them and this part does not.
- **A print keeps a copy.** The first build printed in place and kept none, and CI's rule for prints refused it. The packet is for the client, so it is filed, not listed as a paper for ourselves.
