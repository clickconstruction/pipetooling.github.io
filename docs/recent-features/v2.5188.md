## The change

- The draw goes through [`templateDraftPress`](../../src/lib/gc/schedule/scheduleWindow.ts), the prototype reducer's `draftSchedule` as a kernel: the rough's own copy of a template's lines when the rough was drawn from it, else an offered template's lines, with the rough's stage days when we bid one. The schedule carries the template's name and today, and its line ends *It is drawn from the template {name}.* A template set aside while the window was open is refused in words (`TEMPLATE_GONE`).
- **The Templates card** (`GcTemplatesCard`), on a job being built for those who may move a bar, hidden in a what-if copy: **Save as a template** under the job's name, and every template with where it came from, its size and stages, the jobs drawn from it, **Rename**, and **Set it aside** or **Bring it back**. Each is a record through `saveScheduleTemplate`, `renameScheduleTemplate` and `setAsideScheduleTemplate`, the name refused first in the kernel's words (`templateNameProblem`) and then the table's. Each press shows its own failure line (`useSchedulePress`).
- Ported from the prototype's `GcScheduleTemplates.tsx` with its words: each press is a callback where the prototype dispatched to its reducer. The first draft also takes the rough's stage days and own template now, so 12b's rough feeds it with no further change.
