# v2.8697 — Receipt texts: the office can text a card holder again (punch list #299, Text again) (2026-11-27)

The last piece of [`to-dos/ticket-text/README.md`](../../to-dos/ticket-text/README.md), the press frame T7 of its mock-up draws on the Needs you row for a charge texted twice with no receipt. Taunya's answer of 2026-11-25, quoted on the card: *Yes, build it.* One edge function (`receipt-request`, redeploy), one migration (`20261127064100_receipt_text_again.sql`), and the client.

## Why it is not client only

Until now only the cron's sweep could send a receipt text: `receipt-request` refused every call without the cron's secret. A press by a person needs a door a signed-in person can use, a place to write that the press happened, and a list that knows about it. So this is a function change and a migration, and the card's S was an M.

## What landed

- **The press** (`receiptAgainPress`, `lib/needsYou/fillInReceipts.ts`; `ReceiptChasePresses.tsx`; the deck's `pressReceiptTextAgain`). On the Fill them in deck, the row *Jordan was texted twice about $228.58 at Ridgeway Supply and sent no receipt.* gains **Text Jordan again**, between **Got it** and **Copy the receipt link**. The label carries the holder's first name. No other kind of row has it.
- **The window.** *Text Jordan again?* with *A third text goes to Jordan's phone about $228.58 at Ridgeway Supply, with a new link. It is a real text.* Its button reads **Text Jordan again**. Cancel has the focus.
- **After the press** the toast reads *A third text went to Jordan about $228.58 at Ridgeway Supply.* The row leaves the list, and the trail says *Texted again: $228.58 at Ridgeway Supply.* with no Undo, since a text cannot be taken back. A text that did not go keeps the row and shows the function's own sentence.
- **The door** (`receipt-request`, `POST { again: <request id> }`, `runReceiptAgain` in `_shared/receiptSweep.ts`). The caller's JWT is read and must be the office. An archived caller and a training account are refused. Then the rule (`receiptAgainHold`), the charge, the holder, the claim, the link and the send, in that order. `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` → *receipt-request* has each step.
- **The text** (`receiptAgainBody`): *The office still needs the receipt for $228.58 at Ridgeway Supply. Snap it here: my.clickplumbing.com/r/<code>*. It goes as the other two go: `sendText`, purpose `nudge`, from the team box, to the holder's own team contact, so it lands on Messages.
- **The record.** `receipt_requests.again_at`, `again_by` and `again_count`, and a `receipt_links` row made for `again` by the person who pressed (`docs/migrations/20261127064100_receipt_text_again.md`).
- **The list** (`receipt_requests_to_chase()`): a charge texted again rests off the office's list for twelve hours, then comes back saying *three times* and *The office texted again Thu 9:02 AM.*
- Guides: *sort the team's card purchases from the office* (a new section, Text the holder again), *turn on receipt texts for card charges*, *answer the text about a charge on your card*.

## Three calls, each this session's pick, and Taunya's answer

They went to the managing session as a question on 2026-11-27, each with its pick marked as the session's own. Taunya's words to that sheet, relayed the same day: *Picks stand.* Each is still one constant or one line to change.

1. **The words.** *The office still needs the receipt for …* It says who is asking, so the third text does not read as the reminder a second time. The other choice is the reminder's own line. `receiptAgainBody`.
2. **A cap.** Two presses a charge, twelve hours apart, four texts in all. At the cap the press is gone and the row says *That is the most for one charge.* `RECEIPT_AGAIN_MAX` and `RECEIPT_AGAIN_REST_HOURS` in `_shared/receiptText.ts`; the list's twelve hours is in the migration.
3. **The switch.** Text again obeys both switches and the trial list, as the sweep does. With receipt texts off the press sends nothing and says so. The other choice is a person's press that goes whatever the receipt switch says. The first two checks of `runReceiptAgain`.

## Never twice, and never by accident

- A press is claimed before its text goes, by an update that matches the count it read. Two people pressing at once send one text, and the second reads *Someone else pressed Text again on this charge a moment ago.*
- A text that could not go gives the claim back and drops its link, so the office can press again at once.
- The client shows the press only when the list hands back `again_count`, which only the pushed migration does. Before the push nothing can be pressed.

## Release order

Merge, then **deploy `receipt-request`**, then **`supabase db push`** (`20261127064100_receipt_text_again.sql`). No name is created that the client calls, so the Pages deploy does not wait.

- Between the merge and the deploy: the list hands back no count, so no press shows.
- Between the deploy and the push: the same. The new function's sweep reads none of the new columns.
- After the push: the press shows on a charge texted and reminded with no receipt. No such row exists on prod yet, since the receipt texts switch has never been on.

A types PR after the push adds the two columns the list hands back. The client casts that read, so nothing waits on it.

## Not done here

- **The Team queue's line** does not say a charge was texted again. It reads `receipt_requests` by column name, and a new column in a client's select before the push is refused, so the line waits for the push. It is one clause in `receiptLineWords`.
- **+ Ticket on the row**, as T7 drew it, is still the row's door to the Team queue.

## Proved, and what is not

- The rule, the words and every refusal: `receiptText.test.ts`, 5 new cases.
- The door over a stood-in database and sender: `receiptSweep.test.ts`, 11 new cases. One text with its link and who pressed; either switch off touching no row; a trial; each hold of the rule; a charge taken back and a receipt that came meanwhile; a holder who left, no cell and a STOP; two presses at once; a refused send giving the press back; a STOP, a bad number and a throw at the send; the second press and the cap; and the sweep leaving a charge texted again alone.
- The press, its window and the row after it: `fillInReceipts.test.ts`, 7 new cases; the io, 3; the deck's smoke, 4 (`NeedsYouFillInDeck.receipts.render.test.tsx`).
- The migration applied twice on a throwaway Postgres 15 with stub tables, then 14 checks (`supabase/tests/receipt_texts/40_again.sql`). The whole-schema bed runs it in CI.
- **Nothing was sent.** Every test runs over a sender that records, to a made-up 555-01xx number. No press was made on the live site or on the dev server.
- Not seen: a real row, and a real third text. No receipt text of any kind has gone on prod.
