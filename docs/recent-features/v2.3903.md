# v2.3903 — Bids: who opens which tab is one tested rule (2026-09-27)

Client only; no migration, no edge function, no screen change. Punch list **#46** row 5 (`to-dos/decomposition-queue.md`): the Bids map's step 2, the route / role kernel (`docs/BIDS_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md` → Recommended extraction order). Stage A — pure logic to `src/lib` with tests, nothing moved.

## What is new

`src/lib/bids/bidsTabAccess.ts` (25 tests, the role × tab matrix among them):

- **`canOpenBids(role)`** — the page allowlist (dev · master · assistant · controller · estimator · primary · superintendent). It was written out five times in `src/pages/Bids.tsx`: the role load, the three load gates and the page's "You do not have access" return, the last spelling `assistant` / `controller` where the others called `isAssistantLike`.
- **`resolveBidsTabRoute(tab, role)`** — one decision for a `?tab=` value: the two renamed slugs first (`cost-estimate` → `labor`, `robot-shadows` → `robot-board`), then the role gates on what the alias became. **`bidsTabBounce`** says how a refused tab lands on the Bid board: `silent` (the dev-only Queue and Console, Day book, Bid Costs) or `announced` (a primary off their four tabs, a superintendent on an office tab — the `bids-office-tab` role gate of v2.2882). The order of the gates is kept: a primary on Bid Costs is still a silent rewrite.
- **`bidsTabOpenFor(tab, role)`** — what the two tab strips draw, read from the same rule the router bounces on.
- **`FOLLOWUP_LENS_KEYS` / `isFollowupLens`** beside `ROBOT_LENS_KEYS` / `isRobotLens` (moved from the page) — the Followup group's six keys were written out three times.
- **`BIDS_TABS` / `BidsTabKey`** — the 26 keys once; `activeTab` is typed from them instead of a second literal union.

## What changed in `src/pages/Bids.tsx`

The router's two alias blocks and five gate blocks are one alias write and one bounce block over `resolveBidsTabRoute`; the five allowlists call `canOpenBids`; the strips' `myRole !== 'primary'` / `!== 'superintendent'` conditions call `bidsTabOpenFor`; the three Followup key lists call `isFollowupLens`. 5,107 → 5,049 lines. Behavior-preserving: the same tabs draw for the same roles, the same links bounce, the same ones say so.

## Left, on purpose

- The rest of step 2: the Bid Date Sent attestation rules as pure functions, `getSubmissionSectionKey` in both deep-link appliers, one `switchTradeForBid` helper — each its own PR.
- The lens bars' own `myRole !== 'superintendent'` buttons and the `robot-scoreboard` render gate (`canWorkRobotAudits`) stay where they are until `BidsLensBar` (step 3); the router never bounced the scoreboard and still does not.
- A tab the page does not know (`?tab=nonsense`) still bounces a primary with the announcement and is ignored for everyone else — pinned by a test, not changed.
