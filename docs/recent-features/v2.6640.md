# v2.6640 — A second text inside a tech’s hold lands on the thread: the bio after On my way, an I’m here, an office reply (2026-11-07)

The owner's live walk of the tech's bio (punch list #114, `to-dos/tech-about-lines/README.md`; PR 2 v2.5587, PR 3 v2.5590), 2026-11-07, as a helper test account on a ZZ Test job whose customer was his own number: the On my way line went and was written to the thread; the bio went to the phone and the sheet said *Your bio did not go. The line did.* `quo-send`'s log had the reason: `quo_write_line: new row for relation "contacts" violates check constraint "contacts_has_until_held"`.

## What was wrong

- The tech door (v2.5419, `20261013190000_messages_tech_door.sql`) holds a customer's answer for the tech who texted until their hour: `contacts.has_user_id`, `has_since`, `has_how = 'rule'` and `has_until`, under the check `contacts_has_until_held` (`has_until IS NULL OR has_user_id IS NOT NULL`).
- `messages_move_state`, the AFTER INSERT trigger on `messages` (v2.5274, re-stated v2.6012 for the missed-call reply), takes a thread off Follow up on an outgoing line and clears the holder: `has_user_id`, `has_since`, `has_how`, but not `has_until`. With the hour still set, the check refused the UPDATE, the trigger raised, and the line's insert failed. Quo had already sent the text, so the customer got it and the thread did not. The first text inside a hold never hit it, since the hold is written after that text lands; the second did, every time: the bio seconds after On my way, an I'm here after it, an office reply before the hour. The function logs hold no other hit in the last seven days, so crews had not been sending a second text inside the hour.
- `mark_notice` (v2.6202) clears the holder the same way and had the same fault.

## What changed

- **Migration `20261107083000_hold_ends_with_holder.sql`** (`docs/migrations/20261107083000_hold_ends_with_holder.md`): both functions re-stated with `has_until = NULL` beside the holder. No table, no grant change.
- **The SQL bed** `npm run test:pg:messages-tech-send` (`supabase/tests/messages_tech_send/20_scenario.sql`) gains the case: a thread held for a tech with an hour set takes a second outgoing line, and afterwards nobody holds it and no hour stands.
