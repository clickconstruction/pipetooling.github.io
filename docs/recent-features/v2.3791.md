# v2.3791 — A returned check tells you itself: Needs you card + Accounts Receivable reads Mercury's failed status (2026-09-23)

The gap v2.3784 left: nothing in the app read `mercury_transactions.status = 'failed'` (reason in `raw->>'reasonForFailure'`) until someone opened the job's ③ Payments received table. A check matched to a job and then returned kept counting as paid (Pipeline "35% Paid", the job's `payments_made`, the lien claim) until a person noticed in the bank — Taunya learned about Take 5 – Seguin's $13,680 from Mercury, not the app. And a failed deposit that was never matched still listed in Accounts Receivable's To match (`list_mercury_transactions_for_bank_payments` filters nothing on status) and looked like money to apply. Client only; no migration. PR 1 of punch list #40 (*returned check noticed*) — the read side; the webhook's message to the office and the Pipeline row badge are its PR 2 and 3.

## The rule

A deposit is a bank return only when it **posted and later went failed**, and was money in. Prod on 2026-09-23 (read-only): 17 failed check deposits all time, 6 of them posted-then-failed (Southern Post $13,680 · Insufficient funds, the Take 5 – Seguin one and the only failed deposit still linked to a `jobs_ledger_payments` row; Dudley $8,000; Poolcorp $3,125 twice · Stop payment; M&M Roofing $1,380 · Refer to maker; Maria Ortiz Sauceda $275). The other 11 never posted — Mercury's *"There was an issue with this transaction"*, a processing failure, the check simply re-deposited — and the 13 failed `internalTransfer` rows are account shuffles. The gap between posting and failing was 3–5 days in every true return: that is the window in which AR can match it to a job. The status reaches the table through the `mercury-webhook` upsert and the Banking-page sync, so reading it is enough.

## The change

- **[`bankReturnedDeposits.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/bankReturnedDeposits.ts)** (new, tested) — `mercuryBankReturn({ status, posted_at, amount, failureReason })` is the one rule; `mercuryBankReturnFromRaw(raw, postedAt, amount)` reads it off Mercury's raw payload (the AR RPC returns `raw`, not the column); `bankReturnedChipWords` → *returned by the bank · Insufficient funds*; `summarizeBankReturnedPayments(payments, txById, jobsById)` joins the failed deposits to the ledger payments that still carry them, biggest first, with a `J878 Take 5 – Seguin` label per row.
- **Dashboard → Needs you** — new item `returned-check` (money tier, amber): *A deposit the bank returned is still counted as paid ($13,680)* / *N deposits the bank returned are still counted as paid ($…)*; the detail names up to three `job · amount · reason` rows and says what the button does. [`useBankReturnedPaymentsNudge`](../../src/hooks/useBankReturnedPaymentsNudge.ts) makes two small reads (failed money-in deposits with a posting date, then `jobs_ledger_payments` in those ids, then the jobs' labels); the office roles that can read `mercury_transactions` see it. **Open J878** navigates to `/jobs?tab=stages&edit=<jobId>&editFocus=payments`.
- **Edit Job lands on ③ Payments received** — `OpenEditJobOptions.paymentsReceivedHighlight` rides the bridge to the Job window (Bill tab) exactly like `fixturesSectionHighlight`; `JobFormModal` wraps the payments table in a ringed, scrolled-into-view div (`job-form-payments-received`) that fades after four seconds. `Jobs.tsx` reads `editFocus=payments` beside `edit=`.
- **Accounts Receivable** — `BankPaymentsModal` decorates every row at load with `bankReturn` from `raw`; a returned-by-the-bank deposit leaves **To match** like a hand-marked one (client-side filter; **All** still lists it), never enters the exact-match sweep (`arExactMatchSweep`), the close-out (`arCloseOut`) or the tip strip, and reads as `returned` in [`arDepositRowState`](../../src/lib/jobs/arDepositRowState.ts) — the row chip and the right pane's pill carry the bank's words. `mercury_transaction_ar_returned` is **not** written by this PR: reading the status is the read side; v2.3784's Unlink and remove is the write side and marks the row itself.
- Guides: *match bank deposits to the bills they pay* gains the nudge under **If a check bounces after you matched it**; *work the Needs you list* lists the card under Money.

## Decisions taken

- **Read, never write.** The app could have upserted `mercury_transaction_ar_returned` when it sees `failed`; it does not — the office decides to take a payment off a job, and the RPC behind that button does the AR bookkeeping. A row the bank returned needs nothing from anyone in AR, so hiding it from the pile is the whole job.
- **Posted-then-failed, money in.** Reading `status = 'failed'` alone would raise eleven processing failures and thirteen internal transfers that nobody needs to see.
- **Not touched:** `count_mercury_transactions_for_bank_payments` (the "Allocate N bank deposits" count) still counts an unmatched failed deposit as one to allocate — an overcount of at most one while such a row exists in the 90-day window; fixing it means a migration, left for PR 2/3 of #40.
