# v2.5260 — Messages: where each person stands on their flow, read from the facts (2026-10-12)

Messages wave 1, cluster 4, second of two PRs, on top of v2.5254 (the types). Kernels only: no screen, no table, no migration, no edge function.

- **`readFlow(flow, stamps, now)`** in `src/lib/flows/state.ts`: one person's place on their flow, from their stamps alone. A lane per Once and Any time lane, and one per job for the Per job lane. Along each lane the chain passes a step that is done, lapsed, skipped or has no facts yet, and stops at the first that is none of these, which is on now. After it, one step is next and the rest are later. A step whose facts already read true reads done, and a loop with a round open reads now. An aside step (change orders) reads from its rounds and is never on the chain. A fork's way out (`endsLane`: `declined`, `not_carried`) ends the job's lane. A Per job lane with nothing on it is not started, so nobody sits on its first step.
- **Loops**: `loopEnds: 'rounds'` passes once every round is done; `'next'` holds the chain until a later step in the lane reads true, so a customer between two pay applications stays on Pay application and never reads Closeout.
- **Days**: `since` is the open round's opened day, the last round's finished day for a loop between rounds, the previous step's finished day otherwise, and the day the paper ran out for a lapsed step. `due` is the open round's, else the first round not opened yet, else the step's own.
- **The person's step on now** (`nowStepId`, `nowJobId`): the Once lane's, else the default job's, else the first job with one. Null for a person off the flow, and `placesOf` returns nothing for them, so no nudge reaches them.
- **Changed in the types**: `flowProgress(flow, reading, jobId)` takes the flow, leaves aside steps out and counts a lapsed step as not done; `FlowStep.owedWords` holds Follow up's We owe them words on our-move steps (helper 2's ask); a lapsed step's `since` is the day it lapsed (helper 6's ask).
- **`src/lib/flows/flowFixtures.ts`**: the prototype v1.178's demo people as stamps at its clock (Fri Oct 2 2026, 11:45 AM Chicago), the eight with threads and the board's ten companies, with `FLOW_FIXTURE_JOBS` and `flowFixture(id)`, for the card, the nudges and the boards to test against.
- **Where the read differs from the prototype**: Jonah's step on now is the open Pond question, due Monday, where the prototype typed Addenda; Tom reads on Boerne's ask, not asked yet and due Thu Oct 8, as Greg does on Pad B; a loop between rounds dates `since` from its last round.
