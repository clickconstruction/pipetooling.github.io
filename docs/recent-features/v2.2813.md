# v2.2813 — "Spoke with them": the contacted mark, temperature, and the Temperature board (2026-09-05)

Owner arc (same thread as v2.2812): "adding some functionality where the user can mark I spoke to them recently, taking responsibility for the client without saying specifically they resent this … force an open response when the user presses contacted, and the question is not what did you cover, but what is their temperature?" Mockup approved; temperatures stacked one over another (owner call).

**Schema** shipped in v2.2812's migration `20260905044344`: `gc_statement_round_marks.action` now allows `contacted`; `temperature` (hot/warm/cool/cold) and `expected_pay_by` columns.

**Kernel** ([`gcStatementRounds.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/gcStatementRounds.ts)): `Temperature` + `TEMPERATURES` (label + hint), `temperatureRank` (cold first), `RoundMarkAction`, `RoundMarkRow` gains `temperature` / `expected_pay_by`; `StatementRoundState` gains `contacted` (a mark wins, so a contacted GC leaves the ready queue; `mergeMarksIntoLastSent` still filters on `sent`, so the statement clock keeps running); `senderProgress` counts `contacted` separately; `describeRoundMark` reads "Spoke with them by … · call · warm · no statement / Temperature: …". New [`temperatureBoard.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/temperatureBoard.ts) (`buildTemperatureBoard`: cold first then amount; per-week trend; last word / pay date / last statement; the **guardrail** `contactedOnlyWeeks` — consecutive contacted-only weeks with no sent mark or app email since; `latestTemperatureByGc`; `trailingWeekStarts`), 4 tests. [`paymentChase.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/paymentChase.ts): optional `temperatureByGcId` — a customer's bucket takes the coldest read across its jobs' GCs and the due list sorts cold first, then late dollars.

**Form** ([`GcStatementMarkSentForm.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/GcStatementMarkSentForm.tsx), render-tested): a two-way toggle **Statement sent / Spoke with them · no statement**; on contacted, Email drops out of the channels, the temperature is a stacked radio list (Hot · pay date in hand … Cold · disputing or upset), the note becomes the required answer to **"What's their temperature?"** (a sentence, not a word — under 8 characters is refused), plus **They expect to pay by**. The stamp line says it counts for the week and never as a statement.

**GC Review** ([`JobsGcReviewModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsGcReviewModal.tsx)): `markRound` carries temperature + pay date; rounds chip reads `spoke Thu · call · warm` (green) or, on the guardrail, `⚠ spoke 2 wks running · no statement since Aug 11` (red); the per-sender tally adds `· 1 spoke`; header pills gain the **temperature pill** (newest read, sentence on hover, opens the send history) and a green **pays by** chip; Share menu item reads "Mark sent / spoke with them…". New [`GcTemperatureBoard.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/GcTemperatureBoard.tsx) under the rounds panel: GC · account man, Now, 6 weeks (dots), Last word, Pays by; "no read" rows call out GCs nobody has written a word about; the GC name opens the send history. [`GcSenderRoundCard.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/GcSenderRoundCard.tsx) and [`GcStatementSendHistoryModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/GcStatementSendHistoryModal.tsx) (What / Temp columns; history now includes contacted marks) follow. [`JobsStagesTab.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsStagesTab.tsx) loads six weeks of marks for the chase queue's temperature map.

**Email** (already in v2.2812's renderer): "last word" carries the temperature pill; the third step says "or, if you spoke with them and no statement went out, mark that with their temperature."

**Docs**: help guide [`run-your-gc-statement-round`](../../src/content/help/run-your-gc-statement-round.md) (Spoke with them, temperature, the board), `GLOSSARY.md` → Temperature (GC).

Verified live as dev: the form toggles and refuses a wordless answer; the board renders every round GC as "no read" (no contacted marks exist yet) with the six-week dots; the pills and chips are wired but no real contacted mark was filed against prod. Client only — the schema landed with v2.2812.
