# v2.7335 — Lien desk: each affidavit row shows its five gates as marks (2026-11-15)

Client only: no migration, no edge function. The owner asked on 2026-11-15 whether the pane's five gates should show on each row of the affidavit list too; the spike on `claude/lien-gate-marks-spike-f50766` (its note at `docs/spikes/lien-gate-marks.md` on that branch) measured eight shapes and this is the one chosen: five marks inline after *last work*, on every row of the two piles where a hold is still possible, at no cost in row height.

## What changed

- `src/lib/jobs/lienGateMarks.ts` (+ test): `lienGateMarks(gates, isSub)` folds `entry.gates` to five marks in the pane's fixed order — owner, legal, contractor, notice, homestead — each `ok`, `open` or `skipped`, with the tooltip's words (the pane's own sentence for a clear gate, *Missing <row word>* for an open one, from `affidavitGateMissingWord`, so the strip and the words never disagree). A job contracted with the owner keeps five marks: `affidavitGates` fills its contractor and notice slots as clear, and the strip draws them hollow, not green. `lienGateMarksSummary` is the strip's title and aria-label (`4 of 5 clear · missing a private owner`, `All 5 clear`, `All 3 asked clear`). `lienGateRowParts(pile, phone)`: the strip shows in `needs_property` and `to_draft` only; the *missing …* words show where the strip does not, and on a phone always, since a phone has no hover.
- `src/components/jobs/LienGateMarkStrip.tsx`: the five 9 px marks (`data-lien-gate-marks`, one `data-lien-gate-mark=<slot>` with `data-state` each), green at 75 % so a row of five stays quiet, the ✕ in `var(--text-red-600)`, the hollow dot on `var(--border-strong)`.
- `LienDesk.tsx`: the affidavit list row draws the strip on its chip line after *last work …*, and the words by `lienGateRowParts`. The pane's gate list is untouched (its rows become presses on `claude/gate-rows-open`, v2.7313).
- The guide *send lien notices from the Lien desk* names the marks under the affidavit list.

## Measured in the spike (1400 wide; 375 phone)

Inline after *last work* costs 0 px on every row tried; a strip on its own line cost +14 px per row. On a phone a row with three open gates and the words folded fell from 101 to 77 px; keeping the words on a phone keeps it at 101, the same as before.
