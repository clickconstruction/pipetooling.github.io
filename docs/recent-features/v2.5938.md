# v2.5938 — The Bridge desks: what the first live look and the mock-ups asked for (2026-10-27)

Punch list #192 (`to-dos/bridge-stage-desks/`), the follow-up to the four desks ([v2.5929](v2.5929.md) Build, [v2.5930](v2.5930.md) Bill, [v2.5931](v2.5931.md) Market, [v2.5932](v2.5932.md) Sales). Five things the first live look found on 2026-10-26 (prod data through a worktree dev server, dev-login as the dev), and four places where a desk shipped reading less than its approved mock-up drew because no read had been named. No migration, nothing new on the server, no owner call changed.

## Market and Sales — the asker is the bid's customer (`loadMarketDesk.ts`, `loadSalesDesk.ts`, both kernels)

Live, every open bid read *No GC on the bid*: Sales showed one asker at *0 of 11 · 0%* and *worth about $0*, and Market's book was one row of 226 bids, because the app's bid form never fills `bids.gc_builder_id` (`src/lib/bids/bidFormPayload.ts` writes it null and puts the form's GC in `customer_id`). Both loaders now select `customer_id` and read `customers` for the names; the asker, the GC in the book, the first-ever bid and the lifetime answers key by the customer (`marketAskerId`, `askerKey`), with `gc_builder_id` the fallback and the no-GC bucket last. A win is a bid marked won or started_or_complete, or one a job was created from, the Sales card's own rule (`isWin`; live the funnel had read *Won $0 on 0 bids* against the card's $306k because the decided read took won and lost only; the loader now reads the three outcomes plus the span's jobs by `bid_id`). The estimates' own pace reads 0 days live (customers accept the same day), so `SALES_ESTIMATE_PACE_FLOOR_DAYS` (7, an owner call) floors it and the row says so.

## Bill — the hand-marked bills leave the headline (`src/lib/bridge/bill/billDesk.ts`, `BridgeBillDesk.tsx`)

Live, the headline read *Where billing leaks: $259k will not bill itself.*: the fourth leak kind, *marked billed by hand, nothing on record went out*, summed eleven bills, $218k, most of them SpaceX's and the other GCs' pay applications, which the office records exactly that way (v2.5853) and which are billed already, Collect's $259k. `billHandMarked` now lifts those rows out of `billLeaks` into their own section under the leaks, with the note *N bills, $X, were marked billed by hand with no send on file. A GC's pay application is recorded this way. The question is whether the customer got each one.*; the headline sums the unbilled kinds alone (live: $41k, the card's $41.2k pile). A job with several hand-marked bills had repeated a React key; `BillLeak` carries `billId` and the rows key by it. The meter adds *That is about N days of the cash in the bank.* from the page's cash today (`cashTodayUsd` on `BillDeskPageInput`).

## Build — both percents when the job's word sits below a report (`src/lib/bridge/build/buildDesk.ts`)

Live, two rows read *% 100 → 40*: the newest point before the window was a field report at 100 while the job row says 40 by hand, and the row's own % wins (the repo's rule, as `effectiveBillPct` has it). The row now carries `pctAbove`; the cell reads *40 · a report read 100* (or *was set to 100*) and the why-line opens *The job says 40%. A report read 100% 18 weeks ago. One look at the job says which is true.* before its usual words. Movement stays 0.

## Market — "no answer, ever" (`marketDesk.ts`, `loadMarketDesk.ts`)

The first-bids read, which pages every bid with an asker, now selects the bid's `outcome` too and folds `lifetimeByGc` (bids, answered, won per asker). The dead-weight rule stays the span's (`MARKET_DEAD_WEIGHT_BIDS` in 26 weeks); the words change where the lifetime read says more: *N priced bids in 26 weeks and no answer, ever.*, the pricing-spend panel's *… for $0 won and no answer, ever.*, and the desc *has never answered a bid*. With an answer on record anywhere, the span words stay.

## Sales — the estimates' own pace, the speed line, today's requests, and a door on every unreasoned loss (`salesDesk.ts`, `loadSalesDesk.ts`, `BridgeSalesDesk.tsx`)

- A new read of the last `SALES_DECIDED_DAYS` of estimates (`created_at, sent_at, status, acceptor_consented_at, updated_at, total_cents, doc_kind`) gives the estimates row its own pace, the median of accepted less sent; with none on record the row falls back to the company's bid pace and says so.
- The speed line reads once `SALES_ESTIMATE_SPEED_MIN` (5, an owner call) decided estimates sit on each side of the same-day line: *Estimates sent the day they were asked are accepted N times as often.*, or *Only the estimates sent the day they were asked were accepted.*, or, under `SALES_SPEED_FLAT_RATIO`, *Sending the same day has not changed the answer so far.*; thinner data keeps *Small asks win on speed.*
- The move counts the estimates requested today and not yet sent: *Answer today's N the same day.*
- The decided read gains `bid_number, project_name`; the funnel lists the unreasoned losses biggest first (`SALES_NO_REASON_SHOWN`, 8), each `B<number> · <project>` with **Open the bid ›** (`/bids?tab=bid-board&bidId=<id>`), the Bid Board door last.
