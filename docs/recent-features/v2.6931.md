# v2.6931 — The money emails leave a bill paid in kind out, as the screens do (punch list #301) (2026-11-11)

v2.6834 took a bill an in-kind deal pays out of the Payment forecast on screen. The emailed forecast kept counting it as money coming, and past expected once its bill date aged. A docs catch-up pass on 2026-11-11 found it. Reading the code for this fix found the same gap in **Money waiting**, on the Pay speeds window and in its email alike: a deal's April bills made the customer the slowest on the list.

## What changed

- **The stamp**, `supabase/functions/_shared/inKindPayloadRows.ts`: the payload RPCs (`get_payment_forecast_email_payload`, `get_money_waiting_email_payload`) list every open billed row and do not carry the deal. `stampInKindDeals` reads `jobs_ledger.in_kind_arrangement_id` for the payload's jobs, 100 per read, and puts it on each row. Both dispatch functions call it in `fetchPayload`.
- **The emailed forecast**, `buildForecastFromPayload` (`_shared/paymentForecastCore.ts`): a stamped row is in no bucket and not in `openTotal`; `inKind: { count, dollars }` counts it. The email's last line reads *$9,375 on 8 bills is scheduled in kind, not money coming*, the modal's own words. A board with nothing but such bills reads *No bill is waiting on money*, not *Nothing open on the board*.
- **Money waiting, the email**, `buildMoneyWaitingFromPayload` (`_shared/moneyWaitingCore.ts`): a stamped row is under no customer, off pace or on, and is counted in `inKind`. The email ends *… is scheduled in kind, not money waiting*.
- **Money waiting, the screen**, `buildMoneyWaiting` (`src/lib/jobs/moneyWaiting.ts`): the same rule through `jobIsScheduledInKind`, and the same line under the list (`moneyWaitingInKindLine`) on the Pay speeds window.

## Decisions taken with the build

- **A read in the function, not a new payload RPC.** Both RPCs are long SQL bodies last rewritten for the payer. One column read beside them changes no migration and needs no push; the functions deploy alone.
- **A failed read stops the send.** An email that calls a credit money coming is the wrong this fixes, so the read throws and the outbox tries again.
- **Money waiting counts them apart, as the forecast does.** The on-pace line carries a dollar total, and a total that shrinks with no word reads as money lost.

## Not here

- **The Pay speeds window's By customer list** (`openBillsForCustomers`, `buildPaySpeedsBreakdown`) still lists a deal's bills under the customer with a late colour. It is a list of open bills, not of who to chase, and its totals need their own split.
- **The Billed awaiting payment report email** (`billed-report-email`) still ages these bills into its 30 to 90 and 90+ chips.
- **The residential reminder flow** is as v2.6834 left it.

## Release order

Merge, then deploy both functions: `supabase functions deploy payment-forecast-email-dispatch --no-verify-jwt` and `supabase functions deploy money-waiting-email-dispatch --no-verify-jwt`. No migration. The screen change ships with the client.

## Verified

Kernel tests (`src/lib/inKindPayloadRows.test.ts`: the read, its pieces, its failure, and the email's kernel against the modal's on the same two bills), the two renderers' tests, `moneyWaiting.test.ts` and the Pay speeds window's render test. Not walked live: the functions are not deployed until the merge.
