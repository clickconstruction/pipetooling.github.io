## What is new

- **Four columns on the paper's row** and the timed call: `20261120133000_person_paper_reminders.sql`, in `docs/migrations/`.
- **`remind-person-papers`**, a new edge function pg_cron calls hourly. It emails each signer whose reminder is due, at the address the send kept, and counts it.
- **`send-contract-for-signature`** starts the count again on an emailed send and sets the first reminder three days out, only while the Settings switch is on. **Copy the signing link** stops that paper's reminders: the office has taken the chase into its own hands.
- **Documents → Agreements.** A waiting staff, sub or partner line says *1 of 3 reminders sent · next Nov 23* and carries **Turn reminders off**. A paper turned off reads *Reminders off* and carries **Turn reminders on**. After the third it reads *All 3 reminders sent*. A line says nothing about reminders until one is set or sent.
- **Settings → People & teams → Paper reminders by email**, for the owner and a dev. Turning it on asks first, since the app then emails people by itself. Turning it off is at once.
- **The email** is the signing email's own builder under the reminder's subject and opening words, so the letterhead, the steps, the paper option and the Spanish line are the same. It is in the email catalog and on What the team sees.

## The rules, in one file

`supabase/functions/_shared/personPaperReminder.ts`, dependency-free, read by the send, the timed function and the tab:

- **Which paper:** an agreement filed under a person. Never a trade company's paper, an outside party's, an hours statement, a W-9 or a certificate.
- **A paper sent while the switch is off is never reminded.** So turning the switch on reaches no paper already out.
- **Someone who left the team is not reminded.** The function reads the person and stops that paper's reminders.
- **Each reminder carries a new link.** A person's signing link lasts 14 days and is kept only as a hash, so the one sent before cannot be sent again. A reminder makes a new link, and that ends the one before it. The email says: *This email carries a new link. The link in our earlier email no longer works.*
- **It does what is ready.** A paper that cannot be reminded is held with its reason and its reminders stop. An email that fails is named, is not counted, and is tried again the next hour. The rest still go.
- **`dry_run: true`** writes nothing and emails nobody, and answers who would be emailed. **`only_id`** narrows a run to one paper.
