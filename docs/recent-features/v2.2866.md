## Kernel — [`src/lib/subWorkOrders/sheetRail.ts`](../../src/lib/subWorkOrders/sheetRail.ts) (20 tests)

- `buildSheetRail({ coverage, sheetStage, payableAfter, agreed, open, unpriced })` → `{ steps[7], current, gap, group, position, label, sublabel, tone }`. Seven steps in one order: **Drafted · Sent · Signed** (office) then **Work · Inspection · Customer pays · Paid** (the sub's four — the portal's words; "Inspection" is `walkthrough` office-side, so the two ends of the rail finally use one word).
- **Vocabulary is borrowed, not invented**: agreement states come from `buildJobWorkOrderCoverage` (none / draft / sent+expired / signed / declined), sheet stages from `subSheetStage.ts`, and the paid step mirrors `subPortalRailStep` — `customer_pay` + a `payable_after` date lights Paid ("queued for the pay run") before the money moves, exactly as the sub's portal does (v2.2838).
- **The gap**: `coverage.kind === 'none'` draws the three office dots dashed red; **declined** and **expired** offers are gaps too (Drafted done, Sent/Signed dashed) — the sub is still working and nothing is signed. All three land in `group: 'no_agreement'`, `position: 0`. This is change 2 from the mock-up: Declined and Expired stop being filters and become red row states in the first group.
- **One current dot**: the sheet's own step when nothing is signed (the gap is the finding) or once signed; the agreement step while a draft or a live offer exists. `position` (0 no agreement … 6 paid) is the board's sort key; `SHEET_RAIL_GROUP_LABEL` names the four groups.
- `sheetNextAction(rail, coverage, ctx)` → `{ label, hint, button, buttonLabel }` — the office's end of the sub's "Done with the work?" prompt: *Get it in writing* (draft) → *Price it and send* / *Send it* → *Waiting on \<sub\> · N days* (`nudge` button once `nudgeAfterDays`, default 3, have passed) → *Re-offer or re-price* / *Offer expired — send it again* (`reoffer`) → *Wait for "done"* → *Schedule the inspection* → *Bill and collect* → *Pay \<sub\>* → *Nothing — done*. `daysBetweenYmd` is the small calendar helper behind the nudge timer.
- `sheetRailTone.ts`: the two literal status colours (terracotta current dot, red gap) and `sheetRailLabelColor`.
