# v2.8338 — My Time: one picture of the time you have added up (2026-11-23)

The first of two PRs for punch list #427. The owner circled two parts of the Dashboard's My Time card on 2026-11-23 and asked how they could look better: a row of eight small bars with a four-colour key, and seven lines of text for the week's days. After a first round of options he said what the card is for: *I don't think a user cares if they are above or below their quota. I think a user wants to see how much time they're accumulating and what days they accumulated what.* Client only. No migration and no function.

## What was wrong

Reading the two parts found that they also said things that were not true.

- **A finished day read *On Shift*.** The label was drawn for any salaried day with time on it, a day long over included, and it took the place of the day's hours.
- **A punch nobody closed looked like a normal day.** The end-of-day stop closes an open session at 11:59 PM. On the person's own card that day read the same as any other, and its hours counted as the week.
- **One hour over was red.** *Over* started at anything above 100 percent of the person's hours, so a week an hour over read red and a week exactly on them read green. On a bar 30 px tall for 150 percent, that hour is under half a pixel.
- **This week was judged against a whole week**, so its bar read *light* every Monday to Wednesday.
- **A dash stood for four things:** a day off, a day to come, today before the start and a working day with nothing on it.
- ***Today* was the phone's own date**, while a session's day is the company's.

## What ships

**The kernel.** `src/lib/dashboard/myTimeWeeks.ts`, pure: eight pay weeks of seven days, each day's time, which day is today and which are still to come, and which hold a session the end-of-day stop closed and nobody has approved. That last rule is `isMidnightCappedClockOut`, which the office's approval screens already use. A session still running counts up to now, and only on today. It knows no quota, set week or percent. Its sentences mark their values with `val`.

**The picture.** `src/components/dashboard/DashboardMyTimePicture.tsx`, drawn from the kernel:

- one big figure, the time added up in the week chosen, with **Today** beside it on the current week;
- *On the clock since 8:00 AM*, only while a session is open;
- seven columns, one a day, each with its hours and minutes. Today is boxed. A day to come is faint and empty. A finished day with nothing reads *0h*;
- a stopped day is marked with its own look, a mark and one line: *Check Sunday. Nobody clocked out, so the clock stopped itself at midnight. It shows 14 h 45 m.* Then *Press the day to fix it* for a person who can open the day, or *Ask the office to fix it* for a salaried person;
- eight columns, one a week, each with its time on top and its Sunday under it. A press on a week, or on the arrows, changes the days to that week.

Columns are drawn against the tallest one shown, and a day never against less than 8 hours, so a short day does not look like a full one. One colour. The looks are the `.myTime…` classes in `src/index.css`, and the old `.hoursByDay…` rules are gone.

**The section.** `DashboardMyTimeSection.tsx` makes one read of the person's sessions for the eight weeks, where it made two reads for two weeks and the hours block made a third for eight. The two tiles, the hours block, the seven lines and the **Last week** press are gone. The job-by-job fold follows the week chosen, for this week and last week. An hourly person's day opens the day editor as before: this week's, with time on it, and not one the office marked correct. A salaried person now sees the time on each of their days.

**The quiet line.** `MyTimeCanWorkLine.tsx` keeps an hourly person's **Set hours** or **Change** at the foot of the picture, since the office plans the crew from the answer on People, Utilization. Nothing on the card is measured against it. Its rule is `myTimeCanWork.ts`.

## What does not change

People, Utilization keeps its hours, its percent and its bands: the office's question is a different one. Job Mode's hours card under Your record still shows the older block. It takes the picture in the next PR, which also retires `DashboardMyHoursBlock`.
