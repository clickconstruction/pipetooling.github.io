# v2.4568 — Lien desk: a printed notice has a way forward (2026-10-05)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Rows 15, 16, 18 and 19 of the lien defects card (punch list #83, [`to-dos/lien-defects.md`](../../to-dos/lien-defects.md)): four states of [`LienDeskModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskModal.tsx) with no way forward, or carrying something they should not.

## What changed

- **Row 15 — a printed notice had no footer.** The Notices pane's footer branched on every pile but `printed` (*In the mail · tracking owed*, v2.4119). It now reads *Printed <day> · in the mail. Type its tracking numbers in the run to record it.* with **Record the mailing · N ▸** (the run), **Already mailed? Record it…** (the by-hand sheet, as on the other piles) and **Back to ready** — `clearLienDeskItemPrinted` in [`lienDeskIo.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDeskIo.ts) nulls `printed_at` / `printed_by`; the approval stands.
- **Row 16 — the run's buttons counted ready only.** The run lists ready and printed notices and ready retainage. The header's **Send the run** and the Ready footer's button now show and count `runCount`, the same three, so the run can be reopened when only printed notices are left.
- **Row 18 — a pile from a door stuck.** The desk stays mounted while closed, and the pile was set only when a door named one. Each open now starts on the door's pile, or on every pile.
- **Row 19 — a job switch left things open.** The reset on `selected.jobId` now also clears the skip reason, the word channel, the GC okay box and its note, the counsel ask and its text, and the letter two menu.
