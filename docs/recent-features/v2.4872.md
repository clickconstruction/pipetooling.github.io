# v2.4872 — Lien desk: the leader’s phone buzzes when a notice needs his approval (2026-10-07)

One new edge function (`notify-lien-approval`), no migration. Grace, 2026-10-07, on the live desk: *On the bottom right I send for approval but leader only approves if he's standing over me.* **Send for approval** moved the item to *Awaiting approval* and told nobody; the leader learned of it only by opening the Dashboard and seeing the *Approve a lien notice* card. The plan (mock-up https://claude.ai/artifact/LDQRcauAuVySHHZF7SYFvs, spot 1): his phone buzzes the moment it is sent, one line, the tap lands on the notice. PR 1 of 3; the one-screen phone approve (spot 2) and the named steps in place of the four dots (spot 3) follow.

## What changed

- **[`_shared/lienApprovalPush.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/lienApprovalPush.ts)**, pure, re-exported at `src/lib/jobs/lienApprovalPush.ts`: `lienApprovalPush({ jobLabel, jobId, claim, gcName, dueYmd, todayYmd, senderName })` → the push's title (*Approve a lien notice*), body (*891 · Take 5 Liberty Hill · $27,199 owed by Burd & Assoc. · mail by Oct 15 · 8 days*), tag, the email's subject and text, and the url, `lienApprovalUrl(jobId)` — the door v2.4586 and v2.4854 already made: the desk on the awaiting pile, this job selected. What it does not know it leaves out; a day that passed gets no count.
- **`notify-lien-approval`** ([`EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`](../EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md#notify-lien-approval)), cloned from `notify-bid-mark`'s shape: the body is the item id and, optionally, the mail-by day; the function refuses unless the caller is the drafter and the item is awaiting approval, and writes the words itself. The leader is the job's master when he holds a leader role, else every leader on the roster but the caller (`.match(REAL_ACCOUNT)`). One push per device; with no device the same words go by email (`sendEmailViaResend`, `emailType: 'lien_approval_ask'`, no sent copy — our own staff). `notification_history` gets one row per leader reached, `channel` push or email. `config.toml`: `verify_jwt = false`.
- **The desk** ([`lienDeskIo.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDeskIo.ts) `tellLeaderOfLienApproval`, `leaderToldWords`): `sendToLeader` calls it after `submitLienDeskItem` only when the outcome is `awaiting_approval` — never when a standing rule approved or held the draft — with `selected.earliestDeadline`, best effort, and the toast says which way it went: *Sent for approval. Malachi's phone has it.* / *… has it by email.* / *… will see it on the Dashboard.* The GC run (`GcOnNoticeModal`) is untouched: its Approve all is the leader's own act.
