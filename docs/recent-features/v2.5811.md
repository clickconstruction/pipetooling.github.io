# v2.5811 — Messages: Who is this? makes a trade partner, a supply house or a candidate in one press, and the side folds (2026-10-24)

A design spike with the owner on 2026-10-23, on an Invitation to Bid's thread on `/messages`. Three asks, in order: "Can you help me understand why trade partners, supply houses and candidates are not able to be selected?" then "Yes, prototype all three: a new trade partner, a supply house and a candidate each made and named in one press, like A new customer"; "Should we move the who is this blue button underneath the name into that section? Perhaps in the top right hand corner?"; and, on the empty right rail of a candidate's thread, "Perhaps we should make it more clear that nothing exists there and make it expandable with a greater than sign below the close sign in the top right hand corner."

- **Why the three were greyed.** A press on the card names the sender as a record that exists, through `name_contact`; a guess with no record waited with the place to make it. Only *A new customer* (v2.5795) made its own record. A new trade partner carried no record unless a known trade partner's domain matched; a supply house and a candidate never did.
- **The kernel** ([`newLane.ts`](../../src/lib/followUp/newLane.ts), [tested](../../src/lib/followUp/newLane.test.ts)): `NewRecordMake` on `WhoGuess.makesRecord` (the table, the field's label and starting words, the hint, the press, and for a trade partner the trade); `companyFromDomain` spells the company from the address's domain (*maxxbuilders.com* reads *Maxxbuilders*), a guess the office fixes in the field; `newTradeGuess`, `newSupplyHouseGuess`, `candidateGuess`. [`newLaneRows.ts`](../../src/lib/followUp/newLaneRows.ts): `guessWait` answers null for a `makesRecord` guess; `namedWords` says what each press did; **`laneRowFor` draws no card for a supply house or a candidate**, whom Follow up never holds (`OFF_FOLLOW_UP`), a gap since R7 that the new presses showed at once. The candidate's words were wrong and now read *Puts them on the hiring board under Team. Their lines stay off Follow up.*
- **The presses** ([`newLaneCardIo.ts`](../../src/lib/followUp/newLaneCardIo.ts), `makeRecord`): a trade partner through `gc_add_company` with the sender as its contact and the trade picked, not yet known, so it waits for approval like any company added on Trade partners; a supply house row with the sender as its default rep when the line names them and they wrote from an address, else the thread is the counter; a candidate on the Team hiring board under no role, ranked last there (`nextTeamProspectRank`), source *Messages*.
- **The card** ([`NewLaneCard.tsx`](../../src/components/followUp/NewLaneCard.tsx)): a `makesRecord` row opens its field in place, one at a time, with Back; the trade partner's block adds a Trade picker from the GC plans' templates, since the server refuses a company with no trade. The blue *Who is this?* draws at the card's top right while the card asks who (the search for anyone, the same door as *Someone else*); [`NewLaneSlot.tsx`](../../src/components/followUp/NewLaneSlot.tsx) tells the window through `acts.whoDrawn` ([`threadSlots.tsx`](../../src/components/gc/messages/threadSlots.tsx)) and [`ThreadWindow.tsx`](../../src/components/gc/messages/ThreadWindow.tsx) drops its header button meanwhile.
- **The side folds** ([`ThreadWindow.tsx`](../../src/components/gc/messages/ThreadWindow.tsx), [`FlowTab.tsx`](../../src/components/flows/FlowTab.tsx)): the flow rail reports whether it has anything to show (`onHas`, `SideActs` on the `side` slot); the window keeps the fold keyed by the open person, folds the side by itself when it has nothing, and draws ‹ or › under the × (*Hide their flow*, *Show their flow*). An opened empty side says why in words (`noFlowWords` in [`facts.ts`](../../src/lib/flows/facts.ts)). The phone layout is untouched.
- **The crash.** [`useFlowPerson.ts`](../../src/hooks/useFlowPerson.ts) read a candidate's flow as the unknown flow, which no longer exists, and died on `flow.kind`, drawing a red `!`. `hasFlow` sends a lead, a supply house, a candidate or a teammate to `state: 'none'` instead.
- **Tests**: the kernel's, `NewLaneSlot.render.test.tsx` (the candidate press, the blue button, the window told), `ThreadWindow.flowRail.render.test.tsx` (the fold on a named person, a supply house folded by itself), `facts.test.ts`.
- **Guide**: *sort new messages* § Who is this?. Docs: `MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `GLOSSARY.md`, `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` §22.
