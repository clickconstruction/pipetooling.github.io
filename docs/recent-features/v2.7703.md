# v2.7703 — Trips: the ground for a number on each trip (2026-11-17)

Punch list #379: the number's migration alone, asked for by the owner on 2026-11-17 before *Put on a bill*. No client code and no edge function. Not pushed by this PR. Stacked on the phone's migration (v2.7700), whose two functions it restates.

## Why

A trip was told by its days alone. That is right on a screen, where nobody has to type a name, and thin everywhere else: in a text, an email or a bill line there was only "the Oct 23 trip", and two crews out on the same days were told apart only by who went. A number the database hands out gives a handle to say and to cite, and cannot drift into five names the way typed names did.

## What landed

- `supabase/migrations/20261118040000_trip_numbers.sql`, written up whole in [`docs/migrations/20261118040000_trip_numbers.md`](../migrations/20261118040000_trip_numbers.md): `trips.trip_number`, the counter table that keeps a number spent after its trip is deleted, the trigger that hands the number out and holds it, and `trip_save` and `trip_phone_state` restated to name it.
- The bed `supabase/tests/trips/40_numbers.sql`, run by `scripts/pgtest-trips.sh`.
- `docs/ACCESS_CONTROL.md` and the glossary's *Trip* entry gain the number.

## Owed
