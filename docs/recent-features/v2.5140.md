# v2.5140 — Splitting a bill keeps its trip charge or card fee (2026-10-10)

Punch list #105, gap 2 (`to-dos/bill-riders-survive-delete-and-split.md`). Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## Why

A fee that rides on a bill is a `jobs_ledger_invoices.fee_lines` entry: a turnaway trip charge (v2.5129) or a GC card fee (v2.5113). Every rewrite of the job's revenue adds it back from there (`job_rider_fees`, `riderFeeLineCents`). **Split** (`SplitBillModal`) voids the bill, removes its row and inserts one Ready to Bill part per slice with no `fee_lines`. So the rider left with the old row, and the next money edit dropped it from the job's total while the parts still added up to it. A returned check fee never sits on a Stripe bill, so only these two were exposed.

## What shipped

- **`splitBillFeeLinesByPart`** (`src/lib/splitBillParts.ts`): each of the bill's fee lines goes on the first part with room left for its amount, so the first part takes them all unless one is larger than it. A line no part can hold goes on the part with the most room, so it is never lost. Every entry is carried as it was, not only the riders.
- **Split** reads the bill's `fee_lines` fresh before the void, since the bill view's row may not carry them. A failed read stops the split with *Could not read the bill's fees, so nothing was split*, before anything is voided. Each part's insert carries its entries; a part with none is inserted as before. `create-stripe-invoice` never reads `fee_lines`, so the parts' Stripe bills are unchanged.
- **The help guide** *split a bill so a customer can pay with multiple cards* gains one line under Good to know.
- Left as the card says: a GC card bill's split still loses the card bill's link, which is GC MODE's to weigh.

## Proof

- `splitBillParts.test.ts` (4 new): all on the first part when it has room; a fee larger than the first part goes to the next with room; a fee no part can hold goes to the part with the most room; no fee lines carries nothing.
- `SplitBillModal.riders.render.test.tsx` (3), red on `main` (3 of 3): a $150 trip charge on a $250 bill split $100 / $150 rides on the second part; a $60 one rides on the first; a failed fee read voids nothing and says so.
- The Split suites, the guide checks, eslint, the theme check and the full typecheck pass.

## To see it

On a ZZ TEST job, make a turnaway trip charge and send its bill as a Stripe bill. **Split** it into two parts, then make any money edit in Edit Job. The job's total keeps the charge, and the part that took it shows the trip charge row on its printed bill.
