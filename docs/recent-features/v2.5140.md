# v2.5140 — Splitting a bill keeps its trip charge (2026-10-10)

Punch list #105, gap 2 (`to-dos/bill-riders-survive-delete-and-split.md`). Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## Why

A fee that rides on a bill is a `jobs_ledger_invoices.fee_lines` entry: a turnaway trip charge (v2.5129), a GC card fee (v2.5113) or a returned check fee (v2.5033). Every rewrite of the job's revenue adds it back from there (`job_rider_fees`, `riderFeeLineCents`). **Split** (`SplitBillModal`) voids the bill, removes its row and inserts one Ready to Bill part per slice with no `fee_lines`. So the rider left with the old row, and the next money edit dropped it from the job's total while the parts still added up to it.

## What shipped

- **`placeSplitBillFeeLines`** (`src/lib/splitBillParts.ts`): each of the bill's fee lines goes on the first part with room left for its amount, so the first part takes them all unless one is larger than it. No part holds more fees than its own amount: a fee no part has room for refuses the split, in words (*The $99.00 trip charge on this bill needs a part of at least $99.00.*). Every entry is carried as it was, not only the riders.
- **A bill that carries a GC card fee is not split** (`splitBillCardFeeRefusal`): *This bill carries the GC's card fee, so it cannot be split. A card bill stays one bill.* Its entry names the card bill, and `gc_owner_card_bills` points at this bill, so a part carrying it would name a bill that is gone and leave the card bill `on_card`, and the GC's figures would overstate. The card bill's half is GC mode's, as the card says.
- **`validateSplitBillParts`** takes the bill's fee lines as a third argument: both refusals come back as fee refusals (`fees: true`), which the editor says under the parts, and an accepted split hands back `feeLinesByPart`.
- **Split** reads the bill's `fee_lines` when it opens (the bill view's row may not carry them) and holds the press until they land, then reads them again just before the void and checks the split against that read. A failed read, or a bill row that is not there although the read raised no error, stops it in words before anything is voided. Each part's insert carries its entries; a part with none is inserted as before. `create-stripe-invoice` never reads `fee_lines`, so the parts' Stripe bills are unchanged.
- **The help guide** *split a bill so a customer can pay with multiple cards* gains two lines under Good to know.

## Left for gap 1

A returned check fee's entry (one that names its case) is carried to a part like any other line. But the void deletes the bill's row, so the case's `fee_invoice_id` goes null (its key is `ON DELETE SET NULL`), and the case then reads as if its fee were on a bill it cannot find. That is #105's gap 1, the delete, which the planned trigger covers. `add_ar_return_case_fee` refuses a Stripe bill, so such a fee reaches a split bill only if the bill went out on Stripe after the fee went on.

## Proof

- `splitBillParts.test.ts` (6 new): all on the first part when it has room; a fee larger than the first part goes to the next with room; a fee no part has room for is refused in words; no fee lines carries nothing; a card fee refuses and nothing else does; `validateSplitBillParts` says both as fee refusals and hands back the placement.
- `SplitBillModal.riders.render.test.tsx` (6), red on `main` (6 of 6): a $150 trip charge on a $250 bill split $100 / $150 rides on the second part; a $60 one rides on the first; a failed read, a missing bill row, a card fee and a $200 charge split $100 / $150 each hold the press in words and void nothing.
- `SplitBillModal.render.test.tsx` now hands Split a bill row for its fee read.
- The Split suites, the guide checks, eslint, the theme check and the full typecheck pass.

## To see it

On a ZZ TEST job, make a turnaway trip charge and send its bill as a Stripe bill. **Split** it into two parts, then make any money edit in Edit Job. The job's total keeps the charge, and the part that took it shows the trip charge row on its printed bill. Type a first part smaller than the charge and the editor says which part size the charge needs.
