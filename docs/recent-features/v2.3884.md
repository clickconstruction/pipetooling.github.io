# v2.3884 — Schedule on a phone: the Day starts with the crews (2026-09-27)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Punch list **#30**, PR 4b-1 (`to-dos/taunya-mobile.md`; the mock-up `to-dos/taunya-mobile-pr4-inbox-day.html`). The owner's call of 2026-09-27: the crews are leaders, **superintendents**, subcontractors and helpers, in that order.

## What shipped

- **`src/lib/schedule/dayPhoneGroups.ts`** (new, pure) — `buildDayPhoneGroups` splits the roster three ways: a person with no block on the day is *Free* whatever the role; with a block, a crew role (`DAY_PHONE_CREW_ROLES`) is *Crews*, anyone else *Office*. Each group keeps its role sections (the crews in the owner's order), the chip counts are people, and `crewBlocksWithoutNote` counts crew blocks with an empty note. `dayPhoneDefaultGroup` opens on the crews, or the first group with anyone in it; `dayPhoneVisibleSections` draws the picked group, and every group while a search is typed.
- **`QuickfillScheduleSection.tsx`** (agenda mode — the section under 640 px, wherever it is mounted: the Schedule page's Day tab and Quickfill; Dispatch Mode's Schedule tab is its own agenda and is not touched) — the chips replace the full role list; the compact header loses the *Hide assistants and estimators* toggle, and on a phone the stored toggle filters nothing (the Office chip does that job); the date row and the chips are one sticky bar; an empty group says so in words. Above 640 px nothing changes.
- **The guide** *schedule-dispatch* → *The Day view on a phone*.
