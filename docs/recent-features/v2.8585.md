# v2.8585 — Messages at large text: the reply box's small caption stays on one line (2026-11-25)

Punch list #348, from the simulator's third sitting on 2026-11-25, in Safari at 402 points with the text at 125%. No migration, no edge function.

**Seen.** In the open reply box on a ZZ thread, with nothing typed, the grey words wrapped to two lines. The small caption at the foot of the box, *from the company number …*, wrapped to two lines too, and its first line sat on the grey words' second line. At 100% the caption is one line and clears them.

**The cause.** In `ReplyBox.tsx` the caption is drawn over the foot of the textarea, pinned to the box's right and bottom, with no left edge and no line limit. So a caption too wide for the box grew upward, into the rows the grey words use.

**The mend.** The caption is held to one line between the box's two sides: a left edge, `white-space: nowrap`, hidden overflow with dots, and right aligned as it was. The open box has three rows. Two lines of grey words take the first two and the caption's one line takes the third.

**Not built: one more row on a phone.** The brief's pick added a row of height to the blank open box so the grey words would clear the caption. With the caption held to one line they clear it already. And the same sitting saw the row of *Tie to*, **Attach** and **Send text…** under Safari's keyboard bar at 125%: a taller box would push that row further down. If grey words ever reach three lines, a long name at larger text, they would meet the caption again. That is not seen at 125%.
