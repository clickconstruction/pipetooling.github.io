# v2.2021 — Pricing Workbench guided tour (spotlight coach marks) (2026-08-21)

Option 2 of the 2026-08-21 help-icon mockups (owner picked the spotlight tour over the static modal). New **generic** [`SpotlightTour`](../../src/components/SpotlightTour.tsx) component: portals a dim overlay whose "hole" is the current step's `data-tour="<anchor>"` element (dim rendered as the hole's 200vmax box-shadow so the cutout stays crisp), scrolls the anchor to center (smooth, with a 700ms instant-jump fallback since rAF-driven smooth scrolling never progresses in hidden tabs, and `focus({ preventScroll })` on the card — a plain focus cancels the in-flight smooth scroll), re-measures on a 120ms cadence + resize, and walks steps with Next/Back/Skip, ←/→ keys, and Esc. Placement math is the pure kernel [`spotlightTourPlacement.ts`](../../src/lib/spotlightTourPlacement.ts) (`placeTourCard` below→above→bottom-pinned, `spotlightHole` clamped to viewport; 8 tests). `spotlightTourStepsPresent` filters steps to anchors actually in the DOM.

Workbench wiring in [`BidsPricingTab.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/BidsPricingTab.tsx): a round "?" button after the New-pill caption starts the 4-stop tour — `workbench-scenarios` (cards row), `workbench-summary` (sticky strip), `workbench-solver` (solver grid), `workbench-rows` (rows table; taller-than-viewport anchors clamp correctly). Last stop deep-links `/help?g=price-a-bid-with-the-workbench`; empty Workbench shows an info toast instead. Guide gains a "take the tour" line. Tour stops reuse the v2.2013 vocabulary (viewing vs ★ customer-facing, whole-bid targets, previews discarded on switch). Verified live on BP298 through all four stops. Client-only — no migration.
