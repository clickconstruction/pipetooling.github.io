# v2.1931 — "No bill line" filter for the fix-dates money move (2026-08-20)

The Pipeline New view's "64 bills have no bill date" move was doubly wrong:
its button (`Open Billed`) just scrolled to the full 117-row section with no
way to find the 64, and the copy misdiagnosed the data — prod has ZERO billed
invoice rows missing dates (the `jobs_ledger_invoices_billed_at_fn` trigger
guarantees them). All 64 are **job-shell rows**: billed jobs with open money
but no billed invoice line at all (113 billed jobs vs 58 billed lines),
created by direct status flips (Edit Job stepper / `update_job_status`) or
legacy backfill — Bill Customer always creates the line.

- Money move copy now tells the truth: "N billed jobs have no bill line —
  their money can't age, be chased, or be forecast; each needs its bill line
  created", action **Show them**.
- **Show them** mirrors Chase-90's pattern: activates the new `no_line`
  billed-header filter and jumps to the section. The filter's chip —
  `No line · N · $X` (neutral tint) — sits next to the 30+/90+ aging chips
  and is clickable any time; the active-filter status line explains the fix.
- Filtered rows wear an amber **No bill line** tag (rendered via the
  billed-section chip renderer, which now handles job-shell rows instead of
  skipping them).
- Kernels ([invoiceBilling.ts](../../src/lib/jobs/invoiceBilling.ts)):
  `billedStageRowHasNoBillLine` (row predicate) + `buildBilledNoLineBucket`
  (count + open sum); `countBilledRowsMissingDates` refactored onto them
  (behavior unchanged). `PipelineOverview` gains an `onFixDates` prop.
- Follow-ups shipping separately: a "fix bill lines in one sitting" modal
  (creates the missing lines with real bill-out dates) and a guard on the
  Edit Job status stepper so new shells stop being minted.
- Help guide `read-the-pipeline-money-view` updated.
