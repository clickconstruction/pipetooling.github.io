# v2.5531 — Lien desk: hover any mark on Deadlines and read its card (2026-10-14)

The owner's ask of 2026-10-14: hover any icon on the Deadlines board and see its details; icons side by side get one larger card; a card that grows very wide scrolls left and right. Every mark carried a browser tooltip until now: one line, a second late, one mark at a time, and nothing to press. Designed on a canvas (https://claude.ai/artifact/Fx2Jqmh9ZBjmWGwjuSauex: the first cut, the critique, the refined cards) and built from the refined version. No migration, no edge function.

- **The critique that shaped it** ("is this the best we can do"): the first cut put the tooltip's sentence in a box. What a person wants on a hover is how long they have (*tomorrow*, *in 6 days*, *36 days ago*, in the week's colour), how much rides on it (the money, on the card), which jobs a GC row's *6* stands for (listed, each with its month and money), and what to do (every card ends on the board's own door). Marks that touch read together, a column per mark in their order; past the track's width, the columns scroll.
- **`src/lib/jobs/lienMarkCards.ts`** (new, pure): `lienMarkCard(mark, job, todayYmd, doors)` writes one mark's card — a glyph, a title (*Notice owed · August*, *In the mail · July*, *Lien deadline*, *Lien gone*, *Pay date they gave*, *No pay date yet*, *Last day worked*, *No approved hours*, *Room*, *File first*, *Kind not set*), a dated line (*Send by Oct 15 · tomorrow*, `daysWords` + `deadlineTone`), plain-words facts (`kindWords` reads the property back from the dates, `payVersusLien` says the pay date against the flag) and the door the tab can open (`LienMarkDoors`: `lien_window` · `pen` · `last_work` · `record` · `kinds` · `draft`). `lienMarkClusters` chains the point marks closer than one flag's width (`clusterByPct`); areas (the run, the bracket, the grey wash) keep their own card. `lienGroupFlagCards` turns a GC row's folded flag into a card per kind present (notices owed, in the mail, liens to file), each listing its jobs, the sum across them and the bar's door for exactly those job ids; `lienGroupWordCard` is the GC's dot. A filed lien draws no flag, so it has no card.
- **`src/components/jobs/LienMarkHoverCard.tsx`** (new): the card, `role="group"` labelled *About 881 PLUM* / *About RMC · Dudley Mason*, one column per card (`lien-mark-card-col`, 236 px) in a flex row that scrolls sideways (`scrolls ▸` in the corner when it does), each with its glyph, title, dated line (`lien-mark-card-when`), the jobs (`lien-mark-card-job`), the facts and the door (`lien-mark-card-door`). A layout effect keeps it inside the track (slides left near the right edge) and opens it above the row when the list (`data-lien-cal-list`) has no room below.
- **`LienDeskCalendarTab.tsx`**: `useMarkHover` holds the two timers (open 120 ms into a hover or on focus, close 220 ms after leaving the mark and the card, at once on a press or on Esc, which a window listener in the capture phase hears wherever the focus is and keeps from the desk under it); `touchPct` measures one flag's width as a share of the track when a hover starts; every mark's `title` is gone (its words stay as the `aria-label`, `role="img"` on the marks that are not buttons). `Track` takes `todayYmd` and `onKinds` (the bracket's *Set the kind ›*, the pen's `setKindsOpen`); `GroupTrack` takes `onDraft` and `onRecord` through `GroupHeader`, and folds its dot and its flags by date into clusters too, so the GC's dot on the flag's day shares the card. A press on a mark still does what it did (the card steps aside first).
- **Tests**: `lienMarkCards.test.ts` (15: every card kind, the days words and tones, the clusters, the GC flag cards and the GC's word, and every fact on every card against the plain-words rules); `LienDeskCalendarTab.hover.render.test.tsx` (5: the card and its door, the timers and Esc and focus, the two-column card for a pay dot and a flag on one day with the pen as its door, the run's and the bracket's cards, the GC row's flag listing its jobs with *Draft the 2 ›* and the kinds door); the existing render smoke reads the marks by `aria-label` where it read `title`.
- **Guide** *see what is due on the lien calendar* gains **Read a mark**; the Glossary's *Lien calendar* entry and `docs/LIEN_DESK_ARCHITECTURE.md` carry the note.
