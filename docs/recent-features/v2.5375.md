# v2.5375 — Lien desk: the rules behind sending a notice by certified mail from the app, behind the scenes (Docsmit train, PR 1) (2026-10-12)

PR 1 of the Docsmit train (`docs/DOCSMIT_LIEN_RUN_PLAN.md`, *The PR train*): the pure rules the run window, the provider function and the poll will share. No screen, no schema, no provider call. The playable mock stays at `docs/prototypes/lien-docsmit-prototype.html`.

**A party** (`src/lib/mail/mailAddress.ts`): `mailParty(name, address)` turns the envelope's one-line name and address into what Docsmit's `/parties` takes — `organization` or `firstName` + `lastName`, `address1`, `address2`, `city`, a two-letter `state`, a ZIP or ZIP+4. A name with a company word, a digit, one word or more than four goes whole as the organization (*RIZVI SYED ZULFIQAR & KIZILBASH QURATULAIN FATIMA* stays whole; *John A. Backham* splits on the last space). The address reuses the run's `runAddressLines` and the label CSV's `splitCityStateZip` (v2.4977), including its no-comma rule (*311 E Illinois Ave. Palatine, Il 60067*), and lifts a suite, floor or unit off the street line so the window shows the street alone. `mailPartyProblems` names what stops a party in the provider's terms. CASS stays on the server.

**A piece** (`src/lib/mail/mailPiece.ts`): `mailEnvelopeFor(pages, sided)` — a #10 to 5 sheets, a Flat to 47, held with a reason past that; `DOCSMIT_SEND_TYPES` and `docsmitSendType(receipt)` (D1: *Certified, Electronic Return Receipt* by default, the green card per envelope); `mailClientMsgId(kind, pieceId)` = `kind:pieceId` and its parser, so a poll can match what comes back; `mailStatusFromProvider` folds `sent`, the USPS number and Docsmit's `DeliveryStatus` string into `prepared | sent | mailed | in_transit | notice_left | delivered | returned | failed | unknown` — a string the fold has not seen goes to `unknown`, the needs-a-person bucket, never guessed; `mailStatusOpen` says which statuses still want the poll; `mailSendBy(windowLastYmd, { bufferBusinessDays, cutoffLocal })` and `mailSendIsLate` turn the notice window's last day into *Send to Docsmit by 2026-10-14 at 3:00 pm* (`subtractBusinessDays` walks back over weekends; holidays are not modeled, like `addBusinessDays`).

**Coming, not owed** (`lienSendTracking.ts`): a send in a filing's `sends[]` may carry `via` (the provider) and `piece_id`. `sendTrackingComing` is true for a certified or courier send with a blank number and a live piece; `sendNeedsTracking` is false for it, so the Dashboard's *tracking owed* watch, the Sent footer and the Filings list stop asking the office for a number USPS has not assigned yet. `sendsTrackingComing` lists them; `parseLienSends` keeps both fields and `withTracking` writes the number in beside them.

**Letter two from the later clock** (`lienLetterTwo.ts`, D9): `letterTwoStatus` and `letterTwoByJobFrom` take optional `mailClocks` keyed by the first packet's item id (`{ mailedAt, deliveredAt, status }`). Day 0 is the day the run recorded the packet unless the piece knows better: the day USPS accepted it, then the day it was delivered, never earlier than the record. `LetterTwoStatus.clock` carries `{ kind: recorded | mailed | delivered, at, why }` (*from the day USPS accepted it, 2026-09-06; the piece came back*), and the row words add *· from delivery* / *· from the mailing, returned* when the clock is not the record's. No caller passes clocks yet; that comes with the `mail_pieces` table (PR 2) and the poll (PR 3).

**Tests**: `mailAddress.test.ts` (3), `mailPiece.test.ts` (4), two new cases in `lienSendTracking.test.ts` and four in `lienLetterTwo.test.ts`; the three fixtures that spell a `LetterTwoStatus` by hand gained `clock: null`.
