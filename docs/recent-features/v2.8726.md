# v2.8726 — The office can lift a Build job's wait from the job window (punch list #192, write A, part 3) (2026-11-27)

The last piece of write A of [`to-dos/bridge-stage-desks/README.md`](../../to-dos/bridge-stage-desks/README.md), drawn in [`mockups/build-waits-with-reason.html`](../../to-dos/bridge-stage-desks/mockups/build-waits-with-reason.html) as *The job window · Edit Job*. Client only. The table and the function are v2.8709's and are on prod, so this PR owes nothing.

## Why

v2.8723 put **Lift the wait** on the Bridge's Build desk alone. The owner and a dev open the Bridge. The office does not, and the office is who hears that the GC is ready. `set_job_build_wait` already takes the whole office, so only the press was missing.

## What landed

- **A Waits row on the job window's Edit tab** (`JobBuildWaitFactRow.tsx`, mounted in `JobFormModal.tsx` under the status rail): *Waits · Since Oct 26 · the GC is not ready for us · Dana*. A line the person typed beside a picked reason shows under it. It draws nothing on a job that does not wait, and nothing when the read is refused, so the tab draws as it did.
- **Lift the wait** on that row, for the office alone (`isOfficeRole`: a dev, the owner, an assistant, the controller), the people the function takes. Anyone else who opens the tab reads the row and gets no press. One press, as on the desk. A green toast says the wait is lifted, and the row leaves when the read lands.
- **A refusal says its reason under the row** (`formatErrorMessage`), and the row and its press stay.
- **Values in place.** The day, the reason and the name are values the app worked out, so the kernel marks each with `val` (`buildWaitFactSentence`) and the row draws them with `ValueSentence`, bold and apart from the words. `buildWaitFactWords` is the same sentence read plain.
- **One small read** when the tab opens on a job (`buildWaitsIo.loadOpenForJob`, `useJobBuildWait`): that job's wait that is not lifted, under the reader's own rights. It reads again on `BUILD_WAITS_CHANGED_EVENT`, so a wait marked or lifted elsewhere on the page shows without a reload.
- The chip's tooltip on Jobs → Stages now says the office lifts the wait on the job's Edit tab.
- Guides: *move between a job's Job, Edit, Bill, and Costs tabs* → The Edit tab reads as rows, and *read the Bridge* → A job that waits, with a reason.

## What differs from the drawing, and why

- The mock-up drew the row between Status and Percent in one list. The Edit tab's status is the rail, not a row, so the row is a box of its own under the rail. On a phone the rail is on the bar, and the row still shows on the tab.
- The typed line is drawn under the row. The drawing did not show one.

## Proved, and what is not

- `JobBuildWaitFactRow.render.test.tsx` (7): nothing on a job that does not wait, the three values drawn apart, another reason said once, no press outside the office, the lift and its toast with the row leaving, a refusal under the row, a wait marked elsewhere showing.
- `buildWaits.test.ts`: the marked sentence and the second line. `buildWaitsIo.test.ts` (2 new): the one-job read and its three nulls.
- The job form's own render tests and the guide tests pass with the row mounted.
- **Nothing was written on prod and no live site was opened.** No wait was marked or lifted on any job. Every test runs over a stood-in io with made-up jobs.
- Not seen: the row on a real waiting job. That needs a wait marked first, which is a write, and is Taunya's or a walk's on a ZZ Test job.
