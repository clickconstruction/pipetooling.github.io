# v2.7801 — Lien desk: a closed paper opens a window that asks what to do (2026-11-18)

PR 3 of the closed end on the Lien desk's Board, the owner's ask and pick of 2026-11-18. PR 1 (v2.7793) is the kernel and PR 2 (v2.7796) draws the two closed bins. This one is the window and its presses. Client only: no migration, no edge function, no new write.

## Before

- A chip in a closed bin opened the paper, as a Missed chip always had. The pane's footer said the lien right was gone and offered nothing to do about it.
- The three things a person can do about a passed lien day lived in three places: the last day of work on the job's Lien window, *Not lienable…* on the desk's ⋯ menu, and *Uncollectible…* on the Pipeline's Collections rows.

## What changed

- **A closed chip opens the closed window.** `LienBoardDestination` gained `{ kind: 'closed' }`, the door of every chip in a `missed` bin, and its hover reads *See what closed and answer it*. Both closed bins carry *Answer N ›* on their title line (`lienClosedAnswerPress`, `quiet`, so the bin has no blue edge): it opens the window on the bin's first paper.
- **[`LienClosedWindow.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienClosedWindow.tsx)** draws `buildLienClosedWindow`: *What closed*, *What is still open* for a month a late notice can save, *Where that day comes from* with *This is the app's count. Ask the attorney when it is not clear.*, then *What do you want to do?*
- **A passed lien day, three answers.**
  - *The date is wrong*: **Set the last day of work ›** closes the window and opens the desk's own door to that day (`onOpenCalendarLastWork`). Under it, *Was it filed in time?* **Record the filing ›** opens the job's Lien window.
  - *Keep collecting*, the filled press: the job is taken off the lien path with its reason (`keepCollectingLienClosed` → `set_job_lien_path_answer`), and a job not yet in Collections is put there by the same function. It then stands in the bin *Off the lien path*, where **Put it back…** undoes it.
  - *Let it go*: **Mark N Uncollectible** asks once more on the row itself, *Yes, mark N Uncollectible* or *Not yet*. Then `letLienClosedJobGo` does what the Pipeline's confirm does: the Collections flag when the job is not there, `set_job_uncollectible`, then Stripe's mark on each billed invoice. A failure at Stripe never undoes the mark and the toast says it. The Legal desk's warning shows under the answer when a law firm holds the payer's account (`readLienClosedFirmWarning`, `uncollectibleFirmWarning`).
- **A closed month with the lien day ahead, three other answers**: **Draft the late notice ›** opens the notice, **Set the last day of work ›**, and **Record … closed**, which writes the noted miss of v2.3679 with a blank notice (`recordLienMonthsClosed`). No let-go answer is offered.
- **What is kept.** *A note to keep on the job* sits over the answers when the lien day has passed. The reason stored is the day, what the office chose and the note (`lienClosedReason`).
- **The walk.** The window counts a bin's papers, *1 of 2*. An answer drops its paper and shows the next, and the desk reads again behind it. *Next ›* passes one over. A paper that left the closed end while the window was open is passed over by itself.
- **Who may press.** The lien office answers. For anyone else each write press is dead with *The office answers this.* The functions the presses call refuse the same people.

## Not changed

- The Pipeline's own *Uncollectible…* window and the desk's *Not lienable…* window are as they were. `letLienClosedJobGo` repeats the Pipeline's steps and does not replace them.
- A closed lien day still leaves the desk seven days later, because the affidavit windows stop listing the job. Keeping it until someone answers takes a migration of its own.
