# v2.6089 — Phone first: every press a tech makes daily is a thumb's height (punch list #189, PR 3) (2026-10-29)

Punch list #189's PR 3, the one-thumb pass's second move: every daily target at 44 px or more. The walk of 2026-10-26 at 375 px found 33 px percent chips, 21 px filter pills and 20 px text links on the five screens a tech touches daily. PR 2 (v2.5905) moved the day's press to the bar and made the card's quiet links 44 px presses on a phone; PR 5a (v2.6076) gave the stage rows 48 px picks and 56 px steps; this PR takes the rest.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/phonePress.ts`**: the one source for the two heights (`PHONE_PRESS_MIN_HEIGHT` 44, `PHONE_PILL_MIN_HEIGHT` 32) and `activityPillStyle(narrow)`, the job window's pill at a desk or on a phone. Tested.
- **`ReportTemplatePercentField`** (the whole-job percent on a one-line job's report, and the office's New report): the five quick picks stand 44 px.
- **`JobThreadNotesPanel`** (the job window's activity): All · Notes · Reports and the ＋ Report chip beside them stand 32 px on a phone (`useNarrowViewport640`), with a little more padding and a 12 px word; the office's small chip at a desk is as it was.
- **`DashboardJobModeCard`**: the quiet links' 44 px on a phone (PR 2) now read the constant.
- **`phonePressTargets.render.test.tsx`** holds the heights at 375 px: the percent chips 44, a stage row's picks 48 and its −5 and +5 56 on the report and alone (`StagePctControl`), the pills 32 on a phone; jsdom lays nothing out, so each is held by the inline height it declares, as the job window's bar test holds its own.

## Decisions

- **The percent chips grow everywhere, not on a phone only.** The office's New report draws the same field; 44 px chips cost it nothing and the field has one style.
- **The pills grow on a phone only.** A tech presses them perhaps once a week (the card's *Is this the best we can do?*), so they stay the office's small chip at a desk and take 32 px, not 44, on a phone, as the mock-up drew them.
- **Nothing else moved.** The stepper's chips are PR 5a's control; the card's quiet links were PR 2's; the sheets' close by the thumb (Not now on On my way and I'm here) stays with the card's plan a, item 3, not built in PR 2 and not here.
