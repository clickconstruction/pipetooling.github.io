# v2.4459 — Customer profile: a bill marked billed in the evening counts from that day (2026-10-02)

One of the instant-day fixes that follow [v2.4452](v2.4452.md). No migration. [`_shared/customerProfileStats.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/customerProfileStats.ts) runs on the client (the profile modal and the customer page) and in `dev-mcp` (`get_customer`), which is redeployed after merge.

## What was wrong

The kernel's `ymdOf` cut every day by its first ten characters, and said so: *UTC slice, consistent with the board's date handling*. That is right for its `date` columns (`estimated_bill_date`, `paid_on`), but two readers also fed it `jobs_ledger_invoices.billed_at`, a `timestamptz`:

- `customerDaysToPay`: the billed → paid gap behind *pays in ~N days*. A bill marked billed after 7 pm CDT (6 pm CST) counted from the next day, one day short.
- `profileJobRowMoney`: a job row's oldest open bill date and its age, when the bill has no estimated date (`estimated_bill_date ?? billed_at`). It read one day late and one day young.

## What changed

Both read `billed_at` as `todayYmdInAppTz(new Date(iso))` from [`_shared/appTimeZone.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/appTimeZone.ts); the estimated date and `paid_on` keep `ymdOf`, whose comment now says what it is for. Both callers read `billed_at` straight from `jobs_ledger_invoices` (`fetchCustomerProfile`, `devMcpComposites`), so the kernel never sees a bare day there. Every writer of `billed_at` lands on the right Central day (status trigger `now()`, an office-typed day at 18:00 UTC, the shell RPC at noon Chicago).

## Not changed

The comment's "board" is the Jobs Stages board and its kin, which still slice `billed_at` (`billedReferenceYmd`, the Stages bill rows, the demand letter, the lien tools and more). The profile now reads an evening-billed bill a day earlier than those do, which is the right day. They are filed as their own sweep rather than widened into this fix.

## Tests

`customerProfileStats.test.ts` (+2): a bill marked billed at `2026-10-03T00:30:00Z` (7:30 pm CDT, also as `+00:00`) and paid Oct 12 counts 10 days, not 9; an open bill billed then with no estimated date dates its row Oct 2, 10 days old, and Dec 2 00:30 UTC reads Dec 1; an estimated date still wins. Both fail on main's kernel. Three fixtures that stamped a day as midnight UTC (the evening before in Central) move to 15:00 UTC and keep their expectations.

## Checked against prod

Read only, from the worktree dev server: 16 of the 530 billed or paid bills were marked billed in a Central evening. All 16 are paid: each one's billed → paid gap grows by a day, which moves a customer's *pays in ~N days* only where that gap is the median of the last 12 months. No open bill's row date changes today.
