# v2.4407 — Lien desk: one list of the jobs that also owe a supply house (2026-10-02)

No migration. One edge function changed: `send-lien-desk-summary` (redeploy). Step 2 of 4 of *supply houses on the Lien desk* ([v2.4404](v2.4404.md); canvas https://claude.ai/artifact/2VQBPxmixzF3ckkSaD2Wc2, boards 3c and 3e). The owner's ask was to "show what jobs from the lien desk also have job accounts with supply houses": step 1 marked each such row; this step shows them together and sends them.

## What shipped

- **The lens** ([`LienDeskCalendarTab`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskCalendarTab.tsx)): a storefront button in the Calendar's toolbar with the count of billed jobs that still owe a house (`housesOnly`). Pressed, the board is built from those rows alone, so the pills, the buckets and the date row count only them; the axis is still drawn from every row, so nothing shifts. It is a lens, not a fifth bucket: each job is still counted once at its next date. After the search button on a phone; at the right end on a computer, where its words *Houses owed* drop out below a 1,180 px toolbar (`.lienCalLensWord`, a container query) so the pills keep one line. Not drawn when no job on the board owes a house.
- **The list to send** ([`lienDeskShare.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDeskShare.ts)): `lienShareHouseJobs` takes every job the desk holds (the Calendar's rows, the notice queue, the affidavits) whose houses are not all paid: what is owed to us, what the houses are owed, how many houses, the house whose own notice comes first and that date (`lienSupplierNotice` for the kind on the job's property record), and whether a job account is open. `lienShareScopeOptions(data, houses)` puts **Jobs where a supply house is also owed** second in **What to send** when there is one (scope `houses`; its money is the houses' and reads *14 jobs · $58,061 to houses*). `buildLienStatusPayload` for that scope sends the list and nothing of the notices.
- **The renderers** ([`_shared/lienDeskStatus.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/lienDeskStatus.ts)): the payload's optional `houses`. When present, `lienStatusSubject`, `lienStatusText`, `lienStatusEmailText` and `lienStatusEmailHtml` say the list: three headline sentences, a line per job soonest house notice first (*• 650 ATI Schertz, Loberg Contracting. Us $15,722. 3 houses $15,007. Reece’s notice by Oct 15.*; twelve in the text, then *N more jobs*; all of them in the email, as a table with three tiles), and *A supply house can send its own notice on the same property. Each date is our estimate.* A GC's own full stop is not doubled and a job named for its GC says the name once. `parseLienStatusPayload` reads the list typed and capped and refuses one off its shape; a payload without it reads as before, so an older client and the new function agree.
- [`LienDeskShare`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskShare.tsx) takes the desk's `suppliers`; [`LienDeskSharePanel`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskSharePanel.tsx) words the choice's sub-line *first house notice by Oct 15*.
- Guides: [`see-which-supply-houses-are-owed-on-a-lien-job`](../../src/content/help/see-which-supply-houses-are-owed-on-a-lien-job.md) (two new sections), [`share-where-our-liens-stand`](../../src/content/help/share-where-our-liens-stand.md), [`see-what-is-due-on-the-lien-calendar`](../../src/content/help/see-what-is-due-on-the-lien-calendar.md).
