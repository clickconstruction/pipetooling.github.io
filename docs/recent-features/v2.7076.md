# v2.7076 — Materials: Same part? joins two names for one part (2026-11-12)

Punch list #330, PR 3 of 5. The site's screen (v2.7074) folds two typed names only when their words are the same, which joined about one name in twenty on the first site read. The rest is a person's call. This PR asks for it once and keeps the answer.

## What changed

- **The table** (`20261113040000_on_site_part_ties.sql`, [its page](../migrations/20261113040000_on_site_part_ties.md)): one row per answer, `same` or `different`, between two of the screen's row keys, scoped to a development or to one job on no site. Insert and delete, never update.
- **The rules** (`src/lib/materials/partTies.ts`), pure, 12 tests:
  - `nearlySame`: what may be asked. The numbers in the two names must be exactly the same, and the words differ by one word more (`2" 90` and `2" 90 pvc`) or by one word a letter or two apart (`san tee`, `sani tee`). Two names whose numbers differ are never offered.
  - `tieCanon`: the `same` answers read as groups, so names joined to names joined to names are one row. A group that holds a parts-book part takes the book part as its key, which PR 4 uses.
  - `samePartAsks`: at most one ask per row, each pair once, under the smaller row. A pair answered either way is never asked again, and neither is a pair where any name of one row was said to differ from any name of the other. Two parts-book parts are never asked about here.
  - `joinedTies`, `tieFor`, `askReasonWords`.
- **The roll-up takes the ties** (`sitePartRows(jobs, now, canon)`): a row tracks the keys it folds (`memberKeys`), and a row with a parts-book line reads by the book's name. **The fold still reads the finished lines.** Each spelling keeps its own line and count in its box.
- **The screen** (`SiteMaterialsView.tsx`): **Same part?** under a row, with the other name, how many it holds and where, why the two were offered, **Same part** and **Different**. A chip beside By place, **Same part? · 5**, narrows the list to the names to check. An open row lists **Names joined here** with who and when and **Undo**.
- **Who**: the roles that read the desk with its money answer. A field role reads the joins and is asked nothing.

## Before the push

The screen reads the table when it opens and treats a refused read as no answers. Until the migration is pushed the screen draws as in v2.7074 and offers no ask; a render test holds that.

## Checked

The migration on a throwaway Postgres 15, applied twice, 36 checks as eight made-up people (its page says what and the limits). The ask's rules ran over the first site's real names, read only: five pairs offered, each a fitting or a pump named with and without one word, and none across two sizes. Not walked on the live app: the table is not on prod until the push, and an answer is a write.
