# v2.5448 — Messages: a quick reply keeps its Spanish and its place on the tech’s phone, behind the scenes (2026-10-13)

Residential R3's screens, PR A of 2: the kernel and the IO (`to-dos/gc-mode/messages/RESIDENTIAL_BUILD_PLAN.md` → R3 on `spike/gc-mode`), by Customer communication helper 10, directed by cc. It is a draft carrying GC Review's quick replies editor (#5609 on #5500 on #5497), the blanks and the phone's lines (#5558), R3's SQL (#5588) and the steps per flow (#5596). The rules are the residential prototype's (`customer-messages-prototype.html` v0.8: `phoneTab`, `editRow`, `flags`). No migration and no screen. PR B draws the screens.

- **Deploy order** (the exception to client first): the IO reads `text_es`, `phone_order`, `phone_name`, `from_phone_order` and `to_phone_order`. Migration `20261013210000_quick_replies_phone_es` (#5588) goes to prod before this merges, or the quick replies stop loading.
- **A line's R3 columns** (`src/lib/quickReplies/kernel.ts`): `QuickReply` gains optional `textEs`, `phoneOrder` and `phoneName`. A line read before R3, or a fixture, has none. `phone.ts`'s `PhoneLine` still holds all three as read.
- **The Save** (`editQuickReply`) takes a `QuickReplyEdit`. A part left out keeps what the line has. A blank Spanish or name clears it, and a place of 0 or null takes the line off the phone.
  - It writes a version for each part that changed, in `edit_quick_reply`'s order: words, tags, Spanish, phone.
  - A phone version keeps the places before and after when the place changed, and neither when only the name changed.
  - It refuses the phone on a line that is not the team's for customers, in the SQL's words (`PHONE_ONLY_CUSTOMERS`), and a name over 60 characters.
- **The Phone tab in the kernel**:
  - `QrEditorTab` gains `phone`, `editorCounts` counts the lines on the phone (`onThePhone`), and `editorTabs` shows **Phone** after Mine once a line is there. An office that writes only to GCs never sees it.
  - `editorRows` gives the tab its lines, and the search now finds a line by its Spanish too.
- **`phoneTab.ts`** (new):
  - `phoneTabLines`: every line on the phone by its place, with a tie broken by id as the phone breaks one. It is not capped. `reachesPhone` says which six reach the phone.
  - `movePhone`: a move up or down numbers the list 1 to n and saves each line whose place changed. That is two saves on a list already numbered, and a list with gaps is numbered again in passing.
  - `nextPhonePlace`: a ticked line goes after the last.
  - `phoneTick` says when the tick cannot put a line on the phone.
  - `PHONE_TAB_WORDS` holds the tab's and the phone fields' words.
- **`spanish.ts`** (new): `spanishOffered` puts the Spanish box on a line for customers only. `lineMarks` gives a row its marks: ☎ phone, es or **no es**, and mine. `SPANISH_WORDS` holds the box's words.
- **The IO**:
  - `rows.ts` reads the three columns and the `es` and `phone` versions with their places, and it takes a line for any of the seven kinds (`QR_KIND_ORDER`, R0). Before this, it dropped a line for a lead or a sub.
  - `quickRepliesIo.ts`'s `edit` passes `p_text_es`, `p_phone_order` and `p_phone_name` only when given. A clear goes as blank and off the phone as 0. A Save of words and tags alone stays the four named arguments.
- **Not the prototype**: the editor's add row stays the words, the tags and Just for me. `add_quick_reply` takes no Spanish and no place, so both are set by opening the line once it is added. The prototype's add row had both.
- **Tests**:
  - `kernel.test.ts`: the R3 Save's versions in order, keeps and clears, the refusals, and the Phone tab's count, place and search.
  - `phoneTab.test.ts` and `spanish.test.ts` are new, over the prototype's lines.
  - `rows.test.ts` covers the new columns, the R3 versions and the seven kinds. `quickRepliesIo.test.ts` covers the three arguments.
