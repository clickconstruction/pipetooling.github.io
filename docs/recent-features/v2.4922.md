Owner Billing's O2b in the GC mode real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/OWNER_BILLING_REAL_BUILD.md` → *Kernels that move over with their tests*, on branch `spike/gc-mode`). Everything of billing the customer O2a left, now that the Building lane's kernels it reads are on main (U2), is lifted word for word from `src/lib/gcMode/` into `src/lib/gc/`. The tool is `to-dos/gc-mode/scripts/lift-extract.cjs`, with manifest `owner-billing-o2b.lift.json`, and `lift-same.cjs` reports every declaration and test the same as the spike's.

  - `sentPayAppLines`, `ownerAllBilled`, `ownerCloseout` and `ownerFinalPayAppToSend`;
  - the trades' waiver checks;
  - `ownerPayAppParties` and `ownerPayAppForm`, the G702 and G703;
  - `projectCash` and `allJobsMoney`.
- **New files:**
  - `ownerBillingDay.ts` (`billDay`) and `ownerBillingMargin.ts` (`jobMargin`, `allJobsMargin`);
  - `ownerBillingAhead.ts` (`cashMoves`, `cashAhead`);
  - `billingForecast.ts` (G-97) and `cashForecast.ts` (G-140);
  - `timeExtension.ts` (G-141) and `lateFinish.ts` (G-98), at `src/lib/gc/` because billing reads the schedule's kernels and no schedule kernel reads billing;

- **Tests:**
