# v2.5636 — Reminders, demand letters, lien notices and bills emailed again show in the job's trail (2026-10-19)

A review of the Pipeline row's Job activity (2026-10-19, after v2.5633 and v2.5634) found customer sends that left no line in `job_activity_events`, the table `list_job_activity_events` reads. One migration, `20261019030000_customer_sends_to_activity.sql` (`docs/migrations/`), and the client's registry and box.

- **Four new event types**, each written by an AFTER INSERT trigger on its own record and backfilled through the same function, guarded by `(event_type, source_id)`, all financial and in the Billing bucket (`JOB_ACTIVITY_EVENT_RENDER`): `contract_reminded` from the reminder cron's `reminded` row in `job_contract_events` (a `dry_run` row sent nothing and writes none; tag *Contract*, amber), `demand_letter_sent` from `job_demand_letters` (to whom, how, the amount and the pay-by day; tag *Demand*), `lien_notice_sent` from a `notice_53_056` or `retainage_53_057` row of `job_lien_filings` (each recipient and how, the amount; affidavits and releases of record are filed, not sent, and stay out; tag *Lien notice*), and `invoice_resent` from a paper bill's resend (tag *Resent*).
- **The paper resend's source, chosen**: `send-physical-invoice-email`'s `resend: true` returns before its update, so `sent_to_customer_at` never moves on a paper resend and a trigger on the stamp would catch nothing; it would also double every Stripe resend, which restamps the bill and is logged since v2.5634. The resend's one record is its sent copy (`sent_documents`, kind `bill_resent`, source the bill, since v2.4557), which is the paper path's sends log already, so the trigger reads it and no new table or function deploy is needed. A paper resend before 2026-10-05 left no record and gets no line.
- **Times**: a reminder and a resend at their own time; a letter or a notice at the time it was saved when that is the day the office says it went (in Chicago), else noon of that day (`job_activity_send_at`, which reads a day that is not a date as the saved time).
- **A line never costs the record**: each trigger catches its function's error as a warning, so a legal notice, a letter or a sent copy is kept even when its line cannot be written. The functions are revoked from `anon` and `authenticated`.
- **The Pipeline row's box**: `SENT_EVENT_TYPES` in `jobActivityBoxFeed.ts` adds `invoice_resent`, `demand_letter_sent` and `lien_notice_sent` as unnumbered Sent lines (*Bill emailed again*, *Demand letter sent*, *Lien notice sent* or *Retainage notice sent*), since each is a word to the customer about the bill the box already shows. `contract_reminded` stays out: the cron sends up to three on its own, and the contract's own send is not in the box either. The Sent tag's label reads *Sent to the customer*.
- **Proof**: the bed `scripts/pgtest-customer-sends-trail.sh` over `supabase/tests/customer_sends_trail/` (the migration applied twice over a seed with one row for each way a send can read; 30 checks: words, job, time and sender of each backfilled line, nothing for a dry run, a voided record, an affidavit, a bill's first copy or an orphan, new sends at once, the record kept while the trail refuses, the grants), registered in `scripts/sql-beds.txt`. Registry and box tests in `jobActivityEvent.test.ts` and `jobActivityBoxFeed.test.ts`.
- **Docs**: the Glossary's job activity entry, `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` (remind-job-contracts, send-physical-invoice-email), `LIEN_DESK_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `JOBS_STAGES_TAB_ARCHITECTURE.md`; the guides *read the Pipeline* and *open a job's activity full screen*.
- **Deploy**: client first (an old client hides an unknown type), then `supabase db push` after the merge. No edge function changes.
