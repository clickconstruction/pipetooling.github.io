# v2.8048 — Job pace: the superintendent's card says what it compares, in hours (2026-11-20)

Punch list #406. Client only. No migration and no edge function.

## Why

The owner walked the app on a phone on 2026-11-20 and circled the pace card's headline, *Spending 22 points ahead of the work.* His words: "I don't understand this section's place in the app, 'points' is not readily understandable to me as a user." He asked for a way to present it that teaches the what and the why.

The assessment found the card had no name and three unnamed percents. Its headline was a money number, and the card drew no money. A point was a percentage point of the money spent over the percent of work done. The card also could not say that a percent was weeks old. The owner was shown three ways to redraw it and picked the second, two bars.

## What landed

- **The rule** is `lib/jobMode/paceReading.ts`, pure and tested. It reads the same pace the Projects board reads and gives the crew's card its state, its words and its bar lengths. The states are no percent, no hours budget, too early, an old percent, hours ahead, on track and work ahead.
- **The face** is `PaceReadingFace.tsx`. The card's name, **Job pace**. A headline in hours. **Hours used** and **Work done** as two bars on one scale, with the gap hatched on the hours bar. One sentence of why, and the press that answers it. Each value in a sentence is marked with `val` and drawn bold.
- **Hours, never money.** A superintendent's read carries no dollar, and materials are bought before the work they are for. Money that leads is one grey line, *Money spent is ahead of the work. The office watches that.* It shows only when the hours do not already say it.
- **It asks before it warns.** When 8 or more hours were clocked on days after the percent was last said, the card is amber and asks *Is 40% still right?* with **Set the %** and **Still 40%**. It is red, *Hours are ahead of the work.*, only on a percent said since. Then it says the gap in hours and asks for the finish day.
- **Still 40%** is `lib/jobMode/paceStillIo.ts`. It files a Status Report at the same percent with a note, the save the **Set the %** window makes with the number left alone. The report's day is what the pace reads as the day the percent was last said. With no Status Report template it falls back to the bare percent write, and the card holds its own word for the sitting.
- **What is this?** is a fold of six plain sentences on what the card compares and why. It opens by itself the first time a person sees the card on a phone, kept in that phone's storage, and stays folded after that.
- **The hours left** read *56 hours left in the budget. About 2 days for this crew.* in place of *2 days of hours left*.
- **The folded row** on the short page carries the new headline.

## Faults fixed with it

- **Two weightings for one fact.** The job's percent is built from the stages by price. The rail spread that percent back over the stages by labor hours, and a stage's labor share is not its price share. A first stage marked 100% made the job 40%, and the rail then drew that stage at 98% and still in hand. `paceStageRail` now spreads by price where every row carries one (`paceStagePrices`), and by hours only when a price is missing.
- **A second percent on the press.** The stage in hand read *98% · Set the %*. The press is quiet now and carries no number. It is left off when the face carries **Set the %**.
- **No hours budget** read *Too early to call.* on the crew's card. It says *This job has no hours budget yet.* and that the office sets it.

## The office's words

- `superintendentLine.ts`: the headline for money that leads is *Money is ahead of the work.* The money line keeps both percents, *62% of the money spent at 40% done*.
- `JobCostsLineBudgets.tsx`: *spending 22 points ahead of the work* is now the dollars over where the work stands, *$1,980 over so far*. The other side already read *kept so far*.
- The tone, the sort and the counts on the Projects board are not changed.

## Not done

- The Job Budget card's sentence, *labor is 9 points ahead of progress* (`jobBudget.ts`), stands. It has a copy in the edge functions, so it is its own change with a deploy.
- A gap under one crew day still reads on track. On a small job that can be a large share of the budget.
- The percent on the card is changed through the **Set the %** window, not in place.
