# v2.3179 — Recorded-time costing: closed sessions count toward job cost before approval (2026-09-08)

Migration `20260909022534_recorded_time_costing.sql` (fragment in `docs/migrations/`). Second PR from the owner's 2026-09-08 J1007 question (v2.3178 named the number; this one moves it earlier). The investigation: every cost surface — Cost Timeline 👷 markers, the Job tab/Bill tab Team labor line, Job Summary's Labor column, the Pipeline board's man-hours — read `people_hours` + `people_crew_jobs`, and both are written **at approval**. Salaried sessions are system-materialized (`salary_schedule`) and auto-approved by cron **2 h after clock-out**, so Malachi's 8–4 day on J1007 closed at 4:00 PM and became job cost at 6:00 PM (`approved_at 23:00Z`, verified). Hourly punches waited on a human: 14 pending punches / 60.7 h in the prior 14 days were in no job's cost. Owner decision: **approval is the payroll gate, not the costing gate.**

## Two definitions

- **Approved time** — unchanged: `people_hours`, pay stubs, Draft Payroll, the People → Overhead pool, the Moneyfill week close, the unallocated-time queue (`peopleHoursUnallocatedRows` — deliberately approval-based on both sides, unchanged).
- **Recorded time** — every session not rejected/revoked: closed sessions approved **or awaiting approval**, plus (for the day split only) the session open right now. Job costing reads this.

## What shipped (database — `20260909022534`)

- **View `people_hours_recorded`** (`security_invoker`): `people_hours` + closed-unapproved session hours per (person, day); columns `hours` / `approved_hours` / `pending_hours`.
- **`sync_crew_jobs_from_clock` / `sync_crew_bids_from_clock`** count recorded sessions (was approved-only), open sessions to `now()` floored at one minute — so a salaried person's flat 8 h lands on the right job at clock-in. Shared-denominator math from `20260903153903` intact.
- **Trigger `clock_sessions_sync_crew_recorded_tr`** — INSERT / DELETE / UPDATE OF the nine fields the split reads → resync the person/day (and the old day/person on a move). `SECURITY DEFINER` because `people_crew_jobs` INSERT is pay-access-only and a helper's own clock-in must not fail. No-op guard for value-less `UPDATE OF` fires (the salary sync's passes). Replaces the baseline `clock_sessions_sync_crew_assignments_tr`. `approve_clock_sessions` / `revoke_clock_sessions` / the auto-approve cron still call the syncs explicitly — now redundant, harmless.
- **`get_man_hours_by_job()`** joins the view. 90-day backfill of pending person-days.

## What shipped (client)

- [`src/utils/teamLabor.ts`](../../src/utils/teamLabor.ts): `loadTeamLaborData`, `fetchTeamLaborBreakdownForJob`, `loadTeamLaborDataForBids` read `people_hours_recorded` via `resolveRecordedHoursTable` — a once-per-session probe that falls back to `people_hours` when the view is missing (client deployed before the push, or a database without it). `from('people_hours_recorded' as 'people_hours')` keeps the generated types until the post-push `gen-types` chore. Consumers, unchanged: Job window Team labor row + Bill tab line, Cost Timeline (all three shells), Job Summary Labor column, Crew P&L, Job Summary's Team cost, Team tab.
- Job Summary hygiene chip ([`JobSummaryLedgerToolbar`](../../src/components/jobs/JobSummaryLedgerToolbar.tsx)): "⚠ N sessions await approval · H h **not counted**" → "⏳ N sessions await approval · H h **in Labor, not yet in Hours or overhead**" — true to the new split: the Labor column counts them, the day ledger (Hours · days, overhead day-share) still waits for approval.
- The v2.3178 Team labor row's "includes N h awaiting approval" note now describes hours that ARE in the total.

`jobs_ledger.last_work_date` (+ `fetchJobClockSessionBounds`, the Pipeline "Last work" cells), the Projects / job-window History day grid (`fetchProjectsJobHistoryClockSessions`), the Job Summary day ledger (`loadJobDayLedger` → Hours · days column, overhead day-share, Capacity view), the Job Calendar ✓ marks, `useWeeklyTeamLaborTotal`, People → Review's `people_hours` reads, the vehicle cost-per-field-hour report. The `auto_approve_salary_clock_sessions` 2-hour settle buffer can shrink to the next cron tick (its reason — the salary sync adjusting closed rows — no longer exists) but was left alone here to keep this PR to one idea.

## Known nuances

- Pay-report job attribution (`payReportAssignmentsBreakdown`, `draftPayrollPersonBreakdown`) reads crew pct × approved `people_hours`: pct now comes from recorded sessions, hours stay approved. Dollars paid are unchanged; a day with one approved and one pending session attributes the approved hours across both jobs' shares until the second is approved.
- The Hours grid's cell value was already `max(people_hours, pending closed)` (v2.533) — Job Summary's Labor column now agrees with it.
