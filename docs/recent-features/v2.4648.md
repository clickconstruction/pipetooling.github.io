## Is this the best we can do?

- *Undo, or delete?* Undo with a reason. The stream is the record a court may read; a delete would make it unreliable.
- *Can either side undo the other's rows?* No. Each side undoes its own and asks in the conversation for anything else.
- *Why can the firm not undo an applied payment?* The office has already marked the job paid. Undoing the report would not undo the payment on the job; the office reverses it there.
- *Why can a step not be undone?* Undoing a step would leave the stage it moved. The firm records the right step; a backward one asks the office (item 16).
