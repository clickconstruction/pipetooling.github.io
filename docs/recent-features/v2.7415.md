# v2.7415 — Painted maps: see who opened a map's link (2026-11-15)

Punch list #345, the link that asks who is looking, step D. The page that asks is v2.7414. This is where the office reads the answers.

## What changed

- **Who opened it,** on a map's Share sheet under the link's line. One row a reader: the name they gave, their phone or computer in two words, when they last opened it and how many times. A signed-in teammate's row says *signed in*.
- **Showed where they were.** A reader who pressed **Show where I am** has a line saying so and when, with **See that place**, which opens the latest place kept in a map app.
- **The sheet says what a reader is asked** before the link goes anywhere: *They give their name first. We keep the name, the phone or computer and the time.*
- **Only the office and the map's painter see the list.** Row security hands anyone else no rows, and the sheet then shows no list at all.
- **Share on a Documents row.** Each map on a job's Documents tab, and on the Documents page's Painted maps tab, has a **Share** press that opens this sheet without opening the map. On a job's tab the office gets **Text it…** to that job's customer there.

## How it is built

- `visitLine` in `paintedMapLink.ts`, pure (+2 tests). `loadPaintedMapVisits` reads `painted_map_link_visits` for the map, latest first, fifty at most.
- A read of the list that fails costs the list and never the sheet.
- `JobDocumentsPaintedMaps` mounts `PaintedMapShareSheet` with the map's id and name. Without the map's lines the sheet offers the link and not the copies, which stay in the map's own window.

## Is this the best we can do

- **The place opens in a map app, not on the painted map.** Drawing each reader's dot on the map's own canvas would put other people's whereabouts on a picture the crew opens. A link out to a map app keeps it in the sheet, for the two who may read it.
- **Fifty rows.** A link opened by more than fifty devices shows the latest fifty. The count on the link's line is still the whole count.
