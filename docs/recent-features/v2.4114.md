# v2.4114 — My Time day editor: payroll hours follow a change to approved times (2026-09-28)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. **Payroll fix.** Found while designing the all-or-nothing save (punch list **#46** row 10, the map's quirk 28). Stacked on [v2.4037](v2.4037.md).

## The bug

`people_hours` — the payroll hours per person and day — is maintained incrementally: `approve_clock_sessions` adds a session's duration, reject and revoke subtract it. Editing an approved session's times in place changes neither, which is why Adjust times calls `recompute_people_hours_after_session_edit` after its UPDATE (migration `20260616045050`, the map's quirk 7). The day editor's save writes times in place on five paths — the People → Hours seed update, the punch + salary partition, the ordered and containment updates when a seam moves, and the coalesced partition — and never resynced, so a moved seam between approved sessions left the day's payroll hours at the old total. The assign flow's twin (`persistMyTimeClusterAndGetSegmentIds`) had the same gap.

## The fix

- [`lib/myTimeDayPersist.ts`](../../src/lib/myTimeDayPersist.ts) notes the last approved row whose times a direct UPDATE wrote, and after every cluster is written calls `recompute_people_hours_after_session_edit` once with it. The RPC sets the day's `people_hours` to the sum of its approved, closed, un-revoked, un-rejected sessions, so one call covers the day.
- It is not called for pending rows (nothing approved changed), notes-only updates, or the split / replace RPCs (they subtract and re-add approved hours themselves) — so a day with no approved session is never overwritten.
- [`lib/persistMyTimeClusterForSegmentAssign.ts`](../../src/lib/persistMyTimeClusterForSegmentAssign.ts) does the same after its in-place updates.
- A refused resync rejects the save with its error; the rows written before it stay written (quirk 26 — the all-or-nothing save is the next train).

## Verified

- `myTimeDayPersist.test.ts` (87): a moved seam between approved rows; a pending row beside an approved one; the seed; the punch + salary merge; pending rows; notes only; the split RPC; two clusters resync once after every write; a refused resync.
- `persistMyTimeClusterForSegmentAssign.test.ts` (10): approved rows resync, pending rows do not.

- Not checked against the database or in the app.
