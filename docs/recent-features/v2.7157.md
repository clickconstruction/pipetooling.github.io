# v2.7157 — Bill Customer says what the customer will get before you press (2026-11-13)

Punch list #343, PR 1 of 3: the words. The owner asked whether it is clear, when the assistant sends a bill, what the customer is receiving. A look at the live window on a ZZ test job said no (the card's README holds the look).

## What was wrong

- The window opens on the Stripe bill tab. Its press read **Create Stripe invoice**. It makes the bill and marks it billed. It sends nothing, and nothing on the screen said so. Bills sent listed 24 open bills with no send on record that day.
- **Send to** showed one address and *(primary)*. No name. Its helper named a press, *Send Email invoice*, that is on the next screen.
- Nothing said a Physical invoice is an email with a PDF and no pay link. Its press read **Send email** and sends at once.

## The change

One pure kernel, `src/lib/billing/billCustomerTheyWillGet.ts` (`theyWillGet`, 12 tests), holds the words for the two channels that reach a customer. `SendRecordInvoiceModal` reads it through `sendWords(channel)`:

| Where | Stripe bill | Physical invoice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Under the tabs (`bill-customer-channel-caption`) | *An email with a Pay now button. They pay by card or bank on Stripe's page.* | *An email with the invoice PDF attached. It has no pay link.* |
| Send to | the payer's name, the address, *Phone (512) 555-0142. A text can follow on the next screen.* or *No phone on this job. A text needs a number on the customer.* | the payer's name and the address |
| The box above the press (`bill-customer-press-box`) | made and marked billed; nothing goes yet, the next screen sends it; every send is kept in Bills sent | one email goes now, to the address or to N addresses who each see the others; marked billed, no text; the copy is kept in Bills sent |
| The press | **Make the Stripe bill** | **Email the invoice to Dana**, or **Email the invoice to 2 people** from `countedLabel` |

- The phone line shows only when the customer themself is billed. A text goes to the job's customer, so under a GC payer or a typed bill-to nothing is promised.
- The box is hidden while the paid-job notice holds the presses.
- **See a sample of this email** under the box opens the step in Settings → What customers see (`billSampleHref`; a test holds each link to a step that exists), for the roles that can open that tab.
- No write changed. The three submit paths are untouched. The Stripe timeout's words follow the press's new name.

## Docs

`ready-to-bill-pipeline` gains *What the customer will get* and the new press names. `docs/BILLING_FLOWS.md` and `docs/SEND_RECORD_INVOICE_MODAL_ARCHITECTURE.md` name the presses as they read now.
