# v2.3653 — Sign-in page: the bar of ten marks runs the width of the two tiles (2026-09-20)

Owner-directed follow-up to v2.3651: *"make the bottom bar … be the same width as the two apps above it, side by side."* Client only; CSS only.

## The change

[`authPublicLanding.css`](../../src/components/authPublicLanding.css):

- `tooling-family__more-summary` is a full-width `flex` box (`width: 100%`, `box-sizing: border-box`) instead of a centred `inline-flex` pill, so its edges are the featured row's edges. Its radius is the tiles' `16px`, not `999px` — one shape for the strip.
- `tooling-family__more-marks` takes the free space (`flex: 1`) and keeps its `0.375rem` gap; each mark is an equal share of it (`flex: 1 1 0`, `aspect-ratio: 1`, radius `22%` — the house tile's 112/512), so the ten sit side by side and grow with the bar rather than being spread out small (the owner's call after seeing them spread): 32px at desktop width, 24px at 375px. No wrapping. The chevron sits at the right end.

- **One mark size for the strip** — `.tooling-family` is a size container (`container-type: inline-size`) and defines `--tooling-mark`: a tenth of the bar's inner run, `(100cqw − 1.625rem side padding − 0.75rem gap − 0.5rem chevron − 9 × 0.375rem) / 10`, with a `32px` fallback outside `@supports (width: 1cqw)`. The two featured marks take it (were a fixed 30px, which read small beside the grown ten); the ten keep their flex share, which comes out the same number. The chevron became a fixed `0.5rem` border-box so the sum is exact. The open list's `--small` marks stay 26px.

## Verified

Dev server, signed out: the bar's left and right edges equal the featured row's at desktop width (302–722 px) and at 375 px (20–355 px, under the stacked tiles). Featured mark and bar mark measure the same: 32 / 32 px at desktop width, 23.5 / 23.5 px at 375 px. The bar is 48px tall on a desktop; no horizontal overflow on the phone. The disclosure still opens and closes from anywhere on the bar. `SignIn.render.test.tsx` passes unchanged.
