Finding 7 of the owner's tab review ([`to-dos/submittals/tab-review-before-after.html`](../../to-dos/submittals/tab-review-before-after.html), https://claude.ai/artifact/SJ3skkPrsSDEugjmXe4h2k). Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

A row's own call is the roll-up of its parts, and one part sent back sends the row back (`rollUpPartDecisions`, the room's rule; right for the resubmit and the log). The *Their call* cell printed that roll-up as the headline: BP375's LAV-1 read **Rejected** in red when one faucet of three was rejected and two parts had no answer, with *1 rejected · 2 to go* small underneath. Step 6 read *4 rejected*, which reads as four fixtures gone.

## What changed

- **The kernel** ([`reviewDecisions.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/reviewDecisions.ts)): `rowCallWords(decision, parts)` → the head, its tone and the rest. With parts the GC sees, one word only when every one of them has the same answer; otherwise the worst answer present, counted (*1 of 3 rejected*), and the rest (*1 approved · 2 with no answer yet*). A row with no parts reads its own word. `summarizeDecisions(rows, partsByItem)` counts `rejectedInPart` / `reviseInPart`, and `describeDecisions` words them *N with a part rejected* / *N with a part to revise*. With no parts given, both read as before (the copied text, the journey).
- **The tab** ([`BidsSubmittalsTab`](../../src/components/bids/BidsSubmittalsTab.tsx)): the cell draws `rowCallWords`; the step's line and summary read the part-aware summary.

The stored roll-up is untouched: the row is still sent back, the resubmit carries it, the log holds its lines.
