## The press nothing took back

A § 53.056 notice recorded by hand (**Record it…** on the Lien desk, or the Lien window's own door, or the dev seat's `apply_notice_by_hand`) writes one `job_lien_filings` row with `by_hand` set, and one line on the job's Activity. **Void** takes the record off the tabs and keeps the row. The table has let a dev delete since it was made (`job_lien_filings_delete_dev`, `20260902164655_job_lien_filings.sql`). No screen and no seat verb called it.

## What changed

- `src/lib/jobs/lienByHandRecordDelete.ts`, the rule, pure. `lienByHandDeleteDrawn(role)` is true for a dev alone. `lienByHandDeleteProblem(record, job)` refuses, in a sentence, any record that is not a § 53.056 notice recorded by hand, a live record (*Void this record first.*), and a record on a job that is not a ZZ test job (`isZzTestJob`). `lienByHandTiesProblem(ties)` refuses a record something points at and says what, and refuses when the ties could not be read. `lienVoidedByHandRecords(filings)` is the list.
- `src/lib/jobs/lienByHandRecordDeleteIo.ts`. `readLienRecordTies(id)` counts the four things that can point at a record. `countLienPacketSiblings(record)` counts the other records of the same paper. `deleteLienByHandRecord(id)` reads the record and its job again, runs the rule, reads the ties again, then deletes the one row while it is a voided by-hand notice. The delete is sent once, and a delete that reaches no row is said as a refusal.
- `src/components/jobs/LienVoidedByHandRecords.tsx`, drawn by `LienFilingTabs.tsx` under *Filings on this job* on each tab. The tabs draw live records alone, so this folded line, *Voided by-hand notice records · N*, is where a voided one is reached. Each record reads its months and its days, with **Delete…**.
- The guide *send lien notices from the Lien desk* and the glossary say so.

## What points at a record, and why each one stops the delete

Read in the migrations and the code:

- A desk paper's `sent_filing_id` (`job_lien_desk_items`) and a bill's `lien_offer_filing_id` (`jobs_ledger_invoices`) are both `ON DELETE SET NULL`. The database would clear either silently, and a paper or a pay offer would lose its record with nobody told.
- Another record's `applied_from` has no delete action, so the database would refuse the delete with its own words.
- A sent copy can name a filing as its source (`sent_documents.source_table`), with no foreign key to hold it.

## The Activity line stays

The insert trigger `job_lien_filings_to_activity_ins` writes the line, and `job_activity_events` lets a signed-in person only read. Taking the line with the record needs a function, which is the other brief (card #443, the take-backs that need a migration). The list, the confirm and the toast each say the line stays.

## One paper, several records

One by-hand press writes a record for each job the paper covered, on one `packet_id`. This deletes the one record on the job the window is open on. The confirm says 1 record goes, and how many others of the same paper stay on other jobs.
