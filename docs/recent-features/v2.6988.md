# v2.6988 — The Bridge's guide says how Market is counted, in one place (punch list #318, PR 6) (2026-11-11)

PR 6, the last of six for *Market and Sales on the won rates* (`to-dos/market-sales-won-rates/README.md`), items 6 and 7 under *Not built*. Guide, docs and two test files. No code, no migration, no edge function.

## What changed

- **The guide** (`src/content/help/the-bridge.md`): a new section, *What Market is worth*, holds the whole rule once: the amount, a bid won or lost only by its mark, an unmarked bid half lost at 90 days and 95% at 180, the rate for each size of bid, the four ways an estimate is lost, what is in no count, and where to read today's counts. The first screen's paragraph, which carried a short form of the rule since PR 1, now says the one thing the row shows and links to the section. PRs 1 to 5 each moved the sentences their change made false; nothing in the guide's Market and Sales parts says *asked a week*, *win share* or *dead weight* now. Held by `helpGuidePlainWords.test.ts` and the link checks, with no exemption.
- **The Glossary** (`docs/GLOSSARY.md`, the Bridge): the older sentences are amended where they stand. Market is read against the field at what it is worth; Sales' won is the mark; the Market window's fourth state reads *no answers*; an asker with few marked bids is read at the rate for bids the size of each ask.
- **`PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`** (the Bridge): the stage cards' line says the same.
- **Made-up names in two test files** (`market/marketDesk.test.ts`, `sales/salesDesk.test.ts`): the fixtures carried company and project names from before this work, and one of them is a real asker on the live page. Every such name, key and comment is swapped for a made-up one with the same first letter, so the tests' orders stand. No assertion changed but the names.
