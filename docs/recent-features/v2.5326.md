# v2.5326 — Messages: reply to a person from their thread (2026-10-12)

Cut 3's screens, R1 of three, by helper 3 for the GC Review lead: the reply box and the send window. R2 adds the quick replies strip and *Find a reply*, and R3 adds cut 2's pieces in the box (a note's day to come back, the settling note, *After this sends*). It builds on #5483 (cut 1's thread window) and #5456 (cut 2's data, for `add_note`). Guide: `reply-to-a-person-from-their-thread`.

- **The reply box** (`src/components/gc/messages/reply/ReplyBox.tsx`), in the thread window's box slot (`threadSlots.tsx`). **Text · Email · Note** in the thread's own colours, the chosen kind ringed in orange; the from line floating in the empty box, which names the company, never a person; the count line by kind; **Tie to** at the left and the send at the right. A note goes straight from the box through cut 2's `add_note`. Every word is `compose.ts`'s.
- **Tie to** (`src/lib/messages/replyBox.ts`): the job chosen, else the job the thread shows, else the star. The prototype started from the star alone, so a reply written in a thread narrowed to one job left that view the moment it went.
- **The open thread** (`openThread.ts`): the thread window gives the person's lines, the job in view, the rail and the tiles to everything it draws, so a slot's component reads more than the person without a new slot argument (helper 1's call: in this branch, a plain `.ts`, read inside the component a slot returns).
- **The send window** (`reply/SendWindow.tsx`): an email as their inbox opens it, built by the same `buildMessagesEmail` the function sends with, the subject changeable for that one email; a text on their phone under the texts already there, the opt-out line as the app's own bubble with **Leave it off this time** after a quiet spell. **Email** and **Text** switch in the window.
- **`messages-email`**, a new edge function (lead's ruling B): the office team only, never a training account or a twin. It writes the email's line in `messages` as the service role, sends it through Resend's existing `sendEmailViaResend` under our letterhead (Click Construction, or the plumbing company on a Pipeline job) with Reply-To the sender, and files the sent copy with the line as its source; the thread's overlay of sent copies (`sentLines.ts`) skips such a copy, so the email shows once with the office's words. If Resend refuses, the line is taken back out. It answers the line's id, which R3's *this message gets it done* names. Registered as an outside email: the sent copy, `CUSTOMER_SURFACES`, a step on the GC and the sub journeys with a sample built by the same builder, the person journey's answers, and an `EMAIL_CATALOG` row. Deployed by hand at the lead's go, with `quo-send`.
- **The dev-mcp catalog** names `messages-email` (`node scripts/build-dev-mcp-catalog.mjs`, one line in `dev-mcp/catalog.ts`), so `dev-mcp` redeploys with it.
- **A text waits**: `TEXTS_ON` in `replyIo.ts` holds the send window's **Send as text** until the phone is switched on (cut 5's go); the window says so.
- **Words changed**: `fromLine` and `fromHeader` leave out a number or a mailbox the app does not know, and a missing address now points to their record (the To line cannot add one yet).
- **Not yet**: the To line adding or changing an address, *Send a test to me*, the greeting's name **change**, and the Attach picker (cut 7).
