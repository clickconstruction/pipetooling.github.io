# v2.5101 — Contracts: an agreement signed partly through the link and partly on paper (2026-10-09)

Punch list #64 ([`to-dos/contract-second-signer-names.md`](../../to-dos/contract-second-signer-names.md)), its last item. Edge function `share-job-contract` changed; no migration. Built by PUNCHLIST Helper 14.

## The gap

A PDF emailed to sign by hand keeps its link ([v2.3631](v2.3631.md)), so one spouse can sign there before the paper comes back signed by the other. [v2.4657](v2.4657.md) kept a **second** signature given through the link. Filing the paper still replaced a **first** signature given there. The first frame's columns were rewritten as paper and its consent stamp left the row. Only the `co_signed` event and the consent ledger kept it.

## The write

`fileSignedJobContract` (`src/lib/jobs/jobContractFileWrite.ts`) now builds its frame columns in one pure kernel, `paperFrameColumns`.

- **`firstSignatureOnFile(row)`** reads a first frame signed through the link: a printed name, a consent stamp and a mode other than paper, on a row out on paper (`isAwaitingPaperCopy`, handed or PDF emailed). Only that row converts in place, so only that row keeps it.
- **With the first frame on file**, the write leaves `signer_printed_name`, `signer_mode`, `signer_consented_at` and the rest of that frame as they are. The paper's signer becomes the second frame, marked paper, through v2.4657's `paperCoSignerFields`.
- **`paperSignerAfterLink`** picks the paper's signer: the Second signer box, else the Signed by name unless it is the name already on file. The window's sheet always sends the box. The fallback covers *Add the contract*, one paper for several jobs.
- Every other filing writes exactly what it wrote before.

The record is now part link, part paper. Each frame keeps its own way, `signer_mode` and `co_signer_mode`.

## The filing sheet

`JobContractFileSheet` mirrors v2.4657's line for the second frame. With the first frame on file, **Signed by** gives way to *Sam Owner signed through the link on Oct 1. That signature stays.* The **Second signer** box holds the paper's signer, with the placeholder *Who signed the paper*. It is required: the button stays off with *Enter who signed the paper* until it holds a name. A disabled button with no name now says the name is missing, where it always asked for the link.

## Two predicates, one kernel

`supabase/functions/_shared/jobContractSigners.ts` gains two predicates, read by the app and the edge alike:

- **`filedOnPaper(row)`**: either frame was filed from the paper. The record's document is then the paper, a scan or a link, and it has no stored PDF.
- **`partLinkPartPaper(row)`**: both frames filled, one through the link and one on paper, in either order.

Where `signer_mode === 'paper'` meant "this record's document is the paper", it now reads `filedOnPaper`:

- **`share-job-contract`** sends the scan or the link for such a record. Before, it rebuilt a PDF from the frozen row and printed the paper frame as a typed signature. The email still names both signers through `signerNamesLine`.
- **The signed rail's doors** (`signedDoors`): the uploaded copy or the Google Doc, a copy by email with the paper, no *Print*, no built PDF.
- **The chips** (`jobContractChips`) read *on file · paper*. **The coverage kernel** gives `source: 'paper'`, so the Pipeline chip reads *✍ On file*. `JOB_CONTRACT_COVERAGE_COLUMNS` adds `co_signer_mode`.
- **The Contract window** picks the paper source and the filed-document note the same way.

## What the readers show

- **The rail's banner**: *Signed by Sam Owner and Alex Owner · Oct 9, 2026* over *One signed on their phone, one on paper · filed as a Google Doc* (*on our device* for an in-person signature; *uploaded by the office* for a scan). The Consent, From and Device facts are the link signature's own.
- **The paper in the window**: the first frame keeps its drawn mark and *Signed electronically … consent recorded*; the second is a mark of its own, *Signed on paper by Alex Owner on Oct 9, 2026*. The window's second mark now comes from `jobContractSignatureBlocks` for every signed row, which draws the same marks as before for the other kinds.
- **The prints** (`jobContractSignatureBlocks`): a block per frame, and the paper frame's block is a *Signed on paper* block (`paper: true`).
- **The list line** (`jobContractSignersAuditLine`: the window's History, Documents, the Job window's Documents tab): each frame its own way, in frame order. *Signed electronically by Sam Owner (drawn) · Oct 1, 2026, 3:02 PM CT · consent recorded · signed on paper by Alex Owner on Oct 9, 2026.*
- **The activity line**: the trigger already names both when the second frame has a stamp and a name ([v2.4870](v2.4870.md)), so it reads *Contract signed by Sam Owner and Alex Owner*. No migration.

**Changed for v2.4657's mirror case too**, a paper filed after the *second* signer signed through the link. Its list line named only the paper's signer. It now names both, each frame its own way, by v2.4590's rule that the list line names every signer. Its rail line reads *One signed on their phone, one on paper* where it read *Signed outside the app*. Its paper, prints and doors are unchanged.

## Left alone, on purpose

- **One paper for several jobs** (`isLivePaperFiling`, `paperRowsByJob`) still keys on `signer_mode === 'paper'`. A record whose first frame signed through the link belongs to its own job: copying it to another job would turn that link signature into a paper one. So it is not offered under **Change the jobs…** or **Take it off…** on the Customer page's Agreements card. Its job still reads covered there, as an e-signed agreement does.
- **`legal-portal`** selects no `co_signer_mode`. The firm's page names both signers, as it does for every agreement, and shows no paper link, as for any paper record. No deploy needed there.
- **The shared copy is the paper.** For a record part link, part paper, *Email a copy* sends the scan, which carries one signature. The other is on the record: the window's mark, its audit line and the consent ledger. v2.4657's mirror case has always done the same.
