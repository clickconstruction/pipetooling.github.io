# v2.4545 — Put a GC on notice: each stage lists its own jobs that look wrong (2026-10-05)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. v2.4540 made the head line's *N look wrong* a button; the owner saw a before-and-after of the same on each stage's header row and asked for it.

## What changed

- **Each group's count is a button.** `GroupRows` in [`GcNoticeJobsBand.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/GcNoticeJobsBand.tsx) takes the count as a node: a [`GcNoticeWrongList`](../../src/components/jobs/GcNoticeWrongList.tsx) over `gcNoticeBandWrongItems({ groups: [g] })`, so the list holds that group's jobs only, in whichever order the band is in (by stage, biggest open first, by property). A pick jumps and lights the row as the head line's does.
- **One wrong job jumps.** `jumpWhenOne`: a group with a single wrong job draws *1 looks wrong ↓* and the click goes straight to that row; a one-row list under a header that sits on its row would say nothing. RMC- Dudley Mason on 2026-10-05: Waiting lists its four; Working and Billed (17 jobs, one wrong, eleven rows down) jump.
- **`scope`** names the button and its list for a screen reader (*Billed: 1 looks wrong. Jump to 800 · …*), now that the window has several.
- **Room.** The list measured the room under it against the nearest scrolling box; inside the table that is the table's own card, which runs far below the window's foot. It now takes the lowest floor of every box that clips.

## Tests

`GcOnNoticeModal.render.test.tsx`: the two group buttons' words and kinds, the lone job's jump with no list and without opening the job, the Billed list holding its two and not Working's one, Esc. The v2.4540 case finds the head line's button by the head line. Seen in the dev preview on RMC- Dudley Mason.
