# v2.8450 — Bills sent: the chips and the names stand 44 px tall on a phone (2026-11-24)

One fault of the Bills sent page found on the live test of 2026-11-24 (punch list #422, problem 6). No migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

At 375 px the chips of both views stood 38 px tall. In By day paid's day box a payment's bill name and job name were text links 16 px tall. A phone's own controls take 44.

## What changed

- `chipStyle(pressed, phone)` in `billsSentLook.ts` draws a phone's chip 44 px tall. Both views read it, so the list's chips and the month's chips grow together.
- `linePress(phone)`, new: a name or a word that is a press with no box of its own stands 44 px tall on a phone, its words in the middle, and adds nothing at a desk. It is on a payment's bill name and job name in By day paid, the yellow box's bill and job names and its **Show all** and **Show them**, and the job's name on a bill's record.
- The day chips under *The day it went* in **Say how it went…** were 40 px and are 44.
- `THUMB` reads `PHONE_PRESS_MIN_HEIGHT` (`lib/phonePress.ts`), the app's one source for the height.

## Tests

`billsSentLook.test.ts`, new: the chip on a phone and at a desk, the line press, the one source. `BillsPaidByDay.render.test.tsx`: on a phone the chips and both names are 44 px, at a desk they carry no height. `BillsSentLedger.render.test.tsx`: the list's chips on a phone. The look test failed before the change on 38.

## Left

- A day's cell in the month is 41 px wide at 375 px. Seven days share the row, so a cell cannot reach 44 across.
- The row of chips still slides. It is taller, not longer.

## Not looked at

Not drawn in a browser, so no height was measured on a page. The heights are the styles the tests read.
