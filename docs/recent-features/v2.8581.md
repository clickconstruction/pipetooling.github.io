# v2.8581 — A person's desk on a phone gives its sections the screen, and the short Dashboard waits for its counts (2026-11-25)

Punch list #365, from two sittings in an iPhone simulator on 2026-11-25, in Safari at 402 by 874 points. Three small faults, all the client alone. No migration, no edge function.

## 1. A link to a section landed one swipe short

**Seen.** `/people?tab=users&person=u:<id>&section=dashboard_view` opened the desk at Licenses and Your week, one swipe above Dashboard view.

**The cause, read in the code.** `PersonDeskBody.tsx` scrolled to the section once, 50 ms after the person was known. The sections above the target each open on a short *Loading…* line and grow when their own read lands, which pushed the target down after the scroll had aimed. And a section that draws nothing until it knows the person's role was not there at 50 ms, so the scroll found nothing and never ran again.

**The mend.** `startDeskSectionAim` in `src/lib/people/deskSectionAim.ts` holds the aim instead of taking it once.

- It waits for the section to exist, for at most 4 seconds.
- It scrolls to it, with the short ring as before.
- For a second and a half after that it looks every 120 ms and scrolls again, at once, when the section has moved in the page.
- It lets go the moment the person touches the page: a wheel, a finger, a pointer or a key.

## 2. The desk's head took half a phone

**Seen.** On the sample leader's desk the pinned head took about 445 of 874 points: the name, the login, two amber notices and the row of presses. The sections scrolled in a window of about 315 points.

**The cause.** The head was drawn before the scrolling box, not inside it, so it never scrolled, and nothing folded it.

**The mend**, on a phone's drawer alone (`useIsMobile`, the drawer's own 640 px line):

- The head and the sections share one scrolling box (`[data-desk-scroll]` in `PersonDeskBody.tsx`).
- `PersonDeskHeader` takes `pinName`. It draws the name's row, the avatar, the name, the role and the ×, as a `position: sticky` bar at the top of that box. The three dots, the amber bands, the chips and the presses are drawn under it and scroll away.
- The aim at a section gives the section the pinned row's height as its scroll margin, so the section's head lands under the row and not behind it.

At a desk's width, and on the People → Person page, the head is one block above the sections as it was. Folding the amber bands into one line was not built: it is a bigger change and a call.

## 3. Two lines of the short Dashboard were wrong while it read

**Seen.** On a fresh open of the short Dashboard the list read *Reading what waits on you…*, which is right. For about three seconds the line under it read *Nothing else is open.* and the door read *2 more of yours are in there.* Once the read ended they read the true counts.

**The cause.** In `DashboardShortView.tsx` the count line and the list wait for the read. The office's line and the door's note were drawn from the counts as they stood, whatever the read said.

**The mend.** Until the read is in, the office's line is not drawn, and the door says what it opens with no count. A read that failed is not whole either, so it draws no count, as the line under the greeting already did.
