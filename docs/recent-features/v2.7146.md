# v2.7146 — Pipeline: a Bills sent button on the Billed header (2026-11-13)

Punch list #327. Todd sent a screenshot of Jobs → Pipeline with a red box in the empty space after **Paid notifications** on the Billed Awaiting Payment header, and asked for a button there that opens `/bills-sent`. The card's recommendation had named this door from the start (*a press in the Billed header*); it was the one door not built.

## What changed

- `src/components/jobs/JobsStagesTab.tsx`: the Billed header's row of quiet presses ends with **Bills sent**. It wears the row's own style (`billedHeaderActionStyle`), so it is the same 28 px outline press as its neighbours. It is a `Link` to `BILLS_SENT_PATH`, not a button, since the page is an address: a middle click or *Open in new tab* works. Its title is the page's own line, *Every bill that left, who it went to, and the minute it went* (`BILLS_SENT_DOOR_TITLE`).
- It is drawn only for `canOpenBillsSent(authRole)`: dev, master, assistant, controller, the roles the page lets in. Nobody else sees it, and nobody sees a press that would bounce them to the Dashboard.
- `src/lib/jobs/stagesSectionToolsMenu.ts`: the ☰ Section tools menu mirrors the headers, so its Billed Awaiting Payment group ends with a **Bills sent** row under the same gate (`bills-sent`, the sixteenth key). The tab's door for it navigates to the page, as the Lien desk's row does.
- `src/components/jobs/StagesToolsMenuGlyph.tsx`: a new glyph name, `send`, drawn with the library's paper plane. Nothing in the app had an icon for Bills sent or Sent copies to reuse.
- The guides *see which bills went out and who got them* and *bill a customer and get paid* name the new button. `docs/JOBS_STAGES_TAB_ARCHITECTURE.md`, the page map and the twins' directory list the door.

## Width

The row is `flex-wrap: wrap` and each press is `flex-shrink: 0`, so a seventh press wraps to the next line where the row is full and never widens the page. Checked in the browser as the owner over the lens's invented rows: one line at 1400 px and 1100 px, two lines at 760 px (Paid notifications and Bills sent on the second), four lines at 375 px with Bills sent on the last. `document.documentElement.scrollWidth` equals the viewport's width at each.

## Tests

- `stagesSectionToolsMenu.test.ts`: the row is last in the Billed group for the four roles, absent for the rest, and its title is the page's line.
- `JobsStagesSectionToolsMenu.render.test.tsx`: the row wears the paper plane, sits right after Paid notifications and calls its door.
- `JobsStagesTab.render.test.tsx`: an assistant sees a link to `/bills-sent` as the row's last press, in the row's style. The owner, a dev and the controller see it right after Paid notifications. A superintendent, an estimator and a primary get none.

## Not changed

- The page, its gate and its other doors.
- The phone board (Mobile cards) hides every section header and the ☰ menu, so none of this row's presses show there until a search is typed, which brings the headers back with Bills sent among them. A phone reaches the page by its address, the header search or a Dashboard pin, as before. The ⋯ menu lists none of this row's presses, so it gained no row.
