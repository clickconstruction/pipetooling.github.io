# v2.6741 — Final demand letter: the copies travel with the letter (punch list #174, the copies line) (2026-11-08)

Punch list #174 (`to-dos/legal-matter-people/README.md`), the small PR the card kept apart after PR 1 (v2.6705) and PR 2 (v2.6726). PR 1 put the matter's people on the final demand letter as the *From the matter's people* strip: a To fills *Who owes it*, and the copies were listed to be typed into the letter by hand. Now the letter carries them.

## What changed

- **`demandLetter.ts`**: `DemandLetterFields.copies?` (`DemandLetterCopy`: the person's id on the matter, the name, the role in the people list's words, the mailing address; absent on letters recorded before and on a job whose payer has no matter) and a `copies` block, *Copies to*, after the enclosures and before the jurat — `copiesLines` writes one line a person, *name, role*, a nameless copy skipped and a name once. Rendered in the letter's HTML (`data-demand-copies`), the plain text (`cc:` with one indented line each), and the packet PDF (the enclosures' label style). The field snapshot the recorded letter keeps carries it, so the Lien desk's reprint reads the same line.
- **`legalMatterPeople.ts`**: `letterCopies(people)` — every row marked *copies*, in the list's order, with the role in words; a To and a *never* are not copies.
- **`LegalLetterRecipients.tsx`**: with `copies` and `onToggleCopy`, each copy chip is a press with `aria-pressed`, filled while the person is on the letter and struck in grey while off, a hover saying which, and the row counts *N on the letter's Copies to line*; the never rows stay spans.
- **`LienInstrumentsModal.tsx`**: the letter's copies start as every copy on the matter — from the prefill (through a ref to the people, so the rebuild never re-runs on a people read) or from the people read, whichever lands first; a rebuild keeps the office's picks; a press on a chip takes the person off or puts them back.
- **Tests**: `demandLetter.test.ts` (the block's place, the words, the text and the email, nothing without copies), `legalMatterPeople.test.ts` (`letterCopies`), `LegalMatterPeopleTable.render.test.tsx` (the chip's press, on and off).
- **Words**: the guides *send a final demand letter* (What fills itself in) and *review a collections account before it goes to your attorney* (the Account bullet), `docs/GLOSSARY.md` (the People on a matter entry), the card (status, next, ver; nothing left on it).

## Read closer than the card

- **The copies are words on the letter, not sends.** The letter still goes to one recipient by one channel; the office mails or emails the copies itself, reading the strip's addresses. The line tells the recipient who else holds the letter, which is what a cc is for.
- **An older letter reads as before.** A snapshot without `copies` draws no line; nothing is back-filled.

## Deploy

Client only. No migration, no edge function.

## Verify

On a job whose payer has an open matter with a person marked *copies*: Lien desk → **Final demand** → *From the matter's people* → the Copies chip reads filled and *1 on the letter's Copies to line*; the letter preview ends on *Copies to · <name>, <role>* under the enclosures; press the chip → it reads struck, the count *nobody on the letter's Copies to line*, the preview's line gone; press again and it is back. **Print** carries the line on the packet's letter page.
