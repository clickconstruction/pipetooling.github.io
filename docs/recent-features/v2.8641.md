# v2.8641 — Clock out: the note box says when its words are your plan from clock in (2026-11-26)

Punch list #445, finding 236 of the walking thread's sitting 23 (2026-11-26), briefed by the fixing thread as `445-clock-out-note-opens-on-the-plan.md` and held for the person's answer. Asked *At clock out the note box opens holding the plan typed at clock in. Keep that, or open it empty?*, the answer on 2026-11-26 was the pick: *Keep it, and add one line saying where the words came from.* Client only. No migration, no edge function. Nothing owed after the merge.

**Read.** At clock out, with the day's list empty, the box under *What did you work on?* already held the words typed as the plan at clock in, so **Complete clock out** was live with no bid ticked and nothing typed. `openClockOutReviewModalOnly` in `ClockInOutButton.tsx` sets the box to the open session's note. That is by design and older than card #445: with no report filed, the note is the session's own words, so clock out is one press (v2.6092, punch list #189). A clock session has one `notes` column and the clock out writes over it, so the plan lives on only if the box still holds it. What was wrong was the screen: nothing said where the words came from, and with a bid on the list the box read *Anything else?* over *You can leave this empty.* while it was not empty.

**The mend.**

- A line under the box, *From your plan at clock in. Change it or leave it.*, while the box still holds, letter for letter, the plan it opened on. One changed letter takes the line away. The words are `CLOCK_OUT_NOTE_FROM_PLAN_LINE` in `src/lib/clock/clockPlanWords.ts`, beside v2.8624's two lines, and `clockOutPlanLineShows` decides when the line draws.
- The reports' line wins. When the day's reports fill the box, *Filled from today's reports. Edit it if you like.* shows as before and the plan's line does not.
- *You can leave this empty.* shows only while the box is empty.
- The labels, the press and what the clock out writes are as they were. One press still clocks out.

**Narrower than the brief in one place.** The brief asks for the line whenever the box opened on the session's own note. A session's note is not always a plan: a start from Job Mode writes it, *On my way to …* or *On site at …* (`travelNote`, `hereNote`). Over those words *From your plan at clock in* would be untrue, so `clockNoteIsPlan` leaves them out and the box draws no line there, as before this change. Whether a Job Mode start should get a line of its own is a question for the person and is not built.

The guides *clock out with the bids of the day* and *clock in and out with Job Mode* say the box may open on the plan and what the line reads. The second said *With no report today, you type the note*, which has not been so since the box began opening on the session's note.
