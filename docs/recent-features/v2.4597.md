# v2.4597 — Help: twenty-three more guides in plain words (2026-10-05)

The plain-words sweep ([v2.4233](v2.4233.md); batches 1–5: [v2.4235](v2.4235.md), [v2.4258](v2.4258.md), [v2.4282](v2.4282.md), [v2.4283](v2.4283.md), [v2.4286](v2.4286.md)), punch list #75. The owner un-paused it on 2026-10-05 and the 67 guides still on `LEGACY_PLAIN_WORDS_GUIDES` were cut into three contiguous slices, one PR each, so the PRs never collide on the list. This PR is rows 1–23, `see-how-often-we-go-back` through `share-a-customer-their-portal`. No migration, no edge function, nothing on a screen changes.

## What changed

- **Twenty-three guides rewritten** by the rules in [`src/lib/plainWords.ts`](../../src/lib/plainWords.ts). Frontmatter, headings, `:::example` panels, tokens, links, screen quotes and numbers are unchanged, checked mechanically against the previous text. Every fact is kept (20,532 → 22,070 words). Each guide was then re-read sentence by sentence against its previous text for a changed claim.
- **Screen text with a dash, a dot or parentheses** is quoted in italics, the rule's exemption: backtick quotes of screen text became italics, and a bold label became bold-italic.
- **Two gif captions** in *send a supply house a quote link* became short sentences, since the checker counts a caption. The recordings are the same files.
- **A stray `*`** after "who took the call" in *see when a customer will pay* is gone.
- **Their rows leave the legacy list** ([`plainWordsLegacy.ts`](../../src/lib/plainWordsLegacy.ts)): 67 → 44 on this branch, with the other two slices in flight.

The rule for the sweep is that a rewrite never changes a fact, so these stay as written for someone to route:

- *see when a customer will pay* still says "fewer than 3 measured payments" and "under 3 measured payments"; [v2.4376](v2.4376.md) made it one. It still describes the "+ Weekly hygiene task" link, removed in v2.2288, and receipt chips like *+8 05/01–05/09*, which v2.2288 turned into one row per payment with the job's name.
- *see what the team sees* says "how many of the 25"; the catalog has 26 rows, and the count line reads *N of 26 emails*. The *Their week / By the email* switch is not described.
- *see your email schedule* names a **Digests** tab and an **Every report** tab on Email reports, which has been one list by person since v2.3595.
- *see where you win and lose with a builder* opens on **Bids → Builder Review**, now the **By builder** lens inside Bids → Followup.
- *send a supply house a quote link* names the **Not a supplier we quote from** tick, replaced by the Kind chips in v2.3172.
- *settings basics* names a **Notifications** tab; the tab is **Activity logs**.
- *send a bid for signature* says **Jobs → Stages** for the Pipeline.
- *see what to do next on a roadmap*: the Dashboard card is a Needs You row whose button reads *Open Plan*, and *Assign →* sits on the Next up row, not on the task's card.
- *share a customer their portal* says **Settings → Email streams → Portal requests**; the group is called **Emails & reports**.
