# v2.4597 — Help: twenty-three more guides in plain words (2026-10-05)

The plain-words sweep ([v2.4233](v2.4233.md); batches 1–5: [v2.4235](v2.4235.md), [v2.4258](v2.4258.md), [v2.4282](v2.4282.md), [v2.4283](v2.4283.md), [v2.4286](v2.4286.md)), punch list #75. The owner un-paused it on 2026-10-05. The 67 guides still on `LEGACY_PLAIN_WORDS_GUIDES` were cut into three contiguous slices, one PR each, so the PRs never collide on the list. This PR is rows 1–23, `see-how-often-we-go-back` through `share-a-customer-their-portal`. No migration, no edge function, nothing on a screen changes.

## What changed

- **Twenty-three guides rewritten** by the rules in [`src/lib/plainWords.ts`](../../src/lib/plainWords.ts) (20,532 → 22,594 words).
  - Checked mechanically against the previous text: frontmatter, headings, `:::example` panels, tokens, links, screen quotes, bold labels and every number are unchanged, except the one fact below.
  - Checked by reading: each guide was re-read sentence by sentence against its previous text. Three read-only reviewers then compared every guide with its previous text. A fourth read the second pass.
- **Pronouns across a full stop.** This is the standard PUNCHLIST set for the sweep. A sentence that opens on a pronoun pointing back at the sentence before names its noun again. A paragraph has at most one sentence that starts with "So".
- **Screen text with a dash, a dot or parentheses** is quoted in italics, the rule's exemption. Backtick quotes of screen text became italics, and a bold label became bold-italic.
- **Two gif captions** in *send a supply house a quote link* became short sentences, because the checker counts a caption. The recordings are the same files.
- **A stray `*`** after "who took the call" in *see when a customer will pay* is gone.
- **Their rows leave the legacy list** ([`plainWordsLegacy.ts`](../../src/lib/plainWordsLegacy.ts)): 67 → 44 on this branch, with the other two slices in flight.

## One fact corrected

[v2.4376](v2.4376.md) set `PAY_SPEED_MIN_SAMPLES` to 1 and left the guide's edit for this rewrite. *see when a customer will pay* now says a customer with *no measurable payment yet* gets the company figure. That is the Pay speeds window's own wording. The guide used to say "fewer than 3 measured payments" and "under 3 measured payments". This is the only fact changed in the PR.
