# v2.4535 — Lien desk: the job number opens the job on every tab (2026-10-05)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Follow-up to v2.4531 (the Calendar), the owner's yes to the same door on the desk's other tabs.

## What changed

- **One number button.** `JobNumber` moved out of the Calendar tab into [`LienJobNumber.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienJobNumber.tsx): `LienJobNumber` (the chip; a button that opens the job and stops the click when it has a door) and `LienJobHeading` (a pane's heading). The label *663 · Knight Contracting* is split by `splitLienJobLabel` ([`lienJobLabel.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienJobLabel.ts)).
- **Timeline.** [`LienDeskTimelineTab.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskTimelineTab.tsx) takes `onOpenJob`. A row's number is the door to the job; the row itself (now a `div` that takes the click, with the name as its keyboard button) still opens the job on the pane its next step belongs to.
- **Notices, Affidavits, Retainage.** The heading of the picked job in each pane is `LienJobHeading`: the number as the button, the name beside it. The left lists are untouched — a click there selects, and a second small target in a 320 px row would be mis-clicked.
- **Wiring.** `LienDeskModal` hands its `onOpenJob` (v2.4531) to the Timeline tab and the three panes. The job opens over the desk and the desk keeps its tab and selection.
- Where no door is passed (the panes' own tests, any other host) the heading is the one bold line it was and the Timeline label is whole.

## Tests

`lienJobLabel.test.ts` (3, new), `LienJobNumber.render.test.tsx` (5, new: the button keeps its click from the row around it; the plain chip; the heading with and without a door; a job with no name), `LienDeskTimelineTab.render.test.tsx` (+1). Seen on the live desk in the dev preview: the Notices heading and a Timeline row's number each opened the Job window and closing it returned to the same tab; the Affidavits heading drew its number as the button. Retainage had no rows that day; it draws the same heading component. Nothing was written.
