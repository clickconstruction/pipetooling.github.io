# v2.4352 — A click outside a window closes that window only (2026-10-01)

No migration, no edge function. The audit [v2.4338](v2.4338.md) left open: v2.4338 fixed two windows drawn inside another window (the signing pad, When can a row split?) and did not look for the rest.

## Why it happened

A window drawn inside another window's backdrop but outside its panel sits inside the outer backdrop in React's tree — through a portal too, since React bubbles a click through the component tree, not the DOM. A real click on the inner backdrop ran the inner handler, then the outer backdrop's `onClick`, so a click outside the inner window closed both. The page-wide drag guard cannot stop it: React walks every handler inside one native dispatch.

## The audit

[`scripts/check-nested-windows.mjs`](../../scripts/check-nested-windows.mjs) (`npm run check:nested-windows`) parses every component with the TypeScript compiler. Of the 440 backdrops, about 270 close without a target check; for each it walks everything drawn outside the panel — following components through imports, `lazy()`, `memo()`, render helpers, IIFEs, portals and the props a component draws outside its own stopper — and lists any window there that lets a click through. On main b7097a0d7 it found nine windows holding fifteen such windows and nothing else; run on the code before v2.4338 it also finds that release's two. It exits 1 on a finding; `--rows` also lists clickable rows (not backdrops) holding one.

## What changed

Each inner window's backdrop stops its click before closing — `onClick={(e) => { e.stopPropagation(); onClose() }}` — so the fix holds wherever the window is drawn:

- Review Hours → Assign focus ([`AssignFocusModal`](../../src/components/AssignFocusModal.tsx)).
- User review → Switch user ([`UserReviewSwitchUserModal`](../../src/components/userReview/UserReviewSwitchUserModal.tsx)).
- Customer summary (Dispatch mode) → a report ([`ReportViewModal`](../../src/components/ReportViewModal.tsx), keeping its target check).
- Quick assign / Assign work → the job picker ([`ScheduleDispatchAssignJobPickerModal`](../../src/components/schedule/ScheduleDispatchAssignJobPickerModal.tsx)). The sheet still closes itself when the picker closes with no job picked yet.
- Lien desk and Put a GC on notice → Send the run ([`LienDeskRunModal`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskRunModal.tsx)); Lien desk → The owner called and Practice call ([`LienOwnerCallDialog`](../../src/components/jobs/LienOwnerCallDialog.tsx), a portal).
- Sheet story → portal visits ([`SubPortalVisitsModal`](../../src/components/people/SubPortalVisitsModal.tsx)) and the work order ([`WorkOrderAssemblerModal`](../../src/components/jobs/WorkOrderAssemblerModal.tsx)).
- Hours align → the Apply Schedule % confirm ([`ApplyScheduleApprovedConfirmModal`](../../src/components/clock-sessions/ApplyScheduleApprovedConfirmModal.tsx)).
- Legal desk ([`LegalDeskModal`](../../src/components/jobs/legal/LegalDeskModal.tsx)) → its five sheets (Mark attorney-ready, What the firm will see, Ask a dev to review, Pull back, Who at the firm hears from us) and Apply discount ([`AgreedWriteDownModal`](../../src/components/jobs/AgreedWriteDownModal.tsx)). Apply discount had no backdrop handler and its panel stopped nothing, so from the Legal desk the first click anywhere in it — the amount box included — closed the desk. Its backdrop now stops every click; it still does not close on a click outside. Edit Job's backdrop checks its target, so it was never hit there.

## Not covered

- Clickable rows holding a window (`--rows`): on the Bid Board a click outside the GC notes popover or Mark account opened also opens or shuts the row; on the Checklist a click outside the cost window toggles the row's activity. Same bubbling, a milder effect.
- Escape: an inner and an outer window both listening on `window` can both close on one press. Not audited.
- The check is not in CI.

All three fail without the fix.

## Walked live

Local dev server against prod, logging each press, release and click target; nothing saved. Lien desk → Practice call: a click on the call's backdrop closed the call only; with the fix taken out (hot reload) the same click closed the call and the desk. Legal desk: in Apply discount a click on its text, into the Discount box and on its backdrop left both windows open (closed with Cancel); from Mark attorney-ready, What the firm will see and the firm's emails each closed alone on a click outside, then the sheet alone, then the desk on its own backdrop. User review → Switch user closed alone. Pipeline → Assign work on J1023 → Change job: a click outside the picker closed the picker, and Assign work kept its job (closed with ✕, nothing scheduled).
