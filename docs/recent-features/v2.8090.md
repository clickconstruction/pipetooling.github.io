# v2.8090 — Document builder: the insurance sheet's legend reads in dark (2026-11-20)

Punch list #404, found by the audit of 2026-11-20. No migration and no function.

## What was wrong

On Documents → Document builder → Insurance requirements for subs, the left side shows a legend of the three marks a field wears: **Your usual**, **Filled from the job** and **Changed for this job**. The legend is pinned to the light theme, so it shows the marks as the sheet shows them. A pinned-light element takes the light theme's ink, and *Your usual* has no fill of its own, only a line under it. The legend drew no ground, so in dark the words *Your usual* were dark ink on the dark card and could not be read. The other two marks carry their own fill and read fine.

The comment over the legend said it was "drawn on the page's own light ground". It was not.

## The fix

The legend has a light ground of its own: the light theme's surface, its ink, a border and a little padding, sized to the three marks (`LEGEND` in `FilledBuilder.tsx`). Nothing else on the card changes, and light looks the same but for the thin frame.

## Tests

`FilledBuilder.render.test.tsx`: the legend is pinned light and carries its ground and its ink.

Seen on the running app in dark at desktop and phone width, before and after.
