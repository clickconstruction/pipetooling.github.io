# v2.3932 — My Time day editor: no-call, no-show is its own piece — `useMyTimeNcnsFlow`, `MyTimeNcnsDialogs`, `lib/myTimeNcns` (2026-09-27)

Client only; no migration, no edge function, no screen change. Punch list **#46** row 10 (`to-dos/decomposition-queue.md`): step 3 of `docs/MY_TIME_DAY_EDITOR_MODAL_ARCHITECTURE.md`, after [v2.3919](v2.3919.md). The queue card is left for the one docs PR covering the parallel batch.

## Why

The NCNS flow was the largest self-contained slice of the editor: 8 state values, 2 effects, 6 handlers and two dialogs, about 640 lines, touching the rest of the editor in three places (the session list it reads, `fetchedSessions` it writes after the sweep, and two lines of `closeTopmostSubFlow`). It rejects clock sessions and changes payroll hours, and had no test.

## What shipped

- **[`src/lib/myTimeNcns.ts`](../../src/lib/myTimeNcns.ts)** — Stage A, the pure rules: `ncnsClickAllowed` and `ncnsButtonTitle` over one `NcnsGateInput`, `ncnsEntryPhase` (approved time opens on the warning), `ncnsRecordRpcArgs` (details trimmed, left out when blank), `NCNS_DETAILS_MAX_LEN`, the `NcnsUiPhase` type.
- **[`useMyTimeNcnsFlow`](../../src/components/my-time-day-editor/useMyTimeNcnsFlow.ts)** — the state, the `job_schedule_blocks` probe, the reset on a new day or person, the pre-close sweep (`clock_sessions` UPDATE per open session, then re-read and verify), the record RPC (`record_ncns_and_reject_sessions_for_day`, then `onSaved()` + `onClose()`), and `closeTopmost()` for the editor's sub-flow registry. Handlers moved verbatim; the four inline `onClick` bodies of the dialog became `cancelDialog`, `dismissDialog`, `continueToConfirm` and `backToWarn`.
- **[`MyTimeNcnsDialogs.tsx`](../../src/components/my-time-day-editor/MyTimeNcnsDialogs.tsx)** — `MyTimeNcnsButton`, `MyTimeNcnsPrecloseDialog`, `MyTimeNcnsDialog`; JSX verbatim, each taking `flow`.
- **The editor** calls the hook where the probe effect was (so its effects keep their order), mounts the button in both footers and each dialog where its JSX was (so the DOM order is unchanged, z-index 1305 and 1310 passed in), and `closeTopmostSubFlow` calls `closeTopmost()` in the same position. 3,645 → 3,003 lines; 39 → 31 `useState`.
- **Tests** — `myTimeNcns.test.ts` (17) and `MyTimeNcnsFlow.render.test.tsx` (20): the button's gate and words with and without the schedule probe; the plain path and what the RPC is sent; the RPC's own refusal shown in the dialog; the approved path, its acknowledgement and Back; the sweep, its re-read and the hand-off of the closed rows; a session still open after the sweep; the parent-owned sessions toast; busy holding every way out; the registry entry; the reset on a new day.
- **The map** marks region 9 extracted and step 3 done, and gains quirk 20.
