# v2.5561 — Prospects → Jobs to GC: the jobs on the card, and Asked us ✓ opens New project filled in (2026-10-15)

Punch list #110, PR 3 of four (after v2.5559, the kind and the two lanes, and v2.5560, the Subs lane's bench). The Jobs to GC lane now holds what a GC lead is worth calling about: the jobs, each with its bid day, and a hand-off that lands the job on GC projects without retyping it. The table it reads, `prospect_jobs`, came with v2.5559's migration.

- **`src/lib/prospects/gcLeadLane.ts`** (new, pure; 9 tests): `ProspectJob` and `NewProspectJob`; `newJobProblem` (a name, and a plans link that is a web address); `orderJobs` (open jobs by bid day, none last, then asked, lost, dropped; stable); `jobDueWords` (the day and what it means: days left, today, days ago, no day yet, asked us, lost to whom, dropped); `soonestOpenBid`; `jobToHandOn` (the open job with the soonest bid); `orderLeadsBySoonestBid` (the queue's third order: the soonest open bid, then never called, then the caller's order); `newProjectSeedFromLead` (the lead and the job as New project's first step: an owner, developer or owner's rep is the customer, an architect is the firm that drew the plans, `gc` and `ownersRep` keep their role); `lostOwnerLead` (a lost project's owner as a GC lead with the job marked lost and who won in the note); `gcWorkCounts`.
- **`gcLeadLaneIo.ts`** (new): `loadProspectJobs` (the ids in slices and each slice paged, `fetchAllRowsChunkedIn`, so a long queue never overruns the request or stops at a page), `addProspectJob`, `setProspectJobStatus`, `deleteProspectJob`, `loadCustomerDoubles`, and `sendLostOwnerToProspects(projectId, by)`, which reads the project, its GC row and the customer itself (so the lead carries the customer's own phone and email and who won), adds the lost job to the owner's live lead when there is one instead of a second lead, and never keeps the same lost job twice.
- **`src/components/prospects/ProspectJobsBox.tsx`** (new; 5 render tests): the Jobs box under the lead's name — one row a job with its day, **+ Add a job** (job, address, size, bids due, where the plans are; a refusal's words stay in the box), **Lost** (the page asks who won), **Drop**, **Reopen**, **Remove**.
- **`callingOrder.ts`**: `CallingOrderMode` gains `soonest_bid` (read as coldest by the kernel; the deck orders it).
- **`Prospects.tsx`**: on Jobs to GC the deck loads each lead's jobs beside both loaders (`jobsByProspect`), draws the Jobs box between the name row and the outcome row, adds **Soonest bid** to the order toggle, and heads Follow Up with **GC work in flight** (Bidding, Buying out, Building and Lost, counted from `loadGcProjects`; fail-soft). **Asked us ✓** (`handleAskedUs`) uses the customer on file when the lead is linked (`customer_id`), else opens Add customer as before; then the soonest open job is marked asked, the lead leaves the queue (`leaveQueueAsLanded`), and the page goes to `/gc?new=1` with the seed in the router's state. New Prospect on **A job to GC** warns when the company is already a customer, by name or phone (`benchDouble` over `loadCustomerDoubles`), with **Link this lead to them**, which saves `customer_id`.
- **`GcNewProject.tsx`**: `GcNewProjectWindow` takes `initial` (a `NewProjectSeed`) for the first step's name, address, customer or new owner, architect, role, bid day, size and size words. **`GcProjects.tsx`** reads `newProjectSeed` from the router's state, so the seed is one-shot: closing the window and pressing New project again opens it blank.
- **`cardHead.ts`**: a lost card's doors gain **Send the owner back to Prospects** (`prospects`, group job; `can.prospects` is the prospects staff, `canAccessProspectPipeline(role, false)`); 2 tests. The GC page's press runs `sendLostOwnerToProspects` and opens the deck on the lead.
- **Guide**: `find-jobs-to-build-as-the-gc` reads the Jobs box, Soonest bid, the strip, Asked us ✓ handing on to New project, the customer link and a lost bid coming back. **Docs**: the Prospects map's kind note, the Glossary's Prospects entry, ACCESS_CONTROL (`prospect_jobs` under its own policies; who sees the lost card's door).
