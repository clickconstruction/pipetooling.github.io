# v2.6487 — A STOP number’s reply box says why, Fold closes the roster, a new search clears the old failure, and the Dispatch badge counts a crew apart (2026-11-04)

Four confirmed bugs from a read of main at d03168f0e, fixed in one PR. The folded reply box (v2.6477, v2.6482) did not know a text to a number that sent STOP: `defaultReplyKind` still starts such a contact on Text when they have no email, the field is disabled so no press could wake the box, and the STOP sentence lives in the send row the fold hides, so a phone showed a disabled *Write to …* and nothing said why. The folded sort card (v2.6476) drew **Fold ▴** on *Which of ours?* and pressing it did nothing visible until Back. The Pipeline search (v2.6410) cleared a failed term's line only when the next answer came, so after "smith" failed, "smithson" kept *Only the jobs already on the board were searched* and the Paid in Full header's *not searched* through the debounce and the whole request. The Dispatch Mode Inbox badge (v2.6462 put a stopped crew's stage check among the high rows) called that check a customer waiting.

## What changed

- `src/lib/messages/replyFold.ts`: `ReplyFoldFacts.blocked`; `replyBoxFolded` keeps the box whole when it is set. `ReplyBox.tsx` passes `textBoxBlocked`'s answer. Test: `replyFold.test.ts` (a blocked number keeps the box whole).
- `src/components/followUp/NewLaneCard.tsx`: **Fold ▴** also leaves the roster (`setTeamOpen(false)`), as the guide reads: *Press Fold to take it back to one row.* Test: `NewLaneSlot.render.test.tsx` (Fold on *Which of ours?* leaves the roster and folds the card).
- `src/components/jobs/JobsStagesTab.tsx`: `stagesServerSearchTermRef` holds the term the server lookup last ran for; at the top of the effect a different term clears `stagesServerSearchFailure` before the debounce. Retry runs the same term, so its line stays until the answer. Test: `JobsStagesTab.lookupFailed.render.test.tsx` (a new term starts clean through the debounce and the request).
- `src/lib/dispatchInboxBadge.ts`: `DispatchBadgeRow.pending_action`, `DispatchBadgeCounts.crew` (the high rows that are a stage check, by `customerWaiting.ts`'s `waitsOnTheBanner`, the one predicate), `dispatchBadgeAriaLabel(open, high, crew)` reads *N open · 2 customers waiting · 1 crew waiting* or *1 crew waiting* alone. `DispatchModeFooter.tsx` passes `counts.crew` as `inboxCrewWaitingCount`; the pulse keeps `high`. Test: `dispatchInboxBadge.test.ts` (a stage check among the high rows is counted apart and named a crew).
- Guides and docs: `answer-a-customer-waiting-request.md` says the badge breathes for a crew's stage check too and counts them apart; `docs/MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md` gains the fold rule's sentence with the STOP clause.
