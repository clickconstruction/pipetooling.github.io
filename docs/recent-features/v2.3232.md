# v2.3232 — Pre-rule multi-decision asks leave Standing rulings for the Console's owner memo, split into taps; Dismiss stops promising a re-ask (2026-09-10)

Client only — no migration, no edge function. Grace, looking at the live Audits lens: "I am still seeing this wall of text on audits that I as a human can't respond to." The card was question 836b6c22 — the 2026-08-31 backtest slate's three-decisions-in-one ask, written before the one-decision rule (v2.3210) and named in `twins/HANDOFF.md` as a pending owner ruling. v2.3224 had put a note under it that promised "Dismiss it and the robots re-ask one decision at a time, with taps" — nothing on the server does that (`get_answers` just stops returning a dismissed row; a robot asks again only when a later run hits the same gap). Grace: close the gap — pre-rule multi-decision asks get pulled off Standing rulings and shown in the Console as an owner memo with the decisions split out, and the Dismiss copy stops promising a re-ask.

## What changed

- **Kernel** [`src/lib/bids/legacyAskMemo.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/legacyAskMemo.ts) (+6 tests): `splitLegacyAsk(text)` — the numbered parts `(1) … (2) …` are the seams, the text before the first is the preamble, an `Also confirm: …` clause inside a part is its own decision, a caps label before a dash (`SMALL-TI RESIDUAL —`) becomes the decision's heading and topic slug, an over-long part keeps its asking sentence, and `either A or B?` becomes two taps (else Yes / No). `draftProblems` is the v2.3210 shape checker over a draft; `buildSplitInserts(source, drafts)` builds the rows posted AS the robot (`twin_user_id`, `about_bid_id`, `mission` stamped `· split from <id>`, `audience: 'estimator'`, `kind: 'decision'`); `splitRetirementAnswer(n)` is the note the retired original carries.
- **Standing rulings** ([`standingRulings.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/standingRulings.ts), +2 tests): `groupStandingRulings` sets legacy asks aside in a new `legacyAsks` list — not a ruling, not a single, not in the header count (the same treatment v2.3212 gave plans asks). `legacyMultiDecisionNote` now reads "Written before the one-decision rule: several decisions in one ask, no taps. Split it into one-tap questions below, or dismiss it — nothing re-asks on its own." [`BidsAuditsTab.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/BidsAuditsTab.tsx) no longer renders those cards; the panel carries one pointer line (`data-testid="rulings-legacy-asks"`): *One ask written before the one-decision rule waits for the owner to split into taps — Robots → Console* (the link for devs; other roles read "on the robots' Console").
- **Owner memo** [`TwinOwnerMemoCard.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/TwinOwnerMemoCard.tsx) (render test, 2 smokes) on the Console lens (dev), above Operator questions: per pre-rule ask, the robot's full text (bid numbers linked through `useTwinQuestionBidRefs`), the note, then the split as editable drafts — question, taps (comma-separated), the robot's pick, topic key — with **remove** / **+ Add a decision**. **Post N as one-tap questions** validates every draft with the shape checker (problems print under the draft; nothing posts until all pass), inserts them as the robot's own open questions, then retires the original (`status: 'dismissed'`, `answer: 'Re-asked as N one-decision questions from the Console.'`, `answered_by` the poster) — so the same decisions land on Standing rulings with buttons and the ledger says where they went. **Dismiss** closes it; its toast says nothing re-asks on its own.
- [`BidsRobotConsoleTab.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/BidsRobotConsoleTab.tsx) mounts the memo card.

## Not changed

Staff can insert `twin_questions` rows already ("Bid pricing users can write twin_questions" is FOR ALL; the twin-only RESTRICTIVE policies bind twins) — the memo posts through the app's own client, as Answer does.
