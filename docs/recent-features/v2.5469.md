# v2.5469 — Messages: calls and texts know which of our two numbers they came through, behind the scenes (2026-10-13)

Residential R6 (two boxes, two numbers, one inbox), PR 4: `quo-webhook` and `quo-send` read the boxes. It follows the
kernel (v2.5418) and the SQL (v2.5428, `20261014003000_messages_two_boxes.sql`, on prod). No migration and no screen.
The GC lead deploys both functions together after their switch PR. Until a second number is in
`app_settings` `messages_boxes_v1`, everything goes from the company number as before.

- **The box a call or text came to** (`quo-webhook`): Quo's `phoneNumberId` against the boxes (`loadBoxes` in
  `_shared/quoLines.ts`, `boxOfInbound`).
  - Until the row names a number, `QUO_PHONE_NUMBER_ID` is the team box's (`parseBoxSettings`, `boxSetting`, R6's compose PR's readers). A number we cannot place
    is the team box.
  - A new line is stamped with `messages.mailbox` (`QuoPlanContext.mailbox`, `QuoLine.mailbox`, `rows.ts`), and a line
    already written keeps its own.
  - A call's words name the number it rang ("rang everyone on the bids number").
- **A STOP per box**: STOP or START writes `contact_channels.opted_out_by_box` for the box it came to (`writeStops`,
  `stopScope`), both boxes when our number is not placed.
  - A STOP again keeps the first stamp, its own or the older `opted_out_at`.
  - `opted_out_at` is read as a STOP to every box and never written by a new word.
  - `placeNumber` reads the number's STOP to the box (`stoppedOn`), so the HELP answer and the one confirmation see that
    box only, and go from its number.
- **The box a text goes from** (`quo-send`): `boxForReply`, from the box their newest text on this number came to, else
  the box of their kind.
  - A box with no number sends from the company number and is stamped team (`sendBox`).
  - The STOP is read for that box. The opt-out line's first-text and 30-day rule counts our texts from that box
    (`lastOurTextAt`), since a carrier pairs the two numbers.
  - The answer carries `mailbox`. No caller picks the box yet; R6's compose PR shows it.
- **Kernels**:
  - `boxes.ts` gains `sendBox` and `numberName`, and the keep-the-first-stamp rule in `stopScope`. Its address rule is inlined, so the edge copy needs
    no customer kernels, and words that are not an address never match a box with no address.
  - `events.ts`, `send.ts` and `rows.ts` carry the box. `boxes.ts` joins the edge copy (`gen:edge-kernels`).
- **Docs**: `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`'s `quo-webhook` and `quo-send` sections.
- **Tests**: `boxes.test.ts`, `events.test.ts`, `send.test.ts` and `rows.test.ts`.
