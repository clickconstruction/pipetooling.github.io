# v2.5002 — GC projects: interest on late GC bills, set per job and shown on Money (2026-10-08)

Owner Billing's O6b-1 in the GC mode real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/OWNER_BILLING_REAL_BUILD.md` → decision 7 and PR 8, on branch `spike/gc-mode`), the first of O6's three PRs: interest read and typed. **Bill the interest** is O6b-2 and the late finish's fee is O6b-3. Guides: [`bill-the-customer-on-a-gc-job`](../../src/content/help/bill-the-customer-on-a-gc-job.md) → *Interest on late bills*, and [`see-the-money-on-our-gc-jobs`](../../src/content/help/see-the-money-on-our-gc-jobs.md). No migration.

**The rule, for the owner's list (call 6: check it against Texas prompt pay).** Interest on a late bill runs at the job's rate a month, from the day after the bill falls due by the contract. That day is the certificate's day (or the day the bill went, when there is no certificate) plus the contract's days to pay after the certificate (`gc_projects.owner_pay_days`). A promise the customer gives never moves it. Their usual days to pay never start it, though they still drive when we *expect* the money. No interest runs on a job until both its rate and its days to pay are typed. The plan reads Texas's prompt pay law for private work (Property Code chapter 28) as 35 days from the request and 1.5% a month on what is late. The owner or his counsel should confirm the rule before a rate is used.

- **The kernel** (`ownerBillingInterest.ts`):
  - `ownerInterestFrom` returns the contract's due day; it used to be the earlier of the first promise and the expected day. Its test changed with it: pay application 3, with 5 days to pay, still starts Sep 30 to the cent, and a promise or the customer's days start nothing.
  - `ownerInterestWords` says the rate in the terms.
- **The mapper** (`billCustomer.ts`, `gcIo.ts`): the terms read `owner_late_interest_pct_per_month` and `owner_late_finish_per_day`, and lay `ownerLateInterest` and `ownerLateFinish` on the project. Until now neither was read, so neither was ever on a real job.
- **Bill the customer's terms:**
  - The line reads *No interest on late bills.*, *1.5% a month on a late bill, once the contract's days to pay are typed.* or *1.5% a month on a late bill, from the day after it falls due by the contract.*
  - **Change the interest** starts the field at 1.5 (`OWNER_INTEREST_DEFAULT_PCT`). Nothing is saved until **Save the interest**, a blank saves none, and the rate must be above 0 and at most 10.
  - `setOwnerLateInterest` updates `gc_projects` under the Owner Billing door's trigger.
- **Money** (`GcMoneyInterest.tsx`) shows each job that charges interest: its rate, what has built up, billed, paid, and what is left to bill. A job with no days to pay typed says none runs yet. The "comes later" line for interest is gone.
- **The forecast** (O5d's rule, as the lead sent it here): the draft's `expectPaidOn` and `cashForecast`'s `paidOn` take the customer's days, then the contract's. A new customer's next bill now shows in the weeks ahead.
