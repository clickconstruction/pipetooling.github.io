# v2.7876 — A map far down a page stays on Google Maps (2026-11-18)

Found on 2026-11-18 on the walk of punch list #395 (Time on the maps), on clicktooling.com as the dev account in headless Chrome: the Dashboard's **Your jobs on a map** drew on OpenStreetMap, with the console line *Google Maps unavailable, using OpenStreetMap: render: the Maps API loaded but never painted a map (key rejected or blocked?)*. The key was good. The Jobs → Pipeline and Bid Board maps, near the top of their pages, stayed on Google. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## The cause

- **The paint watchdog** in [`PinsMapGoogleCanvas`](../../src/components/map/PinsMapGoogleCanvas.tsx) (v2.3145) started an 8 s clock when the Maps script loaded and failed the card to OpenStreetMap when no `.gm-style` was in the frame by then. It did not ask where the frame was.
- **Google builds no map off screen.** `@vis.gl/react-google-maps` asks for no version, so Google serves its weekly build, 3.67 on the day. In that build `new google.maps.Map` puts its own `IntersectionObserver` on the map's div and waits for it before it builds anything. Its margin is 2,500 px around the window on a 4g connection, 3,500 on 3g, 6,000 on 2g and 4,000 when the browser does not say.
- **So** a card further than that below the window had an empty frame however good the key was, the clock ran out, and [`PinsMapFrame`](../../src/components/map/PinsMapFrame.tsx) kept the failure for the visit. That account's card sat about 9,000 px down.

## What changed

- **The clock counts only while the frame is in the window.** New kernel [`paintClock.ts`](../../src/lib/map/paintClock.ts): `paintClockLook(waitedMs, { painted, inView })` answers `painted`, `waiting` or `never-painted`, and adds a look's 500 ms only when the frame is in view. Sixteen looks in view are the same eight seconds as before.
- **It counts looks, not the wall clock**, so a browser that slows a page's timers slows the clock with them and never runs it out in one late look.
- **The canvas watches its own frame** (`useFrameInView`): an `IntersectionObserver` with no margin, held in a ref so a scroll does not redraw the map. No margin is never wider than Google's, so whenever ours says yes Google's wait is over. A browser with no `IntersectionObserver` gets no wait from Google either, and its clock runs from the load as before.
- **Not changed:** the 10 s load timeout, `gm_authFailure`, the loader's `onError`, the render boundary, the reason's words, and the frame's rule that a failure is kept for the visit.

## Tests

- `paintClock.test.ts`, 9: eight seconds are sixteen looks, a frame off screen is never given up, the whole eight seconds once it is in view, the time in view adds up across a scroll away and back, a painted map ends the wait.
- `PinsMapGoogleCanvas.render.test.tsx`, 12, new, with the Maps library and the observer stood in: a frame in view that stays empty falls back at 8 s and says so once, a frame off screen for a minute does not, a rejected key far down falls back 8 s after the card is scrolled to, the clock stops while the card is scrolled away, the observer has no margin and is let go at unmount, and the load timeout and `gm_authFailure` act as they did, off screen too.
- **Break-it:** the canvas of v2.7848 fails 7 of the 12. Starting the ref on yes fails *reads off screen until the window says otherwise*, a 4,000 px margin fails *with no margin of its own*, and dropping the disconnect fails *lets go of the window*. All 12 pass before and after.

## Checked in a browser, and what was not

Headless Chrome on a scratch page that mounts the real frame, against Google's own script (3.67):

| Case | Card | Result |
|---|---|---|
| v2.7848, bogus key | 9,000 px down | *never painted* at 8.7 s while off screen: the defect, on this Mac |
| This change, bogus key | at the top | Google paints, then answers `InvalidKeyMapError` and `gm_authFailure`: OpenStreetMap at 0.9 s |
| This change, bogus key | 9,000 px down | on Google and silent for 20 s, then OpenStreetMap 0.2 s after the scroll, by `gm_authFailure` |
| This change, the map's module and the key check held back | at the top | *never painted* at 8.9 s, eight seconds after the load |
| This change, the same | 9,000 px down | on Google and silent for 20 s, then *never painted* 7.8 s after the scroll |
| This change, no key at all | 9,000 px down | on Google and silent for 20 s, `.gm-style` in the frame at the scroll, still on Google 14 s later |

- **A rejected key today is caught by `gm_authFailure`, not by this clock.** The comment of 2026-09-08 says a bogus key left an empty frame and called nothing. Google's build of 2026-11-18 paints and then calls `gm_authFailure`, at once when the card is in view and at the scroll when it is not. The clock is kept as the last door for a frame that stays empty and silent, and rows four and five are that case made by hand.
- **Not seen before the merge:** the deployed key, which is a deploy secret. The walk on clicktooling.com follows the deploy.
