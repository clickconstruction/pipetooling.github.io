# v2.4722 — Lien desk: Share says who it is for (2026-10-06)

**The ask (Taunya, 2026-10-06, a screenshot of the Share panel on Loberg Contracting):** "Make clear this goes to masters and not customers." Run through the three lenses (her seat / the job behind the ask / the whole office), a mock-up with the panel as it was, a banner version and the envelope version, the *is this the best we can do?* pass, then Stephen's go on the envelope with the spare words cut. Mock-up: https://claude.ai/artifact/7E8dUDRNg6aP92oQnkxAEJ.

**The gap.** The panel had no recipient. The share sheet picks the person after the panel closes, so the panel could only name its audience, and it never did; its first heading, *What to send*, held a GC's name and read as *to whom*, and the blue button said only *Send…*. The fine print also said the link "carries no names or money", true of the link and false of the message above it.

## What shipped

- **A To row leads the panel** ([`LienDeskSharePanel`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskSharePanel.tsx), `data-lien-share-to`): *Someone in the office — A master, a controller or an assistant. Never a customer or a GC.* The panel now reads like an envelope: To, Which liens, the message, the buttons. Desktop panel and the phone sheet alike (one more `auto` row in the panel grid).
- **Which liens** replaces *What to send* as the scope heading and the menu's `aria-label`, in the panel and in [`LienDeskEmailSheet`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskEmailSheet.tsx).
- **Send to a teammate…** replaces *Send…* on the share-sheet button; the fine print says what the button does and that the link opens the desk after sign-in, and no longer claims the message carries no money.
- **The email sheet's from-line** reads *Sends from ClickTooling, and replies come to you. Only people who use the Lien desk are on the list.* — the old line told the reader to use Send… for mail outside the company, the opposite of the point.
- **Cut on Stephen's word** ("remove any unnecessary text since the new title says it all"): the mock-up's *About Loberg Contracting. It does not go to them.* line under the scope, and an extra line under the email sheet's people list.
- Guides: [`share-where-our-liens-stand`](../../src/content/help/share-where-our-liens-stand.md) gains *Who it is for* and says office throughout; [`see-which-supply-houses-are-owed-on-a-lien-job`](../../src/content/help/see-which-supply-houses-are-owed-on-a-lien-job.md) names the new heading. GLOSSARY and the Lien desk map amended.

## Not changed

- Who can receive the email (the desk's roles, checked again by `send-lien-desk-summary`).
