# v2.4659 — Release of Lien: a paid bill opens its unconditional waiver (2026-10-06)

Punch list #87, item I (`to-dos/lien-screens-stale-words.md`). The owner chose to fix the app where its own words were stale. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## The bug

On the Bill tab, a GC job's bill shows *Add the unconditional ›* once its money has settled (`lienWaiverCell.ts`). A bill paid in full is marked paid (`mark_invoice_paid`). The Release of Lien window kept only billed and ready-to-bill bills (`selectableInvoices`). So it left that bill out, opened on *Conditional · progress* without it, and asked nothing. `pickLienWaiverForBill` already treated a paid bill as minted and settled, and the amount math already priced an unconditional from the applied payments. Only the window's list of bills left it out.

## What changed

- **Kernel [`lienReleaseOpening.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienReleaseOpening.ts)** (+12 tests):
  - `lienReleaseSelectableInvoices` keeps billed, ready-to-bill **and paid** bills, in bill order.
  - `lienReleaseOpening` decides what a freshly opened window selects and the form it opens on. A named bill the window can cover is selected alone, and picks its own form unless the opener named one. With no bill named, the window selects the billed bills, else the ready-to-bill ones. A paid bill is never selected unasked.
  - `lienReleaseBillStatusWord` names a bill on its chip's hover (*Paid*, *Billed*, *Ready to bill*).
  - `conditionalFormOf` moved here from the window.
- **[`LienReleaseModal`](../../src/components/jobs/LienReleaseModal.tsx)**: the open-reset effect applies `lienReleaseOpening`, and the bill chips list paid bills. A paid bill's chip reads *paid in full*, as the chips already said for a bill with nothing owed.
  - v2.4582 still holds: a window that opens on an unconditional form asks first.
  - Any opening that names no bill selects exactly what it did before.
- [`docs/LIEN_RELEASE_MODAL_ARCHITECTURE.md`](../LIEN_RELEASE_MODAL_ARCHITECTURE.md): the derived `invoices` line and the open-reset line name the kernel.

## Guide lines this makes true

No guide is touched. *send a GC our lien waiver* L36 ("The **Release of Lien** window opens on that bill") and L120 ("The window still opens the unconditional, and its line says when the check clears") now hold for a paid bill too.
