# v2.8339 — Dashboard: the Lien desk is a card of its own beside Needs you (2026-11-23)

Punch list #423, PR 1 of 3. The owner's pick of 2026-11-23, option 1 on the card's mock-up, after his rule the same day: *needs you is only supposed to be a place for action*. No migration and no edge function.

## What was there

The Lien desk's four counts were a strip at the top of the Needs you card, above the card's own title (v2.7334). It was a bordered box inside the card, drawn like a row of the list. Its four facts were one wrapping line, so on a phone they broke where they fell and **The Board ›** landed alone on a third row. It carried four dot colors beside the rows' own, the unpaid amount as its widest figure, and it said *waiting on the leader* to the leader himself. Under the new phone layout the same strip was already a card of its own under the Needs you door (v2.8289).

## What changed

- **One card, beside the list.** `DashboardLienDeskCard` (the strip's file, renamed) is drawn by `DashboardPinnedQuickRow` between the Needs you door and the Needs you card. So it stands under the door on a phone with the new layout, and right above the list's card everywhere else. `DashboardNeedsYouCard` no longer draws or takes it, and its head is always its first line.
- **Three values, not four dots.** The label *Lien desk* and **The Board ›** share the first line. Under them the three counts stand side by side, each over its words, the way the Board draws its tiles. Each count is a `WorkedOutValue`. At a desk the same pieces are one line.
- **One color, and only when it means something.** The day's count is red while its tile on the Board is red. The leader's violet and the run's amber stay on the Board.
- **A red line while a paper is late or at risk.** It is the day tile's own `line`, *3 are past their day.* or *2 will not make Monday as they stand.*, so the card and the page its door opens use the same words.
- **You, to the leader.** For a dev or a leader the middle count reads *wait on you* (*waits on you* for one), the word his own lien rows use. The office reads the tile's words as before.
- **No money.** The Board's fourth tile, the unpaid amount, is not on the card. It is one press away on the Board.
- **It shows whenever the desk holds a paper.** The strip showed only while the list had a line. The card no longer depends on the list.

Who sees it is unchanged: a dev, a leader, an assistant and a controller (`isLienOffice`), on the Dashboard and on an Inbox tab. The office person's phone with Dispatch Mode on still has it in the Inbox tab and not on the Dashboard, and Job Mode's own Dashboard still draws none. Quickfill's card never drew it. The click is still counted as `#lien-board`.

## How it is built

- `src/lib/dashboard/lienDeskCard.ts`, new and pure: `lienDeskCard(board, { viewerIsLeader })` answers the three values and the line off a built Board, and `lienDeskCardShown({ door, cardsStay, bannersHidden })` answers whether a page draws the card. The second took the place of `needsYouLienStripUnderDoor` in `needsYouPlace.ts`. `lienBoardDots` is untouched: the desk's own bar still reads it.
- `useNeedsYouInputs` returns `lienDeskCard` where it returned `lienBoardStrip`.
