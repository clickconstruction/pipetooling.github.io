The owner (2026-10-06, after typing July for October on job 922 and meeting the red toast): *a modal that comes up when a user goes to put in the correct date that asks them to confirm and shows them how the notice and affidavit would change.* Mock-up: artifact VxZPZTPogSg5xe6gPduMGy — the field with the slip, the window recomputing live, five presets, and the critique (a yes-or-no box makes a typo look like the truth; opening after Save still ends in a toast; the whole timeline buries the two dates that moved; sanity lines; the reason asked after the consequences). ## What changed

- **Kernel** `src/lib/jobs/lienLastWorkDayPreview.ts`: `lienLastWorkPreview(input)` → the verdict (`refused` · `look` · `fine`), the shift sentence (*two months earlier than the clock hours say (Sep 2)*; `lienLastWorkShiftSize`), the say line, the five rows (last day · months the notice names · § 53.056 for the last month · § 53.052 affidavit · § 53.158 suit) with `changed`, and the dates on each side (`before` / `after`). `lienLastWorkMonthsFor` mirrors the readers' hand branch (the day's month joins when later than every clocked month, or stands alone). A new month whose window closed before today is named *window closed, would read as missed*. `LIEN_LAST_WORK_FAR_DAYS = 60`. `lienLastWorkDayWords` keeps a year that differs from today's.
- **Window** `LienLastWorkDayConfirm` (`data-testid="lien-last-work-confirm"`, `data-verdict`), z 1200 over the desk or Edit Job: its own date field (recomputes on commit), the shift, the say line toned by verdict, the table (`lien-last-work-diff`, rows `data-changed`), the reason (required), Cancel and **Set the day** / **Set the day anyway**; Esc closes it alone. Nothing is written in it.
- **The line** `LienLastWorkDayLine`: **Save the day** opens the window; the write moves to its Set the day; the editor row keeps only the date. New optional props `jobLabel`, `clockMonths`, `noticedMonths`, `propertyKind` — the Lien desk passes them from the selected entry (months with hours, months already noticed, the property kind); Edit Job's row passes none, so its window shows the words and the verdict without the table.
