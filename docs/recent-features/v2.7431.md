# v2.7431 — Supply houses: a house that is owed nothing takes one line (2026-11-15)

Taunya, on job 523's Supply houses table (2026-11-15): "in this section of the lien desk, I think that rows with no supply house should only take up one line". She was shown two drawings and answered "A: one line each". Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What a person sees

A paid house drew its name over *no job account*, then a dash over *2 invoices, all paid*, then a second dash, then its paid figure and a bold *$0.00*. Three such houses took the room of the one that was owed.

- **On a computer** a house that is owed nothing is one line, with about 5 px over and under it: the name in the House column, one small muted cell across the Unpaid since and notice columns (*2 invoices, all paid · no job account*), its paid figure as before and its *$0.00* muted and not bold. The cell holds one line and ends in an ellipsis when the pane is narrow; hovering it shows all of its words.
- **A house that is owed** keeps its row exactly as it was, with its date and its *They told us…* and *Mark paid…* presses.
- **The total row and the sentence under it** are as they were.
- **On a phone** a paid house took three lines: its name with a bold *$0.00*, its account and invoices, and *paid $…*. It is two now: the name with a muted *$0.00*, then one small line with the invoices, the account and *paid $…*. The line wraps and has no ellipsis, since a phone has no hover to read what an ellipsis cuts.

## What a paid row can still carry

Nothing the old row drew is dropped. The line is built in this order, so the part a reader may have to act on is the last an ellipsis could reach:

1. The house's own balance where it differs from ours (*Reece says $250.00*), in the darker ink. It was a small line under the Owed figure.
2. The invoices.
3. The job account. An open one keeps its teal and its weight.
4. The rep.
5. The note of what the house told us, in quotes.

A paid house had no *They told us…* or *Mark paid…* press before and has none now.

## How

- `lienSupplierQuietLine` in [`src/lib/jobs/lienJobSuppliers.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienJobSuppliers.ts) answers the line's parts and its title, or null for a house that is owed. The test is the one the card already used for a paid house: `owed` is not over half a cent, and so its notice is `none`.
- [`src/components/jobs/LienJobSuppliers.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienJobSuppliers.tsx): both row loops return the one-line row first when the kernel answers. The owed row's markup is not edited.
- The component is shared, so the change lands in three places: the Lien desk's notice pane, the Lien window's folded card once it is shown, and the Legal desk's Paper tab under Supply houses.
- Guide: `see-which-supply-houses-are-owed-on-a-lien-job` says a paid house takes one line.
