# v2.7158 — Bill Customer shows the real email and the real text before the bill goes (2026-11-13)

Punch list #343, PR 2 of 3: the faces. PR 1 (v2.7157) gave the window its words. This one draws what lands.

## What was wrong

The Stripe tab's box was headed *What the customer will see*. It drew the bill's own facts: the amount, the due day, the lines. The customer never sees that box. They see an email with a Pay now button, and maybe a text. The office saw neither before it went.

## The change

`BillCustomerFaces` wraps the box. Under *What Dana will get* sit three pills.

| Face | What it draws | Built by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The bill** | the box the window always drew, headed *On the bill:* | `StripeBillPreSubmitPreview`, untouched but for a `heading` prop |
| **The email** | From, To, Subject, then the message in a frame with `sandbox=""` | `billEmailFace` over `buildStripeBillEmail`, the builder `send-stripe-invoice` calls |
| **The text** | the bubble and the number, or the reason no text can go | `billTextFace` over `lineFor`, `tellFills`, `tellWords`, `numberChoices` and `tellHold`, the Tell the customer sheet's own rules |

- **The amount** is the box's own total: the preview's amount remaining, so a rolled-in fee counts.
- **What does not exist yet is said.** There is no pay page, no Stripe PDF and no read of the customer's portal before the bill is made. Pay now points at a sample, the frame takes no clicks, and the note under it says the button works once the bill is made, the PDF rides along, and a customer with a portal also gets their code.
- **The text is read on the first look**, never before, through the sheet's own reads (`loadJob`, `loadLines`, `loadTextsOn`, `loadMyName`). The customer's numbers are read in `billCustomerFacesIo.ts` and not through the sheet's `loadContact`, which goes through the door that makes a contact. A look writes nothing. A customer with no contact yet reads *Their number is read on the next screen.*
- **The bill stays mounted** behind the other faces, so its Stripe preview keeps loading and a line under edit is not lost.
- `ConvertBillToStripeModal` passes no heading and reads as before. The local preview's hint now says *edit the due date on the bill*.
