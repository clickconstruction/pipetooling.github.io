# v2.8720 — Job pace warns when the money is ahead of the work (2026-11-27)

Punch list #406, call 1, flipped. Client only. No migration, no edge function, and no kernel an edge function copies: `src/lib/jobMode/paceReading.ts` is read by the client alone. Nothing owed after the merge.

**Taunya's word, 2026-11-27.** Call 1 on the card asked: *money ahead, hours not: should the crew's card stay calm?* It was built to stay calm. She asked for a mock-up of before and after, saw the card as built beside a drawing of the warning, and said: "I don't want to stay calm, I want the warning, hours are ahead of the work". The managing session relayed it with the build: warn as the mock-up drew it, keep the hours' own warning exactly as it is, and when both are true the hours' warning wins. The warning's words are the mock-up's and are hers to change.

**What changed.** When a job's money leads the work and its hours do not, the crew's Job pace card:

- is amber, where it was calm, or green when the work was ahead of the hours;
- reads *Money spent is ahead of the work.* as its headline, where the headline was *On track.* or *The work is ahead of the hours.*;
- keeps the hours' own line under the bars, then says *More has been spent on this job than the work done accounts for.* and *If parts were bought ahead, tell the office so they know why.*;
- no longer draws the grey line *Money spent is ahead of the work. The office watches that.*, since the money is the headline;
- shows no dollar and adds no press.

**What did not change.**

- Hours ahead of the work: red, *Hours are ahead of the work.*, or amber with *Is 40% still right?*, each exactly as it was. With the money ahead as well, the reading is the same object as without it. A test holds that.
- A money budget that looks wrong: one calm line, *The office's money budget for this job looks wrong. There is nothing for you to do.*
- A card that cannot compare hours and work, with no percent, no hours budget or under 8 hours: calm, with the grey money line as before.
- The state's name. The reading still says `track` or `under`; its tone and its words are what changed. Nothing reads the state to decide a colour.
- The office's Projects board, which has its own words and its own money line.

**How it is built.** One branch in `paceReading`, after the hours' warnings have returned: `money.leads` and not `money.wrong`. Three sentences in `PACE_READING_WORDS`. `PaceReadingFace.tsx` and `SuperintendentCard.tsx` are not changed: the face already draws an amber tone, and the card already frames it in amber.

**The guide.** `job-mode-clocking` says what the amber money headline means, that the card never shows a dollar, and that the hours come first when both are ahead.

**Tests.** The kernel: the warning on a card that was calm and on one that was green, with the bars as they were and no dollar sign; the hours' warning unchanged with the money ahead; the two calm lines kept. The card's render test: amber, the headline, the three lines, an amber edge and no grey money line. The plain-words test takes the three new sentences.

**The picture.** The built card beside the mock-up she saw, at 375 px, from a scratch page that reaches no server, is with the managing session.

**To see it live.** It needs a job whose money is ahead of its work with the hours on track, read as its superintendent in Job Mode. On a ZZ test project that is a few writes, each on its own yes.
