Punch list #335, row 5, on v2.7181. The phone Pipeline's two-line row took its lien chip from the clock alone (`LienPayRunway.chipLabel`: *lien in 4 d*, *notice in 17 d*), while the same job's billed row on a computer named the step (*Answer the homestead question by Dec 15*). Client only.

## What changed

- **New kernel `lienLineStep.ts`**. `lienLineStep(todayYmd, runway, desk)` is the reading the billed row made inside `buildBilledDatesLedger`, lifted out whole: which paper's day the line races (the notice first on a sub job, a nearer retainage notice, else the affidavit), whether a late notice is the step (v2.7047), whether the desk's act names the line (v2.7043), and the Lien window's tab. `lienLateNoticeOpen(runway)` is the read past the runway's `closed`. The ledger now calls both, so the row and the chip cannot part. The money is not read there: whether the row asks in bold is still the ledger's.
- **`lienPhoneChipWords`** (same file): the chip is the act's words and the days left, *Draft the affidavit · 4 d*, *today* on the last day, and no count for an act with no day (*Public property, no lien*). A closed notice window with the affidavit's day ahead and no notice sent reads *Send the late notice · 25 d*. With no step to name it returns the clock's own words and title, so *file first*, *12 d of room* and *lien gone* read as before. Its title is the act's sentence, the last day and *Tap to open the Lien window.*
- **`jobNextLine.ts`**: `JobNextLineInput.lienDesk` (the act, its paper and that paper's day, as `LedgerLienState` carries them) and `JobNextChip.lienTab`. Whether the lien takes the chip at all is still `lienRunwayWantsTheChip`, and the chip's colour is still the clock's.
- **`JobsStagesTab`**: `phoneNextInput` hands the job's desk word from `lienStateFor` (Billed and Collections only), and the chip's tap opens the Lien window on `chip.lienTab`. It opened on Demand letter.
- **`StagesPhoneRow`**: the chip may not pass 56 % of the row. Past that it takes a second line, centred, and keeps its count. Chips that fit are unchanged.
- **Tests**: `lienLineStep.test.ts` (12), `jobNextLine.test.ts` (the chip with and without the desk's word, and a far flag), `lienOneReading.test.ts` (the phone chip joins every notice step, the affidavit ready to file and the late notice, and a new table of affidavit steps: draft, approve, waiting on the leader, three facts that hold it, public property, a hold).
- **Guides** *read the Pipeline money view* and *work the Pipeline from my phone*. Glossary *Lien desk*; `JOBS_STAGES_TAB_ARCHITECTURE.md`.
