# v2.8094 — Sub papers on a phone: the papers window opens on top (2026-11-20)

Client only: no migration, no edge function. Punch list #350, found in the audit of the card.

## Before

On a phone, **People → Subs** draws what we owe a person in a sheet (`ScheduleSheet`, stacked at 1003). *Statement of account…* and *Work statement…* inside it open the papers window, which stacked at 1002. So the window opened under the sheet: its title showed above the sheet's edge, and the period, the preview and every press were covered until the sheet was closed.

That sheet is the one door to a paper on a phone. The phone's Pay view on **Jobs → Subs** has no paper press: a sub's group and a sheet's actions keep **Print**.

Walked at 390 px on the test sub, with made-up sheets fed on the read: the element on top at the middle of the window was the sheet (*What we owe …*). After the change it is the window.

## What changed

- `ScheduleBlockSheet.tsx` names where the sheet stacks (`SCHEDULE_SHEET_Z`).
- `SubPapersWindow.tsx` stacks one above it. Closing the papers window leaves the sheet as it was.

## Tests

`SubPapersWindow.render.test.tsx` (+1): the window's overlay stacks above the phone's sheet. It fails at the old 1002.

## Not in this change

The same sheet offers **Pay** on a sheet, and the Record payment window it opens stacks at 760, below the sheet. That press was not walked here. It is card #347's window and is left for a look of its own.
