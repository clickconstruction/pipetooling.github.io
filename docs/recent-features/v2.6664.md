# v2.6664 — The walk names why the bids inbox could not be read (2026-11-07)

Punch list #142 (`docs/WALK_KIT.md` § Found on the 2026-11-07 run, item 4). Driver only: `scripts/walk/run.ts` and its test. No migration, no function.

## Why

The first live run with the tester pressing (after v2.6652's fixes and the `dev-login` redeploy) held one step: *Open the estimate from the bids inbox — the estimate email never arrived in the bids inbox*, with *bids@ had no email from the app's sender for this estimate*. Resend had delivered the email to bids@ one second after the send. The `walk` function's `open` was answering a refusal, `Gmail /messages failed (429): User-rate limit exceeded. Retry after …`, the same wall the bids reader had hit since the evening before (v2.6649 holds that reader off now), and the driver's wait read every non-200 answer as *not arrived yet*, so the report said the mail never came and nothing said why.

## Change

- **`run.ts`**: inside the open wait, an answer that is not 200 is thrown with the function's `words` (else its `error`, else the status), so the retrying wait prints it on each poll (*poll N failed, trying again on the next: Gmail /messages failed (429) …*) and the held step's *saw* carries the last one after its own words. A mail that is truly not there still reads as before.
- **`walkDriver.run.test.ts`**: a refused open holds the step with the function's words, skips the email reply, and the text legs still go.

## Verified

The driver suite (13 cases, one new) and lint. The live run that found it is recorded in `docs/WALK_KIT.md` § Found on the 2026-11-07 run, item 4, with the card's state: every leg the kit's own code drives went, the two Gmail legs hold until bids@'s quota is back, and prod is clean.
