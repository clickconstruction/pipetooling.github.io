# v2.4445 — Submittals: pick for each part in Choose from the takeoff (2026-10-02)

PR 5, the last of the train ([v2.4435](v2.4435.md) · [v2.4436](v2.4436.md) · [v2.4438](v2.4438.md) · [v2.4439](v2.4439.md)). Mock-up: https://claude.ai/artifact/VHfgwjigciY5BjkGKmtYiv (the working board: EWC-1 open with its seven parts). No migration; no edge function.

## What changed

- **A parts list where the chips were** ([`SubmittalTakeoffPicker`](../../src/components/bids/SubmittalTakeoffPicker.tsx)): under a fixture, *Show N parts* and one line of how they stand (*3 the GC sees · 3 order only · 1 left out*). Open, each part has the fixture's three buttons, smaller; an assembly's parts still sit under *Inside <assembly>*. The chips switched a part two ways; this is the third (left out), and the same control at both levels.
- **A fixture already on the draft shows its parts as the row holds them.** The tab hands each candidate `onParts`: the row's takeoff parts by their takeoff key and whether the GC sees each. A line the row does not have reads *Left out*. A row with no parts, or whose parts came from the house's file, says *Its parts are set with Edit on its row*.
- **The kernel** ([`takeoffPicks.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/takeoffPicks.ts)): `startingPiecePicks`, `piecePicksOf`, `withPiecePicks` (the fixture as picked: kept lines are its pieces, the GC's are its product, the rest set aside), `piecePicksLine`. `planTakeoffPicks` takes the part picks: a fixture coming on is built from them; a row staying on the draft lands in `plan.parts`; `plan.productKeys` and `plan.leftOut` are what the bid remembers, for the fixtures whose parts were touched only.
- **The tab applies `plan.parts` with the refresh's own writes** (`takeoffRefreshWrites` → `applyPartWrites`): a part the row keeps holds its house, lead time, stage, pages and call; one left out comes off; one brought back comes on; the row's roll-up is read again. `saveTakeoffChoices` writes `left_out_line_ids` whole for those fixtures, so a part brought back is forgotten as left out.
- **The fixture's button rules its parts without erasing them.** With the fixture on *Order only* or *Left out* the list dims and says the picks come back.
- **An ordered part cannot be left out.** `loadBidOrderFacts` now reads each part's own line (`byPartKey`); the tab maps them to takeoff keys for the rows on the draft.
