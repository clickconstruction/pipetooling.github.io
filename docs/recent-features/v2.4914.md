# v2.4914 — People → Review: a person's panel is its own piece (2026-10-07)

Client only; no migration, no edge function, no screen change. Punch list **#46** row 8 (`to-dos/decomposition-queue.md`): the Review map's step 8, Stage B of region E (`docs/PEOPLE_REVIEW_TAB_ARCHITECTURE.md`).

## The change

- **[`review/PeopleReviewPersonPanel.tsx`](../../src/components/people/review/PeopleReviewPersonPanel.tsx)** holds the per-person panel under the Team Summary: the error and Retry, Loading, the headline card, Jobs Worked, Hours and Pay, Reports, Tasks and the Labor contributors window. Its state, the Jobs Worked and Tasks rollups, the four date and pay helpers, `loadReviewData` / `loadReviewDataCore` and the load moved verbatim from PeopleReviewTab. The tab's `showPeopleForReview[selectedReviewPersonIndex]` became the `selectedPersonName` prop. The panel works out the period's range from the same three inputs and the company day as the tab does, and calls `useAuth` and `useLedgerPrefixMap` itself.
- **The tab keeps the selection**: `selectedReviewPersonIndex`, the click that sets it, and the clamp when the roster shrinks past it. The clamp is the first half of the old effect; the load half moved into the panel with the same keys (the person, the period, the paid-only switch, the roster, the users).
- **The panel stays mounted** while the roster has anyone in it and draws nothing with nobody selected. The tab never reset Jobs Worked's and Hours and Pay's collapse, the open job groups or the expanded row when you clicked another name. A panel mounted per selection would have reset them.
- `PeopleReviewTab.tsx`: 1,981 → 855 lines.
