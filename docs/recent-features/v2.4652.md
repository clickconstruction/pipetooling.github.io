# v2.4652 — The path to a lien: one story, the verdict first (2026-10-06)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. The owner, 2026-10-06, from a screenshot of job 890's History tab on the Windows view: *this is confusing, I don't understand the timeline going through these notices. Can you help me look at this view holistically and help me come up with a better way to present it?* Mock-up: artifact `Sw3LyQUWzngKMjB78HuZto` (today's view with the problems numbered, the proposal for 890, the proposal on a job with an open window). He took it as drawn.

## What was wrong

The Windows view (v2.3815) was built for one shape — a sub job with an open notice window — and degraded badly on every other. Job 890 had nothing open and nothing ahead: July's window had closed Sep 15 with nothing sent, so the lien was gone. The code demoted a closed window to a red footnote, which left no bar to chart, and the chart machinery drew on anyway: a one-month axis, the today line cutting through two rows that were sentences, *Oct 15* printed under a lien the row itself called blocked, a legend for hatching and dotted bars that appeared nowhere, serve and suit listed with no state, and the verdict — *Lien: gone* — last, after three fragments that only it explained.

## What changed

- **The verdict band** ([`LienTimelineStrip.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienTimelineStrip.tsx), `data-lien-timeline-verdict`, `data-tone`): on the row, the list and the calendar, *Next on the path*, its aside (and the Windows aside) and *Waiting on* sit in one band tinted by the Next line's tone, **above** the drawing. The mini row keeps its one quiet line under the rail. The old `data-lien-timeline-next` / `data-lien-timeline-waiting` hooks are unchanged inside it.
- **Every window on the calendar** (`windowRows`): a closed § 53.056 month is a grey bar from its first day to its last with a red ✗ at the end (`data-lien-timeline-window-kind="closed"`, `data-lien-timeline-struck`), its caption *open Aug 1 → Sep 15 · closed, nothing sent* and a *not noted* chip (`data-lien-timeline-chip`) when nobody wrote it down. A fold of up to three closed months gets a bar each; a longer run is one bar across the run with the fold's count and *N to note*. The `data-lien-timeline-windows-closed` footnote is gone.
- **The blocked lien** is a dotted ghost bar (`kind="ghost"`) from the latest closed notice's last day to the lien's last day, named *§ 53.052 lien · ↳ needs the notice above* in muted type, captioned *blocked — it would have run Sep 15 → Oct 15, once the notice was mailed*. No date is printed on it. A missed affidavit is a struck bar too.
- **The axis** ([`lienWindowsAxis.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienWindowsAxis.ts), new kernel: `windowsAxis`, `monthStart`, `monthEnd`, `monthShift`): every bar and today, whole months, never fewer than three — a short story gets a month of air on each side. The today label carries the day (*today · Oct 6*).
- **The today line** runs only across bar rows (`data-lien-timeline-today`); a row of words has none. An undated paper draws a dashed ring before its words.
- **Whose move** draws under the row's name as the same pill the Steps row uses.
- **After a filing**: serve, hold, suit and release with nothing live fold into one quiet sentence (`data-lien-timeline-windows-after`): *After a filing: § 53.055 serve · § 53.158 suit Nov 16, 2027 · or a release when paid.* — or, on a job whose lien is gone, *§ 53.055 serve · § 53.158 suit follow a filing. None can follow on this job.* One that is due, missed or done is a row.
- **The legend** (`data-lien-timeline-windows-legend`) names only the marks drawn on that job; *│ today* always.
- **The Job History box** ([`JobHistoryLienTimeline.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobHistoryLienTimeline.tsx)) passes `view="windows"`: the calendar, no switch. The Lien desk pane, the Affidavits pane, the Lien window, the Legal desk and the firm's portal keep the Steps · Windows switch and the remembered choice; their Windows view is the new drawing.

## Not in this PR

The mock-up's *Note the closed window* and *Open Collections ›* doors in the band — the strip has no wiring to the desk's note flow or to Collections today; the band's words say what to do. And the question the screenshot raised beside the strip: the desk's Affidavit pile still counts *9 days left · Fix the property* on 890 while the timeline says the lien is gone, because `lienDeskAffidavits.ts` treats a missing notice as a gate the office can still clear and `lienTimeline.ts` treats a closed, unsent window as the end. Whether a late notice can still carry the affidavit is counsel's question; it is logged in the to-do card `to-dos/lien-screens-stale-words.md` as item L.
