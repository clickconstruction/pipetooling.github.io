# v2.4016 — GC Review call sheet: the bills behind each total (2026-09-28)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Owner ask, on a screenshot of the call sheet: "make it easy for an assistant to see the bills that make up these totals on the same page with the ability to dig into the bills where necessary" — and "when a user hits save answers where does that save?"

## What shipped

- **`src/lib/jobs/gcCallSheet.ts`** — a `CallSheetRow` carries `bills` (the worklist row's own group, so they come to `amount`) and `collections` (the GC's Collections bills, which the round leaves out of what it counts). Both are `CallSheetBill`: a review row with the date its job was last promised. `buildCallSheet` takes `promisedPayDates` and `collectionsByGc`; without them a sheet is what it was.
  - `callSheetBillsSummary(bills)` → count, jobs, total (summed in cents), how many are 90 days old or more and what they come to, how many carry a promise.
  - `callSheetBillsLabel` — "18 bills on 12 jobs · 1 over 90 days, $4,421.26".
- **`GcBillLines.tsx`** — a GC's bills as lines: the job, billed date and age (red at 90 days), a Collections chip, the promised date ("said Sep 15 · late"), what is owed, and a chevron that drops the job's latest four activity events (`list_job_activity_events`, read once per job). The job is a link when `onOpenJobDetail` is given. `compact` is the call sheet's layout: smaller type, and the job and the customer give way so the age and the amount always show.
- **`GcReviewCertifyModal.tsx`** draws its checklist with `GcBillLines` (`leading` is the tick box, `pending` the unticked rows) — the lines, the link and the activity dropdown it had, moved. 241 → 167 lines.
- **`GcCallSheetModal.tsx`** — under each GC's total, a disclosure with the summary line; the bills open **under the answer fields**, so what is being typed stays in view. A sum line repeats the total. Collections bills follow under "In Collections — owed too, and not in the $X above". **Show all bills / Hide all bills** in the header. New props `todayYmd` and `onOpenJobDetail`.
- **`JobsGcReviewModal.tsx`** — `collectionsByGc` (a memo over a rollup with Collections in) and the two props; the call sheet's job link is the shell's `onOpenJobDetail`, the door the certify checklist already uses.
- Help guide `run-your-gc-statement-round`: the bills, and a new **Where the answers go**. Glossary and `GC_REVIEW_MODAL_ARCHITECTURE.md` amended.

## Where Save answers goes

`saveCallSheet` in `JobsGcReviewModal.tsx`, one GC at a time:

1. `upsertGcStatementRoundMark` → **`gc_statement_round_marks`**, one row per GC per week (`week_start`, `gc_customer_id`). `mergeRoundMarkWrite` keeps a statement already marked sent that week. The row holds the temperature, the sentence, `expected_pay_by`, the channel, `acted_by` (the person signed in) and the word's source (`word_from_*`, `word_heard_via`, `word_entered_by*`, `word_at`).
2. A pay date that is today or later → **`job_payment_promises`** through `add_job_payment_promise`, on each of that GC's bills not already on that date (v2.3991).
3. Answers that came by the account man's link are marked read (`gc_word_ask_answers`).

Nothing is written for a row left blank. **undo** deletes the week's mark; it does not take back promises already filed on the bills. A GC that fails stays on the sheet with the reason; the rest are kept.
