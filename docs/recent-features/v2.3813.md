# v2.3813 — Lien desk: “he is here” — the office records the leader’s word without him signing in (2026-09-24)

The owner asked for a tool for an assistant with the master beside them, so he can act on their computer and the desk keeps moving. The first design was a sign-on — a second, memory-only session for the master inside the assistant's desk. The owner's call, 2026-09-24: *"instead of the master signing in every time, I would prefer just a written declaration the assistant can make such as 'he is standing over me' or 'he is typing it in'."* The desk already had the record for that — the leader's spoken word (v2.3405: `approval_mode = 'word'`, who · when · phone / in person / text) — so this PR adds the two declarations to it and opens the door where the assistant is actually waiting. One migration.

## The change

- **Kernel [`lienWord.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienWord.ts)** (new, tested) — `LIEN_WORD_CHANNELS` = phone · in_person · text · **standing_over** · **typing**, with the picker words (*he is standing over me* / *he is typing it in*) and the record words (*standing over the desk* / *typed it in himself*); `leaderPresent(channel)`; `wordRecordBlock(claimGate, channel)` — a claim set by hand over the balance (v2.3682) refuses a remembered word and takes a present leader; `wordRecordWords(item)` → *on the leader’s word · Robert, Sep 24 · standing over the desk*; `presenceLine(channel, recorderName)` — the sentence under the picker saying who recorded it and that he can pull it back with *Not what I said*.
- **[`LienWordRecordRow.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienWordRecordRow.tsx)** (new) — the one *Who said it, when, and how* row, replacing the four inline copies on the notice footer ([`LienDeskModal`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskModal.tsx)), the [affidavit](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskAffidavitPane.tsx) and [retainage](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskRetainagePane.tsx) panes and the [Put-a-GC-on-notice run](../../src/components/jobs/GcOnNoticeModal.tsx). The two presence channels sit after the three remembered ones with a stronger border; the presence line appears under the row once one is picked; a refused Record shows its reason in red instead of only on hover. The panes take `authName` for the line.
- **The awaiting-approval footer** (the office's side, where it read only *Waiting on the leader since Sep 22 · Pull back to draft*) gains **He is here — record it ▸** (assistant · controller · dev, `canSendLienOnWord`), which opens the row with *standing over me* pre-picked and the lead-in *He is here — who, when, and how:*. Recording flips the item straight to approved on his word — the same write as from a draft (`sendLienDeskItemOnWord`; the guard's `word` branch is unchanged).
- **The over-the-balance gate**: on the notice footer `wordRecordBlock` replaces the flat `claimGate === 'leader'` refusal; in the run, `mode === 'word' && (!j.claimOver || leaderPresent(wordChannel))` — a present leader carries the by-hand claim; a remembered word still sends it to him.
- **The record reads the channel**: the pile row's hover and the Ready-pile footer use `wordRecordWords` (were `on the leader’s word · <note>`).
- **Migration [`20260924120000_lien_word_leader_present.sql`](../migrations/20260924120000_lien_word_leader_present.md)** — the `word_channel` CHECK takes the two values. Push it with the merge: the new client sends them, the old CHECK refuses them.

## What it is not

Not a sign-in and not a second session: `approved_by` stays the person who recorded it, the channel says the leader was there, and the leader's *Sent on your word* / *Not what I said* is the check on it. The master-sign-on design (a `persistSession: false` client for one desk) stays in this fragment's history only.
