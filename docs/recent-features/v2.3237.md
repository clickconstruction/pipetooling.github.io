# v2.3237 — Job History on a phone: one range bar instead of the search box, From / To, chips and summary (2026-09-10)

Client only; no migration.

## Why

Above the phone day list (v2.3235) sat four rows saying one thing: the job search box reading *1 job · 181 days* (nothing to search with one job), two big grey date buttons with From and To beside them, the Last 90d / 365d chips, and the summary line — about 180 px before the first day. Grace: "the from [date] to [date] looks poor". Mockup iterated with her: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/037cdc9b-ef69-4e6f-8ad8-f51a9ccc8758

## What shipped

- **Kernel** `src/lib/jobs/historyRangeBar.ts`: `presetRange(n, today)`, `rangePreset(start, end, today)` (90 / 180 / 365 or null for a hand-picked range), `formatRangeDate` ("Mar 14"; "Nov 3, 25" only when the year is not this year), `rangeCaption` ("last 180 days · 142 worked · up to 9 people" on a preset; "312 days · 168 worked · up to 9 people" otherwise; "… · no days worked"). Tested.
- **Component** `HistoryRangeBar`: a two-line bar. Top: the dates as "Mar 14–Sep 10" with the only break opportunity after the dash (one line when it fits, "Mar 14–" over "Sep 10" when it must), the presets as a segmented control (lit when the range is exactly that preset), **Edit**. Bottom: the caption, centered. Edit reveals the same two native `<input type="date">`s under the bar (From / To, min/max cross-linked) and reads Done; picking a preset hides them.
- **Tab** (`ProjectsJobHistoryTab`): in the phone single-job branch the whole controls block (search, From / To, chips, layout toggle) is replaced by the bar, wired to the existing `persistRange`; the day list's own summary line is gone since the bar carries it. The 180-day preset is new and is what a single job opens with (`SINGLE_JOB_HISTORY_DAYS`). Desktop and Projects → Job History unchanged.
- Help guide sentence in `job-window-tabs.md`.
