# v2.5047 — GC mode: the submittal register, for a dev (2026-10-09)

The Building lane's U4b in the GC mode real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/building-u4.md`, on branch `spike/gc-mode`). It is the window over U4a's presses (v2.5037, migration `20261010005000_gc_submittal_writes`) and their generated types, and the email that takes a submittal to the architect. It is dev only, behind the dev door, like the rest of Building.

- **`src/components/gc/GcSubmittalsWindow.tsx`** opens from **Submittals** on a building job's card on `/gc` (`?submittals=<project id>`), behind `canUseGcBuilding`. Ported from the prototype's `GcBuildingSubmittals.tsx`:
  - the counts strip, and one card per awarded trade with its register, in the prototype's words;
  - **Add a submittal**: what it covers, the kind, the spec section with the trade's own lines' sections suggested, the work it holds, the lead days, and a needed-by day only when none of that work is on the schedule;
  - the trade's move: **It came by email**, with the file's name, its Drive link and a note. A link only some can open warns that the architect may not open it (`checkDriveAccess`), and never stops the press;
  - ours: **Send to {architect}** (the email), or **We sent it another way**. A round with no Drive link can only go another way;
  - the architect's: **Approved**, **Approved as noted**, or **Revise and resubmit**, which needs what to change.
- **`src/lib/gc/submittalRows.ts`**: the rows as the prototype's `Submittal` (`withSubmittals(state, tables)` over `boardProjectFromView`'s projects), each round's Drive link, sender and email beside it, and the presses' payloads. **`src/lib/gc/submittalsIo.ts`**: `loadGcSubmittals` and one function per press. The window opens through 6a's `loadSchedule`, so a submittal is needed by the first start of the work it holds less its lead days.
- **`gc-architect-email`** (new, kind `submittal`): reads the submittal as the caller, sends the newest round to the architect with its Drive link from `COMPANY_EMAIL_FROM`, the project manager as Reply-To, files the sent copy, and records the send through `gc_send_submittal_to_architect` as the caller. Its words are `_shared/gcArchitectEmail.ts` (`buildGcSubmittalEmail`), shared with the sample on Settings → What customers see (journey step *Submittal for review*, sample `gc-submittal`).
- **`src/lib/gc/gcIo.ts`** exports `FnResult` and `fnProblem` for the new io file.
- **Docs:** `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` (`gc-architect-email`), `docs/GLOSSARY.md` (*Submittal · submittal round*), `docs/PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` §20 and `docs/ACCESS_CONTROL.md` (the window, Building's dev gate), and the guide *send a trade's submittal to the architect* (`roles: dev` until Building's door).
- **Tests:** `submittalRows.test.ts` (Fair Oaks D's six read back, the kernels' words the same, payloads), `gcArchitectEmail.test.ts`, `GcSubmittalsWindow.render.test.tsx` (each move's press, revise held without a note, the link warning, plain words).
