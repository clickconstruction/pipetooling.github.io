# v2.6102 — The lien path on a job with no GC names no § 53.056 notice as the next step (punch list #213, PR 1) (2026-10-29)

Punch list #213 (`to-dos/legal-desk-two-readings.md`), finding 2. A read of a live account on the Legal desk on 2026-10-29 — a job whose GC had been moved back into the customer slot, so the desk reads Click as the original contractor — found the Paper tab's *Where each job stands* contradicting itself: the § 53.056 row read *none · not required · contracted with the owner*, and *Next on the path* above it read *Draft the <month> notice — N days* with *Waiting on us — the notice to be drafted and mailed*.

## Why

`buildLienTimeline` (`src/lib/jobs/lienTimeline.ts`) drew the step rows by the role — one *not required* row when `isSub` is false — but read the job's work months for the next step whatever the role. The months come from the approved clock sessions through `buildLienTimelineFromWindow` (the Lien window, the Legal desk's Paper tab, the firm's portal) whether or not the job has a GC, so an open month on a direct job made the next step a notice the law does not ask of an original contractor.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/jobs/lienTimeline.ts`**: an original contractor has no notice months. When `isSub` is false the open, missed and sent month reads are empty, so *Next on the path* and *Waiting on* fall through to the § 53.057 retainage notice (when the contract-end day is set) or the § 53.052 affidavit, the steps the row already names. A closed month on a direct job is no closed window and no lien gone. The step rows are unchanged; sub jobs are unchanged.
- **Tests**: `lienTimeline.test.ts` gives an original contractor the fixture's two open months and expects the affidavit as the next step, *the affidavit to be filed* as Waiting on, no Windows aside and no closed window; the same with a dated retainage clock expects the § 53.057 notice; a missed month on a direct job expects no lien gone. `lienTimelineDesk.test.ts` runs a direct job's sessions through the window adapter and expects one *not required* row and the affidavit. `legalLienPaper.test.ts` does the same through `buildLegalJobTimelines`, the Paper tab's door.
- **`docs/GLOSSARY.md`**: the Lien desk's timeline sentence and the Legal desk's *Where each job stands* sentence say so in place.

No migration, no edge function, no guide change: *file a lien and never miss its deadlines* already says the § 53.056 step counts on subcontractor jobs only.
