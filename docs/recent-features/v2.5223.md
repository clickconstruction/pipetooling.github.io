# v2.5223 — Hours approvals: approve the sessions the dispatch schedule already vouches for (2026-10-10)

The first upstream fix of the Bridge's redraw (owner 2026-10-10: "build it all"; the page's ceiling is its inputs, and 585h sat unapproved that day). Client only. No migration, no edge function; the approve path is the existing `approve_clock_sessions_v2`.

- **The rule** (`src/lib/people/scheduleMatch.ts`, `matchSessionsToSchedule`): a pending session matches when `job_schedule_blocks` had the same person on the same job that day and the day's pending hours on that job, every session summed, land within `SCHEDULE_MATCH_TOLERANCE_HOURS` (1) of the blocks' hours summed. A session with no job, or one already flagged long or near-zero, never matches. `onScheduleIds` keeps the queue's order; `scheduleMatchTitle` says what dispatch had and what was clocked.
- **The read** (`fetchScheduleBlocks.ts`): the blocks over the pending days (`pendingDateRange`), five columns, read once with the queue; a failed read leaves the chips off.
- **The queue** (`PeopleHoursApprovalsQueueModal.tsx`): each matched row carries *on the schedule* (an info chip) or *⚠ off the schedule* (a flag chip, with the hours in its title); the header line counts the matches; **Approve the N on the schedule · Xh** beside **Approve everything** takes the matches through the same confirm and approve path, punches only, the typed and held rows set aside as every batch button does.
- **Why a press and not a cron**: approving hours is payroll's attestation. The rule picks, the person presses; nothing is approved unseen.
- **Tests**: `scheduleMatch.test.ts` (5) and two cases in `PeopleHoursApprovalsQueueModal.render.test.tsx` (the chips, the title's hours, the count, the press; no button with nothing on the schedule).
- **Docs**: guide *clear the hours approvals backlog* gains "The sessions on the dispatch schedule"; `PEOPLE_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md`'s render-tests line.
