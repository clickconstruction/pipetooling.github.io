# v2.7234 — Lien desk on a phone: a job opens as its own screen (2026-11-13)

Punch list #352, PR 4 of eight, on the owner's pick of direction A, the one list. PR 0 to PR 3 are on main.

## What was wrong

A row of the first view on a phone held three small doors and one press: the job's name (28 px tall) opened the Job window, the chip opened the paper, the four dots opened the step card as a sheet, and the button acted. To see where one job stood, a person opened a sheet, closed it, and then found the button again on the row. Nothing on a phone showed one job whole.

## The change

- **A row opens its job.** On a phone `LienDeskNextUp` takes `onOpenRow`. A tap on the row, on the job's name or on the dots opens the job as its own screen. The row's press and the paper's chip keep their own doors and stop the tap there. The whole row is the target now, 111 px tall at 390 wide, where the name alone was 28.
- **The screen.** `src/components/jobs/LienDeskJobScreen.tsx` (new) fills the desk's panel, over the header and the list: the way back (44 px) with the job and what it still owes; a band with the step card's own due line (*File by Nov 16 · 3 days left*) and the row's state, red, amber or quiet as the card is; **Where it stands**, the card's four steps with their words in a size a thumb reads; the doors; and the row's press pinned at the foot, 48 px tall, with its exact words. A row this viewer has no move on reads **Open**. A run row lists its jobs, each a door to its job, and keeps its second press.
- **The words.** `src/lib/jobs/lienPhoneJobScreen.ts` (new kernel), `lienJobScreen`. Every word is one the desk already says: the due line and the steps are `lienStepCard`'s, the press is the row's button, the paper's door says how many details are missing as the chip's count does. The steps' list was lifted out of `LienStepCardView` as `LienStepCardItems`, so the card beside the dots on a computer and this screen draw the same list.
- **Back closes it.** The open row belongs to the page. `LienDeskPage.tsx` pushes a history entry at the same address with the row's key in the entry's state, so the phone's Back, the swipe from the edge and the screen's own ‹ all return to the list where it was. A press that opens a notice makes its own entry after it, so Back from the notice lands on the job's screen. A press that takes the desk to another view drops the row from the entry (`lienDeskStateForView`), so coming back to the first view shows the list.
- A run and the Lien window open over the screen as windows, and closing one leaves the screen where it was. The paper opens over it the same way.

Three things the card drew that this PR does not build. *Add the filed copy* is not a door here: it is a write, and the Lien window's door already leads to it. The line that said drafting and printing stay on a computer is left out, since PR 6 fits an open paper to a phone. And the steps are the step card's own four, with their sentences, in place of four short names.

No migration, no edge function, no change to what any press writes or who may press it.
