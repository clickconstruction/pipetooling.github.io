# v2.8074 — Lien desk on a phone: who noted a closed month stays inside its row (2026-11-20)

Found by the Fable 5.2 audit of punch list #361 (the ledger, v2.7989). Client only: one class, two style rules.

## What was wrong

A closed month someone wrote down reads *noted by Christopher Montgomery Nov 17* in its window. [`LienDeskMonths.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskMonths.tsx) held that line to one line with an inline `white-space: nowrap`. An inline style beats the container rule that stacks a narrow card, which is the lesson of v2.7446 that the ledger's own styles follow everywhere else. So on a phone the line could not wrap, and it ran out of the month's column into the tick's.

Measured in a browser on made-up months, the card as wide as the live pane makes it:

| Window | The month's column ends at | *noted by Robert Douglas Sep 17* ended at | *noted by Christopher Montgomery Sep 17* ended at |
|---|---|---|---|
| 390 px | 248 | 220, fits | 274, in the tick's column |
| 375 px | 233 | 220, fits | 274, in the tick's column |
| 320 px | 178 | 220, in the tick's column | 274, under the tick box (232 to 245) |

The ledger's walk of v2.7989 read live notices whose noted lines were short, so it did not show.

## What changed

- The line carries `lienLedgerNoted` and no inline style. In [`src/index.css`](../../src/index.css) the class keeps it to one line, as it was at a desk.
- Under 560 px of card the rule `.lienMonthClosed > .lienLedgerNoted` lets it shrink and wrap inside the month's column.
- After the change every case in the table ends at the column's edge or before it. At a desk (1,037 px of card) nothing moved.

## Tests

`LienDeskMonths.notedLine.render.test.tsx` (new, 2): the line has no inline `white-space`, and the narrow card's block of `index.css` holds the rule that lets it wrap. A jsdom render lays nothing out, so the widths above are a browser's.
