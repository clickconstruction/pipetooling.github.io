# v2.7345 — Job accounts: None needed asks why, and a not needed answer can be changed (2026-11-15)

Client only; no migration, no edge function.

## Why

Taunya opened a test job on 2026-11-13 and found three grey chips on its Job accounts line, each reading *not needed*, with a sheet that said *The office decided this job does not need an account at Moore Supply: New job — no job account needed*. Nobody had decided anything. The rows came from one press of **None needed** on the question that opens when a hand-made job saves: a quiet link beside **Later** that marked every house at once and wrote a canned note where a reason belongs. Every other door to *not needed* (the house roster, the Dispatch errand, the PO line, the Bids lens, the two Needs you decks) asks why. And the sheet on the job had only **Close**, so the one way back was the house's roster under Materials.

Eight jobs on prod carry the canned note, 24 of the 32 not-needed rows.

## What changed

- **The sheet says what happened.** `notNeededSheetWords` words the not-needed sheet. A typed reason reads as before: *The office decided this job does not need an account at Ferguson: Service call*. A row with the old canned note (`isNoReasonNewJobNote`) reads *Marked not needed when the job was made. No reason was given.* A row with no note at all says so too. The chip's hover follows (`notNeededChipTitle`).
- **None needed… asks why.** On [`JobAccountsAfterCreatePrompt`](../../src/components/jobs/JobAccountsAfterCreatePrompt.tsx) the press now opens a reason step and writes nothing. Three one-tap reasons (`NEW_JOB_NOT_NEEDED_REASONS`: *Service call*, *Builder's own account*, *Insurer pays the house*) or the office's own words fill one box. The button counts the houses through `countedLabel` (*Mark 3 houses not needed*) and is dead until there is a reason. `newJobNotNeededRows` builds the rows: one per house still unanswered, the reason as each row's `note`, the way the other doors keep it.
- **A way back, on the job.** For the office (`isOfficeRole`), the not-needed sheet on [`JobAccountsStrip`](../../src/components/jobs/JobAccountsStrip.tsx) gains two presses. **Change this…** closes the sheet and opens `MarkJobAccountOpenedModal` on that row, on *Not needed*, so the office marks the account opened or gives the reason; a canned note is left out of the box (`changeNotNeededExisting`), so staying on not needed takes a real one. **Take the mark off** deletes the row, the chip reads *none yet* again, and the signals that row quieted come back. The field sees the words and neither press.
- **The empty-words check lets a press's label through.** `check:empty-words` read *None needed…* as a bare empty sentence, since it opens on *None*. A `<button>`'s own label is a press, not a list with nothing in it, so `bareEmptySentences` now leaves the direct text of a `<button>` alone (`isPressLabel`), the fourth such case beside a single word, an `<option>` and a cell fallback. A sentence beside a button, or inside an element under one, is still read.
- **Guides**: *open a job account before buying parts* says how None needed… asks, how to change the answer, and what the older sentence means; *open a job account when you win a bid* follows.

## Not changed

- The table, its policies and its stamp trigger. A not-needed row still records no name and no time of its own, so the sheet cannot say who pressed or when. That takes a migration (two columns and the trigger) and is not in this PR.
- The eight jobs' rows. Nothing is rewritten; they read the new sentence, and the office changes each from its chip.
- `NEW_JOB_NOT_NEEDED_NOTE` stays as the words to recognise. Nothing writes it any more.
- The other five doors to *not needed*, the roster's words, and what *not needed* quiets (the *bought with no account* line and the move window).

## Tests

- `jobAccountsNewJob.test.ts`: the rows None needed writes, nothing without a reason, the sheet's three wordings, the chip's hover, and the row handed to Mark opened.
- `JobAccountsStrip.render.test.tsx`: the old answer's sentence, **Change this…** opening Mark opened with the reason box empty, a typed reason carried over, **Take the mark off** deleting the row, and the field seeing neither press.
- `JobAccountsAfterCreatePrompt.render.test.tsx`: the press writes nothing, the counted button is dead until a reason, a one-tap reason and the office's own words each land as every row's note.
