## Two choices worth knowing

- **A list of records on the paper, not one id.** A paper put back can be sent again as a new record, and that record voided in its turn. One id would be overwritten, and the first record would then read as a record with no paper and go back alone. With a list only the last void can be undone, and the earlier record is refused.
- **A stamp, not only the paper's status.** A paper put back can be edited, printed, signed or approved before the un-void and still read untied and approved or drafted. The guard stamps `updated_at` on every write, so a paper whose `updated_at` still equals the void's stamp has not been written since. Any write refuses the un-void with *The paper this record sent has moved on since the void. The record stays voided.*

## No rule for two live records

`job_lien_filings` has no unique index and no check that holds one live record per job or per month, and the kernels read several live records side by side. So a record put back collides with nothing, and no refusal for it was built. The page lists what was read.

## The walk, for the walker

Each write is a write on prod and waits on its own yes. Use a ZZ Test job with a § 53.056 notice paper on the Lien desk.

1. As the sample assistant through View as: open the ZZ Test job's Lien window. No *Voided records* line is drawn, on any tab.
2. As the dev, on a real job's Lien window with a voided record: no *Voided records* line is drawn. Press nothing.
3. As the dev, on the ZZ Test job: record the notice's mailing so its paper reads sent, then **Void** the record. The paper is back on the desk.
4. The window reads *Voided records · N*. Press **Show**, then **Put back…**. The confirm reads *Put this record back?* and its button **Put the record back**. Confirm. The toast reads *The record is back. Its paper on the Lien desk is sent again, tied to it.*
5. Read the record and the paper through the dev seat: `voided_at` is null, the paper is `sent`, tied to the record, on its old sent day.
6. Void the record again. On the desk, change the paper. Press **Put back…** and confirm: the line reads *The paper this record sent has moved on since the void. The record stays voided.*
7. A record voided before the push, on a job that held a notice paper by then, reads *This record was voided before the app kept what a void does to its desk paper. It stays voided.*
8. Leave the ZZ Test job as it was found, and tell `fixing` what was made and what stays.
