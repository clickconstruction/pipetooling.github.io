# v2.7559 — Lien desk: an envelope says where the other jobs at its property stand (2026-11-16)

The last piece of the office's two reports of 2026-11-16 in the notices' run window (see [`v2.7489.md`](./v2.7489.md)). On an owner's envelope that listed one job she asked why, at a property with six invoices. The envelope was right: the other jobs' notices had gone earlier. v2.7489 made the window say which paper it holds. This makes the envelope itself say where the property's other jobs stand. The owner picked the shape on 2026-11-16: **one line for the envelope**. A contractor's envelope that spans properties gets one line that totals them, not one line a property.

## What changed

- **The line.** [`src/lib/jobs/lienRunEnvelopeProperty.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienRunEnvelopeProperty.ts), pure. `lienRunProperties(desk)` groups the jobs the desk holds by property record (`jobs_ledger.customer_address_id`, never a match on typed addresses) and words each job with the whole-property view's own lines (`lienPropertyNoticeLine`, `lienPropertyAffidavitLine` in [`lienPropertyPapers.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienPropertyPapers.ts)). A notice's pile comes from the notices' queue, its sent day from the job's recorded filings (`lienAffidavitOwnerSends`), an affidavit's facts from the affidavits' queue. `lienRunEnvelopePropertyLine({ inside, properties })` gives the envelope's words and one press a property, or null when no other job is at any of its properties.
- **What it counts.** *N notices in this envelope* is the envelope's jobs. The notices after it are the jobs NOT in the envelope, in the property view's tally (`lienPropertyTallies`): a notice that went is *sent*, by its day, the newest first. The lien affidavits are counted for every job at the property, the envelope's own jobs too, because no affidavit is ever in this envelope and the office counts a property's liens whole. One is left out: a job in the envelope whose affidavit waits on its notice, since this envelope is that notice.
- **Where it is drawn.** `LienDeskRunModal` takes `properties` and `onOpenProperty`. Each envelope card under **Envelopes and copies** ends with the line (`run-property-<n>`), and each press (`run-property-open-<n>-<addressId>`) calls `onOpenProperty`. A combined notice hands in each of its jobs with that job's own property record. `LienDesk.tsx` builds `properties` from its own read and passes `openProperty`, so the property card (z-index 805) opens over the run (790) and Esc closes the card alone. The GC's on-notice window hands neither, and its envelopes say nothing more.
- **One word in the property view's tally.** Several affidavits that wait on a notice read *2 waiting on a notice*. Before, the tally fell back to the Board's words for one paper (*2 waiting on its notice*). The property card's count line reads the same list, so it changes with it.

## Limits

- The line counts the jobs the desk holds: the ones started, or inside its 180-day lead. The property card reads By job's book, which looks further back, so the card can list a job the line does not count.
- A job with no property record is not counted, and an envelope whose jobs have none gets no line.
