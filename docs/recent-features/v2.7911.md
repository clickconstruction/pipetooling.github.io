# v2.7911 — Lien desk: voiding a notice’s record puts the notice back (2026-11-19)

The follow-up to [v2.7892](./v2.7892.md), which put a filed lien's paper back when its record is voided and left a notice alone. The owner asked for the same void to be measured on a notice, then picked the same fix, on 2026-11-19.

## Before

Measured on 2026-11-19 on the ZZ test job as the dev, with the app's own writes, before anything was changed:

- A notice paper approved for one month, a `notice_53_056` record naming it, the paper marked sent, the record voided. Read back: the paper still `sent`, still tied to the voided record, with its sent day. No live notice record on the job.
- That paper through `buildLienDeskQueue` and `buildLienBoard`, the month row as the reader answers after a void (`noticed: false`):
  - **The month still open:** pile `to_draft`, bin *To send for Master approval*. The desk asked for a new draft, a new approval and a new signature, and the approved paper was out of reach.
  - **The month closed:** pile `sent`, bin *Sent*, for thirty days, with the missed month behind it.
  - **The job not listed by the reader:** pile `sent`, bin *Sent*, for thirty days.
- `letterTwoStatus` reads the paper's `status` and `sent_at` and never the record. On the voided notice it answered *waiting* on day 0, *due* on day 10 and *overdue* on day 15.
- The live desk had no such paper: 25 notice records, 4 voided, 17 sent notice papers, none tied to a voided record. So nothing old moves.

## What changed

- **Migration [`20261119050000_lien_void_puts_notice_back.sql`](../migrations/20261119050000_lien_void_puts_notice_back.md).** The guard and the trigger's function of `20261119020000`, restated by script with their affidavit-only tests widened. The trigger now fires for an affidavit or a § 53.056 notice going from live to voided, and moves the paper of the record's own kind. The guard's block that reads `sent` back to `approved` as no new approval drops its `kind = 'affidavit'` test. Each body diffs to those lines and nothing else.
- **`src/lib/jobs/lienFilingVoid.ts`.** `lienVoidHasPaper` names the two records that have a desk paper. `lienVoidPaperWords` takes the record's kind and speaks of a notice in a notice's words: *sent* where a lien is *filed*.
- **`src/lib/jobs/lienFilingVoidIo.ts`.** `voidLienFiling` reads the tied papers of the record's own kind around the void, for a notice as for a lien. A retainage notice and a release of record still make one write and no reads.
- **The Lien window.** Void on a notice's record says where the notice went.
- **The guide** *send lien notices from the Lien desk* has a paragraph, *A mailing recorded by mistake*.

## Where the notice lands

- Approved before: approved again, with its signature, its printed mark and its fields as they were. A printed notice stands in *Approved · to mail*, an unprinted one in *Approved · to print*, one approved on his word and not signed in *Approved · to sign*.
- Recorded as already sent before anyone approved it: back to the office's draft.
- A newer notice paper already on the job: the old one is set aside and the newer one stands.
- Its sent day is cleared, so the attorney's letter clock answers *none* (pinned in `lienLetterTwo.test.ts`).

## What a void does not undo

Recording a mailing can email a copy, issue an enclosed release and place a pay offer. A void calls none of those back, as before. The job's History line *Lien notice sent* stays, as before.

**Undo** on a record that applies a paper already on record (v2.7508) is untouched. That paper was set aside, never sent, so the trigger matches nothing there.

## Order

Either order is safe. The client names nothing this migration creates. Before the push a notice's void behaves as before and the window says the notice still reads as sent.
