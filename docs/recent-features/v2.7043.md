# v2.7043 — Pipeline: a billed row's lien line names the next act (2026-11-12)

Punch list #325, PR 2 of five, on v2.7042's trim. v2.7042 stopped the line under a lien date from repeating the row. This PR removes the cause: the bold row named the deadline (*File the affidavit*) while the desk's real next step sat in small type under it, so the row asked for an act the job was not ready for. Grace's pick of 2026-11-12, after a second pass replayed on every live row: **the paper gives the line its words; the clock gives it its day, dot, weight and colour.** No migration, no edge function.

## What changed

- **New kernel `src/lib/jobs/lienActWords.ts`** (pure, tested): `lienActFor({ paper, action, yours, waitingOn, missing })` turns a Do now move on a notice, an affidavit or a retainage notice into a `LienAct` — the line's words (*Find the owner*, *Draft the notice*, *Draft the affidavit*, *Approve the affidavit* or *Waiting on Marisol*, *Sign the notice*, *Send the notice*, *Record the mailing*, *Review the hold*, *Send the late notice*, *File the affidavit*, *Serve the affidavit*), one or two plain sentences on where the paper stands (`why`), a second line when the act carries one (`under`) and `dated`. An affidavit held by its property facts names the one closed gate (*Answer the homestead question*, *Add the GC's address*, *Find the owner*, *Add the legal description*) or *Fix the property record* when several are closed; a homestead reads *Homestead, ask the attorney*; a public owner reads *Public property, no lien* with `dated: false` and *Ask the attorney about the bond* under it. A reminder (the courtesy notice, the attorney's letter, a promise to record), a closed window and a returned envelope have no act. `lienRowTip` writes a row's tip (the `why`, *The last day is Nov 16.*, *Press to open the Lien window.*, and one more sentence when the day is a guess: `lienDayGuessSentence`).
- **`lienArChip.ts`**: `LienArChip.act` (`actOf`: the affidavit entry's closed gates, else the desk row's own words for the two holds it names; a hold's reason as the act's `under`). A filed affidavit's `lead` is what the chip adds to a line that already says the filing day (*served Oct 6*, *serve by Oct 7*).
- **`billedDatesLedger.ts`**: `LedgerLienState.act` and `noted`. The deadline row's label is the act when the act is a step toward the row's own day (on the row's paper, or a notice that must go before the affidavit); the day, the count, `bold`, `dot` and `tone` are computed as before from the runway and the money, so a step with nothing urgent is a ring in regular weight. The act's row draws nothing under it but the act's own `under`; a move with no act keeps v2.7042's line. An act with no day prints no date and no count, amber, with the money's own ask under the rule (`moneyAsk`, the old tail of the verdict ladder as one function). A retainage notice due before the clock's day takes the line with its own day (row key `retainage`). `closed` is one row, **Lien gone** with the closed day and *Nd ago*, red and bold until the desk's chip says the window was written down, with no line under the rule. `filed` with an unserved copy on the desk's list is **Serve the affidavit by** the fifth day, red, with *Filed Nov 9* under it. Every lien row's `title` is `lienRowTip`'s.
- **`BilledDatesLedger.tsx`** + `index.css`: a lien line's act and its day are two units that wrap (the day drops under the act in the 208 px column, the count stays on the first line) and are never cut to dots; the act wears `.ledgerAct` inside a `.ledgerActRow` button — the underline alone in the words' own colour, dotted at rest, solid under the pointer, thicker under the keyboard's focus, no ring. `doorTitle` adds no tail to a lien line's tip.
- **`JobsStagesTab.tsx`**: hands the ledger the chip's `act` and whether a closed window is noted.
- **Tests**: `lienActWords.test.ts` (the table, the gates, and every words / under / tip string on every paper and move through `plainWordsFailures`), `billedDatesLedger.test.ts` (nine cases of the act on the line, the closed row, the tips), `lienArChip.test.ts` (the act on the chip), the render test (the press class, the wrap, the tip).
- **Guide** *read the Pipeline money view*; Glossary *Lien desk*.
