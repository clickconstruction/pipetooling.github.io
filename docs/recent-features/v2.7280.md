## Before

A job with no approved clock hours and no last day of work set by hand takes its lien month from its bill (v2.7224). Every surface flagged that month as a stand-in: the affidavit pane, the Lien window's affidavit tab, the help sheet's ⚠ *Work month.* row (which puts *Ask the attorney first* over the signer's check) and the notice record's first flag line. The only door was the last day of work, which moves every clock and can drop an approved paper off the desk. Jobs from the earlier system have no hours and never will, and on most of them the bill's month is right.

## What changed

- **Five columns on `jobs_ledger`**: `lien_month_checked_month` (`YYYY-MM`), `_at`, `_by`, `_by_name`, `lien_month_check_note`. Additive, nullable; the four lien readers are untouched.
- **[`src/lib/jobs/lienMonthCheck.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienMonthCheck.ts)**, a pure kernel: the check parsed off the job row (tolerant of a row read without the columns), its state (`none` for a month from hours or a hand day, `flag` for a stand-in nobody confirmed, `checked` only while the confirmed month is still the stand-in month), the question, the two presses, the ask, the quiet line, the grid's words, the help sheet's ✓ row and the notice record's line, the patch and the clear. [`lienMonthCheckIo.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienMonthCheckIo.ts) writes the job row.
- **The affidavit pane** ([`LienDeskAffidavitPane.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskAffidavitPane.tsx)): in the flag's place, *The month comes from the bill, not from recorded work. Is August the month the work was done?* with **Yes, August is right ›** and **Set the last day of work ›** (the desk's `onOpenCalendarLastWork`, wired through a new `onOpenLastWork` prop). The first opens a one-line ask, *Who said August is the month, and when?*, with **Confirm August** and *Saved on the job with your name and the day. It changes no date and no paper. It takes the flag off.* Confirmed, the quiet line *August checked · Robert · Nov 14 · note* with **Change ›**, and the month's grid words read *from the bill · checked Nov 14*. Readers who are not the office see the question with the old door words.
- **The desk's job read** (`useLienDeskData`) adds the columns to its first column list and falls back twice (without the check, then without the first work day).
- **The Lien window** ([`LienFilingTabs.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienFilingTabs.tsx)) reads the check off its job row and draws *✓ August checked · Robert · Nov 14 · note* where the ⚠ line was.
- **The help sheet** (`lienHelpChecks` → `monthCheck`): an `ok` *Work month.* row, *August 2026, checked by Robert on Nov 14: note*, in place of the `ask` row, carried through `lienAffidavitBatch.ts` and the desk's batch rows. **The notice record** (`lienNoticeRecord` → `monthCheck`) drops the stand-in flag line and names the check on a note line.
- **The dev seat's `plan_affidavit_draft`** reads the columns tolerantly and replies `month_checked` (month, by, at, note, says); `last_work_month_from` names the confirmation.
- The guide *file a lien and never miss its deadlines* says how; `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` names the columns.

## Not changed

- No clock moves and no paper changes: the four lien readers still date the job from its bill. A last day of work set later, or a bill that moves the month, puts the question back; the check stays on the row and is ignored until the month matches again.
