The sixth cut of `src/components/SupplyHousesTab.tsx`, step 6 of the *SupplyHousesTab extraction order* in `docs/MATERIALS_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md`. No source changes: one new test file.

**Why now.** The tab had no test of its own. The Materials render test mounts it and checks two headings. Steps 7 to 12 move the bill form, the data, the aging windows and the open house into their own files, so the guard is written first, against the tab as it stands, and is meant to pass unedited after each move.

**What it is.** `src/components/SupplyHousesTab.render.test.tsx`, 27 cases over a small stateful stand-in for the database: tables are arrays of made-up rows, `eq` / `in` / `is` filter them, a write changes them and is recorded, and a write can be refused with the database's words.

**What it covers.**

- **The book.** The bare mount Quickfill uses and the mount Materials uses. A bill paid in part counts for what is left on it, in the house's balance and in the total. The most owed house is first. A role the gate turns away draws nothing and does not throw.
- **A house and its bills.** Opened from the summary, closed again, and opened by a link that names it. The paid bills behind *Show paid invoices*, remembered on the device. A bill marked paid in place with one write. Record a payment's window. Create PO's draft and its door.
- **The bill form.** A new bill dated today and due on the house's pay day. A typed bill saved with one insert. A job picked in the form, with the flag an open account turns on. A save the database refuses, which says why at the top of the tab while the form stays open. Edit with one update. Delete after its confirm. The × that writes nothing.
- **The aging windows.** A cell's bills, the door from a bill's number to its Edit window over the cell, a current cell's bills under their job, Record a payment › from a cell, the move window's strip and worked list, the Needs You door, and a job whose account is marked not needed.
- **Payments, the flag and the phone.** A paid bill behind a typed payment opens that payment. Mark it on from a cell's window. The phone's list and a house's own screen.

**What it does not cover.** The fill from a PDF (the bill reader's own kernels are tested; its flow through the tab needs a read function, a signed file and a fetch), the Look window, the bank's lines, and the move modal's two writes.
