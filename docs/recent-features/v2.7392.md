# v2.7392 — Lien desk on a phone: Steps and Windows inside an open paper are a thumb tall (punch list #352, after the leftovers) (2026-11-15)

The one thing the read of v2.7378 on the live desk found. Taunya said to fix it on 2026-11-15. One rule in the stylesheet. No migration and no edge function change.

## What was wrong

v2.7378 gave every press under 40 px inside a phone's open paper an unseen pad to a thumb's square. Read on the live desk at 390 × 720, 16 of an open affidavit's 19 small presses took a tap inside their pad. Two were marked and gained nothing: **Steps** and **Windows**, 50 × 20 and 68 × 20. Their group (`ViewSwitch` in `src/components/jobs/LienTimelineStrip.tsx`) sets `overflow: hidden` so its two halves take its round corners, and a pad drawn outside a press is clipped with them. The pad's walk leaves alone a press that clips its own overflow, but it does not look at the parent.

## What changed

- `src/index.css`: inside `[data-lien-pane-phone]`, the buttons of `[data-lien-timeline-switch]` are 40 px tall, with 12 px of side padding and 13 px words (11 px before). At 40 px the walk no longer marks them, so nothing is padded and nothing is clipped.
- The rule selects under the attribute a pane carries only on a phone, so the switch on a computer, on the pane's title line and in the Lien window is as it was.

## Not changed

The pad's walk. A press whose parent clips is still marked and still gains nothing; the switch was the only such press an open affidavit holds. The switch in the Lien window on a phone is outside a pane and keeps its size.

## Test

`npx vitest run src/components/jobs/LienTimelineStrip.render.test.tsx`. The switch inside a phone's pane carries both hooks the rule selects by and still clips, and the stylesheet holds the rule and the pad's own.

Tried on the live desk before the merge, at 390 × 720 on an open affidavit, by adding the same rule to the page and taking it off after: **Steps** went from 50 × 20 to 60 × 40 and **Windows** from 68 × 20 to 82 × 40, at 13 px words; the two lost their pad marks on the walk's next pass (18 padded presses became 16); a tap on each landed on it; the pane grew 20 px longer and nothing slid sideways.
