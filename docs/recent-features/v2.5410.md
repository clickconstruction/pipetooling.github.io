Residential Messages, R0 · 2 of 3: the SQL (`to-dos/gc-mode/messages/RESIDENTIAL_BUILD_PLAN.md` → R0, on branch
spike/gc-mode), stacked on v2.5390's kernels. Migration `20261013180000_contacts_seven_kinds.sql`, note
`docs/migrations/20261013180000_contacts_seven_kinds.md`, bed `contacts-kinds`. No screen and no fetcher change: R0's
loaders (v2.5402) read the new columns once this is pushed and the types regenerate.

- `contacts_kind_known` holds eight values: the seven kinds (customer, trade, arch, lead, supply_house, candidate, sub)
  and the spine's `unknown`, a number nobody has named.
- Four record columns beside the spine's five: `prospect_id`, `supply_house_id`, `supply_house_contact_id` and
  `team_prospect_id`, each with its one-contact index. A sub is a `people` row whose kind is `sub`, through `person_id`.
- `contacts_one_record` counts all nine and lets a lead point at nothing. The new `contacts_kind_record` pairs each new
  column with its kind and holds a sub to the roster.
- `name_contact` follows `nameContact` in `contacts/kernel.ts`: it names the new kinds, names a number a lead with no
  record, points a lead at its Prospects row, re-points a lead who books to the customer, and refuses a record of
  another kind (`kindDoesNotFit`). The caller now holds UPDATE on the four columns.
- The spine's keep-thread trigger covers Prospects rows, hiring board rows, supply houses and reps, which their screens
  delete outright. A lead keeps being a lead; a candidate, a house and a rep whose house is going become a number that
  keeps the name; a rep whose house stays moves onto it.
- `contacts_subs_from_roster()` keeps a roster person's contact a sub while the roster says so and gives every sub a
  contact; `contacts_from_supply_houses()` gives each supply house's counter and each rep at one a contact. Both run
  once in the migration.
- `follow_up_count` leaves out supply houses and candidates, as `OFF_FOLLOW_UP` does. `contact_display_name` reads
  the new records.
- `write_down` takes from a lead, a supply house, a candidate and a sub the trade partner's promises (a quote, a start
  or papers), as `promiseChoices` in `messages/promises.ts` offers them, so the SQL never refuses what the screen
  shows (the lead's call). Whether a lead makes a customer's promises instead is a later change to both.
- A candidate's contact, numbers and lines read only to a dev and to someone on the hiring board: three restrictive
  read policies (helper 9's review, the lead's yes).
