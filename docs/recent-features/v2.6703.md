# v2.6703 — The Away section: a teammate who left with a note on lists above Archived, with the day back (punch list #256, PR 10) (2026-11-08)

The owner's first real sabbatical on 2026-11-08: a helper's employment ended through the desk (End date her last day on the clock, every row green), an Away note saved from the footer with a countdown three years out and *Tell me when the countdown ends* on. People → Users then listed her under **Archived (38)** → **Logins (26)** as *Archived 11/8/2026* with **Bring back…**, between thirty-seven people who left for good. The owner, looking at that list: *technically they can log in to see that screen, so maybe they should not be in the archive section on this page; maybe a sabbatical section with a time to return.* The date shown was the day the office pressed the button, not the day she left or the day she returns, and nothing separated the one coming back from the rest. No migration, nothing to deploy.

## What changed

- **Kernel `src/lib/people/awaySection.ts`** (tests in `awaySection.test.ts`): `splitAwayFromArchived` takes the Archived lists (the logins, the roster rows) and the `away_notes` rows and moves every archived login with a note row into an `AwayRow`, folding its roster row in by `account_user_id`, so one person is one row; the rest stay under Archived as before. Each row carries the day they left (the roster row's end date, else the day the account was archived in the app's zone: *Left Nov 3, 2026* / *Archived Nov 8, 2026*), the day back and its words (`awayBackWords`: *Back on Thursday, Nov 8, 2029 · 1,096 days*, *Back today*, *Due back Nov 5, 2026 · 3 days ago*, *No day back set*), `due` on and after the day, the alert switch, and the door the Archived lists offered: `bring_back` when the roster row ended, `restore` otherwise. Due rows sort first by the oldest day, then the soonest day back, then the rows with no day, ties by name. A note on a login that is not archived makes no row.
- **`PeopleUsersTab`**: `loadArchivedLogins` reads `away_notes` for the archived ids (RLS: the people who may archive read them, anyone else reads none and sees Archived unchanged); the split feeds the Archived lists and their count, so **Archived (37)** now means sealed and banned. Above the Archived block an **Away (n)** card, open, with *Their sign-in opens to the note you left. Bring back when they return.* and a table: Name with the email under it, Role, Left, Back (amber when due, with *· alert on* when the office asked to be told), and the presses **Edit the note…** (the desk's `PersonDeskAwayNoteModal`, rendered from the tab with the row's left date, reloading the lists on Save or Take the note down) and **Bring back…** (the desk with the window up, as before) or **Restore**. The card draws only when there is a row; the phone's ⋯ menu gains *Away (n)* beside *Archived (n)*.
- **`PersonDeskHeader`**: on an archived desk, for a viewer who may archive, the header reads the note row once per change key and says *· away* and an **Away** chip in place of *· archived* / **Archived** while a note is on.
- Guides: *leave a note for a teammate who is away* gains *Where they are listed*; *bring back a teammate who left* and *archive and restore user accounts* point at the Away section. `GLOSSARY.md` → *Away note*, `PEOPLE_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`, `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` and `docs/twins/APP_DIRECTORY.md` name the section.

## Decisions

- **Away, not Sabbatical.** The app already calls the feature the Away note and the desk chip reads *Away note on*; a sabbatical is one reason among several for the same state (a medical leave, a helper gone for the winter). The reason stays in the note's own words.
- **They leave Archived rather than appear twice.** Two lists with the same name would have kept the half-truth; the counts now say what each list is.
- **A note with no day back still lists under Away**, reading *No day back set*: the sign-in is open either way, and the row is where the office sees that nobody has said when.
- **The tab reads the notes itself** rather than through the Needs you hook, whose read is filtered to the alert and the day; the section needs every note on an archived login.
