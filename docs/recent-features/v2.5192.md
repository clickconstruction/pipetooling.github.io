The Board's loader switch to `gc_company_paper_states` (v2.5179, #5335, migration `20261010120000`, carved out of Building's door D1 at the lead's word). It was owed since Building's call 6, and v2.5179's note promised it as the board's next step.

- **The read** (`gcIo.ts`, `loadGcBoardRows`): a company's papers come from `gc_company_paper_states` for every company (no `p_company_ids`), in place of `person_contract_documents` with its own policies.
  - The function gives the office team (`gc_office_team()`) a company's agreement, W-9 and certificate rows, the eight columns `companyPapers` reads (`created_at` among them, its tiebreak and the agreement's signed-day fallback), and nothing else of the row.
  - The table's policies read for the pay roles only, so an estimator's board had no papers. Every company's master agreement, W-9 and insurance read as missing. Every awarded company owed a W-9 in Follow up (`paperAsks`), and with B2b-ii-b in Follow up's count and By people.
  - The other roles read the same rows as before: `companyPapers` reads only those three kinds, and the function's are the same rows.
- **Paged:** the read goes through `fetchAllRows` in the function's own order (company, created, id), with B2b-ii-b's four, so it never stops at PostgREST's 1,000 rows.
