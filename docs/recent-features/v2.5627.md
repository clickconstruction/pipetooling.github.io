# v2.5627 — The Pipeline asks before it texts the customer when a job moves (2026-10-18)

Punch list #123, PR 2 of 2 (PR 1 is v2.5626, the customer's door behind the row's speech bubble). The second shape the owner picked: the board's own move offers the text, so the common case needs no door at all. The flows already held the kernel for it, `flows/stageSends.ts` (R4 · 4, v2.5407: the moves a homeowner feels, with no caller since); this PR gives it its caller through **Tell the customer**. No migration; no edge function.

- **The moments** (`lib/tellCustomer/tellCustomer.ts`): `TellMoment` gains `working`, `done` and `billed` (`MOVE_MOMENTS`, `isMoveMoment`, `MOMENT_NAMES`), each on the residential step of the same id (`MOMENT_STEP`), with the app's own words in both languages (*{first}, this is {me} with Click Plumbing. The work on {job} is done. Your bill follows by email. Text me here with any question.*), a call script each, the act's row (*Texts Dana that the work is done from the company number*), the hint naming the step, and `TELL_WORDS.noEmail` by moment. Paid stays out: its step already carries the pay-by reminder, and the payment has its own email. **My way** (`myWay.ts`) narrows to its two moments (`MyWayMoment`); a board's move always asks, since the person is there.
- **The move** (`lib/tellCustomer/moveOffer.ts`): `moveMoment(from, to)` names the moment of a forward move to Working, Ready to Bill or Billed over `stageSends.ts`'s `FELT_MOVES` and the board's `MOVE_RANK`, null for a move back (a send-back), a move to Waiting or Paid, or a status it does not know; `moveAsks(sends, moment)` reads the residential step's switch (`appSendMode`), Offers or Sends both asking on the board.
- **The switch** (`flows/appSends.ts`, `FlowStepsTable.tsx`): the homeowner flow's Working, Done and Billed carry **The app and this move**, two ways, **Off** and **Asks** (`appSendSwitch` with the kind, its `move`; `MOVE_SEND_MODES`, `MOVE_MODE_WORDS`, `MOVE_MODE_HINT`, `isMoveStep`), stored as the reminder switch stores Offers; under it the text the move would ask about, the team's line on the step else the app's own, filled for the sample homeowner (`moveSampleWords`). The toast says what the press did (`appSendSetWords` with `move`). The owner and a dev press it; anyone else reads it.
- **The board** (`JobsStagesTab.tsx`): `askAfterMove` on the hook's `followMovedJob` notice, before *Follow cards I move* decides anything: the office team only (`jobBoxOffered`), the job's status before the move through a ref (the hook patches the list before it tells the tab), the switch read once on the first move (`supabaseAppSendsIo().load()`), then the sheet (`TellCustomerSheet`, moment, the job's number and name as its label, `userId`, `role`) over the board; a switch that cannot be read asks nothing. The sheet's own holds apply (no customer, no number, STOP, texts off), and its send lands in the customer's thread tied to the job as a booking's does. The Dashboard's billing moves and a tech's moves do not ask.
- **Tests**: `moveOffer.test.ts`; `tellCustomer.moves.test.ts` (the words plain in both languages, filled by the job alone; the acts, hints and no-email words; My way with no say); `appSends.test.ts` (the move switch, its words, the sample text; Paid and Booked unchanged); `TellCustomerSheet.render.test.tsx` (a Done move asks with the app's words, sends, and the receipt carries the Done script).
- **Docs**: the guide *set up the steps a person goes through* gains **Ask before a text when a job moves**; the Glossary's R4 · 4 line and Tell the customer entry, `MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md`'s surfaces table and kernels line, the Settings tabs map's Flows row and the page map's status line carry the ask.
