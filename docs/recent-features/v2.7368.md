## What changed

- **The Roadmap's Add task window** has the **Map** press in its Where row. *A map for this task* lists the painted maps the planner can open, and one press puts a map on the task. The bar's sentence then ends *It comes with a map.*
- **A roadmap task's card** has the same press under its places. Each pick saves itself, as a place does there. ✕ takes the map off and saves that too. A save the database refuses says why and puts the row back.
- **Someone who does not plan the roadmap** sees a map with no place on the task's card: its name, its line from above, **Directions to the start** and **How far am I?**.
- **The person who gets the task** already sees the map on Today once the stage unlocks. The card made from a roadmap task reads the roadmap task's map, as it reads its place (v2.7363).

## Picking only, on purpose

On the Roadmap a map is picked. It is not painted there and not opened there.

The roadmap's windows sit at a layer far above the rest of the app's, and the painted map's window sits under them. A press that opened it would open something nobody could see. So:

- *A map for this task* has no **Paint a new one** half on the Roadmap. An empty list names the two doors that paint: a job's address and the search.
- The set map's name is words there and not a press.
- The card of someone who does not plan has no **Open the map**.

Lifting the painted map's window over the roadmap's is its own small piece: the window, its share sheet, its confirms, the photo view and the text box each carry a layer. It is on the card as a later step. Until then a map for a roadmap task is painted from a job, the search or a task on the checklist, and opened from the task on Today.

## How it is built

- `TaskMapPress` takes `canPaint` and `TaskMapSet` takes `canOpen`. `TaskFormRows` sets both from one prop, `mapPicksOnly`, which the roadmap's Add task window hands in.
- `ChecklistTechTreeAddTaskModal` keeps the map and hands it to the roadmap as `onSave`'s seventh value. `addTaskToGroup` writes `painted_map_id` with the new row when the task has a place or a map.
- `RoadmapTaskPlaceSection` keeps the task's map beside its place. A change of place is saved with the map the task has after it (`taskMapAfterPlace`). Taking the place off clears the place's columns and then writes a by-itself map again, since those columns carry the map.
- `taskMapIo.ts` gains `loadRoadmapTaskMapAlone` and `saveRoadmapTaskMap`.

## Is this the best we can do

- **Two writes when a place leaves and a map stays.** The place's columns include the map, so clearing them clears it, and the map is written again straight after. One write would need a second save function for one rare press. If the second write fails the row goes back and says why.
- **An assignee of a roadmap task may change its row,** so may set its map. The database lets them point it only at a map they can already open (`task_painted_map_guard`).
