# v2.7224 — A job with no clock hours is dated from its bill, not from the day it was made (2026-11-13)

The owner, 2026-11-13, reading a printed batch of lien affidavits: *"the root of the issue is if a clock session has no hours it should not be from the job creation date but from the bill date"*. A job with no approved clock hours and no last day of work set by hand took its lien month from the day the job was made (v2.3747). Jobs brought in from the earlier system were made weeks after their work, so their notice day, their last day to file and the month their affidavit names all counted from the month they were typed in. Built on v2.7193 (the day set by hand counts).

## The rule, in one kernel

`lienStandInMonth({ bills, createdAt })` in `src/lib/jobs/lienBillMonths.ts`:

- Only a bill that went out and is still unpaid counts (`status = 'billed'`).
- A bill's day is the earlier of the day it was marked billed and the day it went to the customer, in the company calendar. A bill sent again later keeps its first day.
- Each such bill gives a month; the latest is the last month, which the last day to file counts from.
- A job with no dated unpaid bill keeps its creation month, so no job falls out of a reader.
- Approved hours and a day set by hand still come first (`lienClockMonths`).

## A bill is not a record of work

The owner, the same day: *"sometimes we bill a job before it is done"*. Measured read-only on 187 billed or paid jobs that have both approved hours and a dated bill, each stand-in's month against the job's last clocked month:

| Stand-in | Same month | Earlier than the work | Later than the work |
|---|---|---|---|
| The latest bill's month | 132 | 31 | 24 |
| The month the job was made | 117 | 70 | 0 |

So the bill is the better guess and still a guess. Every surface that shows a month from a bill says so, and the owner's call on a paper drawn on one was *"Only flag it"*: nothing is held, for the reason `lienAffidavitNoticeCheck.ts` gives (a hold has no override, and a paper stopped days before its last day to file would cost the lien).

## Every reader, and what changed

| Reader | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| The four SQL readers (`list_lien_notice_months`, `list_lien_affidavit_windows`, `list_gc_unpaid_months`, `list_jobs_owner_to_confirm`) | the creation month, `month_source = 'job_created'` | the bill months from `lien_bill_months()`, `month_source = 'bill'`; the creation month only with no dated unpaid bill ([migration](../migrations/20261113200000_lien_months_from_bill.md)) |
| The billed row and the Deadlines list (`buildLienPayRunway`) | the creation day | the latest sent, unpaid bill's day (`bills`, `datedFromBill`); a notice month per bill month |
| The Lien window (`lienWindowLastMonth`, `buildLienTimelineFromWindow`) | the creation month | the bill's month (`job.invoices`, `fromBill`, `standInMonths`) |
| The affidavit's months and period (`lienWindowSwornMonths`, `buildLienWindowAffidavitFields`) | read off the creation month | read off the same month as the window. No sworn sentence changed |
| The last-day line (`lienLastWorkDay`) | *from the job's creation* | *from the bill* (source `bill`); the desk reads each job's bills for it |
| The legal packet (`legalLastWorkBasis`) | sessions, the ledger's day, the creation day | the bill's day before the creation day |

The flags named `fromCreation`, `datedFromCreation` and `lastMonthFromCreation` since v2.3747 keep their names and now mean *a stand-in month* (`monthIsStandIn`); `fromBill`, `datedFromBill` and `lastMonthFromBill` beside each say which. A sweep to rename them was left out on purpose, since many lien PRs were open on the same files.

## The words

- Under a month, on the desk's rows, the GC notice and the strip's Last work node: *dated from the bill · no clock hours* (`lienStandInWords`), beside the older *dated from the job's creation · no clock hours*.
- The fourth gate on a notice: *Dated from the bill*.
- The row's hover: *The day counts from the bill, since no hours are on the clock.*
- On an affidavit's pane and on the Lien window's Lien affidavit tab: *The month comes from the bill, not from recorded work. Set the last day of work on the job if the work ran in another month.* (`lienStandInFlag`). A creation-dated paper gets the same line with *the day the job was made*.
- On the help sheet: an *Ask* check, **Work month.**, with that line, on a sub job and on a job contracted with the owner.
- On the notice record: that line leads the record's flags, so a paper whose notices all stand reads **Ask first**, never **On record**, while its month is a stand-in.

## What it does not fix

The app keeps no original bill date. A bill typed in when a job was brought over carries the day it was typed, so the jobs that prompted this read the same month after the change. What corrects them is the door that already exists: the last day of work set by hand, which always counts first. Whether a lien right stands on any job is counsel's question.

## Order

The client first, then `supabase db push`, which waits on the owner's named yes. Between the two the billed row and the Lien window count from the bill while the desk's lists keep the creation month.
