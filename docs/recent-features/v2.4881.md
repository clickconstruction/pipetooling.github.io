# v2.4881 — Lien desk: the leader approves on his phone in two taps (2026-10-07)

Client only. PR 2 of 3 from the mock-up https://claude.ai/artifact/LDQRcauAuVySHHZF7SYFvs (spots 2 and 3), after [v2.4872](v2.4872.md) made his phone buzz. Grace: *this is very wordy, we need to make it dead simple for him*, and on the four dots, *we need to be clear about what they are*. On a phone the leader's *What you're deciding* box and the Approve button sat under five sections, two screens down, and a row's four dots said nothing about what each one was.

## What changed

- **One screen for the leader on a phone** (`simpleApprove` = `isMobile && leader && pile === 'awaiting'`): a card first in the pane, `data-lien-approve-card` — *Lien notice · N waiting on you*, **$27,199** owed by Burd & Assoc., **Mail by Oct 15** · 8 days (`selected.earliestDeadline` / `daysLeft`; *window closed* past it), one muted line (*Sent to you Oct 6. All four gates clear.*, else the gate verdict), the four steps as tiles, and **Details ▾**. The pane root wears `data-lien-pane-simple="yes"` while Details is folded; one CSS rule hides every child not marked `data-lien-pane-keep` (the back button, the heading, the card). Details unfolds the pane the computer shows, the deciding box and the standing rule with it. On a computer nothing is drawn differently.
- **The footer** on that screen: **Approve ▸** full width (the same `approve` as Approve & next: it opens the next one waiting) and **Not yet**, which opens *Hold — they promised…*, *Hold — I'll call first*, *Back to the office* and Cancel; a hold reason lands on the existing hold-until row. The pay-offer switch shows only with Details open. `detailsOpen` and `notYetOpen` reset with the job.
- **The four dots are four named tiles on a phone** ([`LienStepRow`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskSteps.tsx) over `lienStepTiles` in [`lienNextUpSteps.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienNextUpSteps.ts)): Owner · Drafted · Approve · Mail (an affidavit: Property · Drafted · Approve · File). Done steps ticked green, the current one blue and saying whose it is — *you*, else *office* or *leader* (step 3 is the leader's; the rest the office's). The Do now rows draw it in place of `LienStepMark` on a phone, and a tap still opens the step card sheet. A computer keeps the dots and their hover card.
