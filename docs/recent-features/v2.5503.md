# v2.5503 — Messages: the app's own emails fold in a thread, with when they were opened and paid (2026-10-13)

Residential **R8 · 1, PR 2 of 2: the screen** for `messages/appLines.ts` (card 1 of
`to-dos/gc-mode/messages/customer-messages-integrations-mockups.html` v0.2 on spike/gc-mode). It was the one kernel of
the build with no caller. Its read is PR 1, `sent_copy_stamps` (migration `20261014140000`, #5677). This PR was held as
a draft until that was on prod. No migration, no edge function.

- **`messages/thread.ts`**: `threadRows`' step is lifted into `pushThreadRow` with its `ThreadRowState`, plus
  `byTimeOldestFirst`. That keeps one home for the day dividers and how a line's job reads. `threadRows` is unchanged
  in what it draws.
- **`messages/appLines.ts`**:
  - `foldedThreadRows(lines, nowIso, { open, at })` draws the thread with each run of the app's lines folded. A closed
    run is one row at its first line's day: its divider comes first when that day is new, and the next line's divider
    follows on from the run's last day. An open run draws its row, then its lines exactly as `threadRows` does.
  - A run holding the line a link opened the thread at is open, so the marked line shows.
  - `paidHowWord` reads the app's spellings of a payment as one word: "Check", "checkDeposit" and "Cheque" are a
    check, "ach" and "wire" a bank transfer, and "stripe" a card. An import's marker says no how.
- **`messages/sentStampsIo.ts`** (new) reads only `sent_copy_stamps`, 150 copies a call. `stampsOfRow` keeps a
  payment's day at noon in Chicago so the day holds whatever the clock. `copyIdsOf` names the copies behind a thread's
  `sent:` lines.
- **The screen**:
  - `ThreadWindow` draws `foldedThreadRows`, its open runs held per window.
  - `AppFoldLine` (in `ThreadLine.tsx`) is the folded row: *the app · 3 emails, Oct 8 to Oct 10 ▸ · Work scheduled ·
    Invoice 1312 · Paid, thank you*. Its words are the press, with `aria-expanded`.
  - `ThreadLine` takes `smallPrint`, drawn side by side under the words: *from office@… · opened Oct 10, 6:12 PM ·
    paid Oct 11 by card*.
  - `useSentLineFacts` reads the thread's copies once through `sentLineFacts`. A read that fails shows no small print,
    and the lines stand.
- **Guide**: `find-every-message-with-a-person-in-one-place` gains **See the emails the app sent on its own**, the one
  home for the fold. Its "What shows in a thread today" says the app's own emails are there too.
- **Tests**:
  - `appLines.test.ts`: the fold closed, open and opened by a link; a thread with no run. `paidHowWord` covers the
    app's spellings.
  - `sentStampsIo.test.ts`: the row read, a winter day, the copy ids, the 150 slice and a refusal.
  - `ThreadWindow.appLines.render.test.tsx`: the fold's words and titles; open and close in place; small print from,
    opened and paid; a copy with none; a link at a folded line; a failed read.
