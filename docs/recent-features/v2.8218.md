# v2.8218 — In kind: the screens that count late money leave a bill an arrangement pays out (punch list #301, the sweep, PR 1) (2026-11-21)

A job on an in kind deal carries `jobs_ledger.in_kind_arrangement_id`. Since v2.6834 the chase, the forecast and the Pipeline's Billed row read its bill as scheduled. The other readers of open bills never looked at the column, so the landlord's April bills sat in the Dashboard's receivables as 230 days late, in the Pipeline's 90+ chip, at the top of Who owes what and in the Bridge's doubtful pile. The card's sweep stopped at its map on 2026-11-12 with the screens not traced. This PR traces them and builds the ones the card settles. The senders are PR 2.

## The rule, and the one helper

The card's rule: a reader skips such a bill, counts it in `inKind: { count, dollars }` and says one line, because a total that shrinks with no word reads as money lost. A job is on a deal or off it, whole, so a payer's bills off the deal still read as money owed.

`supabase/functions/_shared/inKindApart.ts` is that rule once: `splitInKind(rows, onDeal, openOf)` gives the money rows, the rows a deal pays and their count, and `inKindApartWords(inKind, notWhat)` is the line (*$14,643 on 8 bills is scheduled in kind, not money owed*). It lives in the edge's shared folder so PR 2's emails read the same file. `src/lib/inKind/inKindApart.ts` re-exports it for the screens. `dashboardFinancials.ts` is copied for the edge, so it imports the shared file where it lives: a new file in the copy would change the bundle of every function that reads the copy's index.

## The readers that changed

- **The Dashboard's receivables card, and everything that reads its bucket.** `buildArBuckets` (`src/lib/dashboardFinancials.ts`) returns a fourth bucket, `inKind`, beside `ar`, `collections` and `uncollectible`. A deal's bill leaves `ar` and `collections`. Its item carries no date, so no reader of the bucket can age it. `loadDashboardFinancials` selects the column and returns `arInKind`. The card says the line under its total, the drill-down lists the bills under *Scheduled in kind — not money owed*, the Customers view says the line under its list with a door to In kind, and the footer says what its total leaves out (`arFooterTotalLabel`). A deal's bill that is paid off and not marked Paid stays a Mark Paid to-do. One the office marked Uncollectible stays there.
- **The Bridge**, by construction: the Collect pile, the receipts and the doubtful list, the Money owed panel's aging and the two balances all read `fin.ar`. The page names the bucket beside Collections as *Scheduled in kind, not counted*, the Money owed panel gains a source row with a door to In kind (`buildOwedPanel`, `arInKind`), and the Monday email ends its left-out line with the sentence (`buildWeekMailFromReads`).
- **The Pipeline's 30+ and 90+ chips**, and every age on the board. `stageRowBilledAgeReference` (`src/lib/jobs/invoiceBilling.ts`) is the one clock the chips, the chip filter, the money card's age bar, *Chase the 90+ tail* and Total by name read. It answers null for a job on a deal. A new chip, *In kind · 8 · $14.6k*, counts those rows (`buildBilledInKindBucket`) and filters the list to them (`BilledListFilter` gains `in_kind`).
- **Who owes what, and Quickfill's Billed Awaiting Payment.** `buildBilledByCustomerBreakdown` leaves a deal's open bill under no customer. `billedInKindLine` is the line under the total, on both.
- **Pay speeds, By customer.** `buildPaySpeedsBreakdown` leaves the bill out of the payer's open money and `openBillsForCustomers` out of the expansion, as `buildMoneyWaiting` in the same window has since v2.6931.
- **The lean read.** `LEAN_STATS_JOB_COLUMNS` (`stagesHeaderStats.ts`) did not select the column. So the cached chips, Quickfill's station and Call mode's count ran `jobIsScheduledInKind` on rows that could never answer yes, the chase queue's own skip included. It selects it now.

## Decisions taken with the build

- **A bucket, not a filter in bill truth.** `computeBillTruth` is shared with the customer portal and the GC statement, whose words about such a bill are the owner's call. The split sits one level up, in `buildArBuckets`, where only staff screens read it.
- **No date on a deal's AR item.** The drill-down, the card's bars, the Bridge's pile and the owed panel each age an item by its date. Taking the date off is the one change that holds for all of them, the way a settled row already carries none.
- **The clock answers null.** One function already was every age on the board. A skip in each reader would have left the next one to forget.
- **The Pipeline strip's Billed total is unchanged.** The section still lists those rows, each reading *Scheduled in kind*. Its chips are the part that called them late.
