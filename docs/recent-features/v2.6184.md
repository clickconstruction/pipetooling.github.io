# v2.6184 — Legal desk: the Contract with cell reads the signer's name and the payer's city cleanly (2026-10-30)

Punch list #219's live look. On a real subcontract account the strip's Contract with cell read *subcontract signed by Sam Owner electronically · Ste B Amarillo, TX*: the agreement's printed name had been typed with its signing mode after it, and the payer's address had its suite before the city with no comma, so `cityOfAddress` returned *Ste B Amarillo*. Both are the data as filed, and both are cheap for the kernel to read past.

## What changed

- **`signerDisplayName`** (`src/lib/legal/legalParties.ts`): a printed name ending in a signing mode (*electronically*, *on paper*, *by hand*, *in person*, *typed*, *drawn*) is the name alone; `contractStateWords` reads every signer through it. The coverage, the Paper tab and the contract chips are unchanged.
- **`cityStateWords`**: a suite or unit token typed before the city (*Ste B*, *Suite 4*, *Unit 2*, *Apt 3*, *Bldg C*, *#12*) is dropped before the city is read.
- **The #219 card** reads built and merged, with the owner's look as the one thing left before it is retired.

## Verify

`legalParties.test.ts` holds both: a signer typed as *Sam Owner electronically* reads *signed by Sam Owner*; an address with *Ste B Lubbock* reads *Lubbock, TX*.
