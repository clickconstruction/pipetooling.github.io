# v2.6525 — + Hire finds a teammate who left and offers Bring back (punch list #256, PR 4) (2026-11-04)

Punch list #256's fourth PR (`to-dos/bring-back-a-teammate/`). The walk of 2026-11-04 showed + Hire's name check looking at active rows only, so once both halves of a leaver were archived a second person could be made, and the pay row, keyed by name, landed on the old one. PR 3 (v2.6523) is the window this PR opens.

## What changed

- **`HirePersonModal`** reads the archived people once (`listArchivedPeople`, `hireWrites.ts`: the archived accounts with their roster rows, the archived roster rows, samples, twins and the walk tester left out) and runs the kernel's `findArchivedMatch` on the name and the email as they are typed. A match draws the amber line (`archivedMatchWords`: *Corinne Vela left on Nov 3, 2026. Their hours, logbook and HR file are on that account.*) with **Bring back Corinne**, which opens their desk with the Bring back window up (`PersonDeskOpenArgs.flow`) and closes the form, and **This is someone else**, which lets the form go on. The Hire press waits while the match stands. On the press, after the active-name check, an exact archived name is refused too (*… is archived under this exact name — pay is keyed by name …*), "someone else" or not.
- **`mergeArchivedRows`** (`bringBack.ts`): one entry a person from the two tables — an account with its row (the row's end date beside the account's email), an account alone, a row alone; a roster row still active beside an archived account lends its end date, since End employment before v2.6523 could leave it so. `ArchivedPerson` carries `userId` and `personId` for the desk.
- **Guides**: `hire-someone` gains *Someone who worked here before*; `bring-back-a-teammate` gains *Hire by mistake*.
