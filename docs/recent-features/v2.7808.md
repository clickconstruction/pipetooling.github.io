# v2.7808 — The pace card: My finish date (2026-11-18)

Punch list #396, PR 3b of the train. Client only. It saves to `job_finish_calls`, the table of v2.7807 (`20261118110000_job_finish_calls.sql`), so it merges after that migration is pushed.

## Why

The owner's example of what the pace card should let a superintendent do: report when he thinks the job will finish. The card read the office's planned finish and the day this pace lands on. Neither is his own word.

## What landed

- **The press.** **My finish date** sits on the pace card beside **Ask a master**.
- **The window** is `FinishDateSheet`. Quick picks: the working day before the day in force, that day, and the working day after, with the plan's day marked *the plan*. **Another day** opens a date box that takes no day already gone. One line takes what is in the way. **Save my date** writes one row in his own name.
- **His day on the card.** The rail's foot gains a row, **You said**, with the day and what is in the way, between **Finish** and **At this pace**. A job with no rail says it in words.
- **The card asks.** Two days before the day in force, or once it has passed, a box on top of the card reads *Still finishing Fri, Nov 20?* with **Yes** and **Pick a day**. **Yes** says that day again today, in one tap. It also asks when the job is behind and he has never said a day. It asks once a day at most: a saying made today is the day's answer. Once the day has passed the box reads that it has passed and offers **Pick a day** alone.
- **The day in force** is his own last word when he has one, else the plan.

`lib/jobMode/finishCall.ts` is the picks, the rule for the question and every word, pure and tested. `lib/jobMode/finishCallIo.ts` is the read and the one write.

## What it does not do

- It moves neither the planned finish nor the score. No function reads the table.
- It does not change or delete a saying. A new day is a new row, so the office can see what he said and when.
- The office does not see his date on the Projects board yet. That is the next small PR.

## If the read fails

His day is an addition to a card that is whole. A read that fails draws no error and takes nothing away: the rail, the hours and the press stay. A save that fails says its reason in the window and keeps what he typed.
