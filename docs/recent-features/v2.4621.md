# v2.4621 — Send the run: read any copy before it prints (2026-10-06)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. The owner's ask on the run window (2026-10-05): *I would like to be able to click to see a preview of each one*, and the packet paragraph *behind a (?) icon*, its dashes gone.

## What changed

- **One door per copy row.** The `Copy for: owner of record` cell of every copy row in `LienDeskRunModal` ends with a quiet **Preview ›** text button. Envelope rows are addresses and get none.
- **An overlay over the run, not a window.** `LienRunPreviewOverlay` (new, modelled on `GcNoticePreviewModal`) draws the copy's pages on light paper over the run (z 795; the run is 790). ‹ › and the arrow keys walk every copy in packet order, envelope by envelope; Esc closes only the preview, in the capture phase, so the run underneath keeps its tracking numbers, ticks and scroll. Nothing to allow: the desk's own notice preview still opens a browser window, which Safari holds.
- **The preview is the packet.** The per-copy loop body of `runPacketHtml` moved into `runCopyPages(n, r, invoiceSectionsByJob?, payPagesByJob?)` in `lib/jobs/lienDeskRun.ts`: the cover page when it is the owner's copy and the draft carries a letter or a note, the form, the pay codes page, then the unpaid invoices, each with a label (*Cover letter · § 53.056 notice · copy for owner of record · Pay codes · Unpaid invoice 1 of 2*). `runPacketHtml` stacks those same pages, so the preview can never differ from the paper. `stepRunPreview` holds the index inside the packet.
- **What the packet is, behind a ?** The explainer paragraph under the title is gone from first sight: a 20 px round **?** beside the title (`aria-label="What the packet is"`, `aria-expanded`) toggles it. Its em dashes became a colon and semicolons, and it ends by naming the Preview link.

## Tests
