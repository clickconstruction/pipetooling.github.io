# v2.4337 — Release of Lien: click a folded step to look at it again (2026-10-01)

No migration, no edge function. Grace, on job 650 after Malachi signed: "click on 1-2-3-4-5 and be able to jump back to it after it has been filed before I submit." The plan offered *look* (free) and *change* (costs a signature: cancel the request, or void and re-sign); she chose click to look, and declined making the GC's room hold signed waivers as "on its way" until sent.

## What changed

- **Kernel** ([`lienReleaseSteps.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienReleaseSteps.ts)): `releaseStepPagePart(n)` — the part of the page a step filled in (1 and 3 the amount, 2 the title, 4 the project line, 5 the signature, 6 nothing); `releaseStepLookNote(rowStatus, sent)` — the read-only line: *Read only while it waits for his signature. To change it, click Cancel request in step 5 first.* / *Read only. It is signed. To change it, click Void this waiver at the bottom and make a new one. He signs it again.* / the signed-and-sent variant.
- **The step card** ([`LienReleaseStepRow`](../../src/components/jobs/LienReleaseStepRow.tsx)): a folded step's title is a button (`.lienStep-foldBtn`, stretched over the card, `aria-expanded`, the summary as its description) with a quiet *Look ›*; opened, the card shows its content (already read-only — a requested or signed waiver is not editable) under the look note, with a dashed edge and a **Fold** button. The rail's number (`onDot`) opens a folded step, or brings an open one into view. `LienWaiverSignedLook` is step 5 opened after signing: *Signed by X, title for company* and the audit sentence (the signing controls belong to an unsigned waiver).
- **The window** ([`LienReleaseModal`](../../src/components/jobs/LienReleaseModal.tsx)): `looking` (the steps opened) and `lookAt` (the last one, which the page marks); both clear when the window opens or the waiver's state changes (asked, signed, cancelled). Opening or folding moves the keyboard to the step's Fold button or back to its own button, after React draws the card (a `useEffect` on `stepToShow` — `requestAnimationFrame` ran late in a hidden pane). The page: `data-look-part` on `.lienRelease-preview` — the amount and the project line through `MarkedWaiverAmount`, the title and the signature through CSS — and while an earlier step is opened, the current step's *5 · He signs here* tag steps aside (CSS over the foot's inline outline), so only one part is marked. The foot-preview line itself is untouched (the waiver-ink branch edits it too).
- Guide *send a GC our lien waiver*: *Look back at a folded step* under *Follow the six steps*. GLOSSARY amended.
