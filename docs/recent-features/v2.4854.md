# v2.4854 — Lien desk: an emptied pile is not a blank list (2026-10-07)

Client only. Grace, 2026-10-07, with a screenshot of the live desk: *My master just approved something. Why is this blank? Should it be blank?* The desk read *Notices · 24*, *Send the run · 9*, and the list said only **Nothing in this pile.** The approval had worked (the run went 8 → 9). He had come in through a door that narrows the Notices list to one pile — the Dashboard's *waiting on your approval* card (`liendeskPile=awaiting`) or a Do now row's Approve — and after he approved the last one the pile was empty. The only way out, *show every pile* (v2.4672), sits on a pile's title, and an empty pile draws no title; nothing said the list was narrowed.

## What changed

- **A narrowed pile that empties under an act widens by itself.** `pileHadRowsRef` in `LienDeskModal` remembers which pile had rows; when `visibleAll` goes to zero on that pile with the data read, `setPile(null)`. The find box does not count (`visibleAll` is the list before the find), so a search with no hits never widens the pile. A pile a door opens empty stays narrowed, so the reader learns it was empty.
- **An empty, narrowed pile says so and offers the door.** `lienEmptyPileWords(pile, onOtherPiles)` in [`lienDesk.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDesk.ts): the pile in the words of what is no longer there (*Nothing is waiting for approval.*, *Nothing is left to draft.*, …) and *N notices are on the other piles.* The list draws it on `data-lien-desk-empty-pile=<pile>` with **Show every pile ›** (`data-lien-pile-all`, the same door the title carries). The sentence with no pile is unchanged.
- The Affidavits and Retainage lists still read *Nothing in this pile.* on an empty narrowed pile; no door opens them narrowed.

## Tests

`lienDesk.test.ts` (+1, the words), `LienDeskModal.render.test.tsx` (+2: a door onto an empty pile shows the words, the count and the door, and the door widens; a pile that empties on a re-read after the approval widens by itself).

## Not done

- Affidavit and Retainage empty piles get no words of their own; add them when a door narrows those lists.
- The Dashboard card could clear its narrowing itself on the way out; the desk doing it covers every door.
