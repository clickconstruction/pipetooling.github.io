# v2.8506 — Papers before a job opens: the GC downloads a sample of the job's paper (2026-11-24)

Punch list #389, step 5 of eight, its second part: **the sample.** It follows v2.8503, which put the read-only block on the GC's page. One new edge function, `open-job-paper-sample`. No migration.

**After the merge:** deploy `open-job-paper-sample`, then `dev-mcp` (its catalog names the function). Until then the press answers a 404 from Supabase.

## What a GC gets

On the block *How we bind everyone we bring onto this job*, a press: **Download a sample**. It opens, in a tab of its own, the paper for that job's settings:

- **The words a signer reads.** They come from the database's own assembly for the job's picks, the one the signing page reads. A sample and a signed copy cannot disagree.
- **Made-up names.** Under the title: *The job: Job J0000, a sample* and *Signer: Jordan Sample, Sample Sub LLC*. These are the Contract Book's own sample words, and a test holds the two in step.
- **SAMPLE across each page**, and at the foot of each page with its number.
- **A stamp** at the end of the words: *SAMPLE, not for signature.*, the level, and the revision of the settings on this job.
- **No signature block.** It is not for signing.

Under the press: *The sample is this job's paper with made-up names. Have your own attorney read it.*

## Who can open it

Only the GC on that job, through their own page. The function takes the page's token and the paper's id, and checks, in `sampleRefusal`:

- the link is live, and shows the jobs its customer is the GC on,
- the paper is one the job requires now, and is built from settings,
- the job's GC is the link's customer.

Every other case answers the same words, *This sample is not available.*, so the door says nothing about a paper that is not theirs. A paper the database will not put together answers that it is not ready. The sample statement's made-up tokens get no press and no file.

## Choices worth knowing

- **The job's typed blanks are in the sample.** The assembly fills a blank from what the office typed for the job, and takes no other name. A sample is drawn for the GC on that job, who reads the same words on a signed copy. The job's number, its address and every person are made up or left out.
- **Drawn each time, kept nowhere.** The function answers the PDF itself. Nothing is stored, and nobody is told that it was opened.
- **English only**, as the block is.
- **The level on the stamp** is read as the page reads it. If that read fails the stamp says *A mix of its own* and the words are still the paper's.

## What is under it

- **`_shared/jobPaperSamplePdf.ts`** draws it on pdf-lib, which is passed in, so the function and the tests share the file. It imports nothing. It also holds the gate and the sample's words.
- **`open-job-paper-sample/index.ts`**, with `verify_jwt = false` in `config.toml`, and its line in the dev seat's catalog.
- **`PortalJobPapers.tsx`** takes the page's token and draws the press, 44 px tall.
