# v2.3241 — The bid flow folds into the title row on the five workflow tabs (2026-09-10)

Client only — no migration. Grace, on the Pricing tab: "I also don't love how much space this takes up at the top of every bid — how could I make it more compact vertically?" Before/after mockup: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/943a08c4-0e92-4e20-85a1-43740b9d6efc.

## What changed

- **`BidFlowStrip` gains an `inline` variant** ([`BidFlowStrip.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/BidFlowStrip.tsx)): a pill button — the ten hairline ticks in the row's own colors (each tick keeps its step tooltip), `bidFlowSummary` (*7 of 10 done · decided*), the review stamp when there is one, a chevron — with `aria-expanded` and an `onToggleExpanded`. While the bid is unreviewed and the Review door is open, a small **Mark reviewed** button sits beside the pill (the full strip's header button, relocated). The `full` variant gains `hideHeader` for the unfolded case: the "Where this bid is" header row and the Review button are omitted (the pill already carries both), and the card draws dashed on the subtle background so it reads as the pill's drawer.
- **The fold is per device** ([`useBidFlowFold.ts`](../../src/hooks/useBidFlowFold.ts), `pt.bidFlow.inline.expanded`, folded by default) — the same pattern as the Workbench's Solver fold.
- **The five workflow tabs** (Counts, Takeoffs, Labor, Pricing, Cover Letter) render the pill inside the title group right after `BidWorkflowTabTitleWithPreview` (Counts has two title groups, phone and desktop; both get it) and render the full strip in its former place above the header only while unfolded. The v2.3216 landing behavior is untouched: every door still calls `onOpenBidFlowDoor` with its step.
- **Not changed:** the Bid Board's expanded row keeps the full strip with its header; the hairline under the row's jump icons; the `compact` dots; the flow kernel.
