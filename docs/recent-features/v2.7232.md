# v2.7232 — The Bridge: each job with no percent says its address (2026-11-13)

Client only. No migration, no edge function, and no kernel that is copied to the edge functions changed. A follow-up to v2.7221.

The owner, the same evening: "I also need the address. I forgot about the address. The address is actually the most important part", and then "I don't need the state and zip code in the address though." A job's number and name do not say where it is, and the place is how he knows the job.

## What changed

- **Street and city** (`pctFacts.ts`, `streetAndCity`): a stored address with the country, the zip and the state taken off its end, in that order, over `cleanStoredAddress`. A state after a comma always goes. A state after a space goes only when a zip stood behind it, so a street that ends in a quarter of town keeps it. An address that is nothing but a state or a zip comes back as stored.
- **The read** (`pctFactsIo.ts`): one more read beside the other four, `jobs_ledger.job_address` for the listed jobs only. The page's own jobs read was left alone, since the Monday email shares it.
- **The line** (`callDive.ts`, `BridgeMoveTable.tsx`): a line of the list may carry a line under its name (`sub`), and a job with no percent carries its street and city there. At a desk it is one line, cut with its whole text on hover, and the row is no taller. On a phone it sits under the name and may wrap. A job with no address, or one whose facts are not read yet, has no such line.
- **The window** (`BridgePctRun.tsx`): **Address** is the first of the facts above the percent control, in bold. A job with no address says *The job has no address yet.*

## Tests

- `pctFacts.test.ts` (15): the street and city from eleven shapes of address, and the address leading the window's rows.
- `callDive.test.ts` (13), `BridgeMoveTable.render.test.tsx` (11) and `BridgePctRun.render.test.tsx` (8): the line under the name at a desk and on a phone, none for a job with no address, and the address leading the window.

## Verify

Walked on the branch's dev server as a dev over production rows, in headless Chrome, at 1400 wide light and dark and at 375 wide, with every write refused in the browser. Every listed job drew its street and city under its name with no state and no zip, the rows at a desk kept their height, the page did not scroll sideways at 375, and the window led with the same address.
