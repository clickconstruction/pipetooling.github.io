# v2.8284 — Materials on site: loading a box takes fewer presses (2026-11-22)

Phase two of the Materials phone work, on top of v2.8282. No migration.

## What the walk of the live site found

The load took too many presses. The Add a part sheet opened empty and waited for typing. It ignored the box's own *What is in it* words, so a person typed the part twice. **Use as typed** sat under the results, behind the keyboard. The count started at 1 with plus and minus, though real loads are 27, 50 and 94. The sheet closed after every part. Every load was followed by a count of the same number seconds later. Making a box and putting its first part in were two jobs, which is why two new boxes sat with a line and no parts. On the office page at phone width a box card was a quarter of the screen, its table was 425 px wide in a 341 px card, and **Correct…** sat off the screen.

## What changed

- **A new box takes its first part in the same press.** Add a container, on the Tally's sheet and the desk's form, has **How many** beside **What is in it**. Empty is the box alone. Filled, the press reads *Add G101 with 40 Hangers*, makes the box and writes the part through the rows Add a part writes. The words are matched to a name the job already holds, case and spacing aside (`pickForWords`, `firstPartOf`). When the box lands and the part does not, the toast says so with the reason and the box is kept (`boxKeptPartHeldWords`).
- **The sheet opens with choices** (`addPartChoices`): **This box says**, the box's own line as one press, then **Already on this job**, the job's first eight names as `onHandNames` gives them. The data carries no last-touched day, so the order is most pieces first. With something typed, **Use “x” as typed** is the first row under the field and is left out when a listed name is those words. The Take flow keeps its own words and its own opening.
- **Quantity is fast and the sheet can stay.** The number field takes the focus with its number selected and a numeric keypad. The chips **+10**, **+25** and **+100** add to it (`chipAdds`). **Add 40 and another part** writes the part, goes back to the picker and says *40 Hangers added to G101.* in the sheet. The main press closes as before.
- **A part put into an empty line is counted at that moment** (the owner's call; `addIsItsCount`, `addPartRows`). When the box held none of that part, the add also writes the line's count at the number added: the `counted` row Count this box writes, a millisecond after the delivery, with the note *counted as added*. When the line already held some, no count is written. It holds for Add a part, for *and another part* and for a new box's first part, on the Tally and on the desk.
- **On a phone the desk lists one row a box** (`tally/OnSiteBoxRow.tsx`, the Tally's own row moved to its own file, over `lib/materials/boxRow.ts`). A row opens the Tally's box screen (`boxScreenFromMaterials`, `from=materials`), and that screen's back press returns to the desk (`boxBack`, *Back to Materials*). A person who came from the Tally still goes back to its list, since the mark leaves the address when the box closes. A box with no part is pressed to **Add a part** in place. A desktop keeps the cards.
- **An empty box reads *Empty*** in the muted colour (`boxRowState`), on the Tally's list too, where it read an amber *0 parts · never counted*.
- **The held-photo notice folds to one line on a phone** (`BoxPhotoRefileStrip`'s `fold`), on the Tally and the desk. A press opens it.

## Built differently

- A chip pressed on the opening 1 gives its own number, so **+25** twice is 50 and not 51. After a number is typed or a chip is pressed, a chip adds.
- The job's names are listed as given, most pieces first, since no last-touched day is at hand.
