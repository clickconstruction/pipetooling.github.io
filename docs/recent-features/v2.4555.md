# v2.4555 — Submittals: the resubmit button says it starts a draft (2026-10-05)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Words only: nothing about what the button does changed. The owner, on step 7 of the SpaceX bid: "I don't love this button, it doesn't tell the user what creating Rev 2 does? Does it send to someone? if so the user should see a draft first."

It sends nothing. A press opens a question, and on *yes* makes Rev 2 as a **draft**; the GC sees it only when Share is pressed on that draft. The screen did not say so: the button read *Rev 2 from the 4 rows sent back and the 10 with no answer*, and its line read *Fix the rows, then share again. The GC's link shows the new version.*

## What changed

- **The button**: *Start a Rev 2 draft…* on step 7 and on the strip's Next line (`resubmitLabel`). It names what the press does, not the rows.
- **The line beside it** (`resubmitWhat`): *The 4 rows sent back and the 10 with no answer go on it. Nothing is sent. The GC sees Rev 2 only after you press Share. New revision carries every row instead.* With no rows sent back: *New revision starts a Rev 2 draft with every row. Nothing is sent. …*
- **The question before the draft is made** (`resubmitConfirm`): titled *Start a Rev 2 draft*; after where each kind of row goes it ends *Rev 2 starts as a draft. Nothing is sent. The GC sees Rev 2 only after you press Share.*; its button is *Start the draft with 14 rows* (was *Build Rev 2 with 14 rows*). The *New revision* question ends the same way.
- **The strip's Next line**: *structura sent 4 rows back. 10 rows still have no answer. Start a Rev 2 draft to fix what was sent back. The rows with no answer go on it too. Nothing is sent until you share.* It used to say *Fix what was sent back. Then tap the green button.* — the wrong order: the fixing happens on the draft.
- Step 7's sentence, the walkthrough's Resubmit stop, both hover lines and the Dashboard's *rows sent back, no resubmit* line say *draft* too.

## Left as it was

The *New revision* button keeps its name; its line and hover line now say it starts a draft and sends nothing.
