# v2.6407 — The Development page’s Money look gains a Scoreboard (2026-11-02)

Punch list #250, PR 1 of 4. The owner saw the game Squad's end-of-match scoreboard and asked for its shape on the Development page: one grid where the same columns mean the same thing at every level. The card and the mock-up (`to-dos/development-scoreboard/`, PR #6871) weighed four ways and picked the board by job with the contributors under each; the owner took draft 2 and all seven defaults the same evening. This PR is the board by job.

- **Where**: the Money card's head gains two view chips, *Where the money is* and *Scoreboard*; the first stays the default and the device remembers the one you were in (`readMoneyView` / `writeMoneyView` in `lib/developments/devicePrefs.ts`). The sibling spike's cost timeline is meant to be the third chip when it lands.
- **The grid** (`src/components/developments/DevelopmentScoreboard.tsx` over `src/lib/developments/scoreboard.ts`): one header, the flag block (the development's name, the customer, the counts, what is left to collect, the people on it) over the Job column and the nine tiles with the site's totals as the column heads, Hours · Days (green, effort) · Labor · Subs · Parts · Overhead (red, what it cost) · Earned · Cost · Profit (gold, the money); a row per job with the Pipeline's status dot and the page's `JobChip`, folded to Earned · Cost · Profit and opened to the nine; the jobs with no earned dollar and no cost folded under *Nothing on them yet · N jobs · Show them*; the foot the site again; a tile press sorts, the same tile again turns the order, Earned biggest first by default. On a phone the rows are cards and an open card shows the nine as a labelled grid.
- **The figures**: Earned is Job Summary's rule, lifted into `src/lib/jobs/jobEarned.ts` and called from `enrichJobSummaryRows` so the two surfaces earn by one kernel (the contract at 100%, the percent's share while working, half with no percent and the ≈ flag); a paid job is done as the Money look reads it, and paid bills that cover the contract finish a job too. Subs and Parts are the Jobs & bids table's own read (`useSiteCosts`). Hours, Days and Labor are a new batched read of the clock for the site's jobs (`lib/developments/fetchSiteLabor.ts` → `siteLabor.ts`, in parity with `utils/teamLabor.ts`: salaried 8 h on a weekday, hourly the recorded hours, a crew day split by assignment percent), with days counted once each at every level and the lead's days kept for PR 2. Overhead is the day share from People → Overhead under the app-wide allocation, over the pool's first day to today, through the same session cache the Job window's Burn uses (`hooks/useSiteOverhead.ts`); it is the page's heaviest read, so it starts only when the view is open and its cells read *…* until it lands. Cost = Labor + Subs + Parts + Overhead, Profit = Earned − Cost; a read that fails leaves its cells saying *not read*, never a false zero.
- **Flags**: *no price* on a job that costs with no price, *materials only* on a pass-through job (no hours, parts alone), *≈ 50% assumed* with no percent. The Profit tile says the blended margin and, when a materials-only job is set aside, the margin on work alone (the owner's call 7).
- **Who sees what** (call 2): dev and controller all nine; a master or an assistant Hours · Days · Earned, no wage and no cost (`scoreboardColumnsFor`); an estimator keeps the one line. `docs/ACCESS_CONTROL.md` → Development page.
- **Guide**: `see-everything-on-a-development` gains *Read the scoreboard*.

Not in this PR: the rows under an open job (the people, subs, supply houses, the card and the overhead day share, each filling Cost with a share bar, the gold row) are PR 2; the headline under *Right now* is PR 3; the cuts by part of the site and by person and the time window are PR 4. The page seam is a few lines at the Money card; the PR was held unarmed until the Gray Fox stack (#6846–#6849) and the photos PRs (#6839, #6840) were on main, with main merged in after each so their lines and these sit together (the page's reads, its imports and the Glossary and PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION sentences); the Scoreboard reads the page's `counted` members, as the Money look does since v2.6378, so a robot's shadow bid stays out of its counts. Client only, no migration.
