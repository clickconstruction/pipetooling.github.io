# v2.8266 — In kind: a customer's profile and Invoices tab say a bill an arrangement pays is scheduled (punch list #301, the screens traced, PR 1) (2026-11-21)

The sweep of v2.8218 and v2.8219 left five screens that still aged a bill an in kind deal pays. Each needed more than a skip. This PR builds the two on the customer: the profile window and the customer page's Invoices tab. The window's kernel is the one the dev seat reads, so the dev seat's two reads are here too.

## What changed

- **The customer's profile window** (`CustomerProfileModal`). `profileJobRowMoney` (`supabase/functions/_shared/customerProfileStats.ts`) gives a job on a deal `scheduledInKind: true` and takes its age, its bill day and its *no date* warning away. The open money stays, since the open balance counts it. The row's words moved out of the component into `profileJobRowMeta` (`src/lib/customers/profileJobRowWords.ts`): *billed · scheduled in kind*, in the muted grey, where it read *billed Apr 1 · 215d* in red with its dollars bold. The chip under **Open balance** takes `openBalanceInKindChip`, the words the customer's page has used since v2.6829.
- **The Invoices tab** (`CustomerDetail`). `fetchCustomerInvoices` selects `in_kind_arrangement_id` on the customer's jobs, and `buildCustomerInvoiceRows` marks an open bill on such a job `scheduledInKind` with no `agingDays`. The chip's tail is `customerInvoiceChipTail`: *· in kind*, or *· 45d* for a bill out 30 days or more.
- **The dev seat** (`_shared/devMcpComposites.ts`). `get_job`'s select and `get_customer`'s job select carry the column, and `get_job` hands it to `profileJobRowMoney`. `get_customer`'s `stats.scheduledInKind` read zero before, and its aging counted the deal's bills at 90 days.

## Decisions taken with the build

- **The kernel, not the component.** The window could have hidden the age by itself. Then `get_job` would still report it, and the next reader of `profileJobRowMoney` would age the bill again.
- **The open money stays on the row and in the sort.** A job on a deal still owes what is open, and the list still puts the largest open balance first. Only the age is gone.
- **Only an open bill is marked.** A paid bill or a draft on a deal's job reads as any other.
- **No dev-mcp version change.** The verbs and their arguments are the same, and two open PRs hold the server's version line.

## Not here

- **The Pipeline's map, a development's Money look, the aging chart and the printed Billed awaiting payment report**: the next PR.
- **The office's statement week, a GC's statement and the customer's portal**: the owner's call, as the card says.

## Release order

Merge, then deploy `dev-mcp` (`bash scripts/deploy-functions.sh dev-mcp`). No migration. The two screens ship with the client.
