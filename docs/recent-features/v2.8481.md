# v2.8481 — Legal desk: a job counts from the last day of work set by hand (2026-11-24)

Punch list #335, *Also seen* (`to-dos/lien-row-and-window-still-part.md`): the Legal desk and the firm's page never read the last day of work set by hand. They dated a job from its approved hours, else its last work date, else its bill, else its creation. The job's Lien window has counted from the hand day since v2.7193 (the owner's reading of 2026-11-13, to *which last day of work counts for the lien clock?*: "The day set by hand"). So counsel's page could show a later last day, and later lien days, than the Lien window. No migration. One edge function, `legal-portal`, needs a deploy.

This changes which day is shown and the days counted from it. It changes no word of any notice, affidavit, release or letter.

## The rule's order

`legalLastWorkBasis` in `src/lib/legal/legalLienPaper.ts`. The first that holds is the job's last day of work:

1. **The day set by hand** on the job (`jobs_ledger.lien_last_work_on`), whether it is earlier or later than the hours. It carries the reason it was saved with (`lien_last_work_note`).
2. The last approved clock session in the packet.
3. The job's last work date (`jobs_ledger.last_work_date`).
4. The day of its latest sent, unpaid bill (v2.7224).
5. The day the job was created.
6. Nothing.

Steps 2 to 6 are the order from before, unchanged. Step 1 is new, and it is the Lien window's own first step (`lienLastWorkDay`, `lienWindowLastMonth`, `lienClockMonths`). What makes a typed value a day is the window's own test, so a blank or a broken value is no hand day and the order goes on from step 2.

With a hand day, approved hours after it are said and not followed: the basis carries the last approved day when it is later than the hand day (the later of the packet's last approved session and the job's last work date).

## Every reader, and what each now reads

- **The lien clock lines** (`buildLegalPacket`, `paper.lienClock`): the last work day and its source, and the § 53.056 notice day, the affidavit's last day and the year to sue counted from it (`computeJobLienClock`). The two gap lines that name a lien day (*Lien window open on …* and *affidavit possible until …*) read these.
- **Where each job stands** (`buildLegalJobTimelines`): the job's path is built by the Lien window's builder, and it is now handed the hand day, so the months on the clock, every step's day and the LAST WORK stop are the window's. The stop reads *last day set by hand*, or *last day set by hand · later hours do not move it*, the window's own words.
- **The line under the job's name** on the Legal desk's Paper tab, the firm's page and the firm's packet print (`legalTimelineLastWorkWords`, one helper for the three): *last day of work set by hand 2026-06-18, reason: …*, and where hours follow it, *Hours were approved after it, through 2026-08-14. They do not move it.* Every other source reads word for word as it did.
- **The firm's page** builds the same packet in the browser from what `legal-portal` sends, so it reads all of the above once the function sends the two columns.
- **Not changed:** the Field evidence table's *Worked* column still reads the first and the last approved clock day. A hand day is no clock session, and that table reports the hours on record. The firm's Lien grid reads the desk's own readers (`list_lien_notice_months`), which have followed the hand day since migration `20261113190000`.

## The function

`legal-portal` reads each referred job's columns from `MATTER_JOB_COLUMNS` (`supabase/functions/_shared/legalMatterShape.ts`). The list gains `lien_last_work_on` and `lien_last_work_note`. It never carries who set the day or when. Both columns have been on `jobs_ledger` since v2.4676, so the select cannot fail on a missing column.

Old and new in either order read a sensible day:

- **The new page with the old function**: the job rows carry neither column, the order starts at step 2, and the page dates the job as it does today.
- **The old page with the new function**: the old page ignores two columns it does not know, and dates the job as it does today.
- Until the deploy, the office's Legal desk reads the hand day and the firm's page does not. The deploy closes that.

The kernel copy was regenerated (`node scripts/edge-kernels.mjs`) and did not change: the list lives in `_shared`, not in a generated kernel. `npm run check:edge-bundles` passes.
