# v2.5077 — Lien desk: the papers can carry the leader’s electronic signature (2026-10-09)

Lien desk signing, PR 1 of 3. Migration `20261010020000_lien_desk_item_signature.sql`. Taunya, at the printer: none of the signature lines on the run's 154 pages were signed. The owner asked for one button that puts the leader's name on the line in cursive instead of wet signing, and asked what makes that official. Counsel's okay 2026-10-09. The board https://claude.ai/artifact/G1zdfx33BchsUV3w5QidjE holds the six screens, now beside what we add, and the research: under the Texas UETA an electronic signature is any symbol or process attached to a record and adopted with the intent to sign it, and § 322.009 attributes it to whoever performed the act, so the strength is in the record — his own sign-in, his own press, bound to this notice, kept with the packet — not in whether the mark was drawn or typed (*Khoury v. Tomlinson*, Houston 2016, against *Cunningham v. Zurich*, Fort Worth 2011, where an auto-applied block lost). The § 53.056 notice itself has no signature requirement; the affidavit (§ 53.054, signed and sworn) stays with a notary and is untouched.
