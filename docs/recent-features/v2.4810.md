# v2.4810 — Legal desk: documents for the firm (2026-10-07)

One migration (`20261007230000_legal_matter_documents.sql`, push after merge) and the `legal-portal` function (**redeploy after the push**). PR 1 of 2 of *documents and the narrative* (mockup `to-dos/legal-portal-firm-ready/mockup-documents-and-narrative.html`). The owner (2026-10-07), after an evidence pull for a live matter landed in a folder beside the portal: *what does the legal portal ask for and is it easy for us or via MCP to add that? And since we are able to easily create a narrative, should we offer that in a tab in markdown for the legal team?* *Build both.*

## Why

The portal counted evidence (field reports, clock sessions) but could not hold a document, and the printed packet lettered only app records. The billing report, the supplier's invoice, the signed estimate and the email thread a lawyer reaches for first lived in a Drive folder the firm could not see from the matter.

## What changed

- **`legal_matter_documents`** ([migration page](../migrations/20261007230000_legal_matter_documents.md)): a title and one line on what the document shows (both required by check), the file in the private **`legal-matter-documents`** bucket under the matter's folder, the kind and size, who added it and when (stamped from the session by a trigger), a hold reason, a retire stamp. The office cohort reads, adds and updates; nothing deletes.
- **[`_shared/legalMatterDocuments.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/legalMatterDocuments.ts)**, one rule for the function, the desk and the firm's page: the kinds the portal takes (PDF, images, `.eml`, plain text), 10 MB a file, the safe storage path, the words, the keys that may travel, and the defensive parse (a link that is not `https` is dropped).
- **The desk** ([`LegalMatterDocumentsPanel`](../../src/components/jobs/legal/LegalMatterDocumentsPanel.tsx), top of the Evidence tab, once the account has a matter): a drop zone and *Choose files*; each file waits with its title (from its name) and an empty *What it shows* line, and will not save until both are filled; *Hold* with a reason, *Release to the firm*, *Remove* (retire). A title opens the file through a short link from the office's own session. `useLegalMatters` reads the live documents (`documentsByMatter`); a missing table leaves the list empty.
- **`legal-portal`** reads the live documents of the firm's matters, mints a 15-minute signed link for each one not held, and sends `documents[]` and `heldDocumentCount`; `shapeMatterForCounsel` cuts each document to its payload keys, so the storage path and a hold reason never leave.
- **The firm's Evidence tab** lists *Documents from the office · N* under the field work: the title with kind and size, the line, who added it and when, *Open ↗*; then *Links open for 15 minutes* and the held count. The desk's preview draws the same list without links (*on the firm's link*).
- **The printed packet** letters them as one more exhibit (*Documents from the office*, after the packet's own) and lists them under Section D with the held count.
- **The sample matter** carries two documents and one held.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **One line on what it shows, required.** A file named by its export helps nobody; the line is what the firm reads beside the title.
- **Hold like entries.** The office keeps its own internal reports back with a reason; the firm sees the count, as with held entries (#85 item 29's default stands).
- **Who added it comes from the session**, not the browser, by a trigger; removing is a retire stamp, so a document the firm once saw stays explainable.
- **Not done here:** the dev MCP verbs to file a document from a session, and the Narrative tab — both follow.
