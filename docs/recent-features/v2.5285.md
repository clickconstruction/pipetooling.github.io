# v2.5285 — Settings: Contracts and clauses (2026-10-12)

Cut 4c's screens, PR A (GC Reviews helper 9, directed by the lead session GC Review), over the clause tables on prod since v2.5269 (`20261012150000_contract_clauses.sql`) and the kernels of v2.5247. The prototype's `contractsHtml`, `editClause`, `approveClause`, `declineClause`, `clauseNeeds` and `previewContract` (Messages prototype v1.178 on `spike/gc-mode`). PR B writes the signed copy's clauses on sign and shows the version line on the sign page.

- **Settings → Contracts and clauses** (`settings-clauses`, `SettingsClausesTab.tsx`; the office team, `GC_CLAUSE_EDIT_TEAM`):
  - One card per library document built from clauses, in the library's order, with its version, its clause count and how many changes wait.
  - The clauses for everyone under **Everyone**, then one exhibit per trade, A to Z, on the one numbering (`settingsView`). Each clause shows its trades, its version, **Edit**, **Retire** and **History**.
  - A waiting change sits under its clause. A controller or a dev gets **Approve · vN** and **Decline**; anyone else reads *waiting on Maria or Robert*.
  - **+ a clause**, **Preview per trade** (`ClausePreviewWindow`: a company, English or Spanish, assembled client side by `assemblePaper`), and **Build a paper from clauses**, which offers library documents that are not fillable forms.
  - The kernel checks every change first (`editClause`, `approveProposal`), so a refusal is in its words before anything is written. The RPCs check again.
- **The windows** (`src/components/contracts/`): `ClauseEditWindow` (a new clause, an edit, a retire; *Applies to* is Everyone or the trades picked as chips, with *Another trade*), `ClauseDeclineWindow` (a why is required), `ClausePreviewWindow`, all in one shell (`ClauseWindow`). `ClausePaper` draws an assembled paper; PR B's sign page reuses it.
- **Needs you**: the Dashboard's **Contracts** line (`contract-clauses`, rank 40 beside GC Follow up) reads the changes waiting on a dev or a controller through `useClauseNeeds`, which reads nothing for anyone else. **Review** opens the oldest change's paper (`/settings?tab=settings-clauses#settings-clause-paper-<id>`, resolved by `settingsDeepLink.ts`). `ClauseNeedRows` draws the same rows with Approve, Decline and Open in Settings, for helper 2's Follow up (`followUpQueue` → `clauses`).
- **The loader** (`src/lib/contracts/clausesIo.ts`): the library, its packets, the clauses, every change, the trades and companies for *Applies to* and the preview, and the approvers by name, with written-out row types (`clauseRows.ts`) until the types regen. The three RPCs are wrapped; a refusal shows through `formatErrorMessage`.
- **Words that claim no send**: the approvers' push and email, and the proposer's text on a decision, are the messages-notify function's, later. `CLAUSE_NOTIFY_LIVE` in `approval.ts` is off. Until then a proposal reads *It waits for Maria or Robert to approve it.*, and a decline reads *Rosa sees your why on the clause's history.*
- **Kernel** (`clauseRows.ts`): the rows in the kernels' types, the cards, the buildable papers, each clause's history from the approved and declined changes, the trade choices, the approvers, the card anchors.
