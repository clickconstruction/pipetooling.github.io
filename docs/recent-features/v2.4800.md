# v2.4800 — Legal desk and counsel’s portal: press a stop on the timeline to see it as evidence (2026-10-07)

**The ask (the owner, 2026-10-07):** after v2.4793 put the stop door on the desk's notice pane, the Affidavit pane and the Lien window — *how could we present to the user something informative and useful since counsel's portal and the Legal desk still draw the strip without the door?* Mock-up (boards 4 and 5) https://claude.ai/artifact/MJnFkdKZEAhiM9RjL628M3, the *is this the best we can do?* pass, "build it".

**The idea.** Counsel never sends our paper, so on their side the stop is evidence, not a preview. Same window, same walk across the stops, but the left side is the record and the act is the portal's own door to the office.

## What shipped

- **The kernel** ([`lienStopEvidence.ts`](../../src/lib/legal/lienStopEvidence.ts)): `lienStopCounselView` — the title and line in counsel's words (*The July 2026 and August 2026 notice, as mailed · Mailed September 22, 2026; its day was October 15, 2026*), the envelope as it went out as a record card (`envelopeRecordCard`: the exhibit letter, Went out with the method, To, Covers, Property, Document), what the paper does for the claim under the statute (`DOES`), the rule in one sentence (`RULES`), what follows, the move pill (*OURS · DONE*, *COUNSEL*), the suit's venue (`lienForeclosureLine` + `courtWords`), and the whole view as text for the clipboard. `lienSuitChecklist`: the notices, the affidavit, its service, the agreement (`firmAgreementWords`), the sworn account (`swornMissing`) and the property record, lettered A… in that order, each on file or not — and *not needed* where the law asks nothing (an original contractor owes no § 53.056 notice), *window closed* for an affidavit whose day passed with nothing filed. The portal withholds sends and document links (the edge function cuts them), so a card says *method on the office's record* and *held by the office* rather than showing less in silence; the office's desk shows the method and the stored document's link. 4 tests.
- **The window** (`LienStopPaperWindow`): `LienStopPaper` grows `words` (the title and line override), `cards`, `checklist`, `does`, `ruleWords` (inline, for a reader with no rules window), `rail` (What follows, Venue), `moveWords` and `secondAct`. With cards, a checklist or *does* the left column draws those; the footer reads *The record as the office keeps it. Dates and dollars, nothing anyone said.*
- **The fold** ([`lienStopCounselPaper.ts`](../../src/lib/legal/lienStopCounselPaper.ts)): the view as the window takes it, the acts handed in by the host.
- **Counsel's portal** (`FirmMatterView`'s `JobTimelines`): the strip's door in the firm's voice; the act *Reach the office ›* scrolls to the reach strip when the page has one; *Copy the record as text* reads *Copied* for two seconds.
- **The Legal desk** (`PacketTab`, the Paper tab): the same window in the office voice; the act opens the job's Lien window.

## Decisions

- The record, not a rebuild: the portal's Paper tab already lists the envelopes; the window ties each to its stop and the statute and adds the walk. A separate evidence page would have repeated the Paper tab and the packet print.
- The document file stays with the office on the portal, as the edge function decides; the window says so.
- The suit checklist is the strongest card and the most likely to be wrong at an edge, so its kernel carries the most tests.
