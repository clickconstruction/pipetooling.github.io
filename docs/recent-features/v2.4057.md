# v2.4057 — The Job window counts card charges the way Job Summary does (2026-09-28)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Punch list **#52** (`to-dos/fuel-is-a-job-cost.md`), PR 1.

## The mismatch

Jobs → Job Summary has counted a job's card charges by one rule since v2.2692 (`cardChargeAllocationFilter`): an **Internal Transfer** is money moving between the company's own accounts, not a cost, and a card charge that is **also on a supply-house invoice** is the same purchase the invoice already counts, so it counts once. The Job window summed every card line allocated to the job (`mercuryCardTotalFromLines`), so a job with either kind of line read a higher parts cost — and a lower profit — in the window than on its Job Summary row.

## The change

- **`supabase/functions/_shared/jobMaterialsCostLines.ts`** — a card line carries its `mercuryTransactionId`; `jobCardChargesCountedFromLines(lines, exclusions)` is Job Summary's per-job figure (counted charges minus the invoice-linked slice, clamped as Job Summary clamps it); `jobCardLineStatus` / `jobCardLineStatusNote` say how a line counts. The snapshot type gains an optional `cardExclusions` (absent = everything counts).
- **`src/lib/fetchJobMaterialsCostSnapshot.ts`** — loads the rule's two lookups for the job's transactions (`loadCardChargeExclusions`, each failing soft to "everything counts").
- **The Job window** — the Card charges total, the profit band and the Job tab's Costs card use the counted figure; a line left out keeps its row, with a grey note: *Internal Transfer — not a job cost* or *Also on a supply-house invoice — counted there* (`JobCardLineNoteCell`).
- **Edit Job's Parts Cost section** — the same total and notes.

Left as they were: Edit Job's delete / migrate panels and Combine / Separate's preview still total every line attached — they describe rows that move, not what the job cost. dev-mcp's `get_job` still sums every line; bringing it over is an edge-function deploy, left for a follow-up.
