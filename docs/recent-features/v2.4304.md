## What was wrong (v2.4278's section)

A four-column table at the very bottom, under *Your payments*, far from the bills; *on its way* for a bill with no waiver started; a grid that needed ~430 px and ran off a phone; owners never saw it. Pass 2 added a LIEN WAIVER line under each bill like the TEST REPORT line — but that line already squeezes to eight lines at 375 px, and a third card of papers beside Your agreements and Test reports split the page's papers three ways.

## What changed

- **Signed only, two audiences** — [`_shared/portalWaivers.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/portalWaivers.ts): a half shows once its release is `signed` (signed, or sent); a bill with no signed half sends no row. Each row has `audience`: `payer` (`statementRoleFor` = owed) or `owner` (shared with the viewer and the viewer is the job's customer — open or paid). Each half carries `formType` and `signerName`. `customer-portal` reads `status = 'signed'` releases with `signer_printed_name` and the invoices' `shown_to_party`; `bills[]` / `sharedBills[]` carry `invoiceId` ([`portalMergedBills.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/portalMergedBills.ts)). The page also drops a `signing` half from an older function.
- **The note on the bill** — [`portalWaiverPapers.ts`](../../src/lib/portal/portalWaiverPapers.ts) `portalWaiverNote` (the furthest signed half: *conditional, signed Sep 29* · *conditional final, sent Aug 22* · *unconditional, sent Oct 4*) drawn by `PortalWaiverNoteLink` ([`PortalWaiverPapers.tsx`](../../src/components/portal/PortalWaiverPapers.tsx)) in the bill row's note (`PortalBillRow`) and, for the owner, under a shared bill (`PortalSharedBillsCard`). Under 560 px it is its own 44 px row (`[data-bill-waiver]`). Prints as words.
- **Your papers** — `CustomerPortal`'s *Your agreements* and *Test reports* cards become one card with three groups: **Agreements**, **Lien waivers** (*Lien waivers on your property* for the owner), **Test reports** (still folded past five). Waiver rows (`portalWaiverPaperRows`, newest first): the street, *Conditional progress · Bill 2 of 3 · $18,200.00 · signed by Malachi Whites*, *SIGNED* (copper) or *PAID IN FULL* (green) with the date, **View waiver**. `PortalWaiversSection` is gone.
- **The email** — `send-lien-release-email` appends the bill emails' *Your account, any time* card (QR + short address, *Every lien waiver and bill for your jobs, with no login.*) when the recipient has a portal ([`_shared/lienWaiverEmailCard.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/lienWaiverEmailCard.ts): whose portal — the GC when the email is its billing email, else the job's customer; the card appended to HTML and text). Read with the service role as `send-stripe-invoice` does; any failure sends without the card.
- **Sample** — `sample-gc` ([`customerSampleFixtures.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/customerSampleFixtures.ts)) carries three waivers and bill ids, so Settings → What customers see shows the note and the group.
- Guides: *send a GC our lien waiver* (**What the GC sees** rewritten; **What the owner sees** and **The email has the account page** added), *file a test report* and *get a job contract signed* (the groups' new home). GLOSSARY, EDGE_FUNCTIONS.

## Design calls

- **A waiver is a fact about its bill**, so it lives in the bill's note, not a new line type — one row per bill stays one row.
- **One card for signed papers.** Every row has the same grammar; *View waiver* matches *View report*.
- **Signed is enough to show** (the owner's call): the portal may be a step ahead of the email.
- **Owners see only what the office shared**, but keep the paid bills' unconditionals — the paper that protects them most.

## Deploy

Merge → `bash scripts/deploy-functions.sh customer-portal send-lien-release-email`. Either order is safe: the page reads a missing `invoiceId` / `audience` / `formType` as none, and an old page ignores the new fields.
