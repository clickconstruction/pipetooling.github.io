# v2.6292 — My email: untag keeps the office's work, the tag records its own ties, hidden labels stay out (punch list #230) (2026-11-01)

Punch list #230 (`to-dos/messages-my-email/README.md`), fixes to the edge function `gmail-mine` after PR 4's follow (v2.6279, #6715) and PR 6's window (v2.6289, #6725): the independent review's findings 4 to 10, checked by the lead, and one more from the lead (the fold line). Nothing in the client changes. The function's section is [`docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` → gmail-mine](../EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md#gmail-mine); the migration is [`20261101183219_gmail_mine_untag_and_ties.sql`](../migrations/20261101183219_gmail_mine_untag_and_ties.md).

- **1. Untag keeps the office's work.** `message_promises` and `message_highlights` are `ON DELETE CASCADE` on their line, so an untag silently deleted a promise the office wrote down or a highlight on a tagged line. The untag now reads both for the person's lines first and holds back every line that has one, with its files and its `.eml`, as a line `contact_owed` holds (its `ON DELETE RESTRICT` refuses the delete) is held today. `planUntag` gains `kept` and answers `keep`; `MyMailUntagOk` gains `held`, every line that stayed for either reason.
- **2. An untag the follow cannot undo.** The untag deleted the lines first and the `email_tag_threads` row last, so a run of the follow that reached the row in between, or after a failed last delete, landed every mail again. The new `email_tag_threads.untagging_at` is set first. The follow's selection skips a marked row, and it asks again before each row it reads and once more before anything lands (`stillFollowed`, the `Untagging` stop), so a row an untag marks after the run picked it is passed by and nothing is marked on it. The row still goes last, so a stopped untag leaves it marked for the next untag to find and finish, and a tag of the conversation clears the mark.
- **3. Hidden labels are never copied.** `users.my_mail_hidden_labels` only shaped the list's search, so a tag and the follow copied a mail under a hidden label onto the job. The tag and each person's run of the follow now name the hidden labels to Gmail's ids once (`gmailLabels`, `myMailHiddenLabelIds`: the name whole, case-blind), and `planTag` and `planFollow` skip a mail carrying one as `hidden`, held by a line or not. Gmail refusing to list the labels stops a tag before anything is written, and marks that person's rows as a failed read for the run.
- **4. A tag adds the job to the person's jobs.** `landMail` wrote lines through `email_write_line` and never touched `contact_jobs`, so the other person's thread drew no tile for the job and the picker's *their jobs* stayed empty. Each written line's person now goes on the job's people as `tie_message` puts them: the contact and the job in its one column, as the service role, once a call per person and job, a row already there standing (Postgres's `23505`, since the partial unique indexes leave PostgREST no `ON CONFLICT` target). Best effort: a miss is logged `gmail_mine_job_unlisted` and the line stands.
- **5. The tag's own ties, recorded.** Telling the tag's ties from hand ties by `tied_by` alone went wrong both ways: a box's line the first tag tied stayed on the old job after a tag onto another and after the untag, and a line the person tied by hand before tagging was untied by the untag. The new `email_tag_threads.tied_line_ids` records every box line the tag or the follow ties, before each tie. The tag's row is now upserted before its steps, with the record, so a tag that stops partway keeps both, and the follow re-reads the record right before it adds to it. A tag onto another job moves the person's own lines and the recorded ties still where the tag put them (`isTagTie`: recorded, on the row's job, stamped by its tagger), and an untag unties exactly those, through the caller's `tie_message` (the service role with the row's `tagged_by` when there is no caller, `tieLine`). A line placed by hand since stays. A row tagged before the push records none, so its untag unties no box line.
- **6. The person's own sends from another address.** `isTheirs` compared the From with `users.email` alone, so a mail sent as an alias or a send-as, which sits in their Sent, read as coming in: from a new unknown contact, or folded as a notice when the alias was a box. A mail carrying Gmail's `SENT` label is now the person's own (`isOwnMail`): out and never folded in `rows.ts`, and in `planTag` (so `planFollow`) its From counts as ours and never as a box's copy, its line going out to the first To or Cc outside under the person. One sent from an alias with nobody outside lands on the person's own thread.
- **7. To tag never hides a mail by a stale label.** To tag searched with `-label:"Tagged in ClickTooling"`, and the untag swallowed a failed label removal, so a label left behind (a failed removal, or a job deleted with its tag row) hid that mail from To tag for good. To tag now reads past the label, since `viewRows` hides a tagged conversation by the database's own record; Tagged keeps it. The untag counts each mail the label would not leave as `labelLeft` (every mail a tag labels when the box's labels cannot be read), logged as counts alone.
- **8. A mail with no Message-ID.** The tag found held lines by Message-ID alone, so on a second tag such a mail was planned as a write again: its files copied again, its line left on the old job. The tag now also finds the person's own lines by Gmail id (`knownOwnLines`, `planTag`'s `byGmailId`), as `list` and the follow already did.
- **9. No orphan copies.** Files are copied before `email_write_line`. When it answers `written: false` (another reader, another person or this person's last write wrote the line first), or refuses the write, the copies just made that no `message_attachments` row names come back out of the bucket (`orphanCopies`); a copy the line that stood names stays.
- **10. The fold line draws.** `list` answered `rows: view.rows` with `folded` as a count, so the folded rows never reached the tab, which re-applies `viewRows` and draws `shown.folded` behind its fold line: the notices the mock-up folds under one line were simply missing from To tag and All. The answer's rows are now the view's rows, then its folded ones, each part newest first and each folded row flagged with its kind; `folded` stays the count, and Tagged has none.
- **The pure half**: `tagPlan.ts` (the `hidden` skip, `byGmailId`, `tagTie`, `MY_MAIL_SENT_LABEL` and the party of a sent mail), `rows.ts` (`isOwnMail`), `mineFunction.ts` (`myMailQuery` without the To tag label, `myMailHiddenLabels`, `myMailHiddenLabelIds`, `MyMailTagRecord`, `myMailTagRecord`, `withTies`, `isTagTie`, `knownLines` with the record, `knownOwnLines`, `planUntag` with the record and `kept`, `isDuplicateKey`, `orphanCopies`, `planFollow` with `hidden`), and `contract.ts` (`MyMailUntagOk.held` and `labelLeft`, optional since a function deployed before this sends neither). The edge copy is regenerated, and `scripts/edge-kernels.mjs` lists the new exports in its index.
- **Tests**: new `mineFunction.test.ts`, `tagPlan.test.ts` and `rows.test.ts` cases cover each kernel above. `gmailMine.run.test.ts` has a run test for each finding: a promise and a highlight held, what we owe held by the database, a stopped untag the follow lands nothing back on and the next untag finishes, a tag after it clearing the mark, a row an untag marks mid-run passed by before its read and before anything lands, a hidden label skipped by the tag and the follow and Gmail refusing the labels, the job's people for a tag and for a follow across two jobs in one run, a hand tie the untag leaves, a recorded line moved by hand left on a tag and an untag, the follow's tie recorded and untied, a mail sent from an alias listed and tagged as going out, a stuck label and an unreadable label list counted, a mail with no Message-ID moved by a second tag with nothing copied again, the copies taken back when a box wrote first and kept when the line that stood names them, and the folded rows of To tag and All. Each fix was broken on purpose in turn, and at least one run test failed every time. Eight existing tests changed because a fix makes them wrong. In `mineFunction.test.ts`: the To tag search (finding 7) and the two `planUntag` cases (findings 1 and 5: the record replaces `tied_by`, and the answer gains `keep`). In `gmailMine.run.test.ts`: the All page and the To tag page (finding 10, the folded row now arrives, and finding 7, the To tag search), the tag onto another job (finding 5: the first tag's box line now moves with it), and the two untag tests whose answers gain `held` and `labelLeft`. The fakes gained the foreign keys a line's delete meets, `contact_jobs`' unique indexes, `onRpc`, `stuck`, `labelsDown` and `onThreadRead`. The new `untagTiesSql.test.ts` holds the migration and the function to the two columns. The function passes a strict `tsc` with stubs for its two URL imports, with and without `noUncheckedIndexedAccess`.

## After the merge

Push first, then redeploy. `supabase db push` from a clean checkout of `main` pushes `20261101183219_gmail_mine_untag_and_ties.sql`, with PR 4's and PR 6's migrations first if they are not pushed yet. Then redeploy the function (`supabase functions deploy gmail-mine --no-verify-jwt`), then the importers `npm run check:edge-drift` names, since the copy's index changed. Deployed before the push, the function stops every tag and every untag at its first read of the row and runs no follow. The push alone is harmless. After the push, `npm run gen-types:linked` picks up the two columns, shipped as its own `chore(types)` PR.
