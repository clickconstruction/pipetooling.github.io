# v2.4747 — Lien desk: no gap under the last section row (2026-10-07)

Client only. The owner (2026-10-06), on a screenshot of job 891's pane: *below page 3 of 3 there is a floating gap — I would like that fixed.* The screenshot predates v2.4733, when the page labels were the rows stacked at the bottom; on main the same gap sat under *In the envelope*, the last of the section heads.

## Why

A `position: sticky` head with `bottom: 0` sticks to the scroll container's padding edge, not its border edge. The pane had `padding-bottom: 0.9rem`, so the last head stopped 14.4 px above the pane's foot and the paper scrolled through the strip under it.

## What changed

- `LienDeskModal`'s pane: `padding: 0 1.1rem` (no bottom padding); a `data-lien-desk-pane-foot` row of 0.3rem after the last section keeps the same room at the end (the grid's 0.6rem gap plus 0.3rem). Measured in the browser: the last head's bottom is the pane's bottom (0 px), and the room after the last section is 14.3 px, as before.

## Tests

- `LienDeskModal.render.test.tsx` passes unchanged (jsdom does not lay out sticky boxes; the check was in the browser).
