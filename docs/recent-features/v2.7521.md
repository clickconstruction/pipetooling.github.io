# v2.7521 — Lien desk: a job taken off the lien path leaves every lien screen (2026-11-16)

PR 2 of the *not lienable* train (the owner's pick of 2026-11-16). PR 1 (v2.7504, migration `20261117080000`, pushed the same day) gave the answer a home on the job. This PR makes every reader of the desk's jobs read it. No screen gives the answer yet, so nothing changes on prod until one does.

## One rule, the door that was already there

A job an open in-kind deal pays has left the desk since v2.7253 by one rule that seven readers share (`src/lib/jobs/lienInKind.ts`). A job a person took off the lien path leaves by the same door:

- **`src/lib/jobs/lienJobPath.ts`** (pure, imports nothing): `jobOffLienPath` reads `jobs_ledger.lien_path_answer === 'off_path'`, `lienOffPathJobIds` lists them, and `lienJobPathStanding` reads who, when and why beside the answer. A row that carries no answer is on the path.
- **`jobOffLienDesk`** in `lienInKind.ts` is *in kind, or off the path*. `lienJobIdsOffDesk` and the new `lienOffDeskJobIds` use it, and `withLienOffDeskColumns` (once `withLienInKindColumn`) puts both marks on the end of a select, so no reader can forget either.

## Handed on for the bin

`useLienDeskData` also reads, on the open desk, every job that says `off_path` by its own answer, so a job no reader and no paper names is not lost. The ones that still owe money or hold a lien paper are handed on as `offPathJobs` (`src/lib/jobs/lienOffPathBin.ts`): the job, its GC, what its sent bills still owe by the desk's rule, why, who, the day, when it went on the attorney's list, and its papers as they stood. `buildLienOffPathBin` makes the bin's rows from them. A ZZ test job that is hidden is not listed. Nothing is read for it in light mode.

Nothing is voided or deleted, so a job put back is on every list again on the next read, with its papers.

## The job's own Lien window

It opens whole, as it does for a job paid in kind, under one amber note (`lienOffPathWindowNote`, `data-lien-window-off-path`): that someone took the job off the lien path, the day, the reason given, and that its lien dates still run though the desk reminds nobody of them.

## The activity line

`lien_path_change` joins `JobActivityEventType` (tag *Lien path*, under Billing, not a customer event). The function of v2.7504 writes it each time the answer changes. A test holds the type and its two sentences to the migration's own words.
