# v2.8593 — Lien desk: the run says which printed papers have changed since (2026-11-25)

Punch list #362 (`to-dos/lien-to-do-one-run/README.md`), step 7, *changed since printed*: the side that follows the owner's answer of 2026-11-25 to *which changes mark a printed lien paper as changed*. Client only: no migration, no edge function. It stands on v2.8589, which made every lien print keep what its paper said.

## The answer it is built to

Asked on the day's sheet: *"a printed notice or affidavit reads as changed when anything it states has moved since it printed: the money, the parties, the property, the work and its months. Its date, its contact person and the county an affidavit is signed in do not count"*. The pick was *every statement the paper makes*, and the answer to *do all the picks stand* was *Yes, all picks*. That is the list v2.8589 shipped as `LIEN_SWORN_FIELDS`, so no field moved here.

## What it does

- **A card, *Changed since printed*, over *Print the run*.** It is drawn only when a printed paper of the run would say another thing today. Each paper: its kind, its job, when it printed and who printed it, then one line a change with the value printed and the value now, in the paper's order. Money reads as money, a day as a day, a tick as what it does (*sworn*, *left out*).
- **A printed notice is read against the bills as they stand.** `buildLienDeskRun` now puts `moneyNow` on each notice: the claim, and on a § 53.056 notice its split by month and its retainage, as the desk would draw them today. Today's notice is the stored paper with that money over it. So a bill corrected after the print counts though nobody redrew the notice.
- **An affidavit has a printed fact.** A right affidavit whose newest filed print says the same today is left out of the run's print. A line under the press says how many, and that the Board's *Print for notarization* still prints them. Until now nothing recorded an affidavit's print, so after a reload a printed one counted as to print.
- **A changed affidavit prints again with the run**, and the card says so. A changed notice is taken back and printed again in the run window, and the card names the Board's press that opens it.
- **The read.** `useLienRunPrinted` reads each job's newest filed print while the checklist is open, once for a set of jobs, and again once a print's copies are filed. While it reads, nothing counts as printed. A read that fails says so under the press, with *Read again*, and counts nothing as printed.

## A print that kept no values

Prints before v2.8589 kept nothing to compare.

- **An affidavit** counts as to print, as before, and one line says how many such there are.
- **A notice** has a printed stamp, so it is not silent: the notice as stored stands in for what printed, and its line says a stand-in was read. That is right unless the notice was redrawn since the print. The same is done for a notice combined at one property, where one paper spoke for several jobs.

## What it does not do

- **No press on the card.** *Reprint*, *It stands* and *Take it out* are the next PR. *It stands* is kept on record in a table of its own (the owner's second answer of 2026-11-25), so that PR carries a migration.
- **The checklist's own counts.** The strip and the line *N papers are right* still count a printed affidavit as *for the notary*. They come from a kernel over the Board's chips, which reads no print.
- **A changed paper is not held back.** A changed notice still stands in *Approved · to mail* and can be recorded as mailed. The card warns. It does not lock.
- **Only the lien office reads it.** `sent_documents` is the office's to read, and the card is drawn with the print.
