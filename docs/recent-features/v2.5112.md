# v2.5112 — What customers see: the sample estimate offers options like a real one (2026-10-09)

Punch list #103 (`to-dos/customer-samples-through-kernels.md`), PR 4. No migration. Edge functions to redeploy after the merge are listed under *Deploys owed*. Until then the sample shows the old estimate, and nothing breaks.

## What was wrong

Settings → What customers see has shown the sample estimate since v2.2758 (`/estimate/accept?t=sample`, `sample-done`). `sampleEstimateResponse` wrote the four lines and the total by hand and sent `options: []`. So the sample never showed Estimate Options (v2.2457 to v2.2465): no choice to pick, no ★, no add-on to tick. The sample estimate email passed `options: []` too, where the real email prices every option.

A real estimate's options are written by `estimateOptionsDraftPersistFields` when the office saves. That kernel also mirrors the ★ option's lines and total into `line_items_snapshot` and `total_cents`. `get-estimate-for-customer` reads the options back through `normalizeSharedEstimateOptions`.

## What changed

- **The save kernel moved to `_shared/`.** `estimateOptionsDraftPersistFields` moved from `src/lib/estimates/estimateOptions.ts` to a new [`_shared/estimateOptionsPersist.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/estimateOptionsPersist.ts). Its body is the same; it is now generic over the option and line shapes, so the client's types come back out unchanged. The src file keeps the same name and signature as a thin wrapper over it, so `saveDraft` and the tests import as before. It sits beside the shared parse (`_shared/estimateOptions.ts`), not inside it. So `accept-estimate`, `send-estimate-to-customer` and `log-estimate-option-view`, which only read options, keep their bundle.
- **The sample takes a saved estimate's path.** In [`_shared/customerSampleFixtures.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/customerSampleFixtures.ts) the sample is three options. *50-gal gas tank, like for like* is the ★ choice, `SAMPLE_ESTIMATE`'s own lines at $4,380. *Tankless gas heater* is the second choice at $6,200. *Hot water recirculation pump* is an add-on at $650, not pre-ticked. They are saved by `estimateOptionsDraftPersistFields`, round-tripped as jsonb, and read back as `get-estimate-for-customer` reads a row. `_shared/customerSample.ts` is untouched, so the functions that only read the sample names keep their bundle.
- **The sample email** reads its total and options from the sample estimate the page serves. It prices each option the way `send-estimate-to-customer` does.

The page still opens on the same ★ lines and the same $4,380.00. What is new is the choice and the add-on beside them.
