# v2.5595 — Today’s sends: the app’s reminders become texts the office presses, and a booking can go in the morning (2026-10-16)

PR 4 of Tell the customer (punch list #115, `to-dos/tell-the-customer/README.md`; PR 2 is v2.5593, PR 3 v2.5594). The principle's second half: the texts the app would send by itself become **offers** the office presses, unless the owner sets a step to *Sends*. Migration `20261016040000_nudge_offers.sql` (`docs/migrations/20261016040000_nudge_offers.md`); the edge function `messages-nudge-dispatch` redeployed after the push; the types regenerated after.

- **The switch, three ways** (`flows/nudgeDispatch.ts` `AppSends`, `AppSendMode`, `appSendMode`; `appSends.ts`; `appSendsIo.ts`; `useAppSends.ts`; `FlowStepsTable.tsx`; `SettingsFlowsTab.tsx`): `messages_nudge_app_sends_v1` holds `true` (*Sends*, as before) or `'offer'` (*Offers*) per step, read fail-soft by `appSendsFrom` (anything else is off). The Flows editor's control on a step the app may send is **Off · Offers · Sends** (`role="radiogroup"`, label *The app and this reminder*), with a hint under it and the words the run would send; the owner or a dev presses it, the office reads it.
- **The planner** (`planNudgeRun`): a step set to *Offers* takes the same road as a sent one (off, not a text, snoozed, sent lately, one a morning), but its text lands in `run.offers` (`NudgeOffer`, the text plus `held`) instead of `run.texts`; a number that is missing or sent STOP **holds** the prepared text with why (`OFFER_HELD`) instead of keeping it back, so the office sees the door. A text already waiting on Today's sends (`input.offered`, from the open `nudge_offers` rows) keeps the step back as `'offered'`, for *Offers* and for *Sends* alike, so nothing doubles when the owner flips the switch. The pressed text is recorded in `nudge_sent` as a person's, so the next morning waits out the step's days. `stageSendFor` reads the mode too and marks its text `mode: 'offer'` for the day the board's moves are wired.
- **The table and its doors** (`nudge_offers`, `nudge_offer_make`, `nudge_offer_sent`, `nudge_offer_dismiss`): one row per prepared text with the person, the step, the job, the words as composed, the number, who prepared it (the app, or the office with a `send_on`), `held_why`, and how it ended. The office team reads; the service role and the three definer functions write; the three fences cover it. `nudge_offer_sent` stamps the row and writes `nudge_sent` through `nudge_record_sent`; `nudge_offer_dismiss` takes *not this one*, *held*, *stopped* or *sent elsewhere*.
- **The morning run** (`messages-nudge-dispatch`): reads every open offer; writes one `nudge_offers` row per planned offer (`prepared_by` app, `channel_id` null when held); then sends the texts the office prepared for this morning or an earlier one (`prepared_by` office, `send_on` ≤ today, a number) through `sendText` as the office's own: the row stamped `sent_at` and `message_id`, a `nudge_sent` row (`sent_by` person, the preparer), and the thread's event line *The app sent the text the office prepared for this morning.* (keyed `nudge:offer:<id>`). One Quo turned away is held with Quo's words and its `send_on` cleared, so the office sees it instead of the run retrying. The summary gains `prepared` and `morningSent`, the kept-back reason `offered`, and the dry run answers `offers` and `morning` too.
- **Today's sends** (`components/dashboard/TodaysSendsWindow.tsx`, `lib/offers/offers.ts`, `offersIo.ts`, `hooks/useTodaysSends.ts`, the Needs you item `todays-sends`): the Dashboard's Needs you line for the GC office team, *3 texts ready to send, 1 goes in the morning, 2 held*, opens the window: each row with the person, the step in their flow and the words; **Edit** / **Done editing**, **Send** (the reply box's own path through `quo-send`, then `nudge_offer_sent`), **Send all N that are ready** (one by one; held and morning rows left out; a toast says how many went), **Not this one**, **Hold until…** (`nudge_hold_until`, then the row put away as held) and **Stop reminding on this job** (`flow_skip_step`, then stopped). A held row says why with no Send; a morning row says its day. The window loads lazily; the line's count refetches on focus and after each press.
- **Send in the morning** (`TellCustomerSheet.tsx`, `tellCustomerIo.makeOffer`, `offers.ts` `morningSendOn`): on a booking (not an estimate), a quiet press beside Skip hands the text to the run: today's 9 AM when it has not fired yet, else tomorrow's, in the app's zone. The sheet's receipt reads *Ready for the morning* with the words, *Goes at 9 AM on Sat Oct 17 from the company number*, and where to find it until then.
