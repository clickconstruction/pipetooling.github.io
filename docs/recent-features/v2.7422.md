# v2.7422 — Lien desk: signing is the last section of the notice (2026-11-15)

Taunya's ask of 2026-11-15, on a draft whose signing box filled the bottom third of the pane: the signature goes in a section like the rest, its bar blue in place of grey, so the reader has more room for the paper and goes through it to sign at the bottom. Client only. No migration, no edge function.

## Before

Since v2.7314 a monthly notice's footer was the pane column's pinned bottom row on a computer. With v2.7382's signing box in it the footer took about the bottom third of the pane on Taunya's screen, under a pane that also keeps its stacked heads at the top, so a page of the notice showed through a short slot.

## What changed

- [`src/lib/jobs/lienPaneSections.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienPaneSections.ts): the notice pane has a sixth section, `sign`, drawn only where the footer is in the pane. `LienPaneSection.foot` marks it. `lienNoticeFootHead` words its bar by the pile: *Sign and send* with *Not signed yet*, *Waiting on Malachi’s signature*, *Approved, not signed yet*, *Signed, in the run* or *On hold*; *Record the mailing* with *Printed, in the run*; *After the send* with *Sent*; and *Window closed* with *Nothing to sign* on a missed notice with no month left. `lienPaneHeadsOverPages` counts the heads that stack over the pages, which the footer's head is not one of.
- [`src/components/jobs/LienPaneHead.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienPaneHead.tsx): a `foot` head carries `data-tone="blue"` and pins at the pane's bottom edge only (`top: auto`). It never stacks under the strip, so at the pane's end it sits over its own box.
- [`src/components/jobs/LienDesk.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDesk.tsx): on a computer the pane ends with `paneHead('sign')` and an empty body, `data-lien-pane-body="sign"`. The footer is built after the pane, so it lands in that body through a portal, the v2.3753 device the other way round. The pane column's bottom row is empty for a notice. `noticeFooterRef` is still the footer, so the verdict band's press scrolls the pane to it.
- [`src/index.css`](../../src/index.css): the blue head is white words on `#2563eb` in both themes. It and its section draw over a stacked head, so on a short pane the signing box is never under a bar.

A phone and the stacked layout keep the desk's strip under the fold bar. The affidavit's and the retainage notice's footers are still the pane column's bottom row.
