# v2.4488 — Submittals: the walkthrough opens with the words the page uses (2026-10-04)

The owner, 2026-10-04: "I think we should add a terminology page at the start of the preview where we explain what cut sheets and other representative words are." Asked which preview, the owner picked the walkthrough. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What changed

- **The words** ([`submittalTour.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/submittalTour.ts)): `SUBMITTAL_WORDS`, fifteen of them — Submittal, GC, Tag, Schedule, Cut sheet, Vendor PDF, Rev, Package, GC sees it, Order only, Left out, Proposed, Their answer, Lead time, Order log — each with one or two plain sentences. `SUBMITTAL_WORDS_STOP` is the walkthrough's first stop (*The words on this page*); `SUBMITTAL_WORDS_ONLY_STOP` is the same page opened alone, whose line says Done where the other says Next.
- **The card** ([`SpotlightTour.tsx`](../../src/components/SpotlightTour.tsx)): a step may be `center` (a stop about no one control: the card centres over the dimmed page, with no hole and nothing "missing") and may carry `terms`, drawn as a two-column list under the body in a wider card (520 px). The list takes the screen's height and scrolls inside the card when it is short. `spotlightTourStepsPresent` keeps a centred step.
- **The tab** ([`BidsSubmittalsTab`](../../src/components/bids/BidsSubmittalsTab.tsx)): *Words on this page* beside the title's `?` opens the words alone (`startWords`); the steps behind it stay folded as they were, where the whole walkthrough unfolds every step.
- **The guide** (*build a submittal package*): a *The words on this page* section near the top, with the same fifteen.

The stops after it are unchanged, and each still explains a trade word the first time it uses one: the words page is a place to look a word up, not a reason to stop saying what it means.
