# v2.8167 — Lien desk: a sent notice names its months at the top of its pane (2026-11-21)

The first of two older oddities the live walk of 2026-11-21 wrote on punch list #361's card (the ledger, v2.7989). Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

On a notice in the Sent pile with nothing due, the scrolled strip read *Sent Oct 14 ›* and then *No month picked ›*. The Months on this job head read *no months · $285*. The notice had gone out for its months, and the folded gates one line lower named them.

Both read the pane's own months, `monthsList` in `LienDesk.tsx`. Those are the months a notice being drafted names: the draft's, else the months still due, else the closed months a late notice may carry. A sent notice is no draft (`item` is null for a record in `sent`), nothing is due on it, and it is not late. So the list is empty on every sent notice, and both places fell to the draft's empty words. *No month picked* reads as something the office still has to do. Nobody picks a month on a paper that went.

## What changed

- [`lienDeskGates.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDeskGates.ts): `lienSentGatesFold` answers `months` beside `head` and `line`. It is the months the notice named, off the sent record's own `months` (*Jul + Aug*), the same words the fold's line and its fourth row already carried.
- [`lienStripChips.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienStripChips.ts): with a fold, the months chip reads `fold.months` (*Jul + Aug ›*) and its hover says *The months the notice named when it went. Down to Months on this job.* The pane's own months are not read there. A draft is unchanged: *Sep + Oct ›*, or *No month picked ›* with none ticked.
- [`lienPaneSections.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienPaneSections.ts): `sentMonths`, the fold's `months`, replaces the draft's months in the Months on this job head (*Jul + Aug · $285*). `LienDesk.tsx` passes `gatesFold?.months`.

## A sent notice with no month on record

The column allows an empty list. No sent notice on the desk has one today: all seventeen sent records name their months, read on 2026-11-21. If one ever does, the strip reads *No month named ›* and the head *no month named · $285*, the words the fold has used for it since v2.6625. They say what the record holds and ask for nothing.

## Not changed

A Sent row with months still due keeps its four gates and reads the next notice's months, as before. A missed notice with nothing due keeps *no months* in its head: no notice is drafted there and none went.
