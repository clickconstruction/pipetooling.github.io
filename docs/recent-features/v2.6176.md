# v2.6176 — Legal desk: what each path nets, and the walk-away line worked out (2026-10-30)

Punch list #218 (`to-dos/legal-desk-settlement-floor/`), PR 3 of 4 of direction C, on v2.6173 (PR 2, the attorney's read). The owner: *if they don't settle it costs us more in collections and in taking them to trial.* The floor never drew that cost; this PR does, and derives the one number the floor was meant to be from it.

## What changed

- **The kernel** ([`settlementPaths.ts`](../../src/lib/legal/settlementPaths.ts), pure, 6 tests): from `LegalWorth`'s claim, the firm's `contingencyPct` and `filingCost`, whether suit is filed (`legalSuitFiled`: stage `suit` · `judgment` · `post_judgment`), the standing offers (`offerStands`, as the bars read them) and the attorney's read — one path per standing offer, smallest first (*Settle at their offer* · *Settle at our counter*: the offer, the firm's cut, what we keep, the claim let go, *within the month*, with the counter's expiry day), and *Collections and trial* (the claim, the firm's cut, the filing cost unless filed, the costs to try it from the read, what we keep at a full win, *N months to a judgment, then collecting it* or *the firm has not said how long*, the read as the chip, and on a hard read the caveat *A hard case. A full win is not the likely end. The firm's call.*). Every part is a fraction of the claim's scale, so the cards compare. **The walk-away line**: `claim − (filing + costs to try) / (1 − the firm's %)`, the offer at which settling nets what a full win nets; null when the cut takes everything, left off when the costs exceed the claim. One sentence under the cards (`words`).
- **The claim bar marks the line** ([`moneyBars.ts`](../../src/lib/legal/moneyBars.ts): an optional `line` input and a `line` mark kind, green, *above this, settling beats a full win*; drawn only when the claim bar is drawn, which is when retainage is added or an offer stands).
- **The component** ([`LegalSettlementPaths.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/legal/LegalSettlementPaths.tsx)) under the bars: a grid of cards, each a thin bar (green keep, the red hatch for the firm's cut, the filing red, a deeper red for the costs to try it, the neutral hatch for the claim let go), the figure we keep, the rows, the when line, the caveat; the sentence last. `LegalDesk` composes the input from `worth`, the matter's stage, the Offers log it already read for the bars and the current read.
- **Guide** *review a collections account before it goes to your attorney*: a paragraph under the bars paragraph. `docs/GLOSSARY.md` → Legal matter.

## Is this the best we can do?

- No probability. A chance of winning would be a figure the desk invents, so there is none: the read is the attorney's word in two values, and the line is arithmetic on what the desk holds or the firm typed.
- One rate. The firm row carries one contingency percent; a firm that charges less before suit than at trial wants a second rate on the row, and the trial path would read it. Not built; the card names it as the owner's call.
- The costs the firm has already logged on the matter (`fee` / `cost` entries) are not taken off either path yet: they are spent whichever way it goes, so they do not move the comparison.
