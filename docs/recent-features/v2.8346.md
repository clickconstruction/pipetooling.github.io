# v2.8346 — Lien desk on a phone: the flow fits one screen, and one pinned line takes you back to it (2026-11-23)

Punch list #428, the flow. The owner's words of 2026-11-23: *What has become a priority to me … is showing the flowchart in Liens To Do and giving the user the ability to jump out of the flow chart and jump back in.* He picked *One screen, pinned line* on the card's second mock-up (`to-dos/lien-to-do-on-a-phone/flow.html`) and gave the go to build the same day. It is built on the rows of v2.8341. No migration and no edge function. A computer draws nothing differently.

## What was there

The Board's flow is two columns of bins with an arrow down each and one across. A phone stood the bins one over the other behind **Show the bins · N**: about 2,050 px, sixteen bins in one column, every job a chip 22 px tall that showed a number alone. A pressed bin narrowed the list, and the list was under all of it.

## What changed, on a phone

- **A switch at the top of the Board: By day and The flow.** By day is the four tiles over the list, as before, without the bins. The phone opens on By day the first time and then on the view last used (`localStorage`, `pipetooling_lien_desk_phone_view`). An empty desk has no switch.
- **The flow, as counts.** Both columns stand side by side, *Monthly notices* and *Lien affidavits*. Each stage is one press 44 px tall with its name and its count, and no chip. A small mark stands between two stages, and the one arrow across runs from *Sent* to *Waiting on its notice*, measured after layout as the Board's arrows are. An empty stage is dashed and muted. A closed end is dashed and drawn only while a job is in it. A stage's count takes the worst of its papers' colors, in the Board's own meanings: red at risk, late or due within a day, amber this week, violet waiting on the leader.
- **The tiles stand down over the flow.** Three of the four are stages of the flow already. The day stays as one line over it, its count and *by Dec 15*, and its red line still narrows the list to the papers it counts.
- **Under the flow:** the retainage line with its door, the desk's line for public property, its bins for public jobs and jobs off the lien path, the two prints, and the leader's foot.
- **A pressed stage makes its papers the screen.** The list is narrowed to it through the pick a bin's head always made. The Board then draws one pinned line and nothing else: **‹ The flow**, where the stage stands (*Monthly notices, stage 4 of 8*), its name and count, and its column's cells with the one a person is in lit. A cell is a press 44 px tall that moves the pick to that stage. The bin's own press stands on the line with its lines and doors, so no act the open bins offered is lost.
- **The line stays at the top while the list scrolls.** It is `position: sticky` on the desk's scroller. The list's day headings stick under it: the Board tells the desk its height (`onPinned`), the desk sets `--lien-pinned-top` on the scroller, and the headings' `top` reads it. With nothing pinned it is the top, as before.
- **The way back.** **‹ The flow** puts the flow back with that stage lit. The phone's own Back does the same.

## The phone's Back

The page keeps the picked stage in the history entry's state, beside the open row it has kept since punch list #352 (`lienDeskStateWithStage`, `lienDeskStageFromState` in `lienPhoneJobScreen.ts`).

- A pressed stage is an entry of its own at the same address. Back returns to the flow.
- A cell takes the entry's place, so Back is still one step to the flow.
- A job opened from a stage keeps the stage in its entry. Back from the job returns to that stage's papers, the line still pinned.
- Another view drops the stage with the row (`lienDeskStateForView`).
- Without the page's handlers the desk keeps the pick itself, as it does for the row.

## How it is built

- `src/lib/jobs/lienBoardFlow.ts`, new and pure: the view and where it is kept, a stage and its tone from a bin, the pinned line for a picked stage, the day's line, and the path of the arrow across.
- `src/components/jobs/LienBoardPhoneFlow.tsx`, new: the switch, the day's line, the flow and the pinned line.
- `LienBoard.tsx`: the tiles, the prints row, the empty words and the pick line are constants both branches draw. A phone returns its own section. The fold and its state are gone. `onPinned` is new.
- `useLienDeskBoard.ts`: `setBoardStage` sets a stage and never toggles it, and brings the scroller to its top.
- `LienDesk.tsx`: `flowStageKey`, `onOpenFlowStage`, `onCloseFlowStage`, and the scroller's `--lien-pinned-top`. `LienDeskPage.tsx` keeps the stage in the entry. `LienDeskNextUp.tsx`: the day heading's `top`.

## What differs from the mock-up

- The switch stands at the top of the Board in both views. The mock-up drew it under the tiles on By day. A switch that moved when pressed would be worse.
- The line's small words read *Monthly notices, stage 4 of 8*, with a comma. The mock-up drew a dot between them, which the plain-words rule does not allow inside a sentence.
- **The pinned line is not drawn on a job's own screen.** That screen is left as it is. Back from it returns to the stage with the line pinned, which is the place kept.
- A tile's pick, the leader's papers or the day's red line, keeps the line under the tiles it always had. Only a stage of the flow draws the pinned line.
