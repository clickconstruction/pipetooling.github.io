# v2.7447 — Liens: the last day of work can be set earlier than the clock hours (2026-11-16)

Punch list #335, the first of the two *Also seen* items the owner answered on 2026-11-14 (*allow it*), with his call of 2026-11-16 on how early: **any past day, with a second look**.

## What was wrong

Since v2.7193 a last day of work set by hand is the last day on the lien clock, and hours approved after it do not move it. The rule that saves the day still refused any day earlier than the last approved clock day (`lienLastWorkDayProblem`). So once later hours were approved, the office could clear a hand day or set a later one, and could not type or correct an earlier one. A warranty call clocked a month after the work ended fixed the job's last month for good.

## What changed

- **The rule** (`src/lib/jobs/lienLastWorkDay.ts`): `lienLastWorkDayProblem(day, todayYmd)` refuses a day that is not a day and a day after today, and nothing else. It no longer reads the clock hours, so its second argument is gone.
- **The window** (`lienLastWorkPreview` in `lienLastWorkDayPreview.ts`, drawn by `LienLastWorkDayConfirm`): for a day earlier than the hours it says *The hours run to Oct 20, after this day. They stay as they are and do not move it.* and names the months with hours that leave the lien clock. The table gains a row, **Months off the clock**, drawn only when a side has one. The affidavit row reads *already past* when the new day puts its last day behind today.
- **The second look** (`verdict: 'look'`, the button reads *Set the day anyway*) for an earlier day when any of these holds:
  - it is 60 days or more from the last clocked day (`LIEN_LAST_WORK_FAR_DAYS`, as for a later day);
  - it is before the first month with clock hours, when the caller holds the job's clocked months;
  - it takes away a month a recorded notice names (`noticedMonths`), or the last month of an affidavit already approved or filed (`affidavitMonths`, new, from `lienLastWorkAffidavitMonths` on the Lien desk and from the filed affidavit's months in the Lien window);
  - it puts the affidavit's last day to file in the past, where it was ahead.
- **A reason is still required**, and the same people set the day as before (the lien office on the desk and in the Lien window, anyone who can edit the job in Edit Job).
- **Nothing else moves.** The months on the clock were already the hand day's since v2.7193 (`lienClockMonths` and the four SQL readers of migration `20261113190000`), so no reader and no migration changes. The database never refused an earlier day: the write is one update on `jobs_ledger`.

## The dev seat

`plan_last_work_day` runs the window's kernels from the mirror (`supabase/functions/_shared/gcKernels/`, regenerated here), so it plans an earlier day too and holds one that needs the second look until `anyway: true`. Its reply's verdict for that reads *a second look, set anyway*. Only the verb's description and `_shared/devMcpLastWorkDay.ts`'s words changed; the server keeps its number. **`dev-mcp` is deployed after the merge**, or the seat keeps refusing an earlier day.

## Tests

`lienLastWorkDay.test.ts` (the rule), `lienLastWorkDayPreview.test.ts` (seven cases for an earlier day and the affidavit's months), `LienLastWorkDayLine.render.test.tsx` (an earlier day saves with its reason), `devMcpLastWorkDay.test.ts` (the seat plans it and holds the second look).

## Not built

- The Legal desk and the firm's page still date a job from its hours, not the hand day (`legalLastWorkBasis`). An earlier hand day widens that gap. It stays on #335's *Also seen* list.
- An earlier day on a job with no clock hours gets no second look, as before.
