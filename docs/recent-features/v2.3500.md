# v2.3500 — Supply house credits, step 1: the readers learn about a sign (2026-09-16)

Client + two edge function renderers. **No migration, no user-visible change.** First of four PRs from [`to-dos/supply-house-credits/README.md`](../../to-dos/supply-house-credits/README.md).

## Why this lands first

`supply_house_invoices` carries `CHECK (amount >= 0)`, and because it always has, **this is the only money stream in the app with no sign guard of its own**. Card charges take an absolute value everywhere, customer payments filter to amounts above zero, payroll remaining and the collections balance are floored at zero. About twenty-five readers were written against the constraint.

So the constraint stays shut in this PR and opens in v2.3502. Everything here is provably inert on today's data: no negative row can exist while it lands, and the live proof is that no figure moved.

## The rule the whole train applies

> An **owed** number counts invoices only; a credit is carried in its own field beside it. A **cost** number nets, because the job really did cost less.

## Kernels

- [`supplyHouseAging.ts`](../../src/lib/supplyHouseAging.ts) — `SupplyHouseAgingRow` gains `creditsOpen` / `net`, the matrix gains `creditsTotal` / `netTotal`. A negative amount goes to `creditsOpen` and never touches a bucket, `total`, or `missingDueDateCount`. The row filter becomes `total > EPSILON || creditsOpen < -EPSILON` — **the fault that mattered**: the old `total > EPSILON` let one credit drop a house off the table along with its genuinely past-due invoices. `countSupplyHousesPastDue60` and `supplyHouseAgingPhoneNote` needed no edit once buckets are invoice-only, and tests pin that.
- [`dashboardFinancials.ts`](../../src/lib/dashboardFinancials.ts) — `buildApBucket` tested `amount <= EPSILON`, which discarded a credit outright: it never reduced payables and no excluded count said so. Now `Math.abs(amount) <= EPSILON`, with the sublabel *Supply credit*.
- [`jobAccountsFlow.ts`](../../src/lib/materials/jobAccountsFlow.ts) — credits accumulate into `suppliersCredits` (row + house group) instead of `suppliersOwed`, so `classifyJobAccount`, `held`, `holdingTotal` and `holdingOnJobAccount` keep today's exact behaviour and a job with a real unpaid invoice cannot net its way out of the `owe_suppliers` queue. Unallocated credits count in `unallocatedCredits`, apart from `unallocatedTotal`, so they cannot shrink the dollars-missing alarm.
- [`fetchJobMaterialsCostSnapshot.ts`](../../src/lib/fetchJobMaterialsCostSnapshot.ts) — `jobAccountSplitFromLines` skips credits. It is an **exposure**, deliberately the opposite of `supplyInvoiceTotal` in the same file, which nets. Netting here would drop `flaggedDollars` under the `> 0.005` gate in `billTabJobAccountNote` and take away the owner-account warning.
- [`moneyfillWeekClose.ts`](../../src/lib/moneyfillWeekClose.ts) — the gap arithmetic extracted as `supplyInvoiceGapDollars(amount, allocatedPct)` and switched to a magnitude. The old `Math.max(0, …)` floored a credit to zero, so an unallocated credit read as *1 supply invoice · $0*.

## Edge functions (redeploy both)

- `weekly-money-email-dispatch` — `money()` formatted with `Math.abs` and no sign, so a negative money-out printed as money out. Each formatter now takes the magnitude itself and supplies its own prefix (`money` a minus when negative, `signed` a minus or a plus).
- `paid-job-email` — **the sharpest one.** Charge events are built as `delta: -amount` and payments as `delta: amount`, and the month group split on the *sign*, so a credit memo (positive delta) would have rendered as a customer payment on a green row. `TimelineRowEvent` gains `kind: 'charge' | 'payment'` and both the split and the row background read the kind.

## UI

- Aging table: a **Credits open** column and footer, appearing only once a credit exists, with the last column reading *Owed* instead of *Total* when it does. The phone balance bar now guards its division — `row.total` is owed-only and can be zero on a house listed for its credits.
- Materials → Job Accounts: a green `$X credit` chip beside the paid/owed line on any row holding one.

## Tests

19 new cases across the five kernels, each written against negative fixtures and each confirmed to fail without the fix (5 of 6 in the aging suite; the sixth is the no-credits regression test, which passes both ways by design).

## Verified

`npm run typecheck`, `npm run lint`, the theme-token check and the five touched suites all green. Live pass on real data: Materials → Supply Houses, Materials → Job Accounts and the dashboard payables drill-down all read exactly as before, which is the whole proof for this PR.
