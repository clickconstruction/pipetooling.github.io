# v2.4581 — Procurement log: four steps and a Next line at the top (2026-10-05)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. PR 1 of the procurement log redraw (*Procurement log: orders on a calendar*; its card was PR #4527, closed unmerged once the train shipped, with the spec, the working page and the pictures on its branch): sections 1 to 6 of the spec, the top of the card. The rows under it are as they were; the orders, the calendar and the phone cards are PRs 2 to 4.

## What changed

- **One card.** The sentence the tab drew over the log (*9 rows have no call from the reviewer yet… Enter their approval…*) is gone. Its door is on the Next line (`onEnterApproval`).
- **The top row.** *Procurement log* at the left. At the right: the last update (*Last update 10/19 to structura*, or *No update sent yet. The first one sends every row.*), **Send update…**, **+ Add item** and **⋯**, a menu holding *Print the log*, *Download CSV*, *Open in Google Sheets* and *Updates sent (N)*. Once an update has gone and lines have changed since, the button reads **Send update · 14 changes**. The send card and the list of updates open under the top row.
- **Four steps**, each a button: *Waiting on the GC* (`awaiting`, `sent_back`, `not_submitted`; *5 sent back* in amber), *To order* (`released`; *first by 10/27*, or *nothing approved yet*), *On order* (`ordered`; *1 late* in red, else *next arrives 11/03*), *On site* (`delivered`; *last 09/29*). Every line is counted once, by its status. A step pressed shows only its lines under a blue bar, *Waiting on the GC: 44 parts. The other lines are hidden.*, with **Show every line**. A thin bar under the strip shows each step's share.
- **The Next line.** *Next: Nothing can be ordered until the GC answers. They sent 5 parts back. 39 more wait on their answer.* or *Next: Order 6 parts by 10/27, tomorrow. 1 part on order arrives late. The GC sent 3 parts back.* It replaces the To order lens's own line.
- **Before you can order** is one line on a grey ground, amber only when a part that can be ordered now lacks a lead time or a stage. It names a job with no stage dates (**Open the job**), each count (still the link that shows only those lines) with **Set…**, and a fixture with no product (**Open it**). **Set…** opens a small form in place, *Lead time for 43 parts* with **Set on 43**, or the three stages, or the houses. It replaces *Tick the 43*.
- **The lens row** says once what every line shares: *Every part comes from National Wholesale.*
- **No row is painted as changed.** The amber wash is gone, and so is its sentence under the table.

## Two things that were wrong

- The old top line counted a delivered part as ordered too (*3 ordered · 3 delivered* for three parts) and left out the parts that wait. The steps count each line once. Step 8's folded summary reads the same four counts now.
- Before the first update every row with a status was painted amber, because with no update to compare against every row is new. Nothing is painted, and the count shows on the button only after an update has gone.

## How it is built

- [`src/lib/submittals/procurementBoard.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/procurementBoard.ts), new: `procurementSteps`, `rowsForStep`, `stepOnlyWords`, `stepShares`, `procurementNextLine`, `blockersPress`, `sharedHouse`, `sendUpdateLabel`.
- `SubmittalProcurementPanel.tsx`: the new top; `stepPicked` beside the blocker filter (one at a time); `saveFacts` shared by the tick bar and `setOnBlocker`; `onCounts` also hands `steps`. `procurementCounts` is unchanged: the journey's Procure pill still reads released against delivered.
- `BidsSubmittalsTab.tsx`: the sentence over the log goes; `onEnterApproval` is handed while a row has no call.

## Where the build differs from the spec

- *No house* keeps its place among the missing facts, with **Set…** and a house picker. The drawing's bid has one house on every line, so it never showed.
- A bid that is not a job yet reads *The bid is not a job yet, so no part has a needed date*, with no door.
- The draft line (*This version is a draft, so nothing is released yet…*) stays under the Next line. The spec does not speak of it.
- Approved parts with no order-by date read *2 parts are approved and not ordered.* on the Next line.
