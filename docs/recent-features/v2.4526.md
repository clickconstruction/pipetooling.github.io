# v2.4526 — Lien desk Calendar: an overdue job is listed with its property (2026-10-04)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. The owner saw the same customer and address in **Overdue** and in **This month** and asked whether a job with something due this month needs to show in Overdue. No job was in both (each is counted once, v2.4340); the same *property* was — 628 Terrell Rd had four jobs whose window had closed and three with a notice due Oct 15. The mock-up picked the same day: keep the counts, list the overdue job with its property.

## What changed

- **Kernel** — [`lienCalendarBuckets.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienCalendarBuckets.ts) `placeOverdueWithProperty`, run at the end of `buildLienCalendarBoard`. A job in a later bucket with a date still ahead (`lienNextDate`) hosts its property; an Overdue job at that property is listed under the host's group, in the bucket of the property's earliest date. This month first, then Next month, then Later. A filed lien has no date ahead and hosts nothing.
- **One property** — `lienSameProperty`: the same linked record (`addressId`), the same typed address (`lienAddressKey`, now exported from `lienCalendar.ts` and shared with `sharedPropertyNote`), or one typed address that is the other cut short at a word (*628 Terrell Rd* and *628 Terrell Rd, San Antonio, TX*). The short one must name a number and two more words; a city alone matches nothing.
- **What does not move** — Overdue's `jobs` and `total` (the pill: 17 · $37.6k on the day) and each group's `jobs`, `total`, word, flags and *Draft the N*. A listed job's money is not added to the group it sits under.
- **What the board draws** — a group that lists any gets `byProperty`; `lienGroupRows` flattens it to a heading per property (the address, nothing from the state on), that property's jobs, its overdue jobs greyed (biggest first), then *Other properties*. [`LienDeskCalendarTab.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskCalendarTab.tsx) draws them on the desktop axis and in the phone list (`PropertyLabel`, `closedHere` on `JobRow`). The group's grey line gains *· N windows closed*, said before *N at one property*.
- **Overdue** lists the jobs with nothing ahead at their property; its bar reads *17 jobs · 10 listed here · 7 are listed with their property* (or *… listed under This month* when they all went to one bucket). The Overdue pill picked on its own draws `whole`: every overdue job, the old facts.

## Tests

`lienCalendarBuckets.test.ts` (+6): the counts and money hold; what Overdue lists and says; the clustered rows and the group's own numbers; a group that lists none; a property followed into Next month; the property match and a filed lien. The fixture's jobs now sit at their own street numbers. `LienDeskCalendarTab.render.test.tsx` (+1): the row under its property, greyed, in order; the bar; Overdue under All and picked alone. Seen in the dev preview on the live desk: 7 of 17 overdue jobs listed with their property (5 under This month, 1 Next month, 1 Later), pills unchanged. Nothing was written.
