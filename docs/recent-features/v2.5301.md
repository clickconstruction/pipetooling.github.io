# v2.5301 — GC mode: Papers on a job shows every company’s papers, and what is missing first (2026-10-12)

Messages build, wave 2, cluster 6, PR E of six: Papers on this job (cut 4; the prototype's v1.176, `job-papers-mockups.html` B, with v1.175's list), and the round window (v1.168). It's stacked on PR D (v2.5298).

- **Where**: each project's card on [GC projects](/gc) has **Papers** for the office team (the read's audience). It opens `GcJobPapersWindow.tsx` at `?papers=<project>`, the way Draws and Bill open (the lead's answer 2).
- **The read**: `useJobPapers(projectId)` makes one `flow_rows_kind` call with the project and no kind (helper 4's), so it gets everyone on the job. Each kind is read on the office's flow (`loadFlowDef`), and each person's papers for this job (`jobPaperCompanyOf`).
- **The window** draws `flows/jobPapers.ts`:
  - the counts as pills that filter;
  - **Before bid day** on a job in pursuit, until bid day and until our bid is sent;
  - a table per kind with a column per paper and a dot per cell, rows with something wrong first;
  - the legend.
  
  **List** is behind a tab (`jobPaperList.ts`, new): each company's reached papers with their state, the step each finishes and where the person is. "went, waiting on them" is only for a paper we write. Theirs reads its day. **Before bill day** waits on helper 7's one-job gates (the lead's answer 7).
- **The presses**: a dot or a List button opens the person's own window at the paper (`boardOpen.ts`). Ask for it, Send it and Remind open with the send. **Open the compare** closes the window and goes to the project's card.
- **The round window** (`roundWindow.ts`, new; `GcRoundWindow.tsx`; `GcRoundOpenerContext` in `gcRoundOpener.ts`, provided by the page). Helper 4's flow panel opens it from a round pill with the person's reading in hand (the lead's answer 8). The panel on main has no round pills yet, so nothing opens it until they land. It's keyed off facts:
  - a change order: its facts, the money only when passed in, whether they opened it, Ask again, and See their page once cut 8's page is there;
  - a pay application: what it covers, the amount, the certificate, paid with its waivers and the trades' day, Open the application and The trades on it;
  - any other round: its day, Open, and Ask again or Remind.
  
  The page sends Open to Change orders or Bill the customer, The trades on it to Money's Bill day, and Ask again to the person's window with the send.
  
  The panel opens a round with the reading only, since `flow_rows` carries days and marks. So the page fills the detail from its own project state (`roundDetail.ts`, new): the change order by the round's key and the pay application by its number. What it adds, the sum after and the amount go in only for the money team.
- **Needs a hand today lands on the late companies** (the lead's call, 2026-10-13): since #5478 Follow up opens on the people waiting on us, so a board's **Needs a hand today** opens it at **We are waiting on them** instead of its top. The section carries `GC_WAITING_ON_THEM_ID` (`links.ts`) as its id, the board's press carries it as the hash (`boardNeedsAHand` in `GcProjects.tsx`), and the page scrolls there once the list is drawn, once per press. A pill's `?view=` drops the hash. The board guide's tile line says so.
- **Known edge, the boards' Flow tab**: a board row is one contact, but the company's or customer's window finds whose flow to show by its record (`findFlowContactId` in `flowPersonIo.ts`: the record's contact with no name of its own first, else the first by name). Once a company has two contacts on its flow, a press on the second one's row opens the window on the first one's flow. Whoever gives a company its second contact on a flow should pass the row's contact id through to the window.
