# v2.3146 — Help: which stage kind to pick (2026-09-08)

Docs only. The owner, reading the Stage Plan train the evening it shipped, asked what separates Order, Any and — and when to use "not a stage" — the question a new assistant will ask first. The guide *split a job into stages and bill stage by stage* (roles: assistant, master_technician) had three one-line bullets; it now carries:

- **Which kind do I pick?** — two questions (does someone go on site for it and would you want to know when it's done; if so, does it wait its turn), then a table of the common rows: the four Order stages, two change orders as Any, permit / dumpster / mobilization as —.
- **One bill with all three kinds** — the sample job ($44,420, eight units for a GC) and how it bills over its life: draw 1 on Rough-in passing, the change order billing on its own the day it's done, draw 2 (or *waits on stage 1*), the permit line ticked into the last draw, done when draw 3 is paid.
- **Does the customer see a — line?** — yes on the invoice (every invoice lists its line items at real prices; the scope note prints on the Stripe line); no on the GC's stage card, which shows stages only. Billing a permit on its own the day it's pulled stays possible — *with the final draw* is a suggestion, not a lock.

Keywords gain *not a stage*, *permit*, *change order*, *which kind*.
