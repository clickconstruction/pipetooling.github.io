# v2.3672 — Materials by stage, PR 2: the boxes on the sheet and the Stages panel (2026-09-21)

Client only, on top of [v2.3671](./v2.3671.md) (the migration must be pushed before this ships — the client reads the table as "no splits" while it is missing, but writes would fail). PR 2 of 4; PR 3 the paper, PR 4 the book / assembly remembering.

## What shipped

- **The boxes** — [`StageSplitChips.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/StageSplitChips.tsx) (4 render smokes): 1 · 2 · 3, solid when the scope has its own split, dashed when it inherits, `½ · ½` / `70 · 30` beside them (click to type the shares), ↺ on a line or part to follow the scope above again. Click = `toggleStage` (add / drop, re-even), Shift-click = exclusive, the number keys on a focused box.
- **On the sheet** — [`SortableRoughPartLineRow.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/SortableRoughPartLineRow.tsx): the fixture cell (with *N lines have their own*), under every part line's name (and under a bundle line's name), and on every part inside an expanded bundle. The `<tr>` takes 1 / 2 / 3 (not while typing in a field) for the fixture. The no-lines row gets the fixture boxes too. Sheet and One at a time both render through `renderRoughLinesTable`, so both have them.
- **The Stages panel** — [`TakeoffStagesPanel.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/TakeoffStagesPanel.tsx) (3 render smokes), slotted into [`TakeoffCostRailView`](../../src/components/bids/TakeoffCostRailView.tsx) under *What Pricing sees* via the new `stagesPanel` prop: raw → × factor per stage, the total, the factor field (company default vs *this bid*; typing the company number back clears the override; 1–5 only), the staged sentence (`32 of 33 costed fixtures staged · 1 still needs a stage ($191.52) · 2 lines with their own`), the shares, **Fill from rules** with `describeRulePlan`'s sentence.
- **The tab** — [`BidsTakeoffTab.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/BidsTakeoffTab.tsx): loads the splits + the company factor with the bid (rough model only), `stageSummary` from `computeMaterialsByStage` over the same count rows, lines, coverage rounding and cached bundle parts the rail already has; `setStageSplit` (optimistic, rolls back on error), `fillStagesByRules` (`planRuleFill` → `saveFixtureSplitsBatch` → reload → note), `setBidSovFactor` (`bids.sov_material_factor`).
- **Help** — *stage a takeoff for a schedule of values*.
