# v2.6420 — The job form's billing save keeps the job's line ids (2026-11-02)

Punch list #237 (`to-dos/stage-checklist-document/README.md`): the gap v2.6350 read from the code and left on the card, closed before the office types questions. No migration: the client and a bed.

## The defect

`writeBillingSlice` ([`src/lib/jobs/jobFormSliceWrites.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobFormSliceWrites.ts)) deleted every `jobs_ledger_fixtures` row of the job and re-inserted the form's rows one at a time, and `fixtureInsertRows` carried no `id`, so every autosave of the billing slice minted new line ids. The schema took the rest, and nothing on prod protected the rows: no trigger re-links them and no constraint is deferred.

- `ON DELETE CASCADE` on `fixture_id` deleted every `stage_checklist_answers` row, every job item on `stage_checklist_templates`, every `stage_checklist_skips` row and every `job_stage_windows` row on the job.
- `ON DELETE SET NULL` took the stage off every `report_photos` and `job_photo_notes` row that named one.
- `progress_pct`, `progress_at`, `progress_by`, `progress_report_id` and `created_at` were never in the payload, so a stage's crew-reported progress and the line's date reset on every save.
- The activity trigger `jobs_ledger_fixtures_to_activity` dedupes *Specific work added* by the line's id, so every save wrote it again for every line.

One edit to a job's Specific Work, or any edit that dirtied the billing slice, and the stage checklists on the job were gone. Prod held no answer, question or window when this was written (2026-11-02), so nothing has been lost. The materials slice had the same defect until v2.6111; the payments slice has diffed since v2.1121 (B5).

## What changed

- **`diffFixtureRows`** ([`src/lib/jobs/fixtureRowsDiff.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/fixtureRowsDiff.ts)), beside `paymentRowsDiff.ts`: the lines worth keeping (named, as `fixtureInsertRows` filters them), each under its own id with every column the slice owns, in form order, and the ids to keep. `fixtureInsertRows` carries `id` now, as `materialInsertRows` has since v2.6111: a hydrated row keeps the id the database gave it, a new row the one the form minted (`crypto.randomUUID()`). Every upsert row carries the same columns, the five budget columns as null where nothing is stored, since a bulk upsert writes the union of its rows' columns and a row missing one is written as null, which is what the re-insert left them at. Nothing the slice does not own is named, so an update leaves `created_at` and the progress columns as they are.
- **`writeBillingSlice`** upserts the lines in one call (`upsert(rows, { onConflict: 'id' })`), then deletes the job's lines not among them (`.delete().eq('job_id', …).not('id', 'in', '(…)')`); with nothing worth keeping it only deletes. The upsert runs first, so a refused write leaves the old lines standing with everything on them; before, a refused insert after the delete left the job with no lines until the next save. Invoice links (`invoice_id`) and a discount's `discount_basis_positions`, which key on `sequence_order`, are written as before. The office roles' update policy on `jobs_ledger_fixtures` admits the upsert's update path, which the bed runs as an assistant.
- **The form's rows stay the whole truth of the job's lines.** The payments diff keeps rows born mid-edit; this one does not, by choice: a discount's basis positions and an invoice's link by position key on `sequence_order` across the job's rows, so a line the form does not hold is not kept beside them. A line another door writes while the form is open (a tip from a deposit, a check fee, an applied estimate) is removed by the next save, as it was before; the form's own doors re-read the lines after they write.
- **Tests**: `fixtureRowsDiff.test.ts` (the ids kept and the order, nothing worth keeping, a discount's positions over the kept rows, one column set for every row, no column the slice does not own); `jobFormSliceWrites.test.ts` (the order upsert then delete, the payload and the delete filter, nothing worth keeping, a refused upsert deletes nothing, a refused delete is thrown); `jobFormAutosaveSlices.test.ts` (the id rides the payload); `useJobFormAutosaveEngine.render.test.tsx` (the engine's order, an edited line and the lines re-read from the database going up under their own ids, a refused line-item write deleting no line).
- **The bed** `supabase/tests/stage_checklist_items/50_billing_save_keeps_lines.sql`, run last by `scripts/pgtest-stage-checklist-items.sh`: two jobs seeded alike, each with a Rough In carrying an answer, a job item, a skip, a window, a report photo, a job photo and the crew's 40%, made weeks ago; the save before v2.6420 replayed on one as the office under RLS, exactly as PostgREST wrote it, and the save since on the other.
- Docs: `JOB_FORM_MODAL_ARCHITECTURE.md` (the billing slice's row, the tables, gotchas 1 and 33), the v2.6350 migration page's *what this does not cover*, card #237.

## What the bed shows

On the job that got the old save: no line under its old id; the Rough In's answer, job item, skip and window gone; the report photo and the job photo with no stage; the job's sheet, Subs tab and photo lists reading nothing on a stage; the new Rough In made today with no progress; and *Specific work added* once more for every line. On the job that got the new save: the Rough In under its id with its date, its 40% and its new price; every row on it standing and unchanged, compared whole; the Trim Set the office took off gone; the Water heater the office added under the id the form minted; and one *Specific work added*, for the new line. Beds run only in CI. The bed's first run, on the PR, passed all 23 of its lines ([run 41586470995](https://github.com/clickconstruction/pipetooling.github.io/actions/runs/41586470995)): read the `ok:` lines of the `stage-checklist-items` job.

## Verify

Open a job with a stage that has a window or an answered checklist question, change a line's price, wait for the autosave and reload: the window, the answers and the stage's progress read as before, and `select id, created_at, progress_pct from jobs_ledger_fixtures where job_id = …` shows the same ids and dates. The job's Activity writes no new *Specific work added* for a line that was already there. `npm test -- fixtureRowsDiff jobFormSliceWrites jobFormAutosaveSlices`.
