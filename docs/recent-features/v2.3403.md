# v2.3403 — A job's customer and GC are never the same party (2026-09-14)

Client + the shared payer kernel (`supabase/functions/_shared/billToParty.ts` — **redeploy its importers**: `create-stripe-invoice`, `preview-stripe-invoice`, `send-stripe-invoice`, `send-physical-invoice-email`, `customer-portal`, `submit-portal-request`, `send-gc-statement-email`). No migration here; the sweep of the existing rows and the trigger are v2.3404.

## Why

Owner, 2026-09-14, after the Property record fix on J890 (RMC- Dudley Mason as both customer and GC): "I don't think someone should be the homeowner and GC. Once someone is set as the GC it should take them off customer, and when someone is set as a customer they should be taken off as GC — and for people that are listed as both we should clean up by moving them to GC." On 2026-09-14 the ledger held 72 such jobs (RMC 23, Knight 13, H & I 6, Palmer 6 …; 34 billed, 15 paid, 15 working, 8 waiting), 71 of them already on the *gc* bill-to rule from the v2.3350 sweep, and zero jobs with a GC and no customer — so "GC only" is a state the app had never held.

## What shipped

- **Kernel [`jobPartyExclusive.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobPartyExclusive.ts)** (+8 tests): `pickJobGc` / `pickJobCustomer` (the move rule), `jobPartyMoveNotice` (the toast), `isGcOnlyJob`, `jobHasBillingParty`, `jobPartyName` (customer, else the GC standing alone).
- **The form** ([`JobFormModal`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormModal.tsx)): every customer / GC *pick* — the Customer row's picker, the GC/Builder picker, the project link, **Import from bid**, **Link a bid** — goes through `pickCustomerId` / `pickGcCustomerId`. Picking the GC that is the customer clears the customer link and its copies (name, email, phone, date met), sets **Bills go to: GC**, and toasts *RMC- Dudley Mason is now the GC on this job, not the customer…*; picking the customer that is the GC drops the GC (a *gc* rule falls back to *customer*). Load and undo use the raw setters, so opening a job never rewrites it. **A job from a won bid is a GC job**: the GC is the GC, bills go to the GC, and the customer stays whatever the office set — the v2.1182 copy of the GC into the customer row is gone.
- **GC jobs read as such**: the Edit tab's Customer row says *none · GC job — RMC- Dudley Mason is the party* (no "Not in Customers" warning); `jobBillToPartyOptions` offers only *GC* (no *This customer*, no *Split by line*); `effectiveInvoiceParty` returns `gc` for a GC with no customer link whatever the rule or the invoice's pick says (typed *someone else* still wins), so Bill Customer, the Stripe / physical edge functions, the portals, GC Review and the promise payer all bill the GC.
- **Fix-up lists**: the Stages board's *No customer* list skips jobs that name a GC; the *No email* list skips jobs billed to the GC (their email is the GC's billing email, not the job's customer copy).
- **Party names**: the billed-by-customer breakdown, the Job Summary's customer cut, the Subs work board rows and the quick-send / quick-assign rows show the GC's name for a GC job instead of *No customer*.
- Guide *choose who gets the bill on a job* → Good to know; `GLOSSARY.md` → GC on jobs.
