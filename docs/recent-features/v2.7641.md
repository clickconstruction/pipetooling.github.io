## What is new

- **`job_required_papers`**: one row per paper a job requires, with the paper's words as they stood in the Contract Book the day the office required it. `removed_at` takes a paper off and keeps history. It ends with the four fence calls.
- **`person_contract_documents.job_id` and `.job_required_paper_id`**: one person's copy of one required paper. A signed copy is what opens the job to that person.
- **The lock question**: `job_closed_to_me(job)` and `jobs_closed_to_me()`. Both look at the live required papers first and read nothing else when there are none.
- **Seven more functions**: `job_papers_mine` (the prompt's read), `job_paper_start` (a person's own copy), `job_papers_require` and `job_paper_remove` (the office's writes), `job_papers_roster` (who has signed), `job_papers_needing_me` (the Needs you read), and the internal `job_papers_for_person` and `job_paper_job_number`.
- **The `job-required-papers` SQL bed**: `scripts/pgtest-job-required-papers.sh`, `supabase/tests/job_required_papers/`, a line in `scripts/sql-beds.txt` and `npm run test:pg:job-required-papers`.

## Four choices worth knowing

- **The job keeps the paper's words.** A required paper copies the Book entry's name, body, link and form when the office requires it. Everyone on the job signs the same words, and editing the Book later changes nothing a job already asks for. New words mean taking the paper off and requiring it again, which asks for new signatures.
- **A job's copy carries the job's number in its name**, like *Site rules (job J100)*. The Book's own paths find a person's paper by name: a packet assigned, a Book save that mints a new version of a signed paper, a rename. None of them ever takes a job's copy for the person's general one.
- **The person is found by `people.account_user_id`, never by name.** A login tied to no roster person reads as owing and cannot start a paper. The roster marks that row so the office can link it on People.
- **Anyone signed in can read a job's number and its papers' names, and sign.** That is all the prompt shows before signing, and the clock-in search can lead anyone to a job.
