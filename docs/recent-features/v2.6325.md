# v2.6325 — Read panels say read words when the signal drops (2026-11-01)

Punch list #224, whole. v2.5962 put a Retry panel on every crew read and named this gap: the lost-signal sentence on a read was the write sentence, *No connection — the app couldn't reach the server, so nothing was saved. Check your signal and try again.* A read saved nothing, so the words were wrong, and #163's live test of 2026-10-30 found the sentence under a clock-out kept on the phone, where it read as the press failing. v2.6198 gave `offlineRecoveryState` and `OfflineRetryPanel` an `offlineMessage` door and the clock its own words, and its live check of 2026-10-31 still saw the write sentence on the Dashboard's Crew Day panel. This PR gives every read panel read words through the kernel, with no change at the sites. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## The kernel

- `OFFLINE_READ_MESSAGE` (`src/lib/networkErrorMessage.ts`): *No connection, so this could not load. Nothing was lost. It loads again when the signal is back.* Three sentences in the plain words of `plainWords.ts`, pinned by `networkErrorMessage.test.ts` (No connection, never *saved*, says it loads again, no plain-words failure).
- `offlineRecoveryState` draws it in the `offline` phase whenever `idempotent` is true, with *Still no connection.* appended on a second failure as before. A write keeps `OFFLINE_ERROR_MESSAGE`: the person re-sends it. A caller's own `offlineMessage` still wins, so the clock keeps `CLOCK_READ_OFFLINE_WORDS` (v2.6198) and My mail its two sentences. *Back online* reads as before for both kinds, and a refusal keeps its own sentence with no Retry.
- `formatErrorMessage` is unchanged: a failure's `message` still carries the write sentence as the formatter writes it, and the panel swaps the words by the panel's kind. Three smokes pin exactly that, feeding the write sentence in and reading the read sentence out (`CustomerSummaryModal`, `DispatchModeHome`, `DashboardMyScheduleSection.keptDay`).
