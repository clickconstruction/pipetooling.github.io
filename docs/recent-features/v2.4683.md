# v2.4683 — Job Parts Tally: the team's sorted charges sit on the day they were bought (2026-10-06)

Punch list #72 (`to-dos/tally-transactions-refresh.md`), the last place the posting day leaked into the team queue (v2.4654). Client only: no migration, no edge function. It reads `list_card_charges_window.purchased_at` (v2.4665, migration `20261006061356`, applied).

## What changed

- **The queue's history is keyed on the swipe** ([`tallyTeamQueue.ts`](../../src/lib/tally/tallyTeamQueue.ts), `tallyHistoryMadeAt` = `purchasedAt || postedAt`): the store runs and their 30-day lookback, the day's already-sorted charges (the *same-day sorted* chip) and the card each sorted line sits on all follow the day the card was used. A charge swiped Monday and posted overnight is one of Monday's sorted lines, as its unsorted neighbours already were (v2.4654).
- **Sorted lines show their swipe time again** ([`TallyTeamDayCard`](../../src/components/tally/TallyTeamDayCard.tsx)): *6:31 AM 7-Eleven $28.38 went to …*. v2.4654 dropped the time because only the posting time was there.
- **The read reaches past the newest day** (`tallyQueueHistoryRange`): `list_card_charges_window` filters on `posted_at`, so the queue asks for its newest swipe day + 3 (`TALLY_HISTORY_POSTING_LAG_DAYS`; posting lags a median 8 hours, 32 at the 90th percentile), and the store lookback before the oldest, within the read's 366 days.
- **Kernel rule set v3** ([`tallySortSuggestion.ts`](../../src/lib/tally/tallySortSuggestion.ts)): `TallySortedCharge.madeAt` (was `postedAt`) and the same-day fact's `madeAt`, so unsorted charges and history say the same word; `TALLY_SUGGESTION_RULES_VERSION = 3`.

## Rule set v3, measured

Replayed 2026-10-06 over the same 90 days (839 sorted charges, 542 sorting visits), each charge and its history on the swipe day. The likely rules read no history, so their table is v2's: `clock-one-job` 211 of 399, `schedule-one-job` 18 of 29, `clock-one-job-mixed` 9 of 11, `office-category` 14 of 16. **The day's chips** held the answer for 602 of the 839 at the cap of 5 (575 at 4, 609 at 6, 615 uncapped), against 605 in v2 with history on the posting day: the same-day chip now names where the swipe day's charges went, and past sorts followed the posting day the old Assign window showed. Agreement is with one sorter's choices, not correctness.
