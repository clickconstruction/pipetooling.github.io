# v2.5551 — People: the roster takes each person's number from their account (2026-10-15)

The owner, 2026-10-15, after v2.5546's *Which of ours?* showed the live roster with "no number on file" on nearly every row: "add the numbers on People for the roster." The numbers were on the accounts (`users.phone`) for sixteen roster rows, ten of them active accounts and six archived ones whose roster rows are still live; the roster had none of them. Migration `20261015030000_people_phone_from_account.sql`; no client change.

- **Once, for the roster as it stands**: `people_phones_from_accounts()` fills every roster row's blank phone from the account it is linked to (`people.account_user_id` → `users.phone`, trimmed), an archived account's too, never a sample's or a twin's; the migration runs it once, and it runs again without changing anything. A phone the office typed on the roster is never written over.
- **From here on**, two definer triggers (the person editing their own account may not write the roster): `people_phone_from_account` on `users` (after insert or update of `phone`) fills the blank phone on the roster rows linked to the account; `people_phone_from_account` on `people` (before insert or update of `account_user_id`) gives a roster row that gains an account the account's phone when the row has none. Clearing a phone on the roster is left alone: the people trigger fires on the account link, not on the phone.
- With v2.5546's seed and v2.5549's roster read in `quo_place_number`, a teammate whose account has a number is now known the moment they text; the ones with no number anywhere (on 2026-10-15: Behar, Darren, Grace, Isiah, Kyle, Micah and Zoe among the accounts, and the subs with no account) still come through *Who is this?*, where *One of our own team* writes the number onto the roster.
- **Tests**: a new SQL bed, `npm run test:pg:people-phone` (`supabase/tests/people_phone`, `scripts/pgtest-people-phone.sh`, a line in `scripts/sql-beds.txt`): the once-over fills two rows, the archived account's too and never a sample's or a twin's, and nothing the second time; an account that gains a phone fills its blank roster rows and not one typed; a row that gains an account takes its phone, a new row with its own keeps it, a cleared phone stays cleared; the three functions are the database's own. Docker is not running on this Mac, so it runs in CI.
- **Guide** *add someone to the roster*: the phone comes from the account when you leave it blank.
