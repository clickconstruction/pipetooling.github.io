# v2.8173 — Job Mode, short: Is it as told? shows without opening the job (2026-11-21)

Punch list #382, from the audit of PR 10 (v2.8110), on Taunya's go of 2026-11-21. Client only: `DashboardJobModeCard.tsx` and one prop on `JobModeDetailsSection.tsx`. No migration, no edge function.

## The fault: a question asked on arrival, behind a fold

**Is it as told?** leads the job's details in Job Mode, because it is asked before anything is unloaded. The short Job Mode page (punch list #384) draws the details only while **Open this job** is open. So the card was not on a superintendent's first screen, and it was not read at all until the row was pressed. The row's note names the customer, photos, notes, checklists and materials, and no question.

A superintendent set to Short could reach a stage on its first day, with a list to look at, and be asked nothing.

## The fix

On the short page the Job Mode card draws `JobModeArrivalCheck` itself, right above the **Open this job** row, for the job that row opens. It passes the details `arrivalAbove`, so they draw no second card when the row is open.

The card draws nothing when there is nothing to ask. The short page is the same one screen on every other day.

The whole Job Mode page is unchanged: the details still lead with the card.

## Checked

The short page's render test holds the card over the fold, above the row, on the row's job, and drawn once with the row open. It holds that the whole card leaves it to the details. A test of the details holds that they lead with the card, and draw none when the mount has it above. The guides *say whether a site is as we were told* and *clock in and out with Job Mode* say where the card sits on the short page.

Seen on the dev server at 390 px, light and dark, with the signed-in account answered as a superintendent on the Short view in the browser alone and a made-up ZZ list: the card above the row with the row folded, one card with the row open, and no card on a day with nothing to ask. No row was written.
