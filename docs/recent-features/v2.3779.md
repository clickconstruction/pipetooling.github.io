# v2.3779 — The job window on a phone: a bar that knows what is next (2026-09-23)

Punch list #30 (`to-dos/taunya-mobile.md`), **PR 2b** — the job window (surface #13), the second half of the PR 2 mock-up (`to-dos/taunya-mobile-pr2-pipeline.html`). Client only, no migration. Desktop and the plain (non-tabbed) job modal are untouched.

## What changed

- **Kernel `src/lib/jobs/jobWindowBar.ts`** (5 tests): `jobWindowVerb({ status, chip, bar })` — the bar's middle button from the same `jobNextLine` chip the Pipeline row shows plus the bill ledger (`buildEditJobBillingBar`): *Set % done* (red) · *Bill it* (a draw ready) · *Send bill…* (a draft waiting) · else the stage's forward move (*Move to Working*, *Ready to bill*, *Mark paid*; green when done-not-billed money exists); `null` on Paid. `viewerOnCrew(viewerId, teamMemberIds, scheduleAssigneeIds)`. `jobWindowTiles(bar)` — Job total · Billed (paid + open) · Paid.
- **`JobWindowActionBar`** — a `flexShrink: 0` sibling under `.job-window-body` (the tab row is already one), phones only (`useNarrowViewport640`): Status ▾ · the verb · Note. **`JobWindowStatusSheet`** — the Edit tab's `JobStatusStepper` in a bottom sheet, rail and guards intact (send-back reason, the Billed shell guard, Paid through Record payment).
- **`JobWindowModal`**: keeps the job the Job pane loads (`DetailJobModal`'s new `onJobLoaded`) and computes the verb (`pickJobNextChip` with the window's own readings — no crew or follow-up maps here). The verb's target: `bill` → the Bill tab; `pct` / Note → the Job tab and a `JOB_THREAD_COMPOSE_EVENT` the composer answers (opens the % editor, or scrolls to and focuses the note box); `status` → the sheet.
- **`DetailJobModal`** in pane mode at phone width (`phonePane`): the status chip beside the title (Collections when flagged), the three tiles after the customer panel, the street view banner folded behind a *street view ▸* link in the address line. **Arrived / Leaving** are passed to the composer only when the viewer is on the crew — a `team_members` row, or a schedule block assignee (`useJobThreadNotesForModal` now exposes `scheduleAssigneeIds` from the blocks it already fetches). Techs' plain modal (non-pane) keeps the buttons: they only see a job they are scheduled on.
- Help guide `job-window-tabs` (the phone bar), GLOSSARY *Job window on a phone*.

## Verify

Sample assistant via View as, 375 px → Pipeline → tap a Working row: the window opens with the bar (Status ▾ · Ready to bill · Note), the status chip in the title, Job total · Billed · Paid under the customer, no street view banner until *street view ▸*; no Arrived / Leaving in the composer (the assistant is not on the crew). Note → the note box focuses. Status ▾ → the rail; Close. A job with a bill out and no % → the verb reads Set % done and opens the % editor. Desktop: no bar, no chip, no tiles, street view as before.
