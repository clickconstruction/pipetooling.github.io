# v2.8613 — Customer portal window: a suggested address says it is not saved yet (2026-11-26)

Punch list #389, finding 180 of the walk of 2026-11-25 (sitting 18), briefed by the fixing thread as `389-portal-window-shows-an-address-that-is-not-saved.md` and answered by the owner on 2026-11-26: the window says so, way A. Client only. No migration, no edge function.

**Read.** On a ZZ Test customer with the portal just turned on, the window showed the address as `my.clickplumbing.com/<tail>`. Opened, the page read *This link is no longer active* while the portal was on. Nothing was wrong with the page: the address was never saved. `CustomerPortalGlobeButton.tsx` fills the box with a suggestion, the name plus a random tail, and saves it (`set_customer_portal_slug`) and locks it only when **Copy link** is pressed. The box drew the suggestion exactly as a saved address, with *Editable until first shared* under it, so a walker read the suggestion off the screen as the customer's address. The customer had no row in `customer_portal_slugs`.

**The mend.**

- `src/lib/portal/portalAddressLine.ts`, pure: the address's state is *suggested* (nothing ever saved), *saved* (a row, not yet shared) or *locked*. The line under the box is *Not saved yet. Copy link saves it.* for a suggestion, *Editable until first shared* for a saved one, and nothing once locked. A suggestion's line is drawn as a notice (`--text-amber-700`) and the address itself in `--text-muted`; a saved address reads as before.
- The window reads the state from `slugSaved` and `slugLocked`, which it already kept. When an address is saved or locked, the words change at once.
- The guide *share a customer their portal* says that nothing is saved until Copy link, and not to read the address to anyone before that press.

## What did not change

- When an address is saved and locked: **Copy link** alone, as before. The other way, saving the suggestion when the portal is turned on, was not taken: it would take a name in the shared namespace for every portal, wanted or not.
- The Advanced settings' **Address** row, the dice, the meter and the direct link.

## Tests

- `portalAddressLine.test.ts`: the three states, the line for each, and the colours.
- `CustomerPortalGlobeButton.render.test.tsx`: a fresh portal reads the not-saved line in a muted box and never *Editable until first shared*; a saved, unshared address reads the old line in the box's own colour; **Copy link** on a fresh portal saves and locks the suggestion, the box turns static, and both lines go.

## Not seen live

The words were not seen in a browser. The walk recipe is with the walking thread: open a ZZ Test customer's portal window with no saved address, read the line, press nothing.
