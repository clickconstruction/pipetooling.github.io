# v2.4369 — Accounts Receivable: Book it as Income (2026-10-02)

The mirror of v2.4363 (Close out books the label), from the board *Your call · a customer's payment booked as a tax refund* on the canvas https://claude.ai/artifact/SbFoRNPX4CY9qGF6foHmUR. Read Oct 1: the City of Seguin's $4,890 ACH paid bill #908 on Aug 26, and the Banking rule *City of Seguin - Taxes and Licenses* — it labels money in as well as out — booked it as a tax refund. Apply leaves an existing label alone by design (v2.3514), so the modal could only say *Labelled Taxes and Licenses in Banking, not Income* with nothing to press. One migration, no edge function.

## What changed

- **Migration** [`20261002060000_ar_book_applied_deposit_income.sql`](../../supabase/migrations/20261002060000_ar_book_applied_deposit_income.sql) ([doc](../migrations/20261002060000_ar_book_applied_deposit_income.md)): `mercury_transaction_ar_income_labels` gains `previous_label_id`, `relabelled_by` and source `relabel`; new `ar_book_applied_deposit_income(tx)`; `ar_unlabel_deposit_income` puts the replaced label back when the last payment leaves the deposit.
- **Kernel** [`arBankLabel.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/arBankLabel.ts) `arAppliedIncomeFix` + tests: the words for a deposit that paid a bill under another label, with the rule's short name when a rule did it.
- **Modal** [`BankPaymentsModal`](../../src/components/jobs/BankPaymentsModal.tsx): on such a deposit the amber box replaces the quiet note — *Banking books it as Taxes and Licenses. It paid a bill, so it is income.*, the rule line, and **Book it as Income** (the roles that can apply). The booking read (`ar_deposit_booking`, v2.4363) also loads for it, to name the rule. Render smoke [`BankPaymentsModal.bookIncome.render.test.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/BankPaymentsModal.bookIncome.render.test.tsx).
- Guide *match bank deposits to the bills they pay*: one bullet under *Booked as Income on its own*.

Not done here: the rule itself. Limiting *City of Seguin - Taxes and Licenses* to money going out is a Banking → Rules edit, and pressing the button on #908's deposit is the office's call.
