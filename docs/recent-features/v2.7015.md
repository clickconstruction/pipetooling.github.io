# v2.7015 — A teammate’s desk reads cleanly at every width (2026-11-12)

Grace's screenshots of the desk for the account *test* (People → Users, 600 px drawer): the Sign-in row's *signed in 6d ago* broke one word a line behind *Send sign-in email*, and the bio card's explainer sat flush on the card's left edge.

- **`DeskRow` (`src/components/personDesk/personDeskShared.tsx`)** is now two grid columns, label and body. The body is one wrapping flex line (`.person-desk-row-body` in `src/index.css`): the value keeps at least 10rem (`flex: 1 1 10rem`) and the presses (`margin-left: auto`) drop to their own line under it, still right-aligned, when they do not fit. The old third `auto` column grew to every press's max-content width and squeezed the value column to nothing. This fixes every desk section, not only Access & account: at 375 px Field's *None held* no longer runs under *Hand off…*, Paperwork's *Assign* no longer covers the packet menu, and Money's *No pay report yet* no longer breaks a word a line. The label lines up with the value's first line (`align-items: baseline`). The Access & account phone rule (`max-width: 640px`) keeps stacking the label over the body.
- **Header bands (`PersonDeskHeader.tsx`)**: each amber gap band holds its words on the left (the label over the reason) and its presses as one group on the right, which drops under the words on a narrow panel. The bands, chips and the *Day · week · month* row now span under the avatar too, so a phone gives them the panel's whole width.
- **Bio card (`PersonDeskBioSection.tsx`)**: the card's body pads like every other section (`0.5rem 0.7rem`); `DeskEmpty` takes `inset` so the empty line sits on the same edge as the explainer.
- **`PersonDeskBody.tsx`**: the section grid sets `align-content: start`, so a desk shorter than the panel no longer spreads its sections down it.

Checked with headless Chrome at 375, 768 and 1,400 px (the drawer is 600 px from 641 px up), light and dark: no element passes the panel's right edge.

No migration, no edge deploy.
