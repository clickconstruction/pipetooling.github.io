# v2.4550 — Submittals: step 1 is a few short rows, and the robot is one line (2026-10-05)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. The owner, on step 1 of the SpaceX bid: "this section at the top looks funny with the robots section taking up so much room but for a user them only really having one option which is to press a button… make this look much smaller where if the user hovered over it, it shows up bigger like a modal explanation." Then, on the first draft: "take it further and make it even more compact — the text *Optional. A tag is the plan's name…* doesn't matter to the user." The mock-up is [`to-dos/submittals/robot-line-before-after.html`](../../to-dos/submittals/robot-line-before-after.html).

## What changed

- **Each source is one row** (`SubmittalSourcesPanel`): its name and count, then its door. *The takeoff · 26 fixtures, 22 with a part* **Add from the takeoff**; *The plans' schedule · none yet* **Type or paste the schedule**; *Quotes compared* when there are picks. The cards' sentences are the hover line (`title`) on each name; the walkthrough and the guide still teach them. On the SpaceX bid, at a 1,400 px window, the step's body went from about 220 px to 102.
- **The robot is one line** (`RobotNote`, words from `robotNote.ts`): a chip that says the state, the one thing to press beside it, the full story in a card.
  - Nothing asked: 🤖 **Ask the robot to read the schedule** and a small ?. The card holds the three lines the dashed box printed (what it does, what it needs with the awake line, what you do after) and *What is the robot? →*. With no plans link the button is held and *needs the plans link on the bid* shows beside it, without a hover.
  - Asked: a chip — *Queued to read the plans* (Cancel), *Asked Sep 28 · not picked up yet* in amber (Take the ask back), *Reading the plans now*, *Read 12 tags · confirm below* in green, *Could not read the plans* in red (Dismiss). The amber box of five lines is that one chip; its two sentences are the card.
- **The card opens on hover, on keyboard focus, and on a tap** of the chip (or the offer's ?), which pins it until Esc, a second tap or a press elsewhere. The button is never inside the card. The card hangs under the line with no gap to cross.
- **All three robot offers are the same line**: the vendor PDF's (step 3) and the reviewer's file's (step 6) come through the same `RobotOffer`, so "one shape at every spot" (v2.4136) holds.

- **A finished step 1 folds, and opens again from its caret.** The owner, on the compact rows: "once uploaded I would like the section collapsed able to be opened again." A live robot ask used to hold step 1 open for as long as it waited — on the SpaceX bid, a stuck ask from Sep 28 had held it open for a week. Now only a read that is back with tags to confirm holds it open (`scheduleReadHoldsStepOpen`); queued, stuck, working or blocked rides on the folded line instead: *26 on the takeoff · no schedule yet · 🤖 Asked Sep 28 · not picked up yet*, the robot's part in amber (red when blocked). The caret or the title opens the step as before.

## Is this the best we can do — what the second look changed

- The first idea was a grey *Robot* chip with everything on hover. It hid that an ask was stuck, did nothing on a phone, and put the only button inside the card. The chip now carries the state, a tap opens the card, and the button stays out.
- The first build kept the two cards with their sentences. The owner's second note took the sentences off the page, which is what makes the rows possible.

## How it is built

- [`src/lib/submittals/robotNote.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/robotNote.ts) — `robotOfferNote`, `robotScheduleNote`: which words are the chip, which the card, which the button. The words themselves still come from `robotOffer.ts` and `describeTask`.
- [`RobotNote.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/RobotNote.tsx) — the line and its card. [`RobotOffer.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/RobotOffer.tsx) draws through it.
- The walkthrough's anchors did not move (`submittals-robot` is the line; `submittals-takeoff` and `submittals-plug-in` the two buttons). The robot stop says *the small button beside the robot*.
