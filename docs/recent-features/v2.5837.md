# v2.5837 — Quickfill: the Mark says what happened (2026-10-25)

Change 1 of punch list #176 (`to-dos/quickfill-needs-you-review/`), the owner's *"I like it, please build it"* of 2026-10-25 on the review of Quickfill and Needs you. Eight weeks of `quickfill_section_mark_events` read as one press every nine seconds: 814 marks by one assistant, 57% of them under 20 seconds after the previous press, Prospects at 268 for 32 marks running. The Roller's foot was one green press, *Looked · 50 open · next →*, whatever the count said, and nothing in the history could tell a station worked from a station stamped, or a station nobody understood from one nobody needed.

## What changed

- **Three presses instead of one**, on every surface that marks a station: the Roller's bar, the phone round's bar (stacked), the station header's small pair, Focus's folded line at zero and the foot of a station on the Page.
  - **Done · 0 open** (green) is lit when the count is zero. On a station with no count it reads *Looked* and is lit, as before.
  - **Worked it · N left** (blue) is lit when the count is above zero. It records the count and asks nothing more.
  - **Not sure what to do** (grey) is always lit. It opens one line, *Say what is unclear, or send it as it is*, and **Send the question** files a Dispatch inbox request: the title is `Quickfill · Prospects · 268 open: not sure what to do.` plus the words typed, the reference line is the station's own question (*The station asks: Did every prospect hear from us on time?*), `pending_action = 'quickfill_question'` with the station in `pending_payload`. One open request per station: a second press while the first is open marks the station and says the question is already there. The press also marks the station, so the round moves on.
  - On the Roller and the round the lit press also says where it goes, *Done · 0 open · next →*; the header shows the one lit press beside Not sure.
- **`quickfill_section_mark_events.outcome`** (`20261025010000_quickfill_mark_outcome.sql`, additive): `done`, `worked` or `question`; null on a mark from before the column or a plain look on a station with no count. `markSectionUpToDate` writes it: a question whatever the count, else the press's own outcome, else the one the count implies (`markOutcomeForCount`).
- **The Mark history window** reads the outcome: a day whose last mark was a question is a grey dashed cell with a **?** whatever was open, its title ends *· asked*, a worked day's *· worked*, a done day's *· done*; the legend names it; the habit line adds *Not sure what to do at 2 of 37 looks.*
- The Email station keeps its own *Looked · N open* button and writes the outcome the count implies; Texts and the Physical inbox take the three presses like every other station, Texts with the note its ledger writes.
