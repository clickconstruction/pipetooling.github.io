## Is this the best we can do?

1. **Fewer presses on a phone**: Nudge was open the thread, find the strip, find the line, press it, press Send, read, press Send as text; now open, read, press.
2. **Plain words**: every new line (the fallback, the two residential lines, the tips) runs through `plainWordsFailures`; both guide paragraphs pass the sweep.
3. **Do what is ready**: the window opens only when a way to send is open, falling from the text to an email on its own; with none, the words still wait in the box. A failed text's row says why Nudge is gone and offers the two doors that work.
4. **Reuse**: `nudgeLine` is the run's own; the box's reads are the ones it already made; the URL is the thread's own, so Back and a link behave.
5. **Cut**: a one-press send from the row (the owner reads before it goes, the brief's rule); a nudge into Today's sends (the run's list is for the morning); a fresh read of the person for the words (`useFlowNow` already has it); the hold of a row with a written *their word* promise from PR 7, since PR 7 is not on this stack's base and the hold needs its table (it follows once both are on main).
