# v2.4681 — Legal: a settled matter stays with the firm until you close it (2026-10-05)

Punch list #85, item 16, PR 1 of 2 (lane D). Recording *Settled* on the portal stamped `closed_at` and took the matter off the portal at once: the settlement check could not be recorded as a payment, and a later courier cost was refused with *That matter is not with your firm*. And any step moved the stage, backward too. This PR fixes both with no migration, and teaches the page the four stages PR 2's migration adds, so the old page never misreads them.

## What changed

- **One stage list for the page and the functions**: `supabase/functions/_shared/legalStages.ts` (`LEGAL_STAGE_LIST`, the working stages, the firm's ends `settled` · `uncollectible` · `dismissed`, `legalMatterOnPortal`, `firmStepDecision`, the step words). `src/lib/legal/legalMatters.ts` re-exports the list and adds `matterIsWithFirm`, `matterAwaitsClose`, `matterIsClosed`, `stageIsFirmEnd`; `legalStageLabel` and the Pipeline row chip know `post_judgment` (*after judgment*), `payment_plan`, `uncollectible` and `dismissed` ahead of the migration.
- **A firm end stays open.** `submit-legal-portal`'s `settled` step moves the stage and no longer sets `closed_at`. `legal-portal` and `legal-notify-dispatch` read *a portal stage and `closed_at` is null*, so the matter stays on the portal and in the digest. The desk lists it under *With the firm* with **Close the matter…** (office roles; `legal_close_matter`, which already took `settled`). The chip reads *⚖ settled · close it* until then. Matters a firm settled before this PR were closed by that step and stay closed.
- **No silent move backward.** `firmStepDecision` compares the stages: a step back down the ladder (judgment → demand), or any step after an end, is recorded with `meta.proposed = true` and `meta.from`, and the stage stays. The firm's reply says so (`notice`). The desk shows the step as *step · asks to move the stage back* with *The stage stays at judgment until you choose* and a **Keep judgment** button (acknowledge). A payment plan sits beside the ladder, so moving into or out of one is never backward.
- The step picker still offers the four steps the table takes today; PR 2 widens it.
- Docs: the guides *send a customer account to your attorney* and *share your attorney their portal*, GLOSSARY (Legal matter), EDGE_FUNCTIONS (all three functions).

**Deploy**: client first (CI on merge), then `supabase functions deploy legal-portal submit-legal-portal legal-notify-dispatch`. No migration.

## Is this the best we can do?

- *Should settled close by itself after some days?* No timer. The desk lists it with a Close button and the chip asks for it. An open settled matter costs nothing, and closing too early is the bug.
- *Should a backward step be refused?* No. The firm did the thing, so the entry is recorded with its date; only the stage waits. Refusing would lose the record of a second demand.
- *Is a payment plan a rung on the ladder?* It sits beside it. A plan can start after a demand or after a judgment, and a defaulted plan goes to suit.
- *Can the office move the stage back from the desk today?* Not in this PR: there is no office RPC that sets a stage. PR 2 adds `legal_set_stage` with the migration, and the desk's **Move it back** button with it. Until then **Keep** records the office's answer.
- *Should a pulled-back matter keep the whole packet on the portal?* The first draft did. Refined in the mockup: the customer's records leave, and the firm keeps its fees, the conversation and the reason. That needs the reason columns, so it is PR 2.

## Left for PR 2 (migration)

The stage CHECK gains `post_judgment`, `payment_plan`, `uncollectible`, `dismissed`; the picker offers them; `legal_close_matter` closes all three ends; `legal_set_stage` for a backward move the office accepts; `legal_pull_back` takes a required reason, stamped as `pulled_at` / `pulled_reason`, carried in the queue payload and the email; the pulled matter stays readable, slim, on the portal; the release and pull-back triggers cover every portal stage.
