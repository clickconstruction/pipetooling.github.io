# v2.3644 — Contract sweep: How this one gets signed (2026-09-20)

PR 5 of [`to-dos/contract-paper-lane/README.md`](../../to-dos/contract-paper-lane/README.md). Client only. The design the owner approved on 2026-09-19 (mock-up: *Three ways, chosen per row*) — PRs 2–3 built the two paper ways as extra footer buttons; this makes all the ways one stated choice and retires the workaround shape.

## The kernel — [`contractSigningWays.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/contractSigningWays.ts)

`signingWaysForRow(state, gcName)` reads the sweep's own row state and returns the ways, the pre-pick and what is demoted:

| Row | Shown | Pre-picked |
|---|---|---|
| Homeowner, email present | email the PDF · email a signing link · download to print | **email the PDF** (the owner's approval took the proposal: it is what the record supports — 0 e-signed, 3 of 3 on paper) |
| No signer email | the same three, the two emails disabled with *Needs a signer email — type one in To above* | **download to print** |
| `gc_job` (a builder) | **File <GC>'s subcontract** only | that — ours is the wrong document; the three are behind *Send ours anyway* (demoted, not removed — the open decision stays open) |

`effectiveSigningWay` keeps the office's pick unless it has become unusable (the email was cleared), then falls back to the row's default. `signingWayButtons` names the one-job button and the fast path per way. `contractSweepFooterSentence` takes the way: *Emails the PDF to …*, *Emails …*, or *Nothing is emailed — the page is yours to hand over, and the job leaves this list*.

## The pane — [`JobsContractSweepModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsContractSweepModal.tsx)

- A **How this one gets signed** radio group above the preview (hidden while filing and once the agreement is out). The pick is kept per job for the sitting.
- **The footer follows the pick**: one-job button + *& next* (`Email PDF & next` · `Send link & next` · `Download & next`), or the single **File their subcontract**. The *& next* button still dims on a row that is not Ready; for Download it dims only on a one-line scope (no email and no amount do not stop a page being handed over).
- **Download to print is one action**: the page downloads, and only when it did is the draft saved and stamped `handed` (v2.3629's write) — then the row leaves. The separate *Mark as handed to the customer* and *Email the PDF to sign* buttons from PRs 2–3 are gone; **Preview PDF** (the old Download PDF) stays at the left for a look and records nothing.
- The email-the-PDF confirm's button reads *Yes, email it* (it used to repeat the footer's label).
- `contractSweepPrimary` is no longer read by the pane (the kernel keeps it; the board's callers are unchanged). *Send all* under ⋯ still sends signing links to Ready rows — it is a bulk tool with its own confirm, not a per-row way.
