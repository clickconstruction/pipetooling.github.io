# v2.7974 — Robots: an assistant can make a twin's held seat again (2026-11-19)

A new pair on `dev-mcp` (server 0.117.0, 147 write verbs, 94 naming no RPC, 198 tools): **`plan_twin_seat`** → **`apply_twin_seat`**. The owner, 2026-11-19, of a held seat's retry: "It's worth building, help me build it".

## Why

A twin is made in three apps: its account here, then a seat in CountTooling and one in TakeoffTooling, each over a bridge. Either bridge call can fail and the twin still stands, with that seat held. `apply_twin` reports the held step. The way to try again was the link button on the twin's row at Settings → System → Digital twins, a person's press. This was the last press left from the audit of what an agent can stand up alone.

## What it does

- `seat = { twin }` plans every seat of the twin that is held. `app: "counttooling"` or `"takeofftooling"` names one. Any other key is refused: a seat's address and name are the fleet's to give.
- The CountTooling seat stands by `counttooling_user_id` on the twin's `users` row, the panel's own rule.
- The TakeoffTooling seat has no join key here. The plan looks it up over `tt-bridge` by its address, and `ttSeatLookup` reads the answer as the panel does.
- A lookup that fails reads *could not be read*, with the bridge's reason in a warning. It never reads *held*. The plan still offers the create, which is idempotent in the other app.
- The apply calls the bridge's own `create` as the dev, with the body `apply_twin` sends. It then saves the CountTooling seat's id on the twin's row, held to a row that is still a twin.
- Each seat is reported done or held with the bridge's own reason. One that fails does not stop the other.
- The hash commits to where each seat stands. It leaves the warnings out, since a bridge's failure words can differ call to call.
- An archived twin, the pricing robot and a twin with nothing held are refused in words.
- Nothing is taken away, so there is nothing to undo.
