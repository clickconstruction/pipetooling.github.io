# v2.2234 — fix(jobs): Sub Labor job picker offers paid-in-full jobs (2026-08-24)

The Sub Labor form's job picker ("Which job is this sub labor for?") promised
"finished jobs sit under their own divider" but could never show **paid** jobs:
the Jobs page cache loads `statusScope: 'non_paid'` (v2.1823 scoped-load plan),
and the `paid` scope only merged in when the Pipeline's Paid in Full section was
expanded. Searching a paid job (e.g. "888") returned "No jobs match."

- **`JobsSubLaborFormModal`** takes `ensurePaidJobsLoaded` + `paidJobsLoading`:
  opening the form (new or edit) — and clicking the picker field, as a retry —
  calls the cache's guarded `fetchPaidJobsIfNeeded` (no-op once merged per
  snapshot key). Paid rows then flow through the existing
  `subLaborAssignPickerRows` → "Finished jobs" divider path untouched.
- Fixes edit mode too: a sheet on a paid job can now resolve its picked-job
  link (the v2.2142 late-cache linking effect finally sees the job).
- While the paid fetch is in flight the picker subtitle reads "loading
  paid-in-full jobs…" instead of leaving a false "No jobs match." empty state.
- No checkbox/toggle added: the picker's divider already segregates finished
  jobs, so loading the data was the whole fix.
- Render smokes: opening new/edit forms requests the paid scope
  (`JobsSubLaborFormModal.render.test.tsx`).
