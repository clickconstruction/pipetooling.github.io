# v2.6935 — A large HR case fits the dev seat's door: the brief and the file shorten themselves and say so (2026-11-11)

Found 2026-11-11 by a read-only session on the largest HR file in the app. The dev seat cuts any reply at 80,000 characters (`replyText`, `_shared/devMcpDoor.ts`), and the two verbs meant to open a person's case both passed it. Nothing from that file is in this change: the tests run on a made-up case.

## What went wrong

1. **`get_case_brief(person)`** answered well past the cut. Its `documents` block carried every heading of every named document, and a file with about fifty documents, one of them with hundreds of headings, filled the reply by itself. Every block after `documents` was cut off: the entries' count and newest ten, the evidence index, the matter, the theme counts and the developments.
2. **`get_person_file(person, since, docs: false)`** was cut even when narrowed to six weeks. Each Timeline line carries its whole text, and each evidence record its whole memo and file list.
3. **`get_person_file`** read `person_file_entries` in one read of 200 rows. A file with more than 200 entries lost the rest: the chip read *200 HR entries* and `at_cap` named the table.

## What changed

- **`_shared/devMcpReplyFit.ts`** (new, pure): `fitReply(reply, steps)` measures a reply the way the door writes it (`replyChars`) and, while it is over `REPLY_FIT_CHARS` (72,000), runs the composite's own steps in order, mildest first. Each step that ran is named in the reply's `shortened` block: `{ why, blocks: [{ block, kept, read_whole }] }`. A reply under the budget comes back untouched with `shortened: null`. `outlineHeadings` keeps the shallowest heading levels of a document whole and then the next level's first ones, so a shortened section index is an outline and not the first lines of the text. `cutText` cuts a text with an ellipsis and counts what it left out.
- **`_shared/devMcpCaseBrief.ts`**: the small blocks come first and the long lists last (`narrative`, `evidence`, `documents`), so even a cut would take the part another verb reads. `counts` and each text's `section_count` carry the whole numbers. The entries are read a page at a time without their words (`readAllRows`), then the newest ten's words in one more read, so `entries.count` is exact past 200. The fit's steps: the documents' section indexes as outlines of 40, 24, 16, 12, 8, 5 then 3 headings a document; the narrative's and the matter narrative's outlines; the evidence index to its newest 100, 50, 25; the documents' headings to none; the documents list to its newest 60, 30, 15; the summary's text to 6,000 characters; the matter's steps and documents to 40; one History line a development.
- **`_shared/devMcpPersonFile.ts`**: the HR entries are read with `readAllRows`. New asks: `brief` (each line as `{ ymd, lane, source, kindWords, what, more?, notes?, byName, state?, files?, row }`, `row` being the `table:id` behind it; each evidence record short; the docs' sizes in place of their text unless `docs: true`), `line_chars` (40 to 2,000, 160 when left out; asking it asks for brief) and `until` (only lines on or before a day). The fit's steps, run whether or not `brief` was asked: the narrative's text, then the summary's; the evidence short; the documents list and the older files to their newest 20; the lines short; the evidence to its newest 50, 25, 10; the Timeline's newest months that fit, with `next_until` the day to pass as `until`; then lines of 80 and 40 characters. `counts.total` stays the whole file's and `counts.shown` counts the lines in the reply. `filter` now carries `until`.
- **`supabase/functions/dev-mcp/index.ts`**: server 0.63.0; `get_person_file`'s schema takes `until`, `brief` and `line_chars`; both descriptions say what `shortened` is.
- **Docs**: the two rows in `docs/dev-mcp/README.md`; `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` → dev-mcp.

## Tests

`src/lib/devMcp/devMcpBigCase.test.ts`, over `bigCaseFixture.ts`: a made-up case of 250 entries, 60 named documents (one with 400 headings), 80 evidence records and a long narrative, read through `pagingReader`, a fake reader that applies filters, order and the door's 200-row limit after the catalog's checks. Both verbs answer under 80,000 characters as `replyText` writes them, with every block present and `shortened` naming each shortened block; the entry count and the chip read 250; reading on by `next_until` reaches every line exactly once; a short stretch of a small file is untouched. The fit kernel has its own tests. The version pin moved in every `src/lib/devMcp/*.test.ts` that holds one.

## Release order

Merge, then deploy `dev-mcp` (`npm run check:edge-drift` names it). Live check, as the dev: `get_case_brief` and `get_person_file { brief: true }` on the largest file, each under 80,000 characters with `shortened` filled in and the entries' count past 200.

## Not in this change

`get_person_file` still reads the evidence records, the attachments and the other sources in one read of 200 rows each (`at_cap` names one that fills it). Only the HR entries are paged.
