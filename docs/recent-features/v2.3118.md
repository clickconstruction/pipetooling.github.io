# v2.3118 — ESIGN / Texas UETA consent on every signing surface (2026-09-08)

Owner's ask (2026-09-07): consent language citing the federal ESIGN Act and the Texas UETA on every place a person signs — but "not in your face". Designed on the "E-Sign Consent Map" artifact (brief + mock-ups of all twelve surfaces): one muted sentence in the place and style of the disclosure line the pages already had, a link at its end, two paragraphs beneath on tap, and a five-word checkbox. Migration `20260908030946_esign_consents.sql` ([doc](../migrations/20260908030946_esign_consents.md)); five signing functions + `get-estimate-public-terms` + `send-job-contract` redeployed.

## The words (one versioned kernel)

[`src/lib/esignConsent.ts`](../../src/lib/esignConsent.ts) (+18 tests) is the only home of the signer-facing text. `esignConsentText({ audience, documentNoun, lang })` returns:

- **The line** (always visible): *Your typed or drawn signature has the same legal effect as one in ink, and you can ask the office for paper instead at no charge.* + the link **How electronic signing works ▸** at its end. The Bid Room (`gc`) reads *Approving here is an electronic signature…*; Spanish (`es`) carries the whole surface.
- **The dropdown** (two paragraphs): *You're signing this estimate electronically. Under the federal ESIGN Act (15 U.S.C. § 7001) and the Texas UETA (Bus. & Com. Code ch. 322), a typed or drawn signature has the same legal effect as one in ink, and this page and its PDF are your copy.* / *You don't have to sign this way: call or email the office and we'll bring paper, at no charge. You can withdraw consent to electronic records later; anything already signed stays signed. You need a current browser and a way to save or print a PDF.* + **Full disclosure ›** (`/estimate/terms#electronic-signatures`).
- **The checkbox**: *I agree to sign electronically.* — a separate affirmative act (ESIGN § 7001(c)(1)(A); Tex. Bus. & Com. Code § 322.005), ahead of each surface's existing "I have read and agree" box. Submit refuses without it (client-side).
- `clauseText` — everything above joined — is what gets stored. `ESIGN_CONSENT_VERSION = 1`; bump it when any signer-facing string changes (§ 7001(c)(1)(D)).
- `esignDisclosureSections()` — the seven consumer-disclosure elements in full (EN + ES) for the public page; `esignAuditSuffix()` / `ESIGN_STATUTE_SHORT` for the record.

[`EsignConsentLine`](../../src/components/EsignConsentLine.tsx) is the shape: line + inline toggle button (`aria-expanded`) + hidden panel + optional checkbox, in the muted 0.8rem style of the old disclosure sentence. The surfaces' own disclosure sentences lost their "same force and effect" clause (it now lives in the line) and are passed as `lead`.

## Where it renders

| Surface | Audience · noun | How |
|---|---|---|
| `/estimate/accept` (+ change orders) | customer · this estimate / this change order | `EstimateAcceptBody` modal: line + checkbox above the existing agree box; payload carries `consent` |
| `/contract/sign` (job contract) | customer · this agreement | `ContractAcceptSignatureForm consent=` |
| `/contract/accept` (contracts + fill-on-page forms) | sub · this agreement / this form (EN) · este formulario (ES) | same form; `formFillState` disclosure strings trimmed |
| `/sub` work orders | sub · this work order / esta orden de trabajo | same form, `lang` from the portal toggle; `subPortalI18n` disclosures trimmed |
| `/bid-room` | gc · this proposal | `EsignConsentLine` without a checkbox (its one box already says "conduct business electronically") |
| Lien release modal (the office signs) | — | statute sentence in the disclosure; audit line cites the statutes |
| Report signature pads (staff, internal) | — | one small caption under the pad |

`ContractAcceptSignatureForm` gained `consent?: EsignConsentText | null` (internal `consented` state; the payload gains `consent: EsignConsentPayload`). `EstimateAcceptSubmitPayload` carries `consent?` on both modes; the five pages forward it as `esignConsent` in the function body.

## The record

- **`esign_consents`** (new table): `record_type` (estimate · job_contract · person_contract_document · step_commitment · bid_proposal_room) + `record_id`, `consent_version`, `lang`, `audience`, `document_noun`, `clause_text` (verbatim), `printed_name`, `method`, `consented_at`, `ip`, `user_agent`. Office-role read; written only by the signing functions with the service role, best-effort after the signature row commits (`_shared/esignConsent.ts`: `parseEsignConsent` + `recordEsignConsent`). A pre-v2.3118 client sends nothing and its signature still lands — the signed rows' own `consented_at` / ip / ua stay the record of the act; this table is the record of the words.
- **Functions**: `accept-estimate` (estimate), `accept-contract` (person_contract_document), `sign-job-contract` (job_contract), `sign-bid-room` (estimate — the frozen proposal row, and change orders), `submit-sub-portal` `accept_offer` (step_commitment). Body field `esignConsent: { version, lang, audience, documentNoun, clauseText }`.
- **Signed record block** (`SignedSignatureBlock`): facts line adds `ESIGN Act · Tex. UETA ch. 322` on every electronic signature, and `Consent v1 · en` (hover = the clause text) when the ledger row is loaded — `useEsignConsent(recordType, id)` on the estimate's staff record (`EstimateAcceptStaffAcceptedRecord.estimateRowId`).
- **Audit lines**: `jobContractSignatureAuditLine` (+ optional `esign_consent`), `lienReleaseSignatureAuditLine`, and the work-order document's `via` line append `15 U.S.C. § 7001 · Tex. Bus. & Com. Code ch. 322` — so the job-contract PDF/HTML, the lien waiver + lien filing PDFs, and the sub work order carry it.
- **Public disclosure**: `/estimate/terms#electronic-signatures` (`?lang=es` for Spanish) — the seven elements with the office contact from `physical_invoice_issuer_v1` (`get-estimate-public-terms` now returns `issuer`). Version-stamped.
- **Emails**: the contract-signing letter, the Bid Room letter (shared + app twin, parity-tested) and `send-job-contract` add one sentence offering paper before the button (§ 7001(c)(1)(B)(i)).
