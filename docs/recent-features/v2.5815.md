# v2.5815 — Quickfill Physical inbox: the papers table and the deck's kernel (2026-10-24)

Punch list #158, the owner's yes of the same morning on the four Quickfill proposals: *"I like your suggestions, build it all."* The proposal and the mock-up are in [`to-dos/quickfill-physical-inbox/`](../../to-dos/quickfill-physical-inbox/README.md): the station's six-month history is 123 marks with the same seven words retyped and a count that never reached zero, and the pick is **A**, the clarify deck in GTD's shape. This is the first PR of the train: the table and the pure kernel. Nothing on screen changes until the deck ships.

- **The table**: `office_inbox_papers` (migration `20261024040000`, [its page](../migrations/20261024040000_office_inbox_papers.md)): one row per paper that took more than two minutes, with the day it came, its state (`open` on the deck · `waiting` · `someday` · `closed`), the press that took it off the deck (`done` · `trash` · `filed` · `task` · `request` · `waiting` · `someday` · `routine`), where it was filed or whom it waits for, a waiting paper's check day, and a link to the Checklist task, dispatch request or estimator request a press made. RLS is the section marks' gate. `docs/ACCESS_CONTROL.md` carries the row.
- **The kernel**: [`lib/quickfill/physicalInbox.ts`](../../src/lib/quickfill/physicalInbox.ts) (+ 17 tests): `deckRows` (the open papers oldest first, each with its age in days, its colour and *66 d · Aug 19*), `undecidedSummary` / `undecidedWords` (*5 undecided · oldest 66 d*, *Nothing undecided*), `lookedButtonWords` for the round's and the Roller's button, `waitingRows` (*waiting for Marcus · check Mon*, *3 d past the check* on top), `somedayRows`, `decidedCounts` by the app's calendar day, `markAutoNote` (*2 undecided · oldest 4 d · 1 task · 1 filed*, the note the Mark will write so the history reads the day with no typed box), `pressPatch` / `BACK_ON_DECK_PATCH` (what a press and Still here write), `outcomeWords`, `normalizePaperTitle`.
- **The age**: `PHYSICAL_INBOX_PAPER_AGE` in [`ageState.ts`](../../src/lib/ageState.ts), amber at 2 days and red at 7, the convention's own constants for this surface: a paper nobody has decided on for two days is a slip, a week is a pile.
