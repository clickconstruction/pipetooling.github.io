# v2.7209 — Lien desk: the print bar over the Affidavits list says less, and the held papers stand out (2026-11-13)

The owner, 2026-11-13, on the bar at the top of the Affidavits list: "I don't love how much text" in the county block, "and it blends in too much with" the held line. The bar had grown a sentence at a time, all in one small grey: the count, the press, three tick boxes, the county with a paragraph under it, and the line about the papers a gate holds. He was shown a tidier bar beside it and said to build it.

## What changed

- **A status line.** *12 ready to print* in the primary text colour, and beside it an amber press, **2 held, not in this print ›**, in place of the grey sentence *2 ready affidavits are held by a gate and are left out. Each row says why.* Both old sentences stay as the tips of the words that replaced them. With no held paper the press is not drawn. On a phone the press sits under the count.
- **The amber press narrows the list.** The list already filters by one pile from the header's chips, so the press is a second filter of the same kind: `affHeldOnlyPicked` narrows `affVisible` to the print plan's `gated` papers and lifts a picked pile, since the held papers are all in **Ready to file**. A strip over the rows says *Showing the 2 held papers. Each row says why.* with **Show every paper**. The press itself, a pile chip and a deep link to one paper also lift it, and it lifts by itself when the last gate clears.
- **Print with.** The three tick boxes are three chips in a row that wraps, under one small label: **Help sheet**, **Notice record**, **Notice copies, about 14 pages** (the count is still `lienAffidavitBatchNoticeCopyCount`, and the chip is still not drawn at zero). Each chip is the same tick box with the same default, the same `data-` hook and the same stored choice in `lienHelpSheetChoice.ts`.
- **The county on one line.** *Signed in* `[ the notary writes it ]` *County* and a small **?**. The empty box's grey words carry the hint's first sentence. The second, *Type the county where it is signed to print it on both county lines.*, is behind the **?**, and with a county in the box the **?** says what it said before: *Both county lines print Travis County. Signing somewhere else? Type that county for this print.* The tip opens on the mouse, on focus and on a tap, closes on Escape, a second tap or a press elsewhere, and is the box's `aria-describedby`, so a screen reader hears it with the box. `LienSigningCountyBox` is one component, so the Lien window's affidavit tab changes with the desk.
- **What did not change.** The print press and its count, what prints, the second-sheet line under the press (v2.6930), the earlier form's line, the redraw bar above (v2.6867), and every word on the affidavit, the help sheet and the notice record. The Lien window's own two tick boxes keep their names.
