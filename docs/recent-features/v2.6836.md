# v2.6836 — What was given in kind counts as a cost, and every arrangement is on one page (punch list #301, PR 4) (2026-11-10)

The owner's call of 2026-11-10: a line counts as overhead, unless that line is put on a job. And her first question, the day In kind shipped: *where in the app could that fourteen thousand dollars of in kind payments be shown?*

## What changed

- **`src/lib/inKind/inKindCost.ts`**: a live line with an amount as a cost line (`inKindCostLines`, one select every reader uses), its label (*Lone Oak Concrete: 6 yards of 3000 psi*) and its total. A line taken back and a month with no credit count as nothing.
- **Overhead**, `readOverheadOfficePartsByDay` (`src/lib/overheadOfficePartsReads.ts`, source `in_kind`): a line with no job, or on the office job, is a material line of the overhead pool on the day it was given. The pool's day window, trend card, people table and Overhead tab name it *given in kind*.
- **A job's material**: `fetchJobMaterialsCostSnapshot` carries `inKindLines`; the cost timeline gains the source `in_kind` (🤝 *Given in kind*), so the chart, the burn and the verdict count it; the profit band counts it with the other charges; the Costs tab's money table shows a **Given in kind** row when there is some (`useJobInKindCost`), on screen and in print. `readOtherJobsPartsByDay` counts it on the day, so the pool's "other jobs" side agrees.
- **The page**, `/in-kind` (`src/pages/InKind.tsx` over `src/lib/inKind/inKindList.ts`), office only, reached from Accounts Receivable's header: three totals (still to come, given so far as overhead and on jobs, work we owe), then one row a deal, the ones with a month due first, the ended ones behind a press. A row opens the deal on its customer's page (`?inKind=<deal>&month=…`). **Set up paying in kind** finds a customer by name and opens the terms window there (`?inKindNew=1`), which is the door for a customer with nothing open yet.
- **Help**: the guide gains *See every arrangement* and says where each kind of line shows.

## Decisions taken with the build

- **Its own cost source, not an "other charge" row.** `jobs_ledger_materials` rows print on the customer's bill as Materials. Goods a partner gave are a cost of the job they were used on, not a charge to that job's customer.
- **The list is a list.** The ledger, Add a line and a month's credit stay on the customer's page, so there is one place each is done and the list cannot drift from it.
- **A deal with nothing billed is allowed.** The page's set-up press works for any customer, and a line then stands on no bill as *given ahead of our work*. Whether that is a real case is still the owner's call; nothing depends on it.

## Not here

- **The Bridge's profit grid and People Review** sum their own cost rows and do not read in-kind lines yet.
- **Who is not the office reads no in-kind cost.** The lines are the office's to read, so a primary or a superintendent sees the job's cost without them.
- **Edge functions that bundle the overhead read** count in-kind lines once redeployed (`npm run check:edge-drift`).
