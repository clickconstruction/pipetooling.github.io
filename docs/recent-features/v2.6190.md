# v2.6190 — Coloured buttons, chips and counts read in dark mode (2026-10-30)

Punch list #194's sweep, the finding [v2.6130](v2.6130.md) named for a card of its own and [v2.6151](v2.6151.md) left beside the Messages count. A chip, press or count that fills itself with a text token (`var(--text-link)`, `var(--text-red-700)`, …) drew white words on it. A text token is the light ink it is as words in dark mode, so those words read 1.05:1 (`--text-strong`) to 2.77:1 (`--text-red-700`) there. They sit behind presses and windows the lens's frames never open, so its reads could not list them. Colour only; no word changed.

## The rule

Taunya's call on 2026-10-30, while #6592 (v2.6186) did the oranges: white words on coloured buttons, not dark ones. So the rule depends on the token's colour.

- **A coloured token** (blue, red, green, violet, amber) keeps its white words. Its fill becomes the token's light value as a literal, which stays deep in both themes. Light mode is pixel-identical, and dark mode now matches it. That is v2.6151's way for the Messages count. Words that v2.6068 had put in `var(--surface)` on such a fill go back to white: its eleven presses and chips, v2.5981's Bids mode chip and the others built the same way.
- **A grey token** (`--text-strong`, `--text-700`, `--text-muted`) keeps its fill, and its words take `var(--surface)`. That is v2.6068's way: white in light, as before, and the dark surface in dark. The theme check refuses a raw grey, so a grey cannot be a literal fill.
- **A token too pale under white in light** fills with the deeper token of its hue, as v2.6068 moved the settings chips. `--text-blue-500` (3.68:1) takes `#2563eb`, `--text-green-600` (3.77:1) takes `#15803d`, and `--text-faint` (4.05:1) takes `--text-muted`.
- **A token that stays dark in dark** (`--text-faint-300`, `--text-on-bright-solid`, …) is left as it is.

## What changed

- **A codemod**, `scripts/codemods/text-fill-words-sweep.mjs`, parsed with the TypeScript compiler.
  - It reads the tokens from `src/index.css`, and with them which tokens turn light in dark and which are grey.
  - It reads styles and tone maps (`{ bg, fg }`, `{ fill, ink }`), ternaries, a fill's own condition on its words, and same-file spreads.
  - A coloured fill that moves takes its border with it. In the stylesheets, a rule that fills with a token and sets its words in the same block takes the same rewrite.
  - It is re-runnable: on a rebase conflict, run it on the new main.
- **The run**: 114 styles in 93 files and 5 stylesheet rules. Among them:
  - the contract, lien and billing presses in the job window and its sheets, on `#2563eb`;
  - Edit Bid's per-GC *Mark lost* and the contract sweep's red send, on `#b91c1c`;
  - the lien timeline's done step on `#166534`, and its missed step and struck mark on `#dc2626`;
  - the Lien desk's presses, the ☎ for an owner's call, and the lien calendar's *Draft*;
  - the Submittal Room's picked row, picked role and *Send*, on `--text-strong` under `var(--surface)`;
  - the Help guide ring's *Next* and its current step, on `#6d28d9`.
- **By hand**, what the codemod lists or cannot see:
  - The bid flow's done step and *Mark reviewed* filled from consts the strip also uses for words and rings. They take `DONE_FILL` (`#15803d`) and `NEXT_PRESS` (`#2563eb`).
  - People → Employment's *Save dates* and *Add time off* dimmed to `--text-faint-300` under white while they could not be pressed, 1.47:1 in light. That token stays dark in dark, and v2.6122's sweep missed it because it is a token, not a literal grey. Both take `src/lib/deadPress.ts`'s pair.
  - Three stylesheet rules take their white words from another rule: the GC stage's *Week done* pin and its tail, a GC's done step in GC Review, and the statement recipient's ✓. Each fills with `#15803d` or `#2563eb`.
  - The Dashboard's calendar count (v2.6130) drew its box over the calendar icon in the icon's blue. The counted icon now fills `#2563eb` with its box, so the two stay one colour in dark.
  - The dev's Punch list drew a pressed *Do*, *Later* or *Drop* in the dark `--text-on-bright-solid` on its colour's text token. That read 2.50 to 3.53:1 in light, a finding v2.6186 left for this sweep. A pressed pick now fills with `PICK_FILL`, the colour's light value, under white words.
- **CI holds it**: `npm run check:text-fill-words` runs the codemod with `--check`. It fails on white words on a text-token fill, on `var(--surface)` words on a coloured one, and on a pale token filling under words. A rule that takes its words from another rule is not seen.
- **The pins**: `readablePairs.test.ts` holds white on each of the ten literals at 4.5:1 or better, each tied to its token's light value. It also holds `var(--surface)` on the grey tokens in both themes. The lines for v2.6068's chips and v2.5981's Bids mode chip now read white on `#2563eb` and on `#6d28d9`.
