## The one count

[`src/lib/jobs/lienRunParts.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienRunParts.ts). `lienRunParts(rows, envelopes, { leaderName, together })` takes the checklist's rows (`lienRunChecklistRows`) and the run's envelopes and puts every notice in exactly one part:

| part | the notice | reads |
|---|---|---|
| `held` | cannot go as it stands: no address, an owner nobody confirmed, nothing to claim | *held* |
| `wait` | has not printed and has no signature | *wait on Sam's signature* |
| `ready` | can print now | *ready* |
| `owe` | printed, and an envelope it is in owes its number | *owe a number* |
| `record` | printed, every envelope numbered | *ready to record* |

From the parts it gives the step the run is on, the journey's three nodes, the count line (total and parts), the envelope counts and the one press. `lienRunSubLine` is the window's second line and `LIEN_RUN_GROUPS` the row groups' heads. Jobs combined onto one form (`together`) take the part of the most stopped, since one paper prints or does not.

## The presses follow the count

The count is only true if a press takes what it says. Two rules changed for that.

- **Print prints the ready notices, each whole.** Until now the packet printed envelope by envelope, and an envelope with one unsigned notice inside was held whole (v2.5086). With one original contractor on every notice, one missing signature kept the contractor's envelope out, so no signed notice finished printing. Now the window stacks the envelopes by part: what prints now, what printed, what is stopped. `printPacket('ready')` hands `runPacketHtml` the ready notices alone, so the contractor's envelope goes with the ready notices inside, and a stopped notice's copy goes in the next packet's envelope. `runPacketHtml` takes the stopped envelopes as `alsoHeld`, so page 1 still lists in red what stayed out. An owner nobody confirmed is held from the print too, as it already was from *Mark N printed* and the record.
- **A number reaches what printed.** The envelopes of printed notices are their own stack, so the contractor's number typed after a first packet lands on the printed notices only. A notice printed later starts with an empty box.
- **Record takes the `record` part.** `runRecordSplit` and `runRecordWords` are gone. The set is the same but for one case: an unsigned notice printed by hand in this sitting records once its numbers are in, as one printed before the sitting always did (v2.5533, *it records as it went*).
- **Docsmit is offered the ready stack**, so an envelope that already printed is never offered to the mail service.

A paper that printed unsigned is not in `wait`. It is past the signature: it sits under *Printed* with its Signed press open.

## The window

- **Head**: `lienRunWindowTitle` over `notices.length`, then `lienRunSubLine`. With Combine ticked the second line adds *combined: 3 jobs print on 1 form*.
- **Journey**: [`LienRunJourney`](../../src/components/jobs/LienRunJourney.tsx) draws `parts.nodes`. One line on a phone (`lienRunJourneyLine`). The lanes strip under it is gone, and Delivered is not a step.
- **Count line**: `run-parts`, the total then a chip a part.
- **Rows**: [`LienRunChecklist`](../../src/components/jobs/LienRunChecklist.tsx) keeps its rows and draws them in `LIEN_RUN_GROUPS`' order under heads that fold. The ready rows start folded on step 1, the held and waiting rows on step 3. The paid rows' fold sits at the foot of Held and shows whether or not the head is folded. A row that waits on the signature offers *Signed ▸* before it prints. While the cursor is in a number box the rows keep their groups, so a row does not leave from under the cursor on its first digit.
- **Envelope cards**: folded at the start on a computer too.
- **Foot**: `parts.press`'s two lines, More, and `run-press`. Under More: Combine with how many jobs could combine, the Docsmit door and its send-by line, *Mark N printed*, *Mark N signed*, the saved copy, Mailed on, the faces, the labels, the sheet, *Print it again*, *Take back…*. A dead Mark press keeps its noun (*Mark signed*). The Docsmit review folds More when it opens, so Esc after it answers for the run.
