# v2.4243 — Procurement log: dates read with a short year, and say how many days ago (2026-09-30)

Grace, on the Procure table: "On the ORDERED, EXPECTED, and DELIVERED columns, the year can be shortened when presented and we can add (# days ago) below the date in soft text."

A browser's date box cannot show a two-digit year, so the date is presented as text and the real box is kept over it.

- [`procurementLog.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/procurementLog.ts): `shortDateYear(iso)` → `09/28/26`; `daysAgoWords(iso, asOf)` → `today` · `1 day ago` · `12 days ago` · `in 1 day` · `in 12 days`.
- [`SubmittalProcurementPanel.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/SubmittalProcurementPanel.tsx): `dateCell(row, field, shown, …)` draws the three cells. A slot 4.75rem wide holds the date as text in a bordered box (`procurement-date-read`, `aria-hidden`; *mm/dd/yy* in faint text when empty) and, over it, the same `<input type="date">` as before with the v2.4239 handlers. Under the slot, one soft line per fact: *house said* (when the house gave the date), then the days-ago words. `today` is the browser's day, as the rest of the panel reads it.
- `src/index.css` → `.procurement-date-slot`: the input is `opacity: 0` at rest and whole (6.75rem, a light shadow) on `:focus` or while the slot is `is-open`. It lies over the start of the cell to its right while it is open, so no column changes width when a box is entered. `is-open` is React state set on focus and cleared on blur: Chromium drops `:focus` on the box while its calendar is up, and without the class the box vanished under the calendar.
- Nothing about saving changes: the box is the same element, so a click, a tap or Tab lands in it and v2.4239's finished-date rule runs as it did. The panel's existing smokes pass untouched.
- A stored date outside the log's window (`isPlausibleLogDate` — BP375's FD row holds `0001-02-12` from before v2.4239) reads in full, in red, with *check the year* under it. Two digits would have shown it as *02/12/01*.
- The three columns go from 108 px boxes to 76 px; on BP375 the table is 1,180 px wide where it was 1,260.
- Guide *build a submittal package* → a paragraph of its own under the procurement log's columns.

Looked at live in dev (Chromium): BP375 (empty boxes, the 0001 row, a box entered and left with nothing typed, the calendar opened and closed) and BP398 *ZZ Test* (*09/28/26 · 2 days ago*, a derived *10/12/26 · in 12 days*). Nothing was saved on either bid. Not seen: Safari and a touch screen — the box is the browser's own, so a tap should open its picker, but neither was looked at. Not seen live: a house date's two soft lines (the smoke covers them).
