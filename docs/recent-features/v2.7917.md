# v2.7917 — Lien desk: the copies to mail print on one side of the paper (2026-11-19)

## Why

Since v2.7916 an envelope is its cover letter and then every page of the clerk's stamped copy: three pages on a usual lien. The pages are in envelope order, so on a printer set to both sides the next envelope's letter lands on the back of a stamped page. The redrawn copy before it was one page, two with the letter, so a two-sided print happened to come out whole.

## What changed

- The line under the press, in *Filed liens: copies to mail* and on the job's Lien window, ends **Print on one side of the paper** (`LIEN_SERVE_WORDS.oneSide`).
- The run's PDF and each lien's kept file carry `ViewerPreferences`: `Duplex /Simplex` and `PrintScaling /None` (`printAsItIs` in `lienServePdf.ts`). A print window that reads them starts on one side, with each page at its own size, so a stamped page is not shrunk to fit. It is a hint and not a lock, which is why the screen says it too.
- The guide says why.

## Decision

A blank page after an odd envelope would make a two-sided print safe. It would also put a blank sheet in every envelope's stack on a one-sided printer and a blank page in the print kept on the job, which is meant to be what went in the envelope. The words and the hint cost nothing.

## Tests

`lienServePdf.test.ts` reads both preferences back off the stack and off a lien's own file. The window's and the press's render tests read the line.
