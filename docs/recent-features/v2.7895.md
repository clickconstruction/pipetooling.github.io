# v2.7895 — Painted maps: Text it from my phone under Saved opens Messages the moment you press (2026-11-19)

Punch list #392, a fix to its step 3. The Saved band (v2.7866) and the Share sheet's instant press (#394 step 3, v2.7877) were built the same day in two sessions and merged a few hours apart. The band was written against the sheet's older press. #394's walk on an iPhone simulator then found the band still making the link first, and reading *The link is ready. Press Open Messages.* though Messages had opened.

## What changed

- **The band's press is the sheet's own to the end** (`PaintedMapSavedBand.tsx`). When the band is drawn with its press it asks the server once whether a link can be made (`canMakePaintedMapLink`, nothing written). On a yes, with no link out, the press picks the link's code on the phone (`pickPaintedMapLinkCode`), sends the ask for the link (`registerPaintedMapLink`) and hands the phone the Messages address, all inside the press. No wait, and no question from the phone's browser.
- **A text is never left pointing at a dead link.** If the server then refuses the link, loses it, or makes it under another code, the band says *The link in that text will not open. Do not send it.*, with the reason first when there is one.
- **The false note is taken back.** The band listens for the page being left (`onPageLeft`). A page left within a minute of a hand-off did open Messages, so *The link is ready. Press Open Messages.* and *This phone would not open Messages…* are withdrawn and the press reads **Text it from my phone…** again. Any other reason on the band is left alone.
- With no yes from the server (a function from before the question, a refusal, no signal), the band makes the link first and then opens Messages, as before.
- **The guide's keywords.** `paint-a-map` gains the search words for the Dashboard press, the job picker, Find the jobs near me and the Saved band. They were held out of #8814 so it and #8798 could merge in either order, since both wrote the same line.

`PaintedMapSavedBand.render.test.tsx`, six new cases: the phone handed the text before the link is back, with the code it picked; a refused link; a link made under another code; *The link is ready* taken back when the page is left; *This phone would not open Messages* the same; and a page left with no hand-off changing nothing. With the listener switched off on purpose the two take-back cases fail by name, and pass again restored.

Not walked on a phone or a simulator in this change: the press is proven with its opener stood in.
