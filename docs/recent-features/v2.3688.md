# v2.3688 — Lien desk: the “mail goes somewhere else” line only shows on a house (2026-09-21)

Client only; no migration, no edge function.

## Why

The owner, on job 883's owner section: *"should we delete the '✉ Mail goes somewhere other than the job site — normal when a company or an investor holds the property.' since we are in the construction industry this is almost always the case."* Yes: the reading was written for the residential case (v2.3450), and on a commercial job it fired every time and told the office nothing. A sentence that is always true is noise in the one place people are reading carefully.

## What shipped

- **Kernel** — `readsAs` in [`ownerConfirm.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/ownerConfirm.ts) takes `propertyKind` and emits `mail-elsewhere` only when it is `residential`; the label says why: *mail elsewhere — the roll may be stale*. Public, landlord and likely-homestead readings are unchanged. Every caller follows: the Lien desk's owner pane (passes the property record's kind), Put a GC on notice and the owner-confirm list (their rows already carry the kind), and Edit Job's lookup box (does not know the kind, so it never shows it).
- **Words** — the desk's sentence on a house: *✉ Mail goes somewhere other than the house — check the roll is current; the owner may have moved.* Edit Job's phrase likewise.
- Guide *file a lien and never miss its deadlines*: the example rows drop the chip.

## Verified

- `ownerConfirm` kernel suite (commercial: no chip; residential: chip with the new label); `JobFormOwnerLookupBox` render test; `LienDeskModal` and `GcOnNoticeModal` render tests; guide + release-note suites; lint; typecheck.
