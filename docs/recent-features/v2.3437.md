# v2.3437 — Lien notices carry the unpaid invoice (2026-09-14)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. PR 5, the last of the *Demand letter, itemized* train (`to-dos/demand-letter-itemized/`; v2.3425 · v2.3429 · v2.3433 · v2.3436).

## Why

Tex. Prop. Code § 53.056(a-3): "The notice may include an invoice or billing statement." The owner who receives a notice may withhold from the GC what the claimant is owed (§ 53.081) — the invoice tells them how much. The demand letter's Exhibit A machinery (v2.3429) already renders and merges the app's invoice PDF; the notice needed the same.

## What shipped

- **Kernel** [`noticeInvoiceEnclosure.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/noticeInvoiceEnclosure.ts): `unpaidBilledInvoices(job)`, `noticeInvoiceDocs(job)` (the physical-invoice document per unpaid bill, titled by the number the bill shows), `noticeEnclosureRefItem(docs)` (*Invoice #2, August 18, 2026 enclosed* / *2 invoices enclosed*), `noticeInvoiceExhibitInputs(docs, toPdf)` (stamped **INVOICE**, not EXHIBIT — `DemandExhibitInput.stamp`), `noticeInvoicePrintSections(docs)` (the invoice's HTML on its own sheet with the § 53.056(a-3) line). +3 tests.
- **The notice tab** ([`LienFilingTabs.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienFilingTabs.tsx)): *Enclose the invoice — Invoice #…* checkbox (on by default when the job has unpaid bills); when on, the reference strip carries *… enclosed*, Print appends the invoice pages, Download PDF and the courtesy email merge them behind the notice. The modal passes every unpaid bill (not only the demand's selection).
- **The Lien desk run** ([`LienDeskRunModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskRunModal.tsx), [`lienDeskRun.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDeskRun.ts), [`lienDeskRunIo.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDeskRunIo.ts)): the run loads each notice's job once, `runPacketHtml` takes the invoice sections and prints them after every copy, `recordLienDeskRun` merges them behind every emailed copy; the run's summary line counts the invoices enclosed.
- Guides *file a lien and never miss its deadlines* (step 1) and *send lien notices from the Lien desk* (the run).
