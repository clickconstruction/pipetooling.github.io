# v2.4290 — Help: the lien waiver guide shows each step and links to where it happens (2026-10-01)

No migration, no edge function. Grace, after the first real waiver on job 650: *the guide could be significantly enhanced with photos and links to the various parts of how to use it.* Guides take recordings, not photos ([`HELP_MEDIA_PLAN.md`](../HELP_MEDIA_PLAN.md) — a static PNG rots and freezes real data), and until now a guide could link only to another guide.

## What changed

- **Links to pages of the app** ([`helpGuideHtml.ts`](../../src/lib/helpGuideHtml.ts)): the contract sanitizer keeps only absolute http(s) hrefs, so a `/settings?…` or `/jobs?…` link in a guide came out dead (the same trap v2.3516 fixed for guide-to-guide links). A root path of plain URL characters (`isHelpGuidePagePath`: `[A-Za-z0-9/?&=#._%-]`, no `..`, no `//`, not a `/help` address) now rides a second placeholder host (`https://page.help.internal/<percent-encoded path>`) through the sanitizer and is restored as `href="<path>" data-app="<path>"`; `GuideBrowser` turns a click on `a[data-app]` into `navigate(path)`. Marked's `&amp;` in attributes is undone before encoding and re-escaped on restore. Tests in `helpGuideHtml.test.ts` / `.dom.test.ts`.
- **Settings lands on the signer** — `/settings?tab=settings-jobs&focus=issuer.signerName` opens the Physical invoice block and rings *Signs for the company* (`PhysicalInvoiceIssuerDevSettingsBlock` rings `signerName` like `companyName` / `addressText`).
- **The guide *send a GC our lien waiver***: an opening list of the five places with a link to each (Settings, Pipeline, the Bill tab, the Dashboard, GC Review), links at every step that has a page, the sub-side twin and the portal guide linked, and five recordings in `public/help/` — `send-a-gc-our-lien-waiver-settings.gif` (the block, 2.4 MB), `-window.gif` (switches · why line · leader box · the page's foot, 2.4 MB), `-signnow.gif` (the draw-only pad on the scratch job 1054, 2.8 MB), `-billtab.gif` (the two chips and *Sign it ›*, 2.6 MB), `-gcreview.gif` (Knight's bills with the Lien waivers column, 2.0 MB). **Not taken**: the GC's room — the one GC holding a waiver (Loberg Contracting, job 650) has no portal link yet and minting one makes their page live; take `-portal.gif` once a GC with a waiver has a room. Captured headless with Playwright (installed Chrome, light theme, 1280×800 cropped to the painted 712 px, 6–8 fps, ≤128 colours) as the dev account on the local dev server; the scratch job's awaiting row was voided after the take.
