# v2.4288 — Edit Job → Bill: each bill shows the money that paid it (2026-09-30)

Grace, with a screenshot of job 650's Bill tab, the two bank-deposit rows boxed in red: "revise this section of the modal to be more visually attractive and useful." Four mock-ups later (canvas: https://claude.ai/artifact/EkzGZBLY9tC6qAMve3s6h5 — A receipt lines, B grouped under the bill, C the money in date order, D payments under the bills they pay), her call was D with a By bill / By date switch. This is PR 1 of 2: D. PR 2 adds the switch and the By date reading.

## What was wrong

The ③ Payments received table drew every payment as a 150 px row whose loudest element was an empty **Sent** date box, printed the raw Mercury kind (`checkDeposit`) as the type, printed the Mercury transaction UUID abbreviated (`4ed..3c1`) as the reference, drew **Unlink and remove** as the most prominent control on every row, and — on a bank or Stripe row — never said which bill the money paid: the "✓ pays the … bill" summary was only drawn on hand-typed rows. So on job 650 both checks looked unattached though they paid the $26,800 bill.

A read-only pass over prod on 2026-09-30 (payments since 2026-03-01) decided the shape: **0 of 348 carry a check date**, so the Sent box leaves the row; **every Mercury check deposit (352 of 352) names its payer** in `counterparty_name`, so a bank row can say *check from Loberg Contracting*; `payment_type` holds eight spellings of three things (null 103, Cheque 71, Card (external) 46, checkDeposit 39, Check 35, other 21, ACH 7, Other 4), so the source words come from the row's origin, never from that column; 53 of 348 sit on no bill. The job window is 560 px wide at most on every screen (`JobWindowModal.tsx`), full-bleed under 640 px — the first canvas was drawn at 1040 from a 2× screenshot and redrawn at 560.

## What changed

- **Kernel** [`billsAndPayments.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/billsAndPayments.ts) (tested): `paymentSource` (bank / stripe-card / stripe-recorded / hand, with the chip and the instrument word; `instrumentWord` reads the eight spellings), `sourceWords` (*check from Loberg Contracting*, *card through Stripe*, *check recorded in Stripe*, *check · typed by hand*), `billSentYmd` + `daysAfterBill`, `paymentLineWords` (date, payer, days, pinned vs counted here, partial, the check date, the hand-typed detail, the bank's returned verdict), `billPaidBar` (one segment per slice, never past the whole), and `splitBillsAndPayments` — every payment is drawn under each bill that holds a slice of it per `attributeJobPayments` (a pinned row under its own bill, an unpinned one under the bill the oldest-first rule counts it toward); a payment with no slice anywhere, and **a row not yet saved**, stay in ③ (a line that jumped under a bill mid-keystroke would lose the box being typed in; persisted ids only change on refetch).
- **One line per payment** — new [`JobFormPaymentLine.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormPaymentLine.tsx) (render-tested): amount · *Sep 14 · check from Loberg Contracting* · *61 d*; a red *Returned by the bank · Insufficient funds* chip with **Unlink and remove** in the open (the one row that needs the press); an amber *no bill picked* chip with **Pin it to this bill**; a sub-line for the check date, the check number and memo, and *Received 13 d before the bill went out. Check the date.*; a ⋯ menu holding **Edit details** (hand rows; a draft opens its boxes at once), **Use the bank date as the check date** / **Add the check date** (bank rows; one `FinishedDateInput`, per the v2.4239 rule), **Pin it to this bill**, **View the Stripe bill**, **Undo part payment**, **Check didn't clear…**, **Move to job…** (disabled with the reason under it when a sent bill counted it), **Unlink and remove**, **Remove**. The boxes a hand row edits — amount, received, check date, type, check number or ref, memo, **Pays** — sit in a fold under the line; both date boxes are `FinishedDateInput`, so a half-typed date is never handed over.
- **Under each bill** — [`JobFormInvoiceList.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormInvoiceList.tsx) takes `bankFacts`, `persistedLedgerPaymentIds`, `unlinkingMercuryPaymentId` and `paymentLineActions` (the host's seven doors); with them, every non-draft row ends in a thin paid bar (`.jobInvoicePaidBar`, one green segment per payment) and the lines (`.jobInvoicePayments`). Without them (older callers, the render tests' default) nothing is drawn. The send-back hint now says *unlink them first (under this bill)*.
- **③ is the rest** — [`JobFormPaymentsTable.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormPaymentsTable.tsx) rewritten from 1,276 to ~400 lines: its rows are `splitBillsAndPayments(...).onNoBill`, each a `JobFormPaymentLine` with the v2.2570 bill-apply chips and the v2.3692 Stripe hand-off hanging under it; the match bar, the manual-entry flow (blank rows hidden behind **+ Record a cash or check payment**), the grey trace lines and the explainer stay. The heading reads **③ Other money on the job** when the job has a sent or paid bill, **③ Payments received** otherwise; with nothing left it says *Every payment on this job sits under a bill above.* The `mercury_transactions` read moved out of the table into [`useMercuryDepositFacts`](../../src/hooks/useMercuryDepositFacts.ts) (posting date, status, kind, `counterparty_name`, failure reason), called once in `JobFormModal` for both blocks.
- The ② heading reads **② Bills and payments** (`InvoicesSectionHeading`). `index.css` gains the `.jobPaymentLine*` / `.jobInvoicePaidBar` / `.jobInvoicePayments` rules (tokens only; the theme check passes).
- Guide *see which payments paid a bill* (new, plain words). `job-window-tabs.md` still says "the Invoices and Payments received tables" — it is on the legacy plain-words list, so its rewrite is a sweep item, not this PR's.

## Decisions

- **Where an unpinned payment is drawn is the paper's rule** (the owner's 2026-09-18 oldest-first rule, `paymentAttribution.ts`): the line sits under the bill the rule counts it toward, says *no bill picked*, and **Pin it to this bill** makes it that bill's for good. The board-vs-portal question on `to-dos/owner-decisions-pending.md` is untouched — this PR draws the rule in the open rather than deciding it.
- **The check date is a menu item, not a box on the row** — nobody has ever filled it (0 of 348).
- **The Mercury id is gone from the screen.** The deposit's own page in Accounts Receivable carries it; the line names the payer instead.
- A hand-typed row linked to a bill is still removable (the RPC confirm), as before; a Stripe row keeps only Stripe's doors; a bank row keeps Move and Unlink.
