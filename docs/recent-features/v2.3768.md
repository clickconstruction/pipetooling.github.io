# v2.3768 — Lien desk: the Timeline tab — every job on the grid, and Print the grid for counsel (2026-09-23)

Punch list **#37**, PR 2 of 3 (PR 1 was v2.3761, the strip on the pane). No migration, no edge function, no stored rows. Stacked on v2.3761.

## Why

Counsel's first instruction was *"Build the grid. One row per job"* and *"move every job onto that grid today."* The desk's Notices tab lists what is due inside 30 days; a job whose next date is 45 days out, a filed lien whose year is running, and a job whose every window closed are not on it. The strip (v2.3761) shows one job's path; this is the book.

## What shipped

- **`lienTimelineBook.ts`** (pure): `buildLienTimelineBook` runs the desk's own queue kernels over a wide read — `buildLienDeskQueue`, `buildLienAffidavitQueue`, then `buildLienTimelineFromDesk` per job — and gives one row per job with its timeline, a **lens** (`due` — the next step is inside `LIEN_DESK_LEAD_DAYS`, or a service / release / retainage step, or a closed window nobody noted; `later` — the path runs, nothing to do yet; `dead` — every right gone and noted) and a sort key (the next step's date; undated last, then by dollars). `filterLienTimelineBook` applies the GC and the *Something due / All* lens. `lienGridRows` and `lienGridHtml` are counsel's twelve columns (`LIEN_GRID_COLUMNS`), letter landscape, blanks with a footnote for the four facts the app does not hold (they fill from v2.3753's columns structurally — bond and contract-ended — when the job carries them).
- **`useLienTimelineBook`**: the two desk RPCs with `p_within_days = 400` (`LIEN_BOOK_WINDOW_DAYS` — every open month and every affidavit window is inside it), the jobs, the GCs and their standing rules, the property records and owner overrides (the grid's owner and kind through `resolveLienProperty`), the affidavit and release filings. Read the first time the tab opens, kept for the modal's life; the desk's items are handed in.
- **`LienDeskTimelineTab`**: the filter bar (GC · Show · the summary · ⎙ Print the grid) sticky at the top; a row per job — label, GC or *with the owner*, kind, dollars · the strip in `mini` layout · the next step and its aside. Three columns above 700 px, stacked below (its own width, not the viewport's). Rows with every right gone are dimmed.
- **The desk modal**: `kind` gains `'timeline'` (`initialKind` too); the tab reads *Timeline · N* once the book is read (N = the due lens); the body is the tab alone, full width; a row opens the job on the Affidavits pane when its next step is the affidavit, service, the suit or the release and the desk lists it there, else on the Notices pane when the desk lists it, else its Lien window.
- **The mini strip** drops the year to `’27` and the *filed / served / sent* verbs — the row has no room for them; the full strip keeps them.

## Verified

- `lienTimelineBook.test.ts`: four jobs (two open months due; a closed window unnoted; a month 50 days out; a filed and served lien) → the order, the lenses, the counts, the GC list; a noted miss moves to *dead*; the grid's columns and blanks; the print HTML's title, page size, totals, footnote.
- Live at 1400, 768 and 375 px on prod data.
