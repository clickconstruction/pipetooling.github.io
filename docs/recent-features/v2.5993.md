# v2.5993 — Download a sent estimate as a PDF (2026-10-27)

Punch list **#206** PR 3 (`to-dos/estimate-extend-pdf-options.md`), the office's second ask from the
City of Seguin case: *"Need an option to also download the PDF to send to more than one person."*
Client only; no migration, no edge function. Built on the to-do's recommended calls 5 and 6: a real
file, filed as a sent copy; no link token in it.

## What was wrong

Neither the office page nor the customer page had a print or download press, and the customer page
prints its Approve buttons, the picker and the signature box with it. A city routes a quote to
people who are not the link's addressee, and the office had nothing to hand them.

## What shipped

- **`src/lib/estimates/estimatePaper.ts`** — `estimatePaperModel(input)`: every line the PDF draws,
  pure: the company from the brand (`estimateEmailCompanyName`), the title (a change order's display
  title, the experience's fallback), `Estimate #175 · sent Sep 24, 2026`, the For line, `Good through
  Thu Nov 26, 2026`, a change order's facts, the blocks — every option with its tags, pitch, lines and
  total through `estimateOfferedOptionsView` on a sent row, what they took on an accepted row
  (*Accepted "HDPE water line" · $13,410.00 (of 2 offered)*), the frozen lines otherwise — the total
  line, *Accepted by Dana Ruiz on Oct 2, 2026.*, the terms paragraphs and the public terms link,
  how to approve with no token (*To approve this estimate, open the link in the email sent to …. The
  link is not in this file.*; empty once the row is no longer sent), the footer lines;
  `estimatePdfFileName` (*Estimate 175 - City of Seguin.pdf*, file-safe); `paperLines`. 6 tests.
- **`src/lib/estimates/estimatePaperPdf.ts`** — `estimatePaperPdfBlob(model, logo)` over
  `loadJsPDF` on letter paper (the owner's records packet's pattern): the title left and the brand's
  PNG right (`loadEstimateBrandLogo`, best effort; the company's name when it cannot be read), the
  meta lines, a block per option with its line table (Line item · Count · Unit price · Amount) and
  its own total, the total line, the terms, the approve line, and every page's foot with the company
  and *Page n of N*.
- **`EstimateDownloadPdfButton.tsx`** — **Download PDF** beside Copy / Open customer link on a sent,
  accepted, declined or superseded row: draws, `saveBlobAs`, and `fileSentCopy` as `estimate_pdf` /
  `how: 'download'` with the customer, the job and the bid, the estimate as its source. A draw that
  fails says *Could not draw the PDF.* and files nothing. Render smoke (3).
- **`Estimates.tsx`** — the button under the link buttons with the model built from the row, the
  customer's name, the For line, the resolved experience and the public terms link.
- **Docs**: the guide *send an estimate and turn it into a job* gains *Send it to more than one
  person*; `docs/SENT_COPIES.md`; the Glossary; the Estimates map's sent-view dossier.
