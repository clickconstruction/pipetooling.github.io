# v2.8348 — Agreements: a paper with an outside company can be taken back (2026-11-23)

Punch list #403, the first piece of the owner's first decision. Client only. No migration and no edge function.

## The decision

The card's pick for decision 1: the Agreements tab lists every paper that is with an outside party, and one that is not signed carries **Send** and **Take back**. On 2026-11-22 the owner answered "Yes, all four" to the card's four picks. The card left one part to ask: what Take back does. On 2026-11-23 he was asked "Take back: delete the paper and end its link?" and answered "delete the paper and end its link".

This PR is Take back on the lines the tab lists today, which are papers that are out. The list of every kind and state, the *not sent* state and **Send** on its line are the next PRs.

## What was so before

No press removed an outside party's paper. People → Contracts leaves a party's paper out, and Documents → Agreements had no take-back for any paper. A paper sent to the wrong company stayed under **Outside companies**, with a live signing link, until a dev deleted its row by hand.

## What changed

- **Take back** on the line of an outside party's paper that is not signed: a customer's, a supply house's, a trade company's, or someone's on no list.
- A confirm first. It names the paper, who it is with and who holds the signing link. It says nobody is emailed and that it cannot be undone. Its button reads **Take back the paper**.
- The paper's row is deleted. The signing link lives on the row, so it ends with it. Someone who opens the old link reads the signing page's own words for a link that no longer works.
- The toast says what came of it, and the list reads again.

## What Take back refuses, and why

- **A signed paper.** It is the agreement itself, and its e-sign record, its signed copy and the log of who opened it describe it. The line of a signed paper has no press. The delete also asks the database for an unsigned paper only (`status` in *unsent*, *sent*), in the same statement that removes it. So a paper signed between the read and the press is kept, and the office reads *… was signed before it could be taken back. A signed paper is kept.*
- **A paper that is not an outside party's.** A teammate's or a sub's is removed on People → Contracts. A job's contract is voided in its Contract window. A trade company's GC paper is GC mode's.
- **A paper a job requires.** It comes off on the job. The delete asks for a paper with no job too.
- **A paper a newer one replaced.** It is history.
- **A status the kernel does not know.** Nothing is deleted on a guess.

## Who has the press

The leader and a dev, through `canTakeBackPapers`, which is `canDeleteFromContractLibrary`: the one rule People → Contracts and the Contract library already keep for deleting a paper. That rule is the app's own, and it is narrower than the database. The table's delete policy answers the office's four roles, an assistant and the controller among them (`20260714200000_dissolve_assistant_pay_linkage.sql` put `is_assistant()` in it, which covers the controller). So an assistant and the controller see **Send again** and **Copy the signing link** as before, and no **Take back**, by the same choice that keeps deleting a paper on People with the leader and a dev. No policy changed.

Whether the rest of the office should take a paper back is a question for the owner. Nothing in the database stands in the way: it is one function, `canTakeBackPapers`. It is held to the narrower rule here so the same paper is not deletable on one page and not on another.

This section first said the delete policy itself refused an assistant and the controller. That was read from the baseline, which the later migration rewrote. The whole-schema bed of the next PR in the train caught it.

## What a delete takes, and what it leaves

Read in the schema before the press was written.

- **Leaves:** the copy of the email under Sent copies (`sent_documents` names its paper with no key), and every line of the open log, which only a signed form has.
- **Takes:** the row, so the link, the sent time, whether they opened it (`signer_last_viewed_at`) and its reminder counts. A paper is not in Recently deleted, so nothing brings it back. The confirm says it cannot be undone.
- **Has nothing to take:** an unsigned paper has no e-sign record, no signature image and no stored PDF. Each is written when it is signed.

## A gap seen and left

A signer who already has the signing page open when the paper is taken back, and then presses to sign, reads *We couldn't record that. Please try again later.* and not the words for a link that no longer works. Nothing is signed. It is the signing page's own, and is noted for a follow-up.

## The guide

*find an agreement we sent or signed* gains **Take a paper back**.
