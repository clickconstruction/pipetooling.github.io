# v2.8225 — Legal portal: a cap on confirmation emails (punch list #85, a review residual) (2026-11-21)

Punch list #85 (`to-dos/legal-portal-firm-ready/README.md`), one of the residuals the October reviews left: *anyone holding the firm's link can make the company send up to 12 confirmation emails, as often as they like; no rate cap on adding a person or resending.* The card named no number, so the cap follows the pattern the repo already uses (so many in the last hour, a 429 in the firm's words) over the legal portal's own counter. No migration. One function to redeploy: `submit-legal-portal`.

## What changed

- **The rule** ([`_shared/legalPortalActs.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/legalPortalActs.ts)): `LEGAL_CONFIRMS_PER_PERSON_PER_HOUR = 3` (a person's first and two more) and `LEGAL_CONFIRMS_PER_FIRM_PER_HOUR = 24` (a list of twelve, twice over). `legalConfirmCapProblem` says which limit was reached, in the shape of `legalRateLimitMessage`: *3 confirmation emails to ann@firm.com in the last hour is the limit. Try again in an hour.*
- **The count** is `legal_portal_guess_gate`, the throttle that slows a guesser of the firm's address (v2.4756). It counts rows under a text key in the last hour; a confirmation is counted under `confirm:<recipient id>` and `confirm-firm:<firm id>`, which no caller's address can equal. The function reads the count (`misses`) and applies its own limit; the gate's `locked` (ten misses, by caller) is the guess gate's alone.
- **Asked twice** (`askLegalConfirmCap`): first without recording, so a press the cap refuses is never counted and pressing again cannot push the hour out; then recorded and read back, so of presses that asked together the ones over the limit are refused.
- **`recipient_add`** asks before the person is written (the firm's limit; the person has no key yet), so a capped add leaves no row waiting on an email that was never sent. Once the row exists its own email is counted as that person's first.
- **`recipient_resend`** asks for the person and the firm before anything is sent.
- **One email per instant.** `sendConfirm` writes the new link's hash only over the hash the call read (`confirm_token_hash`, compared in the update). Of two presses in the same instant one writes and sends; the other answers 409, *A confirmation email to … went a moment ago. Check that inbox before sending another.* Before, both sent, and the first email's link was dead on arrival because the second overwrote its hash.
- **The answer** is a 429 (409 for the same-instant twin) with `{ error }`. The page already prints a 4xx's words as written (`firmFacingErrorLine`), so nothing on the client changed.
- **Guide**: *manage who at the law firm gets emails* says the two limits.

## What it does not do

- The throttle missing or failing reads as no count, as the guess gate reads it: the portal never closes on a throttle that cannot answer.
- The gate's window is an hour and it answers a count, not times, so the refusal says *in an hour* and not a clock time.
- Two presses that record in the very same database instant can each read the count without the other's row. The count they leave still closes the hour, and for one person the hash swap lets only one of them send.
- The confirmation's wording, who may be added (twelve people, one live row per address) and the office's own adds are unchanged.
