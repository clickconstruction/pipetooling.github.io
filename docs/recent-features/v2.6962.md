# v2.6962 — Needs you says when names on permits look like a customer (punch list #307) (2026-11-11)

PR 10's leftover on [`to-dos/bid-permits/`](../../to-dos/bid-permits/README.md): *a Needs you line while the count is above zero*. Builders has carried the **Looks like a customer** chip since v2.6886, but it is away from the bid, so somebody has to go and do it. The Dashboard now says how many names wait and opens the list.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/research/lookAlikeWorklist.ts`** (new, pure, `lookAlikeWorklist.test.ts`): what the worklist stands on, lifted out of `usePermitLookAlikes` so the chip and the line count with one function. `settledNames` (a name tied to a customer or refused for it), `siteCountsInCity` (the names on permits at addresses we bid to each customer), `permitNamesOf` and `cityLookAlikes`, whose length is the number on a city's chip. For the line: `sumCityLookAlikes` (the chips added up, each city that has some, the city the press opens), `lookAlikeNudgeWords`, and the rules of the count's memory (`freshLookAlikeCount`, `withLookAlikeCount`, `parseLookAlikeCounts`, half an hour).
- **`src/lib/research/lookAlikeIo.ts`** (new, `lookAlikeIo.test.ts`): `loadLookAlikeShared` (the answers, the customers, the newest thousand bids, read once for every city), `loadCitySiteCounts`, `countCityLookAlikes` (a city's Builders read and its site evidence, counted by `cityLookAlikes`), and `loadLookAlikeNeed`, which takes a city's count from memory while it is fresh and reads the rest one city at a time. A city that cannot be read is left out and asked again next time. Two askers at once share one read.
- **`src/lib/research/lookAlikeCountMemory.ts`** (new): the counts in `localStorage` under `permits.lookAlikeCounts`. `recordNameAnswer` in `bidSiteIo.ts` forgets them once an answer is saved (`bidSiteIo.answer.test.ts`), so the line counts again after a sitting on the worklist.
- **`usePermitLookAlikes`**: the same reads and the same counting through the two new files. No change to what the chip shows.
- **Needs you**: source `lookAlikes` in `needsYouSources.ts` (the office and every estimator, the set `user_can_research_permits()` names, which is who may insert into `builder_customer_answers`; held off Quickfill with its reason), `useLookAlikeNudge`, the item `permit-look-alikes` in `dashboardNeedsYou.ts` (gray, the hygiene tier at 60, kicker **Permits**, **Answer them**; `dashboardNeedsYou.lookAlikes.test.ts`), actor `anyone`, and its door in `needsYouDoors.ts`. Wiring smoke: `DashboardPinnedQuickRow.lookAlikes.render.test.tsx`.
- **The deep link**, `src/lib/research/permitsLink.ts` (new, pure, `permitsLink.test.ts`): `/prospects?tab=permits&view=builders&looks=1&city=<city>`. `ProspectsResearchTab` opens on Builders when the address says so and keeps `view=builders` on it while Builders is on screen. `PermitBuilders` starts with the chip on from `looks=1` and writes the chip to the address when it is pressed. The city is taken once and remembered by the browser, as a picked city is, then dropped from the address. `openResearchTab` in `Prospects.tsx` drops the three so the tab's name still opens on Skim. `renderWithProviders` takes a `route` for a smoke that opens at an address.
- The line's press opens the city this browser last had on screen when it has some names, else the fullest city.
- No migration, no edge function.

## What the Dashboard read costs

Measured on prod as a dev on 2026-11-11, with no remembered count: 13 requests one after another, about 3,470 rows, about 3.4 seconds in the browser, and 75 ms of counting.

| Read | Requests | Rows | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| The answers, the customers, the newest bids | 3 | 0 + 498 + 397 | 0.6 s |
| San Antonio: Builders (two pages, so `research_builders` runs twice) and the permits by house number | 3 | 1,225 + 787 | 1.8 s |
| Austin: Builders and the permits | 2 | 371 + 103 | 0.3 s |
| New Braunfels: Builders and the permits | 2 | 67 + 17 | 0.2 s |
| San Marcos and Seguin: Builders, no builders so no second read | 2 | 0 | 0.2 s |

It runs for the office and the estimators only, on the Dashboard only, and not while the Dashboard hides its banners. After it, that browser reads nothing for the line for half an hour, or until an answer on a name is saved in it. The second open of the Dashboard in the walk made no Builders read.

## The numbers on the day

One name and customer are a pair in both San Antonio and Austin, so the line's 108 is 107 answers to give; each city's number is kept equal to its chip, and the sum counts that pair twice.

## Not done

- A chip stops at 150 names (`lookAlikePairs`' limit), so a city past 150 would count as 150 on the chip and on the line alike. No city is near it.
- A count can be up to half an hour behind another person's answers or the morning pull.
- Leaving Permits for another Prospects tab leaves `view=builders` on the address until the Permits tab's name is pressed, which clears it.
- A phone or a web domain in Compare stays unbuilt, and is now half within reach. A customer holds a phone and an email in `customers.contact_info`, and Austin's feed carries `contractor_phone` on every row, kept in `permits.raw` with no column of its own, so a phone could be compared for Austin names with no lookup. A San Antonio name has a phone or a website only where someone pressed the Who card's Google lookup (`permit_lookups`, 13 company rows on hand on 2026-11-11), so that half still waits.
