# v2.8456 — Document builder: the Sub NDA's card of changed pages counts them, and the map outlines the open page (2026-11-24)

Punch list #416, part 3, the build *The card of changed pages: its counts, its legend, and an outline on the open cell*, which #404 lists under *Against the mock-up*. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was there

The card under the Sub NDA's map read *Changed since revision 2* and listed each changed page by one string, *Photos and video, Elevated*, with **Open it** and **Back to revision 2**. It gave no count, and nothing said what the marks on the right side mean. The map marked a changed cell, but no cell showed which page was open.

## What changed

- **The count.** The card's head reads *2 pages changed since revision 2*, *Nothing changed since revision 2*, or *Not published yet* (`changedCardHead`, `lib/documentBuilder/changedCard.ts`). The count and the revision are marked with `val` and drawn through `ValueSentence`, so they stand apart from the words.
- **The legend.** Under the head, the two looks a field wears on the right side, by the fields' own class: *As published* and *Changed, not published* (`CHANGED_LEGEND`). It follows the app's theme, as the flow beside it does. Before the first publish nothing is marked, so no legend shows.
- **A row.** A changed topic reads its name, then its level as a chip in the level's colour (`changedRows`). A topic's own name reads *Its name*. The presses still say the whole page aloud. The row for the page open on the right is marked with a bar and `aria-current`.
- **The open cell.** `DocumentBuilderFlow` takes `onShown` and says the page it shows each time it changes: its step, and for a topic the stop it stands on. `shownPageId` turns that into the map's own name for a page. The cell is `aria-pressed`, and `index.css` outlines it 2 px in its level's colour, 1 px off the cell, at every width.
- **It follows the flow, not the last press.** The outline moves on **Next**, **Back**, a press on the step bar and the slider, since the flow says where it is. On *Before you start*, *The basis*, *Match the paper to the site* and *Review* no cell is open.
- **Docs and help**: the glossary's *Document builder* entry, and *use the document builder* under *Read the map*.

## Against the mock-up

- The drawing lists only a topic at a level. The built card also lists the four pages that are no topic, a topic's name and a topic at Off, since a dev can change those too.
- The drawing's cell carries a count of its lines, *3 breach lines, 1 site line*. That is not built: #416 names the card's counts, and a cell's count would crowd a 44 px square.
- The drawing's bar says *2 pages changed. GCs see revision 2 until you publish.* The bar's line is left as it reads. It shares a few lines with the Publish press's question, which another PR is changing tonight.
