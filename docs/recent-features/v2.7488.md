# v2.7488 — Lien desk: the property card (2026-11-16)

PR 2 of 3 of *one property whole* (the kernel is v2.7485). The owner picked the shape on 2026-11-16: the card first, then a *Group by property* picker on By job, with one quiet line for an owner's other property.

## What a person sees

- **By job** (`/lien-desk?tab=timeline`): a row on a property that holds more than one job carries a blue line under its GC, *412 Ridgeline Dr, Hollow Creek · 7 jobs here ›*. A property with one job gets no line, so most rows read as before. A job with no property record reads *No property record* in amber, with **Set the property ›** for the office.
- **The property card** opens over the desk: the address, the owner of record, the kind and the GC; *7 jobs · $15,870 open*, then two lines that count the papers, each kind apart (*4 notices sent Oct 14 · 2 on Sep 22 · 1 to print and mail* and *6 lien affidavits ready to file*); then a row per job with its months, its notice, its affidavit and what it owes. The row the card was opened from is shaded. On a phone the card is the whole screen and each job is a stacked block with 44 px presses.
- A notice's or an affidavit's words are a press that opens that paper where the desk already opens it. The job's number opens the job.
- The foot names the owner's other property when the owner's name and mailing address both match: *Same owner also at 88 Fenwick Ln · 1 job ›*. When the other record reads the same address it says *a second record for this address*, which is one place on two records.
- The card has no verbs. It reads nothing of its own: the first open reads By job's book (three steps, kept), and a later open reads nothing.

## Two things a second report changed

The office's second report was about the liens: *should have 6 liens … only showing 1*. Her count of a property's papers was its lien affidavits, read off a window of notices.

- **Each kind is counted apart.** `lienPropertyTallies` gives the notices and the lien affidavits a line each. Notices that went are counted by day, newest first. An affidavit is counted by its own stage, in the Board's words. The first draft's *6 at the affidavit* blended the two and is gone. `lienNotInRunGroups` and `lienNotInRunWords` are gone too: the run's envelope line hands the tally the jobs that are not in the envelope.
- **The desk's own entries win.** The first look at the card read *To send for Master approval* on six affidavits the Board held in *Ready to file*. The book builds its affidavit queue from `data.items`, which is the notices alone, and without the facts the desk joins on. `buildLienProperties(rows, desk)` now takes the desk's `queue.entries` and `affidavits.entries` (`LienPropertyDesk`), and a job's entry there wins. `LienDesk` also hands the book the affidavits' items (`bookItems`), so By job's own rows stop reading *Draft the affidavit* of a paper already approved.
