# v2.7225 — Paint a map: a picture and a page to print (2026-11-13)

Punch list #345, PR 4b. The owner asked what sharing a map looks like beyond saving it in the app. The link (v2.7223) is the first answer. This is the second: a copy, for the people who will not open a link and for paper.

One rule sorts the sheet: **a link can be ended and stays current; a copy cannot be taken back and goes out of date.** So the link stays on top, the sheet says so above the copies, and every copy says the day it was made.

## What changed

- **Or hand over a copy**, under the link in **Share this map** (`PaintedMapCopies`).
- **Make a picture** draws one image: the view from above, the lines with the faint stretches as dots, the numbered places, the word *Start* at the first point of the first line, and under the map its name, its length, a key of the places with their words, the company, *Made Nov 13, 2026*, and the link's address when one is out on this device. Then **Share the picture** hands it to the phone's own share sheet, or **Save the picture** puts it in Downloads on a computer.
- **Print, or save as a PDF** opens one Letter page: the company, the map's name and words, the picture, each place with its words and its photo, each line's length with its give or take, and, with a link out, a code to scan for the map as it is today with the day the link ends.
- **A print is filed.** The page that printed is kept as a sent copy (`painted_map_print`), on the job when the map has one, under the standing rule that a print counts as a send (`docs/SENT_COPIES.md`).

## How it is built

- `paintedMapPicture.ts`, pure: the flat-map arithmetic every tile is cut by, the closest zoom that holds every point with room around it, which tiles cover the frame and where each is drawn, the file's name, the words a copy carries. Tiles are never asked for deeper than zoom 18, where the view from above exists nearly everywhere; closer than that they are stretched, as the map on the screen does.
- `paintedMapPictureIo.ts`: loads the tiles with `crossOrigin` set, so the canvas can be read back as a file, and waits eight seconds at most for any one. A tile that never comes leaves a grey square and the sheet says part of the picture did not load.
- `paintedMapPrint.ts`, pure: the page's HTML. Every picture in it is a `data:` address, checked by shape, so the filed copy opens the same a year on and the page reaches out for nothing. A name is written as words, never as markup.

## Is this the best we can do

- **No *File it on the job* press.** The card drew one beside Print. The owner's own rule for sends, set 2026-10-05, is that nobody takes an extra step to file anything: the print files itself, and the office finds it on the job's Documents tab.
- **The picture is made on one press and sent on the next.** A phone lets a page open its share sheet only inside a press, and drawing takes a few seconds of tile loading. One press that did both would be refused on an iPhone. Two presses also show the picture before it goes anywhere.
- **The word *Start*.** The first picture out of the headless walk showed a line with two ends alike. On the page a reader has **Directions to the start**; a picture that travels alone has nothing, so the picture says it.
- **No code is made behind the reader's back.** A copy made with no link out carries no code, and the press says *Make a link first and the page carries its code*. Making a link is opening a door, and that stays a press of its own.
- **The key lists ten places.** Past ten it says how many more are on the map, so a picture stays one a thread can show.
