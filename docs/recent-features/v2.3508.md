# v2.3508 — A real person on the What-customers-see strips, and Their journey on the Customer page (2026-09-16)

PR 8 of the train in `to-dos/what-customers-see-journeys/README.md` — the one that changes what the tab is. Client only; reads only; no migration, no edge function, no new tracking.

## Why

The tab answered "what does it look like" with a sample. The office's question is "what happened": did the customer open the agreement, where did they stop, what do they hold. The app records every one of those moments already (`sent_at`, `first_viewed_at`, `signed_at`, `stripe_invoice_status`, the events tables, the portal's office view stats) and showed them nowhere together; the links were copied from six different surfaces.

## What shipped

- **Kernel [`src/lib/journeys/personJourney.ts`](../../src/lib/journeys/personJourney.ts)** (+12 tests): given a customer (homeowner or builder), a sub, a supply house or the law firm and the rows the survey in the to-do names, one `PersonStep` per journey step — `state` (signed · paid · opened · sent · declined · never · n/a), a headline ("Signed on paper Sep 4", "Waiting 10 days · never opened"), a detail line ("J363 · rev 4 · 3 earlier revisions voided"), the link the person holds, and the office's next move. A builder gets the GC strip as well as the homeowner one. The agreement lane picks the latest non-voided contract and counts the voids as history — J363's rev 1 opened once, revised twice, signed on paper reads exactly that.
- **Loader [`loadPersonJourney.ts`](../../src/lib/journeys/loadPersonJourney.ts)**: fans out from the customer id through RLS (jobs by `customer_id` or `gc_customer_id`, then contracts, invoices, hazmat, test reports, demand letters, lien filings and releases by job; estimates and their events; portal links, slug and the office view stats through the same `?preview=1` staff peek the portal globe uses; bid rooms and their events; submittal rooms and events; GC statements). Subs read `sub_portal_links`/`_slugs`, `sub_portal_visit_summary` and `person_contract_documents`; houses read `bid_rfqs`; the firm reads `legal_portal_links`, `legal_firm_recipients`, `legal_notification_queue`. `.in()` fan-outs are chunked so a builder with hundreds of jobs loads.
- **[`PersonJourneyStrips.tsx`](../../src/components/journeys/PersonJourneyStrips.tsx)**: the same strips, one status card per step with a state pill; **Open as <first name> →** opens the real link in a new tab with the preview flag so the office's look never counts as the customer's visit; the action is a plain link to the surface that owns it (nothing here writes). **[`PersonPicker.tsx`](../../src/components/journeys/PersonPicker.tsx)**: kind pills + a name search through the office's own RLS.
- **The tab**: a mode bar, *Sample · A person*, above the toolbar; a person hides the sample strips (they stay mounted, switching back is instant) and shows the strips for them with an *Open their record →* door. Deep link `?who=<kind>:<id>:<name>`.
- **The Customer page** (`CustomerDetail.tsx`): a **Their journey** panel under the money strip for the office roles (`canSeeWhatCustomersSee`), compact cards, and *See it beside the sample →* into Settings preselected.

**Hash-only links are never reconstructed.** Estimates (`public_token_hash`) and sub contracts (nulled on signing) show their state and offer *Resend* / *Open the estimate* instead of a link. Stripe bills open the hosted invoice URL. The hazmat notice uses `?token=`, as its page expects.
