# v2.8305 — A bill written down twice remembers its first amount (2026-11-22)

Punch list #85, one of the residuals the legal portal's review left (`to-dos/legal-portal-firm-ready/README.md`, *Found by the reviews and left for later*). The fault is in the money, not in the portal. One migration, pushed after the merge. No client change and no edge function change.

## Before

- `jobs_ledger_invoices.agreed_write_down_previous_amount` is read everywhere as what the bill was. Job Summary and the legal desk take written off as that amount less the bill's amount (`jobSummaryLedgerView.ts`, `_shared/legalJobMoney.ts`), and the legal portal prints the bill from it.
- Both writers set it to the bill's amount on every write-down. A bill of 500 written down to 450 and then to 400 read *was 450, written off 50*. The first 50 was gone from the totals, and the firm's page printed the bill at 450.
- On prod on 2026-11-22: four bills written down, three of them through Stripe, and none more than once. Each bill's time and kept amount match its one activity line. No row needs its first amount put back.

## What changed

- **Both writers keep the first amount** (`20261122234400_write_down_keeps_first_amount.sql`): `apply_agreed_write_down_to_billed_invoice` for an in-app bill and `service_apply_agreed_write_down_from_stripe` for a Stripe bill, which the edge functions `stripe-invoice-agreed-write-down` and `stripe-webhook` call. Each is its live body with one changed line. A first write-down stores the bill's amount as before, and a later one leaves the column alone.
- **One case moves it.** A fee added after a write-down (a returned check fee, a card fee, a hazmat fee) can take the bill above the kept amount. The next write-down then keeps the amount the bill stood at, since a *was* below the bill's own amount reads as no write-down at all.
- **The newest write-down still owns the rest**: the note, the time, the person and the Stripe credit note id.
- **No reader changes.** Tests on Job Summary's written-off sum and on the legal packet hold a bill written down twice.
- **The bed.** `npm run test:pg:write-down-first-amount` writes a bill down three times on each writer on the whole schema, and runs the fee case on each. CI runs it from `scripts/sql-beds.txt`. Docker is not available on the build Mac, so the scenario was first run on a local Postgres 15 over stubs of the six tables it touches, with the real activity trigger.

## Undoing a write-down

There is no undo. No function clears `agreed_write_down_at` or the kept amount, and neither writer lets a bill go back up. The nearest act is sending the bill back, which deletes the row and its columns with it. The first write-down's line stays on the job's activity. Keeping the first amount changes none of that.

## What it leaves alone, and what the build found

- **The job's activity holds a bill's first write-down only.** `jobs_ledger_invoices_to_activity` writes its `invoice_write_down` line when `agreed_write_down_at` goes from empty to set, and never twice for one bill. So a second write-down's step is on the bill (its note, time and person) and not on the activity, and a third replaces the second's. The owner's direction was to build no table of write-downs if the activity held each step. It does not, so this PR builds nothing for the history and reports it. The small fix would be the trigger writing a line on every write-down.
- **A fee added after a write-down shrinks what reads as written off**, since the readers take the kept amount less the bill's amount and the fee raises that amount. It was so before this change and is so after.
- The activity trigger, the edge functions, the client and the types.

## Deploy

Merge, then `supabase db push`. The functions keep their names, arguments and answers, so the push is safe on either side of the client deploy. Nothing to deploy for the edge functions.
