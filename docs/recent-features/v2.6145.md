# v2.6145 — A new task's schedule drawn on a small calendar (punch list #217, office-13) (2026-10-30)

Punch list #217, *Show it, don't say it*: the scan's office-13 finding, which Taunya picked to build on 2026-10-30. The New task window said its schedule in one green sentence under a row of weekday buttons, *Every Mon & Wed on Hal's list, starting Tue, Nov 3, until Dec 18 — a missed day doesn't carry over*, and the reader could not see which days land, that the start fell on a Tuesday nobody picked, or what *doesn't carry over* means on a calendar.

## What changed

- **`lib/checklistScheduleCalendar.ts`** (new, 11 tests): `checklistScheduleCalendar` over the form's `when`, `repeatMode`, `startDate`, `todayStr`, `daysOfWeek`, `daysAfter`, `endDate` and `dueDate`, the same fields the sentence reads. Four weeks, Sunday first: this week and the next three, or the start's week and the three after it when the start is past next week. Every day the task lands comes from `materializeDates` (`checklistMaterialize.ts`), the one place that decides them, so the calendar and Save agree. Each cell carries its marks (`lands`, `maybe`, `today`, `start`, `startOff`, `end`, `due`, `between`, `dim`) and its words for hover and a screen reader. The notes under the grid: a start off the picked days with the first day that lands (amber, the warning the words gave), the end with the count of days in all, *Then 7 days after each one is done*, and the due day with the days to do it.
- **`components/checklist/ChecklistScheduleCalendar.tsx`** (new): the drawing. A landing day is filled; the start and the end keep a ring inside the fill; a start off the picked days is ringed amber; today is ringed blue; the chain's days for *days after it's done* are dashed, a guess that moves with each done; a one-off's bar runs grey from its day to the due day. Days before the start or past the end are dim. Theme tokens throughout.
- **`ChecklistAddModal.tsx`** and **`ChecklistItemEditModal.tsx`**: the calendar sits above the green sentence, which stays as its caption. It draws for Repeats and On a date, and for Today once a due day is set; a one-off on today with no due day is the sentence alone.
- **The smoke** (`ChecklistAddModal.render.test.tsx`): the calendar appears on Repeats, follows the weekday buttons on and off, keeps the sentence under it, and a one-off on today draws once it has a due day.
- **Guide** *schedule a task for later or make it repeat*: a section on the calendar.

## Decisions

- Four rows, not three: the mock-up draws today's week and the three after it, so a start next week shows three weeks of landings with today still in frame.
- For *days after it's done*, the first day is solid, since it lands for sure, and the days after it are dashed, since each one moves with the one before. The mock-up's words asked for a dashed first day; the drawing says the same thing the right way round.
- No migration, no function change.
