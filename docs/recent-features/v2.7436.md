# v2.7436 — Lien desk: a place to keep the answer to "Is this public property?" (2026-11-16)

Taunya's ask of 2026-11-16, on job 883 sitting in the Board's Ready to file: *"this job is a government job and we can't lien a government job, how should we amend the flow to handle this going forward?"* She approved the second drawing (*"Yes, build it"*), without the bond claim's own steps. This is PR 1 of the train, the database half. No screen changes.

## Why a stored answer

Until now *public* was a guess from the owner's names, made again on every screen (`ownerKind`). Since v2.6954 the guess holds a paper that is being drafted or sent on. Nothing pulled back a paper already approved, nothing took a public job off the Board, and a private owner whose name only sounds public had no door. All three need the answer kept in one place.

## What ships

Migration `20261116221500_property_lien_public_answer.sql` ([its page](../migrations/20261116221500_property_lien_public_answer.md)):

- **Four columns on the property record** (`customer_addresses`): `lien_public_answer` (NULL, `'public'` or `'private'`), `lien_public_answered_at`, `lien_public_answered_by` and `lien_public_reason`.
- **One function**, `set_property_lien_public_answer`, for the lien office (dev, master technician, assistant, controller). It writes the answer. For `'public'` it also writes the payment bond on the ticked jobs and puts each on the attorney's list through the Collections flag that already exists, all in one transaction. It acts on what is ready and names what it held, a job at a time. `'unanswered'` takes an answer back.

**The answer is derived, never copied.** A job is off the lien path because its property says `'public'`. No flag is written on a job, so one change of the answer brings every job at the address back.

Nothing is filed, voided or deleted. The lien papers already made stay as they are. They will stop counting once the desk reads the answer, which is the client work that follows.

## Not in this train

The bond claim's own notices, days and steps. Taunya's attorney sets those.
