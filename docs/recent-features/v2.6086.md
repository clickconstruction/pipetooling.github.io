## What changed

- **The window honors not needed.** The third answer only clears a row if the window stops counting such a charge, so a job whose account at the house is on record as `not_needed` (`job_supply_house_accounts`) now takes its current charges out of the window everywhere: `count_supply_house_move_window()` gains the predicate, `isMovableAgingInvoice` takes the pair's account status ([`supplyHouseAging.ts`](../../src/lib/supplyHouseAging.ts)), the Accounts payable strip reads the single-job pairs' not-needed accounts into `agingMoveData.notNeeded` and keeps those rows off the strip, the worked list and the move modal ([`SupplyHousesTab.tsx`](../../src/components/SupplyHousesTab.tsx)), and the SQL bed (`supabase/tests/supply_house_move_window/`) gains a row on a not-needed account, never counted, and a scenario that marks a counted job's account not needed live, with the parity test reading the same block. The strip and the deck take the rule with this merge; the line's count takes it with the push.
- **The kind** ([`fillInMoveWindow.ts`](../../src/lib/needsYou/fillInMoveWindow.ts), [its test](../../src/lib/needsYou/fillInMoveWindow.test.ts)): the rows from the strip's own predicate over the tab's reads, soonest window first, titled *$2,210.00 at Bluebonnet Supply · #650 Maple Street remodel · closes in 4 days*, red within three days and amber within seven as the line is (`supplyMoveWindowSeverity`); what the app knows: the invoice and its PO, when the window closes (`supplyMoveWindowCloseWords`), the job account's state there, the rep for job accounts with phone and email, an ask already out and how long ago. ## Is this the best we can do?

- The three answers write exactly what the page writes: the move modal's flag and ask rows, the edge function's email, the sheet's account row. Nothing is recorded that the pages would not show.
- Not needed is honest because the predicate changed with it. Until the push the line's count can read one or two higher than the deck's rows for such pairs; the migration page says so.
- The email is one charge per send, where the worked list sends a house's charges together; the deck is one row at a time by design, and the function's "do what is ready" holds a charge that no longer qualifies and names why.
- Not pressed live: the rows on prod are real charges at real houses. Every answer ran through the injected io; the deck was walked live on the real rows as the dev, read-only.
