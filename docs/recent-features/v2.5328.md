# v2.5328 — GC mode: a statement of work email reaches a Spanish-speaking company (2026-10-12)

A one-line fix found by GC Reviews helper 9 while mapping the statement of work's path for cut 4c, and confirmed by the lead session GC Review. `GcProjects.tsx`'s `sowWrites.send` passed the company's stored language (`langs[companyId]`) straight to `sowEmailRequest`. The other trade emails use `tradeMailLang(...)`: the start email, the answer to a question and the invitation. So while `PORTAL_SPANISH_ON` is off, a Spanish-speaking company's statement of work email was refused as `spanishHeld`. The office read *Sent to their portal. The email did not go: Spanish emails wait until a native speaker reads them.* It now goes through `tradeMailLang` and is sent in English, as the others are.

- The Documents tab's paper sends (`papersIo.sendCompanyPaper`) still refuse Spanish up front, before anything is on record. That is deliberate (decision 8) and unchanged.
- **Test**: `GcProjects.render.test.tsx` gains a case. A dev sends the awarded clinic's statement of work, papers all in, to a company whose language is Spanish, with **Email it now**. The email request goes with `lang: 'en'` and no "did not go" note. The case fails with the old line, which was checked by putting it back.
