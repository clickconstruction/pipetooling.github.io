# v2.3265 — "Make the Job Total $X": the Job Total is the input (2026-09-11)

Client only; no migration. Discount tools round two, PR 1 of 4 (design + the "is this the best we can do" pass: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/f56651a5-0d7b-49c3-bdc0-6bfca39ec454).

## Why

The office agrees a number on the phone — "call it $33,500" — not a percent, and then had to work out the discount. The first draft put a "make the total" box on the discount row; the review threw it out (a third input on a row that already has two, and it only existed once a discount row did). The number you agreed is the Job Total, and the Job Total is already on the screen.

## What shipped

- **The footer's Job Total is a button** (dotted underline, "Tap to type the total you agreed") on the Bill tab. Tap → an inline `$` input prefilled with the current total; Enter or blur commits, Escape cancels. A four-second hint under it says what happened.
- **Kernel `applyTargetJobTotal(rows, targetWorkTotal, newRowId, lockedIds)`** in the shared `discountLine.ts`: the wanted discount is work − other discounts − target. One unlocked discount row exists → it becomes a dollar discount of that amount (a percent row switches to dollars); none → a *Negotiated discount* row (reason Negotiated) is added; target at or above the work → the row clears / nothing is added (`cleared`); the amount exceeds the row's basis → `unreachable` with the max; a locked row → `locked`. The shell subtracts riders first, since the displayed total includes them.
- Hints: *typed total · the discount row takes the difference* · *already at or above the work — no discount* · *the discount's lines can't take that much off — widen it to all work lines* · *the discount is on a bill — send it back to change the total*.
- Help: *give a customer a discount* → "Or type the total".
- Tests: 4 kernel cases (add / reuse-and-switch / clear / other rows + locked + unreachable + invalid) and a render smoke of the footer.
