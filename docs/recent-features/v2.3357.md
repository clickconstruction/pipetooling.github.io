# v2.3357 — Bid Costs: a bar slice opens a modal of its bids (2026-09-12)

Client only. Owner: "click on a bar section inside of a bar and see a modal of the contents" (plan + mockup artifact 8910d0ac). Replaces the inline drill panel under the History & forecast panels.

## What changed

- **Kernel** [`src/lib/bids/bidDrill.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/bidDrill.ts) (tests beside it): `cohortDrill(cohorts, monthIndex, bucket)` — the slice's rows, the month's four slices with counts and value, the slice's share of what the month decided (by value), previous / next month indexes, and rollups by estimator and by GC (`rollupBy`, ties broken by value); `filterDrillRows` (estimator / GC chip), `sliceWords`, `bidListText` (clipboard text, largest first).
- **Modal** [`BidListModal.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/BidListModal.tsx): built like `BidBoardWeeklySentCellModal` — z 900 (under the Bid window at 1000), Escape via `useModalStackEntry` (topmost only), backdrop click closes, focus lands on Close. For a bar slice it carries the month: slice chips (Won / Lost / Still open / Open 120+ days) switch in place, ‹ month › and ← → step months keeping the slice, a small stacked bar shows the whole month with the chosen slice outlined, and the sub-line reads the slice's share of the month's decided value. A "By estimator · By GC" chip row narrows the table. Row click → `onOpenBid`; the list stays open beneath. Footer: Copy list.
- **Lens** [`BidsForecastLens.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/BidsForecastLens.tsx): drill state is `{ kind: 'cohort', monthIndex, bucket } | { kind: 'list', title, sub, rows }`; the inline panel is gone; every drill on the lens (odds cards, age buckets, tiles, person rows, decision-time sample) opens the same modal. New `onOpenBid` prop threaded through `BidsBidCostsTab` to [`Bids.tsx`](../../src/pages/Bids.tsx), which answers with `openEditBid(bid, { tab: 'bid' })` (the page's Bid window).
- Chip styles moved from the `border` shorthand to longhands so a `borderColor` override no longer drops the border (React's mixed-shorthand warning).

## Verified

Dev against prod as dev: Lost · Dec ’25 bar → modal (24 bids · $1.60M · 92 % of what Dec ’25 decided); month › → Jan ’26 Lost; Won chip → Won · Jan ’26; an estimator chip narrows the rows; a row opens the Bid window above the list, Escape closes the top window first, then the list. Unit tests, lint, theme check, `tsc -b` clean.
