# v2.3886 — Customers on a phone: the page is the search (2026-09-27)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Punch list **#30**, PR 5a (`to-dos/taunya-mobile.md`; the mock-up `to-dos/taunya-mobile-pr5-rows.html`). The audit measured `/customers` at 375 px as 444 cards, 67,927 px and 3,370 targets, with the search on the third screen. The owner's calls (2026-09-27): *Recent* is recently active; the phone view is built on the Customers page, not on Dispatch Mode's list; archived customers are never listed on a phone.

## What shipped

- **`src/lib/customers/customerPhoneSearch.ts`** (new, pure) — `matchPhoneCustomers` (name, address, phone with or without punctuation, email, the account person; live before archived, the best name match first), `recentPhoneCustomers` (the newest of last job / payment / bid / estimate, 25), `owingPhoneCustomers`, `azPhoneCustomers` (pages of 50), `phoneCustomerSubline` and `phoneActivityWords` (the row's second line; no dollars for a role that cannot see money), `phoneJobSearchFilter` (the PostgREST `or` filter for the job half, the grammar's characters dropped, two characters minimum).
- **`src/components/customers/CustomersPhoneView.tsx`** (new) — the sticky search with **+** beside it; the three lenses while nothing is typed; rows of 44 px with Call and Email as 40 px links; jobs as their own group, read from `jobs_ledger` 300 ms after the typing settles (eight rows, newest first, under the caller's own row security); a customer opens `/customers/:id`, a job opens the job window.
- **`src/pages/Customers.tsx`** — under 640 px the page hands its loaded customers, rollups and counts to the phone view instead of drawing the stat grid, the filter row and the card list. The load is unchanged (it still reads every customer; what changed is that a phone draws 25 rows, not 444 cards). At 640 px and up nothing changes.
- **The guide** *find a customer from my phone* (new).

## Not on a phone, by decision

The stat tiles, the type and lifetime-value sorts, *Show similar*, *Classify* and *Link jobs* — desk work. The guide says so.
