# v2.8537 — Lien desk: the claim shows its cents, as its bills and the notice do (2026-11-24)

Loose end 1 of punch list #415, the Lien desk's leftovers from the ledger (#361). Client only: no migration, no edge function. The pick was Grace's to make, since it is how her desk reads money. Diane answered it on 2026-11-24 from labeled pictures of both ways: cents on both, the cents small.

## What was wrong

In Months on this job the claim was `formatUsdNoCents` and the bills under it were `demandMoney`. So the claim read *$12,700* over lines that read *$12,700.00*. It was more than a look: `formatUsdNoCents` rounds to the dollar, so a claim of $12,700.50 read *$12,701* over bills that add up to $12,700.50, while the notice itself prints $12,700.50.

## What changed

Every amount on the Months card, and the claim's two echoes, now shows its cents through the app's one door for an amount (`Money` / `MoneyText`, v2.8374), which draws the cents smaller.

- [`LienClaimBox.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienClaimBox.tsx): the claim's figure.
- [`LienClaimBills.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienClaimBills.tsx): each bill's Billed, Paid and Owed, and the *Not billed yet* line, which hands `lienUnbilledWords` its own money.
- [`LienDeskMonths.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskMonths.tsx): the figure under *This notice*, and an earlier notice's amount in its column head.
- [`LienDeskNoticePane.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskNoticePane.tsx): the claim it hands the card, the retainage line, and the strip's chips, which draw an amount's cents small. The cents take no line of their own there, so a chip with an amount is as tall as its neighbours.
- [`LienDesk.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDesk.tsx): the claim in the Months title row and on the strip's Claim chip.
- [`LienPaneHead.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienPaneHead.tsx): a title row's fact draws an amount's cents small.
- The guide [send lien notices from the Lien desk](/help/send-lien-notices-from-the-lien-desk) says the figure shows its cents.

## Not changed

- The row on the list at the left, which keeps whole dollars. It is a column to run the eye down.
- The words on a claim set by hand (`claimDeltaWords`, *$500 under the $9,800 unpaid in the app*). Six screens read them, and they are a difference, not the claim.
- The Lien window's own *Not billed yet* note, which keeps whole dollars beside the notes around it.
- Any paper. The notice printed its cents before and prints them now.
