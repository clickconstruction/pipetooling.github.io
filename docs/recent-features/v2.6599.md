# v2.6599 — A matter reads by theme: the investigation over its documents and events, on the Legal desk and the HR file (2026-11-06)

Punch list #269, the remainder of T2b (and of T4's note): the By theme view shipped on the HR file (v2.6547) over a person's entries and evidence, and the picker reached a matter's composers (v2.6548), so marks landed on matter documents and events with no view to read them by. The seat's `plan_theme_marks` then put 153 proposed marks on one person's record in a day, and the matter's own documents carried marks nobody could list. This PR draws the same investigation on a matter.

## What changed

- `src/lib/legal/matterThemeLines.ts` (new): `matterThemeLines(timeline)` turns the legal timeline's documents and entry-backed items (events, statements, planned steps) into the `ThemeLine` shape the theme kernel reads — a document is its own file, an event carries the documents it names; deadlines, the Record of contact, the packet's steps and anything voided are left out. `matterThemeLineLanding` says where *Open on the Timeline* goes (a paper to Evidence, an entry to the Timeline). Tests in `matterThemeLines.test.ts`.
- `src/components/people/personFile/PersonThemesView.tsx` is generic over the line (`L extends ThemeLine`, default `PersonTimelineLine`); nothing on the HR file changes.
- `src/components/jobs/legal/MatterThemesView.tsx` (new): the matter's view over `PersonThemesView` — the paper's chip opens it through `openLegalMatterDocument`, Keep and Take it off write through `legalThemesIo`, the pick is local to the view. Render test `MatterThemesView.render.test.tsx`.
- The HR file's Matter view (`PersonMatterPanel.tsx`): a fourth sub-tab **By theme** with the live mark count, the catalog and the matter's marks read by `loadThemes({ matterId })` once a matter stands; *Open on the Timeline* switches to the Evidence or Timeline sub-tab.
- The Legal desk (`legalDeskShared.tsx`, `LegalDesk.tsx`, `LegalDeskPacketTab.tsx`): `TABS` gains `themes` (*By theme*), drawn in the strip only when the hand may mark and the account has a matter; the tab reads the same timeline the Timeline tab builds (`buildTimeline`, shared by both), a paper landing on its Evidence row through `onShowOnEvidence`. `/legal-desk?…&tab=themes` opens on it.
- Docs: `HR_FILES.md` → By theme, `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` → `/legal-desk`, the Glossary's *Themes on the record*; the guide *keep private employee files* → Read the record by theme; the card's T2b and T4 rows, status and next.
