PR 2 of [`to-dos/po-generator-stated-need`](../../to-dos/po-generator-stated-need.md) (punch list #25), taken up on the owner's *"help me build #25, asking yourself is this the best we can do along the way"*. Migration `20260922130000_po_generator_stated_need_set.sql`; client on both doors.

## What the ledger said

PR 1 (v2.3599) named the box *What they said they need* on both doors, carried the claim in the text to the tech and read it back on the invoice form. The live ledger three days later, read on this branch's dev server:

- 55 codes on the ledger; **one** real claim ever typed (*Two tubes*, 8/3, from the phone). Four more are keyboard-mash on a test job.
- Two codes minted since v2.3599 shipped (both 9/21, both by the office assistant): **both blank**.
- At Reece, the house with half the payable balance, 3 of 117 invoices with a PO # carry a five-digit code; the other 114 say what the tech said at the counter (*Auto Zone*, *ATI Schertz*, *Take 5 Seguin*, *Tuscarora*).

So the to-do's PR 2 as planned — option C, a Material / Tool / Other kind chip — was **not built**: a chip adds a decision at the counter to a box nobody fills, and its payoff (a tool landing in a job's material cost) cannot be judged until claims exist. The reason the box is empty is not its label; it is its place. The office mints the code first — the tech is asking for a number, not offering a list — and the desktop door showed the code in a toast and nothing more, so the moment to ask passed with nothing on screen to ask it.

## The change

- **The question moves after the code.** [`StatedNeedEditor`](../../src/components/materials/StatedNeedEditor.tsx) (new, shared by both doors): in `ask` mode it is the open box with *What did they say they need?* and **Write it down**, focused, shown when nothing was typed before Generate; once written it is the claim under its *Said they need* label with *change*. In `link` mode it is a ledger row: *add what it was for…* when blank, the claim (and *change*, off on the phone list) when not. Enter writes, Escape cancels; a blank in `ask` mode has nothing to write and the button says so.
- **Materials → PO Generator** ([`MaterialsPoGeneratorTab`](../../src/components/materials/MaterialsPoGeneratorTab.tsx)) — a **just-minted card** between the form and the ledger: the code large (the phone's shape), job · person · house, the editor in `ask` mode, **Copy** (the `poCodeSummaryLine`, as the phone's Copy and Text to carry) and **Done**. The *Said they need* cell of every ledger row is the editor in `link` mode. The toast stays. Nothing about the box before Generate changed — someone who already knows still types it there, and the card then shows it instead of asking.
- **Dispatch Mode → PO** ([`DispatchModePo`](../../src/components/dispatchMode/DispatchModePo.tsx)) — the result screen's claim block is the editor in `ask` mode (the same block when written; the question when not); `PoResult` carries the row id. The Recent POs list's blank rows read *add what it was for…*.
- **The writer** — [`20260922130000_po_generator_stated_need_set.sql`](../migrations/20260922130000_po_generator_stated_need_set.md): `set_material_po_generator_stated_need(p_id, p_notes)`, the table's first update door, changes `notes` and nothing else; gated exactly as the mint (dev / master_technician / assistant with access to the row's job); the read-only statement trigger still fires inside it. [`setPoCodeStatedNeed.ts`](../../src/lib/materials/setPoCodeStatedNeed.ts) is the one client call (typed loosely until the regen after the push). Both doors rewrite their own row with `withStatedNeed` — no refetch, the ledger keeps its order.
- **Kernel** ([`poCodeStatedNeed.ts`](../../src/lib/materials/poCodeStatedNeed.ts)): `STATED_NEED_ASK_AFTER`, `STATED_NEED_ADD_LINK`, `STATED_NEED_CHANGE_LINK`, `STATED_NEED_SAVE_LABEL`, `normalizeStatedNeed` (trim, blank → null — what the RPC's `NULLIF(btrim())` stores), `withStatedNeed`. +4 tests.
- Guides *raise a purchase order* and *run the day from your phone with Dispatch Mode*; `GLOSSARY` (PO Generator ledger), `ACCESS_CONTROL` (the shared gate), `MATERIALS_TABS_ARCHITECTURE` (the RPC and the shared editor).
