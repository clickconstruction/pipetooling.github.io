# v2.6147 — Each Quickfill station's rhythm as a two-week strip (punch list #217, office-8, a prototype) (2026-10-30)

Punch list #217, *Show it, don't say it*: eight scans found 131 places where the app tells instead of shows, and a mock-up page (`to-dos/show-not-tell/mockup.html`) draws each as a before/after pair for the owner to pick from. Of office-8, the Quickfill stations' rhythm line, the owner asked on 2026-10-30 to see it as a prototype, live, side by side with how the screen looks today, before she approves. This is that prototype: a draft PR, no auto-merge. No migration, no edge function.

## The finding

On the Roller's **Board** and the phone's round list every station carried a grey line — *every 3 d · 2 d over · Rosa*, *every 7 d · due Tue*, *marked 9:14 AM · Cora* — from `describeRoundRow` in `src/lib/quickfill/round.ts`: *every 3 d* is a median over the station's last five marks and *2 d over* a subtraction, over marks the row never drew. A steady every-third-day station and a 1 · 1 · 7 · 1 · 3 one both read *every 3 d*. The phone head read its four counts as a sentence, *4 due · 2 due today · 3 not yet · 9 fresh*.

## What changed

- **`stationRhythmStrip.ts`** (`src/lib/quickfill/`, new, beside `round.ts` and `markHistory.ts`): the pure kernel. `stationRhythmStrip(row, marks, now, hourNow)` gives one cell a day from fourteen days back to four days ahead (`RHYTHM_STRIP_DAYS_BACK`, `RHYTHM_STRIP_DAYS_AHEAD`, nineteen cells, today at the fifteenth) — the looks that landed on each company day (`calendarYmdInAppTzFromIso`, the Mark history window's calendar), the last five flagged as the ones the rhythm reads (`ROUND_RHYTHM_WINDOW`), the day the next look falls due (`rhythmDueYmd`: the last mark plus the station's rhythm; the flat 30 h rule under three marks; day one for a station never marked, which is what the empty state means here), where that day lies when it is off the strip (`duePlace` *before* or *after*), the red run from the day after the due day up to today on a due or never-marked station, the state chip (`rhythmStripChip`: *2 d over* · *due today* · *due Tue* · *fresh · 9:14 AM* · *never marked* · *opens the page* · *after 3 p.m.*) and the strip's own sentence for the hover and the screen reader (*5 looks in two weeks · every 3 d · due Tue, Oct 28 · 2 d over · Rosa*). `rhythmStripAxis` is the one label row under a list of strips (the first day, *today*, the last day), since every row runs the same days. `rhythmMarksBySection` groups the round's events by station. `roundHeadChips`, `filterRoundRows` and `roundHeadChipWords` turn the phone head's four counts into chips that filter the list, *due* always drawn and the rest only above zero, as `roundHeadline` prints them; a pressed chip hides the personal doors, which have no state. Thirteen tests in `stationRhythmStrip.test.ts`.
- **`QuickfillStationRhythmStrip.tsx`** (`src/components/quickfill/`, new): the picture — a grid of nineteen cells, a filled dot (`--text-strong`; `--border-strong` for a look older than the five the rhythm reads; a ring for a day with two marks) on a day with a look, `--bg-red-100` on a late day, today on `--bg-blue-tint` with a `--text-link` outline, the days ahead faint, a dashed `--text-red-600` line on the due cell's left edge (the first cell's when the due day is before the strip, the last cell's right edge when it is after). `RhythmStripChip` is the chip, `QuickfillRhythmStripAxis` the label row. The strip is `role="img"` with the kernel's sentence.
- **`QuickfillBoard`**: two shapes over the same rows — `BoardCards`, the Board as it was, and `BoardStrips`, every group as a row (the name with its chip, the strip, the count) with one axis under them. A per-person switch in the header, **Show the strips** / **Show today's look** (`useShapeView('quickfill-rhythm-strip')`, remembered in localStorage per user, off by default), picks the shape; with `?sbs=1` on the page and a dev signed in the dialog draws both, one above the other, captioned **Today** and **With the strip**, with no switch. Two new props from the page: `marksOf` (each station's marks, `rhythmMarksBySection` over `roundEvents`) and `hourNow`.
- **`QuickfillRoundList`**: the same two shapes — the list as it was, and `RoundListBody strip`: the head's counts as chips that filter the list (a chip that leaves nothing says *No sections match the filters* and offers **Clear the filters**), each row with its chip and its strip under the name, the count on the name line, the axis at the foot of the list. The same switch key, so the Board and the phone agree; the press sits above the search box. One new prop, `marksOf`.
- **`Quickfill.tsx`**: `rhythmMarksOf` (a memo over `roundEvents`, the sixty days the chips already read) passed to both; no new read.
- **`src/components/prototypes/SideBySide.tsx`**: the shared helper the #217 prototypes use — `useShapeView(key)` (the switch and the `?sbs=1` dev flag), `SideBySide` (the two captioned blocks), `ShapeSwitch` (the press), with `readShapeView` / `writeShapeView` / `shapeViewStorageKey` / `sideBySideRequested` under them. The same file, byte for byte, as PR #6528 (office-5) carries, written there first; whichever lands second keeps one. `SideBySide.render.test.tsx` (new) holds it: the key per card and per person, off until written, the press that turns the shape on and remembers it, both blocks for a dev under `?sbs=1`, the switch alone for anyone else.
- **Smokes** `QuickfillBoard.rhythmStrip.render.test.tsx` and `QuickfillRoundList.rhythmStrip.render.test.tsx`: today's look first with the grey line and the sentence, the strips and chips after the press and remembered, a chip filtering the list and the empty words when it leaves nothing, both shapes under `?sbs=1`.
- **Guide** *run the office day with Quickfill*: the Board and the phone round paragraphs name the press and say what the strip shows.
- **Docs**: the Board's and the round list's lines in `QUICKFILL_ARCHITECTURE.md`.

## What the mock-up asked for that this does not do

- **The due label on the axis.** The mock-up's label row carried *due Oct 28* in red under one row's due line. Every row has its own due day, so one shared label row cannot name them; the due day is in each row's chip (*2 d over*, *due Tue*) and in the strip's hover sentence (*due Tue, Oct 28*).
- **A wider name column.** The mock-up's second pass widened the names to 116 px and more; the Board's rows give the name up to 230 px and cut it with an ellipsis past that, since the dialog is 960 px at most and the strip needs the room.
- **The Timeline strip as the drawing.** The finding named `timelineStrip.ts` as a reuse; no file of that name exists in the repo, so the strip is its own small grid, in the Mark history window's calendar and colours.

## Seeing it beside today's look

A dev server from this branch, then `/dev-login?as=1&to=/quickfill?sbs=1` (or open `/quickfill?sbs=1` once signed in). On a desktop the Roller's **Groups ▾** opens the Board, which draws **Today** (the cards) above **With the strip** (the rows). Under 640 px wide (the pane at phone width) the page is the round list, which draws **Today** above **With the strip** the same way. Without `?sbs=1` both read as today, and **Show the strips** on either turns the prototype on for that person on that device.
