# v2.3436 — Demand letter: email it with the PDF (2026-09-14)

Edge function **`send-lien-filing-email` changed** (redeploy after merge: `bash scripts/deploy-functions.sh send-lien-filing-email`); no migration. PR 4 of the *Demand letter, itemized* train (`to-dos/demand-letter-itemized/`; PR 1 v2.3425, PR 2 v2.3429, PR 3 v2.3433). Owner decision taken as proposed: email as a second channel, certified mail the default.

## Why

The § 53.056 notice has emailed from the app since v2.2645; the demand letter was record-only by the v2.2640 "keep the legal path physical" reading. Presentment under CPRC § 38.002 needs no method, and a letter that arrives twice — email today, certified mail behind it — is read sooner.

## What shipped

- **`send-lien-filing-email`** gains `email_type` (`'lien_filing_notice'` default · `'demand_letter'`): it sets the send log's `email_type` and the default subject (*Final demand for payment — <job>*) and body. Everything else unchanged — Bearer JWT, RLS read of the job as the access check, Resend, the `resend_email_id` back. The function's own comment already called its shape instrument-agnostic; this is the second instrument.
- **The modal**: **Email with the PDF…** beside *Save & record send…* opens a sheet — the payer's email prefilled from the bill, the sentence that certified mail is what proves delivery, **Send · N documents**. It builds the packet (letter + exhibits, v2.3429), sends it as one attachment with the subject *Final demand for payment — Invoice #… · $balance* and a short body, and records the send with `sent_method = 'email'`, `tracking_number = 'resend:<id> → <email>'`, today as `sent_at`, and the exhibits with their page counts — the same row the deadline watch reads. `recordSend` takes the channel; the existing certified-mail sheet is unchanged.
- **Email catalog** ([`emailCatalog.ts`](../../src/lib/emailCatalog.ts)): `demand_letter` under *Lien paperwork* (server-built, hardcoded wording, sent by `send-lien-filing-email`).
- `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` → send-lien-filing-email: the `email_type` body field. Guide *send a final demand letter*: the email channel.
