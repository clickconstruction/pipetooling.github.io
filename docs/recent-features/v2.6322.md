# v2.6322 — The Job Mode clocking guide says what a punch with no signal does (punch list #225) (2026-11-01)

Punch list #225 (`to-dos/job-mode-clocking-guide-offline.md`, deleted in this PR; `to-dos/README.md` keeps the closing line), found 2026-10-31 in v2.6198's work on #163's live-test defects. The guide *clock in and out with Job Mode* (`src/content/help/job-mode-clocking.md`) still described the clock before the offline queue: a punch with no signal showed *No connection — the app couldn't reach the server, so nothing was saved* with Retry, nothing was recorded, and the person tapped Retry once they had a bar. Since #163's queue (v2.6099) a Clock In or Clock Out with no signal is kept on the phone with the moment it was pressed and sent on its own when the signal returns, and since v2.6198 the Dashboard clock draws the kept press even when the Dashboard is opened again before the signal is back. A crew member reading the guide was told the opposite of what the app does. Words only: no code, no migration, no edge function.

## What changed

- **When you have no signal** says what the app does: the phone keeps the press with the moment you pressed, the sheet closes as it always does, you do not press again, the line at the top shows the press waiting, the phone sends it on its own when the signal returns and the line turns green until Got it, your time counts from the moment you pressed, and a punch that lands late is filed like typed hours. It links *keep working when the signal drops* (`/help/keep-working-when-the-signal-drops`) for the line, the list behind it and what needs a signal, instead of repeating that guide.
- **The example *Clocking in from a basement*** walks the queue: the sheet closes, the line at the top reads *Clock in from 7:58 AM · waiting to send*, the stairs an hour later, the green *sent ✓* line and Got it, the time counted from 7:58.
- **The clock-out sentence** under *What the clock-out review asks, and why*: Complete clock out closes the window the same way, the small message reads *Kept on the phone. It goes out when the signal is back.*, your time ends at the moment you pressed, and the press goes out when the signal returns.
- **The paragraph on screens that only show things** no longer says they show "the same Retry" as a punch, since a punch shows none. It says the Job Mode card draws the day as last read under its stamp, and that the read screens show a No connection line with Retry and read again by themselves when the signal returns. It quotes no sentence from those panels, so punch list #224's read words change nothing here.
- **A job note, a field report and its photos** are named as presses that wait the same way (v2.6100, v2.6265), in place of "the same Retry appears when you save a report offline"; a Turnaway report is named as one that needs a signal.
- **Keywords** gain *no signal*, *kept on the phone* and *waiting to send*, so the help search finds the section.

## Why the words stop short of "the card shows you clocked in"

On `main` the Job Mode card does not draw a kept press by itself. Its reload runs when the press is kept, fails with no signal, and leaves the kept day from the last read on screen under the stamp (`DashboardJobModeCard.tsx`, `reload` and `useKeptRead`; v2.6198's *Known, not built* names the clock-out case). The Dashboard clock does draw the kept press (v2.6198), but on the Job Mode screen its mount is visually hidden (`Dashboard.tsx`, the Job Mode early return), so a crew member does not see it. The guide therefore says the card draws your day as it was last read until the press lands and then reads it again, which is true today, and leans on the line at the top, which is true on every screen. The sentence to amend when the card draws a kept press is *The card draws your day as it was last read until the press lands.*
