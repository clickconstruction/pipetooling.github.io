# v2.5965 — A customer’s page says what went wrong in plain words (2026-10-26)

Punch list #184, sweep D, PR 3, part 2 of 2: the public pages' voice (`to-dos/consistency-sweeps/README.md` → D, patterns P1, P3 and P4 on the public pages). The error codemod (v2.5935) skipped these pages on purpose, because a customer never reads raw server text. Before, every catch on them said *check your connection* whatever had failed (a page bug, a 502 whose body would not parse), and every refused answer showed the function's `error` field whatever it held. Sometimes that is a sentence our function wrote for the person (*This offer has expired — call the office if you still want it.*), and sometimes *Bad request*, *Missing token* or *Internal error*. Now the first kind comes through, in the page's language, and the second never does. No migration, no edge function.

## The kernel and the note

- `src/lib/portal/portalFailure.ts`: three classes, decided by structure (v2.2843), never by text. A thrown value reads through `errorKindOf` (`portalFailureKind`): a lost signal is `network`, any other throw is `refused`. An answer that said no reads by its status (`portalFailureCause`): a load answered 404 or 410 is `gone`, anything else `refused`. `portalFailure(cause, act, lang, { phone })` returns `{ kind, title, detail, message, tryAgain, phone, known }`; `portalFailureFromError`, `portalFailureFromStatus` and `portalFailureFromAnswer` wrap it.
- The class words, in English and Spanish, are the `fail*` keys of `src/lib/gc/portalI18n.ts`: *No connection. Nothing was sent. Check your signal and try again.* (a send) or *No connection. Check your signal and try again.* (a load); *This link is no longer active. Please call the office for a new one.*; *We couldn't record that.* (a send) or *We couldn't open this page.* (a load), then *Please call the office.* where the page has the office's number and shows **Call the office**, or *Please try again later.* where it has none. Try again shows for a lost signal and for a refused load, never for a gone link or a refused send.
- The known sentences: `knownPortalRefusal(text)` matches an answer's `error`, exactly after trim, against the 119 sentences the public functions write for the person reading. Each is a `said*` key of `portalI18n.ts`, its English the function's words verbatim and its Spanish a translation in the file's usted voice. `portalFailureFromAnswer(status, body, act, lang)` makes that sentence the failure's title in the page's language, with *Please call the office.* after it where the sentence leaves the person no step of their own and the page has the number. Otherwise it reads exactly the class words of the status. The status still decides the class, so a dead link's own sentence keeps the gone link's presses and a slug's miss still falls through on the customer portal. A page keeps the cause (`PortalFailureCause`, the class or the class with the key), so the words follow a language picked after the failure.
- The inventory, by page, kept / left to the class words: the sub portal 24 / 8; the customer portal's requests, its six asks and the GC cards (`submit-portal-request`) 35 / 8 and its load 1 / 3; the owner records card 8 / 5; the bid room 8 / 10; the job contract 11 / 4; the estimate 6 / 16; the signing page 3 / 20; the submittal room 19 / 8; the word ask 5 / 7; the pay link 1 / 4; the supply house 2 / 4; the law firm's confirm page 3 reasons; the terms 0 / 4. Left are the technical words (*Missing token*, *Bad request*, *printedName is required*), the pass-throughs and the bare *Something went wrong* / *Could not save signature* failures, which the class words already say better. A sentence built from a name or a date (the word ask's per-GC checks, the second signer's *has already signed*) cannot be matched exactly and reads the class, unless the page words its code or flag (`frame_signed`, `nameMismatch`).
- `src/components/portal/PortalFailureNote.tsx` draws the sentence with **Try again** when it can help and the page passes the re-run, and **Call the office** (`telHrefFor`) from the failure's own `phone`, so the button and *Please call the office.* always agree. `doorsOnly` draws only the presses, for a page that shows the heading and sentence itself or a form that shows its own error line. Single-theme light, in the portal's colors.
- A page keeps its own words for a structured `code` it knows: the bid room's `closed`, `empty` and `stale_revision`; the contract's `voided`, `expired` and `empty`; the estimate's `already_accepted` and `expired` (now on an approval and a decline too, not only the load); the paper's `paper_changed` and `form_invalid`; the review room's `office`, `preview`, `closed`, `not_found`, `watching`, `stale_revision` and `identify` (`roomRefusalWords`, `src/lib/submittals/submittalRoom.ts`); the owner records card's `nameMismatch` flag.

## Is this the best we can do?

Not yet, in three places:

- The known list is exact by design, so a function sentence built from a name or a date reads the class words. The fix that keeps every reason in both languages is for the functions to answer a refusal `code` the page words itself, as the bid room's codes already do. That needs a function deploy, so it is listed, not done. Until then the source test keeps the list and the functions in step.
- *Please call the office.* is the honest next step, since the reason for a class refusal is only in the function's log. Nothing in the app lists a customer's refused sends yet. A small log the office reads would let the page say *The office can see why* and mean it.
- A refused send offers no Try again, and a 429 without a known sentence reads as a refusal rather than *wait an hour*. The offline sentence here is the portal's own, not the app's `OFFLINE_ERROR_MESSAGE`, which says *the app* and carries a dash. The two could become one kernel.
