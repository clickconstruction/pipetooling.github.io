# v2.4947 — Where the checks went: bills list in number order (2026-10-08)

Found while building the checks sheet's PDF (v2.4913, punch list #55). The checks kernel (`supabase/functions/_shared/gcChecksApplied.ts`) sorted a check's lines by job label, then bill label, with a plain `localeCompare`. So a check over twelve bills read *Invoice 1, Invoice 10, Invoice 11, Invoice 12, Invoice 2…*, and *10 Cedar Ln* came before *9 Cedar Ln*.

## The change

- `byLabel` compares with `{ numeric: true }`. It orders a check's lines (job, then bill) and breaks ties in the job table (same still-open amount).
- Every reader of the kernel follows: Find a check and its sheet and CSV in GC Review, and *Your payments* on the customer portal.

## Deploy

The kernel is shared. `customer-portal` and `gc-statement-email-dispatch` carry the new order once redeployed. Nothing breaks before then; their lines keep the old order.

## Tests

`gcChecksApplied.test.ts` +1: one check over twelve bills on each of jobs *9* and *10*. Job 9 comes first, its bills run 1 to 12, and the job table ties in the same order.
