# v2.8138 — Permits: Builders and the call list count our bids the way the table of companies does (2026-11-20)

Punch list #405 ([the card](../../to-dos/permits-market-page/README.md)), the second of three lines its audit of 2026-11-20 left under *Seen while building, not done*: **the practice bids count as our bids on the call list.** This is the migration, which ships alone. The client that reads the same rule follows it.

## What was wrong

Market shows one name twice. **Call this week** tags it *We bid 4, won 0* or *Never bid*. The table under it, **Do the companies we bid build here?**, gives it a row with **Our bids** and **Won**.

- The tag came from `research_builders`, which counted every bid ever made under the name. A digital twin's practice bid sits in `bids` under the customer of the bid it copies, so it counted.
- The row came from the client (`bidCompanies`), which leaves a practice bid out and looks back two years.

So one name could read two counts on one page. Builders reads the same function, so its **Our bids** column had the larger count too.

## The fix

The table's rule is the rule. `research_builders` now counts by it.

- **A bid counts** when it is no practice bid and was made within two years. A practice bid is one whose project name starts with the word ZZ, the ZZ convention. Two years is 730 days back from today, by the day on the company calendar.
- **It counts under** its customer's name and under the old builders list's name on it, once for one name. Never under a ZZ name. Never under a customer named for a bid platform, which is the bucket a platform's bids sit under until a GC is chosen.

On the database the rule has one home: `research_bid_counts` and `research_bid_party_key`, two small functions that `research_builders` calls. [The migration's page](../migrations/20261120150000_research_builders_our_bids.md) has both.

## What the office sees

- **Market, Call this week:** *We bid N, won N* and *Never bid* count the bids the table under them counts.
- **Builders:** the **Our bids** column and the builder's sheet count the bids of two years, with no practice bid.
- The day of a name's last bid is the company calendar's day.
- Nothing else on the pages moved. The function returns the same columns.

A read of prod before the push, counts only, said how many names move and that none turns into *Never bid*. The counts are in the session's report and are not kept in this public folder.
