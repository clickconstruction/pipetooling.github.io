# v2.3326 — Test reports: the Send button moves to the modal's sticky footer; "Test Test" subjects fixed (2026-09-11)

Client + the shared email builder; no migration. Redeploy `auto-send-test-reports` (it builds subjects server-side); `send-test-report` takes the subject from the client.

## Why

Taunya: "Trying to resend the gas test and it won't let me." The row and the email log showed the gas report on job 1020 sent once (delivered) and no second attempt. The send sheet's own **Send** button sat at the very bottom of the sheet, below the message box — off screen in the modal — while the sticky footer's copper **Send again…** button was disabled the moment the sheet opened. Everything visible looked dead.

## What shipped

- `TestReportSendSheet` gains `onSendStateChange` and reports `{ label, canSend, sending, send }`; with it set, the sheet renders no buttons of its own (a one-line hint says where the button is) and scrolls itself into view on open. `TestReportModal` keeps the sheet's state and renders **the footer's copper button as the Send** while the sheet is open (label from the sheet — *Send* / *Send again* / *Send without the link* / *Sending…*; dimmed and disabled until the sheet's blockers clear, with the reason in the title). Without the prop the sheet falls back to its old inline buttons.
- `testReportEmailSubject`: a label that already ends in "Test" gets " Report" (*Gas Test Report — 12925 FM 20*, *Pinpoint Test Report — …*); hydrostatic labels keep " Test Report". Test updated.

## Verified

Kernel tests; typecheck · lint · theme check. Dev server: open a sent report → *Send again…* → the sheet opens in view, the footer reads **Send again** and sends; with the To emptied the footer dims and the sheet lists the blocker.
