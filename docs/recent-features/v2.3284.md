# v2.3284 — "Their Word" PR 3: the reliability line on every Billed row, and the forecast's usual-slip adjustment (2026-09-11)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Stacked on v2.3283 (PR 2) and v2.3280 (PR 1). Reads `list_payment_promise_records` (v2.3280) and the pay-speed RPC's `receipts` (v2.2288+).

## Why

The lean plan's third step: a reliability reading that never says "never asked". The pay-speed model already knows each customer's measurable bill→money gaps; their *spread* is the capture-free version of "how predictable are they", available from day one. The promise record (PR 1's kernel) adds the one thing pay-speed can't — whether their word means anything — when promises exist.

## What shipped

- **Kernel [`src/lib/jobs/paymentReliability.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/paymentReliability.ts)** (+9 tests): `paySpeedSpread(receipts)` → the 25th–75th percentile of `gapDays` (min–max under four samples; null under `RELIABILITY_MIN_RECEIPTS` = 2), `formatPaysIn` ("Pays in 9–41d" / "Pays in ~30d"), `reliabilityBars` (the last `RELIABILITY_SPARK_BARS` = 6 receipts oldest → newest, height relative to the tallest, tone fast ≤ median · slow ≤ 1.5× · late beyond), `buildReliabilityLine(receipts, record)` joining the spread with PR 1's `formatKeptRecord` / `formatUsualSlip` plus a hover title spelling out the sources, and `slipAdjustedYmd(promisedYmd, usualSlipDays)`.
- **[`BilledReliabilityLine`](../../src/components/jobs/BilledReliabilityLine.tsx)** — rendered by the Billed chip renderer in `JobsStagesTab` (so the unified table and the phone cards both get it) right after the chip and the *They said…* link, on invoice rows only (job-shell rows have no bill clock). Receipts come from `billedPaySpeeds.receipts[customer_id]`; the record is looked up by the **payer** (`gc_customer_id ?? customer_id`, PR 1's key) and passed only when `canMarkPromisedPay` — primary sees "Pays in …" alone. Empty when nothing is known.
- **`promiseRecordsByCustomer`** — a new Stages state: `list_payment_promise_records` → `parsePromiseRecordsRpc` → `classifyPromises(records, today in APP_CALENDAR_TZ)` → `buildCustomerPromiseRecords`; office roles only, fail-soft, refreshed after *They said…* saves and after Mark paid. `promiseSlipByCustomer` (usual slip ≥ 1 day) derives from it.
- **Forecast** — `buildBilledPaymentForecast(rows, paySpeeds, today, promises, slipByCustomer?)`: a promised row is bucketed and sorted by `forecastYmd` = promise + the payer's usual slip (`slipDays` on the row; null when unpromised or slip < 1 day); the chip/model keeps the promised date. `ForecastRow` gains `slipDays` / `forecastYmd`. The modal shows *· usually slips ~9d* after the promised marker. +2 tests.
- Help: `know-whether-a-customer-keeps-their-word` "Where this shows up" now describes the line and the forecast; `see-when-a-customer-will-pay` gets a "The line under the chip" section.
