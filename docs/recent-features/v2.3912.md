# v2.3912 — People → Users: the tab loads its own signals (2026-09-27)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Punch list **#46** row 6 (`to-dos/decomposition-queue.md`): the People map's step 2 (`docs/PEOPLE_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md` → "Recommended extraction order").

## What shipped

- **`src/lib/people/personActiveProjects.ts`** — `groupActiveProjectsByPerson(steps, workflows, projects)`: steps → their workflow's project → one list per assigned name (trimmed), each project once, sorted by name. It was the second half of the page's `loadPersonProjects`.
- **`src/hooks/useUsersTabRowSignals.ts`** — `useUsersTabRowSignals({ canSeePushStatus, canAccessContracts })` → `{ pushEnabledUserIds, contractSigningStatusByPersonName, personProjects }`. The same three reads the page made (`push_subscriptions`; `person_contract_documents` through `rollupContractSigningStatusByPersonName`; `project_workflow_steps` → `project_workflows` → active `projects`), behind the same gates.
- **`src/components/people/PeopleUsersTab.tsx`** — calls the hook and owns `archivedSectionOpen` and `loggingInAsId`. Ten props go: the three signal values, `locationEnabledUserIds`, the two `externalSubProjectsExpanded` props, and the two flags with their setters. 41 → 31 props.
- **`src/pages/People.tsx`** — 7 `useState`, 3 effects and `loadPersonProjects` leave. 4,273 → 4,128 lines. `usePeopleRoster` no longer takes `loadPersonProjects` and `loadPeople` no longer awaits it.
- **Dead code deleted** — `locationEnabledUserIds` (its effect called `get_location_enabled_user_ids` on every dev's page load; the tab declared the prop and never read it) and `externalSubProjectsExpanded` (the External Subcontractors group it served was retired in v2.2762).
- **Tests** — `people/personActiveProjects.test.ts` (5), `contractSigningRollup.test.ts` (4: the three lights, newest version wins in either row order, no rows, the icon titles), `PeopleUsersTab.render.test.tsx` (the tab's first render smoke: the active project its own load finds is linked once under the right name).

## What changes on screen

- The three signals load when Users opens (it is the page's first tab), and again each time you come back to it — they used to load once per page visit, on any tab.
- The page's first paint no longer waits on the active-projects read; the **Active projects** line arrives a moment after the rows.
- The active projects are not re-read after a roster write (add, archive, restore). Nothing on this page changes them.
- The Archived fold starts closed each time Users mounts; it used to stay as you left it while you visited another People tab.

## Left in the page

`setTrainingMode` and `setNeedsSupervision`: they call `loadPeople` and sit behind two role gates, so moving them trades props for props. The roster form, the invite confirm and Hire stay page-level.
