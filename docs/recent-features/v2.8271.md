# v2.8271 — Messages: a payment promise marks the line it was written from (punch list #129, the leftovers) (2026-11-21)

Punch list #129 (`to-dos/estimator-money-word/README.md`), the client half of the owner's call of 2026-11-21 (*Yes, build it*: a word written from a line in Messages also marks that line's promise on Follow up). The database half is v2.8268 (`write_down_payment`, `20261122024419`), pushed before this opened. No migration, no edge function, nothing in the kernels copy.

## What changed

- **The sheet says what it wrote** (`SetPromisedPayDateModal`): a new `onWord`, fired before `onSaved` with `{ kind: 'promise', ymd }` after a promise saves and `{ kind: 'never', ymd, claim }` after **Never said that**. **Clear date** says nothing, since the promise stays on record, and neither does a paid claim, which names no day they owe by. Every other caller of the sheet is unchanged.
- **The io** (`followUpThreadIo.ts`): `writeDownPayment(messageId, ymd | null)` calls `write_down_payment`, the day left off the call when there is none so the function's default takes the mark off. Optional on the io, as `loadPushHours` is, so a stand-in without it draws as before.
- **The slot** (`useFollowUpThreadSlots`): the sheet it opens under a customer's line hands `onWord` to `markLine`. A promise marks that line with the day saved, which is the sheet's day and not the day read from the line. **Never said that** on a promise takes the day off every line of the thread that carries it as a payment (`linesMarkedFor`), since the sheet may be open under another line than the one the word was written from. The thread and the rows are read again after either.
- **Words** (`wordFromLine.ts`, held to the plain words rules): a refused mark reads *The promise is saved on the job. Their line was not marked.* and then the database's own reason; a mark that would not come off reads *The word is off the job. Their line still shows the day.* A client ahead of the push skips the mark without a word (`markNotThereYet`).
- **Tests**: `wordFromLine.test.ts` (the lines a voided day comes off, the missing function, the words); `followUpThreadIo.test.ts`, new (the call with a day and without, a refusal's own words); `SetPromisedPayDateModal.render.test.tsx` (`onWord` for a saved promise and for each kind of word taken off, silence for Clear date and a paid claim); `useFollowUpThreadSlots.render.test.tsx` (a save marks the line and reads again, a voided day comes off two lines and a paid claim off none, a refusal's words beside *Promise saved.*, silence ahead of the push).
- **Docs**: the guide *reply to a person from their thread*; `MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md`'s slots; the Glossary's R3 line; the card.

## What reads the mark

Nothing new. The job's thread summary (`threadSummary.ts`) names it on **They promised**: *Dana promised a payment by Fri Nov 27. Said on Friday.*, with a door to the line. The move chip (`courtOf`) reads *Their move · until Fri Nov 27 · payment* once our word is the newest; while theirs is, it stays *Our move*, as for every promise.

## Read closer than the call

- **A refusal never undoes the job's promise.** The Pipeline is the record of a payment promise. The one refusal a person will meet is a line whose day is already written as another kind (*Someone has said what that day is already.*).
- **One day, one thread.** A customer with two jobs promised for the same day has one thread: voiding one job's promise takes the day off the line that marked the other. The next save from a line puts it back.
- **Only from a line.** A promise saved from the Pipeline, the Money lens or the Dashboard has no line to mark.
- **A mark whose day has gone by is not taken off**, since the thread reads only days still ahead; no reader shows it.
