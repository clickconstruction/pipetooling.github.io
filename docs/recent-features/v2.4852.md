# v2.4852 — Lien notices: the enclosed invoice names the bill the customer saw (2026-10-07)

Client only. Grace, reading the run's Preview of 878 · Take 5 Seguin's copy for the original contractor on the live desk: *Invoice # doesn't look right.* The enclosed invoice read **Invoice #0**, **Due date September 16, 2026** (the invoice date) and a **Scope** of *Paper checks can be sent to: Click Plumbing…*.

## Why it read that way

- The app's own document for a Stripe-hosted bill (`buildPhysicalInvoiceDocumentForBilledInvoice`) knows only the bill's position on the job — `#0` for the first — and, with no due day recorded, the send day. Stripe gave the bill its own number (`878-2609161138`) and due date, which is what Southern Post Construction holds.
- The enclosure's heading (`noticeInvoiceDocs`, v2.3445) swapped `#0` for a fallback, but the page itself still printed `doc.invoiceNumberDisplay`; the fallback was a made-up `#1`, not the customer's number.
- The demand letter fixed the same thing in v2.3425: it asks `get-stripe-invoice-details` and overlays the exhibit (`exhibitInvoiceDocument`), because a legal paper citing one number over an attachment printing another contradicts itself. The § 53.056 notice encloses the bill under § 53.056(a-3) and had no such read.
- The Scope is the Stripe memo. On this bill it is the mailing note, which the pay page already keeps off the line (`payLineIsNotTheWork`, v2.4724) but the enclosure printed as the work.

## What changed

- **[`stripeInvoiceFacts.ts`](../../src/lib/stripeInvoiceFacts.ts)** (new): `fetchStripeInvoiceFacts(invoiceIds, mode)` — one `get-stripe-invoice-details` call per bill per sitting, cached by mode and id, an unanswered bill left out and asked again next time, never throws; `stripeUnixToAppYmd` (the day assembled from `formatToParts`, since `en-CA`'s order is not a promise on every runtime), `stripeInvoiceFactsFrom`. The Lien window's inline fetch (v2.3425) moved onto it.
- **[`noticeInvoiceEnclosure.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/noticeInvoiceEnclosure.ts)**: `noticeInvoiceDocs(job, stripeFacts?)` and the pure `enclosedInvoiceDocument(doc, inv, hcp, facts)` — Stripe's number (`stripeBillNumber`, `#` once) and due day through `exhibitInvoiceDocument`; the page's number is the heading's, never `#0` (the first bill takes the job number, a later one its position); the Scope is blank when it is the mailing note and the bill has a real line, kept when the note is all the bill says.
- **Readers**: the run (`LienDeskRunModal`, new `stripeMode` prop; the desk and Put a GC on notice pass a dev's test-mode pick, live otherwise) fetches the facts for each job's hosted unpaid bills before building the enclosures, so the packet, the Preview, the emailed PDF and the pay page agree; the Lien window's notice tab (`noticeDocs`) reads the window's facts, widened from the demand's bills to every unpaid one; the desk's pay-page preview (`loadNoticePayPage`) fetches them too.

## Not done

- **Bill to: Customer** on the enclosed invoice comes from the bill's own record (`bill_to_party`), not from Stripe; whether it should name the original contractor on a GC job is its own question.
- `get-stripe-invoice-details` answers one bill per call; a run of twenty hosted bills is twenty calls, cached for the sitting. A batch endpoint would be the next step if it ever reads slow.
