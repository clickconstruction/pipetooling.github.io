# v2.8464 — On a phone, Undo and a bid window's ✕ are easier to tap (2026-11-24)

What the live walk of punch list #426 found and left (`to-dos/my-bids-refresh/README.md`, *The walk in the lens frame*). My Bids itself held every check. Three small things beside it did not. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was measured

The walk drew My Bids on clicktooling.com inside the lens frame, as the estimator, at 430 by 932 with touch, in headless Chromium. Sizes in px, wide by tall:

| Press | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Undo** on the toast | 55 by 26 | 55 by 44 |
| The toast's × | 14 by 24 | 44 by 44 |
| **Copy for text** in the Bid window | 106 by 33 | 106 by 44 |
| **Edit bid** in the Bid window | 72 by 33 | 72 by 44 |
| The Bid window's ✕ | 30 by 33 | 44 by 44 |

The *After* column is what the styles ask for. It was not measured in a browser.

## What changed

- **The toast** (`Toast.tsx`, `src/lib/toastPress.ts`). At a phone's width (`useIsMobile`, 640 px) the action press and the × take `thumbInLine`'s floor and margins from `phonePress.ts`. The margins give back what each grew by, so the toast is as tall and as wide as it was and nothing in it moves. The × grows 15 px to each side, into the 16 px gap beside it and the toast's 24 px of padding. Every toast in the app is this one, so **Undo** after a swipe on My Bids, on Recent Reports and after an import all get it.
- **The Bid window** (`BidPreviewModal.tsx`, `src/lib/bids/bidWindowPhonePress.ts`). At a phone's width (`useNarrowViewport640`) the three presses in its top row take a 44 px floor, and the ✕ a 44 px width. The row grows about 10 px, and the title beside it loses 14 px of width. One press on a My Bids row opens this window, so its ✕ is the way back on every bid.
- **A sealed lens frame keeps its page on a hard reload** (`hardReload.ts`, `src/lib/lens/reloadPlace.ts`, `seal.ts`). `hardReloadFromRoot` goes to the site's root and keeps the page to come back to in `sessionStorage`, which the inline script in `index.html` reads. A sealed frame's `sessionStorage` is memory, so the page was gone after the reload: the frame came back on its person's home, and an estimator's on the Bid Board. The walk saw it twice, each time just after a new build landed. A sealed frame (`window.__lens`) now asks for its own address with the same `nocache` mark, and the seal takes the mark off with `history.replaceState` before the app reads where it is. A window the lens did not name reloads as it did.

At a desk the toast and the Bid window are as they were drawn.

## Left as found

- A sealed frame's hard reload still unregisters the service workers of the dev's own browser, as it did. The caches are hidden from the frame and are not touched.
- The lens has no answer for `my_customer_words`, `my_week`, `team-scores` and `list_bid_money_strip`. Its tab lists them as gaps.
- The Bid window's title is squeezed beside its three presses at 430 px. The wider ✕ takes 14 px more of it.
