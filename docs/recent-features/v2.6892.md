# v2.6892 — Permits: find a permit on any list, in any city, and Closed on a map (punch list #302) (2026-11-10)

The owner: *maybe they previously passed on something but then they want to look it back up … via the address or via the city or via the permit name. Or maybe we also show them a map of what was passed on.* Before this, only Closed had a search, one city at a time, and the only map of passed permits was the Ledger's lens, which stops at 90 days.

## What changed

- **`lib/research/permitFind.ts`** (new, pure): `findTokens` and `matchesFind` (every word typed is somewhere on the permit: street, zip, city, number, project, work, who pulled it, the plumber); `findPermits` orders a number typed whole first, then a number begun, a street begun, and the most recently touched, and stops at 40 with how many more; `findPlaceWords` (Skim, Investigating, Pursuing, Closed · passed / has a plumber / not pursuing); `findLastMoveWords` (the newest answer through `historyWords`, the plumber who took it, or *Waiting on a skim*).
- **`PermitFindPanel`** (new): the search box, **Every city** or the city on screen only, and the results. The city on screen is searched from the rows the tab holds. The other cities are read **one at a time** and searched as they arrive; the count line names the city still being read; a city that cannot be read is named and the rest stand. A result's name opens the permit's sheet; a pass or a drop has **Investigate** and **Put back** on the row.
- **`ProspectsResearchTab`**: a magnifier first on the strip, so a phone shows it without a scroll, and the `find` view.
- **`PermitPlaceList`**: Closed reads as **List** or **On a map**. The map is `ResearchMapCard` over the chip's rows with no window; the pinned permit's card has its name (which opens it), who answered, the reason, and Investigate and Put back. `ResearchMapCard` takes `noPinsWords` for a caller that is not the ledger.
