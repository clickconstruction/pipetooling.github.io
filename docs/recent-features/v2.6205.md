## Change

- `src/lib/developments/aboutPicker.ts` (new, tested): `aboutRows` orders the site's pieces (the site, jobs and bids newest ledger number first with `labelJobsLedgerStatusForDashboard` / `bidStatusWord` as `meta`, the customer) and `aboutGroups` adds *Written about last*, the pieces of the newest History lines (each once, never the site, only pieces still in the development, at most `ABOUT_RECENT_MAX` = 4). `aboutKeyOfPiece` turns the amber chip's piece into the composer's key.
- `DevelopmentHistoryTools.tsx`: the composer takes `groups` and `preferKey` instead of a flat option list and draws the pick on `SearchableSelect` (search by number and name, labelled separators per group, each row its label and muted meta); a recent row carries a `recent:` value the pick strips. `preferKey` from the amber chip sets About; clearing the chip goes back to the site. An `onClose` draws *Close* in the hint line.
- `DevelopmentPage.tsx`: `composing` state; the composer folds behind a *Write a line* press by default and the header's *Write ›* opens both History and the box. `historyGroups` replaces `historyAbouts`. The search box gets the magnifier (`TakeoffPartSearchIcon`, the app's one) inside its left edge.
- The guide *see everything on a development* names *Write a line*, the typed search in About and the spyglass.

The native select kept the pieces in ledger order with no search; on the real site it would hold thirty jobs after the owner's *Put the 34 in* press. `SearchableSelect` is the app's picker, so the phone gets the same searchable list fitted to the screen rather than a new sheet.
