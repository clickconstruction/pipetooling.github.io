# v2.6848 — A job says when its customer pays in kind, and Bill Customer asks how the bill is paid (punch list #301, mock-up 2) (2026-11-10)

The second half of mock-up 2 (`to-dos/in-kind-arrangements-chip-mockup.html`, frames D1 and D2). A new job for a customer who pays in kind is not on the deal until a person puts it there. Nothing said so: the bill went out as money and the customer was chased, or it sat unbilled.

## What changed

- **The job window** (`JobWindowModal`): the short chip beside the customer's name in the header's quiet line, as words with no press. On a job the deal does not pay yet (`jobIsOffTheDeal`: the customer has an open deal, the job is not on it, the job is not paid) a strip under the tabs, on every tab: **`JobInKindStrip`**, *X pays in kind. This job is not on that arrangement. Its bill will be sent and chased as money.*, with **Put it on the arrangement**. With two open deals the press opens the customer's page, since a person has to pick.
- **`addJobToInKindArrangement`** (`inKindStandingIo.ts`): reads the deal's jobs fresh and calls `in_kind_arrangement_set_jobs` with them and the one more (`setInKindArrangementJobs`, now exported from `inKindIo.ts`), so a job already on the deal stays.
- **Bill Customer** (`SendRecordInvoiceModal`): for such a job, **`InKindBillQuestion`** is the first thing in the window, a required pick with nothing chosen. Until it is answered the three send presses are dead. *In kind, on the arrangement* hides the channels and the missing-email warning, moves to the one channel that sends nothing, and the press reads **Record the bill in kind**: it puts the job on the deal, then records the bill. A refusal stops before the bill is recorded and shows its reason. *With money* is the window as it was.
- **Help**: the guide gains *A new job for a customer who pays in kind*.
