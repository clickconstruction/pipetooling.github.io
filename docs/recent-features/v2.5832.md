# v2.5832 — Liens window: the property kind is the job’s own, and no early residential dates (2026-10-24)

A fix to the Lien window (`LienInstrumentsModal`, *Liens on job N*). Found on 2026-10-24 while filing Click v. Burd: job 891 (Take 5- Liberty Hill, a GC job under Burd & Assoc.) links the commercial record for 11730 TX-29. Edit Job's Property record row showed Commercial, and the Legal desk's Paper tab put the § 53.056 August notice on Nov 16 and the affidavit on Dec 15. Its *Liens on the job…* door opened the window on *Property kind not set: residential dates shown, the earlier ones*, with the § 53.052 affidavit on Nov 16, 2026 and the § 53.158 suit on Nov 16, 2027.

## The cause, measured

The board's rows were not the gap: both Stages selects read `jobs_ledger.*`, and a freshly loaded board hands the window `customer_address_id`. Measured on a local Vite against prod as the dev, sampling the window every 25 ms after the press: the window painted at **36 ms** with no kind, the unknown-kind words and the affidavit on Nov 16, and turned commercial with Dec 15 only at **1,185 ms**. The kind was the last of five reads in the window's open effect, one after another: the full job with every embed (`fetchJobWithDetailsById`), the payers, the contracts, then the property record. Until it landed, the timeline, the filing tabs' clock and the § Rules strip all ran the unknown kind's residential calendar. A look in that first second read the residential dates as the answer.

A second gap stood behind it: the window read the link from the opener's copy of the row (`job.customer_address_id`), and so did the forecast months it draws (`useForecastWorkMonths`, whose notice dates go by the kind). A copy can lag a link made since it loaded. The Pipeline's property-kind badge links a job and keeps the link in memory (`usePropertyKinds.linkJob`) until the board reloads, and a window opened from that row stayed on the unknown kind for good. Job 891's record dates from 2026-09-17, so this was not what was seen there. The Legal desk already re-reads each job's link (`useLegalPacketData`, #85 item 6), which is why the two disagreed.

## The fix

- **`readJobPropertyRecord(jobId, fallbackLink)`** (new `src/lib/jobs/jobPropertyRecordIo.ts`): the link on the job's own row, then the record by id. The caller's link stands only when the row cannot be read; a record that cannot be read is null, the kind unknown.
- **The window reads it first and on its own** (`LienInstrumentsModal.tsx`): one `property` state read by its own effect at open replaces `linkedAddress` and `propertyKind`, and the property block left the five-read chain. Live, the kind now lands at **353 ms**.
- **Nothing that turns on the kind is drawn until it lands.** The timeline (strip and phone fold), the filing tabs, the § Rules door's job strip, the last-work line and the supply-house card wait. The header's timeline frame reads *Reading the job’s property record…*, and so does a filing tab's body. The demand letter draws at once as before; its prefill already takes the property when it loads (v2.3433).
- **The forecast months wait for the job's own link too**: `forecastJobs` carries `property.link`, not the row's.
- **The filing tabs get the job with its own link** (`linkedJob`): the notice by hand (`LienNoticeByHandPane`) finds the jobs at the same property by it.

## Callers

Every opener hands the window a copy of the row, so the fix lives in the window and no caller changes. The callers are the Stages tab's openers (the billed dates ledger, the row chip, both billed lists' lien door, the Lien desk's calendar and affidavit doors, the forecast's *Send notice…*, the Legal desk's *Liens on the job…*, and the desk links' `fetchJobWithDetailsById`) and the Documents tab's `JobDocumentsLienPaper`.

The Release of Lien window (`LienReleaseModal.loadPropertyRecord`) reads `job.customer_address_id` from its opener's row the same way. It is not a caller of this window and is left for its own change.
