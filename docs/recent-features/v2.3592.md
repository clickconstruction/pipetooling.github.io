## The two owner calls, taken 2026-09-18

- **The rule: oldest bill first.** A payment with an `invoice_id` is that bill's, in full. A job-level payment (`invoice_id` NULL — 191 rows, $2.32M) is applied to the job's *sent* bills (billed or paid; a ready-to-bill draft was never in the customer's hands) in `sequence_order` then `billed_at` order, each bill taking no more than it still needs after its own linked money; one payment may split across bills; what no sent bill needs is the job's **surplus**, a job-level line and never a bill's. Not chosen: single-bill-only (would leave job 273 reading $17,585 owed with $38,780 sitting unattributed) and making the office link the 191 (the letters would overstate until it was done).
- **The 11 bills already sent with the old rendering: corrected in place.** Nothing is stored from a physical bill's rendering — `send-physical-invoice-email` sends the PDF and HTML the client builds and keeps neither — so the next view, print, portal load or demand-letter exhibit is the corrected one by construction. Nothing to re-send.

## The kernel

[`supabase/functions/_shared/paymentAttribution.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/paymentAttribution.ts) (pure, no imports; client re-export [`src/lib/jobs/paymentAttribution.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/paymentAttribution.ts), 9 tests beside it): `attributeJobPayments(bills, payments)` → per bill `{ linked, unlinked, applied, slices }` plus `unlinkedTotal` and `surplus`; `paymentsAppliedToBill` and `billPaymentSlices` for the readers. A slice is `partial` when its payment also paid another bill or left a surplus. A payment linked to a bill the reader was not given still counts for that bill — linked money is a fact, not an attribution.
