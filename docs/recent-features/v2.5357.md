# v2.5357 — Messages: a person’s thread fits a phone (2026-10-12)

Cut 10's P1 for the Messages build (GC Reviews helper 7, directed by the lead session GC Review): screen 1a of `to-dos/gc-mode/messages/phone-mockups.html` on `spike/gc-mode`, the prototype's v1.32 (the owner's pick C) and v1.48. The lead approved the ‹ and the face in place of × on 2026-10-12.

- **`src/lib/messages/threadFold.ts`** (new): `isThreadFolded(columnWidth, phone)` folds the header on a phone, or when the thread's column measures 640 px or less (`THREAD_FOLD_PX`), so a narrow column at a wide window folds too. Unmeasured reads wide. `reachOpen` keeps the numbers and addresses open at a desk and folded away on a narrow header until the arrow is pressed.
- **`ThreadWindow.tsx`**: the column is measured with `useMeasuredWidth`. Folded, the header changes:
  - The chevron and the face are one press, **Back to everyone**, in place of ×. Escape still closes.
  - The name and the company take one line each.
  - The corner holds the header slot first, then ☎ and ⋯.
  - The status row keeps to one row that scrolls sideways under a fade.
  - The jobs run in one strip.
  - `data-thread-header` reads `folded` or `wide`.
- **The panel's ground**: the panel's background was `var(--surface-muted)`, which is no token, so the backdrop showed through the lines. It is `var(--bg-page)` now, the ground white cards sit on.
- **`threadSlots.tsx`**: `headerRight(c, look)` gets `ThreadLook { folded }`, so a slot draws its narrow form. Cut 2's who-has-it pill shows as a face only.
- **`JobsRail.tsx`**: `strip` runs the tiles in one row that scrolls sideways, with no label and a fade at its end. A tile in the strip takes `touch-action: pan-x` in place of `none`. A sideways swipe scrolls the strip, a hold that does not move still arms the tile, and a drag down still ties. Off the strip a tile keeps `none`, so a drag at a desk is as before.
- **Guide**: `find-every-message-with-a-person-in-one-place` gains *Read a thread on a phone*.
- **Smoke**: `ThreadWindow.fold.render.test.tsx` covers a phone, a 600 px column at a desk and a 900 px one.
