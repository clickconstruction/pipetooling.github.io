# v2.2688 — Review math fixes 2: one per-hour basis, paid-only hour basis, company-calendar periods (2026-09-03)

Second batch of the Review-only fixes from the 2026-09-03 math audit (findings 6, 12, 17), stacked on v2.2686. None of these change what a job earns; they change what the numbers are divided by and which day the period starts on.

## Client only — no migration

- **One per-hour denominator (finding 6).** The Team Summary's three "/hr" columns, the headline card, and the ranked view's drawer already divided by the person's period hours; the Jobs Worked footer divided by hours that landed on jobs, so the same person read $279/hr in one place and $307/hr in another. The footer now uses the period hours (read off the person's `TeamSummaryBreakdown` row) and states "÷ 176.0 h in period", with the hours-on-jobs rate on a labelled second line when it differs. The three table headers carry a title explaining the basis (`PER_HOUR_BASIS_TITLE` in `TeamSummaryInline`). Day rows are unchanged (per-row hours).
- **Paid-in-full toggle keeps the hour basis (finding 12).** `derivePersonTeamSummary` used to swap `totalHours` for hours-on-paid-jobs and set `fieldHours = totalHours` (no overhead subtraction) under the toggle, so the parts burden was charged on office hours and per-hour rates used a different base than the unfiltered view. Now `totalHours` is always the period basis and `fieldHours = max(0, total − overhead)`; the toggle filters jobs only. Hours on paid jobs are reported as `hoursBreakdown.totals.onPaidJobs` (new optional field) and the Hours drilldown caption (inline + popup) explains the split. The panel headline's loading fallback follows the same rule. +1 kernel test.
- **Periods on the company calendar (finding 17).** `getReviewDateRange` is now a thin call to the pure kernel [`lib/people/reviewDateRange.ts`](../../src/lib/people/reviewDateRange.ts) (`computeReviewDateRange(period, custom, todayYmd)` + `ymdDayOfWeek` / `ymdAddYears`, +6 tests pinning every preset and the custom-range rules) anchored on `denverCalendarDayKey(Date.now())`. The two-year lookbacks in the union and per-person loaders and the per-person `lookbackEnd` use `ymdAddYears` on the strings instead of a browser-local `Date` round-trip. No `toLocaleDateString('en-CA')` remains in the tab. `ReviewPeriod` is exported from the kernel and aliased in the tab.
- Verified live as dev: the tab renders, the Last-30-days window is unchanged for a Central-time viewer, and the footer shows both bases.
