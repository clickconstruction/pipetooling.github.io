## The margin history — "This number vs your history"

- **`src/lib/bids/pricingMarginHistory.ts`** (Stage A) — what the block draws, as a function of the history, the bid on screen and its margin: `pricingMarginHistoryView` (the wins, the losses on price, the recorded tab lows as "the margin that would have matched", the count the current margin would have matched or beaten, this GC's own range once it has two tabs), `marginHistoryVerdict` (winning range · mixed territory · above every win), `marginOfHistoryRow`, `marginHistoryScaleLeft` (the 20–65 scale). The rules it always had are written down and tested: nothing shows under three bids won or lost on price; the bid on screen and a bid with no cost estimate never count; a margin outside −20 %…95 % is left out; a loss is "on price" by its category, or by the word in an old free-text reason.
- **`src/hooks/usePricingMarginHistory.ts`** — the `bid_pricing_history` read, as it was. The tab calls it **where the effect stood**, so the tab's effects run in the order they did and the read happens when it did (once per trade, not once per time the block appears).
- **`src/components/bids/PricingMarginHistory.tsx`** — the JSX, verbatim, over the kernel's view.
