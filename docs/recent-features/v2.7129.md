# v2.7129 — Bills sent: pin it to your Dashboard (2026-11-13)

Punch list #327. Grace asked for a button on the leaders' Dashboard that opens Bills sent. The Dashboard already has a row for that: the pinned row, filled by **Pin** and, for the owner and developers, **Pin for someone** (Pin Mode, `src/lib/pinnedTabs.ts`). A fixed button would show to every office role whether they want it or not. A pin goes to the people who want it, and they can take it off.

## What changed

- `src/lib/pinnedTabs.ts`: `/bills-sent` joins `PINNABLE_PATHS`, with the label **Bills sent** in `PATH_TO_LABEL`. The pin footer now shows on the page while Pin Mode is on, and **Pin for someone** works there as on any pinnable page.
- A pin is drawn only for a role that can open its page (`filterPinnedByRole` over `isPathAllowedForRole`), so the chip shows to dev, master, assistant and controller, the roles the page lets in. A test holds both the path and that filter.
- The guide *see which bills went out and who got them* gains *Put a button on your Dashboard*.

## Not changed

- No fixed button on the Dashboard, and no change to the Dashboard itself.
- Nobody is pinned by this change. The pin is one press on the page, by the person or for them.
