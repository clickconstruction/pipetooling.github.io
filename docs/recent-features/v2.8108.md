# v2.8108 — Papers: an estimator reads every kind of an outside company's paper, and opens its signed form (2026-11-20)

Punch list #403, step 4, the first PR of three. A migration, one edge function and its kernel, three beds and two tests. No screen and no client change. The owner settled calls 9, 10 and 11 on 2026-11-20, each against what the ground built (v2.7977).

## What changed

- **The estimator's read is every kind** (call 9, "No, every kind"). The read policy `person_contract_documents_estimator_outside` lost its `doc_type = 'agreement'` line. An estimator reads a W-9, an insurance certificate and every other kind of paper that is with an outside company: a trade company's own paper, or a party's paper whose contact is a customer, a supply house or a trade company, or a person at one.
- **The rule has a name.** `paper_is_outside_company` says whether a paper is with an outside company, from the four columns that say who a paper is with. The policy asks it. It is the ground's rule word for word.
- **An estimator opens an outside company's signed form** (call 10, "Yes, outside companies'", and call 11, "Yes, W-9s too"). `open-contract-form-pdf` opens the signed PDF, and the scan of a form keyed from paper, for an estimator when the paper is with an outside company. The signed W-9's PDF holds the whole tax number, and he was told so before call 11.
- **The gate asks the policy's own rule.** The function asks the database as the caller, `estimator_reads_paper`, which asks `paper_is_outside_company`. What an estimator may open cannot drift from what they may read.

## What stays closed

- A paper filed under a person: a staff paper, a sub's own, a partner's, a person's copy of a job's paper.
- A party on no list, until **Add to a list** says what they are.
- A digital twin. It reads as an estimator, reads no paper, and is refused by the function itself.
- A signed hours statement. It opens for a dev and the controller alone, the call of 2026-11-02.
- The assistant, who reads the row and is still refused the file.
- Everything the four roles do. No write policy changed, and no other role reads anything it did not read before.

## The gate, as a kernel

`supabase/functions/_shared/contractFormPdfGate.ts` holds who may open a signed form, pure. `formPdfOpener` says what a caller is from their account row: a dev and the controller open every form, a leader needs the pay-approved list, an estimator needs the paper to be an outside company's, and everyone else is refused. `formPdfRefusal` gives the refusal in words. Every caller who opened a file before opens the same files, and every caller who was refused reads the same words.

Three things are new for an estimator.

- The refusal reads the same for a person's paper and for a paper that is not there, so it says nothing about a paper they cannot read.
- A missing answer from the database is a refusal. So the function can be deployed before the migration is pushed.
- A digital twin is refused from its account row, before any paper is asked about.

## The log

Every open writes a line to `contract_form_pdf_opens`: who, which paper and when. A dev and the controller read it, and nobody signed in can write or clear it. Before this release a line that failed to write was noted in the function's own log and the link was handed over anyway. Now the link is handed over only once the line is kept. The link lasts five minutes and comes from a private bucket with no storage policy.

## What was checked before the read was widened

A wider read policy also widens every function that reads a paper as its caller and then acts with the service role. Each function that touches `person_contract_documents` was read, and so was each database function that names the table.

- Three edge functions read a paper as their caller: `send-contract-for-signature`, `complete-contract-form-office` and `open-contract-form-pdf`. Each names the roles it takes before that read. The first got its check in v2.8101.
- `src/lib/paperCallerReadGates.test.ts` holds that list. A new function that reads a paper as its caller fails CI until it is listed with the check it makes first.
- The storage policy on a signature's image names the four roles itself. The bucket of signed PDFs has no policy at all.
- The migration is pushed only after `send-contract-for-signature` with its role check is deployed.

The detail is in [`docs/migrations/20261120080000_estimator_reads_outside_papers.md`](../migrations/20261120080000_estimator_reads_outside_papers.md).
