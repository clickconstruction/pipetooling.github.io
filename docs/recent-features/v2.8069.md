# v2.8069 — Permits, Market: the call list says when it could not be read (2026-11-20)

Found by the audit of punch list #405 ([the card](../../to-dos/permits-market-page/README.md)), on the running page with each read made to fail. The call list is [v2.7993](./v2.7993.md).

## What was wrong

**Call this week** is built from three reads: the city's year of permits, the tab's own rows (the open jobs come off them) and the answers. Each could fail and the list still drew.

- **The year of permits failed.** The list held no row, so it drew its empty state: *Nothing waiting on a call. Every builder with 3 or more permits here is answered.* The tiles said *0 builders to call this week*. The note under the list said the city *shows only issued permits*, in San Antonio, whose feed has applications. The clock said the permits *carry too few pairs of dates*. The reason was on the screen in red, between a row of zero chips and those sentences.
- **The tab's rows failed.** `ProspectsResearchTab` keeps that reason in `allError` and never passed it to Market. The list drew with no job open on any name, the tile said *0 of their jobs are applied for*, and the order lost its second key. Nothing said a read had failed.
- **The answers failed.** The list drew with no answer on any name, which is the builder's choice and stays. The red line gave the reason and not what it costs: a name someone already passed on, or is pursuing, may be on the list. No press read the answers again.
- **While reading**, the chips drew with a zero each.

## What changed

- `PermitMarket` folds the two reads the list is built from into `callError` (the year of permits, then the tab's rows). With it set, the call list's two tiles are not drawn and `PermitCallList` draws the reason, one line (`CALL_LIST_UNREAD`) and **Retry**: no chip, no name, no empty state, no note. The clock says *The permits did not load, so no clock is drawn.*
- `PermitCallList` takes `answersError` apart from `error`. The list still draws under the reason, one line says what it costs (`CALL_ANSWERS_UNREAD`), and **Retry** reads the answers again.
- **Retry** asks only for what failed: `usePermitMarket` gains `reloadPermits`, `reloadAnswers` clears its error first, and `ProspectsResearchTab` passes `rowsError` and `onRetryRows`, which reads the tab's rows again as a first read.
- The chips are drawn once the list is read.

## Checked

- Six render tests fail on the code before this change and pass after it: the three failed reads in `PermitMarket.render.test.tsx`, and the list's own three in `PermitCallList.render.test.tsx`.
- On the dev server, signed in, with each read answered by a refusal in turn, at 390 px and at 1400 px: the reason and **Retry** show, none of the false sentences do, and **Retry** brings the five names back.

## Left as it was

The words of the reasons themselves. The tab's reads wrap a reason twice (*Could not read the permits: Couldn't load the permits: …*), on Builders and the skim as well as here.
