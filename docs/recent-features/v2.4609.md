# v2.4609 — Submittals: a schedule typed later grades the takeoff rows (2026-10-05)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. The last code residual on punch list #17 (Submittals), left by [v2.4107](v2.4107.md): a schedule typed after a takeoff build re-graded the Proposed rows only through *Rebuild rows from picks*, which rebuilds from the schedule and the picks and so loses the takeoff's rows, their parts and what the estimator set on them.

## What changed

- **A new door on step 2 of a draft**: **Grade N rows against the schedule…**, drawn when the schedule names at least one row that reads Proposed (or a Missing row from the takeoff with no specified product yet). It sits beside *Rebuild rows from picks* and appears as soon as the schedule is typed, pasted or read by the robot (`load` re-reads the schedule after the schedule window saves).
- **The window** (`SubmittalScheduleGradeModal`) lists each row: its tag, the status it will take (*As specified*, *As specified · check the model* when the model is the specified one plus a letter or two, *Alternate*, *Missing*), what the plans name, and what the row reads (its parts the GC sees, else its product). A summary leads: *2 as specified (1 to check) · 1 alternate, say why*. Under the rows, why the others stay: *not on the schedule, so they stay Proposed*; *lists two tags the schedule names differently; grade it with Edit*.
- **Grade** writes four columns onto each row and nothing else: `specified_manufacturer`, `specified_model`, `specified_description`, `status`. Parts, house, lead time, reason, cut sheets and any call stay. The rows table then reads in its schedule shape (Tag · Specified · Submitted · Status), and an Alternate asks for its reason as it always has.
- A row whose tag lists two (*WC-1, WC-2*) is graded on either, when the schedule names both the same product. A schedule line that names no product (*by contractor*) lands its description on the row and leaves the status alone.

## How it is built

- [`src/lib/submittals/gradeAgainstSchedule.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/gradeAgainstSchedule.ts), new: `planScheduleGrade` (the rows and the skips), `gradePatch`, `gradeSummary`, `rowProductWords`, `normalizeTag`, `productHead`. The comparison is `deriveProductStatus`, the same rule the picks' rows get, fed the row's product as a label so its model tokens are found wherever they sit.
- `SubmittalRefreshModals.tsx`: the third window beside Refresh and Fold, on the same shell.
- `BidsSubmittalsTab.tsx`: `gradePlan` beside `refreshPlan`, `gradeRowsAgainstSchedule`, the door, the window.
