# v2.7111 — Engagement history: what a person was over time, the database half (2026-11-12)

Punch list #315, upgrade 4a.

## What was wrong

The roster row says what a person is today. Nothing recorded that a person was an employee for one stretch and a subcontractor for another, the day it changed, or what that rests on. On a large file the fact lived in the narrative's prose.

## What changed

- **Migration `20261113090000_person_engagements.sql`**. Page: [`docs/migrations/20261113090000_person_engagements.md`](../migrations/20261113090000_person_engagements.md).
  - `person_engagements`: one row a stretch in one capacity, employee or subcontractor, with its first day, its last day, where the fact comes from and a note. Closed by its last day, withdrawn with a reason, never deleted. Two live stretches of a person never share a day.
  - `dev_engagement_write`: the one door, a dry run by default. `open`, `change` (the running stretch ends the day before and the new kind starts on the day), `close`, `withdraw`.
- **Kernel** `supabase/functions/_shared/personEngagements.ts` (door `src/lib/people/personEngagements.ts`, 6 tests): the stretches that stand, `standingOn(day)`, the fact strip's words, why a stretch cannot be recorded, the door's payloads.
- **Docs**: `HR_FILES.md`, `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`, `GLOSSARY.md`.

## Access

A dev reads the table. Nobody writes it directly: no insert, update or delete grant exists, and the door checks `is_dev()`. The HR agent's key has no grant. The four fence calls end the migration.

## Is this the best we can do?

The card gave the table an insert path of its own. One door with no direct write is stricter and does more: a change of capacity is two rows that must agree, and the door writes both or neither. The overlap rule is held in the database under a lock a person, so two sessions cannot both record the same days.

What it does not do: nothing reads the table yet, and `people.kind`, `employment_stints` and `charge_standings()` are untouched. The fact strip, the Timeline's lines, the seat's verbs and the mark on hours are the next PRs.

The list of kinds. Built: `employee` and `subcontractor`, any other refused.
