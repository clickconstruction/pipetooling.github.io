# v2.7380 — A subcontractor's job window shows our lead's number, not the customer's (2026-11-15)

Punch list #355 ([`to-dos/job-window-phone.md`](../../to-dos/job-window-phone.md), *Direction C, shown and split*). The card held two questions for Taunya about the sub's window. Her answer to the first, 2026-11-15: **"Our lead's"**. Migration `20261116040000_job_lead_contact.sql` ([`docs/migrations/20261116040000_job_lead_contact.md`](../migrations/20261116040000_job_lead_contact.md)), two lines in the SQL bed `plan_sets_author`, and the plain job window.

## Before

A subcontractor on a job's crew opened the same window as a helper: Who was the customer, with the customer's number as a press that calls. On a desk the window also showed the customer's email. Nothing on the window said who of ours runs the job.

## What changed

- **`job_lead_contact(job)`** (new, definer): our lead on a job is the first master technician on its team, else the first superintendent. It answers a caller on the job's crew or in the office. A sub could not read this as themself: the team table shows them only their own row, and a master's account only when that master adopted them.
- **[`src/hooks/useJobLeadContact.ts`](../../src/hooks/useJobLeadContact.ts)** (new): the read. A refused or missing function reads as no lead.
- **[`src/lib/jobs/jobWindowCrewPhone.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobWindowCrewPhone.ts)**: `showsOurLead(role)` (a subcontractor alone) and `ourLeadWords(lead, officePhone)`: the lead with their own number; a lead with no number on file with the office's, and the words *this is the office's number*; no lead yet, *The office*.
- **[`src/components/jobs/DetailJobModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/DetailJobModal.tsx)**, for a subcontractor, outside pane mode:
  - on a phone, Who is the lead's name with *runs this job* under it, their number as the press that calls and its copy square, then Files and Photos. No GC line, no email row. While the read is out the section says *Reading who runs this job…*;
  - on a desk, the customer's name stays in the panel, and under it the lead's name with *runs this job* and one chip, their number. The customer's phone and email chips are not drawn.
  - The customer's name stays on the title's quiet line on a phone.
- **[`src/components/jobMode/JobModeDetailsSection.tsx`](../../src/components/jobMode/JobModeDetailsSection.tsx)**: Job Mode's details card had the same block, the customer's name over their number as a press that calls and their email. For a subcontractor it now reads the customer's name, then our lead with *runs this job* and their number. The rule is one function, so the two screens cannot drift.
- **[`src/components/jobs/JobWindowPhoneSections.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobWindowPhoneSections.tsx)**: `JobWindowWho` takes `nameNote`.
- The office's number is the one the Dashboard's *Schedule wrong? Call dispatch* press uses (`useFirstAssistantDispatchPhone`).

## What a person loses

A subcontractor no longer sees the customer's number or email on the job window. That is the point. A helper, the superintendent and an estimator are ours, and see what they saw.

## What this does not do

- **It is the window, not the database.** A subcontractor on a job's crew can still read the job's row, which holds the customer's number. Closing that would mean taking the row away from them and reading the window through a definer, a change of its own.
- **Other screens were not swept.** The job window and Job Mode's details card are the two places the crew reads a customer's number, and both follow the rule now. If another screen is found to show a sub the customer's number, it takes the same rule through `showsOurLead`.
- **Your work and Your pay**, the sub's own rows, are not built. The second question (is a Your pay row drawn) is still Taunya's.

## Is this the best we can do?

The lead could have been the job's Account Man, the person who owns the customer relationship. One working job with a sub on it has one; seven have a master technician on the team. The team is what the office already keeps, so the lead is read from it and nobody has a new field to fill.
