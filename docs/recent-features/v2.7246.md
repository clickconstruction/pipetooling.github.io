# v2.7246 — Materials on site: a short count with no price is said, not $0.00 (2026-11-14)

Punch list #344, PR 6, on the owner's word to build the rest of the train. From the review of Materials → On site: *the desk's Missing this week is money alone*.

## What was wrong

`jobMaterialsSummary` added `missingMoney(...) ?? 0` for each short line, so a line whose part has no price added nothing. The desk drew the sum alone. A job that came up short on typed parts, which is most parts on a site until they are priced, read `$0.00`, was not amber and sorted last. `missingLines` was already counted and the desk did not show it.

## The change

- **The summary** (`onSiteSummary.ts` and its database-summed twin in `onSiteServerSummary.ts`) counts `missingUnpricedLines`: of the week's short lines, how many have no price. The money is unchanged: the sum of what is priced.
- **The words** (`src/lib/materials/missingWeekWords.ts`): `missingWeekNote` reads *3 lines*, *3 lines · 2 with no price* or *3 lines, none with a price*.
- **The desk**: the note sits under the money in a job's row and in its site's row, amber when the money leaves a line out. A row is amber, and sorts up, on short lines and not on money alone.
- **A site's screen**: the *Missing this week* tile reads the same note and turns amber when the money is partial.
- **A job's window**: the chip adds *N with no price*.
- **The Materials page**: the Track caption says *4 lines came up short* when lines are short and none has a price, where it said nothing.

Client only.

## Tests

`missingWeekWords.test.ts` (6). `onSiteServerSummary.test.ts`: the two summaries agree on the new count with a price and with none. `materialsGroups.test.ts`: the badge. `JobsMaterialsTab.desk.render.test.tsx`: a short count of a typed name reads *1 line, with no price* beside `$0.00` on an amber row and on its site's row, and reads *1 line* beside the money once the part has a price.

## Not changed

The Needs you card already named the short lines when nothing was priced. Pricing a part is #342's screens.
