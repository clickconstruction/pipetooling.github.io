# v2.8403 — Supply houses: see a bill without leaving the page, from every list of bills (2026-11-23)

Punch list #431, PR 4 of seven. The bill viewer (v2.8399) opened from one list, the Job Summary's. The owner's ask named the other places bills are listed. This PR opens the same viewer from three of them. No migration and no edge function.

## What changed

- **A supply house's bills** (`SupplyHouseBillsCard`, Materials → Supply houses). **See the bill ›** was a link to the PDF in a new tab. It is now a press that opens the viewer, with the card's bills beside the paper in the order the card draws them: past due first, then each due day, the largest of a day leading. A link icon beside it copies the bill's own link. The card still draws only: it takes `onSeeBill` and `onShareBill`, and `SupplyHouseDetailPanel` holds the viewer. A bill offers the press when its row has a link or a paper was read from a file. A host that hands in no viewer keeps the older link.
- **An aging cell's window** (`AgingCellWindow`). The **↗** at the end of a row was a link to the PDF in a new tab. It opens the viewer over the window, flipping through the bills the cell sums. The viewer's keys are taken first, so Escape closes the viewer and leaves the window open.
- **Link invoices** (`MercuryTransactionInvoiceLinkModal`). A row gains **See the bill ›** for the office, to check a bill against the card charge before ticking it. The press sits inside the row's label and leaves the tick as it was. A cardholder linking their own charge cannot read a bill's row, so they are offered no bill to see.
- **One link icon** (`src/components/icons/CopyLinkIcon.tsx`) in place of the two copies the list and the viewer each drew.

From these lists the viewer has no job, so *On this job* is not among the facts and the copied link is the bill alone.

## Not in this PR

What we buy and Price over time list purchases by part, a line and not a bill, so they open no viewer here. The job window's Parts Cost totals a job's bills and lists none.
