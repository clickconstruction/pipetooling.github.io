# v2.7350 — Bills sent: say how several bills to one customer went, in one press (punch list #340, R2) (2026-11-15)

The second of the form's three own fixes on the card. Taunya picked Build all three on 2026-11-15. No migration and no edge function change.

## What changed

- **The form lists the person's other bills.** `ByHandForm` (`src/components/billing/BillsSentLedger.tsx`) shows, under the note, every other bill with no send on record for the same customer (`byHandOthers`, `src/lib/billing/billsSent.ts`): the job, when it was billed, *Paid since.* for a paid one, and the amount, each with a tick box. The match is the customer's name as the strip writes it, whatever its case, read from every bill with no send and not only the ones the search box leaves. A bill with no customer name has no others. Nothing is ticked when the form opens.
- **The button counts what it will record.** `byHandRecordLabel`: *Record how it went* for the one bill, *Record how 3 bills went* with two ticked, through `countedLabel` (`src/lib/batchWords.ts`).
- **Each bill is its own record.** The press files one `sent_documents` row a bill through the same `recordBillByHand` and `byHandFiling` as before, with the way, the person, the day and the note the form holds.
- **A refused bill is named and the rest save.** The press keeps going past a refusal. The toast says what took and what did not (`byHandOutcome`, over `batchOutcomeWords`: *Recorded 2 bills. 1 did not record.*). The strip then shows *1 bill did not record. It stays here with the reason.*, and the refused bill's row says its reason in red (`formatErrorMessage`), opening the rest of the strip or the paid bills when the row sat behind **Show all** or **Show them**. The reason leaves when a later press records that bill.
- A press with nothing ticked is the one record it always was: the same toast, and a refusal keeps the form.
- The guide *see which bills went out* gains *Say how several bills went at once*.

## Not changed

What a record stores, who may record, and the day's rules. The ticked bills take the day the form holds, as the hint above them says.
