# v2.4957 — GC mode: the press behind the daily log (2026-10-08)

U3a-i of the Building lane's real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/BUILDING_REAL_BUILD.md`, on branch `spike/gc-mode`): the one press behind the daily log, in `supabase/migrations/20261009120000_gc_save_daily_log.sql` (`docs/migrations/20261009120000_gc_save_daily_log.md`). No client code: the window (U3a-ii) calls it once the types PR after the push has it.

- **`gc_save_daily_log(log jsonb)`**, `SECURITY INVOKER`: a day's log on a GC job being built, with its crews and what held work up, written in one transaction and replacing that day's when saved again (the prototype's `saveDailyLog`). It returns the log's id.
- **It refuses**, in words:
  - a training account and a digital twin;
  - a job not at `building` or with no `started_on`;
  - a day after the writer's own day, or before work started;
  - a writer's day more than one off the server's;
  - a trade not on this job.
- A crew with nobody on site is left off. A sky or a reason the tables do not know is refused by their own checks (`20261008030000_gc_building_records`).
- The tighter rule, our own crew or a trade with a signed statement of work, and the start promises a crew on site keeps (`gc_keep_promises`) come in U3b, after the Board's B6.
- **The SQL bed**: `scripts/pgtest-gc-building.sh` (`npm run test:pg:gc-building`) plays `supabase/tests/gc_building/20_daily_log.sql` against every migration on GitHub's runners (`sql-beds.yml`, job `gc-building`). It saves, replaces and catches up a day, refuses each case in its words, and refuses a trainee, a twin and an estimator.
- Its gate is the tables' dev policies (Building, dev only in `doors.ts`). `EXECUTE` is revoked from `PUBLIC` and `anon`.
