# v2.5552 — Messages: a teammate's thread opens, and their text counts on Follow up (2026-10-15)

Found on the live walk of v2.5546 (the teammate kind), naming the owner's own number as Robert on prod: the toast said *Robert is one of our own team now*, the number moved onto his seeded contact and the roster learned it, and then the thread window said *This person is not in Messages.* while Follow up's lanes read Needs a reply (0). The queue (`followUpIo.loadQueue`) held the 18 teammates and his two lines; the view (`loadMessagesView` → `contactsFromRows`) did not: `load.ts` keeps only the kinds in its own `KINDS` set, which stopped at the seven, so a `team` row was "a kind this build does not know" and left out. The lanes keep only rows whose person is in the view (`queueForPill`), so the lines went with it. The smokes of v2.5546 built their contacts past that loader, which is why they passed. No migration, no edge function.

- **`src/lib/contacts/load.ts`**: the known kinds are a `Record<ContactKind, true>` now, so a kind added to `ContactKind` fails the typecheck here until the loader reads it, the way `CONTACT_KINDS` in `quo/rows.ts` already does; `team` is in it.
- **Tests**: `load.test.ts` (the four kinds of R0 gain the eighth: a `team` row reads with the roster's name and no company, and its roster kind reads back as `team`).
