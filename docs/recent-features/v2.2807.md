# v2.2807 — Timeline "As of" slider (2026-09-05)

Owner ask: "look at the job summary chart over time where I can walk back how it would look a week ago, two weeks ago, three weeks ago… an expandable slider that allows me to slide it backwards and forwards." Mock-up C picked (artifact d00065aa): ghost the future + "what changed since" + Play.

## Client only — no migration

- [`jobRunningTimeline.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobRunningTimeline.ts): `buildWorkedSpans` / `buildStatusSpans` take an optional `asOfYmd` — days after it vanish, open jobs run to it, spans that began after it drop, and buckets come from the new `jobRunBucketAsOf` (paid / billed only when the move had happened by then; no history → working). `JobStatusSpan` may carry `billedYmd` / `paidYmd` so status-span rows get their moves even when the ledger map lacks the job. New `jobRunDeltaSince(rowsToday, asOf, today)` → `{ opened, billed, paid, stillOpen }`; `asOfYmdForDaysBack` clamps to the window start; `JOB_RUN_AS_OF_JUMPS` (today · 1/2/3/4/8 wk); `daysBackLabel`. 22 kernel tests.
- [`JobSummaryTimelineView.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobSummaryTimelineView.tsx): `⏮ As of` chip after Color by (pref `timelineAsOf`, remembered; the position always opens on today). Slider row: ▶ Play (140 ms per day, walks to today), date + "N wk ago", range (left = earlier, right = today, arrow keys step a day), week chips, footnote title. `rowsToday` (full picture) vs `rows` (rewound); series / weekly / summary build on the clipped day list with the as-of day as "today"; `seriesToday` / `weeklyToday` feed the ghost — a `--bg-subtle` wash over the days after, a dashed outline of today's totals (daily) or of each later week's bar (weekly), and a faint dotted line at the real today. The y-axis holds at today's peak while rewound so nothing rescales. Red marker reads "as of · N". Tiles: "Running on Thu Aug 13 · jobs open that day", averages "through the as-of day", median "as of then". `DeltaStrip` under either chart. Legend: "(as of Thu Aug 13)" + "what came after".
- [`jobSummaryLedgerView.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobSummaryLedgerView.ts): `timelineAsOf: boolean` pref (default off) + reader; the tab passes it through.
- Help guide: new "Walk the chart back" section.

## Can't replay

Jobs deleted since then are gone; status moves corrected later show their corrected dates; hours approved after the fact count as if approved on the day. The slider row's footnote says so on hover.
