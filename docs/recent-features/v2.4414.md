## What was wrong

Measured at 390 × 844 on the dev server against production data, job 273, Lien instruments → Demand letter → **Save & record send…**. The panel at the foot was 347 px, which left the letter 222 px (about 125 on a phone whose status bar and home bar make the window 679 px). The four methods were 27 to 29 px tall and wrapped three and one; **Back** was 33 px and **Record** 31 px, beside a date box whose label wrapped. The fields sat at the bottom of the screen, where a phone's keyboard comes up.

## What changed

- **[`DemandRecordSendSheet`](../../src/components/jobs/DemandRecordSendSheet.tsx)** (new, render-tested): the step as a sheet. A title line with ‹ Back and ×; a summary (the paper and its amount, the job, the pay-by date) because the letter is under the sheet; the four methods in two rows of two, 48 px tall, as pressed buttons; **Tracking / receipt number** and **Sent on** at full width, 48 px tall, in 16 px type so an iPhone does not zoom the page; the deadline-watch sentence; one full-width **Record**. It holds no state: the window owns the values and the write, so what is recorded is what the panel recorded.
- **[`LienInstrumentsModal`](../../src/components/jobs/LienInstrumentsModal.tsx)**, at 640 px and under: `recordSheetOpen` draws the sheet over the card (`position: absolute; inset: 0`; the card is now `position: relative` and holds its full height while the sheet is up) and draws neither the panel nor the foot. Because the letter stays mounted under the sheet, **Back** returns to it at the same scroll. Above 640 px the panel at the foot (`data-demand-record-panel`) is as it was.
- Guide [`send-a-final-demand-letter`](../../src/content/help/send-a-final-demand-letter.md), *Record the send*: three sentences on the phone's page.

## Verified

Same job, same size, read only: **Record was not pressed**. The sheet covers the card (34 to 810) and does not scroll; its content ends at 641, so it fits the 679 px window too. Back and × are 44 px, the four methods 159 × 48, the two fields 326 × 48, Record 326 × 48, with no sideways scroll. The tracking box ends at 425, above where a 336 px keyboard would start (508). A press on a method moves the pressed state. With the letter scrolled to 300, Back removes the sheet, brings the foot back and leaves the letter at 300.
