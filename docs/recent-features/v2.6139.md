# v2.6139 — A job's dates drawn against today, behind a Words | Track choice (punch list #217, a prototype) (2026-10-30)

Punch list #217, *Show it, don't say it*: eight scans found 131 places where the app tells instead of shows, and a mock-up page drew each as a before/after pair for the owner to pick from. On 2026-10-30 she picked two of the Jobs findings as **alternative views**, not replacements. Of jobs-pipeline-1 (the Billed row's three dates): "This has some potential, but only as an alternative view that a user can choose to engage in. How could I offer this as an alternative view? Can you help me build a prototype?" Of jobs-pipeline-6 (the Job tab's two date bands): "This can work very well as an alternative view. Could you help me build a prototype where we can see it as an alternative view where the user can choose how they want to view it." This PR is that prototype. No migration, no edge function; it opened as a draft for her to look at.

## The choice

One per-user setting, **Dates · Words | Track**, remembered per browser under `localStorage.jobDatesView` the way the lien strip remembers Steps · Windows (`src/hooks/useJobDatesView.ts`, after `useLienTimelineView.ts`). It defaults to **Words**, so every screen reads as it did until the person switches; one choice drives both surfaces. **How a user finds it:** a small segmented control, *Dates Words | Track*, in the top-right corner of every Billed and Collections row's dates block on the Pipeline (and the phone card's, which draws the same block), and above the date bands on a job window's Job tab. Pressing Track on either flips both. `JobDatesViewSwitch.tsx` draws it.

## Surface 1 · the Billed row's dates (jobs-pipeline-1)

Words is the ledger of v2.4168 / v2.4205, unchanged. Track draws the same ledger on one axis against today, the Lien desk's Deadlines glyphs at row height:

- **Kernel** `src/lib/jobs/billedDatesTrack.ts` (`buildBilledDatesTrack(ledger, todayYmd)`): the ledger's rows become marks with a percent along the axis (7 % of room at each end, never shorter than two weeks). The bill is a filled dot, labelled above the line. The pay date is a dashed ring marked *guessed*, or a solid blue ring marked *their word* on a promise. The one deadline is a flag labelled below (*Lien by Dec 15 · 46d*, *Lien notice by Oct 15 · 10d*, bold when the row is bold). Today is a line. A closed window is a red flag with a red wash to the end; a filed lien a green check on its day. The **band** is the verdict placed on the axis: *37d of room* in green hatching between the pay date and the flag (amber when the flag is inside a week), *4d short* in red hatching from the flag to the ring, *Ask for a date* dotted from today to the flag when no pay date exists, *12d to file* / *2d to send the notice* when the flag is itself the to-do, *Lien gone* on a dead row. The **tag** names the guess's making — *guessed · ~30 d over 6 payments*, *guessed · the GC pace ~35 d over 3 payers*, *guessed · commercial pace ~12 d*, *guessed · company avg ~27 d* — or *their word · Nov 20 · expected Nov 8 by their history*. The notice's state line (v2.5491) is a chip.
- **Ledger rows carry their day** (`LedgerRow.ymd`, additive) and an Expected row carries its `pace` (source, days, samples, payers, the model's pace words); the runway carries `filedYmd`. The Words view ignores both.
- **Component** `BilledDatesTrack.tsx` draws it; `BilledDatesBlock.tsx` is the switch over the ledger or the track, and `JobsStagesTab`'s `billedLienRunwayRenderer` returns it where it returned `BilledDatesLedger`, so the table rows, the phone cards and Collections all carry it. Every door is the Words view's: the ring opens They said… / New date…, the flag and the band open the Lien window. The tag's door is new: it opens the Payment forecast **on its pay-speeds breakdown** (`openOnBreakdown` on `BilledPaymentForecastModal`, through `StagesBilledMoneyModals`).
- Marks keep a browser `title` hover for now; the Lien desk's hover cards (`lienMarkCards.ts`) are written over `LienCalendarMark`s and were not adapted in the prototype.

## Surface 2 · the Job tab's two date bands (jobs-pipeline-6)

Words keeps *Last work date · Last bill date* and *Job Start · Last Work · Reports* as they were. Track replaces the two bands with one strip of the job's days; the Status row and the Reports door stay:

- **Kernel** `src/lib/jobs/jobDaysStrip.ts` (`buildJobDaysStrip`): one column a day from the job's first day (the first clock-in or the first bill) to today at the right edge, at most 120 days with the earlier ones counted off the left edge. A session's day is its work date, else its clock-in day in the company zone; a rejected session is no day. An approved session fills the day, one not yet approved rings it, a bill ticks it blue, the first day is a diamond. **One last work** with its source: *approved Oct 12*, or when a later day is still waiting, *clocked out today, not approved yet* / *clocked in today, still on the job*. The shape is `src/lib/legal/timelineStrip.ts` (v2.6107).
- **Reads nothing new:** the strip reads the clock sessions the *Schedule and recorded time* section already reads (`useJobDetailScheduleAndSessions`, now enabled for either), and the job's sent bills from `fullJob.invoices`. That read caps at 100 sessions; the strip says *the first 100 sessions* when the job has more.
- **Component** `JobDaysStrip.tsx`; `DetailJobModal`'s Job tab draws it in place of the first band and drops the Job Start / Last Work tiles from the second in Track. **A press on a day opens it in the feed:** `JobThreadNotesPanel` takes `focusDay` and scrolls its list to the day's first entry (or the nearest after it), lit for a moment; every feed entry now carries `data-feed-ymd`.
- The crew's plain window (the limited job) keeps its bands; only the office's tabbed window has the switch.

## The side-by-side for the owner (dev only)

She asked to see every prototype live beside today's look, on the same data. With `?sbs=1` on the page URL (or `?sbs=jobs-pipeline-1,jobs-pipeline-6` to name surfaces) and a dev signed in, each surface draws **Today** over **With the track** / **With the strip** on the same row or job, and hides the per-user switch; everyone else, and every URL without the flag, sees the switch and the view it picked. The helper is `src/components/prototypes/SideBySide.tsx` — `useShapeView(key)` reads the flag and the role, `SideBySide` draws the two captions — written here with the names the #217 group agreed on, because the sibling branch (`claude/217-rhythm-strips…`) that writes the same helper was not pushed when this one needed it; the two fold into one when both are on main. On a dev server from this branch: `/dev-login?as=1&to=/jobs?tab=stages%26sbs=1` (Billed Awaiting Payment, every row's dates block) and `/dev-login?as=1&to=/jobs?edit=<job id>%26sbs=1` (the job window's Job tab).
