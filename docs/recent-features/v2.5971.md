# v2.5971 — The search box keeps a Settings card in view, and puts the guide you meant first (2026-10-27)

Punch list #181, found walking the go-anywhere box live on clicktooling.com on 2026-10-27, after PRs 1 to 3 deployed. Two things fell short of the mock-up.

## What shipped

- **A Settings anchor is held in view until the page settles** (`pollScrollToSettingsAnchor`, `src/lib/settingsSearch.ts`). The box's jump to *Office hours* landed on the right tab and anchor, but the card ended 875 px below the fold: Settings' deep-link effect scrolled once, the moment the anchor appeared, and the cards above it on Phone and texts loaded after and pushed it down. A fresh page load of the same link landed it, since there the cards above render first. The helper now finds the anchor (bounded, 5 s), scrolls to it, and re-aligns it on every tick it has drifted more than 8 px, for 6 s (`SETTINGS_ANCHOR_HOLD_MS`), stopping at once on a wheel, a touch, a pointer press or a key, so it never fights a person scrolling. `Settings.tsx`'s deep-link effect now calls it instead of its own one-shot loop, so the Dashboard banners, the Calendar's links and the rail's search all get the hold. It returns a stop function.
- **A guide is matched by its own words** (`scoreDoor`, `src/lib/search/doors.ts`). A guide's where line is its category, and *Office* is the category of 205 guides, so *office hours* listed 18 guides led by *see man hours for the office and the field*, with *turn on the reply push*, whose keywords say *office hours*, tenth. Pages, windows and Settings cards still match their where line (*phone* finds the Phone and texts cards).
- **A phrase typed whole ranks first**: two or more words found together, in order, add 200 when the name holds them and 150 when the door's words do. *Office hours* the card scores 420, the reply-push guide 250, the man-hours guide 200.

## Tests

`src/lib/settingsSearch.dom.test.ts` (new, jsdom, fake timers): the anchor is scrolled once it appears and re-aligned after an 875 px push, a few pixels of drift are left alone, the hold lets go after 6 s, a wheel stops it, a missing anchor gives up, the stop function ends it early. `doors.test.ts`: a guide's category is not matched while a page's where line is, and the three *office hours* doors rank card, reply push, man hours. The 15 test files that reach Settings anchors and deep links pass.

## Verify

On clicktooling.com: ⌘K, *office hours*, Enter from the Dashboard; the Office hours card sits at the top once Phone and texts has loaded. ⌘K, *? office hours*; *turn on the reply push* leads the guides.
