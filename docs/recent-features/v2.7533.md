# v2.7533 — Checklist: Today says where you stand and what comes next (2026-11-16)

Punch list #369, step A1 of the Task Mode train. The owner sent a screenshot of Task Mode and asked for "a visual refresh just like we gave new task a visual refresh recently". He saw the mock-up (`to-dos/task-mode-refresh/mockup.html`), liked the plan and said to build the whole train. This is its first step: the top half of Checklist → Today. No migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

- The page said *Checklist* twice and *Today* twice, and nothing said what to do first.
- Today drew the phone card of the sunlight pass at every width. At a desk its 48 px **＋ Add a note** sat alone across half the window.
- Every finished task wore a solid blue **Waiting on review** chip. Read through the dev seat on 2026-11-16, 21 tasks were finished in 30 days and none had been looked over, so the chip stood on all of them and meant nothing.
- The bell was an icon with no name, and **FWD** was three letters.

## What changed

- **One head.** The date, and one green sentence in the seat New task gives to what Send will do: *1 of 3 done today. 8 older tasks are still open.* (`todaySentence`). A day with nothing on it keeps the empty state and draws no sentence.
- **Next.** The first open task dated today is drawn open, with a thick edge and the page's one solid blue press, **Mark done** (`nextTaskId`). A task with a tick box trades the box for the press. A one-off keeps its ring beside it, and the press takes the % sheet's Done path, so a card that asks for a photo still opens the finishing sheet. With today finished, Next is a task that was sent back, then the oldest one still open, and that task leaves the Outstanding list under it.
- **Then · N** holds the rest of today's open tasks, each with its tick.
- **Done today · N** holds what is finished, each as one struck line: *Done 12:22 PM. Imani hears it is done.* (`doneLineWords`, `taskHearsId`). A task nobody is told about says only when. A signed-off task reads *Signed off by Imani* in green. A task sent back keeps its amber callout.
- **Quiet pills.** The note press is **＋ Note**, then *2 notes*, lit while its thread is open. The bell reads **Mute alerts** and FWD reads **Forward**. All three share `TASK_PILL_STYLE`, the Add row's pill.
- **Task Mode.** The tab strip and the page's title make way for one joined bar, **Today · History** (`TaskDayBar`), the shape of New task's When bar. The full app keeps its four tabs and its title.
- **One column.** Today is at most 720 px wide and centred, so a desk reads the same page a phone does.

## How it is built

- `src/lib/tasks/todayPage.ts`, pure, 14 tests: the head's date, the sentence, the split of the day, which task is Next, the done line, who hears, the labels. Every sentence is run through `plainWordsFailures`.
- `ChecklistInstanceCard` gains a third look, `variant="fresh"`, with `lead` for the Next card and `hearsName` for the done line. The `card` and `row` looks are untouched, so Dashboard's My Inbox, Job Mode, Dispatch Mode and Quickfill draw what they drew.
- `useTaskCardSheets` gains `donePress`, the % sheet's Done without the sheet.
- `ChecklistTodayTab` renders each card through one `renderTodayCard(inst, lead)`, reads the names of the people who hear beside the thread's names, and hands the Outstanding section every older task but the one that is Next.

## Kept from the sunlight pass

The card of v2.1854 avoided tints on purpose, since glare takes a tint before it takes a border. The tick, the ring and Mark done keep their thick dark edges and large targets. A tint here only repeats what words already say.
