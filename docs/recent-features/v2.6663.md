# v2.6663 — Stage jobs: the Pipeline bar shows each stage at the number the crew gave it (2026-11-07)

The stages half of v2.6653. That fix made `lines` mode pour each line's amber at the crew's own number; the `stages` branch of the Pipeline's Progress & payment cell still poured the job's one done-not-billed figure down the stages, even though every stage's `workPct` (from `jobs_ledger_fixtures.progress_pct`, through `buildPipelineStageBar`) was known and only set a `fillPct` nothing on the board draws. No migration; no edge function.

## What was wrong

On an Order-stage job the board's bar is the money bar (v2.4351): each block is a stage, its slices the money poured onto it (green paid, blue billed, amber done-not-billed, grey the rest). The amber came from `pourMoney` pouring `money.doneNotBilled` (% done × bid − paid − billed) left to right, so a Top Out the crew had reported at 30% drew its amber at whatever the job's typed 55% left after the done stages — 38% in the test's job — and a stage the crew said was done but nobody had billed drew nothing past the pour.

## Change

- **`progressPaymentCell.ts`** (stages branch of `buildProgressPaymentView`): the work channel's fills are computed first, and once any stage not yet done carries a stage report (`hasStageReport`, the rule the live stage already follows), `pourMoney` gets an `ownDone` map of each stage's drawn fill — a done stage 1, the live stage its own number, a later stage 0 — so the amber on each block is that stage's number past what is paid or billed on it. Paid and billed still pour as before (a bill naming a stage takes it first). A stage job with no stage report pours the pool down the stages as before.
- **Tests**: the "fresh typed percent pours down the stages" case asserts the poured amber per stage; a new case covers a live stage reported at 30% under a typed 55%, a live stage with a bill on it (blue first, the rest of its number amber) and a stage the crew said is done that nobody has billed (all amber).
- **Guide** `set-job-progress-on-stages.md` (*What it affects*): a sentence says a reported stage's amber is its own number.
