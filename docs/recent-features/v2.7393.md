# v2.7393 — Jobs: a job's painted maps are listed on its Documents tab (2026-11-15)

Punch list #345. Taunya asked on 2026-11-15: "Should we store all of our painted maps in the documents tab?" The answer she said yes to: list them there, and store nothing there.

## What changed

- **The job window's Documents tab has a Painted maps section,** right under Plans. It lists the job's own maps, newest first, then its development's, each marked *from* the development.
- **A row is a door.** The map's name opens the painted map's own window on the map as it is now. Beside the name: its length, its places, who painted it and when.
- **Paint a map** at the section's head starts one for the job, through the start sheet that exists.
- **A job with no map** says *No painted map is on this job.* and offers the same press.
- **One line under the list** says what the rows are: *A map here is the map as it is now.* For whoever can read sent copies it adds where the fixed copy is: under *Sent from this job*, with the day it was made.
- The list reads again when a map is made, renamed, added to or thrown away anywhere in the app.

## Why list and not store

- **A map is live and a document is a fixed copy.** A place added next week is on the map next week. A map filed as a document would freeze, or drift from the real one.
- **Not every map has a job.** A map can hang on a development, on nothing, or come from a task. One job's tab could never hold them all. The search and the Map page's layer stay the doors to those.
- **The crew needs the map, and Documents is where the office looks.** The line under the job's address stays, on the job window's every tab and on a tech's job card.
- **The fixed copy is already filed.** A print or a PDF files itself as a sent copy (`painted_map_print`, v2.7225). This section points at it and does not repeat it.

## How it is built

- `paintedMapDocuments.ts`, pure (+5 tests): `paintedMapDocumentRows` is `mapsForJob`, the list under the address, with each row's words.
- `JobDocumentsPaintedMaps.tsx`: one read with `loadPaintedMapsFor`, the read the line under the address makes, then the painters' names with `loadPainterNames`. The names failing costs the names and never the list.
- Mounted in `JobWindowDocumentsTab` after `JobPlansSection`. No new table, column, policy or function: whoever can read a map reads its row here.

## Is this the best we can do

- **Two reads of the same maps on one job window:** the line under the address and this section each ask. They are small and the same query, and the tab is not the window's first. One shared read would thread a prop through the job window for little.
- **No count on the tab's label.** The Documents tab has none for any section.
- **The Documents page for all jobs is not touched.** A list of every painted map belongs to the search and the Map page, which have it.
