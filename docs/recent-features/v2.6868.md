# v2.6868 — The page a box label opens reads like the label (punch list #294) (2026-11-10)

The owner printed his first cage labels on the Brother PT-D610BT, scanned one with an iPhone and asked whether the page the scan opens should be improved. The before and after is `to-dos/prts-box-page/landing-before-after.html`; he picked direction B, built as two PRs. This is the first, client only: everything here is drawn from what `box-read` already answers.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/materials/boxPage.ts`** (pure, `boxPage.test.ts`): `boxPageCountVerdict(lastCountedOn, todayYmd)` reads the day of the last count against `STALE_COUNT_DAYS`, the 14 days the office's *due a count* badge uses, and answers `fresh` (*Counted 2 days ago*, with the day beside it), `due` (*Due a count*, *last counted 22 days ago*) or `never` (*Never counted*). `boxPagePartsWords` is *6 parts* beside the list's heading. `BOX_SCAN_PATH` is `/b`.
- **`BoxPublicPage.tsx`**, the box:
  - The top is the label: the stencil in a tile, the kind, the place and the code (`4F2A-9C18`), which the function has always answered and the page never drew. The heading still reads *Cage B3* to a screen reader.
  - The count's day is the verdict above, green, amber or grey.
  - The photo is a band 150 tall with its day on it; a tap opens it whole and a second tap folds it. It was drawn up to 320 tall, which left three lines of a six-line cage on a phone's first screen.
  - The search is a field on the page under *Not in this cage?* and holds the page's one blue press, **Find**. Words typed there are asked at once; an empty field opens the search as the old press did.
  - The sign-in sits under *On Click's crew?* as **Sign in to record a use or a count** (*Sign in to add a part* on an empty box), with the line that it is once per phone. It was the blue press, **Sign in to change a count**.
  - An empty box that has been counted reads *Nothing on hand in this cage.*; one never counted keeps *Nothing is recorded in this cage yet.* Both keep the search.
- **`BoxPublicPage.tsx`**, the other answers:
  - **A retired label** no longer offers Find a part on this site. `box-read` answers 410 `retired` before it reaches `?find=`, so that press ended in an error; and a retired label may be anywhere, so it should not open a site's search. It offers **Scan another label** (`/b`), and `?find=1` on a retired code shows the retired words.
  - **A code no box carries** offers **Type the code** (`/b`) and, on a phone that scanned a box in the last day, **Find a part on the site you last scanned**.
- Guide `keep-track-of-the-material-in-the-cages-and-boxes-on-site` (Scan a label), `docs/MATERIALS_ON_SITE_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/GLOSSARY.md`, `docs/twins/APP_DIRECTORY.md` (and the twin briefs built from it), the card.

## Not changed

No migration, no function, nothing to deploy for the page. The privacy line holds: the page still names no job, customer, address, price or person. Lines at zero, the Can't find mark, the text to the office and the stencil on a retired label are the second PR, which changes the kernel and `box-read`.

## Tests

`boxPage.test.ts`: the verdict today, yesterday, inside the 14 days, on the fourteenth day, never counted, a day that cannot be read, a phone a day behind; the part count. `BoxPublicPage.render.test.tsx` (clock held at 2026-11-10): the tile, the code, the verdict fresh and due, the part count, the photo band folding open, the sign-in's words on a full and an empty box, a counted-empty box, the field asking at once, a retired label with no search and with `?find=1`, a wrong code's two presses.
