# v2.8569 — Intro to HVAC: two pale labels in lesson 18's drawing are darker (2026-11-25)

Punch list #419, from the read of the course in light on 2026-11-25. No migration, no edge function.

**Seen.** In lesson 18, *Where water meets air*, the two small labels inside the tank, *the heat goes* and *into the water*, measured 3.96 to 1 against the tinted shapes under them. Every other label of the 538 in the course measured 4.5 to 1 or more.

**The cause.** The two `Label`s in `WaterMeetsAir.tsx` passed no colour, so they fell back to `var(--text-muted)`. That grey is made for the page's own ground. The tank's tints, `var(--bg-red-100)` over `var(--bg-blue-200)`, are darker in light and it loses its margin on them.

**The mend.** Both labels take `color="var(--text-600)"`, the token `WaterHeaterCutaway.tsx` already gives its labels inside the same tank on the same tints. Nothing else in the drawing moved.

**Measured**, by the WCAG sum on the tokens' values in `src/index.css`, not on a screen:

| Label | Ground | Light, before | Light, after | Dark, after |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| *the heat goes* | the red tint | 3.96 | 6.2 | 9.5 |
| *into the water* | the blue tint | 3.96 | 6.2 | 6.8 |
