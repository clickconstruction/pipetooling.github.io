# v2.8209 — Intro to HVAC: a second pass, seventeen more drawings for facts that were words alone (2026-11-21)

A fifth PR on the Intro to HVAC course (v2.8158, v2.8163, v2.8168, v2.8169). Taunya asked for a pass on the live page once all four parts were merged, looking for discrepancies, for words, and for places that told a fact instead of showing it. Every lesson was read on the live page as a preview, the course being off. Content and drawings only: no migration, no edge function, no screen.

## What the pass found, and what changed

**Told, not shown** was the largest gap. A lesson had one drawing and several facts that only a sentence carried. Seventeen new figures, each named by the lesson line that follows the sentence teaching its words:

- `gauge-and-chart` (lesson 3): a gauge reads a pressure and the chart for that refrigerant turns it into a boiling temperature.
- `line-set-care` (6): a kinked line, an open end capped, the locking caps on the filling ports.
- `vent-or-recover` (7): a cut line letting the refrigerant out beside a pump filling a tank.
- `drain-end` (8): the drain tied straight in, with sewer gas coming up, beside the drain ending in the open.
- `four-defences` (9): the code's four ways, each in its own frame. The older `pan-and-float` mixed two of them in one picture and stays as the lesson's opening drawing.
- `defrost` (10): frost on the outdoor coil in heating, and the steam of a defrost.
- `furnace-order` and `two-furnaces` (11): the call, the inducer proved, the flame proved, the blower; and a standard furnace's hot metal vent beside a condensing furnace's cool plastic one with its drain.
- `two-flames` (13): the fed flame, blue and steady, beside the starved one, yellow with soot.
- `left-alone-on-the-vent` (14): the shared vent drawing with the furnace on it, and spilling with the furnace gone.
- `co-scale` (15): 50, 70 and 1,200 ppm as bars drawn to scale.
- `duct-faults` (16): a kink, a sag, a crushed spot, a leak in a supply and a leak in a return.
- `clear-the-drain` (19): a wet vacuum at the far end pulling the slime out, with the capped tee as the other way in, beside the line blown toward the unit and the slime in the pan. The strip showed that the drain is cleared and never how. One Must know line joins the lesson to say which way a clog moves, and the caption keeps the trap's own measure, *can land*.
- `ice-on-the-line` (20): the big wrapped line at the outdoor unit cool and wet, beside the same line white with ice. It is the one sign of a frozen coil a person outdoors can see, and only a sentence carried it.
- `backdraft-signs` (21): the melted rings, the soot and the streaks on top of a water heater, and the smoke test.
- `two-power-cuts` (24): the outdoor disconnect, which cuts the outdoor unit only, and the indoor unit's own switch.
- `attic-way` (25): the opening, the walkway, the work space, the light and the outlet from above, each with the code's size.

**Discrepancies between a lesson and its drawing**, five figures changed:

- `outdoor-unit` (4): the lesson says a 24 V call starts the unit. The drawing showed the 240 V alone and now shows both.
- `furnace-cutaway` (11) now shows the cooling coil on top of the furnace, which lesson 5 says and no drawing showed.
- `the-card` (7) named two tanks by number and the third by its class. All three are named by number, with the class under the third.
- `supply-and-return` (16) draws and labels the plenum its lesson names, the box on the unit where the supply duct begins.
- `inspector-mechanical` (29) was six phrases in boxes. It is a house in section with the six places numbered where they are, and the six named beneath.

**A discrepancy only the live page showed.** Lesson 20's number line held a second bold, `**off** for the thermostat and **on** for the fan`. A number's label is drawn as plain words, so the live chip read *off · for the thermostat and \*\*on\*\* for the fan*, asterisks and all. The line is reworded with one value, and `parseNumbers` now refuses a label that holds `**`, with its test, so no course can ship one again. No other course has one.

**Discrepancies between lessons:**

- Lessons 3 and 6 called the holder of the federal card a *certified technician*. Lesson 26 uses those words for the state's designation, which is a different paper. Lessons 3 and 6 now say *a technician who holds the federal card*, and lesson 3 points at lesson 7.
- Lesson 17 listed the thermostat's wires in one order in its numbers and another in its sentence and drawing. One order now, R, Y, G, W and C.
- Lesson 13's number line said the openings start 12 in *from* the top and the bottom. The code and the lesson's own sentence say *within* 12 in. The line now says how close.
- Lesson 21's number line named four causes of a backdraft and its sentences give five, the sagging or loose vent connector being left out. The line now reads *a vent blocked or sagging*, which keeps the four and loses none.

**Words:**

- Lesson 7 used *lower flammability* in a trap with no sentence teaching it. The Must know line on the A2L class now names it.
- Lesson 13 called carbon monoxide *the poison gas of the lesson after next*. It now says *a poison gas* and names lesson 15.
- Lesson 13 said the two appliances *can need a room far bigger than a closet* with no number. It now works one example from the two rules it had already given: a furnace of 100,000 Btu an hour and a water heater of 40,000 need 7,000 cubic feet at 50 cubic feet per 1,000, and each opening is 140 sq in at 1 sq in per 1,000.
- Lesson 8 named a *standpipe*, plumbing's word, with nothing to say what one is. It now says a washer's open drain pipe.
- Lesson 12's trap called a wrong sediment trap *a drip left over from the pipe*. It now says a leftover stub of pipe.
- Lesson 17's trap gave three effects of a backwards filter that no source had been read for. It now says the one thing a first-timer can act on, that the arrow is the only sign.
- Lesson 28 named a *boot* once and never again. It now says the metal box a register will fit.
- Lesson 16's caption said *treated air*. It now says heated or cooled air.
- Three number lines reworded to read as a sentence (lessons 9, 13 and 28, where the chip read *before · the drywall is when the rough is inspected*).
- Lesson 27 named its sixth place, *the machines with water in them*, with nothing to say which machines. It now points at lesson 18, as its first place points at lesson 8.

## Checked

The first run of CI failed on `readablePairs.test.ts`, which counts the literal inks in the figures folder: `two-flames` had drawn its blue as a raw hex. It now takes the theme's blue, the same hex in light and a paler one in dark. `courseContent.test.ts`, `figures.test.ts` and the render smokes pass on all forty-seven figures and thirty lessons. Every new and changed drawing was shot in light at 420 px and in dark at 340 px, and the collisions the shots showed were fixed before the commit. All thirty lessons were then walked on the dev server at 390 px with a script that measures every label of every drawing: none leaves its drawing, no two overlap, no raw markup shows and nothing scrolls sideways.
