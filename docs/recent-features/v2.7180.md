# v2.7180 — Lien desk: an affidavit sent for approval tells the leader (2026-11-13)

Punch list #305, the last bullet under *Decisions that are the owner's*: should the leader's phone buzz when an affidavit is sent for his approval? Found 2026-11-11, answered yes 2026-11-13.

## What was true

A notice sent for approval has told the leader since v2.4872: `tellLeaderOfLienApproval` calls `notify-lien-approval`, which pushes his phone or emails him. An affidavit told nobody. Neither the Affidavits pane's **Send for approval ▸** nor the list's **Send N for approval…** (v2.6918) made the call, so the paper waited until he opened the desk or the Dashboard.

## What changed

- **The leader's words** (`lienAffidavitApprovalPush` in `supabase/functions/_shared/lienApprovalPush.ts`, shared with the client's tests): the job, the last day to file, and what he is asked to do. *Approve a lien affidavit* over *891 · Tune 5 Liberty Hill · file by Nov 16 · 3 days*. The email adds *The lien right ends after that day.* and *Open the Lien desk to approve it or hold it.* No claim and no mail-by day: those are a notice's words.
- **The batch rule.** One press is one call, and `lienAffidavitTellsByLeader` gives each leader one message whatever the count. Twelve papers read *Approve 12 lien affidavits* over *12 jobs · first to file by Nov 16 · 3 days*, and the email lists each job beside its own day, the soonest first.
- **`notify-lien-approval` learns the kind**: `{ kind: 'affidavit', items: [{ item_id, due_ymd }] }`. The caller must hold an office role. A row counts only when it is an affidavit, awaiting approval, and sent for it in the last fifteen minutes (`lienAffidavitAskable`), so the call can only repeat what the desk just did. A paper's leader is its job's master, else every leader but the caller, as for a notice. Logged as `lien_affidavit_approval_ask`, which the Dashboard's *While you were off* line names *lien affidavit*. The delivery (push through each leader's hours, email with no device, the history row) moved into one `tellLeaders` that every kind shares; the four older kinds send what they sent.
- **Both presses call it** (`tellLeaderOfLienAffidavits` in `lienDeskIo.ts`), after the write and only for a paper that landed in Awaiting approval (`lienAffidavitTellPapers`). The pane's toast is the notice send's own: *Sent for approval. Malachi's phone has it.* The list's follows its count: *Sent 12 affidavits. Malachi's phone has the 12 that wait for approval in one message.*
- **A failed tell never blocks the send.** The call never throws. The papers are already sent, and the toast says *The leader was not told*, the reason, and that the Dashboard shows them.
- **The address opens the paper.** `/lien-desk?tab=affidavits&job=…` now opens the Affidavits list on that job, which is where the push for one paper lands. The Materials line that already used this address gains the same.

## The gap before the redeploy

`notify-lien-approval` is owed a redeploy. Until then the deployed function does not know the affidavit. The new request carries no `item_id` on purpose, so the old function answers it *Missing item_id* and sends nothing. It never words an affidavit as a notice with a mail-by day. In the gap the affidavit is still sent for approval, and the desk reads: *The leader was not told. The part of the app that tells the leader is waiting for its update.* After the redeploy the same press tells him. The notice send is untouched in the gap and after.

## Decisions

- **The office, not the drafter.** A notice's tell asks that the caller drafted the item. A batch sends papers other people drafted, so the affidavit kind asks for an office role and a send in the last fifteen minutes.
- **The day is the client's, the words are the function's.** As for a notice, the only thing a caller may say about a paper is its day, and only when it is a date.
- **No money in the affidavit's line.** The ask named the job, the day and what he is to do. A batch has no single claim.

## Proof

- Unit tests: 11 cases on the leader's words, the batch rule, the request reader and the fifteen-minute rule; 8 on which papers are told and the office's words, every sentence through the plain-words rule.
- Render tests on the desk: a batch of two under no standing rule makes one call with both papers and their day; a batch a rule approves makes none; a failed tell leaves the papers sent and says why; a paper whose send failed is not in the tell; the pane's own press tells of its one paper; the address opens on the named job.
- `deno check` on the function and `npm run check:edge-bundles` pass.

Not observed: a real push or email. The function is not deployed by this change, and nothing was sent for approval on the live desk.
