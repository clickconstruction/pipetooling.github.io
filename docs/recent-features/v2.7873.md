# v2.7873 — Materials on site: a box's screen shows its parts first (2026-11-18)

Punch list #398. The owner, 2026-11-18, with a screenshot of a box on the Tally's On site tab: *Help me improve this page it is hard to follow as a user*. He took every pick of the first mock-up, asked for a second pass (*is this the best we can do? I'm a huge fan of show instead of tell*), and approved it (*I like it, build it all*). It is built on top of Take from a box (v2.7824, v2.7828).

## What the look found

- **The parts started 71% of the way down a 390 by 844 phone**, under a title row, the tabs, a ticket press, the header, a line of what is in it, three chips and a paper row.
- **22 presses of much the same weight**, six of them under 48 high. Every part drew its four presses open.
- **A press was sized by nothing.** The ledger held 162 counts and 94 box photos against 3 presses of Used and 1 of Can't find.
- **Short did not say what it does.** A short count means missing. The press asks the office for more.
- **Office work had a third of the box's presses**: Edit, Say it better, Move, two prints, the label code, Retire.

## What changed

- **The screen's body is its own component**, [`OnSiteBoxScreen.tsx`](../../src/components/tally/OnSiteBoxScreen.tsx), over the pure [`boxScreen.ts`](../../src/lib/materials/boxScreen.ts). It draws and tells. Every write and every sheet stays in `TallyOnSiteTab.tsx`, which hands it the strips, the Photo tile's press, the photos and takes, and the Take bar.
- **The box's name, then More.** The header is the back press, the name, its kind, what its stickers say and a person's own words for what is in it. The app's own line from the parts is gone from the head: the parts are the next thing on the screen.
- **The parts are closed rows with their numbers.** A tap opens a part's presses under it, two by two, one part at a time: **Used**, **Move to…**, **Can't find**, **Ask for more** (`partPresses`). A part with none left keeps **Ask for more** alone. Unsorted's parts stay open and lead with **Put in…** (`partsOpenByThemselves`). A row with something wrong wears amber and says it (`partRowNote`). Under the parts, once: *Tap a part if some are missing, moved or needed.*
- **Short is Ask for more** (`ASK_MORE_PRESS`). The sheet, the request and the rule about who may file it are v2.6742's, unchanged.
- **Four tiles that each carry their own fact** (`boxTiles`): **Count G12** (*Counted yesterday by Mateo*, amber with *Due. Last counted 16 days ago*), **Photo** (*2 so far, Nov 17*, or what a photo taken now would be of in the minutes after a press, *Of G12 as you counted it*), **Add a part** (*1 kind in G12 now*), **Where is it?** (*Level 2, by the north stair, pinned Nov 12*). A box with no line to walk has no Count tile, and the odd tile out takes the whole row. On a 320 phone the tiles stack. `PhotoPress` takes `fact` and `factStyle` and draws itself as the tile.
- **More** (`moreRows`, the sheet `on-site-box-more`): Edit, Say it better or Say what is in it, Move, Print the label, Print the manifest, Copy the label code, and Retire apart at the foot, each with one line that says what it does. The line that says who last touched the box moved into it.
- **Photos** is the caption over the box's own photos, drawn only when it has one. A filed photo there says its day and who took it (`BoxPhotos`' `quiet`, `boxPhotoDayWords`). The box's takes follow under their own heading, *Taken from G12* (`BoxTakes`), so a box with takes and no photo of its own reads one heading, not two.
- **Take from G12 is the screen's only blue press.** The Take bar, the take left open and the pin ask are the train's own, in their places.
- **The Tally's title row folds away while a box is open** (`JobTally.tsx`, beside the tabs and Photo of a ticket that v2.7824 folds).

## The numbers
