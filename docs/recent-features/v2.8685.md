# v2.8685 — The sign-in page on a small phone: the three trades stay on one row (2026-11-27)

No card. Finding 285 of the fixing thread's ledger, met on 2026-11-27 while the sign-in card was being pictured for v2.8674. Client only: one stylesheet. No migration, no edge function. Nothing owed after the merge.

**Read.** `AuthPublicLandingLayout.tsx` draws three trade tiles, Plumbing, Electrical and HVAC, over the photo on every public page that keeps the full hero: the sign-in page among them. In `authPublicLanding.css` the row wraps, each tile is at least 100 px wide at 480 px and under, and the gap is 1.25rem. So the three need 347 px. A phone 375 px wide gives the row 335, and the third tile wrapped alone under the other two. Measured on `main`: two rows at 375, 360 and 320 px, one row at 393 and wider. The second row pushed the sign-in card 119 px down the first screen.

**The mend.** At 480 px and under the tiles keep to one row and share its width.

- `.auth-service-icons` does not wrap there, and its gap is 0.75rem.
- `.auth-service-icon` takes an equal share (`flex: 1 1 0`, `min-width: 0`), and its side padding is 0.5rem where it was 1rem.
- Above 480 px nothing changes.

**What a person sees that is new.** On a phone the three tiles stand on one row and line up with the edges of the header plate and the form. At 375 px the form starts 121 px higher. At 393 px, where they already fit, each tile is a few pixels wider and they reach the same edges, where they were centred with 3 px to spare.
