# v2.7330 — The Lien desk's Board: the kernel (PR 1 of 4) (2026-11-15)

Client only, nothing drawn yet. The owner asked on 2026-11-15 for a Board: the whole lien month as a flow chart, one bin per stage in two columns (the monthly notices on the left, the lien affidavits on the right), every job a small chip in its bin coloured by urgency, thin arrows down each column and one from Sent across to Needs the property facts, four tiles above, the busiest bin lit. After review he asked that it replace The lien month strip and Steps of each paper at the top of To do rather than become a fifth view. This PR is the rule behind it; the next three draw it, make every chip a zoom, and put its four numbers on the Dashboard.

## What landed

- `src/lib/jobs/lienBoard.ts` (+ `lienBoard.test.ts`, 22 tests): `buildLienBoard({ notices, affidavits, retainage, todayYmd, jobNumber, jobName, gcName, leaderName })` over the queues the desk already builds, pure. It answers:
  - the bins in column order (`LIEN_BOARD_NOTICE_STAGES`: needs_owner · to_send · awaiting · in_mail · sent, then held · missed beside; `LIEN_BOARD_AFFIDAVIT_STAGES`: needs_property · to_send · awaiting · ready · filed, then held · missed), each with its chips sorted by due day (`compareLienBoardChips`, the undated last, then by number) and `lienBoardBinsShown` hiding Held and Missed while empty;
  - each chip `{ jobId, label, kind, stage, bin, dueYmd, daysLeft, stepsLeft, urgency, atRisk, destination, tip, pairKey, openBalance, pile }`. `urgency` (`lienBoardUrgency`): `day` within a day or past, `week` within seven, `waiting` in Awaiting approval, else `later`; the day wins over the wait since the bin already says it waits. `atRisk` (`lienBoardAtRisk`): the stages still to pass (`stepsLeft`: 4 · 3 · 2 · 1 · 0 down the flow, 2 for held) outrun the working days from today through the due day (`lienBoardWorkingDaysLeft`, weekdays, both ends counted). `destination` names the desk's door for the stage: Needs the owner → `{ kind: 'property', focus: 'owner' }`; Needs the property facts → the property at the first missing line (owner · legal · homestead_answer), the notice when it is the one gate left, else the affidavit pane; To send, Awaiting approval, Sent, Held, Missed → `{ kind: 'paper', tab }`; In the mail → `{ kind: 'run', itemId }`; Ready to file → the affidavit pane; Filed · Served → `{ kind: 'lienWindow' }`;
  - the four tiles (`by_day`: the count due by the next open day with *N to file · M to send* and *N will not make Monday as they stand.* or *N is past its day.*; `waiting`: the count waiting on the leader by name; `in_mail`: approved and printed with *Type the tracking numbers.* or *Print the run.*; `unpaid`: each job's balance once across both columns);
  - the lit bin (`lienBoardLitBin`: the busiest, the first on a tie), the pairs (a job in Needs the owner and Needs the property facts whose owner gate is the one failing: one `pairKey` on both chips, one door), the arrows (`lienBoardArrows`: four down each column and the cross from Sent to Needs the property facts), the retainage line under the notices (`lienBoardRetainageLine`), the day words (`lienBoardDayWords`: *today*, *tomorrow*, the weekday inside the week, else *Nov 22*), the Dashboard's four dots (`lienBoardDots`) and the leader's view (`lienBoardForLeader`: the two Awaiting approval bins and one counted press from `batchWords`, *Approve 2 papers · 1 is past its day ▸*, a paper past its day held with its reason).
- The two names that wait on sibling PRs are one constant, `LIEN_BOARD_WORDS`: *To send* (PR 8186 renames To draft) and *In the mail* (the run-checklist session renames Printed to *In the run*). When they land the swap is one line.

## The one thing "is this the best we can do" changed

A paper past its day carried the risk mark at first, since its stages outran zero days. The mark is a forecast, *will not make Monday as it stands*, and a late paper is a fact; so `atRisk` is false once the day is past and the top tile names the late ones on their own, *1 is past its day.*
