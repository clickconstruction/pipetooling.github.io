# v2.7120 — The Bridge's Market window: press an amount to see what makes it up (punch list #334, PR 4) (2026-11-13)

PR 4 of the Bridge train (card #334, `to-dos/bridge-pulse/`), the top of stack A, on PR 3. The Market window opens on two amounts: what the bids win a week and what the estimates sell a week. The owner: "click on either of these two sections and see a breakout of what makes it up"; "Those breakouts can be in an additional modal. One modal for each."; then "expand any of those sections and see the specific bids that make it up and specific estimates".

## What changed

- **The two amounts are presses** (`BridgeMarketDesk.tsx`): each reads *what makes it up ›* under its figure, and its label says the amount and what the press opens (`breakoutPressLabel`).
- **A breakout window for each** (`MarketBreakoutWindow`): a sibling window over the Market window, with the pressed amount at its head. Escape and a press outside close it alone; the Market window's frame is handed `escCloses={false}` while one is open, so one Escape closes one window.
- **Bids** (`bidsBreakout` in `market/marketBreakout.ts`): a row for each size of bid, with what went out a week in that size, the won rate that size is read at, and what that wins a week. A quiet line under a row says how the rate is made, and when a size borrows the rate of all bids. The rows add up to the amount on the tile.
- **Estimates** (`estimatesBreakout`): **Approved**, **Declined**, **Passed over** and **No answer** by count and dollars, the won rate they make, then **Still out**, and what went out a week and what that sells at the rate.
- **A row opens on its bids or estimates, by name**, largest first, five then **Show all**. A size of bid opens on what was sent in the window, then every bid won, lost and with no mark that its rate rests on. Each name is a link: a bid to `/bids?tab=bid-board&bidId=<id>`, an estimate to `/estimates/<number>`.
- **The lists cannot disagree with the counts**: `BidSizeWins` and `EstimateWins` gain `members`, the places in the list handed in that are behind each count, kept by the same loop that counts. `BidSent` and `EstimateSent` gain optional `id`, `name` and (estimates) `number`, filled by `loadBidWins.ts` and `loadEstimateWins.ts`; the counting never reads them.
- **The page** (`Bridge.tsx`) hands the Market window `bids`, `estimates` and `todayYmd`: the lists the won rates were built from, in the same order.

Nothing is counted in the breakout: every figure is one the won-rate kernels already held.

## Deploy

`bidWins.ts`, `estimateWins.ts`, `loadBidWins.ts` and `loadEstimateWins.ts` are copied to the edge functions; `npm run gen:edge-kernels` ran. After the merge, deploy what `npm run check:edge-drift` names. The Monday email's figures do not change: `members` and the names ride along unread. No migration.

## Verify

Walked on a dev server as a dev on production data, in headless Chrome, at 375, 768 and 1400 wide, light and dark. Every write to the database was refused; nothing that records is in these windows, and no link was followed.

- Both amounts are buttons that read *what makes it up ›*.
- The bids' window opened with a row for each size and their total; the estimates' with its six rows. Every row that opens was opened: each list showed at most five until **Show all**, then as many items as the row's own count said, every item a link of the right kind.
- Escape closed the breakout and left the Market window open. So did a press outside. With no breakout open, Escape closed the Market window.
- The page never scrolls sideways and nothing passes the window's edge. At 375 wide the window is 343 px.
- No page error at any width.

Left as the snapshot drew it: a row's press is 32 px tall and a named bid or estimate is a line of text, on a phone too. The lists are long and dense by design.
