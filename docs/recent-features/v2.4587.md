# v2.4587 — Procurement log: To order shows orders, not single lines (2026-10-05)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. PR 2 of the procurement log redraw (*Procurement log: orders on a calendar*; its card was PR #4527, closed unmerged once the train shipped): section 7 of the spec. PR 1 was [v2.4581](v2.4581.md). The calendar is PR 3 and the phone cards PR 4.

**Wendi has not seen the grouping.** The to-do says to show her the third draft before this PR, because the groups are a reading of how she places an order. The owner chose to build first. The grouping is one function, `orderSections`; if she orders by fixture or all at once, the groups follow her there.

## What changed

On a wide screen the **To order** lens is a table of four columns (tick, Part, Qty, the right-hand words) in five sections. A section with no lines is not drawn. Each section head folds.

| Section | Lines | Grouped into | Opens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Order now · National Wholesale** | `released` | one group per order-by date, soonest first | the first group open |
| **On order** | `ordered` | one group per PO, late first | folded |
| **Sent back by the GC** | `sent_back` | no groups, a line per part | open |
| **Waiting on their answer** | `awaiting`, `not_submitted` | one group per fixture | folded |
| **On site** | `delivered` | one group per PO | folded |

- **An order to place**: *Order by 11/03* **2** *EWC-1 · Trim Set, needed 12/08 · 5 wk lead*, with **Mark ordered…** at the right. Within three days the name is amber and says how soon (*· tomorrow*) and the button is the primary one; past its date it is red.
- **Mark ordered…** opens a form under the group: *Mark 6 parts ordered*, an **Ordered** date that starts as today, a **PO** box, **Mark 6 ordered** and *Cancel*. It marks the ticked parts of the group when any are ticked, else the whole group (`rowsToMark`).
- **An order placed**: *PO 4502* **3** *WHA-200, WHA-300, WHA-500 · Top Out, needed 11/10 · 2 wk lead · ordered 10/20*, then *Arrives 11/03*, or in red *Arrives 10/29, 9 d late*. Lines with no PO are one group, *No PO*.
- **A part sent back**: the tag, the product, the quantity, then *They wrote “TEL145”* or *No note from them*, **Pick another product** (the row's Edit window on that part) and *Change their answer…*.
- **A fixture that waits**: *LAV-2* then *LAV2 × 6 · 1 part · 5 order only*; a fixture of one part shows the product. *Two carriers* keeps its pill. At the right *answer by 11/10* when it can be worked out, and **Their answer…**. A fixture with no product reads *no product yet* with *Open it*. Inside, the parts bought but not sent to the GC come after *Ordered, not on the GC's copy · 5 parts*.
- **An order on site**: *PO 4471* **2** *FCO, FD · ordered 10/06*, then in green *✓ On site 10/15*.
- **A part inside a group** is one line: the product, its catalog words, the quantity, and *Dates…* at the right, which opens the dates editor as before. The tag shows when the group mixes fixtures; the stage and lead time show under the part only when the group's parts differ.
- **Changed since the last update**: a group whose parts all changed wears a small blue **new**. Before the first update nothing is marked.
- With a step or a missing fact picked, every group is open, so the short list shows its lines.

## How it is built

- [`src/lib/submittals/procurementOrders.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/procurementOrders.ts), new: `orderSections`, `orderBySoon`, `tagsOf`, `rowsToMark`, `theyWrote`.
- `reviewNote` on `ProcurementItemSource` and `ProcurementRow`: `procurementItemsFrom` reads the part's `review_note`, else the row's.
- `SubmittalProcurementPanel.tsx`: the orders table ahead of the old body; `renderRow` takes an `order` option for the compact part line (the editor, the tick and the doors are the same ones); `markGroup`; `foldOpen`, `marking`.
- `procurementSpacex.fixtures.ts`: the to-do's `RAW` and `LATER` tables as rows, shared by the two kernels' tests.

## Where the build differs from the spec

- **A phone keeps today's cards and sections** until PR 4. The old To order grouping (`procurementSections`, `foldByHouse`) lives on for it.
- A line added by hand that is not ordered yet stands as its own group under *Waiting on their answer*, with no answer door. The spec does not speak of hand lines.
- Approved parts with no order-by date are one group, *No order-by date yet*, last in their section.
- The fixture's **Their answer…** opens the row's window with no part ringed.
