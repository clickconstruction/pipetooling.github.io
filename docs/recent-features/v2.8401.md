# v2.8401 — Job Summary: find a job's supply house invoices by a part on the paper (2026-11-23)

Punch list #431, PR 3 of seven. The owner asked what it takes to make supply house invoices searchable. The first size of that needs no new store: punch list #298 already reads each bill's lines into `supply_paper_lines`, with the house's code, the maker's number and the description. This PR lets the job's list search them, and lists them beside the paper in the bill viewer. No migration and no edge function.

## What changed

- **The search reaches the lines** (`searchSupplyInvoices` in `src/lib/jobs/supplyInvoiceList.ts` takes the lines by invoice). A word the row's own four facts do not hold may be on its paper: in a line's description, or in the house's code or the maker's number with or without their dashes (*bvfp200* finds *BV-FP-200*). Every word of a search must still be met, by the facts or by a line, so *valve oct* is the October bills with a valve. A typed day is a day and is never looked for on the paper. A month typed alone is also read as a word.
- **The row says which line found it.** Under the invoice number: *On the paper:* and the line's description, the search's words marked (`markWords`). A row its own facts found says nothing more.
- **The list reads the lines once.** For the office, a list long enough to have a search box reads its bills' papers through `paperIo.loadForInvoices`, under the office's own read of `supply_papers` and `supply_paper_lines`. The box then reads *Search house, invoice, date, amount or a part*. Until the lines come, and if the read fails, the search is the four facts alone. Nobody outside the office reads the lines, and a short list asks for none.
- **The viewer lists the lines** beside the paper (`BillFacts.lines`, `billLinesToList`): the count, the description, the house's code and the amount, the first eight of a long bill with **Show all N lines**. With a search on, the lines that hold its words come alone under *Lines that match your search*, marked, so the reason a bill was found is the first thing read. The PDF is drawn on a canvas and has no words to mark, so the list beside it is where a match shows.
- **The list is a little wider**, 640 px in place of 560, so a line under a number does not wrap the supply house's name.

## What it reaches and what it does not

Exact, and only what was read: about nine bills in ten have their lines. A bill with no PDF, or one the sweep holds, is found by its number, house, date and amount and never by a part. The search is this job's list alone. One search across every bill is PR 5.
