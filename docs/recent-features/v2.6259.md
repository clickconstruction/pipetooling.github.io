# v2.6259 — The courtesy notice before a lien: the rules and the words (2026-11-02)

Punch list #231 (`to-dos/lien-courtesy-notice/README.md`), PR 1 of four. The owner, 2026-11-01: supply houses send a plain letter before they lien, *we are going to lien you, we already sent you a notice, here is where you can pay*, and it converts; does the Lien desk have that step? It did not: between a sent § 53.056 notice and the affidavit the desk offered only letter two, counsel's certified second packet, and on the live desk fifteen sent notices sat at *day 18 · letter two overdue* with none pressed. The owner took every recommendation the same day (*Take all recommended*) and said *Build it*.

- **`src/lib/jobs/lienCourtesyNotice.ts`** (new, pure) — the kernel the press will read. `courtesyStatus` says where the courtesy stands on a sent notice, in this order: nothing out; closed by a payment, the GC's written okay, the owner's call, the filed affidavit or a reply after the last send; held by the demand letter's own gate (`lienLineBlockedReason`: a homestead, no approved work month, the window closed) or when the affidavit's day is inside a week; sent inside a week of the last press; waiting before day 7 (`COURTESY_FROM_DAY`); due. `courtesyPlan` plans each recipient's email and text and says why one does not go; `courtesySendButton` counts them through `countedLabel` (*Send 3 messages*, dead at zero). The words: `courtesyEmailText` for the GC (the notice stands, this is a reminder and not a new notice, the lien's day from the affidavit step, the pay page's link, never a joint check, the signer's name and number) and for the owner (the pay page's owner rule word for word, nothing here is billed to you), `courtesyTextBody` for the text (`quo-send` adds the opt-out line itself), `courtesySubject` / `courtesyOwnerSubject`, `courtesyGreeting` (the one line the office may change), and a live pay offer repeated in `lienOfferSentence`. The record rides the sent item's `fields.courtesy[]` (`parseCourtesyRecords`, `withCourtesyRecord`, `courtesyRecordWords`, `courtesyOutcomeWords`), beside the owner's call and the GC's okay, so no migration.
- **Why the gate.** Texas Finance Code § 392.301(a)(8) forbids threatening an action not permitted by law, and the company collects its own debts under that chapter; the lien's day is never typed, and a message that cannot name one is held with the reason. The words keep counsel's never-say list, checked in the tests.
