# v2.7216 — Materials: Just price, a parts-book price without the re-label (2026-11-13)

Punch list #342, a follow-up to its train. The owner priced his first row in the pricing window and asked what a press on a parts-book offer does. It ties the row's name to that part, so the row reads the book's name as well as its price. He asked for a press that takes the price and nothing else.

## What changed

- **Just price** (`SitePricingWindow.tsx`): each parts-book offer has a second press on its right. It writes one row of the site's own prices (`on_site_prices`) with the part's price for one, the part's name as its source and the day the book last checked that price. No tie is written, so the row keeps the name the crew wrote and is not folded into the part's row. A bill's offer has no such press: it is a price already.
- **The source** (`20261113233000_on_site_prices_book_source.sql`, [`docs/migrations/20261113233000_on_site_prices_book_source.md`](../migrations/20261113233000_on_site_prices_book_source.md)): the table's source check takes `book` beside `typed` and `bill`. Nothing else changes.
- **The words** (`bookPrice.ts`, `sitePrices.ts`): a row priced this way reads *the parts book's price for 1" PEX TEE BRASS · checked Oct 3*, on its chip and under **Priced here**, with **Undo**. Since the price keeps the book's check day, a book price older than 90 days is an old price here too and shows under **An old price**.
- An offer is now a box with two presses, so on a phone **Just price** sits under its offer, on the right.

## What it does not do

- **The price is a copy.** A tie keeps reading the book, so **Check prices** refreshes it. A price taken with Just price stays as it was until it is checked again from **An old price**.
- **No number key.** The keys 1, 2 and 3 still press the offer itself.

## Checked

One new kernel case in `sitePrices.test.ts`, one new end-to-end case in `SiteMaterialsView.render.test.tsx` (the press, the row keeping its name, no tie, the worth, Undo from the row) and one line that the press is not drawn before the site's prices can be written. The window was looked at on the real site with every write to a table refused, at 1400 and 375 wide: three offers, three presses, no sideways scroll. The press itself was not made: it is a write.

## Deploy

Client, and the migration pushed right after the merge. Until the push, Just price is refused by the table's check and the window says the price was not saved; every other answer works.
