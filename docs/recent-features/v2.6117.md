# v2.6117 — Job Parts Tally keeps one width across its three tabs (2026-10-30)

The owner, from his phone: none of the three tabs reached the full width, and clicking through them felt clunky because the width expanded and contracted as each tab loaded.

## What was happening

`JobTally.tsx`'s page wrapper was `maxWidth` + `margin: 0 auto` with no `width`. It sits in Layout's column flex (`main.appMain` → the Outlet's column), and a flex item with auto side margins in the cross axis is not stretched: it takes its fit-content width, clamped to the container. So the page was as wide as the widest line each tab happened to be showing. At 375 px the Transactions and Materials Estimate tabs clamped to the full 359 px, and the On site tab's widest line was 354 px, so the page, and the tab row inside it (`width: 100%` of the page), pulled in by 5 px and re-centred by 2 px; a tab's loading line is narrower still, so every switch went narrow, then wide. On a desk the On site tab drew 354 px wide inside a 785 px window where the other two drew 512.

`.pageWrap` (v2.1003) already carries the cure for the same reason: `width: 100%; min-width: 0; box-sizing: border-box`. The tally page does not use that class.

## What changed

- **`src/pages/JobTally.tsx`**: the wrapper adds `width: '100%'`, `minWidth: 0` and `boxSizing: 'border-box'`; its two max widths (480 for the crew view, 760 for the office's Team queue on Transactions) carry the 2rem of padding, so a desk sees the same 512 / 792 px outer width as before.
- **`e2e/viewport-smoke.spec.ts`** pins it: at 375 px the tab row measures the same as each tab lands and again once it has settled, at least the viewport less the 24 px inset a side.

## Verified

Dev server at 375 px as the dev: the row reads 327 px at left 24 on all three tabs, before and after each tab's content lands; no sideways overflow. Nothing else about the tabs changed: no words, no presses.

No migration, no edge function.
