# v2.3798 — Hiring, the helper try-out loop PR 4: the column share, enforced in the database (2026-09-24)

PR 4 of [`to-dos/helper-tryout-loop`](../../to-dos/helper-tryout-loop/README.md) (punch list #24), the first of the three share PRs. The owner's original ask — "selectively share columns with the assistants so they can help call and bring on helpers that are not risks to their job" — becomes a table and a rule. One migration and one edge-function redeploy; no client change. PR 5 draws *Share with…* on the column header and the Active accounts line; PR 6 draws the assistant's trimmed tab.

## The change

- **Migration [`20260924040000_team_prospect_role_shares.sql`](../migrations/20260924040000_team_prospect_role_shares.md)** — `team_prospect_role_shares` (one row per column and person, written by a full holder), `user_hiring_shared_role_ids()` (the columns shared with the caller, empty without prospects staff access), `user_can_work_team_prospect(card)` (a dev, the board, or a share on the card's column), and the hiring policies rewritten around them: a share reads its column and that column's cards while they are on Screen, Interview or Try-out; edits, ranks, *Talked today*, Advances and adds inside it; and the database refuses everything else — Hire, Passed, *Keep trying*, a move to another column, a delete, a rename, a hired or passed card, another column. The trial verdicts and `team_prospect_trial_tally()` read for a share on the card's column too.
- **`create-user`'s Try out door** now gates on `user_can_work_team_prospect` instead of the board alone, so a share can press *Try out* (the card is still read as the caller, under RLS). Redeploy after the push.
- **What the plan had wrong:** PR 3's note said the share rule joins three gates, `end_team_prospect_trial()` among them. It joins two. Hire and Pass are the office's (the to-do's decision 5 and the mock-up's can / cannot table), so `end_team_prospect_trial()` keeps its gate.
- Docs: `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` § Hiring (the share), `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` → create-user, `GLOSSARY.md` (*column share*), the to-do's status and plan.
