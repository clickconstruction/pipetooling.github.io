## What changed

- **The audit skips words in a transparent ink**, an alpha under 0.15. It already skips an element at that opacity and a drawing's word in so faint a fill. Banking's sort headers hold an inactive column's arrow at its width with `color: transparent`, so the header does not shift when the sort moves. The People day book does the same for a future day's number. Words nobody sees are not words to read. A test holds it: a transparent `▾` and a `12` at 10% are skipped, a `Due` at 30% is still read.
- **The empty-cell dash** on Supply Houses (`SupplyHousesTab.tsx`: the aging cells, the open credits and the Total row) and in the Bid Board's customer review (`BidBoardCustomerReviewModal.tsx`) moves from `--text-faint-300` to `--text-faint`, the ink 46 other empty dashes in the app already wear: 3.60:1 on the page in light, 5.65:1 in dark. The review's `›` hover chevron keeps `--text-faint-300`: it marks a row, not a missing value.
- **A schedule block's `−` and `+`** take `var(--text-red-700)` and `var(--text-blue-700)` in `HubPeopleDayCell.tsx` and `ScheduleDispatchGrid.tsx`, instead of the light-mode literals `#b91c1c` and `#1d4ed8`. Light is unchanged. In dark they read 4.55:1 or better on `--bg-violet-100` and `--bg-blue-tint`. The block's chains icon takes the same blue.
- **The pins** in `readablePairs.test.ts`, both themes: `--text-faint` on the page and the surface at the lens's 3:1, since a dash that says nothing is there keeps its quiet on purpose; the two press inks on the two block tints at 4.5:1.

**Checked** on a build of this branch, served the same way. Every role and screen that flagged was read again in both themes at both widths, 96 frames. The only words under 3:1 left are the `☎` and the copper print, below. In the pane:
