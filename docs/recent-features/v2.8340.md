# v2.8340 — Dashboard: My Bids groups your bids by state, one box a bid (2026-11-23)

Step 2 of punch list #426. The owner's asks and picks of 2026-11-23: group the bids by state so a row stops saying it, give the name the row, one press per bid, and a hidden bid that shows again while it has unread updates. Client only. No migration, no edge function.

## What was there

- Three blue boxes two thirds of the row wide, each ending *Due 4/8/26 · Unsent*, with **Preview** and an eye icon beside it. The role chips sat inside the box and took the name's room: at 320 px a bid with both chips had no room for its name at all.
- Three links under the list: **View all**, **Sent bids (n)** and **View hidden (n)**.
- A press on the box opened the bid on Submission & Followup, whatever the person was doing on it.
- The amber *Unread updates* box was drawn with its row. A sent bid's row was drawn only after **Sent bids** was pressed, and a hidden bid's only after **View hidden**, so a note on either was not seen.
- With every bid hidden the card stopped drawing, and **View hidden** went with it.
- **Preview** was about 31 px tall and the eye 27 px square.

## What changed

- **The groups.** `buildMyBidsCard` in `src/lib/dashboardMyBids.ts` turns the bids into the Bid Board's states in its order: Unsent, Not yet won or lost, Won, Started or Complete. Each heading is a fold with its count. Unsent starts open and is always drawn. A state with no bid is left out. The counts add up to the number in the card's title.
- **What wants a look, on the heading.** *1 past due* in red on Unsent, *1 unread* in amber on any group.
- **One box a bid.** The name across the full width, then the value line: *Due* **Wed 4/8** *229 days past due*, **No due date** *19 days on the board*, or *Sent* **Fri 11/20** *3 days ago* (`myBidWhen`, over the board's `bidBoardDueCellParts` and `bidBoardNoDueDateParts`). The values are `WorkedOutValue`s. The role chips follow on the same line and wrap under it.
- **The edge.** The box and its 4 px edge are the Needs you row's (`NEEDS_YOU_RAIL`): red for past due or for no due date after 14 days, amber for due within three days, grey for the rest. A sent bid is never red.
- **One press per bid.** A press opens the bid's window (`useBidPreview().openBidPreview`), which has *Open in Bids* with a door to every tab. **Preview** is gone.
- **Hide in words.** **Hide** is a word press on each bid, 44 px tall. Step 4 turns it into a swipe on a touch screen and keeps the word press at a desk.
- **Hidden is a group.** The last group, folded, with **Show again** on each bid. Its count is the list under it: a stored id the read no longer holds counts for nothing.
- **The card draws while the person has any bid**, hidden or not. With every bid hidden it reads *Nothing unsent. The bids you hid are below.*
- **A note is seen.** A folded group still draws a bid with unread updates. A hidden bid with unread updates is drawn in its group until **Mark read** (`myBidIsPutAway`).
- **The trade** is left off while every bid shares one, and is on each bid when they do not.
- **One door.** **Bid Board ›** replaces **View all**. When the read came back full, a line beside it says so (`MY_BIDS_CUT_WORDS`).
- **New Bid** and **Mark read** are 44 px tall.

The amber box's two note streams were two copies of one block. They are one function now, with the same words and presses.
