# v2.7562 — Dashboard and Bridge: a supply house bill paid in part counts what is left (2026-11-17)

Punch list #374. The supply house payments train (v2.7450 to v2.7471) let a bill be paid in part and moved Materials to read what is left through `openAmount`. It left the Dashboard and the Bridge out on purpose: those readers also run on the edge through the kernel copy, so the fix wanted its own PR and its own redeploys. No bill on prod was paid in part yet, so no figure was wrong on the day this shipped.

Every amount below is made up: an $812.40 bill with $300.00 paid on it.

## What changes

- **The Dashboard's payables card.** `buildApBucket` counts `openAmount(inv)` for each unpaid bill and skips one with nothing left, so the *Supply* line and the total read $512.40 where they read $812.40. A credit memo used in part counts what is left of it, below zero. `buildApBucketFromAggregates` (the assistant's path) builds on the same bucket and follows. `loadDashboardFinancials` selects `amount_paid`.
- **The card's window.** A bill's row shows what is left, and a muted line under it reads `partPaidWords`: *$300.00 paid · $512.40 left of $812.40*, the words Materials shows. The bill view's title shows what is left, **Amount** stays the bill's whole amount, and **Paid so far** carries the same words. `DashboardApBill` gains `amountPaid` and `open`.
- **The Bridge's owed by you.** It reads the same `ap.total`: the *owed by you* figure, the accrual line, the net position's starting figure and the split in Money owed. Nothing changed on the page for these.
- **The Bridge's Supply invoices due.** The due read selects `amount_paid`, and `supplyDueByDay` adds what is left on each bill by its due day, for the cash forecast and Money owed's due weeks.
- **The bills behind *Ask the supply houses for more days*.** `Bridge.tsx` summed each house's bills due that day at their whole amount from the card's bill detail. It now sums `open`. The card did not name this reader.
- **The Bridge's net position and the Truth check.** `supplyPaidByDay` is now `supplyPaidByDayFromLines`: the lines of the live payments (`supply_house_payment_lines` with their payment), each on its payment's `paid_on`. A bill paid in two parts lands on two days, and a removed payment counts nowhere. Before, the read took `amount, paid_at` off the bill, so a part payment showed nowhere until the bill was paid in full and then landed whole on the last day.

## A credit memo marked used by hand

The card expected every paid bill to carry a line. One kind does not: a credit memo marked used by hand has no payment, since nothing was sent, and the backfill of 20261116230000 made a payment "from before" only for bills above zero. Read from the lines alone, such a credit memo would have dropped out of the history, where the old read counted it on its paid day. So the loader also reads the paid bills below zero in the window, and the kernel adds what each one's lines do not cover (`amount - amount_paid`) on the day it was marked. A bill its payments paid adds nothing there.

## Left as it was, on purpose

- The Bridge's materials read (bills by invoice date through their job allocations) and `supplyDatedByDay`: a bill's cost and the debt it adds are its whole amount on its date, however it is paid later.
- Money kept on account with a house is on no bill, so it lowers what is owed only once it is put on one, and then on its payment's day. The owner's call, 2026-11-17, when asked whether it should lower it on the day it was sent: "Only once on a bill".
- No fallback for a refused read of the payment lines. The lines are read under `is_office_staff()`, the Bridge is the owner's and a dev's, both pass it, and the edge reads as the service role.
