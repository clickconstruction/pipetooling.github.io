# v2.5669 — Pricing: an alternate price shows its own prices the first time you open it (2026-10-20)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Grace's report, relayed by the owner on 2026-10-20: *"When switching to my alternate it took a few clicks back and forth from the original before it populated my alternate prices."* Her two screenshots of one bid on Bids → Pricing: the Alternate 1 card open and reading **No prices yet** at $0.00 (Revenue $0, 0/50 priced, every row's sale price a dash, the footer *Only you see this*, the card offering *copy prices from WENDI*), and, a few clicks later, the same card at $105,075.73, 72% margin, *On their letter · alternate*, 50/50.

## The cause

A read of the price she was **leaving** landed after the alternate's own read and wrote its rows over the screen.

- The Workbench keeps one set of the open price's rows in [`useBidPricingEngine`](../../src/hooks/useBidPricingEngine.ts) — assignments, custom prices, hides (`loadBidPricingAssignments`) and the book (`loadPriceBookEntries`) — and the last read to land won. The switch's own read is abortable, but every price write reloads with the version it was typed into and no abort (`commitWorkbenchTypedPrice`, `applyWorkbenchPreview`, the margin brush's `reloadAssignments`, `copyPricesIntoViewedScenario`, `savePricingEntry` …), and `loadPriceBookEntries` had no guard at all.
- A typed sale price saves **on blur**, and clicking the other card is the blur: mousedown blurs the cell, the save writes to WENDI, the click opens Alternate 1 and its read goes out, then the save's reload of WENDI goes out **after** it and comes back last. The engine now holds WENDI's rows with Alternate 1 open.
- `scenarioPricingRows` prices only the open price's own rows (v2.3853), so WENDI's rows count for nothing and every row reads unpriced. The open card reads the live total (`cardRevenue`), $0 is "unpriced" (`cardFigures`), and the card drew *No prices yet*, *Only you see this* (the footer's unpriced branch) and the copy offer. The Price book chip still read *Alternate 1 · 122 entries* because its name comes from the open id and its count from whatever entries are loaded — the copy's book has the same count. Nothing reloaded the rows until the next switch, which is why going back and forth fixed it.
- A late read of a price's book did the same (`price_book_entries` from WENDI under Alternate 1).

Nothing wrong was written in the window: the save landed on the price it was typed into, and the priced-margin stamp writes only while the ★ price is open.

## What shipped

- **Only the newest read of the open price writes** ([`priceReadGate.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/priceReadGate.ts), `createPriceReadGate`): each read of a price's book or rows takes a ticket, and writes only if it is the newest ticket of its price and that price is open when it lands. The open price is mirrored in a ref the moment it is set (`setSelectedPricingVersionId` is now a wrapper, as `setSelectedBidVersionId` already was), so the check never waits for a render. Both loaders use it — no caller changed.
- **Loading is not empty**: the engine records which price its book and its rows were read for and exposes `pricingPricesReady` (`openPricePricesReady`). `cardRevenue` gives the open card `null` until it is ready, so the card reads … with its real footer, never *No prices yet*, and offers no copy over a price that has prices. A price with nothing priced still says *No prices yet* once its reads land.
- **Tests**: [`useBidPricingEngine.priceSwitch.render.test.tsx`](../../src/hooks/useBidPricingEngine.priceSwitch.render.test.tsx) holds reads back to land late — the save's reload after the switch (failed before: the open alternate read $0), a late book read (failed: WENDI's entries under Alternate 1), back and forth (failed: WENDI open at $0), the loading flag, and an empty price still ready; `priceReadGate.test.ts`; `pricingCardsRow.test.ts` and `PricingCardsRow.render.test.tsx` for the loading card.
- **Docs**: the Pricing map's preserve-quirks list (21).

## Left

- The summary bar (Revenue, Profit, the priced count) and the rows still show the switch's brief loading window as $0 and dashes; only the open card says ….
- A price typed into a row during that window writes against the rows still in state. It is a fraction of a second now that the stale read can't hold the screen.
