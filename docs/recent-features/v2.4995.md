# v2.4995 — GC projects: bill the customer on a won job (2026-10-08)

Owner Billing's O4a-3 in the GC mode real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/owner-billing-o4a.md` → *O4a-3, Bill the customer*, on branch `spike/gc-mode`): the window on `/gc` that presses O4a-1's functions (v2.4984) and prints with O4a-2's files (v2.4990). Guide: [`bill-the-customer-on-a-gc-job`](../../src/content/help/bill-the-customer-on-a-gc-job.md).

- **Bill the customer** on a won job's card (`GcBillCustomer.tsx`, at `?bill=`), for the money team (`canSeeGcMoney`: the owner, the leaders and the controller), like Change orders:
  - **Where we stand:** billed so far, what they hold back, certified or asked, and what waits on the architect (`ownerAccount`);
  - **This month's bill:** `ownerPayApp`'s lines with their worth, this month and so far, then the work so far, what they hold back, less earlier certificates, and what it asks. **Send pay application N** sends `payAppSendPayload` through `gc_send_owner_pay_app`; the first one opens the project's billing job. **See the form in Excel** and **See the form as a PDF** download the form. Until the contract is marked signed, the window says so instead;
  - **Retainage:** the percent and a step that lowers it partway, a plain update of the project's columns; bills that went keep theirs;
  - **Sent:** each pay application with its form, and **Record the certificate** (the amount, which starts at what we asked, the day, and why when it is less) through `gc_record_certificate`, which makes the bill on the billing job.
- **What it reads:** `loadGcBillingRows([project])` laid over the board's project and its change orders by `billingStateFor`, O6a's state builder (`src/lib/gc/billCustomer.ts`, which gains `payAppSendPayload`, `PayAppSend` and `PAY_APP_LINE_OF`). The writes are `gcIo.ts`'s `sendOwnerPayApp`, `recordCertificate` and `setOwnerRetainage`; each reads the bills again after it.
- **Tests:** the window's render test (send and the files, the certificate asking why when it is less, the retainage step, the unsigned contract), and the payload's round trip: what Send hands the database, read back through O5a's mapper, is the record `ownerPayAppToSend` makes.
- **Known limit:** on real data the trades' lines read 0 until Building's draws reach the board's project, so a bill today carries signed change orders at their percent done and our three lines following the trades' share.
- **Next:** our conditional waiver with each sent pay application (O4a-4), through the Pipeline's own waiver window on the billing job.
