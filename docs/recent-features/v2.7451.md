# v2.7451 — Lien desk: the old step rails, step briefs and notice timeline leave the code (2026-11-16)

Client only, nothing on screen changes. The owner's question of 2026-11-16 on a look at the desk: *"what should we do about: About 540 lines have no caller: the old step rail, the step brief and the notice timeline?"* The Board took the two top strips' place in v2.7332 and left `LienStepRail`, `LienStepBrief` and `lienStepBrief` in the code "for now, with no caller". Traced to the end, the set with no caller was wider than the three components: about 815 lines of code and 320 of tests. Nothing open planned to reuse it (punch list #362's three directions build on the Board and the row's card). The owner read the briefs' words, kept a copy and said to delete it all.

- `lienReminders.ts`: `buildLienReminders` with its types and `LIEN_REMINDERS_EMPTY`, `lienReminderWhy`, `lienReminderRungWords`, `lienReminderStepWho`, `lienReminderRungRule`, `lienReminderBrief` with `LienReminderBrief` and `LienBriefMove`. Its words for the sixteen rungs and the paying notice are in git before this version (`git show be60eee7d:src/lib/jobs/lienNextUpSteps.ts`, and `lienReminders.ts` beside it), and `v2.6594.md` records how they were settled. Bringing them back as a fold on the row's card is a separate, small piece if the office asks for it.
