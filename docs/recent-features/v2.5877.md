# v2.5877 — A rating ask rides any quick reply: the {rate} blank (2026-10-26)

Item 6 of the measuring follow-ups, the ask itself (the plumbing is [v2.5876](v2.5876.md)). The owner's rating ask rides the words the office already sends — a quick reply — rather than a send of its own, so it can sit in the done step's line, a thank-you, or anything else.

## The pieces

- **`{rate}`** joins the fill table (`quickReplies/blanks.ts`, sixteen blanks now): *a link to rate us*. The editor's chip row carries it, so the owner puts it in a line the way `{bill}` goes in.
- **The mint on press** (`ReplyBox.tsx`, the `{estimate}` pattern of v2.5645): a pressed line that holds `{rate}` goes into the box at once with its blank, `ratings/ratingLinkIo.ts` mints the ask through `mint_customer_rating` (the thread's contact, the job box's job, the contact's name), and the link takes the blank when it is back; the send waits on *Making the rating link…*, and a mint that fails leaves the blank, so half a line never goes. The RPC reuses a fresh unanswered ask to the same contact and job, so a re-press never forks the asked count.
- **Guide**: *edit the quick replies* gains **Ask the customer how we did**.

## Tests

`blanks.test.ts` / `filling.test.ts` (the sixteenth blank, its chip, a `{rate}` fill); `ReplyBox.rate.render.test.tsx` (a press fills the line and the minted link takes the blank, named by contact and job; no job mints on the person alone; a failed mint leaves the blank).

## Deploy

Needs v2.5876's migration on prod (`mint_customer_rating`) before the press works live. `blanks.ts` is in the kernel copy, so the copy is regenerated and `check:edge-drift` will name its importers to redeploy — the new blank changes nothing they do, so the redeploys can ride the next functional deploy.

## Verify

Open a ZZ test customer's thread, Edit the quick replies, add a line with the **{rate}** chip, press it: the line lands with the link, the send window goes, and `/rate?t=…` from the sent text answers with one tap. Press the line again: the same ask's link comes back.
