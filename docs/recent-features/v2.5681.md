# v2.5681 — Estimates: the Tell the customer sheet answers every press (2026-10-20)

PR 1 of punch list #133 (`to-dos/press-always-answers/README.md`), written after Taunya's live report on v2.5594's estimate send: *"I already hit send and nothing changed, the screen did not go away."* The sheet's email leg answered a bare `null` after any failure, said only in a transient toast, and `go()` returned silently — six legs all looked like "nothing happened". The worst leg: the send succeeded, the page's `load()` refresh after it failed inside the same try, and the sheet heard "failed" — email gone, badge Sent behind the open sheet, no text, no receipt, and every retry answered 400 *"Only draft estimates can be sent"*.

- **The contract** (`Estimates.tsx` `sendEstimateEmailAnswer`, kernel type `TellSendEmailAnswer`): the email leg answers `{ ok: true, acceptUrl, emailed, warning }` or `{ ok: false, kind: 'already-went' | 'refused' | 'slow', why, sentAtMs }`, never a bare null and never its own toast when the sheet is the caller (the legacy `sendEstimateEmail` wrapper keeps the toasts for the change order's and the $0 ask). The refresh moved out of the send's try: a page that fails to reload never reads as a send that failed. The fetch carries `AbortSignal.timeout(20_000)`; a timeout answers `slow`.
- **The band** (`TellCustomerSheet.tsx`, kernel `tellProblem`): a send that answers no draws a `role="alert"` band in the sheet — the reason and the move — and keeps the typed words; the primary reads **Try again**. The same band covers a booking's failed text (decision 4). Mid-send, the X answers *Still sending. One moment.* instead of refusing in silence.
- **Send the link again** (decision 1): the edge's first-send refusal on a sent row answers `code: 'already_sent'` with the row's `sent_at`; the band says when it went and the primary flips to the page's resend (`mode: 'resend'`, fresh link, the old one dead — the band says so). Other non-draft statuses answer `code: 'not_draft'` with the status, worded by `canResendEstimateLink` + `estimateLinkResendBlockMessage`. **Redeploy `send-estimate-to-customer`.**
- **Receipt honesty**: `emailed: false` reads *The email to … did not go. The link was made and copied.* instead of claiming it was emailed; a text that failed after the email leaves its reason on the receipt (`tell-receipt-note`) instead of a toast alone.
- **Guide**: *send an estimate and turn it into a job* § 2 gains the band and Send the link again.
- **Tests**: the kernel's `tellProblem` describe (already-went with and without the time, refused, slow, the new words plain); the sheet's band on a refused email and a failed booking text, the already-went flip that resends and texts the fresh link, the honest receipt lines, the mid-send X.

**Not in this PR**: the What went card on the estimate (PR 2) and the Went out today lane on Messages (PR 3).
