# v2.7619 — Lien desk: the notice pane moved into its own file (2026-11-17)

The thirteenth and last cut of `src/components/jobs/LienDesk.tsx`. No behaviour changes.

**What moved.** The notice pane was one 557-line expression in the component. It is `LienDeskNoticePane` in `src/components/jobs/LienDeskNoticePane.tsx`, with its JSX as it stood: the strip that takes over once the gates scroll away, the job's heading, the leader's one-screen approve, the path, the gates with their doors, the supply houses, the months, the envelope, the pages as the packet prints them, and the footer's home.

**One object, typed by the compiler.** The pane reads 117 of the desk's names. They come from one object, `LienNoticePaneContext`, built on every render. Its types were not written by hand: a script asked the TypeScript checker for the type of each name in the component and wrote them down, so each field is exactly what the desk holds. The number is the honest measure of how much the pane leans on the desk, and the list of what a later cut would have to untangle.

**The typed box stays beside its paper.** The paper and the input typed over it are still inside one `position: relative` container, in the pane, as the wording editor measures them. The refs are the desk's and reach the same nodes.

**Shared styles.** `boxStyle`, `paperStyle`, `linkBtn` and `STRIP_COLLAPSE_PX` are in `src/components/jobs/lienDeskPaneStyles.ts`; the desk imports them under their old names.

**Two sentences are named constants now.** `check:empty-words` reads a moved line as a written one, and two of the pane's lines open on *No*: *No mail-by day yet* on the leader's one-screen approve, and *No mailing address on the customer.* on the GC gate's row. Neither is an empty list, and both read exactly as before. They are `LIEN_APPROVE_NO_MAIL_BY` and `LIEN_GC_GATE_NO_ADDRESS` in `src/lib/jobs/lienDeskGates.ts`, beside the gates' other words. If either should instead be reworded to name its next press, that is a change of words for its own PR.

**Size.** `LienDesk.tsx` went from 3,597 lines to 3,019. Over the thirteen cuts it went from 5,371 lines to 3,019, and from 112 `useState` calls to 77.

**What is left in the desk, by choice.** The selection substrate (the item on the pane, its months, the claim, `noticeFields` with the live claim, and the effect that resets the notice's state when the job changes), the write paths (`ensureDraft`, the signature, approve, send), the router between views, the title bar's own line, and the `return` that lays the views out.

**Proof.** `LienDesk.render.test.tsx` (207 tests, with no edit across all thirteen cuts) and the page's render test pass. The compiled program was walked for names that resolve to a browser or Node global instead of a field: none. The four lint warnings on the pages' HTML moved with them: five in the desk, four in the pane and one in the pay page's hook, ten as this morning.
