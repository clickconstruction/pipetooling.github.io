# v2.6563 — Needs a signature on a stage checklist question: the author's toggle, the signature sheet with the gravity words, the locked row and Take it back (punch list #237, PR 2) (2026-11-05)

Punch list #237's second PR (`to-dos/stage-checklist-document/`, *The signature is the app's own* and *The author decides*). PR 1 (v2.6328) and PR 1b (v2.6347) put the checks on the job, the stamp on every row and the read-out on Documents. The owner, 2026-11-01: "we force a user to sign when they complete certain checklist items so they understand the severity and gravity. And this should be a toggle for the person making the checklist." This PR is that toggle and that signature, built to the app's own rule for one (`lienNoticeSignature.ts`, v2.5077): the record is the signature, not the mark.

## What changed

- **The toggle.** *Needs a signature* beside *Asks for a photo* on every add box: the sheet's and the Documents tab's `AddCheckBox` (with the guide's rule under the tick, *Use it where a wrong answer costs a wall or an inspection*), and each question's row on Settings → Jobs & billing → Stage checklists, whose kind summary now counts *1 with a signature*. `needs_signature` on `stage_checklist_templates`; `promote_stage_checklist_item` copies it with the photo ask.
- **The signature sheet** (`ChecklistSignatureSheet.tsx`, over `SignatureTypeOrDrawInput`): Yes, No or Not here on a signature item saves nothing and opens the sheet on top: the gravity words (*You are signing **Yes** to “…”. It goes on this job's Trim Set record with your name and the time. The office, the GC and an inspector can read it.*), the printed name from the profile, Type or Draw, a note (a signed No needs one, the walks' rule), the agreed line *I did this check myself and the answer is true*, and **Sign and save**. The write is one update (`signAnswer`): the pick, the note, the stamp and the six signature columns on `stage_checklist_answers` (`signed_at`, `signed_by`, `signer_printed_name`, `signer_signature_mode`, `signer_signature_storage_path`, `signed_answer_hash`), the hash binding the question's words, the pick and the note as given (`checklistAnswerHash`, the lien notice's own hash), so a later change voids it (`signatureStands`). A drawn mark's PNG is trimmed to its strokes and kept in the `stage-checklist-photos` bucket under `<job_id>/sig/`, read by the job's own gate and never touched by the photo-save sweep.
- **Not answered until signed; Not here signs too.** `questionWaitsOn` gains `'signature'` between the pick and the photo, so the sheet, the strip, the Documents read-out and the Pipeline's mark all hold the stage until the signature stands. The board's read (`useStageChecklistStates`) carries the signature and the void so its marks agree.
- **The lock.** A signed row's open view (`LockedRow`) has no presses: the pick as a statement, the mark (`SignatureMark`: the ink for a drawn one, the name in the cursive face for a typed one), *Signed by Luis Ortega · Nov 1 · 9:14 am*, the note, the photo it still owes, and **Take it back**. The database holds the same rule (`stage_checklist_answers_signed_row_locks`): a signature is whole and the signer's own (`signed_by = auth.uid()`), and a signed row's pick, note, stamp and signature never change.
- **Take it back.** Asks why (3 to 200 characters), voids the row (`voided_at`, `voided_by`, `voided_by_name`, `void_why`) and leaves it: the question waits on its pick again, the next press writes a new live row (`UNIQUE (fixture_id, question_id)` became the partial index `stage_checklist_answers_live_idx`), and the voided answer reads under the question on the sheet and on Documents as *Taken back · Was Yes, signed by Luis Ortega · Nov 1 · 9:14 am. Taken back by Pat Sample · Nov 5 · 10:20 am · “wrong unit”.* with its mark kept. Anyone who may answer may take back (the walk let the technician). Every reader takes the live row (`liveAnswersById`).
- **The outbox ride.** The press is a new outbox kind, `checklist-sign`: the pick, the signature and the mark as a data URL in the payload, landed through the sheet's own write under the table's policies (`landChecklistSign`), with the press's time as the signed time; a resend finds the row signed with the same hash by the same signer and counts once. The job's readers hear the landing and read again. The outbox guide's *what never queues* now says a customer's or the leader's signature, since the crew's own does queue.
- **The read-out.** A signature row wears *needs a signature* (amber) while open, *signed* (green) with the mark once signed; a pick without its signature reads *Yes, not signed yet*.
- The guide *sign a checklist answer*; a line in *fill a stage checklist on the phone* and *add a check to a job's stage checklist*; `docs/migrations/20261106040000_stage_checklist_signature.md`.

## Decisions

- **One write, pick and signature together.** The mock-up said a press on a signature item opens the sheet "instead of saving at once", so no row ever holds a pick waiting on its signature from this sheet; a row that does (a report photo's answer, a row older than the column) reads *not signed yet* and the open row offers the presses again.
- **The hash binds words, pick and note.** The lien notice hashes the fields as drafted; here the fields are the question's words, the pick and the note. The time is the row's own, not hashed. A promote keeps the words, so a signed answer that moves to the company's row keeps its signature.
- **The void is a column set, not a second table.** The card asked for "a new answer row with a reason; the voided signature stays". A partial unique index on the live rows gives that without a history table, and the lock trigger freezes a voided row the way it freezes a signed one.
- **The mark lives in the checklist bucket.** Under `sig/`, beside the job's photos, so the bucket's job-folder policy reads it and no new bucket or policy is needed; the sweep reads `photo_storage_path` alone. PR 3's PDF will draw it from there.
- **The job label is *this job*.** The gravity words take a `jobLabel`; the openers pass none yet, so the words read *It goes on this job's Trim Set record*. PR 3's record id is the natural label.
