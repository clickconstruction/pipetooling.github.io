# v2.4536 — GC statement emails: the paid-by line follows the new payment rule (2026-10-05)

Edge function `gc-statement-email-dispatch`; no migration, no client change. v2.4534 left one reader on oldest-first alone: the *paid by* line under each bill in a scheduled GC statement, because the rows of `get_gc_statement_email_payload` carry no job total. The owner asked for it to follow.

## What changed

- **The dispatcher reads the totals itself.** After the RPC, [`index.ts`](../../supabase/functions/gc-statement-email-dispatch/index.ts) selects `id, revenue` from `jobs_ledger` for `payloadJobIds(payload)` and `attachJobTotals` sets each row's `job_total` ([`render.ts`](../../supabase/functions/gc-statement-email-dispatch/render.ts)). No change to the RPC: its `job_bills` already list every billed and paid bill of the job, which is what the rule needs beside the total.
- **`rowPaidBy`** passes `total: r.job_total` to `billPaidByWords`, so unlinked money pays the part of the job on no bill before a bill (`_shared/paymentAttribution.ts`).
- **A failed read** of the totals leaves the rows as they came; the statement still goes, worded by the bills alone.
- The single-GC by-property layout was never affected: it lists only a bill's linked payments.

## Tests

`gcStatementEmailParity.test.ts` (+2, and job 4's fixture carries its $1,800 total on both lanes): the job ids named once, totals set and a missing one left alone, and the line reading *nothing applied yet* with the total where it read *paid in full by #3001* without it.
