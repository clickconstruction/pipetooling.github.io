# v2.7991 — HR files: the standing on the fact strip and the Timeline (2026-11-19)

Punch list #315, upgrade 4b. No migration, no seat change. The owner's go for the whole train, 2026-11-11; Grace's word to resume, 2026-11-19.

## What was wrong

`person_engagements` has held a person's standing over time since v2.7111: employee or subcontractor, a stretch at a time, each with where the fact comes from. No screen read it. The file still said what a person is today through the roster row, and nothing of what they were in a past month.

## What changed

- **The fact strip reads the standing.** Its last press wears `standingWords`: *Subcontractor · since Mar 3, 2025 · still engaged* while a stretch runs, the newest closed stretch when none does, and *Standing…* when nothing is on file. A dev sees it. The press opens **Standing…**.
- **Standing…** (`src/components/people/personFile/PersonStandingWindow.tsx`).
  - What the person is now, then the stretches on file, newest first, each with where its fact comes from, its reference and its note.
  - *Record a stretch…*: employee or subcontractor, the first day, a last day when it has ended, the source, which paper or record, a note.
  - *Change to employee…* or *Change to subcontractor…*: from a day. The running stretch ends the day before.
  - *End the stretch…*: the last day.
  - *Take it off…*: a reason, required. The stretch leaves what stands and stays on the table with its reason.
  - Each is one write of `dev_engagement_write` through `src/lib/people/personEngagementsIo.ts`. A refusal keeps the window open and shows the door's words. An overlap is said before the door is asked (`engagementDraftProblem`).
- **The Timeline draws each change**, under Employment (`engagementLines` in `supabase/functions/_shared/personTimeline.ts`).
  - A stretch draws on its first day: *Subcontractor from this day.* The notes say where the fact comes from.
  - A change draws one line that says both sides: *Employee from this day. Subcontractor until the day before.*
  - A closed stretch draws its last day only when nothing follows it the next morning.
  - A stretch that starts ahead of today is drawn as planned. A withdrawn stretch draws nothing.
- The read is a source of its own and fail-soft (`loadPersonEngagements`). A refused read takes only the standing out, and the window says why.
- The help guide `keep-private-employee-files` has the steps.

## Is this the best we can do?

The card drew the standing as one more fact chip. A chip narrows the Timeline to what it counts, and the standing counts nothing, so it is a press of its own beside the Matter press, which already opens a view.

The card did not say what the Timeline draws when one stretch ends and the next begins the morning after. Two lines on two days read as a gap. One line that says both sides reads as the change it is.
