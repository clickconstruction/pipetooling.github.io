# v2.8240 — Send a paper: Send email waits until the address is checked (2026-11-21)

Found by the audit of punch list #403 (finding 5). Asked for by Taunya on 2026-11-21. One window and its kernel. No migration and no edge function.

## What was wrong

Documents → Agreements → **Send a paper** → **Someone not in any list** opens the Send for signature window with an empty address. The window looks the typed address up, to say whose it already is:

- an address that is someone on the roster is refused, since their papers are filed under their own name;
- an address the app already keeps is named, so the paper goes to that record and nobody is added twice.

The look-up ran 350 ms after the typing rested, then made two reads. **Send email** did not wait for it. A press that came first read *nobody holds this address* and sent. A look-up that failed read the same way.

So a sub's address typed there, with a quick press, filed the paper under the sub's contact as an outside party's paper. It was off People → Contracts and carried no **Add to a list**. The audit saw the note still missing 2.5 s after typing on a loaded machine.

## What changed

**Send email** now needs the look-up's answer for the address in the box before anything is written.

- A press that comes before the answer asks for it itself, and waits. The timer that was running does not ask a second time.
- Someone on the roster is refused, as before.
- Someone the app keeps, whom the window had not yet named, is named in the refusal's place with *Press Send email again to send it to them.* The press holds once, so the office reads who the paper goes to. The next press sends.
- Nobody holds the address: the press sends, as before.
- A look-up that fails sends nothing. The window says *The app could not check whose address this is, so nothing was sent.* with the reason. The next press asks again.

The rule is `addressLookUpVerdict` in `src/lib/documents/paperPartyPick.ts`, pure. The window (`ContractSendWindow.tsx`) keeps the address its last answer was for, and asks when the box holds another.

Nothing changes for a person picked from the roster, a company picked from the list, or a paper sent again: those look no address up.

## Tests

- `src/lib/documents/paperPartyPick.test.ts`: the verdict for nobody, someone on the roster, someone the app keeps before and after the window named them, and the words held to plain words.
- `src/components/contracts/ContractSendWindow.render.test.tsx`: a press that beats the look-up for someone on the roster, for someone the app keeps, and for nobody, and a look-up that fails. All four failed on the window as it was.

## Docs

The guide *find an agreement we sent or signed* says **Send email** waits for the check, and what the window says when it finds someone.
