# v2.2377 — Pay speeds: drift dumbbell chart + declutter (2026-08-26)

Owner session on the Pay speeds breakdown (Jobs → Pipeline → Forecast → See
the breakdown): the modal carried decoration that repeated its own numbers,
and its two chart variants (every-customer-a-dot / count-by-speed-bucket)
described the *distribution* of speeds without answering an actionable
question. Two workstreams, one PR:

## The drift chart (replaces both chart variants)

**"Above or below their average"** — a dumbbell chart on a shared days axis
(capped at 60d; beyond-cap rows keep a "→ Nd" label). Per customer with
open billed money: hollow dot = their own 12-month median, filled dot =
where they are now, connector = the drift, dashed line = the company
median (fixed for every row, so vs-company is left/right of the line).
Kernel: `src/lib/jobs/paySpeedDrift.ts` (`buildPayDrift`, unit-tested):

- **"Now" reads two sources, worst wins**: *live* = the longest-waiting
  open bill once it has waited PAST the baseline (a young bill can't prove
  anyone is fast, so sub-baseline waits say nothing); *recent* = median of
  the last 3 completed payments vs their own median — the only source that
  can show green (below average).
- **Thin history** (< `PAY_SPEED_MIN_SAMPLES`) baselines on the company
  median, only moves via live waits, and renders as a hollow red dot with
  no own-average dot.
- Rows sort worst drift first; customers at their baseline collapse into
  one "N more customers are on their usual pace" line.
- The chart needs the company-timezone today: `PaySpeedsBreakdownModal`
  gained a required `todayYmd` prop (passed from
  `BilledPaymentForecastModal`, same clock as the forecast).
- `bucketPaySpeeds` + `DotChart`/`BucketChart` + the variant pills were
  deleted.

## Declutter (owner-requested cuts, no data loss)

- **One customer list**: the separate "Thin history" tier merged into "By
  customer — slowest first" (muted rows, "—" median, subtitle explains).
- **Per-row median bars removed** (duplicated the Median column);
  `maxDays` dropped from `buildPaySpeedsBreakdown`'s return shape.
- **Data health strip is one line**: meter bar removed, zero counts hide
  themselves.
- **Footer legend paragraph removed** — the math lives in hover titles.
