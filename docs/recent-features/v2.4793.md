# v2.4793 — Lien desk: press a stop on the timeline to see what it sends (2026-10-07)

**The ask (the owner, 2026-10-07, a screenshot of job 891's timeline):** "I would like to be able to click on the titles of § 53.056 · JUL, § 53.056 · AUG, § 53.057 RETAIN, § 53.052 AFFIDA, § 53.055 SERVE, § 53.158 SUIT, LAST WORK etc. and have a preview of what would be sent come up in a modal." Plan and mock-up https://claude.ai/artifact/MJnFkdKZEAhiM9RjL628M3 (the strip with the titles as doors, the window on a notice stop, the window on the suit), the *is this the best we can do?* pass, "build it".

**The gap.** The strip said when each stop is and never what it sends; the paper for most stops already existed in the app, each on its own pane.

## What shipped

- **The kernel** ([`lienStopPaper.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienStopPaper.ts)): `lienStopPaperKind` (which paper a stop sends; `none` for last work, the hold and the suit), `lienStopRuleCite` (the rules-guide row each stop points at: last work → § 53.003, the notice → § 53.056, the affidavit and serve → § 53.052, the release → § 53.152, the demand → § 38.001), `lienStopWindowWords` (the eyebrow, the title, the standing line, and the what / who / after words for a stop with no paper). A notice stop whose month the draft carries is titled by every month on it — *The July and August 2026 notice* — and its line says the months go on one notice with July's day.
- **The pages** ([`lienStopPaperPages.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienStopPaperPages.ts)): `affidavitStopPages` and `retainageStopPages` build the affidavit and counsel's cover letter + the § 53.057 notice from the desk's data exactly as the Affidavits and Retainage panes do.
- **The door** ([`LienTimelineStrip`](../../src/components/jobs/LienTimelineStrip.tsx)): `onOpenStep` — with it every stop title in the row and list layouts is a `.lienStopDoor` button (the label's own type, a dotted underline, blue on hover); without it the titles stay plain, so the nine hosts of the strip are untouched. The last-work and set-the-date doors stay where they were.
- **The window** ([`LienStopPaperWindow`](../../src/components/jobs/LienStopPaperWindow.tsx)): built like the Do now paper layer. Left: the pages as the packet prints them, light in both themes; or, for a done stop, the record (*Mailed Oct 3*) with the stored document's link; or the no-paper words. Right: This stop (the day, days left, the first-day line, the standing), In the envelope, Before it can go (the gates not clear), The rule (opens `LienRulesModal` at the cite, over the window), and the stop's one act. ‹ › and the arrow keys walk the stops; Esc closes this alone (capture, so the desk never sees the key; the rules window keeps the keys while open).
- **The desk's notice pane** (`LienDeskModal`): `stopOpen` (reset when the job changes), the strip's `onOpenStep`, and `stopPaperFor`: the notice's pages are the pane's own cover, conditional release, notice and pay page when the stop's month is on the draft (the act scrolls the pane to the notice page); a mailed month reads the filing (`notice_53_056` covering the month) as the record; a closed month says the window closed; a month not on the draft says to tick it under Months on this job. Retainage and the affidavit build their pages, or read their filing once mailed or filed, with the act opening their view; serve reads the affidavit's `served_at`. The release reads its filing or says where it is made.
- **The Affidavit pane** (`LienDeskAffidavitPane`, the owner's second ask the same day): the same door and window from the affidavit's side — its own affidavit page with the gates not clear under Before it can go and *Open it in the Lien window ›*; serve with the copy's envelope; the retainage notice as its pane prints it; a notice stop points at Notices (*Open it on Notices ›*), where its pages are.
- **The job's Lien window** (`LienInstrumentsModal`): the door on the header's strip; a stop that went out shows the paper as it went out; an open stop's act opens the tab here that prints it (*Open the Notice tab ›*, *Open the Affidavit tab ›*, *Open the Release tab ›*, the demand letter's tab), retainage pointing at the Lien desk.
- **As it went out** (`filingSnapshotPage`): a filing stores the fields it printed with and the sends it recorded, so a done stop's page is rebuilt from the record on every host — the Lien window's *View* on a recorded filing does the same — with the stored document's link beside it.
- Guides *send lien notices from the Lien desk* and *file a lien and never miss its deadlines*; the Lien desk map; GLOSSARY; `.lienStopDoor` in `index.css`.

## Decisions

- The desk's pane already shows the notice under the strip; the window earns its place there by holding the stop's facts, its blanks and ‹ › across every stop in one place, and by being the only place the affidavit and the retainage notice can be read from the notice pane.
- A stop that already went out shows the paper as it went out, from the filing's own snapshot, and its stored document — never a rebuild from today's job.
- Stops with no paper of ours open the same window with words, since *what happens here* is the question at every stop.
- Not in this PR: counsel's portal and the Legal desk host the same strip without the prop; they can take it in the firm's voice.
