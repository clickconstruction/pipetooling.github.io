# v2.8058 — Job Budget: the card says dollars over or under, not points (2026-11-20)

Punch list #406, its last piece. Client only. No migration and no edge function deploy.

## Why

The owner's walk of 2026-11-20 took the word *points* off the Job pace card, the Projects board and the Costs tab (v2.8048). One screen still said it: the sentence on the Job Budget card, *labor is 9 points ahead of progress*. A point is a percentage point of a budget spent over the percent of work done. Nobody in the office says it. The owner's word on the last place: "Yes, do it".

## What landed

- **`budgetWhySentence`** in the new `src/lib/jobs/jobBudgetWhy.ts`, pure and tested. It says the same reading as money: the dollars a component has spent over or under what the work done calls for.
  - Before: *Materials are $6,700 over the estimate with 23 % of the work left, labor is 9 points ahead of progress, and subs are 20 points under.*
  - After: *Materials are $6,700 over the estimate with 23 % of the work left, labor is $4,233 over for the work done, and subs are $1,305 under for the work done.*
- **The dollars** are the lead in percentage points, taken of that component's own budget. For labor at 86% spent and 77% done on a $44,840 budget, that is $4,233.
- **When a component is named is not changed.** Past its whole estimate, or five percentage points or more either side of the work done.
- `JobBudgetCard.tsx` reads the new function.

## Why a new file

`src/lib/jobs/jobBudget.ts` is one of the kernels copied to the edge functions (`supabase/functions/_shared/gcKernels`). A word changed there changes the copy, and every function that bundles the copy reads as behind until it is redeployed. The sentence is drawn on one client screen and on no function. So the new words live in a client-only file beside the kernel, and the kernel is left byte for byte as it was.

The old `budgetWhyWords` therefore stands in `jobBudget.ts`, unused, with its test. It goes when the kernel next changes for a reason of its own.

## Checked

- `jobBudgetWhy.test.ts`: the three-part sentence in dollars, the five-point threshold either side, and the two quiet sentences.
- `JobBudgetCard.render.test.tsx`: the card prints the new sentence.
- `npm run gen:edge-kernels -- --check`: the copy matches `src`, so no function drifts.
