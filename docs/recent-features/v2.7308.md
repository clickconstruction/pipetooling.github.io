# v2.7308 — The Lien desk's approval presses say whose okay it is (2026-11-15)

Client, plus one line of the dev seat's words. The owner, reading a drafted affidavit on the Lien desk under his dev sign-in on 2026-11-15, saw *The leader said to file it ▸* beside *Approve ▸* and could not tell them apart. The green press is the leader's own okay (`approval_mode = leader`); the amber one is the office writing down that the leader already said so, with a note (`approval_mode = word`). The dev role is both the leader and the office, so it alone sees the pair; a master sees only the green press and an assistant only the amber one beside *Send for approval ▸*. The grey line above them described the standing send rule, which belongs to the press the dev does not see.
