# v2.8574 — Punch list on a phone: four small presses take a thumb (2026-11-25)

Punch list #444, from the first walk of the page on the live site on 2026-11-25. No migration, no edge function.

**Seen.** On `/punch-list` at 390 px, **Hide the queue** stood 24 px high, **Read GitHub now** 29, **Hide the burndown** 28, and the link *How fast a change ships* 36. The app's rule for a press on a phone is 44 px (#414).

**The cause.** Neither `PunchListQueue.tsx` nor `PunchListBurndown.tsx` asked whether the screen is a phone, so each press kept its desk size. The three sibling pieces of the page that take a thumb hard-code 44.

**The mend.** Both components read `useIsMobile()`.

- The three buttons take `minHeight: PHONE_PRESS_MIN_HEIGHT` on a phone, folded and open. A button centres its own words, so nothing else changes.
- The link sits in a line of words. On a phone it is an `inline-flex` box with `thumbInLine(20)`: 44 px tall, with the margin that gives the line back what the link grew by, so the line stays as tall as it was drawn. 20 is the line, 0.85 rem words at the page's 1.5.

At a desk no style is added.

## What did not change

- What each press does, and the words on it.
- The page's width at 390 px. A fold's row was already allowed to wrap.

## Tests

- `PunchListQueue.render.test.tsx`: on a phone, as a dev, the fold, **Read GitHub now** and the door each stand 44 px, the door as an in-line box with equal negative margins, and the fold keeps its height once folded. At a desk the three carry no height.

Both files put `useIsMobile` behind a switch the cases set, so every older case still draws at a desk.
