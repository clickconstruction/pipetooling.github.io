# v2.4441 — Lien desk on a phone: the tab row shows there is more to the right (2026-10-02)

No migration, no edge function. Noted and left open in [v2.4397](v2.4397.md) and [v2.4398](v2.4398.md); Robert, 2026-10-02: "help me fix the three things you noticed but left alone". This is the first.

## What was wrong

The desk's five tabs (Calendar, Notices, Affidavits, Retainage, Timeline) are about 440 px of labels. Since v2.4311 the row scrolls sideways on a phone instead of pushing the title bar off the screen, but at 390 px it simply stopped at *Retaina*: nothing said Timeline existed. A door that opens the desk on Timeline (`?kind=timeline`, a Timeline book row) left the picked tab out of view.

## What changed

- **[`scrollEdges.ts`](../../src/lib/scrollEdges.ts)** (new, kernel-tested): `scrollEdges` says whether a sideways scroller has content cut off at its left and its right end (4 px of slack for rounding); `scrollEdgeMask` is the CSS mask that fades the cut ends, or null when both are in view.
- **[`useScrollEdgeFade`](../../src/hooks/useScrollEdgeFade.ts)** (new): `ref`, `onScroll` and `style` for the scroller. It reads after every render and on resize, and the same answer is no update.
- **[`LienDeskModal`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskModal.tsx)**: the tab row takes the hook, so its cut end fades (the border fades with it, which is the cue), and an effect on `kind` and `open` brings the picked tab into view (`scrollIntoView`, `inline: 'nearest'`).
- **[`LienInstrumentsModal`](../../src/components/jobs/LienInstrumentsModal.tsx)**: the papers bar's own right-edge fade (v2.4398) is now this hook, so it fades its left end too once scrolled.

The effect acts only when the picked tab or the row's own width changes (a count that loads widens a label), so it never pulls the row back from under a finger.

## Verified

Dev server at 390 × 844, production data, read only. The row is 438 px in a 320 px box. On opening: the right end fades, the left does not. Scrolled part way: both fade. Scrolled to the end: only the left fades. Pressing **Timeline** scrolls the row to its end (144, after the tab's count loads and widens it to 464) with the tab whole; pressing **Calendar** brings it back to 0. A row scrolled by hand to 70 stays at 70. At 1280 × 800 the row fits (438 in 438) and carries no fade.

The hidden preview pane delivers no scroll or resize events, so those two paths were driven by dispatching the event by hand; on a visible page the browser sends them.
