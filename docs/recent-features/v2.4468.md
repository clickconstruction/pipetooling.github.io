# v2.4468 — Lien desk: a notice sent or noted in the evening keeps its day (2026-10-03)

One of the instant-day fixes that follow [v2.4452](v2.4452.md); [v2.4466](v2.4466.md) left the legal packet's lien answers for this one, so the desk and the packet agree. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

The Lien desk and the readers around it cut days out of `timestamptz` columns by their first ten (or seven) characters, so anything stamped after 7 pm CDT (6 pm CST) read as the next day:

- **The desk's rows and footers** ([`LienDeskModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskModal.tsx), [`LienDeskRetainagePane.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskRetainagePane.tsx), [`LienDeskAffidavitPane.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskAffidavitPane.tsx)): *awaiting approval · Sep 24* and *sent Sep 24* on a row, *Waiting on the leader since*, *Approved*, *Sent* and *Filed*, from `job_lien_desk_items.submitted_at`, `approved_at` and `sent_at`.
- **How long Sent keeps an item** ([`lienDesk.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDesk.ts), [`lienDeskRetainage.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDeskRetainage.ts), [`lienDeskAffidavits.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDeskAffidavits.ts)): `LIEN_DESK_SENT_DAYS` counted from `sent_at`'s UTC day, so an evening send stayed a day longer.
- **Letter two** ([`lienLetterTwo.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienLetterTwo.ts)): the day count from the first packet (due from day 10, overdue from day 14, on the desk, the Dashboard and GC on notice), *letter two sent*, *GC authorized direct pay* (the first packet's `gcAuthorizedDirectPay.at`) and *owner called* (`ownerCall.at`). The modal sliced the same okay and the *after the Sep 2 packet* heading itself.
- **The owner's call and the claim correction**: `ownerCallWords` ([`lienOwnerCall.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienOwnerCall.ts)), `correctionSetWords` ([`lienClaimCorrection.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienClaimCorrection.ts), `job_lien_claim_corrections.set_at`) and the modal's *Carrying Taunya's correction from Sep 10*.
- **The call sheet and Share**: *mailed* on the call sheet ([`lienCallerMatch.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienCallerMatch.ts)) and *waiting since* in the Share message and its team email ([`lienDeskShare.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDeskShare.ts)).
- **Months on this job and the affidavit's months**: *noted* on a closed window ([`LienDeskMonths.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskMonths.tsx), `windowClosed.at`), *notice sent* and *skipped* ([`affidavitMonths.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/affidavitMonths.ts)), and a paper with no send day, dated from its row's `job_lien_filings.created_at` ([`lienMonthGrid.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienMonthGrid.ts)). That day decides *as information*, so a notice recorded on the evening its window closed read as mailed after it.
- **The timeline** ([`lienTimelineDesk.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienTimelineDesk.ts)): the desk's months carried the history's instants into the kernel; the Lien window's sent months and a release of record fell back to `created_at`; and a job with no clock hours took its creation month from `created_at`'s first seven characters. `list_lien_notice_months` reads that month in Chicago (`lien_fallback_month`), so a job made on the evening of a month's last day was a month late on the strip.
- **The Pipeline's lien chip** ([`lienPayRunway.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienPayRunway.ts)): the same creation basis; and [`useBilledLienClocks`](../../src/hooks/useBilledLienClocks.ts) dated an affidavit or a release with no `filed_at` by `created_at`'s UTC day.
- **A filing's paper** ([`LienFilingTabs.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienFilingTabs.tsx)): the date on its reference line.
- **The legal packet** ([`legalLienPaper.ts`](../../src/lib/legal/legalLienPaper.ts)): an envelope's *went out* day from `created_at` (which also decides *as information* there), and the three answers under a notice: the owner's call, letter two's *sent* and the GC's written okay.

## What changed

Each instant reads `calendarYmdInAppTzFromIso(iso)` from [`dateUtils.ts`](../../src/utils/dateUtils.ts) (`APP_CALENDAR_TZ`). Every one is a `timestamptz` or a `new Date().toISOString()` stamp in the item's `fields`. The `date` columns keep their slice: a filing's `filed_at` and `served_at`, a send's `sent_on` (the day typed, or the run's day), `hold_until`, `last_work_date` and a demand letter's `sent_at`.

The timeline's adapters read each instant's day where the column's type is known, because the Lien window's sent month and a release can be a `filed_at` date or a `created_at` instant. The kernel's `LienTimelineMonth.at` and `releasedAt` are now days, and say so. `legalLienPaper.ts`'s `ymdOf` now says it is for date columns.

Nothing on the server reads these days: `send-lien-desk-summary` renders the days the browser computed, and `legal-portal` hands the firm's page the raw items, which it reads with the same kernels.

## Not changed

- The timeline's demand-letter step slices `sentAt`, a `job_demand_letters.sent_at` `date`; no caller passes its `paidAt`.
- Comparisons that only order rows (the newest release, `filed_at ?? created_at`) are left.

- `lienLetterTwo.test.ts` (+2): a packet sent at 7:30 pm CDT on Sep 5 is day 10 on Sep 15; the GC's okay, the owner's call and a sent letter two read their Central day.
- `lienOwnerCall.test.ts`, `lienClaimCorrection.test.ts`, `lienCallerMatch.test.ts`, `lienDeskShare.test.ts`, `affidavitMonths.test.ts` (+1 each): the call, the correction, *mailed*, *waiting since*, *notice sent* and *skipped*.
- `lienMonthGrid.test.ts` (+1) and `legalLienPaper.test.ts` (+2): a notice recorded at 7:30 pm CDT on Sep 15, July's last day, names July and is not information; at noon on Sep 16 it is. The packet's three answers read the Central day.
- `lienTimelineDesk.test.ts` (+3) and `lienPayRunway.test.ts` (+1): a job with no hours made at 7:30 pm CDT on Sep 30 is a September job (notice due Dec 15); one made at 7:30 pm CDT on Jul 31 owes its residential lien by Oct 15, not Nov 16. A notice and a release with no filed day, and the desk's sent and noted stamps, read their Central day.
- `useBilledLienClocks.render.test.tsx` (+1) and `LienDeskModal.render.test.tsx` (+2): the clocks, the sent row and footer (*Sent September 2, 2026*, *Day 12*, *GC authorized direct pay: yes · Sep 10*) and the carried correction.

All 20 fail on main; the two render cases fail with main's modal alone.
