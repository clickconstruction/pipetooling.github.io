# v2.6843 — Permits: a second look at what someone else passed (punch list #302) (2026-11-10)

The owner: *is there a way for a user to make another pass on the items another user may have chosen pass on?* Before this, Closed → Passed listed them and **Put back** sent one to the skim for everyone, one row at a time, with no sign on the card that someone had said no.

## What changed

- **`lib/research/permitSecondLook.ts`** (new, pure): `waitsOnSecondLook(row, me)` — passed (not *Not pursuing*, no plumbing permit), by someone else, and not already passed twice; `secondLookDeck` / `secondLookCount` in the skim's own order (`permitSkim.bestFirst`, now exported); `passedByWords` (*Passed by Sam, yesterday: too far*); `secondLookVerdict` — Keep writes `look` with *Kept on a second look, after Sam passed.*, Pass writes `not_for_us` with reason code `sl_<reason>` or `sl_none`. The `sl_` code is what takes the permit off every second look: one second look per permit.
- **`PermitSkimDeck`** takes `mode="second"`: the same card, answers, swipe, keys, held write and Undo, an amber line under the pictures with who passed, and its own bar and caught-up words. In skim mode the caught-up card offers *Second look · N someone else passed*.
- **`ProspectsResearchTab`**: a **Second look N** chip on the strip after Closed, the `second` view, and on a phone the same bare layout as Skim. `stripCounts` reads the deck's `mode`: a held keep on a second look leaves Closed for Kept, a held pass moves nothing.
- **The count**: `funnelLines` words an `sl_` code (*too far, on a second look*, *passed twice, no reason given*).
- No migration: `permit_verdicts.reason_code` is free text and the newest verdict wins.
