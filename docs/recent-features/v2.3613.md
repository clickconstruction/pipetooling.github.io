# v2.3613 — Supervision, PR 3: My crew on the Dashboard — reports owed, and your crew's hours read-only (2026-09-19)

PR 3 of five in [`to-dos/supervision/README.md`](../../to-dos/supervision/README.md) (PR 1: v2.3611 · PR 2: v2.3612). One migration (a function).

## The change

- **RPC** `get_supervised_days_payload(p_from date, p_to date) → jsonb` ([doc](../migrations/20260919155432_get_supervised_days_payload.md)): for the caller, `supervisor` (a master, or a helper / sub with `needs_supervision` off), `job_days` (every job and day in the range where they were listed on a block or clocked in, with the ledger fields, `report_count` / `my_report_count` on the company calendar, and `crew` — everyone else listed or clocked there), `sessions` (the crew's clock sessions on those job-days, pending in, rejected and revoked out, never the caller's own, no wages). SECURITY DEFINER: `clock_sessions` RLS admits a sub or helper to their own rows only, and this is the one narrow window the supervision rule opens. Everyone else gets `supervisor: false` and empty lists.
- **Kernel** [`src/lib/people/supervisedDays.ts`](../../src/lib/people/supervisedDays.ts) (6 tests): `buildSupervisedView` → reports owed (supervised job-days ≤ today with no report, newest first, today's marked), reports filed, the crew's hours by person and day (`sessionHours` counts an open session to now), `reportsSummary`.
- **The section** [`DashboardSupervisorSection.tsx`](../../src/components/dashboard/DashboardSupervisorSection.tsx), lazy, right after My Team, for masters, helpers and subs; self-gates on the payload (nothing for a non-supervisor, nothing for an empty current week). Two cards: *Reports owed* with **Write it** (the Dashboard's `AdditionalReportModal` door, `setLeaveReportJob`) and *My crew's hours* (per person: total, then day · job · hours · *in* / ✓), read-only, "approval stays with the office". Week pager.
- Guide [`supervise-a-crew.md`](../../src/content/help/supervise-a-crew.md) (Field Work); `say-who-can-run-a-job.md` updated. `DASHBOARD_SECTIONS_ARCHITECTURE.md` (row 17b, the matrix, a dossier), `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` §8, `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`, `GLOSSARY.md`.
