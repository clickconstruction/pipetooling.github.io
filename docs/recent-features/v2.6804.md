# v2.6804 — The dev seat loads submittals and says where they stand: get_submittals, plan_submittal_rows, plan_submittal_log (2026-11-09)

The owner, 2026-11-09: *"Today I want to look at the submittals tab in bids, and look at if we measure if materials are delivered to site and I want to add dev mcp verbs to manage submittals so that if a person was talking to an agent like yourself the agent could load in submittals entries and let others know their status."* The look came first (below); the verbs are what she approved from it. No migration.

## What the look found

- **The tab** writes its tables directly under RLS (`is_pricing_sharer() AND can_access_bid_for_pricing`); there is no RPC. Four vocabularies sit on it: the revision (draft · shared · reviewed · superseded), a row against the plans (the eight of `productStatus.ts`), their answer (approved · revise · rejected) and a line of the order log (not sent · waiting on the GC · sent back · to order · on order · on site).
- **Use on prod, read 2026-11-09**: five revisions on two bids, one of them BP398 ZZ Test. The one real bid's Rev 1 is a draft from 2026-09-29, never shared, last written 2026-09-30.
- **Delivery is not measured in a way that can be relied on.** Two facts are called delivered and neither writes the other: the log's Delivered date, typed by the office on a line (3 of 7 log rows on prod), and Materials on site's `delivered` rows (122 on three jobs, every one the phone's *Add a part*, none from the office's Deliveries panel, none naming an invoice or PO). No key ties a submittal row, a purchase order line and a delivery row; `purchase_orders` has no received column. `docs/MATERIALS_ON_SITE_ARCHITECTURE.md` already says *Mark delivered* writes no movement.

## What changed

- **`get_submittals(bid)`** — where every row and every line stands, in one call: the revisions with their lines, whether the working revision takes rows, its rows, the order log (the four steps, the Next line, every line with its key, step, status and dates, what is missing before ordering, the updates sent), the review room and who opened it, the two delivered facts side by side, and `status_text`, the status in plain words.
- **`plan_submittal_rows` → `apply_submittal_rows`** — rows onto the newest draft revision, added or changed by tag, up to 100. Rev 1 is made when the bid has none. A revision the GC holds refuses and names step 7's Resubmit. Held with a reason while the rest goes on: a row the GC answered, a row that carries parts, a house that cannot be found, *Proposed* on a row with a specified product.
- **`plan_submittal_log` → `apply_submittal_log`** — ordered, PO, expected, delivered and the note on the log's lines, by the line's key or its tag (`all_parts` for every part under a tag). A day after today is held for ordered and delivered; a part the GC has not approved is warned, as the tab lets it be marked too.
- **The door**: `rpc: null` in `WRITE_VERBS`, plan_job's shape. The dry run is the server's read of the bid's submittal world (`_shared/devMcpSubmittals.ts` `readSubmittalWorld`), hashed with the payload; apply reads again and writes only on a match. Writes go through `restRows`, which now takes one row or many: `bid_submittals`, `bid_submittal_items`, `bid_procurement_items`, as the dev.
- **One arithmetic**: the seat reads the tab's own kernels from the kernel copy. `procurementItemsFrom` (`procurementLogIo.ts`) kept its reads and gave its mapping to the new pure `procurementItemSources.ts`; `scripts/edge-kernels.mjs` copies it with `standingRows.ts`, `procurementLog.ts`, `procurementBoard.ts` and `productStatus.ts`. `escapeHtml` and `boxDeskLink` moved to files of their own with no browser global (`bidDocuments/escapeHtml.ts`, `materials/boxDeskLink.ts`); `htmlDoc.ts` and `boxSearch.ts` re-export them, so no importer changed.
- Server `0.51.0`; writes are sixty-three verbs.

## What it does not do

Parts under a row, cut sheets and the package, their answer, Share and Send update. It tells nobody: the owner's pick was the status read back in the conversation. A note on a job's or a bid's Activity was the other half of that pick and is **not built**: the seat has no verb for it (`plan_history_entry` writes a development's History, not a job's), so it is a verb of its own. A Delivered date writes nothing to Materials on site; the bridge is its own change.
