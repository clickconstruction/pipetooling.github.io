# v2.5355 — Messages: the rules for a customer’s calls, texts and emails in Job activity (2026-10-12)

This is cut 10's kernel for the Messages build (GC Reviews helper 7, directed by the lead session GC Review): J1 of the cut list in `to-dos/gc-mode/messages/phone-mockups.html` on `spike/gc-mode`, screen 5. The colors are `customer-messages-prototype.html` v0.3, the owner's call of 2026-10-12, which the lead took over `BUILD_PLAN.md`'s "team-only rows yellow". No screen and no read yet. J2 reads the job's messages after v2.5353's indexes are on prod and draws them in the feed.

- **`src/lib/jobs/jobActivityMessages.ts`** (new): a message as a Job activity line. `JobActivityMessage` is the row with who it is with. `messageTone` reads the customer's side as orange (`customerIn` takes the deeper bar), a team note as plain and anyone else as plain. `messageChannelWord` gives the small word after the name, with a call's or a voicemail's length (`callLength`). `messageWho` names her by her first name, ours by ours and an automatic send as the app. `messageLineParts` gives the words, clipped at `MESSAGE_LINE_MAX` with the rest in what opens, a call's summary and transcript, *not tied yet*, and the link to her thread.
- **`src/lib/jobs/jobActivityLine.ts`**: `JobActivityLine` is a union, an item's line or a message's line (`kind: 'message'`, `item: null`, `message`). `buildJobActivityLines` takes `messages` as a second input and numbers them with everything else, the job's own items first on a tie. `jobActivityLineMatches` and `countJobActivityLines` add the Customer bucket. A team note written in her thread counts under Notes, and an event or anyone else's line under All only. `isConversationalLine` counts a message as a person talking, an event as texture.
- **`src/lib/jobActivityFilter.ts`**: `JobActivityFilter` and `JOB_ACTIVITY_FILTERS`, the panel's buckets plus Customer. Job Detail, Job Mode, Quickfill and Dispatch's customer summary keep `ACTIVITY_FILTERS` and never read messages.
- **`src/lib/gc/links.ts`**: `messagesThreadHref(contactId, messageId)`, a person's thread over Messages opened at a line (`useThreadWindow`'s `?thread=` and `?at=`).
- **Fixture**: `src/test/fixtures/jobActivityMessagesDemo.ts`, the prototype's made-up Dana Whitfield on 1312 Oakridge sewer line, with a trade partner's text and a line not tied yet.
