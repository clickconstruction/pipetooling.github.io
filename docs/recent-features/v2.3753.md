## Why

The Code's second instrument for a sub is the notice of claim for unpaid retainage, § 53.057: the same lines as the § 53.056 form with *Total retainage unpaid* in place of the claim amount, due by the **earlier** of 30 days after the claimant's contract is completed, terminated or abandoned, or 30 days after the original contract is terminated or abandoned. Counsel: put unpaid subcontract retainage **inside the § 53.056 claim while the job is open** (an owner may withhold on a § 53.056 notice the day it arrives, § 53.081(b)) and send § 53.057 within the 30 days as belt and suspenders — on a retainage-only notice the owner may withhold only once they receive a copy of the filed affidavit (§ 53.081(c)). The app had no form, no clock, and did not know when our contract on a job ended or what retainage the GC held. Counsel also asked that the master who signs be the callback on the letters (answer 7); `{{phone}}` printed the letterhead's number.
