# v2.4519 — Pipeline stage bar: centered, with arrows between the stages (2026-10-04)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. The owner's two notes on the stage bar the day it shipped (v2.4512): center the stages instead of hugging the left, put arrows between them, and let the arrows go when the bar gets compact.

## What changed

- **Centered.** `.stagesStageSeg` in [`index.css`](../../src/index.css) centers its two lines (`align-items: center`); the numbers line loses the indent that lined it up under a left-hugging name.
- **Arrows.** [`JobsStagesJumpStrip.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsStagesJumpStrip.tsx) draws a `→` (`.stagesStageArrow`, `aria-hidden`) between neighbours, as the strip did before it became the bar. The lit segment's tint still fills its own segment only. The arrow before *Collections* is `←` (the owner's call the same day): Collections is a side road off Billed, and a job leaves it rather than moving on to it.
- **Compact.** `.stagesStageSegs` is a size container. Under 800 px of bar the arrows are not drawn and each segment's side padding tightens, so *Billed Awaiting Payment* keeps its room. It reads the bar's own width, not the window's.

## Tests

`JobsStagesJumpStrip.render.test.tsx`: an arrow between neighbours, none at either end (3 for four stages; with Collections a fourth, pointing left). Seen in the dev preview at 1,000 px (arrows drawn) and 800 px (arrows gone, no name clipped).
