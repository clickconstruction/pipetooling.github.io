# v2.6173 — Legal desk: the attorney's read of how a case tries (2026-10-30)

Punch list #218 (`to-dos/legal-desk-settlement-floor/`), PR 2 of 4 of direction C, on v2.6170 (PR 1, the gate retired). The owner's words: *some cases are hard to try and some are easy to try*. The floor never said which; this PR gives the attorney a place to say it, in two values and a sentence, and the desk a place to show it. PR 3 weighs a settlement against it.

## What changed

- **A read is an entry** ([`_shared/legalCounselRead.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/legalCounselRead.ts), the client's door `src/lib/legal/counselRead.ts`): a `legal_matter_entries` row of kind `note` whose `meta.read` carries `tries` (`easy` · `hard`), `why`, `cost_to_try` and `months`. `currentCounselRead` is the latest one not undone, with whose it is (the firm's, named under Recorded by; the office's, the user's name). No column, no new kind, so the read travels, prints and undoes like any note — `legal_void_entry` is the way back. `counselReadDraftProblem`, `counselReadMetaFromDraft`, `counselReadEntryBody`, `counselTriesWords`, `counselReadFacts`, `counselReadLine` are pure and shared; 4 tests.
- **The firm records it** (`LegalPortal.tsx` → Fees & steps, a fifth form, *How this case tries* · *Costs to try it* · *Months to a judgment* · *Why* · **Save the read**; `submit-legal-portal` kind `read`, validated by the kernel, saved with `meta.recordedBy` like every firm act). The firm's card reads it under the settling line: *How this case tries: hard. Your read, Oct 22: … Costs to try it $2,800.00. 9 months to a judgment.*
- **The office records it** (migration `20261030090000_legal_counsel_read.sql`, `legal_set_counsel_read`, office roles): Fees & steps → **How this case tries** (`CounselReadPanel` in `LegalDeskFirmPanels.tsx`), in house or with the firm — **Record the read…** / **Change the read…**, Easy or Hard, the costs, the months, the why, **Save the read**. The firm reads it on its portal and the note trigger emails it.
- **Can we sue? carries it** (`deskQuestions.ts`): the chip *hard to try* (amber) or *easy to try* (green), and the why ends with *The firm's read, Oct 22: …* or *Our read, Oct 22: …*. No read, no chip.
- **Needs You** (`legalMatters.ts` → `LegalFirmActivity.reads`, `dashboardNeedsYou.ts`): *1 read of how a case tries*, cleared when the office acknowledges it on the matter's table.
