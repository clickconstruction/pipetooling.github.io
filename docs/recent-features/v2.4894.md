# v2.4894 — Help: the lien and attorney guides' last unclear words, and the freshness pass closes (2026-10-07)

PR 7, the last, of the help-guides freshness pass (punch list #91, Helper 10). It is stacked on [v2.4893](v2.4893.md) and takes the leftovers that sat in the lien and attorney guides. Punchlist asked for them as one PR, words only, read against the code. It said to skip any guide an open PR touches and to keep the four counsel words. Then the card goes.

## The lien and attorney leftovers

- **send a customer account to your attorney**: the second "## 6." heading, *Ask the firm*, is **## 9.**, the next after 8. Nothing linked to its anchor.
- **file a lien and never miss its deadlines**
  - "The first bill on a GC job is the last net" now says what it meant (`docs/recent-features/v2.3450.md`): billing a GC job is a last chance to catch a missing owner before the notice is due. The owner line shows on every bill until the owner is confirmed (`ownerConfirm.ts`).
  - A job with no approved hours can also have its last day of work set by hand on the Lien desk (v2.4676).
- **share your attorney their portal**
  - "by the run or by hand" is spelled out. A paper mailed outside the Lien desk's run, and recorded afterward, reads *by hand* (`legalLienPaper.ts`, `lienNoticeByHand.ts`). A run's envelope carries no such word.
  - "the pile counsel named" is spelled out too. The band names the job's pile, A, B or C, from counsel's memo. The pile says what the claim can reach (`lienOwnerCall.ts` `affidavitPileFor`).
- **review a collections account before it goes to your attorney**
  - "Writing it down" lowers a bill to an amount we agree to accept. It does not give up the balance (`AgreedWriteDownModal.tsx`).
  - "the firm's third" is *the firm's share*, since 33% is only the default.
  - The desk's three-part test is introduced as what the desk checks, not as the meaning of *sworn account*.
- **give a customer a lien release** and **understand how liens work**: the legal description is *the lot, block and subdivision that county records use for the land* (`CustomerPropertyRecordPanel.tsx`).

## The four counsel words, kept on purpose

These are statute and counsel's words, so no plain word replaces them. Each gets one plain sentence where it first appears in a guide that did not already explain it:

- **Sworn account**: *a suit on an itemized bill that someone at Click swears is true and unpaid* (Rule 185, `lienRuleCites.ts`). Glossed in *send a customer account to your attorney*, *review a collections account…* and *the Texas lien rules the app follows*.
- **§ 53.056 notice**: *the Texas form we send the owner and the GC for each unpaid month*. Glossed in:
  - *send a customer account to your attorney*
  - *file a lien…*
  - *share your attorney their portal*
  - *give a customer a lien release*
  - *answer an owner who calls…*
  - *job window tabs*
  - *mark a property residential or commercial*
  - *see when a customer will pay*
  - *share a customer their portal*

  It was already explained in *send a final demand letter*, *understand how liens work*, *read the Pipeline's money view* and *the Texas lien rules*.
- **Exhibits**: in the attorney packet, *the records the packet offers as proof, one lettered group for each kind*. That gloss replaces a wrong one in *review a collections account…* and is new in *send a customer account to your attorney* and *share your attorney their portal*. In a demand letter, *a document sent behind the letter as proof, lettered A, B and so on*, in *send a final demand letter* and *the Texas lien rules*.
- **Bond**: the GC's payment bond, *a bonding company's promise to pay the subs when the GC does not*, in *file a lien…* and *share your attorney their portal*. The sub's own bond, *a bonding company's promise that the sub finishes the work and pays its suppliers*, in *assemble a sub work order* and *send a sub a work order from a sheet*.

One guide is left: *send lien notices from the Lien desk*. Its first § 53.056 (line 9) and its *bond* (line 66) wait because open PRs #4909 and #4904 hold that guide. The glosses above are the sentences to use there.
