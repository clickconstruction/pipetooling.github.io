# v2.7176 — Subs pay: paying a sub is the same Record payment window as pay stubs (2026-11-13)

The owner, on the sub's Make Payment window: "I would like the modal to be more similar to the record payment modal, if not exactly like the record payment modal in People Pay stubs. This way we can better track partial payments." He had typed "cheque" into the memo, which is the method going into the one text box the sub reads. Part of punch list #347's train (sub payments as a ledger).

**A migration ships with this PR and is not pushed: `20261113180000_sub_payment_method.sql`.** The client reads and writes two columns only that migration creates. See *The migration and the gap* below.

## The window

`SubLaborPaymentModals`'s Make Payment is now **Record payment**, drawn from the pay stubs' own pieces:

| | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Title | Make Payment | Record payment |
| Sums | two lines: sub and job, then Total · Paid · Outstanding | one grey line: sub · job · Total · Paid · Remaining |
| Amount | a number box on the outstanding amount | **Amount paid**, a text box on what remains, to the cent |
| A part payment | nothing said | *A part payment. $… stays due on the sheet.* or *This pays the sheet in full.* |
| Past what remains | recorded, the sheet read Over | **refused**, with the amount and by how much |
| Date | Date sent | Paid date (sent) |
| Method | typed into the memo | **How it was sent** chips, and a box for a check number, a Cash App id or a confirmation |
| Memo | Memo (optional), shown to the sub | **Memo for the sub (optional)**, shown to the sub, with the hide tick Edit Payment already had |
| Press | Record Payment | Record payment, dead with its reason when the amount cannot be recorded |

**Edit Payment** gets the same chips, opened on the row's method, so an old payment whose memo says *cheque* can be given its method. A back-charge has no method.

## Shared, not copied

- `src/components/pay/RecordPaymentPieces.tsx`, new: `RecordPaymentShell`, `RecordPaymentSummary`, `AmountPaidField`, `RecordPaymentHelp`, `PaidDateField`, `PayMethodPicker`, `PaymentNoteField`, `RecordPaymentFooter`. They were the body of `RecordPayStubPaymentModal`.

- `PaySourcePicker` keeps its props and is now the pay report's kinds on top of `PayMethodPicker`, so Balances' split window is untouched.
- The sub's window is the same pieces with its own kernel.

## The kernel

`src/lib/subWorkOrders/subPaymentWindow.ts`, pure:

- `subSheetMoney(total, payments)`: paid, back-charged and remaining across any run of part payments, rounded to the cent at each sum, never below zero.
- `planSubPayment(amountText, remaining)`: the press's gate. An amount up to what remains is recorded whole. An amount past it is refused with `over` and the reason. Nothing is clipped, because a clipped payment would not match the bank. A blank, a zero, a word and a settled sheet each have their own reason.
- `SUB_PAY_METHODS`: `check`, `zelle`, `mercury`, `cashapp`, `apple_pay`, `cash`, `client_direct`, `other`. A check and Zelle lead, since that is how subs are paid. It is its own list: `pay_stub_payments.source_kind`'s five kinds are tied to the pay-send functions and have no check.
- `subPayMethodRefField`, `subPayMethodWrite`, `subPayMethodWords` (`Check 1187`), `subPaymentAfterWords`, `subPaymentSummaryWords`.

## What a part payment does to the sheet

Nothing but the sums. The sheet keeps its place in the pay run and its Pay when rule, Due shows what remains, and the row reads **Paid** when nothing is left. Each part is its own row in the sheet's Payments list with its date, its method and its amount.

## The sub's portal

Unchanged. `sub-portal` reads its payment columns by name and names neither new column, so the portal shows the amount, the date and the memo, as before, and not the method or its number. The memo is still the only text of a payment the sub reads, and the hide tick keeps one off the portal. A sub with a login can read a hidden memo's row through the API today, and the same is true of the two new columns: nothing here is a secret from the sub, and nothing here is newly shown to them.

## The migration and the gap

`20261113180000_sub_payment_method.sql` adds `method` and `method_ref` to `people_labor_job_payments` (with a check on the list) and to the event snapshot, and re-creates `remove_labor_job_payment` and `restore_labor_job_payment` from their 20260917140000 bodies with the two columns spliced in, so a removed payment comes back with its method. A move re-points the live row and needs nothing. Additive, idempotent, no new table.

Until it is pushed:

- **Reads are safe.** `useSubLaborLedger` asks for the payments with the two columns, and on a refusal reads them as it did before. Walked on the dev build against the un-migrated database: the first read was refused, the second loaded every payment, and the Pay view's counts were as before. Without this a refused read would draw every sheet as unpaid.
- **A payment with no method is the insert it always was.** The two columns are named only when a chip was picked.
- **A payment with a method is refused** by the database with its reason, and nothing is recorded.

So the PR is left unarmed for the push, and nothing breaks if it merges first.

## Checked

- **The migration, on a scratch local Postgres 15**, with the payment tables at their real shape and 20260917140000 applied first: it applies twice; a row recorded the old way has no method; a method off the list is refused; a check with its number survives a move, a removal and the undo, on the row and on both event rows; a row with no method restores with none; `authenticated` may still run both functions and `anon` may not. The restrictive fences and RLS were stubbed: CI's SQL beds prove those.
- **Both windows on the dev build, read only**, on the test sub's ZZ sheet: the line, the amount on what remains, a part payment's words, a check's number box, the refusal past what remains with the press dead, cancel with nothing recorded. The pay stubs' window opened with its five chips and its words. No page error.
- `subPaymentWindow.test.ts` (15), `SubLaborPaymentModals.render.test.tsx` (9, five new), the pay components' 18 unchanged, the guide tests.
