# v2.4895 — Payments: move one to the right job right after you type it (2026-10-08)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Two bugs found on punch list #22's close-out walk (2026-10-08, J904 → J907; the walk is in [v2.3576](v2.3576.md) → *Walked 2026-10-08*).

## What was wrong

- **A payment just typed in had no Move to job….** A row reads saved when its id is in `persistedLedgerPaymentIds` (`useJobFormPaymentActions`). That set was built only from `editing.payments`, the job as it was last read. The billing autosave writes a new row and records its id in `hydratedPaymentIdsRef`, but a ref does not re-render. So the row stayed *unsaved* (`paymentMoveBlock` → `'unsaved'`), and its ⋯ menu offered only *Hide the boxes* and *Remove* until the job was opened again.
- **The Why box held the text *wrong job*.** Clicking into it and typing ran on: the walk stored *wrong jobZZ test move, punch list #22 walk* as the reason, and the grey line printed it.

## What changed

- **Saved means saved.** `useJobFormAutosaveEngine` keeps the ids its last billing write saved as state too (`writtenPaymentIds`, cleared when another job opens), next to the ref the diff reads. `useJobFormPaymentActions` counts a row saved when the job was read with it or the autosave wrote it. Move to job… appears once the autosave lands. Remove on such a row writes at once through the remove RPC, as on any saved row; before, it dropped the line and let the next autosave delete it.
- **The Why box is empty with *wrong job* as its placeholder** (`JobPaymentMoveModal`). [`jobPaymentMove.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobPaymentMove.ts) `paymentMoveReason(typed)` records what was typed, else *wrong job* (`PAYMENT_MOVE_DEFAULT_REASON`). The plain move's RPC, the Stripe-held move's credit-note reason and its trace event all read it.

## Left for the owner

The walk's third finding is a design call, now a row in `to-dos/owner-decisions-pending.md`. A payment moved onto a job and then removed leaves *moved here from …* over no payment, because the trace draws moves only, and the remove event's reason reads *unlinked*.

## Tests

- `jobPaymentMove.test.ts` (+1): `paymentMoveReason`. Typed, trimmed, blank and null.
- `useJobFormPaymentActions.render.test.tsx` (+1): a line the autosave wrote is saved, and its Remove confirms through the RPC.
- `JobPaymentMoveModal.render.test.tsx` (+1): the box starts empty with the placeholder, and typing holds exactly what was typed.

Walked live on J904 on the dev server. A $1 payment typed in and autosaved showed **Move to job…** in its ⋯ menu with no reopen. The window's Why box was empty with *wrong job* as its placeholder. **Remove** then took the payment off through the RPC, and J904 read $10 paid again.
