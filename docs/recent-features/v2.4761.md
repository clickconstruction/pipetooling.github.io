# v2.4761 — Pipeline: Billed and Ready to Bill count every bill, on a phone and with the map hidden (2026-10-07)

Client only. Punch list #93 C (the Collections look of 2026-10-06).

## What was wrong

The phone strip read *Billed 64* and the desktop header *Billed Awaiting Payment 71* on the same data, the same minute. Both read `billedHdr.count`: the cached header stats (lean rows over every active status, 71) until the section's scope is merged, then the live rows. The `billed_all` scope fetches only jobs in `billed` status, but a progress bill on a job still in `working` sits in the Billed section (and a break-off draft in Ready to Bill) — the row exists only once the `working` scope is on the board. On a computer the map card asks for every scope and hid the gap; the phone board loads one stage at a time, so the seven bills on Working jobs were missing and the live header said 64. A computer with the map hidden had the same gap.

## What changed

- `stagesSectionPrefs.ts`: `scopesForStagesSection(section)` — Billed, Collections and Ready to Bill carry every `NON_PAID_SCOPES` entry beside their own (a bill rides its job's scope: the first cut carried `working` alone and the phone still read 70 to the desktop's 71 — a billed line on a job in another status); `scopesForOpenStagesSections` (the page's first fetch) folds them in.
- `JobsStagesTab.tsx`: the fetch-on-expand effect asks for every scope of an open section; `sectionMerged` is true only when every one has landed (so the header holds the cached count until every status's bills are there), `sectionScopeBusy` when any is loading.

## Tests

- `stagesSectionPrefs.test.ts`: the open-sections mapping re-pinned (the default Ready to Bill brings the other three; Collections alone brings billed_all and the rest) and the per-section scopes (+1).
- Live: on the phone preset the strip reads Billed 71 and the list holds 71 cards, as the computer does.
