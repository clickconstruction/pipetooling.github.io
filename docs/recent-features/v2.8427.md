# v2.8427 — Bills sent: a send whose bill was taken back says so (2026-11-23)

Found on the Bills sent walk of 2026-11-23 (punch list #422). No migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

A sent copy outlives its bill. When a bill is emailed and then taken back or replaced, the copy stays in `sent_documents` with the old bill's id, and the bill row is gone. The line was drawn with the bill's number, the address and *Delivered*, and with no amount and no word on payment. Its day's band counted two bills over three bill lines. Nothing said why. Read on prod through the dev seat, read only: the bill the walk's line named is not on record, and a bill for the same job went the next day.

## What changed

- `billsSentLines`: a copy that names a bill the read did not find carries the note `BILLS_SENT_GONE_NOTE`, *This bill is no longer on the job. It was taken back or replaced after it went.* The screen, the phone's card, the print and the spreadsheet's *Note* column all draw a line's note already.
- The note is never said when no bill read at all. That is a read that failed, not a bill that is gone.
- The line still counts in no total, as before.

## Tests

`billsSent.test.ts`: a copy whose bill is gone, beside one whose bill is on record; and no note when the bills map is empty.
