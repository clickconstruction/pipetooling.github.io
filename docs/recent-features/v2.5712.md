## Is this the best we can do?

1. **Fewer presses on a phone**: one line on the page the owner already opens, and each door lands on the exact card; before, eight settings read by hand across two tabs.
2. **Plain words**: every title, why and door and the card's sentences run through `plainWordsFailures`; the guide section passes the sweep.
3. **Do what is ready**: the card only reads; a row that cannot be read reads as off, and a read that fails draws nothing rather than a wrong count.
4. **Reuse**: the app's own parsers decide each state, so the card and the cards on Settings never disagree; `Chip` and `Link` are the house's.
5. **Cut**: the card does not claim the phone seats turn on *Call back in Quo* (always disabled, § 0.3); a row's health (the reader's `gmail_sync_state`) is not read here, since Read bids@'s own card shows it. Written as not carried.
