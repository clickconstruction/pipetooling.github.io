# v2.1990 — Waiting to hear lens: chase recent pending bids (2026-08-21)

Estimator ask (Wendi, via owner): the Why we lost lens works old lost bids, but "it seems more pertinent to focus on the ones in the past couple months that are still not marked lost — more relevant feedback." First PR of the pending-chase train (PR 2 adds one-tap outcome capture on the card).

- **Waiting to hear** joins By builder / By status / Why we lost as the Followup tab's fourth lens ([`BidsWaitingToHearLens`](../../src/components/bids/BidsWaitingToHearLens.tsx)), same call-mode workspace shape as Why we lost: left rail is the builder queue, right is one builder's call with street-name pills per pending bid, tappable phone, tap-to-Google-Maps address pill, and Skip/auto-advance.
- **Queue = sent + no outcome, newest first.** Bids with `bid_date_sent` set and `outcome` empty, filtered by a **Sent within** window (30/60/90/All pills, default 60 days). Builders with bids to chase rank first, ordered by most recent sent date descending (recent bids are where bid tabs and honest feedback still live); fully-chased builders drop to the bottom with a ✓.
- **Chase state is derived, not stored**: a bid "needs a chase" when it has no contact at all, its last contact predates the sent date, or the last touch is older than 7 days (`PENDING_CHASE_STALE_CONTACT_DAYS`). Effective last contact = max of `bids.last_contact` and the latest submission entry (parent's `lastContactFromEntries`). Street pills flip green as touches land — no new writes in this PR.
- **Rollup**: N sent still open · $ total · never chased since sending (count + $) · oldest untouched age. The bid card calls out "Never contacted since sending" in amber.
- New pure kernel [`bidPendingChase.ts`](../../src/lib/bidPendingChase.ts) (sibling of `bidLossCategories.ts`): window filter, needs-chase predicate, builder grouping/ranking, next-bid stepper, rollup — 16 unit tests in [`bidPendingChase.test.ts`](../../src/lib/bidPendingChase.test.ts); `nowIso` is a parameter so tests stay deterministic.
- Wiring in [`Bids.tsx`](../../src/pages/Bids.tsx): tab key `waiting-to-hear` (deep link `?tab=waiting-to-hear`), superintendents gated out (same as By status / Why we lost). Rode along: the top-level **Followup** board tab now highlights when the Why we lost or Waiting to hear lens is active (it previously lost its active state on Why we lost).
- Help guide `follow-up-with-builders.md` updated with the fourth lens. Verified live on prod data: 43 bids to chase in the 60-day window, Knight Contracting (8 pills) and Structura ($17.3M waiting) ranked up top, rollup showing 15 never-chased bids. Client-only — no migration.
