# v2.4096 — Pipeline: the lien runway shows a sub's § 53.056 notice first, and the flag says "file lien by" (2026-09-28)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Mock-up: board 5 of the owner's runway canvas (*The notice on the runway*). The owner asked, reading a live row, whether "lien Dec 15" meant *send a notice by* or *lien by* — and then whether a notice has to go first. On a sub job it does, and the runway said nothing about it.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/jobs/lienPayRunway.ts`** — three inputs: `isSub` (a GC on the job), `noticedMonths` (the 'YYYY-MM' months the job's live § 53.056 notices cover) and `anyNoticeOnFile` (a notice listing no months still counts). One state, `notice_due`: on a sub job whose work month has no recorded notice, the notice date (`noticeDeadlineForMonth`, the same residential-when-unknown clock as the flag) is the first deadline — `lines` read *notice by Oct 15 · lien by Nov 16* / *send the notice · 17 d*, `chipLabel` *notice in 17 d*, tone amber (red inside 7 days), `sortKey` in the file-first band, and `marks.notice` is a hollow flag ahead of the lien flag. A notice window already closed unsent is the `closed` state with *notice window closed <day>* (the lien for that month is gone). Once recorded, the ordinary states carry `marks.notice.done` (a check) and the hover says the notice is recorded; the mark drops once its date is behind us. `noticeByYmd` / `daysToNotice` on the output. **Wording:** *lien X* becomes *file lien by X* in every sentence (*pay Oct 3 → file lien by Nov 16*, *file lien by Nov 16 → pay Nov 20*, *no pay date · file lien by Dec 15*) — the flag is the last day to FILE. 27 tests.
- **`src/components/jobs/LienPayRunway.tsx`** — the hollow flag (tone colour, white inset) while the notice is owed, with the lien flag neutral so the eye lands on the notice first; a 12px green check once recorded.
- **`src/hooks/useBilledLienClocks.ts`** — the filings read gains `notice_53_056` and `months_covered`; the job input gains `gc_customer_id`; the clock returns `noticedMonths` and `noticeOnFile`. Still two chunked reads.
- **`JobsStagesTab.tsx`** — passes `isSub`, `noticedMonths`, `anyNoticeOnFile`. The phone chip rule takes `notice_due` inside 21 days.
- Guides (*read the Pipeline's money view*, *file a lien and never miss its deadlines*) and the GLOSSARY entry say the same.

## Honest approximation

The runway keys on the job's **last work month**, as the whole clock does; a sub job with several unpaid months has one notice per month, and the Payment forecast's month panel and the Lien desk list them all. The runway shows the last month's; its hover points at the Lien desk.

## Verify

Jobs → Pipeline → Billed, a GC job with no recorded notice: a hollow amber flag ahead of the lien flag and *notice by … · lien by …* / *send the notice · N d*. Record the notice on the Lien desk (or the Lien window): the hollow flag becomes a check and the sentence returns to the money and *file lien by*. A direct job: no notice mark, only the new wording.
