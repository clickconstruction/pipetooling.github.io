# v2.5912 — Talk a field report through (2026-10-26)

Punch list #186, item 1, PR 1 of its plan, the owner's pick of 2026-10-26 (*I like it, build it*). The smallest step of the voice note: the phone's own speech into the report's Notes, no key and nothing on the server. PR 2 (`model-config`, `report-draft`) turns the words into a structured draft of the fields.

## The button

`src/components/reports/ReportVoiceNote.tsx` sits over **Report type** on both report forms, `NewReportModal.tsx` (Leave Report from Job Mode, the clock-out, the Dashboard) and `AdditionalReportModal.tsx` (the job window's **＋ Report** and the reports list). A press runs the browser's speech recognizer (`hooks/useVoiceNote.ts`: Safari on an iPhone, Chrome on Android, Chrome on a desk) in the phone's language (`speechLang`), continuous, final results only; the next press, three seconds of quiet after the last words (`SILENCE_STOP_MS`) or two minutes (`LISTEN_MAX_MS`) stops it. The final results are joined once (`joinHeard`: one paragraph, a capital, a stop) and put under what the Notes field already holds (`mergeHeard`), the field being the one named Notes, else the first long text field (`notesFieldLabel`); a report type with no text field draws no button. Nothing is written until **Save report**, as before.

The button's words by state (`voiceButtonWords`, held to the plain-words rule): *Talk it through*, *Listening · 0:14*, *Talk it through again · Heard 22 seconds. The words are in Notes. Read them, then save.*, and the three honest endings: *Use the microphone on your keyboard* when the browser has no recognizer (`speechRecognitionOf` finds neither name), *No signal* when the phone is offline before a press (the recognizer needs a network on both platforms), *The microphone is off for this app* on a refused permission, *Nothing was heard* on an empty note. The keyboard's own dictation key still types into Notes on every phone, so the fallback is never a dead end.

## Not built here, by design

The transcription model and the structured draft (Notes in three lines, the lines' percents with their words, the *What you said* fold) are PR 2, behind the Model keys card. The audio is never kept.
