# v2.8000 — HR files: the dev seat reads and writes a person's standing over time (2026-11-19)

Punch list #315, upgrade 4c. `dev-mcp` server 0.120.0. One new pair, no migration. The owner's go for the whole train, 2026-11-11; Grace's word to resume, 2026-11-19.

## What was wrong

`person_engagements` says what a person was by day, employee or subcontractor, and People → HR has read and written it since v2.7991. A session read it through `read_rows` alone, got no Timeline line for it, and could not write it.

## What changed

- **`get_person_file` and `get_case_brief` carry `engagements`.**
  - `standing`: the fact strip's words. `today`: what the person is on the day read.
  - `stretches`: each one that stands, with its id, kind, days, source, reference, note and a line of words.
  - `taken_off`: how many were withdrawn. Those are counted and not listed.
  - `get_person_file` hands the stretches to the Timeline's own kernel, so each change draws under Employment as it does on the tab.
  - The read is a source of its own. One that fails is named in `held` and takes only the standing out. Another role gets null.
  - The brief's manifest counts the stretches that stand and names the pair that changes them.
- **One new pair, `plan_engagement` → `apply_engagement`**, POSTs `dev_engagement_write`, the door Standing… writes through.
  - Exactly one of `open`, `change`, `close` or `withdraw` a call.
  - The shape goes through the tab's own payload builders. A day must exist on the calendar.
  - The door refuses the rest in words: days that overlap, a change to the kind already running, no stretch to close, a stretch withdrawn already.
  - The dry run performs the write and unwinds it. Its reply names each change and the standing after it, with no clock and no id.
- **The call log keeps a call short**: which of the four it is, the kind and the days. It keeps no reference, note or reason.
- The version's note is in `docs/dev-mcp/SERVER_NOTES.md`, where notes live since v2.7999.
- **Kernel** `supabase/functions/_shared/devMcpEngagement.ts`, over the tab's own `personEngagements.ts`.
- Counts: 152 write verbs, 58 over 25 RPCs, 204 tools.

## Found on the walk of v2.7976

`plan_case`'s words said to take `person_id` from `find_person`. `find_person` answers a login's `users.id`, and `dev_case_write` takes the roster row's `people.id`, so the first plan on `ZZ Test Hand` was refused with *person … not found*. The words of `plan_case` and of the new `plan_engagement` now name the id to send: `person.id` from `get_case_brief` or `get_person_file`. No behavior changed.

## Is this the best we can do?

The card gave the brief a count of stretches and left the rows to `read_rows`. A session that opens a file to answer what someone was in a given month needs the stretches, and they are a handful of short rows, so both reads carry the block.

The card also had `get_hours_statement` say how many rows it left out as placed pay. Nothing marks such a row yet, so the statement has nothing to leave out. It is untouched until the owner reads the report per person that 4d starts with.
