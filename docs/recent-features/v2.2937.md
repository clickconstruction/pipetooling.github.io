# v2.2937 — Guide: schedule a sub across every surface (2026-09-06)

Docs-only follow-up to the three-party scheduling train (v2.2927–v2.2934). No code, no migration.

## The guide

`src/content/help/schedule-a-sub-across-every-surface.md` (Office; order 60, ahead of the per-feature guides at 61–64). A hub guide that walks the plan in job order and links out to the four per-feature guides:

1. The map — a table of the six surfaces (Subs → Work, sub portal, Dispatch People / Day, Forecast → Subs, GC portal, dispatch inbox) and who owns each.
2. Set the window (Subs → Work, Add a stage…).
3. Send the order, the sub picks a start.
4. The sub's days (Your dates · Change, Your days month, Mark this day off + the collision line).
5. Their part, their percent (one control, Send to office).
6. Watch the job (the bell, Settings → My email schedule → Job watches).
7. Where dispatch sees the subs (lanes, badge, Subs on site, Sub Board, crew day email).
8. The GC (share switch, Offer to GC, Ask for other dates, Accept / Answer with…, Re-scheduling).
9. A table of every dispatch inbox line the train writes with its typed next step.
