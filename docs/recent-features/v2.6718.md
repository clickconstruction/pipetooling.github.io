# v2.6718 — The cost chart’s end labels stand apart, and a chip’s cost matches the chart to the cent (2026-11-08)

Punch list #257, the two defects the 2026-11-08 walk on prod found after PR 3, fixed after PR 4 (v2.6711): on the development's Timeline the end labels crowded at the right edge at every width, the *true cost* label over the cash and cost labels and the red label half under the icons, and at 375 wide the three overlapped each other; and a chip's title (*JP1091: $8,838.88 of cost so far*) read a cent under the chart's own end label for the same job ($8,838.89).

- **The end labels as one column** (`JobSummaryChargesTimelineChart.tsx`, the chart the Job Summary, the Job window and the Development page share). Each line's dot renderer drew its own end label beside its own end and knew nothing of the others; now the dots keep only their marks (the source icons, the 💵, the 🚩) and one `Customized` layer, `ChargesTimelineEndLabels`, draws every end label at the last point from the chart's own axis scales. The pure kernel `src/lib/jobs/chartEndLabels.ts` places them: each label wants to sit just above or below its line (`placeEndLabels`, `END_LABEL_GAP`); the column is spread so no two overlap, never crosses a mark the text would reach across (every bucket's icon row, 💵 and 🚩 within the widest label's reach of the end, `marksWithin`, `endLabelWidth`, the mark bands as the dots draw them, `iconRowSpan`, `iconRowY`, `paymentMarkerY`), and stays inside the plot; the column is laid twice, giving way down and giving way up, and the laying that moves the labels least wins, so at a busy end the stack of icons is cleared by the near side (on the site's phone chart the three stand above the end's icons, not under them). A label never swaps places with another: the line that ends higher keeps its label higher.
- **The chip's cent** (`siteTimeline.ts`): `foldSiteChargeEvents` summed a day's lines per job and source and rounded each folded line, so the chart's end was a sum of rounded parts while the chip summed the raw charges and rounded once. The fold now keeps the exact sum and the kernel rounds the running total once at the end, as a job's own chart does, so the chip, the chart's end for one job, the Job Summary's own end and the strip's *Chart ends at* all read one number; the tooltip rounds as it draws.

Tests: `chartEndLabels.test.ts` (labels apart keep their wants; two wanting one height spread a line apart with the higher line higher; a stack off the floor pulled up whole; a label steps under the icon row, or over a mark it is pulled into; the near side of a tall stack wins; a dense stack of twenty-six marks cleared whole; the top holds; `marksWithin` and `endLabelWidth`; the mark helpers mirror the dots), `siteTimeline.test.ts` (two half-cent charges: the chip, the site's end and the job's own chart end all read $2.01 where the folded rounding read $2.00). `JobsJobSummaryTab` and the Job window's render smokes hold the chart's wiring.

**Verified live** on the dev server as the dev on the real development, measured from the drawn SVG: at 1280 wide the three labels sit as one column at the right end with no two overlapping and none touching an icon; at 375 wide the true cost and cost labels stand above the end's icon rows and the cash label under the green line's end, none touching any glyph, nothing past the edge. The chart's end moved from $…650.16 to $…650.15 on the site, the once-rounded sum, and the strip's sum line moved with it.

Client only, no migration, no function. With this the #257 card's defects are closed; the card's deletion is the owner's call.
