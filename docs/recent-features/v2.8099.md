# v2.8099 — Document builder: a send press that cannot go looks dead (2026-11-20)

Punch list #404, found by the audit of 2026-11-20. No migration and no function.

## What was wrong

On a published sheet's **Send the sheet to this job's trades**, a row that was sent to carries a second press: **Send it again**, or **Tell them it changed**. It is dead while nothing is published, while the trade's page is off, while nobody at the trade has an email, and while a revision nobody has checked waits on the box **Send it anyway**. That box sits at the foot of the card, well under the rows.

The press was drawn the same dead or live: link blue on the surface, with a pointer. Pressed, it did nothing and said nothing. The list's main presses already wear the dead pair (`deadPress.ts`); its small ones never did.

## The fix

`smallPress(dead)` in `BuilderSendList.tsx` draws a small press with the dead pair and a not-allowed cursor while it cannot be pressed. The second press takes it for its four reasons, and it and the list's other small presses (**Send it to them**, **Take it off their page**, **Turn their page on**) take it while a send is on its way.

## Tests

`BuilderSendList.render.test.tsx`: with a revision nobody has checked, the second press on a sent row is disabled and drawn dead, and live once **Send it anyway** is ticked.

## Not changed

A dead second press still does not say why beside itself. The reason is the chip on its row (*Their page is off*) or the box at the foot. Putting the reason on the row is a call for the owner.
