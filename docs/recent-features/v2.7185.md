# v2.7185 — Checklist: the ⋮ menu on Manage reaches Delete on a phone (2026-11-13)

Punch list #348, PR 1 of 4. Three things the New task walk (v2.7155) saw on Checklist → Manage.

## What changed

- **The ⋮ menu.** It hung inside its row, `position: absolute; top: 80%`, and the list clips at its rounded edge (`overflow: hidden`). On the last task of a list, or the only one a search found, a phone showed the top of *Edit* and nothing under it, so *Delete* could not be reached. The press and its menu are now `ManageRowMenu` (`src/components/checklist/`). The menu is `position: fixed`, placed from its press's place on screen by `rowMenuPlacement` (`src/lib/checklistManageRow.ts`): under the press when it fits, above it when there is more room there, its right edge under the press's. It sits at `MANAGE_ROW_MENU_Z` (1000), over the page and under every window. A scroll or a resize closes it, since a menu pinned to the screen would be left behind by its row. Its lines are the same four as before, by the same rules: *Open in Roadmap*, *Edit*, *Mute notifications*, *Delete*.
- **No empty line over an error.** When the list's read failed, the page drew the error and `checklistManageEmptyWords` still said *No tasks yet. ＋ New task adds the first.*, because zero rows had arrived. The tab now keeps `loadFailed`, and the kernel returns null for it.
- **notifies on done.** The chip read `notify_creator_on_complete` alone. New task hides the *Tell me* bell on a task that is only the giver's but leaves the stored tick on, so such a task read *notifies on done*. `showsNotifiesOnDone` shows the chip when someone else is told, or when the giver is told and somebody other than the giver has the task.

## Checked

On the local app at 390 by 740, signed in, nothing saved: the ⋮ of the last of 29 tasks opened upward, from 585 to 700 px, with *Edit*, *Mute notifications* and *Delete* all drawn and *Delete* the top element at its own centre.
