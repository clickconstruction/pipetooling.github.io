# v2.5675 — The aging window's Job acct chip is an act, not just a mark (2026-10-20)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Punch list **#132** PR 2 of 3
(`to-dos/ap-aging-cell-invoices/`); builds on v2.5673's cell modal. The owner's ask,
2026-10-20: *"in that modal [I would like to] set some invoices as job accounts."*

## What shipped

- **Kernel** [`jobSupplyHouseAccounts.ts`](../../src/lib/materials/jobSupplyHouseAccounts.ts):
  `agingCellChipState(inv, allocations, account)` — the Jobs cell as a state machine (`on` ·
  `off_open` · `off_no_account` · `off_loading` · `no_job` · `split` · `credit`; taking a flag
  OFF is always allowed, a credit never carries one, the flag needs exactly one job) and
  `agingCellBandWords(account, houseName)` — the invoice form's amber-band words per account
  state. Both unit-tested.
- **The chips** in [`SupplyHousesTab`](../../src/components/SupplyHousesTab.tsx)'s cell modal:
  **Job acct ✓** (tap off), **+ Job acct** solid (account open — the hover names the ref and
  how it was opened; one tap flags), **+ Job acct** dashed (no account — unfolds the band
  with **Mark opened…** / **Flag anyway** / Cancel), a muted chip while the accounts read is
  in flight, and the form's own sentences as tooltips on no-job and split rows.
- **The reads**, folded into the modal's one on-open effect: `job_supply_house_accounts` for
  the single-job (job, house) pairs in view and the houses' `job_accounts` reps (for the
  sheet). **The write** is one `update` of `on_job_account`; every reader re-derives from the
  same state — the matrix, the teal lines, the open modal's footer — and an expanded house
  showing the same invoice reloads.
- **The shared sheet** (`MarkJobAccountOpenedModal`, v2.3423) stacks above the modal; saved
  as open it flags the invoice the press was about (the invoice form's own `onSaved`
  behavior) and the band's account state updates in place.
- **Guide**: *mark an invoice on a job account* — the "already have invoices sitting on job
  accounts" paragraph now leads with the chip.

Verified live as the dev against prod, round-tripped to the starting state: Reece → Current,
`S124687508.001` tapped off (the job-acct subtotal dropped $9,324.50 → $9,217.37, the cell
total unmoved), the dashed chip unfolded the band, **Flag anyway** restored it ($9,324.50
again); the sheet opened over the modal with the right job and house and was cancelled.
