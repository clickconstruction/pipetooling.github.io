# v2.8430 — Agreements: the buttons are easier to tap on a phone (2026-11-24)

Small faults punch list #403 saw and left (`to-dos/outside-signer-record/README.md`, *Faults seen and not fixed*): the presses of Documents → Agreements stood under the 44 px a thumb needs. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was measured

A harness page (not committed) drew the real components over made-up rows, in Chromium at 390 px and at 1,280 px, light and dark, as a dev and as an estimator. Heights in px, at 390 px wide:

| Press | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A line's name as a button (the **Signed contract** window, the Contract window, a work order) | 24 | 44 |
| A line's name as a link (People → Contracts, GC mode) | 18 | 44 |
| A kind or state chip | 27.8 | 44 |
| A line's act: Send, Send again, Copy the signing link, Take back, Add to a list | 27.8 | 44 |
| Contract library, Show more | 27.8 | 44 |
| Send a paper | 35.5 | 44 |
| The find box | 42 | 44 |
| Who is it with?, the × | 20.7 by 21.6 | 44 by 44 |
| Who is it with?, a chip | 25.6 | 44 |
| The Send window, Cancel | 35.5 | 44 |
| The Send window, Send email | 33.5 | 44 |

The session that built the name press (v2.8388) read it at 20 px. This harness loads the app's own stylesheet, whose 1.5 line height makes it 24. The card's other three figures (28, 21 by 22, 34) agree with these.

A row grows with its name: an estimator's signed line went from 106.7 to 126.7, and the office's line with three acts from 150.9 to 183.2. The rows still read as a list, under their month bands. No view scrolls sideways at 390 px, before or after. At 1,280 px every figure is as it was, and the eight desk screenshots are the same files byte for byte before and after.

## What changed

- `src/lib/documents/agreementsPhonePress.ts`, a pure kernel over the app's one height (`PHONE_PRESS_MIN_HEIGHT` in `src/lib/phonePress.ts`): `paperPressFloor` for a press with its own box, `paperChipFloor` for a chip (as wide as it is tall at least, so *All* stays a pill), `paperNamePress` for a line's name (the words centred in the height, level with the tag) and `partyPickerClosePress` for the ×. Each answers an empty style at a desk, so a caller's spread changes nothing there.
- `DocumentsAgreementsTab`, `AgreementsSendPaper`, `PaperPartyPicker` and `ContractSendWindow` read `useNarrowViewport640` and spread the kernel into their presses. The words, the colours and the order are as they were.
- The picker's × takes margins that give back what it grew by, as a phone sheet's × does (`thumbInLine`), so the × is drawn where it was and the title row is 22 px where it was 21.6.
- The Send window is shared, so People → Contracts gets the same two presses on a phone.

## The company's own page

A supply house's window and the Customer page draw a company's papers with `PartyPapers.tsx`, a list of its own. It does not share the tab's press. Its signed name has carried a 44 px floor on a phone since v2.8162, with no test on it. It now reads `paperNamePress`, so the two lists hold one rule, and its smoke holds the floor. Its markup is the same at both widths as before.

## Left for a layout call

- **A job's name inside a line's sentence** (the office's green link, *J1042 · Alder Court*): 16 px. It sits in the middle of a sentence that wraps, right under the name press. A 44 px box there would either break the sentence or lie over the name press above it, and the two open different things. It wants a line of its own, which is a layout call.
- **Pipeline and Estimates in the foot's sentence**: 16 px each, two links in running words.
- **The picker's find box** (37.4) and **the Send window's four boxes** (37.5): fields, not presses. Their height is the windows' own rhythm.
- **The chips could be 32 px.** `phonePress.ts` keeps a 32 px floor for a filter pill a tech presses once a week. The brief asked for a fingertip here, so the chips took 44. The office's five rows of chips now take 244 px of a phone where they took 163, and an estimator's three take 146 where they took 98. A single sliding row would give that back, and is a layout call.
- A link name on a phone is now one box, so a long one drops under its tag whole where it used to wrap beside it (*Statement of work · Electrical*). A button name always did.
