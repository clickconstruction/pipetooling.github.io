# v2.7816 — Lien window: Record service… is now Enter mail numbers… (2026-11-18)

The third change of the owner's pass over a filed lien's copies on 2026-11-18. v2.7798 gave every door on the Lien desk the name of the stage the copies are at, **Print the copies to mail** and then **Enter mail numbers**. The Lien window's own form for the second half still read **Record service…**, so a desk door and the form it led to had different names for one act. He said yes to renaming it.

**The form (`LIEN_SERVE_FORM_WORDS` in `src/lib/jobs/lienServeStage.ts`).**

| Was | Is |
| --- | --- |
| **Record service…**, the press that opens the form on the affidavit tab | **Enter mail numbers…** |
| *Record service of the filed copy (due …)*, the form's heading | *Enter mail numbers for the filed copies (mail by …)* |
| *Record service*, a phone's sheet title | *Enter mail numbers* |
| *Served on* | *Mailed on*, as in *Filed liens: copies to mail* |
| **Record service**, the save while the day is not on record | **Record the mailing**, as the notices' run names its save |
| *Service recorded…*, the toast | *Mailing recorded…* |

**Save the mail receipts**, the save once the day is on record, is unchanged. Nothing about what the form writes changed: the day goes on `served_at` and each copy's number and receipt on `sends`.

**The doors into it.**
- The window's next step (`lienWindowNextStep`) read *Record the service*. It reads the stage now, **Print the copies to mail** or **Enter mail numbers**, and lands on that half: the print press, or the form open at the owner's number.
- The Legal desk's door to a filed lien (`legalPaperNextDoor`) reads **Enter mail numbers…**.
- The Dashboard's card for a lien that still owes its copies cannot tell whether they are printed, so its press reads **Open the Lien window** and its line says to print the copies and enter their mail numbers there.
- The help sheet that prints in front of an affidavit, and the line under **Print the copies to mail**, name the press the new way.

**Left as it is, on purpose.** `src/lib/jobs/lienServiceProof.ts` is copied to the `dev-mcp` server (`scripts/edge-kernels.mjs`), so its words are not touched here: the toast the window shows now comes from `lienMailingSavedWords`, and the server's own messages still say *Service recorded* and *Record service…* until its next deploy takes them up.
