# v2.5443 — Messages: text or email a job's customer from the job (2026-10-13)

Residential Messages R2 PR 2, the job window's box (`to-dos/gc-mode/messages/RESIDENTIAL_BUILD_PLAN.md` → R2 on `spike/gc-mode`). It stands on cut 3's reply box (#5497) and quick replies (#5500), and on R2 PR 3a's `contact_for_customer` (#5608). No migration and no edge function.

- **The kinds row**: the job window's note composer (`JobThreadNotesPanel`) gains **Note · Text · Email** over it, through one optional slot, `customerBox`. `DetailJobModal` passes the slot for the office team only (`jobBoxOffered`, `src/lib/messages/jobBox.ts`).
  - **Note** is the composer as it was: the crew's note in `jobs_ledger_thread_notes`, with *Set % complete* and the stamps.
  - **Text** and **Email** draw the customer box in its place.
  - Job Mode, Quickfill and the crew pass no slot, so their composer is unchanged. A tech's door is R5's.
- **The customer box** (`src/components/jobs/JobCustomerBox.tsx`) opens the customer's own thread through `contact_for_customer`, made when there is none. It reads the person whole with `loadContact` (`messagesIo.ts`, new, beside `loadCustomerContact`) and draws cut 3's `ReplyBox`.
  - The box takes two new optional props. `job` ties every reply to the job and names it under **Tie to** with no picker. `kinds` offers only the kind the panel's row chose, with no row of its own.
  - The quick replies, the count, the send window and the sends are the box's. An email goes through `messages-email` under the plumbing letterhead. A text shows its wait words until the phone is switched on (`TEXTS_ON`).
  - A send asks the window to read its activity again (`threadNotes.reload`), which cut 10's feed (#5532) can hook for the customer's lines.
- **Orange in the job window**: a text or an email to a customer wears `CUSTOMER_LOOK` and `CUSTOMER_BAR` (`reply/kindLook.ts`, which now also holds the box's `KIND_LOOK` and `RING`). These are cut 10's `customerOut` tint and bar. The thread window's look is unchanged, since only `job` turns it on.
- **A job with no customer** says *This job has no customer yet. Add one in Edit Job to text or email them.* A thread that cannot be opened says why.
- **Left to cut 10**: the Customer chip on a line and its **Open the thread with Dana ›** are #5532's, not rebuilt here.
- **Docs**: the guide `text-a-customer-from-the-job` (new). `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` gains the job window's paragraph after cut 7a's. The Glossary is held for the consolidation, and the maps wait for R9.
- **Tests**: `jobBox.test.ts` (5), `JobCustomerBox.render.test.tsx` (5), `JobThreadNotesPanel.customerBox.render.test.tsx` (2). #5497's and #5500's reply box smokes pass unchanged.
