# v2.7455 — Supply houses: Record a payment, with the day, the amount, how it was sent and its number (2026-11-16)

PR 2 of the supply house payments train (the owner's ask of 2026-11-16; the ground is v2.7450). Apply Payment ticked bills and pasted a link. A bill then said paid, and nothing said what was sent, how, or which bills one transfer covered.

## What changes

- **Record a payment** takes the place of **Make Payment** on a house's card, on the phone screen and at the foot of an aging cell's window (**Record a payment ›**). It is the People pay window's pieces with the house's open bills under them.
- **Bills first, the amount second.** Ticking a bill adds it into **Amount sent**. Typing the amount ticks the oldest bills it pays in full. The button stays dead with its reason until every dollar sent is on a bill.
- **How it was sent** is a chip: Card, ACH, Check, Cash, Other, or **Say later**. The chips open on how the house was paid last, and a box takes that method's number.
- **Tick everything due by** the house's next pay day, **Fill oldest first** and **Clear** sit over the bills.
- **The press names what it records**: *Record $5,000.00 on 4 bills*, with *details later* when the method waits.
- **A paid row names its payment**: *paid 11/12 · ACH 88213 · one of 4 bills on $5,000.00*. A bill paid before payments were rows reads its paid day alone, as it did.
- **See the payment** stands where Mark unpaid stood on a bill a recorded payment paid. Its window lists every bill the payment touched, what waits on account, and **Remove this payment…**, which asks first and says what it does to those bills. The database refuses a by-hand "not paid" on such a bill, since that followed by Mark paid would count the money twice.
- **The invoice's Edit window** locks the status and the paid day of a bill a recorded payment paid, and says where to change it.
- **Mark paid** on a row is unchanged for the person. Since v2.7450 it writes a payment for what the bill has open, with the day and no method.
- **A whole bill or none, for now.** Part payments, credit memos as lines and money kept on account are in the kernel and the window behind one switch, and come on in the next PR, when the aging table and Held for suppliers read a bill's open balance.

## Code

- Kernels in `src/lib/materials/payments/`: `supplyPayment.ts` (the methods, what is open on a bill, what a payment holds on account, how a paid row names its payment, a payment's window), `supplyPaymentWindow.ts` (the window's state and plan, in cents), `supplyPaymentsIo.ts` (a house's payments in two small reads that skip the payments from before, the one write through `record_supply_house_payment`, and the removal).
- `RecordSupplyPaymentModal` and `SupplyPaymentWindow` in `src/components/materials/`. `SupplyHousesTab` lost the inline Apply Payment modal, its five states and `applyPaymentUpdate`.
- The guide *record a payment to a supply house* (the slug is still `mark-supply-house-bills-paid`).
