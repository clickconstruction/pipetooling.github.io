# v2.4240 — Submittals: enter an approval the GC gave outside the app, all at once and on the day they gave it (2026-09-30)

Wendi, on BP375's procurement log: every row read *Not shared*, but the GC had accepted the submittal before this part of the app existed, and the log has no control for it. The one door was the row editor's *Their call · on behalf of a reviewer* — one row at a time, and stamped with the moment it was typed, so **Released** would have read today on fourteen rows. Grace: "build both" — a day on the entry, and one entry for the whole submittal.

- [`enteredDecisions.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/enteredDecisions.ts):
  - `enteredDecisionAt(on, now, todayYmd)` — the instant an entered call is recorded at. Today or blank is now; an earlier day is that day at noon UTC, which is the same calendar day in the company's zone (the row's date) and in the log's ten-character slice (**Released**).
  - `enteredOnProblem(on, todayYmd)` — a day after today, before 2000 (`ENTERED_ON_MIN`, a year typed short) or unreadable is refused with its sentence; a refused day never reaches the record.
  - `rowsToApproveAll(items)` — every row with no call and a product; a row that carries a call keeps it, a Missing row has nothing to approve.
  - `enteredEntryBody(…, datedOn)` — the thread line ends *· dated Sep 12, 2026* when the call was made on an earlier day.
- [`reviewerPick.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/reviewerPick.ts) + [`SubmittalReviewerPicker.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/SubmittalReviewerPicker.tsx): "whose call" — a person on the room, or a new one with a name, an email and a role — lifted out of the row editor so both doors ask it the same way.
- [`SubmittalItemEditDialog.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/SubmittalItemEditDialog.tsx): a date box beside Approved / Revise / Rejected (*The day of their call*, today to start, never later). `EnteredChoice.on` rides along only when the day is not today.
- [`SubmittalApproveAllDialog.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/SubmittalApproveAllDialog.tsx) (new): *They approved Rev N* — what the entry covers and what it leaves, who approved it, *Approved on*, their note, **Approve N rows**.
- [`BidsSubmittalsTab.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/BidsSubmittalsTab.tsx):
  - `roomAndReviewerFor(choice)` — the room minted if the bid has none (nothing is shared by that) and the reviewer found or added; the row save and the new entry both call it.
  - `approveAll(choice)` — one `UPDATE … WHERE id IN (…) AND review_decision IS NULL … RETURNING id` per 100 rows, so a row the reviewer answered in the room a moment ago keeps that answer; then one system line on the thread and one `decided` event, both marked `whole: true` (and `decided_on` when backdated). Nothing written: *Every row already has a call.*
  - Two doors on the newest revision while `rowsToApproveAll` is not empty: **They approved all of it…** under *6 · Their call*, and a quiet line over the procurement log — *N rows have no call from the reviewer yet, so they are not released. Approved outside the app?* **Enter their approval…**
- No migration and no edge function: the same columns the row editor already wrote (`review_decision`, `reviewed_at`, `decision_source = 'entered'`, `decision_entered_by`).
- Guide *build a submittal package*; `docs/BIDS_SYSTEM.md` and `docs/GLOSSARY.md` amended where they said an entered call needs a shared revision and that Released is the room's approval.

Left as it was: a draft that takes its approval this way is still a draft — the strip's Package and Share pills stay where they were, and only Procure lights. Not built: a way to take a whole entry back in one press (Edit → *clear it* on each row does it).

Run live in dev on BP398 *ZZ Test* (Rev 3, a draft, one row): the row's entered approval was cleared, the log read *Not shared* and the line over it offered the door; the dialog, dated 09/17 in Dana Whitfield's name, put the row back to *Approved · Dana Whitfield · entered by Robert · Sep 17*, the log to *Approved 09/17 · Released 09/17*, and the thread line and the event landed with `whole` and `decided_on`. Test data left in prod: that one extra thread line and `decided` event on the ZZ bid's room, dated 2026-09-30; the row's `reviewed_at` is now noon UTC on Sep 17 where it was 05:47 UTC. Not run live: a reviewer who is not on the room yet, more than one row, and the row editor's date box (tests only).
