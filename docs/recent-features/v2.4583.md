# v2.4583 — Lien desk: it opens on Next up (2026-10-05)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. PR 2 of 5 for punch list #82 ([`to-dos/lien-desk-next-up/`](../../to-dos/lien-desk-next-up/README.md)); PR 1 was the kernel.

## What changed

- **A sixth tab, first in the row: Next up · N.** [`LienDeskNextUp.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskNextUp.tsx) draws [`lienNextUp.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienNextUp.ts): every notice, affidavit and retainage paper in a state that asks for an act, a filed lien not yet served, a sent notice at letter two's day, and a GC with several notices ready as one row. Two groups, *Needs you now* (late, or within seven days) and *Coming up*, each in date order. A tag for the kind, the job, the state in a few words, the day, one button.
- **A row's button opens the pane that exists** (`actOnNextUp` in [`LienDeskModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskModal.tsx)): the Notices tab on the row's pile with the job selected, the Affidavits or Retainage tab on the job, the run window, or the job's Lien window on its Mechanic's lien tab. Nothing is written from the list. A row this viewer has no move on (the office on a notice awaiting the leader, any read-only role) reads **Open**.
- **A plain open lands on it.** [`JobsStagesTab.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsStagesTab.tsx) passes `initialKind='next'` where it passed `'calendar'`. A door that names a job, a pile or a kind lands where it did, the `?liendesk=1` link included.
- **On a phone** the same rows are cards with a full-width button.
- The list reads nothing of its own: it is a memo over the queues the desk already loads, plus the affidavits in `filingsByJob` that are filed and not served.

## Checked in the app

Prod data, dev login, read only. Next up showed 22 rows. Per state against the Notices tab's piles: Find the owner 3 = *Needs the owner* 3; Draft notice 10 = *To draft* 10; Approve 1 = *Awaiting approval* 1; two Send rows and one Send the run row = *Ready to send* 11. **Find the owner** on job 804 landed on Notices with *Needs the owner* pressed and the job in the pane. At 375 px the cards fit with no sideways scroll.
