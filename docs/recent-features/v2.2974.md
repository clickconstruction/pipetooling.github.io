# v2.2974 — Jobs → Team: the crew board, planned against clocked (2026-09-07)

Owner, 2026-09-06: with man hours on Pipeline, the Team Labor tab had become redundant; the pick was a jobs-by-days crew board with the dispatch plan alongside, and the tab renamed **Team** to sit beside **Subs** as its in-house twin. Mockup artifact `cb56adf2` (two passes). First PR of a train: this one is the board and ledger, read-only; actions (link, split day, add session, not coming in, move to plan) follow, then acknowledgements + the ran-long threshold setting. Client only — no migration, no edge function.

## What shipped

- **Kernel** [`src/lib/teamBoard.ts`](../../src/lib/teamBoard.ts) (+11 tests): `buildTeamBoard({ days, sessions, blocks, subSheets, labels, officeJobId, payFlags, ranLong })` → cells (one person × one target × one day: plan windows from `job_schedule_blocks`, clock windows from `clock_sessions`), `jobRows` (none → busiest field jobs → office) and `personRows` (with a pay-config target: salaried 8/weekday, hourly = planned), `dayTotals`, `summary`, `exceptions` (`unlinked` with the day's dispatch block as suggestion, `miss`, `over`, `unplanned`; newest day first). Cell kinds: `ok` / `unplanned` / `miss` / `unlinked` / `office`; **ran long** = clocked > plan × 1.5 and > 1.5 h over (`DEFAULT_RAN_LONG_RULE`). Time of day is the company wall clock (`wallClockHours`, `APP_CALENDAR_TZ`); cross-midnight sessions clamp to 24 on the clock-in day; the track axis is 6 am → midnight. `teamCellStanding` names the Subs-vocabulary pill; `teamLedgerRows` orders the ledger.
- **Loader** [`fetchTeamBoardWeek.ts`](../../src/lib/fetchTeamBoardWeek.ts) (four reads: live closed sessions incl. pending, blocks, `people_labor_jobs` dated in the week, `list_people_pay_flags`; labels via the ledger-prefix formatters; office job id from app settings) + hook `useTeamBoardWeek`.
- **Tab** [`JobsTeamTab`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsTeamTab.tsx) + `components/jobs/team/*`: week nav (‹ › · This week), **Board / Ledger** segment, **Rows: jobs / people**, Hide Office, Only exceptions, legend; summary strip (field time planned vs clocked bars, on-plan %, not on a job, planned-no-clock); `TeamBoardTable` (sticky job column with planned/clocked week bars; `TeamChip` with the `TeamTimeTrack` under every chip; violet `TeamSubSheetChip` for sub sheets, attached to their job by HCP number); `TeamLedgerTable` (date · job · person · mini track · plan · clocked · hours · plan h · Δ · payroll · where it stands); `TeamExceptionsDrawer`. Chips and drawer take an optional `renderActions` (empty in this PR).
- **Jobs.tsx**: tab label **Team Labor → Team** (key / URL slug `combined-labor` unchanged); the tab renders `JobsTeamTab` instead of `CrewJobsBlock`; the `?teamLaborJob=` deep link (Edit Job → labor cost panel) lands on and flashes the job's row. Quickfill's `CrewJobsSection` keeps `CrewJobsBlock` unchanged.
- Render test `JobsTeamTab.render.test.tsx` (table-aware stub, "today" pinned inside the sample week).
- Docs: guide `read-the-team-board` (new); `put-clocked-hours-on-the-right-job` and `quickfill` re-pointed; `JOBS_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`, `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`, `GLOSSARY.md` renamed the tab.
