# v2.5133 — GC projects: the money team reads the trades' money (2026-10-09)

GC mode's Owner Billing O9. Migration `20261010050000_gc_money_reads_trades` (doc: `docs/migrations/20261010050000_gc_money_reads_trades.md`). No function deploy. The plan is `to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/owner-billing-o9.md` on `spike/gc-mode`, co-signed by Building (gc 10), the Board (gc 2) and the Portal (gc 3).

## What shipped

- **The money team reads the seven tables a trade's line bills from**: the Board's `gc_sows` and `gc_sow_lines`, Building's `gc_draws`, `gc_draw_lines`, `gc_sow_line_reports` and `gc_change_order_trade_sends`, and the Portal's `gc_back_charges`. One `<table>_money_read` policy each, `FOR SELECT` on `gc_money_team()`, beside each table's dev policy. Writing stays a dev's until each lane's door. Since U6b (v2.5121) a leader's or the controller's Bill the customer had drafted every trade at $0 (*Their statement of work is not signed yet*), and Money's job cash read the trades as paid nothing; a dev's read their work.
- **The page reads the draws for the money team** (`loadDraws` in `GcProjects.tsx`: `canUseGcBuilding || canSeeGcMoney`). The Draws window and Closeout keep Building's two gates, so they stay a dev's.
- **The statement of work card reads only** for anyone who cannot write it: **Send to their portal to sign** is for `canUseGcBoardWrites` (`GC_BOARD_WRITE_TEAM` in `src/lib/gc/access.ts`, a dev until the award door, call W), and the card says *A dev sends it to their portal to sign.* (`GcTradeSow`'s new `canSend`).
- **`doors.ts` names a reader beside the door**: `GcTableDoor.reads`, set to `money` on the seven. `doors.test.ts` reads the door from a table's non-`SELECT` policies and the reader from all of them.
- **Guide**: `award-a-trade-on-a-gc-project` says the owner, the leaders and the controller read the statement of work. **Docs**: `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` (the Owner Billing door holds the fact; Building's, the Board's and the Portal's lines point to it), `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`, `BILLING_FLOWS.md`.

## Not done
