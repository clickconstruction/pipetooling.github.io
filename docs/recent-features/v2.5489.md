# v2.5489 — Messages: quick replies in the language a customer reads, once Spanish is on (2026-10-13)

Residential R3, the strip's Spanish, by Customer communication helper 10, directed by cc. It is a small PR on R3's screens, PR B (#5617), and wires R8 · 16's language (#5589: `Contact.lang`, `customerLang`) into the reply box. It is held behind the owner's one switch for Spanish anywhere, `PORTAL_SPANISH_ON` in `src/lib/gc/portalI18n.ts`, which is off "until a native speaker reads the words". So nothing changes on screen until that line flips, and the strip has no switch of its own. No migration and no function change.

- **One home for the pick**: `lineWords(line, lang)` (`src/lib/quickReplies/spanish.ts`) gives a line's Spanish when the language is Spanish and the line has it. Otherwise it gives the English. It is `phone.ts`'s `wordsFor`, read from a line that may predate R3's columns.
- **The reply box** (`reply/ReplyBox.tsx`) reads the person's language with `customerLang(contact)`. That is Spanish only for a contact whose customer reads Spanish, and only while the switch is on. It passes the language to the strip and to *Find a reply*. A press fills the box with the line in that language, its blanks filled as before.
- **The strip and Find a reply** show each line in that language. While it is Spanish, the strip's count reads "3 for customers · in Spanish" (`IN_SPANISH`).
- **The narrow box** (`qrGroups`) finds a line by its Spanish as well as its English.
- **A line with no Spanish** goes in English to a Spanish reader, with no mark on the strip. The editor's **no es** is where the office fills the gap.
- **Guide**: `edit-the-quick-replies-for-the-phone` says a customer who reads Spanish gets it once Spanish is switched on.
