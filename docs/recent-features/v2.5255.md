# v2.5255 — Messages: the step card and the reminders from a flow (2026-10-12)

Messages wave 1, cluster 5: the step card's words and Follow up's *Nudges from the flow*, lifted from the prototype v1.178 (branch `spike/gc-mode`, `to-dos/gc-mode/messages/prototype.html`: `stepCardHtml`, `offCardHtml`, `portalSoFar`, `paperAct`, `factWords`, `whoNow`, `nudgeRows`, `nudgeNow`, `holdNudge`). Built on cluster 4's types (v2.5254). Kernels only: no screen, no table, no migration, no edge function.

- **`src/lib/flows/nudges.ts`**: `PersonFlow`, one person's `FlowReading` with what the reading does not hold (their quiet days, our last message, the thread's court and what we owe, their jobs' names, the Remind now and Hold until marks, a paper's sends, their portal's reads, the team's notes); `nudgeDue`, the one rule for whether a step's reminder is on Follow up today, with its reason; `reminderWords`, the card's Reminder line; `nudgeRows`, the rows (`NudgeRow` with its `NudgeStep`s), and `worstFirst`, their order; `nudgeNow`, `holdNudge`, `nextMonday`; `paperToAsk`, `historyWords`, `lapsedWhy`, `paperSigned`, `paperName`; the company-calendar day words. Free of the browser, for the morning dispatcher in cut 5.
- **One row per person, never one per job** (the lead's rule): Follow up is one queue, and Greg on two bids is still one call. A row carries each of their steps on now that has a reminder, on every job, worst first (due today, then the most days late, then the soonest day); their lapsed steps fold into it; a step of theirs that someone else's waits on joins it instead of making a second row. The nudge is one message: it names the worst step and any other due today, late, or due within the week (`NudgeStep.named`). Cluster 2's queue reads the rows as they come.
- **Change orders nudge** (the lead's ruling): an open change order is their move with the three-day reminder the owner drew, so it joins the customer's row and its card shows a Reminder line. Where Follow up already shows it on a portal row, the portal row wins: the queue passes `NudgeOptions.skip`, and the round stays out before the line is written. A change order's round key (`NudgeStep.round`) is its `gc_change_orders` row id, the same id as cluster 4's `RoundStamp.key` and cluster 8's `PaperView.id`.
- **`src/lib/flows/card.ts`**: `stepCard`, the card as a model (a title, the status sentence as words with the late part marked, then the sections that apply: what we need or have or each round, until then, next, the reminder, so far), each press with its id, what it acts on and its hover card (`CARD_TIPS`); `offCard`; `portalSoFar`; `factWords`; `whoseMove` (the prototype's `whoNow`, with the court and what we owe passed in).
- **`src/lib/flows/demoPeople.fixture.ts`**: the prototype's eight demo people as readings built by hand on `SEED_FLOWS`, until `readFlow` lands. The tests hold seventeen cards line for line against what the prototype draws, and its two rows word for word.
- **Changed from the prototype, by the lead's review**: the card's Reminder line and the rows read one rule, so a lapsed paper's line says the three quiet days its row uses; a lapsed step folds into the person's row once, where the prototype could name it twice; Remind now and Hold until Mon show only on a step with a reminder; a hold ends on its Monday, and Remind now ends when we next send them anything; a wait of one day reads "1 day"; Until then names the waited step's paper instead of always "the certificate"; "Not signed." and "went to sign" stay off the invitation, the bid, the schedule and the pay application, and Send it on those says the step is done once it goes; quiet days count their last word (cluster 4's `quietDays`), and a reminder also waits out our own last message, so it never doubles one we sent.
- **Changed for the real data**: a day or a name inside a line comes from the stamps (`FactReading`, `RoundReading`), where the prototype had demo words typed in ("He called, Mon Sep 14" reads "Mon Sep 14"); a round on now with no day reads "since" the day it opened; a person who has not written yet reads so; the off card reads the office's own word (`contacts.off_*`) and the lane's ended fact; four hover cards the plain-words rules failed (Send it, File one we were handed and Remind them over twenty words, See their page with a semicolon) are rewritten with their meaning kept, and a test holds every card and hover card to `plainWordsFailures`; "the our contract" reads "the contract"; a step aside (change orders) reads by its rounds whatever state the reading gives it.
