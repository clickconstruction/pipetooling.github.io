# v2.8391 — Job Summary: search a job's supply house invoices and copy a link to one (2026-11-23)

Punch list #431, PR 1 of seven. The owner, from a job's **Parts Cost** → **Invoices from Supply Houses**: he wants to flip through a job's invoices without a new tab each, search a long list, and share one with a press that copies a link and says *Link copied to clipboard*. This PR is the list: the search box, the share press, small cents and the short date. The viewer that opens a bill in place is PR 2. No migration and no edge function.

## What was there

One function drew the list in the five places the Job Summary shows it: four columns, each invoice number a press that opened `supply_house_invoices.link`, a Google Drive link the office pastes, in a new tab. A bill with no link fell back to the supply house's website, so its number opened the supplier's home page as if it were the bill. Amounts were whole strings and every date carried its year. There was no way to narrow the list or to hand one invoice to a teammate.

## What changed

- **The list is a component**, `JobSummarySupplyInvoiceList` in `src/components/jobs/`, over a pure kernel, `src/lib/jobs/supplyInvoiceList.ts`. `JobsJobSummaryTab.tsx` hands it the rows through `renderJobSummarySupplyInvoices`, a new function under the old one. The old one still says the loading and empty words; its table is no longer reached and goes in a later PR of this train, once another open PR has left those lines.
- **The share press.** For the office (a dev, a leader, an assistant, the controller) a link icon sits left of each invoice number. It copies `/files/bills/<invoice id>?job=<job id>`, the app's own lasting link to a bill, and the toast reads *Link copied to clipboard*. A browser that refuses the clipboard is handed the link to copy by hand. The press is 44 px on a phone.
- **The link opens a Drive-linked bill.** `/files/bills/<id>` knew only a bill dropped on the Add invoice window, where the app keeps its own copy. Most bills are a Drive link on the row, so `billFileTarget` in `src/lib/files/fileOpenIo.ts` now goes to that link when there is no copy of ours. The row is still read under the reader's own policy on `supply_house_invoices`, so anyone outside the office reads *This file is not one you can open.* `billLinkHref` (`src/lib/materials/bills/billLink.ts`) holds a pasted link to http or https and refuses a map link.
- **Nobody outside the office gets the press.** A primary, a helper or a sub on the job's team sees the list through `get_invoice_allocation_lines_for_jobs` and cannot read a bill's own row, so a link copied by them would open for nobody they hand it to as them. They keep the list as it was.
- **The search.** A list of eight or more rows (`SUPPLY_INVOICE_SEARCH_FROM`) grows a box. Every word must be met by the house, the invoice number, the date or the amount on the job or on the whole bill. A number is matched with or without its spaces and marks (*bw7731* finds *BW 7731-02*). A typed day is that day and never loose digits: *nov 1* does not find the 16th, and the 16 of *nov 16* does not find $1,160.00. A month is every day of it, and a month typed alone also finds a house whose name starts with it. The count beside the box says how many are left and their total; a chip per supply house narrows to one house. A search that finds nothing offers **Clear the search**.
- **Small cents** through `AmountSmallCents`, on each row and on the search's total.
- **The short date** (`invoiceDayWords`): the month and the day, and the year only when it is not this year, by the company's calendar. The printed cost breakdown keeps its full date.
- **A bill with no PDF** reads *no PDF* beside a plain number, with *supplier's site* as a press of its own where the house has one. For the office it has no share press, since the link would have nothing to open until the viewer lands.
- **The list keeps its presses to itself.** It sits inside rows and folds that open on a press or a key, so a press or a key inside the list stops there.
