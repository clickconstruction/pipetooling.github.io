# v2.8552 — Accounts Receivable: after a case is closed, the next case opens (2026-11-24)

Punch list #76, found on its last live walk on 2026-11-24. The card closed the same day, and its Closed line in `to-dos/README.md` names this. The owner's pick of that day, on the question *should it land on the next case instead?*: **Next case**. No migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

Closing a case under **Came back** cleared the pane's selection. The pane then picked the first row of the list by itself, and the first row is a deposit when there is one. On the walk that was a real deposit with **Apply** live, one stray press from the close.

## What changed

- `src/lib/jobs/arReturnCase.ts`: `arCaseAfterClose(shownIds, closedId)`, new and pure. It answers the case that followed the closed one in the list as drawn, the one before it when the closed case was the last, and null when no other case is left.
- `src/components/jobs/BankPaymentsModal.tsx`: `landAfterCaseClose` sets the pane to that answer after each of the three presses that close a case: **Close it as settled** or **Close it as not coming** under **More**, **Put the bill back**, and the close of a case by the bigger payment that holds its new check.
- When the answer is null the pane holds nothing. The list's read and its filter used to pick the first row whenever nothing was selected. They now leave it empty until a person presses a row or the window is opened again (`holdNothingRef`).
- `ArReturnCasePane` carries its case's id on its root (`data-case-id`), for the test.
- The guide *match bank deposits to bills* says where the pane lands.

## What did not change

A deposit applied, a deposit closed out, and the window's first open still pick as before. Only a case's close lands differently.

## Tests

- `arReturnCase.test.ts`: the next case, the one before when it was the last, nothing when none is left, and the first when a search hid the closed one.
- `BankPaymentsModal.returnCases.render.test.tsx`: after **Not coming…** closes a case, the pane shows another case.
