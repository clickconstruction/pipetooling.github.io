# v2.7796 — Lien desk: a window that has closed has its own bin at the foot of each column (2026-11-18)

PR 2 of the closed end on the Lien desk's Board, the owner's pick of 2026-11-18 (*"Build A"*, a closed end at the foot of each column). PR 1 (v2.7793) is the kernel. This one draws the two bins and changes where a late affidavit stands. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## Before

- **Missed** was a small bin on a column's heading line, beside the column's name, drawn only while a job was in it (v2.7714).
- An affidavit stood in Missed only when nobody had drafted it. A paper that was sent, approved or held kept its pile when its day passed. An approved one stood red in *Ready to file*, and *Print N for notarization* counted it.
- A notice whose every month had closed kept its Missed chip after someone recorded the months closed, until the months aged off the desk.

## What changed

- **A closed end at the foot of each column.** `LienBoardBin` gained `closed`, and `beside` is Held's alone. `LienBoard`'s `column` draws the closed bin after the flow (`data-lien-board-closed`), so no arrow runs into it. The bin is dashed with no fill (`data-closed`), and it is drawn only while a job is in it (`lienBoardBinsShown`).
- **Named for what closed** (`lienBoardStageWords`, from `LIEN_CLOSED_BIN_WORDS`): *Window closed · no notice* under **Sent**, *Lien day passed · not filed* under **Served**. `LIEN_BOARD_WORDS` no longer holds *Missed*. The property card's words for a missed pile follow, since `lienBoardPileWords` reads the same names.
- **A closed chip is quiet**: grey, dashed, never red and never at risk. A notice a late notice can still save carries `lateNotice` and is amber (`lienLateNoticeStillCounts`: the job's lien day is known and ahead). The hover says which window closed.
- **An affidavit not filed whose day has passed is Missed, whatever its status** (`pileFor` in `lienDeskAffidavits.ts`). The stored status does not change and nothing is voided. A filing recorded on the job, or a later last day of work, puts the paper back by itself. The batch print, the leader's approval and By paper read the pile, so none of them counts a paper past its day any more.
- **A notice leaves the closed bin once its months are recorded** (`lienNoticeWindowClosed`): a job in pile `missed` has a chip only while a closed month is unrecorded. By paper's Missed lens still lists it.
- **The first tile** no longer counts an affidavit past its day among the papers to file, and its *N are past their day* line no longer names it. Those papers are in the closed bin.
- A bin's name may now run six words with its mark, up from five.

## What it does not do

- A closed chip still opens the paper, as a Missed chip did. The window that says what closed and offers the answers is PR 3, with *Answer N ›* on the bin's title line.
- A closed lien day still leaves the desk seven days later, because the affidavit windows stop listing the job. Keeping it until someone answers takes a migration of its own.
