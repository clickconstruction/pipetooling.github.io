# v2.8080 — Precise Location: Show the steps is a full-height press, and the two numbers stay whole on a small phone (2026-11-20)

Punch list #402, found by the audit of the card. Client only: no migration and no function. Two faults in one component, `PreciseLocationLine.tsx`, both seen in a headless walk at 320 px.

## What was wrong

1. **Show the steps was 32 px tall.** On a task's **Where is it?** sheet the steps to the switch wait behind one press (`foldSteps`, v2.8011). That press was a `summary` with 0.35rem of padding, so it measured 334 × 32 px on a 390 px phone and 264 × 32 px on a 320 px one. The house floor for a press is 44 px.
2. **The two values broke mid-phrase at 320 px.** The box that sets the phone's two numbers side by side was a grid of `auto 1fr`. The label *With Precise Location on* took its whole width first, and the value beside it was squeezed: *within 2* / *miles* and *within 20* / *ft*. A value is the thing a person reads there, so it should be the last thing to break.

## What changed

- The press is 44 px tall (`minHeight` and a line height of 44 px). The margin above the fold is gone, so the folded box grows by about 12 px and not 18.
- The grid is `fit-content(100%) minmax(min-content, max-content)`. The value column takes what its words need, and the label gives way. At 320 px the label wraps to *With Precise* / *Location on* and both values stay on one line. At 390 px and on a desk nothing moves: the value still sits right after the longest label.

Every screen that draws the line gets the second change: the walk, **Put the dot where I am**, a task's **Use where I am** and the box map.

## Checked

- `PreciseLocationLine.render.test.tsx`: the press's height and the grid's columns are pinned (13 pass).
- A headless Chrome as Safari on an iPhone, with a stand-in for the phone's reading and every write refused. On the task sheet at 320 px the press measured 44 px, the map under it kept 274 px (it had 259 px, since the box is two lines shorter), and **Use this place** stayed on screen with the steps open. On the walk at 320 px both values stayed whole. At 390 px the box looked as it did.

## Not done

- With the steps open on a 320 px phone the task sheet's map is down to its 60 px floor, under a sentence that says to drag it. Closing the steps gives it back. Left as it is.
- **＋ Add the way in** on the same sheet is 32 px tall, and **Move the pin by hand** on the box map is 36 px. Both are older than this card.
