# v2.7236 — Lien desk on a phone: the lists keep their room, and no tab slides (2026-11-13)

Punch list #352, PR 5 of eight, on the owner's pick of direction A, the one list. PR 0 to PR 3 are on main; PR 4 is in the merge queue and this PR does not need it.

## What was wrong

Measured on the dev server against live data, before this PR:

- **The Notices list at 320 wide showed no row.** The list keeps every pile's title on screen: the ones passed stack at the top, the ones ahead at the bottom (v2.4672). Five titles are 150 px. The list's window on a small phone is 140 px. At 390 wide the titles took 150 of 359 px.
- **The kinds of paper slid sideways at 320 wide**, 349 px of tabs in 283. The names they are about to get (*Monthly notices*, *Lien affidavits*, *Retainage notices*) are 408 px in 338 at 375 wide. A fourth view, *By job*, makes the views' row slide at 320 too.
- **Small presses.** The find box was 32 px tall with 13 px words, and an iPhone zooms the whole page when a field under 16 px takes the focus. The pills over the deadlines list were 36 px.

## The change

- **One title pins.** `src/lib/jobs/lienPhoneLists.ts` (new kernel), `lienPileHeadPlace`: a computer's titles stack as they did; a phone pins one at the top, the pile being read, and the others scroll with their rows. A phone's title is 40 px tall with 12 px words, and the presses inside it (*Send the run*, *Deliveries*, *show every pile*) are sized to match. `lienPileScrollTop` and `lienPileReached` carry the press on a title and the lit title for both.
- **Tabs wrap, never slide.** `LIEN_PHONE_TAB` is the phone's tab for both rows: it takes its words' share of the row, gives room back when the row is short, and wraps its words in balance (*Notices* over *· 18*). The row stays 44 px tall. Nothing here names a tab, so the renames in flight land without touching it.
- **The find box** on a phone is 44 px tall with 16 px words, and its × is 34 px. The deadlines pills are 40 px.

The card planned more for this PR than it does. By date already has a phone layout of its own (pills, groups with their money, two-line rows with the amount), and the Notices, Affidavits and Retainage rows already carry the job, the money and the state in two or three lines; rebuilding them onto the first view's row would have changed what each list says for no measured gain. The kinds of paper stay tabs, in two lines, in place of chips: chips that wrap made the pinned header taller. The notary's print box on Affidavits stays where it is, at the top of the list where it scrolls away, because another open PR is rewriting that bar. The By job grid's own controls (two pickers at 23 px, *Print the grid* at 25) are left for the PR that renames that view.

No migration, no edge function, no change to what any press writes or who may press it.
