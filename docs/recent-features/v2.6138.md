# v2.6138 — The office's round drawn as ten workday columns (punch list #217, office-5, a prototype) (2026-10-30)

Punch list #217, *Show it, don't say it*: eight scans found 131 places where the app tells instead of shows, and a mock-up page draws each as a before/after pair for the owner to pick from. Of office-5, the Dashboard's **The office's round** card, the owner wrote on 2026-10-30: "This could potentially work, I would like to see it as a prototype." This is that prototype, shipped as a draft PR for her to look at. No migration, no edge function.

## The finding

The card read ten days of `quickfill_section_mark_events` and said one verdict over them: *No round Thu, Oct 29*, or *Today 9:51 AM · Dana · 23 stations · 8 still open · 2 questions*. A day with one stamp read as a round the same as a day with all 23, and a miss was a word, not a gap in a row of days, so the owner could not see a pattern — every Thursday, or every day short at eight stations.

## What changed

- **`roundColumns.ts`** (`src/lib/quickfill/`, new, beside `roundClose.ts`): the pure kernel from the events to the strip. `roundColumnDays` picks the last ten workdays, ending today on a workday and on Friday over a weekend; `roundColumns` gives one column a day — the stations marked that day (each once, through `roundCloseSheet`), the total (every station marked in the window, each once — the stations the round visits, with no second read for the org's hidden list), the fraction, the last mark's time, who pressed most, the still-open and question counts, `miss` (a workday with no mark; today is never a miss while it runs) and `today`. `roundColumnLabels` places the labels under the strip — the first day in full, each Friday as *Fri 23*, each miss as *Thu 29 · no round*, *today* — and lets the lesser of two neighbouring labels give way (first and miss outrank today, today outranks a Friday) so none overprints on a phone. `roundColumnTitle` is a column's hover (*Tue, Oct 27 · 9:40 AM · Dana · 21 of 23*, *Thu, Oct 29 · no round*, *Today · 9:51 AM · Dana · 9 of 23 so far*), `roundColumnsWords` the strip's own sentence for the hover and the screen reader, `roundColumnsLine` the muted line under the strip (the old headline without its counts), `roundCountChipWords` the chips' words, empty at zero so no dead chip is drawn. Eight tests in `roundColumns.test.ts`.
- **`DashboardOfficeRoundCard`**: now two shapes over one read — `OfficeRoundToday`, the card as it was, and `OfficeRoundColumns`, the prototype. The columns sit behind a per-person switch on the card (**Show the columns** / **Show today's look**, `useShapeView('office-round-columns')`, remembered in localStorage per user and off by default), so the card reads as today until a person turns it on; with `?sbs=1` on the Dashboard and a dev signed in, the card draws both one above the other, captioned **Today** and **With the columns**, on the same live marks, with no switch. The owner asked for this on 2026-10-30, to see every prototype live beside today's look. The card reads two weeks of events instead of ten days (`ROUND_COLUMNS_READ_DAYS`; ten calendar days held only eight workdays) and draws the strip under its head — a grid of ten columns in the Legal Timeline strip's shape, a past day's bar in `--text-strong`, today's in `--text-link` on a `--bg-blue-tint` wash, a miss an empty ring in `--text-red-700`, a one-station day a sliver never shorter than four percent. The head keeps the title and the state chip (*ran today* · *2 questions* · *No round yet today* · *No round Thu, Oct 29* · *Weekend*); the headline's counts are now press chips, **questions 2 ›** then **still open 8 ›**, from today's sheet while the round runs and the previous workday's before it starts, each opening Quickfill at the first of its stations (`quickfillStationHref`) with every station named on hover. The per-station door rows are gone; the close sheet on Quickfill still lists them. Under the strip, the muted line and **Open Quickfill ›** in every state. The card is a little shorter than before on a day the round ran.
- **`src/components/prototypes/SideBySide.tsx`** (new): the shared helper the #217 prototypes use — `useShapeView(key)` (the switch and the `?sbs=1` dev flag), `SideBySide` (the two captioned blocks), `ShapeSwitch` (the press), with `readShapeView` / `writeShapeView` / `shapeViewStorageKey` under them. A sibling session on the rhythm-strips branch was writing the same helper under the same names; its branch was not pushed when this card got there, so this PR carries its own copy with those names, and whichever lands second keeps one.
- **Smoke** `DashboardOfficeRoundCard.render.test.tsx`: invented marks over the ten workdays from the company's today (a full day, a short day, a miss, today in progress): today's look first, the columns after the press and remembered, the columns straight away when the switch was on, both shapes under `?sbs=1`; built from the company's today, so it holds on any weekday and over a weekend.
- **Guide** *run the office day with Quickfill*: the close-sheet paragraph describes the strip.
- **Docs**: the card's line in `DASHBOARD_SECTIONS_ARCHITECTURE.md` and the close-sheet line in `QUICKFILL_ARCHITECTURE.md`.

## What the mock-up asked for that this does not do

- **Chips that open Quickfill on all of those stations.** Quickfill's one address form names one station (`/quickfill#<sectionId>`), and its search box has no URL form, so a chip opens the first station of its set and names the rest on hover. A `?stations=` filter on the Quickfill page would finish it; that is the page's change, not the card's.
- **A hover card over a column.** The mock-up drew its own popover and moved the strip down to leave room for it; the card uses the browser's hover (`title`), as the Legal Timeline strip does, so nothing moves.
- **Twenty-three as the total.** The mock-up's *of 23* is the org's station count. The card has no read of the org's hidden list, so the total is the stations marked in the window — the stations the round visits. A station nobody has marked in two weeks is not counted against the round.

## Seeing it beside today's look

A dev server from this branch, then `/dev-login?as=1&to=/dashboard?sbs=1` (or open `/dashboard?sbs=1` once signed in). The card draws **Today** above **With the columns** on the live marks. Without `?sbs=1` the card reads as today, and **Show the columns** on it turns the prototype on for that person.
