# v2.7693 — Checklist Review: older photos of a place on the map (2026-11-17)

The owner asked for a way to scroll through older dates of the satellite view on the Review tab's map, *Where the open tasks are*. v2.7686 moved that map to Google Maps and recorded what was read about older imagery. This is the press that follows from it.

## What changed

- **Older photos**, a press on the map beside Satellite / Streets. It swaps the map for the photo archive's own view, close on one place: the dot that is open, or else the first. The dots stay.
- **A press a date** under the map, oldest first. Each is one photograph, named by the day it was taken, with a sentence that says so.
- **Back to today** returns to the map as it was (Google, or the Leaflet fallback).
- **Open this spot in Google Earth** opens Google Earth over the place. Google's older imagery is shown only there, on its own time slider.

## Where the photographs come from

Esri's World Imagery Wayback: every release of its imagery since February 2014, each its own tile layer, read with no key, as the app's view from above already is.

- **One or the other, never both.** Google's Maps Platform terms (3.2.3 (e)) keep another provider's map off and away from a Google map. The Google canvas is taken down while the archive shows, and the archive view is the Leaflet canvas (`TaskPlacesMapCanvas`, which now takes an `imagery` layer).
- **A release is not a photograph.** A release is a day Esri published the whole map, and most change nothing at a given spot. The archive is walked (`walkOlderPhotos` in `src/lib/map/olderPhotosIo.ts`): a release is asked for the spot's tile, it names the release that tile really comes from, and the walk goes on from the release before that. A tile published again unchanged keeps its size and is passed over. The test spot had fourteen photographs across about two hundred releases.
- **The day taken is not the day published.** A picture first published in 2014 was taken in 2011. The day comes from a metadata service that, measured on 2026-11-17, took between 1 and 25 seconds an answer and answered a repeated question faster. So a photograph is on the strip at once, named **Before** its publishing month, which is all that is true of it, and is renamed when its day lands. A day is asked up to three times, four at a time. A photograph whose day never comes keeps the Before name and its sentence says the archive did not say.
- **Kept.** A finished walk is kept for the visit, and on the device for a week when every photograph has its day (`older_photos_v1:<tile>`), so a second look asks nothing.
- **What leaves the browser.** The tiles the map draws, the tile's address, and the middle of that tile. Never the task, the person, the place's name or the place's own point.

## Pieces

- `src/lib/map/olderPhotos.ts` (tested): the tile math, the release list, the walk's rules, the choices and their words, the device keep, the Google Earth address.
- `src/lib/map/olderPhotosIo.ts` (tested with a stood-in archive): the three reads and the walk.
- `src/hooks/useOlderPhotos.ts`: the walk as state, with a failure that names its reason and reads again on Retry (`OfflineRetryPanel`, `idempotent`).
- `PinsMapCanvas` takes `fitMaxZoom` (Leaflet only, 15 unless said): the archive view frames one spot at 17.
