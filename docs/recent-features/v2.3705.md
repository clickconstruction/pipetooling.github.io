# v2.3705 — People spine, PR 6: pointers and homes — every account control lives in one place (2026-09-22)

The last PR of the People spine train (PR 1 = v2.3698 the roster view, PR 2 = v2.3700 Leave, PR 3 = v2.3701 Hire, PR 5 = v2.3702 the Users lenses; the planned PR 4 was already in place since July). The plan folder `to-dos/people-spine/` is retired with this PR; what it deferred lives in [`to-dos/people-spine-residuals.md`](../../to-dos/people-spine-residuals.md). Client only.

## What was wrong

With the lenses live, three doors still pointed at the old homes: the accounts table sat on Settings → People & teams beside a *Sample accounts* heading (fixtures inside the people list), the Users toolbar carried an *Accounts · dev* button into the same table, and the Your account tab held a dev-only *All salaried users* picker that duplicated what the Pay lens's *Workday…* now does. Nine guides sent people to those doors.

## The change

- **Settings → People & teams** — [`SettingsPeopleTab`](../../src/components/settings/SettingsPeopleTab.tsx): the `ActiveAccountsPanel` card is a one-line pointer to People → Users → Account (the v2.2835 pattern) with *Manage accounts…* (the app-level modal, still the place for password / name / email / merge / the archived list) and *Find duplicates…* beside it.
- **Digital twins & samples** — [`SampleAccountsCard`](../../src/components/settings/SampleAccountsCard.tsx) on the Digital twins tab (relabelled in `settingsGroups.ts`): the sample logins by role, *Create the missing samples* ([`lib/sampleAccounts.ts`](../../src/lib/sampleAccounts.ts), `create-user` with `is_sample` — which writes no roster row for them since v2.3701) and *Manage accounts…* for their switches. The Active Accounts modal keeps its sample rows at the foot for that.
- **Users tab** — the *Accounts · dev* toolbar button and phone-menu item are gone (`onOpenActiveAccounts` prop removed; `People.tsx` no longer opens the modal).
- **Your account** — [`SettingsAccountSchedulingTab`](../../src/components/settings/SettingsAccountSchedulingTab.tsx): the dev *All salaried users* picker is a one-line pointer to People → Users → Pay; its state (`allSalariedDevSectionOpen`, `devPayConfigForSalaried`, the picker rows and selection) left `useSettingsAccount` and `Settings.tsx` with it. Your own salaried block is unchanged.
- **Guides** — *archive and restore user accounts*, *invite someone to sign in*, *link an external person to an account*, *merge user accounts*, *put someone in read-only training mode*, *see the app as someone else*, *start here in the office*, *open a person's desk*: every "Active Accounts" door now names the lens, the desk, or Manage accounts…. Docs: `ACCESS_CONTROL` (sample accounts home, the supervision switch's doors), `GLOSSARY`, `SETTINGS_TABS_ARCHITECTURE`, `PEOPLE_TABS_ARCHITECTURE`.

## Verified

- `releaseNotes`, `helpGuideContent`, `settingsGroups` / `settingsSearch` suites, the People and settings render smokes; `npm run typecheck`; eslint on the touched files.
- Live, read-only, on the branch's worktree server: Settings → People & teams shows the pointer with its two buttons; Settings → System → Digital twins & samples lists the eight samples with no *Create the missing samples* button (none missing); People → Users has no Accounts · dev button; Your account shows the pointer in place of the picker. Nothing was pressed on prod data.
