# v2.7678 — Supply houses: the aging windows are their own files (2026-11-17)

The tenth cut of `src/components/SupplyHousesTab.tsx`, step 10 of the *SupplyHousesTab extraction order* in `docs/MATERIALS_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md`. A pure move: no behaviour changes.

**What moved, in two files.**

- `src/components/materials/useSupplyHouseAgingWindows.ts` is the engine: which cell is open, the open cell's jobs, accounts and reps, the move window's jobs and open asks, the two effects that read them, the Needs You door that opens the worked list, and the three writes (`sendMoveRequest`, `markMovedConfirmed`, `setInvoiceJobAcct`).
- `src/components/materials/AgingCellWindow.tsx` is the window: the 487 lines the tab drew inside an inline function while a cell was open, with the move modal and the Mark opened sheet, moved as they stood. `formatYmdLocal` came with it, since only this window used it.

The map named the engine `useSupplyMoveWindow`. It holds both windows' state, so it is `useSupplyHouseAgingWindows`.

**Why an engine and a window, and not one component.** The window is on screen only while a cell is open, and three things outside it read its state: the page's scroll lock, the form (a save under an open cell makes the cell read its jobs again), and the move strip above the aging table. State in a component that unmounts would be lost and those three would have nowhere to read it. So the tab calls the engine above its access gate and the window only draws.

**What the tab keeps.** The doors: `openInvoiceFromAgingCell`, `openPaymentFromAgingCell`, `openHouseFromAging`, `agingCellDoorProps`, the one busy flag they share and the field a door asks the form to open on. They tie the aging window to the form and to Record a payment, so they belong to neither. The move strip's two derived lines stay beside the aging matrix they read.

**Four sentences became named words.** `check:empty-words` reads a moved line as a written one and takes a sentence that opens on *No* for a bare empty state. Four in this window do and none is an empty list: the *No job yet* heading, the move modal's line when the job has no account, the request preview's line about the account, and the line when the house has no rep to email. They are named in `src/lib/materials/agingCellByJob.ts`, beside the window's other words, with a test that pins each. The words on screen are the same.

**Effect order.** The engine's three effects now run ahead of the form's and of the remembered-toggle effects. None reads what the others write.

**Size.** `SupplyHousesTab.tsx` went from 2,130 lines to 1,396. The engine is 318 lines and the window 578.

**Proof.** The tab's render test (27 cases) and the Materials page's pass unedited: they open a past-due cell and a current one, follow a bill's number to its Edit window over the cell, press Record a payment › and Mark it on, open the worked list from the strip and from the Needs You door, and drop a job whose account is marked not needed. `useSupplyHouseAgingWindows.render.test.tsx` adds 9 cases on the engine alone, among them the two writes the render test does not press: an emailed ask and one the function refuses, and the attested move with and without an ask on record. A check for a browser global standing in for a missing name found none.

**Docs.** The map marks step 10 as shipped and names both files under *What is already out*.
