# v2.7628 — Prospects: the Convert tab is its own piece (2026-11-17)

Step 7 of the order in `docs/PROSPECTS_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md`. A pure move: no behaviour changes.

**Two files in `src/components/prospects/`.**

- `useProspectConvert.ts` (178 lines) is the tab's engine: its eight pieces of state, its three effects (the first interaction's day, the service types, the first contact's prefill), the picker pool with its search results and suggestions, and the submit that makes the customer, its contact persons and its first bids, then marks the prospect through `markProspectConverted`. The code is the page's, cut by script.
- `ProspectsConvertTab.tsx` (302 lines) draws it. Its JSX is the page's block, unchanged.

**Why a hook the page calls, and not state inside the tab.** The tab unmounts when the caller looks at another tab. Today the picked prospect and the typed contact and bid cards are still there on the way back, because the page holds them. A hook called by the page keeps that lifetime; state inside the tab would have lost it. The render smoke pins it.

**What the hook takes.** Whether the tab is on screen (the service types load then), the two prospect caches and the last-call map, the signed-in user and role, and `onConverted`, which the page fills with the two cache patches the submit made inline.

**One sentence is drawn differently.** A search with no match says *No prospects match — converted prospects are not listed.* through `<EmptyState compact>` now. The house check on empty states reads a moved line as a written one and asks for it; the words are the same, centred.

**Size.** `Prospects.tsx` went from 4,438 lines to 4,075, and is under 4,100 for the first time since the lanes landed.

**Proof.** The render smoke grew from 75 cases to 79: a full Convert (the customer, the contact with an empty box as null, the first bid as rough, the prospect marked, the customer's page opened), a bid card with no service type left out, the form surviving a look at another tab, and a recently answered prospect offered as a suggestion. All 79 were run against the page before the move and after it.
