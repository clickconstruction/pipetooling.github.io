# v2.3082 — Robots: the TakeoffTooling leg — a third seat, the manifest door, and cost that travels (2026-09-07)

The estimator-twin pipeline covered plumbing end to end and stopped at electrical, because the app where an electrical takeoff becomes priced, labored material — **TakeoffTooling** — had no twin identity, no headless way in, and nothing PipeTooling could read back. This PR is PipeTooling's half of that leg; TakeoffTooling's half (twin flag, per-twin credentials, the `manage-user` bridge, the `import-manifest` door, the explode kernel, bid stamp + review lane on its projects) ships in its own repo the same day. Design and sequencing: the "Electrical Fleet" plan (artifact, 2026-09-07) — the incoming electrical estimator's first three bids are the reference set; no robot electrical mission runs before they exist.

## Edge functions

- **`tt-bridge`** (new) + **`_shared/ttBridge.ts`** (new): the PT-side proxy of the TT↔PT bridge, the TakeoffTooling twin of ct-bridge. Devs only; forwards `create` / `lookup` / `set_twin_flag` to TakeoffTooling's `manage-user` with `TT_MANAGE_USER_SECRET`. No join-key column on PT — a twin's TT seat is its fleet email at `@twins.takeofftooling.local`. `supabase/config.toml`: `verify_jwt = false` like ct-bridge.
- **twin-mcp v1.3.7**:
  - `mint_session(app: 'takeofftooling')` — mints a TakeoffTooling session (per-twin token hash mirrored over the bridge first, fleet secret fallback, the shared 6/min ledger limit). A missing seat is named in the error.
  - `get_tt_guide` — serves the new `docs/twins/TAKEOFFTOOLING_BID_GUIDE.md` (bundled into `briefs.ts` by the build script, which now exports `TT_GUIDE`).
  - `tt_finish_costing(bid, name, items, note?, explode?, labor_rate?, tax_rate?)` — one-call STG-4: mints the TT session server-side, POSTs payload-v2 items to `import-manifest` with `external_ref = b<bid>` and the bid's `count_tooling_link` as the plans link, marks the manifest `ready` over the bridge, stamps a `[pipeline STG-4]` note and a `twin_runs` row, returns `exploded` / `unpriced` / `book` / `share_url`. Fenced to the twin's own bids like `ct_finish_takeoff`.
  - `get_work_state(bid).tt_manifest` — the twin's TakeoffTooling manifest for THIS bid (bid-stamp match only) with the priced rows and a PipeTooling-format `counts_text`; a plain note on bids with none (plumbing bids never have one).
  - `paste_counts` rows accept **`unit_cost`** and **`labor_hours`**: cost lands as the row's custom materials cost (`bid_count_row_custom_costs`, `source: 'quoted'`, `house_name: 'TakeoffTooling'`, `applied_by` the twin) so the Workbench opens costed with an honest provenance tag; `replace: true` now clears custom costs too; hours are summed onto the STG-5 ledger note (the Labor tab has no per-row column yet — a human step, named in the note). Response adds `costed_rows` and `labor_hours_total`.

## Settings → Digital twins

- A **TT seat · linked / missing** chip beside the CT seat chip, looked up per twin over `tt-bridge`; **link** creates (or finds) the seat. Minting a twin also creates its TakeoffTooling seat, fail-soft.

## Docs

- `docs/twins/TAKEOFFTOOLING_BID_GUIDE.md` (new): the twin's TakeoffTooling brief — access, the electrical loop, exact item/result contracts, guardrails (electrical only; no robot-mousing the flows; book discipline).
- `docs/twins/estimator.md`: an "Electrical bids: TakeoffTooling in the middle" section + vocabulary; `TWIN_HARNESS.md`: the third twin-login; `HANDOFF.md`: what is live; `SUBSTRATE.md`: five electrical schedule shapes (`lighting_fixture`, `panel`, `feeder`, `mechanical_connections`, `device_legend`) and the homeruns-vs-circuits reconciliation pair; `PLACEMENT.md`: an electrical chapter that says, deliberately, that the doctrine is not written yet and what standing facts a counters-only mission may lean on until the estimator's bids write it.
- `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`: twin-mcp v1.3.7 paragraph + the tt-bridge section.

## Roads not taken

- A `takeofftooling_user_id` join column on `users` (the CT precedent): a prod migration for a lookup the bridge answers in one call. Revisit if the fleet grows.
- Landing `labor_hours` on the Labor tab automatically: no per-row labor model exists to write into; a summed hours line on the ledger is honest, and the human still owns Labor.
- A TakeoffTooling review lane surfaced in the Audits cockpit: the CT project review is the human gate the program has; the TT manifest's `share_url` rides the work state until audits need a second door.
