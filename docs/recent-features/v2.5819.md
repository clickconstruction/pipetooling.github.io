# v2.5819 — Quickfill: a station's Mark history reads as a habit, eight weeks and its boxes at a glance (2026-10-24)

Punch list #156, the window of the train the owner's *"build it all"* started (the kernel landed as [v2.5817](v2.5817.md)): option A of [`to-dos/quickfill-mark-history/`](../../to-dos/quickfill-mark-history/README.md) with its boxes band, the to-do's calls taken as recommended (two tints, eight weeks, the band on the desk). The band was drawn as a PR of its own and folded in here before either was pushed: stacked on the window it would have needed a hand merge after the window's squash.

- **`QuickfillSectionMarkHistoryModal.tsx`** redrawn in the checklist ledger's shape. One read of the station's whole history (`quickfill_section_mark_events`, newest first, the `users` join for the name, 1,000 at most; a station holds about 130 today), then the kernel shapes it:
  - **The three tiles** (`markHistoryTiles`), the ledger's own: *This week* (looks against the days expected), *Streak* (looks in a row), *Missed* (of the window's days). The tile style is the ledger's `statTile`, copied; on a phone the small words drop to a line of their own so three tiles fit 375 px.
  - **The grid** (`markHistoryWeeks`): one row a week for eight weeks, one cell a day, Monday to Sunday, the month in bold where it changes. A marked day is a button holding the open count, green for nothing open and amber for something (`--bg-green-100` / `--bg-amber-100` and their text tokens), a ✓ when the station keeps no count; a day the station's rhythm expected a look on with none is a dashed box; weekends, the days before the station's first mark, the days after today and the days the rhythm did not expect draw nothing; today wears a blue ring. The third tint (red above the station's usual) is in the kernel and off here, the owner's call 2 on the to-do. 28 px cells on a desk, 34 px under 640 px (`useNarrowViewport640`).
  - **A tap reads the day**: the line under the grid turns from *Tap a day to read its mark.* into `markHistoryCellTitle`'s *Tue, Oct 20 · 9:37 AM · Dana · 3 open* (a hover reads the same as the cell's title); the cell reads pressed; a second tap clears it.
  - **The habit line** (`markHistoryHabit` over the window's marks): *Dana marked it 36 times and Marisol once. The usual time is about 9:30 AM.*, a 7 AM to 7 PM band with a tick for every mark's hour and the middle half shaded (`--bg-indigo-100`, `--text-indigo-800`), then the count's sentence: *Nothing open at 31 of 37 looks. Lately about 1 open at a look, down from about 3.*, *Something was open at every one of the 38 looks.*, or *This station has no count. A mark is a look.*
  - **The boxes**, on a desk, for a station whose boxes write a row a tick (`MARK_HISTORY_BOX_SOURCES`: Office Arriving and Assistant Dailys in their daily-checks tables with the labels as JSON in `app_settings`, Difficult people with its items table; Office Leaving keeps a done map and no history, so its window shows the grid alone). Two more reads beside the events, in parallel and best-effort (`loadBoxes`: a failed read leaves the band off and the window whole): the ticks of the last four weeks with the `users:checked_by(name)` join the Assistant Dailys page uses, and the labels. Under the habit line, *The boxes · the last four weeks · ticked of 20 days*, then one row a box (`markHistoryBoxes`, `BoxesBand`): the label, a strip of 12 px cells, one a workday the station was marked on or its rhythm expected (`--text-green-600` when ticked, a dashed `--border-400` box when not, a small gap at each Monday), and *19 of 20* at the end, amber and bold under half. A cell's hover: *Fri, Oct 16 · 9:30 AM · Marisol*, *not ticked*, or *no mark that day*. Under the rows: *Dana ticks the boxes.* and, in bold, *Left unticked most days: Trash out when leaving, 18 of 20.* The band keys its cells on `work_date` as the pages wrote it (`denverCalendarDayKey`, a day key of its own) and never recomputes the day from `checked_at`.
  - **Two facts, both true**: the number in the grid is what was open at the moment of the mark; the band is what was still ticked at the end of the day (a box ticked and then unticked leaves no row). On Office Arriving they disagree on most days (the to-do's data section: 53 marks recorded 0 open while the day's rows hold one box of three), and the window now shows both rather than one.
  - **The rows** as before, *Last 7 days* then *Earlier (all time)* folded with its count, When · By · Open, the **Note column only when a mark carries a note** (`markHistoryHasNotes`): the three note-first stations keep it, every other station loses a column of dashes. The fold needs no second read.
  - Loading, the error line, Escape and a click outside: unchanged.
