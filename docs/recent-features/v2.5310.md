# v2.5310 — Customers: merging a duplicate keeps its GC call log and its projects (2026-10-13)

A bug on main found 2026-10-12 by the Messages build (helper 1, cluster 1): `merge_customers` folds the victim's bids, jobs, estimates, projects, the old call log and the people at the customer into the survivor and then deletes the victim, but it did not know two GC tables that came after it. `gc_customer_contacts` (20261010150000) goes with its customer by cascade, so the victim's GC call log was dropped on merge; `gc_projects` names a customer as the architect (`architect_customer_id`) and as the property owner (`property_owner_customer_id`), both on delete set null, so a project drawn by or built for the duplicate lost that link.

- **Migration `20261013010000_merge_customers_gc_folds.sql`** (`docs/migrations/20261013010000_merge_customers_gc_folds.md`): `CREATE OR REPLACE` of `merge_customers`, the body of `20260817221950` plus three `UPDATE`s before the delete: the victim's `gc_customer_contacts` rows, and the two `gc_projects` keys, onto the survivor. SECURITY DEFINER as before, so the call log's append-only grants do not stop the fold. No table changed; not pushed, the lead pushes after merge.
- **The `merge-customers` SQL bed** (`scripts/pgtest-merge-customers.sh`, `supabase/tests/merge_customers/20_scenario.sql`, a line in `scripts/sql-beds.txt`): every migration on Supabase Postgres 17, the merge's migration once more (changing nothing), then the scenario. Nobody signed out merges. A dev merges a duplicate architect into the kept one; afterwards all three call-log lines read on the survivor with the clinic call still naming its job, the clinic names the survivor as architect and property owner, the school and the shop keep their parts, the school is the survivor's job, the old call-log line moved, the survivor carries the chosen address and the victim is gone. Not run on this Mac (Docker is another account's); CI's sql-beds job runs it on the PR.
- **Ordering with the Messages spine** (#5431, migration 20261012010000): it replaces the same function, adding the `contacts` fold. The migration notes it; whichever of the two lands second carries the other's step. The `contacts`/`contact_*` tables are untouched here.

The rewrite landed after the Messages spine's own rewrite of the same function, so it carries the spine's contact fold too, lifted word for word; the two rewrites now agree.
