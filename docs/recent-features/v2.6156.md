# v2.6156 — Bill Customer's RTB as the row's bar and three rows, a prototype behind a switch (punch list #217, jobs-pipeline-4) (2026-10-30)

Punch list #217, *Show it, don't say it*: the jobs-pipeline scan's fourth finding is Bill Customer's header, *1327 · Leak under slab · RTB $1,250.00*, where the amount about to go out on every channel is a remainder the app worked out (bid − payments made − other open bills, `planPrimaryRtbForBillCustomer`) and the header shows only the answer; a wrong bid figure, an unlinked payment or a stale draft would each change it, and nothing on the screen shows which parts made it. Taunya marked it Build on 2026-10-30 and wrote: *"I really like the idea of this. Could I see this as a live prototype where you show me the before and after."* This PR is that prototype, a draft with no auto-merge: the bar and the rows behind a per-person switch that is off by default, and a dev-only side by side.

## What changed

- **`lib/billing/rtbParts.ts`** (new, 8 tests): the job's bid, payments made, invoices and payments and the bill about to go → the bar's segments (**paid** · **open bills** · **this bill** · the rest, each a fraction of the bid, or of the parts when they run past it, with a note saying so), the open bills as `otherOpenInvoiceAmounts` counts them (the ready-to-bill and billed rows other than the never-sent primary bundle and the row being sent, each for what is still unpaid on it, oldest first, with its day and age in days, *draft* for a row never sent), the rows *Bid − Paid · N payments − Open bills · N = This bill* with a door each, and the frame's words (*this bill is the rest* or *$X left after this bill*).
- **`components/jobs/BillCustomerRtbParts.tsx`** (new): the bar (the Pipeline bar's green and blue, this bill a lighter blue with a dashed edge, the rest the bar's own grey), the bid and the frame's words under it, a legend, the rows with their doors and the open bills' unfold. A door is a link in a new tab (`/jobs?tab=stages&edit=<id>` for the bid and the bills, `…&editFocus=payments` for the payments), so the window stays as it is.
- **`components/jobs/SendRecordInvoiceModal.tsx`**: **Show the bars** beside the title (`useShapeView('bill-customer-rtb-parts')`, localStorage key `pt-shape-view:bill-customer-rtb-parts:<userId>`, off by default; *Show today's look* turns it back). With it on, the header's *· RTB $X* gives way to the bar and the rows under the job's number and name; the figure itself is the same plan (`ensuredInvoice`, or the row's own amount from a bill row), and every send is untouched. Until the job's details are in, the header reads as today.
- **The side by side**: `/jobs?tab=stages&sbs=1` with a dev signed in → open Bill Customer on a Ready to Bill row: the header draws **Today** over **With the bars**, no switch. On a dev server from this branch: `/dev-login?as=1&to=/jobs`, then put `?tab=stages&sbs=1` on the address bar, find a Ready to Bill row and press Bill Customer.
- **`components/prototypes/SideBySide.tsx`**: this branch's copy of the shared #217 helper, byte for byte the office-round-columns branch's (#6528); whichever lands first wins.
- **Smoke** `SendRecordInvoiceModal.rtbParts.render.test.tsx` (4): today's header with the switch off; the bar, the rows, the doors and the unfold with it on, remembered for the person; the side by side for a dev with `?sbs=1`; nothing for anyone else.
- **Guide** *bill a customer and get paid* names the switch.

## What the mock-up asked for that this PR does not do

- A bill's door opens Edit Job, where the job's bills are listed; there is no deep link to one bill, so the mock-up's *the bill* door lands one press short.
- The mock-up's bar is the Pipeline row's own `StagesProgressPaymentCell` with its stages; this bar is the three money parts on the bid alone, with no stage names and no % tick, because Bill Customer has no `ProgressPaymentView` at hand and the question here is the money.
- On a job the plan refuses (nothing left, two primaries, a partial with no remainder) the header reads as today, since there is no bill to draw.

## Decisions

- The paid part is the job's `payments_made`, the figure the RPC subtracts, with the count from the payment rows; the open bills are the same rows and the same net-of-applied arithmetic as the plan, so the rows always sum to the header's RTB.
- No migration, no function change.
