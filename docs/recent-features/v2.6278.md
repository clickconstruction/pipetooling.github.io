## What changed

- **The add card is the row it becomes** (`NotificationsPanel` in `src/pages/LegalPortal.tsx`, the rule drawn once as `FirmRuleRow` for a row and for the card): Name, Email, a **Title** (optional, sixty characters, placeholder *attorney, paralegal, billing*, one hint: *Optional. Shows beside their name, here and to the office.*), then **Emails** (*Each event* / *Weekly digest*, with the weekday chips and the Central time under the digest) and **Matters** (*Every matter* / *Only theirs*). The press names the person, *Send Ann a confirmation* (`firmSendConfirmationLabel`), with one line under it: *Nothing else goes to them until they click it.* The add sends `mode`, `scope`, `digestWeekday` and `digestTime`; `recipient_add` already took the first two and now takes the day and time, keeping Mon 07:00 for a missing or odd value, so an old function and a new page, or the reverse, both land the row.
- **Role is a Title.** The column (`legal_firm_recipients.role`) is text that nothing reads but three labels: the person's row, the office's list on the Legal desk, and the *Recorded by* picker. The dropdown of paralegal · attorney · billing promised an access difference the system does not make; the word now says what it is.
- **The count under Only theirs** (`firmHandlingCountLine`): *The office names Bo Sample as handling 0 of 1 open matter today.*, read from the payload's open matters with the dispatcher's own rule (`firmHandlesMatter`: the typed name against the matter's handling name, trimmed and lowercased, as `legal-notify-dispatch`'s `canSee`). A paralegal who picks it and is named on nothing used to hear nothing with no word of why. *Only my matters* reads *Only theirs* on the row too, so the card and the row read one way; the rows gain a *Matters* label.
- **The two paragraphs are gone.** *How this behaves* (67 words) and *What you hear about* (59) and the empty state's second sentence (23). What they said is shown: the confirmation by the press and the *not confirmed yet* word, the office's pause by the red band that already draws, the rules by the switches on the card. The seven events and the stop link are PR 2's sample inbox. The empty state keeps its one sentence and gains the press *Add the first person*, which lands on the Name field. 50 words of prose where there were 171, every one held to plain words by `legalFirmWords.test.ts` (`FIRM_NOTIFICATIONS_WORDS`).
- Docs: the guide *manage who at the law firm gets emails* (Title, Matters, the count, the press's name); `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` → `submit-legal-portal` (the two add fields); the card's status.

## Is this the best we can do?

The card's thinking holds the paths cut: role presets that set the starting rules (a third thing to learn, wrong for half the firms, and the paralegal preset lands on *Only theirs*), per-person event picks (the wordy thing again; open as the card's call 1C), dropping the role (the desk and *Recorded by* read it). One thing the mock-up drew that this PR does not: the sample inbox, PR 2, on its own so the form lands first.
