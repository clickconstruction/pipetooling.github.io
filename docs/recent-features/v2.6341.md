# v2.6341 — A lien notice recorded after the fact starts its clocks on the day it was mailed (2026-11-02)

Punch list #243, PR 2 of two. The by-hand door (*Already mailed? Record it…*, #35, v2.3770) kept the typed mailing day only inside each filing's `sends[].sent_on`; `recordLienNoticeByHand` then marked the covered desk item sent through `markLienDeskItemSent`, whose update set `status` and `sent_filing_id` and no `sent_at`, and the guard trigger on `job_lien_desk_items` (`20261023150000_lien_sign_code.sql`, line 339) filled the gap with `now()`. Letter two's clock (`firstSentAt` → `letterTwoClock`), the courtesy notice's day 7, Do now and the Sent footer's day all read that stamp. On prod, 2026-11-02, three items mailed 2026-09-22 and recorded 2026-10-06 read 14 days short. Client only: no migration (the column is an ordinary `timestamptz` the office's update policy already covers, and the trigger keeps a value it is given), no function change.

- **The kernel `src/lib/jobs/lienSentAt.ts`.** `appTzNoonIso(ymd)` is noon of the day in the app's calendar (the pen record keeps its signing day the same way), so `calendarYmdInAppTzFromIso` reads the typed day back in any zone. `sentAtForTypedDay(ymd, todayYmd)` is that instant when the day is before today, and null when it is today, ahead, blank or not a day — the trigger then stamps the moment of the record, so a notice recorded the day it goes out keeps its exact instant as before. `earliestSendDay(sends)` is the first day a filing's sends name.
- **The doors.** `markLienDeskItemSent(itemId, filingId, sentAt = null)` writes `sent_at` only when given one. `recordLienNoticeByHand` passes the typed day (its `todayYmd` comes from the pane, else the app's today); `syncLienDeskAfterRecord` — the Lien window's *Save & record sends…* path, and a by-hand job with no live item — reads each filing's `sends` and passes its earliest day, and for an affidavit the filing's `filed_at` (a date column); the run (`lienDeskRunIo.ts`) passes the sends' earliest day, which is the *Mailed on* day of a run recorded in a later sitting (v2.5532), or the Docsmit piece's mailing day. Every reader of `sent_at` already goes through `calendarYmdInAppTzFromIso` (`lienDesk.ts`, `lienLetterTwo.ts`, `lienDeskAffidavits.ts`, `lienDeskRetainage.ts`, `lienCallerMatch.ts`, `lienArChip.ts`, the desk footer, the courtesy window's `noticeSentOn`), so none changes.
- **The one judgment taken here**: today's typed day keeps `now()` rather than noon, so a notice recorded as it goes out is as precise as before; only a past day is stored as noon.
- **Guide** *send lien notices from the Lien desk* → *A notice sent by hand*: the day you type is the day the clocks start.
- **Tests**: `lienSentAt.test.ts` (noon in summer and winter, read back as the day, 14 and 10 days on the clock, today and a future day left to the trigger, the earliest send day); `lienNoticeByHandIo.test.ts` (mailed 09-22 and recorded 10-06 → `sent_at` noon of 09-22 on the item's update; same-day → no `sent_at` key); `lienDeskRunIo.test.ts` (+1: *Mailed on* in a later sitting → noon of that day, a same-day run → the trigger's stamp).
