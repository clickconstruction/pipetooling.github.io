# v2.4426 — Dashboard and Calendar: a promised bid call shows on its day (2026-10-02)

Punch list #80, PR 4 of 5. Until now nothing outside Bids said a call was due: the owner's case is a call promised three months ahead, by someone who may not remember it.

## What shipped

- **Kernel** [`bidFollowupsDue.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/bidFollowupsDue.ts): `bidHasStandingDay` (a sent bid with no answer whose own picked day no later contact has spent), `bidFollowupsDue` (the ones due today or missed: count, overdue count, dollars, builder names with the most overdue first) and `bidFollowupCalendarItems`. Only picked days count: the seven-day default reminds nobody.
- **Loader** [`useBidFollowupsDue.ts`](../../src/hooks/useBidFollowupsDue.ts): `loadBidFollowupRows` reads the bids that carry a day at all (`next_followup_on` not null, no outcome), people bids only (`partitionBidsByScope`), and the hook hands the Dashboard its count.
- **Needs You** ([`dashboardNeedsYou.ts`](../../src/lib/dashboardNeedsYou.ts)): item `bid-followups`, rank 40 (the revenue-chase tier, above lost-bids hygiene). *N bid follow-ups are due*, the builders, the dollars; amber when due today, red when any day has passed. **Open the call queue** goes to `/bids?tab=call-queue`, where promised cards sort first. Shown to the Call queue's audience (dev, master, assistant, controller, estimator); it is the whole team's list, like the lost-bids card. Quickfill's twin does not carry it: Quickfill is the billing station.
- **Calendar** ([`Calendar.tsx`](../../src/pages/Calendar.tsx)): each standing day is a blue *☎ builder · project* entry on its day in the month grid, in the day window and in **Upcoming**, linking to the Call queue. Same roles as the calendar's bid-due entries.
- Help: [`needs-you-card`](../../src/content/help/needs-you-card.md) and [`follow-up-with-builders`](../../src/content/help/follow-up-with-builders.md); [`GLOSSARY.md`](../GLOSSARY.md) *Call-again day*.
