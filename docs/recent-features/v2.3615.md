## The change

- **Kernel** — [`lib/journeys/personJourney.ts`](../../src/lib/journeys/personJourney.ts) `customerRowsForJob(rows, jobId)` (+2 tests): the customer's rows narrowed to one job — the job and every per-job row (contracts, invoices, hazmat, test reports, demand letters, lien filings and releases); customer-level rows (estimates and their events, the portal, bid and submittal rooms, GC statements) stay, because the person holds those regardless of which job the office opened.
- **Loader** — [`loadPersonJourney.ts`](../../src/lib/journeys/loadPersonJourney.ts) takes `{ jobId }` and applies the filter after the customer fan-out; the strips ([`PersonJourneyStrips.tsx`](../../src/components/journeys/PersonJourneyStrips.tsx)) take `jobId` and say *'s journey on this job*.
- **The door** — [`DetailJobModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/DetailJobModal.tsx): a *Their journey* row above the thread notes for `canSeeWhatCustomersSee` roles when the job has a customer; collapsed until opened (the loader's fan-out runs only then); *Every job →* opens `/customers/:id`.
- **Guide** — [`see-what-customers-see.md`](../../src/content/help/see-what-customers-see.md) *See a real person's journey* mentions the Job window door.
