# v2.4998 — GC projects: email the pay application to the customer and the architect (2026-10-08)

Owner Billing's O4b-1 in the GC mode real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/OWNER_BILLING_REAL_BUILD.md` → *Edge functions*, on branch `spike/gc-mode`): our emails to a GC project's customer and its architect, from one new sender, `gc-customer-email`. Guide: [`bill-the-customer-on-a-gc-job`](../../src/content/help/bill-the-customer-on-a-gc-job.md). No migration. One new edge function, live only once it is deployed.

- **The sender** (`supabase/functions/gc-customer-email`, [EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md](../EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md#gc-customer-email)): the staff JWT checked in its body (`verify_jwt = false` in `config.toml`), the money team only (`GC_CUSTOMER_EMAIL_ROLES`, held to `GC_MONEY_TEAM` by `access.test.ts`), never a training account or a digital twin. Kind `pay_app` goes to the project's customer and `certify_ask` to its architect, each at the address the Pipeline bills (`customerBillingEmail`). From Click Construction, Reply-To and signed by the project manager, the G702 and G703 attached as a PDF. The window writes the words; `_shared/gcCustomerEmails.ts` reads the request and frames them.
- **Sent copies:** each email is filed after the send with `fileSentEmailBestEffort`: kind `bill_gc_pay_app` (under Bills on the Documents page, never the Pipeline's `pay_application`, whose history reads that kind), on the billing job, source `gc_owner_pay_apps`. Nothing else is written. Bill the customer reads the copies back as each sent bill's *Emailed to Cibolo Creek Partners and Garza Architects on Oct 8.*
- **The window** (`GcBillCustomer.tsx`): the tick **Email it to the customer and the architect now**, off to start. Send records the pay application through `gc_send_owner_pay_app` first, then emails both with the form as it went, dated the day Send recorded. An email that does not go leaves the application sent, and the window says why (`gcCustomerEmailRefusal`).
- **The words** (`src/lib/gc/customerEmail.ts`): the pay application's lines are the prototype's `customerMessages`. The ask to the architect is new, since the architect certifies by email (decision 4). What customers see shows both as samples (`gc-pay-app`, `gc-certify-ask`), and the email catalog lists `gc_customer_email`.
- **Tests:** `customerEmail.test.ts` (the words, the facts, the request's shape, the frame, the answer, each refusal and the filed kind), `access.test.ts` (the roles), the window's render test (the tick's words, the call and *Emailed to*), and the sent-copy ratchet.
- **To go live:** `supabase functions deploy gc-customer-email`. Its first real send waits for Grace's yes, to bids@clickplumbing.com.
