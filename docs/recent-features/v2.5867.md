# v2.5867 — The Bridge: badges out of the buttons, and "reading…" while the cards load (2026-10-25)

PR D of to-do #107's small train (`to-dos/bridge-weakest-link-follow-ups.md`): bug 2 and the last word fix.

- **Badges out of the buttons** (`BridgeStages.tsx`, `BridgeResources.tsx`): each card was a `<button>` with its badge a `role="button"` span inside it — nested interactive content, invalid markup, and a screen reader read the badge as part of the card. Each card is now a `<div>` holding two real buttons: the card's press (name, pile, flow lines — unchanged look, same testid, `aria-pressed`) and the badge, a sibling that opens the order. With no `onOrder` handler the badge is a plain span. The hand-rolled `onKeyDown` is gone; native buttons handle the keyboard.
- **"reading…" while the loader flies** (`stageFlow.ts`, `Bridge.tsx`): Market and Sales showed "not readable yet" for the few seconds their bids-and-estimates loader was in flight on every load — a wrong badge, since the stages do read. `StageFlowInput.demandLoading` (true while `demandInputs` is null with no error) draws "reading…" on both cards; the amber error line under the cards remains the only failure state. Build, Bill and Collect are untouched.

Tests: `stageFlow.test.ts` (the reading… case, and that the pile stages keep "not readable yet"), `BridgeStages.render.test.tsx` / `BridgeResources.render.test.tsx` (the press picks, the badge orders without picking — now against the sibling-button markup). Dev-only surface (`/bridge`): no help guide, no migration.
