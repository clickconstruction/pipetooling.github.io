- **The kernel**, [`lib/materials/siteMaterialsReport.ts`](../../src/lib/materials/siteMaterialsReport.ts), pure and tested (10 tests): `buildSiteMaterialsReport` folds the rows once through `containerLines` (a count resets a line; on hand is the sum): per job its live boxes with their lines on hand (part, maker, count, the unit the newest delivery named through `unitOfPart`, reading `manifest.ts`'s `UNITS` which is exported now, and the line's last touch), the places holding a box, the Unsorted row, and the jobs with nothing on site as one line each (`nothingWords`: *no cages or boxes on this job yet.* · *S1 at Field edge is empty. Nothing on site.*); a retired box is on no paper. `reportSections(report, view)` reads the fold two ways: **by job** (a section per job with material, its places in name order, Unsorted when it holds anything, No place yet last) and **by location** (a section per place name folded across jobs, case and spaces ignored, a group per job under it, then Unsorted by job and No place yet by job). Words: `countsWords`, `footWords`, `lastRecordedWords` (*Nov 2 · Jordan*, the year added when it is not the printed day's), `jobWords`, `boxWords`, `nothingHeading`, `reportLeadWords`.
- **The paper**: the head (*Material location report*, the site, the customer and the address, the printed day and who), the count line, the sections with a block per cage or box (stencil and kind, the where note, the eight-character code, the lines as Part · On hand · Unit · Last recorded; *Empty* for an empty box), the closing **Nothing on site** block, and the foot. In print the paper draws alone at full size through `printRootCss` (v2.6432), a box and its lines never split across a page, a section's or a group's head stays with its first block, a split table repeats its column heads, and the foot rides in the page's margin boxes on every page (the ledger named at the left, *Page N of M* at the right, as the costs packet's does); on screen it is drawn once at the paper's end. The window is `data-theme="light"`; **Print** calls the browser's print, the PDF is its *Save as PDF*; a plain print, no sent copy.
- **The pick**: two chips in the window's head, **By job** and **By location**, the device remembering the last (`siteMaterialsReportPrefs.ts`). The render smoke (5 tests) covers the paper by job, the regroup, the memory, Print, the empty site, the failed read with Retry and the print rules.
- **Docs**: the guide *print the material location report*, `MATERIALS_ON_SITE_ARCHITECTURE.md` (The screens), `GLOSSARY.md` (Development page, Materials on site), `docs/twins/APP_DIRECTORY.md`, `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` (Developments), the card's *Where it stands*, this fragment and the release note.

## Decisions

- **By job is the default and by location the other chip, not a second paper.** The kernel folds once and each view is an order of the same lines, so the counts agree both ways; the owner asked for the reader to pick either at leisure.
- **A press on the Jobs & bids look, not a seventh look.** The page gains no read on open and no seventh chip on a phone's one row, and the screen table would repeat Jobs → Materials.
- **The jobs with nothing on site close the paper in one block.** Gun Dog has 23 such jobs; a section each would spend most of the paper on nothing.
- **An empty box prints with one line, *Empty*.** The box is accounted for: empty, not unrecorded. An empty Unsorted prints nothing.
- **The unit comes from the delivery that named it.** The office's Deliveries writes the invoice's unit on the row; a part added on the phone has none and the cell is blank.
- **The same place name on two jobs folds to one section by location.** A place is a row of one job, so the fold is a guess by name; by job keeps them apart. A site-wide place is a later question for #209.
