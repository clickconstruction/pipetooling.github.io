# v2.7407 — Tag a teammate in a job note, with a count above Jobs (2026-11-15)

Punch list #363, PR 4 of six: where a tag lands, and the list of names opened to the team. Taunya asked for the feature on 2026-11-15 and settled its eight calls the same day, calls 1 and 7 in her own words. Client only: no migration, no edge function. It reads `job_note_tags_mine` and calls `job_note_post_tagged`, `job_note_tag_done` and `job_note_tags_seen`, all on prod since v2.7385.

## What a person sees

- **The count above Jobs.** `JobsTagsBadge`, the Messages pill's look (`src/lib/navBadgeStyle.ts`, now shared by both), on the Jobs link in the bar and in the phone's menu, and on the phone's menu button for a role whose menu holds Jobs. It is the reader's own number: the notes that tagged them and still wait, plus the answers to their own tags they have not seen.
- **Tagged job notes, at the top of the Pipeline.** `TaggedJobNotesSection`. Each tag is a card: the job's name (the door to the job, and the card's only link, Taunya's call), the time and its age, amber after two working days, the note with the reader's chip, an answer box and **No reply needed**. The oldest is on top. It shows three and a **Show the rest** press, and **Fold** shuts it to one line (kept in the browser). Nothing is drawn while nothing waits and nothing was asked.
- **You asked**, under the cards: the reader's own tags that still wait, were just answered, or cleared in the last seven days. An answer not yet seen is first and marked **new**.
- **The Needs you line.** A `tagged-notes` row, amber, for every role that can be tagged. Its press opens `TaggedJobNotesWindow`, the same section in a window, because a superintendent's Jobs page hides the Pipeline and the crew has no Jobs link. For a helper the job's name is plain words.
- **The list of names is open.** `canTagInJobNotes` now answers for the seven roles `job_note_people` lists: dev, master technician, assistant, controller, superintendent, helpers and primary. v2.7400 held it to a dev.

## How it stays in step

One read per tab. `useMyNoteTags` holds the reader's tags for the count, the section and the Needs you line at once, so the three never disagree. It reads when its first follower mounts, when a tag changes in the tab (`noteTagsChanged`, sent after a typed note posts, an answer, a No reply needed press and a seen mark), when the window comes back into focus, and every 90 seconds while the tab is on screen. There is no realtime channel: the card's guess was a handful of tags a week, and focus plus the timer is cheaper than a publication change.

## The seen rule

A question clears when it is answered. An answer clears when it is seen. While the section is open and holds an answer the reader has not seen, it calls `job_note_tags_seen` once and keeps the **new** mark until the list is left. Folded, it marks nothing. The call is made only when an unseen answer is on screen, since the training-mode trigger refuses even a write that changes no row.
