# v2.4457 — Law firm digest: each day reads as the company's day (2026-10-02)

One of the instant-day fixes that follow [v2.4452](v2.4452.md). No migration. [`_shared/legalEmails.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/legalEmails.ts) is bundled by `legal-notify-dispatch` and `submit-legal-portal`; both are redeployed after merge.

## What was wrong

`buildLegalDigestEmail` cut two days out of instants by their first ten characters: a matter's *since* from `legal_matters.released_at`, and each event's day from `legal_notification_queue.created_at`. Both are `timestamptz`, so a matter released or an event logged after 7 pm CDT (6 pm CST) printed the next day.

## What changed

Both read `todayYmdInAppTz(new Date(iso))` from [`_shared/appTimeZone.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/appTimeZone.ts). `legal-notify-dispatch` already passes the raw instants (`released_at`, `created_at`), so the function needs no change of its own. `submit-legal-portal` sends only the confirm email, which carries no date.

The sample under Settings → What customers see ([`customerSampleEmails.ts`](../../src/lib/customerSampleEmails.ts)) passed bare days (`ymdPlusDays(today, -3)`). Read as an instant, a bare day is midnight UTC, which is the evening before in Central, so the sample would have printed every day one early. It now passes each day at 18:00 UTC, as the file's own Stripe due date does.

## Tests

- New `src/lib/legalEmails.test.ts`: a matter released and a referral logged at `2026-10-03T00:30:00Z` (7:30 pm CDT, also in PostgREST's `+00:00` shape) print *since 2026-10-02* and *2026-10-02 · New account referred*; 6:30 pm CST in December prints Dec 1; noon UTC prints its own day; a matter with no release time prints no *since*. The evening cases fail on main's kernel.
- `customerSampleEmails.test.ts` now checks the sample digest's days (*since 2026-09-13*, *2026-09-15*). With the new kernel and the old sample it fails, printing *since 2026-09-12*.

## Checked against prod

Read only, from the worktree dev server: the firm has no recipients yet and no matter has been released, and none of the 3 queued events was logged in a Central evening. Nothing has gone out with a wrong day; the fix is for the first digest.
