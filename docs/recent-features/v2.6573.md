# v2.6573 — The block editor adds a machine that is not on the fleet board yet, from its template, ours or a rental (2026-11-06)

Punch list #270, the owner's flow (2026-11-06): "an office staff member adds a piece of equipment to the job and that piece of equipment is a template that already has the correct qualities to match that piece of machinery, so that when a technician goes and performs on that job they are able to fill in the blanks for the right matching piece of machinery." v2.6560's picker listed the fleet board only, so a rental that showed up for a job meant a trip to People → Vehicles first.

## What changed

- `src/components/machines/MachineStops.tsx`: the picker's foot gains *+ A machine not on the board yet*, which opens `AddFromTemplate` inside the picker: the template first (it shapes the check), the blanks line under it, Ours or Rental, the fields each needs, and one press, *Add E-14 to this job* or *Add the Sunbelt rental to this job*, that runs `addMachine`, `saveRental` when it is one, and `addAssignment` in turn. A rental's unit label is minted as *Rental 48812-3* from the agreement number, or *Sunbelt rental* without one, so the morning's *Is Rental 48812-3 here?* and the rail read naturally.
- `src/components/schedule/ScheduleDispatchJobWeek.tsx`: the job's own week page now hands the block editor its `jobId`, so the Equipment row shows there too (found live: v2.6560 wired only the people board).
- `src/lib/machines/machines.ts`: `templateBlanksWords(levels, questions, templateId)`, the gauges by name and the stop counts of each check, with a unit test.
- `src/lib/machines/checksIo.ts`: `loadQuestions()`; `src/lib/machines/assignmentsIo.ts`: `DayPlan.templates`, read in the same query the day already made.
- The guide *schedule people onto jobs* gains the paragraph under *Equipment on the job*.
