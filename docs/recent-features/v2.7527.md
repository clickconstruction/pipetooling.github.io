# v2.7527 — Liens: an affidavit that waits on its notice names the notice's next step everywhere (2026-11-16)

The leftovers of v2.7457, the owner's word of 2026-11-16 (*"Yes, fix the words"*). v2.7457 gave an affidavit that waits on its § 53.056 notice a bin of its own and made the Lien desk say where that notice stands. Three places outside the desk's own rows still read as if no notice had been started.

## The act table

`lienActFor` (`lienActWords.ts`) takes an optional `noticeStanding` (`lienAffidavitNoticeStanding.ts`). For the move `send_notice`, and for `fix_property` when the notice is the one closed gate, the act is now the notice's own next step (`noticeFirstAct`):

| Where the notice stands | The act |
|---|---|
| approved | *Print and mail the notice* |
| printed | *Enter mail numbers* (`LIEN_RUN_ACT.record`) |
| awaiting approval | *Waiting on* the leader, by name |
| not sent for approval | *Draft the notice* |
| held | *Review the hold* |
| needs the owner | *Find the owner* |
| marked sent, no mailing on record | *Record the mailing* |
| nothing started, or a reader that cannot know | *Send the late notice*, as before |

Each *why* opens *The affidavit waits on its notice.* and then says where the notice stands. With a fact also missing the fact leads, as before.

Who passes the standing:

- **The Pipeline's lien line and the AR chip** (`lienArChip.ts` `actOf`): from the job's affidavit entry and its notice entry, both already in hand.
- **The affidavit pane's header** when the affidavit is the next step (`lienTimeline.ts` `affidavitNextWords`): from the standing the desk's builder already hands the timeline.
- **The Lien window's header** (`buildLienTimelineFromWindow`): from the notice's place on the desk that the opener already hands over (`deskNotice.state`), through `lienAffidavitNoticeStandingOnDesk`. An opener that hands nothing keeps the late notice's words.

## The missing word

`affidavitGateMissingWord('notice')` reads *a mailed notice*, and the timeline's short name for the gate (`GATE_SHORT.notice`) the same. So a list row reads *missing a mailed notice*, the gate strip's mark *Missing a mailed notice*, and the timeline's affidavit stop *29 days · a mailed notice missing*. The words hold in every standing, so nothing had to be passed in.
