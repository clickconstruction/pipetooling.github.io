# v2.7906 — A primary sees the reports on their own jobs (2026-11-19)

The owner's word of 2026-11-19, with a screenshot of a primary's Dashboard listing eight reports from across the company: "A primary should only be able to see reports for jobs they are the account manager of or the listed superintendent of they can no longer see all reports." A permissions change, so it holds in the database. One migration, pushed after the merge; no edge function.

## Before

- A primary was scoped to their own bids, estimates and jobs in v2.2174 to v2.2177, and reports were left open on purpose (D1 in `20260823172141_primary_scope_jobs.sql`).
- The `reports` read policy named the role, and `list_reports_with_job_info`, `list_reports_for_job_ledger` and `list_reports_for_bid` are SECURITY DEFINER with `primary` in their office list. So the Dashboard's **Recent Reports** card, Jobs → Reports, a job's reports, a bid's field reports, the report photos and their bucket all answered a primary with every report in the company.

## What changed

- **The rule, in one predicate.** `primary_can_read_report(job, project, bid, author)` (`20261119040000_primary_scope_reports.sql`): the caller wrote the report, or its job names them as account manager (`primary_can_access_job`, the predicate parts 1 to 3 run on) or lists them as superintendent through its project (`project_superintendents`, or `gc_projects.superintendent_user_id`). A report on a project alone opens to that project's listed superintendent; a report on a bid alone to the bid's own primary (`primary_can_access_bid`).
- **Where it is read.** A RESTRICTIVE `primary_scope_reports` policy on `reports`, the mechanism of parts 1 to 3, so every direct read, the invoker functions and the realtime feed follow. The three list functions and `list_job_crew_position`'s newest-report percent each take the one clause, their bodies otherwise as they were live. `primary_scope_report_photos` on `report_photos` and the `report-photos` bucket's read policy hold the photos to the same jobs.
- **The card and the tab.** Nothing in the client filters: the card lists what the database answers and counts from that list, so the band's *N new · N listed*, the badge and **Mark N opened** follow by themselves. `reportsScopeLine` (`dashboardRecentReports.ts`) gives a primary one line over the list on the Dashboard card and on Jobs → Reports: *You see reports on jobs where you are the account manager or the superintendent, and reports you wrote.* A primary's empty card reads *No reports on your jobs yet.* (`recentReportsEmptyWords`).
- **The guide.** *Read field reports on the dashboard* gains **Whose reports you see**, and *File and review field reports* links to it.

## What it leaves alone

- Every other role's read of reports.
- Writing: a primary still files a report on any job from the New-report picker, and reads it back because they wrote it. Settings → My reports (`list_my_reports`) is their own.
- The two office-picked email streams (field report emails, the digest). No primary is a recipient today and a primary cannot add themselves; a primary the office subscribes would be sent that stream's reports. Named as a follow-up.
- Job-level doors that pass a primary on the role alone and are not reports (`can_report_stage_progress`, `job_test_reports`, the crew columns of `list_job_crew_position`). Named as a follow-up.

## The calls made, for the owner

1. A primary still reads a report they wrote on a job that is not theirs. Without it they could not save one (the save reads the new row back).
2. A report on a bid with no job opens to the bid's own primary; a report on a project with no job to the project's listed superintendent. Read strictly, the sentence names jobs only.
3. Listed superintendent is the project's list or the GC project's named one. Both pickers offer superintendents only, so no primary is listed anywhere today and this leg opens nothing yet.

## Deploy

The migration narrows a read behind names the old client already calls, so it is safe on either side of the client deploy. Push it after the merge from a clean checkout of `main`.
