## Is this the best we can do?

1. **Explained and openable at once**: the line says what the list is for, the folds show what is in each, the tabs stay the home of the full rows. No duplicate tables.
2. **The firm's letters, nowhere else's**: one exported rule, so an exhibit the office calls *C* is the firm's *C*, on the desk and on both prints.
3. **Order a reader expects**: by letter, the statement before the property record under *A*; a title that repeats its section is not said twice.
4. **Cut**: showing every record inside every fold (six and *N more* keeps the tab short), a per-exhibit toggle to hold an exhibit back (the held entries on Record of contact already do that per row), and a letter for a county record with no case.
