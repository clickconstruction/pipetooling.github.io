# v2.6828 — Permits: everything public about a building and a name, and a Google search beside each (punch list #302, PR 4) (2026-11-10)

The owner's ask on his second look at the #302 frames: tap who pulled a permit, or the building, and see as much public information as there is, with a small Google button beside a name. Asked what the tab lacked, the answer was five things: no sheet for the building, the feed's full row stored and shown nowhere, the other permits on an address used only to find a plumber, nothing that searches Google, and one door into the facts. This PR fills them on today's ledger and map, ahead of the skim (`to-dos/permit-skim/`, section L). No migration and no function: every read is a table the researchers' policies already open.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/research/permitPublic.ts`** (new, pure, `permitPublic.test.ts`): the Google search a company, a person and an address ask for (`googleAskForCompany` by name and city, `googleAskForPerson` with the company when the permit names one, `googleAskForAddress` with the city and zip, `googleAsksForContact` over `contactParts`); `feedRowFields`, the feed's own row as lines (the known columns in a reader's order, the rest by name, an empty one and the feed's row id left out, a day and a money column formatted); `sitePermitLines` and `siteWords` (every permit on one address, newest first, and whether a plumbing permit is among them); `builderKey`, `builderSearchWord` and `builderPermitLines` (a name's other permits: one plain word asked of the table, the rows held to the whole name's key); `deedsDoor` and the two door addresses.
- **`permitResearchIo.ts`**: `loadPermitFeedRow` (`permits.raw` with the source, the pull time and the address key), `loadSitePermits` (by number and by address key, two reads), `loadBuilderPermits` (building permits whose contact holds the word, the last 365 days, 300 at most).
- **`PermitBuildingSheet`** (new, `src/components/prospects/`): The building. The permit as the city gave it with **Show all N fields the city sent**; the property at the county through the cached `permit-lookup` parcel call, now with the land and building values and the doing-business-as name on their own lines; every permit on the address; the city's permit search with the number copied and the county clerk's records; **Decide…**, **Look again**, and Pulled by as the door to the Who card.
- **`GoogleSearchPress`** (new): a link that opens a Google search in a new tab, the round G alone or with words.
- **`WhoPulledItSheet`**: Google the company and Google the person under the title, the G beside the owner of record, **Their other permits**, and **The state's business record** (the Comptroller's franchise tax search with the company's name copied, or the owner of record's when the permit names a person).
- **`ProspectsResearchTab`**: the address is a press that opens The building, on the ledger and on the map's card; the G sits beside the hint under each name.
- The render smoke stubs the Maps key, so a worktree with one in `.env.local` no longer mounts the other canvas.
- Guide `research-permits-for-work-to-bid`: the building, the Google press, the two new parts of the card.
