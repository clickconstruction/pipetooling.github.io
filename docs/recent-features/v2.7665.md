# v2.7665 — Papers before a job opens: lists, searches, pushes and schedule emails keep a locked job to its number (2026-11-17)

Papers before a job opens, step 5 of seven: the doors that never ask row security. One migration, `20261118020000_job_papers_lists.sql` (its page is `docs/migrations/20261118020000_job_papers_lists.md`), one shared edge guard, seven edge functions, and the screen's words. The edge functions do nothing until deployed.

## What is new

- **Two functions that ask the lock about a named person**: `job_shut_to_user(job, user)` and `jobs_shut_to_user(user)`, service role only.
- **Five list functions** answer a locked job with its number alone, or leave its reports out: `search_jobs_ledger`, `list_assigned_jobs_for_dashboard`, `list_superintendent_jobs_for_dashboard`, `list_jobs_for_tally`, `list_reports_with_job_info`. No returned column changed.
- **A locked job no longer answers a search by its name, its address or its development.** Blanking the answer alone would have left a way round: type the name, get the number.
- **The two schedule reads** blank a block's job name, address and note for a recipient locked out of its job: `list_job_schedule_blocks_for_schedule_email` and `list_schedule_blocks_for_share`.
- **One shared edge guard**, `supabase/functions/_shared/jobPapersLock.ts`: `jobShutToUser`, `jobsShutToUser`, `recipientsNotShut`, `refuseShutJob`. It imports nothing and fails open.
- **Seven edge functions ask it**: `plan-set-files` (the author's team check), `drive-link` (an estimator's asks about a job's folder, and both searches), `notify-note-tag`, `notify-team-lead-clock`, `send-report-notification`, `notify-dispatch-request` (a locked recipient is left out of the push) and `schedule-share-dispatch` (the board is blanked for a job any recipient is locked out of).
- **The screens** no longer say the prompt is the only lock: `JOB_PAPERS_LOCK_STAGE` is `database`, and the office's guide says what locks and what the app cannot close.

## Choices worth knowing

- **A locked person is left out of a push, not sent a blanked one.** One push carries one title and body for all its recipients. The Needs you line *Papers to sign* tells them the job waits.
- **The guard fails open.** A read that fails reads as not locked. Nothing may change for anyone until a job requires a paper, and the lock must never stop a notice on a job that requires none.
- **`job-photos` and `schedule-day-email-dispatch` did not change.** The first asks `can_report_stage_progress` through the caller's client, which step 4 already closed. The second reads through the schedule function, which now blanks by recipient.
- **A shared schedule is blanked for everyone when one recipient is locked out.** One email goes to all of them.

## Verified

- The `job-papers-lists` bed in CI with the two beds before it: before any paper every list answers as it did; a paper leaves a locked helper the job's number alone in the search, the assigned jobs and the tally jobs; the job does not answer to its name or address; the office and the primary still find it by name; the lock about a named user agrees with the lock's own question for every role; the schedule reads blank the block for a locked recipient; signed, every list names the job again.
