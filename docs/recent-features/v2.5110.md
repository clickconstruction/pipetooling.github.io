# v2.5110 — What customers see: the sample portals' money, through the portal's builders (2026-10-09)

What customers see #103 (`to-dos/customer-samples-through-kernels.md`), PR 2 of 4, the portal's money. Built by PUNCHLIST Helper 14. No migration. One edge function's bundle changes: `customer-portal`.

## Before

`sampleCustomerPortalResponse` wrote every bill and lien waiver of the three sample portals by hand: the homeowner (`?t=sample`), the general contractor (`sample-gc`) and the GC-mode owner (`sample-owner`). It sent no `sharedBills` and no `checks`, so the sample never showed *Your customers' open bills* or *Your payments*. Nothing held it to what the real portal builds, and it had drifted (below).

## The rows and the builders

`supabase/functions/_shared/customerSampleFixtures.ts`:

- **`samplePortalMoneyRows(state, todayYmd, appOrigin)`** holds each sample's rows in the shapes `customer-portal` reads: the jobs, every sent bill (billed or paid), the payments, the signed lien releases, the payment moves and the other parties' names. The sample names stay in `customerSample.ts`.
- **`samplePortalMoney(rows)`** makes the calls the live branch makes for a real viewer:
  - `buildPortalBills` and `buildPortalSharedBills` on the open bills (`billed`, on jobs that carry open bills) and those jobs' payments, with every sent bill as the payment rule's list;
  - `buildPortalChecks` and `buildPortalWaivers` on every sent bill and payment;
  - the AS GC tag only on the merged link;
  - the balance as the sum of the owed bills.
  Each waiver half goes out with `pdfUrl: null` and `pdfPath: null`: the function signs a real PDF into a link, and a sample has no file.
- The three payloads send `bills`, `sharedBills`, `totalDue`, `waivers` and `checks` from it. Agreements, test reports, stages and the rest are untouched: PR 3 takes the sections the sample leaves out.

## What changed on the samples, and why

Every amount, date, balance and waiver state is the same. The differences are where the hand-written sample said what a real portal would not:

- **Check reference**: each bill's is its job number (`1001`, `0994`, `1002`, `0998`, `1010`), as `buildPortalBills` gives it. The sample had made up `WH-1001`, `KF-0994`, `CB-1002`, `HR-0998` and `SR-1010`.
- **The GC's bills are not tagged AS GC.** Only the merged `all` link tags them (`markGcRows`); a scoped `gc` link never does. The sample had tagged both with an owner's name.
- **The GC's check payment** reads *check #4398*, where it read *check*: the payment now carries its check number, so *Your payments* can show the check.
- **The GC's waivers** carry the waiver builder's job label, *1002 · Cedar Bend Apartments*, and each half its release id. *Bill 2 of 3* and *Bill 1 of 3* are now the builder's count of Cedar Bend's sent bills, the owner's change order being the third.
- **New on the GC sample**: *Your customers' open bills* with the $1,850 change order billed to Cedar Bend Owner LLC, and *Your payments* with checks #4417 and #4398.
- **New on the homeowner and owner samples**: *Your payments*, holding the homeowner's $640 card payment and the owner's unpaid pay application.
- `billedTo: null` is gone from each bill. The real payload never had it.
