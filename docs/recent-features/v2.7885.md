# v2.7885 — Stages: the superintendent's doors (2026-11-19)

Punch list #397, PR 5 of the train. Client only. No migration and no edge function.

## Why

The review found that a superintendent could see a stage and not reach it. The Projects board opened for him and only the search led there. A board card on a phone said *3 stages* and showed none, since a stage's card opens on hover. And nothing on his pace card led from a stage to what stands in its way. The pace card's rail (punch list #396) answered the middle of that. This is what was left.

## What changed

- **A stage on the rail opens its checklist.** `PaceRail` takes `checks`, one per row. A stage with questions draws its name as a press, with *2 of 3 answered* under it, and the press opens that stage's sheet. A stage with no question stays words: an empty sheet is nothing to open. The card finds a stage's checklist by its line, so `ProjectStage` now carries the line's `id`.
- **All projects.** A door on the pace card's foot to `/projects-board`.
- **The Projects bar on a superintendent's Dashboard.** `Dashboard.tsx` draws `DashboardProjectsStrip` for him above Recent reports. The leader and a dev have it under Money in, and a superintendent has no money section. He reads it and sets nothing: the cards take `canSetExpectedHours`, which leaves him out.
- **The strip's stage count is a press.** Under a strip, *3 stages ▾* lists each stage with its hours of the budget and the crew's percent, or *not reported*. Hover still works at a desk. The words *hover one* are gone.
- `paceStageCheck` and `paceStageList` in `lib/jobMode/paceCard.ts` hold the words.
- Guides: `job-mode-clocking`, `start-here-as-a-superintendent`, `see-where-every-project-stands-and-set-its-expected-hours`.

## A correction to PR 3's notes

v2.7831's fragment and its guide said a job with Rough In, Top Out and Trim Set lines takes the company's stage checklist, so the Pipeline reads *not ready* until the crew answers. That is the rule, but nothing triggers it today: the company has no questions written. The four questions that exist were each added to one job's stage. The guide `bring-a-bids-stages-onto-its-job` now says *once the office has written its questions*. The owner was told.
