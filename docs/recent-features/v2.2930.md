# v2.2930 — Your days on the sub portal (2026-09-06)

PR 4 of the three-party scheduling train (prototype: artifact 3a540149). Stacked on v2.2929. The owner's rule: the day is the unit, not the booking — a sub can be on two or three jobs in one day, and a day cell says only how many ("one job", "two jobs", spelled out).

## Data

- Migration [`20260906060000_person_availability.sql`](../migrations/20260906060000_person_availability.md): `person_availability` (person × day, `kind = off`, `source`), office-readable, written by the submit function.
- `sub-portal` returns `days: { bookings, offDays }` — bookings from every live signed order (the sub's pick, else the office's span, with the stage's line-item name and the job's address), plus office-set sheets with a `job_date` that no order already covers; off days from a week back.
- `submit-sub-portal` gains `day_off` (`day`, `off: boolean`): upsert / delete on `person_availability`, refuses past days, and when the day sits under a pick drops dispatch note `sub_day_off_collision` ("… marked Sep 22 off — #1004 · Top-out is scheduled over it"). Returns `collisions`.

## Portal

- Kernel [`src/lib/subPortal/subPortalDays.ts`](../../src/lib/subPortal/subPortalDays.ts) (+test): `subPortalDayItems`, `subPortalMonthCells` (Mon–Fri cells for N weeks from the Monday on/before today), `subPortalDayCountWord` (en / es words through six), `subPortalOffDayCollisions`, `mapsUrlFor`.
- [`SubPortalYourDays`](../../src/components/subPortal/SubPortalYourDays.tsx) (+render test): the cells (booked shade, striped off, split bar under a multi-job day), the day sheet (rows with a copper edge for the sub's own dates, grey for office-set; full address; **Map ›**), **Mark this day off** / **Take this day back** with the booked warning; mounted under the offers section; sample mode toggles locally. Strings in `subPortalI18n.ts` (en / es); the demo fixture carries a two-job day and an off day.
- Client payload: `SubPortalPayload.days` parsed defensively (`parseDays`).

## Office

- Sub Board (`subBoardLanes`): `buildSubBoardLanes(…, offDaysByPerson)` — lanes carry `offDays` stripes, bars get `overOffDay` (red outline like an overlap); `ProjectsForecastSubsTab` loads `person_availability` for the window and paints the stripes and legend.
- Dispatch Subs lanes (`subDispatch.buildSubLanes(…, offDaysByPerson)`, `fetchSubOffDaysForRange`): hatched "off" cells in `HubSubsLanes`.
