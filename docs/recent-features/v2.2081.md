# v2.2081 — Bid tab capture: high, low, and rank from the bottom (2026-08-22)

Owner ask (follow-on to the v2.2080 clarity pass): "capture the high, low, and
how close to the bottom we were — like #2 from the bottom — for each bid."
Mockup approved before build. First stage of the compare-and-learn loop: the
structured numbers land on the bid; the aggregate compare view is a planned
follow-up.

- **Migration `20260822062831_bid_tab_capture`**: `bids.bid_tab_low`,
  `bid_tab_high` (numeric), `bid_tab_rank_from_low`, `bid_tab_bidder_count`
  (integer), all nullable — see `docs/migrations/20260822062831_bid_tab_capture.md`.
- **Kernel `src/lib/bidTabCapture.ts`** (+17 tests): call-shorthand money
  parsing ("230k", "1.2m", "$230,000"), whole-number rank/count parsing,
  cross-field validation (low ≤ high, rank ≤ count), derived insight — % over
  the low from our own `bid_value`, "we were the low bid — price wasn't the
  reason" when rank 1, warn when our number exceeds the reported high —
  plus summary line, history-note line, update patch, and 0–100 range-strip
  position.
- **Shared UI `src/components/bids/BidTabCapturePanel.tsx`**:
  `BidTabCapturePanel` (fields phrased like the call: "Low bid · High bid ·
  We were #_ from the bottom, of _ bids on the tab", live insight line, Save +
  secondary action) and `BidTabRecordedLine` (BID TAB pill + one-line summary +
  low→high range strip with an amber us-dot + edit).
- **Waiting to hear**: tapping **Bid tab received** now toggles the capture
  panel instead of logging instantly; **Save bid tab** writes the columns and
  logs the touch with a rich history note ("Bid tab recorded — low $230,000 ·
  …"), **Log without numbers** keeps the old one-tap behavior. Recorded tabs
  render on the bid card with edit.
- **Why we lost**: every lost-bid card shows the recorded tab line or a
  "record the bid tab →" link opening the same panel (patch-only save — no
  contact stamp; recording a tab retroactively isn't a builder touch).
- **Edit + Remove**: the recorded line's *edit* reopens the panel prefilled;
  a quiet *Remove bid tab* (shown only when editing an existing tab) clears
  the columns — patch-only in both lenses, no history note.
- Help guide `follow-up-with-builders.md` updated (both lens sections).
