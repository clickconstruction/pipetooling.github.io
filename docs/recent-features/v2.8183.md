# v2.8183 — Contracts and clauses: a settings list says what puts a person in breach (2026-11-21)

Punch list #389, step 4 of eight, its second part of three: the Contract Book holds the lines the office's settings page will show. No migration, and nothing to deploy. The database's stop on the shape of these lines came in v2.8182.

Taunya chose the whole walked flow for a job's page (the card, settled 10): every topic shows *What puts a person in breach* and *What the site must have*. Those lines have to live beside the choice they describe, in the Book, so a page never says more than its clause. Until this PR the settings list had nowhere to keep them, and saving a list would have dropped any that were there.

## What changed

- **A choice takes breach lines and site lines.** In **Settings list**, under each choice: *What puts a person in breach* and *What the site must have*, one line each. A choice that leaves the topic out has neither. With **Show the Spanish fields** ticked, each has its Spanish twin, and an empty one shows the English.
- **A notice and a site note.** At the foot of the window: *The notice a job's settings page opens on*, with a title, the words beside its box and its lines, and *The note on matching the paper to the site*, with a title, an opening line and its lines. A note with no line is no note.
- **The kernel keeps them** (`paperSettings.ts`): `SettingChoice` gains `breach`, `site` and their Spanish twins, and `SettingsDef` gains `notice` and `siteNote` (`PaperNote`). `parseSettingsDef` reads them, `settingsDefJson` writes them tidied, `settingsDefProblems` holds a choice to twenty lines of each and a note to twelve, and `choiceLines` reads a choice's lines in one language.
- **An old list is untouched.** A list with none of the new keys reads with none and writes byte for byte as it did, so saving it unchanged is still *Nothing changed.*

## How a line is typed

A box is one line each. The list keeps an empty line while a person types, so Enter starts a new one. When the list is saved each line is trimmed and the empty ones are left out.

## What it does not do yet

The page on a job that shows these lines is the third part. Nothing reads them yet but the Settings list window.
