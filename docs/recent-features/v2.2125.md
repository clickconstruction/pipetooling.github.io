# v2.2125 — Partner card: signed, colored balances + zero-crossing note (2026-08-22)

Owner noticed that a partner reading the card could be confused when a week flips sides: Bryan's May 10 read `Week opened $546.39 · you owe Click` → `Labor +$606.64` → `Week closed $60.25 · Click owes you` — two similar-looking positive numbers with a plus line between them that doesn't connect, because the direction words were silently carrying the sign. Four of Bryan's twenty weeks cross zero. Three options were mocked on his real weeks (artifact "Ledger Direction Options"); owner chose **A + C**.

- **A — signed, colored ends.** [`DashboardPartnerLedgerSection.tsx`](../../src/components/dashboard/DashboardPartnerLedgerSection.tsx): `Week opened` and `Week closed` / `So far this week` render `signedBalanceLabel(n)` (`+$60.25` / `−$546.39` / `$0.00`) in `balanceColor(n)` (green / `--text-red-600` / muted); the direction words stay as the sub-label under each end (opening's words move from inline to a sub-label so both ends share one layout; `even` for $0). The card now literally adds: −546.39 + 606.64 = +60.25 — the same convention as the Full ledger's balance column and the print statement (print now uses `signedBalanceLabel` too, so it shows `+` as well). This revisits the v2.2100 "words, not signs" frame, which was chosen looking at a non-crossing week.
- **C — crossing note.** New `findZeroCrossings(opening, lines)` in [`partnerWeeks.ts`](../../src/lib/partnerLedger/partnerWeeks.ts) walks opening → lines (null-amount lines don't move it; landing exactly on $0 is not a crossing; multiple crossings supported) and `WeekCard` gains `crossings: WeekCrossing[]` for closed and live cards. The component renders `crossingText()` — *crossed $0 — cleared the $546.39 you owed and went $60.25 ahead* / *used up the $3.85 you were ahead and went $496.15 behind* — as a muted italic row after the line that crossed. Invisible on weeks that don't cross.
- `partnerLedgerFormat.ts`: `signedBalanceLabel`, `crossingText` (+2 tests); `partnerWeeks.test.ts` +4 (`findZeroCrossings` cases incl. Bryan May 10 / May 24 shapes and card stamping). Help guide updated. Client-only.
