# v2.6829 — A customer who pays in kind has a card and a ledger on their page (punch list #301, PR 2) (2026-11-10)

PR 1 (v2.6827) laid the tables. This PR is the first screen: the owner's pick of 2026-11-10 was the deal on the customer's page, a ledger of lines entered as needed (an amount, a date, a memo), each counted as overhead unless it is put on a job.

## What changed

- **The card**, `src/components/inKind/CustomerInKindCard.tsx`, on the customer page under the money strip, for the office (`isOfficeRole`). One card per open deal: the terms in a sentence (`termsSentence`), a meter of what is paid in kind against what the deal's jobs came to, **Paid in kind so far**, **Still to come**, and for a monthly deal the month the last credit falls in (`lastCreditMonth`). Presses: **Open the ledger**, **Add a line**. A customer with no deal and money open gets one quiet press, **Set up paying in kind**. An ended deal stays as a small link to its ledger.
- **The ledger**, `InKindLedgerWindow.tsx`: each line with its day, its memo, a chip for what it counts as, and *Went to 1171 $885.00*; the lines taken back behind a press, crossed out; the In kind payments typed on the bills by hand before the ledger, counted as paid and listed on **Show them**; the bills oldest first with the next in line marked. **Take back** asks in a window whose press names the amount. **Count as overhead** moves a line off a job.
- **Add a line**, `InKindAddLineWindow.tsx`: amount, date, memo, and **Counts as** (Overhead, or A job found by number, name or address through `search_jobs_ledger`). **Where it goes** is `in_kind_line_add` with `p_dry_run`, asked a moment after the amount settles, so the screen shows the split the save will make. A bill on Stripe is named as passed over. The press is dead until the line is whole and reads *Add the line for $885.00*.
- **The terms**, `InKindTermsWindow.tsx`: what is given, a note, the jobs it pays (the customer's jobs and the ones they are the GC on; a new deal starts with the open billed jobs ticked; a job another deal pays is locked), and the monthly fields for a deal with a schedule. **End the arrangement** and, while nothing stands on it, **Remove it**, each behind a window that names the act.
- **The money strip**, `src/pages/CustomerDetail.tsx` over `src/lib/inKind/customerInKindStrip.ts`: the open balance's chip reads *scheduled in kind, none late* and loses its amber; the collected line reads *$10,500.00 paid, all of it in kind*; Pays in explains a missing speed.
- **`supabase/functions/_shared/customerProfileStats.ts`**: `customerMoneyStats` returns `scheduledInKind` (open money on jobs a deal pays, never aged) and `collectedInKind`; `customerDaysToPay` skips an In kind payment. `fetchCustomerProfile` reads `jobs_ledger.in_kind_arrangement_id` and each payment's `payment_type`.
- **Reads and writes**, `src/lib/inKind/inKindIo.ts`; **words**, `src/lib/inKind/inKindWords.ts`, held to the plain words by its test.
- **Help**: `keep-track-of-work-paid-in-kind.md` (new), a pointer from `record-payments-so-they-count.md`.

## Decisions taken with the build

- **Money is read from the bills, the story from the lines.** What a deal has paid is the In kind payments on its jobs, and what is left is those jobs' open balances. So the payments typed by hand before the ledger existed count without being rewritten, and a payment someone removes from a bill by hand cannot leave the card wrong.
- **The first month of a monthly deal is the first month the app expects a line for**, not the month the deal began. The months before it were typed by hand and stand as payments.
- **The set-up press shows only where money is open.** It is one quiet line, and a page for a customer who owes nothing does not carry it. A partner who gives before any work is billed is still the owner's open call.
- **The windows take turns.** Add a line and the terms open in place of the ledger and hand back to it, so a phone never stacks two sheets.
