## What changed

- **One loader for a billed read** (`src/lib/billing/loadUnlinkedMoney.ts`). What a read of the open bills and their linked payments lacks:
  - the unlinked payments of every job with a billed bill;
  - the paid bills of each job that has unlinked money, because the rule walks every sent bill;
  - with `withPaidBillPayments`, those paid bills' own payments, because a paid bill read without them looks unpaid and takes the money.

  The board's lean header read now uses it too, with no change in what it loads.
- **The Dashboard's financials and the Billed pin** add the loader's rows to what they hand bill truth. Their bill reads carry `sequence_order`. The lists they show are unchanged.
- **The Customer Hub:**
  - `customerProfileStats` (the money strip, its aging and each job row) nets each bill with `appliedByInvoiceUnderRule`.
  - The invoice list (`buildCustomerInvoiceRows`) runs the rule per job. A bill's *last paid* day is the newest payment the rule puts on it, so 273's oldest bill reads *partial*, last paid Mar 10.
  - Its payment read carries `job_id`, and the profile read carries `sequence_order`.
- **The customer timeline** nets each job's bills by the rule, and its bill read carries `sequence_order`.
- **The Customers list** (`customersListLcv`) uses the rule. It shows totals only, and a job's open total does not depend on which of its bills the money lands on, so its rows need no bill order.
- **`appliedByInvoiceUnderRule` finds a linked payment's job through its bill** when a bill-keyed read carries no `job_id`, as the Dashboard's and the pin's reads do. Without that, such a job's linked money would vanish from the rule and its bills would take the unlinked money.
