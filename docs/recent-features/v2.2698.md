# v2.2698 — Review hygiene: office-type card charges on field jobs (2026-09-03)

Follow-up to the parts-overlap query (audit finding 11). Of ~$62k of Mercury card allocations to field jobs in 90 days, ~$3.3k were categorised by the bank as Software / Utilities / Insurance / Internet / Medical / Education — office spend that inflates a job's parts and lowers every crew member's revenue share there. The Review tab's hygiene strip now says so and points at the fix.

## Client only — no migration

- **Kernel** [`lib/people/reviewOfficeLikeCharges.ts`](../../src/lib/people/reviewOfficeLikeCharges.ts) (+4 tests): `OFFICE_LIKE_MERCURY_CATEGORIES` (deliberately excludes FuelAndGas, VehicleExpenses, Retail, ProfessionalServices, Fees, GovernmentServices — those can be real job purchases and are the separate "label fuel as fuel" question), `mercuryCategoryString` (the column is jsonb holding a JSON string), `summarizeOfficeLikeCharges(rows, officeJobLedgerId)` → $ / charge count / distinct field jobs / ranked `category · counterparty` lines. Loader `loadOfficeLikeChargeRows({ startYmd, endYmd })`: transactions posted in the window (fetched a day wide, re-bucketed with `denverCalendarDayKey`, category filtered client-side — a server-side `in.(…)` on the jsonb column fails), then their allocations via the chunked reader.
- **Hygiene item** `officeLikeCharges` in `buildReviewHygiene(rows, approvals, officeLike)` — headline "$X of office-type charges on N field jobs", detail with the top three `counterparty $` and the caveat that a landfill fee filed as Utilities is a legitimate job cost, link **Sort in Banking** → `/banking?tab=sorting`. Sits between the assumed-% line and the salaried line. +2 tests.
- **Tab**: `reviewOfficeLikeCharges` state loaded by an effect keyed on the current rows' identity (ranked view only, request-id guarded, fails soft to no line), office job id via the shared `fetchOverheadOfficeJobLedgerIdFromAppSettings`.
- Live as dev, Last 30 days: "$478 of office-type charges on 3 field jobs" (Post Oak Landfill $397, City of Shavano Park $68, Dropbox $13); Last 90 days reads ~$3.2k on 8 jobs led by Auto Group software $1,700. `BooksAndNewspaper` was dropped from the list after the live pass — the bank files city permit/records fees there.

## The fuel question (not built here)

Banking → Accounting already has built-in Schedule C labels including **Fuel / Gas** (`fuel_gas`), Car and Truck Expenses, and Vehicle Maintenance & Repairs, applied per transaction by drag-sort and by `mercury_accounting_label_rules`. "Labelling fuel as fuel" is therefore a rule (bank category `FuelAndGas` → `fuel_gas`) plus consumers reading the label — Review's drawer splitting "parts, subs & team labor" into parts / fuel & vehicle / other, and Job Summary doing the same.
