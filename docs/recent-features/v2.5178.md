# v2.5178 — GC mode: a trade partner signs its lien waivers in its portal (2026-10-10)

The owner's call 2 of the trade partner portal's P5 (`to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/portal-p5.md` on branch `spike/gc-mode`), answered 2026-10-10 in the owner's words: *"a trade partner may sign its lien waivers electronically in its portal."* The lead relayed them; the owner confirmed in the Portal helper's own chat the same day (*"I approve, yes"*). No migration.

- **The flag**: `WAIVER_SIGN_LIVE` is true in `src/lib/gc/drawEmail.ts` and in its copy in `supabase/functions/_shared/gcTradeSubmit.ts`, which `gcTradeSubmit.test.ts` holds equal. It turns on every lien waiver a trade signs in its portal:
  - `unconditional_waiver` on a paid draw (P5c-3b), or the final release on the final one;
  - `pay_app` and `final_pay_app` (P5c-3c-ii), each signing its conditional waiver.
- **What changes for the trade**:
  - The job page draws **Sign the unconditional waiver** on a paid draw.
  - The pay application's door opens **Fill out pay application** in place of the line that said to email it, and the closeout opens the final one.
  - The home's Needs you lists the waivers' to-dos (`portalHomeTodos` now passes `portalTodos` whole).
  - Each signature is the typed name, the e-sign consent and its ledger row (`esign_consents`, `gc_draw` keyed by the draw), on the Release of Lien window's own form.
- **The emails**: a paid draw's email adds *Sign the unconditional waiver for it in your portal* (or the final release). The closeout's two emails (P5c-4) ask for the final pay application and the final release in the portal.
- **P5a-2's signed PDF** (v2.5175, #5340, merged first) files each signed waiver as a PDF in the job's Drive folder behind the same flag, so it turns on with this one: the Draws and Closeout windows link each PDF.
- **Off again**: setting both copies to false restores the held behaviour. `closeoutEmail.off.test.ts` and `GcTradePortal.payApp.render.test.tsx` keep it tested with the flag mocked off.
- **Tests**: `drawEmail.test.ts` expects the waiver line; `gcTradeSubmit.test.ts` holds no kind back; `tradePortalPage.test.ts`, `closeoutEmail.test.ts`, `GcTradePortal.job.render.test.tsx`, `GcTradePortal.waiver.render.test.tsx` and `GcTradePortal.payAppSend.render.test.tsx` read the flag on, unmocked.
- **Docs**: `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`, `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` and `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`, amended where they said held. Guides: `share-a-trade-partner-its-portal`, `see-what-a-trade-partner-sends-from-its-portal` and `pay-a-trades-draw`.
