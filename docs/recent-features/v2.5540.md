# v2.5540 — Phone and texts: Reply push shows the office’s wait, and the owner can change it (2026-10-14)

The owner's model of 2026-10-14: one company number shared by the whole team, every text seen and answered by anyone. R5's push (v2.5479) already pushes the office after a wait when nobody has answered a reply, but the wait, `app_settings` `messages_office_wait_minutes` (15, R5's SQL `20261013190000`, with its own owner-or-dev UPDATE policy), had no card: the map's switches table read *set by a dev (no card yet)*. So "instant pushes to everyone" needed a dev at the database, when it is only the wait at 0. No migration, no edge function: `messages-notify` already reads the row every minute (`messagesReplyPush.ts`), and `pushesFor` (`notifyRule.ts`) already clamps it to 0 or more.

- **`src/lib/messages/replyPushSwitch.ts`**: the wait's words (`OFFICE_WAIT_LABEL` *The office hears after*, `OFFICE_WAIT_FIELD`, `OFFICE_WAIT_SAVE`, `OFFICE_WAIT_WHAT`, the toast, the refusal, `OFFICE_WAIT_BAD`), `OFFICE_WAIT_MAX` (240), `readOfficeWait(valueNum)` (a whole number 0 or more, else the default 15), `parseOfficeWait(raw)` (a whole number 0 to 240, else null) and `officeWaitLine(minutes)` (*The office hears at once, with whoever has it.* at 0; *after N minutes when nobody writes back* otherwise).
- **`replyPushSwitchIo.ts`**: `load` reads both rows in one `in('key', …)` and returns `{ on, waitMinutes }`; `setWait(minutes)` updates `value_num` on the wait's row and refuses with `OFFICE_WAIT_NOT_SAVED` when no row comes back (the policy, or a row not yet pushed).
- **`ReplyPushSettingsCard.tsx`**: under the switch's state line, a block (`data-reply-push-wait`): the what, *The office hears after* [number] *minutes*, **Save the wait** (lit only when the typed number is whole, in range and different from the row's), and the meaning line (`data-reply-push-wait-line`, or `OFFICE_WAIT_BAD` while the field does not hold a number). The owner and a dev see the card as before (`canSwitchReplyPush`).
- **Tests**: `replyPushSwitch.test.ts` (+3: the read's default and rounding, the parse's range, the line's words, every new sentence plain), `replyPushSwitchIo.test.ts` (new, 4: the two-row read, the defaults, each write on its own row, the refusals), `ReplyPushSettingsCard.render.test.tsx` (+3: the field and its line at 15 and at 0, a save of 0 and the refusals of -3 and 500, a refused write).
- **Guide** *turn on the reply push* gains **Change the wait**; `MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md`'s switches table gives the wait its own row, and the Glossary, `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`, `SETTINGS_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md` 7e and `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` §22 carry the clause.

Left as it was: office hours (`messages_office_hours_v1`) still have no card, and the push's rule itself (the tech at once, the office after the wait, the office first after hours) is unchanged.
