# v2.7800 — Trips: a deleted trip is kept, the ground (2026-11-18)

## Why

The owner asked on 2026-11-18, after the look at Put on a bill: *"Can someone see all the deleted trips in the app? Do we have the option after someone deletes a trip to undo it or permanently delete it"*. The answer was no to both. **Delete this trip** removed the trip at once. Its toast offered Undo for a minute, from the browser's own memory, and after that nothing could bring the trip back. `trips` was never among the tables the deleted-records archive covers. A live read proved it: a job line removed that hour was in the archive, and a trip deleted the same hour was not.

A trip is the record of work past the price: who went, which days, who asked. Since Put on a bill (v2.7763) a customer's line can come from one. Any of five roles can delete it with one confirm. He said *"Yes, build it"* to keeping deleted trips and putting them back.

This is PR 1 of 2, the ground. It carries no client code, so the push can follow its merge at any hour. PR 2 is the **Deleted** fold on the Trips look.

## What changed

One migration, `20261118100000_trips_deleted.sql` (`docs/migrations/20261118100000_trips_deleted.md`). No new table.

- **`trips` joins the deleted-records archive** through the archive's own generic capture, grouped by the trip's id.
- **`trip_people` and `trip_jobs` are captured only when the trip itself went** (`trip_child_archive_on_delete`). `trip_save` deletes and inserts both on every save, and a deleted job takes its link. Neither is a deleted trip. The test is the trip's own row: on a cascade from the trip it is already gone.
- **A trip made again settles its archive rows** (`trips_settle_archive`, after insert). The Undo, Put back, the dev seat and the dev's own restore all make the trip again under its own id, so none of them leaves a living trip listed as deleted.
- **`trip_deleted_list(site)`**, for the trips office: the site's deleted trips that nothing has put back, newest first, each whole in the shape the app reads a trip, with who deleted it and when. A job, a roster row or a login gone since is left out, so putting the trip back still saves.
- **No new write.** Putting a trip back is `trip_save` under the trip's own id, the door the Undo already uses. It gives the trip the number it had.

## Decisions

- **The archive, not a deleted mark on the trip.** A soft delete would have put a filter in every read of `trips`, the phone's and the dev seat's among them. The archive already exists, already purges after 90 days, and already has a dev restore.
- **Rows as they were.** Each archived row is `to_jsonb(OLD)` of its own table, so the dev's `restore_deleted_records` works on a trip's bundle with no change. A single row holding the whole trip would have restored a trip with no people there.
- **No "delete forever" press.** The purge is the forever. Nobody asked to remove a kept trip sooner, and a press that does it is a second way to lose the record.
- **The office reads its own site's deleted trips. The archive table stays dev-only.** The function gates on `trips_office()`, the roles that already read and delete every trip.

## Left as it is

- A trip's line on a job loses its mark when the trip is deleted and does not get it back when the trip is put back. The delete's question says so (v2.7768). Tying it again is a change to that rule and is not in this train.
- In the dev's Recently deleted a trip's bundle reads by the start of its id. That list's labels do not know a trip.
- A trip held by a job with no development is archived and can be restored by a dev. The list reads by site, since no screen shows a job's own trips.

## Tests
