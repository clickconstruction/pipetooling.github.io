# v2.4324 — Pipeline: one button for each thing on a job row (2026-10-01)

No migration, no edge function. Grace, 2026-10-01, on job 1009's Billed row: *"we have reports, but they are also accessible when a user clicks on the button see all-3. I think this might be a repetitive use of buttons. Can you look at this row holistically … where we are not being excessive. But we are being intuitive."* The holistic read found four places the row opened the same thing twice; the canvas https://claude.ai/artifact/WsmWFwKjA9nHxLHRgxv3AM (boards *Today*, *Proposed*, *Details*) proposed one door for each thing, and she approved it as drawn (*"your proposal one door for each thing is great"*), with the recommended answers to its two open calls (narrow screens and phone cards follow; the chip stays, the pen goes).

## What was doubled

| Thing | Doors before | Door now |
|---|---|---|
| The job's notes and reports | **Reports** pill, the activity box's **See all N** — both `ctx.openJobActivityExpand`, the same window, opened on *All* | **See all** |
| The job's clock sessions | **Sessions** link (Session notes pinned, last 30 days) and the ⏱ man-hours chip (`JobHoursStoryModal`, every session with its note) — the same sessions | the ⏱ chip; its window links to Session notes |
| The contract | the ✍ quick action and the contract chip — both `onOpenJobContract` (v2.2681 added both) | the chip |
| The bill date | Crew & Dates *BILLED Thu Sep 3 · 4 weeks ago* and the dates block's *Billed Sep 3 · 28 d ago* | the dates block |

Kept on purpose: the lien hammer and the *Lien notice* row both open the Lien window (the hammer keeps its place in the v2.4305 icon block, and rows with no deadline still need it); the crew name and the hours chip are two depths of one story.

## What changed

- **Kernel** [`stagesRowDoors.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/stagesRowDoors.ts) (+ test, 12 cases): `seeAllDoorWords(count, reports)` (*See all* · *See all 3* · *See all 9 · 1 report*), `datesBlockBilledYmd(inv)` (the dates block's Billed date, `billedReferenceYmd`), `crewBillLineRepeatsDates` (a *Billed* line on a `billed` job whose row draws a dates block with a Billed line; *Paid* always stays).
- **[`jobsStagesRowShared.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/jobsStagesRowShared.tsx)**: `renderStagesViewReportsButton` → `renderStagesSeeAllButton` — the expand glyph and `seeAllDoorWords(null, job.report_count)`, quiet when there are no reports and bordered blue with the count when there are (the v2.1475 contrast), `aria-label` *Expand job activity* like the box's; the Sessions link is gone. `renderStagesJobCellActivityFooter`'s `hideReportsButton` → `hideSeeAllButton`. `renderStagesQuickActionsStack` loses the ✍ button. `renderStagesFieldAndBillingLines(ctx, job, { datesBilledYmd })` drops the bill line by the kernel. The man-hours chip passes `onOpenSessionNotes` (from `openSessionNotesForJob`, office roles) with its work-story target.
- **Rows**: `JobsStagesTable` and `StagesUnifiedJobRow` hide the pill when `useWideViewport1100` is true (the box's strip is the door there, as the ▶ toggle beside the name already was, v2.1588); `StagesUnifiedJobRow` and `StagesUnifiedInvoiceRow` pass `datesBilledYmd` when the table hands them `billedLienRunway`. Standalone bill rows draw no box, so they keep the pill. Phone cards (`JobsStagesCardList`) draw the same pill and pass the Session notes opener with the hours chip.
- **[`JobsStagesActivityBox`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsStagesActivityBox.tsx)**: See all reads `seeAllDoorWords(feed.length, reports in the feed)` once loaded, `job.report_count` before; a report entry's number wears the link blue with the report icon (`aria-label` *Entry N, a report*).
- **[`JobHoursStoryModal`](../../src/components/jobs/JobHoursStoryModal.tsx)** (+ render test): `onOpenSessionNotes` draws **Session notes ›** in the header (under the totals on a phone); it closes the window and opens Session notes pinned to the job. Carried through `JobHoursStoryTarget` in [`JobHoursStoryModalContext`](../../src/contexts/JobHoursStoryModalContext.tsx), since the window mounts above the tab's `SessionNotesOpenerContext`.
- Session notes keeps its 30-day default (owner call 2026-09-03); the hours window is where a job's whole history reads.
- **Guides**: *open a job's activity full screen* (the three ways in), *find clock sessions booked to the wrong job* (the hours window's link; *Three doors*), *get a job contract signed* (the chip), *see what was done on a job* (new *Move a session to another job*), *read the Pipeline* (`ready-to-bill-pipeline`: See all's words, the narrow screen), *see a job's calendar* (the BILLED line steps aside on a Billed row). `GLOSSARY.md` (Contract window), `JOBS_STAGES_TAB_ARCHITECTURE.md` (the renderer).
