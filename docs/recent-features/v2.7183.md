# v2.7183 — Lien affidavits: the batch help sheet lists five blanks, each name on its own line (2026-11-13)

The owner, 2026-11-13, working from a printed batch of nine: he liked that the first line of *Each paper has six blanks* put **COUNTY OF, at the top:** on one line and its words under it, and asked for the same on *Signature* and *Day, month, year*, and for lines 5 and 6 (the notary's signature and printed name, the seal) to become one line, *Notarial certificate: notary signs and stamps*.

## What changed

- **The batch sheet's list is five blanks.** `LIEN_HELP_BLANKS_SHORT` in `src/lib/jobs/lienHelpSheet.ts` ends with `['Notarial certificate:', 'notary signs and stamps.']` in place of the *Notary* and *Seal* lines. With the signing county printed, `LIEN_HELP_BLANKS_PRINTED_COUNTY_SHORT` is three: *Signature*, *Day, month, year*, *Notarial certificate*.
- **Each name is on its own line.** `blanksBox` takes `stacked`; the batch sheet passes it, and the bold lead draws as a block with its words under it. The first line wrapped that way by chance; now every line does by rule.
- **The heading counts the list.** `lienHelpBatchBlanksTitle(printedCounty)` writes *Each paper has five blanks* or *Each paper has three things to write* from the list's own length, so the heading and the list cannot part.
- **The picture has one number for the certificate.** `lienHelpPictureHtml(county, certificateAsOne)`: the batch sheet passes `true`, and the notary's line carries one number, 5, or 3 with the county printed.
- The guide *send lien notices from the Lien desk* says what the list holds.

## Not changed

- **The cover sheet in front of one paper** (`LIEN_HELP_BLANKS`, `LIEN_HELP_BLANKS_PRINTED_COUNTY`) keeps its six blanks, or four things, in full sentences, with the seal on a line of its own and the picture's six numbers. **The guide copy** keeps its marks 5 and 6. The owner named the batch sheet; matching the other two is his call.
- The affidavit. No sworn word, blank or line on the paper moved.
- The dropped words *in ink that shows on a copy* stay on the cover sheet and the guide copy.

## How it was checked

- Unit tests in `lienHelpSheet.test.ts`: the five lines by their words, the three with the county printed, both headings, five and three stacked rows in the sheet's HTML, the picture's numbers 1 to 5 and 1 to 3, and the cover sheet and its picture unchanged. `LienDesk.render.test.tsx` reads the new heading off the desk's own **Print N for notarization**.
- A batch of nine with made-up names was printed to PDF in headless Chrome through `prependToFilingPrintHtml`, the page the print press opens, with the counties blank and with them printed. Both first pages were read: the list as asked, the picture's numbers matching it, the box shorter than before by a line, and the page count unchanged.
