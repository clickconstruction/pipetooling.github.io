# v2.7183 — Lien affidavits: the help sheets list five blanks, with the notary's signature and seal as one (2026-11-13)

The owner, 2026-11-13, working from a printed batch of nine: he liked that the first line of *Each paper has six blanks* put **COUNTY OF, at the top:** on one line and its words under it, and asked for the same on *Signature* and *Day, month, year*, and for lines 5 and 6 (the notary's signature and printed name, the seal) to become one line, *Notarial certificate: notary signs and stamps*. Asked the same day whether the cover sheet for one paper and the guide copy should count the seal the same way, he said to match them.

## What changed

- **The notary's part is one line on every sheet.** `LIEN_HELP_CERTIFICATE_BLANK` in `src/lib/jobs/lienHelpSheet.ts` is *The notarial certificate. The notary signs and stamps.* It ends the cover sheet's `LIEN_HELP_BLANKS` (five, was six) and `LIEN_HELP_BLANKS_PRINTED_COUNTY` (three, was four) in place of the two lines for the notary's signature and the seal. The batch sheet's short lists, `LIEN_HELP_BLANKS_SHORT` and `LIEN_HELP_BLANKS_PRINTED_COUNTY_SHORT`, end with `['Notarial certificate:', 'notary signs and stamps.']`.
- **The batch sheet draws each name on its own line.** `blanksBox` takes `stacked`; the batch sheet passes it, and the bold lead draws as a block with its words under it. The first line wrapped that way by chance; now every line does by rule. The cover sheet keeps a name and its sentence on one line.
- **The headings count their lists.** `lienHelpBatchBlanksTitle(printedCounty)` writes *Each paper has five blanks* or *Each paper has three things to write*, and `lienHelpCoverPrintedCountyTitle()` writes *Three things to write. Nothing else is written on the paper.*, each from its list's own length, so a heading and its list cannot part.
- **The picture has one number for the certificate.** `lienHelpPictureHtml` numbers five spots, or three with the county printed.
- **The guide copy has one mark there.** `lienHelpMarkPaper` puts mark 5 alone beside *Notary Public, State of Texas*; `LIEN_HELP_MARK_NOTES` has no 6, and 5 reads *The notarial certificate. The notary signs and stamps.* The lines to read keep their keys 7 and 8 and print as 6 and 7, since the page counts its marks with no gap.
- The guide *send lien notices from the Lien desk* says what the batch sheet's list holds.

## Not changed

- The affidavit. No sworn word, blank or line on the paper moved.

## Gone from the sheets

The words about the seal's ink (*in ink that shows on a copy*, *A paper with no seal goes back*, *The seal goes beside the notary's signature*) are on no sheet now. Putting one back is one line in `LIEN_HELP_CERTIFICATE_BLANK`.

## How it was checked

- Unit tests in `lienHelpSheet.test.ts`: the five and the three by their words on both sheets, the headings, the batch sheet's stacked rows, the picture's numbers 1 to 5 and 1 to 3 on the cover sheet and the batch sheet, the guide copy's marks with no 6 and no gap. `LienDesk.render.test.tsx` and `LienFilingTabs.helpSheet.render.test.tsx` read the new headings off the two print presses.
- With made-up names, printed to PDF in headless Chrome through the pages the presses open (`prependToFilingPrintHtml`, `lienHelpPrintHtml`), counties blank and counties printed: a batch of nine, a cover sheet with its paper, and a guide copy. The first pages were read. The cover sheet with an *Ask first* band and both codes is still one page with the paper on the next, the guide copy one page, and the batch's page count did not move.
- Not seen: a print from the live desk after the deploy.
