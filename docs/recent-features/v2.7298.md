## The picks

- **The branches' words** are *It is on a box I know* and *It is a new box*, the words the owner's session gave for the build; the card's mock-up read *A box already here* and *A new box*.
- **A row is picked, then the press names it**: two presses and two digits, as the card counted them. The press is dead until a row is picked.
- **The new box's name is the site's next free name**, never the sticker's number (the owner's call 7 of 2026-11-14); `place_sticker` would name it by the number only when the label is empty, and the sheet never sends an empty label.
- **The place is optional** on the new box, where Add a container defaults to the first place; the sheet defaults the same way and lets the chip be unpressed.
- **No outbox.** A press with no signal is refused on the sheet with its reason, not kept: a sticker is not money, but two phones may hold the same blank one.
- **The site's other jobs are read when the sheet opens**, not folded into the desk load, so the sheet costs three small reads and the tab nothing until a sticker is scanned.
