# v2.8003 — Before we start: a list on each stage of what must be true first (2026-11-19)

Punch list #382, PR 4 of eleven: the list on a stage. The first screen of the train. It reads and writes the tables of v2.7997 (`20261120033820_start_checks.sql`), so **it must not reach the live app before that migration is pushed**. No migration and no edge function of its own.

## What a person sees

The job window's **Documents** tab has a new section, **Before we start**, above **Stage checklists**. It shows for a person on the job's desk: the office, an estimator or a primary with a claim on the job, and a superintendent on their own job. The database answers that (`start_checks_desk`), so the screen never offers a press it would refuse.

Each stage line of the job is one line: its name, our days, the ready bar with its percent, and **Open**. A stage with no list reads **Write the list**.

An open stage is the mock-up's:

- **Four lines, one fact each**, with the values in a column: *We start*, *(who) says ready*, *Notice we need*, *We decide by*. The first three change where they sit. The last is worked out. The legend line under them says which look is which.
- **Move our start to (day), three days after theirs**, when their day is after ours.
- **Still open · N** on top and **Has a yes · N** below, dulled. A tick drops a bullet into the dulled list.
- **A bullet on them** is a box. One tap records the contact's word, today. Its line then reads who, how and the day, each changed in place. An open one reads when it was asked and *They say by (day)*, changed in place.
- **⋯** on a row: *How do we know?* (their word, their photo, we saw it), **Ask for a photo**, **Take off the list**, and for the office on a Rough In, Top Out or Trim Set stage, **Add to the company's list**.
- **A bullet that asks for a photo** stays open on their word, reads *Still needs a photo.*, and offers **They sent a photo** and **We saw it**.
- **A bullet of ours** is ticked by nobody. It shows where it stands and its one press.
- **Add a bullet, in plain words**, *from them* or *ours*.
- **A stage with no list** is offered a starter once: **Use the four** or **Write my own**.

Jobs → Subs has a door: **⋯** on a stage's row, **Before we start…**, which opens the job window on Documents.

## What is not here yet, on purpose

- **Ask them** is PR 6 and **Go or hold** is PR 7. An open stage ends at the add row.
- **The eye** is not drawn. A bullet on them is kept as shown, and nothing reaches the GC before PR 9, so no press here speaks of their page.
- **A bullet of ours is ticked by hand.** The readers of the order log, the trip and the permit are PR 8. Until then the starter's fourth bullet and every *ours* bullet read *Not marked done yet.* with **Mark it done**.
- **Who we ask is read, not changed here.** It is the first name of the GC's contact on the job's bid, or the GC, or the customer.

## How it is built

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `src/lib/startChecks/startCheckRows.ts` | The tables' rows read into the rules' `Stage` (`stageFromRows`), the days of notice (`noticeFor`), who we ask (`contactName`), the starter (`starterFor`, `STARTER`) and the words. Pure. |
| `src/lib/startChecks/startChecksIo.ts` | The reads and the writes. The stage lines come through `list_job_stage_progress`, the read the stage checklist uses, and our days through `start_check_windows`. A window is written as Jobs → Subs writes one. |
| `src/hooks/useJobStartChecks.ts` | One job's stages for a person on its desk. A press writes, then reads again. |
| `src/hooks/useSiteIsFar.ts` | Far or near, by the Trips look's own rule, for the default notice. Read only when nobody has set the notice. |
| `src/components/startChecks/StartCheckStage.tsx` | The open stage. PR 5's Schedule look opens the same piece. |
| `src/components/startChecks/ReadyBar.tsx` | The ready bar and its percent. |
| `src/components/jobs/JobDocumentsStartChecks.tsx` | The section on the Documents tab. |
| `src/test/startChecksMemoryIo.ts` | An io that keeps its rows in memory, for the render tests. |

Every date, count and name on the screen is drawn with the values-in-place pieces of v2.7985. The notice uses its fourth kind: on the default it is bold with the small word *default* and **Set this site's own**, and a site with its own number offers **Back to the default**. Our days use its third kind for a role that may not write a window: no press and an *office only* tag.

## Choices made here

- **The notice's default reads the site's distance** with the Trips look's rule, the address as a point against the shop and the one miles setting. An address that cannot be placed reads near.
- **Their photo is a real one.** **They sent a photo** opens the picker. The picture is shrunk, kept in the `start-check-photos` bucket, and the yes then says photo. Filing it to the job's Drive folder comes with the GC's page.
- **The company's lists are written from a stage**, one bullet at a time, and taken off from the starter that offers them. That is the stage checklist's own way, and it adds no editor.
- **The tables are younger than the generated types**, so the io casts the client as the trips io did. The types PR that follows the push removes the cast.
