# v2.6130 — A count of one digit is checked for contrast, and the counts it found now read (2026-10-30)

Punch list #194's lens, its last blind spot. `auditFrame` ([`lensAudit.ts`](../../src/lib/lens/lensAudit.ts)) measured only text of two characters or more, so a lone digit or letter was never read. A pane check during v2.6120 found what that hid: Moneyfill's six `0` counts drew `#15803d` on `#1f2937` in dark, 2.93:1, while the whole catalog read clean.

## The audit

- A one-character text is measured when it is a letter or a digit (`/^[\p{L}\p{N}]$/u`). A lone mark or symbol (`×`, `▾`, `·`, `✓`) is an icon's glyph and is still skipped.
- `scripts/visual-qa.mjs`, the same check from a terminal, takes the same rule.
- Two tests in `lensAudit.test.ts`: a lone `0` in `#15803d` on the dark card flags at 2.93:1, and so does a faint initial; seven lone glyphs in the same faint grey do not, while `× 2` does. Under the old rule the first test fails.

## The read

The whole catalog, on a `vite build` of main (`ff8a766b9`) with v2.6120 and v2.6122 merged in, served by `vite preview`: 767 screens, 3,068 frames, light and dark at 375 and 1,280, read with the new audit. None failed and none was left unsettled. Chrome closed after 639 frames, and the 2,429 it dropped were read on the second run. 718 frames flag text under 3:1, in four pairs, each a one-character count the old rule skipped:

- **The Messages count** (`MessagesBadge.tsx`) fills itself with `--text-red-700`, which is `#f87171` in dark, under white words. Its words take `var(--surface)`, v2.6068's rule for words on a text token used as a fill: white on `#b91c1c` in light as before (6.47:1), the dark surface on `#f87171` in dark (5.31:1).
- **A robot's X grade** (*Not backtestable*): `GRADE_COLORS.X` is `#6b7280`, 4.83:1 under white in both themes, the grey v2.6068 gave People → Users' *More*. The same pair draws the Counts tab's chip, the icon key and the grade window (`RobotReferenceGradeModal.tsx`).
- **A map pin's count**: both canvases drew every pin's and every cluster's count in white, whatever the fill. White read 1.92:1 on the pending bids' yellow, 2.28:1 on paid green, 2.49:1 on ready-to-bill teal and 2.54:1 on a record with no section. `pinLabelInk` ([`pinLabelInk.ts`](../../src/lib/map/pinLabelInk.ts), 4 tests) gives each fill the ink that reads better on it: the dark ink `--text-on-bright-solid` on the bright fills, v2.6070's rule for counts on bright fills, and white on the deep ones. `PinsMapCanvas.tsx` writes it into the pin's and the cluster's HTML. `PinsMapGoogleCanvas.tsx` takes the same choice as a hex (`pinLabelInkHex`), since Google may paint a label where a CSS variable does not reach.
- **The Dashboard's calendar count** (the Currently in table, a person's jobs today) drew white over the calendar icon, which is `--text-link`. That read 5.17:1 in light and 2.54:1 in dark, on `#60a5fa`. The lens measured it against the page instead, 1.12:1, because the blue is the icon beside the count, not an ancestor of it. The count now takes `var(--surface)` (5.77:1 in dark) and sits on the calendar's body drawn again under it in the same blue, so the lens reads the pair the eye sees (`DashboardTeamActiveClockStrip.tsx`). The icon looks the same, and the digit now sits wholly inside the body.
- **The pins** in `readablePairs.test.ts`, both themes: v2.6068's `--surface` on `--text-link` line names the calendar count. New lines hold white on `GRADE_COLORS.X` and `--surface` on `--text-red-700`. A new block holds a map pin's count on every job and bid section's fill, and on the no-section grey, at 4.5:1. The estimates' violet is held at the lens's 3:1, since white reads 4.23:1 on it and the dark ink 4.19:1. The block also checks that the kernel's dark ink is what `--text-on-bright-solid` draws.

## Named for their own cards

- **White words on a text token used as a fill.** 40 lines in 37 files write `color: white` beside `background: var(--text-…)` on one line, 31 of them on `--text-link`, and more spread over several lines. Every text token turns light in dark mode, so the white words read 1.05:1 (`--text-strong`) to 2.77:1 (`--text-red-700`) there. The lens drew only the Messages count. The rest sit behind presses and windows its frames do not open. v2.6068's rule fits every one: `var(--surface)` for the words reads 5.31:1 or better on each token's dark value. It would make a codemod sweep like v2.6120's.
- **The audit reads a background from ancestors only.** Text placed over a sibling's fill, such as a count drawn over an icon, is measured against whatever lies behind both. The calendar count was the one case this read showed, and it now carries its own ground. Reading the element painted under the text's box (`document.elementsFromPoint`) would close the gap for every such overlay.

- **The estimates' violet pin**, white at 4.23:1, the better of the two inks.

Colour only, apart from the calendar count's ground; no word changed. No migration, no edge function.
