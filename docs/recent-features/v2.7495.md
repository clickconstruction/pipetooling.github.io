# v2.7495 — Lien desk: Remind texts the leader the link (2026-11-16)

The owner, 2026-11-16, on v2.7491's open question (*if we pressed remind, should it send the leader a text and a link?*): *Yes, add a text*. One edge function (`notify-lien-approval`, redeploy) and the client. No migration. Stacked on v2.7491.

## Why

The office's **Remind** reached the leader by a push, or by email when no phone was registered for him. A push needs the app set up on his phone with notifications allowed, and an email needs his inbox read. The owner's premise for this work is that the leader does not open the app, so the reminder could go out and reach nobody. A text needs neither.

## What landed

- **`notify-lien-approval`** texts each leader it reminds, beside the push or the email and never in place of one. It is the receipt sweep's kind of text (`receipt-request`, v2.7142): his `users.phone` through `toE164`, placed on his team contact (`placeNumber(…, 'team')`), then `sendText` with purpose `nudge` from the team box's number. So Settings → Phone and texts → **Send texts** gates it, a STOP and the pause hold, the carriers' opt-out line rides the first one, and the line lands on Messages as any text.
- **When**: a reminder only. That is `remind: true` on a notice, a retainage notice or the affidavit kind, and every call of the signature kind. A send for approval, the pen record and the two code kinds are not texted. A leader outside his Push me hours is held from the push and is not texted either.
- **The words** (`lienReminderText`, `_shared/lienApprovalPush.ts`): *Wendi is reminding you: Sign 8 lien notices. 8 jobs, first to mail by Nov 16, today. https://…/lien-desk?tab=notices&pile=ready*. The title and the line are the push's own, so the three channels cannot disagree. The push's middle dot and any curly quote are written plain, since one such character makes the carrier send the whole text in the wide alphabet. The link is last. One text for each message: eight notices to sign are one text, as they are one push.
- **The answer** gains `text_sent` (how many leaders a text went to) and `text_said` (why none did, from `lienReminderTextMiss`): *there is no mobile number on the account*, *Send texts is off in Settings*, *texts are not set up yet*, *that number sent STOP*, *texts are paused*, the two carrier refusals, else *the text line did not take it*. Both are absent when no text was meant. **A text never fails the call**: a throw on the way is caught, logged and answered as a reason, since the push and the email have gone.
- **No row in `notification_history`** for the text: its `channel` check allows `push`, `email`, `both` and `held`, and the `messages` line is the text's record, as for a receipt text.
- **The desk**: the confirm window says *A text with the link goes too.* After the press the toast says *Sam was reminded of 8 papers. A text went too.*, or in amber *… No text went: there is no mobile number on the account.* (`lienReminderTextOf` over every call of the press, `lienReminderOutcome`). A text alone counts as a reminder that reached him (`lienTellMissReason`).
- `customerSurfaceRegistry`: the function is one of the text senders now, to staff alone.

## Old client, new function, and the other way round

The client as it was ignores the two new fields, and the leader is texted all the same. The new client under the function as it was is answered with no text fields and says nothing about a text. So the order of the merge and the deploy does not matter.

## Not done here

- **Who chooses the way.** The text goes on every reminder. A setting on the leader's own account, beside his Push me hours, is the place for a choice, and none is built.
- **The first send for approval** is not texted. If the leader reads texts and not pushes, that send has the same hole the reminder had.
- The secrets are the text line's own (the Quo key and the team box's number), already set for `quo-send`; nothing new to set.

## Verified

Nothing was sent to a real person: no press on the live desk, and the handler ran with the push, the email and the text line replaced by recorders.

- `notifyLienApproval.run.test.ts` (6 new cases): a notice's reminder texts the push's words and the link from the team number, in plain characters; the send for approval is not texted and its answer is as before; the notices to sign are one text, an affidavit's reminder is texted and its send is not; no number, a number that is not ten digits, Send texts off, the pause, a carrier refusal, a STOP and a throw are each a reason with the push still sent; with no phone set up the email goes as before and the text too; the pen record is never texted.
- `lienReminderText.test.ts` (new): the words, the reasons in plain words, the text over one press, the window's sentence and the toast.
- `lienDeskIo.tell.test.ts`, `LienDesk.render.test.tsx` (the window's sentence, *A text went too.*, and the amber reason with the press still marked sent), `customerSurfaceRegistry.test.ts`.
