# v2.3520 — Job accounts on the Bid Board, PR 1a: the won row's chips and the Won count (2026-09-16)

Client only. No migration, no edge function — the RPC exists (`list_bid_job_account_strip`, v2.3451). First slice of `to-dos/bid-board-job-accounts/` (item 3 of the owner's ready-to-build train), cut small: the row and the count now, the lens and the per-house email as PR 1b, the flow-strip steps as PR 2.

## Why

The owner: *"How could an estimator manage job accounts from the bidboard?"* The tools lived inside Edit Bid's Job block (v2.3451/54). On the board a won row showed the won pill and the J#### chip and nothing about whether Ferguson, Moore Supply or Reece had opened the job's account — the estimator opened each bid to find out.

## What shipped

- **One read for the page** — [`useBidBoardJobAccountStrips`](../../src/hooks/useBidBoardJobAccountStrips.ts) calls `list_bid_job_account_strip` once with every bid in the **Won** and **Started or Complete** sections and groups the rows per bid. The per-bid twin (`useBidJobAccountStrip`) stays for Edit Bid. Refetches when a job is created from a bid and on window focus.
- **The chips on the won line** — [`BidBoardJobAccountChips`](../../src/components/bids/BidBoardJobAccountChips.tsx), the compact twin of `BidJobAccountsRow`, drawn by `BidBoardGcLines` under the GC line whose pill reads *won* (table and phone card): `Ferguson ✓` · `Moore Supply · asked` · `Reece · quoted` · `Reece · not needed` · `Reece`. A chip that is not open opens **Mark opened** for that house (the estimator's write on a bid-linked job, as in Edit Bid); the **…** beside them opens the Job accounts question for the job. A won bid with no job yet reads *after the job is opened*.
- **The section headers** — *Won (N) · M missing job accounts* and the same on *Started or Complete*, where M counts that section's bids whose job still has a house that is neither open nor marked not needed.
- **Kernel** — [`src/lib/bids/bidBoardJobAccounts.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/bidBoardJobAccounts.ts) (6 tests): grouping, the chip's word per state, which houses are missing, the header count and its wording.
- Help guide *open a job account when you win a bid* gains *From the Bid Board too*.

**Found on the live pass:** on prod, no bid with outcome *won* has a job — the job-created trigger (v2.3069) moves a bid to *Started or Complete* the moment its job opens, so every row that has a job lives in that section and a still-Won bid reads *after the job is opened*. The first cut read the Won section only and drew seventeen *after the job is opened* lines and no chips; the read now covers both sections.

## Verified

- Kernel tests, a jsdom render smoke for the chips (`BidBoardJobAccountChips.render.test.tsx`), `helpGuideContent`, release-notes drift, to-do drift, typecheck, lint.
- Live on prod data: Bids → Bid Board: *Started or Complete* carries the header count and the chips under each GC with the right words per house; the **…** opens the Job accounts question; every *Won* row reads *after the job is opened*. Mark opened was exercised to the sheet and cancelled — no real account row written.
