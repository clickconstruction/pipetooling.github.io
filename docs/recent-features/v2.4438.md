# v2.4438 — Submittals: choose what the GC sees, for every fixture in one window (2026-10-02)

PR 3 of the train ([v2.4435](v2.4435.md) the flag, [v2.4436](v2.4436.md) step 3 and the log). Mock-up: https://claude.ai/artifact/VHfgwjigciY5BjkGKmtYiv. No migration; no edge function. Wendi's screenshot was this window: every fixture already on Rev 1 showed an empty, locked checkbox and *already on this revision*, so the one place she looked for a way to deselect offered none.

## What changed

- **Three buttons where the checkbox was** ([`SubmittalTakeoffPicker`](../../src/components/bids/SubmittalTakeoffPicker.tsx)): *GC sees it · Order only · Left out* on every fixture, the lit one saying where it stands. The words are the app's own: *GC sees it* and *order only* are the part editor's, *left out* was the window's bar.
- **Rows already on the draft are live.** A candidate carries `onAs` (`'gc' | 'order' | null`: how it sits on the draft). Moving one shows what the press will do under its name (*On Rev 1 now. It moves to order only…*). Nothing is written until **Update Rev 1**; in build mode the button stays *Build Rev 1 with N rows*.
- **The kernel** [`takeoffPicks.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/takeoffPicks.ts): `startingPick` (the draft, else what the bid remembers, else the group's rule), `planTakeoffPicks` (rows to add as the GC's or order only, rows to move either way, rows to take off, and every pick to remember), `planSummary` (*3 rows go on Rev 1 (1 order only, 1 to type with Edit) · 1 comes off Rev 1*), `pickCounts`, `pickChangeWords`.
- **The tab applies the plan** (`confirmTakeoff`): inserts with `order_only` on the order-only ones, an update per row moved, a delete per row taken off (a split fixture's rows move together), then one upsert of `ticked` and `order_only` for every fixture shown.
- **An ordered fixture cannot be left out here either.** Opening the window on a draft reads the log once (`loadBidOrderFacts`); a fixture whose tag holds an order has *Left out* struck through and its row says *Ordered 09/23, on site 09/29. It cannot be left out.*
- **The door in step 3.** *+ Add from the takeoff…* becomes **Choose what the GC sees…** (the same window, now that it does more than add). Under the rows, *Left out · N from the takeoff · not submitted, not ordered · Show them* opens it too.
- The part chips under a fixture coming on as *GC sees it* are unchanged (GC sees / order only per part); *Tick all with a product* is *GC sees all with a product*.
