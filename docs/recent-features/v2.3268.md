# v2.3268 — Discount at the moment you send: Add discount inside Bill Customer (2026-09-11)

Migration [`20260911000000_apply_job_discount.sql`](../migrations/20260911000000_apply_job_discount.md). Discount tools round two, PR 2 of 4 (mockups + review: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/f56651a5-0d7b-49c3-bdc0-6bfca39ec454).

## Why

The moment the office concedes a number is with the bill open, about to send. Until now that meant closing Bill Customer, finding ① Line Items, adding the row, and coming back. The first draft crammed the whole line-item row into the modal and invented a choice ("only this bill's line"); the review replaced it with two lines in the office's own words and a basis decided for them.

## What shipped

- **`BillCustomerDiscountStrip`** — collapsed to a dashed **− Add discount** by the amount on the Stripe and Physical tabs; open, two fields: **Take off** (`10%` or `500`) **or make this bill** (`13,500`), reason chips, and one sentence: *10% off Rough In · this draw only → $13,588.20*. Typing one field clears the other. Cancel / Apply.
- **The basis is decided for you** (`planBillDiscount`, shared kernel): a draw discounts its own linked lines; the whole-job remainder discounts every work line; "make this bill $X" always discounts this bill's own lines so the number lands exactly. On a draw with a percent or dollars, *apply to the whole job instead* flips it and the sentence says *$1,509.80 of it rides this bill; the rest follows the other draws*.
- **One RPC does the write** (`apply_job_discount`): the row, revenue, this draft's new amount, and the `discount_added` event, in one transaction. Then the modal refreshes the job details (the physical preview and the primary's plan read them), sets the shown amount for a draw, and re-runs the Stripe preview, which shows the negative line.
- **An open Edit Job form stays truthful**: `onDiscountApplied` rides the Bill Customer opener contract; the form's `rehydrateFixturesFromDb` re-reads the rows, resets the discount snapshot, and re-baselines the billing autosave on the render that carries the new rows — so the next delete+reinsert keeps the row and no spurious autosave fires.
- Help: *give a customer a discount* → "From the bill itself".
- Tests: 5 kernel cases for `planBillDiscount` (draw / whole job / make-this-bill / remainder / nothing sensible); 3 render smokes for the strip.

## Verification

Push the migration with the merge, then on a Ready-to-Bill job with linked draws: Bill Customer → − Add discount → *10%* → the sentence and the new amount → Apply → the toast, the Stripe preview gains the negative line, the Bill tab shows the row.
