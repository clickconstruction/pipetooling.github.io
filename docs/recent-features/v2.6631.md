## What changed

- **Every card folds** (`src/lib/settings/jobsBillingGroups.ts`): the `fold` flag is gone from the kernel and `SettingsFoldCard` draws the header row for every card. The seven switches (`HideHcpFieldSettingsBlock`, `ReportPhotoSizeSettingsBlock`, `OwnerAutoConfirmSettingsBlock`, `GcCardBillSettingsBlock`, `GcOfficeNoticesSettingsBlock`, `GcCustomerDueNoticesSettingsBlock`, `GcBillDayCutoffSettingsBlock`) lost their own box and `h3`, as the folded cards did in PR 1; their `data-testid` roots and their render smokes stand.
- **Three cards moved** (`src/lib/settings/settingsFoldCards.ts`, new): the fold-card shape (`SettingsFoldCardSpec`, `settingsCardIsTarget`, the child-anchor map the Jobs kernel re-exports) and `MOVED_SETTINGS_CARDS`, each with its new tab, its new anchor and the anchor it had on Jobs & billing. `Settings.tsx` mounts HCP reconcile in a fold under Data & recovery after `SettingsDataTab` (`#settings-data-hcp-reconcile`), the sub portal's pay schedule under People & teams after the review cadence (`#settings-people-sub-pay`), and the office's county under Company right after the office address (`#settings-company-office-county`), each for a dev as before. `SettingsFoldCard` takes `track`, the tab its press is counted under, and Settings → Usage names a *Data & recovery card*, a *People & teams card* and a *Company card* beside the *Jobs & billing card*.
- **The old anchors still land**: `SETTINGS_HASH_ANCHOR_TO_TAB` maps each moved card's new and old anchor to its new tab, `resolveSettingsDeepLink` rewrites an old anchor to the new one before the page looks for the element, and `useSettingsCardOpen` opens a card for an anchor it had before it moved. The two Settings search entries and the HCP dev door point at the new anchors; `doors.test.ts` holds the old anchors as aliases of a door, not doors of their own.
- **Guides**: `reconcile-hcp-billing-history`, `manage-subs-end-to-end`, `see-what-a-sub-sees-on-their-portal`, `sub-labor-outstanding` and `swear-a-statement-on-a-collections-account` say where each card is now; `settings-basics` no longer says a switch stays open.
- **Docs**: `docs/SETTINGS_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md` (§ 3, § 5, § 10, the Company row), `docs/PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`, `docs/GLOSSARY.md`, `docs/BILLING_FLOWS.md`; the card records the seven answers.
