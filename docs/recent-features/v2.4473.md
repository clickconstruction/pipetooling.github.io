# v2.4473 — Hazmat, vehicles and people: an evening incident or task keeps its day (2026-10-03)

One of the instant-day fixes that follow [v2.4452](v2.4452.md). Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

These readers cut days out of `timestamptz` columns by their first ten (or seven) characters, so anything stamped after 7 pm CDT (6 pm CST) read as the next day. Two of them write the day:

- **A hazmat incident** (`job_hazmat_incidents.incident_at`, an instant the Hazmat fee window takes from its date-and-time box): *Biohazard remediation fee — incident Jul 21* on the job's rider row ([`JobFormHazmatRidersStrip.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormHazmatRidersStrip.tsx)) and its *Notice emailed* day (`notice_emailed_at`). The fee's bill memo, written when the fee is billed on its own ([`useJobFormInvoiceActions.ts`](../../src/hooks/useJobFormInvoiceActions.ts)), and the Stripe line when it folds into the main bill ([`hazmatRollIn.ts`](../../src/lib/hazmatRollIn.ts), `formatIncidentDate`) wrote the same day. The notice paper and the public notice page already printed the incident in Central.
- **A vehicle's history** ([`vehicleFleet.ts`](../../src/lib/vehicleFleet.ts), `buildVehicleLedger`): a problem's *Resolved* row from `vehicle_problem_reports.resolved_at` and a task's *Task done* row from `vehicle_maintenance_tasks.completed_at`; and a task's added day on the Vehicles tab ([`PeopleVehiclesTab.tsx`](../../src/components/people/PeopleVehiclesTab.tsx)).
- **People**: a document's *sent* day on the Person Desk's paperwork ([`paperworkRollup.ts`](../../src/lib/people/paperworkRollup.ts), `person_contract_documents.sent_at`), an HR report's *written* day ([`hrPendingReports.ts`](../../src/lib/people/hrPendingReports.ts), `person_reports.created_at`), and the feedback strip's *words this month* ([`feedbackTabRows.ts`](../../src/lib/people/feedbackTabRows.ts)), which compared a submission's UTC month with the review month in `APP_CALENDAR_TZ`.

## What changed

Each instant reads `calendarYmdInAppTzFromIso(iso)` from [`dateUtils.ts`](../../src/utils/dateUtils.ts) (`APP_CALENDAR_TZ`); `formatIncidentDate` now says the incident is an instant. The `date` columns keep their slice: `person_contract_documents.signed_at`, `occurred_date`, `report_date`, `start_date` and `end_date`.

## Not changed

- The contracts queue's *signed* day and the office form's *Signed … on* line read `person_contract_documents.signed_at`, a `date`.
- *Paid out* and pay-run days slice `pay_stub_payments.paid_at`, which every writer stamps at noon on the chosen day ([v2.4464](v2.4464.md)).
- A memo or Stripe line already written keeps the day it was written with.

## Tests

- `hazmatRollIn.test.ts` (+1): the incident as stored, `2026-07-21T00:31:00+00:00` (7:31 pm CDT on Jul 20), writes *incident 07/20/2026*; one at `2026-12-02T00:30:00Z` (6:30 pm CST) writes 12/01/2026. Its fixtures passed bare days (`2026-07-20`), which the writer never stores; they move to noon UTC and keep their expectations.
- `JobFormHazmatRidersStrip.render.test.tsx` (+1): the rider row reads *incident Jul 20* and *Notice emailed Jul 28* for a notice at 8 pm CDT.
- `vehicleFleet.test.ts`, `paperworkRollup.test.ts`, `hrPendingReports.test.ts`, `feedbackTabRows.test.ts` (+1 each): a problem resolved at 7:30 pm CDT on Aug 12 reads Aug 12; paperwork sent then reads Aug 1 while a signed day stays as stored; a report written at 7:30 pm CDT on Aug 24 reads Aug 24; words left at 7:30 pm CDT on Sep 30 count in September. One HR fixture stamped at midnight UTC moves to midday and keeps its expectation.

All 6 fail on main. The memo and the Vehicles tab's added day change one expression each.

## Checked against prod

Read only, from the worktree dev server: the one hazmat incident was logged at 7:31 pm CDT on Jul 20, so its rider row read Jul 21 until this ships. No vehicle problem is resolved yet, the one maintenance task and the 2 HR reports are from daytime, none of the 56 documents still marked sent went out in an evening, and none of the 3 feedback submissions changes month.
