# v2.3394 — People → Review reads card charges by the Job Summary rule (2026-09-14)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Closes item 6 of [`to-dos/job-summary-follow-ups.md`](../../to-dos/job-summary-follow-ups.md) ("Review vs Job Summary parts cost disagree on J963") and the Residue line in [`to-dos/review-into-the-bridge.md`](../../to-dos/review-into-the-bridge.md).

## Why

v2.2688 recorded J963's parts cost as **$1,465 on People → Review** and **$710 on Jobs → Job Summary** and filed it as "card charges / PO-priced tally lines". The read: PO-priced tally lines were never the difference — every surface prices them through the same `list_tally_parts_with_po` RPC with the same formula. Card charges were. v2.2692 gave Job Summary two exclusions through `cardChargeAllocationFilter.ts` (the one rule): an **Internal Transfer** is money between the company's own accounts, not a cost, and a card charge **linked to a supply-house invoice** is the purchase the invoice allocation already counts, so it is counted once. Review's two loaders kept summing gross allocations. A third gap sat inside Review itself: the per-person loader's Net headline (`allocationJobsMap`, the figure shown until the Team Summary row loads) left card charges out of parts altogether.

## What shipped

- **Kernel [`netCardChargesByJob.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/netCardChargesByJob.ts)** (+3 tests): `netCardChargesByJobId(summary)` = counted gross − invoice-linked slice, clamped at zero — the arithmetic `Jobs.tsx` does inline, in one place.
- **Review per-person loader** (`loadReviewDataCore`): selects `mercury_transaction_id`, loads the exclusions through `loadCardChargeExclusions` (buckets + invoice links; each degrades to "everything counts" when RLS hides its table), and builds `cardChargesByJobId` from `summarizeCardChargeAllocations` → `netCardChargesByJobId`. The two `partsCost` lines in `allocationJobsMap` now add card charges.
- **Team Summary union** (`loadTeamReviewUnion`): the exclusions load beside the Wheels snapshot; after the fuel-tag removal (Review's own pricing choice under Wheels, v2.2735 — kept), the rows go through the same summary; the cost-line tag slices are computed over the rows that count, as Job Summary's are.
- `TeamReviewUnion.cardChargesByJobId` documented as net; `derivePersonTeamSummary.test.ts` gains a case pinning parts = tally + invoices + billed + net cards.

## Not changed

Job Detail's profit band (`buildJobProfitSummary`) still adds gross card charges, and Crew P&L counts no parts at all — separate surfaces with their own conventions, noted in the to-dos.
