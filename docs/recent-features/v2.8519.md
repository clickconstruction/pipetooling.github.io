# v2.8519 — Punch list: a card can carry the question it waits on (2026-11-24)

PR 1 of punch list #438, the ground. Diane's ask of 2026-11-24: make the Punch list page easy for a person to see the questions they still have to answer. Her word the same day on the mock-up: *Build D*. Migration `20261124191500_punch_list_answers.sql`, no edge function, no screen change.

## What was there

What a card waits on was prose inside its `next:` line. A person had to open the card to find the question, and the board had no way to list the cards that wait on one person. The board's pick held Do, Later and Drop, which sort a card that is not built.

## What changed

- **`ask:` in a card's front matter**: one line, `who · kind · question`. Who is `owner`, `office`, `anyone`, `counsel`, `time` or `dev`. Kind is `look`, `read`, `walk`, `decide` or `use`. The question is one sentence a person can act on. `src/lib/todos/punchListAsk.ts` reads it (`parseAsk`) and writes it back (`formatAsk`).
- **`npm run check:todos` refuses an ask it cannot read**, in words that name the fault: fewer than three parts, a who or a kind it does not know, no question, no end mark, or a question over 160 characters. The check runs in CI on every PR.
- **The board's rows carry it**: `TodoMeta.ask` and `BoardItem.ask`, null when the card waits on nobody. Nothing draws it yet.
- **Fifteen cards got their ask**, written from the pictures pages of 2026-11-24: #111, #119, #125, #284, #396, #426, #188, #189, #421, #375, #350, #329, #302, #298 and #411. No other line of those cards changed.
- **`punch_list_picks` can hold the answer**: `pick` may be `change`, and `answers_ask` keeps the question the pick answered. See `docs/migrations/20261124191500_punch_list_answers.md`.
- `to-dos/README.md` says how to write the line.

## What it does not do

No screen changes. The view that lists the questions and takes the answers is PR 2.

## Tests

`punchListAsk.test.ts` (8) and five more in `todoBoard.test.ts`: the ask read and written back, a long one read whole after it wraps, a card with none, a card whose ask does not read, and the row in plain text.
