# v2.5485 — Messages: sort everyone in New one at a time (2026-10-13)

Residential R7, **Sort all** (mockup C, the customer Messages prototype v0.8, screen 7, `deskHtml`), on #5652's New
lane and sort card (v2.5477). The desk walks the lane one card at a time with nothing else on the screen, for a day with
a backlog. **No migration and no edge function.** Built on #5652's head as a draft.

- **The press** (`FollowUpSections.tsx`): **Sort all** on New's header once two or more people wait (`canSortAll`,
  `SORT_ALL_MIN = 2`, the mockup's rule), through a new optional `action` on the sections' `Section` and
  `FollowUpSectionActions.sortAll`. One person keeps their row's press alone.
- **The desk** (`SortAllDesk.tsx`, drawn by `FollowUpPanel` from its own queue, so `/messages` and `/gc?view=followUp`
  both have it with no change to either page): a `ResponsiveModalShell` window titled *Sort all*, full screen on a phone
  and by its toggle (`fullScreenKey` `follow-up-sort-all`). It walks New as the panel draws it, kind pills included.
  - Each card has the progress in parts (*New*, *3 to sort*, *1 of 3*), how the line came in and when, who sent it,
    their words, and the lane's own sort card, `NewLaneSlot`, **unchanged**. Its presses (`name_contact`,
    `tie_message`, `take_it`, `hand_to`) go through `newLaneCardIo` as above the thread.
  - After a press the panel reads Follow up again (`q.reload`), the sorted person leaves New and the next card comes up.
    The count keeps its total from when the desk opened and grows if more came in since.
  - **Skip for now** puts a person after the rest (`skipOne`). Once everyone left was skipped, the walk starts over.
  - **Someone else** closes the desk and opens the thread at the line, where the window's own *Who is this?* is, so the
    desk never opens a window over a window.
  - Empty, the desk says *All sorted.* with New's empty words and **Back to Follow up**.
- **The kernel** (`followUp/sortDesk.ts`): `deskStep(lane, skipped, total)` (whose card is up, the progress, "2 more
  after this" or "The last one"), `skipOne`, `canSortAll`, the words, and `deskContact`. That last one is the sort
  card's person from Follow up's read: id, kind, name, company and jobs, with no record or channels, which the card
  never reads.
- **The lead's calls**: a window over Follow up for both hosts; *Someone else* closes the desk at the thread's line; the
  four guesses that need a record made (a new customer, a new trade partner, a supply house, a candidate) still wait
  and say where to make the record, as on the row. Making those records from the desk is its own PR if the owner wants
  it (an open call in `RESIDENTIAL_BUILD_PLAN.md` § 7); the press shows from two people.
- **Guide**: `sort-new-messages` gains **Sort a backlog**. `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` § 22 gains the desk's paragraph.
- **Tests**: `sortDesk.test.ts` (the step, the count past the total, skips coming back round, a skip forgotten once its
  person left, the contact, every word plain) and `SortAllDesk.render.test.tsx` over fake ios through `FollowUpPanel`
  (the press from two people and not from one, the walk, a skip, a press bringing the next person up, *Someone else*,
  and *All sorted.*).
