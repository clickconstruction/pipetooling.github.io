# v2.4400 — The page behind the Lien desk stays still on an iPhone (2026-10-02)

Client only: no migration, no edge function. PR 2 of 4 on the owner's ask "stop background scroll on all modals across the app" (PR 1: [v2.4399](v2.4399.md)).

## What was wrong

The app-wide lock ([v2.2186](v2.2186.md)) counted a fixed layer as a window only when it covered 90% of the viewport in both axes. Since v2.2184 the windows that must not sit under the Dispatch / Job mode bar end above it (`bottom: var(--app-bottom-chrome)`): the Lien desk, Put a GC on notice, the GC notice preview, the email preview, Bank payments, the Person desk, the clocked-in map, the missed clock-in window. The bar is 60 px plus the home-indicator inset, so on an iPhone home-screen app (393 × 852, inset 34) such a window is 758 px tall — **89%** — and the page behind it was never locked. Every iPhone but the SE and the largest Pro Max falls under 90%, and any phone on its side does.

Reproduced on the dev server at 375 × 590 (the same ratio without the inset): Lien desk open, overlay 530 of 590 px, `body` unlocked once the watchdog ran.

## What changed

[`blockingOverlay.ts`](../../src/lib/blockingOverlay.ts) — a fixed layer now counts in one of two ways:

1. **It says so.** A visible `aria-modal="true"` panel that is on screen makes its fixed layer blocking at any size (`DECLARED_MODAL_SELECTOR`, `onScreen`). 408 component files already mark their panel this way (the v2.2188 sweep). A bottom sheet with no backdrop counts too. A panel kept mounted but out of sight does not: `display: none`, `visibility: hidden`, `opacity: 0`, an empty rect, or a layer wholly outside the viewport are all skipped, so a slid-away drawer cannot freeze the page.
2. **It looks like one.** A layer with no declared panel still needs 90% — of the screen **above the bottom bar** (`heightAboveBottomChrome`). The bar is found by `data-app-bottom-chrome`, new on [`DispatchModeFooter`](../../src/components/dispatchMode/DispatchModeFooter.tsx)'s `<nav>`.

`data-page-scroll="allow"` still wins over both (the walkthrough's dim, v2.4121). A small fixed `role="dialog"` without `aria-modal` (a popover) still does not lock.

## Tests

[`blockingOverlay.test.ts`](../../src/lib/blockingOverlay.test.ts), 9 → 20 cases: the iPhone numbers above for a declared panel and for an unlabelled layer with and without the bar, the bar itself, a backdrop-less sheet, a popover, the three out-of-sight shapes, the opt-out over a declared panel, and one layer holding both kinds of candidate.

## Verified live

Dev server, production data, read-only. At 375 × 590 with Dispatch mode on: Lien desk open, overlay 89.8% of the viewport, `body` `position: fixed` and still fixed after two watchdog rounds (before: released after 3 s). With nothing open, `body` is free on Dashboard, Pipeline, Bids, People, Customers, Materials, Checklist, Settings, Quickfill, Banking, Help and the three Dispatch mode pages at 375 px, and on the first five at 1280 px — no page gained a lock it should not have.
