# v2.5854 — The Collect desk, PR 3 of 7: the one move on each payer, and the week's plan (2026-10-25)

Punch list #177, PR C, on [v2.5853](v2.5853.md). The desk stops describing the pile and says what to do about it.

## The moves (`src/lib/bridge/collect/collectMoves.ts`)

`collectMoveFor(payer, opts)` is the first rule that matches, top to bottom, with the opener built only from facts the kernel holds:

| order | when | move | the "Say:" line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | a broken promise on the account | **Call: the \<day\> promise** | *the $X promised for \<day\> has not landed. What happened, and what is the new date?* |
| 2 | a GC payer whose hand-marked bill is at least half of what is open | **Call: is the pay app approved?** when it is late, **Call for the pay date** when it is not yet due | *the pay application for $X, marked billed here \<day\>. When did it go in, is it approved, and which pay run is it on?* |
| 3 | more than six bills on the account | **Send the statement, then call** | *N open bills, $X, the oldest from \<month\>. Which can you pay this week, and when?* |
| 4 | one or two late bills on an account with the rest inside pace | **Ask about the \<amount\>** | *the $X from \<day\> is N times your usual pace. Is something wrong with it?* |
| 5 | under $10k, no promise | **Send the statement** | no call |
| 6 | the rest | **Call for a date** | *N bills, $X, the oldest N days old. Which can you pay this week, and when?* |

Each move carries the lien chip's words and door as its **paper**, the dollars it asks for, and the owner the recorded plan named for it (`CallMove.who`, matched by title; '' when nobody). `buildCollectSheet` sums the sheet: the calls and the statements apart, the ask, the money on the cut that pays itself. `collectSheetAsCallMoves` shapes the rows into the Bridge call's moves (`chase`, the payer's name in the title, the owner as typed) and `collectSheetText` writes the sheet for the clipboard.

## The decisions (`collectDecisions.ts`)

Lifted out of the office's sheet into the owner's strip, money first, three at most: a payer that reads as a GC while the job records it as the customer (*Put X on the job as the GC*, with the clock running anyway), a lien affidavit with its window open (*File the X lien by \<day\>*), a payer whose own pace is 60 days or more (*Terms for X on the next bid*), a dispute recorded this week (their words, your decision). Each with its door: the jobs, the affidavit on the Lien desk, the customer's page.

## The what-if (`collectWhatIf.ts`)

If half of what the moves ask for lands in three weeks on top of today's pace: the pile still grows with what is billed, the pace rises with what landed over the eight-week window, the weeks follow, and the stage flow's own `STAGE_PAIR_MARGIN` says whether Collect keeps the link, hands it to Build, or the two read as a pair. The tile keeps the twelve-week left-alone line under it and the words *a guess, stated as one*.

## The modal

The payer rows gain a sixth column: the move as a button to the customer's page, the paper's link, and a dashed *owner · day* field that the recorded plan fills; the opener sits under where the account stands. The owner's strip draws in red between the rows and the folded facts. The footer counts the moves and the ask, says what Friday asks, and holds **Copy the call sheet** and **Record this as the week's plan**, which writes the moves and their owners through the Bridge's own call (`recordBridgeCall`, link Collect, note *the Collect desk's sheet*); once recorded the owner fields lock and the footer says so.
