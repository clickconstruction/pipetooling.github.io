# v2.4387 — Pipeline: the progress bar fits its cell and each bill's bracket sits on its own money (2026-10-02)

No migration, no edge function. Follow-up to pass 4 of the Progress & payment cell (v2.4349, v2.4351, v2.4353). Grace, 2026-10-01: *"visually check the new progress bar once it is live against jobs stages all the way down and let me know if anything looks funny at any of the various viewports so we can fix it now instead of later."*

## How it was checked

A Playwright sweep (read only, dev login on the local dev server against production data) opened every Pipeline section, Paid in Full included: 875 money cells at 1920, 1440, 1280, 1099, iPad 1024 and 768, Mobile cards at 1280 and 768, a 390 phone (table and cards) and dark mode. Each cell was measured for anything outside its box, clipped text, a wrapped top row, the This bill line wrapping, the tick and bracket inside the bar, and blocks under 8 px. Every bracket was checked against its row's *This bill* words, and every legend's rows were added up against the bid.

The table's money column is a fixed 232 px (208 px inside) at every width, so the table looks the same at every width. Mobile cards (a wider money zone) and the phone board had no flags.

## What was wrong

- **Brackets on the wrong money (14 of 30).** `billMarkFor` placed a bill made by amount by billing order alone, but `pourMoney` draws the paid money first. 273 Dudley's first bill ($13,420, nothing paid; the $38,780 of payments went to no bill) was bracketed on the green, 0 → 19 %. The same happened on 880 Reliant HVAC (an open bill sent before a paid one), 251 and 258. A bill naming a line much bigger than itself spanned the whole bar: 650 ATI Schertz ($6,700 naming the job's only $33,500 line) and 473 Mike Holub ($5,724 naming *Final*, $46,600).
- **The legend's Not done row did not add up (34 rows).** It printed the work not done (bid × (1 − %)) beside the grey part's percent. With billing ahead of the %, those differ: *0% Not done $11,273* (273), *59% Not done $219,750* beside *41% Billed $103,386* on a $249,716 bid (1007). Many Paid in Full rows read *100% Paid $356 · 0% Not done $356*. The rule since v2.3416, and the guide, is that the rows add up to the bid.
- **The report date ran out of the cell (11 rows).** With the box empty and a field report, *· 80% on a report, Jul 30* sat inside the box's nowrap span: 60–92 px too wide, cut at the cell edge (*May 2…*).
- **A six-figure or $30k+ bid wrapped by 2–9 px (11 rows),** e.g. *80 % done · Sep 14 | $44,000 bid*.

## What changed

- **Kernel** [`jobMoneyBar.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobMoneyBar.ts) `billMarkFor` takes the job's `payments`. A bill *covers* the lines it names when they come to no more than the bill (+$1); then it spans them as before. Any other bill sits on the blocks no covering bill claims: from the paid money drawn there plus the open parts of earlier open bills, less its own linked payments, to its open part's end. That maps onto the drawn widths block by block. Callers (`StagesUnifiedInvoiceRow`, the merged `StagesUnifiedJobRow`, the unified cards) pass `job.payments`. The report date reads *30% reported Sep 25*.
- **[`StagesProgressPaymentCell`](../../src/components/jobs/StagesProgressPaymentCell.tsx)** (pass-4 path only): the % date is its own item in the top row (`minWidth: 0`, ellipsis), packed left, so a long one drops a line or ends in … inside the cell. The box is 2rem with `no-spinner` and no space before *% done*; the row gap is 0.3rem. The Not done row prints the grey part in whole dollars: bid − paid − billed − done-not-billed, each rounded as printed.
- Guides *read the Pipeline money view* (where the bracket sits) and *ready to bill pipeline* (*30% reported Sep 25*); `GLOSSARY.md` *Money bar*.
