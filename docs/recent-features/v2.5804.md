# v2.5804 — Banking Approvals: every card says the day the charge was made (2026-10-23)

The owner, from a screenshot of Banking → Mercury → Accounting → Approvals (188 pending): "on this page all of the approvals I am seeing are not showing a date, I would like to know 'Tuesday 3 days ago (10/20/26)' for each one of them to the right of 'Card: Abraham 2112'. Could you help me add this?" Most cards read *Posted —* because the charge is still pending at Mercury (`posted_at` null), so the only day in sight was often none.

- **Kernel** `formatWeekdayAgoPhrase(dayYmd, todayYmd)` in `src/lib/relativeDayPhrase.ts` (tests beside it): the owner's shape on top of the app's relative-day words — `Today (10/23/26)`, `Yesterday (10/22/26)`, `Tuesday · 3 days ago (10/20/26)`; the count stays in days however far back, as `formatRelativeDayPhrase` already does. `weekdayNameFromYmd` and `formatShortUsDateFromYmd` are pure arithmetic on the `YYYY-MM-DD`, no zone.
- **Kernel** `mercuryChargeDayPhrase(tx, todayYmd)` in `src/lib/mercurySwipeTime.ts` (tests beside it): the charge's instant is `raw.createdAt` (the swipe), else the row's `created_at` — the sync stores Mercury's own `createdAt` there, and the Banking list columns leave `raw` out — else `posted_at`; a manual row reads `posted_at` before its `created_at`, which is the upload. The day comes through `calendarYmdInAppTzFromIso`, so a charge at 11 PM Central on a Tuesday reads Tuesday (pinned by a test), never the browser's day.
- **Card** `src/components/banking/AccountingApprovalCard.tsx` (render smoke `AccountingApprovalCard.render.test.tsx`: pending, posted, today's, 8 days old, a row without raw): the day sits to the right of *Card: Abraham 2112* with a middle dot, in the card's muted style; each half is `nowrap`, so on a phone the day wraps under the Card line whole. A row with no card shows the day alone. The *Posted* line is unchanged, so when a charge posted on a later day both lines read true. `todayYmd` is an optional prop (`todayYmdInAppTz()` by default) so the smoke pins today.
- **Docs**: `docs/BANKING_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md` Region A names the line and the fields it reads; the Glossary's *Accounting rules* entry and the guide *sort the bank feed* say what the card shows.
