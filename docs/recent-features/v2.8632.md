# v2.8632 — Lien desk: a payoff card with five stops for each place (2026-11-26)

Piece 4 of punch list #435 (`to-dos/lien-payoff/README.md`). Pieces 1 to 3 gave the door, the safe release and one check over many bills. What they left was the card's critique of the first draw: a payoff takes two weeks, so it has to be a card that stays, with its days in a column, that says so on a day it needs nothing. Client only. No migration and no edge function.

## What it does

In the Lien desk's Payoffs window, each place with a lien still on record carries a card over its liens' rows.

**Five stops.** *Money recorded*, *Check clears*, *Releases*, *Recorded at the county*, *Copies sent*. Each is done, the one to do now, later, or in red when it needs care, with a short sentence of what stands there.

| Stop | Read from | It says |
|---|---|---|
| Money recorded | every liened job's bills (`lienPayoffStep` past `money`) | *Paid Nov 24 by check 1184.*, or what is still open and on how many liens |
| Check clears | the bank's answer (`checkCleared.ts`), or a confirmation of the funds | *The bank says Dec 2.*, *Cleared Nov 21.*, *Alex Rivera confirmed the funds Nov 26.*, or in red *The check came back.* |
| Releases | a release of record saved for each lien | *2 to make.*, *1 made, 1 to make.*, *All 3 made.* |
| Recorded at the county | each release's county day (piece 2's stamp) | *1 recorded, 1 to record.*, *Recorded Dec 4.* |
| Copies sent | nothing yet | *After recording.* See below. |

**One line for today.** *Nothing to do today. The releases wait for the check to clear, about Dec 2.* While money is open it names the sum. When releases are owed it says how many to make. A check that came back turns it red.

**Two days in a column**, values in place:

- *Asked for a release in writing* is a value changed where it sits. Keep writes the day on every lien at the place that is not yet released.
- *Release due* is worked out: ten days after that request (§ 53.152(a)). The line counts the days left, and turns red once the day has passed.
- *Check clears* is worked out from the bank's own day.

**The bank confirmed the funds ›.** On a place whose paid liens wait for the bank, a leader, a controller or a dev sees this press. It opens a box for what the bank said, and **Confirm the funds** is dead until something is typed. Each lien waiting on that check then stands on *Make the release ›*, its row reads *Paid Nov 24 by check 1184. Alex Rivera confirmed the funds Nov 26. The release is owed.*, and the Release of record tab's money line turns green and lets the print and the save go. The release saved then carries the confirmation (`fields.releasedOnConfirmedFunds`). A check that came back, or one the bank could not read, is never lifted by a confirmation.

**The check pane** (*One check for this place ›*) takes the asked day too, beside the amount, the number and the day received, and shows *Release due* and *Check clears* worked out. The day is kept on each liened lien once the payments are recorded. Left empty, nothing is written.

## Where the two facts live, and why not a table

The card planned one small table for a payoff: the place, the check, the day asked, who confirmed funds. Asked on 2026-11-26 in the managing session, the answer was the other pick: both facts live in the `fields` the affidavit row already saves (`job_lien_filings`, kind `affidavit`), one per lien.

- `fields.releaseAskedYmd`: the day a release was asked for in writing.
- `fields.fundsConfirmed`: who pressed (`by`, `name`), when (`at`) and what the bank said (`note`).

The reasons: a written request and a release are per lien in the Code; the check is already named by the payment rows' number, day and note (piece 3's mark); and pieces 2 and 3 each planned a column or a function and shipped with none. So piece 4 is one client PR with no push owed. A later piece may lift the two into columns.

The three roles are checked on the client (`canConfirmFunds`), the same gate the tab's *Release it anyway…* has today. The row's update policy already admits the office.

## The rule

- [`lienPayoffFields.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienPayoffFields.ts), pure, 7 tests: `payoffNote` reads the two facts, `payoffFieldsPatch` writes one and keeps every other field, `fundsConfirmedNow` and `fundsConfirmedWords`.
- [`lienPayoffCard.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienPayoffCard.ts), pure, 13 tests: `lienPayoffCard` (the five stops, the line, the days, whether to offer the press), `releaseDue`, `releaseDueWords`, `canConfirmFunds`.
- [`lienPayoff.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienPayoff.ts): `lienPayoffStep` takes `funds`, and every step now carries the last payment, the bank's answer and the day the county recorded. Each row carries `askedYmd`, `funds` and the row's `fields`.
- [`lienReleaseOfRecord.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienReleaseOfRecord.ts): `releaseMoneyGate` passes on a confirmation once the bank has answered, and `releaseSaveFields` copies it onto the release.
- [`lienPayoffCardIo.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienPayoffCardIo.ts): one update of `fields` a lien, the run going on past a row that fails, each row back with its reason.

Every sentence is held to `plainWords.ts` by the tests.

## What it does not do yet

- **Copies sent has no fact.** Nothing on record says a release's copies went out, so the fifth stop reads *After recording.*, then *Send the stamped copies to the payer.* once every release is recorded. Piece 5 gives it a record.
- **Signed and notarized is not tracked.** The third stop counts releases saved. Piece 5 adds *mark notarized*.
- **No receipt for the payer.** The card's draw has one. It is a paper, and counsel's answers gate the words on the papers.
- **A confirmation cannot be taken back from the card.** It is one field on the lien's row.
- **The place's press keeps its name**, *One check for this place ›*. The draw called it *They paid: take the payoff ›*.
- #437's mend, the tab's *Release it anyway…* held to the same three roles, is not part of this.
