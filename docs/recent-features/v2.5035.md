# v2.5035 — Supply houses: a credit says which invoice it credits (2026-10-09)

Migration `20261010004500`. No edge function. This builds the owner's call of 2026-10-09 in `to-dos/owner-decisions-pending.md`: a supply-house credit pairs to the invoice it credits (`credits_invoice_id`). Since v2.3502 a credit memo has been its own row with a negative amount, and nothing said which invoice it took money off. The office read that from the PDF or the PO line.

## The change

- **The column** (`20261010004500`, `docs/migrations/20261010004500_supply_house_credit_pairs.md`):
  - `supply_house_invoices.credits_invoice_id` references the same table, and a deleted invoice unpairs its credits. It has a partial index.
  - A check: only a credit carries a pair, never to itself.
  - A guard: the paired row is an invoice of the same house, and a paired credit cannot move to another house.
- **The rules** (`supplyHouseInvoiceForm.ts`):
  - `creditableInvoices` offers the house's invoices, newest first, never a credit or the credit itself, as *S123148787.003 · $2,496.94 · Apr 6, 2026*.
  - `creditPairingLine` gives a credit *Credits S123148787.003*. An invoice gets *Credited by S123396858.002 (−$120.00)*, with every credit against it.
  - `creditsInvoicePatch` writes the column only on a change, so a save before the push names no new column, and an invoice clears a pair it held.
- **The credit form** (`SupplyHousesTab.tsx`, `CreditPairPick.tsx`, new): on a credit, **Credits invoice…** sits under the number, date and amount.
  - It lists the house's invoices, with *Not paired yet* first, and a line saying what the pick is for.
  - A house with no invoice on file says so.
  - Opening a credit to edit shows the pair it holds.
- **The rows**:
  - The house's invoice table shows the pair under each row's number, read against every row, paid ones too.
  - The phone screen puts the same words in the row's second line (`supplyHousePhoneInvoiceRows`): *credits 88121*, *credited by CM-4471 (−$150.00)*.

## The April row

One invoice dated Apr 6, 2026 holds an invoice and its return in one number field, *S123148787.003/ Return S123396858.002*, netted to $2,496.94. This change does not touch it, and the office splits it by hand later:
1. Edit the row to the invoice's own number and amount.
2. Add the credit with its own number.
3. Pair the credit to the invoice.

The guide says how. There was 1 credit among 501 rows on 2026-10-09.
