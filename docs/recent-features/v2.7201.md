# v2.7201 — Pay calendar: an estimator writes down the pay terms on a won bid, and the office sets them (2026-11-13)

Punch list #323, call 4 (*Who may set it*), answered by the owner on 2026-11-13 after three drawings (`to-dos/pay-calendar/bid-door.html`). His question was whether an estimator should set a payer's Pay calendar while working a bid. The answer built: no new right. The estimator, who has the GC's contract in hand, writes the terms down on a **won** bid, and the roles that already set a calendar check them against the payer's record and save. No migration, no gate touched, and an estimator reads nothing new.

## Why not widen the gates

`can_write_payment_promises()` also guards the promise writers, the paid claim and the accepted day; `can_read_payment_promises()` also reads every promise and paid claim in the company. Adding estimators to either hands them all of it, and the digital twins sign in as estimators. `set_payer_pay_calendar` is `SECURITY DEFINER`, so it passes the twin fence: today a twin is refused only because the role gate leaves estimators out. A calendar set on a payer also moves the due day on every bill that payer has out, and an estimator sees none of those bills and may not read the payments the window replays.

## What changed

- **The terms are a kernel.** `src/lib/jobs/draftPayTerms.ts`: `DraftPayTerms` is a `PayCalendar` and whose it is, `job` or `payer`. It opens on `job`, the smaller reach. `draftPayTermsOffered` says when a job still offers its terms: for the job until the job carries a row of its own, for the payer only while the bill reads no calendar at all.
- **They ride the job draft.** `JobDraft.payTerms` (`src/lib/bids/jobDraft.ts`), inside `dispatch_requests.pending_payload.job_draft`. A draft with no terms writes no key, so it is stored exactly as before. `jobDraftWords` adds *pay terms: in by the 25th, paid the 10th, only this job* to the Dispatch row's line.
- **The estimator's line.** `DraftPayTermsPart` in `JobDraftSheet`, under the GC that won: *+ Add their pay terms · if they pay on a set day of the month*. Pressed, it opens in place with **These terms are for**, the calendar's fields and the plain-date read-back. **Done** closes it to one line with **Change**. No payment record is drawn. Picking another GC drops the terms. Never drawn for a digital twin or on a draft with no GC.
- **One set of fields.** `PayCalendarFields` holds the cutoff, the pay day, pay when paid and the clause, lifted out of `PayCalendarModal` so the estimator's part and the office's window ask in the same words.
- **The window takes read terms and a job not made yet.** `PayCalendarModal`'s `initial` gains `calendar`, `scope` and `from`: the form starts on the terms, the window opens on whose they are, a line says *Filled in from Dana Whitfield's draft. Check it, then save.*, and moving the choice keeps the terms. `jobToBe` is New Job's job: the window can be for it alone, the button reads **Use for this job**, and the calendar is handed back with nothing written.
- **New Job's line.** `NewJobPayTermsLine`, above the customer rows beside the payer's terms bar: *Pay terms from Dana Whitfield's draft: In by the 25th, paid the 10th · only this job · Check and use*. Agreed for the job alone it reads *sets with the job*, and `createJob` saves the calendar after the job's insert, best effort like the job's kind and budget: a refusal says so and the job stands. Agreed for the payer, the window saves there and then and the line reads what is stored.
- **Skipped, the job keeps the offer.** `JobPayCalendarRow` reads the bid's Dispatch requests (`loadDraftPayTermsForBid`; the office reads a request that carries a bid, open or closed) and `PayCalendarLine` draws *From Dana Whitfield's draft · … · Check and use* beside whatever the job reads now.
- **The job is already open.** `BidPayTermsToOffice` in the won bid's Job block, for a role that sends a draft, once the bid has a job: *+ Send their pay terms to the office* files a request with `pending_action = 'pay_terms_from_bid'` and the terms in `pending_payload.pay_terms`. `PayTermsRequestActions` gives the Dispatch row **Open the pay calendar**, on the bid's job, filled in, whose save closes the row, and **Not a calendar payer**, which closes it with that answer. Both for the roles that set a calendar.

## Who

Writes the terms down: an estimator (`sendsJobDraft`). Checks and saves: dev, master technician, assistant, controller (`isStagesOfficeRole`, the client's copy of `can_write_payment_promises()`). `draftPayTerms.test.ts` pins both for every role. The database is unchanged: `payer_pay_calendars` and `set_payer_pay_calendar` keep their two gates, and the terms are a Dispatch row under `dispatch_requests`' own policies.

A digital twin sees no line. The twin fence lets a twin insert a Dispatch row for a bid it made or estimates, so the client's hide is not a wall; a stray row is an inbox row and can never become a calendar without an office press.
