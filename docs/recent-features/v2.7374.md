# v2.7374 — Box labels: stop a run, print the rest, and pick a range (2026-11-15)

Taunya's ask of 2026-11-15, the same afternoon as v2.7373's shorter strip, with a run of 455 strips stopped after five by a printer that quit answering: "I would also like the ability to stop a print, midprint. and to select what range of what I'm printing I want to print". Then, with a first build done: "mock up what you think we should build and then ask yourself is this the best we can do and then show it to me and then build it." Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What changed

- **Stop printing.** `endsTheRun(index, total, stopAsked)` in `ptouchRaster.ts` (pure): a label ends the run when it is the last, or when Stop was asked before it was sent. `PtouchLink.print` in `ptouchUsb.ts` takes a third argument, `stopAsked`, reads it before each label, sends that label as the run's last (fed out and cut, as a run's end always was) and sends no more. The press sits beside the progress line in both windows and reads *Stopping…* once pressed, with the line *Stopping. One more strip prints, so the printer can cut the tape.*
- **The progress line names the strip.** `tapeProgressWords` takes the strip's box: *Printing 7 of 455 · G2, strip 2 of 5*; a sticker's number in Print blank stickers.
- **The rest, in Print labels.** `labelPick.ts` (pure, new). `runStrips` is the run as its strips in order, each knowing which of its box's copies it is. `afterStop` reads a run that ended part way: the boxes whose last copy went, and the one box part way through its copies (`PartialBox`). The window unticks the first and remembers the second, drawn with a dashed edge, and the next run prints only that box's missing copies. `partialThatHolds` forgets it when its box is unticked or the copies change. The words are `tapeStoppedWords` and `stoppedDetailWords`: *8 of 455 printed, then you stopped the run. G1 is done and unticked. G2 has 2 of its 5 strips to go. The press prints the rest.* The press counts the strips left (*Print 447 tape labels*) and the title's line reads *90 boxes, 447 strips to go.* A printer that stops by itself keeps the rest the same way, after its own reason. Before this the press still counted every ticked box and started again from the first.
- **A range by its two ends.** `tickedRange`, `leftAfterRangePick` and `rangeCountWords`. Print labels shows **From** and **to** over **Copies of each** when the job has more than two boxes: two lists of the job's boxes that read the first and the last ticked box, and a count, *86 of 91 boxes* (*2 left out between* when chips are unticked inside it). Picking an end ticks every box between the two and unticks the others; an end picked past the other pulls it along. The chips, **None** and **Every box** work as before, and the ends follow them. Print blank stickers shows the same two lists over the numbers of the run on the page, and the press names the range, *Print 8 stickers, 0103 to 0110*; *Print the rest* is kept for a run that stopped.
- The two guides and the map's office Labels paragraph say it.

## The mock-up, and what asking "is this the best we can do" changed

The first build was a Stop press, an untick of the boxes printed whole, and two lists. The mock-up of it, drawn before showing Taunya, had four faults, each fixed in what shipped:

- **It wasted tape after a stop.** A box stopped at 3 of its 5 strips stayed ticked and printed all 5 again. Now the window remembers that box and prints its 2 missing strips, so a stop wastes nothing and no box comes up short.
- **It did not say where the run was.** *Printing 6 of 455* does not name the box in the hand.
- **The range had no count.** Two lists over a wall of chips do not say how many boxes that is.
- **Blank stickers were left out**, when a run of stickers is the plainest range there is.

## The picks

- **One more strip, not a dead stop.** Every label but a run's last is sent to wait in the printer for the next, so the run shares one blank lead-in (v2.6881). The label printing when Stop is pressed was already sent that way, and no command that feeds a waiting label out by itself has been proven on this printer. So the next label is sent as the last. To the printer a run stopped at its seventh label is a run of seven, which is the one thing a printer nobody could reach from here is known to do right. A stop costs one strip and about three seconds, and leaves no strip inside to feed out by hand.
- **The chips are the state.** Print labels has no run in the database to resume, so the rest is what is still ticked, plus the one part-way box. A change to the copies makes the run a new one, and the part-way box prints whole.
- **A range is two lists, not a typed box.** Names are not numbers (`G12`, `RM4`, a name of words), so the ends are picked from the job's own boxes in the order the chips show them.
- **Print blank stickers keeps its own rule for a part-way sticker**: a sticker counts once every copy is out, and the rest starts at the first sticker that is not whole (v2.7272). A stopped set prints whole again, a strip or two spare.
