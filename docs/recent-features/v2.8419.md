# v2.8419 — Agreements: find a sub by their company, and add a trade company to a list (2026-11-23)

Punch list #403, step 6, PR 3 of 3. The owner approved the screens on 2026-11-23 and said to build on the defaults. Client only: it reads `person_trade_companies` (v2.8313) and writes through `gc_add_company`, `gc_company_people` and `name_contact`, all live. No migration and no edge function.

## What was there

People → Contracts put a sub under their company (v2.8381) and gave the company its own papers (v2.8394). Documents → Agreements did not know any of it. **Who is it with?** found a sub by their own name alone and said *Signs for themselves* of everyone. A trade company's own paper sat under **GC projects**, away from its workers. **Add to a list** offered a customer, a supply house or vendor, or a company we keep, and could not file someone as a trade company.

## What changed

- **Who is it with?** finds a roster person by their trade company's name too, and their line reads *Signs for ZZ Sample Mechanical LLC* (`rosterRowSub`, `src/lib/documents/paperPartyPick.ts`). The send roster carries each person's company (`sendRosterWithCompanies`, `src/lib/documents/agreementsSend.ts`), read with the Contract Book through `loadSubCompanies`. Only the office's four roles read the links; anyone else's roster is as it was. A read that fails leaves the roster as it was too, since the company only helps the find box.
- **The chip.** `company_agreement` moves from **GC projects** to **Staff and subs** in `AGREEMENT_GROUP_KINDS`, so a trade company's own paper sits with the subs and the trade parties. An estimator's chips are unchanged: they read it under **Outside companies**, as before.
- **Add to a list** gains *A new trade company* (`listOptions`, `src/lib/documents/paperPartyList.ts`). It needs the company's name, *Type the trade company's name.* otherwise. Its press, **Add as a trade company**, makes the company through `gc_add_company` with the trade Plumbing and the person in it as someone who gets its contracts, then names their contact as that person (`name_contact`, kind `trade`). *Someone at a company we already have* finds trade companies by name as it finds customers, with its own Retry, and keeps the person there as a `gc_company_people` row who gets its contracts. Both writes go through `writeOnce`, so a press after a failure makes nothing twice.

## Defaults for the owner's read

- Papers already filed under a person stay where they are. No data is moved.
- The office's four roles do the pressing.
- An estimator's chip is unchanged.
- A trade company made from Add to a list takes the trade Plumbing, as one made from People does.
- **Who is it with?** lists a sub under their own name, with the company on the line under it. The company is not a row of its own there: its papers are sent from its row on People → Contracts.
