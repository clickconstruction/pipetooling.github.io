- **Words**: *give a project its hours baseline at award* gains *The planned finish*; the card's PR 2 entry and Where it stands.

## Read closer than the card

- **The rough schedule holds no per-stage dates.** `gc_rough_schedules` stores a start, the stage-day overrides and the kept whole-job weeks and finish; each stage's dates are redrawn from the whole GC project, which the New Job form does not hold. So the plan is the job's finish, not a date per stage, and it reaches only a job inside a GC project whose rough was kept. The card's *planned stage dates* wait on a stage schedule the job itself carries.
- **An ordinary bid's job has no rough at all**, so most projects take the assumed plan: the start the bid assumed, the baseline hours, 24 crew hours a day, weekends skipped. The line reads it as the guess it is, and a typed day overrides it in one press on the Costs strip.
- **The crew day is a constant here** (24 h); the line itself reads the job's own crew day from its worked days once there are two or more, so the plan and the pace can disagree by design: the plan is what was promised, the pace is what is happening.
