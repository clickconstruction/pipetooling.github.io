# v2.7150 — Punch list: By number lists every card in one list, the newest on top (2026-11-13)

Todd's ask of 2026-11-13: *"I would like a view where I can see all of the cards by number, because I want to be able to sort from newest to oldest, aka highest number first, and work through them that way."* The page grouped the cards by readiness and nothing else; a number is given once and never refilled, so the highest number is the newest card.

## What changed

`/punch-list` has a **View** choice at the start of the filter row: **By group** (the page as it was: the Shortlist, the flagged rows, each group) and **By number**.

- **By number** draws every card the page renders, open cards and standing lists alike, in one flat list, the highest number on top. Its header says how many are shown: *106 cards*, or *12 of 106 cards* under a filter.
- One press flips the list: **↓ Newest first** becomes **↑ Oldest first**. The press names the way the list runs now, and its title says what the next press does.
- A row is short: the number (`#343`, the row's `#n343` anchor), the name (opens the to-do file on main, as in the groups), a chip for its group in the group's own colour, the size letter the Shortlist reads (the card's own words in its title), the shipped versions when there are any, the first mock-up (*1 of 3* when the card has more) or *waiting on a mock-up*, and the **Next** line cut to one line with the whole text in its title.
- A press on the Next line opens the card under the row exactly as the grouped view draws it: the summary, the whole Next, the links, Size, Blocks, the Opinion, Do / Later / Drop and the note. A second press closes it.
- The **Show** filters and **Waiting on a mock-up** narrow the list as they narrow the groups. Chips under the header narrow it to one group, each with its count. When nothing is left the list says *No cards match the filters.* with **Clear the filters**.
- On a phone the row restacks: the number and the name on the first line, the chips under them, then the Next line.
- The Burndown stays above both views. The Shortlist belongs to the groups and is not drawn in By number.

## Where the choice lives

The address names it: `/punch-list?view=number`, and `&order=oldest` for the oldest first. A press on a View pill or on the sort writes the address (replacing the entry, every other parameter and the hash kept) and remembers the choice on the device (`pt-punch-view`, `pt-punch-number-order`). The address wins over the device, so a link opens what it names; a bare `/punch-list` reads the device, and `?view=group` forces the groups on a device that remembered By number. The defaults write nothing, in the address or on the device.

## How it is built

- `src/lib/todos/punchListByNumber.ts` holds the rules: `byNumber` (highest or lowest first; a card with no number last either way and labelled *no number*; two cards on one number keep the board's order in both directions, so a flip never swaps them), `viewFrom` / `orderFrom` / `searchFor` (the address, the device, the defaults), `shownLine`, `groupCounts`, `mockupDoor` and `hasShippedVersion`. `check:todos` already refuses a card with no number or a shared one; the kernel guards a branch that carries one for an hour.
- `src/components/punchList/PunchListByNumber.tsx` draws the view. The page hands in the opened card as `renderCard`, so the grouped row is drawn by the one `Row` in `src/pages/PunchList.tsx` (it takes `anchor={false}` there, since the By number row carries the `#n<N>` anchor).
- `src/pages/PunchList.tsx` reads the view and the order and switches between the two; the View and Show pills share one style.
