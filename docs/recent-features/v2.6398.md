# v2.6398 — Send a homeowner the warranty in place of a contract (PR 2 of 3) (2026-11-02)

PR 2 of the warranty in place of a contract (v2.6397 laid the data and the kernels). The office sends it and the homeowner initials and signs it, through the Contract Desk's own window, page and functions, each worded by kind.

## What changed

- **The Contract window** (`JobContractModal.tsx`): a **Paper** pick above the paper on a draft, *Contract* or *Warranty*. Picking Warranty on a draft with no lines to initial yet prefills the built-in four (`DEFAULT_WARRANTY_INITIAL_LINES`) and takes the Book's warranty document when there is one: a Contract Book customer document tagged `warranty` is the warranty's terms, every other customer document a contract's; the built-in `DEFAULT_JOB_WARRANTY_TERMS_PLAIN` stands in until the office adds one. The row keeps `kind`; the window's title, the history's fallback name and the preview's heading follow it (`jobAgreementHeading`). The paper (`JobContractPaper.tsx`) prints *Price* in place of *Contract amount* and an **Initial here** section, editable in place one line per thing they initial, each line with a box that shows the initials once signed. The signed-copy banner says *agreement*, since either kind may be on file.
- **The signing page** (`JobContractSign.tsx`): a warranty shows *Price*, the lines to initial each with a box to type two to four letters, and the form's words by kind (*Sign the warranty*; the consent names *this warranty*; the agree sentence certifies the work). The signature is refused until every line is initialled, with the line named (`initialsProblem`); the initials ride in the sign request. A signed warranty shows the initials beside each line.
- **The functions**: `get-job-contract` answers `kind` and `initials` and heads the page by kind; `sign-job-contract` checks a warranty's initials against its `fields.initial_lines` (`initialsForLines`, refusing `initials_missing`), keeps them on the row with the first signature, prints them in the PDF (`jobContractPdf.ts` gains `kind` and `initialLines`, a box per line), names the file *Signed-warranty-J…pdf*, words the office notice and the customer's copy by kind, and writes the **thread line** (`_shared/jobContractThreadLine.ts`: *Dana signed the warranty: Warranty for 407 E 6th St. Each line is initialled.*, keyed once per agreement, on the job's customer's own thread, as `estimateThreadLine.ts` writes an estimate's); `send-job-contract` words its refusals and the email by kind and writes the *went to sign* line per send; `remind-job-contracts` reminds by kind (*our warranty is waiting for your initials and signature*); `share-job-contract`'s draft and signed PDFs carry the kind and the lines. `_shared/jobContract.ts` holds the edge twin of the kernel: `agreementKindOf`, `agreementNoun`, `agreementHeading`, `initialLinesOf`, `initialsForLines`.
- **The chip and the coverage** (`jobContractCoverage.ts`): the coverage read selects `kind`; a sent, signed or drafted warranty is worded *Warranty sent · opened 1× · 5d*, *✍ Warranty signed Sep 1 · M. Palvern*, *Warranty draft*; a row from before the column stays a contract. A signed warranty counts as the job's agreement, so *No contract · crew on site* goes quiet.
- `src/types/database.ts` hand-edited for `kind` and `initials` (the catalog regenerated); `jobContractDocument.ts` `fields.initial_lines`, and the HTML preview's Price, boxes and initials.
