# v2.6697 — Legal desk: a claim line marked settlement only (punch list #171, decision 2) (2026-11-08)

Punch list #171 (`to-dos/legal-desk-claim/README.md`), the card's second open decision, the owner's call of 2026-11-08: a line can be marked *settlement only*, claimed in an offer but not in the demand. Stacked on v2.6696 (the firm sees each line). On job 891 the demand letters claimed the materials left on site and the damages; what the office would have let go in a settlement had no home but the narrative. Now it is a line on the claim with a flag, and every figure knows which side of the line it is on: the **demand's figures** (*How much?*, *What's left for us?*, the firm's total demand, both printed packets' tables) leave it out; the **settlement figures** (the claim bar, *What each path nets*, the desk's and the firm's table with a chip) keep it.

## What changed

- **Migration [`20261108123000_legal_claim_line_settlement_only.sql`](../migrations/20261108123000_legal_claim_line_settlement_only.md)**: `legal_matter_claim_lines.settlement_only boolean NOT NULL DEFAULT false`; `legal_claim_line_save` takes `p_settlement_only boolean DEFAULT false` (the ten-argument signature dropped first, so PostgREST has one candidate and the deployed client keeps working until the new one arrives). Additive, idempotent, no new table.
- **Kernel `legalClaimLines.ts`**: `LegalClaimLineLike.settlementOnly` (read off the row, false before the push); `claimTyped` sums the lines in the demand and `claimSettlementOnly` the rest; `claimTable` marks each row `settlementOnly`, keeps the flagged ones out of `total`, carries `settlementOnly` and `settlementOnlyCount`, and with `{ forDemand: true }` leaves the rows out altogether; `claimSettlementOnlyWords` (under the table) and `claimSettlementOnlyPrintWords` (in a print's place for the rows it leaves out), plain words; the draft carries the flag.
- **`legalPacket.ts`**: `worth.claim` stays the claim as demanded (`balance + retainageAdded + claimTyped`); `worth.claimSettlementOnly` and `worth.claimSettlement = claim + claimSettlementOnly` are new. The fee, the net and the verdict follow the demand, as before.
- **`settlementPaths.ts`** `settlementPathsOfMatter`: the paths start from `worth.claimSettlement` (the claim alone on an older worth), so the trial path's gross, the walk-away line and the *let go* row count the settlement-only lines. **`moneyBars.ts`**: `claimSettlementOnly` input, a `settlement` block after the typed one (`#dbeafe` in `LegalMoneyBars.tsx`), the claim bar's length and caption read the settled claim with *$X in the demand*, *What's left* stays on the demand, and one more sentence. **`deskQuestions.ts`** `howMuch`: counts the typed lines in the demand and ends *One more line, $400.00, is settlement only and not in this figure.* when one is.
- **`LegalClaimTable.tsx`**: the chip {{chip:yellow|settlement only}} on the row, the sentence under the table, a **Settlement only** checkbox on the Add and Edit form; `LegalDesk.tsx` sends `p_settlement_only` and hands the bars `claimSettlementOnly`; `useLegalPacketData.ts` reads the column.
- **The firm**: `legal-portal` sends `settlement_only` on each line (`MATTER_CLAIM_LINE_COLUMNS`; the read falls back to the old columns before the push, `MATTER_CLAIM_LINE_PENDING_COLUMNS`); the page parses it; `FirmClaimTable` chips the row and says *What each path nets counts it*; the firm's demand reads `worth.claim`, so the line never enters it. **Redeploy required.**
- **Both prints** (`legalPacketPrint.ts`, `legalFirmPacketPrint.ts`): the table is drawn `forDemand`, and one sentence stands in for the rows left off.
- **Tests**: `legalClaimLines.test.ts` (the flag, the sums, the table both ways, the words), `deskQuestions.test.ts`, `moneyBars.test.ts`, `settlementPaths.test.ts`, `legalPacket.test.ts`, `legalPortalPayload.test.ts`, `legalMatterShape.test.ts`, both print tests, `LegalClaimTable.render.test.tsx` (the chip, the total, the checkbox).
- **Words**: the guide *review a collections account before it goes to your attorney* (How much?, the bars, the paths, the Account bullet, the print), *share your attorney their portal* (the Account bullet), `docs/GLOSSARY.md` (the Legal desk entry's How much? clause), `docs/ACCESS_CONTROL.md` (the claim-line row), `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` (the legal-portal note), the card: both decisions made and built, the owner's look left.
