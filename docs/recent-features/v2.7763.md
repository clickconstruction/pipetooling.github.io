# v2.7763 — Trips: put an Extra trip on a bill (2026-11-18)

## Why

Punch list #391, PR 3 of 4. A trip marked Extra is work past the job's price, and nothing took it to a bill. The owner picked a line on the job over a bill of its own. PR 1 (v2.7746) made the Job window's billing save safe against a line added elsewhere. PR 2 (v2.7756) laid the ground: `jobs_ledger_fixtures.trip_id` and `trip_put_on_job_line`. This PR is the press and the window.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/trips/tripBillLine.ts`**, the kernel, pure:
  - `tripLineName` and `tripLineWords` make the line's name and the words under it from the trip: *Trip 14, crew of 4, Nov 2 to 8* and *Reset the riser the framers moved. Asked by Walt, by phone.* Both are the office's to change, and both reach the customer's bill. The days are said as the app says a trip's days everywhere (`tripSpanWords`), so *Nov 2 to 8*, where the card's example wrote *Nov 2 to Nov 8*.
  - `tripPutOnBillShows`: Extra, out now or back, no line yet, a role that may add a discount to a job, and the lines read.
  - `tripBillJobChoices`: a trip that names its jobs offers those, a trip with none offers the site's jobs.
  - `tripBillWindowState`: the button names the act and its figure, *Add the line · $6,800*, and says why it is dead.
  - `tripBillStanding`: where the trip's money stands, from its line and the bill that took it. *Extra · not billed yet*, *Extra · on J2101's lines, on no bill yet*, *Extra · on a bill, not sent*, *Extra · billed*, *Extra · paid*.
- **`src/lib/trips/tripBillLineIo.ts`** and **`src/hooks/useTripBillLines.ts`**: one small read of the lines the site's trips hold, by `trip_id`, with the status of the bill that took each. The press and its dry run through `trip_put_on_job_line`. The function's own refusals are thrown as its words.
- **`src/components/trips/TripBillLineWindow.tsx`**: the window. The job, a typed amount, the line, the words under it, and the job total before and after from the function's dry run, asked again when the job or the amount settles. A dev or a controller sees *What it cost* under the amount, the trip record's own figure. Nobody else is handed a cost.
- **`DevelopmentTripsLook.tsx`** and **`DevelopmentTripsCard.tsx`**: **Put on a bill** beside **Change** on an open trip. The door to the line, *Open J2101 on Bill*, in the press's place once the trip holds one. The billing chip says the standing on the row and on the open trip, amber while a bill is owed and green once one went out.
- **The Job window opens with lines picked.** `pickLineIds` rides `OpenEditJobOptions` and `JobWindowEditOpenOptions` through `JobWindowModal` to `JobFormModal`. The form ticks the asked lines once the job is in (`pickedLineIdsToSeed` in `billTabMoney.ts`) and scrolls to **Bill the N picked**. A line that cannot be ticked by hand is skipped without a word.
- **History**: `trip_line_added` reads as a *Trip* row under Billing (`jobActivityEvent.ts`).
- **Docs**: the guide *keep a trip to a site away from home* gains *Put a trip on a bill* and *Read where a trip's money stands*. `docs/BILLING_FLOWS.md` says a trip's line is an ordinary line and not a trip charge. `docs/GLOSSARY.md` names the press, the line and the chip.

## The card's defaults, as built

- **1, which trips show the press:** Extra, and out now or back. A planned trip shows none.
- **2, who may press:** dev, master_technician, assistant and controller. A primary sees the look, the chip and the door, and no press.
- **3, a trip on two jobs:** the window lists the trip's jobs and the office picks one.
- **4, a trip with no job named:** the window lists the site's jobs, and the function adds the picked job to the trip's jobs.
- **5, the billing moved off Extra while a line exists:** the chip still reads the line, as *Inside the price · on J2101's lines, on no bill yet*, and the door stays.
- **7, a GC billing job:** the function refuses it. The window shows that refusal in the function's words as soon as the job and the amount are in, and the button stays dead.

## Things the card said to check

- **Who reads a line.** The five roles that see the Trips look (dev, master_technician, assistant, controller, primary) all read `jobs_ledger_fixtures`. A read that fails leaves every trip with its own billing word and no press.
- **A covered line.** A line that amount-only bills already cover to the cent cannot be ticked, so the Job window opens on Bill with nothing picked. The trip then reads *on the job's lines, on no bill yet* until a bill holds the line's `invoice_id`. It stays that way.
- **A stale Job window** on another screen is PR 1's guard: its next save of the lines is refused and it reads the trip's line in.

## Live walk

2026-11-18, after PR 2's migration was pushed, from this branch's dev server against the live database, as a dev, on the ZZ Test development and its ZZ job.

1. A ZZ Test trip was made on the site: one made-up person, three past days, Extra, asked by them, its work on the ZZ job. It read *Extra · not billed yet* and showed **Put on a bill**.
2. The window opened on the trip's one job with the name and the words made. A typed 6,800 put the figure on the button, and the function's dry run said the job total before and after.
3. **Add the line · $6,800** added the line. The job's window opened on Bill, scrolled to the trip's line, ticked, with **Bill the 1 picked · $6,800** beside it. The database held the line with its trip, at the next position, the total raised by the amount, and one money row in History.
4. **Bill the 1 picked** made a $6,800 draft bill for that line alone. Nothing was sent. The trip read *Extra · on a bill, not sent*.
5. The draft was deleted and the trip read *Extra · on the job's lines, on no bill yet*, with the door in the press's place. The door opened the job on Bill with the line picked.
6. Moved to Inside the price, the trip still read its line and kept its door. It was put back to Extra.
7. A reprice of the line in the Job window saved, and the line kept its trip.
8. The line was removed in the Job window. The trip read *Extra · not billed yet* again and the press was back.
9. The trip was deleted. The ZZ job is at its one line and its total, with no bill, and the site holds no trip.

Widths and themes. Against the real database the walk was in the light theme, at desk width throughout and at phone width for the trip's chip and its door. The dark theme, at desk and phone width, was walked before the push with the two database calls stood in for in the page: the press, the chip, the window and the Job window opening on Bill.

Not walked: a role other than dev, a trip on two jobs, a trip with no job named and a GC billing job. The kernel tests, the render tests and the SQL bed hold those.
