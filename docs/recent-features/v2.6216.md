# v2.6216 — Accounts payable: the doors out of the aging cell modal (2026-10-31)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Punch list **#132** PR 4 of 4, the one that
waited on real use: PRs 1 to 3 (v2.5673 the cell modal, v2.5675 the chip as an act, v2.5677
the move window) and the Needs You line (v2.5723) have been live since 2026-10-21. The card's
PR 4 section was the spec: *row press → house detail loads → the existing invoice form opens
(Edit for any row; the No due date modal's rows say Add a due date ›; the Pick a job › chip
lands here too); a Make a payment › footer button on house-scoped modals → `openApplyPaymentForm`.*
The to-do folder `to-dos/ap-aging-cell-invoices/` retires with this PR; the four fragments and
the Closed line in `to-dos/README.md` carry the record.

## What shipped

- **Kernels** [`supplyHouseAging.ts`](../../src/lib/supplyHouseAging.ts): `agingCellRowDoors(inv, allocationCount)`
  — which fix-it doors a row offers: **Add a due date ›** on a charge with no due date, **Pick a
  job ›** on a charge on no job, neither on a credit (it has a size, not an age, and its pairing
  line is its job), no Pick a job until the jobs are read (`null`). `agingCellModalDoors(houseId,
  cell)` — a house-scoped modal opens the house and makes a payment; the credits cell opens the
  house only (credit memos are applied, not paid); the footer row's modal and the worked list span
  every house, so neither. Both unit-tested.
- **The doors** [`AgingCellDoors.tsx`](../../src/components/materials/AgingCellDoors.tsx):
  `AgingInvoiceDoor` (the invoice number as the row's door, `aria-label` *Edit invoice N*),
  `AgingRowDoors` (the two fix-it presses), `AgingMakePaymentDoor` (the footer's). They only
  draw; the decision is the kernel's. Render smoke: each press hands back its act, a row with
  nothing to fix draws no door, a busy row's presses are dead, the footer door follows the modal's
  decision.
- **The wiring** in [`SupplyHousesTab`](../../src/components/SupplyHousesTab.tsx): `openInvoiceFromAgingCell(inv, focus)`
  loads the house detail (`loadSupplyHouseDetail` now returns the invoices it loaded, with their
  allocations) and opens the existing form on that row through `openEditInvoice` — the same door
  the expanded house's pencil is — with the Due date box or the job search focused and scrolled
  into view when a fix-it door asked for it. `openPaymentFromAgingCell` loads the house and opens
  `openApplyPaymentForm`. The modal stays under either form; a save or delete from the form bumps
  `agingCellJobsRefresh`, so the open modal re-reads its jobs and a row whose job was just picked
  leaves *No job yet* without a close and reopen. One press at a time: `agingCellDoorBusy` holds
  the row or house whose detail is loading, and its presses are dead meanwhile. The *No job yet*
  heading now says *Press Pick a job › on the row to choose its job.* A cell with a dash stays a
  dash; the phone bars are unchanged; nothing about paid invoices.
- **Guides**: *mark supply-house bills paid* gains **Fix a bill from the cell**; *mark an invoice
  on a job account* § Work a cell by job names Pick a job ›.

## Verify

As the dev on the dev server against prod: Materials → Supply houses → Accounts payable. Press a
house's past-due cell, then a bill's number: the house detail expands below and the Edit window
opens over the cell's window with that bill's number, date, amount and jobs. Press the No due date
cell, then **Add a due date ›**: the Edit window opens with the Due date box focused. On a row under
*No job yet*, **Pick a job ›** opens the Edit window with the job search focused. **Make a payment ›**
at a house window's foot opens Apply Payment with that house's unpaid bills. The Total row's window
offers no payment button; the credits window offers Open the house only. Escape closes the top
window first, and the cell's window is still there.
