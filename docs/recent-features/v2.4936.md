# v2.4936 — GC mode: one sender for every email to a trade partner (2026-10-08)

P3-a of the trade partner portal's real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/PORTAL_REAL_BUILD.md`; plan `to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/portal-p3.md`, both on branch `spike/gc-mode`). It adds the edge function `gc-trade-email`, the one sender every GC lane calls to email a trade partner company. There is no database change: `gc_trade_messages` and `gc_trade_portal_links` rode in P1a. Nothing calls it yet. P3-b (the questions window's answer), New project's step 7 (a set's plans) and the Ask window (`invite`, `nudge`) switch to it the evening it is deployed.

- **The function** (`supabase/functions/gc-trade-email/index.ts`, `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` → gc-trade-email): `POST { companyId, kind, key, projectId | null, lang, subject, lines, group? }` with a staff JWT. The checks run in the plan's order:
  - the caller: a dev until the portal's door, never a training account or a twin;
  - the shape, then Spanish held;
  - the company, then its ask on the project;
  - the key sent once;
  - who gets it, then the link (made on the company's first email);
  - the email, the send, the `gc_trade_messages` row and the sent copy.
  A failed send writes no row. Errors are keys (`TRADE_EMAIL_ERRORS`).
- **The rules** (`supabase/functions/_shared/gcTradeEmail.ts`, pure):
  - `parseTradeEmail`, the 19 portal kinds plus `paper`, and `tradeEmailGroup`, a copy of `portalMailGroup`.
  - `tradeEmailRecipients` (a copy of `mailRecipients`) and `tradeEmailReach`, which skips anyone with no email and sends to one address once.
  - `tradeGreeting` by first names ("Hello Marcus, Ana and Dana,") and `buildGcTradeEmail`: the portal's paper look, **Open your portal** to `/t/<token>` with the address under it, then the link line and the signer.
  - The five words are copies of `portalI18n.ts`'s, and the From name is a copy of `GC_COMPANY.name`.
- **From and Reply-To**: from `Click Construction` on `EMAIL_FROM`'s address (`mailboxWithName`), not `COMPANY_EMAIL_FROM`, which names the plumbing company. Reply-To is the project's project manager, else the sender, who also signs.
- **The sent copy**: filed after the row (kind `gc_trade_email`, source `gc_trade_messages`). `sentCopyKeptHtml` keeps the link as `/t/…`, the law firm's rule (`docs/SENT_COPIES.md`).
- **The office's side** (`src/lib/gc/tradeEmail.ts`, `tradeEmailIo.ts`), for the callers:
  - `sendGcTradeEmail(request)`, which never throws for a refusal;
  - `gcTradeEmailRefusal(key)`, the office's words for each key;
  - `tradeMailLang(company.lang)`, which is English while Spanish is held;
  - `inviteEmailLines(message, lang)`, an invitation's lines as the email carries them.
  - `canSendGcTradeEmail(role)` in `access.ts` is the client's copy of the function's roles, so a screen offers the send only to someone who can.
- **What customers see**: the sub journey's new step **GC mode: an email to a trade partner** renders the sample company's invitation through the same builder, from the portal's own kernels, linking `/t/sample`. Added alongside: the `gc-trade-email` sender in `CUSTOMER_SURFACES`, `personJourney`'s `na`, and the `EMAIL_CATALOG` row `gc_trade_email`.
- **Guide**: the new `email-a-trade-partner-from-gc-mode`. `GLOSSARY.md`'s trade partner paragraph gets its sentence in the docs PR after the Board's #4972 lands, because that PR rewrites the paragraph.
- **Tests**:
  - `gcTradeEmail.test.ts`: the shape for every kind, and the group and recipients against main's kernels on five made-up companies. Also the greeting for one, two and three names, the frame, the token, the words and the Spanish flag, and the office's side.
  - `sentCopyEmail.test.ts`: the kept copy never has `/t/` followed by a token.
  - `access.test.ts` holds the roles equal. The registry, person journey, catalog and sample-email tests cover the new sender and step.
- **After merge**: `supabase functions deploy gc-trade-email` (the lead). The live send waits on the owner's inbox (his call 3): a dev's press on the test company only, pressed by the lead or confirmed with Grace.
