# v2.6589 — Your week: one progress signal a tech can watch move, every week (2026-11-06)

Punch list #255 (`to-dos/technician-progress-signals/`), the card itself, after the logbook train (v2.6472 to v2.6502), the review credit (v2.6584) and the job target line (v2.6585). Micah's ask of 2026-11-03: "we are not doing a great job showing our teammates the progress they are making week to week." The article's claim behind it: the reward system fires on a signal that the reward is getting closer. So each tech gets one leading measure in their own hands, on a fixed beat, compared only to their own last eight weeks.

## Decisions

- **No card until the office picks** (the mock-up's second pass), with the role's guess named on the picker instead of a silent default: the app does not know an apprentice from a journeyman until it is told.
- **The pay week**, Sunday to Saturday, is the week: the one the clock, Draft Payroll and Your record already use.
- **A callback is a press, never an inference** (call 3, answered 2026-11-03), and it counts against every tech clocked on the first job, not the lead alone (decision 4).
- **The crew signal is the lead's**: counted on finished jobs with a target, with the no-target count named (decision 5). Under the target reads ✓ and *under*; over reads plain, in the grey pill.
- **Hours before the clock** (the opening balance on the Licenses tab) are still to build; the bar reads the clock's approved hours alone, as the logbook does today.
