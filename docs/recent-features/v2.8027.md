# v2.8027 — Lien desk: on a missed notice, a paper already on record can stand for a closed month (2026-11-20)

The read-only live walk of v2.8026 (punch list #361) opened **Record it…** on a missed notice and found its first path a dead end. Grace's yes of 2026-11-20 to offering a missed notice's closed months to tick. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

The already-mailed step (`LienNoticeAlreadyWent`, v2.7508) has two paths. When the property holds a notice on record, **Which paper went?** lists those papers, and the office ticks the month the chosen paper stood for (`LienNoticeApplyPane`). The step is handed the months to offer as `apply.months`, and the desk handed it the notice's own months (`monthsList`). On a missed notice with no month still due, those are empty. The step said *The desk is not asking this job for a month.*, and its Apply could only refuse with *Tick the month this paper stands for.* The second path, *It is not on this list. Record it now ›*, where the months are typed, worked.

So the case the step was built for could not be recorded on the notice that shows it: a paper already on record at the property that named this job's closed month.

## What changed

- [`lienNoticeApply.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienNoticeApply.ts) `lienApplyMonthsOffered({ pile, months, dueMonths, missedMonths })`: on the missed pile with no month still due, the notice's own months and the closed months the desk calls missed, once each, oldest first. Everywhere else, the notice's own months as before, so a draft still opens the step with its own months ticked and no closed month beside them.
- [`LienDesk.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDesk.tsx) hands the step that list as `apply.months`.
- Nothing else changed. The step ticks every offered month that has no notice yet, as it always has. `lienApplyChecks` rules on each ticked month: a paper that went before the month began is refused, a month the paper does not print or a paper after the month's deadline (*It reads as late*) is a warning that asks for the reason, and the reason is kept on the record. `lienApplySetsAside` sets a waiting notice aside only when every month it names has a notice afterwards. A missed notice usually has no waiting notice, so none is set aside.

## Tests

- `lienNoticeApply.test.ts` (+1): the missed pile with nothing due offers its closed months, oldest first and once each; with a month still due, on a draft and on a sent notice, only the notice's own months.
- `LienDesk.render.test.tsx` (+1, 235 in all): the step's read of the property's papers is mocked, so no test reads a real one. On a missed notice with nothing due, a paper on record that names May offers May, ticked. Apply writes May with nothing set aside. The test fails with the step handed the notice's own months.
