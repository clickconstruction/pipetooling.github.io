# v2.4271 — Add clock session: the calendar stops at the assistant's hours window, and so does the database (2026-09-30)

The owner, after the typed-hours train (#70): the **Add clock session** calendar "allows her to go really far back, but in settings it is set right now for her to only go so many weeks back" — make the assistant able to add sessions only as far back as her lookback window, and make the calendar show it.

## What was true

The window (`assistant_hours_window_weeks_v1`, v2.1592, default 3 weeks) clamped the Hours tab, the clock strip and Who's where on the client — and nothing else. Three doors type a session for someone else, all through [`ClockSessionEditSplitModal`](../../src/components/ClockSessionEditSplitModal.tsx): the Hours strip's **+ Add session**, the day audit's, and the Team board's. Each had two unbounded `datetime-local` pickers, and each rejected any day but the door's own *after* Save ("Clock in and out must fall on 2026-09-30") — so the calendar was a trap, not a limit. The edit form's pickers could move a session to any day. On the server the insert policy has no date check and the leader split RPCs bypass their week fence for assistants.

## What changed

- **One day, two times.** The create form has a **Day** date input with `min` = the floor and `max` = today, and **Clocked in** / **Clocked out** time inputs. The native calendar greys every day outside the range on Chrome, Safari and the iPad wheel. The day floats inside the window, so from the strip an assistant picks the person, picks Fri Sep 25, saves — the session lands on that day and the toast names it. A stop time at or before the start reads as the next day, as the worker's missed-day door does.
- **Day-locked doors lose the date field.** The day audit and the Team board pass `dayLocked`: the day is in the title (*Add clock session · Fri Sep 25*) and there is nothing to go back with.
- **A line under the day** says the bounds and who to ask: *Sun, Sep 13 through today. Ask the owner for earlier days.* / *Today only…* / *Any day through today.* / *This session goes on Fri, Sep 25.* A day outside the bounds turns the line red and disables Save. Kernel [`sessionDayBounds.ts`](../../src/lib/clock/sessionDayBounds.ts).
- **The edit form** bounds its three pickers the same way and checks the clock-in day before a save or a split.
- **The database holds the rule** — migration `20260930210418_assistant_hours_window_fence` ([doc](../migrations/20260930210418_assistant_hours_window_fence.md)): `assistant_hours_window_floor()` and a BEFORE trigger on `clock_sessions` for inserts and time-column updates. Every door, every RPC, every old tab. Approvals and notes are not time columns and pass.
- **One hook** — [`useAssistantHoursWindow`](../../src/hooks/useAssistantHoursWindow.ts) — replaces the three copies of the setting read (People, Who's where, the modal). The Settings block's copy and toast say the window is what assistants *see and type*. The guide *set how far back assistants can see and add hours* is rewritten in plain words and off the legacy list.

## Known limits

The Hours grid's typed `people_hours` cells rely on the clamped date range; the fence is on clock sessions. The window is the assistant's for her own sessions too — her own missed-day door already fences at this-or-last week.
