## What the press takes

- The notices of the run that are right and still to print, and the affidavits of the run that are right (`lienRunPrintPlan`).
- Never a paper with an open question, a paper left out of this run, or a paper an earlier run left out that nobody has answered for. The plan reads the checklist's standings, so a left-out paper that is ready on the desk still does not print here.
- Not a ready paper due after the run's day. The Board's prints take those.
- Not a printed notice: it is in the run, and it is not printed again.

## One stack from two documents

The notices and the affidavits are two print documents with two layouts. The notices print on the printer's own margins in a body 44rem wide. The affidavits print on a page with no margin and carry their own gap on every sheet, so the short form fits its sheet. Neither is redrawn.

`lienRunStackHtml` (`src/lib/jobs/lienRunStack.ts`) lifts each document's styles and body as they are. Every `body` rule moves onto the part's wrapper. The affidavits' `@page` becomes a named page, and their wrapper sits on it. So the notices keep the printer's margins and the affidavits keep none, in one print job. A divider page stands between them.

The merge refuses what it does not know: a document with more than one style block, a style block in its body, or a rule on a bare element other than `body` and `html`. Its test merges the real documents, so either shell changing shape fails CI before it prints a paper that moved. One part alone is that part's own document, byte for byte.

## Proof that no paper moved

Printed to PDF in Chromium 149 (Playwright), each paper alone and the merged stack, then every word's position compared page by page (`pdftotext -bbox`, to 0.1 pt).

| Run | Notices' pages | Affidavits' pages | Pages that differ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Printer margins 0 | 28 | 5 | 0 |
| Printer margins 0.4 in | 28 | 5 | 0 |

Six notices (three with a long cover letter, two contractors) and four short-form affidavits behind their help sheet, in three counties. The merged stack is 34 pages: 28, the divider, 5. With margins of 0.4 in the notices' words move with the margin and the affidavits' words do not, in the stack as alone.

## Where one print job cannot hold both

The stack needs a browser that gives a named page its own margins. `lienStackBrowserCan` asks `CSS.supports('page', …)`. Where the answer is no and the run has both kinds, the card shows two presses, **Print N notices** and **Print N affidavits**, each the run's own papers as that kind's own document. The same happens if the merge refuses a document. Chromium was measured. Safari and Firefox were not.

## After the press

- The notices are filed as one packet on every job in it, and each affidavit alone on its job, under the same kinds and titles their own presses file (`sent_documents`). The filed copy is the paper alone, never the stack.
- The notices whose every copy printed are stamped printed, as the run window stamps them (`markLienDeskItemsPrinted`), and the desk reads again.
- No print is recorded for an affidavit, as before. Until step 7 reads the filed copies, a ready affidavit still counts as to print after a reload. In the sitting the card says what printed and when.
