# v2.5892 — Legal desk Timeline: the line reads first, one composer (2026-10-26)

The Timeline tab spent roughly 580px on an always-open Plan a step form, a mode switch and a rarely used select before the first entry. Now the line reads first; the composer is one collapsed door.

- The composer sleeps behind one **Add to the timeline** button at the right end of the lane chips (`data-legal-composer-door`); the first entry starts about 100px down.
- One `ComposerForm` replaces `AddForms` + the add-flavored `PlanForm` + `EventForm`: the day decides the kind (`composerKind` — a day ahead of today is a planned step, today or earlier records what happened) and the tag beside the day overrides it. `PlanForm` stays for the Edit flow on a live step; the portal's own `FirmPlanForm` is untouched.
- The `Serves the deadline` select became a suggestion: `composerDeadlineSuggestion` offers the nearest open lien-clock deadline the step's day lands on or before, within 30 days (`COMPOSER_SUGGEST_DAYS`), as a one-line question with **Serves it** / **No**; **Serves a deadline…** opens the full select for a manual pick. The after-the-deadline warning (`data-legal-plan-late`) stays.
- An event's proving documents pick as `aria-pressed` chips — the first three plus "N more…" — instead of a vertical checkbox list.
- The design round (before/after artboards, the ~580px → ~100px measurement) is on the "Timeline composer, compact" design canvas, Oct 26; the handoff case behind it is Click v. Burd (job 891).

In passing: the v2.5850 document-burst row's grid cell gets `minWidth: 0`, so its one-line document list shrinks with its ellipsis at phone width instead of forcing the panel ~340px wide (found in the v2.5892 viewport pass).

Kernel: `composerKind`, `composerDeadlineSuggestion` in `src/lib/legal/legalTimeline.ts` with unit tests. The panel's render tests were rewritten for the door flow (open, the day flips the tag, suggestion accept/dismiss, manual pick). The help guide "review a collections account before it goes to your attorney" follows the new form.
