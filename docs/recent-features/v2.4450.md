# v2.4450 — Procurement log: enter the GC's answer from the line that waits on it (2026-10-02)

The door the *Their answer* mock-up drew on a line of the procurement log ([`to-dos/submittals/their-answer-before-after.html`](../../to-dos/submittals/their-answer-before-after.html), *Where the door is*), left out of [v2.4442](v2.4442.md) while the order-only train (v2.4435–v2.4445) was rebuilding the same panel. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What changed

- **The kernel** — `answerDoor(r)` and `ANSWER_DOOR_WORDS` in [`procurementLog.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/procurementLog.ts): a line offers the door only while the GC's answer holds it (`awaiting`, `not_submitted`, `sent_back`) and only for a part the GC sees. *Enter their answer…* on a line nobody answered, *Change their answer…* on one sent back. None on a hand line, an order-only part, an order-only fixture (`noGc`), a row with no product, or a line that is released, ordered or delivered: the row's own *Their answer* button covers those.
- **The log** ([`SubmittalProcurementPanel`](../../src/components/bids/SubmittalProcurementPanel.tsx)): a new optional `onAnswerItem({ itemId, partKey })`; the door is its own small link under the line's status (`data-testid="procurement-answer-door"`), in the table's status cell and on a phone card. The status itself still opens the line's dates. With no `onAnswerItem` (the GC's copies, older callers) nothing is drawn.
- **The window** ([`SubmittalAnswerDialog`](../../src/components/bids/SubmittalAnswerDialog.tsx)): `focusPartId` rings the part (an inset blue line, `data-focused`), scrolls it into view and puts the keyboard on its answer: the one it has, else Approved. Opened from the row, nothing is ringed.
- **The tab** ([`BidsSubmittalsTab`](../../src/components/bids/BidsSubmittalsTab.tsx)): `answerFocus` beside `answering`, cleared when the window closes; the handler maps the line's `partKey` to the part by `procure_key`, as the Edit door does, and ignores an order-only row.

## Decided here

- **Drafts get the door.** Estimators send the package by email and record the answer on a draft (BP375 and BP398 are both drafts). The draft note still says once that nothing is released; the door is an action, not a status, so it does not repeat *Not shared*.
- **One door per line, not per fixture.** Each part's line opens the same window ringed on its own part, so *approved, except the flush valve* is still one Save from any of the fixture's lines.
- **Released and ordered lines get no door.** A correction there goes through the row's *Their answer* button.

## Walked live

Worktree dev server against prod, BP398 (ZZ Test) Rev 3, a draft, at 1280 px, *To order*. *Waiting on the GC* read 19 lines; unfolded, Moore Supply's 18 parts showed 10 doors, one on each part the GC sees and none on its 8 order-only parts. The door sits inside the status cell under *Dates…*, and the table does not grow sideways. *Enter their answer…* on Toilets / LEONARD 170A-LF opened *Toilets · what did they say?* with LEONARD ringed, in view, and the keyboard on its Approved; Enter, then *Record 1 answer*: the line moved to *Order now · Moore Supply* (*Released, not ordered*) without its door, and the waiting count fell to 18. The row's own *Their answer* opened the window unringed, and taking the answer back returned LEONARD to *Waiting on the GC* with its door. Another session was recording answers on the same Toilets row at the same minute; its three answers were left as they were.

Seen on the way, not changed here: the log's *released* date is cut from the UTC timestamp (`rowFrom`), so an answer recorded after 7 pm Central reads as the next day. Filed as its own fix.
