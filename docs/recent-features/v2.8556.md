# v2.8556 — Tagged notes: the answer box and its presses take a thumb on a phone (2026-11-24)

Punch list #363, a measure from the live walk of its tagged side on 2026-11-24. No migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

At 380 px, in Tagged notes, the answer box was 175 by 22 px, **@** 30 by 20, **Post** 60 by 26 and **No reply needed** 126 by 30. A press on a phone needs 44 px.

## What changed

`src/components/jobs/TaggedJobNotesSection.tsx`, the card of one tag (`TagCard`):

- the answer box, **Post** and **No reply needed** take `phoneTall` as their least height (`src/lib/phonePressFloor.ts`): their own size at a desk, 44 px where `index.css` sets `--phone-press-min`, which is a phone's width;
- the **@** press is the tag box's big one (`bigAt`, 44 by 44) on a narrow screen, as the job window's box already has it.

The same card is drawn in the Tagged notes window, on the Pipeline and on the Checklist, so all three follow.

## Not in this change

The walk also found that a helper in Job Mode is told of a tag only inside the Inbox tab. That is written on the card with two ways to mend it, and is not built.
