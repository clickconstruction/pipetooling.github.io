# v2.4392 — Materials: check the prices you spend the most on, in one window (2026-10-02)

PR 2 of the material prices plan (canvas *Material prices over time*). PR 1 (v2.4391) showed the number and how fresh the prices behind it are. This PR gives the card its one way to make them fresher. The plan's second call was "one person, on the first Monday of each month; the card shows the reminder when the oldest of the 20 passes 30 days".

## What changed
- **The kernel** (`materialPriceIndex.ts`) adds `checks` to the index:
  - the `CHECK_COUNT` (20) basket pairs with the most spend, oldest check first (no date first, then the larger spend)
  - their share of spend, and the oldest day
  - `due` once that day is more than `CHECK_DUE_DAYS` (30) old
  
  `BasketPair` carries its `priceId`, which the read now embeds. `parsePriceInput` reads `$1,234.50` and refuses anything at or under $0 or at the stand-in.
- **The writes** ([`priceCheckIo.ts`](../../src/lib/materials/priceCheckIo.ts)) touch only the price row:
  - **Same** sets `effective_date` to today. The `track_price_history_on_update` trigger fires on a changed date and logs the price against itself, as the person checking. It is `SECURITY DEFINER`, so history's row security never stops it. A price already dated today logs nothing new.
  - **A typed price** sets price and date together.
  - Each write asks for the row back, so a read-only account's silent no-op reads *This account can’t change prices.*
- **The window** [`CheckPricesWindow`](../../src/components/materials/CheckPricesWindow.tsx) is a `ResponsiveModalShell` (a sheet on a phone). Each line shows the house, the book price and the last check. Its button reads *Same* until a price is typed, then *Save*, and Enter saves too. While you type it shows:
  - the change, a rise in orange and a drop in blue
  - a warning on a big jump (1.5× or half)
  - *Type a price like 12.50.* on a typo, which holds the save
  
  A failed save keeps the typed price. Progress counts the lines checked and their share of spend. The footer says a new price changes the book from today and a bid keeps the prices it already has.
- **The card**: under *How fresh*, the reminder (*The oldest of your top 20 was last checked Apr 14.*) and a blue *Check 20 prices* when due. Otherwise a calm line and a plain button. Then *20 parts are N% of what you bid. About ten minutes.* After a write the card reads again, and `useMaterialPriceIndex` keeps the card up meanwhile for the same trade.
- The guide *track what your materials cost* gains *Check 20 prices*, with the first-Monday habit. The glossary's Price Confirmation and the Materials map say where the check writes.
