# v2.4472 — Pipeline and Job Summary: a percent or a job set in the evening keeps its day (2026-10-03)

One of the instant-day fixes that follow [v2.4452](v2.4452.md). Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

These readers cut days out of `timestamptz` columns by their first ten characters, so anything stamped after 7 pm CDT (6 pm CST) read as the next day:

- **A job's percent on the Pipeline**: *80% typed Sep 4* in the row's progress cell ([`progressPaymentCell.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/progressPaymentCell.ts), `percentClause`) and the date under the money bar ([`jobMoneyBar.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobMoneyBar.ts)), from the newest percent event's `changed_at`, the newest hand-set, or the newest field report's `created_at`, all `timestamptz` in `list_job_crew_position`.
- **When that percent is behind** ([`jobCrewPosition.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobCrewPosition.ts), `percentIsStale`): the percent's day is compared with the last clock-in day, a Central `work_date`. A percent typed at 8 pm on Sep 11 read as Sep 12, so a crew on site Sep 12 did not mark it behind.
- **Job Summary**: a job with no clock hours dated its last worked day from `jobs_ledger.created_at` ([`jobSummaryLedgerView.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobSummaryLedgerView.ts)), and Rework dated a return from it when the ledger had no first day ([`jobSummaryRework.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobSummaryRework.ts)). Capacity read a person's `people.archived_at` the same way ([`jobSummaryCapacity.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobSummaryCapacity.ts)), so one archived in the evening counted as available for one more day.
- **A clock-in with no `work_date`**: Quickfill's *Complete, no Total Bill* work days and its *Started* line ([`quickfillCompleteNoBill.ts`](../../src/lib/quickfillCompleteNoBill.ts), [`QuickfillCompleteNoBillSection.tsx`](../../src/components/quickfill/QuickfillCompleteNoBillSection.tsx)), and the phone's hours waiting ([`hoursPhone.ts`](../../src/lib/people/hoursPhone.ts)), fell back to `clocked_in_at`'s UTC day.
- **The Dashboard's checks that came back** ([`dashboardNeedsYou.ts`](../../src/lib/dashboardNeedsYou.ts)): with no today passed, a case's today fell back to its `opened_at`'s UTC day. The Dashboard always passes today, so this path is not reached.

## What changed

Each instant reads `calendarYmdInAppTzFromIso(iso)` from [`dateUtils.ts`](../../src/utils/dateUtils.ts) (`APP_CALENDAR_TZ`). The `date` columns keep their slice: `work_date`, `last_work_date`, `start_date` and `end_date`.

## Not changed

The clock split window ([`ClockSessionEditSplitModal.tsx`](../../src/components/ClockSessionEditSplitModal.tsx)) writes each part's `work_date` from the first ten characters of its time boxes. Those hold wall-clock times already in `APP_CALENDAR_TZ` (`toDatetimeLocal`), so the slice is the Central day.

## Tests

- `progressPaymentCell.test.ts` and `jobMoneyBar.test.ts` (+1 each): a percent set at `2026-09-04T00:30:00Z` (7:30 pm CDT on Sep 3) reads *80% typed Sep 3*; `2026-12-02T00:30:00Z` (6:30 pm CST) reads Dec 1; noon UTC its own day; the money bar's date reads *Aug 7* for a percent set at 7:30 pm CDT on Aug 7.
- `jobCrewPosition.test.ts` (+1): a percent typed at 8 pm CDT on Sep 11 is behind once the crew works Sep 12; one typed at noon on Sep 12 is not.
- `jobSummaryLedgerView.test.ts`, `jobSummaryRework.test.ts`, `jobSummaryCapacity.test.ts` (+1 each): a job made in the evening dates from that evening's day; a return made at 7:30 pm CDT on Jun 22 is 17 days after a Jun 5 bill; a person archived at 8 pm CDT on Aug 6 is off the roster from Aug 6.
- `quickfillCompleteNoBill.test.ts` and `hoursPhone.test.ts` (+1 each): a clock-in with no work date at 7:30 pm CDT reads its own day.

All 8 fail on main. Quickfill's *Started* line and the Dashboard's unreached fallback change one expression each, and no test draws them.

## Checked against prod

Read only, from the worktree dev server: of the 112 open jobs, 12 have their newest percent event, 1 its newest hand-set percent and 4 their newest field report stamped in a Central evening, so their percent lines read a day earlier. None flips between current and behind. 1 of the 6 archived people was archived in an evening. No clock session lacks a work date.
