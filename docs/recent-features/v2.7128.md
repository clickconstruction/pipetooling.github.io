# v2.7128 — Punch list: a burndown shows the open cards hour by hour, and when the list could clear (2026-11-13)

Grace's ask of 2026-11-12, after a day of reading the same chart in a chat artifact: *"can you help me save this to the punchlist page on the app so it's persistent?"* Earlier that day she had asked for it hour by hour, *"since I tend to work in waves"*.

## What changed

`/punch-list` opens with a **Burndown** section (`#burndown`), above the *Show* filters and the Shortlist:

- A sentence: what is open (cards and points), what the chosen stretch closed and took in, when the list clears with no new cards and at that stretch's pace, and how many open cards wait on an owner decision or on time.
- **Look at** (the last 24 hours, 3 days, 7 days, 14 days, or all of it) and **Count by** (Cards, or Points by size).
- Six tiles: open cards, points left, closed a day, new a day, *Clears with no new cards*, *Clears at this pace*.
- *Open work hour by hour, and where it heads*: the open line, then a dashed line (no new cards) and a dotted one (this pace) running on from now.
- *New and closed, in waves*: what came in above the line and what closed below it, in columns of one, three or six hours or a day as the stretch grows.
- *Where the open work sits*: each group with a small line over the stretch, its open cards and points, the cards that left it (closed or moved on), that rate a day and the days it takes to empty.
- **Hide the burndown** folds it to one line; the device remembers, as the Shortlist does.

## How it is built

Nothing is recorded and nothing generated is committed. The history is the folder's own:

- `scripts/todos/readTodoHistory.ts` replays every commit on main that changed `to-dos/`: one `git log --first-parent --raw` for the changed paths, one `git cat-file --batch` for the to-do files it names (each parsed once, by blob id, with the board's own `readTodoDoc`), and the boards go to `historyFromStates`. About six seconds for sixteen hundred commits. It starts at the first commit where every to-do parses (2026-09-22, when the cards got a stage and a size) and never throws: no git or a one-commit clone gives an empty or short history.
- `todoHistoryPlugin` (vite.config.ts) loads it in place of `src/lib/todos/punchListHistoryData.ts`, packed to 22 numbers a change (`packHistory` / `unpackHistory`), in the Punch list page's own chunk. That file holds an empty history for TypeScript and the tests. It is a real file and not a virtual module because a virtual module needs a declaration in `src/vite-env.d.ts`, a change there makes CI re-check every file, and that full check ran out of the runner's 10 GB twice on this PR.
- `src/lib/todos/punchListHistory.ts` holds the rules (the file's header lists them): points by size (XS 1, S 2, M 4, L 8, XL 16, unsized 4, read by `sizeBucket`), the board at each hour's end, a pace over whole hours only, the two projections, the wave columns on the app calendar, and a stage draining by every card that left it.
- `PunchListBurndown.tsx` draws it with recharts; the cards open now are the board's own (`virtual:punch-list`).
- `deploy.yml` checks out the whole history (`fetch-depth: 0`) so the deployed build has it. A build without it still passes and the section says *This build read only the newest commit*.
