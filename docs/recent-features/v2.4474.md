# v2.4474 — Paid-job email and sample agreement: the evening keeps its day (2026-10-03)

One of the instant-day fixes that follow [v2.4452](v2.4452.md). Migration [`20261003150500_paid_job_email_months_in_app_tz`](../migrations/20261003150500_paid_job_email_months_in_app_tz.md), pushed after merge; `get-job-contract` is redeployed after merge.

## What was wrong

- **The paid-job email's monthly table**: `get_paid_job_email_payload` bucketed its `timeline` with `date_trunc('month', …)` on instants in a UTC session: labor by `clock_sessions.clocked_in_at`, parts by `COALESCE(posted_at, allocation created_at)`, payments by `COALESCE(paid_on::timestamptz, created_at)`. A session, a card charge or an undated payment after 7 pm CDT (6 pm CST) on a month's last day landed in the next month. The email draws this table only when no cost or payment event carries a day ([`_shared/paidJobEmail.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/paidJobEmail.ts), `renderTimeline`); the events' own date keys already read Chicago.
- **The sample agreement** ([`get-job-contract`](../../supabase/functions/get-job-contract/index.ts)), on Settings → What customers see: its wording date was the Contract Book document's `book_version_date`, else its `updated_at` cut to the UTC date, where the Book itself reads the last edit's day in Central ([`contractBookVersionDate.ts`](../../src/lib/contractBookVersionDate.ts), `effectiveBookVersionPlainDate`).

## What changed

The three month expressions read the instants `AT TIME ZONE 'America/Chicago'`, as the payload's date keys do; `paid_on` is a `date` and keeps its own month (`::timestamp`). The body is the newest definition with only those expressions changed (see the migration's doc). `get-job-contract` reads `updated_at` as `todayYmdInAppTz(new Date(iso))`, guarded because it throws on an Invalid Date.

## Not changed

Real agreements store `template_version_date`, a `date`, from the Book's `book_version_date` when they are drafted; only the sample falls back to `updated_at`.

## Tests

- Migration: a local bed of the whole schema (every migration applied in order, this one twice) runs `get_paid_job_email_payload` on a job with a session, a card charge and an undated payment at 7:30 pm CDT on Sep 30 and a payment dated Oct 1. The table reads labor $60, parts $100 and payments $300 in 2026-09 and $200 in 2026-10; without the migration all of it reads 2026-10.
- `get-job-contract` has no test harness; an esbuild pass checks its syntax. `scripts/check-migrations.sh` passes.

## Checked against prod

Read only, from the worktree dev server: `get_paid_job_email_payload` is service-role only, so its inputs were read instead. None of the 2,129 approved job sessions and none of the undated payments falls on a month's last evening. 25 of the 2,354 card-charge allocations do, but a card charge carries a day, so an email with one draws the dated timeline, not this table. The sample's Book document has its own version date, so its date does not change.
