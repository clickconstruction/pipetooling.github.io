# v2.5688 — Leave Report from the job window (2026-10-20)

Punch list #135 (the plan and mock-up in `to-dos/job-window-leave-report/`; the owner, with a screenshot of the window on Kane 1043: "add to this view a button to leave a report", then on the first draft, "it should blend in better"). The window's Job tab read reports well — the *Reports* pill, the *crew report* chip on the percent — and wrote them badly: the *Reports* box in the date band, then the reports list, then **Add additional report**. No migration; no edge function.

- **The chip** ([`JobThreadNotesPanel`](../../src/components/JobThreadNotesPanel.tsx), the `onLeaveReport` prop): **＋ Report** right after the *Reports* pill in the filter row, in the pills' own height, radius and weight, blue words with a plus so it reads as an act, its hairline a shade bluer — the house's *+ bid note* idiom beside *All | Bid | Customer*. Its accessible name and hover word are *Leave Report*, the Dashboard's label. The row reads *Reports · ＋ Report*, and *Reports 3 · ＋ Report* on a job with reports; the empty pill's quiet text now has its "here is how" beside it. Without the prop (the Pipeline's expanded panel, Quickfill, Job Mode) the row is as it was.
- **The window** ([`DetailJobModal`](../../src/components/jobs/DetailJobModal.tsx)): gives the prop to whoever may leave a job report (`canLeaveJobFieldReport`, the Dashboard's gate), mounts [`AdditionalReportModal`](../../src/components/AdditionalReportModal.tsx) over the pane beside the reports list it already mounts (`leaveReportOpen`, in `detailEscBlocked` so Escape never closes the window underneath the form), and on save reloads the feed (`threadNotes.reload()`: the report on top, the *Reports* pill's count) and the job (`loadDetail()`: the percent readout and its *crew report* chip, the date band's box). On a job with two or more priced lines the form asks how far along each line is (v2.5663).
- **Tests**: `JobThreadNotesPanel.leaveReport.render.test.tsx` (the chip right after Reports and before Status, the press, the readout still ending the row; no chip without the prop), `DetailJobModalLeaveReport.render.test.tsx` (the chip opens the form titled with the job's number and name, Escape leaves the window alone while the form is up, the form's Close returns to the window; no chip for a viewer with no role).
- **Docs**: the guide *file and review field reports* gains *From a job's window*; `JOBS_MODALS_ARCHITECTURE.md`'s Job Detail row; this fragment. The to-do's status and its redrawn mock-up follow in a docs PR once #5924 lands.
