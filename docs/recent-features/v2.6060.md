# v2.6060 — Dashboard and Messages: what waited while you were off, who is off on a thread, and where the office's hours leave nobody (2026-10-29)

Punch list #141 PR 4 of 5 (`to-dos/quiet-hours/README.md` → The plan → PR 4, decisions 3 and 5; the owner's yes of 2026-10-21). PR 3 (v2.6029) made the hold real: `messages-notify` writes the push it would have sent to someone outside their **Push me** hours as a `notification_history` row with `channel = 'held'`, and the office hears at once when the holder is off. Nothing read those rows back, and nothing on a screen said who was off. Now three surfaces do, each through the kernel the person's hours already use (`officeOpen`, `nextOpening`, `lastClosing`, `backAt`, `backWords`). One migration, no function: nothing mirrored changed, so `messages-notify` stays as deployed.

- **The Dashboard's line** ([`whatWaited.ts`](../../src/lib/messages/whatWaited.ts), pure): `waitedSince` is the hours' last close before now, whether the person is back or still off; `whatWaited` reads the person's own held rows since then, one wait per customer's line however many pushes it would have been (the push at once and the push at 50 minutes are one reply), leaves out a line someone answered meanwhile by `answeredBefore`'s rule (a text, an email or a call from us, never a note or the app's own text) and names the answer, *Evan answered Dana at 11:50 PM*, counts a line the read did not find without naming it, and says the rest: *While you were off: 3 replies and a work order waited*, *Since 8 PM.*, in the present while the person is still off. The reads ([`whatWaitedIo.ts`](../../src/lib/messages/whatWaitedIo.ts)): the hours through `pushHoursIo.ts`, the held rows (the own-row policy), the lines with the person's name, what went out after them with who sent it, each fail-soft, so the line is never wrong, only absent. [`useWhileOff`](../../src/hooks/useWhileOff.ts) loads them on demand like `useTodaysSends`; the Needs you key `while-off` sits at the Customers line's tier, is the viewer's own (`needsYouActor.ts`), is read for everyone who signs in on both hosts (`needsYouSources.ts` → `whileOff`), and its door (`needsYouDoors.ts`) opens the first line still waiting at the line, Messages when none was read. The card's decision 5: a line when the app is opened, never a catch-up push. A tech's focused Job Mode Dashboard hides every Needs you line by design (v2.917) and draws them on Job Mode's **Inbox** tab instead, so a tech reads this one there, and under **Show full dashboard**.

## Is this the best we can do?

1. **Reuse**: the three surfaces read the hours through `officeOpen`, `nextOpening`, `lastClosing`, `backAt` and `backWords`, the words the office card and the reply push already say; the Dashboard line is one more Needs you source in the one builder, with the one door table; the pill is the pill the thread has, with a suffix and a hover.
2. **Honest**: a reply answered meanwhile is not counted and the line says who answered it; a line the read could not see is still counted; the coverage line draws only when every member shares the gap, and names them; the pill reads as before wherever the hours cannot be read.
3. **Cut**: no catch-up push, no cron, no function; the gray event line the mock-up sketched in the thread (*Rosa is off until 6 AM. Evan and Taunya were pushed at once.*) is not drawn, since the office's push and the pill already say it.
4. **Fail-soft**: every read answers nothing on a failure, and nothing on a screen is wrong for it, only absent.
