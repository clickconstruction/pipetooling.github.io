# v2.8073 — Boxes: Use my location on the Tally's maps no longer pins a box from a rough area, and a map with nowhere to open can still be dragged (2026-11-20)

Punch list #402, found by the audit of the card. It follows v2.8011, which gave the box map's **Use my location** the Precise Location line on one of its three sheets. Client only: no migration and no function.

## The fault, in two halves

**A. The Tally's own map sheets were left behind.** **Use my location** is one press name on three sheets: **Move the pin by hand** inside **Where is it?** (`WhereIsBox`), the Tally's *Pin A7 on the map* sheet, and the Tally's *Site map* sheet (both in `TallyOnSiteTab`). v2.8011 fixed the first. On the other two a phone with Precise Location off handed a 3,200 m reading, the map went to the middle of that area at the map's close zoom, and the sheet said nothing. **Put A7 here** was live. A press wrote the box's pin there with `pin_accuracy_m` 3200, so the box then read *Pinned today by …, within 10,500 ft*.

**B. A map with nowhere to open was a dead end after a rough reading.** This one v2.8011 brought in. The box has no pin, no other box on the job is pinned, the job has no painted map and its address did not geocode. **Move the pin by hand** then draws the empty frame, which says to press **Use my location**. Since v2.8011 a rough reading no longer opened the map, and the line read *The map was not moved. Drag it until the ring is on the spot…* over a frame with no map in it. **Put A7 here** stayed dead. A refusal and a failed reading said *Drag the map by hand* over the same empty frame. The Tally's two sheets had the same hole for a refusal.

## What changed

One rule for the three sheets, the one a task's place sheet (`TaskPlaceSheet`) already kept.

- **A rough reading never moves the map and is never the reading a pin's margin comes from.** `boxMapReading` sorts a reading into a spot, which is flown to and kept, or an area, of which only the margin is kept, for the words.
- **A map with nowhere to open opens wide** when the press gets an area, a refusal or nothing. It opens on the task sheet's own wide start and zoom (`TASK_PLACE_WIDE_START`, `TASK_PLACE_WIDE_ZOOM`), so there is one wide start in the app.
- **The pin press waits for a drag.** The wide view's own centre is nobody's place, so **Put A7 here** stays dead until the ring has left it (`boxPinReady`, over `isTaskPlaceWideStart`).
- **The sentence is true in the wide case.** *The map was not moved.* is not true of a map that has just opened. That case now reads *The map opened wide. Zoom in and drag it until the ring is on the spot, or turn the switch on and press again.* A task's place sheet said *was not moved* in the same case, and now says the same new sentence.

| Sheet | A spot | An area | An area, a refusal or nothing, with nowhere to open |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Move the pin by hand** (`WhereIsBox`) | As before: the map goes there, and the line says how sure. | As before: the map stays, and the line shows the steps. | The map opens wide. The pin press waits for a drag. |
| The Tally's *Pin A7 on the map* | The map goes there. New: the line says *Precise Location is on.*, or asks for the ring on a weak signal, while the ring sits on the reading. | New: the map stays, the line shows the steps, and no margin is kept. | The map opens wide. The pin press waits for a drag. |
| The Tally's *Site map* | The map goes there. No line. | New: the map stays, and the line shows the steps. | The map opens wide. |

Two things differ on the Tally's sheets, both on purpose.

- **The steps wait behind Show the steps** (`foldSteps`). Those sheets fill the screen and the map takes the room the footer leaves, which is what squeezed the task sheet's map in v2.8011.
- **The Site map draws the line for a rough reading alone.** It has no ring, so nothing says when the view has left the reading, and a line reading *Precise Location is on.* would stay after a drag. Its sentences name no ring either: *The map was not moved. Turn the switch on and press again.*, and *The map opened wide. Zoom in and drag it to the site.*

## In the code

- `src/lib/materials/boxMapStart.ts`, new and pure: `onSiteMapStart` is where the box map opens, lifted out of `OnSiteMap`, which now reads it. A sheet asks the same rule whether the map would draw its empty frame. `boxMapOpening` hands the map its centre and zoom, `boxMapReading` sorts a reading, and `boxPinReady` is the pin press.
- `src/lib/tasks/taskPlaceReading.ts`: `WHERE_I_AM_WIDE_WORDS`, `whereIAmRoughWideNoteWords`, and the Site map's `siteMapRoughNoteWords`, `SITE_MAP_WIDE_WORDS` and `siteMapRoughWideNoteWords`.
- `WhereIsBox`, `TallyOnSiteTab` and `TaskPlaceSheet` read them. `taskPlace.ts`, which is copied into the dev seat's function, is read and not changed.

## Checked

- `TallyOnSiteTab.render.test.tsx`, six new cases. On the pin sheet a 3,200 m reading leaves the map where it was, draws the line with the steps folded, and **Put A2 here** writes the ring's own point with no margin. A sure and a weak reading draw the line until a drag. With nowhere to open, a rough reading and a refusal each open the map wide with the press dead until a drag. The Site map does not move on a rough reading and opens wide with nowhere to open.
- `WhereIsBox.render.test.tsx`, four new cases with a map that has nowhere to open: a rough reading, a refusal and a failed reading each leave a wide map to drag with **Put A7 here** dead until the drag, and a sure reading still opens the map on itself.
- `boxMapStart.test.ts` (10) and the new sentences in `taskPlaceReading.test.ts`, held to the plain words rules. `TaskPlaceRow.render.test.tsx` now holds the task sheet's sentence in the wide case.
- Before the fix nine of the new render cases failed. After it all pass. Break-it: three changes run together, an area flown to on the Tally and the pin press live on the wide start on each sheet, failed eight of the new cases.
- The guides `keep-track-of-the-material-in-the-cages-and-boxes-on-site` and `give-someone-a-task`, and the Glossary's *Precise Location* entry.

## Not done, and known

- Not walked in a browser and not read on a real phone. The render tests stand in for the map.
- A map that opened wide starts on the whole country, as a task's does. Finding a site from there takes a good deal of zooming. A person with the switch on, or a job whose address is on the map, never meets it.
- The empty frame still waits for a press. A task's sheet opens wide by itself after a wait, and the box sheets do not.
