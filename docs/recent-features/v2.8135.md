# v2.8135 — Document builder: Make this the usual keeps a job's name out of the usual line under the title (2026-11-20)

Punch list #404, one of the faults the review of 2026-11-20 found and did not fix. No migration and no function.

## What was wrong

The line under the title is the one field the usual and a job do not hold alike. The usual holds words, *For subcontractors on*, and each job's page adds its own name after them. A job that changes the line holds the whole line, name and all.

**Make this the usual** copied the job's whole line onto the usual (`usualFromJobField`, `sheet.ts`). The usual then held one job's name, and every job's page still added its own name after it. Every other job read two names. The job that wrote the line read its own name twice once the usual was published and its copy was let go.

Three smaller things came from the same cause. A job's line was held against the usual's bare words, so the list of what differs showed *Your usual: For subcontractors on* with no name. A line typed back to what the usual reads was still kept as the job's own. And the row went on offering the press after the usual already read the same.

## The fix

The usual never holds a job's name. It holds a blank, and each job fills it.

- `subtitleAsUsual(line, jobName)` turns a job's line into the usual's: the job's name becomes the blank. A line that only ends on the name is kept as the words before it, as the usual has always been. A name anywhere else leaves the blank written out as `[the job's name]` (`SHEET_JOB_BLANK`).
- `subtitleWith(usualLine, jobName)` reads the usual's line on one job: the name goes in the blank, or at the end of a line with no blank. So the usual on prod, which has no blank, reads as it did.
- `usualWordsOn(def, job, path)` is what the usual says on one job. A job's own words are now held against it in `withJobWords`, `pruneJobSheet`, `sheetDiffers` and `layOver`.
- `usualOffer(def, job, path)` says what the row offers: the press, *In your usual now*, or a hold with its reason.
- **A line that does not say the job's name is held.** No other job could put its own name in it, so it stays that job's own and the row says so. The usual is left as it was.

`SheetPaper.tsx` shows the usual's line with its blank in its own words when the line writes one out, and with the blue *the job's name* after it when it does not.

`FilledBuilder.tsx` belongs to another session tonight. This PR changes three lines of it: the row reads `usualOffer` where it compared the usual's bare words.

## Tests

`sheet.test.ts`, *the line under the title, made the usual*, six tests with two made-up jobs. The first fails on the old code: one job's line made the usual, and the other job read both names.

## Not walked on prod

The dev server writes to prod, and **Make this the usual** saves the usual's draft. The usual sheet on prod is to be left exactly as it is, so this press was not walked there. The change is in the pure kernel and its tests hold it.
