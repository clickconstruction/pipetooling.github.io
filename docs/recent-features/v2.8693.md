# v2.8693 — A development's page: Secrecy on this site (2026-11-27)

Punch list #439, piece 4, its first door (4b). Client only: no migration, no edge function. Its ground is the migration `20261127052337_site_secrecy.sql`, a PR of its own (piece 4a), which this change does not wait for: until that file is pushed the block draws nothing.

## What was there

Papers before a job opens (punch list #389) are set one job at a time, on the job's Documents tab. A development carries many jobs, and nothing could ask for one paper on all of them. Piece 4a adds the ground in the database: the table `site_secrecy`, three presses, and a rule that one signature a person covers every job of a site. No screen called them.

## What changed

- **The block.** A development's page has **Secrecy on this site**, under **Papers for this site**. Off, it reads *Off* with two sentences on what turning it on does, and the press **Turn on secrecy for this site**.
- **The Turn on panel.** The press opens a panel in place, with no window. It lists the Contract Book's papers under **The paper our own people sign**. A paper with no words is grey with its reason. A paper built from settings is left out, since its settings are set a job at a time. Under **What happens when you press** it counts the jobs that shut and the people asked, read from `site_secrecy_preview` before anything is written. The last press names its count, **Turn it on and ask 12 people to sign**, and is dead until a paper is picked and the count is read. **Not yet** closes the panel and writes nothing.
- **On.** The block reads *On since Nov 27*, the paper, how many jobs carry it and how many people have signed. **Turn it off…** opens a confirm that says what opens and what is kept, with **Turn secrecy off** and **Keep it on**.
- **Who.** The four office roles press. The estimator reads both states and has no press. Anyone else sees nothing, and nothing is read for them.
- **A job's roster.** A site's paper has no **Take off** on a job. Its row says *The site's paper. It comes off when secrecy is turned off for the site.* A sub who is not asked the paper reads *not asked* on a plain chip, owes nothing, and gets no signing link. The job's count of who has signed counts that sub as signed.

## Before the migration is pushed

- The roster reads two new words, `site_secrecy_id` and `asked`. An older roster has neither. Every paper then reads as the job's own and everyone as asked, which is what is true before the push.

## How it is built

- `src/lib/addresses/siteSecrecy.ts`, pure: who presses and who reads (`canTurnSecrecy`, `canReadSecrecy`), the row that is on (`liveSecrecy`), the missing-table reading (`secrecyNotThereYet`), the papers offered (`secrecyPapers`, over `pickablePapers`), and every word: `SITE_SECRECY_WORDS`, `turnOnButton`, `previewValues`, `onSinceSentence`, `onCountSentences`, `turnOffConfirm`, the two toasts. A site is `{ developmentId }` or `{ address }`, so the same kernel serves a lone job's tab and Send a paper.
- `src/lib/addresses/siteSecrecyIo.ts`: one interface, `readLive`, `readCounts`, `preview`, `readBook`, `turnOn`, `turnOff`, and its implementation on the app's client. The names are cast until the types are made again after the push.
- `src/components/jobs/SiteSecrecy.tsx`: the block, drawn with `ValueLines` and `ValueSentence`. It takes the site, the role and the io.
- `src/pages/DevelopmentPage.tsx` mounts it with the development's id.
- `src/lib/jobs/jobPapers.ts`: `JobRequiredPaper.siteSecrecyId`, `JobPaperCell.asked`, `owedPapers` and `cellWords` read them, `NOT_ASKED_LABEL`, `SITE_PAPER_WORDS`.

## Design calls

Each is open to a flip.

- **A panel in place, not a window.** The same panel can sit inside Send a paper, which is a window already, and a phone has no second layer to lose.
- **The opening press is a link, the last press is blue.** Most developments never turn secrecy on, so the block stays quiet until someone means it.
- **No code name in the panel.** The database keeps one, and nothing reads it until piece 7. A box that does nothing yet would ask the office a question with no effect.
- **The paper is picked every time, never guessed.** With one paper in the Book it is still a press.
- **The count of the signed is a help.** When it cannot be read its line is left out. The jobs' count is the block's own and its failure is not hidden.
- **The estimator sees the off state too**, with *The office turns secrecy on and off.*
- **A sub's chip is plain, not amber.** Amber says owed, and a sub who is not asked owes nothing.
