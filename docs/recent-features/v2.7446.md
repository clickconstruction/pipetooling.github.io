# v2.7446 — Lien desk: an open notice fits its pane in a small window (2026-11-16)

The owner's ask of 2026-11-16, after a read-only walk of the desk at v2.7443: "An open notice fits a 1,024 px window." In a 1,024 by 768 window, a drafting notice on By paper → Monthly notices was 707 px of content in a 656 px pane. The whole pane slid sideways by 51 px, and the Steps | Windows switch, each section head's fact, the supply houses card's *Owed* column and the timeline's last stop were cut at the right. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## The cause

One cause carried every cut. The pane (`[data-lien-desk-pane]`) is a one-column grid, and on a computer its column was `auto`. An `auto` column is as wide as its widest item's min-content, and one item is a single line that never wraps: the pay codes page's bar, *Page 3 of 3 · pay codes · 1 bill · … still owed · a shaded line is yours to change, on this page only* with **Open ↗** and **PDF ↓**. Its button has `min-width: 0` and `overflow: hidden`, which let it shrink inside a fixed width but do not lower what the bar asks of an `auto` track. So the bar set the column to 671 px (707 with the pane's gutters) and every section was laid out at that width. A notice with no pay page asked for 550 to 574 px and fit a 1,024 px window, which is why only some notices slid. A phone has had the column pinned since punch list #352, PR 6.

Measured before, on the same drafting notice (pane width → content width): 1,440 px window 1,072 → 1,072; 1,280 → 912 / 912; 1,124 → 756 / 756; 1,068 → 700 / 707; 1,024 → 656 / 707; 900 → 532 / 707; 768 → 400 / 707; 660 → 292 / 707.

## What changed

- **The pane's column is held to the pane on every screen.** [`LienDesk.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDesk.tsx), [`LienDeskAffidavitPane.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskAffidavitPane.tsx) and [`LienDeskRetainagePane.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskRetainagePane.tsx): `gridTemplateColumns: 'minmax(0, 1fr)'` was the phone's alone and is now unconditional. [`src/index.css`](../../src/index.css): the rule that holds each section body (`[data-lien-pane-body]`) to its column lost its `[data-lien-pane-phone]` condition, and `.lienDeskFooterSlot` got the same one held column.
- **A page bar is one line in a pane of any width.** `pageLabel` takes its muted words as a string. They are `.lienPageLabelMore`, which shrinks first and ends in an ellipsis, with the whole line in its `title`. The page's name is `.lienPageLabelName` and shrinks only after that. The two export presses never shrink.
- **A head's fact keeps its whole words.** [`lienPaneSections.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienPaneSections.ts) `lienPaneHeadFactWords(section)` joins the fact and its tail, and [`LienPaneHead.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienPaneHead.tsx) puts it in the fact's `title`. The fact already ended in an ellipsis; it had no tip.
- **The supply houses card picks its shape by its own width.** [`lienSupplierCardShape.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienSupplierCardShape.ts) `lienSuppliersStacked(isMobile, cardWidth)`: under 600 px of card the five-column table's money columns ran into each other (one five-figure amount drawn over the next column, in a tablet's 400 px pane), so the card draws the stacked rows a phone has always had. [`LienJobSuppliers.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienJobSuppliers.tsx) reads the card's width before paint and on resize; an unmeasured card keeps the table. The card says which it drew in `data-lien-suppliers-shape`. Its foot row of presses wraps.
- **Months on this job stacks its claim over its notes in a narrow card.** `.lienMonthsBody` had its columns inline in [`LienDeskMonths.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskMonths.tsx), which beat the 520 px container rule that was written to stack them. The columns are in the CSS now, so the rule wins. The notes had been squeezed to a sliver and ran 21 px out of the card.
- **The approved chip wraps.** `.lienFootChip` was one unbreakable line, 421 px on an approved and signed notice, in a row of an `auto` grid. It is one line while the row has room and wraps whole words when it has not, with a 12 px corner that rounds a one-line chip as the pill did. A tablet has no pointer to read a cut chip's tip with.
- **The job heading wraps.** [`LienJobNumber.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienJobNumber.tsx) `LienJobHeading` was one unshrinking line. A long job name ran 97 px out of a 292 px pane.
- **A page of the paper slides inside its own frame.** The notice's, the affidavit's and the retainage notice's paper frames take `overflow-x: auto`. A page cannot narrow past its letterhead, which needs about 4 px more than the narrowest two-column pane gives it.

## Measured after

Same notice, same windows, on the dev server against the live data, read only. Pane width and content width are equal at every one: 1,920 px window 1,552; 1,280 → 912; 1,124 → 756; 1,068 → 700; 1,024 → 656; 900 → 532; 768 → 400; 660 → 292. No element's right edge passes the pane's, and nothing is cut without an ellipsis and a tip.

Six notices were opened by address: a draft with a blocked gate, one supply house owed and a pay page; a draft with a long job name and three houses on a job account; one awaiting approval; an approved one with a long name; an approved and signed one with a pay page; and a missed one. Each was read at 1,024, 1,068 (a 700 px pane), 900, 768 and 660 px, with the signing section in the pane. All fit. A phone at 375 px: 359 of 359, one column, the months body stacked.

The affidavit and retainage panes had no slide at 1,024 px before or after. Their heads asked for 433 and 471 px, so they would have slid under about an 870 px window; at 768 and 660 px they now measure equal.

Inside their own frames, and by design: the months grid slides from a 400 px pane down (339 of 356 px), and each page of the paper slides in a 292 px pane (255 of 277 px).

## Not changed, and what was seen

- The timeline's row of stops gives each stop an equal column and cuts a stop's name and date with an ellipsis, with the whole words in the stop's tip. In a 656 px pane a stop is 69 px wide, so *Feb 15, 2027* reads *Feb 15, 2…*. That is the strip's own rule (it becomes a list under 520 px) and it was already so on every notice with no pay page.
- By date at 1,024 px: the page does not slide. Its calendar slides inside its own frame (976 of 1,407 px), a different thing from this cause. To do and By job drew nothing past the window.
- The stacked layout (`layout="stacked"`) has no host in the app today, so it was not opened. It takes the one-column path, which now holds its column as the phone's does.

## Tests

The fix is CSS and inline style, which a jsdom render cannot measure, so the proof is the browser measurement above. Two kernels are held by unit tests: `lienSupplierCardShape.test.ts` (a phone stacks, a computer stacks under 600 px, an unmeasured card keeps the table) and `lienPaneSections.test.ts` (a head's whole words, with and without a tail).
