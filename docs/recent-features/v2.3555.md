# v2.3555 — Estimate options, approve more than one — PR 2: the customer page (2026-09-17)

PR 2 of three (`to-dos/estimate-options-approve-several/`; PR 1 = v2.3554, the kernels, the column and the four functions). Client only. Ships dark in practice: no estimate carries an add-on until the office builder (PR 3) can make one, and a pure-choice estimate renders exactly as it did.

## What the customer sees

- [`EstimateOptionsPicker.tsx`](../../src/components/estimates/EstimateOptionsPicker.tsx): two groups. **Choose one** — the choices as radio cards (`role="radio"` inside a `radiogroup`), exactly one selected, the ★ pre-selected; a pure-choice estimate keeps the v2.2457 heading *Choose your option*. **Add to it · tick any** — the add-ons as checkbox cards (`role="checkbox"` inside a `group`), none pre-ticked, a blue *Add-on* badge and a `+ $` price. An all-add-on estimate has one group, *Pick what you want done · tick any*, priced plainly. Props are `selectedKeys` + `onToggle(key)`; the parent applies the kernel's `toggleEstimateOptionSelection` (a choice replaces the other choice, an add-on toggles).
- [`EstimateAcceptBody.tsx`](../../src/components/estimates/EstimateAcceptBody.tsx) reads one view of the selection from the new [`estimateAcceptSelection.ts`](../../src/lib/estimates/estimateAcceptSelection.ts) (+9 tests): the total card's caption (*Replace 50-gal + 2 add-ons · Total*), every Approve label (button, sticky bar, confirm title — *Approve "Replace 50-gal" + 2 add-ons — $5,740.00*), the document heading (*Your selection — Replace 50-gal · Water softener · Hose bibs (2)*) and the line groups. With nothing valid selected the three Approve doors are disabled and read *Choose an option* / *Choose at least one option*.
- [`EstimateCustomerDocument.tsx`](../../src/components/estimates/EstimateCustomerDocument.tsx) gains `lineItemGroups`: one group per selected option with a small sub-heading (*Add-on — Water softener*); a single group draws no sub-heading, so today's one-option document is unchanged. The frozen `line_items_snapshot` stays a flat list — grouping is a display of the live selection only.
- [`EstimateAccept.tsx`](../../src/pages/EstimateAccept.tsx): the selection is a set seeded by `defaultEstimateSelection` (the ★ choice, no add-ons). The accept POST carries **both** `optionKeys` (the whole selection) and `optionKey` (the choice, or the one key), so the page works whichever of the client and `accept-estimate` deployed first. The *Viewed option* activity row fires when a card turns on, not when an add-on is un-ticked. The client-side refusal uses the kernel's words (*Please choose an option first.* / *Please tick at least one option first.*).
- The staff **Preview as customer** page and the **Customer experience → Page** preview in the builder hold the same set (the builder's `previewSelectedOptionKeys`), so the office rehearses the real thing; the builder cannot yet mark an add-on — PR 3.

## Tests

`EstimateOptionsPicker.render.test.tsx`: a mixed estimate renders the two groups with the right roles, tapping an add-on reports its key, the add-on price wears the `+`; an all-add-on estimate renders one group and no `+`; a pure-choice estimate keeps *Choose your option*. `estimateAcceptSelection.test.ts` pins the labels, the disabled states and the groups.
