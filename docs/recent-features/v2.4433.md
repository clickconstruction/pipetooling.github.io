# v2.4433 — Procurement log: one part sent back no longer marks its whole fixture (2026-10-02)

No migration, no edge function. The owner, 2026-10-02, with a screenshot of BP375's procurement log: "only one item was selected, but all parts of the wc got marked as rejected". WC-1, WC-2's five lines all read *Rejected by the GC* after a Rejected was entered on the flush valve alone.

## What was wrong

The data was right. Read from the running app: of WC-1, WC-2's five parts only *TOTO TET2UB31#SS* held `review_decision = 'rejected'` (entered 2026-10-02 19:11 UTC); the other four held none. The row read Rejected, as designed: since v2.4322 a row's call is the roll-up of its parts, and one part sent back sends the row back.

The log's reader, `procurementItemsFrom`, gave a part its own call, *else the row's*. That fallback was written for a row called whole. With a rolled-up row it painted the neighbour's rejection onto every part nobody had called, and onto the order-only parts.

## What changed

- **One rule, a kernel** — `partLineDecision` in [`procurementLog.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/procurementLog.ts). A part's line reads its own call. A row with no call on any part was called whole, so its call still covers every part. Once any part has a call of its own, the row's call says nothing about a part nobody called: that line stays open (*waiting on the GC*). Only an approved roll-up reaches further, to release the order-only parts with their fixture.
- [`procurementLogIo.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/procurementLogIo.ts) calls it for each part's line. The log, its printed sheet, the counts and the GC's update all read those lines, so all follow.
- [`GLOSSARY.md`](../GLOSSARY.md) says what a part's line reads.

## Decided here

- **An order-only part on a row sent back reads as waiting, not rejected.** Nobody rejected the angle stop. It is still not released, because its fixture is not approved.
- **The row keeps reading Rejected.** The roll-up is what the rows table, the room and a resubmit read; only the log's per-part lines change.

## Tests

- `procurementLogIo.test.ts` (new, 3): BP375's WC shape through the reader and the log (one line *sent back*, four *awaiting*); a row called whole; every part approved releases the order-only part.
