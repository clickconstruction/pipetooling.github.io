## What the first real bid taught

Walked on localhost against prod, read-only, on an open bid whose address carries ten permits. Three things changed before the PR:

- One permit can sit in both feeds; it is counted once.
- Its general plumbing permit was filed under the building permit's own contact. The line now reads *under the building permit's own name*, and the GC's panel marks *Plumbing permit · another name* only when the name differs.
- Ten rows is a long list; the first five show and the rest fold behind a press.
