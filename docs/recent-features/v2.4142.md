## Design calls

- **Roles are chips, not strips.** Draft 1 stacked seven role strips and every digest appeared three times; a reader has one day, so the tab shows one week at a time and each email once, at its hour.
- **The four template emails read the live rows**, the way What customers see reads Settings — an edit on Email templates shows on the next refresh. Their defaults are the senders' strings, checked by a source scan rather than copied by hand.
- **Real previews are honest about their data**: *for your eyes only, over live rows* — they are not sample data, and the row says so. A digest joins the *renders live* set when its builder is lifted into a kernel (the train's next PRs).
- **The client From line is a literal for now**; it joins the shared fallback address (`_shared/emailFromAddress.ts`, v2.4138) once that PR is on main.
