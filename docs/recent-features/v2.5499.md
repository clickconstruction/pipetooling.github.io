# v2.5499 — Messages: a tech’s text from Job Mode goes out through the company number, behind the scenes (2026-10-13)

Residential R5, `quo-send`'s tech path (`to-dos/gc-mode/messages/RESIDENTIAL_BUILD_PLAN.md` → R5 on `spike/gc-mode`), by Customer communication helper 10, directed by cc. It is stacked on #5642 (`quo-send` by box) and armed after the GC lead's switch PR (#5646) lands. Migration `20261014180000_messages_tech_send` (`docs/migrations/20261014180000_messages_tech_send.md`) is pushed first, then `quo-send` is deployed. Job Mode's door stays off (`JOB_MODE_MESSAGES_ON = false`); turning it on is a one-line PR after the deploy.

- **The doors** (the migration, no table):
  - `messages_tech_on_job` is the one home of the span rule: dispatched to or clocked on the job, this week or last, in Chicago. `messages_tech_can_read` is recreated on it, with the same answers.
  - `contact_for_job` is the tech's door. It finds or makes the job's customer's own contact.
  - `contact_find_or_make` holds the body `contact_for_customer` had, and both doors call it. Nobody signed in may run it.
- **`quo-send`'s tech path** (`supabase/functions/_shared/quoTechSend.ts`). A body with `from: 'job_mode'` takes it before the office check.
  - **Who may**: a tech on the job, or the office team, so the owner working a job is not turned away.
  - **The contact**: the sheet's when it is the job's customer's, else `contact_for_job` (`contact_for_customer` for the office), asked as the caller. A sheet's contact that is not the customer's is logged.
  - **The text** is made on the server from the phone's line and the record, with the sheet's own kernel. The client sends only `{ quickReplyId, fills }`, the blanks it typed, and any body is never read.
    - A typed blank holds 40 characters at most, on one line, and fills only a blank the record cannot.
    - A line not on the phone, or one needing `{amount}` or `{link}`, is refused, and so is a blank left open.
    - The line goes in the customer's language behind `PORTAL_SPANISH_ON`.
  - **The number** is the one they last texted from, else their first phone. The box, the STOP, the opt-out line, the pause and Settings' Send texts switch are the reply box's (`sendText`).
  - **After it goes**: the line is tied to the job and keeps `quick_reply_id`. For a tech, the customer's answer is held for them until their hour (`techHoldUntil`, `contacts.has_*`).
- **Kernels**:
  - `src/lib/quo/techSend.ts` (new): `parseTechSendBody`, `composeTechText`, `pickPhone` and `TECH_SEND_WORDS`.
  - Three moves so the edge's copy can hold them:
    - The sheet's fills and preview move to `src/lib/jobModeText.ts`, re-exported from `jobModeMessageCustomer.ts`.
    - `wordsFor` moves to `src/lib/quickReplies/language.ts`, re-exported from `phone.ts`.
    - `customerLang.ts` takes its contact's `lang` by shape.
  - `hasRule`'s `techHoldUntil` joins the edge's exports.
- **The sheet** (`jobMode/MessageCustomerSheet.tsx`, still behind the switch): Send passes the typed blanks instead of the words. The preview reads the customer's language, so what the tech sees is what the server sends.
- **Docs**: `quo-send`'s section in `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` is amended in place. `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` gains the tech's send.
- **Tests**:
  - `techSend.test.ts`: the request, the text from the record and the typed minutes in English and Spanish, typed words never standing in for the record, an open blank, lines off the phone, and the number.
  - The sheet's smoke checks the typed blanks.
  - A new SQL bed, `messages-tech-send`, covers who is on a job, the doors' refusals in words, one contact made once, the office's door, and the closed body. `messages-spine`'s tech door step checks the recreated `messages_tech_can_read`.
