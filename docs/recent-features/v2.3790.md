# v2.3790 — Asks and answers: the office asks the law firm, counsel signs off on a job (2026-09-24)

Punch list **#41**, PR 3 of 3 (PR 1 v2.3787, PR 2 v2.3789). No migration — both entry kinds were in `legal_matter_entries`' CHECK since v2.3313; the `submit-legal-portal` function redeploys.

## Why

The matter's stream ran one way: the firm asked (`question`, via the portal) and the office answered from the desk. Counsel's memo names two moments that need the other direction — *"take counsel's sign-off on that job before you accept the check"* (an owner paying Click direct while the GC is silent) and the office's one question back about the § 53.057 form, which sat in `owner-decisions-pending.md` because there was no channel. The mock-up's first pass drew two acts and two office verbs; the second pass made it one channel with two flavors.

## What shipped

- **`src/lib/legal/legalAsks.ts`** (new, tests) — an ASK is a `question` entry the office wrote (`via_portal = false`) with `meta.flavor` = `question` | `signoff` and, for a sign-off, `meta.jobId` / `jobLabel`; `acknowledged_at` on an ask is the office withdrawing it. The firm's ANSWER is an `answer` entry through the portal with `meta.askId` and, for a sign-off, `meta.signedOff`. `buildLegalAsks` pairs them (open · answered · withdrawn), `openAsks`, `askKindWords` (*Sign off · 273*), `askStateWords` (*waiting on the firm* · *signed off Oct 3* · *not yet · Oct 3*), `legalEntryKindWords` for the tables, `signoffStateForJob` + `signoffWords` for the notice footer, `defaultSignoffAsk` for its prefill.
- **`submit-legal-portal`** — kind `answer` (`askId`, `note`, optional `signedOff`): the ask must be this matter's, the office's, and not withdrawn; body defaults to *Signed off* / *Not yet*. Written `via_portal = true`, `acknowledged_at NULL` — the Needs You card reads it like any firm act.
- **`legalMatters.ts`** — `buildFirmActivity` gains `answers` and `signoffs`; **`dashboardNeedsYou.ts`** words them (*1 answer from counsel (1 sign-off granted)*), amber when a sign-off was granted.
- **The portal (`LegalPortal.tsx`)** — `FirmAsks` at the top of Fees & steps: *From the office · N waiting on you*, each ask with its words and, for a sign-off, *Signed off* / *Not yet* + a note, else an answer box. **`LegalFirmMatterView`**'s *On this matter* table names asks and answers (`legalEntryKindWords`) and says *asks you* / *withdrawn* on the office's asks.
- **The Legal desk (`LegalDeskModal`)** — *Ask the firm…* under *On the matter* (a select: a question / sign off on a job, the job, the words; `legal_add_entry` with `newAskMeta`); the entries table shows each ask's state and *Withdraw* (`legal_acknowledge_entry` on the office's own entry) while open.
- **The Lien desk (`LienDeskModal`)** — a sent notice's footer becomes the one-row form of v2.3776: the sent date, the sign-off words when there are any, and *Ask counsel to sign off…* (only when the job's account is with the firm — `legalSignoff` from `JobsStagesTab`, read through `useLegalMatters`); the inline ask is prefilled with `defaultSignoffAsk` (the GC's name, the open balance) and written the same way, then the matters reload.
