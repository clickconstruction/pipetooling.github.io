# v2.7091 — Supply houses: every bill shows its lines, however many bills the house has (2026-11-12)

A bug fix by itself, found on a read-only walk of the supply house's bills card on 2026-11-12. Opening a house with many bills on Materials → Supply houses printed `[row-cap] supply_paper_lines returned exactly 1000 rows with no limit` in the console.

## What was wrong

`loadForInvoices` in `src/lib/materials/lines/paperIo.ts` read a house's papers and then their lines with two reads that set no range. PostgREST answers such a read with its first 1,000 rows and no error. A house whose bills hold more than 1,000 lines between them lost the rest, in no order anyone chose:

- A bill whose lines fell past the cap read *0 lines* in its **Lines** cell, or fewer lines than its paper has, beside a check that was worked out when the paper was saved.
- The Look window opened such a bill with the lines it was handed. Its Save writes a paper whole, so a save there would have kept the short list as the paper's lines.
- The papers read listed every bill's id in one URL, which grows with the house. A house of several hundred bills would in time be refused for the URL's length, and the card would read *No lines yet* on every bill.

## What changed

- Both reads go through `fetchAllRowsChunkedIn` (`src/lib/supabasePaging.ts`): the ids in chunks of 150, each chunk paged with `.range()` until a short page, up to four chunks at once. `supply_papers` is ordered by `id`, `supply_paper_lines` by `paper_id` then `line_no` (the table's unique pair), so pages do not shuffle between requests. The result's shape is unchanged: a map of invoice id to its paper with its lines.
- **A failed lines read is now the empty map.** Before, a papers read that failed answered the empty map but a lines read that failed answered every paper with no lines. Now any page that fails, of either table, answers the empty map, so the card reads *No lines yet* with no Look press and never a paper with some of its lines.
- `namesOf` (who counted a bill as read) is left as it was: it asks for the handful of people who accepted, never a list that grows with the bills.

## Tests

`src/lib/materials/lines/paperIo.test.ts`, new, seven tests over the fake client that caps a read with no range at 1,000 rows (`makeFakeRowCapSupabase`): 1,500 lines over 30 bills all arrive, on two pages; each read carries a range and its stable order; 320 bills go in three chunks of ids per table; a bill with no paper and a paper with no lines; no reads for no bills; the empty map when the papers read fails and when the second page of lines fails.

## Checked

Read only on the live app from this branch, with every write to the database refused, as the dev: the house with the most lines on file opened from `?house=`. Its bills hold 242 papers and 1,945 lines. The card read all 242 papers in two chunks and all 1,945 lines in three pages, each request with its range and order and no URL past 6,000 characters, and the console printed no `[row-cap]` line. No bill on the card read *0 lines*. Before the fix the same house lost the 945 lines past the cap.
