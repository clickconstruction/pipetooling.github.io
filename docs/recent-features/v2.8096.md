# v2.8096 — Document builder: a send that went but was not recorded says so (2026-11-20)

Punch list #404, found by the audit of 2026-11-20. No migration. No function changed, so nothing to deploy.

## What was wrong

`document-builder-send` sends the email first and writes the send's row after. When the row does not land, it answers its `failed` key with the truth beside it: `detail: "Sent, but not recorded: <what the database said>"`. The client read only the key's words (`sendSheet` in `sendIo.ts`), so the page said *Something went wrong. Try again in a moment.* The email had gone. Trying again sent the trade a second email, and the one press for every trade that is ready named that trade as one that "did not send".

The same read dropped the reason on a send that truly did not go: the mail service's own answer rides in `detail` too, and the page showed only *The email did not go. Nothing was recorded. Try again.*

## The fix

`sendRefusalWords` (`sendIo.ts`, pure) words a refusal from the function's answer:

- *Sent, but not recorded* reads **The email went, but the send was not recorded here. Do not send it again.** with the reason after it. It never says *Try again*.
- Any other refusal with a reason reads the key's words, then **The reason:** and the reason.
- A refusal with no reason reads as it did.

Both the one send and the one press for every trade that is ready take their words from it.

## Left for the function's next deploy

The client tells the case apart by the first words of `detail`, since the function gives it no key of its own. On the function's next change, give *sent, not recorded* its own refusal key with these words, and keep this reader for the functions deployed before it.

## Tests

`sendIo.test.ts`: the three cases above, and that the first never holds the words *try again*.
