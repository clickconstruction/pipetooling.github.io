# v2.7662 — Supply houses: what each bill lists for now has its own tests (2026-11-17)

The first cut of `src/components/SupplyHousesTab.tsx`, step 1 of the *SupplyHousesTab extraction order* in `docs/MATERIALS_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md`, which the owner asked for whole (all twelve cuts, in order). A pure move: no behaviour changes.

**What moved.** `src/lib/materials/supplyHouseBillRows.ts` now holds what the tab built inside its render with no test:

- `supplyBillsToList` and `supplyBillCardRows`: the rows of a house's bills card. A bill a payment has taken part of lists for what is left on it and says what was paid. A paid bill keeps its own amount, and says what paid it. The bill's own receipt link wins over the payment's. The PO warning shows only when the house's PO Generator ledger was read. A bill marked paid on this visit stays listed.
- `supplyPhoneBills` and `typedPaidBillIds`: the phone screen's copy of the part-paid rule, and the paid bills a typed payment stands behind.
- `houseOnAccountTotal`: what the house holds on account.
- `allocationJobLabel`, with `recordPaymentBills` and `paymentWindowBills`: the job label the card, Record a payment and a payment's own window each wrote by hand, and the two lists of bills those windows are handed. The two titles differ on purpose and still do: Record a payment falls back to the PO's words and then *No job on this bill*, and a payment's own window leaves the title empty.

**Size.** `SupplyHousesTab.tsx` went from 3,678 lines to 3,628. Its state, effects and handlers are untouched.

**Proof.** 20 unit tests, all new, on made-up bills and payments: the part-paid amount and its words, a paid bill behind a typed payment, the receipt link's order, the PO warning with and without the ledger, the jobs' labels and shares, a credit read against a bill that is not listed, interest titled by the bill that ran late, the phone's rows, the typed-paid set with a removed payment, money on account, and both windows' titles. The tab has no render test yet: that is step 6, and this step moved only functions that close over nothing.
