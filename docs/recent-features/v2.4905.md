# v2.4905 — GC mode: the trade partner portal can read a company’s asks (2026-10-08)

P1b-i of the trade partner portal's real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/PORTAL_REAL_BUILD.md` on branch `spike/gc-mode`): the portal's reads whose callees are now on main, and the mapper from the portal's slice to the shapes they read. No database, no screen.

- `src/lib/gc/portal.ts`: 12 more of the prototype's `gcPortal.ts` exports, word for word (`to-dos/gc-mode/scripts/portal-p1b-i.lift.json` on the spike):
  - `portalAsks` and `PortalAsk`: bidding, a job, lost, passed or closed, stale, the lines the office could not read, the quote day.
  - `portalPromiseLine`, `bidRanOut`, `linkNeverOpened`, `portalVetting`, `portalPromises`, `portalPreBid` and `portalQuoteDue`.
  - `portalQuestions` and `PortalQuestion`.
  - `inviteMessage`, split out of `portalMessages` on the spike for the Board's Ask window, so it previews an invite it has not written yet.
- `src/lib/gc/planQuestions.ts` (new): `questionsCloseOn`, `questionState`, `questionsFor` and `quotesWantedOn` in the prototype's shape (stage `pursuing`). Main's row-shaped questions (stage `bidding`) stay in `questions.ts`, and the two never mix.
- Types: `PreBidMeeting` comes whole. `GcProject` gains `sizeNote` and `preBid`, and `PlanSet` gains `issuedOn`, each list the latest lift's with these appended. `schedule/testState.ts` is the generator's output with LIFTS and `--with portal-p1b-i.lift.json`. Without P1b-i the same run reproduces main's file byte for byte, and the change is 18 lines.
- `src/lib/gc/tradePortalState.ts` (new): `tradePortalState(slice, today)` turns `gc-trade-portal`'s slice into the prototype's `GcState`.
  - It carries one partner with its people and language.
  - The row's stage reads in the prototype's words.
  - Each money field is 0, because the slice never carries ours.
  - The newest quote on an ask counts.
  - A set's `touches` comes from the company's own `gc_plan_set_sends`.
  - Another company's answered question carries no asker.
  - The award, the statement of work, the papers and the schedule come in empty until their lanes add them to the slice.
- `supabase/functions/_shared/gcTradePortalSlice.ts`: the slice gains the company's own set sends (`set_id`, `touched`) and the project's `size_note`. Neither is ours to hide.
- Tests:
  - `portalReads.direct.test.ts` covers the moved reads on the made-up data, or on the little a case builds.
  - `tradePortalState.test.ts` runs rows through the slice builder, the mapper and the kernels.
  - The never-sees test gains its set-sends case.
