## The words

The rest of the Lien desk speaks in the notice piles' voice, from one kernel with its test: `src/lib/jobs/lienDeskEmptyWords.ts`. Each function takes the press it offers, so the component hands over its own setter. `LIEN_SHOW_EVERY_PILE` is the one label of *Show every pile ›*, the notice list's included; `lienListFindEmptyWords` reuses the notice list's find words (`lienFindNothingWords`) with the find box's own *Clear the find*. The Legal desk keeps its kernels: `firmMap.ts` now holds *Nothing is in Collections.* once (`LEGAL_NOTHING_IN_COLLECTIONS`) for the Map and the Accounts list, and the Timeline's words are `timelineEmptyWords` in `legalTimeline.ts`. The offers log and the Subs tiles use `emptyWords` (make, waiting). Every sentence passes `plainWordsFailures` in the kernels' tests.

## Is this the best we can do?

One sentence is held: the firm portal's lien grid says *switch to Upcoming for the whole book*, a press in words. `EmptyState` draws its press in the app's link blue, and the portal keeps its paper palette, so the grid waits for a tone on `EmptyState` or a portal twin of it. Two presses are doors rather than fills: *Back to the desk ›* closes Records for an owner, since its owners come from the desk's notices, and *Deadlines ›* sends an empty Affidavits list to the board that shows the later windows. The fees list and the Accounts list say who fills them and offer nothing, because the firm adds fees on its portal and a job reaches Collections from the Stages board behind the desk. A later pass could thread the Stages board's *Move to Collections* into the desk, or give the claim's empty bills the Bill it from here door once a job without a price is handled.
