# v2.8434 — GC projects: take an award back while its statement of work is a draft (2026-11-24)

The walks of punch list #416 (`to-dos/document-builder-whats-left.md`, Part 1, walk 1a) awarded two test trades and found no way back: `gc_trade_packages_award_guard` (v2.5100) lets the three award columns change only inside `gc_award`, a dev's plain UPDATE included, and nothing in the app or the database cleared them. An award made on the wrong company had the same dead end. The owner's word of 2026-11-24: build the tool to unaward.

## The design, as a table

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **The press** | **Take the award back…** beside the green **Awarded** chip in Compare quotes, for a reader who can award (a dev until call W). |
| **Dead when** | The statement of work was sent (*The statement of work went to them to sign. Nothing takes a sent one back yet.*) or signed (*They signed the statement of work. A signed one changes through Change orders.*). The reason reads under the press. |
| **The confirm** | *Take the Sitework award back from ‹company›?* It says the award comes off, the drafted statement of work for its price is deleted, the quote and the ask stay, and that the company's portal told them they got the job if they opened it since. The button reads **Take the award back**. |
| **What goes** | The award's three columns, and the drafted statement of work with its lines. |
| **What stays** | The ask, its quote and its story, what the trade carried, the company's messages. The ask gains a note of the undo. |
| **Refused, in words** | A sent or signed statement of work; a pay application, a back charge, a change request, a change order, a report of work or a bill day answer on it; a paper sent for the trade, a submittal, the company's RFI or its promise on the trade. Each as *‹trade› stays awarded to ‹company›, since …* with where it is. |

## The calls taken on defaults

Each is the smallest choice, and each can be flipped.

1. **A sent, unsigned statement of work is refused, not cancelled.** `gc_sows.status` allows `cancelled`, which nothing writes or reads. Using it would need every reader of a statement of work to learn it, so the take-back stops at draft and says *Nothing takes a sent statement of work back yet.*
2. **The draft is deleted, not kept.** A draft nobody was sent is the app's own working copy of the quote; the quote it was drawn from stays, and a new award draws it again. The undo's note keeps its price.
3. **The paper picked for a draft goes with it.** It is a column of the draft (`paper_id`), the office's own choice, not a paper sent.
4. **Records on the award refuse the take-back rather than follow it.** Submittals, the company's RFIs, its promises and a paper sent for the trade name no company of their own: they read the award. Left in place they would read as the next company's.
5. **Emails that already went do not hold it.** A Start email or a word to every trade stays on the company's thread. The confirm names the one thing the company may have seen without us telling them: its portal read *you got the job* from the moment of the award.
6. **The press lives in Compare quotes only**, where the award is made and where the guard's own words point. The statement of work's card on the trade's block gains nothing.
7. **The trace is a note on the ask's story**, not a new table: `gc_company_contacts` with `how` *note*, which is the office's note and counts as no contact with the company. It shows on the ask and in the company's activity.
8. **A training account, a digital twin and an archived caller are refused in their own words**, as `gc_remove_bench_company` does, because the read-only fence hides the trade from the function's row lock and the refusal would otherwise read *No trade with that id.*

## What changed

- **`gc_take_award_back(p_package_id)`** (migration `20261124040000`, `docs/migrations/20261124040000_gc_take_award_back.md`): `gc_award`'s mirror. The same gate, the `gc.award_write` flag around its one UPDATE, one transaction. No table is made; the guard and `gc_award` are not restated.
- **`src/lib/gc/awardBack.ts`** (new, pure): `awardBackHold(pkg)` is why the press is dead, `showsAwardBack` where it shows, `awardBackConfirmWords(trade, company, pkg)` the confirm's title, message and button.
- **`GcCompareQuotes.tsx`**: `CompareWrites.takeAwardBack`; the press beside **Awarded**, the confirm through `useConfirmDialog` (`danger`, `confirmLabel` *Take the award back*), and a refusal drawn in the window's problem line through `formatErrorMessage`. `gcIo.ts` `takeGcAwardBack` calls the function (its name through `as never` until the types regen). `GcProjects.tsx` wires it for a dev and reloads the projects, which reads the board again.
- **The bed**: `supabase/tests/gc_take_award_back/20_scenario.sql` with `scripts/pgtest-gc-take-award-back.sh`, in `scripts/sql-beds.txt`: every role's gate, each refusal with its words, records that are not the award's passing, the take-back, the guard refusing a plain write afterwards, and the trade awarded again.
- **Tests**: `awardBack.test.ts` (the holds, the confirm's words, the flag around the write, and every refusal the function words being played in the bed); the render smoke `GcCompareQuotes.render.test.tsx`, now under `renderWithProviders` for the confirm (Cancel writes nothing, the confirm's button writes, the dead press and its reason, a plain-object refusal read in the window, no press without the write).
- **Guide**: *award a trade on a GC project* gains **Take an award back** and **When the award stays**. `docs/GLOSSARY.md`, `docs/ACCESS_CONTROL.md` and `docs/PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` §20 are amended in place.
