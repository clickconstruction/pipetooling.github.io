# v2.3431 — Pipeline: the capable-to-bill total reads each Working job's stage plan (2026-09-14)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Item 1 of [`to-dos/stage-plan-residuals.md`](../../to-dos/stage-plan-residuals.md), the leftover the Stage Plan train (v2.3083 → v2.3134) filed: the Working header's *Capable of Being Billed* still summed a per-job formula while the Bill tab's *Still to bill* list read the plan's `billable()`.

## What shipped

- **Kernel** [`capableToBillPlan.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/capableToBillPlan.ts) (5 tests): `stagePlanFixturesFromRows` turns the board row's `jobs_ledger_fixtures` into plan fixtures (named rows only, discounts netted into the work rows they apply to — the same netting as `stagePlanFixturesFromForm`, so a draw's figure is what it would bill); `jobStagePlanFromRow` builds one job's plan from those plus its slice of the fetched inputs; `capableToBillFigures` gives one figure per Working job with `source: 'plan' | 'formula'` and the plan's `billableRows`; `capableToBillTotalWithPlans` / `buildCapableToBillBreakdownRowsWithPlans` replace the two board callers.
- **The rule:** a job with at least one **Order** stage reads its plan — the sum of `billable()`: the Order stages that passed inspection with nothing unbilled ahead of them, and the Any rows whose work is done, never a row already on an invoice. Every other job keeps the % complete formula (`jobCapableToBillAmounts`): a job whose rows are all Any (the column default) or plain has no sequence for the plan to enforce, and a job the office never split must not read $0 because no row is "done". Recognized stages (v2.3417, a Pipeline-row reading) do not count — billing follows the Bill tab's selector.
- **Hook** [`useWorkingStagePlanInputs`](../../src/hooks/useWorkingStagePlanInputs.ts): the `job_stage_windows` → `step_commitments` → `people_labor_jobs` chain for every Working job in three id-chunked, paged reads (`fetchAllRowsChunkedIn`) — the client-side twin of `loadGcStageInputs` in `_shared/gcStages.ts`. Keyed on the **unfiltered** Working ids, so a search or GC filter never refetches; returns null until the read lands or if it fails, and keeps the last result while a changed id set reloads.
- **The board** [`JobsStagesTab.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsStagesTab.tsx): the Working header, the jump-bar Section tools figure, the money story's *Ready to ask for* and the Capable list all read the new total. While the hook is null every job reads the formula, so the number never flashes to zero; the lean-stats path (`computeStagesHeaderStats`, collapsed sections) is untouched — lean rows carry no fixtures and stay on the formula by construction. In the Capable list a plan job prints why under its name (*Stage 1 passed inspection · Relocate water heater is done*); its Done / Paid / Open bills columns are still the formula's figures.
- Guides: *read the Pipeline's money view* (the Ready-to-ask-for bullet says which jobs read the plan), *ready to bill: the Pipeline* (one sentence under the section-tools example).
