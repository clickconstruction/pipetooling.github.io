# v2.6886 — Link a customer to its names on permits (punch list #307, PR 9 and 10) (2026-11-10)

The owner, on the first real screenshots of a bid's window: *our names for customers may not perfectly match the permits so it would be cool to be able to link one of our customers to a known customer name on bids to see similarities.* Twelve GCs probed on real rows the same day: one had the same name on a permit, three a look-alike that may or may not be them, one an accidental match inside a longer word, seven nothing. Mock-up: `to-dos/bid-permits/link-a-name.html`.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/research/nameLikeness.ts`** (new, pure, `nameLikeness.test.ts`): `nameLikeness(ours, theirs)` grades two names and says why (the same name, one begins the other word for word, one letter apart, a shared telling word, initials). The legal filler is dropped and the trade's common words (construction, builders, group) count only toward one name beginning another. Every telling word of the shorter name must be met and at least half of the longer one's, so a word that sits inside a long unrelated name is refused. `suggestNames` ranks the names for one customer with a permit on an address we bid to them above any likeness, and leaves out the customer's own name, a name already answered and a name that files for others. `searchNames`, `markName`, `compareRows` (ours beside the city's: the name with the people listed on it, the addresses we bid against the name's permits on them, the zips) and `lookAlikePairs` (every customer against every name through an index of telling words, one customer per keyed name).
- **`FindNamesSheet`** (new): *Find their names on permits*, opened from a press under the names tied to a GC in a bid's window and from the new **Names on permits** card on the customer's page (`CustomerPermitNamesCard` in `CustomerDetail`). The suggestions with their reasons, **Compare**, a search over every name, and what is tied. Two presses: **Same company** (or **Works for them** for a person) and **Not them**.
- **`PermitLookAlikes`** and **`usePermitLookAlikes`** (new): the **Looks like a customer** chip on Builders with its count, and the list behind it. Site evidence comes from the newest thousand bids with a customer on them.
- **`bidSiteIo`**: `loadPermitsOfName`, `loadAllNameAnswers`, `loadCustomerNames`, `loadRecentBidsWithCustomer`. **`nameAnswers.tiedNames`**: a company tied to a customer reads *Same company*, a person *Works for them*.
- No migration: a tie is a `works_for`, every-permit row in `builder_customer_answers` (v2.6872), a No a `someone_else` row.
- Guides `see-what-the-city-has-on-a-bids-address` and `research-permits-for-work-to-bid`; `docs/PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`.

## The read back, held

Found before the first read back was run, 2026-11-10: `research_permits`, `research_permits_v2` and `research_builders` pair every building permit with every plumbing permit in a city through an `OR` no index serves. Each takes 2 to 3.5 seconds on prod today, alone. A read back adds about 7,000 rows a step, and the pairing grows with the product of the two sides, so one step would put Builders past the 8-second statement timeout. Nothing was read back.

- `permit-pull` refuses a read back (`HISTORY_HELD`), deployed the same hour from this branch so nobody could press it in the window; this PR puts the same code on main.
- `HISTORY_CITIES` is empty, so Builders draws no **Read back to …** press. `historyAsk` takes the cities, so its steps stay tested.
- The guide no longer names the press.

The reads are made index-driven next (two indexed lookups in place of the `OR`), proven equal on real rows, and then San Antonio returns to `HISTORY_CITIES`.
