# v2.5984 — The Legal desk's Map fits a phone (2026-10-27)

Punch list #182, the live walk's fix. After [v2.5894](v2.5894.md), [v2.5936](v2.5936.md) and [v2.5941](v2.5941.md) deployed, the desk was walked on clicktooling.com as the dev at 375, 768 and 1024 px against the to-do's mock-up, frame by frame. Every frame matched but one place the mock-up never drew: the Map on a phone.

## What shipped

- **The Map is one column on a phone** (`LegalDeskMapView.tsx`, a `stacked` prop the desk passes in its stacked layout): its grid was `minmax(0, 1fr) 280px` at every width, so in a 357 px panel the legend kept its 280 px and the map's column measured 27 px, the county map and its summary squeezed into it 1,110 px tall. On a phone the grid is `minmax(0, 1fr)`, *Who covers what* under the map, and the view's padding is the account pane's `12px`. On a computer and at 768 px (two columns, the map 388 px beside the legend) nothing changes. The grid carries `data-legal-map-grid` for the tests.
- **The list's Map is an address** (`LegalDesk.tsx`, `LegalDeskPage.tsx`): the desk writes the address from an effect once an account is resolved, never from an empty rail, which would drop a pasted `?account=` before the rows arrive. A phone's list has no account by design, so **Map** there wrote nothing and the view had no address. A press on **Accounts** or **Map** with no account open now tells the page itself (`onAddressChange` takes `account: null`), and the page writes the list's address with the view and no tab: `/legal-desk?view=map`, back to `/legal-desk` on **Accounts**. A tab left over from the last account stays out of it. The effect is unchanged, so the pasted-link case is too.
- **Tests**: `LegalDeskPage.render.test.tsx` (+1: on a phone, an account and its Paper tab, ← Accounts, then Map writes `/legal-desk?view=map` with the map in one column, and Accounts writes `/legal-desk`), `LegalDesk.address.render.test.tsx` (+1: the stacked list's presses carry `account: null`; the map is one column stacked and keeps its 280 px legend on a computer).
- **Words**: the guide *review a collections account before it goes to your attorney* (See every case on the map: on a phone the map fills the width, *Who covers what* under it), `GLOSSARY.md` → Legal desk.
