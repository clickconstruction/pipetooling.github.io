Nothing else of the picture leaves.
- **The slice**: `TRADE_PORTAL_FIELDS.gc` gains `start_date` and `started_on`; the read's `gc_projects` select carries them; `tradePortalState.ts`'s `projectOf` maps them to `startDate` and `startedOn` (both were `null`).
- **`src/lib/gc/tradePortalHead.ts`** (new, pure, with its test): `jobHead(project, partnerId, today, finish)` → the start (the day we pressed Start, else the planned start), the finish, the days left (negative past the plan), and the company's own percent weighted by each signed line's amount, with the dollars and the lines; `jobHeadShown` is a building job with a trade awarded to the company through its own invite.
- **`GcTradePortalJobHead.tsx`** (new) at the top of `GcTradePortalProject.tsx` on such a job: *Started Sep 8 · Click plans to finish Nov 17 · 40 days left*; **Your work on this job** with *36% done · $17,496 of $48,600*, one bar and *Rough-in 60% · Trim and fixtures 0% · from the percents you reported*; then **Who to call** (moved up from the foot on a building job, left at the foot otherwise) and **Needs you on this job**, the home's to-dos for this project, a press on one scrolling to its block's `data-portal-anchor`. `GcTradePortalView` works the to-dos out once (`withScheduleTodos(portalHomeTodos(…), scheduleTodos)`, lifted out of `Home`) and hands each page its own.

- **Sample**: the dental office's `gc` row gets `start_date` and `started_on` 30 days back, and its chart `finish` 40 days out.
- **Tests**: `tradePortalHead.test.ts`; `tradePortalSchedule.test.ts` pins `finish` to the picture's and the answer's keys; `GcTradePortal.head.render.test.tsx` opens Sample Dental Office and reads the three head lines, Who to call under the head, Needs you on this job with the punch line and its scroll, no head on a bidding project, and the head without the finish line on an older answer.
