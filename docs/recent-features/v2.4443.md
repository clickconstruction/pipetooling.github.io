# v2.4443 — Held for suppliers: ask a house about all its jobs at once (2026-10-02)

No migration, no edge function. Step 2 of 2 of the owner's ask ([v2.4440](v2.4440.md)): "help me do it in a way so it is easy to update and we get information from a supply house."

## Is this the best we can do?

**They told us…** on a row (v2.4411, v2.4440) is one job and one house at a time. On the live board Reece alone is owed on 15 jobs; a rep answers for all of them in one call or one reply. So the unit of work is the house: one list to send, one list to fill in. Three things were weighed and decided:

- **The app sends nothing.** The message is copied, or opens in the person's own mail (`mailto:`), so the reply comes back to them and nothing leaves in the company's name unread. A message too long for a mail link is copied and the link carries the subject.
- **The house's words go in the table that already holds them** (`job_supply_house_words`), so the Lien desk, the copied paragraph and each job's statement show an answer the moment it is saved. No second place to keep in step.
- **A page the rep fills in themselves** (a link, like the quote link) would get the information without a call, but it is a public page shown to a third party and a new edge function. It is left as the owner's call, not built.

## What shipped

- **The kernel** ([`houseAsk.ts`](../../src/lib/materials/houseAsk.ts), new, 11 tests): `buildHouseAsks` — every house that is still owed, most money first, each with its jobs soonest date first (the job, its address, what our books show, the unpaid count and month, the unpaid invoice numbers from `unpaidInvoiceNumbers`, the house's notice from `lienSupplierHouseNotice`, whether the customer has paid in full, what is on record). `houseAskMessage` / `houseAskSubject` — the ask, lettered so a rep can answer by letter, with what the house files a job under and what our books show. `houseAskChanges` — the rows worth saving: typed, a number, and not already on record; a word's note is kept; a blank row is left alone. `pickAskRep` — billing, else the job-accounts rep, else the default contact, and it says which. `houseAskMailto` — the whole message when it fits in 1,800 characters.
- **The sheet** ([`SupplyHouseAskSheet`](../../src/components/materials/SupplyHouseAskSheet.tsx), new, render test 5): house tabs, the summary (*15 jobs · $50,149.91 owed by our books · first notice Oct 15 · 3 paid in full by the customer*), who to ask with **Call**, **Email the ask**, **Copy the ask** and **Read the message**, the list with two boxes a row, **Who said it**, and one **Save N answers** across houses. A typed 0 against a balance in our books says *They show nothing owed … mark those invoices paid under Supply houses*. A click outside closes the sheet only while nothing is typed. A phone gets it full screen with stacked rows.
- **The tab** ([`MaterialsJobAccountsTab`](../../src/components/materials/MaterialsJobAccountsTab.tsx)): **Ask a house…** beside Refresh; the load adds `job_address` and `invoice_number`; a save re-reads the words.
- [`lienSupplierWordIo.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienSupplierWordIo.ts): `saveSupplierWords`, one upsert for the lot.
- Guides: [`ask-a-supply-house-what-it-shows-owed`](../../src/content/help/ask-a-supply-house-what-it-shows-owed.md) (new), linked from the tab's guide and the Lien desk's.
