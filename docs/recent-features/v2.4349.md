# v2.4349 — Pipeline: each bill row says what is paid and what is left on that bill (2026-10-01)

No migration, no edge function. PR 1 of the Progress & payment cell's pass 4 (Grace, 2026-10-01; canvas https://claude.ai/artifact/UfRpXq1vGyXioaeMyky9Wz, board *A job with two open bills*). Her question on Springtown's row (*Billed Sep 23 · 8d ago*, *Expected Sep 28*): how does the app handle a job with several bills out? Approved as drawn: *"I like it. Please build it."*

## What was true

- `buildBilledStageRows` gives a job with exactly one sent bill one merged row, and a job with two or more one green standalone row per bill (no job row). Read live 2026-10-01: 11 jobs, 26 rows.
- Every bill row prints the whole job's legend, so the only thing a row said about its own bill was the bill's original amount in the footnote, labelled *draft* (`StagesUnifiedInvoiceRow`, *Amount on this draft billing line*), plus the job's *unallocated* remainder. Springtown (977) bill 2 read *$11,770 draft · $11,182 unallocated* and *25d past* while $11,182 of it was paid and $588.52 was left. Six of the 11 jobs had a part-paid bill like that (Knight Springtown Vet $110 and $78 left, Springtown HVAC $54).
- The right words already existed: *This bill: $X paid · $Y left* (v2.3459) on the merged row, gated on two or more sent bills. A merged row only exists for one sent bill, so it could never render.

## What changed

- **Kernel** [`stagesBillRowLine.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/stagesBillRowLine.ts) (+ test, 5 cases on Springtown's bills): *$11,182 paid* / *nothing paid*, *$589 left* / *nothing left*, and the hover (*This row's bill: $11,770 sent Sep 1. $11,182 is paid on it, and $589 is left.*). A payment counts against a bill only when `payments.invoice_id` links it, as `sumInvoiceAppliedFromJobPayments` and the section totals already count it; `openRemainder` / `isSettledRemainder` from `billTruth`.
- **[`StagesBillRowLine`](../../src/components/jobs/StagesBillRowLine.tsx)**: *This bill* (bold) · paid (muted) on the left, *left* (bold) on the right, full width in the legend's 12 px grammar, between the cell and the dates block.
- **[`StagesUnifiedInvoiceRow`](../../src/components/jobs/StagesUnifiedInvoiceRow.tsx)**: a `billed` bill draws the line and no footnote; a `ready_to_bill` draft keeps *$X draft · $Y unallocated*.
- **[`JobsStagesCardList`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsStagesCardList.tsx)** (mobile cards): a sent bill's card draws the line under the money (where a desktop row has it) in place of *Invoice $X · #N*; drafts keep theirs.
- **[`StagesUnifiedJobRow`](../../src/components/jobs/StagesUnifiedJobRow.tsx)**: the dead merged-row *This bill* footnote is gone (comment says why).
- Guide *read the Pipeline money view*: new *A job with more than one bill out*.
