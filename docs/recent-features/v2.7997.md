# v2.7997 — Before we start: a place to keep what must be true before a stage (2026-11-19)

Punch list #382, PR 3 of eleven: the table. One migration, `20261120033820_start_checks.sql`, with its doc, its bed and the access rules. No screen and no client code. **Owed after the merge: `supabase db push`, then the types.**

## What it adds

Eight tables, three gates, one door for Go and Hold, a read of our days for the desk, a photo bucket, and a trigger that carries a stage line's list when the line moves to another job. `docs/migrations/20261120033820_start_checks.md` has every column and rule.

| Table | What it keeps |
|---|---|
| `start_check_templates` | The company's lists, one a stage kind: a starter a stage copies. |
| `start_check_bullets` | A stage line's bullets, on them or on us. |
| `start_check_marks` | A yes on a bullet on them, with how we know, and its take-back. |
| `start_check_asks` | Every ask, a row a bullet, and a hold note sent. |
| `start_check_their_days` | Every day they gave for ready. |
| `start_check_decisions` | Go and Hold, with who pressed it and what was open. |
| `start_check_arrivals` | What the crew found on arrival. |
| `start_check_settings` | A site's days of notice, and a job's switch for the GC's page. |

## Choices made here

- **New tables, not the checklist's.** The stage checklist's templates apply live to every stage of a kind, and its answers are a pick per question. A start bullet carries its own state on one stage: a yes with how we know, asks, a day they promised, an eye. The company's lists are a starter that a stage copies, as the mock-up's *Use the four* does.
- **Settings in their own table.** The second switch on the job and the site's notice sit in `start_check_settings`, so the migration alters neither `jobs_ledger` nor `developments`.
- **One door for decisions.** Go and Hold go through `start_check_decide`, a definer, for two reasons: the name on a decision is read from the caller's row and cannot be typed, and a Hold moves the stage's window, which a superintendent may not write by hand.
- **A primary is held to `is_job_people`**, in the desk's gate and by being left out of the crew's.
- **Our days are read for the desk** by `start_check_windows`, a definer that names a window's two days and nothing else. `job_stage_windows` is read by no primary, and a primary with a claim on a job is on its desk. The read answers only for a job the caller's desk covers.
- **An ask is a row a bullet**, with one `batch_id` a message. A bullet's line reads its last ask with no join table.
- **The days of notice may be null.** Null reads the default, ten days far and two near, so the screen can say a site is on the default (the fourth kind of value in v2.7985).
- **The crew writes nothing yet.** The look on arrival (PR 10) and the GC's page (PR 9) bring their own writes.
