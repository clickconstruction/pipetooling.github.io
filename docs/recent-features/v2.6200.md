# v2.6200 — The reader reads the body's foot: a newsletter with no list header is bulk (2026-10-31)

Punch list #223 PR 2 (`to-dos/messages-notices/README.md`, piece 2, no owner call). On 2026-10-30 New on the Messages page held thirteen rows, eleven of them mail nobody wrote to us; the one newsletter among them had no `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Id` or `Precedence` header, so `leftOut.ts` took it and `email_place_address` made it a *Who is this?* row. Its unsubscribe line was in the body, which the reader did not read.

- **`src/lib/emailIn/leftOut.ts`** gains `bulkByBody(text)`: the body's foot, its last `BODY_FOOT_LINES` (15) lines with words on them, read against six list cues named exactly — *unsubscribe*, *view this email in your browser* (and *view it online*, *view in browser*), *you are receiving this*, *you received this email*, *manage your preferences* (and *update preferences*). Any one at the foot reads as `bulk`. `leftOut` takes the body as an optional `text` beside the labels, the sender and the headers, and reads it after the headers; a caller with no body gets the headers' answer as before.
- **A bid platform's footer is never a cue**: *You are receiving this because Ridgeway Builders invited you to bid* and *to stop receiving invitations from this contractor* name the reader's own choice about one contractor, a person's, not a list's. A foot line that names an invitation, a bid, a contractor, a plan room or a solicitation is held out of the rule (`PLATFORM_CHOICE`), so an invitation to bid is taken as before, and piece 3's *Bid it?* can read it.
- **The foot only**: a cue above the last fifteen lines is quoted words, not a sign-off, so *please unsubscribe me from the old list* at the top of a long email changes nothing.
- The edge copy is regenerated (`npm run gen:edge-kernels`); `gmail-sync` passes the email it already reads, so the function's own code is unchanged. **Deploy** `gmail-sync` once merged. No migration.
- Guide *turn on reading the bids inbox* says a newsletter stays in Gmail by its headers or by the unsubscribe line at its foot. `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` → gmail-sync names the rule.
- Tests: `leftOut.test.ts` holds a made-up newsletter left out, a made-up platform invitation taken, a person's words taken, every cue, the platform exemption and the foot's window.

Piece 1 (*A notice, not a person*, the press and the left-out list) ships beside this as PR 1; pieces 3 and 4 wait on the owner's calls.
