# v2.6114 — Job Parts Tally: the Choose job sheet finds a job and leads with the jobs you were on (punch list #216) (2026-10-29)

The owner's ask from his phone, 2026-10-29: the Choose job sheet on the tally was every job the viewer could see, newest number first, four hundred rows deep, with no search and nothing of the viewer's own day in it. Mocked up three ways (`to-dos/tally-choose-job-recent-search/mockup.html`), Draft A picked, revised on his *is that the best you can do? I would like to be able to see what I am searching for highlighted*, built on his yes.

## What changed

- **`components/tally/TallyChooseJobSheet.tsx`**, one sheet for both tabs on `PhoneSheet` (so it stays above the keyboard): the search on top, not focused on open; **Recent** under it, the last three jobs the viewer was on with when, **Show N more** to ten; **All jobs** with its count; typing narrows every section and lights the typed run; nothing matching draws `emptyWords({ kind: 'filter' })` with Clear the search; a held All jobs (Show my jobs only) with nothing in it offers Clear the filters. The Materials Estimate picker in `JobTally.tsx` and the On site tab's `Sheet` picker in `TallyOnSiteTab.tsx` both open it. The tally's job rows now carry `click_number`, `customer_name` and `service_type_id`.
- **`lib/tally/chooseJobSearch.ts`**: the fields the app's job searches read (the number with or without its ledger letter, the name, the address, the customer); every typed word must land somewhere (the Clock sessions search's rule); the runs per field, so the rows light them; rank 0 for the number typed, 1 for a number that starts with it, 2 otherwise, stable within a rank. Tested.
- **`lib/tally/recentJobs.ts`**: newest first, each job once, ten at most; the when-words (*Clocked in today*, *Clocked in yesterday*, *Clocked in Tue Oct 27*, *Purchase sorted Sat Oct 24*, the year when it differs); the heading's *jobs you were on* only when every mark is a clock-in; *Show N more*. Tested.
- **`lib/tally/fetchRecentJobsForTally.ts`**: the viewer's `clock_sessions` (not rejected or revoked, newest sixty, the Sort screen's read), else the newest two hundred of their card purchases through `list_my_linked_mercury_transactions_for_tally` and each purchase's job splits on its swipe day; a failed read comes back as a `{ kind, message }` failure the sheet draws on `OfflineRetryPanel` with Retry while All jobs still lists.
- **Guide** *keep track of the material in the cages and boxes on site* → *Find the job*.

## Decisions

- **Amber, not blue, for the lit run.** The app lights search hits two ways: the Jobs page's amber `StagesSearchMark` and the schedule's job picker's blue bold. This sheet's picked row is already blue, so a blue run on it would vanish.
- **Every word lands somewhere, lit where it landed.** *tunnel marcos* finds a tunnel repair in San Marcos whichever way round; the lit runs are what make the rule legible, which is why the Jobs page's single-run matcher was not reused as is.
- **The when-line names its source.** For a viewer with no clock-ins the rows come from sorted purchases, and *you were on* would be a lie for the office: those rows read *Purchase sorted Oct 24* and the heading drops *jobs you were on*.
- **All jobs keeps every job**, in today's order, so a recent job appears under both headings and the number order has no holes.
- **The when-line is the row's third line, not a right column.** The mock-up drew it beside the name; the live walk, with real job names (*1007 · SPACEX BA-02N Architectural*), cut the name to ten letters at 375 px beside *Clocked in Fri Sep 18*. Under the address it costs a line and keeps the name whole. The list is a one-column grid held to `minmax(0, 1fr)`: left at auto, the column grew to a row's nowrap text and pushed the when-words off the screen, the first walk's finding.
- **No Recent section when there is none**, instead of a *No recent jobs* sentence: empty here is simply not yet.
- **The ⊗ is the sheet's own 40 px press**, the input a plain text field with the search keyboard, because the browser's search-field clear is a 16 px target.
