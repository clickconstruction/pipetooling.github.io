# v2.4712 — Legal desk: replace the law firm in one step (2026-10-06)

One migration (`20261006234500_legal_replace_firm.sql`, push after merge); no edge function. PR 2 of 2 of the firm's door, after [v2.4711](v2.4711.md); the owner approved both on 2026-10-06 (*"I like it, build it"*). Mockup: `to-dos/legal-portal-firm-ready/mockup-firm-door.html`, section 3.

## Why

The firm on file was a stand-in, *ZZ Test Firm (temp — replace with the real firm)*. Renaming it in place keeps its people, its portal link and any account with it, so a stand-in's test people would then hear about the real firm's accounts. Settings could not retire a firm at all: the save writes `active: true` on the one active row, and `legal_firms_one_active` refuses a second.

## What changed

- **`legal_replace_firm`** (migration page [`docs/migrations/20261006234500_legal_replace_firm.md`](../migrations/20261006234500_legal_replace_firm.md)): dev only. In one transaction it forgets the old firm's link secret, revokes the link, sets the old firm inactive and paused, and adds the new firm active. It refuses while an account is still with the old firm, using the portal's own stage set.
- **[`LegalFirmReplacePanel.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/legal/LegalFirmReplacePanel.tsx)**, under the Settings block in the firm's window, a dev's only. Closed, it reads *Replace this firm* with **Replace with a new firm…**. Open, it lists what retiring does, built from what hangs on the firm now (`legalFirmReplaceWords`), then the new firm's six fields, then **Replace the firm** and *Cancel*. The contingency and filing cost start from the old firm's.
- **While an account is with the old firm**, the panel shows the count and each account with **Show it on the desk**. That closes the window and selects the account, where *Pull back* is. **Replace the firm** stays disabled.
- **After a replace**, the desk reloads its firm, the window's Settings block remounts on the new firm (keyed on its id), and the old firm's unsaved draft is cleared so it cannot land on the new one. A toast says *<new> is the firm now. <old> is retired.*
- **Errors in the office's words** (`legalReplaceErrorWords`): before the push, *Replacing needs a database update that is not live yet*; a race on the one-firm index, *Another firm became active while you were here*; the function's own refusals as they are.
- **One form check** (`legalFirmInputProblem`) for Settings and the panel. Settings used to save a blank contingency or filing cost as 0; both now ask for a number.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **Two saves from the page?** No: between retiring and adding there would be no active firm, and a failed add would leave none. One database call does both or neither.
- **Move the old firm's open accounts to the new one?** No. A firm that never agreed to take an account should not find it on its portal. The panel points to each account so a dev pulls it back and releases it again.
- **Delete the stand-in?** No. Its matters, entries and sent copies are the record; retiring keeps them and takes the firm off everything live.
