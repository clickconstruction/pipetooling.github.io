Third PR of the train in [`to-dos/lien-desk/`](../../to-dos/lien-desk/README.md) (PR 1 v2.3405 the desk · PR 2 v2.3410 the run). Stacked on `claude/lien-desk-2`.

## Why

The notice preserves the month; the affidavit is the lien. Its window is one date per job — the 15th of the 4th month after the **last** month worked (3rd residential) — and the Lien window's affidavit tab already renders it behind its gate. What was missing was the same queue and approval the notices got: nobody was told when a window was closing, and the master signs the affidavit, so his go-ahead is the natural gate.

## What shipped

- **Migration**: `lien_filing_deadline()` (the § 53.052 date in SQL) and **`list_lien_affidavit_windows()`** — every unpaid billed job with approved sessions whose window is within 30 days (a week behind so a missed one is seen), with the last month worked, sub or direct, whether a live notice is recorded, whether an affidavit is already filed, the gate facts (owner of record, county + legal description, homestead), and the live desk item of kind `affidavit`. Office-gated.
- **`list_lien_notice_months()` replaced** in the same migration, after the first live pass: a job counts when its status is billed **or** it has a billed invoice (J878, still Working with an unpaid bill, was left out), and once any month is inside the window every unnoticed month with an open window comes back, so J650's notice names June, July and August instead of June alone.
- **Kernel [`lienDeskAffidavits.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDeskAffidavits.ts)** (+3 tests): `affidavitGates` (owner · legal · notice on sub jobs · not a homestead), `buildLienAffidavitQueue` → piles *Needs the property facts* · *To draft* · *Ready to file* · *Held* · *Filed · 30d* · *Missed*, severity from the same 7 / 14-day bands. [`lienNoticeDraft.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienNoticeDraft.ts) gains `buildLienAffidavitFieldsForJob` (the Lien window's affidavit recipe, from the job facts).
- **[`LienDeskAffidavitPane`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskAffidavitPane.tsx)**: the gate with doors (owner / legal → Edit Job → Property record; notice → the desk's Notices kind on that job), the claim line, the affidavit as the Lien window prints it, and the footer by state — Send for approval ▸ / The leader said to file it ▸ (office), Approve — file it ▸ / Hold (they promised · I'll call first) / Back to the office (leader), **Open the affidavit tab ›** on a ready item (print for notarization · file with the clerk · record the filing, which moves the row to *Filed*), **Record service ›** and **Refer to the Legal desk ›** on a filed, still-unpaid job, the forfeit sentence on every state.
- **The desk**: a **Notices · Affidavits** switch with counts, the affidavit piles and list (deadline chip *file in 5d*, last work month, the missing gates), `initialKind`; `syncLienDeskAfterRecord` marks an affidavit item sent once a filing with `filed_at` exists. `saveLienDeskDraft` / `skipLienDeskItem` take a `kind`.
- **Doors**: the Dashboard's `lien-file-window` card (and Quickfill's) opens `?liendesk=1&kind=affidavit`; its action reads *Open the Lien desk*; the Stages tab's Lien window opens on the affidavit tab from the pane; the Legal desk from the pane.
- Guide *send lien notices from the Lien desk* → "Affidavits"; glossary; ACCESS_CONTROL (the RPC).
