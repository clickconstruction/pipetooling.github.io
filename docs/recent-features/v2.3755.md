# v2.3755 — Ready to Bill on a stage-billed job: Confirm works, and the stage bill stays billed (2026-09-23)

Taunya, 2026-09-23, on job 978 (Springtown- HVAC, Working, 100% done, a 1% stage bill out with a payment applied): *"I can't click confirm to send to ready to bill."* Client only; no migration, no edge deploy.

## What was wrong

Two things, one under the other.

- **The Stages board's Working → Ready to Bill ran the Billed-section send-back.** `useJobsStagesMutations.moveJobToReadyToBillWithStripePrep` (and its Dashboard twin in `useDashboardBillingInvoices`) called `prepareBilledInvoicesBeforeJobRevertToReadyToBill` for every move into Ready to Bill. That sweep voids the job's Stripe-backed billed lines and deletes the rest by `delete_billed_invoice_on_send_back` — right for Billed → Ready to Bill, wrong for a Working job carrying a stage bill (the v2.2601 round trip promised "its billed line stays billed"). On 978 the RPC refused (*This invoice has recorded payments…*), which was the lucky outcome: a stage bill with no payment yet would have been voided under the customer with no warning. `markJobReadyToBill.ts` (the field's "100% → Ready to bill?" prompt) already said it plainly: *No Stripe/billed-invoice prep is needed for a Working job.*
- **The refusal went nowhere.** The Stages hook wrote it to the page-global `error` (Jobs map quirk #7), which the Stages board never renders — only the Create partial invoice modal shows it — and the Ready to Bill confirm (z-index 60) sat over the board anyway. So: both boxes ticked, Confirm blue and enabled, click, nothing. The Dashboard twin toasted; the Stages twin did not.

## The rule

- **`billedInvoicePrepNeededForReadyToBill(jobStatus)`** (`src/lib/voidStripeInvoiceForRevert.ts`) is true only for `'billed'`. The sweep now reads `jobs_ledger.status` first and is a no-op success for any other status — including a job it cannot read (never delete when unsure; `update_job_status` then gives its own answer).
- The Stages hook **toasts** the refusal (`showToast(message, 'error')`, z-index 9999, above the confirm) instead of `setError`; the confirm stays open so the user can Cancel or fix and retry.
- The Dashboard's Billing Pipeline send-back (Billed → Ready to Bill) and the sub Collect Payment send-back are untouched: the job is in Billed there, the sweep runs as before.

## Verify

- `src/lib/voidStripeInvoiceForRevert.test.ts`: the pure helper; a Working job reads the job only (no invoice read, no edge call, no RPC) and succeeds; an unreadable job is left alone, a failed job read is the answer; the Billed sweep tests unchanged in substance, now seeded with a Billed job.
- Live: Pipeline → Working → a job with a billed line → **Ready to Bill** → tick both → Confirm moves the job; open its Bill tab — the billed line and its payment are still there.

Help guide `ready-to-bill-pipeline.md` ("Billed a stage and the work continues?") says the forward move keeps the bill.
