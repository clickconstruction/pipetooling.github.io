## Is this the best we can do?

- The doors are the Contract window's, byte for byte on the wire: the same edge function and mode, the same link rule, the same filing path. An agreement chased from the deck reads on the window, the sweep and the sign page as one chased from the window.
- Honest about what a chase does: resending does not clear the line, and the trail says so instead of pretending the row is done.
- Cut: a call note (the job has no contract note to write it to; a note that lands nowhere would be a lie on the trail) and *Not needed after all* (that is Void on the window, a decision, not a fill-in).
- Not pressed live: the one agreement out a week on prod is a real customer's. The resend and the filing ran through the injected io; the deck was looked at live on the real row as the dev.
