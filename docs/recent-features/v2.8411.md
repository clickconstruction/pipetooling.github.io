# v2.8411 — Dev seat: the mail tag and the trip name the roster row's id (2026-11-23)

The three lines v2.8316 saw and left, found again by the docs catch-up pass of 2026-11-23, and the copies that stood beside them. `dev-mcp` 0.131.0, and `gmail-mine` for one sentence. Words only: no migration, no new verb, no screen.

## What was wrong

Two of the seat's plans take a person by `person_id`, and both mean the roster row's `people.id`:

- `plan_mail_tag` with `tag.person`. `gmail-mine`'s person tag runs `admin.from('people').select('id, name').eq('id', …)` and refuses when no row answers.
- `plan_trip` with `people[].person_id`. `prepareTripWrite` reads the ids it was given from `people` and refuses one that names nobody on the roster.

Their words sent a caller to `find_person` for that id. `find_person` reads `users` and answers a login's `users.id`. So a caller who did as the words said was refused, and the refusal sent them back to the same verb.

Six places said it:

- `plan_mail_tag`'s description: *tag.person: { person_id } (find_person)*;
- the refusal for a `tag.person.person_id` that is no uuid: *a people.id (find_person)*;
- `gmail-mine`'s refusal for an id no roster row holds: *No person has that id (find_person).*;
- the trip's refusal for a `person_id` that is no uuid: *a people.id (find_person)*;
- the trip's refusal for one that names nobody: *find_person gives a people.id*;
- the trip plan's note on a name alone: *Pass person_id (find_person)*.

The mail-search skill said it twice more.

## What changed

Each now reads as v2.8316 left `plan_hr_entry` and `plan_person_doc`, in the same words.

The description and the note (`supabase/functions/dev-mcp/index.ts`, `_shared/devMcpTrips.ts`): *the people.id get_case_brief answers in person.id; not find_person's users.id*.

The four refusals (`_shared/devMcpMailTag.ts`, `_shared/devMcpTrips.ts`, `gmail-mine/index.ts`): *the roster row's people.id, as get_case_brief and get_person_file answer it in person.id; find_person answers a login's users.id, which this door refuses*. The two trip refusals keep their last sentence, *Leave it out and the roster is searched by name.*

A trip's `user_id` still reads *a users.id (find_person)*. That one is right: the verb answers exactly that id.

`.claude/skills/mail-search/SKILL.md` is corrected in its two lines.

Nothing else moves. What each verb plans and writes is as it was. A plan made before the deploy still applies after it: the hash covers the payload and the dry run's reply, never the words of a refusal or a description. The trip plan's note is in the reply but outside the hash.

The server goes from 0.129.0 to 0.131.0. 0.130.0 was already taken by another open change.
