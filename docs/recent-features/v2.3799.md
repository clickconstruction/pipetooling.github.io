# v2.3799 — Lien notices due sit on the Pipeline's money strip (2026-09-24)

Punch list **#34** (*Lien desk doors on the Pipeline tab*), asked 2026-09-22 and built on the owner's word 2026-09-24. Client only, no migration. The to-do is retired with this PR.

## What the owner looked for

The owner asked whether the Lien desk had a door on the Pipeline. It had three — the ⋯ Pipeline tools menu's first row, the Collections header's ⏱ button, and the jump strip's ☰ Section tools menu — but not where he looked: the ☰ listed it as its thirteenth row under a *Collections* heading, and *Today's Money Opportunities*, the strip that already carries *Bill the finished work*, *Chase the 90+ tail* and *Allocate deposits*, said nothing about the most time-boxed money item the office has.

## The change

- **[`lienDeskMoneyCard.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDeskMoneyCard.ts)** (new, tested) — `buildLienDeskMoneyCard(summary, todayYmd)` folds the desk's own `LienDeskNeedsYou` into one card: **count** = the office's drafting piles (to draft + needs the owner) plus the master's awaiting pile; **dollars** summed the same way; **the earliest window** = the sooner of the two piles' earliest deadlines. The claim reads *5 lien notices due · $28,987 — the earliest by Oct 3*; the why line names the day's distance (*In 9 days: 2 notices under Dudley Mason and RMC* when the office's `next` deadline is the earliest, else *the first window closes*), then the piles (*2 to draft · 1 waiting on the owner of record · 2 awaiting approval · 4 approved for the run*). Tone follows the Dashboard's lien card: **red** inside seven days (with *mail by Oct 3 or the lien right on that work is gone*), **amber** inside fourteen, gray otherwise. Null when nothing is due — approved-and-waiting alone is the run's business, not a notice due.
- **[`PipelineMoneyOpportunities.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/PipelineMoneyOpportunities.tsx)** — `lienNotices` + `onOpenLienDesk` props; the card sits right after the system moves, badge = the count, `data-testid="pipeline-lien-notices-card"`. It counts toward the strip's not-clean state, so a closing window alone keeps the "pipeline is clean" line off. Quickfill's Jobs Cleanup station renders the same component without the props — its Needs you section already carries the desk's cards.
- **[`PipelineOverview.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/PipelineOverview.tsx)** passes both through; **[`JobsStagesTab.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsStagesTab.tsx)** memoises the card from the `useLienDeskData` summary it already holds (light mode is enough) and opens the desk with `setLienDesk({ jobId: null, kind: 'notice' })` after clearing the search, like every other move button.
- **[`stagesSectionToolsMenu.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/stagesSectionToolsMenu.ts)** — the `lien-desk` row leads the *Pipeline* group (the same gate as before: the Accounts Receivable roles) and the one-row *Collections* group is gone; the desk reads the whole board, and the ⋯ Pipeline tools menu already listed it first. The kernel test's two position-based *Recently added* assertions look the row up by key now.
- Guides *send lien notices from the Lien desk* (the doors list) and *read the Pipeline money view* (a *Lien notices due* section); GLOSSARY's Lien desk doors sentence.
