# v2.4493 — AIA G702-G703: an offer to drop retainage to 5% past halfway (2026-10-04)

The owner, 2026-10-04: retainage *"is usually 10% but can sometimes go to 5% after 50% complete"*, and, asked what the 5% covers: *"When it drops to five percent, it covers everything to date."* Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## The change

- [`aiaPayApplications.ts`](../../src/lib/aiaPayApplications.ts) `retainageDropOffer(values)`: null unless the form's retainage is above `AIA_REDUCED_RETAINAGE_PERCENT` (5) and the line's percent complete (total completed and stored over the scheduled value) is above `AIA_RETAINAGE_DROP_AFTER` (0.5). Otherwise the percent complete, the retainage held at the form's percent, the retainage held at 5% over everything to date, and what the payment due gains (both from `buildAiaPreview`, so the offer and the paper cannot disagree).
- [`AiaG702G703Modal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/AiaG702G703Modal.tsx): under **Retainage %**, an amber line — *This job is 60% complete. Past 50% the contract may drop retainage to 5% of everything to date. Held would go from $2,910.00 to $1,455.00, and $1,455.00 more would be due.* — with **Use 5%**, which sets the field to 5. The workbook already applies its one percent to the total to date, so nothing else changes.

It is an offer: "sometimes". Nothing is applied until the button is pressed, and the next application carries whatever percent was saved.
