## The change

- **The rough while we bid** (G-45, [`GcRoughSchedule.tsx`](../../src/components/gc/GcRoughSchedule.tsx)): a job still bidding shows it in its Schedule window in place of the first draft. It has the start we assume, **Draw a rough schedule** with **Start from a template** (12a's pick), then one row per stage with its days, its dates and its bar, the dates to meet, and the weeks to build. A stage's own days take **Redraw**. Once our bid goes in it locks and reads the weeks as they went. Read only for someone who may not move a bar.
- The draw goes through [`roughPress`](../../src/lib/gc/schedule/scheduleWindow.ts), the prototype reducer's `setRough` as a kernel. It keeps only the whole stage lengths that differ from the usual. A template picked copies its lines, none drops them, and a redraw with no pick keeps the rough's copy. It is refused on a lost job, once our bid went in, past bidding and with no start (`roughRefusal`). The rough is a plain upsert (`setRoughSchedule`).
- **Kept at the Board's presses** (call 5): after **We sent our bid** and **We won this** resolve, [`keepRoughAt`](../../src/lib/gc/scheduleIo.ts) reads the job's rough alone, lays it on the board's job and keeps it (`roughToKeep`). It writes only the four kept columns and only while none are kept, so a keep never rewrites who drew the rough. gc 2's conditions, each met:
  - **After the press, never into it.** The keep starts once `markGcBidSent` or `markGcWon` and the projects' reload resolve, and it never throws: `keepRoughAt` answers null or one line. The outcome's own words and the board's reload come first; the line (`ROUGH_NOT_KEPT`) sits beside the outcome strip and replaces neither.
  - **No write when nothing changed.** A second **We sent our bid**, or **We won this** after the bid's keep, does what `keepRough` says: `roughToKeep` answers null once the rough is kept, and nothing is written.
  - **A small hunk.** In `GcProjects.tsx` the keep is one line inside each of the two outcome handlers. Beside them are the line's state and its one render under `GcProjectOutcome`, and Our number's read (call 6), each its own small hunk.
- **Keep the weeks as sent** (gc 4's note, `GcRoughKeep`, `roughKeepPress`): on the locked rough and on a won job's first draft card, while the weeks are not kept. It keeps them with the day our bid went, never today (`gc_mark_won` sets that day to the day we won when no bid was marked sent).
- **Weeks to build on Our number** (call 6, gc 2's nod, [`GcOurNumber.tsx`](../../src/components/gc/GcOurNumber.tsx)): the page reads the open job's rough for the money team alone (`loadRough`, `rows.ts`'s new `roughFromRow`) and lays it on the job. The card adds, after its own rows, **Weeks to build**, the rough's sentence, and *For the proposal: We will build {job} in {n} weeks from the day work starts.* with **Copy**. Without a rough while we bid it says *not drawn*.
- Ported from the prototype's `GcRoughSchedule.tsx` and `GcNumberTab`'s weeks with their words, each press a callback.
