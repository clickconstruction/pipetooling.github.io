# v2.8184 — Papers before a job opens: the office sets a job's settings for a paper (2026-11-21)

Punch list #389, step 4 of eight, its third part of three: **the office's settings page on a job.** The ground came in v2.8182 and is pushed. The Contract Book took the lines the page shows in v2.8183. No migration here, and nothing to deploy. The types are folded for this step's names alone.

It is built to Taunya's four answers of 2026-11-21 (the card, settled 10 to 13): the whole walked flow, the flow first and one page after, the notice's box once for a paper on a job, and any change after a signature.

## Where it is

A job's **Documents** tab, **Papers before this job opens**. Before this, **Add a paper** showed a paper built from settings in grey and held it. Now it is offered with the words *Built from settings. Pick it to set this job's settings.*

## The first time: the walked flow

Picking such a paper opens the settings page. It is the flow Taunya shaped in the Document builder ([#404](../../to-dos/document-builder/README.md)), the same component, fed from the Contract Book entry's settings list (`flowDefFromBook`, `lib/contracts/jobPaperFlow.ts`):

1. **The notice**, with its box. A list with no notice opens on the basis.
2. **The basis**, the level the whole paper should express.
3. **The note on the real site.**
4. **One page a topic**: what the level means, *What puts a person in breach*, *What the site must have*, and the slider.
5. **Review.** The blanks no choice fills are typed here for this job. The press, **Require this paper**, requires it with its picks, its basis and the tick.

A level's stop on a topic is the choice that level picks for the setting, and Off is the choice that leaves the topic out. So the flow's answer reads straight back into the job's picks (`picksFromFlow`).

The paper joins the job at that press. It does not wait for the section's counted button, since its settings are checked whole by the database as it is required.

## After that: one page

A required paper built from settings reads *Built from this job's settings* on its row, with a **Settings** press. It opens one page: the basis, every topic's slider with the basis marked on it, and the typed blanks. The button says what it will do before it is pressed (`changeWords` over the kernel's `settingsChangePlan`):

- **Save the settings**, in place, while nobody has signed.
- **Save, and ask 3 people to sign again**, once someone has. The change may tighten or loosen.
- **Save the basis**, when only the basis moved. It marks the page and never the paper, so nobody signs again.

The notice's box is not asked a second time.

## What it will not do

- **A real job and an unread paper.** The page says so at the top, in the database's own words, and holds the last press. A ZZ Test job takes any revision.
- **A list with no levels** cannot be walked, so it is set on the one page the first time too, with the notice's box.
- **A press outside the window** closes nothing, so a stray press does not lose a walk. It closes on **Close** and on Escape.

## Presses and sliders sized for a finger

Taunya asked that the buttons be easy to press and the sliders big enough to use. Measured at 375 px and at 1100 px before this shipped, then changed:

- **The slider** is a 44 px band with an 8 px track and a 30 px handle. It was 32, 6 and 22. A sideways drag moves the handle and an up or down drag still scrolls the page.
- **A stop's name** under the slider is a press at least 44 px wide, 56 px for the middle stops. *Off* was 19 px wide and *Strict* 32 px. The names keep their places and do not overlap at phone width.
- **A dash of the step bar** takes a 44 px tall press and still draws 24 px of the bar.
- **Close**, the page's own button and every field on the one page are 44 px tall or more.

The slider and the step bar are the Document builder's, so that tab gets the same sizes.

One thing is not a full press: a dash of the step bar is 38 px wide at phone width with seven steps, and narrower with more. **Back** and **Next** are the way through, at 44 px.

## The flow, shared

`DocumentBuilderFlow` takes an optional `host`: where the walk starts, a listener for the answer, the review's press, and something to draw under the review. With no host it is exactly as it was, and the Document builder passes none.

The flow still draws a clause as grey bars, and its topic pages still end on the Document builder's line that the words are placeholders. On a job that line is true today, since no wording has been read by an attorney. It will need its own words once one has.
