# v2.7303 — Materials on site: retire every box on a job with one press (2026-11-15)

Punch list #209, the owner's ask of 2026-11-14: should the app let him archive a job's materials? The answer is one press on the job's Materials page that retires every empty box of the job at once, under the batch rule (`CLAUDE.md` → Do what is ready, never all or nothing). Client only: no migration, no edge function. The map: [`MATERIALS_ON_SITE_ARCHITECTURE.md`](../MATERIALS_ON_SITE_ARCHITECTURE.md) → The writes and their rules, `retireContainer`.

## Before

A box left a job one at a time: the phone's **Retire G4** under Done with this box, or the dev seat's `plan_container` with action retire. A job whose cages had all gone back to the yard took one press a box, and the office page had no Retire at all, only the Retired fold's Bring back.

## What changed

- **`lib/materials/retireAll.ts`** (new, pure): `retireAllPlan` sorts a job's live boxes into the empty ones that go and the ones with parts on hand, each held with its reason (`heldWords`: *G12 holds 24 toilets.*, or *G12 holds 27 pieces on 2 lines.*); a line with a Can't find or a shortfall and nothing left does not hold a box, as the phone's Retire does not; Unsorted and retired boxes are in neither list. `retireAllWords` gives the press its words through `batchWords` (*Retire 2 empty boxes · 1 holds parts ▸* with the foot; *Retire 2 empty boxes* alone; dead as *Retire ▸* with *ZZ1 still holds parts. Move them or mark them used first.*). `retireAllConfirmWords` and `retireAllConfirmLabel` are the confirm window's list and its verb with the count; `retireAllOutcomeWords` the toast through `batchOutcomeWords`, the held boxes named and each failed one with its reason; `retireAllUndoneWords` and `retireAllUndoHeldWords` the words after Undo.
- **`JobsMaterialsTab.tsx`**: the row under the On site section's boxes, above the Retired fold, drawn while the job has a live box. The press opens the app's confirm window (`confirmLabel` carries the verb and the count), then calls `retireContainer` once a box, keeps going past one that throws and names it afterwards, and shows the outcome on an action toast whose **Undo** stays `UNDO_RETIRE_MS` (a minute) and brings every retired box back through `unretireContainer` behind `unretireVerdict`, each one refused named with the verdict's words. A write the office refuses later comes back through the store's own toast and re-read, as every on-site press's does.
- **The guide** *see what is on every job site and what walked away* gains **Retire every box on a job** above Bring back a retired box.
- **The map's** `retireContainer` sentence names the press.

## Is this the best we can do?

The one rule a single Retire keeps, no box with parts leaves the job, holds here box by box, so the press can never hide material: the box with parts is named beside the press before anything is pressed, and again in the window. The writes stay the phone's own `retireContainer`, one box a call through the outbox, so nothing new reaches the database and a refusal is said the way every press's is. A job with no live box leaves the Materials desk by itself once the press has run, which is what the owner asked for in asking to archive.
