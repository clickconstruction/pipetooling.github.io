# v2.8029 — Pay: the Employment tab and the pay guides name a salaried person's own week (2026-11-20)

A docs catch-up pass on 2026-11-20 reported that Draft Payroll's chip still printed *salary 8h* for everyone. It does not. The chip has read the person's own week since v2.7829 (`salaryFlatHoursShortChip`, handed the week by `People.tsx` through `salaryWeek`). What still printed the old flat rule was the Employment tab's Pay setup card and four help guides. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

- **The Employment tab.** People → Pay → Employment → **Pay setup** said *Salaried people are paid a flat 8 hours per weekday* and, beside the switch, *credited 8 hours on weekdays, 0 on weekends*. Since v2.7864 a person's desk can set a week of any shape, and since v2.7829 and v2.7865 pay and cost credit each day its hours in that week. So the card was false for anyone on a set week that is not the default.
- **The guides.** `run-the-pay-week`, `manage-employment`, `hire-someone` and `see-everyones-pay-setup` each stated the flat 8-hour weekday as the rule.
- **The chip had no test through the window.** `draftPayrollBars.test.ts` held the kernel, but nothing proved `DraftPayrollModal` passes the week to it.

## What ships

**The kernel.** `salariedCreditSentence(week)` in `src/lib/people/salariedWeekLine.ts`, pure: *credited 8 h Mon to Fri on all pay and cost screens, whatever the clock says.* The week is `salariedWeekWords` of the person's own week, marked with `val` so the card draws it apart from the words. A week with no paid days reads *credited nothing: their set week has no paid days.*

**The card.** The line beside **Salaried** draws that sentence with `ValueSentence` from `salariedWeekOf(cfg)`. An hourly person reads the week the switch would turn on, since the switch leaves the stored week alone (`usePayConfig` saves without the column). The card's first paragraph says a salaried person is paid their set week, and that a week that is not 8 hours on weekdays is set on their desk under **Edit pay setup**.

**The guides.**

- `run-the-pay-week`: the small line under a salaried figure names the set week's hours, *salary 8h*, *salary 6h* or *salary 36h wk*.
- `manage-employment`: the Salaried chip, the dates example, **Hourly wage**, **Salaried** and **Record hours anyway** speak of the set week, 8 hours on weekdays by default, with a link to the desk guide for setting another.
- `hire-someone`: the form starts every salaried hire on 8 hours on weekdays, which is true of that form (`hireWrites` names no week), and the desk sets another afterwards.
- `see-everyones-pay-setup`: **Salary** pays the person's set week, and the tick leaves the week as it is.

**The Glossary.** The Draft Payroll entry names the salaried chip and its three readings.

## What does not change

No pay, cost or report moves. The chip, its kernel and its hover are as v2.7829 left them. The Hire form's own label, *a flat 8-hour day, weekdays*, stays, since that form only makes the default week.

## Tests

- `salariedWeekLine.test.ts`: the default week reads as the card always said, a short Friday, four tens and a Tuesday to Saturday week read as their own days, the week is the one marked value, a week of zeros says so.
- `DraftPayrollModal.render.test.tsx`: a salaried person on a short Friday reads *salary 36h wk* on their own row and a 6-hour week reads *salary 6h*, with no *salary 8h* drawn.
- `helpGuidePlainWords`, `helpGuideContent` and `helpGuideLinks.guides` hold the four guides.

## Left for later

- The Pay lens's **Workday…** press still titles itself *The salaried 8-hour day*. The workday template keeps its own length until it reads the week, so the words wait for that change.
- `docs/SALARY_CLOCK_SESSIONS.md` still opens `is_salary` with the flat 8/0 credit. The docs catch-up PR owns that file.
