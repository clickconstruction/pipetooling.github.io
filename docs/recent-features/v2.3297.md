## Data

- `job_budgets` — one row per job: `kind` `bid` | `typed`, the bid and version it came from, labor hours / rate / $, materials, subs, other (equipment · permits · waste · other · driving · travel), `total_direct_usd`, a `completeness` report, `taken_at` / `taken_by`, a note. Reads mirror the job; writes = dev · assistant-like · master technician on a job they can read. Both read-only training-mode blocks re-applied.
- `bid_estimate_breakdown(p_bid_id)` — the single-bid form of `bid_pricing_history`'s math with the Labor refresh's rules: task · sub · per-100-ft rows (v2.3291), estimator time and team labor as facts not cost (v2.3294), materials from the stage POs else the base takeoff's rough lines, driving and travel with the tab's defaults; plus the completeness report the Labor tab's head reads (hours on 90 % of rows and a rate → usable).
- `snapshot_job_budget_from_bid(p_job_id, p_bid_id)` — link (when unset) + snapshot in one transaction; refresh = the same call. SECURITY INVOKER: the caller's `jobs_ledger` UPDATE policy decides who may link, and the existing trigger marks the bid `started_or_complete`.
- `set_typed_job_budget(…)` / `clear_job_budget(p_job_id)` — the typed path and the way back to the assumption.
- `suggest_bids_for_job(p_job_id)` — at most three candidates, ranked 1 price equal to a bid value / agreed value (± $1) · 2 same GC and won · 3 same address prefix; with `has_estimate`, `estimate_hours`, `linked_jobs`. Nothing auto-links (the 15 exact-value matches include two-bid ambiguities).

## Kernel

[`src/lib/jobs/jobBudget.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobBudget.ts) (+9 tests): `resolveJobBudget({ row, priceUsd, targetMarginPct })` → `{ source: bid | typed | assumed, glyph ◆ ✎ ≈, directUsd, components, completeness, partial }`; `budgetForBurn` (what `resolveJobBurnBudget` takes as `bidEstimateUsd`); `budgetCompletenessUsable` (the shared rule); `componentBurn` (used vs budget vs % done, at-completion at today's pace, over) and `budgetWhyWords` — the sentence the card prints ("Materials is $6,700 over the estimate with 23 % of the work left, and labor is 9 points ahead of progress").
