# v2.8137 — Before we start: on the line of time, the days they moved stay on the row (2026-11-20)

Punch list #382, from the audit of PR 11 (v2.8111). Client only: `timeLine.ts`, `ScheduleTimeLine.tsx` and four `scLine*` rules. No migration, no edge function.

## The fault: a wrong number on a phone

The red *moved N days* was written beside the row's marks: after the last one, or before the first when the last mark was past 70% of the line (`TIME_LINE_MOVED_FLIPS_AT`). *Before the first* has room only when the first mark is far from the left edge.

The stage that has moved the most is the one whose marks run the whole line: the first day they gave is near today, and the newest is past our days at the far edge. Its words were hung to the left of a mark that sits at the left edge.

Measured on the real component in headless Chrome, with made-up stages:

| Width | The row | Where the words fell |
|---|---|---|
| 320 px | one stage, moved 32 days | 55 px off the left of the screen: *moved 32 days* read *2 days* |
| 390 px | the same | 51 px off the screen |
| 390 px | two stages, the later moved 32 days | 46 px off the screen |
| 320 px | the kernel test's own stage, moved 3 days | 30 px left of the track |
| 760 px | one stage, moved 32 days | 70 px left of the track, over the stage's name and the today line |
| 1400 px | the same | 33 px left of the track, over the name |

A share of the span cannot promise room in pixels, so no rule that picks a side can hold at every width.

## The fix

The words are no longer beside the marks. They sit on a line of their own under them, on rows that moved, and that row's track is 42 px tall in place of 26.

- `movedAt` is one share: the day they say ready now, the mark that moved.
- `movedSlide(at)` places the words at `left: at%` and slides them back by `translateX(-at%)` of their own width. Their left edge is the track's at 0, their right edge is the track's at 100, and between the two they sit under the solid diamond.
- So they are inside the track wherever the marks fall and whatever the width, and they cover no mark.

After, the same six cases: 0 px left of the track and 0 px off the screen in each.

## Checked

`timeLine.test.ts`: the anchor on every row that moved, a row that runs the whole line, nothing when nothing moved, and `movedSlide` at 0, between and 100. The Schedule look's render test reads the slide off each row. The pictures are a scratch page that mounts the real `ScheduleTimeLine` over the real `timeLine` with the app's own stylesheet. The wrapper and the stages are made up.
