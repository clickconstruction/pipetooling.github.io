# v2.7867 — Trips: put an Extra trip on a bill from the job's Bill tab (2026-11-18)

## Why

The owner asked on 2026-11-18 where a trip can be made from: *"I think that we should not just be able to create trips in developments, but also in the job bill modal"*. A trip could be made in six places and none of them was the Bill tab. More to the point, an Extra trip that nothing had billed was visible only on its site's Development page, and the office makes a bill in the Job window. He agreed to keep it to jobs in a development, and on the same day said to build it with the three smaller fixes.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/trips/jobBillTrips.ts`**, the kernel, pure. `jobBillTrips` says which of a site's trips a job's Bill tab shows: Extra, holding no line, and naming this job or no job at all. A trip that names other jobs alone is theirs. A trip that has gone out has the press, for the four roles that may change a job's lines. A planned one is listed with why it has none. Nothing is shown while the lines are not read, since a trip that holds a line must not be offered again.
- **`src/hooks/useJobBillTrips.ts`**: the site's trips and the lines its Extra trips hold, read together when the strip mounts. The roster is read only once a new trip is being made.
- **`src/components/trips/JobBillTrips.tsx`**: the strip, **Extra trips not on a bill**, with its count. Each trip has **Put on a bill**, which opens `TripBillLineWindow` with this job as its one job. **+ Trip** opens `TripSheet` with the job picked and the billing on Extra. With nothing waiting it says *Nothing waiting on a bill.* and keeps **+ Trip**. A read that failed says so.
- **`JobFormModal`** draws the strip under `JobFormFixturesSection`, inside the same lines block, on an embedded edit of a job that is in a development, for the roles that read every trip (`seesJobBillTrips`).
  - A line the strip adds is read into the form (`rehydrateFixturesFromDb`) and ticked once the Bill tab lists it (`tripLinePick`), and the form scrolls to **Bill the 1 picked**. The scroll is one function now, shared with the window opened with a line picked.
  - The strip reads again each time the form saves its lines (`linesSaved`), so a trip whose line was just removed there is offered again.
- **Docs**: the guide gains *Put a trip on a bill from the job*. `docs/GLOSSARY.md`, `docs/ACCESS_CONTROL.md` and the Job form map name the strip.

## Decisions

- **The money end first.** The strip leads with the trips that are owed a bill, because a forgotten Extra trip is lost money. **+ Trip** is beside it, as he asked.
- **The sheet stays whole.** A trip made here still says who went and which days. **+ Add line item** is the press for a line that is not a trip.
- **Jobs in a development only**, as he agreed. A job with no development has no Trips look to see, change or delete a trip in.
- **No door to Change orders here.** On a job billed from the GC desk the window says why and who writes the change order.
- **No new database work.** The press is `trip_put_on_job_line`, as on the site's page.

## Left as it is

- The Job tab's *Trips on this job* line still shows only once a trip names the job.
- The strip costs one small read of the site's trips, and one of their lines, each time the Bill tab mounts on a development job.

## Tests

- `JobBillTrips.render.test.tsx`: the list, the press through the window to the form's callback, a refusal, **+ Trip** through the sheet to the save, nothing waiting, a trip that holds a line, a primary, a failed read, and the read after the form saves.
