# v2.5950 — Friday's Collect scorecard starts the week at midnight Central (2026-10-27)

The Collect desk (punch list #177, `src/lib/bridge/collect/`) read the day of a Payment Chase touch from its UTC timestamp, so a call recorded after 7 PM Central counted as the next day. This PR set out to fix the six places that did it. The app-wide sweep v2.5956 (#6277, `claude/utc-day-slice-sweep`) landed first with the same six fixes and its own evening tests, so this PR keeps what that sweep did not cover.

- **Friday's reads start at midnight Central** (`loadCollectScorecard.ts`): the lien filings were read from midnight UTC on the call's day, which is 7 PM Central the evening before, and the notices drafted, signed or mailed were compared with that boundary as strings. The read now starts at `startOfYmdInAppTzMs(calledOn)` (falling back to the old boundary for a malformed day), and the notices compare as instants, so a `+00:00` stamp exactly at midnight Central counts.
- **Three more evening cases** beside v2.5956's: a payment on the day of an evening call counts as after the call (`collectScorecard.test.ts`), and an evening dispute and a winter-time touch read on Chicago's day (`collectWords.test.ts`). A new `loadCollectScorecard.test.ts` holds the boundary in summer and winter time, and leaves a notice from the evening before the call out.
- **The edge functions reference's counts** (`docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`): 167 functions since `bridge-weekly-sheet` (v2.5883), and the copy of the kernels' line count after v2.5956.
- No migration, no edge function, no guide. The copy of the kernels is unchanged: `loadCollectScorecard.ts` is not in it.

## Verify

On the Collect desk with a call recorded this week, a lien notice signed after 7 PM the evening before the call is not counted in Friday's *signed* line.
