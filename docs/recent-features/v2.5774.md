# v2.5774 — Quickfill: Fill them in, a deck that clears lost bids one at a time (2026-10-23)

Punch list #145, PR 2 of 5 (`to-dos/needs-you-in-quickfill/`, the owner's yes on the mock-up, call 2 taken as recommended: a third view on the station, not four new stations). The Needs you card lists lines; four of them are cleared by typing a few fields per row, and each was a door to a different page. This PR builds the shell and the first kind, the lost bids with no reason (60 rows, $5.9M unexplained on the day it was asked).

- **The shell** ([`fillIn.ts`](../../src/lib/needsYou/fillIn.ts), [`fillIn.test.ts`](../../src/lib/needsYou/fillIn.test.ts)): the four kinds and the line each sits behind (`FILL_IN_KIND_ITEM`), the kinds built so far (`FILL_IN_KINDS_BUILT`, grows a kind per PR), rows ordered by their line's rank then the kind's own order, the walk order with skipped rows at the back (the Walk view's own `needsYouWalkOrder`), the words: *3 of 12 · 2 saved*, *1 of 6 lost bids*, *That is every row. 10 saved, 2 skipped.*, the trail's one-entry-per-row rule.
- **The lost-bid kind** ([`fillInLostBids.ts`](../../src/lib/needsYou/fillInLostBids.ts), [its test](../../src/lib/needsYou/fillInLostBids.test.ts)): the rows are the Why we lost lens's rule (lost, no `loss_category`, not adopted), biggest value first, titled as the Needs you lines say a bid (*b412 Oak Street clinic · Ridgeway Builders · $84,300*); *what the app knows* in short sentences (marked lost when, sent when, last contact or none since it went out, a note already on it); the fields (a reason chip, what they said, who you talked to); the writes: the lens's own patch (`loss_category`, `loss_reason`), and one `bids_submission_entries` row only when a name was given, since that stamps the bid's last contact. [`fillInLostBidsIo.ts`](../../src/lib/needsYou/fillInLostBidsIo.ts) reads and writes; Undo puts the bid back as it was and removes the entry. A multi-GC bid's per-packet reason stays the lens's job; the deck writes the bid's reason, which is what the line counts.
- **The deck** ([`NeedsYouFillInDeck.tsx`](../../src/components/needsYou/NeedsYouFillInDeck.tsx)): the progress bar (saved cells green, the current one in its severity), the row card in the Walk view's tint with the kicker and *1 of 60 lost bids*, the title, the knows list, the kind's fields, **Save and next** (dead until a reason is tapped), **Skip**, **Open the bid ›**; Next and Then under it; the trail with **Undo** on a saved row and **Back to it** on a skipped one; the end card with the counts and **Mark Needs you up to date** / **Back to Cards**. A note in *What they said* suggests a chip (`suggestLossCategoryFromNote`, marked *from the note*) until one is tapped.
- **The card** ([`DashboardNeedsYouCard.tsx`](../../src/components/dashboard/DashboardNeedsYouCard.tsx)): an optional `fillIn` prop, Quickfill's only; with rows, a third chip **Fill them in · N rows** beside Cards and Walk the list, and a line whose rows the deck handles gets **Fill them in** for its button and opens the deck on its kind. A visit's state, never a stored preference. The card's colors moved to [`needsYouPalette.ts`](../../src/lib/dashboard/needsYouPalette.ts) so the deck draws the same rail and tint.
- **The rows** ([`useFillInRows.ts`](../../src/hooks/useFillInRows.ts)): read under the lines' own gates (`needsYouSourceEnabled('lostBids', role, 'quickfill')`); a saved row leaves the list at once and Undo puts it back, so the deck never waits on a reload; `useLostBidNudge` gained `reload`, so the line's count follows each save.
- **Tests**: the two kernels; [`NeedsYouFillInDeck.render.test.tsx`](../../src/components/needsYou/NeedsYouFillInDeck.render.test.tsx) through an injected io (opens on the biggest bid with what it knows, a note suggests and a tap wins, Save writes the patch and the entry and lands the row in the trail and moves on, Undo restores, Skip sends to the back and the trail brings it back, the end counts and the Mark fires, the door opens the bid); the card's and both hosts' smokes unchanged.
- **Guide**: *work the Needs you list on the dashboard* § Fill them in.
- **Docs**: `QUICKFILL_ARCHITECTURE.md` (the `needs-you` station's third view), `DASHBOARD_SECTIONS_ARCHITECTURE.md` (the card's `fillIn` prop), the to-do's status.

## Is this the best we can do?

- The deck is the Walk view with fields in it: same order rule, same tint, same trail idea as the Property kinds station, same Send & next rhythm as the Contract sweep. Nothing new in shape, so an assistant who knows the card knows the deck.
- One shell, a kernel per kind: the next three kinds add a rows kernel, an io and a fields component, and nothing in the shell.
- The write is the lens's own write, so the Why we lost lens, the robots' reference flags and the contact log read a reason from the deck exactly as one from the lens.
- Cut: the keyboard digits the lens offers (1 to 6), the GC's record against us, the estimator's name on the row. Each is one small PR if wanted.
- Not pressed live: prod has no lost bid without a reason on a ZZ test name, so Save was exercised through the injected io, not against prod. The deck was walked live as the dev on 2026-10-23: the chip, the open from the line, the suggestion from a note, Skip and the trail, at desktop and phone width, light and dark.
