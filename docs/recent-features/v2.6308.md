# v2.6308 — Documents: the tab row in the order you use it (2026-11-01)

The Documents page design spike of 2026-11-01, candidate 2: the owner asked whether the tab titles were the right ones, took the assessment, and said make the changes. Client only, no migration and no function.

- **The row.** Search · Estimates · Bids · Jobs · Supply invoices · Sent copies · Photos · Company · Where files go. Search first, since it is the one tab that crosses the four ledgers; the ledgers in the order money moves; what came in and went out; then the office's reference and setup tabs last. The role gates are unchanged (Company for the five roles of the `company_documents` policy, Sent copies for `canReadSentCopies`, Where files go for `canSetWhereFilesGo`).
- **Two renames.** *Bid proposals* → **Bids**, the word the nav, the Glossary and every other page use (a proposal is one paper on a bid, not the record); the Search tab's section heading says Bids too. *Sent* → **Sent copies**, the words the Glossary, the job window's *Sent from this job* and the guide *find a copy of what we sent* already use.
- **One tab gone.** Upload said *Upload coming soon* and nothing else; uploads happen from the phone and through Drive, and since v2.6303 from the job window's Documents tab. `documentsPageTab.ts` drops `upload` from `DocumentsPageTab`; `?tab=upload` reads as an unknown value and lands on Estimates, like any other (`documentsPageTab.test.ts`).
- **Nothing in links changes.** The `?tab=` ids stay (`bid-proposals`, `sent`), so bookmarks and the two in-app links to the Sent copies tab keep working; only the button words moved. The guides *find a copy of what we sent* and *find the current company documents* name the tabs by their new words and places.
