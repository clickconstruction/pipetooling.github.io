# v2.3182 — Job window: a Costs tab between Bill and History; the Job tab keeps a card (2026-09-08)

Client only — no migration, no edge function. Owner call, looking at the Bill tab's tail on a 13-person job the evening v2.3178/v2.3179 shipped: "I don't think it should be in the bill section anymore … its own section, after bill before history called Costs." Agreed and built: **Bill is money in, Costs is money out**, and the Job tab keeps a compact card instead of the duplicate chart.

## What shipped

- **Tab list** — [`jobHistoryTab.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobHistoryTab.ts): `JOB_WINDOW_TABS` = `job · edit · bill · costs · history`; new `jobWindowFormRegionForTab` maps a tab to the embedded form region (`edit` / `bill` / `costs`). Tests updated (+4).
- **Third form region** — [`JobFormModal`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormModal.tsx): `embeddedRegion` gains `'costs'`; the `JobFormLaborCostPanel` (Cost Timeline + Team / Sub Labor lines) and `JobFormPartsCostSection` (the four accordions + **Add other charge**) move out of the Bill display-div into a Costs display-div. Same single form instance, so tab switches still keep state and "Add other charge" still autosaves. The status stepper now renders only on Edit (was: everything but Bill). `onRequestRegion` widens to the three regions. The standalone New Job form is unchanged: with no embedded region, every block still shows.
- **Window** — [`JobWindowModal`](../../src/components/jobs/JobWindowModal.tsx): `embeddedRegion={jobWindowFormRegionForTab(tab)}`, the form tabpanel's `aria-label` follows the region, and the Job pane receives `onRequestTab={setTab}`.
- **The Job tab's Costs card** — [`DetailJobModal`](../../src/components/jobs/DetailJobModal.tsx) in pane mode renders new [`JobDetailCostsSummaryCard`](../../src/components/jobs/JobDetailCostsSummaryCard.tsx) (view-model: kernel [`jobCostsSummaryCard.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobCostsSummaryCard.ts), 4 tests) **instead of** the Parts Cost accordions + `JobChargesTimelineStandalone` + `JobDetailProfitSection`. Lines, one each: **Team labor** (v2.3178 row model: "8.0 h · Malachi · includes 2.0 h awaiting approval"), **Sub labor**, **Parts** (supply + card + tally + other), **Margin before team labor** (the v2.2852 label + tooltip, green/red by sign). Team / Sub / Margin render for the wage roles only (`showJobDetailProfitSection` = dev / master / controller — the same set as `showJobCostBreakdownTeamLabor`); everyone else who sees the materials section gets the Parts line. The whole card is a button → the Costs tab, plus an "Open Costs →" link in its header. Data and gates are the hooks the pane already ran (`useJobMaterialsCostSnapshot`, `useJobDetailSubLaborCost`, `useJobDetailTeamLabor`); nothing new is fetched. **Standalone / read-only Job Detail** (superintendent, estimator, sub-like roles — no window) is unchanged: it keeps its Parts Cost section, chart and Profit band.
- **Opener types** — `initialTab` on `JobFormModalContext` and `JobDetailOpenerBridgeContext` accept `'costs'`. No caller passes it yet; the Job Summary expanded-row buttons were left alone because v2.3180/v2.3181 are mid-flight on that row.

## Verified live (J650 ATI Schertz, dev, port 5179)

- Tabs read `Job · Edit · Bill · Costs · History`; the Job tab shows the card and **no** Cost Timeline; tapping the card selects Costs, which carries the chart, the Team / Sub Labor lines and the accordions; the Bill tab now ends at Payments received with no chart.
- Render tests: the window test asserts the five tabs in order and that `Supply house invoices` is hidden on Bill and shown on Costs while `Remaining to bill` flips the other way; the Job pane's stacked-clicks test still passes.
