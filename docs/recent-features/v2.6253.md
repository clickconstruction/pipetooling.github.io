# v2.6253 — The Bid Board's colour wheel is one dot (2026-10-31)

The owner, on a screenshot of the Bid Board's chip row (2026-10-31): the self-highlight wheel at the right of
*Plumbing: Map · Unsent · Pending · Won · Started · Lost · Health* drew as "a circle inside a circle inside a circle",
and the preference was borderless. The press was a 30 px round button with a `--border-strong` border and a surface
fill, holding a 16 px conic-gradient dot that carried its own surface border and a 1 px `--border-strong` box-shadow
ring: three rings around one dot.

- **`BidBoardSelfHighlightWheel.tsx`**: the outer button keeps its 30 px round hit area but is transparent and
  unbordered, so the tap target and the browser's `:focus-visible` ring are as before; the inner dot loses its surface
  border and its shadow ring and is the conic gradient alone. While the panel is open the button draws a 2 px outline in
  `var(--text-700)` (the theme's ink, never a literal grey, per `check:dead-press`) so the pressed state reads in light
  and dark. The panel the press opens is unchanged.

No migration and no edge function: the client only. Checked in the browser pane on the local dev server in both themes.
