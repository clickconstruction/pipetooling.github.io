# v2.4339 — Lien waiver signing: a shorter checkbox, on one line and centred (2026-10-01)

No migration, no edge function. Grace (2026-10-01, a screenshot of the pad on job 650): the agree line wrapped to two lines and pushed its checkbox to the left edge; she wanted it on one line, shorter, and the checkbox centred.

## What changed

- **The sentence** ([`LienReleaseSignModal`](../../src/components/jobs/LienReleaseSignModal.tsx)): *I, <leader>, have read this release and my drawn signature is as binding as ink.* → **I, <leader>, have read this release and agree to sign it.** (signing your own: *I have read this release and agree to sign it.*). Measured in Chrome at the pad's width (579 px of room at 16 px): the old line needed 612 px with *Malachi Whites*; the new one 437 px, 507 px with a 22-letter name. The leader's **Waivers to sign** seat ([`DashboardLienWaiversToSignModal`](../../src/components/dashboard/DashboardLienWaiversToSignModal.tsx)) takes the same sentence. Since v2.4335 both screens set it under the page (the sign window is 640 px wide, the seat's page column wider), so at the block's 14 px it fits on one line on a desktop.
- **The box** ([`LienWaiverSignAgree`](../../src/components/jobs/LienWaiverSignOnPage.tsx), the agreement under the page since [v2.4335](v2.4335.md)): the row is centred (`justifyContent: center`) and the box sits level with its sentence (`alignItems: center`, the 3 px top nudge dropped). Both waiver screens use this block, so neither needs a switch; the customer estimate / contract pages, the job contract page and the sub portal keep `ContractAcceptSignatureForm` unchanged. (First built as an `agreeAlign` prop on `ContractAcceptSignatureForm`; v2.4335 merged first and moved both waiver screens off that form, so the change was re-applied to the new block and the prop dropped.)
- **Why the words could change**: this checkbox is not the versioned ESIGN consent (`esignConsent.ts`, the customer pages' *I agree to sign electronically*, stored with each signature) — the waiver is signed by our own leader for the company, business to business, and the row stamps `signer_consented_at`. The § 53.284 form text is untouched. The paragraph above the box still states a drawn signature has the force of ink.
