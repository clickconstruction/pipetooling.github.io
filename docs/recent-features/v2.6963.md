# v2.6963 — Builders counts the bids of a customer tied to a name (punch list #307, PR 6's leftovers) (2026-11-11)

PR 6 of #307 (v2.6872) built the answer on a name, kept in `builder_customer_answers`, and asked it under a bid's site permit. Three pieces of that PR were left: Builders' **Our bids** column reading the answers, the same answer on a builder's sheet, and the spec's second grade, *likely*. This is those three. No migration.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/research/builderAnswers.ts`** (new, pure, `builderAnswers.test.ts`): the answers turned to face the names. `answerBook(rows, customers)` groups our customers by keyed name (`customerGroups`, one GC under several customer rows is one customer, as the bid's window reads it) and runs each group's rows through `foldNameAnswers`, the bid side's own fold; it keeps, per name, the customers every one of its permits is tied to, and the customer row the standing answer was written on. `withAnsweredBids(rows, book, bids)` adds a tied customer's bids to a `research_builders` row's `bids`, `won` and `last_bid_on` and records the added part as `by_word`. Left out so nothing counts twice: a customer whose own name is the name (the read's same-name hint counts every bid with it), a bid whose legacy `bids_gc_builders` name is the name (the hint counts it too), and a robot's copy. A tie to one permit ties no bids; a take-back or a No stops the count.
- **`builderAnswersIo.ts`** (new, `builderAnswersIo.test.ts`) and **`useBuilderAnswers`**: one sitting for the list, never a read a row. Every answer and our customers by name (the two reads the *Looks like a customer* worklist already makes: `bidSiteIo` now hands one read to everyone who asks while it is in flight, and drops a held read when an answer is saved), the people who answered once an answer exists, a customer an answer names that the live list leaves out, and the bids of the customers some name is tied to, a chunk of customers a read, a page at a time cut by id.
- **`PermitBuilders`**: the list reads through `withAnsweredBids`, so **Our bids**, the *3 or more permits and never bid* chip and the sheet's *In our books* line all read the answers. The desk column says *through Ridgeway Builders* under a count an answer added to. *Looks like a customer* drops a pair answered since from its list and count (`openLookAlikes`).
- **`BuilderNameTies`** (new), on a builder's sheet as **Our customer under this name**: each customer the name is tied to with whose word it is and when (`wordOfWords`), a customer of the exact same name as *The same name* with nothing to take back, a No as *Not them*, and **Take back** beside each answer, which appends a `taken_back` row on the customer row the answer sits on. Where no customer is tied it says so, with **Tie to a customer**, a search over our customers. A name that files for others offers neither. Every press goes through `recordNameAnswer`, the row the bid's window writes.
- **The likely grade** (`nameAnswers.nameGrade`): one name begins the other word for word, as `nameLikeness` reads *begins*, or the two are a legal word apart. `nameStanding`'s open question carries `grade`, and `questionWords`, `tiePreviewWords`, `answeredWords` and `standingChip` read it: *Same company?* beside *Your customer is …*, the presses **Same company**, **Not them…** and **Not sure**, and a tie that reads *is the same company as*. On a bid the answer still shows the name's other permits first; on a builder's sheet the permits are on the sheet already. The answer writes the same rows.

## Never

The app ties nothing on the likely grade. It only changes what the question says.

## Not built, and why

- The same phone or web domain as evidence for the grade: held, it needs lookups on hand.
- `builders.customer_id` stays as it is, unwritten and not dropped.
- A bid that names the customer as a second GC (`bid_gcs`) is not counted: the column keeps the meaning the read gives it, a bid whose customer is the name's.

## Found in passing, not fixed

`research_builders` counts our bids with a name by joining `bids` to `customers` with no test for a robot's copy, so the same-name part of **Our bids** can count the estimator twins' *ZZ Shadow* and *ZZ Twin* copies (78 of them carry a customer on 2026-11-11). The part this PR adds leaves them out. The fix is a line in that function, in the next migration that touches it.
