# v2.3482 — Put a GC on notice: the cover letter, written once for all (2026-09-15)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. PR 2 of the *Put a GC on notice* train ([`to-dos/gc-on-notice/`](../../to-dos/gc-on-notice/README.md); PR 0 [v2.3469](./v2.3469.md), PR 1 [v2.3470](./v2.3470.md)). Stacked on `claude/gc-on-notice-1-modal`.

## Why

The statutory form is a form. These owners paid the GC in good faith; a plain paragraph that says what happened, what the law asks them to do, and that we release the moment we are paid is what keeps the phone civil. The mock-up's Step 3: one letter for the whole run, edited once, applied to every notice.
