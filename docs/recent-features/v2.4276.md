- **The seat** ([`DashboardLienWaiversToSignModal`](../../src/components/dashboard/DashboardLienWaiversToSignModal.tsx)): rows on the left — the payor (the GC on a sub job, read in one query), the job, the amount, the form as an amber (conditional) or slate (unconditional) chip; the picked waiver's page on the right — title, paragraphs, signature lines, the same rendering the sign modal shows — with the shared `ContractAcceptSignatureForm` under it. *Sign · send to Knight Contracting* signs (one signature per instrument), emails the signed PDF to the payor's address (`resolveLienWaiverRecipient`: the GC's `billing_email` on a sub job, else the customer's) with the job in the subject, refetches the lanes and picks the next row; the last one closes the seat. **Send once signed** is a tick, on by default; unticked, *Sign* alone leaves the row in the office's *Signed — ready to send* lane. - Tests: the Needs You item (count, dollars, gone at zero / disabled / loading), the seat's smoke (list, page, sign → send → next; unticked → no send). Guide *send a GC our lien waiver* gains *Sign several at once*; GLOSSARY amended.

## Design calls

- **A pile gets a seat; one gets a lane.** The inbox lane's *Open & sign* opens a dialog per waiver; the seat keeps the list beside the page so the leader reads each and signs without leaving.
- **Send is the default and a tick, not a rule.** A GC wants the waiver the moment it exists; a leader who wants the office to look first unticks.
- **Sending is a courtesy on top of the signature.** A failed send leaves the row signed and in the office's lane; the toast says *Signed. Not sent — …*.
