# v2.4052 — Pipeline: the stage pills never lose their last word (2026-09-28)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. The owner's Pipeline look pass (item 2 of the mock-up page), after the strip fix v2.4042.

## What was wrong

`StagesStageBar` drew the stage pills in a flex row with `overflow: hidden` and `→` between them. `fitStageChips` (v2.3421) picks the widest tier that fits the measured column, but its budget was a guess: on the live board a three-stage row (① Rough › ② Top Out › ③ Finish) measured 233 px against an estimate of 239, and the column is 208 px. When the measurement was stale or wide (the first paint, before the column settles), tier 0 was chosen and the last pill lost its tail — "Fini".

## What changed

- **`src/lib/jobs/pipelineStageBar.ts`** — the estimator is calibrated on the measured chips: a bold glyph 7 px, a regular one 5.9 px; the chip chrome 28 px (a 3 px inner gap and a 6 px right pad); the separator 10 px (a chevron and two 3 px gaps). The three-stage row now budgets 217 px against a real 213 px — a shade over the truth, on purpose.
- **`src/components/jobs/StagesStageBar.tsx`** — the row is `flex-wrap: wrap` with a 3 px gap and no `overflow: hidden`; `›` in place of `→`; the pill's inner gap and right pad match the estimator. When the estimate is ever wrong the row wraps, it never clips.

## Tests

`pipelineStageBar.test.ts` pins the calibrated budget (217 px for the three short names). The tier walk-down tests are relative to the estimate and pass unchanged.
