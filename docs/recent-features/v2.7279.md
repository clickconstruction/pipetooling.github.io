The guard holds every pick to a scale and a rung the version has.
- **[`src/lib/bids/gcDifficultyRating.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/gcDifficultyRating.ts)**: `newestRatingByCustomer`, `gcDifficultyStanding` (the points counted under the rating's own version, the sentences behind them, and the carry to the published version when it is newer, by the rule of `difficultyCriteria.ts`), the chip's words and title, the rated-by line, the carry line (*Rated under version 1; version 2 is published. A pick from an older version did not carry: its rung is gone.*), the draft rules (a pick and a note) and the history line. `gcDifficultyRatingIo.ts` reads and writes the table and holds every customer's standing for a minute, dropped on a save; `useGcDifficultyStandings` is the hook the chips read.
- **The customer's page → How they are to work with** ([`GcDifficultyCard.tsx`](../../src/components/customers/GcDifficultyCard.tsx), after *Pays in kind*; a commercial customer, or any with a bid or a GC job; the office rates, estimators read): the published scales as `DifficultyScalesPicker` rows, the Paying scale locked with a line saying PR 3 reads it from the bills, *What happened*, the total line and **Save N points** (dead until a pick and a note, and until a pick changes on a rated GC), the rated-by line, *Earlier ratings*. - **The chip** ([`GcDifficultyChip.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/GcDifficultyChip.tsx)): *Rated 4 points* beside the GC's name in the Bid Board's three GC cells (the list row, the opened row's fact, the phone card) and on the Workbench's title line beside *Bids like this*, with the sentences in its title and a press to the card; *Rate them* for the office on a GC with no rating.
- The guide *rate how a GC is to work with* (office and estimators).

## Not changed

- Nothing is priced. The Labor tab, the priced-margin stamp and *Bids like this* are untouched until PR 4.
- A bid that went to several GCs (the in-cell GC lines) draws no chip on the Bid Board: the bid's own GC is the one the chip would name, and those rows name the others.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **Doubted: one chip in three Bid Board cells.** The board is dense, and a *Rate them* link on every unrated GC could read as nagging. Kept it as the canvas drew it, as a quiet link in the GC column, and left it off the rows that list several GCs. If the board grows loud, the first thing to pull is *Rate them* from the list rows, keeping it on the opened row.
- **Doubted: a partial rating.** A person who knows the super but not the office could save one scale. Allowed: an unpicked scale reads *unrated* and counts 0; the alternative, refusing the save, would lose the half they know.
- **Doubted: who may rate before the price list lands.** The office roles (`is_office_staff()`), estimators reading. Masters are office roles here (`master_technician`), so the master who walked the job can rate today from a desk; the phone sheet is PR 5. PR 4 moves the gate to the price list's *who may rate*.
- **Left for Micah, with my pick:** which customers show the card. Every commercial customer, and any customer with a bid or a GC job; a residential customer with neither does not. The other choice is every customer. My pick stands unless he wants it on every page.
- **Left for Micah, with my pick:** the Paying scale's two sentences show on the card locked, with a line that PR 3 reads the point from the bills. The alternative was to hide the scale until PR 3. My pick is to show it, so the card already reads as the canvas drew it.
