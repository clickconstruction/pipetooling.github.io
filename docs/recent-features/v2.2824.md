# v2.2824 — Team feedback on the three bars: the clock-out deck, anonymous crew lane, Feedback tab rebuilt (2026-09-05)

Owner decision (proposal 5d11522c): replace the ten-question Team Feedback wizard with the Ability / Drive / Integrity sliders from Prospects → Team → Review, keep the open words, save the questions somewhere, and make crew ratings **anonymous to everyone but dev**. Team feedback was switched off with three test submissions from March 2026, so nothing but the wording needed preserving.

- **The deck** ([`CrewReviewDeck`](../../src/components/team-feedback/CrewReviewDeck.tsx), replaces `TeamFeedbackWizard` at clock-out and behind the Dashboard **Rate your crew** button): intro (same copy, 30 s auto-dismiss, Not now = 7-day snooze) → one card per teammate → the lead → **Your words** → thanks. A card is the shared `RatingSliders` control with a note per bar; **Save … next** upserts immediately, **Skip** moves on, **◀ Back** keeps drafts. The card names shared jobs and days together. No one to rate → straight to the words card. `preview` prop (dev **Try the deck**) loads real teammates and writes nothing.
- **Who you worked with**: RPC `crew_review_teammates(p_lookback_days, p_extra_user_ids)` (SECURITY DEFINER) — other active accounts with **approved** clock sessions on the same job the same day inside the lookback, most days together first, up to three job labels each; the lead (from the existing `resolveManagerUserIdForFeedback`) is passed as an extra id so they are dealt even with no shared days. Pure deck logic in [`crewReview.ts`](../../src/lib/people/crewReview.ts) (`buildCrewDeck`: skip self, skip anyone already rated this month, lead last; `crewCardContextLine`; drafts → rows; open-prompt parsing; the dev-tab summary and retired-question groups), tested.
- **Storage**: crew ratings are `team_member_reviews` rows with the new **`source`** column (`office` default, `crew`); the unique key becomes (subject, reviewer, month, **source**) so a master can have both an office row and a crew row for the same person. RLS: SELECT office rows for prospects staff as before, **all rows for dev**, own rows for the writer; INSERT/UPDATE crew rows by any signed-in account about someone else; DELETE own or dev. Office reads of crew go only through **`crew_review_aggregates()`** — per subject per month averages + rater count, months with **2+ raters** (dev sees all), never a name or note. Open words → the existing `team_feedback_submissions` open columns plus new `open_anything`; the Likert columns are never written again. `team_feedback_settings` gains `crew_lookback_days`, `open_prompts` (4 headings, null = defaults), `questions_retired_at` (stamped by the migration). `TeamReviewSection` now fetches `source = 'office'` and stamps `source: 'office'` on its upsert (onConflict includes source).
- **People → Feedback / Settings → People & accounts → Team feedback** ([`TeamFeedbackDevSettingsBlock`](../../src/components/team-feedback/TeamFeedbackDevSettingsBlock.tsx), rebuilt): header (Enabled, last words, **Try the deck**) + five pills — **Ratings** (`CrewRatingsPanel`: per person the crew lane over the office lane per dimension, `N crew · M office`, an orange chip when they differ by 15+, crew notes, and a dev-only **Who rated** expander), **Open words** (`OpenWordsPanel`, writer's name dev-only), **Who's due** (the existing eligibility overview), **Settings** (`CrewFeedbackSettingsForm`: cadence, lookback, Dashboard button, intro/thank-you copy, the four open-card headings), **Retired questions** (`RetiredQuestionsPanel`, read-only from the saved columns; repo copy `docs/TEAM_FEEDBACK_RETIRED_QUESTIONS.md`).
- **Deleted**: `TeamFeedbackWizard`, `PeerTeammatePicker`, `TeamFeedbackDevReports`, `TeamFeedbackSettingsSection`, `TeamFeedbackMasterAggregates` (the master-facing Likert averages on Settings — nothing attributed reaches masters now). `teamFeedback.ts` loses `submitTeamFeedback`/peer candidates and gains the deck I/O (`fetchCrewTeammates`, `fetchMyCrewRatedThisMonth`, `upsertCrewReview`, `submitOpenWords`, `markCrewDeckCompleted`, `fetchCrewReviewAggregates`). `list_feedback_peer_candidates` and `team_feedback_aggregates_by_manager` stay in the DB, unused.
- **Still off.** `team_feedback_settings.enabled` is untouched; the deck is dealt only once the owner flips it on. Next PR: the crew lane in Prospects → Team → Review → Reflect (the aggregates RPC already exists for it).
