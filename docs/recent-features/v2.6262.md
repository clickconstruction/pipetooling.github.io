# v2.6262 — Turnaway takes a photo, captioned by the reason (punch list #229, PR 2 of six) (2026-11-01)

Punch list #229, PR 2, on PR 1's hook (v2.6255). A Turnaway files a report and alerts Dispatch for the trip charge, and the locked gate or the empty lot was the one photo the office wanted and could not have. No migration, no edge function.

## What changed

- **`TurnawayModal`** mounts `useReportPhotos` with no stages: the field report's tiles under the reason chips, the camera on a phone for the field roles (`canLeaveJobFieldReport`), the file picker elsewhere, *New photo · 3.8 MB → 290 KB · 1600 px* over the tiles. No chip asks which stage, since a Turnaway is a trip without work. The press reads what it files (`turnawayFileWords`: *File Turnaway · 1 photo*), *Sending 1 photo…* and waits while a shot is on its way, *Filing…* while it files. Cancel and the × drop a sent shot that no report holds.
- **The save** ties each sent shot to the report captioned by the reason (`reportPhotoCaption` from *Site not ready*), so the Drive file reads *2026-11-01 Mateo Ruiz · Site not ready.jpg*; the sweep and the card are as they were.
- **Dispatch's line** (`buildTurnawayDispatchTitle`) ends *· 1 photo on the report* when one rides, so the office opens the report for the proof before the trip charge.
- **Guides**: *take photos for a report* gains **A Turnaway's photo**; *file and review field reports* says the Turnaway form takes one. The Glossary's **Report photos** entry gains its line in the card's closing docs PR, since five PRs amending one line would conflict in turn.
- **Tests**: `turnaway.test.ts` (+2: the title's count, the press's words); `TurnawayModal.photos.render.test.tsx` (new, 1): the camera under the reason, the press's words through a send, the attach captioned by the reason, the dispatch title.

## Decisions

- **No stage chips.** A Turnaway has no stage progress; the photo shows the site, and the reason names it.
- **The Turnaway stays outside the outbox**, as before this PR: it files straight away and alerts Dispatch. The queue PR of this card takes the field report; a Turnaway with no signal fails as it does today, with the shot kept on the form for Retry.
