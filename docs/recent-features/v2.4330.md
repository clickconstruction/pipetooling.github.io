# v2.4330 — Lien waivers: the unconditional waits for a check to clear (2026-10-01)

Punch list **#76** PR 5 of 5 (`to-dos/ar-returned-checks.md`; canvas https://claude.ai/artifact/9qbiCTmbaXnfPVKhFULoCN, board *Your call · the waiver waits*). No migration, no edge function. Independent of PRs 2–4.

## Why

An unconditional lien waiver holds whether or not the check that paid the bill clears. Read against 19 months of prod: every real return came back within six days of posting, and 57 of the last 71 check payments were applied inside that window. `billSettled` read a bill as settled the moment payments covered it, so the waiver cell asked for the unconditional the same day a check landed. The owner's call (2026-10-01): wait **seven days**.

## What changed

- **Kernel** [`checkClearing.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/checkClearing.ts) (+ tests): `CHECK_CLEAR_DAYS = 7`; `isCheckPayment` (*Check*, *Cheque*, Accounts Receivable's *checkDeposit*, *ck*); `billCheckClearsYmd(invoiceId, payments, today)` — the newest check on the bill plus seven days, or null once it has cleared or when no check paid it.
- **The cell** ([`lienWaiverCell.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienWaiverCell.ts) `lienWaiverCellForBill(…, clearsYmd)`): a settled bill whose check is clearing owes no move (`next` null, so nothing is amber) and reads *Unconditional · waits for the check · clears Oct 8*; `clearsYmd` on the cell. Callers pass it: the Bill tab ([`JobFormInvoiceList`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormInvoiceList.tsx)), GC Review ([`JobsGcReviewModal`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsGcReviewModal.tsx)), View bill ([`BillPaperworkCard`](../../src/components/jobs/BillPaperworkCard.tsx)).
- **View bill's waiver row** ([`billPaperworkWaiverRow.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/billPaperworkWaiverRow.ts)): *Paid Oct 1 by check. The unconditional waits for it to clear.* / *It clears about Oct 8. An unconditional waiver holds even if the check comes back.*; the step reads *Unconditional, once it clears*; **Add the unconditional** stays, drawn plain.
- **The window** ([`lienWaiverRelease.ts`](../../src/lib/jobsDocuments/lienWaiverRelease.ts) `pickLienWaiverForBill(job, invoice, today)`): the form stays the unconditional (the money has settled), and the first fact reads *Settled · the check clears about Oct 8*; `clearsYmd` on the pick.
- Guide *send a GC our lien waiver*: one paragraph under *Check the form the bill picked*. GLOSSARY.
