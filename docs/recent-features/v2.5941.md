## Decided here

- **The sheets keep their `stopPropagation` calls.** The plan said fifteen would go; most are the sheets' own panels, which stop a click so it never reaches the sheet's backdrop and must stay. The backdrops' stops cost nothing with no desk backdrop under them, and leaving them avoids a churn of fifteen lines for no behavior.
- **Only the page names the phone.** The desk takes a `layout`, so a test or a future host can stack it at any width.
- **Other to-dos that name `LegalDeskModal.tsx`** in their mock-ups or plans are left as written: they are other work's history, and `git log --follow` reads the rename.
