# v2.8132 — Document builder: two new breach lines are two lines (2026-11-20)

Punch list #404, left by the review of 2026-11-20. No migration and no function.

## What was wrong

On a topic's page of the Sub NDA, with the marks on, **Add a line** under *What puts a person in breach* adds a line that reads *A new line.* until someone types over it. Pressed twice, it made two lines of the same words. The page found each line's place by looking its words up in the level's list (`indexOf`), so both resolved to the first: one React key twice, one path twice. Typing in the second changed the first, and the second's **×** took the first off.

The same matching by words reached across levels. With a new line at Standard and another at Elevated:

- Elevated's new line was drawn grey under *Also applies*, with no field and no **×**, because Standard held the same words.
- Typing over Standard's new line renamed Elevated's too, and taking Standard's off took Elevated's off.

## The fix

- `ownBreachLines(def, key, stop)` in `documentBuilder.ts` answers the lines a level brings, each with its place in the level's whole list. They are the same lines as `breachGroups().own`, in the same order. `DocumentBuilderFlow.tsx` names a line's key, its field's path and its **×** by that place, and no longer looks a line up by its words. Two lines someone typed to the same words are two fields as well.
- `breachGroups` never calls a new line carried. `PAPER_NEW_LINE` moved to `documentBuilder.ts` for it, and `paperEdit.ts` still exports it.
- `withPaperWords` and `withoutPaperLine` do not reach the stricter levels for a new line. **Add a line** writes it on one level alone, so a stricter level's new line is that level's own.

## Tests

- `paperEdit.test.ts`: two new lines on one list have two places, typing at the second's place leaves the first, a new line stays the level's own while the level under it has one, and typing over or removing one leaves a stricter level's.
- `WalkedBuilder.render.test.tsx`: two presses, type in the second, the first still reads *A new line.*, and the second's **×** takes the second. It fails on the flow as it was.

## Not changed

A line someone types to the very words of a line on the level under it becomes a carried line, as before: the same words on two levels are one rule. The site lines and the disclaimer's lines were always named by place.
