# v2.5248 — Messages: the rules for Bill day from the trades (2026-10-12)

This is wave 1 of the Messages build, cluster 7 (GC Reviews helper 7, directed by the lead session GC Review). It adds the kernels of cut 9, *Bill day: From the trades, and the superintendent's walk* (`to-dos/gc-mode/messages/BUILD_PLAN.md` on `spike/gc-mode`). They are lifted from the Messages prototype v1.178: `drawsFor`, `gatesFor`, `gateShorts`, `billSummaryHtml`'s numbers, `linesHtml`'s money, `claimsToCheck`, `askAllNotIn`, `remindDraw`, `askForDraw`, `superText` and `sendBackDraw`'s note. There is no screen, no table and no edge function. Cut 9's SQL (`superintendent_checks`, `bill_day_nothing`, `app_settings.bill_day_cutoff_days`, `gc_sow_lines.where_on_site`) and Money → Bill day → From the trades come in wave 2. The kernels read main's draws (`drawRows.ts` lays them over each statement of work), the waivers and certificates in `ownerBilling.ts`, and the schedule's bars through `billingForecast.ts`. They keep none of their own.

- **`src/lib/gc/billDay/trades.ts`**:
  - `billDayDates` counts back from the owner's pay application for the job. It reads the bill day, the day the application goes (the Monday after when the 25th falls on a weekend), the cut-off (`BILL_DAY_CUTOFF_DAYS`, 6, until the setting lands), the checking days, and the period from the day after last month's cut-off. It also gives the owner's pay-by day and the trades' ten days from approval.
  - `tradeDraws` gives one row per trade with a signed statement of work: `back`, `notin`, `waiting`, `approved` or `closing`. A *Nothing this period* row marks a trade not in, and the gates count that trade as in. Each row carries what it asked (before a back-charge), what we approved, what the schedule says by bill day (`scheduleSays`, the bar's own percent through `forecastPct`), the unconditional waivers still owed (`tradeWaiverChecks`' rule) and its change orders.
  - `billDayGates` gives the seven gates with their words, their shorts for Today and their presses. `canItGo`, `billGroups`, `rowMove`, `tradesSums` and `walkDraws` round it out.
- **`src/lib/gc/billDay/lines.ts`**: `drawAtWeSee` gives a draw's lines (last, claimed, we see, the schedule, stored and on site) and this draw's money at what we see. The money comes from `drawApprovedLess`, less the statement of work's retainage. `heldOutChanges` names the change orders not yet signed both ways and whose signature each waits on. `askedOf` and `walkedWords` complete it.
- **`src/lib/gc/billDay/walk.ts`**: `claimsToCheck` lists the claims in walking order (`WALK_PLACES`, then any other place). A line is walked when its work moved this period or it claims stored material. `LineCheck` is the shape of a `superintendent_checks` row, and the newest one on a line is what we see. `tapChoices`, `underClaim`, `needsWhy`, `checkedGateClear`, `walkCounts` and the phone's words complete it. Nothing in the file names a dollar.
- **`src/lib/gc/billDay/summary.ts`**: `billRiver` reads a draw that is in at what it asked, an approved one at what we approved, and one not in at the schedule's expected. `ownerNumber` is Owner Billing's number as it stands: the forecast's month before bill day, the draft from bill day on, and the bill as it went once it went. Our crew's line comes from the draft. `billMarks` sets the dates in the order they come, today among them. `billToday` and `billRing` complete it.
- **`src/lib/gc/billDay/asks.ts`**: `askDrawText`, `askNotInTexts`, `askWaiverText`, `remindDrawText`, `superintendentText` and `sendBackNote`. They are English for now; the Spanish comes from helper 8's portal words table.
- **Changed from the prototype, and why**:
  1. A draw is priced once, by main's `drawApprovedLess`. The prototype's `drawTotal` read the stored line's on-site tap as 1% and disagreed with its own lines table. Lone Star's draw 2 is $27,180 at what Wendi saw, the lines table's number.
  2. The conditional waiver gate reads a draw in as a waiver in, since main signs the waiver with the application. The prototype's gate and its short counted two different ways.
  3. A first pay application clears last period's certificate. The prototype's gate could never clear on a first application.
  4. The change order gate opens only when a draw claims a change order's line not signed both ways. On main that cannot happen, so the gate lists the held-out ones and stays clear.
  5. The river has one rule per state, and the owner's number is Owner Billing's, never summed here.
  6. The trades are paid ten days from approval, main's rule. The prototype said net 30 from the owner's payment.
  7. The walk takes only the draws waiting on us, never one sent back. A place off the list comes after the known ones instead of first.
  8. Every sentence is short: the send-back note, the waiver ask and the gates lost their semicolons and parentheses, and names are full company names.
  9. The superintendent's path never shows the stored dollars the office's preview of her phone showed.
  10. Expected is the schedule's own percent by bill day, line by line, never below what was billed.

  Also new: stored material not seen on site needs a photo or a note too. A line that claims what was billed before is not walked. The link is the last thing in every text.
