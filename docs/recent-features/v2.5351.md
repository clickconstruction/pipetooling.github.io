# v2.5351 — Messages: where a written promise will also go, behind the scenes (2026-10-12)

The promise write-throughs' kernel (helper 4, after cut 2's C3). C3 writes a heard promise down (`write_down` on
`message_promises`, #5447), and the promise stays written. `src/lib/messages/promiseWrites.ts` says what else the
same press writes, through main's own writes and never a new table. The wiring goes after C3 lands, one target per
commit, and each commit grows C3's written words to name its place. No behavior changes yet.

- **`promiseWriteThrough(promise, at)`** returns the writes and, for each write it skips, why and what to do by hand
  ("No open ask for Hillside Excavation on this job. Ask them from the Board.").
  - quote: a line of the company's ask with the promised day (`logGcAskContact`, `gc_company_contacts.promised_by`),
    which the flow's ask nudge already reads.
  - start or delivery, insurance or a W-9: the Board's promise (`gc_record_promise`), per job for a start or delivery.
  - payment: the Pipeline's payment promise on the job's bill (`add_job_payment_promise`), and a hold on the customer
    flow's Pay application (the lead: the Pipeline keeps the money's record, the hold keeps the chase quiet). Someone
    off the money gate (`can_write_payment_promises`) gets the hold and a skip for the Pipeline.
  - decision: on a bid, a `bids_submission_entries` note with the follow-up day and Owner deciding
    (`bidNextFollowup`'s columns and sentence; a note, so the bid's last contact stays). On a GC job, a hold on Said
    yes.
  - access: a hold on the customer flow's On site. **Follow-up:** when schedule PR 10 opens the schedule, add an
    `access` kind to `gc_schedule_waits` and switch this one case to `gc_schedule_add_wait` (the lead: no `decision`
    stand-in, since a wrong kind on the schedule reads as a fact nobody said).
  - answer and cert: holds on the architect flow's Questions and Certify pay apps. Neither has a promised day of its
    own on main.
  - A hold (`nudge_hold_until`) is set only for a day after today, and never earlier than one already standing on the
    step. A day heard only in words writes nothing past the line.
- **`runPromiseWrites(writes, writers, wordsOf)`** makes the writes in order. The first refused stops the rest and comes
  back as `refusedWords`: "Written down. The Pipeline did not take it. …". What went before stands, as the promise does.
- **Tests** (`promiseWrites.test.ts`): each kind and each skip, the hold's two rules, a day only in words, one promise
  per target written by someone off the money gate, the runner's order and refusal, and every word against the plain
  words rules.
