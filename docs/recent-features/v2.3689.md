# v2.3689 — Balances: the open reports come first (2026-09-21)

The owner, on Wendi's Balances page: *"it is difficult to see what specific bills aren't completely paid … the app likes to have payments to people assigned to specific sections of hours they worked, also known as reports."* Client only, no migration. Mock-up (two revisions, the second after an "is this the best we can do?" pass): claude.ai artifact `GMGj8E6mSCcboHyuwdt3HA`.

## Why the journal could not answer it

Every payment is already recorded against one report (`pay_stub_payments.pay_stub_id`), and `buildPersonLedger` already computes paid / partial / unpaid per stub. The Balances view threw that away: a *Paid out* row never named the week it settled, a partly paid week was a small inline tag inside a 50-row list, and the running balance is the wrong lens for "which weeks are still owed" — Malachi's never touched zero after June (9 unpaid + 5 partial, $27,878.80) and nothing on the page said which weeks those were.

## What shipped

- **Kernel [`src/lib/people/openReports.ts`](../../src/lib/people/openReports.ts)** (+19 tests): `openReportRows` — every not-settled report oldest first with `paid`, `balance` (net − paid), a state and `payToHere` (the running cost of clearing through that week); `openReportState` adds two states Pay run never named — **residue** (short of net by under `RESIDUE_UNDER` = $5: Cash App fees and rounding, not debt) and **overpaid** (payments past net — a Less line added after paying, or a payment landed on the wrong week); `offReportOffsets` (charges and credits with no `pay_stub_id`); `settleUp` + `settleUpSentence` — the one line that does the real sum: *send* (open reports + unreported weeks − charges + credits), *charges* (charges cover the open weeks: send nothing, take them out of the weeks), *residue* (mark it settled), *even*; `allocateOldestFirst` (one send split across the positive balances, oldest first, with the leftover); `moveOverpaymentPlan` (shrink the newest payments on the overpaid week, insert the extra on the oldest open one, memo saying where it came from); `openReportsCaption` (the roster line: `14 open · $27,879 · 9 unpaid · 5 partial · 1 week unreported`, `2 open · $1,502 · 4 charges −$6,618`, `nothing open · 10 credits +$1,436`, `8 reports · all paid`).
- **View [`PeoplePayLedgerView.tsx`](../../src/components/people/PeoplePayLedgerView.tsx)** — the person panel is now, in order: header + equation (unchanged); **Open reports** table with Pay run's column names (*Period (w#) · Hours · Net Pay · Paid to date · Balance · Payment · Pay to here*), a meter beside Paid to date, a state pill, Record payment per row, and a click-to-expand list of the payments recorded against that week (partial, residue and overpaid rows open by default); **Hours with no report yet** — the same rows as Draft Payroll's Earlier-weeks modal, for this person, with the estimate and a **Report** button; **Off any report** — the charges and credits with no report, click to edit, `+ Charge` / `+ Credit` in its header; the **settle-up line** with its one action (*Record a payment on the oldest week…*); and the statement, folded under *Show as a statement*, in two modes: **Grouped by report** (newest report first, its payments / deductions / additions nested under it, a *Left on report* column, settled reports hidden behind *Show N settled*) and **Date order** (the old journal with the kind chips and running balance, plus a *for week of …* chip on every paid-out row). The roster caption is the kernel's, with a mini bar per open report under the name. Phone layout keeps the roster/detail swap and renders the open reports as cards.
- **[`People.tsx`](../../src/pages/People.tsx)** — `loadUnreportedWeeksForPerson(name)`: the catch-up scan (`scanWeeksBefore` + `unreportedPayrollWeeks`) for one person over the 13 completed weeks before this one (the week in progress is left out — a salaried credit for days not yet worked is not owed), passed to the view with `generateCatchUpReport` for the Report button. The pay-config map loads when the Balances view is open (its usual owner is the hours-tab cycle), and the view re-scans when it arrives.

This PR shows the states and the settle-up words; the actions land next.
- **The allocator**: the Cash App reconcile modal's *Record* lane splitting one send across the open weeks oldest first (`allocateOldestFirst`, editable), and the same list behind the settle line's button. Until then the button opens Record payment on the oldest open week.
