# v2.5875 — Messages: one more push before the hour is missed (2026-10-26)

The first of two builds from the owner's ask of 2026-10-26, after the comparison of the Messages measuring strip with Zendesk and Intercom: the strip's **Answered within an hour** read well, but nobody was told at minute 50 — the office heard once at the 15-minute wait and then nothing until the hour was already missed. Zendesk escalates before an SLA breach; now the app does too, with what it already has.

## The rule

`src/lib/messages/notifyRule.ts` gains `BREACH_MINUTES` (50) and a third push template, `message_reply_breach`, planned by the same `pushesFor` the reply push uses:

- **Who**: the holder — the tech who has it (url null, as their reply push) or the office's seat pick — and the whole office when nobody is named. Never the after-hours (`nobody`) branch, so no phone buzzes at night, and never for a legal-line reply, which the office alone heard at once.
- **When**: due at the line's time plus 50 minutes, ten before the strip's hour (`weekStrip.ts`'s `HOUR_MS`), with `cancelIfAnswered`, so any text, email or call of ours drops it (`answeredBefore`).
- **Words**: `breachWords` — *Dana has waited 50 minutes and nobody has answered. The hour is close.* — held to the plain-words rule in the tests.
- **Once**: the `notification_history` (message_id, template) dedupe covers the new template with no schema change; `template_type` is unconstrained text.

`notifyPlan.ts`'s `sweepSince` widens to `max(wait, BREACH_MINUTES) + PUSH_LATE_MAX_MINUTES + 5`, so the minute cron's sweep still sees a line whose breach push is due 50 minutes after it landed. Everything else — the owner's switch (`messages_reply_push_on_v1`), the minute cron, the late cap, the walk kit's dev-only branch — carries the new push unchanged.
