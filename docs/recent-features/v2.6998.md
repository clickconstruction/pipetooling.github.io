# v2.6998 — The Bridge's pipe and its windows are tidier to read (punch list #318, a visual pass) (2026-11-12)

A pass over what *Market and Sales on the won rates* shipped (`to-dos/market-sales-won-rates/README.md`), after the train was live. Four small visual fixes. No rule, no number and no word changed. No migration, no edge function.

## How it was read

The live site asks for a sign-in, so the same code was read on the dev server on `main`, as a dev on the live data, in headless Chrome with every write refused. Widths 375, 768 and 1400 px, each in light and dark: the first screen, the pipe closed and with its working open, the pipe drawn across, the cards behind **The flow**, the Market and Sales windows with their folds, and the Bid Board's chart. Nothing passed the page's edge and no text was cut short at any width. What follows is what the eye caught.

## What changed

- **The pipe's rows** (`BridgeFlowStripDown.tsx`). Each step's words were centred in its row, so a row with more lines started higher. The five names fell at uneven steps, Market's four lines ran past their row, and a wait bar sat right over the next step's name, where it read as a rule above that step. The words now start at the top of each step's own run of pipe, and a row is ten pixels taller (68 on a phone, 74 at a desk). The names fall at even steps and a bar keeps clear air under it. The pipe is 50 px taller in all.
- **Market's working** (`marketDetailBlocks` in `flowStripDown.ts`). *How Market's figure is counted* opened as about twenty lines of one weight. It now draws two blocks with air between, the bids and then the estimates. Each block's first line is bold, and a line that wraps hangs under its own start. The lines are the same ones, in the same order. The split is on the word the estimates' working opens with, and a test builds the working with the page's own builders so a change to that word fails there. The kernel that writes the lines (`bidWins.ts`) is copied to the edge functions and was left alone, so nothing is owed a redeploy.
- **The chart's track** (`BidBoardWonBySizeSection.tsx`). The track is the muted grey, which is nearly the Bid Board's own ground in light. A size with nothing won showed no bar at all, and a short green bar floated. The track now carries a hairline in the border's colour, so it shows in light. In dark the border is the track's own colour and the chart is as it was. A darker track was tried first and read as a full bar in dark. The same bars in the Market window sit on white and already showed, so they were left.
- **Why we lost, on a phone** (`BridgeSalesDesk.tsx`). On the row *No reason written*, the door *Name it on N bids* wraps under the reason at phone width. The dollars sat halfway between the two lines and the reason touched the rule above it. Both cells now sit on the reason's own line, with the same air over it as the other rows.

## Looked at and left

- **The first screen on a phone** is still over the word budget #303 set, and the pipe is under the fold in a week cash leads. That is the owner's call, on the card.
- **At 768 px** the standing pipe leaves the right half of its box empty. The pipe drawn across fills it; which one a tablet gets was not asked.
- **The Bid Board's chart and the Market window's chart** can read different rates for the same size. The board's follows the trade and the filters it is showing.
