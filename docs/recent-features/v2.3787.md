# v2.3787 — The legal portal shows where each job stands and the paper that went out (2026-09-23)

Punch list **#41** (`to-dos/legal-portal-lien-desk/`, the mock-up beside it), PR 1 of 3. No migration; the `legal-portal` edge function's select list changes (deploy after merge — `npm run check:edge-drift`).

## Why

The Lien desk learned a great deal in one day — the job's Chapter 53 timeline (v2.3761, v2.3768), the retainage clock and the bond (v2.3753), the saved copy of each paper (v2.3763), a paper sent by hand or covering several jobs on one packet (v2.3770, v2.3777) — and the firm's portal showed none of it. Its Paper tab computed a five-column *Lien clock* with the older `computeJobLienClock` from the last work month alone, which read *notice open · 22d* on 273 the day after Taunya's notice for that month was in the mail, and listed the one Lenox paper as three rows, one per job, with no claim as printed, no copy and no way to see that April, June and July were named as information only. Counsel's memo asked for the timeline "separately on every job"; the desk had it and the firm did not.

## What shipped

- **`src/lib/legal/legalLienPaper.ts`** (new, tests beside it) — the lien side of the packet's Paper section, pure:
  - `workMonthsFromSessions` folds the packet's approved, undisqualified clock sessions into the forecast's `JobWorkMonths` shape (keys, hours, days; no weeks or people — the timeline reads only the keys).
  - `buildLegalJobTimelines` runs **`buildLienTimelineFromWindow`** (v2.3781's adapter) per job from those months, the job's filings, the job row (`created_at`, `last_work_date`, `lien_contract_ended_on`) and the property kind, and words the § 53.057 facts (`retainageWordsFor`: *contract ended Nov 30 · retainage held $2,400 · no payment bond*).
  - `buildLegalEnvelopes` groups live filings by `packet_id` (a row without one is its own): the day it went out (the first send's date, else `filed_at`), `by_hand`, the sends with method and tracking, the claim (`printed_claim` when set, else the shares summed), every job's share, the months with **as information** where the month's § 53.056 deadline (`noticeDeadlineForMonth`, by property kind) fell before the paper went out, filed / served / serve-by, county, recording number, the copy. Lettered A, B… oldest first. `envelopeWentOutWords`, `envelopeMonthsWords`, `envelopeSharesWords` are the one wording for all three surfaces.
- **`legalPacket.ts`** — `paper.timelines` and `paper.envelopes` join `paper.lienClock` and `paper.lienFilings` (both kept: the gaps, the theory and the exhibits count still read them); the packet carries `todayYmd`.
- **`LegalFirmMatterView`** (the firm's page and the desk's Mark-ready preview), **`LegalDeskModal`** (Paper tab) and **`legalPacketPrint`** — *Lien clock* becomes **Where each job stands**: the job, its kind and role, last on site, the open balance, the retainage words, and `LienTimelineStrip` (auto layout: a rail, a list when the column is narrow). *Lien notices and filings* becomes **The paper that went out**: letter · paper · went out · claim · months as printed · jobs and shares · county · recording · copy. The print lists each job's steps as rows (the rail cannot print) plus a *Next on the path* table.
- **`supabase/functions/legal-portal`** — the jobs select adds `created_at`, `lien_contract_ended_on`, `lien_retainage_held`, `lien_payment_bond`.
