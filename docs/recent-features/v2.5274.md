# v2.5274 — Messages: where Follow up keeps who has a conversation and what we owe, behind the scenes (2026-10-12)

The Messages build's cut 2 SQL (Follow up), migration `20261012160000_messages_follow_up.sql`
(`docs/migrations/20261012160000_messages_follow_up.md`), on the spine's (v2.5258) and after the kernels (v2.5251). No
screen and no edge function; the migration is pushed by hand after merge.

- **On `contacts`**: the conversation's state (`waiting`, the screens' Open; `snoozed` with its day; `done`) and who has
  it (`has_user_id`, `has_since`, `has_how`). **On `messages`**: a note's day in words (`back_words`) and Done with it
  (`back_done_at`). A message moves the state as it lands (`messages_move_state`): theirs opens it, ours or a team note
  closes it and clears who has it, an event or an older line moves nothing.
- **Three tables, the office team's**: `contact_owed` (what we owe, its day as a date or as words, one open row a
  person, done by a team note, by our message, or by a fold), `message_promises` (a heard day written as one of eight
  kinds by who they are, or just talk; a plans question asked or answered) and `message_highlights`.
- **The presses**, `SECURITY INVOKER`, each refusing in words first (no sign-in, a training account, a twin, anyone off
  the office team): `take_it`, `hand_to`, `set_state`, `mark_owed`, `owed_done`, `write_down`, `read_question`,
  `add_note`, `set_back_day`, `back_done`, `highlight`, `unhighlight`. Who has it and every change to what we owe write the
  kernel's gray lines; `hand_to` hands it only to someone on the office team who is not archived (a superintendent
  cannot open Follow up). `follow_up_count()` is the badge, each person once.
- **Who gets a new message**: `app_settings` `messages_assign_rule_v1` holds the seats, the owner's and dev's to set.
- **`contacts_fold_follow_up`**, which the spine's `contacts_fold` runs with every `contacts_fold_%` function (no cut
  redefines the fold), so Who is this? and a customer merge keep what we owe and the state.
- **`src/lib/followUp/followUpSql.test.ts`** holds the migration's words to the kernels': each gray line's `format()`
  rendered against `hasLine`, `owedMarkedLine`, `owedWhatChangedLine`, `owedDayChangedLine` and `askArchitectNote`, and
  every refusal the migration can raise asserted by the bed, in plain words.
- **The bed**: `messages-follow-up` (`npm run test:pg:messages-follow-up`, CI's SQL beds), every refusal, the state's
  moves, the snooze to Monday and its waking, the gray lines, what we owe, the badge by distinct people, the heard
  days, the question, highlights, the fold and who reads what. Its sign-in sets `request.jwt.claim.role` too, which
  `app_settings`' read policy reads through `auth.role()`, as the card switch's bed does. The spine's bed
  (`scripts/pgtest-messages-spine.sh`) now runs the spine a second time right after its first: its grants start with a
  REVOKE on contacts and messages, which also takes back a later cut's column grants, so a second run after every
  migration refused this migration's fold.

## Beyond the brief, and why

- **`read_question`, `add_note`, `set_back_day` and `back_done`**: the plans question's two presses, the reply box's note
  with its day in one statement (helper 3's ask: two writes could leave a note without its day), and a note's Done
  with it and Another day have no other door.
- **`done_how`** on `contact_owed`: a row done by a fold has no note, so how it got done is said rather than inferred.
- **`messages_move_state`**: Open · Snoozed · Done is the app's to set (v1.146), so the rule lives where every writer
  passes, the webhook of cut 5 included.
- **`SECURITY INVOKER` with column grants**, as the spine's: the fences stay behind the words. A person signed in now
  writes a team note or an event as themselves (`messages_client_writes_own_team_lines`); nothing else by hand.
- **The plans question's record is a gray event line**, where the prototype wrote a team note, so the person stays
  waiting on the answer: a team note closes the row.
- **The seats' key is `messages_assign_rule_v1`**, the house's `_v1` for a setting with a shape.
