# v2.6457 — The Scoreboard opens a job to show who put what into it (2026-11-03)

Punch list #250, PR 2 of 4, after PR 1 (v2.6407, #6886) put the Scoreboard on the Development page's Money look. The owner took draft 2 of the mock-up and its seven defaults; this PR is the game Squad's member rows under an open squad.

- **The rows** (`scoreboardMembers` in `src/lib/developments/scoreboard.ts`, drawn by `DevelopmentScoreboard.tsx`): under an open job's nine figures, the people from the clock (a **Lead** led the crew on at least one of the days, `people_crew_jobs.crew_lead_person_name`, the small words *led 40 of 74 days*; the rest **Crew**, by hours), each **Sub** by the names on the sheets (`assigned_to_name`, *2 sheets*), each **Supplier** by house (*11 invoices*), each **Card** holder (*34 charges*), **The tally**, **Other job charges** and the overhead **Day share** (*118 days charged*), then the **Office** line: who sent the bills (`jobs_ledger_invoices.sent_by_user_id`) and who booked the payments (`jobs_ledger_payments.created_by`), words only. Each row fills its own kind's cell and **Cost**, with a thin bar for its share of the job's cost, so the rows add up to the job. A cell that is not the row's to fill is blank.
- **Adding up**: the costs read (`useSiteCosts` → `jobCostsFromRows`) keeps its totals and gains a `breakdown` per job from the rows it already reads: the sheets by name with the shared per-sheet cost (`laborJobSubCost`), the supply allocations by house (read before, unused, now handed in), the card lines the total counts (`jobCardLineStatus` = *counts*: an internal transfer and a charge also on a supply invoice stay out, as the total leaves them), and the tally and other lines counted. A split that falls short of its total (an allocation read cut at its limit, the card rule's clamp) gets one reconciling row, *Other supply invoices* or *Card charges not split*, so the sum holds.
- **The card holders** (`lib/developments/cardHolders.ts`, `fetchCardHolders.ts`, `hooks/useSiteCardHolders.ts`): the card id alone out of each bank record (`raw->details->debitCardInfo->>id`, a few bytes a row, never the whole record the Gun Dog spike took off the page's read), then the card's linked person (`mercury_debit_card_user_links`), else its nickname (`mercury_debit_card_nicknames`), else its short id. Read only while the board is open, for the finance seats; until it lands, or if it fails, the job keeps one *Card* row.
- **The gold row**: the viewer's own rows, amber with a *You* chip: a person by login (`people.account_user_id`, read with the clock as `SiteLaborPerson.userId`), a person with no login by name (`useAuth().profileName`), the viewer's own card, the viewer's office line.
- **Who sees what**: dev and controller every kind with its dollars; a master or an assistant the people alone, with hours and days and no dollar (the lead's three-column board). The page's read gains `sent_by_user_id` on bills and `created_by` on payments, and their names.
- **Phone**: an open card lists the rows as lines under its nine, each with its figure (a person's *$21,420 · 612 h · 74 d*) and its share bar.
- **The middle widths** (found by the live walk across widths): at 768 the page ran 790 px wide with twelve figures clipped, at 1024 two px wide with eight. Between the phone layout and 1180 px (`SCOREBOARD_COMPACT_QUERY`) the cells and tiles now write money short, the Gun Dog phone strip's rule (`scoreboardCompactWords`: *$2.1M*, *$684k*, whole dollars under $10k), with the whole figure in each cell's title; a member row's small words move into its title and its role tag narrows, so the name keeps the room; the Job column narrows to 26%; and the table scrolls inside its card rather than pushing the page.
- **A leader the clock is closed to** (found by the same walk, as the sample master): PR 1's leader board read *0 h* on every job, because `people_crew_jobs` and `people_hours_recorded` are closed to a master without payroll approval. An empty crew read now falls back to `get_man_hours_by_job` (a definer read open to dev, master, assistant and controller, the same allocation as the clock read), filtered to the site's jobs on the server (`siteLaborFromManHours`); it has no days, so the board leaves the Days column out (`scoreboardColumnsFor(seesCosts, daysKnown)`) and nobody reads as a Lead, never a false zero. An assistant and a controller read the clock as before.
- **Guide**: *Read the scoreboard* gains how to open a job and read its rows.

**Verified live** on the real site through the dev server, as the dev and as the sample master, at 375, 768, 1024, 1280 and 1920 wide in light and dark: the board settles in about four seconds, the big job opens to twenty rows that add up to its Cost within a dollar of rounding, and its hours match the dev seat's `get_job` to the hour. Gun Dog's clock rows name no crew lead, so every person there reads *Crew*. The dev seat's `get_job` counts 200 card lines on that job where the page counts 498, so the card-charge gap first seen on PR 1 looks like that read stopping at 200 rows.
