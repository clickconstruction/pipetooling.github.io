# v2.7889 — Needs you: a stage that reads 100% and is not billed (2026-11-18)

Punch list #397, PR 7, the last of the train. No migration, no edge function.

## Why

PRs 3 and 4 put a bid's stages on the job as its lines. The crew reports a percent on each. When one reaches 100% the work is done, and nothing told the office, short of opening the job. The Pipeline's *Bill the finished work* card counts that money across every job, as one sum. This is the same fact one stage at a time, with the job named.

The owner's default was to hold this PR until crews mark stages on real jobs. On 2026-11-18 he said to build the rest of the train, this PR with it.

## The rule

`stagesDone(jobs, lines)` in `src/lib/needsYou/stagesDone.ts` lists a line when all of these hold.

- Its job is Working. A Waiting job has no work to bill, and Ready to Bill and after are past this.
- The job has two or more priced lines. A job on one line at 100% is the whole job, and the crew's own prompt already offers Ready to Bill.
- The line is priced and is not a discount.
- The crew's percent on the line is 100 or more.
- No bill carries the line (`invoice_id` is null).

The rows come back with the most money first.

## The line

- Key `stages-done-not-billed`, source `stagesDone`, the office's (`needsYouSources.ts`), the assistant's to act on (`needsYouActor.ts`).
- Amber, kicker *Billing*, rank 40, with the charges that wait on a receipt.
- Title: *A stage reads 100% and is not billed*, or *N stages read 100% and are not billed*.
- Detail: the first stage, its job and its dollars, then how many more and the sum.
- Figure: the dollars. Press: **Open the job**.
- Door: `/jobs?tab=stages&jobDetail=<the first stage's job>`, the Pipeline with that job's window open. With no job, the Pipeline.

**A percent offers a bill. It never sends one.** The press opens a window and writes nothing.

## The read

`stagesDoneIo.ts` makes three reads. The first is the lines at 100% with no bill, and it is small, since few lines carry a percent of their own. The other two are those jobs, Working only, and every line of those jobs, so the rule can tell a staged job from a job on one line. `useStagesDone(enabled)` answers null while it reads and on a refused read, so the card stays quiet.
