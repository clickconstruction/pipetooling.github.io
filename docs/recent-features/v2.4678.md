# v2.4678 — Legal: each job shows the property it stands on (2026-10-05)

Client plus one edge function (`legal-portal`, deploy after merge); no migration. Item 6 of punch list #85 ([`to-dos/legal-portal-firm-ready/`](../../to-dos/legal-portal-firm-ready/README.md)), stacked on item 5 (v2.4652). Mockup: `mockup-6-property-per-job.html` beside the card, a GC-paid matter with a store and a house.

## The defect

The packet's **Property record** was every `customer_addresses` row of the payer. On a GC-paid job that is the GC's office addresses, not the project. The matter card's *county · owner of record* read the first of them, so the firm would have filed in the GC's county and named the GC as owner. The lien clock, the Chapter 53 timelines and the envelopes' *as information* months all ran every job from `properties[0].propertyKind`, so a house in a GC's account was dated as non-residential, its § 53.056 notice a month later than the law allows. The app's own rule (`resolveLienProperty` in `src/lib/jobs/lienProperty.ts`: the job's `job_property_owners` override wins, the job's `customer_address_id` names the property) was never consulted: matters fetched neither.

## What changed

- **A property kernel**, [`legalProperty.ts`](../../src/lib/legal/legalProperty.ts) `resolveLegalJobProperties(jobs, { labelOf, jobAddresses, payerAddresses, owners })`. Each job's property is the record it names (whichever customer holds it), else a payer record whose address is the job's address exactly (`matched`), else the job's own address with nothing known (`job_address`, gap *a property record linked to the job*). The override wins for the owner. Jobs at one property with one owner share a line with every job label.
- **The packet** (`legalPacket.ts`): `LegalPacketInput` gains `jobAddresses` and `jobOwners`; `LegalJobLine` gains `property`; `account.properties` is the distinct resolved properties (`key`, `source`, `ownerSource`, `jobIds`, `jobLabels`). The lien clock, `buildLegalJobTimelines` and `buildLegalEnvelopes` (both gain an optional `propertyKindOf`) read each job's own kind. The lien theory needs a lien-ready property on the job whose window is open, not any address. The gaps are per property: *No property record linked to 701* and *Property record incomplete · address (jobs)*, both opening Lien instruments on the first job. The *Property record* exhibit counts real records only.
- **The function** (`legal-portal`): the matter path selects `customer_address_id`, reads those records by id and the jobs' `job_property_owners` without `owner_email`, and sends them as `jobAddresses` and `jobOwners`. Noted in `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`.
- **The desk's hook** (`useLegalPacketData.ts`) reads the same three things: the jobs' `customer_address_id` (its own small read, so it does not depend on what the board selected), the records by id, the overrides. The desk and the firm resolve the same property.
- **Payload parsing** (`legalPortalPayload.ts`) reads `jobAddresses` and `jobOwners` defensively; an older function's payload parses as none.
- **The views**: the firm's Account tab, the desk and both prints add a Job column to the Property record (a job with no linked record says so); the matter card's county and owner read the first job's property, naming the job when the matter has more than one; *Where each job stands* says each job's own kind. The guide *review a collections account before it goes to your attorney* says the property record is each job's own site.

- **Integration pass (review fixes).** An unknown kind runs the later (commercial) calendar, as the lien timeline always has; the page, the print and the desk now say so in the kind cell, *kind unknown · commercial dates shown (a residential property is a month earlier)* (`propertyKindCell`), beside the timeline's own warning. A record found by an exact address match on the payer's records says *matched by address, not linked* beside the address on the page, the print and the desk (`propertySourceNote`). The function's selects keep `customer_address_id` and the owner columns; item 23 (#4635) shapes `jobAddresses` and `jobOwners` with those column lists.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **Keep the payer's other addresses somewhere?** No. The payer's address is already under Who owes. Office addresses are not properties the jobs stand on, and listing them under Property record is what misled.
- **Guess a job's property from its address when nothing is linked?** Only on an exact address match against the payer's own records, and the row says it was matched. A street-only match stays a suggestion for a person; legal identity is never guessed.
- **Send the override owner's email to the firm?** No. The firm needs the owner of record and the mailing address. The email is the office's courtesy channel.
- **An older function?** The page reads it as no linked records, so every job falls back to an exact match or its address. Nothing crashes; the deploy brings the records.
