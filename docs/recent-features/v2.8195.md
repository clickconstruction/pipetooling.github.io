# v2.8195 — A photo keeps its spot: the ground for the later steps (2026-11-21)

Punch list #413, the database half of steps 3, 4, 6 and 7. Taunya's word of 2026-11-21 was to build the whole card. No screen changes.

## Why

The first steps made a place quick to set from a photo. The later ones each need somewhere to keep something: where the phone was when a job photo was taken, how often a place is opened, which line a buried place runs along, a place's other photos, and a way for next year's job at the same house to read this year's places. One migration carries all of it, so each screen that follows ships with no migration of its own and no wait on a push.

## What ships

Migration `20261121203000_photo_spot_ground.sql` ([its page](../migrations/20261121203000_photo_spot_ground.md)):

- **A photo's point.** `taken_lat`, `taken_lng` and `taken_accuracy_m` on `job_photo_notes` and `report_photos`, held whole and on the globe.
- **A place's opens.** `opened_count` and `last_opened_at` on a place, raised by `note_painted_place_opened` when someone other than the setter opens a place they can read. The count names nobody.
- **Places follow the address.** A job's list of places is read from every job at the same customer address, through one more read policy on `painted_maps` and `job_address_place_lists`, which a job's window asks. A painted map that is not a list stays its own job's, and a job with no customer address shares nothing.
- **A place's line.** `line_id` on a place, left empty when the line goes.
- **More photos.** `more_photos` on a place, a list of twelve at most.

## Who gains what

- The crew of a later job at the same customer address reads the earlier job's list of places, its places and their photos. They may add a place to it, as every reader of a painted map may. They change nothing of anyone else's.
- Whoever reads a photo's row will read where it was taken, once a screen writes it.
- Nobody signed out gains anything: no new function runs for them.

## The bed

`supabase/tests/painted_maps/95_spot_ground.sql`, a ninth scenario in the painted maps bed: four helpers on four jobs, two of them at one customer address. It holds the point's checks, who reads a list by its address and who does not, who raises a place's count, the line and the photos.

## Order

Additive and idempotent. The push can come any time after this merges. The screens that follow must not merge before the push.
