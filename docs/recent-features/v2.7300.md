# v2.7300 — The job window on a phone: the ✕ rides with the tabs (2026-11-15)

Punch list #355, a fix from the viewport sweep after pass 3 ([`to-dos/job-window-phone.md`](../../to-dos/job-window-phone.md)). Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## Before

Pass A (v2.7271) floated a 38 px ✕ one press down from the corner once the header had scrolled away, the whole way down the job. The sweep of every tab at 375 to 440 px found it sitting over whatever had scrolled under it: on the Bill tab the first line's ⋯ menu, on the Costs rows a row's cost, on the Edit rows a row's chevron. A tap meant for the thing under it closed the window.

## What changed

- **`JobWindowModal.tsx`**: on a phone the tab strip sits in a row (`data-testid="job-window-strip-row"`) that carries the strip's margin, border, background and the sticky pin; the strip itself keeps its scroll, its mask and `data-navtrack`. While the row is pinned (a scroll back up with the header off screen) a 44 px ✕ (`data-testid="job-window-strip-close"`) stands at the row's right, outside the strip's fade. While the reader scrolls down nothing closes the window but the back of the phone: the ✕ is gone with the name and the tabs, and a flick up brings all three back. The floating ✕ (`job-window-float-close`) is gone.
- **`jobWindowPhone.ts`**: the kernel is unchanged; its comments say what the two states draw now.
- **`job-window-tabs.md`**: the guide's phone paragraph says the ✕ scrolls away and comes back with the tabs.

## Is this the best we can do?

A close that stays reachable the whole way down was the aim of the float, and it is what the tap trap cost. Safari's own bar does what the strip does now: chrome goes on a scroll down and returns on the first scroll up, so the ✕ is one small flick away at any depth and never over a press. The ⋯ stays in the title row only, as pass A set it.

## Verify

- At 375 px open a job, flick down past the name: no ✕ anywhere; flick up: the tabs return at the top with a ✕ at their right that closes the window.
- `npm test -- JobWindowModal jobWindowPhone helpGuide` and `npm run check:todos`.
