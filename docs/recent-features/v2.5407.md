# v2.5407 — Messages: a homeowner hears when their job is booked, started, done, billed and paid, behind the scenes (2026-10-12)

R4 · 4 of the residential build (`to-dos/gc-mode/messages/RESIDENTIAL_BUILD_PLAN.md` on `spike/gc-mode`, integration 15 in `customer-messages-integrations-mockups.html`): the stages the customer feels, as a pure kernel, `src/lib/flows/stageSends.ts`. No migration, no edge function, no screen. It stacks on v2.5395 (the residential loader) and reads the residential flow it stamps.

**The moves** (`feltStep`, `FELT_MOVES`): dispatch's block for the job (`job_schedule_blocks`) turns Booked true. The Jobs board's `job_status_events.to_status` values `working`, `ready_to_bill`, `billed` and `paid` turn Working, Done, Billed and Paid true. Waiting and every other move stay the office's.

**The send** (`stageSendFor(event, reading, lines, settings)`): the step's own quick reply (`stepLine`, cut 3, for the customer kind) goes as a text when all of these hold:
- the customer feels the move;
- they are not off the flow;
- the step is switched on in `AppSends.residential` (the morning run's switch, off until the owner turns it on);
- `readFlow` reads the step done on that job (for the Billed and Paid loops, a round done);
- the step did not go today, by a stage text or a reminder of ours (the morning run's `sentLately` over `nudge_sent`);
- the number is on file and did not send STOP.

It fills `{first}` and `{job}` and sends nothing while another blank is left, such as `{day}`. Helper 4's `fillLine` (#5558, `quickReplies/blanks.ts`) replaces the local fill on the rebase after it lands. What it returns has `NudgeText`'s shape: the body, the `nudge_sent` row (`by: 'app'`, `how: 'text'`) and the thread's event line, "The app sent the Working line by text." A move that sends nothing says why: not felt, off the flow, off, not done, sent today, no number, opted out, no line, or blanks left (`stageNotSentWords`).

**The only change to `nudgeDispatch.ts`**: `sentLately` is exported, and its step parameter is widened to `Pick<NudgeStep, 'stepId' | 'jobId'>`, so the once-a-day rule has one home.

**The Jobs board's dot** (`boardDot(rows, messages, names, jobId, now)`): it reads Follow up's Needs a reply rows by job. A row whose newest line from them is tied to this job, with nothing of ours sent to them after it, shows the dot. The hover comes back in parts the board draws side by side, each in plain words: "Dana", "text", "9:05 AM" (or "yesterday"), the first line, and "Nobody has answered." Of two people waiting on one job, the one waiting longest is the hover's.

**Tests**: `stageSends.test.ts` (13) over Dana Whitfield, the prototype's demo customer:
- Waiting to Working sends the Working line once, not twice that day, and again the next day.
- A reminder of ours sent today counts.
- Off, STOP and no number send nothing.
- Dispatch's block sends the Booked line, and a line with `{day}` and `{window}` waits.
- Not done, not felt, no line and off the flow each send nothing.
- Paid sends once the bill is covered.
- Every word passes `plainWordsFailures`.
- The dot shows from a waiting row, and goes once we answer, once the row is done, or on another job.

Three pins were broken on purpose: dropping the sent-today check, letting a team note count as an answer, and sending a step the flow does not read done. Each failed the tests that pin it.
