# v2.7428 — Sub labor: a teammate's own sheet is theirs in the dev seat and in Banking (sub labor train) (2026-11-15)

Punch list #346, the last of its mirrors. Taunya's rule of 2026-11-15, "a sheet that names one teammate and no sub be counted: owed to the teamate", has been on People → Subs since v2.7388 and on Jobs → Subs since v2.7389. Two places still read a teammate's own sheet the old way. No migration.

## What landed

- **The dev seat, `supabase/functions/_shared/devMcpSubLabor.ts`.** `sheetSentTo` names a teammate who is alone on a sheet with no sub as the person its sending is to, the key `sendingSheetsFrom` gives the Record payment window. So `plan_sub_labor_payment`'s `record` passes their id to `record_sub_sending`. Before, it passed nobody and the plan read *a crew pay sheet*. Several teammates and no sub name nobody, and so does a sheet with no name, each in its own words.
- **The seat's words for a primary.** `plan_sub_labor` said the Pay ledger files a primary's sheet under Crew pay and that it pays through payroll. Nothing in payroll reads a sheet. The plan now says the sheet is their own and is counted as owed to them (*Team · sub labor*).
- **Banking, `src/lib/subWorkOrders/bankLineSubPayments.ts`.** `bankSideSheets` sets a sheet's `crew` flag from `sheetOwedToNobody`, as the Subs Ledger does. No Banking screen reads that flag today, and the picker already named the teammate, so nothing on the screen changes. The flag is right for the next reader.
- **`dev-mcp` server 0.91.0**, with its pins in thirty test files.

## Tests

- `bankLineSubPayments.test.ts`: a teammate's own sheet and a sheet several teammates share are not crew, a sheet with no name is, and the teammate's payment is offered under their name.
