# v2.8555 — The dev seat writes a job's line item with its price (2026-11-24)

Punch list #293. dev-mcp 0.134.0. One edge function, no migration, no client change.

## What was wrong

The look at card #293 on 2026-11-24 opened the seat's ZZ TEST job. Its Bill tab drew one empty line with *Job Total $0.00* and *Left to bill $0*, while a $1 bill stood as Billed and the job window said $1.

`plan_job` and `apply_job` wrote `jobs_ledger.revenue` and nothing else. The New Job form cannot make a job in that shape: it works the price out of its line items and saves them (`writeNewJobChildRows`). The Bill tab totals the lines, so a job with a price and no line reads $0.00 there.

The risk was the tab's autosave. `writeBillingSlice` writes `revenue` as the sum of the form's lines, so one edit of a line or a payment on a seat-made job would have written 0 over its price under a standing bill. Nobody pressed it.

## What changed

- **`plan_job` plans the line, `apply_job` writes it.** A price over 0 is planned as one line item: 1 of `line_name` (new, optional), else the job's name, at the price. The plan says it under `line_item` with its words. The apply inserts the job's row, then the line, then the kind. The row is the one `fixtureInsertRows` writes for one named work line, less the id, which the table mints (`priceLineRow`). A line the table refuses leaves the job standing, as the form's child rows do, and the reply names the door.
- **`plan_job_update` moves the line with the price.** It reads the job's line items whenever `revenue` is in `set`, and `priceLinePlan` decides:
  - no line and a price over 0: one line of 1 at the price, even when the price is the one the job already holds. Planning a job's own price again mends a job made before this version.
  - one work line of 1, on no bill, that totals the price today: that line is repriced. A price of 0 clears its unit price, as the form writes it.
  - anything else is refused in words: several lines, a line on a bill, a count over 1, a discount, a fee riding on the price. The lines are changed on the Bill tab.
- **`apply_job_update` writes the line first.** A line down under an old price is mended by the tab's next save. A price down with no line under it is the fault this closes. A line the table refuses stops the apply with nothing changed.
- The plan's hash takes the line in, so lines changed since the plan refuse the apply.
