# v2.6005 — dev-mcp: an answer on a packet gap (2026-10-28)

The dev seat (`dev-mcp`, server 0.8.0) gains a twelfth and thirteenth write verb: `plan_gap_answer(gap)` / `apply_gap_answer(gap, plan_hash)`. The Legal desk's *Before this goes to an attorney* list computes its gaps on every open and stores none of them; the office answers one with the Answer… sheet (v2.5920), which keeps one line per (matter, gap key) in `legal_matter_gap_answers`. Until now the seat could read those rows but not write one, so a session that had just sent a letter or made a call had nowhere to say so beside the gap.

## What changed

- **Migration** [`20261028120000_dev_legal_gap_answer_write.sql`](../migrations/20261028120000_dev_legal_gap_answer_write.md): `dev_legal_gap_answer_write(p, p_dry_run)`, the dry-run wrapper modeled on `dev_legal_plan_write`. `p` is `{ matter_id, gap_key, action? (answer | clear), answer?, label?, document_id? }`; the dry run returns the matter, the answer on record and the row as it would be written, the live answer count and whether the firm reads it; the write goes through the desk's own `legal_answer_gap` (with `p_job_ids` NULL, so the matter's jobs stay as they are) or `legal_clear_gap_answer`, as the caller. The matter must exist and a closed one is refused.
- **Grammar** ([`devMcpWrites.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/devMcpWrites.ts)): the two verbs join `WRITE_VERBS` (thirteen verbs over seven RPCs); `writeRpcBody` checks the gap's shape (a matter id, a key under 120, a line under 500, a label under 300, a document id that is a uuid; `clear` carries nothing more) and POSTs `{ p, p_dry_run }` like the others.
- **Server** ([`dev-mcp/index.ts`](../../supabase/functions/dev-mcp/index.ts), 0.8.0): the two tools, handled in the write branch; `alreadyApplied` says the answer is under its gap and how to change or clear it.
- **Tests** ([`devMcpWrites.test.ts`](../../src/lib/devMcp/devMcpWrites.test.ts), +5): the allowlist gains the RPC and checks the migration's gates and the two office RPCs it calls; the grammar's valid, clear and refused shapes; the server's wiring and version.
- **Docs**: `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` dev-mcp section; the migration doc.

## Deploy

`supabase db push` (the migration), then `supabase functions deploy dev-mcp`. `tools/list` on `https://mcp.clicktooling.com/dev` shows `serverInfo.version` 0.8.0 and both verbs once it is up.
