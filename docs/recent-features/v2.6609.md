# v2.6609 — Add our as-built on the crew's own job window (punch list #276) (2026-11-06)

Client only; nothing to push or redeploy. Found on the walk of v2.6604 (punch list #272): the job window a helper or a subcontractor gets is the read-only one, `DetailJobModal`, which `resolveJobWindowMode` gives every role but dev, master, assistant, controller and primary, and its plans were one row, **Open plans** on `jobs_ledger.job_plans_link`. The Plans section with **Add our as-built** (v2.6298, v2.6345) sat on the full window's Documents tab alone, so the press written for the crew had only ever been pressed by the office.

## Change

- **`DetailJobModal.tsx`**: `DetailJobModalPlans` mounts `JobPlansSection` for the job, for the full read and the limited one alike, where the *Open plans* row sat, under the job's status. The section draws for these roles what it draws for the office minus **+ New set** (`canWrite` is the office set): *Building from* with **Open ↗**, *Older sets*, *Our as-builts* with the author's own **Add photos or a PDF** and ×, and **Add our as-built**. The old **Open plans** row stays only while the section reads no part (`no-part`) or fails to read, for a job whose plans are one pasted link.
- **`JobPlansSection.tsx`**: `onState` reports where the read stands (`JobPlansSectionState`: loading, failed, current, no-current, no-part), the same word `data-job-documents-plans` carries, so the window can keep or drop its row.
- **The database side was already there**: `plan_set_crew_on_job` (v2.6583) holds the crew to an as-built on a job they are on, and `plan_part_crew_can_see` (v2.6604) reads them the part their job builds from, on a development too. A crew member reads no development row, so the section's embedded development name is null for them; it reads fine without it, and the other-plans notice stays quiet since the parts it reads are their own.
- **Smoke `DetailJobModal.plans.render.test.tsx`**: as a helper over a fake plans io, a job building from a set reads *Building from*, **Open ↗** and **Add our as-built** with no **+ New set** and no **Open plans**; a job with no part says the office adds the set and keeps **Open plans** on the job's link.
- **Guide *keep the plans on a job***: `roles` gains subcontractor and helpers; the opening says where the crew opens the job (the Dashboard card, Job Mode's **Job detail**) and that their Plans section sits under the job's status; the as-built step says the same. `docs/twins/APP_DIRECTORY.md`'s job-window line says who has the press.
