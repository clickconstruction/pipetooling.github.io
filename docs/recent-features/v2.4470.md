# v2.4470 — Bids: a version, a decision or a note in the evening keeps its day (2026-10-03)

One of the instant-day fixes that follow [v2.4452](v2.4452.md). No migration. `twin-mcp` is redeployed after merge.

## What was wrong

Bids readers cut days out of `timestamptz` columns by their first ten characters, so anything stamped after 7 pm CDT (6 pm CST) read as the next day:

- **A package sent before per-GC sends** ([`gcPackets.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/gcPackets.ts), `groupVersionsByGc`): with no send rows, a packet takes the bid's `bid_date_sent` only when one of its versions existed then, by comparing the version's `created_at` with that `date`. A version made on the evening of the send day read as made the next day, so its packet read *not sent*. The bid's sends list ([`BidPackageSendsDetails.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/BidPackageSendsDetails.tsx)) re-derives the same rule per version line.
- **Bid Costs** ([`bidPursuit.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/bidPursuit.ts), through its `ymd`, which also reads `bid_date_sent`): an unsent bid's date from `bids.created_at`, which places it in the 90-day and year windows, and the decided day from `bids.outcome_at`, a `timestamptz` (a version's `outcome_at` is a `date`). History & forecast ([`bidForecast.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/bidForecast.ts)) counts the days from sent to decided from it.
- **The robot's reference check**: the Reference grade window ([`RobotReferenceGradeModal.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/RobotReferenceGradeModal.tsx)) handed [`referenceGrade.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/referenceGrade.ts) `bid_date_sent ?? created_at` and the kernel cut it. `twin-mcp`'s `score_backtest` computes the same `stale` flag on the server, twice (the first score and an amend), the same way.
- **One-line readers**: an audit note's stamp on the Audits tab ([`BidsAuditsTab.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/BidsAuditsTab.tsx), `bid_audit_notes.created_at`); a GC's *on notice since* on the Bid Board's customer review, from its sent lien notices' `job_lien_desk_items.sent_at` ([`BidBoardCustomerReviewModal.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/BidBoardCustomerReviewModal.tsx)); a price request with no `requested_on` ([`priceMatrixRequest.ts`](../../src/lib/rfq/priceMatrixRequest.ts), whose sources the pricing robot reads as stored); and the robot queue's *decided Jul 2026* ([`BidsRobotQueueTab.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/BidsRobotQueueTab.tsx)).

## What changed

Each instant reads `calendarYmdInAppTzFromIso(iso)` from [`dateUtils.ts`](../../src/utils/dateUtils.ts) on the client and `todayYmdInAppTz(new Date(iso))` in [`twin-mcp`](../../supabase/functions/twin-mcp/index.ts), guarded because it throws on an Invalid Date. The `date` columns keep their slice: `bid_date_sent`, `requested_on`, a send's `sent_on` and a version's `outcome_at`. `bidPursuit.ts`'s `ymd` now says it is for date columns, and `ReferenceUnsealFields.when` says its caller reads the day.

## Not changed

- The Job accounts lens's *won Sep 4* and the Submittals nudge's days since a win read `outcome_at` as an instant in the device's zone or as elapsed time, not as a UTC date.
- `formatAuditRequestedStamp` slices only an unreadable stamp.

## Tests

- `gcPackets.test.ts` (+1): a version made at `2026-08-01T01:00:00Z` (8 pm CDT on Jul 31) is there when the bid went out on Jul 31; one at `2026-12-02T00:30:00Z` (6:30 pm CST) on a Dec 1 send too; one at noon on Aug 1 is not.
- `bidPursuit.test.ts` (+1): an unsent bid made at 7:30 pm CDT on Aug 31 dates Aug 31; a decision at 6:30 pm CST on Dec 1 reads Dec 1; noon UTC reads its own day.
- `priceMatrixRequest.test.ts` (+1): a request with no `requested_on`, made at 7:30 pm CDT on Sep 9, reads Sep 9.
- `BidsAuditsTab.render.test.tsx` (+1): a note left at 8 pm CDT on Aug 30 reads *staff · 2026-08-30*.

All 4 fail on main. The reference check, the customer review's notice day and the robot queue's month change one expression each; `twin-mcp` has no test harness for `score_backtest`, and an esbuild pass checks its syntax.

## Checked against prod

Read only, from the worktree dev server: 134 of the 268 audit notes were written in a Central evening and now read a day earlier. 20 of the 410 bids are unsent and were made in an evening, so each moves a day earlier in Bid Costs' windows. 4 of the 11 decided bids were marked in an evening, so their decision times read a day shorter. No version was made on the evening of its bid's send, no open price request lacks its own day, no lien notice has been sent, and no reference's stale flag flips.
