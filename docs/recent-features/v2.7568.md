# v2.7568 — Dashboard: the office sees what waits on the leader, and can remind him (2026-11-17)

Punch list #365, PR 2 of four: the office's side of the short Dashboard (v2.7466). Client only. No migration and no edge function.

## What was missing

The leader's short page lists six lines that are his alone. The office could see two of them, the lien notices to approve and to sign, and only on the Lien desk. Nothing showed it the lien waivers, the weekly line budgets or the job notes that tagged him, so nobody chased those.

## What the office sees

A card under Needs you on an assistant's or a controller's Dashboard (`DashboardWaitingOnLeaderCard.tsx`, mounted in `DashboardPinnedQuickRow.tsx`), named for the leader: *Waiting on Marcus*, with a count. It is drawn only when something waits or a read failed.

- **Lien notices to approve**: every notice on the desk's awaiting pile, in a run or out of one. The line says when he was asked, when the first window closes and how many are in a run.
- **Lien notices to sign**: approved and unsigned, by the rule the Board's *to sign* tile counts by (`lienItemUnsigned`).
- **Lien waivers to sign**, **tagged job notes to answer** and **assumed lines to review**.
- **Remind** on the two lien rows. It asks first in the desk's own words, sends through `notify-lien-approval`, then says what went and what did not, and whether the text went. After a send the press reads *Reminded 2:14 PM* and is dead for the visit.
- A waiver, a tagged note and the line budgets have no message to send. The row says *No reminder goes for this yet. Tell Marcus yourself.* A waiver and a tagged note have **Open the job**.
- **Marcus is here, sign with the signing code**: one wide press while a notice waits on a signature. It opens the desk's Ready pile, where the office's footer takes his code (v2.6733).
- A read that failed is named under the rows: *The lien waivers could not be read, so this list may be short.*

## How it is built

- `src/lib/dashboard/waitingOnLeader.ts` (pure): who sees the card (`canSeeWaitingOnLeader`: assistant, controller), the rows, every word, and for a lien row the `LienReminderPlan` its press sends. `WAITING_ON_LEADER_MIRRORS` names the leader's line each row stands for, and the test holds it to `SHORT_DASHBOARD_ALONE_KEYS` both ways: a line added to his page is a row the office is owed.
- `src/lib/dashboard/waitingOnLeaderLien.ts` (pure): the desk's awaiting and ready piles as the card takes them.
- `src/lib/dashboard/waitingOnLeaderIo.ts` and `src/hooks/useWaitingOnLeader.ts`: three small reads (the leaders, the waivers awaiting a leader's signature, the open tags on a leader) and the weekly review's read. The Lien desk's read is the one `useNeedsYouInputs` already makes, handed on as `lienDeskData`, so the desk is read once a page.
- `src/lib/jobs/lienReminderRun.ts`: `sendLienReminder(plan)`, the calls behind one reminder press with their tally. It mirrors the desk's `remindLeader` loop. The desk still runs its own copy.

## No function was needed

The card (#365) expected a definer function to count the waivers, the line budgets and the tagged notes for the office. Reading as the assistant's sample account showed the office already reads all three under its own row-level security: the same rows as a dev on `job_lien_releases`, `jobs_ledger_note_tags` and `job_budgets`. So the reads are plain selects and nothing is pushed.

## Not in this PR

- A reminder for a waiver, a tagged note or the line budgets. PR 3, the text when a paper is ready, is where a send for them belongs.
- The affidavits and retainage notices that wait on his approval. They are not on his short page, so they are not on this card. The Lien desk lists and reminds them.
- An office phone with Dispatch Mode on shows the one-line Needs you door instead of the card, and the Waiting on card is not drawn there either.
- With two leaders the card says *Waiting on the leader* and counts both.
