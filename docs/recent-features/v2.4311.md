# v2.4311 — Lien desk: share where our liens stand (2026-10-01)

**The ask (Grace, 2026-10-01).** "Add a share button to the lien desk so I can quickly push to someone the status of who we are about to lien and for how much", looked at whole, beside how the app already shares and emails. Plan, then *is this the best we can do?*, then a mock-up, then the same question. Canvas: https://claude.ai/artifact/34S5sut7DT1LpkNNnWetAZ (row 1 the first pass with its faults numbered, rows 2 and 3 the approved design). Grace: "I like it, please build it".

**What the two passes changed.** The first plan texted every job on the desk and used a mail link for email. The second: lead with who, how much, by when and what waits for approval, then one line per GC; quote the desk's own number (the desk showed three totals that day: $173,597 on the Dashboard card, $181k on the Calendar's Oct 15 column, $170,282 on the Timeline); a teammate email the app sends instead of a mail link (mail links fail without a mail app and cap the length); one GC as a choice; the time on every message. The mock-up's second look moved the button from line 2 (it changes with every tab) to the title line, swapped a dialog over the desk for a panel under the button (a sheet on a phone, buttons pinned), made one main button, and pointed counsel at the live portal instead of a snapshot. Two owner rules from the history shaped the email: mail to people outside the company leaves from the user's own inbox (2026-08-19), and the app emails only when a person presses Send.

## Found on the way (not in this PR)

- The Calendar's GC and row words read the runway's last work month, so a job with several unpaid months reads late: 878 Take 5- Seguin said *by Dec 15 · 75 d* while its July notice is due Oct 15. Fixed in its own PR (v2.4308, #4258). The share never uses those words.
- An unknown property kind splits the counts: the Calendar assumes the earlier residential date, the desk lists the commercial one (Knight Contracting showed 9 on the Calendar, 5 on Notices). The message's *Set the property kind on N jobs* line names it.
- *Send for approval* notifies nobody; the leader learns from the Dashboard. Share is how the office nudges him today.
- The service worker's tap handler builds `new URL(url)` from a relative push url, which throws when an app window is open. Offered as its own task.
