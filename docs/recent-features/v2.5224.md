# v2.5224 — The Bridge: the Monday sheet, one screen you can email yourself (2026-10-10)

PR 8a of the Bridge's redraw (plan and mock-up at https://claude.ai/artifact/2GciUJPCjaTysjpTEULWyJ, cut B). Dev only. One new edge function, `bridge-sheet-email`, deployed after the merge. No migration.

- **The sheet** (`src/lib/bridge/mondaySheet.ts`, `buildMondaySheet`): three tiles from the page's own kernels — contribution per week (the headline's lead, its hours and the eight bars, with a light: amber while the trend waits on approvals), net position (the light, the billing lag, the costs the paper misses, the finished-unbilled total) and cash (the light, the day that carries the window with its receipts and bills, the receipts by a promise or pace) — then the first five of This week (`MONDAY_SHEET_MOVES`) and the people lines (`peopleLines`: the field rows with a contribution, who waits, the office's billed and collected). `mondaySheetHtml` / `mondaySheetText` / `mondaySheetSubject` render it for mail, escaped, table-laid, inline-styled.
- **The view** (`BridgeMondaySheet.tsx`): `/bridge?view=monday`, reached by **Monday sheet** in the page header; **Open the full Bridge** returns. **Email me this sheet** posts the finished subject, text and HTML to `bridge-sheet-email`, which checks the caller is a dev with a real account and mails it to that account's own address through Resend — never another recipient (`EMAIL_CATALOG` id `bridge_monday_sheet`; `verify_jwt = false`, the JWT read in-body like `gc-office-notices`). A 400 KB cap on the HTML.
- **What is not here yet**: the weekly send. The page builds the sheet in the browser from eight loaders; a cron would need those reads server-side (the schedule's `_shared/gcKernels` recipe, `scripts/edge-kernels.mjs`, copies kernels, not loaders). That is PR 8b, when wanted; today the owner opens the sheet on Monday and presses the button.
- **Tests**: `mondaySheet.test.ts` (3: the tiles from the lights and headline, the mail's html, text and subject, escaping) and `BridgeMondaySheet.render.test.tsx` (1); `emailCatalog.test.ts` counts 53.
- **Docs**: `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` gains the function; guide *read the Bridge* gains "The Monday sheet"; the Glossary's Bridge entry.
