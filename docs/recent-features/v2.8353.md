# v2.8353 — Working hours: set a start and an end for each day of a person's week (2026-11-23)

The third of five PRs for punch list #429, after the database door (v2.8345) and the kernel (v2.8350). The row a dev or the controller sets hours on, and the lock on the two doors it replaces.

## What ships

**The row.** A person's sheet, **Pay & schedule**, a new line **Working hours** under *Paid* (`PersonDeskWorkingHoursRow.tsx`). It draws for an hourly person and a salaried one.

- **Lines.** Days that share their hours are one line, wherever they fall: *Mon to Fri*, *6:00 AM to 12:00 PM*, *6 h a day*. Then the week, the Sunday the hours hold from and who set them.
- **A line changes whole.** A press on a line's hours opens its field under it: a start and an end in 15-minute steps, **This week** or **Next week**, a sentence that says what the change comes to, and **Keep** and **Cancel**. The start and the end are kept together, and every save is a whole week.
- **Small moves.** **Give one day its own hours** asks which day and takes it out of the line. A day on its own has **Half day**, **Same as the other days**, **Add a break**, **Take the break out** and **Make it a day off**.
- **Pay.** For a person on a set week the field says *Each day keeps its length, so pay does not move*, or that pay follows the hours with the week's new total. A change that moves pay starts this week, counts the weeks with no pay report it prices again, and waits for a reason, which goes on the pay log.
- **The first time.** For a person on a set week the row asks one thing, the start. Each day runs its paid hours from there, so pay cannot move. **Set other hours** opens a plain line. An hourly person starts on Monday to Friday, 7:00 AM to 3:00 PM.
- **Coming.** Hours saved for next week read as *New from Sun Nov 29* under the lines.
- **Earlier hours.** Every save, the newest first, with who set it and why. **Take working hours off** ends them from this week, after a confirm.
- **Anyone else.** An owner reads the same lines with no press, a *dev or controller* tag and one sentence that says who sets them.

**The rules.** `workingHoursEdit.ts`, pure: what the open line would save, whether pay moves, when the start is held to this week, when a reason is asked, and the one thing to fix when Keep is dead. `workingHoursIo.ts` is the read and the one save, `set_working_hours`.

**Edit pay setup.** The seven day boxes are typed only by a dev or the controller, and only while nobody has set working hours. Otherwise they read as they stand, with a line that says why. An owner still changes the weekly pay and the dates.

**The lock.** Migration `20261123082812_working_hours_lock.sql` ([its page](../migrations/20261123082812_working_hours_lock.md)):

- A trigger on `people_pay_config` refuses a change to `salaried_week_hours` from anyone but a dev or the controller. A save that leaves the week as it was passes, and so does a new pay row on the default week, which the Hire form makes.
- An answer on `person_available_hours` is refused for a person whose working hours are in force (`has_working_hours`). Hours taken off open it again.

**The person's line.** `MyTimeCanWorkLine` at the foot of My Time reads working hours first (`myTimeHours` in `myTimeCanWork.ts`). With hours in force it says them a value at a time, *Your hours are 6:00 AM to 12:00 PM, Mon to Fri.*, then *The office sets these hours.*, with no press. Hours that start on a Sunday ahead show under a chip, *New from Sun Nov 29*. With none it is the old line and its press. A salaried person, who was never asked for a week, now sees the line too when they have working hours. Nothing on the card is measured against the hours.

**The bed script.** `scripts/pgtest-working-hours.sh` now re-applies every migration named `*_working_hours_*` and plays every scenario in `supabase/tests/working_hours/` in name order, so a later migration adds its file and leaves the script alone.

## Order

The client first, then the push. The row's save calls `set_working_hours`, which v2.8345's migration made. Until the lock is pushed the old doors still work, and the screen already hides them.
