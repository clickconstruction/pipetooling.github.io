# v2.8351 — Dashboard: swipe a report left to mark it opened (2026-11-23)

Step 5 of punch list #426. The owner's ask of 2026-11-23: *a swipe to mark opened feature like we are about to do for bids, the idea being that we can make it super easy for someone to clear work off their list.* Client only. No migration, no edge function.

## What changed

- **Recent Reports draws each report through `SwipeAwayRow`** (v2.8349), with **Mark opened**. It is the row My Bids hides a bid with: one gesture, one component, one rule (`src/lib/swipeAway.ts`).
- **On a device held in the hand** a report has no button, only a small arrow that says it opens. A swipe to the left past the mark marks it opened and moves it to **Opened**, with **Undo** for ten seconds. A shorter swipe springs back, and a touch that moves up or down scrolls.
- **The foot says the gesture** there: *Swipe a report left to mark it opened.*, beside **Mark N opened**. One at a time or all at once, side by side.
- **At a desk** nothing changes: **Mark opened** is a word press on each report and the foot's left side counts the new reports.
- **A report opened in place** swipes away too. Before this step it could only be cleared by a word press.
- The row is opaque now, so it covers the strip it slides over.

## The pair, stated once

| Card | The strip says | The row goes to | The press that brings it back |
|---|---|---|---|
| My Bids | **Hide** | **Hidden** | **Show again** |
| Recent Reports | **Mark opened** | **Opened** | **Mark unread** |
