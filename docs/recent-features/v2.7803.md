# v2.7803 — The pace card: Ask a master (2026-11-18)

Punch list #396, PR 2 of 3. Client only. No migration and no edge function: it rides the tagged job notes of punch list #363.

## Why

The owner's third point on the pace card: it gave a superintendent a reading and nothing to do about a doubt. His example was *requesting clarification from a master on the job*. He then asked whether the press sends the master a to-do card. It does not, on purpose: a Checklist task can be ticked without answering, and a tick sends nothing back to the man who asked.

## What landed

- **The press.** **Ask a master** sits on the pace card under the hours bar, for every role that can tag in a job note (`canTagInJobNotes`).
- **The window** is `AskMasterSheet`, a sheet from the bottom on a phone. It reads who can be tagged on the job (`job_note_taggable`) and offers the masters on the job, or the office's masters when the job names none he can tag. With one master it says *To* and the name. With more, he picks.
- **Quick questions** as chips: *Is 25% right?*, *What counts as done on Rough-in?* with the stage in hand named, and *The planned finish looks wrong.* A press puts the words in the box, where he can change them.
- **The numbers ride along.** The note is his question, then a line: *Pace card: 25% done, 9 of 70 h, finish Fri, Nov 20.* The window shows that line as *Sent with* before he sends.
- **Send names the person**: *Send to* and the first name. It posts through `job_note_post_tagged`, the same call the note box makes, then sends the tag's notice.
- **The card closes the loop.** Under the press it reads *Waiting on* the name while the tag is open, and for three days after it clears, that the master answered or said no reply is needed (`askedLine` over `useJobNoteTags`).

`lib/jobMode/askMaster.ts` holds who is asked, the note's words and every word on the window.

## What it does not do

- It makes no Checklist task and no new table.
- It does not decide who the master is. The server's list of who can be tagged on the job already holds the job's master.
