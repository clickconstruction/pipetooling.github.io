# v2.4825 — Legal portal: the Lien grid by court (2026-10-07)

Client only; no migration, no function. The owner (2026-10-07): *break the Lien grid down by court as well, so a lawyer can easily pick out the cases they want.* Mockup and plan: `to-dos/legal-portal-firm-ready/mockup-lien-grid-by-court.html`.

## What changed

- **The rail reads by GC or by court.** *GCs · Courts* sits at the top of the rail beside the firm's grid ([`LegalPortalLienGrid`](../../src/components/jobs/legal/LegalPortalLienGrid.tsx)). Switching clears the pick and the find box. *GCs* is as before.
- **By court**: *All courts*, then each county with its jobs and dollars, largest first, and under it its courts in precinct order: *Precinct 2*, *Precinct 1 or 2 · on the line*, *Precinct not named yet*, *Over $20,000 · county court*. *County not on the record* comes last. The counts follow *Due in 30 days* / *Upcoming*, and a pick the view empties falls back to *All courts*. A county is a pick too, for a lawyer whose practice is the county.
- **Over the limit stands apart.** A justice court hears $20,000 or less, so a bigger job reads under its county in red and never under a precinct, the way its chip in the Court column already reads.
- **Bands.** With more than one court shown, a band names each court with its jobs and total above its run of rows. The summary line names the pick: *2 jobs · $18,450 open · Hays County · Justice Court, Precinct 2*.
- **The print** names the pick in its title and carries the same bands: `lienGridHtml` takes optional `sections` ([`lienTimelineBook.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienTimelineBook.ts)). Without them it prints as before, so the office's Lien desk print is unchanged.
- **On a phone** the court chips name their county (*Hays · Pct 2*), and each band is a line above its cards (`tr[data-card-band]` in `src/index.css`).
- **Kernel**: [`legalLienGridView.ts`](../../src/lib/legal/legalLienGridView.ts) — `legalLienCourtOf` (county from the property record without the word County, then the precinct, the on-the-line note, not named yet, or over the limit), `legalLienGridCourts`, `filterLegalLienGrid` by `courtId`, `legalLienCourtSections`, `legalLienSectionWords`, `findLegalLienCourts`. `JUSTICE_COURT_LIMIT_WORDS` joins [`jpVenue.ts`](../../src/lib/legal/jpVenue.ts).

## Is this the best we can do?

- **The same rail, not a select.** A select hides the counts and dollars, which is what the GC rail was built to show.
- **A job over the limit is never under a precinct**, so a lawyer choosing a justice court never picks a case that court cannot hear.
- **Bands for every court at once**, so the whole book reads by court without a click, on the screen and on paper.
- **One lens at a time.** GC and court together would double the rail for a rare question.
- **Not done:** each justice court's address, phone and filing fee, which needs a court directory the office keeps; remembering a lawyer's last pick.
