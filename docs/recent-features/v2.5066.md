# v2.5066 — GC mode: move a bar on the schedule, and say why (2026-10-09)

PR 8a of the schedule's real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/SCHEDULE_REAL_BUILD.md`, PR 8, on branch `spike/gc-mode`; the lead cut PR 8 in two, 8a the moves and 8b the form and a part's own move). Moves on real data in the Schedule window, a dev's until the schedule's PR 10. No migration and no edge function: every write is PR 5's and 6b's.

## The change

- **A move goes through Why it moved** ([`GcScheduleMoves.tsx`](../../src/components/gc/GcScheduleMoves.tsx)): a bar dragged, an end pulled or a link drawn on the chart opens it. It says what the move does (the new days, what it pushes, the finish, a place it crowds) and saves only with a reason and a sentence: `moveRecord` and the bars `planMove` leaves, through 6b's `saveScheduleMove` (`gc_schedule_move`), with the version the window read and the log's line.
- **Changes to the schedule** lists every move, newest first, with who made it and why. **Undo** puts the newest standing move back and **Redo** makes it again, replayed on the server (`gc_schedule_undo`, `gc_schedule_redo`).
- **Someone saved first** (G-134): the save is refused with *The schedule changed while you were working.* The window stays open, lists each change since with its time and its line, reads the schedule again, and keeps the reason and the words. One press saves the move worked out afresh on the new dates. Undo and Redo say the same in the record.
- **Ported, not lifted**: the prototype's windows sent each press to its reducer; main's take the presses as callbacks and keep the prototype's words. Left out on purpose: telling the trades (PR 13), what a move does to the bills (Owner Billing's), a part's own move and the form (PR 8b), the what-if copy (PR 11). The record's empty line drops *or press one to change its dates* until 8b brings the form.
- **The kernel** ([`scheduleWindow.ts`](../../src/lib/gc/schedule/scheduleWindow.ts)) gains the log's lines as the prototype's reducer wrote them (`moveWords`, `undoWords`, `redoWords`) and `changeTimeWords`, a change's time on the company's clock.
- **Who may move**: a dev (`canMove`, `role === 'dev'` on the projects page), as the window and the `gc_schedule_*` policies are until PR 10. Anyone else sees the record without Undo.
