# v2.7966 — Permits: Builders drops an empty plumber column, and a quiet city's skim says so (2026-11-19)

Two lines on Prospects → Permits that said something the data could not back.

## Builders: the plumber column only where a plumber is named

Since [v2.7922](./v2.7922.md) no San Antonio builder shows a plumber, because the city issues a job's plumbing permit under the applicant's own name. Read live after that push: 0 of 1,231 builders carried a name, and 570 read *not named on the permits*. The column said one thing on every row.

`cityNamesPlumbers` (`permitBuilders.ts`) answers whether any builder in the city's list has a plumber named. When none has, `PermitBuilders` leaves out:

- the **Plumber they use** column on the desk,
- the *Plumber:* half of the phone card's line,
- the *Plumber they use* sentence on a builder's sheet.

No city is named in the code. Austin (125 builders with a plumber) and New Braunfels (4) keep the column, and a builder there with plumbing permits under its own name still reads *not named on the permits*. A city with no builders listed draws no column either.

## The skim: caught up in a quiet city

The caught-up skim ended *The next pull is tomorrow at 5:30 AM*. In a city whose own source has stopped (New Braunfels, quiet since Nov 3 when [v2.7880](./v2.7880.md) shipped) that reads as a promise of permits.

`caughtUpWords` (`permitSkim.ts`) takes the quiet city and its newest permit day and then ends *New Braunfels has published nothing since Nov 3. Our pull still runs every morning at 5:30 AM.* The tab passes the day only when `cityQuiet` reads `quiet`, so a pull of ours that stopped never makes the skim blame the city. The second-look deck's words never named the pull and are unchanged.

## Decided and left out

On the owner's word, 2026-11-19:

- **A newest-permit date on the Pulled line:** dropped. The city box already says it.
- **A Needs you line for a quiet city:** dropped. No press clears it.
- **The quiet allowances**, 7 days and 10 for San Antonio: kept. Thanksgiving week is their first test.
