# v2.3355 — Bid Costs: History & forecast (2026-09-12)

Client only, on top of v2.3354 (`bids.outcome_at`). Owner ask 2026-09-11: win percentage by money and by count every month, average time from submission to win or lose, a forecast of what we'll win from what's submitted, by person, and "click on the chart to see what constitutes certain bars". Mockup + reasoning: artifact 92caf94e.

## What changed

- **Kernel** [`src/lib/bids/bidForecast.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/bidForecast.ts) (tests beside it). Reads the pursuit ledger's rows for sent bids: `cohortsByMonth` (one entry per calendar month from the first sent bid, buckets won / lost / open / open-stale with the bids behind each, rate by count and by value, `deciding` inside `FORECAST_MATURITY_DAYS` = 120); `oddsBySize` (mature decided bids by `FORECAST_SIZE_BANDS` — under $50k, $50k–$500k, over $500k); `oddsForPerson` (own odds at `FORECAST_MIN_DECIDED_FOR_OWN_RATE` = 10 decided, band by band at `FORECAST_MIN_BAND_DECIDED` = 5, else everyone's); `forecastOpen` (fresh opens at their band's odds → expected count, expected value, one-σ range, the largest bid's share and odds; stale opens set aside); `forecastByEstimator`; `decisionTimes` (sent → `outcome_at`, median / p10 / p90 / by decision month, `enough` at `DECISION_TIMES_MIN_SAMPLE` = 5); `openAgeBuckets`. `PursuitRow` gained `sentYmd` and `outcomeAtYmd`.
- **Lens** [`BidsForecastLens.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/BidsForecastLens.tsx): person chips (everyone + anyone with 3+ sent bids, "No estimator" counted), By count / By value, robot fold shared with the ledger; a recharts `ComposedChart` (stacked won / lost / open / open-stale bars, two rate lines on a right axis, a "still deciding" reference area); decision-time panel with its honest empty state and the open-age buckets; the odds cards; the forecast tiles and the per-person table. **Every bar, card, bucket, tile and row opens a drill list** under the panels (bid, estimator, sent, value, outcome, decided); a drill row selects the bid across the workflow tabs.
- [`BidsBidCostsTab.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/BidsBidCostsTab.tsx): fourth lens chip "History & forecast".

## Why the odds are by size

Bids under $50k win ~36–40 % by count, $50k–$500k ~11 %, over $500k 0 of 4. One blended rate would count the largest open bid (SPACEX BA-2, 56 % of fresh open value) at the average and the forecast would be that bid. The lens counts it at 0 % and says so on the tile.
