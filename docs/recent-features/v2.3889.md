## What shipped

- **`src/lib/people/hoursPhone.ts`** (new, pure) — `whosInRows` (one row per person clocked in, A–Z, the session so far), `leftTodayRows` (closed sessions today summed per person, anyone in now left off), `approvalsPhonePeople` (one row per person with hours waiting — days, sessions, hours, the sessions naming no job — the longest-waiting first), `hoursPhoneClock`.
- **`src/components/people/PeopleHoursPhoneView.tsx`** (new) — the sticky switch *Who's in · Approvals · Week & sessions* with counts; the two lists, read by the component itself (open sessions, today's closed ones, everything pending) under the caller's row security; a person opens a full-screen dialog holding `PersonDeskHoursSection`.
- **`src/pages/People.tsx`** — under 640 px, with hours access, the Hours tab draws the phone view first; the pay tools and the tab's sections are the third view, hidden (not unmounted) under the other two, so nothing reloads when the switch moves. At 640 px and up nothing changes.
- **The guide** *work the hours grid on a phone* opens with the three views.

## One departure from the owner's call

The call (2026-09-27) was that the week grid is not drawn on a phone. It is kept, under *Week & sessions*: its phone mode is the only place a day's hours can be typed from a phone, and taking that away was not what the call was asked about. The phone opens on *Who's in*, so the grid is no longer the first thing met. Leftover L11 puts the question back to the owner.

## Known limits

- The Approvals window the person screen opens still wears its desk layout (96 % of the width).
