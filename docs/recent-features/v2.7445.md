# v2.7445 — Lien desk: the leader's message goes whoever in the office presses (2026-11-16)

The owner, 2026-11-16, after a look at the desk: *fix The leader's message*. One edge function (`notify-lien-approval`, redeploy) and the client. No migration.

## What was wrong

Three holes, all read from code and each then proven against the function's own handler under vitest. On the live desk that day one notice had waited on the leader 40 days.

1. **A notice's message was its drafter's alone.** The function's plain branch (`item_id`, no `kind`) answered 403 *Not your draft* unless `job_lien_desk_items.drafted_by` was the caller. `drafted_by` is set once, when the draft is first saved (`saveLienDeskDraft`), so it names whoever first opened the draft, never whoever sends it or reminds.
2. **The send swallowed the refusal.** The notice pane's **Send for approval** called `tellLeaderOfLienApproval(...).catch(() => null)` and read the null as *Sent for approval. … will see it on the Dashboard.* A second office person sent a paper, the leader was told nothing, and the office had no sign of it. **Remind** (v2.7371) named the failure per paper, so there it showed.
3. **An affidavit's reminder sent nothing and was counted as sent.** The affidavit kind keeps a row only when it was sent for approval in the last fifteen minutes (`lienAffidavitAskable`, v2.7180), so the call can only repeat what the desk just did. Remind is by its nature a later call: for any older affidavit the function answered 200 *Not awaiting approval* with `told: 0`, and the desk added the affidavits to *was reminded of N papers*.

## What landed

- `supabase/functions/notify-lien-approval/index.ts`: the two asks the office makes, a notice and an affidavit, share one gate, read before the paper is: the caller's `users` row must hold an office role (`OFFICE_ROLES`) and not be archived, else 403 *Not the office*. The drafter check is gone. A notice's ask also checks what widening the gate made matter: the paper is on the desk (404 *Paper not found*), it is a notice (400 *Not a lien notice*, so an affidavit or a retainage notice is never worded as one) and it is awaiting approval and not voided (200, nothing sent, as before). `pen_recorded`, `code_used` and `waiver_code_used` are untouched: each proves whose record or screen it is, whatever the caller's role.
- `remind: true` on either ask marks the To do view's Remind press. An affidavit's fifteen minutes are not asked of a reminder (`lienAffidavitAskable(row, now, { reminder })`; kind, status and voided still are). The email's first line reads *Wendi is reminding you of a lien notice that waits for your approval.* (`reminder` on `lienApprovalPush` and `lienAffidavitApprovalPush`, `_shared/lienApprovalPush.ts`); the push's title, line, address and tag are the same. The name was already the caller's and never the drafter's; the type's note said otherwise and is corrected.
- `src/lib/jobs/lienDeskIo.ts`: `tellLeaderOfLienApproval` returns a `LienLeaderTellResult` and never throws, through one `askLeaderTell` it shares with `tellLeaderOfLienAffidavits`. A refusal comes back as the function's own words (`readEdgeFunctionErrorBody`), a lost signal as `formatErrorMessage`'s. The answer now carries `held`, `said` (the function's `message`) and `told`. `leaderToldWords` left the file.
- `src/lib/jobs/lienLeaderTold.ts` (new, pure): `lienNoticeToldWords` (the three sentences as before, and in amber *Sent for approval. The message to Malachi did not go. Not the office. The paper is on the Dashboard. Tell Malachi another way.*), `lienTellMissReason` (a 200 that reached no phone and no inbox: *not waiting for approval now*, *no leader to tell*, *outside the leader's Push me hours, so it waits on the Dashboard*, *no phone set up and no email sent*), `lienReminderTally` (one call counted: done only when a message went, the affidavits by the function's `told`) and `lienReminderAffidavitLabel`.
- `LienDesk.tsx`, two call sites only: `sendToLeader` shows `lienNoticeToldWords` with its tone, and `remindLeader` sends `{ remind: true }` and counts through the tally.

## Old client, new function, and the other way round

The function's change is safe under the client as it was: the body it sends is unchanged, and the only new refusals are for callers and papers the desk never sends. The new client under the function as it was reads its refusal out loud (*The message to Malachi did not go. Not your draft.*), and an affidavit's reminder reads *not waiting for approval now* instead of counting as sent. So the order of the merge and the deploy does not matter, and each half is better alone than before.

## Not done here

A retainage notice still has no message, on a send or on a reminder (`LIEN_REMINDER_HOLDS.retainage`): the press leaves it out and says so. After a send, a leader outside their Push me hours still reads *will see it on the Dashboard*, which is true and does not say why.
