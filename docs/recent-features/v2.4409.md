# v2.4409 — Demand letter on a phone: the buttons at the foot take less room (2026-10-02)

No migration, no edge function. Left open by [v2.4398](v2.4398.md) (*Not done*); Robert, 2026-10-02: "should we fix the demand letter's foot on a phone?"

## What was wrong

Measured at 390 × 844 on the dev server against production data, job 273, Lien instruments → Demand letter. The foot's five buttons (Cancel, Print packet, Download PDF · N documents, Email with the PDF…, Save & record send…) wrapped onto four lines: **194 px** of a 776 px card, the letter got 375. With **Email with the PDF…** open, its panel (235 px) drew *above* the button row and both showed: 429 px of foot, the letter down to **140 px**.

## What changed

[`LienInstrumentsModal`](../../src/components/jobs/LienInstrumentsModal.tsx), at 640 px and under (`useIsMobile`); a computer's foot is as it was.

- **Two rows of two.** The foot (`data-demand-foot`) is a two-column grid: Print packet · Download PDF, then Email with the PDF… · Save & record send…. Each button is half the row and at least 44 px tall, its name on up to two lines (`phoneFootButton`). The names are unchanged.
- **No Cancel.** The × on the title line (v2.4398) closes the window, so the phone does not draw a second door for it.
- **The email panel stands in for the row.** While it is open the button row is not drawn: the panel has its own **Back** and **Send**. The record panel already replaced the row.
- Guide [`file-a-lien-and-never-miss-its-deadlines`](../../src/content/help/file-a-lien-and-never-miss-its-deadlines.md): two lines in *On a phone*.

## Verified

Same job, same size, read only (no send, no record). Foot **118 px** (was 194); the letter **451 px** (was 375). The four buttons are 159 px wide and 44 to 46 px tall, with no sideways scroll. Email open: the letter **334 px** (was 140), the panel's buttons are Back and Send, and Back brings the row back. The render smokes pin both sizes.
