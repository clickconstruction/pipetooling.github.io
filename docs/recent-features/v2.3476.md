# v2.3476 — Supply house invoice form rebuilt: sections, inline job search, job-account card, Paid on, pinned footer (2026-09-15)

Client only — no migration, no edge function. Follows v2.3473 (the scroll fix) with the seven-change proposal the owner approved from the before/after mock-up.

## Kernel — `src/lib/materials/supplyHouseInvoiceForm.ts` (26 tests)

- `poCodeHint(text, ledgerCodes)` / `poCodeHintText(hint, house)` — the PO Generator check the table's red icon did after Save (`parsePoGeneratorCodeFromPurchaseOrderName` + `poGeneratorCodesForSelectedHouse`), now a live hint: hand PO (no code) · on the ledger (green) · not on the ledger (red, `AlertCircle`) · quiet while the ledger hasn't loaded.
- `dueDateHint(house, monthlyPaymentDay)` + `ordinalDay` — "Ferguson's payment day is the 10th." under the prefilled due date.
- `paidAtIsoFromYmd` / `paidOnYmdFromIso` / `paidAtPayload(isPaid, ymd, priorIso)` — "Paid on" ↔ `supply_house_invoices.paid_at` (company noon, so the day survives every locale and DST). The payload carries `paid_at` only when the office typed a day that differs from what's stored; the baseline trigger `sync_supply_house_invoice_paid_at` still stamps `now()` on the flip to paid with no day and nulls it on the flip back.
- `addAllocation` / `removeAllocation` / `setAllocationPct` / `allocationTotal` — the even-split and rebalance arithmetic the chips did inline, with the last job absorbing rounding and an all-zeros rest handed the remainder evenly.
- `invoiceSaveLabel` — "Save invoice" / "Save changes" / "Saving…".

## Form — [`SupplyHousesTab.tsx`](../../src/components/SupplyHousesTab.tsx)

- **Sections** (`InvoiceFormSection` caption + rule): *From the invoice* (Invoice # · Invoice date · Amount with a `$` adornment, right-aligned tabular digits; then Purchase order # + the PO hint) · *Which job* · *Paying it* · *Paperwork*.
- **Inline job search** replaces the stacked "Add job for invoice" modal (`invoiceJobSearchModal` state removed): the same `search_jobs_ledger` RPC and `UnifiedSearchResultRow`, debounced 300 ms, results in-flow under the box (no clipping inside the scroller); Enter never submits the form, Escape clears; a job already on the invoice is dimmed/disabled.
- **Job cards** (`data-invoice-job-card`): plain-J label like the search row (`formatJobLedgerNumberLabel(DEFAULT_JOB_LEDGER_PREFIX, …)`), `stripTrailingZip` address, × to remove. **Percent boxes render only with ≥ 2 jobs**; the total + "job accounts are per property" note likewise. Zero jobs: one muted line.
- **Job account on the card** (single-job only): checkbox "On {house}'s job account", teal left edge when on; the v2.3430 state lines (open / not needed / amber nudge with **Mark opened…**) and `MarkJobAccountOpenedModal` unchanged, `data-invoice-job-account` attrs kept.
- **Paying it**: Due date + hint · Status radios *Not paid yet* / *Paid on* + date (defaults to the stored day or today when flipped; disabled while unpaid). `openEditInvoice` seeds `invoicePaidOn` from `paid_at`.
- **Paperwork**: "Invoice PDF" url field + **Open ↗** anchor (new tab) once the value is an http(s) URL.
- **Pinned footer**: `position: sticky; bottom: 0` with negative margins cancelling the panel padding — Delete (edit) left, Cancel / Save right, mirroring the v2.990 pinned title bar. Title bar subtitle carries the house and, when editing, the invoice number.
