# v2.7536 — Checklist: the old-task list is Still open, and red means late (2026-11-16)

Punch list #369, step A2 of the Task Mode train, stacked on A1 (v2.7533). The lower half of Checklist → Today. No migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

The Outstanding list drew a red frame, a red *N need doing* pill and a red line on every row: *due Thu, Aug 20 · 88 days ago*. Read through the dev seat on 2026-11-16, none of the 17 open one-off tasks had a due day. The day in red was only the day the task was given (`overdueAgeLabel` read `scheduled_date`). So every row was red and none was late, and red said nothing.

## What changed

- **Still open · N**, a small-caps label like the rest of the page, with *oldest first* beside it. The red frame and the red pill are gone.
- **A task with no due day says *given Thu, Aug 20*** and wears its age as a pill at the row's end: grey under a week, amber from a week on (`stillOpenAge`, `STILL_OPEN_OLD_DAYS`).
- **Red is for a due day that has passed**: *due Fri, Nov 6* with a red *10 days late*. A task still inside its window reads its due day in grey, and *due today* in amber.
- **Late leads.** A task whose due day has passed is lifted to the top of the list (`lateFirst`), and the words beside the label read *late first, then oldest*. Sorted by due day alone, a late task with a recent due day sat under the 88-day ones and behind the fold.
- **The fold.** Three rows, then **Show N more** (`stillOpenShown`). A list of four is drawn whole. A row opened from the map is never left folded.
- **The whole tag.** The roadmap chip on Today's cards and on these rows wraps. It was cut at 230 px.
- **The map follows.** On *Where my tasks are*, an older task's dot is amber and a late one's is red (`old` beside `overdue` on `taskPlaceDots` and `taskPlaceDotGroups`, `TaskPlaceLine`). When Next is an older task, its card's number wears the same colour.
- Notes on a row read *2 notes* in words.

## How it is built

- `todayPage.ts` gains `stillOpenAge`, `stillOpenIsLate`, `lateFirst`, `stillOpenShown` and the labels, 11 more tests. `checklistDueDates.ts` exports its `dayLabel` and `daysBetween`.
- `ChecklistOutstandingSection` keeps its name, its props and its rows' behaviour. Only its frame, its head, the age and the fold changed.
- `overdueAgeLabel` in `checklistHistoryLedger.ts` has no caller left. It stays with its test until a later sweep.
