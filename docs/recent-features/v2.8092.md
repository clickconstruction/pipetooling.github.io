# v2.8092 — Permits, Market: the clock's days no longer run into the word beside them (2026-11-20)

Found by the audit of punch list #405 ([the card](../../to-dos/permits-market-page/README.md)), on the running page at 390 px. The clock is [v2.7978](./v2.7978.md).

## What was wrong

Under a clock's bar sit three things: the word at its start (*applied* or *issued*), the middle wait under its mark, and the scale at its end. The middle wait was centred on its mark and never left of 14% of the bar. Where a plumber's permit follows the building permit within days, the mark sits at the bar's start. On a phone the bar is about 350 px wide, so 14% is about 50 px, and the days were drawn over the word: *issued7 days*. At 320 px it was worse.

## What changed

- `clockMiddleNearStart` (`permitMarket.ts`) says when the mark is too near the start for its days to sit under it: under 34% of the bar, which leaves room for the word and the widest days (*the same day*) on a 320 px phone.
- `ClockBar` (`PermitMarket.tsx`) then writes the days beside the word, in one line with a gap, so they cannot overlap at any width. Further along the bar the days sit under the mark as before.

## Checked

`permitMarket.test.ts` holds the rule. `PermitMarket.render.test.tsx` holds that a short wait is drawn once, beside the word, and a long one apart from it. On the dev server at 390 px and 320 px, light and dark, in Austin and San Antonio.
