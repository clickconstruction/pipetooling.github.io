# v2.5637 — Quick replies by keyboard: the / list in the reply box (2026-10-19)

Punch list #126, the owner's pick A (with C, a shortcut per line, next). The office sends the same few messages every day, and the app keeps them as quick replies, but no key reached one: the strip and *Find a reply* were presses only. Now a `/` typed at the start of the reply box, or after a space or a line break, opens a list of the person's quick replies above the box. No migration; no edge function; nothing in the edge kernels copy.

- **The kernel** (`src/lib/quickReplies/slash.ts`): `slashWordAt(text, caret)` finds the `/` word the caret sits in, from the last space before it to the first space after it; it is one only when it starts with the `/`, so *1/2 inch*, *and/or*, *10/19*, a link, `//` and a `/` with a space after it open nothing, and words past `SLASH_WORDS_MAX` (40) read as a sentence. `slashLines` is the strip's own `qrGroups` with the words as its narrow box, flattened: the job's stage, the step they are on, their kind, anyone, the most used first, a line found by its English or its Spanish, blanks' names included (`/pay` finds `{bill}`'s *pay it here* lines). `slashPick` puts the filled line in place of the `/` word and keeps the words around it; `slashMove` wraps ↑ ↓; `slashHead` and the words (`SLASH_HINT`, `SLASH_KEYS`, `SLASH_NONE`, `SLASH_READING`).
- **The list** (`reply/SlashList.tsx`): drawn above the box, so a phone's keyboard never covers it, each row the line as it would go (`FilledLine`, the typed words lit, a blank the app cannot fill in amber) with its step's name at the right, a head *Quick replies for Dana · 3 of 11*, the keys under it. A press on a row picks it without taking the caret from the box (`onMouseDown`).
- **The box** (`reply/ReplyBox.tsx`): the caret is kept (`onChange`, `onSelect`); the lines are read the first time a `/` word appears, as the strip reads them when it opens. ↑ ↓ light a row, Enter or Tab picks it (Shift + Enter stays a line break, and an IME's composing Enter is left alone), Escape closes the list for that `/` word and stops there, so the window around the box (`ResponsiveModalShell`'s window listener) stays open. A pick fills from the same `replyFills` and `lineWords` a strip press uses, counts as a press (`quick.press`; the app's own stage lines are never counted), and leaves the caret after the line. The box names the list (`aria-controls`) and the lit row (`aria-activedescendant`) and keeps its `textbox` role. The placeholder of a text or an email gains *Type / for a quick reply.*; a team note has no list.
- **Where**: every reply box, so the Messages thread, GC projects' thread window, the job window's Text and Email, and the Pipeline row's customer sheet.
- **Tests**: `slash.test.ts` (which `/` opens, the order and the narrowing, the Spanish, the stage and the step first, a trade partner's lines, the pick, the wrap); `ReplyBox.slash.render.test.tsx` (the hint, no read until a `/`, the rows and the lit one, narrow and Enter with the press counted, ↓ ↑ and Tab, a mouse pick, Escape kept from the window, no match, a fraction, a note). `ReplyBox.render.test.tsx` reads the new placeholder. Checked in a browser over the real `ReplyBox` with made-up lines at desktop width and 390 px: the list opens above the box, ↓ and Enter fill it with the caret at the end and the focus kept, Escape closes it with the words kept.
- **Docs**: the guide *reply to a person from their thread* gains **Type / for a quick reply**; *edit the quick replies* opens from the Messages page (not only GC projects) and both take *macro*, *canned reply*, *template*, *saved reply*, *shortcut* and *hotkey* as keywords; the Glossary's R3 line and `MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md`'s reply box paragraph.
