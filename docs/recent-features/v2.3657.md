# v2.3657 — Lien desk: the four gates are numbered steps under a plain headline (2026-09-20)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Follows v2.3522 (the gates as chips) and v2.3656 (dark-mode contrast).

## Why

The owner, from a screenshot of job 883: the *Before it can go out · 2 of 4* row *"blends in too much with the rest of the app and then just becomes text the person has to decipher"* — and asked for the gates laid out laterally without wrapping at full width, or stacked 1-2-3-4 when the pane is narrow. Mocked up in the session, approved as drawn.

Two things were wrong beyond the look. The chips were the same small chips used everywhere on the desk, and they wrapped wherever the names happened to end, so a gate had no fixed place. And **"2 of 4" counted an unknown property kind the same as a missing owner**, though only the owner stops the send (`draftReadiness`) — the footer already said so.

## What shipped

- **Kernel** — [`lienDeskGates.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDeskGates.ts): `buildLienDeskGates(input)` returns the four gates in fixed slots (1 owner · 2 original contractor · 3 property kind · 4 approved hours), each with a `value` and a `tone` — `blocker` for exactly the facts `draftReadiness` refuses on (no owner, no mailing address, a public owner, no GC, no month picked), `check` for an unknown property kind, `ok` otherwise — and a verdict: *Can't go out yet* / *Ready to go out*, with *1 blocker · 1 to check* / *1 to check* / *All 4 clear*. 7 tests.
- **Component** — [`LienDeskGates.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskGates.tsx): the headline with a four-segment bar, one cell per gate (number badge, label, value; the whole sentence stays in the tooltip), and a detail block per gate that is not clear, led by its number and label so the door reads as belonging to its gate even in the one-row layout. The modal passes the details: the owner sentence and `LienDeskOwnerPane` under 1 (the pane's unconfirmed-owner Confirm still shows there when the gate is clear; an empty detail hides itself), a new *Set the GC ›* door under 2, *Set property kind ›* under 3, and under 4 either *tick at least one month* or the pending-sessions note.
- **Layout** — `.lienGates` in [`index.css`](../../src/index.css) is its own container (`container: lien-gates / inline-size`): four equal columns that never wrap, and under 560px of *pane* width a single column in 1–4 order with each detail under its own row. Values ellipsize inside their cell rather than pushing the next gate to a new line. Only a blocker gets the heavy red cell; a check is a lighter amber; a clear gate is quiet. Badge fills are literal, per v2.3656.
- **The pinned strip** carries the same headline and lists any gate that is not clear as *n · label: value*.
- `LienDeskOwnerPane` loses its own left indent — the detail block's rule indents it now.
- Guide *send lien notices from the Lien desk* → "Reading the notice before it goes" describes the numbered gates, blocker vs to check.

## Verified

- Kernel suite; `LienDeskModal.render.test.tsx` — the blocked job reads *Can't go out yet · 1 blocker · 1 to check* with gate 1 a blocker and its detail numbered; owner on file with the kind blank reads *Ready to go out · 1 to check*, tones `ok, ok, check, ok`, and the property-kind door still opens Edit Job.
- Dev server on prod data, dark mode, job 883: four cells on one row at a 637px pane; at a 375px viewport the cells stack 1–4 with the owner detail between 1 and 2, no horizontal scroll.
- Typecheck, lint, theme check, tests, guide suite.
