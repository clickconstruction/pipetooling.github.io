- **Award in Compare quotes** (`GcCompareQuotes.tsx`): once the job is won, a dev's foot row trades *Carry this number* for **Award and draft the statement of work**, with an **Estimator** pick (our team, the one pressing first). A company `awardGate` stops shows the button off with `canAward`'s words, and the quote awarded reads **Awarded**. The press is `awardGcTrade` (`gc_award`), then the projects reload.
- **The statement of work on the trade's card** (`GcTradeSow.tsx`): drafted, waiting on their signature or signed; awarded on and by whom; price, retainage and plan set; our schedule of values beside theirs with the add-up check (`theirSovGap`); what they will not do; and, while drafted, **Send to their portal to sign** (`sendGcSow`: draft to sent with the company's day, under the dev policy).
- **The mapper** (`boardRows.ts`): `gc_sows` and `gc_sow_lines` to `TradePackage.sow` (a cancelled one reads as none; nothing reported or billed until Building's draws), the award's ask, who and day, and what is carried follows the award. `projectRows.ts` carries the award columns and drops the three money columns B6-a took off `gc_projects` (with five test fixtures). `types.ts` gains the spike's `awardedBy`, `awardedOn` and `Sow.theirSov`.
- **The email** (`sowEmail.ts`, kind `sow`, key `<sow id>:sow`, the Portal's own words, agreed with the Portal lane) waits behind `SOW_SIGN_SCREEN_LIVE`, which the Portal's P2c sets when a trade can sign in its portal.
- **Proof**: `boardRows.test.ts` reads the gc_award bed's Lonestar statement of work back through the mapper; `sowEmail.test.ts`; `GcCompareQuotes.render.test.tsx` and `GcTradeSow.render.test.tsx`. Guide `award-a-trade-on-a-gc-project`.
