# v2.4562 — Lien cards and steps open the job's Lien window (2026-10-05)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Rows 10 and 13 of the lien defects card (punch list #83, `to-dos/lien-defects.md` on PR #4528). Stacked on v2.4561 (the same parser).

## The defects

- **Row 10.** The Dashboard and Quickfill cards *A filed lien has not been served* and *demand-letter deadline passed* navigated to `/jobs?tab=stages`: the bare board, no window, no job named.
- **Row 13.** *Open the Lien instruments* on a person's journey (the demand-letter and owner-notice steps) linked to `/jobs?jobDetail=<id>`, the Job window.

No URL opened a job's Lien window, so both needed one.

## The fix

- [`stagesDeepLinks.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/stagesDeepLinks.ts): a ninth door, `?lienwindow=<job id>` with an optional `lientab` (`demand` · `notice` · `affidavit` · `release_record`; the demand letter unless named). `lienWindowHref(jobId, tab)` builds the address.
- [`useStagesDeepLinkParams.ts`](../../src/hooks/useStagesDeepLinkParams.ts): the door runs after `lienDesk`, consumed once and stripped like the others.
- [`JobsStagesTab.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsStagesTab.tsx): the door reads the job by id (`fetchJobWithDetailsById`, as the desk's book rows do, so a job the board has not loaded still opens) and opens `LienInstrumentsModal` on the tab; a job that cannot be read says so.
- [`DashboardPinnedQuickRow.tsx`](../../src/components/dashboard/DashboardPinnedQuickRow.tsx) and [`QuickfillNeedsYouSection.tsx`](../../src/components/quickfill/QuickfillNeedsYouSection.tsx): *serve* opens the lien whose serve-by day is first on `affidavit`; *demand deadline* opens the first overdue job on `demand`. With no job to name they fall back to the board.
- [`personJourney.ts`](../../src/lib/journeys/personJourney.ts): both steps link through `lienWindowHref`.
