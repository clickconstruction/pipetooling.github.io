# v2.7408 — Painted maps: the shared page says how a line was made in one sentence, under its length (2026-11-15)

Punch list #345. Taunya, looking at the shared page on 2026-11-15: *drawn by hand* and *A length here is a guide for how far. It is not a measurement.* should be one sentence, below the line that says *About 930 ft*.

## What changed

- **The length stands alone on its row** on `/way/<code>`: *About 930 ft, give or take 10 ft*. The *· drawn by hand* that trailed it is gone from that row.
- **One sentence under it** says how the line was made and what the length is worth: *Drawn by hand, so the length is a guide for how far, not a measurement.* A walked line reads *Walked, so the length is…*.
- **A measured length is not called a guide.** A line whose length was typed from a wheel or a tape says only how it was made: *Walked.* Before, the foot of the card said *It is not a measurement* under a length that was one.
- **A map with several lines** says how under each (*Driven.*) and the guide once at the foot, as one sentence, when any of its lengths is about.

## How it is built

- `wayLineUnderWords(line, measured, alone)` and `WAY_LENGTH_GUIDE_WORDS` in `paintedWay.ts`, pure (+3 tests). `PaintedWay.tsx` draws them.
- The print, the picture and the files for another map keep their own foot line. They were not asked about and each is one fixed page.

## Is this the best we can do

- **Several lines repeat how under each.** It is one or two words, and a map with a walked line and a drawn one needs both said.
- **The print still says it in two sentences.** A later small step if the page's words read better.
