# v2.6928 — An open price sheet reads itself once (2026-11-11)

Punch list #308, a fix found on the live walk of PRs 4–9 (v2.6908–v2.6923), the night they merged. With a price sheet open beside Takeoffs, the panel read its sheet, its rows, its fixtures and every house number from the server about 220 times a second, until the window closed. The flood starved the browser's connections, so the next press (picking the sheet's house) failed with *No connection*.

## What caused it

Two loops fed each other in `PriceSheetPanel`:

- **The offers.** The panel hands Takeoffs the fixtures it can place (`onRowOffers`), a new list on every plan. Takeoffs keeps the list in state, draws again, and hands the panel a new `takeoff` object, which plans the fixtures again and sent another new list. A render loop on its own.
- **The read.** `reload` listed the door's `onGone` among its dependencies, and the door passes a new arrow on each of its renders. Every Takeoffs draw made a new `reload`, and the effect that runs it read the sheet again.

## What changed

- **`src/components/materials/PriceSheetPanel.tsx`**: the offers go to Takeoffs only when their content changed (a JSON key kept in a ref, cleared when the panel closes), and `reload` keeps `onGone` in a ref, so it depends on the paper's id alone.
- **`PriceSheetPanel.reads.render.test.tsx`**: the panel inside a parent that behaves as Takeoffs does (keeps the offers, a new takeoff, new lists and new callbacks on every draw) reads the sheet at most twice. On the old panel the test never settles: its worker runs out of memory.

## How to verify

Open a price sheet from the *Price sheet ▾* door on a bid's Takeoffs and count the requests to `supply_papers` for ten seconds: one read when it opens, none after.
