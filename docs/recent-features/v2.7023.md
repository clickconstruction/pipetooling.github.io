# v2.7023 — Billed money windows: one close button, easier to press (2026-11-12)

v2.7019 redrew the close press on the **Payment forecast** window and left a note: the sibling windows on Jobs → Stages still drew the old one, each with its own inline copy. This is that change.

## What was there

Read in the installed Chrome at 1400 and 375 wide, in both themes, before any edit:

- Five windows drew a bare `×` character with 4 px of padding: 20 × 33 px, on a phone too. They are **Chart** on Billed Awaiting Payment (`BilledAgingChartModal.tsx`), **Chart** on Paid in Full (`PaidProfitChartModal.tsx`), **Share / Print** (`BilledReportShareModal.tsx`), *Email payment forecast* (`PaymentForecastShareModal.tsx`) and *Email money waiting report* (`MoneyWaitingShareModal.tsx`).
- Two drew a `✕` character with 0.15rem of padding: 17.6 × 21.6 px. They are **Pay speeds** (`PaySpeedsBreakdownModal.tsx`) and **Payment follow-up** (`PaymentChaseModal.tsx`).
- None had a hover look or a focus ring.
- Two presses wore the `✉` text character: **Email this weekly…** on Pay speeds and the **email** link in Payment follow-up.

## What changed

- **One close press.** `src/components/shared/WindowCloseButton.tsx` draws the library's `X` at 18 px in a 32 px square, and a 44 px press at 640 px and under. It takes `onClose`, a `label` (default *Close*) and a `style` for placement only. Its looks are `.windowClose` in `src/index.css`, which is v2.7019's `.forecastHeadClose` moved to a general name: the muted fill on hover, a 2 px ring on keyboard focus, and the 7 px pull into the window's padding that puts the X itself on the content's edge.
- **Eight windows use it**: the Payment forecast window and the seven above. Every accessible name is the one the window had: *Close*, *Close pay speeds breakdown*, *Close payment follow-up*.
- **The Payment forecast header keeps its grid.** `.forecastHead > .windowClose` places the press in the `close` area; the `.forecastHeadClose` rules are gone.
- **Two headers moved a few pixels to share the press's centre line.** Pay speeds centres its title row. Payment follow-up gives its title a 32 px line and takes the difference off the padding above, so the title and its count line stay where they were.
- **The two envelope presses** wear the library's `Mail` icon at 16 px, stroke 2. The icon hangs from the words' baseline (`vertical-align`, not a flex row), because the **email** link sits on a baseline-aligned row beside the phone number. Their accessible names lose the envelope character and are now the words alone.
- The guides *see which customers pay slowly* and *chase late payments with call mode* name the two presses by their new words.

## How it was checked

- `WindowCloseButton.render.test.tsx` (new): found by *Close* or by the window's own name, a press calls `onClose`, the class and the placement style land, the icon stays out of the name.
- The windows' own render tests pass unchanged: `BilledPaymentForecastModal`, `PaySpeedsBreakdownModal`, `PaymentChaseModal`, `StagesBilledMoneyModals`. The forecast test still closes Pay speeds by *Close pay speeds breakdown*.
- Live, on a local copy of the app signed in against the real data: all eight windows at 1400 and 375 wide, in light and dark, before and after. Every request that was not a GET was refused, and a function read was re-sent as a GET, which the database runs read-only. In each of the 32 views the press measured 32 × 32 on the desk and 44 × 44 on the phone, took the muted fill under the pointer and the ring on Tab, closed its window, and left no sideways scroll. No email was sent.

## Left alone

- **Fix bill lines** (`FixBillLinesModal.tsx`) draws the same bare `×`. It opens only while a billed job has open money on no bill line, and the board had none that day, so it could not be walked. Its swap is one line.
- Every other window in the app. No shared window header exists yet; `WindowCloseButton` is the first shared piece, and a window takes it by swapping its own close button for it.
- The `✉` marks that are not presses: the sent-state words on a bill row and in the by-customer window, the lien desk's mail marks.
- Payment follow-up has no phone layout: at 375 wide its call pane is a narrow column beside the queue. That is the window's own layout, not the close press.
