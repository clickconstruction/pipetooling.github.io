# v2.3451 — Job accounts from the bid: the estimator's tools at the win moment (2026-09-14)

Migration `20260914280000_estimator_job_accounts.sql` (fragment in `docs/migrations/`); no edge function. Follows the five-PR train (v2.3423–v2.3440) and the live-test fix (v2.3444); the owner's "reasonable idea, build it" on the second pass of the artifact *Job Accounts from the Bid*.

## Why

The estimator bid the property, knows the GC, and already asked the houses for prices. Estimators read no `jobs_ledger` rows by design, so every job-anchored tool was closed to them. The first draft anchored a second copy of the record on the bid with a trigger; the second pass (this one) keeps the record where it is and gates the estimator on "the job carries a bid" — one helper, no schema change to the record, one inbox.

## What shipped

- **Migration**: `estimator_can_reach_job_account(job_id)`; the fourth branch on the account table's SELECT / INSERT / UPDATE (estimators may mark opened / not needed on bid-linked jobs — owner's call); `supply_house_job_accounts.bid_id` + estimator SELECT / INSERT-as-self policies; the estimator branch in `list_job_account_strip`; `job_account_job_identity(job_id)` (number, name, address, bid — under the two-way gate) so the prompt names the job without a jobs select; `list_bid_job_account_strip(bid_ids)` — the linked job and one row per house that expects an account, quoted the bid (`bid_rfqs`), or holds an account, with the rep's id, name, phone and **email**.
- **The question** ([`JobAccountsAfterCreatePrompt`](../../src/components/jobs/JobAccountsAfterCreatePrompt.tsx)): eligible for estimators; reads the job through the identity RPC; when the job carries a bid, the houses that quoted it come first with a *· quoted* mark and start picked (`orderHousesForBid`); **Ask the rep by email…** beside None needed. Reopenable from a bid through the new `openJobAccountsPrompt(jobId)` on `JobFormModalContext`.
- **Ask the rep by email** ([`JobAccountRepEmailSheet`](../../src/components/jobs/JobAccountRepEmailSheet.tsx)): pick the house; the job-accounts rep with an email (any authenticated user may read those contacts since v2.3423); the note composed by `composeRepEmail` from [`fetchBidPacketFacts`](../../src/lib/jobs/bidPacketFacts.ts) — the bid's address / project, the GC (per-GC row's submitted-to contact, else the bid's GC builder and contact), the start date, the owner of record when the office has it (soft: *to follow from our office* + the find-the-owner errand); Email from my inbox (mailto, the v2.1820 length guard) or Copy for email; then **Sent — log it** → a `requested` row on the job (`ignoreDuplicates`) and a send-log row with `send_method = user_email`, `supply_house_id`, `bid_id`.
- **The bid's Job block** ([`BidJobAccountsRow`](../../src/components/bids/BidJobAccountsRow.tsx) in `BidWonJobActions`, full shape only): chips per house from the bid-gated RPC (hook [`useBidJobAccountStrip`](../../src/hooks/useBidJobAccountStrip.ts), refetching on the job-created event and focus) and the **Job accounts…** door. Visible to estimators and the office alike.
- **Kernel** [`jobAccountRepEmail.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobAccountRepEmail.ts) (+5 tests): `composeRepEmail`, `repFirstName`, `orderHousesForBid`.
- Guide: *open a job account when you win a bid* (Bids & Estimating). ACCESS_CONTROL: the estimator section's job-account paragraph.
- Types hand-added (`job_account_job_identity`, `list_bid_job_account_strip`, the send log's `bid_id`); regenerate after the push.
