# v2.7595 — Prospects: the email templates' rules moved into their own tested file (2026-11-17)

The first cut of `src/pages/Prospects.tsx`, step 1 of the order in `docs/PROSPECTS_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md`, which the owner asked for whole (all nine cuts, in order). A pure move: no behaviour changes.

**What moved.** The Follow Up card's copy-template rules are now `src/lib/prospects/prospectCopyTemplates.ts`: the three keys with their labels and their two `app_settings` key tables, `getBlankPlaceholderFields` (which details a template still lacks for this prospect and this sender), `getBlankFieldsForMail` (the body's blanks and the subject's, each named once, the prospect's email first), and `substituteCopyPlaceholders` (the bracketed names filled in, the first underline by the template's own rule). They were module-level helpers in the page with no test.

**Two inline rules became functions.** `resolveCopyText(override, fallback)` holds the rule the page wrote twice, for the body and for the subject: a person's own words win, and an override that is missing or empty falls back to the company's default. `foldCopyTemplateRows(defaultRows, overrideRows)` holds the fold `loadCopyTemplates` did between its read and its four setters: an empty default body is skipped, an empty default subject is kept, a row for a key nobody knows is ignored.

**One formatter came along.** The templates write two moments through `formatDateTime`, so it is the first export of `src/lib/prospects/prospectFormat.ts`. The page imports it for its other nine uses. The five formatters still in the page follow in step 2.

**The types.** The kernel reads a prospect through `CopyProspect` (five nullable strings) and a comment through `CopyComment` (its kind and its time). The page's own `Prospect` and `ProspectComment` satisfy both, so no call site changed its arguments.

**Size.** `Prospects.tsx` went from 5,600 lines to 5,481. Its hooks, state and render are untouched.

**Proof.** 27 unit tests on the template rules and 2 on the formatter, all new: every blank the rules can name and the order they name them in, the underline on each of the three templates, the mail's extra ask for the prospect's email, the newest answered call as the last successful contact, the override fallback with its empty-string case, and the fold's skip rules. The formatter's test reads the day and the hour in the reader's own zone, so it holds at any hour and in CI. The page has no render test yet: that is step 3, and this step moved only functions that close over nothing.

**Docs.** The map marks step 1 and its Stage-A row as shipped. Its line numbers stay anchored at `6af60c5bcf`; below the module scope they now sit 119 lines lower in the file.
