# v2.4702 — Needs you: an owner signed for the records on their portal (2026-10-06)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. The line the plan for punch list #86 ([`to-dos/owner-records-portal/`](../../to-dos/owner-records-portal/README.md)) had at stop 4 and v2.4650 / v2.4651 did not build: until now the office saw a portal signing only by opening that request's Records for an owner window.

## What changed

- **Who waits.** [`ownerRecords.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/ownerRecords.ts): `ownerRecordsSignedWaiting(rows)` keeps the open `lien_owner_record_requests` whose acknowledgment carries the name the owner typed on the portal (`printedName`) and that are not recorded as sent, oldest signing first. A paper signature is the office's own act and is not counted. The hook [`useOwnerRecordsSignedNudge`](../../src/hooks/useOwnerRecordsSignedNudge.ts) reads the rows with `sent_at` null (RLS: dev, assistant, master), refetches on focus, and shows nothing on a failed read.
- **The line.** [`dashboardNeedsYou.ts`](../../src/lib/dashboardNeedsYou.ts): key `owner-records-signed`, amber, tier 20 with the unconditional releases (something owed to a customer). One reads *Umar Khan signed for the records on 9703 Lenox Hl* with the day they signed and the days waited; several read *N owners signed for their records on their portal* and name the first. Both hosts pass it to the office set: `DashboardPinnedQuickRow` and `QuickfillNeedsYouSection`.
- **The door.** [`stagesDeepLinks.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/stagesDeepLinks.ts): `?ownerrecords=<job id>` (`ownerRecordsHref`), consumed once like the other doors (`useStagesDeepLinkParams`). `JobsStagesTab` opens the Lien desk and the window together; [`LienOwnerRecordsModal`](../../src/components/jobs/LienOwnerRecordsModal.tsx)'s new `initialJobId` lands on the property row that holds the job (`ownerRecordsRowForJob`; the rows now carry `jobIds`), once per opening. A job no row holds leaves the picker.
