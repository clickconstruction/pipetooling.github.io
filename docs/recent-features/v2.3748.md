# v2.3748 — A job made from an estimate is named for the customer (2026-09-23)

Taunya (2026-09-22): *"When I bring over an estimate, it says estimate."* The estimate's title does two jobs: it is the heading on the paper the customer signs (so its default reads "Estimate for <customer>", and should) and it was the seed for the job's name when the estimate became a job. Three Kimberly Coe jobs on the Stages tab were all called "Estimate for Kimberly Coe". One kernel, one migration.

## The rule

A title that still has the shape the app wrote — empty, a placeholder ("New estimate", "Estimate for customer"), or "Estimate for … / Change Order for …" in any case — is not a job name. When the linked customer has a name, the job is named for the customer. A title someone typed for the work is kept. The Job name box in the Create job window stays editable either way.

## The change

- **[`estimateTitle.ts`](../../src/lib/estimates/estimateTitle.ts)** (new) — `defaultEstimateTitle` and `isGenericEstimateTitle` moved here out of `Estimates.tsx` (unchanged), plus `isAppDefaultEstimateTitle`, the rule above. Tested.
- **[`jobFromEstimateDefaults.ts`](../../src/lib/jobFromEstimateDefaults.ts)** — `defaultJobFieldsFromEstimate(row, { customerName })` seeds the customer's name when the title is the app default and a name is known; otherwise the title, as before. Tested.
- **[`CreateJobFromEstimateModal.tsx`](../../src/components/estimates/CreateJobFromEstimateModal.tsx)** — seeds through the kernel with `linkedCustomerPrefill.name` (the list passes the customer with the row; the detail page passes it from its loaded customers). When neither has the name yet and the title is the default, the modal reads the customer's name once and swaps it in; a failed read leaves the title as the seed. "Fill from customer" is unchanged.
- **[`20260923120000_job_name_from_estimate_customer.sql`](../migrations/20260923120000_job_name_from_estimate_customer.md)** — `auto_create_job_from_signed_estimate` (Settings → Signed agreements → Create jobs automatically) applies the same rule in SQL; the hand-typed-twin guard folds the name the job would get instead of the raw title. Push after merge.
- Guide *send an estimate and turn it into a job*, step 5, says what the job is named.

## Not changed

- The estimate title's default and the customer document's heading — that is the paper the customer signs.
- The open question from the same report: what the office would rather name a second job at the same house (customer plus the work?). Today's rule gives every job for a customer the customer's name; a hand edit in the Job name box separates them, as before.
