# v2.7292 — FormValues and the GC entity's name in files of their own: two small slivers off dev-mcp (punch list #359, PR 5) (2026-11-14)

Infra only: no screen and no running code changes. The fifth cut on the #359 card, two slivers in `_shared` that each fell under the card's 50 KB bar on dev-mcp alone but are shared by several functions.

## Why

- `_shared/hoursStatementForm.ts` imported `type FormValues` (`Record<string, string | boolean>`) from `_shared/formSchema.ts`, the contract form schema kernel, 22 KB. dev-mcp reads the form for the hours statement verbs and never fills a schema; accept-contract, hours-statement-signing and the office's form completion do and keep the schema.
- `_shared/messagesEmail.ts` imported `GC_CUSTOMER_EMAIL_FROM_NAME` from `_shared/gcCustomerEmails.ts`, the GC customer email kernel, 26 KB, for the name a mail is signed with; `portalLetterhead.ts` and `lienWaiverEmailCard.ts` did the same. dev-mcp reads `messagesEmail` for the letterhead of the Billed mail and sends no GC customer email.

## What changed

- **`_shared/formValues.ts`**, new: `FormValues`, moved with its comment. `formSchema.ts` imports it for its own signatures and re-exports it; `hoursStatementForm.ts` imports from the new file.
- **`_shared/portalCompany.ts`** holds `GC_CUSTOMER_EMAIL_FROM_NAME` beside `PORTAL_COMPANY`, the two company names in one import-free file. `gcCustomerEmails.ts` re-exports it (its one import now; the header said dependency-free and says so no more), so the app's `SignatureCard`, `customerSampleEmails.ts` and the tests import as before; `messagesEmail.ts`, `portalLetterhead.ts` and `lienWaiverEmailCard.ts` take both names from `portalCompany.ts` in one line.
- **`edgeUploadGraph.test.ts`** holds it: dev-mcp uploads neither `formSchema.ts` nor `gcCustomerEmails.ts`, and reaches `formValues.ts` through the hours statement's form and `portalCompany.ts`; hours-statement-signing keeps the schema and gc-customer-email keeps its kernel.

No kernel copy change: both files live in `_shared`.

## Is this the best we can do?

- `hoursStatementForm.ts` could write `Record<string, string | boolean>` itself and import nothing; the one name in one file keeps the signing function and the statement's form on the same shape.
- The constant could have stayed where it was with `messagesEmail.ts` writing the name; two names of the company in two files is what the owner's call 12 was meant to end, so it sits with the other.
- What is left in dev-mcp is its own files (the catalog 377 KB, the descriptions in `index.ts` 463 KB, the verb modules), the slim database types (315 KB, through eight type imports) and kernels it runs. The slim types are the next piece on the card, on the owner's yes to the row-files design; dev-mcp at 3.70 MB is above the 3.5 MB where step 3 (the descriptions and the catalog) would start, so without the slim-types work step 3 is the next cut.
