# v2.3397 — Pipeline on a map, PR 2: the rail, and the row-hover pulse (2026-09-14)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Second PR of the train in [`to-dos/pipeline-on-a-map/`](../../to-dos/pipeline-on-a-map/README.md), stacked on v2.3396 (the card).

## Why

The Bid Board map is productive partly because its rail says what the map knows but doesn't show. On the Pipeline the numbers that matter are money numbers: what is still to collect, and which bill has waited longest. The second-pass brief changed the list's order from the bid map's *nearest first* to *longest waiting first, then nearest* — distance decides where you drive; on the Pipeline the question is which invoice is oldest.

## What shipped

- **Kernel [`jobsMapRail.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobsMapRail.ts)** (+6 tests): the bid rail's distance buckets (≤ 25 · 25–50 · 50 mi +, re-exported from `bidBoardMapRail`) over the job pins with straight-line miles from the office; each bucket carries the count, **dollars to collect** (the sum of each pin's `owedDollars` — open bills minus payments applied, the Pipeline's own number) and **N to ask** (billed + ready-to-bill pins); `jobsMapPinsInBuckets` as the second pin filter; the ask list — billed and ready-to-bill pins sorted by `billedAgeDays` descending, then miles, capped at five with a *+ N more* remainder — with `jobsMapAskLabel` (*Billed 46 d* red in Collections, amber at 30+ days via `JOBS_MAP_LATE_DAYS`, *Ready to bill* with no age); the pinned total and the total to collect.
- **`jobsMap.ts`**: `billedAgeDays` on each job — the oldest open billed invoice's age by the Billed section's own reference rule (the hand-set estimated bill date, else `billed_at` in the company calendar), so the rail's *46 d* is the same number the Billed section prints. `jobsMapJobs` takes `now` for the tests.
- **[`JobsMapRail`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsMapRail.tsx)**: the three bucket buttons (aria-pressed, the *to collect* line, *N to ask* on desktop), the ask list (section dot with the Collections ring, number + name, the age in its tone, miles), and the footer line that moved out of the card — *112 pinned · $696k to collect · Paid 723 off* plus the unplaced-jobs door.
- **The card**: desktop grid, map left and rail right (the bid card's 3:2); phones stack the rail under the pin bar. The bucket filter composes with the section chips.
- **Row hover → pin pulse, by delegation**: every Pipeline row already carries `data-stages-job-id` (the # jump's scroll target), so the card listens once for `mouseover` on the document while it is visible on a desktop, reads the id off the nearest row, and hands it to the canvases' existing `pulseId`. No row component changed; the table, the unified table and the card list all pulse. The bid map's per-row store was the alternative; three row components made delegation the smaller change.
- Guide *see the pipeline on a map*: "The rail beside the map" and "From a row to its pin".
