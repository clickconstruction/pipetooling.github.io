# v2.6172 — Legal desk: the parties and the venue under the account's name (2026-10-30)

Punch list #219, PR 1 of three. The owner, 2026-10-30: "In the legal desk, I would like to improve the header, I think we should show the person we have a contract with, the person who owns the building, and the venue where a person can click on it and it brings up a map." The mock-up (`to-dos/legal-desk-header-parties/mockup.html`) weighed three shapes of the header and two maps; he picked the parties strip with the desk's own Map.

Before, the account's head read the payer's name, a line of *job numbers · the payer's address* (on a subcontract account the GC's office, hundreds of miles from the building), a twenty-word venue sentence that mostly explained the firm, and the balance. The owner of the building was the *Owner of record* column of the Property record table on the Account tab, under the four questions, the money bars and the fix list.

## What changed

- **The parties strip** (`src/components/jobs/legal/LegalPartiesStrip.tsx` over the pure kernel `src/lib/legal/legalParties.ts`): three cells under the name, in the owner's order. **Contract with** is the payer (`partiesStrip` reads `packet.account.payer`), its role (*general contractor* · *homeowner* · *commercial customer* · *customer*, from `customer_type`), the agreement's state from the Paper tab's coverage (*subcontract signed by R. Vance*, *1 of 2 signed*, *agreement sent, not signed*, *no agreement of ours needed*, *no signed agreement*; the signers from `signerNames`, joined by `joinSignerNames`) and the payer's city (`cityOfAddress`). **Owns the building** is the owner of record as the record spells it (`LegalPropertyLine.owner`, the job's override else the property record), where it mails (`mailsToWords`: *mails to Denver, CO*) and the property's kind (*homestead* · *residential* · *commercial*). **Venue** is the county where the work was done with its justice precinct when the record has one (`precinctWords` over the property line's `precinct` / `precinctNote`, else *justice precinct not yet*), then *where the work was done and where they live* when the payer's county is the same, or *· or Lubbock County, where Northline Builders is* when it differs, and the justice-court chip (`justiceCourtCap`). The counties are the ones `firmForAccount` grouped for the firm segment, so the strip and the firm never disagree; two counties read with their balances, largest first.
- **Doors**: Contract with opens Edit customer; Owns the building opens the same window, where the property record and its owner of record are typed; Venue switches the desk to **Map** (PR 2 opens it on the county with the case held). Every cell is a button with the fact as its accessible name.
- **The states**: a homeowner who is the owner of record is one cell, *Contract with · owns the building*. An owner not on the record, no property record, a county unknown and a name-only payer are amber cells that say what is missing in one sentence and open the record that fills it (`OWNER_NOT_ON_RECORD_WORDS`, `NO_PROPERTY_RECORD_WORDS`, `COUNTY_UNKNOWN_WORDS`, `NAME_ONLY_WORDS`). Two buildings count (*2 buildings · 2 owners*, the owners on the quiet line). A released or given-up matter keeps the strip; the old venue sentence disappeared for both.
- **The line under the name** reads the job numbers and the job's address (`addressLine`; a second address reads *· 1 more*), never the payer's office. The venue sentence (`venueLineWords`, v2.5792) is retired from the head; it remains the firm segment's hover title, so *no counties are colored yet, so the firm takes every account* is still one hover away.
- **Phone**: the cells stack as rows in one box under the name, the balance under them as before.
- **Words**: the guide *review a collections account before it goes to your attorney* (a new section, *The parties and the venue*); `docs/GLOSSARY.md` → Legal matter; the #219 card's status.

## Is this the best we can do?

1. **The building under the name, not the office.** Every other number in the head is about the job; the one address was not.
2. **The record's spelling.** The appraisal district writes the owner in capitals; the lien affidavit names the owner as the record does, so the desk keeps one spelling.
3. **The firm words are not lost.** The sentence the strip replaced was half about the firm; those words stay on the firm segment, which is where the firm is.
4. **Cut**: a fourth cell for Click, a tenant or brand cell (on no record), a cased owner name, a second map.
