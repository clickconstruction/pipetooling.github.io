# v2.5284 — Messages: Follow up reads who is waiting and what we owe from the tables, behind the scenes (2026-10-12)

Messages wave 2, cut 2's screens, PR A of four (data; B the Follow up tab, C the pieces in the thread window, D
Settings → Phone and texts). No screen yet. It reads cut 2's tables (`20261012160000_messages_follow_up.sql`, #5447) and
the spine's, so it goes live once both are pushed; it builds on the spine's loaders (`contacts/load.ts`,
`messages/load.ts`, #5433) and the kernels (v2.5251).

- **`src/lib/followUp/followUpRows.ts`**: cut 2's rows written out (`FollowUpContactRow`, `FollowUpMessageRow`,
  `ContactOwedRow`, `MessagePromiseRow`, `MessageHighlightRow`) until the lead's one types PR swaps them for
  `Tables<'…'>`, and the mappers to the kernels' inputs: the people with their state and who has it by name (a state
  this build does not know reads as done), the lines with a note's day in words and Done with it, the days heard (a
  kind, a state or a line it cannot read is left out), what we owe and each line's highlights. `followUpInputFromRows`
  feeds `followUpQueue`.
- **`src/lib/followUp/followUpIo.ts`**: `FollowUpIo`, one interface a smoke fakes, and `supabaseFollowUpIo` on the app's
  client, read untyped until the regen. It loads what the queue needs and no more:
  - the people with their records embedded in one query;
  - their lines in while open or snoozed, through an inner join on the contact's state rather than a list of ids;
  - every note with a day, the days heard still offered or written ahead, and what we owe still open.
  The presses call the migration's functions: `take_it`, `hand_to`, `set_state`, `mark_owed`, `owed_done`,
  `write_down`, `read_question`, `add_note`, `set_back_day`, `back_done`, `highlight`, `unhighlight`, and
  `follow_up_count` for the badge.
- **`pressProblem`**: a press refuses in plain words (`P0001`, or `42501` when the words are the press's own: a
  training account, a twin, someone off the office team), and the screen shows them as they are. The house's
  `formatErrorMessage` would turn a `42501` into *You don't have permission to …*. Anything else, offline or a policy,
  still reads as the house says it.
- **`src/hooks/useFollowUpQueue.ts`**: loads while the tab is open, builds the queue with today in the app's zone
  (the clause rows and the nudge builder passed through), and reads again after every press; a refused press leaves
  its words in `problem` and resolves false.
- **Tests**: `followUpRows.test.ts` (the prototype's Friday from the rows the tables hold, through the kernel),
  `followUpIo.test.ts` (each query's filter, each press's arguments, the badge, a refusal's words) and
  `useFollowUpQueue.render.test.tsx` (load, closed tab, a press that reads again, a refused one).
