# v2.7806 — Lien desk: Put a GC on notice leaves the title bar for the job's own notice (2026-11-18)

The owner on 2026-11-18, after seeing *Put a GC on notice…* drawn on the title line: *"We're gonna leave put a G C on notice on the jobs associated with the G C, if possible. How many avenues do we currently have to put a G C on notice? I don't think I wanna see it in the header"*. There were three ways to start a run and none was on a job: the desk's title bar, the Pipeline's tools menu filtered to one GC, and the GC's row in Customer review. Three places inside the desk were drawn on the live desk: beside By job's GC filter, beside the GC's name on a job's own notice, and at the top of the Monthly notices list. His pick: *"Build 2"*.

## Before

- On a computer the title bar drew *⚠ Put a GC on notice…* on Monthly notices, at the right of the second line. It opened a picker of the GCs with notices due.
- With the paper tabs up on the title line (v2.7789) that door was the only thing on Monthly notices' second line.

## What changed

- **The press is on the job.** A job with a GC draws *⚠ Put on notice…* in its heading on Monthly notices, after the GC's name and before the address. `lienGcNoticePress` in `lienDeskGcPicker.ts` words it: the accessible name carries the GC (*Put <GC> on notice…*), the hint says what the run does. One press opens the run for that job's GC, with no picker.
- **Who sees it.** The office, on a computer, on a job with a GC, on a desk that was handed the run window. The same readers saw the title bar's door.
- **The title bar has no GC door on a computer.** `lienDeskTitleBarLayout` takes no `gcNotice`. The second line's right span is the tool row alone, drawn only where the title line has no room for it. Monthly notices with the papers up is one line.
- **A phone is untouched.** *Put a GC on notice…* is still a row of More and opens the picker as a sheet. `LienDeskGcPicker` is that sheet alone now: it has no door of its own.
- **The other doors are untouched**: the Pipeline's tools menu with the board filtered to one GC, the GC's row in Customer review, and `?gcnotice=<customer id>`.

## What goes

- On a computer, the picker's overview: one list of every GC with notices due, with its dollars, jobs and first window to close. Customer review still lists each GC on its own row.
