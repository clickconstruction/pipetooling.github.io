# v2.4368 — Approval PDF: a Combined bid counts its materials (2026-10-01)

Found during the By Stage review (2026-10-01): By Stage (`bids.materials_model = 'exact'`) prices materials only through the three draft stage POs on `cost_estimates.purchase_order_id_*`; Combined (`'rough'`, the default for every bid since May 2026) prices them from `bids_takeoff_rough_part_lines`. `downloadApprovalPdf` had no model branch: it summed the three stage POs on page 1 (margins), page 2 (the pricing rows) and page 3 (the Labor page's Materials table) and passed `materialsFromTakeoffByCountRowId: {}`, so every Combined bid's PDF counted $0 of materials and an overstated margin. Pricing and Labor already branched (`useBidPricingEngine` `loadCostEstimate` and the Pricing load). The help guide *choose By Stage or Combined for a bid's materials* already said Combined shows one materials line in the approval PDF; now it does.

## What changed
- **One kernel for a Combined bid's materials.** [`lib/bids/bidMaterials.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/bidMaterials.ts): `combinedMaterials(lines, countByRowId)` is the total with the order rounding (`roughMaterialsTotalWithRounding`) and each count row's slice (Σ its lines' quantity × price, × its count, + its share of the rounding's extra), in the Rough In slot as the engine sets it; `stagePoMaterials` keeps the three POs; `materialsLines` is what a document prints (one *Materials* line for Combined, the three PO lines and *Materials Total* for By Stage). The engine's two hand-written per-fixture loops now call `combinedMaterials` (same numbers; a line whose count row is outside the version still sums at ×1 in the total and gets no row of its own, as before).
- **One door for a document that reads its own data.** [`lib/bids/bidMaterialsIo.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/bidMaterialsIo.ts) `loadBidMaterials(supabase, { bidId, bidVersionId, countRows, costEstimate, fallbackModel })` reads the model fresh (the bid row the caller holds answers if that read fails), then either the version's rough lines (`bid_version_id` = the count rows' version, `IS NULL` for an unsplit bid) or the stage PO totals (`loadPoTotal`, moved out of the PDF). It never reads the store the model parks.
- **The Approval PDF reads it once for its three pages.** Page 1's cost estimate and margins, page 2's pricing rows (`totalMaterials` + the per-fixture materials) and page 3's breakdown all use the one result. Page 3 prints `materialsLines`: a Combined bid gets one *Materials* row instead of three $0 PO rows. A By Stage bid prints what it printed before. Revenue and the cover letter never read materials and are unchanged.

## Tests
- `bidMaterials.test.ts`: the total and per-fixture split with a shared 20 ft stick rule (the slices add up to the total), count fallbacks (null / 0 → ×1, typed counts), an out-of-version line, an empty takeoff, the PO pass-through, and both `materialsLines` shapes.
- `bidMaterialsIo.test.ts`: Combined reads the version's lines and never the POs it still links; the unsplit base reads `bid_version_id IS NULL`; By Stage reads its POs and never the parked lines; an unknown model reads as By Stage; a failed model read falls back to the caller's model; no estimate = $0 with no PO read; `loadPoTotal` on a missing PO or a failed read.
- `approvalPdf.test.ts`: a Combined scenario (the estimate still linking a stage PO): page 1 costs $1,115 with a 70.7% margin, one line read scoped to the version and no PO read, page 3 prints one *Materials* line and no PO rows, revenue and letter unchanged. The By Stage scenario is untouched and still passes. The new cases fail on the old builder.
