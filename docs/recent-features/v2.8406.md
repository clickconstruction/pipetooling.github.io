# v2.8406 — Supply house bills: the database can search every bill at once (2026-11-23)

Punch list #431, the database half of PR 5. One migration, `20261123193100_supply_bill_search.sql`, adds the function `search_supply_bills`. No screen changes, and no edge function. It ships ahead of the search box so the migration is pushed before any screen calls the function.

## What changed

- **`search_supply_bills(p_words, p_year, p_month, p_day, p_limit)`** finds supply house bills. Each word must be met by a bill's own facts (its house, its number with or without its marks, its PO, its amount) or by a line read from its paper (the description, the house's code, the maker's number). A year, a month and a day narrow by the invoice date. A hit says which line found it when the bill's own facts did not.
- **It runs as its caller.** The tables' own policies decide what comes back, so the office finds bills and nobody else finds any.
- **No index.** The bills and their lines are a few thousand rows.

The whole of it is in `docs/migrations/20261123193100_supply_bill_search.md`.
