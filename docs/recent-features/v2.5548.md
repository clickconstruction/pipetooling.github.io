# v2.5548 — Company phone seats: a seat shared by the office (2026-10-14)

The owner's decision of 2026-10-14, to keep costs low: one Quo seat for the whole office, its login signed in on every phone that should ring, rather than a paid seat per person. Company phone seats could only map a Quo user id to one person, so every call that seat took would have read as that person. No migration (the row `messages_quo_seats_v1` keeps its shape, with the word `office` in place of a user id), no new edge function, but `quo-webhook` and `quo-send` carry the regenerated kernels and are deployed after merge.

- **`src/lib/quo/rows.ts`** (copied to the edge): `SEAT_SHARED` (`'office'`), `SHARED_SEAT_NAME` (*the office*), `isSharedSeat`; `quoSeats` maps the shared seat to `{ userId: null, name: 'the office' }`; `quoSeatUserIds` reads no user for it; `seatOf` gives a sender their own seat, else the shared seat, else null, so a text from someone with no seat goes out as the office's login rather than as whoever owns the number in Quo. `QuoPlanContext.users` (`events.ts`) takes `userId: string | null`; `callWho`, `transcriptWords` and `quoSentWho` then read *the office answered*, *the office: …* and *the office · from the company number* with no change, and `mergeCall` writes `whoUserId` null.
- **`src/lib/quo/seatsCard.ts`**: re-exports the sentinel, `SEAT_SHARED_LABEL` (*Shared by the office*); `quoSeatsProblem` lets the shared seat sit on more than one Quo login while a person still has one; `QUO_SEATS_WHY` says the three rules in plain words.
- **`QuoSeatsSettingsSection.tsx`**: the *who answers on it* picker offers **Shared by the office** after *Pick someone*; the office's read names it; the *Someone no longer on the team* fallback skips it.
- **Tests**: `rows.test.ts` (+1), `events.test.ts` (+1: a call the shared seat took, its transcript and a text from Quo's app on it), `seatsCard.test.ts` (+1), `QuoSeatsSettingsSection.render.test.tsx` (+1: the option, the save, the office's read).
- **Guide** *match each company phone seat to a person* gains **One seat for the whole office**. **Docs**: the Glossary's Company phone seats sentence, `SETTINGS_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md` 7e, the switches table in `MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `quo-send` in `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`, and step 2 of `MESSAGES_SWITCH_ON_HANDOFF_2026-10-14.md`.

What the thread cannot say with a shared seat: who picked up. The call, its recording and its transcript are there; the person is not. Texts typed inside Quo's own app read as the office too, so the house rule is text from the app, call from Quo.
