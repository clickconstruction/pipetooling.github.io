## The record

`development_history_entries`: the site, at most one of a job, a bid or the customer, `happened_at` (the writer's time), `source` (email, call, text, on_site, letter, note), `body` whole up to 20,000 characters, the author, `via` (`app` or `mcp`) and `agent_label` for a robot, `link_url` and `file_path` reserved for the email itself. The office team reads and inserts; **no UPDATE or DELETE policy**: a kept line never changes, a correction is a second line. The three fences as every new table.

## The page

- **The composer** (`DevelopmentHistoryComposer`) at the top of History: *What happened*, About (the site, each job, each bid, the customer), When (a `datetime-local` box, now by default, set back to the email's time), the Source chips, **Keep it** (dead until the words are there and the time has come; Cmd-Enter keeps too), the flash *Kept · it reads at the top*, and the line *Kept lines never change. To correct one, keep a second line that says so.* With nothing new the card stays one line and its header gains *Write ›*.
- **The write** (`historyWrites.ts`): one insert as the keeper with `via = 'app'`; the kept line is prepended to the stream at once; a failure names its reason (`formatErrorMessage`).
- **The read**: the page's fetch reads the site's entries newest first (500 at most) with the author's name, and `history.ts` draws each as a line (`entryLine`): the source's glyph on its own tint, the writer leading, *via MCP* with the robot's name in its title for a robot's line, the time at the line's end; *What happened* narrows to them. A line past 260 characters folds behind *more ›*, which opens the whole of it in `DevelopmentLineWindow` (shipped in PR 2, now with lines to open).
- `history.ts` gains the row shapes (`HistoryRow`, `draftToRow`, `rowToEntry`); the device-only store the spike used is gone.
