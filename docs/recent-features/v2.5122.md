# v2.5122 — ZZ test jobs leave search, the Dashboard's job cards and Customers (2026-10-09)

Punch list #61, PR 2b of 3 (`to-dos/zz-test-jobs-hide.md`). The client only: no migration, no edge function. The other office doors, each built on 2a's rule (`hidesZzTestJobs` with the dev's switch) and its shared ZZ id read (`zzTestJobRows.ts`, one answer per user per minute). Every role but a dev who shows them stops seeing ZZ test jobs here.

## What shipped

- **Header search** (`HeaderGlobalSearch.tsx`). Job hits from `search_jobs_ledger` drop by their own name and by the shared ids, since the RPC returns no customer name. That covers the office search and the field lookup. Customer hits drop by the customer's name.
  - Search is a lookup, not a total. A failed id read falls back to names alone rather than wiping every result, so a ZZ customer's job with a real name can show then. The code says so.
- **The Dashboard's job cards** (`useDashboardAssignedJobs`): the assigned, team ready-to-bill and superintendent lists, and the Jobs map card that reuses them.
  - Like Ready to bill (the #5241 review), the lists keep every row they read. They hand out nothing and read as loading until the shared ids land, then drop a ZZ job by its name or by the ids, because two of the three RPCs return no customer name.
  - After a failed read they stay held, and the Dashboard says so above them: *Could not read the test-job list, so your job lists are held.* with **Try again** (the hook hands `zz.retry` through; review on #5246). They never read as "nothing assigned".
  - The first render after the hook turns on (status `off`) reads as loading, not empty. The same fix applies to 2a's Ready to bill and Billed lists.
- **The Needs you nudges**, each with a new `hideZzTestJobs` argument and the user:
  - **Idle open jobs** (`useStaleOpenJobsNudge`): the count and the contract dollars. Its read gains `customer_name`.
  - **Returned deposits** (`useBankReturnedPaymentsNudge`): the payments on a ZZ job, also in the Pipeline's Billed badges. A returned deposit warns about real money, so a failed id read falls back to the job's names rather than emptying the card and the badges (review on #5246). The open return cases carry no job and are left as they are.
  - **Test reports ready** (`useTestReportsReadyNudge`): drafts on a ZZ job. The ids leave in the query itself (`not job_id in (…)`), so test drafts cannot fill the 200-row read and push real ones out (review on #5246); the embedded job name catches any the ids missed.
  - For idle jobs and test reports, a failed id read empties the card like any other failed read.
  - `DashboardPinnedQuickRow` and the Pipeline pass `useZzTestJobsHidden(role)`.
  - **Lien releases owed** is a lien nudge, so it moves to PR 3 with the Lien desk, for the quiet lien lane.
- **Customers.**
  - On the list (`Customers.tsx`), a ZZ customer's row leaves, so the phone view's list follows. Every ZZ job leaves the counts and money by the shared ids (`withoutZzTestJobsInBundle`; the bundle's rows carry only ids), and a hidden ZZ customer's details are never read. A dev's flip reads the money again; a load guard keeps only the newest load's answer, so the role landing or a flip cannot race (review on #5246).
  - On a customer's page (`CustomerDetail.tsx`), the page knows its customer, so no shared read is needed (`customerPageZzTestJobs.ts`). A job is a test job when its own name, its stored customer name (the rule the list's ids use; `fetchCustomerProfile` now reads it) or the page customer's name is a ZZ name. Those jobs leave the page and its money, and their bills and payments leave the Invoices tab.
  - A ZZ test customer's page reached by a link is not drawn at all for those roles: a short notice and the way back, rather than its feed and bids without its jobs (review on #5246).
  - The phone view's job search drops by name only (residue for a later pass).
- **The shared ids hook** `useZzTestJobIds` (2a) now serves Ready to bill, Billed and the job cards.
- **Docs.** GLOSSARY (ZZ convention) and the card are amended in place.
