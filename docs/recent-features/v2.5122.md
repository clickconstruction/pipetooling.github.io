# v2.5122 — ZZ test jobs leave search, the Dashboard's job cards and Customers (2026-10-09)

Punch list #61, PR 2b of 3 (`to-dos/zz-test-jobs-hide.md`). The client only: no migration, no edge function. The other office doors, each built on 2a's rule (`hidesZzTestJobs` with the dev's switch) and its shared ZZ id read (`zzTestJobRows.ts`, one answer per user per minute). Every role but a dev who shows them stops seeing ZZ test jobs here.

## What shipped

- **Header search** (`HeaderGlobalSearch.tsx`). Job hits from `search_jobs_ledger` drop by their own name and by the shared ids, since the RPC returns no customer name. That covers the office search and the field lookup. Customer hits drop by the customer's name.
  - Search is a lookup, not a total. A failed id read falls back to names alone rather than wiping every result, so a ZZ customer's job with a real name can show then. The code says so.
- **The Dashboard's job cards** (`useDashboardAssignedJobs`): the assigned, team ready-to-bill and superintendent lists, and the Jobs map card that reuses them.
  - Like Ready to bill (the #5241 review), the lists keep every row they read. They hand out nothing and read as loading until the shared ids land, and stay held after a failed read. Then they drop a ZZ job by its name or by the ids, because two of the three RPCs return no customer name.
- **The Needs you nudges**, each with a new `hideZzTestJobs` argument and the user:
  - **Idle open jobs** (`useStaleOpenJobsNudge`): the count and the contract dollars. Its read gains `customer_name`.
  - **Returned deposits** (`useBankReturnedPaymentsNudge`): the payments on a ZZ job, also in the Pipeline's Billed badges. The open return cases carry no job and are left as they are.
  - **Test reports ready** (`useTestReportsReadyNudge`): drafts on a ZZ job.
  - A failed id read empties each card like any other failed read.
  - `DashboardPinnedQuickRow` and the Pipeline pass `useZzTestJobsHidden(role)`.
  - **Lien releases owed** is a lien nudge, so it moves to PR 3 with the Lien desk, for the quiet lien lane.
- **Customers.**
  - On the list (`Customers.tsx`), a ZZ customer's row leaves, so the phone view's list follows. Every ZZ job leaves the counts and money by the shared ids (`withoutZzTestJobsInBundle`; the bundle's rows carry only ids). A dev's flip reads the money again.
  - On a customer's page (`CustomerDetail.tsx`), the page knows its customer, so no shared read is needed (`customerPageZzTestJobs.ts`). A job is a test job when its own name or the customer's is a ZZ name. Those jobs leave the page and its money, and their bills and payments leave the Invoices tab.
  - The phone view's job search drops by name only (residue for a later pass).
- **The shared ids hook** `useZzTestJobIds` (2a) now serves Ready to bill, Billed and the job cards.
- **Docs.** GLOSSARY (ZZ convention) and the card are amended in place.
