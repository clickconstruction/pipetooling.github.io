# v2.5905 — Job Mode: the day’s press moves to the thumb (2026-10-26)

Punch list #189 (phone first), PR 2 of the train, from the mock-up the owner approved on 2026-10-26 (`to-dos/phone-first/mockup.html` § 1, README plan a.1). Walked at 375 px, the Job Mode card's two 92 px buttons sat 214 px from the top with no rail and dropped 33 px a job on the day's rail, so the thumb reached up for the day's press; Turnaway was a 56 px wide button under them and Clock out a 20 px text link. Only the tab bar was fixed.

## What changed

- **A press bar pinned above the tab bar, on a phone only** (`useNarrowViewport640`): `position: fixed`, its bottom at `var(--app-bottom-chrome)` so it sits directly on `DispatchModeFooter`, z-index `DISPATCH_MODE_FOOTER_Z_INDEX + 1` (701: over the footer, under every window — `check:window-z` holds them at 700 or above, the lowest at 710), hidden with the footer under the keyboard by the same rule (`useOnScreenKeyboardOpen`, a `translateY` past the footer), `var(--surface)` with a `var(--border)` top. Top row: three 44 px buttons, **Turnaway**, **% done**, **Clock out**; bottom row: **Leave report** (blue, 1fr) and the day's press (right, 1.6fr), both 56 px, 16 px bold. A hidden slot leaves the top row to the rest.
- **The day's press keeps its six states, words, colours and handlers**: Clock In (orange), On my way (green), I'm here (blue), Wrap Up Day (red), Last job of the day (grey, dead), Choose Next Job. Leave report keeps its handler and its grey dead state with no current job; Turnaway keeps its handler and dead state; **Clock out** shows under exactly the quiet link's rule (an open session, hourly, Wrap Up Day not the press) and runs `requestClockOut`; **% done** opens `FieldPctUpdateModal` on the clocked-in job under My Schedule's gate (subs, helpers, superintendents) and is hidden, not dead, outside it or with no job clocked in.
- **The card on a phone** loses the moved grid and the quiet Clock out; Write up a change, Hand the phone and Message the customer stay where they are as 44 px presses. Layout's `<main>` pads by `--job-mode-press-bar`, which the bar sets on the root to its measured height while mounted (a `ResizeObserver`; 125 px with one-line labels, more when a label wraps on a narrow phone or with the phone's text made larger, the bar never held to a fixed height) and clears on unmount, so the card's foot never scrolls under it. The desktop and tablet card is unchanged.
- **The dead Job Report button** leaves `DashboardPinnedQuickRow` in Job Mode on a phone (`hideJobReport`); the Tally square stays. `docs/DASHBOARD_SECTIONS_ARCHITECTURE.md`'s quirk sentence says so.
