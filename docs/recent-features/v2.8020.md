# v2.8020 — Contracts and clauses: a summary and a printed sample of a paper built from settings (2026-11-19)

Punch list #389, step 2 of eight: the summary, the level and the sample. No migration and no function. It touches only the Contract Book's preview.

## What changed

- **Summary**, beside **Paper** in *Preview with settings*. It says the level first, then a line a setting: the setting, the choice picked, and the plain words that choice says. The words are the settings list's own, in English or Spanish, with the English where the Spanish is empty.
- **The level** is a name and the kind of place it sits beside, *Standard, aligned with regular businesses*. Picks that match no named level read *A mix of its own*. There is no score: the owner's pick of 2026-11-17 was a level name and no number.
- Under every summary: *The summary is made from the same settings as the paper, so the two cannot disagree. It is not the contract.*
- **Print the sample** opens the sample as it prints, with **Print** and **Close**. It is the same assembly as the preview and as a signer's copy, put together for a made-up party: *Sample Sub LLC*, *Jordan Sample*, *Job J0000, a sample*. SAMPLE lies across the sheet, and across every printed page. The stamp at its foot says it is not for signature, with the level and the revision.
- A paper that cannot go has no sample. The press is dead and the line beside it says so, under the words that name what stops the paper.
