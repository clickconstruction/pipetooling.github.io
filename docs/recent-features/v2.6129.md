# v2.6129 — Fill them in keeps to the line you opened, and an unpriced draft can be discarded (2026-10-30)

A follow-up to punch list #178 (closed; its note is in `to-dos/README.md`), from the live walk of 2026-10-30 on clicktooling.com: signed in through View as as the sample assistant, a real session with the assistant's rights, every save of the move window and the work orders pressed on ZZ Test rows made and deleted the same hour. The saves held. Two things a first-time assistant would have hit did not.

## What changed

- **The deck keeps to the line you opened.** A line's Fill them in opened the deck on its kind, but the deck forgot the kind at the first Save or Skip (`openedOn` cleared, #146's deck, v2.5774) and jumped to row 1 of the whole deck, another kind. Opened on the move window's line, the assistant priced one charge and landed on the 86 contracts; the second charge was reachable only by skipping through all of them. The order is now `fillInKindFirst(fillInOrder(rows, skipped), startKind, skipped)` (`src/lib/needsYou/fillIn.ts`): the kind's unskipped rows first, past every Save and Skip, then the deck's own order once none is left, the skipped rows at the back. The chip that opens the deck on its first row is unchanged. `fillInStartIndex` is gone with the ref.
- **An unpriced draft can be discarded.** `step_commitments_amount_when_sent_check` is `amount IS NOT NULL OR status = 'draft'`, so the Work board's Discard the draft (`status: 'cancelled'`) answered 400 on exactly the drafts v2.6094 puts on Needs you, and a draft nobody needed kept its line for good. `cancelCommitmentPatch` (`src/lib/workflow/stepCommitments.ts`) cancels an unpriced draft at $0; the board's discard, the sub sheet's work-order panel and the step panel's Cancel all use it. Checked live on the walk's own ZZ draft: the plain cancel answered 23514, the cancel at $0 answered 200.
- The Needs you guide says a line's Fill them in keeps to its rows; the work-order guide says how a draft nobody needs leaves the line.

## Is this the best we can do?

The $0 is a stand-in for "no price, cancelled". A migration that lets a cancelled row hold no amount would say it exactly, but it would need a push before the client could rely on it, and nothing reads a cancelled commitment's amount. Seen on the same walk and left alone: the Needs you office set counts the controller while the database's `is_office_staff()` does not, so a controller would see the move-window and job-account kinds and be refused partway through Moved (no controller today; the choice is the owner's); Unpriced fixtures' read answers 400 on every load (its own PR, `claude/unpriced-fixtures-order-id`).
