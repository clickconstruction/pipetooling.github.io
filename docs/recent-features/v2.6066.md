# v2.6066 — My Schedule's Add job says the job joins your crew (2026-10-29)

Punch list #196, the pick the owner took on 2026-10-29: option A, drop the box. Fix, no migration, no edge function.

## Cause

The add-a-job window on My Schedule (`DashboardAddJobToMyScheduleModal.tsx`) asked *Add me to the job crew (keeps the job visible to you elsewhere)*, ticked by default, and passed the answer to `self_schedule_add_block` as `p_join_crew`, which inserts the crew row only when true. But `job_schedule_blocks_ensure_job_team_member_tr`, AFTER INSERT on `job_schedule_blocks`, runs `ensure_job_team_member_from_schedule_block()` and adds every new block's assignee to the job's crew, `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`, for every path that adds a block. The live press run of punch list #189 on 2026-10-27 unticked the box and found the crew row anyway. The box decided nothing.

## Fix

- **The window says what the database does.** The box and its `joinCrew` state are gone; the slot step reads *This job joins your crew, so it shows everywhere you look.* above **← Back** and **Add to my schedule**.
- **The client sends true.** `selfScheduleAddBlock` (`src/lib/selfScheduleJobs.ts`) no longer takes `joinCrew` and sends `p_join_crew: true`, so the RPC's own insert and the trigger agree. The RPC keeps its parameter, unused by any window; dropping it is a later migration if ever.
- **Guide** *add a job to my schedule and move my day around* → *Add a job*, step 3: *Tap Add to my schedule. The job joins your crew, so it shows everywhere you look in the app.*

## Tests

`DashboardAddJobToMyScheduleModal.render.test.tsx` (new, 1): search, pick the job, read the sentence, find no checkbox, press **Add to my schedule**, and the RPC call carries the picked job, the day, the 08:00–12:00 window and `p_join_crew: true`.

## Verified

Dev server, View as… a helper from Settings, Dashboard → My Schedule → **+ Add job** at 375: the slot step shows the sentence and no box. Nothing was saved on prod.
