# v2.8278 — Escape closes Write down their word and New task (2026-11-21)

Two of the faults punch list #334 wrote down under *Found while building, not built* and never built. Client only: no migration and no function.

## What was seen

On the Bridge, *What to do now* opens two of the app's own windows. **Set a pay date** opens *Write down their word* (`SetPromisedPayDateModal.tsx`). A grey check opens **New task** (`ChecklistAddModal.tsx`). The Bridge's own windows close with Escape. Neither of these did.

Both were confirmed on `main` before the fix, in the code and on the page at `localhost` on live data, read only:

- *Write down their word* had no key listener at all. Escape did nothing, once or twice. **Cancel** closed it.
- **New task** had none either, wherever it was opened from. The card guessed that the *Done when…* box held the focus when a grey check opened the window. It does not: the focus is in the title box, as on every other open. No press of Escape closed the window. The **×** did. The fragment for v2.2742 says it in so many words: *There was never an Escape handler; still none.*

## What changed

Each window now registers on the modal stack (`useModalStackEntry`) and listens for Escape the way the Lien desk's windows do:

- **Only the top window answers.** A sheet opened over New task, such as the place sheet or the map, is on the stack above it and takes Escape first.
- **A key that was already taken is left alone.** The list under *Tell someone else* shuts itself on Escape and marks the key as handled. New task then stays open, and the next Escape closes it.
- **The key stops at the window.** A window underneath that listens for Escape too, such as a job's window, never hears it. Until now, as the code reads, Escape with New task open over a job's window went to the job's window and left New task standing.
- **A save is not cut short.** *Write down their word* waits while a save is on its way, as its **Cancel** does. New task waits while a task is being sent.
- **Never said that keeps its own Escape.** The question it asks listens first and stops the key, so Escape answers the question and the sheet stays.

Escape closes New task as the **×** does: the draft is gone. A press on the backdrop still does nothing (v2.2742), since a stray tap is not a decision and a key press is.

The focus rules are as they were. Nothing moves the focus on open or on close.

## Where each window is used

Both are the app's own windows, so the fix reaches every place that opens them, not the Bridge alone. *Write down their word* opens from the Bridge, the Dashboard's Money in strip, the Pipeline's billed rows, the bank payments window, a bid's money line, a thread in Messages and the Legal desk. New task opens from the header, the Checklist, a job, a bid, a thread, Materials on site, Quickfill and the `/task` shortcut.

## Tests

- `SetPromisedPayDateModal.render.test.tsx`: Escape calls `onClose` once, writes nothing, and a listener on `window` under the sheet never hears it. Escape on *Never said that* answers that question and leaves the sheet open.
- `ChecklistAddModal.render.test.tsx`: Escape closes the window and the key goes no further, while any other key passes through. With the *Tell someone else* list open, the first Escape shuts the list and the second closes the window.

All three new closing tests fail on `main` without the fix.
