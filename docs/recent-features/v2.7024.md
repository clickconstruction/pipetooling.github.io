# v2.7024 — Pay calendar: record the day a payer must have a bill by, and the day they pay (2026-11-12)

Punch list #323, PR 1 of seven. Some commercial payers pay on a calendar, not at a pace: a pay application in by the cutoff (the last day of the month, or an earlier day) is paid on a set day of a later month. The app guesses every pay date as the bill date plus the payer's median days to pay, which can say neither the cutoff nor the day a bill is due. This PR gives the setting a home, a window and a record. It changes no date on any screen yet; PR 2 puts the due day on billed rows.

The design was tested on the payers it was written for before anything was built, and that changed it: two of the three had paid weeks to months after such a day. So the contract's day is a **due** day, never the expected one, and the payer's own record is replayed against it from this first PR.

## What changed

- **The kernel**, `src/lib/jobs/payCalendar.ts`, pure. A calendar is three numbers and a note: `cutoffDay` (1 to 28, null for the last day of the month), `payDay` (1 to 31, a day the month does not have is its last), `monthsAfter` (0 to 2), `payWhenPaid`, and the clause with a few of its words.
  - `dueDayFor(receivedYmd, cal)` is the contract: the day a bill in on that day falls due. `nextCutoff(todayYmd, cal)` is what someone about to bill needs: the last day to send, the due day that makes, and the due day one day late.
  - `latenessAgainst(receipts, cal)` is the record: each measured payment's due day worked out from the day its bill went in, and the days its money landed after it. `expectedFromDue` is the due day plus the middle lateness, never before the due day.
  - `calendarFor(index, { payerId, jobId })` reads a job's own row for its payer, else the payer's, else none. A job's row can switch the payer's calendar off. No reader calls it yet.
  - The words: `payCalendarWords` (one sentence of 20 words or fewer), `payCalendarShort`, `payCalendarPreview`, `cutoffWordsFor`, `latenessWords`, `usuallyLandsWords`.
- **The table and the save**, migration `20261112150000_payer_pay_calendars.sql` (see `docs/migrations/`). Read by RLS through `can_read_payment_promises()`; written only by `set_payer_pay_calendar(p_payer_customer_id, p_job_id, p_calendar)` behind `can_write_payment_promises()`, the two gates a promised pay date already uses.
- **The reads**, `src/lib/jobs/payCalendarIo.ts`: `loadPayCalendars`, `loadPayerPayCalendars`, `savePayCalendar`, and `loadPayerReceipts` (the payer's newest 12 measured payments, through `get_customer_pay_speeds`). Before the migration is pushed a read finds no table and answers none.
- **The window**, `PayCalendarModal.tsx`: the three choices, the pay-when-paid box and the clause; a blue box that says the calendar back in dates (*A bill in by Nov 30 is due Dec 10. A bill in on Dec 1 is due Jan 10.*); an amber box, *Their record against this*, that replays the payer's payments against what is typed and follows every change. A reader who cannot set one sees it with no Save. Removal asks first, and the confirm's button names the act.
- **The opener**, `PayCalendarModalContext.tsx`, mounted in `App.tsx` outside the customer profile's own provider, so the window is never drawn inside another window's backdrop. Any screen that knows a payer can open it.
- **The first door**, `PayCalendarLine.tsx`: one line of small words under **Pays in** on the customer profile window and on the customer's page. *Set a pay calendar…* for the office when there is none, the calendar in a few words when there is. The underline alone marks it as a press (`.payCalendarPress`).
- **The guide** *set a payer's pay calendar*.

## Not changed

- No expected date, no forecast, no email. `billedExpectedPayModel` is untouched.
- How pay speed is measured.
- A payer with no calendar. Nothing reads the table for them.

## Deploy

The client reads a table and calls a function that only the migration creates, so **push right after the merge** (`bash scripts/db-push.sh` from a checkout at `origin/main`), then the types regenerate as their own `chore(types)` PR. Either side of the push the line and the window stand: before it the read answers none, and a Save says the function is missing.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **A setting that changes no date is half a feature.** It ships first so the table is on prod before the rows that read it, and so the window's record line can be read against real payers before any date moves. PR 2 is the other half.
- **The record rests on 12 payments a payer**, the newest the pay-speeds read returns. Recent behavior is what a forecast wants, and it is thin.
- **The clock is the day the bill went in as the app knows it**, not the day the payer accepted it. No such day is recorded; PR 4 adds it.
- **A calendar holds one pay day.** A payer with two check runs a month does not fit, and the guide says so.
