# v2.7341 — One bill can have a due day of its own (2026-11-15)

Punch list #341, PR 4 of five: *This bill is due on…*. Taunya picked the rest of the train on 2026-11-15. A Pay calendar says when a payer's bills fall due, and a job can carry its own. Some bills answer to neither: a retainage release, a dated draw once it is billed, a final payment. The contract names a date for that one bill. The nearest thing the app had was a promised date (*They said*), which is kept per job and so moves every open bill on it.

## The call this stands on

**The owner's call 3 is not answered.** Its first pick was *not yet: wait for the first retainage release or dated draw that reads wrong*. Taunya's pick of the whole train builds it now, **as carded**: a day, a clause and a few words for one bill, at the top of the ladder bill, job, payer, guess. Nothing reads differently until someone sets a day, so the owner can leave it unused. To take it out of sight, the press is one block in `JobsStagesTab.tsx` (`data-testid="bill-due-day-press"`).

## What changed

- **`invoice_due_days`** (migration `20261115170000`): one row a bill, with `due_on`, `clause` and `words`. Read by RLS through `can_read_payment_promises()`, written only by `set_invoice_due_day` behind `can_write_payment_promises()`: the Pay calendar's own two gates. See `docs/migrations/20261115170000_invoice_due_days.md`.
- **The day rides with the calendars.** `loadPayCalendars` reads the days with the calendars and hands them back as rows (`parseBillDueDayRow`); `indexPayCalendars` keys them by bill (`byInvoice`). So no screen loads anything new: each one that already reads a calendar finds a bill's own day by handing `calendarSourceFor` the bill's id.
- **The ladder.** `calendarSourceFor(index, { payerId, jobId, invoiceId })` answers `from: 'bill'` with `ownDue` when the bill has a day, and `under`, what it would read without one. No calendar is handed on for such a bill, and it needs no payer on file: a bill typed to another email reads its own day too.
- **The pay model.** `billedPayModel` reads the day as both the due day and the expected day (`ownDayModel`): no calendar is read and no record is replayed against a date the contract names once. The model carries `ownDue`. A date the customer named still wins the expected day, and the line under it reads *due Feb 15 by this bill's own day*.
- **A billed row.** Under the dates, beside *Counted from*, one press: *This bill is due on…*. Set, the row reads **Due** on that day, the line under it reads *This bill's own day · § 9.4* with the clause's words on hover, the press reads *Change this bill's day…*, and *Counted from* goes, since a named date counts from nothing. The press sits on **every** billed row the office can mark, not only on a row whose payer has a calendar: no payer has one yet, and PR 1 was the fix for a door that hid behind one.
- **The window** (`BillDueDayModal`): the day, the clause number and its words, and two lines said back before the save: *By this job's own pay calendar it is due Dec 30.* and *On its own day it is due Feb 15.* **Clear this bill's own day** puts the bill back on its calendar.
- **Every reader.** The board's rows and phone rows, the Payment forecast, Call mode, the lien runway, the job window's bills, the Development page's Money look, They owe us and the residential flow's Paid round read it through the model. Money waiting, Accounts Receivable and Quickfill's Billed Awaiting Payment read it through `billCalendarRead` and `leanDueByBill`: such a bill is green to its day, amber past it and red 30 days past it, and reads *due Feb 15 · 5d past due*. A payer's summary line reads *12d past due* against *a bill's own due day* when that bill is the one furthest past.
- **Guide**: *set a payer's pay calendar* gains *One bill due on a named date*.

## Not changed

- A bill with no day of its own, on every screen.
- Cutoffs coming and the cutoff lines: they speak of work not yet billed, and a day is set on a bill.
- The morning run (`messages-nudge-dispatch`) selects its own calendar rows and does not read `invoice_due_days`, so a Paid round's nudge still reads the calendar for such a bill. It is the one reader left, and it wants a deploy on Taunya's go.

## Deploy

**Push right after the merge.** The client reads `invoice_due_days` and calls `set_invoice_due_day`. Before the push the read answers none and a save is refused as a missing function, so nothing breaks, and nothing can be saved. Then `npm run gen-types:linked` as its own `chore(types)` PR.

The edge copies of the kernels under `supabase/functions/_shared/gcKernels/` are regenerated (`payCalendar.ts`, `billedDuePay.ts` and the readers that now hand over the bill's id). The functions that read them build their index from their own selects, which hold no bill's day, so a redeploy changes nothing they send; `npm run check:edge-drift` lists them as behind until the next deploy of each.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **One more quiet line on every billed row.** It is the price of a door nobody has to hunt for. If the board reads busier for it, the row's menu is the other home.
- **No record against a named date.** A retainage release paid three weeks after its date still reads as due on the date. One bill gives nothing to replay, so the day is the honest answer, and the row turns amber the day after.
- **The day is typed, not worked out.** *60 days after final acceptance* is a clause the office reads and turns into a date. The app holds the date and the clause beside it.
- **A dated draw not yet billed has no home still.** That is gap 9 on the card, left alone.
