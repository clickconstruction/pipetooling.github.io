# v2.6091 — Phone first: Leaving on the job window opens On my way for the next job (punch list #189, the Leaving PR) (2026-10-29)

Punch list #189's decision 6, answered on the review canvas on 2026-10-29: the mock-up's flow 8 stands, Leaving opens the On my way sheet for the next job when the day has one, and Update Focus when it has none. With it, the card's follow-up: the window Leaving opened stood at z-index 1000 behind the job window at 1010 on a phone.

## What changed

- **`lib/jobs/jobWindowBar.ts` → `leavingOpens({ dayHasNextJob })`**: `on-my-way` with a next job, else `update-focus`. Tested.
- **The bridge** (`UpdateFocusOpenerBridgeContext`) gains `registerOnMyWayOpener` / `requestOnMyWay`, the shape of the clock-out opener: Job Mode's card registers an opener while mounted that opens its On my way sheet on the day's next block and answers false when the day has none; `requestOnMyWay` answers false when no card is mounted.
- **`DetailJobModal` → `stampLeaving`** (the crew's bar, the tabbed window's bar through the pane, and the composer's chip at a desk): after the stamp lands, `requestOnMyWay()`, else `requestOpenUpdateFocus()` as before (v2.1640).
- **`ClockInOutButton`**: the Clock In window, which the bridge opens when the tech is off the clock, moves from z 1000 to 1020 (`UPDATE_FOCUS_OVERLAY_Z_INDEX`, where Update Focus already stood since the v2.1640 fix): above the job window (1010) and the crew's plain window (1004). The On my way sheet stands at 1100 already.
- **Guides** *clock in and out with Job Mode* (the job window on a phone) and *read a job's window*: what Leaving opens.

## Decisions

- **The sheet comes from the card, not the window.** The sheet needs the day's blocks, the suggested job, the clock-in bridge and the text io, all of which Job Mode's card already holds; mounting a second copy in every job window would read the day twice. So the window asks the card through the bridge, as Clock out does, and falls back to Update Focus wherever the card is not mounted (the Jobs page, Job Mode off), which is what a tech off the Dashboard had before.
- **The job window stays open under the sheet.** The tech pressed Leaving on that job; the sheet opens in front and closes back to it, and the card behind has moved on to the next job.
