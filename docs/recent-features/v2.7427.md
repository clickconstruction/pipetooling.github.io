# v2.7427 — Lien desk: In the envelope is one card (2026-11-15)

Taunya, on job 523's notice pane at 756 px (2026-11-15): "In this section of the lien desk, I don't like how it looks. How could we present it better?" She was shown a drawing of the section as one card and answered "Yes, build it". Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What a person sees

The section **In the envelope** on Monthly notices drew two cards over the paper: a wrapping line (*✉ To … and … by certified mail*, three ticks, an amber sentence) and a second card with the key to the shaded boxes and the preview door. At 756 px the ticks wrapped under the line with nothing to line them up, and each amount hung off its words by a dot.

It is one card now, two halves and a foot line.

- **Goes to · certified mail**, at the left: *Owner* and *Contractor*, a labelled row each with the name in bold. The muted line *and a courtesy PDF to …* sits under the contractor when we have an address.
- **With the notice**, at the right: the ticks in a fixed order, one per row, the tick, its words and its amount at the right in tabular figures. The list is at most 400 px wide, so an amount stays near its words.
  - **Counsel's cover letter** (was *Include counsel's cover letter*), with a muted *page 1* at the right while it is ticked.
  - **Name the supply house owed** (*houses* by the same count), with what the houses are owed.
  - **A conditional release** (was *Enclose a conditional release*), with the claim.
  - An unticked row's words are muted.
- **Counsel's caution** moved under the tick it is about and reads *⚠ Counsel has not read this paragraph yet.* The one sentence at the card's foot that named one paragraph, the other or both is gone.
- **The foot line**: the amber swatch and *A shaded box on the paper is yours to change. Hover says where the rest came from.*, with **Preview in a new window ↗** at the right. Once the wording is locked the two sentences that stand in for the key read as they did.

From a card 620 px wide the halves sit side by side with a hairline between them. Narrower, and on a phone, they stack with the hairline across.

## How

- [`src/components/jobs/LienEnvelopeCard.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienEnvelopeCard.tsx) is new and holds no state: the desk passes each tick's `checked`, `disabled` and `onChange`, and `null` for a tick that is not drawn. The conditions, the tooltips and the markers `data-lien-desk-send-line`, `data-lien-desk-houses-tick`, `data-lien-desk-release-tick`, `data-lien-desk-houses-counsel` and `data-lien-desk-paper-legend` are as they were. The release's caution has its own marker, `data-lien-desk-release-counsel`. A caution is outside its label, so a press on it ticks nothing.
- [`src/components/jobs/LienDesk.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDesk.tsx): the two cards inside `data-lien-pane-body="envelope"` are one `<LienEnvelopeCard>`. Nothing below it changed (the page labels and the pages).
- [`src/index.css`](../../src/index.css): the `.lienEnvelope*` block replaces the old four rules and `.lienFootMuted`, which nothing else read. The card is its own container (`lien-envelope`), and the two-column rule is a container query on it, so the layout follows the card and not the window. Theme tokens only.
- Guides: `send-lien-notices-from-the-lien-desk`, `give-a-customer-a-lien-release` and `see-which-supply-houses-are-owed-on-a-lien-job` name the two renamed ticks and say where they sit. [`docs/LIEN_DESK_ARCHITECTURE.md`](../LIEN_DESK_ARCHITECTURE.md): the sentence that lists the envelope body's parts.
