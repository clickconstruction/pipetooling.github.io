# v2.6150 — A trade's moved dates drawn on its own schedule row (punch list #217, outside-13, a prototype) (2026-10-30)

Punch list #217, *Show it, don't say it*: eight scans found 131 places where the app tells instead of shows, and a mock-up page draws each as a before/after pair for the owner to pick from. Of outside-13, the trade partner portal's *Your dates moved*, the owner wrote on 2026-10-30: "I really like the idea of this. I just want to see it side by side in a prototype before I approve it." This is that prototype, a draft PR with no auto-merge, stacked on v2.6149 (outside-12), which carries the portals' shape switch and the side-by-side helper. No migration. Additive fields on `gc-trade-portal`.

## The finding

Under the phases and the trade's own bars, the portal reprints the email: *Your dates moved on Sample Dental Office · The schedule on Sample Dental Office moved. Your work is now planned for these days. Trim and fixtures: Wed Nov 11 to Tue Nov 17, not Sun Nov 8 to Sat Nov 14. Why: weather: Two days of rain held the drywall. In your portal, tell us the dates work, or give us another day.* The page tells, in an email's voice (*In your portal…* inside the portal), a move the bars right above it could draw.

## What changed

- **`gc-trade-portal`** (`_shared/gcTradePortalSchedule.ts`): each of `scheduleWork[job].dates` also carries `moves` — the lines the move changed, each with its `work` and its days `from` and `to`, from `datesNotices`' `lines` — and the move's `reason` and `note`. Additive: a page on a function deployed before them keeps the block. The sample fixture carries the same for its one move (three days later, weather).
- **`movedDatesRow.ts`** (`src/lib/gc/`, new): the pure kernel from a told move's line to its row. `movedRow` reads the direction (*later*, *earlier*, or *none* when the days did not change), the whole days the start moved (the finish when the start stayed), the ghost (the old days; none on *none*), its label (*was Nov 8 – Nov 14*, *era 8 nov – 14 nov*) and the chip (*moved 3 days later · weather · Two days of rain held the drywall.*, the office's reason in Spanish on a Spanish portal); `movedRowsByLine` keeps one row per line, the newest move first; `datesLeftForBlock` the moves no row of the company's draws, which stay in the block; `withSampleMoves` lays the fixture's moves over a sample answer that has none. Eight tests: a move later, a move earlier, no move, a finish moved on its own, Spanish, the newest per line, what the block keeps, the sample overlay.
- **`GcTradePortalSchedule`**: an optional `ghost` by bar — a dashed amber bar behind the company's own bar, clamped to the chart's span, with its label under it; the row grows to hold the label. Nothing changes without it.
- **`GcTradePortalMovedRow`** (new): under the bar, the chip and the two answers of *Your dates moved* — *These dates work*, or *I need another day* with the day and a word on why — posted as `answer_dates` as the block posts them. The block's own form is kept as it was, so a trade on today's page sees no change; the row carries its own copy.
- **`GcTradePortalProject`** draws the job's schedule in one of three ways from the page's switch (`portalShapeMode`, v2.6149): as today; with the move on the row (`?shape=1`), where the block keeps only the moves no row draws; or both one above the other (`?sbs=1`), captioned **Today** and **With the strip**, on the sample token or for a signed-in dev. `GcTradePortalView` and the page pass the mode down; on the sample token the page lays the fixture's moves over the function's answer until the function carries them, so the row draws on a dev server before the deploy.
- **Settings → What customers see**: the trade partner's portal step has a **Side by side** press beside *Open in new tab*.
- **Smoke** `GcTradePortal.movedRow.render.test.tsx`: today's block without the flag; the ghost, the chip and both answers on the row under `?shape=1`, each posting its kind; both looks under `?sbs=1` on the sample; the fixture's moves over a sample answer without them; the block kept for a real answer without them; today's look under `?sbs=1` on a real token with nobody signed in.
- **Guide** *share a trade partner its portal*: the schedule paragraph names the prototype and the Side by side press.
- **Docs**: `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` → gc-trade-portal, the `scheduleWork` line carries the fields.

## What the mock-up asked for that this does not do

- **The strip of days.** The mock-up names the strip of days (`timelineStrip.ts`, the Legal desk's) as the reuse; the trade's chart already draws its days as bars on one axis, so the ghost sits on that axis and no second strip is drawn.
- **One form for the block and the row.** The block's answer form was left as it is so a trade on today's page sees no change; the row carries its own copy of the two answers, to fold into one once the shape is picked.
- **The `ScheduleMoveReason` in Spanish from one home.** The portal's own words hold five of the office's twelve reasons (the late notice's); the kernel carries all twelve beside the row until a home is picked.

## Seeing it beside today's look

A dev server from this branch, then `/t/sample?sbs=1` → **Sample Dental Office**: the schedule draws **Today** (the bars, then the *Your dates moved* block) above **With the strip** (the ghost behind *Trim and fixtures*, the chip and the two answers on that row, no block). `/t/sample?shape=1` draws the row alone. In Settings → What customers see, the trade partner's portal step's **Side by side** press opens the first.

## Deploy

`gc-trade-portal` changes (the fields on `dates`) and is not deployed by the merge. Until the deploy the row draws on the sample from the fixture, and a real trade's page keeps the block whatever the flag says.
