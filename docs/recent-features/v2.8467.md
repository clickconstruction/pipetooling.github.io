# v2.8467 — Sub Labor: the sheet form offers nobody whose account is archived (2026-11-24)

A gap punch list #389's walk of a sub with no login found on 2026-11-24. The dev seat refused a sub labor sheet for a sub whose account was archived, and the app's own **New Sub Labor** form would have offered the same person. Client only: no migration and nothing to deploy.

## What was there

The form's *Paid to* lists come from two reads (`src/pages/Jobs.tsx`): the live accounts, and the roster rows that are not archived. A roster row's own archive mark was the only one read for a row. A person whose **account** was archived while their **roster row** stayed on file was still offered, from the roster row.

That state is real. On 2026-11-24 prod held seven such rows: three subs, three helpers and one test person, their accounts archived between May and November. None had a sheet. They come from the days when a press archived an account alone. Today the Person desk's **Archive…** opens End employment, which archives the roster row with the account, so no new row gets into that state.

The dev seat already reads the account's mark (`_shared/devMcpSubLabor.ts`): *a sheet is not written for someone who has left*. The form now follows the same rule.

## What changed

- **A kernel, `src/lib/people/leftAccounts.ts`.** `linkedAccountIds` names the accounts the roster rows are linked to. `accountsThatLeft` says which of them have left. `withAccountArchived` marks each row, and `accountHasLeft` reads the mark.
- **An archived account is known by its absence.** Row security closes an archived account to the office, so a read for *archived accounts* answers nothing to an assistant. The roster load asks which linked accounts are **live**, and a linked account that is not among them has left. Read as the sample assistant on prod, every live linked account came back and exactly the seven archived ones did not.
- **A reader who sees none of them hides nobody.** A failed read, or a role the accounts are closed to, marks no row. The rows are then offered as they were before this.
- **The form** (`JobsSubLaborFormModal.tsx`) leaves a marked row out of its lists, beside the rule that leaves a benched sub out. A name already on a sheet stays, as with the bench.
- **Help guide**: `manage-subs-end-to-end` says so under *Active or on the bench*.

## Not changed

- The roster row itself. It stays on People, on the Pay ledger and on every sheet it is on.
- The seven rows on prod. Whether each person is ended through End employment or brought back is the office's call.
- The other pickers that read the roster. Only the sheet form is in this change.

## Tests

- `leftAccounts.test.ts`, 7: each linked account asked about once, an account missing from the live ones has left, a reader who sees none is told nothing, and the mark on a row.
- `JobsSubLaborFormModal.render.test.tsx`: a sub whose account is archived is not in the crew lists while their roster row is live. Run once with the rule taken out, the test fails.
