# v2.8409 — Supply houses: one box searches every bill (2026-11-23)

Punch list #431, the screen half of PR 5. The database half, the function `search_supply_bills`, shipped as v2.8406 and its migration is pushed before this merges. No migration and no edge function here.

## What changed

- **Search every bill**, a box on Materials → Supply houses above the houses (`src/components/materials/BillSearch.tsx`). A person types an invoice number, a supply house, a PO, an amount, a day, a month or a part printed on a bill. The bills that hold every word are listed newest first.
- **What a hit shows**: its number, its supply house, its day with no year this year, its amount with small cents, the jobs it is on with each share when it is split, its PO, *paid*, and *no PDF*. A bill a part found says *On the paper:* with the line and the house's code, the search's words marked.
- **What is asked** (`src/lib/materials/bills/billSearch.ts`): the plain words go to the function as words. The first day or month typed is the date to narrow by, read by the same kernel as the Job Summary's list, so *nov 16*, *11/16* and *2026-10* mean the same in both places. A search starts at two letters. The box waits a quarter of a second after the last key.
- **The answer on show is the answer to the words in the box.** An older search that answers late is not drawn under newer words.
- **A press on a number opens the bill viewer** with the bills found beside the paper and the search's words marked on the bill's lines. **The link icon** copies the bill's own link.
- **A search that finds nothing** says so and offers **Clear the search**. **One that fails** says why and offers **Try again**. **Sixty bills** is a full page, and the count says *the first 60 bills*.
- The read is `supabaseBillSearchIo`, one call, as the signed-in reader. The function runs as its caller, so the office finds bills and nobody else finds any.
