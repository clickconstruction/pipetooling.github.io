# v2.5928 — A page to send a GC who asks for a notarized waiver (2026-10-26)

GCs and vendors still routinely demand notarized lien waivers out of habit, though HB 2237 struck "and notarized" from Prop. Code § 53.281(b) effective 2022-01-01. The owner wanted a universal explainer an estimator or assistant can send beside a signed release without souring the relationship. A research session wrote the vetted one-pager (cites checked 2026-10-26); this PR turns it into a help guide whose public `/g/<slug>/` share page staff paste into vendor emails.

- **New guide** `src/content/help/answer-a-gc-who-asks-for-a-notarized-waiver.md` (Billing & Money; dev, master_technician, assistant, controller, estimator). The short answer (§ 53.281(b) as amended, the § 53.284 statutory forms, substantial compliance as the condition of effectiveness — the diplomatic core: the form protects both sides), the one residual notary case (a release recorded with the county clerk, § 12.001 acknowledgment), a what-to-tell-them script, and the send-it steps: make the release from Pipeline the normal way, Copy share link, paste the line into the email. Links to *give a customer a lien release*, *share a help guide* and the § Rules page.
- Rewritten from the research one-pager to pass `plainWords.ts`; cites ride in italic spans, which the glue rule reads as quoted print.

No code, no migration, no edge function. Tests: the four guide conformance suites (`helpGuideContent`, `helpGuideLinks.guides`, `helpGuidePlainWords`, `helpShareCard.guides`).
