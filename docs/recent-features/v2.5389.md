# v2.5389 — Messages: quick replies that fill in the customer’s name and the visit, behind the scenes (2026-10-12)

The residential Messages build's R3 kernel (`to-dos/gc-mode/messages/RESIDENTIAL_BUILD_PLAN.md` → R3, on branch `spike/gc-mode`), lifted from the residential prototype v0.8 (`customer-messages-prototype.html`: `BLANKS`, `fillLine`, `blanksIn`, `phoneLines`, `shortTitle`, `hintFor`, `wordsFor`) by customer communication helper 4, directed by the customer communication lead. Two new pure kernels with their tests over made-up people (Dana Whitfield on 4120 Oakridge Trl, Luis the tech, Rosa at the office). No screen, no table, no edge function, nothing to deploy. R3's SQL PR adds the three columns and the screens PR draws the editor's chips, the Phone tab and the phone's sheet.

- **`src/lib/quickReplies/blanks.ts`**: `BLANKS`, the fill table in the editor's chip order (`first`, `me`, `address`, `job`, `minutes`, `window`, `back day`, `back time`, `day`, `link`, `amount`), each with its chip words and where the app reads it (`BlankSource`: the person, you, the job, the drive, the schedule, their page, the estimate or bill). `THEIRS` holds the chips the app never fills (`{thing}`, `{found}`). `fillLine(text, fills)` returns the filled text and `left`, the blanks it could not fill, each once, in order; a blank the table does not know, or a value that is empty, stays in the text and in `left`. `blanksIn`, `blankWords` (the chip's words, or "yours to fill") and `fillFirst(left)`, the box's refusal: "Fill {found} and {thing} first. The app does not know them."
- **`src/lib/quickReplies/phone.ts`**: `PhoneLine` extends the kernel's `QuickReply` with `phoneOrder`, `phoneName` and `textEs`, so `kernel.ts` is untouched. `phoneLines(lines, stepId)` keeps the team's customer lines marked for the phone, the step the job is on first, then by place, at most `PHONE_MAX` (6, the owner's to change). `shortName` is the phone name, else the line's first sentence past the opening name and any "this is … with Click Plumbing" sentence. `hintFor(text, fills)` is the hint under a button. `wordsFor(line, lang)` gives the Spanish when the person reads it and the line has it. `SOMETHING_ELSE` is the last press with its words.
- **Changed from the prototype**, each the lead's call or a fix: the chips say "their first name" and "their page's link", not "her", since a customer can be anyone; the short name drops only a leading `{first},`, where the prototype's pattern dropped any first word ("Your quote is in…" read "Quote is in…"); a sentence ends at a stop followed by a space, so a filled "9:30 to 10:00" is not cut at its colon; the greeting skip also covers "this is Click Plumbing"; the hint shows the value when the caller knows it ("about 25 minutes, from the drive") and its words when not; a line kept for one person never reaches the phone; Something else names the office, not Rosa.
- **The caller gates Spanish**: `wordsFor` uses the language it is given, so the phone passes `portalShownLang(lang)` and Spanish waits behind the portal's gate, as the page's words do.
- **Waits on**: `stepsByFlow` and the `es` version kind, a follow-up kernel PR after R0 lands; R3's SQL PR for `quick_replies.phone_order`, `phone_name` and `text_es`.
- **Checked**: 41 tests in `blanks.test.ts` and `phone.test.ts`; each of 25 rules was broken on purpose once and a test failed every time.
