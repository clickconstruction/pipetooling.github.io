# v2.5932 — The Bridge: the Sales desk behind its card (2026-10-26)

Punch list #192 (`to-dos/bridge-stage-desks/`), the Sales desk. The owner asked for a desk behind every stage card the way the Collect desk opens (v2.5890's door) and on 2026-10-26 approved the Sales hybrid, "the chase, in expected dollars" (`mockups/sales-desk.html`). The one question: of everything asked, what will actually become work, and which call this week protects the most of it? Built read-only, to the mock-up's content and the three reviews' rulings; one of four sibling PRs (v2.5929 Build, v2.5930 Bill, v2.5931 Market).

## The kernel (`src/lib/bridge/sales/salesDesk.ts`)

- Per asker (a GC by `gc_builder_id`; bids with none fold into *No GC on the bid*): the **win rate** is won dollars over won plus lost dollars across its decided bids of the last `SALES_DECIDED_DAYS`, when it has at least `SALES_RATE_MIN_DECIDED`, else the window's win share (`marketWinShare`; the row says *the window's P%*). The **answer pace** is the median of `outcome_at − bid_date_sent` in days, *as recorded* (the skeptic's ruling: the office's day, not the GC's), or `SALES_NO_PACE_DAYS` when none. An asker with the minimum decided bids, none won and every loss `no_answer` is **dead weight**: expected $0, its open dollars in the legend, a relationship call.
- Per open bid: the age counts from `bid_date_sent`; older than `AWAITING_DAYS` (90) it leaves the pile and is counted apart with a Bid Board door. **Expected** = value × rate × aging, where aging is 1 inside the pace and fades linearly to 0 at `SALES_AGING_PACE_MULTIPLE` × pace, so a bid past twice its asker's pace reads $0.
- The bar by expected dollars: protected (inside pace), at risk (past pace), evaporating (the estimates, fading at the company's median bid pace since no estimate pace is on record), dead weight as a dashed sliver. The tiles: what the pile is worth, at risk on N calls, what thins the reading (bids at $0 value plus losses with no reason).
- The rows: the top six askers by expected dollars, Everyone else folded, The estimates apart, every dead-weight asker by name. Each carries its story (every bid with its age and days past pace), a say-line where the facts give one (the biggest bid's share of expected, the inversion when the biggest open dollars are not the leader, the goodwill line inside a long pace, the relationship line on dead weight), the move (*Call X about Y.* on the past-pace bid a call re-arms the most of, *Leave them.* inside pace, *Decide the relationship, not the bid.* on dead weight) and the door. `sortSalesRows` flips to open dollars; `salesFlipNote` names who leads by open dollars and the win share that says otherwise.
- The funnel over the decided span: won, then each `loss_category` with the label from `BID_LOSS_CATEGORIES`, then the losses with no reason as the blind spot. The headline (`$X asked · worth about $Y`), the verdict (*N calls this week protect $Z of it.*), the sub-line, the rule lines and the footnote are generated and held to the plain-words rule.

## The reads (`src/lib/bridge/sales/loadSalesDesk.ts`)

Lazy on the desk's first open, read-only, nothing new on the server: the open bids (`bid_date_sent` set, `outcome` null or outside won / lost / started_or_complete, in two reads since NOT IN drops nulls) less the ones that became a job (`jobs_ledger.bid_id`, chunked, billing-only jobs ignored as `demandPace` does); the decided bids of the last 365 days by `outcome_at` with `loss_category` and `loss_reason`; the GC names from `bids_gc_builders` for the ids in play; the estimates with `status = 'sent'` inside the 90-day window (`sent_at`, `total_cents`, `customer_id`; `doc_kind` null or `estimate`). The window's win share and estimate share come from the page's `demand` reading rather than a second read.

## The owner's calls, built to defaults

| constant | default | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| `SALES_RATE_MIN_DECIDED` | 5 | fewer decided bids than this and the asker's rate falls back to the window's win share |
| `SALES_AGING_PACE_MULTIPLE` | 2 | expected dollars fade linearly to zero between the asker's answer pace and this multiple of it |
| `SALES_NO_PACE_DAYS` | 45 | the answer pace assumed for an asker with none on record |
| `SALES_DECIDED_DAYS` | 365 | the span the per-asker win rate and answer pace read |

Each is an exported constant at the top of the kernel; the owner changes the number and the desk, its footnote and its tests follow. `AWAITING_DAYS` (90) is `demandPace.ts`'s and bounds the pile.
