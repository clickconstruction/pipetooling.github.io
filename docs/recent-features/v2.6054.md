# v2.6054 — Materials on site: the app makes a job's Drive folders (2026-10-29)

Punch list #209. The end-to-end photo test on test job 972 stopped at Drive: the job's folders were made by hand and the service account could not write there (`Upload failed (403)` on the row since v2.6050). The owner said on 2026-10-29 that long term a tool of ours should make those folders under the jobs folder. This PR is that tool: the job-side press plus a backfill, on the plumbing `drive-intake` already has.

## What changed

- **`job-drive-folders`** (new edge function, `supabase/functions/job-drive-folders/index.ts`, `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` → job-drive-folders): as the service account, finds or makes `<job number> · <job name>` under `DRIVE_JOBS_FOLDER_ID` and under it **Customer Pictures**, **Plans** and **Team only** with **Photos** and **Sent by us** inside, the names `photoFolderFor` and `drive-link` look for. A job whose bid already has a folder (`bids.drive_link`, made by `drive-intake`) takes it as its own, so a bid's folder and its job's meet. It writes `jobs_ledger.google_drive_link`, `job_pictures_link` and `job_plans_link` where they are empty and keeps a link that is set, saying whether the account can write there (Drive's `capabilities.canAddChildren`). Office roles, never a training account or a digital twin; `verify_jwt = false` with the JWT checked in-body, as `gc-drive-access`. Idempotent: a second press finds what is there. Refusals in words with a `why`: a link that is not a folder, a folder the account cannot write in (with the account's address to share it with).
- **The press** *Make the job's Drive folders*: on the materials page's Deliveries card (`JobsMaterialsTab.tsx`, where *Set the job's Drive folder* was; *Paste a link instead* keeps the Edit door) and in the job window's Documents tab (`JobWindowDocumentsTab.tsx`: the folders empty state's press, with *or Edit* after it, or a quiet button under the list when a pictures or plans link exists without a job folder). The window patches its copy of the job, bumps the Job pane and fires `onSaved`, so the page's list follows (`onFoldersMade`). After the press the card reads where the folder came from and links the three folders; a kept folder the app cannot write in is named in amber with what to do.
- **The desk's backfill** (`DriveFolderBackfill` in `JobsMaterialsTab.tsx`): a *Drive folders* card on the Materials desk, its button from `planDriveFolderBackfill` over `batchWords` (*Make folders for 12 jobs · 1 has a link that is not a folder ▸*, dead only when nothing is ready). It runs the ready jobs one after another, keeps going past a job that fails, and afterwards lists the held jobs, the failed ones with their reason and the ones that got folders but keep a hand-made one the app cannot write in, each with *Open the job*; the toast is `batchOutcomeWords` (*Got folders for 12 jobs. 1 did not get folders.*).
- **Kernel** `src/lib/jobs/jobDriveFolders.ts` (+ `jobDriveFolders.test.ts`): the link's state, `driveFolderIdFromUrl`, the answer's shape and the row patch, the line under the press (plain words), the roles, the batch's plan and outcome. The call is `src/lib/jobs/jobDriveFoldersIo.ts`.
- **Docs**: `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` (the section, the TOC line, the count, photo-save's cross-reference), `docs/DRIVE_INTAKE_SETUP.md` → Using it, the guide *see what is on every job site and what walked away* → Land a delivery, `supabase/config.toml`, the release note and this fragment.

## Decisions

- **A kept link is kept.** The press never replaces a folder the office chose. When the app cannot write there, the answer says so and names the address to share it with, or the office clears the link on the Edit tab and presses again. The hand-made pictures folder on test job 972 is that case.
- **A bid's folder becomes its job's** when the job's bid has a `drive_link` the account can write in: one folder per piece of work, named as `drive-intake` named it.
- **Nothing is shared with anyone with the link.** A Pipeline job's Plans folder is a plain folder, unlike a GC project's (`gc-drive-access`); the folders inherit the Shared Drive's membership and nothing more.
- **The batch runs in the browser, one job at a time**: the function makes one job's folders, so a row that fails never stops the rest and the office watches the count.

## Deploy

`supabase functions deploy job-drive-folders` and `supabase functions deploy dev-mcp` (its generated catalog, `supabase/functions/dev-mcp/catalog.ts`, lists the new function) from the main checkout, `npm run check:edge-drift` before and after. No migration.
