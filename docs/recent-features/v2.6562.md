# v2.6562 — The Day tab's time chips read in dark mode (2026-11-06)

Micah, 2026-11-06, with a screenshot of the Schedule page's Day tab in dark mode: "The colors here on yellow and yellow aren't super easy to see in dark mode." The agenda row's time chip (`QuickfillScheduleUserRow.tsx`, the phone agenda of v2.1350) fills with the literal light orange `#fed7aa` and set its words with `var(--text-orange-800)`, which is `#9a3412` in light and `#fdba74` in dark, so the words went pale on the pale fill. No migration; nothing to deploy.

## What changed

- `src/components/schedule/QuickfillScheduleUserRow.tsx`: the chip's fill is `var(--bg-orange-100)` and its words `var(--text-orange-800)`, a token pair that darkens together in dark mode, the way the app's other chips are drawn. A first cut kept the words as the token's light value as a literal under the literal fill, which CI's theme check refuses (a mapped hex in an inline style), so the pair it is.

## Test

`npm run check:text-fill-words`, `node scripts/theme-tokenize.mjs --check src`, lint; the chip read in the pane in dark mode.
