# v2.7500 — Bids: a promised schedule with a start and an end you type, drawn on the plans by date (2026-11-16)

Punch list #367, PR 4. The owner's call of 2026-11-16: *I would like for the user to set the start and end if there is a schedule.* Nothing is counted from the day the bid was sent. It reads the columns of migration `20261117070000` (v2.7496), which must be pushed before this client deploys.

## The rule

`src/lib/bids/bidSchedule.ts`, pure. A schedule is three states and no more:

- **No start**: the bid has no schedule.
- **A start and an end**, the end not before the start.
- **A start and *No end date***: a schedule that runs on.

A start with neither an end nor the tick is half said and does not save (`scheduleDraftProblem` names what is missing, in the order a person fixes it). The rules mirror the database's three checks, so a draft that passes here saves. `scheduleStanding` says where a schedule stands today (*45 days left*, *ends today*, *34 days over*, *starts in 12 days*, *no end date*) and `scheduleLengthWords` how long it is (*18 months*, *6 weeks*).

## Where it is typed

- **Edit Bid**, a **Schedule** row under Bid Date Sent: Start, End, a *No end date* tick, and quiet words beside them (*18 months · 45 days left*). The row is `BidScheduleFields`. It rides the form's autosave: the three columns are one prune group, so any box changed writes all three together, and a schedule half said is left out of the update and not recorded as saved (`buildBidSavePayload`, `holdUnfinishedBidDates`), so it is written once it is whole. The row says what is missing; no toast.
- **The Development page**, from a bid's card on the By date view: **Set the schedule**, or **Change the schedule** on a bid that has one, opens a small window with the same row (`BidScheduleSheet`): **Save the schedule**, dead until something changed, **Remove the schedule**, **Cancel**. The write is `writeBidSchedule`, read back so a refusal is an error with its reason.

Whoever can edit a bid can set its schedule: the columns ride `bids`' own policies, and the card offers the press to the five roles that write plans (dev, the leaders, the assistants, the controller, the estimators).

## On the chart

- A bid with a schedule gets a **Schedule** lane under Bids: a bar from the typed start to the typed end, both named inside the bar where it has room. The lane's sub line says where it stands.
- A schedule with no end date runs off the right edge as an arrow.
- **Past the end** the bar keeps its end, a red stretch runs from the end to today, and the figure turns red and counts the days over. Red is a status colour here and never a series colour, and the words say it too.
- The first figure becomes **Schedule ends** (or **Schedule · No end date**), the schedule of "the bid" when it has one, else the first to start. With no schedule on any bid the figure is *First drawings*, as before.
- The dated list gains a line for the day the schedule starts and one for the day it ends.
- The schedule's bar, its lane's name and its two lines share one card: Starts, Ends, Length, **Change the schedule**, **Open the bid**.

## Both doors

The office door reads the three columns with the bid. The overview door (an estimator) gets them from `list_development_overview`, which hands them over since the migration, and from its direct read of bids at the address.

## Polish from the viewport pass

Looked at with made-up rows at 375, 768 and 1280 px, light and dark, in a headless browser: a schedule with days left, one over, one with no end, the window, the card.

- On a narrow screen the key folds behind **What the marks mean**, so the chart is not pushed six lines down the page.
- The chart's words wear a thin edge in the surface's colour, so a grid line, today's line or a split line never runs through their letters.
- A month's or a year's word on the axis is drawn only where the whole of it fits inside the chart.

## Verify after deploy

Not walked on a live site. On a bid of a development: type a start and an end on Edit Bid, close, reopen, and see both. Open the development, press **By date**, and see the Schedule lane and the figure. Press the bid's mark, **Change the schedule**, tick **No end date**, save, and see the arrow. As an estimator, see the same lane.
