# v2.6497 — The owner's email and phone live on the property record (2026-11-04)

Taunya's report of 2026-11-03 on job 663 (Kestrin Contracting, 4110 N Main St, Taylor): the courtesy notice before a lien (punch list #231) sends the owner of record a copy by email, and nothing on the property window or on Edit Job's property record panel took the owner's email or phone. The only column that held an owner's email was `job_property_owners.owner_email`, the per-job override that only the supply-house share window writes, so on a job whose owner came from the property record the courtesy window read *no email on the property record* with no way to fix it.

## What changed

- **Migration `20261104163131_owner_contact_on_property_record.sql`** (`docs/migrations/20261104163131_owner_contact_on_property_record.md`): `customer_addresses.owner_email` and `owner_phone` beside the mailing address, and `job_property_owners.owner_phone` beside the override's email. Additive; no table made. **Push right after the merge.**
- **The fields.** `CustomerPropertyRecordPanel` draws **Owner email** and **Owner phone** under *Owner mailing address*, wherever the record is edited: the property window (`LienPaperPropertyWindow`, from the Lien desk's paper and from Edit Job's **Fill in the record / Change the record**), Edit customer's property sheet and the owner-confirm boxes. The draft kernel `propertyDraft.ts` reads them off a row that carries the columns and writes them back trimmed (`ownerContactFromRow`, `ownerContactPayload`); a row read before the push carries neither, so the save names no column that is not there. The lien papers never read them, so `customerAddressLienGaps` and the lien-ready chip are unchanged. Edit Job's *What the lien papers read* panel adds the two rows under the four facts, grey when blank (*not on the record yet · the courtesy notice goes here*), never red; `useJobPropertyCandidates` reads the whole row so the columns ride in once pushed.
- **The kernel.** `resolveLienProperty` (`lienProperty.ts`) gives `owner.email` and `owner.phone` from the source that names the owner, the per-job override or the property record, and a blank on the override falls back to the record's (`recordOwnerContact`). The Lien desk's courtesy window and the run's owner email method (`lienDeskRun.ts`) read them from there; the desk reads `job_property_owners` whole so `owner_phone` rides in softly.
- **The owner's text.** The Messages spine knows customers, trade partners, architects, leads, supply houses, candidates, subs and the team, and has no kind for a property owner, so the window never makes a contact for one. `loadCourtesyRecipients` asks whose number it is (`findContactIdForPhone` in `messagesIo.ts`, over `contact_for_channel`, a read) and `courtesyOwnerChannels` (`lienCourtesyContact.ts`) plans the text only on a contact that already carries the number, on that channel, under the GC's own holds (Send texts off, a STOP on the number); otherwise the Text column reads *not in Messages, so the email goes alone*. The press texts through `quo-send` as the GC's does and makes nothing. The record's words and the Timeline's already say which channel went to whom.
- **Guides**: *send a courtesy notice before a lien* says where the owner's email and phone are typed and when the owner's text goes; *file a lien and never miss its deadlines* names the two rows on the Property record panel.
- **Tests**: `lienProperty.test.ts`, `propertyDraft.test.ts`, `CustomerPropertyRecordPanel.render.test.tsx`, `lienCourtesyContact.test.ts`, `lienCourtesyIo.test.ts`, `messagesIo.test.ts`.

## Decisions

- **The property record, not the override alone.** The brief asked for the fields on the property window and the override's phone column. The window saves `customer_addresses`, shared by every job at the address, through one draft kernel, so the owner's contact lives there beside the mailing address and the override gains the phone so its owner block stays whole. One write path, no second one.
- **A blank on the override falls back to the record.** The override's email had one writer, the supply-house share window, and most overrides have none; the record is where the office types the owner from now on. The window's amber note says the courtesy notice reads the job's owner and takes the record's email and phone only where the job's owner has none.
- **No contact is made for the owner.** The brief asked for the owner's text through the GC's path, making the owner a Messages contact only when the text goes. A contact needs a kind the spine has, and a property owner is none of them: a lead would land in the leads' lane and flow, an unknown would ask *Who is this?*. An `owner` kind is a Messages change (the constraint, `ContactKind`, the loader's known kinds, the lanes, the boxes) and the plumber's call, not this train's. So the text goes where it honestly can, on a thread that already holds the number, and the window says so otherwise. No fragment or the architecture map forbids texting the owner; Tex. Fin. Code § 392 governs the words, which are unchanged.
- **Nothing writes `job_property_owners.owner_phone` yet.** The supply-house share window types the override's email and not its phone; its owner block goes to the supply house, so widening it was left out.
