# v2.8667 — A person's desk: a developer sets their seat and their role on CountTooling and TakeoffTooling (2026-11-26)

The fourth PipeTooling piece of the accounts train (one staff account across PipeTooling, CountTooling and TakeoffTooling, managed from People). Client only: no migration, no edge function. The owner approved its drawing on 2026-11-26.

## What was there

The desk's *Access & account* had one **CountTooling** row, for an estimator or a person already linked: *seat linked* or *no seat*, and **Make a seat**. TakeoffTooling had no row. Neither site's roles showed anywhere, so the Sites lens (v2.8657) read a CountTooling overseer as a plain *active* seat. CountTooling's roles are its own: an **overseer** sees every bid, a **lead** hands a bid to another estimator, an **admin** manages its users. PipeTooling's nine roles carry none of them.

## What changed

- **The Sites rows** (`PersonDeskSitesRows.tsx`), for a dev, on anyone with a login. One row a site: *no seat*, *active* or *retired*, the last sign-in where the site sends it, and the flags the lens shows (*by address*, *address differs*, *link gone*, *twin*). One press a row: **Make their seat**, **Retire here** (asked first: *They can no longer sign in there. Their work stays*) or **Bring back**.
- **Role there**, under a site's row once there is a seat. CountTooling's three stand together, so each is a switch: **Overseer**, **Lead**, **Admin**. TakeoffTooling's is one of three: **User**, **Admin**, **Dev**. Making an admin or a dev asks first. PipeTooling's own role decides none of them.
- **The lens shows the roles.** `SiteAccount.role` became `roles`, and `parseSiteRoster` reads CountTooling's `is_overseer` and `is_lead` beside `is_admin`.
- **The kernel decides every press** (`siteAccounts.ts`): `siteSeatPress`, `siteSeatActiveBody`, `SITE_ROLE_OPTIONS`, `siteRoleIsOn`, `siteRoleChange` (a CountTooling press sends the one flag it flips; pressing the role a TakeoffTooling seat already holds sends nothing), `siteRoleDoneWords`, `isFleetAddress`. `siteAccountsIo.ts` gains `sendSiteVerb` and `makeTakeoffToolingSeat`; `useSiteRosters` reads the two sites for one desk.
- **A robot's or a sample's seat** is shown and never pressed from a desk: those are made where the fleet is made.
- A site that cannot be read says why on its own row, with **Read again**, and the other row still works.

## What it needs

The verbs behind the presses: CountTooling's `set_flags` and TakeoffTooling's `set_role`, `deactivate` and `reactivate` (both sites' functions deployed 2026-11-26), forwarded by `ct-bridge` and `tt-bridge` since v2.8666. **Until those two proxies are deployed, a role press and a TakeoffTooling retire answer *Unknown or missing verb*.** Making a seat and retiring a CountTooling seat work today.

TakeoffTooling has no id column on `users`, so its seat is found by address each time and the row says *by address*.
