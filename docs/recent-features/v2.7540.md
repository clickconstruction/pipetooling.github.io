# v2.7540 — Messages: keep a job's email as a paper from the email itself (2026-11-16)

Punch list #368, PR 4 of four, the last. No migration and no function.

## The gap

**Keep this mail as a paper on JP…** (`MailPaperDoor`, punch list #239) drew in one place: the My email reader, on a mail tagged to a Pipeline job. My email shows your own mailbox. A mail another person tagged, one the dev seat tagged, or one a company box's reader placed on the job offered the press to nobody else. The bid's press met the same gap and was built on the mail itself (v2.7534), so the job's press moves there too.

## What changed

- `emailViewerActions.ts`: a line on a Pipeline job that is an email and carries a file (a link is not a file) offers `keep_paper`. A job's line offered nothing in that row before.
- `EmailViewerActions.tsx` draws `MailPaperDoor` for it, on its own row under the tools since its form needs the width. It is handed the job, the job's number from the thread's tile, the subject, the mail's day, the line (so the files follow the paper) and, on a mail that came in, the thread's own address for the *From* guess.

The door, its form and `plan-set-files` are untouched. Who may keep a paper is still the table's own policy, and a refusal shows in the door's words.

My email keeps its press.
