# v2.6076 — Phone first: how far along each stage is, with a precise number (punch list #189, PR 5a) (2026-10-29)

Punch list #189's PR 5a, built on the picks of 2026-10-29 (the review canvas, board #189): section 3 of the mock-up as redrawn (every stage in view with a precise number) and section 4 (the stepper as a sheet from the bottom with the same control), both on the owner's words of 2026-10-26 — *improve how complete is this job so it breaks down by stage* and *make it easy for a user to specifically offer how far along things are*. Built before the camera (PR 5b), as he asked.

## What changed

- **The kernel** `src/lib/reports/stagePct.ts`: the step (5), the quick picks (25 · 50 · 75 · 100, the last reading *Done ✓*), the clamps (`stepPct`, `clampPct`), the typed number (`typedPct`: digits only, clamped, null for a cleared box), the control's words and heights (48 px picks, 56 px steps), and `saveWords` (*Save · job 36%*, *· 2 photos* once PR 5b lands). `stagePct.test.ts`.
- **`StagePctControl`** (`src/components/reports/`): the control itself — four quick picks, then −5 · the number · +5, the number a button that becomes a numeric box (`inputmode="numeric"`) on a tap, committed on Enter or on leaving the box; *Tap the number to type one.* under it. −5 is dead at 0 and +5 at 100.
- **`ReportLinesProgressField`**: the open row takes the control in place of the 33 px chips and the slider; a row nobody has reported reads *not started* (`LINE_NOT_STARTED`; the saved Stage progress field keeps `—`); the question reads **How far along is each stage?** (`LINES_QUESTION`, *each line?* before). The render test holds the heights and the typed path.
- **`FieldPctUpdateModal`** (Update % done): drawn in `PhoneSheet`, so a sheet from the bottom on a phone and a centred box at a desk, with the job's label under the title, the body scrolling, and a foot of **Not now** and **Save 65%** / **Save · job 36%**. A one-stage job takes the control alone for the job, in place of the ±1/±5/±20 chips (the figure, the ▲/▼ readout and the bar stay above it). The door from the press bar's % done is v2.5905's; nothing new to open it. Escape is the sheet's, one layer at a time.
- **`AdditionalReportModal`** (Leave Report): the job's `pct_complete` and `status` ride on the links read it already made, the untouched percent field is seeded with `fieldPctStartValue` (the number the card shows; 0 for a Working job with none), and the line under the field reads *Opens on the job's 40%. Leave it, and the report files 40%.*, then *Was 40%.* once moved. This answers the card's follow-up: an untouched Save on a one-line job wrote 0 to the job through `propagateReportPctToJob`. `AdditionalReportModal.render.test.tsx` opens a one-line job at 40%, presses Save with nothing touched and reads 40 on the insert.
- **Guides** *file and review field reports* (the stage section and the one-stage line) and *update how done a job is from my dashboard* (the sheet, the three ways to a number, the bar's door); the Glossary's Report by line entry.

## Decisions

- **One control, three hosts.** The stage row, the stepper's one-stage job and (behind *Set the whole-job % instead*) the stepper's whole job all draw `StagePctControl`, so a tech learns one thing. Leave Report's whole-job slider (`ReportTemplatePercentField`, a one-line job) keeps its five chips and slider until PR 3 grows them: the brief named it there.
- **Every stage in view, one open.** The rows were already all in view with their numbers; what changed is the open row's press sizes and the typed number. Opening every row's control at once would push Save off a 375 px screen on a four-stage job.
- **The typed box commits on Enter or blur and never on each keystroke**, so a tech typing *45* does not pass through 4.
- **The stepper's title is the question** on a staged job (*How far along is each stage?*) and *How done is this job?* on a one-stage job; the sheet's × stays beside Not now, since the office opens the same window at a desk.
