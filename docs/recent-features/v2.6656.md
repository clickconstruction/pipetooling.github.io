## Read closer than the card

- **Save moves only lines still assumed** by construction, not by a sweep: an assumed line stores nothing and reads the company split at draw time, so a saved change reaches every such line on every job at once and never a line with columns.
- **The weekly Needs you review card moves to PR 1c.** *Looks right · stamp 4* needs a reviewed mark the line row does not have (a stamped line must stay assumed, so `budget_source` cannot carry it); that is a column on `jobs_ledger_fixtures` and its own PR.
- **Spent per line is left out for good** until the clock lands hours on a stage: `clock_sessions` carries no stage, so a line's spend cannot be read from today's data, and the card's own words make it conditional on that.
- **The learning line's material** is one read of the tuned lines (`jobs_ledger_fixtures` where `budget_source = 'tuned'`, up to 1,000) when the card opens for the owner; a failed read means no line, never a wrong one.
