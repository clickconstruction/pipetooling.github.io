# v2.6095 — Supply-house bills: the PDF fills in the Add invoice window (punch list #179, PR 3) (2026-10-29)

Punch list #179, the last of three PRs. PR 1 (#6451) rewrote the card to the approved second redraw; PR 2 (v2.6088) built the reading behind a switch. This PR puts it on the screen, as Taunya approved on 2026-10-29 after her word *augment the manual entry, never replace it*: the Add invoice window stays the one way a bill is recorded, the PDF fills its fields like an autofill, a guess asks for a look in a sentence with a Yes, and two doors open the form from where the PDF already sits. The card is deleted with this PR; the release notes and these fragments carry the record.

## What changed

- **The bill, the form's first question** (`SupplyHousesTab.tsx`): a new first section on the Add invoice window for the office while the switch is on. *Start from:* a chip per PDF or picture on the house's thread not yet on an invoice row and not marked *Not a bill* (`billsToRecordIo.loadCandidates`: `message_attachments` on the houses' contacts' lines, unanswered, with our copy, less the rows' `bill_attachment_id`), *Another file…* (a PDF or a picture from the device, sent to `bill-read` as base64 and kept under `bills/<house>/<uuid>/<name>`), *No PDF, I will type it* (the fields the fill wrote go back to blank; the typed ones stay). A pick calls `bill-read`, plans the fill against the house's ledger, its job accounts, the jobs the printed place finds and its invoices (`billFill.planBillFill`), and sets the form's state for every field the person has not typed (`applyPlanToForm`). The count line under the picker: *Filled in from the PDF. Two things to check, marked below. One thing is yours to set. Change anything you like. What you type stays.*
- **Three looks, no words** (`billTint`): a field from the paper is tinted green with a green left border; a guess is amber with its sentence and a Yes; a field the paper cannot answer is plain and *yours to set* (the Status). The legend under the fields names the three. The words read, guessed and typed live on the row's `bill_read` and nowhere on the screen. Typing into a field marks it typed (`markBillTyped`); the fill never writes over it again, and a re-pick keeps it.
- **The guesses**: the job's card turns amber with *The paper names no job. J1042 is a guess from PO 41207 and the ship-to address. Right job?* and **Yes, J1042** / **Pick another job**; the job-account tick says *A guess, since the account is open. Untick if this bill is not on it.*; a credit's **Credits invoice…** is guessed from the *RE:* number with a Yes, and its job comes from the invoice it credits, said so. Yes settles the field and the count falls.
- **The checks as sentences**, and Save still works: under Amount the lines add to the total with the tax (*…so the total is right. The lines are kept with the invoice.*) or they do not (amber, with **Use $1,248.10** and **Keep $1,284.10**); under Invoice # a number already on this house's list (amber, **Open it** when the row has a link) and a letterhead that is not this house (red); under the picker a statement listing several invoices, one bill at a time.
- **The PDF beside the form**: our copy signed for a minute, the first page on `PdfPageCanvas` at desktop width, a strip with **Open** above the fields on a phone; a picture as itself. Under Paperwork, *Invoice PDF: S1183420.pdf, our copy from the thread.* with **Use a Drive link instead**; our copy's lasting link is the row's `link` (`/files/messages/<attachment>` for a thread file; `/files/bills/<invoice id>` for a dropped-in file, the new `bills` source of `fileRoute.ts`, which reads the path off the row's `bill_read`; the form picks the row's id up front so the link exists before Save).
- **Save** keeps the read on the row (`billRowRead`: the file, the model, the fields with their sources, the lines, the totals, the checks) through the one write (`supplyInvoiceWrite.ts`, now with the optional `id`), marks a thread file *filed* (`answerPaper`) so the offer and the count let it go, and re-reads the counts.
- **The two doors**: on the house's thread in Messages, `PaperOffers` offers *This reads like a bill from Ridgeway Supply.* with **Record this bill** (closes the thread, lands on `/materials?tab=supply-houses&house=<id>&bill=<attachment>`; `Materials.tsx` hands the tab `autoOpenHouseId` and the new `openBill`, which opens the form on that file once the house is open) and **Not a bill** (`paper_answer = 'not_it'`); the line under the file then reads *Recorded as a bill · Marisol, Oct 29* or *Not a bill · …* (`billAnswerWords`). On the Supply houses tab the house's row says *2 bills to record* with **Record them**, which opens the house and the form on the first.
- **The switch**: migration `20261030020000_supply_bill_read_on.sql` turns `bill_read_on_v1` on; the client shows the section and the doors to the office roles only (`useBillReadSwitch`); the owner or a dev turns it off and on from a line beside the Accounts payable heading. With no model key the fill says so in one line and the form stays typeable.
- **Words and docs**: the guide *record a supply-house bill from its PDF*; *mark an invoice as on a job account* and *record a return or credit from a supply house* amended; `GLOSSARY.md` → Materials System, `MATERIALS_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` §11, `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`; `to-dos/supply-bill-capture/` deleted.

## Decisions

- **The job is always a guess**, even from a code on the ledger: the paper never prints the job's id, so the card asks for a Yes and names the evidence. A credit's job is not: the invoice it credits carries it.
- **Nothing is written before Save**, including the thread file's answer: *filed* lands with the row, so a form closed without saving leaves the offer where it was.
- **The switch ships on in this PR**, as the card's plan says, because the client gates by role and the fill with no key is one line of words and a typeable form.
