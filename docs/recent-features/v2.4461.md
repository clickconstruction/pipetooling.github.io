# v2.4461 — Demand letters and printed invoices: a bill sent in the evening keeps its day (2026-10-02)

One of the instant-day fixes that follow [v2.4452](v2.4452.md). Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

The papers a bill goes out on, and the demand letter that chases it, cut days out of `timestamptz` columns by their first ten characters, so anything stamped after 7 pm CDT (6 pm CST) read as the next day:

- **The printed invoice** ([`physicalInvoiceDocumentForBilledInvoice.ts`](../../src/lib/physicalInvoiceDocumentForBilledInvoice.ts)): its *Invoice date* is the day the bill was sent (`sent_to_customer_at`), else billed (`billed_at`), through the file's `ymdFromDbDateish`, which slices. That model draws the invoice in Documents, *Open PDF*, the copy re-sent by email (`resendPhysicalInvoiceEmail`), a GC's unpaid-invoice print, the invoice enclosed with a § 53.056 notice and Exhibit A of a demand letter.
- **The demand letter** ([`demandLetter.ts`](../../src/lib/jobsDocuments/demandLetter.ts)): the statement of account's *sent* day (`buildDemandStatement`, `billed_at` → `sent_to_customer_at` → `created_at`) through the file's `ymdOf`, which also reads the due date, a `date`; and the letter's invoice date (`buildDemandLetterPrefill`'s first bill day), sliced.
- **The notice enclosure's title** ([`noticeInvoiceEnclosure.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/noticeInvoiceEnclosure.ts)): *Invoice #2, October 3, 2026 enclosed*.
- **The notice history the letter cites** ([`LienInstrumentsModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienInstrumentsModal.tsx)), also Exhibit C's delivery record: the bill's billed and delivered days, each Stripe re-send (`jobs_ledger_invoice_stripe_email_sends.sent_at`), each payment promise (`createdAt`, from `list_job_payment_promises`) and each collection call (`job_payment_chase_touches.created_at`). The signed agreement's *signed* day sliced `job_contracts.signed_at` too.

## What changed

Each instant reads `calendarYmdInAppTzFromIso(iso)` from [`dateUtils.ts`](../../src/utils/dateUtils.ts) (`APP_CALENDAR_TZ`). The `date` columns keep their slice: `estimated_bill_date` (the due date), `job_contracts.paper_signed_on`, and `job_demand_letters.sent_at`, which is a `date` despite its name. A stored letter reprints on that day, and only a letter with no sent day falls back to its `created_at`, now read in Central. `demandLetter.ts`'s `ymdOf` now says it is for date columns.

Every caller of the printed-invoice builder passes a `jobs_ledger_invoices` row as read, so none hands it a bare day.

## Tests

- `demandLetter.test.ts` (+2): a bill billed at `2026-10-03T00:30:00Z` (7:30 pm CDT), or sent then in PostgREST's `+00:00` shape, reads *sent* Oct 2 on the statement; `2026-12-02T00:30:00Z` (6:30 pm CST) reads Dec 1 while the due date stays as stored; noon UTC reads its own day; the letter's invoice date is the first bill's Central day.
- `noticeInvoiceEnclosure.test.ts` (+1): the enclosure title and the enclosed invoice's *Invoice date* read October 2 for an evening bill and December 1 for a winter evening send; noon UTC reads its own day.
- `LienInstrumentsModal.render.test.tsx` (+1): an evening bill reads *sent October 2, 2026* on the statement and Exhibit A, and *October 2, 2026 — Invoice sent* in the notice history.

All four fail on main: the kernels' three, and the render case with either main's modal or main's kernels.

## Checked against prod

Read only, from the worktree dev server: 19 of the 563 billed or paid bills now print a different invoice date, all of them paid. 4 of the 137 Stripe re-sends went out in a Central evening, all on paid bills, and none of the 17 collection calls or 7 signed agreements is from an evening. So no open bill's demand letter reads differently today; a paid bill's reprinted invoice carries its own day.
