## What landed

- `src/components/jobs/SubPapersWindow.tsx`, the view over built inputs: which paper, the period, *What it will show*, the paper itself as the preview (the print page under a light wrapper), then **Print** and **Download the PDF**. The whole set is checked with `subPapersAddUp` before any paper is drawn. A set that fails shows what is off, draws nothing and its presses are dead.
- `src/components/jobs/SubPapersWindowConnected.tsx`, the window on real rows: one person's sheets by `paperSheetsFor` (the `sheetOwedTo` rule every sub screen uses, with `TEAM_SHEET_RULE`), the letterhead from the bill's issuer block in Settings, the PDF drawn in the browser with pdf-lib, and a sent copy filed on the person and each job at Print and at Download. A download whose copy could not be filed says so.
- The presses:
  - **Jobs → Subs → Pay**: *Statement of account…* on a person's group, and *Work statement…* in Print's place on the ⋯ menu of a sheet that is one person's. A sheet two people share and a crew sheet keep Print.
  - **People → Subs**: the owed block's *Statement of account…* and a sheet's *Work statement…*. The block had the two props since v2.7401's train and nothing handed them in.
- `src/lib/sent/sentCopies.ts`: the kinds `sub_work_statement`, `sub_payment_record` and `sub_statement_of_account` sort under *Statements and reports* on Documents → Sent copies. They fell under *Other*.

## What differs from the snapshot

- **No register.** The snapshot read `sub_papers` and took a refusal as *not pushed yet*. This window reads nothing that is not on the database, so no paper has a number and its foot carries the day and time it was prepared.
- **No Send and no payment record.** The view still draws both, behind what its caller hands in: `paymentRecords` shows the payment record's pick, `canSend` with an `onSend` shows the send step and the Attach boxes, and a `lastStatement` (null for a sub with none) shows *Since the last statement*. The connected window hands in none of them, so nothing reads an address and nothing calls a function that does not exist. Each later step turns its own part on.
- **A period closes on today at the latest.** The ledger's *This month* and *This year* run to the 31st as a filter bound. On a paper that read *Nov 1, 2026 to Nov 31, 2026*, so the window clamps the end to today.
- **The window leaves room for the status bar** the way its neighbours do (`--app-top-chrome`).

## Hard lines held

No paper says invoice, receipt or owed, and the six sentences for counsel print boxed as draft wording (`SUB_PAPER_COUNSEL_PENDING` is true). A render test reads both papers for the three words. The rule holds the papers' own labels: a line the office typed on a sheet prints as typed, and the card now says so for call 1.
