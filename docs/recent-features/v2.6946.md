# v2.6946 — On a job billed to its builder, the in kind strip reads the builder's arrangement (punch list #301) (2026-11-11)

Found by the live walk of v2.6934. On a job whose bills go to its GC, two screens read two different customers to decide whether "this customer pays in kind". Bill Customer read the payer: its `job` is `applyPayerToJobBillingContext(jobRaw, payerRecipient)`, which swaps `customer_id` for the GC's. The Job window's strip read `headJob.customer_id`, the job's own customer. So the strip could offer **Put it on the arrangement** for the customer's deal while Bill Customer asked nothing and billed the GC for money.

The owner's call, 2026-11-11: the GC's. Whoever the bill goes to owes it, so that party's arrangement is the one that could pay it.

## What changed

- **The kernel**, `jobInKindPayer` (`src/lib/inKind/inKindChip.ts`): whose arrangements a job's bill reads. It is `listPayer(job, null)`, the rule the *who owes us* lists already use: the GC when the job's rule sends bills to its GC (or the job has a GC and no customer row), else the job's customer.
- **The Job window**, `JobWindowModal`: the strip's standing is the payer's, and the strip names the payer. With two open arrangements its press opens the payer's page.
- **The chip in the title row is unchanged.** It sits beside the customer's name and speaks for that customer.

Bill Customer needed no change. On a job billed to its customer, nothing changes anywhere.

## Not here

- **A GC-billed job already on its customer's arrangement.** `in_kind_arrangement_set_jobs` accepts a job whose customer or whose GC is the deal's customer, and **Change the terms** lists a customer's jobs whoever pays them. Such a job reads *Scheduled in kind* on every list, but Bill Customer reads the GC's standing, finds the job on none of the GC's deals, and opens an ordinary money window. No job is in that state today: none of the sixteen jobs on the one arrangement is billed to a GC.

## Verified

`inKindChip.test.ts` (the payer for a customer-billed job, a GC-billed job, a GC job with no customer row, a GC rule with no GC, no job) and `JobWindowModal.inKind.render.test.tsx` (on a GC-billed job the GC's arrangement shows the strip in the GC's name, the customer's shows none, two arrangements link to the GC's page; a customer-billed job is as before). The mismatch itself was seen live on 2026-11-11 with arrangements made up in the browser only, no write. This fix was not walked live.
