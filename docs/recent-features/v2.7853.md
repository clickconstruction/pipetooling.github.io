# v2.7853 — Lien desk: Sign N notices counts only the jobs the leader leads (2026-11-18)

Found on the live look at punch list #383 on 2026-11-18, and built on the owner's word the same day: *"in a new session, fix both of those findings"*. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## Before

- On *Approved · to sign* the office reads *Remind <leader> of N papers ›* and a master reads *Sign N notices ›* (v2.7712).
- The master's N was `readyWaits`: every approved notice whose signature does not stand, whoever leads its job.
- Seen live through View as: the Sample leader, who leads none of the jobs, read *Sign 7 notices ›* on a blue edged bin, under a tile that read *waiting on* another leader's name.
- The door opened By paper's whole *Approved · to print* pile, signed notices and other leaders' notices with it. A press that said 7 opened a longer list.
- The same desk already counted his *Approve N affidavits* by the jobs he leads (`lienBoardForLeader` over `lienViewerLeadsJob`).

## What changed

- **`lienBoardSignSplit(board, leads)`** in `lienBoard.ts`: the chips of *Approved · to sign* in two parts, `mine` on the jobs the viewer leads and `others`. The two add up to the bin's own count.
- **`lienBoardSignDoor({ mine, others, othersLeader })`** in `lienBoardPrints.ts` words three things: the press, *Sign 3 notices ›*; one line under the bin's name for what waits on someone else, *4 wait on Malachi.*, or *on another leader* when they do not share one; and the words over the narrowed list. Null when none is his.
- **The door opens exactly what it counts.** `showNoticesToSign` in `LienDesk.tsx` opens By paper's approved notices narrowed to his jobs, through the list's job filter, whose chip reads *Only the notices that take your signature* with *show every job* (`calendarJobFilter.words` in `LienDeskNoticeList.tsx`).
- **`useLienDeskBoard` hands the split** (`signSplit`), with the one leader the rest wait on when they share one.

## What was settled, and why

- **A leader who leads none of the waiting notices reads the office's reminder**, not an empty bin. A master is the lien office too, To do's waiting rows already give him that press, and the reminder then names only another leader's papers.
- **A leader who leads some keeps his own press alone**, with the line for the rest. The reminder's plan would count his own notices with the others, so it is not offered beside his press.
- **The list is narrowed to match the count.** A press that says 3 and opens 7 is the two numbers the Board was rebuilt to end (punch list #383).
- **A dev follows the same rule as a master**, the one his *Approve N affidavits* already follows: he leads a job with no master, or his own. On a desk where every job has a master he reads the reminder, as before.
- **The blue left edge follows by itself**: it is drawn for a live press that is the viewer's own act, and the reminder is `quiet`.

## Not changed

- The bin's count, the second tile and the notices' print: they count every notice that waits on a signature, as every viewer reads every bin.
- The reminder's plan and its words.
