## Decisions

- **Items are question rows, not a table of their own.** The answers' foreign key, the strict rule, the photo road and the report photo chips all read one table, so a job item is answered, photographed and counted exactly as a company question is. The migration's check keeps a job item's job and stage together.
- **The superintendent's jobs are the database's call.** The press is drawn for the role; `superintendent_report_job_anchor_allowed` (the assigned project or the job's team, the reports' own rule) refuses an add on any other job with its reason.
- **The adder takes off whatever their role, and does nothing else.** The card: who takes off is the adder or the office. The database's update policy says the same (`created_by = auth.uid()`), and it holds the adder to the retire, so an update cannot move a live item onto a job that is not theirs.
- **The strip's empty row is the superintendent's alone.** A superintendent has the read-only job pane with no Documents tab, so the sheet is their one door. The office adds from Documents; listing three empty rows above the tabs of every job window, on a company with no questions typed, would change a screen the office reads all day and #164 promised to leave alone.
- **The read-out lists live questions.** A retired item's answer stays in the table and leaves the list, as a retired company question's does; PR 3's record is where a frozen list reads back.
- **What the spike's walks added to PR 1 is PR 1b.** The card's PR 1 widened while this build was running (Skip on this job, the per-item promote, the plan-sheet chip, reorder, the add box staying open, the estimator as author). None of it is here, and all of it is additive on this migration; the card lists it next.
- **Print, the signature, the record and authoring the company's list on Documents are PRs 2 to 5**, as the card's train says; the sheet's foot keeps the Pipeline rule until PR 3 gives it the filing words.
