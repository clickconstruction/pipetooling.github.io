# v2.6466 — A stopped crew's check stays off the Customer waiting banner (2026-11-03)

Punch list #245 (`to-dos/stage-check-to-dispatch/README.md`): the owner's call of 2026-11-03 on the question v2.6462 left open. A check sent with *Can't do it* is `priority = 'high'`, and `CustomerWaitingContext` feeds every open high dispatch row to the app-wide banner and the Dashboard's **Needs you** item. On the banner such a check read *A customer is waiting*, showed no Call because it has no number, and could never turn amber, since amber means someone called. As the oldest uncalled row it could also take the lead, and the Call button with it, from a customer who had waited less. No migration, no edge function.

## What changed

- **`waitsOnTheBanner`** (`src/lib/customerWaiting.ts`): an open high row waits on the banner unless it is a stage check (`pending_action = 'stage_check'`, the kernel's `STAGE_CHECK_ACTION`).
- **`buildCustomerWaitingBanner`** reads only those rows for the lead, *others* and the waiting and called counts. **`summarizeCustomerWaiting`** counts the same rows, so **Needs you** agrees with the strip.
- The guide *answer a customer waiting request* names the one exception under *Raise the priority*. `GLOSSARY.md` → Stage check and the #245 card say the same.

## Decisions

- **In the kernel, not the query.** Both readers, the banner and `useNeedsYouInputs`, go through these two kernels, which carry the tests. The context's read is unchanged, and the Dispatch inbox keeps the row, its red line and its badge count.
- **Raised by hand too.** A stage check Dispatch raises stays off the banner as well: the banner is a customer's, and the row already leads the inbox.
- **What would bring it back:** stopped crews waiting too long because Dispatch is away from the inbox. The answer then is a crew-specific line, not the customer's strip. The wait reads from each request's `created_at` to its first note or `closed_at`.
