# v2.6777 — The Lien desk is a page: /lien-desk (2026-11-09)

Punch list #182, the Lien desk's PR 1 of 3 ([`to-dos/desks-as-pages/`](../../to-dos/desks-as-pages/README.md)). The owner, 2026-11-09, after two weeks with the Legal desk as a page: move the Lien desk the same way, in PRs of its own. The rule: a desk is a page with its own address when a person stays in it, it has tabs or views of its own, and the page under it goes unread while it is open. The Lien desk is the second case: three views and four lists, a run window, a Calendar, Share, the ☎ door, a full-screen button of its own, and links that carry a job, a kind and a pile.

## Decided here

- **PR 1 moves no door.** The gavel, the ☰ row, the Dashboard's cards, the forecast's door and the Materials lines still open the window, so the risk is one route and one prop, and a rollback is deleting the route. PR 2 turns the doors into links and lifts the window's mount out of `JobsStagesTab`; PR 3 deletes the window frame and draws the phone's list and pane as the address.
- **Only the Notices list's selection is in the address.** The Affidavits and Retainage lists pick their own first row and the old door never named a job on them; `&job=` rides with `tab=notices`, and the desk writes it only there.
- **Records for an owner is a door, not a state.** The window is one act over the desk, so by the rule it is a window: `&records=` opens it and leaves the address at once, as the Pipeline's `?ownerrecords=` left the board's.
- **A window the desk opens stays over the page.** A job's Lien window, Put a GC on notice and Records for an owner mount on the page, so closing one lands back on the desk where it was (v2.4523's rule), and the Lien window's next-step door re-aims the desk through the address.
