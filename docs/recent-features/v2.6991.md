# v2.6991 — Permits: a San Antonio address finds its lot when the county spells it another way (2026-11-11)

Punch list #320, PR 7 of the card's list. Bexar keeps a parcel's address as one string, and the lookup asked for a prefix match on the permit's whole street string, taking the first row. The other counties are asked for the house number and the street's name.

What the prefix missed, read off the shapes of 18 misses with the names masked: a suffix spelled out (LANE for LN, COURT for CT, PLACE for PL, PARKWAY for PKWY, CIRCLE for CIR), a direction on one side only or behind the street, a suffix the county leaves off or writes differently, and a loop the county names ("N LOOP 1604 W" for "N 1604 W", "NW LOOP 410" for "NW 410"). Since v2.6978 those cards are answered at the permit's point with a line that says the county has no parcel under the address, which is not true of them.

- **Kernel** (`_shared/permitParcels.ts`): `bexarAddressWhere` asks `UPPER(Situs) LIKE '<number> %'` and the street's name anywhere in the string, in each of its spellings; a numbered road is asked by its number (`routeOf`: "1604", "LOOP 1604", "FM 78"). `parcelFactsFromBexarAddress` reads each row's street line with `streetParts`, keeps the rows at the asked number on the asked street (the same name in any spelling, or the same numbered road), and takes the asked side of town, then a row with an owner. Twelve rows are asked for, as elsewhere.
- **`permit-lookup`**, Bexar's branch: the new question, then the point. An address with no house number is still asked as one string. A kept "no parcel" whose question is not today's is asked again once.

Measured on 320 San Antonio addresses, old question against new, the lot under the permit's point as a referee: the same lot 114 times, **found only by the new question 18 times** (17 of them the lot under the point), found only by the old one never, neither 186 (new buildings, answered at the point). Once the two named different lots: the old one had matched another street that starts the same, and the new one is the lot under the point. The new question takes about 0.4 seconds.

Tests: `permitParcels.test.ts` (the question, the numbered roads, each spelling the prefix missed, another street, another number, another loop, the side of town, the owner). Every name and number is made up.

Deploy: `permit-lookup` after the merge, from `main`. No migration.
