- **Settings → Phone and texts → Inboxes** (`src/components/settings/BoxesSettingsCard.tsx`), under Company phone seats
  and written the same way. Each box, the **Team inbox** and the **Bids inbox**, shows who it is for (read from

  keeps using today's company number* for the team inbox and *today's bids@* for the bids inbox, and shows no value.

  and the save's words say the same.
- **`src/lib/messages/boxesCard.ts`**: the card's words and checks. A phone typed with ten digits is kept as
  `(NNN) NNN-NNNN`, the way the company number reads, which `phoneWords` turns into plain words. The address and the Quo id
  are trimmed, the address's case kept, since `boxOfInbound` lowercases on compare. `boxesProblem` stops a save in words:
  an address that does not look like one, a phone without ten digits, a Quo id not starting with PN, a phone without its
  id or an id without its phone, and two boxes sharing an address, a phone or an id.
- **`src/lib/messages/boxesCardIo.ts`**: the read and the one write, by `boxes.ts`'s `BOXES_SETTINGS_KEY`. A save that
  comes back with no row was refused, and says so.
