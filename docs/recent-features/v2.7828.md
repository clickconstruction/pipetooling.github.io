# v2.7828 — Materials on site: a take’s photos, side by side, on the box and at the office (2026-11-18)

Take from a box, step 4 of 4. Step 3 made the run; this step reads it back. The owner's design asked who looks at the photos and where: the box's own screen, and the office from the place a shortfall shows, since nobody browses a list of pictures.

## What changed

- **The takes of a job, read from its rows** ([`takes.ts`](../../src/lib/materials/takes.ts), pure): the ledger rows and box photos that share a `take_id` fold into one line a take, with what was taken, what the run recorded as found, the photo as found and the photo after. Its state is read from what is there: `whole`, `no-before`, `no-after`, `no-photos`, or `nothing-marked` for photos with no row.
- **The panel** ([`TakesPanel.tsx`](../../src/components/materials/TakesPanel.tsx)): a card a take, with its chip (*Both photos*, *No photo as found*, *No photo after*, *No photos*, *Nothing marked*) and its pictures side by side. Where an earlier take of the same box has a photo after, it is drawn first as *As left by Jordan · Nov 12* (`takeAsLeft`). Nothing compares two pictures; a person looks. A press opens a picture whole.
- **On the phone**, under a box's parts: **Taken from G12**, the newest two takes with a press for the rest ([`BoxTakes.tsx`](../../src/components/materials/BoxTakes.tsx), a piece that takes the job and the box and reads the store by itself, so another screen can place it). A take's two photos are drawn with their take, and the box's other photos stay where they were.
- **At the office**, History on a job's Materials page opens on **Takes** above the presses, with the chips *All takes*, *A photo missing* and *Nothing marked*, each with its count.
- **From a shortfall**: on Missing, a count that came up short carries a press such as **3 takes before this count** (`takesBefore`, `takesBeforeLabel`). It opens History narrowed to that box's takes, and **Show every box** widens it again.
- Client only. No migration, no edge function.
