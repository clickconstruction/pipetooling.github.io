# v2.4250 — GC Review: Preview shows the email right there, not in a separate window (2026-09-30)

Grace, with a screenshot of the *Email statement to RMC- Dudley Mason* dialog: "when I hit preview I would like for it to open and bring me to the new page instead of just opening the new page in a separate window." Both of GC Review's email previews wrote their HTML into `window.open('', '_blank')` ([`printWindow.ts`](../../src/lib/jobsDocuments/printWindow.ts) → `openHtmlPreviewWindow`), and the browser decided whether that window came to the front; the week's-list preview opened it after an `await`, so a pop-up blocker could refuse it outright. She picked *show it in the app* over a new tab brought forward and over replacing the page (which would have closed the dialog and lost the To, CC and subject), and asked for both previews. Client only, no migration, no function.

- New [`EmailPreviewOverlay.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/EmailPreviewOverlay.tsx): the email in a frame over the window that asked for it — full viewport, a **← Back** button that takes focus, the title and *Preview. Nothing has been sent.* Escape closes the preview alone (a capture-phase listener), never GC Review under it. The overlay ends above the Dispatch / Job mode footer (`--app-bottom-chrome`), which stands over every window. z 66, mounted at the modal's root.
- [`useEmailPreview()`](../../src/hooks/useEmailPreview.ts) holds the state. `show(title, html)` takes the finished HTML or a promise of it: the overlay is up at once and says *Building the preview…*; a build that lands after Back is dropped; a build that fails closes the overlay and rejects, so the week's-list form says why where its button is.
- New kernel [`previewFrame.ts`](../../src/lib/jobsDocuments/previewFrame.ts) → `previewFrameHtml`: puts `<base target="_blank">` in the email's head (or leads a fragment with it), so a link in the preview — the GC's portal address, the office phone — opens a tab of its own as it did from the old window, instead of loading inside the frame.
- The frame's sandbox is `allow-same-origin allow-popups allow-popups-to-escape-sandbox`: no scripts, no forms. `allow-same-origin` is there for speed — a fully sandboxed `srcDoc` frame is given a process of its own and stayed blank for seconds on a loaded machine; in this page's process it painted inside 400 ms. With scripts off it grants the email nothing.
- [`JobsGcReviewModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsGcReviewModal.tsx): the statement dialog's Preview and the Scheduled tab's week's-list Preview both call `emailPreview.show`; the two *Allow pop-ups to preview…* errors are gone with the window. `openHtmlPreviewWindow` stays for its other callers (the lien papers, the job agreement, Documents).
- Guides *track a general contractor on a job* and *run your gc statement round* say where the preview opens and how to get back. [`GC_REVIEW_MODAL_ARCHITECTURE.md`](../GC_REVIEW_MODAL_ARCHITECTURE.md): the two preview lines, the Escape note and quirk 13.

## Verified

- `previewFrame.test.ts` (4), `EmailPreviewOverlay.render.test.tsx` (3: building → email, a late build dropped, a failed build closes and reports) and a new case in `JobsGcReviewModal.render.test.tsx`: Preview opens the overlay with the statement in it and opens no window; Escape closes the preview alone and the typed To address is still in the dialog.
- Walked on the dev server as the dev account, on the live board, sending nothing: *Draft* on RMC- Dudley Mason → a changed subject → Preview showed the statement over the dialog with the changed subject in it; Escape and ← Back each returned to the dialog with the subject intact; the frame ends exactly at the footer's top edge. Scheduled → *Email me the week's list…* → Preview said *Building the preview…* and then showed the week's list (about 0.8 s). At 375 px the overlay fills the phone and the email does not scroll sideways.

## Not run live

- A link inside the preview was not clicked: the portal link carries `?src=gc-statement`, and opening a GC's portal from a walk could read as the GC having looked. The base tag was read off the frame's document (`target="_blank"`).
- Dark mode was not looked at; the overlay's own chrome uses theme tokens and the email paints its own light background.
