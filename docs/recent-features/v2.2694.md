# v2.2694 — Hours approvals queue: every pending session, all weeks (2026-09-03)

The v2.2670/2671 pair made a stalled approvals queue *visible* (Needs You card) but its action dropped you on People → Hours, where every approval surface — the grid banner, its Review & approve modal, the Pending sessions list, the Dashboard My Team card — is scoped to the selected week. On 2026-09-03 the card said 120 sessions / 622h / oldest 122 days; the Hours tab showed 38 of them and "179h pending". Clearing the rest meant paging back week by week and never seeing the pile whole. This PR adds the all-weeks queue and points the card at it.

## Client only — no migration

- **Kernel** [`lib/people/approvalsQueue.ts`](../../src/lib/people/approvalsQueue.ts) (+13 tests): `buildApprovalsQueue(rows, { todayYmd })` → person → company-calendar week (Sunday start, pure YMD math) → session, with per-session flags (`long` > `LONG_SESSION_HOURS` from `myTeamApprovals`; `tiny` < `TINY_SESSION_HOURS` = one minute — 15 was tried and flagged 22 of Taunya's real 3–10 min Update-Focus sessions, one minute catches the 4 sub-minute double-taps; `noJob` = no `job_ledger_id` and no `bid_id`), roll-ups at every level (count, hours, flag counts, session ids for the Approve buttons), oldest-stall-first people ordering (ties by hours desc). Helpers: `weekStartYmd`/`weekEndYmd`/`formatWeekRangeLabel` ("Aug 16–22", "Aug 30 – Sep 5"), `daysBetweenYmd`, `formatFlagCounts`, `withoutSessionIds` (optimistic removal), `describeApproveOutcome` (the approve RPC `CONTINUE`s past zero-length rows without an error — the toast now says "Approved 3 of 4 — 1 zero-length session was skipped" instead of claiming success).
- **Fetch** [`lib/people/fetchAllPendingClockSessions.ts`](../../src/lib/people/fetchAllPendingClockSessions.ts): one `clock_sessions` read with `CLOCK_SESSION_LIST_SELECT`, closed + approved/rejected/revoked all NULL, no date window, oldest first, cap 2,000. Caller RLS — pay roles see the company, a team lead their members.
- **Modal** [`components/people/PeopleHoursApprovalsQueueModal.tsx`](../../src/components/people/PeopleHoursApprovalsQueueModal.tsx) (z 60; the parent's `ClockSessionEditSplitModal` at 1100 and the confirm dialog at 1300 stack above it): summary line, **Flagged only** toggle, **Approve everything / flagged** (confirm), person cards (collapsible; header carries count · hours · weeks · oldest (+age when ≥ 7d) · flag summary · **Approve all N · Xh** with confirm), week rows (**Approve week · N** with confirm; click to open sessions), session rows (day, Denver in–out, hours, the three ⚠ chips with tooltips, job/bid label + `AssignSessionJobPopover` at z 1250 with dispatch quick-picks, note in quotes, **Approve** (no confirm) / **Reject** (confirm, same `rejected_at`/`rejected_by` write as the Sessions list) / **Edit** → parent editor). Approvals use `approveClockSessions`; success removes ids locally, a short count refetches; every mutation calls `onChanged` so the parent's week-scoped lists and `people_hours` reload. Esc closes.
- **Doors**: the Needs You `hours-approvals` action now navigates to `/people?tab=hours&approvals=1` (`DashboardPinnedQuickRow`); `People.tsx` opens the modal on that flag and strips it (`replace`). The Hours header gains an **Approvals** button beside Match sessions — amber with a count badge when `usePendingHoursApprovalsNudge` (now also returns `refresh`, bumped after every queue mutation) reports anything pending, quiet outline at zero. `PeopleHoursPendingBanner` gains an optional `onOpenQueue` → **All weeks** button. The edit modal's `onSaved` bumps `approvalsQueueReloadKey` so the queue refetches after a time/split fix.
- Verified live as dev against prod: 120 sessions / 12 people / 621.8h / oldest 122d, 3 long days, 4 near-zero.
