# v2.8060 — Materials on site: three doors no longer send a primary, a superintendent or an estimator to a page that turns them away (2026-11-20)

Found while mapping every door to Materials on site for punch list #407, and walked by hand at the owner's word the same day. Three doors sent every reader to Jobs → Materials on the job (`/jobs?tab=materials&job=<id>`). That page is the office's. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What the walk found

Walked on the dev server as each role, on the ZZ test job, read-only:

| Reader | `/jobs?tab=materials&job=<id>` lands on |
|---|---|
| The office | the job's materials page |
| A primary | `/jobs?tab=reports`, the one Jobs tab a primary has |
| A superintendent | `/jobs?tab=reports`, since their tabs are Reports and Subs |
| An estimator | `/bids?tab=bid-board`, since an estimator has no Jobs page |

So for those three roles the press worked and the screen it promised never opened. The doors:

- **The search's box rows.** A row of *Boxes on site* opened the materials page for every role but a field one, with **Open on the phone screen** as its second press.
- **The job window's Materials tab.** The tabbed window is the office's and a primary's, and its press was **Open the materials page** for both.
- **The Procure step's line.** The **Materials on site** link under a bid's four steps, which an estimator reads.

## What changed

One pure rule, [`materialsJobDoor.ts`](../../src/lib/materials/materialsJobDoor.ts): the office's door to a job's materials is Jobs → Materials, and everyone else's is the Tally's On site tab on that job, the screen Materials → On site already sends them to (`onSiteJobHref`).

- **The search**: `boxRowsOpenThePhoneScreen(role, fieldLookup)` decides what `boxDoors` is told. A primary's, a superintendent's and an estimator's row now opens the box itself, with no second press, as a field role's always did.
- **The job window**: `JobWindowModal` hands `JobWindowMaterialsPanel` the door by role (`opensMaterialsDesk`). A primary gets the panel a crew gets: **Open Materials on site**, with the money and the credits left out, which are the office's reads.
- **The Procure step**: `BidsSubmittalsTab` hands the panel the reader's role, and the link's address is `materialsJobDoorHref(role, jobId)`. The kernel `onSiteDoor` is untouched, since the edge holds a copy of it.

The office's three doors are as they were.

## Not checked

The walk rewrote the role the app read and kept the dev's own sign-in, so every job was readable. A real estimator opens the Tally's On site tab on a job only if that job is theirs to read. Where it is not, the tab offers the jobs that are.

## Verify

1. As a primary, open the search box, type a box's stencil and press its row. The box opens on the Tally's On site tab.
2. As a primary, open a job's window and its **Materials** tab. The press reads **Open Materials on site** and opens the job's boxes.
3. As an estimator, open a bid that is a job, its Submittals tab and the Procure step. **Materials on site** opens the job's boxes.
4. As the office, all three still open Jobs → Materials on the job.
