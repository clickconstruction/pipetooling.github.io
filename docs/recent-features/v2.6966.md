# v2.6966 — A person matter's text documents are a dev's alone: the fence two tables missed (2026-11-11)

Found 2026-11-11 while mapping the HR files train (punch list #315), on the owner's yes the same day.

## What was wrong

A matter standing on a person is a dev's alone: `20261105220000` put a restrictive `<table>_person_dev_only` policy on the five tables that hung on a matter. Three days later `20261108210000` let `documents` and `document_revisions` hold a matter's text documents, with the office's permissive policies, and neither got the fence. So the words of a `.md` or `.txt` on a person matter were open at the table to a master, an assistant or a controller, while the matter and its Evidence row were closed to them. No screen opened that door. On prod that day only two test rows sat behind it (a `ZZ Test` person's matter), none on a real person.

## What changed

- **Migration `20261112030000_person_matter_document_fence.sql`**: `documents_person_matter_dev_only` and `document_revisions_person_matter_dev_only`, restrictive, every verb, `matter_id IS NULL OR NOT legal_matter_is_person(matter_id) OR is_dev()`. A person's own documents and an account matter's documents are untouched. Page: [`docs/migrations/20261112030000_person_matter_document_fence.md`](../migrations/20261112030000_person_matter_document_fence.md).
- **A bed**: `npm run test:pg:person-matter-document-fence` (`supabase/tests/person_matter_document_fence/`, listed in `scripts/sql-beds.txt`) plays five roles against a person matter's document, an account matter's and a person's own, by read, update, insert and a move between matters.
- **Docs**: `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` and `HR_FILES.md` name the two tables in the sentence that lists the fenced ones; the card for #315 marks its PR 0 done.

## Checked

- The scenario on a local Postgres 15 with the live policies as stand-ins: 19 checks pass with the migration applied twice.
- Three mutants each fail it: no fence, `documents` fenced alone, a select-only fence.
- No client code and no edge function reads a person matter's `documents` rows as a role other than dev: the HR file's Matter view is a dev's tab, `legal-portal` and the file index read as the service role.

## Not done here

The two test rows are another session's walk leftovers and were left as they are.
