# v2.3345 — Who pays the bill, PR 1: "Bills go to" on the job, a party picker on every draft, and Bill Customer follows it (2026-09-11)

Migration `20260911221710_who_pays_bill_to_party.sql` ([`docs/migrations/20260911221710_who_pays_bill_to_party.md`](../migrations/20260911221710_who_pays_bill_to_party.md)); edge functions `create-stripe-invoice`, `preview-stripe-invoice`, `send-physical-invoice-email` changed (**redeploy all three after the push**). First of the four-PR train the owner approved on 2026-09-11 (mock-up artifact `084b417a`); PR 2 makes GC Review, the portals and the payment-terms readers follow the same rule, PR 3 adds the line-level Pays tag, PR 4 sweeps the existing jobs.

## Why

A job knew its customer (`customer_id` / `customer_email`) and, since v2.1175, its GC — but the GC was only a grouping link, and nothing recorded which of the two pays. Every reader guessed: Bill Customer assumed the customer, GC Review and the GC portal assumed the GC, the Their Word train (v2.3280–v2.3285) assumed the GC when one was set. The office worked around the gap three ways, all visible in the data on 2026-09-11: entering the GC as the customer **and** the GC (72 open jobs — all of Knight Contracting and RMC Dudley Mason), typing the GC's AP address into the customer email (job 650 ATI Schertz carries `invoices@lobergconstruction.com`), and opening two jobs at one address, one per payer (14 pairs in 120 days, e.g. Wildflower Springs / Tommy Gillis at the Wimberley ranch). The per-invoice bill-to override built for the tenant hazmat case (v2.1084–v2.1087) had been used zero times in 523 bills — it asks for a typed name and email, and the payer is almost always a customers row the job already names.

## What shipped

- **Schema**: `jobs_ledger.bill_to_party` (`customer` default · `gc` · `split`, CHECK), `jobs_ledger_invoices.bill_to_party` (NULL inherits the job; `customer` · `gc` override it), `customers.billing_email` (where a customer is billed when it differs from the contact email — a GC's AP inbox). The typed `bill_to_email` (someone else) still wins over both.
- **The rule, once**: [`supabase/functions/_shared/billToParty.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/billToParty.ts) — `effectiveInvoiceParty(job, invoice)` → `customer | gc | other` (a typed email → other; the invoice's pick when the job can honor it — `gc` needs a GC; else the job's rule, `gc` only with a GC set), `payerCustomerId`, `customerBillingEmail` (billing_email, else contact_info.email). Shared by the three edge functions and re-exported to the client kernel [`src/lib/jobs/billToParty.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/billToParty.ts) (+`payerRecipientFromCustomer`, `applyPayerToJobBillingContext`, `jobBillToPartyOptions`, `invoicePartyChip`; 14 tests).
- **Edit Job → Edit tab**: a **Bills go to** fact row under the customer's Email (shown once the job has a GC that is not the customer row, or whenever the rule is not the default) — [`JobFormBillToPartyControl`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormBillToPartyControl.tsx), a three-way control (This customer · GC · name · Split by line) with a hint naming who the bills, statement and portal will address. The GC block gains an editable **Billing email** row (saved straight to `customers.billing_email`; "same as email" when blank). The New Job form shows the same control under the GC picker. `billToParty` rides the identity autosave slice (`bp` in the slice JSON; the payload falls back to `customer` when the GC was cleared) and the undo snapshot.
- **Edit customer**: a **Billing email** field under Email.
- **Edit Job → Bill tab**: each draft's *Bill to…* button is now **Bill to ▾** — a menu of the job customer, the GC (when distinct) and *Someone else…* (the existing typed-recipient editor). Picking a party writes `bill_to_party` and clears the typed recipient + its Stripe customer. A **→ name** chip on the row names the payer whenever it is not simply the customer by default (GC, someone else, or any row on a split job); GC/other chips are amber, customer chips blue.
- **Bill Customer** ([`SendRecordInvoiceModal`](../../src/components/jobs/SendRecordInvoiceModal.tsx)): on open the job row (rule + GC) and the target invoice row (pick + typed recipient) resolve the party with the same kernel; a GC payer pulls the GC's customers row and overlays it onto the billing context (`applyPayerToJobBillingContext` — name, billing email, phone **and** `customer_id`), so the Stripe preview/create payloads, the physical prefill, the PDF's *Bill to* and the local line preview all bill the GC. An amber banner reads *Billing Loberg Contracting, the GC on this job — not ATI Schertz*. The *Send to* block offers the **payer's** contact persons and a **Copy <the other party>** tick (the customer on a GC-pays bill, the GC on a customer-pays bill — physical email only, never on Stripe). The missing-email fix writes the GC's `billing_email` when the GC pays; the physical-tab gate names the GC.
- **Edge functions**: `create-stripe-invoice` and `preview-stripe-invoice` select `gc_customer_id, bill_to_party` on the job and `bill_to_party` on the invoice, resolve the party, and require the body's `customer_id` to be the **payer** (the GC's row when the GC pays — its Stripe customer link lives on that row, so the GC pays through its own Stripe customer and the homeowner's is never touched); a mismatch answers *This bill goes to the GC on the job — reopen Bill Customer*. `send-physical-invoice-email` accepts the GC's billing email (or contact email) as the target when the GC pays and refuses when the GC has none.
- Help: new guide **choose who gets the bill on a job** (`choose-who-gets-the-bill`); `bill-part-of-a-job-to-someone-else` now routes through *Bill to ▾*. `GLOSSARY.md` "GC on jobs" + a new "Bills go to" entry; `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` notes the columns ride the existing policies; `BILLING_FLOWS.md` → the three channels.

## Not changed (deliberately, this PR)

- GC Review still groups every job by `gc_customer_id` regardless of the rule; the customer portal still lists a GC's jobs' bills as owed; the terms bar and reliability line still key on `gcCustomerId || customerId`. All three follow the rule in PR 2.
- The 72 jobs entered with the GC as the customer keep working exactly as before (the GC option is not offered when the GC *is* the customer row). PR 4 sweeps them.
- Creating a job from a won bid still copies the GC into both the customer and the GC field (v2.1182 rule); with the rule field present that import can become "customer = site owner, Bills go to = GC" once PR 4 has restored site owners.
- `split` in PR 1 means "each draft picks"; the line-level Pays tag and the per-payer carve are PR 3.
