# v2.7631 — Prospects: the lanes' reads of the bench and the Project Board are their own pieces (2026-11-17)

Step 8 of the order in `docs/PROSPECTS_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md`: hook seams, no JSX moved. No behaviour changes.

**Three hooks in `src/components/prospects/`**, their code cut out of `src/pages/Prospects.tsx`:

- `useSubsBench(kind, newKind, userId)` is the bench's read for the Subs lane: five pieces of state and the effect that fills them. It still reads when the lane or New Prospect's kind is Subs, and again whenever either kind or the user changes, because it keeps the page's three dependencies as they were. A refused read still leaves `benchTrades` null, so the lane draws without its strip.
- `useProspectDoubles({ kind, newKind, newProspectModalOpen, userId })` holds two fail-soft reads that share a piece of state: the prospects of every lane and the customers when New Prospect opens, and the Project Board's counts, its bidding lines and the customers again on the Jobs to GC lane. `customerDoubles` keeps its two writers (the map's quirk 31), in the order the page ran them.
- `useProspectJobs({ userId, currentProspectId })` holds a lead's jobs: `mergeJobs`, which both of the page's loaders call, and the five writes of the card's Jobs box. The status, lost and remove writes still patch the cache under the deck's card (the map's quirk 30).

**One kernel.** `benchReadFromBoard(rows, projects, today)` in `src/lib/prospects/benchRead.ts` is what the bench effect did between its two awaits and its five setters: the dependable counts, the companies that have quoted, the projects a set of plans could be for, the bench with our own trades, and the companies for the double check. This is the map's Stage-A row 9.

**Two state lines moved up.** New Prospect's open flag and its kind are declared above the lane reads now, since two of the hooks take them. The order of state in a component changes nothing a caller sees.

**Size.** `Prospects.tsx` went from 4,075 lines to 3,928: ten pieces of state, three effects and six functions fewer.

**Proof.** The render smoke grew from 79 cases to 83: Add a job, Drop and Remove, Lost with the builder that won, and New Prospect naming a company already on a lane with *Save anyway* and *Open it*. All 83 were run against the page before the move and after it. 5 unit tests on the kernel: an empty board, a trade's bench through the board's own rule, the vetted flag's three values, a project out for a bid with the trades it buys out, and the projects and trades it leaves out.
