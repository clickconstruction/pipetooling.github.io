# v2.6287 — Where files go: the tree under the customer, and Tidy the folders (2026-11-01)

The tab's half of the owner's rule (v2.6284 is the server's): the office sees the tree the app now keeps and can walk the back catalogue into it in one press.

- **The tree card** (`TreeCard` in `DocumentsWhereFilesGo.tsx`) draws the jobs root (its live name), the customer's folder (the example job's customer, or *No customer yet*), the job's folder and the five inside; `UNDER_CUSTOMER_WORDS` under it. `APPLIES_FROM_NOW_WORDS` now says a name change reaches every live job's folder the next time the sweep looks, since the sweep renames.
- **Tidy the folders for N live jobs** (`tidyWords`, `countedLabel`, dead at zero): the primary press under the cards walks every live job through `job-drive-folders`' `sweep` verb with `all` and `offset`, a page of 25 at a time (`tidyDriveFolders`, `tidyNextOffset`), tallying as it goes (`tidyTotals`, `tidyProgressWords`: *Tidying… 75 of 412 · 3 made · 12 moved · 2 held*), stopping at a page that fails with its reason and keeping what the pages before it did; the toast and the line are the sweep's own words over the whole walk (`tidyDoneWords`). The jobs reload after, so the rows carry their new links and holds.
- **The held line** (`treeHeldLineWords`, `heldJobs`): *Held: 3 jobs whose folder is not in the tree ›*, from `jobs_ledger.drive_folder_hold` on the rows before any run, and from the walk's own held list once it ran; each row names the job, its reason and **Open the job**. `JobRows` takes a held job by its id and label too, as the sweep's answer names one.
- `tidySweepFromAnswer` reads the verb's JSON (counts, held, line, `next_offset`, `total`), never throws. The jobs read (`loadWhereFilesGoJobs`) carries `drive_folder_hold` and `drive_folder_tidied_at`, so this PR merges after migration `20261101163412` is on prod.
