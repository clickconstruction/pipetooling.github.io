# v2.5329 — GC mode: a statement of work built from clauses is signed as its clauses (2026-10-12)

Cluster 9, PR 1 of the paper pickers' pair (GC Reviews helper 9, directed by the lead session GC Review): the trade portal reads and signs a statement of work built from clauses. The database side is #5481 (`20261013000000_gc_clause_papers`, v2.5309): `gc_sows.paper_id`, and `gc_trade_sign_sow`'s `p_paper_revision` and `p_paper_lang`. That migration is on prod since 2026-10-12, which this read needs, since it selects `paper_id`. No screen sets a paper yet; PR 2 adds the office's picker. Until then every statement of work reads and signs as before.

- **The read** (`gc-trade-portal`): `gc_sows` adds `paper_id, paper_revision, clause_versions`. Each statement of work sent to sign with a paper gets `clause_paper: { revision, en, es }` (`withSowPapers`), through the new `clausePaperPair` in `_shared/clausePaper.ts`. That is `contract_paper_clauses()` for the package's one trade, with the between line naming the company, both languages at one revision: what `gc_trade_sign_sow` freezes. A paper that fails to read leaves that statement of work with no clauses, and the SQL refuses its signing until a reload reads them. `TRADE_PORTAL_FIELDS.sows` passes `clause_paper`, `paper_revision` and `clause_versions`.
- **The shared read**: `_shared/clausePaper.ts` gains `clausePaperOf` (a paper for any target: its library document, its trades and who the between line names), `clausePaperPair` and `readClausePaperPair`. `clausePaperFor` keeps its reads, their order and its answer, now through the same two helpers. `customer-portal` can read our contract's paper through the same `clausePaperPair`, helper 8's next piece.
- **The map** (`tradePortalState.sowOf`): `Sow.clausePaper` on a sent one and `Sow.signedClauses` on a signed one, each read back strictly. `types.ts` takes the two types from `_shared/clausePaper.ts`, and the kernel copy is regenerated (`types.ts`, `portal.ts`, `portalI18n.ts`).
- **The page** (`GcTradePortalSow.tsx`): a sent one built from clauses shows `ClausePaper` first, with the price, the lines and what they will and will not do folded under **The price and the lines**. **Sign the statement of work** sends `paperRevision` and `paperLang`. A signed one shows its version line under its day.
- **The sign** (`_shared/gcTradeSubmit.ts`): `sign_sow` passes `p_paper_revision` and `p_paper_lang` only when the page sent a revision, a whole number. Without one the SQL's defaults sign a statement of work whose lines are the paper as before, and refuse one built from clauses. `paperChanged` maps to 409 and reads *The paper changed since you opened it. Reload to read the new version.* in English and Spanish (`errPaperChanged`).
- **Your papers** (`portalPapers`): a signed one built from clauses reads *signed Sep 5 · version 3*.
- **The between line** (`ClausePaper`): a name that ends in its own period, like *Sample Electric Co.*, takes no second one, on every page that draws a paper.
- **Guides**: `share-a-trade-partner-its-portal` (what the company reads and signs) and `award-a-trade-on-a-gc-project` (the signed copy keeps the clauses and the version).
- **Tests**: `clausePaperEdge.test.ts` (`clausePaperOf`, `clausePaperPair`, `readClausePaperPair`), `tradePortalSlice.test.ts`, `tradePortalState.test.ts`, `gcTradeSubmit.test.ts` (the revision and the language, a bad revision, `paperChanged`), `portalJob.direct.test.ts` (the version on Your papers), and the smokes `GcTradePortal.sow.render.test.tsx` and `GcTradePortal.papers.render.test.tsx`.
- **Deploys**, after this merges (the lead's): `gc-trade-portal`, `submit-gc-trade-portal` and `customer-portal` (the kernel copy), then `get-contract-for-signer` and `accept-contract`, whose shared file changed with no change in what they do. `npm run check:edge-drift` should then read clean.
