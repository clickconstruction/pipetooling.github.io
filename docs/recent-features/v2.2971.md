# v2.2971 — Test-gap audit, pass 4: the dispatch add-block timeline and its three sibling kernels get their first tests (2026-09-06)

Follow-up to v2.2969, which named the dispatch add-block timeline next. Tests only — no kernel, client, migration or edge-function change.

## Why these four

`scheduleDispatchAddBlockTimeline.ts` (393 lines) is the gap-and-drag geometry under the Add-block slider on the Schedule hub, the job-week grid, Quickfill's schedule rows and the user-review day view — eight importers. `dispatchAddBlockTime.ts` holds the 4 AM–8 PM grid, its 30-minute slots and the minimum-duration clamps the slider thumbs obey. `scheduleDispatchExpectedManpower.ts` rolls schedule blocks into the hub's Expected Manpower person-hours and payroll estimate. `clockSessionsToDispatchSecondaryBands.ts` paints clock sessions under the dispatch strip. None had a colocated test. (`scheduleDispatchMirrorInsert.ts` is a Supabase write path and stays untested by design.)

## What shipped

- **`scheduleDispatchAddBlockTimeline.test.ts`** (29): effective ranges with drafts; occupied-interval merging and the free gaps inside the day; the default new block (8 AM–4 PM on an empty day, the first gap that fits otherwise, null when nothing does); `clampNewBlockRangeToGaps` (inside a gap unchanged, 30-minute floor, pulled back into the gap holding the midpoint, nearest gap when the midpoint is occupied, fills a short gap, clamps to the day with no gaps); `endDragRangeAcrossGaps` — the end follows the pointer inside its gap, pins at an occupied band's start, **hops** into the next gap once the pointer clears its start by the minimum, hops backward to a minimum block ending at the pointer, and moves the start only when the minimum forces it; `applySegmentDragDelta` — zero delta returns the same object, HH:MM drafts, a collision backs off in 5-minute steps to the last clear slot, null when nothing is possible, day clamps, drafts as the starting point, sub-minimum and unknown seeds refused, linked legs on the same job move together while another job in the group stays put; the absolute-start wrapper; draft patching for overlap checks.
- **`dispatchAddBlockTime.test.ts`** (10): the grid constants (240–1200, 33 slots, 8 AM–4 PM preference), slot ↔ minute conversions with rounding and clamping, time-string helpers, the two minimum-duration clamps at both grid edges, 12-hour quick labels, duration labels and aria text.
- **`scheduleDispatchExpectedManpower.test.ts`** (8): block hours (never negative), week and day-key totals, one-decimal formatting, rows for a day (case-insensitive title sort, bid anchors resolve), multi-day rows, job groups (biggest first, distinct people), payroll estimate skipping unknown rates.
- **`clockSessionsToDispatchSecondaryBands.test.ts`** (7): association label fallback; a closed session's slots and label in company time; an open session on a past day runs to the grid's end and says so; an open session today runs to now; sessions outside 4 AM–8 PM are clipped or dropped; a sub-slot session is widened so it stays visible.

All 54 passed on the first run.
