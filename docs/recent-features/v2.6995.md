# v2.6995 — First look: the thank-you names a closing time only when it is a real one (2026-11-12)

Punch list #319, the spike (v2.6981). The live walk's thank-you at 2 AM read *Robert looks at them today, before 11:59 PM.* The office hours in Settings run to 23:59, which is around the clock said in hours, and *before 11:59 PM* is no promise.

- `firstLookClosesWords` in `supabase/functions/_shared/firstLook.ts` names the closing time unless the office answers around the clock or its day runs into the last half hour before midnight. Then the line reads *Robert looks at them today.*
- `first-look`'s `officeNow` uses it. Tested in `src/lib/firstLook/look.test.ts`.

No migration. **Redeploy** `first-look` after the merge. The page's words come from the function, so no site deploy is needed for the change to show.
