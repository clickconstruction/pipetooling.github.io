# v2.5037 — GC mode: the presses behind the submittal register (2026-10-09)

U4a of the Building lane's real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/building-u4.md`, on branch `spike/gc-mode`): the submittal register's presses, in `supabase/migrations/20261010005000_gc_submittal_writes.sql` (`docs/migrations/20261010005000_gc_submittal_writes.md`). No client code: the Submittals window (U4b) calls them once the types PR after the push has them.

- **`gc_add_submittal(s jsonb)`**: a submittal on an awarded trade, numbered by its spec section (`03 21 00-02`) or plainly (`003`) as the prototype's `nextSubmittalNumber` does, with the trade's scope lines it holds. One press at a time on a job takes a number, and a number a removed submittal left is skipped.
- **`gc_submittal_came_in(r jsonb)`**: the office records a round that came by email, with the file's name and Drive link, while it is the trade's move.
- **`gc_send_submittal_to_architect(id, email)`**: the newest round went to the architect today, with the email `gc-architect-email` sends in U4b, or none when it went another way.
- **`gc_answer_submittal(id, answer, note)`**: approved, approved as noted, or revise with what to change, which makes it the trade's move again.
- **`gc_trade_submittal_send(company, id, file, link, note)`**: the trade's round from its portal, the service role's only. It refuses with keys as the Portal's P2a verbs do (`notFound`, `notYours`, `notYourMove`, `fileNeeded`). The Portal's P5 adds the kind and the two new keys' words.
- **`gc_submittal_move(id)`**: whose move a submittal is, the kernel's `submittalState` in SQL, which the five read.
- **They refuse**, in words: a training account and a digital twin; our own crew's trade; a trade not awarded (`gc_trade_packages.awarded_invite_id`, the Board's B6-a); a blank title; a kind the register does not know; a hold on another trade's work; a round, a send or an answer out of turn; and revise with nothing to change. All 27 sentences pass `plainWordsFailures`.
- **The promise**: the last submittal a trade owed keeps its promise to send them (`gc_keep_promises`, decision 9), whether the trade sent it or the office recorded it.
- **The SQL bed**: `scripts/pgtest-gc-building.sh` now re-applies each of Building's presses and plays every scenario in `supabase/tests/gc_building`, so this PR's `40_submittals.sql` runs beside the daily log's. It adds three submittals and walks one through a revise, sends two rounds as the trade's company, keeps the promise on the last one owed, skips a removed number, and refuses each case above, a trainee, a twin, an estimator and a signed-in caller of the trade's verb.
