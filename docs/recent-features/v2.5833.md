# v2.5833 — The Bridge: a bill marked billed by hand counts in “the office bills” (2026-10-24)

A fix to the weakest-link cards ([v2.5232](v2.5232.md) … [v2.5244](v2.5244.md)), found while answering the owner's question about the Bill card on 2026-10-24: *are some of the 15 "finished, not yet billed" jobs commercial jobs billed through the GC's portal and not recorded here?* They were recorded — as bills marked billed by hand — and that is exactly what the card's pace could not see.

## What was wrong

`loadBridgeData.ts` summed "the office bills $X a week" (and the net position history's "invoices sent" flow) from `jobs_ledger_invoices` rows with a `sent_to_customer_at` inside the window. A bill the office marks billed without sending it through the app — a pay application on a GC's portal (Structura, TF Harper, RMC), a paper invoice, a HousecallPro send — has `billed_at` and no send stamp, so it never counted. Read on prod on 2026-10-24, window Aug 29 – Oct 24: **$393,304** of bills carried a `billed_at` in the window and only **$188,651** a send date; **$265,528** (48 bills) was invisible, among them the window's three largest GC bills ($103,386, $29,323 and $8,780, all on jobs in the Bill pile). The card read $23.6k a week and 11.0 weeks to clear; by the billed day the office billed **$49.2k a week**, about 5.3 weeks on the same pile, and Collect, not Bill, is the longer stage.

## The rule

`src/lib/bridge/billedDay.ts` — `invoiceBilledYmd(row)`: the company day the bill went out, else the day it was marked billed, null for a draft. The loader reads `sent_to_customer_at` **or** `billed_at` on or after the day before the window (a UTC stamp's company day can fall a day earlier) and keeps the test-mode exclusion. Paid bills keep their `billed_at` since [v2.2236](v2.2236.md) (`20260824171151_keep_billed_at_on_paid.sql`), so a hand-marked bill that was later paid still counts on the day it was marked.

The map keeps its name (`invoicesSentByDay`), so everything that reads it moves together: the Bill stage's out-rate and Collect's in-rate (`stageFlow`), the dial's "billed a week", the net position history (`netPosition.ts`, whose AR(today) comes from bill truth and already held the hand-marked bills — the walk back now subtracts them on the right day) and the Truth check's "earned but not invoiced".

## Tests

`src/lib/bridge/billedDay.test.ts`: the send day wins when both are set, the billed day stands alone, a draft is null.

## Seen live

On a worktree Vite against prod as the dev, 2026-10-24, the same minute as the read above: "the office bills" went from $23.6k to **$56.8k a week** (the union of bills sent in the window and bills marked billed in it, $454k), Bill from 11.0 to **4.6 weeks** and the badge *the weakest link, with Collect* to *wait*; Collect holds the link alone at 10.2 weeks. The net position line, which had read *down $66.2k since Aug 29*, reads *up $199k*: the walk back now takes the hand-marked bills off AR on the days they were marked, so the history agrees with the paper profit (+$170k) it sits beside.
