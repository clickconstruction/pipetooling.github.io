# v2.7337 — The run’s checklist holds a notice whose job was paid after it printed (2026-11-15)

Client only: no migration, no edge function. Taunya picked this on 2026-11-15 from a look at the run on the day's data: seven notices printed Oct 8 and 9, five of them on jobs paid since. Their envelope cards read *Held · Nothing to send: the claim is $0* (`runEnvelopeHold`, v2.4971), while the checklist (v2.7328) read the same five as *printed · not signed*, counted them in *7 to sign* and offered **Mark 7 signed**, one pen record over all seven.

## What changed

- `src/lib/jobs/lienRunChecklist.ts` (+ test): `lienRunNothingToClaim(notice)` is the envelope's rule read per notice — the run's `amount` is the job's open balance today, so at $0 the row is held with *Nothing to claim: the job owes $0 now.* and, once it printed with a claim, *The paper says $2,400.* (the draft's `claimAmount`). `LienRunRow.nothingToClaim` carries it; the hold comes before an address hold, since an address can be fixed. `lienRunBatchPlans` holds such a row from all three presses with that reason, and it never picks the batch's signer (a paid row first in the run would have made its leader the one *Mark N signed* names, and held every other row for the next press). `lienRunFootWords` already left a held row out of its counts; with nothing but paid rows left its second line reads *Nothing is owed on these jobs now, so nothing here goes out.* in place of *Fix what is marked in red, then print.*
- `src/components/jobs/LienRunChecklist.tsx`: on a row with nothing to claim the Signed tick reads *nothing to sign* (no press), the Mailed boxes are disabled with *nothing to mail*, and the phone's **Scan ›** is not drawn beside them. The ticks already in stay as facts (*✓ Oct 9 · Taunya*).
- Guide *send lien notices from the Lien desk*: a paragraph under the checklist's three ticks.

## Not in this PR

- The desk's pill still counts the paid notices (*The run · 0 of 7 mailed*), and **Take back…** still takes every printed notice at once. A held row's own way out of the run is the next PR.
- A notice that was mailed and then paid cannot be recorded from the checklist while its job owes $0. The envelope cards never took a number for one; whether the row should offer *it went out, record it as it went* is Taunya's call with the next PR.
