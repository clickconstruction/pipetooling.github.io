# v2.6228 — Messages: a hand-off pings the person, and a thread can be sent as a task (2026-10-31)

From the bids-inbox sorting review of 2026-10-31 (punch list #223's follow-up): the owner asked for folders at the top of New that drop an email on a person's to-do list with a notification. The app's answer is the presses it has, *Who has it?* and the Checklist, with the two things they lacked: handing a thread on moved it into a teammate's Mine and told nobody, and an email that is a chore rather than a conversation had no door to the Checklist.

- **The ping** (`messages-notify`, a fourth body `{ handed: { contact_id } }`): `followUpIo.handTo` calls it as the user right after `hand_to`, fail-soft and not awaited, never for a put-back. The function refuses unless the thread's `has_how` is `handed` to someone other than the caller within 15 minutes (`handedFresh`) and the caller wrote an event line on it in that time, the hand's own; it builds the words itself (`src/lib/messages/handedPush.ts`, mirrored: *Rosa handed you Dale Ferris*, the newest line in clipped to 140 characters, else *Open the thread.*, the thread at the line, tag `handed:<contact>`), keeps the person's push hours (`pushDecision` over `pushHoursFor`, now exported from `messagesReplyPush.ts`; held, a `notification_history` row with `channel = 'held'` and no push, so the Dashboard's *While you were off* can list it), sends through `pushToPerson`, the reply push's sender factored out, and records one row as `message_handed`. A hand to oneself (a take) is never pinged.
- **Send as task** (`useFollowUpThreadSlots.tsx`'s `menuRows`, for any thread while the Add-task provider is mounted): `messages/threadTask.ts`'s `threadTaskPreset` puts the thread on the dialog's bar the way a job goes (`checklistJobPreset.ts`, v2.3751): the number is the line's channel word (*Email*, *Text*, *Thread* with no line), the name the person and the line's words clipped to 80, the company under it, link [1] the thread at the newest line in. The stored title is `{{1:Email · Dale Ferris · W-9 needed}} — <typed>`, so every list that reads a task title draws the chip unchanged.
- **Guides**: *give someone a task* gains **Send a message thread as a task**; *sort new messages before anyone answers them* says the person handed a conversation hears of it. `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` → messages-notify, `docs/MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md`.
- **Tests**: `handedPush.test.ts`, `threadTask.test.ts`, `followUpIo.test.ts` (the call after a hand, none for a put-back, a failed push changes nothing, a client with no functions door), `edgeKernels.test.ts`.

Deploy: `supabase functions deploy messages-notify` after the merge. No migration.
