# v2.6264 — The ground under one list of every photo uploaded (punch list #232, PR 1) (2026-11-01)

Punch list #232's first PR, on the owner's go of 2026-11-01 (*build it all, testing along the way*) with the card's five calls taken as recommended. The owner's ask: *a view that shows me all the photos that have been uploaded, sorted by most recent to least recent and where they are saved and who uploaded the photo*. This PR is the view and the kernel; PR 2 is the Documents page's Photos tab; PR 3 the job window's line.

## What changed

- **The migration** `20261101084330_uploaded_photos_view.sql` (`docs/migrations/20261101084330_uploaded_photos_view.md`): the `uploaded_photos` view, `security_invoker`, four branches in one shape: a saved report's photo (`report_photos`), a box photo (`job_box_photos`), a checklist sheet's own photo (`stage_checklist_answers`, never an answer that names a report photo, which is the same shot), and a picture on a text or an email (`message_attachments` with its line). Each row carries the source, the job (or the bid or the GC project for a line), what it came with, who took it, when, the sizes, where it waits and where it landed or why not, and the job's number, name and customer for the server-side search. Every table's own policy decides what the caller sees, and the inner join on `jobs_ledger` makes a primary's and a superintendent's list follow the ledger's scope, which the report photos' own gate (`can_report_stage_progress`, which names them on every job) does not.
- **The kernel** `src/lib/photos/uploadedPhotos.ts` (tested): the row shape and the select, the chips a role sees (Texts & email for the office and the estimator), the search as PostgREST's `or` over the caption, the person, the job, the customer and the stage, the row's words (the source, who took it, the job line, the caption, the sizes), the Drive line as a state with its press (*In Drive*, *On its way to Drive*, *Not in Drive yet* with the sweep's reason and the folders press, *Still on Jordan's phone*, *Shared from the phone*, *Our copy, not in Drive*, *No copy here · still in Gmail*), the day groups (*Today · Sat, Nov 1*, *Yesterday*, the day), and where a thumbnail's bytes come from (the bucket while a shot waits, `job-photos` on the Drive id once filed, our kept copy for a text's picture).
- **The io** `src/lib/photos/uploadedPhotosIo.ts`: the read newest first by a hundred with the chips and the search asked of the server, the waiting count for the chip, and the thumbnail and full-size reads over the two paths. Nothing on screen reads it yet.

## Decisions

- **One view over four tables, not a table of its own.** No copy to keep true, no fences, no sweep; a photo shows the moment its row exists. The union sorts each branch, which is fine at the hundreds of rows the tables hold; an index per branch's time already exists.
- **A checklist photo that is a report photo is listed once**, under the report, since v2.6219 made the answer point at the report's row.
- **A text's picture on no job is listed too**, as *No job tied*, with the thread as its door: a customer's photo of a leak before the office ties the line is still a photo uploaded. The card said tied lines only; the list is more useful with them.
- **The message branch's job is the Pipeline job, else the GC project's billing job, else the bid**, by name and customer, so the row reads the same whatever the line was tied to.
