# v2.4912 — GC Review: Find a check works on a development too (2026-10-07)

Client only. Punch list #55, piece 2 (`to-dos/where-the-checks-went-residuals.md`). Find a check and its sheet (v2.4046, v2.4050) were offered only when GC Review was grouped **By GC** (`!byDevelopment && g.gcId`), because the read went by `gc_customer_id` / `customer_id`. The owner's call on the mock-up was "follow the switch".

## The change

- **IO** (`gcChecksAppliedIo.ts`): `fetchDevelopmentChecksInputs(developmentId)` reads the jobs by `jobs_ledger.development_id`. The rest of the read is shared with `fetchGcChecksInputs`: the move ledger and the deposits.
- **Kernel** (`_shared/gcChecksApplied.ts`): `buildGcChecksReport({ gcId: null })` counts every bill on the jobs. The payer rule still decides who pays a bill. Under a development it no longer decides whether the bill is on the sheet. `GcChecksReport.gcId` is `string | null`. The GC lanes (the modal, the statement email, the portal) pass a GC as before.
- **Window** (`GcFindCheckModal`, `byDevelopment`): reads the development's jobs and words the reading and the empty states for its jobs. Print the sheet files the copy under the development's jobs (`jobIds`) rather than a customer.
- **Menu** (`JobsGcReviewModal`): *Find a check…* on every named group's Share menu. Under By development the group's id is the development's, as the rollup already holds it.
