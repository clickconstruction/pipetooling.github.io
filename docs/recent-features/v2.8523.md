# v2.8523 — Job window: the Lien window opened from Documents names the Lien desk’s step (2026-11-24)

Punch list #335, *Also seen* (`to-dos/lien-row-and-window-still-part.md`): the Lien window opened from a job's Documents tab has no desk queue to read, so its header keeps *Draft the notice* at every step. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## The cause

The Lien window builds its path from the job's filings. It cannot see a notice that is drafted, sent for approval or printed, or an affidavit that is not filed yet. An opener that holds the Lien desk's lists hands it the job's two entries (`deskNotice`, v2.7077, and `deskAffidavit`, v2.7187) and the leader's name (`deskLeader`, v2.8475), and the header then names the desk's step. Two of the window's three openers did that: the Pipeline (`JobsStagesTab.tsx`) and the Lien desk page (`LienDeskPage.tsx`). The third, the job window's Documents tab (`JobDocumentsLienPaper.tsx`), held no desk and handed nothing. It also gave the window no door to the desk, so a step that is worked on the desk had no button at all.

Reproduced before the change on a made-up job (`JobDocumentsLienPaper.render.test.tsx`): with a notice sent for approval and an affidavit to draft on a made-up desk, the window was handed neither.

## What changed

- **One pick, pure.** `lienWindowDeskEntryFor(desk, jobId)` in `src/lib/jobs/lienWindowDeskEntry.ts` returns the job's two entries from the desk's lists, through the same two readers the other openers call (`lienWindowDeskNotice`, `lienWindowDeskAffidavit`). Nulls with no desk, no job, or a job on neither list.
- **A read for one job's window.** `useJobLienDeskEntry(job, open)` in `src/hooks/useJobLienDeskEntry.ts` reads the desk in the light form the Pipeline reads (`useLienDeskData`, `light: true`), only while the Lien window is open, and only for the office roles that may read the desk (`isStagesOfficeRole`). Everyone else, and a closed window, read nothing and get nulls, so the header reads as it did. The Documents tab alone costs no read.
- **The leader's name.** `readLienLeaderName` (`src/lib/jobs/lienLeaderNameIo.ts`) reads the one name for the job's leader, the name the desk's rows print. A failed read is blank and the header reads *the leader*.
- **The door.** The header's button for a step worked on the desk closes the Lien window, closes the job window (`onLeave`, the Plans section's own way out) and opens the desk's page on the job (`lienWindowDeskDoorHref`, v2.8478).
- **It stays current.** A record made in the window, or a last day of work set there, reads the desk again.
- **Not changed:** the Pipeline's and the Lien desk page's own openers, the window itself, and every word the header can say.

## Tests

- `lienWindowDeskEntry.test.ts`: the pick is the job's own entries and never a neighbour's; it is the other openers' pick, entry for entry; nothing is handed over with no desk, no job, or a job on neither list.
- `JobDocumentsLienPaper.render.test.tsx` (new): the window is handed the job's notice, its affidavit, its leader and a door; the desk is read only while the window is open, in the light form; a job on neither list and a role that may not read the desk read as before; the door closes both windows and opens the desk on the job; a record reads the desk again. All five failed on `main`.
- `JobWindowDocumentsTab.render.test.tsx` stands in for the new read.
- Not seen: the window on the live site, until this is deployed.
