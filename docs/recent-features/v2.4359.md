# v2.4359 — Pipeline: the bill email window and a card's notes no longer fold the row (2026-10-01)

No migration, no edge function. The two Pipeline cases [v2.4356](v2.4356.md) found but left, and the gap in `check-nested-windows.mjs --rows` that hid them.

## Why it happened

A Pipeline row opens or shuts the job's thread on any click that is not on a button, link or field: [`shouldSuppressStagesRowJobThreadToggle`](../../src/components/jobs/jobsStagesRowShared.tsx) asks `closest()` in the DOM. Two things drawn inside the row reach that handler through React's tree while sitting outside the row in the DOM, or with no button above them:

- The Send from Stripe confirm, *Email this invoice?* ([`StripeInvoiceSendFromStripeButton`](../../src/components/jobs/StripeInvoiceSendFromStripeButton.tsx)), is a portal on `<body>`, so `closest()` finds no button above its words. Its panel stopped only `mousedown`. A click on its text toggled the thread behind it; since [v2.4347](v2.4347.md) moved its backdrop to `onClick`, a click outside it closed it and toggled the thread too. Drawn by the desktop table rows, the unified table's job and bill rows, and the zoned cards; also by the Payment chase window (under a `preventDefault` span), Bill Customer, View bill and the billed-by-customer breakdown.
- The zoned card ([`JobsStagesCardList`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsStagesCardList.tsx), `renderCardThreadPanel`) draws the open thread inside itself. A tap on the thread's label, its filter strip's gaps or its padding folded the card; in full screen (`FULLSCREEN_SHELL`, a portal) a tap anywhere but a button closed full screen and folded the card. The desktop table draws its thread in its own `<tr>` with no click handler, so it never did this.

## What changed

- The confirm's backdrop stops its click before its target check (`e.stopPropagation(); if (e.target === e.currentTarget) setConfirmOpen(false)`), and its panel stops `click` as well as `mousedown`. The fix is in the component, so it holds in every place above.
- The zoned card's thread wrapper stops the click, inline and full screen. **Owner call (Grace, 2026-10-01):** a tap inside the open thread does nothing to the card; the card above the thread and its ▸ chevron still fold it, as on the desktop table. The [search the Pipeline board](../../src/content/help/search-the-stages-board.md) guide says so.
- [`scripts/check-nested-windows.mjs`](../../scripts/check-nested-windows.mjs) `--rows`: a row is any element whose handler does more than stop or prevent the event (`isInert`: every call in it is `stopPropagation`, `stopImmediatePropagation` or `preventDefault`, in every branch, so `paneMode ? undefined : (e) => e.stopPropagation()` and the Payment chase span are skipped). It used to need a child within two levels that stops the click (`hasStopperChild`, gone), which missed the Pipeline's `<tr>` rows and cards. On the code before this fix it lists three rows (the desktop table row, both card kinds) and nothing else; with the fix, none. The backdrop count is unchanged (441 on main).

## Known limits

- The zoned cards draw only above 640 px with Mobile cards on (an iPad, or a wide window with the ⋯ toggle). On a phone the board draws compact rows ([`StagesPhoneRow`](../../src/components/jobs/StagesPhoneRow.tsx)) that open the job window instead of a thread.
- The unified table's rows get their render helpers through the `t` context object, which the check cannot follow; they draw the same confirm, fixed in the component. A window drawn there through `t` would not be listed.
- A click inside the thread or the confirm now stops at React's root, so `document` listeners do not hear it: an open Pipeline ☰ or section ⋯ menu ([`useCloseOnOutsideClick`](../../src/hooks/useCloseOnOutsideClick.ts)) stays open on a tap there, as it already did on the row's chips and chevron.

## Tests

[`nestedWindows.render.test.tsx`](../../src/components/nestedWindows.render.test.tsx) — five more cases. The Stripe confirm in a stand-in row that uses the row's own rule: a click on its words, a click outside. The real zoned card ([`makeStagesCardListProps`](../../src/test/stagesCardListProps.tsx), moved out of the card list's own render test): a tap in the inline thread, a tap in the full-screen thread, and a tap on the card above the thread, which still folds it. With the two component changes reverse-applied, the four fix cases fail (the row or card toggles once) and the fold case passes.

## Walked live

Local dev server against prod; nothing sent or saved; the confirm was opened from ZZ TEST job 1054 (owner zz-test-owner@example.com) and closed each time. Desktop, Billed Awaiting Payment, unified bill row: a click on *Most recent sends* left the confirm open and the row shut, and a click outside closed the confirm alone; neither click reached the page. With the fix hot-reloaded out, the first click opened the job's thread behind the confirm and the second closed the confirm and shut it again. iPad width (768 px) with Mobile cards on: taps on the thread's label and filter gap, and on *esc to close* in full screen, left the thread open; a tap on the card above folded it. With the fix out, the label tap folded the card and the full-screen tap left full screen and folded it. The card's own confirm, opened with the thread showing, closed alone.
