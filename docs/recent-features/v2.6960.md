# v2.6960 — Mark lost from a permit news line (punch list #307, PR 5's leftover) (2026-11-11)

The mock-up drew two presses on the plumbing news line, and v2.6870 built one. A general plumbing permit that lists another name on a sent bid's address is the answer nobody phoned in, so the line now offers the outcome where the person reads the news.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/research/bidPermitNews.ts`**: a news item carries `markLost`. It is true on a `plumbing` line whose bid has been sent, and false on every other line: an unsent bid has no offer out to lose, and a building permit issued or applied for is the day to call. `MARK_LOST_LABEL` holds the press's words.
- **`BidBoardPermitNews`**: a line with `markLost` draws **Mark lost…** after **Open the bid** when the board passes `renderMarkLost`. The press opens the window under its line, one at a time, and folds it on a second press. When the bid is decided its line leaves and the window goes with it. Found in passing and fixed: the press's style wrote `font: inherit` last, which undid its size and weight, so **Open the bid** drew at 16px regular where the mock-up has 13px semibold.
- **`BidBoardNewsMarkLost`** (new): the window, which is the outcome the board already has and no new write. A bid whose row lists its GCs (`gcRowsWorthShowing`) gets the row's own `BidBoardGcLines` under *Lost with which GC?*, so the pick runs `setGcPacketOutcome` with the board's path and the bid rolls up. Any other bid gets `BidLostQuickPopover`, the note and the six reasons, which writes the bid.
- **`BidsBidBoardTab`**: passes the window to the strip with the row's own packets, note counts, room states and job link.
- Guide `see-what-the-city-has-on-a-bids-address` (See permit news on the Bid Board), `docs/BIDS_BOARD_FORM_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/BIDS_SYSTEM.md`, `docs/PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`, `docs/twins/APP_DIRECTORY.md`.
