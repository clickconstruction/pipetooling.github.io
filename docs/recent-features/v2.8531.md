## What changed

- **A stray pick is not kept.** Picking a person made a draft at once, so leaving the sheet with nothing filled in left *1 not finished* on the press until the tab was closed, and no press cleared it.
  - `writeUpDraftStarted` in [`src/lib/people/writeUp.ts`](../../src/lib/people/writeUp.ts) says whether the writer put anything in: a kind, words, a photo, a witness, an answer about talking, or that they were told. The day, the time and the job are filled in for them and do not count. An addition carries its parent's kind, so the kind alone does not count there.
  - `WriteUpSheet` drops a draft that was not begun on **Close**, on a press outside the sheet, on **Done** and on **Back** to the list. A write-up that was begun is kept as before.
- **The Add Task strip reads in dark.** On a phone the strip's words were a literal brown, `#7c2d12`, on a tint that turns dark at night: about 1.5 to 1. They take `var(--text-orange-800)` now, the token its **Dismiss** already used: 8.5 to 1 in dark and 6.9 to 1 in light. [`src/components/AddTaskShortcutBanner.tsx`](../../src/components/AddTaskShortcutBanner.tsx).
- **Words**: the guide *write someone up from my dashboard* and `docs/GLOSSARY.md` say a pick alone is not kept.
