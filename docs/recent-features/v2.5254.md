# v2.5254 — Messages: the facts that finish each step of a flow (2026-10-12)

Messages wave 1, cluster 4, first of two PRs: the types the card, the nudges and the boards (helpers 5 and 6) build against. Kernels only: no screen, no table, no migration, no edge function.

- **`src/lib/flows/facts.ts`**: the code list a flow step picks its facts from (`FLOW_FACTS`, 30 facts), each with its words, its group, the column it reads, its scope (`person`, `job` or `round`), how well main keeps it (`kept`) and what cut 4's SQL adds where it does not (`gap`); the 17 papers with their four words (`FLOW_PAPERS`); `factById`, `isFactId`, `isPaperId`, `factForPaper`, `factGroups`, `factsWords`.
- **`src/lib/flows/state.ts`**: the flow's shape (`FlowDef`, `FlowLaneDef`, `FlowStep`, lanes `once`, `per_job`, `any_time`), one person's stamps in (`FlowStamps`, `FactStamp`, `RoundStamp`, `StepSkip`, `StepWait`, `OffFlow`), the reading out (`FlowReading`, `LaneReading`, `StepReading`, `StepPlace`, `RoundReading`, `StepDay`, `PaperReading`, `PerJobRow`, `FlowMove`, `WhoMoves`), and the reads: `flat`, `stepById`, `laneOf`, `stampDay`, `factRead`, `stepTrue`, `stepDay`, `quietDays`, `lapsedWhy`, `stepReading`, `stepState`, `placesOf`, `paperRead`, `perJobJobs`, `flowProgress`, `wouldBeNow`, `whoMoves`, `flowMoves`, `moveWords`. `readFlow` throws until the second PR builds it.
- **`src/lib/flows/seed.ts`**: `SEED_FLOWS`, the four flows as the prototype v1.178 draws them, the source of cut 4's SQL seed.
- **Changed from the prototype**: state is a read and never stored, so a non-paper fact comes in as a stamp instead of being inferred from the steps done; every read about one job takes the job's id (no `laneJob`); `stepDay` returns the due day and `lateDays` as numbers beside its words, in Chicago days; `quietDays` counts their last word only; `dec` is `fork`; new step fields `phase` (the boards' tiles), `loopEnds` (`rounds` or `next`), `skipOneJob` (the customer's master agreement); `endsLane` on the facts `not_carried` and `declined`; a step with no facts is never true; "An invitation" and "An addendum" in place of the prototype's "A email" and "A addendum".
- **The gaps on main** for cut 4's SQL: a trade's first portal visit (`gc_trade_portal_links.first_opened_at`), a lost mark on a quote we did not carry (`gc_invites.lost_on`), the day a customer said yes (`gc_projects.won_on`), a paid day on a pay application (it is paid through `invoice_id` today), and the day a customer asked (`gc_projects.created_at` is the day we entered the job; the prototype named a `created_on` that does not exist). The warranty year is computed from `gc_owner_acceptances.accepted_on`, with no column.
