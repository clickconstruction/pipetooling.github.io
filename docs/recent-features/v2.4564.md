# v2.4564 — Lien releases: the unconditional waits for the check and keeps its kind (2026-10-05)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Rows 5, 6 and 7 of the lien defects card (punch list #83, `to-dos/lien-defects.md` on PR #4528). All three are in `lienReleaseTracking.ts` and the two surfaces that read it.

## The defects

- **Row 5.** A check takes seven days to clear (`checkClearing.ts`, v2.4330): the Bill tab, View bill and GC Review hold the unconditional waiver until then. The Bill Customer strip and the Dashboard queue did not: `lienReleaseClearance` called a conditional *cleared* the moment applied payments reached its amount.
- **Row 6.** The Dashboard queue opened every row on *Unconditional · progress* and drew every chip as *Conditional · progress*, a conditional final included. The strip already mapped a final to a final.
- **Row 7.** `lienReleasesOwingUnconditional` treated any live unconditional on the covered lines as the follow-up, an autosaved draft included, so a row left the queue the moment someone opened the form.

## The fix

- [`lienReleaseTracking.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienReleaseTracking.ts):
  - `lienReleaseClearance(release, job, todayYmd?)` — with a day, a check on a covered line still inside its clearing days reads `waiting` (`lienReleaseCheckStillClearing` over `billCheckClearsYmd`). Without a day the answer is as before.
  - `computeLienUnconditionalOwed(…, clearing?)` and `buildLienUnconditionalQueue(…, todayYmd?)` hold the same releases back, so the card's count and the queue's rows still agree.
  - `lienReleasesOwingUnconditional` leaves drafts out on both sides: a draft unconditional settles nothing, and a draft conditional owes nothing. Awaiting-signature, issued and signed rows count as before.
  - `unconditionalFollowUpForm(formType)` — the one mapping, a final to a final.
- [`useLienReleasesOwedNudge.ts`](../../src/hooks/useLienReleasesOwedNudge.ts) passes today and the payments it already read. [`BillCustomerLienReleaseStrip.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/BillCustomerLienReleaseStrip.tsx) passes today; its line already reads *check not cleared yet* while waiting. [`DashboardLienReleaseQueueModal.tsx`](../../src/components/dashboard/DashboardLienReleaseQueueModal.tsx) labels the chip from the row and opens the mapped form.

A release with no bill-line snapshot is unchanged: it has no line to find a check on.
