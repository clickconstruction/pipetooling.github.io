# v2.8475 — Lien: the header names the leader a paper waits on (2026-11-24)

Punch list #335, *Also seen* (`to-dos/lien-row-and-window-still-part.md`): a paper that waits on someone else's approval read two ways. The billed row and the Lien desk's row named the leader, *Waiting on Rosa*. The header above the paper read *Approve the notice* and, on its second line, *Waiting on us — the leader’s approval of the notice*. A notice and an affidavit both read that way. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## The cause

The row's name comes from the desk's To do rows (`buildLienNextUp`, `leaderName`) through the act table (`lienActFor`, `waitingOn`). The header's second line comes from the path (`buildLienTimeline`, `waitingOnFor`), whose two sentences for a paper sent for approval were fixed strings. The path was never handed the leader.

Reproduced on made-up rows before the change (`lienApprovalWait.test.ts`): the path built by the Lien window and the path built by the desk both read *the leader’s approval* with a leader handed in.

## What changed

- **One words kernel**, `src/lib/jobs/lienApprovalWait.ts`. `lienLeaderWords(name)` is the leader as the row prints him, *the leader* with no name. `lienApprovalWaitWords(paper, approver)` is the *Waiting on* line's words: *Rosa’s approval of the notice*, *your approval of the affidavit* for the leader himself, and the old words with no name. `lienApproverFor` is the row's own rule for whose approval it is: a leader on his own job, or on a job with no leader set.
- **Both readers are on it.** The act table (`lienActWords.ts`) takes its *the leader* from `lienLeaderWords`. The path (`lienTimeline.ts`) takes the two sentences from `lienApprovalWaitWords`, through a new input `approver`.
- **The builders hand him through.** `buildLienTimelineFromWindow` and `buildLienTimelineFromDesk` take `approver`. The Lien window gets the name from its opener (`deskLeader`, beside `deskNotice` and `deskAffidavit`): the Pipeline and the Lien desk page pass the name their rows print. The Lien desk's notice pane and affidavit pane pass the job's leader the same way.
- **Not changed:** every other step's line. The firm's page, the Legal desk's packet, the desk's By job tab and a job's History tab are handed no leader and read *the leader’s approval*, as before. No paper prints any of these words.
