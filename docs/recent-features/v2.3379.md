# v2.3379 — Accounts Receivable refresh, PR 1: the header summary, the ⋯ menu, and a state chip on every deposit row (2026-09-13)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. First of the four-PR train in [`to-dos/accounts-receivable-refresh/`](../../to-dos/accounts-receivable-refresh/README.md) (Option A of the mock-up, owner: "build it").

## Why

The modal already reads who paid you, sweeps exact matches and guards against counting a hand-recorded check twice — but only in the right pane, after a click. The chrome explained instead of summarising (a three-line paragraph plus a dev-only disclosure row), and a deposit row said amount · kind · date · name and nothing about what the modal knew.

## What shipped

- **Kernel [`arDepositRowState.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/arDepositRowState.ts)** (+5 tests): `arDepositRowStates` names one state per row from data the modal already computes — `returned` · `applied` · `exact` (the sweep pairs it) · `recorded` (an unlinked recorded payment carries the same amount) · `ambiguous` (a sweep-skipped bucket) · `payer` (`matchArDepositToPayer` finds one) · `hand`; `arDepositRowStateLabel` gives the chip's words and tone (no chip for `hand`); `arDepositSummary` / `arDepositSummaryWords` give the header strip.
- **Header**: *N deposits to match · $X unapplied* beside the title; the instruction paragraph is gone (it is the help guide's first paragraph); the dev-only Mercury filter row is gone. [`ArHeaderMenu`](../../src/components/jobs/ar/ArHeaderMenu.tsx) (⋯) holds **Mark returned deposits** (a ✓ toggle, office roles) and **Mercury filter…** (dev, with the filter summary as its hint). Bank transfer details and Close stay where they were.
- **List**: titled **Deposits**; a **To match · N | All** switch replaces the *Show fully applied and returned deposits* checkbox (same `includeHiddenArDeposits` flag). [`ArDepositRow`](../../src/components/jobs/ar/ArDepositRow.tsx): amount · counterparty · *Sep 11 · check · "memo…"*, the state chip on the right, *$X applied · rem. $Y* on partially applied rows, and the Returned tick in mark mode. [`KindBadgePill`](../../src/components/jobs/ar/KindBadgePill.tsx) is lifted out of the modal for the row and, next PR, the deposit header.
- Render smoke: the modal's existing test asserts the summary and the *1 exact match* chip. Help guide *match bank deposits to the bills they pay* names the switch and the ⋯ menu.

## Not in this PR

The right pane (deposit header, remaining meter, memo fold, allocation rows), the matches list and the footer are PRs 2–4.
