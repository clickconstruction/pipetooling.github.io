# v2.7257 — Held for suppliers: a job paid in kind no longer says it is on the Lien desk (2026-11-14)

v2.7253 took a job on an open in-kind deal off the Lien desk (`src/lib/jobs/lienInKind.ts`) and named what it left: Materials → Held for suppliers reads the desk's two RPCs itself, so a job's open statement there could still read *On the Lien desk · our notice is due by …* and link to a desk that shows no row for it. This closes that gap.

## The change

- `buildHeldLienLines` (`src/lib/materials/heldLienLine.ts`) takes an `offDesk` set and gives no line to a job in it, for the notice and for the lien date alike. With no set it reads every job, as before.
- `MaterialsJobAccountsTab` reads its jobs with `withLienInKindColumn` and passes `lienInKindJobIds(jobs)`, so the tab drops the same jobs the desk does, through the same kernel.
- `docs/LIEN_DESK_ARCHITECTURE.md`'s v2.7253 note and `lienInKind.ts`'s list of readers name the held line, and the Lien desk guide's *A job paid in kind is off the desk* says Held for suppliers shows no line either.

No migration and no edge function. Still not covered, as v2.7253 said: `legal-portal`'s book reads the RPCs itself and can draw such a job's path when the job is on a matter. Whether a firm working a matter should see it is the owner's call, and changing it is an edge deploy.

- `heldLienLine.test.ts`: an in-kind job gets neither its notice nor its lien line, the job beside it keeps its line, and with no set nothing changes.
