# v2.3348 — Legal desk: the release-recipients preview matches the dispatcher (2026-09-11)

Follow-up to v2.3325 (Legal portal PR 5), found on the first live walk-through.

## Why

`releaseRecipients()` (the kernel behind the Mark attorney ready sheet's "who will hear" list) and the ✉ Firm's emails sheet promised two things `legal-notify-dispatch` never did: with no recipients, that the firm row's contact email "always hears" about a release; and that the handling person hears "now" regardless of their mode. The dispatcher only ever emails confirmed, unpaused `legal_firm_recipients` rows — `now` mode right away, `digest` mode on their day — and treats scope `mine` as "named as the matter's handling person". That is the approved rule (confirm-by-click, no email to an address nobody confirmed), so the client copy is what moves.

## What changed

- `releaseRecipients`: no firm-row fallback (empty list → nobody emailed); scope `mine` is a stop only when the person is **not** handling; a handling person in `digest` mode lands in the `digest` bucket ("… digest — handling"), in `now` mode in `now` ("handling — right away"). Tests updated (+1 case).
- ✉ Firm's emails empty state: "nothing is emailed until the firm adds its people on the portal's Notifications page. Send them the portal link…".
- Help guide *manage who at the law firm gets emails*: the handling-person sentence now states the real rule and that the Settings contact email is not a subscription.

No migration, no edge function.
