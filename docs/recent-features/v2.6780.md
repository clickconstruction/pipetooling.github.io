## Decided here

- **Only the gavel becomes a link.** The ☰ row and the ⋯ row are menu items, the header's button keeps its button, and the forecast's door closes the forecast first; each navigates on a press, as the Legal desk's did.
- **The Lien window's next step re-aims through the address.** The window used to re-aim an open desk under it with `aimKey`; on the page it closes and the address names the job and the list, which the desk follows.
- **The push helpers moved with the doors.** They are shared with two functions, so the change is one path in each and a redeploy; leaving them on the redirect would have made the leader's phone the one door that still hops through the Pipeline.
- **The window frame stays in `LienDeskModal.tsx` for one more PR.** Nothing mounts it as a window now; PR 3 deletes it with its full-screen toggle and ✕, and draws the phone's list and pane from the address.
