# v2.7639 — Filed liens: the mail sheet's short columns fit their words (2026-11-17)

The owner, 2026-11-17, over the mail sheet printed for twenty envelopes: "On this page I would like the copy to and mail by and # columns to be no wider than their longest text".

**What was there.** `lienServeMailSheetHtml` (`src/lib/jobs/lienServePackets.ts`) drew its table with a `<colgroup>` of fixed shares (4, 22, 12, 30, 10 and 22 percent) under the help sheets' `table-layout: fixed`. So *Copy to* was 12 percent whatever it held and wrapped *Original contractor* onto two lines, and *Mail by* held a short day in a tenth of the page.

**What changed.** The colgroup is gone and this one table lays itself out by its content (`table-layout:auto`, inline, so the other help sheets keep their fixed layout). The three short columns carry `width:1%;white-space:nowrap` on every cell (`FIT`): each is as wide as its longest text plus the cell's padding, and *Original contractor* stays on one line. *Job* and *Certified mail number* keep 22 percent each. *Name and address on the envelope* takes the rest, which is more than the 30 percent it had.

**Measured**, on a made-up sheet at letter width in print media: the number column is its digit plus padding, *Copy to* is *Original contractor* plus padding, *Mail by* is *Nov 11 · late* plus padding, and the address column grew from about 30 to about 37 percent of the table.

**The held rows** are unchanged: an empty number cell, the job, and one cell across the other four.
