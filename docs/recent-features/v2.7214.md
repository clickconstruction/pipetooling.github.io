# v2.7214 — The Bridge's Monday email shows the cash balance and the accrual balance, as the page does (punch list #334) (2026-11-13)

A follow-up to the Bridge train (card #334, `to-dos/bridge-pulse/`). An edge function changed (`bridge-weekly-sheet`, through the kernels copied to it), so it is redeployed after the merge. No migration.

## Why

The walk of the train's last four PRs read the Monday email as a dry run. The email still led with three tiles: what the field made, *Cash low point, 8 weeks* and the slowest step. The page has shown the cash balance and the accrual balance, each with its runway, since PR 6 (v2.7099). The card's default had kept the rest of the email as it was. The owner was asked on 2026-11-13 and said to match the page.

## What changed

- **The mail carries the two balances** (`weekMail.ts`): `WeekMail.money` holds the cash balance, the accrual balance and the line under both that says what runway counts. The mail draws what the field made and the slowest step in one row, then the two balances side by side in one card. That is the page's own order, laid out as the page lays it out on a phone. The text part says the same lines in the same order.
- **They are built from the page's figures** (`sheetBuild.ts`): `buildWeekMailFromReads` calls the page's own `cashLine`, `accrualLine`, `burnPerDay` and `runwayFoot` (`moneyCard.ts`) with what the page hands them. Cash is the bank's accounts the owner counts. What customers owe and what the company owes are the finance read's two totals. A day's burn is payroll for a week over seven plus overhead for a day.
- **`moneyCard.ts` is copied to the edge functions** (`scripts/edge-kernels.mjs`, `npm run gen:edge-kernels`). `bridge-weekly-sheet` imports it by path through `sheetBuild.ts`, so the portals that read the index do not carry it.
- **The cash tile leaves the mail.** `weekMailTiles` keeps the made tile and the slowest step. In a week cash leads, the card still reads *Keep cash above the floor.* and the cash balance says when cash goes under, in amber or red. The wide tile *Cash comes first this week* is no longer drawn in the mail, as it is not on the page.
- **The subject names the cash balance**: *…: Bill is the slowest step. Cash balance $48.4k. Made last week $31.0k.* With no balance read that day, the line is the forecast's low point and the subject says *Cash low point*.
- **Not read is said, never guessed.** With no forecast the cash balance reads *not read*. With what is owed not read, the accrual balance reads *not read*. With no burn, no runway is claimed and the foot line is left off.

## Tests
