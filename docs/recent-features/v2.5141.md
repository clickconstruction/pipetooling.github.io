# v2.5141 — GC mode: a company's window can open at one of its papers (2026-10-10)

The Board's follow-up to B6-b-ii (v2.5135): no migration, no edge function. Asked by the Schedule lane for 9d, whose not-ready block opens a company's paper. The shape is the design spike's.

- **`openPartner(partnerId, at?)`** (`src/components/gc/gcCompanyOpener.ts`) takes a `CompanyAt`:
  - `tab` is `'about' | 'documents' | 'portal'`.
  - `doc` is a Documents row's key: `'msa'`, `'insurance'` or `'w9'` (`DOC_KEYS`), or `'sow-<package id>'`.
  - `send` opens that paper's send.
  - `CompanyTab` lives here now; `GcCompanyDocuments.tsx` re-exports it.
  - `openPartner(id)` with nothing else still opens About, so every caller today is unchanged.
- **`GcCompanyWindow`** opens at `at.tab`, or at Documents when a paper is named. It shows the paper's row as picked, and opens its send once the Contract Book's entries are read and `paperSendable` allows it for this reader. Otherwise it stays on the row: the master agreement while the Book has no Master Services Agreement, or a reader with no presses.
- **`GcProjects`** keeps where the window opened and keys the window by it, so opening the same company at another paper opens fresh. It is the one provider of the opener; the schedule window renders inside it.
- **Tests:** five window render tests. A not-ready bar's W-9 opens with its send. A paper named without a tab waits for the Book's entries before it opens the send. The master agreement opens at its row with no send. A reader with no presses gets the row, read only. A company opened by its name still lands on About.
