# v2.8201 — A job photo and a report photo keep where they were taken (2026-11-21)

Punch list #413, step 3, on the columns of v2.8195. Taunya's word of 2026-11-21 to build the whole card is her yes to this step, which the card had left as her call: it keeps where a worker's phone was.

## Why

A photo's row said who took it, when and for which job. It did not say where. For a GC's back-charge, a lien or a dispute over what was done, *this photo was taken on the site at this hour* is the claim that matters, and the phone's own reading is better evidence than a dot a person placed. A reading that is not taken when the photo is taken can never be had later.

## What ships

**The phone is read once on a pick.** In the Job photos window and on a report's photos, the moment a person takes or picks a photo the phone is asked where it is (`readSpotNow`, through the shot-tile engine's new `keepPoint`). One reading serves every photo of one pick.

**A photo of that moment keeps the reading.** Its row is written with `taken_lat`, `taken_lng` and `taken_accuracy_m` (`takenPointOf`, `takenPointColumns`).

- A photo whose own time is more than five minutes old keeps no point. It came from the phone's library and is not of where the phone is now.
- A rough reading, which is an area a mile or more across, is kept by nobody. A weak one is kept with its margin, which says how weak.
- No reading, or none within two and a half seconds, and the photo goes without one. A photo is never held for a point.

**A photo that waits for a signal** carries the reading of the moment it was taken in its waiting record (`KeptShot.takenPoint`), and its row gets that point when it sends, wherever the phone is by then.

**Taken here.** An opened job photo or report photo that has a point offers **Taken here · the phone was sure within 20 ft** (`PhotoTakenHere`), which opens the spot in the phone's own maps. The lists of photos read a view that does not carry the point, so it is read by id when a photo is opened.

## Who reads it

Whoever reads the photo's row: the crew of the job and the office. No policy changed. The guides *add a photo to a job* and *take photos for a report* now say that the app keeps where the phone was, that the phone is read once and not followed, and who can read it.

A person who has not let the app read their location is asked by the phone the first time they add a photo. Saying no changes nothing but the point: the photo goes as before.
