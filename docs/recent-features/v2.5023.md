# v2.5023 — Submittals: a design change says whose call it is and who signed off (2026-10-09)

Migration `20261009234500`, and `get-submittal-room` redeploys. This builds the owner's call of 2026-10-09 on Submittals decision 11 (`to-dos/submittals/README.md`): a design change asks whose call it is and records the engineer's sign-off. Before, the room's card stated the changed value and left the call to the reviewer. The case is SpaceX BA-02N's one design change, a 1.28 gpf flush valve against a 1.0 gpf spec. An architect's Approve there could outrun the engineer of record, and the office kept no record of who agreed.

## The change

- **The columns** (`20261009234500`, `docs/migrations/20261009234500_submittal_design_change_call.md`): four nullable columns on `bid_submittal_items`, each held by a CHECK:
  - `call_by`: architect, engineer, gc or owner;
  - `signoff_name`: up to 120 characters;
  - `signoff_on`: a date;
  - `signoff_via`: email, letter, stamped_drawing, meeting or phone.
- **The room's words** (`_shared/submittalRoomPayload.ts`):
  - `DESIGN_CALL_BY` and `SIGNOFF_VIA` hold the choices and their labels, and `asDesignCallBy` / `asSignoffVia` drop a value they do not know.
  - `designCallLine` builds the line: *The engineer's call · signed off by Pat Lee on Oct 9, 2026, by email.* It says only what is recorded, like *The GC's call · signed off on a stamped drawing.*, and is null on any other status.
  - `roomRowFrom` adds it to the row as `designCall`. The key is absent when there is no line.
- **The office's side** (`src/lib/submittals/designCall.ts`, new):
  - `designCallDraft` reads a stored row into the window.
  - `designCallPatch` is what a save writes. On a design change it writes what the office set, the name trimmed and the day only when it is one. On any other status it clears a row that held a record. It writes nothing when there is nothing to write or clear.
  - `designCallCarry` is what a row takes with it when it moves: a design change's record, and only one with something in it.
- **The window** (`SubmittalItemEditDialog.tsx`): on a design change, three headings under the status:
  - **Whose call**, with Architect, Engineer, GC and Owner;
  - **Signed off**, with a name box and a date;
  - **How it came**, with Email, Letter, Stamped drawing, Meeting and Phone.

  Each choice taps off again. The fields count as typing for *Leave without saving?*.
- **The room** (`SubmittalRoomView.tsx`): the line prints under the performance line on the row's card, on the GC's page (`get-submittal-room` selects the four columns) and in the office's *See what they see* (`toRoomItemSource` passes them on).
- **The record moves with the row**, as the cut sheet does:
  - the next revision keeps it while the product is unchanged and the row is still a design change (`buildSubmittalRows`, read through `itemToPrevious`, written by `draftToItemInsert`);
  - a row the next revision carries as it stands keeps it (`carriedRowInsert`);
  - a split row's new rows each keep it (`splitRow` in `BidsSubmittalsTab.tsx`).

## Deploy

1. `supabase db push` for `20261009234500`. A save that leaves the fields empty names none of the columns, so the client works before the push. A save that fills them waits for it.
2. `get-submittal-room`, whose select fails until the columns exist. Ten other functions bundle `_shared/submittalRoomPayload.ts` and read as behind on `npm run check:edge-drift`, with no change in what they do: customer-portal, get-bid-proposal-room, get-contract-for-signer, get-estimate-for-customer, get-job-contract, get-rfq-quote-page, legal-portal, open-submittal-pdf, sub-portal, submit-submittal-review.
3. The types PR, which drops the `DesignCallFields` casts.
