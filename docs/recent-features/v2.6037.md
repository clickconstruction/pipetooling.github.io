## What shipped

- **`src/lib/estimates/estimateGoodThroughChip.ts`** — `goodThroughPassedChip(row, todayYmd)`: the
  chip when, and only when, the Extend door it opens is offered (`canExtendEstimateGoodThrough`: a
  `sent` row with a send on record, not in a bid room; never a draft, an accepted, a declined or a
  superseded row) and `goodThroughState` reads *passed* — the day strictly before today in the
  company's calendar (`todayYmdInAppTz`, never the browser's), the day the accept page starts
  answering *Estimate expired*; the same day is still live. The words are the card's own, *good
  through passed · extend?* (the Pipeline's chips are not held to the plain-words rules, and the
  neighbours read *never opened · sent 7d ago — nudge?*); the hover is `extendLeadWords`, the press's
  address `extendGoodThroughHref` (`/estimates/176#extend-good-through`, `EXTEND_GOOD_THROUGH_HASH`),
  the tone `GOOD_THROUGH_PASSED_CHIP_COLORS` (red-700 on the red tint with the red border, theme
  tokens). 6 tests, with the day boundary and the 10:30 PM Central case.
- **`EstimateGoodThroughPassedChip.tsx`** — the red pill, a `Link`. Render smoke: the words, the hover,
  and the press landing on `/estimates/176#extend-good-through`.
- **`EstimateListTable.tsx`** — both renderings (the table and the phone cards) draw the chip in place
  of the opened / never-opened chip on a Sent row once the day passed; the live row keeps today's chip.
  The table's smoke gains the passed row.
- **`Estimates.tsx` + `EstimateExtendGoodThroughPanel.tsx`** — the panel carries the hash as its `id`;
  the page's hash effect (the one `#estimate-job` uses) scrolls the panel to the centre and focuses its
  date field, so the chip lands on v2.5992's door — one door, never a copy.
- **`readablePairs.test.ts`** pins the chip's pair at 4.5:1 in both themes.
- **Docs**: the chip-list guide *tell if a customer opened an estimate, and record a no* gains the
  red chip; *see the email a customer gets with an estimate* → Extend the good-through date names it
  as the way in; the Glossary's good-through entry; the Estimates map's list-table dossier; the card's
  status and *Left for later*.
