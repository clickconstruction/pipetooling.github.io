# v2.5265 — Messages: Bill day’s walk is kept in the database (2026-10-12)

This is the SQL for the Messages build's cluster 7 (GC Reviews helper 7, directed by the lead session GC Review): cut 9's tables and presses (`to-dos/gc-mode/messages/BUILD_PLAN.md` on `spike/gc-mode`). Migration `20261012140000_gc_bill_day_walk.sql`, documented in `docs/migrations/20261012140000_gc_bill_day_walk.md`. The kernels that read it are v2.5248's `src/lib/gc/billDay/`. No screen yet. The lead runs `supabase db push` after this merges.

- **`gc_superintendent_checks`** holds one tap per draw line: the percent seen or stored material on site, a photo's Drive link, a note, who and when. It is kept as it went, and `LineCheck` is its row.
- **`gc_bill_day_nothing`** holds a trade's word that it sends nothing for a bill day, with who said it. `NothingThisPeriod` is its row.
- **`app_settings.gc_bill_day_cutoff_days`** starts at 6. The money team may set it, and `BILL_DAY_CUTOFF_DAYS` is the kernels' default.
- **`gc_sow_lines.where_on_site`** says where a line is on site, for the walk's order.
- **`gc_record_line_check`** records a tap. A percent under the claim becomes the draw line's `we_see`, the way the Draws window reads a doubted line. One at or over the claim clears it.
- **`gc_say_nothing_this_period`** records a trade's word, and saying it again for the same bill day replaces who said it. *Put it back* deletes the row.
- **`gc_set_bill_day_cutoff`** sets the cut-off, 2 to 20 days.
- **Who**: dev only while it is built, as every Building table is. The money team reads both tables and sets the cut-off. `src/lib/gc/doors.ts` lists both tables. The three fences cover them.
- **Changed from the lead's sketch**: the tables and functions carry `gc_`, so `doors.test.ts` holds them to a door. `photo_id` became `photo_url`, since main keeps photos as Drive links. `by` and `at` became named columns.
- **The bed**: `supabase/tests/gc_building/65_bill_day_walk.sql` in the gc-building bed. It records a tap and reads it back as the line's `we_see`, then checks every refusal, the nothing row, the cut-off and the fences. It runs in CI.
