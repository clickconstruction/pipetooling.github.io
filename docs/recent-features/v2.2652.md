# v2.2652 — Materials Job Accounts tab: per-job money flow vs supply house payables (2026-09-02)

New **Job Accounts** tab on Materials (between Supply Houses and PO Generator;
same office-role gating as Supply Houses: dev / master_technician /
assistant-like). Answers "which jobs have paid me that I haven't paid my
supply houses on?" — the Texas trust-fund / pay-when-paid exposure question —
by joining `supply_house_invoice_job_allocations` × `supply_house_invoices`
against `jobs_ledger.revenue` / `payments_made`.

- **Kernel**: `src/lib/materials/jobAccountsFlow.ts` (`buildJobAccountsView`,
  unit-tested). Allocation pct is 0–100 (matches
  `fetchJobMaterialsCostSnapshot`). Per job: billed / paidIn (jobs_ledger),
  suppliersPaid / suppliersOwed (allocated invoice dollars by `is_paid`),
  owed bucketed via `agingBucketFor` (reuses `lib/supplyHouseAging.ts`), and
  per-house groups (invoice count, oldest unpaid due date + bucket, paid,
  owed). Status: `owe_suppliers` (owed > 0 and any customer money in; `held =
  min(owed, paidIn)`), `awaiting_customer` (owed > 0, nothing received),
  `floating` (houses paid, customer not fully paid), `settled`. Rows sort
  owe → floating → awaiting → settled, biggest dollars first. Unpaid invoices
  allocated to neither a job nor a bid are surfaced as an "unallocated"
  count/total (never silently dropped); an allocation whose job row isn't
  visible still counts as allocated.
- **Tab**: `src/components/materials/MaterialsJobAccountsTab.tsx` —
  self-contained (loads on first activation, always mounted, Refresh button),
  pages every query via `fetchAllRows`/`fetchAllRowsChunkedIn` +
  `withSupabaseRetry`. UI: four stat tiles (holding / floating / settled /
  unallocated), filter chips, paired In/Out bars per job (In: blue paid
  portion of billed; Out: slate paid-to-houses + owed segments in the
  Supply Houses aging palette, all on the job's own scale), expandable
  per-house statement with an aging-heat "oldest due" chip, "Open job in
  Jobs" (`/jobs?tab=stages&edit=<id>`), and per-house **Open house**.
- **Supply Houses hand-off**: `SupplyHousesTab` gained `autoOpenHouseId` /
  `onAutoOpenHouseHandled` — Job Accounts' Open house (and the unallocated
  row's "Review on Supply Houses") switches tabs and auto-opens that house's
  detail, where invoices and Make Payment live.
- **Materials.tsx wiring**: `job-accounts` added to `MATERIALS_TABS`, the
  `activeTab` union, both URL role guards (primary/superintendent + estimator
  redirect to parts-book), the tab strip, and the service-type filter row's
  hide list (like Supply Houses, it doesn't scope by service type).

No migration; read-only over existing tables. Help guide:
`src/content/help/see-which-paid-jobs-still-owe-supply-houses.md`.
