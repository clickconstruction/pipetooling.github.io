# v2.3796 — Contract sweep: a contract for another address is never offered (2026-09-24)

The first look at the Drive finds that [`to-dos/contract-sweep-refresh`](../../to-dos/contract-sweep-refresh/README.md) (#28) said to watch for a week from 2026-09-22. No migration; the edge function is unchanged — the matcher is client-side.

## What was found

The sweep's **In Drive** tab held five rows, all amber *check* and no green *confident* find (so "trust the green ones" cannot be judged yet). Three of the five were proposals, rightly amber. The other two were signed contracts **for another address**, offered because the customer folder matched:

- J950 (141 Encino Ave, Heron Construction) was offered *CLICK PLUMBING - EST. #123 - 105 DOVER RD. - SIGNED* from the folder `_Heron Construction / 105 Dover`.
- J1046 (214 Beechwood Ave, Randolph Field Reality) was offered *9511 Arcade Ridge signed contract* from the customer's folder.

Each was one *It is — use this file* from recording a neighbour's contract on the wrong job.

## The change

[`driveContractMatch.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/driveContractMatch.ts): `streetsNamed(file)` reads every street the file's own name or its folder chain names — the piece that starts with a house number, split on ` / `, dashes and underscores; a year or a count before a plain word (*2025 Contracts*, *2 story addition*) is not a street. `namesAnotherStreet(file, job)` says which one is not the job's. In `matchDriveContracts` a job the paper contradicts is dropped from the file's hits before ranking: with no hit left the file is `none` with the reason *names 105 Dover, not the job's address*; with the right job also in the sweep the file goes to it alone, so the old *2 jobs match this folder* tie is gone too. A job with no house number (*Gun Dog Trail*) cannot be contradicted and keeps its check. The sweep's chips and the *Look in Drive* window both read this matcher, so both follow.
