# v2.4397 — Lien desk on an iPhone: the title bar sits below the clock (2026-10-02)

No migration, no edge function. Robert, 2026-10-02, with a screenshot from his iPhone (the app installed to the Home Screen): "on this modal the header is unreachable". The ⏱ Lien desk's title, Share and × were drawn under the clock, the signal bars and the battery.

## Why

The page is `viewport-fit=cover`, so on an installed iPhone the layout runs under the status bar and `env(safe-area-inset-top)` says how tall that bar is (47–59 px). The desk's backdrop is `position: fixed; top: 0` and centres a card with `max-height: calc(100dvh - 2rem - var(--app-bottom-chrome))`. On a phone the Calendar fills that height, so the card's top edge sat 16 px from the top of the screen and the title bar (0.7rem below it) landed inside the status bar. Most windows pad their backdrop by `calc(1rem + env(safe-area-inset-top))`; the windows built on the `--app-bottom-chrome` shape (v2.2184, v2.3522) took care of the bottom only. A browser tab has no inset, which is why the phone walks on the dev server never showed it.

## What changed

- **`--app-top-chrome`** ([`index.css`](../../src/index.css), `:root`): `env(safe-area-inset-top, 0px)`, the twin of `--app-bottom-chrome`. A variable rather than `env()` in each window so a walk on the dev server can set it by hand (`document.documentElement.style.setProperty('--app-top-chrome', '59px')`) and see what the phone sees.
- **The Lien desk** ([`LienDeskModal`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskModal.tsx)): the backdrop pads its top by the variable (it still dims the status bar), and the card's height and max-height take it off, full screen or not.
- **The windows the desk opens**, same cause: Put a GC on notice ([`GcOnNoticeModal`](../../src/components/jobs/GcOnNoticeModal.tsx)), its preview ([`GcNoticePreviewModal`](../../src/components/jobs/GcNoticePreviewModal.tsx)), Send the run ([`LienDeskRunModal`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskRunModal.tsx), whose `90vh` card could reach 42 px from the top), the email sheet ([`LienDeskEmailSheet`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskEmailSheet.tsx): the phone's full sheet starts at the variable, not at 0) and the phone's Share sheet ([`LienDeskSharePanel`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskSharePanel.tsx), max-height).

## Verified

Dev server at 390 × 844 against production data, read only. With the variable at 0 the desk is where it was (card 16–768, title at 27). With it set to 59 px: backdrop padding 59 px, card 75–768, title at 86, Share 81–121, × 83–116; the Share sheet tops out at 115 and the email sheet starts at 59 with ‹ Back at 74. Not yet seen on the iPhone itself: that needs the deploy.

The render smokes pin the backdrop's `padding-top` on the desk and on Put a GC on notice.
