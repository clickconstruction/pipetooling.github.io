# v2.5848 — The Collect desk, PR 1 of 7: the pile read by each payer's own pace (2026-10-25)

Punch list #177 (`to-dos/collect-desk-weakest-link.md`), PR A of the train. The owner, 2026-10-25, on the Bridge's Collect card: *"I would like to be able to click on something on this card to see a modal of this broken down so that I as a user can have a better idea of what I need to do to overcome this limiting factor."* The modal was designed on three boards and reviewed five times (the to-do links them); this PR is the first of its kernels, with nothing on screen.

## What it adds

Two pure kernels under `src/lib/bridge/collect/`, each with tests on the figures read from prod on 2026-10-25 (SpaceX, RMC, Southern Post, TF Harper, H & I, Knight, a residential customer, a Collections job, a settled row, a shell):

- **`collectPile.ts` — `buildCollectPile`.** Every open bill (the AR rows bill truth already produces: key, job, payer, billed day, remainder) read into one state. `notdue` until the billed day plus the payer's pace; `late` past it; `doubtful` more than `DOUBTFUL_AFTER_DAYS` (60) past it; `promised` while a recorded promise's day has not passed its grace; `broken` once it is more than `BROKEN_PROMISE_GRACE_DAYS` (7) gone; `collections` when the job is parked, kept apart from the headline. The **pace** is the payer's own median with enough samples, else the **GC pace here** for a GC (`gcPaceHere`: the median of the GC payers' own medians, with how many payers it rests on), else the payer's segment median, else the company's. A payer is a GC by record (some job names it as its GC) or, by the owner's decision 1, by a pay application on the job. Returns each bill with its state, pace and source, expected day, days past it and age; the totals by state; the headline (everything but Collections); the late pile (`late` + `broken`, the part a call can move); and the past-due count the card shows. The rules (`CollectRules`) are inputs with `COLLECT_RULES_DEFAULT`, never constants in the kernel, so the owner's four decisions change a parameter, not the code.
- **`collectPayers.ts` — `buildCollectPayers`.** The bills grouped by payer, biggest first: bills, jobs, the oldest bill's age, pace and source, the promise state, the share over 60 days old, money by state, the hand-marked share (bills never sent from here), the worst state on the account for the row's edge, and the newest Payment Chase touch. Then the **cut**: the fewest payers holding nine tenths of the headline, at most six, and everyone else as one line (payers, bills, dollars, the largest, the oldest, the late share).

## What does not change

Nothing on screen. The Collect card, the AR window and the forecast read as before. PR B (the loader and the read-only modal) is next; PRs E and F move the card itself and merge alone.

## Verify

`npx vitest run src/lib/bridge/collect`: the pay-application rule puts SpaceX's $103k at the GC pace, due Oct 29, and at the segment pace, late since Oct 6, with the rule off; Southern Post's Oct 16 promise reads broken at 9 days and promised under a 10-day grace; RMC's March bill reads doubtful by its 35-day pace; the six-payer cut holds nine tenths of a pile shaped like the real one.
