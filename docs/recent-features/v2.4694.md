# v2.4694 — Job window: the History tab is a calendar of the days people were on the job (2026-10-06)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. The owner, 2026-10-06, on the History tab right after the lien strip (v2.4652): *I would like to also see a calendar of the days people were on the job and how many people. Is this possible? If so help me come up with a mockup and then ask yourself is this the best we can do?* Mock-up: artifact `PqEAop9me3rXtUMY7HiT5g` (today's tab with its problems numbered, the proposal, the phone). Taken as drawn. Stacked on v2.4652 (both touch the guide's History paragraph).

## What was there

The History tab was the Projects page's Gantt in single-job mode: one bar, 36 px a day, 181 days wide, the people count on the days worked, with the Projects page's controls above it — a search box whose placeholder read *No working jobs with approved clock sessions in scope* under a job that had them, From / To, Last 90d / 365d, and an Expanded · Compact lane toggle that packs nothing when there is one job. On a phone (v2.3235) a day list stood in for the grid, with its own range bar (v2.3237).

## What changed

- **Kernel** [`lib/jobs/jobHistoryCalendar.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobHistoryCalendar.ts): `buildCrewCalendar(rows, { jobId, todayYmd, startYmd, endYmd, onlyUserId? })` over the same approved clock-session rows the Gantt aggregates — one `CrewMonth` per month with work (Sunday-first `leadBlanks`, every day of the month as a `CrewDay` with its people, minutes, weekend / today / open flags, `dimmed` when a person is picked and was not there), `quietMonthsBefore` for skipped months, the people most days first with their minutes, the first and last day worked. `sessionMinutes` — a quick-add's own minutes, else clock-in to clock-out, an open session counts none. Words: `crewSummaryWords` (*23 days · Jul 14 → today · 4 people at most · 380 h*), `crewDayLabel` (*Wed Aug 5 · 4 people · 34.5 h · Malachi, Jose, Edgar, Luis · still clocked in*), `crewHoursWords`, `crewQuietWords`, `crewRangeFor` (whole · 90 · 365 · custom).
- **Component** [`components/jobs/JobHistoryCrewCalendar.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobHistoryCrewCalendar.tsx) (`data-job-history-crew-calendar`): the summary line and the range chips on one row (Dates… reveals From / To), the **Who** chips (`data-crew-person`, `aria-pressed`; a press picks, a second press or *everyone* clears), the months in an auto-fill grid of 215 px columns (`data-crew-month`, `data-crew-quiet`, `data-crew-month-foot`), each worked day a `<button data-crew-day>` with the people count in `peopleCountColor`'s scale and the label as its `aria-label`; an unworked day a span. Today: an inset ring; still clocked in: an amber corner; a picked person: other days at 0.28 opacity. A legend line.
- **The tab** (`ProjectsJobHistoryTab`, single-job mode): `crewRangeMode` ('whole' by default — the job's first day worked, from the rows already loaded, through today), `pickedUserId`, `useUserDisplayNames` over the people; the calendar in place of the Gantt; the many-jobs controls not drawn at all when `jobId` is set. A day opens `ProjectsJobHistoryDayModal` through the existing `onDayCellClick(bars[0], ymd)`.
- **The fetch** ([`fetchProjectsJobHistoryClockSessions.ts`](../../src/lib/fetchProjectsJobHistoryClockSessions.ts)) selects `clocked_in_at` and `quick_add_minutes` too, so the hours exist; `ProjectsJobHistoryClockRow` carries them as optional fields, so the Gantt's fixtures and callers are untouched.
- **Retired**: `JobHistoryDayList.tsx`, `HistoryRangeBar.tsx`, `lib/jobs/jobHistoryDayList.ts`, `lib/jobs/historyRangeBar.ts` and their tests — the phone list existed because the Gantt was unusable at 359 px; the calendar is not. `useNarrowViewport640` is no longer read by the tab.

## Not in this PR

The mock-up's optional lien line under each month's name (*§ 53.056 window closed · not noted*, *notice sent*, *opens Nov 1*). On a sub job every calendar month is a § 53.056 month, so the two things on this tab are the same months; it would come from the lien timeline the box above already builds (`useJobLienTimeline` lifted to `JobWindowModal`). A follow-up once the calendar has been looked at live.
