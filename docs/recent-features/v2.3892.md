# v2.3892 — Quickfill: section colours follow each section’s own rhythm (2026-09-27)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. The owner's yes of 2026-09-27 on punch list #30's leftover L1: one rule for the desk's chips and the phone's list.

## What changed for the office

Quickfill's section buttons, jump-strip chips and the *N of M fresh* line coloured on a flat rule — green for 12 hours, yellow to 30, red after — so a section the office looks at weekly was red six days out of seven. They now colour by each section's own rhythm, as the phone's list has since [v2.3783](v2.3783.md): green until the next look falls due, yellow the day it does, red past it. A section with fewer than three marks keeps the flat rule.

## What shipped

- **`src/lib/quickfill/round.ts`** — `roundChipColor` (state → green · yellow · red) and `roundChipColors(marks, events, now)`, which reads each section's rhythm from its mark history with `sectionRhythmDays` and `roundState`, the kernels the phone list uses. Personal doors have no rhythm.
- **`src/pages/Quickfill.tsx`** — the 36 section buttons and the jump strip ask `chipColorOf(sectionId)` instead of `getButtonColor(mark)`; the mark history (60 days of `quickfill_section_mark_events`) loads at every width, not only on a phone — one more read on the desk.
- **`freshnessSummary.ts`** — a section handed in with `green` is counted by it, so the *fresh · need a look* line agrees with the chips.
- Unchanged: a marked section still collapses for 12 hours and re-opens on its own.
- **The guide** *quickfill* says what due means.
