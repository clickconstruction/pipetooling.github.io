## What shipped

- **The read**: `OwnWorkCosts.crewJobs` carries U8's links (each crew trade's Pipeline job), and `ownWorkJobIds` reads those jobs in the same batch as general conditions'. The page builds it only for the money team; while the read is out, a linked crew says it waits for its job.
- **The rule** (`crewCost`, `ownWorkCost.ts`), on U8b's `selfPerform.pctDone`: at today's pace, spent ÷ percent done, once the Pipeline's own rule says it is not too early; at what it cost once 100% done or its Pipeline job is billed or paid; at its price when not linked, without pay access, before the read, when the read failed, too early, or when another place counts the job.
- **The Pipeline's own thresholds**: `JOB_BURN_EARLY_PCT` (10) and `JOB_BURN_EARLY_FIELD_DAYS` (3), exported from `jobBurn.ts` and read by `buildJobBurn` and `crewCost` alike; the Costs tab's behavior is unchanged.
- **A job counts once** (`ownWorkHolders`, `holderWords`): project by project, each project's crews first, then its general conditions; any later place counts at its price or budget and names where the job counts.
- **The margin** (`jobMargin`): a crew trade's cost is `crewCost`'s; buying out counts only the trades we hire; **Our own work** adds the crews' under or over to general conditions'; earned so far adds each read crew's billed so far less its spend. With nothing read, every number is as before.
- **Money's margin**: each crew line in its words (`crewCostWords`), and the footnote's crew sentence.
- **Closeout**: our crew's line says what it cost or costs at today's pace (`crewCloseoutWords`), and a closed job says what it made us (`jobMadeWords`).
- **Guides**: `see-the-money-on-our-gc-jobs` (*What our own crew really costs*), `close-out-a-trade-and-close-a-gc-job` (what a closed job made us). **Docs**: `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`, `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` (the Owner Billing bullet's pay rule).
