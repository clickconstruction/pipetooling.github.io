# v2.7197 — Job window: the Contract and Sub work order rows sit in the list (2026-11-13)

The owner boxed two rows on the job window's Edit tab in red: *"i don't love how these two sections look on that modal"*. Both labels wrapped to two lines, the contract row held a text box whose placeholder was cut off, and the two rows carried two filled blue buttons in a list where every other row is a label, a value and a pencil. He was shown three drafts at desktop and phone width and picked **Draft C, the same row, tidied**: the smallest move.

## What changed

- **One-line labels.** *Customer Contract* reads **Contract**, as View bill's paperwork card already calls it. *Sub work order* is a few pixels wider than the label column, so `JobFormFactRow` takes a new `labelNoWrap` and the label runs into the gap before the value.
- **Every press outlined, the main one first** (`JobContractStrip` and `JobWorkOrderStrip`, `variant="inline"` only). The contract row reads chip, **Send contract**, **Paste a link…**, **Their paper…**. The work order row reads chip, then its state's press. Nothing is filled blue in the list of facts.
- **The Drive link box is folded behind Paste a link…** The press turns into the box with the cursor in it. **File it** shows once something is typed and files exactly as before (`fileSignedJobContract`). Left empty, or on Escape, the box folds back. The box has a set width: a growing one made the row size itself too narrow and pushed the press after it onto a second line, which is also why **Send contract** wrapped before.

## What it does not do

- **No press does anything new.** Each opens the window or sheet it opened, in every state: none, draft, not needed, sent, signed, their paper for the contract, and none, draft, sent, signed, declined for the work order.
- **The other mounts are untouched.** Bill Customer's strip, the hosted bill panel's strip and View bill's paperwork rows keep their look, the filled **Send one to sign** included.
- **A phone still takes two lines** for either row when the chip and its presses do not fit 219 px. That was drawn in the draft he picked.
