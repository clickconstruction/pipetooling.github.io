# v2.5535 — Lien desk: Mail through Docsmit never offers an envelope the run holds back (2026-10-14)

Found by the adversarial review of #5719 (v2.5532) and there since the Docsmit train's PR 5b (v2.5454): the run window's **Mail through Docsmit** door and its prepare read every envelope in the run, the held ones too. `envelopes` is `mailing.all` (`runMailing(runEnvelopes(shown))`, the mailed then the held), and both `docsmitDoor` and `openDocsmit` took `envelopes.filter((e) => !envelopeViaDocsmit(e))`; `docsmitCandidates` drops only what is not certified, and its address problems stop only an envelope Docsmit cannot read. So an envelope `runEnvelopeHold` keeps off the paper (an unsigned notice not yet printed, a $0 claim) with a full address was counted on the door, prepared and mailable certified through Docsmit. The reviewer's render test: an unsigned notice whose two envelopes were both held read *Mail through Docsmit · 2 envelopes ▸* and prepare received both. A held envelope with no address never mailed (its address problems held it at prepare) but still reached the review's held list as staying *on paper*, which a held envelope does not. No migration, no edge function.

- **`LienDeskRunModal.tsx`**: the door, prepare and the send's `paperLeft` all read `paperMailed` (`mailing.mailed` less the envelopes already through Docsmit), the set the packet prints. The door's count, the envelopes prepare is asked for and the packet's *Print the packet · N envelopes* now agree, and a held envelope reaches neither path. The certified envelopes the run holds back are counted only to name the door's reason (`docsmitCandidates` over `mailing.held`, less any already through Docsmit).
- **`runDocsmit.ts`** `docsmitRunDoor(settings, candidates, { held })`: with nothing to offer and `held > 0` the dim door's hover reads *Every certified envelope is held. Its card says why.* instead of *No envelope in this run is on certified mail.*; with candidates but none clean, *Every certified envelope that goes out needs … first.*; and whenever something is held, *N held envelopes wait in the run.* rides after the reason (or alone on a lit door). The problem line agrees in number now: *1 envelope with a problem stays on paper.*
- **Tests**: `runDocsmit.test.ts` (the door: the held words in all three cases, the paused door still hidden, the agreement fix); `LienDeskRunModal.render.test.tsx` (+3 under *Mail through Docsmit offers only the envelopes that go out*: an unsigned notice and a $0 notice, each with full addresses, hold both envelopes, the door is dim with the held words and a press asks prepare nothing; a mixed run of a clean notice, an unsigned one and a $0 one offers *Mail through Docsmit · 2 envelopes ▸* beside *Print the packet · 2 envelopes*, its hover reads *4 held envelopes wait in the run.*, and prepare is asked for envelopes 1 and 2 of the clean notice alone). Mutation-checked: putting back the old filter on the door and prepare fails all three (the door read *· 2 envelopes ▸* and *· 6 envelopes ▸*); on prepare alone, the mixed run; on the door alone, all three; dropping `{ held }`, all three.
