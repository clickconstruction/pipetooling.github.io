# v2.4300 — Edit Job → Bill: payment lines fit a phone (2026-10-01)

Grace asked whether v2.4293 (each bill shows the money that paid it) and v2.4294 (By bill / By date) had been tested live against the design. A walk of main on job 650 (ATI Schertz) at the window's 560 px, a 375 px phone and a 320 px phone found two defects; she asked for them fixed. The three gaps from design C found on the same walk (no date column in By date, bills drawn as full rows instead of one line, the switch ~300 px above the list) are a redesign, not part of this fix.

## The defects

1. **A long payment line broke into three pieces.** `.jobPaymentLineMain` was one wrapping flex row: amount, words, chips, then the ⋯ pushed right with `margin-left: auto`. When the words did not fit beside the amount they wrapped as a whole to the next line, and the ⋯ to a third — on job 650 *Sep 28 · check from Lober’s Construction Co · 75 d* did this at 560 px in By date and on every phone width in both views.
2. **By date clipped a bill's money line on a phone.** The rail's 6 px margin + 2 px rule + 1 rem padding narrowed each bill row, and `.jobInvoiceWords .l2 .keep` (*$9,022.49 open* + *$9,022.49 short · 73 d past expected*) is `nowrap`, so at 375 px it ran 1 px past the screen and the end of *past expected* was cut off.

## What changed

- [`JobFormPaymentLine.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormPaymentLine.tsx): the words, the *no bill picked* / *Returned by the bank* chips, **Pin it to this bill** and the open **Unlink and remove** move into one middle cell (`.mid`). The line is now exactly three cells: amount · middle · ⋯.
- `index.css`: `.jobPaymentLineMain` is a grid (`auto minmax(0, 1fr) auto`), so the words wrap inside their own column and the ⋯ stays beside the amount; the amount never wraps; the words break anywhere rather than overflow. `.jobInvoiceWords .l2 .keep` may now break between its parts, each part staying whole — on a wide row it still reads as one line. Under a 420 px ledger the By date rail is slimmer (5 px margin, 0.7 rem padding).
- `JobFormPaymentLine.render.test.tsx`: the line's children are `amt`, `mid`, `jobPaymentLineMenu`, with the chips and buttons inside the middle cell.

## Verified live in dev on job 650

Measured in the page, both views, at 1024 px (dialog 560), 375 px and 320 px: no element of the bills list or ③ reaches past the dialog's right edge; every ⋯ sits on its amount's line; the words start right of the amount. The ⋯ menu opens inside the screen at 320 px (left 46, right 306). At 320 px the long Sep 28 line takes four short lines in By date — the price of the narrow column.
