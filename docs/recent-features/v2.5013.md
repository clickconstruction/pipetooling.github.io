# v2.5013 — People → Review: a sub sheet with several names counts an equal share for each (2026-10-09)

Client only. No migration, no edge function. This builds the owner's call of 2026-10-09 on *Review: who carries a sub sheet with several names on it?*. The owner chose equal shares everywhere.

## The gap

A sub sheet can list several assignees, crew members and a sub company together. Team Summary gave each name an equal share (`laborJobShareForPerson`, person identity phase E). The per-person Review panel counted the whole sheet for each name. JP1007 has one: a $2,150 line under four names. Team Summary booked a quarter to each, while each crew member's Review panel counted all $2,150. Helper 11 found it from the JP1007 contributors gap (v2.4911).

## The change

- **`laborJobShareOfMatchedPerson`** (`src/lib/people/laborJobPersonMatch.ts`): a person's even share of a sheet they are on, 1/N for the sheet's N distinct names. It uses the same split as `laborJobShareForPerson`.
  - The match stays `laborJobMatchesPerson`, the junction first. So a person the junction puts on a sheet under another spelling still books 1/N, not nothing.
  - A sheet with an empty name list is the person's whole.
- **`buildReviewPersonAllocation`** (`src/lib/people/reviewPersonAllocation.ts`) takes that share wherever it counted the person's own sheet:
  - the sheet's row on the panel: hours, labor and drive
  - the person's lifetime labor, drive and hours on the job
  - so *sub labor by others* (the job's sheets less the person's own) now holds the other names' shares
- **The contributors window** (`laborByJobAndPerson`) is unchanged. It keeps the sheet as one row under all its names, so its rows still add up to the job.
- The job-level totals are unchanged, and so is the crew side.

On JP1007's shape, each of the four names' panels reads *$538*, with *25% of $2,150* under it. The four add up to the sheet once.
