# v2.7971 — Sub Labor: an assistant can add a sheet for a teammate (2026-11-19)

`dev-mcp` server 0.116.0. No new verb, no migration. **`plan_sub_labor`** → **`apply_sub_labor`** take anyone the New Sub Labor form's **Paid to** step offers. The owner, 2026-11-19: "Widen the seat verb".

## Why

The office records a teammate's job bonus as a Sub Labor sheet in their name: money owed beyond their wages, on the job it was earned on. The form has offered teammates for that all along, under **Office Team**, and since v2.7428 a sheet that names one teammate and no sub is theirs (*Team · sub labor*, `teamSheetRule`).

The seat read the form's Subcontractors list alone. A dry run for a teammate with a superintendent login was refused as "not a subcontractor or a primary", so a list of bonuses could not go in through the seat.

## What changed

- `laborPersonFacts` (`supabase/functions/_shared/devMcpSubLabor.ts`) now reads both of the form's lists.
  - The Subcontractors list, as before: an account whose role is subcontractor or primary, or a roster row of kind sub or primary with no account.
  - The Office Team (`rosterNamesEveryoneElse`): an account whose role is master technician, assistant, controller, estimator, superintendent or dev (`SHEET_TEAM_ROLES`), or a roster row of kind master technician, assistant, estimator or superintendent with no account (`SHEET_TEAM_KINDS`).
- An account is read by its role, whatever its roster row says. A superintendent whose roster row is kind sub is a teammate, as `isRosterSub` already rules.
- A helper is still refused, since the form offers none. The refusal names what the step offers and where a helper's pay is written.
- The bench rule holds for the Subcontractors list alone. The form benches a sub, not a teammate.
- The roster row a teammate's sheet is tied to is one that still stands, since the name trigger (`resolve_pay_person_id`) reads no archived row.

## What the plan says for a teammate

- `person.account_role` and `person.offered_by` name the role and the Office Team group.
- The warning says the sheet is their own, owed as sub labor on top of their wages, and that no payroll screen reads a sheet.
- With no roster row, the plan says no assignee row would tie the sheet. It also says the Pay ledger reads a name it cannot find on the roster as an outside sub, so the sheet would not read as *Team · sub labor* until People gives them a row.

## What did not change

`change`, `remove` and the payment pair carry no person gate. They name a sheet, and read whose it is through `sheetSentTo`, which has ruled a teammate's own sheet theirs since v2.7428.
