# v2.4725 — Lien desk: the owner's cover letter names the supply houses still owed (2026-10-06)

**The ask (Taunya, 2026-10-06, a screenshot of job 273's Supply houses card under the notice):** "I want to be able to include the supply house amounts in the cover letter." Stephen added the message the office had just sent a debtor by hand — the house will be noticing separately, its balance, the day it told us, not covered by our conditional release, not in our figure, paying us clears it. Three lenses, mock-up (https://claude.ai/artifact/EyKmS86NApfASP3Ar2TMTP: the tick, the letter page, the paragraph's shapes, the critique), the *is this the best we can do?* pass, the owner's question *what if we don't know the date?* answered with two more shapes, then "build it all" (the conditional release of lien is PR 2).

**The gap.** The owner reads our claim as the whole debt on the job; the house's own notice then surprises them and our release looks like it should have covered it. Counsel's letter knew nothing about the houses; the office typed the paragraph into an email afterward. The desk's card already held every figure and date that email used.

## Decisions

- A paragraph in the letter, not a second page; on by default with a tick out, because forgetting to say it was the failure. The house's figure when it gave one. One kernel beside *Copy for an email* so the owner and the office read the same facts. Not on the GC's copy (counsel's bare form). No free-text box.
- Counsel's rule of one number per letter is bent, so the amber line stays until counsel reads the words.
