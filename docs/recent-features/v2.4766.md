# v2.4766 — Lien desk: nothing shows through above the Deadlines date row (2026-10-07)

Client only. The owner (2026-10-06, Safari), on a screenshot of the Deadlines view: *there is a gap in the text here* — a strip between the pill row and the date row, 12 px or so, holding the bottom of a GC row and the today line, nothing a reader could use.

## Why

The date row (the find box, the 15ths, Owed) was a `position: sticky` row at the top of the list's scroll box, and the bucket bars stuck under it at `top: 40`. Chrome and headless WebKit lay that out exactly; a live Safari can draw a sticky row a frame behind the scroll, and in that frame the rows under it show above it. The screenshot's strip was that: the scroll box's own content, above its sticky row.

A second strip of the same kind showed in every browser: a bucket bar being pushed out by its section's end sat half under the date row until the next bar took its place.

## What changed

- `LienDeskCalendarTab`: the date row is a fixed row between the toolbar and the list (`flex: none`, `overflow: hidden`), no longer inside the scroll box, so nothing the list scrolls can be above it. `scrollbar-gutter: stable` on that row and on the list keeps the axis columns aligned when the list draws a scrollbar (a Windows machine, or a Mac set to always show them).
- The bucket bars stick at `top: 0`. Each section ends with a `BUCKET_SPACER` of one bar's height (`BAR_H`, 39 px) and pulls the next section up by the same (`BUCKET_SECTION`), so a bar stays at the top until the next bar arrives and covers it — the incoming bar slides over the outgoing one. Padding would not do it: a sticky box is held inside its parent's content box. The phone board's sections take the same spacer.
- Measured in Chrome and in Playwright's WebKit: the date row's bottom is the scroll box's top; This month's bar sits at 0 while Next month's bar comes up from 18 and covers it; the list's scroll height is unchanged and the last section ends on the box's edge.

## Tests

- `LienDeskCalendarTab.render.test.tsx` and `LienDeskModal.render.test.tsx` pass unchanged (jsdom does not lay out sticky boxes; the check was in the browsers).
