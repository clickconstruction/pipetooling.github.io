# v2.5805 — Quickfill: the Percent complete card's bar is the slider for the % done (2026-10-23)

Punch list #154, from the owner's session the same afternoon: *Quickfill Percent complete: a slider to set the % done. Come up with a mock up of how you think this could work and then ask yourself is this the best we can do and then build it.* The mock-up and the critique are in [`to-dos/quickfill-percent-slider/`](../../to-dos/quickfill-percent-slider/README.md): Today, **A** a plain slider under the box, **B** the card's own bar as the slider with the stage ends as stops, **C** the stops alone; the pick is B.

- **The bar is the slider** (`QuickfillPercentBarSlider.tsx` over `StagesStageBar`, which is untouched): the Pipeline's dark tick stays where the job is today; a blue knob rides the same bar at the number about to be saved. A press anywhere on the 44 px band over the bar moves the knob there, a drag follows the pointer (`touch-action: pan-y` leaves the page's scroll alone on a phone), and the arrow keys walk it: Shift and the Page keys by ten, Home and End to the ends. The knob's place for a percent is the Pipeline's `tickLeftPct`; a pointer's place becomes a percent through its inverse, the new pure `pctFromTickLeft` in `lib/jobs/jobMoneyBar.ts` (tested as the inverse on every block, the floored block included), so the knob and the tick always agree on where a number sits.
- **The stops** (`percentStops` in `lib/quickfill/percentComplete.ts`, on the card as `stops`): one chip per stage end, Deposit left out, on the same share the ticks use (a stage's price over the bid when the bid is known), a running total rounded as the box would hold it (*Rough ✓ 40%*, *Top Out 80%*, *Trim 100%*: the Pipeline's short names); then *Paid N%* when the paid share is not already a stage end. A lines job has only the paid share. The chip the knob sits on reads pressed. Each stop is also a small stub hanging from the bar's bottom edge, and a knob let go within two points of one lands on it (`snapPercentToStops`, `PERCENT_SNAP_POINTS = 2`): on release only, never during the drag, and never from the keyboard, so the arrow keys still step by one past a stop.
- **The ticked share is the bar's own share now**: a stage's share is the row's view segment's, the one the Pipeline's tick walks (`tickLeftPct`), so the suggestion and every stop put the tick exactly at the end of a stage's block. v2.5764 divided a stage's price by the bid, which is the same number whenever the bid equals the stage prices' sum (every live card checked, 892 among them) and a different one on a discounted bid or a job with a plain line beside its stages, where it landed the suggestion short of or past the stage's block. The tolerance rules, the cases and the paid share (payments over the bid) are unchanged; the kernel test holds a discounted-bid case (a $3,000 discount moves Rough's end from 40 to 37 the old way, and leaves it at 37.5 the bar's way).
- **The box stays** for an exact number; the knob follows it while it holds one, and a half-typed box leaves the knob where it was. A stop or the knob writes the box.
- **The move line** (`percentMove`): *0% → 57% ▲ 57*, *75% → 60% ▼ 15*, *No % → 40%* on a job with no percent yet, and *40% · no change* when the knob sits where the job already is, which also makes **Save** dead. Save names the number otherwise, as before.
- **The card is one column now**: the head, the bar, the numbers, the sentence, the hint (*Drag the knob, press a stage, or type.*), then one action row: the stops on the left, and the move, the box, Save and Dismiss as one unit on the right that wraps under the stops when the row is short, and wraps again on a phone.
- **Unchanged**: the save (the Pipeline box's own note and column), Dismiss for one week, the roles, the header's count, the four cases.
- **Not reused, and why**: the Legal desk's settlement floor (`LegalDeskModal.tsx`) and v2.5760's `AccountWriteDownPanel.tsx` each draw an inline native `<input type="range">` with `accentColor` beside their boxes, not a shared component, and a native range maps its track linearly, so it could not sit on the stage bar, whose blocks are floored to a minimum width (`tickLeftPct` walks the money shares block by block). The pattern is the same (the box and the slider move together in whole numbers) and the knob is drawn by hand. Nothing on the card is a focused number input, so a wheel over it scrolls the page (the v2.5765 lesson); the box stays `type="text"` as before.
- **Words**: the guide *fix a job's percent done in Quickfill* § What the list shows and § Fixing the number; the Quickfill guide's station line; the station's intro line. `docs/GLOSSARY.md`, `docs/QUICKFILL_ARCHITECTURE.md` and `docs/PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` amended.

## Is this the best we can do?

The to-do's README holds the full critique. In short: the facts and the answer are one picture; the two numbers an assistant jumps to most, a stage's end and the paid share, are one press each; nothing is written that the person has not read; nothing new on the server. Left out on purpose: a note box beside the slider (the move line is the note the save writes) and snapping the knob to a stage end as it passes (a knob that jumps mid-drag fights the hand; the snap is on release only, within two points, and the chips reach any stop in one press). Inherited from the tick: on a job with a paid Deposit stage the suggested number leaves the deposit out, so the knob at the suggestion sits a little short of the stage block's end, exactly where the Pipeline's own tick sits for that number today.
