## What changed

- **The door joins the verdict band.** `LienTimelineStrip` takes `nextDoor?: ReactNode` and draws it at the right of the verdict band (`data-lien-timeline-verdict-door`); the band is a flex row now, its words in a column at the left. The Lien window passes its `nextStepButton` there on a computer, and the *Your next step* box (`data-lien-window-next-step`) draws only on a phone (whose folded steps have no verdict band) or when there is no timeline.
- **Two chips, one line** (`data-lien-window-chips`, `bandChip`): *Waivers are their own paper ›* (`data-lien-window-waivers`, `onOpenRelease`) and the supply house with `lienSupplierMark(job).words` (`data-lien-window-supply`), plus *Details ∨* at the right (`data-lien-window-details`, `bandDetailsOpen`). The supply-house card (`LienJobSuppliersCard`, unfolded) draws under the chips only while Details is open; a phone keeps the old sentence and the folded card.
- The tab row is untouched: the window's header does not scroll, so the tabs and § Rules were always in view; what the fold gives back is the paper's height.
