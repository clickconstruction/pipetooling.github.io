# v2.4600 — Procurement log: orders as cards on a phone (2026-10-05)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. PR 4 of the procurement log redraw (*Procurement log: orders on a calendar*; its card was PR #4527, closed unmerged once the train shipped): section 9 of the spec, the last of its four PRs after [v2.4581](v2.4581.md), [v2.4587](v2.4587.md) and [v2.4592](v2.4592.md). The spec did not draw this one; it gave the rule.

## What changed

Whenever the log is narrower than its table (a phone, a tablet, a side pane), the **To order** lens draws the same five sections and the same orders as the wide screen, as cards.

- **A section** is a grey band: its tick, its name, its count and its note, with the fold.
- **An order is a card**: its tick, then its name and count on the fold, its note under them, and its right-hand words under that. *Order by 10/13 · in 8 days* **2**, then *EWC-1 · Trim Set, needed 11/17 · 5 wk lead*, then **Mark ordered…**.
- **No calendar on a card.** Its fact is said in words (`groupDateWords`): how far off an order-by date is, in the card's name (*in 8 days*, *tomorrow*, *3 days ago*); *arrives 10/29, 9 days late* on an order placed; *answer by 11/10* on a fixture that waits; *✓ On site 10/15*.
- **A part is a short card under its order**: the product, then *Qty* and its one door (*Dates…*, *Enter their answer…*, or the sent-back note with **Pick another product** and *Change their answer…*). The dates editor opens under it as before.
- **Mark ordered…** opens its form under the card.
- The calendar's key, and its *No dates to draw yet.*, stay with the wide table. A phone has no calendar to explain.

## What went with it

The old To order grouping had no reader left once the phone drew orders: `procurementSections(rows, 'to_order')`'s branch, `foldByHouse`, `houseFoldNote`, the `HouseFold` type, the panel's house folds and their tests are removed. `procurementSections` still groups By tag and By house.

## How it is built

- `procurementOrders.ts`: `groupDateWords`.
- `SubmittalProcurementPanel.tsx`: the orders branch draws cards when `narrow`, from the same `orders`, `isOpen`, `groupRight` and `markForm` the table uses; `renderRow`'s `order` option draws the part's card when `narrow`.
