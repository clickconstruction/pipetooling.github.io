# v2.3550 — Labor tab decomposition: the Labor book panel is its own component (2026-09-17)

Client only, behavior-preserving. PR 3 of the Pricing train in `to-dos/engineering-hygiene.md` — region L6 of [`docs/BIDS_PRICING_LABOR_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md`](../BIDS_PRICING_LABOR_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md).

## What moved

[`BidsLaborBookPanel.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/BidsLaborBookPanel.tsx) — the collapsible **Labor book** section (the book chips, each with its rename pencil, *Add book*, and the browsed book's entries table with its task and per-100-ft marks and alias line), plus the two dialogs that belong to it: the New / Edit book form with its guarded Delete, and the entry form with *Reads as* / *Hours are per* and the three stage-hour boxes.

Props are grouped the way the Pipeline's menus are: **`book`** (what is listed and the four doors), **`versionForm`** and **`entryForm`** (each dialog's state and its submit / close / delete). Every write stays in the tab — `saveLaborVersion`, `deleteLaborVersion`, `saveLaborEntry`, `deleteLaborEntry` and the `getOrCreateFixtureTypeId` call inside them are untouched, and the panel only reports.

**Two things deliberately stayed behind.** The **add-missing-fixture dialog**, which the map files under L6, belongs to the apply-hours flow: saving from it re-runs `applyLaborBookHoursToEstimate`, so it stays beside the HOURS section it serves. And the **bid's applied book** (`selectedLaborBookVersionId`) is never touched from this panel — the panel reads `browsedVersionId` only, keeping the two selections separate exactly as map quirk 15 requires.

One shape change, forced by TypeScript and noted in the file: the delete buttons sit behind an `editing && …` guard, and a guard on an object property does not narrow inside an async callback the way a plain variable does. The component reads `versionForm.editing` and `entryForm.editing` into two consts at the top, which is the same truth the inline guards had.

Numbers: `BidsLaborTab.tsx` 2,155 → 1,940 lines.
