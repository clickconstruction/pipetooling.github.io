## Why this cut

The map's order of 2026-09-27 ended at 3,583 lines with two cuts held: the solver strip and the grid rows. Features since then took the tab to 4,586 lines, and the largest block they added that owns its own state is *What the price is made of* (punch list #128, v2.5660): the bid's material markup, each row's kind, tags and split, the Markup and Tax tools, the tag box, the markup cell and the open splits. It shares none of the solver's eighteen values, so the hold does not cover it. Its numbers were written inline and had no test of their own.

## What shipped

- **`src/lib/bids/rowMarksEdits.ts`** (pure, 38 tests): every number the block writes. `sovFactorFromMarkup` (a 35% markup saves the factor 1.35, to four places), `readRowMarkupText` / `rowMarkupPreviewOf` / `rowMarkupCommit` (the markup cell: percent with one decimal, empty clears, a negative or a word writes nothing, the markup already on the row writes nothing), `withSplitMarkup` / `withSplitTaxable`, both tools' Undo lists and their way back, `putBackFailedRowMarks`, `seedRowMarks`, and the open splits' key, read and toggle.
- **`src/hooks/usePricingRowMarks.ts`**: the ten state values, the four effects, the ref and the twelve handlers, moved as they were. The hook takes the bid, the open version's count rows, the lock and the tab's `guardPricingWrite`, and returns 25 values.
- **`BidsPricingTab.tsx`**: calls the hook where the block stood and destructures its values under the names the JSX already read, so no line of the render changed. 4,440 lines (was 4,586): 146 out. 67 `useState` (was 77), 9 effects (was 13).

## What stays as it was

- **The effects' order.** The block's four effects were contiguous, between the drawer's offer effect and the solver's stashed-preview effect. The hook is called at that spot, so they run in the same order among the tab's.
- **The lock.** Kind and split wait behind the sent-bid lock through `guardPricingWrite`; tags skip it; the chip's preview does nothing on a locked bid.
- **The writes.** One `bids_count_rows` update per row through `saveRowMarks`, a failed row put back as it stood with its reason, the not-there-yet note shown once; one `bids` update for the bid's factor, then `onPatchBid`.
- **The JSX.** The two tool buttons sit in the solver strip and the split's child rows in the grid, the two cuts that are held. They were not touched.
