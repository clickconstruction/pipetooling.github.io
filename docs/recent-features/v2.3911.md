## Why

The map's Stage-A inventory had four items left after the save and dirty kernels: the strip-Y → time math inlined three times in the pointer handlers, the `clock_sessions` column list written twice (v2.3676 had to add `quick_add_minutes` to both), and two small lib files the editor leans on with no tests. All four want to be settled before the gesture engine or the data engine moves into a hook.

## What shipped

- **[`src/lib/myTimeDayTimeline.ts`](../../src/lib/myTimeDayTimeline.ts)** gains `clusterStripRangeMs(c, nowMs)` (first clock-in to last clock-out, or now while open), `clampStripY`, `stripYToMs` (tap and Alt-click) and `stripDragYToMs` (the relative-delta boundary drag). The modal's `pointerMoveRef`, `stripTapEndRef` and `handleStripPointerDown` bodies call them; `startDrag` takes its grab Y from `clampStripY`. Same arithmetic, same order.
- **[`src/lib/clockSessionSelect.ts`](../../src/lib/clockSessionSelect.ts)** gains `CLOCK_SESSION_DAY_EDITOR_SELECT`; the day-fetch effect and `fetchDaySessionsForEditor` both read it.
- **The modal** is 3,835 → 3,829 lines.
- **Tests** — `myTimeDayTimeline.stripGeometry.test.ts` (12): linear top to bottom, a pointer off the strip lands on its ends, a strip with no height maps to the start, a handle grabbed off-center does not jump, the travel moves the boundary. `clockSessionSelect.test.ts` (1): the list is exactly the fields of `DayEditorSession`. `forceClockOutDefaultOut.test.ts` (7, fake clock): the start of the current minute on the Central wall clock, never after now, a clock-in ahead of now closes at now. `peopleHoursManualDraftSession.test.ts` (7): 8:00 AM Central through the entered hours in standard and daylight time, a fresh draft id, a note so an untouched draft can save, negative hours as zero length.
- **The map** marks the three inventory rows and step 1 done, amends quirk 17 and gains quirk 19.
