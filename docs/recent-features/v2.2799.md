- **`docs/forms/w9-2024-03.schema.json`**: the shipped FormSchema for IRS Form W-9 (Rev. March 2024) — 22 boxes: line 1 name (required, prefilled from the roster), line 2 business name, the seven classification checkboxes as one required exactly-one group (LLC letter, Other, 3b, exempt / FATCA codes, account numbers marked *rarely needed*), address + city / state / ZIP (required), the requester block as a constant ("Click Plumbing and Electrical, 12925 FM 20, Kingsbury, TX 78638"), SSN (`###-##-####`) and EIN (`##-#######`) as sensitive masked digits in a required one-of set with each segment bound to its IRS field, signature and date drawn on the Sign Here line. Labels and help in English and Español. Drafted with `forms:draft`, finished by hand, previewed with `forms:preview`.
- **Data (prod, 2026-09-04)**: the form was created in the Form Studio from the IRS PDF, the schema imported from this file, published into the **Subs** packet as **W-9** (paperwork type `w9`, audience `sub`). Existing Subs-packet assignees see a W-9 placeholder row on People → Contracts to send; new assignments mint an unsent copy that resolves to the form via the trigger.
- **End-to-end verified on prod (2026-09-04)**: sent the W-9 to the test sub through the Agreements panel's Send to… (the paper email went out from Click Plumbing and Electrical), opened the link, filled name (prefilled from the roster), classification, address, city / state / ZIP, and an SSN, typed the signature, submitted. Result row: `status = signed`, `doc_type = w9` (stamped by the trigger), `form_source = portal`, `form_values` holds the four non-sensitive answers, `form_hints = { ssn: "6789" }`, `form_pdf_storage_path` set and the 152 KB flattened PDF present in `contract-form-pdfs`.
