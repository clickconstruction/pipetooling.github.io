# v2.5388 — Legal portal: counsel sees what the post office said about each mailed notice (Docsmit train, PR 8) (2026-10-13)

PR 8 of the Docsmit train (`docs/DOCSMIT_LIEN_RUN_PLAN.md`, *Delivery drives the desk* → *Counsel's packet assembles itself*, D12). The firm still sees only what the owner allows.

**The function** (`legal-portal`, `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`): each matter carries `mailPieces`, the `mail_pieces` rows behind its filings (by the job, or by `source_ids` overlapping the matter's filing ids), cut by `shapeMailPieces` (`_shared/legalMatterShape.ts`) to when USPS accepted each, the delivery and who signed, the provider's last word, and the Certification PDF as a 15-minute signed link; never the address as sent, the price, the events or a path. `shapeFilingSends` keeps `via` and `piece_id`. Until the table exists the list is empty and the portal still opens.

**The firm's envelope** (`src/lib/legal/legalLienPaper.ts`): `buildLegalEnvelopes` takes `mailPieces`; each send finds its piece by id, else the piece on its filings for its recipient, and carries `mail` (`LegalSendMail`: the Central days, who signed, the tracking, the link). `sendMailWords`: *mailed Oct 13 through Docsmit · delivered Oct 16, signed R SMITH*, *returned to sender*, *notice left, unclaimed so far*, *in transit*, *sent to Docsmit, not yet accepted by USPS*. `envelopeWentOutWords` adds it, per recipient when they differ; `envelopeCertificationLinks` lists the PDFs, and the printed packet (`legalFirmPacketPrint.ts`) links each as *Certification (owner of record)*. The payload (`legalPortalPayload.ts`) and the packet input (`legalPacket.ts`) carry `mailPieces`; the office's own packet preview passes none yet (6b).

**The affidavit's served day** (`_shared/mailDocsmitRefresh.ts`, § 53.055): a filed affidavit's copy mailed through the provider (`lien_affidavit_copy`) serves it the day USPS accepted it, written to `served_at` once, beside the `sends[]` number.

**Tests**: two in `legalLienPaper.test.ts`, one in `legalMatterShape.test.ts`, one in `mailPoll.run.test.ts`.
