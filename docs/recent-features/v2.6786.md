# v2.6786 — Research: Who pulled it, the card behind a permit's name (punch list #292, PR 4) (2026-11-09)

The owner's question on the morning after the ledger went live: the feed names a permit's contact in one line, "a person, a company", and nothing more. The card behind the name answers it from three places the app can read without a key or with one it already has.

## What changed

- **Migration `20261109130000_permit_lookups.sql`**: `permit_lookups` (kind company | parcel, the lookup key, what was asked, found, the facts as JSON, the error, when and by whom), one row per kind and key; researchers read, the function writes. The four fences applied.
- **`supabase/functions/permit-lookup`** (new) over **`_shared/permitLookup.ts`** (pure, tested from `src/lib/research/permitLookup.test.ts`): `company` asks Google Places Text Search (the New API the address autocomplete already uses, with the server-side key) for the contact's company, or the person when there is no company, biased to the service area; the result whose name keys the same is an exact match, else Google's first is a loose one the card flags. `parcel` asks Bexar County's own parcel layer (`maps.bexar.org`, the `Parcels` MapServer, no key) for the street address: owner, mailing address, DBA, values, year built, building area, and the appraisal district's detail page by parcel id. Both cached thirty days; a source's failure is cached with its reason too. A researcher's JWT and the archived-caller guard.
- **`contactParts`**: "Milton G. Ramirez, Synergy Construction, LLC" is a person and a company; "Luis S. Faraklas, P.E., PLLC" is one name with its suffixes; "BOSSTX INC." is a company; "Richard Kenly" is a person.
- **`WhoPulledItSheet`** (`src/components/prospects/`): the four sections (the company on Google, the property at the county, our history with the name, the city's own record with Copy and the portal door), **Look again**, and **Approach them with these facts…**, which opens the Decide sheet on Approach with the facts named. **`leadFromPermit`** takes `LeadFacts`: the lead's company name (Google's exact match or the contact's company), contact name, phone, website and office address, the owner of record in its notes; the job keeps the site address.
- The ledger's contact cell and the map card's Pulled by are presses that open the card.
