# v2.7837 — Liens: the dev seat checks a stamped scan against the lien on record, and replaces a wrong saved copy (2026-11-18)

## Why

The owner handed the dev seat the stamped stacks two county clerks returned and asked to have them filed carefully. Every lien in the stacks was on record already. `plan_lien_filing` answered each with a refusal (*One affidavit a job*), so the check had to be made by hand: the filing rows read whole, each saved copy's file facts read from Drive, each paper compared by eye.

That check found one record wrong in two ways. Its saved copy was the whole stack, every lien of the visit inside one job's folder. And its stored paper named another month than the paper the clerk stamped: the job's last day of work was moved after the paper was sworn, and the filing was recorded after that, so the record drew the later month. *Print the copies to mail* redraws the copy from the stored paper, so that copy would not have matched the filed lien.

The seat could not fix the saved copy. A filing on record took a scan only while it had none.

## What changed

- **`supabase/functions/_shared/devMcpLienFiling.ts`** (server 0.103.0):
  - A recording number that is the job's own filing answers **`on_record`**, a third verdict beside `ready` and `held`. It carries no `plan_hash` and `apply_lien_filing` refuses it in words.
  - `lienFilingRecordCheck(p, record)` reads `paper_states` against the record: the row's `amount`, `county` and `filed_at`, and `workMonthsWords`, `noticesSentWords`, `propertyAddress`, `legalDescription` and `ownerName` of its `fields` snapshot. `daysInWords` reads the days a paper writes out. The reply carries `paper_on_record`, `agrees`, `differences` and `not_checked`. A difference in the paper's words adds `copy_to_mail`.
  - `replace_saved_copy: true` with `replace_because` (10 to 300 characters) makes the entry `ready` on a filing that carries a saved copy: mode `replace_scan` or `replace_link`, the write `attach_scan` or `attach_link` with `replaces`, the link the plan read. Without the flag the same call is a check, never a write.
  - `LIEN_FILING_FILING_COLS` gains `amount` and `fields`.
  - `lienFilingPlanReply` counts the entries on record, and how many of them differ.
  - `lienFilingAppliedWords` says where the old file went (`old_copy`), and what to send when a replace did not land.
  - `savedCopyFileWords` words the saved copy's file beside the scan sent.
- **`supabase/functions/dev-mcp/index.ts`**:
  - The plan reads a saved copy's file for an entry on record or a replace (`saved_copy_file`), through the intake service account on a read-only token, as `read_drive_folder` reads one. It is added after the hash.
  - The link's write is held to the copy the plan read: `document_url=eq.<the old link>` for a replace, the empty link as before for an added copy.
  - After a replace the old file goes to Drive's trash through `drive-link`'s `trash` door (v2.7699), only once the filing reads back with the new link. The door takes a file in the job's Sent by us folder that no lien record names. A file it leaves is named with its reason.
  - The call log keeps `replace_saved_copy` and `replace_because`.
- **`_shared/devMcpLienService.ts`**: `read_lien_filings` names the check and the replace in its `writes` line.

## Not changed

No screen, no migration, no new verb. No verb changes a recorded paper's words: a record that differs from its filed paper is named, and the way to a matching copy today is a photocopy of the stamped scan.

## Deploy

`supabase functions deploy dev-mcp` from `main` after the merge. `whoami` answers 0.103.0.
