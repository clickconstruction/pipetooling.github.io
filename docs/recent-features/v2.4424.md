# v2.4424 — Submittals: the Edit window fits the screen, and "no house" opens on its part (2026-10-02)

No migration, no edge function. Wendi, 2026-10-02, on BP375's procurement log: "i click on the “no house” and it doesn’t take me to that part that isnt assigned to a house" and, of the Edit window, "this window is cut off, this is as high as i can scroll up". One cause for both.

## What was wrong

[`SubmittalItemEditDialog`](../../src/components/bids/SubmittalItemEditDialog.tsx) drew a backdrop that centred its window (`align-items: center`) and scrolled itself (`overflow-y: auto`). A window taller than the screen, centred, sticks out above the top edge by half the difference, and a scroll box cannot scroll above its own start. So the top of the window was out of reach.

Since v2.4319 a row lists its parts, about 130 px each, and since v2.4354 a Procure line opens the window on the part tapped. Reproduced on BP375 at 1440 × 900 before the fix: EWC-1 has seven parts, the window was 1,394 px tall and its top sat 247 px above the screen. The part tapped (*ELKAY LZSTL8WSLK*, the first part and the one with no house) was ringed and its house box had the cursor, at −129 px to −41 px. The focus worked; the part could not be seen.

## What changed

- **The window is held to the screen.** The backdrop no longer scrolls. The window takes `max-height: 100%` of it (the screen less the 1 rem margin and the phone's safe areas). Its title block and its Save row are pinned, and the fields between them scroll (`data-testid="edit-row-body"`). Save and Cancel are always in reach on a long row.
- **The part tapped is brought into view.** `SubmittalPartsEditor`'s `scrollIntoView` now has a box that can scroll to it. No change to that file.
- **A row with no parts: its house cell opens on the Supply house box.** The log's house button says so (`onOpenItem({ …, house: true })`), the tab keeps it on `editFocus`, and the window takes `focusHouse`. The line's name and the *Open it* door on a row with no product leave the cursor alone.
- [`BIDS_DOCUMENT_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md`](../BIDS_DOCUMENT_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md) SU-11 says how the window is laid out.

## Tests

- `SubmittalItemEditDialog.render.test.tsx` (+2, +1 line): the title and the buttons sit outside the body that scrolls and every field inside it, the window is held to the backdrop and the backdrop does not scroll; `focusHouse` puts the cursor in Supply house; the ringed part is inside the scrolling body.
- `SubmittalProcurementPanel.render.test.tsx`: the house button passes `house: true`; the line's name does not.
