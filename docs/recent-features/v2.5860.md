# v2.5860 — A written-down bill trims its fees with it (2026-10-25)

Punch list #105, the write-down cap (`to-dos/bill-riders-survive-delete-and-split.md`, "After #5255" item 1 — the last build on that to-do; gap 1 shipped v2.5144, gap 2 v2.5140). Migration [`20261025170000_rider_cap_at_bill_amount.sql`](../migrations/20261025170000_rider_cap_at_bill_amount.md). No edge function.

## Why

A rider — a returned check fee, a GC card fee, a turnaway trip charge — is a `fee_lines` entry on its bill, and every rewrite of the job's total adds the entries back (`job_rider_fees`, `riderFeeLineCents`). `apply_agreed_write_down_to_billed_invoice` lowers the bill's amount but not its entries. On a trip charge's bill the bill *is* the charge: write a $250 trip charge down to $100 and the next money edit put the whole $250 back in the job's total, while the waterfall attributed $250 to a $100 bill. Found in helper 12's review of #5255.

## What shipped

- **`job_rider_fees`** (the migration): each bill's rider entries are summed and capped at the bill's amount (`greatest(round(amount, 2), 0)`). A bill whose amount is null keeps the uncapped count; a bill that covers its riders reads as before. No revenue rewritten — the next rewrite of each job writes the capped figure, as every rewrite does.
- **`riderFeeLineCents`** (`src/lib/jobs/arReturnCaseFee.ts`), the client twin, caps per bill the same way: a bill whose amount does not read as a number keeps the uncapped count. `jobFormRiderFeesDollars` (Edit Job's total), the segments waterfall and `returnedCheckFeeCents` all read through it.

## Proof

- The revenue riders bed (`scripts/pgtest-revenue-riders.sh`) applies the migration twice and gains nine checks: the full count while the bill holds it, the capped count after a write-down, the rewrite writing the capped figure, two riders capping together, a bill at zero, a null amount, a bill above its riders, and job D unchanged.
- `arReturnCaseFee.test.ts` gains a describe block: the cap on a number and a string amount, per-bill independence, the unreadable-amount fallback, zero and negative amounts, and `returnedCheckFeeCents` capping the same way.
