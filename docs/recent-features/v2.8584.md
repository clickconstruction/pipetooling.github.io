# v2.8584 — Accounts Receivable: the offer after an Apply lands the pane too (2026-11-25)

Punch list #76, read in the code on 2026-11-25 by the docs pass. Not seen on the live site. The card is closed: this is a leftover of v2.8552. No migration, no edge function.

**The fault.** v2.8552 made the pane land on the next case after a case closes, so it never falls to the first deposit, whose **Apply** is live. It gave that landing to the pane's own three closes. A fourth press closes a case: the offer shown right after an Apply, *It made the check good — close the case* (`offerMadeGood` in `BankPaymentsModal.tsx`). It did not land.

**What that did.** The offer stays up while the person works on. If they opened the case under **Came back** and then pressed the offer, the pane held a case that had just left the list. The list's next read then picked the first deposit by itself.

**The mend, and where it differs from the brief.** The brief asked for the landing on every close by the offer. That would also move a person off a deposit: after **Apply & next** the pane is on the next deposit, and the offer's close would pull them to a case. So the landing is held to the case it is about (`arOfferCloseMovesPane` in `src/lib/jobs/arReturnCase.ts`):

- The pane is on the case the offer closes: it lands as after any other close, on the next case, or holds nothing when none is left.
- The pane is on anything else, a deposit or another case: it stays.

Whether the offer's close should move a person off a deposit is a question nobody has put to the owner. The pick here is no.
