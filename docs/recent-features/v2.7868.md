# v2.7868 — Painted maps: every map starts on a job, and picking the job is one press (2026-11-18)

Punch list #392, step 4, on the owner's word of 2026-11-18 to build every step. The owner's second rule for the card: every map is tied to a job, and picking the job is easy. Until now a map started with no job open landed on no job, with a small **Pick the job it is for** that opened a bare search.

## What changed

- **Which job is it for?**, `PaintAMapJobPicker.tsx` over `src/lib/paintedMap/paintedMapJobPicker.ts`. Three parts, in the order a person on site is likely to need them:
  - **Today**: the jobs on the person's schedule today, in order of start, with the time.
  - **Lately**: the jobs they clocked onto in the last 45 days, newest first, up to six, with *yesterday*, a weekday or the day.
  - **Or search every job**: the app's own job search, billing-only jobs left out.
- **Who may paint on a job.** The tables' own rule is `on_site_can_touch_job`: the person can read the job, or is on its team. `paintedMapJobPickerIo.ts` reads both halves in two batched selects. A job that fails it is left out of Today and Lately, and is listed grey in the search with *You are not on this job, so you cannot paint a map for it.* It takes no press.
- **The start sheet** (`PaintAMapStartSheet.tsx`). Opened with no job it asks the question first, and the sheet is drawn once a job is pressed. *For* leads the sheet in a box with the job, its address and **Change the job**, which opens the picker over the sheet so what was typed is kept. A job in a development keeps its two chips. *Leave it on no job* and the small job search are gone. The paragraph about drawing by hand is gone too: the sheet is 775 tall at a phone's width, so **Draw it by hand** and **Start with a place** need no scroll.
- **Every door.** The Dashboard's press with nothing known, the header's search and the Documents page's tab all open the same sheet, so all three ask the job first.
- **Move it to another job…** in the map's window, beside *Rename this map*, for whoever painted the map and the office, on a map that is on a job. **Put it on a job…** on a map on no job opens the same picker. A move the database refuses closes the picker and says why on the map.
- **One way onto no job is kept**, and it is a change from the card's call 3. With no signal the lists cannot be read. The picker draws the retry panel, reads again by itself when the signal returns, and offers **Paint now and pick the job later**. That map waits for **Put it on a job…**, as maps on no job always have.
