## What changed in the lift

- **A stage may have no days.** A stage line has a window only after the office plans it on the Stage Plan, so `window` may be null. That stage reads *We have not planned our days for this stage.* with **Plan our days**, it is asked about by nobody, and it sorts after the stages that have days. This row is the build's own. The card's table has ten rows and the kernel has eleven.
- **The GC's page reads two switches.** `sharedBullets(stage, { sharesStageDates, showsWhatWeNeed })` returns nothing with either off. It returns only bullets on them, whatever a row of ours says.
- **The ask points to their page only when told.** `askText(site, stages, { page, wastedTrip })`: the line *Or tap to answer on your page.* is off by default, since nothing reaches the GC before PR 9. The wasted-trip sentence is off until the owner writes it (call 5).
- **The hold note lists what must be true** as numbered lines, the way the ask does. The spike joined them with semicolons, which the plain-words rules refuse.
- **The line under a bullet is sentences**, with its values marked: *Asked Nov 14 by text. No answer yet. They say by Nov 26.*
- `byTrouble`, `readyMarks`, `siteBanner`, `saidNo`, `settledWords` and `toLookAt` were drawn inline in the spike's page. They are rules, so they moved to the kernel.
- The marker functions are not here. They are the shared ones from v2.7985.
