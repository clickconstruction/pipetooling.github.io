# v2.4127 — The bill email comes from Click Plumbing and Electrical (2026-09-29)

No migration. Three edge functions redeployed: `send-stripe-invoice`, `send-contract-for-signature`, `send-estimate-to-customer`. Punch list #53 (`to-dos/customer-email-sender-name.md`), PR 1 of 3 — the owner's ask of 2026-09-28: *"help me come up with a plan to make the sender name Click Plumbing and Electrical"*, raised when v2.4020 made the bill email ours and the payer's inbox began reading **ClickTooling &lt;team@noreply.clicktooling.com&gt;**.

## What changed

- **One mailbox helper** — [`_shared/mailboxWithName.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/mailboxWithName.ts): `mailboxAddress` (the address out of `Name <addr>`), `mailboxDisplayName` (bare when RFC 5322 atext allows it, quoted with `\` / `"` escaped otherwise — *"Click Plumbing, LLC"*), `mailboxWithName(name, mailbox)`. Dependency-free; 12 tests in `src/lib/mailboxWithName.test.ts`, one of them asserting it builds exactly what the two senders' inline regex built.
- **`COMPANY_EMAIL_FROM`** in [`_shared/emailFrom.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/emailFrom.ts) = `mailboxWithName(PORTAL_COMPANY.name, EMAIL_FROM)` → `Click Plumbing and Electrical <team@noreply.clicktooling.com>`. The `EMAIL_FROM` secret is untouched; only the display name differs, so SPF/DKIM are the verified address's as before.
- **`sendEmailViaResend` takes `options.from`** ([`_shared/resendSendEmail.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/resendSendEmail.ts)), defaulting to `EMAIL_FROM`; `email_send_log.from_email` records whichever was used.
- **`send-stripe-invoice`** sends the bill email (`stripe_bill`) and every copy (`stripe_bill_copy`, the test copy included) as `COMPANY_EMAIL_FROM`.
- **`send-estimate-to-customer`** and **`send-contract-for-signature`** swap their inline `/<([^>]+)>/` for `mailboxWithName` — the estimate keeps its per-trade name (*Click Plumbing* / *Click Electrical* / *Click Plumbing and Electrical*), the contract its company name; what they send is byte-for-byte what they sent.
- Guides *turn a bill into a Stripe bill*, *send a bill to more than one person* and *ready to bill (Pipeline)* say the bill and its copies come from Click Plumbing and Electrical; `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` notes on the three functions.

## Design calls

- **The company name comes from `PORTAL_COMPANY.name`**, the letterhead the portal and the bill email body already print — one place to change the company's name, not a second constant.
- **The estimate email keeps its own per-trade name** rather than switching to the company's: it already does the right thing for its reader (the owner's plan, 2026-09-28).
- **Staff and team emails keep `EMAIL_FROM`** (*ClickTooling*): the team reads both kinds in one inbox and the name tells them apart.
