# v2.8364 — Green presses are a deeper green, so their white words read (2026-11-23)

Punch list #414, item 1, the sweep. The owner's word of 2026-11-23 to build the card's four app-wide things, with the mock-up of the two greens (`to-dos/small-presses-and-a-pale-green-greens.html`, a docs PR of its own) in front of him before this lands. A mechanical sweep, cut from fresh `main` and merged alone (`CLAUDE.md` → Mechanical sweeps). No migration and no edge function.

## What was there

The app's most used green, `#16a34a`, filled over a hundred presses under white words: the crew's **Is it as told?** send, Approve, Hire, Send, the settings cards' On switches, the Job Mode day press's green. White on it reads 3.30:1; small words need 4.5:1. The audit of punch list #382 measured it at 390 px on 2026-11-20.

## What changed

- `scripts/codemods/press-green-sweep.mjs`, new, the press-blue sweep's twin (v2.6218): in any object literal with a fill and white words, its own or from a same-file spread, each `#16a34a` in the fill becomes `#15803d`, and so does each `#16a34a` in the same object's borders, so the press keeps one edge. A named palette the app draws only under white words (`DAY_PRESS_COLORS`, whose `DAY_PRESS_TEXT` is white for green) takes the same rewrite on its green. In the stylesheets, a rule that fills with `#16a34a` under white words in the same block takes the same rewrite.
- Run once, and once more after `main` was merged in (a card `main` deleted, a rule it added): 137 styles in 104 `.ts`/`.tsx` files and 4 rules in `src/index.css` (`.modeRowNew`, `.jobMoneyBillIt`, `.jobMakeBillPartBtn`, `.scArrivalSend`). White words on `#15803d` read 5.02:1. It is the green the app already names `--text-green-700` in light.
- By hand, as the sweep listed it: `.jobMoneyBillIt:hover:not(:disabled)` filled with `#15803d` already, so it steps to `#166534`. And the help guides' `{{button:green|…}}` token (`src/lib/helpGuideIllustrations.ts`, an HTML string the sweep does not read) takes the same deeper fill, with its test.
- Left alone on purpose: green lines, borders, rings, dots, bars and icons with no words on them, green words, a green edge on a card, and a switch's pill with no words. `#16a34a` stays in 160 or so of those places.
- `npm run check:press-green` (`--check`) fails CI when a green press fills with `#16a34a` under white words again, beside `check:press-blue`; `CLAUDE.md`'s theme bullet names it.

## Contrast, for the record

| Fill | White words on it | Its edge on a light card | Its edge on the dark surface |
|---|---|---|---|
| `#16a34a` | 3.30:1 | 3.30:1 | 4.44:1 |
| `#15803d` | 5.02:1 | 5.02:1 | 2.92:1 |

The words are what must read. The edge is a shape and needs 3:1; on the dark surface the deeper fill's edge is a touch under that, as the press blue's is, and the words on it are what the eye reads.
