The direct-`fetch` senders swap `from: EMAIL_FROM` for `from: COMPANY_EMAIL_FROM` on the send and on the `email_send_log` line (physical invoice, test report + the auto-send, hazmat, GC statement manual + scheduled, supply-house job account, lien filing, lien release); the senders on `sendEmailViaResend` pass `options.from` (legal referral/digest, the legal recipient confirmation, the bid room link, the RFQ's three sends).

- **The guard** (`src/lib/emailCatalog.test.ts`): for every customer row's `sender`, the function's `index.ts` must name `COMPANY_EMAIL_FROM` (or build a company name with `mailboxWithName`, as the estimate does per trade) and must not send as bare `from: EMAIL_FROM` nor alias it (`const FROM = EMAIL_FROM`). A new customer email cannot ship under the software's name.
- **Client strings** that promised a copy "from ClickTooling": the Bill Customer window's Send-to line and the physical-invoice copies note (`SendRecordInvoiceModal`), the Send Email invoice confirm's *goes to … from* and copies sentences (`StripeInvoiceSendFromStripeButton`; its render test follows), and the Package-and-send button's title (`PackageAndSendBidPricingModal`) now say Click Plumbing and Electrical.
