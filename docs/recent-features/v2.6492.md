# v2.6492 — A combine moves every job link the delete would have set to null, and every soft pointer (punch list #252 PR 3) (2026-11-04)

Punch list #252 sorted every row a job combine must carry, and v2.6423, v2.6458 and v2.6460 moved them. The card read the list from `ON DELETE CASCADE` keys, so the docs pass of 2026-11-04 (#6998) found a second list it never sorted: fourteen `ON DELETE SET NULL` links to `jobs_ledger`, and the six plain-uuid job pointers the card itself had noted. After a combine each kept the source's id and the row stood with no job on it. `messages.jobs_ledger_id` was the worst of them: every text, call, email and team note tied to the source fell to the person, while the guide *combine two duplicate jobs into one* said everything real moves. A sub sheet read unlinked. A certified envelope lost its job.

## What changed

- **Migration `20261104130000_combine_moves_soft_job_links.sql`** (`docs/migrations/20261104130000_combine_moves_soft_job_links.md`): `migrate_job_ledger_costs_and_delete` restated from 20261103170000's body with one block after the money and legal rows, inside the same subtransaction and before the delete. A plain move each; none has a key that asks for a merge. `gc_projects_owner_terms_guard` restated with one gate, so the combine's own write of a project's billing job passes under `gc.combine_write` for its one statement, as `gc_link_crew_job` passes under `gc.crew_job_write`.
- **The sort**, read from prod's catalog on 2026-11-04 (`pg_constraint` with `confdeltype = 'n'`, and every uuid column named like a job with no key):

- **The words, corrected where they stood**: the guide now says the texts, calls, emails and team notes move and stay on their contacts, that the sub sheets, the certified letters and the statements of charges move, and that the AR cases and an owner's records request go too; `docs/GLOSSARY.md`'s *Combine (jobs)* entry and `docs/BILLING_FLOWS.md`'s combine line name the migration and the rows.
- **Bed** `supabase/tests/combine_rows/20_scenario.sql`: the second combine's source holds a row of every kind above (a text and a team note on Kay, a prepared text, a mail piece, a sub sheet, a History line, an indexed file, a submittal package, an owner's records request covering the source and the split job, a profit-share posting, a partnership's farm job, a deposit's Income label, a try-out verdict, a GC project's three job links, two AR cases, a rating ask, a watcher email, a schedule change, a charge adjustment and a statement of charges). Each reads on the target after the combine, the sub sheet's number text as the target's and the records request's `job_ids` with the target in the source's place; the held combine before it moves none of them, and both guard flags are off at the end. Run by hand on a local Postgres 15 on 2026-11-04 (every migration applied, pgvector stood in by a domain), 167 checks, no miss; CI runs it on the PR.
