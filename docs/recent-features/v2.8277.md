# v2.8277 — In kind: Today's sends holds a pay reminder on a job an arrangement pays (punch list #301, found on the senders' walk) (2026-11-21)

v2.8219 stopped the morning run from preparing a pay reminder for a job an in kind deal pays. The walk of the two senders on 2026-11-21 found what that left behind. The run had prepared one such reminder every morning from Nov 12 to Nov 21, and two of them were still open on Today's sends. Nothing held a pay reminder once its job went on a deal. Both open ones were held for no phone number, so no press could send them. With a number on file such a text would have been ready, and **Send all** would have texted a customer to pay a bill a credit pays down.

## What changed

- **The hold** (`src/lib/offers/offers.ts`). `payJobsOf` names the Pipeline jobs of the app's open Paid reminders, and `withInKindHolds` holds each one whose job is on a deal with `HELD_IN_KIND`: *An in kind arrangement pays this job's bills. Put this one away with Not this one.* A held row has no Send button and `readyRows` leaves it out, so **Send all** skips it.
- **The read** (`src/lib/offers/offersIo.ts`). `loadOpen` asks `jobs_ledger` which of those jobs carry `in_kind_arrangement_id` (`jobsOnDeal`), one small read and only when a pay reminder waits. The Dashboard's window, the Needs you line and Quickfill's count all load through it.
- **The guide** *work today's sends* lists the new reason under *When a text is held*.

## Decisions taken with the build

- **A hold on the window, as the other passed reasons are.** An answered review, a step already texted and a passed visit day are each held with the reason and the door. The run puts away only a text about a passed day, and it still does.
- **This hold wins over the run's own.** The two open rows said *No phone number on file. Add one on the customer.* Adding a number is not the fix, so the row says the true reason.
- **Only the app's own reminder.** A text a person wrote for the Paid step is their call, as **Remind now** on the step's card is since v2.8219.
- **A read that fails holds nothing.** The window is then no worse than before the hold, and the run prepares no new one.
- **The words live in the client's kernel**, not in `OFFER_HELD`. That list is in the edge kernel copy, and a change to it would send `messages-nudge-dispatch` back for a deploy over words only the window says.

## Not here

- **The press itself is not guarded.** A window opened before a job went on a deal still shows its reminder as ready until it reads again.
- **The two open rows stay until a person presses Not this one.** They now say why.

## Release order

Merge. No migration and no function to deploy: every file is the client's.

## Verified

Kernel tests in `src/lib/offers/offers.test.ts` (a covered job's reminder, a bill paid with money beside it, the run's own hold under it, a person's own text and no deal at all) and `offersIo.inKind.test.ts` (the read, no read with nothing waiting, a refused read and a lost signal).
