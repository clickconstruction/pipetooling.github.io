# v2.3273 — Job Summary: where the discounts went (2026-09-11)

Client only; no migration. Discount tools round two, PR 4 of 4 (mockups + review: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/f56651a5-0d7b-49c3-bdc0-6bfca39ec454). Closes the round: v2.3265 (type the total) · v2.3268 (Bill Customer strip) · v2.3272 (standing discount) · this.

## Why

The reason chips exist so this could. The first draft's table averaged a 10% discount with a flat $500 into a meaningless "Avg %" and never said the one thing an owner asks — how much of what we billed walked out the door. The review replaced it with share of revenue, per reason and per giver.

## What shipped

- **The chip opens a fold.** On Job Summary's toolbar (money view), *− $6,394.50 discounted on 4 jobs ▾* is now a button; open, `JobSummaryDiscountFold` shows a headline — *$6,394.50 given away · 3.8% of $168,400 revenue · this window · 4 of 22 jobs* — then two tables: **Reason** (jobs · given · of revenue · bar) from the rows' reason chips, legacy negative rows under *No reason given*; **Given by** (jobs · given · of their billed) from the `discount_added` trail events, which carry the dollars and the actor.
- **Kernel `jobSummaryDiscounts.ts`** (`jobSummaryDiscountLeakage`, 4 tests): revenue is the rows' shown revenue (contract or earned, the same figure the tiles use); shares are null when revenue is zero; a giver's share is of the revenue of the jobs they discounted; events for jobs not in view are ignored.
- **Reads**: the fold fetches `discount_added` events for the discounted jobs in view (chunks of 200) and the actors' names from `users`; both follow the viewer's RLS. Errors show inline and never hide the reason table.
- **The chip had never lit in production** — Job Summary loads jobs through the slim enrich path (`enrichJobsLedgerPrimaryRowsJobSummarySlim`), which left `fixtures` empty, so v2.3256's `discountUsd` was always 0 there. The slim path now batch-loads ONLY the discount rows (`line_kind = 'discount'` or a legacy negative price) into `fixtures`; nothing else on that path reads them. Found by the live check, fixed here.
