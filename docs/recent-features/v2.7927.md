# v2.7927 — The Bid Board's tools row: one height, words that fold, a row that wraps (2026-11-19)

The two things v2.7912 left for another change, and a third found while measuring them. No migration, no edge function, no new page. The owner approved the mock-up on 2026-11-19 with its three questions answered as drawn.

## What was wrong

The tools row is the search, the development filter, the ask to rate a GC when one is open, and five presses. Measured on a local preview against live data on 2026-11-19:

| Width | Ask | Before | After |
|---|---|---|---|
| 375 px | open | four lines, 172 px; controls 42, 36 and 34 px tall | three lines, 124 px; every control 36 px |
| 768 px | none | search 18 px; the page's `scrollWidth` 861 | one line, search about 202 px (computed) |
| 768 px | open | search 18 px; `scrollWidth` 1078 | two lines, search 327 px; `scrollWidth` 768 |
| 1024 px | none | search 101 px | one line, search about 410 px (computed) |
| 1024 px | open | search 18 px; `scrollWidth` 1102 | one line, search 219 px; `scrollWidth` 1024 |
| 1280 px | none | search 357 px; presses 39 px beside a 36 px search | search about 371 px (computed); every control 36 px |
| 1280 px | open | search 139 px; presses 42 px beside a 36 px search | search 475 px; every control 36 px |
| 1366 px | none | not measured | one line with every word, search 457 px |
| 1440 px | open | not measured | one line with every word, search 340 px |

The rows marked *computed* are the no-ask view at widths walked while the preview's account had an ask open. They are the measured row less the measured filter and presses. The 1366 px row is the no-ask view measured, and its presses are the widths the computed rows use.

Three causes:

- **The heights.** The ask's style opened with `font: 'inherit'`, which took the page's 16 px over the 14 px every `button` gets from the stylesheet. Its line was 42 px and stretched its neighbours. An icon press has no words, so on a phone its line was 34 px. Without the ask the presses were 39 px, from the Marked press's taller line.
- **The search.** On a desk the search was the only part of the row that could shrink, and it shrank to its 18 px of border and padding. Six worded presses are 834 px.
- **The spill.** Over 660 px the row never wrapped, so it ran past the screen under 829 px of row, and under 1046 px with the ask open. At 768 px that was true for everyone, ask or no ask.

## The four rules (`src/lib/bidBoardToolsRow.ts`)

1. **One height.** Every control is `BID_BOARD_TOOLS_HEIGHT`, 36 px: the search's height, the owner's call in v2.1709. No press pads itself top or bottom.
2. **The ask leads the presses.** It is its own part of the row, ahead of the group, in the row's 14 px type. On a phone it shares the filter's line (120 px and 178 px in a 343 px column), which is what keeps the five presses together on the next.
3. **Words fold before the search is squeezed.** `bidBoardToolsRowLayout({ phone, rowWidth, askOpen })` answers `words`. Pricing rules, Reply book and Customer review keep their words from 1117 px of row, or 1308 px with the ask open: the widths that leave the search 320 px. Under that they are 36 px squares of their icons, each with its hover line and its name for a screen reader. Customer review now draws its icon beside its words too, so folding only drops words.
4. **The row wraps, it never spills.** The row is `flex-wrap: wrap` at every width and the search's basis is 200 px on a desk. When the icons leave it less, the group takes the next line. A line the search is not on keeps its presses to the right (`justify-content: flex-end`), and the filter keeps the left with `margin-right: auto`.

The row's width is `useMeasuredWidth` on the row itself, not the window's, so a side panel that narrows the page folds the words too. `GcDifficultyAsksPress` gained `onOpenChange`, which tells the row whether the ask draws. `MarkedBidsToggle` gained a `style` prop for the row's height; its other homes pass none and draw as before.

## What the mock-up weighed

- **Heights only** left the 139 px search, the 18 px search and the sideways scroll.
- **Wrap only, never fold** gave a master with an ask open a two line row at 1280 px, and everyone a two line row under about 1100 px.
- **Fold one press at a time** mixed worded and icon presses and changed the row's shape at five widths in place of two.
- **The ask in a band above the row** stacked a second amber band over the plans band, and undid the choice made when the ask shipped.
- **A More menu for the three books** hid three presses behind a fourth.

## Seen on the walk

Only tabs were opened. Nothing was pressed. At 375, 768, 1024, 1280 and 1440 px the page's `scrollWidth` equalled its width, and at every width walked every control measured 36 px. At 320 px the page read 321: the header's gear ran past the screen, which `v2.7949.md` fixed, and the tools row ended inside it. At 320 px with an ask open the row is four lines: the 288 px column cannot hold the filter and the ask together.

## Tests

- `bidBoardToolsRow.test.ts` (15): the words at each measured width with and without an ask, the exact fold widths, a desk before its first measure, the wrap, the search's floor, the one height, the icon square, and the ask's type.
- `GcDifficultyAsk.render.test.tsx`: the press tells its row whether it draws.
- `e2e/viewport-smoke.spec.ts`: the Bid Board at 768 and 1024 px holds no sideways scroll. jsdom lays nothing out, so this is the only test that sees the spill.

## Left as it is

- A phone with no ask open keeps three lines. The filter and the five presses are 34 px too wide to share one at 375 px.
- The fold widths are measured constants. A long count on Marked or Reply book leaves the search a little under 320 px at the fold; the wrap still holds the row on screen.
- The phone's row is 36 px tall, the search's height. The ask's own sheet uses 44 px presses.
