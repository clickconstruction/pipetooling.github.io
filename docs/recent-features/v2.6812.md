# v2.6812 — The ground for a photo of the supply house ticket, taken at the counter (punch list #295, PR 1) (2026-11-09)

The owner's ask of 2026-11-09 ([`to-dos/ticket-photo/README.md`](../../to-dos/ticket-photo/README.md)): a tech takes a photo of the supply house ticket or the receipt right there in the app. Tally had no camera press on its purchase side, a card receipt never reached the office, and the charge was sorted to a job from the clock's guess. She picked the shape *snap the ticket first, match it to its charge later* and said build as drawn. This is PR 1 of four, the ground; no screen changes.

## What changed

- **`supabase/migrations/20261109190000_counter_tickets.sql`**: `counter_tickets` and `counter_ticket_pages`, their gates, the guard trigger, the private bucket `counter-tickets` and the photo-save cron's ninth clause ([`docs/migrations/20261109190000_counter_tickets.md`](../migrations/20261109190000_counter_tickets.md)).
- **`supabase/functions/photo-save/index.ts`**, a ninth source (`sweepTickets`): a saved ticket's pages are filed under **Team only › Tickets** in the job's Drive folder (`_shared/driveTicketFolder.ts` → `ticketFolderOrWhy`), named *2026-11-04 Jordan · Ticket $228.58.jpg* (`_shared/ticketNames.ts` → `ticketDriveName`). A ticket moved to another job has its Drive file moved, not copied. The bucket copy stays as the app's picture.
- **`src/lib/tickets/ticket.ts`**: the ticket's state (`ticketState`: removed, open, matched, billed, paid another way, on account, waiting), the one line each state reads as (`ticketStateWords`, held to the plain words by its test), and `takerMayChange`.
- **`src/lib/tickets/ticketMatch.ts`**: the pure matcher. `made` by `minute` (exactly one charge on a card of the taker's in the half hour before the photo) or by `total` (a typed total equal to exactly one charge within a day); `ask` the taker (one charge that fits more loosely) or the office (one charge on another person's card); `none` with the charges that fit when several do. `matchTickets` settles the made matches first so two tickets never take one charge.
- **The bed** `scripts/pgtest-counter-tickets.sh` over `supabase/tests/counter_tickets/20_scenario.sql`.

## What the feed said (read 2026-11-09 as the dev seat, 200 card charges from 2026-10-10 to 10-23)

- `mercury_transactions.created_at` is the swipe itself and is on the row while the charge is still pending, so a ticket can meet its charge the same minute.
- A field card's swipes mostly stand alone. Two cards ran five and more charges inside ten minutes, and a few visits made two charges seconds apart. So the minute rule matches only when exactly one charge fits.
- Many rows had failed, a card declined and run again. A failed charge is never a candidate.

## Decisions taken with the build

The owner's calls, built to the picks she was shown: a ticket for no one job is held for the office; both matches that make themselves are on; a ticket is seen by its taker, the office and the card's holder; the press takes any receipt. Whether a cash ticket means money owed to the tech is still hers, so `paid_other` only records how it was paid.
