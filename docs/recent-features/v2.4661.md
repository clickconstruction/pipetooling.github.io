# v2.4661 — Release of Lien: Cancel request lets you change the waiver (2026-10-06)

Punch list #87, item C (held for the owner in the guide PR #4605). The owner chose to fix the app where its own words were stale. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## The bug

While a waiver waited at the leader's desk, every folded step said *Read only while it waits for his signature. To change it, click Cancel request in step 5 first.* (`releaseStepLookNote`). But Cancel request set the row to `issued`, and an issued row is minted and locked (`lienReleaseIsEditable` is true for drafts only). So after Cancel request the waiver still could not be changed.

## What changed

- **`lienReleaseCancelTarget`** ([`lienReleaseLifecycle.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienReleaseLifecycle.ts), +4 tests) says where Cancel request takes a waiting waiver:
  - **draft**: the waiver was minted by its own signature request. It was sent to his desk, or opened for him to sign, straight from a draft, so `minted_at` equals `signature_requested_at`.
  - **issued**: the waiver was printed or marked issued first. A printed copy may be out, and the owner's mint gate is *no paper without the record*.
- **`LienReleaseModal`**:
  - Cancel request writes `status: 'draft'` and clears `minted_at`, `minted_pdf_path`, `signature_requested_at` and `signature_requested_by` for the first kind. A toast says the waiver is a draft again. For the second kind it writes `issued`, as before.
  - The button's hover says which will happen.
  - A draft is editable, autosave resumes, and the footer offers *Discard this draft*. Minting it again records a fresh audit PDF at the same path. The activity log keeps its *issued* and *signature requested* events, and a second mint does not log a second *issued*, because the trigger dedupes by row.
- **`releaseStepLookNote`** ([`lienReleaseSteps.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienReleaseSteps.ts)) takes whether cancelling unlocks. For a printed waiver it reads *…It was printed for a paper signature, so to change it, click Void this waiver at the bottom and make a new one.* The footer already offers *Void this waiver* on a waiting row.
- [`docs/LIEN_RELEASE_MODAL_ARCHITECTURE.md`](../LIEN_RELEASE_MODAL_ARCHITECTURE.md): the `cancelSignatureRequest` line is amended.

## Guide lines this makes true

No guide is touched. *give a customer a lien release* L59 ("Cancel request unlocks it") and *send a GC our lien waiver* L96 (the window's own note, "click **Cancel request** in step 5 first") now hold for a waiver sent to the leader's desk without printing it. That is the case those guides describe.
