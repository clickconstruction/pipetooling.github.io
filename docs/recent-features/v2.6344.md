# v2.6344 — The Pipeline row's plans icon and the History line on a part switch (punch list #239, PR 6) (2026-11-02)

The card's sixth PR. The design's rule *a job follows its part* meant a switch moved every job at once and nobody typed the event; the Pipeline's rows, meanwhile, still had no door to a job's plans at all (the Bid Board's rows had one since v2.6169).

## Change

- **Migration `20261102063000_plan_part_switch_history.sql`** (`docs/migrations/20261102063000_plan_part_switch_history.md`): `plan_part_switch_words(part, set, old)` and the trigger `plan_parts_history_on_switch` (`AFTER UPDATE OF current_set_id ON plan_parts`): when a development part switches its current set, one `development_history_entries` line per job naming the part (or one for the site when none does), `happened_at = now()`, source `note`, as `auth.uid()`, with the set's link. `SECURITY DEFINER`, since only the office may keep lines through the app and the switch may be an estimator's, the hand-off trigger's or a robot's. A job's own part outside a development writes nothing.
- **Kernel `src/lib/plans/jobPlanSetsIcon.ts`** (pure, `jobPlanSetsIcon.test.ts`): `jobPlansPress(entry, jobPlansLink)` → nothing, open the one set, or pick with the count (the title names the set the job builds from); `jobPlansPickerView` and `jobPlansPickerWords` for the window. A job with no part falls back to `job_plans_link`.
- **Hook `src/hooks/usePlanSetsByJob.ts`**: one chunked read of `plan_parts` and `plan_sets` for every row that names a part, never one per row, as `useBidPlanSetsByBid` does; fail-soft.
- **`StagesPlansButton`** (`StagesRowActionButtons.tsx`) in every Stages row's icon cluster after the job folder, and **`JobPlanSetsModal.tsx`** (`JobPlanSetsModal.render.test.tsx`), the picker: the current set first with *building from*, the older sets, *Our as-builts*, each with Open ↗, and **Open the Documents tab**. Wired in `JobsStagesTable.tsx`.
- **Docs**: guide *keep the plans on a job* (open from the Pipeline; the History line); `GLOSSARY.md` → *Plan sets by part (Jobs)*; the card's front matter.
