# v2.7237 — Subs Ledger: tick each payment against the bank (2026-11-13)

Punch list #347, PR 4. The Ledger listed every payment by date to hold beside a bank statement. This is the mark a person leaves as they go down it, so the next sitting starts where the last one stopped.

## What it is

- **A ✓ box per payment** on the by-date list at a desk. Ticking it stores who and when on the payment row. The box's hover reads both. A phone shows *✓ checked* on a card and has no box.
- **A tick is on the sending.** Lines that were one check are ticked and cleared together, since the bank shows one line. A payment from before sendings is ticked by itself.
- **No box** on a back-charge or on a line paid from credit: no money left the bank.
- **Checked against the bank**, a tile: how many of the period's payments are ticked, how many are left and what they add up to. It counts the period, not the filtered list, so ticking a line never moves its own total.
- **Not checked**, a chip, keeps the lines still to find. **How paid** narrows to one method, with *No method given* for payments recorded before the chips.
- **An edit clears the tick.** Changing a ticked payment's amount or Date sent clears it in the database, and the Edit window says so before the save.
- **A back-charge takes its day.** The Backcharge window has a Date box, today unless changed, so the ledger files a back-charge under the day it was raised. One recorded before the box still shows the day it was typed.
- The CSV gains *Checked on* and *Checked by*.

## Code

- Migration `20261114030000_sub_payment_checked.sql`: three columns, `set_sub_payment_checked` and the clearing trigger (see its note in `docs/migrations/`).
- `subPaymentsLedger.ts`: a line gains `checkable`, `checkedAt`, `checkedBy`; `filterLedger` takes `check` and `method`; `ledgerTotals` counts `checked` and `uncheckedTotal`; `ledgerCheckedWords` and `ledgerMethodOptions` are new.
- `useSubLaborLedger.setSubPaymentChecked` marks the rows on the page at once and then calls the function, so ticking down a list does not wait on a reload. A refusal reads the truth back. The payments read asks for the two columns first and falls back three times.
- `SubPaymentsLedger` takes `onSetChecked`. A caller that hands none in gets no ✓ column and still sees the tile.

## Order of deploy

The client calls a function and reads columns that only the migration creates: *push right after the merge*, after the credit migration it builds on. Until the push every sheet still draws, no tick is read, and pressing a box says ticking is not switched on yet.

## Checked

- The migration on a scratch Postgres 15, twice, with a scenario for each rule above.
- Kernel tests: what can be ticked, the totals and their words, both filters, the CSV, and a dated back-charge.
- Render tests: no column for a caller that cannot tick, boxes only where money moved, the press, Not checked, How paid, the empty words and a refusal. The Backcharge window's date and the Edit window's warning.
