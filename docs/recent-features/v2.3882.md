# v2.3882 — Inbox on a phone: Needs You first, the inboxes one line each (2026-09-27)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Punch list **#30**, PR 4a-2 (`to-dos/taunya-mobile.md`; the mock-up `to-dos/taunya-mobile-pr4-inbox-day.html`). Builds on [v2.3881](v2.3881.md) (the deck).

## What shipped

- **`src/lib/dashboard/phoneFolds.ts`** (new) — `myInboxFoldHeadline` (open items due today and overdue, from the instances the card shows), `requestInboxFoldWords` (*5 open · oldest 4 d*; closed rows not counted), `teamsInboxFoldHeadline` (each inbox the role can see, by name, on one line), and the per-device, per-section fold state (`readPhoneFoldOpen` / `writePhoneFoldOpen`; closed unless this device opened it).
- **`src/components/dashboard/PhoneFold.tsx`** (new) — a section as one 48 px line: its name, its headline in the headline's tone, *Open ▾* / *Close ▴*. The body stays mounted while folded (`hidden`), so the section's reads run and a half-typed note survives a fold. With `fold` false it renders its children bare.
- **`DispatchModeInbox.tsx`** — folds when the screen is a phone and the role is an office role (`isPhoneOfficeRole`): My Inbox and the Teams Inbox card each inside a `PhoneFold`; Needs You and the signature lanes stay above them, unfolded; the Gmail link moves to the foot of the page. Nothing changes for a desktop or a field role.
- **The guide** *dispatch-mode* → the Inbox tab says so.

## Two departures from the drawing

- **One fold for the team inboxes**, not one per inbox: the Dispatch and Estimator sections live inside one card with the team reviews, so the fold wraps the card and its line names each inbox with its count.
- **Open Gmail sits at the foot of the page**, not in the More sheet: the sheet lists pages, and a link out of the app is not one.
