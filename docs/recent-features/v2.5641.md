# v2.5641 — Bills and estimates in a message, PR 1: the papers read and the fills (2026-10-19)

Punch list #127, PR 1 of 3 (the plan and mock-up at `to-dos/bills-and-estimates-in-a-message/`, PR #5866; the owner's pick B for the estimate's link, the rest as drawn). The reply box knew one paper, `{bill}`, the job's newest Stripe bill, and only in a job's box; `{amount}` was never filled there. Now the box reads the customer's own papers and fills a line about money from the right bill. No migration. No edge function changes what it does, though `quickReplies/blanks.ts` is one of the kernels copied under `_shared/gcKernels/`, so the copy regenerates here and `check:edge-drift` names the importers to redeploy after the merge.

- **The kernel** (`src/lib/messages/papers.ts`): `billPapers` reads a customer's bills by the bill-truth kernel the AR page totals with (`computeBillTruth`): open ones first with what is still open after the payment rule, then paid ones (billed or paid and no longer open, which takes in a bill on a job paid in full) with their own amount, the newest billed first in each; Owner Billing's (`billing_only`) and a job marked Uncollectible offer nothing, a draft never. A bill goes by its job's number (`effectiveJobLedgerNumber`), as They owe us names it, and has a pay page when it is on Stripe (`payLinkUrl`). `estimatePapers` offers an estimate once sent: waiting on an answer first, then approved (`customer_accepted`), the newest sent first; no draft, declined or superseded. `papersFor` puts a job's own papers first. `paperFills` is what a paper puts in the blanks; `defaultPaperFills` is the bill a line speaks of when nobody picked one: a job's box its job's newest open bill and no other (`jobOnly`), a thread the tied job's when it has one open, else their newest open bill anywhere.
- **The read** (`papersIo.ts`): the customer's jobs and estimates, then their bills and payments, under the reader's own policies; a read that fails gives no papers, and without the payments no bill is offered rather than a wrong amount. Checked read-only on prod through the app's own client: a customer's two open bills with their open amounts and pay pages before 21 paid ones, and another's sent estimate.
- **The box** (`reply/ReplyBox.tsx`, `reply/usePapers.ts`): the papers are read once per customer when the quick replies are (the strip open, Find a reply, a `/`), and `replyFills` takes the bill's fills (`paper`): its pay page wins over the job's newest (`billLink`), and an open bill with no pay page leaves `{bill}` to fill rather than pairing its amount with another bill's page.
- **The blanks** (`quickReplies/blanks.ts`): `{number}` (the bill's or estimate's number) and `{estimate}` (the estimate's link, filled once PR 3 makes it), so the editor's chips offer them.
- **Tests**: `papers.test.ts` (open before paid, the open amount after a payment, a paid job's bill as paid, no draft, Owner Billing or uncollectible bill, the number and pay page; estimates' order and statuses; the job first; the fills; a job's box and a thread's default); `filling.test.ts` (the three fills from one bill, no amount with another bill's page, the job's newest kept with no paper); `ReplyBox.papers.render.test.tsx` (a job's box and a thread fill from the right bill on a press, no read for someone who is no customer); the pinned blanks and chips.
- **Docs**: the guide *text a customer from the job*; the Glossary's R3 line; `MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md`'s reply box paragraph; `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`'s copy count; to-do #127's state.
