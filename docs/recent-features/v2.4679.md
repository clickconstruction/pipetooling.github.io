# v2.4679 — Release of Lien: a waiver waiting for a signature reopens with the leader it asked (2026-10-06)

Punch list #87 item M (`to-dos/lien-screens-stale-words.md`), assigned by the owner's coordinating session. Found on 2026-10-05 while the lien release guide was checked against the signer reset ([v2.4567](v2.4567.md)).

## The fault

The Release of Lien window resumes the job's newest live draft, or a waiver still waiting for a signature (v2.2619, v2.2641). It restored the row's fields, form and bills, but not the leader the row asked (`signer_user_id`).

- **The pick showed the default.** Since v2.4274 the **Signs** pick has come from the users load. Since v2.4285 that load picks the company's signer from Settings, then the job's leader. Every fresh opening landed there. Since v2.4567 the pick is also cleared on each opening, so a reopen in the same session lands on that default too. A waiver asked of another leader reopened with the default in the pick, locked while the waiver waits.
- **The press moved the request.** *✍ He is here, he signs now* reads the pick. `signNow` rewrites `signer_user_id` when the pick differs (v2.4274), so pressing it moved the request to the default leader before the pad opened. The pad then locked to that leader's hand (v2.4285's signer of record).
- **The same button, for the default leader.** When the default leader is the signed-in user, the button reads *✍ Sign it now*. Pressing it moved the request to him and opened the pad for him to sign, typing allowed, in place of the leader asked.
- **What else the window said.** The amber strip read the asked leader (*✍ Waiting for Robert Douglas to sign*), because it comes from the saved fields. The line *The record will say: drawn by …* is built from the pick, so it named the default.

## The fix

All in `LienReleaseModal.tsx`, and all scoped to a waiting request:

- **The resume seeds the pick.** When the resumed row is waiting for a signature and names its leader, the pick takes him. The window remembers him (`waitingAsk`, cleared on each opening), named from his own users row, read by id. It never uses the page's Signed by line, which can print someone else.
  - The users load keeps a pick that is already set, and the resume overrides a default that landed first, so the order of the two loads does not matter.
  - A draft keeps the job's default, as before. On the next opening that includes a draft that **Cancel request** turned back, which still carries the asked leader's `signer_user_id`. In the same opening, a listed asked leader stays the pick after the cancel. Drafts are #87 item N.
  - v2.4567's rule stands: a fresh waiver on a job starts from that job's default.
- **The asked leader still signs when he is off the list.** If he has been archived, or is no longer a leader, `signerOfRecord` still names him while his request waits, under his own name, and the pad stays locked to drawing. The pick shows him as an extra option.
  - Without this, seeding his id would have left the signer of record empty, and the pad would have opened for the signed-in user to sign: job 650's fault, closed in v2.4285.
  - If his users row gives no name, nothing off the list is named, and the pad opens as it would have before.
  - No other id off the list is ever named. A fresh waiver behaves as before, whether its job leader is off the list or the leaders read failed.
- **Cancel request hands the pick back.** **Cancel request** puts an off-list asked leader's pick back on the job's default (`defaultSignerRef`, worked out by the users load and cleared with the pick). The next request then goes to the job's default, as a fresh waiver's would. A listed asked leader stays the pick.
- **Only this job's rows resume.** `loadHistory` had no guard for a slow read. Closing the window on one job and opening it on another before the first read came back could land the first job's rows in the second job's window. Its *Already on this job* list then showed them, and the resume took the first job's waiver. This predates v2.4679, which would otherwise have moved the pick with it. Two parts fix it:
  - the resume takes only the open job's rows;
  - a read that comes back for a job the window has left is dropped, and so is its effect on `historyReady`. The current job is tracked in a ref (`currentJobIdRef`), synced in a layout effect so a late read cannot land first.

## Guides

- *give a customer a lien release*: the **Signs** pick starts fresh each time the window opens. When a waiver already waits for a leader's signature, the pick opens on him.
- *send a GC our lien waiver*: step 5 names the leader a waiting waiver waits for each time the window opens. "It opens on the company's signer" now opens on its noun, and so does "While the waiver waits…".

## Tests

`LienReleaseModal.signerReset.render.test.tsx` gains seven cases. The `job_lien_releases` stub now reads by job and can hold a read back to come in late. The users list can archive Robert Douglas, reads one person by id, and holds a third leader, so the pick still shows as a list.

- **After a reload:** a fresh mount with a waiting waiver asked of Robert Douglas, where the job's leader is Malachi Reyes. The pick shows Robert and is locked. *He is here, he signs now* opens *Robert Douglas signs here — Job j1* and writes no `signer_user_id`.
- **In the same session:** the window open on that waiver, closed and reopened without unmounting (Jobs → Stages keeps it mounted). The pick shows Robert again.
- **Archived since he was asked:** the page's Signed by line prints *Taunya*, yet the pick keeps a *Robert Douglas* option from his own row, and the pad is still his to draw on.
- **A slow read:** job 1's releases come back after the window moved to job 2. Job 2's window keeps its own default, resumes nothing, and lists nothing under *Already on this job*.
- **Taken back with Cancel request:** an archived asked leader leaves the pick, and the next **Send it to his desk** asks the job's default.
- **Scope:** a draft with a `signer_user_id` keeps the default. A fresh waiver whose job leader is off the list opens the pad as before, never in another person's name.
- **Someone the window cannot name:** a request asked of an id with no users row, whose page prints *Taunya*, never opens the pad as *Taunya signs here*.

Each rule was removed in turn, and its own test failed, with three exceptions that no test reaches alone: the resume's job filter (a second line behind the read guard), the layout-effect timing of the job ref, and the reset of `defaultSignerRef` with the pick.

## Verify after merge

On a ZZ TEST job whose leader is not the company's signer:

1. Start a waiver. In **Signs**, pick a leader other than yourself, since **Send it to his desk** is hidden when you are the pick. Press **Send it to his desk**.
2. Close the window and reopen it. **Signs** shows the leader asked.
3. Press *He is here, he signs now*. The pad reads *that leader signs here*. Press **Not now**.
4. Clean up: **Cancel request**, then **Discard this draft** and **Confirm discard**.
