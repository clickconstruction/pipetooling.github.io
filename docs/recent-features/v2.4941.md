# v2.4941 — GC mode: Follow up's calls to make as one Needs you line on the Dashboard (2026-10-08)

The plan is `to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/follow-up-needs-you.md` on branch `spike/gc-mode`, built after door 2 (v2.4937) at the lead's word, with calls A and B at their defaults. The prototype put Follow up's count on the Dashboard so the chasing comes to the person (the owner, 2026-10-04: "make them match"). The real build does the same, with the pill on `/gc` and the line saying one number.

- `src/lib/gc/followUpNeeds.ts`: `gcFollowUpNeeds(state)` gives the count, red or amber, the title and the first two reasons, in Follow up's order. The count is every ask `followUps` lists but the ones waiting on a day still to come, which is `followUpsToCall`, the pill's number. `followUpStateFromSlice` turns a slice of the board's rows into the state `followUps` reads through the Board's own mappers (`gcProjectFromRows`, `boardStateFromRows`), with scope, plans, promises and money left empty. Its test pins the count to the pill's, and the slice's answer to the whole board's, on the clinic fixture. `followUps` itself does not change.
- `src/lib/gc/followUpNeedsIo.ts` reads the slice in seven small reads under door 1's and door 2's policies: `gc_projects`, `projects`, `gc_trade_packages`, `gc_invites`, `gc_quotes` and `gc_company_contacts` for those asks, and `gc_companies`. `src/hooks/useGcFollowUpNeeds.ts` imports it on demand, so the Dashboard's chunk does not carry the GC kernels. It reads on the Dashboard's load and again on focus.
- `dashboardNeedsYou.ts`: the key `gc-follow-up` at rank 40, the revenue-chasing tier, under the kicker *GC projects* with the action *Follow up*; the inputs `gcFollowUpEnabled` and `gcFollowUp`. Its tests are in their own file, `dashboardNeedsYou.gcFollowUp.test.ts`. `DashboardPinnedQuickRow` turns it on for `canOpenGcProjects` (call A: the GC office team, who see the pill) and sends the action to `/gc?view=followUp`.
- `src/lib/gc/links.ts`: `GC_FOLLOW_UP_HREF` and `gcViewFromSearch`. `GcProjects.tsx` opens on the view `?view=` names (`partners` or `followUp`); a dev's Trade portals and Money open from their pills only.
- Guide `follow-up-on-a-quote-from-a-trade-partner` gains the Dashboard line. `docs/DASHBOARD_SECTIONS_ARCHITECTURE.md` lists the new block.

Call B stands: the line counts asks to call now. When the Board's B2b counts people owed an answer, a paper or a waiver, the line, the pill and the board's rows move together.
