# v2.3682 — Lien desk: correct the claim by hand, and it carries until you clear it (2026-09-21)

One migration (a new table), no edge function. Built as drawn in the Claude artifact *Claim, By Hand* (second pass), after the owner's "amount off, office can clear it, build it as mocked up".

## Why

The owner: *"sometimes we have a discrepancy in a bill and need to do these by hand, we would need to add some sort of override that we would apply on a month to month basis"* — and then *"give the user as much autonomy as possible to overcome issues they may need to correct."* The notice's claim amount was one blue value, the job's unpaid balance, with nowhere to say "the real figure is $8,300". The first mock-up split the balance by hours per month; the second pass threw that out — a per-month figure nobody billed would have printed on a statutory notice — and settled on one figure, one reason, and a correction that carries.

## The shape

- **Migration [`20260921201500_job_lien_claim_corrections.sql`](../../supabase/migrations/20260921201500_job_lien_claim_corrections.sql)** — one row per job: `amount_off` the app's unpaid balance (negative = over it), optional `per_month`, the required `reason`, `carry`, who/when, and the last `looked_at`. Office roles insert, update and delete. Detail: [`docs/migrations/20260921201500_job_lien_claim_corrections.md`](../migrations/20260921201500_job_lien_claim_corrections.md). The client tolerates the table's absence.
- **Kernel [`lienClaimCorrection.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienClaimCorrection.ts)** (4 tests): `correctedClaim` (an amount off the *moving* balance, never a snapshot), `claimDeltaWords`, `claimSplit` (typed months print what was typed, one blank month takes the rest, two blank months print no split — the rest would be a formula), `correctionNeedsLook`, `correctionSendGate` (`leader` over the balance; `look` when carried and not looked at since the last notice), and the words. IO in [`lienClaimCorrectionIo.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienClaimCorrectionIo.ts): save (upsert; clears the last "still true"), clear, look, and `clearOneShotLienClaimCorrection`, which the run calls after a notice goes so a this-notice-only correction is done.
- **[`LienClaimBox.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienClaimBox.tsx)** — the claim slot on the Months card is the editor: the figure, the delta line (amber under, red over with the fraudulent-lien sentence), *Why* (required), the carry tick, *Say it per month ›*, Cancel / Apply (*Apply — the leader decides* when over). Set: `set by hand` chip, the delta and *carries until cleared*, who · when · why, *Change ›* and *Back to the job's figure*.
- **The desk** ([`LienDeskModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskModal.tsx)) — the notice's `claimAmount` is re-read live from the corrected figure (a source, like the balance) and `claimSplit` prints *Of which, by month:* when typed; the preview marks the amount yellow with the correction line (`handSetClaim`); the pinned strip and the leader's card say *Claim set by hand*; a carried correction not looked at since the last sent notice shows the amber strip with **Still true** / **Clear it**; `correctionSendGate` turns a standing-rule send into *awaiting approval · claim_by_hand*, and the spoken-word button is off while the claim is over the balance.
- **The affidavit** ([`LienDeskAffidavitPane.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskAffidavitPane.tsx)) — `claimAmountOff` reaches `buildLienAffidavitFieldsForJob`, so the sworn unpaid amount is the balance less the correction; the gates card and the months card say so.
- Guide *send lien notices from the Lien desk* → "The claim, by hand".

## Decisions taken with the owner

- Amount off, not a set figure: the balance moves, the correction rides on top.
- The office can clear a correction; only over-the-balance goes to the leader.
- The app's balance is never touched — Billing and Collections keep chasing the real number.
