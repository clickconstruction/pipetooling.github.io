# v2.7685 — Trips: a job says its trips, a clock line wears its trip, and Plans by date gains a Trips lane (2026-11-17)

Punch list #379, PR 6 of nine: the Job window's line, the tag on a clock line, and the Trips lane on Plans by date. Client only. It reads the tables of v2.7655 (`20261117234500_trips.sql`) and saves through `trip_save`, as the Trips look does. No new migration, no edge function.

The owner's own example is why this PR exists: a trip that was part of one job had been given a job of its own, only so it could be seen alone. Now the job says its trips, and no extra job is needed.

## What landed

- **Trips on this job.** One line in the Job window's Job tab, under the address (`components/trips/JobTripsLine.tsx`, mounted once in `DetailJobModal.tsx`). It draws on a job that is in a development and that a trip names under *Work goes on* (a `trip_jobs` row), for the Development page's office door (`developmentDoorFor(role) === 'office'`). Every other Job window draws nothing new. The sentence is `jobTripsLine` in the new `lib/trips/jobTrips.ts`: *3 trips · last Oct 23 to Nov 4, Luis, Priya · next Dec 1 to 5*. It counts the trips that have gone out, names the newest of those with who went, and names the first planned one. A part that does not exist is left out, and a trip that is out today reads *out now* in place of *last*.
- **The job's hours in the five figures.** Under the sentence: the bar and each figure with its hours, At the site, On the road, Getting ready, No place on the punch and At home for the site (`jobTripHours`). The hours are `fetchSiteLabor` asked for this one job, laid over the site's trips with `splitTripHours`, each person's day read from the punches on this job against the site's point (`placesByPersonDay`). While the hours are out, closed to the reader or unread, one quiet line says which.
- **Open the site's trips.** Closes the Job window and goes to the Development page at its Trips look, `/developments/<id>?look=trips` (`tripsLookHref`). The page had no way to land on a look from a link, so it gained the smallest one: `hooks/useLandOnLook.ts` opens the look if it is folded and scrolls to it, once, when the link asked and the look is on the page.
- **+ Trip.** Opens the same sheet (`TripSheet`) on a new trip kept on the development, with this job already picked under *Work goes on*. It saves through `saveTrip`. The sheet's list of jobs holds this job alone; the site's other jobs are added from the Trips look.
- **The words on the press that adds the log to the list.** On a site with trips the By date list's press reads *Also show the N trip, crew and History lines*, and its hover line names the trips too (`plansByDateLogWords`, `plansByDateLogTitle`).
- **The tag on a clock line.** In the Job window's feed (`JobThreadNotesPanel`, one optional prop, `clockTripTags`), on a job whose development has a trip. A line by a person on one of their trip days wears **TRIP · AT THE SITE**, **TRIP · ON THE ROAD**, **TRIP · GETTING READY** or **TRIP · NO PLACE ON THE PUNCH**, in the kind's colour, grey for no place. A line by a person on no trip that day wears the quiet chip *at home for the site*. The decision is `clockTripTag`: `tripHourBucket(the day's kind for that person, the place of that person's day on this job)`, the day's place being `placesByPersonDay` over the job's punches, which is what the bar reads. So two punches by one person on one day wear the same tag, and a tag never disagrees with the bar or the Trips look. A job on a site with no trip is handed no tags, so its lines never change.
- **A Trips lane on Plans by date.** `buildPlansByDate` takes the site's trips (`lib/trips/tripLane.ts` makes them from the kept trips) and adds a lane under the sets and over the crews, only when the site has a trip. Each trip is a bar over its days on the view's own axis, one rectangle for each run of days of one kind (`tripRuns`, `tripBarShape`), a planned trip faint inside a dashed edge. A bar too narrow to split is one mark in the kind that leads the trip. A press on a bar pins the trip's card, like every other mark on the chart, and on a phone opens it as a sheet. The card says the trip and carries one press, **Open the Trips look**, which jumps to the look on the same page. The lane has its mark in the key and a line a trip in the dated list, kept with the crews' days and the History lines.
- **The print.** The paper draws the same shapes, so the lane is on the print with no more work, in paper's own colours. The note under the printed list names the trips when the list leaves the log out.

## Reads the Job window gained

All for the office door, and none for a job with no development.

1. A job in a development: one read of the site's trips (`readSiteTrips`: the development's, and any this job holds by itself).
2. Once the site has a trip: the roster, the clock's rows on this job with their places (`readSitePunches`), the addresses of the development's jobs and of its bids (`readSiteAddresses`: `jobs_ledger.job_address` and `bids.address` by `development_id`, one column each), and the point of the address they share (`sharedAddressWords`, then `useAddressGeocodeCoords`: the shared geocode cache, and the geocoder for an address it has not seen). That is the Trips look's own point, so a job's bar and the look's figures stand on one point. The job's own address is placed only when the shared read gives nothing.
3. Once a trip names this job: `fetchSiteLabor` for this one job.

A job on a site with no trip stops after the first. A read of the trips that fails leaves the window as it always was.

## Owed

- **A job with no development gets no line and no + Trip.** A trip held by a job alone needs a database change first, so that combining two jobs does not lose it. Until then a trip is added on a job that is in a development.
- **My Time and People hours** do not wear the tag. They draw their clock lines through their own components (`DashboardMyTimeSection.tsx`, `DashboardMyTimeDayEditorModal.tsx`, `people/PeopleHoursSessions.tsx` and the People hours grid), not through the Job window's feed. The decision is a pure function and can be handed to them.
- **The feed's filter by trip** on the mock-up (a *Trips* chip beside Notes, Reports and Crew) is not built.
