# v2.3522 — Lien desk: the notice is the pane, and a preview window marks what may be changed (2026-09-16)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Mock-up with the before, both passes and the critique: *Lien Desk, Paper First*.

## Why

Grace, from a screenshot of job 258 on the desk: *"visually it does not make sense that I can barely see the lien as I scroll through it."* The notice — the one document the desk exists to produce — rendered in a frame capped at **360 px with its own scrollbar, inside a pane that also scrolled**; at her scroll position it was a 34 px strip with its title broken across the edge. The same screenshot showed the footer's buttons drawn **under the Dispatch Mode tab bar** (fixed at z 1000; the desk's overlay is z 80 and sized to the viewport), two full cards of gates and months standing in front of the paper, *Send* said three times (a card, the footer sentence, a header chip), a pills row mixing the Notices/Affidavits switch with the pile filters, a two-line explainer costing the desk room on every open, and a red *Property kind: unknown* with no door while the owner line above it had one.

Then: *"the ability to preview the lien in a new window before sending with the parts that are editable highlighted."* Two facts shaped that half. The § 53.056 form is the statute's, word for word — the app varies only nine values on it (`buildLienNoticeBlocks`: "values are the only variable part"). And none of those nine could be typed anywhere: the desk filled them from the job and stored them in its draft, but showed no inputs.

## The pane

[`LienDeskModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskModal.tsx):

- **One header row.** Title (the explainer moved to its tooltip; the guide has the whole flow), the Notices · Affidavits switch, the pile pills — all in the `label · N` form — and the desk's actions; the close button sits absolute at the right. The modal grid drops a row.
- **The strip, then the paper.** The pane scrolls once. Title line → the gates as one row of chips → **Months** as one line with the claim on the right → the **Wording** line → the notice with no `maxHeight`. The affidavit pane loses its 420 px frame the same way.
- **The sticky strip.** Past 72 px of pane scroll a one-line strip mounts at the top of the pane (`position: sticky`, first in DOM so scroll anchoring absorbs the insert): job, *N of 4 gates*, the failing gates as chips, the months, the claim, the wording line when edited, and **Show gates ▴** which scrolls the pane home. It unmounts when the pane is back at the top.
- **Gates.** *Before it can go out · 3 of 4*, as chips: a passing gate is a green chip with the whole sentence in its tooltip; only a gate that is not clear is spelled out under the chips with its door — the property-kind line gains **Set property kind ›** (Edit Job → Property record), the same door the owner line has. The owner pane (the roll's answer, Confirm, the bond-claim sentence) stays mounted and renders nothing when the owner is fine. The month chips read *Jul · 41.4 h · by Oct 15* with the year and the crew's evidence (*4 people · 41.4 h · 4 days*) in the tooltip; the line under the chips is gone.
- **Send is said once.** The Send card is gone; *Certified mail to <owner> and <GC>* with the courtesy-email chip and the **cover note** checkbox now sit in the footer's first line, beside the button they belong to. The header's ask-reason chip is dropped and its fact folded into the footer sentence: *No standing rule for Loberg Contracting, so this goes to the leader — first notice we've sent this GC.* A blocked item says it too: *Blocked until the owner of record is on the property record. Once it can go: first notice we've sent this GC.*
- **Above the bar.** The overlay's `bottom` and the modal's `maxHeight` read `--app-bottom-chrome` (v2.2184, the variable Layout sets for the Dispatch / Job mode footer), so the buttons clear it.
- The queue narrows from 380 px to 320 px; the rows are unchanged.

## The preview and the wording

Nine values, two kinds. **Four are wording the office may shape**: the type of labor (job 813 is electrical; the default says plumbing), the project description, the party contracted with if different, and the contact person who signs. **Five are facts with a home elsewhere**: the date, the original contractor, the claim amount, the claimant's name and address — the demand letter's rule applies (*fix it at the source, the document re-reads it*).

- **Kernel [`lienNoticePreview.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienNoticePreview.ts)** (+9 tests): `LIEN_NOTICE_FIELD_GUIDE` (label, typed or derived, source, door) and `LIEN_NOTICE_TYPED_FIELDS`; `applyWordingEdits` (only the four ever take an edit); `noticeWordingDiff` and `wordingLineText` (*Wording · standard* / *Wording · edited (2) by Taunya*); `buildLienNoticePreviewHtml` — the print HTML plus a marking layer only this page carries: a legend, yellow (typed) and blue (derived) tints keyed off `data-field`, a right-hand list of the four you can change (with *Change ›*) and the five filled from the job (with their door), the *edited by* line, a **Print this preview** button, and a print stylesheet that strips every mark. A click on a typed value posts `{ type: 'lien-notice-preview-field', field }` to `window.opener`.
- **The form lines know their field.** `FilingDocBlock`'s `formLine` gains an optional `field`; `buildLienNoticeBlocks` sets it on the nine; `filingDocHtml` emits it as `data-field`. Print and PDF ignore it. The omitted contracted-with line (owner decision 2026-09-02) stays omitted and untagged.
- **The desk's Wording line** (closed by default) holds the four inputs; a typed edit layers over the draft (`applyWordingEdits`), redraws the paper live, and turns the line amber; **Back to the job's wording** resets. Inputs lock once the item leaves *drafted* and for non-office roles. The draft gains `wording: { editedBy, editedAt }` (jsonb — no migration; `parseLienDeskDraftFields` reads it), and the leader's *What you're deciding* says *Wording · edited (1) by Taunya — the notice below carries the changed wording.* The preview's list says the same.
- **Preview in a new window ↗** opens the page as a blob URL in a new tab through the blob pattern the Lien window already uses — **without `noopener`**, because the preview posts back. The desk listens for the message (same origin only, typed fields only), opens Wording and focuses that input.
- A re-save now **keeps `batchReason` and `coverLetter`** on the draft (Put a GC on notice wrote them; `draftFields()` used to drop both on the next save).
