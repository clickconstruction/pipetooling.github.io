# v2.8291 — Materials on a phone: the desk is three figures and one row a job (2026-11-22)

The Materials desk at a phone's width, with no job open. The owner sent a picture of it from his phone and said he did not like how it looked, then asked for a second drawing before he said build. No migration, no edge function. The desk at a computer is not changed, and neither is Jobs → Materials at any width.

## What was wrong

The desk is a table of seven columns: Job, Containers, On site lines, Missing this week, Counts, Credits due, and a row's two presses. v2.8282 and v2.8284 reworked a job's own page for a phone and left this table as it was.

- **Five of the seven columns were off the right edge.** A phone showed Job, Containers and a cut *On site,*. Which boxes are due a count and what came up short could only be reached by sliding the table, and nothing said they were there.
- **The loudest link was the rarest act.** **Move everything on site to another job…** sat under the first site's name on two lines.
- **The sentence under the heading named columns a phone could not see.**
- **Every name was a blue link squeezed into half the width**, so most wrapped, and a site read the same as its jobs.

## What a phone gets

- **Three figures where the sentence was.** The sentence promised *what is on every site, what went missing this week, which containers have not been counted in 14 days*. The figures are those three across every job: **Boxes on site**, **Due a count**, **Missing this week**. They are the job page's own tiles (`FlowTiles`).
- **A figure narrows the list.** **Due a count** keeps the jobs with a box due, **Missing this week** the jobs that came up short. A second press, or **Boxes on site**, brings every job back. A figure with nothing behind it is grey and its press is dead. The narrowing is a visit's state and is not kept.
- **One row a job.** The number is a small tag on the left, so numbers read down a column. The name has the rest of the row. One quiet line says what the job holds: *94 boxes · 2 places*. Then a line for each thing it needs, in the words the desk already writes: *94 due a count · G1, G2, G3…* (`staleWords`), *$85.00 missing this week · 2 lines* (`missingWeekNote`). A row that needs something has an amber edge. A row that needs nothing says *all counted this fortnight*. The whole row opens the job.
- **A site is a heading.** Its name sits over its jobs with **Whole site ›** beside it, the press the site's row had. A site with more than one job here says its boxes, since no row adds them up. Jobs on no site sit under **Not on a site**.
- **Open a job** is one wide press under the list that says its own name, with the same searchable list.
- **A job that did not load** is not a press. It says *Did not load.* with its reason and offers **Read again**, as its row on the desk does. It stays in the list under every figure.

## Where each thing went

Phone width only. Nothing is removed from the desk at a computer.

## How it is built

- `src/lib/materials/deskPhone.ts` is the kernel. `buildDeskPhone({ jobs, groups, filter })` takes each job's figures as the desk already computed them and the groups from `groupDeskBySite`, and answers the three tiles, the filter in force and the sections with their rows. It counts nothing itself, so the table and the list cannot disagree. A filter that would leave nothing to show falls back to every job.
- `src/components/materials/MaterialsDeskPhone.tsx` draws that model.
- `FlowTiles` in `FlowRow.tsx` takes `role`. As `filters` it is a group of presses with `aria-pressed`. The job page's tiles are tabs, as before.
- `JobsMaterialsTab` draws the phone desk when `phone` is set, which is the Materials page's mount under 640 px. The desk's rows, sort and groups are computed once and feed both layouts. **Open a job** is one definition for both.

## What the look changed

Seen on the dev server as the dev, on live data, one page load: 393 × 852 in light and dark, and 375 × 812.

1. **The rail is the row's own edge.** First drawn as a bar inside the row, it floated. It is now the row's left border, flush with the card.
2. **Rows are square inside their card.** The app's buttons round their corners, which bent the line between two rows.
3. **Open a job is link blue.** The list's placeholder is grey, which read as a dead field.

At both widths `innerWidth` and `scrollWidth` were equal. At 393 px the whole desk, four jobs, is one screen. **Due a count** was pressed and the list fell to the three jobs with a box due. A row was pressed and its job opened. Nothing that writes was pressed.
