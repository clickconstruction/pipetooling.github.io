# v2.7897 — Lien desk: the red line on the first tile shows its papers (2026-11-18)

The owner's pick of 2026-11-18, one of the last five pieces of the Board's drawing (punch list #383, option B) that a live look found still different. He saw each redrawn on the Board as it stands and picked all five; each is its own PR. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## Before

- The first tile's red line reads one of two ways. While a paper is at risk it reads *3 will not make Monday as they stand.*, an underlined press since v2.7371 that narrows the list to those papers. With none at risk and a paper late it reads *6 are past their day.*, and that one was words alone: the desk said six papers were late and gave no way to list them.

## What changed

- **The late line is a press.** `tiles` in `lienBoard.ts` gives the day tile `lineDestination: { kind: 'late' }` whenever its line is the late one. The count and the door share one rule, `lienBoardChipLate`: a paper past its day that is still to send or file, on a property that takes a paper.
- **Both red lines say they are presses.** `Tile` draws either as one button: the red sentence, then *Show them ›* in the link colour (`LIEN_BOARD_DAY_LINE_DOOR`). The underline that marked the at-risk line is gone, so the two lines read alike.
- **What the press shows.** `lienBoardLatePick` (`lienBoardDayLine.ts`) is the late papers as a pick with no bin lit, as the at-risk pick is. A paper's own row is in, and so is a run row that holds a late notice. The line under the bins reads *Showing 3 rows with a paper past its day.* with *Show every row*, worded so it agrees when one run row stands for several notices.
- **The desk.** `useLienDeskBoard` holds `boardLate` beside `boardRisk`; a bin, the leader's tile or the other line clears it, and a second press puts the list back.

## What it leaves alone

- **Which line shows.** The at-risk line still wins while any paper is at risk, so the late line shows only with none at risk, as before.
- **The pinned find box's counts** (`lienDoNowBarParts`) still carry the at-risk count alone.
- **The second tile's way back.** The line keeps *Show them ›* while its pick is on; the press under the bins says *Show every row*.
