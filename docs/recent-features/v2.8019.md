# v2.8019 — Contracts and clauses: a paper can be built from settings (2026-11-19)

Punch list #389, step 1 of eight, the second half: the screens. The ground came in v2.8014 and is pushed. No migration here.

**Deploy**, from `main` after the merge: `accept-contract`, `get-contract-for-signer` and `gc-trade-portal`, the three functions that read a clause paper through `_shared/clausePaper.ts`.

No clause wording rides with this. No paper has a settings list until a controller or a dev gives it one, and the first one is a test entry.

## What changed on Settings → Contracts and clauses

- **Settings list**, a press on every paper's card for a controller or a dev. Its window names each setting, its choices from loosest to strictest, the plain words each choice says in a summary, the blanks a clause may hold and the named levels. A key is made from a name when the thing is added, and is never typed. The window lists every gap before it saves, and **Take the settings list away** asks first.
- **Only when**, one line in a clause's window on a paper with a settings list: *In every paper*, or a setting *is* a choice. Under it, **For the other choices** says whether the clause is left out for every other choice, is another wording of a place a clause already holds, or is the first wording of a new place.
- **The paper's card** gains a strip: *Built from settings*, the revision with whether an attorney read it, and either *Every choice has its wording* or each gap in words. Each clause shows what puts it in the paper. The wordings of one place share its number and take a letter, 2A and 2B.
- **Mark revision 4 as read by an attorney**, for a controller or a dev: a name and a day. A later publish leaves the paper unread again, with no press.
- **Preview with settings** puts the paper together for a mix of picks: a press a level, a select a setting, and an input for each blank no choice fills. It says in words what stops the paper, with the same words the database raises.
- A clause keeps the line breaks it was written with, on the preview, the sign page and the signed record.

## A paper built from settings is never signed bare

`clausePaper.ts` reads the library document first and throws `SETTINGS_PAPER_BARE` for one with a settings list. It is thrown and never answered as null, because a null there means *not built from clauses* and the accept then signs the copy's body. `accept-contract` answers it with a 409 in the same words, whatever the page showed. The database already refuses the copy and the signed copy (v2.8014), so this is the second stop.

The pickers for a statement of work's paper and for our contract's paper leave a paper built from settings out.
