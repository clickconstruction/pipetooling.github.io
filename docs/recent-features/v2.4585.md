# v2.4585 — A job's Lien window names your next step (2026-10-05)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. PR 3 of 5 for punch list #82 ([`to-dos/lien-desk-next-up/`](../../to-dos/lien-desk-next-up/README.md)). Stacked on PR 2.

## What changed

- **The rename.** The window's title is *Liens on job N*; the name *Lien instruments* is gone from every label a person reads: the row icon's label (*Liens on this job*), the Legal desk's doors (*Liens on the job*), the journey steps (*Open the job's liens*), two Dashboard card lines, two desk lines, and four guides. Prose calls it *the job's Lien window*, as most guides already did. The component is still `LienInstrumentsModal`.
- **Your next step.** [`lienWindowNextStep.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienWindowNextStep.ts) reads the timeline's own *Next on the path* and adds where its button goes: a § 53.056 or retainage notice opens the Lien desk on this job (those are drafted, approved and sent there); the affidavit, its service and the release of record switch this window's tab; counsel's step and a finished path have no button. The card sits under the timeline in [`LienInstrumentsModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienInstrumentsModal.tsx), tinted by the step's tone, with the days left and who we wait on.
- **The desk can be re-aimed while open.** The Lien window opens over the desk, so its button closes the window and aims the desk at the job. [`LienDeskModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskModal.tsx) applied a door's tab only on a closed-to-open change; it now also applies it when an open desk is handed a different job.
- **Waivers on the bills.** A line under the card, *Open Release of Lien ›*, opens that window over this one (for the roles that can issue a release).
- The guide *understand how liens work and which lien tool to use* is rewritten in plain words around Next up and the two names, and leaves `LEGACY_PLAIN_WORDS_GUIDES`. It also said retainage was not in the app; it has been since v2.3753.
