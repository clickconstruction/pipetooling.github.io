# v2.6855 — Lien affidavit: a short form that says what the statute lists, drawn for every new draft (2026-11-10)

The owner, 2026-11-10, on the Mechanic's and Materialman's Lien Affidavit: why does it print *Work month 2026-08* at its top, then the whole paper read against Texas Property Code § 53.054, then the lens, *the minimum required by law, short and sweet*, then *can you make the lien changes we discussed?* The design is punch list #305 (`to-dos/lien-affidavit-contents/`), whose page was written for the lien attorney. Counsel has not answered: the owner chose to build first, so every statute reading here is the app's, and the wording is kept where one edit changes it. This is PR 1 of the card's train; the homestead notice and the two new gates follow.

## What changed

- **The short form.** `buildLienAffidavitBlocks` (`src/lib/jobsDocuments/lienFilingDocuments.ts`) draws the form its fields name. `form: 'short'` is `buildShortLienAffidavitBlocks`: the title, STATE OF TEXAS and a blank county for the notary, the *BEFORE ME* opening unchanged, then seven numbered paragraphs on a job under a general contractor (the signer's authority and the claimant's mailing address; the trade and the property; the GC with its last known address as the original contractor; the owner; the months; the day and method of each notice to the owner; the claim and the lien) and four on a job contracted with the owner (the first two, the owner as the hiring party with the claimant as original contractor, the claim). The signature block, then the jurat with a printed-name line under the notary's signature (`notarial.printedName`, `JURAT_PRINTED_NAME_LINE` in `juratWords.ts`). No letterhead and no reference strip: the builder drops both whatever the caller hands it.
- **Gone from the paper:** *My name is*, the first and last day of work, the contract amount and the amount paid, the general sentence that all notices were sent, *together with all interest, costs, and attorney's fees allowed by law*, and the property's county above the opening.
- **The months and the notices come from the record.** `src/lib/jobs/lienAffidavitNotices.ts` reads the job's live `notice_53_056` filings: `lienAffidavitNoticeMonths` (every month `months_covered` names, once, in `describeNoticeMonths`' words) and `lienAffidavitOwnerSends` → `lienAffidavitNoticesWords` (each send to the owner, its method in plain words and its day; one envelope that carried two notices counts once). `buildLienAffidavitFieldsForJob` takes `filings`; the desk hands over `data.filingsByJob`, the Lien window its live filings. A fact not on the record prints a blank.
- **A paper keeps the form it was drafted on.** The desk never stored an affidavit's words: its item's `fields` hold the notice draft, and every draw (the Affidavits pane, the paper preview, a stop's window, the Lien window's tab) fills the paper from the job. So a new template would have changed an affidavit already approved. `src/lib/jobs/lienAffidavitForm.ts`: a desk item whose fields carry `affidavitForm: 'short'` is on the short form; one without the mark is on the earlier ten paragraphs, through approval, the notary and the clerk; a job with no item is on the short form. The pane stamps the mark on every draft it saves from a short-form draw. The Lien window reads the job's affidavit items (`readLienAffidavitForm`), draws no paper until the read lands, and on a failed read draws the earlier form with a red line.
- **Redraw on the short form…** on the Affidavits pane, over an earlier-form paper not yet filed. It asks once, then pulls the item back to a draft that carries the mark. The paper needs approval again.
- **A filing keeps its own form.** The recorded snapshot (`job_lien_filings.fields`) now carries `form`, `isSub`, the months and the notices; a snapshot with no `form` draws the ten paragraphs, as it was filed.
- The Lien window's tab reads **The claim it swears** on a short-form paper; the earlier form keeps *Sworn amounts (¶8)*.
