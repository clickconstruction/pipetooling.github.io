## What landed

A box past what a sheet owes, or boxes that do not add up to the amount, is a plan that is not ok, with its reason. `sendingConfirmLabel(plan)` names the act: *Record $2,200.00 on 2 sheets*, with *and their credit* when some is kept.
- `src/components/jobs/SendingByHandGrid.tsx`: the grid, one box a sheet and one for the credit, with the placed and sent line under it.
- `SubLaborPaymentModals.tsx`: *Oldest first | By hand* over the plan, shown when the sub has another open sheet. By hand swaps the fill's list for the grid. The press reads `sendingConfirmLabel` whenever the payment is by hand or goes past its sheet, and stays *Record payment* for a payment that stays on its sheet.
- Guide `pay-a-sub-across-sheets-or-keep-a-credit`: a section on placing the payment by hand. Card #347's *Not built* list loses the item.
