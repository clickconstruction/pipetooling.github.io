# v2.4252 — A contract or a lien release does not go out with a half-typed date (2026-09-30)

The follow-up to [v2.4246](./v2.4246.md), whose "Left alone on purpose" list named the click paths. v2.4246 made the draft autosave on the job contract and the lien waiver wait for a finished date; the buttons still wrote what was on the screen. A date box reports the year "2026" as `0002-…`, `0020-…`, `0202-…`, `2026-…`, and a year typed as two digits stays `0026-…`, so a contract could go to the customer reading *Start: 0026-09-30*, and a waiver could be minted — locked, a document of record — with a through or signature date in the year 0026.

Each click that sends, hands over, files or mints now stops while a date is unfinished, with an error toast that names the box: *Finish the “Start” date before this goes out. Type the year in full, like 2026.* Nothing is saved by the stopped click.

- [`dateBoxEntry.ts`](../../src/lib/dateBoxEntry.ts) — `unfinishedDateStopsMessage(box, before, year)`, the one home of that sentence. `estimateDraftUnfinishedDateBlocksSend` (v2.4246) now calls it; its words are unchanged.
- [`jobContractDocument.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobContractDocument.ts) — `jobContractUnfinishedDates(fields)` (the boxes caught half typed, in paper order; `jobContractDatesUnfinished` is now its length) and `jobContractUnfinishedDateBlocks(fields, 'out' | 'filed', year)`.
- [`lienWaiverRelease.ts`](../../src/lib/jobsDocuments/lienWaiverRelease.ts) — `lienWaiverUnfinishedDates(formType, fields)` and `lienWaiverUnfinishedDateBlocksIssue(formType, fields, year)`. As in v2.4246 the through date counts only on a form type that has one.
- [`jobContractFileWrite.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobContractFileWrite.ts) — `fileSignedContractDateBlocks(signedOn, year)`.

## The contract window, path by path

[`JobContractModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobContractModal.tsx) — `unfinishedDateStops('out' | 'filed')` toasts and answers true. It stops a **draft** only.

Stopped:

1. `invokeSend` — **Send the link**, **Copy the link**, the link by text, **Open the signing page** (Sign here, now). All four flush the draft and call `send-job-contract`, which mints the link and locks the row.
2. `downloadHanded` — On paper → **Download & mark handed over**: flushes the draft and marks it handed over.
3. `emailPdf` — On paper → **Email the PDF**: flushes the draft and calls `share-job-contract`.
4. The door to the filing sheet — **File their signed contract** under the rail, and the rail's own button on a builder's job (**File their subcontract**). `fileSignedJobContract` converts the draft in place, or inserts a signed row from the window's payload, so the draft's dates land on the signed record. The sheet does not open; the message ends *before this is filed*.
5. The filing sheet's own **Signed on** box ([`JobContractFileSheet.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobContractFileSheet.tsx) `record`). That date becomes `signed_at` and `paper_signed_on` on a row nobody edits. The sheet is shared, so the Contract sweep's inline filing card gets the same stop.

Not stopped, and why:

- **A row that is already out** — Resend email, Copy link, Text the link, Open the signing page, File the signed copy. Its fields are locked and the window shows the row's own saved dates; nobody can retype them, so a stop there could not be cleared.
- **Void & redo** — copies the locked row's fields into a fresh draft. It is how a date that already went out wrong gets fixed, and the draft it makes is stopped by the gates above until the date is retyped.
- **Edit & re-send** — unlocks the row in place; writes no date.
- **Download the PDF** and **Open full size** — they write and send nothing, and show the paper as it reads.
- **Not needed** — a fact on the job, not on the agreement.

## The lien waiver

[`LienReleaseModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienReleaseModal.tsx) — one gate, in `ensureMinted`, after the "already minted" return and before the payload is built. Every output mints first, so **Mark issued**, **Print for signature**, **Download PDF** and **✍ Request signature** all stop on a draft with a half-typed date (*…before this is issued*). On a release that is already minted the same buttons print and download the locked row as before, and Request signature on an issued row only changes its status.

## Tests

Kernels: `dateBoxEntry.test.ts`, `jobContractDocument.test.ts`, `lienWaiverRelease.test.ts`, `jobContractFileWrite.test.ts` (and `estimateDraftPersist.test.ts`, unchanged, still reads the same sentence). Render: `JobContractModal.dateHold.render.test.tsx` — Send the link, Copy the link, both On paper buttons and the file door send, save and open nothing; the finished year sends; the sheet's Signed-on box stops and then records. `LienReleaseModal.dateHold.render.test.tsx` — each of the four buttons mints nothing; a through date is named first; the finished year mints. With the three gates switched off, 10 of the new render cases fail.

Walked live in dev (Chromium) on a test job with no contract, on paths that save nothing: `10/15/2` in Start left `0002-10-15` in the box and *Not saved: a date is not finished* under the paper; **File their signed contract** did not open the sheet and said *Finish the “Start” date before this is filed…*; **Download & mark handed over** said *…before this goes out…* and downloaded nothing. Closed and reloaded: the job still had no contract, and the window opened with no dates. Not walked: the waiver (its date boxes open full, and a finished date there writes a draft) — the render test covers its four buttons.

**Not changed:** any saved date. A draft that already holds a bad year is stopped until the date is retyped. A waiver already minted with one is locked: void it and issue a new one.

**Left alone on purpose:**

- **A box with a part emptied.** Typing `0` into the month of a full date fires one change with `''` and `validity.badInput`, and the debounced forms read that as a cleared date. Measured in Chromium for this PR, on a controlled React date box over `2026-09-30`, typing `0 1 1 5` to make it 01/15:
  - *Ignoring the change* (leave the state alone when `badInput` is set) does not work. React puts the box back to the saved date after the `0`, the `1` then does not move on to the day, and the box ends on **11/05/2026**.
  - Emptying all three parts by keyboard fires one event, on the first part. A box that ignores it is never told the date was cleared.
  - What does work: a flag inside the box that renders `''` while `badInput` is set (so React leaves the half-typed parts alone) and does not report the change; the typed date then reports `2026-01-30`, `2026-01-01`, `2026-01-15` exactly as today. It needs a check on key-up and on blur for "every part is empty now", and on blur with a part still missing it puts the saved date back.

  That is a shared box with its own rules on eleven inputs in five files (`JobContractPaper`, `LienReleaseModal`, `BidFormModal`, `JobFormPaymentsTable`, `Estimates`), so it is its own PR.
- The Contract sweep and the one-click sends (`quickSendJobContract`, the in-person door). They have no date box: they send a fresh prefill, whose dates are empty, or a saved draft, which v2.4246 keeps free of half-typed dates. A draft saved with a bad year before v2.4246 would still go out from there.
- A stale through date under a final waiver. The box is hidden on that form type, the date is not printed, and `through_date` is written null; the value stays inside the `fields` snapshot as before.
