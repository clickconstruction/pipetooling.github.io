# v2.7115 — The Bridge's Monday email shows What to do now, with no call to make (punch list #334, PR 11) (2026-11-12)

PR 11 of the Bridge train (card #334, `to-dos/bridge-pulse/`), on PR 10 (v2.7113). An edge function changed (`bridge-weekly-sheet`, with the kernels copied to it), and a migration drops two functions. Both are done after the merge, and only once PR 7 (v2.7105) is live on the site.

The page stopped making a call in PR 7. This takes the call out of what was left: the Monday email, the kernels the page and the email share, and the database.

## What changed

- **The Monday email** (`weekMail.ts`): its card is **What to do now**, with the slowest step, or cash in a week it leads, and the first three moves. Gone: the press that read *Open the Bridge to make the call* or *to answer*, the person on a move, the done mark, the note, Friday's foot and **The last call**. Its one press reads **Open the Bridge**. The three tiles and the strip of pipe are as they were.
- **The model reads today** (`weekCall.ts`): `buildWeekCall` takes no calls, no pick, no people and no marks, and hands back the tiles and the card. The card's name and its not-read line live here (`WEEK_CARD_LABEL`, `WEEK_NOT_READ_LINE`), so the page and the email say them once. A move is its key, kind, title, why, door and number. Gone: the proposal's press and foot, the standing, Friday and answered states, the last call, the names (`topPerson`, `topPersonId`, `firstName`), `movesShown`, `movesToRecord`, `deskMovesFoot`.
- **The links** (`calls.ts`): what is left is the links' names, `isStageLink`, the move keys, `shortUsd`, `deskStatus` and `stageKindOf`. Gone: the reading of `bridge_calls` (which call stands, the streak, the scorecard, the verdict, Friday's ask) and the three client calls that loaded, recorded and answered one.
- **The reads** (`sheetReads.ts`, `sheetBuild.ts`, `bridge-weekly-sheet`): no read of `bridge_calls` and none of the Done presses. `callPeople.ts` is deleted and leaves the list of kernels copied to the edge functions.
- **The database** (`20261113100000_drop_bridge_move_functions.sql`): drops `bridge_my_moves()` and `bridge_mark_my_move_done(uuid, integer)`. Their SQL bed goes with them. The table `bridge_calls` stays.
- **The page** follows the trimmed model: no names are read for the moves, and the step's desk status takes the link alone.
- **Settings → Team emails** and the email catalog name the mail *The Bridge, what to do now*.

## Not in this PR

- `src/types/database.ts` and `supabase/functions/dev-mcp/catalog.ts` still list the two functions. They are regenerated after the push, in a `chore(types)` PR of their own.
- `stageFlow.ts` still knows how a held link would stand. Nothing hands it one.
- The email's tiles still show cash's low point, not the two balances the page shows.
