# v2.8386 — Messages: a payment promise from a line names its job (punch list #421, PR 5 of 5) (2026-11-23)

Punch list #421 (`to-dos/messages-heard-day.md`), the small extra #129 left: a line's mark carried no job. The column and the argument are PR 2's migration (v2.8383, `20261123185942`: `message_promises.job_id`, `write_down_payment`'s `p_job_id`), so this waits on that push. The client reads `job_id` in two selects, which a database without the column would refuse. No edge function, nothing in the kernels copy.

## What changed

- **The mark names its job** (`useFollowUpThreadSlots` → `markLine`): a promise saved from the sheet under a line passes the sheet's job to `writeDownPayment`, which sends `p_job_id` with a day and never without one.
- **The job's They promised** (`threadSummaryIo.ts`, `promisesForJob` in `threadSummary.ts`): the read asks for `job_id`, and a mark naming another of the customer's jobs is that job's. A mark naming none, from before or of any kind but a payment, speaks for every job, as it did.
- ***Never said that*** (`linesMarkedFor` in `wordFromLine.ts`): the marks taken off are the day's on this job and those naming no job. A mark naming another job stays. `dayStillPromised` (v2.8307) still keeps a day while another promise names it.
- **The line's own job** (`tiedJobId` in `wordFromLine.ts`), a fault found on the way. A line names its job by key (`pipeline:<id>`, `bid:<id>`), and `wordLanding` compared the whole key with a money line's job id. So a line's own job never matched, and the press fell back to the customer's one job with money open, or asked which. A Pipeline key is now its job, and a bid's key the job its bid became, when one of their money lines carries that bid.
- **Tests**: `wordFromLine.test.ts` (`tiedJobId`; the marks taken off with a job); `threadSummary.test.ts` (`promisesForJob`); `followUpThreadIo.test.ts` (the job sent with a day and never without); `useFollowUpThreadSlots.render.test.tsx` (the save sends the sheet's job; Never said that leaves another job's mark; a line tied to a bid opens on the job its bid became; the lines' job keys are now real keys).
- **Docs**: the guides *reply to a person from their thread* and *see where a job stands with the customer*; `MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md`; the Glossary; the card.

## Defaults for the owner's read

- **A mark from before names no job** and keeps speaking for every job. Nothing is backfilled: prod holds one payment mark, a test customer's.
- **Only a payment mark names a job.** A customer's answer on our bid or a day on site is about the line's own bid or job, and the thread reads it as it did.
