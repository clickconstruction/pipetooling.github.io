# v2.3062 — Lien waivers from the pay row: the payment picks the Texas § 53.284 form, seeds the sub's boxes, sends for signature (2026-09-07)

Owner ask (2026-09-07): publish the four Texas statutory lien waivers (v2.2805 drafts) and make sending one from a sub's pay row explain itself, so the office never has to know which of the four is which. Mock-up artifact `04f0cfdd` ("Lien Waiver Picker") approved as drawn.

- **Kernel** [`src/lib/subWorkOrders/lienWaiverPick.ts`](../../src/lib/subWorkOrders/lienWaiverPick.ts) (+12 tests): `LIEN_WAIVER_FORMS` (the four statutory document names, § 53.284(b)–(e), one-line *why* / *when*), `lienWaiverKindFor(settled, final)`, `guessLienWaiver({ payments, balance, todayYmd })` — the payment rows carry no cleared flag, so *settled* is a presumption (newest positive payment ≥ `LIEN_WAIVER_SETTLE_DAYS` = 5 days old) the office confirms, and *final* is `balance <= 0` after at least one payment — and `lienWaiverSeedValues(...)` (project, job number, amount, owner, location, the standard extent sentence; only keys the published form has; the unconditional-final form has no amount box).
- **Dialog** [`LienWaiverSendModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienWaiverSendModal.tsx) (+render smoke), opened from [`JobsSubLaborTab.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsSubLaborTab.tsx) — **Lien waiver…** in the row's ⋯ menu and in the expanded row's action strip. Facts as chips + two checkboxes (*The money has landed*, *This is the final payment*), the picked form with its cite and reason, the amber § 53.284(c)/(e) warning on the unconditional pair, a four-dot rail (with each check · after it settles · with the last check · after it settles), *Not this one?* → the other three with *Use this instead*, the sub's roster email (editable), what will be pre-filled, and prior waivers on this sheet. **Send** inserts the `person_contract_documents` row itself (`applied_contract_template_document_id` = the Book entry; the v2.2794 trigger resolves `form_template_id`; `form_values` = the seeded boxes; `note` = `Lien waiver · sheet:<id> · <hcp> · $amount · <kind>`) and calls `send-contract-for-signature` — the same function People → Contracts uses. If the four forms are not published, the dialog says so and the button stays off.
- **Seeded boxes** (new, general): [`get-contract-for-signer`](../../supabase/functions/get-contract-for-signer/index.ts) now selects the unsigned row's `form_values` and returns them as `form.values` (sensitive boxes stripped; a signed row never reaches this path); [`ContractAccept.tsx`](../../src/pages/ContractAccept.tsx) seeds `applyPrefill(schema, values, person)` from them instead of `{}`. Any office surface can now pre-fill a form's non-sensitive boxes by writing `form_values` on the unsent row; the signer sees them filled and may change any of them. **Deploy: `supabase functions deploy get-contract-for-signer`** (deployed 2026-09-07 before this merged; additive).
- **Forms** (`scripts/forms/author/lienWaivers.ts`): letterhead address on three lines. Published the same day through the Form Studio into a new **Lien waivers** packet with no assignees (the four statutory titles, audience `sub`).
- Help: new guide `send-a-lien-waiver.md` (the pay-row flow, why four, the unconditional warning, the override). `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` → get-contract-for-signer (`values`). `to-dos/contract-forms-publish-authored.md` closes.
