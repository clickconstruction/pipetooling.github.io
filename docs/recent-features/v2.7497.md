# v2.7497 — Lien desk: a filed lien's copies have their own bin, a clock on the row, and one press on the Board (2026-11-16)

Punch list #366, step 2 of 4, built last because it had to wait for the Board's other changes (#8356, #8363 inside #8368). Steps 1, 3 and 4 (v2.7472, v2.7476) drew the letter and the COPY page and built the window that prints every filed lien's copies and takes their mail numbers. This step puts that work where the office looks first: the Board and the list under it. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## Before

- The affidavit column's last bin was *Filed · Served*, one name for two states. A lien on file with its five days running looked the same as one whose copies went out weeks ago, and its chip was always grey.
- The To do row read *Lien filed · not yet served* with the press *Record service*, always red, and stood in no bin: on a computer it sat under *Other lien papers*.
- The window that mails the copies together opened only from that row.

## What changed

- **Two bins.** `LIEN_BOARD_WORDS.filed` is *Filed · copies to mail* and a new stage `served`, *Served*, follows it in the flow, with its arrow. `buildLienBoard` takes `serve`: every filed affidavit whose copies are not mailed, with its serve-by day (`lienServeJobsFromDesk`, the same list the window reads). A filed entry on that list stays in `filed`; one that is not moves to `served`.
- **The chip races the serve-by day.** In *Filed · copies to mail* a chip's day is the filing's `serve_due`, so it is red within a day or past and amber inside the week, as every other chip is. It has one stage left, *Mail its copies and enter their mail numbers* (`LIEN_BOARD_SERVE_STEP`). Neither bin counts in the tiles: they count papers still to send or file.
- **No lien left off.** A filed lien with copies owed and no affidavit on the desk (filed long ago, or outside the app) gets a chip of its own, so the bin, the list and the window count the same liens.
- **The bin's press.** `LienBoard` takes `binPress`, a press inside a bin under its chips. The desk hands it *Mail N liens' copies ›* (`lienServeDoorWords`) on *Filed · copies to mail*, which opens `LienServeWindow`. It is an outline: #8368 put one filled press at the foot of each column, the owner approved one press a column, and this one is the bin's, not the column's. The leader's own bins draw none, and a reader outside the lien office gets none.
- **The row.** `src/lib/jobs/lienServeClock.ts` (the clock's words, moved out of `lienServePackets.ts` so the list's kernel reads them without the papers' builders; `lienServePackets.ts` still exports them) adds `lienServeRowWords`: *Lien filed · mail its copies by Nov 18*, *… today*, *Lien filed · its copies were due Nov 11. Mail them today.* The row is amber, and red from the day before its day. Its press is *Mail the copies* (`BUTTON.record_service`; the action keeps its name).
- **The list by bin.** `lienBoardRowBin` gives `serve:` rows the *Filed · copies to mail* bin, so they group under its name and a press on the bin's head narrows the list to them. `lienBinFolds` folds two or more into one row, *N filed liens owe their copies*, with the Board's own press; its target is a new `LienNextUpTarget`, `serve_copies`.

## Words of law

A late copy is still mailed and still recorded. The row says the day it was due and to mail it today, and nothing about what the law makes of a late copy: § 53.055 names no consequence, and the desk gives no advice.
