# v2.7799 — Painted maps: text one place to someone (2026-11-18)

Punch list #393. The owner, 2026-11-18, while reading C's mock-up: *I do want to eventually add a share button to both painted routes as well as places so a user can hit share and then immediately take that link into text and text it to someone so someone else can identify a location.* Asked whether it was this edition or another, he said: *Rather than another addition, let's just build it all right now, so we can close that card.*

## What was already covered

- **A painted map, his routes.** The Share sheet of #394, built the same day by its own session, leads with **Text it from my phone…**, and every sending press makes the link itself. Nothing of it is in this step.
- **The job's list of places** is a painted map (v2.7795), so it has **Share** and that sheet.

What was missing was one place alone.

## What changed

- Each place's row has a press that sends that one place (`PaintedPlaceShare`). It is on a map's own places, on the job's list, and on the job's places under **On this job** on the job's other maps.
- **On a phone it reads Text this place…** and is a plain link, `sms:?&body=…`, with nobody picked. The phone's own Messages opens with the words written and the sender picks who gets it. It is a link and not a script, so nothing can stop the phone from opening Messages after the press.
- **At a desk it reads Copy this place**, since a desk has no Messages to open. It copies the same words and says *Copied. Paste it into a text or an email.* A browser that will not copy says so and points at the phone.
- **The words** (`placeShareWords`): what the place is, the word left on it, and on a line of its own a map link to the spot. The kind leads when the name does not say it, since a text draws no mark: *Hazard: Open trench. Keep the truck off it.*
- **The link** (`placePointUrl`) is Google Maps' own search form on the point, to six places of a degree. It needs no key and opens the Maps app on a phone that has it.

## The call, as built

What the reader opens was a call with two ways. It is built at its pick and is the owner's to change.

- **Built: a plain point in a map app.** The reader needs no sign-in, gives no name, and sees that one spot and nothing else of the map. Nothing is written and no link into the app is made. So anyone who can see a place can send it.
- **Not built: the painted map opened on that place.** A map's link is its maker's and the office's to make, asks a signed-out reader for their name first, and shows the whole map. A helper who did not paint the map could not send a place that way.

## Not changed

- The Share sheet and `painted-map-link`. No function is deployed for this step.
- The shared page at `/way/<code>`. A reader of a link has no press to send a place on.

## Checked

- `paintedPlaceShare.test.ts`: the link on the point and none off the globe, never a link into the app, the words with and without a kind, a full stop not doubled, and the press for a phone and for a desk.
- `PaintedMapWindow.render.test.tsx`, four new cases: on a phone the link's address decodes to the words and nothing is sent by the app, at a desk the words are copied and it says so, a browser that will not copy says so, and a place kept on the job is sent from another map of the job.
- Not walked on a real phone. That Messages opens with the words is the one thing a test cannot show.
