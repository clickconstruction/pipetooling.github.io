# v2.4606 — Submittals: What the GC sees shows their page's own header and list of revisions (2026-10-05)

Punch list #62 (*See what they see*), PR 1b-i. Client only: no migration, no edge function. Stacked on #4584 (v2.4593), whose kernel and window it builds on; it merges after that one.

## What was wrong

The window drew its own copy of the GC's page header. It read *Product review · Rev 4*, a line the page never prints, and it had no revision chips. After a share, the GC's page lists the revisions with a share date. On BP398 that is Rev 4 and Rev 2: Rev 3 was answered by email and Rev 1 replaced before a share, so neither is on it. The office could not see that gap before sharing.

## What changed

- **The page's own pieces** ([`SubmittalRoomView.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/SubmittalRoomView.tsx)):
  - `RoomHeader` (the letterhead, *Product review*, the project and address) and `RoomRevisionChips` move verbatim out of `pages/SubmittalRoom.tsx`, as v2.4187 moved the body. The page renders them with the same DOM; its tests pass unchanged.
  - Without `onSelect` the chips are read only: `aria-disabled`, the same look, nothing to press.
- **The list the GC will see** ([`seeWhatTheySee.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/seeWhatTheySee.ts)):
  - `linkRevisionsAfterShare(revisions, rev)` gives this revision as current, then every other revision with a share date, newest first, as `get-submittal-room` lists them.
  - `neverShared` names the older ones it skips.
  - `linkListLine` says it under the chips: *Older revisions stay under it as the record. Rev 3 and Rev 1 are not on their page, because they were never shared.*
- **The window** (`SeeWhatTheGcSees`) draws `RoomHeader`, the read-only chips and that line in place of its own header. The tab hands it `revisions` (one prop).
- `linkShowsRevOf`'s comment says it restates the function's rule on purpose, with the BP398 test pinning it.
