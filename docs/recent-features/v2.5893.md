# v2.5893 — The Lien Release tab speaks the statutory forms (2026-10-26)

The Bids page's Lien Release tab predated the Release of Lien window: one home-grown
"Conditional Waiver and Release" document wrapped in invoice and payment-terms language
(`src/lib/bidDocuments/lienRelease.ts`), copy-paste only, no unconditional or final forms, no
record of what went out. The incident that surfaced it: a GC on the Take 5 Seguin job sent
their own "unconditional release minus retainage" form whose operative release language
swallowed the retainage carve-out, and the review of who could answer with our paper found
estimators had no door to the statutory forms at all (they never reach `/jobs`, the GC billing
surfaces are the money team's, and `job_lien_releases` RLS shuts them out).

The rebuild:

- `BidLienReleaseTab` now draws the Release of Lien window's own form family from
  `src/lib/jobsDocuments/lienWaiverRelease.ts`: the four § 53.284 forms, the statute cite and
  `lienWaiverWhy` line under the two toggles, and `TradeWaiverPaperView` as the live preview.
- New kernel `src/lib/bids/bidLienWaiver.ts`: bid-scoped prefill (GC as payor, the bid's
  agreed/bid value as the amount, name — address as the project), the preview paper, and the
  leader gate.
- Every route into Unconditional asks `UNCONDITIONAL_WAIVER_WARNING`, as the window does
  (v2.4582 rule).
- The leader gate: `bidLienWaiverOutputsHeld` — unconditional outputs (Copy for email / Print /
  Download PDF) render only for dev, master_technician and controller
  (`BID_LIEN_WAIVER_SENDER_ROLES`); everyone else gets the amber hold line. Conditional forms
  are self-serve for every role on the tab.
- Outputs are kernel builders: `buildLienWaiverEmailHtml/Text` (clipboard),
  `buildLienWaiverPrintHtml` (print window, with the new optional `refLabel` so the letterhead
  reads the bid number), `buildLienWaiverPdfBlob`.
- Help guide `make-a-lien-waiver-from-a-bid` (estimator-visible) ships with it.

Not in this change (follow-ups filed): recording bid-scoped releases in `job_lien_releases`
(needs a nullable `bid_id` + estimator policies) and the request-a-leader's-signature loop from
the tab; until then the tab's unconditional sends stay with the money team, and signed releases
of record still come from the job's Release of Lien window.
