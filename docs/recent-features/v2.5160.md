- **`gc_owner_contract_sends`** keeps every send of our contract: the price by line it went with, the file and its SHA-256, and once signed, the signer's fields.
  - It is a dev's until award's door, then the money team's. It is never award's wider audience: the price by line is our markup.
  - No one signed in can change a send or write a signature. Only the service role signs.
- **The private bucket `gc-owner-contracts`** holds the files. Nothing replaces one, so a signature binds to its bytes.
- **`gc_send_owner_contract`** sends it. The first send stamps `owner_contract_sent_on`, and a new price is a later send. Each refusal is in words.
- **`gc_customer_sign_owner_contract`** (service role only) signs the newest send for its own customer and keeps that send's price. Its refusals are keys with the customer's reason.
- **`gc_owner_contract_keep`** is the first sign that the portal and Owner Billing's **Mark it signed** now share, so the two cannot drift. Mark it signed refuses to undo or move the day of a contract signed in the portal.
