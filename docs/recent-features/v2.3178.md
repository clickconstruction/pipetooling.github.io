# v2.3178 — Job window: the Team labor row, the marker key inline, and Job Summary's hidden-match hint (2026-09-08)

Client only — no migration, no edge function. First of two PRs from the owner's 2026-09-08 question on J1007 (SPACEX BA-02N): "all of Malachi's time was assigned to this job, but I don't see the cost of his time in this chart … I also don't see his labor on the job summary." The investigation found both statements false-but-reasonable: the Cost Timeline's red line already carried Malachi's 8 h × $57.73 = $461.84 (the 👷 marker at Sep 8 — $530.12 card charges + $461.84 = the $991.96 end label), and Job Summary had the row under **In progress** while the tab opens on **Finished (100%)**. Nothing on the Job tab ever *named* the labor number: the accordions above the chart list parts only, the marker key hid behind a link, and the Profit band below reads "Margin before team labor" by design. The second PR (recorded-time costing — closed sessions count toward job cost before approval) follows.

## What shipped

- **Team labor row** — [`JobDetailMaterialsCostSection`](../../src/components/jobs/JobDetailMaterialsCostSection.tsx) takes an optional `teamLabor` prop; when present (and the breakdown did not fail) the block's heading reads **Labor & Parts Cost** and a **Team labor** row leads it: total cost right-aligned, a sub-line **"8.0 h · Malachi"** / **"277.5 h · 7 people"**, expandable to a Person · Hours · Cost table. Empty jobs read **"no recorded time"** with an explainer (a salaried day counts as 8 h on the job it was clocked to). [`DetailJobModal`](../../src/components/jobs/DetailJobModal.tsx) passes it only when `showJobCostBreakdownTeamLabor(authRole)` — **dev, controller, master_technician** (owner call: the dollars derive from wages) — and reloads it with the edit form's saves via `materialsCostRefreshKey`, like the parts snapshot.
  - Data: new hook [`useJobDetailTeamLabor`](../../src/hooks/useJobDetailTeamLabor.ts) — `fetchTeamLaborBreakdownForJob` (the crew-days × pay-config math the chart's 👷 stream and Job Summary's Labor column already use) plus one `clock_sessions` read of the job's closed-but-unapproved hours (degrades to 0 on error). Kernel [`jobTeamLaborRow.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobTeamLaborRow.ts) (7 tests): sort by cost, the sub-line, `pendingSessionHours` (closed · not approved · not rejected · not revoked), and the **"includes N h awaiting approval"** note — which only becomes true once PR 2 lands and recorded time counts; until then pending hours are not in the total, and the note reads as a heads-up.
  - [`MaterialsCostAccordionRow`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormMaterialsCostAccordion.tsx) gains an optional `subtitle` line under the title.
- **Marker key always on** — [`JobSummaryChargesTimelineChart`](../../src/components/jobs/JobSummaryChargesTimelineChart.tsx) drops the `legendHelpOpen` toggle; the key line (💵 payment · 🚩 report · the six cost-source icons + the situational notes) renders under the legend on every chart: Job tab, Bill tab, Job Summary's expanded row.
- **Job Summary hidden-match hint** — kernel `jobSummaryRowsHiddenByStatus` in [`jobSummaryLedgerView.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobSummaryLedgerView.ts) (+1 test) counts rows the search + window find but the Show chip hides; [`useJobSummaryView`](../../src/hooks/useJobSummaryView.ts) exposes it as `hiddenByStatus`, and the tab's empty state reads **"1 job matches but isn't finished yet — show all statuses."** (a one-tap `setPrefs({ status: 'all' })`) instead of the generic three-suggestion line. Zero-hidden keeps the old copy. Kept deliberately small — two parallel sessions (v2.3176 one-column, v2.3177 tiles-cents) are on this tab.
