# v2.6039 — Materials on site, the ground: the tables, the kernels, the store and the box code (2026-10-29)

Punch list #209, PR 1 of 4. The owner asked on 2026-10-28 for a way to know what material is in the stencilled cages and boxes a crew leaves on a big site; a design spike on 2026-10-28/29 built the whole thing against a local store, and on 2026-10-29 the owner said to build all of it and take it to live testing. This PR lays the ground; the phone's On site tab (PR 2), the office's Materials tab with the labels and the search (PR 3) and the photo sweep's second source (PR 4) follow.

## What changed

- **Migration `20261029010000_materials_on_site.sql`** (`docs/migrations/20261029010000_materials_on_site.md`): `job_places`, `job_containers` (with the generated eight-character `code`), `job_container_moves`, the append-only ledger `job_material_movements` with its eight reasons, `job_box_photos`, the private bucket `box-photos`, and the gate `on_site_can_touch_job(job_id)`: you read and write a job's materials when you can see the job in the ledger under your own RLS or are on its team. The three house calls run at the end.
- **The kernels**, pure and tested, under `src/lib/materials/`: `onSite.ts` (the ledger's arithmetic: on hand is the sum, a count resets a line and keeps its difference, what a line shows and when a box is due a count), `onSiteSummary.ts` (one job's facts for the desk and the window), `flowRow.ts` (the four figures of the flow row and the phone's three), `labels.ts` (the box code, the short address `PRTS.CO/B/<CODE>` in the letters a QR packs tight, the label's ruled rows), `photo.ts` and `photoShrink.ts` (the shot's name as the sweep names it, the shrink to 1,600 pixels), `manifest.ts` (an invoice's text read into lines, and the three-way comparison of bought, reported and left).
- **The store** `onSiteStore.ts`: a cache of each job over the tables with the io beside it. Reads are plain functions, so a screen renders without waiting; `loadJob` fills a job and `loadDesk` every job with a live container; a write lands in the cache at once and goes to the table behind it, and a refusal is said through the screen's toast and the job read again. The ledger is never edited. `useOnSiteJob` and `useOnSiteDesk` (`src/hooks/useOnSiteStore.ts`) load on open and report how the read stands for `OfflineRetryPanel`.
- **The box code's door** `/b/:code` (`src/pages/BoxCode.tsx`): prts.co forwards the label's short address here; the page finds the box by its code and opens it on the On site tab, or says the code is not a box and offers the tab. prts.co itself went live on 2026-10-29 (`scripts/cloudflare/prts-co-redirect.md`).
- **Photos** (`photoUpload.ts`, `photoBlobs.ts`, `photoShare.ts`): the shrunk shot goes to the bucket as `<job>/<photo>.jpg` after a copy is kept on the phone, so a shot with no signal is not lost; the share sheet stays as the by-hand way out.
