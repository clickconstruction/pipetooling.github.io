# v2.4467 — Bills: a bill sent in the evening keeps its day on the job and in Documents (2026-10-02)

One of the instant-day fixes that follow [v2.4452](v2.4452.md); [v2.4460](v2.4460.md) fixed the Pipeline's own bill days. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

A bill's sent and billed days come from `jobs_ledger_invoices.sent_to_customer_at` and `billed_at`, both `timestamptz`, and these readers cut them by their first ten characters, so a bill sent or marked billed after 7 pm CDT (6 pm CST) read as the next day:

- **The job window's Bill tab**: the Invoices list's *sent Sep 5 to …* and its order ([`JobFormInvoiceList.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormInvoiceList.tsx)), a payment's bill picker (*$9,800 bill · sent 2026-09-05*, [`JobFormPaymentLine.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormPaymentLine.tsx)), and the Stripe panel's *Sent:* line, twice ([`HostedStripeBillPanel.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/HostedStripeBillPanel.tsx)).
- **A payment's days after the bill** ([`billsAndPayments.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/billsAndPayments.ts), `billSentYmd`), which the payment line and the money-by-date list read.
- **The bill chips** that ask which bill a payment pays ([`paymentBillMatching.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/paymentBillMatching.ts), `billSentYmd`), through the file's `ymdOf`, which also reads the estimated bill date, a `date`.
- **The paid-before-billed warning** ([`paymentInvoiceLinking.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/paymentInvoiceLinking.ts), `paymentDateBeforeBilled`): a payment taken at the door on the evening a bill was marked billed read as paid the day before the bill. The pay-speed RPCs already read `billed_at` in Chicago, so the warning and the pay speeds disagreed.
- **The Job Summary's Cycle view** ([`jobSummaryCycle.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobSummaryCycle.ts)): the first bill's day behind work → bill and bill → paid.
- **Documents**: an invoice row's *Sent* day ([`Documents.tsx`](../../src/pages/Documents.tsx)).

## What changed

Each reads `calendarYmdInAppTzFromIso(iso)` from [`dateUtils.ts`](../../src/utils/dateUtils.ts) (`APP_CALENDAR_TZ`). The `date` columns keep their slice: `estimated_bill_date`, `paid_on` and `last_work_date`. `paymentBillMatching.ts`'s `ymdOf` now says it is for date columns.

## Not changed

The Stripe conversion window already reads `billed_at` in Central and hands `convertBillToStripe.ts` a day.

## Tests

- `billsAndPayments.test.ts`, `paymentBillMatching.test.ts`, `paymentInvoiceLinking.test.ts` and `jobSummaryCycle.test.ts` (+1 each): `2026-10-03T00:30:00+00:00` (7:30 pm CDT) reads Oct 2, `2026-12-02T00:30:00Z` (6:30 pm CST) reads Dec 1, noon UTC reads its own day; a payment on Oct 2 for a bill marked billed at 8 pm CDT that evening does not warn, and one on Oct 1 does; an evening bill after an Oct 2 last work day is 0 days work → bill and 10 days bill → paid on Oct 12.
- `JobFormInvoiceList.render.test.tsx` (+1): a bill sent at 7:30 pm CDT on Sep 4 reads *sent Sep 4 to Maria Delgado*.

All fail on main. A fixture that stamped `billed_at` at midnight UTC, 7 pm CDT the evening before, pinned the UTC day; it moves to midday and keeps its expectation.

## Checked against prod

Read only, from the worktree dev server: 19 billed or paid bills now show a different sent day, and 16 jobs a different first bill day in the Cycle view. One of the 695 payments was flagged paid before billed only because its bill was marked billed in the evening; the flag clears.
