# v2.3828 — Every lien notice carries counsel's cover letter (2026-09-25)

The owner, 2026-09-25: *"let me know if we are using the proper wording for the cover letter across both… I think one of them has shortened cover letter wording."* They were not the same.

- **Put a GC on notice** printed counsel's letter from the memo of 2026-09-22, in the commercial, residential, homestead or GC-unresponsive version. A word-by-word diff against `docs/COUNSEL_MEMO_GC_ON_NOTICE_2026-09-22.md` matched the body of all four. The only gap was the commercial letter's *Enclosed: … (Tex. Prop. Code § 53.056), with invoices.*: the app dropped *with invoices*.
- **The Lien desk** printed `lienNoticeCoverNote`, a four-sentence *"This is a routine notice … It is not a claim that you are in default"* paragraph written before counsel's memo. It never told the owner they may withhold. It was on every desk-drafted notice; the four open ones (878 and 273 awaiting approval, 650 and 258 drafted) would all have printed it. Counsel's letter reached a desk notice only when it came from a GC run.

The owner's decision: *counsel's letter everywhere*. Client only, no migration.

## The change

- **`counselCoverLetterTemplate({ stored, gcName, claimantName, property })`** (`gcOnNotice.ts`, new): the letter saved on the item (a GC run's, or letter two) when there is one, else `defaultGcNoticeCoverLetter` for `coverLetterKindFor(property)`.
- **The desk's run** (`buildLienDeskRun`): `coverNote` is null for § 53.056 notices. `coverLetter` is that template, filled, whenever the item's `cover_note` box is ticked or a letter is stored. The printed packet (`runPacketHtml`), the emailed PDF (`lienDeskRunIo`) and the desk preview (`LienDeskModal`) all read it. Drafts saved before this change pick it up at print, so nothing needs re-drafting.
- **The Enclosed line**: `runCoverNoteBlocks` takes `withInvoices`, from the invoice sections in the packet, the invoice docs in the email, and `isBilled` in the GC preview. It adds *, with invoices*.
- **The desk's box** reads *Include counsel's cover letter*, with the property's kind in its title. Page labels read *cover letter*, and so does the desk preview window's note (`lienNoticePreview.ts`).
- **Put a GC on notice**: unticking *Include the cover letter* now saves `cover_note = false`, so the owner's copy is the form alone rather than the removed note. The preview's `cover` is `'letter' | 'none'`, and the step status reads *left out · the form alone*.
- `lienNoticeCoverNote` is removed. The § 53.057 retainage notice keeps its own counsel note (`lienRetainageCoverNote`).
- **Guides**: *send lien notices from the Lien desk* and *read the Texas lien rules the app follows* describe counsel's letter on every notice.
