# v2.8254 — Permits, Market: Austin's clock runs to a job's first plumbing permit (2026-11-21)

Punch list #405 ([the card](../../to-dos/permits-market-page/README.md)), the line *Austin's clock runs to a job's newest plumbing permit* on its *Seen while building, not done* list. Taunya said to build it on 2026-11-21. Step two of two: [v2.8252](./v2.8252.md) gave the read the day, and this makes the clock run to it.

## What was wrong

Where a city's permits name plumbers, Market's clock runs from a building permit to the plumber's permit. The read kept one plumbing day per job, the newest. A job with two plumbing permits was measured to its later one. The clock is there to say how soon a job is taken, and a job is taken the day its first plumbing permit lands.

## The fix

- `loadResearchPermits` asks for `research_permits_v4` first. Each older read still stands in when a newer one is not on the database.
- A row carries `plumber_first_permit_on` beside `plumber_permit_on`.
- `plumberClockDays` in `permitMarket.ts` measures to the first day. A row from an older read has only the newest day and is measured to that, as before.
- A first plumbing permit dated before the building permit still reads as no wait.

`plumber_permit_on` is unchanged. The skim and the ledger say the newest plumbing permit's day on a row, as they did.

## What the clock reads

Read on prod, counts only, a year of commercial permits.

| City | Jobs on the clock | With an earlier first day | Quarter marks before | After |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Austin | 300 | 26 | 1, 7, 16 days | 1, 6, 15 days |
| New Braunfels | 5 | 0 | 0, 6, 9 | the same |

San Antonio, San Marcos and Seguin draw no plumber clock: no builder's permits there name a plumber.

## Tests

- `permitMarket.test.ts`: a job with two plumbing permits is measured to its first, a job with one reads the same, a first permit before the building permit is no wait, and a row from an older read is measured to its newest.
- `permitResearchIo.paging.test.ts`: the read asks v4, then v3, then v2, then the first read, and stops at the first that answers.
