# v2.7343 — Pay calendar: terms can be a number of days after the bill (2026-11-15)

Punch list #341, PR 5 of five: a number of days after the bill. Taunya picked the rest of the train on 2026-11-15. A Pay calendar held one shape of terms: bills in by a cutoff are paid on a set day of a later month. A contract that pays *45 days after an approved application* had no home. #323 left that out on purpose for a payer, since a payer's measured pace is the better guess. A big job on a new contract has no pace of its own, and its contract names the number.

## The call this stands on

**The owner's call 4 is not answered.** It asked whether any big job pays a number of days after the bill, and said: *if none does, PR 5 is never built*. Taunya's pick of the whole train builds it now, **as carded**: the window's first choice becomes how they pay. Nothing reads differently until someone picks the second answer. If no contract ever needs it, the choice can be hidden in `PayCalendarFields.tsx` (the *How they pay* group) and the column stays empty.

## What changed

- **`payer_pay_calendars.days_after`** (migration `20261115190000`): one column, and `set_payer_pay_calendar` takes `{daysAfter}`. A row is a calendar, days after the bill, or a job's off switch, and never two. See `docs/migrations/20261115190000_pay_calendar_days_after.md`.
- **The kernel's second shape.** `PayCalendar` gains `daysAfter`. `dueDayFor` adds the days to the day the bill was accepted, else the day it went in: the same clock a calendar counts from. `nextCutoff` answers none, so every line that speaks of a cutoff draws nothing for such terms: the Ready to Bill and Working cutoff lines, the header chip and Cutoffs coming. The record, the expected day, the waiting lists and the job's own record are built on the due day and read it unchanged.
- **The window.** *How they pay* is the first choice (`PayCalendarFields`): **On a set day of the month**, as before, or **A number of days after the bill**, which asks for the days alone, 1 to 180, starting on 45. The blue box says it back in dates, *A bill accepted today, Nov 12, is due Dec 27.*, and the amber box replays the record against it. It works on the payer's side and on a job's.
- **The words.** *Paid 45 days after the bill* wherever a row says its calendar, *This job's own · Paid 45 days after the bill* on a job's own (`payCalendarShort`), *A bill is paid 45 days after it is accepted.* in a hover (`payCalendarWords`). Where a line names the thing it reads *pay terms*, not *pay calendar*, and *due day*, not *pay day* (`payTermsNoun`): the model's label and hover, Money waiting's small line, the Accounts Receivable hover and the call opener. Bill Customer says *Sent today, this bill is due Dec 27. They pay 45 days after they accept it.*
- **The estimator's part.** A job draft's pay terms use the same fields, so an estimator can write days after the bill on a won bid, and the office's *Check and use* opens the window on them.
- **Either side of the push.** `loadPayCalendars` reads `days_after` and falls back to the old columns on a database without it. A save of days carries no pay day (`payCalendarSavePayload`), so the old function refuses it in words.
- **Guide**: *set a payer's pay calendar* gains *A number of days after the bill*, and its opening and *When to leave it unset* say both shapes.

## Not changed

- A calendar of the first shape, on every screen. It carries no `daysAfter` key at all.
- A payer with no terms set reads the pace guess, as before. The guide still says to leave such a payer unset.
- The window is still called **Pay calendar**, and so is the job window's row.
- The morning run (`messages-nudge-dispatch`) selects its own calendar columns, with no `days_after`. Until it is deployed with the column, a Paid round on such terms reads the pace guess. It wants a deploy on Taunya's go.

## Deploy

The edge copies of `payCalendar.ts`, `billedDuePay.ts` and `siteMoney`'s readers under `supabase/functions/_shared/gcKernels/` are regenerated; `npm run check:edge-drift` lists the functions as behind until the next deploy of each.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **After the bill is accepted, not after it is approved.** The card's gap said *or after it is approved*. The app holds one day a payer took a bill, the accepted day, and the office sets it under *Counted from*. A contract that counts from an approval the office hears of later is set there.
- **The name.** Terms in days are not a calendar, and the window is still called Pay calendar. Renaming the window, the row and the guide is a sweep of its own, and the owner has not asked for it.
- **A payer with a real pace.** For a payer with many payments the measured pace may still be the better guess than *net 45*, which is why #323 left this out. The amber box shows the record against the days before the save, so the office can see which is closer.
- **180 days is the ceiling.** A longer term is a retainage release, and that is a bill's own due day (v2.7341).
