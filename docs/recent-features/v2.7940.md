# v2.7940 — Permit news: a place to keep the answer on a line (2026-11-19)

Punch list #401, PR 2 of the train in [`to-dos/permit-news-on-the-bids/`](../../to-dos/permit-news-on-the-bids/README.md). One migration, [`20261119130000_bid_permit_answers`](../migrations/20261119130000_bid_permit_answers.md), and nothing else.

## Why

A permit news line had one way out, marking the bid lost. A line that was true but not yet an outcome, or not about the job at all, stood for good, and in nine days nobody answered one. The lens on Followup (v2.7941) asks about each line and counts the ones nobody has answered. That count needs the answers kept where every person and every device reads them, so it is a table and not the browser.

## What changed

- **`bid_permit_answers`**, append-only: the bid, the permit by the city's own number and type, the answer (`this_job`, `not_this_job`, `taken_back`), who and when. Read and insert for `user_can_research_permits()`, an insert only as oneself. No update and no delete.

## Why it ships alone

The deploy refuses a client that names a table the database does not have yet (`check:calls-ahead -- --prod`), and it goes on refusing until the migration is pushed. A migration merged with its client would hold every deploy from the merge to the push. Merged alone, with no line of `src` naming the table, it holds nothing. The read, the write and the lens follow in v2.7941, after the push.
