# v2.6906 — Permits: New Braunfels cards name the general contractor (punch list #292) (2026-11-10)

Seen in the first pull of New Braunfels: 1,826 rows made 561 permits. The city's layer writes a permit once for every person on it (each applicant, the general contractor, each trade's contractor), and the pull kept whichever row came last, so who pulled it was left to chance.

## What changed

- **`_shared/permitCities.ts`**: a `FeedReader` may carry `prefer(held, next)`, which of two rows of one permit to keep. `preferNewBraunfels` ranks a row by `newBraunfelsNameRank`: a general contractor's row, then a row with no contractor (the applicant), then a trade's; among equals the row with the lower `OBJECTID`, the one the city wrote first. The answer is the same whichever order the rows arrive in.
- **`permit-pull`**: where a page hands the same permit and type twice it asks the feed's `prefer`, and keeps the later row for a feed that has none, as before.

## Read live before building

75 days through the reader, read-only: of 101 commercial building permits the new rule changes the name on 14, one of them from a trade to the general contractor.

## Not handled

Two rows of one permit that fall on either side of a page boundary are not compared: the later page's row is written over the earlier. Rows of one permit sit together in the read's order, so this takes a permit split exactly at row 1,000.
