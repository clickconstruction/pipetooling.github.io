# v2.4471 — Customers: a job or a customer made in the evening keeps its day (2026-10-03)

One of the instant-day fixes that follow [v2.4452](v2.4452.md); [v2.4459](v2.4459.md) fixed the customer profile's bill days. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

Customer readers cut days out of `timestamptz` columns by their first ten characters, so anything stamped after 7 pm CDT (6 pm CST) read as the next day. Two of them write the day:

- **The HCP payment backfill and the tips sweep** ([`backfillHcpPayments.ts`](../../src/lib/customers/backfillHcpPayments.ts), [`hcpTipsSweep.ts`](../../src/lib/customers/hcpTipsSweep.ts)): the payment's `paid_on`, a `date` the Apply step writes, falls back to the job's `jobs_ledger.created_at` when the HCP export has no paid, completed or created day. A job made in the evening got the next day. The export's own cells are wall-clock times (`2026-02-01 08:00`) and keep their slice.
- **Customer since** on the customer page ([`CustomerDetail.tsx`](../../src/pages/CustomerDetail.tsx)) and the profile window ([`CustomerProfileModal.tsx`](../../src/components/customers/CustomerProfileModal.tsx)): `date_met`, a `date`, else `customers.created_at`. A customer added on the last evening of a month read the next month.
- **An estimate's day** on the customer snapshot ([`CustomerSnapshotModal.tsx`](../../src/components/customers/CustomerSnapshotModal.tsx), `estimates.updated_at`).
- **The phone's last activity** ([`customerPhoneSearch.ts`](../../src/lib/customers/customerPhoneSearch.ts), `phoneActivityWords`): *today*, *yesterday*, *3 d ago* from the newest of a job's or a bid's `created_at` and a payment's `paid_on`. A job made last evening read *today*.

## What changed

Each instant reads `calendarYmdInAppTzFromIso(iso)` from [`dateUtils.ts`](../../src/utils/dateUtils.ts) (`APP_CALENDAR_TZ`); `date_met` and the export's cells keep their slice. The phone's activity is a day or an instant by source, so `phoneActivityWords` reads an instant's day and keeps a bare day, as `loadBridgeVectors.ts` does.

## Tests

- `backfillHcpPayments.test.ts` and `hcpTipsSweep.test.ts` (+1 each): a job made at `2026-02-27T00:30:00Z` (6:30 pm CST on Feb 26) pays on Feb 26, and one at `2026-10-03T00:30:00Z` (7:30 pm CDT) on Oct 2; noon UTC keeps its own day.
- `customerPhoneSearch.test.ts` (+1): a job made at 7:30 pm CDT on Sep 26 reads *yesterday* on Sep 27; a bare paid day still reads as itself.

All 3 fail on main. Customer since and the snapshot's estimate day change one expression each, and no render smoke draws them.

## Checked against prod

Read only, from the worktree dev server: 283 of the 457 customers have no date met, and 2 of those were added on a month's last evening, so their Customer since moves back a month. 3 of the 36 estimates were last updated in a Central evening. Of the 2 paid jobs still without a payment record, neither was made in an evening, so a backfill run today writes the same days.
