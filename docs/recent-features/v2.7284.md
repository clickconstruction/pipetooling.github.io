# v2.7284 — The job window on a phone: the Bill tab in a phone's order (2026-11-14)

Punch list #355, PR F ([`to-dos/job-window-phone.md`](../../to-dos/job-window-phone.md), pass 3; the mock-up [`to-dos/job-window-phone-tabs.html`](../../to-dos/job-window-phone-tabs.html)). Client only: no migration, no edge function, and no kernel that is copied to the edge functions changed.

## Before

Measured at 375 px, Bill ran 2.9 screens with 72 presses, 22 of them under 32 px: the line items first with a 24 px trash and 10 px ▲▼ arrows on every row, a 12 px *Multiple Segment Generator* link, and the money card and Make a bill a screen and a half down. The office types lines at a desk and sends a bill from a phone, so the phone opened on the wrong half.

## What changed

- **`JobFormModal.tsx`**: the line items section sits in a `data-job-form-lines` wrapper with `order: 9` on a phone in the embedded form, so the Bill tab reads money card, Make a bill, bills, payments, then the lines. The desk's order is unchanged.
- **`JobFormFixturesSection.tsx`**: on a phone the ▲▼ column and the trash leave each row; a 40 px **⋯** at the end of the count and price line opens a `PhoneSheet` named for the line, with Move up, Move down (dead at the ends) and Remove this line (a job's sole line has no Remove, as before). The count and the price stay live fields. *Multiple Segment Generator* is a 40 px outline press, + Add line item and − Add discount are 40 px, the scope pencil is a thumb's square. A discount row keeps its own stacked line and trash.
- `JobFormFixturesSection.render.test.tsx`: the phone's row has the ⋯ and no trash or arrows; the sheet's three rows, Move up dead on the first line.

## Is this the best we can do?

The card first asked for the line items as cards with the fields behind ⋯; the owner caught that a price change would then take two taps, so the fields stay live and only the moves and the remove go behind ⋯, which is also the safer place for Remove on a phone. The order puts a phone's act first: the office sends a bill from a phone and types the lines at a desk. What is not done: the money card's draw rows keep their 22 px tick boxes, which the row's whole height makes easy enough to hit, and the discount row keeps its small trash.

## Verify

At 375 px open a job, press Bill: the money card is first, the line items last; a line's ⋯ opens the sheet; Move up is dead on the first line; + Add line item is 40 px. At a desk the lines are first and every press is as it was.
