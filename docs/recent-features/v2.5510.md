# v2.5510 — Follow-ups: a job with no crew on site in 21 days joins the deck, with a Next line (2026-10-13)

The owner's ask of 2026-10-13: Taunya's Dashboard card *18 open jobs have sat idle 21+ days* promised "a bill, an inspection, or to be closed" and opened Job Summary's Cycle view, an analytics page whose list said how long, not what; a row click opened Job Summary's Jobs table; and the Follow-ups deck, where she follows up, said *All caught up*, because its clock counts notes and status changes while the card's counts approved field days. The approved recommendation makes idle jobs part of the one follow-up flow, with a reason, a door and a memory per job. This is PR 1 of 4: the kernel and the store. No migration, no edge function.

- **`src/lib/jobs/jobFollowupQueue.ts`** (pure, tested): the idle rule. `jobFollowupIdleDays` is the Cycle view's clock (`staleOpenJobs`): days since `jobs_ledger.last_work_date` (the clock-session trigger's max approved work day), else since the day the job was made; only Working and Waiting jobs have one, and a Waiting job only once a crew has been on site. `computeJobFollowupQueue` admits a card when `idleDays ≥ JOB_FOLLOWUP_IDLE_DAYS` (21) whatever its quiet days, after the same Looks fine rest, snooze and next-visit exclusions as a quiet card. The entry gains `why` (`idle` wins over `quiet`), `idleDays`, and `need` (`jobFollowupNeed`: Bill it for Ready to Bill and for Working at 100%, Schedule the next visit or close it for Working under that, Put the next visit on the schedule for Waiting, Nudge the customer for payment for Billed, Work the account for Collections). An idle card's reason reads *No field work in 46 days · 70% done · $41,550 bid* (Waiting: *· nothing on the schedule*). The sort ranks an idle card by its field-day gap, a quiet card by its quiet days, so the longest-neglected job deals first. `jobFollowupIdleSummary` (count, contract total, how many are the reader's by `master_user_id`) and `jobFollowupQuietEntries` feed the two Dashboard cards.
- **`jobFollowupStore.ts`**: the candidates select adds `last_work_date`, `created_at`, `master_user_id`; the activity RPC is unchanged.
- **`useJobFollowupNudge.ts`**: the *waiting on a follow-up* card counts the quiet cards alone, so the idle card (PR 3) and it never count one job twice.
- **`JobsFollowupModal.tsx`**: the revisit card's hand-built entry carries the new fields; the deck already shows idle cards with their Why it's here line through the shared kernel. The Idle lens, the Next line and the door come in PR 2.
- **Tests**: `jobFollowupQueue.test.ts` gains seven cases (the clock per stage, an idle card beside a quiet one, the twenty-day edge, the three exclusions, the sort, the Dashboard figures, the Next line per stage).
- **Guide** *follow up on quiet jobs* says what an idle card is and that notes do not clear it.
