# v2.3797 — The law firm's portal reads the owner's answers under each notice (2026-09-24)

Punch list **#41**, PR 1b — the last of the train (PR 1 v2.3787, PR 2 v2.3789, PR 3 v2.3790); the to-do folder `to-dos/legal-portal-lien-desk/` is retired with this PR (its mock-up is in git history at the deleting commit). No migration; the `legal-portal` edge function's select list changes (deploy after merge — `npm run check:edge-drift`).

## Why

PR 1 drew the envelope table and left the mock-up's band empty: the owner's three answers (#33 PR 3, v2.3767), letter two's clock (#33 PR 2, v2.3760) and the GC's written okay live on the § 53.056 desk item that sent the paper, and the portal function never read `job_lien_desk_items`. Counsel's grid on the portal showed the answers as columns; the paper they answer did not. The desk's own Paper tab and the printed packet had the same hole.

## What shipped

- **`src/lib/jobs/lienNoticeDraft.ts`** — `parseLienNoticeSentFacts` reads the three sent-notice facts (`letterTwo`, `gcAuthorizedDirectPay`, `ownerCall`) on their own; `parseLienDeskDraftFields` spreads it. **`lienLetterTwo.ts`** reads through it, so an item without the notice block (the portal's) still clocks; its input is `LetterTwoItemLike` (a Pick of the row) and `LETTER_TWO_NONE` is exported.
- **`src/lib/legal/legalLienPaper.ts`** — `LegalEnvelope` gains `filingIds`, `letterTwo` and `answers`. `attachEnvelopeAnswers(envelopes, { items, openBalanceOf, todayYmd })` joins each § 53.056 envelope to the desk items whose `sent_filing_id` is one of its rows: an item carrying `letterTwo` makes the envelope a letter two (named by `envelopeKindWords`, no band); otherwise the band — the latest owner's call across the items, `affidavitPileFor`, `reservationHoldEndsOn`, the GC's okay, and `letterTwoStatus` per covered job (the clock belongs to the job's first packet, so an older notice reads `none`). `envelopeAnswersWords` is the one wording for the three surfaces: *Oct 1, Taunya — still owes Lenox $41,200 · reserved the 10% and still holds it · their contract completes Nov 30 → pile A: the claim is trapped under § 53.081; the § 53.101 hold runs to Dec 30*; letter two's clock folded to one line when the covered jobs agree, named per job when they differ; *no call recorded yet* when nothing is.
- **`legalPacket.ts`** — `LegalPacketInput.lienDeskItems` (optional; absent reads as no answers); the envelopes are attached in the build. **`legalPortalPayload.ts`** — `LegalPortalMatter.lienDeskItems`, parsed defensively, `[]` from an older function. **`useLegalPacketData.ts`** — the desk reads the jobs' live notice items itself.
- **`LegalFirmMatterView`**, **`LegalDeskModal`** (Paper tab) and **`legalPacketPrint`** — a full-width band row under each first-packet notice (`PortalTable` / `Table` take `subRows`); the Paper cell names a letter two.
- **`supabase/functions/legal-portal`** — selects the jobs' live `notice_53_056` desk items and hands each over with `fields` shaped down to the three facts; the sample matter carries none.
- Guide *share your attorney their portal* and `docs/GLOSSARY.md` → Legal desk describe the band; `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` → legal-portal lists `lienDeskItems`.
