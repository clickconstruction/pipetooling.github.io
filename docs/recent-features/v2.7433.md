# v2.7433 — Lien desk: the press reads Change the last day of work… (2026-11-16)

Taunya, on two To do rows whose notice on record misses a month (*September 2026 has no notice. The one on record names April, June, July and August 2026.*), asked: "why does it say 'the last day of work is wrong'?" The words were a press, a door for the case where the job's last day is the thing that is off. Under the red heading *Will not make tomorrow as they stand* they read as the app's finding about the job. Offered the press worded as an act, she answered "Yes, reword it". Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What changed

- [`src/lib/jobs/lienNoticeOwed.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienNoticeOwed.ts): `LIEN_NOTICE_OWED_DOORS.lastDay` is **Change the last day of work…**, with one ellipsis character as the desk's other presses that open a window write it (*Take back…*). It was *The last day of work is wrong*, since v2.7269. Every row that offers the door reads the constant, so the To do view on a computer and on a phone change together.
- The press does what it did: it opens the Lien window with the last day ready to change.
- The row's presses now read **Open the notice**, **The paper named September 2026 too** and **Change the last day of work…**. The second is as it was; she approved rewording this one only.
- Guide `send-lien-notices-from-the-lien-desk` names the press by its new words. The comment in `LienDesk.tsx` that quoted the old words follows.

## Not changed

- The release note and fragment of v2.7269 record the words that shipped then and are left as they are. So is the punch-list card `to-dos/affidavits-picked-set-and-notice-owed.md`, which records the design as it was drawn on 2026-11-14.
