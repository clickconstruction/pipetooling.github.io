# v2.8199 — Punch list: tick the cards you are working on (2026-11-21)

The screen for [v2.8196](./v2.8196.md)'s table. No migration.

## Why

Taunya's ask of 2026-11-21, in her words: *"on the https://clicktooling.com/punch-list page, I would like a checkbox that when I can click next to each punchlist item for myself to know if I am working on it or not, I would like the checkbox to be persistant between sessions if I unclick it or click it, and when a card is moved from ready to build to close out or any section to another the checkmark should be wiped"*.

## What changed

- **A checkbox beside every card**, in both views. In **By group** it sits under the card's number. In **By number** it opens the row, and the card opened under a row draws no second one.
- **A tick is the person's own.** It is a row in `punch_list_ticks` by the card's number, read and written by that person alone. It is there after a reload, after signing out and in, and on a phone as well as a desk. The page reads the ticks again each time the tab comes back to the front.
- **A move wipes it.** A tick remembers the section its card was in. The page shows it only while the card is still there. A card that moved to another section, or left the list, shows no tick and its row is deleted.
- **Working on**, a pill in the filter row, carries the count of ticks and narrows both views to those cards.
- A card with no number yet takes no tick. That is a to-do being written on a dev server.

## The rules

`src/lib/todos/punchListTicks.ts` holds them, with tests.

- The same section: the tick **shows**.
- Another section, or the card is gone: the tick is not shown. The row is **cleared** when this page's build is newer than the build that made the tick, and only **hidden** otherwise.
- Flagging is not a move. A flagged card keeps its group, so it keeps its tick.

The hidden case is for an old copy of the page left open on a phone. To that copy, a card filed since looks like a card that left the list. It must not delete a tick made on a newer board. The build's newest version rides on every tick for this, and the page compares it with its own.

A card can move in a PR that ships no version. The first build after such a move hides the tick and leaves the row. The next build with a newer version deletes it.

The page clears with no press only on the built site, and never under View as. A branch's board on a dev server can be behind `main`, and a look writes nothing by itself. Both still hide what does not match.

## Failures

- **A save that fails** puts the box back as it was. A toast says which card and why, through `formatErrorMessage`.
- **A read that fails** leaves every card on the page. A line under the board's notes says the ticks could not be read, with the reason, on `OfflineRetryPanel` with `idempotent`. A lost signal offers Retry and reads again by itself.
- A press made while a read is in flight is not undone by that read.

## Who sees what

- **A dev or a master** opens the page and has their own ticks.
- **Training mode:** the page opens and the person's ticks read. A tick or an untick is refused by the write fence. The box goes back and the toast says why.
- **Archived:** meets the Away note before the page.
- **View as:** the look shows the imitated person's ticks, since the session is theirs. A press there saves as that person, as every press under View as does. The page deletes nothing by itself.
