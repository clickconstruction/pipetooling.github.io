Migration `20261116123000_my_sub_sheets_on_job.sql` ([`docs/migrations/20261116123000_my_sub_sheets_on_job.md`](../migrations/20261116123000_my_sub_sheets_on_job.md)), lines in the SQL bed `plan_sets_author`, and the plain job window on a phone.

## Before

A subcontractor read their pay in one place, their portal: each sheet with its agreed price, what has been paid, what is open and the day it pays. The job window, which they open on site, said nothing about money.

## What changed

- **`my_sub_sheets_on_job(job)`** (new, definer): the caller's own sheets on one job, with the items and the payments, exactly the rows the portal's function reads, and the caller's own portal address. Whose a sheet is comes from the roster: the person whose account is the caller, and the sheets the junction ties to that person.
- **[`src/lib/jobs/subPayRow.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/subPayRow.ts)** (new, with tests): `mySubSheetsFromRpc` runs the portal's own kernel, `buildSubSheets`, on those rows, so agreed, paid, back-charged and open are the portal's numbers and not a second sum. `subPayWords` adds the job's sheets up and puts them in words:
  - *$2,100.00 open*, then *$4,200.00 agreed · $2,100.00 paid* (*nothing paid yet*, and a back-charge when there is one);
  - then what decides when it pays, the first that applies: the office's hold and its reason, the earliest day the office has promised (*Payable after Nov 21, 2026*, the portal's words), else the stage of the sheet furthest behind (*Stage: Work*, *Pre-inspection*, *Post-inspection: Trigger draw*);
  - *Paid in full* with *Last payment Nov 24, 2026* when nothing is open;
  - *2 sheets on this job* above the money when there are several;
  - *No sheet in your name here. The office has not put your work on this job yet.* when there is none.
- **[`src/hooks/useMySubPay.ts`](../../src/hooks/useMySubPay.ts)** (new): the read. A refusal or a missing function draws no row: it never says *no sheet* on a read that failed.
- **[`src/components/jobs/DetailJobModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/DetailJobModal.tsx)**: for a subcontractor on a phone, the row sits under What's happening, after the scheduled block and before Last note, with **Open your portal** when they have an address or a live link. What's happening is drawn for a sub whenever the row is.

## What was wrong in what Taunya was first told

Two things. First she was told the app could not say where a sub's pay stands; it can, on every sheet and on their portal, and that was corrected with the picture. Then the picture's own notes said a sheet is tied to its sub by a typed name, so the row would be right for 1 to 3 of 12 and wrong for the rest. That was also wrong, and she picked with it on the page: the tie is through the roster and holds for all 12 crew rows read on prod. Nine of those 12 have no sheet of their own on the job they are on, and for them *No sheet in your name here* is the fact. The page is corrected and she was told with this change.

## What a person loses

Nothing. Helpers, the superintendent and estimators see no row. The office's screens are untouched.

## What this does not do

- **A desk.** The row is on the phone's window, where What's happening is. A sub at a desk width reads their portal as before.
- **A sheet with two names.** The portal shows such a sheet whole to each person on it, and the row does the same. Punch list #346 is the work that makes a sheet one person's.
- **The sub's *Your work* card** (their work order and price in place of Today's work) is not built.

## Is this the best we can do?

The row could have summed the sheets in SQL. Then the window and the portal would be two sums of the same money, and the first rule change would split them. Handing the rows over and running the portal's kernel costs one import and keeps one sum.
