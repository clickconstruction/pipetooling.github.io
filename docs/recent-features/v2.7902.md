# v2.7902 — Permits on a phone: the lit step in view, a lit chip bold from the first draw (2026-11-19)

The two things v2.7878 saw and left alone. Each was measured on a local preview against live data at 375 px before the change and again after it. Only steps and view chips were pressed. No migration, no edge function, no new page.

## 1. The step strip opened with its lit step off screen

Under the filters `ProspectsResearchTab` draws **Find**, **Skim**, **Investigating**, **Pursuing**, **Closed**, **Builders**, **Ledger** and **Map** in one strip, a sideways scroller on a phone. v2.7878 read from the code that nothing brought the lit step into view. Measured, it is so: at 375 px the strip is 351 px wide and holds 623 px of steps, and `?tab=permits&view=builders` opened it at `scrollLeft` 0.

| At 375 px, `?tab=permits&view=builders` | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| The strip's `scrollLeft` | 0 | 168 |
| The strip on screen | x 12 to 363 | x 12 to 363 |
| The **Builders** step | x 431 to 507, off screen | x 263 to 339, lit and whole |
| The page's own scroll | 0 | 0 |

- **The same hook serves it.** `useLitPillInView` takes a third argument, the mark the row puts on its lit pill. It stays `aria-current="true"` for a row of tabs (`LIT_CURRENT`, the default). The step strip passes `LIT_PRESSED`, because its steps are toggle presses that already carry `aria-pressed`.
- **When it looks again:** when the view changes, when the counts arrive, when the second-look segment joins or leaves Skim, when the city changes and when the window changes width. A count that ticks down during a skim does not move the strip.
- A step pressed near the edge slides in whole with 24 px of its neighbour showing. **Map** pressed from Builders ends at x 363, the strip's own edge.
- A desk draws the strip wrapped, not scrolled, and it stays at `scrollLeft` 0 (seen at 1280 px).

## 2. A lit chip was not bold until a press

`PermitPlaceList` styles the Investigating chips, the Closed chips and **List** / **On a map** with one `chip(on)`. It set `fontSize: '0.78rem'` and `fontWeight`, then `font: 'inherit'` last. The shorthand resets both, so every chip drew at the page's 16 px and weight 400. A press changed `fontWeight` alone, which is why the lit chip turned bold only then, and React warned in the console on each press.

| At 375 px, Closed in New Braunfels | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| The lit chip when the list opens | 16 px, weight 400 | 12.48 px, weight 600 |
| An unlit chip | 16 px, weight 400 | 12.48 px, weight 400 |
| A chip's height | 36 px | 32 px |
| React's warning in the console on a chip press | yes | none |

- The chip now takes `fontFamily: 'inherit'` alone, so the size and weight it was written with hold on the first draw and on every press.
- **The chips are smaller than they drew before.** 0.78rem is what the style always said, and it sits one step under the strip's 0.8125rem above it. **Passed** and **Has a plumber** now end at x 234 where they ended at 266.
