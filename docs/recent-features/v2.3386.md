# v2.3386 — Contract sweep PR 1: the readiness kernel and the list (2026-09-14)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Third PR of the train in [`to-dos/contract-sweep-seen-first/`](../../to-dos/contract-sweep-seen-first/README.md) (PR 0 v2.3384 the floor + Not needed; PR 0c v2.3385 the job-form door).

## Why

The sweep's "ready" meant "the email parses", so J683 — named "Job", no fixtures, no amount — counted among the 87 ready and Send all would have mailed *Work we'll do: Job · Billed at completion* to a customer. Builders were listed for "send our agreement" when their own subcontract is the paper. Send all was the footer's only primary.

## What shipped

- **Kernel [`contractSweepRowState.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/contractSweepRowState.ts)** (+6 tests): `assessContractSweepRows` names each row — `ready` · `thin_scope` (the scope is empty or only the job's name) · `no_amount` · `no_email` · `gc_job` (a GC that is not the customer row) — plus `sameEmailAs` (the *+ J798* chip), `readyForBulk` (email ok, real scope, an amount, not a GC job) and the row's one `action` (`send` · `fix_email` · `file_theirs` · `add_scope`). `contractSweepSummary` counts rows, dollars and **distinct customers** Send all would email; `contractSweepFilterMatches` splits *To send* from *Needs a look*. `CONTRACT_SWEEP_FLAG_LABELS` carries the words and the hover text.
- **The list** [`JobsContractSweepModal`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsContractSweepModal.tsx) (+2 render cases): the instruction paragraph is gone; the header reads *104 without a contract · $1,349,981 of work · 17 need a look* (and *under $2,500 left out* when the floor is set); **To send · N | Needs a look · N | All · N**; each row is job · customer, the amount in its own column, address · stage, the signer email (still editable here until the pane lands), the chips, and one button — **Send**, **Fix email**, **File theirs** (the Contract modal opened onto the filing sheet), or **Add scope** (the Contract modal). Rows are assessed from the same prefill the send mints.
- **Send all** moves to the **⋯** menu (`ArHeaderMenu`, shared with Accounts Receivable): *Send all 87 ready… · 81 customers · Built-in service agreement terms · skips Needs a look*; choosing it swaps the footer for a confirm bar that names the customers, the agreements, the terms and the reminders, then sends only `readyForBulk` rows. ⋯ also holds **Filter the Pipeline to these jobs** (closes the sweep, sets the No-contract filter).
- **The sweep's document equals the modal's**: the list batch-reads each row's customer-accepted estimate (lines + total) and hands it to [`quickSendJobContract`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobContractQuickSend.ts), which gains `estimateLines` / `acceptedTotalCents` — the prefill the Contract modal already used.
- Help guide *get a job contract signed* → "Clear the backlog" rewritten for the states and the ⋯ menu.

## Not in this PR
