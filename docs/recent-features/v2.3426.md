# v2.3426 — Job accounts at the counter, PR 3: the PO moment (2026-09-14)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Third of the five-PR train (v2.3423 the record and the roster · v2.3424 the strip and the errand).

## Why

The PO Generator names the job and the supply house right before the tech buys — it is the natural place for "reach Curly before ordering parts". It said nothing about the account. Now it does, and it never blocks the code.

## What shipped

- **[`JobAccountPoLine`](../../src/components/materials/JobAccountPoLine.tsx)** — one component, rendered once the job and the house are both picked: in Materials → PO Generator under the supply house field, and in Dispatch Mode → PO under step 3 (compact) and again on the result screen under the big code. Reads the same `list_job_account_strip` RPC through `useJobAccountStrips([jobId])` and picks the house's entry.
- **Kernel** `poMomentLine(entry, jobLabel)` in [`jobAccountStrip.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobAccountStrip.ts) (+4 tests): null when the house neither expects an account nor has a row; amber *No job account at {house} for {job} yet. {house} expects one per property. {rep} opens them: {phone}.* with Call · Mark opened… · Send the packet · Not needed; amber *asked {date} from the counter — not open yet* for a pending ask (same actions); teal *{house} job account open · ref … · {date} · by phone · rep …* with no actions; muted *not needed · {reason}* with Mark opened only.
- **Mark opened / Not needed** open PR 1's `MarkJobAccountOpenedModal` with the rep from the strip; on save the strip reloads and, if the row was `requested`, the job's open `open_job_account` errand is closed with the note (`closeOpenJobAccountRequests`) so the inbox card clears and the tech gets the push. **Send the packet** opens the job window with the Share-with-supply-house modal on top (`openJobDetail({ openSupplyHouseShare: true })`).
- Guides: *raise a purchase order* (the line under Generate), *dispatch mode* (the PO bullet), *open a job account before buying parts* (a section for the office's PO moment).
