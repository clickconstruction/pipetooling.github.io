# v2.6136 — Supply-house bills from a thread: name the sender, and a rep's bill counts (punch list #179, a follow-up) (2026-10-30)

The thread door's live walk (v2.6095) stopped at two gaps. A ZZ test forward to team@ was filed on a new *Who is this?* sender, as `gmail-sync` designs, but nothing in the app could name that sender as a supply house. And a bill on a rep's line was offered on the thread but counted nowhere. On prod every supply-house contact came from the 2026-10-13 backfill: 16 house counters, which carry only the house's phone, and 7 reps with their address. None has been made since. So before this, an emailed bill was offered only from those 7 addresses, and never counted.

## What changed

- **Who is this? finds supply houses and their reps** (`searchContactRecords` in `src/lib/messages/messagesIo.ts`):
  - It finds the `supply_house` kind of vendor by name, and an unarchived rep at one by name or by address. The hit reads *Supply house* or *At Ridgeway Supply*.
  - `orderHitsByBox` already puts them first for bids@.
  - `name_contact` already took both records (`20261015010000`). Naming the address as the house folds it into the house's counter when it has one. A house with none, or a person named at the house, ties this contact to it. A rep's address folds into the rep's contact.
- **The window** (`WhoIsThisWindow.tsx`):
  - A supply house's record fixes the kind, as the roster does. The select gives way to *On Supply houses, so a supply house. Their PDFs and pictures can be recorded as bills.*
  - A house asks for the person's own name, as a customer or a trade partner does.
  - The empty line names the supply house beside the customer and the trade partner.
- **A rep's bill counts at the house** (`loadCandidates` in `billsToRecordIo.ts`, through the new pure `houseOfContacts` in `billsToRecord.ts`):
  - The bills to record are read on the house's own contacts and on each rep's contact at the house.
  - So the row's *N bills to record* and the form's *Start from* chips hold a rep's PDFs, as the thread's offer (`houseOfContact`) already did.
  - A rep read that fails leaves the house's own lines.
- Guides: *record a supply-house bill from its PDF* says how an unnamed sender's bill gets its offer. *find every message with a person in one place* says a supply house needs no pick.

The client only. No migration and no function.
