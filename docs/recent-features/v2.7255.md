# v2.7255 — Lien affidavits: the notice record explains itself (2026-11-14)

Four changes to the words and the layout of the notice record, each from the owner's reading of a printed batch on 2026-11-14 (punch list #322). The first and largest: a block's closing line read *Sentences 5 and 6 match the record.* His words: *I don't understand… What does that mean? I need better context.* The line named two sentences of a paper the reader did not have in hand, and did not say what had been checked or why it mattered.

## What the line checks (unchanged)

The short-form affidavit's fifth sentence swears the months of work, and its sixth the method and the day each notice went to the owner. The record compares the paper's months to the months the job's recorded § 53.056 notices name, and the paper's method and days to the owner's copies on record. It is a proofreading check: the paper says what the mail record says. It is not a check that a notice was on time. The grey box at the foot of the block says that, and v2.7218 left it there. This change touches no comparison and no condition; a line is `ok` or `ask` exactly when it was.

## What changed

- **One line, and it says what the paper swears.** `lienNoticeRecordPaperLine` in `src/lib/jobs/lienNoticeRecord.ts` writes the paper's line, the first of `LienNoticeRecord.lines`. Until now the kernel wrote two, led *Sentence 5.* and *Sentence 6.*, and the batch block folded them into *Sentences 5 and 6 match the record.* Now, when both agree: *The paper matches the record.* (`LIEN_RECORD_MATCH`), then *It swears August 2026, and certified mail on September 22, 2026.*
- **A difference is said plainly, once.** *The paper and the record differ.* (`LIEN_RECORD_DIFFER`), then what each says, then one instruction: *The paper says certified mail on September 22, 2026. The record shows certified mail on September 24, 2026. Fix one before this is signed.* The months case reads *The paper says the work was in … The record shows …* When both differ the lead is said once and the line closes *Fix both before this is signed.* No line a reader sees names a sentence by its number.
- **A blank is not called a difference.** A paper with no month, or a blank where the day goes, and nothing on record to fill it, reads *The paper is not complete.* (`LIEN_RECORD_BLANK`), the two blank sentences as they were, and *Add what is missing before this is signed.* A blank the record could fill is a difference and says what the record shows.
- **The single page and the batch block share the line.** Both draw it through `lineItem` in `src/lib/jobs/lienNoticeRecordSheet.ts` over the kernel's lines, so the box headed *The paper against the record* and a block's closing line cannot drift. The block keeps its tail, *Nothing is missing from the record.* (`LIEN_RECORD_NOTHING_MISSING`), where it printed before.
- **How to read it, once, at the top.** `LIEN_RECORD_HOW` is a small box under the batch page's lead (*How to read a block*) and under the job's facts on the single page (*How to read this page*). Five lines: the paper swears two facts about notice, the table is the mail record behind them, a ✓ means the two say the same thing, a ✓ does not mean the notice was on time, and keep the receipts. A batch made only of jobs contracted with the owner has no table and no line, so it has no box.
- **The grey box is untouched.** Its place, its fill and its lead are v2.7218's. When no month is late and the paper differs, it carries the new line's words in place of *Sentence 6. …*, because it reads the same line.
- Both guides say it: *file a lien and never miss its deadlines* in the paragraph on the notice record, and *send lien notices from the Lien desk* in a short paragraph under the batch print that links there.

## The same day, three more from the owner

**The *Left off the paper.* line is one sentence.** He read *The notices also name April, June and July 2026. The paper leaves them off, as outside this job's work dates.* and said it *could almost fit on one line*. It now reads *April, June and July 2026 are on the notice but outside this job's work dates.* One month reads *is*. The lead and when the line prints are unchanged. On the sample print the three-month line sits on one line of the block.

**A missing line leads with what to do.** He asked whether *Missing. Notice 1 has no copy to the original contractor on record.* could be better: the block said that fact in the table and in the grey box too, and never what to do. He chose *To add, if mailed. The original contractor's copy of notice 1.* The words *if mailed* are deliberate: the line asks someone to record a copy that really went out, never to fill a blank. A gap now carries its own lead (`LienNoticeRecordGap`, `{ lead, words }`, from `lienNoticeRecordGaps`), where the page had put *Missing.* in front of every one. What counts as a gap, and their order, are unchanged. The first row is the owner's wording. The rest give the sibling lines the same shape and are for his look:

| Was (after *Missing.* in a block) | Now |
| --- | --- |
| Notice 1 has no copy to the original contractor on record. | **To add, if mailed.** The original contractor's copy of notice 1. |
| Notice 1 has no copy to the owner on record. | **To add, if mailed.** The owner's copy of notice 1. |
| Notice 1 has no day mailed for the copy to the owner. | **To add.** The day mailed for the owner's copy of notice 1. |
| Notice 1 has no mail number for the copy to the owner. | **To add.** The mail number for the owner's copy of notice 1. |
| Notice 1 has no saved copy of the paper. | **To add.** A saved copy of notice 1. |
| Notice 1 names no month of work. | **To add.** The months of work for notice 1. |
| Notice 1 waits on the mail provider for the number of the copy to the owner. | **Waiting.** The mail provider has yet to send the number for the owner's copy of notice 1. |
| Notice 1 has the same mail number on both copies. Each envelope has its own. | **To check.** Notice 1 has the same mail number on both copies. Each envelope has its own. |
| Notice 1 has no record of its words, so no copy of it prints. | **No copy prints.** Notice 1 has no record of its words. |

The single page keeps its box headed *Missing from the record* and the line under it that says where to add (`LIEN_RECORD_FIX`); a batch says that line once, above the blocks.

**The table has no row of column titles.** His words: *I don't love how the header line is separated from the only other line that matters as a result of it.* The row COPY TO / DAY MAILED / HOW / MAIL NUMBER sat above a notice's grey band, and the copies it was about sat below the band. The row is gone on both shapes. A notice's band is first, its copies sit right under it, and each cell names itself:

| Was | Now |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Copy to the owner |
| Original contractor | Copy to the original contractor |
| Sep 22, 2026 | Mailed Sep 22, 2026 |
| a certified mail or courier number | *No.* and the number |
| **No day on record** | **No day mailed on record** (`LIEN_RECORD_NO_DAY`) |
| **No number on record** | **No mail number on record** (`LIEN_RECORD_NO_NUMBER`) |
| Coming from the mail provider | Mail number coming from the mail provider (`LIEN_RECORD_NUMBER_COMING`) |
| None for this method | No mail number for this method (`LIEN_RECORD_NUMBER_NONE`) |

A copy by hand keeps who signed for it and an email keeps its id, as recorded and with no *No.* in front: only a certified letter or a courier's parcel has a number. *No copy on record* keeps its one wide bold cell, and the row for a note on the saved copy and the small *Receipt photo on file* line are as they were. The first column is 29% of the table and does not wrap, so *Copy to the original contractor* stays on one line. Which rows print and what the record holds are unchanged.

## One word changed from the approved wording

The approved fourth line of the box read *An Ask first box says when it was not.* On these pages no box is named *Ask first*: since v2.7218 the grey box leads *Ask the attorney first.*, and the black **ASK FIRST** band is the cover sheet's. The plain-words rules ask for a control's exact name, so the line reads *The grey box headed **Ask the attorney first** says when it was not.*, built from `LIEN_RECORD_ASK` so the two cannot drift. In the example of a difference, the record's half keeps its method (*The record shows certified mail on September 24, 2026.*), because the method is half of what is compared and can be the half that differs.
