# v2.5518 — Messages: the speech bubble is easier to press (2026-10-13)

A fix from helper 9's live walk on prod for the customer communication lead: on the Customers list, the speech bubble
beside a customer's name (`MessagesButton`, v2.5451) measured 19×19 px, under the 24 px minimum a press needs each way
(WCAG 2.5.8). The same 15 px bubble sits beside the name on Jobs' rows and the job window, and on Estimates.

- **`src/components/messages/MessagesButton.tsx`**: on a list the button's box is at least `MESSAGES_BUTTON_MIN_HIT`
  (24 px) each way, `border-box`, and a negative margin gives back what it grew by (2.5 px a side for the 15 px bubble),
  so the row keeps its height and the bubble keeps its place. The icon stays 15 px. A caller that passes its own look,
  the Customers phone row's 40 px tile, sizes itself and takes no margin back.
- **Tests:** two new cases in `MessagesButton.render.test.tsx` pin the box, the margin, the icon's size and the tile's
  own size. Two breaks on purpose each failed a case: the minimum height left out, and the margin taken back from the
  tile too.
