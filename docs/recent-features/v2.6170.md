# v2.6170 — Legal desk: the settlement floor is retired — the firm settles for what can be had (2026-10-30)

Punch list #218 (`to-dos/legal-desk-settlement-floor/`), PR 1 of 4 of direction C, the owner's pick of 2026-10-30. His words: *"not all of the legal items need a settlement floor. Some cases are hard to try and some are easy to try. They should be settled for what they are able to be settled for, and that should be done by the attorney, and we get what we get. If they don't settle it costs us more in collections and in taking them to trial."* The floor (v2.4643, punch list #85 item 20) was one number the office picked on a slider with nothing beside it about what trial would net; the one thing it gated was the firm's *Settled* step, which it turned into an ask the office signed off while the stage waited. This PR takes the gate out. PRs 2–4 put the attorney's read of the case, the two paths side by side and a derived walk-away line in its place.

## What changed

- **`submit-legal-portal`**: a `settled` step always takes an amount (*Enter the settlement amount.*) and always moves the stage, with the amount on the entry (`meta.settlementAmount`). The ask under a floor (`meta.flavor = 'settlement'`) is no longer written. The matter is still read with `select('*')`.
- **`legal-portal`**: `settlementFloor` no longer travels; the matter read names the counsel columns alone.
- **The desk** (`LegalDesk.tsx`, `LegalDeskPacketTab.tsx`, `LegalDeskFirmPanels.tsx`): the *Settlement authority* block and `SettlementFloorEditor` are gone, as are the ready sheet's floor field and the `floor` / `floorUnit` on the `Sheet` type; *Mark attorney ready* releases in one call. `SettlementAnswer` (Sign off / Not yet) stays for an ask from before, and `legal_answer_settlement` is unchanged.
- **The firm's view** (`LegalFirmMatterView.tsx`): the card line reads *Settling: Settle as you can. Record the settlement with its amount.* on every matter. The portal's *Settled* step form requires the amount always (`LegalPortal.tsx`).
- **The money bars** (`moneyBars.ts`, `LegalMoneyBars.tsx`): no `floor` input and no `floor` mark kind; the claim bar is drawn for added retainage or a standing offer, and the hatched stretch names an offer alone.
- **Needs You** (`dashboardNeedsYou.ts`): *1 settlement to sign off*, for an ask from before.
- **Gone**: `_shared/legalSettlement.ts` (`settlementFloorOf`, `settlementFloorDollars`, `settlementBelowFloor`, `matterOpenBalance`, `settlementFloorWords`) and its test; `settlementFloorControl.ts` and its test; the `settlementFloor` field of the portal payload (`legalPortalPayload.ts`).
- **Kept**: the two columns on `legal_matters`, null and unread, and `legal_set_settlement_floor` with no caller, so a hard floor can come back without a migration. No migration in this PR.

## Deploy

The client deploys with the merge. Then `supabase functions deploy submit-legal-portal legal-portal`: until the redeploy, the old function still gates a *Settled* step on a matter whose floor columns hold a value, and still sends `settlementFloor`, which the new client ignores. No migration.
