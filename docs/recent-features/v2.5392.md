# v2.5392 — Needs you: a Customers line, ready for when Messages has its page (2026-10-12)

Residential build, R8 · 14 (`to-dos/gc-mode/messages/RESIDENTIAL_BUILD_PLAN.md` on spike/gc-mode; the after state is card 14 of `customer-messages-integrations-mockups.html`). The Dashboard's Needs you card gets one line for the plumbing company's own customers: **Customers**, *3 need a reply, 1 we owe, 2 to sort.*, with **Messages** opening `/messages?kind=customer`. It is built, counted and wired, and held off by `CUSTOMER_MESSAGES_LINE_ON = false` in `dashboardNeedsYou.ts`, because `/messages` has no route yet. R2 turns it on with the route. No migration and no edge function.

- **The counts** (`src/lib/followUp/customerCounts.ts`, `customerMessageCounts`): read off Follow up's own queue (`followUpQueue`, cut 2), so the line and the page agree. *Need a reply* is each customer once on Needs a reply, plus a customer who wrote on their portal page (`portalNeeds`'s write row; a paper left unsigned waits on them, not on us). *We owe* is the customers' rows on We owe them. *To sort* is the Who is this? rows, senders nobody has named, which are never customers too; R7's `followUp/newLane.ts` replaces it with the New lane's count. Trade partners and architects are not counted.
- **The read** (`customerCountsIo.ts`, `loadCustomerMessagesCounts`): `FollowUpIo.loadQueue` for today, then the queue, then the counts. Null when all three are zero. The hook `useCustomerMessagesCounts` sits beside `useGcFollowUpNeeds` and is built the same way: the file loads on demand, only while the line is on, and refetches on window focus. While the flag is off nothing is read.
- **The item** (`customer-messages`): kicker *Messages*, title *Customers*, the counts as the detail with each zero dropped (`customerMessagesLine`; "1 needs a reply" in the singular), the sum as the figure, amber, rank 40 beside `job-followups`. The mockup's `·` between the counts became commas, since `plainWordsFailures` reads a `·` inside a sentence as glue.
- **Who sees it**: the GC office team, the same gate as the GC projects line (`canOpenGcProjects`: dev, master technician, assistant, controller, estimator), which is also the spine's RLS on `contacts` and `messages` (`gc_office_team()`).
- **Tests**: `customerCounts.test.ts` over the prototype's Friday (Elena and Priya need a reply, Priya is owed, the unknown number is to sort) and the mockups' Dana Whitfield and Kevin Alvarez; `customerCountsIo.test.ts`; `dashboardNeedsYou.customerMessages.test.ts` (off draws nothing, on draws the three parts and drops the zeros, plain words); `DashboardPinnedQuickRow.customerMessages.render.test.tsx` (off reads nothing; on, the five office roles get the line and its press, three field roles do not).
- **Docs**: `GLOSSARY.md` → Needs You card lists the key. `APP_DIRECTORY.md` and the `needs-you-card` guide wait for R2, so neither names a press that opens nothing.
