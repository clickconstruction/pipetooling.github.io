# v2.7475 — Sub papers: a payment record for each payment (sub labor train) (2026-11-16)

Client only, no migration and no function. Punch list #350, step 4 of the six under the card's *Where it stands* (the plan's PR 4), cut from the design spike's snapshot (`claude/sub-labor-spike-82d6a9` at `7616bc399`). It went ahead of step 3, the numbers on the papers, which waits on Taunya's answer to call 1.

## What landed

- **The payment record in the papers window.** `SubPapersWindowConnected.tsx` hands the view `paymentRecords`, so the pick beside the other two papers shows, and a window can open on one payment (`{ kind: 'payment', sendingKey }`). Print and Download file it as `sub_payment_record`, on the person and each job the payment touched.
- **Payment record…** beside a payment in a sheet's opened row on Jobs → Subs → Pay (`JobsSubLaborTab.tsx`). One per sending. A row with no sending is a record of its own (`row:<payment id>`). Money from a credit and a back-charge get none, and neither does a sheet that is not one person's.
- **The window after Record payment** (`AfterRecordPaymentWindow` in `SubLaborPaymentModals.tsx`): what was recorded, that the sub has been given no paper, then **Done** or **Payment record…**. The press opens the papers on the newest payment of the sheet the sending led with. When the reload has not landed, it opens on the statement of account, with the record one pick away. It shows for a sheet that is one roster person's, and not for money used from a credit.

## No number yet, on any paper

A sending has had a number of its own since v2.7235, and the kernel prints it as the payment record's (`PR-<n>`). Since v2.7453 a live work statement and statement of account printed that number in their *Payment record* column for a payment on a sending. The card's rule is that the names are settled before a number prints under one, so `SubPapersWindowConnected.tsx` now leaves every sending's number off the sheets it hands the kernel (`withoutNumbers`). The column reads a dash, a payment record reads *Not numbered*, and a sending is still one record, since it is grouped by its id. Step 3 takes the strip out with the rest of the numbers.

## What differs from the snapshot

- The after-payment window offered *Send the payment record…* and opened the papers on the send step. Send is step 5, so the press reads **Payment record…** and opens the paper.
- `TEAM_SHEET_RULE` in place of `useTeamSheetRule()`.
- The name on the paper is the roster's, not the words typed on the sheet.
- The after-payment window closes on a click outside, as the house's windows do.
