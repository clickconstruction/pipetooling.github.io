# v2.7858 — Pricing calculator: drag a row or a breakdown line onto it (2026-11-18)

The last screen piece of the Percent row train (v2.7848 the column, v2.7855 the row and its window, v2.7857 the calculator's two presses). The owner's ask of 2026-11-18: *drag and drop in a pricing line and have that user choose the value from that line they want*, then do math such as *x .4*, then one press to a row.

## What ships

- **Grips.** While the Pricing Tape is unfolded, each grid row and each line of the Kind breakdown card wears a grip of six dots. Folded, there are none.
- **The drop.** A line dragged over the calculator turns the tape's place into *Drop the line here*. Dropped, the tape's place asks **Which number?** with a press for each number the line has: a group's Revenue, Count and Cost; a row's Revenue, Sale price per unit, Count and Cost. A number the line does not have is a dead press with its reason. **Never mind** closes the question. The owner asked for a pop-up window here; the question is asked inside the calculator, where the line was dropped, as the final mock-up drew it.
- **A named number.** The pick lands on the display and prints its own tape line, *Pipe revenue*. The math prints the name, *Pipe revenue × 0.4*. A digit, a backspace or % makes the display a plain number again. A named line rolled back from the tape comes back named.
- **A rule from the tape.** A sum that is exactly one group's revenue or cost times one plain number, in either order, carries its rule: 40% of Pipe revenue. **Make a new row…** and **Put on a row…** then open the Percent row window filled with that rule and *Keep following these rows* on. A count, one row's number, a plus, or a third number carries none and is sent as a plain number.

## How

- `src/lib/tapeCalculator.ts`: `TapeSource`, `TapeRule`, `applyNamed`, `tapeRuleOf`; `CalcState` gains `named` and `firstNamed`, a `TapeEntry` gains `source` and `rule`. A saved tape reads back as before.
- `src/lib/bids/tapeDrag.ts`: what a drag carries (`tapeLineForRow`, `tapeLineForGroup`), read back defensively (`parseTapeLine`). A group's numbers are the ones a rule would read: saved prices, with no row that carries a rule of its own. They can differ from the Kind breakdown card's line, which adds up what is on screen.
- `src/lib/bids/pricingTapeBridge.ts`: the calculator says whether it is unfolded (`setPricingTapeOpen`), so the Workbench draws grips only while a line has somewhere to land.
- `TapeDragGrip.tsx`, a `gripFor` prop on `KindBreakdownCard.tsx`, and the drop and the question in `BidsPricingCalculator.tsx`.

## Tests

`tapeCalculatorNamed.test.ts` (14), `tapeDrag.test.ts` (14), five drop cases added to `BidsPricingCalculator.render.test.tsx`, and two cases added to `BidsPricingTab.render.test.tsx`: the grips and what one carries, and the calculator's answer opening the window both ways.

## Limits

- A grip is for a mouse. The keyboard and a phone make the same row with **＋ Percent row**.
- A tag cannot be dragged: the Tag breakdown card has no grips. A rule by tag is picked in the window.
