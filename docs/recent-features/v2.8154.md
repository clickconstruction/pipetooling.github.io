# v2.8154 — Papers before a job opens: a job's own settings are read, signed and kept (2026-11-20)

Punch list #389, step 3 of eight, the second half: the kernel, the sign path and the signed record. The ground came in v2.8145 and is pushed. No migration here.

**Deploy**, from `main` after the merge, on Taunya's go by each function's name: `accept-contract` and `get-contract-for-signer`, and `gc-trade-portal`, which bundles the same shared file and does nothing new. Until then the functions deployed today refuse every paper built from settings, so a job can require such a paper and nobody can open or sign it. They are safe against the new columns: they read none of them.

The types are folded here for this step's names alone: the seven columns of `job_required_papers`, `job_papers_require`'s third name and the new functions. Two other sessions' objects that the regeneration also carried are left to their own types PRs.

No clause wording rides with this. The only paper built from settings is a ZZ test entry.

## What changed

- **The sign page reads a job's paper.** `clausePaperFor` (`_shared/clausePaper.ts`) reads `job_paper_clauses` for the required paper the copy names, in place of step 1's bare refusal. The paper carries the job's picks, their plain words and its seal, exactly as the database hands them back. A copy that names no job is still refused bare.
- **It signs only the words it showed.** The page sends `paperSeal`, the seal of the paper in the language read. `accept-contract` reads the job's paper again and signs only at that revision and that seal. A job's settings can change with no new revision, so the revision alone was not enough. The database checks the frozen paper against its own read at the write, and its refusal is answered as *The paper changed since you opened it.*
- **A paper that is not ready says so.** When the database will not put the job's paper together (taken off the job, not read by an attorney at this revision, a wording missing), both functions answer `paper_not_ready`, and the page reads *This paper is not ready to sign yet. Ask the office that sent it.* The reason stays in the function's log. Such a paper has no body to show in its place.
- **The signed record shows the settings.** *The job's settings when this was signed*: a line a setting, the choice and what it says, in the plain words kept on the copy, with the settings' revision. Under it a line says whether the words still match their seal.
- **Add a paper holds a paper built from settings.** It is grey, with the words *Built from settings. A job takes it with its settings, on a page still to come.* Before this it read *No words yet*, which sent the office to the wrong place.

## The kernel

`src/lib/contracts/jobPaperSettings.ts` is the database's twin for a screen: whether a Book entry is required with settings or without (`requireProblem`), which way a change of picks goes (`picksChange`), what a change will do before and after the first signature (`settingsChangePlan`), the read-revision rule (`readRuleProblem`), and the seal (`sealText`, `paperSeal`, `sealHolds`). Step 4's page stands on it.

One list of cases lives in `supabase/tests/job_paper_settings/20_scenario.sql`. The SQL bed runs it through the database, and `jobPaperSettings.cases.test.ts` reads the same list out of that file and runs it through the kernel: ten changes of picks, three sets of plain words and five seals.

## What it does not do yet

- No screen sets a job's settings. That is step 4, which waits on the look. Until then a job takes such a paper only through `job_papers_require` itself.
- The GC sees nothing. That is step 5.
