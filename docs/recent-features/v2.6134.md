# v2.6134 — Job Parts Tally on a desk is back to its width (2026-10-30)

A correction to v2.6117, found by the wrapper sweep (v2.6131) that followed it.

v2.6117 pinned `JobTally.tsx`'s page wrapper to its container (`width: 100%`, `minWidth: 0`, `boxSizing: border-box`) and added the wrapper's 2rem of padding to its two max widths (480 → 512, 760 → 792), believing the desk would keep its outer width. It did not: `src/index.css` opens with `* { box-sizing: border-box }`, so every inline `maxWidth` in the app is an outer width already, and the desk page drew 32 px wider than before. The v2.6117 fragment's "same 512 / 792 px as before" measured the new width, not the old one.

## What changed

- **`src/pages/JobTally.tsx`**: the max widths are 480 and 760 again. The three properties that pin the wrapper stay.
- **`docs/recent-features/v2.6117.md`**: its sentence about the max widths now says what happened.

## Verified

Dev server as the dev, headless at 1280 px: the page is 760 px wide on the office's Transactions and 480 px on Materials Estimate and On site, and holds as each tab lands. At 375 px nothing changes: the row is 327 px on every tab.

No migration, no edge function, no words changed.
