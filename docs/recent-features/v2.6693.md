# v2.6693 — Archive a named document on the HR file: the Archive door, the Archived fold, Unarchive, and the seat's refusal (punch list #277, open call 1) (2026-11-08)

Punch list #277 (`to-dos/hr-named-documents/README.md`) left two calls open after the three PRs shipped on 2026-11-07. The owner answered the first on 2026-11-08: a named document can be archived in the app. The row carried `archived_at` since v2.6613 and the function already took `archive` from the seat; this is the press, its reading, and the hold the function was missing. The second call (whether the two Drive-linked evidence records retire once the documents are in) stays open.

## What changed

- **The Documents view** (`src/components/people/personFile/PersonFilePanel.tsx`): an open named document has **Archive…** beside Edit the document and Copy as text. The press opens a `ConfirmDialog` whose button names the act, *Archive the document* (`archiveConfirmWords`); on confirm the document leaves the *Named documents* list for an **Archived (n)** fold at the foot of the section, under Add a document, drawn only when there is one. A document opened from the fold is read-only: an amber line *Archived Nov 8, 2026 · read-only until Unarchive* (`archivedDocumentWords`), no Edit press, Copy as text still (not on a counsel-only one), and **Unarchive**, which puts it back with its text and its revisions as they were. The tab's count is the kept documents alone. Toasts from `archiveOutcomeWords`.
- **Kernel** `src/lib/people/personDocuments.ts`: `splitDocumentsByArchive` (the kept newest first, the archived by the day they were archived), `archivedFoldWords`, `archiveConfirmWords`, `archivedDocumentWords`, `archiveOutcomeWords`; unit tests beside it. **Io** `personDocumentsIo.ts`: the list keeps every row now (the view splits them) and `setPersonDocumentArchived` sends `{ person_id, doc_id, archive }` alone to `dev_person_doc_write`, as the seat does.
- **Migration `20261108090000_person_document_archive_guard.sql`**: `dev_person_doc_write` replaced whole with one check added where the `doc_id` row is read — an archived document takes `archive: false` and nothing else; markdown, ops or `counsel_only` on it are refused naming the day it was archived and the way back. The revision trigger is untouched. The old client reads nothing differently, so the push can follow the merge at any time; `docs/migrations/20261108090000_person_document_archive_guard.md`.
- **The seat** (`dev-mcp` 0.41.0): `get_person_file`'s `documents` lists the archived ones after the kept, each with its `archived_at`, and `documents_note` says an archived document is read-only until `plan_person_doc { doc_id, archive: false }`; `plan_person_doc`'s description says the same. `devMcpPersonFile.ts`; the seat's tests follow.
- Smokes in `PersonFilePanel.documents.render.test.tsx`: the fold and the tab count, Open from the fold read-only with Unarchive, Archive… asking first and archiving through the io. `docs/HR_FILES.md` (the Documents paragraph), `docs/dev-mcp/README.md`, `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`, the help guide *keep private employee files* (*Archive a document*).
