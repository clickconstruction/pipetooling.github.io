# v2.8292 — Materials on a phone: Missing, Used and Came in read as rows (2026-11-22)

The rest of a job's Materials page at a phone's width. The owner liked the search's rows (v2.8291), asked where else the same was needed, and said build the first on the list. No migration, no edge function. The page at a computer is not changed, and neither is Jobs → Materials at any width.

## What was wrong

v2.8282 drew a job's four figures as tiles and v2.8284 its boxes as rows. Three of the four tiles still opened tables:

| Tile | Its tables | On a phone, on a job with 106 parts and 285 presses |
|---|---|---|
| **Missing** | What each part did, twelve columns. What was reported, eight | The first table is 1,659 px wide in a 359 px box. Only the part's name shows: not one number |
| **Used** | History, eight columns | 593 px wide. When, What and Part show. Qty, Where, Who and Note do not. All 285 rows are drawn |
| **Came in** | Deliveries so far, five columns | Source and When show |

## What a phone gets

A row is laid out as the search's finds are: the thing on top, its facts under it in the table's own words, and the one number the table is about as a figure at the row's end, the number over its words.

### Missing · what each part did

- **The figure is what walked away**, amber above zero and green at zero, as the table's chip is.
- **The first line is the table's two bold columns**: *bought 100 · left 94*.
- **The second line is what was reported**, in the table's order, and only where there is something to say: *added on site 4 · used 2 · returned 1 · taken or damaged 3 · can’t find 1*. The table leaves a zero blank in those columns.
- **The last line is the money and the last count**: the priced gap with its price chip, or *no price*, then *last count* with its stamp, or *never counted*.
- **The parts with a gap lead, and the rest wait behind one press** that says how many it will draw: *Show 104 parts with nothing walked away*. Once drawn they come sixty at a time. On the job walked, the reports under this list were 106 rows down. They are now on the first screen. A job where nothing walked away says so.
- **Walked away, priced** stays as the list's last line, and the paragraph under it is unchanged.

### Missing · what was reported

- **A short count**: an amber edge, how many are *missing* as the figure, then the box with what was expected and what was counted, the money or *no price*, and who counted with the stamp.
- **A count that found more**: the figure is how many were *found*, in blue.
- **A Can't find**: a red edge, its count as the figure, the box, who and the note.
- **The press to the takes before a short count** is in its row, as it was in its cell.
- **7 days** and **30 days** are 36 px tall, where they were 28. The total, *In the last 7 days*, stays as the last line.

### Used · every press

- **A row a press**: the part, then what was done and where (*Count · G1*, *Delivered → G89*, *Taken · G12 → the shop*), then who and when with the note. The figure is the quantity as History signs it: *+5*, *−6*, *94*.
- **Sixty at a time.** The table draws every press a job has. A phone draws sixty and a press that says how many it will add: *Show 60 more*, then *Show the last 45*.
- **The two filters** sit side by side on a row of their own under the heading, 40 px tall, where they were 30.
- **Takes**, over History, was rows already and is unchanged.

### Came in · deliveries so far

- **A row an invoice or a PO that landed**: its name, when, who and how many lines, with its pieces as the figure.
- **Land an invoice as a delivery**, over it, is unchanged. Its presses already wrap. The table of lines it reads from pasted text is still a table: pasting an invoice's text is a desk job.

## How it is built

- `src/lib/materials/jobSectionsPhone.ts` is the kernel. `comparisonPhoneRow`, `countDiffPhoneRow`, `cannotFindPhoneRow`, `deliveryPhoneRow` and `historyPhoneRow` each turn one of a table's rows into a phone's row, from the data the table is built from. `splitComparison` and `quietPartsLabel` lead Missing with the parts that have a gap. `pagePhoneRows` draws a long list a page at a time.
- History's sign and its *Where* words were written inside the table. They are now `movementSign` and `movementWhereWords` in the kernel, and the table calls them, so the two layouts cannot say different things.
- `src/components/materials/SectionPhoneRow.tsx` is the row, with the line a table kept for its total and the press that draws more.
- `JobsMaterialsTab` draws the rows where each table stood when `phone` is set. The sums and the filtered list both layouts read (`reportedMoney`, `historyRows`) are worked out once.

## What the look changed

Seen on the dev server as the dev, on live data, one page load and then presses inside the page: job 224, 106 parts and 285 presses, at 393 × 852.

1. **Missing folds the parts with no gap.** The first build drew all 106. The page was 11,945 px tall and the reports were at its foot. With the fold it is 1,075 px and both lists are on the first screen.
2. **A delivery's place joins its word with no dot.** *Delivered · → G89* read wrong. It is *Delivered → G89*.
3. **The day presses and the two filters are a thumb tall.**

`innerWidth` and `scrollWidth` were equal on each section. **30 days** was pressed and the list drew a short count, a find and a Can't find. Nothing that writes was pressed. The old tables were photographed from the same code through a local switch that was taken out again.

Not seen: a delivery's row on live data, since job 224 has no invoice landed. Its render test holds it. Not seen at 375 px or in dark, and not on a real iPhone.
