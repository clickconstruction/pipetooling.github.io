# v2.7105 — The Bridge is a pulse: it reads today, and the week's call is off the page (punch list #334, PR 7) (2026-11-12)

PR 7 of the Bridge train (card #334, `to-dos/bridge-pulse/`), on PR 6 (v2.7099). Client only. No migration and no edge function changed. One kernel that is copied to the edge functions lost exports nothing there reads (`callPeople.ts`), so the copy was regenerated and no function is behind for it.

The owner: "Why would Friday ask if the numbers move? Shouldn't this page always be a direct reflection of whatever the current status is, like a pulse?" Then "Yes, take it off", and "Take the Needs you call code out too". No call was ever kept: `bridge_calls` is empty in the live database.

## What changed

- **The card is What to do now** (`BridgeWeek.tsx`, `pulseWords.ts`, new): it names the slowest step, or cash in a week it leads, and its first moves as rows with their doors. Gone from it: the name chip on each move, *take off* and *put back*, the record press, *Add a note*, *Pick another step*, Friday's answer presses and **The last call** tile. The words of the step and its moves are still `buildWeekCall`'s. The page hands that kernel no calls, no pick and no marks, so it always reads today.
- **The page reads and writes no call** (`Bridge.tsx`): no read of `bridge_calls`, of the Done marks or of the people a move can go to, no record, no answer, no scorecard, no held stage. The red on the flow follows today's reading. `BridgeLastCall.tsx` is deleted.
- **The step's desk** (`BridgeLinkDesk.tsx`): no sentence about a call. Its press reads **Go to what to do now ↑**. A move's chip reads *on the list* or *not on it*, and the line under the list says how many the card shows.
- **The usual fixes** (`BridgeWaits.tsx`): the foot no longer says Friday asks.
- **The Collect window** (`BridgeCollectDesk.tsx`, `BridgeCollectCounted.ts`): no **Record this as the week's plan** press and no scorecard card. The foot line says what the sheet asks for and stops. The owner and day typed on a row still ride on **Copy the call sheet**. `collectScorecard.ts`, `loadCollectScorecard.ts` and `collectSheetAsCallMoves` are deleted, as nothing reaches them.
- **After the call** (`collectOutcome.ts`): the sheet's grey lines no longer say what Friday counts. Nothing it writes changed.
- **How this is counted**, in the Build, Sales and Market windows: the sentence that said the week's call is recorded on the link desk is gone.
- **Needs you** (`dashboardNeedsYou.ts`, `needsYouSources.ts`, `needsYouActor.ts`, `needsYouDoors.ts`, `useNeedsYouInputs.ts`, `useNeedsYouPress.tsx`): the `bridgeMoves` source, the `bridge-moves` line, its door and its Done press are gone. `useBridgeMoves.ts` is deleted. `callPeople.ts` and `callPeopleIo.ts` keep the list of accounts and the shape of a Done mark, which the Monday email still reads.

## Not in this PR

- **The Monday email** still speaks of the week's call, and `weekCall.ts` and `calls.ts` are as they were. PR 11 changes the email and trims both.
- **The two database functions** the Needs you line called, `bridge_my_moves` and `bridge_mark_my_move_done`, stay until PR 11 drops them. Nothing calls them now. The table `bridge_calls` stays.
- **`loadCallPeople`** has no reader in this PR. PR 9 reads it again for the name on a to-do card.
