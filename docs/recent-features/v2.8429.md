# v2.8429 — The older confirm windows’ buttons are easier to tap on a phone too (2026-11-24)

The leftover of v2.8397, which gave the shared confirm window 44 px presses at a phone's width and named the older `ConfirmDialog` component as a separate call. This is that call. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## The cause

`src/components/ConfirmDialog.tsx` (14 files import it) sized Cancel and the act of its plain two-press window by their padding and words alone, `0.5rem 1rem`, so they stood 35.5 px tall at every width. Only its `keep` window (three stacked presses, v2.7690) carried `minHeight: 44`.

## What changed

- The component reads `useNarrowViewport640` and spreads `confirmPressSize(narrow)` from `src/lib/confirmDialogPress.ts` into Cancel and the act, the way `ConfirmDialogProvider` does. One size rule for both confirm windows; the kernel's values are untouched and only its comment now names both.
- At 640 px and under the plain window's presses get `min-height: 44px` with `box-sizing: border-box`, and the window grows by their 8.5 px. At a desk they keep the padding with no floor.
- The `keep` window is left as it was. Its 44 px holds at a desk too, where the kernel gives no floor, so the kernel cannot carry it without changing a desk pixel: the `keep` press keeps its own `minHeight: 44` and the other two keep their `{ order, minHeight: 44 }` override on top of the spread.
- The colours, the words, the order and every caller are unchanged.
- `docs/APP_SHELL_ARCHITECTURE.md`: the sentence that said the older component was unchanged by v2.8397 now says this version moved it.

## Tests

- `ConfirmDialog.render.test.tsx` (new): with `matchMedia` stubbed narrow, both presses of the plain window carry `min-height: 44px`; stubbed wide, neither does and the padding is the same; the colours, words and order hold at both; the `keep` window's three presses carry 44 px and their order at both.
- `confirmDialogPress.test.ts` and the render smokes of the importers that have one (12 of the 14, plus `NewReportModal.photos`) pass unchanged.

## Looked at

A harness page (not committed) drew the component beside `origin/main`'s copy of it, in the Browser pane's Chromium:

- Plain window at 390 px, light and dark: both presses measure 44 px tall (35.5 px before), the window 154.5 px (146 before), the widths and the left edges the same.
- Plain window at 640 px: 44 px. At 641 px and at 1,280 px: 35.5 px with no floor and a 146 px window, light and dark, the same rectangles as the old copy measured at 1,280 px in dark.
- `keep` window at 390 px and at 1,280 px in light: every press and the window measure the same as the old copy, to the fraction of a pixel.

Not looked at: WebKit or a real phone; the `keep` window in dark; the old copy at a desk in light; a label long enough to wrap; any of the fourteen callers in the running app.
