# v2.4363 — Accounts Receivable: Close out books the money in Banking too (2026-10-02)

Grace's ask after the Texas Mutual $119.56 fix: make a vendor refund easy to mark right from the Accounts Receivable modal. Plan, critiques and mock-up on the canvas https://claude.ai/artifact/SbFoRNPX4CY9qGF6foHmUR (*Second pass: the proposal*). One migration, no edge function.

Read Oct 1: of 135 deposits through Accounts Receivable since Apr 1, one was a vendor refund, and its books were already right — the Banking rule *TEXAS MUTUAL - Insurance* labelled it Insurance on Aug 16. 190 of 193 vendors paid 3+ times in the last year have a rule; the org's auto-approve switch is off, so the usual case is a match waiting for approval. The old strip only said "Banking's label still books the money". "We have paid them before" was rejected as a reason guess: the City of Seguin (paid 19 times) also pays bills (#908).

## What changed

- **Migration** [`20261002050000_ar_close_out_books_label.sql`](../../supabase/migrations/20261002050000_ar_close_out_books_label.sql) ([doc](../migrations/20261002050000_ar_close_out_books_label.md)): sidecar `mercury_transaction_ar_close_labels`; read `ar_deposit_booking(tx)`; write `close_out_ar_deposit(tx, reason, note, label)`; Reopen in `set_mercury_transaction_ar_closed` takes off the close-out's own label (internal `_ar_close_label_undo`).
- **Kernel** [`arCloseBooking.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/arCloseBooking.ts) + tests: the books line's state (kept · approve · set · none · unread), the label sent, the words (footer, confirm, toasts), the parts door (`job_materials_parts`, `cogs_part_iii`), the remembered reason, the row line.
- **Strip** [`ArCloseOut`](../../src/components/jobs/ar/ArCloseOut.tsx): four reason buttons (44 px, `aria-pressed`, group label kept for the old select's name), the books line with **Change** → label picker (**Leave it for Banking** while nothing labels it), the Supply houses link, the footer sentence beside **Close out $X**, the confirm saying what happens to the books. - **Modal** [`BankPaymentsModal`](../../src/components/jobs/BankPaymentsModal.tsx): reads the booking while the strip or the closed record shows; the payee's last close-out presses the reason unless the person already pressed one; Close out calls `close_out_ar_deposit`, and the old `set_mercury_transaction_ar_closed` when the read failed or the RPC is not pushed; Reopen's toast says what happened to the label. - Guide *match bank deposits to the bills they pay*: the close-out section rewritten (*What Banking does with it*). [`ACCESS_CONTROL.md`](../ACCESS_CONTROL.md): Close out may approve the deposit's own rule match.
