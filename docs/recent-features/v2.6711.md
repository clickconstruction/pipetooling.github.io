# v2.6711 — The site’s cost timeline lists the jobs it cannot draw (2026-11-08)

Punch list #257, PR 4, the owner's pick from the mock-up on 2026-11-08: the jobs with no dated charge or payment were only a count under the chips (*7 jobs with no dated charge or payment are left out* on the real site), and a job whose only sub sheet carried no date held its cost nowhere on the chart. The chart is time, so a job with no date has no place on its axis; the strip puts the money beside the chart instead of inventing a day for it.

- **The ledger strip** (`DevelopmentTimeline.tsx`): under the chart's key and above *Print or save as PDF*, when any job is left out, a strip on `var(--bg-subtle)` with a head row *Not on the chart · N jobs* / *no dated charge or payment*, one row per job in the Money look's order — the job's name as a `LinkButton` through the new `openJob` prop (the page passes the one `DevelopmentMoney` takes), the reason muted, the cost tabular and right-aligned with `$0.00` muted — and a sum line over a `var(--border-strong)` rule: *Chart ends at $X · Not on the chart $Y · Together $Z, the Scoreboard's cost before overhead*. On a phone the row is two columns with the reason on its own line. Nothing is drawn when every job is dated. Test ids `timeline-strip`, `timeline-strip-row-<jobId>`, `timeline-strip-sum`.
- **The kernel** (`siteTimeline.ts`): `buildSiteTimeline` keeps `leftOut` and adds `leftOutJobs` (`siteTimelineLeftOutJobs`: each job `siteJobHasDatedEvent` refuses, its `endExpense` the round2 sum of every charge, undated ones included, and the undated charges counted by source) and `siteEndExpense`, the whole site's end whatever the chips narrowed (the same fold over every dated job), so the strip's sum holds to the site. `siteTimelineLeftOutReason`: *1 sub labor sheet and 2 card charges with no date · date them and the job joins the chart* (the nouns from `JOB_CHARGE_SOURCE_META` lowercased, a sheet for sub labor, a day for team labor, a charge for fuel; *it* for one, *them* for more), or *nothing charged yet*. `siteTimelineStripSum` → `{ chartEnd, offChart, together }`; `together` is the jobs' `jobCostsFromRows` total plus the clock, the Scoreboard's Labor + Subs + Parts, by the parity the existing test holds. `siteTimelineLeftOutWords` now reads *N jobs have no dated charge or payment. They are listed under the chart.* (*1 job has … It is listed …*). `siteTimelineStripLines` writes the strip as lines for the print and `siteTimelinePrintHtml` gains `stripLines`, drawn left-aligned under the key lines in the key's size, the head bold.
- **Guide**: *see everything on a development* → *Read the timeline* says the job is listed under the chart with its cost and why, that the name opens it, and that the last line's sum is the Scoreboard's cost before overhead. Glossary amended; the #257 card and its mock-up's picks box record the ship.
