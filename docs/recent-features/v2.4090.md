## The road — `BidsSubmittalsTab.tsx`

- `RoadSection` (module-level, replaces `Tile`): the numbered dot on a rail, the title (a button that folds/opens), a one-line summary, the body. `data-tour` on the section, `data-testid="road-<n>"`, `data-status`, `data-open`.
- Eight sections in stage order: **1 Schedule & picks** (the plug-in door, the robot's offer), **2 Rev N / Build Rev 1** (the build card on a bid with none; the revision chips as the summary, Rebuild rows from picks, Delete draft as a quiet link), **3 Reasons & cut sheets** (the rows table, Drop a vendor PDF, + Add a row by hand, the sheet strip; the summary is `describeRevision(tiles)` — the six tiles are gone), **4 Package** (Build / Rebuild / Open package, File in Drive), **5 Share** (the Share button, the room line with its people, Copy link / Close the room), **6 Their call on Rev N** (the decisions line, Drop a reviewer's file, the reviewer's files with the robot's redline read, the thread), **7 Resubmit** (Rev N+1 from the rows sent back, New revision), **8 Procure** (the log; a side track — never the Next stage the strip points at).
- The open rule (`sectionOpen`): a done stage folds; the current or waiting stage is open; a stage the current one reads from stays open (`readsFrom`: package ← rows; review ← rows, since their calls land on the rows; resubmit ← rows + review); stage 1 stays open while a schedule read is live; **Open every stage** (`hasOpenEveryStage` / `rememberOpenEveryStage`, `pt.submittals.road.openAll`) and the walkthrough open everything; a click on a title toggles that section for the visit.
- The header gains the working-revision line (`data-testid="revision-line"`): *Working on Rev 3 · draft · 3 rows … · started from Rev 2 · package built*, and *an older revision, the record* when an older one is selected. The two hidden file inputs sit at the top level so every section's button reaches them, folded or not.
- The tab render test: the tiles assertion reads the summary line; a by-hand case; the tour walks fourteen stops.

## By hand — no robot anywhere

- Stage 1: **Type or paste the schedule** (`PlugInScheduleModal`, the same one Pricing opens, `rows=[]`) — on a bid with no schedule it is the primary button and the strip's Next (`plug_in_schedule`, a new `JourneyAction`); with a schedule it reads *Add to the schedule*. The robot's offer sits under it.
- Rev 1 with no picks: the Next line now says "build Rev 1 now and type each row's product with Edit" (`build_rev1`) instead of sending Wendi to Pricing; the build card's amber line says the same.
- `SubmittalItemEditDialog` takes `canEditProduct` (true on a draft): the tag and the submitted product are inputs; the patch carries `tag` (upper-cased) and `submitted_label`. **+ Add a row by hand** (`addRowByHand`) inserts an empty missing row on the draft and opens its editor.
- `canEnterDecision` is always on: a call that came by phone or email is entered on any revision, recorded as *entered by you*.
- The walkthrough gains a stop for the typed schedule and says the road folds; the guide gets a *Without the robot* section.
