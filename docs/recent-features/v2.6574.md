# v2.6574 — A pressed step on the Lien desk opens a brief and sorts the list, and § Rules shows our schedule after a notice (2026-11-06)

Grace, 2026-11-06, on v2.6522's *Showing 3 · Letter two · the rest are faded, not held* line: it tells the user there is a system without explaining it; the app should feel robust, the user guided but always with final say. Then, on the brief's words "overdue from day 14": it is not inherent that someone knows that, so where is the schedule presented? Nowhere as a schedule: the days lived in the clocks' constants and in sentences on a notice's footer, and § Rules held the law's dates alone. The board (private artifact, Now = live renders, My suggestion drawn at 1,400 and 375) was approved with the schedule strip in both places.

## What changed

- **`lienReminderSchedule.ts`** (new, pure, 3 tests): `LIEN_REMINDER_SCHEDULE` — day 0 the packet, `COURTESY_FROM_DAY` a courtesy may go, `LETTER_TWO_FROM_DAY` due, `LETTER_TWO_BY_DAY` overdue — each with its source (`law` / `owner` / `counsel` / `ours`), `LIEN_SCHEDULE_TAIL` (the `COURTESY_AFFIDAVIT_MARGIN_DAYS` before the affidavit's day, then § 53.052's day), a four-week axis (`lienSchedulePct`, `lienScheduleMarks` gathering the notices' days with a count per day and a plus past the axis), `lienScheduleWords` and `lienScheduleSourceLines`. Every day is read from the clock that enforces it.
- **`lienReminders.ts`**: `lienReminderBrief(step)` → title, who, what, why, the usual press, the other moves (the presses a sent notice's footer really offers: *Record the owner’s call…*, *The GC authorized direct pay…*, *Send a courtesy notice ▸* while one may go, *Send letter two ▸* while unpaid; *Open the notice*, *Not what I said* on Closed), and what closes the step by itself. `lienReminderRungRuleWords` (v2.6522) is gone; `lienReminderRungRule` stays underneath.
- **`lienNextUpSteps.ts`**: `lienStepGroups(rows, on)` — *On this step* then *On other steps*, each in the list's order — and `lienStepName`; `lienStepShowingWords` (v2.6522) is gone. `LienNextUpGroup` gains `step_on` / `step_other`.
- **`LienDeskSteps.tsx`**: `LienReminderScheduleStrip` (the axis, the four points, the marks, the tail; a list of the points on a phone) and `LienStepBrief` (the head with the rung's name, whose step and the count, *Every step*, the strip on the REMINDERS rail, the four parts and the closer, and children for the rail's no-row list). `LienStepShowingLine` is gone.
- **`LienDeskNextUp.tsx`**: a pressed rung draws the brief under the rails and `lienStepGroups` in place of the day groups; the fade (`dimmed`, `data-dim`, opacity 0.3, v2.4631) is gone on every rail. The REMINDERS rail's notices with no row read inside the brief as *Also on this step, with no row on the list*. The Notices, Affidavits and Retainage rails get the head, the count and the sort now and their brief words in the next PR.
- **`LienRulesModal.tsx`**: *Our schedule after a notice* under the job's dates — the strip and the four source lines (`lien-rules-schedule`, `lien-rules-schedule-sources`).
- **Tests**: `LienDeskNextUp.render` (the brief's title, count, points, marks, instead line, the sort's headings, no `data-dim`, Every step, the no-row list inside the brief), `LienInstrumentsModal.render` (the Rules window's schedule), the three kernels.
- **Guide** `read-the-reminders-ladder-on-the-lien-desk`: *Press a step* rewritten for the brief and the sort; new *Our schedule after a notice*. `docs/twins/APP_DIRECTORY.md` amended.

## What it never does

Change a day, a rule or a press. The alternatives are named, never invented, and the strip follows a clock when it changes. Making the days the owner's to change is the next card.
