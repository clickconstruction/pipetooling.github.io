# v2.4900 — Settle up owes the same as Balances and Record payment (2026-10-07)

Found by the help-guides freshness pass (punch list #91). Punchlist asked first for a read-only check of which number is right. Its answer: "this is not a new payroll rule, it is Settle up disagreeing with the formula the database enforces". So it ships as a fix.

## The disagreement

A pay report's **net pay** is `gross − Less lines + Additional lines`. Most places already owed net − paid:

- `pay_report_net()` (migration `20260917200000_pay_sources.sql`), and `validate_pay_stub_payments_vs_net` caps payments at that net.
- Payroll → Balances (`PeoplePayStubsTab` `ledgerOpenBalanceSummary`, through `stubNetPay`).
- Record payment (`payStubBalance`).
- `docs/PAY_RECONCILE_AGENT.md`: "each report's net (gross − deductions + additional lines — the formula the database enforces)".

But `personSettleUp` (`src/lib/people/personMoneyLedger.ts`), and the People → Offsets **Settle up** board and person window built on it, owed **gross − paid**. Its stubs carried no Less or Additional lines at all. So:

- A **$1,000** report with a **$200** Less line, paid **$800** (paid in full by the database's rule): Settle up said **$200 still owed**, and Balances said $0.
- A **$1,000** report with a **$150** Additional line, paid **$1,000**: Settle up said **$0**, and Balances said $150.

## The fix

- **`payStubNetById`** (`personMoneyLedger.ts`) builds each report's net from its Less and Additional lines with `stubNetPay`. A report with no lines nets its gross.
- **`personSettleUp`**, **`buildSettleUpBoard`** and **`buildWeeklyHistoryGroups`** take a required `netByStubId` and owe `net − paid`. The person window's **Needs action** list reads the same net. Its history and Settle up can no longer disagree with each other.
- **Where it loads.** The Less and Additional lines come from `usePayStubsData`, which already loads them with the stubs. `People.tsx` passes the two maps to `PeopleOffsetsTab`, which builds the net once for the board and the person window. There are no new queries.
- **Left alone on purpose:** the pay statement and `paidTotalInRange`, which count what was paid, not what is owed. `buildOffsetPaymentTimeline` too, since no screen calls it.
- **Help:** *see where someone stands on pay* says a report owes its net pay.

## The effect on prod today

The figures below were read **read-only** through the dev seat on 2026-10-07. They are counts and a total only, with no person's pay.

- 30 pay reports carry a Less or an Additional line: 15 Less lines and 24 Additional lines.
- **6 people's Settle up changes.** All 6 now show less owed, and none shows more.
- **The total drops by $3,961.94.** That money was shown as owed on reports already paid in full at their net. The database would never have let anyone pay it.
