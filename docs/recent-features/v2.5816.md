# v2.5816 — Quickfill Texts: the station reads Messages instead of asking for a note (2026-10-24)

Punch list #157, option A of [`to-dos/quickfill-texts-station/`](../../to-dos/quickfill-texts-station/README.md), the owner's "I like your suggestions, build it all" on the four Quickfill proposals of 2026-10-24. The Texts station was built with the first Quickfill, when the company's texts lived in the Mac's Messages app: an `sms:` link, a free box and a Mark that refused without a note, counting the typed lines as its *open* number. Its history read what the box became: 128 marks since April 15, one most mornings, 126 of them the words *up to date*, every one with *Open items* 1. Every company text goes through Quo into `/messages` now, which already knows the three ways a text falls through the cracks.

- **The ledger** ([`lib/quickfill/textsLedger.ts`](../../src/lib/quickfill/textsLedger.ts), pure, 9 tests): three of Messages' lanes narrowed to texts, in Messages' own words and colors. **Needs a reply**: the queue's rows (`followUpQueue`, the New lane's people left out as on the page) whose landing line is a text, in the queue's order, each with their first words, the time, *waiting 2 h* (`weekStrip.ts`'s `waitWords`), their number and the thread's door at the line (`messagesThreadHref`). **Not delivered**: Went out's rows (`wentOutRows`, texts alone) from today and yesterday whose `deliveryMark` is bad, with the number for the call. **Went out today**: the receipt, every text sent today newest first, with Quo's answers counted (delivered, waiting on Quo, not delivered). The count is the first two groups; the receipt is not work. A text from a number nobody has named waits in New on Messages and is counted apart (`newTexts`), pointed at, never in the count.
- **The read** ([`textsLedgerIo.ts`](../../src/lib/quickfill/textsLedgerIo.ts)): the reads Messages already makes and nothing new. `loadQueue` built into the queue exactly as `customerCountsIo.ts` does for Needs you's Customers line (the same `followUpInputFromRows` and `followUpNewLane`), `loadWentOut` for the week's lines, and `supabaseOffersIo().loadOpen()` for how many texts wait on Today's sends, fail-soft. The hook ([`useQuickfillTexts.ts`](../../src/hooks/useQuickfillTexts.ts)) sits at page level in `Quickfill.tsx` beside the Percent complete hook, so the metric reports from the page and stays live while the strip is collapsed; it loads the kernels on demand, for the office team alone (`canOpenMessages`), and refetches on focus.
- **The body** ([`QuickfillTextsSection.tsx`](../../src/components/quickfill/QuickfillTextsSection.tsx), 5 render smokes): the three bands (`.stagesSectionBand .messagesLaneBand` with the lanes' colors from `jumpStrip.ts`), a row a person with their words and the time, **Reply** opening the thread at their text and **Call** the one `CallButton`; a Not delivered row red with **Call them** and **Send it as an email**, the two presses Waiting on them gives a failed text (the thread opened with the step's reminder written as an email and the send window open, `?nudge=email`); the Needs a reply band's **Answer all ›** opening Messages with its Answer all desk, through a new link, `?answer=all` (`answerAllHref`, `answerAllAsked` and `withoutAnswerAll` in `followUp/answerDesk.ts`, tested; `Messages.tsx` reads it once as the desk's opening state and takes it off the address when the desk closes); the receipt folded behind **Show** with its summary on the band (*5 ✓ delivered · 1 waiting on Quo*); two quiet lines while they apply (*2 texts the app prepared wait on Today's sends · Open ›*, the same window Needs you opens; *1 text from a number nobody has named waits in New on Messages · Sort ›*); and **Anything else?**, one optional line whose words the page holds (`typed`). The body draws no Mark of its own. A clean day is one green line, *Nothing on texts is waiting on us. 6 went out today, all delivered.*, with the receipt behind *Show them*. On a phone the row's presses drop under the words and a band's sentence takes its own line in full (`.quickfillTextsRow` and `.quickfillTextsNote` in `index.css`); the station's columns are `minmax(0, 1fr)`, so no long line widens a phone's page (measured on a made-up day at 390 wide: the page scrolled to 569 before, 390 after).
- **The Mark** writes its note from the rows (`ledgerMarkNote`): *2 need a reply · Marcus Calloway 2 h, Dana Whitfield 1 h · 1 not delivered · Tom Reyes · 6 went out, 5 delivered, 1 waiting on Quo*, the typed words on their own line, *nothing waiting · 6 went out, all delivered* on a clean day; when the read failed, *the texts could not be read*; for someone who does not read Messages, the typed words (`textsMarkNoteFor`). The station gave up its own button and `omitDefaultMarkButton`: the page's foot Mark, the header's ✓ Mark, Focus's folded line at zero (so a clean day is one press in Focus too) and the round's and the Roller's Looked all go through `markTexts` in `Quickfill.tsx`, which writes that note and clears the typed words. `texts` left `QUICKFILL_NOTE_FIRST_SECTIONS`, so the foot draws **Looked · N open · next →** like every other station. The history's *Open items* finally counts texts, since the metric is the ledger's count.
- **The banner** (`sectionBanners.ts`, now with a test): *Is anything on texts still waiting on us?*, the question the rows answer. The banners setting saves every section's words at once, so the org's row held the old default for Texts and would have hidden the new one; `RETIRED_QUICKFILL_SECTION_BANNERS` names the old question and `effectiveQuickfillSectionBanner` lets the new default stand over it. A banner a dev wrote by hand still wins.
- **Not drawn, and why**: We owe them rows are things we owe, not texts, and stay on Messages; a *Resend* press does not exist on Messages either (the thread's box resends), so the failed row offers what Waiting on them offers a failed text, the call and the same reminder as an email; the reply box itself, since the thread is the one place to write. A text sent from someone's own phone outside Quo never reaches the app, which is what Anything else is for.
- **Needs you's Customers line** still counts a customer's unanswered text on Quickfill, so a customer who texted is on both stations today; the to-do's decision 3 (the Customers line gives its texts to this station on Quickfill) is the next PR.
- **Words**: the guide *run the office day with Quickfill* (the round's and the phone's lines that said Texts keeps its mark inside, and the stations list); `docs/GLOSSARY.md` (Quickfill as a round), `docs/QUICKFILL_ARCHITECTURE.md` (the `texts` dossier and quirk 17), `docs/MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md` (the surface table); the to-do's status.

## Is this the best we can do?

The station stops asking a person to remember what the app keeps, and the three groups are the three ways a text falls through the cracks. Nothing new is stored and no new read shape exists: the rows are Messages' own, through Messages' own kernels, so the two can never disagree. Taunya's morning goes from typing *up to date* to reading one line. Accepted for now: Quickfill reads Follow up's queue twice on load, once for the Customers line and once here, as the Dashboard already does on every load; one page-level read handed to both is the follow-up once the station has earned its place.
