# v2.3240 — Edit Job status rail: no dead band, Status as a field label, and you can see which stages you can move to (2026-09-10)

Client only; no migration. Mockup A, approved: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/246c66f7-a278-4ec4-8fff-2c6b2ac03874

## Why

After v2.3238 moved the status strip under Stages, three things showed: an empty band and hairline above it (the strip's own "above the footer" spacing, now pointless), a tiny muted *Status* caption that did not read as part of the form, and — the real one — every stage looked the same: a word and an arrow, with only a tooltip saying which moves were dead. Grace: "hard to distinguish between what it can be moved to and what it can't."

## What shipped (`JobStatusStepper.tsx`, render only — the moves, the Paid window, the send-back reason and the invoice guards are untouched)

- **No band**: the root drops its top margin, padding and border. **Status** is a form label — the same `fontWeight: 500 / 0.875rem` the Job Name and Job Address labels use.
- **The rail**: five steps joined by short lines (dark before the current step, light after). Three looks: **current** — black pill, white number bubble; **reachable** — white pill with a blue outline and a blue ← / → bubble, one tap moves the job (`moveTo`, as before); **locked** — dashed grey pill with a dashed number bubble. Each button carries `data-state` and `aria-disabled` on locked ones (not `disabled`, so the tap still lands).
- **Locked stages explain themselves**: tapping one puts the kernel's reason (`jobStepperMoveDisabledReason`) in a `role="status"` line under the rail for five seconds — "Mark the job Ready to bill first", "Bill the job first — Paid comes from Billed" — instead of only in a hover tooltip a phone never shows. The tooltip stays for the desktop.
- **Collections is a switch** (`role="switch"`, `aria-checked`) on its own row under the rail: red and on when the job is in Collections, grey and disabled with "· applies once the job is Billed" otherwise. Same confirm-with-note flow as before on toggle.
- Help guide `edit-job-autosave.md` paragraph rewritten around the three looks and the switch. New render smoke `JobStatusStepper.render.test.tsx` (Working job: reachable/current/locked map, the locked note, the disabled switch; Billed job in Collections: switch on, Paid reachable, Waiting locked).

## Notes for the next session

- The Job pane's read-only `JobLedgerStatusPipeline` (Detail modal) still draws the old arrow line. If the rail reads well, the same three-look treatment (minus the taps) could replace it so both views match.
