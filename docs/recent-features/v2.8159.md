# v2.8159 — State roll: a place to keep who holds a state license near us (2026-11-21)

Punch list #411, step one of the train in [`to-dos/state-roll/`](../../to-dos/state-roll/README.md). One migration, [`20261121140000_license_roll`](../migrations/20261121140000_license_roll.md). No screen changes.

## Why

Taunya cannot find experienced plumbers, and they must be licensed. On 2026-11-20 she asked whether the state's public rolls could say who was recently licensed and whose license just lapsed. A read of the sources that day found that the plumbing board gives its whole roll away as free files, and that TDLR's open list covers electrical and A/C. On 2026-11-21 she said to build it as drawn, under a new tab.

The first thing that needs is somewhere to keep the roll, and somewhere to keep what changes on it.

## What changed

- **`license_roll`**: one row a license as the state's file has it today, for the counties in `license_roll_counties` or because the number is one of our own.
- **`license_roll_changes`**: what a pull saw change on a row. The state keeps no history, so this table is the only one, and it starts on our first pull. That is why it ships before the pull does.
- **`license_roll_pulls`**: the ledger of files read.
- **`license_roll_counties`**: the counties kept, seeded with the five the permits cover.
- **`license_roll_left_off`**: a person who asked not to be listed, kept by number and by name so a new rank does not bring them back.
- **`user_can_read_license_roll()`**: the one gate, true for the people who hold the Hiring switch.

Nobody signed in writes a roll row. The pull, an edge function of its own, writes with the service key. The four fences close the file.

## What is not kept

No phone, street address or email for a person: the state does not give them. No apprentice row, unless the number is one of our own people's. A responsible master plumber's row keeps the company and the day the insurance on file ends, and not the business address or phone.

## Verified

- A stand-in rig on a local Postgres 15, not the app's schema: a stub of the four things the file leans on (`users`, `auth.uid()`, `user_has_team_prospects_access()` and no-op fences), then the file twice, then a scenario with made-up rows.
  - The service role writes a pull, two roll rows of one number at two classes, a change and a left-off row.
  - A second row of one number in one class is refused, and so is a left-off row that names nobody.
  - A Hiring holder reads every table, adds and removes a county as themself, and is refused a write on the roll and on left off. A lower-case county is refused.
  - Someone without the switch reads no row and adds no county. Signed out, the roll and the gate are both refused.
  - Deleting a roll row takes its history with it.
- Not run against the app's own schema before the merge: there is no staging. It is pushed and checked right after the merge, with the queries in the migration's doc.
- `bash scripts/check-migrations.sh` passes.
