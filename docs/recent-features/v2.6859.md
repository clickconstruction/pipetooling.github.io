# v2.6859 — Permits: Austin joins San Antonio, and the ground for more cities (punch list #292, revision 4) (2026-11-10)

The owner asked for the permits of Austin, New Braunfels, San Marcos and Seguin. All four publish permits a program can read (the card lists what each one carries). This is the ground they all stand on, and Austin, whose feed is the richest.

## What changed

- **`supabase/functions/_shared/permitCities.ts`** (new, pure): `FeedReader` is one feed of one city whatever its door: `pageUrl(sinceDay, limit, offset)`, `recordsOf(body)`, `rowOf(record)`. San Antonio's two CKAN feeds are wrapped unchanged. Austin is the city's Socrata dataset of issued permits: `classifyAustin` keeps a commercial new build, shell, remodel and addition (class 329, a structure that is not a building, reads as site work), a house's new build and addition, and plumbing permits less irrigation, fire line and demolition; `permitRowFromAustin` reads the contractor's company, else the applicant's, as who pulled it, a zero valuation as none declared, and the description cut to a title. `PERMIT_CITIES` holds each city's county, centre, permit search, deed search, whether its county parcels are read, and its feeds.
- **`permit-pull`**: runs `feedsToPull(city)`. The body takes `city`; an unknown name is refused with the list. With no city every city is read under the one time budget.
- **`permitCityDoors.ts`** (new, pure): the county a card names, `noParcelWords`, the appraisal district (`txCountyLookup`), the city's permit search, the deed search, `scoresMiles` (a city more than 50 miles from the office keeps its miles on the card and off the score), and the last city this browser showed.
- **The app**: the city box lists `PERMIT_CITY_NAMES`; Pull now sends the city; `lookupParcel` answers without a call where the county is not read; the deck, the building sheet and the Who sheet take their county words and doors from the kernel.
- No migration: `permits` was city-shaped from the start, and the nightly job's empty body now means every city.

## Read live before building

75 days of Austin through the reader: 4,247 rows in 5.5 seconds; 857 commercial building permits (554 remodels, 165 site work, 122 new, 16 additions), 854 with a name, 436 with a point, 208 with a declared value.
