# v2.5855 — The Collect desk, PR 4 of 7: after the call (2026-10-25)

Punch list #177, PR D, on [v2.5854](v2.5854.md). The piece that joins Monday's sheet to Friday's answer: what gets recorded after each call, and what the app does with each answer. Nothing new in the record.

## The kernel (`src/lib/bridge/collect/collectOutcome.ts`)

`collectOutcomePlan(input, { graceDays, quietDays, dayLabel })` turns one of four answers into the writes the app already keeps and the sentences the sheet shows:

| answer | the record | what then happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Money is in the bank** | a `note` touch *Paid after the call*; the payment itself is recorded where money lives | the pile falls once the payment is recorded; Friday counts it under landed, after a call; the payer gets a pay speed |
| **They named a date** | a promise on each of the payer's open jobs with the day, who said it and their words (`recordPromiseForJobs`, which writes the promise and the `promised` touch) | the forecast moves; past the day and the 7-day grace the row turns to past a promise and is Monday's first call; a second miss carries the owner's name; the Lien desk's clock does not move |
| **No date** | `cant_reach` for no answer or a wrong contact; a `note` with their words when they spoke and named no day; a `resend` touch when the bill goes again | the row keeps its state and is back on the sheet after `TOUCH_QUIET_DAYS`; Friday counts it under no date; the forecast does not move |
| **They dispute it** | a `dispute` touch with what they dispute (the amount, the work, who owes it, a pay app they rejected) and their words | the row reaches the owner; the bills stay in the pile marked disputed; the lien clock keeps running; Friday counts it under on the owner |

`mailedCheckYmd` dates *"the check went out Friday"* by the mail day plus `MAILED_CHECK_DAYS` (5). Every answer, reason and sentence passes the plain-words rule in `collectOutcome.test.ts`.

## The sheet (`src/components/bridge/BridgeCollectOutcome.tsx`)

Opens from **After the call: record the answer ›** on any call on the desk's sheet, over the modal, with the payer and its promise in the title. Four answer buttons; the fields each needs (the day with a *Mailed today, so \<day\>* chip, who said it, their words; what happened; what they dispute; the resend tick); the grey line *What the app then does*; one button named for the answer. The writes go through a `writer` the page provides (`addPaymentChaseTouch` and `recordPromiseForJobs` from `paymentChaseIo`), so the sheet renders and tests without the database. On success the desk reloads its reads and the page its data, so the row's promise state, last touch and Friday's counts move at once. Esc closes the sheet, not the desk under it.

## Tests
