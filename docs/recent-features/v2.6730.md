# v2.6730 — My email: a door on the job and the bid (punch list #263 PR A, call 1) (2026-11-08)

Punch list #263 (`to-dos/messages-my-email-doors/README.md`), the owner's call 1 of 2026-11-08, option A: the My email tab (#230, v2.6274) shipped as a place to go, and four days of the usage ledger showed no clickplumbing.com person had gone. The people who would tag live on Jobs → Stages and the Bid Board. This PR puts the tab's one press where they already are. Client only: no migration, no function change; `gmail-mine`'s `list` gets a second caller.

- **The line** ([`MyMailJobLine.tsx`](../../src/components/messages/myMail/MyMailJobLine.tsx)): *Your mail with Dale Okafor · 2 conversations not on this job*, then up to three conversations newest first, each with its way (↓ in, ↑ out), its subject, who and the day, *3 in it* and 📎 when they apply, and **Yes**, the tab's own `tag` onto this job. After Yes the row turns green, *on JP1077 ✓ · 3 lines and 1 file*, with *open the thread ›*; the count beside the head goes down by one. A refusal of the tag shows the function's words on the line; a lost signal `formatErrorMessage`'s. Under the rows the quiet line *Read from your Gmail as this opened. Nothing is kept until you press Yes.*, *N more.* when the page holds more than three, and *All your mail with Dale ›*, which opens `/messages?tab=my-email&q=<the same search>`. The read happens once per window open, keyed by the job and the people; nothing counts or nags. No line, and no words, when the switch is off (`loadMyMailSetting`), when the function refuses (off, offWorkspace, imitated, readOnly, google), when the read fails, when there is nothing to search for, or when every conversation on the page is on the job already.
- **The two surfaces.** The job window's Job tab (`DetailJobModal.tsx`), under **Where it stands** and offered on the same gate (`jobBoxOffered`, a job with a customer), the job's people being the summary's own (`summaryRead.facts.people`, the contacts its lines name and the customer's people), their addresses read off `contact_channels`; Yes reads the summary again, *open the thread ›* closes the window and opens the person's thread. And the thread window narrowed to a job (`OpenThread.jobFilter`, how the Bid Board opens a GC's thread on a bid), as a new `aboveBox` slot ([`useMyMailJobLineThreadSlots.tsx`](../../src/components/messages/useMyMailJobLineThreadSlots.tsx), merged last in `useThreadSlots`): the person's email channels and the tile's number; Yes asks the page to read the thread again; the door to the tab closes the thread first. The thread showing every job draws nothing.
- **The kernel** ([`jobDoor.ts`](../../src/lib/myMail/jobDoor.ts)): `jobDoorQuery(addresses, jobNum)` = `from:a OR to:a OR … OR "JP1077"`, flat with no braces or brackets (the function's `fencedQuery` strips them), an address that could break the fence left out; `jobDoorHref(query)`; `jobDoorRows(rows, jobKey)` groups the page by conversation newest first, drops a conversation tagged onto this job, one a line of this job holds (`inMessages.jobKey`) and a folded notice, keeps `JOB_DOOR_MAX` (3) with the count of the rest (a conversation tagged onto another job stays: Yes moves it, as the tab's tag does); `JOB_DOOR_WORDS` and `jobDoorRowFacts`, held to `plainWords`. **The io** ([`jobDoorIo.ts`](../../src/lib/myMail/jobDoorIo.ts)): `emailAddressesOf(contactIds)`, `readJobDoor` (the switch, then `list` To tag with the search; null for no line on any refusal) and `supabaseJobDoorIo`.
- **The guide** *tag an email onto a job from your own mail* gains *Tag from the job or the bid*, and its reads-and-keeps list says the job window and a bid's thread read your mail with that person too.
- **Docs**: the My email row and the slots paragraph in `MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `list`'s second caller in `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` → gmail-mine, the Messages sentence in `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`, the `/messages` line in `twins/APP_DIRECTORY.md`.
