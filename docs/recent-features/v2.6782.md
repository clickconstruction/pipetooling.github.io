# v2.6782 — Research: the permits a city issues, on a ledger under Prospects (punch list #292, PR 1) (2026-11-09)

The owner's ask of 2026-11-09: take every permit a city pulls onto a map and a ledger, sort them, and signal to the estimators doing research which to look into and whose work to approach, so the company gets more of the work it wants. PR 1 is the ledger and the feed (`to-dos/permit-research/`): San Antonio first, the map in PR 2.

## What changed

- **Migration `20261109070000_permit_research.sql`**: `permits` (one row per permit, keyed by city, source, number and type, since a commercial job's trade permits share the building permit's number), `permit_verdicts` (append-only, the newest per permit wins, a reason on every row), `permit_pulls` (each run of the feed), the predicate `user_can_research_permits()` (the office and every estimator), the ledger's read `research_permits(city, since, residential)` with the plumber pairing (a plumbing permit on the same number or address within the job's window) and the newest verdict, and the pg_cron row that POSTs `permit-pull` at 11:30 UTC.
- **`supabase/functions/permit-pull`** (new) over **`_shared/permitFeed.ts`** and **`_shared/permitCoords.ts`** (pure, tested from `src/lib/research/`): two CKAN datastores read through the SQL door (issued permits, submitted applications), the kept building types and every plumbing type since ten days before the newest row held, paged oldest first, upserted; a State Plane point (EPSG:2278) converted when the feed gives feet instead of degrees. `X-Cron-Secret` or a researcher's JWT.
- **`src/lib/research/permitResearch.ts`** (pure): the four verdicts and their words, the lenses (waiting, look, approached, not for us, already have a plumber), the window, the Pulled line, the Work cell's words, the GC lead and job `Approach them` makes, the hold on an empty reason. **`permitResearchIo.ts`**: the reads and writes, `as never` until the types regen.
- **`ProspectsResearchTab`** and **`PermitDecideSheet`** (`src/components/prospects/`): the ledger with its filters and one Decide press per row; the sheet with the four verdicts, the reason, the look-again day and the company's part on an Approach.
- **Prospects**: a Research top tab for the office and every estimator; an estimator with no Prospects flag lands on it and sees no calling-deck tabs; the nav shows *Research* in place of *Prospects* for that estimator; `/prospects` is open to every estimator in `layoutRouteAccess`.
- Guide `research-permits-for-work-to-bid.md`.

## Release order

Merge, `supabase db push`, `supabase functions deploy permit-pull`, then one POST with the cron secret (or Pull now in the app) for the first 75-day read. The next `chore(types)` PR picks up the tables and the RPC.
