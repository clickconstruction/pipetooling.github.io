# v2.8229 — Papers before a job opens: the presses on the way to a job's settings take a finger (2026-11-21)

Punch list #389, step 4, after its live test. Taunya asked on 2026-11-21 that the settings page be tested live once it was all out, for buttons that are easy to press and sliders big enough to use. The page shipped in v2.8184. This is what the live test found and fixed. No migration and nothing to deploy.

## The live test

On prod, a `ZZ Test` paper built from settings on the `ZZ TEST sink` job, with placeholder words, at 1100 px and at 375 px, with real presses and real drags.

- **The flow and the one page passed.** Every press and field measured 44 px tall or more. The slider is a 44 px band at both widths, and a stop's name is 44 px wide or more.
- **Each way of moving a slider did what it should, at both widths:** a drag end to end, a press on a stop's name, a press on the bare track, and a row of the list of levels.
- **Three saves landed as the page said they would:** the paper required with its picks, basis and tick, a change saved in place while nobody had signed, and the basis saved alone.
- **A refusal kept the walk.** The test paper had no wording for one choice. The database said which, the page showed it, and the picks and the typed blank stayed.

## What was small, and is fixed here

All of it was on the way into the page, in the papers section of the Documents tab (`JobDocumentsRequiredPapers.tsx`).

- **Settings and Take off** on a paper's row were words 20 px tall, side by side, about 60 px apart. Each is now a press 44 px tall with room either side. Take off still asks before it does anything.
- **Add a paper** was 28 px tall, **Close the list** and **Copy the signing link** 20 px, and a row of the list of papers 37 px on a desktop. Each is 44 px or more.
- **Next jumped.** On the notice step the line *Tick the box to go on.* left when the box was ticked, and **Next** moved 27 px under the finger. The line keeps its room (`DocumentBuilderFlow.tsx`), so the Document builder tab gets the same.
- **A blank under the fold.** On the review the typed blanks sit under the review itself, below the fold on most screens, and the line *Type … first.* was words only. For a host that asks, that line is now a press 44 px tall that scrolls to the first empty blank and puts the cursor in it (`FlowHost.confirm.onNote`).

## Still small, and left

- **A dash of the step bar** is 38 px wide at phone width with seven steps, and narrower with more. It is 44 px tall, and a real press on one landed. Back and Next do the same job at full size.
- **The confirm window's two buttons** are 36 px tall. That window is shared by the whole app and is not changed here.
- **The door on the Papers to sign window**, *This job's papers and who has signed*, is 28 px tall. It belongs to the gate's own window.

A finger's drag was not tested: the browser pane sends a mouse. The slider is the browser's own range input with `touch-action: pan-y`, so a sideways drag is the slider's and an up and down drag scrolls the page.

## What the office meets after requiring a paper

Requiring a paper shuts the job to the person who required it too, until they sign. The job window closes and the Papers to sign window opens, with the job's papers and their Settings press one press away. That is the gate as built in its own steps, and it is said here because it is the first thing the office sees after the flow.
