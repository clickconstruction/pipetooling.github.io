# v2.4516 — Overhead: Man hours fits the screen it is on (2026-10-04)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. The owner's ask after v2.4513–v2.4515: look at the finished card with screenshots, find what can be better, and build it. Six things came out of a pass at 1280 px, at 375 px and in dark.

## What was wrong, and what changed

1. **A click on a bar changed a list out of view.** The who list sat under the table, about 700 px below the bars. At a card width of 980 px and up (`WIDE_FROM` in [`ManHoursCard.tsx`](../../src/components/people/ManHoursCard.tsx)) the list now stands beside the picture (`ManHoursWho layout="side"`): its doors sit under the title, its rows scroll inside the panel under a pinned header, and a person's *N h waiting* drops under the name so the Total column stays in view. Narrower cards keep the list under the table. At 1280 px the card went from 1,439 px tall to about 825.
2. **The picture scaled with the window.** It was a 760-unit drawing stretched to the card, so the bars were 350 px tall at 1280 px and the type grew with them. [`ManHoursChart.tsx`](../../src/components/people/ManHoursChart.tsx) now measures its own width (`useMeasuredWidth`) and lays the columns out at real pixel size: 220 px of bars and 92 px of share line at every width, 11 px axis type.
3. **On a phone the picture scrolled sideways and opened on the oldest months.** With real-pixel layout the 620 px floor and the sideways scroll are gone: thirteen weeks fit in 333 px. Bars may be as narrow as 6 px. When the slots are too narrow to name every column, `manHoursLabeledColumns` names every nth counted back from the newest, always names the picked and the hovered column, and drops a regular label that would collide with one of those.
4. **The picked column was a slab.** The band filled the whole slot (the full plot on the Year zoom), and the bars' 2 px gap stroke stayed the card's color, which read as dark outlines inside the band. The band is now the bar plus 8 px a side (`bandX` / `bandWidth` from `buildManHoursBars`), and the gap stroke takes the band's color.
5. **A person with a few minutes was a row of dashes.** `formatManHours` (in `manHoursByPeriod.ts`, shared by the card's table and the who list) prints `<1` for time over zero and under half an hour.
6. **On a phone the names scrolled away with the numbers.** The Period column of the card's table and the Person column of the who list are pinned (`position: sticky`); under 560 px (`COMPACT_UNDER`) a row's chips drop under its name so the pinned column stays narrow.
