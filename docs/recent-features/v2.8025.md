# v2.8025 — Sub papers: the paper presses are the office's alone (2026-11-20)

Client only: no migration, no edge function. Punch list #350, found while building the Send press (v2.7983) and left for a change of its own.

## Before

A superintendent reaches **Jobs → Subs → Pay** (the Pay view is theirs, with no payments list). Since v2.7453 the view showed them *Statement of account…* on a sub's group, *Work statement…* in Print's place on a sheet's ⋯ menu, and *Payment record…* beside a payment. The papers window opened for them on figures with no payments in them, since the payments table hides its rows from that role. The register refused them at Print or Download (*Only the office roles who see sub pay can make a sub paper.*), so no paper was ever made. The card's rule is that a superintendent gets no press.

Read on prod on 2026-11-19: two active superintendent logins.

## What changed

- `JobsSubLaborTab.tsx` reads the role (`useAuth`) and draws the three presses only where `isOfficeRole` holds, the client's mirror of `is_office_staff()`, which `mint_sub_paper` and `send-sub-paper-email` hold too. For anyone else a one-person sheet's ⋯ menu keeps **Print**, as a shared sheet and a crew sheet always have.
- `PeopleSubsTab.tsx` hands a sub's open row (`SubOwedBlock`) its *Statement of account…* and *Work statement…* only under the same rule.
- An account in training keeps the presses. The register refuses its paper with the reason, as before.

## Tests

- `JobsSubLaborTab.papers.render.test.tsx` (+1): a superintendent gets no statement, no work statement and no payment record, and the ⋯ menu's Print prints. It fails with the gate taken out.
- `PeopleSubsTab.render.test.tsx`: the open row offers *Statement of account…* to a dev and none to a superintendent. It fails with the gate taken out.

## Docs

The guide `make-a-statement-of-account-for-a-sub` says who gets the presses. `GLOSSARY.md` → *Sub papers* and `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` (the `sub_papers` bullet) each have their sentence amended. The card's *Where it stands* marks the item done.
