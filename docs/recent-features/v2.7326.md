# v2.7326 — The job window on a computer: a new job reads as what is next, not a stack of empties (2026-11-15)

The owner's look at the desk job window after the phone pass (punch list #355, [`to-dos/job-window-phone.md`](../../to-dos/job-window-phone.md)). Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## Before

On a new job the desk window read as eight empties in a row: no photos, no email, three accounts none yet, hours none yet, no calls, questions none yet, nothing asked. Two of those were the same fact: the questions head said *none yet* and an `EmptyState` under it said *Nothing asked about this job yet*, each with its own **Ask the estimators**. The calls line with nothing written sat in a blue-bordered, blue-tinted box, the shape of a field waiting for words. **Paint a map** was a dashed box the full width of the window, the loudest thing on the screen for a side act. And the six icons on the money-story row had titles on hover and nothing to read.

## What changed

- **[`src/components/developments/RecordQuestions.tsx`](../../src/components/developments/RecordQuestions.tsx)**: with nothing asked, *none yet* beside the title and the two presses at its right are the whole empty; the `EmptyState` under them is gone. The same section serves the bid window and the Development page, so they lose the second line too.
- **[`src/components/jobs/JobThreadSummary.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobThreadSummary.tsx)**: the empty line is plain muted words at the photos line's size, no border and no tint.
- **[`src/components/paintedMap/JobPaintedMapsStrip.tsx`](../../src/components/paintedMap/JobPaintedMapsStrip.tsx)**: the desk's **Paint a map** is an ordinary press at the strip's left, solid border, 34 px tall; the phone's centred words (v2.7281) keep their own width and height.
- **`DetailJobModal.tsx`**, **`ShareJobButton.tsx`** (a `word` prop) and **`JobWindowModal.tsx`**: each head icon carries `data-word`, and above 640 px the window's stylesheet turns the press into a column and draws the word under the glyph as a `::after` in muted 0.6 rem. The markup of each press is as it was; the phone, which draws the ⋯ sheet instead, never sees the rule.

## Is this the best we can do?

The words could have been typed into each of seven presses, with the column layout repeated seven times; one attribute per press and one rule in the window's stylesheet does the same and leaves the full-page job detail (not the pane) as it was, since the rule is scoped to `.job-window-body`. The remaining empties (photos, email, the three accounts, hours) each say what fills them and are one line, so they stay. What this does not do is reorder the Job tab or change its 760 px width, a reading width chosen on purpose.
