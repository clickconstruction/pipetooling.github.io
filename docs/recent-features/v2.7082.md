# v2.7082 — Tasks: the groundwork for a simpler New task window (2026-11-12)

Punch list #331, PR 1 of 4. Grace asked for a visual upgrade of the *Add checklist item:* window, saw the card and the pressable mock-up (`to-dos/new-task-window/`), and said to build it. This PR is the part with no screen in it. No migration, no edge function, nothing a person can see.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/tasks/newTaskWindow.ts`**, pure, 26 tests:
  - **The faces.** `recentAssigneeIds` lists the people a giver gave to last, newest task first, each once, the giver left out, up to eight. `taskFaces` orders the row: Me, then anyone the window opened on or who was picked from the full list, then the recent people. `toggleFace` and `dropSoloSelfWhenAddingOthers` carry the window's old rule that the giver drops off when a first other person is added.
  - **The day bar.** `whenBarChoice`, `whenBarPick` and `whenBarDayLabel` put four choices (Today, Tomorrow, Pick a day, Repeats) over the three the save already stores. Tomorrow is stored as a date, so nothing downstream changes.
  - **The Add row.** `taskAddRow` names the rare things in the order of their use and shows each one's value once set: *✓ Remind · Morning 7:00*, *✓ 2 links*, *✓ Due by Fri, Nov 20*. A repeating task leaves out *Due by* and *Just for the day*.
  - **The sentence and Send.** `newTaskSentence` says what Send will do: *Theo gets it today. It stays until it is done. You hear when it is done.* While something is missing it names that one thing and marks Send dead. `taskSendLabel` gives *Add to my list*, *Send to Theo*, *Send to Theo and Imani*, *Send to 4 people*.

## Why a new sentence

The window already writes a sentence (`checklistScheduleSummary`), and the Edit window still uses it. The new one is for the bar a first-timer reads at the moment of sending, so it keeps to `plainWords.ts`: short sentences and no dash. The test runs every shape of it through `plainWordsFailures`.
