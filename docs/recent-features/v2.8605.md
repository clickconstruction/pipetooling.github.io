# v2.8605 — The bids of the day: the ground under the Clock out list (2026-11-25)

Punch list #445, `to-dos/clock-out-bids-of-the-day/`. The owner said *I like it, build it* to the mock-up's pick on 2026-11-25. This is the first PR of the build. A migration and its test, no client change, no edge function.

**What it is for.** At clock out an estimator types what they worked on. The app already keeps every change on a bid with its person and its time (`bid_changes`, #73). The list will read that, plus a new beat for a bid that was open and read with nothing changed.

**What this PR makes** (`20261125223610_bid_day_ground.sql`, `docs/migrations/20261125223610_bid_day_ground.md`):

- `user_bid_activity`: the seconds a bid was in front, one row a person, a bid, a Bids tab and a five-minute bucket.
- `bid_day_entries`: the list a person left at clock out, each bid kept or taken off.
- `bump_user_bid_activity`, `get_my_bid_day` and `save_my_bid_day`.

**Who reads what.** The beat's table follows the People page's Activity tab: the person, the owner, a dev and anyone granted. The kept list follows the clock sessions: the person, a dev, payroll access and the office. `docs/ACCESS_CONTROL.md` holds both.

**How it is tested.** `npm run test:pg:bid-day`, 44 checks on the whole schema: the bucket's cap, who writes no beat, who reads each table, a day's read with the app's own writes counted apart, and the list kept, changed and refused for an old day.

**Owed after the merge.** `supabase db push`, then the types PR. Nothing on screen waits on it.

**Next.** The beat from the Bids page, then the list in the Clock out sheet.
