# v2.6256 — A report's photos show wherever the report is read, with a count on the folded rows (punch list #229, PR 3 of six) (2026-11-01)

Punch list #229, PR 3. The report card (`ReportDetailBody` → `ReportPhotosStrip`, v2.6083) showed a report's photos, but the office reads a report three ways before it ever opens the card, and each drew the report's words by hand and never its pictures: the Dashboard's Recent reports, the Reports tab's cards and grouped rows, and the job window's thread. No migration, no edge function. PR 2 (Turnaway) follows PR 1's merge.

## What changed

- **`lib/photos/reportPhotoCounts.ts`** (new, tested): the ids in chunks of 150 with empties and repeats dropped (`chunkIds`), the count per report from the rows a read answered (`countPhotosByReport`), the badge's words (`photoBadge`: *📷 2*, read as *2 photos*, null for none) and which ids a list still has to read (`idsToRead`). **`loadReportPhotoCounts(ids)`** in `reportPhotosIo.ts` reads `report_id` only, one read per chunk. **`hooks/useReportPhotoCounts(ids)`** reads once for the ids a list does not hold yet, never one a row; a list that grows reads only its new ids; a failed read draws no badge and asks again on the next list.
- **`ReportPhotoCount`** (new): the badge, `quiet` blue or `new` green, with the count in words for a reader.
- **`ReportPhotosStrip`** gains `compact`: 56 px thumbnails with no words, the Drive door on a filed one, the stage, the sender and where it stands in the title. The card's strip is unchanged.
- **The Dashboard's Recent reports** (`DashboardRecentReportsSection`): the badge beside the author and the day on every row, green on a row not yet opened; the strip under the answers of an expanded row.
- **The Reports tab** (`JobsReportsListView`): the badge on the feed card's author line and on an open group's row; the strip under an open group's row that has photos.
- **The job window's thread** (`JobThreadNotesPanel`, every host that hands it a report in its activity): the badge after the time on a report item, and the compact strip under its field lines when it has photos.
- **Guides**: *read field reports on the dashboard* and *file and review field reports* say what the badge and the strip are. The Glossary's **Report photos** entry gains its line in the card's closing docs PR, since five PRs amending one line would conflict in turn.
- **Test**: `JobsReportsListView.photos.render.test.tsx` (new, 2): one count read for the rows, the badge on the card with photos and none on the Note, the strip in an open group's row with the Drive door and the waiting thumbnail; `reportPhotoCounts.test.ts` (4).

## Decisions

- **One read per list.** `report_photos` is read by `report_id in (…)`, counted on the client, keyed by the ids' join so a re-rendered array reads nothing new. A strip still reads its own report's rows once, but only where a row is open and the count says there is something to draw, so a thread with thirty reports and one photo makes two reads, not thirty-one.
- **The Dashboard's expanded row always mounts the strip.** The person pressed the row; one read is what the card does too. The group rows and the thread gate on the count, since many are drawn at once.
- **The thread's row is compact.** The thumbnails say a picture is there and open it in Drive; the words stay on the card the row opens.
