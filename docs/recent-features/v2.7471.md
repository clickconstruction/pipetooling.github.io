# v2.7471 — Supply houses: the bank agrees with a payment, and a bank line opens Record a payment (2026-11-16)

PR 7 of the supply house payments train, the last. The owner, 2026-11-16: "most of our supply house bills are paid via credit card or ach". His one open call was which card pays the houses and whether it is in the bank feed.

## What the feed holds

Read from `mercury_transactions` on 2026-11-16, read-only:

- **Card: in the feed.** A statement-sized payment to a house is a debit card line on the company's Mercury card, with the house as its payee. The same card makes small counter purchases there, and a failed attempt sits beside the one that went through.
- **ACH: in the feed.** A house that pulls by ACH shows as a line of kind `other` with the house's name.
- **Not everything.** Two of the larger houses show no line at all in three months, so whatever pays them is not in Mercury. Those payments stay typed. Nothing has to be configured for that: a payment with no line simply gets no offer.

## What changes

- **A payment is offered its bank line**: the same amount to the cent, within three days, not taken by another payment, the house's own line first. **Match** ties them and the window reads **Bank agrees ✓** with the line; **Take it off** undoes it. Nothing is matched without a press.
- **Use this line** on the finish-later list: a payment made by Mark paid takes the line's day and its kind as the method (a card line is card, anything else is ACH) and leaves the list. It is offered only when the line's payee reads like the house.
- **Bank lines no payment claims.** Under the finish-later strip, Accounts payable draws **2 bank lines to supply houses have no payment recorded** with their total while there are any. **Say what it paid** opens that house's **Record a payment** with the amount typed (the oldest bills filled), the day and the method set, and the payment recorded there is matched to the line.
- **What is not listed**: a line the Tally already settled (put on a job or a bid, or tied to a bill of its own), a line before 2026-11-16 (every older bill carries a payment "from before"), a failed or cancelled line, and a line to a vendor that is not a supply house.
- A match lets go by itself when its payment's amount changes or the payment is removed.
