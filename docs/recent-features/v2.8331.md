# v2.8331 — Dashboard: the office's round is a week you can see (2026-11-23)

Punch list #424, the second card on it. The owner, 2026-11-23, with the card circled on his phone: *the office round is not context rich and as a result a user looking at it for the first time in a while has no idea what it means.* Then, on the first drawing: *show instead of tell*, and *clearly distinguish the values and variables that can change so a user knows what is syntax and what is signal.* He picked the week of bars. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was there

`DashboardOfficeRoundCard` drew a chip, a grey line and a sentence. The morning after a missed round all three said the miss: a red chip *No round* with the day, *Last round* with a day and a count of stations, and *The round did not run* that day, *Today's starts on Today's lines*. *Today's lines* is the name of the first station, so one word meant two things. The card never said what a round is. Its one press was a link at the end of the sentence. The red was the same after one missed day or ten, and on a weekend after a missed Friday.

## What changed

- **Six cells.** `officeRoundWeek(events, today, label)` in `src/lib/quickfill/roundClose.ts` gives the last five workdays and today. A day the round ran is a bar with the stations looked at and the first name of whoever pressed most. A missed workday is an empty dashed cell that reads *missed*. Today is the outlined cell. On a weekend with no mark it reads *none due*.
- **The miss is said once**, by its cell.
- **What a round is** is one grey line in the card's head: *each workday on Quickfill*.
- **Amber, then red.** One or two missed workdays are amber. Three in a row (`ROUND_RED_FROM_WORKDAYS`) turn the card's edge, the holes and the press red. A mark today is green whatever came before.
- **What the last round left** sits under the cells: counts for an earlier round, and doors into each station for today's once a station is marked.
- **One small press at the bottom right.** *Start the round* opens the station `roundStartStation` picks, as before. Once today has a mark, or on a weekend that owes no round, it reads *Open Quickfill*. It is drawn 32 px tall inside a 44 px touch area (`thumbInLine`).
- **Values apart from words.** Each count is a `WorkedOutValue`. The fixed words are `ROUND_WEEK_WORDS`, in grey.

## What the drawing had that the code does not

The mock-up drew *19 of 21 stations* and two states for today, *under way* and *done*. Reading `src/lib/quickfill/round.ts` for the build showed a round is the stations due that day, each on its own rhythm (`roundQueue`), so no day has a fixed count to be measured against. A bar is therefore that day against the fullest day drawn, and the number under it is the fact. Whether today's round is finished is the queue's to say, and the card reads marks, not the queue, so its press after the first mark claims nothing. Reading the queue on the Dashboard is a step of its own, left on the card.

## How it is built

- `roundClose.ts`: `officeRoundWeek`, `roundCellDay`, `roundCellTitle`, `roundLeftLabel`, `roundWeekPress`, `ROUND_WEEK_WORDS`. `officeRoundState`, `officeRoundHeadline`, `roundStartWords` and `roundStartPress` are gone: the card was their only reader.
- `DashboardOfficeRoundCard.tsx`: the read is unchanged, ten days of `quickfill_section_mark_events` once per visit. The drawing is `OfficeRoundWeekView`, apart from the read.
- Who sees the card is unchanged: `isNeedsYouOfficeRole`.
