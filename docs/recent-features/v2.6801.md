# v2.6801 — Tape labels from the app: one strip per box for a Brother P-touch (punch list #294, PR 5) (2026-11-09)

The owner's tape printers (a PT-E720BT and a PT-D610BT) for boxes in the sun ([`to-dos/prts-box-page/README.md`](../../to-dos/prts-box-page/README.md), section 7). The rule: let the printer's own driver do the work. The app lays the strip out at the tape's size and prints it through the browser's print window, as the sheet labels print today; no printer code in the app.

## What changed

- **`src/components/materials/LabelsPrintModal.tsx`** (the Labels window, moved out of `JobsMaterialsTab.tsx` into its own file as the manifest's is, with a `print` prop for its smoke): **Sheet** · **Tape** chips, remembered on the device (`labelPrefs.ts`); under Tape a width chip, 18 or 24 mm, and the hint *The first time, pick the printer and the N mm paper in the print window*; a chip per box leaves one out; the press reads `Print 3 tape labels` / `Print 2 labels` from `countedLabel` and is dead at zero with the dead-press pair.
- **`src/lib/materials/tapeLabel.ts`** (pure, tested): the strip's sizes per width (24 mm: 72 × 24 with a 22 mm QR; 18 mm: 60 × 18), the `@page` rule at the tape's size with no margin and a page break per strip (written after `printRootCss` so it wins), and the press words. The strip: the QR at level M with a quiet zone of four modules, the stencil in the largest type that fits, `PRTS.CO/B/4F2A-9C18` and *Scan for what is inside*, dark on light.
- The guide's Scan a label section; `MATERIALS_ON_SITE_ARCHITECTURE.md`.

## Release order

Merge. Nothing to push or deploy. **The walk**: on the office Mac with each printer on USB and Brother's driver installed, Labels → Tape → Print 3 tape labels → the print window with the Brother printer and the 24 mm paper → three strips cut between, the QR scanning at arm's length to the right box page; once on each printer, once on 18 mm, once on black-on-yellow tape.
