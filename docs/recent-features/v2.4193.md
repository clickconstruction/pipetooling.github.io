# v2.4193 — Pipeline Billed rows: the bill's dates block is drawn the way the money legend is (2026-09-29)

Owner, the day after v2.4168 shipped, with the cell on screen: *"I think it looks terrible but the section above it looks so good."* The money legend works because it has one grammar — a small swatch, a label in one grey, a value right-aligned in ink, a hairline, one bold total. The v2.4168 block broke every one of those at once: a third glyph set (numbered badges) after the squares and the bar, a second track in a different language from the stage bar, dotted underlines on half the rows, three greens, a sparkline indented under one row, a centred floating verdict; with the bill sent today, badges ① and ② also sat on top of each other. Mock-ups: artifact `32AtpkP9DWpVcrTB3a9wkR` — Before beside A (a second ledger, no track), B (the money bar's twin for time over A's rows) and C (the bar plus two lines); the second board runs B through every state the kernel makes. The owner picked B.

## What changed

- **Kernel** [`lib/jobs/billedDatesLedger.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/billedDatesLedger.ts) — `buildBilledDatesLedger` now returns `{ rows, bar, verdict, full }`. `track` (markers, `todayPct`, the gap) and `roomLine` are gone; rows lost their marker number `n` and gained `dot: 'filled' | 'ring'` (filled = behind us, the money, or the row that asks; a ring = a deadline still ahead). **`bar`**: from the bill date (today on a shell row) to the farthest deadline, one `LedgerSegment` per stretch between consecutive dates — `days`, `usedFrac` (how much is behind us), `live` (today falls in it), `kind` (`wait` · `room` between the money and the lien when the money is still ahead and lands first · `short` past the lien when it lands after · `notice` up to a § 53.056 date · `closed`), a tone and a label drawn only when it fits (*66 d of room* · *42 d left* · *notice* · *closed 14 d ago*); `startLabel` / `endLabel` are the two dates, `caption` is *77 days to the lien* or *the window closed Aug 17*. Room and short are decided here from the dates (the runway's own `marks.gap` is null in `notice_due`), so a sub job with a notice owed still shows the green stretch after it. **`verdict`**: the bold line — *Room after they pay · 72 d* (green; amber inside a week of the lien) · *Send the notice · 16 d* · *File the lien first · 5 d short* (red) · *File the lien · 12 d* (a `no_pay` window inside three weeks) · *Ask for a date · 12 d past* (amber, red in Collections; opens They said…) or *Ask for a date · 42 d left* when nobody has named a date · *Lien gone* (red); null for a filed lien and when there is no runway. A closed window is now a grey *Window closed Aug 17 · 44 d ago* (or *Notice window closed …*) row with the verdict carrying the red word. 7 tests (was 6; the `no_pay` case is new).
- **Component** [`BilledDatesLedger.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/BilledDatesLedger.tsx) — the time bar reuses the stage bar's rules (14 px, radius 4, 2 px gaps, `flex: days 1 0px`, 14 px minimum, the label hidden when `textPx` says it will not fit, the `#2563eb` live outline), `useMeasuredWidth` now exported from `StagesStageBar.tsx` for it; the rows are the legend's rows (8 px dot, `var(--text-muted)` words, the value in `var(--text-strong)` or the tone's ink, 600 when the row asks or is amber/red); the sub-line and the evidence indent under the dot; the verdict is `border-top: 1px solid var(--border)` + 600, the label in ink (amber/red when the tone is), the value in the tone. Doors are whole-row `<button>`s with no underline; `.billedDatesLedger button:hover` in `index.css` underlines them. 3 render tests.
- **`BilledReliabilityLine`** takes `showBars` (default true); the tab passes `false` here, so the pay history under Expected is words alone — the sparkline was one of the things fighting the legend.
- **`JobsStagesTab`** — `billedLienRunwayRenderer` unchanged but for the comment and `showBars={false}`.
- **Guides**: *read the Pipeline money view* (the section is rewritten row by row: the bar, the rows, the bold line), *see when a customer will pay* (no more ① / ②); GLOSSARY *Bill dates ledger (Pipeline)*.
