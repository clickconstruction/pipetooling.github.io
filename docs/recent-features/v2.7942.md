# v2.7942 — The permit chip names the name and the day, and opens its card (2026-11-19)

Punch list #401, PR 4 of the train in [`to-dos/permit-news-on-the-bids/`](../../to-dos/permit-news-on-the-bids/README.md). No migration.

## Why

An answered card is no longer news, but the person about to call the builder should still see it. And the chip on the board's row said only *Plumbing permit · another name*: it named neither the name nor the day, and it could not be pressed.

## What changed

- **The chip's words** (`anotherNameChip` in `bidPermitNews.ts`): *Permit lists Copperline Plumbing · Aug 27*, the name the city lists and the permit's day. `PermitChip` cuts a long chip with an ellipsis and never wraps. The other chips read as before.
- **The chip as a door** (`PermitNewsChip` in `PermitChip.tsx`, over `PermitNewsDoors`). On a bid with a line in Followup's Permit news the chip is a press, drawn with an arrow and an outline, and it stops the row's own press. On any other bid it is the label it was: a permit older than its thirty days, the grey chip of a permit that was there before the bid.
- **Where it sits**: the Bid Board's row and phone card, beside the bid's name on a builder's card in the Call queue (`bidHeadline`), and beside the open bid on Waiting to hear. `Bids.tsx` hands the three the same `permitNewsDoors`.
- **Where it lands**: the lens takes `focusBidId`. It switches to **Everyone's** when the bid is not the reader's, opens **Answered** when the bid's line stands, scrolls to the bid and outlines its card. The mark leaves with the lens, and the red number opens the lens with no mark.

## Tests

- `bidPermitNews.test.ts`, `bidPermitNewsOurs.test.ts`: the chip's words, with and without a day.
- `BidsPermitNewsLens.render.test.tsx`: a chip on another person's bid opens the lens on Everyone's with that card outlined, a chip on an answered bid opens Answered, and a chip with no line is not a press.

## Left for later

- A permit taken off a bid does not yet say so in the bid's **This site** list. The list reads the address alone and does not know the bid it is drawn for.
- The bid's suite is read from its address. A bid that names its suite only in its project name (*Suite 100, Tradewind Drive*) still gets a card for a permit on another suite, with the question mark *The permit names suite 120 and the bid names none*. One live card on 2026-11-19 was of this kind.
