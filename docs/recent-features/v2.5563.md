# v2.5563 — Prospects: every other page keeps to the customer deck, and a callback opens on its own tab (2026-10-15)

Punch list #110, PR 4 of four (after v2.5559 the kind and the two lanes, v2.5560 the Subs lane's bench, v2.5561 the Jobs to GC lane's jobs). Once subs and jobs to GC share `prospects` with the customer deck, every other reader of the table has to say which it means. This PR is that sweep, and the two places the train stops short.

- **Quickfill** keeps to the customer deck, as its Prospects section always meant: `loadProspectWarmthCounts` (`src/lib/prospectWarmthCounts.ts`) and `useQuickfillCantReachProspects` read `kind = 'customer'`; the Can't reach section's own presses act by id and are unchanged. Its team chart (`loadProspectTeamActivity` with no kind) still counts every kind, since a call to a sub is the team's work too.
- **Add customer**'s *Started as a prospect?* search (`NewCustomerForm.tsx`) leaves out subs (`kind <> 'sub'`): a sub lands on the bench, never as a customer; a job to GC's owner can become one.
- **The deck opens an inbound prospect on its own lane**: when `?prospect_id=` (Calendar's callback links, any link that names no kind) names a prospect of another kind, `loadFollowUpProspects` sets `?kind=` to that kind and lets the lane's loader take it, instead of laying a sub's card on the Customers lane with the customer's last press.
- **Activity's 30-day table** follows the lane: `loadProspectTeamActivity(supabase, kind)` joins each read on `prospects!inner(kind)` when a kind is given.
- **The copy templates** (No Response, Phone Follow-up, Just Checking In) are the customer deck's words, written to win plumbing customers; they show on the Customers lane only. A sub's or an owner's email waits on the owner's own words, a residual on #110.
- **`leadThread.ts`**: `prospectRowFor` takes the lane (`kind`, default customer) for the day Messages draws a lead's card with **Add to Prospects**. That card is not on screen yet (R8 · 9 has been kernel-only since v2.5416, with the owner's calls on leads' home and who reads a lead's prospect still open), so the press itself is a residual on #110, not built here.
- **Docs**: the Glossary's Prospects entry, the Prospects map's kind note, the page map's §17, and `docs/twins/APP_DIRECTORY.md` (the two lanes, their `?kind=` doors and guides).

Verified by the touched files' tests (33 files: Quickfill's sections, Add customer, the prospects kernels and components, Calendar), `npm run typecheck` and lint; the live walk follows the push with the train's.
