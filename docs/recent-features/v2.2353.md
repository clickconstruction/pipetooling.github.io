# v2.2353 — Profit bar: hover tooltip, click detail card, collapsible legend (2026-08-26)

Estimator request (Wendi): the "Where the profit lives" bar on the Pricing
Workbench only had native `title` tooltips (~1s delay, tiny, unhittable on
2px slivers) and a colorless text caption — no way to tell which color was
which line item.

- `BidsPricingTab.tsx` — the bar's segments are now buttons: **hover** shows
  an instant tooltip (color chip · label · profit $ · share of job) with the
  hovered slice vivid, others dimmed to 0.35 and the slice scaled 1.25y;
  **click** pins a detail card under the bar (qty, unit cost, sale/unit,
  revenue, cost, profit, margin, share, book-entry chip) with a **Jump to
  row** action that clears the unpriced/no-cost filters, smooth-scrolls to
  `#wb-row-<countRowId>` and flashes it blue for 2s. ✕ / Esc / outside click
  close; clicking another slice or chip switches the card.
- The caption is replaced by a **color-keyed legend**: top 12 chips
  (swatch · label · share) + "+N more"; chip hover highlights its slice and
  vice versa; chip click pins the card. **Hide legend ▾ / Show legend ▸**
  toggle at the panel's right, persisted in
  `localStorage.wbProfitLegendCollapsed_v1`.
- New kernel `src/lib/bids/profitBarLegend.ts` (+tests):
  `buildProfitLegend` (chips + moreCount, colorIndex = segment index),
  `formatProfitShare` ("<1%" floor so real slivers never read 0%),
  `clampTooltipLeft` (keeps the tooltip inside the bar).
- Tooltip colors are theme-inverting without new tokens: background
  `var(--text-strong)`, text `var(--surface)`.
- Detail card economics come from the already-derived `eff` rows (no new
  queries); the card is keyed by count-row id so solver re-sorts keep it.
