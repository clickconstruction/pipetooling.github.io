## Decisions

- **Four stars and up reach the tech, with or without words.** The mock-up drew the strip for a 4 or 5 star answer with words; five stars with no words is still the customer's signal, so the strip reads the stars alone. Three or fewer stay with the office (call 1, answered 2026-11-03: the lead talks them through in person).
- **Every tech with a session, not the lead alone**, as the card's decision 6. The one who pressed On my way is always among them and is named first.
- **Got it is a row, not browser storage**, so a tech who reads on the truck's tablet and then their phone presses once.
- **Reading Google's own listing is not built.** The app learns only that the customer pressed Post it on Google; a read of the reviews Google holds needs a Places key and the business's listing id, Micah's to decide.
