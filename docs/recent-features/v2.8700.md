**Read.** The portal draws the office's own `Btn` (`src/components/gc/gcUi.tsx`), 75 times over 17 files. `Btn` takes no fact about the screen, so a press is the same height at 375 px as at a desk. Beside it the portal draws its own plain buttons, links, ticks, pick lists and fields, several with a desk height written into their style (32, 36 and 40 px). **The rule.** At phone width, the app's 640 px line, everything a thumb lands on inside the portal's page stands 44 px tall: a button, a link, a tick's label, a pick list, a fold's line and a field. Above 640 px nothing changes. Outside the portal's page nothing changes at any width.

**How it is built.**

- The portal's page says it is the portal: `GcTradePortal.tsx` puts `TRADE_PORTAL_PAGE_CLASS` (`src/lib/phonePressFloor.ts`) on its `<main>`.
- One rule in `src/index.css`, inside `@media (max-width: 640px)`, grows what is under that class. It sits beside the app's other phone floor, `--phone-press-min`.
- `Btn` is not changed. So no office GC page can change: they draw `Btn` outside the portal's page.
- The rule is `!important`, because several of the portal's presses carry their desk height in their own style.
- A link is no box by itself, so it is drawn as one and then stands as tall. A link in a table's row gives back what it grew by, so the row keeps its height and the link keeps to the line of the day beside it.
- A fold's line and the file picker take the room as padding, so their words sit in the middle.

**Why the stylesheet and not `Btn`.** A fact handed to `Btn` would grow the 75 `Btn` presses and leave the links, the ticks, the pick lists and the fields, each of which would need its own edit, and the next press added to the portal would be small again. The page's class covers what is there and what comes.

**After, measured the same way:** none under 44 px on any of the four screens, and no row wrapped. The pages are longer on a phone: the quote form 1,785 to 2,175 px, a job's pay block 979 to 1,106. At 730 px and at 1,100 px the before and after pictures are the same bytes.

**Tests.** `src/lib/phonePressFloor.test.ts` holds the rule to the class, to the fingertip and to the phone's width, and holds that the class is read nowhere outside that one block. `src/pages/GcTradePortal.render.test.tsx` holds that the page's `<main>` carries the class and that every button of the page sits inside it. A test page has no layout, so the heights themselves are proven in a browser, as above.

**Not measured.** A real phone. A Spanish portal, whose longer labels may wrap. A signed-in trade's own data, which may draw a block the sample does not.
