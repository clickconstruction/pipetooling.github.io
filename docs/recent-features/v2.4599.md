# v2.4599 — Submittals: the GC's review page no longer takes answers from the office (2026-10-05)

Punch list #62 (*See what they see*), PR 1c: the hole found while planning a door from the office to the real room. No migration. One edge function to redeploy after the merge: `submit-submittal-review` (PUNCHLIST deploys).

## What was wrong

`get-submittal-room` has refused to count an office open since v2.2922: a `?preview=1` request or a verified staff session is not a view. But `submit-submittal-review`, the room's writes, never read either signal; the token was its only credential. So an office user who opened a GC's link in their signed-in browser could:

- **identify** as a person on the room: a `bid_submittal_people` row, an `identified` event, a personal token, and the room's anonymous views from the same address in the last half hour moved to that person;
- **ask**: a message row, an `asked` event and an inbox row;
- **decide**: the rows' and parts' review columns and a `decided` event.

The room then recorded a GC answer nobody at the GC gave. The page alone stood in the way, and it did not try.

## What changed

- **The verdict** (`_shared/submittalReviewActions.ts`): `officeWriteVerdict(action, { preview, officeRole })` refuses `identify`, `message` and `decide` with a plain reason. A verified office session reads *You are signed in as the office. Enter their answer from the Submittals tab.* (for an ask: *Answer their questions from the Submittals tab.*). The office's preview reads *This page is open as the office's preview, so nothing here is saved.* The office wins when both are present.
- **Verified, never read off a header** (`officeRoleOf`): the bearer must be a user's token, not the anon key a reviewer's page sends. The auth server must resolve it (`requestStaff`), and that user's `users.role` must be one of the roles that open Bids (`OFFICE_ROLES`, pinned to `canOpenBids`). Subcontractors and helpers are not the office here. Anything that cannot be verified reads as no session.
- **The function** checks first, before anything is read or written, and answers 403 with `{ error, code: 'office' | 'preview' }`.
- **The page** (`SubmittalRoom.tsx`) sends its writes with `?preview=1` when it was opened that way. It already shows the function's `error` where the press was (the identify sheet, the send bar, the thread). The tests now prove each one.

## Who could be both the office and a named reviewer

Nobody today. The four people named on review rooms are at structurainc.com, two test domains, and one with no email. None of them is a company account, and no company account shares their domain (read through the office's own session, 2026-10-05). The case to watch is a company person reviewing for a building the company itself owns or builds. That person enters the answer from the Submittals tab under their own name, or opens the link in a private window, as an outsider would. The room's own tests have always used a private window.

## Tests

- `submittalReviewActions.test.ts` (+6): every action refused for an office role and for the preview, the office over the preview, a reviewer with neither going ahead, `OFFICE_ROLES` equal to `canOpenBids` for all nine roles, and `officeRoleOf` asking the auth server only for a user's token. It returns null for a forged token, a field role, a missing row, or a failed read.
- `submitSubmittalReview.run.test.ts` (4, new): the function's own `index.ts` handler run with its URL imports stood in for and a fake database that records every call.
  - The preview is refused for all three actions with no call at all.
  - An office session is refused after only the auth check and the role read.
  - A reviewer with no session goes straight to the room with no auth call: 404 / 404 / 401 for the unknown test tokens, as before.
  - A forged token or a subcontractor's session is not refused.
- `SubmittalRoom.office.render.test.tsx` (3, new): the identify sheet, the send bar and the thread each show the reason, and a preview's writes go to `submit-submittal-review?preview=1`.

Nothing was pressed on a real room. BP398's room was not opened.
