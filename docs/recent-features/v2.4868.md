# v2.4868 — Help: links between guides open again (2026-10-07)

Found by the help-guides freshness pass (punch list #91) while reading the start-here guides: 112 guide-to-guide links in 40 guides did nothing when tapped. A live check on `/help?g=start-here-as-a-master` showed each of them as an `<a>` with no `href`.

## Why they were dead

`helpGuideMarkdownToSafeHtml` reuses the contract sanitizer, which keeps an `href` only when it is absolute http(s). `helpGuideHtml.ts` carries the in-app forms through on a placeholder host (v2.3516 for `/help/<slug>` and `/help?g=<slug>`, v2.4290 for app paths, v2.4529 for `#<anchor>`). The short `?g=<slug>`, the form the address bar shows and the one the start-here guides were written in on 2026-08-01, was never one of them. Nothing tested the real guides' links, so the gap went unnoticed for two months.

## What shipped

- **`?g=<slug>` rides like `/help/<slug>`** (`IN_APP_GUIDE_HREF` in `src/lib/helpGuideHtml.ts`). The 40 guides are left as written, since both forms now work and the sweep would collide with every session editing a guide.
- **A heading of another guide**, `/help/<slug>#<anchor>`, is carried through too. It is restored with a `data-guide-anchor` hook, and `GuideBrowser.openGuide` puts the anchor on the address, so the existing heading-anchor effect scrolls to it. The Lien desk guide's two links of this kind were dead before.
- **An example email address prints as text.** GFM autolinks it to `mailto:`, the sanitizer drops that `href`, and the bare `<a>` drew in link blue doing nothing (11 guides). The renderer now unwraps a bare `<a>` around an email address.
- **Three bare-slug links** (`(move-a-jobs-costs-onto-a-bid)`, `(read-the-bid-board)`, `(recover-a-deleted-job)`) are written as `/help/<slug>` in *read what bidding costs* and *move a job's costs onto a bid*.
- **One misspelled heading**: *send lien notices from the Lien desk* linked `#name-the-house-in-the-owners-letter`; the heading's anchor is `name-the-house-in-the-owner-s-letter`.

## The guard

`src/lib/helpGuideLinks.guides.test.ts` (jsdom) renders every real guide the way the Help page does and fails on a link with no `href`, a guide link naming no guide, or a link to another guide's heading that is not in it. It failed 51 guides before this change and passes all 328 after. The node and DOM tests of `helpGuideHtml` gain the two new link forms and the email case. `CLAUDE.md` (Help guides) and `docs/HELP_MEDIA_PLAN.md` name the cross-guide heading form and the new test.

## Checked live

On the dev server: *get started as a leader*'s *your subs* opens *review your subs*. The Lien desk guide's link opens *see which supply houses are owed on a lien job* and scrolls to its heading. *track a general contractor on a job* shows its example address as text, with no dead link left.
