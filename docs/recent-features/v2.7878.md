# v2.7878 — Prospects on a phone: the lit tab in view, Closed opens on a chip that holds permits, the count reads whole (2026-11-18)

Three leftovers of a read-only walk of Prospects → Permits on 2026-11-18. Each was seen on a local preview against live data, and each was measured again before and after the change. No migration, no edge function, no new page.

## 1. On a phone the lit tab was off screen

The top row of `Prospects.tsx` draws five pills in a sideways scroller beside the `h1`. At 375 px the scroller is 222 px wide and holds 538 px of pills. It opened at `scrollLeft` 0 whatever tab the address named.

| At 375 px, `?tab=permits` | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| The row's `scrollLeft` | 0 | 316.5 |
| The row on screen | x 12 to 234 | x 12 to 234 |
| The **Permits** pill | x 449 to 550, off screen | x 132 to 234, lit and whole |
| The page's own scroll | 0 | 0 |

- **The kernel** is `scrollLeftToShow` in `src/lib/scrollLeftToShow.ts`. It takes the row's place, its width, its content's width and the pill's box, and answers where the row should sit. It moves the row as little as it can, keeps a 24 px peek of the neighbour where the row has room, and never passes either end.
- **The hook** is `useLitPillInView` in `src/hooks/`. It finds the pill marked `aria-current="true"`, asks the kernel and sets the row's `scrollLeft`. It never calls `scrollIntoView`, which can move the page. It runs when the row first draws, when the lit pill or the set of pills changes, and when the window changes width.
- **The pills** now carry `aria-current` on the lit one, which also tells a screen reader which tab is open.
- **The title keeps its room.** The `h1` stays as it is on a phone. Jobs and People draw the same row with the same title, and the lit pill fits beside it with part of its neighbour showing. Hiding or shrinking the title would not remove the need to scroll: 538 px of pills do not fit in 375 either.
- The browser rounds the row's two widths, which left the last pill 0.7 px short of the end. The hook gives the kernel one more pixel of content, and the browser stops the row at its true end.

## 2. Closed opened on an empty list

`PermitPlaceList` started its Closed chip on **Passed** always. In New Braunfels the step strip read **Closed 5**, all five sat under **Has a plumber**, and the view opened on **Passed 0** with *No closed permits match the filters. Clear the filters*, though no filter was set.

- **It opens on the first chip that holds a permit**, in the order the chips are drawn: Passed, Has a plumber, Not pursuing (`openingClosedWhy` in `permitBuilders.ts`). Passed when nothing is closed yet.
- **While Closed is on screen the chip holds still.** Emptying a chip with **Put back** does not jump to another, so the person sees what their press did. Away from Closed, a chip left empty is let go, and Closed opens again on one that holds permits. A picked chip that still holds permits is kept.
- **An empty chip says what fills it** (`closedEmptyWords`, drawn by `EmptyState`):
  - Passed: *No passed permits yet. Pass on the skim adds the first.* The press opens the skim.
  - Not pursuing: *No permits marked not pursuing yet. Not pursuing on a permit you are investigating adds the first.* The press opens Investigating, through the new `onInvestigating` prop.
  - Has a plumber: *No permits with a plumber yet. The nightly pull adds a permit here once the city shows its plumbing permit.* No press, because nothing a person does hurries the city's feed.
  - A search that finds nothing still says so and offers **Clear the search**.

Seen after the change, New Braunfels at 375 px: **Has a plumber 5** lit, five rows, no empty words.
