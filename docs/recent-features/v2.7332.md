## The one thing "is this the best we can do" changed

A pressed rung on the old rails narrowed the list to that step's rows, and the first drawing of the Board had no press at all on a bin. A picture that cannot ask the list for its rows is a poster. So every bin's head is the press the rung was, and the words under the bins say what the list shows.
