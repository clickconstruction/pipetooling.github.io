## What was wrong

- The Pipeline's search shows a job two ways. The words match what the board holds, or the server lookup finds the job by something the board's own match does not read, an old job number for one.
- The jump to a bill (`applyStagesInvoiceFocus`: the row's Open Invoice chips, the `?stagesInvoice=` link, the handle's `focusInvoice`) builds the board's rows again to find the bill's section. Its body read the lookup's finds (`stagesCombinedExtraJobIds`) while its dependency list named only the schedule-and-clock finds (`stagesSearchExtraJobIds`). When the lookup answered and nothing else changed, the jump kept the rows from before the answer.
- So for a job the lookup alone had found, the row was on screen and the jump said *Clear the Stages search to see this invoice*, and did not move.

## What shipped

- **`JobsStagesTab.tsx`**: the dependency list names `stagesCombinedExtraJobIds`. One line. The lint warning the old list carried is gone.
