# v2.5142 — GC mode: walk the schedule each week, pull work in, get days back, for a dev (2026-10-10)

The schedule's PR 9d in the GC mode real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/schedule-pr9d.md`, on branch `spike/gc-mode`), the last of PR 9's four cuts. No migration and no edge function: every press is 8a's move save (`gc_schedule_move` with the version read), 8b's real days, or the walk's record. Dev only, behind the dev door, like the rest of the schedule.

- **The holds' io (catch 2):** `loadScheduleWithHolds` in `scheduleIo.ts` lays the job's submittals and RFIs over the board before the schedule, as their own windows read them, so their holds show on the chart and stop a pull.
- **The walk (G-52), `GcScheduleWalk.tsx`:** *Update the week* over the chart on a job being built, for those who may move a bar. One bar at a time, the list fixed when it opens: **Yes, keep it**, its real days (**It started today**, **It finished today**), **Take {day}, its pace**, or a new day with why (**Move it**). **Finish the walk** keeps it once as a record (`recordScheduleWalk`), with the ids of the moves it made: `gc_schedule_move` answers the version, so `schedule/savedMove.ts` finds each move in the read after its save by its bar, its new days and its note. A save someone else beat is said under the bar, the schedule is read again, and the bar stays to answer. Two of the prototype's sentences are reworded for the plain-words rule.
- **Pull earlier (G-37), `GcPullEarlier.tsx`:** the green line over the chart, the box in the bar's form and in the walk, and the window with a tick for each bar that comes in, re-planned at the press (`planPull`) and saved as one move (`pullMove`) with its line in the log (`pullLogWords`).
- **Days back (G-82), `GcRecovery.tsx`:** the card under the measures on a job past its date to meet for substantial completion (`lateFinish`), each offer with the days it gives back, who has to agree with **Call**, and **Look at it**; the window saves it as one move (`recoveryMove`, `recoveryLogWords`). The measures' late money, best offer and *Ask for the days* stay off (7b's call 4).
- **Not ready (G-77, G-138), `GcNotReady.tsx`:** first in the bar's form (`GcActivityEditor`'s new `ready` slot), the trade's papers that hold its start, or its insurance run out on work under way, from the kernel's words, each paper's button opening the company's window.
- **The gate (the lead's call 1):** the walk follows `canMove`. Pull earlier and Days back also need `canPull`, which the page sets to `canUseGcBuilding`, since they read Building's dev-only submittals and RFIs.
- **Kernels:** `pullLogWords` and `recoveryLogWords` in `scheduleWindow.ts`, `savedMoveId` in `schedule/savedMove.ts`, and the tests' `schedule/testReports.ts` (a line's report as the prototype's reducer made one).
- **Docs:** the guide *walk the schedule each week*, `GLOSSARY.md` (*Walk · pull · days got back*), `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`'s schedule paragraph.
- **Tests:** `savedMove.test.ts`, `scheduleWindow.test.ts` (the two lines), the render tests of the walk, the pull, days back and not ready (ported from the prototype's onto main's test state), and the window's cases (the holds' read, the walk's line, Pull earlier only with `canPull`, not ready in the form).
