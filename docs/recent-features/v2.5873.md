# v2.5873 — The Collect desk keeps its word after the call (2026-10-26)

Punch list #177, found testing the desk live on 2026-10-26 after PRs A–G shipped ([v2.5848](v2.5848.md) … [v2.5858](v2.5858.md)).

## What was wrong

- **The after-the-call window promised what the sheet did not do.** Its *What the app then does* lines said a no-date call is back on the sheet after `TOUCH_QUIET_DAYS`, a dispute leaves the sheet for the owner with the bills marked disputed, and a second miss puts the owner's name on the next step. `collectMoveFor` read none of it, so a payer called an hour ago, a disputed payer and a payer with a fresh promise all stayed on the copied sheet and the recorded plan.
- **The third tile read the whole pile.** After [v2.5856](v2.5856.md) the card and the dial read what is already late (8.3 weeks, $573k in twelve weeks); `collectWhatIf` still divided the whole billed pile (10.2 weeks, $629k).
- **"Invalid Date".** `collectOutcomePlan` formatted the empty date field before a day was picked.
- **Raw day and run-on chips.** The legend printed a broken promise's day as `2026-09-15`; the payer rows printed the lien chip's words and then its sentence, so *Notice to draft* read twice; the affidavit decision glued a lowercase chip sentence to the next one.
- **The GC-record door** opened `/jobs?tab=stages`, not the job.

## The rules

`collectPayers.ts` counts `misses` per payer: could-not-reach touches since its oldest open bill, plus a promise now broken. `collectMoves.ts` gains three waiting kinds, checked before the call rules: `owner_dispute` (the newest unresolved touch is a dispute), `quiet` (the newest unresolved touch is `cant_reach`, `note` or `resend`, younger than `TOUCH_QUIET_DAYS`; title *Back on the sheet \<day\>*), and `promised_wait` (a live promise with nothing else late or doubtful). Waiting rows keep their row and paper with `onSheet: false`; `buildCollectSheet` counts `moves` and `waiting` and leaves them out of the ask, the calls, `collectSheetAsCallMoves` and `collectSheetText`. At two misses a call move is `escalated` with owner *the owner*. `collectStandsWords` reads *Disputed \<day\>… Their words*, *Called \<day\>… Back on the sheet \<day\>* and *N misses on the account*, and the chip line is `chipSentence(chip.sentence)` only, its words riding on the move's paper link. `collectWhatIf` takes `pileUsd`, the card's pile (late, past a promise, doubtful), so its weeks start from the card's and its twelve-week number is the dial's. `collectDecisions` takes `jobsHref(jobIds)`; one job opens `/jobs?tab=stages&edit=<id>`. The outcome texts name the waits and the Only you can decide strip; the help guide's *Collect, broken down* says the same.
