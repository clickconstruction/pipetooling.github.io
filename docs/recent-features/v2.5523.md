# v2.5523 — Messages: on the phone, it is under More (2026-10-14)

A finding from the phone walk after the residential build, by Customer communication helper 10, at cc's ask, verified on prod at v2.5507 as the Sample assistant at 390 px. On a phone, an assistant or a controller gets the dock (Jobs, Schedule, PO, Inbox, More), not the menu, and the More sheet's pages had no Messages. So the office reached Messages on a phone only through the Dashboard's Customers line or a link. The owner and an estimator get the menu, which has it.

- **`src/lib/phoneDock.ts`**: a Messages page (`/messages`) joins `PHONE_DOCK_PAGES` after Customers, for `canOpenMessages`, the same office team as the header's Messages door. Like any page there, it can go on the dock.
- **`PhoneDockGlyph.tsx`**: a speech bubble (`chat`), so the tile does not borrow Inbox's tray.
- **No badge**: the sheet's tiles carry minutes on the page, not counts, so Messages shows its minutes like the rest. The header's count stays where it is.
- **Guide** `answer-everyone-waiting-on-us-from-one-page`: one sentence for the phone's More.
- **Tests**:
  - `phoneDock.test.ts`: the office roles see Messages, the field never does, and it opens `/messages` and can take an assistant's dock slot.
  - `PhoneDockMoreSheet.render.test.tsx`: an assistant's sheet lists Messages, and a primary's does not.
