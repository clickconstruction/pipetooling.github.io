# v2.5207 — GC mode: what a customer owes us, in their window (2026-10-10)

The Board's B2b-v-iii (`to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/board-b2b.md` on branch `spike/gc-mode`), the last of B2b-v's three cuts, with gc 5's read for Owner Billing: V3 the money gate, V4 the rows read only, V5 the contract's file, and the catch on the customer's copies.

- **The kernel** (`customerMoney.ts`, new):
  - `customerMoneyView(board, rows, customer)` takes each job's board customer before `billingStateForAll` lays the customer's buyout and building jobs (`customerBilledJobIds`).
  - So each laid job keeps its own copy of the customer (`customer@project`), with its retainage and the customer's usual days to pay, as Bill the customer reads it. The copies are never mapped back (gc 5).
  - The summary is `customerSummary` over each record the jobs read, with the money summed and their past jobs counted once. `owedSplit` sums `ownerAccount` and `interest` sums `ownerInterest`.
  - `jobMoneyDocs` is the design spike's rows word for word, read only: the pay applications sent, each late bill, interest bills, change orders waiting on them, and all change orders.
  - `customerMoneyDocuments` puts our contract's row first, read from the board, since its sends are kept by the customer's own id, then each job's money.
  - `customerMoneyActivity` adds each laid job's money lines through its copy, in place of any the board held.
- **The window** (`GcCustomerWindow.tsx`):
  - About opens with totals for everyone: live projects, priced and waiting on them, under contract, and they picked us. Outside the money team these are the trades alone.
  - The money team adds **They owe us now** (certified, and waiting on the architect), **They are holding**, and the interest on late bills.
  - Documents' rows offer **Open the bill** (Bill the customer, where a late bill is reminded) and **Open the change orders**. There is no reminder press here.
  - **Open the contract** signs a two-minute link on the press, for whoever the bucket's select lets read it. `canOpenOwnerContractFile` is a dev today, and `access.test.ts` pins it to `gc_owner_contracts_select`. A failure is said in words.
- **The page** (`GcProjects.tsx`): for the money team only (`canSeeGcMoney`), the window reads its billed jobs' billing when it opens. It lays that on the board with the change orders the page already reads. Anyone else's window makes no money call.
- **Not the spike's:** no paper pane beside the list. main's Documents shows a send there, so the rows carry their facts and open the real windows.
