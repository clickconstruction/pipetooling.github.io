# v2.1917 — Stages header stats bounded to the active cohort (2026-08-20)

Part 1 of the OOM-crash-loop remediation (prod crashed 16× Aug 19–20; onset tracked the scoped-load train deploy, crashes tracked office hours). The v2.1821 lean stats fetch selected **all** of `jobs_ledger`, `jobs_ledger_invoices`, and `jobs_ledger_payments` per board visit — per-row RLS evaluation over the whole tables, refired after every load/mutation/visibility return, stacking across office users.

- [`fetchStagesHeaderStats`](../../src/lib/jobs/fetchStagesHeaderStats.ts) now issues four bounded queries: active-status-or-NULL jobs (`paid` arrives as a bare head-count), `ready_to_bill`/`billed` invoices only, and payments that are invoice-linked or inside the trailing collected-by-week window. `paid.count` and `collectedByWeek` are computed off the raw rows (new kernel helper `collectedByWeekFromPayments`) so paid jobs' rows never ship. Stats cost now scales with work-in-flight, not company age (~380 rows vs ~1,600 today; the gap widens as history grows).
- [`JobsListCacheContext`](../../src/contexts/JobsListCacheContext.tsx) gives `refreshHeaderStats` a 60s freshness TTL keyed by user+customer filter; post-mutation refreshes (`scheduleLoadJobsAfterMutation` in Jobs.tsx) pass `force: true` and bypass it.
- **Documented delta**: a `billed`-status invoice stranded on a `paid` job no longer surfaces in the chips (orphan invoices are dropped in assembly). That state is a billing bug when it exists — verified empty in prod at cutover. Pinned by test.
- **Growth triggers recorded**: add an index on `jobs_ledger(status)` once the table passes ~5k rows; if per-row RLS cost dominates later, consolidate `jobs_ledger`'s four ORed SELECT policies behind a capability function. The full-SQL stats RPC alternative was mocked up and rejected (dual-maintained bundling rules, float/numeric cent-equality risk, no SQL test harness) — see the 2026-08-20 investigation plan.

Client-only — no migration. Parts 2 and 3 (staggered dispatcher crons, set-based `list_job_followup_activity`) ship separately.
