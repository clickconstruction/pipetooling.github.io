# v2.7322 — Lien desk: the header takes less room (2026-11-15)

The owner's screenshot of 2026-11-15, By paper on a computer, with three bands boxed: the space between the app's nav and the title row, the space between the title row and the pile row, and the space between the pile row and the columns all took too much. "Compress the header, keep it stylish." Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## Before

The bar (`.lienDeskTitleBar`) had 11.2 px of padding over the title line and 9.6 under the pile line, under the page's own 8 px, and a 7.2 px row gap. The break between its two lines was a 0-height span on a flex line of its own, so the row gap was taken on each side of it: 14.4 px between the lines before the items' own centering, 20.6 from the views to the paper tabs. The way back sat 19.2 px under the nav; the Jobs page's tabs sit 8.

## What changed

- **`LienDesk.tsx`**: on a computer the bar's row gap is 0 and its padding `1px 1.25rem 3px`; the break spans keep their 0 height. The pile span (`data-lien-desk-piles`) keeps a 6 px row gap of its own for its wrapped chips, with the bar's column gap, since the bar's row gap is 0 now.
- **[`src/index.css`](../../src/index.css)**: on a computer (`min-width: 641px`, the phone rule's other side) every direct child of the bar carries `margin-block: 3px` and the two break spans none, so lines sit 6 px apart whether a break put them there or a line wrapped by itself (the run under the title at the 768 px tablet) — the first cut set the row gap to 0 and let the breaks be 6 px tall, and the tablet's wrapped run then touched the title line.
- **[`src/lib/jobs/lienDeskTitleBar.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDeskTitleBar.ts)**: `LIEN_DESK_TITLE_BAR_ROW_GAP_PX` (6) with the rule written out. `chrome` (88) is unchanged: the page's 24 px margins and the bar's 20 px side padding are what they were, and the readers count the sides alone. The test's built bars carry the new padding, with a line saying why the read width is the same.
- **`LienDesk.render.test.tsx`**: the 808 px By paper render checks the bar's row gap, its padding and the breaks' 0 height; the phone render checks its own row gap, padding and no break on To do.
- Release note `v2.7322.ts`.

## Not changed

- The band between the bar and the columns: the bar's own bottom went from 9.6 to 6 px over the border, but the 13 px from the border to the find box's input is the find box's — v2.7315 (PR #8173, in flight) redraws it as a 47 px line with no margin, so the input sits about 12 px under the border there and the first pile head about 12 under the input; a tighter find line would be that box's own change, not the bar's.
- Every control, its order and its fold rules; the measured widths; the leader's one-line bar. A phone's header, its padding and its 0.4rem row gap are untouched: its breaks come only with the paper tabs, which are full-width there anyway, and the phone's rows are 44 px presses that want their room.
