## Why

Counsel's memo: *"Build the grid. One row per job. Move every job onto that grid today."* The desk's Timeline tab prints it per GC (v2.3768) and the office emails the printout — stale the next morning. The mock-up's owner call, taken as drawn: the portal's grid covers the desk's whole book (every billed job with money open and a lien month), not only the referred matters' jobs — the grid holds dates and dollars, nothing a customer said, and it is the same rows the office prints and emails today.

## What shipped

- **`src/lib/jobs/lienTimelineBookAssemble.ts`** (new, tests) — `assembleLienBookInput(raw, todayYmd)`: the book's raw rows (the two RPCs' rows, the desk items, the affidavit and release filings, the jobs, GCs, property records and owner overrides) → `LienTimelineBookInput`. Pulled out of `useLienTimelineBook`, which now reads through it; `parseLienBookRaw` reads the function's payload; `LIEN_BOOK_JOB_COLUMNS` is the one jobs select and carries `lien_payment_bond` and `lien_contract_ended_on` directly (v2.3786 had the hook fetch them separately through `fetchLienClockColumns`; the regenerated `database.ts` makes the one select enough).
- **Migration** — `list_lien_notice_months` and `list_lien_affidavit_windows` add `OR auth.role() = 'service_role'` to their office gate and grant execute to `service_role`; bodies unchanged (v2.3747's).
- **`legal-portal`** — `readLienBook(admin)`: the two RPCs at 400 days, then the rows the hook reads, returned raw as `lienBook` on both the empty-matters and the full response; null on any failure (the portal still opens, the panel hides).
- **`legalPortalPayload.ts`** — `LegalPortalPayload.lienBook: LienBookRaw | null`.
- **`LegalPortalLienGrid`** (new) — the third panel: GC and *Something due / All* selects (`filterLienTimelineBook`), the table from `LIEN_GRID_COLUMNS` / `lienGridRows` on the portal's paper, a red `?` where the app holds no fact, *Print the grid* through `openHtmlPrintWindow(lienGridHtml(...))` — the desk's print page. `LegalPortal.tsx` shows the panel only when the payload carries a book (the sample portal and an older function do not).
