# v2.7109 — The Bridge: What to do now names each customer, job and supply house, as tables you can act on (punch list #334, PR 8) (2026-11-12)

PR 8 of the Bridge train (card #334, `to-dos/bridge-pulse/`), on PR 7 (v2.7105). Client only, with one kernel that is copied to the edge functions changed (`dashboardFinancialsLoad.ts` reads one more column), so the functions that carry it are owed a redeploy after the merge. No migration.

The owner, of the card: "I can't easily dive in and it's not very actionable". Asked whether each named thing should be its own press: "Yes, press each one". He asked to "mark when we are getting paid and see when we were promised to get paid by these different customers and their different projects", with the app's existing tools. Of *Open the jobs*: it "just opens the jobs page ... should we just remove the button?" And of the look: "Visually, I don't like how this looks, but I like how it functions".

## What changed

- **A kernel for what is behind a move** (`src/lib/bridge/callDive.ts`, new, pure): `moveDive` hands each move its things, each with its own target, its facts as cells and the move's column headings. *Chase the late invoices* names the customers to call (late, oldest bill, pay date) with their open bills as projects. *Bill the finished jobs*, *Set a percent* and *Price the jobs* name the jobs. *Ask the supply houses for more days* names the houses with bills due that day. Nothing is counted here: every list is what the page already read.
- **The table** (`src/components/bridge/BridgeMoveTable.tsx`, new): one grid a move, with small headings, amounts right-aligned, the projects press in its own column and row actions as blue words. A column no line fills takes no room. Three lines, then **Show all**. On a phone, 768 px and under, a line is its name and figure, then the rest with its press.
- **The card** (`BridgeWeek.tsx`): a move is a heading line (number, title, why, and its one press on the right, such as **See who to call ›**), then its table. The card is framed by a rule down its left side in place of the box. A move the page names nothing for keeps its door on its title.
- **The presses** (`Bridge.tsx`): a customer's name opens the Collect window on that customer, first on the list with where it stands open (`focusPayerKey` on `BridgeCollectDesk`). **Set a pay date** opens the app's `SetPromisedPayDateModal`, and the page reads again after a save. **Set the percent** opens the app's `FieldPctUpdateModal`, and a job set on this visit stays listed and says so. A supply house opens `/materials?tab=supply-houses&house=<id>&due=<ymd>`. **See the bills ›** lands on the bills coming due behind Money (`bridge-bills-due`).
- **A bill knows its supply house** (`dashboardFinancialsLoad.ts`): the read takes `supply_house_id`, and `DashboardApBill` gains `houseId`.
