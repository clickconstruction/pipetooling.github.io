# v2.7844 — Painted maps: Send more… beside Text this place… (2026-11-18)

No card. The owner, 2026-11-18, asked whether a texted place should stay a plain point in a map app (v2.7799) or open the painted map: *Keep the plain pin, but now that you've said that maybe we should prompt the user to offer them the option to share more, can you think through this addition, it's value, what it could look like?* He was shown a sketch of two screens and three ways, with an offer recommended over a prompt, and said: *no need for a card build it all here and let me know when it is live?*

## An offer, not a prompt

**Text this place…** stays one press. A question before every send would cost a press on the path he asked to be immediate (*hit share and then immediately take that link into text*), and a nudge after a send cannot know that anything was sent. So the offer is a second, quiet press beside the first: **Send more…**. It opens a sheet of up to three ways, each a small picture, a name and one short line.

## The three ways

- **This place with its photo.** A text link carries a spot and no picture. The phone's own share sheet carries both, so this way hands it the photo as a file and the place's words (`navigator.share` with `files` and `text`). Whoever gets it signs in to nothing. It is drawn only for a place that has a photo, on a device whose share sheet takes a file (`canShareAPlacePhoto`, over Materials on site's `canSharePhoto`).
- **All N places on this job**, or **on this map**. Every place the row stands with, each as the words and plain map link one place sends, a blank line between, in one text (`placesShareWords`). On a phone it is a plain `sms:` link, as **Text this place…** is. At a desk it copies. It is drawn from two places up. Two places read **Both places on this job**.
- **The whole map**, or **The whole list** on a job's own list. The door to the map's own Share sheet (#394). It is drawn for a place that is the map's own, to whoever may share that map: its maker and the office, the rule the **Share** press already had (`mayShareMap`, now named once in `PaintedMapWindow`).

A sheet with no way to offer is not offered. A map's one place with no photo, seen by someone who may not share the map, has **Text this place…** alone.

## Which places a row stands with

- A row of a map's own places stands with that map's places.
- A row on a job's own list stands with that list's places, named as the job's.
- A row under **On this job**, a place of the job's drawn on another map, stands with the job's places. It never offers that map: a map's link does not carry the job's places (v2.7795), so the offer would send a map the place is not on.

## The photo is read when the sheet opens

A phone opens its share sheet only inside the press that asked for it. So the photo is signed and read into a file when the sheet opens (`loadPlacePhotoFile`). Until it is in hand its press is dead and reads *Getting the photo…*. The press then calls the share sheet before anything is awaited (`sharePlacePhoto`). A photo that will not load leaves the press dead and says so. A phone that refuses its share sheet is told to text the place by itself. Backing out of the share sheet says nothing and leaves the sheet open.

## A place with a photo says the photo stays behind

Under **Text this place…**: *A text carries the spot, not the photo.* Under **Copy this place**: *A copy carries the spot, not the photo.* It is drawn for every place with a photo, whether or not this device can carry it, since it is true either way.

## Files

- `src/lib/paintedMap/paintedPlaceSendMore.ts`: which ways a sheet offers, their words, the words of several places. Pure.
- `src/lib/paintedMap/paintedPlaceSendMoreIo.ts`: the photo as a file, the share sheet, the copy.
- `src/components/paintedMap/PaintedPlaceSendMore.tsx`: the sheet, on the Share sheet's own layer and shell.
- `src/components/paintedMap/PaintedPlaceShare.tsx`: the second press and the photo line.
- `src/components/paintedMap/PaintedMapWindow.tsx`: each row is handed its set, its photo and the door to Share.

## Not changed

- What one texted place opens: a plain point in a map app, on his word.
- Who may share a map. Nobody gains the Share sheet through this press.
- The Share sheet, `painted-map-link` and the `/way/<code>` page. Nothing is deployed for this step and there is no migration.

## Checked

- `paintedPlaceSendMore.test.ts`, 12 cases: the ways by photo, device, count and who may share, their order, the names and lines, the words of several places with a place off the globe left out, and the photo's file name.
- `PaintedPlaceShare.render.test.tsx`, 17 cases: the one press unchanged, the second press drawn only with something to offer, every place as a link on a phone and a copy at a desk, the photo's press dead until the file is in hand, the share sheet asked inside the press with the file and the words, backing out, a refusal, a photo that will not load, the door to Share, and the sheet closing without the press reaching the window behind.
- `PaintedMapWindow.render.test.tsx`, 6 new cases and one changed: the wiring of each kind of row, and that a row under **On this job** never offers the map, even to the map's maker.
- Each rule was broken on purpose with a passing control at both ends: the map offered to everyone, a job's place offering the map it is drawn on, the share sheet asked for after a wait, and every place offered from one place. Each break failed a named test. The second one passed at first, and the case for the map's maker was added to catch it.

## Walked

On 2026-11-18, before the merge, on a local server against the live database, as the dev, on a ZZ Test job. The rows were made up and thrown away after, and the tables were read after and held none of them.

- **At a phone's width**, on the job's list: a place with a photo read *A text carries the spot, not the photo.* under its press. Its sheet drew the photo's way dead with *Getting the photo…*, then live once the photo was read from the bucket. The press handed over the photo as a JPEG named for the place and the place's words with its map link, inside the press, and the sheet closed. **The whole list** closed the sheet and opened the Share sheet.
- With a second place on the list, its sheet drew **Both places on this job**, a link whose text held both places and both map links.
- **On another map of the same job**: the map's own place offered **The whole map** alone. A place of the job's with a photo offered its photo and **Both places on this job**, and no map. A place of the job's without one offered **Both places on this job** alone.
- **At a desk's width**: the presses read **Copy this place** and *A copy carries the spot, not the photo.* The sheet offered no photo. **Both places on this job** copied both places and said *Copied. Paste it into a text or an email.*
- The sheet in dark.

## Not walked

- **A real phone.** The browser the walk ran in has no share sheet, so a stand-in took the photo and the words in its place. That a phone opens its share sheet with the photo, and that Messages then carries the photo and the words together, is unseen. Some apps a share sheet hands to keep the photo and drop the words.
- **A photo that will not load and a phone that refuses** are held by tests only.

Guide `paint-a-map` and the Glossary's *Painted map* entry amended.
