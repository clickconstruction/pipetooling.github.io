# v2.8279 — The Bridge on a phone: Money owed and Crew hours fit the screen (2026-11-21)

One of the faults punch list #334 wrote down under *Found while building, not built* and never built. Client only: no migration and no function. No number changes.

## What was seen

The card's words: at 375 px wide the page grows to 404 px once the accrual balance's press opens Money owed behind the Money door. It was confirmed on `main` before the fix, on the page at `localhost` on live data: `window.innerWidth` read 375 with the panel closed and 404 with it open.

The child that was too wide is the panel itself (`BridgeOwedPanel.tsx`), in three places:

- **The panel's own grid** was `minmax(0, 2fr) minmax(260px, 1fr)`. On a phone the summary column kept its 260 px and the charts were left 41 px, so the two bar charts were drawn under the summary's words.
- **The foot's grid** was two columns, so the sources table had a column 164 px wide.
- **The sources table** was `90px minmax(0, 1.1fr) minmax(0, 1fr) auto`. Its name column and its door column ask for 219 px between them. The two middle columns went to 0 px wide, and the doors hung 55 px past the panel's edge. Those doors are what made the page 404 px wide.

Crew hours behind the People door (`BridgeHoursPanel.tsx`) is the same frame, copied. It had the same fault, to 395 px. The card did not list it. It is fixed here with the same change, since it is the same three grids.

## What changed

- **`chartPanelFrame(phone)`** (`src/lib/bridge/chartPanelFrame.ts`, new): how each grid of a chart panel is cut. At a desk it is the frame there was, to the character. On a phone every grid is one column, and no track keeps a fixed width.
- **`BridgePanelSources`** (`src/components/bridge/BridgePanelSources.tsx`, new): the sources table, which the two panels each drew for themselves. At a desk it is the same four columns. On a phone a source is three lines: its name with its door beside it, where it is read from, what can be wrong. The heading row is left off there, since the lines no longer sit in columns.
- **The two panels** read `useIsMobile` (640 px and under) and draw the frame it names.
- **The two bar charts in Money owed** wrap by the app's usual rule, `minmax(min(260px, 100%), 1fr)`, so a phone narrower than 300 px does not push them wide either.

The page's overflow is not hidden anywhere. The panel is narrower because its children are.

## What does not change

- At a desk and on a tablet the panels draw as before. The desk frame is held by `chartPanelFrame.test.ts` as three literal strings.
- No figure, no word and no door of either panel changes. `owedPanel.ts` and `hoursPanel.ts` are not touched.

## Tests

- `chartPanelFrame.test.ts` (new, 3): the desk frame is the old one, the phone frame is one column, and the phone frame asks for no fixed width where the desk frame asks for 260 px and 90 px.
- `BridgePanels.render.test.tsx` (3 more): Money owed at a desk keeps its grids and its table's order, door last. On a phone it is one column, the table is stacked with the door beside the name, and every source and door is still there. Crew hours on a phone is cut the same way.

A render test cannot measure a width. The widths below are from the page.
