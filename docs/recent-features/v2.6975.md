# v2.6975 — The Bid Board shows what we win, by the size of the bid (punch list #318, PR 2) (2026-11-11)

PR 2 of six for *Market and Sales on the won rates* (`to-dos/market-sales-won-rates/README.md`). The owner, on the spike's chart, 2026-11-11: "I really like your chart of what bids we are winning based on size. I would like to add that to the bid board somehow." Asked whether the board's Health section is the place: "Yes, keep it there". Client only. No migration, no edge function. Stacked on PR 1 (v2.6973), which holds the counting.

## What changed

- **The chart** (`src/components/bids/BidBoardWonBySizeSection.tsx`): *What we win, by the size of the bid*. One row for each size (`BID_SIZES`: under $10k, $10k to $50k, $50k to $500k, over $500k): a bar for the share won by dollars, the percent, and one line of counts: won, lost, and with no mark, each with its dollars. A foot line says how many sent bids have no mark and how many of those are past 180 days, since marking them is what makes the rates true.
- **The counting is the Bridge's** (`buildBidWins`, v2.6973): a bid is won or lost only when it is marked, *started or complete* counts as won, an unmarked bid is half lost at 90 days and 95% at 180, and the rate is by dollars. A bid never sent and a ZZ test bid are left out.
- **Where it sits** (`BidBoardEstimatingHealthSection.tsx`): in the board's Health section, under the Pulse and above the owner's labor table. It reads `filteredBids`, the bids the board is showing, so it follows the trade pill and the board's filters.
- **A send day is read as the date it is.** `bids.bid_date_sent` is a date. The spike read it as an instant, which lands a day early in company time, so a bid would have aged into a loss a day too soon. The chart reads its first ten characters, as the Bridge's loader does.
- **The empty state** is `EmptyState`: *No sent bid on this board is marked won or lost yet. Mark a bid won or lost and its size fills in here.*

## Not in this PR

- The note on each bid's row, *bids this size: N% won*, was offered to the owner and not asked for.
- The Pulse's week buckets and the weekly sent table read the same date through `calendarYmdInAppTzFromIso`, as they always have. Whether a bid sent on a Sunday lands in the week before is a question for its own change.
