# v2.4796 — Map: the same map as the Bid Board and the Pipeline (2026-10-07)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. PR 2 of the Map page refresh ([`to-dos/map-page-refresh/`](../../to-dos/map-page-refresh/README.md)), after [v2.4791](v2.4791.md). The owner, 2026-10-07: *start PR 2*. Cut from PR 1's branch and rebased once it merged.

## What changed

- **The page draws on [`PinsMapCanvas`](../../src/components/map/PinsMapCanvas.tsx)**, the Leaflet canvas the Dashboard, Bid Board, Pipeline and clocked-in maps share, in place of its own `MapContainer`. The canvas gains a **`children` slot** (Leaflet only; the Google canvas ignores it) so the page mounts what is its own inside the map: the one-shot fly-to, the Geoman draw tool and the court areas layer. The anchor, the rings, the home fit and Fit all now come from the canvas (`fitPoints` is the home fit until Fit all, then every near pin and the office, the Bid Board's rule); the page's own `MapFit`, the default-view remount and the `map_default_view_v1` read are gone — the office anchor already falls back to that setting (`resolveOfficeAnchor`).
- **[`mapPagePins.ts`](../../src/lib/map/mapPagePins.ts)** (pure): the page's entities with coordinates → `MapCanvasPin`s, id `${kind}-${id}` so a job and a bid can share a record id, colored by kind (the status colors are PR 2b) except in builder focus, where a bid takes its outcome color for that GC as before; `mapPagePinTitle`, `mapPageEntitiesByPinId`, `mapPageDirectionsUrl` (the cards' Google Maps link).
- **The pin card.** A clicked pin's popup (desktop) is `MapPinBody`: the record and its number, *Job · paid* / *Bid · Won* / *Estimate · sent $1,240*, the address, **Open** (the job window for a job, the record's page otherwise) and **Directions**. On a phone the canvas draws no popup and the same body is the bar under the map (`data-map-phone-bar`) with two 44 px buttons. Opening a record from the table, the geocode list or the far list selects its pin too.
- **The scroll gate**: `scrollZoomAfterClick`, so the wheel scrolls the page past the map until the map is clicked once (desktop; phones keep their gestures).

## Is this the best we can do?

- **One canvas, five maps.** Every lesson the cards learned (the gate, the phone bar, clusters, the pulse) is a prop away for this page now, and a fix lands on all of them.
- **The page keeps what is only its own** through the children slot rather than by growing the canvas: drawing and the court layer stay Leaflet-only and page-only.
- **Not done:** status colors, the chips as switches with counts, Paid / Lost / Estimates off by default, the Cluster toggle (PR 2b, next); places and the rail (PR 3).
