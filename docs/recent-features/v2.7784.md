# v2.7784 — Lien desk: the view tabs read Date, Paper and Job on a computer (2026-11-18)

The owner's ask of 2026-11-18, with a screenshot of the title bar's four tabs: *"I think we can shorten this header line a lot by removing the repeating By's and tightening up the buttons, would you be able to help me do so"*. Three shapes were drawn on the live desk and measured: A, *Date*, *Paper*, *Job* with tighter padding (305 px); B, the same with one small *by* before the three (326 px); C, the old words with tighter padding alone (349 px). His pick: *"Build A"*.

## Before

- On a computer the tab row read *⏱ Lien desk · 35 to do*, *By date*, *By paper*, *By job*, 373 px with a two-figure count and 383 with three figures.
- Every tab had 10 px of side padding in a window of 1,132 px or wider.

## What changed

- **The three tabs after the title read *Date*, *Paper* and *Job* on a computer.** `lienDeskViewTab(view, { isMobile })` in `lienDeskTitleBar.ts` answers a tab's `words`, its `name` and its `hint`. `LienDeskViewTabs` draws the words.
- **The views keep their names.** `LIEN_DESK_VIEW_NAMES` holds *By date*, *By paper* and *By job*. Where the words differ from the name the tab carries the name as its `aria-label`, and the name holds the visible word, so a spoken *Paper* still finds the tab. The hover hint starts with the name. The guides, the ? card, the empty states and the doors named *By date ›* say the names as before.
- **A tab's side padding is 8 px where it was 10.** `.lienDeskKindTab` in `index.css`, which the view tabs and By paper's three share. A window from 641 to 1,131 px keeps its 7.
- **A phone is untouched.** Its four tabs fill the row either way and it has no hover to say a name, so its tabs read the names whole.
- **The first tab is untouched.** It is the page's title with its count, the owner's pick of v2.7465.
