# v2.3979 — Draft Payroll: Generate Remaining skips a week that comes to $0 (2026-09-27)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. The owner's call of 2026-09-27 ("same rule on both"), asked in `to-dos/owner-decisions-pending.md` by punch list **#46** row 6 — it clears the way for the People map's step 7 (`useDraftPayroll`).

## What was wrong

**People → Payroll → Draft Payroll → Generate Remaining** picked everyone with no report for the period and an estimated gross above zero, and estimated with the Hours grid's rule: a salaried person is a flat 8 hours every weekday. The window's own count beside the button, the preview and the report all price with the payroll rule, which takes unpaid time off and the employment window out first (`salariedPayrollDays.ts`) and splits office from field rates.

So the two could disagree. With anyone else still missing, a salaried person out unpaid all week — or not started yet — was on the button's list and got a $0 report, while the count beside the button left them out.

## What shipped

- **`src/lib/pay/missingPayReports.ts`** — `peopleMissingPayReports({ people, payStubs, start, end, days, costForPersonDate })` and `payStubOverlapsPeriod`. Pure; 8 tests.
- **`src/pages/People.tsx`** — `bulkGenerateMissingPayStubsInModal` builds its candidates with it, priced by `getPayrollCostForPersonDate`. The confirm says who is skipped.
- **`src/components/pay/DraftPayrollModal.tsx`** — `bulkMissingCount` is the same function's length (it already priced with the payroll rule). The line reads *N with pay due and no report yet*.
- **Guide** — `run-the-pay-week` says who the button picks.
- The People map's Bulk + delete row and step 7, the queue card's row 6 and the pending-decisions list record the answer.

## Not changed

Generating one person at a time still makes whatever report that person's week comes to, $0 included. The Hours grid, the cost views and the Teams section keep the flat 8-hour rule.
