# v2.6272 — The job window's Documents tab has a Photos line (punch list #232, PR 3) (2026-11-01)

Punch list #232's third and last PR, on the owner's go of 2026-11-01: the ask named the Documents tab, and the job window has one. The card's call 1: a one-line door on the job window's Documents tab to the strip the window already has, never a second list of one folder.

## What changed

- **A Photos section** on the job window's Documents tab (`components/jobs/JobDocumentsPhotos.tsx` over `lib/photos/jobPhotosLine.ts`, tested), after the Job folders section: *15 in Drive · 1 on its way · the newest Nov 1*, read through `job-photos`' `list` as the strip under the address reads it, and the line a press that opens the strip's own `JobPhotosViewer`. An empty folder says what fills it; a job with no folder link says so and points at **Make the job's Drive folders** above it or the Edit tab; a read that fails says why with **Try again**. Under the line: *Every photo across every job is on Documents → Photos.*
- The guide *see a job's photos* names the Documents tab's line; `docs/PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`'s job window Documents tab line names the section.

## Decisions

- **A line, not a tab or a second strip.** The strip under the address is the job's photo list; the Documents tab's job is to say the count and open the same viewer, so a person looking for a job's papers finds its photos without leaving the tab.
- **One more read when the tab opens**, the same list the strip made: cheaper than threading the strip's state across the window's tabs, and the function answers from Drive in under a second.
