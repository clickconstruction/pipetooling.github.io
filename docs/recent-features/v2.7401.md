# v2.7401 — Sub papers: the kernels for the three papers (sub labor train) (2026-11-15)

Client only, nothing drawn yet. Punch list #350, cut from the design spike Taunya walked on 2026-11-15, when she said to ship the train. Kernels with their tests, and one read the papers need.

## What landed

- `supabase/functions/_shared/subPapers.ts`, import-free so an edge function can bundle it: the three papers as plain data.
  - `buildWorkStatement(sheet, ctx)`: one sheet's lines, payments and back-charges, what it comes to, what was paid and what is still open.
  - `paperSendings(sheets)` and `buildPaymentRecord(sending, sheets, ctx, received)`: one payment and the sheets it was placed on. Rows that share a sending are one record; a row with no sending is its own.
  - `buildStatementOfAccount(sheets, range, ctx, { number })`: the opening figure, new work, back-charges and payments in the period, the closing figure, a credit held, the last payments, and *Paid this year* with client-direct payments left out.
  - `subPapersAddUp({ statement, work, payments })`: the cross-check. A paper is not drawn when the set does not add up.
  - `SUB_PAPER_COUNSEL_SENTENCES`: the six sentences that wait on counsel, as placeholders. `SUB_PAPER_COUNSEL_PENDING` is true, and while it is every paper prints them in a box marked as draft wording. `SUB_PAPER_LABELS`: no label says invoice, receipt or owed, and a test holds that.
- `supabase/functions/_shared/subPapersPdf.ts`: the three papers drawn with pdf-lib through `PdfLibLike`, so the browser and a function draw the same bytes.
- `src/lib/subWorkOrders/subPapersInputs.ts`: `paperSheetFrom` and `paperSheetsFor` turn the page's sheets into the kernel's shape, one person's at a time by `sheetOwedTo`. A memo the office hid from the sub is blank.
- `src/lib/subWorkOrders/subPapersHtml.ts`: the same three papers as a print page, with a narrow layout.
- `src/lib/subWorkOrders/subPaperSend.ts`: the plan for sending a paper by email, with its subject, its words, its files and its confirm label.
- `useSubLaborLedger.ts` and `laborJob.ts`: a payment now carries `hidden_from_sub`, read in all four of the hook's selects, so a paper can leave a hidden memo off.

## Tests

`subPapers.test.ts` (18): a part payment on all three papers, the cross-check against `owedView` and against the Ledger's `buildSubStatement` for a period's opening and closing, the cases the card lists, and the names. `subPapersPdf.test.ts` (5): each PDF draws; `SUB_PAPERS_OUT=<dir>` keeps the files.

## Not in this change

No window, no press, no number on a paper and nothing sent. The register that numbers a paper is a migration in a later change.
