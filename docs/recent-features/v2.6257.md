- **The guide** (`src/content/help/tag-an-email-onto-a-job-from-gmail.md`, Office, `where: /messages`, order 120, the office team): forward from the address you sign in with (`readForwarded` takes a forward only from one of ours), to the inbox that person writes to, team@ or bids@; about a minute later the email is on the writer's thread at the time they sent it, with its files up to 25 MB and the note above it as a team note; an unknown writer waits in New as **Who is this?**. Then which job it lands on, how to move it, an email sent from your own Gmail (copy a box, or forward it from Sent), and what it does not do (a personal address, a mail between teammates, a second forward is a second copy).
- **Read against the code, two corrections to the card's words.** (1) A forward is tied by the forwarded mail's own subject, the block's `Subject:` line (`emailLine.ts`: `fwd.subject ?? unforwarded(email.subject)`, and `jobFor` reads `line.subject`), so a job number typed into the forward's own subject is not read; the guide says so, sends the reader to the thread to tie it, and teaches the number in the subject of mail you start, which the replies keep. (2) **Tie to** is the reply box's job for the reply; a line moves with **no job** (desk: *Tie this line to a job*, phone: *Tie this message to*), **change** beside **Tied to** in the opened message, or a phone's press and hold, all through `tie_message`. The guide links *find every message with a person in one place* → *Tie a message to a job* for the rest.
- **Which inbox.** A forward of someone's mail is a line in from them on that box, and `lastWroteTo` sends the thread's next email and text from the box they last wrote to, so the guide asks for the inbox that person writes to rather than bids@ for everyone.
- **The send window's line** (`SendWindow.tsx`, the words `compose.ts`'s `ownMailboxNote` and `OWN_MAILBOX_GUIDE_HREF`): under the email's head, where the From line is, and outside it, since the head is what they see: *Answering from your own mailbox? Copy team@clickplumbing.com, and this thread gets it too.* and **How to tag an email onto a job**, which opens the guide (`/help?g=tag-an-email-onto-a-job-from-gmail`). The box is the window's own Replies to, else bids@, which the reader always reads.
