# v2.4632 — Lien desk: the Notice or Affidavit chip opens the paper, with what is missing marked (2026-10-06)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. The owner (2026-10-05): *just like how clicking on the address opens the job detail, I would like to be able to click on the affidavit or notice button and when hovering over it see that it is selected and when clicked show a mockup of the affidavit or notice with a big red notices of the details missing.* Mock-up: artifact RpaTpoxkTRwNqSQcBnXvK7 — a first pass with big red boxes, its critique (red boxes shout; the notice's gap is not on the form; it said what was missing, not what to do; one paper at a time), and the refined shape built here.

## What changed

- **Kernel** `src/lib/jobs/lienPaperGaps.ts`: `lienPaperGaps(kind, facts)` lists the statutory blanks in page order — a notice's owner (or the owner's mailing address) on the *envelope*, the GC, signer and company address on the form; an affidavit's county, legal description, owner (or address) and GC on the form, plus the entry's failing gates the paper does not print (notices recorded, homestead) as *record* gaps. Each gap carries its words, where it sits, the act that fills it and the paper field it marks. `withGapTokens` puts a token in each form field; `paintGaps` turns the tokens in the rendered HTML into the numbered red `.lienPaperGap` mark; `gapToken` serves the envelope line; `lienPaperBannerWords` writes the banner (red with the count, green with the next step).
- **Component** `LienPaperPreviewOverlay.tsx`: the paper on light paper with the envelope line above a notice, the banner, the rail of gaps (the row's own button when its rung is the fix — *Find the owner*, *Fix the property* — else the words), ‹ › and the arrow keys across the rows, Esc in the capture phase. Modelled on the run preview.
- **`LienDeskNextUp`**: the chip is a `lienPaperDoor` button when `onOpenPaper` is passed (hover ring in its own color, `index.css`); `gapsFor` gives the red count or the green tick. A GC's run row and retainage rows keep the plain tag.
- **The desk** builds `paperEntries` on open from what it holds — `resolveLienProperty` over the job's address and owner override, the GC, `signerNameFor`, the issuer's address, the queue or affidavit entry (its `item` fields through `parseLienDeskDraftFields` for a drafted notice, its gates for an affidavit) — through the same builders that print the paper, so the preview can never differ from it. `paperGapCount` feeds the chips from the property facts alone.
