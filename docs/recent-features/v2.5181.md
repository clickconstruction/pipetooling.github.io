- **Who to call** (`GcPeoplePill.tsx`, new): the design spike's pill, ported. Each live row of the Project Board shows its job's share of the people we wait on (`projectPeople`), such as *6 to call · 2 late*. Pointing at it, or pressing it, opens the card on `PeopleRows`, which main already has. The card lists each person, the late ones first, every reason under their name, and **Call**. **Open Follow up** goes to Follow up.
  - A person on two jobs shows on both rows. A row with nobody to call says *Nobody to chase*. A lost job shows no pill.
  - **Follow up** on a person and **Work the list** come with the Follow up sheet (B2b-viii). Until then `onFollowUp` and `onWorkList` are unset, so Call only dials.
- **The board** (`GcBoard.tsx`): `onChase` draws the pill, and `GcProjects` passes it to open Follow up. With no `onChase` there is no pill, so the board's other callers are unchanged.
