# v2.8529 — Jobs → Pipeline: Addresses that look alike, a list of places that may be written two ways (2026-11-24)

Punch list #439, piece 2 of seven, its first part: the list and its answers. One migration, `20261124200000_address_place_answers.sql`. No edge function.

## What was there

Addresses on file stay as they were typed. v2.8520 helps a new address match one on file, and does nothing for the ones already there. A read of the jobs on 2026-11-24 found 24 pairs with the same house number, a street written alike and the same map point, 5 more written alike where the map says apart, and 22 pairs within 40 m written differently.

## What changed

- **The door.** Jobs → Pipeline → the tools menu, under **Combine / Separate…**: **Addresses that look alike…**, for the office. Its count shows on the item and on the tools button while a pair waits, and goes when none does. The count reads a moment after the page opens, so the Pipeline's own reads go first, and is kept five minutes.
- **The window**, `AddressLookAlikesWindow.tsx`. One line of counts, *5 pairs to look at. 1 answered.* Four tabs: *Written alike, same spot on the map*, *Written alike, the map says apart*, *Different words, same spot* and *No street number*, each with its count. A pair leads with the busier address, the one people know, and shows the other beside it with the differing words marked. Each says what is at it (`2 jobs · J1078, J1114`), and the second says how far apart the map puts them.
- **The answer.** **Same place** or **Not the same**, kept in `address_place_answers` under the pair's two cleaned keys in order. An answered pair moves under the ones still waiting and reads *Same place · Cora Sample, Nov 24* with **Undo**, which asks it again. An answer changes no job's address.
- **A zip code two addresses share is no longer marked.** `markedWords` read one address's words against the other's cleaned key, which drops a zip code at its end, so every pair with a zip showed it as a difference, here and in the job form's nudge from v2.8520. Both sides are now cleaned a word at a time. A zip only one of them has is still marked.
- **A refusal says why.** A read that fails draws `OfflineRetryPanel` with its reason. An answer that is refused says its reason in a toast, and the pair stays waiting.

## How it is built

- `src/lib/addresses/lookAlikes.ts` (pure). `writtenAlike` is the same house number and the same street, a space moved, or a letter or two apart, whatever unit follows. `lookAlikes` pairs within a house number for the first two groups, and within map cells of about a hundred metres for the third. A map point four or more addresses share is left out, since it is a town's or a road's centre. `samePlaceKeys` follows chains of **Same place**, for the site match that the next pieces read.
- `src/hooks/useAddressLookAlikes.ts` reads every job's and bid's address, the map's own points (`address_geocodes`) and the answers. It throws on a failed read, so an empty list is never a lost read.
- `addressesOnFile.ts` now keeps each address's spellings and labels, which the pairing and the rows read.
- `developments/notInIt.ts` exports `ADDRESS_STREET_WORDS`, the street words a suffix cleans to.

## Design calls

- **No batch press.** The first group is the likeliest, and a unit or a building is in it. Whether *Building 16* and *Building 20* are one place is the person's call, one pair at a time.
- **Not the same is kept too**, so a pair of neighbours is asked once.
- **Different thresholds.** Written alike counts as the same spot within 75 m, since two buildings of one site sit that far apart. Different words must be within 40 m.

- **One line of counts, not three tiles.** The first cut drew three tiles over the tabs. A phone look showed they repeated what the tabs say and pushed the first pair off the screen.
