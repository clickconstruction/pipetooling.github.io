# v2.6022 — GC mode: a Templates page, where a schedule template is made as phases and rows and saved as a new version (2026-10-28)

PR 3 of punch list #204 (`to-dos/schedule-builder/README.md`, the mock-up's D1 and D2), on the kernel of PR 1 (v2.5995)
and the rows of PR 2 (v2.6020). The **Templates** pill on GC projects (`/gc?view=templates`), for the schedule team
(`canUseGcSchedule`), the owner's call 6.

## The page (`GcTemplatesPage.tsx`)

- **The list**: every template's current version, the newest first (`templateListRows` in `schedule/templates.ts`):
  its name with *version N* or *from a job* (a template saved before the builder, lines alone), its kind or *none*,
  rows, phases or *by stage*, weeks, *Tue Oct 27 by Dana*, the jobs drawn from it and from the versions before it,
  and **Open**. A **Kind** filter over the list. **New template** opens an empty editor; **From a job's schedule**
  picks a job being built with a schedule and saves it with its rows (`saveTemplateFromJob`), the fit words of
  `templateSaveProblem` first. An empty list says what fills it.
- **The editor**: the name, *version N*, **Kind**, **Days are** (*Mon to Fri* or *every day*, call 4), **Add a phase**
  (a prompt for the name), **Add a row** (to the last phase; each phase heading has its own), the line
  *Makes 20 weeks from a Monday start. 50 rows, 8 phases, 6 milestones.*, then the table: each phase as a heading
  with its name in a box, its days read from its rows and its count, with Add a row, move up, move down and Take
  it off (refused with its rows); each row with its name, **Who** (Office, Us, or a trade from the jobs' trades
  and the template's own), **Days**, **Waits on** (the start day or another row, by the name a wait writes),
  **Gap**, **Milestone** and **⋯** (move up, move down, Move to… another phase, Take it off). **Save as version
  N+1** (or **Save the template** for a new one) is dead while `templateDraftProblem` names a problem, shown
  under the toolbar. **Rename** (a prompt, the name refused in the kernel's words) and **Set it aside** / **Bring
  it back** act on the version open. `?view=templates&template=<id>` opens one in the editor (`templatesHref`).

## The kernels

- `schedule/templateEditor.ts` (new): the draft (`TemplateDraft`), `templateDraftOf` (a template saved before
  reads as rows with the stage as the phase), `addPhase` / `renamePhase` / `movePhase` / `removePhase` (with
  `phaseRemoveProblem`), `addRow` / `updateRow` / `setRowWaits` / `moveRow` / `moveRowToPhase` / `removeRow`,
  every change resolving the waits to rows first and writing their names again after, so a wait follows a rename,
  a move or a removal; `rowWaitChoices`, `waitWords`, `gapOf`; `templateDraftWeeks` and `templateDraftPhases`
  from the kernel of PR 1 drawn on a bare job from a Monday (`EDITOR_MONDAY`), over the rows alone;
  `templateDraftWords`; `templateDraftProblem` in the save's words; `templateSaveBody` as
  `gc_schedule_template_save` reads it.
- `schedule/templates.ts`: `templatesCurrent` (a replaced version is history), `templatesOffered` and
  `templateNameProblem` over the current versions, `templateVersions` / `templateVersion`, `templateListRows`,
  `templateSavedByWords`; `templateSavedWords` and `templateSizeWords` for a template with no job it came from.
  `ScheduleTemplate.from` is optional and `supersededOn` new (`types.ts`); `schedule/rows.ts` reads the six
  version-2 columns, and `writes.ts`'s `templateRowOf` writes a null job.
- `scheduleTemplatesIo.ts` (new): `loadScheduleTemplates`, `saveTemplateBody` (the RPC, *Another template has that
  name* read off the index), `saveTemplateFromJob`, `renameTemplate`, `setTemplateAside`, and `templatesIo` for the
  page. `scheduleIo.ts`'s `saveScheduleTemplate` (the job's Templates card) now saves through the RPC with the
  schedule's rows beside its lines, and exports its `taken`, `rowsIn` and `changedOne`.
