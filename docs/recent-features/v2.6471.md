# v2.6471 — The Job Summary's premium total leaves out a job whose premium waits (2026-11-03)

The final live pass of punch list #254 (charges by crew), after v2.6461 reached the app: a job whose days price at nothing showed its premium as a dash, waiting, but the totals row under it read **$0.00**, because `summarizeJobSummaryRows` added a waiting premium as zero. An even premium says something about a job; an unknown one does not.

## What changed

- `summarizeJobSummaryRows` (`src/lib/jobs/jobSummaryLedgerView.ts`) adds only the premiums that exist and counts the priced rows whose premium waits (`JobSummaryTotals.premiumWaitingJobs`). With none counted, the total is null and the cell reads a dash.
- The totals row and each Cut by group's row title the cell with `premiumTotalWaitsWords` (`src/lib/charges/premium.ts`): *Leaves out 1 job whose premium waits until every day prices.*, or *The premium waits until every day prices on 3 jobs.* when none is counted.
- Tests: the totals with one waiting row and with every row waiting; the words.

## Decisions

- **Leave it out, and say so.** A total that names the jobs it leaves out is honest; a zero standing in for an unknown is not.
