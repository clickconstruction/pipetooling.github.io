## Why

The whole point of the train: replace the Gmail Taunya writes by hand (report PDF attached, Stripe pay link pasted in, sent to Done Right, cc Malachi) with one button on the job that also keeps the record.

## What shipped

- **Email builder [`_shared/testReportEmail.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/testReportEmail.ts)** (door `src/lib/jobs/testReportEmail.ts`, +4 tests): subject `"<Sewer Pre-Test Hydrostatic> Test Report — <street>"`; body from the Settings template with `{report} {address} {payLink} {company} {phone}`; the link line and the words "and below is the invoice link" drop when there is no bill; mail-safe HTML with the one anchor. Settings gain **Report email body** and **Always copy** (`emailBodyTemplate`, `emailCc`; `parseEmailList`).
- **Edge function [`send-test-report`](../../supabase/functions/send-test-report/index.ts)**: Bearer JWT, user-scoped read of the `job_test_reports` row (RLS decides who may send), ≤ 6 To / cc, PDF ≤ 6 M base64 chars; **uploads the exact bytes** to `job-test-reports/<job_id>/<report_id>-v<n>.pdf` (never overwrites — a re-send is v2), sends through Resend (To + cc + attachment; `email_type: 'test_report'` in the email log), stamps the row (`status 'sent'`, `sent_at/to/cc/by`, `sent_pay_url`, `pdf_path`, `pdf_version`, the certifier snapshot), and posts the job activity line *"Sent Sewer Pre-Test Hydrostatic report to Done Right Foundation Repair (billing@…), cc …, with the invoice link."* A Resend failure leaves the row unsent (the stored file is harmless); a stamp failure after a successful send returns a "mark it sent" 500.
- **Client [`sendTestReport.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/sendTestReport.ts)** renders the PDF in the browser (jsPDF) and calls the function. **[`TestReportSendSheet`](../../src/components/jobs/TestReportSendSheet.tsx)** inside the modal: To prefilled from the last send → the GC's email (`customers.contact_info`) → the customer's; cc from Settings; the subject; the editable message (*Reset to the template*); the PDF chip; **Include the Stripe pay link ($250.00)** when the job's newest billed Stripe invoice has one, else the **Bill first** nudge and *Send without the link* as the button's honest label; the kernel's blockers listed in red. The modal's footer gains **Send to GC…** (saves first when new or dirty) / **Send again…**; a sent report shows *Sent Sep 11 to … · cc … · with the pay link · v2 · certified by …* and warns when unsaved edits differ from what the GC has.
- **Needs You** (`test-reports-ready`, rank 40, office roles): [`useTestReportsReadyNudge`](../../src/hooks/useTestReportsReadyNudge.ts) reads every draft the caller can see and splits them with `testReportSendBlockers` into *ready* (nothing but the recipient missing) and *waiting on a verdict*; "3 test reports are ready to send — J1014 Johnson Pretest · …"; the card opens the first one in the modal (`fetchJobWithDetailsById` → `openTestReport`).
- Email catalog: `test_report` (Settings → Emails). Help guide `file-a-test-report.md`: the Send section is real now.
