# v2.4804 — Map: one pin per address, and a rail in place of the table (2026-10-07)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. PR 3 of the Map page refresh ([`to-dos/map-page-refresh/`](../../to-dos/map-page-refresh/README.md)), after [v2.4802](v2.4802.md). The owner's first call on the proposal: *places, not records*; the third: *the rail alone*.

## What changed

- **[`mapPagePlaces.ts`](../../src/lib/map/mapPagePlaces.ts)** (pure): `mapPagePlaces` folds the placed records into places by address key; `mapPagePlacePins` gives each place one pin — the id is the address key, the color is the liveliest record's (`mapPageRecordRank`: a working job over a paid one, an unsent bid over a lost one, estimates between), the ring the most urgent any record wears (Collections, then overdue, then due soon — `mapPagePlaceRing`), the badge the count (`placeBadge`, *99+* past two digits), the title the address with *3 jobs · 1 bid*; `mapPageBands` counts places and records in the Bid Board rail's three bands from the office and `placesInBands` applies the band switches; `mapPageNearest` sorts by the straight-line miles with a cap; `mapPageTotalsLine` reads *244 places · 373 records*. `mapPageStageWords` (in `mapPageSections.ts`) reads a record's stage for a card row: *Working*, *Billed · Collections*, the bid stage's title, an estimate's status and total.
- **[`MapPageRail`](../../src/components/map/MapPageRail.tsx)**: the filter box (moved up from the table heading), the selected place's card (`PlaceCard`: the address, *3 jobs · 1 bid at this address · 31 mi from the office*, each record with its dot, number, name, stage and **Open**, *+ N more*, **Directions**; a single record reads as before), **By distance from the office** (three boxes, places and records, tap to hide a band), **Nearest the office** (twelve rows with the dot, the number and name or the address with its count, and the miles; *+ N more on the map*), and the totals line. A row's hover pulses its pin (`pulseId`); a row's click selects and flies. `PlaceCard` in `compact` is the pin's popup (four rows, *+ N more in the rail*).
- **The page**: the desktop is a `3fr / 2fr` grid of map and rail (the phone stacks them, and the selected card is the bar); the drawn area now filters the map and the rail (`filterEntitiesByPolygon` moved up from the table); the home fit and Fit all are over the near places; the table, its title and empty hints, `MapEntityTable`, `MapPinBody` and the phone bar are gone. The empty state reads why — a search with no match, every chip off, every band off, no pins in the drawn area.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **A place is what the office means by "there".** Nine jobs at one house were nine rows and nine pins on top of each other; now they are one pin with a 9, one card and one Directions.
- **The pin's color is the most useful record's.** A paid history never hides a working job, and the ring says if anything there is in Collections or overdue.
- **The table's three jobs went to better homes**: the filter box to the rail head, the per-record Open to the card, the "what is on the map" to the bands and the nearest list.
- **Not done:** the address sheet for the records the map cannot place (PR 4; the geocode progress list still sits in the header); the Precincts chip and the precinct on the card (PR 5).
