# v2.6833 — Permits: a street view and a map on every skim card, and a swipe to answer (punch list #302, PR 3 and 5) (2026-11-10)

The owner's first look at the #302 frames asked for a map and a street view; his original ask was a swipe. Both land on the skim card (v2.6830).

## What changed

- **`supabase/functions/street-view-preview`**: the metadata answer carries `date`, the month Google took the picture (`"2024-03"`), or null. Additive: older readers ignore it. **Deploy after the merge** (`supabase functions deploy street-view-preview`); until then the tile reads *Street view* with no month.
- **`src/lib/fetchStreetViewPreview.ts`**: `fetchStreetViewMetaWithMonth`; `fetchStreetViewMeta` keeps its shape and reads through it.
- **`src/lib/research/permitLook.ts`** (new, pure, `permitLook.test.ts`): `streetViewLocation` (the address with its city and zip, so the camera faces the lot), `monthWords`, `streetViewWords`, `tilesAround` (the web-map tiles that cover a still with a point in its middle), the two tile addresses (OpenStreetMap, and the Esri imagery the On site map already draws), and the swipe's rules: `swipeThreshold` (a third of the card, never under 90 pixels), `swipeAnswer`, `swipeProgress`, `swipeMayStart` (never a mouse, never within 24 pixels of the screen's edge, where the browser goes Back) and `swipeIsScroll`.
- **`PermitSkimLook`** (new): the two tiles across the top of the card. The street view is asked for through the function for the top card only (one paid picture per card that is read) and never stored; with no picture the tile is satellite at the permit's point. The map is a still of street tiles with a pin in the middle and the miles. Each tile is a link: Street View in Google, or the site in Google Maps.
- **`PermitSkimDeck`**: the card takes pointer drags from a touch or a pen, turns with the finger, shows KEEP or PASS as it passes the threshold, answers past it and springs back short of it. The buttons and the keys are unchanged. The miles moved from the address line onto the map.
- `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`: the function's metadata answer.
- Guide `research-permits-for-work-to-bid`: the pictures and the swipe.

## Walked live

2026-11-10, the dev login on localhost: the top card drew Google's picture of the lot and a street map with the pin and the miles; both tiles opened Google. Before the redeploy the tile read *Street view* with no month, as designed.

## Release order

Merge, then `supabase functions deploy street-view-preview`. No migration.
