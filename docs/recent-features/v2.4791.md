# v2.4791 — Map: the first view is the office (2026-10-07)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. PR 1 of the Map page refresh in [`to-dos/map-page-refresh/`](../../to-dos/map-page-refresh/README.md) (the plan of record, new in this PR with the mock-up). The owner, 2026-10-07: *this page could use a general refresh, I think it could do more and look better*; the four calls were taken as recommended (places, estimates off by default, the rail alone, 300 miles).

## Why

The page fitted the map to every pin, and two jobs are geocoded to the wrong continent (one in California, one in Assam). Opened as the dev on 2026-10-07, the map showed open ocean at zoom 12 on a desktop and the whole world with four dots on a phone, with no Fit all to recover. The drawing toolbar showed nine Geoman tools for the one that is used.

## What changed

- **[`mapPageFirstView.ts`](../../src/lib/map/mapPageFirstView.ts)** (pure): `splitFarFromOffice` keeps the pins within `MAP_PAGE_FAR_MILES` (300) of the office and lists the rest farthest first with their miles — with no anchor nothing is far; `farFromOfficePlaces` folds the far pins into one line per address with its count by kind (`farPlaceCountWords`); `mapPageHomeFitPoints` is the Bid Board map's home fit (the office, its 50 mile ring and the near pins inside it, `bidBoardMapHomeFitPoints`); `mapPageFitAllPoints` is every near pin and the office; `farFromOfficeLine` / `farMilesWords` are the words.
- **[`MapPageView`](../../src/components/map/MapPageView.tsx)**: `useOfficeAnchor` and the diamond with its 25 / 50 mile rings; `MapFit` replaces the one-shot fit to every pin — the home fit on load and whenever its bounds change (the anchor arriving), **Fit all** on the toolbar on demand; the far pins are still drawn and still in the table, and a `data-far-from-office` block under the map lists each far address (*Gun Dog Trail, Neeses, SC · 29 jobs · 1,024 mi · Show*; *Ranch · 1 job · 1,462 mi · Show · Open*) — Show flies the map there, Open is for a single record. Geoman's `addControls` keeps only the polygon.
- **[`LeafletOfficeAnchor`](../../src/components/map/LeafletOfficeAnchor.tsx)** is the anchor diamond and rings lifted out of `PinsMapCanvas` so the page and the canvas draw the same thing; the canvas's behavior is unchanged.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **The office is the home, not the data.** A map of the company's work should open where the company is; a stray geocode must not be able to move it.
- **Far pins are listed, not hidden, and by address.** The first live look found 29 of the 31 far records at one real job site in South Carolina, so a far pin is a far job as often as a wrong address, and the line says neither; it lists the addresses with their counts and lets a person decide. The single wrong ones (a ranch pinned in California, a house pinned in Assam) stand out as one-record lines with Open.
- **Not done:** status colors, clusters, the places grouping and the rail (PRs 2 and 3); the no-location and far-away sheet (PR 4); the Precincts chip (PR 5).
