## Why these two are simple

Time on a Google card takes the Google canvas down and mounts the Leaflet one. These two are already on the Leaflet canvas, so Time changes one tile layer and the frame's fit, and nothing remounts. That is why the Map page's drawn area and its layers survive a press on Time and on a date.
