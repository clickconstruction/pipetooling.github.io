# v2.4248 — Submittals: set the supply house on a row, for when you buy it somewhere else (2026-09-30)

Grace, after B375's carriers were bought from National Wholesale: "we ended up going with another supply house for some of these parts and have purchased." A row's `supply_house_id` came only from a Pricing pick or the takeoff, so a row added by hand had no house and a house could not be changed — the three carrier rows had theirs set straight in the database. She asked whether the row editor should take it: "I like it, build it."

- [`SubmittalItemEditDialog.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/SubmittalItemEditDialog.tsx): a **Supply house · office only** picker (`houses` prop; *No house* first; a house the list no longer carries still reads as *The house on this row*, so opening a row never drops it). It shows on every revision, not only a draft — the house is who the part is bought from, which the GC's room never shows (`submittalRoomPayload.ts`), so changing it after sharing changes nothing they approved. `supply_house_id` rides the patch **only when the picker was changed**, so a save of something else cannot move a house. No `houses` → no picker.
- [`BidsSubmittalsTab.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/BidsSubmittalsTab.tsx): one `supply_houses` read (`id, name`, by name) feeds the picker and a muted house line under each row's submitted product (`data-testid="row-house"`). `saveItem` already spreads the patch into the insert and the update, so a row by hand lands with its house. The Rebuild confirm says what happens to a hand-set house.
- [`buildSubmittalRows.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/buildSubmittalRows.ts): `houseForRow(prev, pick)` — a rebuild, or the next revision, used to take every row's house from the pick again. Now the row's own house stands **while the pick behind it is the same quote line** (`PreviousItem.sourceQuoteLineId` = `PickInput.quoteLineId`); a different pick brings its own house; a row nobody quoted has none. No marker column was needed: the same quote line always means the same quoted house, so a row whose house differs from it was changed by hand. `itemToPrevious` carries the two fields; both are optional on `PreviousItem`, and a previous row that says nothing about its house takes the pick's.
- Not changed: rows built from the takeoff keep their house until edited (a rebuild from picks does not rebuild them); the procurement log reads the row's house as before (`procurementItemsFrom`), so its Item line and the printed sheet follow the picker with no change of their own.
- Guide *build a submittal package* → the Edit paragraph and the Rebuild line; the Bids document-tabs map → SU-11.

Walked live in dev on BP375's Rev 1 draft: the rows read their houses (Moore Supply, Reece, National Wholesale); *WC CARRIER* opened on National Wholesale, was saved as Moore Supply — the row, the rows table and the procurement log's Item line all followed — and was put back. Not walked: the picker on a shared revision (covered by the dialog's render test), and a rebuild from picks on a real bid (the kernel test covers the rule).
