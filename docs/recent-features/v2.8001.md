# v2.8001 — Painted maps: Put the dot where I am leaves the dot where it is on a rough reading, and says why (2026-11-19)

Punch list #402, PR 3: the place panel's press. The kernel is v2.7984 and the walk's line is v2.7994. Client only: no migration and no function.

## The wrinkle

**Put the dot where I am** takes one reading and moved the dot to it, whatever its margin. A phone with Precise Location off hands back the middle of an area a mile or more across. So the dot jumped miles from the job, the map flew after it, and the panel said *Your phone is only sure within 10,500 ft. Drag the dot onto the spot.*, with the spot no longer on the screen.

## What changed

- **A rough reading moves nothing** (`readingMovesDot` in `src/lib/paintedMap/paintedPlaceDot.ts`). The dot and the map stay where the painter had them. The window keeps the reading's margin for the words and nothing else of it.
- **The panel says why**, with the same line the walk draws (`PreciseLocationLine`): *Precise Location looks off.*, the phone's two numbers side by side, *The dot was not moved. Drag it onto the spot by hand, or turn the switch on and press again.*, and the steps for this phone. On a desk the note stops at *by hand*, since a desk has no switch.
- **A press has nothing to wait for.** One reading was asked for, so no better one is coming, and the line calls it rough at once. The 4 second wait is for a screen that follows the phone.
- **A sure reading** reads *Precise Location is on. Sure within 15 ft.* with the tick, in place of *Found you. Your phone is sure within 15 ft.* **A weak one** still moves the dot and keeps its amber box, now led by the line: *Precise Location is on, but the signal is weak here. Your phone is only sure within 120 ft. Drag the dot onto the spot.*
- **The answer comes into view.** On a phone the press sits near the foot of the panel and the alert lands under it. A rough reading moves nothing on the map, so the panel scrolls its words into view when they come.
- **A place added during a walk** starts its dot in the middle of what the map shows when the walk's last reading is rough (`placeOnWalk`). Before, it started in the middle of the rough area.
- A later rough press keeps an earlier good reading: the dot stays on it and its margin is still saved with the place.

## One thing that was there before

A place opened to change showed *Found you. Your phone is sure within 15 ft.* from the margin saved with it, days after anyone was found. The line would have turned that into *Precise Location is on.*, said of a phone that was never read. So the panel now speaks of the phone only from a reading taken on this visit, by the press or by the walk (`Placing.live`). A saved place's own margin is still kept with the place while its dot is not moved, and a place opened to change shows the plain hint.

## A fix to the walk's map, from v2.7994

On a short phone the dashed circle could lose its top and bottom. The way to the switch is a tall answer, and on a phone the map gives it room in the same draw that hands the map its area. The fit was worked out for the box the map had, 11 px too tall for the box it has at a margin of 2,500 m or 5,000 m on a 390 × 667 screen. `FrameMargin` in `PaintedMapCanvas` now has the map measure its box before it asks the frame rule. A walk at eight margins on two phone heights then showed the whole circle every time.

That is a thing of the Leaflet map, which measures its box once and is told when it changes. A canvas whose map follows its own box needs nothing for it.
