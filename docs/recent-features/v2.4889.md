# v2.4889 — GC mode: the Project Board on real data, for a dev (2026-10-08)

The Board's B3-a in the real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/BOARD_REAL_BUILD.md`, on branch `spike/gc-mode`): the Project Board, read only, on real data at `/gc`. A dev sees it above door 1's list of projects. Everyone else sees the page as before until the board's own door opens it. Guide: `see-where-every-gc-project-stands`.

- **`src/lib/gc/boardRows.ts`** turns the rows the board reads into the shapes B2's kernels read (`GcState`, `GcProject`, `Partner`, `Invite`, `SubBid`, `TradePromise`), so no kernel changes for real data. It reads New project's projects, the Board's dates on `gc_projects`, and the company record (B1): companies, asks, the newest quote on each, the call log, promises and their moves.
  - Read from the rows, never stored: how many asks and quotes a company has, the day an ask was last chased, and the town in a company's address standing in for its drive.
  - Our own trade is "our own bid to price" until the board reads its Trades mode bid.
  - `boardTestRows.ts` is the test data.
- **`loadGcBoardRows`** in `gcIo.ts` loads those rows. The page loads it only for a dev, and only when there is a project.
- **`GcBoard.tsx`** has the sections (Bidding to the customer, Buying out, Building, Closed, Lost), each with its heading and worth, and the jump strip (`GcBoardStages.tsx`, `useJumpStrip.ts`).
  - Each row has its days left or days to start, its customer and architect, its plans and Drive folder links, and its price so far with the holes.
  - `GcPriceCard.tsx` and `usePriceCard.ts` are the card behind the price, read only. Carrying, asking and following up come with B4 and B5.
  - All of it is ported from the spike's board.
- **Waits for B2b:** the ring, Who to call and By customer, whose kernels read the other lanes.
- **Tests:**
  - `boardRows.test.ts`: the mapper, read back through the kernels.
  - `GcBoard.render.test.tsx`.
  - A page test that a dev sees the board above the projects.
  - The tour's anchors are unchanged.
