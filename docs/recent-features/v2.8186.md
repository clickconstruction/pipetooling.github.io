# v2.8186 — Painted maps: room for a description on a place (2026-11-21)

Punch list #413, step 0, the database half. Taunya asked on 2026-11-21 for an open-ended description on a place, as one of three changes to the card before the build. No screen changes.

## Why

A place on a painted map carries one line for whoever comes next, held to 200 characters by the table and to 160 by the app. The card's plan lets a crew keep a spot from a photo: a cleanout, a shutoff, a buried line. A buried line wants more than a line. It wants what is buried, how deep, which way it runs and what was left in the ground beside it.

## What ships

Migration `20261121184500_painted_place_words_room.sql` ([its page](../migrations/20261121184500_painted_place_words_room.md)):

- The check on `painted_map_places.words` goes from 200 characters to 4,000, under a name of its own, `painted_map_places_words_room`.
- No new column. A place's words are its description, so everything that reads a place already reads it.

Nobody new reads or writes anything. The table's four policies are by row, so whoever may set or change a place may write its words.
