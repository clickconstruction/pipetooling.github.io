# v2.7959 — Who owes what: Email again reaches the GC on a paper bill that went to the GC (2026-11-19)

Client only: no migration, no edge function. Found on 2026-11-19 while reading the callers of the billed invoice's paper for v2.7956.

## Before

**Email again — PDF attached** (v2.2605) re-sends a paper bill's PDF through `resendPhysicalInvoiceEmailForBilledInvoice`. Its address was the invoice's `bill_to_email`, else `job.customer_email`. `send-physical-invoice-email` resolves the payer itself (`effectiveInvoiceParty`) and, on a bill to the GC, accepts only the GC's billing email. So:

- on a GC-billed job with its own customer, the confirm window named the customer's address and the send came back *customer_email must match the GC's billing email*;
- on a GC job with no customer, the card had no address to show and did not draw the button.

No bill on prod was in this state on 2026-11-19: two bills have ever gone out as paper bills, both to the job's customer and both paid. The fix is for the first one that goes to a GC.

## What changed

- `paperBillResendRecipient(job, inv, gc)` in the new `src/lib/jobs/paperBillResend.ts`: the function's own rule on the client. A bill typed to someone else goes to that address, a bill to the GC to the GC's billing email (`customerBillingEmail`, the contact email standing in), any other bill to the job's customer email. `paperBillNoAddressWords` says where to add a missing address.
- `resendPhysicalInvoiceEmail.ts`: the send uses it, reading the GC's `customers` row (`id, billing_email, contact_info`) only when the bill went to the GC. `readPaperBillResendRecipient(invoiceRef)` is the same answer for the confirm window: three small reads, nothing written. The shared job embeds are not widened.
- `BilledBreakdownBill.billedTo` (`billedByCustomerBreakdown.ts`): who the bill went to. The card (`BilledByCustomerBreakdownModal`) draws the button for a paper bill to the GC whether or not the job has a customer email, and passes `readRecipient`.
- `PhysicalInvoiceResendButton`: with `readRecipient`, the window reads the GC's billing email when it opens and names it. While it reads, and when there is no address, it offers no send: the reason shows with **Close**. A bill to the customer or to a typed recipient reads nothing and is as before.
- The guide `read-the-pipeline-money-view` says a bill that went to the GC goes to the GC's billing email.

## Tests

## Not changed

**Never got it? Resend** on a Stripe bill is handed the job's customer email for its window. Stripe sends to its own customer on the invoice, so the send is right. Whether that window names the right address on a GC-billed bill was not looked at here.
