# v2.4969 — Lien desk: the claim is what is billed, and the bills are listed under it (2026-10-08)

One migration (`20261009150000_lien_claim_is_billed.sql`), no edge function. Grace, on the live desk at job 922 · Michael Palmer: *this shows two invoices for 4000 but a total of 5000, what could be going on?* The header read **Claim $5,000** and the Months row *Sep · $5,000*, while page 3's line and the pay page behind it read *2 bills · $4,000.00 still owed*. Both were honest: the lien readers took the job's whole balance (`revenue − payments_made`) as the money at stake and as the notice's claim, and the pay page summed the sent bills. 922 is a $5,000 job billed in stages, two $2,000 bills sent, the last $1,000 not yet billed. The owner's rule settled it: *"I can't lien work I have not done yet."* Then: *the goal of our app is to give the user all the information so they can make great decisions — list those bills*, and *give the user the option to bill out the remaining so it can be claimed from this screen*. Mock-ups: https://claude.ai/artifact/BDYRMJE77ix4qrAumjs4UM (the first pass, a warning line) and https://claude.ai/artifact/VaQjRCsJcJVfTf2SD7FbTY (the one built: the number fixed at its source, the bills listed, the door).

## What changed

- **The desk's money is what the sent bills still owe.** [`lien_billed_open(job_id, status, revenue, payments_made)`](../migrations/20261009150000_lien_claim_is_billed.md) is the bill-truth rule the Bill tab already uses (`_shared/billTruth.ts`), in SQL, with one lien-only reading: once any bill has gone out (status `billed` or `paid`), each `billed` invoice net of the payments tied to it, clamped at zero, the paid ones nothing; a job billed as one shell (status `billed`, no bill ever sent) is `revenue − payments_made` as before; anything else 0. Bill truth draws a shell row of the unbilled remainder for a billed job whose sent bills are all paid; the lien rule does not, because that remainder is work not yet billed (922 that afternoon: both bills paid, claim 0, the gate). `list_lien_notice_months`, `list_lien_affidavit_windows` and `list_lien_retainage_windows` take it as `open_balance`, bodies otherwise verbatim from `20261007190000`. Their row filters are unchanged, so no job appears or vanishes: a billed job whose sent bills are paid off now reads 0 and gets the fifth gate below. Everything that hung on `openBalance` moves together — the queue row, the pile totals, the Do now dollars, the leader's phone card, the claim default, the word-record preview, the affidavit's sworn unpaid amount (`LienAffidavitJobFacts.openBalance`, the correction still off it; `contractAmount` and `paidAmount` stay the job's facts).
- **The bills behind the claim** (kernel [`lienClaimBills.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienClaimBills.ts), component [`LienClaimBills.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienClaimBills.tsx)): under the claim box on the Months card, one line per sent bill — Stripe's number when the office has it (the same `enclosedInvoiceDocument` read as the enclosure), the bill's line (*Rough In*), the day it went out, Stripe or paper, the due day — with **Billed · Paid · Owed**; a bill marked paid (status `paid`) stays on the list reading *paid*, so the column adds up to the claim and the reader sees the money that came in (on the live walk 922's two bills had been paid that afternoon, which is what showed the case); Stripe's facts are fetched for the paid ones too, so they carry the number the customer paid. The pane's Months row counts them: *Sep · $4,000 · 2 bills*, *1 of 2 bills*, *2 bills paid*. Then, in amber, **Not billed yet · $1,000 of the job’s $5,000 · not claimed until it is billed** (`lienUnbilled`: price − payments − what the sent bills owe, never below zero). `useNoticePayPage` carries `bills` and the job it read, so the list costs no second request. On a narrow card the Billed and Paid columns fold away.
- **Bill it from here ›** on that line (and on the fifth gate) opens the app's own Bill Customer window over the desk — `useBillCustomerModal().openBillCustomer({ kind: 'job' })`, the same door the Pipeline's Bill button and the Dashboard use, drawn above every window — on the job as the desk read it. When the bill goes, `onSuccess` calls `payPage.refresh()` (drops the job's cached read and reads again) and the desk's `onChanged()` (the queue re-runs the RPC), so the claim, the row and the pay page fill in by themselves. Only an office role sees the door; a job with no customer gets the Pipeline's own refusal.
- **The fifth gate, Nothing billed** (`lienDeskGates.ts`, `key: 'billed'`, `n: 5`): drawn only when the sent bills owe nothing and money is still on the job — no bill sent yet, or the sent ones paid and the rest unbilled. Value *Bill the work first*; the section reads *Nothing is billed on this job yet. A notice claims what is billed. Bill the work, then come back. The claim fills in by itself.* (or *The sent bills are paid. What is left on the job is not billed yet…*), with the door and *The bills ↓*. The footer's sentence: *Don't send yet. Bill the work first. A notice claims what is billed.*; the stop chip: *nothing billed*. The grid takes five columns on that job (`data-count`). Every other job keeps its four gates and *All 4 clear*.

## Is this the best we can do

The first board put an amber warning under the $5,000 (*$1,000 of this claim is not on a sent bill*) with two doors. Grace's rule made the warning the wrong fix: a claim that includes work not done is not a number to explain, it is the wrong number. So the base moved to the sent bills in SQL, where every reader shares it, and the amber line survives only as the honest leftover — what the job still has that no bill carries — with the door that bills it. A fifth gate rather than a fourth-gate rewording, because *Approved hours* is a fact about the months and this is a fact about the money; it is conditional so the four gates stay four everywhere else.
