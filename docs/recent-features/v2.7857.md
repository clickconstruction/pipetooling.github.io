# v2.7857 — Pricing calculator: send the answer to a row, or make a new row from it (2026-11-18)

The second screen piece of the Percent row train (after v2.7855). The owner's ask of 2026-11-18: from the calculator, *in the click of a button add that value to another line on pricing OR make a new line*. Dragging a line onto the calculator follows.

## What ships

- Under the Pricing Tape's display, while a bid's numbers are on the Workbench: **Put on a row…** and **Make a new row…**. With no bid open on Pricing they are not drawn. A number below zero holds both, with the reason in their title.
- Each opens the **Percent row** window (v2.7855) with the number in it, the tape's own line at its top right (*From the tape: 6,200 × 0.4 = 2,480*), and the row drawn live as the grid will show it. A plain number opens with *Keep following these rows* off: it is a typed price.
- **Put on a row…** shows a **Row** list where the name box stands: every row on the open version with what it sells for now. The press is held, *Pick the row first.*, until one is picked. The number is the row's whole price, spread over its count, and a rule the row carried is dropped.
- **Make a new row…** opens on a name box. A name already on the bid sets that row, as it does from ＋ Percent row.
- A sent bid answers either press with the lock's own message and opens no window.

## How

- `src/lib/bids/pricingTapeBridge.ts`: the calculator is drawn by the page, beside the tab, so neither holds the other. The Workbench sets a sink while its numbers are settled and clears it when they are not; the calculator reads it with `usePricingTapeSink` and sends `{ value, words, rule }`. `rule` is null in this change: the tape cannot name a group of rows until a line can be dragged in.
- `BidsPricingCalculator.tsx`: the two presses, and the armed ring drops on a send so the window's boxes take the keys.
- `percentRow.ts`: the window's value gains `rowId`, and `percentWindowPreview` / `percentWindowStart` gain `pickRow`.
- `usePercentRows.ts`: `openPickRow`. `PercentRowWindow.tsx`: the Row list. `BidsPricingTab.tsx`: the sink.

## Not in this change

Dragging a row or a breakdown line onto the calculator, and with it a rule made from the tape.
