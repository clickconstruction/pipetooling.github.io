# v2.6428 — Hours statements read as cards, so the job name stays whole and Print · Open stay on the screen (2026-11-03)

Diane looked at Trace's desk at about 1024px on 2026-11-02 and did not like the **Hours statements** section (punch list #212, PR 2, v2.6097): the Job cell wrapped *629 · ROUGH IN: bunker and lodge, 2026* one word a line and Print was cut off at the pane's edge. A headless pass over the dev server at 375, 768, 1024 and 1280, light and dark, measured why.

## What was measured

- The Person desk drawer is `min(600px, 96vw)` wide at every desktop size, so its section is 573px at 768, 1024 and 1280 alike, and 349px on a phone. The job window's Documents tab is about 556px wide at every desktop size (the section 520px). The section never has a wide home.
- The table (Record · Job · Period · Saved · presses) needed 635px: four of its five columns were `nowrap`, so the Job column took what was left. In the desk the job name stacked eight lines (a 162px row) and Print · Open sat 60px past the pane's right edge behind an `overflowX: auto` wrapper nothing pointed at; at 375 the Period column was cut mid-word and Saved and both presses were off screen.
- Three options were drawn with made-up names (`to-dos/signed-hours-statement/mockup-statement-cards.html`: a two-line row at every width; the same row under 640px of the section with a grid table above it, which no home reaches; and a card a statement). Diane picked the cards, with the presses small at the right unless the card has to wrap, when they keep the full-width foot row that is easy to press.

## What changed

- **One card a statement** in [`HoursStatementsSection.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/HoursStatementsSection.tsx), both scopes: the title is the job (`629 · ROUGH IN: bunker and lodge, 2026`, whole, `overflow-wrap: anywhere`) on the desk and the worker's name on the job's own window; under it a `dl` of **Record** (the id and its state chip), **Period** (*March 31 – June 30, 2026 · hours and pay*) and **Saved** (*November 2, 2026 by Robert*), the same words from `hoursStatementRowWords`. On a signed statement the press group opens with PR 3's **Open the signed PDF** (v2.6411, merged the same night; this branch was rebased onto it). The heading, the press with its count, the reads, the window, Print from the snapshot and the empty state are unchanged; `data-testid="hours-statement-row"` stays on each card.
- **The presses follow the card's own width** (`.hsCard` … `.hsCardPresses` in `index.css`, a container query): 360px or wider, Print · Open sit small at the right end of the title row; narrower (a phone at 375 gives a 349px section), they drop to a full-width foot row at 40px tall. The list is a grid of `minmax(min(100%, 24rem), 1fr)`, one card a row in both homes today and two across only where each card still gets 384px, so a two-across card never falls back to the foot row.
- **The statement window loads only when opened.** `HoursStatementsSection` imports `HoursStatementModal` through `React.lazy` with a `Suspense` fallback of nothing, so the window, its two sheets and the paper's kernels leave the main chunk. The reason is the deploy: after PR 3 (v2.6411) merged, `vite build` failed on main and on this branch — vite-plugin-pwa's precache cap (`injectManifest.maximumFileSizeToCacheInBytes`, 7 MiB = 7,340,032 bytes) against a main chunk of 7,347,392 bytes, 7 KB over — so no client could deploy. With the window lazy the chunk measured 7,320,448 bytes locally, under the cap by about 19 KB, which is too thin for a queue that lands a code change every few minutes. Main raised the cap to 8 MiB the same morning (#6935, the reason beside it in `vite.config.ts`, as the 2026-10-20 raise to 7 MiB was: about 0.7 MiB of headroom again), and this branch's own raise merged into it with nothing left to change; against main at c7bbed155 the chunk measures 7,379,324 bytes, over the old cap and under the new one. The main chunk is due the code-split pass the config's comment already asks for; that is a task of its own.
- The smoke asserts the card's title by scope, the three labels, no `<table>`, and the presses by their names. The help guide *make a signed hours statement* says *card* where it said *row*; `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` §6 and the People paragraph, `PEOPLE_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md` and the Glossary's entry name the cards.
