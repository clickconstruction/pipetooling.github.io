# v2.2136 — Roadmap quick-fix bundle: Timeline month labels, row a11y, clamped caption, fullscreen header (2026-08-22)

Four small items from the Roadmap deep-look, bundled (client-only, no migration):

- **Timeline calendar month labels no longer collide on phones.** At 375px the 12-month band printed "AUG SEP OC NOVDEC JAN F21 MARA'2…". New kernel `monthLabelStride(bandPx, monthFraction, minLabelPx = 44)` in `src/lib/roadmapCalendar.ts` (+3 tests) returns how many months to skip between labels; the view measures the band (`ResizeObserver`, `resize` fallback, 1 while unmeasured) and labels every Nth month. Every month column keeps a `title` tooltip.
- **Timeline stage rows are named for assistive tech.** The `role="button"` rows had no accessible name; now `aria-label="Stage N: title — done of total done | milestone | reached | not planned yet[, locked]"`.
- **Clamped-goal caption leads with what's actionable.** When the projected finish sits past the 12-month horizon (Farm 1: "🎯 goal ≈ Jul '43" at 0.1 tasks/week), the caption now reads *88 tasks left · 1.8/week would finish by Jul '27 · at your all-time pace — 0.1 tasks/week · 🎯 at that pace ≈ Jul '43*. Unclamped order is unchanged (goal · pace · left).
- **Fullscreen Map overlay header uses `var(--surface)`** instead of a hard-coded light `rgba(248,250,252,.97)` that rendered as a pale strip in dark theme.
- Help: `read-the-roadmap-timeline.md` → "The calendar" paragraph updated (caption order; label thinning on phones).
