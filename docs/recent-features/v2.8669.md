# v2.8669 — A development's page lists the papers for its site (2026-11-26)

Punch list #439, piece 3 of seven, its part 3c. Client only: no migration, no edge function. It reads the `site_` columns of `person_contract_documents` and writes nothing.

## What was there

v2.8665 shows a site's papers on each job at its address. A development carries all its jobs, and its page showed none of their papers: a person had to open each job to see which companies had signed.

## What changed

- **Papers for this site**, a section on the development's page, after the photos. It lists outside companies' papers that name the development, or the address of a job or a bid in it, with every spelling the answers in **Addresses that look alike** make the same place.
- The lines are a job's own: the company, the paper and who signs, a state chip and its day, the ones still to sign first, and a line that counts the signed.
- **Drawn only when there is one**, or when the read failed, for the office's four roles and the estimator. It reads and presses nothing.

## How it is built

- `sitePapers.ts`: a `SiteScope` is the address keys of a place and the development a paper may name. `jobSiteScope` is one address, `developmentSiteScope` is every address of the development, two hundred keys at most. `rowIsInSiteScope` and `sitePaperLinesInScope` are the one rule, and the job's functions call them.
- `useSitePapers.ts`: `readPapersOfSite` asks forty keys a request, then the development by its id. `useDevelopmentSitePapers` is the page's read.
- `SitePapersList.tsx` draws the lines for both places. `JobDocumentsSitePapers.tsx` now uses it. `DevelopmentSitePapers.tsx` is the page's section, and `DevelopmentPage.tsx` hands it the addresses of the jobs and bids it shows.

## Design calls

- **Outside the page's looks.** The section is a box of its own, not one of the folding cards with a jump chip: it is absent on almost every development, and a card that is never there should not hold a chip.
- **What the page shows is what is read.** The addresses are those of the jobs and bids on the page. With *include the address* off, a job at the address that is not in the development is not counted.
- **No link to Agreements.** The mock-up draws *See them on Agreements*. The tab finds by one address (v2.8668), and a development is many. The link comes when the tab can be asked for a development.
- **The rest of the mock-up's page** is later pieces: secrecy on and off, our own people, the sends held, the proof.
