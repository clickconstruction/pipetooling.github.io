# v2.4850 — Lien desk: the approval button says what it is (2026-10-07)

Client only; words and a colour. Grace, on job 878's awaiting footer: *In the bottom right hand corner it is not very clear that the brown button is the approval stage, perhaps if it was blue and said "Leader here, Approve".* The footer of a notice in *Awaiting approval*, seen by the office, drew three buttons — *Already mailed? Record it…*, *Pull back to draft* and, in amber, *He is here — record it ▸*. The amber one is the approval step (v2.3813: the leader stands at the desk, the office writes down that he is standing there or typing it in, and the notice goes to Ready to send on his word), but neither its colour nor its words said so: amber is the desk's hold colour, and *record it* reads like the two by-hand doors beside it.

## What changed

- **`LienDeskModal`**, the office's awaiting footer: the button is `btn('primary')` (blue, the desk's colour for the step that moves a notice forward — *Send for approval*, *Put it in the run*, *Send the run*) and reads **Leader here, Approve ▸**, the owner's words. Its hover line ends *This is the approval step.* `data-lien-desk-leader-here` is unchanged.
- The row it opens (`LienWordRecordRow`) leads in with *Leader here — who, when, and how:* so the words carry through; its channels, its presence line and *Record it and send ▸* are as they were.
- The leader's own footer is untouched: he sees *Approve & next ▸* (green), never this button.

## Tests

`LienDeskModal.render.test.tsx`: the two *he is here* smokes read the new words and the row's new lead-in; the leader's footer still has no such button.
