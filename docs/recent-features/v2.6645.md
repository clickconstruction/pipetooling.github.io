# v2.6645 — The lien month: the rule and the kernel (punch list #235, PR 1 of four) (2026-11-07)

The owner set an office rhythm for lien work on 2026-11-01 — reminders in the first days of the month, notices next, liens by the 15th, nothing from the 16th on — and refined it in chat on 2026-11-07, one call at a time against the card's recommendations (`to-dos/lien-month-rhythm/README.md`, *The owner's refinement*): the card's days (1 to 5 · 6 to 9 · 10 to 13 · 14 to 15 · 16 on), one courtesy reminder in reminders week of the affidavit's month with the day-7 floor kept, notices week counting only this month's deadline, the Dashboard card for everyone who can open the desk, and next month's queue in the quiet zone. This PR is the rule and the kernel; the bar, the card and the clock are PRs 2 to 4.
