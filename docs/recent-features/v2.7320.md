# v2.7320 — Lien desk: the affidavit and retainage panes get the notice pane's grey section bars (2026-11-15)

The owner's ask of 2026-11-15, on Lien affidavits: the pane's section titles — *Before this affidavit can be generated (§ 53.052 · window from Aug 2026, the last month worked)*, *The owner's answers*, the bold *Months the affidavit claims* with its *Worked, not noticed* caption — were tiny muted captions inside bordered boxes, and "those section titles should look like the notice pane's grey bars across". Monthly notices has drawn its five sections under full-width grey sticky heads since v2.4733; the affidavit pane and the retainage pane, which has the same shape, now draw theirs the same way. Client only: no migration, no edge function, no CSS change.

## What changed

- **[`src/lib/jobs/lienAffidavitPaneSections.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienAffidavitPaneSections.ts)** (+7 tests): `lienAffidavitPaneSections(input)` answers the affidavit pane's heads in the notice pane's `{ key, label, fact, tail }` shape — *Where this affidavit is* (the timeline's next step), *Before it can be generated* (`✓ all five clear` or `✗ 2 missing`, the missing gates' short words from `lienAffidavitGateShortWords` as the tail, else *window from Aug 2026*), *The owner's answers* (`Pile A · owner still owes the GC`, *answers do not sort it yet* or *no call yet*; the bond's state as the tail), *Months the affidavit claims* (the months the paper swears, `lienAffidavitSwornMonths`, with the claim, and *N worked, not noticed* as the tail; drawn only when the pane draws the months), *The paper* (*short form* or *earlier form*, the signer as the tail). `lienAffidavitWindowWords` keeps the old caption's window sentence as the first line of the gates section. `lienRetainagePaneSections(input)` answers the retainage pane's three: *Before it can go* (the verdict; the retainage and the contract's end, or the missing gates short), *The § 53.056 claim* (*inside a recorded notice* / *not yet inside one*), *The paper* (*2 pages · cover letter + the form*). `LienPaneSection` took a key type parameter so `LienPaneHead` draws any pane's section unchanged.
- **[`src/components/jobs/useLienPaneHeads.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/useLienPaneHeads.tsx)**: the notice pane's head wiring (`paneHead(key)`, `litSection`, `goToSection`, `relightPane` — v2.4733, the strip's landing of v2.4726 and the phone rule of punch list #352) lifted into one hook, `useLienPaneHeads({ paneRef, sections, phone, strip?, resetKey? })` → `{ head, goTo, relight, onScroll, lit }`. `LienDesk.tsx` calls it with its strip (`stripH`, `paneScrolled`, `STRIP_COLLAPSE_PX`, the live read of `stripRef`) and `selected?.jobId` as the reset; its behavior is unchanged and its head smokes stay green. The two panes call it with no strip.
- **`LienDeskAffidavitPane.tsx`**: the pane is the scrolling box its heads stick in (`data-lien-affidavit-pane`, `position: relative`, the notice pane's `0 1.1rem` padding and one phone column), each section a `data-lien-pane-body` sibling of its head: the timeline (its box kept, as the notice pane keeps its own), the gates (the window line, then every gate row, the month check, the notice check and the claim line as they were), the owner's answers (`data-lien-affidavit-owner-answers` on the body), the months (`data-lien-affidavit-months`; the row grid, *Worked, not noticed* as a row-group caption, Set the months by hand and the sentence as they were; the rows are built once, above the JSX, so the head can count them), the paper (the earlier-form line and the white sheet). `boxHead` is gone.
- **`LienDeskRetainagePane.tsx`**: the same, with `data-lien-retainage-pane` as the box and the bodies `gates` (`data-lien-retainage-window` leads it), `claim` (`data-lien-retainage-in-claim` on the body; the tinted box is gone, the words and *The monthly notices on this job ›* stay) and `paper` (both pages). `boxStyle` and `boxHead` are gone.
- **`LienDesk.render.test.tsx`**: the affidavit pane's four heads in order with their facts and offsets, siblings of their bodies in the pane, the old captions gone, the doors kept, a press on the paper's head landing at its body under the stacked heads and lighting it; the phone's one pinned head, 40 px, in one column; the retainage pane's three heads, its captions gone, its doors kept. Three older smokes that read the box captions now read the heads.
