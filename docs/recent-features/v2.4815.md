# v2.4815 — Customer timeline: the doors (punch list #97, PR 3 of 3) (2026-10-07)

Client only. Punch list #97 ([`to-dos/customer-timeline/`](../../to-dos/customer-timeline/README.md)), on the window of v2.4813. Guide: *see everything that happened with a customer* (its *Open the timeline* section names every door).

## What changed

- **The GC's name on a Pipeline row** is a door to the GC's timeline (`renderGcTimelineDoor` in [`jobsStagesRowShared.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/jobsStagesRowShared.tsx), both customer lines; plain text where the window cannot open). It opens on the Timeline, since the Profile reads only the jobs a customer pays for. `StagesRowRenderContext.openCustomerProfile` takes the view.
- **The Pipeline search**: [`StagesCustomerTimelineChips`](../../src/components/jobs/StagesCustomerTimelineChips.tsx), one line in `JobsStagesTab` above the paid-search hint. When the search names one to three live customers (three or more letters), a chip per customer opens their timeline (`customerTimelineSearchMatches`).
- **The link** `?customerTimeline=<customer id>` on any page: `CustomerProfileModalProvider` reads it once (`readCustomerTimelineParam`), strips it with `replace` and opens the Timeline; anything but a customer id is stripped and opens nothing. **Copy link** in the timeline's title bar copies `appUrl(customerTimelineHref(id))`, the Pipeline with the timeline on top.
- **The Customer Hub** (`/customers/:id`): a **Timeline** button beside ✎ Edit customer.
- **show the days ›** on a crew card opens the job's hours story (`JobHoursStoryModal`, drawn by the timeline itself: its provider sits below the profile window's).
- The kernel folds three or more jobs made, or paid in full, on one day into one card (a GC's office opened seven service visits in one sitting on the live check), and a fold's list shows five lines with **show all N**.
- [`customerTimelineSearch.ts`](../../src/lib/customers/customerTimelineSearch.ts) holds the search, the link and its reader.
