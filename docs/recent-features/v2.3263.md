## Why

Wendi, 2026-09-10: a button that queues a robot to read her supply-house fixture quotes and build a best-price matrix, the way the robots spend compute on backtests — and teach it to put the WC-1 carrier with the WC-1 fixture. Owner decisions the same day: the quote PDFs live as links in Drive folders the twins already read; a **separate pricing twin** that never bids (so it can read a live bid's fixture list without touching the shadow blindness rule); the robot never sends, never writes costs, and asks instead of guessing where the plans decide.

The real quote reshaped the store. National Wholesale Supply S6277623 (SpaceX BA-2 Core & Shell, b359) prices every fixture as a **kit by subtotal** — "Subtotal ------- WC-1 & WC-2 EACH 1010.00" with the bowl, flush valve and seat listed but unpriced — sends **carriers to a second sheet** keyed by tag with eight Josam variants for one heading, lists **six RPZ sizes whose printed subtotal is the sum of the alternatives**, and prints an Amount Due ($72,291) that is one of everything plus tax, not a job total (her counts come to $92,420 on the 23 priced rows). None of that fits a one-line-one-price store.

## What shipped (no visible change yet)

- **`bid_quote_lines`** gains `component_role` (kit · bowl · seat · flush_valve · carrier · faucet · drain · trap · supply · stops · trim · mixing_valve · accessory · freight · loose), `label`, `option_group` / `option_label` / `option_chosen`, `page_ref`, `pick_reason`, `pick_source` (human | robot). **`bid_quotes`** accepts `source = 'robot'` and records `source_doc_url` + `robot_request_id`.
- **`bid_price_matrix_requests`** — one row per "price it with the robot": the fixture-row snapshot (`scope`), the folder links from the Price-requests table (`sources`), `status` queued → working → ready (blocked / cancelled / done), the claiming twin, heartbeats, and the robot's `result` for the card and the Scoreboard.
- **`fixture_component_rules`** (the pricer's rulebook, plain words + optional pattern/role, human- or robot-authored with a mirror note) and **`fixture_component_corrections`** (every move / unpick / repick / not-a-component / choose-option Wendi makes in the compare, with `remember` and the rule it minted).
- **`users.twin_kind`** — `estimator` (null reads as estimator) | `pricer`. PR 3's twin-mcp refuses a pricer every bid verb and the bid dispatchers never hand it a shell.
- **Kernel [`quoteKits.ts`](../../src/lib/rfq/quoteKits.ts)** — `aggregateKit` collapses one house's lines for one fixture into a cell: a `kit` line + separately priced components = one $/each; unpriced components ride the kit; a role any house quoted that this house neither priced nor covered makes the kit **incomplete** (no $/each, never ★); an option group with no `option_chosen` is **needs a choice** with the range, never summed. `expectedRoles` derives the must-have roles from every house's lines plus rule-required roles. `splitTagHeading` / `tagsInCommon` join "WC1&2" to "WC-1 & WC-2 WATER CLOSET", "RPZ" to "RPZ-1", "CO" to "FCO/CO". 12 tests, the NWS shape verbatim.
- **[`quoteCompare.ts`](../../src/lib/rfq/quoteCompare.ts)** calls the kit kernel per (house, fixture) whenever a quote is structured (several lines for one fixture, or any role / option group) and exposes the parts on `CompareRowCell.kit`; `unitPriceEachCents` is the kit's $/each so every existing reader — best-★, apples-to-apples totals, picked totals, Apply picks — keeps working unchanged. Plain quotes take the old path; the existing 10 compare tests pass untouched.
