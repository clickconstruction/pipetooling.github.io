# v2.5399 — Messages: the app’s own emails in a person’s thread, behind the scenes (2026-10-12)

Residential customers in Messages, R8 · 1 (the app's own emails in the thread), lifted from the Messages integrations mockups v0.2, card 1 (`to-dos/gc-mode/messages/customer-messages-integrations-mockups.html` on branch `spike/gc-mode`; the plan is `RESIDENTIAL_BUILD_PLAN.md` → *R8* beside it). A pure kernel and one change to cut 1's sent-copy lines. No screen, no migration, no edge function.

- **`src/lib/messages/sentLines.ts`** (cut 1's overlay, still wired in `messagesIo` as it was): a sent copy's line now reads its sender alone (*Rosa*) when a person pressed Send, and `APP_WHO` (*the app*) when nobody did. A copy is the app's when `sent_by` is null, which leaves `sent_by_name` empty. Before, a pressed send read *Rosa · the app*. `sentLineFacts` keys each copy's small print by its line's id (`sent:<id>`).
- **`src/lib/messages/appLines.ts`:**
  - `APP_WHO` and `isAppLine`. The flows' automatic texts fold too once they write that who.
  - `foldAppLines(lines)` folds each run of two or more app lines, with no line of a person's between them, into one row: `fold:<first id>`, the words (*3 emails, Oct 8 to Oct 10*, from `foldWords`) and the folded lines kept on it, so a press opens them in place. A lone app line stays a line. Day dividers stay the thread's own.
  - `SentCopyStamps` and `sentCopyFacts(row)`: the small print, one fact each and only those the row has: *from office@…*, *opened Oct 10, 6:12 PM*, *paid Oct 11 by card*. `sent_documents` holds none of the three, so the loader fills them: the address and the open from `email_send_log` by `resend_email_id`, the payment from the invoice that `source_table` and `source_id` name. Resend's webhook keeps only the last event, so a missing open means not known, never unopened. Each fact passes `plainWordsFailures`.
- **Tests:** `appLines.test.ts` over Dana on the sewer line (made-up rows: an estimate email by Rosa, the app's work scheduled email, its invoice email opened and paid, its paid email), and a new case in `sentLines.test.ts` for the who. Two breaks on purpose each failed a test: the lone line kept unfolded, and the day read in Chicago.
