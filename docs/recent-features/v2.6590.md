# v2.6590 — The day back: the Needs you item an Away note's countdown raises, with Bring back… on it (punch list #256, PR 9) (2026-11-06)

Punch list #256's ninth and last PR, the fourth of the Away note's four. PR 8 (v2.6587) lets the office switch on *Tell me when the countdown ends* on a teammate's Away note (`away_notes.alert_on_day`, off by default: a return date three years out is a hope, not a promise). This PR is what the switch does: on the day the countdown ends, and every day after until they are brought back, the Dashboard's Needs you card (and Quickfill's) lists the teammate, and the press opens their desk with the Bring back window already up.

## What changed

- **Kernel `src/lib/people/awayBackDay.ts`** (tests in `awayBackDay.test.ts`): `awayBackDayDue` keeps the rows whose day has come and who are still archived, oldest day first; `awayBackDayNeed` builds the item's words, one person by the Away note's own alert words (`awayAlertWords`, v2.6566: *Corinne Vela's countdown ended today.* / *They left Nov 3, 2026.*), the day named when it has passed, or the count and first names for several.
- **Hook `useAwayBackDay`**: reads `away_notes` where the alert is on and the day is today or past, the account's name and archived stamp through the `away_notes_user_id_fkey` embed (two FKs point at `users`, so the embed names its own), and the roster row's end date for the sub line; refetches on focus like its neighbours. Under RLS the people who may archive read the rows and anyone else reads none, so the line stays off for them.
- **Needs you**: source `awayBackDay` (the office set on both hosts), item `away-back-day` in the people tier (rank 30, blue, kicker *Away*, action *Bring back…*), door `/people?tab=users&person=u:<id>&flow=bring_back` for the first teammate, the archived list when none is known.
- **The desk deep link takes `&flow=bring_back`** (`PersonDeskDeepLinkHandler`), opening the desk with the Bring back window up, as Users → Archived's press does; the param is stripped with the others.
- Release note; the Bring back guide names the item under *On the day they are back*.

## Decisions

- **Every day after, not the day alone.** A Needs you item is a work queue, not a notification: it stays until Bring back takes the note down (restore-user deletes the row, v2.6587), so a day the office was away does not lose the reminder.
- **The office set reads the line**, the table's RLS decides who gets rows: a master who is not pay-approved reads none, which is the same answer the archive gate gives.
