# v2.7040 — Pay calendar: the job window and Call mode read the due day too (2026-11-12)

Punch list #323, the first half of PR 6, stacked on v2.7039. After PR 2 a bill for a payer on a Pay calendar read *Due Nov 10 · 2d past* on the board and *expect ~Dec 7* in its own job window, and Call mode judged the payer by a pace in days. Two screens a person moves between in one minute should not disagree, so these two move first.

## What changed

- **The job window's bills list.** `useJobBilledExpectedPay` loads the calendars and the accepted days beside the pay speeds (each fail-soft) and returns `billedPayModel`. `invoiceLedgerRow` words a `terms` model as the Pipeline row does: *due Dec 10*, *due Nov 10 · 2 d past* (late tone), and *27 d past expected · due Oct 10* once the money is later than even this payer usually is.
- **Call mode.** `buildPaymentChaseQueue` takes the calendars and the accepted days and reads `billedPayModel`, so a payer on a calendar is late against its due day plus its usual lateness; both builders pass them (`JobsStagesTab`, `StagesBilledMoneyModals`). The card's speed line reads the model's own words for such a payer (*lands ~6d after their pay day*), not *usually pays in ~41d*.
- **The Pipeline's money chip** (`usePipelineMoneyOpportunities`) loads the calendars and builds its count the same way, so *Ask when they'll pay* and the list it opens agree on who is late.
- **Guides**: *A customer on a pay calendar* in *chase late payments*, and a paragraph in *see when a customer will pay*.

## Not changed, and still on the pace guess

Money waiting (window and email), Dashboard Accounts Receivable, the Pay speeds breakdown's lists, the forecast email, the Bridge's Collect pile and cash runway and the Monday Bridge email. Each has a kernel of its own, most with a copy under `supabase/functions/_shared/`, so moving them is edge kernel copies, two payload functions and deploys; the Bridge part waits for punch list #318 to land. The card lists them under *Where it stands*.

Also still to do from the card: the Needs you line, the pay application's line, the phone's two-line rows for a bill not yet sent, GC Review's *expected pay by* box and the Pay speeds card's one-line hint.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **Half a PR.** The readers that are left are the ones that send mail, and a forecast email that disagrees with the board is the worst place for the two to differ. It is left because it cannot be done safely from here today: it changes two live payload functions and three deployed functions, one of which another session is rebuilding.
- **The Pipeline's money chip has no accepted days and no GC facts.** It counts a calendar payer by its due day, as Call mode does, but a bill with an accepted day, or a first-job GC, can still read one more or one fewer on the chip than in the list it opens. The GC half of that was true before this PR.
