# v2.3255 — Discount line items PR 3: the discount prints on every bill (2026-09-10)

Client + two edge functions (`create-stripe-invoice`, `preview-stripe-invoice` — redeploy both together); no migration. Design: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/7109f02d-bf06-4fff-a5f2-2f3c0bfdff0a (Mockup B's "what the customer gets").

## What shipped

**One kernel for every channel.** `discountLine.ts` moved to `supabase/functions/_shared/` (the app re-exports it, the stagePlan pattern), so the Stripe edge functions, the PDF/email builder, the Bill Customer previews and the Bill tab all split a discount the same way.

**Stripe** (`stripeInvoiceItemsFromFixtures.ts`, shared): the fixtures select carries `line_kind`, `discount_pct`, `discount_basis_positions`; `buildStripeInvoiceItemsFromFixtures` takes `allFixtures` (the whole job — shares split over the full basis). When the bill is exactly the scoped work minus those rows' discount shares, the items are the work at real prices plus **one negative item per discount** — *Negotiated discount (10%) −$1,509.80*, `source.kind = 'discount'` — and Stripe records it as a credit line. Any other target (a payment took a bite, a dollar carve) keeps the historical proration, now over **net** row cents, so the customer never sees a price the discount already lowered. `scopeFixturesToInvoice` never scopes a discount row as a line and reads work rows net, so the auto remainder still composes on a discounted job (edge and the client mirror `invoiceScopedFixtures.ts` agree, tested side by side).

**Physical PDF / email** (`physicalInvoiceLineItems.ts`, `physicalInvoiceDocument.ts`, `physicalInvoicePdf.ts`): `resolvePhysicalInvoiceLinePresentation` takes `allFixtures`; when the Services block at real prices plus the negative discount rows matches the bill, that is what prints (`breakdownMatches`), otherwise proration over net-priced rows. Negative amounts format as *−$1,509.80* (ASCII hyphen in the PDF font). `physicalPreviewRowsAreDbBacked` nets the discount lines so the Bill Customer physical preview stays editable per row; discount rows carry no edit ref (they are not editable there — the row on the Bill tab is).

**Previews**: `parseStripeLineSource` accepts `kind: 'discount'`; the pre-submit preview treats it as a plain (non-clickable) line.

**Tests**: 9 in `stripeInvoiceItemsDiscountLines.test.ts` — draw 1 of job 892 prints *Rough In $15,098.00 · Negotiated discount (10%) −$1,509.80* summing to $13,588.20; the three draws print the whole $3,774.50 exactly once; a bitten target prorates net with no discount line; a job without discounts builds byte-identically; the remainder bundle composes on net for edge and client; the PDF branch and the DB-backed check.
