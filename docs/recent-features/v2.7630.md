# v2.7630 — Box labels: a retired box's label names the box when you are signed in (2026-11-17)

Two small things the ZZ walk of the box page found on 2026-11-17 ([`to-dos/prts-box-page/README.md`](../../to-dos/prts-box-page/README.md), the section *The ZZ walk*), fixed on the owner's word the same day.

## 1. A retired box's own label, signed in

Signed out, a label from a retired box names the box and says it was retired. Signed in, the same label read *did not open a box* over a line that the box may be retired or on a job the reader is not on, so someone signed in read less than a stranger.

`resolveBoxCode` (`onSiteStore.ts`) asked for live boxes only (`retired_at is null`), so a retired box came back as no row, the same as a box out of the reader's reach. The read now keeps a retired box, a live one first. `codeLookup` (`stickers.ts`) answers the new verdict `retired_label` with the box, and the page ([`BoxCode.tsx`](../../src/pages/BoxCode.tsx)) says *Pile ZZ3 was retired* and *It left the job on Saturday, Nov 14. The office brings a retired box back from the Retired fold on its Materials page.* (`retiredLabelTitle`, `retiredLabelDetail`, the day in the app's time zone). The On site tab's own scan says the same name in its toast.

A retired box on a job the reader is not on still comes back as no row under their RLS, so `noBoxTitle` keeps its words for that: the page cannot tell which reason it is.

## 2. The tab's title

The Scan a label page ([`BoxScan.tsx`](../../src/pages/BoxScan.tsx)) set no title, so the tab read *ClickTooling*, where a box's own page reads the box and the company. It reads *Scan a label · Click Plumbing & Electrical* now.

Nothing in the app sets the title back, so a box's name stayed on the tab through the sign-in and every page after it. `useTabTitle` (`src/hooks/useTabTitle.ts`) names the tab and puts back the title it found when the page closes; the Scan a label page and the box page ([`BoxPublicPage.tsx`](../../src/components/materials/BoxPublicPage.tsx)) both use it.

## Release order

Client only. Nothing in the database or the functions changes.

## Test

Signed in on a job with a retired box, open `/b/<the retired box's code>`: the box's name, *was retired*, and the day. Signed out, open `prts.co`: the tab reads *Scan a label · Click Plumbing & Electrical*. Scan a box, press **Sign in to see what is inside**: the tab reads the app's name again on the sign-in page.
