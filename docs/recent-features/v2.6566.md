# v2.6566 — The Away note's kernel: the countdown, the miles, the city, the call and the signature (punch list #256, PR 6) (2026-11-05)

Punch list #256's sixth PR, the first of the Away note's four. PRs 1 to 5 (v2.6480 to v2.6526) bring a teammate back on the same account. While they are away, the owner's call 5 puts one screen behind a sealed sign-in: a note the office writes, a countdown to the day back, where the office is and how far that is from them, and the office line. Draft 2 of `away-note.html` puts the office's live preview beside the switches, drawn by the same component the person gets. This PR is that component's model, pure and tested, with nothing visible yet: the seal (PR 7) must ship and its probe pass before PR 8 lets any archived account sign in and draws the screen.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/people/awayNote.ts`** (tests in `awayNote.test.ts`):
  - `AwayNoteDraft` and `AWAY_NOTE_DEFAULT` (the city and the call on, the countdown off, the alert off, the owner's call); `awayNoteProblem` refuses a note over 600 characters, a day back that is not a date or not after today, and the alert with no day to count to; `awayNoteIsEmpty`.
  - `awayCountdown`: whole days, hours and minutes to midnight of the day back on the phone's clock, no seconds (the mock-up's second-pass finding: a number that ticks says nothing), `done` once the day has begun and after, and the bar's share of the way from the day they left (`pct`, `awayBarPercent` never under one); `awayCountdownLine` for a screen reader; `awayBackOnWords` (*Back on Monday, Nov 5, 2029*).
  - `awayMiles` from two points through `haversineMeters`: the crow's miles rounded to the mile, to five, then to ten (`roundAwayMiles`), *About 1,180 miles* or *Under a mile*, and the drive at a highway pace over long days (`awayDriveWords`: *Under an hour's drive.*, *About 3 hours of driving.*, *Roughly a two-day drive.*). The position never leaves the phone; the kernel takes two points and returns words.
  - `awayCityFromAddress` reads the city and state out of the office address (*12925 FM 20, Kingsbury, TX 78638* → *Kingsbury, TX*); `awayWhereWords`; `awayCallHref` makes the `tel:` and `sms:` links from the office line's digits, none without a number.
  - `awayHello`, `awayNoteHelp`, `awayFromLine` (*The team · updated Nov 3, 2026*: signed by the team, since whoever pressed Save may be gone in three years too), `awayAlertWords` for PR 9's Needs you item.
  - `buildAwayScreen`: everything the one screen draws from the note and the moment, for the preview and the person alike; `onTheDay` draws the day-of screen for the preview's *Today · On the day* switch; with nothing on, the resting line.

## Decisions

- **One model for two renders.** The preview and the sealed page will call `buildAwayScreen` with the same arguments, so what the office sees is what the person sees; the component (PR 8) draws a model and reads nothing.
- **The countdown ends at the start of the day back, on the phone's clock.** Their day, where they are, not the office's.
- **The miles are rough on purpose.** A pin and a position give the crow's distance; rounding it and calling the drive *roughly* keeps a number nobody should plan a trip by from reading as precise.
