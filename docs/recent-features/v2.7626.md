# v2.7626 — Prospects: the Activity tab is its own piece (2026-11-17)

Step 6 of the order in `docs/PROSPECTS_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md`: the first whole tab out of `src/pages/Prospects.tsx`. A pure move: no behaviour changes.

**The component** is `src/components/prospects/ProspectsActivityTab.tsx` (228 lines). Its JSX was cut out of the page by script on exact anchors. It holds the four pieces of state only this tab read (the 30 days' rows and their loading flag, the week report and its loading flag) and the two loaders that fill them.

**How it loads.** The page mounts the tab while it is the one on screen, for dev and the assistant-like. So it reads on entry, and again when the lane or the week changes: the same moments the page's two effects fired, which were keyed on the tab, the lane and the week.

**What it takes.** The lane and its words, the ZZ rule, the lane's rows for Source success (the page still loads them for a lane that is not Customers), and the week the pager is on with its setter.

**One state stayed on purpose.** `weekOffset` stays in the page. The tab unmounts when the caller leaves it; state inside it would put the pager back on this week on the way back, which the page does not do today. The render smoke pins that.

**The week's read** is `src/lib/prospects/prospectWeekReportIo.ts`: `loadProspectWeekReport(kind, offset)`, the four reads the page's `loadWeekReport` made, word for word, then `buildProspectWeekReport`.

**One sentence is drawn differently.** Source success with no rows says *Nothing added yet.* through `<EmptyState compact>` now. The house check on empty states reads a moved line as a written one and asks for it; the words are the same.

**Size.** `Prospects.tsx` went from 4,681 lines to 4,438.

**Proof.** The render smoke grew from 71 cases to 75: the week surviving a visit to another tab, a caller's week from the calls on record with the lane's landing word, an empty week with its press to the week before, and a lane's Source success with its counts. All 75 were run against the page before the move and after it, and pass both ways.
