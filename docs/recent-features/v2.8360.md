# v2.8360 — Before we start: the Schedule look says it is reading, and a No says when no phone rang (2026-11-23)

Punch list #382, the fourth of the faults the audit of PR 10 saw and left as they were, built on the owner's word of 2026-11-23 ("build it all"). Client only: `DevelopmentScheduleCard.tsx`, `JobModeArrivalCheck.tsx`, `lib/startChecks/startChecksIo.ts` and one word line in `lib/startChecks/arrival.ts`. No migration, no edge function.

## The two gaps

- **The Schedule look drew nothing while its reads were out.** `useSiteStartChecks` asks each job whether the reader is on its desk, then reads the lines and the rows. Until that landed the card was not drawn at all, so a desk reader on a slow signal could not tell a site with no stage lines from one still being read, and the look appeared from nowhere.
- **A push that failed after a No was dropped without a word.** `arrive` kept the arrival and Dispatch's line through `start_check_arrive`, told the inbox, and fired `notify-dispatch-request` with its failure swallowed. A dispatcher whose phone never rang was not on it, and the crew thought one was.

## The fix

- The card draws **Schedule** with *Reading the schedule…* while `read.loading` is true and the look has nothing to show yet. It then draws the cards, or leaves where this person is on no desk or no job has a stage line. `shows` is unchanged, so the strip of looks and `?look=schedule` behave as before.
- `arrive` awaits the push and answers `pushed` beside the line's id. The line is kept before the push, so a failed push loses nothing but the ring. The card's answered state adds *The line is in the inbox, but no phone rang. Call Dispatch too.* in amber under *Dispatch has it: N things not as told.* A **Yes, all as told** sends no line, so it says nothing about a phone.

## Checked

`DevelopmentScheduleCard.render.test.tsx` has one new test: the reading line while the first read is out, then the cards, and the same line then nothing for a reader on no desk. `JobModeArrivalCheck.render.test.tsx` has one new test: a No whose push failed says so, and a yes does not. The guide *say whether a site is as we were told* says what the card reads when no phone rang. 267 tests pass across the Schedule look, Job Mode and the start-checks kernels.

Not seen on production: the parent session owns live looks.
