# v2.3853 — Bids → Pricing: one place prices a scenario — the `scenarioPricingRows` kernel (2026-09-26)

Client only; no migration, no edge function, no screen change. Punch list **#46** (`to-dos/decomposition-queue.md`), row 1's first PR — Stage A of `docs/BIDS_PRICING_LABOR_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md`.

## Why

The scenario pricing adapter — a price scenario's assignments, custom prices and submission hides (all keyed by `price_book_version_id`) projected onto the count rows it prices, through `computeBidPricingRows` — was hand-written six times: four in `BidsPricingTab.tsx` (`packageRowsFromInputs` for Share / print / CSV, the workbench cards' effect, the alternates' `altVersionData` effect, `copyPricesIntoViewedScenario`) and twice in `useBidPricingRows` (the package source and the grid). Two of the copies shipped bugs of the same shape: v2.3685 (every Share row at \$0.00 when an alternate ★ priced against the on-screen rows) and v2.3841 (every other version's card at \$0 in the legacy fallback). The map ranked it first of the untested money paths: ~60 lines, zero state, and it unblocks the Quote-desk and Workbench cuts.

## What shipped

- **[`src/lib/bids/scenarioPricingRows.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/scenarioPricingRows.ts)** — `scenarioPricingRows({ scenarioId, countRows, entries, assignments, customPrices, hides?, costs? })` → the pricing kernel's result; `scenarioRevenue(...)` (the cover-letter sum, what the cards and alternates show); `scenarioPackageRows(result)` (the Package-and-send row shape with the total); `scenarioCustomPriceMap` and `scenarioAssignments`, the two projections on their own. Rules kept from the copies: only the scenario's own overlay rows count (another scenario's assignment, custom price or hide is ignored whatever the caller loaded; entries carrying a `version_id` are filtered the same way); the count rows are the caller's — a scenario on another bid version prices on that version's rows, never the on-screen ones; `costs` is the grid's cost side (labor rows, materials, rate, tax, takeoff materials, the applied-quote override) and leaving it out means prices only — `PRICES_ONLY` — which is what every copy that passed zeros meant.
- **The six call sites converge**: the tab's `packageRowsFromInputs` (with costs), the `altVersionData` effect (`scenarioRevenue`), `copyPricesIntoViewedScenario` (prices only on the source's own rows; its reads are already scoped, so the filter is a no-op there); `scenarioCardRevenues` (v2.3841) keeps its own-rows lookup and calls `scenarioRevenue` per card; `useBidPricingRows`' `pricingPackageSource` and `pricingRowsForGrid` (both with costs, the override map included). The tab loses its `computeBidPricingRows`, `coverLetterTotalsFromPricingRows` and `submissionHiddenIdsForVersion` imports.
- **Tests** — `scenarioPricingRows.test.ts` (10): the scenario on its rows; an alternate ★ on its own rows against the on-screen rows (the caller's responsibility, stated); a custom price on a row with no assignment, another scenario's ignored; an assignment's override over the entry and a fixed price as one line; a hidden row omitted and still in revenue, another scenario's hide ignored; entries with no `version_id` all taken; prices only by default (no cost, hours or materials on any row); the cost side flowing through (takeoff materials taxed); the package rows; the two projections alone. `scenarioCardRevenues.test.ts` (5) unchanged and green through the new path.
- **Docs** — the map's untested-money item 1 and the two tables say done; the queue's row 1 and its `next` (the `loadScenarioInputs` half comes next).
