# v2.8423 — Bills sent: a bill with nothing left out reads Paid (2026-11-23)

Found on the Bills sent walk of 2026-11-23 (punch list #422). No migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

`billPaidState` called a bill paid only when its status was `paid`. A billed bill with money on it was part paid, whatever the money added to. A check typed in and held until it clears leaves the bill billed with its whole amount on record, so the line read *Part paid* with *$0.00 out*, the bill counted in the **Not paid** tile and under the **Not paid** chip, and its day's band counted it as not paid with nothing still out. On By day paid the same payment read *Part of the bill. The bill is still open.*

## What changed

- `billPaidState`: money on record that leaves nothing out has paid the bill. The bill's status is not changed; the page reads the rows. A bill with no amount on record cannot be covered, and a cent short is still part paid.
- `billsPaidLines`: a payment is part of its bill only while the bill is billed and the payment does not cover it alone.

## Default chosen

A check held until it clears reads *Paid* with its way, *Check · typed by hand*. The page does not say the check has not cleared, because it does not read that.

`billsPaid.test.ts`: one check that covers a billed bill, two payments that add to it, one that runs over, a cent short, no amount on record. `billsPaidDays.test.ts`: a payment that covers its bill is not part of it.
