# v2.8332 — Dashboard: one row at the top switches between Task Mode, the Dashboard and Job Mode (2026-11-23)

Punch list [#425](../../to-dos/job-mode-offer/README.md). Client only: no migration and no function.

## The ask

The owner, 2026-11-23, with a phone screenshot of the green card *Working in the field? Turn on Job Mode.*: he likes that it is on top of many people's Dashboard, but nobody reads its small text. How could it work for someone impulsive?

The small grey text was the only place the card said how to get back. **Turn on Job Mode** swapped the whole page and the way back was in the gear menu. **Not now** removed the offer from that phone for good. Four ways were drawn (`to-dos/job-mode-offer/mockup.html`). He picked the switch with no card, and turned it: the switch lives above the top of the page, a person who scrolls all the way up can pull it down, and Task Mode is offered too, on the left, since it is pressed least. A second drawing let him try three ways to hide the row (`switcher-mockup.html`). He picked **Tucked**.

It was first built for the four field roles, with the office left on its gear menu. Asked whether the office should get the row too, he said *give it to everyone*.

## What changed

- **The mode row.** `ModeRow` (`src/components/layout/ModeRow.tsx`) draws **Task Mode · Dashboard · Job Mode** as three thirds, the first line above the header, inside an `AppTopBanner`. The third of the mode drawn is filled. It is drawn for every role whose first page is the Dashboard, which is all nine but the estimator (`modeRowOfferedToRole`), on the Dashboard in any mode and on the checklist while Task Mode is on.
- **The rule** is `src/lib/modeRow.ts`, pure, with every word on the row. A press goes through `usePageMode`'s `set`, the one door that switches a mode. Task Mode and Job Mode each switch on. The middle third switches both off and never writes Dispatch Mode, so a leader or the office keeps the Dispatch Mode bar and the third reads **Dashboard** for both.
- **Tucked on a phone.** The row shows on the first five opens of the page, or until a third is pressed. An open counts once the row has stayed on the page for a second and a half (`MODE_ROW_OPEN_DWELL_MS`): the office's phone opens on the Dashboard and is sent on to the schedule at once, and five of those would have tucked a row nobody had seen. After that the page opens with the row out of sight and a 24 px handle in its place. A pull down at the top of the page, or a tap on the handle, brings the row down for that visit. Leaving the page or putting the phone away tucks it again. The row also shows after every switch, so the way back is where the thumb is.
- **Two guards hold it open.** In Task Mode the row is the way out, as the green strip was, so it never tucks there. A sub or a helper who is off Job Mode keeps it in sight until they are back on it, since Job Mode is their first screen.
- **On a desk** the row always shows, at 32 px.
- **Gone:** `DashboardJobModeFirstRunCard`, its smoke, `showJobModeFirstRunCard` and the put-away key. A phone that stored `'card'` still reads Job Mode on.
- **Out of the gear menu** (the owner, the same day: *since we're adding this, we can remove the job mode and task mode from the gear wheel dropdown*): a role with the row has no **Job Mode** or **Task Mode** row in the gear menu, nor in the phone dock's More sheet. `gearModesRows` (`src/lib/gearMenuSections.ts`) is the rule. Whoever can hold Dispatch Mode (a dev, a leader, an assistant, the controller) keeps **Dispatch Mode** and its PO tab under *Modes*; a primary, a superintendent, a sub and a helper have no *Modes* group at all, and no label over nothing.
- **Kept for the estimator:** an estimator who opens the app lands on Bids, since `Layout`'s route guard sends `/` to `roleHomePath`. Only a fresh sign-in lands an estimator on the Dashboard, and the desk header's Home icon goes there too. So the row would sit on a page the estimator opens now and then. The gear menu's Job Mode and Task Mode rows, the *Task Mode · Exit* strip and **Show full dashboard** at the foot of the Job Mode page are drawn for the estimator alone. The owner saw the row drawn on top of Bids, with **Bids** as its middle third, and left the estimator out (2026-11-23).
- **The look at another person's Task Mode** (`TaskModeLookWindow`, Checklist → Review) draws the top of that person's screen as it is now: the three thirds with Task Mode filled (`ModeRowThirds`, with no press) for a role that has the row, the green strip for the estimator, and neither until the person's role is read.
- The Dashboard clears its *full page for this visit* flag whenever Job Mode goes off, so switching back on opens the Job Mode card.

## How the pull differs from the drawing

The drawing tucked the row by opening the page scrolled just past it. The build does not. The header is not sticky, and on a Home Screen iPhone the first line of the page pays the status bar. A page that opens scrolled would put the header under the clock, and every jump to the top of a page would land on the row.

So the tucked row is not on the page at all. `index.css` already sets `overscroll-behavior-y: none`, which leaves a downward drag at the top of a page with nothing to do. `ModeRow` listens for that drag, 36 px of it (`modeRowPull`), and for a wheel turned up there. A drag that starts inside something scrolled, on a map or while a window holds the page still is left alone. To the thumb it is the same move: at the top, pull down.

## Usage rows

`ui_nav_clicks`: control `mode_row` with target `#task`, `#full` or `#job` for a press, and `mode_row_shown` with `#pull` or `#handle` for a tucked row brought down. Job Mode switched on from the row records `job_mode_enabled` with `#row`.

## Defaults the owner did not rule on

Two calls were drawn with a default and built that way. Each is one line in `modeRow.ts`.

- The row starts to tuck after the first press or five opens: `MODE_ROW_FIRST_VISITS`.
- The handle is drawn: `MODE_ROW_HANDLE`.

The owner ruled on who gets the row: everyone, and then the estimator left out, for the reason above.

## Looked at

Walked on the dev server through the lens frame, over invented data, at a phone's width (390 px and 320 px) and a desk's (1280 px), in the light and dark looks.

- **A leader on a phone:** ten presses in a row across the three thirds each landed on the page they named, with the stored values to match and Dispatch Mode's value never written. The gear menu listed Dispatch Mode and neither other mode.
- **The tuck:** leaving the Dashboard and coming back left only the 24 px handle. A real pull by touch events, a wheel turned up and a tap on the handle each brought the row down.
- **What must not pull:** a drag with the page scrolled, a drag that went up first, one that went sideways first, a short one, two fingers, and a drag under the open Clock In window. None brought the row down.
- **A sub at 320 px:** three thirds of 100 px by 44 px with no sideways scroll. After **Dashboard** the row held open through a trip to another page. After **Job Mode** the same trip tucked it. No *Modes* group in the gear menu and no **Show full dashboard**.
- **A superintendent:** the green *New* on Job Mode until the first press, the Job Mode tabs after it, the checklist with the row and no strip in Task Mode, and the Dashboard again from there.
- **A leader on a desk:** the row always showing at 32 px, no handle, still there after a trip away.
- **An assistant on a phone:** the app sent the phone to the schedule on opening, and that open was not counted. On the Dashboard the row showed, and its open was counted after it had stayed. Eight presses in a row each landed on the page they named. Task Mode showed the row and no green strip. A trip away tucked the row, and the handle brought it back. The gear menu listed Dispatch Mode and neither other mode.
- **A controller and a dev on a desk:** the row at 32 px above the header, Task Mode and Job Mode and back, and Dispatch Mode alone under *Modes*.
- **A primary, in the dark look:** the row, and no *Modes* group in the gear menu.
- **An estimator:** no row on the Dashboard, both rows in the gear menu, the green strip in Task Mode and its Exit.

**Live, after the deploy:** the same walk through the lens frame on the deployed app, all nine roles, at 320 px, 390 px and 1280 px, in the light and dark looks, with a real wheel as well as touch events. Every press landed on the page it named, each gear menu held what it should, and no request left the frame. An estimator who opened the app landed on the Bid Board with no row. The frame caught one console error once, for a superintendent switching to Job Mode, and none in six more switches. The invented world has no answer for several Job Mode reads, the likely source.

The lens frame empties the phone's memory on every load, so the tuck on the sixth open with no press is held by the tests alone. The invented world has nobody under Review, so the look at another person's Task Mode is held by its smoke alone. It was not tried on a real phone. The pull under a thumb, and the row under a Home Screen iPhone's status bar, are what is left.
