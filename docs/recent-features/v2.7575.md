# v2.7575 — Materials on site: Where is it? finds a cage or box and draws the way to it (2026-11-17)

Punch list #372, *Find a lost box*, step 2 of 5. The owner's ask of 2026-11-16: *show someone where their crate is if it is lost and draw a path to where they can find it*. He confirmed on 2026-11-17 that a crate is a cage or a box from Materials on site, and said *Build all the steps*. The mock-up is [`to-dos/find-a-lost-box/mockup.html`](../../to-dos/find-a-lost-box/mockup.html). Step 1 (v2.7573) gave the pin its day, its person and its margin. This is the sheet that reads them.

## What ships

One body, [`WhereIsBox.tsx`](../../src/components/materials/WhereIsBox.tsx), over the pure kernel [`whereIsBox.ts`](../../src/lib/materials/whereIsBox.ts).

- **The words.** The place and the where note, then the pin: *Pinned on Nov 12 by Marcus Reyes, within 20 ft.* Then who counted the box last. A box nobody pinned leads with that, then says where it stands and who put it there. The names here are made up.
- **The newest photo** of the box, when there is one.
- **The site from above** (`OnSiteMap`): the box's pin in red, the other pinned boxes of its place faint, the job's painted maps under them.
- **Show where I am.** The phone is followed with `watchPosition` while the sheet is open. The map draws the reader's dot, the phone's margin as a ring and a dashed straight line to the pin. The sheet says *About 240 ft north-east of you. Your phone is sure within 25 ft. The line is straight. It knows no fence, wall or gate.* Inside about 30 ft it says *You are at the pin.* A margin past about 150 ft turns the words to *You may be indoors. The line is a rough guide here.* Nothing is sent.
- **Directions** opens the phone's maps app at the pin. With no pin it reads **Directions to the site** and goes to the job's address.
- **I found it.** One reading, one pin, with the phone's margin and the presser's name. A reading the phone is not sure of within about 150 ft is refused in words, with **Try again** and **Move the pin by hand**. After a pin the sheet asks *Is A7 still at Laydown yard?* with **Yes, same place** and **Move box**.
- **Move the pin by hand.** The same map with the ring at its centre, **Use my location** and **Put A7 here**. A pin put by dragging carries no margin. **Remove the pin** is here too.
- **An old pin.** Past 30 days: *That pin is 6 weeks old. Boxes get moved.* The pin turns amber on the map.

## Where it sits

- **The phone.** The middle chip on the box screen reads **Where is it?**, pinned or not, in place of *Pin on map* and *On the map*. The line under the box's name says *pinned Nov 12* where it said *pinned on the map*. The site map's **Pin a box…** still opens the older placing sheet, retitled *Pin A1 on the map*, and that sheet now sends the phone's margin too.
- **The office.** **Where is it?** beside **Edit** on a box's head in Jobs → Materials opens the box's window on a third tab of that name. **Move box** there goes to the Cage tab, whose form holds the place.

## What differs from the card

- **The distance uses a task place's words, with a direction.** The card planned a painted line's *give or take*. That is about a length that was walked. This is one point to another, which `taskPlaceHowFarWords` already says.
- **The office gets a tab, not a window of its own.** The box already had a window with two tabs.
- **The photo is only as good as today's photos.** A box photo's bytes stay on the phone that took it until Drive files them, and the app draws no picture from Drive. On another phone the newest photo shows its name and who took it, with no picture. That is how box photos read everywhere today.

## Walked

On a dev server against the live database, in a headless Chromium at 390 wide and at desk width, as the dev account, with a made-up location, on a test job's test cage. The migration was already on the live database. The sheet opened on a box nobody had pinned. **I found it** pinned it, and the sheet read *Pinned today by* the presser, *within 20 ft*. **Show where I am** from 240 ft away drew the dot, the ring and the dashed line and read *About 240 ft north of you.* A margin of about 260 ft read *You may be indoors.* **Move the pin by hand** opened the ring, and **Remove the pin** took the pin off. The office's tab opened from **Where is it?** beside **Edit**. No request failed and nothing ran off the side of the phone.

Three things the walk changed. **I found it** uses the reading already on the screen while the dot is on, since asking the phone a second time took several seconds. The photo has a line over it, *The newest photo, from Nov 10.* A box with no place said so twice after a pin, and now says it once.

Dark mode was not looked at: the walk's browser stayed light.
