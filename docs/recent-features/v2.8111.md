# v2.8111 — Before we start: the Schedule look on a line of time, and the days they gave on paper (2026-11-20)

Punch list #382, PR 11 of eleven, without the dev seat's verb. Client only: no migration, no edge function, nothing to push or deploy. Finished from the parked draft on `claude/382-line-of-time`.

## The line of time

On the Development page's Schedule look, a word press over the cards: **Show it on a line of time** / **Hide the line of time**. It is shut until asked for, so the first screen is still the sentences and never a chart.

`ScheduleTimeLine` draws what `timeLine` (`src/lib/startChecks/timeLine.ts`, pure) works out: one row a stage ahead, in the cards' own order (`byTrouble`).

- A bar for our days, in the stage's colour.
- A solid diamond on the day they say ready, a hollow one on each day they gave before.
- A dotted line on the day we decide.
- A red line on today, down every row, named in the legend.
- *moved 11 days* in red beside the row's marks: after the last one, or before the first when the row ends at the right edge (`movedAt`).
- Each row is one picture whose name is its sentence: *Top Out, JP2101: Dec 8 to 12. They say ready Dec 12. We decide by Dec 6. They have moved 11 days.*

The span opens on a Monday, covers every day drawn and today, and is never under 21 days. Every place is a share of the span.

## The days they gave, on paper

**Print the days they gave**, beside the link, for whoever may set the notice (the office). `movedSheet` gathers each stage where they gave a day, and `movedSheetHtml` is the sheet, printed through `printHtmlInNewWindow`: the site, who gave the days, the total (*13 days moved across 2 stages.*), then a table a stage with **Ready on**, **Said on**, **Who**, **How** and **Moved**.

It is the office's record. Its foot says so: *An office record. It is not sent to the customer.* The GC's page never shows a day they gave before or the days they moved (the owner's call 6, on its default).

## What changed from the draft

Four faults a walk at desk and phone width found, each with a test:

- **The days they moved did not show.** They were written after the job's name, which is cut short at every width. They are now inside the row's own picture.
- **Today was a thin grey line with no name.** It is red, and the legend names it.
- **A diamond was lost on a bar or on the day we decide.** It wears a ring of the surface.
- **On a phone the line was 520 px in a scrolling box, with its legend cut.** A row's name now sits over its track, the span fits the screen with no sideways scroll, every other week mark is left out, and the legend wraps.

## Where it departs from the mock-up

The mock-up's link is under the cards. Here it is over them. Two reasons: a site can hold dozens of stages, and at the foot of the page the app's own *Tell the dev* press covers that corner at desk width.
