# v2.3827 — Show a property owner their bills when the GC pays (2026-09-25)

Punch list #45, PR 1 (`to-dos/owner-portal-property-view/`). The owner, 2026-09-25, on Umar Khan's portal reading *"You're all paid up"* with $30,387 open on 9703 Lenox Hl, asked for *"a checkbox next to his portal link on jobs stages row"*, covering the whole property (their call the same day). Sharing already existed (v2.3375, Share this bill), but it was unusable for this. The job memory `show_bills_to_other_party` stamps only the **next** bills (`shownToPartyFor`: a stamped invoice answers for itself), and a bill's `shown_to_party` was ticked one bill at a time at Bill Customer. All 12 jobs at Lenox Hl and 628 Terrell Rd were off. Client only, no migration.

## The change

- **Kernel** `src/lib/jobs/ownerBillShare.ts` (new, 12 tests):
  - `ownerShareApplies`: a GC-billed job (`effectiveInvoiceParty(job, null) === 'gc'`) whose customer is a different party.
  - `ownerShareBills`: its open GC bills (`PORTAL_OPEN_INVOICE_STATUS`, not sent to a typed address).
  - `ownerShareState`: *on* (memory set and every open GC bill stamped `customer`), *off*, or *partly*.
  - `ownerSharePropertyState` combines the jobs' states.
  - `ownerShareScope`: every job at the same `customer_address_id` with the same customer; a job with no saved property stands alone.
  - `ownerShareWrites`: which job memories and which invoice stamps a flip writes. Off clears only `customer` stamps on open bills; a paid bill stays in their history.
  - `ownerShareChipWords` gives the chip's text.
- **IO** `ownerBillShareIo.ts`: `loadOwnerShareProperty` reads the property's jobs and open bills; `applyOwnerShare` updates `jobs_ledger.show_bills_to_other_party` and `jobs_ledger_invoices.shown_to_party`. These are the columns and RLS that Edit Job and the Bill tab already write.
- **Chip** `OwnerShareChip.tsx`, beside the customer's 🌐 in `renderJobCustomerLine` (Pipeline and every stages list that renders the customer line):
  - It reads *☐ owner sees $0*, *☑ owner sees the bills* or *owner sees some bills*.
  - The popover reads the property and asks *Show Umar Khan the bills at 9703 Lenox Hl?*, listing each job with its open bills (*no bill yet — shows once billed*, with money only on billed jobs). Then *Show them* or *Stop showing*.
  - After a flip it reloads the jobs. Office roles only, as the 🌐 is.
- **Portal window** `PortalOwnerShareSection.tsx` in `CustomerPortalGlobeButton`: *On <owner>'s jobs, billed to someone else*, one line per property with the chip (state from the property). The live preview iframe is keyed and re-reads after a flip.
- **Guide**: *share a customer their portal* gains *When the GC pays: show the owner their property's bills*.

## Verified

`ownerBillShare.test.ts` (12, on the Lenox Hl shape). The portal-window and stages suites are green. Live on the Pipeline 2026-09-25, read-only: 7 chips, all *owner sees $0*; the popover for 173 Atlantis read the owner, the address, the GC and the job, and nothing was applied. Found live and fixed: the words had assumed *he / his* (now *they / their*), and a job with no bill showed its contract value.
