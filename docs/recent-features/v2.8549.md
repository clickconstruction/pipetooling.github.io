# v2.8549 — Final demand letter: the amount box drops its small Pay online label (2026-11-24)

Punch list #333, the owner's look at the letter as *What customers see* draws it. His words, 2026-11-24: *My only comment is the "pay online" words on this particular final demand letter could be removed, reducing the total height.* No migration, no edge function.

## What changed

`src/lib/jobsDocuments/demandLetter.ts`, the amount box's third cell, which holds the letter's one pay code:

- **The page and the email** (`amountBox` in the HTML): the small grey *Pay online* line over *Scan to pay* is gone. The code, *Scan to pay* and *or go to* with the address stay. Where the cell is narrow the words sit under the code, as in the Settings preview at 390 px and in an email on a phone, and the box is one line shorter there. Where the words sit beside the code, the code sets the height and the box is as tall as it was.
- **The PDF**: the same line is gone, and the two lines left are drawn a line higher. The box is 26 mm, which the 20 mm code sets, so nothing below it moves and the letter is signed on page 1 as before.

## What did not change

- The plain text of the email still reads *Pay online:* and the address. It has no box.
- A letter with several codes still has its *Pay online* heading over them, on the account's page. That is a different block (`payCodes`), and the owner's words were about the box.
- No other letter draws this box. `demandLetter.ts` is the final demand letter alone, so no other paper changed.
- The letter is built in the app. No edge function draws the box, so nothing is owed after the merge.
