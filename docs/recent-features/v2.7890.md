# v2.7890 — Pricing calculator: Put on a row… waits for the row before it shows a change (2026-11-18)

A fix to the Percent row window as the calculator's **Put on a row…** opens it (v2.7857), seen on the deployed site on a test bid and asked for by the owner on 2026-11-18.

## What was wrong

Opened with 900 on the display and no row picked yet, the window drew a made-up row: the name *the row*, a count of 1, a kind picker, $900.00 a unit. Its top line read *Bid revenue $15,375.00 → $16,275.00* and its dead press read *Add at $900.00*. Nothing could be saved in that state, since the press holds with *Pick the row first.*, but every word on it described a new row that this press never makes.

## What ships

- `percentWindowPreview` returns `awaitingPick`: the number goes on a row already here and none is picked. While it is true the bid's revenue after is the revenue before, and the press reads *Set a row to $900.00*, or *Set a row* with no answer yet.
- `PercentRowWindow` draws that state as a row still to pick: the caption *The row, as Pricing will show it*, the muted words *Pick a row below*, dashes for count, price per unit and margin, no kind picker, and the number waiting under Revenue.
- Once a row is picked nothing differs from v2.7857.

## Tests

The pick-a-row case in `percentRow.test.ts` now pins the unchanged revenue and both press labels. The Put on a row… case in `PercentRowWindow.render.test.tsx` pins the drawn state, and fails against the old window.
