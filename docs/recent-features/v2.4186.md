## What shipped

The paper then shows two frames (*Sam signs here · Alex signs here*); the unsigned PDF prints two pairs of pen rules, each with its name; the customer's page shows a frame per signer. Either may sign first — the page asks *Who is signing now?* only while both frames are open — and the row reads **signed** (with `signed_at`, the stored PDF and the emails) only when both frames are filled. Until then the chip and the pill say *1 of 2 signed*, the sent rail says who it is waiting on, and reminders keep going to the recipient.

## Decided here

- **The first frame keeps its columns.** Renaming `signer_*` to a frames table would touch every reader of a signed row (the coverage kernel, the chips, the rail, three functions, the customer page); a second column set is additive and every old row reads as before.
- **`signed_at` is the agreement's, not a frame's.** Coverage, the Documents page and the reminders lane all key on `status`/`signed_at`; a half-signed agreement stays *sent*, keeps its link alive and keeps its reminders. The first frame's own stamp is its consent time.
- **Either frame first, one link.** A shared phone at the kitchen table, or a forwarded email, signs both; a second token per signer would mean a second send flow. The nudge email carries the same link.
- **The paper names the second signer; the rail does not ask.** It is a fact about the agreement, so it lives on the paper beside the frame it creates — the rail stays one question.
