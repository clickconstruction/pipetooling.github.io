# v2.3852 — Render smokes settle before they assert: one helper, one rule (2026-09-26)

Test harness + three render smokes + docs. No source component, no migration, no edge function, no user-facing surface. Punch list **#39**, PR 1 of 2 (`to-dos/render-tests-settle-first.md`); PR 2 is the sweep.

## Why

Three `*.render.test.tsx` files went red in six days on branches that never touched the component under test, each passing alone or on a re-run: the Accounts Receivable close-out smoke (v2.3551, 2026-09-17 — the seeded select read before the seeding effect landed), the PO generator smoke (PR #3575, 2026-09-22 — the editors' mount-effect reset landed after the click's `setOpen(true)`; it ejected #3565 and #3568 from the merge queue), and the Job window's History tab (v2.3771, 2026-09-23 — the lazy pane's Suspense reveal queued behind the first-load cascade on a Mac at load average in the hundreds).

One shape: `renderWithProviders` resolves its mocks outside `act`, so when a `findBy*` on a container resolves the component has painted **once**, with its effects, Suspense retries and effect-seeded state still pending. A synchronous read or click on the next line races that work — and wins on an idle machine, loses under a full suite or a busy queue. Three fixes used three idioms (`waitFor` on a value; `act(async () => {})` then `waitFor`; await a loaded marker), all correct, none shared, and no doc stated the rule.

## What shipped

- **`src/test/renderSmokeMocks.tsx`** — `settle()` (`await act(async () => {})`, the #3575 flush) and `renderSettled(ui, { loaded })`: `renderWithProviders`, then `settle()`, then `await` the caller's loaded marker — a `findBy*` on a value the load paints, a `waitFor` on a seeded field, the markers a sibling test awaits — returning the render result with the marker's value on `loaded`. The rule is in the harness header: *assert on something the data load produces; never read or click on the line after `render()` or after a `findBy*` on a container; never widen a `waitFor` timeout to hide the race — wall time on a loaded machine measures load, not code.*
- **The three fixes re-expressed through the helper**, every assertion kept: `BankPaymentsModal.closeOut.render.test.tsx` (`openWithSuggestedReason` is one `renderSettled` whose marker waits for the strip and the seeded select), `MaterialsPoGeneratorTab.render.test.tsx` (`await settle()` before the click), `JobWindowModal.render.test.tsx` (T5-05 renders through `renderSettled` awaiting the Job pane's header button and the form's name field; `renderWindow` now wraps a `windowUi` element so both paths share the props).
- **`docs/AI_CONTEXT.md`** — one clause on the render-smoke sentence naming the rule and the helper.

Not built, on purpose: a lint rule. The pattern is a line-pair heuristic; PR 2's sweep plus the helper make the rule the default without a false-positive fight.

## Verified

- The three files (13 tests) green; eslint on the four files; typecheck.
- `npm test` in full twice back to back on a Mac whose load average ran from 85 to 864 across the two runs (the condition the flakes need): 1,670 files / 13,811 tests green both times, 75 s then 169 s.
