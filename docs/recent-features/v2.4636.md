# v2.4636 — Help: the lien release guide says who signs, as it works now (2026-10-05)

The last piece of the lien-guide freshness pass ([v2.4614](v2.4614.md), [v2.4617](v2.4617.md), [v2.4620](v2.4620.md) and [v2.4623](v2.4623.md)). ## The fixes

- **Signed by.**
  - The pick is the company's signer from Settings → Jobs & billing when that name matches a master, else the job's leader (`LienReleaseModal.tsx`, the masters load).
  - Since v2.4567 the *Signs* pick in step 5 starts fresh each time the window opens. A saved draft keeps its own Signed by line.
  - The signer's title fills in only when he is the company's signer, and the window's *His title* field takes one by hand.
- **He is here, he signs now.**
  - On someone else's screen the pad takes a drawn signature only (`LienReleaseSignModal.tsx`, `allowTyped={!present}`).
  - The person signed in signs with *✍ Sign it now*, and the pad offers *Type it instead* (`LienWaiverSignOnPage.tsx`).

## Two names the rename overtook

[v2.4630](v2.4630.md) renamed the desk's views *Do now*, *Deadlines* and *All filings*.
