# v2.8442 — Bills sent: a part-paid bill's record draws its cents small (2026-11-24)

One fault of the Bills sent page found on the live test of 2026-11-24 (punch list #422, problem 4). No migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

A bill's record says where the bill stands on the line under its amount: *Part paid, $1,620.00 still out*. The line was the kernel's sentence drawn as plain text, so its cents stood as tall as its dollars. Every other amount on the page, 513 of them on the live list, draws its cents smaller.

## What changed

`RecordPanel` in `BillsSentLedger.tsx` draws the line through `MoneyText`, the door for words a kernel already wrote. The words are the same (`billsSentRecord`'s `stateWords`), so the printed record and a copy of the text read as before.

## Tests

`BillsSentLedger.render.test.tsx`: a part-paid bill's record reads *Sable Ridge Builders · Part paid, $1,620.00 still out* and holds the cents in their own span. The case failed before the change with no such span.

## Not looked at

The record was not drawn in a browser. The live screen was not walked again.
