# v2.6061 — The lien affidavit swears the real period of the work, not one day (2026-10-29)

The § 53.054 affidavit is a sworn, notarized paper. Its paragraph 5 states the period in which the claimant furnished labor and materials: *from `workStart` through `workEnd`*. The draft that filled those fields (`buildLienAffidavitFieldsForJob` in `src/lib/jobs/lienNoticeDraft.ts`) set both to the same day, the 28th of the last month worked, so every affidavit the Lien desk drafted swore a single day. The job window's own Mechanic's lien tab (`LienFilingTabs.tsx`) did the same with the job's last work day. Noticed 2026-10-29 while exploring punch list #212 (the signed hours statement); no affidavit has yet been filed from the desk's draft, so nothing on record is wrong.

## What changed

- **A first day on the job.** Migration `20261029190000_jobs_ledger_first_work_date.sql` ([doc](../migrations/20261029190000_jobs_ledger_first_work_date.md)) adds `jobs_ledger.first_work_date`, the earliest approved clock day, kept by the same trigger that keeps `last_work_date` (`refresh_jobs_ledger_last_work_date` now reads MIN and MAX in one pass; the three `clock_sessions` triggers are unchanged), with a backfill over the jobs that have an approved session.
- **The span kernel** `src/lib/jobs/lienWorkSpan.ts` (`lienWorkSpan(job, months)`, pure, 7 tests): the end is the later of the day set by hand and the hours' last day (the RPC's rule since v2.4676), else the last day of the last month known, today while that month runs; the start is the first approved day, else the first of the first month known, else the first of the end's month. Nothing known prints the paper's blanks. `lastDayOfMonth` replaces the hard-coded 28th.
- **The draft reads it.** `LienAffidavitJobFacts` takes `workDays` (the job row: `first_work_date`, `last_work_date`, `lien_last_work_on`) and `todayYmd`; the desk's affidavit pane, its paper preview (`LienDeskModal` `paperEntries`) and the stop window's page (`lienStopPaperPages`) pass the desk's job row and today. The Lien window computes the span from its fresh last-day read and its work months (`affidavitWorkSpan`) and hands it to `LienFilingTabs` as `workSpan`, so a day set by hand reaches the paper the moment it is saved; the tab reads the job row alone when no span is given.
- **The desk's job read** carries `lien_last_work_on` (it never did; the desk's last-day line read the clock hours alone) and tries `first_work_date` first, falling back to the list without it until the migration is on prod (`LIEN_DESK_JOB_COLUMNS_WITH_FIRST_DAY`).

## Decisions

- **The first day lives on the job, not in the affidavit RPC.** The desk reads it from the job row the way it reads the last day, the Lien window gets it through its `select *`, and punch list #212's hours statement will want the same two days. One trigger keeps both.
- **A month alone reads first-to-last, never one day.** A job with no approved hours (dated from its creation, v2.3747) swears the whole month the desk knows; that overstates less than a single day misstates, and the office can set the last day by hand to narrow it.
- **Start before end is folded, not refused.** A stale month beside a day set by hand folds the start to the first of the end's month; the paper never prints a period that runs backwards.

## Not done

- The Lien window's tab still names the job's name as the kind of work (`workDescription: job.job_name || 'Plumbing labor and materials'`); v2.4719 fixed the desk's draft to the trade. Its own fix.
