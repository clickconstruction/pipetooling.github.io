# v2.3999 — Bids: what the page loads is its own piece (2026-09-28)

Client only; no migration, no edge function, no screen change. Punch list **#46** row 5 (`to-dos/decomposition-queue.md`): the Bids map's step 8 (`docs/BIDS_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md` → Recommended extraction order).

## What is new

**`src/hooks/useBidsPageData.ts`** (29-case hook test), moved out of `src/pages/Bids.tsx` verbatim, in two hooks:

- **`useBidsPageData`** — who is looking and what the page loaded: the role and its trade scope, the trades, the bids with their two contact-recency maps and their GC recipients, the customers and their contacts, the estimators, the twin user ids; and the nine loaders that fill them. It runs no effect, so it is called at the top of the page, where that state stood.
- **`useBidsLoadGates`** — the three load effects: the trades once the role is known; the page's data for the picked trade; every trade's bids on By builder. It is called where the effects stood in the page, so they run in the order they ran. The four book loads the pricing engine owns are handed in.

The loads had no test. The hook test pins, by recording what each asks the database for: the role load keeps an estimator's, a primary's or a superintendent's own trade list and nobody else's; the page starts on Plumbing, then Electrical, then the first trade, inside the person's scope, and a scope that matches no trade falls back to every trade; `loadBids` asks for the picked trade, never an adopted bid, and — for a primary — only the bids they are on; the gates fire 80 ms after the trade or the tab changes, once.

## What changed in `src/pages/Bids.tsx`

3,610 → 3,338 lines. Behavior-preserving. The page keeps the trade that is picked, its `loading` and `error` lines, and the role load's own three-line effect (moving it would have changed when it runs relative to the effects above it).

## Left, on purpose

`loadBids` still reads every `bids_submission_entries` row it may see, with no filter, on every call. The map names that as a fix; it changes what is asked of the database, so it ships as its own PR, not inside a move.
