## How to verify

The to-do's recipe, now from the card: on a ZZ test contact whose number is a dev's own phone, with Send texts on, turn **Text back a missed call** on and call the company number until it rings out. The phone gets the in-hours line within a minute; the thread shows the call and *The app texted back · from the company number*; Needs a reply still holds the row; **Last texts back** reads *Sent, in office hours.* Call again inside three hours: nothing, and the list says *Not sent. We texted this number at …* Turn Send texts off and call: *Not sent. Send texts is off.* Nothing is pressed on a real customer.
