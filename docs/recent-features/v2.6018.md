## The live walk

The to-do's recipe, from the card, once v2.6012's migration is pushed and the seven functions are deployed. Nothing is pressed on a real customer: the caller is a ZZ test contact whose number is a dev's own phone, named on the contact so the row lands in Needs a reply rather than the New lane.

1. Settings → Phone and texts: **Send texts** reads On. On **Missed calls**, press the switch, then **Turn it on**. The state line reads *On. A missed call gets a text back within a minute.*
2. From the dev's phone, call the company number and let it ring out. Within a minute the phone gets the in-hours line with the opt-out line under it (a first text to that number).
3. Messages → the contact's thread: the missed call's line, and under it *The app texted back · from the company number*. The header still reads *Our move*. Follow up → Needs a reply still holds the row. Waiting on them does not list them.
4. Back on the card, **Last texts back** reads *today · <time> · <name> · Sent, in office hours.*
5. Call again inside three hours: no text. The card's new row reads *Not sent. We texted this number at <time>.*
6. After three hours (or on a second ZZ number), leave a voicemail: the voicemail line, and the row reads *Sent, with a voicemail.*
7. Turn **Send texts** off and call: no text, and the row reads *Not sent. Send texts is off.* Turn it back on.
8. Turn **Text back a missed call** off when the walk is done, unless the owner wants it live.

What to watch if a step fails: the claim row in `missed_call_replies` (`outcome`, `reason`, `words`), quo-webhook's log line *the text back did not go: …* or *fell over: …*, and the Quo delivery event on the text's line (*not delivered* on a landline).
