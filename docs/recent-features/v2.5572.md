# v2.5572 — Messages: a new text shows without reloading (2026-10-16)

The owner's report of 2026-10-16, texting his own number from his phone: the reply never showed in the open thread until the page was closed and reopened. `useMessagesHost` loaded the whole view once per tick, and the tick moved only after the window's own presses (a send through `useMessagesReread`, a tie, a name, a take); Follow up's queue and the header's badge read on mount and on focus; nothing subscribed to the tables, and `messages` was not in the realtime publication, so a line `quo-webhook` wrote on the server reached nobody's screen. The rest of the app already does this (the Pipeline's thread notes, the working board's inbox count, the job activity ledger's migration).

- **Migration `20261016010000_messages_realtime.sql`** (`docs/migrations/`): `messages` and `contacts` join `supabase_realtime`, idempotently. Realtime applies the tables' own row policies per subscriber.
- **`src/lib/messages/liveReread.ts`** (new kernel): `makeLiveBus({ attach, detach, settleMs })`: `attach` on the first listener, `detach` after the last, `bump` settles a burst into one `fire` `LIVE_SETTLE_MS` (400 ms) after the last bump, `fire` reaches every listener, nothing pending survives the last listener.
- **`src/components/gc/messages/useMessagesLive.ts`** (new): one channel `messages-live` on `postgres_changes` for `messages` and `contacts` (`MESSAGES_LIVE_TABLES`), plus the window's `focus` and `visibilitychange`, all bumping the one bus; `useMessagesLive(enabled, onChange)` subscribes a component and always calls its newest `onChange`.
- **Wired**: `useMessagesHost` (`reread`, so the Messages page, the thread window and GC projects' Messages re-read), `FollowUpPanel` (`q.reload()`), `MessagesBadge` (its read, which also keeps the focus read).
- **Tests**: `liveReread.test.ts` (3: attach and detach around the listeners, the settle, fire and the empty bus), `useMessagesLive.render.test.tsx` (1: two readers share one channel, a change and a focus read both, the channel closes after the last leaves).
- **Docs**: `MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md`'s thread-window paragraph, `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`'s Messages line, the migration doc.

Not in this cut: the job window's Note · Text · Email box and Job Mode's Job updates (`useJobMessages`) still read on their own presses; they take the same hook next.
