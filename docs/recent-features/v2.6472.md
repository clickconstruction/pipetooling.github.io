# v2.6472 — Each teammate has a logbook toward their next license (2026-11-03)

Punch list #255 named three builds behind the weekly progress signals; this is the first: the teammate's logbook. The owner asked for "the teammates logbook and license information where we can easily print a users logs". A design spike ran it against live data first, and the spike's findings shaped what shipped (below).

## What changed

- **`/logbook/:userId`** (`src/pages/Logbook.tsx`): one page a teammate, for the people who can see People → Licenses (`usePeopleAccess().canAccessLicenses`; `list_user_license_hours_log` enforces the same gate). Working toward, one card a trade; hours that count toward no license; licenses held; the log week by week; Print.
- **Kernel** `src/lib/logbook/logbook.ts`:
  - `parseLicenseType` reads the Licenses tab's free text ("Plumber Apprentice #191717", "AC Contractor TACLA39079E") into trade, class and number. A license with no trade, such as a notary, is listed but never laddered.
  - `goalsFor` takes the highest rung held in each trade and the next rung `REQUIREMENTS` names. The requirements are seeded from the boards' pages (2026-11-03): TSBPE journeyman plumber 8,000 h under a master; TDLR journeyman electrician 8,000 h, with a caveat that TDLR's web page says 7,000; TDLR certified technician 24 months; AC contractor 48 of the last 72 months.
  - `rowCounts` gives an hour the trade of its job's service type. Estimating, office and a job with no trade are not counted. `withOfficeJob` reads the overhead office job (`overhead_office_job_ledger_id_v1`) as office, though its service type says Plumbing.
  - `progressFor` counts hours or HVAC months (`MONTH_MIN_HOURS` = 80) and the next 500-hour mark. `logWeeks` and `totalsOf` build the log and the summary.
  - `supervisorCandidates` lists the teammates who can sign.
- **Signer**: the first teammate by name who holds the trade's supervising class (master; contractor for HVAC) with an unexpired date and an account and roster row that are not archived.
- **Print** (`src/components/logbook/LogbookPrint.tsx`, `printRootCss`, `data-theme="light"`): two faces.
  - *Summary sheet*: Teammate, licenses held, toward the next license, hours by year and trade, the not-counted line, and a signature block for each trade with hours.
  - *Full log*: every week and job.
- **Doors**: **Open logbook** on each expanded Licenses tab row, and **Logbook** on the desk's Licenses row (`PersonDeskFieldSection`).
- Guide `keep-a-teammates-logbook-and-print-it-for-the-board`. Tests: `src/lib/logbook/logbook.test.ts` (10) and `src/pages/Logbook.render.test.tsx` (3).

## Decisions

- **Every license counts for each trade** (owner, 2026-11-03): an hour counts toward the license of the trade its job was in.
- **"Teammate", not "Employee"** on the printout (owner).
- **Print behind the board's form, never in place of it**: TSBPE and TDLR accept only their own signed original.
- **Hours with no license get their own card.** The spike showed Paige with 1,462 plumbing hours and only electrical and HVAC licenses on file.
- **A teammate who has left never signs.** The spike named Bill, who is archived, as the HVAC signer.
