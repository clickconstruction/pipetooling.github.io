# v2.5137 — GC projects: the office's notices, their record and what is due (2026-10-09)

GC mode's Owner Billing O10a. Migration `20261010060000_gc_office_notices` (doc: `docs/migrations/20261010060000_gc_office_notices.md`). No function deploy; the sender and its cron are O10b. The plan is `to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/owner-billing-o10.md` on `spike/gc-mode`, approved by the lead with its five defaults and the on-date rule.

## What shipped

- **`gc_office_notices`**: one row per notice sent, written by the service role before the send; two partial unique indexes make each go at most once. The money team reads it.
- **`get_gc_office_notices_due(p_today, p_since)`**, the service role's: bill day on the 23rd and 24th to the project manager on the money team (else the company's owner), with the trades owing an unconditional waiver; the architect's reminder from the third day after a pay application went uncertified; the project manager's from the fifth. A pay application's notices only for one sent since the switch's day.
- **The switch** `gc_office_notices_on_v1` = `'false'`; on, the day it went on. The owner and dev flip it (O8c's pattern).
- `doors.ts` lists the table (Owner Billing, money). `access.test.ts` holds the payload's role list to `GC_MONEY_TEAM`; `ownerBillingNotices.test.ts` holds its 25 to `OWNER_BILL_DAY`. **Docs**: `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`'s Owner Billing door.
