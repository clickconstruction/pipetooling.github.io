# v2.6038 — Messages: the email's shape and the two signature lines (2026-10-28)

Punch list #207 PR 1 (`to-dos/messages-estimator-mailbox/README.md` → Finding 2, the owner's calls 3 and 4 at their recommendation, the mock-up's shape A), from Wendi's finding of 2026-10-27 on the email the app sends: *"This is what emails look like that we send, I don't love it."* The reply box's email wore the papers' letterhead (`buildGcCustomerEmail`, unframed): a navy bar, a cream card, a greeting the app wrote from the name they go by, the owner's *Email back, text, or call.* under every signature, and a footer with the company and the job. A reply from the box is a person's reply; the papers (an estimate, a bill, a contract, a lien notice) keep their letterhead. Migration `20261028230000_users_signature_lines.sql`; **the lead pushes it and redeploys `messages-email` after the merge.**

## The shape

- **`supabase/functions/_shared/messagesEmail.ts`**: `buildPlainMessagesEmail(words, links)` replaces `buildMessagesEmail` (nothing else called the letterhead builder once its three readers moved, so it did not stay beside it as the card first said): the body as typed in one paragraph, the files that went as links a line each under it (cut 7e, unchanged), then the signature, its first line in ink and the rest small and grey, and nothing after it; no bar, no card, no colour. `MessagesEmailWords` is `subject`, `body`, `signature`, with `greeting` and `footer` optional and drawn when given, so a tab built before this still sends its greeting; `parseMessagesEmailBody` takes a signature of six lines (was four). `letterheadFor` names the company on the From and on the signature's company line.
- **`messages-email/index.ts`** calls the plain builder; the From stays the company's name on `EMAIL_FROM`, the Reply-To the box (R6). **Redeploy after the merge.**
- **The send window** (`SendWindow.tsx`) previews with the same builder; its note reads *The subject comes from the job and your first line after the greeting, unless you change it. The signature is the one on Settings → Your account. Nothing here is typed twice.*
- **What customers see** (`customerSampleEmails.ts`, `customerJourneys.ts`): the sample reply email draws the plain shape, and the two journey rows say so.

## The greeting, the box's own

- **`compose.ts`**: `emailGreeting` and `emailFooter` are gone. `emailOpening(first)` is *Greg,*; `withEmailOpening`, `withoutEmailOpening` and `onlyOpening` are the box's; `greetingLineOf(body)` reads a first line of at most five words ending in a comma and asking nothing as the greeting, and `bodyAfterGreeting` feeds `subjectFor`, so *Greg,* never becomes the subject. `EmailDraft` is `from`, `to`, `subject`, `body`, `signature`; `emailDraft` takes no `greetingName`; `replyIo.sendEmail` sends `subject`, `body` and `signature`.
- **`ReplyBox.tsx`**: when the kind is Email and no caller's words are coming (`startWords`, a nudge), an empty box takes the opening on its first line; a switch back to Text takes it out when it is all the box holds; the box reads as empty while it holds the opening alone (the quick replies strip stays open, the from line floats, the count is blank, Send says *Write the message first.*); after an email goes the box opens on the greeting again. A person nobody has named gets no greeting.

## The signature

- **`signatureLines(sender, company)`**: the name; the title and the company as one line (*Estimator · Click Construction*; the plumbing company on a Pipeline job); *cell …*; then the two lines the person keeps. No line of the app's follows: `SIGNATURE_REPLY_LINE` is gone, and the owner keeps his line by typing it into his own.
- **`src/lib/messages/signature.ts`** (new, pure): `SIGNATURE_FREE_LINES_MAX` 2, `SIGNATURE_LINE_MAX` 80, the card's words, `freeLinesOf`, the draft from the row and back, `signatureDraftProblem`, `sameSignature`. **`signatureIo.ts`** (new): `readSenderRow` reads `users` with `signature_lines` and `title`, then without each in turn, so a client ahead of the push still reads a name and a cell; `senderOf`; `supabaseSignatureIo` loads and saves (the lines only while their column answered). `replyIo.loadSender` reads through it.
- **Settings → Your account → Your signature on an email** (`SignatureCard.tsx`, the office team, `#settings-signature`): the name and the cell from the account, the title, two lines, *As it goes* drawing `signatureLines` live, **Save the signature** lit on a change. Before the push the two boxes wait with the words and the title saves alone.
