# v2.5300 — GC mode: see where a trade partner or customer stands on their flow (2026-10-12)

Messages cut 4's first screens (helper 4), a draft stacked on #5435 (the flows' SQL) and #5448 (the loader). No migration of its own; it reads through #5435's `flow_rows` and writes through its presses, so it goes live only after #5435 is pushed.

- **The Flow tab** on the company window and the customer window (`src/components/flows/FlowTab.tsx`; `CompanyTabStrip` gains `flow`, `CompanyTab` and `CustomerTab` gain `'flow'`). Guide `see-where-a-person-stands-and-what-comes-next`.
- **The flow panel** (`FlowPanel.tsx`) and its rail (`FlowRail.tsx`) draw `flows/panel.ts`'s model: the title by kind, the lanes the picked job reads (the **Which job** box), each step's mark, paper marks, day, lapsed reason, whom it waits on and whose move it is (helper 5's `whoseMove` over `moverOf`), how far along, **See their page** with the globe, and **Take off the flow** with why.
- **The thread window's side** (`threadSlots.tsx`'s `side`, helper 1's #5458 and #5483): `FlowTab side`, keyed by the contact. It starts as the rail beside the thread. At a desk **‹** opens the panel at 340 px and a step's card covers the side (`position: absolute` in the side's own `position: relative` root). On a phone (the window's own 760 px) the rail stays in the row and the panel and the card open as sheets over the window (`position: fixed`, z 760 inside the window's layer, top padded by `--app-top-chrome`). A customer's page comes from the card's `customer_id` when the window gives only the contact. Nothing goes in `statusRow` (the lead: the thread kernel's move strip already says whose move it is).
- **Escape**: the card takes it first (capture on `document`, then `preventDefault`), then the side's sheet folds; the thread window, the company window and the customer window skip an Escape already taken, so closing a card no longer closes the window behind it.
- **The step card** (`FlowStepCard.tsx`) draws helper 5's `stepCard`: at a desk beside the panel, on a phone a sheet from the bottom. Its presses: **+ a note**, **Skip for <name>** (with why), **Put it back**, **Hold until Mon**, **Remind now** (then Activity, to log the call), **See their page**, **Put <name> back on**; a paper's press opens the window's own Documents, so nothing new sends. A press the screen cannot do yet reads greyed.
- **The read**: `useFlowPerson` over `flowPersonIo.ts` finds the window's contact, reads their kind's flow (the seed until the flows are on the database; helper 6's `loadFlowDef` replaces the reader once #5460 lands), `flow_rows`, their holds, reminders sent, step notes (`messages.step_key`) and their thread's last lines, and `flows/person.ts` makes helper 5's `PersonFlow`.
- **Remind now and the app's reminders**: `marksFrom` reads `nudge_sent.sent_by`, and only a person's Remind now sets `nudgedAt`; the app's own morning reminders are no press (the lead, through helper 5).
- **What we owe them and what their page says**: `oweFrom` (cut 2's `contact_owed`: a row of theirs not done) and `portalFrom` (cut 8's read-back: when they opened each kind's newest paper sent, contract → `contract`, change order → `co`, pay application → `payapp`, and the newest change order not answered as `openChange`; a customer who never opened their page has no views, and a change order out to them still shows, so the card can say they have not opened it). `personFlowFrom` takes `owed` and `portal`; the reads come with #5456 (`followUpIo`) and #5459 (`loadCustomerPortalReadBack`).
- **Kernels**: `flows/person.ts` (`personFlowFrom`, `marksFrom`, `sendsFrom`, `courtFrom`, `oweFrom`, `portalFrom`), `flows/panel.ts` (`flowPanel`); `facts.ts` gains the runtime family for a paper made in the New paper window (`doc:<uuid>`, facts `doc:<uuid>:on_file|current|signed|sent`, `stepFactById`, `docFact`), which `load.ts`'s `docStamps` stamps from the library rows; `load.ts` reads a pay application whose invoice reads paid with no day (`invoice_paid`), the paper sends and the preview link.
- **Tests**: `panel.test.ts` and `person.test.ts` over the demo people; render smokes `FlowPanel.render.test.tsx` (the panel, the rail, the card at a desk and as a sheet) and `FlowTab.render.test.tsx` (the read, a note, Off the flow, nobody to read, and the side at a desk and on a phone with Escape's order); the two windows' smokes open the Flow tab.
