The words: `quotesWords` (*1 of 2 quotes*, red at none), `dueWords` (*due Oct 24, tomorrow*, red inside a week or past), `benchLineParts` (*3 on the bench, 2 answer · 2 not asked yet · 1 to call here*), `bestDoor` (the bench, then the subs here, then Add a sub), `needsHeadWords`, `NEEDS_RULE` (*2 quotes on every ask, 3 a trade on the bench*, read from `BIDS_WANTED` and `BENCH_WANTED` so the two pages never disagree), `tradePartnersHref`.
- **The panel** ([`Prospects.tsx`](../../src/pages/Prospects.tsx)): in place of *The bench needs*, **What GC mode needs right now** over the Subs deck. A row names the trade, its short asks (the project a door to its card, the quotes, the day), the bench line, and the doors: **Ask them →** (Trade partners at the trade, when a bench company is not asked yet), **Call for ‹trade›** (the `?trade=` filter the chips made, shown only when subs wait here; reads **All subs** while on), **Add a sub** (New Prospect as a sub with the trade in Trades). The best next press is blue. The book's chips open Add a sub on their trade, or filter the deck when a sub here names the trade. Three columns on a desk, stacked under 640 px (`.gcNeedRow` in `index.css`). The By need order and the Trades picks still read `benchNeeds`.
