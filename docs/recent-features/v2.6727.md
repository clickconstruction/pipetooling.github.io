Punch list #171 (`to-dos/legal-desk-claim/README.md`). The 2026-11-08 walk on prod found one defect on the ZZ TEST matter: with two typed claim lines and no open bill, the money bars and *What each path nets* did not draw. `LegalDesk.tsx` gated them on the open balance (`w.balance > 0.004`) from v2.6105, before the claim could exceed the bills; `moneyBars` has drawn the claim bar for typed lines alone since v2.6680, so the kernel was ready and the gate was not.

## What changed

- **`moneyBars.ts`**: `moneyBarsDraw({ claim, offers })`, the gate as a kernel: true while the claim has a figure or an offer stands (open, countered or accepted, over zero). The claim holds the balance, so every matter that drew before draws still. The sentence under the bars on a matter with nothing open reads *Nothing open on the bills.* instead of *$0.00 owed on no sent bill.*, and the typed sentence drops its *more* when nothing stands before the typed lines.
- **`LegalDesk.tsx`**: the block that draws `LegalMoneyBars` and `LegalSettlementPaths` opens on `moneyBarsDraw` over the worth's claim and the standing offers.
- **`moneyBars.test.ts`**: the walk's matter (no bill, $750 typed): the gate says yes, the bars are the claim bar with one typed block and *What's left, on the claim · $152.50 left*, the words; the gate on the balance alone, on a standing offer alone, on a withdrawn offer and on an empty matter; the *more* kept while a bill is open.

No migration, no edge function.
