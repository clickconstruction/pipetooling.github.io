# v2.7829 — Pay: a salaried wage shows what the week comes to (2026-11-18)

The second step of a per-person salaried week, after the column (v2.7826). The owner asked on 2026-11-18.

## Why

A salary was entered as an hourly wage with a tick, and nothing on the card said what that came to. To put someone on a weekly figure, the office divided by 40 by hand. And the 8 hours on five weekdays was written into the payroll kernel, so a shorter day, a short Friday or a Saturday could not be paid even with a column to hold it.

## What ships

**The shared rule.** `src/lib/salariedEffectiveHours.ts` gains `salariedWeekOf(cfg)`, which reads `salaried_week_hours` and answers 8 hours on the five weekdays when the row carries none. A week that is malformed reads as the default whole, never patched day by day. `salariedFlatDayHours(date, week)` answers that weekday's hours. `effectiveHoursForCost` and `effectiveHoursForDisplay` read the week from the config they are handed. `salariedWeekHours`, `salariedWeeklyPay` and `wageForWeeklyPay` turn a wage, a week and a weekly figure into each other. `salariedWeekWords` says a week in words, days that share their hours together: *8 h Mon to Thu, 4 h Fri*. The edge copy under `gcKernels` is regenerated.

**Payroll.** `SalariedPayrollWindow` gains `week`. `fetchSalariedPayrollWindows` reads each person's week from the pay row, so the five payroll readers that take a window get it with no change of their own: pay report generation, the Draft Payroll preview, the person breakdown, the unreported weeks list and the catch-up. A day pays its own hours. A Saturday pays when the week says so. A weekday the week leaves out pays nothing and carries the new reason `day_off`. Unpaid time off and a day outside the employment window still pay 0, and a paid day off pays that day's hours. A pay row that cannot be read is thrown and never read as the default, so a short week is not paid long.

**The card.** On a person's sheet, *Pay & schedule* shows a salaried wage with its week: *8 h Mon to Fri · $300.00 a week* (`salariedWeekLine`). The wage and the week are stored. The weekly figure is worked out from them each time, so the two cannot disagree.

**Draft Payroll.** The small line under a salaried figure names the person's own day when every paid day is that long, *salary 8h* or *salary 6h*, and the week when the days differ, *salary 36h wk* (`salaryFlatHoursShortChip`).

## What does not change

Every pay row reads 8 hours on the five weekdays, and no screen sets the column yet. So no pay report, estimate or cost moves with this PR. The cost readers, the five functions and the workday template still carry the 8. The press that sets a week ships after they read the column, so a person is never paid on one week and costed on another.

## Tests

- `salariedEffectiveHours.test.ts`: the week from the row and its fallbacks, the flat day on each kind of week, cost and display, the week added up, the wage and the weekly pay turned into each other, a week in words.
- `salariedPayrollDays.test.ts`: the credit on a 6-hour day, a short Friday, four tens and a Saturday, the fetch putting each person's week on the window, and a pay row that cannot be read thrown.
- `generatePayStub.test.ts`: five weekdays of 6 hours at $10 come to $300, and a short Friday with a Saturday prices each day on its own.
- `salariedWeekLine.test.ts` and `draftPayrollBars.test.ts`: the card's line and the chip.
- Broken on purpose: with the workday credit put back to 8, the new payroll tests fail.
