## Is this the best we can do?

1. **A number where a number belongs**: large, alone, aligned, and the claim becomes a plain verb phrase a person reads first.
2. **One shape for every card**: claim, figure, caption. The dash's three meanings collapse into position.
3. **The subject is the title**: the house, not the house and its bucket; the bucket is a state, and a chip says that.
4. **Said once**: "47 invoices · unpaid only — exactly what the cell sums" said one thing three ways.
5. **Cut**: a middle dot (a different character, the same problem), and recoloring the figure by tone (the card's edge already carries the tone).
