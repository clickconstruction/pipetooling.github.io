# v2.2236 — Paid invoices keep billed_at (2026-08-24)

Root-cause fix from the "why does the app have 802 jobs but only 1 customer with pay history?" investigation. `jobs_ledger_invoices_billed_at_fn` v1 nulled `billed_at` whenever status was anything but `billed` — including the billed→paid transition, so **all 172 paid invoices in prod had billed_at NULL**: the app stamped the date at billing time and the trigger erased it at payment time. Since the pay-speed model (`get_billed_customer_pay_speeds`) samples require `billed_at` on the invoice behind a paid payment, history evaporated as fast as it was made (5 measurable samples across 2 customers, on 616 payments).

- Migration `20260824171151_keep_billed_at_on_paid.sql`: trigger v2 — paid invoices keep `billed_at` (on both UPDATE→paid and INSERT-as-paid, enabling backfills); `billed` stamping and `ready_to_bill` send-back clearing unchanged.
- No client changes: audited every `billed_at` consumer — all use it as a display/aging/activity date behind explicit `status='billed'` filters; none infer "open bill" from its presence.
- Companion one-time data repair (run separately over psql, not a migration): restore erased dates from surviving `invoice_billed` activity events, create+link invoices for HCP-imported payments from the owner's HCP invoices export (send date → `billed_at`, status `paid` so the board never sees them as open), and stamp remaining undated singles. Investigation numbers and method in this session's audit scripts.
