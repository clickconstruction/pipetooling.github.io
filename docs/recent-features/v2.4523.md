# v2.4523 — Lien desk: closing a job's window brings you back to the desk (2026-10-04)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. The owner's ask: in the Lien desk, a click on a row opens a new window; × or Cancel on it should land back on the desk, not on the Pipeline.

## Why it landed on the board

Four of the desk's doors in [`JobsStagesTab.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsStagesTab.tsx) ran `setLienDesk(null)` before they opened their window: `onOpenCalendarJob` (a Calendar row), `onOpenLienInstruments` (a job's notice, a book row), `onOpenLienAffidavit`, and `onPutGcOnNotice`. The desk was gone by the time the window closed.

## What changed

- Those four doors no longer close the desk. `LienInstrumentsModal` (z-index 1100) and `GcOnNoticeModal` (790) already draw above it (780), and all three are siblings in the tab, so a click on the upper window's backdrop closes only that window.
- The desk is never unmounted in between, so it comes back as it was: tab, filter, folded groups, scroll. A notice recorded in the window still refreshes the desk (`onRecorded` → `refetchLienDesk`).
- The **Legal desk** door still closes the Lien desk: it is a desk of its own with its own way back.

## Tests

`JobsStagesTab.render.test.tsx` (+1): a Calendar row opens the lien window with the desk still drawn; closing it leaves the desk on its Calendar. Seen in the dev preview on live rows: the desk kept its tab and its scroll position (260 px) through open and close. Nothing was written.
