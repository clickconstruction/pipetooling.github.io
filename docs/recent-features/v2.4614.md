# v2.4614 — Help: the lien desk guides match the screens again (2026-10-05)

PR 1 of the lien-guide freshness pass. The owner's coordinating session asked for every lien help guide to be read against main's code. Six read-only readers checked about 700 app-fact claims, and every fix below was re-read in the code. This PR fixes facts in the nine guides about the desk, the Lien window, filing, the demand letter, owner calls and the property kind. It changes no structure. Releases and waivers, supply houses and the attorney, and the guides that mention liens in passing follow as their own PRs.

## The fixes, by guide

- **understand how liens work and which lien tool to use**
  - The retainage notice "is not modeled in the app yet" is replaced. The Lien desk sends it from *All paper → Retainage* (`LienDeskModal.tsx`, `lienDeskRetainage.ts`).
  - The desk row now names *Next up* and the other two views.
  - The Dashboard's lien card names the next deadline, *Next lien deadline: …* (since v2.3704), and turns red inside seven days as *N lien windows close …*. What it counts is said again: the notices still to draft.
  - The Collections header button is *Lien desk* with the orange gavel.
  - The sub waiver path starts at *Jobs → Subs*.
  - The leader's word button is the outline *The leader said to send it…*, and the leader's approval card names what the office drafted.
  - Recording the leader's spoken word is dev, assistant and controller only (`lienDesk.ts`).
  - The portal line now says customers see signed lien waivers, and an owner sees our § 53.056 notice while the job still owes (`customer-portal`, `_shared/portalPropertyNotices.ts`).
  - *Find the owner ›* opens Edit Job, whose Property record holds the appraisal link.
  - The v2.4585 fragment says this guide was rewritten around Next up and its retainage line fixed. That rewrite did not ship: the guide changed by three lines then. This is the patch. Whether it also gets the rewrite is the owner's call.
- **read the Texas lien rules the app follows**
  - Only the desk's Notices list is GC-only (the affidavit read has no GC filter).
  - The *Lien desk* button lost its ⏱.
  - Counsel's cover letter is on the owner's copy of every notice the desk sends, and can be unticked on a draft.
- **send lien notices from the Lien desk**
  - The Pipeline card's door reads *Lien desk →*.
  - *Find the owner ›* opens Edit Job, not its Property record row (see *Left*).
  - The timeline list gains the owner's § 53.101 hold, and the chips are *the owner* for the hold and *counsel* for the year to sue. There is no *county* chip.
  - The green "open since" line sits above the date.
  - The leader's word button is the outline one, and *Not what I said* is on that notice's footer.
  - *Already mailed? Record it…* sits left of *Save draft*.
  - A missed month's row turns red under *Months on this job* (the strip went in v2.3782).
  - The *Wording* line (gone in v2.3694) is replaced by the envelope line and the shaded-box key.
  - *Correct the claim ›* opens the claim editor, and a typed claim is yellow in the preview only.
  - The run's buttons carry their counts, and letter two turns red on day 14.
- **see what is due on the lien calendar**: phone pills show counts only.
- **share where our liens stand**: the "same count as the Dashboard card" line is removed (it never matched), and jobs with no kind get one counting line.
- **file a lien and never miss its deadlines**
  - The steps include the § 53.057 retainage notice.
  - Each month gets its own notice date.
  - A job with no approved hours is dated from its creation month (since v2.3747), on the owner list, the nightly lookup and the timeline.
  - A desk notice goes out in the run, or alone through *Just this one, from the Lien window ›*.
  - The Edit-tab lookup's links read as printed, and the desk's owner pane reads *Found at:* with the district and year, and *Use this owner*.
  - The steps list's "notice step counts on subcontractor jobs only" now names the § 53.056 notice, since the retainage notice sits beside it.
  - The Legal desk's old lien clock is *Where each job stands*, and its suit step reads *counsel by*, *counsel now* and *the year ran out*.
  - The Dashboard watch reads *Next lien deadline: …*, red inside seven days, and three button colours match the screen.
- **send a final demand letter**: the red chip counts closed windows.
- **answer an owner who calls about a lien letter**: the Dashboard door is the card that names the next lien deadline, and a card offers *a few* replies.
- **mark a property residential or commercial**

  - The shared switch is the desk's and Edit Job's.
  - The month-earlier jump shows in the Lien window. The Billed runway already showed the residential date while the kind was unset.
