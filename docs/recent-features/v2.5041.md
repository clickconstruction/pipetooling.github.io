- **A company's papers** are `person_contract_documents` rows keyed to it (`company_id`), stored as `gc-company:<id>` with no person, held both ways by `person_contract_documents_company_paper`. The person trigger (`contract_docs_set_person_id`, now firing on `company_id` too) keeps a company's paper free of any person and takes a lost company back from its name; a person's paper finds its person as before.
- **`gc_company_paper(company, book entry)`** makes a company's copy of one Contract Book entry (the master agreement, the W-9 form) as `materializePacket` does a person's, with no assignment row. **`gc_send_paper(p)`** records a send (`gc_paper_sends`) and its promise (`gc_record_promise`) in one transaction. Both are a dev's until award's door.
- **A paper keeps its promise**: a trigger (`gc_company_paper_kept`, whose WHEN lets only a company's signed agreement, W-9 or certificate through, so `accept-contract` is untouched and a license or 'other' paper keeps nothing) keeps the msa, w9 or insurance promise when a company's paper is signed or filed, the rule the Portal's `msaFirst` gate reads, and `gc_record_company_coi` files the office's certificate.
- **`send-contract-for-signature`'s company branch**: a request with `trade_email` goes to the company's contracts people in GC's frame, the signing link as the email's own step (the frame's new optional action, agreed with the Portal lane), with a `gc_trade_messages` row and a sent copy whose `?t=` is kept as `…`. The person path is pinned byte for byte against main first (`src/lib/contractSigningSend.pin.test.ts`), its pure parts moved to `_shared/contractSigningSend.ts`; the GC frame is pinned the same way (`gcTradeEmailFrame.pin.test.ts`).
- **The six places a stored name would show**: People → Contracts never lists or counts a company's paper, the signed record names it in words, and the signing email, its sent copy, the W-9's name and the form PDF's download name are the company's (`_shared/companyPaper.ts`).
- **Proof**: the SQL bed `supabase/tests/gc_papers` (`npm run test:pg:gc-papers`, GitHub's `SQL beds`); the pin tests; `companySigningSend.test.ts`, `gcTradeEmailAction.test.ts` and `companyPaper.test.ts`.
