# v2.6790 — The dev seat sets up a job: plan_job → apply_job (punch list #293, PR 1) (2026-11-09)

The owner, 2026-11-09, after the short bill link's walk (#290) had to be driven through the app in Chrome because no verb made a job or sent a bill: *"I would like to add verbs for devs to setup and update jobs and send bills"*, and long term *"I want MCP to do everything a user can."* This is the first step of that road: the job. No migration.

## What changed

- **`supabase/functions/_shared/devMcpJobs.ts`** (new, pure): `jobInput` shapes `job = { job_name, job_address, customer_id, service_type_id, revenue?, hcp_number?, click_number?, customer_name? / customer_email? / customer_phone?, development_id?, project_id?, bid_id?, kind?, bill_to_party? + gc_customer_id?, the three links, allow_duplicate_name? }` and refuses a bad id, price, kind, link or email in words; `readNewJobWorld` names the reads the door makes as the dev (the customer with its `contact_info`, the service type, the development, the project with its master, the bid, the owner rule's three `app_settings` keys, the live jobs on the customer wearing the name); `jobMasterUser` is the New Job form's owner rule (`resolveEffectiveJobMasterUserId`: the project's master, else the company owner account, else the dev's or the org's override, else the dev); `newJobRow` is the form's insert payload column for column, the address through `titleCaseAddress` (now mirrored into the kernel copy by `scripts/edge-kernels.mjs`), the customer's email and phone filled from the customer when left out; `newJobDryRun` is the plan — the row as it will land, who it belongs to and why, the live jobs already wearing the name (refused unless `allow_duplicate_name`), what a bill will still need (the price, the customer's email, the move to Ready to Bill) and what the verb does not do (child rows, the award baseline, the Drive folder, which `job-drive-folders` places).
- **`dev-mcp` 0.49.0**: `plan_job` / `apply_job` join `WRITE_VERBS` with `rpc: null` (fifty-three write verbs). The apply re-reads the world, refuses on a changed hash or a prior apply, POSTs one `jobs_ledger` row as the dev under the table's own RLS (training mode, a twin and an archived account refused by the table, as on the screen), then PATCHes the kind when one was given (punch list #238's order; a refused kind is said and the job stands), and replies with the job id, its label and the next press.
- **Tests**: `src/lib/devMcp/devMcpJobs.test.ts` — the shape, the door's queries pinned, the owner rule held to `chooseJobOwnerFromOverrideRows` on every branch, every column of the form's insert present on the row, the GC fallback (v2.3345), the refusals, the hash's stability and sensitivity, the words after. The older seat tests move their version pin to 0.49.0 and the verb count to fifty-three.
- **Docs**: `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` → dev-mcp, `docs/dev-mcp/README.md` (the verbs table, the write-path paragraph), `to-dos/mcp-servers.md` (the verb table), the new card `to-dos/dev-seat-job-and-bill-verbs.md` (#293) with the design's roadmap table.

## Read closer than the design

- `plan_job` requires a customer, though the form allows none: a job with no customer cannot be billed, and this road leads to the bill (the owner's call, 2026-11-09).
- `click_number` is not minted by the database: the form writes what the office typed or a blank, and so does the verb. The label falls back to the job's name while both numbers are blank.
- The GC pick holds only when the GC's customer row is visible to the dev, as the form's `resolveGcCustomerIdForJobPayload` holds it; otherwise the bill goes to the customer (v2.3345).
