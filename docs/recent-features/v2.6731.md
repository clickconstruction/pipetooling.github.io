# v2.6731 — Messages: each box's own Sent folder is read, so an answer sent from bids@ in Apple Mail lands on the thread (punch list #207, PR 3) (2026-11-08)

Punch list #207 (`to-dos/messages-estimator-mailbox/README.md`), PR 3 of the train, on the owner's call 1 of 2026-11-08: read the boxes' own Sent. Wendi's first finding: a bid request she had already answered from her own mailbox still read *Our move*, because the reader read only the two boxes' Inboxes. Now an answer sent from bids@ itself, in Apple Mail or in Gmail, lands on the person's thread as ours within a minute, the court flips to theirs, and Went out lists it with the sender's name. No migration.

## What changed

- **The walk** (`src/lib/emailIn/historyWalk.ts`, mirrored; `supabase/functions/_shared/gmail.ts`): `gmailHistory` asks Gmail's history with no `labelId`, since Gmail takes one label at most and the reader wants two. `HistoryRecord.messagesAdded` carries each message's `labelIds`, and `recordMessageIds` keeps a message added only when they hold `INBOX` or `SENT` (`SENT_LABEL`, `WALK_LABELS`), so a draft, a chat or a Spam mail never counts against the run's 40; a message with no labels named is kept and the take decides. A label added still counts by the Inbox's alone.
- **The rule** (`src/lib/emailIn/leftOut.ts`): `sentByTheBox(email, isTheBox)` is a mail in Sent, not Spam or Trash, from the box's own address (the app's sender as the box reads the same). `outsideInbox` and `leftOut` are unchanged for everything else, and the walk kit's rule holds: while the kit carries bids@, a mail from bids@ is a person writing in, never the box's own.
- **The party and the line** (`src/lib/emailIn/emailLine.ts`): `sentParty(email, { isOurs })` is the first To or Cc not ours, going out, null when the box wrote only to the team or to itself. `emailLine` takes a `how` (`EmailLineHow`, `'copied'` or `'sent'`): `Wendi Doll <bids@…> to Rusty Cottar <rusty@…> · sent from bids@` beside v2.5722's `· copied to bids@`.
- **The reader** (`supabase/functions/gmail-sync/index.ts`): `takeMail` reads `sentByTheBox` first; on a sent mail the forward and the leaving-out rules are skipped, the party is `sentParty` (none outside answers `'left'`), the notice list is not asked (it is about mail in), `heldLine` by Message-ID holds the app's own send as the box (v2.6702 writes `rfc_message_id` and `gmail_id` on the line before the box's Sent shows the mail), which only gets the label. `who_user_id` is the one team member whose `users.name` equals the From's display name, case-blind (`loadTeam`'s `memberByName`; two members of one name name nobody, so no line lands under the wrong person), else null; `who_text` the sent line's. Files, `email_write_line`, the HTML part and the label follow as for a line in. The 404 fallback search reads `(in:inbox OR in:sent)`.
- **The reply box** (`src/lib/messages/compose.ts`'s `ownMailboxNote`, drawn by `SendWindow`): *Answering from your own mailbox? Answer from bids@ in Apple Mail, or copy it, and this thread hears it.*, the guide link kept.
- **Tests**: `historyWalk.test.ts` (a sent message taken beside the Inbox's, a draft, a chat and a Spam mail passed over and never filling the cap, a message with no labels kept), `leftOut.test.ts` (`sentByTheBox` in every case, the kit's rule), `emailLine.test.ts` (`sentParty`, the sent line's words and its Message-ID), `compose.test.ts` and `ReplyBox.render.test.tsx` (the new words), `edgeKernels.test.ts` (the mirror).
- **Docs**: `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` § gmail-sync, `docs/MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md` (Email in), the guides *turn on reading the bids inbox* (**An email sent from the inbox**, the never-does list) and *tag an email onto a job from Gmail* (one sentence), the card's status, `next`, `ver` and *Where it stands*.

## Read closer than the card

- The card had `recordMessageIds` keep a `labelsAdded` entry carrying `SENT` too. A label added is kept by the Inbox's label alone, as before: Gmail writes the Sent label at the add, never later, and a Sent label put on by hand would be a filed copy, not a send.
- The line's name comes from the From as the mail app wrote it, so the guide says to set the inbox's sending name in Apple Mail to one's own. A From with no name reads the address.
