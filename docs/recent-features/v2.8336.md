# v2.8336 — Dashboard: the Today's sends row shows the texts (2026-11-23)

Punch list #424, the first card on it. The owner, 2026-11-23, with the row circled on his phone: *Today's sends doesn't make any sense to me as a user. There's not enough context for this item.* Then, on the first drawing, which answered with sentences: *show instead of tell*, and *clearly distinguish the values and variables that can change so a user knows what is syntax and what is signal.* He picked the row that shows the texts. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was there

The row's title was a place, *Today's sends*. Under it sat a line of counts, *3 texts ready to send, 1 goes later, 1 held*. Its number added the three together, so it was larger than what a person could send. *Held* had no cause. The press read **Open**. Every neighbour in the list said a count and what was wanted.

`useTodaysSends` already read every waiting text in full, then kept a count and that one line. So the row could show far more with no new read.

## What changed

- **The title counts what can go**: *3 texts to send*. The number is the texts a person can send now.
- **The names sit under it**: up to three, then how many more.
- **A timed text is a chip with a clock and its time.** It needs nobody, so it is not counted in the title.
- **A held text is an amber chip with its cause** in a word or two, such as *no number* or *sent STOP* (`heldCause`). The window still says the whole reason and its door.
- **The oldest is named** once a text has waited two days: *oldest 4 days*.
- **One text shows itself**: the words of the text, with the person and the step.
- **None can go**: the row reads *2 texts cannot go*, each name with its cause, and the press reads **Fix them**.
- **Only timed texts**: the row is grey, *1 text goes Tue 9:00 AM*, with **See it**.
- **The press reads Read them.** It opens the window and sends nothing.
- **Values apart from words.** The kernel marks each value with `val`, and the card draws it bold with the fixed words in grey.

## How it is built

- `src/lib/offers/offers.ts`: `todaysSendsNeed(rows, now)` returns the count, the marked title, the names, the peek, the chips, the press and the severity, and one plain `detail` line. `heldCause` shortens a hold. `TodaysSend` carries `preparedAt`. `todaysSendsLine` is gone: the hook was its only reader.
- `src/lib/dashboardNeedsYou.ts`: a Needs you item may carry `shown` (`NeedsYouShown`). Its `title` and `detail` stay plain words, for Walk the list, a fold's summary and a trail chip. The `todays-sends` item's kicker is *Texts to customers*.
- `src/components/dashboard/NeedsYouRowWords.tsx`: the words of a row. A plain row draws as it always did. A row with `shown` draws its values with `ValueSentence`. `DashboardNeedsYouCard.tsx` calls it where the title and the detail were, and changes nothing else. Where the press and the count sit is not this change's.
- The window behind the press is unchanged. So are Quickfill's line and the name *Today's sends*, which the window and the guides still use.
