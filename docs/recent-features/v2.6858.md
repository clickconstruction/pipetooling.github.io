# v2.6858 — The ground under permits on a bid: a bid's address read the city's way (punch list #307, PR 1) (2026-11-10)

The first PR of `to-dos/bid-permits/`, on the owner's word (*Yes, start PR 1*). A kernel and a count: no migration, no function, nothing on a screen.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/research/bidSite.ts`** (new, pure, `bidSite.test.ts`, 30 tests on made-up addresses):
  - `readBidAddress(address, readCities)` reads a bid's one-line address into a **site** (the city, the house number a read asks the table for, the street as typed, and its compared shape), a **town we do not read** (named when it can be picked out), or the **reason** it is not a street address: empty, no street number, no street name, a crossing, no city. The city is taken from the end of the line, so a street named for a town is still a street.
  - `canonOfStreet` and `sameSite`: both the bid's street and the feed's are read into one shape and compared there, since neither is tidy. The house number, the street's words in any order, and the street type when both carry one. A highway is its number (*N Loop 1604 E* is the feed's *N 1604 E*; *US Hwy 281 N* is *N US HWY 281*; an interstate in any spelling, with or without the frontage road; *Farm to Market 471* is *FM 471*). Exact on that shape and nothing fuzzier.
  - `permitsOnSite` keeps the permits on the bid's own address; `plumbingTypeOf` tells a general plumbing permit from gas, sewer and irrigation, which the feed files under plumbing; `looksLikeThisJob` is the hint that a permit's project name shares a telling word with the bid's; `leadPermit` picks the permit the verdict speaks of.
  - `bidSiteState` and `bidSiteWords`: the four honest states (found, none seen, not read, unreadable) and their two lines, none of which reads as *No permits*. `siteSourceLine` and `seenOutOfWords` are the scope every later screen says: the source, the first day of the records, what a count is out of.

## The count

Run on 2026-11-10 through the dev seat, read-only, counts only. The people's open bids whose address names San Antonio: **28**. The kernel read 26 as sites, **25 different sites**. One is in another town on a street named for San Antonio, and one is a crossing.

Of the 25 sites, **7 have a permit on the address**. All 7 carry a building permit or an application. **2 carry a general plumbing permit**, on bids that are still open.

The permits asked for each site were narrowed by hand to its house number and one word of its street, then decided by the kernel. PR 2's read asks by house number alone.

## What the real addresses taught

Three things went into the kernel after the run: the feed's fourth plumbing type (sewer), a state highway written *Texas 211*, and *Farm to Market* spelled out. One of the 7 sites met its permit only because *Loop* is left off.

## Not changed

No screen, no table, no read. The robots' copies of a bid are left out of the count by name here; PR 2 uses `isRobotBid`.
