# v2.5643 — Bills and estimates in a message, PR 3: /estimate and an estimate's second link (2026-10-19)

Punch list #127, PR 3 of 3, on PR 2 (v2.5642, bills in the `/` list). The owner's pick B: each time the office puts an estimate in a message, the app makes a second link to it, kept as its own hash, so the link in the customer's estimate email keeps working. Estimates join the `/` list and `{estimate}` fills. The to-do is retired. Migration `20261019050000_estimate_share_links.sql` (`docs/migrations/20261019050000_estimate_share_links.md`); three edge functions changed.

- **The link** (migration): `estimate_share_links` keeps a share's token as its SHA-256 only, with who made it and its own fourteen days; `share_estimate_link(uuid)` makes one for a sent or approved estimate for the office team and returns the token once, refusing in words a training account, a twin, anyone off the office, and an estimate not there or not sent.
- **The functions that open a link** (`_shared/estimateTokenLookup.ts`): `get-estimate-for-customer`, `accept-estimate` (accept and decline) and `log-estimate-option-view` ask `estimateRefForTokenHash` which estimate a hash is, the row's own first and then a share's, and read the estimate by its id; a shared link stops on its own day (`linkHasExpired`), the emailed one on the row's. The share read is fail-soft: a table not there, or a failed read, is no match, so the emailed link opens as before.
- **The box** (`messages/estimateLinkIo.ts`, `ReplyBox`): `/estimate`, `/quote` or `/price` lists the customer's sent then approved estimates (`ESTIMATE_LINKS_ON` on). A pick, or any quick reply that holds `{estimate}` (the estimate picked, else their newest sent one, the job's first), calls `share_estimate_link`, then goes in filled with the accept page on the live app (`estimateAcceptUrl`) and shows a note under the box (`estimateLinkNote`): a second link was made and the emailed one keeps working. A failed call toasts and leaves `{estimate}` for the office to fill. The strip, the `/` list and Find a reply preview a line with `{estimate}` with that estimate's number and amount (`fillsFor`).
- **Tests**: `estimateTokenLookup.test.ts` (the row first and nothing further; a share with its day; no match for an unknown hash or a missing table; the two ends); `estimateLinkIo.test.ts`; `papers.test.ts` (estimates listed); `ReplyBox.papers.render.test.tsx` (Enter on an estimate makes a link and the note; a line with `{estimate}` makes one for the newest sent estimate; a failure leaves the blank; a bill makes none). The new SQL bed `estimate-share-links` walks the links, the hash, the day, the untouched emailed hash, every refusal, who reads them and the fences.
- **Docs**: the guide *reply to a person from their thread*'s section becomes **Put in a bill or an estimate**; `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`, the Glossary's R3 line, `MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md`'s reply box paragraph and the three functions' sections in `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`.

Release order: merge, deploy the three functions, then push the migration (until the push the share call fails with a toast, so no link is made before a function can open it); a types PR after the push.
