# v2.8174 — Write someone up: add photos, and one door instead of two (2026-11-21)

Punch list #410, PR 5 of the train in [`to-dos/write-someone-up/`](../../to-dos/write-someone-up/README.md), with the old card's retirement. Client only: it uses the table and the bucket [v2.8172](./v2.8172.md) put on prod.

## Why

Taunya asked on 2026-11-20 for a write-up *with details on their phone that we can record*. Of the three things that could mean, she kept photos and spoken words typed by the phone, and set a kept voice recording aside. Spoken words shipped with the sheet in [v2.8160](./v2.8160.md). This is the photos. With the sheet walked live, the HR Report card it replaces can go, so a leader has one door and not two.

## What changed

- **Photos in the sheet.** Under the words, **Take a photo** opens the camera. Each shot is shrunk on the phone (`shrinkPhoto`) and sits as a tile with a press that takes it off. A write-up carries up to four. The read-back shows them and counts them.
- **Sent ahead of the write-up.** On **Send write-up to HR** the press id is minted first, since a photo is named by it. Each photo goes to the private `person-report-photos` bucket with its row, then the write-up lands on the same id.
- **Kept with no signal.** A photo that cannot go for want of signal is kept on the phone (`keptPhotos`), and the whole write-up waits with it. `person-report` is now a photo-carrying kind of the outbox, so the replay sends the photos first and lands the write-up after. **Your write-ups** reads *1 photo kept*.
- **The office's card.** Pending reports shows a write-up's photos under its facts. A press on one opens it.
- **Evidence when filed.** Filing a write-up with photos saves them as one evidence record on the person's file, tied to the new entry, through `savePersonEvidence`. It takes the next exhibit number and goes to Drive like any record. The toast says how many photos it held, or why the record was not made.
- **The HR Report card is retired.** It leaves the Dashboard and Dispatch Mode's home, where **Write someone up** stands in its place. Its dock chip is gone. The guide `send-an-hr-report-about-someone` answers as `write-someone-up`.

## Decisions

- **The press id is minted by the sheet's send, not by the outbox.** A photo's path holds it, so it has to exist before the first upload. `pressThroughOutbox` takes it through a new option and keeps it on every try as before.
- **One evidence record a write-up, made by the filer's browser.** The door for evidence already stages files, numbers the record and sends it to Drive. Copying bytes between buckets on the server would have been a second door.
- **A photo that fails for a reason other than signal stops the send.** The words stay on the read-back with the reason. Nothing is half sent.
