# v2.8312 — Legal portal: on one person's link, Recorded by is that person (punch list #85, a review residual) (2026-11-22)

Punch list #85 (`to-dos/legal-portal-firm-ready/README.md`), one of the residuals the October reviews left: *Recorded by is the firm's own claim, since anyone holding the link can pick any name, and the link never expires.* Two edge functions to redeploy, `submit-legal-portal` and `legal-portal`. No migration.

## What was found

- Half of the card's structural fix was built after the line was written. [v2.4750](v2.4750.md) gave a firm several links: its own, and any number of one person's links, each with a label (`legal_portal_links.purpose` `firm` or `person`, `label` required for a person).
- Nothing read the label when an act was saved. `submit-legal-portal`'s `resolveLink` selected `firm_id, revoked_at` and no more, and the page drew the same picker on every link. So a person on their own link could still pick a colleague's name, and the record could not tell which link an act came in on.
- On prod on 2026-11-22: one live firm link and one live person link, one act saved through the portal, none with a name.

## What changed

- **The rule** ([`_shared/legalPortalActs.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/legalPortalActs.ts)): `legalLinkPerson(link)` is the label of one person's link, else null. `recordedByForLink(link, picked)` decides the name. On one person's link it is `{ id: 'link:<link id>', name: <label>, how: 'own_link' }`, and `picked` is not read. On the firm's shared link it is the pick with `how: 'shared_link'`, or nobody.
- **`submit-legal-portal`** reads the link's `id`, `purpose` and `label` with the key. A matter act on one person's link never reads `body.recordedById`. An undo's `meta.voidedBy` takes the same value. Start here's answers are written under the link's person, and under the typed name on the shared link.
- **`legal-portal`** sends `linkPerson` in both answers: the label, or null on the shared link, the office's preview and the sample.
- **The page** (`src/pages/LegalPortal.tsx`): with `linkPerson` set, *Recorded by* is the name in bold with one line beside it, *This link is yours alone, so everything saved here is recorded under your name.* There is no select, and the act carries no `recordedById`, whatever an earlier visit on the shared link left on the browser. Start here's *Answered by* is drawn the same way (`LegalPortalIntakeForm`).
- **The desk** (`src/lib/legal/legalAsks.ts`): `firmPersonForOffice` writes *Dana Reyes · firm · own link* or *Dana Reyes · firm · name picked*, and a question reads *Dana Reyes at the firm asked (own link)*. It reaches the Fees & steps tables, the conversation and the Timeline through `entryRecordedByWords` and `conversationWho`. An act saved before this has no `how` and reads *Dana Reyes · firm* as before. The firm reads the name alone.

## What a firm sees

| | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| On one person's link | A *Recorded by* select listing the firm's people, *the firm* by default | *Recorded by* **the person's name**, nothing to pick |
| On the firm's shared link | The same select | The same select |
| The office, on the desk | *Dana Reyes · firm* either way | *· own link* or *· name picked* after the name |

## Not built, and why

- **An expiry.** The card leaves it until the firm asks who can see the portal, and the owner said not to build one now. It would take a nullable `expires_at` on `legal_portal_links`, read beside `revoked_at` wherever a key is resolved (`legal-portal` with its probe, and `submit-legal-portal`), with a date on the office's link window. The larger half is renewal: an expired link needs a fresh address sent to its person, and the firm's shared link sits in every email already sent, so it needs either an exemption or a notice that goes out before it ends.
- **A person link tied to a row on the firm's people list.** The label is free text the office types. Tying it to `legal_firm_recipients` would let *Only theirs* emails and the name agree, and needs a column and a pick in the link window.
- **A mark on Start here's answers.** `intake_sent_by` is one text column, so the office cannot tell a pinned name from a typed one there. The acts carry the mark.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **Is a pinned name proof?** No. The portal has no sign-in, so the name is as sure as the link is private. What changed is that the office decides the name when it makes the link, and can turn that one link off.
- **Why keep the pick on the shared link?** One link in every email is what the firm asked for, and a pick is better than *the firm* on every row. The record now says it was a pick.
- **Why `link:<id>` and not the label alone?** Two people can share a name, and a link can be rotated. The id says which link, without holding its key.
