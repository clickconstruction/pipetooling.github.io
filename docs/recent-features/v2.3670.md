# v2.3670 — Lien desk: click a gate to bring up its section, and every gate has one (2026-09-21)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Fifth of the pane clean-ups (v2.3657 gates · v2.3658 owner envelope · v2.3661 months · v2.3662 footer).

## Why

The owner, from a screenshot of job 883's gate row: *"I would like to be able to click on one of these boxes and have it bring up that section. I see that in the area below it sections complete are not shown, I would still like them to be shown."* Two things were true. The cells were plain `div`s — the number, the label and the value with nothing behind a click — and a clear gate's section was hidden by design (v2.3657 rendered a detail only for a gate that was not clear), so on a ready job the row sat over nothing and the facts the notice was about to print (which owner, at what address; which GC; which months) were nowhere on the desk except inside the paper.

## What shipped

- **[`LienDeskGates.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskGates.tsx)** — each cell is a `<button>` with `aria-controls` on its section; `active` / `activeAt` / `onPick` are the parent's, so the footer's *Go to gate N* rings the same way. Bringing a section up scrolls it into the pane only when it is out of view (the pinned strip covers the top 48px; `block: 'center'`) and moves focus to it (`tabIndex={-1}`). Every section carries its `n · label` lead now, not only the ones that are not clear.
- **[`LienDeskModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskModal.tsx)** — `activeGate` (cleared after 4s) and the four sections in full. **1**: on a filed owner, the envelope through `rollMailingLines` (the roll's own shape, v2.3658), *Check on <county> CAD ↗* when the owner is on the property record, *Change ›* (→ Property record, or Edit Job for a job-level override), and `ownerSourceWords` so an override is never mistaken for the record; `LienDeskOwnerPane` still renders its Confirm on an unconfirmed nightly save. **2**: the GC and the address the certified copy goes to (amber *no mailing address on the customer* when there is none), *Change the GC ›*. **3**: on a linked property, **`PropertyKindSwitch`** in place — `savePropertyKind` on the row (the column the property sheet, Edit Job and the claims table write), toast, `onChanged`; *Saved on the property record — same property as 881, which follows it* from `jobsSharingProperty`; without a linked property the *Set property kind ›* door stays, since there is no row to write on. v2.3667 had left this pane's gate 3 for "another session" — this is it. **4**: one `lienGateMonthLine` per work month with approved hours (the cell's list, from the Months card's own evidence), the ticked ones *· on this notice*, the pending-sessions note, and *Months and deadlines ↓*.
- **Kernel** — [`lienDeskGates.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDeskGates.ts): `propertyKindClockWords` (*Commercial · Comal County — each month's notice is due by the 15th of the 3rd month after the work.* / 2nd for residential / the caveat while unknown), `ownerSourceWords`, `lienGateMonthLine`. 3 tests.
- **CSS** — `.lienGate` is a button (font/colour reset, hover border, focus ring); `--lien-gate-ring` per tone (green / red / amber) drives `[data-active='yes']` on the cell and a 4s `lienGateBringUp` keyframe on the section; a clear section's left rule is green now that it is always there; `.lienGateFact` / `.lienGateFactActions` put the fact left and its doors right, wrapping under it in a narrow pane.
- Guide *send lien notices from the Lien desk* → "Reading the notice before it goes" says all of this in the owner's words.

## Not done, on purpose

- The owner's saved name prints as saved (*SCHERTZ STATION LTD*): the cell already did, and title-casing a saved legal name is the record's business, not the desk's.
- Gate 4 does not tick months — that stays the Months card's job one box down; the section only lists and links.

## Verified

- Kernel suite (10); `LienDeskModal.render.test.tsx` — the ready job's four sections carry the owner envelope, the GC's address and the months; a cell click sets `data-active` on the cell and its section only; the switch's *Commercial* pick calls `savePropertyKind('addr1', 'non_residential')` then `onChanged`; the blocked job still reads *Fix gate 1* with *Go to gate 1*.
- Dev server on prod data: job 883 (no linked property) — four sections, gate 3 keeps its door, gate 2's click rings cell and section; job 650 — the envelope with *Check on Guadalupe CAD ↗*, the GC's address, the switch reading Commercial, four months with Jun *on this notice*. **No kind was written on prod.**
