- **The rule** (`leadDeck` in `src/lib/research/permitResearch.ts`): the role picked in **They are** decides the deck. `gc` → `prospects.kind = 'customer'`; `owner` and `architect` → `gc_lead`. `LEAD_DECK_WORDS`, `leadLandsWords(role)` and `leadMadeWords(lead)` hold the words for the two sheets and the toast.
