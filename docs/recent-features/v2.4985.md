# v2.4985 — Lien desk: what only the leader can approve comes first (2026-10-08)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. PR 3 of 3 for punch list #101 ([`to-dos/lien-printed-run/`](../../to-dos/lien-printed-run/README.md)), stacked on PR 2 (v2.4982).

## Why

Approving is the one step only the leader can take, and on 2026-10-08 his three approvals sat between nineteen printed rows, sorted by day alone. A master is office too (`isLienOffice`), so "your moves first" would have been every row; the scarce move is the approval.

## What changed

- **Kernel** [`lienNextUp.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienNextUp.ts): `LienNextUpGroup` gains `mine` and `waiting`. `groupLienNextUp(rows, { waitingOn })` draws an approval with the viewer's button as **Only you can approve** first, then *Needs you now* and *Coming up* by day, then an approval with no button as **Waiting on <leader>** last (`waitingOn`, else *the leader*). `lienNextUpCount` is every row but the waiting ones. `buildLienNextUp` takes an optional `leaderName(jobId)`; the office's awaiting rows read *Waiting on Sam · since Oct 4* on notices, affidavits and retainage notices.
- **View** [`LienDeskNextUp`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskNextUp.tsx): `waitingOn` prop; the *Only you* title is link-blue, *Waiting on* muted. A waiting row keeps its **Open** button.
- **Desk** [`LienDeskModal`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskModal.tsx): passes `leaderName` from `leaderNameFor` over the job's master, names the group when every waiting row shares one leader, and the Do now tab counts `lienNextUpCount`.
- **The run row on a narrow desk** (seen live at 581 px, the leader's view): with *Only you* above it, its two buttons crowded the last column until the title vanished and *Record the mailing* clipped. On a computer *Take back…* now sits small on the row's second line after the question it answers, and the title spans the question's column with the dots.
- Guide [`send-lien-notices-from-the-lien-desk`](../../src/content/help/send-lien-notices-from-the-lien-desk.md): the two groups.
