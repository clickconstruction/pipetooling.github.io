# v2.1912 — Workbench iterations 2 & 3: scenarios and the win/loss strip (2026-08-20)

The two features deferred from the v2.1906 Workbench ship, built on the prototype's design:

**Scenarios (iteration 2)** — a card strip above the totals: one card per bid-owned Pricing (revenue · estimated margin · profit; the active one tagged **Active** — that's the pricing Cover Letter/Share already use), click to switch (`handlePricingVersionChange`), and **+ Duplicate as new scenario** (clone RPC + `attachAndActivateNewBidPricing`) to fork a what-if without touching the original. Per-scenario revenue reuses the cover-letter bundle computation (fetch each pricing's entries/assignments/custom-prices/hides, run `computeBidPricingRows`); cost is scenario-independent. **Legacy bids** whose active pricing is a shared (non-bid-owned) version get a "Current pricing (shared)" card so Duplicate can birth their first real scenario.

**Win/loss calibration (iteration 3)** — "This number vs your history": green dots for won bids and red dots for price-losses (loss_reason matching /price/i) of the same service type, plotted by **estimated margin** (`bid_value` vs the new [`bid_pricing_history`](../migrations/20260820230000_bid_pricing_history.md) RPC's cost estimate), with a ▼ marker tracking the current pricing's blended margin and a data-driven verdict ("In your winning range — 5 of 7 wins priced at or below 44%" / "Mixed territory" / "Above every recorded win"). Hidden until ≥3 usable history points; margins labeled "estimated" (no clocked team labor in the RPC). The RPC is typed by hand in `database.ts`/`database-functions.ts` pending post-push regen.

Verified live in the worktree: scenario strip renders with the legacy fallback card + Active tag + Duplicate on B273; history strip correctly hidden pre-deploy. **`supabase db push` after merge.** Client + migration.
