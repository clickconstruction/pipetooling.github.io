# v2.5862 — The firm portal shows who covers what (2026-10-25)

PR 4 of punch list #144 (legal firms by county), the last of the train: PRs 1–3 gave the office the county picker (v2.5756/v2.5758), the desk's Map (v2.5784) and the firm by venue (v2.5792); this one gives the firms their view. The owner's ask — "law firms are mature enough that they can see what other firms are representing what" — is held to **counts only** by the to-do's blocker: counsel has not yet answered what one firm may see of another's cases, so no case names, no dollars and no detail of another firm's matters reach the page. Widening past counts stays gated on counsel's answer.

- **The function** (`supabase/functions/legal-portal/index.ts`): the payload adds `coverage` — every active firm's `id`, `name`, `created_at` (the hue order `firmHues` uses, so the portal colors as the picker does), counties (the live `legal_firm_counties` rows) and open-matter count (the portal's with-firm stages, not closed), plus open matters per county. A matter's county is its first job's property-record county (the venue rule's first leg; a matter whose records name no county counts for its firm but for no county). Shaped by `_shared/legalFirmCoverage.ts`, whose client door is `src/lib/legal/legalFirmCoverage.ts` and whose tests run beside it; null on any failure so the portal still opens. **Deployed with the merge**; an older function sends no `coverage` and the page hides the panel.
- **The page** (`src/pages/LegalPortal.tsx`): a **Who covers what** panel tab between Lien grid and Notifications, shown when the function sends coverage with at least one firm. `LegalPortalCoverage.tsx` draws the Settings picker's county map (`LegalFirmCountyMap` in `lens` mode, no dots — the new `idleWords` prop keeps the readout honest), each firm's card row (its hue, counties line, matter count, *your firm* on the reading firm's row), a tapped county's case count, and the plain line *Counts only. Another firm's case names and amounts stay with that firm.* The tour gains a stop for the panel.
- **The to-do** (`to-dos/legal-firms-by-county/README.md`): PR 4 shipped counts-only; the widening past counts (dollars per firm, anything of another firm's cases) stays in the blocker, gated on counsel.
