## Why

The card's date for a house's own notice is an estimate from its unpaid invoice dates, and its figure is our books'. On the job that started this work the house had said both itself: a notice going out on Oct 14 (the estimate said by Oct 15) and a balance of $7,393.33 against $12,114.55 in our books. The house's own words are what an email should quote, so they need a place.

## What shipped

- **The table** `job_supply_house_words`: per job and house, `their_balance`, `notice_on`, `said_by`, `note`, and who wrote it (`noted_by`, `noted_by_name`, `noted_at`, the first and last stamped by a trigger). One row is the latest word; clearing deletes it. The Lien desk's office set reads and writes.
- **The kernel** ([`lienJobSuppliers.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienJobSuppliers.ts), +5 tests): a house carries its `word`. `lienSupplierHouseNotice` answers kind `said` with the house's day (a past day reads as past) and falls back to the estimate. A card row carries `theirBalanceWords` (*Reece says $8,950.00*, only when it is not our figure) and `saidWords` (*Dana said so · noted Oct 2 by Grace*). The Owed column stays our books', so the rows still add up to the total. `lienSupplierEmailText` says *Reece says its balance is $8,950.00. Reece says its own notice goes out on October 14.* `parseSupplierWordBalance` reads the typed balance.
- **The form** ([`LienJobSuppliers.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienJobSuppliers.tsx)): given `word` by the desk's office roles, each owed house's row offers **They told us…** (**Change what they told us…** once one is on record). The form opens under the row: their balance, the day their notice goes out, who said it (the house's job-accounts rep to start), a note; **Save**, **Clear**, **Cancel**. Save waits until something is typed and refuses a balance that is not a number.
- **The read and the writes**: [`useLienJobSuppliers`](../../src/hooks/useLienJobSuppliers.ts) reads the words with the rest (a missing table reads as none) and returns `reload`; [`lienSupplierWordIo.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienSupplierWordIo.ts) upserts and deletes.
- **The Share list** ([`lienDeskShare.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDeskShare.ts)) names the house's day when it gave one and that day is still ahead.
- The Lien window's card shows a word and offers no change.
- Guide [`see-which-supply-houses-are-owed-on-a-lien-job`](../../src/content/help/see-which-supply-houses-are-owed-on-a-lien-job.md): *Write down what the house told you*.

## Changed from the mock-up

The mock-up put the house's figure in the Owed column with ours under it. The build keeps ours in the column and the house's under it, because the column's total is from our books and would no longer add up.
