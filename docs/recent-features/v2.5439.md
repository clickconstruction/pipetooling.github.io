Guide: `edit-the-quick-replies` (new); `reply-to-a-person-from-their-thread`'s *Add a quick reply* becomes *Add or change a quick reply* and points at it. No table and no function change: every read and write is cut 3's (#5457) through R2's `quickRepliesIo`.

- **The window** (`reply/QuickRepliesEditor.tsx`, the prototype's `editQuick`, v1.73 to v1.145): the search, **+ Add a line** and the tabs from `editorTabs` (All · Trade partners · Customers · Architects · Anyone · Mine, Removed once a line is removed, each with its count from `editorCounts`). The rows from `editorRows` in `editorGroups` (by who a line is for, then the flow's steps in order, no step last, the most used first), as a table: Line, Used with "N by you", Added, Last change (`lastChange`). The foot is `editorFoot` with **Done**. It reads the lines and every version on open and after each change, and the strip reads again (`useQuickReplies`'s new `reload`).
- **A line opened in place** (`reply/QuickRepliesEditorLine.tsx`): the words and the two tags, **Save** (checked by `editQuickReply` first, so "Nothing changed." never reaches the table) through `edit_quick_reply`, **Remove this line…** asking with `REMOVE_ASK` in the app's confirm, then `remove_quick_reply`; on the Removed tab the line reads only with **Bring it back** (`restore_quick_reply`). Under it, the history: `historyHead`, every version newest first by `versionWords` (the old words struck, the new lit), then **Used by** (`usedBy`) and the last use (`lastUsedWords`, new).
- **The add row** (`reply/QuickRepliesEditorAdd.tsx`): R2's *Add a quick reply* window moved inside the editor, the same form and guess (`qrGuess`, `guessWords`), with the tag fields shared with an opened line (`QuickReplyTagFields`).
- **The two presses, the prototype's**: the strip's **Add a line** is **Edit** again, and its empty words are "No lines for this kind of person yet. Edit adds one." again. *Find a reply* has **Edit quick replies** beside its narrow box, and closes as the editor opens. Every "Add “x” as a quick reply" opens the editor on its add row with the words.
- **Room for residential R3**: the tabs are the kernel's, so a **Phone** tab joins with its own panel. An opened line's fields sit in one section (`data-qr-line-fields`) for the Spanish box, the phone tick and the short name. #5588's `edit_quick_reply` keeps these four named arguments.
- **What main's tables cannot hold yet**: who used each version (uses are per line), live step names (`step_key` has no key to `flow_steps`, so names are the flows' seed), and the name of anyone but the open thread's person in *last used … on* (the day alone).
