# v2.4578 — Sub lien waivers: a payment counts as settled after seven days (2026-10-05)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. The owner's call on the question the lien defects card (punch list #83, row 25) raised: *make it seven days for subs too*. Stacked on the guide rewrite of that row.

## What changed

- [`lienWaiverPick.ts`](../../src/lib/subWorkOrders/lienWaiverPick.ts): `LIEN_WAIVER_SETTLE_DAYS` is `CHECK_CLEAR_DAYS` (`lib/jobs/checkClearing.ts`, seven), not its own five. The **Send a lien waiver** window therefore opens a payment to a sub as *Not settled yet*, and picks the conditional form, until the payment is seven days old. One number now says "settled" for money coming in and money going out.
- The office's tick box (*The money has landed in the sub's account*) still overrides the guess either way.

## Tests
