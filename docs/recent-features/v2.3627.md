# v2.3627 — Hiring: Try-out is a stage — one press makes the helper's login (2026-09-19)

PR 1 of [`to-dos/helper-tryout-loop/README.md`](../../to-dos/helper-tryout-loop/README.md) (Todd, 2026-09-18: *"for the assistant to be able to feed master plumbers helpers while the master plumbers figure out which they like and which they don't like"*). One migration, one edge function, the Hiring tab, two kernels.

## What the to-do had wrong, and the owner's call

The to-do said *Try out* "runs the existing Hire → roster hand-off (`create-user`)". Those are two things: the hand-off inserts a **`people`** row (no login), and `create-user` is **dev-only**. A trial helper has to clock, so they need a login. Asked 2026-09-19, the owner chose **one press makes the login too** over "roster row now, a dev makes the login later". So `create-user` gains a second, narrow door.

## The door — `create-user` with `trial_prospect_id`

A Hiring-board holder (`user_has_team_prospects_access()`; a dev passes too) may call it, and then chooses nothing: the function reads the card **as the caller** (RLS says whether they can see it), refuses one that is not on Screen / Interview, already has a `trial_user_id`, or has no well-formed email, and builds a `helpers` account from the **card's** name and email with a random password nobody is told — the helper signs in by emailed link (`send-sign-in-email`). `email`, `password`, `name`, `role`, `read_only` and `is_sample` in the body are ignored on this door. It sets `users.trial_prospect_id`, then moves the card with an update guarded on the status it read (`trial_user_id IS NULL`, status `active | calling`) — a second press loses with a 409 and its account is deleted again. Every other call is dev-only as before. `verify_jwt = false` stays; the function verifies the token itself, as it always has.

## The migration — [`20260920033000_helper_trial.sql`](../migrations/20260920033000_helper_trial.md)

`team_prospects.trial_user_id` / `trial_started_at`, `users.trial_prospect_id`, status `trial`, and `end_team_prospect_trial(p_prospect_id, p_outcome)` — Hire / Pass in one call, so the card's status and the helper's flag cannot disagree.

**A guard restored.** Reading `users_guard_privileged_columns()` to add the new column showed that `team_prospects_access` had lost its dev-only check: 20260717250000 added it, the 20260903191916 rewrite started from an older body and dropped it, and 20260919044528 dropped the column from the trigger's list. *Users can update own profile* is row-scoped, so any prospects-staff account could have PATCHed its own row to open the Hiring board — and from this release that board can make a login. The check and the column are back; `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` had described the guard as present throughout. `estimator_prospects_access` has never been guarded and is not changed here.

## The tab — [`TeamProspectsTab.tsx`](../../src/components/prospects/TeamProspectsTab.tsx)

- The strip is **Screen → Interview → Try-out → Hire → Review** (`?stage=tryout` deep-links like the others). `groupTeamProspects` gains a `trial` bucket — without it a `trial` card would have fallen back onto the Screen board and held a rank — and the hygiene pass and the drag guard skip it too.
- **Try out** (green) on Screen and Interview cards of a **helper column** — `isHelperColumn` reuses `suggestRosterKind`, so *Helper*, *Apprentice* and *Laborer* columns qualify and an office column never shows it. `tryOutBlocker` names the reason before the function has to (no email, malformed email, already tried). A confirm says what is about to be made and for which address.
- **Try-out cards**, grouped by role column: name, *on trial since Sep 12* (`trialSinceLabel`, company calendar — the v2.3625 lesson), phone, source, the sign-in address, and **Hire** · Talked today · **Pass**. Hire lands on the Hire stage with the checklist; Pass confirms first and says the login still needs archiving (`archive-user` keeps its own gate: dev, controller, pay-approved master).

Not in this PR, by the plan: the leader's verdict card (PR 2), the tally and the nudge and *Keep trying* (PR 3), the column share (PRs 4–6). Column service types do not exist on `team_prospect_roles`, so the login is made with no trade restriction.
