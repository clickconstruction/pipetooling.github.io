# v2.5787 — Quickfill: the Roller, one group at a time on a desk, with the Board as its index (2026-10-23)

Punch list #147, PR 2 of 2 (`to-dos/quickfill-views/`). The owner's own picture of the page: "the user scrolling through the groups like a roller and only seeing one at a time." The phone round (v2.3783) was that already, gated on 640 px; this PR lets a desk use it, with Previous, a strip of dots, a deck of its own and the Board as the index.

- **The kernel** ([`lib/quickfill/views.ts`](../../src/lib/quickfill/views.ts), [its test](../../src/lib/quickfill/views.test.ts)): `QuickfillView` gains `roller`; `rollerDeck(rows, showDone)` (the round's rows in the round's order, without the stations marked today unless the switch is on, the doors last — wider than `roundQueue`, which holds only the due ones); `rollerWords` (*7 of 23 · 12 left with work*, the work from this group on); `rollerDot` (the chip rule's colour, grey before due, hollow for a door); `rollerNext` / `rollerPrev`; the show-done switch's device memory.
- **The screen** ([`QuickfillRoundScreen.tsx`](../../src/components/quickfill/QuickfillRoundScreen.tsx)): a `wide` layout — no ‹, a larger name, a `strip` slot under it, and the bar ← Previous · Skip · Looked · N open · next → · Next → (`onPrev` null at the top of the deck draws it disabled). The phone's layout is untouched when `wide` is off.
- **The strip** ([`QuickfillRollerStrip.tsx`](../../src/components/quickfill/QuickfillRollerStrip.tsx)): one dot button per group (`aria-current="step"` on the current one), the words, *Show the N marked today*, **Groups ▾**.
- **The Board** ([`QuickfillBoard.tsx`](../../src/components/quickfill/QuickfillBoard.tsx)): a dialog over the Roller (Escape, the backdrop or Close shuts it) with a card per group in the deck — a rail in the group's colour, the name, the count (*open* or *at last look*, the round rows' own figure), the group's question, `describeRoundRow`'s line — and the current card tinted. A tap goes to the group. No items on the cards yet: no station reports its rows, only its count; a `previewLines` report would earn a station its two lines.
- **The page** ([`Quickfill.tsx`](../../src/pages/Quickfill.tsx)): `rollerActive = !narrowViewport && view === 'roller' && layoutSettingsLoaded`; the station is the URL's (`/quickfill#<sectionId>`, the station deep link, so a link can open the Roller on a group with `?view=roller#vehicle-odometers`); with no station on the URL the page starts at the top of the deck once the marks' history is in (`roundEventsLoaded`, since the rhythm rule sorts the deck), with `replace` so Back leaves the page. `rollerLooked` takes the next id before the mark, marks, then goes there; `rollerSkip` moves on; ← and → on the keyboard do the same unless a field has focus or a dialog is open. The jump grid, the search, the dock and the dev panel are not drawn in the Roller; the title row and the view switch are. The wrapper renders the body only, through `QuickfillRoundScreenContext`, as on the phone.
- **Walked live** as the dev through headless Playwright: `?view=roller` starts on the first due group with *1 of 22 · 10 left with work* and Previous disabled; Next, Previous and the arrow keys walk the deck and the URL follows; Groups opens the Board with 22 cards and the current one marked; a card jumps to *14 of 22*; the switch widens the deck to 27; Page keeps the hash and lands expanded on that station; the phone round draws no switch. Looked was not pressed on prod.
- **Guide**: *run the office day with Quickfill* § One group at a time.
- **Docs**: `QUICKFILL_ARCHITECTURE.md` (the views section), `GLOSSARY.md` → Quickfill (page), the to-do's status; the v2.5778 fragment's note on a cross-device table is amended, since the memory stays per device.

## Is this the best we can do?

- The Board is the index, not a view of its own: a dot strip at 22 dots needs a way to find a group by name, and a grid of cards is that. If people open it for itself, it becomes a view with one line in `QUICKFILL_VIEWS`.
- The view memory stays per device (the Needs you card's precedent) rather than the table the to-do sketched: the office works Quickfill from a desk, and a table would have meant a migration, a types PR and a fallback for the window between the client deploy and the push, for a preference a second machine re-learns in one press.
- The deck is "not marked today", not the round's "due". On a desk at ten in the morning the round's queue held one station and the rest fell due at noon; walking everything not yet looked at is what "scroll through the groups" asks for, and the dots say which are due.
