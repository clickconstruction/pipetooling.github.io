# v2.4466 — Legal desk: an agreement signed in the evening keeps its day (2026-10-02)

One of the instant-day fixes that follow [v2.4452](v2.4452.md); [v2.4465](v2.4465.md) fixed the days the held rule reads. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

The Legal desk's own lines cut days out of `timestamptz` columns by their first ten characters, so anything stamped after 7 pm CDT (6 pm CST) read as the next day:

- **An agreement's day**: *Signed 2026-04-03 by …* from `job_contracts.signed_at` (or an accepted estimate's `acceptor_consented_at`), and *Sent 2026-12-02 · viewed 2×* from `last_sent_at` / `sent_at`. The desk's Paper tab ([`LegalDeskModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/legal/LegalDeskModal.tsx), `coverageText`), the printed packet ([`legalPacketPrint.ts`](../../src/lib/legal/legalPacketPrint.ts)) and the firm's page ([`LegalFirmMatterView.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/legal/LegalFirmMatterView.tsx)) each sliced it.
- **The desk's header and Evidence tab**: *With the firm since* from `legal_matters.released_at`, a closed matter's day from `closed_at`, and *latest* from a field report's `created_at`.
- **A review request** (`review_requested_at`): the desk's own day count (`daysAgo`, which sliced) and the Dashboard's legal review card ([`legalMatters.ts`](../../src/lib/legal/legalMatters.ts), `buildLegalReview`).
- **The firm's link**: *active since* from `legal_portal_links.created_at` ([`LegalPortalLinkButton.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/legal/LegalPortalLinkButton.tsx)).

## What changed

Each reads `calendarYmdInAppTzFromIso(iso)` from [`dateUtils.ts`](../../src/utils/dateUtils.ts) (`APP_CALENDAR_TZ`).

## Not changed

The packet's lien answers (the owner's call, letter two, the GC's written okay in [`legalLienPaper.ts`](../../src/lib/legal/legalLienPaper.ts)) come from Lien desk items and are left for the Lien desk's own sweep, so the desk and the packet keep agreeing.

## Tests

- `legalMatters.test.ts` (+1): a review asked at `2026-09-10T00:30:00+00:00` (7:30 pm CDT on Sep 9) is 2 days old on Sep 11; `2026-12-02T00:30:00Z` (6:30 pm CST) counts from Dec 1; noon UTC from its own day.
- `legalPacket.test.ts` (+1): the printed packet reads *Signed 2026-04-02 by Aaron Smith* for an agreement signed at 7:30 pm CDT on Apr 2 and *Sent 2026-12-01 · viewed 2×* for one sent at 6:30 pm CST on Dec 1.

Both fail on main. The desk's Paper tab prints its line with the same expression; its render smoke loads no contracts, so the printed packet's test stands for both.

## Checked against prod

Read only, from the worktree dev server: none of the 12 agreements was signed or sent in a Central evening, none of the 17 accepted estimates was accepted in one, no matter has a review request or a close, and the one firm link was made in daytime. Nothing reads differently today.
