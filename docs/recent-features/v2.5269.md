# v2.5269 — Messages: the database side of contracts built from clauses (2026-10-12)

Cut 4c's SQL (GC Reviews helper 9, directed by the lead session GC Review), after its kernels (v2.5247): migration `20261012150000_contract_clauses.sql`, a draft PR for the lead's review, not pushed. Full detail, the verify queries and the rollback: `docs/migrations/20261012150000_contract_clauses.md`.

- **The tables**: `contract_clauses` (a paper's clauses: `sequence_order`, `text_en`, `text_es`, `scope` as `{all}` or trade names, `version`, who published it and when, `retired_at` / `retired_by`; never deleted) and `contract_clause_proposals` (every change: `kind` new, edit or retire, `base_v`, the words, `state`, who decided it, `decline_why`, and once approved the version and revision it made). A dev's or a controller's own change is written already approved, so the approved rows are each clause's history. `contract_template_documents` gains `revision` and `published_at`.
- **The RPCs**: `edit_clause`, `approve_clause_proposal` and `decline_clause_proposal`, the only writers. They refuse in the kernels' words: `gc_office_team()` edits and the new `gc_clause_approve_team()` (dev, controller; `access.test.ts` holds it equal to `GC_CLAUSE_APPROVE_TEAM`) approves. They also refuse a digital twin, a stale proposal (*That clause changed since Rosa proposed this.*) and a decline with no why.
- **The paper per company**: `contract_paper_clauses(paper, trades, lang)`, `assemblePaper`'s server copy, for the sign paths to freeze.
- **The signed copies**: `person_contract_documents`, `gc_owner_contract_sends` and `gc_sows` gain `paper_revision` and `clause_versions`. A trigger refuses any change to them once written.
- **The bed**: `contract-clauses` (`scripts/pgtest-contract-clauses.sh`, `supabase/tests/contract_clauses/`) walks Rosa's proposal to Maria's approval, a stale proposal refused, a decline with its why, the refusals for the superintendent, training mode and a twin, the paper per trade with nothing inherited, and a signed copy that keeps its words after a retire.
- **For later**: the approvers' push and email on a proposal, and the editor's text on a decision (`messages-notify`); the types regen after the push; the Settings screen and the sign paths that write `clause_versions` (wave 2).
