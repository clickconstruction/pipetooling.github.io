# v2.3712 — Day book PR 3: the month as a rhythm (2026-09-22)

PR 3 of [`to-dos/day-book`](../../to-dos/day-book/README.md) (punch list #20). Client only.

## What was missing

The tab listed days. A manager's question about a month — *is the work getting done, by whom, and where are the gaps* — needed the grid the first "best we can do" pass drew: kinds of work as rows, days as columns, initials in the cells, an empty run as the signal. The Month pill was drawn disabled.

## The change

- **Kernel [`dayBookRhythm.ts`](../../src/lib/people/dayBookRhythm.ts)** (+9 tests) — `buildRhythm(view, { today, queueHeldWork, gapAfterDays })` → rows from the chips that name kinds (Billing, Deposits, Contracts, Approvals; Schedule joins with its ledger, Estimating with PR 4), one cell per calendar day of the range with a state: `done` (the initials of who did it, with counts), `none`, `closed` (nobody clocked in — a weekend or a holiday; skipped, never counted), `today` (outlined, never part of a run), `future`, and `gap` — a run of at least three working days of `none` on a row while `queueHeldWork(chip, day)` answered `true`. `null` from `queueHeldWork` never turns amber, so the grid is honest before history carries the queue (PR 7). Two people with the same initials are told apart (`TA` / `ToA`). `rhythmRowSentence`: *applied on 9 of 20 working days · longest gap 4 days* — coverage, not volume. Month helpers: `dayBookMonthOf`, `dayBookShiftMonth`, `dayBookMonthLabel`, `dayBookDaysBetween`, `dayBookInitials`.
- **[`PeopleDayBookMonthGrid.tsx`](../../src/components/people/PeopleDayBookMonthGrid.tsx)** — the grid as a `role="table"`: weekday letter and day number over each column, the row label with its coverage sentence, a button per cell (`data-state`, a title naming who and how many), the legend of initials and the two swatches. Scrolls sideways on a phone.
- **The tab** — Week / Month switch; Month snaps the range to the calendar month, the stepper moves a month at a time, *This month*; the grid replaces the day list under the same range strip; tapping a cell sets the range to that day and returns to the list (a single-day range now reads *Wed, Sep 16*). The grid reads every kind whatever the chip says — its rows are the kinds.
- **Door** — `dayb_view=month` (`dayBookDoor.ts`), written back as the view changes, so a link opens a person's month.
- Help guide *see what the office got done on any day* gains *Reading a month*; the glossary's Day book entry names the grid.

## Verified
