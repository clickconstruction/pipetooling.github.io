# v2.7683 — Legal desk: copy a letter straight into an email (2026-11-17)

## Why

The owner asked whether a letter draft kept in the app can be reached by an owner or the master technician and copied quickly into an email. A letter kept as a text document on a matter could be read on the Legal desk (**Read**, v2.6716) but not copied. The HR file's **Copy as text** is dev-only and copies the raw markdown: the draft banner, the internal notes and the stars with it.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/legal/letterCopyText.ts`** (pure, tested): markdown in, the part to send out.
  - Drops a **draft banner**: the first block of the document when it is a heading or a paragraph that says `DRAFT` in capitals or opens with the word *draft*, and the italic paragraphs right under it.
  - Drops every **internal section**: a heading of any size whose words open with *internal*, with everything under it up to the next heading of its size or larger. An HTML comment goes too.
  - Answers **pieces**: a `#` or `##` heading starts a piece and names it, and the heading's own line is not in the text. Words before the first such heading are a nameless piece. A smaller heading stays inside its piece as a plain line. A heading with nothing under it is a title, not a piece.
  - **Plain text**: no `**`, `*`, `_`, backticks, `#` or rule lines. A list keeps its dash (a star or plus bullet becomes one), a numbered list its number, a link reads *words (address)*. A line break stays where it was written, since a signature block is lines.
  - **Blanks**: a bracketed run such as `[DATE]` or `[counsel: …]` stays as written. Each piece carries its blanks once each with a count, and the total places. A task box, a footnote mark and a bare number are not blanks.
  - `everything` is the whole document as plain text, the banner and the internal sections included.
  - The words: `letterPieceLabel` (one piece, or the nameless first one, is *Copy the letter*; a named piece among several is *Copy “heading”*; a draft says *Copy the draft …*), `letterCopiedWords` (the toast, with the open blanks counted), `letterLeftOutWords`, `letterBlanksWords`.
- **`LegalMatterDocumentsPanel.tsx`**: **Read** on a text document now draws a bar over the words. One press per piece (the first filled, the rest outlined), a quiet **Copy everything as text**, one line on what the copy leaves out, and an amber line per piece listing the blanks still to fill. The toast is a warning when blanks are open. A refused clipboard shows its reason on the panel's alert line. A paper is a draft when its row's state is draft or its text carries a banner. **A counsel-only paper has no copy press**, as on the HR file. The presses need no edit right: whoever can read the row can copy it.
- **`PersonFilePanel.tsx`**: an open named document keeps **Copy as text** (the raw markdown, as before) and adds the piece presses beside it when the document has a draft banner or an internal section. Counsel-only still has none.

## Not in this change

A paper put on a matter before v2.6716 has no text document behind it, so it has no **Read** and no copy press. Text files in that state need filing again as a `.md` or `.txt`, or a backfill that reads each stored file into `documents` and points the row at it. Nothing here writes to those rows.

## Checked

- `letterCopyText.test.ts`: the banner, the internal sections at every size, the pieces, the plain text, the blanks, the labels and the toast, on made-up letters.
- `LegalMatterDocumentsPanel.copy.render.test.tsx`: the presses, what reaches the clipboard, the toast's count, the left-out line, the blanks line, the draft label from the row's state, a refused clipboard, and no press on a counsel-only paper.
- `PersonFilePanel.documents.render.test.tsx`: the HR file's press shows only on a document that leaves words out.
