# v2.4809 — GC mode: the tables for moves and the schedule’s records (2026-10-07)

PR 3 of the schedule's real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/SCHEDULE_REAL_BUILD.md`; its plan `to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/schedule-pr3.md`, both on branch `spike/gc-mode`): moves and the records as tables, in `supabase/migrations/20261007220000_gc_schedule_moves_records.sql` (`docs/migrations/20261007220000_gc_schedule_moves_records.md`), after PR 2's tables (v2.4798).

- Eleven tables: `gc_schedule_moves` with `gc_schedule_move_pushes`, `gc_schedule_move_tells` and `gc_schedule_move_answers`; `gc_schedule_walks` and `gc_schedule_lookahead_marks`; `gc_schedule_waits` with `gc_schedule_wait_holds`; `gc_schedule_late_notices`, `gc_schedule_crew_counts` and `gc_schedule_sends`.
- Append only, by privileges: moves, their pushes, tells and answers, walks, late notices, crew counts and sends. Two column grants are the doors: Undo's pair on a move, and the office's push back on a late notice. A notice's kept day is the company's, from its portal.
- A walk keeps the ids of the moves made during it, a move's version is keyed to its line in `gc_schedule_changes`, and the Board's company record is `company_id`, with its key to come with that table.
- Dev only while it is built: one policy a table for `(SELECT public.is_dev())`, `anon` revoked, and the three house sweeps. Nothing reads or writes them yet; the types come in the PR after the push.
