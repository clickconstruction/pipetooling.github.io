# v2.8293 — A development's page on a phone: every look keeps its whole name (2026-11-22)

The owner asked for the Development page next, after the Materials tables. No migration, no edge function. The page at a computer is not changed.

## What the page turned out to need

The phone audit of this page said *three tables scroll sideways*. That was wrong. At 393 px the page draws no table at all: its money, its jobs and bids, its trips and its questions were given phone layouts in earlier work. The three things that slide are rows of chips, which slide on purpose. So there was no table to turn into rows.

What the walk of the live page did find, on a site with eight looks:

- **Every name on the strip of looks was cut.** The strip is one row with a cell for each look, built for six. Trips (punch list #379) and Schedule (punch list #382) made eight, and eight cells share 377 px. They read *Histo…*, *Jobs & bi…*, *Pla…*, *Questio…*, *Phot…*, *Tri…*, *Sched…*, and the money's figure read *$18…* for *$186k*.
- **Three chip rows slid with nothing to say so.** History's **Show** chips are 714 px in a 347 px row. The Photos look's **Job** chips are 556 px and its **Source** chips 632 px. Each ended wherever the edge fell.

## What changed

- **The strip slides, and a name is never cut.** A cell grows to share the row and never shrinks under its words. Six looks still fit a phone and fill the row as before. Eight are 473 px, and the row slides inside the strip. The look you are in is kept whole in view with the start of the next beside it, and a small arrow marks an edge with more past it. It is the row Jobs and People got in v2.8288 (`ScrollableTabStrip` with `activePeek` and `edgeMarks`).
- **The three chip rows carry the same arrow.** They slide as they did, inside the same strip, and now say where they go on.
- **The strip is the same height**, 53 px, pinned as before, and a press on a look still jumps to it.

## How it is built

- `DevelopmentJumpStrip.tsx`: the phone's cells sit inside `ScrollableTabStrip`, each with its look's key as `data-tabkey`, so the strip keeps the one you are in whole in view as the page scrolls.
- `ScrollableTabStrip.tsx` takes `fill`: the row is at least the strip's width, so cells that grow share the room while they fit. It is opt-in. Bids, Jobs and People pass nothing and are unchanged.
- `src/index.css`: `.developmentPhoneLook` is `flex: 1 0 auto` with a 44 px floor, where it was `flex: 1 1 auto` with no floor, which is what cut the names. The strip's one child takes its whole width.
- `DevelopmentHistoryTools.tsx` and `DevelopmentPhotosLook.tsx`: the phone's chip rows are drawn through the same strip.
