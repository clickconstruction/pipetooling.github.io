Client only; no migration, no edge function. After [v2.4154](v2.4154.md) (the rail) and [v2.4175](v2.4175.md) (the paper). ## Decided here

- **The record is the paper.** The old view showed the signed document in an iframe of the record HTML; the paper already lays out the same facts, so the signed state prints the row's saved fields with the signature closing it. A record filed from an outside document prints heading, property, one note (*the Google Doc filed with this record is the agreement*) and the signature block — never a body the office typed after the fact — and the Keep row carries the document. An uploaded scan of our own handed-over paper keeps its fields: they are what was printed.
- **Doors in rows, not a menu.** The Share ▾ menu hid seven acts behind one button; the sent rail already lays its acts out as labelled rows (Nudge · Sign here · Change it), so the signed rail does the same (Share · Keep · Later). Only the doors this record can honour are drawn — the kernel decides.
- **Neither rail until the rows are read.** A signed job would otherwise flash the draft rail for one round trip; the paper stays, the right column says *Loading the agreement…*.
- **The rail owns its I/O.** The window is 1,100 lines; the last share, the PDF build and the share sheet stay with the rail, as the strip keeps its own coverage read. The window owns only which row is shown.
- **History views in place.** *View* on a signed history row swaps the shown row and offers *← Back*; a second window over the first was the thing being removed.
