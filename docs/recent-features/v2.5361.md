# v2.5361 — Messages: a thread’s windows open from the bottom on a phone (2026-10-12)

Cut 10's P2 for the Messages build (GC Reviews helper 7, directed by the lead session GC Review): screen 1c of `to-dos/gc-mode/messages/phone-mockups.html` on `spike/gc-mode`. Stacked on P1, v2.5357. P3 (hold a line to tie it) and P4 (the flow rail's sheet) build on the sheet.

- **`src/components/PhoneSheet.tsx`** (new): a window that is a centred box at a desk and a sheet from the bottom on a phone (`PHONE_SHEET_QUERY`, 760 px, the width the thread fills the screen at).
  - At a desk it draws the frame the thread's windows drew themselves: the backdrop at z 1300, `min(width, 100%)`, the title row with ×.
  - On a phone the panel is full width with a grab bar and 85% of the room at most. The backdrop is as tall as the visual viewport (`useVisualViewportHeight`), so the sheet's foot sits on the keyboard's top. Where the browser has no visual viewport it fills the screen.
  - Escape closes only the window on top (`useModalStackEntry`) and marks itself handled, so the thread behind stays open.
  - A press on the backdrop stops there before it closes, which is the nested-windows rule.
- **`MessageWindow`, `WhoIsThisWindow`, `JobPickerWindow`** draw their bodies inside `PhoneSheet` in place of their own frames and their own Escape listeners. At a desk they look as before. A fix comes with it: the job picker opened from a message's *change* used to lose Escape to the message window under it, which had registered first.
- **`ThreadWindow`'s ⋯** is a `PhoneSheet` on a phone, with the window's rows full width and Cancel. At a desk it stays the menu under its button.
- **Guide**: `find-every-message-with-a-person-in-one-place`'s *Read a thread on a phone* says the windows open from the bottom.
- **Tests**:
  - `PhoneSheet.render.test.tsx` covers the lead's case. A window opened inside the sheet with the keyboard up opens and follows the keyboard down and up, and closes by Escape, its backdrop and its ×. Each press closes only the window on top, and a stand-in thread behind closes last. The suite also covers the desk frame.
  - `ThreadWindow.sheets.render.test.tsx` covers ⋯ as a sheet and Escape's order through the picker, the message and the thread.
