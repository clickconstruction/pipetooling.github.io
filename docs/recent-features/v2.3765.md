# v2.3765 — Bill tab: the ⋯ menu on a bill opens where you can read it (2026-09-23)

The owner's screenshot: Edit Job → Bill, the ⋯ next to **Record payment** on the $15,200 bill open, and the menu reading "ayment cash, check · ount credit note · peline · mo & f… · k unbills $15,200.00" — its left third gone behind the modal's edge.

## Cause

The row's ⋯ (v2.3478) anchored its menu to the button's **right** edge (`right: 0`, min-width 240px). That is the right shape for a ⋯ at the end of a row, but this one sits at the *left* of its row, right after Record payment, with well under 240px between it and the modal's left edge. The modal body clips overflow, so the menu's first 80-odd pixels were never visible.

## Fix

- `src/lib/jobs/invoiceRowMenuSide.ts` — a pure kernel: given the button's edges, the menu's width and the container's edges, answer `'left'` (anchor the menu's left edge, open rightward) or `'right'` (open leftward). Rightward when it fits; leftward when only that fits; the roomier side when neither does. Unit-tested with the screenshot's numbers and a narrow-phone case.
- `JobFormInvoiceList` measures once per open in a layout effect (button rect, panel width, the ledger's rect as the bounds) and sets `left: 0` or `right: 0` on the panel accordingly. Default is `left`, so the common case is right even before the measurement lands.

No migration, no edge function.
