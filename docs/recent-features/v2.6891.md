# v2.6891 — Permits: the page no longer times out when it opens (punch list #292) (2026-11-10)

Found on a walk an hour after the first pull of Austin, New Braunfels, San Marcos and Seguin (v2.6859, v2.6861): a plain open of Prospects → Permits drew *Could not read the permits: canceling statement due to statement timeout*.

## Why

Each read behind the page (`research_permits_v2`, `permit_skim_wait`, `permit_funnel`) takes about two and a half seconds on its own, measured through the dev seat after the pull put 5,400 more rows in `permits`. The tab ran the ledger's windowed read and the every-permit read together on mount, with the month's count beside them. Three heavy reads at once crowd each other past the database's eight-second statement limit; one alone does not.

## What changed

- **`ProspectsResearchTab`**: the ledger's windowed read runs only where its rows are drawn, on Ledger and Map (the effect on `wide`). The Pulled line reads `loadLatestPull` on its own. The ledger's loading and error lines show only on those two views.
- After a write (`reloadLists`) the every-permit read runs first and the ledger's after it, one at a time.
- **`PermitCountStrip`** mounts once the lists have arrived.

## Not changed, and worth doing

The reads themselves are slow for what they return. The plumbing pairing matches a plumbing permit to a building permit by number or by address, per row. A rewrite of that join is a migration of its own.

## Tests

`ProspectsResearchTab.render.test.tsx`: the tab opens on one call of the heavy read, and the ledger's windowed read is made when Ledger is opened.

## Walked

1360 wide on localhost against prod: the page opens with its card, its strip counts and the Pulled line and no error; Ledger opens with its 163 rows.
