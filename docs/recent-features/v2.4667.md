## What changed

- **One rule, `supabase/functions/_shared/submittalRecord.ts`.** A revision is on the GC's record when it was shared, or when it holds a reviewer's answer on a row or part the GC sees **and** has a built package (`package_path`). It went out by email as that package, and the answer came back the same way.
  - The answers that count are 'entered' (typed in by the office), 'robot' (read from their file, confirmed by a person) and 'room' (given on the link once the revision was on the record, so the GC answering the rest there keeps it on the page).
  - A part's 'carried' call and an order-only row or part never count, so a new draft that inherits approvals is not published.
  - The newest revision on the record is current.
  - `loadRevisionStandings` reads the calls only on revisions nobody shared. `isTypedAnswer`, `recordStanding`, `onRecord`, `linkShows` and `answeredByEmailAt` are pure.
- **The package is the guard.** An answer typed on a revision that never had a package does not publish it. It `waits_for_package`, and the office is told to build the package.
- **The three functions read the rule, and nothing else changed in them:**
  - `get-submittal-room` lists the revisions on the record, each with `answeredByEmailAt`, the newest typed answer's own day.
  - `open-submittal-pdf` serves an emailed revision's package.
  - `submit-submittal-review` takes answers on the newest revision on the record. `decideVerdict` reads `submittalOnRecord`, and the stale message reads *A newer revision has been added since you opened this page*.
  - `_shared/submittalRoomPayload.ts`, bundled by eleven functions, is untouched. The new types live in `submittalRecord.ts`, so no other function's bundle changes.
- **The GC's page.**
  - The chip reads *Rev 3 · answered by email · Oct 2*.
  - Above that revision's rows, one line: *You answered this revision by email. Our office typed your answers in here, as the record.*
  - Each row shows the reviewer's answer and name, never staff's.
  - The procurement card reads the revision like any other.
- **The office.**
  - The line under the rows says *Until you share Rev 4, the link shows Rev 3, answered by email.*
  - What the GC sees lists that revision in its chips.
  - Its list line names a revision that `goes on their page once it has a package`.
  - Their call (step 6) gains a heads-up on a revision nobody shared, worded for both cases and before and after the first answer (`emailedRecordLine`).
  - The tab reads the record with the same loader (`standings`), re-read when a share, a package or an answer changes.
- **Docs**: `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` (all three sections), `GLOSSARY.md` → Review room, `BIDS_DOCUMENT_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md` → SU-9, the guide *build a submittal package*, and the Submittals to-do with the no-package case.
