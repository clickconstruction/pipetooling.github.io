# v2.7939 — Robots: an assistant can change a price in the robot's price book (2026-11-19)

A new pair on `dev-mcp` (server 0.111.0, 137 write verbs, 84 naming no RPC, 188 tools): **`plan_robot_book_price`** → **`apply_robot_book_price`**. The owner, 2026-11-19: "build the next gap: the robot book price change".

## Why

The audit of what an agent can stand up alone listed it as the sixth gap. The twin seat reads a 🤖 Robot Default price book (`get_robot_book`) and adds to it (`extend_robot_book`). It never changes a price that stands. A wrong price was a person's edit on the Pricing tab's book panel.

## What it does

It makes that panel's own write (`BidsPricingTab.savePricingEntry`, the editing branch): the total and the three stage prices on the entry's own `price_book_entries` row, as the dev.

- `prices = { service_type_id | version_id, reason, entries }`.
- The book is named by its trade or by its own id. It must be `is_robot` with no bid.
- `entries` holds up to twenty, each `{ name | entry_id, price }`.
- `reason` is required, 10 to 500 characters.

## Why the plan counts bids

A robot book is never copied into a bid (`pricingWriteTarget`'s `robot` kind). Every count row assigned to an entry with no unit price of its own reads the entry live. A changed price moves every such bid at once.

The plan reads the assignments that point at the named entries, and the bids they are on.

- Each entry answers before → after, with the rows and bids that read it.
- A sent bid among them is warned by name. Its grid would show a new number where its paper keeps the old one.
- A robot bid with a locked or scored run is warned by name. The locked total on the run stays, and the bid's grid moves.
- A row with its own unit price is not reached, and is counted apart.
- A refused read of the rows is said. A read that stops at its limit says at least.

## An entry keeps its shape

- The whole price in rough-in, as `extend_robot_book` makes an entry: rough-in follows the price.
- No stage prices: none are written.
- Stages split by a person: the entry is held until `rough_in`, `top_out` and `trim_set` come with the price.

## Held, and the rest go on

A name the book does not hold is held, with the door that adds one. A name two entries share is held, with their ids. An entry named twice is held. The others are planned and applied. A plan that changes nothing is refused.

## The record

`price_book_entries` keeps no history. The reason rides the logged ask. The apply's line in `dev_mcp_calls` carries the book and each landed price before and after (`robotBookLogLine`), cut by whole entries at 900 characters. The way back is the same pair with the before prices.

## What it leaves alone

- It adds no entry. `extend_robot_book` on the twin seat does.
- It touches no assignment and no bid's own pricing copy.
- The labor book and the takeoff book have no door here.
