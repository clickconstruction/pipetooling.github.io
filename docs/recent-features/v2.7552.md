# v2.7552 — Sub papers: every paper carries a number (sub labor train) (2026-11-16)

It stands on the register that reached prod on 2026-11-16 (`20261117040000_sub_papers.sql`, v2.7474) and on Taunya's answer to call 1 the same day: the papers keep their names, so a number may print under one.

**This step was written, not cut.** The design spike's snapshot read the register and never called the mint.

## What landed

- `src/lib/subWorkOrders/subPaperRegister.ts`, a pure kernel:
  - `subPaperRegister(rows, dayOf)`: each sheet's live work statement (not replaced, not void) and the last statement of account, by the day it ended. One made to date ended the day it was made.
  - `subPaperFingerprint(paper)`: a short text of a paper's figures. It leaves out what moves by itself (the number, the revision, the day issued, the time prepared), so a reprint matches and a change does not.
  - `mintSubPaperArgs(paper, personId, range)`: what `mint_sub_paper` is handed. A sending's record goes by its sending, a payment from before sendings by its own row.
  - `numberedSubPaper(...)`: the paper drawn again under what the register answered. A new revision's foot says which copy it replaces, by the day that copy was issued.
  - `mintRefusal` and `mintedFrom`: a database the migration has not reached is told from a refusal, and a refusal keeps its reason.
- `SubPapersWindowConnected.tsx`:
  - Reads this person's rows of `sub_papers` and hands the kernel's numbers to the sheets, so a sheet already numbered previews under its number.
  - **Print and Download register the paper first**, then draw the PDF, name the file and file the sent copy under the number that came back. A print registers while its tab waits, and its filing is read after.
  - **A paper the register refuses is not made**, and the window says the register's reason. A training account, a digital twin and an archived account are refused by the functions themselves.
  - Where the register is missing, a paper is made as it was before numbers.
  - The window waits for the register's first read, so the period it opens on does not depend on which read lands first.
- `SubPapersWindow.tsx`: the period opens on *Since the last statement* once the person has one (the card's call 5), *What it will show* says a statement takes its number when it is made, and the line under the buttons says the file is named for the number.

## What a number means here

- **Work statement:** `WS-<job>-<n>`, the sheet's for good. The same figures again are a reprint. Different figures are the next revision under the same number.
- **Statement of account:** `SA-<n>`, a new paper every time.
- **Payment record:** its sending's own `PR-<n>`, since v2.7475. The mint records that the paper was made.
- **The snapshot** the register keeps is the paper as drawn before its number. A reprint from it takes the number and the revision from its row.
