# v2.8191 — Painted maps: Photo of a spot, a place on a job that starts with the camera (2026-11-21)

Punch list #413, step 1b. Taunya's idea of 2026-11-21: tie a photo to a place on a job's map. The card found the places already in the app and unused, and planned a quicker door onto them.

## Why

Setting a place on a job took a map first: open the job, press **＋ Place on this job**, drag a dot, press a kind, type a word, add a photo, save. A plumber standing over an open trench has a phone in one hand and wants the photo, since the trench is filled within the hour. The photo should come first, and the dot should already be where the person stands.

## What ships

**The press.** **Photo of a spot**, under a job's address with **＋ Paint a map** and **＋ Place on this job** (`JobPaintedMapsStrip`). It is on every tab of the job window and on the job card in Job Mode. On a phone it is the full width and 56 px tall, over the two quiet presses. On a desk it is an ordinary press, first in the row. A sheet that shows a job's maps alone, such as On my way, does not have it.

**One press, two things.** The press opens the camera and reads the phone once, in the same press (`readSpotNow`). So the reading is of the moment the photo was taken, and not of wherever the person is when the photo comes back.

**The sheet.** When the photo comes back, the job's list of places opens already adding a place (`PaintedMapWindow`, through the door's new `spot`). A job with no list yet gets one, saved with this first place. The sheet shows the photo first and large, then *What is here?*. One press on a kind and one on **Add this place** finish it.

**Where the dot starts** (`spotStart` in `paintedSpot.ts`):

- A good reading: on the reading, with the phone's margin kept on the place.
- A weak reading: on the reading too, with the amber line that says to drag the dot. This is the place sheet's own rule for **Put the dot where I am**.
- A rough reading, which is an area and not a spot: the dot is not moved. It starts on the job's address, or where the map is looking, and the sheet shows the way to the Precise Location switch.
- No reading: the same, and the sheet says why under the press.
- A photo more than five minutes old: no reading is used at all (`spotPhotoIsFresh`). It was picked from the phone's library and is not of where the person stands. A line at the top of the sheet says how old the photo is and that the dot was not put where the person stands.

**A photo that waits.** If the sheet has nowhere to put the dot yet, the window says *Your photo is ready* over **＋ Place**, and the photo goes onto the place that press starts. A photo put down with its place is gone: the next place starts with none.

## What a phone gives a web page

Nothing here reads a spot from inside a photo. Chrome on Android takes it out before a page gets the file, and an iPhone takes it out of a photo taken through the page. The phone's own reading at the press is the one source.

## Not done here

- **No signal.** A place set with no signal is not kept on the phone. The sheet says the save failed and keeps the photo and the words while it stays open. A place that waits as a job photo does is the card's step 2b.
- **Not walked on a real phone.** Whether an iPhone opens the camera cleanly while it asks for location the first time is the first thing to watch. The second press works either way.
- The photo is the place's photo and is not filed in the job's Drive folder, which is the card's call 6.
