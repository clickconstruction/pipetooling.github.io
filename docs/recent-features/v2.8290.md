# v2.8290 — The new phone layout: a leader's and a superintendent's phone starts in Job Mode (2026-11-22)

Behind the **New phone layout** switch (v2.8285). With the switch off, or at a desk, nothing here draws differently. No migration, no edge function, no new component.

## The rule

Job Mode is the phone-shaped Dashboard: the day's row, the day's card, the press bar and four tabs at the foot. A stored choice per person per device always won. With nothing stored it was on for helpers and subcontractors and off for everyone else.

Under the switch, at a phone's width, nothing stored is on for a **master technician** and a **superintendent** too. They are the people who lead a job, and their phone now starts where a helper's does.

- **A stored choice still wins.** `'0'` is off and `'1'` or `'card'` is on, for every role, switch off or on. A person who turned Job Mode off on that phone stays off.
- **The gear menu works as it did.** The Job Mode row is ticked, and one press stores `'0'`. The page then falls to the mode under it: Dispatch Mode for a master technician, the full app for a superintendent.
- **Show full dashboard works as it did.** It opens the whole Dashboard for the visit.
- **The first-run prompt is not drawn where Job Mode is already on.** *Working in the field? Turn on Job Mode.* would have shown on the page behind Show full dashboard, offering what the person has.

Of the 36 cells of role by stored value, two change: a master technician with nothing stored and a superintendent with nothing stored. A test names the two.

## How it is built

- `src/lib/jobModeToggle.ts` holds the rule. `jobModeDefaultForRole`, `isJobModeEnabled`, `readJobModeEnabled` and `showJobModeFirstRunCard` take `JobModeRuleOptions`, `{ newPhoneLayout }`. Left out, each answers as before. `JOB_MODE_PHONE_HOME_ROLES` is the one list of the two roles.
- `useJobModeEnabled` reads `useNewPhoneLayout().on` and hands it to the rule. The shell and the Dashboard both read the mode through `usePageMode`, which holds this hook, so the two turn together and no screen has a second rule for *phone*.
- `DashboardJobModeFirstRunCard` reads the same hook for the prompt.
- No change to `Layout.tsx`, `Dashboard.tsx`, the Job Mode card, the press bar or the pins row.

## What a leader had, and where it is now

A leader's phone with nothing stored started in Dispatch Mode, on the full Dashboard. Under the switch it starts on Job Mode's Dashboard.

| Was on the first screen or its tabs | Is now |
|---|---|
| The full Dashboard, every card on it | **Show full dashboard**, at the foot of Job Mode's Dashboard, for the visit |
| **Clock In** | The press bar's **Clock In** |
| **Job Report** | The press bar's **Leave report**, and the full Dashboard |
| The Tally square and its count | The **My spend** square, with the same count |
| **Paint a map** | Its square in the day's row |
| **Write someone up** | Under the day's card |
| My Schedule | On Job Mode's Dashboard, under the hours block |
| The Needs you card and the office's round | Job Mode's **Inbox** tab, and the full Dashboard |
| His own blue buttons | At the top of Job Mode's Dashboard, untouched |
| Dispatch Mode's tabs: its home, the team's Schedule, the team's Inbox, Customers and PO | Gear menu, **Dispatch Mode**: one press brings the tabs back and stores Job Mode off on that phone |
| The red count on Dispatch Mode's Inbox tab | Not on Job Mode's tabs. It is on the Teams Inbox card of the full Dashboard, and comes back with Dispatch Mode |
| The home press in the top bar | Job Mode's first tab, **Dashboard** (v2.8285) |
| *Working in the field? Turn on Job Mode.* | Not drawn: Job Mode is on |

A superintendent's phone with nothing stored started on the full Dashboard with no tabs. The same rows hold, without the Dispatch Mode rows. A superintendent whose Dashboard view is **Short** gets the short Job Mode page (punch list #384), as he does today with Job Mode on.

## Three things the owner should know

1. **The count of what waits is not on Job Mode's Dashboard.** Job Mode keeps Needs you in its Inbox tab, and has since v2.917. v2.8289, the other half of this work, puts the one-line door on Job Mode's Dashboard. The two share no code file and merge in either order. Until v2.8289 is live, a leader who starts in Job Mode reads the count in the Inbox tab.
2. **Dispatch Mode's Inbox count has no place on Job Mode's tabs.** Job Mode's Inbox is the person's own, so it has no team inbox to count. Giving a leader's Job Mode Inbox the team inboxes would be new drawing, and it was not built here.
3. **A leader on the Short view lands on Job Mode's card, not the short Dashboard.** That is today's rule for a leader with Job Mode on (`shortPageFor`). His short Dashboard is behind **Show full dashboard**.

*Not now* on the prompt is not a stored Job Mode choice. A leader who pressed it has nothing stored, so his phone starts in Job Mode under the switch. If *Not now* should count as off, that is one line in `jobModeDefaultForRole`'s caller and a test.

## Tests

- `jobModeToggle.test.ts` (26): every role, the switch off and on, every stored value. Off answers as before for all 36 cells. On differs in two cells and the test names them. The prompt and Job Mode never answer yes together.
- `useJobModeEnabled.newPhoneLayout.render.test.tsx` (6): the nine roles off, on at a phone and on at a desk. A stored off wins for every role. A leader's off press stores `'0'` and holds on the next visit. Ticking the switch turns the page at once and stores nothing for Job Mode.
- `usePageMode.newPhoneLayout.render.test.tsx` (8): the mode each role starts in, off and on. A leader's Job Mode row and Dispatch Mode row each go back to Dispatch Mode with Job Mode stored off, and Dispatch Mode is never written to the server. A leader comes back from Task Mode to Job Mode.
- `DashboardJobModeFirstRunCard.newPhoneLayout.render.test.tsx` (4): the prompt is drawn for the two roles off and at a desk, and for nobody on a phone with the switch on.
