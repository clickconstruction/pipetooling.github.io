# v2.8417 — Smaller cents in Jobs: Billing, the Pipeline, AR and Sub Labor (2026-11-23)

Part 2 of the owner's ask of 2026-11-23, the cents smaller than the rest of an amount across the app: the last of three Jobs pieces. The door is v2.8374's `Money` and `MoneyText`, and the sweep is v2.8376's `scripts/codemods/small-cents-sweep.mjs`. No migration, no edge function.

## What moved

54 screen files under `src/components/jobs` that the job window (v2.8413) and the Lien desk (v2.8415) pieces did not take: the Billing tab, the Stages row and its bill windows, `BankPaymentsModal`, `SendRecordInvoiceModal`, the Stripe bill panels, the GC worklist, call sheet and review windows, the AIA window and its history, Crew P&L, Parts, Materials, the weekly money windows, Sub Labor with its ledgers and payment windows, and the subs tiles. 69 plain amounts to `Money` and 146 pieces of words to `MoneyText`, by the script. The characters on the screen are the same. `scripts/codemods/small-cents-swept/jobs-billing.txt` lists the 54 files, so `check:small-cents` now refuses a bare amount written there.

**One line by hand.** The Billing tab's *paid*, *billed* and *unbilled* lines under each total come from the kernel `billingRowMoneyTokens` as labels, which the script cannot follow. `JobsBillingTab` draws each label through `MoneyText`. The look found it: the totals had small cents and the lines under them did not.
