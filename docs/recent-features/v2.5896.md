# v2.5896 — Customer reviews: the review page, the table and the function (2026-10-26)

Punch list #185, the owner's yes of the same morning on the design (*"I like all of this, could you please build it"*): the plan and the mock-up are in [`to-dos/customer-reviews/`](../../to-dos/customer-reviews/README.md) (PR 1, #6185). This is PR 2 of the train, the smallest shippable piece: the page a customer opens, the row it writes, the function that serves it. Nothing in the office changes yet, and no text carries the link until PR 3.

- **The table**: `customer_reviews` (migration `20261026180000`, [its page](../migrations/20261026180000_customer_reviews.md)): one row per job, the link's SHA-256 and a 60-day expiry (the estimate's pattern; the token is never stored), the language, the ask's stamps, `opened_at`, `answered_at`, `stars` 1 to 5, `words`, `better` (what we could have done better), `google_pressed_at`, and the office's `called_at` / `called_by` / `call_note`. The office team reads, updates and deletes; the three fences cover it. `customer_review_ask(p_job_id)` makes or remakes the link and hands the token back once; a job already answered is refused in words. `docs/ACCESS_CONTROL.md` carries the row.
- **The function**: `customer-review` ([`docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`](../EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md#customer-review)), `verify_jwt = false`: GET answers the page by the token's hash (404 unknown, 410 run out) and stamps the first open; POST takes the answer once, then *better*, then the Google press. Each writes the customer's thread through `_shared/reviewThreadLine.ts` over the pure [`reviewEventLine.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/reviewEventLine.ts) (+ 5 tests under vitest): *⭐ Dana left 5 stars on job 1312: “…”*, *⚠* below four stars, *⚠ What Dana says we could have done better on job 1312: “…”*, *⭐ Dana pressed Post it on Google.* The Google link is `app_settings.google_review_link_v1`, read when it is an https address; the block that sets it is PR 4's.
- **The page**: [`pages/CustomerReview.tsx`](../../src/pages/CustomerReview.tsx) at `/r/:token`, the estimate accept page's shell, light, 48 px stars, the word under them (*Great*), the words box, *Send 5 stars*. After Send: at five stars the Google card (*Would you put this on Google for us?*, **Post it on Google** opening the link in a new tab with their words copied), at fewer the question *What could we have done better?*, the promise *Someone from the office will call you*, and the same Google line under it. The words are [`lib/reviews/reviewPage.ts`](../../src/lib/reviews/reviewPage.ts) (+ 6 tests) in English and Spanish (the Spanish waits behind `PORTAL_SPANISH_ON`, as the portal's does); `GOOGLE_ASK_AT = 'everyone'` is the owner's call, with `'five'` one line away. `CustomerReview.render.test.tsx` (4) walks both paths on the sample and a live token's 410.
- **The registrations**: the route in [`customerSurfaceRegistry.ts`](../../src/lib/customerSurfaceRegistry.ts); two homeowner journey steps, *Review page* and *Review thanks*, drawn on What customers see from the sample tokens (`/r/sample`, `/r/sample-done`); the person journey answers them from `customer_reviews` (asked, opened, answered with the stars, the Google press). `APP_DIRECTORY.md`, the Glossary's *Customer review* entry, `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`'s public routes.
- **The ask kernel, for PR 3**: [`lib/reviews/reviewAsk.ts`](../../src/lib/reviews/reviewAsk.ts) holds the step's own line (*{first}, thank you for paying for {job}. Would you tell us how we did? It takes two taps: {review}*), its Spanish, the call script and the link's address; `reviewAskIo.ts` mints through the RPC.
- `src/types/database.ts` carries the table and the RPC by hand until the regen after the push.
