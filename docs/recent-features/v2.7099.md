# v2.7099 — The Bridge shows a cash balance beside an accrual balance, each with its runway in days (punch list #334, PR 6) (2026-11-12)

PR 6 of the Bridge train (card #334, `to-dos/bridge-pulse/`), the bottom of its second stack, on PR 1 (v2.7089). Client only. No migration, no edge function, and no kernel that is copied to the edge functions changed.

The owner, of the red band *Under the floor today*: it is "not super productive". Then: "show a cash balance next to an accrual balance. The cash balance is our bank account and the accrual balance is our accounts receivable minus our accounts payable minus payroll plus our bank balance"; it "should also subtract subs"; "next to both we should show runway in days", "runway is just counting payroll and overhead"; and "move the bridge slowest step to the left of the cash section".

## What changed

- **A kernel for the two balances** (`src/lib/bridge/moneyCard.ts`, new, pure): `cashLine` is cash on hand today with one sentence against the floor (green while the eight weeks clear it, amber with the day it goes under, red when it is under today; with no balance read, the forecast's low point stands in). `accrualLine` is cash, plus what customers owe, less what the company owes: the page's own net position, so the first screen and the chart behind Money cannot read two amounts. What the company owes is the supply bills, the payroll owed and the sub labor owed, and the line says *subs included*. `burnPerDay` is payroll for a week over seven plus overhead for a day, the two figures the cash forecast already spends. `runwayDays`, `runwayWords` and `runwayFoot` say how long a balance carries that burn, and what the burn is. `accrualPressLabel` is what a screen reader hears on the press.
- **The first row** (`BridgeWeek.tsx`): *Made last week*, *Slowest step*, then one `BalancesTile` holding both balances, each with its runway under the number, and one foot line that says what runway counts. On a phone the first two tiles share a row and the balances take the next. The amber line that names the accounts left out of cash sits under the cash balance and opens the same place.
- **The red band is off the page.** The kernel `weekCall.ts` still hands a wide cash tile in a week cash leads, because the Monday email draws it. The page no longer draws that tile or the *Cash low point, 8 weeks* tile. The card under the tiles is as it was, including *Keep cash above the floor* in a week cash leads.
- **The accrual balance is a press** (`Bridge.tsx`): it opens the Money door with **Money owed** open, and lands on it (`bridge-money-owed`).
