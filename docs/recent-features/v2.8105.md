# v2.8105 — Painted maps: a shared map's reader whose phone gives only an area is shown as an area, not as a spot (2026-11-20)

Punch list #402, found by the audit of the card and decided by the owner on 2026-11-20: *send it labelled*. Client only: no migration, and the function `painted-map-link` is unchanged.

## What was wrong

While **Show where I am** is on, the shared map at `/way/<code>` sends the reader's point to the company. Three things were off once v2.8004 taught the page what an area is.

1. **The office was shown a pin on the middle of an area.** The page sent every reading, a rough one too. The Share sheet's **Who opened it** read the point and not its margin, so it said *Showed where they were* with **See that place** on a spot that could be miles from the reader.
2. **A first reading off a cell tower was kept.** The page sent the first reading at once. A phone with Precise Location on can open with one rough reading, which the page itself was still waiting on. If the reader closed the page within a minute, that was the only point the office had.
3. **The page said two things.** Under an area it read *the map cannot show where you stand*, and two lines down *Made Up Plumbing sees where you are*, with *the dot moves with you* where no dot is drawn.

## What changed

**The page sends what it has settled on.** Sending moved from the phone's callback to the reading the page has made up its mind about (`wayReading`).

| The reading | What is sent |
|---|---|
| None yet, or a rough one under 4 seconds old | Nothing |
| A spot, sure or weak | The point and its margin, at once and then about one a minute |
| An area, once the readings have stayed rough for 4 seconds | The area's middle and its margin, the same way |
| The first spot after an area | As soon as 20 seconds have passed since the area, not a minute (`WAY_SPOT_AFTER_AREA_MS`, the function's own gap between two points) |

**The office reads the margin.** `loadPaintedMapVisits` now reads `first_accuracy_m` and `last_accuracy_m`, which the table has kept since v2.7411. `visitPoint` picks the point a visit's line speaks of: the latest one that is a spot, then the first when that is a spot, and an area only when neither is. A point with no margin is taken as a spot, as it always was.

- A spot reads as before: *Showed where they were, Nov 15, 3:05 PM.* with **See that place**.
- An area reads *Showed only the area they were in, 4 miles across, Nov 15, 3:05 PM.* and has no place to open. Its width is `areaAcrossWords`, the same words the dashed circle's pill uses.

**The page's line under an area says what is sent.** *Made Up Plumbing sees only this area while this is on. Press Hide where I am to stop.* (`wayAreaSeenWords`). The sample reads *Where you are stays on your phone. We never see it.* Neither speaks of a dot.

Rows kept before this change still hold rough points. They now read as areas too, since the margin was always kept beside the point.

- `paintedWay.test.ts`: when a point is due, and the words. `PaintedWay.render.test.tsx`: nothing is sent at 3,999 ms of a rough reading and the area is sent at 4,000 ms, a reading off a cell tower that a spot follows is never sent, and a spot after an area goes at 20 seconds and not before.
- The guide `paint-a-map`, the Glossary, `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`, the function's section in `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` and the journey step `painted-map-page` say what the page sends and what the office reads.

## Not done, and known

- Not read on a real phone. Every reading in the tests and the walk was made up.
- The function still takes whatever point a page sends. An old copy of the page, open in a tab, sends rough readings as before. The office's sheet reads those as areas, by their margin.
- An area's line has no way to open the area on a map. Its middle is nobody's place, so no pin is offered. A link to the whole circle would need a map of the app's own.
