# v2.2839 — Customer portal statement shows every open bill, including bills on jobs still in progress (2026-09-05)

Journey-map Phase 4, Tier-1 #2(b) "One bill truth" — J21-F1 = J22-F1 (blocker, live). Owner decision 6 (2026-09-05): **an "open bill" is defined by invoice status** — a billed, unpaid invoice is owed regardless of the job's stage.

**The live gap.** On the specimen GC (Knight Contracting) the office AR drill showed **$38,036.27 / 16 bills** while the portal preview rendered **BALANCE DUE $19,453.47 / 11 bills** — a delta of **$18,582.80 (48.86%), exact to the cent** = the five 9/1 progress/change-order bills on jobs 977/963/978, all in `working` status (963 was on that day's Crew Day schedule). A customer told "check your portal" paid the visible half and the office chased the rest from a statement the customer's own page never showed.

**Mechanism (before).** The portal enforced *job* status twice: [`customer-portal/index.ts`](../../supabase/functions/customer-portal/index.ts) fetched invoices only for `jobs.filter(j => j.status === 'billed')`, and [`_shared/portalMergedBills.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/portalMergedBills.ts) `buildPortalBills` re-filtered `billedJobs` the same way. The promote helper only flips a job to `billed` when *fully* invoiced (BILLING_FLOWS.md), so a billed invoice on a working job is a designed progress-billing state with live Stripe pay links — and the company's own outbound GC statement (`get_gc_statement_email_payload`, migration `20260806232759`) already includes "billed-status invoices from jobs of any NON-PAID status". The portal was the lone outlier; a GC receiving both artifacts saw two totals from one company.

**Rule (after)** — one kernel, [`_shared/portalBillMembership.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/portalBillMembership.ts), aligned line-for-line with the GC payload RPC:
- **Invoice rows**: `status = 'billed'` invoices on jobs of **any non-paid status** (`jobCarriesOpenBills`: `status !== 'paid'`; a null/empty status is excluded, mirroring SQL `j.status <> 'paid'` on NULL).
- **Shell rows** (invoice-less fallback, `revenue − payments_made`): **`billed` jobs only** (`jobPrintsShellRemainder`). This is deliberately NOT widened — a working job with zero billed lines has nothing owed yet, and printing its revenue remainder would invent a debt (the 2b verification note).
- `openBillJobIds(jobs)` feeds the edge function's `.in('job_id', …).eq('status', PORTAL_OPEN_INVOICE_STATUS)` fetch; `buildPortalBills` uses the same two predicates so the builder can never disagree with the fetch.
- Per-job recap arithmetic (Billed to date − payments = Balance) is unchanged and now covered on a working job by test.

**Surfaces**: `customer-portal` edge function (all three audiences — `all`, `customer`, `gc`); the globe modal's office-only "Jobs on this statement" mirror follows the payload with no client-side status filter, so it agrees automatically. **`sub-portal` is NOT affected** — it has its own `_shared/subPortalStatement.ts` and only references `portalMergedBills` in a comment. The v2.2760 sample-token branch (`token=sample` / `sample-gc`) is untouched — fixtures, no rows.

**Telemetry**: `public_page_views` has no payload/jsonb column and its `surface` CHECK is closed, so no DDL was added. Instead the function logs one structured line per rendered statement — `{"event":"portal_statement_rendered","customer_id","audience","via","statement_total_cents","bill_count"}` — queryable in the edge-function logs against the office AR figure. Revisit if the Settings → Usage panel (v2.2342) wants it as a column.

**Tests**: `src/lib/portal/portalBillMembership.test.ts` (new — the predicates, the J21 shape: 2 billed-job bills + 5 working-job bills → 7 rows / $38,036.27, working job with no line → nothing, paid job with a billed line → nothing, recap arithmetic on a working job) and `src/lib/portal/portalMergedBills.test.ts` (updated: "ignores non-billed jobs entirely" became "a non-billed job with no billed invoice contributes nothing", plus a working-job bill case).
