# v2.7171 — Lien desk Share: a job with no GC no longer prints a blank name (2026-11-13)

## What was wrong

The Lien desk's Share words each notice job with its GC's name. A job with no GC set on it reaches the payload with `gc: ''` (`gcName` in `src/lib/jobs/lienDeskShare.ts` returns the empty string when the entry has no `gcCustomerId`), and the kernel printed that as it was. Seen on the live desk on 2026-11-13, while walking v2.7138 (the job's name and the amount here are made up):

- under **By GC**: `• , 1 job, $1,200`
- under **Waiting for approval**: `• Tire Barn, , $1,200`

The email had the same hole: a group head with an empty bold name, and a waiting row whose second line began with the dot before the months.

## The fix

`supabase/functions/_shared/lienDeskStatus.ts` gains `lienStatusGcWords(gc, mid?)` and the constant `LIEN_STATUS_NO_GC`. An empty name reads **No GC on the job**, and **no GC on the job** inside a line. These are the notice gate's own words (`lienDeskGates.ts`, gate 2), since a notice goes to the owner and the GC and the desk holds it until one is set.

- `lienStatusGroups` names the group with it, so the text's *By GC* list, the email's plain twin and the email's group head all read the same.
- The three *Waiting for approval* lines (text, plain twin, email row) use it.
- The lien lines are unchanged: an affidavit with no GC already says *We contracted with the owner.*

The payload is unchanged. It still carries `''`, and `parseLienStatusPayload` still takes it, so a page from before this change and a function from before it read each other as before.

Checked and left alone: the subject and the desk link read the scope's GC, which is `null` or a real name, and the **Which liens** menu offers a GC only for a job that has one.

## The deploy

The share text and the email's preview are drawn in the browser, so they are right from the merge. The sent email is drawn by `send-lien-desk-summary`, which is owed a redeploy before it says the new words.

## Tests

`src/lib/jobs/lienDeskStatus.test.ts`, *a notice job with no GC on the job never prints a blank name*: the group's name, the text, the plain twin, the email, and the parser still taking the empty name. Four of the five failed before the fix.

Seen on a screen before the merge: this branch's dev server on the live desk's data (2026-11-13, a read-only walk, nothing sent). The one job with no GC read *no GC on the job* on its waiting line and sat under *No GC on the job, 1 job* in By GC, and the email's preview headed the waiting row's second line *No GC on the job*.

Seen after the merge and the redeploy: one **Email me a test** from the live desk, to the dev's own address. The function answered 200, the send log reads delivered, and the deployed kernel renders the posted payload with the same words. The delivered mail itself was not opened.
