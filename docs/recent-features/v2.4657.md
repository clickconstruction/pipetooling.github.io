# v2.4657 — Contracts: a paper signed by two is filed as two signatures (2026-10-06)

Punch list #64, the follow-up the owner approved. [v2.4590](v2.4590.md) and [v2.4596](v2.4596.md) taught every reader of a job's service agreement to name both signers. Filing a signed paper still recorded one typed *Who signed*: since v2.4590 it defaulted to *Sam Owner and Alex Owner*, typed into the first frame, and the second frame stayed open. Now each person who signed the paper is a frame of their own, the second one marked paper, and no typed *and* carries a signature.

App only: no table change, no migration, no edge function to deploy. `co_signer_mode` is plain text with no CHECK (`20260929210000_job_contracts_co_signer.sql`), no trigger reads `co_signed_at`, and the edge readers of a signed paper row need nothing new. `share-job-contract` and `customer-portal` name its signers through `signerNamesLine`. `legal-portal` passes the second frame's columns to the firm's page, which names them the same way.

## The write

`fileSignedJobContract` (`src/lib/jobs/jobContractFileWrite.ts`) takes an optional `coSignerName`. A pure kernel, `paperCoSignerFields`, gives the second frame:

- `co_signer_name`: the name the draft expected, else the name typed. An e-signed frame keeps its expected name the same way. `expectedCoSignerName` reads the Contract window's payload first, so a second signer the window just took off is not brought back from the saved row.
- `co_signer_printed_name`: the name typed.
- `co_signed_at`: the record's `signed_at`, the *Signed on* date at noon UTC or now.
- `co_signer_mode`: `'paper'`, with `co_signer_consented_at` null.

All three paths write it: a live draft, a copy out on paper (handed over or PDF emailed), and a new row. A blank box writes nothing, so one signer files exactly as before.

**A signature already given through the link stays.** A PDF emailed to sign by hand keeps its link (v2.3631), so one spouse can sign there before the paper comes back. `coSignatureOnFile` reads a second frame that is already filled, and the kernel then writes nothing to it: the paper never replaces a signature on file. The window and the print keep that signature as its own. The list line names only the paper's signer, as before.

## The sheets

- **The filing sheet** (`JobContractFileSheet`): a **Second signer** box between **Signed by** and **Signed on**.
  - It starts with the second signer the draft names. The Contract window passes its live name. The sweep reads the draft row's `co_signer_name`, and its draft can land after the sheet opened: the name then fills the box once, unless the office already typed in it.
  - It holds a real value, so clearing it files one signer.
  - When the second frame was already signed through the link, the box gives way to *Alex Owner signed through the link on Oct 1. That signature stays.*
  - **Signed by** goes back to the recipient (else the customer) alone. v2.4590's joined default retires: each name has its own box now.
  - **Signed by** now starts with that name as a real value too, where it used to show it as a grey placeholder. Side by side, a grey first name read as empty next to a filled second one. What it files is unchanged: an emptied **Signed by** still files the default, as before.
- **A builder's subcontract** starts with the GC in **Signed by** and an empty **Second signer**.
  - The Contract window does this while its way is *File their subcontract*, the way the rail leads with on a builder's job until *Send ours anyway*, and no copy of ours is out on paper (`filingTheirs`).
  - The sweep does it on its *File Summit GC's subcontract* sheet.
  - Before, both defaulted to the homeowner, as a grey hint. As a real value that would read as decided, and our draft's second signer would have been offered a line on the builder's paper.
  - The Pipeline's GC sheet already started with the GC.
- **Add the contract** (`AddJobContractSheet`, one paper for several jobs): the same **Second signer** box beside **Signed by**. It starts with the second signer the anchor job's draft (or its copy out on paper) names, through `anchorCoSignerName`, and not when that signer already signed through the link.
- Unchanged, one name each: the Edit Job field (*Already have their contract?*) and the Drive pass (*File the N confident*, and **File** on a row to check). Both file the job's customer as the signer, else its GC.

## What the readers show

A paper with both frames filed from it. These name both through `signerNamesLine`, unchanged:

- the window's pill and the signed rail's banner
- the share sheet's line and the signed copy's email
- the customer portal's *Your agreements*, the legal desk's agreement line and the firm's page
- the covers kernel: the Customer page's Agreements card (*Signed Sep 30, 2026 by Michael Palmer and Grace Palmer*) and the **Change the jobs…** sheet

The Pipeline chip of a paper record names nobody, before and after: *✍ On file · Google Doc · Sep 30*.

Two lifecycle readers moved, because v2.4590 sent every paper record to the one-frame line:

- **`jobContractSignersAuditLine`** (the window's History, Documents, the Job window's Documents tab): a paper record names the frames filed from the paper, *Signed on paper by Michael Palmer and Grace Palmer · recorded Sep 30, 2026, 7:00 AM CT*.
- **`jobContractSignatureBlocks`** (every browser print): still one *Signed on paper* block, since the signatures are on the scan. Its name and its line carry the frames filed from the paper.

A one-frame paper reads exactly as before in both. In the window's paper view:

- The second mark is drawn by the second frame's own mode. A second frame filed from the paper is named in the paper's one mark, as its print does, and draws no mark of its own.
- The *for …* line on a signed record reads `signerNamesLine`, by v2.4590's rule. An e-signed two-signer record now names both there too.

**A paper filed after the second signer signed through the link.** The paper's line names only its own signer: *Signed on paper by Michael Palmer · recorded …*. The second signature keeps its own mark in the window, as it already did, with its image and *Signed electronically by Grace Palmer (drawn) · … · consent recorded*. The print now draws it as its own block too, where before it dropped it.

The History chip still reads *on file · paper*, and the frame count still shows only while an agreement is out.

## One paper, several jobs

`fileContractForJobs` passes the second name to every job's write; each row keeps the name its own draft expected. `addJobToPaper` (a job added to a paper on file, or **Change the jobs…**) copies the paper's second frame when it is marked paper, with its `co_signed_at` beside the `signed_at` it already copied. A second frame signed through a link belongs to that job's own agreement and is not copied.

## Left

On the to-do (`to-dos/contract-second-signer-names.md`, under *Left after this row*): an agreement signed partly through the link and partly on paper. Filing the paper keeps a second signature given through the link. It still replaces a first signature given there, and that frame's consent stamp leaves the row. The `co_signed` event and the consent ledger keep it. Doing it right needs a record that is part link and part paper, and `share-job-contract` rebuilds that kind of record as if its paper frame was typed. So the fix is an edge change and a deploy, in its own PR.
