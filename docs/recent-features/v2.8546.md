# v2.8546 — Punch list: the Burndown shows three weeks, and says how fast the list grows or shrinks (2026-11-24)

Punch list #444, PR 2 of four. The owner asked on 2026-11-24 to see how fast punch-list cards are being closed against how fast they are opened. The Burndown on `/punch-list` already counted both from the folder's git history; this change fills the three gaps the card names. Client only, no migration, no function.

## What changed

- **Look at** gains **The last 21 days** (`BURNDOWN_RANGES`, `RANGE_HOURS` 504). Past 336 hours `waveHours` already answers a day, so *New and closed, in waves* draws whole-day columns at three weeks with no new chart. The open line, the tiles and the stage table follow the stretch as they do for the others.
- **The sentences over the chart** (`takeawayWords`). The stretch's sentence now leads with what came in, *In the last 7 days, 47 new cards came in and 19 closed.*, and a new one gives the rate: *That is 6.7 new and 2.7 closed a day, so the list is growing by 4 a day.* A shrinking list reads *shrinking by 2.7 a day*, a level one *holding level*. A stretch where nothing moved reads *no card came in and none closed*. The sentence *At this pace it does not clear, because new cards arrive faster than old ones close* is gone: the rate has said it. *At this pace it clears by …* stays for a shrinking list. The held-cards sentence reads *1 card waits … clear it* for one card.
- **Values in place.** Every figure and day in the sentences is marked with `val` (`BurndownValueKind`: `count`, `day`, `growing`, `shrinking`, `level`) and drawn by `ValueSentence`, so the eye can run down the numbers. *Growing* draws in red and *shrinking* in green (`WorkedOutValue`'s tones), as the tiles' net change does.
- **The folded line.** With the section hidden, the header's counts gain the direction: *121 open · 540 points · growing by 4 a day in the last 7 days* (`foldedWords`, `directionWords`). Open, the header is unchanged, since the sentences say it right below.
- **The stretch is remembered** on the device (`punchListBurndownRange` in `localStorage`, read through `rangeFromStored`, which answers the default 7 days for nothing stored or a stretch this build does not know).
