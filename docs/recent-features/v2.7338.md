# v2.7338 — Paint a map: text the link to the job's customer (2026-11-15)

Punch list #345, *Text it…*. The card left it out on purpose and waited on a word. On 2026-11-15 Taunya reviewed the card and said, of this and of a task's place carrying its map: "I want to build both". A painted map's link could be copied, handed to the phone's own share sheet or shown as a code. Each of those leaves the app: nothing landed in the customer's thread and the answer came back to somebody's own phone.

## What changed

- **Share this map** has a new row under **Copy the link**: **Text it…**, *To the job's customer, from the company's number*.
- The press opens the customer's own text box in the sheet, under the head **Text the map's link**. The box holds the words *Here is a map for you, <the map's name>. Open it here: <the link>*, and the person may change them.
- **Nothing is sent in the press.** The box's **Send text…** opens its send window, which shows what the customer will read and is the confirm. The text is tied to the job, goes from the company's number and lands in the customer's thread. Their answer comes back to Messages.
- **Who sees it:** the office team, the roles that write to a customer everywhere in Messages. A tech who painted a map keeps Copy and the phone's share sheet.
- **A map on no job** has no customer to text. The office reads one line that says so, in place of the press.
- Ending the link closes an open text box, since its words carry a link that no longer opens.

## How it is built

- **No new send path and no edge function change.** The press mounts `ContractTextDoor`, the door the Contract window's *Text the link* opened in v2.6700: `JobCustomerBox` on Text over `ReplyBox`, whose send is `quo-send`. So the holds are the box's own and say why in its words: Send texts off, STOP, a prospect not asked, texts paused, a job with no customer.
- `src/lib/paintedMap/paintedMapText.ts`, pure (+6 tests): `paintedMapTextDoor` says *hidden*, *offered* or *no-job*; `paintedMapTextWords` writes the box's opening words, the link last; `paintedMapTextJob` names the job as the box does.
- `src/lib/paintedMap/paintedMapTextIo.ts`: the one read, the map's job from `jobs_ledger` under the reader's own RLS. A job that cannot be read says so and points at Copy.
- `PaintedMapShareSheet` takes `mayText` and `jobId` from the map's window (`jobBoxOffered(role)`), and reads the job only on the press.

## Is this the best we can do

- **The customer's box, not a new composer.** The card said *opens the composer on the job's customer*. The box already has the To chip, the count, the quick replies, the first-text rules and the send window. A second composer would have to earn each of those again.
- **The job's customer only.** A driver or a sub is not a contact with a thread, and `quo-send` texts only a number on a contact. They get the link by Copy, the share sheet or the code, as before.
- **Not for a development's map.** It serves every lot and has no one customer. The line says to copy the link.
