# v2.4353 — Pipeline: a bill row marks its own bill on the progress bar (2026-10-01)

No migration, no edge function. PR 3 of the Progress & payment cell's pass 4 (Grace, 2026-10-01; canvas https://claude.ai/artifact/UfRpXq1vGyXioaeMyky9Wz, boards 9 and 11 — *"The bracket marks this row's bill on the bar … only on a job with more than one bill. On a stage job the stage name goes bold instead."*). PR 1 v2.4349 (the This bill line), PR 2 v2.4351 (the money bar).

## What was true

Read live 2026-10-01: 26 Billed rows belong to 11 jobs with more than one bill, and every one of a job's bill rows drew the same bar (Springtown Hospital's two rows). Walking the new bar on them showed two more faults:

- **A part-paid bill that names a line read all billed.** `pourMoney` gave a line named by a `billed` invoice that invoice's whole amount from the billed pool, never its linked payments. Knight Springtown Vet (963): the change-order line's $2,200 bill has $2,090 paid on it and drew solid blue, while the payment was poured onto the next line. The old 3 px edge had the same fault, hidden.
- **A finished stage job bolded a stage.** The live stage is the first one the money does not cover, so on a Billed row (1035, *2 Final*) the name read as where the crew is.

## What changed

- **Kernel** [`jobMoneyBar.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobMoneyBar.ts) `billMarkFor(view, { billId, fixtures, invoices })` (+5 cases): only with two or more `billed`/`paid` bills. A stage job whose bill names one stage → `{ kind: 'stage', key }` (that name bold, the crew's bold off); a bill that names lines → a bracket over those blocks; a bill made by amount → a bracket from where the earlier bills (by `sequence_order`, then `billed_at`) leave off to where it ends, mapped onto the drawn widths by `tickLeftPct`. Springtown's Sep 1 bill: 33.5 % → 63 %; its Sep 23 bill: 63 % → 72.1 %. `bold` is off once the % reads 100; the % date is left off at 100 unless stale.
- **The pour** ([`progressPaymentCell.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/progressPaymentCell.ts) `pourMoney`): a named line takes its invoice's paid part (the payments linked to it, capped at the amount), then its open part; a `paid` invoice covers its lines as before; the rest pours in order. `ProgressPaymentInput.invoices` carries `amount` and `applied`, filled by [`progressPaymentForJob`](../../src/lib/jobs/progressPaymentForJob.ts) from `job.payments`. +1 case on 963's numbers (95 % / 5 %, then 32 % / 68 %).
- **[`StagesStageBar`](../../src/components/jobs/StagesStageBar.tsx)** `billMark`: the bracket (2 px, open at the top, `var(--text-strong)`) in a 9 px strip under the bar, or the bill's stage bold with *This row's bill: $X* in its hover. Wired through `StagesProgressPaymentCell` from `StagesUnifiedInvoiceRow`, the merged `StagesUnifiedJobRow` and the unified cards (each now builds `progressPaymentForJob` once).
- **[`StagesBillRowLine`](../../src/components/jobs/StagesBillRowLine.tsx)**: a large bill wraps *$15,200 left* under the words instead of cutting *nothing paid* to *nothing p…*.
- Guides *read the Pipeline money view* (the bracket and the bold stage) and *ready to bill pipeline* (no date at 100, no bold stage on a finished job). `GLOSSARY.md` *Money bar*.
