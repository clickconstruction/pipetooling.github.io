# v2.7192 — Lien affidavits: a flagged row on the batch help sheet says Ask the attorney first, then why in one sentence (2026-11-13)

The owner, 2026-11-13, working from a printed batch of nine with eight rows flagged. A row read **Ask first. Notices. The owner's notice went after its date, or is not on record, for April, May, June and July 2026. The original contractor's copy went after its date for April, May, June and July 2026.** His words: *Ask first.* and *Notices.* each feel like an incomplete sentence, and the two long sentences are more than the row needs. He was shown two wordings as printed pages with made-up names, one with no months and one with the months kept short, and picked the months.

## What changed

- **The row starts with a whole sentence, once.** `LIEN_HELP_BATCH_ASK_LEAD` in `src/lib/jobs/lienHelpSheet.ts` is *Ask the attorney first.* A paper with two or three reasons says it once, where each reason used to open with its own *Ask first.*
- **Each reason is one sentence.** `lienHelpBatchAsks` returns the reasons for each paper: a check's `row` (new on `LienHelpCheck`), and *This is the same property as paper N.* The lead words *Notices.* and *Homestead.* are gone from the row. The homestead's sentence is `LIEN_HELP_HOMESTEAD_ROW`, *The property record says this is a homestead.*
- **A late or missing notice names its months once.** `lienNoticeCheckRowLine` in `src/lib/jobs/lienAffidavitNoticeCheck.ts` reads the same `LienNoticeCheck` the long lines read and says *Notices for April to July 2026 went late or are not on record.*, or *The notice for July 2026 went late or is not on record.* for one month. The months are every month any of the three lines names, each once (`lienNoticeCheckMonths`). Three or more months in a row read as a span and anything else as a list (`noticeMonthsSpanWords`). The desk hands the sentence in as `checked.noticeRow`; a caller that hands none gets the long lines under the one lead.
- **Only the lead is bold.** The row is drawn as `<div data-lien-help-row-ask><strong>…</strong> …</div>`. A row in all bold was the hardest thing to read on a page where most rows are flagged.
- **The box and the count agree with the row.** `lienHelpBatchSummary` writes *1 clear to sign. 8 wait for the attorney.* The box's sentence comes from `LIEN_HELP_BATCH_ASK_RULE`, which is built from the lead: *A row that says **Ask the attorney first** is not signed until the attorney answers.* The band on the batch's notice records counts the same way, *8 wait for the attorney*.
- The guide *send lien notices from the Lien desk* says what a flagged row reads.

## Not changed

- **Which papers are flagged.** The rule behind the flag is the app's reading of Texas Property Code § 53.056, and that is counsel's question (`to-dos/lien-affidavit-contents/README.md`, question 3). The same check flags the same papers. Only the row's words moved.
- The affidavit, the cover sheet for one paper and its **ASK FIRST** band, the notice record's own blocks, and the Lien window's *Ask the attorney before this is filed.* lines. They keep the check's full lines, which say which copy went late.

## What the row no longer says

Which copy raised the question: the owner's, the original contractor's, or both. The notice record behind the sheet lists each copy with its day, and the Lien window's affidavit tab keeps the full lines. With **Print the notice record** unticked, the packet names the months and not the copies.
