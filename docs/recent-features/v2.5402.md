# v2.5402 — Messages: the four new kinds of contact read their names, behind the scenes (2026-10-12)

Residential Messages, R0 · 3 of 3: the loaders (`to-dos/gc-mode/messages/RESIDENTIAL_BUILD_PLAN.md` → R0, on branch
spike/gc-mode), stacked on v2.5390's kernels. No migration, no fetcher and no screen yet: R0's SQL PR adds the id
columns, and the fetchers select them only after that push.

- `contacts/kernel.ts`: a rep at a supply house is its own record, `supply_house_contacts`, the table the RFQ desk
  keeps reps in since `supply_houses.email` was retired (20260908185956). `kindOf` reads a rep as a supply house. The
  house's own row is its counter, and someone named on it is at it, as at a trade partner.
- `contacts/kernel.ts`: *Who is this?* (`nameContact`) names a lead who booked once more, as the customer they became
  (a `customers` or `customer_contact_persons` record, kind customer): the contact re-points and keeps its jobs and
  thread, or folds into the customer's contact. A lead named as anything else, and every other named contact, is still
  refused. R0's SQL PR gives `name_contact` the same rule.
- `contacts/load.ts`: `contactRecordOfRow` reads four more columns, `prospect_id`, `supply_house_id`,
  `supply_house_contact_id` and `team_prospect_id`, optional on `ContactRow` until the types regenerate after R0's
  SQL PR. `fromRecord` names each:
  - a lead from `prospects`: the person named at the company with the company under the name, or the company itself
    when Prospects names nobody;
  - a supply house's counter from `supply_houses`, with the house as the company of someone named on its row;
  - a rep from `supply_house_contacts`, at the house its row names;
  - a candidate from `team_prospects`;
  - a sub from `people`, as anyone on the roster, with the kind the contact keeps.
- The loader now knows all eight kinds, so a row of a new kind is read, not left out.
- The loader reads names and companies only. A record's phone and email reach the thread as `contact_channels` rows,
  which R0's SQL PR copies in (`contact_for_record`).
- `docs/ACCESS_CONTROL.md` and `docs/GLOSSARY.md` name a rep beside the supply house in their seven kinds sentence.
- **Tests:** new cases in `contacts/load.test.ts` (each of the four, a supply house with a person named on its row,
  *Who is this?* folding into a rep and into that person) and `contacts/kernel.test.ts` (a lead who books, re-pointed
  or folded, and refused as anything but a customer). Six breaks on purpose each failed a test.
