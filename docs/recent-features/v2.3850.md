# v2.3850 — The retainage cover letter redrafted to counsel's persuasion rules (2026-09-26)

The owner, 2026-09-26, reading v2.3844's letter: *"Please take a look at this again with fresh eyes. Is this the best we can do?"* It was not: it applied counsel's retainage *rules* (memo of 2026-09-22) and skipped the memo's *persuasion rules*. Client only, no migration. Stacked on v2.3849.

## What was flat

Next to counsel's commercial § 53.056 letter, v2.3844's retainage letter had no owner's risk (the § 53.056 letter says *if you pay the GC anyway, those dollars can follow the property*), one door instead of three, no dates (neither the day our contract ended nor when the affidavit copy comes), no reason the notice arrives now (retainage is often not yet due, so "has not been paid" read as an accusation), "the next payment" where the payment that matters is the release of retainage or the final draw, no homestead line although the form carries the § 53.254(g) statement, no closer, and a missing comma after the signer's title.

## The letter now (`lienRetainageCoverLetter`, `src/lib/jobs/lienNoticeDraft.ts`)

- **New inputs**: `endedOn` (the day our contract ended, as words — the run and the pane pass `demandDate(contractEndedOn)`), `kind` (`coverLetterKindFor(property)`: commercial / residential / homestead).
- Opening: *Under that subcontract {GC} holds back {amount} of our pay as retainage. Our work on this project was completed on {date} / Our subcontract … was terminated|abandoned on {date}, and that retainage has not been paid to us.*
- Why now: *Texas law gives us 30 days from the day our contract on this project ended to send this notice if we are going to keep lien rights on that retainage, whether or not it is yet due under our subcontract. That is why it comes now.*
- The two pots (§ 53.101 and our retainage), then counsel's own sentence from the paid-out letter: *If the 10% is paid to {GC} when it should have been reserved, the property can still be reached for that amount (§ 53.105).* With a payment bond: only our retainage, no § 53.101, no § 53.105.
- The withhold rule: on a retainage-only packet, *your right to withhold … begins when you receive a copy of our filed lien affidavit (§ 53.081(c)). If the retainage is not paid, we will file that affidavit and send you the copy within five days of recording (§ 53.055). Payments to {GC} after that day can follow the property (§ 53.084).* When a § 53.056 notice already named it: § 53.081 now, and § 53.084 on paying anyway.
- Three doors *before you release retainage or make a final payment to {GC}*, no joint check: the GC pays us; the owner keeps the amount and calls; the GC writes that the owner may pay us directly.
- The GC copied; the call line; counsel's closer by kind (*your property* / *a house* / *an affidavit on a homestead*). A homestead's second paragraph adds counsel's § 53.254(g) line.
- **`callLineName`**: a contact with a title closes its appositive — *Call Malachi Whites, Master Plumber, at …* — in this letter and, through `fillCoverLetter`, in every `{{contact}} at {{phone}}` of counsel's § 53.056 letters.

`buildLienRetainageRun` and `LienDeskRetainagePane` pass the new facts; the guide's Retainage section and the counsel row in `to-dos/owner-decisions-pending.md` say what changed and put two questions to counsel (the bond case; asking the owner to keep the retainage before the affidavit copy).
