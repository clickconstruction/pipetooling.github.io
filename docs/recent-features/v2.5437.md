Residential customers in Messages, R2 PR 3a (`to-dos/gc-mode/messages/RESIDENTIAL_BUILD_PLAN.md` → R2, on branch
spike/gc-mode), by customer communication helper 9 for the customer communication lead. Migration
`20261014020000_contact_for_customer.sql`, note `docs/migrations/20261014020000_contact_for_customer.md`, bed
`contact-for-customer`. No screen: R2 PR 3b puts a Messages button beside a customer's name on Customers, Jobs and
Estimates, and it calls this.

- **Why:** the spine's seed made contacts only for the customers on GC projects, and nobody signed in may insert a
  contact, so most of our own customers had no thread to open.
- **`contact_for_customer(customer)`**: the customer's own contact, the one on their row with no name. With none
  there it makes one, kind customer, with the number and the address `contact_info` keeps, once however often it is
  pressed. `SECURITY DEFINER` with the office team in its body, each refusal in words. A training account and a twin
  find a contact and never make one.
- **`contacts_keep_thread_on_customer_delete`**: the screens delete a customer outright, and a contact holds its
  customer by `ON DELETE RESTRICT`. Before the delete, the customer's contacts and those of the people at the customer
  become numbers nobody has named that keep the name and the lines. A function of its own, so the spine's trigger
  function, which R0's SQL redefines, is untouched.
- **`messagesIo.ts` → `contactForCustomer(customerId)`**: the client's read of the door, through the module's untyped
  `rpc` until the types regenerate after the push.
- `docs/ACCESS_CONTROL.md` gains the door's line after the other Messages lines.
- **Tests:** a new case in `messagesIo.test.ts` (the function's name and argument, and a refusal), and the bed. Two
  breaks on purpose each failed the case: the function's name and the argument's name.
