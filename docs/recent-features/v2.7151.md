# v2.7151 — Pay calendar: a job's own calendar is one press from the job (2026-11-13)

Punch list #341, PR 1 of five: the door. A job has been able to carry its own Pay calendar since v2.7036, and every screen that reads a calendar takes the job's over its payer's. The way in was a press on a board row, drawn only when that row's payer already had a calendar. No payer has one yet, so the override could not be reached from a job at all. Walking the build also found the second door shut: the Their paper sheet's *Set <payer>'s pay calendar…* (v2.7039) drew nothing when the sheet was opened from the job window or from Bill Customer.

## What changed

- **Whose calendar a job reads.** `calendarSourceFor(index, { payerId, jobId })` in `src/lib/jobs/payCalendar.ts` answers the calendar and where it came from: `job`, `payer`, `off` or `none`, with the payer's own calendar beside it. `calendarFor` is now its `.calendar`, so the precedence has one home. `jobPayCalendarWords` says it in the job window's words.
- **The job window's Pay calendar row.** `JobPayCalendarRow` in the Edit tab's customer rows, above *Customer Contract*, for the office roles: *Follows Larkfield Construction · In by month end, paid the 10th*, *This job's own · In by the 25th, paid the 30th*, *No pay calendar on this job · Larkfield Construction has one*, or *Set a pay calendar…*. The words are a press that opens the Pay calendar window on that job. The payer is whoever the job's bills go to (`theirPaperPayer`). No row for a reader who cannot set a calendar, or on a job that names nobody to bill.
- **One line, two forms.** `PayCalendarLine` takes an optional `job`. With one it reads `calendarSourceFor` for that job and opens the window with it; without one it is the payer's line, as before.
- **Pay speeds carries the row's job.** `PaySpeedsPayer` gains `job`. A billed row's *Pays in* press hands Pay speeds the row's job, and the payer card's pay calendar line opens on it. Opened from the Payment forecast, with no row behind it, the line is the payer's.
- **The provider moved up.** `PayCalendarModalProvider` now wraps `BillCustomerModalProvider`, `JobFormModalProvider` and `JobDetailModalProvider` in `App.tsx`. It sat below them, so a press inside the job window or Bill Customer found no opener and drew nothing. `PayCalendarModalContext.order.test.ts` holds the order.
- **Their paper's door carries the job as the board names it**, with its development (`payCalendarJob` on `TheirPaperSheet`, from `payCalendarJobOf`), so the window can offer *Also set on the other N jobs*. It read *Job 1431* and offered none.
- **Every screen reads again after a save.** The window says `dispatchPayCalendarChanged()` once per save, however many jobs it wrote. `useBilledMoneyData` (the board, its windows, the Lien desk), `useJobBilledExpectedPay` (the job window's bills) and every `PayCalendarLine` listen through `onPayCalendarChanged`. A calendar set from Pay speeds or the job window used to leave the board on yesterday's dates until a reload.
- **`PayCalendarJob` and `payCalendarJobOf` moved** to `src/lib/jobs/payCalendarJob.ts`, out of the window and the Stages tab, so every door builds the job the same way.
- **Guide**: *One job on a different calendar* in *set a payer's pay calendar* now starts from the job window and says what each of the row's four readings means.

## Not changed

- Which calendar any bill reads. `calendarFor` answers as before on every input (a test compares the two).
- A row on the board still shows the same words whether the calendar is the job's own or the payer's. That is PR 2, the mark.
- A job's own calendar is still scored against the payer's payments on every job. That is PR 3.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **The row is in the Edit tab, not on the job's first tab.** It sits with *Bills go to* and *Customer Contract*, which is where the question of who pays lives. If the office looks for it on the Bill tab, a second line there costs one more `PayCalendarLine`.
- **A job that bills someone by a typed email has no payer on file**, so the row names the job's own payer and that one bill still reads no calendar. The card's gap 5.
- **The row reads its payer's calendars alone**, one small read per open job window. It does not use the board's loaded index, since the job window opens from a dozen screens that have none.
