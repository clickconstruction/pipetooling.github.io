# v2.7167 — Bills sent: the office adds to and edits the list under How the bill went (punch list #340, PR 3) (2026-11-13)

Punch list #340, PR 3: Option B of the card, the chips, as the owner picked it. The list under *How the bill went* in **Say how it went…** was a native select with four fixed choices. It is now a row of chips read from the office's own list (`bill_hand_ways`, v2.7166), with the two presses that change the list beside them.

## What changed

- **The chips** (`src/components/billing/BillHandWays.tsx`, `BillHandWayPicker`): every way on the list as a chip in the list's order, one press to pick (`aria-pressed`), under the label *How the bill went*. The field takes the form's full width, so nothing is drawn over the next field. On a phone a chip is 40 px tall.
- **＋ Add a way**: the last chip opens a box in the form. Typed words are trimmed and refused before the database is asked when they are blank, already on the list or the list is full. **Add to the list** saves, reads the list again and picks the new way. The line under the box says *Everyone who opens Bills sent gets the new way. 5 of 12.*
- **Edit list** opens **How a bill went: the list** (`BillHandWaysWindow`, on the app's `ResponsiveModalShell`: a centred window at a desk, a full sheet on a phone). Each way has ▲ and ▼, **Rename** (the row turns into a box that says *Was: Mailed. A bill already recorded as Mailed still reads Mailed.*, then **Save the name**) and **Take off**, dead on the last way with its reason. A way a person added says who and when. **Taken off (n)** folds the ways taken off, each with who and when and **Bring back**, dead with its reason when the list is full or the words are taken. Each change saves when pressed and the list is read again; **Done** only closes.
- **When the list is not the office's own** (the read was refused: before the migration is pushed, a lost signal), the form offers the four ways kept in code as chips and draws neither press, since there is no list to change.
- **The kernel** (`src/lib/billing/billHandWays.ts`, 11 tests): `cleanHandWayLabel`, `handWayNameProblem`, `handWayAddProblem`, `handWayTakeOffProblem`, `handWayBringBackProblem`, `handWayMove` (two rows trade places; a tie is numbered again), `handWayCountWords`, `handWayAddedWords`, `handWayTakenOffWords`.
- **The writes** (`billsSentIo.ts`): `addBillHandWay`, `renameBillHandWay`, `setBillHandWayOnList`, `moveBillHandWays`. Each throws its reason; an update that reaches no row is said, not passed over.
- **The screen's tests** (`BillsSentLedger.render.test.tsx`, 14): the chips and the filing, a way added in place with a refusal first, and the window's rename, move, take off and bring back.
- **The guide** *see which bills went out* gains **Change the list of ways**.

## Not changed

- Every way, whichever it is, is a record by hand: the chips above the list, the tiles, Print and the CSV count as they did.
- No migration. The table and its guard came in v2.7166; the guard holds the same refusals for two people pressing at once.
