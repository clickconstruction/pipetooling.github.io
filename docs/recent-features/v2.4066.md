# v2.4058 — Cover Letter: a real Schedule of values, and the payment schedule says what it is (2026-09-28)

The owner's ask (2026-09-28): "on the cover letter there is no schedule of values." The letter could say when we get paid (**Payment schedule**, headed *Schedule of Values:* since v2.596) and what the material costs by stage (**Materials by stage**, v2.3673), but not what each stage of the work is worth. Mock-up with the self-review and the two decisions taken (rename the payment heading; dollars *and* share on each line): https://claude.ai/artifact/7bNozSYGuexo746k1vdvfb.

## The kernel — `scheduleOfValues.ts`

- `scheduleOfValuesLetter(summary, contractAmount)` spreads the amount across the three stages by each one's share of the staged material (`scaleToContract`'s rule) and reconciles to whole cents by largest remainder, so the rows add to the amount exactly — a GC checks the total first. A stage with nothing staged gets no row; null when nothing is staged or the amount is not positive. Returns the rows (label, reconciled dollars, one-decimal share) plus `scaled: StageMoney` for the printed "Of contract" column and the total.
- `buildScheduleOfValuesSectionLines` words it: `Schedule of values:` / `Rough In — $35,596.80 (41.2%)` … / `Total — $86,400.00`. `SCHEDULE_OF_VALUES_HEADING`. 5 tests, including the ⅓ · ⅓ · ⅓ of $100.00 case (33.34 / 33.33 / 33.33).
- `paymentSchedule.ts`: the heading is `PAYMENT_SCHEDULE_HEADING = 'Payment schedule:'` (was `Schedule of Values:`). The three test files that pinned the old words follow.

## The letter — `coverLetter.ts` · `approvalPdf.ts`

- Both builders take a trailing `scheduleOfValues: CoverLetterScheduleOfValues | null` (`{ summary: Pick<MaterialsByStageSummary, 'byStage' | 'assignedRaw'>; amountDollars }`) and render the section **after the terms, before the payment schedule** — reading order: what it is worth, when it is paid, what it costs. Each letter passes its own amount (the bundle section's `revenueSum`, the same-page headline), the same path the payment schedule takes. 3 tests.
- The Approval PDF reads `include_schedule_of_values` in the same fresh `bids` read as the other flags; one `loadMaterialsByStageForBid` now serves both stage sections; the new heading and the renamed one are bolded.

## The tab — `BidsCoverLetterTab.tsx`

- **Schedule of values** pill (`#cover-letter-schedule-of-values-pill`, `bids.include_schedule_of_values`, the same optimistic + `bidUpdateRefused` path as the other two pills) sits before Payment schedule. The Payment schedule pill's hover, and its editor's label, now say *Payment schedule*.
- The stage read (`loadMaterialsByStageForBid`, active version, the bid's factor) runs when **any** of the three pills is on and keeps the whole `MaterialsByStageDocument` (`materialsByStageDoc`), which the editor, the letter and the print all read.
- The editor (`data-testid="cover-letter-schedule-of-values"`): the three lines and the total at the studio's `effectiveRevenue`; *N of M costed fixtures staged*; **Print the full schedule** → `printScheduleOfValuesOfContract` = the Takeoffs print (`buildScheduleOfValuesHtml`) with its `contract` column filled from the letter's amount (the reconciled `scaled`), which the Takeoffs tab could never offer because it holds no priced total; an amber line when `unassignedRaw > 0` naming the fixtures still needing a stage and the money left out. Waiting and nothing-staged states in words.
- Preview key carries the pill.

## Not here

Typed custom lines (mobilization, gas piping, a water heater as its own line) — a GC who wants a line-item schedule beyond the three stages gets an editor with typed lines, on top of this. A hand-typed schedule for a bid with no takeoff.
