# v2.6738 — A stage checklist shows a report photo before the pick (punch list #189, PR 6's live run) (2026-11-08)

Punch list #189's PR 6 (v2.6219) ran live on 2026-11-08 as the Sample helper on the ZZ test job 1088, at 375 px: a photo from the camera-first report answered the one Rough In question that asks for a photo, the answer row took the `report_photos` id and the file name, and the report's Stage progress read *1 photo on Rough In, 1 answers a checklist question*. The run's one finding, fixed here. No migration, no edge function.

## Cause

`StageChecklistSheet` drew a question's `PhotoRow` (the thumbnail, the camera press and the words) only when the answer held a pick other than Not here. A report photo lands on the answer row at Save with `pick` null, as v2.6219 decided (the pick stays the crew's), so the sheet showed the question untouched: the *photo* tag, Yes · No · Not here, and no sign a shot was already on it. On a stage with one question the pick filed the checklist as a record at once, so the words *Taken on a field report.* the guide promises were never on screen.

## What changed

- **The open row** draws `PhotoRow` when the question asks for a photo and the answer either holds a pick other than Not here or names a report photo (`a?.reportPhotoId`). Before the pick the row shows the thumbnail from the report bucket, **Take the photo again** and *Taken on a field report. On its way to the customer's Drive folder.*
- **A closed row** whose answer names a report photo reads *photo taken on a field report* in place of *asks for a photo* (`CHECKLIST_PHOTO_WORDS.onReportTag` in `src/lib/photos/reportPhotoChecklist.ts`).
- The locked (signed) row and the filed paper are unchanged: both always hold a pick.
