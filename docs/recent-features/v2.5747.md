# v2.5747 — Guides: cards, Try it, folds and a ring you can tap (2026-10-22)

The owner, on the live guide after v2.5746: "doesn't look like what you mocked up." The mock-up was a skim page with six cards, a tappable ring and numbered steps; the guide system drew every guide as one column of prose. So the renderer learns the blocks, and the guide uses them.

- **Five block kinds** (`src/lib/helpGuideIllustrations.ts`, `BLOCK_KINDS`): `:::cards` (a grid), `:::card <title>` (a titled card), `:::tryit` (the TRY IT eyebrow over the words), `:::fold <summary>` (a closed `<details>`, *More* by default), `:::steps` (an ordered list drawn with big numerals) and `:::ring` (the robot's ring). Fences nest; the encoder walks lines with a stack so each `:::` close emits the close of the kind it closes, and `:::example` keeps its old markers. Templates are emitted after the sanitizer as before; the classes are styled in `src/index.css` under `.help-guide-body`, every colour a token.
- **The ring** (`src/lib/helpGuideRing.ts`): the seven arcs are the Bid Board ring's own steps (`HEARTBEAT_STEPS` and their words); `ringState(at)` is the pure half, `stepHelpRing(ring, 'next' | 'reset')` moves the drawn one, and `GuideBrowser`'s click handler routes a press on `button[data-help-ring]` to it. Each step's sub-line is plain words.
- **Checks**: `helpGuideIllustrations.test.ts` (the fences, nesting, each template, the ring's seven segments and chips), `helpGuideRing.test.ts` (the state and the drawn ring under jsdom), and `helpGuideContent.test.ts` now refuses a guide with an unknown or unclosed fence, so a typo never prints as text.
- **The guide** *use the robots and the new bid tools as an estimator* takes the layout: six cards with Try it and a fold each, the ring to tap, the day as steps. `CLAUDE.md` → Help guides names the blocks.
