# v2.7173 — Materials on site: a press made with no signal is kept and sent later (2026-11-13)

Punch list #344, PR 1, the first of what the review of Materials → On site ranked: *a press with no signal is lost after a green toast*.

## What was wrong

Every write in `onSiteStore.ts` drew its row in the cache, the tab toasted green, and the insert was sent once (`afterWrite`). Nothing kept it. With no signal a red toast followed, five seconds long, and the next good read put the old number back. If the row had landed and only the answer was lost, the toast said nothing was saved, and a second press doubled the row, since each press minted a new id. Two rows that depend on each other, a new box and the part put in it, were sent side by side.

The outbox #163 built for the clock, notes and reports was never given an on-site press.

## The change

- **A press is data.** `src/lib/materials/onSiteWrites.ts`: `OnSiteWrite` is an insert under an id minted on the phone, or a few columns changed on a box or a photo. `applyOnSiteWrites` is the one place a write becomes what the screen shows. `parseOnSiteWrites` shape-checks a kept press. The ledger, the places and the box moves take inserts alone.
- **The sender.** `src/lib/materials/onSiteSend.ts`: a press is written to the phone's store (`writePress`) before its first send. With a signal and nothing ahead of it, it goes at once and its kept copy is taken back when the office answers, so the bar never speaks for an ordinary press. With no signal, or behind a press that waits, it joins the outbox as a tenth kind, `on-site-press`, and the replay sends it in the order pressed. One press goes at a time, so a new box is at the office before its part.
- **Landing.** `landOnSitePress` in `landFieldPress.ts` sends the rows through the tables' own policies (`onSiteWritesIo.ts`). A resend carries the same ids. An insert that meets its own row answers *duplicate key* on the table's primary key, which is read as a landing (`insertAlreadyLanded`); any other duplicate, a stencil a live box already carries, keeps its reason as a refusal.
- **The screen.** `commit` in the store replaces `afterWrite`. `loadJob` draws the presses still on their way over every read (`withPending`). A press the office refuses, at once or on a later try, or one the person drops from the list, is taken off by a fresh read. The first press that starts to wait says *No signal. What you press is kept on this phone and sends by itself.*
- **A box's move** is one press of two rows, and its move row now carries the time it was pressed.

No migration and no edge function: the rules are the tables' own, as before.

## Tests

`onSiteWrites.test.ts` (11), `onSiteSend.test.ts` (9: signal, no signal, a lost signal, order, a refusal, a session that never queues), `onSiteWritesIo.test.ts` (5), `landFieldPress.test.ts` (2 more), and `onSiteStore.write.test.ts` (7), the store, the sender, the outbox and the replay together over a stand-in for the tables: kept through a fresh read, landed once after a lost answer, a new box before its part, a refusal off the screen.

## Walked

On the dev server at 375 wide as the dev, on the ZZ test job, with every on-site write answered by the walk's own script, so nothing reached the tables. A use with signal: one send, 8 to 7, no bar. The signal cut: the next use read 6, the message showed, and the bar read *On site press from 1:25 PM, waiting to send*. A second use read 5 and *2 waiting to send*, and only the first was tried, four times under one id. The page reloaded: both presses were drawn over the fresh read. The signal back: both landed in order, once each, and the bar read *2 sent ✓*.

## Not changed

- Not walked on a real phone with the radio off.
- A photo's bytes still wait for **Send now** (PR 2).
- A session that views as someone else, or a twin, never queues, as on every other press.
- The ledger's `at` is still the phone's clock (PR 5).
