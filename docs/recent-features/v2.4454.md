# v2.4454 — Needs You: a lead time counts from today, evenings included (2026-10-02)

Named in [v2.4452](v2.4452.md) and left for its own fix. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

`summarizeSubmittalNudge` in [`submittalNeedsYou.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/submittalNeedsYou.ts) took today as `opts.todayYmd ?? ymd(now)`, and `ymd(now)` is `now.toISOString().slice(0, 10)`, the UTC date. Its only caller, [`useSubmittalsNudge`](../../src/hooks/useSubmittalsNudge.ts), passes no `todayYmd`. So after 7 pm CDT (6 pm CST) the Dashboard's lead-time card counted from tomorrow: *ordered today it lands Oct 31 … 29 days late* where the answer is Oct 30 and 28.

## What changed

The default is `todayYmdInAppTz(now)` from [`dateUtils.ts`](../../src/utils/dateUtils.ts). `ymd` stays for `addDays`, which is arithmetic on a UTC-midnight day. The other three cards count elapsed days between instants (`daysBetween`) and are unchanged. `job_stage_windows.window_end` is a `date`, so the hook's slice of it stays.

## Tests

`submittalNeedsYou.test.ts` (+3), with no `todayYmd`: `now` at `2026-10-03T00:30:00Z` (7:30 pm CDT on Oct 2) lands WC-1's 28 days on Oct 30, 28 days past the Oct 2 window; `2026-12-02T00:30:00Z` (6:30 pm CST) counts from Dec 1; noon UTC reads its own day. The two evening cases fail on main's kernel.

## Checked live

Worktree dev server against prod at 21:44 CDT on 2026-10-02 (02:44 UTC on Oct 3). No real bid fires the lead-time card today: the Dashboard's Submittals cards showed only *32 won bids have no submittal started*. So both kernels were loaded into the page and given one 28-day part against an Oct 2 window at the real clock. Main's said *lands 2026-10-31, 29 days late*; this branch's said *2026-10-30, 28*.
