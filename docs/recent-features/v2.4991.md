# v2.4991 — Pipeline: the billed-money windows are one host, StagesBilledMoneyModals (2026-10-08)

Client only, no screen change. Punch list #46 row 2, [JOBS_STAGES_TAB map](../JOBS_STAGES_TAB_ARCHITECTURE.md) step 8, after the billed-money reads moved to `useBilledMoneyData` (v2.4099, step 2).

## What moved

**`src/components/jobs/StagesBilledMoneyModals.tsx`** (new, 355 lines) draws the nine billed-money windows, verbatim:

- who owes what (`BilledByCustomerBreakdownModal`)
- the aging chart
- the payment forecast and its Email… window
- call mode (`PaymentChaseModal`)
- Fix bill lines
- the promised pay date
- the paid profit chart
- the billed report share

With them comes what only they read:

- **The forecast's share flag**, `forecastShareModalOpen`. Only the forecast opens that window.
- **The two scope kicks.** While who owes what, the aging chart or the forecast is open, and while call mode is open, every non-paid scope keeps loading. The host reads the cache's scope API from `useJobsListCache()`, as the tab does.
- **Call mode's full queue**, which waits until all four non-paid scopes have merged.
- **The forecast's work months**: one clock-sessions read for its open-bill jobs, only while it is open.

Every prop keeps the tab's name, so the JSX is the tab's.

**`JobsStagesTab.tsx`** keeps the open flags, because the handle, the money overview, the ☰ menu and the Billed header write them. It mounts the host in the modal tail and passes the boards, the billed-money reads and the doors back into the board. It also keeps what the card and the round seam read too: `unfilteredBoardLists`, `gcTemperatureById`, `chaseTodayYmd` and `forecastTodayYmd` (the Lien desk's work months read that last one). The tab goes from 5,070 to 4,902 lines, and nine imports leave it.

`SessionNotesModal` sat between the aging chart and the forecast, but it is not money, so it stays in the tab, just before the host. It draws at z 1001 and the money windows at 760–770, so the new order does not show.

**The kicks now run in the host.** A child's effects run before its parent's in the same commit, so the two kicks now run before the tab's own scope kicks instead of in the tab's source order. Every kick is the same idempotent `fetchScopeIfNeeded(scope, filter)`, so only which call starts a fetch first can change.

## Tests (+12)

`StagesBilledMoneyModals.render.test.tsx` draws each window as a probe that records its props:

- **Nothing open:** no window and no scope kick.
- **Who owes what, the aging chart and the forecast:**
  - They read the unfiltered board. Fix bill lines, the paid chart and the billed print read the filtered one (map quirk 4).
  - Each kicks the four non-paid scopes with the customer filter. Loading holds until all four merge.
  - Who owes what's doors: a bill focuses its invoice, a whole-job bill opens Billed on the job and flashes it, and 90+ filters.
- **The forecast:**
  - Work months are asked once per open-bill job and never for a whole-job row.
  - Its doors: the Bill tab, stacked or not, the Lien desk and the pay speeds.
  - *Email…* is for the office only and opens the share window the host holds.
- **Call mode:**
  - It kicks the scopes, and its queue stays null until every scope merges.
  - The queue is built from every bill, with the GC temperatures.
  - A recorded call reloads the touches and the promises.
  - *Move to Collections* is for the office only: it asks first, and a job that has left the board gets a toast.
- **The promised pay date:** saving reloads the promises and the records.

**Mutants.** Ten edits to the host, each made alone, and each failed exactly one case:

- who owes what reading the filtered board
- dropping who owes what from the kick
- call mode's kick never running
- *Email…* for every role
- call mode's queue built before the scopes merge
- whole-job rows in the work months
- Fix bill lines reading every bill
- *Move to Collections* for every role
- a whole-job bill not flashing its row
- the paid chart reading every paid job
