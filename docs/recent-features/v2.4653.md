# v2.4653 — Review follows the job: a vehicle deal's fuel stays on the jobs (2026-10-06)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Punch list **#52** (`to-dos/fuel-is-a-job-cost.md`), PR 5 — the owner's decision 1 ("fuel stays on the job everywhere, Review included"), built to the checkpoint-3 plan the owner approved 2026-10-06.

## Why

Since v2.2735 (Wheels on Labor) People → Review took a vehicle-deal person's fuel off the jobs and charged it to them alone, as field hours × a 90-day rate. Every other screen — the Job window, Job Summary, People → Spending — kept that fuel on the jobs it was put on, so what a job cost read one way on Review and another everywhere else.

## The change

- **`loadTeamReviewUnion`** — the vehicle-deal fuel removal (and its `kind === 'debitCardTransaction'` test) is gone: the job card sums follow the one card rule, as Job Summary does. A deal's fuel on jobs reaches each person through the ⛽ line by labor share, like every job cost.
- **The vehicle line charges only what is not on a job** (`derivePersonTeamSummary`): `vehicleFixedCost` = −(field hours × the deal's fixed rate) plus `vehicleFuelOffJobs` = −(the person's fuel on no job in the period). Company truck: the fixed rate is the truck's insurance + registration + service ÷ the holder's field hours (90-day). Own vehicle: $0. The override (`vehicle_rate_override`) is now the fixed part only — fuel is never in it.
- **Fuel on no job** (`lib/people/reviewVehicleFuel.ts`) — read through the same window and kernel as People → Spending (`list_card_charges_window`, `buildSpendingRollup`): fuel-tag card charges, or the part of one, not on a job, the Office job, a supply-house invoice or a payroll mark; a refund comes off. A period longer than 366 days is read in 366-day windows.
- **The per-person drawer** — the ⛽ line now carries the deal's own fuel on the jobs; the vehicle line reads *field h × $fixed + $X of their fuel on no job in the period; their fuel on jobs is in the ⛽ line above*, and says so when a company truck has no fixed costs on file. The verdict's own-vehicle segment reads *Own-vehicle fuel on no job*. The deal chip shows a fixed rate only when there is one.
- **Wheels** (`lib/people/wheels.ts`, `wheelsData.ts`, People → Vehicles → Wheels) — onto the card rule: any row carrying a card is a card charge (a refund to the card, kind `other`, comes off), sums are signed (`Math.abs` gone). Each row carries `allInRate` (the report's comparison) and `fixedRate` (what Review charges); the truck's cost gains `fixedRatePerFieldHour`. The column reads *Fixed / field h*; the override is a fixed $/field h.
- **A company truck with no insurance, registration or service on file charges only its fuel on no job** until those costs are entered on People → Vehicles. Today that is the 2007 Ram 3500, the one company-truck deal.

Help guides: *see what each person's vehicle costs per field hour*, *read the team review*, *see everyone's pay setup*, *name and link debit cards*. GLOSSARY → Wheels.
