# v2.8172 — Write someone up: a place to keep a write-up's photos (2026-11-21)

Punch list #410, the ground under PR 5 of the train in [`to-dos/write-someone-up/`](../../to-dos/write-someone-up/README.md). One migration, [`20261121143500_person_report_photos`](../migrations/20261121143500_person_report_photos.md), and its bed. No screen changes.

## Why

Taunya asked on 2026-11-20 that a leader could write someone up *with details on their phone that we can record*. The mock-up she approved draws a photo taken in the sheet, kept with no signal, shown to the office, and made a numbered evidence record once the write-up is filed. That needs somewhere to put the bytes that only the writer and the office can open.

## What changed

- **`person_report_photos`**: one row a photo, keyed to the write-up's press. A report carries the same press id once it lands, so the two are joined by it and the landing function needed no change.
- **`person-report-photos`**, a private bucket of images up to 2 MB. An object sits under the writer's own folder, then the press.
- **Who.** The writer adds and reads their own. A dev reads all, as a dev reads the reports. The person written about and every other leader read nothing.
- **A photo can be dropped until the write-up is sent.** After that it stays with it.
- **`can_write_someone_up()`**, the role rule in one place for the table and the bucket.

## Decisions

- **Keyed to the press, not to the report.** A photo is sent before its write-up, and with no signal it may be days before. The press id exists from the first moment and never changes.
- **No copy into the HR bucket here.** When a write-up is filed, the office's browser reads the photos and saves them as an evidence record through the door that already exists. This migration only has to let a dev read them.

## Verified

## Not done here
