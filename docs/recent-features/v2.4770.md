# v2.4770 — Court areas: the map fills itself in (2026-10-07)

One migration (`20261007120000_court_precinct_nightly_cron.sql`, push after [v2.4768](v2.4768.md)'s), one new edge function (`court-precinct-nightly`, **deploy after the push**), and the client. Step 4 of 5 of the owner's justice-court plan (2026-10-06).

## What changed

- **One kernel for the server and the Map page.** The classification moved to [`_shared/courtAreasClassify.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/courtAreasClassify.ts), over a dependency-free [`_shared/courtGeometry.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/courtGeometry.ts) (ray casting, and metres to the nearest edge on a flat projection around the point) in place of turf, so Deno and the browser put an address in the same precinct. `src/lib/legal/courtAreas.ts` is the client's door; its tests are unchanged and pass against the new geometry.
- **[`court-precinct-nightly`](../../supabase/functions/court-precinct-nightly/index.ts)** ([EDGE_FUNCTIONS](../EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md#court-precinct-nightly)): every record not typed by hand, with a point in the geocode cache, classified against the active areas of its county; written only on a change (`jp_precinct`, `jp_precinct_note`, `jp_precinct_source = 'map'`, `jp_precinct_at`). Outside every area clears a stale precinct. pg_cron at 06:20 UTC ([migration page](../migrations/20261007120000_court_precinct_nightly_cron.md)), or an office user's own session.
- **Classify now** on the Map page's Court areas panel runs it by hand and reads back *40 placed · 6 outside · 2 on a line · 0 with no point · 3 records written*.
- **The import** ([`courtAreasImport.ts`](../../src/lib/legal/courtAreasImport.ts), [`scripts/import-court-areas.ts`](../../scripts/import-court-areas.ts)): Bexar, Travis and Hays publish their precinct lines as ArcGIS layers (field names checked 2026-10-07: Bexar `Precinct` as *Precinct 2*, Travis `PRECINCT`, Hays `PRECINCT` as an integer). The script signs in as the owner, asks each layer for GeoJSON in WGS84, folds features that share a label into one MultiPolygon, retires the county's earlier imported rows and inserts the new ones, each with the layer's name, page and the fetch date in its source note. Hand-drawn rows are never touched. `--dry-run` fetches and reports.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **The night and the button do the same thing**, through one function, so what the office sees after Classify now is what tomorrow's run would write.
- **Hand wins, always.** A typed precinct is a person's answer to an on-the-line question; the map never overwrites it.
- **Import, then draw.** Three counties come in exact in a minute; the hand-drawing is for Guadalupe, Comal, Williamson and whatever else holds a job.
- **Not done: the hand override on the property record and the precinct on the firm's portal** — step 5, with the desk gap.
