# v2.7647 — Where files go: a Signed contracts card shows which contracts are in Drive (2026-11-17)

Punch list #377, PR 5 of six: the card on Documents → Where files go. The Contract window (PR 4) says where one paper's copy is. This says where they all are, and files what is waiting.

## What changed

- **A Signed contracts card**, its own file (`WhereFilesGoContractsCard.tsx`), mounted after the tree card. Two lines say where a signed contract goes and who can open it there, the second being the owner's call 1 in the app's own words.
- **The counts**: signed contracts, in Drive, waiting, held, from `contractDriveCounts` over a paged read of every signed contract in force (`whereFilesGoContractsIo.ts`). The read throws with its reason and a failed one is drawn on `OfflineRetryPanel`, which reads again by itself.
- **One press**, *File 2 signed contracts*, through `batchWords`: it says its count, names the held ones beside it, and is dead when nothing waits. It runs `job-contract-drive`'s sweep a page at a time until nothing is left or a page looks at nothing, twelve pages at most, and shows the tally as it goes. Afterwards it says what took and what did not through `batchOutcomeWords`, and reads the counts again.
- **The held papers**, each with its job, its reason and the door to its job. A held job folder leads to the job's Edit tab. The others lead to the job, where the Contract window holds the press for that one paper.
- **The good empty state**: *Nothing waiting on Drive. Every signed contract is filed.*, and before the first one is signed, *A signed contract is filed here by itself.*
- **The tree card** gains the **Contracts** leaf under Team only and a line saying it is made on first use. The five names it already listed stay as they are.
- **The guide** *set where a job's files go* gains *See where the signed contracts are*. `docs/DOCUMENTS_PAGE_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` and `docs/GLOSSARY.md` name the card, the folder, and what *filed*, *adopted* and *held* mean for a contract.
