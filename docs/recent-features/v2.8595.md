## The three presses

On each paper of the *Changed since printed* card (v2.8593), for the lien office:

- **Reprint this one.** An affidavit prints again now, alone, as its own press prints it, and files its copy. That copy is its newest print, so it reads as printed and the same. On a notice the press reads **Reprint in the run window ›** and opens the run window on that one notice, where a printed notice is taken back and printed again. A notice cannot be reprinted from the card: its paper is the stored one, and a notice whose bills moved has to be redrawn first.
- **It stands as printed.** One press. It writes a row with who, when, what had changed and the filed print it was read against. The paper leaves the list of papers that ask and joins **Stands as printed** under it, with who said so and a **Take back** press.
- **Take it out of the run.** The run's own *Leave out* (step 5), with its reason. Nothing new is kept: it is the same row in `lien_run_left_out`.

## A second change asks again

The row is kept under a key of the difference it was given for (`lienPrintChangeKey`): which fields moved, from what to what, as the compare sees the values. The key is the same however the money is written or the words are spaced. When a statement moves again, the difference is another, its key is another, no standing row matches, and the paper is back among the ones that ask. The earlier row is not touched. **Take back** stamps every standing answer for that paper.

## An affidavit that stands does not print again

`useLienRunPrint` takes `stoodAffidavitJobIds` beside the printed ones. The press leaves both out, and the card says each count on its own line: *N printed already and say the same today*, *N stand as printed*.

## What it does not do

- **A paper that stands is not locked or changed.** No notice, affidavit, filing, desk item or sent copy is written. A notice that stands still reads *Approved · to mail* and its stored paper is as it was.
- **No reason is asked for.** The sheet's line named who, when and what had changed. A reason can be added to the row later with no change to the rule.
- **The strip and the tally.** The run's third step, *Out the door*, and its tally line (*changed since printed N*) are step 8.
