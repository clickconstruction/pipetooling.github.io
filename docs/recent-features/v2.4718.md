## What changed

- **`LienDeskGates`**: a clear gate's section is folded until its cell is pressed (`aria-expanded`, and the cell still rings and scrolls its section up) or *Details ∨* (`data-lien-gates-fold="open"`) opens them all; *Fold the details ∧* closes the clear ones. A gate that is not clear (`blocker` / `check`) is open whatever the fold says. An open section is a row: `.lienGateRowNum` · `.lienGateRowLabel` (the title as a 150 px column, stacked above the body in a narrow pane) · `.lienGateRowBody`; the body's first line is the fact (`.lienGateFactValue`) with its doors at the right (`.lienGateFactActions`), then `.lienGateRowMeta` lines. The sections' text reads `1Owner of record…` now (the number and the label are two spans).
- **The sections** in `LienDeskModal`: owner — the name with *Check on <county> CAD ↗* and *Change ›* first, the mailing address as one line, then *From the property record · every job here uses it.*; GC — the name with *Change the GC ›*, the address under it; **property kind** — one line `propertyKindLine` (*Residential · Bexar County*) with *Change ›* (`data-lien-gate-kind-change`), which opens the `PropertyKindSwitch` beside `propertyKindSwitchWarning` (*Commercial makes each notice due the 15th of the 3rd month after the work, and the affidavit a month later. Every date on the job moves.*) and *Cancel*; a pick saves and closes (`kindOpen`); while the kind is blank the chooser is open on its own; under it `propertyKindRuleWords · saved on the property record · sharedWithWords` (*shared with 273, 866, 1009, 858*); months — unchanged lines, the muted parts in the row's meta style.
- **Kernel** `lienDeskGates.ts`: `propertyKindLine`, `propertyKindRuleWords`, `propertyKindSwitchWarning`, `sharedWithWords`; `ownerSourceWords` shortened to the middle-dot form. `propertyKindClockWords` stays for the Lien window.
