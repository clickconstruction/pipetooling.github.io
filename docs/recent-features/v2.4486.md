## What was in the way

Since [v2.4169](v2.4169.md) *Build package* was held until every row had its reason **and** its cut sheet, and *Share* until the package was built. BP375 has six small items with no page in the vendor's 75-page file (FCO, FD, HB-3, WHA-200, WHA-300, WHA-500), so the whole Send half stayed shut and the package went out by email. The package itself never needed the lock: `buildCoverModel` has always printed *to follow* in a sheetless row's Sheet column and the note *Cut sheets to follow for: …*.

## What changed

- **The kernel** ([`submittalJourney.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/submittalJourney.ts)): on a draft, only a row that owes a reason holds the package. With reasons done and cut sheets short, *Reasons & sheets* and *Package* are both live; the Next line keeps *Drop a vendor PDF* and adds *You can build the package now too. Those rows will read cut sheet to follow.* A package built that way makes Share the next step (*The package is built. 6 rows in it read cut sheet to follow.*). `stageGate(stages, key, draft?)` takes the newest draft's facts (`DraftFacts`), so the Package and Share buttons follow them even where the strip leaves those pills unlit (a draft answered by email, v2.4484 — which had also left Share held on a built package).
- **The words** ([`submittalRevision.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/submittalRevision.ts)): `rowsOwingSheet`, `buildPackageLabel` (*Build package · 6 cut sheets to follow*), `sheetsToFollowConfirm` (the question, naming up to twelve tags).
- **The tab** ([`BidsSubmittalsTab`](../../src/components/bids/BidsSubmittalsTab.tsx)): the button and the strip's action go through `askBuildPackage`: with sheets missing it asks first. The caption says how many rows the cover will list. Share's own *rebuild the package* line is unchanged.
- **The walkthrough**: stop 4 and the sentence under step 4 say a row with no cut sheet reads *cut sheet to follow*.

## Decided here

- **A reason still holds it.** An alternate with no reason is a sentence the office types; it should not reach the GC blank.
- **The question, not a second lock.** A package that goes out short is a choice, made with the tags in view; the GC may hold those rows until the sheet arrives.
- **No per-row "none for this row".** It needs a column; *to follow* covers the case.
