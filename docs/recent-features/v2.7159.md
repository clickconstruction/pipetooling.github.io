# v2.7159 — Materials: a price of the site's own, and rows that are not a part (2026-11-13)

Punch list #342, PR 2 of 3. PR 1's window could take one answer, a parts-book part. On the first site measured that covers 34 of 96 rows. The bills fit 39, and 48 rows fit nothing: about 20 are not a part and about 15 are fixtures. This PR gives the other answers a home.

## What changed

- **The table** (`20261113160000_on_site_prices.sql`, [`docs/migrations/20261113160000_on_site_prices.md`](../migrations/20261113160000_on_site_prices.md)): one row per answer about a row's price on a site: a price for one with its source (a bill line, or typed) and its day, or `left_out`. Insert and delete, never update. Read and written by the roles that read the Materials desk with its money, and nobody else reads a price.
- **The rule** (`src/lib/materials/sitePrices.ts`, pure, 7 tests): `sitePriceLookup` is the old rule with the site in it: a parts-book price checked inside 90 days, then the site's own price, then the invoice price on the device, then a stale book price. It reads a line through the site's ties, so a typed name tied to a parts-book part is priced as that part. The newest answer of a row is the one read. A site's price has a day and is flagged past 90 days.
- **The store** (`onSiteStore.ts`): the site's prices and its `same` ties are read once beside the first job's boxes (`loadSitePricing`), with each job's site. `priceForJob` and `priceOfJob` are what a job's page reads, so the Missing table, the manifest comparison, the week's flow and the desk's *Missing this week* show the same price as the site's Worth. A refused read leaves the old rule standing.
- **The window**: a bill's offer is a numbered answer. A box takes a typed price for one. **Not a part**, or N, sets the row aside. The foot's **Undo** takes back any of them.
- **The row**: an open row lists **Priced here**, each answer with its source, who and when, with **Undo**, or **Bring back** for a row left out. A row left out says so on its chips.
- **Worth**: a row left out leaves the money and the cover, and the small line says *14 left out*. `siteWorth` also lists the rows on a price older than 90 days, for PR 3.

## What it does not do

- **It does not write the Parts Book.** A bill's price into the book is punch list #298's PR 2b.
- **A price does not travel to another site.**
- **No unit.** The box says *for one, as counted*. A bill that sells by the foot says so on its offer.
