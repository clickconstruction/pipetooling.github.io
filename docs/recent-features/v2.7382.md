# v2.7382 — Lien desk: the master's footer is one compact box (2026-11-15)

Taunya's ask of 2026-11-15, on a screenshot of job 891's awaiting notice: *"I really dislike how this looks, how could we improve it and make it more compact? perhaps putting it to the right of the signature field?"* Client only. No migration, no edge function.

## Before

The footer a leader sees on an awaiting notice (a computer, not the phone's approve screen) stacked two things. First the signature line, a dashed box capped at 470 px with the code field, its hint, the signed day and *Draw instead* each on a line of their own. Under it a `lienFootRow` held *Your call on …*, the offer switch, four plain buttons and the green press. At her window (a pane about 1,125 px wide) the row wrapped and the green press dropped to a line by itself. The two together were about 420 px tall, and two thirds of the pane beside the signature stayed empty.

## What changed

- **[`src/components/jobs/LienNoticeSignLine.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienNoticeSignLine.tsx)**: a new optional `frame` prop (`LienNoticeSignLineFrame`: `call`, `note`, `press`, `strip`). With it the line draws the host's footer inside its own dashed box:
  - the paper on the left, with the printed name under the rule;
  - the call as the box's title, with the signed day and the host's note under it;
  - the code field beside the host's press. The label reads *Malachi's code* above a field six digits wide. The field keeps *Malachi's signing code* as its accessible name;
  - the hint (*Four to six digits, his alone. The record names this screen.*, a wrong code's answer, or why no code can be taken) under that row;
  - the host's strip under a rule;
  - *Draw instead*, *Code instead*, *Press instead* and *Clear* on the paper's top corner.
  Without `frame` nothing changes: same markup, same tests.
- **[`src/components/jobs/LienDesk.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDesk.tsx)**: the awaiting leader branch (not `simpleApprove`, not `holdOpen`) calls `signLine(false, frame)`. The press is *Sign and approve & next ▸* (`.lienSignFramePress`, the same green). The strip is *Not yet*, then *Hold — they promised…*, *Hold — I'll call first*, *Back to the office*, the offer switch and *Already mailed? Record it ›*, all `.lienFootLink`s. `data-lien-desk-next` wraps the box. The offer's details still open above it.
- **[`src/index.css`](../../src/index.css)**: `.lienSignFrame*`. The box reads its own width through a container query (`lien-sign-frame`), not the window's:
  - under 380 px: one column, the press full width and 44 px tall under the code;
  - from 380 px: the label over the field, the press beside it;
  - from 580 px: the call is a title line, the paper sits beside the code and the press;
  - from 962 px: three across. A flex row, so the call keeps its one line and the paper is what gives way;
  - never wider than 1,112 px.

## Checked

- The mock-up first, photographed at nine pane widths before any app code. That found a press running off a 375 px box and the call breaking as *August / 2026*.
- The dev server on job 891's awaiting notice, read only, nothing pressed: 1,920, 1,500, 1,440, 1,366, 1,280, 1,024 and 820 px windows, light and dark. Nothing sits outside the box at any of them. At 1,500 px the footer's box is 168 px tall.
- At 430 px the desk still draws its own phone approve screen (`data-lien-desk-phone-approve`), not this box.

## Not in this change

The draft footer's pad, the office's *Leader here* line and the Ready footer's line still draw the 470 px box. They can take the same frame with their own presses.
