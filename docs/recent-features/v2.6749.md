## What changed

- **Deleted, not kept with a `removed_at`.** A company with no history is a name, a contact and its trades, nothing the office would ever look back for; the sub it came from keeps the calls on Prospects. A `removed_at` would have put a filter on every reader of `gc_companies` (the board, the portal, the edge functions, the dev seat) to hide one row that holds nothing, and the database's own foreign keys could no longer be the backstop. So the row goes, and the server refuses to let it go when anything hangs on it.
- **`gc_remove_bench_company(p_company_id)`** (migration `20261109004500`, `docs/migrations/20261109004500_gc_remove_bench_company.md`): the one write. The office team already has `FOR ALL` on `gc_companies` (door 2), so a plain `DELETE` from the client would go through today and cascade the company's asks, its call log, its promises and its paper sends with it; the rule therefore lives in the function, which looks at every table that holds a company's history and refuses in words naming the first it finds (*ZZ Test Sprinkler Co stays on the bench, since they were asked to quote.*), then puts every Prospects sub that landed as this company back on the lane (`prospect_fit_status` off *converted*, a dated comment *Removed from the bench: ‹name›. Back on the Subs lane to call.*) and deletes the row. `SECURITY DEFINER` with `gc_office_team()` in the body; a training account, a digital twin and an archived caller are refused in words first.
- **`src/lib/gc/benchRemove.ts`** (new, pure): `benchRemoveHold(partner)` is the same rule over what the client's `Partner` already carries — a work order (`won`), a quote (`bids`), an ask (`invited`), a contact logged (`contacts`), a promise, papers either way (`msa`, `msaSentOn`, `w9`, `w9SentOn`, `coiExpires`, `coiReceived`), an opened portal, a vetting form sent — answering the reason or null; `canRemoveFromBench` gates the press; `benchRemoveConfirmWords(company, fromProspects)` is the confirm's title, message and button, the Prospects sentence added when the row carries a `BenchOrigin`.
- **`GcTradePartners.tsx`**: `TradePartnerWrites.removeCompany`; `PartnerLine` draws **Remove from the bench…** under the coverage line only when `canRemoveFromBench`, asks through `useConfirmDialog` (`danger`, `confirmLabel` *Remove from the bench*), reads *Removing…* while the write runs, and draws a refusal's words on the row through `formatErrorMessage`. `gcIo.ts` `removeGcBenchCompany` calls the function (its name through `as never` until the types regen). `GcProjects.tsx` wires the write and refreshes the board.
- **Tests**: `benchRemove.test.ts` (the fresh company may go; each piece of history holds it with its words, the heaviest first; the confirm's words and their plain-words shape); the render smoke (`GcTradePartners.render.test.tsx`, now under `renderWithProviders` for the confirm) — the press is absent on the two companies with history, present on a fresh one, Cancel writes nothing, the confirm's button writes, a sub from Prospects reads the lane sentence, and a plain-object refusal shows its words on the row.
- **Guides**: *add a trade partner* § Remove a company with no history (new); *find subs for the bench* one paragraph on the card coming back. `docs/PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` § 20 one clause.
