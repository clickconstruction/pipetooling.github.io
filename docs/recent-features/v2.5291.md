# v2.5291 — Messages: the boards read every trade partner and customer on their flow in one go (2026-10-12)

Messages build, wave 2, cluster 6, PR A of six: the read under cut 4's boards and the job's papers window. No screen yet. It reads through `flow_rows_kind` (#5435) and the loader (`load.ts`, #5448), and feeds cluster 6's kernels (#5424, #5425, #5450).

- **`src/lib/flows/boardRead.ts`**, pure:
  - `parseFlowRowsKind` checks the definer's answer. It drops an entry with no contact and reads a missing name as empty.
  - `readBoard(flow, answer, now)` runs each contact through `flowStampsFor` and `readFlow`. It returns the board's companies A to Z, each contact's reading, the jobs' names and what we call each contact.
  - `boardCompanyOf` turns a reading into a board company: the places, the Once lane's done steps, the jobs done, and each ended job's way out (`jobsEnded`). The name is the company's, else the person's, and `firstName` follows the contacts kernel.
  - `jobPaperCompanyOf` reads each of the flow's papers for one job.
  - `portalCellWords` gives By company's Portal column (helper 8's read). A trade partner's is their Portal opened step, "opened" or "never". A customer's is the last outside open of their page, "today", "N d ago" or "never", in the app's days.
- **`src/lib/flows/flowsIo.ts`**: `loadFlowRowsKind(kind, projectId)` calls `flow_rows_kind`. It is a definer for the office team, since the facts sit behind doors an assistant cannot open, so there are no client selects (the lead's call).
- **`loadFlowDef(kind)`**, also in `flowsIo.ts`: the office's own flow, kept per kind until `clearFlowDefCache`, which Settings → Flows' Save calls. The read itself is the person panel's `readFlowDef` (`flowPersonIo.ts`, #5471), so there is one copy of it. When it answers the seed (`SEED_FLOWS`, no flow yet or a failed read), nothing is kept.
- **`src/hooks/useFlowBoard.ts`**: one kind's board, or one job's people with a project, as `read` and the boards' `ctx`, with `refresh`. Each step's usual is the flow's own until the done ones are read.
- **Tests**: `boardRead.test.ts` reads the demo people's readings (`flowFixtures.ts`): Greg's company, Marcus declined and off the flow, Dr. Priya Raman's title kept in what we call her. It reads a small answer from rows end to end, and the Portal words across midnight in Texas. `flowsIo.test.ts` covers the office's flow read and joined to its kind, kept until cleared, and the seed when nothing is kept, the read fails or it throws. `useFlowBoard.render.test.tsx` covers the read, the one-job read, refresh, the error in words, and nothing read while disabled.
