# v2.7910 — The pace card: work that is not a stage is one row (2026-11-19)

Punch list #396, after its live walk. Client only. No migration.

## Why

The pace card was rebuilt to say less and show more (v2.7797). Its rail drew every piece of the job's labor budget, and `stageSharesOf` keeps the any-time bid lines as pieces beside the stages. A line that is not a stage is named by the office for the office. The live walk of 2026-11-19 read the card on two real projects. One rail carried a row whose name was a paragraph of scope, about fifty words, and the open card ran 106 words against 48 on a one-stage project. The other carried an office note about who had paid whom. The owner's word on what to do with them: *Yes, fold them*.

## What landed

- **`paceRailStages`** in `lib/jobMode/paceCard.ts`. It takes the pace's pieces with their kind and gives the rail its rows: the in-order stages by name, then one last row, **Other work**, for every other piece (`any`, and the board's own folded `other`). The row keeps the lines' hours, so the rail still adds to the job. Its percent is the lines' own reports weighted by hours, an unreported line reading 0 as an unreported stage does. With no line reported it has none and the job's percent spreads over it like any stage.
- **One line is folded too.** A single line outside the stages reads *Other work*, never its own name.
- **No stage, no rail.** A bid of any-time lines and no stage folds to one row, which is fewer than two, so the card draws its **Work** bar. Before, it drew a rail of bid lines.
- **`SuperintendentCard`** reads each piece's kind from `pace.shares`, which sits index for index with `pace.stages`. `projectsBoard.ts` and `stageShares.ts` are not changed, so the Projects board's strip and its stage list read as before.
- **Checklists** (v2.7885's press on a rail row). A stage still finds its checklist by its own line. The folded row opens the first of its lines' checklists with a question left, else the first with any, and is no press when none has a question.

## Tests

- `paceCard.test.ts`: the fold, one line, the weighted percent, a job of stages left alone, no stage and no rail, and the folded row in hand once the stages are done.
- `SuperintendentCard.render.test.tsx`: two long-named lines beside three stages draw four rows, the last *Other work*, and no word of either name is on the rail. Passing every piece as a stage fails it.

## Not changed

- **Ask a master** names the stage in hand in its second quick question. When the folded row is in hand it reads *What counts as done on Other work?*
- The Projects board still lists each line by name. That page is the office's.
