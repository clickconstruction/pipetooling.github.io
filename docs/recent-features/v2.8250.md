# v2.8250 — Write someone up: the kept note goes once the write-up has sent (2026-11-21)

Punch list #410, a fix from the walk at a phone's size in [`to-dos/write-someone-up/`](../../to-dos/write-someone-up/README.md). Client only.

## Why

Taunya asked on 2026-11-21 for a walk on mobile, and said what matters is that the company records a write-up. The walk cut the signal on a send. The phone kept the write-up and its photo, and both reached HR by themselves when the signal came back. **Your write-ups** then showed two things at once: the row said **Waiting on HR**, and the yellow note above it still said *Kept on this phone at 7:57 pm. It sends by itself when the signal is back.* A leader reading the note would think it had not gone.

## What changed

- **The note follows the phone's own list.** The sheet already hears the outbox each time a kept press lands or is refused. It now takes the kept note off once no kept write-up is waiting.
- **Sent says so.** When the kept write-up has gone and nothing else is kept, the note reads *Sent to HR at*, with the time it went.
- **Refused says why, once.** When a kept write-up is refused, the note goes and no word of sent is shown. The write-up's own line, **Not sent**, carries the reason as before.

## Decisions

- **The time on the sent note is when it went, not when it was pressed.** The press time is on the write-up's row. The note answers the leader's question, which is whether it has gone.
- **The time the note was set lives in a ref beside the state.** The outbox's listener outlives the render that set it, so it cannot read the state.
