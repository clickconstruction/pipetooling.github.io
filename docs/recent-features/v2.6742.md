# v2.6742 — Materials on site: the Short press as a tally request (2026-11-08)

Punch list #209 (`to-dos/materials-on-site/README.md`), the third leftover. The design gave a box's line four presses — Used, Move to…, Can't find and **Short**, *a tally request, so what we have and what we need are one screen* — and v2.6041 shipped three, holding Short *until the tally request behind it is real*. The request behind it was always the Job Parts Tally's own: a `jobs_tally_parts` row and, for a book part, the draft purchase order `create_po_from_job_tally` makes. Short files that. No migration, no edge function.

## What changed

- **The kernel** `src/lib/materials/shortRequest.ts` (4 tests): `shortRequestRow` is the Tally's row for *n* more of a line's part in the presser's name (the part with its maker as `fixture_name`, the book part's id when it has one, a whole number never under one); `shortPoEntries` is the purchase order's one entry for a book part and none for a typed name; the words — `shortLabel` *Ask the office for 4* (`countedLabel`), `shortAfterWords` *A2 has 8. The office gets a request for 4 more.*, `shortToast` for the three outcomes, and `shortRefusalWords`, which turns the table's refusal into *Only the office can file a parts request on this job. Tell them: 4 more ZZ test elbow.* `SHORT_MAX` is 9,999: a request is not capped at what the box has.
- **The writes** `src/lib/materials/shortRequestIo.ts` (4 tests): the row, then for a book part the function and the row's `purchase_order_id` — the Materials Estimate tab's Send to Office path for one line; a purchase order the function refuses is an outcome with its reason and the request stands; a refused row throws its reason.
- **The tab** (`TallyOnSiteTab.tsx`, 3 render smokes): **Short** as the fourth press on every line (four across at 360 and up, two by two under it), and beside *A2 has none left.* on a line with nothing, since that is the line most often short; the sheet *How many more do you need?* with the quick presses, the button carrying its count, and the line under it; the toast names the Parts tab and whether a draft purchase order was made.
- **Docs**: the guide *keep track of the material in the cages and boxes on site*; `MATERIALS_ON_SITE_ARCHITECTURE.md` (The writes, The screens, The gaps); `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`'s Materials on site entry; the card's status, next, ver, The plan and Left list; the release note and this fragment.

## Read closer than the card

- **Nothing in the ledger.** A shortfall is not a movement; Short writes no `job_material_movements` row, so the box's numbers do not move and the office's Missing section reads the counts alone.
- **Who may press it.** `jobs_tally_parts` takes an insert from the office set and a primary on a job they reach, never from a helper or a subcontractor on the job's team — the Materials Estimate tab's Send to Office has the same rule. The press is drawn for everyone the tab serves, and a crew's refused ask reads what to tell the office instead of the policy's words. Opening the table to the job's team is a migration the week of use may ask for; it is on the map's gaps.
- **No note.** The Tally's row has no note column, so the sheet takes a number alone; the part's name and maker are the request's words.
