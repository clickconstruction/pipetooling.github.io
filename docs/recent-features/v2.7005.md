# v2.7005 — First look: the look follows the job, and the tech sees it before the visit (2026-11-12)

Punch list #319, PR 6 of the train.

- **`first_looks.job_id`** (migration `20261112130000_first_look_job.sql`). Two roads set it. A trigger on `estimates` gives the look its job when an estimate that remembers a look gains `job_ledger_id`, which both the accept's auto-create and Create job set, so neither function changes. Book a visit on the look's card hears the job Add Job made (`onCreatedJobId`, passed through `useLookDoors` and `useLeadDoors`) and calls `set_first_look_job`. The trigger never raises: an estimate's write must not fail because of a look.
- **Who reads it.** A look with a job is read by whoever may report on that job (`can_report_stage_progress`), on both tables and on the bucket. A look with no job stays the office team's.
- **The Job Mode card** mounts `JobFirstLookStrip` (`src/components/firstLook/`) above the stage checklists: *What the customer sent*, the photos, the video in a player, the customer's place and words. It draws nothing for a job with no look.
- **In Drive** a look with a job files under the job's own **Customer supplied photos** (`jobSuppliedFolderOrWhy`). A look whose files reached Drive before the job existed has its folder moved there once by the sweep.
- **`LookFile` is its own small file** now, and `showUsLink` lives in `look.ts`, so a tech's card does not load the thread's card or the reply box's ask to draw a picture.
- `src/test/fakeFirstLookIo.ts` is the smokes' one stand-in for the look's io.

- **The sweep is woken for the move** by a second migration, `20261112170000_photo_save_look_move_cron.sql`: a clause on the cron for a look with a job and a Drive folder and no move stamped.
- **Two things the live walks of PRs 2 and 5 showed, fixed here.** The estimate's strip read *asked nov 12 by robert*: a whole line was lowercased to follow a comma, and `lookAskedLower` now drops only the first letter. And the card's two doors told a customer *Makes them a customer…* on hover; a customer now reads what the press does for them.

**Push both migrations after the merge, then deploy `photo-save`.** Any order is safe: `looksOfJob` reads a database with no `job_id` as no look, and the sweep's move step is a no-op there.

Not in this PR: the job window's Documents tab does not list the new folder, and the look's photos are not in the `uploaded_photos` view, so the job's Photos window does not count them.

Tested: `JobFirstLookStrip.render.test.tsx`, `FirstLookCards.render.test.tsx` (Book a visit ties the look), the Job Mode card's own smokes.
