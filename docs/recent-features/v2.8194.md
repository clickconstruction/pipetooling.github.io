# v2.8194 — Document builder: a link says where it stands and who opened it (2026-11-21)

Punch list #404. The review of 2026-11-20 asked Taunya whether **Take it off their page** should also close the sheet's link, which is in the email. Her answer on 2026-11-21: close it with the last trade, let the person see where the link stands at all times, and track every open of every link, with whether the person has an account. For someone with no account she asked for the address the open came from, or what can be told of the device.

The database's part is v2.8187: the table of opens, why a link ended, and the trigger that closes a sheet's link with the last trade. This is the functions and the screens.

## Who opened a link

- The page a link opens sends a few details of its device with the open: language, time zone, screen, platform and whether it is a touch screen (`linkDeviceOf`). Each is something a browser tells every site. Nothing is drawn or measured to tell one device from another, and the function drops anything else (`documentBuilderLinkDevice`).
- `document-builder-link` writes one row an open (`documentBuilderLinkOpenRow`). A person signed in to the app is kept by their account alone. Someone with no account is kept by the address the open came from, the browser's name for itself and the device.
- The link card lists the newest twenty under **Who opened it**, with the count of all of them (`documentBuilderLinkOpenWords`). An account is named and marked *Has an account*. Someone with none reads *Someone with no account*, then the address, the browser in a few words and the time zone.
- The count the card already had, of opens by someone outside, is unchanged.

## Where a link stands

- **Under the trades.** The send card says *1 trade has this sheet. Taking it off their page also switches the sheet's link off.* or *3 trades have this sheet. The sheet's link stays on until the sheet is off the last trade's page.* (`documentBuilderLinkWithSendsWords`). It says so before the press, since the press is what closes the link.
- **On the link card.** A link that is off says the day it ended and why (`documentBuilderLinkOffWords`): it closed with the last trade and the next send switches it on again, or it was switched off. The card reads the link again after a send or a take-off.
- **The next send.** `sheetLinkCodeFor` makes a sheet's link again when every link has ended and the newest ended with the last trade (`documentBuilderLinkSendRemakes`). A link a dev switched off is left off, as before. `desk.end` now writes the why `off`, and a new link over an old one writes `replaced`.
