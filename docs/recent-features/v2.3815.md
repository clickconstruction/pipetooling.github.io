## The change

- **Kernel** (`src/lib/jobs/lienTimeline.ts`): each step may carry `opensOn` + `opensWords`. A month's § 53.056 notice opens the 1st of the next month (`lienNoticeOpensOn`); `opensWords` is set only while the notice is due. The affidavit on a sub job opens on the earliest sent notice's day, or reads *opens when the notice is mailed*; an original contractor's opens the month after the last month worked. A dated § 53.057 clock opens the day the contract ended. `lienWindowSpan` measures a window (total / used / left days); `lienOpensWords` words it (*open since* once the day has come, *opens* before). The timeline also returns `windowsAside` (*Once it is mailed, the lien can be filed any day until Dec 15. Filing it is the leader's call.*, only while a sub's notice is next and the affidavit is still ahead) and `todayYmd`. Existing `words`, `dateWords` and `next` are unchanged.
- **Strip** (`LienTimelineStrip.tsx`): the row and list layouts gain a **Steps · Windows** switch. Steps is the old strip plus the green `opensWords` line. Windows is a calendar chart from the earliest first day (or today) to the latest last day: amber bars for open notices with the used days hatched, green for sent, violet for the lien (dashed while it waits on the notice), text rows for undated, filed or closed steps (the *set the date ›* door kept), missed months listed under the chart with their open-to-closed dates, and the steps with no window (serve, hold, suit, release) on one quiet line. The `mini` layout (the Timeline tab's rows) has no switch and never changes; a `view` prop forces one view and hides the switch.
- **The choice** (`src/hooks/useLienTimelineView.ts`): a module store on localStorage key `lienTimelineView`, defaulting to Steps when storage can't be read (the BidPickerSortToggle pattern). The desk pane, the Affidavits pane, the Lien window, the Legal desk and the law firm's portal strips all share it.
- **Months on this job** (`LienDeskMonths.tsx`): in Windows the Window column reads *open since Aug 1 · mail by Oct 15* with a bar and *21 of 75 days left*, and a closed, unnoticed month adds *it was open Apr 1 → Jun 15*. Steps is unchanged.
- **Guides**: *send lien notices from the Lien desk* → Where a job stands describes the green line and the switch; *read the Texas lien rules the app follows* → Not yet verified lists the three first-day readings for counsel.
