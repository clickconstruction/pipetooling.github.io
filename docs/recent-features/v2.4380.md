# v2.4380 — Person desk: the last row stays above the bottom bar (2026-10-02)

No migration, no edge function. Found on 2026-10-02 while walking [v2.4370](v2.4370.md) at iPad size; Grace asked for it before the next working day.

## Why

The Dispatch / Job mode footer (`DispatchModeFooter`, 60 px + the safe area) is fixed at z 1000. The Person desk drawer ([`PersonDeskDrawer`](../../src/components/personDesk/PersonDeskDrawer.tsx)) is a full-screen layer at `DESK_Z` (60) whose panel scrolls its own content to the bottom of the screen, so the bar sat on top of the desk's last 60 px and the desk could not scroll them clear. The Access & account section's last row — Status: Merge a duplicate…, Archive / Restore — was unreachable whenever the desk's content was taller than the screen: at 1024×768 the row sat at 719–743 under the bar (708–768), and `elementFromPoint` there returned the bar's Customers button. Windows inside a page already end above the bar with `bottom: var(--app-bottom-chrome, 0px)` (v2.2184 — the Lien desk, the GC notice windows), but the desk is mounted in `Layout.tsx` after `<main>`, where that variable was set, so it could not read it.

## What changed

- **The variable moved up** ([`Layout.tsx`](../../src/components/Layout.tsx)): `--app-bottom-chrome` is set on the layout's root wrapper instead of `<main>`, with the same value (the footer's height while Dispatch or Job mode shows it, else `0px`). Everything inside `<main>` reads the same value as before; `<main>` keeps its `paddingBottom`. The windows mounted outside `<main>` read it now too — the Person desk, and the header's windows that already asked for it (`MissedClockInModal`, `ClockedInMapModal` when opened from the header) — so they also stop at the bar instead of under it.
- **The desk ends above the bar**: its layer is `top: 0; left: 0; right: 0; bottom: var(--app-bottom-chrome, 0px)` instead of `inset: 0`; the panel is `height: 100%` of it. The bar stays visible and tappable below the desk.
- The other app-wide windows mounted beside the desk were checked: they sit at z 1000 or above (the bar's comment: an overlay whose bottom matters stands above it), and the Find a person sheet (z 61) ends at 70 % of the screen. The team-feedback drawers are portaled at z 1000 after the bar, so they already cover it.
- The v2.4338 click guard still sees the desk as a backdrop: it covers 92 % of the height at 1024×768, 93 % on a 375×812 phone.
- [`APP_SHELL_ARCHITECTURE.md`](../APP_SHELL_ARCHITECTURE.md) amended: where the variable is set, and its full list of readers.
