# v2.7468 — GC difficulty rating: an estimator can rate a GC (2026-11-16)

Punch list #357 ([`to-dos/gc-difficulty-rating/README.md`](../../to-dos/gc-difficulty-rating/README.md)), a gap the 2026-11-16 docs catch-up found after the train of six closed. One migration (`20261117003000_gc_difficulty_raters_from_price_list.sql`, [its page](../migrations/20261117003000_gc_difficulty_raters_from_price_list.md)), no edge function. The owner's word, 2026-11-16: *Estimator should be able to rate.*

## The gap

The price list's **Who may rate a GC** (PR 4, v2.7301) names the office, the estimators and the masters, and the client has obeyed it since. The rating table's insert rule was written a PR earlier as `is_office_staff()`, "until the Bid Board's price list says who may rate", and was never moved. So the two disagreed in both directions: with the estimators ticked an estimator was offered **Save** and refused by the database, and with the masters or the office unticked they lost the press and could still write.

## Which reading was built

The owner's sentence reads two ways: an estimator always rates whatever the list says, or the database obeys the list and the list names the estimators. The second was built. PR 2's fragment says in words that PR 4 moves the gate to the price list's *who may rate*; the card's call 4 makes that list the owner's and the controller's switch; and a rule that ignores the switch is the bug, not the switch. So the owner's word became a line on the list (version 2), and the list stays theirs to change.

## What changed

- **The database asks the list** (`gc_difficulty_can_rate()`, the insert rule `gc_difficulty_ratings_insert_raters`): a dev always; the owner under `master`, an assistant and a controller under `office`, an estimator under `estimator`, as the newest published price list names them; nobody else. With no list, every group may. It is the twin of `difficultyCanRate()` in [`difficultyPrice.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/difficultyPrice.ts), and the kernel's test reads the migration to hold the default list and each role's group equal.
- **Price list version 2**, published by the migration as *the app*: version 1's numbers with the estimators added to who may rate. Version 1 is unchanged and bids priced under it keep it.
- **One answer for every press.** The GC's card, the Bid Board's chip and **Rate them**, the sheet from a bid and the sheet from the ask already asked `difficultyCanRate`. The ask at the end of a job asked only the role; it now draws only while `difficultyCanRate` lets that master rate ([`GcDifficultyAsk.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/GcDifficultyAsk.tsx)), so nobody is asked to open a sheet that cannot save. The Workbench's chip is read-only for every role and stays so. With no list read, the client's default is now every group (`DIFFICULTY_RATERS_DEFAULT`), as the database answers.
- **An estimator's rating keeps the Paying point** ([`difficultyPaying.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/difficultyPaying.ts), `difficultyPayingPick`'s third argument). The pay-speed read is the money roles', so an estimator's reads *could not be read*. Before, a save made while the bills could not be read left Paying off the new rating, and since the newest rating stands, the GC lost its point and every later bid its paying line. Now the point the rating holds is carried on word for word, and the card says *Their bills could not be read here. The rating keeps the 1 point it holds.* with the numbers behind it. The same holds for anyone whose read fails. The point is kept only while the published criteria still have that scale and rung (`gcDifficultyKeepableBillsPick`), so the card never says it keeps what the save would drop. For someone who cannot read the bills the by-hand press reads **Take it back** (back to what the rating holds) and does not draw while the point on screen is the one the rating holds.
- Guides *rate how a GC is to work with* (who may rate; what an estimator sees under Paying) and *set what difficulty costs* (version 2 ticks all three); ACCESS_CONTROL's two rows, the glossary entry and PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION amended.

## What an estimator can do now

Open a GC's record, or press the GC's chip or **Rate them** on the Bid Board, pick the sentences on **Field and superintendent** and **Their office**, write what happened and save. From a bid the sheet also takes the site pick and shows what the points add to that bid, as it does for a master. Paying stays what the rating holds. An estimator is not asked at the end of a job: that ask is the job's master's.

## Not changed

- Who reads a rating (everyone signed in), who deletes one (a dev), the guard (who and when stamped, picks held to the published criteria, no update).
- Training mode, the digital twin fence and the archived seal: each still refuses the row. The function reads the role alone, so a twin, which is an estimator by role, is told yes by it and refused by its fence.
- Who edits the price list (the owner, the controller, a dev) and who edits the criteria (a dev).
- Primary, superintendent, sub and helper: never raters, in the client and in the database.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **Doubted: publishing a price list version from a migration.** The list is the owner's and the controller's, and a migration writing it is the app speaking for them. Kept, because the owner said the words and the alternative leaves the fix waiting on a tick nobody was asked to make. Narrowed: the numbers are copied, the row says whose word it was, and it runs once, so taking the estimators off later stays their choice.
- **Doubted: a dev always may.** The client has said so since PR 4 and the walks depend on it. The database now says the same. Kept.
- **Doubted: the owner can untick the masters and lose the save.** That is what the list means, and the sheet refuses to publish a list that names nobody. Kept; before this the database let the owner save anyway while the client hid the press.
- **Doubted: carrying the Paying point instead of letting an estimator read it.** A narrow read of one GC's pay speed for estimators would let their rating refresh the point. Pay speed is money, and the roles that read it were chosen on purpose. Changed nothing there: the point is kept, and the office's next look at the card reads it fresh and says when it has drifted.
- **Left for the owner, with a pick:** an estimator on the GC's card can still press **Set by hand…** on Paying, since the door is for whoever may rate. Pick: keep it. A by-hand point carries the person's name and reason and every rating is kept. Say so if Paying by hand should be the office's alone.
- **Left:** while the price list is still being read, the card draws as if every group may rate, so a group taken off the list sees the picks live for a moment before they lock. It was so for the office and the masters before this. A save in that moment is refused by the database with its reason.
