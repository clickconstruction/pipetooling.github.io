# v2.8636 — Pricing: a price typed on a sent bid no longer stays in the field as if it saved (2026-11-26)

Punch list #445, finding 239 of the walking thread's sitting 23 (2026-11-26), briefed by the fixing thread as `445-locked-price-keeps-the-typed-value.md`. The fault is older than card #445: as built since the lock shipped in v2.3591. It is cited against #445 because that card's walk met it. Client only. No migration, no edge function. Nothing owed after the merge.

**Read.** On a ZZ Test bid that was sent, so its pricing is locked, a walker typed a new *Sale price per unit* twice and pressed Enter. Nothing was saved, which is right. The field went on showing the typed number until a reload, and the reload read the old price. The only word of the refusal was a toast, which leaves in a few seconds. In `BidsPricingTab.tsx`, `commitWorkbenchTypedPrice` asked `guardPriceRowsWrite()` and returned on a refusal before the row's draft was dropped, and the field draws the draft while one exists. Every other way out of the function drops it.

**The mend.** When the sent-bid lock refuses a typed price, the row's draft is dropped, so the field reads the saved price again as the toast shows, and the strip's Revenue and Profit follow it back. All four typed cells of a row go through this one function, so the sale price, Revenue, Profit and Margin mend together. The brush already dropped its drafts on a refusal (v2.5671).

**The loading refusal is left as it was**, the brief's pick. `guardPriceRowsWrite` also refuses in the short window while the open price is still loading. That refusal passes by itself in a moment, so the typed number waits in the field and saves on the next Enter or blur. Only the lock, which stands until **Revise…** is pressed, drops the draft.

The guide *price a bid with the workbench* says a sent bid's prices are locked and the box goes back to the saved price.

## What did not change

- What the lock refuses, the toast's words and **Revise…**.
- A typed price on a bid that is not sent, or on one being revised: saved on Enter or blur as before.
- The toast itself. The app's toasts carry no status role, briefed apart as `445-toasts-carry-no-status-role.md`.
