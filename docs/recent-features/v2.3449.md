# v2.3449 — Owner of record: the job form's Property record row looks the site up on the appraisal roll and offers Use; the builder-as-customer question; the homestead line (2026-09-15)

No migration, no edge function. PR 2 of the owner-of-record train ([`to-dos/owner-of-record-prompts/`](../../to-dos/owner-of-record-prompts/README.md); PR 1 is [v2.3447](./v2.3447.md) — the Fix-ups list, the kernel and the shared write this PR reuses). Decisions 2 and 3 as the owner picked them on 2026-09-14: the lookup runs silently on GC and builder jobs only (A), and a likely homestead is said on the spot with the CAD link beside it (A).

## Why

Moment 1 of the design: the owner of record should appear the moment a GC job exists, not weeks later on the Lien desk. The roll answered ten of ten live addresses, so the job form never asks anyone to type — the Property record row looks the site up by itself and a person presses **Use**. The one question the roll cannot answer is the builder-in-the-customer-row shape (3 open jobs): "we contracted with the owner" or "they are building it for someone?" — the second answer is what starts the § 53.056 clock, so it is asked once, where the contract question already lives.

## What shipped

- **Kernel [`ownerConfirm.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/ownerConfirm.ts)** (+4 tests): `jobFormOwnerLookupApplies` (a GC set, or a builder in the customer row; an address; no linked row with `owner_confirmed_at`), `builderQuestionApplies` (a builder customer and no GC), `isBuilderCustomer(customerId, knownGcIds)` (a customer that is `gc_customer_id` on other jobs — the RPC's `builders` CTE, client side), and **`HOMESTEAD_LINE`** — the one exported sentence every surface and the guide reuse: *Likely a homestead — the owners get mail at the property. A lien on a homestead needs a contract signed by both spouses and recorded with the county before work starts. Confirm the exemption on the CAD page and talk to the attorney before the crew goes out.* **Placeholder wording — the attorney is to confirm the sentence.** The Fix-ups list (`OwnerConfirmListModal`) now reads the same constant.
- **Reads [`ownerConfirmJobFormClient.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/ownerConfirmJobFormClient.ts)**, each cached for the session: `fetchIsBuilderCustomer` (one head count on `jobs_ledger.gc_customer_id`), `lookupPropertyRecordCached` (once per property key, never on a render), `fetchJobsAtProperty` (every job at the same street number + street, this job first, so one Use links them all — the list gets these from the RPC; the form asks the ledger), `fetchCustomerAddressRow`.
- **[`JobFormOwnerLookupBox`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormOwnerLookupBox.tsx)** (+5 render cases) — mounted under the Edit tab's **Property record** row (`JobFormEditFactRows`; the shell's candidates now carry `owner_confirmed_at` and a new `onOwnerConfirmed` upserts the saved row and links the job). When the rule applies it runs the lookup and renders the green **Found on the appraisal roll · from the job address** box: Owner of record · Mail to · Legal + district · tax-year provenance · County, the reads-as chips (`readsAs`), then **Use — save the owner on <street>** (→ `confirmOwnerForProperty` with `{ kind: 'proposal' }` for this job and every job at the address; the parent links the job and the row reads the linked property as before), *Not right — paste the CAD page…* (unfolds `CustomerPropertyRecordPanel`'s paste box, reused; *Save the owner on <street>* writes `{ kind: 'record' }`) and *this parcel on <County> CAD ↗*. A miss reads *No parcel under the pin — paste the CAD page…*; a lookup error shows its message with the same door. A direct job renders nothing; so does a confirmed row and an unsaved form.
- **The homestead line**: when `homesteadHint` says likely the box adds `HOMESTEAD_LINE` in red with the CAD link beside it (the `likely homestead` chip rides above it). A public owner keeps PR 1's bond-claim note.
- **The builder-as-customer question** on [`JobContractAfterCreatePrompt`](../../src/components/jobs/JobContractAfterCreatePrompt.tsx) (+2 render cases): a box above the contract doors — *Is <customer> building this for someone?* → **No — they own the site** (records nothing, closes the question, no lookup — the customer is the owner) / **Yes — they are the builder** (writes `jobs_ledger.gc_customer_id = customer_id`; `customer_id` is not cleared — the office chooses the site owner later). On a GC job — or once Yes set the GC — the same prompt shows the Found box above the doors, so the owner is confirmed the moment the job exists; the contract doors stay beneath.
- Guide *file a lien and never miss its deadlines* → "The app finds the owner; you confirm it" gains the job-form moment, the builder question and the homestead line; `docs/JOB_FORM_MODAL_ARCHITECTURE.md` (§5) one paragraph; the to-do README marks PR 2 shipped.
