# v2.3449 — Pipeline: the words line stops repeating the money (2026-09-14)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Follows v2.3446 (the cell's overflow) from the same live look at the *Where the Job Is* cell.

## Why

v2.3446 let the sentence wrap to two lines instead of clipping at one. A live count on the board then showed **40 of 92** rows still cut — and every one of them was cut inside its **money clause**, which repeats verbatim the legend printed directly beneath it:

> `nobody clocked in · 75% set Aug 7 · $4,520 paid, nothing billed · $520 done, not billed`
> ■ 100% Paid $4,520 · ■ 0% Billed — · ■ 12% Done, not billed $520 · □ … Not done · **Left on Job**

The approved brief put the money in the sentence because the mock-up drew the cell at **372 px**; the real Pipeline column is **176 px**. Two lines of 176 px hold about 55 characters and the median sentence was 63.

## What shipped

- [`progressPaymentCell.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/progressPaymentCell.ts): `words` becomes `{ text, full, tone }`. **`text` is what the row prints** — the live stage, the crew clause and the percent clause. **`full`** appends the money clause and feeds the bar's `aria-label` and the line's `title`, where there is room and no legend beside it. The money still decides the **tone** (amber when a bill is out unpaid or work is done and unbilled, green at paid in full), so the urgency survives without the words.
- A **no-bid row keeps the whole sentence** (`text === full`): it has no legend money to repeat, and *nothing to bill against* is the point of that red line.
- [`StagesStageBar`](../../src/components/jobs/StagesStageBar.tsx) prints `text`, and names/annotates with `full`.

## If the owner wants the money back in the sentence

One line: print `full` instead of `text` in `StagesStageBar`. The alternatives considered were a third clamped line (still cuts the longest rows) and a wider Progress & payment column (takes width from Job, which carries the address and the activity box).
