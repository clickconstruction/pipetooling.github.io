# v2.6997 — Permits: a San Antonio centre with several lots names the lot the permit is on (2026-11-12)

Punch list #320. Found on the walk after v2.6991 was deployed, with the lot under each permit's point as the referee.

## What the walk showed (read-only, counts only, 2026-11-12)

The 60 newest San Antonio commercial building permits with a point, one per address, none looked up before. 58 name an owner, 39 by the address and 19 at the permit's point, 2 have no parcel, no error. Of the 39 by the address, 11 are lots the old whole-string question would have missed (a loop the county names, PLACE for PL, an interstate spelled out).

The referee: of the 39 answers the function kept, the lot named is the lot under the permit's point 32 times and another lot 7 times, with another owner on 6. Of the 11 found only by the new question it is another lot 4 times. v2.6991's own measure had said 1 in 18.

Looked at one by one: on 5 of the 7 the county answers **several lots at the asked number on the asked street** (2 to 5 of them) and the lot under the point is one of them. A centre on Loop 410 is one address and a parcel per pad. One of the 5 is an interstate address where the row picked carries the other direction (E for the permit's W). The other 2 have one lot at the address and a point that sits on a lot with another number.

## What changed

- **Kernel** (`supabase/functions/_shared/permitParcels.ts`): `bexarLotsAtAddress` counts the lots at the asked number on the asked street in the county's answer. `bexarLotIdsOf` reads the lot ids off an answer. `parcelFactsFromBexarAddress` takes a third argument, the ids of the lots under the permit's point: a row at the address whose lot is under the point wins over the side of town and over a row with an owner. With no ids, or a point on none of the address's lots, the pick is as before.
- **`permit-lookup`**, Bexar's branch: when the address answers more than one lot and the permit has a point, the point is asked for its lot ids (`PropID` alone) and handed to the pick. The kept `query` then holds the point question after the address question. One lot at the address asks nothing more. The answer is still by the address, so the facts carry no `matched_by` and the card's words do not change.
- **Guide** `research-permits-for-work-to-bid.md`: two sentences on a centre with several lots.

## Measured through the new kernel, before the deploy

The same 39 addresses against the live layer: 9 have several lots at the address and 30 have one. Through the old pick the named lot is the one under the point 33 times (the county's row order is not fixed, so one of the 7 came out right this time). Through the new pick it is 37, and every one of the 9 is the lot under the point (5 before). The pick changes on 4, each to another owner, the interstate address among them. The two left are the two with one lot at the address.

Not done, on purpose: a kept answer is not asked again. A San Antonio card opened between the two deploys keeps its answer for its thirty days unless it is looked up again. A direction behind the street (W against E on an interstate) is still not compared. The point settled the one case seen.

Tests: `permitParcels.test.ts` (the lot under the point over the first row, over an owner and over the side of town; a point on none of the lots; a lot counted once; another street or number not counted). Every name and number is made up.

Deploy: `permit-lookup` after the merge, from `main` (`permit-pull` bundles the same file; `check:edge-drift` says). No migration.
