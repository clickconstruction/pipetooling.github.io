# v2.5345 — GC mode: our contract's Paper waits for the customer's portal (2026-10-12)

A follow-up to v2.5334 (#5507), by GC Reviews helper 9 at the lead session GC Review's call. v2.5334 put **Paper** on our contract's send. The customer's portal doesn't yet show a contract built from clauses or send the revision it showed. That's helper 8's clause read: `customer-portal`, and `submit-portal-request` on `gc_customer_sign_owner_contract`'s `p_paper_revision`. So a send with a paper would be refused at the customer's signing (paperChanged).

- **The flag**: `OWNER_CONTRACT_PAPER_LIVE` in `src/lib/gc/customerContract.ts`, off. While it's off, `GcCustomerContractSend` reads no paper and shows no **Paper**, and a new send goes with no paper. **Helper 8's clause-read PR turns it on** and changes the test that pins it (below).
- **A statement of work's Paper** is unchanged. Its portal side shipped with v2.5329 (#5503).
- **The guide**: `send-our-contract-to-a-gc-customer` drops its **Paper** lines while the flag is off. The PR that turns it on puts them back:
  - Step 4 of *Send it to sign*: `4. Under **Paper**, keep **Today's paper: our contract file** or pick a paper built from our clauses.` The day, the line and the press become steps 5 to 7.
  - After the steps: `**Paper** shows once a paper is built from clauses in [Contracts and clauses](/settings?tab=settings-clauses). Press {{button:outline|Preview}} to read the paper as they will get it. The file still goes with it.`
  - At the end of *Remind them*'s first paragraph: `**Paper** starts on the paper that went before.`
- **Tests**: `GcCustomerWindow.render.test.tsx` pins the shipped value off. With it off, the send shows no **Paper**, reads no paper and sends none. The window's and the page's paper tests run with it on.
