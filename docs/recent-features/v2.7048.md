## What changed

- **`lienArChip.ts`**: a chip that asks for a step takes its words from `lienActFor` — *Find the owner*, *Draft the notice*, *Draft the late notice*, *Sign the notice*, *Send the notice*, *Draft the affidavit*, *Approve the affidavit*, *Waiting on Robert*, *Send the late notice*, *File the affidavit*, *Serve the affidavit*, and the fact that holds an affidavit (*Answer the homestead question*, *Add the GC's address*). It keeps its own day and count after them, since the chip stands alone; *Public property, no lien* carries none. Chips that say a state keep their words: a hold, a notice in the mail, paper that is out, a closed window, the reminders.
- **New test `lienOneReading.test.ts`**: for a notice at each step, an affidavit ready to file, and a notice window closed with the affidavit's day ahead, the billed row's label (`buildBilledDatesLedger`), the chip (`lienArChipFor`), the act table (`lienActFor`) and the Lien window's Next on the path (`buildLienTimeline`) name the same step. The window's header keeps its own sentence (it names the month and counts the days), so the test holds the verb it opens on.
- **Guide** *drill into the Dashboard money cards*; Glossary *Lien desk*.

## Not done, and why

- **The Lien window's header for an affidavit's earlier steps.** It reads *File the affidavit — 4 days* whether or not a draft exists: the window builds its timeline from the job's filings and cannot see the desk's record (`buildLienTimelineFromWindow` passes no notice or affidavit state). Giving it the desk's step means handing the window the job's Do now row, and rewording its *Waiting on* line to match. It is listed on the card.
- **The Lien desk's Do now rows** keep their shape, a state (*Affidavit to draft*) beside a button that names the act (*Draft affidavit*). Their words are read by the step briefs, the groups and the chip, and their button names are quoted in the guides, so they were left.
