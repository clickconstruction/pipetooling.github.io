# v2.7356 — Job accounts: a not needed answer says who marked it and when (2026-11-15)

One migration (`20261115230000_job_account_not_needed_stamp.sql`, [its doc](../migrations/20261115230000_job_account_not_needed_stamp.md)); no edge function. The second of two PRs: v2.7345 made **None needed…** ask why and gave the sheet its truthful sentence and its two ways back. Taunya asked on 2026-11-15 for the stamp as its own PR.

## Why

A not-needed row recorded no name and no time. The sheet could say *The office decided* and nothing about who, and on the test job that started this the job's history had no line for it either. The requested and opened statuses have had their stamps since the table was made.

## What changed

- **The record.** `job_supply_house_accounts` gains `not_needed_by` and `not_needed_at`, filled by the table's stamp trigger: when a row becomes not needed, and again when its reason changes. A value the client sends is not kept, and a row that is opened or asked for carries none.
- **The rows already marked** take the day of their last write and no name.
- **The strip's read.** `list_job_account_strip` hands the day and the marker's name with each row, so the crew's sheet can show them without reading `users`.
- **The sheet.** Under the sentence, [`JobAccountsStrip`](../../src/components/jobs/JobAccountsStrip.tsx) draws one line from `notNeededStampWords`: *Marked by Tess on Nov 13, 2026.*, *Marked on Oct 7, 2026.* for a row marked before the stamp, and no line where the row carries none. The day is the company calendar's.
- **Guide**: *open a job account before buying parts* says the sheet names who marked it and when.

## Order

The client reads the two columns as absent until the migration is pushed and draws no stamp line, so nothing breaks in between. The push follows the merge.

## Not changed

- The words of the sheet's first sentence, **Change this…** and **Take the mark off**.
- The house roster under Materials and the Bid Board's chips, which do not show the stamp.
- The job's Activity list: a mark is still not a line there.

## Tests

- The `job-account-not-needed-stamp` SQL bed, in CI: the migration twice over the old shapes, then ten scenarios on the trigger, the backfill, the strip and its grants.
- `jobAccountsNewJob.test.ts`: the stamp's four wordings and the company calendar's day. `jobAccountStrip.test.ts`: the stamp carried from the row, null where the row or the database has none.
- `JobAccountsStrip.render.test.tsx`: the line with a name, with the day only, and absent.
