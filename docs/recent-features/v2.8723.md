## Why

The Build desk ends a stalled row with *Crew it, or name why it waits*, and there was nowhere to name it. A job the GC is not ready for counted against the crew in the quiet list and its money every week it sat.

## What landed

- **Name why it waits ›** in a quiet job's detail on the Build desk, beside its doors (`BridgeBuildDesk.tsx`). It shows only on a job that reads stalled, and only once the waits could be read.
- **The small window** (`BuildWaitWindow.tsx`, on `PhoneSheet`): six reasons, a line to type, **Cancel** and **Mark it waiting**. The press is dead until a reason is picked, *Something else* needs words, and the line under the press says which.
- **A fifth group in the kernel** (`buildDesk.ts`, group `waits`): a waiting job leaves Stalled, its money and the *biggest money idle* chip, sits grey at the end of the ledger, and its dollars get a fourth tile, *Waits, with a reason*. The verdict gains *1 job waits with a reason.* The headline, the hours and the work done do not change.
- **Waiting, with a reason (N)** at the foot of the window (`buildSteps.ts`): the waiting jobs, each opening to its why-line and **Lift the wait ›**.
- **A job with hours is read by its hours.** A wait only takes a quiet job out of the count. A waiting job that gets hours reads as it would have, says it is still marked waiting, and offers Lift the wait.
- **The job's Activity** draws the two lines the function writes, *Marked the job waiting: …* and *Lifted the wait…*, with the status changes under a *Waits* tag (`jobActivityEvent.ts`). An older client drops an event type it does not know, so nothing broke before this was live.
- **The chip on the job's card and table row** on Jobs → Stages: *Waits · the GC is not ready for us · since Oct 26* (`BuildWaitChip.tsx`, `useOpenBuildWaits.ts`: one small read as the board mounts, read again when a wait changes). It shows to whoever reads the job.
- The words and the rule are one kernel, `src/lib/bridge/build/buildWaits.ts`. The read and the two writes are `buildWaitsIo.ts`: a read the database refuses answers null and never throws, so every screen draws as it did.
- Guide: *the Bridge* → Build, broken down → A job that waits, with a reason.

## What differs from the drawing, and why

- **The desk is the step frame now** (punch list #334), not the ledger the mock-up drew. So the press sits in a quiet job's detail, and the waiting jobs sit behind a press at the foot and at the end of **Every open job**.
- **Not built: the fact row in the job window**, with its own Lift the wait. Until it is, a wait is lifted from the Bridge, which the owner and a dev open. The office reads the chip and cannot lift. It is the card's next small piece.
- The chip on the card is a label, not a press.
