# v2.3299 — Burn against the bid, PR 2: the Budget card on the job's Costs tab (2026-09-11)

Client only (reads the v2.3297 table and RPCs). Rung 2 of [`to-dos/burn-against-the-bid/`](../../to-dos/burn-against-the-bid/README.md).

## What shipped

- [`useJobBudget`](../../src/hooks/useJobBudget.ts): the `job_budgets` row; while there is none, `suggest_bids_for_job`; when the job carries a `bid_id`, `bid_estimate_breakdown` for the linked bid (to offer "Take its estimate" or to compare a snapshot against the bid value). Writes through `snapshot_job_budget_from_bid`, `set_typed_job_budget`, `clear_job_budget`, reloading after each.
- [`JobBudgetCard`](../../src/components/jobs/JobBudgetCard.tsx) (+4 render tests), mounted by [`JobCostsTabCharts`](../../src/components/jobs/JobCostsTabCharts.tsx) above Burn for the wage roles:
  - **Frame A** (a budget exists): *◆ Budget from bid B66 · bid $123,600 = job price / ≠ job price · estimate taken Sep 11 · by you · estimate complete / 3 rows without hours / no labor rate on the bid · materials from takeoff*; then the component table — **labor in hours first** (used h vs budget h, dollars muted beneath, the bid's rate), materials, subs, other — each with used, budget, a bar whose fill is used ÷ budget with the % done as an amber marker (red past the budget), and at-completion at today's pace (`componentBurn`); then the **why** sentence (`budgetWhyWords`). *Refresh from bid ↻* re-snapshots; *Clear* returns to the assumption (the link stays); a typed budget has *Edit*.
  - **Frame B** (no budget): *≈ Burn is reading an assumed budget · price × 65 % (35 % target) = $80,340. No bid is linked.* — the ranked candidates with the reason, the bid value / GC / outcome, whether the bid has an estimate and how many hours, **Link this bid**; **Find another bid** by number or name; **or type a budget…** (hours · materials $ · subs $ → `set_typed_job_budget` with the company crew rate from `useBidCrewRate` for the labor dollars). A job already linked but never snapshotted gets *Linked to B375 … · Take its estimate as the budget*.
- [`JobCostsBurnSection`](../../src/components/jobs/JobCostsBurnSection.tsx) takes the resolved budget: `bidEstimateUsd = budgetForBurn(resolved)`, so ◆ / ✎ feed Burn's budget and the header and Budget tile read *◆ budget from the bid · price $…* / *≈ assumed · price $…*.
- [`useJobChargesTimelineInputs`](../../src/hooks/useJobChargesTimelineInputs.ts) exposes `teamHours` (recorded team-labor hours) for the labor row; [`jobBudget.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobBudget.ts) gains `spendByComponent` (+1 test).

Role gate: the card renders with Burn (wage roles); Link / typed / Refresh / Clear show for dev · assistant · controller · master technician (the server's `can_write_job_budget` is the real gate).
