# v2.6626 — License targets: the class, the board’s number and the hours before the clock (2026-11-07)

Punch list #255 (`to-dos/technician-progress-signals/`), the train's PR 3, built after PR 7: the opening balance and the license class on the Licenses tab, read by `my_week()`, the Monday email and the logbook. Until now the logbook passed `openings={[]}` and every goal came from the seeded ladder (`REQUIREMENTS`, v2.6472), so a tech who brought 1,920 hours from another shop saw a bar that started at zero, and a journeyman electrician saw *Its rule is not seeded yet* with no way past it.

## What changed

- **Migration `20261107050000_license_targets.sql`** (`docs/migrations/20261107050000_license_targets.md`): the table `license_targets`, one row a person and trade: `target_class` and `target_amount` (set or null together; the license worked toward and the number the board asks for), `opening_amount` (hours, or HVAC months, before the clock) and `opening_source`. The row's own teammate or the Licenses audience reads; the audience alone writes. The four fences close it. `week_card_data(user)` and `my_logbook()` answer a `targets` array; `my_week()` is the wrapper and needs nothing.
- **Kernel `src/lib/logbook/logbook.ts`**: `LicenseTarget`, `parseLicenseTargets`, `openingsFrom`, `unitOfTrade`, `goalName` (*AC contractor* keeps its capitals), `requirementToward` (the seeded rule toward that class for the unit, signer, board and form, with the office's number and no caveat). `goalsFor(held, targets)`: a target above the rung held wins over the seeded rule and resolves a `noRuleYet` rung; one at or below it is ignored, so a license added later never strands a goal. `Goal.fromOffice` marks it.
- **Kernel `src/lib/logbook/licenseTarget.ts`** (+ 8 tests with the kernel's): the window's options (`targetOptions`, the classes above the one held; `ladderOptionWords` for the default), `targetMissing` (what stops the save), `targetFromDraft`, `targetSummary` (*Saves as working toward journeyman plumber, 7,000 hours, with 1,200 hours before the clock.*) and `targetLines`, the Licenses tab's one line a trade.
- **`LicenseTargetWindow`** (`src/components/logbook/`, + 2 render tests; `licenseTargetIo.ts` upserts the row): Working toward, the board's number (prefilled from the seeded rule when the class has one), the hours before the clock, where they came from, and **Save the goal**.
- **People → Licenses**: a **Goals** block under a teammate's licenses, one line a trade they hold a license in, with **Set the goal** or **Edit the goal**.
- **The logbook** (`/logbook/:userId`, `/my-logbook`): reads the targets (the table for the office, `my_logbook()` for the teammate), passes them to `goalsFor` and the openings to `progressFor` and the print. The goal card says *set by the office* and *Includes 1,200 hours before the clock started: Hours at Rio Plumbing.*; the office gets **Edit the goal** on the card and **Set the goal** beside a rung with no rule.
- **Your week and the Monday email** (`weekSignal.ts`): `MyWeek.targets`; `licenseHoursRead` and the eight-week dots take the office's goal and add the opening balance to the approved hours.
- **Guides**: *keep a teammate's logbook and print it for the board* gains *Set the goal and the hours before the clock*; *see your week move* and *see my own logbook* say the hours before the clock count once the office puts them in.

## Decisions

- **Keyed by the user, not the name.** The logbook, `my_week()` and `my_logbook()` all stand on `users.id`; licenses still hang on `person_name` (the link by id is still to build, v2.6472's list).
- **A target below the license held is ignored, never refused.** The office sets *journeyman* for an apprentice; when the journeyman card is added later the row goes quiet instead of blocking the save or stranding the goal.
- **One opening balance a trade, no history.** The office types the total and its source once; the signed form stays in the HR file.
