# v2.8458 — Reload on a page that could not load asks the network first (2026-11-24)

Punch list #85. Two of the three things the live look of 2026-11-24 found and left (`docs/recent-features/v2.8377.md`, *What differed*): **Reload** with no network, and the size of the press. The third, a window's next press, is its own change. Diane said to fix them on 2026-11-24. Client only: no migration and no edge function.

## Before

A signed-in page whose code did not come draws *This page could not load — the app may have just been updated.* with **Reload** (`RouteChunkBoundary`). It draws it when the one guarded reload is spent, and since v2.8327 when the browser says it is offline. v2.8377 put fifteen more routes on it.

The press ran `hardReloadFromRoot` at once. That unregisters the service worker and deletes every cache before it navigates. With no network the navigation has nothing to fetch. The look pressed it offline on `bills-sent`, on the live site, with the build's service worker in control: the page ended on Chromium's own error page, and the installed app was gone from that browser until the network came back. v2.8379 named this risk for a customer's page and built around it. The signed-in fallback still had it.

The press was also 86 by 36 at a phone's width.

## Now

The press asks first (`src/lib/routeReload.ts`). It is the ask a customer's **Try again** sends (`probeForSignal`, v2.8379): one `fetch` of `/favicon.svg?signal=<the time>`, `no-store`, with no cookie, given up after five seconds. No cache and no service worker can answer it, and only the site's own icon counts as an answer.

| The ask | What happens |
|---|---|
| out | *Checking for a signal…* under the press. The press is dead, in the theme's dead-press pair, so a second press sends no second ask. |
| answered | `hardReloadFromRoot`, as before. The press stays dead while the page leaves. |
| not answered | *No signal, so nothing was reloaded. Press Reload when the signal is back.* The press is live again. The caches and the service worker are untouched. |

It asks whatever the browser says about the network. A browser can say it is online with nothing behind it, and the harm is the same. Where the network is whole the ask costs one small request.

The same press stands on the fallback's other words, *Something went wrong loading this page.*, and asks there too.

The press is 44 tall (`ROUTE_RELOAD_PRESS_MIN_HEIGHT`).

## Seen

On 2026-11-24, on the worktree's dev server, signed in through dev-login, with a headless Chromium at 390 by 844 in light and 1,280 by 800 in dark. `messages` was opened with its code refused inside a minute of a recovery reload, so the fallback drew.

- **The ask refused.** The line showed under the press, the same document stayed and nothing navigated. A second press sent a second ask and reloaded nothing.
- **The ask held open.** *Checking for a signal…*, the press grey and dead. A press on it sent no ask.
- **The ask answered, the page's code let through.** The page left for `/?nocache=…` and came back on Messages.

The press was 86 by 44 at both widths, and nothing was wider than the screen.

## Seen on the live site

On 2026-11-24, on clicktooling.com, after the deploy that carried this finished (11:29 UTC). A headless Chromium at 1,280 by 800 and 390 by 844, signed in with the dev-login account, with the build's own service worker in control and 1,540 files in its caches. `bills-sent`'s file was taken out of this browser's precache, the browser was set offline, and the route was opened inside the app. This is the press that ended on the browser's error page before. Read only: every failure was forced in the browser, and nothing was pressed but **Reload**.

- **The page could not load.** *This page could not load — the app may have just been updated.* with **Reload**, 86 by 44 at both widths. The same document, nothing wider than the screen.
- **Reload pressed with no network.** One ask left (`/favicon.svg?signal=…`). *No signal, so nothing was reloaded. Press Reload when the signal is back.* showed under the press, and the press was live again. The same document stayed and nothing navigated. The service worker was still in control and its 1,539 files were still there. No browser error page.
- **Pressed again with no network.** One more ask, the same line, and everything still kept.
- **The signal back, Reload pressed.** One ask, then the page left for `/?nocache=…` and came back on `/bills-sent`, with one request for the route's file. Bills sent drew.

Both widths gave the same result.

## Not proved

- *Checking for a signal…* and its dead press on the live site. With the browser offline the ask fails at once, and the look, reading the page every tenth of a second, never caught it. It was seen on the dev server only, with the ask held open.
- A browser that says it is online with nothing behind it, on the live site. The look's browser said offline.
- Chromium only.

## What is left

- **The other callers of `hardReloadFromRoot` do not ask.** The automatic recovery reload goes on the browser's word that it is online (`startChunkRecoveryReload`). The update pill's reload and Settings → Fix app are a person's own ask on a page that works. None was seen to fail in the look.
- **Fix app** under the press is a link to `/fix-cache.html`. With no network it ends on the browser's error page, with the caches kept.
- Between the answer and the reload the signal can still drop, as on a customer's page.
