# v2.7410 — Tagged job notes: a notice on your phone (2026-11-15)

Punch list #363, PR 5 of six: the push. One new edge function and three client calls. No migration.

## What is new

- **`supabase/functions/notify-note-tag/`** (new): `index.ts` reads the rows and sends, `words.ts` writes the title, the body and the door of each push. The client sends an id and no words. The function refuses a caller who did not write the note, or who is not the tagged person for a No reply needed press, and pushes nothing for a note or a press more than fifteen minutes old. Its section is in `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`.
- **Three pushes**: to the people a note tags (*Marcus tagged you on Cedar Bluff Lofts*), to the people whose tags the note answered (*Dana answered on Cedar Bluff Lofts*), and to the asker of a tag cleared with no note (*Chris: no reply needed*). A person the answer tags back gets the tag alone.
- **The door**: `/jobs?tab=stages` for the office and `/dashboard` for everyone else, by the recipient's role.
- **`notifyNoteTag`** in `src/lib/jobs/noteTagsIo.ts`, called after a typed note posts on the Pipeline (`useJobThreadNotes.ts`), after an answer from the section and after **No reply needed**. Its failure is dropped.
- **`heldKind`** (`whatWaited.ts`) names the two templates, so While you were off reads *tagged job note* and *answer to your tag*.
- `config.toml`, the kernel copy's importer list and the dev-mcp catalog carry the new function.
