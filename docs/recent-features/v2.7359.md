## What changed

- `supabase/migrations/20261115233000_lien_closed_paid_to_activity.sql` (`docs/migrations/20261115233000_lien_closed_paid_to_activity.md`): a trigger on `job_lien_desk_items` writes one `lien_notice_closed_paid` line when a monthly notice is set aside with `fields.runClosedPaid` on it — who, the months, what the paper claimed, at the close's time, financial. One per notice, and a backfill for the closes made before the push.
- `src/lib/jobActivityEvent.ts` (+ test): the new type, tagged *Lien notice* in grey under Billing. It is ours, not something the customer got, so it stays out of `CUSTOMER_EVENT_TYPES` and the Activity box's sent lines. The test reads the migration and holds the type's word and the record's keys to it.
- Bed `lien-closed-paid-activity` (`supabase/tests/lien_closed_paid_activity/`, `scripts/pgtest-lien-closed-paid-activity.sh`, a line in `scripts/sql-beds.txt`): stand-in tables, the migration applied twice, nine checks.
