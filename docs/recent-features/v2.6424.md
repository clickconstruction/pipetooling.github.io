# v2.6424 — People → Licenses shows the expired licenses first (2026-11-02)

The second of the four findings the People → Paperwork design spike left (v2.6392 → *Left*). The Licenses tab's one strip, *Licenses expiring in the next 30 days*, kept only today ≤ expiry ≤ today + 30, so a license already past its expiry showed nowhere, and the tab said *Nothing expires in the next 30 days* while 14 of the 24 licenses on file had lapsed. Client only: no migration, no function change.

- **The kernel** `src/lib/people/licenseExpiry.ts`: `licenseExpiryBuckets(licenses, todayYmd)` sorts the rows into `expired` (the days since, the most recently lapsed first), `expiringSoon` (the days left, the soonest first; today reads 0) and `current`, in civil days through `ymdDaysBetween`, so a date the kernel cannot read goes to `current` and never to Expired. `expiredLicensesHeading` (*14 expired licenses*, *1 expired license*, bare at zero) and `lapsedWords` (*232 days ago*). 8 tests.
- **The tab** (`PeopleLicensesTab.tsx`) opens on an **Expired** section, red-tinted, above the amber strip: Person · License and # · Date of Expiry · Cost to Company · **Lapsed**, each person a press that opens their licenses in the roster below and scrolls the row into view (`pickPersonFromStrip`; the roster rows carry `data-license-person`). The strip keeps its columns, its days left now counted in civil days by the kernel instead of the local clock, and its person is the same press. Both sections draw from one `LicenseExpiryTable`. Empty, the Expired section reads *Nothing on file has expired.* (`LICENSES_EXPIRED_EMPTY`).
- **The sweep is not built.** `notify_dispatch_license_expiry_if_needed` is asked for only after a Save on this tab, so the March import's licenses never asked Dispatch for a notice before they lapsed. Punch list **#253** (`to-dos/license-expiry-notices-sweep.md`) proposes the sweep in two homes, a `pg_cron` function or the tab's load, and names the two calls the owner makes first: whose name a notice goes out under, and whether a lapsed license gets one at all.
- **Guide.** *find my way around the People page* → Paperwork names the Expired section.
- **Docs.** `PEOPLE_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md` → the licenses row and bullet; `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` → the Licenses row.
