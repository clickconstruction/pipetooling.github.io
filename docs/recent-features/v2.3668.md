# v2.3668 — Put a GC on notice: read each notice before you approve the run (2026-09-21)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Mock-up with the first pass, the critique and the final design: the Claude artifact *Read Before You Approve*. Stacked on [v2.3667](./v2.3667.md) (same file).

## Why

The owner, on Step 2: *"I would like to be able to preview the notices, and I think the way to do that is to click the items in the 'Windows still open' and 'Also named · window closed' columns and have a modal appear on top that when closed still lets the user see the screen below it."* It closes a real gap: *Approve all N and send the run* was pressed without anyone having seen a page — the paper first appeared inside the run, after approval.

One change to the ask, made in the mock-up: the run writes **one notice per job**, naming all of its months, so *Jul* and *Aug* on a job are the same document. Months as the only door would imply a document per month, and rows reading *None open* would have nothing to click. So the months open it as asked, **and** every row has *Preview ›*, the row itself is clickable, and the step has *Preview all N ›*. The first pass (a single-notice window) was rejected for making a six-notice review six open-and-close trips, for showing one page where the owner gets two, and for hiding that the GC's copy differs.

## What shipped

- **Kernel** — [`gcNoticePreview.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/gcNoticePreview.ts) (4 tests): `buildGcNoticePreview` builds the pages from the window's live state with the builders the desk and the run print from — `buildLienNoticeFieldsForJob` (the call `approveAll` makes), `runCoverNoteBlocks`, `buildLienNoticeBlocks` — so what is read is what is saved: `pages.owner` = the cover (the run's letter through `fillCoverLetter`, or `lienNoticeCoverNote` when Step 3 is unticked or empty — `approveAll` saves `coverNote: true`) then the form; `pages.original_contractor` = the form alone; each copy's reference strip names its recipient. `gcNoticePreviewableJobs` (no public owner, at least one month), `gcNoticePageLabel`, `stepGcNoticePreview` (stops at the ends).
- **[`GcNoticePreviewModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/GcNoticePreviewModal.tsx)** — a layer at z 95 over the window (z 90), ending above `--app-bottom-chrome`. Header: the job, *To <owner · mail to …>* (amber when Step 1 has not found one), **‹ n of N ›**, ×. A switch *Owner's copy · GC's copy*. The pages as paper (`data-theme="light"`, `filingDocHtml`, as the desk), labelled *Page 1 of 2 · cover letter*. A side list of the months the notice names — open with date and days left (red inside a week), closed muted, the clicked one ringed — the claim, the affidavit date, and *Edit it in Step 3 ›* (closes and jumps the step bar to the letter). Read-only by design: the letter is edited once for all in Step 3, per-notice wording on the Lien desk, which has the editable preview.
- **Keys and clicks** — a capture-phase `keydown` on `window`: Esc closes only the preview, ← → walk the run; both stop there, so nothing underneath sees them. The layer stops click propagation (it renders inside the window's backdrop, whose click closes the window); its own backdrop closes only itself. The window never unmounts, so its scroll, the letter being edited and the ticks are untouched.
- **[`GcOnNoticeModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/GcOnNoticeModal.tsx)** — Step 2: the open-window chips and the closed month names are buttons (the closed ones dotted-underlined, their tooltip keeps the date), *Preview ›* beside the job, the row's `onClick` ignores its own controls (`closest('button, input, a, [role="group"]')` — the kind switch keeps its clicks), *Preview all N ›* in the step header.
- Guide *send lien notices from the Lien desk* → Step 2 describes the preview.
