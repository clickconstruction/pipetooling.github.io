# v2.6872 — A bid asks once who the city lists on its permit (punch list #307, PR 6 and 7) (2026-11-10)

A permit lists one contact, and it is not always the builder: on real rows it was the bid's GC once in four. So a bid's site teaches the app whose names are whose, one answer at a time.

## What changed

- **Migration `20261110223054_builder_customer_answers.sql`** (`docs/migrations/20261110223054_builder_customer_answers.md`): the append-only table of answers.
- **`src/lib/research/nameAnswers.ts`** (new, pure, `nameAnswers.test.ts`): `foldNameAnswers` (the newest decisive answer per name stands; permits tied one at a time; a take-back; who is not sure; one GC under several customer rows read as one), `nameStanding` (ask, the GC's own name, tied, someone else, files for others, not sure, nobody listed), the question's words, `tiePreviewWords` (what a tie would do, before it does it), `answeredWords`, `tiedNames` and `listedOnWords`.
- **`bidSiteIo.ts`**: `loadNameAnswers`, `recordNameAnswer`, `loadBuilderKinds`, and `onNameAnswered`, which lets the two panels in a bid's window agree after an answer.
- **`BidNameQuestion`** (new), under the building permit the site's verdict speaks of: *The city lists Dana Whitlow on this permit. Do you know who that is?* **Works for the GC** reads the name's other permits and offers *Show all N under the GC* or *This permit only*. **Someone else…** asks which (the owner, architect or engineer, files for others, another GC) and writes the kind to `builders`. **Not sure** hides the question from that person alone, with *Answer it* to come back. A settled answer shows its chip, whose word it is, and **Undo**.
- **`BidGcTiedNames`** (new), the second half of the GC's panel: the GC's own name and each name tied to it, how it was tied, what the city lists it on as far back as the records go, the list behind *See them*, and **Take back**.
- `PermitChip` moved to its own file. Guide `see-what-the-city-has-on-a-bids-address`, `docs/PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`, `docs/ACCESS_CONTROL.md`.

## Not built as planned

- `builders.customer_id` is not dropped (see the migration's doc).
- Builders' *Our bids* column still counts by an equal name, not by the answers: that is `research_builders`, and it waits.
- The grades *likely* (one key begins the other) and the same phone or web domain are not built. The question is asked on a bid's own site only.
