# v2.7456 — Lien desk: the To do list follows the Board (2026-11-16)

Punch list #362, step 4 of the train: the mock-up's **A**, and the first half of **D**. The owner's pick of 2026-11-16 was *D, the list by bin first*.

## What changed

On a computer the list under the Board was grouped by the day and by who moves next (B, v2.7371): the papers that will not make their day, the papers waiting on the leader, the rest as *On track*, then *Coming up*. Twelve affidavits ready to file were twelve rows with the same *File the affidavit* press.

- **One group a bin**, under the bin's own name and in the Board's order, the notices' column first (`groupLienNextUpByBin` in `src/lib/jobs/lienNextUpByBin.ts`, over `lienBoardRowBin` in `lienBoard.ts`). A head says how many papers the bin holds and what their jobs still owe. One head over them, *Needs you now*, carries the count, the shared day and the total.
- **Inside a bin** the order is the day's own (`groupLienNextUp`): the papers the Board marks as not making their day first, then by day, the largest first.
- **Papers that share one act fold into one row** where the desk has a press that takes them all today (`lienBinFolds`): the affidavits ready to file under the print door's own words (*Print 10 for notarization ›*, v2.7398), and the affidavits to send under *Send N for approval ›*. Each press opens the Affidavits list at that pile (`{ open: 'affidavit_pile' }`, a new place a row's press can go), where the batch press of the same name stands with its options. The folded row's jobs wait behind *The N jobs*, each with where its notice stands, its day and what it owes. A fold needs two papers, and a press that would take two.
- **A bin whose papers all wait on the leader** names him on its head (*Awaiting Master approval · 2 notices · waiting on Malachi*) and carries the reminder press of v2.7371. With two such bins the first carries it; it reminds of every waiting paper, as before.
- **Later**: everything not due inside the week is one group, in the Board's order, with a line that says which bins hold how many. It is folded while something needs someone now and open when nothing does. A press, a find or a pressed bin opens it.
- **The leader's own rows stay on top** (*Only you can sign*, *Only you can approve*), **Reminders** keep their group, and a row with no bin (retainage, a served copy, a returned envelope) sits under *Other lien papers*.
- **A row under its bin's head does not say the bin again.** It says the one thing to know. It keeps the bin's name where that is all it has (the name opens the step card), and under Later, whose rows are of many bins. It does not repeat the day the head carries.
- **A phone keeps its list by the day** (`lienPhoneGroups`, punch list #352). Nothing there changed.

The list's rows, their words and their presses are `buildLienNextUp`'s, unchanged. A bin's name comes from the Board (`LIEN_BOARD_WORDS`), so a rename of a bin reaches the list by itself.
