# v2.5672 — Pricing: Undo sweep only undoes on the price card you swept (2026-10-20)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Found while building [v2.5671](v2.5671.md) (the price card switch window, Grace's report).

## The bug

The margin brush's one-level undo (`brushUndo` in [`useMarginBrush.ts`](../../src/hooks/useMarginBrush.ts)) held each swept row with the price it had before, and nothing tied it to the price it was painted on. `undoBrushSweep` writes through the Workbench's `writeUnitPriceOverrideRow`, which keys every write to the price open *now*. Clicking another price card neither put the brush down nor cleared the undo. So sweep on WENDI, open Alternate 1, press **↩ Undo sweep**: WENDI's earlier prices were written into Alternate 1's rows, and a row that was unpriced on WENDI had its typed Alternate 1 price cleared.

## What shipped

- **`brushUndoOnScreen`** ([`marginBrush.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/marginBrush.ts)): a sweep's undo carries the price it was painted on (`BrushUndo`), and the hook offers it only while that price is open. On another card the button hides and `undoBrushSweep` does nothing. Back on its own card it returns, still one level. The hook's `brushUndo` keeps its shape, so the tab did not change. Undo now checks for an undo before the guard, so a hidden Undo never toasts.
- The brush stays up across a switch: painting the card you just opened is fine, and v2.5671's guard holds a stroke while its prices load.
- **Help**: [price a bid by margin](../../src/content/help/price-a-bid-by-margin.md) says the undo belongs to the card you swept.
- **Tests**: `useMarginBrush.render.test.tsx` — a sweep on `pv1`, then `pv2` open: no undo, and pressing Undo writes nothing (failed on the old hook: the undo showed on `pv2` and wrote `pv1`'s old prices); back on `pv1` the undo returns and writes to `pv1`. `marginBrush.test.ts` holds the kernel.
