# v2.4556 — Submittals: one button starts the next draft, and you choose the rows (2026-10-05)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. What a draft holds and how it is written did not change; the two doors to it became one.

After v2.4555 renamed step 7's green button, the owner asked of the pair: "do both buttons do the same thing?" They did not, and neither name said how they differed. *Start a Rev 2 draft…* carried the rows sent back and the rows with no answer; *New revision* carried every row and put the approved rows in front of the GC again. The choice was offered before the screen explained it.

## What changed

- **One button on step 7**: *Start a Rev N draft…* (`data-testid="new-revision"`, the tour's `submittals-resubmit` anchor). *New revision* and the separate `resubmit-sent-back` button are gone. N is one past the newest revision, whichever revision is on screen.
- **With rows sent back and something approved, it opens a chooser** — `SubmittalResubmitChooser`, words from `resubmitChooser` in `reviewDecisions.ts`. Titled *Start a Rev 2 draft*, it asks *Which rows go on Rev 2?*:
  - **Only the rows that need it** (ticked): *4 sent back and 10 with no answer. The 9 rows approved stay on Rev 1 and on the procurement log.* Order-only rows are named when there are any.
  - **Every row**: *The 9 rows approved go on Rev 2 too. The GC answers every row and every part again. Use it when a product changed.*
  - It ends *Rev 2 starts as a draft. Nothing is sent. The GC sees Rev 2 only after you press Share.* The green button counts the ticked choice: *Start the draft with 14 rows* / *Start the draft with all 23 rows*.
  - Cancel, Esc and a click outside close it and write nothing; while the draft is being built they hold still.
- **With rows sent back and nothing approved, both drafts would be the same one** (the SpaceX bid on the day this was built: 4 sent back, 10 with no answer, none approved), so there is nothing to choose. The button asks once (`resubmitOneKind`): *Every row goes on Rev 2. 4 sent back and 10 with no answer. No row was approved on Rev 1. Rev 2 starts as a draft. Nothing is sent. …*, button *Start the draft with 14 rows*. `resubmitHasChoice` decides: a row approved, or a part approved on a row that goes on (`approvedPartsGoingOn`), since *Every row* asks those parts again.
- **With no rows sent back there is one kind of draft** too, so the button asks once with the plain question it had: now titled *Start a Rev 2 draft*, opening *Every row goes on Rev 2, built from today's picks.*, button *Start the draft with N rows* (was *Rev 2 from today's picks* / *Build Rev 2*).
- **The line beside the button**: *You choose the rows next. Nothing is sent. The GC sees Rev 2 only after you press Share.* (`resubmitCaption`), or *The draft starts with every row. …*. On an older revision it reads *Only the newest revision can be revised.* The held line is *You can start the next draft once the package is built.*
- The strip's Next action opens the same chooser.

## Inside

`newRevision(onlySentBack)` became three: `planNextRevision` reads the newest revision once (rows, parts, the split, the rebuilt preview, the carried rows) and returns `rowsFor('need' | 'every')` with both totals; `startNextDraft` opens the chooser or the plain question; `buildNextRevision(plan, rows)` writes from the plan the question counted from. `resubmitConfirm` and `resubmitWhat` are gone with the two-button wording.
