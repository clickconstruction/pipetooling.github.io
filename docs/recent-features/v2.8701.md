## What was there

Send a paper names a site for an outside company's paper (piece 3). Secrecy for a site was turned on from the development's page, or from a lone job's Documents tab. Someone sending a site's paper to a vendor was not told whether the site's secrecy was on, and had no press for it where they stood.

## What changed

- **The question.** In the email window of an outside company's new paper, a named site whose secrecy is off draws **Secrecy on this site** under the site's line and its print box. It reads *Secrecy is not on for this site. This paper is sent and kept with the site. Nothing is held, and our own people are not asked to sign.* Two presses follow: **Turn on secrecy for this site** and **Only send this one**.
- **Turn on secrecy for this site** opens piece 4b's panel in place, inside the window: the paper our own people sign, the jobs that shut and the people asked, counted before the last press. **Not yet** goes back to the question. Turning it on writes nothing of the paper being sent, and sends nothing.
- **Only send this one** takes the question away for that site. Naming another site asks again.
- **A site that is on** says so in two lines, since when and the paper, with no press. **Turn it off…** stays where the site lives.
- **When it is not drawn.** A paper that names no site. A copy sent again, which keeps its site as sent. Once **Send email** has written the paper's row. Someone outside the four office roles. A read that fails: the question is a help, and a send does not wait on it.

## How it is built

- `src/lib/addresses/siteSecrecy.ts`: `SecrecyMode` gains `offer`. `paperSecrecySite(site)` turns a paper's site into the block's. `secrecyDraws({ mode, on, failed, canTurn, passed })` is the one rule of whether the block draws, for all three modes. Two new sentences in `SITE_SECRECY_WORDS`: `offerLead` and `onlySend`.
- `src/components/jobs/SiteSecrecy.tsx`: in `offer` the off state drops its *Off* line, since the lead says it, and adds the second press. The on state draws its first two lines alone and reads no counts. A new site clears the panel, the pick and the answer to the question.
- `src/components/documents/AgreementsSendPaper.tsx` mounts the block in the email window's site slot, under `PaperSiteLine`.

## One change to the other two doors

A block whose site changes now reads again from nothing, where it used to hold the old site's state until the new read came back. On a development's page the site never changes. On a lone job's tab it changes when the job's address is edited.

## Design calls

Each is open to a flip.

- **The words say *this paper*, not *this NDA*.** The mock-up's line names an NDA. Send a paper sends any paper.
- **No *Off* value line in the question.** The lead's first sentence says it, and the window is short.
- **Only send this one leaves nothing behind**, not even a line that secrecy stays off. The site's line above still names the site.
- **No Turn it off… in the email window.** One door a site for turning it off, and a send is not the place to open a site's jobs.
- **A failed read says nothing.** The send does not depend on it.
- **An address is asked as an address.** A named address whose jobs sit in a development with secrecy on still reads as off here: the site of a paper is the address the office picked. This is the known gap of this door.
