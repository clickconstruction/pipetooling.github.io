# v2.2323 — Job address comma nudge: statement preview + one-tap fix (2026-08-26)

Owner-approved mockup: claude.ai/code/artifact/75791127-266e-4e4f-86c6-e63fdd69d4f9.
Companion to the portal job-grouping train (v2.2318–v2.2322): addresses saved
without a comma before the city render with the city stuck in the statement's
bold street line, and most arrive that way by paste.

- New kernel `src/lib/addressCommaNudge.ts` (+tests):
  - `buildAddressStatementPreview` — repackages `splitPortalAddress` (the
    portal's first-comma split) so the preview can never disagree with the
    real statement.
  - `suggestAddressComma` — strips a trailing state+zip (normalizing
    `Tx`/`Texas` → `TX`, keeping the zip exactly as typed), and when the
    comma-less remainder ENDS with a known city, returns the corrected
    address. Suffix-only matching (stricter than the display's
    anywhere-match) because this one rewrites the field. Vocabulary = the
    existing `TX_JOB_ADDRESS_LOCALITY_KEYWORDS` + the org's dev-added extras
    (`Settings → Jobs & dispatch → Job address city line breaks`) — one city
    list powers Pipeline line-breaks, lien/AIA prefill, and now this.
- `TX_JOB_ADDRESS_LOCALITY_KEYWORDS` gains **Round Rock** and **Liberty
  Hill** (real jobs at both weren't splitting) — Pipeline/Billing two-line
  display and lien prefills benefit too.
- New `JobFormAddressNudge.tsx` (+render smokes) slotted under the address
  input in `JobFormIdentityFields.tsx` (New Job + Edit Job together): quiet
  "On statements" preview line (✓ / amber "city is stuck in the street" /
  faint "no city found"), copper Add-comma chip with per-address "ignore".
  Never blocks submit; fires on typing and the paste button alike.
- Help guide `job-address-city-line-breaks.md` updated (the nudge shares the
  city list + Settings flow).
