# v2.8497 — The mode row opens the app, then shrinks into the gear (2026-11-24)

Punch list [#433](../../to-dos/mode-row-five-seconds/README.md). Client only: no migration and no edge function.

## The ask

The owner, 2026-11-24, after trying v2.8461 on a real phone:

> I tested it on a real phone and I decided that the little bar to pull down takes up too much space at the top when it is closed, but it does work, which is great. When a user clicks on dashboard or job mode, I would like the closing animation to show it minimizing to the gear window. That way a user knows they can always change it in the gear window. I would like to use this animation to be the first thing a user sees when they open the app, but then all future changes to be in the gear window, allowing for it to be hidden from the gear window.

Two pictures came with it: the grey handle circled under the phone's clock, and the gear menu's **Modes** group circled, which on a phone held Dispatch Mode alone.

## Before (v2.8461)

On the Dashboard the page's first line was a 24 px handle, for good. The row was drawn over it for five seconds on each visit and slid up behind the phone's bar. A tap on the handle or a pull down brought it back. A press on a third got five fresh seconds. A phone's gear menu listed neither Job Mode nor Task Mode, since the row was their one door there.

On an iPhone opened from its Home Screen the handle cost more than its 24 px. The header pays the status bar as `max(its own padding, the inset)`. Under a line that pays the inset the header pays its own padding as well, so the header sat lower by the handle and by that padding.

## Now

- **No handle and no pull.** `MODE_ROW_HANDLE`, the handle's words and rule, `modeRowPull` and `MODE_ROW_PULL_PX` are deleted. With the row gone `ModeRow` draws nothing at all, and the top of the Dashboard is the top of every other page.
- **The row takes no room.** Its host is `.modeRowHost`, a line of no height that is not an `AppTopBanner`. The header's own rule for the status bar is untouched whether the row is there or not, so nothing moves when it comes or goes. The clip starts at `env(safe-area-inset-top)`, under the phone's bar. `topBannerInset.test.ts` now counts one banner in `ModeRow.tsx`, Task Mode's.
- **An opening, not a visit.** The row shows on its first draw after the app loads (`opened` in `ModeRow`), and on a return to the app after a minute out of sight (`modeRowBackIsVisit`, unchanged). Another page and back, and a press on the Dashboard's own link, bring nothing: `modeRowSamePlaceVisit` and the `search` and `locationKey` props are deleted. An app that loads on another page shows the row on its first Dashboard, once.
- **A press closes it.** In the kernel `press` now leaves from `showing` and from `held`. A press on the lit third writes nothing and closes the row: the choice was to stay. From `gone` a press leaves too. That is a press on the row Task Mode holds, which is a line of the page: the mode switches, and the row is drawn over the Dashboard for its way out alone.
- **The way out is into the gear.** As the row leaves, `ModeRow` reads the gear's box (the press marked `data-mode-row-home`, `MODE_ROW_HOME_ATTR`) and its own, and `modeRowFlight` answers how far the row's middle travels and how small it ends. The row ends the gear's own size, on the gear, clear. `MODE_ROW_OUT_MS` is 380 ms, from 240. The gear then swells once inside a blue ring for the dead moment (the Web Animations API, on the kernel's numbers). With no gear on the screen, as under the role dock, the row slides up as before.
- **Less motion:** the row fades in place and the gear's answer is the ring alone.
- **The gear menu holds the three at every width.** `gearModesRows` no longer reads whether the row is drawn. On a phone the Modes group is the list with one dot, then Dispatch Mode. The phone dock's More sheet has its Job Mode and Task Mode switches again.
- **The switch.** **Modes at open** in the gear menu's Show group and in the More sheet, for the roles the row is drawn for, at a phone's width. Ticked until someone unticks it. Kept on the phone as `pt_mode_row_at_open_off`, with no user id, and only while unticked (`readModeRowAtOpen`, `useModeRowAtOpen`). Unticked, `modeRowDraw` answers `none` on the Dashboard. Ticking it from the Dashboard shows the row there and then. Task Mode's row does not read it.
- **Show full dashboard** stands under the Job Mode card at every width. A phone hid it while the row was always a tap away.
- **Task Mode** is as it was: the row is a line of the page, with no count.

## Read two ways

*Allowing for it to be hidden from the gear window* reads as a switch that hides the row, and also as the row being put away into the gear. Both are built: the row goes into the gear, and the switch stops it showing. If the switch was not meant, it is one row of the menu and one line of `modeRowDraw` to take out.

The minute away still counts as an opening. A phone rarely loads the app afresh, so without it a person who leaves the app open would seldom see the row. `MODE_ROW_AWAY_MS` is the line to change.

## Usage rows

`ui_nav_clicks`: `mode_row_sent` with `#lit` for a row closed by a press on its lit third, beside `#swipe`. `mode_row` is a press that switches, as before. `mode_row_shown` is no longer written.

`modeRowCount.test.ts` (20): a press from every state, the press on Task Mode's row, the 680 ms way out, and `modeRowFlight` with a gear, a larger gear and none. `modeRow.test.ts` (18): the switch's storage over a plain map and a broken one, the Dashboard with it unticked for the eight roles, and Task Mode's row not reading it. `ModeRow.render.test.tsx` (26), with fake timers: the host that is no banner, nothing left after the row, no second show on another page and back or on a pull, the flight's four numbers and the gear's one answer, the slide up with no gear, the switch, and each press. `gearMenuSections.test.ts` and `Layout.gearSwitches.render.test.tsx`: the list of three on a phone, the switch ticked, unticked, kept across a second opening, and absent at a desk.
