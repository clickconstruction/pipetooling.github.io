# v2.6423 — Combining two jobs carries the source job's history, materials, messages and plans to the target (punch list #252) (2026-11-02)

Found by the session that built #237 (v2.6350) and left as out of scope: `migrate_job_ledger_costs_and_delete` moves a named list of tables and then deletes the source row, and `ON DELETE CASCADE` takes everything else that names the job. On 2026-11-02 that was 52 tables on prod's catalog, read here from the migrations' `REFERENCES jobs_ledger … ON DELETE CASCADE` lines since the dev seat answered revoked; 31 of them held rows, `job_activity_events` about 13,700. Combines are in real use (six import shells into one job on 2026-11-01), and the guide said everything real moves. The sorted list, every table with its rule and reason, is on the card `to-dos/combine-rows.md` (#252).

## What changed

- **Migration `20261103054522_combine_moves_every_row.sql`** (`docs/migrations/20261103054522_combine_moves_every_row.md`): `migrate_job_ledger_costs_and_delete` restated from 20261102143000's body with the moves and merges after the team members. **Move** (a plain change of job): `job_activity_events` (each row's `detail` gains `combined_from_job_id`, as a moved status event keeps `source_job_id`), `job_pct_events`, `job_followup_reviews`, `job_test_reports`, `job_share_links`, `job_photo_notes`, `job_hazmat_incidents`, `hours_statements`, `material_po_generator_entries`, `nudge_sent`, `drive_links`, `email_tag_threads`, `supply_house_job_accounts`, `supply_house_invoice_move_requests`, `step_commitments`, `plan_sets.job_id` (the as-built's job, `SET NULL` before). **Merge** (the table's own unique key asks for a rule): `job_places` by name (the target's takes the source's containers and moves); `job_containers` by live label (the Unsorted row folds into the target's with its movements, moves and photos; a cage or box keeps its row and its printed code and takes `Cage A (2)`); `contact_jobs` (once per contact, the star kept); `contact_step_skips`, `nudge_holds` (the later `until_on`) and `call_tasks` (an open one stands once); `photo_saves` (the target's tick stands); `job_watchers` (added with their picks, as the team is); `job_supply_house_accounts` (an open account on the source opens the target's row); `job_supply_house_words` (the later word stands, keeping its stamp); `team_board_acks` (one per cell, `target_key` rewritten); `job_expected_hours` (the target's open number stands, the source's comes over closed and stamped); `plan_parts` (a target with no part takes the source's and names it in `plan_part_id`; a target with its own keeps it and the source's sets join it); `job_baselines`, `job_budgets`, `job_property_owners` (the target's stands, else the source's moves). **Let go** on purpose: `jobs_ledger_thread_note_stats_cache` (the notes' move recomputes the target's row by trigger), `job_travel_times` (a routed drive-time cache between two jobs), `paid_job_email_queue` (a mail log about the card that is going), `common_jobs` (deleted by the RPC since the start) and `job_collect_payment_flows` (under the billing guard).
- **One refusal before anything moves**: `step_commitments_job_person_live_uniq` allows one live job-anchored work order per sub on a job, and neither order should be cancelled by a rule, so a sub both jobs hold a live order for answers `work_order_collision` with *Both jobs hold a live work order for <sub>. Cancel one of them, then combine.*, in the house's do-what-is-ready shape.
- **`jobs_ledger_fixture_carries_checklist`** restated with `job_stage_windows`: the split (`split_job_ledger_fixtures_to_new_job`) moved a line and its window kept the old job, where no read found it; now the window follows through either door, and a repair rejoins any window an earlier split stranded. The `step_commitments.stage_window_id` pointer from a work order left on the old job is noted on the card.
- **`job_supply_house_words_stamp`** restated: a write that changes a word's job and nothing the house said keeps `noted_by`, `noted_by_name` and `noted_at`; before, every write re-stamped the word as the writer's.
- **Not changed, by the hand-off's rule**: the money and legal rows wait on the owner's word, shown as a sorted list with a proposed rule each on the card: `jobs_ledger_invoices` and `jobs_ledger_payments` (let go under the billing guard, by design), `jobs_ledger_payment_events`, `job_payment_promises`, `job_promised_pay_dates`, `job_payment_chase_touches`, `job_lien_filings`, `job_lien_releases`, `job_lien_desk_items`, `job_lien_claim_corrections`, `legal_matter_jobs`, `job_contracts`, `job_pay_applications`, `job_demand_letters`. A second migration restates the body with them once decided.
- **Guide** *combine two duplicate jobs into one*: what moves, how a row the target already has is merged, the work-order hold, and *What stays behind*.
- **Bed** `supabase/tests/combine_rows/20_scenario.sql` (`scripts/pgtest-combine-rows.sh`, `combine-rows` in `scripts/sql-beds.txt`): two combines and a split on made-up jobs. The first combine moves a plan part, a baseline, a budget, a property owner and a photo-save tick onto a target with none; the second meets a target that has each of them and every merge rule above, after a refused attempt on a work-order collision that proves nothing moved. It writes down each thing lost and raises at the end with the whole list, so one CI run says everything. Beds run only in CI; read the `ok:` lines in the job log.
