# v2.4987 — GC mode: each project has a Schedule window (2026-10-08)

PR 7b of the schedule's real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/SCHEDULE_REAL_BUILD.md`; its plan `to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/schedule-pr7.md`, approved at its six picks and cut in three, both on branch `spike/gc-mode`): the window that draws 7a's chart on real data. A dev's only, as the `gc_schedule_*` tables are until the schedule's PR 10. No migration and no edge function.

## The change

- **The window** ([`GcScheduleWindow.tsx`](../../src/components/gc/GcScheduleWindow.tsx)): a dev's **Schedule** on each project's card on `/gc` opens it at `schedule=<projectId>`, the way the plans, the questions and the change orders open theirs (call 1). It reads the board's job; the page's board is never read again.
- **Its body** ([`GcSchedule.tsx`](../../src/components/gc/GcSchedule.tsx)), a component of its own so a project page can mount it unchanged:
  - `loadSchedule` (6a) when it opens, the reads 6a counts (23 for one job), and again after a draw;
  - on a job being built, the measures (`Measures` with the finish against weather and crews, `LookAhead`); the late finish's money, the best offer and **Ask for the days** stay off (call 4: change orders are the money team's, and the schedule reads them with its PR 16);
  - while buying out, *Drawing the schedule* in read-only words, with the first draft against the weeks we bid and the template it came from;
  - the chart (`GcGantt`) with its holds, waits, change-order tails, lost days, a trade's own late day, the daily log's notes, uninsured trades, people on site, too many in one place, Print or PDF and Export, and the one company it shows (G-13), held for the call list (7c). Nothing drags, links or moves (PR 8);
  - a read that fails says so, with **Try again**.
- **Draw a first draft** (call 2): `draftSchedule` on the board's job from the day the work starts (the job's start day, the rough's, or the Monday after next), sent through 6b's `drawSchedule` with no version and the prototype's log line as its words. No template until PR 12. Closed while we bid and on a lost job, in `gc_schedule_draft`'s own words. A draw someone else beat says so and reads the schedule again.
- **The opened bar** (call 3, [`GcScheduleBar.tsx`](../../src/components/gc/GcScheduleBar.tsx)): a read-only card under the chart for the bar pressed. Its days, how far along, its spare days and the day it can finish by are the hover card's words; then what holds it, its real days, what it waits on and what waits on it (one bar a row, each line named with its trade, never in parentheses), its parts and its place. The editor comes with PR 8.
- **The kernel** ([`scheduleWindow.ts`](../../src/lib/gc/schedule/scheduleWindow.ts)), main's own, not a lift: `draftRefusal`, `draftStart`, `draftWords` and `barCardRows`.
