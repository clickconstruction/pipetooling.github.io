# v2.4495 — Job window: bills and test reports on the Documents tab (2026-10-04)

The owner, 2026-10-04: *"the Documents tab should also hold the job's contract, test reports, lien paper and bills."* This is the first half — bills and test reports; the contract and lien paper follow. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## The change

- **Bills** — [`JobDocumentsBills.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobDocumentsBills.tsx) over `jobDocumentBillRows(job.invoices, dayOf)` ([`jobDocumentsTab.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobDocumentsTab.ts)): the bills that went out (`billed` and `paid`, never a draft) in their own order, each *Bill N* · how it was billed (`billingTypeLabel`) · amount · *Paid* / *Sent 10/02/2026* / *Billed …*, a link to the customer's hosted page when the bill has one, and **PDF**. The name mounts `BilledBillViewModal` at z-index 1030 (the job window is 1010) and holds the job window's Escape while it is open; **PDF** calls `openBilledInvoicePdfInNewTab` inside the click. No new read: the invoices are on the job the window loaded.
- **Test reports** — [`JobDocumentsTestReports.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobDocumentsTestReports.tsx) over `loadJobTestReportRows(job.id)`: the rows the Documents page draws (`testReportDocumentRow`: title, *tested Sep 10*, the verdict and status chips). A sent report with a stored PDF opens it through a five-minute signed link; anything else opens the Test report window (`useTestReportModalOptional`), which reloads the list after a change. **Test report** in the heading starts one.
- [`testReportDocumentOpen.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/testReportDocumentOpen.ts) (new) is the one home of the bucket name, the row select, `loadJobTestReportRows` and `testReportPdfSignedUrl`; `Documents.tsx` now opens a report's PDF through it.
- [`jobDocumentsStyles.ts`](../../src/components/jobs/jobDocumentsStyles.ts): the table styles the tab's sections share.
- `JobWindowDocumentsTab`'s `onAiaOpenChange` is `onOverlayOpenChange`: any window a section mounts reports through it.
