# v2.3781 — The lien timeline's tail: the year to sue is watched, the Lien window shows the path, the Legal desk says when a lien dies (2026-09-23)

Punch list **#37**, PR 3 of 3 — the to-do folder (`to-dos/lien-timeline/`, with the three drawings) retires with this PR; v2.3761 and v2.3768 carry the first two parts. No migration, no edge function, no stored rows.

## Why

The strip (v2.3761) and the book (v2.3768) showed the § 53.158 suit date but nothing watched it; a filed lien left the desk after 30 days and the year ran unwatched. The per-job Lien window still showed the pre-timeline *Notice by · File by* pair from `last_work_date` alone — the one lien surface the creation fallback (v2.3747) never reached, so a job with no hours opened a window with no dates. The Legal desk's lien clock, the table counsel reads, stopped at *affidavit filed*.

## What shipped

- **`lienDeadlines.ts`** — `suitDeadlineFor` (one year after the last day the affidavit could file, weekend-rolled) and `LIEN_SUIT_COUNSEL_LEAD_DAYS = 90` move here from `lienTimeline.ts` (which re-exports them). `assessLienWatch` gains **`suitDue`**: a job over the $500 floor with a filed affidavit, no release of record, whose suit date is inside the lead — or past it; a run-out lien stays until released or paid (owner call 3 in the drawing, taken as *stays*).
- **`dashboardNeedsYou.ts`** — the `lien-suit-year` card (tier 20, between the serve-copy and the window cards): *A filed lien's year to sue ends <date>* / *… has run out* / *N filed liens' years to sue end soon (first: …)*, the dollars as the figure, the counsel-by date in the detail, door **Open the Timeline** → `/jobs?tab=stages&liendesk=1&kind=timeline` (the desk's URL door now accepts `kind=timeline`).
- **`buildLienTimelineFromWindow`** (`lienTimelineDesk.ts`) — the Lien window's builder: the forecast's work months (`useForecastWorkMonths` for the one job), sent months from the notice filings' `months_covered`, the affidavit and release filings, the creation month when there are no sessions. A closed month reads *window closed* and nothing about noting — the window cannot see the desk's record (`LienTimelineMonth.noteUnknown`).
- **`LienInstrumentsModal`** — the header's two bold dates become the strip in a box under the subtitle (`data-lien-window-timeline`); `computeJobLienClock` keeps serving the demand letter's fields.
- **`legalPacket.ts`** — `LegalLienClockLine` gains `suitDeadline`, `suitLeft`, `served`, `released`; **`legalLienClockWords`** is the one wording for the desk's pill, the firm's matter view and the packet print — *filed · served · suit in 200d*, *… · counsel* inside 90 days, *… · year to sue ran out*, *filed · released*. All three tables gain a **Suit by** column.

- A Timeline row (v2.3768) for a job the Pipeline board has not loaded used to answer with a toast (*open that job from the Pipeline board…*); the desk's Lien window door now fetches the job (`fetchJobWithDetailsById`) and opens it.
