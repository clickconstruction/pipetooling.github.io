# v2.6010 — Retainage held: the kernel (2026-10-28)

Punch list #165, PR 1 of 4 (`to-dos/retainage-receivable/README.md`; the owner's picks of 2026-10-26: source C, show A, release C). On our own trade jobs the GC holds back a share of every pay application. The app knew the number twice — the saved AIA application's `job_pay_applications.retainage_held` and the lien details' typed `jobs_ledger.lien_retainage_held` — and showed it nowhere as owed to us. This PR is the one read the next three PRs share. Nothing on a screen changes.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/ar/retainageHeld.ts`** (new, pure): `retainageHeldLine(input, todayYmd)` turns one job's facts into its Retainage held line, or null when there is nothing to show.
  - **The one rule (source C)**: with live pay applications, the held figure is the latest application's retainage to date (the highest number wins, whatever order the rows come in); without, it is the typed lien-details figure. An application beats the typed figure even when they disagree; applications that never held retainage make no line, whatever was typed.
  - **Less what was released (release C)**: a latest application at zero retainage after one that held is the final application, releasing what the one before it held; its tied bill (`invoice_id`, read from the job's bills) carries the release. On a job without applications, the release bill is the one the caller names (`releaseBillId`, PR 3's mark). The release's state is `none` · `unbilled` (final application saved, no bill yet) · `drafted` (ready to bill) · `billed` · `paid`. `released` counts only once the GC has been asked (billed or paid), so nothing is billed early; `owed = held − released` is the line's figure; `cleared` is a paid release, which AR drops the way any paid bill leaves. A release bill for less than the held figure leaves the rest owed; one for more releases only what was held; a typed figure cleared after the release bill was drafted still reads the bill's amount.
  - **The age (show A)**: `ageDays` from `finishedOn` (`jobs_ledger.lien_contract_ended_on`, the Lien desk's own clock, typed by the office and never inferred); null while the job is open; a finish ahead of today reads 0.
  - `retainageSourceFigure(applications, typedHeld)` is the number for the screens that show the figure rather than the line (the lien details, PR 4): the latest application's figure where one exists, even zero, else the typed figure.
  - `buildRetainageHeldSection(inputs, todayYmd)` is AR's section (PR 2): cleared lines dropped, the oldest finish first, open jobs last, the larger figure first among equals; `total` is Σ owed; `releasedCount` the lines whose release is out and awaiting payment.
  - Words: `retainageSourceWords` ("from pay application 3, saved Oct 18" / "typed in the job's lien details"), `retainageAgeWords` ("job still open · not aging yet" / "finished today" / "26d since the job finished · Sep 30"), `retainageReleaseWords` (one line per release state, '' for none).
- **`retainageHeld.test.ts`** (23 tests): a job with applications (the latest wins, the typed figure ignored, a step down reads as the smaller figure, cents), a job without (the typed figure, the age, finished today, a future finish, nothing typed), a job whose retainage was released (the final application with no bill, a drafted bill, a billed bill, a paid bill, a tied bill the job no longer lists; the no-application release bill drafted → billed → paid, for less and for more than held, the typed figure cleared afterwards, a release id the job does not list), the source figure, and the section's filtering, sum and order. Three mutants each fail a test: a drafted bill counting as a release, the typed figure winning over an application, the age taken from today's side.
- **`docs/BILLING_FLOWS.md`**: one sentence after the AIA window's paragraph names the read.
