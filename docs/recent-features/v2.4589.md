## What changed

- **Twenty-two guides rewritten** by the rules in [`src/lib/plainWords.ts`](../../src/lib/plainWords.ts). Frontmatter, headings, every `:::example` panel, every mock-UI token and link, and every number are unchanged, checked mechanically against the previous text; each guide's first paragraph is now two plain sentences, the share card's line (14,760 → 15,987 words of prose; no guide got shorter). Screen text printed with a dash, a dot or parentheses is quoted in italics, and a bold label that holds one became bold-italic, the rules' exemption. Two long lists became bullets: the Bridge's data gaps and the Punch list's five groups. One formula lost its parentheses: the Bridge's red-day rate now reads as two steps, a job's contract ÷ its expected hours, and expected hours = hours to date ÷ % complete.
- **Plain words beside trade words**, each the first time it appears; among them: change order, toast, GC, takeoff, overhead, sub sheet, accrual, office parts (checked against `loadBridgeData`: office parts are materials charged to the office job, drained per day at the 90-day rate), RFI, riser, addendum, exclusion, packet, schedule of values, hosted pay page, portal, test mode, AR aging, submittal, lead time, statute, owner of record, homestead, legal description, release, KPI, internal transfer, stepper, voided, prospect, wizard.
- **Their 22 rows leave the legacy list** ([`plainWordsLegacy.ts`](../../src/lib/plainWordsLegacy.ts)) as one contiguous run; the other two slices (rows 1–23 and 24–45) ship in their own PRs.
