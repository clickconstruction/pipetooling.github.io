## What it adds

`src/lib/paintedMap/preciseLocation.ts`, pure: thresholds and words, with no browser and no clock.

- **The states** (`preciseStateOf`). No reading is `finding`. Up to 20 m is `on`, up to 1,000 m is `weak`, and past 1,000 m is `rough`. The two edges are `paintedTrack.ts`'s own (`isPoorFix`, `isRoughFix`), so a reading of exactly 20 m is sure and one of exactly 1,000 m is only weak.
- **The wait** (`PRECISE_ROUGH_HOLD_MS`, 4 seconds). A rough reading reads as `finding` until the caller hands in `roughHeld`. A phone with the switch on can open with one reading off a cell tower, so a screen that follows the phone waits before it says anything.
- **The proof** (`provesPrecise`, `PRECISE_PROVEN_METERS`, 100 m). The line calls the switch *on* only at or under 100 m, a margin no rough area gives. From 20 m to 100 m it says *Precise Location is on, but the signal is weak here.* From 100 m to 1,000 m it says only *The signal is weak here.*
- **Never *is off*.** A rough reading says *Precise Location looks off.* A page is not told whether the switch is on, and a phone that hears only a cell tower reads the same. A test walks every kind of phone and every band of margin for the words *is off*.
- **The margin in words** (`marginWords`). Feet up to half a mile, worded by `taskPlaceAccuracyWords`, then whole miles. Rounded up either way. A test reads the words back into metres across 0 to 30 km and holds them at or over the phone's margin.
- **The rough area** (`areaAcrossWords`, `roughCircleWords`): *You are somewhere in this circle, 4 miles across.* Null for a reading that is not rough.
- **The phone** (`phoneKindOf`), from the browser's name for itself: Safari on an iPhone, an app saved to the Home Screen, Chrome on an iPhone (`CriOS`), the other iPhone browsers by name, an iPad that calls itself a Mac (told by its touch screen), Android, and `other` for a desk or a phone the page cannot tell.
- **The steps** (`preciseSteps`, `preciseSwitchName`), for that phone alone. `other` gets one line for each kind of phone and no switch named in the line.
- **The two numbers side by side** (`roughCompareWords`): *Your phone now · within 2 miles* and *With Precise Location on · within 20 ft*, for a rough reading on a phone.
- **The shared map's words** (`wayWeakWords`, `wayRoughWords`), for a stranger. The switch is named only when the reading is rough.

## Three things the prototype did not have

The prototype's kernel is on `claude/precise-location-proto`. This one differs in three places, each a reading of the owner's picks and not a new call.

1. **A page inside another app's own window is another browser.** On an iPhone such a page does not call itself Safari, and its row in Location Services is that app's. The prototype sent it to *Safari Websites*. It now gets *Tap the browser you are using.*
2. **A desk is not called a phone.** On `other` a weak reading says *This device is only sure within 120 ft*, and the shared map says *This device only knows the area it is in.* The card gave a desk's sure and rough lines and was silent on these two.
3. **Nothing is set beside a reading that is not rough.** `roughCompareWords` and `areaAcrossWords` answer null for one, so no screen can put the two numbers beside a weak signal, which is not the switch's doing.
