# v2.8302 — Customers: a Permits tab on the customer's page (2026-11-22)

One of the three things punch list #307's plan held when the card closed: *a Permits tab on the customer's page*. The owner's word on 2026-11-22: build it now. No migration, no edge function.

## What was there

The customer's page had four tabs: Profile, Estimates, Jobs and Invoices. For the people who research permits, Profile carried a **Names on permits** card (v2.6886): the names a city lists that are tied to the customer, with **Take back** and **Find their names on permits**. The other half of what the app knows about a GC and permits, *the jobs you priced for them and what the city shows on each address*, was only in a bid's window, under the GC's card.

## What changed

- **A fifth tab, Permits**, for the office and estimators, at `?tab=permits`. It draws the bid window's own panel, **What we can see about** the customer: the cities read with the day each one's records start, how many bids it covers, the jobs priced for them with what the city has on each address, the towns the rest are in, then **Other permits under names tied to them** with **Take back** and **Find their names on permits**.
- **One GC under two customer rows of one name is read as one**, as the bid window reads it. The tab says so under the panel: *Read together with 1 other customer row of the same name.*
- **On a phone the first six jobs show**, with **See N more** for the rest. In a bid's window a phone still folds them all behind **See the jobs**, since the window has less room.
- **The tab strip is one row that slides.** Four tabs with their counts already filled a 393 px phone. A fifth would have been cut off. The row is the one Jobs, People and a development's page use: the open tab stays whole in view and a small arrow marks a side with more past it.
- **A role without the tab never lands on an empty page.** `?tab=permits` reads as Profile for anyone the strip does not draw the tab for.

## Where each thing went

Nothing the Names on permits card did is gone.

| Was on Profile, in the card | Is now |
| --- | --- |
| The customer's own name and each tied name, with whose word it is | Permits tab, under **Other permits under names tied to them** |
| How many permits the city lists each name on, and **See them** | The same rows on the Permits tab |
| **Take back** | The same rows on the Permits tab |
| **Find their names on permits** | Under those rows on the Permits tab |
| The card's title, **Names on permits** | A line of that name where the card stood, with **Open Permits →** |

The line on Profile reads nothing. The card read the answers, the people who gave them and each name's permits in every city on every open of a customer's page, whether or not anyone looked at it. Those reads now happen when the Permits tab is opened.

## How it is built

- `src/lib/customers/customerDetailTabs.ts`: which tabs a role has (`customerDetailTabs`), the tab a link names held to them (`parseCustomerTab`), the rows of one keyed name (`sameNameCustomerIds`, the rule `BidFormModal` applies inline) and the line that says so.
- `src/components/customers/CustomerPermitsTab.tsx`: reads our customers by name once (`loadCustomerNames`, the worklist's own read), then mounts `BidGcPermits` with no current bid. When that read fails, the tab shows this row's bids alone and names the reason. The door on Profile, `CustomerPermitsDoor`, is in the same file. `CustomerPermitNamesCard.tsx` is deleted.
- `src/components/bids/BidGcPermits.tsx` takes `onItsOwnPage`: a phone shows the first jobs as a desk does. The bid window passes nothing and is unchanged.
- `src/pages/CustomerDetail.tsx`: the strip is drawn through `ScrollableTabStrip`, each tab and the rest of the row carrying the rule under them, so the open tab's blue line sits on the grey one as before.

## Tests

- `customerDetailTabs.test.ts`: the tabs by role, a tab a role does not hold, the rows of one name (a longer name is not the same name, a name with nothing to key stands alone, an archived customer missing from the list still finds its rows), and the line's words.
- `CustomerPermitsTab.render.test.tsx`: the panel gets this row first and the others of its name, with no current bid and as its own page. The line shows only when more than one row was read. A failed read shows this row alone with its reason. The door keeps the card's name and opens the tab.

## Seen in passing, not changed

On Profile at 393 px the **Open jobs** rows run 8 px past the right edge when a job shows its price and what was paid. It was so before this change. The row never wraps.
