# v2.8665 — A site's papers show on its jobs and on each company's own list (2026-11-26)

Punch list #439, piece 3 of seven, its second client part (3b). Client only: no migration, no edge function. It reads the `site_` columns of `person_contract_documents` (v2.8642), which v2.8660 writes. It writes nothing.

## What was there

v2.8660 lets a paper sent to an outside company name a site. The site was on the paper's row and in the email window, and no list showed it. A person looking at a job could not tell which companies had signed for the place it is at.

## What changed

- **Papers for this site**, a section on a job's Documents tab, after *Papers before this job opens*. It lists the papers outside companies hold for the job's site: the company, then the paper and who signs, a state chip and its day, in the words a company's own page uses. The ones still to sign come first, the newest first. One line counts the signed: *1 of 3 papers signed.*
- **Which papers.** The ones that name the job's address by its cleaned key, a spelling the answers in **Addresses that look alike** make the same place, or the job's development. A paper a newer one replaced is left out.
- **Drawn only when there is one**, or when the read failed. Most jobs are at no site with papers.
- **Who reads it.** The office's four roles and the estimator, the roles a company's papers are listed for. Nothing is asked for anyone else, and row security answers what each reads.
- **A company's own list** (a supply house's **Papers**, a customer's **Agreements** card): a paper that names an address says *for the site at …* under its name.
- **Nothing is pressed.** The section reads. A paper is sent again or copied from its line on Agreements.

## How it is built

- `src/lib/addresses/sitePapers.ts` (pure): `rowIsForJobSite`, `siteKeysToRead` (forty keys at most, so a long chain of answers stays one request), `sitePaperLines` over `partyPaperLine`, and the count's sentence.
- `src/hooks/useSitePapers.ts`: the answers first, soft. Then two reads of `person_contract_documents`, by `site_address_key` and by `site_development_id`, outside parties only, five hundred rows at most each. A failure draws `OfflineRetryPanel`.
- `JobDocumentsSitePapers.tsx` draws the section. `JobWindowDocumentsTab.tsx` places it.
- `paperSite.ts`: `siteKeysOfAddress` and `paperSiteNote`. `partyPapers.ts`: a line carries `site`. `partyPapersIo.ts` reads the three site columns through a client cast once, since the generated types do not know them yet.

## Design calls

- **Read only.** *Send again*, *Copy link* and *Send the NDA to another company* are drawn on the mock-up. They come with the hold and the site's own page, where a send from this list has its site and its checks.
- **Outside companies only.** A teammate's own paper for a site is piece 4's gate and its own list.
- **No empty state.** A heading that says nothing waits on every job would be noise.
- **A development's site by its id, with no name read.** No paper names a development yet. The development's page, part 3c, reads the name.
