# v2.5828 — Quickfill Email station, PR 5 of 5: the trend in its Mark history window (2026-10-24)

Punch list #159, the owner's "build it all", and the last of its five PRs. The window showed the
same list retyped every morning; it now shows how the counted inboxes trend, read from what the count
keeps, so a day nobody looked is a row of its own.

- **The model** [`lib/quickfill/emailTrend.ts`](../../src/lib/quickfill/emailTrend.ts) ([tested](../../src/lib/quickfill/emailTrend.test.ts)):
  per counted label the viewer may see, a bar a day for four weeks from `email_box_days` (the count,
  red when the oldest was past the limit, marked when someone looked); two weeks day by day, newest
  first, the days nobody looked included: the day's first mark (*Marisol · 9:55 AM*), how many sat, the
  oldest, what came and cleared, and what sat past the limit at the end of that day by name, rebuilt
  from each thread's arrival, first sight and leaving (`email_box_threads`); how many of the fourteen
  days nobody looked at.
- **The reads** [`emailTrendIo.ts`](../../src/lib/quickfill/emailTrendIo.ts): the station's own reads,
  four weeks of `email_box_days` for the boxes the policies allow, two weeks of the station's marks
  with who made them.
- **The panel** [`EmailStationTrend.tsx`](../../src/components/quickfill/EmailStationTrend.tsx):
  *Last 4 weeks* (a row per label: the title, the bars, today's count and oldest; a key), *Day by day*
  (*Nobody looked on N of the last 14 days.*, then the table), nothing at all while the station is by
  hand. It sits in the station's Mark history window (`QuickfillSectionMarkHistoryModal.tsx`, the
  #156 redraw) above the marks, for `email-inbox` alone.
- **Smoke** `EmailStationTrend.render.test.tsx`: the bars per label (a missing label left out), the
  day by day with a day nobody looked and the names that sat, nothing while by hand.
- **Guide** *Quickfill* (the history window's Email trend). Docs: `QUICKFILL_ARCHITECTURE.md`,
  `GLOSSARY.md`, the to-do (built; left: the owner's admin step and switch, then a week of use).

## Deploy

Client only.

## Is this the best we can do?

- The rows come from what the count kept, not from the notes, so a day nobody looked still says what
  sat; the notes stay in the marks below for what a person wrote.
- The bars are the count's own numbers, one a day, the last count of the day winning.
