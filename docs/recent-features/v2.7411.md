# v2.7411 — Painted maps: a place to keep who opened a map's link (2026-11-15)

Punch list #345, the link that asks who is looking, step A. Taunya's ask of 2026-11-15: *share any individual map with a public URL where anyone without having to sign in can see it and use it so long as if they're not signed in they enter their name. So we can record the fingerprint of the device that they were on and the location … if they choose to show where they are on a map in an additional step.* She took all five calls at the card's pick the same day: *I like everything you've suggested.*

## What changed

- **A table, `painted_map_link_visits`, and nothing else.** One row a device a link: the name given, the device, when and how often, and where the reader was when they chose to show it. The column list and the checks are in `docs/migrations/20261116173000_painted_map_link_visits.md`.
- **Nothing on a screen changes.** Nothing writes the table until the `painted-map-link` function's next deploy, and the page that asks for a name follows that.

## The five calls, as Taunya took them

1. **Showing where I am shares it.** The press says so before the phone asks.
2. **The first point and the latest one** are kept per visit. Never a trail.
3. **The office and the map's painter read the record.** Not everyone who can open the map.
4. **Links already out ask for a name too.**
5. **The record lives as long as the map** and goes when the map is thrown away.

Calls 2, 3 and 5 are this table: two points and no more, the select policy, and the cascade from the map.

## What a web page cannot give

- **A device's fingerprint is an id the page puts there.** A page cannot read a serial number. The id stays until the browser's site data is cleared, and a private window reads as a new device. The browser's words, the screen, the language, the time zone and the request's address are kept beside it.
- **A name is not proven.** Anyone can type any name.

## Is this the best we can do

- **A shipped column ahead of its screens,** on purpose: a table ships alone first, is pushed and proved, and the function and the page come after.
- **The address is kept as the edge saw it,** not hashed. The office reading a visit wants to tell two visits from one network apart, and the row is the office's and the painter's alone.
- **No trail.** Two points say where somebody was when they started following the map and where they were last. A trail would be following a person.
