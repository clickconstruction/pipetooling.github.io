# v2.7070 — What customers see: the sample notice of claim agrees with its cover letter (2026-11-12)

Punch list #326, a correction to v2.7044. Found on the first export from the live site: the owner asked to see one of each printable, and the eight PDFs were made by clicktooling.com and read page by page.

## What was wrong

The Lien letters group draws every letter on one made-up job (`src/lib/journeys/lienLetterSamples.ts`), and its header says they share one month and one set of days. Seven papers did. The notice of claim form did not:

- Its reference line read `Unpaid: 2026-09`, a month worked out on its own (today less 60 days) and printed as digits. The cover letter in the same envelope, the attorney's letters and the affidavit all said August 2026.
- Its **Date** line was today. The affidavit's sentence 6 swears the notice was mailed 21 days ago, and the courtesy notice names that day too.

Both came with the form when it moved from the General contractor group, where it stood alone and nothing sat beside it to disagree with.

## What changed

`sampleOwnerNoticeInputs` now reads `sampleLienDays`: the form is dated `noticeSentOn`, and its reference line is the three items a real notice carries from the Lien desk: `Job #1077`, `Work months August 2026` (`describeNoticeMonths`) and the day. The project and the builder left that line; the form's own fields already name both. No builder changed and no letter's wording changed: only the sample's inputs.

The retainage notice keeps today's date. It sits beside the order and no other letter names its day.

## Checked

- `lienLetterSamples.test.ts`: a new test holds the form to the month and the mailing day the cover letter and the affidavit name, and refuses today's date and a month of its own. `paperSamples.test.ts` follows.
- The whole suite, and the eight PDFs built again from the corrected code and read. The first try kept the project and the builder on the reference line beside the month, and the PDF ran the line off the right edge of the sheet, so the line took the real notice's shape instead.

## Not changed, named

- The PDF draws a notice's reference line on one line and never wraps it (`refstrip` in `lienFilingDocuments.ts`). A real notice's three items fit. A real notice naming about six months or more may not. Not measured on a real job, and that builder also draws sworn papers, so it is left for its own change.

- The sample affidavit's first county line is blank and the release's reads Hays. That is the forms' own difference: the affidavit's venue is where it is notarized.
