# v2.6832 — The Bridge opens on one screen: three numbers and the week's call (punch list #303, PR 1) (2026-11-10)

PR 1 of the Bridge's refresh under one story (`to-dos/bridge-one-story/README.md`, the owner's pick of 2026-11-10: one decision a week). Client only. No migration, no edge function. The page was measured at 13.7 phone screens with the press that records the week's call five screens down, and `bridge_calls` held no row a month after the call shipped. This PR draws the first screen above the page as it is, so nothing moves yet.

## What changed

- **The kernel** (`src/lib/bridge/weekCall.ts`, `buildWeekCall`): the first screen as one model, built from readings the page already makes.
  - **Three tiles.** *Made last week* is field contribution on the last full pay week, with the eight weeks as small bars and ≈ when hours still wait; from Friday it leads on this week so far. *Cash low point, 8 weeks* is the forecast's lowest point, green above the floor, red with the day it goes under. *Slowest step* is `buildStageFlow`'s link with its weeks line.
  - **The call, proposed.** `proposedMoves` takes the link's presets from `LINK_DESK`, the ones with a number first, drops a keyed move whose number is zero, and makes each concrete with today's number, its door (`THIS_WEEK_DOORS`) and a person: whoever sent the most bills in the window for a bill move, whoever took the most payments for a chase, the caller for hours, prices and percents (`topPerson`). At most three (`WEEK_CALL_MOVES`), two on a phone. A stage pressed on the cards below is proposed in place of the page's reading, and the card says which the page reads.
  - **Once a call stands.** The same card shows each move against today (*done*, *part*, *not yet*, *yours*) and the link's weeks and pile when called against now. From the week's fifth day (`fridayAsks`) it asks *Did Bill move?* with what the numbers say. An earlier call nobody answered is asked for beside this week's proposal.
  - **Cash leads, built and off.** When the forecast goes under the floor and the page may trust its cash figure, the cash tile takes the row, the call is proposed on Capital with cash moves first (the late invoices, the supply bills due on or before the low point) and the slowest step as the move after them. `WEEK_CASH_MAY_LEAD` is `false` until the owner says the bank the page reads is the whole bank (#303, his call 2); the cash tile shows red either way. A cash call keeps no snapshot of the low point yet: `calls.ts` is copied to the edge functions, so its shape changes with the PR that turns this on.
  - **A budget.** `weekCallWords` lists every sentence a phone's first screen prints and `weekCallBudget` counts them. The test holds every state to 150 words and 24 numbers and every sentence to `plainWordsFailures`.
- **The component** (`src/components/bridge/BridgeWeek.tsx`): the tiles, the card, the last call or, at a desk until a first call exists, *How the week runs*. A move with a door is a link to it. Its person is a chip that opens a box on a press. *take off* strikes a move from the call and *put back* returns it. The record press is dead with its reason while no move is on the call, so an empty call never records. The doors *The flow*, *Money* and *People* and the soft-numbers count jump to the sections under the screen.
- **The page** (`Bridge.tsx`): `weekModel` beside the scorecard's reads; `recordCall` takes the link the first screen proposes; four anchors for the doors; a *Behind the first screen* rule over the page as it was.
- **A label changed from the drawing.** The frames said *Earned last week*. The number is contribution, earned less wages, so the tile says *Made last week* and the guide says what that is.
