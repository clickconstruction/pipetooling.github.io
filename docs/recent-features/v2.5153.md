# v2.5153 — GC mode: our own crew from its Pipeline job (2026-10-10)

U8b of the Building lane's real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/building-u8.md`, on branch `spike/gc-mode`, with amendments 1 and 2): the screens over U8a's column and functions (`supabase/migrations/20261010063000_gc_crew_job.sql`). No migration.

- **The head count** (`src/lib/gc/dailyLogRows.ts`, `dailyLogIo.ts`):
  - `withCrewClockIns` lays our crew's clock-ins into the logs right after `withDailyLogs`, the one function the log window and the schedule's readers share (the Schedule lane's call).
  - Clock-ins win only on a day with a log and a count. A day with no count keeps its typed one, and clock-ins make no log.
  - `crewsToSave` leaves the counted crew off a save, so the count is read and never stored.
  - `loadGcCrewOnSite` reads `gc_crew_on_site` in 92-day pages from the job's first day (`crewCountPages`).
- **The percent** (`src/lib/gc/crewJobRows.ts`, `crewJobIo.ts`):
  - `crewPercentOf` reads each stage line from the Pipeline job's stage of the same `stageRank`, two of one rank averaged by their weight, when every line matches and one stage was reported.
  - Otherwise it reads the crew report while it is the newest word, then `pct_complete`. With none, the trade keeps what it had, so a missing number never reads as a measured zero.
  - `withCrewPercents` lays it into `selfPerform` with its `source`, over the page's `board` itself, so the Schedule, Draws, Closeout and Bill the customer read one percent.
  - The read takes O9's gate (`canUseGcBuilding || canSeeGcMoney`).
- **Our own crew on Draws** (`src/components/gc/GcOwnCrew.tsx`, from the prototype's `GcBuildingCrewCard`):
  - its stages read only, each with the day it was reported, or the whole job's percent, with the words for where it came from;
  - for a dev, **Pick its Pipeline job**: suggested jobs first, then a search, **Use this job** and **Unlink it** (`gc_link_crew_job`);
  - one Pipeline job per crew trade: a job another crew trade holds comes last, *On Electrical already*, and cannot be picked (`crewJobsHeld`, `heldByOthers`). Building's door refuses it in SQL.
- **The daily log** (`GcDailyLog.tsx`): our crew's count reads **clocked in** with no box on a day with clock-ins. A note says where the count comes from.
- **Bill the customer** (`ownCrewSource` in `ownerBilling.ts`, co-signed by the Owner Billing lane): our crew's line says *from Pipeline job J 1071's stages*, *from the crew report of Oct 8* or *from Pipeline job J 1071's percent*. An unlinked trade keeps today's words.
