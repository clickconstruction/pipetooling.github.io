# v2.7833 — Lien desk: the Public jobs and Off the lien path boxes fold to one line (2026-11-18)

Punch list #390, part 1. On 2026-11-17 the owner picked option B of the three drawings of the Lien desk's To do page (#383): "Build B but with the action button in the same line as the title of the card it is in instead of at the bottom." PR #8628 built B except two parts, which the owner had saved as their own card: "Save these last two fixes as a punch list card for another session to build instead". Part 2, the records door, was replaced by a later ask and shipped as v2.7780. This is part 1, the last of B.

## What was wrong

The Board's box *Public jobs · bond claim* stands under **Served** in the lien affidavits' column, with *Off the lien path* under it when a job is off the path. Each was as tall as its rows. On the live desk on 2026-11-18 two public jobs made a box 191 px tall at 1,440 wide and 209 px at 1,280, between the chart and the find box, for jobs nobody works from the Board.

## What changed

- **Both boxes open folded to one line.** The line is the box's name, then *2 jobs · $9,400 unpaid*, then **Show ▾** at the right. The whole line is the press. Open, the box is the box as it read before, under **Hide ▴**.
- **The header counts the jobs in words on both states.** The header's bare count at the right gave its place to the press, so the count moved into the words. The line reads the same folded and open, and only the press's word changes.
- **What needs a person stays on the folded line.** A row whose bond nobody knows carries the chip *get the bond*, and a fold would hide it. The folded line counts those rows in the chip's amber, *1 to get the bond*. Open, the chips say it and the count is not drawn. This was the builder session's recommendation on the card, not the owner's ask.
- **The fold is never stored.** Each box holds its own `open` in component state, closed whenever the Board is drawn again.
- **A phone gets the same fold** inside **Show the bins**, with a press 44 px tall. Folding *Public jobs* also drops a half-made pick of which job to put back.

## How it is built

- `src/lib/jobs/lienBinFold.ts` (new, pure): `lienBinFoldLine(bin)` reads either bin and answers `{ words, ask }`, the rows' chips counted in the chip's own words; `lienBinFoldPress(open)` is the press's word and mark.
- The words are **not** in `lienPublicProperty.ts` beside `totalWords`, where the card put them. That kernel has a generated copy in the edge functions' `_shared/gcKernels`, so a change to it turns `check:edge-drift` red for every function that imports the copy, for words no function reads. `lienBinFold.ts` reads the bin the kernel builds and changes nothing there.
- `src/components/jobs/LienBinFoldHeader.tsx` (new): the header as one button inside the box's `h4`, `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls` on the open body, `data-lien-bin-fold="folded|open"`.
- `LienPublicBinView` and `LienOffPathBinView` draw that header and mount their rows only while open. `LienBoard.tsx` and `LienDesk.tsx` are untouched, since other Lien PRs had both open that day.
- `data-lien-public-bin-count` and `data-lien-off-path-bin-count` are gone with the bare count. A test or a walk that reads `[data-lien-public-bin-row]` or `[data-lien-off-path-bin-row]` presses the header first.
