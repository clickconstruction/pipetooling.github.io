# v2.6447 — Statement of charges, PR 2 of charges by crew: the window, the two papers and the Documents section (punch list #254) (2026-11-03)

PR 1 (v2.6441) set the standings and the rate card; PR 3 (v2.6445) the Job Summary's columns, the Charges window and the adjustments; #251's PR 2 (v2.6446) the charged paper for one person. This PR is the job-level paper the customer reads: a Statement of charges for a period, drawn from the same `buildJobCharges` read, saved with its snapshot and numbered per job.

## What changed

- **The kernel** (`src/lib/charges/statementOfCharges.ts`): `cutJobChargesToPeriod` keeps the sessions and dated lines inside the span and every undated line; `jobChargesSpan` finds the first and last day anything happened; `chargeStatementWords` says how the paper was priced (the rule with its rate or *the crew on each day, from the rate card*, the markup both ways, the imported days named, a line passed through); `buildChargeStatementHtml` draws the office copy (Cost, Markup, Charged on the material lines, the crew-days by week with who was out and the rate, an *Office only* box with the premium or shortfall and every adjustment) and the customer copy (charged amounts alone, *not an invoice*), the cents smaller than the dollars on every figure; `nextChargeStatementRecordId` mints `SC-<job number>-<n>`.
- **The table** (migration `20261103100000_job_charge_statements.sql`, `chargeStatementsIo.ts`): `job_charge_statements` keeps the snapshot and the figures beside it; a save reads the job's ids and takes the next, and a race on the unique key reads again.
- **The window** (`StatementOfChargesWindow.tsx`): the span defaulted to what the job holds, the job's rule shown and pointed at the Charges window to change, the markup line, the office-copy and imported-days switches, the counts of the span (crew-days, crew-hours from person-hours, material lines at cost), the notes a day the card cannot price leaves, the charged total with the premium's words on the office copy, Save and Print, and the paper beside in a frame; a saved statement opens frozen on its snapshot and prints again from it.
- **The section** (`StatementsOfChargesSection.tsx`, mounted after Hours statements in `JobWindowDocumentsTab.tsx`): every statement with its record id, state, period, labor, materials, charged, who saved it, Print and Open; the press *Statement of charges… · N people, M material lines*, dead with nothing; an empty section naming the press.
- **Sent copies**: Print on the customer copy files the page it printed (`fileSentCopy`, kind `statement_of_charges` in the statements group, the saved statement as its source, `statementOfChargesFiling`), so it lists under *Sent from this job*. The office copy and the section's Print (the office copy again from its snapshot) are not a send: `StatementsOfChargesSection.tsx` sits on `NOT_A_SEND` in `sentCopiesCoverage.test.ts` with its reason.
- **Tests**: the kernel's ids, cut, span, words and filing, the two prints; the window's read, switches, Save, Print (the customer copy filed, the office copy not), failure and frozen open; the section's rows, press, Print, Open and gate.

## Decisions

- **The job decides the rate, never the paper.** The window shows the rule and the markup and points at the Charges window; nothing on the paper is typed in.
- **A print counts as a send** (the owner's rule, `docs/SENT_COPIES.md`): the customer copy is kept; the office copy holds the premium and the shortfall, the office's own report, and is not.
- **A saved statement is a snapshot.** It prints as it read; today's rows make a new one, so the office never wonders which rate a customer saw.
- **The gate is the hours statement's** (`can_touch_hours_statement(false)`): a dev or the controller, as v2.6450 narrowed it the same day; the customer copy carries no pay, but the paper is the office's.
