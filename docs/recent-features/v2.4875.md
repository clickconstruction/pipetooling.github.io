## What shipped

- **`src/hooks/useScheduleDispatchNotComingIn.ts`** (new) — a verbatim move out of `ScheduleDispatchHubPage.tsx`. It holds the 4 atoms (`notComingInBusy`, the off-button's confirm target, the undo target and its busy flag) and `markNotComingInForPersonDay`. It also holds the off-button's confirm trio and the chip's undo trio, plus the NCNS body as `recordNcnsOnPersonDay(userId, workDate, details)`. Its inputs are the data seam's names map, person-day blocks, time-off cells, `loadHub` and `refreshHubUserTimeOff`, plus `canEdit`, `showToast` and the vestigial `jobId` / `load` (map quirk "`jobId = ''` vestigial dual-mode").
- **The page** keeps the two assign-picker entries. Each reads the picker's cell, closes the picker and calls the hook. The NCNS entry now closes the picker before the name and block ids are read. That still happens in one synchronous call over the same render's values, so nothing observable changed. `canRecordNcns` stays beside `canTimeOff`. 2,370 → 2,184 lines.

## Tests (+12)

- `useScheduleDispatchNotComingIn.render.test.tsx` covers four flows.
  - **Mark not coming in.** Time off first, then the day's blocks, the toast and the quiet reload. A refusal touches nothing else. A day already off still clears its blocks. The off confirm names the person and day, and cancel writes nothing. A running mark opens no second confirm.
  - **NCNS.** The details and block ids are sent, then the incident's toast and the reload. A refusal or a throw is one error toast.
  - **Undo.** The NCNS copy is sterner. Already cleared and salary sync are warnings. A refusal keeps the window open.
  - **Undo gates.** A viewer gets no window, and cancel is ignored while the undo runs.
