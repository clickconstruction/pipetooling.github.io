# v2.4839 — Bill tab: a bill marked paid with no payment behind it can be removed (2026-10-07)

One migration (`20261008013000_delete_marked_paid_invoice_without_payment.sql`, push after merge); no edge function. Grace, on job 258's Bill tab (Malachi, Taunya; RMC-Dudley Mason): *"This payment wasn't received and I can't remove it but the balance owed is correct at $9800."* The row read **Paid $8,900 · bills RMC-Dudley Mason · $8,900 marked paid · no payment on record**, under a paid $8,000 bill and an open $9,800 one; the sum line read paid $8,000 · open $9,800 = billed $17,800, the bid. The first bug of the Jobs Stages bugs session.

## What was wrong

- The row is a `jobs_ledger_invoices` row in status `paid` that was never sent (no `billed_at`, no `sent_to_customer_at`) and that no payment references. The Bill tab's words for it (v2.3478) and its sum line were right: it counted for nothing there.
- **It had no door off the job.** `delete_ready_to_bill_invoice` wants a draft; `delete_billed_invoice_on_send_back` wants `billed`; the row's ⋯ held See in Pipeline and the statement-sharing options only.
- **It still counted elsewhere.** `jobBilledContribution` (bill truth) sums every `billed`/`paid` invoice by its amount, so the customer's lifetime billed carried $8,900 never billed; `billSettled` (lien waivers, v2.4318) reads status `paid` as settled, so the row offered **Add the unconditional ›** — an unconditional waiver for money never received.

## What changed

- **`delete_marked_paid_invoice_without_payment(p_invoice_id)`** ([migration page](../migrations/20261008013000_delete_marked_paid_invoice_without_payment.md)): the send-back's roles and job gate; refuses a row not in status `paid`, one a payment references (*Unlink it under the bill first*), and one with a `stripe_invoice_id` (*Stripe holds a paid mark on this bill. Undo it under the bill first.*, the v2.4082 door); deletes the row.
- **The Bill tab** ([`JobFormInvoiceList`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormInvoiceList.tsx)): a paid row with nothing paid on it gets **Remove bill** (*marked paid, no payment*) at the foot of its ⋯, in the send-back's place; dead with the Stripe words when a Stripe invoice backs it. The confirm says what happens: *The $8,900 bill is marked paid, but no payment is recorded on it. Removing it changes no payment and no balance owed. Its amount goes back to unbilled. Remove it only if it was never a real bill.* After the delete the job is re-read and a thread note keeps the trace (*Removed a $8,900 bill that was marked paid with no payment on record. No payment or balance changed.*).
- **[`markedPaidInvoiceRemoval.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/markedPaidInvoiceRemoval.ts)** holds the item's state and the note's words.
- A paid row with a payment behind it is not a stub and gets no item; Send back stays off every paid row as before.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **Remove, not Send back.** A send-back returns the amount to unbilled and demotes the job when its last billed row goes; this row was never billed, the job's money already read without it, and a job whose only remaining rows are paid must not fall to Ready to Bill. The confirm still says the amount goes back to unbilled, because the job's *Still to bill* can grow when the stub covered real work.
- **The HCP stubs stay.** The handful of rows the HCP reconcile stamped paid (`external_send_channel = 'housecallpro'`) were real paid bills whose payments were never imported; the confirm's last sentence is the guard, and Show note on the row says where it came from.
- **Not done:** making `billSettled` and lifetime billed ignore a paid stamp with no payment on it. For the reconciled stubs those readings are right; for a phantom row the office now removes it. Revisit if a second phantom turns up.
