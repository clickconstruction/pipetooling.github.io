# v2.8526 — Bills sent: on a phone, the deposits line keeps its total beside the sentence (2026-11-24)

A small fault of v2.8493's line (punch list #422), seen on its own look at 375 px. No migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

The line under a picked day of By day paid, *2 deposits landed that are on no bill yet.*, was a wrapping row. On a phone the sentence and the total do not fit on one row, so the total fell to a row of its own under the sentence, with *Not added to the day.* and **Open deposits** on a third.

## What changed

`NoBillLine` (`BillsPaidByDay.tsx`) is two columns, as a payment's line is: the sentence, which wraps, and the total beside it. The words under them take the line's whole width, and so do the words for a bank that did not read.

## Looked at

On a local dev server over live data, read only, 2026-11-24. At 375 px the sentence wraps to two lines with the total at its right, the press is 44 px tall, and nothing scrolls sideways. At 1280 px the line is as it was: two rows, 52 px tall.

Not looked at on the live site, or on a real phone.
