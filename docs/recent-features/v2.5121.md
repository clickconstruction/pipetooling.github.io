# v2.5121 — GC mode: pay a trade's draws while we build, for a dev (2026-10-09)

The Building lane's U6b in the GC mode real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/building-u6.md`, on branch `spike/gc-mode`). It is the window over U6a's presses (v2.5084, migration `20261010021000_gc_trade_draws`) and their generated types. No migration and no edge function. It is dev only, behind the dev door, like the rest of Building.

- **`src/components/gc/GcDrawsWindow.tsx`** opens from **Draws** on a building job's card on `/gc` (`?draws=<project id>`, and `&charge=<id>` outlines and scrolls to a back-charge), after RFIs, behind `canUseGcBuilding` and `canSeeGcMoney`. Ported from the prototype's `GcDrawsTab` with its send-back form, pay days, changes and back-charges:
  - **To pay**: the approved draws and the day each is due, late first;
  - a card per trade with a signed statement of work: the contract, billed, paid, retainage held and left to bill, each line's reported and billed bar, and each draw with **Approve**, **Approve less** and **Send back** while it waits (`GcDrawForms.tsx`), **Mark paid** once approved, **Their unconditional waiver came in** once paid, and its pay application to read (`GcDrawPayApp.tsx`, with `tradePayAppParties` for the form's Excel and PDF). Approve waits on the company's papers (`partnerBlockers`), which read as missing until B6-b-ii's papers mapper is on main;
  - **A pay application came by email**: each line's percent from their newest report, stored materials, the period, who signed and their title, and its Drive link, with `checkDriveAccess`'s warning;
  - the signed change orders to send to the trade, and the back-charges to send, keep, drop or take off a draw (`GcDrawBackCharges.tsx`).
- **The email tick** (the lead's call, the house rule that sends start off): *Email the trade about what I press here* in the window's head, off until a dev turns it on, shown only to `canSendGcTradeEmail`. With it on, Mark paid (`paid`), Approve less (`less`) and a charge's sent, settled and taken stages (`backCharge`) email the trade through `gc-trade-email`, from the rows read again after the press, each keyed once per record (`drawEmail.ts`). A change's email (`change`) waits for the Portal's P5c (`DRAW_PORTAL_LIVE`). With it off, nothing sends, and the less form and the charge's hint say so. A refusal shows in the window after the press is saved.
- **`src/lib/gc/drawRows.ts`**: `withDraws(state, tables)` lays each statement of work's money over `boardProjectFromView`'s (each line's newest report and billed percent, the draws that stand net of the charges taken off them, the ones sent back, the back-charges), `withTradeChanges` lays `ChangeOrder.tradeChange` over the change orders, and the came-in form's draft, money and payload. **`src/lib/gc/drawsIo.ts`**: `loadGcDraws` reads the four tables and the charges in one round, one function per press, and `emailTheTrade`.
- **The page** reads the draws for every job on the board when a dev opens it and lays them innermost: `withChangeRequests(withTradeChanges(withChangeOrders(withDraws(board, draws), changeOrders), draws), changeRequests)`, so Owner Billing's Money, Bill the customer and Closeout read the same draws (Helper 15's seam). `withTradeChanges` sits outside `withChangeOrders`, which lays the change orders over whole.
