# v2.4573 — Documents: a Sent tab to find any copy (2026-10-05)

Step 4 of the Sent copies train ([`docs/SENT_COPIES.md`](../SENT_COPIES.md)), first part. Steps 2 and 3 file many papers that name no job — a statement to a GC, a price request, a bid letter, a law-firm notice — and the only list was a job's own *Sent from this job*. The owner's words: *"office can find it in documents when they need it."* Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## The change

- **Documents → Sent** (`/documents?tab=sent`, office only — `canReadSentCopies`, as the table's SELECT policy): [`DocumentsSentLedger.tsx`](../../src/components/documents/DocumentsSentLedger.tsx). Every copy, newest first, a hundred at a time with **Show more**; a search box over the title, who it went to and the subject (asked once the typing rests); a row of kinds of paper — *All · Bills · Lien and legal · Contracts and estimates · Bids and suppliers · Statements and reports · Other*. Both narrow on the server.
- [`sentCopies.ts`](../../src/lib/sent/sentCopies.ts): `sentKindGroup` (a kind's group, by how the kind begins), `sentKindGroupFilter` (the group as a PostgREST filter; *Other* is "like none of them"), `sentSearchFilter` (what was typed as an `or` over three columns, with everything a filter could read as syntax dropped).
- [`sentCopiesIo.ts`](../../src/lib/sent/sentCopiesIo.ts): `loadSentCopies({ group, typed, limit })`; `self_test` rows are left out.
- [`SentCopyRows.tsx`](../../src/components/documents/SentCopyRows.tsx): the rows the job window drew, now shared; `JobDocumentsSent` uses it and is otherwise unchanged.
- [`documentsPageTab.ts`](../../src/lib/documentsPageTab.ts): the `sent` tab.

## Tests

`sentCopies.test.ts` (+3: the groups, the filter, the search), `documentsPageTab.test.ts` (2, new), `DocumentsSentLedger.render.test.tsx` (3: the list and a line's door, a kind asked at once and typed words after a rest, the two empty sentences and Show more), `JobWindowDocumentsTab.render.test.tsx` unchanged (14). Opened on the live project in the dev preview: the tab, the kinds, and each filter shape answered by PostgREST without error (nothing is on file yet, so the list read empty).
