# v2.3183 — Job search rail: note · chip · amount, in fixed columns (2026-09-08)

Client only — no migration. Owner, searching "diamondback" on the Pipeline: "I don't like how paid versus unpaid is to the left versus right of the number and some jobs have numbers of zero and some jobs don't have any at all." Diagnosis: the rail was `chip · $line-items · payment tag` — the chip is the **Pipeline stage** (Waiting / Billed / Paid) and the tag is **payment history** ("paid 6 mo ago" from `jobs_ledger_payments`, "unpaid" when line items had value and no payment existed), two facts sharing a vocabulary; the amount was the line-item sum, so a job with no line items (J548) showed nothing and $0 service visits showed a bold $0; and "unpaid" fired on Waiting rows, where it says nothing the chip didn't.

## What shipped

- **Kernel [`jobSearchRail.ts`](../../src/lib/jobSearchRail.ts)** (`buildJobSearchRail`, `daysSinceYmd`; 10 tests) — the words:
  - **amount**: line-item total when there are line items, else `jobs_ledger.revenue`; `$0` muted for a genuine no-charge job; `—` muted when there is no total anywhere. Every money-mode row gets one.
  - **note**, only when it adds to the chip: Paid → the recency alone ("6 mo ago"); a payment on a not-yet-Paid job → "paid 2 wk ago" (partial); Billed with no payment and a non-zero total → "unpaid 47d" from `last_bill_date` (plain "unpaid" without a date), **red** when `collections_at` is set, amber otherwise; Waiting / Working / Ready to Bill → nothing.
- **Order and alignment** — [`UnifiedSearchResultRow`](../../src/components/search/UnifiedSearchResultRow.tsx): the money rail is a three-column grid **note · chip · amount** (owner call on the order: the note sits left of the chip) with fixed slots (`minmax(0, 7.5rem) 6.25rem 5.25rem`, right-justified), so chips and numbers land in the same place on every row. The "N this wk" count shares the note slot. Stacked hosts (phones, the assign-session popover) keep the same order in a wrapping row. Lines-only mode (field roles) is unchanged: chip · "N lines" · "N this wk", no dollars.
- **Evidence** — [`jobSearchEvidence.ts`](../../src/lib/jobSearchEvidence.ts): `JobSearchEvidence` gains `revenue`, `lastBillDate`, `collectionsAt`; the money-mode `jobs_ledger` read selects `revenue, last_bill_date, collections_at` beside `id, status` (lines-only still reads status only). `mergeJobSearchEvidence` now keeps an entry for any id the ledger returned, so a lineless, blockless job with a revenue still gets its amount. Callers of the row: the header search, the Schedule Dispatch pickers, the assign-session popover, Supply houses, the PO generator, and the rest of the `UnifiedSearchResultRow` hosts — all read the new shape through the same component.
