# v2.6750 — Quickfill: the Jobs Billing station folds into Complete, no Total Bill (2026-11-08)

Decision 4 of punch list #176 (`to-dos/quickfill-needs-you-review/`), answered by the owner on 2026-11-08: *fold it into Complete, no Total Bill*. The Jobs Billing station had been hidden by the org since April (`quickfill_hidden_section_ids`) while its question, *Is every finished job billed?*, had no owner; its body counted three fields over every job at or above the Min HCP, Specific Work, Other job charges and the Total Bill, and offered one link to the Jobs Billing tab. Complete, no Total Bill already listed the finished jobs missing the third.

## What changed

- **The kernel** ([`lib/quickfillCompleteNoBill.ts`](../../src/lib/quickfillCompleteNoBill.ts)): `completeBillGaps(job, billLinesKnown)` reads the three checks on one job, worst first — `total` (`revenue` unset or $0), `work` (no fixture with a name and a count above zero), `charges` (no material with a non-zero amount), the Jobs Billing station's own rules. `completeBillListed` says which gaps put a job on the list: `total` or `work`, since a bill with no total or no work lines cannot go out; `charges` alone is a note on a listed row. The walk of this PR on the dev server, 2026-11-08, read 40 open with all three listing, every row *no Other job charges* alone, pre-tests, post tests and service visits with nothing to charge beyond the work; the hidden station had counted the same over every job, and a standing backlog of forty is what the review fights. A candidate is a non-paid job at or above the Min HCP with a listing gap; `completeNoBillRows` keeps the ones resolved 100% (the Job Summary % rule, unchanged) and orders them no Total Bill first, then the lowest number as the row shows it (`completeNoBillJobNumber`, the Click number when there is no HCP number). `buildQuickfillCompleteNoBillList` still returns the jobs alone.
- **The jobs cache's enrichment**: fixtures and materials are laid over the cache's rows after they paint (`jobsListEnriching`), so until then the hook judges the Total Bill alone and reports the station loading; an empty line is not a gap while the lines are unread. [`useQuickfillCompleteNoBillJobs`](../../src/hooks/useQuickfillCompleteNoBillJobs.ts) returns `rows` with their gaps beside the jobs.
- **The station** ([`QuickfillCompleteNoBillSection`](../../src/components/quickfill/QuickfillCompleteNoBillSection.tsx)): each row wears a chip per gap (*no Total Bill* red, *no Specific Work* amber, *no Other job charges* grey), the intro names the fields and links the **Jobs Billing tab** (the retired station's door), Edit job's words say *fill in the bill*, the body reads *Reading the finished jobs' bills…* while the hook's read is in flight (the walk as the Assistant sample saw the foot read *counting…* for some fifteen seconds while the body claimed the list empty), and the empty state reads *No complete job is missing its Total Bill or its Specific Work.* The Next line leads with the first row's first gap: *Set the Total Bill on 995 Locks*, *Add the Specific Work lines to 1060 Carrow Camera*. The count is the rows.
- **The retirement**: `jobs-billing` leaves `QUICKFILL_SECTIONS` and joins `QUICKFILL_RETIRED_SECTIONS` folded into `complete-no-bill`, so its marks and history read under the Complete station, a deep link to it lands there, and the round card still names its old events. Its banner is retired under the new one (the station now asks *Does every finished job have its Specific Work and its Total Bill?*; the old *Does every finished job have a bill total?* and *Is every finished job billed?* are `RETIRED_QUICKFILL_SECTION_BANNERS`, so an org row holding either reads the new question). The dev panel's **Min HCP (inclusive)** input moves to the Complete row; the setting key `quickfill_jobs_billing_min_hcp` and its default 406 are unchanged. `JobsBillingReminderSection.tsx` is deleted; the station's legend row goes with it. The stale `jobs-billing` in the org's hidden list is dropped by the parse helper as any unknown id is.

Tests: `quickfillCompleteNoBill.test.ts` (the three checks, the listing rule, unknown lines, candidates, the order), `nextLine.test.ts`, `sections.test.ts`, `sectionBanners.test.ts`, and the new `QuickfillCompleteNoBillSection.render.test.tsx` (the chips, the door, Edit job, the empty words). Guides *run the office day with Quickfill* and *bill a customer and get paid*; `docs/QUICKFILL_ARCHITECTURE.md`. No migration.
