# v2.7546 — Checklist: say you can't do a task, and why (2026-11-16)

Punch list #369, step C6 of the Task Mode train (tool 2), stacked on C4 (v2.7545). Built ahead of C5, the clean-up pass, which uses it as one of its answers. It writes the `stuck` and `unstuck` lines C1's migration lets a person insert like a note. No function is called.

## Why

A person who could not do a task had a note box and nothing else. A note told nobody: read through the dev seat on 2026-11-16, task notes sent no push, no email and no count. So a task waiting on a part looked the same as a task nobody had looked at.

## What changed

**For the person doing the task**

- **Can't do it**, a row of **⋯ More** on every open task, a repeating one and a roadmap one too (`taskMoreChoices`).
- **The sheet** (`TaskStuckSheet`) asks *What is in the way?* Four reasons are one press each: *Waiting on a part*, *Need a hand*, *Not sure how*, *Not mine* (`STUCK_REASONS`). A box takes the person's own words. The press is dead until something is said, then names who is told: **Tell Imani** (`stuckPressLabel`).
- **The task stays theirs.** It wears an amber line, *Stuck: Waiting on a part* (`stuckChipWords`), on its card and on its Still open row. On Today it sits under its own **Stuck · N** label, below Then, and is never Next.
- **The giver is told** by a push: *Theo is stuck on "Check the truck fluids": Waiting on a part*.
- **No longer stuck** replaces the row in More once a task is stuck. It is one press with no sheet. Finishing the task takes the mark off too.

**For the office**

- **A Stuck list at the top of Checklist → Review** (`ChecklistStuckSection`): each row names the task, who is stuck and what is in the way, oldest first, within 90 days. **Sorted** takes the mark off and tells that person: *Imani says "Check the truck fluids" is sorted. You can carry on.* With nobody stuck the list is not drawn.

## The risk the mock-up named

A stuck mark could become a place to park work. Two things answer it. The giver is told at once, and the office has one list of every stuck task that stays until someone presses Sorted.

## How it is built

- `stuckReason` and `stuckSaidBy` in `checklistCardEvents.ts`: the last of `stuck`, `unstuck` and `completed` decides.
- `sayTaskStuck` inserts the line directly. The policy of C1 lets whoever reads the row write it as themself, so no function stands between.
- `useTaskDoerActs` gains the sheet, `onStuck`, and a pick that takes No longer stuck without a sheet.

## Release order

The `stuck` kind is allowed only once C1's migration is pushed, so this merges after that push. Before it, the insert is refused by the table's check and the sheet shows the refusal.
