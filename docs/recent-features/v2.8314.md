# v2.8314 — In kind: the customer's page reads a bill an arrangement pays as scheduled (punch list #301, the owner's call) (2026-11-22)

The owner's call of 2026-11-22 had four answers. This is the second: the customer's portal keeps a bill a deal pays on the page, reads it as scheduled, with no pay link and not in the amount owed. The GC's statement and GC Review are v2.8309.

Before this the page listed such a bill as owed, in the balance, with **PAY ONLINE** on it when the bill had a Stripe link. A customer could pay with money a bill a credit pays down, and the office would owe a refund.

## What changed

- **The ledger leaves it out** (`supabase/functions/_shared/portalScheduledBills.ts`). `customer-portal` hands `buildPortalBills` the money jobs alone (`moneyJobsOf`), so a bill, or a billed job's remainder, on a job with `in_kind_arrangement_id` is not in `bills`. That list feeds the statement, the balance, the print packet and every pay press, so all of them follow.
- **Its own list.** `buildPortalScheduledBills` runs the same builder over the deal's jobs, so the membership and the payment rule are the ledger's own, and answers the viewer's such bills oldest first: `amount` still to come, `billedAmount`, `totalPaid` and `billedOn`. No field carries a pay link. `customer-portal` reads the column in its job select and answers the list as `scheduledBills`.
- **The card** (`src/components/portal/PortalScheduledBillsCard.tsx`). **Scheduled in kind**, under the ledger: each bill with where the work was, the day it was billed, what was paid in kind and what is to come, then the total. No press and no link.
- **The words above it.** With no bill owed and a bill scheduled, the page says *Nothing is owed in money. Your open bills are scheduled in kind. They are listed below.* It no longer says *No open bills* over a card that lists some.
- **The pay-by promise.** Its scope is the money jobs alone, in `customer-portal` and in `submit-portal-request`, so the page asks for no pay-by date on a deal's job and reads none.
- **The guides** *see what a customer sees on their portal* and *keep track of work paid in kind* say it.

## Decisions taken with the build

- **Out of `bills`, not flagged inside it.** Every reader of the list would have had to learn the flag: the statement, the balance, the money story, the print packet, the office's globe preview. A page from before this release would have kept showing the bill as owed. Left out at the source, the old page shows less and the new page shows the card.
- **The office's words.** The card is named *Scheduled in kind*, the owner's fourth answer, so a call about the bill uses one name for it.
- **A paid-off bill is listed nowhere**, as on the ledger.
- **A file of its own.** `portalMergedBills.ts` and `portalBillMembership.ts` ride in a dozen functions through the sample pages. A change to either would send each back for a deploy, so the split lives in `portalScheduledBills.ts` and at the two call sites.

## Not here

- **A bill the office shares with the other party.** A deal's bill shown to a GC or an owner who does not pay it still reads as an open bill on their shared card. That card is never in a balance and has no pay press.
- **The office's globe preview** on a customer lists the statement's rows, so it no longer lists a deal's bill and does not name the card.

## Release order

Merge, then deploy `customer-portal` and `submit-portal-request`, then `npm run check:edge-drift`. No migration. Until the deploy the page reads as before.

## Verified

Kernel tests in `src/lib/portal/portalScheduledBills.test.ts` (a covered bill, a customer part covered, an ordinary customer, a paid-off bill, a bill someone else pays, the promise's scope, the parse and its absence), the card's render test, and one on the page for the words above the ledger. The existing portal tests pass unchanged.
