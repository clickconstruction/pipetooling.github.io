# v2.7956 — Lien notices: the enclosed invoice names who the bill went to (2026-11-19)

Client only: no migration, no edge function. Found on 2026-11-16 on a § 53.056 packet built by hand for a job billed to its GC, and left open by v2.4852's *Not done*.

## Before

`buildPhysicalInvoiceDocumentForBilledInvoice` draws a billed invoice's paper again from the job. It read the job's customer and nothing else, so its **Bill to** was `job.customer_name`, or the word *Customer* when the job had none.

Bill Customer does better while it sends: it resolves the payer with the shared rule (`effectiveInvoiceParty`) and overlays the GC, or the typed recipient, on the billing context. The paper drawn later never ran that rule. So:

- a GC job with no customer printed **Bill to: Customer**;
- a GC-billed job with its own customer printed the customer's name, though the bill went to the GC;
- a bill typed to someone else printed the job's customer.

On a lien notice the enclosed invoice goes by certified mail to the property's owner and the original contractor (§ 53.056(a-3)). An owner read an invoice addressed to nobody, or to themselves.

## What changed

- `billedInvoiceBillTo(face, job, inv)` in the new `src/lib/jobs/billedInvoiceBillTo.ts`, its own file because `billToParty.ts` is copied to the edge functions and this rule is for papers the client draws: the Bill to block for a billed invoice, by the rule every billing reader shares.
  1. A bill typed to someone else (`bill_to_email` set) takes that name, email and phone (`applyBillToToJobBillingContext`).
  2. A bill to the GC takes the GC's name (`JobWithDetails.gcCustomer`). The job's email and phone stay only when the job has no other customer: a GC job, or the GC entered as the customer. On any other job they are the customer's, who was not billed, and the block prints the name alone.
  3. Anything else keeps the job's customer, as before. A job that names nobody still prints *Customer*.
  A GC whose name the job did not load changes nothing.
- `buildPhysicalInvoiceDocumentForBilledInvoice` applies it, so every reader agrees: the notice's enclosure (`noticeInvoiceDocs`), a lien claim's bills (`lienClaimBills`), the GC's unpaid-invoice print (`gcUnpaidInvoicePrint`), the Lien window, the Documents window's billed invoice, the opened PDF (`openBilledInvoicePdf`) and the bill emailed again (`resendPhysicalInvoiceEmail`).
- `payLineIsNotTheWork` (`noticeInvoiceEnclosure.ts`) reads *cheque* and *cheques* as it reads *check* and *checks*. A memo that began *Cheques can be sent to* printed as the enclosed invoice's Scope.

## Not changed

Bill Customer, the Stripe bill and the first email of a paper bill already named the payer. Notices already printed and filed keep what they printed.

## Tests

`billedInvoiceBillTo.test.ts` (6): the customer's bill, a GC job with no customer, a GC-billed job with its own customer, the GC entered as the customer, a bill typed to someone else, and a GC with no name loaded. `noticeInvoiceEnclosure.test.ts` (+4): the enclosure's Bill to for each, the printed page, and *cheques* as the mailing note.

## Follow-up, not in this change

*Email again* on a paper bill that went to a GC sends to the job's customer address, which `send-physical-invoice-email` refuses when it is not the GC's billing email. The recipient needs the same rule.
