# v2.5547 — GC projects: each trade on a project's card is one row (2026-10-14)

To-do #108 (`to-dos/gc-project-card/`), PR 3 of four: the trade rows. A trade was said three times on the card, a tile under How the stage is going, a heading with its budget and scope count, then the companies asked with their stories, with nothing tying them. Shape D's answer, as both mock-ups drew it. No migration, no edge function.

- **`src/lib/gc/tradeRows.ts`** (new, pure): `tradeRow(facts)` writes one trade's row from the page's own row (the scope, the budget, ours), the board's package, the strip's tile and the partners: the stripe (the tile's state, or none until the ring reads the stage; ours is violet before that), who has it (the company awarded, *our own crew*, or nothing while bidding), the scope (*3 lines, leaves out 1*), the money (the budget while bidding; the contract from the signed statement of work, else the carried number with *carried*), where it stands (**quotes**: *1 of 2 in*, good from two, *no company asked yet* a hole; **dots**: the five papers and *2 to go*; **pct**: the share done and the tile's bar), the companies asked as chips (`ASKED_WORDS`: *has not opened it*, *looking*, *quote in · $91,200*, *passed*), what waits on whom once awarded (the tile's note: *Draw 2 waits on our approval*), and the one press: *Compare quotes (N)* once a quote is in, else *Ask for quotes* while a company that does the trade is left to ask. `tradesWords` is the band's caption (*4, one ours*); `STRIPE_WORDS` and `BUYOUT_DOT_WORDS` are the key.
- **`src/components/gc/GcTradeRows.tsx`** (new): the rows as a table with a heading line past 680px (Trade, Scope, Budget, Quotes, Asked, Next while bidding; Trade · company, Scope, Contract, Papers or Done, Waiting on after), cells that wrap on a phone, the stripe at the left, the arrow at the end that folds the details (`aria-expanded`, *Fold Sitework* / *Open Sitework*), open by default since the asks are the day's work; `GcTradeRowsKey` is the band's key.
- **`GcProjects.tsx`**: **The trades** is the page's own band (`GcBand`, now exported from the strip) with its Key link, and the rows' details keep the scope lines with **Save as a set**, the statement of work (`GcTradeSow`) and the asks (`GcTradeAsks`) exactly as they were, so every press and every `data-gc-trade-asks` scroll target is where it was. The page reads each project's stage health once on the ring's state (`healthByProject`), the same read the strip makes, for the rows' tiles.
- **`GcStageHealth.tsx`**: the tiles and their key leave the strip, which is the verdict band and The clock now; `Tile`, `Key`, `DOT` and `BUYOUT_STEPS` go with them.
