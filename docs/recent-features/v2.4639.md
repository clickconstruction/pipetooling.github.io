# v2.4639 — Legal portal: the firm's page works on a phone (2026-10-05)

Client only; no migration, no function. Item 8 of punch list #85 ([`to-dos/legal-portal-firm-ready/`](../../to-dos/legal-portal-firm-ready/README.md)). Stacked on items 3, 4 and 11 (v2.4625, v2.4634, v2.4635). Mockup: `mockup-8-phone-layout.html` beside the card — screenshots of `/legal?t=sample` at 375 px before and after (the top, the forms, Notifications, Paper).

## The defect

`LegalPortal.tsx`, `LegalFirmMatterView.tsx` and the grid had no media query. The page was a fixed inline grid (`minmax(0, 1.25fr) minmax(260px, 0.85fr)`), so at 375 px the side column kept its 260 px and the matter column shrank to about 60 px, with the side cards drawn over it. The fee form was four inline columns and the step and payment forms three, one word per line. Notifications used the same grid. Each job's timeline sat beside a 150 px label.

## What changed

- `src/index.css`: `.legalPortalPage`, `.legalPortalSplit` (grid areas *main · list · aside*), `.legalPortalPair` (Notifications), `.legalPortalForm` with `--fee` / `--note` / `--ask` columns, `.legalJobTimeline`, and one `@media (max-width: 720px)` block: one column, matters list first, the matter, then the particulars and the side rail; forms stacked with a dotted rule between them and full-width buttons; the matter's total demand and Print packet left-aligned and full width; each job's timeline under its label; the recipient's scope buttons wrap.
- `LegalPortal.tsx`: the page, the split, the four forms and Notifications use the classes; the side column is two grid areas (matters list, side cards) so the phone order can put the list first; the panel tabs wrap. The fee form's first label reads *Fee or cost* instead of *Kind*.
- `LegalFirmMatterView.tsx`: the matter's sums and Print packet carry phone classes; the five tabs wrap instead of running off the edge; a table whose first column is *Date* keeps the date on one line (the table scrolls inside its card).
- Guide `share-your-attorney-their-portal`: the portal works on a phone, matters list first.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **Where does the matters list go?** First. A firm opens the portal on a phone to find one account; a list below the whole packet is no list. With one matter it is one card.
- **Hide the side rail on phones?** No. The particulars are what a paralegal copies into a petition; they follow the matter.
- **Cards instead of tables?** Not now. The ledger and the jobs are records a lawyer reads in columns; their own sideways scroll keeps the columns, and dates no longer split across lines.
- **Inline styles with a width check in JavaScript?** No. The repo's pattern is classes in `index.css` with a media query (the Lien desk title bar, the Submittals road). Colors stay inline and literal, since the portal is pinned light.
- **Why 720 px?** The side column needs 260 px and the matter about 420 px to read a ledger row. Below that, one column reads better.
- **Tabs that scroll sideways?** No. They wrap, so *Fees & steps* is never hidden.

## Left out, and why

- The Lien grid keeps its 1,040 px table with its own sideways scroll: it is counsel's twelve columns and prints landscape.
- The lien timeline's *aside* line on Paper (*The Legal desk, or Collections.*) is the office's wording from the shared timeline kernel; it is a vocabulary item for a later pass, not a layout one.
