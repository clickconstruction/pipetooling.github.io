# v2.8652 — Accounts Receivable: each closed case says what kind of case it is (2026-11-26)

Punch list #443, finding 255 of the walking thread's sitting 25 (2026-11-26), briefed by the fixing thread as `443-closed-cases-do-not-say-their-kind.md`. Client only. No migration, no edge function. Nothing owed after the merge.

**Read.** Accounts Receivable → **Closed cases** listed fifteen rows, each with **Reopen…**. A walker reopened one on a ZZ test job to show the returned-check path, and the page called `reopen_ar_stripe_case`: the case was a Stripe case, and nothing on the row said so. `arCaseReopenCall` in `src/lib/jobs/arCaseReopen.ts` picks the function from the row's `source`, and `ArClosedCases.tsx` drew no `source` and no kind. The office reads the same list and could not tell a returned check from a card dispute or a check the bank never took.

**The mend.** A pure `arCaseKindWords(source)` beside `arCaseReopenCall`, and each row draws its answer on a line of its own under who paid, in bold. The words are the **Came back** list's own chips, with the thing they are about in front, as the brief asked where such words exist:

| Source | The row says | The Came back chip |
|---|---|---|
| `bank` | *Check came back* | *came back* |
| `hand` | *Check came back, marked by hand* | *came back* |
| `rejected` | *Check never reached the bank* | *never reached the bank* |
| `unbanked` | *Check never deposited* | *never deposited* |
| `stripe_dispute` | *Card disputed* | *card disputed* |
| `stripe_debit` | *Bank payment failed* | *bank payment failed* |

A source the app does not know reads *Check came back*, since **Reopen…** treats it as a returned check.

The guide *match bank deposits to bills* says each closed case names its kind.
