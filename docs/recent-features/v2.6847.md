# v2.6847 — The Bridge draws the five steps as one strip of pipe (punch list #303, PR 3) (2026-11-10)

PR 3 of the Bridge's refresh under one story (`to-dos/bridge-one-story/README.md`): from the picture story, one strip. Where we stand should be seen, not read. Client only. No migration, no edge function.

## What changed

- **The kernel** (`src/lib/bridge/flowStrip.ts`): `buildFlowStrip` takes the flow the page already built and each step's pace today (`todayPace`), and returns five sections in the order work flows.
  - **How wide a section is** is the dollars the step moves in a week, sized the way pipe is: flow goes with the bore's area, so four times the dollars is twice the width (`STRIP_MIN_THICK` keeps the thinnest at three tenths of the widest). The word under each number says which dollars they are: asked, sold, built, billed, paid. Market's are what was asked of us at the asks' own value, so its end is far the widest.
  - **The block on top** of Build, Bill and Collect is the weeks of work waiting in front of that step, the tallest on the most weeks (capped at twelve). Market and Sales feed the field and hold no pile, so they have none.
  - **The lit section** is the page's reading, `StageFlow.linkId`. The frames drew the narrowest section lit. On the real numbers the step with the most weeks waiting is not always the one that moves the fewest dollars (Sales sold less a week than Build built, and Build held the most weeks), so the strip draws both and lights the reading.
  - `layoutFlowStrip` turns the sections into one outline, each section's flat run and its block, for an SVG of any width. `stripWords` lists what the strip prints, for the first screen's budget.
- **The drawing** (`BridgeFlowStrip.tsx`): one SVG behind five presses. A press is its whole column, the pipe and the words under it, and opens that step's desk, the window its card's *Break it down* opens; a step whose desk cannot be read yet opens The flow. The SVG is for the eye; each press carries the reading for a screen reader. A phone prints the lit step's weeks alone; a desk prints every block's.
- **Where it sits** (`BridgeWeek.tsx`, `Bridge.tsx`): under the three tiles in a usual week, as drawn. In a week cash leads it sits under the card, so the cash call and its press stay on a phone's first screen.
