# v2.7000 — Three presses over a text: Ask for photos, An estimate, A bill (2026-11-12)

Punch list #319, PR 1b. The owner's ask of 2026-11-11: make `/show`, `/estimate` and `/bill` easy for an assistant or an estimator on a text. The reply box answered the typed words already and nothing on it said they exist.

- **The row.** *Send Dana* **Ask for photos** · **An estimate** · **A bill** over the box, drawn by `ReplyBox` when `sendPressesShown` says so: the kind is Text, the person is a customer, a lead or an unnamed number (`SEND_PRESS_KINDS`), the box is open, and the reader is on the office team (`canOpenMessages`). A tech's Job Mode box, a teammate's thread and a trade partner's never draw it. The head names the person and never guesses a pronoun (`sendPressesHead`). The words and rules are `src/lib/messages/sendPresses.ts`.
- **A press and its word run the same code.** Ask for photos calls the same ask `/show` picks (v2.6999). An estimate and A bill write `/estimate` or `/bill` on a line of its own under what is written (`withSlashWord`), so the `/` list opens on that person's papers and a pick goes through `pickPaper` as a typed word's does.
- **The list's two new ends** (`SlashList`'s `extra` and `note`), which a typed word gets too. `/estimate` ends with a row that starts one, for a customer or a lead: **Start a new estimate**, or **Start an estimate** when they have none. It runs `useLookDoors`, the First look card's own door, now shared. `/bill` with no open bill says *Nothing is owed. Bill Customer makes a bill.*
- **A number that sent STOP** has the three dead, with the box's own reason as their title, on the dead press's pair (`deadPress.ts`).

- **The doors' io is made on a press, never on a load** (`defaultLeadDoorsIo` in `leadDoorsIo.ts`; `useLeadDoors` and `useLookDoors` take their io and their contact read as optional). The reply box draws on every thread and in every job's window, and seven render smokes that stand in for part of `messagesIo` failed to load while it made the doors' io as the module loaded.

No migration and no function.

Tested: `sendPresses.test.ts`, `ReplyBox.sendPresses.render.test.tsx`, the reply box's other suites.
