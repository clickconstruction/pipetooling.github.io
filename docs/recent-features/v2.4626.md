# v2.4626 — Legal portal: questions and answers read as one conversation (2026-10-05)

Punch list #85, item 17, PR 1 of 2 (lane D, the firm's month-one workflow). The firm's acts never stopped reading *waiting on the office*, a withdrawn ask kept asking the firm, and the office's answer to a firm question floated free of the question. One cause for the first two: `legal-portal` never returned `acknowledged_at`. No migration.

## What changed

- **`legal-portal` returns `acknowledged_at`** on the matter's entries. The firm's *On this matter* table reads *seen* once the office acknowledges an act. An office ask the desk withdrew (it acknowledges the ask) reads *withdrawn* and leaves *From the office*, because `buildLegalAsks` already read that stamp. **Redeploy `legal-portal` after merge.**
- **The office's answer carries the question's id.** The desk's *Answer…* writes `meta.askId` (`officeAnswerMeta`), the same link the firm's answers to office asks carry since #41 PR 3.
- **One conversation kernel** in `src/lib/legal/legalAsks.ts`: `buildLegalConversation` threads every question with its answers, whichever side asked; `conversationRows` flattens it for a table; `conversationWho` and `conversationStateWords` word each row for the reader (the firm reads *You asked*, the office reads *The firm asked*). An office answer saved before the link threads under the newest unanswered firm question before it, which is how the desk wrote them.
- **The conversation gets its own table** on the firm's Fees & steps tab (`LegalFirmMatterView.tsx`) and on the desk (`LegalDeskModal.tsx`), each answer indented under its question. Questions and answers leave *On this matter*, which keeps steps, payments and notes. The desk's acts moved with them: *Answer…* on an open firm question, *Withdraw* on an open office ask, *Acknowledge* on the firm's answer.

## Is this the best we can do?

- *Should questions and answers stay inside the steps table, with answers indented?* The first draft did that. It buried a three-line exchange among demand, suit and payment rows, and the *Office* column had to mean four things. Refined: a conversation table of its own on both sides.
- *Does a withdrawn ask need its own stamp (a `withdrawn_at` in meta)?* No. The desk already acknowledges an ask when it withdraws it, and the kernel already reads that as withdrawn. The firm kept seeing it only because the payload dropped the stamp. Returning the column fixes both bugs with no migration.
- *What about answers saved before today, with no question id?* The kernel threads them by time, under the newest firm question before the answer that has none yet. Nothing is rewritten in the table.
- *Should the firm's "From the office" also list answers to its own questions?* Not without a read state: the list would never empty. The answers sit in the conversation table, and PR 2 emails each one with its question.
