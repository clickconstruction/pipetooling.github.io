# v2.5646 — The job's people: the customer's word on paying opens to the estimator who sold the job (2026-10-19)

Punch list #129, PR 1 of 6 (the plan and mock-up at `to-dos/estimator-money-word/`, PR #5876). On a job an estimator bid, the estimator often hears first when the customer will pay or that they paid, and today phones the assistant to have it marked: an estimator reads no `jobs_ledger` row, and the promise gates name roles (dev, assistant, master, controller; primary for the read). Migration `20261019060000_job_people.sql` (`docs/migrations/20261019060000_job_people.md`); no edge function; nothing in the kernels copy.

- **The job's people** (`is_job_people(job)`): the office, or an estimator or primary who is the job's account manager (`jobs_ledger.account_manager_user_id`, the field `primary_scope` runs on) or the estimator or account manager of the bid the job came from. The scope is the job's, not the role's, so one predicate serves the estimator now and primary's own gap (their jobs, no promise chip) in PR 6.
- **The gates**: `add_job_payment_promise`, `void_job_payment_promise` and `set_job_promised_pay_date` take the job's people beside `can_write_payment_promises()`; `heard_by` stays the signed-in person. `list_job_payment_promises_for(job)` is the per-job read beside the company-wide one, which the office's readers keep (the Lien desk, the legal packet, Owner Billing). Nothing on `jobs_ledger`'s policies changes.
- **The job opened from a bid carries its person** (`bidImportAccountMan` in `lib/bids/jobImportFromBid.ts`, `useJobFormImport`): the bid's account manager, else its estimator, joins the team as the job's account manager (relationship *primary*) when the form has none, so the field means what it says from the first day; the office unticks them in the New Job form if that is not right.
- **The readers**: `SetPromisedPayDateModal` and `useJobThreadNotes` read the job's own promises, falling back to the company-wide read until the migration is pushed (PostgREST's "could not find the function").
- **Tests**: the SQL bed `scripts/pgtest-job-people.sh` over `supabase/tests/job_people/` (the predicate for six people and nobody; the estimator's write with `heard_by` themselves, read, void and clear on their job and the refusals on another's; the primary account manager's write and a stranger primary's refusal; the company-wide read still NULL to the estimator), registered in `scripts/sql-beds.txt`; `jobImportFromBid.test.ts` (the account manager first, else the estimator, nobody when the form has one); `useJobFormImport.render.test.tsx` (the three cases through the hook).
- **Docs**: `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`'s promise gate paragraph and the Glossary's Account Man entry; to-do #129's state.
- **Held for the owner**: `estimator_can_reach_job_account(job)` still opens any bid-born job's supply-house account to any estimator; narrowing it to the job's people would change the Job accounts lens for estimators working another's bids.
