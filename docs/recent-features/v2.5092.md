# v2.5092 — Lien emails: a reply reaches the office (2026-10-09)

`send-lien-filing-email` (**redeploy after merge**) and the client's courtesy-email preview. No migration.

## Why

Every lien email went out from `team@noreply.clicktooling.com` with no Reply-To: the GC's courtesy PDF from the run, a notice sent by email, and the final demand letter from the Lien window. That name has no mailbox (`docs/runbooks/CLICKPLUMBING_SENDER.md`), so a GC who answered reached no one. The owner's call of 2026-10-09: replies to customer email land in office@clickplumbing.com, and for these emails the office inbox, not the person who sent them.

## What changed

- **`_shared/lienFilingEmail.ts`** gains `LIEN_FILING_REPLY_TO = 'office@clickplumbing.com'`. It lives with the lien email's body builder, not in `_shared/emailFromAddress.ts`, so only `send-lien-filing-email` needs a redeploy. The runbook's sender switch for every customer email stays its own change.
- **`send-lien-filing-email`** sends every email with `reply_to: LIEN_FILING_REPLY_TO`. That covers the notice of claim, the courtesy copy and the demand letter (`email_type`). Nothing else in the send changes.
- **The courtesy preview** (`lienRunCourtesyPreview.ts`, v2.5073) shows *Replies to office@clickplumbing.com* between To and Sent, from the same constant.
- **Guides:** *send lien notices from the Lien desk* (The courtesy email) and *send a final demand letter* (Email it too) say where a reply goes.

## Tests

- `lienRunCourtesyPreview.test.ts` (13, +1): the preview names the office as the reply-to. `send-lien-filing-email`, read from its source, imports the constant and sends `reply_to: LIEN_FILING_REPLY_TO`.
- Two mutants, each failing named cases: the function without `reply_to`, and the preview without its *Replies to* row.
