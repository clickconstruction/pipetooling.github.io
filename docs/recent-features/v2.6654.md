# v2.6654 — The dev MCP reads the charged paper and a sub’s sheets too (punch list #212, residual E) (2026-11-07)

The first of the three residuals left on the card (`to-dos/signed-hours-statement/README.md`) after the four of 2026-11-07: `get_hours_statement` and `plan_hours_statement` read the paid clock paper only, so an agent could not read or make the two papers the window had learned since — the charged basis (v2.6446, punch list #251 over #254's crew-days) and a sub's sheet lines (v2.6636). The owner's word of 2026-11-07 on the residuals that needed one: *"just do it, I would prefer you do it"*.

## What changed

- **The charged pricing is a kernel.** `src/lib/people/hoursStatementCharged.ts` (`hoursStatementChargedRows`) is the block the window's read ran inline: the person's counted days as the crew-days they were part of (`buildCrewDays` over the job's approved sessions with each login's standing, the rate card, the job's rule and the open adjustments), the crew's hours or the person's own when nobody out counted, the note *with Toby · master with a helper*, the headline rate and the words under the signature, the days the card could not price and who counts as nobody. `hoursStatementRead.ts` calls it; `HoursStatementChargeFacts` is re-exported from there, so the window and its tests are untouched.
- **The crew-days kernels are on the edge.** `scripts/edge-kernels.mjs` mirrors `lib/charges/crewDays.ts`, `rateCard.ts`, `adjustments.ts`, `chargeStandings.ts`, `lib/people/hoursStatementCharged.ts` and `lib/people/hoursStatementSheets.ts` into `supabase/functions/_shared/gcKernels/`, so `dev-mcp` prices a charged paper and reads a sheet paper with the window's own code.
- **`get_hours_statement(…, basis?, kind?)`** (`_shared/devMcpHoursStatement.ts`): `basis: 'charged'` reads every approved session on the job in the span through `readAllRows` (whoever punched it: the crew each day), the standings through `charge_standings()`, the rate card from `app_settings`, the job's rule and `job_charge_adjustments`, hands them to the kernel and skips the pay reads; the reply carries `basis`, `kind`, `rows_from` and `charge` (the rate, the words, the unpriced reasons, who counts as nobody), and the reading says *charged 16 h as the crew on each day · $285.00 the crew’s hour = $3,040.00*. `kind: 'sheet'`, or nothing for a sub (`people.kind = 'sub'`) on the job's sheets, reads the sheets' lines, their payments and the first sheet day through `hoursStatementSheets.ts`, always as paid, the reading in sheet words (*7 h on 2 lines over 1 sheet, 1 sheet paid in full*); `kind: 'clock'` reads a sub's own punches. A charged sheet paper is refused in words at the ask and again once the sub is found on the sheets, as the window holds the switch.
- **`plan_hours_statement` → `apply_hours_statement`** take `basis` and `kind` on `make` and write `basis`, `charge_rate`, `charge_words` (and `with_pay` off on the charged basis) through the replaced `dev_hours_statement_write` (migration [`20261107170000`](../migrations/20261107170000_dev_hours_statement_write_basis.md)), which also learns `labor_company` for the card's third residual. The server is 0.39.0.
- **Tests**: `devMcpHoursStatement.test.ts` — the ask's new fields and refusals, the charged read against the catalog fake (the reads made, the pay reads not, the two priced days and the pending one, the facts, the reading, the composite's reply), the sheet read (the rows by sheet, the pay group, the counts, the first recorded day as the earlier of the clock's and the sheets', the reading, the clock by name, a sub with no sheet reading the clock), `make` on each basis and the sheet refusal; `edgeKernels.test.ts` holds the copy.
- **Docs**: `docs/dev-mcp/README.md` (the two rows), `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` → dev-mcp, the migration's page, the card's status.

## Decisions

- **One kernel, two readers.** The window's charged block moved into a pure kernel rather than being ported by hand to the seat, so a change to how a crew-day is priced is made once and the seat's number is the paper's.
- **The source follows the window's Who list.** A sub on the job's sheets reads the sheets unless the clock is asked for by name; a sub with no sheet on the job reads the clock. The rule is the list's own (v2.6636), not a new one.
- **`approved_at` on or after the epoch stands for not null**: the door has no not-null filter, and the crew read needs only approved sessions.
- **The wrapper takes `labor_company` now**, one push instead of two: the third residual (the company on a sub's attestation) is a client change alone once this migration is applied.

## Deploy
