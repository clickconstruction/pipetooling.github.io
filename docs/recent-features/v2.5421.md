# v2.5421 — Quick replies: room for the Spanish words and the tech’s phone, behind the scenes (2026-10-12)

Residential customers in Messages, R3's SQL (quick replies, the four additions): migration `20261013210000_quick_replies_phone_es.sql`, documented in `docs/migrations/20261013210000_quick_replies_phone_es.md`. From `RESIDENTIAL_BUILD_PLAN.md` → *R3* and section 3's data row on branch `spike/gc-mode`, for helper 4's `PhoneLine` kernel (#5558). The lead pushes it with `supabase db push` after it merges. No screen and no edge function.

- **`quick_replies`** gains `text_es` (the line in Spanish), `phone_order` (its place on the tech's phone, 1 first; null for off the phone) and `phone_name` (the button's name, 60 characters at most). No unique index on the place: the kernel's `phoneLines` breaks a tie by the line's id, and a unique index would let a removed line block its place and fail a swap.
- **`quick_reply_versions`** gains the kinds `es` (the Spanish before and after) and `phone`, with `from_phone_order` and `to_phone_order` when the place changed, so helper 3's history words tell put on, taken off and moved apart. A change to the name alone keeps no place. The CHECK that holds each kind to its columns gains both.
- **Who a line is for** widens to the seven kinds of contact, as #5565's `QR_KINDS` names them: leads, subs, supply houses and candidates join trade partners, customers, architects and anyone, on the line, on its versions and in `quick_reply_step`.
- **`messages.quick_reply_id`**: the quick reply a sent text came from, for `quo-send` to fill later. A deleted line lets go of it. No policy changes.
- **`edit_quick_reply`** keeps one Save: three trailing parameters that keep by default (`p_text_es` and `p_phone_name` blank clears; `p_phone_order` 0 takes the line off the phone). The four-argument calls already written still resolve. Only the team's lines for customers go on the phone.
- **The bed:** `scripts/pgtest-quick-replies.sh` runs this migration again after its second run of cut 3's, which made the old signature again; step 9 of `supabase/tests/quick_replies/20_scenario.sql` checks the Save, the versions, the refusals, the CHECKs and the sent text's line. Beds run in CI only.
