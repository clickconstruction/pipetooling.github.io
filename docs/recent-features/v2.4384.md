## The facts behind the rule (prod, 2026-10-02)
- No assembly in the price book holds another assembly (0 of 1,316 `material_template_items` have a `nested_template_id`), so a line is at most two steps in.
- Of the 357 takeoff fixtures priced with an assembly, 302 are one assembly and nothing else; 20 hold two or more; 35 mix an assembly with loose lines. A carrier folded in by hand (v2.4332 / v2.4381) is the other mix — BP375's EWC-1.

## What changed
- **`procurementTagBlocks.ts`** (new, pure): `tagBlock(rows)` turns one tag's lines (in the log's own order — the GC's parts, then order-only) into blocks. A single line is the fixture itself (level 0, no heading). A fixture that is one assembly and nothing else keeps every part at level 1 and names the assembly (`from`). A fixture that mixes gets an `assembly` label line per assembly (level 1) with its parts at level 2, and its other parts at level 1 after them. The tag's own line (logged before its parts) leads. Each line carries its connectors per column, as a folder tree draws them (`tee` ├, `end` └, `pass` │, `blank`), and the first order-only line of each block says how many follow.
- **`procurementLog.ts` / `procurementLogIo.ts`**: lines carry `assembly` (the part's `bid_submittal_item_parts.assembly`) and `addedByHand` (`source = 'hand'`).
- **`SubmittalProcurementPanel.tsx`**, By tag only: the Part cell steps in 1.5rem a level with `Rails` (`.procure-rail--tee|end|pass` in `index.css`); an assembly label row (*EWC1 assembly · 6 parts*) whose tick picks its parts; *from LAV 1 assembly SPACEX* on a one-assembly fixture's heading; the order-only divider per block, with the connectors running through it and through an open line's dates (both rows now start at the Part column). A part added by hand says *added by hand* (unless it reads *in place of the priced …*). On To order and By house a part names *in <assembly>* on its quiet line. The other lenses, the columns, the status and the tick bar are #4336's, unchanged.
