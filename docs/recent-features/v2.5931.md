# v2.5931 — The Bridge: the Market desk behind its card (2026-10-26)

Punch list #192: the owner asked for a desk behind every stage card, one press from the card the way the Collect desk opens (v2.5890). The approved Market design (`to-dos/bridge-stage-desks/mockups/market-desk.html`) is the intake over the relationship book: one question, *is enough work being asked of us to keep the field fed, and who actually feeds us?* A 26-week strip of asks by value over one row per GC by what the relationship pays, the narrowing verdict, the pricing-spend verdict, and an honest panel naming what the desk cannot see. No record ceremony: the desk reads and opens doors.

## The kernel (`src/lib/bridge/market/marketDesk.ts`)

Pure. An **ask** is a bid opened (`created_at`, drafts included) at `agreed_value ?? bid_value`, or an estimate requested at `total_cents / 100`, in the pay week it arrived (`payWeekContaining`). The strip is one cell per pay week over the span, GC and customer asks apart, each with the label *Week of \<day\>: $X asked, $G from GCs, $C from customers*; the feed line is `fieldPerWeekUsd / winShare` (omitted, saying why, when either is missing); the 8-week average is the Market card's own pace over its window; an *N empty weeks since \<month\>* note counts the empty cells before the current one. **Won** is a bid marked won (or `started_or_complete`) or one that became a job, at the bid's value; an estimate is won when `customer_accepted`. **Answered** is won or lost. The answer pace is the median of `outcome_at − bid_date_sent` over the GC's answered bids in the read, worded *as recorded* because `outcome_at` is the office's recording day.

The book has one row per GC that asked in the span (a bid with no `gc_builder_id` folds into *No GC on the bid*) plus *Customers (estimates)*. Each row carries its state, its quarters (the span cut into 13-week quarters, the last *This quarter so far*), asked and pays, a det line and a door. Rows sort feeding by asked, then new names, fading, dead weight; beyond `MARKET_BOOK_ROWS` the rows with the least asked fold into one line that says how many of them are quiet. The three tiles read asked this quarter against the 13 weeks before, the top three GCs' share of GC asks this quarter against last quarter (narrowing / widening), and the new names this quarter against last, naming the newest. The verdict (owner ruling R1) is built from facts only: the fading GCs and the GC with the most $0 bids, or *The asks are steady. Keep the book warm.* The pricing-spend line draws per dead-weight GC; the $0 line counts bids sent with no value. The footnote names every column the figures rest on. Every sentence passes `plainWordsFailures`.

## The reads (`src/lib/bridge/market/loadMarketDesk.ts`)

Lazy on the first open, read-only, nothing new on the server: bids with `created_at` since today − 26 × 7 − 90 days (`id, created_at, bid_date_sent, bid_value, agreed_value, outcome, outcome_at, gc_builder_id, project_name`), the jobs those bids became (`jobs_ledger` by `bid_id`, chunked, billing-only rows skipped), every GC's first-ever bid (`bids.gc_builder_id, created_at` paged and folded to the earliest per GC), the GC names (`bids_gc_builders`), and the estimates since the same start with `doc_kind` null or `estimate`. The 90 extra days feed the answer pace; the span's asks are the book.

## The desk

`src/components/bridge/BridgeMarketDesk.tsx`, the Collect desk's chrome: the label line, the headline (red when Market is the link), the one bold verdict, the muted sub-line with the paces, the three tiles, the strip (each week a `<button>` with its label; a press shows the same line under the strip), the book (each row's head a `<button aria-expanded>` opening the quarters table, the det line and the door), the pricing-spend and $0-bids cards, the amber *What this desk cannot see* panel, the footnote and the line that no plan is recorded here, and a footer with **Open the Bid Board ›**, **Open Estimates ›** and **Close**. Esc and the backdrop close it. `Bridge.tsx` adds the `market` entry to the cards' `desks` while `demand` is in, reads the desk on the first open (`marketReads`, keyed on the day and the card's counts), and draws the header from the card's own numbers while the reads are in flight.

## The owner's calls, built to defaults

- `MARKET_DESK_WEEKS = 26`: the strip's and the book's span in pay weeks.
- `MARKET_FADING_SHARE = 0.5`: fading is asks this quarter under this share of the GC's quarterly norm over the span.
- `MARKET_DEAD_WEIGHT_BIDS = 6`: dead weight is at least this many priced bids in the span, nothing won, nothing answered.
- `MARKET_NEW_NAME_DAYS = 91`: a new name's first-ever bid is inside this many days.

Each is an exported constant at the top of the kernel; the owner changes the number there. `MARKET_QUARTER_WEEKS = 13` and `MARKET_BOOK_ROWS = 8` are layout constants beside them.
