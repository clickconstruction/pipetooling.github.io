# v2.6243 — A declaration names its signer one way (2026-10-31)

Punch list #220 (`to-dos/legal-desk-affidavits/`), a second follow-up, found reviewing the live screenshots. The print sheet asked a declaration's declarant for a first, a middle and a last name of their own, and prefilled only the first and last words of the affiant's name. A witness typed *Thanh Van Nguyen* printed *My name is Thanh Van Nguyen.* in ¶1 and *My name is Thanh Nguyen, my date of birth is …* in the § 132.001(d) block unless someone typed the middle name again, so one paper could name the declarant two ways. Separately, under the business-records preset the role line's help still read the RMP's *Add the license number* over the preset's *custodian of records* line.

- **The kernel** (`affidavitPaper.ts`): `DeclarantDetails` drops `firstName` / `middleName` / `lastName` (session-only and never saved, so nothing to migrate). `declarationJurat(details, county, name)` prints the draft's full name. `declarantProblem(details, name)` holds an empty or one-word name (*Their full name is one word. Texas asks for a first and a last name. Go back and type it as it reads on their ID.*) before the date of birth and the address. New `fullNameWords` (trimmed, one space between words) names the affiant in the opener, the signature, the jurat line and the *attached to* line; `isOneWordName` flags a single word. `printNeedsDeclarant` (`statementPrint.ts`) takes the draft, so a one-word name waits even with the details held.
- **The print sheet** (`LegalStatementPrintSheet.tsx`): the declarant form shows **Name on the paper** read-only (amber when one word) with **Change the name** back to the window, then the date of birth and the address.
- **The window** (`LegalAffidavitWindow.tsx`): **Their full name** says *One word so far…* while it holds one word, with the statute's reason under the declaration. Under the records preset the role help reads *The business-records form wrote it from the pick above…*; `pickTeam` gives a new pick the preset's role line whatever the stale `roleTouched` held; **What someone saw or heard** puts an untouched role line back to the roster's.
- **Guide** *swear a statement on a collections account*: the declaration's print paragraph.
