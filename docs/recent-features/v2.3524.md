# v2.3524 — Customer Waiting: the caller can hide the banner for themselves (2026-09-16)

Client only. No migration, no edge function. The "mute for the caller" residual in `to-dos/customer-waiting-residuals.md`; item 5a of the owner's ready-to-build train (5b, the `tel:` sweep, is a mechanical PR that merges alone).

## Why

The Customer Waiting strip (v2.3248) is deliberately undismissable: five people see a customer standing at the counter until someone lowers or closes the request. Once one of them calls, the strip turns amber and reads *Sam called Jane Doe 2:14 pm* — for Sam too, who has the row open and needs no reminder. The residual named the next lever: hide it for the viewer who made the call, never a dismiss for anyone else.

## What shipped

- **Kernel** — [`customerWaiting.ts`](../../src/lib/customerWaiting.ts): the row now carries `last_called_by_user_id`; `canHideForMe(banner, viewerId)` is true only on the called state when the lead's caller is the viewer (a still-waiting customer keeps the strip for everyone); `rowsVisibleToViewer` drops a hidden row only while the viewer is its caller, so a later call by someone else, or the request going back to waiting, brings the strip back; `pruneHiddenIds` keeps the list to live rows. Four tests.
- **Context** — `CustomerWaitingContext` selects `last_called_by_user_id` and exposes `viewerId`; a `CustomerWaitingValueProvider` exists for render tests.
- **Banner** — on the full strip's called state, the caller sees **Hide for me** (dotted, quiet, beside Open). It stores the request id in `localStorage` under this viewer's key, per device — a convenience, never a record; unavailable storage just means nothing is remembered. The collapsed inbox line has no control. Render smoke (3): offered to the caller and hides the strip; never to another viewer; not while anyone is waiting.
- Help guide *answer a customer waiting request* → *The banner that follows you* gains the paragraph.

## Verified

- Kernel tests, the render smoke, `helpGuideContent`, release-notes drift, to-do drift, typecheck, lint.
- Live on prod data: no customer-waiting request was open at the time, so the strip could not be shown live; the app loads clean with the widened select (no console error), and the behaviour is pinned by the smoke over a hand-built called row.
