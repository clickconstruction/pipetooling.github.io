# v2.4418 — Pricing: "Bids like this" replaces the history box (2026-10-02)

The owner, on a Pricing tab at 82% (a screenshot of *This number vs your history*): "this box is not very informative but takes up a lot of space. Can you think of how we can make it better beyond just being collapsable?"

## What was wrong
Read from the live Plumbing history on 2026-10-02 (71 decided bids):
- **The scale ran 20–65% and every usable past bid sat at 79–95%.** All five dots drew at the right edge, under the pointer.
- **Only 5 bids reached the scale** (1 won, 4 lost on price) of the 15 won or lost on price. Most old bids carry a near-empty cost estimate (a $41,951 bid over $185 of cost reads 99.6%), and the 95% sanity band leaves those out.
- **The verdict could be wrong.** BP398 at 54%, below the only win at 79%, read *In your winning range — 0 of 1 wins*.
- **It ignored what all 71 bids do have:** a price, an outcome, a loss reason and a GC.

## What changed
The block is now **Bids like this** ([`PricingBidsLikeThis`](../../src/components/bids/PricingBidsLikeThis.tsx), renamed from `PricingMarginHistory`). It leads with facts that need no cost estimate.
- **Folded (the default): one line.** Three chips and a *Compare N bids* button. *This size* counts the decided bids from half to double the Workbench's price: won and lost on price. The second chip names the bid's GC and counts its decided bids (*Journeyman · 2 won of 7*). It says the name because some bids are filed under a catch-all customer: 16 decided Plumbing bids sit under *PlanHub - Subcontractor Prime Opportunity*, and *This GC* would have read as one builder. The third chip is the margin and where it stands (*82% · above your one win*). The fold is per device (`useBidsLikeThisFold`).
- **Unfolded.** A bar of what happened to every bid this size: won, lost on price, the GC lost the project, other (the reasons on hover). Then the bids won or lost on price by name, closest in size first by ratio, six at most. A row with a recorded bid tab says how far over the low we were, which needs no cost. Then the GC's record in a sentence, and the margin scale.
- **The margin scale fits what it draws**, in tens, never narrower than thirty points. It is marked *Thin evidence* below ten dots and says how many of the bids won or lost on price have a usable cost estimate.
- **The verdict is rewritten**: above every win, below every win, level with the one win, or among the wins, with the price losses at or below it. One win reads as *your one win*.
- Kernels: new [`lib/bids/bidsLikeThis.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/bidsLikeThis.ts) (size, GC, the named list, 20 tests); [`pricingMarginHistory.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/pricingMarginHistory.ts) gains `marginHistoryScale`, `isPriceLoss` and the new verdict (27 tests). The component takes two more props, `currentPrice` (`effRevenue`) and `gcName`. `isPriceLoss` now lets a recorded loss category beat the word *price* in the note.

Price per fixture was checked as a comparison and ruled out: count rows mix fixtures with feet of pipe.

No migration, no edge function: every field was already in `bid_pricing_history`'s rows.
