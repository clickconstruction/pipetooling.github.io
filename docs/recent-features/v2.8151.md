# v2.8151 — Document builder: send the Sub NDA to a GC (2026-11-21)

Punch list #404, step 9 of ten. No migration: `document_builder_sends` has held a customer and the job a send is about since v2.7988. Two functions changed and take a deploy: `document-builder-send` and `customer-portal`.

A GC here is a customer who is the GC on a job with us (`jobs_ledger.gc_customer_id`). Their page is the customer portal. No real GC has been sent anything. No attorney has read the Sub NDA's words, and every word this step writes is a placeholder.

## The send, pointed at GCs

Step 5 built the send once, for a job's sheet to its trades. This step points the same list at GCs.

- **The rows.** `readBuilderParties` (`sendIo.ts`) reads every job with a GC on it, a thousand a read, then those customers, their people (`customer_contact_persons`), their page's links and the builder's live sends. `builderPartiesFrom` turns that into the list's rows: who was sent to first, then the GC with the most jobs. A GC's contacts are the customer's own address, then its people, each with an email, none twice.
- **Their page.** `gcPageLink` (the function) and `gcPageFromLinks` (the app) agree on which of a customer's links is a GC's page: the view made for the jobs they are the GC on, else the whole account's. A link that shows only their own jobs is not it. On is used, never made gets one from a send to that GC alone, and turned off stays off.
- **What it is about.** The box under a GC's row asks **What it is about**: *All their jobs with you*, the default, then each job (`aboutChoices`, `aboutJobId`). A reminder keeps what the first send was about.
- **The find box.** `BuilderSendList` takes `findWords`. With it the list shows who was sent to and the first twelve others until someone types (`shownParties`), since the list of GCs is the whole book.
- **The one press asks first.** `BuilderSendList` takes `beforeBatch`, and `BuilderGcSendCard` hands it a confirm window: *Send to 12 GCs? Each gets an email now, about all their jobs with you.* That question is not in the drawing. One press on the trades of one job is a few emails. One press on every GC is many.
- **The function.** `document-builder-send` tells the two sends apart by who the ask names (`sendAskKind`). `sendBuilderToGc` checks in the sheet send's order: the caller, the builder (published), the read mark on its newest revision, the customer (the GC on a job), the person, the reminder's address, the job it is about, the page. Then the email, in the trade email's frame, and after it the row and the sent copy.
- **The email.** `builderSendEmail`: *Secrecy on your jobs with (us)*, the note, and where it sits on their page. It is logged as `document_builder_email`, a new row in the email catalog.
- **The filed copy.** The email carries the customer's own page link, which is a key. The copy under Sent copies keeps it cut short, by the rule v2.8152 gave a sheet's send.
- **The mount.** `DocumentsBuilderTab.tsx` hands `BuilderGcSendCard` to `WalkedBuilder` as `share`, so the card sits under the builder's link.

## On the GC's page

- `customer-portal` answers `builders`: each builder sent to this customer and still on their page, as published, with the job it is about (`_shared/documentBuilderCustomerPage.ts`). Only a link that shows the jobs they are the GC on gets any.
- `customerPageBuilders.ts` parses them, places them and words the block. One about all their jobs leads the page. One about a job sits under that job's name. A builder that does not read as a paper is left out.
- `PortalBuilders.tsx` draws the block: **Secrecy on your jobs** or **Secrecy on this job**, *New* until they open it, *Changed since you opened it* after a newer revision, and **Open the Sub NDA builder**, which opens the flow right there with no fields and, by step 8's `look`, no press on its last step. It loads the flow only when pressed.
- Opening tells the office once a visit: `{ action: 'opened', portalToken, builderKey }` to `document-builder-send`, which keeps the day and the revision on the send. A preview and the sample page tell nobody, and a signed-in teammate's look is not counted.
- A GC can look only. Sending their picks is step 10 and waits on Taunya's yes.

## Deploy

After the merge, on Taunya's go by each name, from `origin/main`: `document-builder-send`, then `customer-portal`. Until `customer-portal` is deployed a GC's page shows no builder, and the page reads an answer with no `builders` as none. Until `document-builder-send` is deployed a send to a GC is refused as a bad ask, and nothing goes.
