## Access

The table is a dev's alone by its RLS, and People → HR is a dev's tab. The list writes nothing.

## Is this the best we can do?

The mock-up drew Apply and Drop on every row. Who may press Apply in the app, any dev or the owner alone, is his call, and a press built before he makes it would decide it for him. So the list is words: it ends the hunt across sessions for what is waiting, and the yes is still said in a session, where the plan is checked again before it is written.

What that leaves: until the seat's verbs land, nothing puts a row in the table, so the list stays empty on prod. A stale row cannot be cleared from the tab; it leaves the list by itself after seven days.
