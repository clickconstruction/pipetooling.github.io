# v2.4541 — Put a GC on notice: undo an Approve all pressed by mistake (2026-10-05)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. The owner's ask: once a run has been started, offer a way to undo it on the run window, for a button pressed by accident.

## What the click does

*Approve all N and send the run* (`approveAll` in [`GcOnNoticeModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/GcOnNoticeModal.tsx)) saves and approves a desk item per ready job, and with its four ticks (all on by default) moves the GC's standing rule to *send*, sets the payment terms to *Winding down*, saves a Legal desk matter and shows each owner their property's bills. Then it opens the run window. An undo of the approvals alone would have left four other changes behind.

## What changed

- **A receipt.** `approveAll` fills a `GcRunReceipt` ([`gcNoticeRunUndo.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/gcNoticeRunUndo.ts), new kernel) as each write lands: the item ids, the rule it moved and what it was, the payment-terms columns read just before they change, the owner-share rows it turned on, the Legal matter. A click that stops halfway keeps what did land.
- **Owner share, exactly.** `ownerShareTurnedOn` ([`ownerBillShare.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/ownerBillShare.ts)) lists the jobs whose memory was off and the bills not yet shown; `shareBillsWithOwnersOfJobs` returns it beside its owner count, and `unshareBillsTurnedOn` turns those rows off again and no others.
- **The strip.** [`LienDeskRunModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskRunModal.tsx) takes `undo` and draws a strip under its title, above the steps: *You just approved these 11 notices for RMC- Dudley Mason. Pressed it by mistake?* and **Undo the approval…**. Only Put a GC on notice passes it; the Lien desk's own run has no single click to undo.
- **The confirm.** `undoRun` asks through the app-wide confirm (`gcRunUndoMessage`): what goes back, what stays. *Keep the run* is the blue, focused answer.
- **The undo.** `undoLienDeskApprovals` ([`lienDeskIo.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDeskIo.ts)) moves the items still approved or awaiting approval back to `drafted`, clears the approval and the printed stamp and stamps `pulled_back_*` (a row already recorded as sent is never touched). Then the rule (`set_customer_lien_notice_policy` with a note), the terms columns as they were, and the owner share. Each step is tried; the toast names any that could not be put back.
- **What it cannot do.** A Legal desk matter has no remove; the confirm and the toast say it stays.
- **How long.** The receipt is the window's state: cleared when the window closes, when another GC opens, and when the run is recorded.
