# v2.8156 — Papers before a job opens: a job's paper built from settings gets its signing link (2026-11-21)

Punch list #389, step 3, a gap its first signature on prod found. No migration.

**Deploy**, from `main` after the merge, on Taunya's go by each function's name: `send-contract-for-signature` and `get-contract-signing-link-for-self`. Until then both refuse a job's paper built from settings with *Add contract text, a canonical document URL, or a reference link*, so the office cannot copy its link and a person with a login cannot open it from the job. The page of a sub with no login (`submit-sub-portal`) never had the check and hands out the link today.

## What was wrong

A job's paper built from settings has no body, form or link of its own. Its words are the Contract Book's clauses, put together for the job's picks when the paper is read. Step 3 (v2.8145) let a job require such a paper, and v2.8154 taught the sign page to read and sign it. The two functions that hand out a signing link still asked every paper for a body, a document address, a reference link or a form, and refused this one. The bed and the local walk both began at a token, so neither met the refusal.

## What changed

- **`jobPaperBuiltFromSettings`** (`_shared/contractSigningSend.ts`) reads the `settings` of the required paper a copy names. `hasSigningContent` counts a copy as having something to sign when that read says yes (`built_from_settings`).
- **`send-contract-for-signature`**, the person path, reads the copy's `job_required_paper_id` and asks before it refuses (`withJobPaperSettings`). The company branch is as it was: a company's paper is never a job's.
- **`get-contract-signing-link-for-self`** asks the same, after it has said whose paper it is.
- The read happens only for a copy that names a job and has nothing else to sign. Every other paper takes no new read and is refused or sent as before. A read that fails is an error, never the old refusal: its words would send the office to add a body the paper must not have.

## The three functions of v2.8154 are deployed

`accept-contract`, `get-contract-for-signer` and `gc-trade-portal` were deployed from `main` on 2026-11-21 on Taunya's go. Then a `ZZ Test` paper was signed on prod, on the `ZZ TEST sink` job, by the roster's `ZZ Test Papers Sub`:

- The office's link was refused, which is this fix. For the walk alone the ZZ copy was given a reference link, and the link was made.
- `get-contract-for-signer` read the job's paper in English and Spanish, with the picks' plain words and a seal equal to the database's own.
- `accept-contract` refused a wrong seal and no seal with *The paper changed since you opened it.* and wrote nothing.
- The live page showed the three placeholder clauses, posted the seal it showed, and signed.
- The copy kept the paper frozen with its settings, their plain words and the seal, and the seal worked out again from the frozen words is the same.
- After the signature the job's row refused a looser change and a write by hand, and the signed copy refused a change to its frozen words.
- Documents → Agreements opened the signed record: *The job's settings when this was signed*, and *Their seal matches.*

The ZZ copy, the Book entry and its packet were deleted. Two things stay, because the app cannot delete them: the required paper's row on the `ZZ TEST sink` job, taken off, and one `esign_consents` row for the ZZ signature.

## Not changed

People → Contracts shows **Send** on a line only for a paper with a body, a link or a form. A job's paper is sent from its job and from Documents → Agreements, so that line is left as it is.
