- **The migration:** `gc_remind_customer_to_pay`, SECURITY INVOKER.
  - It files the reminder (the day, the pay-by day, the office's line, the email trimmed) and puts one note on the Payment Chase queue through the Pipeline's own `add_payment_chase_touch`: the customer's, pinned to the billing job, never a promise.
  - It refuses, in words: a bill waiting on the architect, one certified at nothing, one paid in full, a day other than today, a pay-by day before today, an email with no subject or lines, and a project with no customer.
  - The money team's policy and O1's read-only and twin blocks are its gate.
- **The email:** `gc-customer-email` gains `reminder`.
  - It sends the subject and lines as the reminder was filed, with the customer's portal line when they have a link.
  - It files the copy as `bill_gc_reminder` under Bills.
  - It writes `email_send_log_id` back on the reminder, and refuses a second send of one already emailed (`alreadySent`).
- **The words** (`payReminderMail` in `ownerBillingRemind.ts`, the lead's calls on 2026-10-08):
  - *Reply with the day you will pay.* in place of the prototype's portal Pay line, since the bill is not on Stripe.
  - *Our unconditional lien waiver for it follows once it is paid.*, since the waiver is a press after the payment: the app never promises what a press has to do.
- **The window** (`GcBillRemind.tsx` on each sent bill):
  - **Remind them to pay** shows where `payReminderStep` says the bill is late. It opens the pay-by day (five days out), **Your line in it**, the email as it will read, and **Send the reminder**.
  - Once sent, the bill reads *Reminded today · pay by Wed Oct 14.*, adding *The email did not go.* when the reminder has no log.
