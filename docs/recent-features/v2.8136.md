# v2.8136 — Job Mode: Is it as told? reads the list again after a refused send (2026-11-20)

Punch list #382, from the audit of PR 10 (v2.8110). Client only: `JobModeArrivalCheck.tsx` and its render test. No migration, no edge function.

## The fault: a card no press could answer

The crew's card is read once, when the job's details open, and answered later. A send can be refused because the card is older than the list:

- One of the crew answered first. The stage is asked about once.
- The office changed a bullet, so a No names a bullet that is no longer on the list to look at.

The card showed the refusal's words and stayed exactly as it was. Every later press sent the same old list and was refused the same way. It read again only when the phone found its signal or the page came back to the front, so a person had to leave the app and come back.

Two of ours who reach a site together both have the card open, so the second of them met this every time.

## The fix

- A send that is refused reads the list again. The card then shows the list as it is now, or leaves when there is nothing to ask. A No stays pressed on a bullet that is still on the card.
- A later read that fails no longer takes the card down. Before, a phone that came back to the front with no signal lost the card and the Nos pressed on it until the signal returned. The first read still draws nothing when it fails: that default is the owner's to flip.

## Checked

Three new render tests, each failing on the old card: a refusal because someone answered first (the card leaves), a refusal because the list changed (the new list, the No kept, the count right), and a later read that fails (the card and its Nos stay). The five tests before them pass unchanged.
