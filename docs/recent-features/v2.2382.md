# v2.2382 — Pay speeds: Money waiting replaces the drift dumbbells (2026-08-27)

Estimator feedback (Wendi, day after v2.2377 shipped): the drift chart was
"okay but not super useful and some numbers are obscured", and she wanted to
see **each job a customer owes**, not one combined figure. All three traced to
real causes:

- `DRIFT_AXIS_MAX = 60` clipped anything slower to a full-width bar and drew
  the real number ("→ 164d") on top of it — the strike-through she screenshotted,
  on exactly the rows the chart existed for.
- Open dollars lived only in the hover tooltip.
- Per-bill data (job, address, amount, billed date) already rode the StageRows
  the modal receives but was summed into one "Open now" number.

Changes:

- **New kernel `src/lib/jobs/moneyWaiting.ts`** (+ tests):
  `buildMoneyWaiting(rows, paySpeeds, todayYmd)` — per customer, every open
  bill with wait-days and a tone vs that customer's baseline (own 12-mo
  median, company median for thin history): ok ≤ baseline, warn over, late at
  2×, undated without a date. Off-pace = oldest dated bill waited past
  baseline; undated bills never set the pace. `openBillsForCustomers(...)`
  groups the same bills for every customer with open money.
  `billWaitTone(waitDays, baselineDays)` exported for the UI.
- **`PaySpeedsBreakdownModal`**: the DriftChart/`driftPos`/axis block is gone;
  the section is now **Money waiting** — per-customer rows whose bar is their
  open bills (one segment per job, width ∝ dollars, tone colors), a numbers
  column that can't collide ("164d waiting · usually ~35d · $X open on N
  jobs"), row-click expansion listing each bill (wait badge, job · address,
  billed date, open $, click-through via `onOpenJobDetail`, board jump via
  `onOpenCustomerBills`), the tone legend, and the on-pace collapse line.
- **"By customer" expansion** gains an "Owes now — by job" list above the
  existing "Paid before" payment history (same `openBillRow` renderer).
- **`paySpeedDrift.ts` deleted** (+ its test) — superseded. Note: the old
  model's "recent form" source (last-3-payments median, the only thing that
  could show a customer running *faster* than their average) is intentionally
  dropped — Money waiting is strictly about money on the board today; payment
  form still shows in each customer's history drill-down.
- Help guide `see-when-a-customer-will-pay.md` retold (Money waiting bullet,
  two-half expansion); render tests updated (undated-bills-on-pace case,
  dated-bill row + per-job expansion case).
