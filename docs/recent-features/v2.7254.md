# v2.7254 — Lien desk, By date: a band for each month, a quiet line for each GC, and Later as a short list (2026-11-14)

The owner, looking at By date on a wide screen: the desk "is covering too many jobs", the month titles should look different from the GC titles, and Later could show the days since the job was sent for billing. He approved a mock-up of four moves with one instruction: "Build it and later should start folded and not open."

## What was wrong

The tab has three levels and they read alike. Only This month had a coloured bar; Next month and Later were grey bars that looked like a GC's row, and a GC's row was a bold two-line title with a lane of its own. The direct jobs' heading was cut off with an ellipsis. Later held the jobs with nothing to do for two months or more, each drawn as a full timeline row. On a job with no clock hours the words *Nov · no hours* sat on the lane in large amber type.

Measured on the dev server before the change: the dated label under a work tick was a button whose style set `fontSize: 10` and then `font: 'inherit'`, so the shorthand won. The label drew at the row's size in a 24 px box that ran 8 px past the bottom of its 48 px row. That is why the labels read large and sat on the row below.

## The change

1. **Every bucket is a band.** A tinted bar the whole width with a 4 px rail at its left: Overdue red, This month amber, Next month blue, Later grey. It carries the fold, the name, the date in a pill (*by Nov 16 · in 2 days*, *Jan 15 and after*), the short summary, *Record the mailing* and *Draft the N* where it had them, then the job count and the money. `bucketOf` writes a bucket's facts in three parts, so `facts` reads as before and `datePill` and `summary` are its pieces.
2. **A group is one quiet line.** `lienGroupLine`: *Lowbeck Contracting · GC · 2 jobs · 2 notices owed*, *Direct to owner · 2 jobs · no notice step*, then a hairline, the group's next move and its total. The line wraps before it cuts a word. It still folds its jobs.
3. **Later is a list, and starts folded.** Overdue and Later are folded on the first view and whenever All is picked, on every width. Opened, Later is one line a job in four columns: the job (number, name, then the GC or *direct*), **Billed** (*39 d ago* with a meter whose length is the age, full at 90 days), **Lien by** (*Feb 15 · 93 d*, with *notice by Jan 15* beside it when a notice comes first) and **Open**. The oldest bill is first; a job with no bill day goes last. The age is muted under 30 days, amber from 30, red from 60. A line opens the job's Lien window, and the job's number and the chip keep their own doors. An overdue job listed with its property stays under the job that hosts it, greyed, with *Worked later? ›*.
4. **No hours is a chip.** *Nov · no hours* is a small amber chip beside the job's name, in a full row and in a Later line. It carries the tick's own explanation as its hover and is the door to the last day of work, as the label was. The hollow tick stays on the lane without words.

The dated label under a work tick inherits the family alone, so it is 10 px again and stays inside its row.

## Days since billed

`src/lib/jobs/lienBilledAge.ts` (new kernel, unit tested). `lienBilledOnYmd` takes the job's bills of status `billed` that still owe money under the one payment rule (`attributeJobPayments`, as `lienBilledOpen` reads it) and returns the oldest one's day. A bill's day is the desk's own reference, `billedReferenceYmd`: `billed_at` as a civil day in the company's time zone, else the bill's estimated day. A job with no bill row falls back to `jobs_ledger.last_bill_date`, else it has no age and reads —. `buildLienCalendarRows` puts the day on the row as `billedOnYmd`. `lienBilledAge` counts whole civil days to today and never goes below zero.

Days since billed is on the Later list only. The Overdue, This month and Next month rows keep their timeline.

## What was on a GC's lane, and where it went

- **The folded flags with a count** (notices owed, in the mail, liens to file, one flag per date). The counts of notices owed, in the mail and late notices are the words of the line. The liens to file are counted on the band. Each job's own flags are on its own row, as before.
- **The hover card on a folded flag**, which listed the jobs behind the count and offered *Draft the N* or *Record the N mailings* for exactly that GC's jobs. It is gone. The band's *Draft the N* and *Record the mailing* act on the whole bucket, and each job's own mark still opens its own door.
- **The GC's pay dot**, the pen that records the GC's word for every job in the group. It is a press on the line now: *Record what they said ›*, or *They said Dec 14 ›* once a date is on record. It opens the same window.
- **An envelope whose date has passed** drew on the GC's lane as well as on the job's row. The job's row keeps it, with its door.

The key at the bottom loses *Jobs on one date*, which explained the folded flags.

## What a Later line does not carry

A timeline row in Later had a pay dot (the pen for one job's pay date) and the striped bracket for a property with no kind. A Later line has neither: it opens the Lien window. The toolbar's *N kinds not set ›* still counts and lists those jobs.

## Not changed

No deadline, no rule for which jobs are listed or which bucket a job falls in, no count, no word of any paper. No migration, no edge function, nothing a press writes. The phone layout from punch list #352 keeps its rows; its bands gain the rail, the pill and the count, its group heads are the quiet line, and its Later is the same lines, folded at the start.
