# v2.6467 — The Scoreboard says which job to look at right now (2026-11-03)

Punch list #250, PR 3 of 4, after PR 2 (v2.6457, #6959) opened each job to the rows that put something in. The game's board carries a match headline; this is the Development page's: one line under *Right now* in the board's flag block.

- **The line** (`scoreboardHeadline(model, seesCosts)` in `src/lib/developments/scoreboard.ts`, drawn by `Headline` in `DevelopmentScoreboard.tsx`): on the full board, a job with no price that is costing comes first, the costliest of them (*JP13 has no price yet: $4,200 spent over 31 h.*); else the job that carried the site, the most profit, leaving out a job whose earned dollars are the assumed half (*… carried the site: $50,000 profit, 42% of what it earned.*); else the job that has cost the most past what it earned. It waits for the reads it stands on (`scoreboardCellStands('profit')`), so it never names a job on half a read, and says nothing on a site with nothing on it. The job's number is the page's `JobChip`, so a press opens the job and a rest of the pointer shows its card.
- **The leader's board** reads no cost, so its line speaks in hours and earned dollars: a job with no price that has hours on it (*… has no price yet: 31 h on it.*), else the job that earned the most (*… carried the site: $120,000 earned, 38% of the site.*). Draft 2 of the mock-up drew the full board's line, cost and all, on the leader's phone too; the build keeps cost words off the board that reads no cost. A job row now says whether it carries a price (`ScoreboardJob.priced`), since that board cannot tell *no price* from a cost it never read, and a job with no price wears the *no price* flag when it has hours on it as well as when it has a cost, so the leader's row says what its headline says.
- **The fold counts hours**: a job folds under *Nothing on them yet* when it has no earned dollar, no cost and no hours. Before, the leader's board, which reads no cost, folded a job with no price and hours on it there, the very job its headline names. Its flag line says *N with work on them*, the full board's *N with money on them*.
- **Where it sits**: at full width under the flag block's line; at the middle widths, where the flag block's column is a quarter of the board, in a row of its own across the board's width at the top of the table; on a phone under the banner's line.
- **Guide**: *Read the scoreboard* gains the line and the leader's version of it. Glossary, ACCESS_CONTROL, PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION and the #250 card amended.
