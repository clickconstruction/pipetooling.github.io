# v2.8699 — A job's Lien window says why it stayed open, and a slip beside it no longer closes it (2026-11-27)

**Taunya's word, 2026-11-27.** Three rows of that day's sheet, each with the fixing thread's pick marked, and her answer to the sheet, relayed by the managing session: "Picks stand".

1. *Escape closes a job's Lien window unless a field holds typed words.* Pick: keep it. So v2.8690 stands.
2. *When Escape is held over typed words the window says nothing.* Pick: say why in one quiet line.
3. *A press on the Lien window's backdrop still closes it over typed words.* Pick: hold it by the same rule as Escape, in one PR with row 2.

**What changed.**

- **The line.** When Escape or a press on the backdrop leaves the window open over typed words, one line is drawn under the window's title, in the amber text token, with `role="status"`: *This window stayed open because you typed something that is not saved. Save it or cancel it, or press × to close without it.* It goes with the next press or typed word inside the window, with a save, and with the window. No toast and no confirm.
- **The backdrop.** `onClick` of the dark area around the card called `onClose` with no question. It now asks the same fact Escape asks, whether a field holds words typed and not saved, and stays when one does.

**How it is built.**

- `src/lib/jobs/lienWindowEscape.ts`, the kernel of v2.8690, gains `lienWindowBackdropAction({ unsavedWords })`, which answers `stay` or `close`, and `LIEN_WINDOW_HELD_WORDS`, held to the plain-words rules by its test.
- `LienInstrumentsModal.tsx` keeps one fact, whether the last Escape or backdrop press was held. The card's capture handlers for a click and an input drop it. The effect that already forgets the typed fields when a save lands drops it too.

**What does not change.** The **×**, a form's **Cancel** and the footer's close each close the window at once, typed words or not. With nothing typed, Escape and the backdrop close it as before. A write in flight still makes Escape wait, and the backdrop is not held by it, as before. A window drawn over this one still takes the key and the press first. On a phone the window is the whole screen, so there is no backdrop to press.

**Known edges.**

- A select or a tick box changed and not saved does not hold the window, as in v2.8690. The rule is about typed words.
- The line does not go by itself when a form's own **Cancel** takes the typed words away by some way other than a press or a key inside the window. Every way the window offers is one of those two.

**Tests.** The kernel: the backdrop's two answers and the line's words. The real window: the line is drawn on a held Escape and goes with a press inside; the backdrop closes a clean window, stays over typed words with the line, and closes again once the field reads as it did; the **×** closes over typed words. The window's other render tests pass unchanged.

**To see it live.** Reads only, if nothing is saved: open a job's Lien window on a ZZ test job at desk width, type a day into the demand letter's pay-by field, press Escape, read the line, press the dark area beside the card, then press **×**.
