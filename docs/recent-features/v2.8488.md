# v2.8488 — Take back now covers a trade company’s own paper (2026-11-24)

Punch list #403, the last of the six small faults its walks of 2026-11-24 found on prod: no press took back a trade company’s own paper, so one sent by mistake stayed with its signing link live for 14 days. One migration, `20261124103000_take_back_company_paper.sql` ([its note](../migrations/20261124103000_take_back_company_paper.md)); no edge function.

## What was there

**Take back** (v2.8348) deletes an outside party’s paper in one guarded statement under the table’s own policy. It left a trade company’s own paper out on purpose (*a trade company’s GC paper is GC mode’s*), and a render test held that. GC mode records each paper it asks a company for, with a promise that only the signed paper keeps, and only a dev reads those rows. Since step 6 the office also sends a sub’s company its papers from People → Contracts, with no GC ask behind them, and nothing took one of those back.

## What changed

- **The function** `take_back_company_paper` deletes a trade company’s own paper that is not signed, required by no job and replaced by no newer copy. It refuses in words a paper GC mode is waiting on: an open promise of the paper’s kind about the company itself. So a paper the office sent from People is taken back, and GC mode’s own paper stays GC mode’s. A definer, since the leader cannot read GC mode’s promises: its door is the leader and a dev.
- **Which door a paper takes** is `takeBackHolder` (`paperTakeBack.ts`, pure): a party’s the plain delete as before, a trade company’s own the function (`takeBackCompanyPaper`, `paperTakeBackIo.ts`). The function’s three answers are read by `companyTakeBackOutcome`, and an answer it does not know is a failure, never a paper taken back.
- **Documents → Agreements**: a trade company’s waiting line carries **Take back** for the leader and a dev, with the confirm and the words a party’s paper has.
- **People → Contracts**: under a company’s row, a paper that is out or was never sent carries **Take back** on its own line (`SubCompanyHeadRow`), for whoever may delete a paper there. The tab lists a company’s agreements alone, so a company’s W-9 is taken back here. After the press the company’s rows are read again.
- **A refusal** is shown with the function’s own words, behind what the press was doing: *Failed to take back the paper: W-9 stays with ‹company›, since GC mode asked for it by Nov 30. It is GC mode’s paper to chase.*
- **Docs and help**: the glossary’s and the access notes’ Take back lines, *find an agreement we sent or signed* and *put a sub under their company*.

## What it does not change

- **The company stays on the bench.** `gc_remove_bench_company` keeps a company we emailed, by its own rule. Taking the paper back ends the link, and the company’s row stays.
- **The table’s delete policy**, which a party’s Take back still uses.
- **GC mode has no press of its own** for a paper it asked for. That paper is closed by its signature, as before.
