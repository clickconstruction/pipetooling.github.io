# v2.3209 — Bids on a map, tightened: map + a distance/due rail, 50-mile home fit, no instruction line (2026-09-09)

Client only — no migration. Owner ask: "way too much white space and non-useful things going on this map… make everything information-rich and not have wasted space." Mock-up *Bid Map Card, Tightened* (before/after) approved 2026-09-09.

## What changed

- **Two columns** on desktop ([`BidBoardMapCard.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/BidBoardMapCard.tsx)): `grid-template-columns: minmax(0, 3fr) minmax(0, 2fr)` — the map on the left at 300 px (was 380, canvas-wide), the new **rail** on the right; phones stack (map 220 px, was 260). Card padding 12 px (was 16), gap 0.45 rem, margin-top 0.5 rem; the map loses its inner border and radius (one frame). Card height on a wide screen ≈ 380 px, was ≈ 520.
- **Home fit frames the 50-mile ring** (`BID_BOARD_MAP_HOME_FIT_MILES` 150 → 50, [`bidBoardMap.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/bidBoardMap.ts)); `bidBoardMapHomeFitPoints` now adds the ring's four compass points (`ringExtentPoints`) so the whole ring is in frame even when the pins sit on one side; the "nothing within range → fit everything" fallback is unchanged. **Fit all** still widens to every pin. This is the one owner-decision change (v2.3162 chose 150).
- **The rail** ([`BidBoardMapRail.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/BidBoardMapRail.tsx), kernel [`bidBoardMapRail.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/bidBoardMapRail.ts) +13 tests): read entirely off the pins the card already has —
  - **By distance from the office**: `≤ 25 mi` · `25–50 mi` · `50 mi +`, each count · compact value (`formatMoneyCompact`: `$9.1M`, `$820k`) · `N due soon` (unsent pins wearing a due ring). Miles = the bid's recorded Distance to Office when it has one, else the straight line from the office anchor (`pinMilesFromOffice`, the rings' own yardstick). The buckets are **toggles** (`aria-pressed`) — a second filter over the section chips (`pinsInBuckets`; a pin with no miles is never filtered out). Bucket stats count the section-visible pins so a switched-off bucket keeps its number.
  - **Unsent and due · nearest first**: unsent pins with a due date — overdue first, then due-soon, then by miles; five rows plus "+ N more"; tap → `select(pin.id)` (selects the pin, lights the board row).
  - **Totals line**: `146 pinned · $34.8M · Lost 71 off` (when the Lost chip is off), and the **"N bids have no map location yet · add their addresses"** door (v2.3205) moved here from the footer, with the "Placing N more bids…" line while the geocoder runs.
- **Header**: the how-to sentence ("Click a pin… Rings are 25 and 50 miles… Chips toggle pins only") leaves the footer for the title button's `title`; **Fit all** is a link (`LINK_BUTTON_STYLE`, the unused `OUTLINE_BUTTON_STYLE` removed) beside **Hide map**. The footer row is gone entirely.
