# v2.4046 — GC Review: Find a check (2026-09-28)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. PR 3 of "Where the checks went" (after [v2.4043](v2.4043.md) the kernel and [v2.4044](v2.4044.md) the line under each bill). The second shape of the GC's question — *"what did you put #48211 against?"* — answered at the keyboard while they are on the phone.

## What shipped

- **`GcFindCheckModal`** (`src/components/jobs/GcFindCheckModal.tsx`, z 64) — opened from a GC group's Share menu (**Find a check…**, By GC only). A search box (number, amount or day), the newest five checks before anything is typed, then for each match: the headline (`checkHeadline`: check, amount, received, mailed, deposited), the applied sentence (`checkAppliedSentence`: one clause per job and bill, *which it paid in full*), the trail (`checkMoveWords`, one line per move from the ledger) and a *no number* chip on a check recorded without one. A miss says to try the amount or the day.
- **`fetchGcChecksInputs(gcId)`** (`src/lib/jobs/gcChecksAppliedIo.ts`) — the GC's jobs (where it is the GC, or the customer outright) with bills and payments, the `jobs_ledger_payment_events` rows that touch those jobs, and the Mercury deposits the payments were matched to (posted date, and how much of each every job together has applied). Deposits are best-effort: a reader without banking access gets no deposit dates and no unapplied remainder, never an error.
- `JobsGcReviewModal` gains one state (`findCheckGroup`) and one Share-menu line; the modal owns its read.
- Guide *run your weekly GC statement round*: a **Find a check** section; GLOSSARY and the GC Review map carry the modal.

## Decided here

- **The modal reads on its own.** GC Review's rollup covers open bills only; a check that paid a job in full is on no board row, so the lookup reads every job the GC pays on.
- **Newest checks before anything is typed** — often the one they are asking about, and the window is useful at rest.
