# v2.5334 — GC mode: pick the paper a statement of work or our contract is built from (2026-10-12)

Cluster 9, PR 2 of the paper pickers' pair (GC Reviews helper 9, directed by the lead session GC Review): the office picks what a statement of work and our contract are built from. It stacks on PR 1 (v2.5329, #5503), which reads and signs a statement of work built from clauses in the trade's portal. It needs #5481 (`20261013000000_gc_clause_papers`, v2.5309) on prod, whose `paper_id` columns and setters these reads and writes use. No migration and no edge function here.

- **The kernel** (`clauseRows.paperChoices`): the library documents a picker offers. A document qualifies when it is no form, is published at revision 1 or more, and has a clause still live. They come in the library's order, labelled as *Build a paper from clauses* labels them, each with its book for the preview.
- **The reads and writes** (`clausesIo`): `loadPaperChoices`, `loadSowPaper` (a trade's statement of work not signed yet, read by its package since the board's statement of work carries no id), `loadOwnerContractPaper`, `setSowPaper` over `gc_set_sow_paper` and `setOwnerContractPaper` over `gc_set_owner_contract_paper`. The setters refuse in their own words. Neither table's new column is in the generated types yet, so they read through `as never` as the clause tables did.
- **The picker** (`GcPaperPicker.tsx`): **Paper** offers today's paper first, then each paper built from clauses. **Preview** opens `ClausePreviewWindow` for one party with no choice (its new `as`).
- **A statement of work** (`GcTradeSow.tsx`): a dev's card shows **Paper** until it is signed, once the library has a paper built from clauses or one is picked. Each pick saves at once. **Preview** puts it together for the company and this trade, its package's one, as `gc_trade_sign_sow` freezes it. The board's own read is unchanged, so a board can never fail on the new column.
- **Our contract** (`GcCustomerContractSend.tsx`, `GcProjects.tsx`): the send has **Paper**, starting on the paper the last send went with. The page sets it on the new send right after `gc_send_owner_contract` returns and before any email. A refusal leaves the send on our contract file, and the words say so. An email sent again picks no paper, since nothing is sent anew. **Preview** runs as the customer: no trade, the clauses for everyone.
- **Guides**: `award-a-trade-on-a-gc-project` (pick its paper before you send it) and `send-our-contract-to-a-gc-customer` (the **Paper** step, and a reminder starting on the last send's paper).
- **Tests**: `clauseRows.test.ts` (`paperChoices`), `GcTradeSow.render.test.tsx` (the pick saves, Preview for the company and trade, a refusal keeps the paper, none when signed, read only or with none built from clauses), `GcCustomerWindow.render.test.tsx` (a first send, a reminder's starting paper, none on an email sent again) and `GcProjects.render.test.tsx` (the paper set on the new send before the email, and a refusal's words).
