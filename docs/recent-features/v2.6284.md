# v2.6284 — One Drive folder per job, under its customer (2026-11-01)

The owner's rule, given right after the Photos tab went live and its first walk found J1091's photo refused by Drive's quota and J550's waiting on a press: every job gets a folder when it is made, the folder sits under the customer's folder, the back catalogue is brought into the same shape, and the app links straight to each. The first photo on J1091 had shown why: the office's hand-made folders sit in a person's My Drive, where the service account can make folders but can never own a file (*Service Accounts do not have storage quota*), while the app's own write check said *The app can write here* because Drive reports it may add children. This PR is the server side; the Where files go tab's press and counts and the customer window's doors follow.

- **The tree** (`_shared/whereFilesGo.ts`): root › `<customer>` › `<job pattern>` › the five. `customerFolderName` names the customer's folder (Drive-safe, `Customer <id>` when the name is empty; `customerFolderNameApart` adds the id when another customer already holds the name); `NO_CUSTOMER_FOLDER_NAME` (*No customer yet*) holds the jobs with none. `placeFolder` is the one decision: a folder outside a Shared Drive is held, one under the wrong parent moves, one with the wrong name is renamed, both when both. `folderPathWords` and `tidyOutcomeWords` write the path and the sweep's line.
- **The write check tells the truth**: `checkFolderWrite` answers `outside_drive` (with the owner) for a folder in a My Drive even when Drive says it may add children, in `outsideDriveWords`: *This folder is in taunya's My Drive, where the app can make folders but cannot save a file. Move it into the app's Shared Drive, or clear the link and press Make the job's Drive folders.* `driveFailWords` reads Drive's quota refusal as the same words, so the Photos tab's row and the job's hold say one thing.
- **`_shared/jobFolders.ts`**, `FolderTree.placeJob`: the customer's folder kept (a link in the Shared Drive; one under the root follows the customer's name), found or made under the root and written on `customers.google_drive_link`; the job's own folder kept and moved or renamed into place, its bid's folder the same way, a flat folder under the root from before the tree moved under the customer, else made; the five inside as the office's up-front / as-needed choice says; `google_drive_link` written when the folder changed, the pictures and plans links where empty, `drive_folder_tidied_at` always and `drive_folder_hold` with the reason or null. Drive keeps a folder's id through a move or a rename, so every link holds.
- **`job-drive-folders`**: the press places through the tree and answers `placed` (`act`, `path`, `customer_folder`, `hold`); a hold is a 409 with its `why` (`outside_drive`, `not_a_folder`, `cannot_open`, the customer's three). The new `sweep` verb (the cron secret, or the office) runs the rule over the live jobs never placed or placed more than seven days ago, 25 at a time, or with `all` and `offset` over every live job for the tab's press, and answers the counts, the held jobs with reasons, and the line. A job Drive refuses mid-run keeps its reason on the row, unstamped, so the next run tries it again.
- **`photo-save`**: `photoFolderOrWhy` places the folder for a job that has none (or whose hold may have cleared) before the first photo lands, and a held job's photo carries the hold's words instead of *That job has no Drive folder yet*; the three sweeps write `driveFailWords` of what Drive said.
- **Migration `20261101163412`**: `jobs_ledger.drive_folder_tidied_at`, `drive_folder_hold`, the partial index, and the cron `job-drive-folders-sweep` every 10 minutes while a live job waits (`docs/migrations/20261101163412_job_folders_tidy.md`).
- **The client's press words** (`src/lib/jobs/jobDriveFolders.ts`): the line says *under Oak Hollow HOA* and what the press did (moved, renamed, both); `placed` is parsed, null from a function that predates the tree.
- **Deploy** `job-drive-folders` and `photo-save` after the merge, right after the push. The back catalogue: Taunya's hand-made folders (188 jobs, in her My Drive) are held until she drags *Click Customers* into the PipeTooling Jobs Shared Drive; ids survive the drag, so the next sweep adopts, renames and moves them with no further step.
