# v2.5288 — Messages: Bill day reads the walk and the cut-off (2026-10-12)

This is wave 2 of the Messages build, cluster 7, PR 1 of 5 (GC Reviews helper 7, directed by the lead session GC Review). It adds the reads, writes and the one hook behind cut 9's screens. It reads the tables of migration `20261012140000_gc_bill_day_walk.sql` (v2.5265) and lays them through the kernels of v2.5248. No screen yet: Money's Bill day switch is PR 2.

- **`src/lib/gc/billDay/billDayIo.ts`**:
  - Reads: `loadLineChecks(drawIds)` (`gc_superintendent_checks`), `loadBillDayNothing(sowIds)` (`gc_bill_day_nothing`), `loadWhereOnSite(sowIds)` (`gc_sow_lines.where_on_site`) and `loadBillDayCutoff()` (`app_settings.gc_bill_day_cutoff_days`). The cut-off falls back to 6 when the read fails, so Bill day still draws.
  - Presses: `recordLineCheck`, `sayNothingThisPeriod`, `putBackNothing` (deletes the row) and `setBillDayCutoff`. Each refusal is thrown in its function's words.
  - Pure mappers: `lineChecksOf` gives each tap its line by the kernels' id; `photoLinksOf` keeps the newest photo on each line; also `nothingOf`, `whereOnSiteOf` and `cutoffOf`.
  - The three tables are not in the generated types until the types PR after the push, so the reads use the house `as never` cast.
- **`src/components/gc/useBillDay.ts`**: `useBillDay(state, project, drawTables, superintendent)` reads the four for one job. It works out the dates, one row per trade, the walk in walking order, the seven gates and the river, and gives each press with a read of what it changed. `billDayIds(project)` lists every draw of the job's trades, sent back ones too, and their statements of work.
- **`src/lib/gc/links.ts`**: `billDayHref(projectId)` (`/gc?view=money&billday=<id>`) and `billDayFromSearch`. Helpers 2, 5 and 6 call it from Follow up, the flow's pay application round and By step's Draws row.
- **Tests**: `billDayIo.test.ts` (8, with a stub client that records every call), `useBillDay.render.test.tsx` (6, over the prototype's demo draws: Wendi's walk in walking order, the ring at 3 of 7, a tap and Nothing this period read again, a failed read, a refused press), and `links.test.ts` gains one.
