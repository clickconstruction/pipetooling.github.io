# v2.6397 — A warranty in place of a contract: the paper and the step exist (2026-11-02)

The owner's call of 2026-11-02: when the office is uncomfortable sending a residential customer a contract, it sends a warranty the homeowner initials and signs. To the customer it reads "we are showing up for you"; it binds both sides because the customer certifies the work on it. Nothing in the app made, sent or read one. PR 1 of three: the data and the kernels. PR 2 is the Contract window's Warranty pick, the signing page's initial boxes and the functions; PR 3 the flow's read, the step card, the thread's `{warranty}` blank and the guide.

## The design (the owner's yes, 2026-11-02; the note `residential-warranty-design-choices.md` in the session's shared folder)

- **A kind of job agreement in the Contract Desk**, not a table of its own: `job_contracts.kind` (`contract` | `warranty`). The window, the three ways to get it signed, the page `/contract/sign`, the signed copy, the chip, the sweep and the reminders already exist for a contract and serve the warranty with their words by kind. A signed warranty counts as the job's agreement, so the chip's amber *No contract · crew on site* goes quiet.
- **An aside step on the residential flow**, as the GC customer's change orders are: `warranty_paper`, their move, a loop (one round per warranty sent), finished by `warranty_signed`, carrying the `warranty` paper. `readFlow` draws an aside step apart from the lane: *now* while a round is open, *done* once every round is signed, *later* when none went. Nobody is held on it. Its id is not `warranty`, since the GC customer flow's year-after-closeout step is and a quick reply keeps only the id.
- **Initials, typed.** Each line the office writes for the paper (`fields.initial_lines`, prefilled from `DEFAULT_WARRANTY_INITIAL_LINES`) takes two to four letters; the row keeps them as `initials` `[{line, initials}]` once they sign. The default lines read for a warranty sent before the work, in the slot a contract would take; the office rewrites them for one sent at completion. The owner's own words for the paper replace the defaults when he sends them.
