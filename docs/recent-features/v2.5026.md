# v2.5026 — Submittals: send the review link from the app (2026-10-09)

Migration `20261009235500`, and a new edge function, `send-submittal-room-link`. This builds the owner's call of 2026-10-09 on Submittals decision 11 (`to-dos/submittals/README.md`): the app may send the room link. Until now the Share window said *No email leaves the app*, and the office pasted each link into the email chain. That still works. Now the app can also email each reviewer their own link, but only when the office presses for it at that moment. PUNCHLIST chose how the default behaves (reading A): a box on the Share window, unticked on a bid until the office has sent a link there once, and ticked after.

## The change

- **The function** (`supabase/functions/send-submittal-room-link/index.ts`, `verify_jwt = false`): `POST { person_id, note? }` with a staff JWT.
  - It refuses a training account and a digital twin.
  - It reads the person as the caller, so RLS admits the pricing sharers on bids they can price, as on the tab.
  - It refuses a closed link, a closed room, a room nobody shared, and a person with no address.
  - A person with no token gets one, as the reply email does.
  - It sends through the one sender, `sendEmailViaResend`, as gc-trade-email does, from `COMPANY_EMAIL_FROM` with reply-to the sender.
  - It files the sent copy itself (kind `submittal_room_link`, keyed to the bid), with the token kept as `?t=…`.
  - Then it writes a `link_sent` event on the person. A failed send writes nothing.
- **The rules and the email** (`_shared/submittalRoomLinkEmail.ts`, pure): `parseRoomLinkRequest`, `roomLinkRefusal`, `roomLinkUrl`, `roomLinkBidLabel`, `roomLinkKeptHtml` and `buildSubmittalRoomLinkEmail`.
  - The subject names the newest shared revision and the bid: *Click Plumbing and Electrical shared Rev 2 of the submittal for B375 SpaceX BA-02N*.
  - The body greets them by first name and puts the office's line first. It says what the submittal is and what they can do on the page; a watcher reads that they can read and ask. Then the button to their own link, *This link is yours. There is no password.*, and the company's phone.
  - The kept copy's redaction lives here, not in `sentCopyKeptHtml`, which 46 email functions bundle.
- **The Share step** (`SubmittalRoomPanel.tsx`): each person on an open, shared room with an address on file gets **Send the link** beside **Personal link**.
  - It opens a short form: *Email Dana Whitfield their own link at dana@arch.test. It comes from the company, and a reply comes back to you.*, a box for a line of your own, then **Send the link** and **Cancel**.
  - A send that went closes the form and reloads the room. A refusal says why in the office's words (`roomLinkRefusalWords`) and keeps the form open.
  - The person's line then leads with *link sent Oct 9* (`personTrail`, `describeTrail`), and the door reads **Send it again**.
- **The Share window** (`SubmittalShareModal.tsx`): the footer's *No email leaves the app — paste the link…* became a box, **Email each person their link**.
  - Unticked, it reads *No email leaves the app until you tick this.* Ticked, it reads *Emails 2 people their own link when you share.*
  - It starts ticked on a bid with a `link_sent` event (`roomHasSentLink`). It is off, and cannot be ticked, while nobody on the room or typed in the window has an address.
  - After the revision is shared, it emails each person it can reach, one at a time: those on the room with an open link and an address (`canEmailLink`), then each person typed in the window. One line says how many went and who was not sent one (`sharedLinksLine`). The room link is still copied for the GC.
- **What customers see**: the GC journey gains *Submittal link email* before the review room, rendered with the function's own builder over the sample room. The person strips read the newest send as *Link emailed Oct 9*. The email catalog gains *Submittal review link*, a customer email from the company; the catalog now has 48 rows.

## Deploy

1. `supabase db push` for `20261009235500`. **The push waits behind GC MODE's 09:12 batch, as #5093's does.** This stamp sorts after the batch's claimed stamps.
2. `supabase functions deploy send-submittal-room-link`, after the push. Deployed before it, the emails would go and their events would be refused, so the Share step would not show them.
3. `supabase functions deploy dev-mcp`: its generated catalog (`supabase/functions/dev-mcp/catalog.ts`) now names the new function. No other function changes: the new shared file is imported only by the new function and the app.
4. `npm run check:edge-drift`. No types PR is needed, because a check constraint is not in `database.ts`.

Until step 2, a press of **Send the link**, or a share with the box ticked, says *Something went wrong on our side*, and nothing is sent.
