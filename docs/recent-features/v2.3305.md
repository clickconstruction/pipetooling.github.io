# v2.3305 — Banking is controller and above: plain assistants lose the page, its doors and its data (2026-09-11)

Migration `20260911183606_banking_controller_and_above.sql` ([`docs/migrations/20260911183606_banking_controller_and_above.md`](../migrations/20260911183606_banking_controller_and_above.md)); edge functions `mercury-reconcile` and `get-mercury-account-balances` changed (**redeploy both after the push**).

## Why

The owner saw Taunya (assistant) on Banking → Accounting. It was not a broken gate: assistants have had full Banking since 2026-04-01 ("banking now useful by assistants"), `docs/ACCESS_CONTROL.md` documented it, and the controller role (v2.662) was layered on top as "everything an assistant can do plus money" without ever taking Banking back. Her door was the "bank-label suggestions have waited 3+ days" Needs You card on Quickfill, whose *Open approvals* button navigates to `/banking?tab=accounting`; the gear-menu Banking link would have done the same. Owner's call: Banking only, controller and above.

## What shipped

- **Kernel [`src/lib/bankingAccess.ts`](../../src/lib/bankingAccess.ts)** (+4 tests): `BANKING_ROLES` = dev · master_technician · controller, `canAccessBanking(role)`, `isStaffBankingRole(role)` (the non-dev Banking view). Every Banking door reads it, so the audience is one edit.
- **Doors closed for assistants**: the gear-menu Banking link ([`Layout.tsx`](../../src/components/Layout.tsx)); the route — [`layoutRouteAccess.ts`](../../src/lib/layoutRouteAccess.ts) gains `BANKING_ONLY_PATHS` and bounces the office cohort off `/banking` unless `canAccessBanking` (+2 tests: assistant off Banking but still on AR, Tally, Quickfill; controller/master/dev on); the page itself ([`Banking.tsx`](../../src/pages/Banking.tsx) — every `dev || assistant-like || master` guard is now the kernel, and `parseBankingView` gives master/controller the staff view); the **label-approvals Needs You card** on the Dashboard ([`DashboardPinnedQuickRow`](../../src/components/dashboard/DashboardPinnedQuickRow.tsx)) and Quickfill ([`QuickfillNeedsYouSection`](../../src/components/quickfill/QuickfillNeedsYouSection.tsx)) — its RPC returns the zero row for non-staff too; the **Quickfill Banking sorting snapshot** section ([`BankingSortingSnapshotSection`](../../src/components/quickfill/BankingSortingSnapshotSection.tsx) + the page's `sectionWouldRenderOnPage`).
- **Data follows the page** (migration): `is_banking_staff()` is dev / master / controller; Banking-only tables narrow with it (label rules + suggestions, org notes, duplicate dismissals, reconcile runs, card ↔ user links); category-tag management too; nickname *edits* are banking-staff while *reads* stay office-wide; eight Banking-only RPCs drop assistant from their role literal (the reverse of the v2.2920 sweep, same live-definition technique). The shared Mercury tables the Jobs, Tally and AR pages read move to `is_office_staff()` — the same set as before — so **nothing an assistant does on Job Parts Tally, the Jobs board (bank-deposit matching, card-charge splits, job costs), Job Summary or Accounts Receivable changes**.
- **Edge functions**: both Mercury functions' `ALLOWED_ROLES` drop `assistant`.
- **Help**: `sort-the-bank-feed`, `reconcile-the-books-against-bank-statements`, `manage-bank-category-tags` and `banking-visuals` no longer list assistant; `sort-the-bank-feed` says who opens Banking. `match-bank-deposits-to-bills` keeps assistant (a Jobs-board flow). `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`: Banking row, controller section, the Banking data-layer note. `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`: both Authentication lines.
