# v2.8575 — Lien desk on a phone: the list gets its room back (2026-11-25)

Punch list #428 (`to-dos/lien-to-do-on-a-phone/README.md`), the first two of its three phone calls. Punch list #352's walk found the same fault. Client only. No migration, no edge function. The third call, the Add Task line, is v2.8573.

## The fault

A walk on 2026-11-25 in an iPhone simulator (Safari, 402 by 874 points) found two things.

1. The list showed through about 355 of 874 points on every view of the desk. With **The flow** pressed, 5 of 8 stages a column stood on one screen, where #428 had measured both columns whole. The **Payoffs** strip, about 85 points, was the first row of the page's grid and never scrolled. It came in v2.8504, the day after #428's measure.
2. After a stage was pressed, the find box and the *This run* line stood between the pinned line and the stage's rows. One row's first line showed at the foot of the screen. *This run* came in v2.8474.

## The calls, and the answers

Both were asked of the owner on 2026-11-25.

| The call | The answer | The other answers |
|---|---|---|
| On a phone, where does Payoffs stand? | **It scrolls away with the list.** | A chip in the desk's header row. Pinned, as it was. |
| While a stage is picked on a phone, what stands between the pinned line and its rows? | **Nothing.** The find box and *This run* come back when the stage is let go. | They move under the rows. They stay. |

## What was built

**Payoffs scrolls away with the list.**

- The desk draws a list-top slot on a phone (`data-lien-desk-list-top`), the first thing inside whichever list is on the screen: To do's one scroller, By date, By job and the three By paper lists. It tells the page the slot's element (`onListTop`).
- `LienPayoffs` takes `stripHome`. Given the slot, it draws its strip into it through a portal. Given null, no list is up and it draws no strip. Left out, as on a computer, the strip stands in its own row.
- The window is the component's own either way. So it stays open while the strip moves, and `?payoffs=1` still opens it on any view.
- One `LienPayoffs` stays mounted on the page, so a change of view reads nothing again.

**Nothing under the pinned line.**

- `lienFlowUnderLine` (`src/lib/jobs/lienBoardFlow.ts`) says what a phone's To do draws around the Board. With a stage picked: no strip over the Board, no find box, no run door.
- `LienGetItRightHost` takes `door`. Off, the run's door is not drawn while the run is shut. An open checklist is drawn all the same.

## Where the build went past the answers

- **A find box that holds words stays** while a stage is picked. Those words still narrow the rows, and a person must see why a row is missing and be able to clear them.
- **Payoffs is not drawn while a stage is picked.** A picked stage brings the page to its top, where the pinned line is. A strip over that line would push the rows down again.
- **Payoffs is not drawn over an open paper or a job's own screen.** No list is up there. It is at the top of the list on the way back. #352's walk named both as part of the fault.
- **On the three By paper lists the find box stays pinned over the list**, as before, and the strip is the first thing under it.
- **A failed read of the left-out papers is not said while a stage is picked.** Its line stands in the run's door's place and comes back with it.
