# v2.8086 — Dashboard, Needs you: a ZZ test job no longer owes the office answers (2026-11-20)

Found by the Fable 5.2 audit of punch list #382 (Before we start), on the live walk of the Dashboard as an office role. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

The line `start-answers-owed` (v2.8010) counts the answers customers owe us on every stage whose day to decide is near. Its read took every job with a bullet on them, test jobs included. The walk that proved the feature on production left a list on a ZZ test job, with two bullets open and a day to decide nine days out. So the office's Dashboard read *The customer owes us 2 answers on 1 stage*, *ZZ Test development*, with **Open the list**.

Every other line of Needs you that reads jobs leaves ZZ test jobs out for every seat but a dev who shows them (punch list #61): the lien lines, the contracts, materials on site and the follow-ups each take `hideZzTestJobs`. This one did not.

## The fix

- `src/lib/needsYou/startAnswersOwed.ts`: `withoutZzTestStartJobs(sources)` drops the jobs the Pipeline's own rule calls a test job (`isZzTestJob`: the job's name or its customer's name starts with ZZ). The lines and rows of a dropped job are never read, since a site is built from its jobs. It hands back the same object when there is no ZZ job.
- `src/hooks/useStartAnswersOwed.ts` takes `hideZzTestJobs` and filters before it places a site or counts an answer, so a hidden job costs no address lookup either.
- `src/hooks/useNeedsYouInputs.ts` passes the same `hideZzTestJobs` the other lines read.

The Development page's Schedule look and the job window are unchanged: a person who opens a ZZ site still sees its list.

## Tests

`src/lib/needsYou/startAnswersOwed.test.ts`, two new cases: a ZZ job beside a real one counts three answers shown and one hidden, by the job's name and by its customer's; with no ZZ job the sources come back untouched. The first case fails without the filter.
