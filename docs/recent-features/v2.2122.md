# v2.2122 — "Version" means one thing: the copy sweep (2026-08-23)

F3 of the "Send or Compare" train (review gap 4 + 6, decisions D3c/D6). The word "version" meant four things in the Bids UI: a sendable bid (`bid_versions`), a takeoff-book edition (`takeoff_book_versions`), a labor-book edition (`labor_book_versions`), and a price scenario (`price_book_versions`). Copy only — no behavior, no schema.
