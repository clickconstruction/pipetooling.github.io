# v2.4533 — Overhead: the Man hours who list fits a phone (2026-10-05)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. The owner asked for a look at the moved card (v2.4530) at phone width. The card, the picture, the pinned Period column and the day-table door all held at 375 px. One thing did not: in the who list the pinned Person column was 154 px of the panel's 310, because *N h waiting* sat beside each name, so Field and Office showed and Total needed a sideways scroll.

## What changed

- [`ManHoursWho.tsx`](../../src/components/people/ManHoursWho.tsx) takes `compact` (the card passes its own under-560 px flag) and treats a phone the way it treats the side panel: the waiting note drops under the name, the cells tighten, and the *Not on a job* header may take two lines. A phone gets 0.3rem beside each number where the side panel has 0.4rem, and the panel's own side padding drops to 0.4rem.
- At 375 px the table is now 321 px wide in a 318 px panel on the Month zoom, and within 6 px on the other three: all six columns are in view.

## Tests

`ManHoursWho.render.test.tsx` (+1): the waiting note is inline on a wide card and a block under the name on a compact one. Seen in the dev preview on prod data at 375 px on all four zooms.
