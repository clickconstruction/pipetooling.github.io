## What the office sees

**Ask for** on a row of **Call this week** was an empty state. It now shows what a one-time lookup found about the name, one fact a line:

- **What it is**: a builder, an owner, a design firm, a permit service or a trade, with a few words on it.
- **Office**: an office phone, a link to call on a phone.
- **Ask for**: one person and their title.

Each fact names the page it was read on, as a link, and the day.

- **Looked up, not checked.** The fact is grey and regular weight, with **Checked** and **Wrong** beside it. The label beside *Ask for* is a dashed outline.
- **Checked.** The fact is plain bold, the look of a permit fact, with who checked it and when. It keeps a quiet **Not right**. The label counts, as in *2 of 3 checked*.
- **Marked wrong.** The value is off the row for everyone. Its line says who marked it and when, with **Put back**.
- **The kind could not be told.** The line says so, and nothing is guessed. No tag is drawn.
- **Not found.** A line says which of the phone and the person no public business page gave.
- **Nobody looked.** As before: *Nobody looked this name up yet*, with **Google the builder**. The Google press also returns on a row with a gap.

A press writes one row and a toast says what it did. The lookups read again, so the row and the order follow.

## The tag and the order

- A looked-up kind tags the name: *Looked up: a design firm* as an outline, then *A design firm, checked* as a fill. It takes the place of the reading of the name (*May file for others*, *Could be a person*).
- **A lookup nobody checked moves no name.** The owner's choice of 2026-11-20: a trial of twenty lookups got two kinds wrong.
- **A checked builder leads**, with the names that say they build. **A checked design firm, permit service or trade waits** behind the other companies, even where its name has a building word. A checked owner stays where its name put it.
- **No lookup removes a name.** That is still an answer, or the kind on the builder's sheet.
- **The office's kind wins.** Where a kind is said on the builder's sheet, its tag shows and it alone orders. The looked-up kind still sits in the block with its page.

## How it is built

- `builderLookups.ts`, a kernel: `foldLookups` takes the newest row per name and fact and the newest press on it, and a press put back leaves the fact not checked. `lookedUpKind`, `lookupTag` and `lookupOrder` are the rules above. The words mark each value with `val`.
- `permitCallList.ts`: a row carries its `lookup`, `leads` and `waits`. `lookupCandidates` picks the names a run takes: the first on the list that do not read as one person and have no lookup yet.
- `permitMarketIo.ts`: `loadBuilderLookups` reads both tables oldest first, and reads as none before the tables are on the database. `recordLookupCheck` writes one press as the person signed in.
- `usePermitMarket`: the lookups are read once and again after each press. The list waits for them, since a checked lookup moves a name.
- `PermitAskFor.tsx` draws the block with the values-in-place pieces: one fact a line, a checked fact as a worked-out value, a fact nobody checked in its own grey.

## When a read fails

When the lookups cannot be read, the list still draws. A red line gives the reason, says no name shows who to ask for, and offers **Retry**. Each block then reads *What was looked up could not be read*, so no row claims that nobody looked.

## Decisions

- **Each fact has its own presses.** The phone is checked by calling it and the person by asking for them. One press for the whole block would mark a person checked on the strength of a phone.
- **A wrong fact leaves a line.** A value that vanished would leave a gap nobody could explain, and a slip could not be undone.
- **Put back returns a fact to not checked**, also where it was checked before it was marked wrong. The check is made again.
- **No edit in place.** A person does not retype a looked-up phone. The fact is right, or it is wrong and gone.
