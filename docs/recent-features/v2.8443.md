# v2.8443 — Bills sent: a credit's mark is whole on a phone (2026-11-24)

One fault of By day paid found on the live test of 2026-11-24 (punch list #422, problem 5). No migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

A day that holds a credit in kind and no money is marked *1 credit* in its cell (v2.8426). At 375 px a day's cell holds 33 px of words and the mark needs 37, so the live page read *1 credi*.

## What changed

- `billsPaidCellCredit(cell, phone)` takes the width. A phone's cell says the word alone, *credit*, whatever the count. A desk's cell and the printed month keep *1 credit* and *2 credits*.
- The count is left off on a phone as a paid day's *3 paid* already is. The day's box lists each credit once the day is pressed, and a screen reader still hears *1 credit in kind, not added*.
- The mark is set a little tighter on a phone, as the day's figure is.
- The guide says what a phone reads.

## Measured

Text widths in headless Chromium, the page's font at the mark's size (10.5 px): *1 credit* 37.1 px, *2 credits* 44.1 px, *credit* 29.1 px, and 27.9 px set tighter. The live test measured the room at 33 px.

## Tests

`billsPaidDays.test.ts`: the phone's word for one credit and for three, and none on a day with money. `BillsPaidByDay.render.test.tsx`: at a phone's width the cell reads *credit* and its label keeps the count. Both failed before the change with *1 credit*.

## Not looked at

The cell was not drawn inside the app at a phone's width. A phone 320 px wide has about 25 px of room, and the word would be cut there.
