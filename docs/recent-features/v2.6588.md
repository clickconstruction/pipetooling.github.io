# v2.6588 — A matter is read by theme too: the By theme tab on the Legal desk and on a person’s matter (2026-11-06)

The HR file reads its record one theme at a time (v2.6547); a matter's documents and events take marks (v2.6548) and the firm reads them grouped (v2.6549), but the office had no isolated view of a theme on a matter, and the HR file's Matter view, though its fragment said so, handed no themes to its composers. No migration. **Redeploy `legal-portal` after the merge**: it bundles the kernel this change extends, so `check:edge-drift` names it until it is current, though its behaviour is unchanged.

## What changed

- `supabase/functions/_shared/legalThemes.ts`: `matterThemeLines(entries, documents, dayOfIso)` — a matter's live documents (`doc:<id>`, dated the day the paper is about, else the day it was added; the paper is its own file) and its unvoided entries (`entry:<id>`, an event, a planned step, a statement, with the event's documents as its files and its notes under it) as `ThemeLine`s in the record's order, so `themeItems`, `nearbyLines` and `themeListText` read a matter as they read a person; `MATTER_ENTRY_KIND_WORDS`.
- `PersonThemesView` is generic over the line (`ThemeLine`, not the HR Timeline's), with `openLabel` for the door's words. `src/components/jobs/legal/MatterThemesView.tsx` wraps it for a matter: the lines from the kernel, a document chip in the window that opens the paper, Keep and Take it off… through `keepMark` / `removeMark`, *Open on the matter ›* handing the line back to the caller, the pick held in the view.
- The Legal desk: **By theme** is the seventh tab (`TABS`, `TAB_LABELS`, the address word `themes`), drawn for the hand that may mark (a dev) once the catalog has a standing theme, with the count of things marked; it reads the catalog and the matter's marks the desk already loads, Open on the matter lands on the Timeline tab or the document's Evidence row.
- The HR file's Matter view (`PersonMatterPanel`): the same **By theme** sub tab; and the composers now carry what v2.6548 promised — the catalog and the marks go to `LegalTimelinePanel`'s acts and to `LegalMatterDocumentsPanel`, Record what happened writes its marks after `legal_event_add` answers with the entry id (`legalRpcData`) and names them in the toast, the rows wear their chips. `namesById` comes down from the file for the marks' names.
- `HR_FILES.md` → Themes on the record and the guide *keep private employee files* carry the matter's view; the Legal desk's tab list in the Glossary and `legalDeskRoute` read seven tabs. The card: T2b and T4 shipped whole; the code steps done; the card closed to the load, which is the owner's.
