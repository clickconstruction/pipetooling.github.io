# v2.3484 — Sent vs today: a sent bid says both numbers (2026-09-15)

Client only — no migration, no edge function, nothing written. PR 2 of the *Frozen bid prices* to-do ([`to-dos/frozen-bid-prices/`](../../to-dos/frozen-bid-prices/README.md), landed by PR #3225); PR 1 is [v2.3481](./v2.3481.md) (the first price on a bid freezes it).

## Why

- The v2.2720 backfill (2026-09-03) froze 188 legacy bids at the book **as it stood that day**, not as it stood when each was sent, so every template edit between a bid's send date and Sept 3 is baked into its "frozen" copy. The to-do's proof: of 165 sent bids priced in the app, 105 read a different number than they were sent at (74 higher, +$437,951 in all); 90 of those were frozen on Sept 3. BP315 Prue Event Center: sent Jul 1 at $379,895.70, the grid and the letter both read $385,506.07 today — and the letter body would print the higher number.
- Not recoverable: `price_book_entries` has no history, so the per-line prices at send cannot be rebuilt. The sent **total** is safe in `bids.bid_value` (stamped from the cover-letter amount at *Mark sent*). This line puts that number beside the live one wherever the live one is read aloud.

## What shipped

- **Kernel** [`sentVsToday.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/sentVsToday.ts): `compareSentVsToday({ bidDateSent, bidValue, today })` → null (unsent, no sent value, live number not ready), `agrees` (within 1¢ — `SENT_VS_TODAY_TOLERANCE`), or `differs` with the signed delta; `sentVsTodayText(r, { where: 'grid' | 'letter', currentYear })` for the sentence; `formatSentOn` drops the year only when it is the current year (a 2025 bid reads *Sep 22, 2025*). 7 tests.
- **Pricing tab** ([`BidsPricingTab.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/BidsPricingTab.tsx)): inside the sticky summary card, after the REVENUE · PROFIT · MARGIN · MULTIPLE strip — *"Sent Jul 1 at $379,895.70 · the book prices it at $385,506.07 today (+$5,610.37)"*, amber and bold when they differ, muted when they agree (*"· the book still prices it there"*). `today` is the strip's own revenue (`effRevenue`), so the line always compares against the number directly above it.
- **Cover Letter tab** ([`BidsCoverLetterTab.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/BidsCoverLetterTab.tsx)): under the green total in the Scope & pricing card, same sentence with *"the letter would say $… today"* / *"the letter still says so"*; `today` is the letter's headline amount (custom amount, bundle total, or the ★ pricing — whatever the letter is about to print).
- **Help guide** `price-a-bid-with-the-workbench.md` → "Price from the book": one sentence on the line and why it can differ.

## Left alone

- No repair of the 90 backfilled copies (nothing to rebuild them from) and no lock on sent bids — that is PR 3 in the to-do, the owner's call. The 10 bids in the report sent *after* their copy was taken are real post-send edits or hand-typed values; the line shows them as they are.
- Multi-GC packets: the letter line reads the selected GC's headline against the bid's own `bid_value` (the bid's own GC stamps it; another GC's send rolls up only the date), which is the number the Bid Board shows too.
