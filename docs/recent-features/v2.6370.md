# v2.6370 — A job photo waits on the phone with no signal (punch list #244, PR 5) (2026-11-02)

Punch list #244's last PR, the card's call 8: the queue as the train's last PR, as #229 did for a report's photos. Until this PR a shot that could not send kept its tile with Retry and trapped the person on site.

## What changed

- **A fifth press kind, `job-photos`** (`lib/offline/outbox.ts`: `OutboxKind`, `OUTBOX_KIND_WORDS` *Job photos*, `JobPhotosPayload` with `KeptJobPhoto`, a kept report photo's facts plus the description; `keptPhotoCount` and the bar's *1 photo with it* count it). `parseOutboxPress` and the schema are unchanged.
- **The landing** (`landFieldPress.ts` → `landJobPhotos`): a job photos press never goes to the `land_field_press` RPC. Its rows already exist under the phone-minted ids (the replay sends each kept shot's bytes and row first through `sendKeptPhotos`, which now picks the job photo's own send and bucket for this kind), so landing is the same update the window makes online, the words, the stage, the caption and `added_at` on each sent row that has none yet (`landJobPhotoNotes`), under the table's own policies; a repeat changes nothing and counts once. The outcome is classed as any press's: a lost signal or a timeout fails and waits, a refusal keeps the server's reason. `RPC_KIND` names the kind for the record only.
- **The replay** (`useOutboxReplay`) sends kept photos first for any press that carries them, not only a field report; **after landing** (`afterLanding.ts`) a job photos press drops its bytes and writes the one thread line (`threadLineWords`) through the new `postThreadLine` io.
- **The shot-tile hook** (`useShotTiles`) keeps a shrunk shot's bytes beside the presses before the send (`keptPhotos.keep`); a send lost to the signal leaves the tile *Kept on the phone. It goes when the signal is back.* (`kept`), counted as ready; `queuedPhotos` hands the press the riding shots with their words; `markQueued` lets a close drop nothing; Remove and drop let the bytes go.
- **The window**: with any shot kept, the press reads *Add 1 photo to the job* as before and goes through `pressThroughOutbox` with `forceQueue`, the kind `job-photos`, the job's label and the job window as its door; the toast reads *Kept on the phone. The photos go to the job when the signal is back.* and the tiles leave the form. With none kept, today's direct path.
- The guide *add a photo to a job and find it later* gains *With no signal*; the Glossary's waiting bar and *Job photos* entries are amended.
