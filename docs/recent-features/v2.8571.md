## What was there before

A GC's own form already went onto a job: as a Form Studio form in the Contract Book, required like any paper, and the gate held the job shut on it. Nothing said it was the GC's. And the GC's page skipped a job unless a paper on it was built from settings, so a job on the GC's own form showed that GC nothing.

## What the office sees

In **Papers before this job opens**:

- **In the Add a paper list**, a form's line has a tick beside it: **This is the GC's own form**. The line is one whole press, so the tick is a control of its own beside it, a finger tall. Ticking it picks the form with the mark on: it moves up to the unsaved rows.
- **On the unsaved row**, the tick shows and can be changed. A marked row reads *The GC's own form* in place of *A form from the Contract Book*.
- **On the required form's row**, the tick is saved in place at once. That is also how the mark comes off. A toast says what it did and that nobody signs again.
- Only a form with words and no settings takes the tick (`canBeGcOwn`).

The save requires the form and then marks it, two calls. A mark that fails leaves the form required, and the section names the form and says to tick it on its row. A read of the marks that fails draws no tick on a required row, never a wrong one.

## What a GC sees

Under the job's name, a block titled **Subs sign your own form**:

- The form's name.
- *Every person (company) brings onto this job signs the form you gave us before they see the job.*
- The same signed card a paper has: the count, the meter, the gate line, and each sub business with its day.
- One line of small print: it is their form, and it does not change the agreement between the company and them.

It has no level, no topics, no **Download a sample** and no **Propose a stricter setting**: the form has no settings. A job with a paper built from settings and the GC's own form draws both under the one job name.

## The picks inside the build

Three were made by the build thread and can each be overturned:

1. **Who has signed, for an own form:** the count and each sub business with its day, the way every paper starts. There is no **What the GC sees** control for a form in this step, since that control sits in the Settings window only a paper built from settings has.
2. **The tick stays on the required form's row** as well as in the picker, so a mark is set or taken off later without taking the form off the job.
3. **A job with no GC set still takes the tick.** It shows nowhere until the job has a GC with a page.

## Why a second list

`job_papers_gc_page` answers each job with `papers` as before and a new list beside it, `own_forms`. The page's payload does the same with `ownForms`. A form is never one more entry in `papers`.

- Between the push and the function deploy, the live `customer-portal` reads the new answer. It reads `papers` and leaves a key it does not know, and it drops a job with no paper. So it draws nothing for an own form, as before the push.
- A customer's browser that still holds the older build does the same with the new payload.
- Had forms sat in `papers`, either would have drawn a form as a paper built from settings, with a sample link that fails.

## The rules, and where each is held

The database holds them (`job_paper_set_gc_own`, the table's check):

- Only the office marks a form, never a twin, an archived account or training.
- Only a form with no settings carries the mark. The setter refuses any other paper in words, and a check on the table holds it whatever writes the row.
- The mark changes no form. It is set and taken off in place whoever has signed.
- A form taken off the job is not read, marked or not.
