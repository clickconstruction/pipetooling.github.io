# v2.5253 — Messages: the boards read where every company stands, by step, by trade and by company (2026-10-12)

Wave 1 of the Messages build (`to-dos/gc-mode/messages/BUILD_HELPERS.md` on `spike/gc-mode`), cluster 6, first of three kernels. A pure kernel and its test: no table, no screen, no edge function, no migration.

**What it lifts.** The prototype's boards as of v1.178 (`prototype.html`; `MESSAGES_PLAN.md` → Prototype log v1.172 to v1.174): `boardLines`, `boardSummary` and `boardSummaryHtml` (the tiles), `boardHtml`'s six pieces (the tiles, empty steps folded, chips by job, days against the usual, the row's press, the job and seat pickers), `byTradeHtml`, `companyTableHtml`, `boardPasses`, `boardFilters` and `personOn`. These become `src/lib/flows/board.ts`: `boardLines`, `lineDayWords`, `boardSummary`, `tileJobWords`, `boardRows`, `rowHeadParts`, `rowPress`, `boardPasses`, `boardFilterOptions`, `byTrade`, `companyTable`, `onStep`, `phaseOf`, `dueWords`, `lapsedWords`, `landsWords`. The test runs over `src/lib/flows/boardFixtures.ts`, the prototype's demo people at its clock (Fri Oct 2 2026).

**The input is places, not state.** The board reads one `StepPlace` per company per step it is on now or lapsed, one per job in the per-job lane. `flows/state.ts` makes them with `placesOf` (v2.5254, the flow's types, which this stacks on). The flows are `FlowDef`s, and the fixtures use `flows/seed.ts`. The paper words and whether a paper is ours come from `flows/facts.ts`. The prototype's `boardLines` had three paths: the per-job map, a per-job step on the default job, and the Once lane. That was because its state lived in three shapes. Here it is one path.

**Changed from the prototype, and why** (numbered as in the cluster's prepare report to the lead, who took the calls):

1. *Every read takes the job.* The prototype read the panel's job picker (`laneJob`) inside its state reads. Places carry their job, so no read here depends on what a panel shows.
2. *Numbers in, words out.* The prototype parsed "late 3 days" back into a number and dated every lapsed line Sep 30. A place carries `lateDays` and `since` (the day the paper ran out, on a lapsed one).
3. *The tiles count companies, never lines.* A company with two asks is one company and one dot, colored by its worst line. The tiles read each step's own phase (`FlowStep.phase`: setup, pricing, building, seeded in `flows/seed.ts`), where the prototype listed step ids. Change orders sit under building: the prototype left them out of every tile. Needs a hand counts each company once, by its worst line, so its pills add up: late plus lapsed is the count.
4. *A day still ahead is on time, never over usual.* Over usual is a watch in orange. It colors a chip and a dot and is counted under its step. It never counts toward Needs a hand, so never toward Follow up.
5. *The usual is an input* (`BoardContext.usual`, from `flows/going.ts`). A step with none yet (under five done) has nothing over it.
7. *The row's press is keyed off fact ids*, which are the code's list and never change. Step ids are the office's to edit. "Invite a company" appears where the step reads `invite_sent` or `portal_opened`, "Bill day" on `draw_paid`, and "Open the compare" on `carried`. "Ask" is for a step that waits only on papers they give us (a fact in `flows/facts.ts` with a paper that is not ours), and "Remind" is for the rest. The person's name is used when there is one ("Remind Greg"), in place of the prototype's list of company suffixes. What lands on an empty step stays seeded words, and a step the editor adds reads "Nobody on it."
10. *A company with two trades on its record* (`gc_companies.trades` is a list) sits under each. *The architect's flow has no Once lane*, so By company draws no track for it, where the prototype titled an empty column "Per job".

Also changed:
- A company waiting on someone else's step is left out of the row's nudge. Elena's pay application waits on Jonah's certificate, so nobody presses "Remind Elena". Follow up's nudge rows already skip it.
- A loop that repeats by the calendar counts its rows, not months. The pay application reads "1", not "1 month". Addendum counts as "2 addenda".
- By trade shows a company's late step before the step it is setting up on, so a late quote is never hidden. Its words follow the same order as its color, so late and red never read "insurance expired". Its chips read over usual, like the board's.
- A line whose day the app does not know says nothing, not "today".

**The fixtures** read the prototype's demo people as places, written by hand in `StepPlace`'s shape, so the test exercises the board and not the reading. Elena's pay application stands on Stone Oak Pharmacy, the job being built, where the prototype drew it on her default job, a bid. Tejas Power's "Electrical, low voltage" is two trades. The two customers with no job in the prototype each get a job with their own name, which tests the rule that leaves such a job unnamed. Once `flows/flowFixtures.ts` lands, one test will run `readFlow` → `placesOf` → the board over the same people.
