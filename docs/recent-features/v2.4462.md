# v2.4462 — Lien waivers: a waiver signed or sent in the evening keeps its day (2026-10-02)

One of the instant-day fixes that follow [v2.4452](v2.4452.md). Client only: no migration, no edge function. The customer portal's own waiver days were fixed server-side in [v2.4456](v2.4456.md).

## What was wrong

Our lien waivers to the GC cut days out of `timestamptz` columns by their first ten characters, so anything stamped after 7 pm CDT (6 pm CST) read as the next day:

- **The waiver chip on a bill** ([`lienWaiverCell.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienWaiverCell.ts), `halfFor`): *Conditional ✓ sent Oct 3* from `job_lien_releases.sent_to_customer_at`, and the signed, asked and issued days from `signed_at`, `signature_requested_at`, `minted_at` and `created_at`. The Bill tab's Invoices list, the bill paperwork card and the GC review read it.
- **The waiver window** ([`LienReleaseModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienReleaseModal.tsx)): a row under *Already on this job*, *Asked for his signature …* and step 6's *Sent*. The issued line on the Bill tab's strip ([`BillCustomerLienReleaseStrip.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/BillCustomerLienReleaseStrip.tsx)) did the same.
- **The Dashboard's unconditional queue** ([`lienReleaseTracking.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienReleaseTracking.ts), `buildLienUnconditionalQueue`): *Issued* from the release's `created_at`, and *Cleared* from a payment's `created_at` when it has no `paid_on`.
- **The amount math** ([`lienWaiverAmountMath.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienWaiverAmountMath.ts)): a bill's *billed* day from `billed_at` (else `created_at`), and a payment's day from `created_at` when it has no `paid_on` or `sent_on`, through the file's `ymd` helper.
- **A new waiver's through date** ([`lienWaiverRelease.ts`](../../src/lib/jobsDocuments/lienWaiverRelease.ts), `buildLienWaiverPrefill`): the last bill's `billed_at` (else `created_at`), through `ymdFromIso`. That date prints on the statutory form.

The waiver's own *signed on* line in the amount math and the signed foot on the page already read `signed_at` in Central, so a chip and the page could disagree by a day.

## What changed

Each instant reads `calendarYmdInAppTzFromIso(iso)` from [`dateUtils.ts`](../../src/utils/dateUtils.ts) (`APP_CALENDAR_TZ`). The `date` columns keep their slice: `paid_on`, `sent_on` and `last_work_date`. The two files' local helpers now say they are for date columns.

## Tests

- `lienWaiverCell.test.ts` (+1): a waiver sent at `2026-10-03T00:30:00+00:00` (7:30 pm CDT) reads Oct 2 and *Conditional ✓ sent Oct 2*; signed then reads Oct 2; asked at `2026-12-02T00:30:00Z` (6:30 pm CST) reads Dec 1; a draft made at noon UTC reads its own day.
- `lienReleaseTracking.test.ts` (+1): the queue's *Issued* and the *Cleared* fallback read the Central day; a `paid_on` still wins as stored.
- `lienWaiverAmountMath.test.ts` (+1): an evening bill's billed day and a payment's `created_at` fallback.
- `lienWaiverRelease.test.ts` (+1): the through date from an evening `billed_at`, from a winter evening `created_at`, and at noon UTC.
- `LienReleaseModal.steps.render.test.tsx`: job 650's signed waiver, made at 9:17 pm CDT on Sep 30 (`02:17` UTC on Oct 1), reads *September 30, 2026* under *Already on this job*.

All fail on main. One fixture stamped a payment at `2026-09-03T04:00:00Z`, 11 pm CDT the evening before, and pinned the UTC day; it moves to midday and keeps its expectation.

## Checked against prod

Read only, from the worktree dev server: of the 21 waivers, the 3 live ones carry no evening stamp, and 3 voided ones do. 2 payments have no `paid_on`. Nothing live reads differently today.
