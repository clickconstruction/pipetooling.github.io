# v2.6952 — Lien affidavit: the county blank at the top says *where signed* (2026-11-11)

Punch list #314, the first small piece. The office signed and notarized its first batch of short-form affidavits on 2026-11-11, and every sheet had the property's county written in the blank above *BEFORE ME*, with the notary's own block below correctly naming the county they signed in. The form leaves that blank for the venue, the county where the paper is sworn (punch list #305: the property's county is in paragraph 2), and nothing on the page said so.

## What changed

- **`AFFIDAVIT_VENUE_NOTE`**, *where signed*, in `src/lib/jobsDocuments/lienFilingDocuments.ts`. The `jurisdiction` block takes an optional `note`, drawn in parentheses after a blank county on the county's own line: in the HTML (`data-county-note`, a little smaller than the caption), in the text twin and in the PDF. A named county draws no note.
- **`buildShortLienAffidavitBlocks`** hands the note to its top venue. The paper keeps its height, so the short form still prints on one sheet.
- The notary's venue blank in the jurat is unchanged: a notary knows which county that is, and the first batch had it right.

## Not changed

- The earlier ten-paragraph form prints the property's county there, as it was drafted.
- No sworn statement changes. The wording of a sworn paper is counsel's to confirm; this says only which county the blank wants.

`lienFilingDocuments.test.ts` (the short form's text whole, the note once in the HTML on the county's line), `lienFilingDocuments.pdf.test.ts` (the note on the top venue's line, the jurat's blank plain). The one-sheet print test still passes.
