# v2.8203 — A place holds more than one photo, and a buried line carries its length (2026-11-21)

Punch list #413, steps 6 and 7, on the columns of v2.8195.

## Why

One photo shows a trench open. It does not show the same ground a week later, when the next person has to find the line under sod. And a buried line is a line: where it starts, where it ends and how long it is are what a locator, a landscaper or the next plumber asks for. The app can already walk a line and say its length. It could not tie that line to the place that says what is buried.

## What ships

**More than one photo** (step 7). A place keeps up to twelve photos after its first.

- On the place sheet, once the place has its first photo: **＋ Another photo**, 48 px tall. Each one is shrunk on the phone as the first is, and shows as a small picture with **Remove** under it.
- One press saves the place and sends the new photos. If one does not send, what went up for that save is taken back out and nothing is written.
- In the list, the extra photos sit under the place's words, each opened large on a press.
- A photo taken off a place is taken out of the bucket.
- The row's list is written only when the person changed it, so a phone still on an older build that saves the place does not empty it.

**A buried line with its length** (step 6). On a buried place in a list, for whoever set it and for the office:

- **＋ Walk this line** and **＋ Draw this line** start a painted map of its own on the job, named for the place, opening on the place's dot. A job's list of places holds places only, so the line cannot live on the list.
- When that map closes, its first line is tied to the place (`painted_map_places.line_id`), and the list opens again on the place. A map closed with no line saved ties nothing.
- The place's row then says the length the way every line does, *About 60 ft, give or take 10 ft · walked*, with **Open the line**.
- The line is drawn in the color a locator paints for what is buried, on its own map and on any map of the job that draws it. That finishes the first of Taunya's three changes, *its line and its mark draw in that color*.

## Not done here

- **A shared link shows a place's first photo only.** The link's function names the columns it hands on, and the extra photos are not among them.
- **A line read on Google's map is drawn in the paint's own color.** Only the old map draws a line in a color of its own.
- **Take the line off** is not a press yet. Removing the line's map, or the line, leaves the place with no line.
- A caption on each extra photo is not asked for. The row can hold one.
- Not walked on a signed-in phone.
