# v2.7834 — Materials on site: answer a press that should not stand (2026-11-18)

The fifth PR of the train on seeing what one person does with the material on a site. Client only. It reads and writes the table and function of `20261118170000_on_site_press_answers.sql` (v2.7827), so that migration is pushed before this deploys.

## What landed

- **Pick and answer.** For a dev the By person look grows a tick box on each ledger press with no answer, and **Answer N presses…** under the list. A box move or a box change takes no answer.
- **The window** ([`PressAnswerWindow.tsx`](../../src/components/materials/PressAnswerWindow.tsx)): one pick a press, offered by kind (`suggestedAnswer`: a second count for a count, a mark for material that left, a take-back for a part added), a panel of what the take-backs would do to the numbers (`takeBackPlan`, the ledger summed with `containerLines` as it stands and without the rows picked, job by job), a press that moves no number said so, one reason, and a press that names each act with its count (`answerPressWords`). It writes one answer at a time, keeps going past one that fails and names it with the database's reason, and the press that failed stays ticked.
- **A second count** hands one task to the New task window, one at a time. More than one is said, and the rest stay ticked.
- **Read by the office.** Each answered press wears *taken back by* or *marked not allowed by* with the reason. A press taken back is read from `on_site_taken_back_rows`, drawn struck through, and is in no figure. The pieces marked are counted under the pieces taken or used.
- **The way back** (`AnswerWithdrawWindow`): **Let it stand…** or **Take the mark off…**, each with its own reason. The answer stays on record as withdrawn.
- After a take-back or its withdrawal the desk reads the job's ledger again (`loadJob`, forced), so every figure on the screen moves at once.
- **Who.** The mount passes `canAnswerPresses` for a dev alone. The database holds the same line: only a dev's insert or update passes the table's policies.

## The limits

- The answers are read newest first, at most 1,000 live ones. They are rare; a site with more has a different problem.
- The tally of marked material is in pieces. Dollars wait for a price on each line.
- No dev seat verb yet: a session answers a press through the app.

## Tests

`sitePersonActs.test.ts` (an answer on its press, a press taken back drawn once and in no figure), `pressAnswers.test.ts`, and five new cases in `SitePeopleView.render.test.tsx`: a dev picks, sees the number a take-back gives and writes each answer with the reason; a refused answer keeps its press ticked; the office sees the answers and the struck row with no press to change them; a dev lets a press stand with a reason.
