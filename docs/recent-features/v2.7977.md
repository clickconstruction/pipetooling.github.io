# v2.7977 — Papers: the ground for an outside signer, a paper names a contact and says who signs (2026-11-19)

Punch list #403, PR 1 of seven: the ground. A migration, a kernel and a bed. No screen, no client call and no edge function change. Grace's word to build was given on 2026-11-19, over the owner's seven settled calls.

## What is new

- **A paper can name the party it is with.** `person_contract_documents` gains `party_contact_id`, the `contacts` row of a customer, a person at one, a supply house, a rep, a trade company or someone on no list. `party_name` freezes the party's name on the paper.
- **A paper can keep who signs.** `signer_name`, `signer_title` and `signer_email` are the signer as the office sends the paper. `signing_for` is the business they sign for, in words. Nothing writes them yet: the second PR writes them on every send.
- **A party's paper is filed under a name no person can have**, `party:<contact id>`. A check holds the shape both ways, and the name trigger leaves such a paper with no roster person. A supply house named like a teammate never lands on the teammate.
- **A fold carries the paper.** `contacts_fold_papers` runs with the spine's `contacts_fold`, so Who is this?, a customer merge and a record deleted by a screen all leave the paper with the contact that stays.
- **A door for the party's contact.** `paper_party_contact` finds the contact of a record, and makes one when the record has none, for the four roles that send a paper.
- **Documents → Agreements can answer the new lines.** The `agreements_on_file` view gives a party's paper one of four kinds, the record's own name and who signs. No party's paper exists yet, so the list reads as it did.
- **The estimator reads an outside company's agreement.** One new read policy. See the next section.
- **The e-sign ledger takes the audience `outside`**, for a signer who is neither a customer nor a sub.

## Who reads what

The owner's calls 4 and 7. The office's four roles read every paper, as before. An estimator now reads an agreement that is with an outside company: a trade company's own paper, or a party's paper whose contact is a customer, a supply house or a trade company.

The estimator still reads none of these:

- A paper filed under a person: a staff paper, a sub's own, a partner's, a person's copy of a job's paper.
- A party on no list, until **Add to a list** says what they are.
- A W-9, a certificate or any other kind that is not an agreement.

A digital twin reads as an estimator and reads none. A primary, a superintendent and a sub read none, as before. The beds hold every one of these.

## Two readings the owner should see

Both are narrower than the card's plan read. Each is one policy line or one gate to widen, and neither has any effect today, because the estimator has no screen that lists these papers.

- **Agreements only.** The card's plan said the estimator reads an outside company's paper. On 2026-10-10 the Board's call kept a trade company's W-9 and certificate to their states for the office, because the row carries the tax number's last four, the answers' PDF and the signer's details (`gc_company_paper_states`). The policy keeps that call: the kind `agreement` alone.
- **A signed form's PDF.** The card expected the estimator's rule to be copied into `open-contract-form-pdf`. That function opens a signed form for a dev, the controller and a pay-approved master. It refuses an assistant, who reads the row. Copying the rule would let an estimator open a file an assistant cannot. The gate is unchanged.

## The kernel

`src/lib/contracts/paperParty.ts`, pure, with 21 tests:

- The stored name: `partyPaperName`, `isPartyPaperName`, `partyContactIdFromPaperName`.
- What the party is: `paperPartyKind` reads the view's `facts.party_kind`, `partyAgreementKind` gives the view's kind, `paperPartyTag` gives the tag (*Customer*, *Supply house*, *Trade company*, *Not on a list*), and `partyNeedsAList` says when **Add to a list** belongs on a line.
- Who signs: `paperSignerLine` (*Lena Park signs, Branch manager*, then *signed by Dana Ruiz, Project manager*), `paperSaysLine` (*Lena Park, Branch manager, signing for Tallgrass Supply Co.*), `signingForOf` and `signingForTail`.
- Three tests read the migration and fail when the kernel's kinds, party words or prefix leave it.

## Choices worth knowing

- **Four kinds, not three.** The plan named three for a customer, a supply house and a trade company. The owner's call 6 added a party on no list, which is the fourth, `outside_agreement`.
- **The line's name is the record's own name today**, and the frozen `party_name` is what stands when the record is gone. The bed deletes a supply house and reads its papers back under the frozen name.
- **A trade company's `company_id` papers are untouched.** A party's paper whose contact is a trade company is its own kind, `trade_agreement`, so the GC page's papers and this card's stay apart until the card's fifth and sixth steps.
- **The helper and the door are definers**, so each refuses a training account, a digital twin and an archived account itself, behind the row fences.
- **No signer address in the view's facts.** The list does not need it, so the view does not hand it out.
