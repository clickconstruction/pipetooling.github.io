# v2.5369 — Lien desk: the rules behind a run that shows its steps, behind the scenes (run train, PR 1) (2026-10-12)

The first of the run train (`docs/DOCSMIT_LIEN_RUN_PLAN.md`, *The run, shown not told*; approved from the before-and-after page on 2026-10-12, decisions D16 six nodes, D17 the office's cards show Sign here, D18 the live checklist is for the phone and the desk). Kernels only; the run window is untouched until Run-2.

- **`runEnvelopeStage.ts`**: one envelope's place on its way out. `runLaneOf(method)` (certified, courier, hand, email); `runStageLabels(lane)` (a hand delivery is *Handed over*, an email is *Emailed*); `runEnvelopeProblems(env)` (the run's own `runNoticeProblems` words, a recipient-named problem belonging to its envelope only); `runEnvelopeSigned(env)`; `runEnvelopeStage(env, { printed, recorded?, delivered? })` → the lane, the stages in order (Built, Checked, Signed, Printed, Mailed, Recorded, and Delivered once a provider says), the first stage not done, why it waits, and the glyph (open, sealed, postmarked, delivered). An email copy has no Printed stage and is mailed when the run records; a number typed before the print does not count as mailed.
- **`runJourney.ts`**: the six nodes across the top (Built, Signed, Sent, In the mail, Recorded, Delivered) with `n of total` counts, the first node not done as *now* or *blocked* (the blocker's note on it: *2 wait on Robert*, *1 has no address*), and the fill ratio.
- **`runFootAction.ts`**: the one button at the foot: *print* what can print and say who waits; *record* once numbers are in (*1 stays in the pile*); *wait* while numbers are owed or everyone waits on the leader; *done* when all are recorded; *nothing* for an empty run. Two status lines each.
- **`runChecklist.ts`**: the printed checklist sheet's rows for the window: one per printed paper envelope with the lane's three boxes (*stuffed · slip on · mailed*, *in the truck · handed over*, *label on · picked up*), the number box's placeholder, and whether the row is done; `runChecklistWords` (*1 of 2 has their number*).

Twelve tests in `runJourney.test.ts` cover the lanes, the holds, the glyphs, the journey's counts and fill, the foot's five kinds and the checklist. No screen changes.
