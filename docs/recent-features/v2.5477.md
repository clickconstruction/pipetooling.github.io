# v2.5477 — Messages: a New list on top for what still needs sorting (2026-10-13)

Residential R7, PR 3: the New lane and the sort card (mockup A of the customer Messages prototype v0.8, screen 7), on
the kernel (`followUp/newLane.ts`, v2.5394) and R7's `messages.tied_by` (v2.5425) and R6's `messages.mailbox` (v2.5428),
both on prod. No migration and no edge function. Sort all (mockup C) is a later PR on top.

- **New, first on Follow up** (`FollowUpSections.tsx`, on `/gc?view=followUp` and `/messages`):
  - One row per person who needs **Who is this?**, **Which job?** or **Who has it?**, the longest waiting first.
  - Each row's press opens the thread at the line. The row keeps the who-has-it pill.
  - The queue builds it from the people Needs a reply holds (`FollowUpPassThrough.newLane`, `followUpNewLane` in
    `followUpRows.ts`), and they leave Needs a reply. Each person is still counted once on the badge.
  - The kind pills narrow it (`queueForPill`).
- **What it reads** (`followUpIo.ts`):
  - Every line carries `mailbox, tied_by`.
  - `loadQueue` reads the open people's `contact_jobs`, then their jobs' states.
  - `activeJobKeys` in the new `followUp/newLaneRows.ts`: a bid not won or lost, a GC project not closed or lost, a
    Pipeline job waiting, working, ready to bill or billed.
- **The sort card**: the thread window's new `aboveLines` slot (`threadSlots.tsx`, `ThreadWindow.tsx`), filled by
  `useNewLaneThreadSlots.tsx` over `newLaneCardIo.ts`. It asks the same rule as the lane (`laneRowFor`), so the card and
  the row agree.
  - **Who is this?**: `whoGuesses` over the customers with the line's last name and the trade partners on its domain.
    A guess with its record, or a new lead (R0's `name_contact` with no record, `nameAsLead`), names the number in one
    press, and a fold moves the window to the thread it kept. A new customer, a new trade partner, a supply house and a
    candidate wait with where to make the record (`guessWait`). **Someone else** opens the window's **Who is this?**.
  - **Which job?**: one pick, since a line sits on one job (`messages_one_job`), the starred job picked. **Keep it on**
    or **Move it to** writes `tie_message`, which stamps `tied_by`, so the line leaves the lane.
  - **Who has it?**: the office team, *Who gets a new message*'s pick first (`rulePick` over `whoGetsIt`: who answered
    last, else the seat for their kind), through `take_it` or `hand_to`. A tech's hold from R5's Job Mode already puts
    someone on the conversation, so it never reaches the lane.
- **The badge**: **Messages** in the header shows `follow_up_count` (`MessagesBadge.tsx`, read on demand and on focus).
  New's people are among those counted.
- `messagesIo.ts`: `loadJobTilesAndActive` (the tiles and the active jobs from one read) and `nameAsLead`.
- **Guide**: `sort-new-messages` (new). `see-who-is-waiting-on-us-and-what-we-owe-them` and
  `answer-everyone-waiting-on-us-from-one-page` now say four lists.
- **Docs**: `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`'s Messages paragraph and `APP_DIRECTORY.md`'s `/messages`.
- **Tests**:
  - `newLaneRows.test.ts`, plus `queue`, `followUpRows`, `followUpIo` and `kindPills`.
  - Smokes: `FollowUpPanel` (New first, its people out of Needs a reply), `NewLaneSlot` (the three sorts and their
    presses) and `MessagesBadge`.
