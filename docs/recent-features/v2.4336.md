# v2.4336 — People → Users → Account: real sign-in times and training boxes (2026-10-01)

PR A of the *Account on the desk* train (Grace, 2026-10-01; canvas `UC4ZYvy8qDy3XLsz41TCM3`). Found on the live look at `/people?tab=users&lens=account`: every account read *never signed in* while the desk header said *signed in today*. Client only, no migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

The Account lens ([v2.3702](v2.3702.md)) reads `read_only` and `last_sign_in_at`, and `usePeopleRoster` selects both, but [`buildUsersTabKindRoster`](../../src/components/people/peopleUsersTabShared.ts) rebuilt each account row without them, and the Devs group's own map did the same. `lensCellsFor` then cast the row back to `UserRow` and read `undefined`. So the Sign-in cell said *never signed in* for everyone, and the Training box was unticked for everyone. That second part had not bitten yet only because nobody on the roster was in training mode: a trainee's box would have looked off, and the lens could never have switched it off.

## The change

- **The two fields ride through** `buildUsersTabKindRoster` and the Devs map; `PeopleUsersTab` reads them with an `in` check instead of the cast.
- **[`lensSignInLabel`](../../src/lib/people/usersTabLens.ts)** — the desk's `describeLastSeen` without the words the column header already says: *today*, *12d ago*, *2mo ago*, *never*. The exact time is the cell's hover title.
- **[`UsersTabRow`](../../src/components/people/UsersTabRow.tsx)** drops the *needs supervision* / *can run a job* chip whenever a lens draws columns (the Account lens's Supervision column says it; the Pay lens is about pay). That chip was what pushed helper and sub rows off the right edge at 768: nine ⋯ buttons sat past it and the page scrolled sideways.
- **[`UsersTabAccountCells`](../../src/components/people/UsersTabLensCells.tsx)** — a roster row with no login shows one muted dash under Sign-in (hover: the ⋯ menu invites them) instead of a second *no login* beside the row's own chip, and its empty cells keep the columns lined up.

## Verified

- `usersTabLens.test.ts` (5, the label's four words) and `PeopleUsersTab.render.test.tsx` (2: the Account lens shows *12d ago* / *today* / *never*, a trainee's box ticked, the chip gone and the column's *needs* there).
- Live on prod data, the worktree dev server as Robert: the lens reads Taunya *today*, Malachi *1d ago*, Kyle *7mo ago*; at 768×1024 the page is 768 wide and no ⋯ button sits off the edge. Nothing was changed on prod.
