# v2.5211 — GC mode: the customer sees their schedule in their portal (2026-10-10)

PR 15b of the schedule's real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/schedule-pr15b.md` on branch `spike/gc-mode`, amendments 1 to 3), co-signed by gc 3 (Portal, every call), gc 5 (Owner Billing, calls 4, 7 and 8), and gc 4 for the Building lane (the weekly report's line). Cut after #5362 (14b), #5374 (P5d-ii-1) and #5373 (`gcJobsPortalUrl`). No migration. **Redeploys:** `customer-portal`, `gc-customer-email` and `gc-trade-portal`.

## The change

- **The copy of the kernels grows** (call 1): `scripts/edge-kernels.mjs` gains three entries, `customerSchedule.ts` (`customerSchedulePicture`, `customerBarWords`), `lateFinish.ts` and `changeOrderOfficeRows.ts` (`withOfficeChangeOrders`), and `customer-portal` joins `IMPORTERS`. The generator writes 51 files and 13,528 lines, the index aside, against main's 43 and 12,107 after the Board's vi-b. It adds eight: `customerSchedule`, `gantt`, `changeOrderDays`, `lateFinish`, `ownerBillingFinish`, `timeExtension`, `daysLost` and `changeOrderOfficeRows`.
- **One read, shared** (call 4, gc 3's amendment): `readScheduleRows` moves from `gc-trade-portal/index.ts` into `_shared/gcScheduleRead.ts`, as P5d-ii-1 left it. Each audience's extra rows sit behind an option:
  - `{ trade: { companyId } }` keeps P5d-ii-1's own reads as they were (its tells, answers, notices, counts, marks and parts);
  - `{ customer: { customerId } }` reads the dates to meet, the waits and their holds, the change orders by the office view's twelve columns from the table (so no price is read at all, which is stronger than call 4's *read and dropped*), and the customer's name;
  - the moves and their pushes are read for either.
- **The answer is words and dates only** (call 2): `_shared/gcCustomerSchedule.ts`'s `customerPortalSchedule` copies each field by name from the lifted picture. A bar as the office draws it carries its company, its line's worth, its trade package and its spare days, and a date to meet carries its company. None is copied.
- **Which jobs** (call 3): `customerPortalJobs`, the customer's own jobs being built with a schedule drawn.
- **`customer-portal`** (call 6): for a customer with a GC job of ours, it loads `gcCustomerSchedule.ts` with a dynamic `import()` and lays `schedule` on each job (`loadCustomerSchedules`). A failed read leaves the job as before. `gcPortal.ts` names the type only, so its other importers never load the copy.
- **The page** (call 7): `PortalGcSchedule.tsx`, the prototype's `GcCustomerSchedule` drawn from the answer, opens each job being built, above our contract and the change orders. It shows the finish, the work done, the next date to meet, the stages on one axis with Today, the dates to meet, **See every bar** for a GC or an owner's rep, the contract's days and the late finish's words, what changed this week and what we need from them. It is light, on the portal's paper. `parsePortalGcSchedule` keeps only the answer's fields, and drops a broken schedule.
- **The portal line** (call 8): `gc-customer-email` turns on *You can see your schedule in your portal:* for `schedule` and `weekly`, through `gcCustomerEmailPortalWords`. It uses gc 5's `gcJobsPortalUrl`, and comes on only on a job with a schedule drawn and never on a weekly report that copies the architect (`gcCustomerEmailPortalOn`, Building's two conditions). It lands after the row's own sign-off and never offers a card.
- **What customers see** (call 9): the sample owner's job carries a schedule. A new step, *GC mode: their schedule in their portal*, points at the sample portal. Both email samples show the new line.

## Proof on the local edge runtime (call 6)

On `edge-runtime` v1.70.0 with only `supabase/functions` mounted, a throwaway function's dynamic import of `gcCustomerSchedule.ts` loaded in about 31 ms and worked out a two-trade job in 6 ms, with no company, dollar or *spare* in the answer. `customer-portal`'s cold boot to the sample owner's answer was about 1.68 s with the dynamic import and 1.70 s with a static one, so a homeowner is spared the copy's load and little else. Whether the deploy bundler keeps a dynamically imported file is checked at the deploy (`check_edge_boot`, then a GC customer's portal); if it does not, the import goes static.
