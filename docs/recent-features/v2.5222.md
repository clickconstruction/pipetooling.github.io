# v2.5222 — The Bridge: the people tables fold under one strip (2026-10-10)

PR 6 of the Bridge's redraw (plan and mock-up at https://claude.ai/artifact/2GciUJPCjaTysjpTEULWyJ), the last of the page's own six. Client only, dev only. No migration, no edge function.

- **What was wrong**: Vectors (one pay week, eleven rows) and Vectors by the day (a month grid) stood between the Truth check and the cash forecast, a screen and a half of per-person detail on a page whose job is the company's three questions; and the page closed on a 90-word *How it's counted* paragraph that the guide already holds.
- **The strip** (`src/lib/bridge/peopleStrip.ts`, `BridgePeople.tsx`): both panels sit under **People — who moved the number**, closed by default; **Show the rows and the days** opens it and **Hide the rows** closes it, the choice kept in `localStorage` (`bridge_people_open_v1`, `readPeopleOpen` / `writePeopleOpen`). While closed, `peopleSummary` reads the pay week in one line: the top two on approved hours (by contribution, first names) and how many more, the people with hours waiting, billed and collected, and what was collected on nobody's row; *reading…* until Vectors' inputs are in.
- **The page** (`Bridge.tsx`): the day grid's month is read only while the strip is open (the effect gates on `peopleOpen`; the cache keeps a month once read), and the footer is one line — *How it's counted is in the guide* with the door to `/help/the-bridge` — plus the window's five totals.
- **Tests**: `peopleStrip.test.ts` (3: the summary's parts and order, the no-hours and loading cases, the remembered state) and `BridgePeople.render.test.tsx` (1: closed, open, closed).
