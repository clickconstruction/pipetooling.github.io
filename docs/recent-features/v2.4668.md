# v2.4668 — Job Parts Tally: a purchase's jobs come from the day it was bought (2026-10-06)

Punch list #72 (`to-dos/tally-transactions-refresh.md`), the follow-up PR 2a (v2.4654) found. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

Mercury creates a card transaction at the swipe (`raw.createdAt`) and posts it when it settles (`posted_at`): a median 8.2 hours later, overnight, and on a later company day for two charges in five. Every window that offers a purchase's jobs read the day from `posted_at`, so for those charges it offered the next day's clocked and scheduled jobs. Over the last 90 days, where the swipe day and the posting day each had one clocked job and the two differed, the office chose the posting day's job 48 times and the swipe day's 6. About 48 charges in 90 days may sit on the next day's job; the office's fix-up of those waits on the owner (read-only list, no code).

A second defect in Sort mode: `fetchSortModeDayJobs` pushed the clocked jobs of the day before, the day and the day after in database order, so the day before's job could lead.

## What changed

- **[`src/lib/mercurySwipeTime.ts`](../../src/lib/mercurySwipeTime.ts)** — the one rule: `mercurySwipeAtIso(raw, postedAt)` is `raw.createdAt` when `isMercuryIsoInstant` takes it (the ISO shape and a real day and time, the same test the database applies to `list_card_charges_window.purchased_at`, v2.4665), else `posted_at`; `mercurySwipeDayYmd`; `mercuryPostedOnALaterDay`. The team queue's `tallyChargeMadeAt` (v2.4654) now reads through it.
- **The Assign window** ([`MercuryTransactionAllocationsModal`](../../src/components/MercuryTransactionAllocationsModal.tsx), tally mode only, the holder's own card and the office follow-up): "Jobs on their schedule that day" and "Their clock sessions that day" are read for the swipe day, and *today* means the swipe day. The summary's first column reads **Bought** with the swipe day; a charge that posted on a later day adds *Posted Wed, Sep 16* under it, so the sorter sees which day the picks are for. Banking's own use of the window is unchanged.
- **Sort mode** ([`TallySortPurchaseModal`](../../src/components/tally/TallySortPurchaseModal.tsx) → [`fetchSortModeDayJobs`](../../src/lib/tally/fetchSortModeDayJobs.ts)): the day jobs are the swipe day ±1, ordered by [`orderSortModeDayJobIds`](../../src/lib/tally/sortModeDayJobsOrder.ts): the swipe day's clocked jobs (in clock-in order), its scheduled jobs, then the day before's and the day after's, clocked before scheduled. The screen reads *Bought Tuesday Aug 4. Your jobs from the day before through the day after.*
- **The own-card table's Posted button** ([`TallyClockWindowAllocateModal`](../../src/components/tally/TallyClockWindowAllocateModal.tsx), prop `swipeAtIso`): its three days centre on the swipe day.
- The lists keep their **Posted** column (the own-card table, the Team purchases rows): there it is a true fact, and the tables sort by it. The date floor, the over-2-days count and the staff queue stay on `posted_at`.

## Found while reading, for PR 4

A card charge stays `pending`, with no `posted_at`, until Mercury settles it, and the date floor drops a row with no `posted_at`. Over the 90 days, 337 of the 585 purchases made before that day's clock-out (58%) were still pending when the holder clocked out; 7 of 32 recent card rows ended `failed`. The clock-out sheet can only show most of a day's purchases if it shows pending charges, so the to-do's PR 4 now says so and waits on the owner.
