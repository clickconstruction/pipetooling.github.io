# v2.7585 — Lien desk: the Lien affidavits list moved into its own files (2026-11-17)

The third cut of `src/components/jobs/LienDesk.tsx`, after the By date view (v2.7571) and the Retainage list (v2.7583). No behaviour changes.

**The hook.** `useLienDeskAffidavits` in `src/components/jobs/useLienDeskAffidavits.ts` holds 140 lines of the component as they stood: what the list shows and which row is open, the held-only narrowing, and the three batch presses over the list. Those are redraw every ready paper on the short form, send every drafted one for approval, and print every ready one for the notary with its help sheet and notice records. Five state values that only this view read moved in with it: the two presses in flight, their two failed lists and the phone's print-box fold.

**The list.** `LienDeskAffidavitList` in `src/components/jobs/LienDeskAffidavitList.tsx` holds the 180 lines of JSX as they stood: the three batch boxes, the held-only strip, then each pile under its sticky title with one row a job. It takes the hook's result whole and holds no state of its own, so nothing is lost while the list is off screen.

**What stayed, and why.** The pick (`affSelectedJobId`), the pressed pile and the held-only press stay in the desk, because To do's rows, the Board's prints and the title bar's chips set them. The desk still calls `useLienPileHeads` for the list and hands it in, so the lit title is kept while the list is off screen. The pane was already its own component and its mount is unchanged.

**Shared button style.** `btn` and `FILL` were private to the desk and the list's presses use them, so they are `lienBtnStyle` and `LIEN_FILL` in `src/components/jobs/lienDeskButtonStyles.ts`. The desk imports them under their old names. The footers will read the same two when they move.

**Size.** `LienDesk.tsx` went from 5,052 lines to 4,718, and from 102 `useState` calls to 97.

**Proof.** `LienDesk.render.test.tsx` (207 tests, 56 of them opening Lien affidavits) and the page's two render tests pass with no edit. New: `LienDeskAffidavitList.render.test.tsx` (eight tests: the hook with nothing to list, the held-only press, the fold, the three presses with nothing ready, and the list's empty states).
