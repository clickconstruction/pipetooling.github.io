# v2.7021 — Lien affidavit: a notice record and a copy of each notice print with the paper (2026-11-12)

Punch list #322. The owner printed a short-form affidavit whose sentence 6 named two days, beside a help sheet that said **Ask first**. Two days is right: the job has two § 53.056 notices on record. But nothing on the packet laid out what went, when, to whom and by what mail number. Her ask: *a fact sheet of what we've sent and the details like certified mail details as a part of that print out so if an attorney or team member is looking at it they can be 100% sure*.

## What prints now

In front of the paper, in this order: the help sheet, the **notice record**, a **copy of each notice**, then the paper. The record and the copies are help pages: a striped top edge, *do not file this page*, and they are in no job's filed copy. No sworn word changed.

- **The notice record** (`lienNoticeRecordSheetHtml`), one page. The job's facts and what the paper claims. A band that says **On record** or **Ask first** with the help sheet's own lines. A table with a group for each notice (its months, its amount, the day it was entered, whether the paper was saved) and a row for each copy: who, the day mailed, the method, the mail number. A copy the record lacks is a row that says *No copy on record*. Then two boxes: *The paper against the record* (sentence 5's months and sentence 6's days, each ✓ or !) and *Missing from the record* (what to add, and where).
- **A copy of each notice** (`lienNoticeCopiesHtml`), a page each. The delivery record leads it: a big check, *Sent 51 days ago*, what went, and a line for each envelope. Under it the notice's own form, drawn from the words its row keeps (`job_lien_filings.fields`), with COPY across it.
- **The batch** (the Lien desk's **Print N for notarization**): a block for each paper behind the batch sheet (`lienNoticeRecordBatchHtml`), packed and numbered as the sheet's rows. Copies of the notices are a box that starts unticked and says how many pages it adds.

## Three turns on the way

The owner saw each draw as it was made.

1. The first draw had a table, *Each month the paper swears*, with every month's last day to mail and how many days late each copy went. She asked not to see it and to see copies of what was sent. The table is gone. The record still works the months out (`LienNoticeRecord.months`, off `lienAffidavitNoticeCheck`), because the **Ask first** lines come from them.
2. The copy page first carried its delivery record at the foot of the notice. She asked for it at the top, with a big check on its left and the number of days ago.
3. The copies' slanted COPY mark was wider than the sheet. A print wider than the sheet is shrunk whole by Chrome, the sworn paper with it. The paper box now clips the mark (`overflow: hidden`), and a test holds the rule. The made-up renders had hidden this: they were all being shrunk.

## The help sheet is back on one sheet

Found on the real print, not in the made-up data: with the county left blank, a cover sheet for a real job took **two sheets**, its box *After it is signed* moved whole to page 2. That was true before this change and with or without an **Ask first** band. Two things fix it. `LIEN_HELP_STYLES` is a point tighter (leading 1.3 to 1.26, a point off the gaps between boxes and between list items; the type size is as it was). And a sheet with the record behind it shrinks its notices line to a pointer (`LIEN_HELP_NOTICES_SEE_RECORD`, `LienHelpExtras.recordFollows`), since the next page says each notice in full. Spare room measured on the captured prints: 19 pt on a clean sheet, 19 pt on a sheet with **Ask first** and the record behind it, 4 pt on a sheet with **Ask first** and the record box unticked. That last one is thin: a longer name could still tip it.

## Not done, and not verified

- **A copy is a redraw, not a scan.** It is the form with the words the app kept. For a notice entered after the fact those words were typed in afterwards, and the page says the paper that went is the saved copy. Printing the saved file or the page kept in Documents needs a server function; it is a later idea on the card.
- **The envelope's address** is not on the record, and the page says so.
- **The sheets were counted from the page the print window was given**, laid into a fresh tab: headless Chrome refused to make a PDF from the print window itself. The page is the one captured at the moment `print` was called, with every code loaded (3 of 3).
- **Nobody has printed it on paper yet.** The counts are from PDFs.
- A job with a voided notice was not found to walk; the voided line is covered by tests alone.
