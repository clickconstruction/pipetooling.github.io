# v2.5258 — Messages: a place for every person’s thread, behind the scenes (2026-10-12)

The Messages build's cut 1 SQL (the spine), migration `20261012010000_messages_spine.sql`
(`docs/migrations/20261012010000_messages_spine.md`), after the kernels (v2.5249). No screen and no edge function;
the migration is pushed by hand after merge.

- **Four tables, the office team's** (`gc_office_team()`): `contacts` (exactly one record of the five people tables,
  or none for a number nobody has named; a company's row holds its own person and others by name), `contact_channels`
  (a number or an address, matched by `contact_channel_key`; a STOP per number), `contact_jobs` (the office's ties and
  the star) and `messages` (every call, text, email, note, event and portal line, with its channel and its job).
- **The functions the first screens call**, `SECURITY INVOKER`: `contact_for_channel`, `name_contact` (*Who is this?*,
  re-pointing or folding, keeping the thread), `tie_message`, `set_default_job`, and `contacts_fold` under them.
- **Threads survive the app's deletes**: `BEFORE DELETE` triggers on `customer_contact_persons` and `people` move a
  contact off the row first; `merge_customers` folds the victim's contacts into the survivor's. That is the one existing
  function changed. Any other hard delete of a record with a contact is refused (`ON DELETE RESTRICT`).
- **The seed** (`messages_seed_contacts()`): today's trade partners and their people, and the people at every GC
  project's customer, architect and property owner, with their numbers and addresses.
- **The bed**: `npm run test:pg:messages-spine` (`supabase/tests/messages_spine/`), in CI's SQL beds workflow.
