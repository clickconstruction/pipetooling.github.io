# v2.5709 — Settings: the office hours get their own card (2026-10-21)

Punch list #140 PR 1 of 9 (`to-dos/messages-desk/BUILD_PLAN.md` § 2, PR 1; the owner's go of 2026-10-21 on the recommended defaults). The office's hours were an `app_settings` value a dev set by hand (`messages_office_hours_v1`, read by `hasRule.ts` for who has a reply and by `_shared/messagesReplyPush.ts` and `_shared/quoTechSend.ts`); nothing on Settings showed or changed them. No migration; no edge function; no mirrored kernel touched.

- **The card** ([`OfficeHoursSettingsCard.tsx`](../../src/components/settings/OfficeHoursSettingsCard.tsx)), last on Settings → Phone and texts, for everyone who sees the group: **Office hours**, *When the office is in. After hours a reply waits for the morning, and the office hears it first.* The owner and a dev (`isOwnerOrDev` in `lib/gc/access.ts`, the client's one copy of the SQL's `is_master_or_dev()`) get seven day toggles, **Opens** and **Closes** as time inputs, the line that reads the draft back (*Mon to Fri, 7:30 AM to 5:00 PM*) or what stops the save (*Pick at least one day.*, *Close must come after open.*, *Pick an open time and a close time.*), **Save the hours** (live only when something changed and the draft is good) and **Put them back**. Everyone else reads the line and *The owner or a dev sets the hours.* Its id `settings-phone-texts-hours` is a deep-link anchor (`settingsDeepLink.ts`), for PR 2's switch card.
- **The kernel** ([`officeHoursCard.ts`](../../src/lib/messages/officeHoursCard.ts)): the words, `draftFrom`, `sameDraft`, `toggleDay`, `draftProblem`, `serializeOfficeHours` (the draft's days and times, the row's `techUntil` and `emergency` as they are, and `savedAt`), `officeHoursSet` (the row carries a `savedAt` the card wrote, for PR 2's chip), `timeWords`, `daysWords` and `hoursLine`. `parseOfficeHours` reads only its five fields, so `savedAt` rides in the same JSON and `hasRule.ts`, a mirrored kernel, is untouched.
- **The io** ([`officeHoursCardIo.ts`](../../src/lib/messages/officeHoursCardIo.ts)): one read of the row, one update-only write; no row back is a refusal in words.
- **Tests**: `officeHoursCard.test.ts` (the draft, the problems, the round trip through `parseOfficeHours`, the words of the days and times, plain words), `officeHoursCardIo.test.ts`, `OfficeHoursSettingsCard.render.test.tsx` (the owner edits and saves with the kept fields and the stamp, a bad draft cannot save, Put them back, an assistant reads only, a refused save, a failed read).
- **Guide**: *turn on the reply push* gains *Set the office hours* and says what after hours means today.
- **Docs**: `MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md`'s switches table (the Office hours row has a card).

## Is this the best we can do?

1. **Fewer presses on a phone**: a dev no longer edits JSON; the owner presses the days and picks two times on the page they already use for the other switches.
2. **Plain words**: every sentence runs through `plainWordsFailures` in the kernel test.
3. **Do what is ready**: one row, one save; a bad draft says the one thing that stops it.
4. **Reuse**: the card copies the reply-push card's shape and io pattern; `hasRule.ts`'s parser stays the one reader of the JSON.
5. **Cut**: the tech's *texts until* hour and the after-hours number stay as the row keeps them. Not carried: they belong to the tech door's words (R5), and the after-hours auto-answer that would use the number is not live (`afterHoursAnswer` has no caller), so the card promises nothing about it.
