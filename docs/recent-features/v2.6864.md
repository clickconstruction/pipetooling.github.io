# v2.6864 — The Bridge's call reaches the people named on it (punch list #303, PR 4) (2026-11-10)

PR 4 of the Bridge's refresh under one story (`to-dos/bridge-one-story/README.md`), on the owner's yes to his call 4, 2026-11-10: each move on a call lands in its person's Needs you. Until now a name on a move was a word in a box, and most of the people a call names cannot open the Bridge. One migration, no edge function.

## What changed

- **The migration** (`20261110213000_bridge_moves_reach_people.sql`, `docs/migrations/`): `bridge_my_moves()` hands a signed-in account the open moves of the standing call that carry its own id, and `bridge_mark_my_move_done()` stamps one of them done. Both are `SECURITY DEFINER` and answer only about the caller's own moves; the table stays closed to everyone but the owner and the devs.
- **A move carries its person** (`src/lib/bridge/callPeople.ts`, `weekCall.ts`): `RecordedMove` is a `CallMove` with `whoId`. The page proposes the account with the name (`peopleIds`: who sent the most bills, who took the most payments, the caller; `topPersonId`), and `movesToRecord` writes it. A name changed by a pick carries the picked account; a name typed carries none. `calls.ts` is copied to the edge functions and is untouched: the account rides in the same jsonb.
- **The chip is a pick** (`BridgeWeek.tsx`): pressing a name opens the list of accounts (`callPeople`: not archived, by name), with *Nobody* and *Type a name…*. A typed name's chip has a dashed edge and says on hover that it reaches nobody's Needs you.
- **The week's call on Needs you** (`useBridgeMoves.ts`, `dashboardNeedsYou.ts`, `needsYouSources.ts`, `needsYouDoors.ts`, `needsYouActor.ts`, `useNeedsYouPress.tsx`): a new source, `bridgeMoves`, on for anyone signed in on both hosts, and a new line, `bridge-moves`, ranked with the week's hard deadline and the viewer's own. It reads the first move and who made the call, and counts the rest. Its button opens the page where the move is done (`moveDoor`: Stages, Billing, the approvals queue, the jobs), with **Done** beside it; a move with no page has one button, **Mark it done**. Until the migration is pushed the read answers with no moves, so the card is as it was.
- **The Bridge says who marked it** (`callPeopleIo.ts` → `loadMoveMarks`, `scoredMoves`): on a move the app reads, the numbers keep the verdict and the card adds *Dana marked it done.*; on a move it cannot read, the person's word is the verdict.

## Not in this PR

- The line leaves when Done is pressed, when Friday is answered, or when the pay week ends. It does not yet leave by itself when the number moves: the Dashboard does not load the Bridge's readings.
- A person named on a move gets no push or email. The Needs you card is where it lands.
- The floor, the typed cash and the account choice still save for a dev only.

## Verify

Open `/bridge` as the owner or a dev. Press a name on a move: a list of people opens. Pick one and record the call. Sign in as that person: the Dashboard's Needs you card shows **The week's call** with the move. Press **Done**: the line leaves, and the Bridge's card says who marked it done.
