# v2.3697 — Lien desk: click a plain value on the notice and land where it is set (2026-09-22)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Mock-up: the Claude artifact *Change It at the Source*, built as drawn. Stacked on [v2.3694](./v2.3694.md) (the paper is the editor).

## Why

The owner, after the paper became the editor: *"for the items not in those boxes, when a user clicks on them I would like the modal where they can be opened to be presented, where when they open the user is brought to the entry field and have it highlighted temporarily."* One gesture for every plain value: hover says where it is filled from, click lands there on the field, ringed for four seconds (the ring Edit Job already used for the property record), and the paper rings the value when it comes back changed.

## What shipped

- **Edit Job takes a `focusRow`** (`'gc'`) through the whole chain — `OpenEditJobOptions` → `JobFormModalContext` / `JobDetailModalContext` / the opener bridge → `JobWindowModal` → `JobFormModal` → [`JobFormEditFactRows`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormEditFactRows.tsx), which opens the row, scrolls to it and rings the GC picker for four seconds. `propertyRecordFocus` is unchanged beside it.
- **Settings takes `?focus=issuer.<field>`** (`settingsFocusParam` in [`settingsDeepLink.ts`](../../src/lib/settingsDeepLink.ts)): [`PhysicalInvoiceIssuerDevSettingsBlock`](../../src/components/settings/PhysicalInvoiceIssuerDevSettingsBlock.tsx) opens, scrolls to and rings *Company name* or *Address*. The desk navigates to `/settings?tab=settings-jobs&focus=issuer.companyName`.
- **The paper's marks** ([`lienFilingDocuments.ts`](../../src/lib/jobsDocuments/lienFilingDocuments.ts)) carry `door` (`gc` · `company` · `claim` → `data-door`, a pointer cursor) and `ring` (`data-ring`, a blue ring on the text). [`LienDeskModal`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskModal.tsx): the derived titles say where and whether a click lands there; a click on a plain value with a door remembers what the value read and opens Edit Job on the GC row, Settings → Company on the field, or the desk's own claim box (`LienClaimBox.openSignal`, scrolled to); when the desk re-reads and the value differs, it rings on the paper for four seconds. The date has no door and says why.
- Guide *send lien notices from the Lien desk*: the doors, and the ring on return.

## Verified

- `LienDeskModal` render test: doors and titles on the plain values, the GC click → `onOpenEditJob('j650', 'gc')`, the claimant click → `onOpenCompanySettings('addressText')`, the claim click opens the claim box, a re-read with the GC renamed rings the value; the v2.3694 paper tests; release-note + guide suites; lint; typecheck.
- Dev server on prod data: job 650's GC opens Edit Job on the GC row, ringed; the claimant opens Settings → Company on the field, ringed; nothing saved.
