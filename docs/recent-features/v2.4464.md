# v2.4464 — Partner statement: a card charge posted in the evening keeps its day (2026-10-02)

One of the instant-day fixes that follow [v2.4452](v2.4452.md). Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

Three partner readers cut a day out of a `timestamptz` by its first ten characters, so anything stamped after 7 pm CDT (6 pm CST) read as the next day:

- **A job's card charges** on the partner's costing sheet: `partner_job_costing_payload` (migration `20260916190000`) returns each charge's `mercury_transactions.posted_at` whole, and both readers sliced it: the partner's Dashboard ([`DashboardPartnerJobsSection.tsx`](../../src/components/dashboard/DashboardPartnerJobsSection.tsx)) and the statement paper ([`PartnerStatementPaper.tsx`](../../src/components/partner/PartnerStatementPaper.tsx)), *Home Depot · 2026-10-03*.
- **The partnership timeline** ([`partnerTimeline.ts`](../../src/lib/partnerLedger/partnerTimeline.ts), the office's Timeline tab): a declined work order's day from `step_commitments.declined_at`, and a job's *confirmed as partner-majority* day from `jobs_ledger.partner_confirmed_at`, both stamped `now()` by their writers.

## What changed

Each reads `calendarYmdInAppTzFromIso(iso)` from [`dateUtils.ts`](../../src/utils/dateUtils.ts) (`APP_CALENDAR_TZ`). `PartnerJobCosting.card_charges` now says its `posted_at` is an instant.

## Not changed

- *Paid out* on the ledger (`partnerLedgerJournal.ts`, `partnerPayReportHtml.ts`) slices `pay_stub_payments.paid_at`, a `timestamptz`. Every writer stamps it at noon on the chosen day (Record payment, a split payment, Cash App reconcile, and `pay_paid_at_noon` in Chicago; a moved overpayment keeps its source's stamp), so the slice is that day.
- *yours since* on the statement's job list prints `confirmed_at` in the viewer's own zone, not as a UTC date.

## Tests

- `partnerTimeline.test.ts` (+1): a decline at `2026-10-03T00:30:00+00:00` (7:30 pm CDT) reads Oct 2; a job confirmed at `2026-12-02T00:30:00Z` (6:30 pm CST) reads Dec 1; noon UTC reads its own day.
- `PartnerStatementPaper.render.test.tsx` (+1): a card charge posted at 7:30 pm CDT on Oct 2 reads *Home Depot · 2026-10-02* on the costing sheet.

Both fail on main.

## Checked against prod

Read only, from the worktree dev server: the 2 partner jobs carry 37 card charges, and 15 of them posted in a Central evening, so they read a day late on the partner's costing sheet until this ships. One of the 2 confirmed jobs was confirmed in an evening and moves a day earlier on the timeline. No work order has been declined yet.
