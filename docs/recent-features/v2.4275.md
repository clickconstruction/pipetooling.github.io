# v2.4275 — Our lien waiver to the GC, PR 2: the tick on Bill Customer, the cell on every bill (2026-09-30)

No migration, no edge function. PR 2 of the train (PR 1 [v2.4274](v2.4274.md) is the window). Mock-up boards 1 and 5 of artifact `R9VKFzFEwMcY7oUb4ZyG3v`: the waiver travels with the bill; a bill already sent gets its waiver from the Bill tab.

## What changed

- **Kernel** [`lienWaiverCell.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienWaiverCell.ts): `lienWaiverCellForBill(releases, invoiceId, settled)` — the two halves of a bill's waiver pair, each *none · draft · awaiting · signed · sent* from the job's `job_lien_releases` rows whose `invoice_ids` cover the bill (the newest, furthest-along row decides a half); `next`, the one move owed (*add_conditional* while the money is out and nothing is sent, *sign*, *send*, *add_unconditional* once settled); `chips` as words (*Conditional ✓ sent Sep 30*, *Unconditional · when paid*, *Unconditional owed · settled*). `billSettled(invoice, payments)`. Five tests.
- **Bill Customer** ([`SendRecordInvoiceModal`](../../src/components/jobs/SendRecordInvoiceModal.tsx) + [`BillCustomerLienReleaseStrip`](../../src/components/jobs/BillCustomerLienReleaseStrip.tsx)): on a GC job billing one draft, the lien strip leads with **Send the lien waiver with this bill** — ticked by default — naming the form the bill picks (`pickLienWaiverForBill`), its cite, the two facts and the why line. All three send paths (Stripe, physical, outside bill) call the new `onSentWantWaiver(jobId, invoiceId)` after `onSuccess` when ticked. [`BillCustomerModalProvider`](../../src/contexts/BillCustomerModalContext.tsx) owns the follow-up — [`BillCustomerWaiverFollowUp`](../../src/components/jobs/BillCustomerWaiverFollowUp.tsx) re-reads the job (the bill is `billed` now) and opens the Release of Lien window on it — so Bill Customer's own close does not unmount it. The Stripe path leaves Bill Customer open on its success view; the waiver window opens over it.
- **The Bill tab's cell** ([`JobFormInvoiceList`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormInvoiceList.tsx)): on a GC job (or any job that already has a release), every sent or paid bill row carries the two chips and one door — *Add waiver ›*, *Sign it ›*, *Send it ›*, *Add the unconditional ›*, or *Waivers ›* when nothing is owed — opening the Release of Lien window on that bill. The list reads the job's releases itself (fail-soft) and re-reads when the window closes.
- Guide *send a GC our lien waiver* gains *Send it with the bill* and *A bill already sent*; GLOSSARY entry amended.

## Design calls

- **On by default, on GC jobs only.** A GC wants the waiver with every pay app; a homeowner does not. The tick is there to untick, and never shows on a direct job (the strip's *+ New release* still does).
- **The window opens after the bill has gone, not before.** The waiver must name a sent bill (its amount and date), and a failed send must not leave a waiver behind. So the hand-off fires after `onSuccess`, and the follow-up re-reads the job.
- **The cell says the next move, not the history.** Two chips and one verb; the window holds the rest.
