# v2.4275 — Accounts Receivable: All reads by what happened last (2026-09-30)

Punch list **#74** PR 3 of 4 (`to-dos/ar-where-the-cheque-went.md`). All was the whole history by bank date, so a cheque applied today on a deposit banked three weeks ago sat three weeks down. The owner's ask was "the cheques we've assigned in the last 30 days"; this is that, as an order on the list that exists rather than a new list. Client only; no migration, no edge function.

## What changed

- **[`src/lib/jobs/arAllByLastAction.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/arAllByLastAction.ts)** (new, +test) — `orderArAllByLastAction`: the rows whose last action (the trail's `lastTouchedAt` from v2.4274, else the bank date) falls inside 30 days, newest first, grouped under `arAllDayHeading` (*Today*, *Yesterday*, *Mon 9/28*); then every older row under *Older · by bank date*, newest bank date first. A row with no date at all goes last.
- **`BankPaymentsModal.tsx`** — under All the list renders those groups with a heading line each (`data-testid="ar-all-day-heading"`); the search still filters first. `orderedFiltered` is the rows in the order shown, and *Apply & next* and the selection fallback follow it. To match is unchanged.

## Decisions taken

- **An order, not a list.** The rows, the chips and the pane are the same; only the sequence and six words of heading are new.
- **The order settles once the trails land.** Before the trail read returns, All is by bank date as before; then the rows re-sort once. One reflow, not a spinner.
- **30 days is fixed.** "Since you last looked" would need a per-person marker; the owner did not ask for one.
