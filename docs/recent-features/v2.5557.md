# v2.5557 — Customers: a list of cards, like Trade partners, as the first view (2026-10-15)

The owner's ask of 2026-10-15, after the By step bands (v2.5555): "A Customers list like Trade partners, build it." Customers had no first view of its own, only By step and By company, while Trade partners opens on a card per trade. Mocked up on a canvas (https://claude.ai/artifact/5iFX9FjfUCkgf9GFnoDf8q: a first cut with a labelled facts block, then the refined cut with the facts on one line, the stage as a Board-coloured chip, the late card's red edge and the off-the-flow card at the foot), then built as the refined cut. No migration, no edge function.

- **`src/lib/gc/customersList.ts`** (new, pure): `customerCards({ customers, projects, flow, pageOpens })`, one card per customer with at least one GC project: the person, phone, email and address from the customer record; the master agreement from the Once lane's track (`companyTable`: *signed*, *not yet* with the step's small words while on it, *lapsed*); the portal from their last page open (`portalCellWords`); then a row per project with its stage from `boardSectionOf` (`SECTION_WORDS`, the Board's five, and `SECTION_TONE`, its colours), the step it is on with its day (`companyJobLine`, new in `flows/board.ts`, with `lineDayWords` and *our move*), and the step's own press (`rowPress`). Cards sort by the worst line (late, lapsed, waiting), the ones off the flow last; `headChip` is the band's one phrase and `listSummary` the line over the cards. `NO_CUSTOMERS`, `READING` and `OPEN_THE_CARD` are held plain.
- **`GcCustomersList.tsx`** (new): Trade partners' card shape (`Card`, the blue band with a 5 px edge, `Chip`, `th`/`td`); a late customer's card has a red edge, one off the flow a grey band with the reason and no table. **Open** on the band opens the customer's window on its Flow tab; a job's name opens its card on the Project Board; the press at the right of a row is the step's own (**Remind …** opens the window at the step's paper with its send, `customerAtForStep`; **Bill day** opens Money; otherwise **Open the card**). It reads the customer flow itself (`useFlowBoard('customer')`) and each customer's last page open.
- **`GcFlowBoard.tsx`**: Customers' views are **List**, **By step**, **By company**; **`GcProjects.tsx`** passes the list and opens Customers on it (`?by=step` and `?by=company` still open the others).
- **Tests**: `customersList.test.ts` (5: the cards and their order, each job's stage, step and press, a customer with no project left out and a job with no place as *not started*, the agreement and the portal and the unread words, the chips and the summary); `GcCustomersList.render.test.tsx` (3: the cards, the presses, the empty and reading words); `GcProjects.render.test.tsx` (the Customers test opens on the list with By step one press away).
- **Guide** *see every customer and where each job stands* (new). **Docs**: the Glossary's Trade partners entry, the page map's boards sentence and the app directory's `/gc` section name the list.

Left for the owner: whether the list should carry Trade partners' *Actions for assistants* band for customers (what good looks like: a master agreement signed before the first bid, a portal opened within a week), and whether By company still earns its pill once the list has the same facts.
