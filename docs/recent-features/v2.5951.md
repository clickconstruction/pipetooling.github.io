# v2.5951 — Empty lists in Materials, Banking and Settings name the next press (2026-10-26)

Punch list #184, sweep C, the hand PR for Materials, supply houses, Banking, Settings, contracts and write-ups (`to-dos/consistency-sweeps/README.md` → *C. Empty states*). The finder (`bareEmptySentences`) listed 175 bare empty sentences in the area. 102 are converted, drawn by 92 `<EmptyState>` elements, since a conditional pair shares one. 73 are left as they are, because they are cell, chip or field fallbacks and not a list's empty state. By kind: **make** 29, **filter** 33, **waiting** 21, **data** 19. About a third draw `main` alone: the good states, and the sub-lists whose add row sits right beside them.

## The words kernels

- `src/lib/materials/materialsEmptyWords.ts` (+ test): the assembly list the Assembly Book and the PO Builder share, the Parts Book, Purchase Orders, Held for suppliers, the supply-house directory, and one house's invoices and POs. `materialsTabPath(tab)` is a Materials tab as a link, since the page's tab effect reads `?tab=`.
- `src/lib/banking/bankingEmptyWords.ts` (+ test): the Accounting and Drag Sort tables, where the Banking filters (account, kind, search) can hide every row, and the unlabeled view's good state. `ACCOUNTING_LABELS_PATH` is the Drag Sort tab, where labels are made.

## New props, all optional

- `BankingAccountNicknamesModal.onRefreshFromMercury`: the page's Refresh from Mercury (`handleSync`).
- `BankingSortingConfigModal.onReload`: Banking passes its Reload table handler. Jobs → Accounts Receivable's copy of the window does not pass it, so there the lists keep a sentence alone.
- `BankingMercuryAccountingTab.onClearBankingFilters`: passed only while a Banking filter is on; it clears all three. The Drag Sort tab already held the setters and builds the press itself.
- `MercuryReconcileRunsList.onCheck`: the Reconciliation tab's Check against bank statements.

Small refactors that keep behavior: `MaterialsAssemblyBookTab`'s two Add Item openers are one `openAddItem(type)`, `WriteupsContractsSubTab`'s New writeup is `openNewWriteup`, and `Materials.tsx`'s Price coverage door is `coverageToSupplyHousesTab`. Each serves its header button and the empty state's press.

## Is this the best we can do?

Seven presses are links, not handlers, because the page that fills the list cannot be reached from the list: Billing for the Stripe invoices, Drag Sort for the labels, the Supply houses tab for Held for suppliers, the Assembly Book and the PO Builder between the Materials tabs, People for the Settings rosters, GC projects for the Customer page, and Jobs → Reports for the report templates. People and Reports would be one tap better with a deep link that opens + Hire or Templates, and neither exists yet. A few sentences carry facts read from the code that the owner may want to word himself: *A GC job joins it at buyout* (the loader reads the buyout, building and closed stages), the Stripe webhook line, and the Usage tab's *60 days*. The Email streams cards were left as they are: each card's header already names where to schedule more, and a later PR could turn that header into the press. Jobs → Accounts Receivable's copy of the sorting configuration window keeps a sentence alone, since wiring its reload is the Jobs area's file.
