# v2.4727 — Quickfill: a Property kinds station marks every unpaid job residential or commercial (2026-10-06)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. The owner (2026-10-06): *some jobs don't have a property type (residential) vs (commercial) — for all jobs in Waiting, Working, Ready to Bill, Billed Awaiting Payment, Collections but not paid, build a tool for Quickfill for Assistants to fill these out: click on the address and have it open in Google Maps, then set the property type.* Mock-up: artifact `Tbqrd91HNzykSpAGBbdekL` (the *Property kind station* canvas — the first draft, the second look, the phone round screen).

## What was there already

The kind is a fact on the customer's property (`customer_addresses.property_kind`, v2.3667), the fact the lien clock reads (§ 53.056 gives a residential property's notice a month less). The Pipeline's red **?** badge (v2.4160) saves it with `savePropertyKind`, and on a job with a typed address and no saved property (v2.4212) `linkJobPropertyAndSaveKind` saves the address as a property on the customer (or reuses the one it matches) and links the job. The Lien desk's Calendar has a *Set kinds, biggest first* sheet for billed jobs only. Nobody had a queue over every unpaid stage, and assistants do not scan the Pipeline for **?**s.

## What changed

- **`lib/quickfill/propertyKinds.ts`** (8 tests): `propertyKindRows(jobs, kindByJobId, customerTypeById)` — one row per PROPERTY (a saved property's id, else the customer + typed address, so two jobs at one address are one question), the unpaid jobs at it under the address with their stage and open balance; paid jobs, jobs already marked and (for the foot line) jobs with neither a customer nor a GC left out. Lien-clock rows (Billed, Collections) first, biggest balance first. `propertyKindHint`: a GC on the job → commercial; the customer's own `customer_type` → that; a telling word in the job's name or address (*suite, tenant, finish-out, restaurant, office, church, apartments…* → commercial; *house, home, residence* → residential); never a decision. `propertyKindFollowWords` for the green line (*1211 and 1240*, *863 and 2 other jobs*).
- **`hooks/useQuickfillPropertyKinds.ts`**: the jobs cache (the list Missing job info reads), `usePropertyKinds` for the kinds (the Pipeline's own chunked read; the property's home is the customer, else the GC, as v2.4222 has it), one chunked read of `customers.customer_type` for the hint; office roles only (dev, master, assistant-like — the roles the badge lets pick).
- **`QuickfillPropertyKindsSection.tsx`** (4 render smokes): the address is a Google Maps door (`googleMapsSearchUrl`, new tab); the customer and the GC; the jobs as chips; *The lien clock is running on this job…* on lien rows and *N jobs follow this pick*; *Not one of X's saved properties yet…* on typed-address rows; the hint line and the switch. A pick on a saved property is `savePropertyKind`; on a typed address `linkJobPropertyAndSaveKind` per job (the first saves the property, the rest reuse it); either way the row becomes a green line (*8507 Culebra Road marked commercial · 1211 and 1240 follow · the lien window reads the new deadline now*) with **Undo** (`savePropertyKind(id, '')`). The foot counts the no-customer jobs and links to Missing job info. Empty: *Every unpaid job's property is marked.*
- **`PropertyKindSwitch`** gains `hint`: that half is ring-outlined in its badge colour and reads *· looks right*; nothing is pressed until the tap.
- **`Quickfill.tsx`**: the 32nd station, `property-kinds` *Property kinds*, after Missing job info (`SECTION_INSERT_AFTER` for orgs with a saved order); the hook and the metric (a count of properties) at page level so the header count stays live while the strip is collapsed (quirk 18); eligibility = the hook's `fetchEnabled`. Default banner: *Is every unpaid job's property marked residential or commercial?*
- Guide *mark property kinds from Quickfill*; a bullet in *run the office day with Quickfill*; Glossary, ACCESS_CONTROL, PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION and the Quickfill map amended.

## Not changed

No lien dates on the row (the mock-up drew *Lien by Dec 15 if commercial · Nov 15 if residential*): that needs the runway's work-month read per job; the row says the clock is running and that residential is a month sooner. No Homestead tick on a residential pick (the lien screens offer it; the owner's call). No Street View door (needs coordinates the job does not carry).
