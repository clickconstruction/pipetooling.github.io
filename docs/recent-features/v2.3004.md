# v2.3004 — Property lookup: county, legal description and owner of record from the Texas parcel roll (2026-09-07)

Customer properties train, PR 1 of 4 (owner-approved mock-ups 2026-09-07: one Properties list, a property sheet that finds the legal record, the county ladder). This PR ships the finder; the modal refresh follows. One migration (`20260907090000_customer_address_parcel_record.sql` — **`supabase db push` after merge**) and one new edge function (**deploy `property-lookup`**; `geocode-one` and `geocode-address-batch` import the changed `_shared/googleGeocode.ts` and should be redeployed with it).

## Why

Every "Additional address" row on Edit customer carries the property's legal identity for lien paperwork (v2.2614), but filling it was homework: the county came from a 50-city table that maps Schertz to Guadalupe (Schertz sits in three counties), and the legal description, owner of record and mailing address meant a trip to the appraisal district's site and six copy-pastes. The Texas Geographic Information Office publishes a statewide parcel layer fed by the appraisal districts, with a public, keyless map service that answers a point lookup with exactly those fields. Verified live on 2026-09-07 before building.

## What shipped

- **`property-lookup` edge function** — address in, property record out: geocode (the `address_geocodes` cache → Google → US Census), then TxGIO `identify` at the pin. Returns the parcel (owner, legal description, mailing address, county, source district, tax year) plus the geocoder's county as the fallback rung. Role gate mirrors `customer_addresses` (dev, master, assistant, controller, estimator). `_shared/googleGeocode.ts` now also returns `county` from `administrative_area_level_2` (additive).
- **Shared kernel** [`supabase/functions/_shared/txParcelRecord.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/txParcelRecord.ts), re-exported as `src/lib/customers/propertyRecord.ts` (+22 tests): `parseTxParcelIdentify`, `proposeCounty` (the ladder: parcel → geocoder → city table, with every distinct answer as a candidate and a straddling-city flag), `ownerLooksLikeCompany`, `homesteadHint` (the roll has no exemptions; an individual who gets mail at the property is "likely"), `proposePropertyRecord`, and `applyProposalToFields` — **fill-blanks never overwrites what a person typed; replace is explicit**.
- **`CustomerPropertyRecordPanel`** replaces the inline legal fields under each address row's "Property legal info": *Look up the property record* (auto-runs the first time the panel opens on a row never looked up), the county with source pills when the rungs disagree ("Bexar · from the parcel" / "Guadalupe · from the city") and a straddle warning, legal description with its provenance tag (`Comal Appraisal District · 2025 · Prop ID 178402`), kind + homestead with the reason for the suggestion, owner pills / name / company / mailing address, a "use the record's: …" link under any field whose typed value differs, the lien-ready checklist, and the CAD link for the day-of-filing check.
- **Migration** — provenance columns on `customer_addresses`: `county_source` (parcel / geocoder / city / manual), `parcel_id`, `parcel_source`, `parcel_tax_year`, `parcel_looked_up_at`. `database.ts` carries them by hand until the next gen-types run.
- Guide `see-a-customers-full-history-and-lifetime-value` → "Track more than one property" rewritten around the lookup.

## Verify

Edit customer → Additional addresses → a row with a real Central-Texas address → ▶ Property legal info: the lookup runs, blank fields fill, the green "Found on the appraisal roll" line shows, the provenance tag names the district and tax year, and the checklist goes green when owner + mailing are present. A Schertz address shows two county pills. Type over a filled field, click *Look up again*: the typed value survives and a "use the record's" link appears under it. Save address → reopen: the provenance tag persists.
