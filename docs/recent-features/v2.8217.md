# v2.8217 — Sub papers: the subject names the number, the payment record reads Got it, and an old line goes (2026-11-21)

Client only: no migration, no edge function. Punch list #350. The three faults the audit of 2026-11-20 confirmed and nobody had built. Each was read again on `origin/main` before it was touched, and each was as the audit said.

## 1. The first send's subject

**Before.** The send step builds its subject from the paper as previewed, and the press numbers the paper only when it is made. `SubPapersWindowConnected.tsx` posted `plan.subject`, so a sheet's first work statement went out as *Work statement from <company>* with the file `WS-1113-1.pdf` beside it. The walk of 2026-11-19 read so.

**Now.** The subject is its own kernel, `subPaperSendSubject(lead, company)` in `subPaperSend.ts`. The step draws it from the paper it has, and the send builds it again from the lead paper as the register numbered it (`made[0]`), so the email reads *Work statement WS-1113-1 from <company>*. Under the files, the step's line adds *The subject names it too* when the lead is a work statement with no number yet.

A payment record already carried its sending's number. A statement of account reads *Your statement of account from <company>* before and after its number, as it did: its words are not changed here.

## 2. The office's payment record and Got it

**Before.** `SubPapersWindowView` built every payment record with no marks, so the *Received* box always read *Not marked yet. <sub> can press Got it on their page.*, also after the sub had pressed it. The Pay view's chip already read the marks.

**Now.**

- `paymentRecordReceived(answers, key)` in `subPaperAnswers.ts` turns the marks the Pay view reads into what `buildPaymentRecord` takes, by the paper's own key: a sending's id, or `row:<payment id>` for a payment from before sendings. The day is worded as the sub's own page words it.
- The connected window reads this person's rows of `sub_paper_acks` before it draws, and waits for them as it waits for the register. A payment record drawn ahead of them would be made saying *Not marked yet*.
- The view takes `answers` and builds each record with its mark.
- `registerSubPaperSet` takes `received`. It builds a payment record again over the numbered sheets, so without it the PDF and the register's snapshot would have said *Not marked yet* under a preview that said otherwise. The mark is not in the paper's fingerprint, so it makes no change to its figures.
- A read that fails leaves every record at *Not marked yet*, which is what every record said before. The view then says under a payment record: *Whether <sub> pressed Got it could not be read just now. This paper says Not marked yet either way.*

The paper's own words are the kernel's and are not changed: `_shared/subPapers.ts` is not touched.

## 3. The last press's line

**Before.** The window drew `said` until the next press cleared it. *WS-2041-1.pdf is downloaded* stayed under another sheet's work statement, and *Sent 3 papers* stayed over a send step opened again.

**Now.** `subPaperSaid.ts` holds the rule. A line belongs to the paper and the step it arrived on (`subPaperPlace`). It goes when either changes and stays gone on the way back (`subPaperSaidHeld`, `subPaperSaidShown`). A new line always shows wherever the window is when it arrives, since the paper picker stays live while a press is under way. A statement's period is not a place: the line names its file.
