# v2.7590 — Lien desk: one press at a time, in its own file (2026-11-17)

The sixth cut of `src/components/jobs/LienDesk.tsx`. No behaviour changes.

**What moved.** `run`, `busy` and `busyRef` are the hook `useLienDeskRunner` in `src/components/jobs/useLienDeskRunner.ts`, as they stood. `run` takes a press's name and its work: it refuses while another press is in flight, says the done words and has the desk re-read when the work lands, says `<name>: <reason>` when it throws, and answers whether it landed. Thirty-one presses on the desk go through it.

**The one seam.** `run` used to clear the draft's autosave timer itself. The timer is the desk's, so the hook takes a `beforeRun` and the desk hands it the same four lines. They run at the same point: after the press is marked busy and before the work.

**The error line is unchanged.** The map said moving `run` would force a rewrite of its catch under `check:error-words`. It does not: the check passes the line as written, so the words a failed press shows are the same.

**Why it is its own cut.** Every footer calls `run` and reads `busy`. With the rule in one tested place, the footers can move next and take it as a prop.

**Size.** `LienDesk.tsx` went from 4,544 lines to 4,532, and from 90 `useState` calls to 89.

**Proof.** `LienDesk.render.test.tsx` (207 tests) and the page's render test pass with no edit. New: `useLienDeskRunner.render.test.tsx`, five tests: a press that lands, one that lands quietly, a throw with a reason, a throw with none, and a second press refused while the first is in flight.
