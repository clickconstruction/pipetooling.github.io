# v2.6164 — Other job charges keep the trash can on the first line (2026-10-30)

Punch list #215, PR 4, the look pass after v2.6110, v2.6111 and v2.6112 went live on 2026-10-30. No migration. The to-do folder `to-dos/other-job-charges-date-memo/` retires with this PR; the release notes and the three fragments before this one carry the record.

## What was wrong

The live row wrapped. With the date box, the Estimate chip, the description and the amount filling the first line, the trash can fell onto a line of its own under the date, and **+ memo** sat under that: three lines where the mock-up drew two.

## What changed

- The charge row in [`JobFormPartsCostSection`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormPartsCostSection.tsx) is a CSS grid with named areas instead of a wrapping flex row. Wide: `date desc amt trash` on the first line, the memo under the description and the amount. Under 640px (the modal's own phone breakpoint, `useNarrowViewport640`): the date alone on the first line, the description and the amount on the second, the memo on the third, as the mock-up's phone view drew it.
- Nothing about the data, the save or the inputs changed. The render test pins the grid areas.

## Verify

Any job → Costs → Other job charges: each row shows the date, the chip, the words and the amount on one line with the trash can at the right, and **+ memo** or the memo text under the description. Shrink the window under 640px: the date moves to its own line. `npm test -- JobFormPartsCostSection`.
