## What changed

All in **`src/components/materials/BoxPublicPage.tsx`**, the box's own view. No kernel, no function, no words changed but the two noted.

- **The plate.** The stencil (46px, smaller as it grows longer), the kind as a chip, the place, the code, the photo across the top and the count's verdict along the foot are one card on the portal's ink with white words. It replaces the ruled tile, the verdict's own box and the photo's own box. The verdict's dot and *Due a count* use a brighter green and amber, which read on navy where the paper's own would sink. The heading still reads *Cage B3* to a screen reader: the kind is read aloud before the stencil and the chip is hidden from it.
- **The list.** The table sits in a bordered card, so its corners have their outline. Counts are 20px and heavier. **None left** is a small pill. The count cells lost their left padding, because the pill's width set the column and wrapped a line's Can't find note.
- **The search.** The field and **Find** are one control in one card. Its heading, *Not in this cage?*, is set as the list's heading is.
- **The next steps.** Text the office, Call the office and the sign-in are rows of one card, each an icon, a title and one line. The call was a link inside the text's caption; it has a row of its own, with the number as its second line. The sign-in's second line is *On Click's crew? Once on this phone.*, shortened from two sentences so the row stays two lines.
- **The foot.** The ownership sentence is centred under a hairline.

The search's own view, a retired label, a wrong code and the Scan a label page are as they were.
