# v2.6557 — Machines on the fleet board: a card per excavator, drill or skid steer with its template, its gauges, its label and where it is (2026-11-06)

Punch list #270 PR 3, the first screen of the equipment pre-use check, ported from the owner's spike (2026-11-05) onto the tables of v2.6555. The office's side first: a machine on the board with its template, its place, its gauges, its label and its rental, so Dispatch can put machines on jobs before the phone's check ships. Pushed after `20261106030000_machines.sql` (the board reads the new tables; `check:calls-ahead` labels it).

## What changed

- `src/lib/machines/machines.ts`: the pure kernel from the spike, now with `templateId` on a machine and a question (a question is every machine's, a template's or one machine's), `photos` as rows rather than data URLs, and the folder words. `machines.test.ts` holds the walk, the levels, the leaving line, rentals, the plan and the pair per day.
- `src/lib/machines/machinesIo.ts`: the reads and writes. `loadFleet` reads the machines (`vehicles` with `kind = machine`), the active templates and questions, the open possessions by job, sixty days of checks with their photos, and the job words, and maps them into the kernel's shapes; `addMachine` (meter hours, a minted code), `saveRental`, `saveLevels` (null goes back to the template's), `saveTemplate`, `placeMachine` (the open possession ends today and one starts on the job, or none for the yard).
- `src/components/machines/MachinesSection.tsx`: the board at People → Vehicles above the trucks' strip: a card per machine with the template chip, the rental chip, the place, today's line, the hours, checks and label tiles, Put on a job / Hand off / Back to the yard through `JobLookupBox`, Print the label (`MachineLabelModal`, the QR to `/m/<code>`), Gauges (`GaugeEditor`, the four looks drawn small by `LevelGauge`, Save or Use the template's), Rental… (`RentalEditor`), the Drive folder line, and Days on jobs; `AddMachine` with the template picker; the empty state through `emptyWords`.
- The truck surfaces read trucks alone (`kind = truck`): the fleet tab's list, Quickfill's odometers, the Needs you vehicle gaps, the person desk's field section. A machine never shows as a truck.
- The guide *manage company vehicles and track their odometers* gains *Machines on the board*; `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`, `GLOSSARY.md` and `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` name the board.
