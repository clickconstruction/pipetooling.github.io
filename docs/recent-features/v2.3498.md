# v2.3498 — An invoice's payment history no longer borrows another bill's payment (2026-09-16)

One-line behaviour change in one kernel, plus tests. No migration, no edge function, no schema.

## Why

Taunya, on the Lien instruments window for **job 258 (Dudley Mason)**: *"Notice at top says $9800 due but there's a payment applied here."* The header read **$9,800.00 open** while the enclosed invoice read **Balance due $1,800.00**.

The header was right. The invoice was wrong.

Job 258 has three bills. The June 4 check belongs to the second one:

| Bill | Amount | Status | Payment linked |
|---|---|---|---|
| seq 0 | $8,900 | paid | none |
| seq 1 | $8,000 | paid | the $8,000 check, 2026-06-04 |
| seq 2 | $9,800 | **billed**, sent 2026-09-04 | none |

`filterPaymentsForPhysicalInvoiceHistory` ([`src/lib/physicalInvoiceLineItems.ts`](../../src/lib/physicalInvoiceLineItems.ts)) took the payments linked to the bill being rendered and, **when that list came back empty, fell back to every payment on the job**. Bill seq 2 has none of its own, so it borrowed seq 1's check and credited the customer twice. The fallback dates to `af0c222` (2026-04-17) and its only test covered the case where a linked payment *does* exist, so the empty case was never pinned.

**Measured on prod (read-only) before the fix:**

| | Count | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Open bills showing another bill's payments | 16 | |
| Of those, already sent to the customer | 11 | $59,999 genuinely owed |
| What those 11 told the customer was due | | $4,184 |

Nine of the eleven read as nothing due. Job 273 (Dudley Lennox) is the worst: three open bills of $3,500, $13,420 and $665, each separately subtracting the same $38,780, so all three print zero.

This is not display-only. [`SendRecordInvoiceModal`](../../src/components/jobs/SendRecordInvoiceModal.tsx) builds the outgoing PDF and email through the same builder, so the bills went out that way. The same reconstruction is **Exhibit A on a demand letter** and, since v2.3437, the invoice enclosed with a **§ 53.056 notice** — a notice enclosing a bill that reads *nothing due* undercuts the claim it makes.

## The fix

```ts
return sorted.filter((p) => !p.invoice_id || p.invoice_id === invoiceId)
```

A payment recorded against a **different** bill is excluded. A payment with **no** bill attached is kept.

Keeping the unlinked rows is deliberate and load-bearing, not caution. Prod carries **191 unlinked payments worth $2.32M**, and on a single-bill job they plainly are that bill's payment — **job 102** is one $5,355 bill and one unlinked $3,000 check, which reads *Balance due $2,355* correctly today. Dropping them would have told that customer they owed the whole bill again. Six open bills sit in that shape.

`billingKind === 'job'` is untouched: whole-job bills still show every payment, which is right, because job-level billing never links payments to a bill.

**Blast radius is exactly one call site** — [`physicalInvoiceDocument.ts`](../../src/lib/physicalInvoiceDocument.ts) line 206 — feeding the screen preview, the PDF, the email HTML and the demand-letter exhibit together, so they cannot disagree. There is **no edge-side twin**: `send-physical-invoice-email` receives the PDF and HTML the client built, so no function needs redeploying. When the filter returns nothing, all three renderers already skip the card (`paymentHistory.length > 0`, `if (!rows.length) return y`, `if (… === 0) return ''`) and `buildPaymentHistoryTotals` returns `null` — the same path a never-paid bill has always taken.

## What this does NOT fix

The **opposite** error, on the claim amount. `sumApplied` in [`demandLetter.ts`](../../src/lib/jobsDocuments/demandLetter.ts) counts only invoice-linked payments, so on a job with unlinked payments the demand header can *overstate*: job 102 reads **$5,355.00 open** against a real balance of $2,355. Which bill an unlinked payment settles is genuinely ambiguous on a multi-bill job (job 273 has $38,780 unlinked across three open bills; job 251 has $18,880 unlinked against two already-paid bills), so it needs a rule someone picks, not a guess. Recorded in `to-dos/` — do not "fix" it by smearing unlinked money across open bills, which is the bug this PR just removed.

The **11 bills already sent** are unchanged by this PR; it corrects what the app renders from here on. Whether to re-send them or correct them in place is the owner's call.
