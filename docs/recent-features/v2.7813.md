# v2.7813 — People: the buttons on a person's sheet all read as live (2026-11-18)

The owner sent a phone screenshot of a person's sheet on the People page on 2026-11-18, with **Imitate** and **⋯** circled: "I don't like how these buttons look". Shown three rendered options, the owner picked A, one weight and nothing else, and added: make the ⋯ align right.

## What was wrong

- Both presses were live. `PersonDeskHeader.tsx` drew them in `BTN_QUIET`: the hint grey for the words, the divider grey for the border and a lighter weight. Beside **Day · week · month** and **Email** in `BTN` that read as switched off.
- **⋯** was 24 px tall beside neighbours of 27 px.
- One press really was dead. `imitate()` returns at once when `login_attached_at` is null, and the row drew **Imitate** there all the same, so a press did nothing and said nothing.

## What changed

- Every press on the head row wears one look, `HEAD_PRESS`: the desk's `BTN` with one line height, so the Email link sits level with the buttons. The row stretches its presses to one height.
- **⋯** sits at the row's right edge (`margin-left: auto` on its wrapper) and its menu opens from its right edge, so the menu stays on a phone's screen.
- Which presses draw is a kernel, `src/lib/people/personDeskHeadRow.ts` (`deskHeadPresses`). A press that cannot work is left off, never drawn dead: **Imitate** needs a login that is attached. The line above the row already says *Login not attached yet*.
- A row with no press draws nothing, where it used to leave an empty line.
- The sizes, the words and what each press does are unchanged. The menu's items keep their own quiet look.

## Tests

`personDeskHeadRow.test.ts` holds the five states of the row. `PersonDeskHeader.render.test.tsx` pins the look: every press drawn wears the plain press and none is disabled, the menu is pushed right and opens from its right edge, and Imitate is absent where no login is attached, for a viewer who is not a dev, and on a roster row with no account. Five mutants (Imitate or the menu back in the quiet grey, the menu not pushed right, the menu opening from its left, Imitate drawn with no login) each failed a named test, with a passing control before and after. jsdom lays nothing out, so the heights, the level words and the menu's place were measured in a headless Chrome on the Sample helper at 390 wide, in light and dark.
