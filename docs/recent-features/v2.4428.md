# v2.4428 — Two more windows fit the screen: They approved it, and Email reports (2026-10-02)

No migration, no edge function. The follow-up v2.4424 left: the same shape that fixed the Submittals Edit window, on the other windows that centred a panel over a backdrop that scrolled.

## What was wrong

A backdrop that centres its window (`align-items: center`) and scrolls itself (`overflow-y: auto`) cannot show the top of a window taller than the screen: the window sticks out above the top edge by half the difference, and a scroll box cannot scroll above its own start.

- [`SubmittalApproveAllDialog`](../../src/components/bids/SubmittalApproveAllDialog.tsx) had no height cap at all. It is about 330 px tall, so it cut off only on a very short screen (a phone on its side, a keyboard up).
- [`EmailReportsModal`](../../src/components/jobs/EmailReportsModal.tsx) capped its panel at `92vh` and scrolled the whole panel, so its top was reachable, but *Close* scrolled away with the list, and `92vh` is taller than the screen on a phone whose address bar is showing.

## What changed

- **They approved Rev N.** The backdrop no longer scrolls and pads for the phone's safe areas. The window takes `max-height: 100%` + `min-height: 0`. The title and the line that says what the entry covers are pinned, as are *Cancel* and *Approve N rows*. The fields between them scroll (`data-testid="approve-all-body"`).
- **Email reports.** Same backdrop. The panel is a column held to `max-height: 100%`; the title and *Close* are pinned and everything under them scrolls (`data-testid="email-reports-body"`): the intro, the list, the person editor and the schedule editor.
- [`BIDS_DOCUMENT_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md`](../BIDS_DOCUMENT_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md) SU-11 names the approve-all window beside the Edit window.

## The sweep

A brace-matched scan of every object literal in `src` holding `position: 'fixed'` with a scroll (`overflow` / `overflowY` `auto` or `scroll`) and a vertical centre (`alignItems: 'center'` in a row, `justifyContent: 'center'` in a column, `placeItems` / `placeContent`, or a spread), plus the same test over every rule in the `.css` files. It reads multi-line style objects and shared `CSSProperties` constants, which a one-line grep misses. Three matches, all one-line: the two windows above and `JobPaymentMoveModal`, which caps its card at `92vh` with `overflow: auto` and is left alone. `DailyGoalsGateOverlay` scrolls a fixed layer but starts its column at the top (`justifyContent: 'flex-start'`), so nothing is cut off.

## Tests

- `SubmittalApproveAllDialog.render.test.tsx` (+1): the title, the scope line and both buttons sit outside the body that scrolls and every field inside it; the window is held to the backdrop and the backdrop does not scroll.
- `EmailReportsModal.render.test.tsx` (+1): the title and *Close* sit outside the scrolling body; the list, and the person editor once opened, sit inside it; neither the panel nor the backdrop scrolls.
