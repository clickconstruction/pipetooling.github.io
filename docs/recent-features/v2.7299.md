# v2.7299 — Receipt texts: one text for each charge (2026-11-14)

Punch list #299 (`to-dos/ticket-text/README.md`), the owner's call 3, answered on 2026-11-14 in the main session: "One text per charge" over the hour's fold the card drew and v2.7142 built. Calls 1, 4 and 5 stayed as built (the day's jobs on the page, text only with no push, every linked card). Edge side only: `receipt-request` is redeployed after the merge; no migration.

## What changed

- **`foldReceiptCharges`** (`supabase/functions/_shared/receiptText.ts`): every waiting charge is its own run, oldest first across holders; `RECEIPT_FOLD_MAX` is one and the hour window is gone. The text words for two or more charges stay in `receiptTextBody` and `receiptReminderBody`, since a link the office makes may still open several, but the sweep never sends them.
- **The reminder** (`_shared/receiptSweep.ts`): one a charge, each on its own link, inside the sweep's cap of thirty sends.
- **The guides** *turn on receipt texts for card charges* and *answer the text about a charge on your card* say two charges minutes apart get two texts.
- **The card** records the four answers under Where it stands, and its `next` and `blocker` lines no longer wait on them.
