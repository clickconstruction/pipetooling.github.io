# v2.8031 — Training: a course's size reads in one short line (2026-11-20)

The owner's phone walk of 2026-11-20 marked the Dashboard's Training card: under each course name, *30 lessons of about five minutes · about 3 hours in all* wrapped to two lines beside **Start**. The owner's words: "I don't like how long this text is, how could we shorten it up across the app". Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## The line

`courseSizeWords` (`src/lib/training/courseFacts.ts`) now writes **30 short lessons, about 3 hours**. Measured in the real card at 390 px wide, where the column beside **Start** is 261 px at 12.8 px type:

| Wording | Width | Lines | Spare |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *30 lessons of about five minutes · about 3 hours in all* (before) | 319 px | 2 | none |
| **30 short lessons, about 3 hours** (shipped) | 190 px | 1 | 71 px |
| *30 lessons, about 3 hours* | 155 px | 1 | 106 px |
| *30 short lessons · 3 h* | 129 px | 1 | 132 px |

The shipped line keeps both numbers a first-timer needs before the first press, and *short* carries in one word what *of about five minutes* said. It has a comma and no dot, so it holds to `plainWords.ts`. The second wording drops *short*. The third is the shortest, but *h* is an abbreviation and the dot is a dot list.

- **`SHORT_LESSON_MINUTES`** (7): a course whose lessons average more than seven minutes reads *12 lessons, about 2 hours*, with no *short*. The average takes the final check's 15 minutes off first (`FINAL_CHECK_MINUTES`), as before.
- A course with no `minutes:` reads its lesson count alone. A course with no lessons reads nothing, as before.
- `courseSizeWords` takes any `{ lessons, minutes }`, so a caller with the bundle's own numbers can use it.

## One rule across the app

- **`DashboardTrainingSection.tsx`**: unchanged. Both rows, required and **Available**, already read the kernel.
- **`SettingsTrainingTab.tsx`**: the course's facts line wrote its own sentence (*30 lessons and a final check · about 3 h · ships with the app*). It now reads the kernel: *30 short lessons, about 3 hours · ships with the app*.
- **`src/content/help/take-a-training-course.md`**: **Start a course** gains a step that quotes the line. The guide's own sentence about thirty lessons of about five minutes each stays, since the guide has the room for it.
- Not changed: the course page's chip *N of 30 lessons* and the card's *N of 30 lessons done* are progress, not size.

## To swap the wording

The sentence is the last line of `courseSizeWords`. For *30 lessons, about 3 hours*, set `SHORT_LESSON_MINUTES` to 0. Then update the three tests that pin the words.

## Tests

`courseFacts.test.ts` (new): the three real courses' words, every course on disk at 36 characters or fewer and clean under `plainWordsFailures`, the seven-minute edge, the singulars and the empty cases. `DashboardTrainingSection.render.test.tsx` and `SettingsTrainingTab.render.test.tsx` pin the new line.
