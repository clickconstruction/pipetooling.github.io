# v2.4927 — GC mode: compare quotes and carry one, for a dev (2026-10-08)

The Board's B5-b (`to-dos/gc-mode/BOARD_REAL_BUILD.md`, on branch `spike/gc-mode`): Compare quotes on real data at `/gc`, dev only. It writes the office's numbers on the ask (B1, migration `20261008020000`) and what a trade carries (B5-a, v2.4923, migration `20261008130000`). Guide: `compare-quotes-and-carry-one`.

- **Lifted word for word** with `to-dos/gc-mode/scripts/board-b5-b.lift.json` (branch `spike/board-b5-b-config`): `exclusionRows` with `ExclusionCell`, `ExclusionRow`, `inList` and `cellFor`, appended to `src/lib/gc/exclusions.ts`. `SubBid` gains `exclusionsAnswered`.
- **`GcCompareQuotes.tsx`** is ported from the spike's `LevelPanel` and `GcExclusionRows`.
  - Each quote is a column: the quote as sent, then *Is it in their price?* line by line, with a **cost to cover** box under a line left out or not clear.
  - **Exclusions** each get a row: expected, *included*, *not said* or *excluded*, with a cost box or the unit price they gave.
  - **All in** is `leveledTotal`; a quote with an uncosted line reads *needs a cost* and a plus sign and a question mark, and the lowest is named once every line has a cost. Then *Against our budget*, the drive, the plans they priced (a stale chip), their note, how long their number holds, and their alternates with **Take it** / **Put it back**.
  - A cost box saves on blur or Enter, never on each key.
  - **Carry this number** / **Carrying. Stop** and **Carry our budget of $X instead** show only while the project is pursuing and not lost.
  - Out of this step: nudges, marking an exclusion on a quote, adding an exclusion, award and paperwork.
- **Writes** in `gcIo.ts`: `setGcAskPlugs`, `setGcAskExclusionCovers` and `setGcAskTakenAlternates` update the ask (`gc_invites.plugs`, `exclusion_covers`, `taken_alternates`); `carryGcTrade` sets `gc_trade_packages.carried_invite_id` or `carry_budget`. An ask's write then reloads the board. A carry reloads the projects instead, since the carry sits on the trade, which only the projects' load reads; the board follows (the live walk caught a carry that never showed).
- **`boardRows.ts`** maps `carried_invite_id` and `carry_budget` onto the trade's `carried` (`'self'` for our own trade, the ask's id, `'plug'` for our budget, or none), and `exclusions_answered` onto the quote. `boardProjectFromView`'s signature is unchanged.
- **`GcTradeAsks`** shows **Compare quotes (N)** once a quote is in. The price card's trade line shows **Compare quotes** too, or **Set a cost** while the carried quote has an uncosted line; it closes the card first (`GcBoard`'s and `GcPriceCard`'s optional `onCompare`). The price card's *Trades with a number* and *Price so far*, and a trade's line, show the plus sign and question mark (`proposalUncostedWords`, `carriedUncosted`).
