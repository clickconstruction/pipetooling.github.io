# v2.7386 — Lien desk: every signing footer is the compact box (2026-11-15)

Taunya's go of 2026-11-15, after v2.7382 boxed the footer a leader sees on an awaiting notice: the draft, the office's *Leader here* line and the Ready footer take the same box. The held footer draws the same line, so it takes the box too. Client only. No migration, no edge function.

## Before

v2.7382 left four places drawing the 470 px signature box over a separate row: a draft signed with the pad or a code, the office's awaiting footer on someone else's job, an unsigned Ready notice, and a held notice on a leader's screen.

## What changed

All in [`src/components/jobs/LienDesk.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDesk.tsx), each handing v2.7382's `frame` to the line:

- **The draft** (`draftBoxed`: a leader, not blocked, the pad or his code, the kernel's press is `approve`). The footer's state words are the call (*Malachi's code signs it, and it goes in the run.*). The why and *Saving…* / *✓ Saved* are the note. The green press sits beside the paper. *He already okayed it ▾*, *Sign another way ▾*, the offer switch and *Already mailed? Record it ›* are the strip. With his typed name there is still no box.
- **The office's *Leader here* line.** The call is *His code or his hand here*, the note *The record names your screen.*, the press *Sign and approve ▸*. The awaiting row (the chip, *Pull back to draft*, *Leader here, Approve ▸*) stays under the box.
- **Ready, unsigned** (`readyBoxed`). The call is *Your signature on …*, or *Malachi's signature on …* on someone else's screen. The press is *Sign ▸*. The Ready row (the chip, the links, *Send the run*) is handed over whole as the strip, so the row's markup and its `data-lien-desk-foot="ready"` are unchanged.
- **Held**, on a leader's screen. The call is *Your call on …*, the press *Release the hold, sign and approve ▸*, the held row the strip.

[`src/index.css`](../../src/index.css): a row handed over whole fills the strip and keeps a wrapped press on the right. A long press takes two lines beside the code where one will not fit.
