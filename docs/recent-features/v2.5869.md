# v2.5869 — A card payment counted twice comes off its job (2026-10-26)

Found while testing the Collect desk live: a working job read one card payment more paid than it was.

## What was wrong

Stripe's webhook wrote one payment twice on two bills, 2 ms and 63 ms apart, on 2026-08-09 and 2026-08-10. A third bill, not on Stripe, was recorded twice by hand in April, 316 ms apart. Each bill read paid at twice its amount. No double has appeared since 2026-08-10 across 41 later Stripe payments. The third row comes off through Remove as it is. The two Stripe rows could not: `remove_jobs_ledger_payment_and_reconcile` refused every row on a bill Stripe marks paid, and the client's `stripeHoldsPaymentReason` locked the same rows read-only, pointing at Unwind and Void, which act on Stripe itself and would have undone the real payment.

## The rule

Stripe holds one record per payment it took: the earliest rows on the bill, by `created_at` then `id`, that pay it in full. A row past them is a double. Migration `20261026030000_stripe_double_row_comes_off.sql` lets the function remove such a row, keeps the bill paid (the reconcile is unchanged) and writes the history line with reason `double_on_stripe_bill`. `stripePaidBillHoldsRow` in `src/lib/jobs/jobFormPaymentPredicates.ts` reads the same rule from `job.payments`, so the row's Remove shows; an unsaved row stays held. `jobPaymentRemovedReasonWords` reads the reason as *a double of the payment Stripe recorded*. SQL bed `stripe-double-row` holds the scenario; the help guide *see which payments paid a bill* gains *A card payment counted twice*.
