# v2.4616 — Legal portal: the Lien grid carries dates and dollars, nothing anyone said (2026-10-05)

One edge function (`legal-portal`, deploy after merge); client only otherwise; no migration. Item 2 of punch list #85 ([`to-dos/legal-portal-firm-ready/`](../../to-dos/legal-portal-firm-ready/README.md)). Mockup: `mockup-2-lien-grid.html` beside the card — the grid row unchanged, and what travels behind it before and after.

## The defect

The firm's Lien grid shows every billed job with money open and a lien month, by the owner's call (punch list #41, PR 2: *dates and dollars, nothing anyone said*). But `readLienBook` sent whole table rows: `job_lien_desk_items` with `fields` (the cover letter, a skip's reason and who skipped it, the owner's answers), `hold_reason`, `word_note` and who drafted, approved or held; `job_lien_filings` with the office's note on the copy, every address it was sent to and the Drive link; `job_property_owners` with the owner's email; `customer_addresses` with the office's free-text note. The page drew none of it, and a firm with no matters at all received it.

## The decision

Asked again 2026-10-05: **the whole book, stripped.** The grid is counsel's early look at where a lien is about to be lost, and a matter is referred after that point. The firm reads dates and dollars; the office's words stay home. The GC's standing notice rule (ask · send · hold) stays in the payload: it is a rule, not a thing anyone said, and the book's *held* state depends on it.

## What changed

- [`_shared/legalLienBookShape.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/legalLienBookShape.ts): one column list per table (`LIEN_BOOK_DESK_ITEM_COLUMNS`, `…FILING…`, `…OWNER…`, `…ADDRESS…`, `…GC…`), the select strings (`LIEN_BOOK_COUNSEL_SELECT`), and `shapeLienBookForCounsel(raw)`, which cuts every row to its list. `src/lib/legal/legalLienBookShape.ts` re-exports it for the client and its test.
- `legal-portal` `readLienBook`: the four selects name their columns, and the result goes through the shaper before it is returned — a select widened later cannot leak by itself.
- The portal's *What you cannot do* no longer promises that the firm sees nothing of unreleased accounts; it says what the grid is.
