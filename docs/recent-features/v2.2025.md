# v2.2025 — Payment chase loop: card + call mode + touches (2026-08-21)

Owner-approved "Payment Chase Loop" v2 mockup (rebuilt from a walk-through of the actual calls), built whole. The loop: a billed row past its expected date with no promise owes an "ask when" call; a promise unpaid 7 days past its date returns as a broken promise; can't-reach snoozes (1/3/7d, caller's pick) then re-enters; any touch quiets the customer 3 days; unresolved disputes hold their bill out of the ask queue; a paid bill falls out on its own.

- **Migration `20260822000000_payment_chase_touches.sql`**: the call log (`job_payment_chase_touches`) + gated RPCs `add_payment_chase_touch` / `resolve_payment_chase_dispute` / `list_payment_chase_touches`, mirroring the promised-pay-dates access pattern (see [`docs/migrations/20260822000000_payment_chase_touches.md`](../migrations/20260822000000_payment_chase_touches.md)). Client is fail-soft pre-`db push` — the card just stays hidden.
- **Kernel** [`paymentChase.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/paymentChase.ts): `buildPaymentChaseQueue` (due/waiting/disputes per customer over billed StageRows + pay speeds + promises + touches), `summarizePaymentChase`, `parseChaseTouchesRpc`; 8 tests. Constants: `BROKEN_PROMISE_GRACE_DAYS` 7, `TOUCH_QUIET_DAYS` 3, `DEFAULT_SNOOZE_DAYS` 7, Collections nudge at 2 broken promises.
- **Card** in [`PipelineOverview`](../../src/components/jobs/PipelineOverview.tsx) (office roles only): "📞 Ask N customers when they'll pay — $X" + tier chips (Never asked / Broken promise / Waiting / Dispute), quiet "everyone asked" state, hidden when nothing anywhere. Derives from the **lean stats spine** — `LEAN_STATS_JOB_COLUMNS` gains `customer_id` (additive; parity fixtures updated) and `fetchStagesHeaderStats`/`JobsListCacheContext` expose `leanBilledRows`, so the card is live on first paint without loading the billed scope.
- **Call mode** [`PaymentChaseModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/PaymentChaseModal.tsx): queue snapshotted on open (no mid-session reshuffle); rail = due (biggest late $ first) / waiting / disputes; per-customer card with tel: phone (from `customers.contact_info`), pay-speed line, evidence-bearing bill checkboxes (billed date · channel · partials · chip label), not-late bills ride along; outcomes = Friday quick-picks + date (promise per checked bill via `set_job_promised_pay_date` + touch per job), Stripe **Resend** inline on eligible bill lines (send + touch), dispute flag, can't-reach with snooze select, note-only; wrap-up with session receipts. `?chase=1` deep link (consume-once, gcReview pattern).
