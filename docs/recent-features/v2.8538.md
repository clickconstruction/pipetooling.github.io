# v2.8538 — Lien desk: the pinned strip takes a thumb on a phone, and a long chip no longer runs off it (2026-11-24)

Loose ends 4 and 6 of punch list #415, the Lien desk's leftovers from the ledger (#361). Client only: no migration, no edge function. Loose end 4 was written as the owner's call. Diane answered both on 2026-11-24 from labeled pictures: one sliding line for a phone, and wrap for a chip too long at a desk.

## What was wrong

The strip is the line that pins to the top of a notice pane once its gates scroll away (v2.3522). Every chip on it is a press (v2.7318).

- **On a phone** each chip was 18 px tall, and so was its touch area. The strip wrapped to three lines and pinned 81 px of the screen. The owner's answer 5 of 2026-11-20 grew every press on the months card to 44 px, and not the strip's.
- **A chip could not wrap** (`lienRowChipStyle`, `whiteSpace: 'nowrap'`). The path's next step on a missed notice is a whole sentence. Walk 1 of #415 saw it live on 2026-11-24: the chip was cut at the right edge and the pane slid sideways, 150 px at 380 px and 152 px at 730 px.

## What changed

- [`lienDeskPaneStyles.ts`](../../src/components/jobs/lienDeskPaneStyles.ts): the strip's two shapes are a kernel. `lienStripStyle(phone)` is the wrapping strip at a desk and one line 44 px tall on a phone, which slides sideways. `lienStripChipFit(phone)` lets a chip wrap inside its own shape at a desk and keeps its one line on a phone. `lienStripLabelStyle(phone)` cuts the job's name at two fifths of a phone's line, so the first chip is on the screen whatever the job is called.
- [`index.css`](../../src/index.css): on a phone every press on the strip takes a 44 px touch area, 13 px over and under its 18 px. No scrollbar is drawn, and the line fades at its right edge, where a cut chip says there is more.
- [`LienDeskNoticePane.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskNoticePane.tsx) and [`LienDesk.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDesk.tsx): the pane draws the strip from the kernel, and a chip's fit rides its style.

## Not changed

A chip's look: its colours, its 18 px, its words. The strip at a desk when every chip fits. The affidavit and retainage panes, which have no such strip.
