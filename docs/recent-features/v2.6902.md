# v2.6902 — The Bridge's old sheet email is retired (punch list #303, after PR 5) (2026-11-10)

The owner's yes of 2026-11-10 to the question PR 5 left: retire `bridge-sheet-email`. The function mailed the Monday sheet to whoever pressed **Email me this sheet** on `/bridge?view=monday`. v2.6840 took that view and its press off the page, and v2.6888 moved the weekly email to the first screen, so nothing built or sent the sheet any more.

## What changed

- **The function is gone** (`supabase/functions/bridge-sheet-email`): its folder, its `config.toml` block, its name in the copy's `IMPORTERS` (32 now) and in the dev-mcp catalog, and its section in `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`.
- **The old sheet's kernel is gone** (`src/lib/bridge/mondaySheet.ts` and its test): `buildMondaySheet`, the three renderers and `peopleLines` had no caller left but the catalog's sample.
- **The catalogs** (`emailCatalog.ts`, `teamEmails.ts`, `teamSampleEmails.ts`, `customerSurfaceRegistry.ts`): the entry `bridge_monday_sheet` leaves each. 57 emails in `EMAIL_CATALOG`.
- **A miss from v2.6888, put right** (`teamEmails.ts`): Settings → Team emails still drew the weekly email with the old sheet's label, subject and sample, because its row pointed at the sheet's sample. The row now reads *The Bridge, the week's call, every Monday*, shows the first screen's sample, and points at **The Bridge → The flow → Weekly email**.
- **The words** (`weeklySend.ts`): the comment on `BRIDGE_ROLES` names the one function under the page. `weeklySend.test.ts` holds that one function to the list.

## Not in this PR

- The weekly email, its switch and its cron are untouched.
