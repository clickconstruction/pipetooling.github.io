# v2.3665 — Put a GC on notice: the four steps read as four steps (2026-09-21)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. PR 2 of 2 on the window (PR 1 is [v2.3664](./v2.3664.md), the footer under the bar and the dark-mode fills). Mock-up with the before, the first pass, the critique and the after: the Claude artifact *On Notice, Step by Step*. Approved as drawn.

## Why

The owner, on the *Put RMC- Dudley Mason on notice* window: *"the concept of this is pretty good. But as a user goes down the page, I think it could look better … one of the places it could look better is in distinguishing each of the four steps."*

Measured on that GC (6 jobs, $54,850): the body was 2,125px tall in a 614px window — three and a half screens that looked the same at every scroll position. A step's label was the quietest text on the page (10px grey capitals, the style of a table header), the two preamble boxes and the four steps were six identical boxes, a finished Step 1 still spent six rows and a dead *Use all found · 0*, twelve red *window closed* chips out-shouted the five months with a live deadline, *! kind unknown — commercial dates shown…* repeated on every row, the money was said twice and never totalled, and three permanent changes to a GC read as wrapping fine print. A first pass — numbered headers and a status pill — was rejected in the mock-up: the numbers scroll away with their headings. A wizard was rejected too: the leader arrives from his Dashboard card to review everything and approve.

## What shipped

- **Kernel** — [`gcOnNoticeSteps.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/gcOnNoticeSteps.ts) (10 tests): `buildGcNoticeSteps` returns the four steps with a live `status` and a `tone` (`done` · `attention` · `open` — owners is `attention` while any owner is missing or unconfirmed and says what is left: *1 found · press Use · 1 to find · 1 public · left out*); `gcNoticeOwnersSettled`; `splitNoticeMonths` (windows still open / closed); `gcNoticeClaimTotals` (notices, open and closed windows, rows with no property kind, the total — public owners are not rows); `gcNoticeNextWindow`; `gcNoticeChanges` + `countGcNoticeChanges` (each tick as a named change with `label: from → to` and `alreadySet`); `sortOwnerRowsAttentionFirst`.
- **Shell** — [`GcNoticeStepShell.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/GcNoticeStepShell.tsx): `GcNoticeStepBar` (sticky at the top of the scrolling body; disc, name, status; `aria-current="step"`; names only on a phone), `GcNoticeStepSection` (a numbered disc on a connecting line, a 1rem title, one sentence, a right slot) and `GcNoticeStepPill`. [`useGcNoticeStepSpy`](../../src/hooks/useGcNoticeStepSpy.ts) reads which `data-gc-notice-step` is in view and jumps to one; the bottom of the scroll is the last step, and a container with no height (a test's DOM) keeps the first.
- **[`GcOnNoticeModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/GcOnNoticeModal.tsx)** — the header keeps the title (an inline warning mark instead of the ⚠ glyph) and the *first notice we've sent them* chip; the statute paragraph is a *What this does* disclosure. The strip and *What you're deciding* are one unboxed brief: the total, a two-part bar (on bills / not yet billed), and the facts as label–value pairs with the next window (red inside a week, *a run recorded today keeps it*). Step 1 folds to one line when `gcNoticeOwnersSettled`, opens by itself otherwise (the user's toggle wins for the sitting), lists the rows that want someone first, and shows *Use all found* only when there is something to use. Step 2's columns are *Windows still open* (blue chips with the days left, red inside a week, *None open*), *Also named · window closed* (month names; the date is the tooltip — `gcNoticeMonthWords`), *Affidavit by*, *Claim* (right-aligned, tabular) and a total row; the kind sentence is one callout with *set it ›* on the row (Edit Job). Step 3's letter is paper — a `data-theme="light"` wrapper, as the desk's notice — and the three side boxes are one amber note, the fills as a list, and a line. Step 4's ticks are rows from `gcNoticeChanges`. The footer is one row: the words, then *The leader said to send them…* (plain) beside the role's one filled button, a size up (`footBtn`).
- No kernel, RPC, write path or role gate changed; `approveAll` is untouched.
- Guide *send lien notices from the Lien desk* → "Put a GC on notice" describes the step bar, the brief, the fold, the open / closed columns and the change rows.
