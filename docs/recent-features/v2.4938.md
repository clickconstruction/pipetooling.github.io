# v2.4938 — GC mode: an answer about the plans reaches the companies on the trade (2026-10-08)

P3-b of the trade partner portal's real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/PORTAL_REAL_BUILD.md`; plan `to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/portal-p3.md`, both on branch `spike/gc-mode`). It is the first caller of `gc-trade-email` (P3-a, v2.4936), kind `answer`. The four choices follow the prototype's answer form and were approved by the lead. There is no migration.

- **Who hears an answer** (`answerRecipients` in `src/lib/gc/tradeEmail.ts`, the prototype's `questionRecipients`):
  - while we bid, every company with an ask on the question's trade, except one that passed;
  - once the job is ours, only the awarded ask, so nobody until B6;
  - nobody for a question about the job as a whole or on a bid we lost.
  - It reads the board's company record, which only a dev loads, so everyone else's window records the answer as before.
- **The open question's form** (`GcQuestions.tsx`):
  - A dev who may send (`canSendGcTradeEmail`) sees **It goes to** with a tick per company. A company that asked from its portal reads **asked it**.
  - **Send the answer to N companies** records the answer (`gc_answer_question`) and emails each company ticked: kind `answer`, key `<question id>:answer`, in its language through `tradeMailLang`.
  - It then adds the companies it reached to the question's `answer_sent_to` (`addAnswerSentTo` in `gcIo.ts`). That is a plain update under the office team's policy, as **It went to them another way** is. Each company's portal shows the answer from then on.
  - With nothing ticked, or for anyone else, the press reads **Record the answer**. Anyone who may not send reads *Emails to the companies go out once the portal opens.*
- **An answered question** reads *Sent to A and B.* For a dev, a company on the trade that has not had it gets **Send it to …**. That covers a failed send and an answer recorded before this. The key is the same, so a company that has it is sent nothing again.
- **The words**: main's `mAnswerSubject`, `mAnswerWhat`, `mAnswerQ`, `mAnswerA`, and `mAnswerSet` once a set carried the answer (`answerEmail`).
  - The office reads who has it in a toast (`answerSentWords`), and each company it did not reach in the window, with `gcTradeEmailRefusal`'s words.
- **The loop** (`emailTheAnswer` in `tradeEmailIo.ts`) sends one company at a time and never throws for a refusal.
- **Guides**:
  - `ask-the-architect-about-the-plans` gains *Email the answer to the companies*, and its old line that telling the companies would come with the company record is gone.
  - `email-a-trade-partner-from-gc-mode` names the questions window as the first sender.
  - `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`'s questions sentence joins the docs PR after the Board's #4972.
- **Tests**:
  - `gcTradeEmail.test.ts`: who hears it while bidding, once ours, lost, job-wide and with no record; the email's words in both languages and the set line; the office's words.
  - `GcQuestions.render.test.tsx`: the ticks and one press; *Sent to* and **Send it to …**; the line for someone who may not send.
- **Live**: waits on P3-a's deploy and the owner's inbox (call 3). A dev answers a question on the test project with the test company ticked, and the lead presses it or Grace confirms.
