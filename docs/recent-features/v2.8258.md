# v2.8258 — Lien desk: the last day of work is changed where it sits (punch list #399, the first screen, its press) (2026-11-21)

Punch list #399 (`to-dos/values-in-place/README.md`). v2.8222 drew the last day of work bold with no press and left one question for the owner. He answered it the same day: *Yes, build it.* No migration, no edge function, no change to a kernel that works out a date, and no change to the window's write.

## Why

The rule says a value a person can change is changed where it sits, with no *Change* link and no edit window for a value already on the screen. The last day of work kept its *Change ›* link and its editor box, because it moves every lien deadline and that call was the owner's. With his yes, the day follows the rule, and the window that shows what moves still stands between the press and the write.

## What changed

- **The day is the door.** `LienLastWorkDayLine.tsx` draws the day with `ChangeableValue`. The *Change ›* link and the editor box under the line are gone.
- **A day nobody typed is a default.** A day from clock hours, a bill or the job's creation is bold, with its source chip after it and **Set by hand** beside it. The chip keeps its colour: blue for clock hours, amber for a stand-in.
- **A day set by hand is underlined.** A press on it opens its date box right there, on that day. The box offers *Back to clock hours*, or *Back to the job's creation day* on a job with no hours.
- **Keep writes nothing.** It opens `LienLastWorkDayWindow` on the day typed, with the reason field in hand. The window shows what moves and asks why, and its *Set the day* is the one write, as before. Cancel there leaves the job as it was.
- **Back to clock hours writes at once**, as it always did. It takes a day off, so it moves the dates back to what the app works out. No window stood before it and none does now.
- **A stand-in opens empty.** A day from a bill or the job's creation is no day anyone said, so its box opens with nothing in it, as the window does from the timeline's press (`lienLastWorkOpeningDay`). A day from clock hours opens on that day.
- **The box holds a day after today**, with *Pick Nov 21 or earlier.* under it. The window refused such a day before, and still does for a day changed inside it.
- **The same day keeps nothing.** Keep on the day the job already holds closes the box. The window read *Nothing changes* for it and kept *Set the day* off.
- **The Lien window's press opens the window straight away.** Under LAST WORK, *change ›* or *set the day ›* drew the line with its editor open, and *Save the day* then opened the window. It now draws the line with the window up, the day to pick inside it, as the desk's two panes have done since v2.7684. Closed with nothing saved, the line folds away again. *Worked later? ›* on the By date tab arrives the same way.
- **A role that cannot set the day** reads it bold with its chip, and has no press.

## The shared piece

`ChangeableValue`'s `fromDefault` took a string. It takes a drawn word now, so the Lien desk's source chip is the default's word and keeps its look. No other caller changed.

## The Months card on a narrow card

Every button on the card is 44 px tall there (v2.8119). A tinted value would have become a 44 px tinted box. `src/index.css` leaves `button.vipChange` the height of its words inside `.lienLedger`, and its 44 px touch area is the value piece's own. **Keep** stands at its own height, as the old editor's presses did.

## What did not change, on purpose

- **The window and its write.** `LienLastWorkDayWindow`, `LienLastWorkDayConfirm`'s table and verdicts, and `useLienLastWorkDayWrite` are as they were. One sentence in the window named *Change › Back*, a press that is gone. It now reads *To undo it, press the day and then Back to clock hours.*
- **No date and no kernel.** `lienLastWorkDay.ts`, mirrored into the edge functions, is untouched. The new words live in `lienLastWorkDoor.ts`.
- **The reason.** It is words a person typed, shown on the *Why* line. It is still changed in the window, with the day.

## Seen in passing, not fixed

- *Back to the job's creation day* is offered on a job with no clock hours. Since v2.7224 such a job may be dated from its bill, so the press can land on the bill's day and say the creation day. The words after it, `lienLastWorkClearedWords`, say the same.
- The note under an open box is as wide as the box, by the value piece's own rule, so the hint runs to three short lines.
- The console still warns about `flex` with `flexShrink` in `LienDeskViewTabs.tsx`, and about `font` with `fontWeight` on a button in `LienDesk.tsx`.
