## What changed

- **Kernel `src/lib/techWeek/weekSheet.ts`** (+ 5 tests): `isLastClockOutOfWeek` (no schedule block after today inside the pay week; with no block at all this week, Friday's or Saturday's clock-out, never a mid-week day off), `hoursThisWeek` (the closed hours inside the pay week, approved or waiting), `clockOutTime` (the company clock), `weekSheetWords` (*Clocked out at 5:31 PM. 41.5 hours this week.*, then the card's signal, head, move, mark and note, the customer's words of the week by first name and last initial, and the closing: *Have a good weekend* on a Friday or Saturday, *Have a good one* on an earlier last shift), `weekSheetSeenKey` (once a week a person on this device).
- **`YourWeekSheet`** (`src/components/techWeek/`, + 3 render tests): the bottom sheet with the lines above and one press; it closes itself when the office has picked no signal, and remembers the week once drawn.
- **`ClockInOutButton`**: the post-clock-out hook reads the person's `job_schedule_blocks` for the pay week beside the crew deck's eligibility and the trial verdicts (`weekSheetDue`), and deals the sheet only when no trial card waits and the crew deck does not deal (the card's decision 7). The sheet mounts beside the deck.
- **Guide** *see your week move* gains *After your last clock-out of the week*.

## Decisions

- **The schedule decides, the calendar is the fallback.** A tech with blocks reads the week from them; a tech with none gets Friday, so a Wednesday off never deals the week early.
- **Once a week, on the device.** A second clock-out the same evening (a forgotten tool, a second site) does not say the week twice; the memory is browser storage, a convenience, so a private window may show it again.
- **The deck wins the day.** Two sheets after one clock-out is one too many; the crew deck's day keeps the card on the home for the week's numbers.
