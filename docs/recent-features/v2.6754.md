# v2.6754 — Quickfill: the standing backlogs run oldest first (2026-11-08)

Decision 6 of punch list #176 (`to-dos/quickfill-needs-you-review/`). The review proposed a daily portion on the three stations that never reach zero, Prospects, the lost bids with no reason and Billed Awaiting Payment: *3 for today, oldest first, with Done lit when the three are handled*. The owner on 2026-11-08: *"No portion but I love the idea of oldest first."* So the order changes and the Mark does not: Done lights at zero as on every station, Worked it records the count above it.

## What changed

- **Prospects** ([`lib/quickfill/prospectsDue.ts`](../../src/lib/quickfill/prospectsDue.ts)): the three folds stay, missed then past then on time, and inside each the prospect waiting longest comes first. `prospectWaitDays` is the key: the days past the promised day for a missed callback, else the days since the last touch, a never-touched prospect counting from the day it was added. Until now the past fold ran by the days *over* its warmth's days, which put a warm-3 prospect touched 26 days ago (19 over its 7) above a warm-1 prospect touched 42 days ago (12 over its 30); the owner wants the one waiting longest on top. Ties go to the warmest, then the name. The Next line and the mark's note follow the first row as before.
- **The lost bids on Fill them in** ([`lib/needsYou/fillInLostBids.ts`](../../src/lib/needsYou/fillInLostBids.ts)): `compareLostBidRows` ran biggest value first; it now runs oldest first by the day the bid is old from (`lostBidAgeMs`: the day it was marked lost, `outcome_at`, else the day it went out, `bid_date_sent`), a bid with neither after those with one, the biggest value breaking a tie. The first cut keyed on the lost stamp alone and the walk through the page's own io on 2026-11-08 showed why that was wrong: six of the 63 rows carry a stamp and 56 have only the day they went out, so the fifty-six older bids sorted behind the six. Each row carries `lostAtMs` and `sentAtMs`. The Needs you line's count and words are unchanged; the deck orders kinds by their line's rank and each kind's rows as its kernel gives them (`useFillInRows` sorts with the same comparator), so the lost bids walk oldest first.
- **Billed Awaiting Payment** was already oldest first: `leanRows` (v2.5885) orders each fold by the oldest bill's age, ties to the most owed, and `leanRead.test.ts` holds it. Nothing to change; the card records it.
- **No portion.** Nothing counts *3 for today*; no station's Done lights above zero.

Tests: `prospectsDue.test.ts` (the order across the fixture and a case where the longest wait and the most over disagree; a never-touched prospect from its arrival), `fillInLostBids.test.ts` (the dates' order, missing dates last, the value as the tie-break). Guides *run the office day with Quickfill* (Prospects) and *work the Needs you list on the dashboard* (Fill them in); `docs/QUICKFILL_ARCHITECTURE.md`. No migration.
