# v2.4361 — Holding a press inside a window no longer marks or moves the row behind it (2026-10-01)

No migration, no edge function. The press half of what [v2.4356](v2.4356.md) found: [v2.4352](v2.4352.md) and v2.4356 stop a window's backdrop **click**; two rows react to a **press**, and a press inside a window drawn in them still reached them.

## Why it happened

React bubbles every event through the component tree, through a portal too. Two rows start something on a press held still:

- **Bid Board** — the hold that marks a bid ([`bidMarkHold.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/bidMarkHold.ts), spread onto the desktop `<tr>` and the phone card). Half a second still anywhere in a GC's notes ([`BidGcNotesPopover`](../../src/components/bids/BidGcNotesPopover.tsx)) or in Mark account opened ([`MarkJobAccountOpenedModal`](../../src/components/materials/MarkJobAccountOpenedModal.tsx), from a won row's job account chip — the chips stop the click, not the press) toggled the bid's mark, a write. The row then swallowed the click that followed, so the button held never ran: the notes' Save did not save, Cancel did not close. On a touch screen the row also blocked the long-press menu inside the window.
- **Checklist → Review** — the whole row is the drag surface (dnd-kit `MouseSensor` and `TouchSensor`, 280 ms). A 280 ms press anywhere in Cost this task ([`ChecklistCostModal`](../../src/components/checklist/ChecklistCostModal.tsx), drawn inside the `<li>`) lifted the task behind, a release dropped it, and dnd-kit stopped the click after the drop, so Cancel did not close.

`scripts/check-nested-windows.mjs` could not see either: both rows spread their handlers (`{...handlers}`), not named attributes.

## What changed

The rows ignore a press that starts inside a window drawn in them. One rule, [`windowInsideRow.ts`](../../src/lib/windowInsideRow.ts):

- `startedInWindowInside(e)` — the event's target sits outside the row's own DOM (a portal), or a `position: fixed` layer sits between the target and the row: the backdrop, and so everything in its panel. A press on the backdrop itself is the case `closest('[role=dialog]')` misses (the panel carries the role, not the backdrop).
- `bidMarkHoldHandlers` checks it in `onPointerDown` (no hold starts), `onClickCapture` (a window's click is never swallowed) and `onContextMenu` (the window keeps its long-press menu). The board's two rows and the bid picker's rows all spread it, so all three are covered.
- `ignoreWindowsInside(listeners)` wraps the Checklist row's dnd-kit listeners.

Why the row and not the window: stopping `pointerdown` / `mousedown` / `touchstart` at each window's backdrop would also stop the native event before the document, where ~30 menus (`SearchableSelect` among them) listen for a press elsewhere to close; and every window ever drawn in such a row would need the three stops. The row's rule covers any window drawn in it, now or later. The page-wide guard ([`modalBackdropGuard.ts`](../../src/lib/modalBackdropGuard.ts)) listens in the capture phase and is untouched.

`OutstandingByPersonSortableList` is exported from [`Checklist.tsx`](../../src/pages/Checklist.tsx) for its render test.

## Tests

- [`windowInsideRow.test.ts`](../../src/lib/windowInsideRow.test.ts) — the row's own content and a sticky cell are the row's; a backdrop, its panel, a box in it, a portal and a text node in a panel are not; the wrapper passes the row's presses and drops a window's. A `closest('[role=dialog]')` version fails the backdrop, portal and text-node cases.
- [`windowInsideRow.render.test.tsx`](../../src/components/windowInsideRow.render.test.tsx) — a stand-in row spreading the real hold handlers around the real GC notes and the real `BidBoardJobAccountChips` → Mark account opened; the real Checklist list around Cost this task, by mouse and by finger. Each holds a press on the backdrop and slow-taps Save or Cancel under fake timers. With the two behaviour changes taken out, every window case fails with the whole story: three marks, the fill line running, Save never saved and the long-press menu blocked; two marks and Cancel swallowed; the task lifted, dropped twice and Cancel swallowed. A press on the row's own content still marks or lifts (three control cases).

## Walked live

Local dev server against prod, as the owner; no hold or drag completed and nothing saved. A watcher logged the row's `data-holding` and every 500 ms / 280 ms timer (the hold's and dnd-kit's activation delay); a press released in the same tick on a row's own content showed both, so the watcher sees them. Bid Board → PlanHub's notes on Galloway Park: real clicks on a note, the hint and the note box, and a press on the backdrop (drawn inside the row, `position: fixed`) started no hold; closed with ✕. Started or Complete → b7's Ferguson chip → Mark account opened: the same, closed with Cancel. Checklist → Review → Robert → Cost this task: real clicks on the title, the task, a label and the Hours box, and a mouse and a finger press on the backdrop, started no lift; closed with Cancel.
