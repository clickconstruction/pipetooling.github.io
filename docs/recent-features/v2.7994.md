# v2.7994 — Painted maps: the walk says Precise Location is on, and shows the way to the switch when it looks off (2026-11-19)

Punch list #402, PR 2: the walk. The kernel is v2.7984. Client only: no migration and no function.

## What a person sees

While the bar is red, one line sits under the walk's length in every state (`PreciseLocationLine`, drawn by `PaintedWalkPanel`).

| The reading | The line |
|---|---|
| None yet, or a rough one still waited on | *Finding you…* in grey |
| 20 m or under | A green tick and *Precise Location is on. Sure within 15 ft.* |
| 20 m to 100 m | One amber box: *Precise Location is on, but the signal is weak here.* with the margin and *This part is drawn faint.* |
| 100 m to 1,000 m | The same box, led by *The signal is weak here.* |
| Over 1,000 m for 4 seconds | An alert: *Precise Location looks off.*, the phone's two numbers side by side, *A line cannot be painted from a reading that wide.*, and the steps for this phone alone |

The chip at the right of the length and the faint-line box are gone: the line says both. Paused or refused, the phone is not read and the line says nothing.

The map pulls back to the whole circle, dashed and with no dot, under the pill *You are somewhere in this circle, 4 miles across.* The first reading that is a spot brings it in to the person at the walk's zoom.

## How the wait is counted

`useRoughHeld` (`src/hooks/usePreciseLocation.ts`) holds the answer in state. When the readings turn rough an effect starts one timer, and the timer sets the state. A better reading, or no reading, clears both. Nothing reads the clock while it draws, which is what the prototype did.

`PaintedMapWindow` counts it once and hands the one answer to the panel and to the map. So the line and the map turn on the same draw. In the four headless walks the panel and the pill turned 4,010 to 4,020 ms after the rough reading was fed, and the dashed circle with them or one frame later.

The window hands the hook nothing while the walk is paused. So coming back from the phone's settings reads *Finding you…* and not *looks off*, and a whole new wait starts.

During the wait the map draws nobody and moves nothing. A first reading off a cell tower no longer slides the map to the middle of a rough area, as `follow` did before.

## What a second canvas must do

Card #387 draws painted maps on a Google map. Its canvas mirrors this, and the rule is in one pure file so that it can: `src/lib/paintedMap/marginFrame.ts`.

1. **Take `me` as `PaintedCanvasMe`.** It is the point, `accuracyM`, and `area`. `area` is true for a rough reading that has stayed rough. The canvas never judges that itself and counts no time: the caller holds a rough reading back for the wait, and hands no `me` at all while it waits.
2. **Draw an area as an area.** The whole circle of radius `accuracyM`, a 2 px dashed edge (`6 6`), fill at 0.18, and no dot in its middle. A spot keeps the 1 px edge, fill at 0.15, and the dot. Leaflet merges a new style over the old, so its circle is keyed by which it is. A canvas that sets styles outright has no such trap.
3. **Never slide to an area.** `follow` and `frameMe` both skip a `me` marked `area`.
4. **With `frameMargin`, ask `marginFrameStep` on each new reading and do what it says.** Hand it what the frame last acted on (`none`, `area` or `spot`, kept for as long as `frameMargin` stays on, across readings with no `me`), the reading, the map's own lines and places, the zoom and the bounds on the screen. It answers one of three things.
   - `{ kind: 'area', box }`: fit the view to the box with `MARGIN_FRAME_PADDING_PX` (28) on each side, with no animation.
   - `{ kind: 'person', at, zoom }`: put the view on the point at that zoom (18), with no animation.
   - `null`: move nothing.
5. **Keep Time off.** The painting window hands no `time` outside a map that is only being read, and that has not changed.

The pill over the map is the window's, not the canvas's, so a second canvas draws nothing for it.

## What changed in the code

- New: `PreciseLocationLine.tsx` (the line, `PreciseSteps`, `PreciseTick`), `usePreciseLocation.ts` (`usePhoneKind`, `useRoughHeld`), `marginFrame.ts`.
- `PaintedMapCanvas`: `me.area`, `frameMargin`, the dashed circle with no dot.
- `PaintedMapWindow`: counts the wait, gates `me`, and draws the pill. The pill sits 42 px up on a phone, clear of the map's credit line, which runs to two rows there.
- `paintedWalkWords.ts`: `walkSureWords`, `walkPoorWords` and `WALK_ROUGH_WORDS` are removed, since nothing reads them. The walk's two notes are `WALK_WEAK_NOTE_WORDS` and `WALK_ROUGH_NOTE_WORDS`.
- The guide `paint-a-map` gains the line, *When Precise Location looks off* and the steps. The Glossary gains *Precise Location*.

## Two choices the card left open

- **The lead is what is spoken.** Only the line's lead is a live region, so a screen reader hears the state change and not the margin that moves each second.
- **A desk's two lines are not numbered.** A phone's own steps are done in order and are numbered. A desk gets one line for each kind of phone, and a person does one of them.

## Not done, and known

- **Not read on a real phone.** The first walk on an iPhone with Precise Location switched off is the proof, and the margin it shows goes on the card.
- **The first reading after a pause can be the old one.** The walk keeps its last reading across a pause. If that one was rough and no new reading comes for 4 seconds after **Keep painting**, the line says *looks off* once more until one does.
- The two older pills over the map, for placing and for drawing, still sit 26 px up and touch the credit line on a phone. They are not part of this card.
