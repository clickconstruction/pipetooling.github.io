# v2.6121 — My Schedule and My day show a job's Click number (2026-10-30)

Fix, no migration, no edge function. Found during the live check of v2.6066 (punch list #196): a job added to My Schedule from the Add job window read *1085 · ZZ Test* in the window and *— · ZZ Test* in My day and on the card.

## Cause

`useDashboardSubSchedule` builds the label every My Schedule surface reads (the card's number line, My day's rows, the Add job window's overlap warning) with `subScheduleJobLabel(hcp_number, job_name)`. A job not in the person's assigned list is looked up in `jobs_ledger` by id, and that read selected `hcp_number` alone. A job with a Click number and no HCP number got the dash. Jobs from the assigned list were already right, since the older assigned-jobs reads bake the Click number into `hcp_number` on the server.

## Fix

- `subScheduleJobLabel(hcpNumber, clickNumber, jobName)` (`src/lib/dashboardSubSchedule.ts`) takes the Click number and reads the number through `effectiveJobLedgerNumber`: the HCP number when present, else the Click number, else the dash.
- The hook passes `click_number` from the assigned rows and selects it in the `jobs_ledger` lookup.

## Tests

`dashboardSubSchedule.test.ts` (+3): a Click-only job reads its Click number, trimmed; a job with both keeps its HCP number; a job with neither keeps the dash.

## Verified

Dev server, signed in as the dev, Dashboard at 375. The page's own read of today's blocks was answered with two made-up visits, nothing written: one on test job 1085 (the dev is on its crew, so its label comes from the assigned list) and one on test job 1064 (a Click number only, nobody on its crew, so its label comes from the `jobs_ledger` lookup this fix changes). The `jobs_ledger` reads were real. My day reads *1085 · ZZ Test* and *1064 · ZZ TEST sink*, the 1064 card reads *1064 · 1 Test Street…*, and no dash is left on the page. The stub and the kept copy of the stubbed day were cleared after.

## Left as is

- The card's **Link job pictures** request to dispatch still sends `hcp_number` alone; that request's own words are dispatch's, not this label.
