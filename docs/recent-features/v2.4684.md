# v2.4684 — GC mode: the New Project kernels from the October rounds move into the app (2026-10-06)

The second pure-code step of the GC mode real build (PR 1b of `to-dos/gc-mode/NEW_PROJECT_REAL_BUILD.md`
on branch `spike/gc-mode`), on the owner's word of 2026-10-06 that the prototype's shape is settled.
Like the first (#4448), nothing touches the database or a screen: the kernels the prototype proved
move into `src/lib/gc/` word for word, with tests that stand on their own data.

- **`sheets.ts`**: the sheet index as one table (rows from a plan PDF, a paste or typing, what is wrong
  with each row, the next sheet number), the disciplines a sheet can be put in, and a sheet's number
  and title read from its page's title block. pdf.js stays in the screen; the kernel takes its text items.
- **`budgets.ts`**: each trade's budget over the project's size, to the cent.
- **`drive.ts`**: what a Google Drive link points at, and what stops one. The sharing check itself is
  the `gc-drive-access` function of a later step; the prototype's stand-in stays on the branch.
- **`setKinds.ts`**: what a later set can be called, the next one's name, and the owner's words for a
  bid, pricing and permit set.
- **`customerRole.ts`**: the owner, another general contractor, or an owner's rep, in words.
- **`exclusions.ts`**: each trade's usual exclusions and the shared name for what a company writes.
- **`scopeBook.ts`**: the scope book, read from every scope on our jobs, the usual lines and the lines
  added late on finished jobs. One change on the way, as the plan said: it takes the projects, the
  store and the finished jobs' lines as its input instead of the whole prototype state, so the real
  build passes its closed jobs.
- `types.ts` gains the customer role, the scope book's store, and a sheet's discipline and page.

Next in the plan: the first migration (projects, trades, scope and the scope book), then plan sets and
questions, then New project on real data behind the dev gate.
