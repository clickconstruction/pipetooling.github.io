# v2.5546 — Messages: a teammate's number has a home on Who is this? (2026-10-14)

The owner's ask of 2026-10-14, on the *Who is this?* card: "Sometimes a number is an internal team member. I think it's important to be able to list them because there are some benefits that we can get from automated messaging from the app to internal team members." A teammate's text on the team number landed as a number nobody has named, and the card had no answer for them: *Someone else* gave the thread a name and nothing the app could send to. Designed on the canvas with the GC board's bands (https://claude.ai/artifact/Jf7WpMF6pGMYVjWCT6RnD8, the bottom row: the card with the fifth choice, then *Which of ours?*); the owner's one change to the draft: a teammate's text belongs in Needs a reply like anyone's. Migration `20261015010000_contacts_team_kind.sql`; the edge copies of `contacts/names.ts` and `quo/rows.ts` change, so `quo-webhook` and `quo-send` are redeployed after the merge.

- **The eighth kind, `team`** (`ContactKind`): someone on the roster whose kind is not sub, through the spine's `person_id`, as a sub is. `kindOf({ table: 'people' }, rosterKind)` reads `sub` as a sub and anyone else as a teammate, where it read a trade partner before; the migration moves the rows already pointing at a roster person over, widens `contacts_kind_known` and `contacts_kind_record` (a roster row is a sub or a teammate, nothing else), and `name_contact` takes `'team'` on a roster row that is not a sub and refuses the spine's three kinds on a roster row. `contacts_subs_from_roster()` now gives everyone on the roster, not archived, a contact with the number and address the roster holds, so a text from a number on file lands on them with no card at all; it runs once in the migration.
- **The roster learns the number**: `contact_phone_to_roster(contact, person)`, a `SECURITY DEFINER` the office alone may call (`gc_office_team()`), fills a roster person's blank phone with the first number on the contact; `name_contact` calls it for a teammate. The office may not write the roster itself (`people` updates are the dev's and the row's own master's), which is why it is a definer.
- **The card** (`newLane.ts`, `NewLaneCard.tsx`, `NewLaneSlot.tsx`, `newLaneCardIo.ts`): *One of our own team* is the fourth choice, on both boxes, above *Someone else*; it turns the card into *Which of ours?*, the roster by name with each person's role and whether a number is on file (`rosterChoices`), a name filter, and a press per person that names the number as them (`teamGuess`; `io.name` sends `kind: 'team'` with the `people` record). `loadKnown` reads the roster with the guesses' records (subs left out, nobody archived). *Back* returns to the guesses. *Sort all* has it too, through the same slot.
- **Messages**: a new pill, **Our team** (`kindPills.ts`, between Subs and Supply houses and candidates), and teammates count on Follow up like anyone (`OFF_FOLLOW_UP` unchanged). The *Who is this?* window's search reads a roster hit's kind from the roster (`searchContactRecords`) and shows it in place of the kind picker, since the kind follows the roster. `kindWord('team')` is *teammate*; the exhaustive kind maps (`GcMessages` faces, `fromMessage` words, `OWED_USUAL`, the quick replies' kinds, `contactFlowKind`, which gives a teammate the unknown flow) carry the kind.
- **Tests**: `kernel.test.ts` (a roster row that is not a sub is a teammate), `newLane.test.ts` (+2: the roster rows and their words, a pick's guess and its words; the guess order on both boxes), `kindPills.test.ts` and `Messages.render.test.tsx` (the pill row), `NewLaneSlot.render.test.tsx` (+2: the roster opens in the card, filters by name, a pick names the number as that person; Back), and the SQL bed `supabase/tests/contacts_kinds` (the seeds give the sub and the two teammates a contact; a teammate is on the roster; a roster row is never a trade partner or a customer; a sub is not a teammate; the helper's number folds into the helper; the office person's number goes onto the roster, which had none; the roster flip makes the old sub a teammate).
