## The one thing "is this the best we can do" changed

The owner's words were *one press for the lot*, and the first cut approved every paper that waited. A notice is approved by the leader's signature on it (v2.5087: the run holds an unsigned one), so a batch cannot approve a notice without signing in his name. The press now approves the affidavits and holds each notice with *needs your signature*, the Lien desk's own batch shape: do what is ready, say what is held and why, and leave each held paper its door.
