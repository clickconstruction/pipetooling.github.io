# v2.7381 — Lien desk: point at a job on the Board to see which job it is before you press (2026-11-15)

Client only. Taunya's ask of 2026-11-15, on the Board: before she clicks a job and it takes her somewhere, hover over it and see the job's detail, with the address, the name, the customer and its status.

## What was wrong

A chip is a job's number, and since v2.7333 its press is a zoom to the right place for its stage. The only thing that said which job a number was stood in the browser's own tip: one line with the job's name, its GC and its money, after the browser's delay, with no address and no customer.

## What landed

- `src/lib/jobs/lienBoardChipCard.ts` (new): `lienBoardChipCard(chip, board, facts)` words the card: the number and the job's name; the address; the customer; the GC when it is not the customer; the paper (*Monthly notice* / *Lien affidavit*) and its bin by the bin's name; the one thing to know (the To do row's `detail`, else its `sub`); the day (*Due Mon Nov 16 · 1 day left*, *Was due …*, none for a paper that went); the risk (*Will not make its day as it stands. 3 stages left and 1 working day.*, from `atRisk`, `stepsLeft` and `lienBoardWorkingDaysLeft`); what is unpaid; the job's other paper on the Board in a sentence; and where the press goes (`lienBoardDoorWords`). `lienBoardCardPlace` puts the card under the chip, flush with its left edge, held inside the Board's width, and over the chip when the screen has no room under it.
- `LienBoard.tsx`: `cardFor?: (chip) => LienBoardChipCard | null`. A chip hands its element up on mouse enter and focus (`onCard`), and null on leave and blur; the Board measures the chip in its own box and draws `ChipCard` (`data-lien-board-chip-card`, `role="tooltip"`, 320 px, `pointer-events: none`). A 90 ms wait before it goes lets the pointer cross to the next chip without a blink; any scroll takes it away, since it is placed once. With a card the chip drops its `title`, so the browser's tip does not draw over it; the `aria-label` keeps the same words. A phone gets no `onCard` and keeps the tip. The leader's held chips take the card too.
- `LienDesk.tsx`: `chipCardFor(chip)` over `data.jobsById` (`job_name`, `job_address`, `customer_name`), `data.gcsById` and the chip's To do row.
- The Board guide gains *Point at a chip*.

## What "is this the best we can do" changed

The card could have been a window that stays until closed. A window in the way of the next chip makes reading the Board slower, and the ask was to see the job *before* pressing. So the card is a tip that follows the pointer, takes no clicks and needs no closing, and the press under it still lands.

The first cut showed only the name, address and customer the ask named, with the stage. The job's other paper was added: a job that needs its owner stands in both columns, and the card on either chip now says where the other stands, so the pair reads without hunting for the second chip.

## Seen live (dev server 5271, as the dev, no writes)

1440 x 900 on prod data: a **To send** chip's card stands 6 px under the chip, 320 px wide, inside the screen, with the title, the address, the customer and the GC, *Monthly notice · To send*, the owed-month sentence, *Due Mon Nov 16 · 1 day left*, the risk line, the money and *Its lien affidavit is in …*, then *Press to open the notice.* The last chip of **Ready to file** holds its card inside the Board's right edge. A **Needs the property facts** chip reads *The affidavit still needs the owner of record, the legal description and the homestead answer.* and *Press to open the property record at the owner.* Each card went when the pointer left, and no chip kept a browser tip.
