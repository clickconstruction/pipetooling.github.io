# v2.6203 — Linked crews on Schedule & Dispatch read in dark mode (2026-10-30)

Punch list #194's lens, a finding named by v2.6197. Schedule & Dispatch → People can highlight a linked crew: **Highlight linked** gives every block of one crew the same top border and card tint, from `src/lib/scheduleDispatchLinkedGroupPalette.ts`, picked by a hash of the crew's id. Five of the palette's ten tints were tokens, and five were light literals: `#eef2ff` (two entries), `#f5f3ff`, `#ecfeff`, `#fdf4ff` and `#f7fee7`. In dark mode the literals stayed light while the card's words turned light. A crew card on one of them read 1.27:1 (the title) to 1.64:1 (the time), and its `−` 2.47:1. On a token tint the same card read 7.3 to 9.27:1. Half the crews, picked by their id, could hardly be read in dark. The lens's sealed frames never switch the highlight on, so no sweep found it.

## What changed

- **Five tint tokens** in `src/index.css`, beside the emerald, amber, red and orange ones the palette already used: `--bg-indigo-tint`, `--bg-violet-tint`, `--bg-cyan-tint`, `--bg-fuchsia-tint` and `--bg-lime-tint`. Each light value is the literal it replaces, so light mode draws exactly as before. Each dark value is a deep tint of its hue, as dark as the other dark tints: `#232a52`, `#2c2350`, `#0f3139`, `#3a1a40` and `#232f10`.
- **The palette** takes them, so every crew tint is now a theme token. The two indigo entries keep their two borders (`#1d4ed8`, `#3730a3`).
- **The pins** in `readablePairs.test.ts`, both themes: every palette tint is a token. On every tint, a job card's title (`--text-blue-900`), address (`--text-600`) and time (`--text-blue-800`), a bid card's title (`--text-violet-800`), and the block's `−` (`--text-red-700`) and `+` (`--text-blue-700`) read 4.5:1 or better. The worst is the `−` on the amber tint in dark, at 4.76:1. `scheduleDispatchLinkedGroupPalette.test.ts` reads the lime entry's new token.

The `−` and `+` take their tokens in v2.6197 (#6600). Without it they keep their light-mode literals, which read about 2.1:1 on any dark tint, the five old token tints included.

**Checked** in the pane, on `vite build` + `vite preview`, as the dev in a sealed frame. The page's reads of `job_schedule_blocks` were answered with eight of the lens world's invented blocks paired into four crews, landing on the indigo, violet, amber and emerald tints. - **Light:** the cards draw `#eef2ff` and `#f5f3ff` as before, at 5.79:1 or better.

The before and after screenshots were shown to Taunya in the session.
