# v2.6734 — Lien desk: Do now names the master on his jobs instead of calling every leader *you* (2026-11-08)

Punch list #150 (`to-dos/lien-sign-on-any-screen/`), residual B; residual A is v2.6733. The desk's "leader" was a role (dev or master), so a dev's Do now put Malachi's awaiting notices under *Only you can approve · 3* with **Approve**, his unsigned ones under *Only you can sign*, his unconfirmed code signature under *Signed with your code, as recorded* with **Yes, it was me**, and the phone's step tiles said *you* on every one — the wrong man's words, and a confirm press the guard would refuse (only the signer's own sign-in confirms a pen or code signature).

## What changed

- **[`lienNextUp.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienNextUp.ts):** `LienNextUpInput` gains `viewerUserId` and `masterUserId(jobId)`; `lienViewerLeadsJob(input, jobId)` is the rule — a lien leader on a job whose master is him, or that has none, leads it; a dev on another master's job does not. The awaiting rows, the unsigned ready row and the unconfirmed pen or code row read `leads(jobId)` in place of `leader`, so on another master's job a leader gets the office's rows: *Waiting on Malachi · since Oct 4* with no button (the row still opens the pane, where his line with his code waits since v2.5791), *Approved · ready to send · unsigned, waits on Malachi*, and the plain send row in place of the confirm row. The group labels did not change; they are true now. Without a viewer or a master lookup (the Pipeline's and the AR chips' callers), every leader leads every job, as before.
- **[`LienDeskModal`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskModal.tsx):** hands Do now `authUserId` and the job's `master_user_id`; `stepFactsFor`'s `viewerIsLeader` and `LienDeskNextUp`'s new `leadsRow` are per row, so the step card's *Waiting on you. One press, or hold it.* and the phone tiles' *you* appear only on the viewer's own jobs. The Ready footer's confirm group (**Yes, it was me ▸** / **Not me**, **Yes, I signed it ▸** / **Not what I signed**) draws only when the viewer is the job's master (`!otherScreen`).
- **Guide** *send lien notices from the Lien desk* under *Start on Do now*: the two leader bullets say whose rows lead, and the phone bullet says *you* is the viewer's own job. The Glossary's Lien desk entry carries the clause.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **The piles say what is true.** *Only you can approve* holds what only the viewer can approve; another master's notice waits on him by name, as it does for the office, and the dev reaches his code field by opening the row.
- **No dead doors.** The confirm group a dev could press on Malachi's code signature would have met the guard's refusal (*only the leader who signed the paper confirms it, from his own sign-in*); it is not drawn for him now.
- **One rule, three readers.** `lienViewerLeadsJob` decides the rows, the step card and the tiles, so the list, the card and the phone agree.
- **Left as it is:** the rail's amber *you* chip on step 3 (`LienDeskSteps`) is the leader role's legend, not a row's; a dev with only another master's jobs still sees it. A per-row rail would need the rail to know the rows' masters; not asked for.
