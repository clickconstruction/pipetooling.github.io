# v2.4928 — GC mode: our bills to the customer read back from the database (2026-10-08)

Owner Billing's O5a in the GC mode real build. The plan is `to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/owner-billing-o5.md` → *O5a*, on branch `spike/gc-mode`. It is the record read back: the GC tables O1 made (migration `20261008010000`), turned into the prototype's `OwnerBilling`, so every billing kernel lifted in O2a and O2b reads real data unchanged. No migration, no function, no screen.

- **`src/lib/gc/ownerBillingRows.ts`** (pure) has these parts:
  - **`OwnerBillingRows`:** one project's rows, typed from `database.ts`. They are `gc_owner_pay_apps`, `gc_owner_pay_app_lines`, `gc_owner_pay_reminders`, `gc_owner_interest_bills` and `gc_owner_acceptances`.
  - **`payAppLineKey`** keys a line the kernels' way. A trade or our own crew goes by `package_id`, a change order by `change_order_id`, and `gc`, `contingency` and `fee` by name.
  - **`payAppFromRows`** turns one pay application into `OwnerPayAppSent`. It carries the period, the sent day, the work to date, the retainage and what it asked. Its lines become `doneToDate`, `worthByLine` and `storedByLine` (the last only where something was stored). It also carries the certificate (null while it waits, with its note when there is one), the retainage step, `final`, and the reminders, oldest first.
  - **`ownerBillingFromRows`** builds the project's `OwnerBilling`. `billed` and `retainageHeld` are the last progress pay application's, the rule `ownerAccount` reads. It also carries the interest bills and `acceptedOn`. It returns null when the project has no pay application, interest bill or acceptance.
- **What waits for later:**
  - Payments, paid days and the customer's promises are O5c's. They come from the Pipeline's own records on the billing job once O4a makes the bills.
  - Until then `paid` is 0 and every `paidOn` is null.
- **`src/lib/gc/gcIo.ts`** gains `loadGcOwnerBillingRows(projectIds)`. It reads the five tables, two at a time, into a map by project id. Bill the customer (O4a) will lay `ownerBillingFromRows` over the Board's `boardProjectFromView` result, as O3-ui lays the change orders.
- **Test** (`ownerBillingRows.test.ts`, 6 tests):
  - It is a round trip. Fair Oaks D's made-up billing is written out as rows, the way O4a's send will insert them, then read back. The pay applications come back field by field, less the payments.
  - The kernels answer the same on the mapped project: the next bill, the lines on each sent one, and the G703.
  - The rarer fields read back: a waiting certificate, a retainage step, stored materials, a reminder, an interest bill and the acceptance.
