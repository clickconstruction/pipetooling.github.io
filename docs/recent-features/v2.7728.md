# v2.7728 — Bids: the Pricing tab has a test that opens it on a priced bid (2026-11-17)

Tests only; no code moved, no screen change. Punch list **#46** row 1 (`to-dos/decomposition-queue.md`): the safety net for the cuts that follow on `BidsPricingTab.tsx` (`docs/BIDS_PRICING_LABOR_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md`).

## Why

No test mounted the Pricing tab. Every cut so far was checked by its own kernel and hook tests, the typecheck and a walk on the dev server. A cut that moves JSX out of the tab needs a test that draws the tab before and after.

## What shipped

**`src/components/bids/BidsPricingTab.render.test.tsx`**, 19 cases through `renderWithProviders`. The tab is a props component, so the test fills its 70 props; supabase is stubbed for the tab's own reads. The bid on screen is small and priced: six water closets at $800, four lavatories at $500, a floor drain with no price, $1,600 of takeoff material and 20 hours at $50. The grid's rows come from `scenarioPricingRows`, the kernel the page uses, so the figures are the app's own arithmetic.

The cases, by block of the screen:

- **The picker** with no bid, and **the start panel** on a bid with nothing counted.
- **The loading panel** while prices resolve, and **Retry** after a failed resolve.
- **The bar**: Revenue $6,800.00, Profit $4,068.00, Margin 60%, Multiple 2.5×, and its four tools.
- **The rows**: three rows with cost per unit, the price cell, the kind chip, the tag and the tag box.
- **The split**: a chevron opens Material, Sales tax and Labor ($1,800.00 of material at the company's 50%), the device remembers it, and the header opens and closes every row.
- **The Markup and Tax tools** open with their scopes and close.
- **The book-match bar** and the door to the price book.
- **The three cards and the profit bar**, with the bid's line reading $2,732.00, $6,800.00, $4,068.00, 59.8%.
- **A row's ⓘ** opens its margin breakdown.
- **A sent bid** wears the lock chip and the Sent line, and **Revise…** unlocks it for the session.
- **The header chip** follows the quote desk, and is absent when the desk says nothing.
- **The robot rows door** prices the rows that landed before a book was picked.
- **The two windows the tab draws itself**: the ✎ on a price card opens Price, and ＋ Add price, then Another price, opens the name window.

## Checked

- The 19 cases pass three runs in a row on the tab as it stands.
- Ten wiring mutants of the tab (a door that reports nothing, Revise that does not flip, a chevron that does not open, the markup dropped from the split, the saved price dropped from the row) each fail at least one case. The control passed first.
- A narrow `tsc` over the tab, the page and the test, and `eslint` on the test.
