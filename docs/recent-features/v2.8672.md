# v2.8672 — ClickTooling has a Privacy page and a Terms page (2026-11-26)

Punch list #447, the step Google asks for before its sign-in screen can be published. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was there

clicktooling.com had no privacy page and no terms page. The Google app behind Continue with Google (v2.8670) sat in Testing, where only listed test users can sign in, because Google's **Publish app** press stays dead until the app's Branding page carries a privacy policy link.

## What changed

- **`/privacy/` and `/terms/`**, two static documents in `public/`, the way `/fix` is one. A reader with no account opens them, and GitHub Pages answers 200 with the page itself, where an app route would answer through the 404 shell.
- **The service worker leaves them alone.** `src/sw.ts` denylists both paths, so a signed-in phone gets the document and not the app shell.
- **The sign-in page links to both**, one quiet line under "Issue logging in?". Plain anchors, so the browser loads the document.
- **The Privacy page's Google section** says what Google hands over at sign-in (name, address, picture, Google's id), the one use it has (to open the account that already exists under that address), that it reads no mail, calendar, contacts or files, and that the use follows Google's API Services User Data Policy with its Limited Use rules.

## What the owner still decides

The words are the owner's to approve, and this PR is not a lawyer's work. Five lines to check before it merges: the company's legal name, the contact address, the list of companies that run parts of the site, the line that no card number is kept, and Texas as the governing law.

## After the merge

In the Google Cloud console, project ClickTooling: Branding gains the two links, then Audience → **Publish app**. Both are the owner's presses.
