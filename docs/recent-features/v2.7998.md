# v2.7998 — Papers: add someone a paper went to onto a list (2026-11-19)

Punch list #403, step 3, the second of its two PRs: **Add to a list**. No migration and no edge function change. It rests on PR 1's fold helper (v2.7977) and on **Who is it with?** (v2.7995).

## What is new

- **Add to a list**, on the line of a paper whose party has no record, signed or not (`agreementNeedsAList`). The press is blue: it is the one act on a line that makes a record.
- **The same press on a signed paper's record**, under who the paper is for.
- **The window** (`PaperPartyAddToList.tsx`) names the person in its title, *Add Noor Haddad to a list*, and asks what they are:
  - **A new customer.** With a company, they become a person on file there. With none, they are the customer.
  - **A new supply house or vendor.** They become its rep. It needs a company's name.
  - **Someone at a company we already have.** A find box over customers and supply houses.
- **It says which papers come with them**: *1 paper comes with them: Mutual secrecy agreement.*
- **The button names the act**: *Add as a customer*, *Add as a supply house*, *Add to Ridgeway Builders*.
- **Who is it with?** now says, under its list, that someone typed in can be added to a list later.

## How they are added

Nothing is written until the last press. Then, in order:

1. The record is made when the choice needs one. A new customer is made in the app's own **New customer** form, opened on their name, so a customer is made one way. A new supply house is made by its name, and its kind and details are set on Materials.
2. The person is kept with the company as a row of its own list (`customer_contact_persons` or `supply_house_contacts`). A rep needs an address. With none on the paper, they are named on the supply house's own row.
3. Their contact is told what it is, through `name_contact`, the act Messages calls *Who is this?*. It points at the record, or folds into the contact that already is that record.

The paper points at the contact, so it comes along and nothing is copied. On a fold, PR 1's `contacts_fold_papers` moves it.

The list reads again, the line's tag reads *Customer* or *Supply house*, and the press is gone from it.

## Choices worth knowing

- **A trade company is not offered.** The mock-up drew it. The office has no page for a trade company until the card's sixth step, and the card's second look kept a party off no page of the app. It joins the window with that step.
- **The company's name can be fixed before the add.** It opens on the business the paper says they sign for.
- **A customer is never made by a bare insert here.** The app's form files it under the company's owner account and asks what it asks everywhere.
- **A contact someone already named** is refused in words: *Someone has already said who they are. Close this and read the list again.*
