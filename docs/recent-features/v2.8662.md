# v2.8662 — A check that came back keeps its lien releases held after the payment is taken off (2026-11-26)

Punch list #435 (`to-dos/lien-payoff/README.md`), the returned check's mark: what piece 6 (v2.8639) named as its second shortfall. Client only. No migration and no edge function. It adds one write, by the office, from a press.

## The gap

Piece 6 made the Lien desk say *Do not record it* on a saved release while the bank says the job's last payment came back. The desk reads that answer off the payment. The first thing the office does with a returned check is **Take it off**, and then the payment is gone: the release fell back to *Record the county’s stamp ›* with a red line about open bills, and the run's *Releases* card called it *short*. Only the case in Accounts Receivable still knew why.

## What it does

**One press in the case.** Under *Liens on this money*, when a lien there has a saved release that this case has not marked: **Hold 2 releases on the Lien desk**, its count from `countedLabel`. One line under it says what it is for. Pressed, it keeps a mark on each of those release rows and says what it did: *Held 2 releases. The Lien desk is told. Each release stays held until a payment holds.* A release that does not take the mark is named with its reason, and the rest are kept. Once every release is marked the press is gone and the box says the desk is told.

**The mark** is a fourth fact on the release row's own `fields` (`lienReleaseFacts.ts`), `fields.moneyCameBack`: the day the check came back (`ymd`), its number as the bank read it (`check`), the case (`caseId`), and who pressed and when (`by`, `at`). The paper's own fields are never touched. A release with no saved row has nothing to mark: nothing has been made, and the release tab's own money gate holds it.

**The desk heeds it** (`releaseMarkHolds`) until the job's bills read paid by a payment made on or after the day the check came back, and that payment has cleared. So it holds while the bills owe, while the payment that came back is still the last one on the job, and while a new check is at the bank. Cash, a card or a wire lets it go at once.

- **Payoffs:** a saved release reads *The release was made Nov 24. Do not record it.* with *Check 1184 came back Nov 27. The release waits for a payment that holds.* and **Open the bills ›**. A recorded one reads *Released, money came back*, with *The county’s record stands. Ask counsel.*, and stays in the open list. The card's line reads *The check came back. Record a payment that holds before a release goes out.*
- **The Release of record tab:** the same line in red over the county's stamp. The stamp's two values stay open.
- **The run's *Releases* card:** a marked release is held as *the check behind it came back*, and it does not print.

While the payment is still on the job, the bank's own words are said, as in piece 6: the mark speaks only once they are gone.

**Nobody takes a mark off.** A payment that holds does. There is no unmark press: a mark is true for as long as no good money stands behind the release.

## How it is built

- `lienReleaseFacts.ts`: `ReleaseMoneyBackMark`, read and patched with the other three facts (2 new tests).
- `payoffReturned.ts`: `releaseMarkHolds`, `releaseMarkWords`, and the view's `mark` (the rows, the press's words, the change) and `marked` (5 new tests).
- `useReturnedCaseLiens` takes who is signed in and the writer, gives the pane `mark`, `marking` and `said`, and reads the liens again after a mark is kept (3 new tests). The write is `keepReleaseFacts`, piece 5's, one update a release, past a row that fails.
- `ArReturnCasePane` draws the press and what it said (2 new render tests). `BankPaymentsModal` passes `authUserId`: one line.
- `lienPayoffStep` reads the mark after the bank's answer and sets `moneyBackMarked`; `lienPayoffCard` words its line by it; `releaseMoneyBackWords` takes the mark and what the bills owe (5, 1 and 1 new tests).

## Who may press

Whoever may apply a deposit in Accounts Receivable (`canRecordArPayments`), the gate every press in the case has. A client gate over the release row's own update policy.

## Where it stops short

1. **The mark is a press, not a side effect of Take it off.** The office may take a check off and never press it. Tying the two together would put a second write inside a money press.
2. **A lien with no saved release takes no mark.** Its release is held by the tab's money gate when someone goes to make it.
3. **No press opens a matter for counsel**, as in piece 6.
4. **A mark kept by mistake** is let go only by a payment that holds. A dev can clear `fields.moneyCameBack` on the row.
