# v2.5529 — Lien desk: a notice in the mail whose date has passed no longer reads as overdue (2026-10-14)

A fix to v2.5522, found in its live check of 2026-10-14. The runway let a month in the mail set the notice date whenever nothing else was owed, even when that month's date had passed. Job 258 (May printed and unrecorded, its residential date Jul 15, June to August on file) therefore read *notice by Jul 15 · -91 d* and sat with the overdue jobs, and the envelope's hover asked for the number "by Jul 15". No migration, no edge function.

- **`lienPayRunway.ts`**: the in-the-mail months that may set the date are only those whose date is today or later. A passed one keeps `state: 'mailed'` (the calendar still draws its amber envelope and its door to Record the mailing) but the runway reads on to the lien date, the way it does when every month is on file; `noticeMailed` stays true only when a mailed month sets the date, so the bar's *N in the mail*, *Record the mailing · N* and the GC row's words keep their v2.5522 meaning.
- **`lienPayRunway.ts`** also words it: with a passed month in the mail, the no-pay / pay / short titles add *The § 53.056 notice for May 2026 is printed and in the mail but not recorded; record the mailing on the Lien desk before the lien is filed.*
- **`LienDeskCalendarTab.tsx`**: `markTitle` takes the board's today; a mailed mark whose date has passed reads *The June 2026 notice is printed and in the mail; its date, Aug 17, has passed — record its tracking number and the day it went out, and it is on file*. `Track` gets `todayYmd` from the row's axis. The GC row's folded envelope on a passed date reads *1 in the mail · its date, Sep 15, has passed* instead of *by Sep 15*.
- **Tests**: `lienPayRunway.test.ts` (+3: 258's shape with its title, the only month the day after and on its date, 650's shape unchanged), `lienCalendar.test.ts` (+1: the mark stays amber, nothing counts as due), `LienDeskCalendarTab.render.test.tsx` (+1: the row envelope's hover and door, and the GC fold's hover). The 258, day-after, calendar and board cases fail with their change removed, and `> 0` in place of `>= 0` fails the on-the-day case; the 650 case pins that an owed month still leads, so it passes either way.
- **Guide** *see what is due on the lien calendar*: two sentences on an envelope whose date has passed.

**The first cut was dropped before it shipped.** It read a month printed after its own date as *closed*, from `job_lien_desk_items.printed_at`. Two things sank it: `markLienDeskItemsPrinted` restamps `printed_at` on every print (a *Print it again* or a later packet that carries the notice), so an on-time mailing reprinted after its date would have read *lien gone*; and the desk's own rule (*send lien notices from the Lien desk* → *A month nobody sends*) is that a late notice still claims its month while the affidavit window is open, and recording it makes the month *on file*. The runway therefore never judges a print's timing; the record step, which takes the day the envelopes went out, does.
