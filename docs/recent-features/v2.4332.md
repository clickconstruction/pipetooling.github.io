# v2.4332 — Submittals: a draft catches up with its takeoff (2026-10-01)

PR 5 of the parts train Grace asked for on Wendi's request ("NEED TO SHOW PARTS NOT JUST ASSEMBLIES IN SUBMITTALS"; v2.4319 parts on a row, v2.4322 the GC calls each part, v2.4326 the house's file builds the parts, v2.4327 procurement per part). Two gaps were left: a draft built before its takeoff opened an assembly (or before parts existed at all) kept its old rows, and BP375's three Josam carriers sat as their own hand rows ("Carrier for WC-1 and WC-2.") instead of as parts of the fixtures they hold up. Mock-up canvas https://claude.ai/artifact/JtoZEiwKP7zbABzXEy7hH8 (the refresh panel).

## What changed
- **A blue box over the row buttons on a draft** names what it could catch up on: the rows the takeoff reads differently (*The takeoff reads differently for LAV-1, EWC-1.* + **Refresh from the takeoff…**) and each hand row whose note names every tag of exactly one other row (*CAR-1 reads like a part of WC-1, WC-2.* + **Make it a part…**). Shared revisions never show it.
- **Refresh from the takeoff…** reads the takeoff fresh and lists each row as it reads now and as it will read, with how many parts and how many the GC sees; the rows left alone are named with why (*keeps the parts read from the house's file*, *reads the same as the takeoff*, *is no longer on the takeoff*). A row whose parts came from the house's file (v2.4326) is the newer truth and is never refreshed.
- What a refresh writes (`takeoffRefreshWrites`): a takeoff part the takeoff still has (same assembly item, else same takeoff line) keeps its record — house, lead time, stage, pages, call, procurement key — and takes the takeoff's name, count and GC switch; a new piece is a new part; a dropped part comes off. A part that opened into pieces (the assembly line read as one part before its contents loaded) hands its procurement key, lead time, stage and pages to the first piece off the same takeoff line, so a date typed on the log for it holds. Hand parts follow the takeoff's. The row's label, house and lead time are then read from its parts.
- **Make it a part of…** (`foldWrites`; the box's button, or **Part of…** on any hand row with no parts on a draft): pick the row; the window shows what it will list for the GC. The folded row's product, house, lead time, cut-sheet pages, note and call become a hand part after the row's parts. A row with no parts yet first lists its own product as its first part (with its call), so the GC still sees the fixture. Each tag's procurement line moves onto its part (`part_key`, v2.4327) unless another row still carries that tag. The row's call is rewritten from its parts, so a carrier the GC has not called leaves the fixture open. Then the folded row leaves the draft.
- Before v2.4327's migration is pushed, a fold leaves the procurement lines on their tags (the log keeps the fixture's line *as logged before its parts*) instead of failing.

## Viewport pass (the whole train, 1440 · 768 · 375)
Grace asked for the parts work to look great on several screen sizes; the pass over every surface the train touched found and fixed:
- **Rows table at 1440** ran 95 px past its box (the Edit column scrolled off): each part line's catalog words were `nowrap`, so the Submitted column's min-content was the whole string (496 px). `SubmittalPartsCell` now clamps them to one line with `-webkit-line-clamp`, which keeps the ellipsis but lets the column narrow; the table fits at 1440 and scrolls inside its box at 768 as before.
- **Parts editor**: an order-only part read *☐ Order only* (an unticked box naming the state it is in). The box now always reads **GC sees it**, with *· no, order only* beside it when unticked; the stage box is wide enough for *the fixture's stage*.
- **Choose from the takeoff** on a phone: the × dropped under the intro paragraph (the header wrapped) and the overlay sat at z-index 1000, level with the phone's bottom bar, which covered the footer. The header no longer wraps and the overlay is at 10060 like the other Submittals windows.
- **Procurement log** on a phone: the Item column's 19rem minimum (and one unbreakable *BRASSCRAFT/PLUMBSHOP*) made it wider than the screen. It is now `min(19rem, 52vw)` with long words breaking, so the tick and the item fit and the dates scroll beside them; desktop is unchanged at 19rem.
- **Refresh window** on a phone: the tag column took half the card; each card now stacks the tag and its counts, then *Now*, then *Will read*. A row with no parts reads *Now: one line, no parts: …* so the change shows.
- **The GC's room, end to end at 375** (get-submittal-room + submit-submittal-review deployed after v2.4322): two parts added to BP398 Rev 2's WC-1, the test reviewer approved the bowl and sent the flush valve back from a phone; the parts took the calls and the row rolled up to Revise. The thread line read *decided 2 rows · 1 approve · 1 revise* for two parts on one row; `decisionEntryBody` now takes the rows and parts (*decided 1 row · 2 parts · 1 approve · 1 revise*). **Redeploy submit-submittal-review after merge.**
- Checked and fine as they were: the catch-up box, the fold window, the bulk *Mark N ordered* bar and the GC's view (*See what the GC sees*) at all three widths. The GC room's own per-part buttons wait on the get-submittal-room deploy after v2.4322 merges.
