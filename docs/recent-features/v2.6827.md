# v2.6827 — The ground for one place for work paid in kind (punch list #301, PR 1) (2026-11-10)

In kind became a kind of payment in v2.6816. What the app still did not know is the deal behind the payments. The owner's calls of 2026-11-10 ([`to-dos/in-kind-arrangements.md`](../../to-dos/in-kind-arrangements.md)): the deal lives on the customer's page with a line on Needs you; nothing is entered by itself; what was given is a ledger of lines, each an amount, a date and a memo; a line counts as overhead unless it is put on a job. This PR is the ground: no screen reads it yet.

## What changed

- **`supabase/migrations/20261110071633_in_kind_arrangements.sql`**: `in_kind_arrangements`, `in_kind_lines`, `in_kind_line_payments`, the column `jobs_ledger.in_kind_arrangement_id`, their gates and the four verbs ([`docs/migrations/20261110071633_in_kind_arrangements.md`](../migrations/20261110071633_in_kind_arrangements.md)).
- **`src/lib/inKind/inKindLedger.ts`**: the reading of a deal. `inKindTotals` takes the money from the bills (the In kind payments on the deal's jobs and those jobs' open balances) and the story from the lines, so a payment typed by hand before the deal was recorded counts the same as one a line wrote. `inKindMonths` gives a monthly deal's months with their state (entered, no credit, due, waiting); `suggestedCredit` is the monthly bill less what left the bank, never more than is left; `lastCreditMonth` projects the last month; `parseInKindAddResult` reads the add verb's answer.
- **The bed** `scripts/pgtest-in-kind.sh` over `supabase/tests/in_kind/20_scenario.sql`.

## Decisions taken with the build

- **The jobs a deal pays are a column on the job**, not a list on the deal. Every reader that judges a bill late already holds the job row, and a job is paid by one deal at most.
- **One line is one transaction.** A month's credit that spans two bills was two windows typed by hand. The add verb writes both payments or neither.
- **The split is decided in one place.** The window's preview is the same function with `p_dry_run`, so the screen can never show a split the save would not make.
- **A bill on Stripe is passed over, and named.** Stripe has to be told through the bill's own window. The line acts on what is ready and says what it held.
- **Taking a line back keeps the row.** Its payments leave their bills; the line stays as the record of what was undone.
- **The office only**, for now: the same people who read the bank. Who else may see or enter is still the owner's call.
