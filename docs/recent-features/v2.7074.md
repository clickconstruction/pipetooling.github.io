# v2.7074 — Materials: a whole site on one screen (2026-11-12)

Punch list #330, PR 1 of 5. Grace asked whether the app gives a company manager a view of all the materials across an entire site, saw the card and the mock-up (`to-dos/site-materials-view/`), and said to build the whole train. This PR is the screen. It is read only and needs no migration.

## What changed

- **The rules** (`src/lib/materials/siteRollup.ts`), pure, 19 tests:
  - `sameWordsKey`: the same-words fold of a typed name. Case, spaces, punctuation, the inch mark as a word, a curly quote or glued to its digit, word order and a plural. Two sizes of one fitting keep the order typed (`3x4` is not `4x3`), a mixed number reads either way (`1-1/2`, `1 1/2`), and a bare `2` is never read as `2"`. Nothing folds by closeness.
  - `sitePartRows`: one row per part across the site's jobs, with its total, its spellings, and every box that holds it with the place, the job, the line's own count, a Can't find and a shortfall. **The fold reads the finished lines, never the ledger**: `containerLines` still sums each spelling in each box by itself, and a row adds lines. A count resets its line, so two spellings made one key before the sums would let one count wipe the other's pieces and call them missing; a test holds two spellings in one box at their own counts.
  - `siteGlance` and `siteWeekFlow`: the newest press on any line (the record's freshness), the boxes, the jobs, the place names, the lines and pieces, the boxes due now and the ones that fall due inside a week, the Can't find and short lines, and what came in and was used in seven days.
  - `sitePlaceSections`: the boxes by place, a name folded across jobs with the jobs named, then Unsorted, then No place yet.
  - `searchPartRows`, `filterPartRows`, `shownTotalWords` (*206 pieces in the 2 rows shown*), `groupDeskBySite`.
- **The screen** (`src/components/materials/SiteMaterialsView.tsx`): `?site=<development id>` on both mounts of the desk. Eight figures, Last recorded first and amber past seven quiet days; **By part** and **By place**, the pick remembered on the device; the search with its running total; Due a count, Can't find and Short at a count as filters; a row opens to its boxes and each box is a door to its job's page.
- **The desk** (`JobsMaterialsTab.tsx`): job rows grouped under a site row with the site's totals, *Not on a site* over the rest. `useOnSiteSites` reads each job's `development_id` and the developments' names, two small reads.
- **The field roles** (`MaterialsOnSiteTab.tsx`): a primary and a superintendent get a site row over their jobs, **Open the site**, with no money and the phone screen as each box's door. An estimator gets none.
- **The Development page**: **Open the site’s materials** beside the print press, for the office door.

## Where the build left the card

- **The screen reads the on-site store, not `on_site_job_summary` per job.** It is a state of the desk, and the desk has already read every job with a live box, so the numbers are the desk's own and a press on the phone shows at once. A job past the 5,000-row cap hands in the database's lines, as the desk's summary does. The card's section 6 is amended.
- **The Development page's door is a press, not a line with counts.** A count there would be a read with the page, which the report's own rule avoids.
- **The Go anywhere row and the print press on the screen move to PR 5**, with the report's By part.
