# v2.7985 — Values in place: one way to draw a value you can change (2026-11-19)

Punch list #382, PR 1 of eleven, alone. No screen changes. No migration, no edge function. The owner approved this look in #382's working spike on 2026-11-17 and named it for the whole app (#399).

## Why

On the spike's first screens the owner could not find the dates: "Dates that can change and words that can change are not underlined and clarified as changeable. And as a result, when I look at the sentences they all blend together." The fix he approved has two looks with two meanings, and no detour to an edit window. #399 then surveyed the app and found 60 other places that want the same look, so the piece is shared from its first day.

## The rule

It is written in `CLAUDE.md` → Working conventions, as **Values in place**.

- Every value is drawn apart from the words. A date, a count, a name or an amount is never plain text inside a line.
- **Underlined and tinted** means a person can change it. A press turns it into its own field right there, with **Keep** and **Cancel**. Enter keeps and Escape cancels.
- **Plain bold** means the app works it out, and it updates by itself.
- One fact sits on each line, the label on the left and the values in a column.
- There is no *Change a date* link and no edit window for a value already on the screen.

## The pieces

`src/components/valuesInPlace/ValuesInPlace.tsx`:

- `ChangeableValue`: the press and the field it becomes. Its types are a date, words, a number and a choice from a list.
- `WorkedOutValue`: plain bold, no press. `tone` turns it red or green when the value is itself the warning.
- `ValueSentence`: draws a sentence a kernel marked, the changeable kinds as the screen's own presses and the rest bold.
- `ValueLines` and `ValueLine`: one fact a line.
- `ValuesLegend`: *Underlined values change where they sit: press one. Bold values are worked out.* Each marked word is drawn in the look it names.
- `ValuesInPlaceOff`: everything inside draws with no press.

`src/lib/valuesInPlace.ts`, pure:

- `val`, `sentenceParts`, `plainSentence`: a value inside a sentence, marked `⟪kind|text⟫`.
- `keepVerdict`: what Keep means for a draft. It answers *same* (nothing saved), *keep* (a finished value) or *hold* (the field stays open and says why).
- The words: the press's name, the reasons a field is held, the legend.

## What #399 asked the piece to hold

| The need | How the piece holds it |
|---|---|
| A third look, shown but locked | `locked={{ tag, why }}`: no press, a dashed line under the value, a small dashed tag, and the sentence that says who can change it as the tip. |
| A company default this record may override | `fromDefault="default"` draws the value bold with the small word after it and a press that sets this record's own. A record with its own value passes `backToDefault`, and the open field offers that press. |
| Room around the field | `hint` (what the value is for), `preview(draft)` (what the change will do) and `reason` (a second box, required or not) sit with the field. |
| Saves of more than one shape | `onKeep(value, { reason })` is the screen's own function. It may return a promise: the field waits, Keep reads *Keeping…*, and a failure shows its reason under the field with what was typed still in it. |
| Kernels that return finished strings | A kernel marks its values with `val` and the screen draws them with `ValueSentence`. `plainSentence` gives a text or an email the plain words. |
| A look and a marker that clash with nothing | The underline is dotted and the words keep the text colour, so a value never reads as a link or as a name that opens a window. The marker is `⟪ ⟫`, not the `⟦ ⟧` pair `lienPaperGaps.ts` uses. |
| Phone height | At 640 px and under, and on any touch screen, a value's press is 44 px tall without moving the words, the lines stand 44 px apart, and the open box, Keep and Cancel are 44 px tall. The box types at 16 px so a phone does not zoom. |
| An off switch | `ValuesInPlaceOff` around a screen, or `off` on one value. The legend says nothing inside it. |

## How the field behaves

- A draft that matches the value it opened on saves nothing.
- An emptied field is held (*Pick a day first.*) unless `canClear` is set.
- A date is held until it is a real day with its year typed in full. The check is `isPlausibleDate` from the date box kernel, so a half-typed year is never kept.
- A number is held while it is not a number, is a fraction where `whole` is set, or sits outside `min` and `max`. A day is held outside `min` and `max` too, and the reason names the day.
- The screen's own `check` runs last and its words are the reason.
- A click or a key on a value never reaches a row or a card that opens on a press.
- Escape is taken in the capture phase and marked handled, so a window the field sits in stays open. The windows already check `defaultPrevented`.
- When the field closes the press takes the focus back.

## The looks

The classes are `.vip…` in `src/index.css`, because hover, focus and a phone's taller press need a class. Keep is the press blue under white words, and a waiting Keep wears the dead press pair.
