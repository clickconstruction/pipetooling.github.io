# v2.4019 — They said…: take a wrong promise off the record (2026-09-28)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. `void_job_payment_promise` has existed since `20260911051414` and the help guide has promised "They never said that" since v2.3280, but no screen called it. The gap showed in the live test of v2.3991, where undoing a test promise meant calling the function by hand.

## What shipped

- **`PromiseRecordList`** (new, 3 render tests) — under the form in `SetPromisedPayDateModal`: "On record for this bill · N promises", each with the day (marked "on the board" when it is the one showing), who said it, how, who heard it, the day it was written, and **never said that**. The note rides the detail line's title.
- **`SetPromisedPayDateModal`** loads `list_job_payment_promises` for the job, confirms through `useConfirmDialog` (danger), calls `voidJobPaymentPromise`, and — when the voided promise is the one on the board and no other promise on record names that day — `clearJobPromisedPayDate`. It reloads the record and tells the shell to reload its maps. The dialog scrolls at 92vh.
- **`paymentPromises.ts`** (+4 tests): `promisesOnJob`, `promiseSaidLine`, `voidTakesDateOffBoard`.
- **`paymentChaseIo.ts`**: `voidJobPaymentPromise`, `clearJobPromisedPayDate`.
- Help: `know-whether-a-customer-keeps-their-word` → "Taking a wrong promise off the record".

## Decisions

- **An older date is not put back.** Voiding the promise on the board clears the date; restoring the one before it would write a promise nobody made (the legacy upsert logs an event).
- **Void is for wrong entries.** The confirm says so, and points a changed date at Save — re-promising is what makes the earlier one read broken.
