# v2.6476 — On a laptop, the sort card over a thread is one row (2026-11-03)

The second of three PRs that fold a person's thread by height (the owner's pick A of three mock-ups, 2026-11-03), after v2.6474 folded the header. On a laptop window about 700 px tall the sort card took 258 px and scrolled on its own; folded it takes 53 px, and the lines of a Bid it? thread go from 222 px to 323 px.

## What changed

- `foldedLead` (`src/lib/followUp/newLaneFold.ts`): the press a folded card leads with. *Bid it?*: the first customer it found (`Start a bid on Marrex Builders`), once the invitation names a project. *Which job?*: *Keep it on* the default, or *Move it to* the job picked. *Who is this?*: the search. *Who has it?* leads with none, since the right person is a choice the card shows whole.
- `NewLaneCard` takes `foldable`: one row with the head cut to a line (the whole head in its title), the lead press and *Other ways ▾* (*Open ▾* with no lead), which opens the whole card in place for the card's life; the open card's head row gets *Fold ▴*. *One of our own team*'s roster never folds while it is open.
- `NewLaneSlot` folds the card when the open thread says `short` (v2.6474). Sort all and the Answer all desk give no `short`, so their cards stay whole.
- Guides: a paragraph under *Sort one* in `sort-new-messages`, and a line under *Read a thread on a short screen* in `find-every-message-with-a-person-in-one-place`.

## Decisions

- **Open stays open.** The card does not remember *Other ways* across threads or browsers; a new thread starts folded, and a card opened by hand never folds under the person's press.
- **The lead is the press the card already put first**, so folding changes where the answer sits, never what it does.
