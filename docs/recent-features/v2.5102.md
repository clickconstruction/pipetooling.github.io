# v2.5102 — Edit Job: a returned check fee no longer reads as covering the next line (2026-10-09)

Client only. No migration, no edge function. The ② waterfall residue that v2.5091 set aside (punch list #76).

## Why

`add_ar_return_case_fee` (v2.5033) puts the $30 on the bill the check paid: in its `amount` and as a `fee_lines` entry naming the case. `dollarCoverageForSegments` counts a bill's whole amount as spoken for and takes away only the dollars of the lines linked to it. The fee is on no line, so its $30 was left over and ran down the waterfall onto the first unbilled line. On Southern Post's shape (bill 1 = $13,680 rough-in + the $30 fee, Final $2,000 unbilled) the Bill tab read *Left to bill $2,000*, which is right, but the Final line read *$30 of $2,000 covered*. A bill of that line alone came to $1,970 and left $30 to bill: the money came out whole, split oddly.

## What changed

- **`dollarCoverageForSegments`** (`src/lib/jobs/jobSegmentsCoverage.ts`) attributes the returned check fees (`returnedCheckFeeCents`) on the bills it already counts as spoken for: paid, or open (`ready_to_bill` / `billed`) and not the elastic primary bundle. The remainder (`remainingDollars`) is unchanged, since the Job Total holds the fee as a rider (v2.5091) and the bill holds it in its amount.
- Everything that reads the coverage follows: the Bill tab's line words and hatch, which lines can be ticked, **Bill the N picked** (`segmentSelectionNetSummary`), a typed amount matching one line (`exactSingleSegmentMatchForAmount`) and the split-payer carve.
- A bill made by amount that carries a fee now covers its work dollars only.
- A `fee_lines` entry that names no case is not a returned check fee and still counts as money on the bill, as before.

- **`JobFormModal.returnedCheckFee.render.test.tsx` (+1):** the real Edit Job on Southern Post's shape; the money card shows no *covered* words and the Final line reads *not billed*. It failed before the fix.
