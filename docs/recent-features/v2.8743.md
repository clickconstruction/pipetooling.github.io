# v2.8743 — Which site is it for?: the title and the × stay on a phone's screen with the keyboard up (2026-11-27)

Finding 337 of the fixing ledger, from the simulator's sitting 3 of run 8 (an iPhone SE, 375 by 667, Safari, the live window, nobody named as the signer). Punch list #439, piece 3's site picker. Client only: no migration, no function, nothing owed after the merge.

## What was wrong

In **Which site is it for?**, with the address box in hand and the keyboard up on a small phone, the window's head and its **×** were above the top of the screen. The rows found stood whole over the keyboard, so the picker worked, and its way out was hidden until the keyboard went.

`PaperSitePicker.tsx` pinned its backdrop to the page, `inset: 0`, and centred the window in it. An iPhone does not shrink the page for the keyboard: it shrinks the part that shows and slides it down the page to the field. A window centred on the whole page then has its top above the screen. It is the fault the thread window had before v2.8267.

## What changed

- **The backdrop follows the part of the page that shows** on a phone (`useVisualViewportBox`, `visibleBand`), as the thread window and the task windows do. A page pinched larger is left pinned.
- **The head never scrolls.** The title and the **×** sit outside the part of the window that scrolls. Before, the whole window scrolled as one.
- **While the room is short the window has an order**, in `src/lib/addresses/paperSitePickerFrame.ts` (`pickerRoom`, pure). Under 480 px of screen showing: the lead gives way, and **It is not for a site** gives way once something is typed. An empty box brings it back. The address box and the rows keep the room.
- At a desk, and where the browser cannot say what shows, the window is as it was, but for the head, which no longer scrolls away under a long list.

Both doors to the window get it: **Name a site…** in Send a paper, and **Find by site…** on Documents → Agreements.

## Why the fold, and not the follow alone

A small phone shows about 280 px with the keyboard up. The head, the lead, the first choice and the box are about 215 px tall. Following alone would have put the head back and hidden the rows under the window's own foot. The first choice answers *no site*, and a person typing an address is not choosing it.

Open to a flip: the first choice could stay and the rows scroll under it.

## Tested

- `paperSitePickerFrame.test.ts`, 6 cases: a desk, a browser that cannot say, the backdrop's place with the page slid, room to spare, the lead, the first choice with a typed and an empty box.
- `PaperSitePicker.render.test.tsx`, 5 smokes with a faked phone and a faked visual viewport: the backdrop moves when the keyboard comes up and the head is outside what scrolls; the lead and the first choice give way and the choice comes back; nothing gives way with the keyboard down; a desk is pinned as it was; a phone whose browser cannot say is pinned too.
- The 15 cases of `AgreementsSendPaper.site.render.test.tsx` pass unchanged.
