# v2.4530 — Overhead: Man hours moves up the page (2026-10-05)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. The owner's call after the screenshot pass (v2.4516): the card was the one thing on the Overhead tab about hours, and it sat about 1,500 px down, under the pool chart, the people table and the maintenance strip.

## What changed

- **The mount moved** in [`PeopleOverheadTab.tsx`](../../src/components/people/PeopleOverheadTab.tsx): `ManHoursCard` now renders right under the three-lenses strip and above `OverheadPoolTrendCard`. At 1280 px its top is 481 px down the page, on the first screen.
- **The day-table door had to be fixed for the distance.** *Show these days in the table below* scrolled to the day table's toolbar on the click. With the card beside the table that was enough. From the top of the tab it was not: the table reloads for the new week and is one line tall while it does, so the page was too short for the scroll to reach the toolbar, and it stopped with the toolbar at the bottom of the window. `showOverheadWeekOf` now returns whether the dates changed; when they did, an effect scrolls again once the table's four loads are done. Pressing the door for the week already shown scrolls once.
- The table's combined loading gate is one named value, `overheadDayTableLoading`, read by the render and by that effect.

## Checked

Dev preview on prod data at 1280 px: the card is on the first screen; from the Week zoom the door landed on the loaded table for Sep 27 – Oct 3 (office 67.68 h and field 230.10 h in the table's footer against 68 and 230 on the card), and a second press on the same week landed there too. No test covers the tab itself (it has no render smoke); the card's own tests are unchanged and pass.
