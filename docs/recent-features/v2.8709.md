# v2.8709 — The Bridge: the ground for a Build job that waits, with a reason (punch list #192, write A, part 1) (2026-11-27)

The first of two PRs for write A of [`to-dos/bridge-stage-desks/README.md`](../../to-dos/bridge-stage-desks/README.md), drawn in [`mockups/build-waits-with-reason.html`](../../to-dos/bridge-stage-desks/mockups/build-waits-with-reason.html). Taunya's answer of 2026-11-25, quoted on the card: *Yes.* One migration and its bed. No client change, no edge function.

## Why it ships alone

The screens will read a new table and call a new function. A PR that held both would make the deploy wait on the push from the moment it merged, for every session's merge behind it. So the ground goes first, is pushed in a quiet moment, and the screens follow as a PR that owes nothing.

## What landed

- **`job_build_waits`** (`docs/migrations/20261127074500_job_build_waits.md`): one row a wait. The job, the reason picked from six, the person's words, who marked it and when, who lifted it and when. A job holds at most one wait that is not lifted.
- **`set_job_build_wait(job, waits, reason, note)`**: the one write, for the office. It marks a job waiting or lifts the wait, and writes one line on the job's Activity each time.
- **Who reads a wait:** whoever reads its job. **Who writes:** the office, through the function alone.
- **The job's status does not change.** It stays Working.
- A SQL bed, `npm run test:pg:job-build-waits`, 48 checks on stand-in tables.

## The six reasons

The GC is not ready for us, waiting on materials, waiting on an inspection, the customer asked us to hold, weather, and something else, which needs the person's words.

## Release order

Merge, then **`supabase db push`** of `20261127074500_job_build_waits.sql`, at any quiet moment. Nothing reads it until the next PR, so nothing is true or false in between.

## Not done here

Everything a person sees: **Name why it waits** on a stalled Build row, the small window, **Lift the wait**, the *Waits, with a reason* tile, the chip on the job's Stages card, the fact row in the job window and the two words the Activity draws the new lines with. That is the next PR.

## Proved, and what is not

- The bed on a local Postgres 15, the migration applied twice, 48 checks. It caught one fault before CI: a person's own words that end in a full stop read with two on the Activity line.
- Not seen: the table on prod. It is not pushed.
