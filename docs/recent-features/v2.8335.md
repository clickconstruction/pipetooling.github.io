# v2.8335 — Payments: a Stripe payment made in the evening is filed on the day it was made (2026-11-23)

Punch list #422, the second of three PRs. Migration `20261123074206_stripe_paid_day_is_the_company_day.sql`. No edge function, no client change.

## What was wrong

When Stripe says a bill was paid, `stripe-webhook` calls `mark_invoice_paid_from_stripe`, which writes the payment row. The row's day defaulted to the UTC date. A note beside the line said the webhook always sends the day, and it does not: it sends one only for a bill marked paid outside Stripe. So a customer who paid after 6 PM Central in winter, or 7 PM in summer, was filed on the next day.

Read on prod through the dev seat, read only, counts only: 4 of the 9 Stripe payments in the 31 days before 2026-11-23 sat a day late, three of them Sunday-night payments that read as Monday.

Found while planning Bills sent's second view, which lists money by the day it came. Every screen that reads `jobs_ledger_payments.paid_on` shared it: the Dashboard's Money in bar, the Bridge, Weekly money, a customer's timeline.

## What changed

- The default is `public.app_today()`, today in the company's calendar. One line of the function, and its comment. The rest of the body is the newest definition's, line for line, and its grants are named again.
- A day the webhook does name still stands.

## What did not change

- No row already written. Correcting the older rows is a separate step for the owner to call, after their list is read. `docs/migrations/20261123074206_stripe_paid_day_is_the_company_day.md` says what it would touch.
- `mark_invoice_paid` and `mark_job_paid`, which default the same way and are called with a day by the app's payment windows.

## Tests

- A new SQL bed, `stripe-paid-day` (`scripts/pgtest-stripe-paid-day.sh`, `supabase/tests/stripe_paid_day/`), on the whole schema: the function's body names no UTC day, a customer's own payment lands on the company's day with the note *Stripe*, Stripe's second event adds no row, a named day stands, and only the service role may run it.
