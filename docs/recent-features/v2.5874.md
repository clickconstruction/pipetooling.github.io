# v2.5874 — The Bridge: a resource call holds its stage, and Market reads through the win share (2026-10-26)

PR F, the last of to-do #107's small train (`to-dos/bridge-weakest-link-follow-ups.md` — deleted with this PR, every item shipped: v2.5244 (A), v2.5833/v2.5834 and v2.5856/v2.5857 closed calculations 3 and 5 from their own trains, then v2.5861 (B), v2.5865 (C), v2.5867 (D), v2.5872 (E) and this).

- **A resource call holds the stage it throttles** (`calls.ts`, `Bridge.tsx`): `heldLinkId` is a stage, so a call on Decision, Capital or Talent held nothing and the red could wander while the owner cleared a pile. A call now snapshots `measures.throttles` — the lit link at call time — when its link is a resource, and `heldStageFor` gives the stage a call holds: itself for a stage call, the throttled stage for a resource call. Resource calls recorded before this version hold nothing, as before.
- **Market compared through the window's win share** (`stageFlow.ts`, `dial.ts`): the card compared asks-in per week by bid value against what the field builds, so one large invited bid read "not short" indefinitely (calculation 4). `marketWinShare` reads the window's win share by value (won + started over sent, bids and estimates together); the badge now compares asks × that share against the field, and the card says which basis it stands on — "about $308k a week asked · ≈ $15.4k a week at the window's 5% win share", or "win share not readable: compared on the asks alone". The dial's Market pile reading says the same in plain words.

Tests: `stageFlow.test.ts` (the share, the fallback, the card copy), `calls.test.ts` (`heldStageFor` for a stage call, a resource call, and an old call without the snapshot), and the dial's plain-words sweep covers the new reading. The entry docs' two pointers at the to-do now say it is retired. Dev-only surface (`/bridge`): no help guide, no migration.
