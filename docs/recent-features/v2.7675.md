# v2.7675 — Papers before a job opens: a sub with no login signs on their own page before the address and scope show (2026-11-17)

Papers before a job opens, step 6 of seven: the sub's own page. The owner's call 7: a sub with no login signs on their own page before the address and scope show. No migration. Two edge functions change, `sub-portal` and `submit-sub-portal`, and neither does anything until deployed. Deploy them after `20261117220000_job_required_papers.sql` is pushed: both ask `job_papers_for_person`, and a job whose papers cannot be read is held shut.

A sub with no login has no row security to hide a job from them. Their page is read and written with the service key, behind a link. So these two functions are the lock itself for that person.

## What is new

- **One shared kernel**, `supabase/functions/_shared/subPortalJobPapers.ts`. It holds the rule (a job is shut to a person while one of its papers is not signed), which Pipeline job each row of the page belongs to, the questions to the database, and the pure functions that hold a shut job's rows back. It imports nothing.
- **`sub-portal` (the read)** asks `job_papers_for_person(job, person)` once for each Pipeline job the page shows something of: the sheets' jobs (`people_labor_jobs.job_ledger_id`) and the work orders' jobs (`step_commitments.job_id`, else the order's sheet). One look at `job_required_papers` first finds the jobs that require any paper, so a sub whose jobs require none costs one read and no function call.
- **A shut job keeps** its number, each row's id, its stage, its days and window, and its money: agreed, paid, back-charges, open, every payment with its day and how it was sent, and an offer's amount and expiry.
- **A shut job loses** the address, the job's name, the sheet's line labels, the signed order's scope, exclusions, references and acknowledgements, an offer's title, lines, start words and special provisions, the plans link, the pay-hold reason, the progress note, the office's change-request note, a booking's stage name and address, and every payment memo.
- **`job_papers`** in the reply lists the jobs that wait on the sub's signature: `[{ job_id, job_number, papers: [{ id, paper_name, is_form, status, signed_at }] }]`. A job held shut because its papers could not be read carries `unread: true` and no papers. A held-back sheet, offer or booking carries `papersFirstJobId`.
- **`submit-sub-portal` (the presses)** takes one new kind, `sign_job_paper` with `{ required_paper_id }`. It checks that the paper's job is one the sub has work on, makes the sub's own copy with `job_paper_start`, and mints a signing link exactly as `sign_link` does (both now call one `mintSignPath`). It answers `{ ok, signPath }`.
- **Every press on a shut job is refused**: `mark_work_done`, `progress`, `accept_offer`, `decline_offer`, `pick_dates` and `cant_do_dates` answer **403** *Sign this job’s papers first.* A job whose papers cannot be read answers **503** *We could not check this job’s papers. Please try again.*
- **The page** (`src/pages/SubPortal.tsx`) draws one card per waiting job, `SubPortalJobPapersCard`, at the top of *Your jobs right now*, in place of that job's sheet cards and offer cards: **Work on J2391**, how many papers are left, each paper with its state, and a blue **Read and sign: <paper>** on the first one not signed. Under the papers each held-back sheet and offer is one line: the number, *Papers to sign first*, and the money.
- **Your days** shows a held-back booking with its dates and the job's number, *Papers to sign first* where the address was, and no Map link. The Statements view names a shut job's statement *#J2391 Papers to sign first*.
- **The way back**: the card remembers this page's path before it leaves for `/contract/accept`. The thank-you there offers **Back to your page** for a sub's page (`jobPapersBackLabel`, `isSubPortalPath`), ahead of any call to sign in, and the press ends the trip with no job armed.
- **Four known sentences** (`saidJobPapersFirst`, `saidJobPapersUnread`, `saidJobPaperNotYours`, `saidJobPaperGone`) so a refusal reads in the page's language. The card's own words are in English and Spanish (`subJobPapersCardView`).
- **The office's guide** has a new section, *A sub with no login*.

## Choices worth knowing

- **It fails closed.** The edge guard for people with a login fails open, because the database still refuses them. Nothing stands behind this page. A job whose papers cannot be read is held back, and the card says *We could not check this job’s papers just now*.
- **Nothing changes for a sub whose jobs require no paper.** With no job shut, every blanking function hands back the very rows it was given, and a test holds that. The reply gains one key, an empty `job_papers`.
- **Money is never hidden.** The totals at the head are read from the true sheets. A held-back sheet's agreed, paid and open show in the card.
- **A shut statement loses the office's number.** The Statements view draws its papers on the page from the sheets. A held-back sheet no longer carries the office's words, so its paper reads as the page's own copy, with no number.
- **A sheet with no job link is not held back.** A legacy sheet that carries a job number as text and no `job_ledger_id` belongs to no Pipeline job here, by the rule that a sheet's job is its link and never a number match. A work order on a project step with no job is not held back either.
- **The sub's own answers on a statement are left alone.** *Got it* and *Something is wrong* (`sub-portal` POST) are about the sub's money and write to `sub_paper_acks`, not to the job.
- **The job's papers also show under *Your paperwork on file*.** Once started, a copy is one of the sub's own papers, named with the job's number, and **Sign now** there opens the same paper.
