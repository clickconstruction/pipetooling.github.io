# v2.5779 — GC mode: a quote's exclusion with no cost to cover it is a hole in our number (2026-10-23)

The gaps walk (the owner, 2026-10-23: "as we take in bids those bids have exclusions and we need to list them to fill in what we still need to find"), walked live on the test project *GC test bidding project, delete me*: a quote sent from the test company's portal with **Rebar supply** ticked as left out, carried in Compare quotes with no cost to cover it. Compare quotes said *No cost is set for rebar supply yet* and marked the All in *needs a cost*, but the card read **On track · Every trade has a number · Send our bid**, Our number carried the trade as a real $48,000, the board said *$48,000 priced so far*, and the window's Against our budget row said *$2,000 under*. The exclusion was counted as $0 in silence.

- **The cause**: `uncostedLines` in [`bids.ts`](../../src/lib/gc/bids.ts), the one kernel every number screen reads for "+ ?", looked only at scope lines the quote did not tick, never at what the quote wrote it leaves out. `uncoveredExclusions` ([`exclusions.ts`](../../src/lib/gc/exclusions.ts)) knew, but only `compareBids` read it.
- **The fix**: `uncostedLines` now returns the quote's uncovered exclusions after its uncosted lines, as items with the id prefix `exclusion:`. Every reader follows without a change of its own: `carriedUncosted` → the price standing (`gap`, *leaves out pump truck. No cost set yet, so it counts as $0.*), `tradeHasNumber` and the stage's Next (*Set the missing cost on Concrete*), the progress ring's detail, the price card's and Our number's *+ ?*, the board's *+ ?*, and Compare quotes' Against our budget row (*not known yet*). `uncostedWords` counts lines and exclusions apart: *1 line and 1 exclusion have no cost yet: roof curbs and roof drains.*
- **The portal**: a sent quote reads back what the company ticked (*Your quote leaves out rebar supply.*, in both languages), which it never did.
- **Tests**: `bids.direct.test.ts` (open, covered, Known and unit-priced exclusions through `uncostedLines`, `carriedUncosted` and both words), `priceStanding.test.ts` (a carried quote with an uncovered exclusion stands as `gap`, a cover makes it `real` at the quote plus the cover).
- **Docs**: the Compare quotes paragraph of `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`.

## Is this the best we can do?

- One kernel, every screen: the fix is eight lines in the place all of them already read, so no screen can disagree with Compare quotes again. Adding a second check per screen would have fixed the five that were found and left the next one.
- The words say which kind is open, because the office fixes a line and an exclusion in different rows of the same window.
- Left for the owner (the gaps walk's findings, in the same train's guide PR): a Known exclusion or a scope line cannot be changed once the project is made, so a gap on a live project has no door but a new set of plans; a quote's exclusion cannot be marked as the owner's or another trade's after the fact, only given a cost.
