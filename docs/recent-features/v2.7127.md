# v2.7127 — Pay calendar: Money waiting and Accounts Receivable read the due day (2026-11-13)

Punch list #323, the second half of PR 6, the part that needs no deploy. After v2.7040 a bill for a payer on a Pay calendar read its due day on the board, in its job window and on a call, and the two lists that ask *whose money is slow* still judged that payer by a count of days. A count of days cannot judge them: a bill in on the 1st and one in on the 30th are paid the same day, so their waits differ by a month and prove nothing. On Nov 22 a bill in on Oct 20, due Nov 30, read 33 days against a 20-day median and put its payer on the chase list.

## What changed

- **One read, in the kernel.** `calendarBillRead(clockYmd, calendar, lateness, today)` in `payCalendar.ts` reads one open bill against its calendar: the due day, the expected day (the due day plus the payer's usual lateness), the days past each, and a tone. Green until the expected day, amber past it, red at `PAY_CALENDAR_MISSED_RUN_DAYS` (30) past it, when a payer who pays once a month has missed the next run too. `calendarBillWords` and `calendarBasisWords` are its words.
- **Money waiting** (`moneyWaiting.ts`). `buildMoneyWaiting` and `openBillsForCustomers` take the calendars and the accepted days. A bill with a calendar takes its tone from the read, any other from its wait as before, so a job with *This job has no pay calendar* reads its wait beside the payer's other bills. A payer is off pace once one bill is past its yardstick, which for waits is the old rule exactly. The row carries `worstTone`, `calendar`, `lateness` and `pastExpectedDays`, and `moneyWaitingLead` / `moneyWaitingBasis` word it: *37d past expected* over *pay calendar · lands ~6d after the pay day*.
- **The Pay speeds window** passes them through from both of its mounts (the forecast and a Pipeline row's *Pays in* press). A bill's small text reads *due Oct 10 · 37d past expected* in place of *billed 09/28*, in Money waiting, in the By customer bills and on the payer's own card.
- **Dashboard Accounts Receivable** (`arCustomerRollup.ts`). The section loads the calendars beside the pay speeds, fail-soft, and the rollup reads each bill the same way. The row's small line reads *37d past exp.*, or *pay calendar* when nothing is late. `arCallOpener` opens on the due day (*2 bills past expected on their pay calendar*) and `buildArCallSummary` writes the calendar and each bill's due day.
- **Guides**: *A customer on a pay calendar* in *see which customers pay slowly*, a line in *drill into the Dashboard money cards* and in *set a payer's pay calendar*.

## Not changed

The two emails (the payment forecast and Money waiting) still read the pace guess. They are parked: they run on the edge, so they need the calendar kernel there and two deploys, and on 2026-11-13 Grace asked that what is built land before anything more is. The card says what that work was found to need. The Bridge's Collect pile, its cash runway and the Monday Bridge email wait for punch list #318. Still to do from the card: the Needs you line, the pay application's line, the phone's two-line rows for a bill not yet sent, GC Review's *expected pay by* box and the Pay speeds card's one-line hint.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **Thirty days is a chosen number.** Red means a whole pay run was missed. For a monthly calendar that is one month, and 30 days says so without reading the calendar a second time. A payer whose calendar skips a month (*months after* 2) still pays monthly, so the number holds.
- **The Dashboard has less to go on.** Its items carry the job's customer and its own bill clock, not the bill's payer or its accepted day. A bill a GC pays on a job whose customer is someone else reads that customer's calendar there, or none. The Pipeline's lists read the payer and the accepted day.
- **The lists still sort by the longest wait.** A calendar payer's rows sit where their oldest bill puts them. Sorting them by days past expected would mix two measures in one column.
