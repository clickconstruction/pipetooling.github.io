## What changed

- **Kernel** `src/lib/jobs/lienDeskFind.ts`: `lienFindWords` (every typed word must land), `lienFindMatch(facts, words)` → `{ ok, hits }` where `facts.shown` is what the row says and `facts.hidden` the address and the owner (the hits come back so the row can show them), `lienFindParts` for marking, `lienFindCountWords`, `lienFindPileCount` (*2 of 12*), `lienFindNothingWords`, `lienFindSingle`.
- **`LienDeskFindBox`** (`data-testid="lien-desk-find"`, `data-finding`): the box, the count, × (`lien-desk-find-clear`); `/` focuses it from anywhere on the desk that is not a field, Esc clears it while focused. `LienFindMarked` marks a text (`data-lien-find-mark`), `LienFindHits` writes the hidden facts into a row (`data-lien-find-hit="address|owner"`).
- **The desk**: one `find` state, cleared when the desk closes; `findFactsFor(jobId, gcId, shown)` builds a row's facts from the job's label, the GC, the supply-house words, the months and the pile, plus `job_address` and the owner of record (`resolveLienProperty`). The Notices list keeps every pile that had rows (`pilesShown` with `total`), the title reading *n of total* and greyed (`data-empty`) at zero; the row's title and GC are marked and its hits ride in the chips line; the empty state (`lien-desk-find-nothing`) says what to try; one match selects itself. The Affidavits and Retainage lists narrow the same way; Do now narrows its rows (`nextUpRowsFound`, titles marked through `LienDeskNextUp`'s `markTitle`). The box sits above each list in a two-row column (`withFind`, `data-lien-desk-find-column`).
