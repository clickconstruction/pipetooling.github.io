# v2.8063 — Lien desk: a month a notice named in time says so (2026-11-20)

Found by the Fable 5.2 audit of punch list #361 (the Lien desk's Months card, the ledger) and confirmed on the live desk. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

In **Months on this job**, the Window column of a closed month that a paper names always read *named after the window — information*. That is false when the paper went out in time.

The live example: a paper sent Sep 22 names August, whose deadline was Oct 15. The cell under that paper is the green ✓ (the cell is `named`, in time, from `buildLienMonthGrid`: `p.sentOn > deadline ? 'info' : 'named'`), and the Window column beside it said *closed Oct 15 · named after the window — information*. On a lien screen that told the office a timely notice was information only.

The words are older than #361: `windowCell` wrote the one literal for every noticed closed month, and v2.7989 carried it into the ledger unchanged.

## What changed

- [`lienMonthGrid.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienMonthGrid.ts): a new kernel, `lienGridNoticedWords(cells)`, reads the row's own cells, the same ones the checks are drawn from. It leaves out this notice's cell (the office's tick, not a paper that went out) and answers *named in time* when any earlier paper's cell is `named`, *named after the window — information* when every paper that names the month is `info`, and *a notice names it* when no loaded paper names it (the month is noticed by the RPC's word or a sent desk item alone, so neither claim is made).
- [`LienDeskMonths.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskMonths.tsx): the `if (w.noticed)` branch of `windowCell` takes its words and its tip from the kernel in place of the literal. Nothing else in the card moved.

## Not changed

- The cells, the checks and the *as information* mark under a paper: they were already right.
- A month named after its deadline keeps its words.
- No help guide, `LIEN_DESK_ARCHITECTURE.md` or `GLOSSARY.md` sentence quoted the old words, so none is amended.

## Tests

- [`lienMonthGrid.test.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienMonthGrid.test.ts): the three answers, that a `this` cell never counts, that one paper in time settles a month a later paper named again as information, and the words read off `buildLienMonthGrid`'s own rows for the live case (Sep 22 paper, August closed Oct 15) and for a month named late.
- [`LienDeskMonths.noticedWords.render.test.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskMonths.noticedWords.render.test.tsx) (new): a closed, noticed row whose paper cell is `named` reads *closed Oct 15 named in time* with no *after the window*; one whose cell is `info` keeps *named after the window — information*; both on one card keep their own words, and a month no loaded paper names reads *a notice names it*.
- `LienDeskMonths.render.test.tsx` and `LienDesk.render.test.tsx` pass unedited: no assertion pinned the old words on a month named in time.
