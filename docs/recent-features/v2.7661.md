# v2.7661 — Trips: a look on the Development page for trips to a site away from home (2026-11-17)

Punch list #379, PR 3 of nine: the Trips look. Client only. It reads and writes the tables of v2.7655 (`20261117234500_trips.sql`), so **that migration is pushed before this reaches the live app**. No edge function.

## What landed

- **The look.** `DevelopmentTripsCard` mounts in the office door and draws `DevelopmentTripsLook` when `tripsLookShows` says so: the site has a trip, or its address is 150 miles or more from the shop. The strip of looks gains **Trips** as a seventh look on those sites only (`DevelopmentJumpStrip` takes an optional `trips`), and every other development is unchanged.
- **The band, the bar, the strip, the rows.** One model, `buildTripsLook` in `lib/trips/tripsLook.ts`, feeds all of them, so a count on the band, a bar on the year strip, a row and an open trip come from the same days. The band: trips gone out and planned, days at the site, hours on the site, hours at the site with their share, and what was not at the site in its four figures.
- **The hours.** `tripHourRowsFromLabor` turns the Scoreboard's own read (`useSiteLabor`, hours by person, job and day) into the rows the split takes, so the band and the Scoreboard cannot disagree on a site's hours. Each person's day gets its place from the clock's punches on the site's jobs (`readSitePunches`, `placesByPersonDay`) read against the point of the site's address. A day with hours and no punch behind them reads as no place.
- **The open trip.** A row opens in place: the days, who went with each person's own days and hours, who asked, how it went, the jobs with their hours and the billing on the left; the hours by kind and the days by kind on the right. A trip that went with no punch at the site says so in amber and is never blocked.
- **The sheet.** `TripSheet` over `lib/trips/tripSheet.ts`: who went (the roster, or someone not in the app by name), which days (a first and a last day paint every day At the site, and a tap turns one), what for. Everyone shares the trip's days until one person is given days of their own. Who asked, the jobs, the billing and how it went wait under *More*. The foot says where the trip would stand if saved now.
- **Save, delete, undo.** A trip saves whole through `trip_save` under an id made on the client, so the delete's **Undo** saves the same trip again. The delete asks once, by the trip's days, and the toast offers Undo for a minute.
- **History.** `tripHistoryLines` gives two lines a trip, the day it leaves (its first day on the road or at the site) and the day it is back. They are read from the trip's days and are never kept rows. A new line kind, `trip`, with its mark in the legend under *What happened*.
- **The first trip on a near site.** A site with no trip and near the shop has no look. The line under its address gains *Trips · none yet · add one*, which opens the sheet. This is the owner's call 1, *a person can turn trips on for any site*, with no setting to keep: the first trip turns the look on.

## Reads the page gained

For the office door only: one read of the site's trips, the shop's point (`resolveOfficeAnchor`) and the point of the site's address (`useAddressGeocodeCoords`). When the look shows: the roster and the punches on the site's jobs, and the clock's days while the look is open.

## Not in this PR, by the card's plan

*Looks like a trip* and *What shows it* (PR 5), the Job window line, the tag on a clock line and the By date lane (PR 6), the phone (PR 7), dollars and Print (PR 8), Put on a bill (PR 9). On the open trip, a tap on a day opens the sheet to change it; **Split in two** is not built yet. The 150 miles is `TRIP_FAR_MILES_DEFAULT`; a setting for it has no screen yet.
