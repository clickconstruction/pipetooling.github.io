## Hardened in review before the merge

An independent read of the diff found twelve things, each checked against the code before any fix.

- **Who reaches what.** The function read a mail's file as the service role while it admits a set's author, and those files are the office's by RLS, so a crew member with an attachment's id could copy it: the row is read through the caller's client now. An author's update policy leaves `plan_sets.job_id` open, so a crew member could aim their row at a job they are not on and file under it: an author now files only under a job they are on whose part is the set's. A read of the caller that failed let a training account through: it refuses now.
- **Nothing made for nothing.** The folder was made and stamped before the file was checked, so a mail whose only file stayed in Gmail left the paper linked to an empty folder: everything an ask carries is checked first. A stamp that failed was dropped while the function answered the link: it is an error now.
- **Two sets, one folder.** Folders were found by name and names are not unique, as with two as-builts of one day or two mails of one subject: a folder another set's link holds is that set's, and this one takes *name · 2*.
- **The words.** A failed read of the mail's files read as *the mail had no file*, and *the paper holds its link* was said with no link: the read throws, and the words follow what happened. *Did not land* was drawn in green. Failed and held files vanished while the words said they stay, and the only retry was a second row: the failed ones stay with a press, the held ones stay named.
- **Smaller.** The mail door took the list's snapshot of the line, so a mail just put on the job left its files behind silently. Each pick replaced the last, so three camera shots filed one. A read that failed read as *no Drive folder*. The oldest job on a part won even with no folder. A long name could be cut through an emoji, which made Drive's query throw. An old filing's line outlived the next set.
- **Left as it was, on purpose.** `message_attachments.drive_file_id` is not stamped, though the review suggested it: the photo sweep reads it as filed, so a mail's photo copied to a paper would never reach the job's pictures. A second try matches on name and size, since a mail may carry two files of one name.
