# v2.5129 — Jobs: a turnaway trip charge stays in the job's total (2026-10-09)

Migration [`20261010044000_trip_charge_rider.sql`](../migrations/20261010044000_trip_charge_rider.md). Built by PUNCHLIST Helper 14. After [v2.5091](v2.5091.md), which closed the same gap for a returned check fee.

## The bug

`create_turnaway_trip_charge` puts a trip charge on its own bill and raises the job's revenue by the same amount. Four writers set the revenue from the line items again:
- Edit Job's billing save;
- Add discount (`apply_job_discount`);
- a deposit's leftover as a tip (`record_job_tip_from_deposit`);
- the field's Collect Payment (`add_collect_payment_fixture_from_job_book`).

Since v2.5091 each adds the riders: the hazmat fees, a returned check fee, and since v2.5113 a GC card fee. None of them knew a trip charge. So the first to run after a trip charge wrote it away while its bill kept it, and the job's total no longer covered its bills. Proven before the fix: after a $99 trip charge, Add discount wrote $1,720 for a job whose lines and riders come to $1,819. Edit Job's save wrote $2,800 where $2,899 was right.

No trip charge had ever been made on production (2026-10-10 02:42 UTC), so no job lost one.

## Why a rider entry, not the memo or a line item

The smallest fix counts what records a trip charge in `job_rider_fees`. But a trip charge had no record to count, only its bill's memo:
- Bill Customer writes whatever memo the office types when it sends;
- a draft takes a memo edit;
- Split renames the memo for each part;
- a bill typed by hand as *Trip charge* would count as one.

A line item would have reached far wider: Edit Job's lines, stage plans and the bill's line allocation. So the trip charge gets the record the other bill riders already use: a `fee_lines` entry on its own bill, `{trip_charge: <reason>, amount}`. It has no description, so the reprinted bill draws no row of its own for it (the bill is the trip charge), and Stripe never reads `fee_lines`.

## What changed

- **The function** writes that entry on the bill it makes (restated byte for byte from `20260927230000` but for its `INSERT`).
- **`job_rider_fees`** counts an entry that names its trip charge (restated byte for byte from `20261010026000`, its newest body). The three SQL writers call it, so none of them changed. `gc_owner_billing_revenue` adds `job_rider_fees(billing_job_id)` since v2.5113, so a GC billing job follows with no further change.
- **The client.** `riderFeeLineCents` (`src/lib/jobs/arReturnCaseFee.ts`) counts the same entry, so Edit Job's Job Total and its billing save keep the trip charge (`jobFormRiderFeesDollars`).
- **The money waterfall.** `dollarCoverageForSegments` (`jobSegmentsCoverage.ts`) attributes every rider on a bill to its bill, where since v2.5102 it attributed only a returned check fee. Before, a trip charge bill read as *$99 of $800 covered* on the first unbilled line. Its bill is all trip charge, and none of it is a line's money. A GC card fee is attributed the same way.
- **The backfill** marks a trip charge made before this file, but only a bill the function made: its exact memo, not the primary bill, no line item, no `fee_lines` entry yet, and a dispatch request on the same job closed the same day with the function's exact note for that amount and reason. It matched nothing on production.
- **Deleting a trip charge's bill** now takes the trip charge out of the total at the next rewrite. Before, the bump stayed in the total and folded into the job's remainder bill. The function's comment had named that as a caveat.

## Found, not fixed

Deleting a bill that carries a returned check fee clears the case's `fee_invoice_id` (`ON DELETE SET NULL`) but keeps `fee_amount`, `fee_added_at` and `fee_added_by`. Its `fee_lines` go with the bill, so the next rewrite drops the $30 from the revenue. The case still reads *The $30 fee is on its bill, added …*, and `add_ar_return_case_fee` refuses a second press (*This case already has its fee*). So the office cannot put the fee back on another bill. The same holds for a split: `SplitBillModal` copies no `fee_lines` to the parts, so any rider on a split bill drops at the next rewrite.
