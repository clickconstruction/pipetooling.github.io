# v2.6137 — What everyone sees reads the words in drawings and charts (2026-10-30)

Punch list #194's follow-up. The lens's text check (`auditFrame`, `src/lib/lens/lensAudit.ts`) measured CSS `color` on HTML text only. An SVG `<text>` paints with `fill`, so the check read a figure's labels by a `color` they never draw with. The whole-catalog sweep of 2026-10-30 called the training figures clean while their words read 1.77 to 2.93:1 in dark, and v2.6133 (#6524) fixed those inks by hand after an in-page read of the SVG. The tab's Check every screen, the headless sweep and anything else that runs `auditFrame` now read a drawing's words.

## What changed

- **A drawing's words** (a `<text>`, `<tspan>` or `<textPath>` that owns words, in an outermost `<svg>`) are read by their `fill`, its alpha times `fill-opacity` and every `opacity` up to the drawing. Each word is read at six points across its box: three across, at 30% and 60% of its height. A point's ground is the shapes painted under it, in paint order, composited down to the drawing's HTML background (`effectiveBg`, as for HTML text). The worst point is reported like any other text under 3:1, with the size the word draws at. The shapes are found by geometry, `isPointInFill` and `isPointInStroke` in each shape's own coordinates through `getScreenCTM`, so no viewport is needed: a figure below the fold of a 780 px frame is read. The in-page prototype's `elementsFromPoint` returned nothing there.
- **A line across a word is an overlap, not a ground.** A stroke under every one of a word's points is the band it sits in, such as a pipe's bore, and is its ground. A stroke under only some of them crosses the word, at any width, and a shape drawn after the word covers it. Both are listed apart as `overlaps`: a badge *N texts overlapped*, a line under *What the check found* and a count in the sheet's summary. The stroke's width was tried first and failed: the inspector-tests figure's riser crosses *50 psi*, its 5.6 px bore under half the word's height and its 9.2 px wall over, so the wall read as a 1:1 ground.
- **Unknowable grounds are skipped point by point**: a gradient or a pattern paint, an `<image>`, a `<use>` or a `foreignObject`. A transparent hit area, such as a chart's hover rect, paints nothing.
- **The HTML walk skips SVG elements.** It had measured a figure's labels by the `color` that `SVG_STYLE` sets, which is why they read clean.
- **A lone character is measured.** The check skipped text of one character, so a lone `0` or `✓` was never read; Moneyfill's `0` counts read 2.93:1 in dark while the sweep called the screen clean. Any text with a character in it is read now.
- **Kernels**: `svgPaint`, `composite`, `svgGrounds` (the band rule and the overlaps) and `worstGround` are pure and tested; `readSvgWords` exports one read per word for a sweep that wants every word, not only those under the floor. The file stays self-contained, because the headless sweep transpiles it alone and evaluates it in the page.
