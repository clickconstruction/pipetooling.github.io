# v2.2054 — Portal modal: office-only Edit-Job chips (2026-08-21)

Owner-requested ("Portal Job Shortcuts" mockup): the globe modal grows a
dashed **"Jobs on this statement"** strip under the live preview — one chip
per billed job (trade tag · number · open amount, statement order), clicking
closes the modal and opens that job's Edit Job window via the existing
`/jobs?tab=stages&edit=<id>` deep link. The strip sits in the modal's own
chrome (the preview iframe IS the customer page — nothing is injected into
it), labeled "only you see this — not on their page."

- Kernel `src/lib/portal/portalStatementJobLinks.ts` (+tests): the public
  payload carries job NUMBERS only (no ids, by design) — match them to the
  office's own `jobs_ledger` rows (hcp → click fallback), one chip per job
  with amounts summed across its bills; unmatched/numberless rows drop.
- `CustomerPortalGlobeButton.tsx`: fetches the portal payload (same token as
  the preview) + an office jobs query when the modal opens; failures silently
  hide the strip (it is a shortcut, never load-bearing). Trade colors from
  `PORTAL_TRADE_COLORS`.
- Help guide `share-a-customer-their-portal.md` updated.
