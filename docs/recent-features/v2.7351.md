# v2.7351 — Bills sent: Say how it went… reads the record back before the press (punch list #340, R3) (2026-11-15)

The third of the form's three own fixes on the card. Taunya picked Build all three on 2026-11-15. No migration and no edge function change.

## What changed

- **The form reads the record back above the button.** `ByHandForm` (`src/components/billing/BillsSentLedger.tsx`) draws a dashed box over the button: *The record will read:* and then `byHandReadBack` (`src/lib/billing/billsSent.ts`), the day, the way, the person and the recorder in the order a line on the list says them. *Mon, Nov 9 · Handed over · to Harlan Voss · recorded by hand by Priya on Fri, Nov 13.* With other bills ticked (R2, v2.7350) it opens *Each record will read:*.
- **The recorder's name is the one the stamp writes.** The form reads `profileName` from `useAuth`, which is `users.name`, the same column the `sent_documents` stamp trigger copies into `sent_by_name`. A name not read yet is left out of the sentence, never guessed.
- **It replaces the small grey line.** The line beside Cancel said what was missing, or that the record cannot be changed. What is missing now reads in the box, in the read-back's place, and under it the box always says *It is your word, with your name. It cannot be changed once saved.* (*It cannot be changed once saved.* on a phone).
- **The billed day's chip is one line.** Read on the live page after R1 (v2.7349) at 1,280 wide: the date's column is 177 px, and *Mon, Oct 12 · the day it was billed* wrapped to two lines, 43 px beside **Today**'s 25. The chip now reads *Mon, Oct 12 · billed* at every width, never wraps, and keeps the long words as its tip. On a phone it was already that, in one row, 40 px tall.
- The guide *see which bills went out* tells the person to read the line above the button.

## Not changed

What a record stores and the words a saved line reads with. The read-back is the form's own sentence. It is not kept on the record.
