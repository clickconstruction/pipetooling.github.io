# v2.8522 — View as: the sign-in page says why Exit signed you out (2026-11-24)

v2.8241 made Exit sign the browser out when the dev's own session cannot be put back, so that nothing saves under the person's name. The dev then landed on the plain sign-in page with no reason given. Punch list #417 listed it as a loose end: *Exit's sign-in page says nothing.*

## What changed

- **`leaveViewAs`** (`src/lib/impersonationExit.ts`) leaves one line of why before it loads the sign-in page, on every path that signs out: a refused swap, a swap that throws, a stash with no session, a stash that cannot be read. The words are `VIEW_AS_SIGNED_OUT_WORDS`. A dev who gets back leaves none.
- **`src/lib/signInNotice.ts`** is the one home of the line the sign-in page shows after a forced sign-out: `leaveSignInNotice` writes it to this tab's session storage and `takeSignInNotice` reads it once. The key is the one the session-expiry sign-out has always written, so that path is unchanged, and `authErrorHandler.ts` and `SignIn.tsx` now go through the module in place of the literal key.
- The reason is a courtesy. A browser that refuses storage shows no line, and the sign-out and the load to the sign-in page go on regardless.
- The guide *see the app as a role or as a person* says the line is there.

## What it does not change

- **A lost signal at the press still signs out.** The swap cannot tell a refusal from a signal that dropped, and there is no retry. The line now explains the sign-out in both cases. Still open on #417.
- Exit's order is as it was: sign out, drop the stored sign-in, take the flag off, then load.

## Tests

`impersonationExit.test.ts` holds the reason on each sign-out path, none on the way back, and a load that goes on when the reason cannot be left. `signInNotice.test.ts` holds taken-once and a storage that refuses. `SignIn.render.test.tsx` draws the line above the form and finds it gone from storage after.
