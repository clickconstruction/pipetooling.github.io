# v2.7916 — Lien desk: the mailed copy of a filed lien is the clerk's stamped copy (2026-11-19)

## Why

The owner, 2026-11-19: "I want the uploaded copy of the lien to be a part of the mailing to a customer, because I am required by law to share with them a copy of the lien I filed. Because I have to submit the copy I filed I cannot share with them the redraw from our record." Counsel's suggestion was the same: the mailed copy must be a copy of what was signed and filed.

Until now *Print the copies to mail* drew the copy from the filing's stored fields (`job_lien_filings.fields`), on a page marked COPY that showed no signature, no notary's seal and no clerk's stamp. The code carried the doubt as `LIEN_SERVED_COPY_READ_BY_COUNSEL = false`. The stamped scan was already on every filing as its saved copy (`document_url`), a file in the job's Drive folder under Team only, Sent by us.

## What changed

- **The copy is the saved copy, every page.** Each envelope takes its cover letter and then the stamped scan's own pages. The pages are copied into the print as they are (`pdf-lib`'s `copyPages`) and never drawn again. A saved copy that is a JPEG or a PNG goes whole on a page of its own.
- **One PDF a run.** The mail sheet first, then every envelope in the sheet's order. It opens in a new tab inside the click and asks for the print window. The HTML print is gone from this path.
- **A lien with no printable saved copy is held, and the rest print.** No saved copy, a link typed by hand that is not a Drive file, a file the app could not read (with the door's own reason), a file that is not a PDF, a JPEG or a PNG, a PDF that does not open. Each has its reason and its door. Nothing is drawn in a stamped copy's place.
- **A copy that looks like more than one lien is asked about.** More than three pages, or over 4 MB, holds the lien until a person has opened the file and pressed **It is this lien alone. Print it**. On 2026-11-18 one filing carried an 18-page stack of eight liens.
- **The kept print is what went in the envelope.** Each lien's papers are built as a PDF of their own, and the stack is the sheet and then those files. The same file is kept on the job (`sent_documents`, kind `lien_affidavit_copy`), and its row says the copy was the stamped scan, which Drive file and its SHA-256 (`source_snapshot`).
- **The stage is honest about what was printed.** A lien that has a saved copy reads **Print the copies to mail** until a print of the stamped copy is filed. A receipt or a mail number on the filing no longer moves it on, and a print of the redrawn page does not count. A lien with no saved copy is still read by its numbers and receipts.
- **A lien the app did not draw prints.** A filing that keeps no fields used to be held (*mail a photocopy*). It now prints its stamped copy with no cover letter, since the letter states the affidavit's words and the record holds none.
- **The mail sheet says each envelope's pages**, so the stack can be split without reading it.
- The job's Lien window and the phone use the same run, of one lien (`LienServeCopiesPress`).

## Decisions

- **`pdf-lib`, not pictures of the pages in an HTML print.** Copying the scan's pages keeps them exactly as filed, at their own size, and the kept file is small and is itself the proof. Drawing each page as a picture would re-make the page the law asks us to copy, shrink it inside the browser's margins and leave the browser's header and footer on it.
- **Asked about, not just warned.** A warning line beside a press that prints eleven liens is easy to miss, and the cost of missing it is other customers' liens in an owner's envelope. One press lifts it.
- **The redraw is removed, not kept as a preview** (the owner's answer). Nothing else called it.
- **`LIEN_SERVED_COPY_READ_BY_COUNSEL` is removed with the page it doubted.** It gated one amber line about a page drawn from the record. `LIEN_SERVE_LETTER_READ_BY_COUNSEL` stays false: counsel has not read the letter's words.
- **The file's kind is read from its own bytes**, never from its name or the type Drive guessed.
