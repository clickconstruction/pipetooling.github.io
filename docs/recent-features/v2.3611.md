# v2.3611 — Supervision, PR 1: one switch says who can run a job; Who's where marks who is supervising and what is unsupervised (2026-09-19)

PR 1 of five in [`to-dos/supervision/README.md`](../../to-dos/supervision/README.md) (Todd, 2026-09-19: *"it does not matter what leader is on a job, so long as there is someone competent jobs can progress, and helpers can't go do most of anything without oversight"*; levels: helper and sub each *supervision required* / *not required*). One migration; the client reads the column.

## The change

- **Migration** `20260919151642_users_needs_supervision.sql` ([doc](../migrations/20260919151642_users_needs_supervision.md)): `users.needs_supervision boolean NOT NULL DEFAULT true`, backfilled false for every role but `helpers` and `subcontractor`; `users_guard_privileged_columns()` gains the rule — dev, master or assistant may change it, never their own row except a dev — and the trigger fires on the column. No RLS change.
- **Kernel** [`src/lib/people/supervision.ts`](../../src/lib/people/supervision.ts) (11 tests): `isSupervisor` (a master; a helper or sub with the switch off), `needsSupervision`, `supervisorsOf` (masters first, then subs, then qualified helpers), `coverage(people) → covered | unsupervised | empty`, `supervisionLabel`. Office roles, superintendents and unknown roles are neither supervisors nor supervised.
- **Who's where** reads it: `WwPerson.needsSupervision` from the roster read; `islandsAt` gives every island a `coverage` for the day on that job (anyone listed or clocked there who can run it; the office job and no-job sessions are always covered); `weekCrews` gives every crew its `supervisors` and `unsupervisedDays`, and the week its `unsupervisedByDay`; `crewSupervisors(people)` replaces the crown's `derivedLead` (kept for a release as the first supervisor). The week view puts a green **SUP** badge on every head that can run a job, reads *N supervising* on the card, a red dashed border and *unsupervised — nobody on this block can run it* on a crew with none, the total under the legend; the strip shows *N unsup.* under a day; the day view marks an island *UNSUPERVISED* and badges its heads.
- **The switch**: Settings → Active accounts — a checkbox under the last login on every helper and sub row (*Needs supervision* / *Can run a job*), saved at once and reconciled from what the database returned like the read-only switch; People → Users — a chip on helper and sub rows and a ⋯ menu item to flip it, for dev, master and assistant.
- Guides: new [`say-who-can-run-a-job.md`](../../src/content/help/say-who-can-run-a-job.md); `see-who-was-on-which-job.md` re-worded. `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` (the guard paragraph amended to the five columns; a *Supervision* section), `GLOSSARY.md` (*Needs supervision*), `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` (`users` switches).
