Three tests.
- **Settings → What customers see**: every sample email carries `from` (`buildSampleEmail` → `sampleEmailFrom(id)`: the estimate sample is the plumbing brand, every other sample the company) and the expanded step prints **From:** beside **Subject:** (`SettingsWhatCustomersSeeTab`, `SampleEmails.from`). The sample test asserts the From on nine ids.
- **Two client lines caught up**: GC Review's *share the whole week* note said *team@noreply.pipetooling.com* — now `COMPANY_EMAIL_FROM_LABEL`; the Estimates page's own `ESTIMATE_EMAIL_FROM_ADDRESS` constant and inline From are `estimateEmailFrom(brand)`.
- **`_shared/emailFromAddress.ts`** — `EMAIL_FROM_FALLBACK_ADDRESS` / `EMAIL_FROM_FALLBACK`, dependency-free, read by `emailFrom.ts` and the client kernel, so the address lives in one place (the mirror comment in `Estimates.tsx` is gone).
- Guide *see what customers see* names the From line; release note; the to-do retired (`to-dos/README.md` Closed; the `clickplumbing.com` address question moves to `owner-decisions-pending.md`).

## Design calls

- **The client reads the fallback, not the secret.** The browser cannot see `EMAIL_FROM`; the fallback has mirrored the secret since 2026-09-01 (`docs/DOMAIN_CUTOVER.md`), and one constant beats two mirrors.
- **The estimate sample says *Click Plumbing***, not the company: the sample is the plumbing brand's estimate, and the live sender names the trade.
