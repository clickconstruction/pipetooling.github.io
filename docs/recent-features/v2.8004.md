# v2.8004 — Painted maps: the shared map says when a reader's phone gives only an area (2026-11-19)

Punch list #402, PR 4: the shared map at `/way`, the last of the four. The kernel is v2.7984, the walk's line v2.7994 and the place panel's press v2.8001. Client only: no migration and no function.

## The wrinkle

**Show where I am** drew the reader's dot at whatever the phone handed back. A phone with Precise Location off hands back the middle of an area a mile or more across. So a stranger got a dot miles from where they stood, a sure-sounding *The start is 1.9 mi north of you.*, and *Your phone is sure within 10,500 ft.* under it.

## What a reader sees now

`wayReading` in `src/lib/paintedMap/paintedWay.ts` sorts each reading, and the page draws what it says.

| The reading | The page |
|---|---|
| None yet, or a rough one still waited on | *Finding you…*, and nobody on the map |
| 20 m or under | Today's words, with a green tick beside *Your phone is sure within 15 ft.* |
| 20 m to 1,000 m | Today's lead, then an amber line: *The signal is weak here. Your phone is only sure within 120 ft.* |
| Over 1,000 m for 4 seconds | *Your phone is only giving the area you are in. It is only sure within 2 miles, so the map cannot show where you stand.*, then *To see where you stand, turn on Precise Location:* and the steps for this phone |

- **The switch is named in one place.** A stranger was never told what Precise Location is, so the page names it only for an area, where it is the thing to press. A weak signal never names it, even at a margin that proves it on.
- **An area gets no dot and no word about where the reader stands.** No *You are on the painted line.* and no direction to the start: the middle of an area is nobody's spot.
- **The map pulls back to the circle** when the area's edge is within `WAY_FRAME_METERS` of the map, so the margin is added to how near counts (`wayNearMap` now takes the distance). The page then adds *You are somewhere in this circle, 4 miles across.* An area nowhere near the map leaves the picture on the map.
- **The same 4 second wait** as the walk (`useRoughHeld`), started over each time **Show where I am** is pressed.
- On a desk, or a phone the page cannot tell, the words speak of *this device* and give one line for each kind of phone.

The canvas needed nothing new: `me.area` and `frameMargin` came with v2.7994, and its own frame for a reader's dot already stands down for an area.

## The page's registrations

`/way/:code` is an outside page. Its row in `customerSurfaceRegistry.ts` and its line in `customerContractCatalog.ts` quote none of these words and are unchanged. The journey step `painted-map-page` in `customerJourneys.ts` describes what a reader can do there, and gains one sentence: the page says when their phone gives only a rough area and shows the steps.
