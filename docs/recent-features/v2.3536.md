# v2.3536 — Stages tab decomposition, PR 6: the two send-back dialogs are their own components (2026-09-16)

Client only, behavior-preserving. PR 6 of the train in `to-dos/engineering-hygiene.md` — the two remaining inline confirms in the modal tail (region 6 of [`docs/JOBS_STAGES_TAB_ARCHITECTURE.md`](../JOBS_STAGES_TAB_ARCHITECTURE.md)) apart from the partial-invoice dialog, which waits for its Stage-A kernel in PR 7.

## What moved

| Dialog | File | What the tab keeps |
|---|---|---|
| Send back / delete a bill line (the invoice dialog) | [`StagesSendBackInvoiceModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/StagesSendBackInvoiceModal.tsx) | the target, the Stripe-explainer flag it raises after a failed revert, `busy`, and the confirm: delete or revert behind the re-entry lock (`stagesInvoiceSendBackConfirmLockRef`) |
| Send Job Back / Send back (the job dialog) | [`StagesSendBackJobModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/StagesSendBackJobModal.tsx) | the target with its v2.2601 billing context, `needsAttestation`, the collect-payment notice, the last status-event line, the required reason (v2.2065), `busy`, and the confirm: the Stripe void prep on Billed → RTB, `updateJobStatus`, the reason note |

**`sendBackChecked` stays in the tab and shared** between the two dialogs, as map quirk 12 says; both closes reset it, exactly as the inline versions did. The two `onClick` bodies became `confirmSendBackInvoice` and `confirmSendBackJob` in the tab, with `closeSendBackInvoice` / `closeSendBackJob` for the three-setter resets that were repeated in each dialog's Cancel and success paths. The invoice dialog's three-way busy expression, written out twice in the original, is one `busy` prop.

Numbers: `JobsStagesTab.tsx` 5,377 → 5,241 lines; three imports the tab no longer needed (`SendBackReasonField`, the two reason constants) went with the job dialog.
