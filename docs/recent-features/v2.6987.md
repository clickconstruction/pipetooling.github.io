# v2.6987 — Permits: a missing owner is asked for again sooner (2026-11-11)

Punch list #320, PR 6 of the card's list. `permit-lookup` kept every answer thirty days, whatever it was.

- **Why thirty days is right for a found owner**: the counties rebuild their parcel layers about monthly.
- **Why it is wrong for a "no parcel"**: the lot the layer does not hold today is often a new one. Of 32 Austin house addresses whose id Travis's layer did not hold on 2026-11-11, 28 were new houses and 29 had no point to ask instead. Such a card found its lot at the next rebuild, then waited out the rest of its month.
- **Why it is wrong for a failure**: a county that timed out once left the card saying *Could not ask* for a month. The skim card has no *Look again* press.

`keptDays` in `_shared/permitLookup.ts` now answers by how the row ended: a found answer 30 days; a company Google does not know 30 days, because asking again is a paid call; a county's "no parcel" 7 days (`NO_PARCEL_FRESH_DAYS`); anything that failed one hour (`FAILURE_FRESH_DAYS`), which is still long enough that a dead source is not hammered. `permit-lookup` passes it to `lookupIsFresh`. Nothing else in the function changed.

Prod held 42 kept parcel rows and no kept failure on 2026-11-11, so nothing changes on a card today. The guide's sentence on *once a month* gained the week.

Deploy: `permit-lookup` after the merge, from `main`. No migration.
