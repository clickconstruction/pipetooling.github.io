# v2.7726 — Papers before a job opens: each paper a job requires shows on Documents → Agreements with its job (2026-11-18)

Papers before a job opens, step 7 of seven: where the papers show. One migration, `20261118050000_agreements_job_papers.sql`, which restates the view `agreements_on_file` (punch list #376) from its newest definition, `20261117210000_agreements_on_file_gc.sql`. No client code: the Agreements tab already draws a job on any line that carries one. The detail is in `docs/migrations/20261118050000_agreements_job_papers.md`.

## What is new

- **A person's copy of a paper a job requires carries its job** on Documents → Agreements: the job's id, numbers, name and address, read through a left join on `jobs_ledger` under the caller's own policies.
- **The line's name is the paper's own name** from `job_required_papers`, such as *Site rules and photo policy*. The copy is filed as `<paper> (job <number>)` so the Contract Book's name-keyed paths leave it alone, and that name stays off the screen.
- **A paper a job requires is listed whatever its form's kind.** The view listed only papers of the kind `agreement`. An NDA filed as a form of another kind would have been left out.
- **The find box finds a job paper** by the job's numbers, its name, its address and the words *job paper*.
- **Help guide**: `find-an-agreement-we-sent-or-signed` gains *A paper a job requires*.

## Choices worth knowing

- **A copy shows once it goes out**, like any person's agreement. A copy goes out when someone presses **Read and sign** or the office copies its signing link. A paper nobody has opened yet is not a line: the job's own section lists who owes it.
- **The tag stays Staff or Sub.** A job paper is a person's agreement, and the job on its line says what it is for.
- **The bed now re-applies the view's newest definition.** The Agreements bed re-applied `*_agreements_on_file_gc.sql` as its second run, which after this migration would have put the old view back. It re-applies this one now.
- The step 1 bed's default port moved off the Agreements bed's, so the two can run side by side on one machine.

## Verified

The `agreements-on-file` bed in CI with this migration applied twice: the office reads each job paper with its job and its own name, a signed one and one still waiting, a form of another kind included; the line carries the job's id; the find box finds it by the job's number, its name and the words *job paper*; a person's paper with no job reads as before; a primary and a sub read none. The three papers beds pass beside it. Then on prod, on a ZZ Test job's two signed papers.
