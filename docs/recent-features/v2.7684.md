# v2.7684 — Lien desk: set the last day of work from under the Last work stop (2026-11-17)

The owner, 2026-11-17, on the Lien desk's By paper view with a notice open: a box drawn in the empty space under the timeline's LAST WORK stop, and *"in this read area I would like the ability to update the last day on a job with a reason just like we can elsewhere in the app"* (the red area). The way to do it on that screen was the **Change ›** line inside Months on this job, a screen below the strip.

## What was there

- `LienTimelineStrip` has drawn a last-day door since v2.4735 (`onChangeLastWork`), but only the Lien window handed it. The desk's notice pane and affidavit pane mounted the strip without it, so the stop had no press there.
- The door trailed the stop's words on the same line and always read *change ›*.
- The write and the window (`LienLastWorkDayConfirm`) lived inside `LienLastWorkDayLine`, so nothing else could open them.

## What landed

- **One window, one write.** `LienLastWorkDayWindow` (new) holds the confirm window and its write; `useLienLastWorkDayWrite` (new, its own file) is the one update of `jobs_ledger`'s four hand-day columns. `LienLastWorkDayLine` saves through both: *Save the day* opens the window on the day typed in the editor, *Back to clock hours* writes through the hook. No migration, no edge function, no change to the mirrored kernel `lienLastWorkDay.ts`.
- **The press under the stop.** The strip draws the door on its own line under LAST WORK, in the row and the list layouts; the mini row has none. `lienLastWorkDoorWords` (new kernel `lib/jobs/lienLastWorkDoor.ts`) gives *change ›* over clock hours or a hand day and *set the day ›* over a stand-in; `lienLastWorkStepIsStandIn` in `lienTimeline.ts` says which a stop is. The Lien window's door takes the same words.
- **The desk's two panes hand it.** `LienDeskNoticePane` and `LienDeskAffidavitPane` pass `onChangeLastWork` when `lienLastWorkDoorOffered({ office, hasJob })` holds, and open `LienLastWorkDayWindow` in place. From the press the window keeps the day itself: it opens on the job's day over hours or a hand day and empty over a stand-in (`lienLastWorkOpeningDay`), with the day field in hand (`startOn="day"` on the confirm; the line's editor keeps the reason first). A save calls the page's `onCalendarChanged('last_work')`, the same re-read as the months' line; the affidavit pane takes it as `onLastWorkChanged`.
- **A phone.** Inside `[data-lien-pane-phone]` the stylesheet grows the press to 44 px, on its own line.
- **Read-only mounts are unchanged:** the By job mini rows, a stop's paper window, the job history, the Legal desk and the law firm's page hand no door.

## Not in this PR

- The desk's job read (`LIEN_DESK_JOB_COLUMNS`) carries the hand day but not its reason, who or when, so the window opens with an empty reason on a day already set by hand. The Edit Job row and the Lien window read all four.
