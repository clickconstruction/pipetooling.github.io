# v2.7044 — What customers see: every lien letter, in the order it goes (2026-11-12)

Punch list #326. The owner asked for *a what customer sees for all the different letters that go out and the different stages we have available to us as relate to liens*, and gave the order as she understood it: a notice, then a demand, then a final notice, then a lien. The tab had three lien papers, in two groups, and nothing that said the order. Five letters the app sends were not on it at all.

## What is on the tab now

**Settings → What customers see → Lien letters**, a sixth group (`JourneyId` `'lien'`, between General contractor and Subcontractor).

- **The order, over the cards** (`LIEN_STAGES`, drawn by `JourneyStrip` for any journey with `stages`): Step 1 Notice of claim, Step 2 Courtesy notice, Step 3 Attorney's letter, Step 4 Lien affidavit, Step 5 Release, and beside the order the Final demand letter and the Retainage notice. Each box says when it goes in one or two plain sentences. So the owner's *final notice* is the attorney's letter, and the demand letter is not a step of the notices.
- **Twelve cards**, each opening large as every other step does: the cover letter with the notice, the notice of claim (§ 53.056), the courtesy notice as the owner's email, the owner's text, the builder's email and the builder's text, the attorney's letter in counsel's two kinds, the lien affidavit, the release of record, the final demand letter, the retainage notice (§ 53.057). Each step's `when` opens with its stage.
- **One made-up job** carries all of them (`src/lib/journeys/lienLetterSamples.ts`): Cedar Bend Apartments under Sample Contracting, owner Sample Owner LLC, one unpaid month, $12,400.00. Its days count from today by the Lien desk's own rules (`sampleLienDays`: the work month, the packet 21 days ago, the attorney's letter at day 10, the affidavit's day by `filingDeadlineForMonth`, the copy served by `serveDueForFiling`), so the reminder names the day the affidavit swears.
- **Drawn by the code that sends**: the cover page by `runCoverNoteBlocks` over `defaultGcNoticeCoverLetter` / `letterTwoTemplate` and `fillCoverLetter`; the forms by `buildLienNoticeBlocks`, `buildLienRetainageNoticeBlocks`, `buildLienAffidavitBlocks` (the short form, sentence 6 from `lienAffidavitNoticesWords`) and `buildReleaseOfRecordBlocks`; the courtesy email by `courtesyEmailText` in `lienFilingEmailHtml`, the frame `send-lien-filing-email` sends in; the text by `courtesyTextBody` through `textToSend`. No letter's wording is written here.

## What moved, and what did not

- The notice to the owner of record left the General contractor group for step 1 of this one (`owner-notice`, same id and paper). The contract catalog's card for it now opens on `lien/owner-notice` and the cover letter.
- The homeowner's **Demand letter** and **Lien release** stay where they were: *A person* reads a real customer's state on those two steps (`personJourney.ts`). The demand letter shows in the lien group too, as `lien-demand-letter` over the same paper.
- The count line now reads six audiences.
- **What everyone sees** frames an audience's pages, and this group has none (papers, emails and texts). `LENS_AUDIENCES` now lists only the journeys with a page on this origin, so that tab's *Who, outside* pills are the five they were.

## Limits

- **Samples, not a job's own letters.** A real job's letters are read on the Lien desk and in its Lien window. *A person* mode draws nothing for this group: no kernel reads a property owner's lien state per step yet.
- **A commercial job.** The sample is apartments, so the days are the later clock's and the cover letter is the commercial one. The house and homestead letters are not drawn.
- **The pay page and the invoice enclosed** with a notice are named on the cover letter and not drawn here.
- On a phone the tab runs 70 px wider than the screen on any step. That was so before this change (a 390 px frame in a 375 px screen).
