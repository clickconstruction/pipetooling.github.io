# v2.4158 — The home-screen icon is centred on its tile (2026-09-29)

Owner-reported, with a screenshot of the *Add to Home Screen* sheet: the ClickTooling mark sat off to the side of the yellow tile. Static asset + script; no app code, no migration.

## What was going on

- v2.4095 pulled the mark in to 0.62 of the tile to clear iOS 26's glass band, and computed the inset as `(512 − 512·s) / 2` — the right formula for art drawn in the tile's own 512 box. The gear is not: it is drawn in a 640-unit space around (320, 320) (the SVG's own `translate(25.60)` is `256 − 320·0.72`, not `(512 − 512·0.72) / 2`).
- So the shipped PNG had the mark 8% right and 8% down: ink box centred at 591 px on the 1024 tile, left margin 276 px against right 116, top 254 against bottom 95. The bottom teeth sat 9% from the edge — deeper in the glass band than the 12% v2.4095 set out to fix. The fragment's "19% out" was computed, never measured.

## What shipped

- **`scripts/generate-app-icon.mjs`** — `MARK_CENTRE = 320`; the inset is `256 − 320·MARK_SCALE` (57.60 at 0.62). The header says why.
- **`public/apple-touch-icon.png`** — regenerated (`npm run gen-app-icon`). Measured on the decoded pixels: ink box 196–827 × 175–848, centred at 511.5 on both axes; margins 196 px left and right, 175 px top and bottom; the bottom teeth 17% from the edge. The dark ink's centroid sits 18 px (1.7%) right of centre because the wrench handle cuts yellow out of the left half — the outline reads centred, so no optical nudge.
- The v2.4095 fragment's *To see it* paragraph now says what the phone showed.

## To see it

A phone that already has the icon keeps the old bitmap: after the deploy, remove the icon and Add to Home Screen again. Safari's sheet previews the new tile before you tap Add.
