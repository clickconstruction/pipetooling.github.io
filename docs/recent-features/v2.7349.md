# v2.7349 — Bills sent: Say how it went… opens on the day the bill was made (punch list #340, R1) (2026-11-15)

The first of the form's three own fixes on the card. Taunya picked Build all three on 2026-11-15. No migration and no edge function change.

## What changed

- **The day opens on the day the bill was made.** `ByHandForm` (`src/components/billing/BillsSentLedger.tsx`) opened its date on today, on a bill in the yellow box that may be weeks old, and a record made by hand cannot be changed once saved. It now opens on `byHandDayOffered(gap.billedYmd, today)`: the bill's own day in the company's time, or today when the bill's stamp does not read or is after today.
- **Today is one press away.** Under the date sit the days `byHandDayPicks` gives: the billed day (*Mon, Nov 9 · the day it was billed*, *Mon, Nov 9 · billed* on a phone) when it is an earlier day, then **Today**. The pressed one is the day the form holds. The date box still takes any day up to today.
- `BillsSentGap` carries `billedYmd`, the day `billsSentGaps` already read to word *Billed Mon, Nov 9 at 3:26 PM.*
- The guide *see which bills went out* says the form offers the billed day and names **Today**.

## Not changed

What a record stores, the refusal of a day after today, and the list's other words.
