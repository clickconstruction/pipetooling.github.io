# v2.8527 — Global Reload moved to Settings → Advanced, with when to press it (2026-11-24)

Punch list #85. Diane, 2026-11-24: *Move the Global Reload button out of the gear wheel drop down and put it somewhere in settings where below it we explain cases where someone would want to press it.* Client only: no migration and no edge function.

## Before

A dev's gear menu ended on three rows: **Sign out**, **Hard Reload**, **Global Reload**. **Global Reload** makes every other open copy of the app hard-reload at once (`ForceReloadContext`: a broadcast on the Realtime `force-reload` channel). It sat one row under **Hard Reload**, a press a dev makes many times a day, fired with no question, and said nothing about what it does to the people it reaches.

The look that led here (`docs/recent-features/v2.8513.md`, *What is left*) found that it reloads over typed words in every tab it reaches. Diane weighed removing it and kept it, out of the menu and explained.

## Now

**Settings → Advanced**, for a dev, between **Fix app** and **Email domain monitoring** (`src/components/settings/GlobalReloadSettingsCard.tsx`). The words are one kernel, `src/lib/settings/globalReloadWords.ts`.

- **Above the press:** *Makes every other open copy of the app reload now, on every phone and computer that is signed in.*
- **The press**, **Global Reload**, 44 tall.
- **Under the press**, four short lists:

| Heading | What it says |
|---|---|
| Press it when | A fault is doing harm and the fix is live. A database change the old app would misread is about to be pushed: deploy the app, press, then push. Many people are stuck on an old version at once. |
| Leave it alone when | An ordinary deploy, since the app updates itself. One person is stuck, who has **Hard Reload**. The new version is not live yet. |
| What it does to people | It reloads over what each person is doing and does not ask. Typed words not sent and unsaved forms are lost. **Hard Reload** and the update notice ask first; this does not. |
| Who it reaches | Copies open and signed in now. Not a copy that sat hidden five minutes. Not a copy that reloaded in the last five seconds. Without the press, a copy finds a new version within the hour and waits for a quiet moment. |

**A window asks first.** *Reload every open copy of the app? Everyone else who is signed in reloads now. Words they typed and have not sent are lost.* with **Reload everyone** and **Keep them working**. The gear menu's press had no window.

After **Reload everyone** the card says *Sent. Every other open copy is reloading. This copy stays as it is.* With no channel to send on it says *Not sent.* and why.

The **Advanced** group is folded. The link `/settings?tab=settings-advanced-tools#settings-global-reload` opens it, and the dev doors list (`devDoors.ts`) carries that link.

The gear menu ends on **Sign out** and **Hard Reload** for everyone.

## What each line under the press rests on

Read from the code on 2026-11-24.

- **Every other copy, and this one stays.** A Supabase broadcast is not sent back to the client that sent it unless the channel asks for `self`, and this one does not.
- **It does not ask.** A copy that hears it runs `hardReloadFromRoot` at once.
- **Five seconds.** `ForceReloadContext` keeps `force-reload-last` and ignores a second signal inside five seconds.
- **Hidden five minutes.** `RealtimeLifecycleProvider` drops every channel after five minutes hidden. `ForceReloadProvider` joins once and does not join again, so that copy hears nothing more until it reloads.
- **Within the hour.** `UpdatePrompt` asks for a new version each hour, and when a tab is looked at again after fifteen minutes hidden.
- **Hard Reload and the update notice ask first** is v2.8499 and v2.8513.

- **The gear menu** ended on **Sign out** and **Hard Reload**, with no **Global Reload**.

- **The card** drew its line, the press at 139 by 44 in orange under white words, and the four lists under the press with thirteen lines. It was 984 wide at the desk and 374 on the phone. Nothing was wider than the screen.
- **The press** opened the window with its question, what people lose, and the two buttons. **Keep them working** closed it, and the card said nothing.

**Reload everyone was not pressed.** The dev server talks to the real Realtime service, so a yes would have reloaded every real person's open copy. The Realtime socket was also closed in that browser, and no `force_reload` frame left. That the yes sends the signal once is held by the card's render test.

## Not proved

- **Reload everyone** in a browser, for the reason above.
- That another copy reloads when the signal is sent. This change does not touch the listener.
- The live site.
- Chromium only.

## What is left

- **It still reloads over typed words.** That is now said in the window and under the press, and it is unchanged. Making it wait for typed words in each tab was offered and not taken.
- **A copy that was hidden five minutes never hears it again** until it reloads. The card says so. Joining the channel again when the tab comes back would be a small change to `ForceReloadProvider`.
- **The signal is an ordinary public Realtime channel.** The press is shown to a dev, and nothing in the code limits who may send the signal. The project's Realtime settings, which could, were not looked at.
