# v2.7941 — Permit news moves to Followup, with a red count and one press to answer (2026-11-19)

Punch list #401, PR 3 of the train in [`to-dos/permit-news-on-the-bids/`](../../to-dos/permit-news-on-the-bids/README.md). The mock-up is [`followup-mockup.html`](../../to-dos/permit-news-on-the-bids/followup-mockup.html). No migration: the table is [`20261119130000_bid_permit_answers`](../migrations/20261119130000_bid_permit_answers.md) from v2.7940.

## Why

An estimator asked for the strip to leave the top of the Bid Board. The owner chose Followup, with a red number when new permit news comes in, for the estimator to verify or ignore. Two looks at the mock-up settled how a line is answered: the card asks what the estimator wants to know, and one press answers it.

## What changed

- **The answers, read and written.** `src/lib/research/bidPermitAnswers.ts` folds the rows of `bid_permit_answers` into the answer that stands per bid and permit: the newest stands, and a take-back leaves none. `bidPermitAnswersIo.ts` reads every row a page at a time, reads a table that is not on the database yet as no answers, and appends one answer as the person signed in.
- **`bidPermitNews` takes the fold.** A permit that is not this job is taken off the bid's site before anything is read, so the chip and the news are the ones the address would have without it, and it is listed under `takenOff` with who said so. A line on a permit that is this job stays and carries the answer (`answered`). Every line names the permit it is about (`city`, `permit`) and the building permit beside a plumber's (`beside`).
- **`permitInbox`** sorts the result for one reader: their own bids apart from everyone else's, and on each side the lines nobody has answered apart from the ones that stand.
- **The Bid Board** has no strip. `BidBoardPermitNews.tsx` is deleted. The rows keep their chips, which the board now takes as a prop (`permitChips`).
- **One read for the page.** `Bids.tsx` runs `useBidPermitNews` once over the board's bids, for the chips, the lens and the count. The hook also reads the answers (`loadPermitAnswers`) and reads them again after each answer saved in the window (`onPermitAnswered`).
- **The red count** is a press of its own beside **Followup** in the Bids tab row, on every Bids tab: `permitInbox(...).mine.fresh.length`, the lines on the reader's own bids that nobody has answered. It opens `?tab=permit-news`. The word Followup still opens the Call queue.
- **Followup → Permit news** (`BidsPermitNewsLens.tsx`) is an eighth lens, last in the bar, with the same count on a red badge (a new `alert` tone on `BidsLensBar`).
  - A card's words come from `permitNewsCard` (`src/lib/research/permitNewsCard.ts`): the kind as a tag, the bid with its GC, value, sent day and last contact, the one large sentence, the work in the city's words with the permit's number, and the reasons.
  - **The reasons** are what the app compared. A tick: the bid's own suite, issued after the bid was sent, the building permit lists the bid's GC, the city's project line looks like the bid's name. A question mark: neither side names a suite, the permit names none, the permit is dated before the bid was sent.
  - **The answers** stand in one column on the right, and under the card on a phone. Another name on a plumbing permit of a sent bid asks *Did we lose this bid?*: **We lost it…** opens `BidBoardNewsMarkLost` across the card and writes no answer, **Still waiting** writes `this_job`, **Not our job** writes `not_this_job`. Every other card takes **Got it** (`this_job`) or **Not our job**. An issued building permit on a sent bid also has **Open the Call queue ›**.
  - An answered card folds to one line with **Undo** where it stood (`taken_back`). A save that fails says why on the card.
  - **Answered on your bids** lists the lines answered before, each with who and when, and **Take back** or **Put back**.
  - **Yours** and **Everyone's** switch whose bids are listed. The count is the reader's own either way.
- **Who.** The lens is for the roles that read permits (`canResearchPermits`). `permit-news` joins `BIDS_TABS`, the Followup group and the tabs a superintendent is turned away from. Search knows it as *Permit news*.

The count and the lens read the trade on screen, as every count in that tab row does.
