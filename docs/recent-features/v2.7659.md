# v2.7659 — Papers before a job opens: the database now holds the lock for crew and subs (2026-11-17)

Papers before a job opens, step 4 of seven: the lock in the database. One migration, `20261118010000_job_papers_lock.sql`, and its SQL bed. No client code and no edge function. The detail is in `docs/migrations/20261118010000_job_papers_lock.md`.

Until this step the prompt was the only lock. From the push on, the database refuses a job to a locked person who owes it a paper.

## Who is locked

The owner's call 8 of 2026-11-17, in his words: "Prompt is enough" for the office. So the database lock applies to everyone except the prompt-only roles, `job_papers_prompt_only()`: dev, master technician, assistant, controller and primary. A primary is with the office here because a primary has a Pipeline and money totals too, and hiding the job's row would take the job out of both until they sign. Helpers, subcontractors, superintendents and estimators are locked.

## What closes

- **The job's own row**: a restrictive policy `job_papers_fence` on `jobs_ledger`. It closes direct reads, the schedule's and the calendar's job embed, and the clock-session grant.
- **What the crew reaches through its helpers**: `helper_on_job`, `subcontractor_can_read_jobs_ledger_row`, `can_report_stage_progress`, `on_site_can_touch_job`, `superintendent_report_job_anchor_allowed` and `superintendent_can_touch_job_thread` each answer false for a job shut to the caller. That closes notes, report photos, stage checklists, plans, materials on site, and the `job-photos` edge function, which asks `can_report_stage_progress`.
- **Seven tables that grant by themselves**: the same policy on `jobs_ledger_thread_notes`, `jobs_tally_parts`, `job_stage_windows`, `file_index`, `messages`, `painted_maps` and `step_commitments`.

## What stays open, on purpose

- `job_schedule_blocks` and `jobs_ledger_team_members`: a locked person still sees that they have a block, by its time. Their list shows the job's number and a lock.
- `can_access_project_row`: it is keyed on a project, so there is no job to ask about.
- The list functions that skip row security, the edge functions that read with the service key, the push notices and the schedule email: step 5.
- Google Drive itself. A person the job's folder is shared with reads it there.

## Safety

The lock's question, `job_shut_to_me(job)`, looks at the live required papers first and answers before it asks who the caller is. With no required paper anywhere, the bed's reads of a job's row, its notes and every helper started no scan of `person_contract_documents`. The policies are `TO authenticated`, so the database agent roles and the service role never meet them. Each helper keeps its only definition with one condition added at the end, so the question is asked only about a job the caller could otherwise reach.

## Verified

The `job-papers-lock` bed in CI, on the whole schema with the migration applied twice: before any paper every role reaches what it reached; a paper closes the job's row, its notes and every helper to a helper, a sub, a superintendent and an estimator, and nothing of another job; the office and the primary keep every read; a locked helper cannot write a note; signing opens the job to that person alone; taking the paper off opens it to all. The beds of the helpers it replaces (`helper-on-job`, `on-site-move`, `on-site-guards`, `stage-checklist-items`, `stage-check-self-close`) pass with it.
