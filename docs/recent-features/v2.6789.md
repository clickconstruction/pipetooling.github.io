# v2.6789 — Adding a teammate: one window at a time, Attach a login at the end, Delete for a row made by mistake (2026-11-09)

The owner, 2026-11-09, as a dev on clicktooling.com, adding an office account for a new assistant: *+ Add to roster* with both boxes ticked saved the person, then opened the Person Desk drawer **and** the page's Send-invite confirm at once, the confirm under the drawer (`zIndex: 710` against `DESK_Z = 760`). Closing the drawer to reach the confirm, Send invite was refused by `invite-user` ("on the roster with no login. Attach a login from their desk instead"), because since v2.6514 the add already makes a users row with no login and the invite would mint a second id. The owner then had no idea how to reopen the drawer (the doors were the dotted name and ⋯ → Open desk), and the test row, named wrong, had only Archive, which is for someone who worked here. Related history: punch list #260 (Attach a login, v2.6518), #265 (`people.account_user_id` `ON DELETE RESTRICT`, v2.6530), #256 (Bring back), v2.3701 (the Hire form).

## What changed

- **One window at a time.** Every window `People.tsx` opens (the roster form, the name-and-title editor) sits on `DESK_EDITOR_Z` (1100), the rung the Hire form and the desk's own editors already use, above the drawer's `DESK_Z` (760); confirms keep 1300 and popovers 1250. The invite confirm is gone. The roster form's *Also invite them to sign in* is, for a dev, *Also attach a login (sends them a sign-in email)*: `attachLogin` runs on the new users row right after the save (the same `create-user` + `send-sign-in-email` pair as the desk), then the desk opens if asked; a failed attach opens the desk on the Attach form instead. The ⋯ menu's *Invite as user* (left only for a roster record with no users row at all) confirms through the app's `confirmDialog` (*Send the invite*) and no longer through a page window.
- **The Hire form ends with Attach a login** (`HirePersonModal.tsx`): hired without the invite as a dev, the footer offers {{button:blue|Attach a login}} beside *Open their desk*; it closes the form and opens the desk on Access & account with the Attach form open and the address filled (`PersonDeskOpenArgs.flow: 'attach_login'`, read by `PersonDeskBody` → `PersonDeskAccessSection.openAttach`). The Users tab's ⋯ *Attach a login* uses the same door.
- **The way back**: `PersonDeskContext` remembers the desk closed last (`lastClosed`), and People → Users shows *Back to <name>'s desk* in both toolbars while the desk is shut.
- **Delete for a row made by mistake** — migration `20261109110000_delete_fresh_person.sql`: `delete_fresh_person(p_user_id, p_dry_run)` deletes the users row with no login and its People record in one transaction, with the pay row and workday template the Hire form wrote; every foreign key into `users(id)` or `people(id)` is read from `pg_constraint` and counted first, and any row that points at the person (a clock session, a pay report, paperwork, a customer, an HR file entry, a matter, a message) is a hold the call answers instead of deleting. Gate: a dev, the controller or a pay-approved master (archive-user's set), never in training mode, never one's own row; a login (or an `auth.users` row of the same id) and a sample or twin are refused with plain words. The kernel `src/lib/people/freshPersonDelete.ts` holds who is offered the press (`canOfferFreshDelete`), the answer's shape, the words (`holdWords` folds two keys from one table into one line) and `runFreshDelete`: dry run → the typed confirm (`Delete <name>`) → the delete, or the holds named with *Archive instead* opening the archive door. Doors: the desk's Status row *Delete…* beside *Archive…* (`PersonDeskAccessSection`), and the Users row's ⋯ *Delete…* (`PeopleUsersTab` ← `People.tsx`). Bed: `supabase/tests/users_without_login/70_delete_fresh_person.sql` (D1–D7).
- **Guides**: *hire someone* (the Attach step at the end, *Made a mistake*, *Find the desk again*), *add someone to the roster* (the box's new words, Delete…, Back to the desk), *archive and restore user accounts* (a fresh row is deleted, not archived). `docs/ACCESS_CONTROL.md` has the Delete gate and the new Attach doors.
