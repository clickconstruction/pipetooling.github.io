# v2.6509 — The email viewer offers Start a bid and Put it on a bid (2026-11-04)

Feature, one migration (`20261104181239_email_on_bid_log.sql`, **push right after the merge**: the client calls `bid_log_message_in`). Grace, 2026-11-04, with a screenshot of the Messages thread window's email viewer on Glenn Gelwin's *Northeast Bible Church Elementary Building @ San Antonio - Invitation to Bid*, tied to job 579, its row *Start a change order · Copy · Print or save as PDF · Share…*: "I like that we have buttons like start a change order, but this item is a new bid, or it could be we found the bid elsewhere and just want to attach this history to the bid so we know it is coming in from other sources, we need to add buttons to offer this."

## What changed

- **The row reads the email** (`src/lib/messages/emailViewerActions.ts`, `viewerPresses`): one pure decision for the message window's first slot, from the line's tie, its channel and direction, whether it reads as an invitation to bid (`messages.invitation` as the reader kept it, else the same kernel over the subject and words, `lineInvitation`) and the viewer's role. A line on a bid → **Open the bid**. An email that came in and reads as an invitation, or that is on no job → **Start a bid** and **Put it on a bid…**, and no change order, even when the invitation sits on a job (Glenn's did, on 579). A line tied to a GC project that is not an invitation → **Start a change order**, as cut 4b drew it; a line on a Pipeline job still goes to its estimate (R8 · 8). Someone off the office team, a note and an event get nothing. Copy, Print and Share are the window's own and stay on every line. Cut 4b's hook (`useChangeOrderThreadSlots`) asks the same kernel, so the change order no longer draws on an untied customer line either (its press only ever answered *Tie the message to a job first*).
- **Start a bid** (`src/components/messages/EmailViewerActions.tsx`, drawn by `useEmailViewerThreadSlots`, merged after cut 4b's in `threadSlots.tsx`): a panel under the tools in the change order panel's place, the *Bid it?* card's own pieces: the **Bid** field starting with the project the reader read, else the subject (`startBidName`); the contact's customer as one press, *Start a bid on Marrex Builders*, else **A customer we know** over `bidCustomerChoices` from the card's `loadKnown` and **A new customer** from the domain with *Make the customer and start the bid*. The press is the card's (`NewLaneSlot`'s `startBid`, now `customerFor`): the customer made and the sender named through the card's io, then `start_bid_from_message` with the name, the due day and time and the portal link; the toast is `startedBidWords`, the thread re-read, closed, and the Bid Board opened on the bid (`bidHref`).
- **Put it on a bid…**: the window's job picker (`JobPickerWindow`, *Put it on a bid*) over the person's bids and the open bids a name or a number finds (`searchOpenBids`: on the board, not adopted into another bid, not lost, newest first, twelve at most; no *On the person* row). The pick ties the line through the Tied to row's own writer (`messagesIo.tieMessage`, the function the window's picker calls), so the Tied to row updates in place, then writes one line on the bid's log through `bid_log_message_in` (the words from the kernel, `putOnBidLogLine`): *Invitation to bid came in by email from Glenn Gelwin, Oct 22.*, *Addendum came in by email from …*, or for a plain email *An email came in from Glenn Gelwin, Oct 22: Re: fixture schedule.* The toast: *Put on B520 Elm Street Shell. Its history says it came in by email.* A refusal's words sit under the tools with no Retry.
- **The migration**: `bid_log_message_in(p_bid_id, p_message_id, p_words)`, SECURITY DEFINER in the shape of `adopt_logs_both_bids` (v2.6250). The log's one INSERT policy admits devs, masters, assistants and estimators and the bid's service type, while `follow_up_press` (the office team that may tie a line) also holds the controller: written as the caller, a controller's press would fail on RLS after the tie landed. The helper gates on `follow_up_press`, needs an email that came in and is on that bid already, `can_access_bid_for_pricing`, a twin on its own bids; the read-only statement trigger still fires inside it. An invitation with no answer is answered as a bid on the jsonb, the shape `start_bid_from_message` writes, so the New lane stops asking *Bid it?* of it. `docs/migrations/20261104181239_email_on_bid_log.md`.
- **The line carries the reader's facts**: `MESSAGE_COLS` selects `invitation`; `MessageRow` and `ThreadMessage` carry it raw (`invitation?: unknown`), read through `invitationOf` where a line needs it.
- **Words**: the presses' hover cards in the shape of the change order's (`VIEWER_PRESS_WORDS`): *Makes a bid from this email, with the name, the link and the due day. The customer is made if new. Ties this email to the bid and opens it.* and *Ties this email to a bid you already have and writes it on the bid’s history, so the bid says it came in from here too.*
- **Guides**: *find every message with a person in one place* → Open one message says the four presses and when each shows; *sort new messages before anyone answers them* → Bid it? says the viewer's two presses for an invitation found in a thread; *start a change order from a customer's message* says the press shows only on a message tied to a job. `docs/MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md` (the window's first slot), `docs/GLOSSARY.md`.
