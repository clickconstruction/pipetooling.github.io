# v2.3727 — Day book PR 4: estimators, and the estimating strip (2026-09-22)

PR 4 of [`to-dos/day-book`](../../to-dos/day-book/README.md) (punch list #20). One migration (the RPC), client.

## What was missing

The tab read office roles only. The owner's *"let's also think about measuring estimators"* had a design — lines on the days an estimator clocks into a bid, and a range strip that measures the person only against their own trailing months — and no code.

## The change

- **[`20260922160000_day_book_estimators.sql`](../migrations/20260922160000_day_book_estimators.md)** — `get_day_book_payload` re-created on PR 5's body: estimators join the population; sessions carry `bid_id`; seven estimator sources join the union (a bid sent from `bid_version_sends`, valued at the send; the human touches on `bid_pricing_assignments` grouped per bid per day; `bid_best_efforts`; `bid_rfqs` with the quotes that came back; a verdict on `bid_audit_notes`; `twin_questions` answered; `bids_submission_entries` with a `contact_method`); `bids` in the result for labels; and `estimating` — the strip for one picked person, each measure for this window and the equal window before it. Money on a bid row: a payroll viewer, or the person looking at their own bids.
- **Kernel** — seven kinds, the Estimating chip, bid refs as `BP483` linking to the bid, the lines (*Sent 2 bids · BP483 BP485 · to 5 GCs · $662,000*; *Asked 2 houses for prices · 1 quote in*; *Followed up 1 GC · BP483 BP485*), the bid session named on the clock line, `summary.bidsSent`.
- **[`dayBookEstimating.ts`](../../src/lib/people/dayBookEstimating.ts)** (+4 tests) — the nine tiles; a hit rate under five decided bids shows its count and reads muted; an empty window reads *—*, never *0 %*; zero clocked bid hours is a dash, not *0.0 per $100k*; money hidden reads *amounts hidden*. Wendi's census figures reproduce (*4 of 5* after due date, *0% · 2 decided* grey, *+44.2% median · 1 sealed run*).
- **The tab** — the strip under the range totals when a person is picked, with *Bid vs actual →* (Bids → Bid Costs); a *Bids sent* tile; the Month grid's Estimating row.
- Docs: `BIDS_SYSTEM.md` (the Day book reads the bid tables), `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` (estimators' rows are read by the tab's roles; the role has no door of its own), the glossary, the guide's *Estimators* section.

## What this PR does not do

An estimator's own view of themselves. `/people` is closed to the role (v2.2920, on purpose — a two-tab roster with every address), and the Layout guard is path-based, so a `tab=day_book`-only allowance or a door from Bids is its own small PR (**PR 4b** on the to-do, an owner call on which). Until then the estimator rows are read by dev, controller, pay-approved masters and assistants.
