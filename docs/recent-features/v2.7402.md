# v2.7402 — Job window: Subs on this job (sub labor train) (2026-11-15)

Client only, no migration. Punch list #346, on the kernels of v2.7383 and the hook of v2.7388. Taunya walked it in the design spike on 2026-11-15 and said to ship the train.

## What landed

- `src/components/jobs/JobSubsOwedBlock.tsx`: *Subs on this job*, in the Costs region of `JobFormModal.tsx` under the labor cost panel. `useJobSheets` reads the job's sheets by its link, and by its number for a sheet that never got a link, with their lines, payments and assignees. `useSubsOwed` gives each sheet its parties and its *Pay when*, and `buildJobOwedBlock` groups them by who they are owed to under `TEAM_SHEET_RULE`.
- The view, `JobSubsOwedView`: one line per person with their sheets, the chip for when each pays or *Paid*, a shared sheet set apart, the sum and *✓ adds up*, and an amber line from `sameLines` when two sheets of the job carry the same line and amount. *Open the sheet ›* opens the sheet on the Pay view, a name opens the person's row on People → Subs (`?owed=`), and *Show the sheets ›* opens the Pay view narrowed to the job (`?subSearch=`).
- Drawn for dev, master technician, assistant and controller, the roles who open Jobs → Subs → Pay. It draws nothing on a job with no sub sheets.
- Guide `job-window-tabs`: a section on who the job still owes for sub labor.

## Tests

`JobSubsOwedBlock.render.test.tsx` (3): the people and the teammate with their own sheet, the sum that adds up, the same-line warning and its door, and a sheet's door. The job form's own smokes pass with the block mounted.
