# v2.8259 — Bid Board: a link to a bid now brings that bid into view (2026-11-21)

Found on the live walk of v2.8215 (punch list #306, PR 9). A client change. No migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

**Open the bid**, on a bid's photo in Documents → Photos, goes to `/bids?tab=bid-board&bidId=…`. The Bid Board took the link, opened the bid's section and rang its row, and the page stayed at the top with the row about 16,600 px down. Pressed twice on the live app, the same both times. The same link loaded fresh did scroll, and stopped about 2,200 px short of the row.

The cause was in `applyBidBoardDeepLinkToBid` ([`useBidsDeepLinks.ts`](../../src/hooks/useBidsDeepLinks.ts)): one `scrollIntoView`, 150 ms after the link landed. A row not drawn by then was never scrolled to. A row that was drawn was scrolled to where it stood at that moment, and what the board loaded above it afterwards pushed it back out of view. Every door that lands on a bid on the board shared it; the photo door is where it was seen, because the ZZ Test bid sits far down the Won section.

## What changed

- **A scroll that holds** ([`scrollToRowAndHold.ts`](../../src/lib/scrollToRowAndHold.ts)): it looks for the row five times a second, for eight seconds at most. The first time the row is there it scrolls to it, gently. After that the rule `holdStep` decides each look: a row that sat somewhere else at the last look is still moving, so wait; a row at rest and out of view is scrolled to again, at once; a row at rest and in view counts toward three in a row, and then the hold lets go. A wheel, a touch, a key or a pointer press from the reader lets go at that moment.
- **The Bid Board's link** uses it in place of the timer, cancels a hold still running when a second link lands or the page closes, and rings the row again once it rests, since the first ring lasts 2.5 seconds and a long board can take longer than that to arrive.

`lib/scrollWhenVisible.ts` and `lib/landOn.ts` are unchanged: each scrolls once, which is right for a short window. This one is for a long list that is still being drawn.

## Checked

- `scrollToRowAndHold.test.ts`: the rule on its own, and the clock with a row that is not drawn yet, a row pushed out of view after the first scroll, a reader who scrolls, the time running out and the cancel.
- The same rule pasted into the live Bid Board from the console, before the deploy: the ZZ Test bid's row, 15,100 px down, came to rest in the middle of the view in 2.3 seconds, with one correction.
- The Bids page's render tests pass.
