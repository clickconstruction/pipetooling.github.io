# v2.4623 — Help: four more guides name the lien screens as they are (2026-10-05)

PR 4 of the lien-guide freshness pass ([v2.4614](v2.4614.md), [v2.4617](v2.4617.md) and [v2.4620](v2.4620.md) are PRs 1 to 3). It fixes the lien sentences in five guides that are mainly about other things. Each fix was read against main's code, then given an adversarial second read.

## The fixes, by guide

- **see a customer's full history and lifetime value**
  - The *▶ Property legal info* panel was removed in v2.3009. A property's legal record is now under its **Edit**, then *Property record*.
  - The lookup runs the first time that property is opened (`CustomerPropertiesSection.tsx`, `CustomerPropertySheet.tsx`, `CustomerPropertyRecordPanel.tsx`).
- **read the Pipeline's money view**
  - The billed date starts the pay estimate, not the lien clock, which counts from the last work month (`lienPayRunway.ts`).
  - The runway row: a notice recorded anywhere counts; a ring means the row is not asking yet; red also at any distance when the money is expected after the deadline; *File the lien* also asks at once then (`billedDatesLedger.ts`).
  - The *Owner of record to confirm* chip counts open GC jobs with or without approved hours (since v2.3747; `pipelineOverview.ts`).
  - The GC's name sits in its own chip (since v2.4054; `PipelineMoneyOpportunities.tsx`).
  - The first row of the ☰ Section tools menu is *Lien desk*, with the orange gavel and the count, and it opens the desk on Next up (`stagesSectionToolsMenu.ts`, `JobsStagesTab.tsx`).
  - The piles run in the card's order and include *approved for the run*, and the confirm button reads *Use all N found* (`lienDeskMoneyCard.ts`, `OwnerConfirmListModal.tsx`).
- **get started in the office (assistant or controller)**: the Collections header's *Lien desk* button wears the orange gavel, not ⏱ (v2.4527).
- **the Needs you card**: a lien window closing soon opens the Lien desk; a filed lien not served, or a demand deadline, opens the job's Lien window (`DashboardPinnedQuickRow.tsx`).
- **know whether a customer keeps their word**: there is no *Final Demand Desk*. The notice history is on the job's Lien window, on the *Demand letter* tab (`LienInstrumentsModal.tsx`).
