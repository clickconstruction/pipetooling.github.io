# v2.4896 — GC mode: Trade partners on real data, for a dev (2026-10-08)

The Board's B3-b in the real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/BOARD_REAL_BUILD.md`, on branch `spike/gc-mode`): Trade partners on real data at `/gc`, beside B3-a's Project Board behind a dev-only **Project Board | Trade partners** switch. The first screen where the office types a real trade partner. Guides: `add-a-trade-partner`, `approve-a-company-new-to-us`.

- **`GcTradePartners.tsx`** is ported from the spike's `GcTradeBench.tsx` and `GcVetting.tsx` onto B2's kernels (`tradeBenches`, `partnerAsks`, `assistantRules`, `partnersToVet`, `vettingWords`, `travelFor`).
  - **New to us**: each company waiting on our decision, with its vetting form. **Approve**, **Approve up to this** and **Decline** go through `gc_vet_company`, with a note. The signed-in user is who decided.
  - Each trade's card: its companies with how they answer and what they are asked on now, and the projects still short of quotes. A trade on our projects with no company yet still gets a card.
  - **Add a company** goes through `gc_add_company`. Not vetted unless **We have worked with them** is ticked.
  - A company's address line opens its address and miles, saved by a plain update of `gc_companies`.
  - Actions for assistants is read only; its Add buttons open the trade's form.
- **`gcIo.ts`**: `addGcCompany`, `vetGcCompany`, `setGcCompanyCoverage`. Each write reads the board's rows again. `loadGcBoardRows` also reads the vetting forms of the companies still new to us; `boardRows.ts` maps them onto `Partner.vetting.form`.
- **Waits:** asking a company is B4 (the Ask window), papers are B6, and the company window (its About, language and people) comes after the Portal's `contactGets`.
- **Tests:** `GcTradePartners.render.test.tsx` (decide, add, address), a mapper case in `boardRows.test.ts`, and a page test for the switch.
