# v2.5756 — Legal: color the Texas counties each collections law firm covers (2026-10-23)

Punch list #144, PR 1 of 4 (`to-dos/legal-firms-by-county/README.md`, the mock-up beside it). The owner is engaging a second collections law firm, one for San Antonio and one for Austin, and territories are counties because venue for a collections suit is the county, which every customer address already carries. This PR lays the table and the picker; the desk's pick by venue (PR 2), the Map lens (PR 3) and the portal's Who covers what (PR 4) follow.

- **Migration `20261023010000_legal_firm_counties`** (`docs/migrations/`): `legal_firm_counties (firm_id, county, since, until, set_by)`, one live row per county (`until IS NULL`, partial unique index), history kept as closed rows; the office reads, a dev inserts and updates, nobody deletes; the three house calls. It also **drops `legal_firms_one_active`** (v2.4641): with territories, two active firms are the feature. `legal_replace_firm` still works. `legalFirmSaveErrorWords` now reads the old index's refusal as "push the v2.5756 migration first", since the client deploys before the push.
- **Settings → Jobs & billing → Collections law firms** (`LegalFirmSettingsBlock`, plural; `LegalFirmCard`, `LegalFirmCountyMap`): a card per active firm on the left (press to pick it; its hue on the card's edge; its counties in words; *Edit details* unfolds the form; **Add a firm** opens an empty card), the Texas county map on the right. Click a county to color it for the picked firm, click again to clear it; a county another firm holds leaves the map as it was and asks *Travis is Austin firm's. Move it to Snell? Matters already with Austin firm stay there.* Nothing writes until **Save counties** (close the old live rows, insert the new, `countyWritePlan`); **Undo changes** puts the draft back; the line between them counts the changes. The desk's firm window keeps the one firm's form (`inWindow` + `firmId`). Click's particulars for filing stay under everything, shared by every firm.
- **The county file** `public/geo/tx-counties.json` (68 KB, 254 counties; Census cartographic boundaries, public domain, simplified with mapshaper) — its source, license and the regenerate command in `docs/TX_COUNTY_MAP.md`. `src/lib/legal/txCountyMap.ts` projects it to SVG (pure, tested); `txCountyMapIo.ts` fetches it once.
- **Kernels** `src/lib/legal/firmCounties.ts` (+ tests): the saved map and the draft, `pressCounty` (clear / color / `held`), `moveCounty`, `countyChanges`, `countyWritePlan`, the legend and each firm's hue (six theme-token pairs, by the firm's age), every sentence the screen says. `useLegalMatters` gains `activeFirms` and `countiesByFirm`; nothing reads them before PR 2.
- Guide `set-which-law-firm-covers-each-county` (dev). `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`, `GLOSSARY.md`, `docs/README.md`, `docs/twins/APP_DIRECTORY.md` amended.

After merge: deploy the client (CI), then `supabase db push` from a main checkout.
