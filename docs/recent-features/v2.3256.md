## What shipped

**The activity trail.** `discountActivity.ts` keeps a snapshot of the discount rows as last persisted (hydration, then every successful billing-slice write) and diffs it after each write: one `discount_added` / `discount_changed` / `discount_removed` event per real change, never per keystroke, never on a save that left the discounts alone. Summaries read as the ledger should — *Discount added: Negotiated discount (10%) −$3,774.50 · all 3 work lines*, *Discount changed: Negotiated discount 10% (−$3,774.50) → 12% (−$4,529.40)*, *Discount removed: …* — with the structured before/after in `detail`. They are logged through the new `log_job_discount_event` RPC (office roles; financial; best-effort — a failed log never fails the save) and render in the Job activity feed with a **Discount** tag (green added, amber changed, red removed; billing bucket). The fixtures trigger no longer logs *Specific work added* for discount rows, which the delete+reinsert save engine would have repeated on every autosave.

**Job Summary.** Every row carries `discountUsd` (its discount rows' signed amounts, plus legacy negative rows) and a `discount` flag; the ledger card shows a green **− discount** chip beside ✂ write-down (money view only), and the toolbar's leakage line adds *− $X discounted on N jobs* next to the write-down figure. Revenue already reflects discounts; the figure names what was given away up front.

**Add discount on a draft.** On a Ready-to-Bill (draft) row in ② Invoices, **Add discount** adds a discount row in ① Line Items and flashes the section — the discount prints on that bill and every other bill that carries its work. Billed rows keep the agreed write-down under the same label.

**Tests**: `discountActivity.test.ts` (snapshot; added / changed amount, basis, name / removed, including an amount that dropped to nothing); Job Summary rows and totals; the event registry's known-type list.
