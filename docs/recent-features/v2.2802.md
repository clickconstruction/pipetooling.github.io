# v2.2802 — Contract Forms PR 7: two-party forms (the office section) and the I-9 (2026-09-05)

The owner asked for the USCIS **Form I-9** (Edition 01/20/25). It is a two-party form: the employee fills and signs Section 1, the employer examines documents and completes Section 2. This PR teaches Contract Forms about parties and ships the I-9 schema.

- **Parties.** `FormBox.party?: 'signer' | 'office'` (default signer). Kernel: `partyOf`, `hasOfficeBoxes`, `schemaForParty(schema, party)` (boxes filtered, groups / one-of sets pruned — every kernel function works unchanged on the result). `FillContext.office?: { signature, todayLabel }` fills office signature and office "today" date boxes; without it they produce nothing. Studio: a **Filled by** select in the inspector; office boxes hatch-shaded on the layer; the summary counts them.
- **Signing and paper entry** validate and fill only the signer's half (`schemaForParty(…, 'signer')`). A two-party form is filed **unflattened** with the filled fields set read-only (`flatten: false, readOnlyFilled: true`), so the office can still fill its fields.
- **New edge function `complete-contract-form-office`** (`verify_jwt = false`; gate dev · master · assistant · controller; the row must be readable under the caller's RLS): `prepare` → office schema + a 15-minute URL of the filed PDF + stored office values + completion state; `complete` → fills the office boxes on the filed PDF, types the office signature in cursive, dates office boxes today, **flattens**, overwrites `<id>/signed.pdf`, stores `office_values` (non-sensitive) + `office_completed_at/by` + `office_signer_printed_name`. One-shot (409 `already_completed`).
- **Record modal**: two-party forms show an **Office section** line (amber *not completed yet* / green *completed … signed as …*) with **Complete the office section** → `ContractFormOfficeModal` (the filed PDF with only the office boxes over it, `FormFillOverlay`, name-and-title input, required-box check) or **View office section** afterwards.
- **Executor** (`fillFormPdf`): field ops → flatten → **then** draw ops, so drawn text is never covered by a field's flattened appearance (the I-9's State dropdown taught us). **Dropdowns** are read as `kind: 'dropdown'` and filled by selecting the matching option (case-insensitive). Every field op is tolerant: unknown names, `maxLength` overflow, an option the dropdown lacks fall back to drawing at the box's rect (bound ops now carry `page` / `rect` / `align`) and are reported in `skipped` as `name (reason)`. `readOnlyFilled` for the two-party signer stage. `draftSchemaFromPdfFields` drafts dropdowns as bound text boxes.
- **`scripts/forms/preview.ts`** previews both parties (Sample Signer / Office Signer).
- **The I-9**: `docs/forms/i9-2025-01.schema.json` — Section 1 (employee: names, address, State drawn over the dropdown, ZIP, date of birth and SSN as masked digits (SSN 9 straight digits to fit the comb, sensitive, optional), email / phone prefilled, the four-way citizenship group, box 3 / box 4 details under *rarely needed*, signature + date) and Section 2 as office boxes (List A documents 1–3, List B, List C, additional information, the alternative-procedure box, first day of employment, employer name and title, employer signature + date, the employer's business name and address as constants). Published into the **All Teammates** packet as **Form I-9** (audience staff, paperwork type other). Tests: I-9 fixture `src/test/fixtures/fi9-2025-01.pdf` — 128 fields / 4 pages, State classified as a dropdown, two-stage fill (signer stage unflattened + read-only, office stage flattens).
