# v2.7827 — Materials on site: a press can be answered (2026-11-18)

The third PR of the train on seeing what one person does with the material on a site. One migration, `20261118170000_on_site_press_answers.sql`. No client change and no edge function.

## Why

The ledger is append only, so a press that should not stand could only be pressed against, one at a time, and the wrong row stayed in every sum. The office asked for a way to undo what a person did. Undo alone is the wrong tool for a press that really happened: taking back a real take makes the app say material is there when it is gone. So a press takes one of two answers.

## What landed

- **Taken back**: the press did not happen or should not stand. `job_material_movements.taken_back_at` is set and a RESTRICTIVE SELECT policy closes the row to every ordinary read.
- **Marked**: the press happened and was not allowed. No number changes. The answer is the record.
- **`job_movement_answers`** holds both, with why and who, one live answer a press, withdrawn with a reason and never deleted. Two triggers keep it honest: one stamps the caller and freezes an answer, the other sets and clears the switch on the ledger.
- **`on_site_taken_back_rows`** reads the closed rows for History, for the office.
- A dev gives and withdraws an answer. The office reads them. A helper or a primary reads neither the answers nor the rows taken back.

## Why one policy and not a rule in every sum

The first plan taught each sum to skip a taken-back row. There are several: the screens' kernel, the database's job summary, the line-on-hand function, the dev seat. One missed sum would make two screens disagree. Every one of them reads under the caller's own row-level security, so one policy on the ledger closes a row to all of them at once. The only owner-run functions that touch the ledger carry rows between jobs or rename them, and those must carry a taken-back row too.

## Checks

`supabase/tests/on_site_guards/60_press_answers.sql`, a fifth scenario of the on-site guards bed, which now applies this file a second time: a press cannot arrive taken back and a direct caller cannot set the switch; a count taken back leaves every reader summing as if it was never made, with the row kept; one live answer a press; a marked press stays in every sum; the office reads the rows and the answers and a helper reads neither; an answer's press, kind and reason stand; a withdrawal needs its reason, brings the row back and closes the answer; nobody deletes one; only a dev gives one. The bed runs in CI.
