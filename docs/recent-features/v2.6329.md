# v2.6329 — A job's kind: service call or project, read from the job's own facts and settled by one press (punch list #238, PR 1) (2026-11-02)

The owner, 2026-11-01, after the stage-checklist walk: *how can I better differentiate when a job is a service call versus a job being a commercial construction project that we bid?* And the cut, later the same day: a service call is priced by the work and billed at the end; a project is priced before the first hour is worked and moves only by change orders. The app had no job kind, only four signals that each said part of it (an HCP number against a Click number, a bid or a GC project behind the job, Stage Plan rows, the customer's Res/Comm), and #234's hours baseline and #237's stage checklists were about to gate on the customer's kind, which misfires both ways: a restaurant's clogged drain is a service call for a commercial customer, a custom home's rough-in is a project for a residential one. The card: `to-dos/job-kind/README.md`.

## Change

- **The kernel** `src/lib/jobs/jobKind.ts` (`jobKind`, `jobKindFacts`, `jobKindOfRow`; tests on every rule): the office's explicit kind wins; else a billing-only job → project (it carries a GC project's bills, the card's guard rail); else a price on the job before any work (a price and no first day of work, or a priced-on day before it) → project; else a bid or a GC project behind the job → project; else Stage Plan rows (an explicit Order row, or `recognizeStages`) → project; else an HCP number with none of those, or a price that came with the work → service call; else not set. A rule whose fact is not loaded is skipped, never guessed: a row never remembers the day its price was typed, so a priced job already worked falls to its other signals, and a blank first day on a job already at Ready to Bill, Billed or Paid says the clock never saw it, not that no work was done, so the price rule is skipped there too. Every read carries its source and a reason line (*Project · priced $112,000 from bid B917.*), with the bid named whenever one is behind the job.
- **The column** `jobs_ledger.kind` (`service` · `project`, nullable, `jobs_ledger_kind_known`, left `NOT VALID` since every row is null; `docs/migrations/20261102043000_job_kind.md`): the office's press, which the kernel reads first. No backfill: PR 2's Quickfill deck line lists the jobs the signals cannot settle.
- **The press** (`JobFormIdentityFields`'s `kind` prop; `JobFormModal`'s `kindRead`, `onKindPress`; `jobKindForNewJob`): New Job draws **Kind** under the job's name as two presses, Service call · Project, with the kernel's default already pressed and its reason under them (*Change it if that is wrong.*), or neither pressed and the mock-up's sentence when the signals settle nothing. A press is saved right after the row goes in, never in the insert, so a kind the database refuses says *Job saved, but its kind could not be saved* and the job stands. With no press the column stays null and the signals read on, so a bid linked later or stages typed later still move the read and *the office said so* stays true; the one exception is a price the form saw before any work that no other signal would keep, saved as a project, since the row never remembers the day its price was typed. Edit Job carries the same control over the saved column; a press writes `saveJobKind` at once (`jobKindWrite.ts`), refreshes the window, and names its reason on a failure, an update RLS filtered away included.
- **The chip** `JobKindChip.tsx` (blue Project, green Service call, amber Kind not set; the reason as the hover text): in the job window's header beside the name (`DetailJobModal`) and on every Pipeline row: after the number in both tables (`renderStagesJobHcpSubline`), under the name on the Mobile cards (`JobsStagesCardList`), and leading the second line on a phone row (`StagesPhoneRow`), so a phone keeps the job's name.
- **The board filter**: the ⋯ tools menu's kind select (All kinds · Projects · Service calls · Kind not set, each with its count from `jobKindCounts`), the command bar's tap-to-clear chip, `?kind=` as a deep link, `filterJobsByKind` after the contract filter in `JobsStagesTab`'s board lists; an empty group reads as filtered.
- **Words**: the guide *say whether a job is a service call or a project*; `GLOSSARY.md` → *Job kind*; `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` → `jobs_ledger.kind`.

## Not here

What then stops guessing is each reader's own one-line PR, as the card orders it: #234's hours baseline and the superintendent's line (its PR 1, #6745, is not on `main` tonight), #237's stage checklists (another session's), pay applications, the labor book's calibration, and the reports that split service revenue from construction revenue on the HCP-number heuristic. A warranty call on a finished project is a parent link, not a kind.
