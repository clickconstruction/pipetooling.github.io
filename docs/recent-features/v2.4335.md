# v2.4335 — Lien waivers: the signature he drew shows on every copy (2026-10-01)

No migration, no edge function. Grace (2026-10-01), on job 650's conditional waiver: Malachi drew his signature at her desk, but the PDF she downloaded showed his name in italic type over the line (a 5.9 KB file), and she asked for the wet signature and the printed name to sit better on the page.

## Why the download had no ink

Signing ([`signLienRelease`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienReleaseSignIo.ts)) stores the drawn PNG (`signer_signature_storage_path`) and the whole signed page (`signed_pdf_path`, 198 KB on 650, image inside). The GC's email and portal read that stored PDF. Every other copy rebuilt the page from the row through `lienReleaseRowSignature`, whose `pngDataUrl` is always null, so the PDF fell back to the name in italic times and the HTML to Great Vibes cursive: the window's Download PDF, Print and history View, the Documents page, the signature inbox, the cleared-releases queue, and the email's fallback when the stored PDF is missing.

## What changed

- **The ink, read back** [`lienReleaseInk.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienReleaseInk.ts) (5 tests): `loadLienReleaseInk` downloads the stored PNG as a data URL, trimmed, cached per path (a failed read is not cached); `lienReleaseRowSignatureWithInk` is the row's signature with it; `lienReleaseSignedPdfBlob` hands back the stored signed PDF byte for byte, or rebuilds the page with the ink when there is none. Wired into the window (Download PDF, Print, View, and the page itself once signed via `LienWaiverFootPreview`'s new `inkUrl`), [`Documents`](../../src/pages/Documents.tsx), [`LienSignatureInboxSection`](../../src/components/jobs/LienSignatureInboxSection.tsx), [`DashboardLienReleaseQueueModal`](../../src/components/dashboard/DashboardLienReleaseQueueModal.tsx) and [`sendLienReleaseEmail`](../../src/lib/sendLienReleaseEmail.ts).
- **Open now, fill when ready** `openHtmlWindowWhenReady` in [`printWindow.ts`](../../src/lib/jobsDocuments/printWindow.ts) (3 tests): the window opens inside the click with *Loading the page…* (popup blockers allow it), takes the page when the ink is read, and with `print` waits for its pictures before printing.
- **Trimmed to the strokes** [`signatureInkTrim.ts`](../../src/lib/signatureInkTrim.ts) (6 tests): `inkBounds` / `padInkBox` / `trimSignatureInk` crop a drawing to its ink plus a margin on white. `signLienRelease` stores the trimmed PNG; older drawings are trimmed as they are read.
- **Printed on the line at one size** [`lienWaiverRelease.ts`](../../src/lib/jobsDocuments/lienWaiverRelease.ts): `lienWaiverInkBox` sizes the ink 17 mm tall at its own proportions (80 mm at most); the PDF draws it with its bottom 1 mm above the rule, compressed (`'FAST'`: raw, jsPDF stored the pixels and a 13 KB drawing made a 300 KB page; now about 15 KB). The print HTML draws it 64 px tall, bottom-left. Tests in `lienWaiverRelease.pdf.test.ts` with a recording jsPDF stand-in (6).
- **Signing on the page** [`LienWaiverSignOnPage`](../../src/components/jobs/LienWaiverSignOnPage.tsx): the pad is the signature line at the foot of the page (a dashed *Sign here, on the line* box, the canvas sized to the line at the screen's pixel ratio and refitted with its strokes when the line changes width), the printed name under the rule as text, the title and *Signed <today>*. A leader at someone else's screen draws only; someone at their own desk may click *Type it instead*. `LienWaiverSignAgree` is the agreement and the button under the page; [`lienWaiverSignPayload`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienWaiverSignPayload.ts) (5 tests) reads the line: *Sign on the line first.*, then *Tick the box to agree first.* Used by [`LienReleaseSignModal`](../../src/components/jobs/LienReleaseSignModal.tsx) (the name box is gone: the name is the leader of record, else the waiver's Signed by) and the **Waivers to sign** seat. `ContractAcceptSignatureForm` (contracts, estimates) is unchanged.
- Guide *send a GC our lien waiver*: *Get the leader to sign*, *What prints under the signature*, *Sign several at once* and *Send it or download it*; the *He signs now* clip re-recorded on the new line.
