# v2.8374 — Job Summary: the cents are smaller than the dollars on every amount (2026-11-23)

The owner's ask of 2026-11-23, with a picture of the Job Summary chart's hover box: *I would like to see the cents smaller than the rest of the text, in fact I would like to see the cents smaller than the rest of a value's text across the app.* This is part 1, Job Summary, and the one door the rest of the app will use. Part 2, the sweep across the app, waits on the owner's go and his pick of the size. No migration, no edge function.

## What was there

Three components already drew small cents, each with its own kernel and two sizes between them.

- `src/components/AmountSmallCents.tsx` (v2.2246, Sub Labor; Bills sent since v2.8333; 18 files): a bare fragment over `splitFormattedAmountCents`, a string cut at its last point, the cents at 0.7em. A negative figure handed to it read `$-5.00`.
- `src/components/jobs/MoneySmallCents.tsx` (punch list #215, the job form; 5 files): `splitMoneyForDisplay` and `splitMoneyString`, the cents at 0.78em, no dollar sign.
- `src/components/charges/Money.tsx` (punch list #254, the charges surfaces; 3 files): `moneyParts`, the only kernel that took a number and gave back a sign, the dollars and the cents, with the cents at 0.78em and softer.

On Job Summary the tiles and the table's cells already went through the first one. The chart's hover box, the amounts drawn on the chart, the breakdown tables under a job, the discount fold, the delta under the *Per field hour* tile and the average wage in the costs verdict wrote `$` and `formatCurrency` straight into the screen.

## The one door

`src/components/Money.tsx`, over the kernel `src/lib/moneyParts.ts`.

- **Why this one.** `moneyParts` is the kernel the task asks for: a number in, its sign, its dollars and its cents out. The other two cut a string that was already formatted. So the charges kernel moved up from `src/lib/charges/` to `src/lib/` and the charges component's shape became the door, at the app's top level where `AmountSmallCents` sat.
- **The kernel.** `moneyParts(amount, { symbol, sign, cents })`. The digits come from `toLocaleString`, the call `formatCurrency` makes, so a screen that moves to the door never shows a figure a cent away from before (a test holds the two equal on the awkward halves, 1.115 among them). A loss wears a true minus before the dollar sign. A figure that rounds to nothing is not a loss. `sign: 'both'` marks zero and above with a plus, for the chart's cash position. `cents: false` is whole dollars, rounded as `formatCurrencyNoCents` rounds, with no cents to draw. `symbol: ''` is a column of bare figures. `moneyTextParts` cuts words a kernel already wrote at each amount that carries its `$`, so `8.50 h` and `12.50%` stay words. `moneyFigureParts` reads a money column's cell that holds one bare figure.
- **The size.** `MONEY_CENTS_EM`, 0.7, one named constant. It is in `em`, so the cents follow the text around them. The owner picks the size in part 2 and one line changes it everywhere.
- **The drawn half.** `Money` draws a number in a span that never breaks between the dollars and the cents. `MoneyText` draws words a kernel wrote. `MoneyTspans` is `MoneyText` inside an SVG `<text>`, the cents in a smaller `tspan`. The cents carry `data-cents`, for a test and for a print that wants them back at full size.
- **The older names draw through it.** `AmountSmallCents`, `SignedAmountSmallCents`, `MoneyWithSmallCents`, `MoneyStringWithSmallCents` and the charges `Money` are now a line or two each over the door, so there is one drawing and one size. Their callers are untouched. `splitMoneyForDisplay` and `splitMoneyString` had no reader left and are gone, their cases moved to `moneyParts.test.ts`.
- **Values in place.** `WorkedOutValue` and a closed or locked `ChangeableValue` draw their words through `MoneyText`, so an amount there (a `ValueSentence` value among them) gets small cents by the same door. Words that are not money draw as they did.

## What changed on Job Summary

- **The chart's hover box** (`JobSummaryChargesTimelineChart.tsx`): each charge and payment line's amount, Cost, Overhead to date with *by hours* and *carry*, True cost, Paid, Cash position and Value created.
- **The amounts drawn on the chart**, red, amber, green and blue: `MoneyTspans`. The label's words are still one string, so the kernel that keeps labels off each other measures what it measured. The label sits on its alphabetic baseline now (`dy="0.27em"` in place of `dominant-baseline: middle`), so the cents sit on the dollars' baseline; the dollars moved by 0.02 px.
- **The breakdown tables under a job** (`JobSummaryCostCellDrilldownModal.tsx`): the cost column and its total, and each card charge through `CardChargeCostAmount`. The supply house invoice lines and the card charges table in the tab itself.
- **The discount fold**, the delta line under the *Per field hour* tile, the overhead math sentence under a job, and the average wage in the costs verdict (`JobCostsVerdict.tsx`).
- **The Cut by rows** call the door by its own name.

`CardChargeCostAmount` also draws on the job window's materials section and the job form's parts section, so a card charge's cents are small there too.

## What did not change

- **Whole dollars.** Most of the tab already reads in whole dollars (`formatUsdNoCents`, the verdict's `usd0`, the chips). Those have no cents and are as they were.
- **Plain strings.** A `title`, an `aria-label`, an SVG `<title>`, the CSV and the cost breakdown's print builder. The breakdown window's print copies the table's HTML, so its print style puts `[data-cents]` back to full size and the paper reads as before.
- **Hours.** Four cells on the tab format hours with `formatCurrency`. They are hours and stay whole.

## Seen outside Job Summary

One size now holds for all three older components, so the job form's cells and the charges windows moved from 0.78em to 0.7em, and a negative figure through `AmountSmallCents` reads `−$5.00` where it read `$-5.00`.
