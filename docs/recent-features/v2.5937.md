# v2.5937 — Money in: the week is read, and every dollar sits in one place (2026-10-26)

Punch list #191, PR 2 of 4 (`to-dos/money-in-this-week/`). The reads behind the coming Money in
bar, and the kernel's rules tightened against double counting after a second read of v2.5926
against the mock-up. No UI yet.

**New edge function `get-stripe-payouts`** (`docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`): the next Stripe payout
(earliest `pending` / `in_transit` by arrival day) and the pending balance, read-only, gated to
dev / master_technician / assistant / controller — the bar's roles, deliberately not Banking's
gate, which refuses assistants. Nothing in the app read payouts before. `config.toml` keeps
`verify_jwt = false`; the dev-mcp catalog lists it.

**Loader** `src/lib/moneyInWeekLoad.ts` + hook `src/hooks/useMoneyInWeek.ts`, every source fail-soft
(a failed source is named, the rest still draws):

- payments since three weeks before last Monday, with their deposit link and the day that
  deposit posted;
- the Accounts Receivable window's own reads: `list_unlinked_payments_for_bank_payments` (the
  drawer), `list_mercury_transactions_for_bank_payments` with the org's deposit filter from
  `app_settings` (posted deposits not yet applied), `list_ar_return_cases` (open cases, worded by
  `arReturnCaseView`);
- pending deposits straight from `mercury_transactions` (RLS already admits the bar's roles), the
  same deposit filter applied in code;
- the Collect pile (pay speeds, promises, GC reads) built with `buildCollectPile`, open claims,
  hosted Stripe bills, the payout.

**Kernel rules** (`src/lib/moneyInWeek.ts`, 27 tests, one of them the mock-up's whole week to the
dollar: Came in $16,040 · 2, Still expected $60,830 · 10, Last week still out $18,430 · 3):

- Landed = a Stripe payment on its paid day; a payment matched at Accounts Receivable on the day
  its deposit posted; a posted deposit not applied yet; a non-check payment typed in by hand.
  A check typed in but not seen at the bank is in the drawer only (v2.5926 counted it twice).
- A deposit meets a typed-in payment of the same amount first (the drawer check reaching the
  bank, named by its payer and check number), else the one open bill it can be (that bill draws
  no second chip). Two same-sized bills with no name match stay unmatched.
- Mercury deposits from Stripe and our own transfers are never customer money — the payout chip
  carries Stripe (v2.5926 would have counted a pending payout as a bank chip).
- Payments still carrying a returned check never land; this week's returns draw red and
  uncounted; last week's carry.
- The drawer holds checks for `AR_UNBANKED_CHECK_DAYS` (10), Accounts Receivable's own line;
  older ones are its "never deposited" cases, counted on the shelf only.
- A pending deposit posts two bank days out (Friday's posts Tuesday). Last week's carried rows
  are open bills whose expected day fell last week; the old expected/landed sums are gone.
- A promise outranks the Stripe tag on the same bill.
