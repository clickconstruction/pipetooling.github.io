# v2.6493 — The Development page’s Money look draws the site’s cost timeline (2026-11-04)

- **The view** (`DevelopmentTimeline.tsx`, the third chip on the Money card's head, `canSeeSiteCosts` only; `MoneyView` gains `timeline` and a device that remembered it for another role opens on the money): the shared `JobChargesTimelineChartView`, the Job Summary's own chart, over the site: **red** cost to date, every job's charge events merged in date order with the source icons on the steps, and **green** the cash position, payments received minus cost, rising only on a payment. The amber overhead band is PR 2; value created on the right axis and Print are PR 3 (the view passes `valueHiddenNote: null`, a new prop, so the key says nothing about a value line it does not have yet; `compact` shortens the plot on a phone).
- **The kernel** (`src/lib/developments/siteTimeline.ts`, pure): `JobChargeEvent`, `JobPaymentEvent` and `JobValueEvent` gain an optional `jobId`, nothing the Job Summary chart draws changes; `siteChargeEventsFromCostRows` builds each job's five streams the way `loadJobChargesTimelineInputs` does (the supply invoices by allocation, the card under the one card rule with fuel as its ⛽ stream, the tally, the other charges, the sub sheets) from the very rows `useSiteCosts` totals for the Costs column and the Scoreboard, so the three say one number; `siteTeamLaborEvents` the clock's events per person per day; `foldSiteChargeEvents` one tooltip line per job and source on a day, *JP603 · 3 card charges*, biggest first, the amounts summed; `buildSiteTimeline` the merge, the chips (the jobs with a dated event, in the Money look's order with the device's sort), the selection, the count of jobs left out, and `thinChargeIcons`: a site's chart holds hundreds of days, so the icons stay on the forty biggest steps and the scope line says so.
- **The reads**, every one batched over the site's jobs and paged, never one round per job: `useSiteCosts` now keeps the rows it read (`rows` on the read, `reason` on a failure) and selects the dates a sheet and an other charge carry and the bank's category on a card line; `siteLabor.ts` keeps each person's hours and dollars by day (`byWorkDate`), so `useSiteLabor` serves the Timeline as it serves the Scoreboard; `useSiteTimeline` (new) reads the tags and the accounting labels once for the site's card charges for the fuel split, fail-soft (the card line keeps the fuel and the key says so); the page's payment read gains `payment_type` and `note` for the payment line. All start only while the view is open.
- **Parity by construction**: `siteTimeline.test.ts` proves the site's end expense and end payments equal the sum of each job's own `buildJobChargesTimelineChartData` ends, and that the events built from the cost rows sum to `jobCostsFromRows`' totals, the Costs column's figures.
