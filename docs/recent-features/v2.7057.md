# v2.7057 — Bills sent: say how a bill went when the app did not send it (punch list #327) (2026-11-12)

Punch list #327, PR 6 (the by-hand half; the send functions' own holes stay open, below). A bill handed over on site, mailed, sent from a person's own mailbox or from Housecall Pro leaves no record, so it sits in the strip *No send on record* however it really went. This lets the person who knows say so.

## What changed

- **The kernel** (`src/lib/billing/billsSent.ts`, 39 tests in all):
  - `byHandProblem(draft, todayYmd)`: what the form still needs, one sentence at a time (how, who, the day, a day not after today).
  - `byHandFiling(gap, draft)`: the sent copy to file. Kind `bill_by_hand`, the bill as its source, the job in `job_ids`, the person's words for who it went to in `recipient_name`, and `source_snapshot` `{ by_hand, how, went_on, note }`. **No address is filed**: an address in `recipient_emails` would read into a customer's Messages thread as an email the app sent (`sentLines`), and this is a person's word, not an email of ours. `how` is `hand`, `mail` or `email`, the words Sent copies already has.
  - `billsSentLines` reads such a copy as a line of its own shape: on the day the person named (noon, shown as *Day only*), route `by_hand`, the chosen way as its words (*Handed over*), the recorder as *by*, and the note *Recorded by hand on Thu, Nov 12. The app sent nothing and kept no copy.* with what they typed. It counts as a send of its bill, so the bill is no longer a gap, adds to that day's total, and a Housecall Pro stamp on the same bill is not drawn beside it.
  - The chip **Recorded by hand**.
- **The write** (`billsSentIo.ts` `recordBillByHand`): one insert into `sent_documents` through its own INSERT policy and stamp trigger (the database writes who and when). No copy body. It throws its reason, so the form shows it (`formatErrorMessage`): a training-mode account is refused by the table's fences and told so.
- **The screen**: **Say how it went…** on each row of the strip opens a small form in the row: *How the bill went*, *Who it went to* (the customer's name offered), *The day it went* (today offered, never after today), *A note, if you want one*, then **Record how it went** and **Cancel**. The press is dead until the form is whole and the line beside it says what is missing. On save the list reads again.
- **The guide** gains *Say how a bill went*.

## Not changed

- No table, policy or function. `sent_documents` is append-only, so a record made by hand cannot be edited or removed by the office; the form says so before the press.
- **Still open at the send itself**, left for a session of its own because `_shared/sentCopyEmail.ts` rides in every email function's bundle: a row with the address when Stripe emails a bill for us, the bill's number and amount filed with its copy, and the bills a statement listed filed with the statement.
