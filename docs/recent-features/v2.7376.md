# v2.7376 — The crew's job window on a phone: Today's work (2026-11-15)

Punch list #355, direction C, the crew half, second step ([`to-dos/job-window-phone.md`](../../to-dos/job-window-phone.md), *Direction C, shown and split*; the drawing is [`to-dos/job-window-phone-c.html`](../../to-dos/job-window-phone-c.html), pair 2). Client only. It stands on v2.7375's read rule, which opens the job's row to its crew.

## Before

v2.7355 put the crew's window in B's order: Where · Photos · Who · What's happening · More on this job. The work itself was still scattered. The stage checklists sat under the photos with a label of their own. Plans was the second fold at the foot, with nothing on its line to say what was in it. The window had no word on which stage the job is on, though the crew reports it on every Leave report. And materials on site, the crew's own screen for the boxes, had no door from the job at all: the only press was on the Job Mode card.

## What changed

- **[`src/components/jobs/CrewTodaysWork.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/CrewTodaysWork.tsx)** (new): the section, fourth on the crew's phone, after Who.
  - **The open stage** as a card: the stage's name, the job's percent by its stages at the right, a line that says where each stage stands (*Rough In done · Top Out 50% · Trim Set not started*; past four stages, *3 of 8 done · Trim Set open*) and a bar. The open stage is the one the report form opens on by itself (`defaultOpenLine`): the first stage in order not yet done. A job with fewer than two stages draws no card: its one line is the bid's price, not a word the crew uses. When a Leave report lands the card reads the stages again.
  - **The stage checklists**, the same strip and the same sheets, without the strip's own label. On the crew's phone they are no longer drawn under the photos.
  - **Plans** as a fold whose line names the current set: *Permit set · came Oct 20*, or *none yet* for a part with no current set. A job with one pasted link and no part says nothing there and keeps its **Open plans** row inside.
  - **Materials on site** as a fold whose line counts what the site holds: *3 containers · 14 lines*, or *nothing yet*. Inside, the chips the tabbed window's Materials tab draws, without the money and the credits, and **Open Materials on site**, which closes the window and opens the Tally's On site tab on this job.
- **[`src/lib/jobs/jobWindowCrewPhone.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobWindowCrewPhone.ts)**: `crewStageCard`, `crewMaterialsWords`, the fold key `materials`, `CREW_WORK_FOLDS`; `crewFolds` (More on this job) no longer lists Plans. One storage key remembers the open folds of both sections.
- **[`src/hooks/useJobStageRows.ts`](../../src/hooks/useJobStageRows.ts)** (new): the stages as plain rows from `list_job_stage_progress`, the definer the report form already reads. A refused read draws no card.
- **[`src/lib/plans/planSets.ts`](../../src/lib/plans/planSets.ts)**: `currentSetFoldWords`. `JobPlansSection` reports them through `onCurrentWords` once a part is read.
- **[`src/components/jobs/JobWindowMaterialsPanel.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobWindowMaterialsPanel.tsx)**: `door="tally"` for the crew's window. The tabbed window's tab is unchanged.
- **[`src/components/stageChecklists/JobStageChecklistStrip.tsx`](../../src/components/stageChecklists/JobStageChecklistStrip.tsx)**: `label={false}` under a section that already names it.
- **[`src/components/jobs/jobWindowPressPad.ts`](../../src/components/jobs/jobWindowPressPad.ts)**: the pad's watcher also hears a fold open (`data-open`). A press inside a fold stays mounted while the fold is shut and measures as nothing, so it got no pad when the fold opened: *Try again* under Plans was 20 px. The tabbed window's folds get the same fix.
- The guide *clock in and out with Job Mode* → *The job window on a phone* names the section.

## What is different from the drawing

- **Every stage's checklist is a press, not the open stage's alone.** The drawing showed one row. A stage that is done can still have an unanswered check, and the strip already says each one's standing.
- **Materials on site says containers and lines, not cages, boxes and shorts.** The site's summary counts containers as one kind, and a short is a request, not a count the summary holds. The missing-this-week chip is inside the fold.
- **A job with one line has no stage card.** Most service calls are one line.
- **The sub's *Your work* is not here.** A sub sees the same section. Their work order and price wait for Taunya's two answers on the card.

## What a person loses

Nothing. Plans and the checklists moved up; the foot's folds are Notes and reports, Job accounts and Parts cost as before. The desk and the tabbed window are untouched.

## Is this the best we can do?

The section reads two things the window did not read before, the stages and the site's ledger, on every open. Both are small and the second is the store the Tally already keeps, so a crew that came from the Tally pays nothing. What it does not do is let the crew move a stage from the card: the number moves on Leave report, which is one press away on the bar, and a second way to say it would be a second place for it to go wrong.

- On a one-line job the section is the two folds.
