# v2.3376 — Share this bill, PR 2: the office doors — the Bill Customer tick, the Bill-tab eye chip, the Edit-tab memory row, the globe mirror, the guides (2026-09-13)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Second of the train in [`to-dos/gc-portal-customers-bills/`](../../to-dos/gc-portal-customers-bills/README.md) (PR 1 v2.3375 shipped the stamp, the kernel, the function and the portal card).

## What shipped

- **Bill Customer** ([`SendRecordInvoiceModal`](../../src/components/jobs/SendRecordInvoiceModal.tsx)): the *Send to* block gains **Show it on <other party>'s statement** directly under *Copy <other party>*, with the sub-line *Their portal lists this bill as billed to <payer> — no Pay button, not in their balance* and, when the tick differs from the job's memory, *This job's next bills will start ticked / unticked*. The other party is resolved by role (`otherPartyOf`), so the tick is offered even when they have no email; never on a typed someone-else bill, a split draft's own pick decides its payer first. The tick starts from `defaultShowOtherParty(job, role)`. **The stamp**: `ensurePrimaryRowForCommit` now wraps the ensure step and writes `jobs_ledger_invoices.shown_to_party` (the party, or NULL) on the row every channel bills against — Stripe send, Copy link, PDF, Record outside bill — before the send; when the tick differs from the job's memory it writes `jobs_ledger.show_bills_to_other_party` back too. A refused write is a toast, never a blocked send. The existing render smoke still sees exactly one invoice write on the HouseCall Pro path (a single-party job offers no tick).
- **Edit Job → Bill** ([`JobFormInvoiceList`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormInvoiceList.tsx)): every bill row on a two-party job — draft or sent — wears an eye chip beside its payer chip: `👁 shown to Done Right Foundation` (green) or `👁 ▾` (muted). Its menu, *Who else sees this bill*: the non-paying party (both parties on a typed someone-else bill), and *Only the payer* to hide it. `pickShownTo` writes the stamp, refetches, toasts. Render test.
- **Edit Job → Edit tab** ([`JobFormEditFactRows`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormEditFactRows.tsx)): a **Show <other party>** fact row under *Bills also go to* — collapsed *the bills they don't pay · on new bills* or *not shared*; opened, one checkbox writing `jobs_ledger.show_bills_to_other_party` **directly** (optimistic, toast on refusal). Deliberately not in the identity autosave slice, so a stale open form can never revert a tick made in Bill Customer. Hidden on a split job, a one-party job, or a job not saved yet.
- **Globe modal mirror** ([`CustomerPortalGlobeButton`](../../src/components/customers/CustomerPortalGlobeButton.tsx), [`portalStatementJobLinks.ts`](../../src/lib/portal/portalStatementJobLinks.ts)): *Jobs on this statement* appends the shared card's rows after the ledger's with a green **shared** tag (+1 test).
- **Kernel** [`billVisibility.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/billVisibility.ts): `shownToChipText`. [`gcPortalLink.ts`](../../src/lib/portal/gcPortalLink.ts)'s stale "can never show them anything but the bills they owe" comment corrected.
- **Docs**: guides *choose who gets the bill on a job* (new section *Show the other party a bill they don't pay*), *send a bill to more than one person* (Copy vs Show), *share a customer their portal* (the shared card, the mirror's tag); `GLOSSARY.md` → **Share this bill**.
