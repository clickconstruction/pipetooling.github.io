# v2.2803 — Contract Forms PR 8: the office flow — queue, shading, attestation, hand-off (2026-09-05)

Follows PR 7's two-party forms with the owner-picked mockups (artifact `5dd3904e`): the office is told about pending sections, the signer sees the office half shaded, the office attests before completing, and day-one hand-off works.

- **Office sections to complete** — a strip at the top of People → Contracts lists every signed two-party form the office has not finished (`officeQueue`, oldest signature first), with **Complete** opening the office modal directly; the I-9 line carries "Section 2 is due within 3 business days of the first day of work". Pending office sections count a person into **Needs attention** (`contractsRosterBucket(counts, officePending)`), and the document row shows an amber **office section pending** chip. The Person Desk paperwork line ends in `· office section pending`.
- **Signing page** — the office's regions are hatched and labelled ("This part is completed by the office" / "Esta parte la completa la oficina"; `partyRegions(schema, 'office')`, one padded band per page), so the signer is never puzzled by Section 2's blank boxes. The lens already skipped them.
- **Office modal** — a context line (who signed the signer's half and when, or "keyed from paper"; the I-9 deadline), the signer's half shaded as **Signed by the employee · locked** (`signerRegions` from `prepare`), a "What you examined" card that explains List A vs B + C and counts required boxes, and an **attestation checkbox** (`OFFICE_ATTESTATION`, under penalty of perjury) that gates the complete button. Stacks under 900 px. The function requires `attested: true` (400 `not_attested`) and stamps `office_attested_at`; the record line reads "… · attested".
- **Record modal** — the PDF door reads **Open the PDF so far** while the office section is pending and **Open the finished PDF** after.
- **Day-one hand-off** — `accept-contract` returns `documentId` + `officeSectionPending`; when a staff member is signed in on the same device, the thank-you page offers **Complete the office section**, deep-linking to `/people?tab=contracts&doc=<id>`, which the Contracts tab opens as the signed record.
- **Kernel** `formParties.ts` (shared with Deno; + tests): `partyRegions`, `officeSectionPending`, `officeQueue`, `twoPartyTemplateIdSet`, `OFFICE_ATTESTATION`.
- **Migration** `20260905020000_contract_form_office_attested.sql`: `person_contract_documents.office_attested_at`.
