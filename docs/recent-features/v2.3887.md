# v2.3887 — Subs on a phone: Pay as rows, the sheet, the bulk door (2026-09-27)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Punch list **#30**, PR 5b (`to-dos/taunya-mobile.md`; the mock-up `to-dos/taunya-mobile-pr5-rows.html`). The owner's calls (2026-09-27): the ledger table is not drawn on a phone; *Get all N in writing* shows the list before anything is sent. Builds on [v2.3885](v2.3885.md) for the bottom sheet.

## What the code had

The Pay view's *Who's owed* was already one row per sub from a tested kernel (`buildSubLaborPayRun`), but drawn on a four-column grid 500 px wide at its narrowest; the ledger under it was an eight-column table at `minWidth: 980` with no phone branch (the audit wrote nine columns and an Open column — there are eight and no Open). Both views switch on `useIsNarrowScreen` (768 px).

## What shipped

- **`src/lib/subWorkOrders/subPayPhoneRows.ts`** (new, pure) — `subPayPhoneAmount` (the row's one number: *$4,200 due* · *Paid* · *Over $120* · *unpriced*) and `subPayPhoneVerbs` (the sheet's verbs in order, the first the row's next move: the agreement when the pay-when is a gap and the desk row would draw its button, the payment when the sheet is ready; the payable-after date wherever the desk row offers it).
- **`JobsSubLaborTab.tsx`**, under 768 px:
  - *Who's owed* stacks — name and amount, the bar and its reason under them, the action under that.
  - The ledger is a list: a sub's header row (name, sheets, the reason, the amount) opens to its sheets; a lone group and the group *Who's owed* jumped to open by themselves. A sheet row is its number and job, the pay-when chip with its detail, and the amount.
  - A sheet row opens `ScheduleSheet` with the verbs; each calls the handler the desk row's button or ⋯ menu calls. *Set a payable-after date* turns the sheet into the date, Queue and Clear.
  - The table, its expand row and its ⋯ menu are not drawn. The line items and the invoice link of the expand row are reached through *Edit the sheet*.
- **`JobsSubsWorkView.tsx`**, under 768 px — above the cards, when more than one sheet is on a handshake: *N sheets on a handshake* and **Get all N in writing**, which opens the *On a handshake* tile's window — the queue with its own *Send the rest as drafted* and confirm. Nothing is sent from the board.
- **The guide** *sub-labor-outstanding* → *On a phone*.
