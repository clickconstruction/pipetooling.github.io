# v2.6735 — The Development page opens lighter: History reads a long line’s first words (2026-11-08)

Punch list #280, PR 1. The Gray Fox spike (2026-11-02) left the History look's entries read as it was: `select('*')` over `development_history_entries` for the site, up to 500 rows. Measured on 2026-11-08 on the dev server as the dev, Gray Fox's open made 76 to 105 Supabase calls over three client-side opens and moved 1.7 to 1.9 MB (StrictMode's double effects included); the entries read was the second biggest call at 299,111 bytes, twice per open, 281 to 515 ms, co-slowest in the fan-out with the job notes. Of its 218 rows, 200,917 characters were body and 89,872 bytes the other columns; 132 lines were longer than the 260-character fold, and the page shows those first words alone until *more ›* is pressed. The whole bodies were weight on every open that almost no open reads.

- **The heads view** (`20261108234500_development_history_heads.sql`): `development_history_heads`, `security_invoker`, every column of the entries table but the body, with `body_head` (`left(body, 400)`) and `body_chars` (`char_length(body)`). `SELECT` to `authenticated`; the table's policies decide what a reader sees. The table and MCP's `get_history` are untouched.
- **The read** (`fetchDevelopmentPage.ts`, `readHistoryHeads`): the view through `HISTORY_HEAD_COLUMNS`; when PostgREST answers that the relation is not there (`PGRST205` / `42P01`, the window between the deploy and the push) the read falls back to the table whole, so the page stands either side. `readHistoryBody(entryId)` reads one body for the window; `readHistoryBodies(developmentId)` reads every body once for a search. Both through `withSupabaseRetry`.
- **The kernel** (`history.ts`): `HistoryHeadRow`, `headRowToEntry` (the entry's `body` is the head, `bodyChars` the whole's length), `entryBodyIsHead`, `withBodies` (the whole swapped in where read), `HISTORY_HEAD_CHARS = 400` and `HISTORY_FOLD_AT = 260` (the feed's fold constant, moved here so a test holds the head past the fold). `entryLine` carries `entryId` and `detailIsHead` on the `ChangeLine` (two new optional fields in `whatChanged.ts`).
- **The window** (`DevelopmentLineWindow.tsx`): a `readWhole` prop; when the line's detail is a head it reads the whole on open, shows the head with *Reading the rest…* until it lands, the whole after, and *The rest did not come: <reason>* under the head when the read fails (`formatErrorMessage`). A line read whole asks for nothing.
- **The page** (`DevelopmentPage.tsx`): the entries go through `withBodies`; typing in the search reads every body once (`readHistoryBodies`) while any entry is a head, a failure toasts its reason and the search reads the first words until the box is cleared and typed in again; a read holds across the page's reloads, since a kept line never changes.

Tests: `history.test.ts` (a heads row to an entry, a short line's head is the whole, the head past the fold, `withBodies` swapping only what was read, the migration cutting at `HISTORY_HEAD_CHARS` and naming every column the read asks for, `entryLine`'s id and head flag), `DevelopmentLineWindow.render.test.tsx` (new: the read by id with the head shown while it reads, the whole after, the reason on a failure, no read for a whole line); the three page smokes' mock of the fetch module gained the two reads.

Deploy: the client calls the view, so `check:calls-ahead` labels the PR *push right after the merge* and the deploy waits for `supabase db push`; the fallback keeps a client that deploys first on the table. After the push: `npm run gen-types:linked` as its own `chore(types)` PR. The after measurement goes on the card (`to-dos/development-page-reads.md`) once the view is on prod. Not in this PR: the pay-speed read by one customer (PR 2).
