# v2.6087 — Stage checklists: the Pipeline reads not ready (punch list #164, PR 4) (2026-10-29)

Punch list #164, PR 4 of five. The strict rule reaches the Pipeline: a stage whose checklist has a question left reads *not ready* under the bar.

## What changed

- **The mark** (`StagesStageBar.tsx`): in the names row under a stage job's bar, a stage left reads in amber with `☐` before its name, and its title says *Stage 1 · Rough In · the crew is on it · checklist 2 of 3 answered · not ready*; a filled checklist adds *checklist filled* to the title and nothing to the name. `data-stage-checklist` carries the standing for tests. The cell (`StagesProgressPaymentCell.tsx`) passes the map through; the rows (`StagesUnifiedJobRow`, `StagesUnifiedInvoiceRow`, `JobsStagesTable`, `JobsStagesCardList`) read it from the shared context's `checklistMarksFor(job)`.
- **The read** (`useStageChecklistStates`): one read of the live questions and, only when any exist, one chunked read of the answers on the board's jobs (`fixture_id, question_id, pick, photo_file_name`), fail-soft like the crew feed. `stageChecklistMarks` (`lib/jobs/stageChecklistMarks.ts`, tested) turns a row's line items into each stage's standing by fixture id, null while no question exists anywhere, so the board costs nothing and draws nothing until the office types the questions.
- The guide *read the Pipeline's money view* says what the mark means.

## Decisions

- **The mark is on the name, not the bar.** The bar's fill is money and its tick is work (v2.4351); the checklist is evidence, so it rides the name like the crew's bold does, in amber, with the words a first-timer reads in the title.
- **No question, no read.** The answers are read only once a question exists, so a company that never types one pays nothing on every board load.
