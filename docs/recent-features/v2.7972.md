# v2.7972 — Send Email invoice: the window never names the customer on a bill that went to the GC (2026-11-19)

Client only: no migration, no edge function. Found on 2026-11-19 beside v2.7959, the same fault on the Stripe side.

## Before

`send-stripe-invoice` sends a Stripe bill to the email Stripe holds for the invoice's own customer (`customerEmailFromStripeInvoice`). The send was always right.

The window that asks first (`StripeInvoiceSendFromStripeButton`) is handed an address by its host. Three hosts handed it `job.customer_email`: the Who owes what card (*Never got it? Resend*), the Stages table's Last activity line and the Stages cards. On a bill that went to the GC the window read *The bill email goes to* the job's customer's address, or, on a GC job with no customer, *the customer (no email on file in the app preview)*.

On prod on 2026-11-19, 35 unpaid Stripe bills were on jobs that bill their GC: 22 with their own customer, 13 on GC jobs with none.

## What changed

- `stripeBillEmailHint(job, inv)` in the new `src/lib/jobs/stripeBillEmailHint.ts`: the address a host may hand over, by the shared rule (`effectiveInvoiceParty`). The typed recipient's on a bill to someone else, the job's customer's on a bill to the customer, and none on a bill to the GC, whose address the job does not carry.
- `stripeBillAddressWords(payer)`: the words that stand in for a missing address. *the GC, at the address on the Stripe invoice* for a GC, *the address on the Stripe invoice* otherwise. They are true whatever the app holds, since the send reads Stripe's own. The old *no email on file in the app preview* line is gone.
- `StripeInvoiceSendFromStripeButton` takes `payer` and uses those words when `customerEmail` is empty.
- The three hosts pass the hint. `BilledByCustomerBreakdownModal` reads it off the row (`billedTo`, v2.7959). `StagesJobCellActivityFooter` and `JobsStagesCardList` call the kernel.

## Not changed

`HostedStripeBillPanel` already names Stripe's own address once it has read the invoice. `SendRecordInvoiceModal` hands over the payer it resolved. `PaymentChaseModal` hands over the contact of the customer being chased, not looked at here. The Stages hint's hover text still reads *emailed to the customer*.

## Tests
