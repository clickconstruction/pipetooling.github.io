## What shipped

- **`submit-submittal-review`** (POST, no JWT — the token is the credential): `identify` (room or personal token + name · email · role, a honeypot field, a gentle 20-per-hour-per-address cap) attaches to a pre-named row by email, recognises the same person on their own link, else inserts a person (`how = identified`, or `forwarded` when they arrived on someone else's personal link), mints or returns the personal token, writes an `identified` event; `decide` (personal token + revision + decisions) refuses a closed room or link (410), a person marked watching (403 `watching`), a revision that is not the newest shared one (409 `stale_revision`), rows not on that revision (404), and otherwise writes `review_decision · review_note · reviewed_by_name · reviewed_by_email · reviewed_by_person_id · reviewed_at` and a `decided` event with the counts. The rules are pure in [`_shared/submittalReviewActions.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/submittalReviewActions.ts) (`parseIdentifyBody`, `parseDecideBody`, `resolveIdentify`, `decideVerdict`), twin-tested from the app.
- **The room** ([`SubmittalRoom.tsx`](../../src/pages/SubmittalRoom.tsx)): the identify sheet (name · email · *I am* architect · owner's rep · designer · builder / GC · other; *Just looking* clears the tapped decision) opens on the first decision when nobody is signed in; *That's me* posts `identify`, remembers the person on the device (`localStorage` per room token) and rewrites the address to the personal link (`history.replaceState`); decisions are local until **Send my review** posts them all at once with optional notes per row; the page folds the answer in (*Approved · Tom Reyes · Sep 17* on the row, the counts, *2 recorded as Tom Reyes. Thank you.*); **Approve all N as marked**; *Reviewing as … · not you?* re-opens the sheet with the held token as `viaToken`; a personal link that says `mayDecide: false` reads *Watching only* and cannot send; the function's refusals read on the page.
- **The tab** ([`BidsSubmittalsTab`](../../src/components/bids/BidsSubmittalsTab.tsx)): a **Their call** column (the decision, who, when, the note) whenever any row was decided; the line *Their call: 19 approved · 2 revise · 1 rejected · by Dana W. · 3 still open* with **Copy their decisions as text** ([`reviewDecisions.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/reviewDecisions.ts): `summarizeDecisions`, `describeDecisions`, `decisionsAsText`, `itemsSentBack`); **Rev N+1 from the K rows sent back** builds a draft carrying only the rows marked Revise or Reject (the plain New revision stays for everything).
