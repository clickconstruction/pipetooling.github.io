# v2.5115 — GC mode: closing out a trade and the job, in the database (2026-10-09)

The Building lane's U6c: migration `20261010041000_gc_trade_closeout`, pushed after merge. No client change, no edge function. The plan is `to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/building-u6.md` → *U6c: closeout's presses* (amendments 2 and 3) on branch `spike/gc-mode`; the migration and its scenario are the plan's blocks byte for byte, with only the stamps and the version filled in. The doc is `docs/migrations/20261010041000_gc_trade_closeout.md`.

## What changed

- **`gc_projects.closed_on`**, set only on a closed job (`gc_projects_closed_on_when_closed`), and three columns on `gc_change_order_trade_sends` for a signature the office records (`recorded_by`, `file_name`, `drive_url`).
- **Three read-only helpers**: `gc_retainage_held` (`retainageHeldNow`), `gc_sow_all_billed` (`workAllBilled`) and `gc_owner_retainage_paid_on` (`billMoney`'s paid day for our final pay application's bill, kept equal with Owner Billing).
- **The presses**, each SECURITY INVOKER:
  - `gc_accept_work`: every line billed and no punch item without its check;
  - the final pay application, both ways in through one `gc_final_pay_app_ask`: the trade's `gc_trade_final_pay_app` (service role only) and the office's `gc_final_pay_app_came_in`. Its money is the retainage held, so a back-charge taken off a draw is never paid back. With no waiver owed it keeps the closeout promise;
  - `gc_approve_retainage`: 10 days after the customer pays us ours;
  - `gc_trade_change_signed_in`: a change the trade signed on paper or by email, the office's twin of `gc_trade_sign_change`;
  - `gc_close_job`: the stage closed and `closed_on` today, a dev's while Building is built.
- **Tests**: `supabase/tests/gc_building/70_closeout.sql` in the `gc-building` bed (its runner re-applies the migration a second time), and `finalSent` and `finalNotYet` in `gcTradeSubmit.test.ts`'s WAITING for the Portal's P5.
