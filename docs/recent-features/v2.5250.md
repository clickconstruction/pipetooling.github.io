# v2.5250 — Messages: the reply box’s words and the quick replies, behind the scenes (2026-10-12)

The Messages build's cut 3 kernels, wave 1 (`to-dos/gc-mode/messages/BUILD_PLAN.md` → Cut 3 and `BUILD_HELPERS.md`, on branch `spike/gc-mode`), lifted from the prototype v1.178 by helper 3, directed by the GC Review lead. Two pure kernels with their tests over the prototype's made-up people. No screen, no table, no edge function, nothing to deploy.

- **`src/lib/messages/compose.ts`**: the reply box. The kinds row (Text, Email, Note, each with its Send), the start kind, the from line, the email's subject, greeting, signature and footer (`emailDraft` assembles them so the send window and the send never differ), the opt-out line's two rules (`optoutReason`: our first text to the number, or the first after 30 days since our last text, `daysSinceOurLastText`), the hold on the silence reason only (`optoutRecord`: first, silence or held, for `messages.optout_line`), the text as it goes (`textToSend`: one text, the line after a blank line, counted in the pieces), the send window's warnings, the count line by kind (v1.149) and the carriers' piece count (`textSegments`).
- **`src/lib/quickReplies/kernel.ts`**: the strip's groups (`qrGroups`: the step they are on, the rest of their kind, anyone's; mine first, then the team's), its head count, the narrow box past ten lines, the lit words, Find a reply's groups and its where-they-are line, `stepLine` (the most used line on a step), `qrGuess`, the editor's tabs, rows and groups (by who, then the flow's steps), and the versions as pure changes (add, edit with a words version and a tags version, remove, restore, a press).
- **Inputs, not imports**: the greeting name, the company (its number, mailbox and letterhead follow the job), the sender's title, the flow's steps and the step a person is on, and the link lines all come from the caller, so nothing waits on the spine's or the flow's kernels.
- **Changed from the prototype**, each the lead's call or a fix: a text's pieces follow the GSM and UCS-2 rules, so an á or a phone's curly apostrophe counts 70 a text, where the prototype counted 160; the opt-out line goes in the same text and the bubble's hover says it is drawn apart; opt-out is per number; an unknown number's lines for anyone show once, not twice; the editor shows only the team's lines and the person's own; a subject cuts at a whole word; a step waiting on someone names them in Find a reply; "draw" and "contract" are whole words in the guess; the sent copy's note is ours, apart from their footer; a tags version keeps the tags, not their words; a reply to a number that sent STOP starts as an email; and the sentences that broke the plain-words rules are two sentences now.
- **Waits on**: the spine carrying the `contact_channels` id on a message and `held` in `optout_line` (helper 1); `users.title` in cut 3's SQL (wave 2); `quo-send` sending the line in the one text (cut 5); the flow's step type replacing `StepRef` (helper 4); the link lines from `documents/outgoing.ts` (helper 9).
