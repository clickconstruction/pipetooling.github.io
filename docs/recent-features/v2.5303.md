# v2.5303 — Messages: the customer's page on a GC job (2026-10-12)

Cut 8b-i of the Messages build, by GC Reviews helper 8, directed by GC Review: the customer's page, shape B (the prototype's v1.162 to v1.165, `customer-portal-mockups.html` and `money-tab-mockups.html` on `spike/gc-mode`), on each GC job in their portal. It reads the columns of #5436 (v2.5263) and #5455 (v2.5286) through `customer-portal`. The function is deployed after both are on prod; until then a job reads as before. Its own migration, `20261013060000_customer_page_words_screens`, seeds the page's 20 new sentences into `customer_page_words`. Until it is pushed, those sentences read from the code and Settings cannot edit them. Dev only in the notes.

- **The edge** (`supabase/functions/_shared/gcPortalPage.ts`, `customer-portal`): each GC job gains `page`. It carries:
  - our contract's sum before the changes, the change orders and the pay applications;
  - the architect's record name and the days to pay;
  - the newest daily log customer line, with its photos link only while the job's photos switch is on;
  - the project manager and the superintendent;
  - the schedule's moves since the first letter, by reason and never the note;
  - the three switches.

  The payload gains `pageWords`, the office's edits. `?paper=<kind>:<id>` writes one `portal_paper` view for an outside open of a paper on one of the customer's jobs and answers at once. A failed read leaves the page as it was.
- **The page model** (`src/lib/portal/customerPage.ts`): `parsePortalGcPage` and `customerPageModel`.
  - It builds Money from the pay applications and the statement's own bills: paid once the bill is paid in full, due on the certificate plus the days to pay, else with the architect.
  - It builds Papers with lien waivers counted from the statement's waivers, This week, Your people (the office line first), and What moved, each move's reason in their words.
- **The page** (`src/components/portal/PortalGcPage.tsx`): Home, Money, Schedule and Papers, drawn from the model with `wordsWith(pageWords)`. Español shows only while both the job's switch and `PORTAL_SPANISH_ON` are on.
  - The sign, decline and accept forms moved unchanged into `PortalGcForms.tsx`, which both the old view and the page draw.
  - Reading a pay application, opening the contract or answering a change order sends the paper view.
  - A job without `page` reads as before.

**Interim, which 8b-ii replaces:**
- *Your move* reads the job's own facts: our contract waiting on their signature, the change orders sent, and the work to accept. They go through `customerMove` as two steps laid out like the flow's (`interimSteps`).
- 8b-ii replaces those steps with the customer's flow read by `readFlow` (`customerJobSteps`), once the spine (#5431), cut 4's columns (#5435) and the loader (#5448) are on prod and main. It also adds *Where you are*, held until then.
