# v2.4807 — Map: the justice precincts as a layer, and the precinct on a place (2026-10-07)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. PR 5 of 5 of the Map page refresh, after [v2.4805](v2.4805.md); the train is done and its to-do folder retired with this PR (the plan and mock-up live in git history at the deleting commit; the closure line is in `to-dos/README.md`).

## What changed

- **A Precincts chip** at the end of the chips row (`MAP_PAGE_PRECINCT_COLOR`, the layer's first county tint), for every viewer of the page — `court_areas` is readable by every signed-in user. On, it loads the areas and mounts `CourtAreasLayer` without the drawing mode; the count appears once the areas are loaded. The Court areas mode still shows the layer as before; the layer no longer lingers after the mode is turned off.
- **The place card names the justice court.** With the layer on screen, `PlaceCard` (the rail and the popup) reads *Justice court: Comal · JP Pct 4*, or *on the line: 2 or 3* for an address within 100 m of a line, or *outside every drawn area* — `classifyCourtPoint` + `courtClassificationWords`, the same kernel the nightly and the Court areas panel use, over the place's point.
- `SectionChip`'s count is optional, so a chip can wait for its number.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **The layer the office drew is for everyone**, not only for the hand that drew it; the drawing mode stays the office's door.
- **The answer sits where the question is asked.** Someone looking at a place on the map wants the court as much as the miles; the card says both.
- **Not done:** the precinct on the Legal portal and the property record is v2.4771's, from the nightly; this card reads the layer live and may differ for a record typed by hand, which the record itself shows.

## Tests
