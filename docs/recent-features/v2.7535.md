# v2.7535 — Lien desk: retainage nobody recorded shows on To do and the Board (2026-11-16)

Fix A of the retainage clock check, second piece. v2.7509 listed the jobs whose bills or notes show retainage held with none recorded, at the top of By paper → Retainage notices. That is one tab, and the check's own finding was that a fact told on one tab is told to nobody. The owner's word on the next piece: *"Yes, build it"*.

## What changed

- **One read, on the desk's data** (`useLienDeskData.ts`): `loadRetainageUnrecorded` joins the desk's first reads and lands as `retainageUnrecorded`, with `retainageUnrecordedFailure` when the read is refused. Not read in light mode, so the Dashboard and the Pipeline's lien line make no new request. ZZ test jobs leave it with the desk's switch.
- **To do** (`lienNextUp.ts`): a row per job, key `retainage-unrecorded:<job>`, kind `retainage`, action `record_retainage`, under **Needs you now** with no day of its own and an amber dot. It reads *Retainage not on the job · $2,460.00 looks held · 3 bills paid at 90%*. The reason rides in the row's own words because a retainage row has no bin on the Board, and a bin-less row prints no detail. The button, *Record the retainage*, is the office's; a master is office on this desk. Its target, `retainage_unrecorded`, opens the Retainage list whole, clears the find box, and rings the job's row above the piles.
- **The Board** (`lienBoard.ts`): `lienBoardRetainageLine` takes the count and ends the line with *2 look held, not on the job*. The line now shows when these jobs are the only retainage on the desk.
- **The tab's count**: *Retainage notices · N* adds these jobs.
- **`LienRetainageUnrecorded.tsx`** takes its rows from the desk instead of reading them itself. A job just answered leaves the list at once and the desk's next read drops it for good.
- **The act and the chip** (`lienActWords.ts`, `lienArChip.ts`): *Record the retainage*, an act with no day to race. The AR and Pipeline chips can carry it; they do not today, since their reads are light.

## What it does not do

- *Clock not started* still speaks on the Retainage notices tab only. That is fix B.
