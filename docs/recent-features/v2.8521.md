# v2.8521 — Punch list: the questions that wait on you, with a picture and one answer (2026-11-24)

PR 2 of punch list #438, the mock-up's refined cut D. Diane's ask of 2026-11-24: make the Punch list page easy for a person to see the questions they still have to answer, and what the card looks like. Her word on the mock-up the same day: *Build D*, on the seven picks. No migration (the columns came with v2.8519), no edge function.

## What was there

A card's row is about 520 px tall at a desk and 1,111 px on a phone. What the card waits on was inside its Next line. Its pictures were one press away. The row's control was Do, Later, Drop, which sort a card that is not built.

## What changed

- **A third view, Waiting on me** (`?view=waiting`). It lists the cards that carry an `ask:`, the newest card first. The View pill reads *Waiting on me N* when a card asks the reader, and *Waiting on a person N* when none does. A reader with a question of their own lands on it until they choose another view. With no card asking anything the pill is not drawn.
- **Whose questions.** The page opens to the owner and the devs. The owner's own are `owner` and `anyone`. A dev's are `dev` and `anyone`. Pills over the list show **Me**, each other person with a question waiting, and **Everyone**. The device remembers the pill.
- **The row.** The question comes first, with one word in front for its kind: Look, Read, Walk, Decide or Use. The card's name is under it and opens the card. A small line says how many pictures and the day they were drawn, with **See all**.
- **The lead picture.** `pictures-lead.webp` beside the card is the built screen. `pictures-today.webp` with `pictures-if-built.webp` is the app today beside the card's mock-up, the second with a dashed edge. A press opens the pictures page. The build copies every `.webp` under `to-dos/` beside the pages. The fifteen cards of v2.8519 each got theirs.
- **One answer control: Go ahead, Change, Not now** (`PunchListAnswer.tsx`). It is the board's own pick: Go ahead is `do`, Not now is `later`, Change is `change`. The row keeps the question it answered in `answers_ask`. Change opens a box, *What should change?*, saved when the box is left. A pressed answer pressed again is taken back.
- **An answer belongs to its question.** A pick counts as the answer only while `answers_ask` equals the card's question. When a session writes the card's next question, the card waits again.
- **Answered cards fold** into one line at the foot, *Answered N* with each card's answer. A press opens them, where an answer can be changed.
- **The card's own row** in By group and By number says *Waits on the owner:* and the question under its Next line, and carries the same answer control in place of Do, Later, Drop.
- **Every view is remembered by name.** By group used to be remembered as nothing. A bare address can now land on Waiting on me, so a press on By group has to be kept.

## Where it differs from the mock-up

- The row does not say *Built and live*. No field of the card holds that.
- The mark *The screen changed after these were drawn* is not here. It is PR 3.
- The opened row, frame B, is not here. **See all** opens the card's pictures page, which holds the question, the card's own answers and every picture.

## The kernels

- `lib/todos/punchListWaiting.ts`: who a card waits on, what counts as an answer (`answerOf`), the rows and the counts, the pills, the words.
- `lib/todos/todoBoard.ts`: `picturesFilesFor`, `picturesPageFacts` and `picturesFor` read a card's lead and its pictures page by name. `scripts/todos/readTodos.ts` reads the page's text.
- `lib/todos/punchListView.ts`: `StoredPick` adds `change`. `pickOf` reads a Change as no sorting pick, so the Do, Later, Drop counts are as they were.
