# v2.8248 — Before we start: two presses on a narrow phone no longer sit on each other (2026-11-21)

Punch list #382, finding 8 of the audit of PR 11 (v2.8111), on Taunya's word of 2026-11-21. Client only: two rules in `src/index.css` and one class on `DevelopmentScheduleCard.tsx`. No migration, no edge function.

## The fault, measured

Over the Schedule look's cards sit two presses that are words alone, **Show it on a line of time** and **Print the days they gave**. Each is 19.5 px tall, with a 44 px hit area on a phone.

The audit had only read that they would wrap at 320 px. Measured now with the app's own stylesheet in headless Chrome:

| Width | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| 320 px | Two lines. The hit areas overlap by 20.5 px. | Two lines, no overlap |
| 360 px | Two lines. The hit areas overlap by 20.5 px. | Two lines, no overlap |
| 390 px | One line. The hit areas overlap by 4 px at their sides. | One line, no overlap |

The later press is drawn on top. So on a 320 or 360 px phone a tap on the low half of **Show it on a line of time** opened the print sheet.

## The fix

The row of presses is a class now, `.scWords`. On a phone its lines sit 26 px apart and its presses 20 px apart, so two hit areas of 44 px never meet. At desk width it is as it was, 4 px and 16 px, and has no hit area to keep apart.

## The rows of the line of time

A row's name over its job line is 31 px tall and the row had no margin. At desk width one stage's job number sat on the next stage's name. `.scLineRow` has 4 px above it now. Measured at 1280 px: 0 px of air before, 4 px after. A phone's rows keep their own 8 px.

## Checked

The measures above, on a scratch page that loads the stylesheet before and after the change, at 320, 360, 390 and 1280 px, with no sideways scroll at any of them. `DevelopmentScheduleCard.render.test.tsx` passes unchanged. Not seen in the app itself.
