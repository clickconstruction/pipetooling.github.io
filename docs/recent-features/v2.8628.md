# v2.8628 — The Bridge lights the narrowest step, the one that moves the fewest dollars a week (punch list #321, PR 2) (2026-11-26)

PR 2 of three for *The Bridge lights the narrowest step* (`to-dos/bridge-narrowest-step/README.md`). The owner's two answers of 2026-11-25 are the rule: *the scaling function* is the narrowest step, the one that moves the fewest dollars a week, and the Bridge should light it in place of the most weeks waiting. PR 1 (v2.8622) put each row's direction on the pipe and left the red where it was. This PR moves the red. **An edge function changes** (`bridge-weekly-sheet`, through the kernels copied to it), so it is redeployed after the merge on the owner's yes; until then the Monday email lights the old step and the page the new one. No migration.

## Why

With all five steps on one scale since punch list #318, the old rule could light the one step that was ahead: a big backlog of sold work in front of a field that builds faster than Sales sells is a backlog, not a jam. Market and Sales hold no pile, so the weeks rule could never light them, and when too little was sold the page could not say so in red. A company moves only as many dollars a week as its narrowest step; widening any other only makes a pile.

## What changed

- **The rule** (`buildStageFlow` in `src/lib/bridge/stageFlow.ts`). Every readable step carries `paceUsd`, the dollars a week it moves: Market's worth at the won rates (`marketWorthPerWeek`, so the dollars that came in never stand in for it), Sales's sold work, and what Build, Bill and Collect each turn. The candidates are the steps with a pace; the narrowest is lit. Kept, in pace terms: a held call stands unless a step is narrower by the hold margin (`STAGE_HOLD_MARGIN`, 15 %); a second step within a tenth of the link reads as its pair (`STAGE_PAIR_MARGIN`); a step with more than a third of its pile a guess takes the link only by the hold margin (`BUILD_GUESS_GATE`), since the same thin percents feed its pace. A step that turns nothing has no pace and is no candidate, as before. The weeks are still read, for the bars and each card's last line.
- **The words.** The lit badge reads *the narrowest step* (*, with Build* on a pair, *· set the %s* on a thin pile); the thin and held badges read the pace (*reads $20k a week on a thin % · set the %s*, *reads $22k a week · the call holds Bill*). A feeder that is not lit keeps its badge against the field. The tile reads **Narrowest step**; the card's lead *Bill is the narrowest step.*; the pipe's lit row *Bill, the narrowest step* and its screen-reader words *The narrowest step.*; the rule line *Find the narrowest step, the one that moves the fewest dollars a week. Everything waits on it.*, and the long reading names the link as the narrowest step. The Monday email's subject, tile, card and strip follow (`weekMail.ts`, `weekMailSample.ts`), as do the two sample subjects in the email catalog.
- **The dial** (`dial.ts`). The mark where the link moves sits at the next narrowest step's pace, *above here Market is the link* on the lit step and *below here Sales is the link* on any other, feeders included. The link line reads in dollars a week: *Bill is the narrowest step: $22k a week. Market is next, $29k a week.*, *Market becomes the narrowest step: $29k a week. Bill is up to $40k a week.*, *Bill stays the narrowest step, $22k a week. Sales is at $49k a week.* The old feeder line, that a feeder could only be the link once the field ran out of work, is gone with the rule that made it true.
- **What to deal with first** (`order.ts`) tiers by pace: the narrowest first, the next narrowest next, and a step with no pace to read yet waits. The desk's status reads *nominated by the page: the narrowest step, the fewest dollars a week* (`calls.ts`). The stage cards' footnote says each step reads the dollars it moves a week.
- **The copy the edge functions carry is regenerated** (`npm run gen:edge-kernels`): `stageFlow.ts`, `flowStrip.ts`, `weekCall.ts`, `weekMail.ts`, `dial.ts` and `calls.ts` in `supabase/functions/_shared/gcKernels/` (`order.ts` and `weekMailSample.ts` are not copied). One function's bundle reaches them: `bridge-weekly-sheet`.
