# v2.7901 — Attach a login with a password you set (2026-11-19)

## Why

The owner made a new helper with no login and asked for a sign-in the man could use without opening his email (2026-11-19). The Hire form has offered **Set a password now** since PR D2, but only for a person it is making. For a person who already exists, the desk's **Attach a login…** could only send the sign-in email, so the office had to attach by email and then press **Set password…** on the Sign-in row: two presses and an email nobody needed.

## What changed

- **The desk's Attach form** (`PersonDeskAccessSection`, *Access & account* → **Login** → **Attach a login…**) asks *How they sign in*, in the Hire form's own words: **Email them a link**, the default and the press it always was, or **Set a password now**, which shows **Password** and **Type it again**.
- **The press names what it does**: **Attach and send the sign-in email**, or **Attach with this password**. The line under the form and the toast say whether an email goes. With a password the toast reads *Login attached. No email went. Give … the password in person or by phone.*
- **The kernel** (`src/lib/people/attachLogin.ts`): `attachFormProblem` checks the address first and the password only when one is being set (`passwordProblem`: six characters, typed the same twice), with `attachPressLabel`, `attachHintWords` and `attachDoneWords` for the words.
- **Closing the form forgets what was typed**, the password above all: Cancel and a login that landed both clear it and put the choice back on the email.

## What did not change

- No server change. `create-user`'s attach door has taken a `password` beside `user_id` since v2.6518 (`attachDoorFields`), `attachLogin` has passed one through since then, and the dev seat's `plan_login` with `mode: password` uses the same door. Only the form was missing.
- Who may press it: a dev, as `create-user` holds. A sample, a twin, an archived person and a row that already has a login are refused by the door as before.

## Tests

- `attachLogin.test.ts`: the address is checked before the password, a short or mistyped password is refused in words, the press, the hint and the toast say whether an email goes, and none of them says the password back.
- `PersonDeskAccessSection.render.test.tsx`: **Set a password now** shows the two boxes and changes the press; a mismatch is refused with nothing called; the press calls `create-user` once with the id, the address and the password, and `send-sign-in-email` is never called. Switched back to **Email them a link**, the press sends the email and the body carries no password.
