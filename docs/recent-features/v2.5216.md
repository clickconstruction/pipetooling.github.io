A week whose pending hours are at least a quarter of its recorded hours (`HEADLINE_WAITING_SHARE`) is *waiting*: hatched in the bars, left out of the settled range.
- **The verdict sentence**: the settled weeks' range first, then either the trailing waiting weeks named as the reason the recent numbers read low ("…only because 564h still wait on approval, not because the field slowed"), or, with nothing waiting, the last four weeks' average against the four before. No field hours at all reads *thin*.
- **The two lights** (`netPositionLight`, `cashLight`): net position's move since the window start (red when down, with paper profit and the billing lag beside it), and the forecast's lowest point against the floor (green, amber when the typed cash figure is a week or more old, red under the floor), each one sentence.
