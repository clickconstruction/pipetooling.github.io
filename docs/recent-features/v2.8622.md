# v2.8622 — The Bridge: each step on the pipe says which way its work is heading (punch list #321, PR 1) (2026-11-26)

PR 1 of three for *The Bridge lights the narrowest step* (`to-dos/bridge-narrowest-step/README.md`). The owner answered the card's two questions on 2026-11-25: *the scaling function* is the narrowest step, the one that moves the fewest dollars a week, and the Bridge should light it. This PR is the piece that changes no rule: the smallest useful step the card named, so the two readings can be seen side by side before the red moves. Client only. No migration, no edge function: `flowStripDown.ts` is not among the kernels copied to the edge functions, and `stageFlow.ts`, which is, was not touched.

## What changed

- **Each row's direction** (`pileDirection`, `feederDirection`, `stripDownDirections` in `src/lib/bridge/flowStripDown.ts`). Under a step's weeks on the standing pipe, one line says which way the work in front of it is heading. For Build, Bill and Collect it is what comes in a week less what goes out, from the two paces each card behind **The flow** already holds (`PileStageInput.inPerWeekUsd` and `outPerWeekUsd`): *Grows $15k a week.*, *Shrinks $14k a week.*, or *Keeps up.* when the two paces are within a twentieth of each other (`DIRECTION_KEEPS_MARGIN`), so a wobble does not read as a trend. A step that clears more than it is fed with nothing waiting reads *Keeps up.*, not a pile shrinking. Market and Sales hold no pile, so their line is their pace against the field's (`fieldPerWeekUsd`): *$14k a week short of the field.*, *$4k a week ahead of the field.*, or *Keeps up with the field.* Market's line comes only once its worth is read (`marketWorthPerWeek`), since the dollars bid are not on the field's scale. A step that does not read has no line.
- **The pipe draws it** (`BridgeFlowStripDown.tsx`, a `directions` prop the page fills from `stageInput`, today's paces and never the dial's what-if). The line sits under the wait bar, or under the pace on a feeder, in its own colour: a growing pile in red and bold, a shrinking pile or a feeder ahead of the field in green, short of the field in amber, keeping up in the muted grey. The red run of pipe and the red words stay where the page's rule puts them. With the line on the rows each row is one line taller (86 px on a phone, 92 at a desk, from 68 and 74), so the pipe is 90 px taller in all. The press on a row says the direction to a screen reader after the step's own sentences (`stripDownLabel`). `stripDownWords` lists the line with the rest of what the pipe prints.
- **The guide** *read the Bridge* says what the line means, in the paragraph on the pipe.

## Not in this PR

- **The rule.** The page still lights the step with the most weeks of work waiting (`buildStageFlow`). PR 2 moves the red to the narrowest step. That kernel is copied to the edge functions and read by `bridge-weekly-sheet`, so PR 2 carries a regenerated copy and owes that function a redeploy after its merge, as v2.7230 did.
- **The tile and the call.** *Slowest step* and *What to do now* read as before. PR 3.
- **The phone's word budget.** The first screen was already over the budget #303 set once the pipe stood up (v2.6973 names the count), and five short lines add to it. The card's suggestion of folding Market's two part lines behind the press on a phone to pay for it was not taken here: it changes what Market's row shows, and nobody has asked for it.
- **The pipe drawn across** (`?pipe=across`, `flowStrip.ts`, copied to the edge functions) has no direction line.
