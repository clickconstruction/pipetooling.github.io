# v2.6883 — Bid Board links keep their place, and bid photos read by name (punch list #306, the second look) (2026-11-10)

The owner asked of the photos icon and its window (v2.6860): *have you tested it live and do you think it is the best we can do?* This is what a walk on the deployed site showed, fixed. No migration and no edge function change.

## What the live walk showed

On clicktooling.com as the dev login, on the ZZ Test bid, at desk and phone width: the camera drew on all 85 rows; the window read the bid's Drive folder through `bid-photos`; a photo added at 23:40 UTC was filed by the sweep at 23:45:07 and was not adopted a second time.

## What changed

- **Every link in its own place** (`boardLinkSlots`, `src/lib/bids/bidBoardLinkSlots.ts`, tested): after the camera come four fixed slots, folder, plans, CountTooling and submission. A link the bid does not have leaves its slot empty; a second CountTooling project follows the slots; a won bid's chips sit on their own line above the icons. On the live board the camera's left edge had sat at seven positions in the first twenty rows, since the column centred whatever a row had. After, the camera, the folder and the plans icon each measure one position across all 85 rows.
- **A picture the app filed reads by its caption** (`appFiledNameParts`, `gridTileWords`): such a file is named *2026-11-01 Priya Raman · Gas meter*, and the tile cut it off before the caption, so five photos read alike. The tile now shows the caption, with the sender as who. Display only: the row keeps Drive's name. A photo a person named keeps their name.
- **The board's count follows an adoption**: `loadBidPhotos` carries how many pictures the read took on, and the window tells the board to read its counts again.
- **The drop target is the whole window** when the bid already has photos; it was a box under the grid.
- **Plain words when Drive is silent** (`driveSilentWords`): *Drive could not be reached just now. The bid's photos still show. Its folder does not.*
- The guide `add-photos-to-a-bid`, `docs/BIDS_SYSTEM.md` and the card.

## What it did not change

- **`DRIVE_IMPERSONATE_USER` is not set on prod.** A bid whose project folder is in a personal Drive keeps its photos in the app, shown and counted, until the folder moves into the shared Drive or the secret is set. That state is covered by tests and has not been walked live. It is on the card for the owner.
- A missing link still leaves a gap and not a grey icon with a press. That is the card's PR 5.

## Verified

Walked on the dev preview against prod as the dev login: the slots on the real board at 1,400 wide; the ZZ Test bid's window with its six adopted pictures reading by caption and sender; the drop overlay. The live-site test photo's row is stamped removed; its file *ZZ Test 306 live site.jpg* stays in the ZZ Test bid's Drive photo folder.
