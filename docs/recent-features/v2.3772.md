# v2.3772 — Pipeline strip menus: the page scrolls while a menu is open (2026-09-23)

Grace, 2026-09-23, on the Jobs → Pipeline ☰ section-tools menu (iPad screenshot): *"When a user opens this drop down, I would like for the background to not block scrolling. Otherwise sometimes the user can't access the bottom part of it."* Client only; no migration, no edge deploy.

## What was wrong

Both strip menus — `JobsStagesSectionToolsMenu` (☰, fourteen doors) and `JobsStagesToolsMenu` (⋯, filters and toggles) — closed themselves with a `position: fixed; inset: 0` backdrop div. The backdrop caught every wheel tick and every finger, so the page could not move while a menu was open. The strip is not sticky, so the menu scrolls with the page; a menu that ran past the bottom of a short viewport was therefore unreachable — the only way out was to close it.

## The fix

- **`useCloseOnOutsideClick(ref, open, onClose)`** (`src/hooks/useCloseOnOutsideClick.ts`) replaces the backdrop in both menus: while open it listens for a document `click` whose target is outside the menu's root, and for Escape. Listening for `click` rather than `pointerdown` is the point — a scroll gesture never produces a click, so scrolling leaves the menu open and only a real tap outside closes it. The trigger button sits inside the root, so its own toggle click is not treated as "outside".
- The menus keep `position: absolute` under the strip at `zIndex: 121`; nothing else changed in their look or their items.

## Tests

`JobsStagesSectionToolsMenu.render.test.tsx`: no fixed div while open, a click on the menu's own frame keeps it open, a click on the body closes it without calling a door, Escape closes it. `JobsStagesTab.render.test.tsx`'s Mobile cards smoke reopens the ⋯ menu before toggling back off — its clicks on a card, its ⋯ and Cancel are outside the menu and now close it, which is the point (under the old backdrop those clicks could not have happened at all).
