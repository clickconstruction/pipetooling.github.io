# v2.5532 — Lien desk: a mailing through Docsmit is kept the moment it goes (2026-10-14)

Found by the adversarial review of v2.5530 (Esc on the run window, which holds Esc and a click outside over a Docsmit send but leaves the × alone). The trace, confirmed in vitest before the fix: `mailDocsmit` patched only the window's own `notices` state and set `printedAt` locally; it never called `onPrinted`, and `prepareDocsmitRun` made each `mail_pieces` row with `source_ids` empty, linked to filings only inside `recordLienDeskRun`. Close the run with the × before **Record the run**, and nothing in the app knew the letters went: the notices read Ready to send, and the next run offered **Mail through Docsmit** for the same envelopes (a second paid certified mailing) or printed them for paper. No migration and no function change. A piece prepared before this landed names no desk item, so one already caught this way stays invisible to the run: a lien notice's `mail_pieces` row (`lien_notice_53_056` or `lien_retainage_53_057`) with `source_ids = '{}'` and a status past `prepared` (not `failed`) is one of those envelopes. Add the envelope's desk items to its `source_ids` and the next run knows it.

- **The piece names its notices from prepare** (`src/lib/mail/runDocsmit.ts`): `docsmitPrepareDraft` fills `source_ids` with the envelope's desk items when no filings are given, and `docsmitCandidates` lists every job's item of a combined notice (`parts`), not the lead's alone. The record still adds the filings beside them (`linkMailPiecesToFilings`); the poll's `writeTrackingToFilings` matches filings only, so the item ids are inert there.
- **The send is the desk's at once** (`LienDeskRunModal.tsx`, `mailDocsmit`): the notices with every copy out are stamped through `onPrinted`, the same stamp a print writes, and the foot moves to recording once every notice is out. `onRecorded` re-reads the desk once anything mailed, or when the answer cannot say what went (no answer came back), so the next run is built from the pieces; a refusal that mailed nothing leaves the desk alone. On Put a GC on notice the re-read also drops the approval's undo strip, whose words say nothing was mailed. `noticesFullyPrinted` counts a copy mailed through Docsmit as out, so a notice that went part by Docsmit and part on paper is stamped once its paper copy prints.
- **The function's own answer** (`runDocsmitIo.ts`, `realIo.invoke`): `mail-send-docsmit` answers a refusal with an error status (402 credit short, 422 an address that will not mail, 502), and `supabase.functions.invoke` hands those back as `data: null` with a `FunctionsHttpError`. The client read none of them: a send that mailed envelope 1 and stopped short on envelope 2 read as *Nothing mailed*, and the next press of the door would mail envelope 1 again; a refused address was held as *Edge Function returned a non-2xx status code*. The body is now read from `error.context`, so `sent`, `stopped_at`, the shortfall and the correction reach the window, the resend door and the review.
- **A later run knows** (`lienDeskRun.ts` `runRecipientWithPiece`, `lienMailPieces.ts` `mailPieceForCopy`): `buildLienDeskRun` and `buildLienRetainageRun` give a copy whose piece Docsmit took (not prepared, not refused, not a plain copy) `via`, `pieceId`, `pieceStatus`, the USPS number and `pieceMailedOn` (the Central day of `mailed_at`) once the poll has them. The run's own rules then keep it out of the Docsmit door and the packet, and Record the run writes its piece with that day as `sent_on`, as the poll would (`recordLienDeskRun`; a filing made after the poll read the day never hears it again).
- **Its own envelope** (`runEnvelopes.ts`): a copy that went keeps the envelope it went in, keyed `docsmit:<piece id>`, so a new notice's copy to the same GC or owner gets an envelope of its own instead of being taken for mailed (found by the review: with one name-and-address envelope, B's new GC copy rode in A's mailed envelope, never printed or mailed, and recorded as sent). `recipientViaDocsmit` moved here for it and `lienDeskRun.ts` re-exports it.
- **The reads** (`src/lib/mail/lienMailPiecesIo.ts` `readMailPiecesBySource`): one loosely typed read by `source_ids`, keyed by the ids asked for. `useLienDeskData` reads the Sent row's pieces by filing (as before, now through it) and the run's by approved item (`mailPiecesByItem`, full mode only); `useGcOnNoticeData` reads its run's the same way.
- **Take back** (`lienRunTakeBack.ts` `runPrintedItemIds`): a notice with a copy mailed through Docsmit is never offered back to Ready to send.
- **Tests**: the run's render harness (a full send stamps `it1` and re-reads before the ×; a partial send re-reads, then stamps once the GC's copy prints; a lost answer stamps nothing and re-reads; a refusal that mailed nothing leaves the desk alone; a send finishing a notice whose paper copy printed first moves the foot to recording; a run built after a send shows the mailed card, offers Docsmit and the packet only the other envelopes, and has no take-back; a new copy to the GC beside a mailed one goes on its own), `runDocsmitIo.test.ts` (the prepare's `source_ids`, a combined notice's items, a 402 and a 422 read through the real `invoke`), `lienMailPiecesIo.test.ts`, both hooks' `zz` render tests (the by-item read), `runEnvelopes.test.ts`, `lienDeskRunIo.test.ts` (`sent_on` from the piece), `runDocsmit.test.ts`, `lienMailPieces.test.ts`, `lienDeskRun.test.ts`, `lienRunTakeBack.test.ts`. Each fix was mutation-checked: reverting it fails its test.
- **Left**: (1) Two windows open on one run, or a send whose answer never came back and is tried again in the same window, can still mail an envelope twice: nothing on the server refuses a second piece for a desk item and recipient that already went. (2) The run's door and prepare read every envelope, held ones too, so an unsigned notice or one with nothing to claim can be mailed through Docsmit (found by the same review, older than this). (3) **Combine** is not remembered: a combined notice mailed through Docsmit and recorded in a later sitting records per job unless the office ticks Combine again; the desk item's `fields` cannot hold the choice, since the signature hashes all of `fields`. (4) A failed pieces read leaves the run as if nothing went. (5) The desk's own doors (**Back to ready**, **Already mailed? Record it**) do not read the pieces.
