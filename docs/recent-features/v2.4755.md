- **The function** reads `users` (`name, phone, role`) for real, unarchived accounts in the `assistant` and `controller` roles and sends `officeContacts`; the office number is `PORTAL_COMPANY.phone`. The page's parser falls back to the letterhead's number alone from an older function.
- **[`LegalPortalReachStrip.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/legal/LegalPortalReachStrip.tsx)** sits under the letterhead on every view, above the tabs. On a phone (`src/index.css`) it is a column, one line per number.
- **The firm's print** (`buildFirmPacketPrintHtml`, `officeContacts`) puts the same two lines under the letterhead's *Referral packet · prepared …* line.
- **The Legal desk's firm window** reads the same rows (`legalOfficeContactsIo.ts`) and says *The firm's page says to call: …*; with no controller, or one without a phone, an amber line names the gap and where the phone goes (Settings → People).
- **The sample portal** carries sample people (Robin Ortega, Dana Whitlock; controller Morgan Ellis on a 555 number).

## Is this the best we can do?

- **First names, not a list.** *Ask for Robin or Casey* is what the person answering will say; the assistants share the office line rather than each carrying a mobile on the firm's page.
- **What each number is for, not *at any time*.** The office may not pick up at midnight; the controller's line is the one that answers when the office does not. That still gives a lawyer someone at any hour, without a promise the office may not keep.
- **From the users table, not the particulars.** The particulars block was the other home; it is still 0 of 8 filled and names change with staff. The desk's firm window says when the page cannot name a controller, so nobody learns it from the firm.
- **No email addresses.** The question box on a matter already reaches the office and lands in the record.
