# v2.6834 — A bill paid in kind is not chased, not forecast as cash, and not counted in pay speed (punch list #301) (2026-11-10)

The card's first complaint: the remainder of an in-kind deal *looks like money owed and late*, the chase could queue it, and pay speed read 0 days. v2.6829 fixed the customer's own page. This PR carries the same fact to the readers that judge a bill late.

## What changed

- **Pay speed**, migration `20261110081541_in_kind_leaves_pay_speed.sql` ([`docs/migrations/20261110081541_in_kind_leaves_pay_speed.md`](../migrations/20261110081541_in_kind_leaves_pay_speed.md)): an In kind payment gets a `pay_speed_exclusions` row as it is written, and the ones already recorded get theirs. The sampler and the Data health list already read that table, so neither function is rewritten and they cannot disagree.
- **The chase**, `buildPaymentChaseQueue` (`src/lib/jobs/paymentChase.ts`): a bill on a job with `in_kind_arrangement_id` is skipped, so it is on no customer's call card.
- **The forecast**, `buildBilledPaymentForecast` (`src/lib/jobs/billedPaymentForecast.ts`): such a bill is in no bucket and not in `openTotal`; `inKind: { count, dollars }` counts it, and the modal's last line reads *$9,375 on 8 bills is scheduled in kind, not money coming*.
- **The Billed row's dates block**, `buildBilledDatesLedger` (`src/lib/jobs/billedDatesLedger.ts`, input `inKind`): the money row is **Scheduled in kind** with no day, a promise does not bring an Expected row back, and the verdict never reads *Ask for a date*. The lien rows stand, since a credit does not move a deadline. The phone row's next line no longer reads *N d past expected*.

## Decisions taken with the build

- **Exclusion rows, not a rewritten sampler.** Changing `pay_speed_samples()` alone would have left Data health calling the same payment *measurable*. The exclusion table is the one place both read, and it keeps the reason and lets a person count a payment again.
- **The forecast counts them apart instead of dropping them silently.** A total that shrinks with no word would read as money lost.
- **The lien clock is left alone.** A deal to be paid by credit over months does not extend a lien window. The row says so by staying.

## Not here

- **The Dashboard's receivables card, Who owes what, the Bridge's Collect pile and the 30/90-day chips on the Pipeline** still total these bills as open and aged. They read their own row lists, each with its own select, and are the next sweep.
- **The residential reminder flow** (`messages-nudge-dispatch`, `residentialLoad`) still reads an in-kind bill's expected day from pay history. It is untouched here. A hold on the contact (`nudge_holds`) stops a reminder today.

## Release order

Merge, then `supabase db push`. The client change needs v2.6827's column and reads it through the board's own job rows.
