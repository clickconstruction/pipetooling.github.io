# v2.3699 — Quick clock: the quick-time door is a square in the clock row (2026-09-21)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Owner-picked from the mock-up (Option A, "quick / clock").

## Why

The door PR 2 (v2.3659) shipped was a right-aligned text link *＋ quick call or email* under the whole clock row, portaled into a slot there because anything taller inside the clock's own slot stretched the Tally and Job Report squares. It read as an afterthought and cost a whole line above Estimate/Change Order. The owner asked for it up in the row, beside the clock, as a box.

## The change

- **[`ClockInOutButton.tsx`](../../src/components/ClockInOutButton.tsx)**: the door is a 48 × 48 blue square with the two-line label **quick / clock** (accessible name *Quick call or email*, so the guide's words and the screen reader agree), rendered inside the clock's top row directly after Clock In and before the history-clock button. Same `canUseQuickAdd` gate, same `QuickTimeAddSheet`. Because it is a square like its neighbors the row keeps one height, so the portal, the slot lookup and the `react-dom` import go. Clock In's side padding drops from 1.5 rem to 0.5 rem so the word fits its narrower flex share (about 95 px at 375 wide).
- **[`DashboardPinnedQuickRow.tsx`](../../src/components/dashboard/DashboardPinnedQuickRow.tsx)**: the under-row slot element is gone; **[`quickTimeAdd.ts`](../../src/lib/clock/quickTimeAdd.ts)** drops `QUICK_ADD_DOOR_SLOT_ID`.
- **[`QuickAddSettingsBlock.tsx`](../../src/components/settings/QuickAddSettingsBlock.tsx)**: the Settings sentence names the square.
- Guide *add a quick call or email to my hours*: *Where it is* draws the row; step 1 says *Tap quick clock*.

## Not changed

Who gets the door, the sheet, the rules and the RPC. Dispatch mode never had the under-row slot, and the tile now sits in the clock's own row there as everywhere.

## Verified

Typecheck, lint on the touched files; `helpGuideContent`, `releaseNotes` and `quickTimeAdd` suites; the row looked at in the browser off the clock at phone width.
