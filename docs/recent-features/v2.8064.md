# v2.8064 — Lien desk: Months on this job asks for a tick only where one can be made (2026-11-20)

Found by the Fable 5.2 audit of punch list #361 (the Months card as the ledger, v2.7989) and confirmed on the live desk. Client only: no migration, no edge function, no kernel change.

## What was wrong

The Months card invited a tick nobody could make.

- **A notice in Sent.** The grid drew a last column *This notice* whose every box was disabled, under the lead line *Tick a month to put it on this notice.* The kernel `buildLienMonthGrid` leaves that column out when `thisItem.status === 'sent'` (the notice is among the papers), and v2.7989's fragment says a notice that already went has no column to tick. But the desk handed the kernel `thisItem: item ?? null`, and the desk's `item` is null for a sent or a missed item, so the kernel's test never fired there.
- **Ready, Printed and Awaiting notices, and a notice whose months are all closed.** Every tick is locked, and the lead line still asked for one. Before v2.7989 the foot of the column said that sentence only when a row could be ticked; the rebuild lost the test.

## What changed

- [`LienDesk.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDesk.tsx): the grid's `thisItem` is the desk's `item`, else the selected row's item on the Sent pile (there `selected.item` is the sent item, `lienDesk.ts`). The kernel then drops the column, as it was written to. Nothing else reads `monthGrid` but the months card.
- [`LienDeskMonths.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskMonths.tsx): `canTick` is true when one row's tick is not locked. The lead line says *Tick a month to put it on this notice.* only with a column for this notice and `canTick`. The second sentence, *A check is a paper that already names the month.*, is as it was. The line is drawn only when it has a sentence. The table carries `data-this`, `yes` or `no`.
- [`index.css`](../../src/index.css): a phone's row keeps a 6.5rem column at the right edge for the tick. With `data-this='no'` the row is one column, so a sent notice's months use the card's width.

## Not changed

- The kernel, the ticks' own locks, the claim's foot and the quiet rows.
- The help guide: its sentences on the last column and the tick sit under *Drafting a notice* and hold for a draft.

## Tests

- `LienDeskMonths.render.test.tsx` (the lead-line test, edited in place; 6 in all): with one row open the lead asks for a tick; with every row locked it does not, and with no earlier paper the line is not drawn; locked with an earlier paper it is the second sentence alone; a grid with no column for this notice has no `[data-lien-grid-paper="this"]`; the table carries `data-this`.
- `LienDesk.render.test.tsx` (+1, 236 in all): on a notice in Sent the card keeps paper A, has no `[data-lien-grid-paper="this"]`, no tick cell, `data-this="no"`, and does not say *Tick a month to put it on this notice.* It fails on the desk as it was.
- `lienMonthGrid.test.ts` passes unchanged. No existing assertion expected the dead column.

## Not looked at

The phone layout of a sent notice was not looked at in a browser by the session that wrote this change. jsdom lays nothing out, so the tests hold the attribute and not the width. The auditing session checks it.
