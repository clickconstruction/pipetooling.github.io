# v2.3528 — Submittals stage 5a: the conversation on the review room (2026-09-16)

Client + one migration + three edge functions (`submit-submittal-review`, `get-submittal-room`, the new `send-submittal-reply-email`). PR 5a of `to-dos/submittals/STAGES-5-6-BUILD.md`; item 8 of the owner's ready-to-build train.

## Why

The review room (stage 4a) is where the customer's architect decides on rows. A question about a product had nowhere to go but an email chain the office loses. Stage 5 gives the room a thread, so the question is one conversation on the submittal itself, and the decisions and shared revisions post their own lines so the thread is the timeline and the rows are the summary.

## What shipped

- **Schema** — `bid_submittal_messages` ([migration doc](../migrations/20260916204602_bid_submittal_messages.md)): one thread per room; `author_kind` office · reviewer · watcher · system · robot; `kind` message · reply · decision · shared; `tags`; `metadata` carrying the inbox row an ask opened. RLS: pricing sharers read on rooms of bids they can price and insert only office / system entries; outsiders write through the function. `asked` joins the event types.
- **The ask** — `submit-submittal-review` `action: 'message'`: identify first (403 `identify_first` for a room-token caller; the page opens the same sheet reworded *Before you ask*), a watcher may ask (decision 10), a closed room 410, **five an hour per person** then 429 (*Five an hour is the limit — call the office if it cannot wait*). The ask becomes a message, an `asked` event, and one high-priority inbox row — the estimator's when that group has anyone, else Dispatch — titled *Dana Whitfield (architect) asked about WC-1 on B398 Rev 2*. Kernels `parseMessageBody` / `messageVerdict` / `askTitle` / `decisionEntryBody` in `_shared/submittalReviewActions.ts` (+3 tests).
- **The thread on the room** — `get-submittal-room` adds `messages` (oldest first; the office reads as the company, never a staff name) and `person.messagesThisHour`. The room draws **On this submittal** under the rows: the entries (system lines quieter, the office in copper), tag chips for the revision's rows, the ask box and **Ask**. The `decide` branch posts *Dana decided 3 rows · 2 revise · 1 reject*; Share posts *Rev N is up*. Client kernels `parseRoomMessage` / `threadOrder` / `describeThreadEntry` / `summarizeThread` (+3 tests).
- **The answer** — the tab's **Thread** panel (collapsed to *3 entries · last: Dana asked Sep 17*) with a reply box; `src/lib/submittals/replyToRoom.ts` (+4 tests) inserts the office reply, calls `send-submittal-reply-email` (the subject names the bid and the tags, the body quotes the question, the button is the person's personal link; `_shared/submittalReplyEmail.ts`, +2 tests), and closes the inbox row with the reply as its note. The inbox card for this kind shows the question and **Answer on B398 ZZ Test · Rev 2 →**, the door to the tab.
- Guides: *answer a customer waiting request* → *A question from a submittal room*; *build a submittal package* → *The thread*. `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` (three sections), `docs/ACCESS_CONTROL.md`.

Deliberately not in 5a (the brief's inbox-card Reply sheet): the card opens the tab, where one reply path lives, so the two doors cannot drift. 5b (the drop zone and *entered by*) is next.
