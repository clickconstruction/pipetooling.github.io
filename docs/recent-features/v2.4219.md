# v2.4219 — The Bridge: Vectors by the day zooms out to weeks and months (2026-09-30)

Punch list #69, PR 2 of 4 (PR 1: v2.4217). The kernel already folded days into any period; this PR gives the panel the zoom row and the page the wider load.

- **Panel** [`BridgeVectorDaysPanel.tsx`](../../src/components/bridge/BridgeVectorDaysPanel.tsx): a **Days · Weeks · Months** segmented control beside the title (`aria-pressed` on the pick); Weeks and Months draw a `PeriodCell` per column — the day cell's six shades, wider, the ≈ mark when any of it rests on an assumed-half job, hours alone for a period with no field hours; the total column reads *13 wk* / *12 mo*; ‹ › are labelled *Previous month* / *Previous 13 weeks* / *Previous 12 months*. +1 render test (the period cells, the total label, the zoom row reporting its pick).
- **Page** `Bridge.tsx`: a zoom state beside the anchor; the period comes from `vectorRangeFor(zoom, anchor)` and the anchor for Weeks and Months is the period's END (the kernel takes the last week / month as the anchor, the Days zoom the month's first day); picking a zoom resets to today's period; the per-period input cache already keyed on start:end serves each zoom's range — a year of sessions for Months, priced through `loadEarnedJobs` like the rest.
- Guide *read the Bridge* and the glossary entry say what the zooms are.
