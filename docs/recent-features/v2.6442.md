# v2.6442 — The Plans look is never wider than a phone’s screen (2026-11-03)

The second fault from the live walk of v2.6356 (#6816) on 2026-11-03, left out of v2.6435 (#6915) and named there as a follow-up: on the Development page's Plans look, drawn as cards on a phone since v2.6377, the page scrolled 4 px sideways at 375 × 812 even with the older sets' presses fixed. Measured on a ZZ test development with one part, one set and no job: the look's box is 329 px wide (x = 23 to 352), but its root grid's one column resolved to 355.6 px, so every child of the look (the part cards, the chips row) ran to x = 379 on a 375 px screen, and the page read 378–379 px wide. The cause: the root grid was `display: grid; gap: 10` with no column template, so its one implicit column was `auto`, whose floor is the widest child's min-content; the all-good chip *every job that builds from plans names a part with a set* (`.developmentCountChip`, `white-space: nowrap`) is 356 px of words in one line, and the column grew to it. Client only; no migration, no edge function.

- **`DevelopmentPartsCard.tsx`, the root grid.** `grid-template-columns: minmax(0, 1fr)`: one column as wide as the box and never wider, on every screen. A child wider than the column now overflows alone instead of widening its siblings.
- **The all-good chip** gets `data-testid="site-chip-all-good"` and, on a phone (`useMatchMedia(DEVELOPMENT_PHONE_QUERY)`), `white-space: normal` and a 12 px corner, so the sentence wraps onto two lines inside the chips row as a rounded tag instead of running past the screen; on a desk it keeps the class's `nowrap` pill, where it fits in one line. The words are unchanged. The two count chips beside it (*N parts with no set*, *N jobs follow no plans yet*) are short and were never the cause.
- **Measured after, same development, 375 × 812:** the page is 375 px wide, the look's column 329 px, the cards and the chips row end at x = 352 inside it, the chip is 329 × 41 (two lines). On the desk, reloaded at the pane's width, the chip is one `nowrap` line as before and the column is the box's width.
- **Tests.** `DevelopmentPartsCard.render.test.tsx`: a new case builds the all-good view (one part with a set, its one job on it), renders it on a phone and pins the chip's words, its `white-space: normal` and the root's `grid-template-columns: minmax(0, 1fr)`, then re-renders on the desk and pins the chip's `white-space` unset and the same column template.
- **Not in this PR.** The History look's chips strip (`developmentHistoryChip`) reads past 375 px inside its own scrolling row and does not widen the page; it was not touched. The e2e viewport page is not given this surface (it needs a development with a part and a set, against prod data that moves).
