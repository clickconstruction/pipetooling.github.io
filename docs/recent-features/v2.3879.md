## What shipped

- **`src/hooks/useJobLienTimeline.ts`** — one job's timeline for a surface that is not the Lien window: the job's live filings and demand letters, the property kind off the linked address, the work months from the approved sessions (`useForecastWorkMonths`), through `buildLienTimelineFromWindow` — the window's own adapter, with the letters ([v2.3877](v2.3877.md)). Null until the reads land; `hasPaper` says whether anything is out.
- **`src/lib/jobs/jobHistoryLienTimeline.ts`** — `showJobHistoryLienTimeline({ openBalance, hasPaper })` (money owed or paper out) and `jobOpenBalance` (revenue less payments, floored at zero, as the window reads it).
- **`src/components/jobs/JobHistoryLienTimeline.tsx`** — the box above the History grid: a one-line title and `LienTimelineStrip` (row or list by width; Steps · Windows switch; Next on the path and Waiting on). Nothing at all when the gate says no.
- **`JobWindowModal.tsx`** — mounts it in the History pane from the job the Job pane loaded. The mock-up drew the mini row with an *Open the timeline ›* link; the full strip is drawn instead, since the History tab has no door to the Lien window and the strip itself is the thing to read.
- **The guide** *job window tabs* → *The History tab* says so.
