# v2.7790 — Painted maps: a Share sheet you can use almost without reading (2026-11-18)

Punch list #394, step 2 of 2: the sheet, over the rules of v2.7788. The owner asked on 2026-11-18, with a phone screenshot of **Share this map** before a link exists: *how could I offer this page with less words and in a more intuitive way where the user doesn't have to put in the effort to read so much?* His word the same day was *Build it*, every call at the card's pick.

## What changed

- **No Make a link step.** Every press that sends makes the link itself, and reads *Making the link…* while it does. Two presses in the same moment share one make.
- **One blue press.** On a phone it is **Text it from my phone…**: the phone's own Messages opens with the words the office's text already carries, and the person picks who gets it. At a desk, where there is no Messages to open, **Text the customer…** leads for the office on a job and **Copy the link** leads for everyone else.
- **Tiles with a picture each:** **Show a code to scan**, **Copy the link**, **Make a picture** and **Send it another way…**, the last only where the browser has a share sheet. Each kept its name and lost its second line.
- **Print or a file** folds the page to print and the two files behind one press, under the copy's rule.
- **One line for what a reader meets:** *Anyone with the link can open it. They give their name first.* **What they see** opens today's four sentences, word for word.
- **The office's press is Text the customer…** with *From the company's number*, where it read **Text it…**. On a map with no job it keeps its place, dead, and says *This map is on no job.*
- **The code and the picture each take the sheet alone.** **Hide the code** and **Every way to share** go back.
- **A copy makes the link too.** A picture and a printed page make the link when none is out, so each carries the way back to the map as it is today. The page's link is made inside the print build, after its window opened, so a pop-up blocker still sees the press. With no signal the copy is made without the link and nothing is said about it.
- **Once a link is out,** the same presses stay where they were. The link's line, **End this link now** and **Who opened it** sit under them, as they read before.

## When the phone refuses

A link takes about a second to make, and a phone's browser may refuse to open another app that long after the press. Nothing tells a page it was refused, so `openPhoneMessages` watches for the page being left, hidden or blurred within 1.5 seconds.

- Not left, and the link was just made: the sheet says *The link is ready. Press Open Messages.* and the blue press reads **Open Messages**. That press has nothing to wait for.
- Not left, with the link already made: *This phone would not open Messages. Copy the link and paste it into a text.*
- **Send it another way…** refused after the wait: *The link is ready. Press Send it another way… again.*
- **Copy the link** hands the clipboard a promise inside the press, which is what Safari asks for (`copyWhenMade`). Where the clipboard is closed, the link shows to press and hold, with the words the sheet already had.

The link's list is no longer read between the make and the send: the sheet empties its list and reads it again behind the press, so the wait before Messages is the make alone.

## What was dropped from the sheet

- *Or hand over a copy*, the sentence under each copy, and the two second lines under **Send it another way…** and **Show a code to scan**. The guide keeps what they said.
- *This map already has a link out. A link can be read only on the device that made it…*. There is no step to take: the first press on a device makes its link. The link's line still says how many are out.
- The 21-word sentence on a map with no job, for six words on the dead press.
