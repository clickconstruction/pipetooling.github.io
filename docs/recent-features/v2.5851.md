# v2.5851 — Legal desk: one timeline with lanes, six tabs (2026-10-25)

The third of four refinements from the owner's walk of the Legal desk (design spike, 2026-10-25). The desk told the same story in four places — Record of contact (what was said), Fees & steps' *What we did, in order*, the Paper tab's per-job clock strip, and the Timeline — so on job 891 one lien notice showed as a gap line, a planned step, a deadline diamond and a stop on the strip.

- **Lanes** (`src/lib/legal/legalTimeline.ts`): every item carries `lane` — *said*, *did*, *paper* or *planned*. `buildLegalTimeline` takes two more sources: `said` (the Record of contact's entries, `packet.theirWord.timeline`, each keyed as its hold is — `contact:<id>`, `promise:<id>`, `call:<id>`, `note:<job id>` — drawn as kind `said` with the kind and job as its chip, held when the override says so or the entry is not shared, its reason from the matter) and `did` (the packet's steps, `packet.feesAndSteps.steps`: billed and payments on the did lane, demand letters, lien paper and contracts on the paper lane; the promise, call, contact and collections kinds are left to the said lane so nothing shows twice). `timelineHoldKey` answers a said item's own key; `timelineLaneCounts` and `timelineInLane` serve the chips.
- **The panel** (`LegalTimelinePanel.tsx`): `lanes` draws the chips **All · Said · Did · Paper · Planned** with counts; `doors` draws the Record of contact's doors (Customer notes, They said…, Call mode) in the header; a said item takes **Hold from counsel…** and **Share with counsel** like a planned step; each row carries `data-legal-timeline-lane`.
- **The desk** (`LegalDeskModal.tsx`): six tabs — Narrative · Account · Paper · Evidence · **With the firm** · Timeline. The Record of contact tab is gone (a `legalTab=their_word` link lands on the Timeline), *What we did, in order* is gone from With the firm, and the exhibit folds' doors map the Record of contact exhibit to the Timeline. The Paper tab's per-job clock strip stays as the graphic.
- **The firm's portal** is unchanged: it keeps its own tabs, Record of contact among them, and its plain line.

Tests: `legalTimeline.test.ts` (the lanes, the two sources, the hold key and reason, the kinds left to the said lane), `LegalTimelinePanel.render.test.tsx` (the chips, the doors, a said hold), `LegalDeskModal.render.test.tsx` (six tabs, the collections note on the Timeline's Said lane). Guides *review a collections account before it goes to your attorney* (the tabs, the Timeline, Curate what the firm sees), *send a customer account to your attorney*, *share your attorney their portal*; `docs/PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` (the Legal desk's tabs), `docs/GLOSSARY.md` → Planned step. No migration.
