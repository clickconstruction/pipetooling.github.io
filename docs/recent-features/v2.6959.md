# v2.6959 — Bids and permits: our own plumbing permit reads Ours (punch list #307) (2026-11-11)

Punch list #307, PR 3's leftover (`to-dos/bid-permits/README.md`, plan step 3: *A setting holds the names that are us on a plumbing permit*, and the mock-up's **Ours** chip). Until now a plumbing permit on a bid's address was marked whenever it listed a name other than the building permit's contact, so our own permit read *Plumbing permit · another name* and made the red news line on the Bid Board.

## Where the names live

One row of `app_settings`, key `permit_our_names_v1`, JSON `{ "names": [...] }` in `value_text`. No new table. The owner or a dev edits it; everyone signed in reads it, as every `app_settings` row. The migration `20261112003000_permit_our_names_policy.sql` adds the key-scoped insert and update policies for `is_master_or_dev()` (`docs/migrations/20261112003000_permit_our_names_policy.md`). The client calls nothing only the migration creates: a dev saves before the push, and the owner's save is refused in words until it.

**The company name on paper bills counts without being added.** It is the company's own word for itself (`physical_invoice_issuer_v1`, Settings → Jobs, bills and liens), it rides the same read, and the card says so. Nothing else is seeded: with no company name set and no name added, every screen reads exactly as before.

## How a name is compared

`src/lib/research/ourNames.ts`, pure. A name is keyed by `builderKeyOf`, the feed's own key and the one `permits.builder_key` holds: the company on the contact line, else the person, lower case, letters and digits, LLC and Inc dropped. A typed name goes through the same reading, so a name typed as a city writes it always meets itself. Exact on that key and nothing fuzzier, since a wrong *ours* hides a lost job. Our plumber listed beside another company is that company's line, by the feed's key, and does not read ours.

## The setting

Settings → Company → **Our names on plumbing permits** (`PermitOurNamesSettingsCard.tsx`, a fold card, `isOwnerOrDev`): the words, the line that says the company name counts already, the names with **Take back**, and one field with **Add the name**. Nothing else, which is the smallest shape the mock-up leaves room for, since it draws the chip and no setting. An empty list names the press. A refusal reads its reason: a blank, a name with nothing to match on, a name already on the list under another spelling, the company name itself, a full list of 20. The list is read fresh before a save, so two people adding do not drop each other's name. The card is in Settings search, on the anchor `#settings-company-our-permit-names`, and on Dev doors.

## What reads it

`plumbingListing` answers `ours` beside `same` and `name`, and ours is read first: our own permit is never another plumber's, whoever the building permit lists.

- **This site** (`bidSiteWords`, `sitePermitLine`): the verdict reads *A general plumbing permit on this address lists us, as NAME. Issued Jun 2.*, and every plumbing permit under one of our names, of any plumbing type, carries a green **Ours** chip on its row. The row still says the name the city lists.
- **What we can see about the GC** (`gcJobsView`): the job's chip is **Ours**, its line reads *a general plumbing permit, Jun 2, lists us*, and the verdict gains *A general plumbing permit lists us on N.* The site is not counted under another name.
- **The Bid Board** (`bidPermitNews`, one extra argument): the chip reads *Plumbing permit · ours*, green. A permit of ours under 30 days old makes a news line of its own kind, sorted after another name and before a building permit: *A general plumbing permit that lists us was issued on this address Nov 5, as NAME. The bid is not yet won or lost.* An older one keeps the chip and lets the building permit's news through, since it may be an earlier job on a shared address.

The newest general plumbing permit on the address still decides, as before: another name after ours reads another name. Nothing marks a bid won. `useOurPermitNames` reads the names once a session and again after a save on Settings; a failed read leaves every line as it was.

## Not changed

Prospects → Permits does not read the list: the skim's *Has a plumber* and Builders' columns are as they were. The news strip's count badge and its one press are untouched.

## Found in passing, not fixed

`nameKey` and its database twin `research_name_key` drop `l.l.c.` only when a word character follows it, so a name ending in a dotted *L.L.C.* keeps `llc` in its key and does not meet the same name written *LLC*. Here that spelling is a second name to add; on Builders the two spellings are two builders.
