# v2.2268 — Fix: ⏳ Coming up nested inside the Outstanding card (2026-08-25)

v2.2264's Checklist-page insertion of `<ChecklistComingUpSection />` landed
**inside the `titleFor` callback** of `ChecklistOutstandingSection`
(`src/pages/Checklist.tsx`), so the whole Coming-up list rendered inside every
outstanding card's title cell: one overdue card ballooned around ~10 ghost
cards, its done-checkbox floated mid-list (vertically centered against the
huge title), and its due-date footer landed below the ghosts.

Fix: the section moved out to a page-level sibling, after
`ChecklistOutstandingSection` and before the ▼ Upcoming section. The
Dashboard inbox card's usage was already a sibling and is unchanged.

Follow-up (planned, separate PR): group Coming-up rows by their blocking
task, fix the "… is on it" unknown-name fallback, rename the header away
from the "Upcoming" collision, and cap the list with an expander.
