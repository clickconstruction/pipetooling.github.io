# v2.3651 — Sign-in page: the row of ten marks is the disclosure; the word "apps" leaves the page (2026-09-20)

Owner-directed, on the live page: *"Drop down button and show the 10 icons. And if someone clicks on that bar of icons, it opens up that row."* Follows v2.3622 / v2.3624 / v2.3626. Client only; no migration, no edge function.

## The change

- [`ToolingFamilyStrip.tsx`](../../src/components/ToolingFamilyStrip.tsx): the `<summary>` no longer reads *More apps* with a count badge — its face is the ten 20px marks (`tooling-family__more-marks`, `aria-hidden`), with the chevron. The separate preview row that sat beside `<details>` (v2.3626) is removed: the marks are inside the summary now, so they show closed and open, and a press on the bar toggles the list. Still a native `<details>`, no React state.
- The summary has no visible words, so it carries `aria-label="More from the Tooling family (10)"` (the count from `MORE_APPS.length`).
- The strip's label and the nav's `aria-label` read **The Tooling family** (was *The Tooling apps*).
- [`authPublicLanding.css`](../../src/components/authPublicLanding.css): `tooling-family__more-count`, `tooling-family__more-preview` and its `[open] +` hide rule are removed; `tooling-family__more-marks` is a wrapping flex row, so the ten wrap inside the pill on a narrow phone.

## Tests

`SignIn.render.test.tsx`: the nav is found by its new name; the summary holds ten images and the spoken label; the strip's text never contains the word "apps".

## Verified

Dev server, signed out: closed, the pill shows the ten marks and a chevron; a click on the marks opens the ten rows and flips the chevron; a second click closes it.
