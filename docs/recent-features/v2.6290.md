# v2.6290 — My email never opens in a session started with Imitate or View as (2026-11-01)

Punch list #230, a privacy fix to the train's PR 2 and PR 3 (`gmail-mine` v2.6275, the tab v2.6274), live today. The card on Settings → Your account promises *Only you see this tab. Nobody on the team reads your mail through the app.* An independent review found the promise broken, and the lead confirmed it: `login-as-user` (the app's **Imitate** and **View as**) lets a dev, a master or an assistant (an assistant never a master) open a real magic-link session as any account but a dev's. That session's JWT is the person's own, so `gmail-mine` passed its gate (`gc_office_team()`, the switch `users.my_mail_on` on by default) and read the person's own mailbox, as their `users.email`.

- **A record of every imitation**, `20261101175329_user_imitations.sql`: `user_imitations` (`operator_id`, null once that account is deleted; `target_id`, the rows going with the person; `created_at`), an index on `(target_id, created_at DESC)`, read by a dev alone (`USING (public.is_dev())`), written by the service role alone, the three fences at the end. `docs/migrations/20261101175329_user_imitations.md`.
- **`login-as-user`** writes `{ operator_id: <caller>, target_id: <target> }` as the service role after its role checks and before it makes the link. When the insert fails it answers 500 *The app could not record this imitation, so it did not open one.* and makes no link: an imitation nobody recorded would read the person's mail. Nothing else in it changed. One narrow change follows from the foreign key: an auth account with no `users` row can no longer be imitated.
- **`gmail-mine`'s gate**, after its existing refusals and before the Google token (only the gate changed, so the follow's PR merges clean): `sessionLinkAt` reads the caller's JWT payload (no check of the signature, which `auth.getUser()` already made) for the newest `amr` entry whose method is `otp` or `magiclink`. A real magic-link session on prod carries `amr: [{ method: 'otp', timestamp }]`, the sign-in in seconds, kept through every refresh; GoTrue's own redirect may write `magiclink`. With such a time, the person's `user_imitations` rows from 24 hours before it are read as the service role, and `openedByImitation` answers whether one was made in the day up to the sign-in or the 5 minutes after it (the link is made before it is used; the day covers the longest Supabase lets a link live, the minutes the two clocks). One was: 403 `imitated`, *This session was opened with Imitate or View as. Sign in as yourself to read your own mail here.* The read failed: the same refusal, logged `gmail_mine_imitations_unread`. A password session never reads the table and passes as before.
- **The address**: `gmail-mine` reads `users.email` only when it is the address the person signs in with (`auth.getUser()`'s email, both trimmed and lower-cased, `isSignInAddress`), so a `users.email` changed by hand never points the read at another mailbox. Otherwise 403 `offWorkspace` with its own sentence, *The email on your account is not the address you sign in with. Ask a dev to make them the same.* `offWorkspace` and not `badRequest`: the refusal is about which address the app will read for the person, the kind the function already answers for an account with no address, while `badRequest`'s words say to reload, which never helps, and its 400 blames the request.
- **The kernel** [`src/lib/myMail/imitation.ts`](../../src/lib/myMail/imitation.ts), pure, with no imports, mirrored to the edge (`scripts/edge-kernels.mjs`, the regenerated `_shared/gcKernels/lib/myMail/imitation.ts`): `sessionLinkAt`, `openedByImitation`, `imitationsSince`, `isSignInAddress`, `MAGIC_LINK_METHODS`, `IMITATION_WINDOW_SECONDS` (86,400), `IMITATION_SKEW_SECONDS` (300) and `MY_MAIL_NOT_SIGN_IN_WORDS`. An `amr` of plain method names carries no time: when it names a magic link the token's `iat` stands in, so such a session is still checked. A row whose time cannot be read counts as an imitation. The contract gains the kind `imitated` (`MY_MAIL_REFUSAL_WORDS`, `MY_MAIL_REFUSAL_STATUS` 403).
- **The client** needs no change: the tab and the reader draw a refusal as one calm line in the function's words, and `isMyMailRefusal` knows the new kind from the shared contract.
- **Tests**: the kernel's own (both methods, the newest of several, a password session, plain names, a token that is no JWT, the window's edges to the second); `gmailMine.run.test.ts` (a magic-link session just after an imitation of the person refused `imitated` on all four actions with Google never asked for a token and the record read as the service role; the same session with the imitations out of the window, or someone else's, passing; a password session passing with an imitation on record and never reading it; a failed read refusing; a `users.email` that is not the sign-in address refused, and the same address in another case passing); a new `loginAsUser.run.test.ts` (the record written before the link, as the service role, for a dev and an assistant; a failed insert answering 500 with no link made; the three refusals recording nothing). The migration ran twice on a throwaway Postgres 15 with every rule above held.
- **The guide** `tag-an-email-onto-a-job-from-your-own-mail` says a session started with **Imitate** or **View as…** never shows your mail. Docs: `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` (Imitate's rule and the table, the My email sentence), `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` (both functions), `MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md` → Who sees what, `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`'s Impersonation flow.
- **What it leaves**: a password sign-in is never refused, so whoever can set the person's password can still sign in as them (a dev through `set-user-password`, or an imitator who changes it from inside the imitated session, which the person would see the next time they sign in). `dev-mcp`'s `view_as` mints a named person's session with no record, but it only GETs PostgREST and never calls an edge function. A person's own magic link (the desk's **Send sign-in email**) in the day after someone imitated them is refused too, until they sign in by password: the safe side.
- **Deploy order** (by hand, after the merge): push the migration first (`scripts/db-push.sh`), then `supabase functions deploy login-as-user` (`config.toml` keeps `verify_jwt = false`) and `supabase functions deploy gmail-mine --no-verify-jwt`. Either function deployed before the push fails closed: `login-as-user` refuses every imitation (it cannot write the record) and `gmail-mine` refuses every magic-link session (it cannot read it).
