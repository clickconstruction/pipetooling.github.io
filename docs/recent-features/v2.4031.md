# v2.4031 — New Job: a line that could not be saved is now said (2026-09-28)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. The first of the three behaviours row 4 of punch list **#46** wrote down and did not change. Stacked on v2.4029.

## What was wrong

`createJob` inserts the `jobs_ledger` row (checked), then hands the rows the job is born with to `writeNewJobChildRows`: payments → materials → line items → team, one insert at a time. Each insert was awaited and its answer never read, so a row the database refused was dropped with no word. A call that failed outright threw into `createJob`'s catch, which left the New Job form open over a job that already existed — a second press of Create Job would have made a duplicate.

## What shipped

- `writeNewJobChildRows` reads every answer, goes on past a refused row, catches a call that throws, and hands back `NewJobChildRowFailure[]` (`kind` + the database's message). It never throws.
- `newJobChildRowFailureWords` turns that into one sentence: "Job saved, but 1 payment and 2 line items did not save (reason). Open the job and add them again." — null when every row landed.
- `createJob` shows it as an error toast for 15 s (`NEW_JOB_CHILD_ROW_FAILURE_TOAST_MS`), logs the rows to the console, and carries on as before: the job exists, so the form closes and the list refreshes.
- Not changed: the rows are still written one at a time and nothing is rolled back — the transactional `TODO(billing)` at the head of `jobFormSliceWrites` stands.
- Guide: *Edit Job auto-save* → "New jobs still use a button" says what the red note means.

## Tests

- `jobFormSliceWrites.test.ts`: the case that pinned the silence is turned over; refused rows come back in order and the rest are written; a thrown call is a row that did not land; the words for none, one, several kinds, and no reason.
