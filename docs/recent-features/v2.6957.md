# v2.6957 — Lien affidavits: the county prints, held papers stay out of the batch, and the help sheet says what the app checked (2026-11-11)

Punch list #314, the best-we-can-do pass on the help sheets (v2.6953). The owner read the eleven changes on the card and said: *do 1-3*. These are the first three. Changes 5 to 11 are mock-ups on the card, and change 4 was not asked for.

## 1. A ready paper with a hold is not printed

`pileFor` keeps an approved paper in **Ready to file** when a gate closes after the approval (a public owner, a homestead, an address gone), and its Lien window draws no paper while the gate is closed. **Print N for notarization** printed it anyway. The office's first signed batch held a paper against a city.

- `lienAffidavitPrintPlan` (`src/lib/jobs/lienAffidavitBatch.ts`) now returns `gated`: the short-form papers in Ready to file with `ready === false` or a closed gate, each with `lienAffidavitGatedReason`, the hold line's own sentence without its *Don't file yet.* `ready` leaves them out. An entry that carries no gates is not judged.
- `lienAffidavitPrintWords` counts them in the line under the press and in `dead`.
- The batch sheet lists each as *Not printed.* with the reason, beside the earlier-form papers.

## 2. The sheet says what the app checked

The first sheets carried a list of warnings for the reader to check by eye, the same on every paper. The app already holds most of the answers.

- `lienHelpChecks` (`src/lib/jobs/lienHelpSheet.ts`) answers three: the owner is private, what the property record says of a homestead, and whether each sworn month has a timely notice to the owner and the original contractor (`lienNoticeCheckLines`, v2.6955). Each is `ok` or `ask`. The homestead line says only what the record says: *not marked a homestead*, never *not a homestead*.
- The cover sheet draws them as **What the app checked on this paper**, with a tick or an exclamation mark, then **ASK FIRST** when any wants an answer, then **YOUR CHECK**: read every name, address and amount. They replace the list of warnings. A paper nobody checked keeps the list.
- The batch sheet counts them (`lienHelpBatchSummary`: *10 clear to sign. 2 with a question: ask first. 1 not printed.*) and each row says what to ask (`lienHelpBatchAsks`, which also carries the second-paper-at-one-property line).
- `lienHelpPaperFromFields` takes `checked` (the property kind, the homestead mark, the notice lines); the desk fills it for each paper of the batch in `LienDesk.tsx`, the Lien window from its own property and notice check.

## 3. The county where it is signed prints

Every paper of the first batch had the property's county written in the blank at the top. v2.6952 put *(where signed)* beside the blank. Better that there is no blank.

- `signingCounty` on the company block (`src/lib/physicalInvoiceIssuer.ts`), set in Settings as **County where lien papers are signed**.
- `LienAffidavitFields.signingCounty`: the short form prints it on both county lines (the `jurisdiction` block and the jurat's own county). Blank, the paper is as v2.6952 left it.
- **Signed in** `[county]` **County**, a box beside each print press (`LienSigningCountyBox.tsx`, `lienSigningCountyChoice.ts`). It opens on Settings' county and can be typed over for that print alone: `withSigningCounty` (`src/lib/jobs/lienSigningCounty.ts`) hands the paper a copy of the issuer with that county, and nothing is saved.
- With the county printed the sheets list four things to write, and say *The counties are printed: signed in Travis County.* A batch that mixes printed and blank keeps the six.
- The guide copy marks no county blank on such a paper, and its marks count 1, 2, 3 in order whichever landed.
