# v2.8756 — The addresses on file read tries again when it loses the sign-in lock (2026-11-27)

Beside punch list #439. Finding 343 of the fixing thread's ledger, from the simulator thread's sittings 3 and 5 of 2026-11-27 (SE, 375 by 667, iOS 26.4, Safari, the live site). Client only. No migration, no edge function. Nothing owed after the merge.

**As seen.** Documents → Agreements → **Send a paper** → **Name a site…**: *Which site is it for?* opened on a red line, *The app was busy renewing your sign-in, so this did not load. Try again.*, with **Retry**. **Retry** read the picker clean. It showed on both opens of the picker that day.

**Read, and the brief's read held.** The picker reads every address on file when it opens (`readAddresses`, `src/hooks/useAddressesOnFile.ts`). That asks three tables at once: the jobs, the bids and the developments. Each went through `everyRow`, which paged a bare read and threw its first error. It did not try again, where the app's other reads go through `withSupabaseRetry`. So three reads start in the same instant, each waits on the sign-in lock, and on a slow phone one of them loses it. A lost lock is the one failure always safe to send again, since the request never left.

The red line and its **Retry** were right. The fault was that the first read gave up at once.

**The mend.** `everyRow` sends each page through `withRetry`, the rule `withSupabaseRetry` itself goes by. A page that fails in a way that is safe to send again is tried again. A refusal is not, and throws at once.

**It throws the read's own error in the end, as before.** The brief named `withSupabaseRetry`. That wraps a failure in the app's own error class, which changes the words every caller shows: one window's refusal read *You don't have access to this the kept answers.* in its test. `withRetry` keeps the read's error as it is, so no caller's words change.

**Every caller, read.** `everyRow` is called in five places, in two files:

- Three in `readAddresses`: the jobs, the bids and the developments.
- Two in `useAddressLookAlikes.ts`: the map's points, and the kept answers (`readAnswers`).

The screens that reach it, eight in all: the site picker; the address box on the job form; the *Addresses that look alike* window and its count on the Pipeline's tools button; **Send a paper**; the Agreements tab; a site's papers (two reads); and the job form's same-spot question. Each gets the second try. None of their files changes.

**What a person may notice.** A read that fails for a lost signal now waits through its tries before it says so, as the app's other reads do. For the address box that is a hint arriving later, never a wait on the form.

**Tests.** `useAddressesOnFile.everyRow.test.ts`: a page that loses the lock once is sent again and the caller sees no failure, in both browsers' words and when it is thrown; a refusal throws at once with the read's own error; a lock lost every time gives up after its tries; a later page is tried again without asking the first twice; and `readAddresses` with one of its three tables losing the lock still answers. The six test files of the screens that reach the read pass unchanged.
