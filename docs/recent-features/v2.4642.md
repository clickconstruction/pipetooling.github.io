# v2.4642 — Legal portal: the firm reads the contact log about its own matter (2026-10-05)

One edge function (`legal-portal`, deploy after merge) and a guide sentence; no migration. Item 24 of punch list #85 ([`to-dos/legal-portal-firm-ready/`](../../to-dos/legal-portal-firm-ready/README.md)). Mockup: `mockup-24-scope-contacts.html` beside the card — Their word on the portal before and after, and the rule as a table.

## The defect

`customer_contacts` is the payer's general contact log: calls and emails about any job, any bid or the account. It has no job column. `legal-portal` read the whole log for the payer and filtered it only by the held-before-first-bill date rule, so a firm referred one job read calls about the customer's other jobs. For a GC payer that log is mostly other work.

## The rule

In [`_shared/legalContactScope.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/legalContactScope.ts) (`src/lib/legal/legalContactScope.ts` re-exports it):

- An entry whose text **names one of the matter's jobs** goes to the firm.
- An entry that names **only the payer's other jobs** stays with the office.
- An entry that **names no job** is account history and goes.
- "Names a job" (`textNamesJob`): the job's number (HCP, else Click; `jobNumberOf`) standing alone, optionally after `#` or `J`: `1042`, `#1042`, `J1042`, `job 1042`. The digits inside money, a decimal, a date, a phone number or a longer number do not count. Numbers shorter than three characters never count.
- `contactScopeNumbers(matterJobs, payerJobs)` builds the two sets; a number shared with a matter job is the matter's.

The held-before-first-bill rule applies after, unchanged.

## What changed

- `legal-portal`: one more read, the payer's jobs (`jobs_ledger` where `customer_id` or `gc_customer_id` is the payer; `id, hcp_number, click_number, customer_id, gc_customer_id`), and the contact entries pass `contactGoesToCounsel` before the held rule.
- `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` (legal-portal) states the rule.
- Guide `review-a-collections-account-before-it-goes-to-your-attorney`, Their word: two sentences saying the portal leaves out a contact that names only another job, and a contact that names no job goes as account history.
- **Integration pass (review fixes).** A bill of the job names the job: *Invoice 1042-1*, *#1042-2* and *job#1042* count as 1042 (a dash and one or two digits after the number, and a `job#` prefix), while *1042-2026* or a phone's *512-1042-5555* still do not. An explicit share wins: an entry the office shared by hand (`held_overrides[key] === false`) goes to the firm whatever job it names (`contactGoesWithShare`). Known false positives, left as they are: a year or a phone extension written on its own that equals a job number (*in 2026*, *ext 1042*) reads as naming that job, which sends an entry rather than holding one back. When item 29 (#4620) makes sharing the default, only a by-hand share overrides the job scope; a default share does not.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **Why not only entries that name a matter job?** Most entries name no job ("statement re-sent, no reply"). They are the account's history; dropping them would empty Their word.
- **A bid follow-up still goes.** It names no job. Leaving it out needs a job or bid column on the log; the call-mode and bid follow-up writers know the bid, so a later migration could record it.
- **A false match?** An account entry that carries another job's number standing alone stays home. That errs toward saying less about unreferred work; the office sees every entry on the desk.
- **Does the office see the same?** The desk's own Their word still shows the whole log. The office's Preview of the portal shows exactly what the firm gets.
