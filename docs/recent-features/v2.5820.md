# v2.5820 — Quickfill Email station, PR 2 of 5: gmail-count, the counter beside the taker (2026-10-24)

Punch list #159, the owner's "build it all". PR 1 (v2.5814) laid the kernels and the tables; this PR
is the edge function that fills them. Nothing shows on the station yet (PR 4), and the switch stays
off until the owner's admin step below.

- **The function** [`gmail-count`](../../supabase/functions/gmail-count/index.ts) ([its section](../EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md#gmail-count)):
  for each box on `email_box_watch` that is on and each label the row names, it reads Gmail as that
  mailbox under the service account's domain-wide delegation with the metadata-only scope and keeps
  `email_box_threads`, `email_box_days` and the watch row's health and `labels_state`. One sweep of a
  label: `labels.list` finds the label (a missing one is written as such and the others still count),
  `labels.get` gives the exact count, `threads.list` lists up to three pages of 100, the new threads are
  read whole with `threads.get` format=metadata (oldest first, at most 100 a box a sweep across its
  labels, the rest next time), the still
  ones keep their last-seen, the gone ones are marked on a complete listing, and the day's trend row is
  written. Each label is its own try; a 429 stops the box until the next sweep. It writes nothing to
  Gmail.
- **Two doors**: the cron every 15 minutes with `X-Cron-Secret` while `email_count_on_v1` is on, and
  **Read again** from the station, a signed-in office member's JWT with `{ watch_id }`, swept now when
  `email_box_can_see` says they may and the box was not swept in the last minute.
- **The Gmail helper** [`_shared/gmail.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/gmail.ts): `GMAIL_METADATA_SCOPES`
  and four read-only calls, `gmailLabels`, `gmailLabelCounts`, `gmailThreadsByLabel`, `gmailThread`.
- **The mirrored kernels**: `scripts/edge-kernels.mjs` adds `lib/emailIn/countedBoxes.ts` and
  `lib/emailIn/labelSweep.ts` and the importer `gmail-count`; `npm run gen:edge-kernels` wrote 119
  files and the index. `config.toml` keeps `verify_jwt = false`; the dev-mcp catalog names the function.

## The admin step, the owner's

The counter asks Google for `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.metadata`, which the delegation row
does not hold yet. As the Workspace admin: admin.google.com → **Security** → **Access and data
control** → **API controls** → **Manage domain-wide delegation** → the service account's client (the
one `gmail-sync` uses, `docs/DRIVE_INTAKE_SETUP.md`) → **Edit** → add the scope beside the two Gmail
scopes already there → **Authorize**. Until then every box fails at the token exchange with Google's
`unauthorized_client`, written to the box's `last_error`, and nothing is read.

## Is this the best we can do?

- The scope makes the "never the words" promise Google's, not the code's. The price is one admin step,
  named here and in the to-do, and the function says so in its own error until it is done.
- The fetch budget (100 threads a box a sweep, oldest first) keeps a first sweep of a busy box inside
  Gmail's minute per mailbox without a sleep, and the stuck threads are read first; a 4,000-thread Inbox reaches its 300 listed rows in three sweeps and its count is
  exact from the first.
- Not verified live: no mailbox can be read under the new scope until the admin step, so this PR is
  checked by the kernels' tests, the mirror's test and CI's `deno check`; the first live sweep is the
  owner's switch after his step.
