# v2.3518 — What customers see: the firm's confirmed page renders, and captions in office words (2026-09-16)

Follow-up to the train (v2.3505–v2.3513) after Grace's first look at the Collections law firm strip. Client only.

## What was wrong

- **The Confirmed page card showed the page's source** as white monospace on black. The step loaded the function's own URL (`legal-notify-dispatch?confirm=sample`) in the frame, and the platform serves that response as `content-type: text/plain` with `nosniff` even though the function sets `text/html` — a JSON function's `application/json` passes through, the HTML one does not. **This means the firm's real confirm and unsubscribe pages have rendered as raw HTML for recipients since the legal train shipped (v2.3405+); the tab only made it visible.** Recorded as a residual in `to-dos/what-customers-see-residuals.md` — the fix is to serve those two pages from the app (a small public route that posts the token to the function), not from the function.
- **Captions named functions and routes** ("submit-legal-portal — …", "/contract/sign — …") in a tab that has been open to the office since v2.3507.

## What shipped

- **A `html` render kind** in `customerJourneys.ts`: a plain page built in the browser from the same builder the function serves it with. `buildSampleHtml('legal-confirmed-page')` in `customerSampleEmails.ts` runs `legalPageHtml(legalConfirmedPageBody(…))` for Ann Sample; the tab frames it as `srcDoc`. The `absolute` page path from v2.3512 stays available on the type but nothing uses it now.
- **Every caption in office words**: "Jobs → Contract sweep → Send", "the link in that email", "sent by the app when an agreement sits unsigned a few days", "Bids → Submittals → Share — the one link the GC forwards to the architect", "sent when the firm adds a person on its portal — nothing else goes out until they confirm", and so on. The technical names live where developers look: `customerSurfaceRegistry.ts`. A journeys test now fails on a function folder name or a route path in any caption.

## Verified

Typecheck, lint, build; journeys (6), registry, sample-email, help and release-note tests. Live: the Confirmed page card shows *You're confirmed, Ann Sample.* in the frame; every caption reads as office words; the count line is unchanged at 32 rendered live.
