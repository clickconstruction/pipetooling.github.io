# v2.7892 — Lien desk: voiding a filed lien puts its paper back (2026-11-18)

**Back from the clerk** (v2.7856) records a filed lien the moment its document number is typed, which makes a lien recorded by mistake more likely. The way to take one back was broken in an older place. The owner picked the fix on 2026-11-18, between two: the database puts the paper back, or the Board reads a stuck paper differently. This is the first.

## Before

Measured on 2026-11-18 on the ZZ test job as the dev, with the app's own writes, before anything was changed:

- Recording a filed affidavit inserts a `job_lien_filings` row, and `syncLienDeskAfterRecord` marks the job's affidavit paper in `job_lien_desk_items` as `sent`, tied to the filing (`sent_filing_id`, `sent_at`).
- The Lien window's **Void** (`voidFiling` in `LienFilingTabs.tsx`) set `voided_at` on the filing and nothing else. Read back after the void: the filing voided, no live filed affidavit on the job, the paper still `sent` and still tied to the voided filing.
- That paper through the Board's own kernels (`buildLienAffidavitQueue`, then `buildLienBoard`): pile `filed`, bin **Served**. `pileFor` answers `filed` for a `sent` paper whatever the filings say.
- A second record on the job never reached the paper, since `syncLienDeskAfterRecord` only looks at papers that are approved, drafted, awaiting or held.
- Moving the paper back by hand was refused for an assistant when the leader had approved it with his own press. `job_lien_desk_items_guard` read any move to `approved` as a new approval. Read from the function, and now pinned by the bed.
- The live desk had no stuck paper: 16 affidavit papers, 11 sent, each tied to a live filing. So nothing old moves.

## What changed

- **Migration [`20261119020000_lien_void_puts_paper_back.sql`](../migrations/20261119020000_lien_void_puts_paper_back.md).** A trigger on `job_lien_filings` fires when an affidavit filing goes from live to voided. Every live affidavit paper that is sent and tied to it goes back in the same transaction: to `approved` when it carries an approval, which is left exactly as it was, else to `drafted`. Its tie and its sent day are cleared. When a newer live paper is already on the job, the old one is set aside. The guard is restated with one block: for an affidavit paper, a move from `sent` back to `approved` is no new approval while the filing it was tied to is voided and the approval is untouched.
- **[`src/lib/jobs/lienFilingVoid.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienFilingVoid.ts)**, pure, with its unit tests. `lienVoidPaperOutcome` turns the papers tied to the filing before the void, and their rows after it, into one of five answers: `approved`, `drafted`, `set_aside`, `still_sent`, `none`. `lienVoidPaperWords` and `lienTakenBackWords` are what the two windows say.
- **[`src/lib/jobs/lienFilingVoidIo.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienFilingVoidIo.ts).** `voidLienFiling` is the one writer both windows use: a read of the tied papers, the filing's `voided_at`, a read of where the papers went. The client writes nothing to the paper. A read that fails never fails the void.
- **The Lien window.** `voidFiling` calls `voidLienFiling` and its toast says where the paper went. A paper that did not move is said as an error: *Its paper still reads as filed on the Lien desk. Tell your lead.*
- **Back from the clerk.** A recorded line has **Take it back** in place of *Open its Lien window ›*. The first press asks, with **Confirm take back** and **Keep it**. The second voids that line's filing, the line opens again with an empty box, and the desk reads again on the way out even when the only record was taken back.
- **The guide** *file a lien and never miss its deadlines*: the sentence that said the paper does not go back by itself is replaced, and the Lien window's Void has a paragraph.

## Where the paper lands

- Approved before: approved again, so the Board shows **Ready to file**, or **Held** when a fact holds it.
- Its last day to file has passed: the Board shows it as a lien day that passed, until the lien is recorded again. That is `pileFor`'s rule for any lien not on file.
- A paper the leader was holding when the lien was recorded goes back to approved, not to his hold. The status a paper had before it was sent is not kept.

## Order

The client reads only names that exist today, so it is safe before the push. Until the migration is pushed a void behaves as before, and both windows say the paper still reads as filed. After the push the same client says it is back.

## Not in this change

Voiding a § 53.056 notice's record leaves its paper as sent in the same way. That was not measured and the trigger is for affidavits only.
