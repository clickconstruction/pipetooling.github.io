## What changed

- **`legal-portal`** (`supabase/functions/legal-portal/index.ts`): one more read, the matters' live `legal_matter_claim_lines` (`MATTER_COUNSEL_SELECT.claimLines`, fail-soft), sent on each matter as `claimLines`; `shapeMatterForCounsel` cuts each to `MATTER_CLAIM_LINE_COLUMNS` (`id · kind · title · amount · basis · document_id · added_at`, [`_shared/legalMatterShape.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/legalMatterShape.ts)), never who typed it; a line whose Evidence document the office held back travels with no document id. **Redeploy required.**
- **`legalPortalPayload.ts`**: `LegalPortalMatter.claimLines` (`parseLegalPortalClaimLines`, [] from an older function); `buildMatterPacket` hands them to the kernel as `claimLines`, each document's title read off the documents that travelled, so the firm's `worth.claim`, its fee, its net and its paths read the office's figure.
- **`legalMoney.ts`**: `firmDemand`'s first argument is the claim (the firm's callers pass `worth.claim`; the balance before), and `firmDemandWords` says what the demand is made of — *total demand · the claim $16,240.00, open bills $14,400.00 inside it + fees and costs $450.00*, or *balance $14,400.00* when nothing reaches past the bills.
- **`LegalFirmMatterView.tsx`**: `FirmClaimTable` under the statement's *Balance owed* on the firm's Account tab — the same rows `claimTable` draws for the desk, *fills in* on the two read rows, the Evidence cell a 15-minute link when the document travelled, the total row, and one line on what the table is. The column the desk calls *Basis* is *Grounds* on the portal, which keeps *Basis* for the office (punch list #85 item 4). The matter card's demand reads the claim.
- **`legalFirmPacketPrint.ts`**: *The claim* under Section A between the jobs and the statement, the claim beside the balance in the money block when it reaches past the bills, the demand from the claim.
- **Tests**: `legalMatterShape.test.ts` (the columns, no secret), `legalPortalPayload.test.ts` (the parse, the worth through the kernel, a held document not named), `legalMoney.test.ts` (the words), `legalFirmPacketPrint.test.ts` (the figure and the table's place), `LegalPortal.sample.render.test.tsx` (the demand's words on the sample matter).
