# v2.3844 — The retainage notice's cover is now a letter in counsel's form (2026-09-25)

The owner, 2026-09-25, after v2.3828 put counsel's cover letter on every § 53.056 notice: *"Update the retainage notice to use counsel's wording too."* Client only, no migration.

## What counsel wrote

Counsel's memo of 2026-09-22 (`docs/COUNSEL_MEMO_GC_ON_NOTICE_2026-09-22.md`) has **no retainage letter text**. It gives rules: *"Cover letter on a retainage notice follows the same rules: own page, no joint check, no GC smear, copy the GC. Ask the owner to hold the reserved 10% and Click's subcontract retainage and not release either to the GC. If the packet is § 53.057-only, say the § 53.081(c) withhold starts when they receive a copy of the affidavit."* It also says: *"Check each job for a bond before you tell an owner to hold 10%."*

The app printed `lienRetainageCoverNote`, one paragraph that followed those rules but ended with the routine note's *"It is not a claim that you are in default"*, a sentence counsel's letters never use. The emailed owner's copy carried no retainage cover at all, because `emailNoticePdf` attaches only `coverLetter`.

## The change

- **`lienRetainageCoverLetter`** (`lienNoticeDraft.ts`) replaces `lienRetainageCoverNote`. It is a letter in the § 53.056 letters' form and sentences:
  - *To the owner of {property}* and *This page is a cover letter. The enclosed Notice of Claim for Unpaid Retainage is given under Texas Property Code § 53.057.*
  - *We are the plumbing contractor on your project, working under {GC}. Under our subcontract, {GC} held back {amount} of our pay as retainage. Now that {our work is complete / our subcontract has been terminated / abandoned}, that retainage has not been paid.*
  - *You did not hire us, and this is not a lawsuit…*
  - The two pots, held and not released: the § 53.101 10% and our retainage. With `paymentBond = 'yes'` the 10% line is dropped and the letter says so.
  - The withhold rule: § 53.081 when a § 53.056 notice already named the retainage, § 53.081(c) and the affidavit copy when it did not.
  - No joint check, payable only to us, release the day it clears.
  - *A copy of this letter and the notice is going to {GC}*, then *Call {signer} at {phone}*.
- **The run** (`buildLienRetainageRun`) sets `coverNote: null` and puts the letter in `coverLetter`, so the printed packet, the emailed PDF (new) and the Enclosed line (*Notice of claim for unpaid retainage (Tex. Prop. Code § 53.057)*) all carry it. It now takes `signerPhoneFor`: the signer's own phone, else the letterhead's.
- **The retainage pane** previews the same letter, labelled *cover letter (counsel's retainage rules, 2026-09-22)*.
- **Docs**: *send lien notices from the Lien desk* → the retainage section describes the letter. Counsel is asked to read it, especially the bond sentence (`to-dos/owner-decisions-pending.md`).
