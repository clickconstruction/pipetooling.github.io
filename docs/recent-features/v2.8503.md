## What a GC sees

On the customer portal, under each job they are the GC on that requires a paper built from settings, a block titled **How we bind everyone we bring onto this job**:

- **The level** the job's settings come to, as a chip, with the kind of place it is aligned with. Settings that match no level read *A mix of its own*.
- **Each topic**, with the choice the office picked and that choice's plain words from the Contract Book's settings list.
- **How many have signed**, such as *3 of 9 people have signed*, with a meter and the line *Nobody opens this job in <company>'s system until they sign.*
- **Each sub business**, with *Signed <day>* once all of its people have signed, and *Not yet* until then. A business with more than one person says how many of them signed.
- **The small print**: whose paper it is, and that it does not change the agreement between the company and the GC.

Nothing on the block is a press. It stays off a printed statement.

## Choices worth knowing

- **Its title is not *Secrecy*.** The Document builder's block on the same page already reads *Secrecy on your jobs* (#404). That one shows a builder the office sent to look at, tied to no job's paper. This one is the job's real paper and its signatures. The title is the one on the card's mock-up.
- **The count is the office's roster.** The office, the crew and the subs are all in the total, as on the office's own table. Both read one function, so they cannot disagree.
- **No worker is named** (the owner's call of 2026-11-17, settled 4). Only a person who comes to the job as a sub has a line, under the trade company on their file. People of one company fold into one line.
- **A sub with no company on file shows under their own roster name.** A sub's roster name is the name they trade under. This is the one place a person's name can show, and it is the owner's to change.
- **The office cannot yet set what a GC sees.** Settled 4 also lets a job show the count only, or every signer by name. That needs a setting on the job and a press on the office's page, and is not in this change. Every job shows the count and the businesses.
- **Only a paper built from settings shows.** A paper in prose or a form on the same job is not listed.
- **English only**, as the customer's statement page is.
