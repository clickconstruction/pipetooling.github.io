## The move

`loadTeamReviewUnion` — the one read that fetches the whole team's period and lifetime rows and shapes them into a `TeamReviewUnion` — was a 406-line function inside `PeopleReviewTab`. It read no state and wrote none; the only thing it took from the component was the `users` prop, by closure, for the Wheels fuel exclusion.

- **`src/lib/people/loadTeamReviewUnion.ts`** — the function, verbatim, one indent level out. `users` is its last parameter (`ReadonlyArray<{ id, name }>`, what `loadWheelsSnapshot` already asked for).
- **`PeopleReviewTab.tsx`** — imports it; the two callers (the prior-period effect and `loadTeamSummaryData`) pass `users`; fourteen imports only the loader used are gone. 4,034 → 3,610 lines.

## The tests — the loader's first

`loadTeamReviewUnion.test.ts` runs it against a mocked client (15 tests):

- **What it asks for** — period reads carry both ends and lifetime reads open two years back, or at the period's start when that is older; bid sessions only when no office job is set; no ledger, invoice or bid RPC when the period names no job; the paid-only RPC when asked.
- **What the union carries** — a hand-built week: crew hours by Convention 1, the sheet costed by the Jobs page's rule and keyed by its link, tally parts, invoices, billed materials, card charges as cost, the statuses beside the ledger rows, the settings defaults, a null bid day as an empty list; pay config found by person id before name; overhead sessions bucketed by person, with rejected, revoked, unapproved, open, field and nameless sessions left out.
- **Wheels** — a fuel purchase by a person with a vehicle deal leaves the job; it stays when the buyer is not among the `users` handed in, when it was not a card purchase, and when Wheels cannot be read; an Internal Transfer drops by the one card rule.
- **A failed read** throws — never an empty union.
