# v2.3543 — Submittals stage 5b: the drop zone and "entered by" (2026-09-17)

Client + one migration (`20260917010000_submittal_reviewer_files_entered.sql`); no edge function. PR 5b of [`to-dos/submittals/STAGES-5-6-BUILD.md`](../../to-dos/submittals/STAGES-5-6-BUILD.md).

## Why

Some architects never open the room: they mark up the PDF or answer in the GC's email chain. Their calls still have to land on the rows, with the record saying whose call it was and who typed it — and nothing about the office's typing may leak onto the room.

## What shipped

- **Schema** ([migration doc](../migrations/20260917010000_submittal_reviewer_files_entered.md)): `bid_submittals.reviewer_files` (jsonb; objects under `<bid>/<rev>/reviewer/` in the `bid-submittals` bucket) and on `bid_submittal_items` `decision_source` (room · entered · robot, default room), `decision_entered_by`, `decision_entered_by_name`. No policy change.
- **The drop zone** — on the Submittals tab, beside *Drop a vendor PDF* on a shared revision: **Drop a reviewer's file** (`input[aria-label="Reviewer's file"]`; a PDF is a redline, .eml / .msg / .txt / .html a forwarded email — `reviewerFileKind`). The file card under the sheet strip on the room's blue tint lists each file (*Dana Whitfield's redlined PDF · dropped Sep 17 by Wendi*, `describeReviewerFile`), with **Open the file** (a 300 s signed link) and **Remove this file**, and reads *N rows entered by hand on this revision*. When the room has exactly one deciding person the file is attributed to them; a `file_dropped` event goes on the room. The room shows none of it.
- **Entered on their behalf** — the row editor (`SubmittalItemEditDialog`) gains *Their call · on behalf of a reviewer* on a shared revision: a picker of the room's people or *a reviewer not on the room…* (name · email · role → a new `bid_submittal_people` row, `how = named`, a personal token), Approve / Revise / Reject and their note. Save writes `enteredDecisionPatch` (the reviewer as `reviewed_by_*`, `decision_source = entered`, the office user as `decision_entered_by`), posts a system line on the thread that never names staff (*from Dana Whitfield's file, entered by the office · 1 row · 1 revise*, `enteredEntryBody`) and a `decided` event with `entered: true`, so `personTrail` counts it. An entered call can be cleared from the same place (`CLEAR_DECISION_PATCH`). A bid with no room yet gets one minted silently (nothing is shared by that).
- **The words** — the Their call column and the decisions line read *Revise · Dana Whitfield · entered by Wendi · Sep 17* and *… · 2 entered by Wendi* (`enteredSuffix`, `summarizeDecisions.entered / enteredBy`, `describeDecisions`, `decisionsAsText`). The Needs You "rows sent back" card already reads `review_decision`, so entered calls count like room ones.
- Kernels: `src/lib/submittals/reviewerFiles.ts` (+4 tests), `enteredDecisions.ts` (+2), `reviewDecisions.ts` (+1). Smokes: the editor's on-behalf section (4), the tab.
- Guide: *build a submittal package* → **A reviewer who marked up the PDF**.
