# v2.7421 — Lien desk: the He already okayed it menu opens whole (2026-11-15)

Client only. Taunya's find of 2026-11-15, with a picture: on a notice on By paper, *He already okayed it ▾* opened a menu whose left side was cut off.

## What was wrong

`LienFootMenu` pinned its menu to the press's right edge (`right: 0`) and let it grow to the left. The menu is 260 px wide and the press 160. When the compact signing box (v2.7386) made that press the first thing in the footer, the menu began 64 px left of the paper's pane, and the pane (`data-lien-desk-pane-column`, `overflow: hidden`) cut that part off. Measured at 1440 px: menu 280 to 540, pane from 344.

## What landed

- `src/lib/jobs/lienFootMenuPlace.ts` (new): `lienFootMenuPlace({ press, menu, bounds })` puts the menu flush with its press's left edge and pulls it inside `bounds` on either side, 8 px from the edge. It opens over the press, and under it only when it does not fit over and there is more room under. `lienFootMenuBounds(screen, cuts)` is the screen cut down by every box that cuts its contents.
- `LienFootMenu.tsx`: the menu is measured as it opens, in a layout effect, so it is placed before it is painted. The bounds are every ancestor whose overflow is `hidden`, `clip`, `auto` or `scroll`, and the screen. It is placed again when the window changes size. Its width, rows, keys and acts are what they were.

All three hosts get it: the notice footer in `LienDesk.tsx`, `LienDeskAffidavitPane.tsx` and `LienDeskRetainagePane.tsx`. None of them changed.

## Verified

`lienFootMenuPlace.test.ts` (her case by its measured boxes, a press at the right end, a phone, over and under), `LienFootMenu.render.test.tsx` (new: the menu's place from mocked boxes, a row still runs its act, Escape shuts it), `LienDesk.render.test.tsx`; eslint on the touched files; typecheck.

On the dev server, read only, no row of a menu pressed: both menus on a notice, and the menu on an affidavit and a retainage notice, opened whole at 1440, 1280, 1024, 768 and 375 px wide. Every corner of each menu hit-tested as the menu itself.
