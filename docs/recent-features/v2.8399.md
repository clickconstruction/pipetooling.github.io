# v2.8399 — Job Summary: flip through a job's supply house invoices in place (2026-11-23)

Punch list #431, PR 2 of seven. The owner: "I don't love that every time I click on one it brings me to a new tab. I would like to be able to flip through them faster." This PR is the viewer: a bill drawn over the list it came from, with the list beside the paper. The door it reads a PDF through, `drive-link`'s `read_bill`, shipped ahead as v2.8396. No migration, and no edge function in this PR.

## What was there

An invoice number opened the bill's Google Drive link in a new tab (v2.8391 left that as it was). Reading ten bills meant ten tabs, and the app had no way to draw a bill's PDF itself: the bills sit in one folder of an office login's My Drive, so a browser can read one only through a Google sign-in.

## What changed

- **The bill viewer**, `src/components/materials/BillViewer.tsx`, over the rules in `src/lib/materials/bills/billViewer.ts` and the reads in `billViewerIo.ts`. For the office, an invoice number on the Job Summary's list opens the bill over the page, in a portal on the body so no row around the list clips it.
  - **The list beside the paper.** On a desk the job's list sits at the left, the bill on show marked. A press opens any bill. The list's own search box is there too.
  - **Previous and Next**, the right and left arrow keys, and Escape. They follow the list as it is searched or narrowed to a house. A bill the search has left behind reads *not in the search*, and Next goes to the first bill left. With the cursor in the box the side arrows move the cursor and the up and down arrows flip. The keys are taken in the capture phase, so Escape closes the viewer and not a window under it.
  - **The paper** (`BillPdfPages.tsx`): pdf.js, loaded on first use, draws the bill's pages one under the other at the pane's width. One PDF often holds several bills, so the viewer draws the pages the reader found for this bill (`supply_papers.page_from` and `page_to`, carried through `paperIo`), says *This bill is page 4 of a 12 page file.* and offers **See the whole file**. A long file draws six pages and holds the rest behind a press. A picture is drawn as a picture.
  - **The facts** beside the paper, one a line: supply house, invoice, date, PO, bill total, what is on this job, the lines read (`rowLinesWords`) and whether it is paid.
  - **Open in Drive** is the way out to a tab of its own.
  - **The share press** copies the same link as the list's.
  - **A bill with no PDF** says so and offers **Open the bill** (its Edit window on Materials → Supply houses) and the supplier's site. **A file the app cannot open** says why in the door's own words, offers **Try again** when the ask may go again, and still offers Drive.
  - **A phone** gets the viewer as the whole screen, Previous and Next at the bottom at 44 px, the facts under the paper.
  - **The next bill's paper is asked for ahead**, and a file's bytes are kept by the file, so a scanned stack behind nineteen bills is read once.
- **The bytes** of a Drive-linked bill come from `drive-link`'s `read_bill` (v2.8396), which reads the bill's row as the caller and reads only a file inside the bills folder. A bill dropped on the Add invoice window is read from the app's own copy in the bucket, under the office's read.
- **A bill's link is a page of the app.** `/files/bills/<invoice id>` used to go to the file. It now draws the viewer (`src/pages/BillOpen.tsx`, loaded when a bill's link is opened). With `?job=` and the bill on that job, the job's list is beside the paper and the address follows the bill on show. A reader the policy does not let read bills sees *This file is not one you can open.* and no amounts. A signed-out reader comes back to the bill and its job after the sign-in, since the return address now keeps what follows the path.
- **Every bill has a share press** for the office now, a bill with no PDF among them: the link has the viewer to open.
- **Anyone else** who sees the list (a primary, a helper or a sub on the job) keeps the bill's Drive link in a tab of its own.
- **The old table is gone** from `JobsJobSummaryTab.tsx`. v2.8391 left it in place, unreached, to stay off lines another open PR was editing. `renderJobSummarySupplyInvoices` is now the one function: the loading and empty words, then the list.

## Looked at

In a headless browser on a throwaway page: the real list and viewer, a stand-in for the reads, and the repo's made-up fixture PDFs drawn by pdf.js. At 1,440, 1,000 and 375 px, light and dark.

- A bill opened from the list with its paper, its facts and *1 of 20*.
- The right arrow moved one bill. A one-page bill inside a three-page file drew one page and said *This bill is page 1 of a 3 page file.*, and **See the whole file** drew three.
- *cedar* typed in the viewer's box left three bills, the count read *not in the search*, and Next went to the first of them. The down arrow flipped from inside the box.
- The share press put the bill's link with its job on the clipboard.
- A file outside the bills folder read *The app cannot open this file.* with the door's sentence and **Open in Drive**. A bill with no PDF offered **Open the bill** and the supplier's site.
- Escape closed the viewer and the list kept the search.
- On the phone the viewer filled the screen, Next was 44 px tall, and nothing scrolled sideways.

The look found two things, both fixed here: a dead Previous press mixed two border styles, and the box beside the paper cut its own hint short.

Not walked on the live Job Summary, and not against the real Drive. The first bill a dev opens after this deploys is that check.
