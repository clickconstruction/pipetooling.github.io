# v2.7137 — The Bridge on a phone: a name on What to do now is easy to press again (punch list #334) (2026-11-13)

The final walk of the Bridge train (card #334, `to-dos/bridge-pulse/`) on the live site found one fault of the train's own. On a phone each line of *What to do now* leads with its name, and the name is a press: a customer opens the Collect window on that customer, a job opens its page, a supply house opens on its due day. PR 8 ([v2.7109](v2.7109.md)) drew that name as text alone, 16 px tall at 375 px wide. The card it replaced gave the same press at least 36 px, and the row's other presses are 44 px.

Client only. No migration, no edge function, and no kernel that is copied to the edge functions changed.

- **The pay date window does not close with Escape.** *Write down their word* closes by its own *Cancel* alone. The other windows on the page close with Escape. The window is the app's own and is used beyond the Bridge.
