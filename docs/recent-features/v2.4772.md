# v2.4772 — GC mode: the schedule’s rules move into the app (2026-10-07)

PR 1a of the schedule's real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/SCHEDULE_REAL_BUILD.md`; its plan `to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/schedule-pr1a.md`, both on branch `spike/gc-mode`): the schedule, the chart and moving, lifted from the prototype word for word. No database, no screen.

- `src/lib/gc/schedule/`: 148 of the 174 functions in the prototype's 14 schedule kernels, each word for word with the private helpers it needs: `schedule.ts` (the items, the plan to date, pushes, the finish, milestones, the look-ahead, the summary), `draft.ts` (the first draft from the stages), `gantt.ts`, `splitBars.ts`, `moves.ts`, `baseline.ts`, `changeOrderDays.ts`, `addedActivity.ts`, `actualDates.ts`, `places.ts`, `whatIf.ts`, `pullEarlier.ts`, `recovery.ts`.
- What they read from other lanes lands under those lanes' names, for their own lifts to extend: `bids.ts` (the number we carry), `building.ts` (day math, our crew's stages, a pay application sent back), `ownerBilling.ts` (a change order's days), `lookups.ts`, `words.ts` (dates in words), and `exclusionCoversTotal` added to `exclusions.ts`.
- Types: `schedule/types.ts` holds the schedule's own shapes word for word. `types.ts` gains the job as these kernels read it, under the prototype's names with only the fields they read (`GcState`, `GcProject`, `TradePackage`, the bid, statement of work, draw and change order shapes).
- The 26 functions that read another lane stay in the prototype until their step: the chart's holds and the trades' papers (`gcChartHolds`, `gcNotReady`), the pull and recovery offers (PR 9), the crowded weeks (PR 1b, with crew counts), and the what-if's billing line and kept words.
- Tests: 52 of the prototype's tests moved as they are, on `schedule/testState.ts` (the made-up data cut to what the kernels read, written by `to-dos/gc-mode/scripts/schedule-test-state.ts` on the spike). 33 direct tests cover what the moved tests do not call. The 153 tests that play the prototype's reducer or read another lane stay on the spike, where they run against this copy once the spike reads it.
- `to-dos/gc-mode/scripts/lift-same.cjs` on the spike checks every moved declaration and test against the spike's, word for word.
