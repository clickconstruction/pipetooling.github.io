# v2.4309 — Edit Job → Bill: each bill reads on one line, and its waiver row waits its turn (2026-10-01)

No migration, no edge function. PR B of Grace's Bill tab redesign (PR A is [v2.4307](v2.4307.md), the money card and Make a bill); board *KeepsEverything* on https://claude.ai/artifact/8Tfh1JZydNQmPALe9HwmD8. Her one change to the approved board, on the waiver row: *"instead of having to start on the left, start on the right so it is held to the right."* Her must-keep: the waiver options stay in view on every GC bill.

## Why calm

The Bill tab drew *Conditional · none — send it* in amber on every open bill of every GC job. Read against prod on 2026-10-01: 43 billed GC jobs, 3 of them had ever had a lien waiver, 2 of 12 GCs. An amber reminder on every bill teaches the office to ignore amber, so the Bill tab now keeps it for a waiver that is under way.

## What changed

- **Kernel** [`lienWaiverCell.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienWaiverCell.ts): `lienWaiverCellForBill(…, { calm: true })` reads a bill with no waiver started in grey — *Conditional · not added*, *Unconditional · when paid* (open) or *not added* (settled) — and keeps amber for a waiver under way (*✓ signed · send it*, *awaiting signature*, *Unconditional owed · settled* after a sent conditional). The cell gains `underWay` and `nextIsOwed` (sign it, send it, or the unconditional after a conditional). Without `calm` nothing changes: GC Review still marks a bill with no conditional amber, *the move before the call*. +3 kernel cases.
- **[`JobFormInvoiceList`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormInvoiceList.tsx)**: the waiver chips, the door and the bill's ⋯ sit on their own line held to the right (`.jobInvoiceWaivers`); a bill with no waiver row keeps its ⋯ on the head line. The door is amber only when `nextIsOwed`. **Record payment** follows the status words in the row's order: on a wide ledger (container ≥ 421 px) the words run as one line and the button sits at their end; under 420 px the stacked phone layout is unchanged and Record payment stays the last full-width button. Text · Copy link · Email · QR and View are unchanged. A touch screen (`pointer: coarse`, e.g. an iPad at the window's full 560 px) gets 44 px Record payment, Send bill…, the waiver door, View, the chase cluster and a 44 × 44 ⋯ — the other session's iPad fix for the bill row. +2 render cases (the ⋯ at the end of the waiver row on a GC bill and on the head line otherwise; Record payment after the words).
- **Guide** *send a GC our lien waiver*: the chips' place and colors on the Bill tab, how GC Review differs, and the Bill tab recording re-taken (`send-a-gc-our-lien-waiver-billtab.gif`, read-only on job 650 as the dev account: the bill rows, a hover on *Send it ›*, the Release of Lien window opened and nothing edited — the job's five release rows were read before and after and are unchanged).
