# v2.6534 — The Scoreboard prints a cost summary for the whole development (2026-11-04)

The owner, 2026-11-04, after a site's costs had been summed by hand outside the app for the owner's own question: *what would it take to create something in the app that has the ability to export a summary like this in the form of a PDF for jobs or developments?* A job already had one, the Costs tab's **Print** (v2.5749). A development had the Scoreboard's figures on the screen and the Timeline's chart on paper (v2.6499), but no page that put the board's numbers and the rows behind them together. This is that page, for a dev or a controller.

## What changed

- **Print cost summary** under the Scoreboard (`DevelopmentScoreboard.tsx`'s `print` prop, drawn under the legend on the desk and under the cards on a phone, for a reader `canSeeSiteCosts` lets in). A press opens the page in a print window through `openHtmlPrintWindow`, where the print dialog prints it or saves it as a PDF, the way the Timeline prints. While the costs are still reading the press says so and does nothing; a blocked window says to allow pop-ups.
- **The kernel** `src/lib/developments/costSummaryPrint.ts`: `buildCostSummary` takes the Scoreboard model, the Costs column's read (`SiteCostsRead` with its rows), the clock's read and the overhead read, and returns one plain shape; `costSummaryPrintHtml` draws it. The sections, in order: the head (the site's name and the board's two lines), the nine tiles, a row per job with money in the board's order (the state in words: status, a working job's percent, the board's flags), *Who put it in* (the clock's people summed over the site, the subs by the names on their sheets), *Parts by bucket* (supply-house invoices, the card charges the total counts, the tally, the other charges, with row counts), *Card charges by payee* (the counted rows under the one card rule, largest first, the tail past `COST_SUMMARY_PAYEE_LINES` folded into one *Everything else* line that keeps its count and dollars), the supply-house invoices by date with the allocated share and paid or not, every other charge with its day and words (a description starting ESTIMATE wears the Costs tab's *Estimate* chip, and the notes give the estimates' subtotal), *What the total leaves out* (the internal transfers and the charges also on a supply-house invoice, with their rows and dollars), the notes (a read that failed or was still out, days not known to this reader, the overhead rule with the day the pool began, the earned rule) and the printed stamp. A read that is not in draws a dash in its cells, never a zero. The page is letter portrait with literal colours under `data-theme="light"`, as the other print documents.
- Tests: `costSummaryPrint.test.ts` (the buckets against the Costs column's figures, the payee grouping and its fold, what is left out, the estimates, the notes, the page's escaping and dashes); the Scoreboard render smoke gains the press and its note.
- Docs: `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` (the Development page's Money look); guide `see-everything-on-a-development` (*Print the cost summary*).

## Test

`npx vitest run src/lib/developments/costSummaryPrint.test.ts src/components/developments/DevelopmentScoreboard.render.test.tsx src/pages/DevelopmentPage.timeline.render.test.tsx`; `npm run typecheck`; `npm run lint`. By hand on the dev server as a dev: open a development with money on it, Money → Scoreboard, press **Print cost summary**; the print preview shows the tiles, the jobs, the payees and the stamp. A site of twenty jobs and a thousand card rows printed to a PDF comes to four letter pages with no column clipped.
