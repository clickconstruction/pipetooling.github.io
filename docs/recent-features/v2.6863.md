# v2.6863 — The Away group on People → Users is drawn as roster rows (2026-11-10)

The owner sent a screenshot of People → Users → **Away (1)** on 2026-11-10 and asked how the section could look better. The roster above it is full-width borderless rows under small headings; Away was a boxed card half the page wide with a table. *Left* and *Back* were said in the header and again in the cell, the day back wrapped to four lines, *alert on* explained nothing, three buttons stacked with the rare one in solid blue, and the note the person reads was not shown. A mock-up drew three directions with made-up people at desktop and phone width; he picked **C**. No migration, nothing to deploy.

## What changed

- **Kernel `src/lib/people/awaySection.ts`** (tests in `awaySection.test.ts`): `awayWhenWords` writes the row's one line of days, *Left Nov 3 · back Nov 8, 2029, in 3 years*, from `awayShortDate` (the year left off in the year we are in) and `awayDistanceWords` (*tomorrow*, days up to 13, weeks up to 8, months up to 23, years after; the same steps behind today). After the day it reads *due back Nov 7, 3 days ago*, on it *back today*, without one *no day back set*. `awayNoteFirstLine` takes the first line of the note with words on it. `awayGroupCount` is the heading's count (*4 · 1 due back*). `awayDoorPlace` says where Bring back… or Restore sits: the ⋯ menu, or the row once the day back is here. `awayWhenTitle` keeps the long day and the count of days for the hover. `awayRestoreConfirm` is the window Restore asks through. `AwayRow` gains `when` and `noteLine`; `AwaySectionNote` gains `words`.
- **`src/components/people/UsersTabAwayGroup.tsx`** (render smoke beside it): the group. Wide: a heading in the roster's style with *Their sign-in opens to the note you left.* where the other groups carry Add, then one `li` per person in the roster row's own metrics. Line one is a quiet dot (amber when due), the name through `PersonNameDoor`, the role as the roster's chip, the days, and a bell whose title says what the alert does. Line two, where an active row lists its projects, reads **They read:** with the note's first line and ends in **See what they see**. The ⋯ menu holds **Open desk**, **Edit the note…** and the door; when `awayDoorPlace` answers `row` the door is an amber press on the row and leaves the menu. Phone: one card like the kind cards, each row an avatar with a ring, the name and role, the days, the note, and the three presses behind the left swipe; the door sits under the row when due.
- **`src/components/people/useSwipeRow.ts`**: the left-swipe drag lifted out of `UsersTabPhoneRow` unchanged, so both rows slide the same way. `UsersTabRow`'s chip is exported as `UsersTabChip`.
- **`PeopleUsersTab`**: the boxed table is replaced by the group; the notes read adds `words`; Restore from an Away row asks first (`useConfirmDialog`, the button reads **Restore**), where it used to act on one press. The row shares the directory's one open swipe.
- Guide *leave a note for a teammate who is away*: *Where they are listed* rewritten, with *On the day back* and *On a phone*. `GLOSSARY.md` → *Away note*, `PEOPLE_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`, `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` and `docs/twins/APP_DIRECTORY.md` amended where they describe the row.

## Decisions

- **C over A (one strict line) and B (the card kept).** On one line the note gets what room is left, about forty letters on a laptop and none on a narrow window; the roster's second line gives it the width and is not a new shape. B fixed the words and the buttons and still read as another page under the roster.
- **The door follows the day.** A button that is always on the row is too loud for three years or too quiet on the day. In the menu until the day back, on the row from then.
- **The chip says the role, not *away*.** The heading says Away, and the group mixes the roster's groups.
- **The email left the row.** It is on the desk, one press on the name; the room went to the note.
- **Nobody away draws nothing**, as before. A standing *Away 0* heading would sit on the page every day for a rare thing.
- **Restore asks first.** It takes the note down, and it now sits in a menu beside Edit the note….
