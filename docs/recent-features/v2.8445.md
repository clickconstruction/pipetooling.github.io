# v2.8445 — Bills sent: a test bill is in no total, in either view (2026-11-24)

One fault of the Bills sent page found on the live test of 2026-11-24 (punch list #422, problem 1). No migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

The two views did not agree about test rows, and each counted some.

- **By day paid** left out a payment on a bill made in Stripe's test mode. It knew nothing of ZZ test jobs, so a payment typed by hand onto one was added to its day and its month.
- **By day sent** knew neither rule. A test-mode bill stood in its day's total, read *Not paid* under the **Not paid** chip and was added to the **Not paid** tile, the figure a leader quotes as what is owed. A paid test bill made its day read *1 of 1 paid* while By day paid showed nothing that day.

## What changed

- `src/lib/billing/billTestRows.ts`, new and pure, holds the one rule: `isBillsTestRow(bill, job)` is true for a bill made in Stripe's test mode, and for any bill or payment on a ZZ test job (`isZzTestJob`, the app's own rule for a test record). Both views read it.
- **By day sent** keeps the send on the list, since the email did go, and marks it: *A test bill. It is in no total.* The line carries `test`. It adds to no day's total and no paid count, has no stamp, and is out of the **Not paid** tile and both payment chips. Its bounce is never among the sends that **Did not arrive**. A bill's record reads *Test bill* where a real one says whether it is paid. The spreadsheet and the print carry the same words.
- A ZZ test job's bill is no longer listed in the yellow box of bills with no send on record. The read already left out test-mode bills there.
- **By day paid** leaves out every test payment by the same rule, so the **Paid this week** tile on the list does too.
- The list's read asks for each bill's `stripe_mode`, a column the second view already read.
- The guide has a section on a test bill.

## A choice made here

A test send could be kept and marked, or hidden. It is kept, because the list is the record of what left and a dev checks a test send on it. The Pipeline hides ZZ jobs from every role but a dev (punch list #61). If the owner wants the same here, it is a filter over `test` lines.

## Tests

`billTestRows.test.ts`, new: the rule, then each view against the same three bills (a real one, a test-mode one, one on a ZZ job). Eight of its cases failed before the change. `BillsSentLedger.render.test.tsx`: a ZZ bill's line draws its mark, no stamp, *0 bills* on its day and no **Not paid** tile.

## Not looked at

Not drawn in a browser. The live list was not read again, so the four lines the test named were not seen with their marks.
