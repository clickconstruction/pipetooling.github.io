# v2.4813 — Customer timeline: the window (punch list #97, PR 2 of 3) (2026-10-07)

Client only. Punch list #97 ([`to-dos/customer-timeline/`](../../to-dos/customer-timeline/README.md)), on the kernel and reads of v2.4811. Guide: *see everything that happened with a customer*.

## What changed

- The Customer profile window has two views: **Profile** (the window as it was) and **Timeline**, behind a switch in its title bar. The pick is remembered per device (`customer-profile-view`, [`customerProfileView.ts`](../../src/lib/customers/customerProfileView.ts)); `openCustomerProfile(customerId, { view })` lets a door name the view. [`CustomerProfileModal.tsx`](../../src/components/customers/CustomerProfileModal.tsx): the old window is `CustomerProfilePanel`; the default export holds the view and draws the Timeline in a wide window (`min(1160px, 100%)` × `min(92vh, 100%)`, the same layer, z-index and backdrop as the Profile).
- [`CustomerTimelineView.tsx`](../../src/components/customers/CustomerTimelineView.tsx): the title bar; the bar (who line, four tiles, Show chips and the open jobs' chips, what could not be read) that collapses to one line on scroll and adds what stood on the day under it; the rows from `buildCustomerTimeline` (office cards, rails, field cards on a computer; one column on a phone, each card tagged Office or Field); focus from a job chip (every other card and rail fades; a grey-lane job lights the grey rail where it runs); a job's name on a card opens the Job window; the legend.
- [`customerTimelineWords.ts`](../../src/lib/customers/customerTimelineWords.ts): the tiles, the as-of line, the who line and a job's words.
- The kernel learned one rule from the live check: a job named after its GC (or its payer) reads as its street, so the GC names are read with the jobs (`gcName`).
- `src/index.css`: `--timeline-job-0…7`, the eight job colors, light and dark.
- Docs: GLOSSARY → **Customer timeline**; PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION's Customer Hub paragraph names the view.
