# v2.5930 — The Bridge: the Bill desk behind its card (2026-10-26)

Punch list #192 (`to-dos/bridge-stage-desks/`), the Bill desk, from the mock-up `mockups/bill-desk.html` ("Where billing leaks") the owner approved on 2026-10-26 after the three-perspective review. The Bill card on the Bridge now ends in **Break it down ›** (the door pattern of [v2.5890](v2.5890.md)), opening a read-only window on one question: when the work finishes, does the bill go out that day, and what is the sitting costing? Client-only, no migration, nothing new on the server.

## The kernel (`src/lib/bridge/bill/billDesk.ts`)

Pure, every sentence held to the plain-words rule. `buildBillDesk` returns:

- **The leaks**, biggest dollars first, each a crossing of two reads with its amount, how long it has sat (weeks from the ready day, else from the job's own percent points, else *no clock*), a head, a detail naming the job, the folded *The read that proves it* paragraph and one door. (1) *Finished, never billed*: a working job at 100% by hand or by report whose `coveredUsd` is 0 → **Bill it ›**. (2) *A remainder aging behind a part-bill*: a working job whose finished share exceeds what bills or payments cover, with the larger side named (the desk's honesty rule: *payments cover* when payments win) → **Bill the rest ›**. (3) *No price, so no number ever rings*: a no-price job with field hours this window, counted as `fieldPaceByHours` counts a session; its amount cell reads the hours, its dollars are 0 so it sorts after the dollar rows → **Price the job ›** on the job window. (4) *Marked billed by hand, nothing on record went out*: a bill of the window with `billed_at`, no `sent_to_customer_at` and not yet paid, clocked from the day it was marked → **Open the bill ›**. The ready-to-bill jobs and the grey guesses never leak. The leaks' total is the headline's first number.
- **Billable today, the fast lane**: the pile's `ready` items, biggest remainder first, each with *sat N days* from the job's own ready day (or *no ready day on record*), *bills cover $X of $Y* / *payments cover $X of $Y* / *nothing billed yet*, the remainder and **Bill it ›**. Its total is the verdict's number.
- **The rule, lately**: over the window's bills with a ready day, *N of M* went out the same day, per pay week (`payWeekContaining`) as `same/sent` counts, and per bill: ready day, billed day (`invoiceBilledYmd`), lag, who sent it (*by hand* when the bill never went out from here), cash-days (lag × amount, `$X·d`). Bills with no ready day sit in the table with dashes and a counting line. Newest first, folded past 8 rows.
- **What a week of sitting costs**: the pile's known dollars × 7 as `$X·days`, with the company's median pay pace in one sentence when there is one.
- The headline (*Where billing leaks: $X will not bill itself.*), the one bold verdict (*$Y the office already called done waits on a send.* or *Nothing waits on a send today.*), the paces sub-line, the pipe strip (finished by the field this window, finished with no bill yet split into ready and by the %, billed, billed not yet paid as Collect's, paid) and the footnote.

`shapeBillDeskJobs` / `shapeBillDeskBills` cross the page's reads (`billPile`, `billJobs`, Build's percent points, the weeks' sessions) with the loader's; `readyDayFor` picks the newest ready-to-bill change on or before a day; `pct100SinceYmd` finds the day a job's percent reached 100 in its current run.

## The reads (`src/lib/bridge/bill/loadBillDesk.ts`)

Lazy, on the first open, read-only, with `withSupabaseRetry` and the paging helpers: `jobs_ledger_invoices` (`id, job_id, amount, billed_at, sent_to_customer_at, sent_by_user_id, status`) for the scorecard's window plus every bill on the pile's jobs, the same live-bill filter the Bridge's billed-per-week uses; `job_status_events` (`job_id, to_status, changed_at`) with `to_status = 'ready_to_bill'` on every touched job, so the ready day is the job's own record (the skeptic's finding: a real read, not an owner call; a job with no such row predates the table and reads *no ready day on record*); `jobs_ledger` labels (`id, job_name, hcp_number, click_number`, numbered with `effectiveJobLedgerNumber`) for every touched job, since the page labels only the open working jobs; `users` (`id, name`) for the senders.

## The desk (`src/components/bridge/BridgeBillDesk.tsx`)

The Collect desk's chrome: the fixed overlay closing on a backdrop click, the dialog *Bill, broken down*, the × and Close, Esc. The header draws at once from what the page knows (the verdict, the paces, the pipe, the meter) while the rows read *Reading the Bill desk…*; a failed read shows one line. The scorecard folds under one line carrying *N of M* and the late sends' cost; inside, the eight pay-week cells with a small bar each, the table and the no-ready-day line. The footnote sits at the foot above the footer, which says the week's call is recorded on the link desk above, not here. `Bridge.tsx` adds the `bill` entry to the cards' `desks`, guarded by the pile being read, the open state, the lazy reads keyed on the pile, the kernel memo after the stage flow (its facts read the Bill card's paces and weeks) and the render after the Collect desk's.

## The owner's calls, built to defaults

- `BILL_SAME_DAY_TOLERANCE_DAYS = 0`: a bill reads same-day when billed − ready is at most this many days. The owner can widen it.
- `BILL_SCORECARD_WEEKS = 8`: the pay weeks the scorecard, its table and the hand-marked leak read, this week included. The owner can change it.

Both are exported constants at the top of the kernel, each with its comment; the footnote reads them.
