# v2.6365 — Job photos: a camera on the Pipeline row adds a photo with its words and browses the job’s photos (punch list #244, PR 3) (2026-11-02)

Punch list #244's third PR, the window itself, on PR 1's table (v2.6359) and PR 2's sweep (v2.6363). The owner's ask: *a photos icon next to the Job folder icon and a modal that works just like the reports photo upload, where we make it very easy for a user to upload a photo to a job and leave a text description that can be referenced later, and browse job photos if they are office or field staff*. The card's self-critique picked one window with two faces over an upload-only modal; the mock-up is `to-dos/job-photos/mockup.html`.

## What changed

- **The icon** (`StagesPhotosButton` in `StagesRowActionButtons.tsx`): a camera right after the Job folder icon in both desktop clusters (`JobsStagesTable.tsx`; `StagesUnifiedJobRow.tsx` through the row context's new `openJobPhotos`, set by `JobsStagesUnifiedTable.tsx`), amber with a Drive folder and grey without, never dead; a ⌘-click opens the photo folder in Drive (Customer Pictures, else the job's folder). The phone cards' two ⋯ sheets (`JobsStagesCardList.tsx`) gain a **Photos** line after Job folder.
- **The window** (`components/photos/JobPhotosWindow.tsx`, a `ResponsiveModalShell`, 720 px at a desk, a full sheet on a phone): opens on what is there. **Drive is the list, our rows are the words**: the view's rows for the job (every source) laid over the job's two Drive photo folders as the strip reads them (`job-photos` → `list`), so a file put in the folder by hand shows too, read by its file name as *Added in Drive* (`gridItems`). The grid by day, newest first (`dayGroups`), the words under each tile (`gridTileWords`: the description's first line, the stage, the box's words, the file's name, else *No description*), the person and the time; a search box over the words, the person, the stage and the source (`gridSearch`, two characters); source chips with counts only when the photos came through more than one door (`gridChips`); the foot with the count, the folder and the hand-added files (`gridFootWords`) and **Open the folder ↗**. An empty job draws `EmptyState` naming **Add a photo** (the camera's words on a phone) and says when the first photo makes the folder; a search that finds nothing offers **Clear the search**; a read that fails draws `OfflineRetryPanel` with the reason and Retry.
- **Add a photo** is the first tile (several files from a computer; `capture` on a phone for the field roles, one shot a press). Each shot is a tile in the report form's shape through `useShotTiles` (shrinking, *Sending…*, *Sent*, or the reason with Retry; Remove), with **What does this show?** under it (optional, up to 500 characters), **Use these words for the rest** under the first when several are chosen and another has none, and the job's stage chips on a job with two or more lines (`useJobLineProgress`, a paid draw never). The press reads **Add 2 photos to the job** (`addPress` over `countedLabel`), dead while a shot is on its way or nothing is ready, never OK; it writes the words, the stage, the caption and `added_at` on each row (`addJobPhotoNotes` over `addWrites`), the toast counts what added and names the first reason for what did not (`addedWords`), the added tiles leave the form (`clear`) and the grid reads again. Cancel or a close with shots on the form drops them (`drop`: the rows and the bucket objects go).
- **The viewer** is Documents → Photos' `UploadedPhotoViewer` with two new props: `titleOf` (the caption alone, never the job again) and `onEditWords`, offered on a `job` row for the taker and the office and the estimator (`canEditJobPhotoWords`); **Edit the words** opens a small window over it whose **Save the words** writes the description and the caption (`editJobPhotoNoteWords`), the Drive file taking the new words on the sweep's next pass. A text's picture opens its thread; every other source and **Open the job** open the job window through the caller's `onOpenJob`; **Only Carla's photos** types the name into the search.
- **Guides**: *add a photo to a job and find it later* (new: the doors, the words, where a photo lands, browsing, the viewer, changing the words, a photo or a report); *ready to bill pipeline* names the sheet's Photos line; *see a job's photos* points at the window. **Docs**: the Glossary's *Job photos* entry, `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`'s Jobs line, `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`'s table and bucket, `JOBS_STAGES_TAB_ARCHITECTURE.md`'s icon note.

## Decisions

- **A window of its own, not the job window's Documents tab** (call 1): two taps from the row to a photo with words; the job window's Photos section opens the same window in PR 4.
- **A hand-dropped file is a tile, not a count**: the owner's rule is that photos live in Drive, so the folder is the list; our rows only add the words.
- **The report form's `useReportPhotos` is untouched**: the window mounts the new `useShotTiles`; folding the report hook onto it is a follow-up refactor.
