## Tests

`src/lib/scheduleDispatch/recordNcns.test.ts`, 12 cases; it mocks `./supabase`, so it runs with no environment set. They pin the map's quirk #19:

- The incident is recorded first, then the day off, then the blocks.
- A refusal — a message in the row, no row at all, or the call itself failing — stops everything: no day off, no block delete.
- Once the incident is on record the blocks are still cleared when the day-off marking fails, and the result says how it failed.

## Found while pinning

The hub and the My Time day editor call the same RPC, and differ in two ways. Both are kept as they are and recorded on quirk #19:

- The hub sends the details **as written**; the day editor trims them (`ncnsRecordRpcArgs`).
- The hub treats **no row back** as a refusal ("Could not record NCNS."); the day editor treats it as done.

Making the two agree is a behavior change and would be its own PR.
