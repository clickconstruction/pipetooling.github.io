# v2.5812 — Flows: a candidate has a flow, a sub coming on has a lane, and both carry their qualifications on one scale for the trades (2026-10-24)

The second half of the 2026-10-23 design spike with the owner on `/messages` (the first is v2.5811). The owner, on the empty side of a candidate's thread: "Perhaps a candidate should have flow." Then: "I think that those candidate steps work for hiring, but I don't think they would work for subs. Can you help me make an additional one for subs?" Then the scale, in his words: "For someone to be rated by us, we need to know their license. We need to know what they can do and that would be a scale for the three trades of electrical plumbing and HVAC, and we need to know their location. That scale would be: has no tools and has some experience. Has no tools, but claims they can do jobs on their own. Has tools and claims they can do jobs on their own. That would be a one, a two or a three. If the person has zero experience, they would be at zero. This is a universal scale for the trades." Then "Qualifications" over "Rated", the screening call before it, the words inside the step, and the home as "where they are driving in from", found on "a Google search enabled map, which is already enabled in the app."

- **The candidate flow** ([`seed.ts`](../../src/lib/flows/seed.ts), kind `candidate` in [`facts.ts`](../../src/lib/flows/facts.ts)): one Once lane, Screening call, Qualifications, Try-out, Hired or passed (a fork; passed ends the lane). [`candidateLoad.ts`](../../src/lib/flows/candidateLoad.ts) reads the marks from the hiring board's row (`team_prospects`: `status` at or past calling or a `last_contact`, `trial_started_at`, hired or passed) and the qualifications below; [`flowPersonIo.ts`](../../src/lib/flows/flowPersonIo.ts) reads the row itself, since `flow_rows` does not carry it. `contactFlowKind('candidate')` is its own; Settings → Flows gets a **Candidate** tab ([`editorKinds.ts`](../../src/lib/flows/editorKinds.ts)), with nobody to try a change on yet. The board's phase maps and the papers' section title learn the kind.
- **A sub coming on** ([`subOnboardingLoad.ts`](../../src/lib/flows/subOnboardingLoad.ts)): the sub flow gains a Once lane before its jobs: Reached out (our first line out on their thread), Qualifications, W-9 in and Insurance in (their papers on People through the same `paperStamps` a trade partner's go through, the master agreement filtered out), Agreement signed (the sub agreement on People), First job done (their earliest sheet past Working, through `people_labor_job_assignees`), Kept or let go (a second sheet done, or their record archived, which ends the lane). The read gathers the three for a roster sub; `withSeedOnceLane` lends the seed's lane to a sub flow read from the table without one until the migration lands it. `dayOf` is exported from `load.ts`.
- **Qualifications** ([`hiring/tradeScale.ts`](../../src/lib/hiring/tradeScale.ts), [tested](../../src/lib/hiring/tradeScale.test.ts)): the three trades, the four levels in the owner's words, `Qualifications` (a level per trade, the license or none, the home and its pin), `qualificationsMissing` (a zero is known, no license is an answer), `qualificationsWords` and `qualificationsStepWords` (*PLUM 1 · ELEC 0 · HVAC 0 · Journeyman plumber, TX M-55555 · Home: Round Rock*), `bestTrade`, and the row converters `qualificationsFrom`, `qualificationsRows`, `qualificationsRecord`. [`qualificationsIo.ts`](../../src/lib/hiring/qualificationsIo.ts) reads and writes `trade_levels` (one row a trade) and the record's `location`, `location_lat`, `location_lng`, `license`, `license_asked`. The fact `qualifications_noted` (group **Hiring**) marks the step on both flows once all three are known.
- **The window** ([`QualificationsWindow.tsx`](../../src/components/flows/QualificationsWindow.tsx)): three fieldsets of four levels, the License field with **No license**, **Home, where they drive in from** with **Find it on the map**, the foot saying what is still needed, **Save their qualifications**. [`HomeMapWindow.tsx`](../../src/components/flows/HomeMapWindow.tsx): a search box through `geocode-one` (the office address's own geocoder) over the app's map canvas, Google when the browser key is set and OpenStreetMap otherwise, one pin, **Use this place**. [`FlowTab.tsx`](../../src/components/flows/FlowTab.tsx) draws the words inside the Qualifications step's box (`PanelBox.words`, drawn by [`FlowPanel.tsx`](../../src/components/flows/FlowPanel.tsx)) and puts **Add their qualifications** / **Change their qualifications** on the step's card (press id `qualifications` in [`card.ts`](../../src/lib/flows/card.ts)); a save re-reads the person everywhere.
- **Migrations**: `20261024020000_candidate_and_sub_flows.sql` widens `flows_kind_known` to the candidate, adds the ten facts to `flow_fact_ids()`, reseeds `flows_seed()` and inserts the sub flow's Once lane into the sub flow prod already holds; `20261024030000_trade_levels.sql` makes `trade_levels` and the five columns on `team_prospects` and `people`. Both additive. The nudge run's mirrored kernels regenerated (`gen:edge-kernels`), since `FLOW_KINDS` grew.
- **Tests**: `candidateLoad.test.ts`, `subOnboardingLoad.test.ts`, `tradeScale.test.ts`, the pinned lists in `facts.test.ts`, `state.test.ts`, `seed.sub.test.ts`, `residentialLoad.sub.test.ts`, `editorKinds.test.ts`, `remindersCard*.test.ts`, `quickReplies/kernel.test.ts`, and `flowsSql.test.ts` against the new migration.
- **Guides**: *set up the steps a person goes through* (the Candidate tab), *see where a person stands and what comes next* (noting qualifications, the map). Docs: `GLOSSARY.md`, `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`, `docs/migrations/`.

## Is this the best we can do?

- The seats on the office's steps are the seeds' placeholder name, as on every other flow.
- No reminders on either lane yet; the hiring board keeps its own call list.
- A trade partner's qualifications would be the same scale again; the Trade partners page has its own vetting, so it waits.
- The types regenerate in their own PR after `db push`; the io is untyped until then.
