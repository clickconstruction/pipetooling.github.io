# v2.5157 — GC mode: Get started, one window before a job starts (2026-10-10)

The Board's B6-c-ii, the plan `to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/board-b6c.md` on branch `spike/gc-mode`, approved 2026-10-09. It draws Get started on B6-c-i's `gc_start_project` (v2.5045). It is a dev's, as Start and the schedule it reads are, until award's door. Guide: `get-a-gc-job-ready-to-start`.

- **Get started** on a won job's card (buyout or building) opens `GcStartWindow.tsx` at `?start=<project id>`. It is the spike's `GcStart.tsx` on real rows, with the job's schedule read by `loadSchedule` as Bill the customer's is.
  - **The strip:** ready or how many things are missing, the steps-done bar, and **Start the project and tell the N on the job**, shut until ready.
  - **Start anyway** takes why and sends what was missing as the window listed it (call A).
  - **With the customer:**
    - **Mark it signed** and **Undo** on our contract go through `gc_sign_owner_contract` with today and `ownerContractWorthNow`. They are the money team's, with Our number read.
    - The permit and the start date are plain `gc_projects` writes (`setGcProjectStartItem`).
  - **The trades:** each **Next** opens where main does that work, so no press is built twice.
    - Award opens Compare quotes.
    - A paper opens the company window at Documents with its send open (`openPartner`).
    - A drafted statement of work closes the window and scrolls to the trade card's Send.
    - Our own crew links to its bid on Bids' Pricing tab.
  - **After Start:** who was told, anyone the email missed named with why, and **Send again**. After Start anyway: who, why and what is still owed, with the steps open until it is in.
- **Start's email** (`startEmail.ts`, pure): one `gc-trade-email` per company awarded on the job.
  - The kind is `start`, the group `job`, and the key `<project id>:start`, so a second press sends nothing.
  - The words are the Portal's start block.
  - A company carried but not awarded is not told.
- **Our own crew (call C):** the board reads each `own_bid_id`'s `bid_value` (`selfPerformOf`).
  - Once that is above 0, `selfPerform` is that bid, priced, and **Our number moves to our own bid on a job with our own crew**.
  - Until then it is our budget, not priced.
- **The trade card** (`GcTradeSow.tsx`):
  - **Send to their portal to sign** waits on `partnerBlockers`, showing "Cannot send yet." and what is missing.
  - **Send the master agreement** sits beside it while none has gone.
  - The company's paperwork chips (`GcPaperworkChips.tsx`, the spike's `PaperworkChips`) sit beside its name.
- **Proof:**
  - `startRows.test.ts`: `startChecklist` on mapped rows, ready, our own crew priced and not, and the Start anyway owed list;
  - `startEmail.test.ts` in English and Spanish;
  - `GcStartWindow.render.test.tsx`, ten cases;
  - two new `GcTradeSow` gate cases.
