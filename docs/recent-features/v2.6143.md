# v2.6143 — The chase clock as a strip of days (punch list #217, bids-7, a prototype) (2026-10-30)

Punch list #217, *Show it, don't say it*: eight scans found 131 places where the app tells instead of shows, and a mock-up page draws each as a before/after pair for the owner to pick from. Of bids-7, the Call queue's and Waiting to hear's chase clock, the owner asked on 2026-10-30 to see it "live side by side" with today's look. This is that prototype, shipped as a draft PR with no auto-merge for her to look at. No migration, no edge function.

## The finding

`/bids?tab=call-queue` read a header sentence (*1 builder worth a call · Plumbing · 1 bid to chase · 0 losses need a reason · $0.00 unexplained · 0 tabs gettable*), chips (*Due 0 · Overdue 0 · No date yet 1 · Later 0*), a paragraph, and per builder *CHASE 1 pending — quiet 5d · 0 of 1 fresh*; `/bids?tab=waiting-to-hear` read *A bid needs a chase when nobody's talked to the GC in over 7 days* and, on the bid, *sent Oct 25 (5d ago) · Last contact Oct 27 (3d ago)*. The words come from `buildCallQueue` over `resolveBidFollowup`: *fresh*, *quiet 5d*, *to chase* and *gettable* are verdicts from two silent defaults (seven days since a contact, `PENDING_CHASE_STALE_CONTACT_DAYS`; twenty-one days since the send, `TAB_GETTABLE_AFTER_DAYS`) the reader never saw as a day, and a promised day read the same as a default one.

## What changed

- **`chaseClockStrip.ts`** (`src/lib/bids/`, new): the pure kernel from one bid's send, contact instants, `BidFollowup` and tab to cells. The window runs from the send to a day past the farthest of today plus three, the call-again day and the gettable day, never over `CHASE_CLOCK_MAX_DAYS` (42); a bid sent long ago keeps today in view, trims the past and says *Sent Jul 1, before the strip starts*. Each day carries its marks: `sent`, `contact` (a contact before the send is dropped, as the rule drops it), `promised` (a day the bid or the builder stands on) or `default` (the rule's day, never before the send; a never-contacted bid's diamond sits on its send day) and `gettable` (the send plus twenty-one days, none when a tab is on record). `toChase` is `followupNeedsCall`, so the strip and the queue never disagree. `chaseClockLabels` places the words under the strip and lets the lesser of two neighbours closer than four days give way (the call-again day outranks today, today the send, the send the gettable day), the first leaning right and the last left so none runs off the edge. `words` is the whole strip in one sentence for the hover and the screen reader. Ten tests in `chaseClockStrip.test.ts`: a promised day, a default day, past the diamond, past gettable, never contacted, the builder's day, the 42-day cap, no send, and the labels.
- **`bidContactInstants.ts`** (`src/lib/bids/`, new): every contact's instant per bid, the method entries of the bids in hand, chunked and paged as `loadBidEntryRecency` reads them. Read only while the strip is on.
- **`ChaseClockStrip.tsx`** (`src/components/bids/`, new): the picture — the kernel's days as columns in the Legal Timeline strip's shape, today a `--bg-blue-tint` column outlined in `--text-link`, weekends washed `--bg-muted`, the send a `--text-link` dot, a contact a `--text-strong` dot, a promised day a solid `--text-amber-800` diamond on `--bg-amber-tint`, the rule's day the same diamond dashed, the gettable day a `--text-green-700` tick; the labels under it from the kernel. `ChaseClockLegend` draws the key once.
- **`BidsCallQueueTab`**: behind `useShapeView('chase-clock')` (per person, localStorage, off by default) with **Show the strip of days** / **Show today's look** at the end of the header row. With the strip on, the header sentence goes and the two chip rows keep the counts; under each builder's header line every pending bid gets a line (*482 · Library restrooms · $18,725 · to chase · quiet 3 d*) and its strip, to chase first, then parked, then fresh; the Chase row keeps *N pending* as the door to the one-tap chips, loses *quiet 5d* and *0 of 1 fresh*, and its Done cell reads *N later* or a dash. The tab reads the pending bids' contact instants itself while the strip is on, fail-soft (a lost read still draws the send, the bid's own stamp and the days), or takes them as the new optional `contactInstantsByBid` prop. With `?sbs=1` and a dev signed in, the tab draws the sentence and today's cards under **Today** and the legend and the strip cards under **With the strip**, the search, the pills and the chips once above both, with no switch.
- **`BidsWaitingToHearLens`**: the same switch (one press for both doors). With the strip on, the paragraph drops its *over 7 days* sentence (the dashed diamond says it), and the open bid draws its strip and the legend in place of the *Last contact Oct 27 (3d ago)* line; the contacts are the story panel's method entries plus the bid's own stamp, the follow-up `resolveBidFollowup` over the bid's own call-again day. Under `?sbs=1` the card draws the Last contact line under **Today** and the strip under **With the strip**.
- **`src/components/prototypes/SideBySide.tsx`**: the shared helper the #217 prototypes use (`useShapeView`, `SideBySide`, `ShapeSwitch`), copied byte for byte from the office-5 branch (PR #6528), which was pushed when this card got there; the rhythm-strips branch the brief named was not. Identical adds merge clean, so whichever lands first leaves the others nothing to resolve.
- **Smokes**: `BidsCallQueueTab.chaseClock.render.test.tsx` (today's look first; the press drops the sentence and the verdict words, draws the send, the contact, the rule's diamond, the gettable tick and today, keeps the chips and the rows, and is remembered; a bid past its diamond is marked *to chase*; both shapes under `?sbs=1` with no switch) and `BidsWaitingToHearLens.chaseClock.render.test.tsx` (the Last contact line and the seven-day words first, then the strip in their place; both under `?sbs=1`). The dates are counted from the company's today, so the smokes hold on any day.
- **Guide** *follow up with builders on their bids*: the Call queue section and the Waiting to hear section each name the press and what the strip draws.
- **Docs**: the Followup lenses dossier in `BIDS_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md`.

## What the mock-up asked for that this does not do

- **A hover card over the diamond.** The mock-up drew its own popover (*call again · Nov 3 · the rule's 7 days, nobody promised a day*). The strip uses the browser's hover: each day's `title` carries its words, and the whole strip's `title` the sentence.
- **Dropping the "$0.00 unexplained" figure.** It was in the header sentence and no chip carries it; with the strip on it is not on the Call queue. The Why we lost lens still prints it.
- **One strip per bid where a bid went to several GCs.** The queue lists one row per GC packet, so a bid under two GCs draws two strips, as it has two rows; the contact dots are the bid's, shared by both, as the rule shares them.
