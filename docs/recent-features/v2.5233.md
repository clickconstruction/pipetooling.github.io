# v2.5233 — The Bridge: the link’s desk, the week’s call and Friday’s answer (link PR 2) (2026-10-11)

PR 2 of the weakest-link build (`to-dos/bridge-weakest-link/README.md`). Dev only. One migration, `20261011000000_bridge_calls.sql` (`docs/migrations/20261011000000_bridge_calls.md`), pushed after the merge; the client reads the table through `as never` casts until the types are regenerated, so either order works.

- **The record** (`bridge_calls`): one row per call on a pay week — the link (a stage or a resource), who and when, the moves with owners, a `measures` snapshot of the page's reading at call time (the link's weeks to clear, every stage's weeks, and the numbers behind the move keys: finished-unbilled dollars, late dollars, pending hours, unpriced jobs, jobs assumed half done), and Friday's answer. Dev-only RLS through `is_dev()`; the three write fences.
- **The kernel** (`src/lib/bridge/calls.ts`): `callForWeek` (the newest row stands), `lastCallBefore`, `scoreCall`, `streakFor` (distinct weeks on the same link), `moveDone` (yes at half or less, part when it fell, no), `callVerdict` (moved when the link's weeks fell by a tenth; a recorded answer wins), `fridayAsks` (from the week's fifth day until answered), `buildScorecard` (the consequence by streak: first week keep the call, second week other owners or change the call, three weeks elevate), `callChip` (the header chip), `deskStatus`. The client calls `loadBridgeCalls` / `recordBridgeCall` / `answerBridgeCall` sit at the bottom. `linkDesk.ts` holds each link's what / why / how and its move presets, keyed so Friday can read the numbers again; every sentence passes the plain-words rule.
- **The desk** (`BridgeLinkDesk.tsx`, under the cards for the pressed stage): the four readings, the moves as checkboxes with an owner field each and today's number beside the keyed ones, a note, and **This is the link this week: Bill**. Once recorded the desk shows the call instead of the button; pressing another stage and recording replaces the week's call (a new row; the old one stays as history).
- **The held call**: the week's call feeds `buildStageFlow`'s `heldLinkId` (PR 1), so the red stays on the called stage unless another reads 15 % longer; the overtaking stage's badge says "reads 10.3 weeks · the call holds Bill".
- **The scorecard** (`BridgeLastCall.tsx`, `#bridge-last-call`): the link, "called Oct 3 by Todd · 2nd week running", a verdict chip (moved / did not move / open), the moves table (move, owner, then → now, done), the link's measure then → now, the consequence in amber or green. From Friday: "Friday asks: did Bill move?" with **It moved** / **It did not** and a note; the answer is written to the row. The header chip reads "last call: Decision · did not move" or "Friday: did Bill move?" and jumps to the card.
- **Tests**: `calls.test.ts` (8), `BridgeLastCall.render.test.tsx` (4: the scorecard and Friday's answer, no call yet, the desk's owners and record, the recorded state).
- **Docs**: guide *read the Bridge* gains "The week's call"; the Glossary's Bridge entry; the to-do's status line.
