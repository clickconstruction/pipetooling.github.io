# v2.3166 — Supply house Directory pane + the Materials tabs kernel (2026-09-08)

Client only. No migration or edge-function change. PR 1 of six from [`to-dos/supply-house-directory/`](../../to-dos/supply-house-directory/README.md) (the owner approved the refined mock-up, artifact *Supply House Directory*, 2026-09-08).

## Why

Wendi asked for the supply houses' names and contacts. The data already admitted her; the only door was the Parts Book button v2.2903 renamed to "Price coverage". The to-do's decision: split the office's Supply Houses tab into a **Directory** pane and an **accounts-payable** pane, and later render the Directory alone for estimators — one component, three doors, never a second roster. This PR builds the pane and the role kernel; the office sees it first.

## What shipped

- **[`SupplyHouseDirectory`](../../src/components/materials/SupplyHouseDirectory.tsx)** (new) — the Directory pane. Per vendor: name and address, **Kind** (Supply house / Insurer, from `is_insurer` until `vendor_kind` lands), **reps** with the starred default first (each rep's tooltip says who added it and when), phone as a `tel:` link, **Prices on file** (priced parts from `get_supply_house_stats_by_service_type`) with the newest **price request** beneath — when, by whom, and whether it was answered (`bid_rfqs.status`: `quoted` → answered, `sent` → no reply yet). A **coverage line** above the list: houses, houses with a rep, houses that need one. Houses with no rep sort last under a **"Needs a rep"** band so adding one is the obvious next tap. Expanding a row shows the reps editor (`SupplyHouseContactsSection`, now with **added by** labels and an `onChanged` callback), the house's notes, and its three most recent requests with bid, requester and outcome. Under 640 px the table becomes cards with 44 px call / email targets. Search matches house name, address, and rep label / name / email. Who-added / who-asked names resolve through the archived-safe `get_user_display_names` RPC.
- **[`supplyHouseDirectory.ts`](../../src/lib/materials/supplyHouseDirectory.ts)** (new kernel, 12 tests) — joins houses, reps, requests and price counts into rows; group order (with a rep → other kinds → needs a rep, alphabetical within); coverage counts over supply houses only; search; `requestOutcomeLabel`; `priceCountsByHouse` (optionally narrowed to one service type); `directoryAgoPhrase`. Generic over the house row so the component keeps the full `supply_houses` row.
- **[`materialsTabs.ts`](../../src/lib/materials/materialsTabs.ts)** (new kernel, 11 tests) — `materialsTabsFor(role)` is now the one list of who may open which Materials tab: office roles everything, estimator the two books plus PO Builder / Purchase Orders, primary and superintendent the two books. `resolveMaterialsTab(role, ?tab)` decides the landing and redirect; `supplyHousesPaneFor(role)` says which pane the Supply houses tab renders (`office` today; PR 2 adds `directory` for estimators). `Materials.tsx` reads the kernel for its four access checks, the `?tab=` guard effect, every tab pill (each office pill is gated on its own so PR 2 can admit one without the others) and the tab body — the dozen literal role comparisons are gone. Behaviour is identical for every role; the render smoke pins it.
- **[`useSupplyHouseEditor`](../../src/components/materials/useSupplyHouseEditor.tsx)** (new hook) — the one add / edit / delete path for a house, lifted verbatim from `SupplyHousesTab`: the host renders `{editor.modal}` once and calls `openAdd()` / `openEdit(house)`. The office tab's accounts-payable drawer **Edit** button and the Directory's **Edit** / **Add vendor** now share it; the nine-field `supplyHouse*` state cluster and the bottom-of-page **Add Supply House** button are gone from the tab.
- **[`SupplyHousesTab`](../../src/components/SupplyHousesTab.tsx)** — renders two labelled panes: **Directory** (the new component) above **Accounts payable** (the aging heat map, summary table, invoice drawer, job accounts — untouched JSX). The AP drawer keeps its own selected-house state; the Directory keeps its own expanded row.
