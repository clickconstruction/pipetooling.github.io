# v2.5894 — The Legal desk is a page: /legal-desk (2026-10-26)

Punch list #182, PR 1 of 3 ([`to-dos/desks-as-pages/`](../../to-dos/desks-as-pages/README.md)). The owner, 2026-10-26, on the rule that a desk is a page with a route: "I like your suggestion, please build it testing along the way." The rule: a desk is a page with its own address when a person stays in it, it has tabs or views of its own, and the page under it goes unread while it is open; a window is right when it is one act and the page under it is where you go back to. The Legal desk is the first case: six tabs, two views, four doors, two links, a full-screen button, and a phone on which the window's rail took 300 of 351 px with every tab off the screen.

## What shipped

- **The address** ([`lib/legal/legalDeskRoute.ts`](../../src/lib/legal/legalDeskRoute.ts), 6 tests): `/legal-desk` is the rail; `?account=<payer key>` one account; `&tab=` one of `narrative · account · paper · evidence · firm · timeline` (`fees` still reads as `firm`); `&view=map` the county map. The default tab and the default view are never written. `legalDeskRedirect` maps the links in the wild: `/jobs?tab=stages&legal=1` → `/legal-desk`, `?legal=<key>` → `?account=<key>`, `&legalTab=fees|timeline` → `&tab=firm|timeline`. `/legal` was not available: it is the firm's own portal.
- **The page** ([`pages/LegalDeskPage.tsx`](../../src/pages/LegalDeskPage.tsx), lazy in `App.tsx` beside `accounts-receivable`; 3 render cases): gated by `canManageCollections` the way `/accounts-receivable` gates itself (wait for the role, then a known outsider lands on the Dashboard; `isPathAllowedForRole` already keeps estimators, primaries, superintendents and subs off a path not on their lists). It reads for itself what the board used to hand the window: the Collections and given-up jobs from the app-wide jobs cache, asking it for the `billed_all` scope alone, every row unfiltered (the window read the board's filtered rows, the stages map's quirk 4); the contract coverage; the users the Jobs page shows; `useLegalMatters`. The doors back: Edit job through the app-wide job form; Reports and the thread through the job window; the Pipeline at the job's row (`?stagesJob=`), call mode (`?chase=1`) and a job's Lien window (`?lienwindow=`) by the doors the board already answers; Accounts Receivable by its own address; the Contract window, Session notes and the promised-pay sheet mounted on the page. The page measures its own top and is the screen below it, so the rail and the account scroll inside and the document never does. **← Pipeline** at the header's left goes to the page behind when there is one and to the Pipeline on a cold open (`hasInAppHistory`).
- **The desk** ([`LegalDeskModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/legal/LegalDeskModal.tsx), 3 new cases): one new prop, `frame: 'page' | 'window'`. On the page: no overlay, no ✕, no full-screen button (the hook gets no key), the page's `backDoor` before the title, `data-legal-desk-panel="page"`, and `onAddressChange` told every change of account, tab and view once the account is resolved, never from an empty rail (which would drop a pasted `?account=` before the rows arrive). The address is the truth on the page: a tab word gone from it means the default tab, `?view=` sets the view. The window is as it was.
- **The redirect** ([`Jobs.tsx`](../../src/pages/Jobs.tsx)): the 32-line `?legal=` effect, which waited for the role, the tab and the whole jobs list before opening the window, is a `navigate(legalDeskRedirect(search), { replace: true })`. The tab-forcing prefetch no longer reads `legal`.
- **Words**: the guide *review a collections account before it goes to your attorney* (Open the desk: the page, its address, the door back); `docs/twins/APP_DIRECTORY.md` (`### /legal-desk`, the five doors rewritten); `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`, `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`, `APP_SHELL_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `JOBS_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md`; the to-do's status.

## Decided here

- **PR 1 moves no door.** The scales, the ☰ row and the Collections header's ⚖ Legal still open the window, so the risk is one route and one prop, and a rollback is deleting the route. PR 2 turns the doors into links and lifts the window's mount out of `JobsStagesTab`; PR 3 deletes the window frame and draws the phone's rail as a list.
- **The map keeps the account and the tab in the address** (`?account=…&tab=…&view=map`), so Accounts brings them back as they were. The mock-up had the map drop them; the desk keeps `selectedKey` while on the map, and the address should not lie about it.
- **Reports and the thread open the job window**, not the tab's activity window, which needs the tab's thread state. One door fewer, and the window the office already knows.
- **A cold cache takes the Pipeline's scoped first load.** `fetchScopeIfNeeded` is a no-op until a board snapshot exists for the user's unfiltered key, so a pasted link on a fresh tab left the rail at *Loading Collections…* for good. The page reads `jobsListDataKey` against `buildJobsListCacheKey(user, null)`: warm, it asks for `billed_all` the way the doors did; cold, or holding another customer filter's board, it runs `runFetchScopes(['billed_all'], null)`, the same scoped load a fresh Pipeline visit makes. Found on the phone preset during the live look; held by the page's fourth render case.

- **The ? card is a row of its own on the page.** Punch list #187's ? and its card (v2.5908) landed on the desk's header while this PR was open. The window's `auto 1fr` rows give the open card its height; the page's two fixed rows squeezed it to 0 px over the rail, so the page frame takes `auto auto minmax(0, 1fr)` while the card is open (measured on the dev server: card 150 px, the rail 67 px and scrolling; a render case holds it).
