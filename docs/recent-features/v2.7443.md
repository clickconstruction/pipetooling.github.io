# v2.7443 — Lien desk: take a public property off the lien path (2026-11-16)

PR 3 of the public-property train (Taunya's ask of 2026-11-16 on job 883: *"this job is a government job and we can't lien a government job, how should we amend the flow to handle this going forward?"*, the second drawing she approved, all four parts). PR 1 (v2.7436, migration `20261116221500`) stores the answer and its function. PR 2 (v2.7439) makes the desk read it. This PR gives the answer, and draws the Board's line and the bin.

## The property record asks once

While a property's owner names read as a public body and nobody has answered, the record asks *Is this public property?* (`LienPublicQuestion`):

- in Edit Job's **Property record** row, inside *What the lien papers read*, once the row carries the answer's column;
- on the Lien desk's owner line (`LienDeskOwnerPane`), in place of the fixed public-owner sentence, when the job has a property record to answer on. A job with no record keeps the sentence.

Two presses, for the lien office only (`isLienOffice`; Edit Job passes it from the signed-in role). The box says *The lien papers here hold until someone answers.* Anyone else reads the question without presses, after *The office answers this.*

- **Yes, public property…** opens the window below.
- **No, it is private** stores `private` through `set_property_lien_public_answer`. The name guess is overruled, so gate 1, the affidavit's owner gate and the job's own Lien window open (PR 2).

**Not sure, ask the attorney** from the drawing is left out, Taunya's call of 2026-11-16 (*"Yes, two presses"*). Nothing in the app can ask counsel about a property: every way to reach the firm hangs off a Legal desk matter, and there is no property kind. Nothing stands in its place.

Once answered, the record shows one line with the door that changes it (`LienPublicAnswerLine`): *Public property, answered Nov 16. Every job here is off the lien path.* with **Put it back on the lien path…**, or *Not public property, answered Nov 16. Its lien papers can go out.* with **Ask again**.

## The window: Take this property off the lien path?

`LienPublicPropertyWindow`, one window per property, reads its own data (`readLienPublicProperty`), so it opens the same from Edit Job or the desk:

- **Jobs at this property**: each job at the address that owes money (the desk's rule, `lienBilledOpen`) or has a lien paper of the desk's, the most money first, with what already went out on it (`lienPublicWhatWentOut`). A job that owes money starts ticked. A ticked job goes on the attorney's list.
- **A job the Collections flag will hold is named before the press** (`lienPublicListNote`): *523 is still being worked, so it cannot go on the Collections list yet. It still leaves the lien path.* `set_job_collections_flag` takes a billed job only, and its rule is not changed.
- **Why · kept on each job**: the reason, filled in and editable. It is required. It goes on the property and on each ticked job's Collections note, followed by what went out on that job.
- **Is there a payment bond on the project?** Yes, No, or Not known yet. Not known yet leaves each job's own answer alone.
- **What this press does**: every listed job leaves the lien bins with nothing filed or deleted, every job at the address stays off the path now and later, and which jobs go on the attorney's list.
- The press names the act and its count, *Take 2 jobs off the lien path ▸*.

After the press the toast says what happened (`lienPublicPressWords`): *The property is off the lien path. 883 is on the attorney's list. 523 did not go on it.* When a job was held, the window stays open and lists it with the function's own reason.

## The Board says so, for the ones already in (part 2)

- **The mark.** `buildLienBoard` takes `publicJobIds` (the read's `publicAskJobIds`): a chip for a job on a property that reads as public and has no answer carries ⊘, drops its red day to *later* and its *!*, since no paper can be made there. Its hover says *public property, no paper until the property is answered*.
- **One amber line over the bins** (`LienPublicBoardLineView`, the Board's new `over` slot) while such a job still has a paper open, its chip in any stage but sent or filed: *2 jobs sit on public property and are still on the lien path · $32,241 unpaid* with **Review them ›**. The press, for the office, opens the window for each property in turn, and the desk reads again after an answer.

## The bin, afterwards (part 4)

**Public jobs · bond claim** (`LienPublicBinView`, the Board's new `underAffidavits` slot), from the read's `publicJobs`, the most money first with the total owed. Each row has the job, its money, *get the bond* while the bond is not known, the GC, what already went out, and where it stands with the attorney: *with the attorney since Nov 16*, or for a job the Collections flag held, *not on the attorney's list, the job is still being worked*. **Put a job back on the lien path…** opens the put-back window. With jobs at more than one property in the bin, it first asks which job.

## Putting it back

`LienPublicPutBackWindow` lists the jobs at the property and the reason it came off, asks why it goes back (required), and presses *Put 2 jobs back on the lien path ▸*. The answer is taken back (`unanswered`), so every job at the address returns to the desk. The jobs stay on the attorney's list and keep their bond answer, as PR 1's function does.
