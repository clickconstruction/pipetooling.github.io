# v2.7217 — Lien desk on a phone: the lien month is one line (2026-11-13)

Punch list #352, PR 2 of eight, on the owner's pick of direction A. It builds on PR 0 (the first view scrolls as one page) and PR 1 (one header row and More).

## What was wrong

Under the header, a phone drew the lien month as five cards one over the other, 516 px at 390 wide, then the find box, then sixteen step tiles in four rails, about 640 px. The first row of work was 1,526 px down. PR 0 made that reachable; this PR takes most of it away.

## The change

- **The month is one line.** `LienMonthBar` on a phone draws `LienMonthLine`: one button at least 56 px tall. Its words are a new kernel, `src/lib/jobs/lienMonthLine.ts`.
  - The headline is the zone today is in, said as what is left: *9 liens to file*. With nothing left it says what went, in the zone's own words (*Liens: all 12 filed*). In catch-up days it says what is left to catch up, and after the 15th what is queuing for next month.
  - `lienMonthLineDue` finds the first due day among the rows of today's zone, from the rows the desk already holds. With it the headline ends *by Mon Nov 16* and a chip says the days left: red at three days or fewer, amber to seven.
  - The line under it names the month, the day and where that falls (*liens week*). What slipped in the zones already passed is counted once and drawn in red, so a slip is not hidden behind the fold. Catch-up counts the same slips again and is left out of that sum.
  - A strip of five segments, each as wide as its days, marks today's zone and any zone that slipped.
- **The five zones are a sheet.** A press on the line opens them in a `PhoneSheet`, drawn by the same `Zone` component as before, with the same words and presses. A card narrows the list to its zone and closes the sheet. A pressed zone shows under the line with its count and a 44 px **Show all**.
- **The step rails fold.** `LienStepRail` on a phone is one 44 px row, **Steps of each paper**, naming the kinds of paper it holds (`src/lib/jobs/lienStepFold.ts`). A press unfolds the rails in place. A pressed step keeps them open, and **Hide the steps** lets go of the step and folds them.

The card drew the steps as a button inside the month's sheet. They are a row above the rows they narrow instead: the sheet and the rails live in two components, and the fold is one press closer to the list.

No migration, no edge function, no change to what any press writes.
