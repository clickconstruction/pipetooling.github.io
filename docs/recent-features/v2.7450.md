# v2.7450 — Supply houses: a payment becomes a record of its own, the ground (2026-11-16)

The owner's ask of 2026-11-16: the People pay way of recording a payment (the day, the amount, the method and its number, already on sub labor) on supply house bills, where one transfer often covers several bills, the house sometimes applies the money to a different bill, and interest is charged past 30 days. He skipped a punch-list card and asked for the whole train. This is PR 1 of 7: the ground. Nothing on screen changes.

## What changes

- **A payment is a row.** `supply_house_payments` holds one money movement to a house: the day, the amount sent, how it was sent and its number, a receipt link. `supply_house_payment_lines` says which bills it paid, one row per bill.
- **A bill reads paid when its lines cover it.** `is_paid` and `paid_at` stay where they are and are kept true by the lines, so the aging table, Held for suppliers, the Lien desk, the move window, the dev seat and every other reader work unchanged.
- **Mark paid still works from anywhere.** A by-hand `is_paid = true` (the row's press, the invoice form's Paid status, a tab opened before this shipped) makes a payment for what the bill still has open, with the day and no method. A by-hand "not paid" takes it away again.
- **A typed payment is money that was sent, so a by-hand write never unmakes it.** Marking not paid a bill that a typed payment covers is refused with the reason. A bill's amount lowered under its typed lines trims them, and the difference waits on account.
- **Money past the bills is on account with the house.** It is the payment's amount less its lines, stored nowhere twice.
- **A credit memo is a line below zero**, so a payment's lines add up to what was sent, the way a house's statement nets its credits.
- **Every bill already paid has one payment "from before"**, for its whole amount on the day it was marked paid, with the receipt link that was pasted on it.

## How it is held together

Migration `20261116230000_supply_house_payments.sql` (its note is in `docs/migrations/`): two tables, the triggers that keep `is_paid` true to the lines in both directions, `record_supply_house_payment` (the payment and its lines in one statement, for the window PR 2 brings), and the backfill.

## Tests

`supabase/tests/supply_house_payments/` is a bed with the baseline's real triggers on `supply_house_invoices`: the backfill (twice, writing no bill row), Mark paid and not paid by hand, a typed paid day and an edited amount, one payment over several bills, a part payment, every refusal, a payment removed and put back, a bill moved or deleted, money on account placed later, a credit memo as a line, the office-only gate as the API's role under row-level security, and a closing pass that holds every bill against its lines. `npm run test:pg:supply-house-payments` runs it in Docker. It was run here under PGlite (Postgres in WASM), where seventeen deliberate breaks of the migration were each caught.

A fresh reader reviewed the money arithmetic before the merge and found six defects in the first draft. Each is closed and has its scenario; the migration's note lists them.
