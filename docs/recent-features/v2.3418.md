## Why

The 2026-09-14 census: 149 clock-ins in 60 days on 15 of the 31 Working rows, 54 sub sheets on open jobs, 26 status reports — and the Progress & payment cell reads none of it, only the two numbers the office types. The redesign needs one round trip that says where the crew is on every row.

## What shipped

- **RPC** `list_job_crew_position(job_ids, today)` — last clock day + who was there, 60-day counts, the newest sub sheet (stage / names / date / progress), the newest report percent + time, the newest hand-set percent time. Office roles only; SECURITY DEFINER; read-only. Details in the migration doc.
- **Kernel** [`jobCrewPosition.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobCrewPosition.ts) (+9 tests): `crewPositionsFromRpc` (parse, `onSiteToday`), `splitSheetNames` / `firstName` / `crewShortName` (*Miguel* · *Behar & Malachi* · *Behar +2* · *Behar's crew* · *Texas Rooter*), `newestPercent` (the v2.3372 newest-wins rule on the row: a hand-set at or after the newest report wins), `percentIsStale` (the percent on record predates the last clock-in — Heron's Aug 7 seed against a Sep 12 clock-in; Mission Hills' Sep 3 hand-set against Sep 11).
- **Hook** [`useJobCrewPositions`](../../src/hooks/useJobCrewPositions.ts): one call for the board's ids, order-insensitive re-fetch, fail-soft to an empty map (RPC missing, role refused, any error).
- `src/types/database.ts`: the Functions entry hand-added; the post-push regen replaces it.
