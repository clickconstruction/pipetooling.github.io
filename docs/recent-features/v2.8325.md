# v2.8325 — Send a paper: a second person at the same company gets their own copy (2026-11-22)

Punch list #403, the owner's third decision, which is the audit's finding 3. Client only. No migration and no edge function.

## The decision

The card's pick: the window says who already has the paper out, and offers to send it to them again or to send a second copy to someone else. On 2026-11-22 the owner was asked "Take the card's pick on all four of #403's decisions?" and answered "Yes, all four".

## What was so before

A company's paper is filed under the company's own contact. The same paper sent to a second person there found the first person's copy and reused it. The first signer's name was replaced, their link stopped working, and the office saw one line. Nothing in the window said so.

## What changed

- **Picking a company that has the paper out opens a small window first.** It says who has it and the day it went, one line a copy. Nothing is written by it.
- **Send it to … again** opens the email window on that copy, as **Send again** on its line does. It says the earlier link stops working.
- **Send a second copy to someone else** opens the email window empty. Its send writes a row of its own from the Contract Book, so the copy that is out is never reused. The tick that keeps a new signer with the company works as on a first send.
- **A person on file who has it out** is offered the same two presses. A second copy is then the company's, since someone else signs it.

## How it reads the copies

The look is a read alone. `findPartyContact` reads the contact a record already has, where `readPartyContact` would make one. A record with no contact has no paper. `partyPapersOut` then picks the copies that are sent and not signed from the papers the window already read.

## What stays as it was

- Someone on no list is found by their address, so a second send to the same address is the same person, and goes to their copy again.
- A copy that was made and never sent is not out, and is reused by the next send as before.
- A person on the roster: People's rule, the same copy again, and the window already said so.

## The guide

*find an agreement we sent or signed* said "The window says so" of a paper that is already out. That was true for a roster person only. It gains the two presses for an outside company.
