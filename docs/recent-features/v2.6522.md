# v2.6522 — The Lien desk says what a pressed step is showing, so a faded row reads as filtered, not blocked (2026-11-04)

Grace, 2026-11-04, with a screenshot of Do now's REMINDERS strip: eight courtesy rows pale and four letter-two rows bright, and the question why some could be done and some could not. Nothing was blocked. Rung 3 of the REMINDERS rail was pressed, and a pressed rung draws every row off it at 0.3 opacity (`dimmed` in `LienDeskNextUp.tsx`, v2.4631). Nothing on the screen said the fade was a filter. The explainer Grace showed the owner is a private artifact; its section 5 is the mock of this change.

## What changed

- **`lienNextUpSteps.ts`**: `lienStepShowingWords(on, shown, total)` → `{ head, count, clear }` — *Showing 3 · Letter two*, *4 of 15 on the list · the rest are faded, not held* (or *every row on the list is on this step*, or *none of the N … · all are faded, not held*), *Show all 15*. Pure, one test.
- **`lienReminders.ts`**: `lienReminderRungRule(step)` → `{ when, press, who, movesOn }` and `lienReminderRungRuleWords(step)`, the rule as one line, built from `COURTESY_FROM_DAY`, `COURTESY_AGAIN_DAYS`, `LETTER_TWO_FROM_DAY` and `LETTER_TWO_BY_DAY` so the words follow a rule when it changes. One test.
- **`LienDeskSteps.tsx`**: `LienStepShowingLine` (`data-testid="lien-step-showing"`, `data-ladder`, `data-step`) in the ladder's own tone under the rails, with `Show all N` (`lien-step-show-all`) that clears the rung as a second press on it does.
- **`LienDeskNextUp.tsx`**: draws the line while `on` is set, counting the rows `dimmed` leaves bright over the list's rows; the REMINDERS rung's list under the rail (`lien-reminder-step-list`) opens with the rung's rule (`lien-reminder-rung-rule`).
- **Guide** `read-the-reminders-ladder-on-the-lien-desk`: the press and the fade, the four steps as cards, why two rows at day 21 offer different buttons, the held courtesy's four reasons in a fold, the names behind a count. `docs/twins/APP_DIRECTORY.md` names it on the Lien desk's courtesy line.
- **Smokes** in `LienDeskNextUp.render.test.tsx`: the line's words and count at 1,400 and 375, *Show all* clears the rung and every `data-dim`, the REMINDERS rung's rule reads under its name.

## What it never does

Change a rule, send anything, or move a row. The fade is as it was; the desk now says what it means.
