# v2.3014 — Test-gap audit, pass 26: the job day-ledger loader gets its first tests (2026-09-07)

Follow-up to v2.3013. Tests only; no kernel, client, migration or edge-function change.

## Why this one

`jobs/loadJobDayLedger.ts` (192 lines) feeds Job Summary's Days and Timeline views: the same scans the 90-day overhead snapshot runs, widened to any window, plus each touched job's label, status spans (working → billed / paid) and approved hours before the window. The kernel (`buildJobDayLedger`) and the labor builders were tested; the loader was not.

## What the tests found

Nothing wrong. Contracts now pinned that were only implied:

- The overhead read takes office-job sessions plus any bid session, the field read real jobs minus the office job, both paged and bounded to the window and carrying notes and the assignee name; the invoice read is a day wide on both sides with Stripe test-mode excluded and re-bucketed to Chicago days.
- Touched jobs are the ones with approved field sessions in the window. Their labels use the HCP number, else the Click number, else an em dash; status spans take the first working / ready-to-bill / billed / paid event as the start, the first billed or paid as the end, with events sorted by time and days keyed in Chicago; "waiting" opens nothing. Prior hours count only approved, closed, unrejected sessions before the window, summed per job.
- Label and status-event reads fail soft to empty maps; a failed prior-hours read throws. Without an office job the parts loader is not called. No touched jobs means no lookups at all.
- Cancellation returns null at whichever checkpoint is reached first: after the session reads, after the parts hand-off, after the invoice read, or after the job lookups.
- In a three-day scenario the numbers reconcile by hand: pool $160 / $0 / $50, field 0 / 8 / 3 hours, $240 labor, $1,500 invoiced, one pending session of three hours, 3.5 prior hours on one job.
