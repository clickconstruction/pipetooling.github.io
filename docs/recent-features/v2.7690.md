# v2.7690 — Painted maps: closing an unsaved walk can keep it or throw it away (2026-11-17)

The owner's ask from a walk on a phone: **Close the map?** offered one way out, *Close, and keep the walk on this phone*, over *Stay*. Throwing the walk away took a trip back to **Look it over** and its **Throw it away**.

## What changed

- **Close the map?** now has three presses, stacked the width of the window: **Close, and keep the walk on this phone** (blue), **Close, and throw the walk away** (red), **Stay**. The body says what each does.
- **Close, and throw the walk away** clears the walk and the lines looked over, writes nothing to the phone's kept walk and closes the window, so the app has nothing to offer back. It is the confirm of its own question: no second window.
- **Close, and keep the walk on this phone** does what the one press did before.
- `ConfirmDialog` takes an optional `keep: { label, onPress }`: a second way to go ahead that loses nothing, drawn in blue over the red press. With it the presses stack and each is 44 px tall. A caller that passes none draws as before.

## Words

The owner said *close and discard*. The press reads *throw the walk away*, the words **Throw it away** already uses on Look it over and on the walk the app offers back.

## Not changed

- Closing a line drawn by hand still asks *Stop without saving?* with two presses: a line drawn by hand is not kept on the phone, so there is nothing to keep.
- **Throw it away** on Look it over keeps its own confirm.

## Checked

- `PaintedMapWindow.walk.render.test.tsx`: the three presses in order, Stay, and the throw-away closing with the phone's kept walk cleared and no pause.
- Not walked on a phone before the merge.
