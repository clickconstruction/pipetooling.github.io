# v2.5722 — Messages: a teammate's email lands too (2026-10-21)

The owner, on the desk train's live walk (punch list #140 § 3): his own reply to a test customer's email never landed, because the reader leaves out mail from any staff address. "I don't like that rule. I want email to come in from team members' addresses. This could eventually turn into a way where the field can communicate with the office." Both steps he picked are here.

- **The rule** (`src/lib/emailIn/leftOut.ts`): `leftOut` takes `isTheBox` — the boxes' own addresses and the app's sender (`EMAIL_FROM`) — and leaves out only those as ours; every staff address was left out with them before. Spam, trash, other tabs, bulk, auto-submitted and no-reply are unchanged.
- **Whose thread** (`src/lib/emailIn/emailLine.ts`): `emailParty` takes `OurAddresses` (`isOurs`, `isTheBox`) and reads `to` and `cc`. A teammate's mail to the box with nobody outside named is theirs, coming in, so it lands on their Our team thread (every roster person has one since v2.5546) and shows in New or Needs a reply like their texts. A teammate's mail to a customer with the box copied is ours to that customer, going out: the line sits on the customer's thread with `who_user_id` the teammate, named *Rosa Diaz <rosa@…> to Elena Marchetti <elena@…> · copied to team@* (`emailLine`). The box's own or the app's sender still names nobody. Forwarded copies read as before.
- **The reader** (`supabase/functions/gmail-sync/index.ts`): `loadTeam` returns `isTheBox` beside `isOurs`; `takeOne` passes the box set to `leftOut` and both to `emailParty`; the out line's sender is the teammate whether the mail was forwarded or copied. The edge copies regenerated (`npm run gen:edge-kernels`). **Deploy `gmail-sync`** after merge.
- **Placement**: `email_place_address` is unchanged; a teammate's address lands on the contact that holds it with the latest message, which is their Our team contact unless a customer record carries the same address (the owner's own test customer does).
- **Words**: the guide *turn on reading the bids inbox* gains *A teammate's email*; `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` § gmail-sync, `MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md` email in, the Glossary's Read bids@ entry.
- **Not done**: a teammate's reply-all that names the customer first still lands as ours to the customer, which is what it is; an out-of-office is caught by the auto-submitted rule as before.
