# v2.6352 — Projects on the leader's Dashboard: one line under Money in, open to the board's cards (punch list #242, PR 6) (2026-11-02)

The owner, 2026-11-02, after the board shipped: *on a leader's dashboard I would like to see the projects section in a collapsible way below the finances section with Accounts Receivable and below the Money in row … where it can collapse down to the size of Money in.* Client only.

## What landed

- **`src/components/dashboard/DashboardProjectsStrip.tsx`**, mounted in `DashboardFinancialsSection` right under `DashboardMoneyInStrip`, for `dev` and `master_technician` (the office has the board and the Pipeline press; the Dashboard's read is the leader's). Money in's frame, padding and type sizes, so closed it is the same height: *Projects · 13 working · 8 behind · 3 ahead · 2 no reading yet · J1050 · The Bunker · money ahead · Open ▾*. Pressed open: *Projects · 13 working, worst first · Open the board → · Close ▴* over the board's own `ProjectCard`s (the strip, the slider, the reason, the card large), or the empty state's door to the Pipeline. The choice is kept per device (`dashboard-projects-open`), as Money in's is. While the projects read: *Projects · reading the projects…*; a refused read draws `OfflineRetryPanel` in the frame.
- **`src/hooks/useProjectsBoard.ts`** — the board's read and cards, lifted out of `pages/ProjectsBoard.tsx` (the Working jobs with their lines, the Costs tab's bulk cost read, the budget rows, the checklists' count, the expected hours, `projectPace` worst first) so the page and the strip share one assembly; the page keeps its own JSX.
- **`paceCounts`** and **`worstWords`** in `lib/jobs/projectsBoard.ts`: the counts by tone (the money leaders counted inside behind) and the worst project's three words (*money ahead*, *N d behind*, *budget?*).
- The guide *see where every project stands and set its expected hours* gains *The leader's Dashboard*; `APP_DIRECTORY.md` (`/dashboard` and the board), `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`, the Glossary.

## Decisions

- **The leader and a dev only.** The owner asked for the leader's Dashboard; the assistant and the controller keep the board page and the Pipeline press, and the Dashboard's heaviest read (each project's cost timeline) stays off their home.
- **The same cards, not a digest.** Open, the strip is the board: a leader moves a number from here with the same slider and the same reason, and the page's Recent changes and Undo stay on the page.
