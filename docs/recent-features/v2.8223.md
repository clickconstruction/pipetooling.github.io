# v2.8223 — Job window on a phone: the Note box is whole above the keyboard, it tags, and the Activity box is one box (2026-11-21)

Three fixes to one flow, writing a note in the job window on a phone, asked for by the owner after a phone walk on 2026-11-21 (an iPhone 17 simulator, Safari, iOS 26.2, and a 375 px browser, on two ZZ Test jobs). Client only: no migration and no function.

## 1. The bar was drawn over the Note sheet

**What was seen.** With the Note sheet open and the keyboard up, the window's bar (**Status ▾ · Ready to bill · Arrived · Note**) was drawn over the sheet, right above the keyboard. One line of the note box showed, **Save** was hidden, and two presses that write sat where a thumb lands. In a 375 px desktop browser the sheet covered the bar.

**The cause, as read from the code and the picture.** The sheet was cut, not covered: in the picture its backdrop and its panel both end on the bar's top edge, and the bar is not dimmed. The sheet is `position: fixed`, and `DetailJobModal` mounted it inside the tabbed window's body, the box that scrolls. That body carried `-webkit-overflow-scrolling: touch`. An iPhone has scrolled every box that way by itself since iOS 13, and what the property still does there is make the box a layer of its own (WebKit gives it a stacking context). A window pinned to the screen from inside such a layer is clipped to the layer, so the sheet ended where the body ends, which is where the bar starts. A desktop browser ignores the property, which is why the fault never showed at a desk. `PhoneSheet`'s own keyboard rule (v2.7188, the visible band) was right all along: the sheet's foot was on the keyboard's top, under the bar.

This was not proved on a device by the session that built the fix: it had a 375 px Chromium pane, where the fault does not show. So the fix does not lean on the diagnosis alone. Three things changed, and each holds by itself.

- **The sheet is drawn at the top of the page.** `JobWindowNoteSheet` renders its `PhoneSheet` through a portal to `document.body`. Nothing in the window can clip it or sit over it: the sheet's layer is z 1300 at the root, the window's is 1010. React still hands its presses up the window's tree, so the sheet's × closes the sheet alone, as before.
- **The bar stands down while a sheet is up.** `jobWindowBarStandsDown({ status, more, note })` in the new kernel `src/lib/jobs/jobWindowSheets.ts` is true while any of the window's own sheets is open: Status ▾, the ⋯ list, or Note. `JobWindowActionBar` takes `standsDown` and draws itself `visibility: hidden` with `aria-hidden`: it keeps its room, so the body behind does not change height, and it takes no press. The pane tells the window its Note sheet is open through `JobPanePhoneBar.noteOpen`. The crew's plain window does the same with its own bar.
- **The body scrolls without the layer.** Both windows' bodies now take `JOB_WINDOW_BODY_SCROLL` (`overflowY: auto`, `overscrollBehavior: contain`) from that kernel, with no `-webkit-overflow-scrolling`. Any other window opened from inside the body (Reports, the calendar, a link's window) is no longer cut to it on an iPhone. Scrolling itself does not change on iOS 13 or later.

## 2. The Note sheet had no @ row

The card for punch list #363 and its fragments (v2.7400 to v2.7426) say nothing of leaving the sheet out. PR 6 gave the tags to `JobThreadNotesPanel`, and the sheet is a second box over the same draft that nobody wired.

- `JobWindowNoteSheet` now calls the composer's own `useNoteTagBox` (`src/components/jobs/NoteTagBox.tsx`) with the job's id. Nothing is copied: the names (`job_note_taggable`), the ranking, the tint under the name (`TagMarkLayer`), the **Will tag** line with its ×, the keys and the one-Backspace rule are the hook's.
- **A third place for the row.** `useNoteTagBox`'s `place` takes `'inline'` beside `'above'` and `'below'`. The row is then in the flow, not hung over the box, at most 124 px tall and scrolling past that. In a sheet from the bottom a hung row would be cut by the sheet's own edge. In the flow the sheet grows by the row, so the row is above the box, and the box is on the keyboard.
- The **@** press is the 44 px one (`bigAt`), over **Save**. The sheet's body scrolls inside the sheet if it ever outgrows it.
- **Save** hands `threadNotes.submitNote` the ids of the names still in the words, the same call **Post note** makes, so a note from the sheet tags exactly as one from the Activity box does: through the outbox, `land_field_press` and `job_note_tags_write` (v2.7412).
- Who is offered it: `canTagInJobNotes(role)` and a loaded job, as in the composer.

One thing to know: the sheet and the Activity box share the words but not the picks. A name picked in the sheet, with the sheet then closed unsaved, is plain words in the Activity box. The box shows it untinted and without **Will tag**, so the screen says so.

## 3. Fewer boxes around the Activity note box

The owner: "I don't love how many boxes are going on here. I think we have limited space on mobile and we're not using enough here."

Measured in Chromium at 375 px, on the tabbed window's Activity fold:

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Boxes around the words you type, counting the note box | 4 (the fold's card, the panel, the composer, the note box) | 2 (the fold's card, the note box) |
| The note box's width | 273 px | 320.2 px |
| The note box's words | 14 px | 16 px |
| The hint | *Type a note… (Enter to post, Shift+Enter for new line)*, on two lines | *Type a note…* |
| Note · Text · Email | 24.8 px tall | 44 px |
| Set % complete | 26.6 px | 44 px |
| @ | 19.6 px | 44 px, a square |
| Post note | 27.4 px | 44 px |
| The row under the box | one line | one line (Set % complete 114 px, @ 44, Post note 82, in 320) |

- **`src/lib/jobs/noteComposerLook.ts`**, a kernel: `noteComposerLook({ phone, hostCard })` gives `flat`, the hint, the words' size and the least height of a press. `phone` is the job window's own test, `useNarrowViewport640`.
- **`JobThreadNotesPanel`** takes `hostCard`. `DetailJobModal` passes it on a phone (`phoneShape`), in the tabbed window's Activity fold and in the crew window's Notes and reports fold, where a `PhoneFold` card is already around the panel. Flat, the panel has no border, fill or side padding, and the composer has no border, fill, radius or side padding: one hairline above it. That hairline lies on the list's foot, over the last entry's own rule, so the two never read as a double line.
- The short hint, the 16 px words and the 44 px presses are for every phone mount. The % editor's Cancel and **Set to N%** are 44 px too. The Text and Email boxes (`JobCustomerBox`) are their own component and were not touched.
- At a desk nothing changes: measured at 1,024 px, the panel and the composer keep their boxes, the words are 14 px, the hint is the long one, and the presses are the heights in the Before column.

**The other mounts, at phone width.** They pass no `hostCard`, so each keeps its boxes.

- The estimates list's card (`EstimateListTable`): seen at 375 px. The panel and the composer are boxed as before, the hint is the short one, the words 16 px, **Post note** 44 px, no sideways scroll.
- Job Mode's card (`JobModeDetailsSection`) and Quickfill's (`QuickfillCompleteNoBillSection`): not seen. Job Mode's card draws the panel only for a person clocked onto a job, and clocking in on the live record is a write. Their render tests pass, and the estimates card shows the same un-hosted look.
- The Pipeline row does not mount this panel. Its box is `JobsStagesActivityBox`, which is unchanged.

## Walked

A 375 px Chromium pane on the dev server, signed in as the dev, on the ZZ Test Walk Project job. Nothing was posted or saved.

- The Activity fold: the numbers in the table above, and one hairline over the composer.
- **Note** on the bar: the sheet's layer is a child of `body`, the bar is `visibility: hidden`, and `elementFromPoint` at the bar's centre answers the sheet. Typing `@sa` opens the row above the box with four names, in the flow. A tap writes the name, tints it and shows **Will tag**. One Backspace takes the whole name off. The sheet's × closes it and the bar is back.
