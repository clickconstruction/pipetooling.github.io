# v2.7310 — Materials desk: Open a job is a list you can search (2026-11-15)

Client only. The owner, looking at the Materials desk on 2026-11-15: *I really don't like this dropdown menu, can you help me improve it, I would like a search at the top as well.* The **Open a job** pick under the desk was a native `<select>` of every job the office can reach, newest number first, which could only scroll.

## What changed

- **A kernel** (`src/lib/materials/deskJobPick.ts`, pure, 3 tests): a row's search words are the job as the desk reads it plus its address, so a typed street finds the job as a number does; the jobs that hold a box come first in the desk's own order under **On the desk**, then every other job under **Every other job**, nothing listed twice.
- **The pick** (`JobsMaterialsTab.tsx`, the desk's foot on both mounts) is the app's own `SearchableSelect` with *Type a number, a name or an address* as its search, the list filling the screen on a phone as the Development page's About pick does. The empty state's **Open a job** press puts the focus on the new trigger.

## Tests

`deskJobPick.test.ts`; the desk's render tests (`JobsMaterialsTab.desk.render.test.tsx`, `MaterialsOnSiteTab.site.render.test.tsx`) pass unchanged.
