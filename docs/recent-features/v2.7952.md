# v2.7952 — Projects board: a long job name is cut inside its card (2026-11-19)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Noticed in passing on the live walk of the superintendent's pace card (punch list #396) and measured again before the fix.

## What was wrong

Measured 2026-11-19 on a local preview against live data, as the dev, on `/projects-board`. Of 18 cards, two hold long job names (88 and 75 characters).

| Window | Card's room | The two cards ran past their edge by | The page scrolled sideways by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1024 wide | 463 px | 202 px and 87 px | 160 px |
| 1280 wide | 504 px | 161 px and 46 px | 32 px |
| 1440 wide | 504 px | 161 px and 46 px | 0 |
| a 375 px phone | 337 px | 328 px and 213 px | the page drew 690 px wide |

Two causes, each found by taking one thing away and measuring again.

- **The card's column.** `ProjectCardBody` is a grid with no `gridTemplateColumns`, so its one column is `auto` and grows to its widest row. The title is one line (`whiteSpace: 'nowrap'`), so the header row's least width is the whole name: the column drew 652 px wide inside a card with 478 px of room. The title's own `minWidth: 0` and `textOverflow: 'ellipsis'` never applied, because its row was never narrower than its words. Hiding the header row alone cleared the overflow; hiding any other row did not.
- **The grid's floor on a phone.** With the first cause fixed, a 375 px phone still scrolled 5 px. The cards' grid is `repeat(auto-fill, minmax(340px, 1fr))`, and the page has 327 px of room there (8 px of the app's inset and 16 px of the page's a side), so one 340 px track was wider than its room.

## What shipped

- `src/components/projectsBoard/ProjectCard.tsx`: the card's grid takes `gridTemplateColumns: 'minmax(0, 1fr)'`. Every row is now held to the card's width, so the title's ellipsis draws. The large view is the same component; its window's title wraps and shows the whole name.
- `src/pages/ProjectsBoard.tsx` and `src/components/dashboard/DashboardProjectsStrip.tsx`: the cards' grid is `repeat(auto-fill, minmax(min(340px, 100%), 1fr))`, so the 340 px floor gives to a narrower room. The Dashboard's strip had the same floor inside a frame that clips (`overflow: 'hidden'`); it was not measured before the fix.

## Checked

- Measured after the fix at 375, 1024, 1280 and 1440 wide, on the board and on the Dashboard's Projects strip pressed open: no card's `scrollWidth` exceeds its `clientWidth`, both long titles are cut, and the page does not scroll sideways. The large view at 1280: no overflow, the window's title wraps.
- `e2e/viewport-smoke.spec.ts`: *Projects board: a long job name stays inside its card* at those four widths. It writes a made-up long name into every card's title on the page, then holds that no card's rows run past its edge, every title is cut and the page does not scroll sideways. The same script run in the preview with the card's column taken out read 890 px past the edge and 18 titles drawn whole. E2E smoke runs in Actions after a deploy, not on a PR.
- `ProjectCard.render.test.tsx`, `ProjectsBoard.render.test.tsx` and `DashboardProjectsStrip.render.test.tsx` pin the three styles, since jsdom draws no layout.

## Left

- At 320 px wide every card still runs 23 to 47 px past its edge. That is a third cause: the expected-hours row holds its words, the hours, the days left and the budget's word at fixed widths beside the slider, and has no room at that width. It needs a call on which part takes a second line.
- Three other grids keep a bare `minmax(340px, 1fr)` floor (Settings → Usage, the person desk as a page, Match clock sessions). Not measured here.
