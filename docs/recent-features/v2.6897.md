# v2.6897 — A bill takes its lines when the paper prints its number with a suffix the office did not type (2026-11-10)

Punch list #298 (`to-dos/supply-bill-lines/README.md`), the first change the measure after PR 2 ([v2.6882](v2.6882.md)) asked for, on the owner's call of 2026-11-10: *yes, match them*. The sweep gave each bill its paper by the invoice number exactly, and 23 of the 42 bills held as *the number is not in its file* were in their file under a longer number: the paper prints a shipment or a release after it (`.001`, ` 01`), and the office typed the number without it. Each of those papers sat in its file with no bill.

- **`matchRows`** (`supabase/functions/_shared/billSweep.ts`) now runs in two passes. Every row first takes the bill that carries its number exactly, as before. A row still without one then takes a bill whose printed number is its own with a suffix of one to three digits (`numberWithSuffix`: both folded, the typed number the start of the printed one), and **only when that paper's total is the row's amount to the cent**, since the number alone no longer says it. The exact pass runs for every row first, so a shorter number never takes a paper another row names outright. A suffixed paper whose total is not the row's stays with no bill and the row stays held.
- **Every hold gets one more read.** A hold's `seen_key` now ends with the count of the match's rules (`MATCH_RULES`, 2), so a hold made under the old rule is due again (`holdDue`) and the sweep reads its file once more. The 51 holds on file come back by themselves over the next runs; the ones another try cannot help are held again with the same reason.
- Tests in `src/lib/materials/lines/billSweep.test.ts`: the suffix match, the total that must agree, the exact number's precedence, what counts as a suffix, the hold that is due under new rules.

No migration and no screen. Deploy `bill-lines-sweep` after the merge.

Not in this PR: the four rules the check kernel does not know yet (a price rounded on the paper, a pack priced by the piece, a charge outside the subtotal, a rebate line that is negative), which the measure also found.
