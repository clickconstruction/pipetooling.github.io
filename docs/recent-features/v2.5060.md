# v2.5060 — GC mode: questions about the plans while we build, for a dev (2026-10-09)

The Building lane's U5b in the GC mode real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/building-u5.md`, on branch `spike/gc-mode`). It is the window over U5a's presses (v2.5050, migration `20261010012000_gc_rfi_writes`) and their generated types, and the kind `rfi` on the email that takes a question to the architect. It is dev only, behind the dev door, like the rest of Building.

- **`src/components/gc/GcRfisWindow.tsx`** opens from **RFIs** on a building job's card on `/gc` (`?rfis=<project id>`), after Submittals, behind `canUseGcBuilding`. Ported from the prototype's `GcBuildingRfis.tsx`:
  - the counts strip: waiting on us, with the architect, answered, and due now;
  - **Ask a question**: the question, the trade or our own work, who asked (our superintendent, or the company awarded the trade, the lead's call 5), the sheets, the days before the work, and the work it holds. Only the job's own scope lines on the schedule are offered, and a line whose own sheets the question names is ticked as it matches (`holdsForSheets`); one the office unticks stays off;
  - each RFI, open first and the most urgent first: **Send to {architect}** (the email), **We sent it another way** or **Answer it ourselves** while it is ours, then **Record their answer**, with what it changes: nothing, the plans, or cost and days. One sent by our email says so;
  - **Start a change order** on a cost answer, for the money team only (`canSeeGcMoney`, the lead's call 4). It drafts the change order through `gc_rfi_change_order` with the kernels' words and price (`rfiChangeOrderDescription`, `changeOrderPrice` with the job's fee, call 2) and opens **Change orders** on the job. An answer that adds days only says to ask for the days on the schedule (call 3). A started one reads *Change order 3 was started from it*, or *A change order was started from it* to anyone who does not read change orders.
- **`src/lib/gc/rfiRows.ts`**: the rows as the prototype's `Rfi` (`withRfis(state, tables)` over `boardProjectFromView`'s projects, with `change_order_id` as `changeOrderId`), each RFI's email and recorder beside it, the presses' payloads, and `holdsForSheets`. **`src/lib/gc/rfisIo.ts`**: `loadGcRfis` and one function per press. The window opens through 6a's `loadSchedule`, so an RFI's answer is needed before the first start of the work it holds, and the change orders are laid over as the money team reads them.
- **`gc-architect-email`** gains the kind `rfi`: it reads the RFI as the caller, sends the question, its sheets, who asked, the work it holds and the day we need the answer from `COMPANY_EMAIL_FROM` with the project manager as Reply-To, files the sent copy (kind `gc_rfi`), and records the send through `gc_send_rfi_to_architect` as the caller. The kind `submittal` sends as before. Its words are `buildGcRfiEmail` in `_shared/gcArchitectEmail.ts`, shared with the sample on Settings → What customers see (journey step *RFI for an answer*, sample `gc-rfi`).
- **Docs:** `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` (the kind `rfi`), `docs/GLOSSARY.md` (*RFI*), `docs/PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` §20 and `docs/ACCESS_CONTROL.md` (the window, Building's dev gate, the money team's change order), and the guide *ask the architect a question while we build* (`roles: dev` until Building's door).
- **Tests:** `rfiRows.test.ts` (Fair Oaks D's four read back, the kernels' words the same, payloads, the sheet match), `gcArchitectEmail.test.ts` (the `rfi` email), `GcRfisWindow.render.test.tsx` (each state's press, the money team's button, the days-only words, the sheet ticks, plain words), and the page's cases in `GcProjects.render.test.tsx`.
