# v2.6679 — Legal desk: the claim, line by line, on the Account tab (punch list #171, PR 1) (2026-11-08)

Punch list #171 (`to-dos/legal-desk-claim/README.md`, rank 6 of the ten to-dos drawn from the Burd matter, job 891), the owner's Do of 2026-11-07; PR 1 of 3, the card's step 1. The desk sized a case by its open bills, and since v2.5849 by the open bills plus the retainage no sent bill carries (v2.6103). A dispute can claim more: materials left on site, damages, interest, attorney fees. On job 891 those lines lived only inside the demand letters, and the settlement floor could only be written into the narrative as a sentence. This PR gives them a home on the matter.

## What changed

- **Migration [`20261108070000_legal_matter_claim_lines.sql`](../migrations/20261108070000_legal_matter_claim_lines.md)**: `legal_matter_claim_lines` (kind · title · amount · basis · an Evidence document of the same matter · who and when · `removed_at`), office `SELECT`, writes only through `legal_claim_line_save` (add or edit; the matter is made when the account has none, the `legal_answer_gap` ensure; refused once closed) and `legal_claim_line_remove` (kept as removed). The four fences close the table.
- **Kernel `src/lib/legal/legalClaimLines.ts`** (pure, 10 tests): the kinds and their words, `claimLinesFromRows` (the rows in the order added, the document's title resolved), `claimTyped`, the Add form's draft and `claimLineDraftProblem` (the RPC's rules client side), `claimAmountOf` (`$5,310.00` reads), and `claimTable(worth, jobLabelOf)` — the bills row always (its basis counts the billed lines), the retainage row when a job holds some (its basis names the jobs and *Our contract on this job*, its amount the part no sent bill asks for, with v2.6103's sentence under the table when part is already on a bill), the typed rows, and the total `balance + retainageAdded + claimTyped`.
- **`legalPacket.ts`**: `LegalPacketInput.claimLines` (optional) and on `LegalWorth` `claimLines`, `claimTyped` and `retainageHeldByJob`. `claim` itself is unchanged in this PR: the tile folds the typed lines in at the card's step 2 (PR 2), so for the span between the two merges the table's total can read above the tile.
- **`useLegalPacketData.ts`**: one more read, the matter's live claim lines with each document's title, when the account has a matter.
- **`LegalClaimTable.tsx`** (new) on the Account tab above *Invoices and payments* (`LegalDeskPacketTab.tsx`, the `claim` prop): the table with a *fills in* chip on the two read rows, the Evidence cell a door to the document or the job, **Edit** on a typed row, **＋ Add a claim line** (Part of the claim · Name · Amount · Basis · Evidence document; the name follows the kind until typed), **Save the line**, **Remove the line…** behind a confirm whose button says *Remove the line*. Read only for anyone outside the office's edit gate, and on a closed matter. `LegalDesk.tsx` wires `saveClaimLine` / `removeClaimLine` over the RPCs; a save on an account with no matter reads the matters again so the new one is known.
- **Words**: the guide *review a collections account before it goes to your attorney* (the Account tab's bullet), `docs/ACCESS_CONTROL.md` (a Legal desk row), `docs/PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` (the page's line), the card's status.

## Open calls, met with the card's own words

- The retainage row reads the one source #165 picked (v2.6010 to v2.6027): the packet's `claimRetainage` figure, `jobs_ledger.lien_retainage_held` less what a sent bill already carries.
- Whether the firm sees each line or only the total: the firm's portal is unchanged in this train (it never computed the worth); the printed packet, which the firm receives, draws the table in PR 3. Whether a line can be marked *settlement only*: not built; the owner's look decides. Both stay on the card.

## Deploy

`supabase db push` right after the merge (the client calls the table and the RPCs by name, so `check:calls-ahead` holds the deploy until the push), then `npm run gen-types:linked` as its own `chore(types)` PR. No edge function.

## Verify

On a ZZ TEST matter with one open bill and retainage held: Legal desk → Account → The claim shows both rows on its own, the retainage row naming the job. **＋ Add a claim line** → Materials left on site, $500, a basis, an Evidence document → **Save the line** → the row sits under the two, its document a door, the total $500 higher. **Edit** → **Remove the line…** → *Remove the line* → the row is gone and the total is back. On a closed matter the table draws with no Add press.
