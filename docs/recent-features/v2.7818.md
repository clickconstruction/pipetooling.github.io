# v2.7818 — Materials on site: a site's presses, by person (2026-11-18)

The first PR of a train on seeing what one person does with the material on a site. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## Why

The owner asked to see what one person did with a site's material without opening each job's History in turn. A site's boxes sit under several jobs, and History is one job at a time with a person filter. The rows were already there, append-only and stamped with who pressed; nothing read them across a site by person.

## What landed

- **By person**, a third look on a site's materials screen beside By part and By place ([`SitePeopleView.tsx`](../../src/components/materials/SitePeopleView.tsx)). Every ledger row and every box move across the site's jobs is one act, newest first and grouped by day, with when, what, the part, the quantity, the box and its place, who, and the note. A box's name opens the box.
- **The pure kernel** [`sitePersonActs.ts`](../../src/lib/materials/sitePersonActs.ts): the acts and their words, the people (the name on the row, as a job's History filters by it, since a box move carries a name and no id), the filters, the four figures and the day groups. A count reads what the ledger expected against what was found, from one pass over the rows in time order that sums as `containerLines` does; a box is one job's, so one job's count never reads another job's line.
- **Worth a look**: three kinds of act wear an amber chip and have their own filter. A count that changed a number the app already had for the line. A part added by hand to a line that already had a number, with no invoice or PO behind it. A take or a use with no note. A first count of a line wears no chip, nor the first part added to one, nor a count that found what was expected: on the first live site nearly every row was a box being counted in, by Count or by Add a part, and a chip on each said nothing. The chips are the same for everyone: whose process a press breaks is not known here.
- **Who sees it.** The desk's mount offers the pill to the office's roles (`isOfficeRole`: dev, master_technician, assistant, controller). A primary reads the same desk and is not offered it, and the crew's mount never passes the prop. It is a client gate over rows those roles already read, not a new rule in the database. The device remembers By part or By place and never By person.
- **No new read.** The look draws from the on-site store the desk has already filled, with the box moves the store already holds (`listContainerMoves`). A job past the store's ledger cap shows the rows the store holds.

## What it does not do yet

- A box retired, brought back, renamed or edited is not in the list: those changes do not record who made them. The next PR of the train adds that trail.
- It does not say when a press reached the server, only when it was made.
- Nothing here writes, and nothing tells anyone when a press lands.

## Tests

- `sitePersonActs.test.ts`: the words of each kind, a count against the ledger's expectation, a first count and a right count with no chip, two jobs kept apart, the three chips and no other, a box move with a box or a place that is gone, the people, the filters, the figures, the day groups.
- `SitePeopleView.render.test.tsx`: the list by day with its figures, one person picked, the worth a look filter with its way out, a box's door, the two empty states.
- `SiteMaterialsView.render.test.tsx`: the pill is offered only with `canSeePeople`, and the device does not remember it.
