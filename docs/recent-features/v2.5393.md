# v2.5393 — Messages: a job’s activity inside a person’s thread, behind the scenes (2026-10-12)

Residential customers in Messages, cut R1's third PR (the thread's switch), lifted from the customer messages prototype v0.8, screen 8 (`to-dos/gc-mode/messages/customer-messages-prototype.html` on branch `spike/gc-mode`: `ROWS`, `paintCmp`'s `herJobs` and `personRows`, `counts`, `showAct` and `setAct`). The plan is `RESIDENTIAL_BUILD_PLAN.md` → *R1* beside it. A pure kernel with its test. No screen, no migration, no edge function. The other direction, the customer's lines inside the job activity panel, is cut 10's registry work and is not touched here.

- **`src/lib/messages/activityRows.ts`:**
  - `threadWithActivity(messages, activityByJob, { on, noMoney })` merges a person's lines with the job activity items of the jobs their lines are tied to, oldest first, by the job panel's own `activitySortMs`. `activityByJob` is kept per job, keyed by `messages/load.ts`'s `jobKey` (`pipeline:<id>`), the same string a line's `jobId` carries. Each row carries its `jobId`; a line on the person alone carries null. A line and a row at the same moment keep the line first.
  - The tied jobs (`tiedJobs`) are the jobs the person's lines carry. A job in their `contact_jobs` with no line stays out. With the switch off, the lines come back alone.
  - A tied job brings all of its activity: notes, reports, schedule blocks, clock sessions and events. `noMoney` (the tech's door) drops an event the ledger marks `financial` or one in the billing bucket. A line's own money stays: the tech's door for `messages` rows is the database's (R5).
  - `activityCounts` counts the rows by the job panel's buckets (`activityItemMatchesFilter`) plus the person's lines alone; `threadRowMatches` is the filter the counts come from, so a count matches what its press shows.
  - `switchState(read, write, door)` is the **Job activity** switch over a storage the caller passes, one key per door (`JOB_ACTIVITY_SWITCH_KEY`). On for the office and off on the phone by default (the plan's decision 7). A storage that throws reads as the door's default, and a press still flips it for the visit.
