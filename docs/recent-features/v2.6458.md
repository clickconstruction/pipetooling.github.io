# v2.6458 — Combining two jobs carries the source job's payment promises, lien paper, agreements and pay applications to the target (punch list #252, PR 2) (2026-11-03)

v2.6423 restated `migrate_job_ledger_costs_and_delete` so every row that is not money or legal moves or merges instead of going with the source job's delete, and left fifteen tables on today's cascade until the owner's word, each with a proposed rule on the card `to-dos/combine-rows.md` (#252). The owner said build on 2026-11-03. Bills and payments keep the billing guard (a source with either is refused unless `p_allow_billed`, the Delete flow's *Reassign to another job…*, where they go with the source by design); everything else that tells the money's or the paper's story now moves, or merges as its own key asks.

## What changed

- **Migration `20261103160000_combine_moves_money_and_legal_rows.sql`** (`docs/migrations/20261103160000_combine_moves_money_and_legal_rows.md`): `migrate_job_ledger_costs_and_delete` restated from 20261103054522's body. **Move**: `jobs_ledger_payment_events` (`from_job_id` and `to_job_id` that name the source), `job_payment_promises`, `job_payment_chase_touches`, `job_lien_filings`, `job_lien_releases` (a bid's waiver names no job and is untouched), `job_demand_letters`, `job_contracts`, `job_pay_applications`. **Merge**: `job_promised_pay_dates` (one per job; the later `marked_at` stands, and the move keeps the date so `job_promised_pay_dates_log_promise` writes no second promise), `job_lien_claim_corrections` (the target's stands, else the source's moves), `legal_matter_jobs` (the matter links to the target once), `job_lien_desk_items` (a live item, not voided and not sent or missed, of a kind the target holds live comes over with `voided_at` set and the target's stands; every desk screen skips a voided item, and v2.6460 names it on the target's activity; nothing the desk tracks is lost, since it works due months out from the job's bills and work; the rest move as history). The activity triggers on releases and agreements fire on state changes only, so a move writes no event.
- **Two refusals before anything moves**, after the work-order one: `agreement_collision` (*Both jobs hold an open agreement. Void one of them, then combine.*) when both jobs hold a draft or sent agreement, since `job_contracts_one_live_per_job_idx` allows one and a sent agreement's link is in the customer's hands; `pay_application_collision` (*Both jobs hold pay applications. Delete one job's applications, then combine.*) when both hold live applications, since the numbers are on paper the GC holds and no rule should renumber either run.
- **`job_pay_applications_stamp`** restated from 20261010002000 with one gate: a write that changes `job_id` and nothing else keeps `updated_by`, `updated_at` and `deleted_by`, so a combine does not re-stamp the application as the combiner's, as the supply-house word stamp learned in v2.6423.
- **The one call the owner left open**, a live Lien desk item on both jobs, is built as *the source's comes over voided* rather than a refusal. A voided draft leaves every desk screen, so this fragment's first wording, *the desk still reads it*, was wrong; but no due month goes with it, and no press clears a live desk item, so a refusal would have had no door. The owner kept the void on 2026-11-03, and v2.6460 names the draft on the target's activity.
- **Guide** *combine two duplicate jobs into one*: a *Money and legal paper* section (what moves, the desk-item rule, the two new holds), and *What stays behind* shrinks to bills and payments under the guard and the three caches let go on purpose.
- **Bed** `supabase/tests/combine_rows/20_scenario.sql` (`scripts/pgtest-combine-rows.sh` now re-applies this migration a second time): the first combine also moves a promised date and a claim correction onto a target with none; the second combine is refused three times in turn (a work order, an agreement, a pay application on both jobs), each time moving nothing, and then moves a payment's trace, a promise, a chase touch, lien paper, desk items (one voided by the rule, one sent, one live), a legal matter's links, an agreement and a pay application whose stamp stands, and keeps the later promised date and the target's claim correction.
- **Card #252**: the *Wait on the owner* section becomes *Money and legal rows — shipped in v2.6458* with each rule as built; what is left is the six soft job pointers under *Also noted*.
