- Docs: guides *send a customer account to your attorney* and *share your attorney their portal*, GLOSSARY, ACCESS_CONTROL, EDGE_FUNCTIONS (three functions), migration doc.

The firm's stage words are one `Record` keyed to `LEGAL_STAGE_LIST` (`LEGAL_FIRM_STAGE_WORDS` in `_shared/legalEmails.ts`), so a new stage that is not worded fails the typecheck; item 3's words and this PR's agree (*after judgment*, *uncollectible*), and the digest reads them. A pulled-back matter is one string everywhere, `LEGAL_REFERRAL_WITHDRAWN` (*Referral withdrawn*), in the subject, the digest's events and the stage word.

## Is this the best we can do?

- *Should a pulled-back matter keep showing the whole packet?* No. Counsel no longer has the matter, so the customer's records leave. The firm keeps what is its own: its fees, the conversation and the reason.
- *Why a new constraint name (`_v2`)?* The migration finds the old CHECK by definition, not by a guessed name, and the new name keeps a re-run harmless.
- *Can the office set any stage from the desk?* Only to accept a firm proposal, and only to a stage the firm works. The desk is not a second place to run the firm's case.
- *Is `review` the right stage after a pull-back?* Yes: the office decides again from review, and `pulled_at` tells the portal which review matters were once this firm's.
