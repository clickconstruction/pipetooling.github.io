# v2.7347 — Bids: a priced bid says when it cannot be scored, before it goes out (2026-11-15)

Punch list #360, PR 2 of the team scorecard's train. PR 1 (v2.7325) shipped the Scores tab and found the estimators' number empty: of the priced bids in play none could be judged, because every one had labor hours on fewer than nine rows in ten and most had priced rows with no cost. The Scores tab says so to the owner; this PR says it to the estimator, on the screens where the bid is priced and sent, while there is still time to fix it. Taunya picked the whole train on 2026-11-15.

## What ships

- **One rule, said in four places.** `src/lib/scorecard/scoreReadiness.ts` (`bidScoreReadiness`, `laborCompleteness`) is the Scores tab's own hold (`estimateHoldWords`) for a bid with a priced margin: labor is estimated when nine rows in ten carry hours, counted in the browser the way `bid_estimate_breakdown` counts it (a sub's row aside, hours on any stage), and no priced revenue sits on a row with no cost. A test holds the two rules together. A bid with no priced margin says nothing.
- **The Pricing workbench**: under the priced-margin line, *Can't be scored. Labor not estimated: 3 of 14 rows have hours. The cost has little or no labor in it, so this bid cannot be scored. Fill the hours on the Labor tab before it goes out.* with **Open Labor**. On a bid still on the desk it reads the numbers on screen (the stamp's inputs as they stand), so it lifts as the hours go in. On a sent bid it reads the margin the bid went out at, in the past tense, with no door.
- **Share with a teammate** (the workbench's send window): the same line at the top, its Open Labor closing the window first.
- **The Cover Letter's Mark sent**, both shapes: the line under the press, read fresh from the bid when it opens (`useBidScoreHold`), since that press is the moment `bids.priced_*` freezes. Not in the card's plan; added because a warning that stops at the share window misses the send that matters.
- **The Bid Board**: a *Can't be scored* chip beside the GC on the list row, the phone card and the opened row, for every bid with a priced margin that is not lost (`useBidScoreReadiness`: two reads for the whole board, `scoreReadinessIo.ts`, failing soft to no chips). On an unsent bid the chip is a press that opens the Labor tab; on a sent bid it is words.
- **Nothing is stopped.** A hold is said, never enforced: a bid may go out unscored, and the Scores tab then holds it with the same reason.
- The Scores tab's own words for a Labor tab with no rows read *the Labor tab has no rows* where they read *0 of 0 rows have hours*.

## The award day for old awards (a data change)

`bids.outcome_at` is stamped by a trigger since v2.3354, so a bid won before 2026-09-11 had none. Counted on prod before the migration was written: 47 awarded bids, 41 with no day, 16 of those with a linked job. Migration `20261115213000_bids_outcome_at_from_linked_job.sql` gives those 16 the earliest linked job's creation, never a day before the bid was sent (3 take their send day). The other 25 have no job and stay empty. Detail and what the UPDATE sets off: [`docs/migrations/20261115213000_bids_outcome_at_from_linked_job.md`](../migrations/20261115213000_bids_outcome_at_from_linked_job.md).

## What it does not do

- **A sent bid cannot be repaired.** The priced margin freezes at send, so hours filled afterwards make the bid read as judged while its kept cost still has no labor in it. Today that is 25 stamped bids. The honest fix is to keep the labor count with the stamp at send; it belongs with `team_scores` (PR 4), which moves the arithmetic into the database.
- The chip reads the bids as the page loaded them, so a stamp written on the workbench shows on the board after the next load.
