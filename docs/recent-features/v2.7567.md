# v2.7567 — Stickers: a job says which reserved stickers have not printed yet (2026-11-17)

Punch list #373 ([`to-dos/sticker-print-record.md`](../../to-dos/sticker-print-record.md)), PR 2 of 2: the screens. PR 1 was the column, v2.7564 ([`20261117140000_sticker_printed.sql`](../migrations/20261117140000_sticker_printed.md)), pushed before this merged.

## What was wrong

A run of stickers is reserved at the press, before any strip leaves the P-touch, and `sticker_runs.printed_at` is the moment the run was made. Every screen read *Printed Nov 16 by Dana* whether or not anything printed, and a run that stopped part way could only be picked up again by finding the right two ends in **From** and **to**.

## What ships

- **The mark is written.** The stickers window writes `box_stickers.printed_at` through `markStickersPrinted` (`stickerRunsIo.ts`). Over USB it marks after every print, a stop and a failure included, and only the stickers whose every copy came out (`stickersWhoseStripsWent`). Through the browser's print window it cannot see the tape, so it marks when that window closes, says *58 stickers are now marked as printed. Did they not print?* and offers **Take the mark off 58 stickers**, which counts only the stickers that had no mark before. A test strip marks nothing.
- **A refused mark is quiet.** A training account's write is refused by the database. The window says why in a muted line under what printed, and never in the red line: nothing went wrong with the print.
- **The words.** The job's reserved line, the *Which job?* card and the window's ask say what printed after the count: *None printed yet.*, *40 printed, 18 not printed yet.* or *All printed.* A Runs fold row says the same of its run. The by-line reads *Reserved Nov 16 by Dana* everywhere, since the day is the day the run was made.
- **Print only what never came out.** A run whose reserved stickers printed in part has a second press before the first on all three places: *Print the 18 not printed yet, G133 to G150*. It opens the window on those stickers, with the whole run still within reach of **From** and **to**.
- **Read.** `printed_at` is in both reads of a job's stickers (`fetchStickerRuns`, `fetchStickerRunsOfJobs`). `onPrinted` has the job's page read its line and its Runs fold again.

## Which stickers count as not printed yet

The kernel is `src/lib/materials/stickerMark.ts`. A sticker with no mark counts as *not printed yet* only where the app has been keeping the record for its run: the run was made since `STICKER_MARK_SINCE` (midnight of 2026-11-17), or one of its stickers carries a mark. Nothing is said of an older run until one of its stickers prints.

The second half of that rule is one step past the card, which counted by the sticker's own day alone. Under that reading an older run that stops part way on its first real print would say *40 printed.* and offer no press for the rest, which is the case the press exists for. Once the app has seen a run print, it is keeping that run's record.

The by-line is the other step past the card, which changed it to *Reserved* only where the mark applies. The day is the reserving on every run, so the word is true on every run and says nothing about printing.

## Not here

- The phone's On site line still counts the reserved stickers and says nothing of what printed.
- No press says a run printed without printing it. An older run gets its marks the next time it prints.
- Who printed is not kept: the mark is a time on the sticker.
