# v2.4313 — Accounts Receivable: a check that came back cannot pay a bill (2026-10-01)

Punch list **#76** PR 1 of 5 (`to-dos/ar-returned-checks.md`; plan and mock-up on the canvas https://claude.ai/artifact/9qbiCTmbaXnfPVKhFULoCN). Migration `20261001220000_returned_check_cannot_pay.sql` ([doc](../migrations/20261001220000_returned_check_cannot_pay.md)); no edge function.

## What was wrong

Found walking Accounts Receivable live on 2026-10-01. Loberg's $5,622.49 check was taken off #650 on 9/30 and stopped by the bank on 10/1. It left To match, as a returned deposit does, but:

1. **It could still be applied.** Under All, or through the search's *found in All*, its pane drew *Who paid you* with #650's two bills, the allocation lines and **Apply**. Neither `applyDisabled` / `canAllocateRemaining` in `BankPaymentsModal` nor `apply_mercury_bank_payment_allocations` looked at a return. Pressing Apply would have put a stopped check back on #650 as paid.
2. **The Pipeline said "Allocate 1 bank deposit" while To match was empty.** `count_mercury_transactions_for_bank_payments` counted a deposit the bank failed; the list drops it on the client (v2.3795 named this overcount and left it for later; nothing picked it up).
3. **Unlink and remove on a bounced check said the money would be "available again"** in Accounts Receivable. The RPC marks the deposit returned, so it never is.

## What changed

- **Kernel** [`arApplySentence.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/arApplySentence.ts): `arDepositCameBack(d)` (marked returned, or the bank's return) and `AR_CAME_BACK_SENTENCE`; `arApplySentence` takes `cameBack` and says *This check came back, so it cannot pay a bill.* before anything else (+2 tests).
- **[`BankPaymentsModal`](../../src/components/jobs/BankPaymentsModal.tsx)**: `canAllocateRemaining` is false for a deposit that came back, so the payer matches, the allocation lines, the tip and close-out strips, Apply and Apply & next all go. The pane shows `ar-came-back-note` with the sentence; a hand-marked one adds how to untick it. New render smoke `BankPaymentsModal.cameBack.render.test.tsx` (hand-marked in To match, bank-returned under All, a plain deposit unchanged).
- **Migration**: trigger `jobs_ledger_payments_refuse_returned_deposit` (`BEFORE INSERT OR UPDATE OF mercury_transaction_id`) raises the same sentence when a payment is linked to a deposit Mercury failed or one marked returned. Every path that puts a deposit on a bill writes that column — the apply, its sweep, *Link that payment instead* and the tip — so one trigger covers them without rewriting the 350-line apply. An update that keeps the same deposit passes. The count gains `t.status is distinct from 'failed'` outside *include hidden* (body from `20260927230000`).
- **[`JobFormMercuryUnlinkConfirm`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormMercuryUnlinkConfirm.tsx)** takes `bankFacts` (already loaded by `JobFormModal`): on a `failed` deposit it reads *The bank sent this check back: Insufficient funds. Taking it off puts the money back on the bill as owed.* and *It cannot pay another bill.*, and drops the double-count and available-again words (+2 render cases).
- Guide *match bank deposits to the bills they pay*: three sentences under *If a check bounces after you matched it*.
