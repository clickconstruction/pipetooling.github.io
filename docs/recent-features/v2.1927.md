# v2.1927 — Capable of Being Billed nets out open invoices (2026-08-20)

Owner-reported: the Pipeline New view's "READY TO ASK FOR" card showed $102k when ~$86k of it was already billed and sitting in Billed Awaiting Payment (billing a job — e.g. Springtown's full $32k, invoiced a minute before the report — didn't move the number, and the same dollars were double-counted against "Waiting on customers"). Root cause: `jobCapableToBillAmounts` ([`jobsStagesBoard.ts`](../../src/lib/jobsStagesBoard.ts)) computed `toBill = valueCreated − payments_made`, blind to invoices. Fix: new `jobOpenBillingRemainderDollars` (Σ over `ready_to_bill` + `billed` invoices of `max(0, amount − payments applied to that invoice)` — same per-invoice remainder basis as the Billed board) is subtracted too. RTB drafts are included because the story card's `readyToAsk = capableToBill + readyToBill.total` already counts working-job break-off drafts in the RTB exposure term. Kernel Pick widened to `invoices`/`payments` — both data paths already carry them (the lean stats fetch pulls all `ready_to_bill`/`billed` invoices and all invoice-linked payments). The breakdown modal ([`JobsStagesTab`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsStagesTab.tsx)) gains an "Open bills" column and its subtitle now says "value not yet paid, billed, or queued to bill"; help guide `read-the-pipeline-money-view.md` updated. Verified live against prod: card went $102,384 → $12,063 with exactly the two genuinely-unbilled jobs remaining. Client-only — no migration.
