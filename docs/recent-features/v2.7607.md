# v2.7607 — Estimates: what a row opens as becomes a kernel (2026-11-17)

Client only; no migration, no edge function, no screen change. Punch list **#46** row 3 (`to-dos/decomposition-queue.md`): cut 2 of eight on the Estimates page, its third and last kernel — Stage-A row 4 of the map (`docs/ESTIMATES_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md`). Stacked on v2.7605.

## What shipped

- **`src/lib/estimates/estimateDetailHydration.ts`** — `estimateFormFromRow(row, today)` is the form a row opens as: the brand, the terms, the options (none on a change order) with the viewed one and the preview's selection, the lines (the recommended option's, else the row's, else the stub line for an empty estimate draft and nothing for an empty change-order draft), the change-order fields, the customer, the good-through date and its preset (today + 30 for a draft with none), the For line, the project, the notes, who is told on acceptance (the saved list, or `'draft-default'` for a draft that never had one), the ids a sent row's locked list shows, and the attachment (a draft's live columns, a sent row's frozen copy). `defaultAcceptNotifyIds(userId, masterIds)` is that default list. `customerFieldsFromEstimate(row, customers)` is the customer box and the Send to box. `estimateInitialTitle(row, customers)` is the title rule for a generic-titled draft with a customer.
- **`Estimates.tsx`** — `load()` asks the kernel once and sets each piece from the answer, in the order it always did; it still makes every read (the row, the master technicians, the locked list's names, the customers), the redirect and the accept link's session read. `hydrateCustomerFieldsFromEstimate` is two setters. The page's `lineItemsFromJson` wrapper had one caller and is gone. 5,257 lines (was 5,325).
- **26 tests** (`estimateDetailHydration.test.ts`): the lines for an empty estimate draft, an empty change-order draft, a draft with lines, a sent row, a row with options, a change order that carries options, a credit line; the date for a draft with none, a draft whose date matches 7, 15 or no preset, a sent row; the notify list never set, saved, empty, and on a sent row; the default list; the live and frozen attachment; the plain fields; the change-order fields; the customer and Send to boxes in four cases; the title in four.

## What stayed the same, on purpose

- The awaits in `load()` sit where they did, so React still sets the same pieces together.
- A draft with no signed-in user gets an empty notify list, and a failed read of the master technicians falls back to the person editing alone; both branches stay in the page.
- `refetchCustomersAfterEdit` keeps its own two lines: it sets the Send to box only to clear it, which is a different rule.
