# v2.6015 — Retainage held: AR's band (2026-10-28)

Punch list #165, PR 2 of 4 (`to-dos/retainage-receivable/README.md`; show A of the owner's picks). The kernel shipped in v2.6010; this PR puts its lines on the screen. The Accounts Receivable window (`BankPaymentsModal.tsx`, `/accounts-receivable`) gains a **Retainage held** band under its header: the money GCs hold back on our trade jobs, one line per job, apart from the deposits and the bills, read only, with a door to the job.

## What changed

- **`src/components/jobs/ar/ArRetainageHeld.tsx`** (new): the band. Closed, one line — *Retainage held · $8,960.00 on 2 jobs · money GCs hold back on our trade jobs*; open, a table (Job with its name, address and source words · Held by · Held · Aging · *Open the job*), or stacked cards under 640px. A line whose release has gone out shows its figure struck through with the release words, and is not in the total; a line still held shows *job still open · not aging yet* or *26d since the job finished · Sep 30* (amber). Nothing held is the good state (`EmptyState`, *Nothing waiting on retainage a GC holds.*); a refused read says why (`formatErrorMessage`, in the hook). The open/closed choice is remembered in `localStorage` (`ar.retainageHeld.open`), wrapped in try/catch. Every figure and word comes from `lib/ar/retainageHeld.ts`; the file only draws.
- **`src/lib/ar/retainageHeldRows.ts`** (new, pure): the three tables' rows folded into the kernel's inputs (`assembleRetainageHeld`) and the facts a line shows beside the money (`retainageJobFacts`: the job's number by HCP, then Click, then the id; name; address; who holds it — the GC, else the customer). `releaseBillId` stays null until PR 3 marks the release bill.
- **`src/lib/ar/retainageHeldIo.ts`** (new): `loadRetainageHeldRows` — the live `job_pay_applications` (`deleted_at` null), the `jobs_ledger` rows with a typed figure (`lien_retainage_held > 0`) plus any job a live application names, embedding the GC's name through `jobs_ledger_gc_customer_id_fkey`, and those jobs' `jobs_ledger_invoices` (id, amount, status) in chunks of 200. RLS applies as the viewer; an error throws.
- **`src/hooks/useRetainageHeld.ts`** (new): builds the section (`buildRetainageHeldSection`, today in the app's zone) when the window opens and again when its `billedRows` refresh — the page refetches jobs after a deposit is applied, so a paid release clears its line without a reload.
- **`BankPaymentsModal.tsx`**: the hook and the band, rendered after the bank-transfer panel and before the role note; `onOpenEditJob` is the door.
- **`lib/jobs/bankPaymentsLegend.ts`**: a *Retainage held* row on the window's ? card.
- **Guide** `match-bank-deposits-to-bills.md`: a *Retainage a GC holds* section (plain words), keywords `retainage`, `retainage held`, `pay application`.
- **Tests**: `retainageHeldRows.test.ts` (4: the fold, numbers as numbers, a duplicate row, a null tie), `ArRetainageHeld.render.test.tsx` (7: the three lines in order with their source, age, held-by, struck figure and door; the phone cards; no door without a handler; the empty and failed reads; reading; the band closing and remembering; the closed words). `deskLegends.test.ts` holds the legend row; the guide tests hold the section.
- **Docs**: Glossary *Retainage held (Accounts Receivable)*; `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` (the `/accounts-receivable` route); `BILLING_FLOWS.md` (the kernel's sentence names the band).
