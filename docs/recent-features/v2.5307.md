# v2.5307 — Messages: add a new paper to a step of a flow (2026-10-12)

Messages cut 4, wave 2's F (helper 6): the prototype's New paper window (v1.177, `newPaperWindow` / `npSave`) on main. It opens from the flow editor's paper picker on Settings → Flows (helper 5's, the editor kernel #5442), which wires it; until then nothing on screen opens it. Stacked on #5470 (v2.5305, the `doc:<uuid>` paper and fact ids). The database half is v2.5306 (`20261012220000_flow_papers`: the four columns, the two packets and `flow_paper_create`), held behind the migration hold; the window's Save fails with the function's absence until that migration is on prod.

- **`src/lib/flows/newPaper.ts`** (pure): the draft (`NewPaperDraft`, `emptyDraft`), the three kinds' cards (`KIND_CARDS`: they give it to us, we write it and they sign it, we send it to inform), what a draft still needs (`newPaperProblems`), `flow_paper_create`'s arguments (`newPaperCreateArgs`), the clauses `edit_clause` takes (`newPaperClauses`), the Done when line, and what the window says once saved (`savedWords`). **One fact a paper** (`newPaperFacts`): an upload is `doc:<uuid>:on_file`, or `:current` when it runs out (current holds on file too); a contract is `:signed`; a notice is `:sent`. One fact keeps a step's `and`/`or` join meaning what it meant before the paper was added, which settles helper 5's question on the editor.
- **`src/lib/flows/newPaperIo.ts`**: `saveFlowPaper(flow, draft)` calls `flow_paper_create`, then cluster 9's `edit_clause(paper, 'new', …)` once per clause with its scope (`all` is the base; a trade keeps it to that trade's exhibit). A dev's or a controller's clauses publish as v1 at once; anyone else's wait as proposals for a controller or a dev to approve, and the window says which happened (`state: 'published' | 'proposed'`). The clauses that wait reach the approvers once for the whole paper, a push and an email (`tellClauseApprovers`, `messages-notify`, v2.5317), and the window's saved line says whom it reached and how (`savedWords`' `told`). It hands back the paper as the step's runtime id `doc:<uuid>` with its one fact, the label, the clause count and `told`.
- **`src/components/gc/flows/GcNewPaperWindow.tsx`**: the window, props `{ flowKind, step: { id, name }, trades?, onClose, onSaved(paper: SavedPaper) }` (the shape sent to helper 5). Kind first, then an upload's names and run-out days (30 by default, `INSURANCE_ASK_DAYS`'s number), a contract's clauses in English and Spanish with who each is for, or a notice's words in both languages; the Done when line; the problems in an alert; the saved words in a status line.
- **No Form Studio template for a clause contract.** The build plan's cut 4 line ("a Form Studio template behind one that is filled or signed") predates cut 4c: since 4c, `assemblePaper` builds a contract from its published clauses, and Form Studio's `createFormTemplate` uploads a PDF to place boxes on. A clause contract has no PDF to frame, so the window makes none; Form Studio stays the home of PDF forms (the W-9 and its kind).
- **The e-sign consent** stays the app's own words on every sign page; the window never edits it.
- **Help guide**: `add-a-new-paper-to-a-step-of-a-flow` (how to pick the kind, an upload that runs out, a contract's clauses and who approves them, a notice).
- **Tests**: `newPaper.test.ts` (problems, facts, arguments, clauses, label, the words in plain words), `newPaperIo.test.ts` (the create call, a clause per call, published versus proposed, a refused create), `GcNewPaperWindow.render.test.tsx` (an upload that runs out, a contract with a trade's clause waiting, a notice, a refused save, Escape).
