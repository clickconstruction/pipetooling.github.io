# v2.6938 — The case search finds a quoted phrase wherever it sits in a long text (2026-11-11)

On 2026-11-11 a session put two long named documents on a person's HR file (237,000 and 319,000 characters, every section under 42,000). The seat's `search_case` found a quoted phrase in the early months of each and never in the later ones. The same words unquoted were found everywhere.

## The cause

A tsvector keeps a word's position only up to **16,383**. Every later word is filed at 16,383, so a phrase test (`'a' <-> 'b'`) can never match there. `dev_case_search` tested the query twice on a sectioned text: `to_tsvector(d.content) @@ v_q AND to_tsvector(s.body) @@ v_q`. The first test ran the phrase over the whole document, so a document whose phrase sat past its 16,383rd word was thrown out before its sections were read. The section test would have matched.

Read on a local Postgres 15 with made-up text before any change: a phrase after 17,000 words is missed quoted and found unquoted, both words filed at position 16,383.

Three more of the same family turned up there:

- **A tsvector keeps 255 positions a word.** A phrase after a word's 255th use in one section is missed even in a short section. A name in a long month can pass that.
- **The texts that are not cut into sections** have one test and the same cap: a file's text (up to 200,000 characters), a document with no headings, a long entry.
- **`ts_headline` does not show a phrase reliably.** It starts at the first use of any of the phrase's words and shows 32 words from there. When one of the words was used earlier, the snippet never reached the phrase. Past the 16,383rd word it shows the start of the text.

## What changed

Migration [`20261111180000_case_search_phrases.sql`](../migrations/20261111180000_case_search_phrases.md):

- **`fts_prefilter(tsquery)`**: the query an index answers for certain. The same words, every phrase operator read as AND, everything left out read as true. `dev_case_search` asks each GIN index this (`v_pre`), never the phrase. It can only narrow.
- **`fts_hit(text, tsquery, headline options)`**: one text against the query. It returns no row, or the rank and the snippet. Each phrase is looked for 200 words at a time, a new window every 100 words, where neither cap can bite. The rest of the query is tested on the whole text. The snippet is `ts_headline`'s when that shows the phrase, and otherwise the 32 words around the phrase. The rank falls back to the phrase's rank in its window when the whole text's is 0.
- **`fts_windows`** and **`fts_query_tokens`** serve those two.
- **`dev_case_search`** is `20261108230000`'s body with each branch's test changed and nothing else: the index test on `v_pre`, then `CROSS JOIN LATERAL fts_hit(...)` for the match, the rank and the snippet. Nine branches, seven of them with an index. Sources, columns, gate, order and the reply are as they were. No index is added or changed.

**One meaning changed: a word left out.** `-word` used to be tested over the whole document too, so one use of the word anywhere dropped every section of it. Now it leaves out the section, entry or file that holds the word. A hit is a section, and the test is the section's.

No edge function changed. The seat's `search_case` calls the same function with the same arguments and reads the same reply.

## Tests

- **`supabase/tests/case_search_phrases/`** (`npm run test:pg:case-search-phrases`, in `scripts/sql-beds.txt`), 60 checks on the whole schema with made-up text. A shop log of twelve sections and 24,000 words holds the phrase in sections 10 and 11: found quoted and unquoted. The words in the other order, and two words that sit in two sections, are found nowhere. A word left out drops its section and keeps the document. A phrase left out, and a phrase or a word. A document with no headings, the summary, a file's text and a matter's text document each hold a phrase past the cap. One section uses a word 300 times before the phrase. A late hit's snippet marks the phrase and its rank is above 0. An estimator is refused.
- **The plan.** With the sequential read and the plain index walk switched off, each of the six index tests reads its own GIN index for a phrase with a word left out (`Bitmap Index Scan on idx_documents_fts` and the five others). The bed asserts it.
- **The mutant.** With `20261108230000`'s body put back, the bed fails on its first search: the quoted phrase finds the short entry alone.
- **`src/lib/devMcp/devMcpCaseSearch.test.ts`** pins the migration: no branch tests the phrase on an index, nine `fts_hit` joins, seven index tests on `v_pre`.

Run on a local Postgres 15 with every migration applied. On the bed's fixture, five long texts on one person, a search takes about 130 ms.

## Limits, as built

- A phrase longer than 100 words is found only where the plain test finds it.
- A phrase is found inside one section. Two words on either side of a heading are not a phrase, as before.
- The order of hits is still by rank. A phrase past a cap ranks by its window alone, so other words of the same query do not lift it.
