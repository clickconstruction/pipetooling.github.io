# v2.6980 — Permits: the owner read from the county the lot is in (2026-11-11)

Punch list #320, PR 2. The lookup asked one county per city: the one the city is filed under. A city's limits cross county lines.

## What was measured (counts only, 2026-11-11)

Building permits of the 75-day window that have a point, 150 addresses a city, through the lookup's own code.

- New Braunfels: Comal County finds a parcel for 83, and holds nothing at the point for 64. For 63 of those 64 Guadalupe County's layer, already in the code, holds the lot with an owner.
- San Marcos: Hays County finds a parcel for 110. For 18 the lot is in Guadalupe County. For 20 Hays has a polygon at the point with no owner (PR 4 of the train). 2 are in a county not read here.
- Through the new kernel, 200 addresses a city with or without a point: New Braunfels cards with an owner go from 119 to 199, San Marcos from 145 to 166, Seguin stays at 200 of 200. A card the city's own county misses costs one more question in New Braunfels and one or two in San Marcos.

## What changed

- **Kernel** (`_shared/permitParcels.ts`): `NEIGHBOURS` lists, by the county a city is filed under, the counties its limits reach into and whose layers are read here (Comal → Guadalupe; Hays → Guadalupe, Comal; Guadalupe → Comal; Travis → Hays). `neighbourSources`, `parcelFactsAtNeighbour` (read at the point, with `county`), `neighbourPointQuestion` (`point <lng>,<lat> in <County>`, which starts like the point question so `askedOtherQuestion` never reads it as the address question) and `askedWithoutNeighbours`. `ParcelFacts.county` is set only when the parcel is in a neighbouring county.
- **`permit-lookup`**: after the id, the address and the point at the city's own county all find nothing, each neighbour is asked at the point, in order, until one holds a parcel with an owner. Bexar has no neighbours listed: every San Antonio point sampled sat on a Bexar parcel.
- **Kept answers**: a kept "no parcel" whose `query` does not hold the point question for each neighbour is asked again once when the permit has a point. A kept failure is left to its month.
- **Words**: the skim card's lot line reads *read at the permit's point in Guadalupe County*. The sheets' line reads *The permit's point is in Guadalupe County, so this is that county's parcel there.* and then the county's address for it, as before. The link opens the neighbouring county's appraisal district, since the facts come from its layer. The guide gained three sentences.

Not built: a neighbour asked by the street address when the permit has no point (9 of 357 New Braunfels addresses have none), and Williamson County for Austin (PR 3).

Tests: `permitParcels.test.ts` (the neighbours, the facts with the county, the question, the kept-answer rule), `permitCityDoors.test.ts` (both lines). Every name and number is made up.

Deploy: `permit-lookup` after the merge, from `main`. No migration.
