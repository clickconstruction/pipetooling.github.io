# v2.6175 — The law firm's portal shows the parties and the venue at the top of a matter (2026-10-30)

Punch list #219, PR 3 of three (PR 1 v2.6172 the strip, PR 2 v2.6174 the venue press). The mock-up's portal question, answered as recommended: the firm reads the same three facts first, from the same kernel, so the office and the firm never disagree about who the owner is.

## What changed

- **The matter card** (`LegalFirmMatterView.tsx`, `FirmMatterView`): under the matter's name the line reads the building's address (`partiesStrip(...).addressLine`; before, the payer's address, the first job's county and *owner of record: …* ran on one muted line), and the parties strip sits under the head. The counties come from the packet's properties (`portalVenueCounties`: each property's county with its jobs' balances, largest first), since the portal has no firm-by-venue read; everything else is the packet the `legal-portal` function already sends, so **no edge deploy**.
- **Doors on the portal**: `LegalPartiesStrip` takes `doors`, the doors a surface offers. The portal offers only `venue` (`onOpenCoverage`): the press opens **Who covers what** on the county (`LegalPortalCoverage` `openOn`, a county and a nonce). A cell with no door is a `div` with the same words and no chevron (`data-legal-party-door="none"`), never a button that does nothing. The desk's office preview of the firm's page passes no door, so its cells are words.
- **Tests**: the sample matter's render test reads the owner and the county as cells and the contract cell as doorless. **Words**: the guide's *The firm* section (the Venue cell, the firm's hover, the portal's mirror); the release note.

## Is this the best we can do?

1. **One kernel.** The firm's cells are the office's cells; a change to the words is one change.
2. **No dead buttons.** The firm cannot open the office's customer or property record, so those cells are words, and the one door it has is the map it already had.
3. **No edge deploy.** The packet already carried the payer, the properties, the agreements and the payer's county.

## Verify

The firm's portal (`/legal` with a firm link), a matter: three cells under the name, the first two words, Venue a press that lands on Who covers what with the county outlined. Jobs → Pipeline → Collections → ⚖ → With the firm → the preview of the firm's page: the same cells, none a door.
