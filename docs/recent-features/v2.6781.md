# v2.6781 — The Lien desk's window frame goes: the phone's list, then one notice (2026-11-09)

Punch list #182, the Lien desk's PR 3 of 3 ([`to-dos/desks-as-pages/`](../../to-dos/desks-as-pages/README.md)). After [v2.6777](v2.6777.md) (the page) and [v2.6780](v2.6780.md) (every door to it). The window frame goes, and a phone gets the desk the rule asks for, as [v2.5941](v2.5941.md) gave the Legal desk: the list, then one notice.

## What shipped

- **`LienDeskModal.tsx` is `LienDesk.tsx`** (`git mv`, the export `LienDesk`, `LienDeskProps`; the test file renamed with it). Gone with the window: the `open`, `onClose` and `frame` props, the overlay and its dialog, the ✕, `useModalFullScreen('lien-desk')` and `ModalFullScreenButton`, the effects keyed on `open` (the find and Share resets, the reopen that re-applied a door's kind), and the `if (!open) return null`. The desk is a named region on the page (`<section aria-label="Lien desk">`) holding the panel and its own sheets and windows; the panel fills the page's cell. The remembered full-screen key is never read again. The by-hand sheet's × closes the sheet (it closed the window).
- **A phone** (`layout: 'stacked'`, chosen by the page from `useIsMobile`): with no notice in the address the Notices list is the whole desk, nothing is picked and nothing is written; a press on a row, a Do now row's notice or a Deadlines or Timeline row's notice asks the page to open it (`onOpenNotice(jobId, pile)`), which it does as a new history entry carrying `state.fromList`; an open notice is the whole desk with **← Notices** in the page door's place (`onShowList`: back one entry when the notice came from the list, else the list in its own place, keeping the pile). The pane's *← Back to the list* does the same. `oneColumn = isMobile || stacked` drives the list-or-pane switch and the first-row auto-pick, so a test or another host can stack the desk at any width. Do now, Deadlines and Timeline are one column already; the Affidavits and Retainage lists keep their own switch, since the address carries only the Notices selection.
- **The address is the truth** (`LienDesk.tsx`): `kind` follows `initialKind` on every change (a view word changed from outside moves the desk; the desk's own press writes the same word back), the pile follows `initialPile`, and the address write is told once the rows are in, as before.
- **Tests**: `LienDesk.render.test.tsx` (the desk's cases less the full-screen toggle case; the window case of v2.6777 becomes the region case; a stacked case: the list picks nothing, a press asks the page, the open notice wears ← Notices, the address losing the notice brings the list back); `LienDeskPage.render.test.tsx` (+2: the phone's list and a notice as a new entry with ← Notices back to the list; a notice reached by a link going to the list in its own place, keeping its pile).
- **Words**: the guide *send lien notices from the Lien desk* (the phone's list and ← Notices), `docs/twins/APP_DIRECTORY.md`, `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`, `LIEN_DESK_ARCHITECTURE.md` (the file and the contract renamed, a dated note), `PAGE_DECOMPOSITION_PLAYBOOK.md`, `JOBS_STAGES_TAB_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/README.md`; the to-do's table.

## Decided here

- **The sheets keep their `stopPropagation` calls.** As the Legal desk decided (v2.5941): most are the sheets' own panels, which stop a click so it never reaches the sheet's backdrop, and the backdrops' stops cost nothing with no desk backdrop under them.
- **Only the page names the phone.** The desk takes a `layout`; `useIsMobile` inside it still shapes the title bar and the footers, which is why `oneColumn` reads both.
- **Only the Notices selection is in the address**, as v2.6777 decided; the Affidavits and Retainage lists open their pane in place on a phone as they did.
- **Other to-dos and fragments that name `LienDeskModal.tsx`** are left as written: they are other work's history, and `git log --follow` reads the rename.

On a computer the page is as it was, with no ✕ and no full screen button. `check:nested-windows`, `check:dialog-role`, `check:window-z`, `check:status-bar` and `check:backdrop-click` pass with the window gone.
