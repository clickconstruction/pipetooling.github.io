# v2.5926 — Money in: the week line is computed (2026-10-26)

Punch list #191, PR 1 of 4. The owner (2026-10-26) asked for a strip under the Financials cards
showing "cheques that have not been deposited yet but are pending and if they fail as well as
stripe and what is set to be paid out", answering Master Malachi's Monday question — "what is
coming in this week and what has not yet come in from last week" — then shaped it across three
looks: a small expandable bar, the open state as a week line, and every chip a pen.

This PR is the kernel alone: `src/lib/moneyInWeek.ts` (+ tests), pure, no reads and no UI.

- `buildMoneyInWeek` sorts the week's money onto Monday–Sunday days: landed payments by `paid_on`;
  pending Mercury deposits on the day they should post (`PENDING_POSTS_DAYS` = 2, never before
  today); failed ones red on their day, uncounted; open paid claims on the day the mail should
  land (`MAILED_CHECK_DAYS` from the Collect desk); promises on their promised day; pace guesses
  on the payer's expected day (the Collect pile's `expectedYmd` — the pile is an input, so decision
  1's GC rules ride along); hosted Stripe bills marked by invoice id; the payout on its arrival
  day, drawn but never summed.
- Checks recorded but not deposited sit on the drawer shelf (no day). A pending `checkDeposit`
  within a dollar of a drawer check drops the drawer twin, so the money counts once.
- One chip per job on the expected side, surest kind first (bank > drawer > claim > word > pace);
  a claim or drawer row quiets the job's pile chips.
- Carried from last week: bills whose expected day fell in last week's Mon–Sun window with no
  payment landed on the job since (the `landedSince` idea from paid claims); what did land is
  credited, returned deposits ride along red.
- An expected day that already passed this week slides to today, remembering the real day
  (`wasYmd`) for the "was Thu" tag.

Weeks run Monday–Sunday (`weekMondayContaining`), per the to-do's decision 3. The reads land in
PR 2 (`get-stripe-payouts`, the pending read, the loader), the bar in PR 3, the pen in PR 4; the
full plan and mock-up live in `to-dos/money-in-this-week/` until the train ships.
