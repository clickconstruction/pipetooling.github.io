# v2.5230 — GC mode: three fixes in Bill the customer from the first live walk (2026-10-10)

Three of the live walk's punch-list items (`to-dos/gc-mode/LIVE_CHECKS.md` on spike/gc-mode), all Owner Billing's, read back to the lead and approved as one PR. All three are client only: every line is built in the browser and sent or stored as it is, so nothing deploys.

- **Remind them to pay keeps a stale Pay by** (step 8). `GcBillRemind` set its day once, at mount, from `step?.by ?? state.today`. A bill that turned late while the window was open had no step at mount, so its box opened at today. Pressing **Remind them to pay** now sets the day to `step.by` (today plus 5) as the box opens, and the box still edits.
- **The greeting named the company's first word** (step 8 read *Hello GC,*). `customerGreeting` (`ownerBillingRemind.ts`) fell back from the contact to the customer's name. It now greets the contact only, keeping the title rule (*Dr. Raman*), and names no one without a contact, as the schedule's letter does (15a). One `helloLine` gives *Hello Elena,* or *Hello,* at all six sites: the reminder, and in `customerEmail.ts` the pay application, the certified bill, the change order, the interest bill and our contract. The architect's *Hello there,* stays. The trade-off, accepted by the lead: a person customer with a name on the row but no contact loses *Hello Elena,*, since the app cannot tell a person's name from a company's.
- **The What they said box's placeholder** (step 7) read *Who said it*. It now reads *Who said it, and what* (`GcBillMoneyIn.tsx`).
- **Tests:**
  - `GcBillCustomer.render.test.tsx`: a bill mounted before it is late, made late with the window open, opens Remind at today plus 5. A mutant without the fix fails.
  - `ownerBilling.direct.test.ts`: the greeting with a contact, a titled one, none and blank, and `helloLine`.
  - `customerEmail.test.ts`: the first line of all five customer emails with a contact and without.
- **Guides:** *remind a customer to pay a GC bill* (the greeting) and *record what a GC customer paid* (What they said).
