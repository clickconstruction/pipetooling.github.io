# v2.4010 — Job window: the Costs tab follows what you change on Bill (2026-09-28)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Found in the live pass on punch list **#46** row 4 (2026-09-28): on job J1053 the Costs tab read "$1,500" after the Bill tab's total had been changed to $1,700 and saved; it read right after the job was closed and opened again.

## Why

`useJobChargesTimelineInputs` reads the price, the other job charges and the payments off the job row it is handed, and re-reads only when the job id changes. The job window hands it `editing` — the job as loaded. The form's autosave writes the database but does not re-read `editing`, so the tab kept the loaded figures for as long as the window stayed open. This predates the #46 decomposition.

## What shipped

- **`src/lib/jobs/jobCostsLiveInputs.ts`** — `withLiveJobFormValues` (the loaded inputs with the form's price and other job charges in place of the loaded ones; labor, supply houses, card charges and tally parts untouched) and `liveOtherCharges` (the rows autosave would save; a loaded row keeps its date, a new row is dated today).
- **`JobCostsTabCharts`** takes an optional `live`; the verdict, the budget card, the burn chart and the Cost Timeline all read the merged inputs. Without `live` — the read-only Job Detail — nothing changes.
- **`JobFormLaborCostPanel`** builds `live` from the form's Job Total with riders and its other job charges; the shell passes both.
- **Not changed:** payments on the Cost Timeline still come from the loaded job. The loader is not re-run on a save — the tab follows the form's own state, so it shows a change before autosave has written it.
- **Tests** — `jobCostsLiveInputs.test.ts` (7); `JobFormLaborCostPanel.render.test.tsx` (3): the form's price over the loaded one, a typed charge as money spent, nothing on a new job.
- The Job form map's row 13 and §13–14 say what the panel is handed.

## Verified
