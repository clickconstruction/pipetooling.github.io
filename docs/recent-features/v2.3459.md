# v2.3459 — Pipeline: the Progress & payment cell stops repeating itself (2026-09-14)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Owner's review of a Collections row after v2.3446 / v2.3449 (the cell bounded to its column, the money out of the sentence): four things on the cell said something the row already said, or stacked where they should sit side by side.

## What changed

- **The words line** ([`progressPaymentCell.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/progressPaymentCell.ts)): `crewClause` gains `{ names: false }` — *on site today* · *on site Fri* · *on the sheet, no clock-ins* · *nobody clocked in*. The printed `words.text` uses it and, on a stages job, drops the live stage's name from the front: `Top Out · Bryan on site Jul 22 · 90% set Aug 7` → **`on site Jul 22 · 90% set Aug 7`**. The row's **Crew & Dates** column lists the people and the lit chip above the bar names the stage. `words.full` (the tooltip and the bar's accessible name) keeps the stage, the names and the money. The no-bid red line follows the same rule (*on site today · no lines on the job · nothing to bill against*).
- **The live chip** loses its name suffix (`liveChipSuffix` is now `today` or null): `② Top Out · Bryan` → `② Top Out`; `② Top Out · today` stays while the crew is clocked in there.
- **Send back · Collections** ([`JobsStagesUnifiedTable.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsStagesUnifiedTable.tsx)): the three button rows under the cell (job rows, merged-billed rows, primary-RTB rows) are `flex-wrap: nowrap` with the buttons at `flex: 1 1 0` and tighter side padding, so the pair shares the 176px cell on one line instead of wrapping to two.
- **`This bill: $X paid · $Y left`** on a merged-billed row prints only when the job has **two or more** sent bills. With a single bill the legend's Paid and Billed rows carry the same two numbers; with several, the line still tells this row's bill from the others.

## Verified

Kernel suite (14) and the bar / cell / unified-table render smokes (23) updated and green; typecheck, lint, theme check. Live on the dev server (Chromium, 1024px): Billed Awaiting Payment rows read `nobody clocked in · 100% typed Sep 11` under `Post Test · 100%`, Send back and Collections on one line inside the column, no *This bill* line on single-bill jobs.
