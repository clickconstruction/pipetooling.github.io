# v2.7921 — A primary's money actions hold to their own jobs (2026-11-19)

Punch list #400, PR 1 of 5 (`to-dos/primary-job-doors.md`). The owner read the audit and said to build the whole train. One migration, pushed after the merge. No client change and no edge function.

## Before

- A primary has been held to their own jobs at the tables since v2.2177, and v2.5655 held the five paid verbs to the job's people.
- Ten more money verbs run as the owner and carried their own check, and its job leg let a primary through on the role alone. On a copy of the whole schema a primary on no job wrote down another job's bill, moved its payment to a different job and deleted the bill.
- No screen of a primary's reaches these verbs. The hole was at the API.

## What changed

- **Seven job verbs** (`20261119100000_primary_money_verbs.sql`): write down a billed bill, delete a billed bill, a draft or a paid bill with no payment, take a Stripe payment back off a bill, move a payment to another job, and send a paid job back to billed. In each, the primary clause of the job's check takes `is_job_people(job)`, the clause v2.5655 wrote. Moving a payment needs both jobs.
- **Three estimate verbs**: applying a signed estimate to a job, and both shapes of making a job from one. A primary passes when `primary_can_access_estimate` does, the scope their estimates already have at the table.
- **The bed.** `npm run test:pg:primary-money-verbs` walks a primary on nothing, a primary on their own jobs and the assistant through each verb, on the whole schema.

## What it leaves alone

- Every other role. The role gate at the top of each verb is as it was.
- A primary on their own jobs: each verb answers as before.
- The reads, the money desks, photos and test papers. They are PRs 2 to 5 of the train.

## Deploy

Merge, then `supabase db push`. The verbs keep their names, arguments and answers, so the push is safe on either side of the client deploy.
