# v2.4420 — Call queue: say when to call a GC again, and see what is due today (2026-10-02)

Punch list #80, PR 2 of 5. The owner's case: a city says "next budget year, call us in January". The Call queue brought the bid back every seven days, and nothing held the day or who to ask for. The owner approved the mock-up (the before-and-after mock-up (kept in git history at `to-dos/bid-follow-up-date/before-after.html`)) and the queue re-sorting. Reads and writes the columns of [v2.4419](v2.4419.md).

## What shipped

- **The rule, one kernel**: [`bidNextFollowup.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/bidNextFollowup.ts). `resolveBidFollowup` gives an open bid one of five states from its own day (`bids.next_followup_on`), else its builder's (`customer_followup_prefs`: a snooze still ahead, else the call window's *Next follow-up*), else seven days after the last contact: `overdue`, `due`, `later` (picked days), `none` (no day and stale), `fresh`. A picked day is spent once a contact is logged on or after it, in the office's time zone. Quick picks land on a weekday. `applyFollowupToEntry` and `buildFollowupChangeEntry` build the log rows; `bidFollowupColumns` reads the roll-up off a bid row and tolerates a row with no such columns.
- **The queue sorts by what is due**: [`callQueue.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/callQueue.ts). `chase.todo` is now overdue, due, then the quiet bids with no day; `chase.later` holds the parked ones; a parked bid's tab is not asked for; builders order overdue (earliest day first), due, then the old order. Totals gain `dueCount`, `overdueCount`, `noDateCount`, `laterCount`, `laterValue` (bids counted once across GCs).
- **The three questions**: [`FollowupPickPanel.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/FollowupPickPanel.tsx) under **Left message**, **Still pending** and **Rebid / RFQ** in [`BidsCallQueueTab`](../../src/components/bids/BidsCallQueueTab.tsx). **Call again** (Next week, 2 weeks, Next month, 3 months, pick a date), then **Ask for** (the customer's people, **+ person** inserts a `customer_contact_persons` row) and **Waiting on**, which show once a day is picked. Save writes one log row: the same contact as before, plus the three columns and a plain sentence in the note (*Call again Tue, Jan 5, 2027. Ask for J. Rayburn. Waiting on their budget.*), so every screen that prints the log says the day. No day picked writes the row it always wrote, with no new keys. **Won**, **Lost…** and **Bid tab received** are unchanged.
- **Four pills** over the list (Due, Overdue, No date yet, Later) narrow it; a second tap clears.
- **The due card**: a **Due today** or **N d overdue** tag on the card and the bid; under the bid, who to ask for with a tap-to-call number (on a bid sent to two GCs, only on that person's GC) and *Last time*: what was said when the day was set, the date, and who logged it (the entry `bids.next_followup_entry_id` points at).
- **Later**: one line over the cards that unfolds the parked bids, soonest first, each with **change date**. A changed or removed date is a method-less log note (`next_followup_cleared` for a removal), so `last_contact` does not move.
- The note box sits above the questions while they are open. The **Show** chips' style no longer mixes the `font` shorthand with `fontWeight` (a React warning; they render at their designed `0.78rem`).
- Help: [`follow-up-with-builders`](../../src/content/help/follow-up-with-builders.md) gains *Say when to call again*. [`GLOSSARY.md`](../GLOSSARY.md) gains *Call-again day* and the Call queue entry says the new order.
