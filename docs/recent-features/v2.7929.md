# v2.7929 — A primary reads the money of their own jobs only (2026-11-19)

Punch list #400, PR 2 of 5 (`to-dos/primary-job-doors.md`). One migration, pushed after the merge. No client change and no edge function.

## Before

- The tables that hold a job's activity, bills, payments, lines and parts have been fenced to a primary's own jobs since v2.2177.
- Six functions that read those tables run as the owner. Each let a primary through on the role alone, or on the adoption table that pairs every master with every primary. On a copy of the whole schema a primary on no job read another job's activity with its money lines, its supply house bills, its parts and its payment's lines.

## What changed

- **The activity gate** (`20261119101000_primary_money_reads.sql`): `can_read_job_activity` holds a primary to `is_job_people` in both its halves. The job's activity feed, its name on a job account, its supply house accounts and a trip put on its bill all ask that gate, so they follow.
- **Supply house bills and parts**: `get_invoice_amounts_for_jobs`, `get_invoice_allocation_lines_for_jobs` and `list_tally_parts_with_po` ask `is_job_people` in place of the adoption row.
- **A payment's lines**: `get_payment_line_items` and its bulk shape ask it of the payment's job.
- **The bed.** `npm run test:pg:primary-money-reads` reads every door as five people, on the whole schema.

## What it leaves alone

- A primary on their own jobs reads every number as before: the account manager, the one who sold it, or the author of its signed estimate.
- A primary on a job's crew still reads the plain activity lines, the job's name and its supply house accounts, as a helper does.
- Every other role.

## Deploy

Merge, then `supabase db push`. Safe on either side of the client deploy.
