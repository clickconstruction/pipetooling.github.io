# v2.5281 — Messages: the link to the phone service is written, not yet switched on (2026-10-12)

The Messages build's cut 5 edge functions, `quo-webhook` and `quo-send`, by helper 3, directed by the GC Review lead. A draft: stacked on v2.5273 (types) and v2.5268 (#5443), and it calls cut 5's SQL (v2.5278, #5446). It is not deployed: it waits on the migrations on prod and the Quo account.

- **`quo-webhook`**: Quo posts each event of the company number here. The function checks Quo's signature (`verifySignature`), takes the delivery once (`quo_webhook_claim`), reads it (`readQuoEvent`), places each number on its contact (`quo_place_number`), and writes the line the kernels merge through `quo_write_line` over the revision it read. Then it mirrors the number's STOP, writes a team event keyed so a retry never doubles it, and sends the app's answer last. A failure gives the delivery back and answers 500, so Quo sends it again. `verify_jwt = false`.
- **`quo-send`**: the send window's text. It checks a real office account (not a sample, a twin or training mode, and `gc_office_team()` asked as the caller), takes one of the contact's numbers, then plans, posts to Quo and writes the line under Quo's id, as `_shared/quoLines.ts`'s `sendText` does for the webhook's HELP answer too.
- **Kernels for the edge**:
  - The rules for a text going out move from `messages/compose.ts` to `messages/textOut.ts`, which compose re-exports.
  - What a contact is called moves from `contacts/kernel.ts` to `contacts/names.ts`, which the kernel re-exports.
  - New `quo/rows.ts` maps a row to the kernels' line and back. It also places a line's job column, reads the seats from Settings → Phone and texts (`app_settings` `messages_quo_seats_v1`, v2.5335) with the `QUO_SEATS` secret as the fallback while the row is empty (`seatsSource`), and parses `quo-send`'s request, with its tests.
  - `scripts/edge-kernels.mjs` adds the Quo kernels and the thread to the edge copy, and names `quo-send` and `quo-webhook` as importers. Every copied file's header changes with that one line, so `customer-portal`, `gc-trade-portal` and `gmail-sync` read as behind on `check:edge-drift` until their next deploy. Nothing in them behaves differently.
- **Deno-checked clean**: both functions pass `deno check`. They import the Quo kernels straight from the copy, not from its index, which would walk the GC kernels whose type imports point back at `src`. `webhook.ts` hands WebCrypto a plain `ArrayBuffer` copy, which Deno's newer TypeScript wants.
- **On What customers see**: texts are outside messages too, so they are registered the way an outside email is, where a text fits.
  - **The registry**: `customerSurfaceRegistry.ts` finds a text sender (a function that sends through `_shared/quoLines.ts`'s `sendText`, or straight to Quo) beside the Resend senders. `quo-send` and `quo-webhook` are listed on two new journey steps.
  - **The journey steps**: `gc-text` (a customer or an architect) and `sub-text` (a trade partner), with a new `text` render. The tab draws the bubbles from `journeys/sampleTexts.ts`, which builds each text from the kernels' own words: `textToSend` with its opt-out line, and `helpAnswer`.
  - **The person journey** answers both steps "read in their thread", and `know-what-a-text-from-the-company-number-says` is their guide (devs only until texts start).
  - **Not for a text**: the sent copy. The line in their thread is the copy.
- **Docs**: `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` has both sections and TOC lines, with the secrets and an enabling checklist. `supabase/config.toml` has both `verify_jwt = false` blocks.
- **The dev-mcp catalog** names both functions (`node scripts/build-dev-mcp-catalog.mjs`, one line in `dev-mcp/catalog.ts`), so `dev-mcp` redeploys with them.
