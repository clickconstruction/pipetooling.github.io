- **The row** (`StagesUnifiedJobRow` / `StagesUnifiedInvoiceRow`, `JobsStagesUnifiedTable`): a `rowStamp` prop draws [`StagesUncollectibleStamp`](../../src/components/jobs/StagesUncollectibleStamp.tsx) under the money — red, tilted, the word in Impact, the reason in a hand-written face, the day and the dollars — and the row wears `data-stages-row-stamped` (faint stripes, `index.css`); an `onJobMarkUncollectible` prop puts *Uncollectible…* under the icons beside *Collections* / *Send back*.
- **The tab** (`JobsStagesTab`): the Collections site renders the chased rows, then the **Uncollectible band** (count, dollars, *given up on — in no total, on no lien clock, still on the books*) over the given-up rows with their own action props (Mark Paid, View Bill, Edit, no lien door, *Put it back in Collections*); [`StagesUncollectibleConfirmModal`](../../src/components/jobs/StagesUncollectibleConfirmModal.tsx) (reason required, 12+ characters; says where the job goes and what still works) → `setJobUncollectible`, then `invokeMarkStripeInvoiceUncollectible` for each billed line with a Stripe invoice (a Stripe failure is a toast, never an undo), then the reload and the follow-moves focus. The phone stage lists both; `phoneNextInput` hands `jobNextLine` the reason, and the card wears the UNCOLLECTIBLE chip with `needsMe` false.
- **Words**: the guide `mark-a-bill-uncollectible`; Glossary → *Collections*; `BILLING_FLOWS.md`'s transitions table; the Stages map's Collections row; the edge-function reference.
