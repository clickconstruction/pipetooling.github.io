## What was wrong

## What it does

- **A second pass over the built checklist**, `lienGetItRightWithFacts(run, facts)`, re-derives the counts, the step and the strip's three lines with what the print and the printed read know. The lines, the door, the Leave out list and the totals do not move. With no facts it is the checklist as built, so a viewer with no one print, and the moment before the prints are read, read as before.
- **The Print step counts what the press prints.** *2 notices to print · 1 affidavit for the notary*, then apart: *1 affidavit printed*, *1 notice waits in the run window*, *1 notice goes without paper*. With nothing to print it reads *Nothing waits to print* and the aside, and the run moves on to **Out the door** when a printed notice or a printed affidavit is left to mail or file.
- **The line under the right papers** says the same: *1 affidavit ready to file · 1 affidavit printed · 1 notice to print · 1 right notice does not print here · 4 notices printed*.
- Two counts more on the checklist: `affidavitsPrinted` and `noticesStopped`.
- The component applies the pass itself from the `print` and `printed` it already receives, so the desk's wiring is unchanged.
