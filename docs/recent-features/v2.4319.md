# v2.4319 — Submittals: a row lists its parts, not just the assembly (2026-10-01)

Estimator Wendi, through Grace, 2026-10-01: "NEED TO SHOW PARTS NOT JUST ASSEMBLIES IN SUBMITTALS". On BP375, LAV-1, EWC-1 and DWH-1 read "LAV 1 assembly SPACEX", "EWC1 assembly" and "DWH1 & ET assembly SPACEX" on the row, the GC's page and the package. The takeoff priced them from price-book assemblies, and the takeoff kernel treated an assembly line as one piece by its name. Grace then pushed it further: "Realistically each of these parts may need to be separately procured." Mock-up canvas https://claude.ai/artifact/JtoZEiwKP7zbABzXEy7hH8 (today, the picker, the rows, the editor, the procurement log by tag and by house, the GC's page, the refresh, and the "is this the best we can do" row). This is PR 1 of the train; the GC's call per part, the house's file building the parts, procurement per part and the draft refresh follow.

## What changed

- **Assemblies open.** [`takeoffCandidates.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/takeoffCandidates.ts) `productPiecesOf` takes the assemblies' contents (`TakeoffAssemblyItem`, read level by level by `loadAssemblyContents` in `takeoffCandidatesIo.ts`, paged) and turns an assembly line into the parts inside it, nested assemblies opened and quantities multiplied through (`expandAssembly`; a loop or depth over six stops). Each piece now carries `quantity` (per fixture), `lineId`, `templateItemId` (its key), `assembly` (the name) and `manufacturer`. An assembly whose insides were not read stays one piece, as before. A stored choice that named a whole assembly line stands for its parts the GC sees by default.
- **Every part is bought.** The chips no longer switch a part off; they switch it between *the GC sees it* and **order only**. Trim (by name, `TRIM_NAME`) starts order only. The picker groups chips under *Inside <assembly>* / *On the takeoff*, shows × N on a chip, a *N parts · the GC sees K · M order only* line, and its tick boxes sit on the fixture's line.
- **Parts are stored.** New table `bid_submittal_item_parts` ([migration doc](../migrations/20261001200000_submittal_item_parts.md)). Kernel [`itemParts.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/itemParts.ts): `partsFromPieces`, `rollUpFromParts` (the row's label = the GC's parts joined with " + ", the house of the first of them, the longest lead time among them), `splitPartLabel` (maker and model first, the catalog words after), `orderOnlyLine`, `assemblyLine`, `carryPartInsert` / `copyPartInsert`, `diffPartDrafts`. Reads and writes in `itemPartsIo.ts`; a missing table reads as no parts.
- **Rows.** [`SubmittalPartsCell`](../../src/components/bids/SubmittalPartsCell.tsx) draws a row with parts: one line per part the GC sees, *Ordered, not submitted: …*, then *from <assembly>* and every house. A row with no parts reads as before.
- **Edit.** [`SubmittalPartsEditor`](../../src/components/bids/SubmittalPartsEditor.tsx) inside `SubmittalItemEditDialog`: each part's name, how many on a fixture, house, lead time, stage and *GC sees it*; ↑ ↓ ×; *+ Add a part*. On a shared revision only the house, lead time, stage and count change. The row's own house picker and lead time give way to the parts (the lead time line reads the longest). A one-line row can be **listed as parts**. Save writes the parts, then the row's roll-up.
- **Built and carried.** Build / Add from the takeoff writes each row's parts. **Split** copies them to each tag's row (the first keeps the procurement key, the rest get their own). **New revision** carries parts with their row when its product is unchanged.
- **Package.** The cover's Submitted cell lists the GC's parts one per line (× N when more than one on a fixture); the sheet stamp's footer joins them.

## Fixed on the way

- **New revision dropped rows built from the takeoff or by hand.** `newRevision` rebuilt every row from the schedule and the Pricing picks; on a bid with neither (BP375) the new draft came out empty, and *Rev N+1 from the rows sent back* did the same. `rowsToCarry` + `carriedRowInsert` ([`submittalRevision.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/submittalRevision.ts)) carry the rows the rebuild does not cover — a takeoff row, a row typed by hand — as they stand. **Rebuild rows from picks** keeps those rows too, after the rebuilt ones, and is hidden on a bid with no picks and no schedule.
