# v2.4529 — Help: the Texas lien rules page is easier to read and to get around (2026-10-04)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. The owner asked for the guide behind the Lien desk's *§ Rules* button to be easy to use and easy to navigate. It was nineteen rules in one flat list, sentences of forty and fifty words, and a closing section that was one sentence of about 150 words.

## The guide

[`texas-lien-rules-the-app-follows.md`](../../src/content/help/texas-lien-rules-the-app-follows.md), rewritten by the plain-words rules and taken off `LEGACY_PLAIN_WORDS_GUIDES`. **No rule, date, section number or case cite changed**; every statement was checked against the page it replaces.

- **How to use this page**: the three parts of a rule, where *§ Rules* opens it, where to start if liens are new.
- **Find a rule**: a jump list in seven groups; **Back to Find a rule** after each group.
- **Groups** (`##`) with the rules under them (`###`): the clock, the notice, the lien, the demand letter, interest, limits on a collection letter, not yet verified. The rule headings keep their words, so every anchor the app links to is the same.
- **A worked example** on top of the clock: work done in June, the notice and lien dates for a commercial job and for a house.
- Lists where a sentence carried a list: the affidavit's four needs, the homestead statement's two conditions, the five things the collection act forbids, the unverified readings.
- A statute or case cite is set in italics (what the papers print, quoted as printed), since a cite carries parentheses the plain-words rule forbids in prose.
- The button is named **§ Rules** (v2.4528).

## Jump links inside a guide

A guide could link to another guide and to a page of the app, not to its own headings.

- [`helpGuideHtml.ts`](../../src/lib/helpGuideHtml.ts): `href="#<anchor>"` rides through the sanitizer on a placeholder host and comes back as `href="#<anchor>" data-anchor="<anchor>"`.
- [`GuideBrowser.tsx`](../../src/components/GuideBrowser.tsx): a click on `a[data-anchor]` scrolls the article to the heading with that id (the ids `applyHeadingAnchors` stamps on `h2` and `h3`).
- `CLAUDE.md` names the form beside the other two.
