# v2.3346 — Who pays the bill, PR 2: GC Review, both portals and the payment-terms readers follow "Bills go to" (2026-09-11)

Migration `20260911223500_who_pays_readers_follow_the_rule.sql` ([`docs/migrations/20260911223500_who_pays_readers_follow_the_rule.md`](../migrations/20260911223500_who_pays_readers_follow_the_rule.md)); edge functions `customer-portal` and `submit-portal-request` changed (**redeploy both after the push**). Second of the four-PR train (PR 1 v2.3345 shipped the rule and the columns).

## Why

PR 1 gave the job one answer to who pays. Three readers still assumed the GC whenever a job had one: GC Review and the statement RPC grouped every billed row by `gc_customer_id` (Done Right Foundation's group carried 251 homeowner-paid pretests), the customer portal listed a GC's jobs' bills as owed by the GC, and the Their Word readers (terms bar, reliability line, forecast slip) keyed on `gc_customer_id ?? customer_id`.

## What shipped

- **GC Review + the statement RPC**: a row files under a GC only when the GC pays it — the invoice's own `bill_to_party` pick, else the job's rule, never a typed `bill_to_email` recipient — and always when the GC *is* the job customer row (the 72 jobs entered that way). Client: [`gcThatPaysRow`](../../src/lib/gcReviewRollup.ts) in the rollup (+1 test covering all five cases); server: the same CASE in `get_gc_statement_email_payload` (the round RPC, the cron dispatcher and Draft Message all read it). Everything else lands in the no-entity bucket, relabeled **Not billed to a GC** in both.
- **Portals**: [`buildPortalBills`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/portalMergedBills.ts) resolves each bill's payer (`viewerOwesBill` in [`portalBillMembership.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/portalBillMembership.ts)) and sets `billedTo` — null when the viewer owes it, else the other party's name (owners **and** GCs are looked up; a typed recipient reads as their name or "someone else"). `customer-portal` sums `totalDue` and logs `bill_count` over owed bills only, and scopes the portal promise (`job_promised_pay_dates` lookup) to `owedJobIdsForViewer`. [`CustomerPortal.tsx`](../../src/pages/CustomerPortal.tsx) builds the ledger, the promise ask and the bank-transfer memo from owed bills and lists the rest under **On your jobs, billed to someone else** (address · billed to · date · amount, no Pay button). `submit-portal-request` uses the same owed scope for `payment_promise` (job rule + open bills' picks) so a GC cannot promise on the owner's bill.
- **Payer keys**: Edit Job's terms bar / Terms modal key on `billToParty === 'gc' ? GC : customer`; the Billed row's reliability record and the forecast's usual-slip use [`stageRowPayerCustomerId`](../../src/lib/jobs/billToParty.ts) / `payerCustomerId(job, effectiveInvoiceParty(job, inv))`. Pay-speed receipts still key on `customer_id` (server RPC; unchanged).
- Help: `choose-who-gets-the-bill` drops its "for now" caveat; `track-a-general-contractor-on-a-job` and `share-a-customer-their-portal` describe the new bucket and the billed-elsewhere list. `GLOSSARY.md` "Bills go to" updated.
