# v2.4856 — Lien desk: the Leader here bar on one line, with his name and a preview (2026-10-07)

Client only. Grace, with job 878's word row open: *The bottom bar is too tall … "Not what I said" to the right of cancel instead of below it … I want to be able to hover over something on that bottom bar to see a modal preview of what that signature looks like on what it goes on … perhaps instead of "the leader" it is the leader's name in the app by default.* Mock-up: https://claude.ai/artifact/KpYc83TYB9ms3TXbBQENH5. Stacked on v2.4850 (the blue *Leader here, Approve ▸* door).

## What changed

- **One line** ([`LienWordRecordRow`](../../src/components/jobs/LienWordRecordRow.tsx)): the lead-in (*Leader here*, or *Who said it, when, and how:* from the remembered-word door), the box, the five channels, **Approve on his word ▸** in blue (`btn('primary')`, the desk's colour for the step that moves a notice forward), Cancel, and after them the short note — *Recorded by Taunya. Malachi can pull it back with “Not what I said”.* — with **Preview the record ›**. The two-sentence line under the row is gone (112 px → one row). A refusal (`actionBlock`) still draws its red line under.
- **His name** (`lienWord.ts`): `leaderFirstName`, `onWhoseWord` → `wordRecordWords(item, leaderName)` reads *on Malachi’s word · Malachi Whites, October 7, 2026 · standing over the desk*; `presenceLine(channel, recorder, leaderName)` is one sentence each; the chips read *standing over me* / *typing it in*; the box opens with `defaultWordNote(leaderName, today)` (the kernel had it since v2.3813; the desk typed *the leader* until now). The desk takes `leaderNameFor(masterUserId)` from `JobsStagesTab` (`users.find(id).name`, the plain name, not the signer line the letters print) and uses it in the row, the Ready footer and the queue row. A job with no master, or a stored record of one, still reads *the leader*.
- **The preview** (`wordRecordPreview`): a `role="tooltip"` card over the row on hover, focus or a press (`data-lien-word-peek` / `data-lien-word-preview`; Esc or a press elsewhere closes it, Esc never reaching the window behind) — the record in each place it lands: the notice's Ready footer, the leader's *Sent on your word* strip, the queue row, who recorded it and what pulls it back, and that the notice itself does not change (the word is written to the desk item only, never onto the paper). It is drawn from the same `wordRecordWords` the desk draws afterwards, so it cannot drift.
- `GcOnNoticeModal`, `LienDeskAffidavitPane` and `LienDeskRetainagePane` share the row: they get the one-line layout and the blue action; with no `leaderName` passed their words read *the leader* as before.

## Is this the best we can do?

- *Why not put the preview on the Approve button itself?* A card that opens on the button people reach for would be in the way on every press; a dotted link beside it is a deliberate second act.
- *Why keep the box?* The name and the day are the record's spine, and sometimes a different master or a different day gave the word. Pre-filled, it costs one glance.
- *Old records*: a notice approved before this reads *on the leader's word* where its item has no name to draw; nothing is rewritten.
- *Phone*: the row wraps to two or three lines under 640 px as it did; the preview card is a press, not a hover, there.
