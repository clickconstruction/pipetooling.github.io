# v2.7819 — Materials on site: a box’s photos show on every phone (2026-11-18)

Take from a box, step 1 of 4. The owner asked for a photo of a box before and after a person takes parts out of it. The look behind that ask found that a box's photo drew only on the phone that took it: `BoxPhotos` read the bytes from that phone's own IndexedDB store and nothing else, so every other phone and the office read a dashed square beside *in the job's folder*. A before and after pair is worth nothing if only its taker can see it, so this step comes first and stands alone.

## What changed

- **The picture comes from the first place that holds it** ([`boxPhotoSource.ts`](../../src/lib/materials/boxPhotoSource.ts), pure, and `boxPhotoBytesIo.ts`): this phone's own store, then the job's Drive folder through the job gallery's function (`job-photos`, `thumb`), then the `box-photos` bucket while the row still waits for the sweep. A filed row keeps its `storage_path` after the sweep deletes the bucket's copy, so the bucket is asked only for a row that is `sending` with no Drive id.
- **A press on a thumbnail opens the whole picture** (`BoxPhotoViewer` in `PhotoPress.tsx`): the same three places, `full` from Drive, with what the shot is of, who took it and when. **Close the photo**, a press anywhere or Escape closes it.
- **A dashed square says why** in its title when no place holds the picture: still on the phone that took it, shared from a phone, or not opened from the job's folder.
- **Send now** and **Share instead** are dead where this phone holds no bytes. They used to be dead for want of any picture; a picture read from Drive has a url and no bytes to send.
- No migration, no edge function, no new read on the page's open: the thumbnails go through the gallery's own queue (six in flight, the bytes kept).

## Checked

- Live, as the dev, before the build: `job-photos` answered `thumb` and `full` with `image/jpeg` for filed box photos on the test job and on a real job, 404 with words for a photo whose file sits in Drive's trash, and 500 for an id that is no file.
- `boxPhotoSource.test.ts` holds the order and the words. `PhotoPress.render.test.tsx` draws a photo this phone never held, keeps the dashed square with its reason when no place answers, and opens and closes the whole picture.

## Not done

- `job-photos` answers to its own gate, `can_report_stage_progress`, not the on-site gate. A person the on-site gate lets in and that one does not keeps the dashed square. Not measured for a helper or a subcontractor: the Sample accounts are on no job.
- The kept bytes of a sent photo are still never cleared from the phone.
