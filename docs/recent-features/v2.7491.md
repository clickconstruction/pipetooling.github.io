# v2.7491 — Lien desk: remind the leader of notices that wait on a signature (2026-11-16)

The owner, 2026-11-16, after a look at the live desk: *Yes, build it*. One edge function (`notify-lien-approval`, redeploy) and the client. No migration. Stacked on v2.7478 (the retainage notice's message) and v2.7483 (the prints under the tiles), which touch the same function and the same Board line.

## What was wrong

**Remind** (v2.7371) was for *what waits on the leader*, and it covered half of it. The desk counts two things as waiting on him (`lienBoardLeaderWaits`, v2.7448): papers to approve, and approved notices to sign. The press took the first alone:

- `LienDesk.tsx` built the reminder from the To do rows with `action === 'approve'`, and drew the press only when one existed.
- `notify-lien-approval` answered a notice that was not awaiting approval with 200 and nothing sent.

An approved notice with no signature cannot print, and nobody told the leader of it: a standing rule or his spoken word approved it, or the draft changed after he signed. On the live desk that day the second tile read *8 waiting on the leader · 8 to sign*, both **Awaiting Master approval** bins were empty, and there was no Remind press anywhere on the page.

## What landed

- **`notify-lien-approval`**: a new kind, `{ kind: 'signature', items: [{ item_id, due_ymd }] }`, beside the affidavit's and through the same code: the office's gate (403 *Not the office* before a paper is read), `lienAffidavitAskItems` for the body, the job's leader or every leader but the caller, and **one message for each leader whatever the count** (`lienAffidavitTellsByLeader`). Its own row rule, `lienSignatureAskable`: a monthly notice (`notice_53_056`), `approved`, not voided, not printed, and unsigned. With none left it answers 200 *Not waiting on a signature*, and `told` says how many it told him of. Logged as `lien_signature_ask` (*lien notice to sign* on the **While you were off** line).
- **One rule for *unsigned***: the function cannot import the desk's kernel, so the hash that ties a signature to the draft moved to `supabase/functions/_shared/lienFieldsHash.ts` with `lienRowUnsigned` beside it, and `lienNoticeSignature.ts` re-exports `lienFieldsHash` from there. A notice signed and then changed waits on his signature again in both places. `lienFieldsHash.shared.test.ts` holds the function's rule and the desk's `lienItemUnsigned` to one answer, row by row.
- **The words** (`lienSignatureReminderPush`, `_shared/lienApprovalPush.ts`): one notice reads *Sign a lien notice* over *650 · Tune 5 Liberty Hill · mail by Nov 20 · 4 days* and opens `/lien-desk?tab=notices&job=…&pile=ready`, where he signs. Eight read *Sign 8 lien notices* over *8 jobs · first to mail by Nov 16 · today* and open `/lien-desk?tab=notices&pile=ready`; the email lists each job beside its own day and says none can be printed or mailed until he signs. Always worded as a reminder: no press sends a notice *for signature*, so there is no first ask to tell apart.
- **The plan** (`lienLeaderReminder.ts`): a paper of kind `signature` goes in `plan.signatures`, counted in `ready`, and the confirm window gains *Malachi gets one message for the 8 notices to sign.*
- **The desk** (`LienDesk.tsx`, the reminder's memos and handler only): the unsigned approved notices (`queue.piles.ready` through `lienItemUnsigned`, the rule the second tile and the run count by) join the plan; the press names the leader when every paper in it waits on one; `remindLeader` sends them in one call (`tellLeaderOfLienSignatures`, `lienDeskIo.ts`) and counts by the function's `told` (`lienReminderTally(…, 'signature')`, *not waiting on a signature now*; the lot is named by `lienReminderSignatureLabel`).
- **Where the press stands** (`LienBoard.tsx`): on the Board's notice print, in the line that counts them: *8 wait on Malachi's signature · See them › · Remind Malachi of 8 papers ›*. It is the same press as To do's waiting head carries, with the same count, so it stands on a day when no row waits on an approval. Dead after a send, it reads *Reminded at 2:14 PM*. The leader's own desk has none.

## Old client, new function, and the other way round

The function's change is safe under the client as it was, which never sends the kind. The new client under the function as it was is answered 400 *Missing item_id*, which the desk already reads as *The part of the app that tells the leader is waiting for its update.* (`lienTellFailureReason`), and the papers to approve are still reminded. So the order of the merge and the deploy does not matter.

## Not done here

- The message goes by push, or by email to a leader with no phone registered, as every message of this function does. It is not a text message. Whether the leader should also get a text, and who chooses the way, is an open question the owner raised with this build.
- A retainage notice that is approved and unsigned is not in the reminder: the desk does not count it as waiting on a signature either (`readyWaits` reads the monthly notices alone).
- The phone's short pair beside the find box has no Remind; the Board's line does on every width.

## Verified

Nothing was sent to a real person: no press on the live desk, and the handler ran with its three senders replaced by recorders.

- `notifyLienApproval.run.test.ts` (the function's own `index.ts` over a fake database, 8 new cases): three notices are one push with the count; one opens on its job, and with no phone the same words go by email and name who pressed; a notice signed, printed, awaiting approval or voided is left out and `told` says so; a notice changed after signing is told; nothing left answers 200 with nothing sent; an affidavit and a retainage notice are never worded as a notice to sign; each leader gets his own jobs in one message; a helper is refused before a paper is read.
