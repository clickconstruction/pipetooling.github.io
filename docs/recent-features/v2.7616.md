# v2.7616 — Waiting on card: a window that has passed says closed (2026-11-17)

Punch list #365, a fix to PR 2 (v2.7568) found on its live walk. Client only. No migration and no edge function.

## What was wrong

The office's Waiting on card words the earliest deadline of the notices that wait on the leader as *First window closes* and the day. The walk of the live card, the day after a window, read *closes* for a day already behind us. The sentence never looked at today.

## What landed

- `buildWaitingOnLeader` takes `todayYmd`, and `windowWords` picks the sentence: *First window closed Nov 16.* once the day has passed, *First window closes today.* on the day, *First window closes Nov 18.* before it. With no date given the sentence is the old one.
- `useWaitingOnLeader` hands it `todayYmdInAppTz()`, the app's own day.
