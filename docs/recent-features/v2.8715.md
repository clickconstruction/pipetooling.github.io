# v2.8715 — The proof: download the packet with the signed copies (2026-11-27)

Punch list #439, piece 5, **Download** of part 5b. Client only: no migration, no edge function, nothing owed on prod. It stands on part 5a (v2.8710, #10050) and merges after it.

## What was there

Part 5a's packet is the list of who signed, to look at and print. Its foot said the signed copies were not attached. The client is to be handed the copies too.

## What changed

- **Download.** The packet's window has a third press, between **Close** and **Print**. It names its count: **Download with 3 signed copies**, or **Download the list** when nobody has signed. It is dead for the moment our own people's count is being read, so the PDF says what the screen says.
- **One PDF.** The list is drawn as its own page, on Letter: the client, the site's name, the count, **Signed**, **Asked, not yet signed**, our own people, and a foot with the day and how many copies follow. Then each signed copy, in the list's order. The browser saves it as *The proof - * and the site's name.
- **It does what is ready.** A copy that cannot be had is left out. The line under the presses says so, such as *Downloaded, with 2 of 3 signed copies attached. Not attached: * the company and the reason. The list in the PDF says *Signed copy attached.* or *Copy not attached.* with the reason, beside each company.
- **A copy is kept.** A download goes to the client as a print does, so the PDF itself is filed under Documents → Sent copies as `site_proof_packet` with `how: 'download'`, by `fileSentCopy`, as the estimate's **Download PDF** does. Once the copy is kept the line under the presses adds *A copy is kept under Documents, Sent copies.* A copy that could not be kept is not said to be.
- **The screen's foot** now reads *This is the list. Download adds the signed copies.* The page that prints keeps its own foot, *This is the list. The signed copies are not attached.*, since a print is the list alone.

## Where a copy comes from, and who gets one

Each signed copy is the paper's own PDF, the one **Open the PDF** opens on its record. `person-paper-pdf`, already deployed, answers a short-lived link, and the file is read from it. Read in its code and against prod's `estimator_reads_paper` on 2026-11-27:

- The four office roles get every signed copy.
- An estimator gets the copy of a paper with an outside company, which is every paper on the packet. A digital twin and an estimator in training are refused, and their packet names each copy as not attached with the function's words.
- A signed **form** is refused with *This paper is a form. Open its PDF from the form's own press.* A form's copy is therefore never attached in this part.
- A paper filed as received has no PDF of this kind and is not attached.

**Two things it leaves on prod, each by a path that exists today:**

- The function makes a paper's PDF the first time anyone asks for it and keeps it in storage, as **Open the PDF** does. A download can be that first asking.
- Each download files one row of `sent_documents` and one file in the `sent-documents` bucket: the packet with its signed copies. A second download of the same bytes points at the first file. That table's own policy decides who may file: a download whose copy is refused still saves, and its line does not say a copy is kept.

## How it is built

- `src/lib/addresses/siteProofPdf.ts`: `proofPdfLines` (pure, the list page line by line), `buildProofPacket(input, fetchCopy, loadPdfLib)` (draws the list with pdf-lib's own fonts, fetches each copy, merges, holds what fails), `downloadLabel`, `downloadOutcomeWords`, `proofFileName`, `copiesFollowWords`. Text a standard font cannot write is dropped by `pdfSafeText`, never thrown on.
- `src/lib/addresses/siteProofCopiesIo.ts`: `fetchSignedCopy`, the call to `person-paper-pdf` and the read of its link.
- `SiteProof.tsx`: the press, its dead state and the line that says what happened. One filing serves **Print** and **Download**. The browser's save is `saveBlobAs`.

## Design calls

Each is open to a flip.

- **No page numbers on the list.** The mock-up says *page 2* beside each company. Each line says *Signed copy attached.* instead, and the copies follow in the list's order.
- **A form's copy is left out and said**, not fetched by another road. Its PDF is the form's own function's.
- **The asked and our own people are on the PDF's list**, as on the screen.
- **The download keeps a copy.** The first build kept none. The packet goes to the client, and v2.8710's **Print** files its copy for that reason, so a download files the same way under the same kind.
- **The line waits for the copy.** It says a copy is kept only once the row is written, which a print's line does not wait for.

## Tested

- `siteProofPdf.test.ts`, 10 cases, the merge with real pdf-lib: the press's label, the outcome's words, the file's name, the foot, the list's lines with attached and held copies, the page count of a whole packet, a held copy and a file that is not a PDF, nobody signed, and thirty companies with names a standard font cannot write.
- `SiteProof.render.test.tsx`, 3 new smokes: the press dead until our own people are read, one PDF handed to the browser named for the site and its copy filed with `how: 'download'`; a copy held with its reason, and a file that was not kept not said to be; the list alone with no copy asked. Part 5a's eight smokes pass beside them, **Print** and a blocked print window among them.

## Not verified

- **Not seen live.** No copy was fetched from the real function and none was filed on a real database: the fetch and the filing are exercised with fakes. The function's answer is read in its code and in one read of prod's catalog.
- The list page was looked at as a PDF made by the kernel from made-up rows, not downloaded from the real page.
- A signed copy's own page size is kept as it is. A copy that is not Letter makes a packet of mixed sizes.
