## The autosave slice's own tests

`src/components/jobs/useJobFormAutosaveSlice.ts` is the autosave behind three forms: Edit Job (through `useJobFormAutosaveEngine`), the Estimates draft, and Edit Bid. Until v2.4027 it was tested only inside Edit Job's engine; v2.4027 added three `markSavedNow` cases. **`useJobFormAutosaveSlice.lifecycle.render.test.tsx`** (25 cases, fake timers) pins the rest of what the three forms rely on:

- **The baseline** — the first snapshot of a record is what is saved; with no record open nothing is dirty; opening a record after a blank form baselines it.
- **The debounce** — a change saves once, 1.2 s after it, and becomes the new baseline; each change restarts the clock; changing back before the clock runs out saves nothing; its own debounce is honoured; it reads *saving* while in flight; leaving the form, or switching to another record, drops a pending save.
- **Disabled** — a disabled slice stays dirty and never saves, not on the clock, on `flush` or on the close flush; enabling it starts the clock; disabling it cancels one.
- **A failed save** — reads *error*, stays dirty, calls no `onSaved`, and the next change tries again.
- **A change during a save** — queues exactly one follow-up, which saves what is on screen then; the baseline is what was on screen when the save started.
- **`flush`, the close flush, `clearBaseline`, `cancelPending`** — the close flush answers *clean*, *saved* or *failed*, and waits out a save in flight before writing what is still unsaved.

Checked the other way round: five deliberate breaks of the hook (a disabled slice saving, no queued follow-up, the baseline taken when the save ends, `flush` ignoring *disabled*, `cancelPending` saving) were each caught by at least one case, and the hook was restored.

The cases sit in their own file beside v2.4027's, which is unchanged — another session is changing Edit Job's use of the hook.
