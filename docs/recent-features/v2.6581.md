# v2.6581 — The company's checklist questions are written on the job's Documents tab; Settings reads them back (punch list #237, PR 4) (2026-11-06)

Punch list #237's fourth and last PR (`to-dos/stage-checklist-document/`, *One door*: "The office authors and reads in the same place"). PR 1 (v2.6328) and PR 1b (v2.6347) put the checks on the job, PR 2 (v2.6563) the signature, PR 3 (v2.6571) the record. This PR moves the authoring of the company's list out of Settings and onto the Documents tab, where the answers are read, and leaves Settings → Stage checklists as the read-only mirror the card asked for, "for one release, then gone".

## What changed

- **The add box's scope** (`AddCheckBox` in `StageChecklistSheet.tsx`, `allowCompany`): on the Documents tab the office's add box asks *Where the check goes*: **This job** (the default, as before) or **Every Trim Set in the company**. The company pick retitles the box, drops the plan sheet and the why (they are for one job), swaps the hint for what the pick does, and the press reads **Add to every Trim Set**. The write is `addCompanyQuestion` (`stageChecklistIo.ts`): a `stage_checklist_templates` row with no job, placed after the kind's live company questions (`nextCompanyOrder`), with the adder's name; the hook puts it in place, so every stage of the kind on the job counts it at once. The sheet's own add box offers no pick: the superintendent and the estimator author for their job alone.
- **Use as the company's list** (`companyListFromJobWords`, `promoteJobList`): under *Added for this job*, for the office when the job has two or more of its own checks, **Use as the company's Trim Set list** asks once with the counted press (`countedLabel`: *Add 3 checks to every Trim Set*) and runs `promote_stage_checklist_item` on each check in turn, past a refusal: each that takes moves to the company's group with its answer, as the per-item press did; the ones the company's list already asks stay on the job and are listed under the group head with the database's reason, in `batchOutcomeWords`' shape (*Added 2 checks. 1 did not add.* / *1 check did not add. It stays here with the reason.*). One check alone keeps its own press.
- **Take off the company's list** (`companyTakeOffWords`, `retireCompanyQuestion`): on a company question, for the office, two presses with a reason of three characters or more; the row is retired with `retired_by`, `retired_by_name` and `retired_why` (migration `20261106130000`, `docs/migrations/20261106130000_stage_checklist_company_authoring.md`), never deleted, so every answer keeps its words. The toast reads the stage's new count. The database's settle trigger already fires on a retire, so a stage the question was holding files itself.
- **▲ ▼ on the company's order** (`reorderCompanyQuestions`): the same arrows the job's own checks have, on the company's questions, for the office; one row at a time as the old editor wrote them, so a half-done order still reads.
- **Settings → Jobs & billing → Stage checklists is a mirror** (`StageChecklistSettingsCard.tsx` over the rewritten `stageChecklistEditor.ts` and `loadCompanyMirror`): the three kinds, each question with *asks for a photo* / *needs a signature* and a source line, *From Job 1042 · Riders Walk, where Pat Sample added it · Nov 3 · 10:14 am* for a question promoted from a job (`promoted_from` → the retired job item → its job), *Written by Jo Sample · …* for one written from a Documents tab, *Written on Settings · …* for one the old editor saved; under each kind a fold *Taken off · N* with the questions taken off with a reason, newest first. An empty kind points at **Jobs** and the Documents tab. Nothing is pressed; the card's row under Jobs & billing says what it is now.
- The read-out's foot says where the company's questions are written now. The guide *write the questions for a stage checklist* is rewritten to the new door (the company pick, the whole-list press, the order, the take-off with its reason, the mirror); *add a check to a job's stage checklist* points at it from the Documents box and the order section.

## Decisions

- **One migration more than the card counted.** The card's four migrations had all landed, and PR 4 planned none, but "taking a question off the company's list with a reason" has nowhere to keep the reason: the row's `why` is the author's why for the question. Two nullable columns, additive, no table. The adder-only trigger is written again with the two among the columns an adder may change, so a later PR can ask a job item's adder why without another migration.
- **A client loop, not a new RPC, for the whole list.** `promote_stage_checklist_item` already does one check in one transaction with the right refusal; running it per check gives "do what is ready" for free and names each refusal in the database's own words. A list of a dozen checks is a dozen short calls.
- **The company's order is written row by row.** The old editor did the same; a one-transaction RPC would be a migration for a tidier failure on a table of a few rows.
- **The mirror keeps the taken-off lines to the ones with a reason.** The old editor retired rows with no name and no reason, and a promote retires the job's row; neither is a thing the office took off the company's list, so neither is listed.
- **The sheet's add box stays job-only.** The card gave the company scope to "the office's on Documents" and "the company's list when it is authored"; the superintendent's and the estimator's door is the sheet, and their authoring is their job's.
