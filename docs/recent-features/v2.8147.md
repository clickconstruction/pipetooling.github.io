# v2.8147 — Permits: a customer tied to a name adds only the bids the rest of the page counts (2026-11-20)

Punch list #405 ([the card](../../to-dos/permits-market-page/README.md)), the second of three lines its audit of 2026-11-20 left under *Seen while building, not done*: **the practice bids count as our bids on the call list.** [v2.8138](./v2.8138.md) was the migration. This is the client, and it closes the line.

## What was left

A name's count of our bids has two parts.

- **Under the name itself.** `research_builders` counts these. Since v2.8138 it counts a bid of the last two years that is no practice bid.
- **Through a tied customer.** When a person ties a name on the permits to one of our customers, the client adds that customer's bids (`answeredBids`). This part still counted a tied customer's bids of any age. It told a practice bid by an older, narrower rule: only a project name of *ZZ Twin* or *ZZ Shadow*.

So a tied name could still read more bids on the call list than its customer's row in the table of companies.

## The rule, in one place in the client

`src/lib/research/ourBids.ts` is the client's one home for the rule, and it names the database's.

- `bidCounts(bid, today)`: no practice bid (a project name that starts with the word ZZ), made within `OUR_BID_DAYS`, 730 days. A bid with no day counts.
- `bidPartyKey(name)`: the permits' key for a name on a bid, or none for a ZZ name and for a customer named for a bid platform.
- `bidWon(outcome)`: won, or started.

Two counts read it:

- `bidCompanies` in `permitMarket.ts`, Market's table of companies. It read these same tests inline before. Its numbers do not move.
- `answeredBids` in `builderAnswers.ts`, the tied customers' part. It now takes today's day and counts by the rule. A tied customer that is no company adds nothing.

The database's copy is `research_bid_counts` and `research_bid_party_key`. `ourBids.test.ts` reads the newest migration that writes each and fails when its days or its platforms are not the kernel's.

## What the office sees
