# v2.7863 — Painted maps: Share no longer says Messages would not open when it did (2026-11-18)

Punch list #394, found on the walk the card asked for. The owner asked whether the walk could be done on an iOS simulator, and it could: an iPhone 17 Pro simulator on iOS 26.4, in Safari, against a local server signed in as the dev, with real links from the live function on a made-up map.

## The bug

With a link already out, **Text it from my phone…** opened Messages with the words written. Back on the sheet, an amber note read *This phone would not open Messages. Copy the link and paste it into a text.* That was false.

Nothing tells a page whether a phone opened another app, so `openPhoneMessages` watches for the page being left, hidden or blurred, and gives up after 1.5 seconds. The simulator took longer than that to switch to Messages, so the wait ended with the page still in front.

## The fix

- `onPageLeft` in `paintedMapShareIo.ts` calls back whenever the page is left, hidden or blurred, or comes back from that.
- The sheet listens for as long as it is open. A page that is left within a minute of a hand-off did open Messages, so the sheet takes back *This phone would not open Messages…* and *The link is ready. Press Open Messages.*, and the press gets its own name again.
- Other words are left alone: a reason a link was not made stays on the sheet.
- The 1.5 seconds stay as they are, so a phone that truly opens nothing still gets the fall back without a long wait.

## What the walk showed on an iPhone

- **Text it from my phone…** before a link is out: the link is made, then Safari asks *Open this page in “Messages”?* with **Cancel** and **Open**. **Open** opens a New Message with nobody picked and the words written. Safari does not refuse.
- The same press with the link out opens Messages at once, with no question.
- **Send it another way…** before a link is out opens the phone's own share sheet after the wait.
- **Copy the link** before a link is out puts the link on the clipboard.
- **End this link now** and **Throw this map away** worked, and the made-up map is gone from the live database.

## Checked

- `PaintedMapShareSheet.render.test.tsx`, three new cases: the false note is taken back when the page is left, *The link is ready* the same way, and another reason on the sheet is left alone.
- The same press on the same simulator, before the fix and after: the note showed before, and the sheet came back clean after.
