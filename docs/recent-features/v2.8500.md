# v2.8500 — The email that asks someone to sign now fits a phone (2026-11-24)

Punch list #376, the loose end *The ask scrolls sideways on a phone*. One line of one shared template: `supabase/functions/_shared/contractSigningEmail.ts`. No migration. The functions that build the email owe a redeploy, listed below.

## What was seen

The walk of 2026-11-24 opened the ask from Documents → Sent copies at 375 px. The email was 455 px wide, so it scrolled sideways, and the letterhead's right column and the paper's name were cut at the edge. The widest line was *Or open* with the signing link written out, 419 px.

## Why

A signing link is one run of about 100 characters with no space. A browser breaks it once, after the `?`, and the 64-character token after it cannot break. That one word set the least width of the whole email, since the email is a table.

## What changed

The written-out link's style gains `word-break:break-all;overflow-wrap:anywhere`, inline, where a mail app keeps it. The link breaks inside itself wherever the line ends. Nothing else moves: the `href`, the **Read and sign** button, the plain-text part and every word are as they were.

## Measured

The template was rendered with a made-up 64-character token in headless Chromium, before and after:

| Width | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 320 px | 457 px wide, 43 elements past the edge | 320 px, none |
| 375 px | 457 px wide, 39 elements past the edge | 375 px, none |
| 1,280 px | fits | fits |

At a desk the small-print line under the button now takes two lines where it took three, since the link starts beside *Or open* and wraps at the line's end. That is the only change there.

## Who sends this email

`buildContractSigningEmail` is called by two functions: `send-contract-for-signature`, for the ask, and `remind-person-papers`, for the reminder. The Send window's preview and the sample emails in Settings draw it in the app. Five more functions import the file for a name or a helper and send nothing built by it.

## Tests

- `src/lib/contractSigningEmail.test.ts`: the written-out link carries the two properties, the link is in the email twice and whole, and the plain-text part is unchanged.
- `src/lib/contractSigningSend.pin.json`: the byte-for-byte pin of the person path moves by that one attribute in its two cases, as it moved with the look in v2.6080.

## Not looked at

- A real mail app. Chromium only, on the rendered template. `word-break` is the older of the two properties and the one a mail app is likeliest to keep, so both are written.
- A sent email. No email was sent and no row was written for this change.
