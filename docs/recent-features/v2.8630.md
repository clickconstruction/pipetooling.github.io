## What changed

- **Two lines on the step tile** (`stepTile` in `src/lib/bridge/weekCall.ts`, `WeekTile.sub2`). The first line says what growth waits on: the narrowest step with its paces, in the words its card uses. A feeder gives its own pace and the field's (*Growth waits on Sales. Sales sold $30k a week. The field builds $44k a week.*); a pile step gives what it turns and what feeds it (*Growth waits on Bill. The office bills $19k a week. The field finishes $33k a week.*). The second line says what cash waits on: Collect, with what customers pay of what the office bills a week (*Cash waits on Collect. Customers pay $31k of the $46k billed a week.*), since the last step before the bank is where cash waits whichever step is narrowest. When Collect is the narrowest, one line says *Growth and cash wait on Collect.* and there is no second. The weeks the tile said until now stay on the step's card behind **The flow**. The page draws the second line under a rule (`BridgeWeek.tsx`), the Monday email draws it in its HTML and its text (`weekMail.ts`), and the sample email's Collect carries pace lines so its tile shows both (`weekMailSample.ts`). `weekCallWords` counts the second line toward the phone's first-screen budget.
- **The call on a lit feeder** (`buildWeekCall`). A feeder's card has no pace lines, so the card at the top said its pile alone. It now says the feeder's pace and the field's after the pile (*$120k awaiting an answer. Sold $30k of work a week. The field builds $44k a week.*), as a pile step's card says its two paces. The moves were already the lit step's (`proposedMoves`), Sales's four and Market's three, and a test now holds that on the card's own week.
- **The guide's rule section** (*read the Bridge*, *The rule, and where the work piles up*): the rule in plain words, what each step moves, why the fewest dollars a week is the step to widen, a pile against a jam and the direction line, and the tile's two lines on the first screen.
