# v2.3679 — Lien desk: a window that closes with nothing recorded is named the day it happens (2026-09-21)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Option 4 of the missed-month review on job 258 (the mock-up *Missed Month, Money Kept* holds options 1–3, pending the owner's word).

## Why

The owner, on job 258's June dot (*Missed · Deadline Sep 15 · Approved hours 8 · the lien right on Jun 2026 work is gone*): the window had closed six days earlier and nothing had said so. The exploration found why it was silent:

- `buildLienDeskQueue` tests `dueMonths` before `missedMonths`, so a job with July and August open sat in *To draft* and its closed June was carried on the entry but surfaced nowhere — not the pile, not the count, not the Dashboard.
- `summarizeLienDeskForNeedsYou` never read `piles.missed`; no Dashboard card existed for a loss that had already happened.
- Nothing stamps a closed window: the only writer of `status = 'missed'` is the office's Skip. The RPC keeps a closed month for seven days (`deadline >= app_today() - 7`), then it ages out of every client-side computation.

## What shipped

- **Kernel** ([`lienDesk.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDesk.ts)) — `missedUnrecorded` on the entry: the closed months no `missed` row names (a skip or a noted miss). `counts.missed` is the lens count — every job with a closed month, whatever its pile; `piles.missed` is unchanged. `summarizeLienDeskForNeedsYou().missed` = `lienDeskMissedSummary`: jobs, months, dollars and one line per job (biggest balance first); the hook fills each line's `label`.
- **The record** — `LienDeskDraftFields.windowClosed { name, at }` (+ parse); [`noteLienWindowMissed`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDeskIo.ts) inserts a `missed` row for the months with no reason and never touches a live draft. [`lienMonthHistory.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienMonthHistory.ts) reads it: outcome stays *missed*, `at`/`byName` say who; `lienMonthMissUnnoted` is the "still silent" test.
- **Dashboard** ([`dashboardNeedsYou.ts`](../../src/lib/dashboardNeedsYou.ts)) — `lien-window-missed`, red, tier 10 (a loss that has happened outranks a deadline to meet): *The lien window for June 2026 closed on 258 · Dudley Mason with no notice* / *N lien windows closed with no notice on N sub jobs*, the dollars, and *Note it on the Lien desk so the record says who saw it*. Dispatch in `DashboardPinnedQuickRow` and `QuickfillNeedsYouSection` → `?liendesk=1&liendeskPile=missed&liendeskJob=<first>`; `JobsStagesTab` reads `liendeskPile`, the modal takes `initialPile`.
- **Desk** ([`LienDeskModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskModal.tsx)) — the Missed chip filters by the lens; a row outside the Missed pile with a closed month carries *Jun window closed · not noted*; the pane gets a strip between the gates and the months — red until noted (*The window for June 2026 closed on Sep 15 with no notice — the lien right on that work is gone. The balance is still owed and rides on this notice.* + **Note it as missed**), quiet grey with *noted by Taunya Sep 21* after. [`LienDeskMonths`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskMonths.tsx)' dot dialog shows *Noted · date by name* and, while unnoted, the same button.
- Guide *send lien notices from the Lien desk*: the lens, the strip, the Dashboard line, and that noting is not a skip.

## Not done, on purpose — and what is next

- **The seven-day floor stands in this PR.** The alarm fires the day the window closes and lives as long as the RPC returns the month. A follow-up migration widens `list_lien_notice_months` so an unrecorded closed month is returned since the desk went live (2026-09-14) rather than for seven days, and a recorded one drops — then the line truly cannot age out unseen. Written as its own PR so the client lands first and the DDL goes in a quiet moment.
- No nightly stamp: the record is a person's, on purpose — the point is that someone saw it.
- The Missed pile's own footer sentence is unchanged; the strip is the one door.
