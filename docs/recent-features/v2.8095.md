# v2.8095 — Document builder: the link and the trades are the ones of the job you picked (2026-11-20)

Punch list #404, found by the audit of 2026-11-20. No migration and no function.

## What was wrong

On Documents → Document builder → Insurance requirements for subs, **This sheet's link** and **Send the sheet to this job's trades** were one card each for every job. Picking another job in the **Job** list kept each card's state:

- The last job's link stayed on the page, with **Copy the link** live, until the next job's read landed. When that read failed, it stayed for good beside the reason. A person could copy one job's link while looking at another job's sheet, and hand a sub the wrong sheet.
- The last job's trades stayed listed until the next job's were read, with their send presses live. The open send box, the ticked **Send it anyway** and the line *Sent to 2 trades.* carried over too.

## The fix

Each card is keyed by its job (`FilledBuilder.tsx`), so a job switch starts both cards fresh. The link card reads *Reading the link…* and the send card *Reading this job's trades…* until the picked job's own are in. Keyed by the job and not by the sheet's row, so the first save of a new job's sheet, which gives it a row, does not start the cards over.

## Tests

`FilledBuilder.render.test.tsx`: with one job's link on and one trade listed, picking another job whose reads have not landed shows neither, and shows both reading lines. The test fails without the keys.
