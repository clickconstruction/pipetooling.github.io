# v2.7487 — Reserved stickers: the job says which names are held, and by whom (2026-11-16)

Punch list #329, the third PR of the owner's word of 2026-11-16 (v2.7484 the name, v2.7486 the preview). He wants printing a sticker to reserve a place that is filled in later. This PR is where a person sees those places on the job, and what makes a reserved name stay reserved when the next run is made. Client only, no migration.

## What was there

A job's page counted *12 stickers not yet on a box* over its boxes, with **Runs** opening the fold. Which names, who printed them and when were inside the fold, closed by default, and so was **These are on boxes**. The print window's Names field opened on the site's next free name read from its boxes alone, so with G92 to G200 reserved and on no box it opened on G92 again and met the hold, *Print them anyway*.

## What changed

- **The job's line.** `reservedOfJob` (`stickerPlace.ts`) reads the job's runs into what the line says, and `useJobStickers` hands it on as `reserved`. One run holding them: *109 stickers reserved and not on a box yet, G92 to G200. Printed Nov 16 by Dana. Scan one on the phone to say which box it is on.* A broken stretch is said by its first names, *G93, G95 and 105 more* (`namesSpanWords`). More than one run: *, in 2 runs*, and the fold has each. A run with no names: the count alone.
- **The press that fills them in.** **These are on boxes** sits beside **Runs** on that line whenever a reserved sticker has a name, and opens the form on the reserved names when one run holds them. The fold's own press stays.
- **The phone.** The On site tab's line under the boxes says the same head: *2 stickers reserved and not on a box yet, A3 and A4. Scan one to say which box it is on.*
- **The Runs fold.** A run's row reads *96 reserved and not on a box yet, G105 to G200* where it read *96 stickers not used yet* (`unusedStickersWords`).
- **A new run starts past the reserved names.** `nextNameToReserve` (`stickerNames.ts`) is the site's next free name with every reserved name counted as a box of that name. With G92 to G200 reserved the window opens on G201. A site with no box yet and G1 to G50 reserved opens on G51, where the field opened empty.

## What is not held, and is the owner's call

**Add a container**, on the phone and in the office, offers the site's next free name from its boxes alone. With G92 to G200 reserved it still offers G92. That is right when the stickers go on in order: a box made by hand as G92 takes sticker G92 when it is scanned, and the sheet opens on it. It is wrong when someone adds a box that will never carry a sticker. Making the offer skip reserved names is one more read on the phone's Add sheet, which has no signal half the time, so it was left for his word.
