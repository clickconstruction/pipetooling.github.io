# v2.5104 — GC mode: waits, places, parts and a new baseline on the schedule (2026-10-09)

PR 9b of the schedule's real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/schedule-pr9.md` on branch `spike/gc-mode`, the second of PR 9's four cuts). A dev's until the schedule's PR 10. No migration and no edge function: every write is PR 5's and 6b's.

## The change

- **What the work waits on** (G-73 to G-75, `GcWaits` in [`GcScheduleCards.tsx`](../../src/components/gc/GcScheduleCards.tsx)): a delivery, the customer's decision, a permit or the utility's work, added with its name, whose work it is for, who we wait on, the day it is expected and the work it holds (`newWait`, the reducer's `addScheduleWait`; `gc_schedule_add_wait`). Each next step, a new expected day with who said so, and taking one off are records (`setScheduleWaitStep`, `removeScheduleWait`).
- **Where the work is** (G-83, [`GcPlaces.tsx`](../../src/components/gc/GcPlaces.tsx)): the card under the chart and its window, where the office keeps a place on each bar (a guess counting once kept), and the opened bar's place line in its form (the new `place` slot). Each keep is refused whole (`placeChanges`) and is a record (`gc_schedule_set_places`).
- **A line in parts** (G-39, [`GcSplitBars.tsx`](../../src/components/gc/GcSplitBars.tsx)): the parts card under the opened bar and the split window. A split and a join are plan writes with the reducer's lines (`splitPress`, `joinWords`; `gc_schedule_split`, `gc_schedule_join`); a part's new dates go through **Why it moved** like any move (8b). A part's percent comes from its reports (PR 16), so the pickers stay on the spike.
- **A new baseline** (G-41, `GcBaseline`): the plan as it stands, named, with why, a plan write (`baselinePress`, the reducer's `setScheduleBaseline`; `gc_schedule_baseline`). The card lists every baseline and says when a new one is due. The log's line ends why with its own full stop, which the prototype's left out.
- A plan write someone else beat shows 8a's refusal, keeps what the person typed and reads the schedule again. The press state moved to [`useSchedulePress.ts`](../../src/components/gc/useSchedulePress.ts), shared by the cards and the parts.
- Ported from the prototype's `GcBuildingSchedule.tsx` (`WaitsCard`, `NewWait`, `BaselineCard`), `GcPlaces.proto.tsx` and `GcSplitBars.tsx`, their words kept: each press is a callback where the prototype dispatched to its reducer.
