# v2.7912 — The Bid Board no longer scrolls sideways on a phone (2026-11-19)

Seen on 2026-11-19 while measuring v2.7907, and there with that change and without it. No migration, no edge function, no new page.

## What was wrong

The Bid Board's tools row is the search, the development filter and a group of presses. The group was written as four presses that never shrink. It has since grown to six: **Marked bids**, **Archived**, the ask to rate a GC, **Pricing rules**, **Reply book** and **Customer review**. The ask shows only while one is open, and it is the wide one.

Measured on a local preview against live data at 375 px, with one ask open (*Rate a GC after a job · 1*):

| At 375 px | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| The page's `scrollWidth` | 478 | 375 |
| The press group, in a 343 px column | 462 px wide, x 16 to 478 | 343 px wide, x 16 to 359 |
| The presses | one line, the last three ending at x 388, 433 and 478 | two lines: Marked, Archived and the ask, then the three icons, each line kept to the right |

The cause was found by hiding and measuring again. With the group hidden the page was 375 px. With the ask alone hidden it was 375 px too: the other five presses are 257 px and fit.

## The fix

On a phone the group may shrink to its line and wrap its own presses (`bidBoardToolsRow.ts`): `flex: 0 1 auto`, `min-width: 0`, `flex-wrap: wrap`, `justify-content: flex-end`. A group that fits its line is drawn as it was, so the board without an open ask is unchanged. Over 660 px the group still never shrinks or wraps.

- **Why wrap, and not a sideways scroller:** a scroller would hide the last presses behind an edge with nothing to say they are there. Wrapping keeps all six on screen.
- **Why not shorter words on the ask:** the group with a short ask is still wider than the column at 375 px, so it would have to wrap anyway.
- The group's whole style now comes from `bidBoardToolsGroupStyle`, so the board writes none of it by hand.

## Tests

- `bidBoardToolsRow.test.ts`: the layout's three new answers on a phone and on a desk, and the group's style: it may shrink, it wraps, it keeps to the right, and a desk does none of that.
- `e2e/viewport-smoke.spec.ts` already holds the Bid Board to no sideways scroll at phone widths. It did not catch this because its account has no open ask. Its comment now says what the row is.
