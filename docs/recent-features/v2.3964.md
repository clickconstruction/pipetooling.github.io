# v2.3964 — Bid room: a change order signature now records the electronic-signature consent (2026-09-27)

Client only; no migration, no edge function change, nothing to deploy.

## Why

`sign-bid-room` has stored the consent words for a change order since v2.3118 (`recordEsignConsent` with `parseEsignConsent(body.esignConsent)`), but the bid room's change order card never showed the consent line and never posted `esignConsent`. `parseEsignConsent(undefined)` is null, `recordEsignConsent` returns early on a null consent, and so every change order signed in the room landed with its acceptor fields and no `esign_consents` row.

## What shipped

- **`src/lib/bidRoom/changeOrderSign.ts`** (pure, 11 tests) — `changeOrderSignError` (name, then consent, then the agree box, then the drawing — the order `ContractAcceptSignatureForm` uses) and `changeOrderSignBody(documentId, input, consent)`, the `sign-bid-room` body with `esignConsent` always on it. `CHANGE_ORDER_CONSENT_NOUN` is `this change order`. It lives outside `src/lib/bids/` and imports nothing from it.
- **`src/pages/BidRoom.tsx`** — `RoomChangeOrderCard` renders `EsignConsentLine` with `esignConsentText({ audience: 'gc', documentNoun: 'this change order' })` and the line's own checkbox, above the card's agree box; Approve runs `changeOrderSignError` and posts `changeOrderSignBody`. The card now hands the page the whole body (`onAnswer(body)`), so a decline is `{ action: 'decline', documentId, note }` as before. The consent box also holds the auto-reload off (`useHoldsUnsavedWork`).
- **`src/components/EsignConsentLine.tsx`** — optional `checkboxAlign` (`'center'` by default, `'start'` on the left-aligned card). No other surface changes.
- **Stored equals shown.** `clauseText` ends with `☐ I agree to sign electronically.`; the card renders that checkbox and requires it, the arrangement `EstimateAcceptBody` uses. The render test reads every part of the clause off the card.
- Help guide `send-a-bid-for-signature` says what the GC ticks on a change order.

## Found on the way (not changed)

- **The proposal section stores a sentence it does not show.** It passes no `checkbox` to `EsignConsentLine` and shows its own box — *I agree to conduct business electronically and accept this proposal, its inclusions, exclusions, and terms.* — while the posted `clauseText` still ends `☐ I agree to sign electronically.` Making the two equal means either a second required box on the proposal (a change to what every GC signing a proposal sees) or a stored clause that differs from the versioned text; both are a decision, so the proposal is left as it was.
- The consent line's lead sentence on the proposal (*Your typed or drawn signature below applies to the option selected above…*) is shown and not stored. The change order card passes no lead.
- Change orders signed in the room before this release have no `esign_consents` row; their `acceptor_consented_at`, IP and device are on the estimate row. Nothing here backfills them.

## Verified

- `changeOrderSign.test.ts` (11), `BidRoom.render.test.tsx` (8: the three from v2.3159 and five new — the line and both boxes on the card, refused without consent, refused without the agree box, the posted body with `esignConsent`, a decline); `releaseNotes.test.ts`, `helpGuideContent.test.ts`, `customerSurfaceRegistry.test.ts`; typecheck, eslint, the theme token check; `npm test` in full.
- The render test keeps the file's own `MemoryRouter` — the page reads `?t=` from the location and `renderWithProviders` starts at `/` — and uses the harness's `installDomShims` and `settle`.
- Not run in the live app from this worktree. No `esign_consents` row was observed being written; the server path is unchanged and was read, not exercised.
