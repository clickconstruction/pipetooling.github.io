# v2.4521 — Pipeline: a Stripe bill you mark paid moves to Paid in Full right away (2026-10-04)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. The owner marked a Collections job paid (cash, $5,355, on a bill that had gone out through Stripe) and it stayed in Collections until the page was refreshed.

## Why it stayed

Closing a Stripe bill in full goes through `record-stripe-invoice-out-of-band-payment`: Stripe marks the invoice paid, and **our ledger is written by `stripe-webhook`** (`mark_invoice_paid_from_stripe`) when Stripe calls back. [`BilledPaymentConfirmationModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/BilledPaymentConfirmationModal.tsx) waited a fixed 700 ms and then ran `onSuccess` (the board's `loadJobs`). When the webhook took longer, the reload read the job still `billed`, and nothing reloaded it again. A bill that never went through Stripe (`mark_invoice_paid`, written in the call itself) was never affected.

## What changed

- **Wait for the write, not for a clock.** [`waitUntilLanded.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/waitUntilLanded.ts) (new kernel) asks a `read` at once and then every interval until it says yes or the time runs out; a read that throws counts as "not yet". The window's `read` is `jobs_ledger_invoices.status = 'paid'` for the bill, every 350 ms for up to 10 s. `onSuccess` runs once it reads paid, so the reload sees the job `paid`, it leaves Billed or Collections, and Paid in Full takes it.
- **The button says so.** It reads *Confirming with Stripe…* while it waits.
- **A slow webhook.** After 10 s the window closes with a toast (*Stripe is still confirming this payment. The row will move when it lands.*) and keeps asking every 2 s for a minute; when the bill reads paid it runs `onSuccess` again.
- **A part payment** is written by the function itself and still waits for nothing.

## Not done here

The function could write the ledger itself on a full close, as it does for a part payment, so the board would not depend on the webhook's timing at all. That is a server change to the money path (the webhook's call would have to be a no-op the second time) and wants its own PR.

## Tests
