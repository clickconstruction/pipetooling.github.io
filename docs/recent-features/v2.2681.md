# v2.2681 — Contract Desk PR 2: the Contract modal, send, and the customer's signing page (2026-09-03)

The loop closes (schema + coverage: v2.2679). The office sends a job's agreement from the Pipeline row; the customer opens one durable link on a phone, reads the scope, price, payment line and terms, and signs — typed or drawn — with the same block they already use on estimates. The chip on the row turns amber on send and green on signature.

## DB (migration `20260903144259` — fragment in docs/migrations/)

- `job_contracts.public_token` (plaintext, partial unique) — the durable link: minted on the first send, reused by every resend, moved to the replacement draft on Void & redo. Push before deploying the functions.

## Client

- **Document kernel** [`lib/jobs/jobContractDocument.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobContractDocument.ts) (+ tests): `JobContractFields` (scope lines, exclusions, amount, payment-terms preset or custom text, dates, note), tolerant `parseJobContractFields`, `buildJobContractPrefill` (scope from the accepted estimate's lines else the job's fixtures; amount from the accepted total else `revenue`), `paymentTermsSentence` with the deposit filled in, `jobContractHeading`, `DEFAULT_JOB_CONTRACT_TERMS_PLAIN` (built-in terms until the Contract Book has a customer template), and `buildJobContractDocumentHtml` — the one rendering the office preview and (PR 3) the PDF share.
- **Lifecycle kernel** [`lib/jobs/jobContractLifecycle.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobContractLifecycle.ts): status parse, editable/live, history chips + colors, `jobContractSigningUrl`, the signature audit line.
- **`JobContractModal`** ([`components/jobs/JobContractModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobContractModal.tsx)): lien-modal rules — autosaved draft from the first real edit, Send is the gate, fields lock once sent, ✕ closes. Who signs (name / email / mobile / also-send-to, prefilled from the job), what they're signing (Contract Book template with `audience = 'customer'` or the built-in terms, scope textarea, not-included, amount, payment chips, dates, note), sending (message, reminders checkbox for PR 5), Preview as customer (print window), **Send by email**, **Copy link**, **Text link** (`sms:` with the link prefilled), **Sign in person** (opens the page with `&inperson=1`), and once sent the amber strip: sent/opened/link-good-until, Resend, Copy, Text, Sign in person, two-click **Void & redo** (voids the row, moves its token to a fresh draft at revision + 1). History rows with chips + View.
- **Entry points**: the Pipeline row chip is now a button, and the ✍ quick action joins the Crew-column stack (rings orange while the job has no contract). Both open the modal; `job-contract-changed` refreshes the board's chips.
- **Public page** [`pages/JobContractSign.tsx`](../../src/pages/JobContractSign.tsx) at `/contract/sign?t=…` (route registered): the bid room's shell, pinned light — letterhead from the invoice-issuer setting, heading, Job # / for / date / rev, scope bullets, not-included, dates, contract amount + payment line, terms collapsed behind "Read the full terms", `ContractAcceptSignatureForm` (Type / Draw, consent, button names the amount), "Questions first? Call …". Signed state shows the record (drawn image or typed line + audit line); voided / expired / empty links get plain messages; a stale revision or double submit refreshes to the current version.
