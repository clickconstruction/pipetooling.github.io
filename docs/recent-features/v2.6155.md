## What changed

- **`lib/pay/draftPayrollBars.ts`** (new, 11 tests): one row's facts (hours, the live estimate, the report's gross · net · paid sum · last paid day, salaried, the review count, the pending sessions) → its bar of parts (**paid** · **owed on the report** · **withheld** by the deductions · an **estimate**, hatched), each with a fraction, a short label for inside the part and a hover sentence; whether the figure wears *est.* and the row *salary 8h*; the amber dot's count and words; and the period's bar summed from the rows with the caption *This period · $11,482.50 · 4 of 9 paid*. The $ column stays `draftPayrollRowCashDue`; a report whose additional lines lift its net past its gross draws its bar to the larger figure, so no part is hidden. `draftPayrollRowStatus` keeps the six Status words for the screen reader.
- **`lib/pay/draftPayrollPendingApprovals.ts`**: the read gains `user_id` — `fetchPendingApprovals` returns the count and a tally by user (`pendingApprovalsFromRows`, pure); `useDraftPayrollPendingApprovals` carries `draftPayrollPendingByUserId` beside the count (its smoke gains a case). `People.tsx` turns the user ids into pay names through the roster, so a name one login user carries gets its dot; a name no user has gets none, and the chip in the button row still counts it.
- **`components/pay/DraftPayrollBars.tsx`** (new): the period bar with its caption, the row's bar cell (the Review press stays beside the bar when the row still needs one), the *est.* and *salary 8h* chips (the hover is the approvals queue's `SALARY_FLAT_HOURS_CHIP` and its title) and the amber dot (*● 2 waiting*, a press to the Hours tab). The green and the blue are `MONEY_BAR_TONE`'s paid and billed, so money reads the same colour as the Pipeline row; the hatch is the Legal desk's.
- **`components/pay/DraftPayrollModal.tsx`**: **Show the bars** beside *Show 0 hours* (`useShapeView('draft-payroll-bars')`, localStorage key `pt-shape-view:draft-payroll-bars:<userId>`, off by default; *Show today's look* turns it back). With it on, the *Total | Left* sentence is the period bar, the banner is one chip in the button row (*3 sessions await approval ›*, its sentence on the hover), the Status column is **Cash due** with a bar per row, Cash Due is **$** with its cents drawn smaller and *est.* on an estimate. The body is one `renderBody(withBars)`; the print summary keeps today's words. Two new props: `pendingByPerson` and `isSalaryPerson`.
- **The side by side**: `/people?tab=pay_stubs&sbs=1` with a dev signed in → Draft Payroll draws **Today** over **With the bars** in the same window, on the same live rows, no switch. On a dev server from this branch: `/dev-login?as=1&to=/people` then press Payroll → Draft Payroll with `&sbs=1` on the address bar (the dev login's `to=` drops a second query part, so add `?tab=pay_stubs&sbs=1` by hand and open the window).

## The owner's first look (2026-10-30, on the live side by side)

Taunya looked at the prototype on a dev server the same afternoon and asked for four things, all in this PR: *owed on a report $2,309.20* "should become *owed $2,309.20*" (the period bar's parts now read *paid $X · owed $X · withheld $X · no report $X*, `PART_SHORT_WORDS`, with the long words on the hover); "we can remove the *paid* and *owed on a report* index" (the legend under the period bar is gone); "we can move the trash icon to the right of view instead of below it" (the Actions cell no longer wraps); and "we can hide" the key paragraph under the table (gone). Then: "for the $ column, $1,200.00 should have the cents .00 look smaller" (`DraftPayrollMoney`: the cents at 0.72em in the muted colour, in the bars view only). And: "I don't love seeing *salary 8h* where it currently is, I would prefer to see it under the $ rows in small font" (a small muted line under the figure, not a chip beside the name). And: "*owed on the report* can become *report due*" (the row bar's words when nothing is paid on the report yet). Then: "I would like Record payment button to replace the *report due* in cash due and I would like it to be a blue button" (a report with nothing paid on it draws a blue **Record payment** in the Cash due cell instead of its bar, and the green one leaves that row's Actions; a part-paid report keeps its bar and its Actions button). And: "this also allows us to get rid of the Paid column" (gone in the bars view; today's look keeps it). And: "*paid Tue* bar should be no wider than its text" and "the Cash due column should be no wider than the Record payment button" (a row's bar is as wide as its words, each part its label, and the column shrinks to its widest press or bar; the period bar still fills the window). And: "center the Cash due and $ titles" (both headings centred). And: "I don't love *Every report for this period is generated. Next: mark the payroll transactions on Tally…* Perhaps we should move this to the bottom of the page" (under the table in the bars view; today's look keeps it over the table), and "center the contents of Cash due column" (done).

## What the mock-up asked for that this PR does not do

- The printable summary (Print) keeps *Total | Left* and the Cash Due column as words; the mock-up did not draw it.
- A pay name two login users share gets both users' pending sessions on its row; a pay name no login user carries gets no dot, only the chip's count. The pricing hook has the same edge (`uniqueUserIdByPayName`).
- The period bar sums everyone on the roster, as *Total* does today, while the table lists only the people with hours unless *Show 0 hours* is on.

## Decisions

- A fourth part, **withheld** (gross − net, grey), because a report's deductions are real money the mock-up's three parts could not place; it draws only when there is one.
- The bars never change what the window pays: the $ column is still `draftPayrollRowCashDue`.
- No migration, no function change.
