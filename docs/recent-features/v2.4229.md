# v2.4229 — Help: the submittal guide in plain words (2026-09-30)

Punch list #58, item 2 (after [v2.4228](v2.4228.md), the Workbench walkthrough). No migration, no edge function, no code on the page. The guide *build a submittal package* was the last of the Submittals surfaces still in the 2026-09-1x voice: 150 lines of 30-word sentences glued with dashes, parentheses and `·` lists, while the page and its walkthrough had read plain since v2.4123–v2.4126.

## What changed

- **The guide** ([`build-a-submittal-package.md`](../../src/content/help/build-a-submittal-package.md)) is rewritten section by section by the rules (`to-dos/plain-words-elsewhere.md` holds them): one idea per sentence, none over 20 words; *you* + a verb and the control's exact name (every `{{button}}` and `{{chip}}` token kept); a trade word explained beside itself the first time (*a revision is one version of the submittal*, *the room is the page where the GC reads your rows*, *the cut sheet, the maker's page for the product*); no dashes, semicolons, parentheses or `·` lists inside a sentence. The headings are the same words (one `—` became a colon), every `:::example` panel is kept word for word, and the first paragraph is two short sentences so the share card reads whole. Two stale names were corrected on the way: the robot's schedule button writes the same rows as *Type or paste the schedule* (the guide still said *Plug in the fixture schedule*), and *Assign pages…* sits on the file's line, not a strip.
- **The rules test** — [`helpGuidePlainWords.test.ts`](../../src/lib/helpGuidePlainWords.test.ts): the sentence rules over the body's paragraphs and list items, with the frontmatter, the headings and the example panels left out, a token read as its label, and an italic span read as one quoted word (what the screen prints is quoted as printed, so *57 of 75 on rows · 18 not used* passes). `PLAIN_WORDS_GUIDES` names the guides held to it — one today; item 3 of the to-do (an owner call) decides whether that becomes every guide.
- The to-do's status: items 1 and 2 done; the convention call is what is left.
