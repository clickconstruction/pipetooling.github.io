# v2.8480 — Takeoffs: the What Pricing sees rail folds to a strip (2026-11-24)

The second of two changes to **Bids → Takeoffs** in a narrow window. An estimator who works with the plans in one window and the app beside it asked on 2026-11-24: "can we add an option to minimize the 'what pricing sees'? while it is valuable information, in this case it is in the way." v2.8476 made every row press reachable. This one gives her the fold. No migration, no edge function.

The look was drawn first, twice: https://claude.ai/artifact/DayYuapLAAtVDdiUJXMv5F. The page moves at 900 and 1,400 px, light and dark, and holds the first draw, what was wrong with it, and the second draw this was built from.

## What it does

- **The fold.** The rail folds to a strip: a bar across the top of the sheet, pinned as the list scrolls, as the rail beside the list is pinned. The strip keeps the rail's headline: **Materials**, **Costed** as N of M, and the red no-cost count when there is one. One press opens the rail: **Show**.
- **In a wide window** the rail is beside the list as before, with **Hide** in its heading. Hide folds it and the list takes the whole width. The choice is kept on that browser.
- **In a narrow window**, where the list and the rail do not fit side by side (a sheet under 1,096 px, a window of about 1,274), the rail always starts folded. Show opens it over the list, 320 px wide and never taller than the window, scrolling by itself. The press turns into **Hide** where it stands. Hide, Esc or any press on the list puts the rail away, and the press on the list still lands. Nothing about a narrow window's open rail is kept, so it cannot come back by itself over the rows.
- **Nothing lost.** The rail is the same panels in the same order in all three places. Fill from rules & book, Print schedule of values, the Factor, Refresh Sold in rules from the catalog, Request quotes, Copy from a previous bid and the per-fixture unit costs are each behind Show.

## How it is built

- **`lib/bids/takeoffRailFold.ts`** (new, pure): `railMode({ fits, choice, panelOpen })` answers `beside`, `folded` or `over`; `railPressOutcome` says what Show or Hide does at a width; `railStripFacts` writes what the strip says; `readStoredRailChoice` / `writeStoredRailChoice` keep the choice under `bids_takeoff_rail_fold_v1`, each inside a try and catch, so a browser that refuses still draws, folds and opens.
- **`TakeoffRailStrip`** (new): the bar. Its figures are values in place (`WorkedOutValue`), the amount through `Money`, the red chip's whole sentence on its hover and for a screen reader. The look is `.takeoffRailStrip` in `index.css`, in theme tokens.
- **`TakeoffCostRailView`** draws the rail's panels once and places them: in the grid's second column, or hung under the strip. The v2.8476 layout that put the rail under the list is gone.
- Esc is heard on the window while the rail is open over the list, and left alone while a dialog is open on top of it.

## What it leaves

- At the top of the sheet the strip repeats Costed and Materials from the tiles just above it. The tiles scroll away and the strip does not.
- The red count on the strip is not a press. The tiles' **Costed** already filters the sheet to the fixtures with no lines.
- On a phone the strip is three lines tall while pinned.
- Chromium only.
