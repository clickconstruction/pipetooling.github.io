# v2.6815 — Take a photo of the supply house ticket at the counter, right in Tally (punch list #295, PR 2) (2026-11-09)

PR 2 of four on [`to-dos/ticket-photo/README.md`](../../to-dos/ticket-photo/README.md), the mock-up's frames A to G: the tech's press. PR 1 (v2.6812) laid the tables, the sweep's source and the matcher.

## What changed

- **`src/components/tickets/TicketPress.tsx`**, mounted by `JobTally.tsx` under the tab bar for every role on every tab: **Photo of a ticket** opens the camera; the link under it takes a photo or a PDF the person already has. The jobs the sheet will offer are read when the press is drawn, and kept on the phone for the day, so a counter with no signal still gets them.
- **`TicketSheet.tsx`** over **`src/lib/tickets/ticketJobs.ts`**: the chips in order (a PO code made for the person today, the job clocked in now, the day's other jobs, a job found through **Another job…**), the first of the first two picked already, a second tap for a second job, **Not for one job**, **Pick the job later**, **+ Another page**, **Retake**, **+ total**, **+ note**. The save button names what it will do (`ticketSaveLabel`: *Save the ticket to JP1042 and JP1038*).
- **`src/hooks/useTicketCapture.ts`**: the pages wait in the hook, shrunk to the large size, until Save. Save writes the row, the pages and `added_at` (`ticketsIo.ts`); a send lost to the signal keeps the bytes and queues a `counter-ticket` press.
- **The offline queue** gains its eighth kind, `counter-ticket` (`outbox.ts`, `keptPhotos.ts`, `landFieldPress.ts` → `landCounterTicket`, `afterLanding.ts`): the row first, then each page, then the landing, each the same write on a second try.
- **`MyTickets.tsx`** on the Transactions tab and **`TicketWindow.tsx`**: a person's tickets with where each stands, and the taker's three presses, Change the job, Add a page and Remove this ticket, until the office has used the ticket.
- **Migration `20261109200000_po_code_read_by_its_person.sql`**: a person reads the PO codes made for them and the supply house such a code names ([`docs/migrations/20261109200000_po_code_read_by_its_person.md`](../migrations/20261109200000_po_code_read_by_its_person.md)).
- **Guide** *take a photo of a supply house ticket*; the signal guide names the new kept press. `GLOSSARY.md` → *Counter ticket*, `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` §6a, `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`, `JOB_TALLY_ARCHITECTURE.md`.

## Not in the mock-up, and why

- **Pick the job later.** A phone with no signal and nothing kept from the day has no job to offer. The photo still saves, and the taker or the office names the job afterwards.
- **A PO code whose job the person cannot read** is left off the sheet; Another job… still finds what they can read.
