# v2.6808 — Material counted under the wrong job can move to the right one, every box keeping its label (punch list #209) (2026-11-09)

A whole site's count was recorded on the phone under the wrong job, and the owner said so on 2026-11-09. The architecture map had read those rows as the company's own yard stock; that reading was wrong. Nothing could fix it: no screen, RPC or dev-seat verb changed a row's job, the ledger takes no update under RLS, and the only code that carried on-site rows sat inside the whole-job combine, which deletes the source job. Rebuilding the boxes by hand on the right job would have changed every printed QR code, lost the history and written false shortfall rows.

## Where the shape changed from the brief, and why

- **The move's wrapper is `SECURITY DEFINER`**, where the other on-site wrapper runs as the caller. `job_material_movements` and `job_container_moves` have no UPDATE policy for anyone, so no caller's RLS could make this write. The gate is inside, as on the other dev wrappers.
- **Box moves take no `job_id`**: `job_container_moves` has none. They follow their container.
- **A trigger nobody asked for**: a new row takes its container's job. A combine deletes the source job, so a stale phone's press there fails on the foreign key. A move leaves the source standing, so the same press would have landed under it, on a job with no boxes, where no screen reads it. The count would have gone missing silently on the days after the correction.
- **A note on each job**: a combine leaves one on its target; the office reading the first job needs to see where its boxes went.
- **The carry differs from the combine's copy on three edges** (a fixed read order, numbered labels past `(8)`, Unsorted folding only into Unsorted), each named in the migration's doc.
- **The parts on hand are reported, not enforced**: when two Unsorted piles become one, a count on one can reset a line the other fed. The plan says so and leaves the call to its reader.

## Box photos

The rows move. A photo still waiting in the bucket is filed by the sweep for the row's job, so it lands in the new job's folder with no change. A file already in Drive stays in the first job's photo folder; the row names its file by Drive id, so it still opens from its box. Moving those files is not small: a Drive move cannot be dry-run or unwound with the plan, it has an outcome per file, making the target's photo folder is itself a write, and `photo-save` takes only the cron secret today. The plan counts the files and says in words that they stay; the rest is [`to-dos/box-photo-files-follow-the-job.md`](../../to-dos/box-photo-files-follow-the-job.md) (#297).

## No office press

Jobs → Materials gets no press for this. It is a rare correction that re-homes every printed box of a job at once; a dev's plan then apply, with the owner's word, is the right weight. The map's gaps say so and say when to build it.
