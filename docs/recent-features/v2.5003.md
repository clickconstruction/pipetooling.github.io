# v2.5003 — GC projects: bill the customer for interest on late bills (2026-10-08)

Owner Billing's O6b-2 in the GC mode real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/OWNER_BILLING_REAL_BUILD.md` → PR 8, on branch `spike/gc-mode`): **Bill the interest**, the second of O6's three PRs after v2.5002 made interest readable and typable. One migration, [`20261009220000_gc_send_owner_interest_bill`](../migrations/20261009220000_gc_send_owner_interest_bill.md). Its SQL is the spike mockup's *O6b-2's SQL as built*, byte for byte. Guide: [`bill-the-customer-on-a-gc-job`](../../src/content/help/bill-the-customer-on-a-gc-job.md) → *Bill the interest*. The function needs redeploying.

- **The migration:**
  - `gc_send_owner_interest_bill` files the job's next interest bill today, to the cent, with its bill on the billing job, billed at noon Central. It refuses a job with no rate, a project with no billing job, and nothing to bill.
  - The billing job's revenue becomes the contract plus the interest billed (`gc_owner_billing_revenue`), the lead's call on 2026-10-08. O4a held it at the contract so a payment marks the job paid only once the whole contract is in. Paid interest beyond the contract would otherwise mark the job paid while a bill is open, and the portal drops bills on paid jobs.
  - O4a's `gc_send_owner_pay_app` and `gc_record_certificate` are restated word for word, with only their revenue reset calling the new helper. `gc_owner_contract_now` stays the contract the G702 reads.
- **The email:** `gc-customer-email` gains `interest_bill`, to the customer, with their portal line when they have a link. It files as `bill_gc_interest` under Bills.
  - The words are the prototype's, but for two: the bills *went past the day they were due* (some are still open, not all paid late), and there is no Pay, so they reply with their day.
- **The window** (`GcBillInterest.tsx`, under the interest terms):
  - once a dollar or more has built up, *$X of interest has built up and is not billed yet.*, the tick **Email the customer the bill now** (off to start) and **Bill the interest $X**, which offers `ownerInterest().toBill` and never more;
  - each interest bill's day, amount, paid or open, and *Emailed to … on …*, read from its sent copies.
- **The registry:** a journey step with a `gc-interest-bill` sample, the surface registry and the person journey's answer.
- **The SQL beds:**
  - local Postgres 15 with the real interest bills, money policy and blocks: 20 checks pass, with the migration applied twice;
  - on GitHub, `30_interest.sql` runs the full chain: the revenue at the contract, the interest bill raising it, the restated send and certificate keeping it, and the next number;
  - `20_scenario.sql`'s O4a readings run unchanged on the restated functions.
- **Tests:** `customerEmail.test.ts` (the kind's party, source, filed kind and portal line, and the words) and the window's render test (the bill with the tick off and on, and the list with its paid chip and emailed line).
