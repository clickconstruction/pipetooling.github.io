# v2.6296 — My email: a label you leave out stays out of an open conversation too (2026-11-01)

Punch list #230 (`to-dos/messages-my-email/README.md`): a leftover of the build's review, found by the lead after v2.6292. The edge function `gmail-mine` alone, no migration and no client change.

- **`open` showed what the list hides.** A person leaves Gmail labels out on Your account (`users.my_mail_hidden_labels`), and the guide promises *A label you leave out is never read either.* The list's search leaves them out by name, and since v2.6292 a tag and the follow skip a mail under one. But `open` read the mail's whole conversation and drew each of its other mails as a row, a mail under a hidden label among them, and opened a mail under one when asked by its id. Now `open` reads the person's hidden labels as Gmail's ids (`hiddenLabelIds`, once a call, as a tag does), answers 404 for a mail under one, and leaves such mail out of the conversation's rows and of `threadCount`.
- **The list holds it by id too.** Gmail's search reads `-label:"<name>"` its own way, and a label's name can reach it in a form it does not match. A mail under a hidden label that the search lets past is now never drawn, held by its id like a draft that reached the page.
- The new kernel `leftOutOfTab` (`src/lib/myMail/mineFunction.ts`, mirrored to `_shared/gcKernels/`) is `neverRead` plus the hidden ids. A person who leaves no label out costs no extra Gmail call. One who does costs one label read per list and per open, and Gmail refusing it stops that call with Google's reason, as it stops a tag.
- Tests: `mineFunction.test.ts` (`leftOutOfTab`), and in `gmailMine.run.test.ts` a list page whose search let a hidden mail past, an open whose conversation holds one and an open of one, and an open when Gmail will not list the labels. Putting `neverRead` back fails the first two.
- Deploy: `supabase functions deploy gmail-mine --no-verify-jwt` after the merge. The kernel copy's index is unchanged, so no other function drifts.
