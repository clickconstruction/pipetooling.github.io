# v2.6376 — Lien desk: a sent notice keeps the job's GC on its gates (2026-11-02)

Taunya's report through the owner: *GC is listed but under the lien desk it's red and says there's no GC assigned.* Job 890 · Dunmere Mallin, in the Sent · 30d pile (sent Oct 14), gate 2 red with **Set the GC ›**, while the job's Edit tab showed *RLC- Dunmere Mallin* with a phone, an email and an address. No migration, no function.

- **The cause.** `buildLienDeskQueue` (`src/lib/jobs/lienDesk.ts`) built an entry's `gcCustomerId`, `customerId`, balance, owner flag and property kind from the **first month row** `list_lien_notice_months` returned for the job. A job the RPC no longer lists — every notice window closed more than a week ago (890: last work set by hand in August, the notice sent in October, so its deadlines fell out of the look-ahead), or nothing billed, or the balance paid, or marked uncollectible — stays on the desk for thirty days through its **sent item alone**, with no month row to read. Its entry then had `gcCustomerId: null`, and the gate said *No GC on the job* about a job that has one. The `customerId` fell back to the item's **job id**, a wrong id in the customer's slot; nothing read it, so it did no harm, but it is gone.
- **The fix.** The kernel takes the jobs' own rows (`jobs?: Record<string, { customer_id, gc_customer_id }>`) and falls back to them when there is no month row; a month row still wins. `useLienDeskData` builds `jobsById` before the queue and passes it (the hook already fetched the GC rows for item-only jobs — `gcIds` took every job's `gc_customer_id` — so the name, address and standing rule were waiting); `useGcOnNoticeData` passes its `jobsById` the same way. `lienTimelineBook` is unchanged: it only draws jobs the RPCs list.
- **What the gate now shows on 890.** Gate 2 green, *RLC- Dunmere Mallin*, with the address under it and **Change the GC ›**; the Rules card reads the job as a subcontract (`isSub`), the standing-rule box, the GC's open total and the envelope line name the GC. Gates 4 and 5 still read *No month picked* and *Nothing billed* on such an entry: the entry has no months and no billed-open figure, since both are the RPC's. That is the next defect on the list, not this PR's.
- **Tests.** `lienDesk.test.ts` feeds 890's shape (no rows, a sent item, the job row) and expects the GC and customer from the job, the row to win when present, nulls without the jobs, and `buildLienDeskGates` to read the gate green; `useLienDeskData.zz.render.test.tsx` runs the hook over a sent-only job and expects its entry to carry the GC from `jobs_ledger`.
