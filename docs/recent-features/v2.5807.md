- **The kernel** ([`subsStages.ts`](../../src/lib/prospects/subsStages.ts), [tested](../../src/lib/prospects/subsStages.test.ts)): a stage is never stored. `subStage` reads it from what the lane records: a Didn't Answer or an Answered on record is *Called*, an Answered is *Talked*, `prospect_fit_status = 'converted'` with a company id is *On the bench*, and *Quoted* once that company has a quote in on any ask (`state.partners[].bids > 0`, read with the bench). `stageCounts` gives the strip's five counts and the two exits folded; `rowPress` says what a row offers (Call now → until talked, then Add to the bench ✓ beside it, nothing after); `groupSubsByTrade` groups the lane in `gcNeeds`' order (short of quotes, the bench, the book, then trades only a sub names), a two-trade sub in both groups, the picked project's trades first and lit; `plansChoices` lists the projects out for a bid (`gcBiddingLines`' rule) with the trades each buys out and the package behind each, for PR 4's ask; `callForTradeHref` is a group's door to the deck on `?trade=`, carrying `?for=`.
- **The page** ([`Prospects.tsx`](../../src/pages/Prospects.tsx)): `renderSubsStrip` draws the strip over Follow Up's needs panel and over the list (the deck's counts from every sub row, which the Subs loader now reads without the Not a fit filter; the list's from its own rows), `renderPlansPicker` the **For** select on `?for=` (dropped with `?trade=` when the lane changes), `renderSubsList` the lane by trade with the stage chip (`STAGE_TONE_STYLE`), the last touch, the presses and the folded landed and exit sections. `openBenchModal(p?)` takes the row, `benchDraft` carries the prospect with the draft, and `handleBenchSave` advances the deck only when the landed prospect is its card, else it patches the caches and keeps the index straight. The answered set (`answeredProspectIds`) rides both loaders' call rows. Activity's count reads the kind's `landedCount` (*to the bench* on Subs, *asked us* on Jobs to GC).
- **Trade partners** ([`GcTradePartners.tsx`](../../src/components/gc/GcTradePartners.tsx) `PartnerLine`, [`benchOrigin.ts`](../../src/lib/prospects/benchOrigin.ts), [`benchOriginIo.ts`](../../src/lib/prospects/benchOriginIo.ts)): `GcProjects` reads the subs that landed as the bench's companies and their call log once per board (fail-soft under `prospects`' policy) and passes `origins`; a company with no quote yet reads *from Prospects · Marisol, Thu Oct 29 · answered 1 of 2 calls* under its record chip.
