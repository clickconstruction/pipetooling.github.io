# v2.2505 — Line engine groundwork: doorway calibration + pipe tracing support (2026-08-30)

The pipe leg of the placement engine (follow-on to v2.2504's counters), built around the
owner's calibration rule: **doorways are the ruler** — stated scales lie on reduced
prints and can change per page; door openings are always 3 ft and every sheet has
several, so per-page calibration is measured, median'd, and self-verifying.

- **Kernel** (`src/lib/takeoffPlacement.ts`, 15 tests): `pixelsPerUnitFromSpan` +
  `calibrateFromDoors` (median of door samples, >10% outliers flagged by index — never
  averaged in); `PlacedLine` tracing support in `assembleTakeoff` (+ `doorSamples` in
  the manifest → per-page scale computed at assembly); validation parity with
  import-takeoff for lineTypes/polylines **plus a new refusal: polylines on an
  uncalibrated page are rejected by name** ("doorways are 3 ft" in the message);
  `feetByLineType` (takeoff-eval's denomination) and `marksFarFromLines` (connectivity:
  every fixture within ~6 ft of a run, point-to-segment in feet).
- **Assembler CLI** prints the calibration report per page, feet by line type, and the
  connectivity check alongside counts-vs-schedule.
- **PLACEMENT.md** gains the "Scale: doorways are the ruler" and "Lines v1" chapters
  (per-system polylines; sanitary from P200, water from P201, vent via riser
  reconciliation; trace-topology-at-medium-DPI, junction and RFI rules).
  ESTIMATOR_TWIN_PIPELINE_PLAN records the calibration standard as an owner rule.
- **Live on LIVSTE**: doorways measured on both plan sheets — P201 = 5.220 px/ft
  (2 samples), P200 = 5.200 px/ft (cross-check) — 0.4% agreement across pages,
  consistent with the ~80 ft footprint; the twin's M4 takeoff re-imported with the
  calibrated P201 scale (marks unchanged, replace-idempotent, review lane untouched).
- Next: legend extraction (P001 → line-type roster, hex-tag decode) into substrate
  v0.5, then mission M6 (trace LIVSTE's runs).
