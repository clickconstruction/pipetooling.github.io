# v2.7357 — The dev seat records a sub payment as the Record payment window does (2026-11-15)

Punch list #347, the second of the two *Not built* lines this session took. Taunya, 2026-11-15: "Build all four". The window has written a method since v2.7176 and a numbered sending since v2.7235. The seat's `plan_sub_labor_payment` still inserted one bare row and joined the method into the memo, so a payment recorded from the seat read blank under How paid, shared no sending and showed its method to the sub on the portal.

## What changes

- **The door.** `record` POSTs `record_sub_sending` as the dev under RLS, with one line and no credit, the call `recordSubSending` makes. One numbered `sub_payment_sendings` row, one `people_labor_job_payments` row tied to it.
- **The method.** `method` is one of the window's chips, by its stored key or its words in any case. It lands in `method`. Anything else is refused with the list, and the words for a bank transfer (*Mercury*) and for the rest (*Other*, with the word in `note`).
- **The reference.** `reference` lands in `method_ref`. It is refused with no method, and with a method whose chip opens no box for a number (Apple Pay, Cash, Client direct, Other), as the window drops one there.
- **The memo.** `note` alone. The method is no longer in the text the sub reads.
- **Who it is to.** The plan reads the sheet's assignee rows, their roster rows and those accounts' roles, or the names typed on the sheet when it has no assignee row. `sheetSentTo` rules as the Pay view does: one sub on the roster is the sending's person; a crew sheet, a shared sheet and a sub off the roster name nobody.
- **The read-back.** `records` holds the sending (to whom, why, what is sent, that its number is minted on apply), the payment row as it will land, and how How paid will read. Each payment already on the sheet shows `how_paid` and its `sending_id`.
- **Past the sheet is refused**, where it was a warning. The refusal gives the figure the sheet takes. A sheet with no priced line still takes a payment, as before.
- **The apply** reads the new row back and replies with it, its sending's id and number, and the sheet's balance.

## Code

- `supabase/functions/_shared/devMcpSubLabor.ts`: `SHEET_PAY_METHODS` and its labels (a test holds them to `SUB_PAY_METHODS`, `subPayMethodLabel` and `subPayMethodRefField`), `sheetPayMethodFrom`, `sheetSentTo`, `readSheetSentTo`, `sheetSendingArgs` (a test holds its keys to the catalog's arguments for the function), `sheetPayHowWords`. `SHEET_PAY_COLS` gained `method`, `method_ref`, `sending_id`.
- `supabase/functions/dev-mcp/index.ts`: the apply branch, the two tools' descriptions, server 0.90.0. One `restRows` fewer and one `restPost` more.
- No new verb, no wrapper RPC, no migration. The generated catalog already held the function and the columns, and regenerating it changed nothing.
