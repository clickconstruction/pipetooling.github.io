# v2.4431 — Every window stands above the Dispatch / Job mode dock (2026-10-02)

No migration, no edge function. A mechanical sweep: one codemod, 208 numbers in 152 files, merged alone.

## The problem

The dock ([`DispatchModeFooter.tsx`](../../src/components/dispatchMode/DispatchModeFooter.tsx)) is a fixed 60 px bar at the foot of the screen, at z 1000. A window whose backdrop stood lower was drawn under it, so when the window filled the screen's height — a short laptop window, a phone — its last 60 px, usually the Save row, was under the bar. [v2.4073](v2.4073.md) fixed one (the Sub Labor form, 50 → 1100). There were about 210 more at 10–121, three at 900, and some forty that tied with the dock at exactly 1000 and lost to it because they are drawn earlier in the page.

## Why not 1100

The ask was to lift each one to 1100. That would have put windows behind the windows they open:

- The Job form (1010), the Job window (1010) and the Bid window (1000) stand at 1000–1030 with about a hundred others, and they open **on top of** the low windows — a job from GC Review (60), a job from call mode (70), a bid from a bid list. A list window at 1100 hides every one of them. The Follow-Up deck made this exact mistake once: at 1040 the Job window opened invisibly underneath it (v2.1739).
- The low windows open over each other by their numbers (GC Review 60 → its dialogs 61–64 → 65, 66 → 70 → 80 → 90 → 95). At one flat number the order falls to where each happens to sit in the page.
- A `SearchableSelect` list (1100, on `body`) stays over a window at 1100, but not over a dialog that had to go to 1110 to stay above its parent.

## What changed

- **The dock moved down, the windows moved up under 1000.** `DISPATCH_MODE_FOOTER_Z_INDEX` is 700 (was 1000). Each window under it kept its old number on top of the dock's: 10 → 710, 60 → 760, 64 → 764, 121 → 821. Adding the same number to every one keeps every order there was — window over window, `overlayZIndex + 10`, a prop's default against its caller — and everything at 900 and from 1000 up still opens over all of them. Nothing between 700 and 899 existed before, so nothing else changed place. The windows at 900 and at 1000 needed no edit: they are above 700 now.
- **The codemod** ([`window-z-above-dock.mjs`](../../scripts/codemods/window-z-above-dock.mjs)) reads every `position: fixed` element with the TypeScript parser. A window is one that spans the screen's height with a background and holds something; a fixed layer drawn through `createPortal` counts too. It follows the z-index to the number it comes from — a literal, a constant here or imported (`DESK_Z`), a prop's default (`overlayZIndex = 60`), the base of `x + 10` — and raises that number once. It also raises a number passed as a z-index prop to a window component (`zIndex={110}`) and a fixed full-screen rule in a stylesheet (`.lienMonthDialogScrim`).
- **Left alone, by rule:** a see-through click-catcher behind a menu and a backdrop with nothing inside it (their menu or panel is the next element, one above — the pair moves together or not at all, and each one found sits inside a window); a fixed element that is not a window (a toolbar, the map's key).
- **Left alone, by a `window-z: allow — <why>` comment:** the Follow-Up deck (58) and a person's hours page on a phone (50) — page-level takeovers that leave their last rows clear for the dock and keep it on top; the sign-in picture behind `/task`; the one window on the public submittal room.
- **CI holds it** — `npm run check:window-z` (the codemod with `--check`) in [`ci.yml`](../../.github/workflows/ci.yml) and [`deploy.yml`](../../.github/workflows/deploy.yml). A window merged under the dock is now under every raised window too, so it fails its pull request. `--list` prints every window with the number it resolves to.
- Comments that named the old numbers say the new ones. [`docs/AI_CONTEXT.md`](../AI_CONTEXT.md) → Windows names the rule and the check.

## What a user sees

- In Dispatch Mode or Job Mode, every window opens over the bar; its bottom row can be reached.
- The windows that end above the bar on purpose (the Lien desk and its notices, the Person desk) look the same. The Follow-Up deck and the phone hours page keep the bar.
- The three widgets on the 900 shelf (the section dock, the pricing calculator, the estimate's accept bar) now sit above the bar's number. The first two already sit above it on the screen (`--app-bottom-chrome`); the third is on a page with no bar.
