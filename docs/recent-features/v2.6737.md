## Read closer than the card

- **Which company rate.** The Labor tab's company rate is the roster's crew rate (the last 90 days of wages at the burden factor, $29.01 on the walk); the block and the strip price the book and the typed hours at Settings → Line budgets' rate ($45), and the saved rate is that one, so the words name it and never call it the Labor tab's. The owner's fourth call on the card (*Two labor rates*) is exactly this; nothing here decides it.
- **The rate goes on the bid, not only on the job.** The card asked the block to read the rate the Labor tab reads. Reading it is not enough: `snapshot_job_budget_from_bid` recomputes the breakdown from the cost estimate, so a snapshot of a rate-less bid freezes `awarded_labor_usd = 0` and the score's labor kept reads negative. Saving the company rate on the bid first is what the owner did by hand on the walk (*save it on the bid made it usable at once*) and what the Labor tab's own press does; the alternative, a fallback rate argument on the snapshot function, is a migration and a second PR. The save touches only a null or zero rate, under the bid's own pricing policy (`can_access_bid_for_pricing`), so a bid the person cannot price refuses the award with its reason instead of freezing zero.
- **The sign-off reaches the board through the Scoreboard's read.** `list_project_board` carries the manual percents by day but not who set them, and `job_pct_events` carries the setter without the role; `superintendent_scores` already joins the two for the office's newest percent and counts the subs' sheet hours, so both surfaces read it rather than a migration adding columns to the board read. The read lists only awarded projects with a superintendent, so a project with no superintendent keeps *No office sign-off on the percent yet*, which is also what the score would say of it.
- **Slip 9 did not reproduce.** On the dev server as the dev, B398's Job block on the Edit Bid window (the Win/Loss row) now reads *J1113 opened from this bid · Open the job · Create another job*, since the job exists; *Create another job* runs the same `openNewJob({ prefillBidId })` the no-job *Open the job* runs, and it opened New Job over the Bid window (z 1010 over 1000) with the *A job already exists from this bid* confirm and then the *Start the job at $56,343.00?* question over both; Cancel on the confirm closed New Job alone and left the Bid window open, and Carry filled the form with the award block reading the bid. Nothing closed the Bid window. The walk's press was made on the deployed site in the owner's Chrome before J1113 existed; a bid with no job and the deployed build are the two things this check could not hold, so the slip stays on the card for the owner to show again, with the steps that work here beside it.
