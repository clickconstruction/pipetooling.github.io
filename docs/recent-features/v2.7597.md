# v2.7597 — Sub portal: a Statements view with their work statements, payments and statement of account (2026-11-17)

Punch list #371, step 2 of four: the switch and the list. Step 1 (v2.7569) made `sub-portal` hand over the `papers` block, live since 2026-11-17. This step draws it. The owner read the Spanish words before they shipped (the card's call 5).

## What changed

- **A switch under the head: Today and Statements** (call 1). `SubPortal.tsx` draws it only when the payload carries `papers`, so a page without the block is the page as it was. Today stays mounted while Statements shows, so nothing a sub typed is lost, and a link can open on Statements with `?view=statements`.
- **The view**, `src/components/subPortal/SubPortalStatementsView.tsx`, in the portal's own look. The head says *Still open* (call 6). Then the statement of account with **Save as PDF** and **Pick dates**, the work statements with open ones first, and the payments newest first with what went to each statement.
- **Everything** (call 3): every sheet that is the sub's alone and every payment, paid ones under open ones.
- **The rows and the papers**, `src/lib/subPortal/subPortalStatements.ts`, pure. They are built by the kernel the office draws from, and checked again (`subPapersAddUp`) before anything is listed.
- **The page never gives out a number** (call 2). A statement of account saved here has no number and its foot reads *Saved from your page* with the day and time. A work statement reads as the office's copy where today's figures are that copy's (`asIssued`). Where the sheet changed since, it keeps the number, drops the revision, and says it changed since the office's copy. A payment record carries its sending's own number.
- **The PDFs** are the office's, drawn in the sub's browser by the same builders. The draw helper moved to `src/lib/subWorkOrders/subPaperPdfDraw.ts`, and the office's window calls it too. They are in English, and the Spanish page says so.
- **A set that does not add up** shows no figure: *The office is checking your statements*, with the office's phone as a link.
- **Print** still prints the Today paper. The Statements view is screen only, so a list of buttons never prints.
- **The block is read defensively** (`subPortalPapersPayload.ts`). A block the page cannot read is no Statements view at all, never half a paper.
- **Both languages.** 42 new strings in `subPortalI18n.ts`, the dates and the payment method in Spanish too, and a *Your statements* entry in the sub's own guide sheet.
- **A shared sheet** stays on Today. The view says how many are left out.
- **Guide.** `see-what-a-sub-sees-on-their-portal` gains *Their statements*.

## Not in this step

- *Got it* and *Something is wrong* are step 3.
- An earlier copy of a changed statement is not offered. It needs a rule for a memo hidden after that copy was made.
- The sample page (*What customers see*) carries no papers, so it shows no switch.
