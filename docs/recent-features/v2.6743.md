# v2.6743 — Materials on site: the summary RPC for a job past 5,000 ledger rows (2026-11-08)

Punch list #209 (`to-dos/materials-on-site/README.md`), the fourth leftover. The on-site store reads a job's whole ledger and sums it on the device, capped at 5,000 rows (`MOVEMENTS_LIMIT`); past the cap every screen summed a part of the ledger and said nothing, and the oldest part at that, since the read was oldest first. One migration (`20261108221500_on_site_job_summary.sql`, `docs/migrations/20261108221500_on_site_job_summary.md`); no edge function. **Push right after the merge**: the client calls the function by name on a capped job, so `check:calls-ahead` holds the deploy until the push.

## What changed

- **`on_site_job_summary(p_job_id, p_since)`**, `LANGUAGE sql`, `SECURITY INVOKER`: the kernel's arithmetic over the whole ledger in one pass of window functions — each touch of a (container, part) a row, a count opening a new segment, a segment's total the count plus the changes after it, a line's on hand its newest segment's total, a count's expected the segment before — answering every line with its on hand, last touch, last count and Can't find presses since; the last count per container; the counts and Can't find presses since `p_since` (30 days); the returns. The caller's RLS decides what it sums.
- **`src/lib/materials/onSiteServerSummary.ts`** (4 tests) reads the answer into the kernel's own shapes — `serverLines` = `containerLines`, `serverCountDiffs` = `countDiffs`, `serverLastCountedAt` = `lastCountedAt`, `jobMaterialsSummaryFromServer` = `jobMaterialsSummary` — and the tests hold each pair equal over one ledger, so a screen reads the same thing whichever side did the arithmetic. The price lookup is the device's, as before (the invoice price lives there).
- **The store** (`onSiteStore.ts`) reads the ledger **newest first** and keeps the newest 5,000 rows, marks the job `capped` when the read came back full, and then calls the function (`serverSummary`, `jobLedgerCapped`); **`jobMaterialsSummaryFor(jobId, now)`** (`onSiteSummary.ts`) hands back the server's summary on a capped job and the device's otherwise, and the desk, the job's page, the Job window's tab, the ⋯ menu's count and the Needs you hook all read it now.
- **The job's page** says, on a capped job: *This job has more than 5,000 ledger rows. History and the boxes show the newest 5,000; the totals come from the database.*
- **Docs**: the migration page; `MATERIALS_ON_SITE_ARCHITECTURE.md` (The arithmetic, The writes, The gaps); the office guide; the card.

## Read closer than the card

- **The totals are the database's; the lists are still the device's.** A capped job's box lines, Missing table and History are drawn from the newest 5,000 rows; the counts that reset each line are in them, so a line counted since the cut reads right and a line never counted may not. Drawing the lists from `lines[]` too is the next step if a job ever gets there; the function already answers them.
- **No job is near the cap.** The yard job holds a few hundred rows. The first caller will be a test; the parity tests and the live check in the migration page's Verify stand in until then.
- **Not the agent's read.** `read_on_site` keeps its own paged read and cap and says so; wiring it to the function is a dev-mcp deploy for another day.
