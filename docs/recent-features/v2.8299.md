# v2.8299 — Lien desk: a retainage notice's facts are changed where they sit (2026-11-22)

Values in place, punch list #399, the Lien desk's second screen: `LienDeskRetainagePane.tsx`. The owner asked for it on 2026-11-22 with *Build the next screen*. No migration, no edge function, and no change to a day the desk works out.

## What the pane draws now

The gates section is one fact a line (`ValueLines`). Each gate's own words sit on the left, unchanged, and its value on the right.

| The line | Its value | The look |
|---|---|---|
| Owner of record | the owner's name | bold, worked out |
| Original contractor | the GC's name | bold, worked out |
| Our contract on the job | how it ended, and the day | underlined, changed in place |
| Retainage the GC holds | the dollars | underlined, or locked when a pay application is the source |
| The § 53.057 notice is due by | the desk's deadline | bold, red once it has passed |
| Retainage the notice claims | the dollars | bold |
| Still unpaid on this job | the dollars | bold |
| Payment bond on the project | yes, no or unknown | underlined, changed in place |

The chip in the heading, the window line, the source line and every footer draw their days and counts as values too. The words are the kernels' own: `retainageDeadlineValues` marks the chip's count or day, and `lienDayValues` marks the rest.

*Change ›* and *Set on the job ›* are gone from the pane. Both opened Edit Job for a value that was already on the screen. *Property record ›* stays on a missing owner, which is another record's fact.

## Changing a fact where it sits

The lien office (`isLienOffice`) gets the presses. Any other role reads every value bold.

- **The day our contract ended** asks first, because it moves the § 53.057 deadline. The open date box says under it which day the notice would be due. **Keep** opens a confirm window: *The day moves from Sep 3 to Sep 10. The § 53.057 retainage notice is then due by Oct 12. It was due by Oct 5.* Its press, *Move the day*, is the one write. Cancel writes nothing.
- **How it ended** is saved on Keep when one way of ending replaces another, since the day stands. *Still open* on an ended contract asks first, because the day comes off and the clock stops.
- **The retainage** is saved on Keep. The box holds the plain number, and the line under it says what the notice would claim. A 0 is kept and means the GC holds none. The box cannot be emptied here, because an empty figure takes the job off the Retainage list while the pane is open. Edit Job can still clear it.
- **A figure read from a pay application** has no press. It wears the dashed line and the tag *pay application*, and its tooltip says the AIA window is where it changes.
- **The payment bond** is a list of three and is saved on Keep.

A write the database refuses keeps the field open with what was typed, and the reason under it.

## Starting the clock on the line

A job whose contract is still open has two facts to say at once: how the contract ended and the day. The line asks for them in that order.

1. The value reads *still open*. Press it and pick Complete, Terminated or Abandoned. Keep writes nothing.
2. The day's box opens beside the pick, with the cursor in it. The line says *Not saved yet. Set the day.* The box's hint names the last approved clock day when the job has one. The day is never filled in from it.
3. Keep on the day opens *Start the 30-day clock?*, which names the day, how it ended and the day the notice is then due. *Start the clock* writes both facts.

The Clock not started footer's press, *Set the day our contract ended*, opens the first field on the line. It opened Edit Job before.

The shared piece took one prop for step 2: `startOpen` on `ChangeableValue` draws the field open when the value first draws.

## The write

`saveLienContractFacts` in `src/lib/jobs/lienRetainageFactsIo.ts` updates the job row, as Edit Job's *Our contract on this job* row does. The patches are in `lienRetainageFacts.ts`. The day's patch is the dev seat's `contractEndedPatch`, column for column, so it stamps who set the day, which Edit Job's row does not. A test holds the two patches equal.

After a save the pane asks the desk to read again (`onChanged`) and reads the job's retainage line again.

## Two calls made on the card's defaults

Neither was asked of the owner. Both follow what #399 already settled.

- **The confirm window is how the day asks first.** The last day of work has a window of its own that shows what moves. The retainage pane has none, so Keep opens the app's confirm window with the old and the new due day in it.
- **The footer's press opens the line's field.** The rule is no edit window for a value already on the screen, and *still open* is now a value on the screen.
