# v2.3987 — Contracts & terms: a history for the wording customers agree to — `contract_text_versions`, `contract_text_reviews` (2026-09-28)

Migration only; no client change, no edge function. Punch list **#50** ([`to-dos/contracts-and-terms`](../../to-dos/contracts-and-terms/README.md)), PR 5 of 7, after [v2.3977](v2.3977.md).

## Why

Five of the texts on Settings → Contracts & terms live in `app_settings`, which has `key`, `value_num` and `value_text` and no date. The Contract Book overwrites a document's wording in place. So the tab says *No date is kept* on five cards, and nothing can say what a text read before its last edit — or which terms were in force the day a customer accepted an estimate, since an acceptance keeps no copy of the terms page.

## What the history answers, once the tab reads it

- *When did this last change, and who changed it.*
- *What did it say before.*
- *Which terms were in force on a given day* — the newest row at or before that day. This is the record an estimate acceptance never kept.

## Decided here

- The history is append-only from the app: no write policy. A dev can still correct it in SQL.
- A failed history insert warns and lets the save through. The five keys sit in the same table as every other Setting; a history that could block a save was the wrong trade.
- Reviews are keyed by the catalog entry, not by the source, so wording fixed in the app (the consent, the signing sentences) can be reviewed too.
