# v2.3455 — Bill Customer: the owner-of-record line shows even when the customer row has no email (2026-09-15)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Found in the 2026-09-15 live pass of v2.3450.

## Why

v2.3450 mounted `BillCustomerOwnerLine` inside the Send-to block, and that block only renders when the job's customer has an email or saved contacts. A GC job whose customer row has no email (J608 at 628 Terrell Rd is one) opened Bill Customer with no Send-to block — and so no owner line, on exactly the jobs the line exists for.

## What shipped

- [`SendRecordInvoiceModal`](../../src/components/jobs/SendRecordInvoiceModal.tsx): the line moves out of the email-gated block and renders on its own under it, for both channels. Its own rule (`shouldShowBillCustomerOwnerLine`) is unchanged — GC or builder job, no confirmed owner, never a gate on sending.

## Verified

Render smokes for the modal and the line, typecheck, lint. Live on 2026-09-15: J775 (Nexgen, GC job with a customer email) showed *Owner of record for 169 Resort Ct: Arch Ray Development LLC (Gillespie Appraisal District 2025) — not yet on the job*; Use wrote the property record with provenance and the line read *on the job*.
