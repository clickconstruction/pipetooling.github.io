# v2.3526 — Price requests PR 2: each house card chooses "app emails the rep" or "I'll send it" (2026-09-16)

Client only. No migration, no edge function (the existing `send-rfq-email` is called exactly as the compose modal calls it). PR 2 of `to-dos/price-requests-loop/`; item 6 of the owner's ready-to-build train.

## Why

PR 1 (v2.3495) made **+ Add a request** take several houses in one pass, each card with its own day and quote link — but every card was a *record*: the estimator still emailed each house by hand. The plan's PR 2: the card carries a **how**, and the plus **asks** the houses.

## What shipped

- **The how, per card** — `app emails <rep>` or `I'll send it`, a two-way toggle on each house card in Edit Bid → Price requests. A house whose job-accounts rep has an address defaults to the app; a house with none starts as *I'll send it* and takes a typed address. The app card hides the day and link boxes (the send stamps its own day; the vendor's page is the link); the hand-sent card keeps them.
- **Preview the email** on an app card — `send-rfq-email` in `preview` mode, no writes: the subject and the exact text, in place.
- **Ask** — `planAskHouses` (kernel, +5 tests) validates the block: the hand-sent cards under the same rules as before, the app cards for a usable address, naming the house at fault. The press sends the app cards through `send-rfq-email` in `send` mode (the function mints the row, the token and the email per house) and records the hand-sent cards in one insert. Emails go **first**; a failed send records nothing, so the block is retried whole. The button says what the press will do: *Ask 3 houses*, *Ask 2 houses · 1 by email*, or *Add 2 requests* when nothing is app-sent.
- **The scope** — [`src/lib/bids/bidRfqScope.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/bidRfqScope.ts): the bid's **Base** count rows (`bids_count_rows` with no version) as `{ lines, text }`, the text from the same price-free builder the copy menu uses. A named version's counts still go out from Pricing, where the version is chosen; the guide says so. Edit Bid passes the bid's display name for the email's subject.
- Help guide *get supply house prices on a bid* → *Requests you sent yourself* gains the paragraph.

Left for the owner, as the to-do lists: the usual-houses pre-pick (last 10 bids in the trade, or a remembered per-user set), and whether a house already asked on the bid is unchecked or hidden.
