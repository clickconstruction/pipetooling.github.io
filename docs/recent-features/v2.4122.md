# v2.4122 — Procurement log: the printed sheet reads as a letter to the GC (2026-09-29)

The owner, on Print the log (2026-09-29): "when a user clicks this they are preparing to submit it to a vendor for acceptance. How can I make that page more client facing?" The vendor is the GC (confirmed the same day). Before this the sheet was the office table on paper: no letterhead or addressee, *update 3 · first update* on a plain print, our words in every cell (*Revise*, *(house)*, *order by*, *−14 d*), a staff-voice legend, nowhere for anyone to stand behind it — and the Notes column printed the change diff, so a plain print lost every note typed for the GC. The before/after drawing and its self-critique are in `to-dos/submittals/procurement-log-before-after.html`.

## The sheet — `buildProcurementUpdateHtml` in [`procurementLog.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/procurementLog.ts)

- `ProcurementUpdateInput` grows optional fields, so every old call still prints: `kind` (`print` = the log as it stands, no update number, nothing marked; `update` = numbered, *changes since &lt;date&gt; marked ●*), `letterhead` (`ProcurementSheetLetterhead`: the test report's company name, tagline, phone, mailing address, the logo as a data URL), `projectAddress`, `gcName`, `preparedBy`, `roomUrl` + `roomQrSvg`.
- The page: the company block (logo left, name/tagline/phone/address right, the submittal cover's shape); *Procurement log* or *Procurement log — update N*; a Project / To / Schedule you gave us / From grid; **What we need from you** (`procurementAsks`: rows awaiting approval or returned, each with `approveBy` = required − lead time as *needs your approval by Nov 3 to make Trim Set*, *now* once past; late rows as *the supplier says Oct 20, 14 days after Rough In starts*; the row's note appended; the estimator's typed line leads); the rows **grouped by stage** (`groupRowsByStage`, build order, *Rough In · needed on site Oct 6 · 3 items, 2 behind*, no-stage rows last); the cells in the GC's words (`gcSubmittalWord`: Approved / Returned for revision / Rejected / Awaiting your approval / Not yet submitted; `gcScheduleWord`: *delivered Sep 26* · *15 days ahead* · *14 days behind* · *on time* · *approve by Nov 10* · *needs approval now* · *we order by Oct 10*; Ordered as *Sep 21 · PO 48211* / *not yet*; Expected with *supplier's date*); Notes = the row's note, and on an update the changed row's dot plus *since Sep 21: …* under it; a legend in their voice; the room's code and link (*This log lives at …*); *Verified true and current by Wendi Aguilar, Click Plumbing and Electrical — signature / Date* — the owner's call: no acceptance signature from the GC, a signature and date from the person verifying it is real.
- Dates in words: `monthDay` (*Sep 5*), `longDate` (*September 28, 2026*), `stageDatesWords`.
- `procurementUpdateText` (the copied email) opens with the same *What we need from you* list; its row lines are unchanged.

## The panel and the tab

- [`procurementSheetAssets.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/procurementSheetAssets.ts): `loadPublicImageDataUrl` (the logo, fetched once per session — a print window cannot wait on an image) and `loadProcurementSheetAssets(roomUrl)` (the logo + the room's code via `payQrSvgMarkup`). [`SubmittalProcurementPanel.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/SubmittalProcurementPanel.tsx) loads them on mount so every print stays synchronous inside its click (Safari blocks a popup opened after an await); new optional props `letterhead`, `projectAddress`, `gcName`, `roomUrl`; one `letter` object feeds Print the log (`kind: 'print'`), Record, print and copy, and every **Open** under Updates sent.
- [`BidsSubmittalsTab.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/BidsSubmittalsTab.tsx) keeps the whole `TestReportSettings` row (was the company name alone) and passes the bid's address, the GC's name (`customers.name`, else `bids_gc_builders.name`) and `roomLink(origin, room.token)` when the room is minted.
