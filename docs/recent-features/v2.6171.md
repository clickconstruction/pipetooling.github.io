# v2.6171 — The Legal desk header reads the firm's name on a phone (2026-10-30)

Punch list #182's stacked layout, a follow-up seen live on the dev server on 2026-10-30: at 375 px the desk header's firm group showed only its quiet tail, *· no counties colored yet*, the firm's name gone entirely, and Emails and Portal link drawn over the tail. The desk-level firm segment only; the account's head and the venue line under it are punch list #219's.

- **The cause** (`LegalDesk.tsx`, the first button inside `data-legal-firm-group`): the name and the tail were two flex items of the segment. The name's span had `overflow: hidden`, which makes a flex item's automatic minimum width zero, while the tail was `white-space: nowrap` with its automatic minimum, its whole text. When the group wrapped onto its own line on a phone and squeezed the firm segment (the only segment that can shrink, `FIRM_SEG`'s `minWidth: 0`), the name gave up every pixel first and the tail kept all of its own; squeezed past that, the tail bled out of the button under Emails, since the button clips nothing. On the computer the same fight ran inside `FIRM_SEG`'s 220 px, where the name ellipsized to a few letters beside a whole tail.
- **The fix**: the name and the tail are one block (`data-legal-firm-words`: `overflow: hidden; text-overflow: ellipsis; white-space: nowrap; min-width: 0`), the tail an inline span inside it with a 6 px left margin where the flex gap was. The ellipsis eats from the end, so a tight segment shortens the tail first and the name only after it. Nothing bleeds: the block is the segment's only child that shrinks, and it clips.
- **The pin**: `LegalDesk.render.test.tsx` holds the name as the block's first child, the tail inside it, the three styles, and a tail with no `white-space` or `flex` of its own.
- **Not pinned in `e2e/viewport-smoke.spec.ts`**: it has no Legal desk entry, so the phone shape stays with the render test.

**Checked** in the browser pane on the dev server, signed in as the dev, at 375 px with RMC- Dudley Mason open (the shape reported): the group is 333 px, the firm segment 151, Emails 80, Portal link 100, the three abutting with no overlap. The words block is 112 px holding 275 px of text, so it reads *Snell Law Firm, …* and the tail is clipped whole, its box starting 19 px past the block's right edge. The list view, no account open, carries no tail and reads the same name.
At 1,280, the columns layout: the firm segment sits at `FIRM_SEG`'s 220 px with Emails and Portal link abutting it, the words block 181 px holding the same 275 px of text, so it reads *Snell Law Firm, PLLC · no co…*, the whole name and 50 px of the tail. Before the fix the same block gave the tail its 144 px and the name the 31 px left.

No migration, no edge function.
