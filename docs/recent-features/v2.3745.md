# v2.3745 — Put a GC on notice: the cover letter is now counsel's (2026-09-22)

Counsel's memo of 2026-09-22 answered all eight questions of the *Put a GC on notice* brief (punch list #16; the memo is kept at [`to-dos/gc-failure-playbook/counsel-memo-2026-09-22.md`](../../to-dos/gc-failure-playbook/counsel-memo-2026-09-22.md)). This PR builds what the memo asks of the letter and the notice; what it asks beyond them is punch list #33. Client only, no migration.

## What the memo changed

- **The letters** ([`gcOnNotice.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/gcOnNotice.ts) `defaultGcNoticeCoverLetter`): counsel's three, by property — commercial (the default), residential, homestead — and the **unresponsive** letter for a GC that is not answering. `coverLetterKindFor(property)` picks by the saved record (homestead → residential → commercial). The persuasion rules the memo set: owner-protective frame; **one number** (`{{amount}}`, the claim on the form); **one real deadline** (*before the next payment to the GC*); **three doors, none a joint check** (the GC pays us, payable only to us · the owner withholds and calls · the owner pays us only with the GC's written authorization); the release the day funds clear; the master plumber signs and takes the call. Struck, as counsel ordered: *pay us directly and deduct it* and *we have reason to believe it is not paying its subcontractors generally*. Each list item is its own paragraph so it prints on its own line.
- **New fills** (`COVER_LETTER_FILLS`): `{{amount}}` (the form's Claim amount as money), `{{stale_note}}` (blank or one sentence), `{{contact}}` (the signer), `{{phone}}` (the letterhead's phone — the signer's own number is #33), `{{affidavit_month}}` (*fourth* on commercial work, *third* on residential, `affidavitMonthWord`). `fillCoverLetter` resolves them and tidies the gap a blank stale note leaves. The run, the Lien desk and the read-before-you-approve preview all pass them ([`lienDeskRun.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDeskRun.ts), [`LienDeskModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskModal.tsx), [`gcNoticePreview.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/gcNoticePreview.ts)); the stale note is kept on the item (`LienDeskDraftFields.staleNote`).
- **The form claims the timely months only** (answer 6): `timelyClaim(months, claim, split)` — a month the office priced by hand keeps its figure, else the claim is spread by approved hours (by count when no hours are known); the stale remainder goes to `staleNoteWords` and the letter's footnote, never the form. `approveAll` writes the timely figure and the timely months on the item, so `months_covered` on the filing names only what the notice claims. A job whose every window has closed reads `no_months` and is left out (`readinessOf`). Step 2's column reads *Window closed · letter only*.
- **Step 3** ([`GcOnNoticeModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/GcOnNoticeModal.tsx)): a tab per kind present in the run, the unresponsive tick (*<GC> is not answering — send the unresponsive letter to every owner*), the side panel lists every fill, and the *Attorney wording pending* callout is replaced by *Counsel's wording · 2026-09-22*. The preview takes `letterFor(job)` so each notice previews with its own letter.
- **§ 53.254(g), the statute's words** ([`lienFilingDocuments.ts`](../../src/lib/jobsDocuments/lienFilingDocuments.ts)): v2.3744 had printed counsel's paraphrase. Read against the Code (Tex. Prop. Code § 53.254(g), as amended by HB 2237): *completion of your contractor's work* (not *of construction*), *reserve* (not *retain*), and a closing paragraph the paraphrase left out — what compliance protects, and that the owner is not liable for amounts paid before notice except the 10 percent. All three fixed; both copies print it.

## One thing the memo asked that the Code does not

The memo says the § 53.056 form is *not usable until the statutory WARNING block is restored*. The current § 53.056(a-2) form (read 2026-09-22 from the Code as amended by HB 2237, eff. 2022-01-01) has no warning block — it runs *Date · Project description and/or address · Claimant's name · Type of labor or materials provided · Original contractor's name · Party with whom claimant contracted if different · Claim amount · contact person · address*, which is what the app prints. The warning lines counsel remembers were in the pre-2022 forms. Nothing was added; the question goes back to counsel with the statute text ([`owner-decisions-pending.md`](../../to-dos/owner-decisions-pending.md)).
