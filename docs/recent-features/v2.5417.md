# v2.5417 — Messages: the rules behind handing a job seeker’s text to the hiring board, behind the scenes (2026-10-12)

Residential Messages R8 · 11 (`to-dos/gc-mode/messages/RESIDENTIAL_BUILD_PLAN.md` on `spike/gc-mode`, card 11 of `customer-messages-integrations-mockups.html` v0.2): the kernel for one more answer in **Who is this?**, *a candidate on the hiring board*. It is a new file, `src/lib/hiring/candidateHandoff.ts`, with its test. It is built on v2.5390 (R0, the `candidate` kind, the `team_prospects` record and `OFF_FOLLOW_UP`). No screen and no schema. The Prospects page, `contacts/kernel.ts` and `followUp/queue.ts` are untouched.

- **The press** (`candidateFromWhoIsThis(message, ctx)`) returns its two writes, in order:
  - **The card**: the `team_prospects` row the board's own **Add candidate** would insert (`saveNewCandidate`). It has the name, the number the text came from, the text's first line as the notes, the source *text to the office*, status `active`, Unsorted (`role_id` null) at the next rank there (`nextTeamProspectRank`), and no links. Email, trade, the ratings and `last_contact` stay empty. `last_contact` is the office's own Contacted press, not their text.
  - **The name**: what the text gives after *this is*, *my name is*, *I’m* or *it’s* (`nameInText`, capitalised words only, so "I’m interested" names no one). Otherwise it is the number, as an unknown contact's name already is. `name` is required on the board.
  - **The re-point**: the spine's `nameContact` with `{ table: 'team_prospects', id }`. The caller mints the card's id so the plan can name it before the insert returns, then calls `name_contact`. `afterCandidatePress` is `applyNamePlan`: the same contact, now a candidate, keeps every line.
- **The board's side** (`handoffToBoard(contact, messages, now)`): the hiring board keeps no thread table, and nothing on main stores a candidate's texts. So nothing moves or is copied. The card finds the lines by its row and opens the thread window at the newest line (`?thread=<contact>&at=<line>`), as any thread opens. The card's line is *texted the office Tue 7:30 AM* within the week, or *texted the office Tue Oct 6* before it. Messages keeps the contact as a candidate, which Follow up never shows or counts (`OFF_FOLLOW_UP`, v2.5390). Card 11's "Messages keeps no row for him" was read as no row on Follow up, the brief's call.
- **The words** (`candidateOption`, `candidateWords`): the answer's label, *Marco Ruiz, a candidate on the hiring board*, or *A candidate on the hiring board* with no name. Its small print is *Makes a card in Prospects, Hiring. The text goes with the card and stays off Follow up.* The toast after the press is in `candidateWords` too. All of them pass `plainWordsFailures`.
- **Not yet**: the `docs/GLOSSARY.md` clause waits until the *Messages (GC mode, the build)* line is quiet. Three open PRs edit that one-line paragraph, this PR's base among them.
