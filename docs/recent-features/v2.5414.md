# v2.5414 — Price requests: a supply house’s email finds the request it answers, behind the scenes (2026-10-12)

Residential customers in Messages, R8 · 10 (supply houses on bids@), lifted from the Messages integrations mockups v0.2, card 10 (`to-dos/gc-mode/messages/customer-messages-integrations-mockups.html` on branch `spike/gc-mode`; the plan is `RESIDENTIAL_BUILD_PLAN.md` → *R8* beside it). A pure kernel with its test. No screen, no migration, no edge function; the RFQ functions, `contacts/kernel.ts` and `followUp/queue.ts` are untouched. Before this nothing matched a house's reply: it was pasted into the Plug-in window by hand (`parseVendorReply`).

- **`src/lib/rfq/messageToRfq.ts`:**
  - `rfqForMessage(message, sender, rfqs)`: an email in from a supply house lands on one price request, strongest first. First the request's token, from the `/q/<token>` link our email carried and the reply quoted back (`bid_rfqs.token`, 32 hex characters). Then the bid's `bid_number`, which our subject carries (*Price request · 2210 · …*), on a request to the sender's house, read in the subject and then the body, a whole number only, so *$2210*, *22100* or *2210.5* is never bid 2210. Last, the house's one open request when the email names none. A token or a number lands on `sent` or `quoted` (a revised price after the page answer); the fallback reads `sent` alone; a draft or a closed request never takes a line.
  - A token from another house's request is refused with the reason (*The link is Ferguson’s, the email is from Winsupply.*), since a forwarded link is how a wrong price lands on the wrong house. Two requests named, two open and none named, none open, a text, our own email or a sender who is not a supply house each give no request and a plain reason, and the line stays on the house's thread.
  - The sender is typed by shape (`name`, `company`, `record`): a supply house is a contact whose record is its `supply_houses` row (helper 2's #5565), and a second rep at the house is another contact on the same record, so it matches the same way. `OFF_FOLLOW_UP` stays #5565's, in `followUp/queue.ts`; this kernel needs none.
  - `rfqLineFacts(message, rfq, counts)`: the small print under the email on the request page, one fact each: *answered RFQ 2210*, *pricing attached*, *14 lines*. The counts come from the caller (cut 6's files or a parse of them). With none, the email's subject stands in for them.
- **Tests:** `messageToRfq.test.ts` over Ferguson's reply to RFQ 2210 (made-up rows): by the number in the subject, by a second rep, by the token before a number, a forwarded token from Winsupply refused, the one open request, two open and none named, the body and the whole-number rule, a quoted request taking a number and a closed one not, two named, the plain lines, and every reason and fact through `plainWordsFailures`. Two breaks on purpose each failed a test: the token's house check and the whole-number rule.
