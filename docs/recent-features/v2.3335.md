# v2.3335 — The Bridge: Truth check — paper profit vs the net position change, the gap split exactly (2026-09-11)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Dev-only page (`/bridge`).

## Why

The owner asked what the company should maximize and how the app should show the way there. The Bridge already carries two readings of the same 8 weeks — a daily profit rate (accrual: earned − job costs − overhead) and the net position line (cash + AR − AP, rebuilt from dated flows) — and on 2026-09-11 they disagreed by ~$197k: $194k profit on paper, net position down $3.4k. Nobody can steer by either number until the page says why they differ. This PR makes the disagreement a first-class reading with an exact decomposition, so the profit rate earns trust before anyone is judged by it.

## What shipped

- **Kernel [`src/lib/bridge/truthCheck.ts`](../../src/lib/bridge/truthCheck.ts)** (+7 tests). `buildTruthCheck` sums both sides over the chart's moving days (after `windowStart` through today — the exact days `buildNetPositionHistory` walks; the first test asserts `net.deltaUsd` equals the chart's own first-to-last difference). With `netCosts = payments received − bank net flow + supply dated − supply paid`, the net change is `invoices sent − netCosts`, and the gap splits **exactly**:
  - `billingLagUsd = earned − invoices sent` — earned but not invoiced; legitimate, and the company's biggest lever.
  - `costGapUsd = netCosts − paper costs` — costs the paper doesn't see; positive when the bank charged more than the paper counted. Money in that isn't a customer payment (a loan, an owner deposit) pushes it negative — stated on the panel.
  - `costGapShare` = |cost gap| ÷ paper costs. **Verdict**: `thin` (paper costs under $5k), `costs_disagree` (share over 15%), `billing_lag` (lag over a quarter of paper profit), else `agree`. `truthCheckVerdictText` renders the one sentence.
  - **Signals** (sized where the loader can, counts otherwise, never claimed to sum): hours awaiting approval × the window's average field wage, unsorted bank transfers, unlinked card purchases, earned $ resting on the assumed-half-done jobs, worked jobs with no contract price.
- **Page**: `TruthCheckPanel` in [`Bridge.tsx`](../../src/pages/Bridge.tsx) sits between Net position and Cash — profit on paper, net position moved, the gap's two exact halves with the share, the signal rows with sized effects, and a footnote spelling the net-side identity. Reads from flows only, so it renders before cash on hand is typed.
- Help guide `the-bridge.md`: new **Truth check** section.
