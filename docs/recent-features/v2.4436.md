# v2.4436 — Submittals: buy a fixture without sending it to the GC (2026-10-02)

PR 2 of the train that answers Wendi's "some items do not need to be on submittals" (PR 1: [v2.4435](v2.4435.md), the flag and the GC-side filters). Mock-up: https://claude.ai/artifact/VHfgwjigciY5BjkGKmtYiv. No migration; no edge function. **Merge only after `20261002193129` is pushed and the three edge functions of v2.4435 are deployed**: this is the first screen that sets `order_only`.

## What changed

- **The × asks.** On a draft row the × opens [`SubmittalTakeOffDialog`](../../src/components/bids/SubmittalTakeOffDialog.tsx): *The GC will not see it. Do you still buy it?* **Order only** sets `bid_submittal_items.order_only` and remembers it on the bid (`writeRowOrderOnly` in [`orderOnlyIo.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/orderOnlyIo.ts): the choice row reads `ticked = true, order_only = true`). **Left out** is what the × did before: the row is deleted and the fixture is unticked.
- **A fixture already ordered cannot be left out.** Deleting the row takes its lines off the log, and before this an order typed on them went with it, unseen. The window reads the log first (`loadRowOrderFacts`, by the row's tag) and, when a line holds an order date, a delivery or a PO, greys *Left out* and says *Ordered 09/23, on site 09/29. It cannot be left out.* (`boughtWords`). If the log cannot be read the row is treated as bought.
- **Step 3's Order only group** ([`SubmittalOrderOnlyRows`](../../src/components/bids/SubmittalOrderOnlyRows.tsx)): under the rows the GC sees, each order-only fixture with every part it buys and its houses, *Put on the submittal*, *Edit* and a × that leaves it out after one question. The rows above it are `gcItems`, so the tiles, *N still need a cut sheet* and the package never count the group.
- **Its Edit window** asks nothing the GC would read: no status, reason, note, cut sheet or call (`orderOnly` on `SubmittalItemEditDialog`). The parts keep their house, lead time, stage and quantity; each reads *order only*, with its fixture (`fixtureOrderOnly` on `SubmittalPartsEditor`). The parts' own *GC sees it* picks are kept underneath and come back with the row.
- **The procurement log.** `procurementItemsFrom` marks every line of an order-only row `orderOnly` and `noGc` and reads no call for it. `rowFrom` gives a `noGc` line the status `released` with no `releasedOn`, so To order lists it under *Order now · <house>* and `lineStatus` reads *Ready to order · no GC approval needed* (or *Order by 09/22* once it has a stage date and a lead time). `gcProcurementRows` already drops `orderOnly` lines, so the update, the print and the snapshot for the GC leave it out. `procurementCounts.released` still counts the GC's releases only. `logIsDraft` ignores these lines; the draft note adds one sentence when there are any. By tag says *order only, no GC approval* in the fixture's heading.

## Decided here

- **Order only waits for nobody**, on a draft too (the owner's call on the mock-up). An order-only *part* of a fixture the GC sees still waits for its fixture, as before.
- **The flag does not touch the parts.** Putting a fixture back restores the mix of parts the GC saw.
- **Draft only.** A revision the GC has read keeps its rows, as it keeps them from the × today.
