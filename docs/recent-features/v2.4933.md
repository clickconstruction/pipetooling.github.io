# v2.4933 — GC mode: the money on our GC jobs, for a dev (2026-10-08)

Owner Billing's O6a in the GC mode real build. The plan is `to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/owner-billing-o6-money.md` on branch `spike/gc-mode`, approved 2026-10-08. It is O6's read-only half, so the money roles have a screen to test before our bill exists. It is read only: no migration, no function.

- **Money**, a fifth pill on the `/gc` dev switch beside Project Board, Trade partners, Follow up and Trade portals. It shows every job that is ours (buying out or building). It is dev only until the Owner Billing door opens it to the money team (`canSeeGcMoney`), after door 2.
- **`src/components/gc/GcMoney.tsx`** ports the prototype's Money tab read only (`GcOwnerBillingMoney` and its pieces). Its sections:
  - **The top line:** `allJobsMoney().totals`. With nothing paid in or out yet it says so, and adds that paid in counts once customers pay our bills in the app and paid out once the trades draw.
  - **Who owes us:** `allJobsMoney().owed`, late first, waiting on the architect last.
  - **Bill day:** `billDay`. It says that until the trades report their work, a bill carries the signed change orders at their percent done.
  - **The next six weeks** (`GcMoneyAhead.tsx`, `cashAhead`): it counts the work reported so far, since the schedule's bars are not read on real data yet. The trades' draws join once they ask in the app.
  - **Each job:** its price, billed so far, what the customer owes and what waits on the architect, flagged when the contract is not signed. Each job is a card on a phone.
  - **What each job makes us** (`GcMoneyMargin.tsx`, `allJobsMargin`).
  - **Coming later,** one line each with no number: the month-by-month forecast (waits on the schedule), and interest and the late finish (wait on our bill, O4a and O6b).
- **`src/lib/gc/billCustomer.ts`** lays billing over the board's projects:
  - `billingStateForAll` (and `billingStateFor`, one job) adds the signed price by line, the job's retainage and step, the property's owner and O5a's record.
  - Each laid job reads its own copy of its customer (`jobCustomerId`), so two jobs of one customer can hold different retainage (decision 6).
  - The customer's usual days to pay come from the app's own pay speeds (`get_billed_customer_pay_speeds`, their own median, none when they never paid us). They never come from `owner_pay_days`, which is when a bill falls due (decision 7).
- **`gcIo.ts`** gains `loadGcBillingRows(projectIds)`: the terms, the prices as signed, O5a's record, the property owners' names and the pay speeds.
- **Docs:** the guide `see-the-money-on-our-gc-jobs` (dev), and `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`'s `/gc` paragraph.
- **Tests:**
  - `billCustomer.test.ts`: two jobs of one customer with their own retainage, the pay speeds, the jobs not laid left as they were, and `allJobsMoney` over the laid state;
  - `GcMoney.render.test.tsx`: the sections, Bill the customer only when its window exists, and the nothing-yet words.

  - the price reads $100 with "contract not signed";
