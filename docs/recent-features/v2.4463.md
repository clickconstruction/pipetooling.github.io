# v2.4463 — Deposits: a check posted or bounced in the evening keeps its day (2026-10-02)

One of the instant-day fixes that follow [v2.4452](v2.4452.md); [v2.4455](v2.4455.md) fixed the same reading in Find a check. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

Four readers cut a day out of a `timestamptz` by its first ten characters, so a deposit posted, or a check that came back, after 7 pm CDT (6 pm CST) read as the next day:

- **A payment's bank line** ([`useMercuryDepositFacts.ts`](../../src/hooks/useMercuryDepositFacts.ts)): `postedYmd` from `mercury_transactions.posted_at` is *the bank posted it on Oct 3* in the payment's menu on the job window, and **Use the bank date as the check date** writes it to `jobs_ledger_payments.sent_on`, a `date`. An evening deposit would have stored the next day.
- **A returned payment** ([`bankReturnedDeposits.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/bankReturnedDeposits.ts), `summarizeBankReturnedPayments`): each item's `postedYmd`.
- **A payer's returned checks** ([`arReturnedCheckPayers.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/arReturnedCheckPayers.ts), `arReturnsByPayer`): the latest day from `came_back_at`, a `timestamptz` that `list_ar_returned_check_payers` returns whole. The Billed row's pay history and the Bill tab say *2 checks came back · Nov* from it, so a check back on the evening of Oct 31 read as November.
- **The new check on a bounced check's case** ([`ArReturnCasePane.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/ar/ArReturnCasePane.tsx)): *This looks like the new check. … · Oct 3* from the replacement deposit's `posted_at`.

Banking, the AR deposit row, Bank payments, the case's own story (`arReturnCase.ts`) and the server's bank-return notice already read `posted_at` in `APP_CALENDAR_TZ`.

## What changed

Each reads `calendarYmdInAppTzFromIso(iso)` from [`dateUtils.ts`](../../src/utils/dateUtils.ts). Mercury stores its own `postedAt` instant, and a manual import stamps `<date>T12:00:00Z`, so every writer lands on the right Central day.

## Not changed

`import-manual-transactions` keys its de-dup on `posted_at`'s first ten characters. It compares only manual rows on a manual account, which the function itself stamps at noon UTC, so the slice is the day as typed.

## Tests

- `bankReturnedDeposits.test.ts` (+1) and `arReturnedCheckPayers.test.ts` (+1): `2026-10-03T00:30:00+00:00` (7:30 pm CDT) reads Oct 2; a check back at 7:30 pm CDT on Oct 31 reads Oct 31 and *· Oct*; `2026-12-02T00:30:00Z` (6:30 pm CST) reads Dec 1; noon UTC reads its own day.
- New `useMercuryDepositFacts.render.test.tsx`: the hook reads the same days, and none for a deposit not yet posted.
- New `ArReturnCasePane.render.test.tsx`: the new check posted at 7:30 pm CDT on Oct 2 reads *· Oct 2.*

All four fail on main.

## Checked against prod

Read only, from the worktree dev server: none of the 157 bank-linked payments' deposits posted in a Central evening, and none of the 6 returned-check rows came back in one. Nothing reads differently today; the fix is for the next one.
