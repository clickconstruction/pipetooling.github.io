# v2.7132 — My email: See the record opens on the record (2026-11-13)

Two flaws in the My email work of 2026-11-12, both found on the first walk of the live site after the train merged.

## The door landed above the record

v2.7068 tells a mailbox's owner when a developer saves their mail to a job. The Needs you line has one press, **See the record**, which goes to `/settings?tab=settings-recent-push#settings-recent-push`: the Activity logs tab, and the card **Most recent notifications** on it.

The tab opened. The page did not move. `resolveSettingsDeepLink` read the hash as a tab's name, because the card carries the same id as its tab, and a tab's name is no anchor to scroll to. For most roles that cost nothing: the card is the first thing on the tab. For an owner or a dev, **Most recent emails sent** sits above it with 25 rows, and on the live site the record began 1,101 px down a 768 px window.

The fix is one rule in the resolver: when the hash repeats the tab the link names (`?tab=X#X`), the link means the card of that name, so it is the anchor too and the page lands on it. A hash that names a different tab than `?tab=` still loses to `?tab=`, as before.

## The guide said one thing twice

*Save an email to a job from your own mail* held the section on the owner's notice twice: **When a developer saves your mail for you**, and under it **When a developer tags your mail for you**, word for word but for the old verb. v2.7068 wrote the section and v2.7073 renamed it on another branch, and bringing `main` into the rename kept both. The test that refuses a heading written twice passed, because the two headings differed by the one word. The stale copy is removed.

## Tests

- `settingsDeepLink.test.ts`: `?tab=settings-recent-push#settings-recent-push` answers the tab and the anchor, and the same tab with no hash answers no anchor.
- `dashboardNeedsYou.mailSaved.test.ts`: the notice's own door resolves to an anchor, and the card still carries the id the door names.
