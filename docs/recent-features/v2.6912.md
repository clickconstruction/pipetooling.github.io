# v2.6912 — Permits: the owner of record in Austin, San Marcos, New Braunfels and Seguin (2026-11-10)

Punch list #292. Until now only Bexar County's parcel record was read; the four newer cities' cards said the county's record "is not read here yet" and opened the appraisal district's search page.

- **The layers** (`supabase/functions/_shared/permitParcels.ts`, pure): Travis County republishes the appraisal district's parcels on `gis.traviscountytx.gov`; Hays, Comal and Guadalupe each have a hosted layer from one vendor with one set of columns. All are public, need no key, and were asked live by address and by point before this shipped.
- **The match**: none keeps the address as one string. `streetParts` splits a permit's address into house number, direction and street name (suffix and unit dropped; "100 WEST AVE" stays West Avenue). Each `ParcelSource.where` asks for the number and the name every way the county spells it: Travis keeps a numbered street as its bare number, Comal and Guadalupe spell it out, Hays sometimes carries the direction inside the name, Guadalupe the suffix. `pickParcel` then holds the answer to the street asked for (never "MAINE" for "MAIN"), prefers the asked side of town and a row with an owner.
- **The point**: new construction often has no parcel under its street address. When the address finds nothing and the permit has a point, `pointQuery` asks for the parcel under it and the facts carry `matched_by: 'point'`. The cards say so (`parcelPointWords`) and name the county's own address for the parcel.
- **The function** (`permit-lookup`): the parcel branch takes the county from `permitCity(city)`; Bexar's path is unchanged. The body takes `lat` and `lng`. Google Places leans toward the permit's city, not always San Antonio.
- **The cards**: `PERMIT_CITIES[].parcels` is true for all five. `lotWords` moved to `permitCityDoors.ts` and says "buildings on the roll at $1.6M" where a county gives no built area. The building's sheet leaves off the DBA, the use, the year and the built area when the county's layer never had them (`countyGivesBuildingFacts`).
- **Detail links**: Travis's own `hyperlink`; Hays `…/Property-Detail/PropertyQuickRefID/R{id}`; Comal and Guadalupe `…/Property/View/{id}`. Each was opened once to confirm.
- Tests: `permitParcels.test.ts` (the split, each county's question, the pick, the facts, the point), `permitCityDoors.test.ts`, `permitCities.test.ts`, `ProspectsResearchTab.render.test.tsx` (Austin's card reads Travis with the point).

Deploy: `permit-lookup` after the merge. No migration.

Not built: the year built and the built area for Hays, Comal and Guadalupe (their layers do not carry them).
