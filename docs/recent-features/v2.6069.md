## What changed

- **The kernel** `supabase/functions/_shared/hoursStatement.ts`, with no imports, re-exported by `src/lib/people/hoursStatement.ts` (as `whereFilesGo.ts` is by `documents/folderName.ts`), so the window and the dev MCP read one implementation. `buildHoursStatement(input)` takes one worker's rows on one job for a period (a punch's day, in, out, hours and note; a sub's sheet lines with their own rate and amount, grouped by sheet; a salaried person's schedule rows), the pay reports that touch those weeks (`HoursStatementPayGroup`: the week's net, its payments with day, amount, memo, source kind and id), the rate on each pay report day (`dayRates`) with the current wage as the fallback, the first day the app recorded anything, and the two switches. It answers the groups by company week (Sunday first) or by sheet, each day's rate and amount, the totals under *Recorded in the app, this job* (*the Office job* when it is, *On the sheets* for a sub, *On the schedule* for a salaried person), one pay line per report (*Week of May 31 · $756.00 earned on this job of $864.00 for the week · paid in full $864.00 on June 12, 2026 · Cash App #D-7K2M4Q*; *not yet paid*; *paid $400.00 of $912.00: …*; a week with hours and no report at all named as such), the gap (`hoursStatementGap`: the period's start to the day before the first recorded day) with its heading and the company's words, the stated block, and the attestation (`hoursStatementAttestation`) that changes with the kind, the pay switch and whether every week read paid. A pending session is named under its day and never counted. A payment's reference is its memo, else its source's words (`Cash App #<id>`, `bank transfer`, `Apple Pay`, `paid by the customer directly`). Tested in `src/lib/people/hoursStatement.test.ts` on a helper's clock rows, the pay switch off, a pending session, an unpaid and a part-paid week, a week with no report, the current-wage fallback, a span with no rows, a sub's sheets, a salaried person and the office job.
- **The print** `buildHoursStatementHtml` in `src/lib/peopleDocuments/buildHoursStatementHtml.ts`, beside the pay report's builder: one Letter page in the Costs packet's manner (`<html data-theme="light">`, `@page { size: letter; margin: 0.5in 0.5in 0.6in }`, the stamp and the page numbers in the margin boxes), the company header, the record id (*draft, not yet filed* until PR 2 files one), prepared by and as-of, the worker and their standing, the job's number, name, customer and site address, the period, the approver, the table by week and day, the stated block, the pay lines, the attestation and two signature lines. **No column says how a row was recorded** (a punch, a typed day, a quick add, a sheet line): the owner took it off the paper on 2026-10-29; the app keeps that and the window will show it. The office job needs nothing of its own: it is a job.

## Decisions

- **A payment proves the week, never the job alone.** Pay reports are per person per week and carry no job, so the paper's pay line gives the job's share (the hours above at the rate on the report) beside the week's net and what was paid, and one plain line says a week's pay covers every job that week. A sub's payment is the sheet's, so the job's alone.
- **The paper never claims a payment it does not have.** An unpaid week prints *not yet paid*, a part payment prints *paid X of Y*, a week with no report *no pay report for this week yet*, and the attestation keeps *paid as shown* only when every line read paid.
- **The span before the app is the worker's declaration, marked.** Nothing in any hours table precedes 2026-03-12. The gap's heading names the span and the words say the company kept no clock before that day and that the rows are as the worker states them; the stated block appears for a gap, or on the switch inside the recorded span under *Days the worker adds*.
- **The kernel knows no time zone.** The reader formats a punch's wall-clock times in the app's calendar (`formatDenverTimeOnly`) and passes the day as `work_date`; the kernel's calendar math is on `YYYY-MM-DD` alone.
