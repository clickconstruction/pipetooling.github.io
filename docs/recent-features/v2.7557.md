# v2.7557 — Lien notices: record each envelope with a photo of its certified receipt (2026-11-17)

The owner, 2026-11-16, reading the § 53.056 notice's help sheet: step 5's blanks for the two certified numbers had no room to write in, and *they really should be uploading it instead directly with a photo of the receipt*. Before the sheet could say so, the notice's **Save & record sends…** had to take one. It took a method, a typed tracking number and a day per copy. The receipt photo existed only on a filed affidavit's service step (punch list #314), and the barcode reader only in the run window (v2.7331). Client only, no migration.

## What changed

- `LienFilingTabs.tsx`: each copy's row on the notice's record step (the computer's box and the phone's sheet) draws `LienReceiptPhotoPress` while the copy goes on paper. A copy sent by email has no receipt and no press. `SendDraft` carries `receiptUrl`, and the row's two writers (the photo's link, the number read) update the draft with functional updates so neither drops the other.
- `recordNoticeSends` writes each photo as `receipt_url` on its send (`noticeSendsWithReceipts`, `lienServiceProof.ts`), the key a filed affidavit's copies already use, so `parseLienSends` and `serviceProofLines` read it unchanged.
- `LienReceiptPhotoPress.tsx` takes `paper` (the file is named *Lien notice, Job …, mail receipt, owner, …*) and `onRead`: after the photo is saved, `readTrackingFromPhoto` (`lienReceiptReadIo.ts`) reads the full-size shot's barcode with the browser's `BarcodeDetector`, or `@zxing/browser` fetched on that press, and hands the number to the box. `receiptReadWords` says under the press what was read, or that nothing was and to type it.
- The Filings list shows *receipt ›* under a recorded notice for each copy with a photo.
