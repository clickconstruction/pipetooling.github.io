# v2.7202 — Paint a map: the groundwork (2026-11-13)

Punch list #345, PR 1. The owner's idea of 2026-11-13: a person walks with precise location on, the phone draws their line on a map, and they share it. He read the card and the mock-up (`to-dos/paint-a-map/`), asked for the sending options, the doors and a magnifying glass, and said to build it all. This PR is the painted map itself. It has no screen.

## What changed

- **The tables** (`20261113220000_painted_maps.sql`, documented in `docs/migrations/`): `painted_maps` (a name, a line of words, a job or a development or neither), `painted_map_lines` (one stroke: its points, how it was made, the stretches the phone was not sure of, its length and its give or take, a measured length typed in) and `painted_map_places` (a named dot with a word and a photo). A private bucket, `painted-map-photos`, holds the photos.
- **The gate**: whoever can open a map's job reads the map and adds to it, through Materials on site's own gate. A development's map is read by whoever can see the development or a job in it, or is on the team of one. A map tied to nothing is its maker's and the office's. Changing or removing a stroke or a place is its maker's and the office's.
- **A saved line is a shape.** `points` is a list of pairs of numbers. No time, speed or margin is stored for a point, and `pointsToJson` in the kernel is the one door a line leaves the phone through.
- **The rules and the words** (`src/lib/paintedMap/`), pure, 74 tests:
  - `paintedLine.ts`: a line's length with its unsure stretches left out, straightening (`simplifyLine`), the spot on a line nearest a point, trimming an end, redrawing a stretch, what a dragged corner catches (`snapTarget`), and the stored shape.
  - `paintedTrack.ts`: a walk turned into a line. A reading is kept only when the phone moved further than its own margin, so standing still draws no knot. A stretch the phone was not sure of is kept, marked and left out of the length. Walked or driven is told from the middle speed. The give or take on a length. When painting stops by itself: five minutes standing still, or an hour.
  - `paintedMap.ts`: rows to maps and back, and the words. *About 830 ft, give or take 40 ft.* A measured length wins: *612 ft, measured with a wheel.*

## Is this the best we can do

Three things changed from the card while building.

- **The standing-still rule.** The first rule kept a reading that moved more than six tenths of the phone's margin. A test of a person standing for thirty seconds at each end of a 100 m walk came out 13 m long. The rule now asks for a step longer than the new reading's own margin, and the same walk comes out within 6 m. Using the new reading's margin, not the wider of the two, also means a sure reading after an unsure one is taken at once, so a faint stretch ends where the phone became sure again.
- **No customer on a map.** The card let a map hang on a customer. A customer's property is reached through their job, so the map has two homes, a job or a development, and one fewer way to lose it.
- **One photo for a place**, not several. A second photo is a second place.

## Checked on a database

Docker was not available on this Mac, so the bed could not run here. The migration was applied twice to a throwaway local Postgres 15 with stub tables, and the scenario was played on it without the fence cases. That found a fault before it shipped: the photo bucket's policies compared a map's id with the map's own name, because `name` inside the `EXISTS` meant the map's and not the file's. They now write `objects.name`. The bed (`npm run test:pg:painted-maps`) runs the whole scenario on the real schema in CI.
