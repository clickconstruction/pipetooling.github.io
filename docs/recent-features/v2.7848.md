# v2.7848 — Pricing: a row can keep a percent rule, the ground (2026-11-18)

The database half of the Percent row on Bids → Pricing. The owner asked for it on 2026-11-18 after an estimator's request, *a way for 40% of the cost of pipe to be calc. for the connections auto*, and said *build it all* on the mock-up the same day. No screen changes.

## Why

An estimator adds one row, Connections, priced at 40% of what the pipe rows sell for, in place of counting fittings. The number was worked out on the calculator and typed, so it went stale when a pipe row changed, and nothing recorded the rule.

## What ships

Migration `20261118220000_bid_count_row_percent_rule.sql` ([its page](../migrations/20261118220000_bid_count_row_percent_rule.md)):

- `bids_count_rows.percent_rule`, a nullable `jsonb`: the percent, the basis (`revenue` or `cost`) and the rows it reads, by kind or by tag. A check holds it to that shape.
- `clone_count_rows_to_bid_version` and `duplicate_bid_to_service_type` are redefined with the one column added, so a new version and a bid copied to another trade carry the rule.

The row's price stays an ordinary saved price, so every reader of a bid's prices is unchanged.

## Tests

`supabase/tests/bid_copies/20_scenario.sql` gains the rule on one seeded row and four checks: an estimator may write it, five malformed rules are refused, a new version carries it, a duplicate carries it. The bed runs in CI's SQL beds.

## Not in this change

No screen reads the column. The Percent row window, the row's chip and the price that follows come in the next change, after `supabase db push`.
