# v2.6958 — Permits: a map point for Austin permits the city sends without one (2026-11-11)

Punch list #310, PR 1 of 2. Austin's feed (`3syk-w9eu`) sends about four in ten of the permits the pull keeps with no `latitude`, and the gap falls on the cards the skim ranks highest (the ones with a declared value). Almost every one of them carries `tcad_id`, which is `geo_id` on Travis County's parcel layer. `permit-pull` now asks that layer for the lot's shape and writes a point inside it.

- **Kernel**: `supabase/functions/_shared/permitParcelPoints.ts`. `parcelIdsToAsk` (rows with no point and an id, each id once), `idBatches` (100 ids a request), `travisParcelQuery` (`geo_id IN (…)`, shapes in degrees), `pointOfRings`, `parcelPointsOf`, `fillPoints`, `withoutPointColumns`, and the source `TRAVIS_PARCEL_POINTS`.
- **The point**: the middle by area of the biggest ring, measured from the ring's own first corner (summed in whole degrees the terms cancel and the middle drifts by metres). When that middle is outside the shape (an L, a flag lot, a hole) the point is the middle of the widest stretch of the lot along the line through it. Held to Texas's box.
- **The hook**: `FeedReader.fillPoint`, set on `AUSTIN_READER` only. The other feeds are untouched.
- **In the pull**: per page, after the dedupe and before the upsert, the page's ids go to the layer by POST. A hundred ids in a URL is turned away by the county's firewall with a page that is not JSON; the same list as a form is answered. One request has 20 seconds; the first refusal ends the asking for that run.
- **A layer that does not answer never stops the pull.** Its rows are upserted without `lat` and `lng`, so a point filled on an earlier night stands. Nothing goes in `permit_pulls.error`, which the Pulled line reads as *the last pull stopped* and the records' start reads as a failed run. The feed's outcome in the answer and the log carries `points: { asked, filled, held, error }`.

Measured live on 2026-11-11 through the new code, without writing (the 75-day window, every kind the pull keeps): 4,238 rows, 1,742 with no point, **1,325 would take one** (363 of 414 commercial building permits, 333 of 437 houses, 629 of 891 plumbing permits), 11 requests, under 8 seconds for the whole window. 417 stay without: 5 carry no id, 341 carry a ten-digit id the county's layer (rebuilt monthly) does not hold, 71 carry an id that is not Travis's (70 an R and six digits, in the zips of Austin over the Williamson County line; 1 six digits). 67 of the points were asked back of the layer and every one fell inside the parcel it was made from.

The other cities, measured the same day and left alone: San Antonio 685 of 5,341 issued and 53 of 960 applied with no point, New Braunfels 14 of 554, San Marcos 1 of 265, Seguin 0 of 268.

Tests: `src/lib/research/permitParcelPoints.test.ts` (24, every id and shape made up; eleven mutants each fail one).

Deploy: `permit-pull` and `permit-lookup` after the merge (both bundle `_shared/permitCities.ts`). No migration.
