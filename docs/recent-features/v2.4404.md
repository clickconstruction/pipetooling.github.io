# v2.4404 — Lien desk: see which supply houses are still owed on a job (2026-10-02)

No migration, no edge function. **The ask (Robert, 2026-10-02)**, after writing a GC an email about an unpaid balance, a stopped check and the supply house's own notice: "I would like to be able to show what jobs from the lien desk also have job accounts with supply houses in a tasteful way … being able to present someone with this information going forward would make writing emails like this easier." Plan, then *is this the best we can do?*, then a mock-up, then the same question. Canvas: https://claude.ai/artifact/2VQBPxmixzF3ckkSaD2Wc2 (row 1 the two screens apart; row 2 the first pass with its faults numbered; row 3 the proposal; row 4 the later steps). Robert: "I like your suggestions. Please build all the steps". This is step 1 of 4.

## What was missing

The Lien desk never named a supply house. What a job's houses are paid and owed lived only on Materials → Held for suppliers, so the office carried the numbers across by hand. On the live desk 11 of the 25 jobs on the Notices tab had a house still owed. The first pass (a *Job acct* chip per row and a paid/owed table) was cut for four reasons: the account is not the signal, the money is (the job that prompted this has no job account at the house that is sending its own notice); a table of paid and owed repeats Held for suppliers and leaves out the date the house can act; nothing joined the two debts; and nothing could be taken into an email.

## What shipped

- **The kernel** ([`lienJobSuppliers.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienJobSuppliers.ts), new, 13 tests). `buildLienSupplierJobs` adds up, per job and house, paid, owed, the job-account slice, the months of the unpaid invoices and the first day a house was paid. The money follows `jobAccountsFlow` (allocated = amount × pct ÷ 100; an open credit memo never joins an owed figure), and a test holds the two kernels to the same figures on one fixture. `lienSupplierMark` is the row's mark, null unless a house is still owed. `lienSupplierNotice` is the house's own § 53.056 window: `noticeDeadlineForMonth` on the house's unpaid invoice months for the property's kind, the next open one, else *closed*. It is our estimate and the screen says so. `buildLienSupplierCard` builds the rows, the total and one bold line (*The $X <payer> owes us covers what the houses are owed.* or *The houses are owed $Y more than <payer> owes us.*), plus *We paid a house first on …* only when we did. `lienSupplierEmailText` is the paragraph: one fact per sentence; a house whose window closed is named with its money only.
- **The read** ([`useLienJobSuppliers`](../../src/hooks/useLienJobSuppliers.ts), new): the given jobs' invoice allocations, those invoices, the houses' names, the jobs' customer payments (`jobs_ledger_payments.paid_on`), and each job's accounts through the existing `useJobAccountStrips`. A failed read answers an empty map, so the desk draws no mark and no card.
- **The mark and the card** ([`LienJobSuppliers.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienJobSuppliers.tsx), new): `LienSupplierMarkLine` (a storefront and *3 houses owed $13,058*; the storefront and the money alone on a Calendar row; teal when a job account is open, the job window's sign) and `LienJobSuppliersCard` (a table on a computer, stacked rows on a phone; folded to one line when every house is paid or when `startFolded`; **Copy for an email**; **Open in Held for suppliers ›**).
- **The desk** ([`LienDeskModal`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskModal.tsx)): one `useLienJobSuppliers` call over every job on the three lists and the Calendar while the desk is open; the mark at the end of each list row's words; the card under the gates in the notice pane; `supplierMarks` passed to [`LienDeskCalendarTab`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskCalendarTab.tsx), which draws it after the job's name (computer) and at the right of the dates line (phone).
- **The Lien window** ([`LienInstrumentsModal`](../../src/components/jobs/LienInstrumentsModal.tsx)): the card as one folded line in the header; opened, it scrolls inside 42dvh so the paper keeps the window (v2.4398).
- **Held for suppliers** ([`MaterialsJobAccountsTab`](../../src/components/materials/MaterialsJobAccountsTab.tsx)): `?job=<id>` opens that job's statement and scrolls to it.
- **A text token**: `--text-teal-700` (light `#0f766e`, dark `#5eead4`) for the job-account words; the storefront in the job window still uses the literal.
- Guide [`see-which-supply-houses-are-owed-on-a-lien-job`](../../src/content/help/see-which-supply-houses-are-owed-on-a-lien-job.md) (new), linked from the desk's and the Calendar's guides.
