# v2.4719 — Lien desk: fix what the paper is missing without leaving it (2026-10-06)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Assistant Taunya, by way of the owner (2026-10-06, a screenshot of job 838's affidavit with four red blanks): *I want clicking on the fix the property button to open a modal above this one where I can fill in the information and when I close it see it complete on this window.* Before this, **Fix the property** closed the paper and opened the Affidavits pane, and **Original contractor** only said *Set it in Edit Job*. Mock-up and critique: artifact RUJPGiCcgyuVFFVssm9c2F (the three lenses: her seat, the job behind the ask, the whole office). The first draft was a window per blank; the critique made it one property window, because the county, the legal description and the owner live on one record and the parcel lookup answers all three at once.

## What changed

- **`LienPaperPreviewOverlay`** takes `onFix` and `paused`. With `onFix` (the desk passes it for office roles) a property blank's button keeps its words and opens the property window; the GC blank's button reads **Pick the GC**. The signer, the company address, the notices and the homestead stay words. While a window is open the paper's keys are paused, so Esc and the arrows belong to the window. An entry's `filled` list draws green **Just filled** cards above *What is missing*.
- **`LienPaperPropertyWindow`** (new, z 805): `CustomerPropertyRecordPanel` over the job's linked `customer_addresses` row (`draftFromRow`, moved to `lib/customers/propertyDraft.ts`), the pressed field focused, the lookup on open when the row was never looked up. It says when the record is shared (*N other jobs sit at this address*) and when the job names its own owner (the paper reads that one). No linked row: the job address is inserted as a property on the customer (the GC when there is no customer) and the job is linked. Done, ×, Esc and the backdrop save; Discard does not.
- **`LienPaperGcWindow`** (new): the desk's GCs first, a `customers` name search after two letters; the job's customer is shown disabled (the customer and the GC are never the same party); a pick writes `jobs_ledger.gc_customer_id`.
- **`LienDeskModal`**: on a save it records the row's blanks (`paperFilledFrom`, merged by key across fixes), calls `onChanged`, and keeps the paper on the row by key when the fix moves it in the list. `lienPaperFilledSince` names the blanks that filled; `withFreshMarks` wraps their values so `paintGaps` paints them as `.lienPaperFresh` (green, fading after two seconds; still under reduced motion).
- **Bug fix:** `buildLienAffidavitFieldsForJob` took the job's name as the kind of work before the trade, so job 838's affidavit swore *the kind of work … was: Bruce Hall*. It reads `lienTradeWords(serviceTypeName).laborMaterials` now, on the desk's paper and the Lien window's affidavit alike.
