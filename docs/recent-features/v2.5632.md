# v2.5632 — The thread's last lines in the customer sheet, and the waiting dot on the Pipeline row (2026-10-19)

The owner's yes after punch list #123 ("Build the thread's last lines in the customer sheet and the waiting dot on the Pipeline row as one small PR after #124 PR 2"), asked on 2026-10-18 when he wanted to know whether a user could quickly see the thread between the company and a customer from the Pipeline. The sheet behind the row's speech bubble (v2.5626) said whose word came last and offered Open their thread; it now shows the last lines, and the bubble says from the board that someone waits. No migration; no edge function; nothing in the kernels copy.

- **The sheet's last lines** (`lastLinesOnJob` in `messages/customerDoor.ts`): the newest three call, text, email or portal lines between us and them from the read the sheet already makes (`loadJobMessages`), oldest first so they read down to the newest; notes, events and team lines stay out. Each line is its meta (`lastLineMeta`: *Dana · text · yesterday*, *Tanya · email · today at 8:44 AM*, *the app* on a line it sent) over its words (`clipWords`, 160 at a word: an email's subject or first line, a call's summary, a voicemail's words, *No voicemail.* on a missed call). Theirs carry the customer's orange bar, as the job's activity draws a line from them. The *Waiting on us* line stays above; Open their thread stays the door to the rest.
- **The dot's kernel** (`messages/boardDots.ts`): R4 · 4 (v2.5407) drew `boardDot(rows, messages, names, jobId, now)` in `flows/stageSends.ts` and nothing called it. `boardDotsByJob(contacts, lines, todayYmd, now)` builds Follow up's Needs a reply rows with the queue's own `needsReply` and calls `boardDot` once per job on the rows, longest waiting first, so of two people waiting on one job the longest-waiting one is the hover's and a row whose newest line is tied to no job marks nothing. `boardDotTitle` joins the hover's parts side by side.
- **The read** (`messages/boardDotsIo.ts`, `loadBoardDotRows`): the two reads the queue's Needs a reply needs and nothing else: the customers whose conversation is open or snoozed (`contacts`, kind customer, state waiting or snoozed) and their lines in (`messages`, direction in, newest first under PostgREST's cap per slice of 100 people, written down as `LINES_MAX`), through `followUpContactOf` and `followUpMessagesFromRows`, so the shapes are Follow up's. An open conversation has no reply of ours after its newest line, which is what the state means, so `boardDot`'s own check for a later line of ours stands on the state.
- **The hook** (`hooks/useBoardDots.ts`): the office only (`canOpenMessages`, the bubble's own gate); reads on mount, again when the board's jobs array changes (a load of the board refreshes the dots) and on `reload` (a send from the sheet, since our reply closes their row); a read that fails keeps the last dots and says nothing.
- **The bubble** (`MessagesButton`'s `dot` prop): an 8 px dot in the customer's orange at the bubble's corner, the title and the label *Open Dana Whitfield’s messages · Dana · text · 9:05 AM · Can you do Thursday? · Nobody has answered.*; `data-messages-dot` for a smoke. The row context carries `boardDotByJobId` (both customer-line renderers, the three stages tables' props, `JobsStagesTab`'s `stagesTableShared`).
- **Tests**: `customerDoor.test.ts` (the three lines oldest first, the names and words by channel, the clip, the meta), `boardDots.test.ts` (the map, an untied newest line, done and snoozed, the longest waiting wins, one entry per job, the title), `useBoardDots.render.test.tsx` (the office's read, the key and the reload, no read for anyone else, a failed read keeps the dots), the sheet's smoke (three lines, theirs marked, notes out, no list on an empty thread) and the row's (the dot and its hover on both customer lines, none off the map).
- **Docs**: the guide *text a customer from the job*; the Glossary's R2 and R4 · 4 lines; `MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md`'s door row; to-do #123's status.
- **Not in this PR**: the Dashboard's and Customers' bubbles draw no dot (the board's read is the Pipeline's); a dot for a GC's or a trade partner's waiting line (the bubble is the customer's).
