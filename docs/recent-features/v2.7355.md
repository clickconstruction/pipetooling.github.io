## Before

A and B (v2.7271, v2.7278) rebuilt the job window on a phone, but only the tabbed one, which `resolveJobWindowMode` gives to the office roles and the master techs. Helpers, the superintendent, subs and estimators open the plain `DetailJobModal`, and at 375 px it was still the old layout, read live on 2026-11-15 as the sample superintendent: a sheet over the Dashboard about 1,800 px long, the number chip and the name over three small icons with no names, a boxed Paint a map, the customer with two pills, the job accounts chips, the hours target card, a notes feed scrolling inside the scroll, then the two dates, the Status rail, Plans, Parts Cost, *Payments and invoices are not shown in this view* and a Close at the foot. The bar (Arrived or Leaving · Leave report · Note, punch list #189) was right, and it is what the week's count says gets pressed: the superintendent opened this window 11 times in the week of 2026-11-09 and pressed Arrived 5, Leaving 9 and Leave report 3.

## What changed

- **[`src/lib/jobs/jobWindowCrewPhone.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobWindowCrewPhone.ts)** (new, with tests): the title's quiet line (`crewQuietLine`), the scheduled block's label (`crewBlockLabel`: *Next on site* for today and ahead, *Was scheduled* once the day has passed), the newest note or report as one row (`lastNoteWords`, cut at a word at 90 characters), the ⋯ sheet's rows (`crewSheetRows`: the tabbed window's doors that are open to this viewer, without the ? card), and the folds with what the phone remembers of them (`crewFolds`, `readCrewFolds`, `keepCrewFolds`, under their own storage key).
- **[`src/components/jobs/DetailJobModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/DetailJobModal.tsx)**, outside pane mode and under 640 px (`phoneCrew`):
  - the window fills the screen, as the tabbed one does, instead of a sheet with a margin;
  - the title is the name with the status chip, a quiet line of the number and the customer, and ⋯ over ✕ at the right, 44 px each; the icon row is not drawn, and its doors that are open to the viewer are named rows in `JobWindowMoreSheet` (for a helper, Share the job); both presses keep `data-navtrack="job-window"`, so *Close job detail* goes on being counted;
  - the body reads **Where** (the banner, the painted maps, Paint a map centred), **Photos** (the banner, Add a photo first, which opens `JobPhotosWindow` on the limited row), **Who** (`JobWindowWho`: the number as a press that calls, with Copy; Files and Photos as presses), **What's happening** (the block the window was opened from, when there is one, and **Last note**, whose blue words open the notes), then **More on this job**;
  - the folds are **Notes and reports** (the whole feed and the composer, with its count), **Plans**, **Job accounts** once the job has a supply house on its list, and **Parts cost** with Job Total for the roles that read them today; the project workflow link stays as a row under them;
  - the two dates, the Status rail, the hours target (none of these roles sets it), the foot's sentence and the foot's Close are not drawn on a phone.
  - a press under 40 px in the body gets the unseen 44 px pad the tabbed window's body has (v2.7309, `watchSmallPresses`), under its own `.crew-window-body` rule.
- **[`src/components/JobThreadNotesPanel.tsx`](../../src/components/JobThreadNotesPanel.tsx)** marks its list `data-activity-scroll`. A feed inside a fold is laid out hidden, so its own jump to the newest entry landed nowhere and the fold opened on the oldest; `DetailJobModal` now sends the list to its newest entry when the fold opens, in the crew's window and in the tabbed window's Activity fold alike.
- **[`src/components/jobs/JobWindowPhoneSections.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobWindowPhoneSections.tsx)**: `JobWindowWho` takes `emailRow`; the crew's window passes false for a helper or a sub, and passes no customer id, so the portal press, which is the office's, is not drawn.
- The guides: *clock in and out with Job Mode* → *The job window on a phone* names the sections; *move between a job's Job, Edit, Bill, and Costs tabs* says the field roles' view reads in sections.

## What is different from the drawing

- **Job accounts are folded, not gone.** The drawing dropped them. A helper at a supply house counter may need the job's account, so the strip is a fold whenever the job has a house on its list.
- **The email row stays for the superintendent and the estimators.** The drawing dropped it for every crew role; the count does not cover that press, so only the helper and the sub lose it.
- **No Directions press and no address row.** B's live Where is the banner with the address pinned on it, which opens Maps; the crew's window draws the same.
- **Today's work is not here.** The open stage, its checklist, the plans and materials on site as one section is the next PR, with the reads it needs. The stage checklist strip stays where it was, under Photos.
- **The sub's own rows are not here.** *Your work* and *Your pay* wait for Taunya's two answers on the card.

## What a person loses

Nothing that was theirs to act on. A helper and a sub no longer see the customer's email, the two dates, the Status rail or the hours target on a phone; the superintendent keeps the email and Parts cost. The notes feed is one tap away under Notes and reports, with the newest note shown above it. The desk is untouched.

## Is this the best we can do?

A separate `JobWindowPhone` with a role kernel (C as first drawn, sized L) would have meant two windows to keep in step. This is the one component, with the phone's order under one flag and the words in a kernel, so a later change to a section lands in both windows. What it does not do is put the work itself on the screen: that is Today's work, next.
