# v2.6043 — Materials on site, the office: Jobs → Materials, the labels and the box search (2026-10-29)

Punch list #209, PR 3 of 4, stacked on v2.6041 (the phone). The office side of Materials on site, built in the design spike of 2026-10-28/29 and now reading the tables.

## What changed

- **Jobs → Materials** (`src/components/jobs/JobsMaterialsTab.tsx`, the tab in `Jobs.tsx`, the jobs-list load gate): the desk of every job with a live container, sorted by the week's missing money, and a page per job. The page's header flows across widths (the back chevron, the job and its address, Open the job), and under it the **flow row** (`FlowRow.tsx`): came in → on site → used → missing, each figure in its colour, an amber badge for what waits in Unsorted or needs a count, a red badge for the week's missing money; List and Map at the row's end are the two views of On site.
- **On site**: the boxes grouped by place, the filters All, Due a count and Can't find, Add a container with its place, Correct… on a line (a count row in the office's name), Label on every box. **Missing**: the three-way comparison table (`manifest.ts`) and Reported this week with its 7 and 30 day window. **Deliveries**: the Drive-first intake (the job's folder, or Set the job's Drive folder), Paste the text instead with Read the lines and the review table, Add N lines to Unsorted as a delivery; Deliveries so far by invoice. **History**: every row with reason and person filters.
- **Labels** (`LabelsModal`): one box or every box, a sheet each with the stencil, the job, the place, the code and the table *In this box / On hand / Counted* with ruled rows for the pen; printing draws the labels alone. The QR carries `PRTS.CO/B/<CODE>`, a 21-module code; prts.co forwards to `/b/:code` (live since 2026-10-29, `scripts/cloudflare/prts-co-redirect.md`).
- **Doors**: the job window's Materials tab (`JobWindowMaterialsPanel.tsx`, `jobHistoryTab.ts`), the Pipeline tools menu's Materials desk row with the week's count (loaded when the menu opens), the Job Mode card (PR 2), and Go anywhere: the doors Materials desk and On site, and the live group **Boxes on site** (`src/lib/materials/boxSearch.ts`, kind `box` in `doors.ts`): a door per box by stencil, part on hand or place; the office lands on the materials page with Open on the phone screen a second press away, a crew lands on the box.
- **Words**: the empty states are `EmptyState`s; a failed read draws `OfflineRetryPanel`; the Unsorted panel reads *delivered, not assigned storage*. The spike's agent door on Deliveries names the dev-mcp verbs as coming, not as there.
- **Docs**: the guide *see what is on every job site and what walked away* (Office), the app directory's `/jobs`, `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`, the Glossary, the card's mock-ups (`to-dos/materials-on-site/`), the release note and this fragment.
