# v2.8564 — Bills sent: the developer can delete a by-hand record of a test bill (2026-11-24)

Punch list #443, row 4: the second of the take-backs the owner approved on 2026-11-25 (*Yes, the six small ones (Recommended)*): one small PR each, invisible to every non-dev, refusing any row that is not a test row or that a real record depends on. No migration, no edge function, nothing owed after the merge.

## The press nothing took back

**Record how it went**, on the form under **Say how it went…**, writes one `sent_documents` row a bill, kind `bill_by_hand`. The table has no update policy, so the record cannot be changed, by design, and nothing deleted one. A record a walk makes on a test bill would stand on that bill for good. The table has let a dev delete since it was made (`sent_documents_delete_dev`, `20261005173119_sent_documents.sql`). No screen and no seat verb called it.

## What changed

- `src/lib/billing/byHandRecordDelete.ts`, the rule, pure. `byHandRecordDeleteDrawn(role, line)` is true for a dev on a by-hand line and for nothing else. `byHandRecordDeleteProblem(line)` refuses, from what the page holds, a send that is not a by-hand record, a record that names no bill, and a record of a real bill. `byHandRowDeleteProblem(row)` runs the rule again on what the database holds: the kind, a copy path, the bill and its job, through `isBillsTestRow`. A job or a bill that does not read is taken as real.
- `src/lib/billing/byHandRecordDeleteIo.ts`. `readByHandRecordRow(id)` reads the record, the bill it names and that bill's job. `deleteByHandRecord(id)` reads them, runs the rule, then deletes the one row while it is a by-hand record with no copy. The delete is sent once, and a delete that reaches no row is said as a refusal.
- `src/components/billing/ByHandRecordDelete.tsx`, drawn by `RecordPanel` in `BillsSentLedger.tsx` under every line of a bill's record. It draws nothing unless the rule says so. On a real bill's by-hand record the press is dead and the line says why. On a test bill's it asks in a confirm that reads the record back, whose button reads **Delete the record**, then reads the page again.
- The guide *see which bills went out* and the glossary say so.

## Why the delete of the row is the whole take-back

Read in the migrations: nothing points at `sent_documents`, a by-hand record keeps no file in the bucket, the table's only triggers are on insert, and the insert trigger that writes an Activity line is for kind `bill_resent` alone. The delete policy is `is_dev()`, and the table's grants are the default ones, which include DELETE for a signed-in role.

## Who sees it

A dev. The press reads the signed-in role (`useAuth`), and View as a sample account is that account, so it draws nothing there. The record reads as it did for the office, by test.
