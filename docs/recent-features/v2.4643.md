# v2.4643 — Legal: give the law firm settlement authority with a floor (2026-10-05)

Punch list #85, item 20 (lane D). The owner's decision of 2026-10-05: settlement authority is a threshold. The office sets a dollar or percent floor per matter; the firm settles freely at or above it and asks below it. Until now a firm could record *Settled* at any figure and the office learned it afterward.

## What changed

- **Migration `20261006140000_legal_settlement_floor.sql`**: `legal_matters.settlement_floor_amount` / `settlement_floor_pct` (one or neither), `legal_set_settlement_floor`, `legal_answer_settlement`. See `docs/migrations/20261006140000_legal_settlement_floor.md`.
- **One rule, shared**: `supabase/functions/_shared/legalSettlement.ts` — `settlementFloorOf`, `settlementFloorDollars` (a percent of the open balance, to the cent), `settlementBelowFloor`, `matterOpenBalance` (the packet's `jobOpenBalance` rule over raw rows), `settlementFloorWords`. The function decides with it, the portal and the desk word it with it.
- **The office sets it**: a *Settlement authority* block on the desk's Fees & steps tab (office roles, while the matter is with the firm) with *Save floor* / *No floor* and, for a percent, *Today that is $X*; and an optional floor on the Mark attorney ready sheet, saved right after the release (`legalRpcData` returns the new matter's id). Each save writes a note on the matter, which the firm sees.
- **The firm sees it**: `legal-portal` sends `settlementFloor`; the matter card reads *Settlement authority: You may settle at $14,000.00 or above (70% of the balance). Below that, ask.* The *Settled* step form gains *Settlement amount*, required when a floor is set.
- **Under the floor, it asks**: `submit-legal-portal` saves the step as a `question` entry with `meta.flavor = 'settlement'`, `proposedAmount`, `floor`; the stage does not move, and the firm's notice says why. The conversation (item 17) threads it as *Dana Reyes at the firm asked to settle at $9,000.00*; the desk answers with **Sign off** / **Not yet** and a note (`legal_answer_settlement`, which writes the answer, acknowledges the ask and, on a sign-off, moves the stage to settled in one transaction). The Needs You card counts it first: *1 settlement below your floor to sign off* (`LegalFirmActivity.settlements`). The office's answer emails the firm through the existing answer trigger.
- Docs: the guide *share your attorney their portal*, GLOSSARY (Legal matter), ACCESS_CONTROL (a new row), EDGE_FUNCTIONS (`submit-legal-portal`, `legal-portal`).

**Deploy**: merge (client via CI) → `supabase db push` (`20261006140000`) → `supabase functions deploy submit-legal-portal legal-portal`. Additive, so the order is forgiving: before the push, the desk's *Save floor* answers with the RPC's missing-function error and the functions read no floor.

## Is this the best we can do?

- *Percent of what?* Of the open balance on the day the firm proposes, by the packet's own rule. A percent fixed at referral goes stale as payments land. The page and the function compute it with one shared kernel.
- *Dollar and percent together?* One or the other; the database refuses both. Two floors raise *which one wins* on every proposal.
- *Should the firm see the floor?* Yes: authority it can act on without a call. Nothing shows it to the customer.
- *Settled with no amount?* Required when a floor is set; optional without one, and then the step moves the stage as before.
- *Why one database function for the sign-off?* So a sign-off can never land without the stage moving, or the stage move without the answer the firm reads.

## Left out

The firm cannot propose terms in a structured way (dates, installments); terms ride in the step's detail. A settlement the office declines leaves the stage where it was; the firm records a new step or a new proposal.
