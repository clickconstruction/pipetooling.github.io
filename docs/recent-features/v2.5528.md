# v2.5528 — Job Mode: a text you send shows in Job updates right away (2026-10-14)

The last piece of the field walk's fixes, by Customer communication helper 10, at cc's ask, after v2.5525 (the card's `updatesRev`) and v2.5527 (the tech's Customer pill) were both on main. A note from **Something else** showed in Job updates at once since v2.5525. A text sent from **Message the customer** showed only on the next open, since `messages` is not in the realtime publication.

- **`src/hooks/useJobMessages.ts`**: an optional `rev`. Raising it reads the job's lines again under the same key, so the lines already on screen stay there until the new read is in. Pipeline's Job activity and the Job detail window pass none and read as before.
- **`JobModeDetailsSection.tsx`**: passes the card's `updatesRev` to `useJobMessages`. The card bumps it after the sheet sends a text or a note, so Job updates reads the notes (v2.5525) and the customer's lines again.
- **Test** `JobModeDetailsSection.updatesRev.render.test.tsx`: with the real hook and the read mocked, a bump re-renders. Her line stays while the read is out, the read runs once more, and the Customer pill counts the tech's new text (1 → 2). Without `rev` in the hook the case fails.
