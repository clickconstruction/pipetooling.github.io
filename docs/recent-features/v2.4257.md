# v2.4257 — A worker reports a day the clock missed (2026-09-30)

Punch list #70, PR 4. The ask began with a worker's text to the owner — *on Friday it didn't let me clock in… my hours were 10 AM to 9 PM* — which the owner then had to relay. A worker's own fix door (*Your record*) opens only for a clock left running, never for a day with no punch at all. This gives him the door; the office approves instead of retyping.

## What changed

- **`MissedClockInDoor` + `MissedClockInModal`** ([`src/components/clock/`](../../src/components/clock/MissedClockInModal.tsx)): *It did not clock me in* — the day (start of last week through today, the same window the day editor allows), started, stopped, the job, what happened. It inserts the person's own `clock_sessions` row (the own-row insert RLS has always allowed — read-only trainees included, as for a punch); the typed-hours ledger (v2.4242) stamps it *typed by him*, and it lands pending like any session. No migration.
- **Two doors**: under the Job Mode card (Job Mode shows no My Time — a line, *Worked a day the clock missed? Tell the office*) and in My Time's footer (**The clock missed a day…**). Neither shows for a salaried person.
- **The rules are a kernel** — [`missedClockIn.ts`](../../src/lib/clock/missedClockIn.ts): at most 16 hours; a stop at or before the start reads as the next day; not in the future (*if you are still working, clock in instead*); not over time the clock already has, closed or running (*report only the part the clock missed*); what happened is required. The form starts on **today** on purpose: a forgotten day picker then fails loudly instead of landing hours on the wrong day.
- **The job**: chips for that day's schedule blocks and the jobs he clocked on in the two weeks; otherwise *the office will set the job* and the row wears the queue's existing *no job* flag.
- Guide: *report a day the clock missed*.

## Known limits

- The week fence is the form's, not the database's: the own-row insert policy has never checked dates.
- The office is told by the Needs You card (v2.4254), not by a push or an email.
