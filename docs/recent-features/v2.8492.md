# v2.8492 — Bills sent: Not paid says everything that is still out (2026-11-24)

An idea from the look-only live test of the Bills sent page on 2026-11-24 (punch list #422, idea 2). The **Not paid** tile was right for what it said: the bills on the list that are not paid in full. A leader quotes it as what the company is owed, and it left out the open bills in the yellow *No send on record* box, which had a tile of their own with a count and no amount. No migration, no edge function.

## What changed

- `billsSentOut(lines, openGaps, todayYmd)` in `src/lib/billing/billsSent.ts`, new and pure, is everything still out: the whole, its two parts, the bills among them with no amount on record, and the oldest. The sent part is `billsSentTiles`' own figure, bill for bill, through one shared helper.
- The **Not paid** tile draws it: one count and one amount over both parts, then the two parts by name, **Sent** and **No send on record**, each with its own count and amount. Each part is a press. **Sent** turns on the **Not paid** chip, **No send on record** turns on its own. Neither chip shows anything it did not show before.
- The figure itself is no press, since no one chip shows both parts.
- The tile says how old the oldest bill is (`billsSentOldestWords`, the age marked with `val` and drawn through `ValueSentence`).
- The yellow *No send on record* tile is gone into the tile as its second part, in the yellow box's colours, so no bill is counted in two tiles' figures. The yellow box, its chip and the phone's folded line are as they were.
- The page's foot, the guide and the print say what **Not paid** counts. The print's heading gains the sentence (`billsSentOutPlain`), since the tile is not on the paper.

## The defaults chosen

- **Age.** A sent bill is counted from its first send, as the list counts *days out*. A bill with no send has no first send, so it is counted from the day it was billed, and the tile's words say so: *Oldest is 62 days out* against *Oldest was billed 62 days ago*. A tie goes to the sent bill.
- **A bill with no amount on record** is counted and adds nothing to the amount. The tile then says how many there are (*1 bill has no amount*), and the foot says the amount leaves them out. Holding the bill out of the count would hide a bill that is still owed.
- **A test bill** is in neither part, as v2.8445 made it: its line carries no payment state, and it is never in the box.
- **A zero part is still drawn**, with *0 bills* and no amount, so the tile always says both parts. Its press leads to the chip's own *Nothing waiting on …* words.
- **The layout.** The wide tile takes a row of its own under the small tiles, at every width. In a row with four small tiles a tile of three figures had 419 px at a desk and its words wrapped unevenly, so it was moved. On a phone the two parts sit under the figure. Where there is room they sit beside it.

## Two things the box now says, so it agrees with its part

- A bill with no send whose payments on record leave nothing out now sits with the paid ones in the box (`billsSentGaps` reads the payment state, as the list has since v2.8423). A check typed in and held leaves the bill billed, and the box called that bill not paid.
- A part-paid bill's row reads *Part paid, $700.00 still out.* where it read *Not paid.* (`billsSentGapStands`), on the screen and in the print. The tile adds what is left of that bill, and the row says the same figure.

## Drawn and measured

The real `BillsSentLedger` over made-up rows on a harness page (not committed), in headless Chromium at 375, 768 and 1400 px, light and dark, in six cases: four small tiles, three, two, the sent part alone, the no-send part alone, and a bill with no amount.

- Nothing ran past the right edge in any of the 36 draws. Every row of tiles ended at the row's right edge, so no draw had a hole.
- 375 px: the wide tile is 319 px wide and 105 px tall, 121 px with the no-amount line. Each part is 146 by 44 px.
- 768 px: the wide tile is 680 px wide and 55 px tall. Each part is 175 by 38 px.
- 1400 px (the page holds its content to 1000 px): the wide tile is 960 px wide and 55 px tall, the same height as the small tiles. Each part is 237 by 38 px.

Only Chromium is installed here, so no WebKit draw.
