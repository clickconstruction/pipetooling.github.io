# v2.7960 — Trips: the ground for joining a trip with the next (2026-11-19)

## Why

Join with the next is the last press on the trip record's mock-up (card #379), and the way back from **Split in two**, which has none. The owner said yes to eight rules for it on 2026-11-19. This is the database half, shipped alone so no deploy waits on its push. The press comes in the next PR.

## What changed

One migration, `20261119180000_trip_join.sql` (`docs/migrations/20261119180000_trip_join.md`). No table, column or index.

- **`trip_join(trip, next_trip, dry_run)`**: the joined trip through `trip_save`, the line's tie, then the delete of the next trip, in one call. It takes both ids and refuses a second that is not the next trip on the same home.
- **`trip_next_id(trip)`**: the trip that went out next on the same home, in the Trips look's own order.
- **`trip_join_line(trip, next_trip, move)`**: a definer with its own checks that reads and moves a line's tie, since the lines table's policies are narrower than the trips office.
- **`trips_archive_on_delete`** and **`trip_deleted_list`**, restated from prod's live bodies: a trip that `trip_join` deletes keeps `joined_into` beside its archive row, and the site's deleted trips hand it out.

Nothing on screen changes. No trip is joined until the press ships, so both restated functions answer as before.

## The eight rules

1. The next trip is the one that went out next on the same home: first day, then last day, then id. Same home is the same development, or the same job for a trip with no development.
2. The open trip keeps its id, number, words and via. Where what it was for, who asked or how it went is empty, it takes the next trip's. The next trip's number is not given again.
3. People merge by name, as `trip_save` tells them apart. Each keeps every day from both. On a day both give one person, the open trip's kind stays.
4. The jobs are both trips' jobs.
5. An empty billing takes the other's. Two different ones refuse until **Change** makes them match.
6. A line only the next trip holds is tied to the joined trip. A line on each refuses.
7. `ended_at` is the next trip's, so a trip still growing on the phone keeps growing.
8. The next trip is deleted and kept 90 days, marked with the trip it was joined into, so the screen can offer no **Put back** for it. A join is taken back with **Split in two**.

## Decisions

- **The migration ships alone.** A migration merged with the client that calls it holds every deploy until the push. Alone, it holds nothing.
- **Rule 8, the mark.** Put back as things stood would return the trip's days beside the joined trip, every one twice. The capture keeps `joined_into` beside the row, as it keeps `held_line_id`.
- **The line's step is a definer.** An invoker that could not read a line would lose its tie without a word when the next trip went.
- **The save does not refuse a joined trip made again.** Only the screen will hide the press.
