# v2.8599 — Hard Reload says Reloading… from the press (2026-11-25)

Punch list #406, from the simulator's fourth sitting on 2026-11-25, two recordings. Client only. No migration, no edge function.

**Seen.** The gear menu's **Hard Reload** closed the menu. The page then stood as it was for 3.3 and 3.4 seconds, every press on it live, and left. On v2.8562 the same press had left in under 1.5 seconds.

**The cause.** `hardReloadFromRoot` waits on `wipeBeforeLeaving`, which settles on the wipe or on `WIPE_DEADLINE_MS`, three seconds. `unregister()` waits behind a worker's install, and an install is longest right after a deploy. Five builds went live inside that sitting, so both presses left at the deadline. The deadline did its job. Nothing was drawn while it ran.

**The mend.** The moment the reload starts, the page draws *Reloading…* over itself: `ReloadingScreen`, a fixed cover on `document.body` with `role="status"`. It covers the page, so no other press lands, and it has no press of its own, since the reload always leaves. `Layout.tsx` raises it from the gear menu's press, inside the function it hands to `askReloadOverWords`. So over typed words the page is not covered while the person decides, and is covered once **Reload anyway** runs the reload.

**The deadline is as it was.** Three seconds is safe for the reasons in `hardReload.ts`, and v2.8562's walks rest on it.

**The other callers were read and left.** `RouteChunkBoundary` draws *Updating app…* on its own path, `PublicPage` draws its own leaving words, and `ForceReloadContext` is the reload a dev sends to everyone, with nobody's press to answer. Putting the words inside `hardReloadFromRoot` would have drawn two lines on the first two.

**Tests.** `Layout.hardReload.render.test.tsx`: the cover is on the page at the press, with its words and its role, drawn on the body. It is not drawn while the bar asks over typed words, it is drawn after **Reload anyway**, and it is not drawn after **Not now**.

**Not walked.** The recipe is looks only: press **Hard Reload** three times, one of them right after a deploy.
