# v2.7026 — Messages: old mail filed onto a job no longer sits in New as mail to answer (2026-11-12)

Punch list #324, PR 1 of 5: the database's half. The dev asked on 2026-11-12 why a batch of old email, tagged onto jobs so anyone could catch up, showed in Messages as new mail to answer.

## Why

Every line keeps when it was written (`at`) and when it landed (`created_at`). `messages_move_state`, the lanes and the move line read only the first. A mail from March filed in November opened its thread, asked *Who is this?* in New and read *Our move · 254 d*. The read of 2026-11-12 (counts only, every write refused): the tags of 2026-11-09 wrote 38 lines, 37 of them 3 to 12 months old, and left 4 stale threads open at the top of New.

## What changed

Migration `20261112180000_messages_history.sql` (`docs/migrations/20261112180000_messages_history.md`):

- **`messages.history`**: the mark on a mail filed for the record.
- **`email_write_line`** keeps the mark a caller sends. No caller sends it yet.
- **`messages_move_state`**: a filed mail of theirs opens nothing and holds nothing open. A filed mail of ours still answers.
- **`set_state`**: Reopen takes the mark off the thread's mail. A close also clears `has_until`, so **No reply needed** on a thread a tech held is no longer refused by `contacts_has_until_held`. Found while restating the function, not reported by anyone.
- **The mail already there** is marked by the same test the tag will use (from a person's own box, landed more than 14 days after it was written), and a thread only such mail opened goes back to Done.

## Changed from the card

Three things changed when the plan was read again against the code. The card's *Final changes* section has the reasons.

- The age rule belongs to the tag, not to `email_write_line`. A reader of a company box that fell 15 days behind would otherwise file real customer mail in silence.
- A filed mail of ours still counts as our answer.
- Reopen takes the mark off, so a reopened thread never reads Open with nothing waiting.

## How it was checked

- `supabase/tests/messages_follow_up/20_scenario.sql`, section 18, in the `messages-follow-up` bed: a filed mail of theirs opens nothing, a new mail after it opens the thread, a filed mail leaves a thread closed by a press Done, a filed mail of ours answers past a filed mail of theirs, Reopen takes the mark off, a close ends a hold.

- Nothing in the app reads the mark yet, so a filed thread still says *Our move* in its header until the next PR.
