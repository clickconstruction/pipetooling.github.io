# v2.7770 — Bill Customer: a smoke for the physical send (2026-11-18)

Tests only; no screen change, no migration, no edge function. `src/components/jobs/SendRecordInvoiceModal.tsx` is untouched. This closes the gap the map recorded while the window was being cut: render smokes pressed the HouseCall Pro **Save** and the Stripe submit, but nothing pressed **Email the invoice to …**. So the cuts that move or edit `submitPhysicalInvoiceEmail` (rows 18, 19 and 21 of the *Remaining cuts* table in `docs/SEND_RECORD_INVOICE_MODAL_ARCHITECTURE.md`) had no test under them.

## What shipped

- **`SendRecordInvoiceModal.physicalSend.render.test.tsx`** (10 cases). No PDF is built (`buildPhysicalInvoicePdfBlob` and `physicalInvoicePdfToBase64` are stubbed and keep the document they are handed). The edge function `send-physical-invoice-email` is a stub that keeps its body and headers and answers as each case asks, and so does `maybePromoteJobToBilledAfterCustomerInvoice`. One ordered log keeps every write and send the press makes. The clock is held at local noon on 18 November 2026, so the service date and the file name are fixed in any time zone.

## What is now pinned

- **The order of a kind:'job' send:** the PDF, then `ensure_single_ready_to_bill_invoice_for_job` once, then one send on the row it returned, then the promote, the measurement row (`ui_nav_clicks`), `onSuccess` and `onClose`, each once. Opening the window and the Physical tab writes nothing and sends nothing.
- **The whole send body on a plain send:** the row id, the job id, the amount, `sent_to_customer_at` at local noon of the service date, the memo box's words as `external_send_note`, the customer's email, the subject (`Click Plumbing Invoice [#978]`), the PDF and its file name (`Invoice-978-2026-11-18.pdf`), and the email's text and HTML. No `allow_rebill`, no `additional_emails` and no `extra_attachments` ride unasked. The call carries the session's bearer token.
- **What the office typed:** a line item override and a memo reach the PDF's document and the body, and a ticked contact and a one-off address ride as `additional_emails`.
- **A paid job's old draft (kind:'invoice'):** the press stays dead until **Bill this job again anyway**. Then the send carries `allow_rebill: true` on the draft's own row, with no ensure RPC (quirk 21).
- **A refused send**, as a 400 with its reason in the body or as an answer that carries one: the reason shows, nothing is promoted, the window stays open and the press comes back.
- **A promote that fails after the send:** its reason shows and the window stays open, before the measurement row (quirk 18).
- **The ensure refusing:** the amount left changed, every dollar is already on a bill, or the database answers with an error. The reason shows and nothing is sent.
- **A PDF over 5,500,000 characters of base64** is refused before anything is written or sent (quirk 14).
