# v2.7353 — The run closes a notice whose job was paid (2026-11-15)

Client only: no migration, no edge function. Taunya asked on 2026-11-15 whether **Take back…** was the way out for the notices v2.7337 holds, and picked this in its place. Take back only clears the printed stamp: the notice goes back to Ready to send with its approval, on a job that still owes $0, so the run lists it again, held one pile earlier. Nothing in the app set a desk notice aside; only a job combine did (`voided_at`).

## What changed

- `src/lib/jobs/lienRunClosePaid.ts` (+ test, 12): `runPaidNotices(notices)` splits the run's paid notices (`lienRunNothingToClaim`) into the ones a close takes and the ones it leaves with a reason — a retainage notice (*paid* is not its word) and a notice with a copy already out through Docsmit. The press's words come from `batchWords` (*Close 5 paid notices*, *Close 4 paid notices · 1 stays ▸*), the confirm's from `runClosePaidConfirm`, and the line kept on the item is `fields.runClosedPaid` — who, when, when it printed and what the paper claimed (`paperClaimOf`).
- `closePaidLienDeskItems` in `src/lib/jobs/lienDeskIo.ts` (+ `lienDeskIo.closePaid.test.ts`, 4): one write per notice, `voided_at` and the line, filtered to a monthly notice still live (`kind = notice_53_056`, not voided, not sent or missed). A notice that fails or moved meanwhile does not stop the rest; it comes back in `failed` with its reason. Every reader of the desk already skips a voided item, so the run, the pill, the To do view's run row and the piles drop it with no change of their own. A paid job has no months to draft (`lien_notice_months` lists a job only while it owes), so the job leaves the desk with its notice and returns by itself when it owes again.
- `src/components/jobs/LienRunChecklist.tsx`: the paid rows leave the working list for a fold below it, *5 paid notices · nothing to send ▾*, closed unless nothing else is in the run. The heading counts the live rows only. The fold's line carries **Close N paid notices…**; an open paid row carries its own **Close…**, or the reason it is left.
- `src/components/jobs/LienDeskRunModal.tsx`: the press only asks. The confirm names each notice and says what happens, with *Closing does not record a mailing. Leave a notice that went out.* under it. After, a green line says how many closed, and a notice that did not close stays listed with its reason (`batchOutcomeWords`). Escape closes the confirm first. `LienDesk.tsx` and `GcOnNoticeModal.tsx` each pass the one new prop, `onClosePaid`.
- Guide *send lien notices from the Lien desk*: the paid paragraph names the fold, and a new one, *Close a paid notice*.
