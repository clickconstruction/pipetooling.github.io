# v2.8439 — Document builder: Publish asks first (2026-11-24)

A small fault the #416 walk found and left (`to-dos/document-builder-whats-left.md`, walk 1g): **Publish revision 1** published at once, though a publish cannot be taken back and everyone outside then reads that revision. Switching the link off already asked. Diane's call of 2026-11-24: a publish asks. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## The cause

`WalkedBuilder` (`src/components/documentBuilder/WalkedBuilder.tsx`) sent the bar's press straight to `publishBuilder`, after saving what was typed last.

Reproduced before the change: the new render test, run against the old component, found no confirm window after **Publish revision 3**.

## What changed

- `walkedPublishConfirm` in `src/lib/documentBuilder/paperEdit.ts` writes the question, by the revision the press makes. From the second revision on: *Everyone outside sees revision 3 from now on. That is each GC you sent it to and anyone with the link. A publish cannot be taken back. A later revision can only replace it.* The first publish has nobody reading yet, so it says what revision 1 becomes: *Revision 1 is what a GC sees once you send it, and what the link opens.*
- The window's press reads **Publish revision 3**, the number the bar's press carries. Cancel reads **Not yet**, and it is the safe answer: it takes the focus, so Enter does not publish.
- The builder asks through the shared `useConfirmDialog`, before the last save and the publish. **Not yet**, Escape or a press outside publishes nothing and leaves the draft as typed.
- A dead press still opens no window: the button is disabled, and the handler reads `walkedPublishPress` again before it asks.
- The tone follows the link's **Switch the link off**: the act as the title, short sentences on what happens, the act on the press.
- Not changed: the insurance sheet's publish presses (`FilledBuilder`), which this fault did not name. Revision 1's own words, which name the placeholder company, are the owner's call.
- Help: *use the document builder*, **Publish a revision**, gains the window's two steps and the line that a publish cannot be taken back.
