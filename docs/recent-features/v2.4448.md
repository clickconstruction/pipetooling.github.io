# v2.4448 — Pricing: "Bids like this" moves up beside the bid's name (2026-10-02)

The owner, on BP403's Pricing tab the day v2.4418 shipped: "I don't like how much space this takes up. I would like for it to be less tall and here floating" — an arrow from the block to the title line, beside *Mark* and *For someone…*.

## What changed
- [`PricingBidsLikeThis`](../../src/components/bids/PricingBidsLikeThis.tsx) is no longer a card. It renders three chips and the *Compare N bids* button inline, and `BidsPricingTab` places it on the title line after `BidWorkflowTabTitleWithPreview`. The chips' numbers (the Workbench's effective revenue and margin) are derived inside the Workbench block further down, so that block portals the component into a slot in the header (`createPortal`; the slot is state, not a ref, so the chips draw on the first render after it mounts).
- *Compare* opens a floating panel (`role="dialog"`, 44 rem wide, clamped to the window's right edge) under the chips with the same content as before: the outcome bar, the bids won or lost on price by name, the GC's record, the margin scale. It closes with *Hide*, Escape, or a click outside. The per-device fold memory (`useBidsLikeThisFold`) is gone with the card; a floating panel is not something to leave open.
- Tests: the render smoke covers the closed line, the open dialog and the three ways to close it.

No migration, no edge function.
