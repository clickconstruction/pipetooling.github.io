# v2.8620 — Form Studio: a form a job requires or once required says it cannot be deleted (2026-11-26)

**Read.** `job_required_papers.form_template_id` references `contract_form_templates(id)` **ON DELETE RESTRICT** (`20261117220000_job_required_papers.sql`). A required paper's row is kept when the paper is taken off its job (`removed_at` is set), so a form any job requires or once required cannot be deleted: Postgres refuses with a foreign-key violation, `23503`. The other two references to a form (`contract_template_documents`, `person_contract_documents`) are `ON DELETE SET NULL` and do not refuse. In `FormStudio.tsx` the confirm read as if the delete would go through, and the refusal, drawn through `formatErrorMessage`, which has no words for `23503`, read Postgres's own words about a constraint and a table. `deleteFormTemplate` runs the delete before the storage removal, so a refused delete left the PDF in place: good, and unchanged.

**The mend.**

- `src/lib/forms/formDeleteHold.ts`, pure: `formHoldsByForm` counts the jobs that hold each form (a job once, however many of its papers point at it); `formDeleteHoldWords(n)` is the reason, *N jobs require this form or once required it. Their records keep it, so it stays.*; `isFormHeldByJobs(error)` reads the `23503` from `job_required_papers`, as a result's error object or an Error, and no other refusal; `FORM_DELETE_REFUSED_WORDS` for the race.
- `readFormHolds(formIds)` in `formTemplateRepo.ts`: one read of `job_required_papers` for the listed forms, removed rows included. Form Studio is the office's (a dev or the controller), who may read that table.
- `FormStudio.tsx`: after the list is read, the holds are; a held row's **Delete** is a dead press (`deadPress.ts`) with the reason as its title and in a line under the row's detail, and the press opens no confirm. A refusal the database sends anyway, a job having required the form between the read and the press, is said in the plain words and the holds are read again so the press goes dead.
