# v2.3433 — Demand letter: every line names its basis (2026-09-14)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. PR 3 of the *Demand letter, itemized* train (`to-dos/demand-letter-itemized/`; PR 1 v2.3425, PR 2 v2.3429). The statute reading behind each line is in the to-do's law table, verified against the statute text on 2026-09-14.

## Why

A homeowner's plumbing bill is a consumer debt, and Texas's Debt Collection Act binds the original creditor: it forbids threatening an action that is not available (Fin. Code § 392.301(a)(8)), collecting a charge no agreement or statute authorizes (§ 392.303(a)(2)), and saying fees "will" be added (§ 392.304(a)(13)). The letter threatened a Chapter 53 lien regardless of the window, said "late fees may accrue" with no basis, and never named the one date that changes what a lawyer can recover — the 30-day presentment clock under CPRC § 38.002.
