# v2.3189 — Costs tab: the Burn section (spend vs budget vs percent complete, overhead only in the projection) (2026-09-09)

Client only — no migration. First PR of the owner-approved mock-up *Burn, Budget and Stages* (artifact, 2026-09-09; owner: "I like everything you've presented … we should build it"). The question the section answers: **are we spending faster than we are finishing?** Overhead decision (owner, same day): keep it out of the burn signal, put it in the projection.

## What shipped

- **Kernel [`jobBurn.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobBurn.ts)** (11 tests) — pure. Inputs: the job's charge events (the Cost Timeline's six streams), its report % events, the job-level % fallback, the price, a budget, an optional overhead figure, today. Outputs, in the order the tiles read them:
  - **spent** = every charge (undated ones counted, flagged); **% done** = the latest dated report %, else the job's own % (`resolveJobCurrentPercentFallback`); **budget** via `resolveJobBurnBudget`: the bid estimate when snapshotted (not yet — always null today), else **price × (1 − target margin)** with the Job Summary Target chip as the rate and **35 %** when the chip is off (`JOB_BURN_DEFAULT_TARGET_MARGIN_PCT`).
  - **% of budget vs % done**, **lead points** (spend % − done %), **status**: `hot` when lead > 5, `early` while fewer than 3 field days or under 10 % (no projection is offered), `no_budget` without a price.
  - **cost at completion** = spent ÷ % done; **margin** = price − that; **burn rate** = direct $ per field day over the last ten field days (null after 30 idle days); **budget runs out** = remaining ÷ burn, walked forward in weekdays; **work left** = (100 − %) ÷ (% per field day so far).
  - **overhead, projection only**: given this job's day-share to date and per-field-day rate, projected remaining = rate × work-left days, and **true margin** = margin − share so far − projected remaining. Spent, burn, lead and EAC never see it (tested).
  - **series**: `daily` = the last 14 weekdays, zero-filled, split team / sub / parts with a trailing 7-weekday mean; `cumulative` = actual from the first dated charge to today, earned value stepping at each report (% × budget), then a forecast at today's burn until it meets the budget.
- **[`JobCostsBurnSection`](../../src/components/jobs/JobCostsBurnSection.tsx)** — four tiles (Spent to date · Budget · % of budget vs % done · At completion), the daily bars (recharts `ComposedChart`, stacked bars + average line), the cumulative chart (actual · earned value · dashed budget `ReferenceLine` · dotted forecast · a "budget gone" marker), and three rows (Burn rate · Budget runs out · Work left). The header names the budget rule and the % source. The At Completion sub-line carries the overhead: `+ $4,140 overhead share ($1,590 so far + 8 d × $318) → true margin $12,528 · 26%`. Hot tiles take a red rail; ok tiles read green.
- **Overhead source** — new hook [`useJobBurnOverhead`](../../src/hooks/useJobBurnOverhead.ts): `loadJobDayLedger` over [first charge day … today] capped at 120 days, then `allocateJobOverheadDayShare(ledger, jobId)` (Job Summary's day-share method); per field day = share ÷ this job's field days in the window. Fail-soft: the projection omits overhead on any error.
- **One fetch for Burn + chart** — the Cost Timeline's loader moved out of [`JobChargesTimelineStandalone`](../../src/components/jobs/JobChargesTimelineStandalone.tsx) into [`useJobChargesTimelineInputs`](../../src/hooks/useJobChargesTimelineInputs.ts) (an `enabled` flag lets a host preload); new [`JobCostsTabCharts`](../../src/components/jobs/JobCostsTabCharts.tsx) runs it once and renders Burn + the chart, and [`JobFormLaborCostPanel`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormLaborCostPanel.tsx) mounts that on the Costs tab. The standalone still self-fetches where it renders alone (the read-only Job Detail).
- **Roles** — Burn renders only for `showJobCostBreakdownTeamLabor` (dev / controller / master): without team labor the spend is not the spend the bid estimated. The chart's gating is unchanged.
