# v2.5845 — Quickfill: the round ends on a close sheet, and the Dashboard shows the office's round (2026-10-25)

Change 5 of punch list #176 (`to-dos/quickfill-needs-you-review/`), the last of the train after [v2.5837](v2.5837.md), [v2.5839](v2.5839.md), [v2.5841](v2.5841.md), [v2.5843](v2.5843.md) and [v2.5844](v2.5844.md). *Round done ✓ · Every group has been looked at today* was true after a six-minute stamp pass; it said nothing of what was still open, and the owner's Dashboard never heard that the round ran.

## What changed

- **The close sheet.** Kernel [`lib/quickfill/roundClose.ts`](../../src/lib/quickfill/roundClose.ts): `roundCloseSheet(events, ymd, label)` reads the company day's mark events, takes each station's last press and sorts them by the outcome change 1 writes: **clean** (done, or a count of zero before the column), **worked and still open** (the count, biggest first), **questions for the owner**, **looked** (no count). It times the round from the first press to the last and names whoever pressed most. `roundCloseTitle` (*Round done · 9:51 AM · 23 stations*) and `roundCloseSummary` (*11 clean · 8 worked and still open · 2 questions for the owner*). Nothing new is written; the events are the record.
- **Where it is drawn.** [`QuickfillRoundCloseSheet.tsx`](../../src/components/quickfill/QuickfillRoundCloseSheet.tsx) replaces the Roller's *Round done ✓* card (the *Show the N marked today* switch rides under it) and sits under the phone list's head once the queue is empty; every still-open station and every question is a door back into the station. The page's 60-day events read now carries the marker's name for the sheet.
- **The office's round on the Dashboard.** [`DashboardOfficeRoundCard.tsx`](../../src/components/dashboard/DashboardOfficeRoundCard.tsx), under the Needs you card for the office (`officeEligible`), over `officeRoundState`: *Today 9:51 AM · Dana · 23 stations · 8 still open · 2 questions* with the questions (**Answer**) and up to four open stations (**Open**) as doors into Quickfill; *No round yet today · Thursday 11:52 AM · 22 stations · 9 still open* before it runs; *No round Thursday · last round …* in red the morning after a missed workday. One read of the last ten days of `quickfill_section_mark_events` per visit.
- **The registry moved**: `QUICKFILL_SECTIONS` and its label map now live in [`lib/quickfill/sections.ts`](../../src/lib/quickfill/sections.ts), so the Dashboard can name a station without importing the page; the page's `SECTIONS` is the same array.
