# v2.6532 — A login can be taken away and the person stays (punch list #266) (2026-11-04)

Card #266. Since v2.6511 deleting an auth account detaches it — `on_auth_user_deleted` clears `users.login_attached_at` — instead of taking the person and every clock session with them; the bed's scenarios E and E2 proved it, but nothing in the app deleted a login, so a login that had to go was archived instead, and an archived person leaves every roster. No migration.

## What changed

- **`detach-login`** (new, dev only; `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`): `{ user_id }` → the auth account is deleted by the row's id, the trigger stamps the row (and the function stamps it too if the trigger did not), and the answer names what went and what stayed. The rules are a kernel, `_shared/detachLoginDoor.ts` (`detachUserIdOf`, `detachRowRefusal`, `DETACH_WORDS`), held by `src/lib/people/detachLoginDoor.test.ts`: refused for a malformed id, no such person, the caller's own account, a sample or twin, a person with no login. Deploy after the merge.
- **The desk's Sign-in row** gains **Remove the login…** for a dev (not on their own row), with a confirm (`confirmLabel: 'Remove the login'`) that names what goes — the sign-in, any password, their push devices — and what stays; `removeLogin` in `src/lib/people/attachLogin.ts` calls the function; the desk reloads to the Login row, where *Attach a login…* is the way back on the same id.
- **Guide** *invite someone to sign in* gains *Take a login away*; ACCESS_CONTROL.

## After the merge

`supabase functions deploy detach-login`; then the walk on a `ZZ Test` person: attach, remove, attach again — the id, the People link and a made-up clock session never change, `login_attached_at` NULL between.
