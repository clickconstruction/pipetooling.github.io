# v2.6412 — The Signed hours statement prints as one full-width paper (punch list #212) (2026-11-02)

Diane printed the first real statement (a primary's quarter, five pages) on 2026-11-02 and did not love how it looked: the facts (Worker, Period, Job, Customer, Site, Approved by) sat in a left column with the table squeezed into the right half of every page and the left column blank below them; in that narrow table *Day* wrapped to three lines, *In* and *Out* to two, the note header to five, and a free-text note ballooned its row; the week rows' subtotals sat under the wrong columns; the rate repeated on every row; hours printed as *8*, *2.17* and *26.17*; and the Paid block, the attestation and the signature lines were two-column at the foot. The in-app preview showed the same shape. Mock-up → critique → build in one session, the owner's calls of 2026-10-29 untouched. Client only: no migration, no function, the kernel unchanged, the window untouched (the sibling train PRs 3–5 owns it and makes PR 3's pdf-lib paper follow this layout).

- **The root cause of the two-column print was one missing `</div>`** on the Worker line of the facts block in `buildHoursStatementHtml.ts`: the facts grid (`grid-template-columns: 1fr 1fr`) never closed, so the table, the Paid block, the attestation and the signature lines all became cells of a two-column grid. The builder is rewritten rather than patched, since the squeeze was only the first of the list.
- **The paper's shape now** (`src/lib/peopleDocuments/buildHoursStatementHtml.ts`): the company header and the title as before; the facts as one band across the width in three columns (Worker · Period · Rate / Job · Customer · Approved by / the Site on its own line); the table full width with one line per day — *Day · In – Out · Hours · What they were working on · Amount* — a colgroup fixing the narrow columns so the note takes the rest; a week heading row spanning Day and In – Out with its subtotals under Hours and Amount; the totals row the same way; then **Paid**, the stated block, the attestation and the two signature lines as full-width bands, each pay line the week in bold with the rest of the kernel's words after it. The preview in the window (`<iframe srcDoc>`, about 830 px) shows the same string, so `.sheet` is `width: 100%; max-width: 8.5in` on screen and the paper fills the frame.
- **The rate is stated once when it is the same on every counted row** (`hoursStatementUniformRate`): *Rate · $50.00 an hour* in the band and no Rate column; when the rate changes inside the period (two pay reports at two rates, a sheet with two line rates) the band says nothing and the Rate column is drawn per row. That phrase is the one new wording on the paper. A salaried person's paper (the schedule kind) and the hours-only paper carry no rate, as before.
- **Hours always to two decimals** (`hoursStatementHours2`: *8.00*, *2.17*, *26.17*) in the table, the week rows, the totals and the stated rows. The kernel's own sentences (*1 session of 4 h waiting for approval*) are the kernel's and keep its words.
- **A long note drops to its own line under its day** (`HOURS_STATEMENT_NOTE_FITS`, 68 characters; 58 with the Rate column drawn): the day row keeps its one line with an empty note cell and a `tr.nt` row under it carries the words across the width, the two kept together at a page break.
- **Page breaks.** `thead` repeats the column header on every page; `tr { break-inside: avoid }` keeps a row whole; `tr.wk, tr.hasnote { break-after: avoid }` keeps a week heading, or a day, with the row under it; `.attest, .sig { break-before: avoid }` keeps the attestation and both signature lines with what is above them, so a signature line never opens a page alone (on the real quarter this moves the Paid band onto the signature page whole, the table ending page 2 on its totals row). Proven in Chromium's print engine, which is what Chrome's print uses: a sweep of filler rows that walks a week heading and a day-plus-note pair across the page boundary never split either.
- **A sub's paper** drops the Sheet column: the sheet is already the heading row above its lines, so the columns are *Day · Line · Hours · [Rate] · Amount*.
- **What stays as the card decided.** Letter, light (`data-theme="light"`), the Costs packet's page rule, the stamp and *Page N of M* in the margin boxes, the company header, the record id, every word the kernel gives, and no column saying how a row was recorded.
- **The mock-up** `to-dos/signed-hours-statement/mockup-print-v2.html` (made-up names and amounts, a 57-day quarter) is the static export the paper was built from; the card's *Where it stands* names the restyle.
- **Tests.** `buildHoursStatementHtml.test.ts` rewritten to the structure: the facts band closes before the table and nothing is a two-column grid; the band's cells and the rate once; the table's header, a day row, the pending row, the week row and the totals row cell by cell; the page-break rules; a long note's own line; the Rate column only on a changing rate; Paid as a band; the hours-only paper with no Amount column; two-decimal hours; the sub's sheet by its heading row. The window's render smoke and the people kernels' tests pass unchanged.
