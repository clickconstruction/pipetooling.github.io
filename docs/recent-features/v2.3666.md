# v2.3666 — Job → Edit: the appraisal roll's owner reads as a suggestion, laid out like the envelope (2026-09-21)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. The Job modal's twin of v2.3658 (the Lien desk's owner envelope); reuses its kernel.

## Why

The owner, from a screenshot of job 883's Edit tab: *"I don't love how this looks on the Job modal, is this the best we can do?"* — then, of the mock-up, *"will that work on all viewports?"* The review in the session:

1. **Green said "done", but nothing was saved.** The box is a suggestion; the row above it said *not linked*. The two contradicted each other.
2. A form inside a form — its own label/value grid at different column widths from the rows around it.
3. The same address problems the desk had: the districts' raw `%` (care of), *Irvine, Ca*, a wrap mid-street; the Legal line dragged the district and year along and orphaned "2025".
4. Three actions stacked on three lines, the button oversized with a sentence for a label.
5. *mail elsewhere*, an unexplained chip.

## What shipped

- [`JobFormOwnerLookupBox.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormOwnerLookupBox.tsx) — all three states are one neutral card (`.ownerCard`): *Looking <street> up…*; **found** — caption (*The appraisal roll's answer for <street>* · district · year · *Check this parcel on <county> CAD ↗*), the owner and mailing address as an `<address>` from `rollMailingLines` (c/o from the string, else the roll's `nameCare`), a `dl` beside it (Legal as the roll writes it — abbreviations do not survive title-casing — County, *Reads as*: the landlord / public / homestead chips, and *Mails somewhere other than the job site* as a phrase), the red homestead and public-owner lines unchanged, then **Save this owner** · *Not right? Paste the CAD page…* · *Saves to the property record for <street> and links this job*; **miss** — *The appraisal roll has no answer for <street>*, the reason, and the paste door. What is saved is untouched.
- `onSuggestion(waiting)` — the box tells its parent when a found answer is waiting; [`JobFormEditFactRows.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormEditFactRows.tsx) shows *Not linked yet* · **1 suggestion** on the Property record row. The after-create prompt mounts the same box and gets the card with no change.
- **Viewports** — `.ownerCard` in [`index.css`](../../src/index.css) is its own size container, because the modal's width is not the window's. The breakpoint is **400px of card**, not the mock-up's 460: measured live, the desktop sheet is 560px and the card's inside about 450px, so 460 had put the desktop in the phone layout. Under 400px the facts drop below the address, the CAD link and the note take their own lines, and Save is a full-width 44px target. Address and fact lines wrap (`overflow-wrap: anywhere`) rather than ellipsize — on a form you need the whole owner name before saving it.
- Guide *file a lien and never miss its deadlines* → "Earlier still — on the job itself" redrawn.

- Dev server on prod data, job 883 → Edit: desktop sheet — card 479px, two 214px columns, every address line on one line, card 254px tall (384 when it was wrongly stacked); 375px and 320px viewports — one column, Save 44px and full width, no line wraps, no horizontal scroll on the card or the page. A contrast walk over the card and the row's chip: 23 text runs, none under 4.5:1 in light or dark.
