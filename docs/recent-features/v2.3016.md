# v2.3016 — Paste the CAD page, the parcel deep link, and a surer parcel hit (2026-09-07)

Customer properties train, PR 4 of 4 (v2.3004 lookup · v2.3008 primary row · v2.3009 modal). No migration; **redeploy `property-lookup`**.

## What shipped

- **Paste the CAD page** ([`cadPagePaste.ts`](../../src/lib/customers/cadPagePaste.ts), +7 tests): when the statewide roll has nothing under the pin, the record panel's *Paste the CAD page…* opens a box; select-all-copy on the district's property page, paste, and the parser lifts **legal description** (across its wrap), **owner name** (the first, not the deed-history repeat), **mailing address**, **Prop ID** and the **exemptions line** — "HS" sets homestead, an exemptions line without it clears it, no line leaves it alone. *Use these* applies them (a company-looking owner lands in Owner company with Building owner pre-selected) and stamps the provenance `<County> CAD (pasted)`. Written against the BIS "esearch" page shape (Comal, Hays, Guadalupe, Kendall and the rest of the service area) and plain `Label: value` rows (Bexar's TrueAutomation, spreadsheets).
- **Deep link to the parcel**: `txCountyCadPropertyUrl(county, propId)` — once the lookup has the district's Prop ID, the CAD link opens **that parcel's page** instead of the search form: `esearch.<district>/Property/View/<id>` for every BIS county, `bexar.trueautomation.com/clientdb/Property.aspx?cid=110&prop_id=<id>` for Bexar (both verified live 2026-09-07). Other counties keep the search link. The prefilled *keyword search* URL the mock-ups floated is out: BIS bounces a GET search to its "session expired" page.
- **A surer parcel hit**: the identify call in `property-lookup` was sized so tightly the layer's scale rule returned nothing for points a sweep across Comal, Hays and Bexar found fine at a normal extent. It now asks at ±0.005° / 400px, tolerance 1px first (the containing polygon only) and 3px (≈8 m) when a geocoded pin lands on the street.
- Guide section gains the paste and deep-link steps.
