# v2.4806 — Lien desk: the stop window says where you are and what blocks it (2026-10-07)

**The ask (the owner, 2026-10-07):** after v2.4797 put *Don't send yet, then the one thing to do first* on the desk's draft panes, on the stop windows: *or I should show them some visual that signals where this preview is and where they are and what is blocking it* — then *collapsible and part of the bottom where it says don't send yet. How could I offer this tastefully?* Mock-up board 6 (the board folds and unfolds on press) https://claude.ai/artifact/MJnFkdKZEAhiM9RjL628M3, "please add it".

**The idea.** One footer with two states, and the path's own dots as the handle. The handle is the content, not a label: seven dots with one ringed is the path already, and pressing a path to see the path needs no explanation.

## What shipped

- **The strip** (`LienTimelineStrip`): `lit` rings the stop a window is open on (`data-lien-timeline-lit`, the blue ring, in every layout); `blocked` draws the red chip under it (`data-lien-timeline-blocked`, *✗ owner of record missing*, the row layout). `LienTimelineNode` is exported for the dots.
- **The window** (`LienStopPaperWindow`): takes the whole `timeline` and a `holdFor(step)`. The footer (`lien-stop-paper-foot`, `data-hold`) is one band in two states: folded, the hold line or the plain line, then at the right the dots (`lien-stop-paper-dots`: every stop's node, this one ringed, *stop 2 of 7 ▸*) and the key hints; open, the path (`lien-stop-paper-path`: the strip's row layout with the lit stop and the red chip, *the blue ring is this preview · the dashed line is today · the red mark is what blocks it*) above the line, the dots reading ▾. The fold is remembered in the browser (`lienStopPathOpen`), folded by default. With the path the header's *stop N of M* moves to the dots. The hold line is amber: ⚠, the two sentences, the gate's door (`lien-stop-paper-hold`, `lien-stop-paper-hold-act`).
- **The words** ([`lienDeskGates.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDeskGates.ts)): `lienGateShortWords` (the chip: *owner of record missing*, *owner has no mailing address*, *no GC on the job*, *no month picked*, *property kind unknown*), `lienAffidavitFootBlockedSentence` (*Don't file yet.* then the one thing: the owner and the mailing address, the county and the legal description, the § 53.056 notice first — a late one counts while the window is open — or the attorney on a homestead, § 53.254) and `lienAffidavitGateShortWords`.
- **The hosts.** The desk's notice pane: a live notice stop's first blocking gate through v2.4797's `lienFootBlockedSentence`, the door *Go to gate N ▴* (closes the window, scrolls the pane to the gates). The Affidavit pane: the first gate not clear through the affidavit sentence, *Fix the property record ›* where that is the fix, *Open it on Notices ›* when the notice is. The job's Lien window, counsel's portal and the Legal desk pass the timeline for the path and no hold.

## Decisions

- Folded by default, always: the hold line already says what to do; the dots carry the place.
- A stop that went out, or whose window closed, shows no hold line — there is nothing to do first.
- On a phone the path's legend line is dropped; the dots and the hold line stay, since the hold line is what changes what someone does next.
