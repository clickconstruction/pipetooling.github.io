# v2.6406 — Materials on site: bring back a cage or box retired by mistake (2026-11-03)

Punch list #209, the owner's question of 2026-11-03: *if someone retires a box, is there a way to bring it back?* There was not. `retireContainer` set `job_containers.retired_at` and nothing ever cleared it; both pages, the search and the QR lookup skip retired rows, so a mistaken retire meant a new box and a new label.

## What changed

- **The kernel** [`lib/materials/unretire.ts`](../../src/lib/materials/unretire.ts) (pure, 8 tests): `unretireVerdict(containers, id)` says whether a retired box can come back — refused with its words when it is not on the job, already live, or when a live container on the same job carries its stencil now (stencils compared without case or spaces, as the search reads them); `retiredContainers` (newest retire first, never Unsorted); the words for the toast, its Undo, the Bring back press and the fold's heading; `UNDO_RETIRE_MS` = one minute.
- **The store** `unretireContainer(id)` in `onSiteStore.ts`, the mirror of `retireContainer`: cache first, then `retired_at: null` through `afterWrite`.
- **The phone** (`TallyOnSiteTab.tsx`): the *G4 retired.* toast is now `showActionToast` with **Undo** for a minute. Undo runs the verdict, warns with its words if the stencil was taken meanwhile, else brings the box back and says *G4 is back on the job. Its label works again.*
- **The office** (`JobsMaterialsTab.tsx`): [`RetiredBoxesFold`](../../src/components/materials/RetiredBoxesFold.tsx) under the container list, a `<details>` headed *Retired · N*; each row names the box, its kind and the day it was retired (the page's `Stamp`), with **Bring back G4** or the refusal's words. Smoke: `RetiredBoxesFold.render.test.tsx`.
- **Docs**: both Materials guides (the phone's new *Retire a box* section, the office's new *Bring back a retired box* section), `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`'s Jobs → Materials line, `docs/twins/APP_DIRECTORY.md`, and the #209 card.

## Decisions

- **No confirm on Bring back.** Retiring is the guarded step (refused while parts remain, then a second press); coming back is its reversal and can be retired again at once, so one press and a toast.
- **Refuse a stencil clash rather than renumber.** Two live G4s on one job would make the label's QR ambiguous and the manifest's stencil a lie; the fold says which live box holds the stencil and leaves the choice to the office.
- **No migration.** `retired_at` was nullable from the start; the fence policies and read-only blocks already cover the update.
- **The ledger is untouched** either way: a retire never wrote a movement, so neither does the return.

## Left

- The Retired fold lists every retired box of the job for good. If a long job gathers dozens, a *show the last 10* cut is a one-liner on `retiredContainers`.
