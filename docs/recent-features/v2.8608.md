# v2.8608 — Word to every trade: one email a person when the mail service refuses the one message (2026-11-25)

Punch list #205, the mend its two live checks of 2026-11-24 turned up. One edge function, `gc-trade-email`, which **owes a deploy** after the merge. No migration.

## What was wrong

A trade email goes as one message: the first person on *To* and the rest on *CC*. The mail service refuses the whole message when it refuses one address. So one bad address at a company stopped **Word to every trade** for everyone there, no row was written, and the function's log said nothing.

## The call

Asked *When the mail service refuses one address, the whole word fails today. What should it do?*, the owner answered "Send one email a person (Recommended)" on 2026-11-25. The two ways not taken: leave out an address on a reserved test domain before the send, or leave the send as it was.

## What changed

- **`supabase/functions/_shared/gcTradeEmailAlone.ts`**, new and pure. `sendTradeEmailToReach` tries the one message first. When the service refuses it and the caller asked (`eachAlone`), it sends each person the email alone, keeps going past a person the service refuses, and answers the emails that went and each person held with the reason. Nobody reached is a refusal, as before. `tradeEmailOwed` is who a second press still owes, and `tradeNamesWith` the row's names after it. The file sits beside `_shared/gcTradeEmail.ts` and not in it: four functions bundle that one, and only `gc-trade-email` bundles this.
- **`gc-trade-email`** reads `eachAlone` off the request and sends through the kernel. The row's `to_names` are the people the emails that went reached. Each email that went has its own log line and its own sent copy. The answer gains `emails` and `held`. A send that reached nobody, and each person held, is a line in the function's log.
- **A second press of the same words** sends nothing to a person on the row. A person who gets these emails and is not on it is sent the email alone, and the row gains the name. That is the retry for a person held the first time.
- **The window** (`GcWordToTrades.tsx`, `wordToTradesIo.ts`, `wordToTrades.ts`) asks for `eachAlone`, counts emails in its outcome line, lists each held person as *company, person: reason* under the companies not reached, and says how to send to them. It keeps the words of a press that left someone out while the page is open, and **Back to the words** returns to them.
- Every other caller of the function sends no `eachAlone` and is sent to as before. Their screens do not list a held person, so they must not get a send that went to some.

## What a record means after this

- **The message row** (`gc_trade_messages`): still one for the word a company. `to_names` holds who it reached and never a person who was held. A second press that reaches someone adds the name to the same row: the first update this function makes there.
- **The mail log** (`email_send_log`): one line for each email the service took. A refused email never had a line and still has none. The row's `email_send_log_id` is the first email's.
- **The sent copy** (`sent_documents`): one for each email that went, with its own recipient. A person who was held has none.
- **Who was not reached** is not stored in a column. It is read as the people who get the job's emails and are not in `to_names`, which is what a second press sends to. The function's log names the reason for a few days.

## Limits

- The row keeps names and no addresses. Two people of one name at a company count as one on a second press, and neither is sent to twice.
- A person added to the company after the first send gets the word on a second press of the same words.
- Only a refusal the mail service gives at the send is caught. An address the service takes and that bounces later is not.
- The client ships with the merge and the function with its deploy. Between the two the window reads the old answer, and a refused address stops the word to that company as before.

## Tests
