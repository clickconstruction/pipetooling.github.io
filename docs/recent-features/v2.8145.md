# v2.8145 — Papers before a job opens: the ground for a job's own settings (2026-11-20)

Punch list #389, step 3 of eight, the first half: the migration and its bed. No screen changes and no function is deployed. The kernel, the sign path and the signed record come in the next PR, after this migration is pushed.

**Push**: `20261120163000_settings_on_the_job.sql`, from `main` after the merge. It is safe under the app and the functions deployed today: the app calls `job_papers_require` with the old two names and gets the default, and the functions refuse every paper built from settings until they are redeployed.

## What changed

- A job's required paper can carry **settings** (`job_required_papers.settings`, with the typed blanks in `fields`). They are null on every paper a job requires today.
- `job_papers_require` takes a paper built from settings **with its picks**, and refuses it without. A plain paper takes none and is required as before.
- `job_paper_set_settings` changes them. **Until someone signs**, in place. **After the first signature the row is locked**: a change makes a new row that everyone signs again, and the old row keeps its signatures.
- **The read-revision rule**: a job that is not a ZZ Test job takes such a paper only at the revision an attorney read. It holds where the paper is required, where its settings change, and where it is read or signed.
- `job_paper_clauses` is the job's paper as the database puts it together, with the picks in plain words and a **seal**, a SHA-256 of its settings, blanks and clauses with their versions.
- A copy of such a paper is made for the job's row and still never bare. **It is signed only with the paper frozen on it exactly as the database reads it at that moment.**

## One pick taken from the card

*A change after signing starts* is open on the card, with the pick *allowed, as a new revision*. This step takes it, and holds a change after the first signature to **stricter only**. A looser paper is taken off the job and required again. Flipping it is one refusal in `job_paper_set_settings`.

## What it does not do yet

- No screen sets a job's settings. That is step 4, which waits on the look.
- The edge functions do not read a job's settings. Until `accept-contract` and `get-contract-for-signer` are redeployed with the next PR, a job can require such a paper and nobody can open or sign it.
- The signature's *Signing for* line needs nothing here: card #403 keeps it on the copy at the send (v2.7977, v2.7986), and its step 5 fills it from a sub's own company.
