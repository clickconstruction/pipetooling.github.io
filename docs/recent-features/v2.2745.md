# v2.2745 — Timeline: Color by state on the day, or by run length (2026-09-03)

Owner-approved plan (PR 1 of 2) after the Timeline review: keep every job on one chart and fix readability with coloring, not by hiding series. A **Color by** control on Jobs → Job Summary → Timeline with three choices over the same running counts.

## Client only — no migration

- **Kernel** [`lib/jobs/jobRunningTimeline.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobRunningTimeline.ts) (+5 tests): `JobRunRow` carries `billedYmd` / `paidYmd` from the ledger's status spans; `JobRunColorBy` = `status` | `stateOnDay` | `runLength`; `JOB_RUN_BANDS_BY_COLOR` fixes the stack order per coloring (bottom → top: working/billed/paid, or 6+ days / 2–5 / 1 day — the calm carry sets the floor, v2.2734's rule); `stateOnDay(row, ymd)` (working until the Billed move, billed until the Paid move, paid after); `runLengthBand(runDays)`; `buildRunningSeriesBy(rows, days, today, colorBy)` (per-day `counts` per band, same totals/peak/average as before — `buildRunningSeries` stays as the status-shaped wrapper); `colorSegmentsForRow(row, colorBy)` splits a bar at the Billed and Paid moves for state-on-the-day.
- **Ledger** [`jobDayLedger.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobDayLedger.ts) + loader: `JobDayLedgerStatusSpan` gains optional `billedYmd` / `paidYmd` (first Billed / first Paid move from `job_status_events`); session cache key bumped to `jobDayLedger:v4:`.
- **Prefs** [`jobSummaryLedgerView.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobSummaryLedgerView.ts): `timelineColorBy` (default `status`), persisted with the other view prefs.
- **View** [`JobSummaryTimelineView.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobSummaryTimelineView.tsx): Color by segmented control; the area stack, legend, per-day tooltips, and the bars panel all follow the chosen bands (state-on-the-day bars change color at the Billed and Paid moves); a note when the chosen coloring is state-on-the-day but no job in the window has a recorded move. Band colors: working/6+ blue, billed amber, paid green, 2–5 days purple, 1 day cyan.
