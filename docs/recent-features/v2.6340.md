# v2.6340 — The courtesy notice window makes no Messages contact until a text goes (2026-11-02)

Punch list #243, PR 1 of two. The courtesy notice's live test (2026-11-01, #231) found that opening *Send a courtesy notice* made the GC a Messages contact before anything was sent: `loadCourtesyRecipients` (`src/lib/jobs/lienCourtesyIo.ts`) opened the GC's thread with `contactForCustomer`, whose RPC `contact_for_customer` → `contact_find_or_make` makes the contact when there is none. A window opened and cancelled, or a send by email alone, left an unnamed contact with one channel and no messages. Two such *ZZ Test* contacts came out of the live test. Client only: no migration, no function change.

- **The window reads, it never makes.** `findCustomerContactId` (new in `src/lib/messages/messagesIo.ts`) selects the customer's own contact — on their `customers` row, with no name, the same row `contact_find_or_make` finds first — and answers null when there is none. With a contact, the window reads its channels as before. Without one, it reads `customers.contact_info` (`phone`, `email`), which is exactly what `contact_find_or_make` copies onto the contact it makes, so the plan shows a text as going exactly when the press will send one.
- **The kernel `src/lib/jobs/lienCourtesyContact.ts`** holds the rule: `phoneChannelKey` mirrors `contact_channel_key('phone', …)` (digits, a leading 1 dropped from eleven, at least seven), so a record number the spine would keep no channel for is never planned as a text; `courtesyGcChannels` gives the number, the hold (Send texts off, STOP) and the channel, with `contactId` and `textChannelId` null while there is no contact; `textChannelFor` finds the planned number on the contact the press made, by key however it is written, else the first live number.
- **The press makes the contact, only when a text goes.** `sendCourtesyNotice` takes `gcCustomerId`; for a recipient whose text goes with no contact yet, `textDoorFor` calls `contactForCustomer` (the office's own door), reads the new contact and texts the channel that holds the number. A refusal to make it (a training account, a twin) or a contact with no number that can be texted is the text's reason in the record and the toast; the email still goes. Before, a text with no contact was skipped in silence.
- **The modal** (`LienDeskModal.tsx`) carries the GC's customer id in the window's context and hands it to the press. The window component is unchanged.
- **Guide** *send a courtesy notice before a lien*: step 3 says a GC with no contact shows the record's number; a new paragraph says opening the window and an email alone make nothing, and a text makes the contact when it goes.
- **Tests**: `lienCourtesyContact.test.ts` (the key, the three contact states, the record's number, the channel on the new contact); `lienCourtesyIo.test.ts` (opening with no contact makes none and reads the record; with a contact reads it and not the record; email alone makes none; a text makes the contact and texts the channel on it; an existing contact is texted and not remade; a refusal and a numberless contact are the text's reason); `messagesIo.test.ts` gains the finder (a read, no RPC, no write). Not covered by a render smoke: the modal's one new field, held by the typecheck.
- **Left for the owner**: the two stray *ZZ Test Builder Co* contacts from the live test are deleted by hand (one line, no messages on them).
