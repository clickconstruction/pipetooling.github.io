# v2.8231 — Dashboard on a phone: Your people fits the screen (the walk of 2026-11-21) (2026-11-21)

The owner's main session walked the full Dashboard as the Sample leader (Settings → View as… → Leader) on a 375 px browser on 2026-11-21, looking only. The page was 2 px wider than the phone: `main.appMain` had a scroll width of 377, and the browser's own width read 377 at the phone preset. Client only: no migration and no function.

## The cause

One row of the **Your people** card. A row is a ring, the person's name with their open card under it, and at the right a chip with **🔔 Remind**. The chip and the press were one group with `flexShrink: 0`, so it could neither shrink nor wrap, and the name beside it was `flex: 1` with nothing it had to keep.

Read on the dev server at 375 px, the row has 247 px for the name and the group. A short chip (*stuck 208 days*) made the group 199 px and left the name a column 48 px wide, its words one to a line. The long chip (*nothing moved · oldest 1 days*) made the group 284 px: the name's column was 0 px wide and 130 px tall, and Remind's right edge stood at 376.8 px, 27 px out of its card and 1.8 px past the screen.

## The fix, in the row and not on the page

[`src/lib/dashboardYourPeopleRow.ts`](../../src/lib/dashboardYourPeopleRow.ts) holds the row's three style objects, and `DashboardYourPeopleSection.tsx` draws them:

- **The row wraps.**
- **The name keeps 9 rem** before it gives any room up (`flex: 1 1 9rem`), so a chip and a press that do not fit beside it drop to a line of their own, at the right.
- **The chip and Remind may shrink** to the row (`flex: 0 1 auto`, `max-width: 100%`), and then the press goes under the chip.

No overflow is hidden anywhere.

Read after the change, as the Sample leader: at 375 px the page and `main.appMain` are 375 wide, every row is 89 px tall with the name and the card's title on a line each and the chip and Remind under them, inside the card. At 320 px the page is 320 wide, and the long chip's row puts Remind under the chip (117 px tall). At desk width every row is the one 58 px line it was, with the group at the right end: each box read the same with the old styles put back on it.
