# v2.7432 — Lien desk: open or save one page of a notice by itself (2026-11-16)

Taunya's ask of 2026-11-15, on the bar *Page 1 of 3 · cover letter* of a monthly notice: *"at the top of each of these letters I would like export tools to download them as PDFs or open them in a new tab where it is just the letter"*. The lien desk session drew two ways to place the presses, and she approved the drawing with the two quiet presses at the bar's right end. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What a person sees

- **Two presses on every page bar** of a monthly notice: the cover letter, the conditional release, the notice and the pay codes. **Open ↗** (*Open this page alone in a new tab, as it prints*) and **PDF ↓** (*Save this page as a PDF*). They sit beside the bar's own button, so the rest of the bar still scrolls to its page. A long note on the bar is cut with an ellipsis before it reaches them.
- **Open ↗** opens the page in the browser's own PDF viewer. The tab reads *Making the PDF…* until the file lands. A blocked popup says so in a toast.
- **PDF ↓** saves the same file. While it is made the press reads *Making the PDF…* and neither press can be pressed.
- **The name** is the job's number, its name and the page: *523 Mission Hills, cover letter.pdf*. The tab's title is the same words.
- **The draft line.** Until the notice is approved (the piles To send, Awaiting approval, Held, Needs an owner and Missed), the foot of every exported page reads *Draft, not sent · November 15, 2026*, with the day it was made. Once approved, the line is gone.
- **Keeping a copy.** A **PDF ↓** on an approved, printed or sent notice files the file under Sent from this job as *Saved as a file* (`lien_notice_page`, `how: 'download'`). A draft's download is not filed. **Open ↗** is a look and is never filed.
- **A phone** draws the arrows alone, 32 px tall in the 40 px bar. Each keeps its name for a screen reader: *Open the cover letter in a new tab*, *Save the cover letter as a PDF*.
- **Who.** Nothing here writes to the notice, so training mode and every role that opens the desk can export.

## How a page is made

`src/lib/jobs/lienPageExport.ts` is the pure kernel. For a page key it says:

- **the recipe** (`lienExportRecipe`), the builder and options `buildRunNoticePdf` uses for that part, so an exported page is the page that mails: the cover through `filingDocPdfBlob` with `fit: 'one-page'` and no citation, the notice and the pay page through `filingDocPdfBlob` with `filingDocFooter('notice_53_056')`, the release through `buildLienWaiverPdfBlob`;
- **the name** (`lienExportTitle`, `lienExportFileName`), safe for a file: no path or reserved marks, one space between words, the job's words held to 80 characters;
- **the draft line** (`lienExportIsDraft`, `lienExportDraftLine`) from the notice's pile;
- **where the line sits** (`lienExportDraftStamp`);
- **what a download files** (`lienExportFiling`), null for a draft.

`src/lib/jobs/lienPageExportIo.ts` draws. `buildLienPagePdf` makes the page with its builder. `finishLienPagePdf` then opens the finished PDF with pdf-lib, sets its title, and on a draft draws the line on every page in bold dark amber.

## The draft line is stamped, so no builder changed

The brief asked whether the release's builder could carry the draft line, and said to leave it alone if not. Stamping the finished PDF answers it for all four pages: `filingDocPdfBlob` and `buildLienWaiverPdfBlob` are untouched, and so is everything the run mails.

- The cover's foot holds only the page number, so the line starts at the left margin, as the drawing shows it.
- The notice and the pay page hold the § 53.056 citation at the left, and the release holds its form's title there. On those the line is centred at 135 mm, in the clear middle of the foot, on the foot's own baseline (272 mm, and 274 mm on the release).

## The pane

`LienDesk.tsx`'s `pageLabel` calls `pageExport(key)`, which draws `LienPageExportPresses` after the bar's button. The page's content is what the pane already holds: `coverBlocks`, `payBlocks`, `noticeRelease`, and the notice's blocks as `previewInput()` builds them, with no ghost lines. Styles are `.lienPageExport` in `src/index.css`, beside `.lienPileHead`.
