# v2.4320 — Returned checks: the office hears about every one, on a job or not (2026-10-01)

Punch list **#76** PR 2 of 5 (`to-dos/ar-returned-checks.md`; canvas https://claude.ai/artifact/9qbiCTmbaXnfPVKhFULoCN). Migration `20261001230000_ar_returned_check_cases.sql` ([doc](../migrations/20261001230000_ar_returned_check_cases.md)); new edge function **`ar-returned-checks`**; **`mercury-webhook`** redeploys.

## What was wrong

`mercury_bank_return_notices` held zero rows on 2026-10-01: the notice (v2.3804) fired only while a recorded payment still carried the check, and only from the webhook. Read against 19 months of prod:

- **Loberg's $5,622.49** was taken off #650 on 9/30 and stopped by the bank on 10/1. No payment carried it, so nobody was told.
- **Peter Garza's $2,700** came back for *Insufficient funds* in July 2025 with no posting date. The rule (posted, then failed) never counted it; #120 still reads paid on a $2,700 HCP payment.
- **Sal Iannotti's $600** — Mercury could not take the check in on 9/25. $600 was recorded on #1040 on 9/29 with no deposit. #1040 reads paid in full; no $600 has arrived.
- The Banking page's **Sync** (`sync-mercury-transactions`) writes the same `failed` status and sends nothing.

## What changed

- **The rule** (`_shared/bankReturnedDeposits.ts`): `mercuryBankReturn` takes `kind`; a `checkDeposit` that failed before posting counts when Mercury's words are a bank's return reason (`BANK_RETURN_REASON_PHRASES`, `isBankReturnReason`). Mercury's *There was an issue with this transaction* is not one. The same eleven phrases are `mercury_bank_return_reason()` in SQL; a test reads the migration to keep them equal. `useBankReturnedPaymentsNudge` drops its `posted_at` filter and lets the rule decide; `mercuryBankReturnFromRaw` takes the kind.
- **The case** (migration): `mercury_transaction_ar_returned` gains source (bank · hand · rejected), reason, opened, closed (replaced · settled another way · not coming), closed by, note, replaced by. A trigger on `mercury_transactions` opens a bank case whichever writer stores the return. The backfill makes a case of every return already stored and closes as *replaced* the four with a later same-payer, same-amount check (Dudley, M&M Roofing, Poolcorp twice). `open_ar_rejected_check_cases()` opens a *rejected* case only when a hand-recorded payment matches and the check was not deposited again within 5 days. `list_ar_return_cases()` returns each case with the payments still carrying it, the job it was on last and the hand-recorded payment it matches. `set_mercury_transaction_ar_returned` refuses to untick a bank return (it used to delete the row).
- **One notice per case** (`_shared/arReturnCaseNotify.ts`, moved out of the webhook): the webhook tells the office the moment it stores a return; **`ar-returned-checks`** (pg_cron hourly at :17) runs the rejected sweep and tells the office about any open case not yet told. The words (`noticeInputFromCase`, `bankReturnNoticeSentences`) follow where the check sits — *still counted as paid on J878* with the Edit Job link; *on no job now · it was on J650 until Sep 30 · J650 owes the money again*; *never on a job*; *Mercury could not take in … J1040 still reads paid* — in plain sentences, subject *A check came back · Loberg · $5,622.49*. Recipients unchanged: the Payment made list, else the office roles. Settings → What the team sees renders the new words. The sender (`sendEmailViaResend`) is handed in by each function, so the *What customers see* registry still finds both senders; `ar-returned-checks` is registered there as a staff sender.
- Guide *match bank deposits to the bills they pay*: *You don't have to go looking* says the office hears on a job or not, and a new paragraph covers a check that never reached the bank. GLOSSARY, ACCESS_CONTROL, EDGE_FUNCTIONS (new section + the webhook's bullet amended), dev-mcp catalog.
