# v2.6846 — See what they see: the Away row shows the office the screen a teammate who is away meets at sign-in (2026-11-10)

The owner, looking at People → Users → **Away (1)** on 2026-11-10: he likes the section and wants a button to imitate the person, so he can see the screen she sees when she goes to sign in while she is away. The want is to check the note as the person will read it, not to act as her. No migration, nothing to deploy.

## What changed

- **Kernel `src/lib/people/awayPreview.ts`** (tests in `awayPreview.test.ts`): `awayPreviewNote` takes what the office may already read (the `away_notes` row, the Away row's login name and day left, the office settings) and builds the `MyAwayNote` the person's page draws, field for field as `my_away_note()` answers it: the first word of the login's own name (`awayLoginFirstName`, the roster name never standing in), the day the note was updated in the app's zone, the alert switch left off since the person is never sent it, null when the note is gone. A test holds it equal to `parseMyAwayNote` of the function's answer for the same person. The words live there too: the press (**See what they see**), the window's name (*Preview of …'s sign-in screen*), whose screen it is, **Close the preview**.
- **`src/components/away/AwayPageView.tsx`**: the Away page's markup (the header with Sign out, the note over `buildAwayScreen`, the resting words, the failed read) lifted out of `pages/Away.tsx`, which now reads `my_away_note()`, keeps the clock and the location press, and hands them to the view. With `preview` the view fills its frame in place of the window and every press is drawn and dead.
- **`src/components/away/AwayPreviewModal.tsx`**: the window. It reads the note and the office settings through `loadAwayNote` and `loadAwayOffice` (the two reads the Leave a note window makes; both or neither), shapes them with the kernel and draws `AwayPageView` with `preview`. An amber bar above the page is the window's own: *Preview*, whose screen it is, *You are still signed in as yourself*, a *Today · On the day* switch (held when the note has no day back) and **Close the preview**; Escape and the backdrop close it too. Under the page: *The buttons do nothing here. Theirs do.* Past 520px it is a 430px column; on a phone it fills the screen, so the page under the bar is the width the person holds. With no login behind the account the bar says nothing opens yet.
- **`PeopleUsersTab`**: **See what they see** on each Away row, before **Edit the note…** and **Bring back…** or Restore. `AwayRow` gains `loginName`.
- Guides: *leave a note for a teammate who is away* gains *See what they see*; *see the app as a role or as a person* points at it for a teammate who is away. `GLOSSARY.md` → *Away note*, `PEOPLE_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`, `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` and `docs/twins/APP_DIRECTORY.md` name the press.

## Decisions

- **A look, not a sign-in.** The app has a real view-as: Settings → **View as…** and the older **Imitate** on a People row sign a dev in as the account through `login-as-user`, which records the imitation and mints a session. It cannot reach an away account (View as lists active people, the Away row has no Imitate), and it was not extended: a session as an archived caller would meet the seal, and the want here needs none. The preview reads as the viewer, writes nothing, and leaves the seal as it is.
- **One page, two callers, not a copy.** The Leave a note window already draws `AwayScreen` beside the form with a hand-drawn header. Lifting the whole page into `AwayPageView` means the office's look and the person's page are one component over one model, header and resting words included.
- **The presses stay dead.** *Use my location* would show the office its own miles, the call and text links would dial from the office's phone, and Sign out would sign the office out.
- **For whoever sees the section.** The Away card draws for the people who may archive, who are the people RLS lets read `away_notes`; the press adds no new reader.

## What the preview does not show

- The sign-in page itself, where the person types their email and password before this screen.
- The miles and the drive under *We're in …*, which only appear after the person shares their location.
- The person's clock: the countdown and the turn to *Today's the day.* run on the viewer's device here and on the person's phone there. *On the day* draws that screen on demand.
- Proof that the person's own read works. The preview reads the note as the office; the person's page reads `my_away_note()` through the seal, which the seal's own bed proves.
