# v2.7591 — Lien desk: a sent notice’s footer moved into its own file (2026-11-17)

The seventh cut of `src/components/jobs/LienDesk.tsx`, and the first of the footers. No behaviour changes.

**The facts are a kernel.** `lienSentPacketFacts` in `src/lib/jobs/lienSentPacket.ts` holds the lines that read a sent notice's packet, as they stood: where letter two stands, the first packet's item, the owner's call, the courtesy notice and the facts it sends with, the recorded packet and its sends that still owe a tracking number, and what the sent bills still owe. Eight unit tests, among them `jobBalance`, which the old map named as never asserted. This is step 2 of the architecture doc's twelve.

**The footer is a component.** `LienDeskSentFooter` in `src/components/jobs/LienDeskSentFooter.tsx` holds the sent branch as it stood: the facts line, then one of four rows (what they promised for the rest, the GC's written okay, the ask to counsel, or the sentence with the doors). It holds no state.

**The forms' state stays the desk's.** The eight values behind the three inline forms are the hook `useLienSentFooterForms`, called by the desk and handed in. The desk clears them when the job changes, To do's rows open the promise form with a day in it, and a typed line is kept while the footer is off screen. Had the footer held them, each would have been lost when it left the screen.

**What stayed.** The three doors a sent notice keeps (the courtesy record, the courtesy notice, the attorney's letter) stay in the desk and are handed in as one node, because a drafting footer draws the same three. The owner's call dialog and the courtesy window stay too.

**Size.** `LienDesk.tsx` went from 4,532 lines to 4,394, and from 89 `useState` calls to 81.

**Proof.** `LienDesk.render.test.tsx` (207 tests) and the page's render test pass with no edit. New: `lienSentPacket.test.ts` (eight tests) and `LienDeskSentFooter.render.test.tsx` (five tests).
