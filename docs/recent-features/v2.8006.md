# v2.8006 — Developments: a Schedule look says where every stage ahead stands (2026-11-19)

Punch list #382, PR 5 of eleven: the Schedule look. It reads the tables of v2.7997 through the pieces of v2.8003. No migration and no edge function. Nothing here reaches the GC.

## What a person sees

The Development page has a look named **Schedule**, after the six and after Trips when the site has it. The strip of looks counts it: *4 stages · 3 answers owed*. `?look=schedule` lands the page on it.

The look draws only on a site where the reader is on the desk of a job that has a stage line. The site's jobs are asked one by one (`start_checks_desk`), and only those jobs are read.

- **A banner on top.** *Ray owes us 3 answers on 2 stages.* Under it: who the contact is, and *They have moved 11 days on this site so far.*
- **One card a stage ahead**, sorted by trouble: red, then amber, green and grey, and by first day inside a colour. A finished stage has no card.
- **A card** has a coloured dot, the part and the stage's name, our days, the ready bar, one sentence and one press.
- **The sentence** is `stageStanding`'s, with its values in place. Our first day and their day change where they sit. The rest are bold.
- **The ready bar is a press of its own.** Resting on it, or pressing it on a phone, lists **What it takes to start (the stage)**: the count and the percent, the open bullets first with where each stands, then the ones with a yes, dulled.
- **The one press** opens the card to the four lines and the two lists, the same piece the job window draws. It reads **Write the list**, **Plan our days**, **Move our start** or **Open the stage** when that is what comes next, and **Open** otherwise.

## What is not here yet, on purpose

- **Ask (contact) about all N** on the banner is PR 6, with the Dashboard's line.
- **Go or hold** is PR 7. Until then a card that waits on a decision opens with **Open**.
- **The line of time** is PR 11.
- **Who we ask is read, not changed here.** The page's head already changes the GC's contact on the bids.

## Choices made here

- **The Schedule look stands beside Trips** as its own look (the owner's call 7, on its default).

- **The office and an estimator see it.** A superintendent and a primary do not open this page, and write a stage's list on the job window.
