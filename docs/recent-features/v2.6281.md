# v2.6281 — The law firm's weekly digest reads by matter (2026-11-01)

Punch list #233, PR 3 ([`to-dos/legal-portal-notifications-show/`](../../to-dos/legal-portal-notifications-show/README.md)), decided by the owner on the first digest sample the same day: *"It puts the date and then what happened and then the context which is what account it matters for last. I would instead like this to be sorted by the Ridgeway Builders and then everything for Ridgeway Builders underneath it…"* No migration; **one function to redeploy** (`legal-notify-dispatch`, which bundles the builder). Stacked on [v2.6280](v2.6280.md).

## What changed

- **`buildLegalDigestEmail`** (`_shared/legalEmails.ts`, re-exported to the client) drops its two lists, *Open matters* and *Since your last digest*, for one reading by matter. Each matter is a heading, *Ridgeway Builders — referred · handling Dana Whitlock · since 2026-10-26*, and the week's lines sit under it oldest first as *Oct 27 · The office asks — Do you have the signed change order?* (the day short, in the company's zone, `shortDayInAppTz`; the *since* day stays a date of record). The matters with news come first, newest news first; then a matter the office withdrew or closed that week, which is off the dispatcher's open list, under its own heading (*— referral withdrawn*, or *— no longer open*); then the quiet matters, each with *Nothing new this week*, so the digest still lists every open matter. With nothing open and nothing new, one line says so. The subject is unchanged. Events are matched to matters by payer name, trimmed and lowercased, as the dispatcher writes them.
- The dispatcher's query and the per-person stamp are unchanged; the reach of the digest (which acts write a queue row) is the card's call 3, PR 4.
- The sample inbox on the portal's Notifications tab (v2.6280) and the digest sample on Settings → What customers see draw the new shape with no change of their own.
- Docs: the guide *manage who at the law firm gets emails* (the digest sentence, the sample-week paragraph); `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` → `legal-notify-dispatch`.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **Order of the headings.** By the newest news, not by balance or stage: the digest is a week's reading, and the account that moved last is the one to open first. Quiet matters last, since their heading is a reminder, not news.
- **A withdrawn matter keeps a heading.** The dispatcher's open list no longer carries it, so its lines would otherwise have had nowhere to sit; a heading of its own with *referral withdrawn* reads as the news it is.
- **Short days on the lines, the date of record on the heading.** The week's lines are within days, so *Oct 27* reads; *since* can be months back, so it keeps the full date.
- **Not done here**: the digest's reach beyond the seven queued acts, and a per-person stamp so two weekdays no longer split the week — call 3 on the card.
