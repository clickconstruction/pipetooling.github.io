## The tab

**People → Spending** (`?tab=spending`, in the People group after Person), for the office roles (`canOpenSpending` — dev, master, assistant, controller; the read enforces the same set).

- **The period**: This week · Last week · This month (the default) · Last month · Last 30 days · Custom (`lib/people/spendingPeriod.ts`; Sunday weeks as on Review; a custom range keeps its last 366 days, as the read allows, and says so).
- **The totals**: Card spend, ⛽ Fuel (its share of card spend), On jobs, Not on a job yet (amber when there is any, with its fuel), Not tied to anyone.
- **The table** (desktop): one row per person, then Company cards and Not tied to anyone, then Everyone — card spend, ⛽ fuel, other, on jobs, Office and Payroll only when the period has any, not on a job, jobs. A person's name opens their desk.
- **On a phone** (under 640 px): one card per person instead of the table — the name and card spend, then ⛽, on jobs, Office, Payroll, not on a job (amber) and jobs, and *Show the details* opening the same breakdown with the charges and their buttons. Seen at 375 px on the dev server: the nine-column table showed only Who, Card spend and Fuel, and opening a person scrolled the names off.
- **A person opened**: their jobs (each opens the Job window), On supply invoices, Office, Payroll, Not on a job yet with *See the charges*, Before sorting began; each line carries its own fuel, so the breakdown adds up to the row. A line says when charges count only because the card is theirs.
- **Put on a job**: loads the charge's own bank row and opens today's Assign window (`MercuryTransactionAllocationsModal`) — for a held card as Team purchases opens it (`tallySelfService` acting for the holder, `replace_mercury_job_splits_for_linked_card_as_staff`), for any other card in its Banking mode (`replace_mercury_transaction_splits`, with the person picker). Where the write would refuse (`viewer_can_sort` false: outside the holder's circle) there is no button and one line says who can — *Only someone who sees {holder} in Team purchases can put this on a job*, since Team purchases lists a holder to exactly the people the write admits; a charge before Tally's sorting floor says so, with no button. After a save the tab reads again.
- **Payroll**: a viewer without payroll access gets no charges settled by a payroll mark alone (the read's rule); the tab says so in one line. #72's replay measured that slice over Jul 7 – Oct 5: 196 charges, $82,630.
- Not pushed yet: the tab says *This is not live in the database yet*.

`lib/people/loadSpending.ts` — the one load: the window, the tags (`pickFuelTag`), the Office job, Tally's floor (`job_tally_min_posted_ymd`), and the names of the people the charges belong to; the tags, the Office job and the floor fail soft. `spendingRollup.ts` — each bucket (On supply invoices, Office, Payroll, Before sorting began) now carries its fuel.
