# v2.3809 — Pipeline: the Ready-to-ask tile and the Capable list agree (2026-09-24)

Taunya, 2026-09-24: *"Showing $400 capable and there's no jobs under the breakdown window."* The Pipeline's **Ready to ask for** tile read *$400 capable in Working*, the **Bill the finished work — $400** card sat under it, and the Capable list said *No jobs with billable amount*.

## What was wrong

v2.3431 (`to-dos/stage-plan-residuals` item 1) taught the Pipeline header and the Capable list to read a job's stage plan: a job the office split into Order stages counts `billable()` — the stages that passed inspection — not the formula `revenue × pct_complete − billed`. The tile and the money card read a different kernel: `computeStagesHeaderStats` (fed by `fetchStagesHeaderStats`, the lean spine the Dashboard AR card and Quickfill also read) still summed the formula. J1031 (*Estimate for Kimberly Coe*, 100 % complete, $400, one Order stage with no window passed) read $400 by the formula and $0 by its plan.

## The change

- **`computeStagesHeaderStats(jobs, now, stageInputs)`** takes the Working jobs' stage-plan inputs and reads `capableToBillTotalWithPlans` — the Capable list's kernel — for `capableToBill`. `null` inputs keep the formula, so every caller without them is unchanged.
- **`fetchStagesHeaderStats`** loads, for the Working jobs only, their fixture rows (the columns the plan reads: `LEAN_STATS_FIXTURE_COLUMNS`) and the stage inputs, then hands both to the kernel. A failed plan read logs a warning and keeps the formula figure rather than failing the stats.
- **`lib/jobs/fetchWorkingStagePlanInputs.ts`** — the three chunked reads moved out of `useWorkingStagePlanInputs` (which re-exports it) so a lib fetch does not import a hook.
- Tests: `stagesHeaderStats.test.ts` (the third argument), `fetchStagesHeaderStats.test.ts` (the fixtures and windows reads scoped to Working ids; $360 by formula, $0 with an Order stage, $360 again when the plan read fails).

## Verified

On the dev server as the dev: the tile read *$0 capable in Working* and the money card disappeared, matching the empty Capable list.
