# v2.7943 — Lien desk: every filed lien's copies carry the job's number (2026-11-19)

## Why

Building the packet preview for v2.7930 showed it. Eleven liens were waiting in **Filed liens: copies to mail**, and six of them were on jobs made in the app, which have a Click number and no HCP number. The mail sheet listed those six by the job's name with no number. The office splits the printed stack by that sheet and writes each certified mail number against its row.

## What was wrong

Two kernels built a filed lien's job number from `hcp_number` alone:

- `serveJobOf` in `src/lib/jobs/lienServePackets.ts`, behind the copies window (`lienServeJobsFromDesk`, `lienServeMailedJobsFromDesk`);
- `lienScanTargetsFromDesk` in `src/lib/jobs/lienScanBatch.ts`, behind **Add the stamped scans**.

The rest of the desk reads `effectiveJobLedgerNumber(hcp, click)`: the HCP number, else the Click number. The job's own Lien window already did, so its one-lien press was right.

## What changed

Both kernels read `effectiveJobLedgerNumber`, and their `jobsById` shapes take `click_number`. The desk's read already carried the column. No component changed.

What follows from the number, for a job with only a Click number:

- the window's cards, held lines and the mail sheet's Job column read *863 · Name* where they read *Name*;
- the copy kept on the job is titled *Copy of lien affidavit — Job 863* and its file is named with the number (`lienServedCopySentTitle`, `lienStampedPrintFileName`), where both had none;
- a receipt added from the window is named with the number;
- liens due the same day sort by number, so the stack's order changes: a job with no HCP number used to sort first;
- in Add the stamped scans, a file named with the Click number matches its lien by the `job_number` rule, and the list's order by number holds.

A job with neither number is still labelled by its name alone, and its kept copy is still titled with no job.

The cover letters and the stamped pages never used the number and are unchanged.

## How it was checked

- Unit tests with made-up names in `lienServePackets.test.ts` and `lienScanBatch.test.ts`: a job with only a Click number, one with both (HCP wins), one with neither; the label, the mail sheet, the kept title, and a scan matched by its Click number.
- The 19 test files around both windows, 411 tests.
- Against prod, read only, with `npm run lien:packet` from the branch of PR #8910: all eleven liens carry a number on the mail sheet, 68 pages as before. Nothing was printed, kept or marked.
