# v2.3786 — Lien desk Timeline: the book reads the contract-end date and the payment bond (2026-09-23)

A fix to v2.3768 (punch list #37 PR 2). No migration.

## Why

`lienRetainageClockFromDesk` (v2.3761) reads the § 53.057 clock from the desk's data, so the strip on the Notices and Affidavits panes lit its retainage step the day v2.3753 landed the contract-end date on the job. `useLienTimelineBook` never fetched those columns: every book row kept the retainage node dashed, and *Print the grid* printed **Bond** and **Contract completed** blank even when the job carried both.

## What shipped

- `useLienTimelineBook` calls `fetchLienClockColumns` (v2.3753's tolerant reader — empty until the columns exist) alongside the GCs, property records and owner overrides, and maps `lien_payment_bond` (through `parsePaymentBond`) and `lien_contract_ended_on` onto each `LienBookJob`.
- `buildLienTimelineBook` hands the kernel a dated retainage clock (`lienThirtyDayClock` of the contract-end date) instead of a null deadline, so the step reads *due* / *missed* with its date, and the row's next step can be the retainage notice.
- `lienTimelineBook.test.ts`: a job with a contract-end date lights the step on its date and the grid prints `Y` and the date.
