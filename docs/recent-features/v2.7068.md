# v2.7068 — My email: you are told when a developer saves your mail to a job (2026-11-12)

A developer can save a conversation out of a teammate's mailbox onto a job, through the dev seat. The Gmail label was the mailbox owner's only sign that it had happened, and v2.7035 took the label out. So the owner needs to hear it some other way: who did it, how much, and onto which job.

## The choice

Two options were put to the owner on 2026-11-12: an email and a push, or the app only. His answer: *In the app only.* That fits what he asked of the label's removal, that to a user it should look as if the app was never in their inbox. So nothing is emailed and nothing is pushed.

Two more rules came from the lead session, not from the owner, and are applied here so he can overrule them:

1. **One notice a batch.** Seventeen saves in two minutes read as one line, not seventeen.
2. **Saves to a job only.** Mail filed privately onto an HR file, or as a counsel-only evidence record, writes no notice. Those doors are for devs and counsel by design.

## What changed

- **The notice.** After the dev seat saves a conversation onto a job out of someone else's box, `gmail-mine`'s `noteSavedForYou` writes that person one `notification_history` row of their own: *Nadia Reyes saved a conversation from your mail to JP1077 4 Elm Ct.* The second line says it came through the dev seat, where the mail is now, and that nothing changed in their Gmail. Each person reads only their own rows of that table, so nobody else sees it.
- **One a batch.** A notice written in the last 30 minutes is rewritten, not added to. Its count comes from the owner's `email_tag_threads` rows that someone else tagged onto a job since the batch began: *… saved 17 conversations from your mail to 7 jobs.* With every conversation on one job, the job is still named.
- **A new word in the record.** `notification_history.channel` allowed email, push, both and held. None is true of this row, so migration `20261113001500` adds `app`: shown in the app only.
- **Where the person sees it.** A new line on the Needs you card, kicker *Your mail*, blue because it is news with nothing to do. It is on for every role. It stays seven days. Its press, **See the record**, opens Settings → Activity logs, where the row is listed with the badge *In the app*. That list's heading is now *Most recent notifications*.
- **Never told**: the developer's own box, a person's own press, the seat's plan, and a save onto an HR file.
- **Never in the way.** A notice that will not write is logged and the save stands.

## Is this the best we can do? How likely the owner is to see it

Read from the code, not yet seen on a phone:

- **On a desktop** the Needs you card is on the Dashboard for every role. Someone who opens the Dashboard in the seven days sees the line.
- **On a field role's phone** the card is not on the Job Mode home screen. It is under the **Inbox** tab, where Job Mode keeps the Dashboard's banners. So a field teammate sees the line when they open Inbox, and not otherwise.
- **After seven days** the line is gone and the record is only under Settings → Activity logs, which few people open.

So an office person will very likely see it, and a field person will see it if they open Inbox that week. That is weaker than an email, which was the option not taken. Two cheap ways to raise the odds, neither built: a count on the Inbox tab of the phone's dock, or the line on the Job Mode home screen.

The line cannot be dismissed. It has no "seen" state, since the record has no column for one; it leaves by age.

2. `gmail-mine` from `main`, after the push. Deployed before it, the notice is refused by the old check and logged; the save still stands.
