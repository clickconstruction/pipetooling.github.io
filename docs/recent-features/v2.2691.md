# v2.2691 — Crew split counts bid time in the day (2026-09-03)

Finding 1 of the Review math audit, and the first fix that is not Review-only: the crew-split trigger's denominator. Migration `20260903153903_fix_crew_pct_denominator_includes_bids.sql` — fragment in `docs/migrations/`.

## DB only — no client change

- `sync_crew_jobs_from_clock` and `sync_crew_bids_from_clock` now share one denominator: every approved, closed session that day with a job or a bid. Before, jobs were split among job sessions only and bids among bid sessions only, while `people_hours` held the whole day — so a job + bid day credited the whole day to the job *and* counted the bid hours as overhead. Job pcts + bid pcts now sum to 100 across `people_crew_jobs` + `people_crew_bids`; job-only and bid-only days are unchanged.
- One-off resync inside the migration for the person-days that actually change (job + bid sessions on the same day, last two years).
- Every consumer inherits the fix with no code change: People → Review (period and lifetime labor, revenue shares, field hours vs allocated hours), Jobs → Job Summary team labor, pay-report breakdown lines, Quickfill / Moneyfill unallocated-time queues. Pay amounts are unaffected (payroll pays `people_hours` × wage).
