# v2.3770 — Record a lien notice that already went out (2026-09-23)

Punch list **#35** (`to-dos/lien-notice-sent-by-hand/`), PR 2 of 3. Client only — the columns it writes (`packet_id`, `by_hand`, `printed_claim`, `document_url`) landed with v2.3763's migration.

## Why

Taunya's Lenox notice of 2026-09-22 (jobs 273 + 858 + 866, one paper, $28,987 for April, June, July and August) was printed from the app and mailed by hand, and the app had no door to say so: 273 sat in *Awaiting approval* asking the leader for a notice already in the mail, and 858 and 866 were "ready" in the Dudley run, one click from a second mailing. The only two doors that wrote a filing recorded the app's own single-job notice at the app's amount.

## The change

- **Kernel [`lienNoticeByHand.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienNoticeByHand.ts)** (5 tests): `byHandProblems` (the date, the recipients, the claim, the months, an email's address), `byHandSends`, `byHandFilingPayloads` — one `job_lien_filings` row per covered job on one `packet_id`, `by_hand: true`, the job's own share as `amount`, the paper's months as `months_covered`, `printed_claim` when the paper's total is not the job's amount, the saved copy — `byHandClaimWords` (*printed $28,987 · the app's timely claim would have been $9,802*, '' when they agree), `otherJobsAtProperty` (the same saved property record, else the same street number and street; money open; biggest first), `parsePrintedMonths` ("April, June, July and August 2026" → keys).
- **IO [`lienNoticeByHandIo.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienNoticeByHandIo.ts)**: `loadJobsAtProperty` (the candidates with their live desk items), `recordLienNoticeByHand` (the inserts, `markLienDeskItemSent` for every covered job with a live item, `syncLienDeskAfterRecord` for the rest).
- **Pane [`LienNoticeByHandPane.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienNoticeByHandPane.tsx)** (render smoke): sent on · how · tracking · owner / original contractor · the claim and the months as printed (prefilled with the app's) · the saved copy · the tick list of the other unpaid jobs at the property (*on the desk* when one has a live item) · the difference line · *Record it on N jobs ▸*.
- **Doors**: the Lien desk pane's draft footer and its approved footer (*Already mailed? Record it…*, [`LienDeskModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskModal.tsx)), and the Lien window's notice tab beside *Save & record sends…* ([`LienFilingTabs.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienFilingTabs.tsx)). The desk passes the timely claim and the due months; the window passes the balance and its months.
- **Read-back**: a by-hand filing's months make the job's months read as `noticed` on every lien reader, so the Dudley run lists 273, 858 and 866 as noticed and the desk moves 273 to Sent. The Lien window's *Filings on this job* shows the record with its saved copy (v2.3763).
- The Pipeline's Fix-ups chip *Owner of record to confirm* ([`pipelineOverview.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/pipelineOverview.ts)) no longer says "with approved hours" — since v2.3747 a job with none is on the list too.
- Guides *send lien notices from the Lien desk* (Drafting a notice — *Already mailed?*) and *file a lien and never miss its deadlines* (Step 1); GLOSSARY → Lien desk.

## Decisions taken

- **Recorded as printed, flagged, never rewritten.** The paper claimed what it claimed; the record keeps the paper's total and the job's share side by side, and the words name the difference. Whether the office may claim closed months against counsel's timely-months rule is the owner's call (the to-do's blocker) — the door records either way.
- **One packet, one row per job.** The GC run's *noticed* reading, the Customer review's *on notice since* chip and the affidavit's sworn notice all key on the job's own filing, so the paper is written on every job it covered rather than once.
