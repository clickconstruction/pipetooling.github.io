# v2.4496 — Job window: the contract and lien paper on the Documents tab (2026-10-04)

The second half of the owner's ask (2026-10-04: *"the Documents tab should also hold the job's contract, test reports, lien paper and bills"*), after [v2.4495](v2.4495.md) (bills and test reports). Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## The change

- **Contract** — [`JobDocumentsContract.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobDocumentsContract.tsx) over `loadJobContractRows(job.id)`: the job's contracts that were sent, signed or voided (drafts stay in the Contract window), drawn as the Documents page draws them — template name, *rev N*, the chips (`jobContractChips`), the signature line or *Sent …*, and **Signed copy** when the row carries a link. A signed row mounts `JobContractModal` on that record (`initialRecordId`); one only sent opens the page that went out (`buildJobContractRecordHtml`). **Contract window** in the heading opens the window with no record picked. The list reloads on `job-contract-changed`.
- **Lien paper** — [`JobDocumentsLienPaper.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobDocumentsLienPaper.tsx) over `loadJobLienPaper(job.id)` → `jobLienPaperRows` ([`jobLienPaperRows.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobLienPaperRows.ts)), oldest first:
  - filings (`job_lien_filings`): *§ 53.056 notice*, *§ 53.057 retainage notice*, *Lien affidavit*, *Release of record*, with *Served …* / *Filed …* / the recording number, the amount (a release of record has none), and **Saved copy** when a link was filed with it;
  - demand letters (`job_demand_letters`): *Sent 09/30/2026 by certified mail · reply by 10/14/2026*, or *Not sent yet*;
  - releases of lien (`job_lien_releases`, never a draft): the form's name, its chips, the amount.
  Voided paper is left out. A release opens the page as signed, with the stored ink, as the Documents page does; a filing or a letter mounts `LienInstrumentsModal` on the tab that holds it (`notice`, `affidavit`, `release_record`, `demand`), where it is viewed and tracked. **Lien window** in the heading opens it.
- [`jobLienPaperIo.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobLienPaperIo.ts): the two reads, each failing soft.
- Both windows report through `onOverlayOpenChange`, so Escape closes them and not the job.

Demand letters and filings were not on any Documents list before: this tab is the first place a job's lien paper is listed together.

## Who sees what

The tabbed job window opens for dev, master, assistant, controller and primary. `job_contracts` and the three lien tables are read by the first four; for `primary` the two sections say there is nothing.
