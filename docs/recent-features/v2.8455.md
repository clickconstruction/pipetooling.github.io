# v2.8455 — The mode row: the *New* mark comes off Job Mode (2026-11-24)

Punch list [#433](../../to-dos/mode-row-five-seconds/README.md), PR 1 of the train. Client only: no migration and no edge function.

## The ask

The owner, 2026-11-24, after a look at the mode row on a phone: *We need to remove the new icon from job mode.* It was the first of four things he asked of the row. The other three, a row that shows for five seconds on each visit, slides up and out, and runs a bar that says when, are the PRs after this one.

## Before

`modeRowNewMark` drew a green *New* on **Job Mode** while the page was not in Job Mode and the phone held no press of a third for that person. The first press of any third stored `mode_row_pressed_<user>` on the phone and the mark was gone there for good. So it read *new* on every fresh phone, and for anyone looking through View as, whose sample account had never pressed anything on that phone. The owner's screenshot was that case.

## Now

The mark is not drawn. `modeRowNewMark`, `MODE_ROW_WORDS.newMark`, the `isNew` prop of `ModeRowThirds` and the `.modeRowNew` rule in `src/index.css` are deleted. Nothing takes its place: no third carries a mark.

The phone's memory of a press stays for now. `modeRowFirstVisits` still reads it to decide when the row starts to tuck. It goes with that rule in the next PR.

## Tests

`modeRow.test.ts` loses the mark's case. `ModeRow.render.test.tsx` holds that a phone with nothing stored draws the three thirds by their words alone, with no mark element.

## Looked at

The tests only. The change removes one element and its rule, and the next PR of the train is walked at a phone's width.
