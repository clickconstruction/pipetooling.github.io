# v2.5190 — GC mode: the Project Board by customer (2026-10-10)

The Board's B2b-iii (`to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/board-b2b.md` on branch `spike/gc-mode`), after the design spike's By customer. No kernel moves: `customerGroups`, `priceToOwner` and `boardSectionCounts` came with B2b-i. gc 5 read the money for Owner Billing (M1).

- **The switch** (`GcBoard.tsx`): **By stage | By customer** sits in the strip's lead slot. It opens on By stage each time the board opens (the owner, 2026-10-04).
- **By customer:**
  - The strip has a pill per customer we build for (`customerGroups`), each with its open count and a dot for each stage it has jobs in: *Jump to a customer*.
  - Each customer's band (`GcCustomerHeading`, ported to `GcBoardStages.tsx`): their name opens their window (`useCustomerOpener`), with the count of open jobs, the contact, and *bidding $X · under contract $Y*.
  - A small heading for each stage they have jobs in (`GcStageSubheading`) sits over that customer's rows. The rows leave the customer's name out (`byCustomer`).
  - *Nothing open with them right now.* when nothing is open. Their closed and lost jobs fold into one *Also:* line, each name opening the job.
  - New project stays in the page's header.
- **The money (gc 5's M1):**
  - `customerWorth(open)` and `customerWorthWords` (`boardGroups.ts`, new) count a customer's **open** jobs only, each at `priceToOwner`: Bidding for *bidding*, Buying out and Building for *under contract*. So the customers' numbers add up to the sections' worth exactly. A closed job, which the customer window's `customerSummary` counts under contract, counts here in neither.
  - The band never says what is billed or owed. *Owes us* comes with B2b-v, for the money team only, once Owner Billing's rows are on the board and their customer copies (`billingStateForAll`'s `customer@project`) map back.
  - The groups are built from the board's own state.
- **The section worth:** By stage's headings read `boardSectionCounts` (`priceToOwner`) in place of a sum of `proposalTotals`. No number changes today, since the board lays no signed price yet. Once B2b-v does, a signed job is its signed price plus its signed change orders.
- **Tests:**
  - `boardGroups.worth.test.ts`, new: on four states the customers' bidding equals Bidding's worth, and their under contract equals Buying out and Building's. The four are the made-up data, the clinic for a money reader ($87,323.40), the clinic with no Our number ($66,500), and the made-up data with a job closed and one lost. The closed job counts in neither, and no band reads billed or owes.
  - `GcBoard.render.test.tsx` covers the switch, a customer's band, subheadings and worth, rows without the name, the window opener, the *Also* line, and back to By stage.
- **Guide and docs:** *see where every GC project stands* gains *See the board by customer*. `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`'s board paragraph and `GLOSSARY.md`'s Project Board entry each gain a sentence.
