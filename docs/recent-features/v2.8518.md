# v2.8518 — Trade partners: an ask we took back no longer counts against a company's quotes (2026-11-24)

Found on the live walk of v2.8490, the same day. The walk asked the test company *ZZ Test trade no page, delete me* on Electrical, logged a call, and took the ask back: the ask closed as **we took it back**, as designed. Its row on Trade partners then read *quoted 0 of 1 asks*. The ask we closed ourselves was still counted as one the company had not answered, and two of those would have marked a company *mostly silent*. v2.8490's own call 5 says the close is never the company's answer.

## What changed

- **`Partner.tookBack`** (optional): of a company's asks, the ones we took back after they heard. `partnerFromRows` (`boardRows.ts`) and the portal's `partnerOf` (`tradePortalState.ts`) set it only when it is above zero, so a company with none reads exactly as before.
- **`asksToAnswer(partner)`** in `reliability.ts`: every ask but those. `answerRecord` judges on it (two asks to answer is still the least), and the bench's sort by quote rate divides by it.
- **`quotedWords(partner, never)`**: *quoted 2 of 3 asks* over the asks they had to answer; *asked once, and we took it back* (or *asked 3 times, and we took each back*) when none is left. Trade partners' row and the company window's **Quotes** line read it; the Ask window's chip counts the same way.
- **Unchanged on purpose**: `Partner.invited` still counts every ask, so **Remove from the bench…** still stays off a company that was asked and told (`benchRemoveHold`), and *never asked* still means no ask at all.
- The kernel copy is regenerated (`types.ts`, `boardRows.ts`, `reliability.ts`).
- **Tests**: `reliability.test.ts` (the count, the mark, the words, the count read off the rows, a pass by phone still counted); the render smoke in `GcTradePartners.render.test.tsx` for the row's words both ways.
- **Docs**: `docs/GLOSSARY.md` and the guide *follow up on a quote from a trade partner* amended in place.

## Order

Client only, no migration. After the merge, redeploy the importers of the kernel copy that `npm run check:edge-drift` names.
