## What a report's photos field stores today

The trade ERP comparison's capability map said a field report carries a photos field. It does not: `report_template_fields.input_type` is `long_text`, `percent_0_100` or `signature_png` (a PNG as a data URL in text), `reports.field_values` is a map of field id to text, and no template field holds a photo. A test report (`job_test_reports`) keeps its paper (`pdf_path`) and no photo. The photos the app does keep each wait in a bucket until Drive has them: a text's photo on `message_attachments` (`bucket_path`, then `drive_file_id` once the `photo-save` sweep files it under the job's tick); a Quick Estimate's field photos in `estimate_field_photos` until Quickfill's Field photos → Drive station writes `estimate_photo_handover` (v2.2300); a box photo in `job_box_photos` (`storage_path`, `file_name`, `drive_file_id`, `filed_at`, `fail_why`), filed by the same sweep (v2.6045). So a checklist photo follows the box photo's shape, and #189's PR 5b plans `report_photos` the same way.

## Decisions

- **A photo question is answered by its pick and its photo.** Not here needs no photo, since there is nothing there to photograph. The photo counts once taken; a photo the sweep could not file yet does not hold the stage.
- **The kind is read from the stage's name**, the way the Pipeline already recognizes Rough In · Top Out · Trim Set (v2.3417), never set by hand, so every job that has the stages has the checklists.
