# v2.2932 — Watchers: an email when the sub reports (2026-09-06)

PR 6 of the three-party scheduling train (prototype: artifact 3a540149). Stacked on v2.2931. The owner's ask: subscribe specific superintendents or masters to a job so every sub update emails them.

## Data

- Migration [`20260906080000_job_watchers.sql`](../migrations/20260906080000_job_watchers.md): `job_watchers` (job × user, `hear_progress / hear_done / hear_dates`, `source`) and `job_watcher_notices` (the send log that folds repeats).

## Kernel — `supabase/functions/_shared/jobWatchersCore.ts` (tested from `src/lib/subs/jobWatchersCore.test.ts`)

- `resolveWatchers(explicitRows, assignedSuperintendentIds)`: assigned supers with `WATCH_DEFAULTS` (progress + done on, dates off), explicit rows override.
- `recipientsFor(watchers, kind)`, `shouldFold(lastSentAt, now)` (one per user × job × kind per hour), `buildWatchEmail(notice)` — subject "#1004 · Behar Kraja · 50% along on their part", a plain-text body and a small HTML card with the tag (Sub progress / Sub says done / Sub dates), the job label and address, the note quoted, an **Open Jobs → Subs** button, and the manage line.

## Sender — `_shared/jobWatchers.ts`

`notifyJobWatchers(admin, { jobId, kind, subName, line, detail })`: loads the job, its `job_watchers` rows and its superintendent team members, resolves recipients for the kind, skips archived or email-less users, folds within the hour via `job_watcher_notices`, sends through Resend (`RESEND_API_KEY`, `APP_ORIGIN`), logs every decision. Best-effort — never throws into the caller. Called by `submit-sub-portal` on **progress** (a percent or a note), **mark_work_done** (`done`), and **accept_offer** with a pick / **pick_dates** (`dates`).

## UI

- [`JobWatchersPopover`](../../src/components/jobs/JobWatchersPopover.tsx): the 🔔 **Watching · N** bell on every Subs → Work job group header; rows with name, role, "assigned to this job" / "subscribed", three checkboxes, ✕ (removes a subscription; turns everything off for an assigned super); **+ Subscribe someone…** (office roles + superintendents, not archived).
- [`SettingsJobWatchesSection`](../../src/components/settings/SettingsJobWatchesSection.tsx) inside **Settings → My email schedule**: your rows with the same switches and **Stop watching**; hidden when you watch nothing.
