# v2.7027 — Messages: old mail you tag onto a job is filed for the record (2026-11-12)

Punch list #324, PR 2 of 5. v2.7026 gave the database the mark. This is the tag that sets it and the app that reads it.

## Why

A batch of old email was tagged onto jobs on 2026-11-09 so anyone could catch up. Each mail landed at its own date, months back, and Messages read it as mail nobody had answered. The tag had no idea of history: a mail from last year landed exactly as one from this morning.

## What changed

- **The rule** (`src/lib/messages/filed.ts`): a mail more than `FILED_AFTER_DAYS` (14) days old when it lands is filed for the record. Judged per mail, so a conversation of eight old mails and one from this morning files eight and opens on the ninth.
- **Only the tag decides.** `planTag` (`myMail/tagPlan.ts`) marks each write step from the clock `gmail-mine` hands it: the tag, the follow and the Sent read. The company boxes' readers never file. Mail in a person's own box was seen by that person when it came; mail in the team box was seen by nobody until the app read it, and a reader that fell two weeks behind must not file real customer mail in silence.
- **The line** carries `history` in its row (`myMailLineRow`), kept by `email_write_line`.
- **Whose move.** `lastIn` skips a filed mail of theirs, so the move line (`courtOf`), Needs a reply's wait (`queue.ts`) and New's sort (`newLane.ts`) never count from one. A thread with nothing live says *Nothing waits*. A filed mail of ours still answers what came before it. Waiting on them skips a filed mail of ours and still takes a filed mail of theirs as their answer.
- **The chip** (`filedMark`, `ThreadLine.tsx`): *filed Nov 9* beside who, with a hover that says when the mail is from and when it was filed.
- **The grey line** (`myMailTagEventWords`) adds *This is old mail, filed for the record. Nothing went on Follow up.* when every mail the tag wrote was old, after PR 7778's words for the dev seat.
- **The seat's plan** says `lands: 'record' | 'followUp'` on each mail it would write, and counts `filed`. `plan_mail_tag`'s commitment hashes the counts, so a plan made before a mail crosses fourteen days is refused at apply and planned again.

## Deploy

Migration `20261112180000_messages_history.sql` must be on prod first: the client selects `history` by name. Then `supabase functions deploy gmail-mine`. `dev-mcp` reaches the same kernels through the copy and is redeployed with it.

## How it was checked

- `thread.test.ts`: the age test at its edge, the move line over every mix of live and filed mail, the chip's words.
- `tagPlan.test.ts`, `mineFunction.test.ts`: the mark per mail, the counts, the grey line's words in plain words.
- `gmailMine.run.test.ts`: a whole tag three weeks after the mail writes two marked lines and the grey line says so. Its clock is now held on the fixtures' day, since the function reads the real one.
- `queue.test.ts`, `newLane.test.ts`, `waitingOnThem.test.ts`: a filed mail is never the line a row waits on.

## Not verified

- No tag has run against prod with this code. The first live check is a plan through the seat on a ZZ Test job, read before any apply.
- The nudges (`flows/nudges.ts`) read each person's quiet days from `lastIn`, which now skips filed mail. Not walked.
- Job Activity draws no chip. The line shows there as before.
