# v2.6084 — Stage checklists: the crew fills them on the phone (punch list #164, PR 3) (2026-10-29)

Punch list #164, PR 3 of five, on the tables of v2.6077. The sheet the crew fills for one stage of a job, the strip that opens it on Job Mode and the job window, and the photo's road to Drive.

## What changed

- **The strip** (`JobStageChecklistStrip.tsx` over `useJobStageChecklists`): the job's stages with a checklist, each one press reading *Rough In · 2 of 3 answered, not ready* or *Checklist filled*, on Job Mode's details (`JobModeDetailsSection.tsx`, above Customer) and under the job window's address (`DetailJobModal.tsx`, for every role the window opens for). A stage whose kind has no questions is not listed, a job with none draws nothing, and a read that fails draws nothing: the checklists are an addition, never a gate on the screen.
- **The sheet** (`StageChecklistSheet.tsx`, a `ResponsiveModalShell`): each question with Yes, No and Not here at 44 px, a note that saves when the box is left, and where the question asks for one a **Take the photo** press that opens the camera, shrinks the shot (`shrinkPhoto`, the box photo's), names it *2026-10-29 Rough In · Did the water test hold.jpg* (`checklistPhotoName`) and sends it; the thumbnail while it waits, *In the customer's Drive folder.* once filed, the reason in amber when the sweep could not file it. Every press writes at once (`stageChecklistSheetIo.ts`: the row for the stage and question made on the first press, `pick`, `note`, the photo's columns), so a sheet closed early keeps what was answered. The foot counts the answers and carries the rule; **Done** lights once every question is answered.
- **The sweep** (`photo-save`): a fourth source after the boxes and #189's report photos (v2.6083), `sweepWaiting` over a `WaitingSource` per table (the boxes' and the checklists'), the box photo's rules to the letter: the shot from the `stage-checklist-photos` bucket into the job's photo folder under the row's name, the Drive id and the filed time on the row, the reason on the row when it cannot file, the bucket copy deleted. Answers `checklists: { saved, failed }` beside `boxes` and `reports`. `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` → photo-save.
- **The cron** (`20261030070000_photo_save_cron_all_sources.sql`): #189's `20261029220000_report_photos.sql`, written the same evening, re-scheduled `photo-save` an hour after v2.6077's migration and without its checklist clause, so this writes all four clauses again: a job's tick, a box photo, a saved report's photo, a checklist photo. `docs/migrations/20261030070000_photo_save_cron_all_sources.md`.
- **The guide** *fill a stage checklist on the phone* (Field Work).

## Decisions

- **Each press saves by itself.** A crew member on one bar of signal closes the sheet whenever; what was answered stands, and the strip's count moves with the press. No Save button to forget.
- **The photo's road is the box photo's.** The bucket is a waiting room and Drive the home (the owner, 2026-10-24); the sweep already knows the job's photo folder, the free name and the reasons, so the checklist photo joins it as a source rather than a second copier.
- **The strip never gates the window.** A failed read, a job with no stages, a kind with no questions: the window stands as before.
