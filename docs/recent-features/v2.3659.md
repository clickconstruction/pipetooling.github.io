# v2.3659 — Quick time add, PR 2: the door and the composer — ＋5, ＋5, a few words, *Add 10 min* (2026-09-20)

PR 2 of [`to-dos/quick-time-add`](../../to-dos/quick-time-add/README.md). PR 1 (v2.3655) put the rules in the database; this is the screen. No migration.

## Redrawn before it was built

The first mock-up was a nine-control form. The owner asked "is this the best we can do?" and it was not: four widgets said one number, two fields were dead weight (*For* cannot exist — quick adds are Office-only; *Ended* is "just now" nineteen times in twenty), the door was a permanent dashed box that stretched the squares beside it, the button did not say the number back, and it was never drawn with the keyboard up. The mock-up keeps that list, collapsed. What shipped is the redraw: **one control per idea**.

## The change

- **[`QuickTimeAddSheet.tsx`](../../src/components/clock/QuickTimeAddSheet.tsx)** — *How long* is one bar: six cells and **＋5**; ＋5 accumulates (the owner's gesture), a cell jumps there, the lit last cell steps back (so there is no −5), and ＋5 goes quiet at 30 — the bar is the meter. *What it was* is **Call · Email · Text** and a few words, stored `Call — Acme, the Oak St invoice`. *When* is one line — ***7:40 – 7:50 pm** today · Office · ended just now* — whose *just now* opens five choices only when tapped. The button says the number: *Add time* → *Add 10 min — say what it was* → **Add 10 min**. Anchored to the **top** of the viewport and built short, because the words are typed with the keyboard up. Enter saves; Escape closes. The RPC's own sentence is shown when the database refuses, and the call is **not retried** — a first attempt that landed would turn a retry into an overlap refusal.
- **The door** — a quiet right-aligned link, *＋ quick call or email*, shown by `canUseQuickAdd` (assistant · controller · estimator · dev; off the clock; not on a salary schedule; not in training mode). It renders **under the whole clock row**, not inside the clock's slot: the Tally and Job Report squares stretch with whatever that slot holds (their "tall" mode), so `DashboardPinnedQuickRow` gains a slot element beneath the row (`QUICK_ADD_DOOR_SLOT_ID`) and `ClockInOutButton` portals the link into it; where there is no such row (Dispatch mode) it renders inline.
- **Kernel** (`quickTimeAdd.ts`): `tapQuickAddCell`, `QUICK_ADD_KINDS` / `quickAddNote`, `QUICK_ADD_AGO_CHOICES` / `quickAddAgoLabel`, `quickAddButtonLabel`. The sheet pre-checks length, words, today and the clash with the day's sessions the clock already holds; the **daily ceiling is left to the server**, so this client never selects `quick_add_minutes` and is safe to deploy before the migration is pushed (until then Save answers "function not found").
- Action colors are literal (`#2563eb`, `#16a34a`): the text tokens are pale in dark mode and made the lit cells and the button read as disabled — caught in the browser.
- Guide `add-a-quick-call-or-email-to-my-hours.md`; the to-do and its mock-up carry the redraw.

## Verify

As an assistant, off the clock: the link is under the clock row and the squares keep their height. ＋5 ＋5 → **10**, two cells lit; *Email*, a few words → **Add 10 min**; *just now* → *30 min ago* moves the window back half an hour; save → a toast, and My Time shows 0.17 h on Office. Clock in → the link is gone. A second add over the first → the sentence names the first's times. As a helper → never there.

## Live check (2026-09-21, after the migration push)

One real add from the Dashboard as a dev, labelled *ZZ TEST*: ＋5 → a few words → **Add 5 min** → the sheet closed. The row read back as `origin = 'user_punch'`, `quick_add_minutes = 5`, five minutes, note `Call — …`, on the **Office** job, dated today, pending. A second add over it was refused by the database in its own words — *You already have hours 12:01 am – 12:06 am. Pick an end time outside that, or edit that day on My Time.* The test entry was then rejected the way the pending list rejects one, so it never reaches pay.

The composer's render tests pin the clock to midday: the first full local gate ran across midnight Central, where a window "ending now" starts yesterday and is rightly refused — three tests failed until they stopped reading the wall clock. A sixth test now covers that midnight refusal on purpose.
