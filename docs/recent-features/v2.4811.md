# v2.4811 — Customer timeline: the kernel and the reads (punch list #97, PR 1 of 3) (2026-10-07)

Client only, no screen. Punch list #97 (the to-do card retired once v2.4815 was live; its ask, decisions and mock-up stay in git history at the retiring commit), the owner's ask of 2026-10-07: look up a customer and scroll one vertical timeline where each job is an arrow from the day its card was made to the day the final payment landed, office acts on the left, what we put in on the right, and what they owe us floating above. The owner took the mock-up's recommendations on all five calls ("I like what you've proposed, please build it").

## What changed

- [`customerTimeline.ts`](../../src/lib/customers/customerTimeline.ts): `buildCustomerTimeline(input, todayYmd, nowMs)`, pure. Its docblock holds every rule once: whose jobs (payer or GC), a job's name (the street when the name repeats the customer's), first seen and *First record* for imported jobs, paid in full, the rail's state on a day, four colored lanes plus one grey lane (open jobs by what they owe, paid history packed into free lanes, jobs open at once never share a color), the folds (a job's crew days until an office card or a two-week pause; three notes one person wrote on one day; three bills on one day; one notice step on two jobs), owed now through the bill-truth kernel, owed on a past day replayed bill by bill, unpaid hours and supply tickets on jobs not paid in full, and the rows (newest first, month labels, spacers that say how long, quiet folds over sixty days with nothing open).
- A billed job with no dated bill line (a job shell) gets a *Marked billed* card on its move to billed, so the money it holds has a day.
- [`fetchCustomerTimeline.ts`](../../src/lib/customers/fetchCustomerTimeline.ts): the customer and every job where they pay or are the GC (newest 200), then bills, payments, status moves, notes, crew hours, field reports, test reports, supply tickets, promises and lien papers in batches of 80 job ids, GC statements by customer, then the Mercury deposits, note and report authors and report names. Each part fails soft into `missing`, and `customerTimelineMissingWords` says what could not be read.
- Read against a live GC with a few dozen jobs as the dev: every part read, under a second to load and about 25 ms to build. PR 4 of the card's plan (one RPC) is not needed.

## Tests

`customerTimeline.test.ts` (24) plays a made-up GC with six jobs: two open of its own, one it is GC on in Collections, one imported, two paid last year. `fetchCustomerTimeline.test.ts` (5) pins the reads, the batches, the second wave, the mapping and the soft failures.
