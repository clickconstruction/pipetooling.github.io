- `BidsBidBoardTab.onNewBid`, `BidSubmissionFollowupTab.onNewBid`, `BidsBuilderReviewTab.onNewBid`: the page's New Bid. `Bids.tsx` names it `newBidPress`, the one condition the header button already used (a trade to bid in, and not a primary), so a role with no New Bid gets the words alone.

## Is this the best we can do?

Two presses are links, not handlers: the bid tabs panel's *Waiting to hear* goes to `/bids?tab=waiting-to-hear`, since the panel sits two components below the page's tab switch and the page's deep link already reads `?tab=`. Six lists keep `main` alone because their press sits right beside them, and a second copy of the same button would crowd a small card: the price requests table, Direct costs, the fixture schedule, the two assembly windows and the notes table under Submission follow-up's own add buttons. Four sentences are mine and could use the owner's words: the Won and Started or Complete piles (*No won bid is waiting for its job.* · *No won bid has a job yet.*) lean on the rule that opening the job moves a won bid to Started; *Nothing waiting to go out.* for Unsent; and Builder review's *snoozed or marked PIA* keeps the app's own PIA. The README's *No wins in this window yet* did not fit, because Submission follow-up has no date window, only the trade. The Robot Board's piles and the Estimating pulse could say more once someone decides what a person does when a robot has not run.
