The window that shows a person's Task Mode is a look: no press on their screen works. A dev who needs to press something as that person had to close the window and find them under Settings → View as. The window now has that door.

The sign-in is recorded in `user_imitations`, the dev lands on the same address as that person, and **Exit** returns to it. A role with no Review is sent to its own Today, which is the screen the look drew.

## Who sees it

`lookOffersViewAs` in `src/lib/tasks/taskLook.ts` is the whole rule. A dev sees the line, and nobody else does. The owner's window is unchanged: giving the owner a door to sign in as a teammate is a new permission, and nobody asked for it. A dev does not see it on their own row, for another dev (`login-as-user` refuses one), or for a person with no login attached.

The line sits outside `TaskLookGate`, like **Read again** and the empty week's **Add task for**. It is the viewer's own press. The look itself still writes nothing.
