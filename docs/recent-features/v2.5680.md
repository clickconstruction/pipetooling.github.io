# v2.5680 — Cover letter: deselect an alternate on the letter itself (2026-10-20)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Punch list **#138** (`to-dos/cover-letter-deselect-alternates/`; renumbered from #133 after the 2026-10-20 numbering race — #133 is the Addressed-to to-do, and this fragment first merged citing #133), PR 1 of 2. Wendi's ask, 2026-10-20: *"i want the ability to deselect alternates from the cover page in click tooling."* Her screen was a version-less bid whose two offered price copies printed the same Add twice, and Step 1's only advice was *manage offers on the Pricing tab*. All five of the to-do's calls were taken as recommended.

## What shipped

- **The version-less branch of Step 1 gets offer rows.** Three kinds of alternates reach a letter, and two already had checkboxes under *In this cover letter*: a split bid's packets with their offered prices (v2.2117 / G1 v2.2154) and the with-and-without group alternates (v2.4195). The third kind — a version-less bid's offered prices (v2.2392) — had only a sentence naming them and pointing at the Pricing tab. That branch of [`BidsCoverLetterTab`](../../src/components/bids/BidsCoverLetterTab.tsx) now lists every non-★ price of the bid as a row: a checkbox, the name with ✎ rename (`commitRenameOption` — the panel's name; a standalone offered price still prints as a bare *Alternate N* until its wording is written on the preview), and the split-bid branch's caption — *alternate · $54,990.00* / *alternate · unpriced — left off the letter* / *not offered*. The ★ base keeps its sentence, now with its price.
- **The checkbox is the offer, not a letter-only hide.** It writes `price_book_versions.include_in_submission` through the tab's existing `setScenarioOffered` — the same flag the Pricing card's bottom bar toggles — so the bid room's options, the Approval PDF's letter, the Version picker's badge and the Pricing card's caption follow by themselves, and nothing is stored twice. Unticking the last offer returns the plain single letter (`planUnsplitLetterSections` already answers empty then). A sent bid is no special case: the stamped send record never recomputes.
- **The same-amount nudge.** `sameAmountTwinNames` in [`coverLetterSamePage.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/coverLetterSamePage.ts) (tested) names, for each offered alternate, the earliest one before it at the same amount to the cent; the row wears an amber note — *same amount as "Copper water lines" — probably a copy*. That is the exact shape of Wendi's screen: the Pricing tab's **＋ Add price** door offers the new price by default (right for its purpose), so a copy made only to compare rides the letter. Unpriced rows never match; an alternate equal to the base already prints *no change*.
- **The render smoke** `BidsCoverLetterTab.offerRows.render.test.tsx` holds the branch: rows for the other prices and none for the ★, both checkbox writes, the pointer sentence only when no other price exists, and the old *manage offers on the Pricing tab* words gone.
- **The guide** *use the cover letter studio* rewrites its "One GC, one price?" example box for the rows.

PR 2 (same to-do) adds **Take off this letter** to the preview's wording editor and deletes the to-do folder.
