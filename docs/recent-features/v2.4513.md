# v2.4513 — Overhead: Man hours, the office against the field (2026-10-04)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. The owner's ask: where can I see man hours per week, month, quarter and year, between the office and the field? Nowhere did. The Overhead day table printed both for one week of days, the Bridge's Vectors had them per person for devs, and the Hours grid had a week's total with no split. This is PR 1 of 3: the numbers and the table. The picture (bars and the office-share line) and opening a number (who made it up, the jump to the day table) follow.

## What changed

- **A Man hours card on People → Overhead**, between the maintenance strip and the day table's toolbar: [`ManHoursCard.tsx`](../../src/components/people/ManHoursCard.tsx). A Week / Month / Quarter / Year switch and one row per period, oldest first: Field, Office, Bids, Not on a job, Total, Office share, Per week (hidden on Week) and People. A row wears *so far* while its period is open, *from Mar 12* when the clock started inside it, and *N h waiting* for hours recorded and not yet approved.
- **The kernel**, [`src/lib/manHours/manHoursByPeriod.ts`](../../src/lib/manHours/manHoursByPeriod.ts), folds clock sessions by the day table's own three rules (`isRecordedClockSession`, `approvedClosedSessionHours`, `overheadBucketForSession`), so the card and the table cannot disagree. Office is the Office job, bids are bid sessions, field is any other job. It also counts what the day table leaves out: time on no job and no bid, so the total ties to the Hours grid. A session on both a field job and a bid is field, once.
- **Periods**: 13 pay weeks (Sunday to Saturday, `payWeekStart`), 12 months, 8 quarters, every year. A period with no hours is a row of zeros. Office share is (office + bids) ÷ (office + bids + field). Per week is the total over the days on record, clipped to the clock's first day and to today, so a four-day month reads against a full one.
- **The read**, [`loadManHoursSessions.ts`](../../src/lib/manHours/loadManHoursSessions.ts): one paged read of closed, standing `clock_sessions`, nine columns, on mount. About 2,500 rows today. The tab passes the Office job id it already holds, so changing the Office job re-folds without a refetch. Hours only: no wage is read.
- The tab itself gains two lines (the import and the mount).

## Checked against the data

Read through the dev preview on 2026-10-04: every month from April to August is within 8 hours of the hours payroll paid except April (+56, not traced), and September is over by the 186 hours still waiting for approval. Clock sessions start on 2026-03-12; hours typed into payroll before that carry no job and are not on the card.
