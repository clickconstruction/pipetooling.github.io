# v2.8119 — Lien desk on a phone: every press on Months on this job fits a thumb (2026-11-20)

The owner's pick of 2026-11-20, out of three put to him after the Fable 5.2 audit of punch list #361 (the ledger, v2.7989): every press on the card, not *Note it as missed* alone. Client only, and styles only.

## What was wrong

The ledger gave the tick a 44 by 44 target on a phone and left the rest as they were at a desk. Measured at 390 px on made-up months: *Note it as missed* 118 by 26, *Change ›* 58 by 20, *Correct the claim ›* 111 by 18, *Record it…* and *Set on the job ›* 15 tall, *Bill it from here ›* 20. The house rule is 44.

## What changed

All of it is inside the narrow card's block of [`src/index.css`](../../src/index.css) (`@container lien-months (max-width: 560px)`), so a card wider than 560 px is untouched.

- Every `button` and `a` on the card is 44 px tall with its words centred.
- A press of words alone keeps its line. Its margin takes back what the height adds (`calc((1lh - 44px) / 2)`, with 13 px for a browser that has no `lh`), so the 44 px is the press's touch area and not its row. The words look as they did and sit on the sentence's own baseline.
- A press with an edge is that tall itself: *Note it as missed*, and the buttons of the claim's editor and the last day's editor.
- A quiet row that holds a press (the retainage line, the by-hand line) has 13 px over and under its words, the room of a 44 px row. Without it the touch areas of two such rows, one under the other, overlapped.

## Measured

In a browser on made-up months, at 390, 375 and 320 px, three notices (a draft, one with every state, one with fourteen months and three papers), light and dark:

- Every press on the card is 44 px tall or more. None was before, but the tick.
- No two presses' touch areas overlap, and none passes the card's edge.
- The card is 26 px taller (1,546 to 1,572 on the notice with every state), the two quiet rows' room and the taller *Note it as missed*.
- At 1,440 px nothing moved: the card is 897 px tall before and after.

Asked, I told the owner each word press would get a 44 px row and make the card taller. The margin keeps the line instead, so it is barely taller.
