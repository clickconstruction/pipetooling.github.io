# v2.6291 — My email: Untag on every tagged conversation, and Another job to move one (2026-11-01)

Punch list #230 (`to-dos/messages-my-email/README.md`): the review of PR 3 (v2.6274), its finding 3 and its low on the empty page, and the lead's case of a mail no line holds yet. Client only, no migration and no function: `gmail-mine`'s `tag` and `untag` stand as v2.6275 and PR 4 (v2.6279) leave them.

- **Untag on every tagged conversation** (finding 3). `reader.ts`'s `canUntag` held **Untag** back unless some mail of the conversation was not a box's line, reasoning that an untag leaves the boxes' lines where they are. It does not: it unties the box lines the tag tied, takes off the grey line and the `email_tag_threads` row (which stops the follow) and the Gmail label. With bids@ copied on every mail, as the guides advise, a wrong tag could never come off, and the follow kept tying each new box line to the wrong job. `canUntag` is now true whenever `tagged` is set, and its test says why. The confirm window keeps `confirmLabel: 'Untag'`, in words true of a conversation the boxes hold: *Untag takes this conversation off the job. The lines the tag wrote go, with their files and the copy of each mail. A line the bids@ or team@ reader took goes back to where it was.*
- **Another job… on the green bar** (finding 3). The bar after a tag, and on a tagged conversation opened later, offers **Another job…** beside **Untag**: the same `JobPickerWindow` the guess bar opens (their jobs first, then a search; a `PhoneSheet` on a phone), titled *Move this conversation onto*, with no confirm. A pick calls `tag` with the new job, which the function treats as the move; a pick of the job it is on does nothing (`picker.ts`'s `pickedJob`). The bar names the new job at once (`reader.ts`'s `taggedJob`: this sitting's tag over a mail read before it, the reader keeping the `readKey` it read at), the toast says *Moved to JP1081.* and what came along when anything did (`list.ts`'s `tagActWords`; a move never says every mail was on the job already), and the page reads again, so the row shows its new job in place.
- **A mail no line holds yet** (the lead's case). A mail of a tagged conversation whose `tagged.messageId` is null, a reply that came after the follow stopped or before it got there, opened on the green bar as if it were on the job. Its bar now says *This mail is not on the job yet.* with **Put it on the job**: the same `tag` onto the same job, safe to run again, which lands the missing mail and writes nothing twice. The toast says *Added to JP1077.* (or *Nothing new went on JP1077.*), and the bar and the row read again. **Another job…** and **Untag** stand beside it, since the conversation is tagged all the same.
- **An empty page is not "nothing waiting"** (the review's low). A page the view drew nothing of while Gmail held an older one (`nextPageToken`) drew To tag's good state, *Nothing waiting to tag.*, as if the box were clean. It now draws one quiet `EmptyState` line in `emptyWords`' data shape, *Nothing to tag on this page.* with **Older ›** as its press (`list.ts`'s `emptyPageWords`, one line per view: Tagged's *No tagged conversations on this page.*, All's *Only notices and newsletters on this page.*), and the pager under the list does not offer **Older ›** twice. *Nothing waiting to tag.* is kept for the true end. The words sit in `list.ts`, not in `view.ts`'s `MY_MAIL_WORDS`, because `view.ts` is part of the edge copy.
