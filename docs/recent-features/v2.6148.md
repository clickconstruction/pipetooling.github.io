# v2.6148 — A person's hours as fourteen days, and the pending days on the clock strip (punch list #217, people-docs-6 and -15, prototypes) (2026-10-30)

Punch list #217, *Show it, don't say it*: eight scans found 131 places where the app tells instead of shows, and a mock-up page (`to-dos/show-not-tell/mockup.html`) draws each as a before/after pair for the owner to pick from. Of people-docs-6 (the person desk's **Hours & approvals**) and people-docs-15 (People → Hours' pending banner) the owner asked on 2026-10-30 to see each as a prototype, live, side by side with how the screen looks today, before she approves. This PR is both, since they share one kernel and one surface family; a draft PR stacked on v2.6147 (the shared `SideBySide` helper), no auto-merge. No migration, no edge function.

## The findings

- **people-docs-6.** The desk read *Now · On the clock · P1318 · since 7:05 AM*, *Waiting · 1 session · 8.5h · oldest Thu 10/29*, *This week · 8.5h closed*, *Reviewed through · never*: three dates and counts in prose, the header saying the waiting hours again, the week's 8.5 h *closed* the same 8.5 h waiting. On People → Hours the **Review Hours ✓** press drew a green ✓ whether or not a review existed (the ✓ a literal in `People.tsx`).
- **people-docs-15.** The Hours grid's banner read *⚠ Pending: 3 people · 12.50 h not yet in payroll across 4 days. +7 sessions in earlier weeks.* The grid's amber cells already showed the week; the banner added a sentence about weeks the screen does not draw.

## What changed

- **`hoursDayStrip.ts`** (`src/lib/people/`, new): the pure kernel both shapes read. `hoursStripDays(sessions, { firstYmd, todayYmd })` turns clock sessions into one day each — approved hours, waiting hours, an open session's day, the kind (*waiting* outranks *approved*; *open* only with nothing closed), today, weekend; rejected and revoked rows left out; days are `work_date`, the company day the grid keys on. `reviewedThroughRule` places the grey rule after the last day on or before the review, before the strip when the review is older, nowhere with none; `reviewedThroughWords` is its hover (*reviewed through Oct 18* · *no review yet*). `personHoursHeadChip` is the header's one chip (*1 waiting · 8.5 h* · *on the clock* · *nothing waiting*), `hoursStripDayTitle` a day's hover, `hoursStripWords` the strip's sentence for a screen reader, `formatStripHours` the hours as the strip prints them. For the Hours tab: `pendingHoursByWorkDate` (closed pending hours by day), `earlierPendingCount` (the all-weeks count less the closed pending sessions on the strip's days), `earlierPendingStubWords` (*7 earlier ›*, empty at zero), `reviewApproveLabel` (*Review & approve · 12.5 h*) and `reviewHoursButtonWords`: the Review Hours press's words and whether its ✓ is drawn. Nine tests in `hoursDayStrip.test.ts`.
- **The person desk** (`PersonDeskHoursSection`): one read more and one read changed. The week's hours came from a read of closed sessions since the week's Sunday; it now reads the last fourteen days (`PERSON_HOURS_STRIP_DAYS`) with `approved_at`, and the week's hours are summed from the same rows, so the *This week* line reads as before. The open session's read adds its `work_date`. Behind a per-person switch, **Show the strip** / **Show today's look** (`useShapeView('person-desk-hours-strip')`, remembered in localStorage per user, off by default), the four lines become **`PersonDeskHoursStrip`** (new, beside the section): fourteen columns, a filled `--text-strong` dot on an approved day, an amber ring (`--text-amber-700`) on a day with a session waiting, a hollow dot for the day on the clock, a faint dot for a day with nothing, the hours under each day (amber when any wait, *now* under an open day with nothing closed), a `--border-strong` rule after the last reviewed day with *reviewed through Oct 18* on hover, today on `--bg-blue-tint` with a `--text-link` outline, the first day's date and *today* under the row, and a legend. Above the strip the header keeps one chip (`personHoursHeadChip`) beside *on the clock since 7:05 AM · P1318* or *not clocked in*, and the flag chip when the queue has flags; under it **Open approvals · 1**, **Hours grid** and **Force clock out**, the same presses as the lines had. With `?sbs=1` on the page and a dev signed in the section draws both shapes, one above the other, captioned **Today** and **With the strip**, with no switch.
- **The Review Hours press** (`People.tsx`, a plain fix, not behind the switch): a new hook, **`useHoursReviewedThrough`** (`src/hooks/`, new), reads each shown person's newest `hours_reviewed.end_date` in one query (re-read when the Review Hours window opens or closes), and `reviewHoursButtonWords` gives the press its words: *Review Hours* with no ✓ while nobody shown has a review; *Reviewed through Oct 18* with no ✓ while some shown have none (the hover says how many); *Reviewed through Oct 18 ✓* only when everyone shown has one. The date is the earliest of the shown people's newest reviews, so the press never claims more than is recorded.
- **People → Hours** (people-docs-15): `People.tsx` holds one `useShapeView('people-hours-pending-strip')` and hands it to the clock strip and the banner, so one press changes both. **`PeopleHoursDashboardClockStrip`** takes `pendingStrip`, `allWeeksPendingCount` (the `usePendingHoursApprovalsNudge` count the Approvals button already shows) and `onOpenApprovalsQueue`; it sums the closed pending hours by day from the sessions it already loads (`pendingHoursByWorkDate`) and counts the closed pending sessions on its eleven days for the stub (`earlierPendingCount`), draws the **Show the strip** press above the mini calendar, and under `?sbs=1` the mini calendar twice, captioned. **`PeopleHoursClockStripMiniCalendar`** takes `strip`: each day with hours waiting wears an amber ring (a `--text-amber-700` outline around the pill) and reads its hours under it (*3.5 h*) in place of the count, its label says *3.5 h waiting on approval*, and a stub at the row's left edge, **7 earlier ›**, opens the all-weeks queue (none at zero or while the all-weeks count is unknown). **`PeopleHoursPendingBanner`** takes `strip`: on, it keeps ⚠ and its two presses, **Review & approve · 12.5 h** (the sentence's facts on its hover) and **All weeks**, and loses the sentence and the *+7 sessions in earlier weeks* link, which the stub now carries. Under the Team scope the strip's list is the team's, so the stub counts the org's rest; the queue it opens is org-wide either way.
- **Smokes**: `PersonDeskHoursSection.hoursStrip.render.test.tsx` (today's lines, the strip after the press with its legend and presses, both under `?sbs=1`), `PeopleHoursClockStripMiniCalendar.render.test.tsx` (the counts as they are; the rings, the hours, the stub's door; no stub at zero or unknown), `PeopleHoursPendingBanner.render.test.tsx` (the sentence and its link; the presses alone with the hours; nothing pending draws nothing; both under `?sbs=1`).
- **Guides**: *open a person's desk* (the Hours & approvals bullet names the press and the strip) and *clear the hours approvals backlog* (the banner paragraph names the press, the rings and the stub).
- **Docs**: the Clock strip and Hours grid rows in `PEOPLE_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md`.

## What the mock-up asked for that this does not do

- **The person's name in the header line.** The mock-up's lead read *Hal Sample · on the clock since 7:05 AM*; the desk's own header already names the person above every section, so the section's line starts at the chip.
- **The *reviewed through* label on the strip.** The mock-up's second pass moved it to the rule's hover card; the strip does that with the browser's hover (`title`) on the rule's column and names it in the legend, so nothing moves.
- **Day labels on the Hours tab's strip.** The mock-up's second pass shortened them to the hours; the pills keep their date and weekday, and only the row under them changes from counts to hours.

## Seeing it beside today's look

A dev server from this branch, then `/dev-login?as=1&to=/people?tab=hours%26sbs=1` (or open `/people?tab=hours&sbs=1` once signed in): the clock strip's mini calendar and the amber banner each draw **Today** above **With the strip** on the live sessions. For the desk, `/people?tab=person&id=u:<userId>&sbs=1` (any person's desk, the `id` from the Users tab's link) draws **Hours & approvals** both ways. The Review Hours press on the Hours tab reads its new words either way. Without `?sbs=1` everything reads as today, and **Show the strip** on either surface turns the prototype on for that person on that device.
