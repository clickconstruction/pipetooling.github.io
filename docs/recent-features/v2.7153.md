# v2.7153 — Pay calendar: a job on its own calendar says so (2026-11-13)

Punch list #341, PR 2 of five: the mark, stacked on v2.7151. A job on its own Pay calendar read the right days and said nothing about whose they were: two rows for one payer could show two sets of days with the same words and the same hover. A job switched to no calendar looked like any payer who has none, and the payer's own window claimed every bill.

## What changed

- **The pay model knows whose calendar it read.** `billedPayModel` takes `calendarSourceFor`'s read as well as a bare calendar (`PayCalendarArg`, `asPayCalendarRead`; a bare one is the payer's, as every caller meant before). The model carries `calendarOwn`, and on a job's own calendar its hover opens *This job is on its own calendar (§ 5.2): …* where it said *<payer> pays on a calendar*. The five builders hand the read over: the board's dates ledger and phone rows, the Payment forecast, Call mode, the lien runway and the job window's bills. The days are the same either way.
- **A billed row.** The calendar's words under the dates read *This job's own · In by the 25th, paid the 30th* (`payCalendarShortFor`, over `OWN_CALENDAR_WORDS`). They are still the press that opens the window on that job.
- **A job switched off.** Under a billed row's *Pays in* line, one quiet press: *No pay calendar on this job*. It opens the window on the job, where **Follow <payer> again** sits. Before, that row had no press that reached it.
- **The cutoff line.** `PayCutoffLine` takes `own`: a Ready to Bill row's *Send by* line and a Working row's *Bill by* line end *This job's own calendar.*, and the hover leads with it.
- **Cutoffs coming.** `buildCutoffsComing` reads `calendarSourceFor`. A line that is one job's own calendar is keyed by the job and named for it: *Larkfield Construction · 1431 only · in by Nov 25, due Dec 30*. `CutoffWork` gains `jobLabel` and `CutoffComing` gains `ownJob`. A job on its own row gets its own line even when its days match the payer's.
- **The payer's window lists the jobs that do not follow it.** On the payer's side of `PayCalendarModal`: *2 jobs do not follow this*, each job by its board label with *Its own: In by the 25th, paid the 30th* or *No pay calendar on this job*. Each is a press that reopens the window on that job (`onOpenJob`, handed in by the provider, which keeps the opener's `onSaved`). The foot reads *Every other bill this payer pays reads it.* `loadPayCalendarJobs` reads the jobs' names, developments and who pays each today, after the window has drawn.
- **A calendar held for a payer who no longer pays the job** is said in that list, in amber: *Larkfield Construction no longer pays this job, so no bill reads it.* The press opens it, and **Follow <payer> again** clears it. This is the card's gap 5, at the owner's first pick on call 5: the calendar stays with the payer it was set for.
- **Guide**: two paragraphs in *One job on a different calendar*.

## Not changed

- Which calendar a bill reads, and every date. `calendarFor` and the model's days are as they were; a test compares a job's own read with the payer's on the same days.
- The summary line for a payer in Money waiting and Dashboard Accounts Receivable still reads the payer's own calendar. It rests on whose record scores a job, which is PR 3 and the owner's call 2, so it moves with that.
- The job window's bills and Call mode carry the mark in the hover only. Their rows have no line for the calendar's words.
- The four screens v2.7140 moved onto the calendar (the Development page's Money look, Messages' They owe us, the residential flow, Quickfill's Billed awaiting payment) read a job's own calendar over its payer's, as every reader does. They hand the model a bare calendar, so their hover does not carry the mark.

## Deploy

No migration. The edge copies of `payCalendar.ts` and `billedDuePay.ts` under `supabase/functions/_shared/gcKernels/` (they arrived with v2.7140) are regenerated here and in v2.7151, since both PRs add to those kernels. The functions that read them pass a bare calendar, so a redeploy changes nothing they send; `npm run check:edge-drift` lists them as behind until the next deploy of each.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **The mark is words, not a chip.** The mock-up drew a chip. On a billed row the words sit in a line that is already one press, and a chip inside a press reads as a second control. The words are the same ones the job window's row leads with.
- **The list in the payer's window has no count limit.** A payer with thirty jobs on their own calendars gets thirty lines. If *Also set on the other N jobs* is ever used on a big development, the list wants a fold.
- **The stale word is only in the payer's window.** A job whose payer changed says nothing on its own row or in its job window, since both read the new payer. Reaching it means opening the old payer's window.
