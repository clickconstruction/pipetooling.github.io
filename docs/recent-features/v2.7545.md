# v2.7545 — Checklist: close a task that no longer applies (2026-11-16)

Punch list #369, step C4 of the Task Mode train (tool 3), stacked on C3 (v2.7544). It calls `task_no_longer_applies` from C1's migration.

## Why

A task had two ends: done, or deleted by the office. A person holding a task that had stopped mattering could only leave it, so it stood in the list for months beside the ones that still wanted doing.

## What changed

- **No longer applies**, the second row of **⋯ More**, on an open one-time task (`taskMoreChoices`). Not offered on a task that repeats, a roadmap task or a vehicle task.
- **The sheet** (`TaskDropSheet`): three reasons as one press each, *Done another way*, *Plans changed*, *A double of another task* (`DROP_REASONS`), and a box for the person's own words. The press is dead and reads *Say why first* until the reason is three characters long, then reads **Close this task** (`dropRule`, `dropPressLabel`). A green line says what happens: *It leaves your list. Imani is told and can put it back.*
- **The task closes** (`dropTask` → `task_no_longer_applies`). It leaves Today and Still open as a finished task does.
- **It says what it is.** Under Done today its line reads *Closed 12:22 PM as no longer applying. Plans changed.* (`droppedLineWords`), not *Done*. History's row wears a grey **No longer applies** chip beside its green tick, with the reason as its tip. The Review row reads *closed* where it read *done*, with the same chip.
- **The giver is told** by a push: *Theo closed "Set a marker at every mile" as no longer applying: Plans changed*.
- **Putting it back** is the Reopen that exists, on History for the person and on Review for the giver. A reopened task is open again and no longer reads closed.

## Why a roadmap task is left out

A close is a completion, so that every list that hides finished work hides it. Completing a roadmap task's day row marks the roadmap task done at 100 percent, and a vehicle task's marks the service done. Neither is true of work nobody did, so the database refuses both and the screen does not offer them. The tasks in the owner's screenshot wore a roadmap tag, so this tool will not clear those: they can be moved to another day, and the roadmap's lead takes them off the roadmap. That is the one place this train falls short of the mock-up's promise, and it is said here so it is not found by surprise.

## How it is built

- `droppedReason` in `checklistCardEvents.ts`: the last `dropped` line stands unless a `reopened` came after it. One rule for the card, History and Review.
- `taskDoerActs.ts` gains the reasons, the rule, the words and the push. `useTaskDoerActs` gains the sheet and `onDrop`.
