# v2.7762 — Stages: the ⚖ chip of a job's legal matter draws on its row (2026-11-18)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. A fix that puts something on the screen. Punch list **#46** row 2 (`to-dos/decomposition-queue.md`): the Stages map's step 21 (`docs/JOBS_STAGES_TAB_ARCHITECTURE.md`), §9.

## What was wrong

- v2.3313 shipped a ⚖ chip beside the contract chip on Pipeline rows and phone cards, and the Glossary says a Collections row carries it. The chip's code is there (`legalRowChip`, drawn by `StagesJobCellActivityFooter` and the card footer) and tested.
- It never drew. The chip reads the row context's `legalMatterByJobId`. `JobsStagesTab` put the matters in its bundle, but neither table's props had the field and no list copied it into the context it built.
- Step 10 (v2.7719) typed the bundle, which refused the key nobody took, and wrote the finding down.

## What shipped

- **Three lines of app code**: `legalMatterByJobId` is a field of `StagesTableShared`; the tab's bundle fills it from `useLegalMatters`; `stagesRowContext` hands it to the rows. All four lists get it through that one builder (step 13).
- **Who sees it**: the office (dev, master, assistant, controller). The board asks for the matters only for those roles, so no other role's rows can carry a chip.
- **What it says**: *⚖ review requested* in blue; *⚖ new*, *⚖ demand sent*, *⚖ suit filed* and the other with-the-firm stages in amber; a closed matter in grey. A job with no matter, or one simply under review in house, wears none.
- **Help guide**: *review a Collections account before it goes to your attorney* gains four sentences under *Ask a dev to review*: what the Pipeline row wears after a request and once the account is with the firm.
- **Docs**: the Stages map's §9 note and step 21; the bundle's key count; the playbook's Stages row. The Glossary already said the row carries the chip.

## Tests (+4)

- `JobsStagesTab.legalChip.render.test.tsx` (4), through the real board with the matters faked: a table row wears *⚖ demand sent* or *⚖ review requested*, and a Working row wears its matter's too; a phone card wears the same words; with no matters there is no chip anywhere; a role outside the office is never handed the matters.
- **The first two cases fail on the code before the change** (no chip) and pass now. The other two pass on both.

- With no matter in a state that speaks, the board's HTML is byte for byte the same before and after, in ten modes.
