# v2.3888 — Supply houses on a phone: the card's balance, the house screen (2026-09-27)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Punch list **#30**, PR 5c (`to-dos/taunya-mobile.md`; the mock-up `to-dos/taunya-mobile-pr5-rows.html`). The owner's calls (2026-09-27): a house's balance is on its card for office roles only; the notepad is the house's existing notes field, editable in place. Builds on [v2.3885](v2.3885.md) for the bottom sheet.

## What the code had

The aging matrix already drew rows under 640 px (v2.2191). The summary table (Outstanding · Due · Updated) and the ten-column invoices table inside an opened house did not. The directory card carried Call and Email but no money, by a rule written in its header. Notes were `supply_houses.notes`, edited only through the Edit window. The balance per house was computed inline in the tab's loader, with no test.

## What shipped

- **`src/lib/materials/supplyHousePhone.ts`** (new, pure) — `summarizeSupplyHouseBalances` (the loader's arithmetic, moved verbatim in behaviour: every unpaid paper nets, credits included; the newest update and payment; most owed first), `supplyHouseCardMoney` (balance, aging shares from the matrix kernel, *pays on the 10th*), `supplyHouseInvoiceTabCounts` and `supplyHousePhoneInvoiceRows` (unpaid · paid · open credits; a row's number and PO, due day, job account, jobs with their shares, one amount, days past due), `ordinalDay`.
- **`src/components/materials/SupplyHousePhoneScreen.tsx`** (new) — a full-screen dialog: Back · the name · Edit; the balance, pay day and *Call counter*; the aging bar; **Notes on this house** as a textarea with Save (disabled until changed; *not saved yet* → *saved to the house*); Add invoice and Make payment; the three tabs; rows of 52 px; a row's sheet (`ScheduleSheet`) with Mark paid / unpaid (*Mark applied* on a credit), View the invoice, View the receipt, Edit. Escape closes it.
- **`SupplyHousesTab.tsx`** — the loader calls the kernel; under 640 px the summary is rows that open the house's screen, and the expanded table row is the desk's alone; the notes save is one `supply_houses` update, after which the directory refetches; on the desk the opened house's phone is a `tel:` link.
- **`SupplyHouseDirectory.tsx`** — `moneyByHouseId`: the phone card's balance line and aging bar, drawn only for `audience="office"` and only when the accounts-payable tab hands it in. The estimator's directory passes nothing and shows no money. The header's rule is amended to say so.
- **The guide** *mark-supply-house-bills-paid* → *On a phone*.
