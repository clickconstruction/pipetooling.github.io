# v2.5484 — Messages: turn on the reply push from Settings (2026-10-13)

Residential R5's push, the Settings press (the lead, after helper 2's PR 3, v2.5481): the switch for the minute reply
push on Settings → Phone and texts, so the owner turns it on after the live test instead of a dev setting the row. No
migration and no edge function: the row, `app_settings.messages_reply_push_on_v1` ('false' as it ships), and its UPDATE
policy `master_or_dev_update_messages_reply_push_on` came with v2.5481 (`20261014110000_messages_notify_cron.sql`).
Built as the bids@ reader's card is (v2.5380). Guide `turn-on-the-reply-push`.

- **Reply push** (`ReplyPushSettingsCard.tsx`, last in Phone and texts): for the owner and a dev (`canSwitchReplyPush`,
  the client's copy of the policy's `is_master_or_dev()`), the rest of the office sees no card. A switch
  (`role="switch"`) sets the row to `'true'` or `'false'` (`replyPushSwitchIo.ts`). Turning it on asks first
  (`REPLY_PUSH_CONFIRM`), since every reply after it reaches a phone; turning it off does not. A refused change keeps
  the switch and says why (`REPLY_PUSH_NOT_SAVED`), as does a row that is not there before v2.5481's migration is pushed.
- **The words** (`replyPushSwitch.ts`): what the push does for a customer, a lead or a sub, and the state under the
  switch, *Pushes go out within a minute.* or *Off: nobody is pushed when a customer answers.* No health line: nothing
  records the sweep's runs.
- `replyPushIsOn` reads only `'true'`, the word the cron's statement and `messages-notify` read. The key is
  `REPLY_PUSH_ON_KEY` from `messages/notifyPlan.ts`, its one home, shared with the edge copy.
- **Not yet**: the three switch cards on Phone and texts (Read bids@, Send texts from #5646, and this one) are one shape
  three times; folding them into one card is a later mechanical sweep.
- **Tests**: `replyPushSwitch.test.ts` (the role rule, the word the cron reads, the state words, every string plain) and
  `ReplyPushSettingsCard.render.test.tsx` over a fake io.
