# v2.7719 — Stages: the section lists take the shared bundle as one typed prop (2026-11-17)

Client only; no migration, no edge function, no screen change. Punch list **#46** row 2 (`to-dos/decomposition-queue.md`): the Stages map's step 10 (`docs/JOBS_STAGES_TAB_ARCHITECTURE.md`).

## What shipped

- **`src/components/jobs/stagesTableShared.ts`** (new): `StagesTableShared` (62 fields) and `StagesUnifiedTableShared` (11 more, what a bill row reads). Every field moved word for word out of `JobsStagesTableProps` and `JobsStagesUnifiedTableProps`, which had each declared the 62 common ones. Beside the types sits `stagesTableFlatProps`, which lays a list's bundle beside its own props as one flat object.
- **The four lists** (`JobsStagesTable`, `JobsStagesUnifiedTable`, and the two card lists in `JobsStagesCardList`) take the bundle as the one prop `shared`, with the section's own rows, labels and moves beside it: 15 fields on the job lists, 27 on the bill lists. Each flattens the two at its door, so no line under a door changed. The two unified row files read the same flat `rowCtx` and did not change.
- **`JobsStagesTab.tsx`**: the two bundles are typed (`StagesTableShared`, `StagesUnifiedTableShared`) and handed whole, `shared={…}`, at the twelve sites that used to spread them: the seven board sections and the five follow-up deck rows.
- **A key no list ever read is gone.** Typing the bundle refused `legalMatterByJobId`: the tab has passed it since v2.3313, and neither table's props ever took it, so it reached nothing. The row context still has the field and the ⚖ legal chip still reads it, which means that chip has never drawn on a board row. Wiring it would change the screen, so it is left for its own PR and the owner's call.
- **Cut by script on exact anchors**: the field split is computed from the tab's own bundle literals, and the script stops if a field's declaration differs between the two tables or if any anchor is not found exactly once. The render tests' prop helpers were converted the same way, each override moved to its side.
- **Sizes**: `JobsStagesTable` 596 → 531 lines, `JobsStagesUnifiedTable` 508 → 429, `stagesTableShared.ts` 116. The tab stays at 5,208 lines, since the step moves a seam and no lines.
- **Docs**: the Stages map is re-stamped (534 line numbers carried forward from `58caa94ee` by the diff's hunks, 476 checked against their line's text by the script and the rest read again by hand; `mapped_at` is `1ace2247c`), with step 10, the substrate's row-render paragraph, the two table dossiers and the master summary amended; the playbook's Stages row; the decomposition card's row 2 and `next`.
