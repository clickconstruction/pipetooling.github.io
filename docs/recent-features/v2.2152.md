# v2.2152 — Partnerships on a phone: contained card, tabs in view, stacked Ledger rows (2026-08-23)

Owner screenshot of **Partnerships → Ledger** on an iPhone: "this doesn't look right".
Reproduced at 375px — four problems. Mockup (before/after):
claude.ai/code/artifact/b0a5bfb0-268e-436b-b61f-aeb48c6ca61e

- **Card overflowed the screen** — [`Partnerships.tsx`](../../src/pages/Partnerships.tsx)'s
  mobile grid column was `1fr` (min-content auto), so the detail card grew to the ledger
  table's width and pushed the Balance column *and the Ledger tab itself* (the default tab)
  off the right edge. Now `minmax(0, 1fr)`, matching desktop.
- **Active tab stays in view** — tab buttons get refs; an effect `scrollIntoView`s the
  active one on open/switch; on phones a 28px gradient fade at the strip's right edge
  signals more tabs instead of a hard cut.
- **Ledger rows stack on phones** — [`PartnershipLedgerTab.tsx`](../../src/components/partnerships/PartnershipLedgerTab.tsx)
  renders (`useIsMobile`) the same two-line shape as the partner's Full ledger (v2.2116):
  short date (`shortDate`) over the posting on the left, amount over `bal …` on the right.
  Labor rows read `Labor · 12.85 h · week of Aug 9` (`postingLabel` + the stub's
  `period_start`); pending charges show amber **pending** where the balance would be; note
  rows keep the grey band + italic memo + "partner sees" chip and tap to edit (no drag
  triangle on touch); the draft-preview row keeps its dashed style; every drillable row ends
  in a muted › (the hover-only chevron never showed on phones). Tap-to-place while the
  composer is open still works; the tip says "tap" and drops the drag sentence.
- **Balance header** — on phones the headline and **+ note** share line one, the caption
  sits under them (flex `order`), instead of the three wrapping around each other.
- Desktop: the four-column table and everything else unchanged. Client-only — no migration.
