# v2.5051 — Submittals: the watching switch reads in dark mode (2026-10-09)

Client only. No migration, no edge function. Seen live on BP398's Share step on 2026-10-09: Pat Ortega, set to watching, showed a blank white box where the switch's pressed half should read *watching*.

## The change

- **The pressed *watching* segment** (`SubmittalRoomPanel.tsx`) was `white` text on `var(--text-strong)`. In dark mode `--text-strong` is `#f9fafb`, so the word vanished into its own fill. Its text is now `var(--surface)`, the inverse pair the app's other dark switches use (the Keep switch in `SubmittalHouseFileModal.tsx`, `segBtn` in `OverheadDialsPopover.tsx`). In light mode `--surface` is `#ffffff`, so light mode is pixel-identical.
- **The *deciding* segment keeps its saturated `#16a34a` green with white text**, since saturated status colors stay literal.
- The other `white`-on-`--text-strong` buttons in the room's family (`RoomRevisionChips` in `SubmittalRoomView.tsx`, and the public page `SubmittalRoom.tsx`) render only inside `data-theme="light"` wrappers, so they always read light and are left as they are.

## Tests

- `SubmittalRoomPanel.render.test.tsx`: a person deciding and a person watching. It reads both theme blocks from `src/index.css` and requires the *watching* segment, pressed or not, to be theme tokens whose text contrasts with its fill at 4.5:1 or better in light and in dark. The *deciding* segment keeps its literal green and white. It fails on the old `white` text, on text in the fill's own token, on a tokenized green, on a faint unpressed text, and on a near-white dark `--surface`.
