# v2.8012 — Before we start: Go or hold, with a name on the decision (2026-11-19)

Punch list #382, PR 7 of eleven: Go or hold, the last check and the hold note. It closes the office's loop. It writes through `start_check_decide` and `start_check_take_back` (v2.7997). No migration and no edge function. Nothing here reaches the GC.

## What a person sees

The foot of an open stage has **Go or hold** for a person who may decide: the office and the job's superintendent (the owner's call 3, on its default). Beside it: *Your name goes on the decision.*

The panel, **Go or hold?**, says what is still open and lists it, then two boxes.

- **Go.** *Send the crew for Tue Dec 1.* With nothing open the press reads **Go**. With something open it reads **Go, on their promise**, and the box says *This is a go on their promise. It comes back to you Mon Nov 30, the day before the crew leaves.*
- **Hold.** *Keep the crew home, and move our start to this day.* A date, a week on from our first day to start. Under it is the note for the contact, and **Hold until (day)**.
- *Nothing is blocked. A crew can be scheduled with no Go.*

After a Go the foot reads *Go. Dana, Nov 17.* with **Take the Go back**. After a hold it reads *On hold. Dana, Nov 17.* with **Take the hold back** and **Go or hold** again.

On the Schedule look, a card's one press now reads what comes next and opens on it: **Ask (contact)** opens the stage with the ask in hand, **Go or hold** and **Go** open it on the decision. A person who may not decide is offered **Open**.

## What each press does

| Press | What is kept | What moves |
|---|---|---|
| Go | Who pressed it, the day, the words of every bullet open then, and our first day as it stood | Nothing |
| Hold | The same, and the day our start moved to | Our window in the Stage Plan moves to that day, its length kept |
| Take it back | Who took it back and when. The decision stays as a row. | Nothing. A hold taken back moves no day. |

The name is read from the caller's own row by the database, so it cannot be typed. A hold on a stage with no days of ours is refused in words, and the panel says to plan our days first.

## A hold tells nobody by itself

The owner's call 4, on its default. After **Hold until (day)** lands, the note is in hand in the same panel the ask uses, titled **The note for (contact)**: *A hold tells nobody by itself. Change any word, then send the note.* **Text it** and **Email it** open the customer's box in Messages filled, and send nothing in the press. **I texted it**, **I emailed it** and **I called** keep it without sending. A hold note sent is kept as one row for its stage (`start_check_asks.kind = 'hold_note'`).

The note is `holdText`, written from the stage as it stood before its days moved: what we planned, what must be true first as a numbered list, and our next opening.

## The last check

`stageStanding` gained a row. A go on their promise with something still open turns red on the day before the crew leaves: *Dana pressed Go Nov 21 with 1 thing open. The last check is today.* Its press is **Go or hold**. After that day it reads *The last check was Sun Nov 29.* The office's Needs you line already counts what is still owed on that stage.
