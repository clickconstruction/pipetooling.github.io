# v2.2669 — Job-account invoice flag: owner-secured supply house debt (2026-09-03)

Taunya's ask: when entering a supply house invoice and assigning the J#, record that it rides on the house's **job account** — the per-property account the v2.1605 "Share with supply house" packet sets up. We still pay the house normally; the flag classifies whose exposure an unpaid balance is (the property owner's, not ours). Mockup-approved before build.

## DB (migration `20260903100000` — fragment in docs/migrations/)

- `supply_house_invoices.on_job_account boolean NOT NULL DEFAULT false`. Classification only — nothing excludes flagged invoices from AP totals, job costs, Dashboard financials, or Moneyfill.

## Entry — `SupplyHousesTab` Add/Edit Invoice modal

- "On job account" checkbox under Job allocations; per-property, so enabled only when the invoice is allocated to **exactly one** job (0 jobs / multi-job states explain why). Save forces the flag off when the single-job condition no longer holds.
- Cross-check against the `supply_house_job_accounts` share ledger (read-only): checked + packet on record → green "Job account on file — packet sent to {contact} {date} by {name}"; checked + no record → amber nudge (accounts opened by phone exist; not a block) pointing at Job Detail → Share with supply house.
- Pre-push safety: the payload includes `on_job_account` only when the value changed, so untouched invoice saves keep working in the merge-to-`db push` window; the Job Accounts tab likewise falls back to a select without the column (flags read false) until the push lands.
- Flagged invoices show a teal "Job acct" chip in the house's invoice table (Jobs column) and the Make Payment picker.

## Risk lens — `MaterialsJobAccountsTab` + `jobAccountsFlow` kernel

- Kernel input gains `on_job_account`; unpaid flagged dollars accumulate into `owedOnJobAccount` (row + house group) **instead of** the aging `owedBuckets` — a past-due job-account invoice is the house's collection problem and stays out of the red heat. `suppliersOwed`/`owed`/`held` are unchanged.
- View totals: `onJobAccountTotal`/`onJobAccountJobs` (owed job-account dollars across rows) and `holdingOnJobAccount` (job-account-first attribution: per owe_suppliers row, `min(owedOnJobAccount, held)`).
- UI (all conditional on nonzero job-account dollars, so the page is unchanged until the flag is used): Holding card gains a "Your account / Job accounts" split line; new teal "On job accounts" stat card; "On job account" filter chip; teal-striped legend swatch + Out-bar segment (rendered between paid and the aging ramp); teal per-row "$X on job acct" / "all on job acct" line under the right-hand figure; expanded house rows badge their job-account share and the footer restates the split.
- Teal is literal (`#0f766e`/`#ccfbf1`/`#14b8a6`) — no teal theme tokens exist; saturated status colors stay literal per CLAUDE.md.
- Kernel tests: 4 new cases (split vs aging buckets, paid invoices contribute nothing, held-capped attribution, awaiting_customer counted in view total but not holding).
