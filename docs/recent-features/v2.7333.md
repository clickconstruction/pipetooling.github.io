# v2.7333 — The Lien desk's Board: every chip and every job number is a zoom (PR 3 of 4) (2026-11-15)

Client only. The owner's call after the Board's first drawing: every chip is a press to the right place for its stage, and the same press goes on the job numbers in the To do rows and the list rows, so a number is a zoom everywhere on the desk. PR 1 (v2.7330) gave each chip its `destination`; PR 2 (v2.7332) drew the Board; this PR walks the doors.

## What landed

- `LienDesk.tsx`: `zoomTo(chip)` opens the chip's `destination` through the desk's own doors — `property` → `setPaneFix` (the office; anyone else reads the paper, whose gates name the line), `run` → the run window, `lienWindow` → `onOpenLienAffidavit` (else `onOpenLienInstruments`), `paper` → the notice or the affidavit pane, through `onOpenNotice` when stacked as before, else `onZoomAddress` plus the desk's own `setKind` / selection. `zoomJob(jobId, kind)` finds the job's chip (`lienBoardChipFor`, the notice's first for `either`) and falls back on the Job window for a job not on the Board; `zoomFor` hands a row the press and its hover words (`lienBoardDoorWords`). `<LienBoard onChip={zoomTo}>`; `LienDeskNextUp` takes `zoomFor`; the calendar takes `onZoomJob`; the notice and affidavit list rows draw their label through `listLabel`, the number a focusable span (`data-lien-list-zoom`, `role="link"`, Enter and Space) inside the row's button that keeps the click, the name as before.
- `LienDeskNextUp.tsx`: with `zoomFor` a computer row's title is two presses, the number (`lien-next-up-zoom-<job>`) with the zoom's words and the name (`lien-next-up-job-<job>`) with the job's door; a row the Board has no chip for, or a phone row, keeps the one door.
- `LienDeskCalendarTab.tsx`: `onZoomJob` is the number's door when given (`numberDoor`), on the grid, Later and the phone rows; `onOpenJobWindow` stays the fallback.
- `LienDeskPage.tsx`: `onZoomAddress` writes the paper's address as an entry of its own (`state: { fromBoard: true }`), so Back returns to To do, where the Board sits at the top; the desk's own address effect then finds the address already written and replaces nothing.
- Kernel: `lienBoardChipFor` and `lienBoardDoorWords` in `lienBoard.ts` (+ tests).
- Smokes per destination kind in `LienDesk.render.test.tsx` (property, paper notices, run, paper affidavits, Lien window, the To do number, the By paper number), the number in `LienDeskNextUp.render.test.tsx` and `LienDeskCalendarTab.render.test.tsx`.

## The one thing "is this the best we can do" changed

A paper opened from a chip went through the desk's `setKind`, and the page wrote the new address with `replace`, so Back left the desk for the Pipeline. The owner asked for Back to return to the Board. So a zoom onto a paper goes through the page as a pushed entry, the way a phone's notice already did (`onOpenNotice`), and the record and the run, which open over the Board, need nothing.
