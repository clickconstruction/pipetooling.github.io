# v2.7058 — Tasks: the groundwork for a place on a task (2026-11-12)

Punch list #328, PR 1 of 8. Grace asked for a geographic point on a task, a photo beside it and a map of a person's tasks at the top of Task Mode, saw the card and the mock-up (`to-dos/task-places/`), and said to build the whole train. This PR is the place itself. It has no screen.

## What changed

- **The columns** (`20261112220000_task_places.sql`): six nullable columns on `checklist_items`, the task as written, so a repeating task keeps its place every day. `place_name`, `place_lat`, `place_lng`, `place_accuracy_m`, `place_set_by`, `place_set_at`. Three checks hold a place whole: a point is both numbers or neither and on the globe, a name never stands without its point, a margin is not negative. No new table and no new policy: whoever may read or edit the task may read or edit its place.
- **The rules and the words** (`src/lib/tasks/taskPlace.ts`), pure, 21 tests:
  - `taskPlaceFromRow` and `taskPlaceColumns`: a row to a place and back. No place writes six nulls, so a save can never trip the table's check.
  - `recentTaskPlaces` and `matchingTaskPlace`: the places to offer as chips, one a name, newest first, and the chip a task's own place lights.
  - `taskPlaceDistanceWords`, `taskPlaceDirection`, `taskPlaceHowFarWords`: *250 ft north-east of you.* Feet to the nearest ten under a fifth of a mile, miles past it, and *You are at the place.* inside 30 ft.
  - `taskPlaceAccuracyWords`, `isPoorTaskPlaceFix`, `taskPlaceFixWords`: *within 20 ft*, rounded up so the words never claim more than the phone did. A reading wider than about 150 ft is not taken quietly: *Your phone is only sure within 400 ft. Drag the map until the ring is on the spot.*
  - `taskPlaceDots` and `tasksWithoutPlaceWords`: the numbered dots for a list of tasks and the line for the tasks a map cannot show.
- **Directions to a point** (`directionsUrlForPoint` in `src/lib/directionsUrl.ts`): the app's directions links took an address. A spot on a property has none.
