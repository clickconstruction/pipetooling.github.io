# v2.7957 — Lien letters: one period after a name that ends in one (2026-11-19)

Client only: no migration, no edge function. Found on 2026-11-16 on a § 53.056 packet built by hand, whose original contractor's name ends in *Assoc.*.

## Before

Counsel's cover letters (`src/lib/jobs/gcOnNotice.ts`) end several sentences on the original contractor's name: *working under ${gc}.*, *any further payment to ${gc}.*, *before the next payment to ${gc}.* A name that ends in a period brought its own, and the sentence added a second: *working under Acme & Assoc..* It happened three times on the standard letter, and the same way after our own name (*payable only to ${us}.*). The retainage cover letter (`lienRetainageCoverLetter`) is written the same way.

## What changed

- `oneClosingPeriod(text, names)` in the new `src/lib/jobs/nameClosesSentence.ts`: for each name that ends in a period, the period the letter adds straight after it goes. A name with no period at its end changes nothing. The name inside a sentence (*Acme & Assoc. has not paid us*), its possessive, and a typed ellipsis are left alone.
- `defaultGcNoticeCoverLetter` (the commercial, residential, homestead and unresponsive letters) and `paidOutOwnerLetter` run it over the original contractor's name and ours. The templates themselves are untouched: counsel's words are as counsel wrote them.
- `counselCoverLetterTemplate` runs it over a letter saved on a desk item, so a letter saved with the doubled period prints with one. The saved text is not rewritten.
- `lienRetainageCoverLetter` (`lienNoticeDraft.ts`) runs it too.

## Tests

`nameClosesSentence.test.ts` (4). `gcOnNotice.test.ts` (+3): each of counsel's letters under *Acme & Assoc.* and *Example Plumbing Co.* has no doubled period and equals, character for character, the same letter under stand-in names with the names put in; a name with no period leaves the letter as it was; a saved letter prints with one period. `lienNoticeDraft.test.ts` (+1): the retainage letter, the same two checks.

## Not changed

The courtesy notice and the text reminder (`lienCourtesyNotice.ts`) end a sentence on our own name only, and the company's name has no period.
