# v2.5638 — Quick replies by keyboard: a one-word shortcut per line (2026-10-19)

Punch list #126, the owner's pick C, after A (v2.5637, the `/` list in the reply box). A line may now carry one short word of its own, so `/bill` then Enter lands on one line. One migration, `20261019040000_quick_replies_shortcut.sql` (`docs/migrations/`). No edge function changes what it does, though `quickReplies/kernel.ts` is one of the kernels copied under `_shared/gcKernels/` and now imports `shortcut.ts`, so the copy regenerates here (`npm run gen:edge-kernels`, 110 files; CI's first run caught the stale copy) and `check:edge-drift` names the importers to redeploy after the merge.

- **The column** (`quick_replies.shortcut`): lowercase letters, digits and dashes, 24 at most, starting with a letter or a digit (`quick_replies_shortcut_word`), null for none. One live team line a word (`quick_replies_shortcut_team_one`) and one of a person's own lines a word among theirs (`quick_replies_shortcut_mine_one`), both partial on `removed_at IS NULL`, so a removed line frees its word.
- **The function** (`set_quick_reply_shortcut(uuid, text)`): sets or clears (null or blank) a line's word, a leading `/` and the case forgiven; refuses a bad word, a word another live team line holds and, for a line of one's own, a word one's own other line holds (two people's own lines, and a team line beside someone's own, may share one); a `shortcut` version with the word before and after in `from_text` and `to_text`, as `es` keeps the Spanish (`quick_reply_versions_kind_known` and `_kept` widened); returns 1, or 0 when nothing changed. The quick replies' own refusals through `quick_reply_caller`. `restore_quick_reply` now brings a line back without its word when another live line took it meanwhile, with a shortcut version first, rather than failing on the index.
- **The kernel** (`src/lib/quickReplies/shortcut.ts`): `shortcutWord` cleans and checks a word as the function does, `shortcutHolder` finds the line that would refuse it, `shortcutTakenWords` and `SHORTCUT_WORDS` say so. `kernel.ts`: `QuickReply.shortcut`, `QuickReplyEdit.shortcut`, `editQuickReply`'s `shortcut` version after the phone's, `versionWords` (*gave it a shortcut*, *cleared its shortcut*, *changed its shortcut*, the words as `/bill`). `slash.ts`'s `slashLines`: a line whose shortcut is the typed words first, then the shortcuts that start with them, then the lines whose words hold them, each in the strip's order, every line once.
- **The read and the write** (`quickRepliesIo.ts`, `rows.ts`): the word is read apart (`select('id, shortcut')`), so a client ahead of the push still reads the lines (`isNotThereYetError`); `setShortcut` calls the function, a clear going as blank.
- **The screens**: the editor's opened line takes **Shortcut** (`/` and one word), checked before the Save goes (`shortcutHolder`), saved through its own function after the words' (`QuickRepliesEditor`'s `onSave`, as the big button's is); every row with a word shows a `/bill` chip; each row of the `/` list shows its `/bill` at the left.
- **Tests**: `shortcut.test.ts` (the cleaning, who holds a word, the Save's version and refusal, the history's words, the rows); `slash.test.ts` (the word first, the starts next, a line found by its word alone, the case, each line once, no reorder with nothing typed); `quickRepliesIo.test.ts` (the word read apart, the function's arguments, the lines still read before the column); `QuickRepliesEditor.shortcut.render.test.tsx` (the chip, a word set and cleaned, a clear, a taken word and a bad one said before the Save, a team line beside someone's own); `ReplyBox.slash.render.test.tsx` (a typed shortcut first with its `/thx`). The SQL bed `scripts/pgtest-quick-replies-shortcut.sh` over `supabase/tests/quick_replies_shortcut/` (the migration applied twice; set, repeat, clear, the team's word, one's own words, every refusal, a removed line freeing its word and coming back without it, the two indexes and the word's check below the functions), registered in `scripts/sql-beds.txt`.
- **Docs**: the guide *edit the quick replies* gains **Give a line a shortcut**, *reply to a person from their thread*'s **Type / for a quick reply** names it; the Glossary's R3 line, `MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md`'s reply box paragraph and `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`'s cut 3 paragraph.
