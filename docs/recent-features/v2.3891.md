# v2.3891 — Dashboard on a phone: counts on every fold, Job report and Tally as quiet links (2026-09-27)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Two of the owner's picks of 2026-09-27 on punch list #30's leftovers (L5, L6), in one PR because both are the Dashboard.

## What shipped

- **Counts on the three folds that had none.** `DashboardCrewDaySection` (`onSummary`), `DashboardRecentReportsSection` (`onCounts`) and `DashboardMyTimeSection` (`onWeekSeconds`) each report their own number to the host; `phoneFolds.ts` gains `crewDayFoldHeadline` (*12 people · 14 jobs · 8 flags*, amber with flags), `recentReportsFoldHeadline` (*3 new · 12 listed*) and `myTimeFoldHeadline` (*6.2 h this week*). Each is null until its section has read, so the line shows the name alone while loading. The sections draw as before.
- **Job Report and Tally are quiet on an office login's phone.** `DashboardPinnedQuickRow` takes `quietSquares`: the clock row stands alone and *Job report* and *Tally · N* are two text links under it. The pick was *hide them*; they are made small rather than removed, because Job Report opens a window that lives on this row and has no other door on a phone — removing the button would have removed the feature.
- **The guide** *start-here-in-the-office* → *Your day*.
