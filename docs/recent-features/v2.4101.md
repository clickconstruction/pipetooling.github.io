# v2.4101 — Lien desk: the Calendar tab — every billed job on its lien clock, grouped by GC (the shell) (2026-09-28)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. PR B of punch list #56 (`to-dos/lien-calendar/README.md`; mock-up `mockup.html` beside it, board 7 of the design canvas). PR A (v2.4103) put promises in the job's activity; this PR lands the Calendar as the Lien desk's first kind and widens what the desk shows to every billed job. PR C draws the shared axis; PR D puts the pen on the pay dot.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/jobs/lienCalendar.ts`** (new, 5 tests) — `buildLienCalendar(rows, query)`: the rows (each a job with the `LienPayRunway` its Pipeline row carries) filtered by a search over number · name · customer · GC · address (every token must land), sorted tightest first (`runway.sortKey`, then the bigger balance), and grouped: one group per GC for sub jobs, *Direct — we contracted with the owner* for the rest, *Lien gone* for every closed window, always last. Each group carries its total, a grey line (*GC · 6 jobs · 3 notices owed*) and one next move from its tightest job — *send 3 notices · 17 d* when notices are owed, else the tightest verdict (*49 d to the flag*, *44 d of room*, *file first on 2*), *money still owed* on Lien gone. Totals: jobs, open dollars, notices owed, liens gone.
- **`src/components/jobs/LienDeskCalendarTab.tsx`** (new, render smoke) — the header line (*every billed job on the statute's calendar · 64 jobs · $247k open · 45 notices owed · 12 liens gone*), the search box, collapsible group headers (Lien gone starts closed), and one row per job: number · name · customer · address (+ *kind?* when the property kind is unset), the `LienPayRunway` in a 15rem cell, the open balance. A row, and the runway's sentence, open the job's Lien window.
- **`LienDeskModal.tsx`** — a fifth kind, `calendar`, first in the tab row; `calendarRows` / `onOpenCalendarJob` props; the body renders the tab full-width like the Timeline. Nothing about the notice, affidavit, retainage or timeline kinds moved.
- **`JobsStagesTab.tsx`** — `lienCalendarRows`: every billed job through the same `lienRunwayFor(job, its one billed invoice)` the row uses (so the calendar and the board never disagree), with the GC from the job's embed. The desk's default kind becomes `calendar` — the Pipeline's ⋯ menu and the Collections header's Lien desk door land on it; a door that names a job (the forecast's *Send notice…*, the row icon) still lands on its notice, and the doors that pass a `kind` or `pile` (the *lien notices due* card, the Dashboard's cards) land where they say.

**Why the rows come from the Pipeline and not from `useLienDeskData`.** The desk's data hook is built around GC jobs and the notice piles; widening its query would touch every pile. The Pipeline already holds every billed job, its clocks (`useBilledLienClocks`) and the runway kernel — handing the rows in costs one `useMemo` and keeps one source of truth for the dates.

## Verify

Jobs → Pipeline → ⋯ menu → **Lien desk** (or the Collections header's door): it opens on **Calendar**. Groups by GC with a next move and a total; Direct after the GCs (Mike Holub-type homeowner jobs appear here — they never did on the desk); Lien gone last, closed. Type a GC's name in the search — only its group stays. Click a row — the job's Lien window opens. The other four tabs are as they were; the Dashboard's filing-window card still lands on Affidavits.

Live census on the local build, 2026-09-28: 64 rows — RMC · Dudley Mason the largest GC group; 45 *send the notice*; 12 under Lien gone.
