# v2.5114 — GC mode: the schedule opens to the office team and the job's project manager, and its print files a copy (2026-10-09)

PR 10 of the schedule's real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/schedule-pr10.md` on branch `spike/gc-mode`, the lead's calls of 2026-10-09). The schedule was a dev's since PR 7b; it opens to the people who run a job, the superintendent waiting for Building's door.

## The change

- Migration `20261010040000_gc_schedule_team_door` (`docs/migrations/`, with the bed's matrix and the five mutants): one helper, `gc_on_schedule_team(project)`, holds the audience: the GC office (`gc_office_team()`) and the job's project manager (`gc_projects.project_manager_user_id`). `gc_on_any_schedule_team()` does the same for the templates, which belong to no job. Two lookups, `gc_schedule_activity_project` and `gc_schedule_move_project`, give the seven child tables their job. The 23 tables swap `<table>_dev` for `<table>_team`; a what-if copy stays its own person's. Every schedule function is `SECURITY INVOKER`, so nothing else changes. Building's door adds the superintendent in the two helpers and nowhere else.
- [`GcProjects.tsx`](../../src/pages/GcProjects.tsx): three gates, not two, read [`canUseGcSchedule`](../../src/lib/gc/access.ts) (`GC_SCHEDULE_TEAM`, the office's five roles): the **Schedule** button on each card, the window, and who may move a bar in it. The team reads and writes alike, since the schedule carries no money.
- [`GcGanttPrint.tsx`](../../src/components/gc/GcGanttPrint.tsx): **Print or PDF** files through `printAndFile` (kind `gc_schedule_print`, the print's title, *The job's team* or the customer's name, the customer, source `gc_schedules`). A blocked pop-up files nothing and says so. `GcSchedule.tsx` passes the job and its customer through `GcGantt`'s new `printFiling`. `sentCopiesCoverage.test.ts`'s `PRINTS_OWED` is empty.
- [`doors.ts`](../../src/lib/gc/doors.ts): a fourth door, `schedule`, for the 23 tables, and `GC_SCHEDULE_APPEND_ONLY`, the ten append-only tables with the only columns each may change. `doors.test.ts` reads `gc_on_schedule_team(` as the schedule's door, and now reads every `GRANT` and `REVOKE` in the migrations: it fails when an append-only table gains a table-wide write or a column grant the list does not name.
- Two other readers see real rows for the first time: the money lens and the bill window (Owner Billing's O6b-3) read each job's schedule for its late finish, so a leader or the controller sees the real late finish; and `gc_draft_time_extension` stops refusing them. `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`'s Owner Billing paragraph says so (gc 5's ask); the fee itself stays behind the money team's terms guard.
