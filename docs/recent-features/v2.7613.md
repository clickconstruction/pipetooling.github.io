## What shipped

- **`src/hooks/useEstimateSentActions.ts`** — what the office can do to an estimate once it has left: Mark declined (`record_estimate_decline`), Extend with the letter again (`extend_estimate_good_through`, then `postSendEstimate` in `remail`), Resend link (`postSendEstimate` in `resend`), and Unlink job (the UPDATE gated on `customer_accepted`). It owns their six busy and window states and the three verdicts. The handlers are the page's own text. Its whole input is the row, the user and role, `load`, the events reload, `setLastAcceptUrl`, the What went bump and `showToast`.
- **`src/components/estimates/EstimateSentView.tsx`** — the `!isDraft` block of the page: What went, the locked notify list, the paper or its total, the customer line, the acceptance record and the Job section on an accepted row, the link's presses on a sent row, the note on a declined row, and the Unlink job window, which moved in beside it. The JSX is the page's own. It takes the row, the read-only values and the hook's bundle as `actions`.
- **`src/components/estimates/EstimateCustomerActivitySection.tsx`** — the Customer activity list and its `<details>`, which only the sent view mounts.
- **`src/lib/estimates/estimateDetailRow.ts`** — the `EstimateDetailRow` type, now shared by the page, the hook and the view.
- **`Estimates.tsx`** — calls the hook and mounts the view. 4,571 lines (was 5,257): 686 out.
- **The smoke** (`Estimates.render.test.tsx`) — 12 cases, two new: Mark declined records the no with its note and channel, and the two empty lines read as written. The ten older cases pass with no edit, which is the proof of the move.
