Migration `20261006130000_bid_submittals_sent_outside_at.sql` (`docs/migrations/20261006130000_bid_submittals_sent_outside_at.md`, **push owed**); no edge function. Punch list #89, item 3: BP375's header read *Rev 1 · draft · Sep 29* while four of its parts were rejected. Step 5 explained (*Not shared from the app · answers typed in*), but the header, the pill and the revision chips are what a reader sees first, and *draft* means unsent.

## What changed

- `bid_submittals.sent_outside_at` — a third state a revision can be in, as a column rather than a new `status` value (every reader of `status` would need the case). Set once by `markSentOutside(day, { onlyIfUnset: true })` in `BidsSubmittalsTab.tsx` after `saveAnswer` or `approveAll` writes a call on a draft (the answer's own day, `enteredDecisionAt`), and set or changed by **Sent by email on…** on step 5 (`SubmittalRoomPanel`: a day box beside Share, shown on an unshared draft while the room has shared nothing; once set the door reads *Sent by email Sep 29 · change*).
- `describeRevisionChip` (`submittalRevision.ts`) reads *Rev 1 · sent by email · Sep 29* on a draft or a superseded revision with the stamp (a superseded one that holds answers keeps *answered Oct 2*). `sentByEmailLine` is step 5's summary and the room line, *Sent by email · Sep 29 · answers typed in*, until the room shares a revision.
- `submittalJourney`: `rev.sentOutside` lights Share as done on the newest draft, and an older revision reads *Rev 3 was sent outside the app. Its answers were typed in.*
- `src/types/database.ts` carries the column by hand in the shape the generator emits.
- Beside v2.4667 (merged the same day): that build puts a revision answered by email on the GC's page as the record, derived from its typed answers and its package (`_shared/submittalRecord.ts`). `sent_outside_at` is the office's own statement that it went, which can come before any answer; the room keeps reading the answers, so nothing in `get-submittal-room` changes.
- Guide *build a submittal package*: three sentences under *Share it*.
