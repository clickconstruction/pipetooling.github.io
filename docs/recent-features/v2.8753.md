# v2.8753 — The shared press pad reaches 44 on a press with a border (2026-11-27)

Punch list #355, retired. Finding 340 of the fixing thread's ledger. No walk reported it: the `small-fixes` thread measured it twice on scratch pages, on the Lien desk's band press (finding 322) and on a site's materials screen (finding 324). Client only. No migration, no edge function. Nothing owed after the merge.

**As measured.** On a phone, a press under 40 px in the job window, the crew's job window or a Lien desk pane gets an unseen pad meant to grow its tap to 44 px. On a press with a 1 px border the tap read 42, or 43.2 on the band press, which is 27.2 px tall.

**Read.** `padSmallPresses` (`src/components/jobs/jobWindowPressPad.ts`) sized the pad from `getBoundingClientRect()`, the press's border box. Each stylesheet draws the pad as a placed `::after`, and a placed child is measured from inside the border. So each border's width was lost on each side.

**The mend.** A kernel in `src/lib/jobs/jobWindowPhone.ts`, `pressPadFromInside(pad, border)`, adds the border back on each side that grows. `padSmallPresses` reads the press's border widths and hands it the wider of the two on each axis, so a tap is never under the 44 it was sized for. A side that does not grow takes nothing. No stylesheet changes.

**Every caller, read.** `padSmallPresses` is called by `watchSmallPresses` alone, and `watchSmallPresses` has three callers on `main`:

- `JobWindowModal.tsx`, the tabbed job window's body.
- `DetailJobModal.tsx`, the crew's job window's body.
- `useLienPaneHeads.tsx`, which three Lien desk panes use: the notice pane, the retainage pane and the affidavit pane.

Each draws the pad with the same two offsets, `calc(-1 * var(--press-pad-y))` and its `x` twin, so all three are lifted by the one function.

**A fourth caller is on its way.** v2.8746 (finding 324) puts the pad on a site's materials screen, with a rule of its own that reaches 1 px further on each side to make up this shortfall. Once both are in, that screen's tap is 46, which is 2 px over and harms nothing. Its extra pixel is then no longer needed and can come out of that one rule.

**Measured** on a scratch page at 402 px: the real `LienTimelineStrip` in a phone's pane, made-up rows, the press saying **Sign and approve ›**, never pressed. No host was asked beyond the page.

| The band press, 27.2 px tall | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| The pad's custom property | 9 px | 10 px |
| A tap lands this far over and under the pill | 8 px | 9 px |
| The tap's height | 43.2 px | 45.2 px |
| A tap 22 px over the centre, and 22 px under | lands, misses | lands, lands |

The pad is unseen, so the two pictures are the same file, byte for byte.

**Tests.** `jobWindowPhone.test.ts`: a press 28 px tall with a 1 px border reaches 44 from inside its border, where it reached 42; the band press reaches 44 or more; no border is as before; only the sides that grow take it; a border a browser cannot say is none. `jobWindowPressPad.test.ts`: a bordered press gets 9 px, borders that differ take the wider, and no border gets 8. The older cases now give their presses no border, since a test page's own button comes with 2 px.

**To see it live.** On a phone, open a paper on the Lien desk and read the band's press: its `--press-pad-y` is one px more than half the way to 44. Read it, do not press it: that press signs a paper.
