- **When to press it** — the moment the caller is an owner talking about a letter from us: "a lien", "a Notice of Claim", "retainage", "am I being sued?", "I already paid my builder", "can I just pay you?". Not for the GC (the door says so beside a GC's name), not for an ordinary bill.

- **Find the letter they are holding** — name, street or job number; what a match shows; what to do when nothing matches (ask for the street or the job number; a notice sent by hand that was never recorded — take the facts, tell the master, record the notice first).
- **Read the sheet** — the top line is their letter; say the green line; tap what they say; the small boxes some replies open; the chips jump, Back undoes; the sentence is the record.
- **What you may and may not say** — the attorney's lines; the Never list; no payment on the phone (counsel's sign-off first); no promise but the release the day funds clear; "let me have Robert call you" for anything the cards don't cover.
- **After the call** — the note, Save, what Save does (the pile, letter two off, *wants to pay us → counsel*), tell the master the same day.
