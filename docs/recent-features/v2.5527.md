# v2.5527 — Job Mode: see the customer's texts in Job updates (2026-10-14)

A finding from the prod field walk after the residential build, by Customer communication helper 10, at cc's ask. Since v2.5471 a tech may read a job's lines with its customer in Job activity, but Job activity lives on Jobs → Pipeline, which a primary and a helper cannot open. On the walk neither the Sample primary nor the Sample helper could see the ZZ customer's lines anywhere. This gives them a door where they already work.

- **Gate** (`src/lib/gc/access.ts`, `readsCustomerLinesInJobUpdates`): the field roles among `canReadJobMessages`, primary and helpers. The policies keep the read to a job they were dispatched to or clocked on this week or last, tied to it, never an office-only note. The office's Job detail stays as it was; the office reads the same lines on Pipeline's Job activity and in Messages.
- **Kernel** (`src/lib/jobs/jobUpdatesCustomerLines.ts`):
  - `customerLinesForJobUpdates`: the customer's side only (`isCustomerSide`), oldest first, never an office-only row. A team note in her thread, an event and anyone else's line stay out.
  - `mergeJobUpdates`: each line goes before the first newer item, and the job's own activity keeps its order. With no lines the panel's rows are what they were.
  - `filterJobUpdates` and `countJobUpdates`: the Customer pill shows and counts only her lines, and the other pills never show one.
- **Panel** (`JobThreadNotesPanel.tsx`): a `customerLines` prop. Given, the pills are `JOB_ACTIVITY_FILTERS` (Customer last) and the rows are merged. Left out, the panel is as it was, so Quickfill and the estimate list do not change.
- **Row** (`components/jobs/JobUpdatesCustomerLine.tsx`): the panel's row look in Job activity's orange, a deeper bar when the line came from her. It shows how it came (text, email, call with its length), who, when, the subject and the words. It has no link to the thread and nothing to reply with. The tech answers through **Message the customer**.
- **Wired** in `JobModeDetailsSection.tsx` (Job Mode's Job updates; its job read adds `status, created_at`) and `DetailJobModal.tsx` (the Job detail window), each through `useJobMessages`.
- **Guides**: `open-job-activity-full-screen`'s tech sentence now names where a tech sees the lines, and `message-a-customer-from-job-mode` gains **See what they said**.
- **Docs**: the tech's lines in `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` (Messages, cut 1), `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` (The field and the push) and `GLOSSARY.md` (Job activity events), amended in place. `jobActivityFilter.ts`'s comment on who keeps which set is amended too.
- **Tests**:
  - `jobUpdatesCustomerLines.test.ts`: the customer's side only, the merge order, the pills, and no change with no lines.
  - `access.test.ts`: the gate opens for primary and helpers only.
  - `JobModeDetailsSection.customerLines.render.test.tsx`: a primary sees her two lines in orange beside the note, with no link, and the Customer pill shows only them. A subcontractor reads none and gets no pill.
- Not here: a text a tech sends from **Message the customer** shows in Job updates on the next open, since `messages` is not in the realtime publication.
