# v2.8435 — Document builder: a long list of held GCs reads as one line with a count (2026-11-24)

A small fault the #416 walk found and left (`to-dos/document-builder-whats-left.md`, *Faults seen and not fixed*): under the one press of **Send it to a GC**, the held list was one line for each GC with no page, which is most of them. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## The cause

`BuilderSendList` drew `sendBatch`'s `held` a line a party. A job's trades are a handful. The GCs are the company's whole book, and a GC has a page only once someone made one, so nearly every GC is held for the same reason.

Reproduced on made-up rows before the change: one GC whose page is on and forty with none drew *Send to 1 GC who is ready* over forty lines of *Held: … has no page yet. Send to them alone to make one.*

## What changed

- `sendBatch` (`src/lib/documentBuilder/send.ts`) says each held party's reason as a key beside its words: `alone`, `no-page`, `page-off`, `nobody`. `heldReason` is the one place that picks it, in the order the words were picked before.
- `heldLines` turns the held list into lines. A reason that holds three or fewer (`HELD_NAMED_AT_MOST`) names each on its own line, in the words it had. A reason that holds more reads as one line with its count from `pluralNoun`: *Held: 40 GCs have no page yet. Send to one alone to make its page.*
- The list draws a counted line as a native fold, and a press on it opens the names, one a line. Nothing is dropped from what the press held: the count, the reason, the door and the names are all still there.
- The one press, its count, its question and the line after it (*Sent to 2 GCs. Held 43.*) are unchanged. A job's sheet uses the same list, so a job with more than three trades held for one reason reads the same way.
- Help: *use the document builder* says the held lines are there and that a counted one opens.

## Tests

- `send.test.ts`: a few held parties keep a line each; forty with no page read as one line with forty names under it while three whose page is off keep their own lines; the fourth is where a reason turns into a count; each reason's counted words, held to the plain-words rules.
- `BuilderSendList.render.test.tsx`: fifteen GCs with no page draw one fold, closed, with fifteen names inside, and no line for each.
- Not seen: the fold in a browser. The render test is jsdom's, which holds the words and the structure and draws no pixels. The live list was not opened.
