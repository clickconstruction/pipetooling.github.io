## The law the words follow

- Gov't Code § 27.031: a justice court hears a claim to $20,000; fees count toward it, interest and costs do not. The app already applied the cap in the demand letter (`JUSTICE_COURT_LIMIT`).
- TRCP 502.4: the defendant may be sued in the county and precinct where the contract was to be performed (the job site) or where the defendant resides. The portal shows both; the choice is counsel's.
- § 27.031(b): a justice court cannot foreclose a lien on land. The debt suit may go to the precinct court; a § 53.158 foreclosure goes to district court in the property's county.
