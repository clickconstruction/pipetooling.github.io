# v2.4704 — Lien desk: the pay offer reaches the bills (2026-10-07)

PR 2 of 2 for the pay offer (v2.4700 is the choice and the words). The money side, with one rule: the run puts a Stripe credit on each enclosed bill while the offer is live, the ledger's own amount changes only when a bill is paid in full, and a bill still open after the day goes back to full. Nothing writes Stripe from the public `pay-link` endpoint; one place puts the credit on (the run), one takes it back (the nightly sweep), the webhook records it.

Migration `20261007020000_lien_pay_offer_nightly_cron.sql` (`docs/migrations/…md`): the `lien-pay-offer-nightly` pg_cron job, 06:05 UTC. Edge functions: `lien-pay-offer` (new), `stripe-webhook`, `pay-link` (deploy all three).

## What changed

- **Shared kernel** `supabase/functions/_shared/lienPayOffer.ts` (re-exported as `src/lib/jobs/lienPayOfferShared.ts`, tested from `lienPayOfferShared.test.ts`): `lienOfferCreditCents`, `lienOfferState` (none · live · ended · taken; the day passing counts as ended even before the sweep), `lienOfferForPayLink` (the full amount beside the lower one only while live), `lienOfferWriteDown` (the ledger amount less the credit, with the note; never twice, never after the sweep), `lienOfferExpired`, `lienOfferCreditMemo`. The client kernel's `lienOfferCreditCents` now re-exports this one.
- **`lien-pay-offer`** (`verify_jwt = false`): `apply` (the office's JWT, dev · master · assistant · controller, not a training account) reads the offer off the desk item the filing closed (`sent_filing_id`), and for every enclosed Stripe bill still open creates a credit note (`order_change`, memo *10% off if paid in full by Nov 15 — lien notice offer*, metadata `pipetooling_lien_offer`, `filing_id`, `offer_by`) for the percent of what Stripe then asks for, writing `lien_offer_*` on the row; a bill already carrying a live credit is skipped, a row write that fails voids the credit again. `expire` (`X-Cron-Secret`) voids every live credit whose day passed and marks `lien_offer_ended_at`; a bill Stripe says is paid is marked taken with a warning line. `dry_run` reports and writes nothing.
- **The run** (`recordLienDeskRun`): after each filing is inserted and its item marked sent, the function is called for that filing (every part of a combined notice, since each part is its own item); the result's `offers` says what went on; a failure never un-records the notice.
- **`stripe-webhook`** (`invoice.paid` / `invoice.payment_succeeded`): before `mark_invoice_paid_from_stripe` — which records the ledger's remaining amount, not Stripe's — a bill with a live credit is written down through `service_apply_agreed_write_down_from_stripe` (actor = the leader who gave the offer, note *Lien notice offer: 10% off, paid in full Nov 3 (by Nov 15) (Stripe credit note cn_…)*) and marked `lien_offer_taken_at`; a refused write-down is logged and the bill is still marked paid.
- **`pay-link` + `/pay`**: the payload carries `offer { pct, by, state, fullCents }` (`parsePayLinkOffer` on the client); the page shows the full amount struck through and *10% off if paid in full by November 15. Today it is.* while live, *The 10% offer ended November 15. This is the full amount owed.* after, and *paid on November 3, 2026 with the 10% offer* once taken.
