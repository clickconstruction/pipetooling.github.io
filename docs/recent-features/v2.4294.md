# v2.4294 — Edit Job → Bill: read the money by bill or by date (2026-10-01)

PR 2 of 2 of Grace's Bill tab redesign (PR 1: v2.4293, PR #4233). Her call on the mock-up canvas (https://claude.ai/artifact/EkzGZBLY9tC6qAMve3s6h5, board *By bill / By date · the switch*): "give the user a toggle to view it as C or to view it as D." D is PR 1. This adds the switch and C.

## What changed

- **Kernel** [`billsAndPayments.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/billsAndPayments.ts) `orderMoneyByDate(invoices, slicesByBill)` (tested): a bill dated by the day it went out (`billSentYmd`), a payment by the day it came; oldest first, the bill before the money on the same day, undated rows (a draft, a payment with no date) last. A payment counted toward two bills is one row whose `billWords` names both (*pays the $1,000 bill and the $500 bill*). It reads the same slices as By bill, so the two views never disagree on where money went.
- **The switch** — `InvoicesSectionHeading` ([`JobFormSegmentsBar.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormSegmentsBar.tsx)) takes `view` + `onViewChange` and draws **By bill** / **By date** (`aria-pressed`, `data-testid="bills-view-switch"`) at the right of the ② heading; without a handler it draws nothing, so other callers are untouched. Type `BillsAndPaymentsView`.
- **By date** — [`JobFormInvoiceList.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormInvoiceList.tsx) takes `view`; with `'date'` (and the line doors) it walks `orderMoneyByDate` instead of the bills: a bill row is drawn as in By bill with its paid bar but without the lines under it; each payment is its own row (`.jobMoneyDateRow`, `data-testid="date-payment-row"`), a `JobFormPaymentLine` with `billWords`, so it reads *$11,700.00 · Sep 14 · check from Loberg · pays the $26,800 bill · 61 d* and keeps its whole ⋯ menu; a closing *Today · $15,722.49 still open* row (`data-testid="date-today-row"`) when anything is open. The ledger carries `data-view`. [`JobFormPaymentLine.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormPaymentLine.tsx) gains the optional `billWords`.
- **Remembered per browser** — `JobFormModal` keeps `billsView` and writes it under `BILLS_VIEW_STORAGE_KEY` (`pipetooling.jobBill.view`), every read and write in try/catch so a blocked storage still opens By bill and keeps a choice for the visit.
- `index.css`: the By date rail — a 2px left rule with a dot per row (hollow blue for a bill, green for bank or Stripe money, hollow amber for money typed by hand, dashed red for *Today*); tokens only.
- Guide *see which payments paid a bill* gains **By bill or by date**.
