# v2.6446 — Hours charged statement, PR 2: the window's charged choice, the paper's Charged band, and crew-days on one person's paper (punch list #251) (2026-11-03)

PR 1 (v2.6431) gave the kernel a charged basis priced at one rate. By the time this PR was cut the owner had set the rate per crew-hour by who was out (punch list #254, v2.6441), so the charged paper for one person lists the crew-days they were part of, each at the rate card or the job's rule, and the single-rate card folds into the rate card. The window's basis choice, the print's title and Charged band, the saved row's basis, and the section's words are here; the gate needed no change.

## What changed

- **The window** (`HoursStatementModal.tsx`): a switch **Charged to the customer, not paid to the worker** above the pay switch, open to every maker. On, the read runs with `basis: 'charged'`, the line under it says how the job charges its labor (*master with a helper throughout · $285.00 the crew’s hour*, or *the crew on each day, from the rate card*) with any day the card cannot price named once, and points at the Job Summary's Charges window to change it; the pay switch reads *Off while the paper is charged to the customer*. The rate field, presets and slider the card imagined are not built: under the crew model the rate card and the job's rule decide, and a per-paper rate would be a second basis for the same hour.
- **The read** (`hoursStatementRead.ts`): on the charged basis the job's approved sessions in the span, every person's standing, the rate card and the job's rule become crew-days (`buildCrewDays`); the person's counted days become those crew-days — the crew's hours, a note *with Joseph · master with a helper · the job’s rule* (or *alone · …*), the person's own in and out, the day's rate on the row — so two papers never sell the same hour twice; pending rows stay pending; `charge` facts come back for the window and the saved row.
- **The kernel** (`supabase/functions/_shared/hoursStatement.ts`): a row may carry its own `chargeRate`; a week with one rate reads as before (*40 h at $285.00 = $11,400.00*), a mixed week by its total with the unpriced days counted, the total line *as <the job's rule>* when the rows are rated one by one; `chargeWords` on the input feeds `preparedWords`.
- **The print** (`buildHoursStatementHtml.ts`): the title *Statement of hours charged*, the Rate and Amount columns once the days are priced, a **Charged** band in place of Paid (the week lines, the total in bold, the note that nothing on the paper is what the worker was paid, any line left out), and the prepared line from the kernel's words.
- **The saved row** (migration `20261103090000_hours_statement_basis.sql`, `hoursStatementsIo.ts`): `basis`, `charge_rate`, `charge_words`; a charged row carries `with_pay = false` by a check. The section lists it as *hours charged* (`hoursStatementRowWords`).
- **Settings**: the v2.6431 card *Rate charged for an hour* is gone; its key is the rate card's master-with-a-helper row, so a rate saved there stands.
- **Tests**: the kernel's row-by-row pricing (a mixed week, one rate), the print's title, band and columns, the list row's words.

## Decisions

- **The job decides the rate, never the paper.** The window shows the rule and points at the window that changes it.
- **A person's charged paper is the crew's.** The day's hours are the crew's, the note names who else was out, and the same day on another person's paper would price the same, so the office prints one.
- **No gate of its own.** A charged row is a row with the pay left off; it takes the hours statement's gate, which v2.6450 narrowed to a dev and the controller the same day.
