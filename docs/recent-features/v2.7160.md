# v2.7160 — Materials on site: a job reads every press, past the first 1,000 (2026-11-13)

Found by the review of Materials → On site (2026-11-13). No job is past the limit today, so nothing was wrong on a screen yet. The largest ledger is about 290 rows, and every count walk adds one row per line.

## What was wrong

`loadJob` in `src/lib/materials/onSiteStore.ts` read the ledger with `.order('at', desc).limit(MOVEMENTS_LIMIT)`, 5,000 rows, and no `.range()`. PostgREST cuts an un-ranged answer at `max_rows`, 1,000 on this project, with no error (`src/lib/supabasePaging.ts` records the incident class). So:

- A job past 1,000 ledger rows would have loaded its newest 1,000. A line delivered early and not counted since loses its `delivered` row and leaves the phone, the desk and the site's screen. Its box reads *never counted*. A count whose earlier rows fell off reads as a false *found*.
- `capped` is `rows.length >= 5000`, which a 1,000-row answer never meets. The summary function of v2.6743 (`on_site_job_summary`) would never have been asked for, and no screen would have said the read was short.
- The material location report reads through `siteMaterialsRead.ts`, paged from the start, so the paper would have disagreed with the screens.

The same shape sat on four more reads: a job's photos (oldest first, so the newest were the ones cut), a job's boxes and places, the desk's list of jobs with a live box (`.limit(5000)`, no order), and `fetchPartTies` (`.limit(2000)`). The box moves were one `.in()` over every box id of the job.

## The change

- `loadJob` pages the ledger with `fetchAllRows` and `maxRows: MOVEMENTS_LIMIT`, newest first as before, so the 5,000 cap is real and `capped` can be true. Places, boxes and photos are paged with a stable second order on `id`. The box moves go through `fetchAllRowsChunkedIn` over the box ids.
- `loadDesk` pages its `job_id` read, ordered by `id`.
- `fetchPartTies` pages its read.
- A page that fails throws a `DatabaseError`, which the job's load already reports through `formatErrorMessage` and the offline panel reads for its class.

No migration, no edge function.

## Tests

`src/lib/materials/onSiteStore.read.test.ts`, the store's first test: the client is stood in for by a table that cuts every answer at 1,000 rows, as the project does. A job of 1,300 rows reads all 1,300 and is not capped. A job of 5,200 keeps its newest 5,000, is capped and asks for the summary. A job with 1,100 boxes, 1,050 photos and 1,100 box moves reads each whole. A desk with 1,005 live boxes on one job still finds the other jobs. The first two fail on the code before this change.

## Not changed

A capped job's lists still draw from the newest 5,000 rows (the map's Scale gap). The store still reads a job once a page session. Both are on the review's list.
