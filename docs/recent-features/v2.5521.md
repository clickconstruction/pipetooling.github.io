# v2.5521 — Needs you: a card when Docsmit credit runs low (Docsmit train, 6b, the credit line) (2026-10-14)

The credit line of 6b (`docs/DOCSMIT_LIEN_RUN_PLAN.md`, *Delivery drives the desk*). A run's send stops at the first envelope with a 402 when the prepaid credit is short; this says so before the run.

**`mail-poll`** — after the read-back, `GET /account/info` once; the balance is written to `app_settings` `docsmit_credit_balance` (`value_text`) and `docsmit_credit_as_of` (the poll's instant), the rows made on the first write (`writeSetting`); the answer carries `credit_balance` and `credit_error` (a refusal is reported, never fatal). **`src/lib/mail/docsmitSettings.ts`** — `DOCSMIT_CREDIT_KEYS`, `DOCSMIT_READ_KEY_LIST` (the switches plus the two), `docsmitCreditFrom(rows)`, `docsmitCreditLine(credit, warn, nowIso)` → `{ state: none | ok | low | out, words, ago }` (*Docsmit credit $12.40, under your $25 line · read 2 h ago*). `useDocsmitSettings` returns `credit`.

**`src/lib/dashboardNeedsYou.ts`** — inputs `docsmitCreditEnabled` / `docsmitCredit` (balance, as-of, the warn line, now); item `docsmit-credit` (tier 10 beside the tracking-owed card): amber *Docsmit credit is low: $12.40* under the line, red *Docsmit credit is out* at or under zero, nothing above the line, before the first read, or before a dev set the service up. The Dashboard's pinned row and the Quickfill section feed it from `useDocsmitSettings` for the lien-eligible roles; the card opens the Lien desk on Ready to send.
