# v2.3761 — Lien desk: the job's timeline — every Chapter 53 date on one line, and what comes next (2026-09-23)

Punch list **#37**, PR 1 of 3 (the to-do and mock-up are in `to-dos/lien-timeline/`). No migration, no edge function, no stored rows.

## Why

Counsel's memo (2026-09-22, `to-dos/gc-failure-playbook/counsel-memo-2026-09-22.md`) says *"Timeline — run it separately on every job"* and *"move every job onto that grid today."* The desk dated two of the six Chapter 53 steps and showed them in four places — the months card, the affidavit pane's chip, the Lien window's header, the Dashboard's watches — and nothing said, for one job, *this is step 3 of 6, step 2 was missed, the lien dies Dec 15 unless…*. Once an affidavit is filed the job leaves the desk in 30 days and the year-long suit clock runs unwatched.

## What shipped

- **`lienTimeline.ts`** — a pure kernel: from the job's months (approved hours, or the creation month — v2.3747), their outcomes (sent / skipped / missed, from `lienMonthHistory`), the desk item's pile, the affidavit entry and filings, the release of record and the open balance, it builds the steps in order — `last_work`, one `notice` per month (§ 53.056), `retainage` (§ 53.057), `affidavit` (§ 53.052), `serve` (§ 53.055), `hold` (§ 53.101, after filing), `suit` (§ 53.158, one year from the last day the affidavit could file, weekend-rolled), `release` — each with a state (`done` / `due` / `missed` / `blocked` / `undated` / `later`), the words under it, and one **next** line with a kind and a tone. `lienGone` is a sub job whose every window closed with nothing sent: the affidavit and everything after it read *blocked*. `kindUnknown` carries the gate-3 caveat. `suitDeadlineFor`, `lienThirtyDayClock` and `lienDateWords` are exported for the tab and the Lien window.
- **`lienTimelineDesk.ts`** — folds the desk's data into the kernel's input. `lienRetainageClockFromDesk` reads the § 53.057 clock *structurally* — a retainage queue entry or the job's `lien_contract_ended_on` — so the step lights up the day v2.3753 (#33 PR 1) lands, with no change here; until then it is drawn undated with its door (*set the date ›* → Edit Job).
- **`LienTimelineStrip`** — the strip: nodes on a rail with the step names, the date and the words under each, the today marker at the boundary between past and pending steps, and the *Next on the path* line. `layout="row"` on the desk, `"list"` on a phone (seven columns would be seven slivers), `"mini"` for a list of jobs (PR 2).
- **The desk pane** (Notices and Affidavits): the title's one-date chip (*Jul notice by Oct 15* / *affidavit · file by …*) is replaced by the strip in a box under the title; the sticky strip (v2.3522) carries the next line as a chip. Nothing under it moved.
- **`useLienDeskData`** reads `last_work_date` with the jobs and, in full mode, the jobs' affidavit and release-of-record filings (`filingsByJob`) — the tail of the timeline.

## Rules the strip keeps

- A missed month is never re-dated (v2.3681); a miss is *not noted* until a person writes it down (v2.3679); a stale month never reaches the form (v2.3745); a kind is never guessed (v2.3670). The strip reads those states and changes none of them.
- Dates come from the same functions the desk and the RPCs use (`filingDeadlineForMonth`, `serveDueForFiling`, the RPC's per-month deadline), so the strip, the months card and the GC run cannot disagree.
- *Counsel by* on the suit step is 90 days before the deadline (`LIEN_SUIT_COUNSEL_LEAD_DAYS`) — a default, an owner call.
