# v2.7716 — Copy a letter: a paper tagged INTERNAL in its title is held back, and a copy that still says DRAFT warns (2026-11-17)

## Why

The copy press of v2.7683 was walked on prod, read-only, and its rules were then run over the real text papers (the HR files' documents and the older text files on matters), with counts and masked lines as the only output. Every paper built with a draft banner and a section headed *INTERNAL* was read right. One shape was missed: a paper whose **title ends with a tag in capitals**, `(INTERNAL, <date>)`. The rule read only a heading's first word, so every section of that paper read as sendable and no line said anything was left out. A second, softer shape: a paper that says `INTERNAL` and `DRAFT` in a status line under its title, with no banner, so the words went out with the first piece and nothing warned.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/legal/letterCopyText.ts`**
  - **Internal sections**: a heading is internal when its words open with *internal* (any case, as before) **or carry `INTERNAL` in capitals anywhere**. The section drops with everything under it up to the next heading of its size or larger, so a title so tagged leaves no piece. The word in small letters inside a heading stays an ordinary word (*Our internal records* is a piece).
  - **`stillSays`** on each piece: `DRAFT` and `INTERNAL` where they stand in the piece's text as whole words in capitals (`letterMarks`). Nothing is dropped for it. The rules above decide what leaves; this says what stayed.
  - `letterCopiedWords` adds *The copy still says DRAFT.* (or *DRAFT and INTERNAL*) after the blank count and sets `warn`, so the toast is a warning. `letterStillSaysWords` is the read view's line, naming the piece among several.
- **`LegalMatterDocumentsPanel.tsx`**: an amber line per piece that still says a mark (`data-legal-document-copy-still-says`), under the blanks lines. A paper with no piece keeps its line *Every word here is a draft note or an internal section, so there is no letter to copy.* and **Copy everything as text**.
- **`PersonFilePanel.tsx`**: no change in the file. Its *Copy the letter* presses read the same kernel, so a tagged title now counts as words left out and the toast warns the same way.

## Not in this change

A privileged banner written as a quote block (`> **PRIVILEGED …**`) is not a draft banner and is not dropped. A paper that opens with one is for counsel, and marking its row counsel-only is what takes the copy press away.

## Checked

- `letterCopyText.test.ts`: a title tagged at its end leaves no piece, a section tagged mid-heading drops, the small-letter word stays, `stillSays` per piece, the read view's line and the toast, the marks as whole words in capitals only.
- `LegalMatterDocumentsPanel.copy.render.test.tsx`: a tagged title shows no piece press and the no-letter line, a copy that still says DRAFT shows the amber line and a warning toast.
- Not walked in the running app after the change. The shapes came from a read-only walk of prod; the fixtures are made up.
