# v2.7788 — Painted maps: the rules for a Share sheet with fewer words (2026-11-18)

Punch list #394, step 1 of 2: the kernel. No screen changes. The owner asked on 2026-11-18, with a phone screenshot of a painted map's **Share this map** sheet before a link exists: *how could I offer this page with less words and in a more intuitive way where the user doesn't have to put in the effort to read so much?* The design is on the card with its mock-up, and the owner's word the same day was *Build it*, every call at the card's pick.

## What is new

- `src/lib/paintedMap/paintedMapShare.ts`, pure. `paintedMapShareWays` says what the sheet draws for whoever is looking: the one blue press, whether the person's own text is drawn, the office's text to the customer, the tiles in order, and whether the fold for paper and files is there.
- **On a phone** the person's own text leads for everyone, the office too, so the first press never moves. **At a desk** there is no Messages to open: the office's text leads on a job, and **Copy the link** leads for everyone else and is not drawn twice.
- **Send it another way…** is a tile only where the browser has a share sheet. **Make a picture** and the fold are there only when the sheet was handed the map, so a Documents row still shares the link alone.
- `PAINTED_MAP_SHARE_SHEET_WORDS` is the one home for the words the new sheet stands with, and for what it says when a phone will not open Messages after the wait for the link.
- `paintedMapLinksOutWords` and `paintedMapEndWords` are the link's line and its press, lifted from the sheet as they read today.
- `paintedMapOwnTextHref` is the address that opens the phone's own Messages with the words written and nobody picked. The words are the ones the office's text already carries.

## A count that holds the words down

`paintedMapShareStandingWords` lists what the sheet stands with before anyone presses, and the test counts them: 43 for a tech on a phone, 50 for the office on a job, 52 for the office on a map with no job, 34 at a desk. No state may pass 55. Today's sheet stands at 163.

## Not changed

- The sheet itself, its words and the guide. Step 2 puts the sheet over these rules.
- The office's press still reads **Text it…** until then.

## Checked

- `paintedMapShare.test.ts`, 16 cases: every state the card's mock-up draws, exactly one leading press across all 32 combinations, each sentence against the plain-words rules, the counts above, the link's line, and the Messages address.
