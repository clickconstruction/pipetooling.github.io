## track a general contractor on a job

- **Set a GC** is the Edit tab's **GC/Builder** row with the hard hat. **Clear GC** is in that row. The Customer section and *GC/Builder (customer)* are on New Job only, and that GC saves with **Create Job** (`JobFormEditFactRows.tsx`, `JobFormCustomerSection.tsx`).
- A job made from a bid also gets **Bills go to** = GC (v2.3403). A multi-GC bid asks *Which GC gave you this job?* The bid button prints its GC's name (`useJobFormImport.ts`, `PickWinningGcModal.tsx`).
- **Who gets the bills** replaces *What the GC does not change*, which predates [v2.3345](v2.3345.md). It covers the job's **Bills go to** row (*This customer*, the GC by name, *Split by line*), the move when the customer is picked as the GC, and **Bill to ▾** on a draft row of the Bill tab. GC Review files a job under its GC only when the GC pays (`billToParty.ts`, `JobFormInvoiceList.tsx`). The intro no longer says the GC leaves billing alone.
- The ⋯ menu's **Filters** sit under **Sort**. **Total outstanding** matches the section header only with Include Collections unticked and no search or filter. The Not billed to a GC bucket sits under *Nothing to check this week*.
- The certify section links to the round guide for the stage track and the word, and says certifying is the office's own sign-off. That settles the overlap the card named.
- The statement phone is set by a dev in **Settings → Jobs & billing** (`settingsGroups.ts`). The changed chip prints cents.
- The Dashboard reminder is an amber item in **Needs you**: *GC review is due today*, or *still due this week* from Thursday, with **Open GC Review** (`dashboardNeedsYou.ts`). The tab is **This week**.
- The Share menu gains **Find a check…** ([v2.4046](v2.4046.md)) and **Mark sent / spoke with them…**.
- A send this week shows a blue **Sent Oct 7** pill, and an earlier week reads *last sent*. This settles the kept word "statemented".
- **Reply to** is the fourth of five lines, not the third. It shows when more than one office person can take replies. The account man is picked when he has an office role and an email (`StatementRecipientHeader.tsx`, `_shared/gcStatementReplyTo.ts`).
- The statement template is a dev's, under **Email templates & testing**. A scheduled send uses the standard subject.
- The Scheduled list shows every office send, and **Cancel** shows only to its scheduler or a dev. The development toggle is the **By GC** and **By Development** pills. **Share all** is the blue button. The email section is **Email once**, with **Send report** or **Schedule send**.
- The guide's roles gain controller, who works GC Review like an assistant (`stagesRoleGates.ts`).
- A development is *a named group of jobs, like a subdivision*. It was *a group of properties built as one project*, and Projects mean something else in the app.

## run your weekly GC statement round

- GC Review opens on its **This week** tab ([v2.3997](v2.3997.md)). The email goes when a person presses **Send statement**, and the row's **Send** opens the window headed *Email statement to* and the GC's name.
- "A statement never goes out unchecked" and "Nothing is ever emailed uncertified" are removed. Only the row's Send waits for the check (`GcWorklistPanel.tsx`, `gcStatementSendGuard.ts`). See *Found for the owner*.
- The account-man order reads as the code has it: the standing pick, else the account manager on most jobs, else the leader (`gcWorklist.ts`). The name chip is the newest word in six weeks.
- The opened row is a table of bills. The paid-by line is in **Check bills**, the call sheet, the statement and the portal. Share holds all seven items. **or mark sent** shows once the bills are checked, until a statement is out.
- **Find a check**: the number goes on the payment in the job window's **Bill** tab. The sheet is *where your checks were applied*, and its Retainage held column shows only when a job has any (`gcChecksAppliedReport.ts`).
- **The word**: *Mine — I talked to Knight Contracting*. The channel chips run Email, Text, Call, In person, Other, with Text picked (`gcStatementRounds.ts`). A date of today or later becomes a promise. The link asks only about GCs owing $10,000 or more. A link answer shows in What went out as *Personal · other*. The Knight examples now agree with each other.
- **Where the week finds you**: the Needs you row is the office's week, *N GC statements are ready to send*, with **Open the list** (`dashboardNeedsYou.ts`). The morning email is rewritten from `renderOfficeWeek.ts`, the office version since [v2.3976](v2.3976.md). It covers:
  - one email for everyone
  - the office standard
  - the groups *Your accounts*, *Call Malachi* and *No account man yet*
  - one next step per GC
  - the AP contact on Send only
  - one *Open Malachi's call sheet →* per group
  - the Friday line
  - **Edit** and **Stop emailing** in the Scheduled box, with any office person able to set it up for someone else
- **Spoke with them**: the Word dot opens on *Spoke with them · no statement*, and Share's **Mark sent / spoke with them…** opens on **Statement sent**. The guardrail is the Temperature board's red line *⚠ spoken to 2 weeks running · …*, not a row chip (`GcTemperatureBoard.tsx`).
- The temperature sits in What went out's details, not a Temp column. The chase list is **Payment follow-up**, opened by *Ask when they'll pay*. *no read* means no temperature in six weeks.
- Under-$10,000 GCs are in the week's list with the word optional (v2.4149). A scheduled send is once or weekly, from **Schedule…**. The skip reason is not on screen. The portal card is in **Share → Copy** and the email's **Preview**. The group header carries its dollars, and the Scheduled tab shows while a send waits or a word is due. On Wednesday, the Dashboard item counts every GC with a balance (`gc_review_week_status`).

## Found for the owner and the app

- **Unchecked statements can be sent.** Share → Draft Message, Copy, Print and scheduled sends all work on an unchecked GC. Neither send function reads `gc_review_certifications`. Yet the app's own words say *nothing goes out unchecked* (`GcWorklistPanel.tsx:397`, `PipelineMoneyOpportunities.tsx:330`). Whether to enforce the check or reword is the owner's call. The guides now state only what the code does.
- **The Statements to send card's "N broke a promise" never shows.** `gcWorklist` sets `promise` only when `todayYmd` is passed, and neither caller passes it (`JobsStagesTab.tsx:1630-1639`, `usePipelineMoneyOpportunities.ts:144-153`). The guide's card example drops the tail.
- **Stale app words**: *Find a check*'s tooltip still says Edit Job → Payments received (`GcFindCheckModal.tsx:181`). A word heard by link is stored but no screen shows it. A skipped scheduled send's reason is written to its row, but no screen reads it.
