# v2.7174 — Subs: a Ledger of every payment, by date (2026-11-13)

Punch list #347, PR 2. The owner: "I want to have the ability to see things as a ledger so I can quickly reconcile payments we have made to people." PR 1 (v2.7172) put the dates on the Pay rows. This is the screen itself, on **Jobs → Subs**. People → Subs and one sub's statement are PR 3.

## What it is

A third view beside Work and Pay, at `/jobs?tab=subs&view=ledger`. One line per payment or back-charge on any sub sheet, newest first, under its month with the month's count and total.

- **Columns:** Date sent, Paid to, Job, Memo, Amount, Left on the sheet.
- **Periods:** This month (where it opens), Last month, This quarter, This year, All, and two date boxes for any range.
- **Paid to** narrows to one person. **Search** matches a sub, a job, an address, a memo or an amount (`2,400` and `2400.00` both find it), every word wanted.
- **Tiles:** paid in the period with how many payments to how many people, back-charged, and how many payments have no Date sent.
- **Print** and **Download CSV** take the shown lines out in the screen's order. The CSV's amounts are plain numbers, a back-charge below zero, and a memo that opens like a formula is led with an apostrophe.
- **On a phone** each line is a card that opens its sheet, and Print and the file sit under the list.

## How a line is read

- `src/lib/subWorkOrders/subPaymentsLedger.ts`, new, pure. `buildLedgerLines(sheets)` turns each sheet's payments into lines. A line's day is `paymentDay`, in the same file (the Pay row's `sheetPaymentDates.ts` from PR 1 reads a payment the same way, and PR 3 folds the two into this one, since the two PRs merge in either order): the Date sent, else the day the row was recorded in the app's timezone, flagged. A back-charge is a row below zero and has no Date sent, so its day is always the recorded one.
- **Left on the sheet** is the sheet's agreed total (`subLaborJobBalance(sheet).totalCost`) less every payment and back-charge up to that line, in day order. Zero reads *paid in full*, below zero *over by $…*. Sums are rounded to the cent at each step.
- **Paid to** is `sheetParties`, the Pay view's own rule: the sheet is its sub's and teammates ride along. A sheet with no sub is crew pay and its lines carry a *crew* chip.
- `buildLedgerTraces` says each move and removal once (the Pay view says a move on both sheets), with the two jobs, who did it and why. A removal that was restored draws nothing.
- `filterLedger`, `ledgerPeriodRange`, `ledgerMonths`, `ledgerTotals`, `ledgerPaidToOptions`, `subPaymentsCsv`, `subPaymentsLedgerHtml` are the rest.

## Wiring

- `SubPaymentsLedger.tsx`, new. It reads the page's `laborJobs` (every sheet already carries `payments` and `payment_events` from `useSubLaborLedger`), reads the roster through `useRosterSubKinds` as the Pay view does, and writes nothing. No migration, no new query on payments.
- `JobsSubsTab`: `SubsView` gains `ledger`, the control draws a third tab when the page hands a `ledger` slot in, and the Ledger shows no toolbar on the control's row since it carries its own search.
- `Jobs.tsx` hands the slot in for the roles who see Work. A superintendent sees Pay only, has no policy on the payments table, and gets no Ledger: `subsViewFromParam('ledger', false)` is `pay`.
- An empty list names its way out through `EmptyState`: *Show all dates*, *Clear the search*, *Show everyone*, or **Pay** when nothing was ever paid.

## Walked

The worktree's dev build against prod, read only (headless Chromium; the route guard refused the nav-click log and nothing else was attempted):

- **All:** every payment and back-charge on record came up as a line under its month. The CSV held one row per line, and its payments summed to the tile's figure to the cent. The print window opened with every line and every month head. The figures themselves are left out of this note: the repo is public.
- **Most of the payments read *no date sent*.** They were saved before the Date sent field (2026-08-14) and show the day they were recorded. The card's call 1 is about these.
- **Search** by a memo word narrowed the list, and **Paid to** listed each person paid and narrowed to one, with the tile following.
- A line's job opened the sheet's window. It was closed with nothing saved.
- **Phone, 390 px:** a card per line, no table, no sideways scroll. **Dark** at a desk reads the same.
- No page error on any of the three.
