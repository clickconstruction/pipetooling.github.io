# v2.8603 — Dashboard: swipe a hidden bid or an opened report right to bring it back (2026-11-25)

Punch list #426, the swipe back. The person who runs the session tried the swipe on their own phone on 2026-11-25 and asked: *when looking at a hidden bid, I would like a swipe to put back from the other direction.* Their pick from the mock-up (`to-dos/my-bids-refresh/swipe-sign-and-back.html`) was *A and no press*: a put-away row has no press on a phone. Client only. No migration, no edge function.

## What changed

- **`SwipeAwayRow` takes a side.** `side="right"` is the way back: the strip is on the row's left, reads the press the row had, and turns blue past the same 112 px mark. `useSwipeRow` mirrors the drag for it, so the left swipe's kernel (`swipeOffset`, `swipeSettles`) is read turned round and not written twice. People's phone rows pass nothing and behave as they did.
- **My Bids, Hidden.** On a device held in the hand a hidden bid has no **Show again** press. A press opens the bid and a swipe to the right shows it again. The name has the whole box. At a desk **Show again** stays a word press.
- **Recent Reports, Opened.** The same row with **Mark unread**.
- **A toast says what came back**, with **Undo** for ten seconds: *Shown again:* and the bid's name, *Marked unread:* and the job's. The desk's presses say it too. Before this the way back said nothing. **Undo** puts the row away again and says nothing more.
- **One toast for one row.** `showActionToast` takes an optional `key`. A newer toast with the same key takes the older one's place, so a bid hidden and shown again inside the ten seconds shows one line and one **Undo**. Toasts with no key stack as they did.
- **The line under the group's heading says the move** on a phone: *You hid these. Swipe one right to show it again.* and *You marked these opened. Swipe one right to mark it unread.* A desk keeps *Show again puts one back.* and *Mark unread puts one back.*
- **The screen's left edge.** A right swipe counts only when it starts 32 px or more from the screen's left edge (`SWIPE_BACK_EDGE_GUARD`, `swipeMayStart`). Nearer than that is the phone's own Back, which a page cannot stop, so the row stays still under it. A card's row begins about 24 px in, so about 8 px of the row is left out.
- **A swipe the wrong way answers.** A right swipe on a shown row, or a left swipe on a put-away one, moves nothing away (`swipeWrongWay`, 24 px). The row slides by itself far enough to read its strip's word (`swipeShowReach`: 64 px for **Hide**, 108 px for **Mark opened**, always short of the mark), holds for 0.9 seconds and slides back. The rest of that touch moves nothing and is not a press on the row. A touch stops a row that is showing its strip. A person who asked their device for less motion gets no slide.

## The pair, stated once

| Card | Left, the strip on the right | The row goes to | Right, the strip on the left |
|---|---|---|---|
| My Bids | **Hide** | **Hidden** | **Show again** |
| Recent Reports | **Mark opened** | **Opened** | **Mark unread** |
