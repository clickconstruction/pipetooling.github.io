# v2.7417 — Your pay: no door to a portal that is turned off (2026-11-15)

Punch list #355, a follow-up to v2.7399 found the same evening, with the generated types after that change's push. Client only.

## What was wrong

`my_sub_sheets_on_job` hands back the sub's short address and the token of their newest live link, each when there is one. The row built its door from the short address first. A portal is on only while a live link exists (`my_sub_portal_address`, v2.3067, answers nothing without one), so a sub whose link had been revoked but whose short address stayed would have been offered **Open your portal** onto a portal that is off. Read on prod on 2026-11-15: two people have a short address, and both have a live link, so nobody saw it.

## What changed

- **[`src/lib/jobs/subPayRow.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/subPayRow.ts)**: no live link, no door. With one, the short address when there is one, else the link.
- **The generated types and the dev-mcp catalog** after `20261116123000_my_sub_sheets_on_job.sql` was pushed: one name, `my_sub_sheets_on_job`, in each. A sibling's columns that the same regeneration found on prod are left for that sibling's own types change.

## After the push of v2.7399

The migration is in prod's ledger and the drift check is clean. The function is a definer with row security off; `anon` cannot execute it. The bed proved its behaviour on the PR. It was not called on prod as a real subcontractor.
