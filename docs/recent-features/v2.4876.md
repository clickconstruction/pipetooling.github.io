# v2.4876 — Contracts: a paper shows the day it was signed, with no made-up time (2026-10-07)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Found in punch list #64's live walk (`to-dos/contract-second-signer-names.md`, retired after v2.5101) on J1064. A paper filed at 6 PM read *Signed on paper by … · recorded Oct 7, 2026, 7:00 AM CT*.

## Why it said 7:00 AM

`fileSignedJobContract` (`src/lib/jobs/jobContractFileWrite.ts`) is the one path that files a paper:

- **With a Signed on date**, it keeps `signed_at` as that day at noon UTC (`<date>T12:00:00Z`) and the date in `paper_signed_on`.
- **Without one**, it writes `signed_at` as now and leaves `paper_signed_on` empty.

The lines read `signed_at` as a moment, so a *Signed on* day printed as 7:00 AM Central and was called *recorded*. Two things were wrong with that. Nobody recorded anything at 7:00 AM, and the day is when the customer signed, which can be days before the office filed it. [v2.4657](v2.4657.md)'s own example read that way: the Palmers' paper, signed Sep 30, read *recorded Sep 30, 2026, 7:00 AM CT*.

## What changed

[`jobContractLifecycle.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobContractLifecycle.ts) has two new functions:

- **`isSignedOnDayMarker(iso)`** knows the marker by its shape: exactly noon UTC, as the client writes it and as the database returns it. It does not go by `paper_signed_on`, because older paper rows carry a `paper_signed_on` beside a real `signed_at`, and those keep their time.
- **`formatContractSignedStamp(iso)`** gives the day alone for the marker, read in the app's zone (`calendarYmdInAppTzFromIso`), and the full stamp otherwise.

Where the words changed:

- **The paper's line** (`jobContractSignatureAuditLine`, and through it `jobContractSignersAuditLine`). A *Signed on* day reads *Signed on paper by Michael Palmer and Grace Palmer on Sep 30, 2026*. A real stamp still reads *· recorded Oct 7, 2026, 6:02 PM CT*. This reaches the window's History and its paper, Documents → Jobs, and the Job window's Documents tab.
- **The printed block** (`jobContractSignatureBlocks`): the record's stamp beside the name is *Sep 30, 2026* for a marker.
- **The signed rail's banner** (`JobContractSignedRail`): *Signed by … · Sep 30, 2026*, not *Sep 30, 2026, 7:00 AM*.

An electronic signature never reads as a day, whatever its stamp.
