Someone else named: their address is the reply-to and the sender is added to the CC — never twice, never when she is the To, never past 10. A person with no address or outside the office roles is refused by name.

- **`send-gc-statement-email`** — takes `reply_to_user_id`, reads that user with the service role, runs the rule, sends with its reply-to and CC, audits the CC it used, and echoes `reply_to` / `reply_to_name` / `on_behalf`. A refusal is a 400 before anything is sent.
