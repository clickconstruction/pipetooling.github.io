# v2.7362 — Sub payments and the bank: a transfer between the company's own accounts is never offered (2026-11-15)

Punch list #347. A fix found by walking v2.7352 (the Banking side of the match) on the live data, read only, the same day.

## What was wrong

The matcher offers a bank line to a sub payment on the amount and the day alone: the same amount to the cent, within three days. Mercury's rows for money moved between the company's own accounts are money out of one account, so they were offered too. On Banking → Ledger 27 of the 103 lines that drew **Match to a sub payment…** were such transfers. The Subs Ledger's **Find bank lines** offered the same rows from its side since v2.7239.

## The fix

- `bankLinesFromRows` (`src/lib/subWorkOrders/subPaymentBankMatch.ts`) leaves out a row whose `kind` is Mercury's `internalTransfer` (`OWN_ACCOUNT_TRANSFER_KIND`). Banking's side reads its rows through the same function, so both sides drop them together, and the test that holds the two sides to the same offers still passes.
- `useSubPaymentBank` asks for `kind` in the bank read, which the Subs Ledger's side needs to tell one. A line already matched is still shown whatever its kind.
- The guide *see every payment made to subs by date* says so under **Match it from Banking**.

## The walk's counts

Banking's Ledger drew its 16,000 rows as before, and the sub payments read in the same pass. Every offered amount was the bank line's own.

| | Lines offered a payment |
|---|---|
| Before | 103: 28 payments out, 48 card charges, 27 own-account transfers |
| After | 76, across 53 payments |

Of the 48 card charges, 35 are to a pay-a-person app, which is how some subs are paid. 13 are store charges that share an amount and a week with a payment. Nothing in a bank row says a store charge is not a payment to a person, so those are still offered and the press stays a person's.

Nothing is matched on prod yet, so no line read *paid*.

## The press, walked later the same day

On Taunya's go ("Yes, walk it"), after `dev-mcp` was deployed at server 0.90.0:

1. `plan_sub_labor_payment` then `apply_sub_labor_payment` recorded $10.79 on the Claude Test Sub's ZZ sheet, dated the day a card charge of the same amount posted. It landed through `record_sub_sending` as **sending 1**, the first sending on prod, with `method: other`.
2. Banking → Ledger offered that bank line the payment by name: *Nov 14 · Claude Test Sub · 1113 ZZ Test Walk Project 2026-11-08 · $10.79*. Choosing it ran `match_sub_payment` for the first time on prod. The line read *paid Claude Test Sub · sending 1* in green with **undo**, and the database held the match row and the payment's tick, stamped the same instant.
3. **undo** ran `unmatch_sub_payment`: the match row was gone, the tick was cleared, and the picker was back.
4. The payment was removed from the seat with its reason. The sheet reads $900.00 owed and nothing paid, as before, and the bank line says nothing.

Left behind, by design: a grey removed line on the ZZ sheet's trace, and sending number 1, which is never reused. The Subs Ledger's own picker calls the same two functions and was not pressed.
