# v2.5213 — GC mode: our contract can be signed on a job that already started (2026-10-11)

A fix the lead's walk found on the test project (ef8905d1, its `started_on` set straight on 10-08, before Start anyway existed).

- **The bug** (`GcStartWindow.tsx`): our contract's row showed **Send to sign** and **Mark it signed** only while the job was not started, or was started anyway (`!started || owed`).
  - A job started with neither had no way to mark its contract signed.
  - Bill the customer then said only "Mark it signed before the first bill."
- **The fix:** the row keeps its presses on any job while our contract is unsigned (`!started || owed || !c.done`). Once a started job's contract is signed, there's still no **Undo**, as before. `gc_sign_owner_contract` has no refusal for a started job, so the database already allowed it; only the window hid it.
- **Tests** (`GcStartWindow.render.test.tsx`):
  - A job started without Start anyway, its contract unsigned, offers Send to sign (opening the customer's window at the contract) and Mark it signed.
  - The same job with its contract signed shows neither, and no Undo.
  - Put the old condition back and the first fails.
- **Guide:** *get a GC job ready to start* says the contract can still go out after the job starts.
