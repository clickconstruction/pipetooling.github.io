# v2.7077 — The Lien window's header names the step a notice is on (2026-11-12)

Punch list #325, PR 6, on v2.7048. Found on the read-only walk of the train: a billed row read *Find the owner* or *Record the mailing*, and the Lien window it opened read *Draft the Sep notice — 4 days*. The window builds its path from the job's filings (`buildLienTimelineFromWindow`) and passed `noticeState: ''`, so every notice short of sent read *to draft*. Client only.

## What changed

- **`lienTimelineDesk.ts`**: `buildLienTimelineFromWindow` takes `deskNotice?: { state, holdUntil }` and hands it to the kernel as `noticeState` and `holdUntil`. New `lienWindowDeskNotice(entry)` reads it off the job's desk entry (`LienDeskEntry.pile` and the item's `hold_until`); null when the job has no entry or sits on the *missed* pile, which is no step.
- **`lienTimeline.ts`**: `LienTimelineNoticeState` gains `printed`, a notice in the mail whose certified number is not typed in. Its Next line is *Record the mailing of the Sep notice — 4 days.*, its step words *in the mail · tracking owed*, its *Waiting on* line *the envelope's certified number to be typed in*. The desk's own adapter (`noticeStateOf`) now passes it, so the Lien desk's timeline stops reading *Draft* for a printed notice too.
- **`LienInstrumentsModal`**: new prop `deskNotice`. Read by value into the timeline's memo, so an opener may pass a fresh object each render.
- **Openers**: `JobsStagesTab` (the Pipeline, from its light desk data) and `LienDeskPage` pass the job's entry. `JobDocumentsLienPaper` holds no desk queue and passes nothing, so its window reads as before.
- The words were already in the kernel for the other steps (*Find the owner, then send the … notice*, *Approve the … notice*, *Send the … notice*, *Held — the … notice re-asks …*); the window now reaches them.
- **Tests**: `lienTimelineDesk.test.ts` (each pile's Next line, step words and *Waiting on*; a hold; no entry; an original contractor is untouched), `lienOneReading.test.ts` (the printed step joins the table), `LienInstrumentsModal.render.test.tsx` (the header with and without the opener's word).
- **Guides** *send lien notices from the Lien desk* and *file a lien and never miss its deadlines*. The second also drops a stale sentence that said the Pipeline's row reads *file lien by …* (it has named the next step since v2.7043).

## Not done, and why

- **An affidavit's earlier steps.** The header still reads *File the affidavit* for an affidavit that is not drafted or approved. The kernel has no words for those steps; they are a new sentence each, with a *Waiting on* line, and want their own look.
- **A held notice.** The row reads *Review the hold* and the header reads *Held — the Sep notice re-asks Oct 5*. One is the act and one is the state; both are true and neither was changed.
- **The window opened from a job's Documents tab** has no desk queue to read, so its header keeps *Draft*.
