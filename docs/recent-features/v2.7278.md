# v2.7278 — The job window on a phone: the job on one screen (2026-11-14)

Punch list #355, PR B ([`to-dos/job-window-phone.md`](../../to-dos/job-window-phone.md), the mock-up's direction B as the owner picked it), on PR A (v2.7271). Client only: no migration, no edge function, and no kernel that is copied to the edge functions changed.

## Before

After PR A the top of the window was right, but the Job tab under it still ran six and a half screens in the desk's order: the customer with pills and three unlabelled marks, the money, the target card, Their journey, the Where it stands card, Questions, the whole activity feed with its filters, then the dates, the status rail, plans, the team, reports, the schedule, the costs and the bills. The owner: "throughout it it's not easy to follow".

## What changed

- **[`src/lib/jobs/jobWindowPhoneSections.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobWindowPhoneSections.ts)** (new, pure, tests beside it): `nextOnSite` picks the earliest block on or after today and hands the day, the hours and who apart (the owner's rule: the hours on their own line, never joined to the day); `waitingOnWords` says who the job waits on and for what from the bar's verb (*The office, to bill it*; *The customer, to pay*; *The crew, to finish the work*), the verb inside the headline the door when it is ours and the bar's act at the end when it is theirs, with *Nothing has happened for N days* from the newest note or report; `threadRows` turns the summary's three lines into labelled rows (Last word · They promised · We owe them), the first sentence as the answer and the rest under it, ending in Reply, Write it down, Mark it or Change it; `newestCustomerLines` picks the two newest calls, texts or emails with the customer for the bubbles; `PHONE_FOLDS`, `readPhoneFolds` and `keepPhoneFolds` hold the six folds and which ones this phone keeps open (`localStorage`, as the summary card's fold does).
- **[`src/components/jobs/JobWindowPhoneSections.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobWindowPhoneSections.tsx)** (new): `PhoneSectionTitle` (a band above, a blue bar, the title; a count or a door at its right), `PhoneRow` (a label at the left; the answer, its quiet line and its act in blue words at the right), `PhoneFold` (a 44 px row with the title and a count, the content kept mounted while closed so a typed draft survives), `JobWindowWho` (the name; the number as a CALL press with 💬 Text and ⧉ Copy beside it, the address as an EMAIL press with ⧉ Copy; Portal, Files and Photos as presses with their words, grey with *set* when a link is missing and the role may add it; the Copy press writes the clipboard with a toast, or opens the copy-from window when the app cannot reach it, as the address's did), `JobWindowThread` (the three rows, the bubbles theirs on amber at the left and ours at the right, *Open the thread · N ›*).
- **`DetailJobModal.tsx`** (pane mode on a phone): *Where* titles the shared band on the Job tab; `JobPhotosStrip`'s banner title is the Photos section title with the count and *open all ›* at its right; `JobWindowWho` replaces the customer panel and the link icons; What's happening draws Next on site (the schedule blocks read on a phone's pane, the window's own block as the fallback), Waiting on (through `onPhoneVerb`, the bar's press) and `JobTargetLine` as a row (`row`); the thread section replaces the Where it stands card, the Reply and the written rows opening the line's own thread, the empty rows the customer's thread through `contactForCustomer`; *More on this job* holds the folds Questions on this job, Activity (with its count), Money (the money and job-account lines from PR A, then the costs, the specific work, the total, the payments and the invoices), Plans and papers, Work and time (the dates, the status rail, the team, reports and the schedule) and Their journey. The desk keeps its order to the line.
- **`threadSummary.ts`**: the second and third lines are *They promised* and *We owe them* on every screen, and empty they read *Nothing promised yet. A day ‹name› gives for a payment or an answer lands here.* and *Nothing open. A call, a price or a date we said we would give lands here.* (the owner: the old words named the mechanism, not the meaning).
- **`JobWindowModal.tsx`** (also on PR A): the window hands the pane the bar's verb, so the Money line and Waiting on end in it.

## Verify

- At 375 px open a job: the sections read Where, Photos, Who, What's happening, the thread, More on this job, each with a titled band. The number row has CALL, 💬 and ⧉; the email row EMAIL and ⧉; Portal, Files, Photos are presses. Next on site reads the day, then the hours, then who. Waiting on names the person and the act, the act in blue when it is ours. The thread's three rows end in blue words. Open a fold, close the window, open it again: the fold is still open.
- On a desk nothing moves but the Where it stands card's two labels and empty words.
