## What shipped

- **Settings**: `TestReportSettings.autoSend` (`'off' | 'pass'`, `TEST_REPORT_AUTO_SEND_CHOICES`, `TEST_REPORT_AUTO_SEND_GRACE_MINUTES = 15`); a **Sending** radio pair on Settings → Jobs & billing → Test reports.
- **Kernel**: `testReportDataFromRowLike(row)` moves the row → data mapping into `_shared/testReport.ts` so the Deno function and the browser read one mapper (`testReportRow.ts` delegates). **[`_shared/testReportSend.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/testReportSend.ts)**: the bucket name, `testReportStoragePath`, `testReportActivityLine` (with an *automatic* flavour), the Resend call with the attachment, `bytesToBase64`.
- **[`auto-send-test-reports`](../../supabase/functions/auto-send-test-reports/index.ts)** (cron every 10 min): reads the settings row; when `autoSend = 'pass'`, takes up to ten `draft` rows with `result = 'pass'`, a hydrostatic type, a system, and `created_at` older than the grace period; for each requires a `gc_customer_id` with a valid email on the customer card **and** a billed Stripe invoice with a pay link; renders the paper with pdf-lib (PR 8), files it as the next version, emails the GC (cc the standing copy) through Resend (`email_type: 'test_report'`), stamps the row sent with `sent_by NULL` and the certifier snapshot (guarded by `status = 'draft'`), and posts *Sent automatically: …* on the job as the job's master. Every skip reason is returned in the response (`no GC on the job`, `GC has no email`, `no Stripe bill`, `incomplete: …`) and nothing is fatal.
- Help guide and `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` describe the switch and the service-role cron.
