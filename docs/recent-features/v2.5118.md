# v2.5118 — Job Parts Tally: mark a card's Cash App pay sends as payroll in one press (2026-10-09)

Punch list #72, the second half of PR 3: the pay bar (`to-dos/tally-transactions-refresh.md`). Client only. No migration, no edge function. The owner chose on 2026-10-09 to show the bar to payroll-access roles only, with no migration. Undo per line, the first half, shipped as v2.5107.

## Why it is gated

Marking a charge as payroll goes through `set_tally_payroll_flag`, which requires `has_payroll_access()`: dev, controller or a pay-approved master. Migration `20260714120000` left assistants out on purpose. The drawn bar also widened the payroll rule that missed the sends, but `mercury_tally_payroll_rules` is `is_dev()` only (`20260906130000`). So the owner's two options were a migration that opens both writes to the assistant, or the bar shown only to the roles the write already admits. He chose the second.

## What shipped

- **The kernel, `src/lib/tally/tallyPaySends.ts`.**
  - `isTallyPaySend` matches money going out (an incoming Cash App credit is not pay) whose counterparty or Mercury bank description names Cash App (`/cash\s*app/i`).
  - **Only sends to a person** (the owner's call, 2026-10-09). `tallyCashAppSendKind` reads the queue row's `note` with `classifyCashAppNote`, the Cash App lane's own words. A note that reads as an expense (gas, Home Depot, materials, a reimbursement, lunch and the rest of `EXPENSE_NOTE_KEYWORDS`) keeps the send off the bar. A pay or advance note, or none, keeps it on. The bar counts the sends it left off (*One more send has a note like gas or Home Depot. Sort it by hand.*), and a card whose Cash App sends are all expenses has no bar.
  - `tallyPaySendPayee` takes the name after the `*` in the bank description, in title case, so `CASH APP*ISAIAH WHITES` becomes Isaiah Whites. The payroll rule seed reads the same field (`tallyPayrollRuleSeed.ts`).
  - `tallyPaySendGroups` makes one group per card holder across their days, ordered by name.
  - `tallyPayBarWords` writes the bar's title, its payees (three names, then *and N more*) and the button.
  - `tallyPayMarkToast` and `tallyPayUnmarkToast` write the messages.
- **The bar, `TallyPayBar`.** It sits on the Team queue above the day cards, one per card with sends, and follows the person filter.
  - It reads, for example, *2 Cash App pay sends on Rob's card · $750.50*, then *To Isaiah Whites and Paige Doe. Pay goes to payroll, not to a job.*
  - Its button is **Mark 2 payroll**, or **Mark it payroll** for one send.
  - The press calls `set_tally_payroll_flag(id, true)` for every send at once (`Promise.allSettled`). A send the server refuses, such as one already on a job, keeps its words in the message after the count.
  - Each bar has its own busy state, so a press on one bar never frees or blocks another. A second press on a bar still writing is ignored.
  - The message carries **Undo**. It deletes the manual payroll marks this press made (`mercury_tally_payroll_flags`, `source = 'manual'`, `is_payroll = true`; the table admits payroll-access roles for every write). So the sends are undecided again, and a payroll rule may still mark them later. A `false` mark would have left a tombstone the rules skip.
  - The queue and the Dashboard count reload after the press.
- **The gate.** `TallyTeamQueue` takes `canMarkPayroll`. `JobTally` passes the value it already computes for My card's **Mark payroll** (`canMarkTallyPayroll` over `usePeopleAccess`), so there are no new reads and no new access rule. Without it, no bar is drawn, and an assistant sorts a pay send line by line as before.
- **It marks; it does not change a rule.** Rules stay in Payroll rules on My card, for a dev.
- **Docs.**
  - The guide *mark payroll transactions in the tally* gains **Cash App pay sends on the team's cards**. Its roles are already dev, master and controller.
  - GLOSSARY (Team purchases), JOB_TALLY_ARCHITECTURE (the Team view) and PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION (`/tally`) are amended in place.
  - The card's status, next, blocker, ver and plan are updated.

## Proof

- **`tallyPaySends.test.ts`, 15 tests:**
  - a send found by the counterparty, by the bank description, and as *CashApp*, but not a store or fuel charge, and not money coming in;
  - the payee from the description, or null;
  - one group per holder across two days, with a card with no sends skipped;
  - the bar's words for one send, for two, for three named payees plus one more, and with no names known;
  - the mark and undo messages, with and without the server's words for a refusal;
  - a send to a person (no note, a pay note, an advance), an expense send (gas, Home Depot, materials, reimbursement, lunch), a mixed card counting two sends off its bar, and a card of expenses only with no bar.
- **`TallyTeamQueue.render.test.tsx`, 6 new tests (12 in all):**
  - no bar without `canMarkPayroll`;
  - with it, a $500 Cash App send to Isaiah Whites shows *1 Cash App pay send on Ann's card · $500.00*. **Mark it payroll** writes `{ p_mercury_transaction_id: 't-cash', p_is_payroll: true }` and says *Marked 1 Cash App pay send as payroll.* The message's Undo deletes that press's manual mark, with no second flag write, and says *1 pay send is back to sort.*;
  - two bars at once: each press writes once, a second press on a busy bar is ignored, and the other bar finishing does not free it;
  - a refused send: the message reads *Marked 1 of 2 as payroll.* followed by the server's reason;
  - a mixed card: only the send to a person is on the bar, the gas send is counted off it and never marked;
  - a queue with no Cash App send has no bar.
- **`JobTally.payBar.render.test.tsx`, new, 3 tests:** the page wires the gate. An assistant's queue gets `canMarkPayroll` false, a pay-approved master's and a dev's get true, and a master who is not pay-approved gets false.
- The tally suites pass, along with the guide tests, eslint, the theme-token check and the full typecheck.

## To see it

As the dev on the dev server, open `/dev-login?as=1&to=/tally?tab=transactions` and go to Team. A card with Cash App sends shows the purple bar. As an assistant there is no bar. These are production charges: mark only sends that are pay, or press Undo straight after.
