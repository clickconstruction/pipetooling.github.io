# v2.5118 — Job Parts Tally: mark a card's Cash App pay sends as payroll in one press (2026-10-09)

Punch list #72, the second half of PR 3: the pay bar (`to-dos/tally-transactions-refresh.md`). Client only. No migration, no edge function. The owner chose on 2026-10-09 to show the bar to payroll-access roles only, with no migration. Undo per line, the first half, shipped as v2.5107.

## Why it is gated

Marking a charge as payroll goes through `set_tally_payroll_flag`, which requires `has_payroll_access()`: dev, controller or a pay-approved master. Migration `20260714120000` left assistants out on purpose. The drawn bar also widened the payroll rule that missed the sends, but `mercury_tally_payroll_rules` is `is_dev()` only (`20260906130000`). So the owner's two options were a migration that opens both writes to the assistant, or the bar shown only to the roles the write already admits. He chose the second.

## What shipped

- **The kernel, `src/lib/tally/tallyPaySends.ts`.**
  - `isTallyPaySend` matches a charge whose counterparty or Mercury bank description names Cash App (`/cash\s*app/i`).
  - `tallyPaySendPayee` takes the name after the `*` in the bank description, in title case, so `CASH APP*ISAIAH WHITES` becomes Isaiah Whites. The payroll rule seed reads the same field (`tallyPayrollRuleSeed.ts`).
  - `tallyPaySendGroups` makes one group per card holder across their days, ordered by name.
  - `tallyPayBarWords` writes the bar's title, its payees (three names, then *and N more*) and the button.
  - `tallyPayMarkToast` and `tallyPayUnmarkToast` write the messages.
- **The bar, `TallyPayBar`.** It sits on the Team queue above the day cards, one per card with sends, and follows the person filter.
  - It reads, for example, *2 Cash App pay sends on Rob's card · $750.50*, then *To Isaiah Whites and Paige Doe. Pay goes to payroll, not to a job.*
  - Its button is **Mark 2 payroll**, or **Mark it payroll** for one send.
  - The press calls `set_tally_payroll_flag(id, true)` once per send. The message carries **Undo**, which calls it with `false`. That is today's unmark: it leaves a manual tombstone, so no rule marks the charge again.
  - The queue and the Dashboard count reload after the press.
- **The gate.** `TallyTeamQueue` takes `canMarkPayroll`. `JobTally` passes the value it already computes for My card's **Mark payroll** (`canMarkTallyPayroll` over `usePeopleAccess`), so there are no new reads and no new access rule. Without it, no bar is drawn, and an assistant sorts a pay send line by line as before.
- **It marks; it does not change a rule.** Rules stay in Payroll rules on My card, for a dev.
- **Docs.**
  - The guide *mark payroll transactions in the tally* gains **Cash App pay sends on the team's cards**. Its roles are already dev, master and controller.
  - GLOSSARY (Team purchases), JOB_TALLY_ARCHITECTURE (the Team view) and PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION (`/tally`) are amended in place.
  - The card's status, next, blocker, ver and plan are updated.

## To see it

As the dev on the dev server, open `/dev-login?as=1&to=/tally?tab=transactions` and go to Team. A card with Cash App sends shows the purple bar. As an assistant there is no bar. These are production charges: mark only sends that are pay, or press Undo straight after.
