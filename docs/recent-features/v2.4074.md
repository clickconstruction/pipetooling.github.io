# v2.4074 — The phone dock: change a slot from More, and PO on the assistant's bar (2026-09-28)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Punch list **#30** (`to-dos/taunya-mobile.md`) — two of Taunya's four asks from the walk on her own iPhone (2026-09-28; the walk and its answers are recorded on the card). Follows [v2.4073](v2.4073.md).

## What she said

- *The bar itself*: "Would like to not have Quickfill at the bottom dock but the PO generator."
- *More*: "Can't swap out pages on dock for different ones under More." The walk page had told her to press and hold a slot; on her phone that did not open the sheet. The hold was the only door, and its hint lived inside the sheet it was meant to open.

## What shipped

- **`src/lib/phoneDock.ts`** — `roleDockDefault` for assistant-like roles (assistant, controller) is `['jobs', 'schedule', 'po', 'inbox']`. A phone that stored its own four keeps them (Quickfill is still a visible page, so a stored set carrying it still parses).
- **`PhoneDockMoreSheet.tsx`** — a **The dock** row at the top of the sheet: the four slots as tiles (glyph, name, *change*). A tap sets `pickIndex` and the sheet enters the same swap mode the hold does (`swapAt = swapIndex ?? pickIndex`): the title reads *Swap ‹slot› for…*, the row hides, a page tile puts that page in the slot and closes. Entered by a tap, the header's Close becomes **‹ Back** (to More, the sheet stays open); entered by the hold it stays Close. **Reset to the role's four** shows under the row on a device with its own set, as it did in swap mode. The hint reads *Tap one of the four to change it — or press and hold it on the bar.* `pickIndex` clears whenever the sheet closes.
- The hold on the bar is unchanged. Why it did not fire on her phone is not known: `useLongPress` fires on release after 450 ms, so a touch iOS ends early on that button would never release. The tap door makes it moot for her; the card notes a simulator run as the way to see it.
- **Guide** `dispatch-mode` (the four, the row, the hold as the second way), **GLOSSARY** *Dispatch Mode*, **`docs/twins/APP_DIRECTORY.md`** (the dock line).
