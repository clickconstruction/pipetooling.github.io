# v2.6895 — Lien affidavit: the short form prints on one sheet (2026-11-10)

Punch list #305, after the short form (v2.6855), the one-press redraw (v2.6867) and the homestead notice (v2.6884). The owner, 2026-11-10: test it live, "something I am worried about [is] having part of it be cut off, I think if any part of the notary box gets cut off it should push that section to the next page and say so but with our new shorter version this should not be an issue."

## What the test showed

Nothing is cut: since v2.6810 the last numbered item, the signature and the notary's block are one run a page does not split (`filingKeepTogetherRuns`), in print and in the PDF, and the short form's closing is such a run.

But the short form was not one page in print. The 13 affidavits on the desk (12 ready, 1 to draft) were drawn on the short form in the local preview, read only and with nothing saved, and each was printed with Chromium's own print engine (Playwright `page.pdf`, `preferCSSPageSize`) and built as the PDF, then read page by page with `pdfinfo` and `pdftotext`:

| Paper | Print for notarization | Download PDF |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 contracted with the owner (four paragraphs) | 1 page, 85% and 88% of the sheet | 1 page |
| 11 under a GC (seven paragraphs) | **2 pages**: 100% to 106% of one sheet, so item 7, the signature, the notary's block and the statute line moved whole to page 2 | 1 page |

So the paper the notary would get under a GC was page 1 with six paragraphs and page 2 with one paragraph and the closing, beside a PDF of the same paper on one page. The print sheet's leading is 1.75; the PDF draws 11.5 pt type on 5.6 to 6 mm lines, about 1.45.

## What changed

- **`FILING_PRINT_TIGHT_LEADING` (1.45) and `lienAffidavitPrintOptions(fields)`** in `src/lib/jobsDocuments/lienFilingDocuments.ts`: the short form prints its body at the PDF's leading. `filingDocPrintHtml` takes a fourth argument, `{ leading: 'tight' }`, and writes the leading inside `@media print` only, so the screen keeps the paper's own.
- **The Lien window** (`LienFilingTabs`): **Print for notarization** and a recorded affidavit's **View** pass the option from the fields the paper is drawn from.
- The type size, the margins and every word are as they were. The earlier ten paragraphs, the notices, the release and the legal statements print on the sheet they had.

## Decisions

- **Leading, not type.** v2.6810 refused smaller type on a recorded instrument. The leading is the PDF's own, so the two break alike.
- **Only the short affidavit.** The earlier form runs to two pages at either leading, and an approved paper keeps the look it was approved with.
- **A paper that is still too long moves its closing whole**, as before. A homestead paper with the longest text on the desk fills 99% of the sheet, so one more line (a long legal description) puts its closing on page 2.
- **Not built: saying so.** The print sheet cannot know where the browser broke the page, so a line on the paper or in the window that says the closing moved needs a measured preview. Asked of the owner.

## Proof

The same 13 papers on the changed builder, the same engine:

- Print: all 13 on one page, 75% to 93% of the sheet, the closing whole on page 1. PDF: all 13 on one page.
- With the homestead notice added to each: all 13 on one page in print, 81% to 99%, and in the PDF.

Unit tests: the short form's print sheet carries the leading inside `@media print` and keeps its keep-together run; the earlier form and a notice carry none.

Not observed: Chrome's own print window and a real printer (a session cannot open one, and a print on a real job files a sent copy). A printer that scales the page changes nothing about where it breaks.
