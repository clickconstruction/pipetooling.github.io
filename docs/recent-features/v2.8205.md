# v2.8205 — A place set with no signal waits on the phone (2026-11-21)

Punch list #413, step 2b, the last piece of the card's build.

## Why

A crew sets a spot standing in a yard, and a yard is where the signal drops. Until now a place whose save failed stayed on the sheet with an error, and was gone the moment the sheet was left. A place that is lost that way is a place nobody sets twice, and the trench is filled by the time anyone notices.

## What ships

**A new place is kept.** When the phone says it is offline, or the save fails the way a lost signal fails, the place is kept on the phone: its name, its kind, its words, its dot and the phone's margin, with its photos (`keepForLater` in the map's window, `keepSpot`).

- The sheet closes as it does on a save, and an amber line in the window reads *Kept on this phone. It goes to the job when the signal is back.*
- The press that began the place, **Photo of a spot** or **Keep this spot on the job**, hears it is kept, so a second press does not make a second place.
- A refusal by the database is not a lost signal. It says why, as before, and nothing is kept: it would be refused again.
- A change to a saved place is not kept. It says it did not save, and the place still has its row.
- If the phone cannot hold the place either, with no room or in a private window, the sheet stays open with what was typed and says to press again when the signal is back.

**The app sends what waits** (`sendKeptSpots`, from `PaintedMapHost`): when the app opens, when the phone says the signal is back, and when a map's window closes. A line above the dock says how many places wait, with **Send now**. It is gone once they have gone.

**Nothing is made twice.** The place's id is made on the phone, and so is its list's when this place starts the job's list. Each step of a send is the same write on a second try: a map, a photo or a row found already there is an earlier try that landed with its answer lost, and counts as done.

**Seven days.** A place waits as long as a kept photo does. After that the phone drops it with its bytes.

## How it is kept

Not as a press in the app's waiting queue. A walk that was never saved is already kept by the painted maps themselves, in the phone's own storage, and offered back by their host. A place is kept the same way, beside it, with its photos' bytes in the store a job photo's wait in (`keptPhotos`). So the line at the top of the app, which counts the queue's presses, does not count a place. The painted maps' own line does.

## Not done here

- **Not tried on a real phone with the radio off.** Every path is held by tests with the browser's own offline flag set. A phone in airplane mode on a ZZ Test job is the walk that proves it.
- A place kept on one phone is not seen on another until it goes.
- The line of a walk still waits its own way, as it did.
