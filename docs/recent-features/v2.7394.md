# v2.7394 — New Sub Labor: a sheet names the one person it pays (PR 5 of the sub labor train) (2026-11-15)

Client only, no migration. Punch list #346, on the kernels of v2.7383. Taunya, 2026-11-15: "people being on jobs flooding subs may be the root of the issue"; asked whether a sheet should ever name more than the one person it pays, "No, one person"; and for the form, "Only one name".

## What was found

`JobsSubLaborFormModal.applyPickedLaborJob` ticked every person on a picked job's team as crew, from three doors: the job picker in the form, *Sub Labor* from a job, and the billing prefill. On prod that left 8 sheets, all made 2026-08-25 to 2026-09-17, each naming one sub and two or three teammates. Nobody put a teammate there on purpose. The names did nothing for what was paid, listed the sheet on the teammate's own screens, and gave the teammate an even share of the sheet's cost in People → Review and the Team Summary.

## What landed

- **One pick.** `JobsSubLaborFormModal.tsx`: a chip, the job-team offer and a sub added inline all go through `nextCrewPick`, so ticking a name takes the place of the one ticked before. The step is called *Paid to* (`crewStepTitle`) on the wizard, its Next press and its label. The search reads *Search for a person*.
- **The job's team is offered, not ticked.** `applyPickedLaborJob` and the billing prefill keep the team in `laborJobTeamOffer` and tick nobody. `SheetJobTeamOffer` (`SheetCrewHints.tsx`) draws it under the chips and goes quiet once someone is ticked.
- **Whose the sheet is.** `SheetCrewWhoLine` under the chips, from `crewStepLine`: the sub's, whole; or a teammate's own, as sub labor. Any second name is a warning with a press: *Take … off, for a sheet of their own* for a second sub, *Take the teammates off this sheet* for teammates beside a sub.
- **The same line twice.** `SheetSameLineWarning` over the lines, from `sameLinesOnOtherSheets`: a line the job already carries on another sheet, words and amount alike.
- Guide `record-sub-labor-on-a-job`, step 2.

## Old sheets

A sheet saved earlier with several names keeps them when it is opened to edit. The form drops no name by itself and refuses no save for it, so the 8 old sheets stay editable until the clean-up in the change that follows.

## Not done

The form is the only guard. The database does not refuse a second name on a sheet.
