# v2.7513 — Lien notices: the enclosed invoice is a paper invoice, with no portal link on it (2026-11-16)

Found on a printed § 53.056 notice the owner scanned on 2026-11-16, and agreed as a fault the same day. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## Before

The invoice behind a notice (§ 53.056(a-3), v2.3437) is made by `noticeInvoicePrintSections`, which printed `buildPhysicalInvoiceEmailBodies(doc).html`: the invoice's **email** body. Two things on the sheet were wrong for paper going to a third party:

- **The customer's portal address.** `create-stripe-invoice` appends *See your updated statement any time at <portal address>* to a Stripe bill's footer (`stripeInvoiceFooter`, v2.2878) and stores it in `stripe_invoice_footer`. The address opens the bill-to customer's statement with no sign-in. The enclosure printed the whole footer, so a notice mailed to the property's owner and the original contractor carried it. The run's packet, the Lien window's print, the courtesy PDF and a piece mailed through Docsmit all read the same document.
- **Email words.** The page opened *Please find your invoice attached as a PDF.*

## What changed

- `footerWithoutPortalLink` in `src/lib/jobs/noticeInvoiceEnclosure.ts` drops any footer line that carries a portal address: the return line by its own lead (`portalReturnFooterLine`), or any line holding `/portal?t=` or the short portal host. The company's own block stays as typed. `enclosedInvoiceDocument` applies it, so the printed page, the PDF and the mailed piece all lose the line at one place.
- `buildPhysicalInvoiceEmailBodies(doc, { paper: true })` leaves the attached-as-a-PDF line off the text and the HTML. `noticeInvoicePrintSections` passes it. Every other caller (the bill email, its preview, the samples) passes nothing and is unchanged.

## Not changed

The bill itself, on Stripe and in the customer's own email, keeps the return line: it is the customer's address and theirs to read. Notices already printed and filed on a job keep what they printed.

## Tests

`noticeInvoiceEnclosure.test.ts` (+3): the footer with the token line, with the short address, with a link typed by hand, alone, without one, and with CRLF; the enclosed document and its printed page carry no portal address; the printed page has no attached-as-a-PDF line while the email's body still opens with it and is otherwise the same.
