# v2.7508 — Lien desk: apply a notice that already went to a job the desk still asks about (2026-11-16)

The owner, 2026-11-16, about a job whose lien went out that day: its notice *"was a part of a ... notice sent last month that we failed to apply. I think we need a route to apply edge cases like this."* The record this writes landed in v2.7505. His two picks from the drawn route: **same property only**, and **a month the paper does not print goes through with a reason**.

## What was stuck

One certified notice went to an owner for every unpaid job at a property. The desk held it on some of those jobs. On one it was recorded with the months the paper printed, and the job's unpaid money had since moved into a later month, when its last day of work was set by hand. The desk read that month as not noticed, kept an approved and printed notice waiting, and offered *Print and mail* for a second notice of the same claim. *Already mailed? Record it* asks for the months as printed and records a paper anew, so it could not say that the paper on record stands for this month.

## What ships

- **One door.** *Already mailed? Record it* on a notice's footer now mounts `LienNoticeAlreadyWent`. When the job's property holds a notice on record under the same original contractor, it first asks *Which paper went?* With none on record, or on *It is not on this list. Record it now ›*, it is the by-hand record as before. A read that fails falls back to the by-hand record.
- **The papers.** `lienRecordedPapers` (`src/lib/jobs/lienNoticeApply.ts`) groups filing rows into papers by the day the owner's copy went and that envelope's number. A packet id is not the test: one paper recorded in two sittings holds two. Each paper shows its day, how it went, its total, the jobs it is recorded on, the months it names, who received it and its saved copy.
- **The same property.** `loadLienApplyContext` takes the job's saved property record and any other record with the same parcel in the same county, or the same street and the same owner's name (`sameLienProperty`). One property can sit on two saved records, one under the contractor and one under the homeowner. A paper to another property is never listed.
- **The checks** (`lienApplyChecks`). Refused: no month ticked, a paper with no dated send to the owner, a month the job already has a notice for, a paper that went before the month began. Warned, and let through with a typed reason of a sentence or more: a month the paper does not print, no copy to the original contractor on record, a day after the month's deadline (it reads as late, as any late notice does), a day before the job's last day of work.
- **The record** (`lienApplyPayload`, `applyLienNoticePaper`). One `job_lien_filings` row on this job: the months it stands on, the paper's own sends, packet, total and saved copy, `applied_from` pointing at the paper's row, the reason and who applied it. The paper's own row is never written.
- **The waiting notice is set aside, not sent.** Its desk item takes `voided_at` and `fields.setAsideNoticed` (the record that covers it, who, when), only when every month it names has a notice afterwards. It is not marked *sent*, because the items guard lets only a master or dev put a status back to approved, and the office must be able to undo. A set-aside item keeps its approval and signature.
- **Undo.** In the Lien window's *Filings on this job*, an applied row reads *as part of the notice on job N · applied by …* with its reason, and its *Void* reads *Undo*. `undoLienNoticeApply` voids the row and puts the notice back as it was. If the job has started another notice since, the old one stays set aside and the toast says the record is voided.
- **Nothing else reads differently.** `list_lien_notice_months`, `list_lien_affidavit_windows` and the affidavit's notice paragraph read filing rows, so the month reads as noticed, the gate is met and the paper's day is the day sworn, with no change to any of them.
- The guide *send lien notices from the Lien desk* has a section, **A notice already on record**.

## The edge cases this came from

| Kind | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| One paper named several jobs, recorded on some | the by-hand tick list, at recording time only | pick the paper, apply it to the job left out |
| The paper's months differ from the month the desk asks about | nothing; a duplicate offered | apply with a reason |
| Mailed and never recorded | the by-hand record | the same, behind *It is not on this list* |
| Recorded on the wrong job | void and type it again | apply it to the right job, then void the wrong row |
| An affidavit waits on a notice that went | held in *Waiting on its notice* | the applied row meets its gate |

## Questions for counsel

1. Does a notice that states the whole unpaid balance and lists earlier months stand as notice for a later month's work that added no new charge?
2. Does a notice sent to the owner alone hold when no copy to the original contractor is on record?
3. Can one paper about several jobs at one property stand as each job's notice? The desk never applies a paper across properties.
4. An affidavit names the day its notice went. When a second notice goes after the affidavit was signed, must the affidavit name it?
