# v2.5919 — An error line says what it means, and links to a guide (2026-10-26)

Punch list #187's PR 6 (`to-dos/help-at-the-point/`), the owner's lens of 2026-10-26: *the guide that opens from an error line*. None of the 438 guides answered an error line until now.

- **`lib/help/errorGuides.ts`** (pure): one card per family of error, not per surface. `errorFamilyOf` reads the class the formatters already decide (network, the `42501` access refusal, the `22P02` · `PGRST116` broken link, the sign-in lock, a timeout, the server's own words, the page itself) and, for a line that kept the words and lost the code, the formatters' own sentences. `errorDoor` answers three rows (what happened, what was saved, what to do), the family's guide and whether a Retry makes sense. `ERROR_DOOR_OPENED_CONTROL` records a press as `error_door_opened` with `#<family>`.
- **`components/help/ErrorDoor.tsx`**: `ErrorDoorButton` (*What this means*, after the reason) and `ErrorDoorCard` (the same `LegendCard` the desks' ? open, the guide in a new tab with `via=error`). Two pieces, because a line is often a `<p>` and a card cannot sit inside one.
- **On three lines**: the Retry panel (`OfflineRetryPanel`, nine surfaces: clock in and out, the reports, the dispatch schedule, the desk queue, the estimate, the users row queue; the reason keeps its own span), the Physical inbox's *Could not read the papers*, and the Legal desk's *Could not read the legal tables*.
- **Four guides**, in Getting Started: *know what No connection means and what was saved*, *know who to ask when the app says you do not have access*, *read the server's words on a failed save* (a table of the six phrasings Postgres puts on a line), *know what to do when the app hangs or a link is dead*. The page family opens the existing *update the app when a new version is ready*.
- **Not built, on purpose**: a door on the error toasts. About 1,400 places show one; v2.5903's `error_shown` rows will say which families people meet, and those toasts get the door first.
- **Tests**: `errorGuides.test.ts` (the families, every row in plain words, every guide on the registry under its title, the door's address, which families retry), `ErrorDoor.render.test.tsx`, `OfflineRetryPanel.render.test.tsx` (+1: the door beside the reason, Retry still there).

No migration, no edge function.
