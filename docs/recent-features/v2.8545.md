## What was wrong

The box above **Make the bill and email …** ended *Every send is kept in Bills sent.* on Test as on Live. The walk made a test bill, saw its email arrive, voided the bill and found no line for it on `/bills-sent`.

Read in the code, two rules make that so, and both are meant:

- `send-stripe-invoice` files no `sent_documents` copy for a test-mode bill. Its email goes to the sender and is not a send.
- The line Bills sent draws for a Stripe email is the bill's own send row (`jobs_ledger_invoice_stripe_email_sends`). **Void Stripe invoice…** deletes the bill, and that row goes with it.

So a test send is on the list, marked as a test, until its bill is voided. Then nothing is left.

## What changed

- `theyWillGet` (`src/lib/billing/billCustomerTheyWillGet.ts`) ends the Stripe box, when `testMode` is on, with `TEST_SEND_KEPT_LINES`: *Bills sent lists a test send as a test and keeps no copy.* and *Voiding the test bill takes that line off.* On Live the last line is unchanged.
- The guide `see-which-bills-went-out`, under **A test bill**, says no copy of a test send is kept and that voiding the test bill takes its line off.
