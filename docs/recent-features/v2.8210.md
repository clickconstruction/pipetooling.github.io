# v2.8210 — Painted maps: the page a link opens draws on Google Maps (2026-11-21)

Step 4 of punch list [#387](../../to-dos/painted-maps-on-google.md), its switch. v2.8208 built what the page needed and left it off. Client only: no migration and no function.

## The yes

The rule that picks the map under a painted map kept the public page `/way` off Google until a look at Google's billing console said yes, and a test held the line. On 2026-11-21 Taunya was shown what the look could read and answered *Yes, turn it on*:

| Read | Answer |
|---|---|
| Links to a painted map ever made (`painted_map_links`) | 0 |
| Opens of `/way` ever kept (`painted_map_link_visits`) | 0 |
| People's own map loads, from the card's reading of 2026-11-20 | roughly 10 to 40 a day, against 10,000 free a month |

Google's console was not read again that day: it needs a sign-in the session did not have. From here each open of a link by a reader is one map on Google's count, made after the reader gives a name and never before.

## What changed

- `PAINTED_GOOGLE_REACH.screens` gains `'way'` (`paintedMapProvider.ts`). The page already hands the rule its ask (v2.8208), so this one word is the change.
- The rule's test now holds the page **on** Google for looking, and still holds drawing, placing and walking off it.
- The page's map is `PaintedMapGoogleCanvas`: Google's photograph with street names for **From above**, its road map for **Streets**, light on the page's light paper, with the reader's dot and the line kept in the picture together (`frameMe.ts`).
- The page has no **Time** and makes no picture, so nothing of Esri's shares its screen with Google's map.
- The fall back is unchanged: no key, a refused key, a script that never comes or a map that never paints, and the Leaflet canvas draws the map.

## Docs

The guide `paint-a-map` says the page a link opens is on Google Maps too. The Glossary's *Painted map* entry and the environment note in `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` are amended.
