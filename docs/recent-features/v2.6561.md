# v2.6561 — Machine templates at Settings: small, medium and large excavators, skid steers and drills, each with its gauges and its walk-around questions (2026-11-06)

Punch list #270 PR 6, the owner's ask after the spike (2026-11-05): "create templates for certain kinds of machines, such as excavators that are small, medium and large, as well as skid steers". The schema of v2.6555 carried templates from the start and seeded nine; this is the card the office edits them on, beside Stage checklists.

## What changed

- `src/lib/machines/templatesIo.ts`: `load` (the active templates and the questions with no machine of their own), `saveTemplate` (insert or update), `retireTemplate` (inactive, so machines that picked it keep working), `saveQuestions` (one owner's rows in order: gone rows made inactive, new ones inserted, `sort` by place).
- `src/components/machines/GaugeListEditor.tsx`: the gauge list edited in place, lifted from the fleet card's Gauges editor, which now draws it too.
- `src/components/settings/MachineTemplatesSettingsCard.tsx`: tabs for every machine's walk, the leaving check, each template and a new one; a template's name, kind and size, its gauges and its own questions with *a No holds it*, the arrows and Take off; Save and Take it off the list. The io is injected for the render smoke.
- Registered as the `machine-templates` card of the job's record group (`lib/settings/jobsBillingGroups.ts`, `pages/Settings.tsx`, `lib/settingsSearch.ts`); the groups test counts it.
- The guide *set up machine templates with their gauges and walk-around questions* (Office).
