# v2.4611 — Card refunds come off in the card-charges window (2026-10-05)

Migration `20261005235207_card_charges_window_refunds` (`docs/migrations/20261005235207_card_charges_window_refunds.md`); no client change. Punch list **#52** (`to-dos/fuel-is-a-job-cost.md`), PR 4b-1b.

## The mismatch

People → Spending was compared with the Job window by the Job window's own loaders (three people's job cells over 90 days; two jobs over their whole lives). It matched to the cent wherever no refund was involved — J667 read $7,870.24 and ⛽ $3,811.70 on Spending, the Job window and Job Summary alike — and every difference was a card refund: 16¢ on J363, $168.06 on J1033, $1,045.69 on J583. Mercury files a refund to a card as kind `other` with the card still on it; `list_card_charges_window`'s rows function (v2.4594) kept only the card kinds, so refunds never came off Spending.

## The change

`_card_charges_window_rows` keeps a card kind **or** any transaction carrying a card id (duplicates still out). Every `other` row with a card is money in — merchant returns and fuel pre-authorizations given back: 27 refunds and $1,083.34 over the 90 days to 2026-10-05, 135 and $11,618.37 all time. The kernel nets them by their sign, as the Job window does. The wrapper, the signature and the columns are unchanged; the body is still `LANGUAGE sql`, checked when the push creates it.
