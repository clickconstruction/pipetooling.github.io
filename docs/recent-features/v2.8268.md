## What changed

- **`write_down_payment(p_message_id, p_day)`**, `SECURITY DEFINER` behind Follow up's guard (`follow_up_press`: the office team, no training account, no digital twin). With a day it writes the line's `message_promises` row as a payment, written, with that day: made when the line has none, written over an offered day, just talk or an earlier payment day, refused in words over a day written as another kind. With no day it takes a payment mark back off as just talk. Only a customer's own call, voicemail, missed call, text or email takes a mark.
- **Why a function**: the table grants a client no insert, `write_down` takes an offered row, and nothing makes one. The mark had no door.
- **The bed**: `supabase/tests/messages_follow_up/40_payment_word.sql`, twenty-five checks, added to `scripts/pgtest-messages-follow-up.sh`.
- **Docs**: the migration's page; `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`'s definers among the Follow up presses.

## What reads the mark

Nothing new. Two screens already read a written promise from `message_promises` and will read this one once the client writes it: the job's thread summary (*Dana promised a payment by Fri Nov 27.*, in place of *Nothing promised yet*), and the thread's move chip, which reads *Their move · until Fri Nov 27 · payment* once our word is the newest. While their word is the newest the chip stays *Our move*, as `courtOf` has it for every promise.
