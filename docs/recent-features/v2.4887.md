# v2.4887 — Pipeline: Statements to send counts the GCs that broke a promise (2026-10-07)

Found by the help-guides freshness pass (punch list #91) and listed in [v2.4882](v2.4882.md)'s *Found for the owner and the app*. Punchlist sent it back as its own XS PR.

## The bug

The Pipeline's 📬 *Statements to send* card ends with *· N broke a promise* when `ready.late` is above zero (`PipelineMoneyOpportunities.tsx`). `buildGcWorklist` gives a row its `promise` only when it is handed a pay date per GC and today. Neither card caller passed either: `JobsStagesTab.tsx`'s `gcRoundCards` and `usePipelineMoneyOpportunities.ts`'s `gcRound`. So `late` was always 0, and the tail never showed. GC Review itself always passed both, with six weeks of marks and today, so its rows and the call sheet were right all along.

## The fix

- **`pipelineRoundCards`** in `src/lib/jobs/gcWorklist.ts` is the one way the Pipeline builds its two round cards. It takes this week's marks for the steps, plus `recentMarks` (six weeks) and `todayYmd`, both required. It reads the newest pay date per GC with `latestExpectedPayByGc`, as GC Review does. A caller that drops either input no longer compiles.
- **`JobsStagesTab.tsx`** already loads six weeks of marks for the temperature chips (`roundTempMarks`). It now passes them and `todayYmdInAppTz()`.
- **`usePipelineMoneyOpportunities.ts`** loads six weeks of marks once with `listGcStatementRoundMarksSince`, where it loaded only this week's before. It takes this week's out of them, so the Pipeline makes no extra request.

## The owner's call it sits beside

`to-dos/owner-decisions-pending.md` gains *GC statements: may one go out unchecked?*. The app says *nothing goes out unchecked*, but Share → Draft Message, Copy, Print and the scheduled sends work on an unchecked GC, and neither send function reads `gc_review_certifications`. The two builds are named: a gate, or the two strings reworded. Nothing changes until the owner answers.
