# v2.7904 — My email: a save of your mail is in your record only, off Needs you (2026-11-19)

The owner's choice of 2026-11-19. Client only: no migration, no edge function, no stored row changed.

## Before

v2.7068 tells a mailbox's owner when a developer saves their mail onto a job through the dev seat: one `notification_history` row of their own (`template_type` `mail_saved_for_you`, `channel` `app`). Two places showed it. The Needs you card drew a blue line for seven days, kicker *Your mail*, press **See the record**. Settings → Activity logs lists the row for good, badge *In the app*.

The line asked for nothing and could not be dismissed. The owner saw it on a user's Dashboard on 2026-11-19 and took it for a notice that should have stopped.

## The choice

Asked "Take the Your mail line off Needs you and keep only the record in Settings?", he answered: *Yes, record only.* The other option, to stop writing the notice, was not chosen: the mailbox's owner keeps a trail.

## What changed

- **The line is gone.** `buildNeedsYouItems` builds no item from the notice. The item key `mail-saved`, its rank, its actor, its door and the source `mailSaved` are removed, with the inputs `mailSavedEnabled` / `mailSaved`.
- **The code only the line used is gone.** `useMailSavedForYou`, `savedForYouIo.ts` (`loadMailSaved`), and from `savedForYou.ts` the line's kernel: `mailSavedNeed`, `mailSavedShownSince`, `MAIL_SAVED_SHOWN_DAYS`, `MAIL_SAVED_DOOR` and the words `kicker` and `action`. The Dashboard no longer reads `notification_history` for it, on mount or on focus.
- **The record is as it was.** `gmail-mine`'s `noteSavedForYou` still writes the notice, batched as before. `SettingsRecentPushNotifications` still asks for the `app` rows and still draws the badge from `MAIL_SAVED_WORDS.badge`. `savedForYou.ts` now holds the record's words alone.
- **A line already showing clears by itself.** It was drawn from the read, so the new client draws nothing; no row is touched.
- **The guide.** *Save an email to a job from your own mail* now sends the reader to Settings → Activity logs, and says nothing shows on the Dashboard.

## Tests

- `dashboardNeedsYou.mailSaved.test.ts` holds the decision: a notice handed in builds no item for any role, there is no source, key or actor for it, no Needs you file names it, and the hook and the read are gone.
- `SettingsRecentPushNotifications.render.test.tsx` (new) holds the record: the card asks for `push`, `both` and `app`, and an `app` row shows its words under *In the app* with no template name.
- `savedForYou.test.ts` is removed with the kernel it tested.

## Left stale on purpose

Two comments on the edge side still say the Dashboard reads the notice: `noteSavedForYou`'s in `supabase/functions/gmail-mine/index.ts`, and the header of `src/lib/myMail/savedForYouNames.ts`, which is copied to `_shared/gcKernels`. Correcting either asks for a regenerate and a redeploy of every importer, for a comment. Fix them with the next change that deploys `gmail-mine`.
