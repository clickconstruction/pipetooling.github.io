# v2.3991 — GC Review: the pay date on a word goes on the GC's bills (2026-09-28)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Owner ask: Jobs Stages records when a customer says they will pay ("They said…"); the GC Review word form asked the same question and kept the answer to itself. The date lived on `gc_statement_round_marks.expected_pay_by` (one per GC per week) and never reached `job_payment_promises`, so the Stages chip, the payment forecast, the Lien Desk and the reliability record did not see it. Owner call: file a promise for every date, whatever the temperature.

## What shipped

- **`src/lib/jobs/gcWordPromise.ts`** (24 tests) — the bridge, pure: `gcWordBills` (a GC group's review rows folded to one bill per job, with the date each job carries), `standingPromiseYmd`, `wordPromiseHeadline` (what they said last time; late once passed with money open), `payDateShortcuts` (the standing date, this Friday, next Friday, the month end — the next month's when this one is under a week away), `planWordPromise` (picked bills not yet on the date are filed; same-date bills are skipped; other-date bills are counted as moves), `wordPromiseSummary`, `promiseChannelForWord` (call → phone; other → none), `wordPromiseNote`, `wordPromiseSavedMessage`.
- **`addJobPaymentPromisesSettled`** in `paymentChaseIo.ts` — one date on many jobs through `add_job_payment_promise`, four at a time, never throws; returns the jobs saved and the jobs that failed. No chase touch, the same as "They said…".
- **`GcStatementMarkSentForm`** — the date moves from under the note to under the temperature and asks "When did they say they'll pay?"; shortcut chips beside the picker; "Hot means they gave a date" when hot has none. Given `bills` it shows the headline, the cover line ("Goes on all 4 bills · $27,499.49"), the notes, and a Choose bills checklist, and the payload carries `promiseJobIds`. Without `bills` the payload is what it was.
- **`JobsGcReviewModal`** — three props from the shell (`promisedPayDates`, `canFilePromises`, `onPromisesChanged`); `markRound` saves the mark, then files the promises and says where the date now shows; `saveCallSheet` files a row's date on every bill of that GC when the date is today or later (a repeated date that has passed is not promised again). A promise that fails never undoes the word: the toast names how many bills the date did not reach.
- **`JobsStagesTab`** passes its promise map, `canMarkPromisedPay`, and the reload.
- Help: `run-your-gc-statement-round` (the date, the chips, "The date goes on their bills"), `know-whether-a-customer-keeps-their-word` (a fourth place a promise is recorded). `GC_REVIEW_MODAL_ARCHITECTURE.md` → Mark sent dialog.

## Decisions

- **The mark keeps its date.** `expected_pay_by` is still written; the worklist, the header pill and the Temperature board read it as before. The promise is written beside it.
- **Re-promising is visible, not blocked.** A bill on another date takes the new one and the earlier promise reads broken under the existing rule in `paymentPromises.ts`; the form counts those bills before the save.
- **Roles.** The mark admits controller; `can_write_payment_promises()` does not. `canFilePromises` follows the Stages board's gate, and a refused write is reported, not hidden.
