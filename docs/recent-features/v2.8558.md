## What the office sees

Until now a GC's proposal showed only under its paper's row on that job's Documents tab. The office heard of one only by opening the job.

The office's Needs you card now has one amber line, on the Dashboard and on Quickfill:

- One proposal reads **A GC proposed stricter settings**. More read **3 GC proposals wait on an answer**. The figure is the count.
- Under it: who proposed, from the name the GC typed, and the job. Then *Nothing changes until you answer. Open the job to take it or decline.*
- **Open the job** opens the job that has waited longest, in the Pipeline, with its window on **Documents**. The proposal is under the paper's row there, with **Take it** and **Decline…**.
- The press answers nothing. A person takes the proposal or declines it on the job.

The line leaves when the proposal is taken, declined or set aside. It is read again when the window comes back into focus and when a job's papers change in the tab, so a take or a decline clears it at once.

It sends no push and no email, as the card's design says. It ranks with the customers who wait on the office (40).

## Who has the line

The office: a dev, a master technician, an assistant and the controller. That is the set the table's policy shows a proposal to (`is_office_staff()`), and the set that may answer one. Anyone else reads no row and has no line.

A person in the office who has not signed the job's paper yet meets the job's prompt first, as on any job that requires a paper. Its door **This job's papers and who has signed** holds the same proposal.

## A door that names its tab

`/jobs?jobDetail=<job>` opens a job's window on **Job**. It now reads one more name, `&jobTab=<tab>`, for the tab the window opens on: `job`, `edit`, `bill`, `costs`, `history`, `documents`, `materials` or `map`. A name the window has no tab for is ignored. Both names leave the address once the window is open. The line's door is `/jobs?tab=stages&jobDetail=<job>&jobTab=documents`.
