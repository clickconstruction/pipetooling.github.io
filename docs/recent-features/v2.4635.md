# v2.4635 — Legal portal: matters list largest balance first (2026-10-05)

Client only; no migration, no function. Item 11 of punch list #85 ([`to-dos/legal-portal-firm-ready/`](../../to-dos/legal-portal-firm-ready/README.md)). Stacked on items 3 and 4 (v2.4625, v2.4634). Mockup: `mockup-11-matter-order.html` beside the card — three matters in today's order and in the new one.

## The defect

`share-your-attorney-their-portal` promised the firm sees its matters *largest first*. `legal-portal` returns them by `released_at` (oldest referral first) and the page drew them in that order, opening the oldest. A $2,100 matter referred in September opened ahead of a $14,400 one referred last week.

## What changed

- New kernel [`src/lib/legal/legalFirmMatterOrder.ts`](../../src/lib/legal/legalFirmMatterOrder.ts): `orderFirmMatters(matters, balanceOf)` — largest balance first, the newest referral first on a tie, the function's order after that; a matter whose packet could not be built keeps its place after the rest.
- `LegalPortal.tsx` sorts with it, by the packet kernel's balance, and opens the first matter in that order. The list's caption reads *Matters · largest balance first* when there is more than one.
- The guide says the order, the tie and that the largest opens by itself.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **Which order?** Largest balance first: a firm works the money, and the biggest claim is the first call. The newest referral breaks a tie because it is the one the firm has not read yet.
- **Balance, or total demand with the firm's fees?** The balance. Ranking by the firm's own fees would move a matter up because the firm billed it.
- **Sort in the function or on the page?** On the page. The balance is the packet kernel's figure, built on the page; the function cannot rank by a number it does not compute, and a second copy of the money rule would drift. The function's order stays as the fallback.
- **Say the order on screen?** Yes, in the caption, so a new referral sitting third surprises nobody.

## Left out, and why

- The digest email keeps the function's order: it carries no balances.

## Tests

`legalFirmMatterOrder.test.ts` (5): largest first; the newest referral on a tie; a full tie keeps the function's order; a matter with no packet sorts last; the input is not changed. `LegalPortal.sample.render.test.tsx` (+1): two matters sent oldest-first draw largest-first, and the largest opens.
