# v2.8684 — A job's Activity reads again after a developer clears test notice lines (2026-11-27)

Punch list #443, row 1, finding 283 of the fixing thread's ledger, from the walking thread's sitting 30 (2026-11-27, walk W2). Client only. No migration, no edge function, and no kernel an edge function copies. Nothing owed after the merge.

**Read.** The walk pressed **Clear…** and **Clear 3 lines** in a ZZ test job's Lien window (v2.8644). The window's own line went at once, and the rows were gone from the database. The job window open behind it, opened before the press, still listed the three lines in Activity until the job was opened again. So it was a stale list and not a failed delete.

Why: the two lists of a job's Activity hear a new line from the database. `useJobThreadNotesForModal.ts` (the job window, Job Mode's details, Quickfill) and `useJobThreadNotes.ts` (the Pipeline's rows) each listen for an `INSERT` on `job_activity_events`. Nothing told them of a delete, and v2.8644's two functions are the first presses in the Lien window that take a line off.

**The mend.** The press that deletes says so, and the lists read again.

- `src/lib/jobs/jobActivityChanged.ts`, new: `announceJobActivityChanged(jobId)` sends a window event that names the job, and `onJobActivityChanged(listener)` hears it until its returned function is called. It is the shape the counter tickets use (`ticketEvents.ts`). A list that is not mounted hears nothing and loses nothing, since it reads on its next open.
- `LienVoidedByHandRecords.tsx` announces the job after **Clear…** when the function cleared one line or more, and after **Delete…** of a voided by-hand record, whose Activity line goes with it. Nothing is announced on a cancel, a refusal or a clear that cleared none.
- `useJobThreadNotesForModal.ts`: while its window is open, an announcement that names its job reads the list again, quietly, through the read its database signals already use.
- `useJobThreadNotes.ts`: an announcement reads the open thread again when it is that job's, and reads the row's counts again.

**Why not the database's own delete signal.** A delete's signal carries the row's key and not its job, so a list could not tell whose line went without reading on every delete of the table. The press knows the job.

**What it does not reach.** Another tab, another device or another person's screen still reads on its next open. The two presses are a developer's, on test jobs.

**Tests.**

- `jobActivityChanged.test.ts`: a listener hears the job named, hears nothing once stopped, and is not called for an event that names no job.
- `useJobThreadNotesForModal.activityCleared.render.test.tsx`, new: the list holds three notice lines, the job is announced and the list reads again and holds none; another job's announcement reads nothing; a shut window and an unmounted one hear nothing.
- `LienVoidedByHandRecords.render.test.tsx`, three more cases: **Clear…** announces the job once the function cleared lines; nothing is announced when it cleared none, on a cancel or on a refusal; **Delete…** announces the job, and a refused delete does not.

**Pictures.** None: nothing is drawn differently. A list that was stale reads again.
