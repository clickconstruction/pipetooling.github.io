# v2.7423 — Lien desk: More details sits right under an open gate (2026-11-15)

Taunya's ask of 2026-11-15, on the Lien desk with a notice open whose first gate held the send: *"I think that this space can be removed and details can become 'more details'"*. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What a person sees

- **The press reads More details.** The gate box's fold press read *Details ∨* closed and *Fold the details ∧* open. It now reads **More details ∨** and **Fewer details ∧**. The words live in one place, `LIEN_GATES_FOLD_WORDS` in `src/lib/jobs/lienDeskGates.ts`, and both drawings read them: `LienDeskGates` (the four gates) and `LienGatesSentFold` (a sent notice's one line).
- **No band above the press.** With a gate's row open, the press stood a band below the row's words. It now sits right under them, 4px down.

## What the band was

Nothing held content there. An open row (`.lienGateDetail`) ends in 0.5rem of bottom padding, the box (`.lienGates`) puts a 0.5rem gap between its grid and the fold row, and the press had a line of its own under both. On a one-line row, such as the public-property sentence, the 16px read as an empty row the width of the words.

## How it is drawn

- `LienDeskGates` marks the fold row `data-under-row="yes"` when any row is open above it, a gate that is not clear or one a person opened.
- `src/index.css`: under that mark the last open row gives up its bottom padding and the fold row takes back half the gap (`margin-top: -0.25rem`). The 4px left is the room the row's bring-up ring needs (`lienGateBringUp`).
- The rule is held to the wide box, `@container lien-gates (min-width: 561px)`. Stacked under 560px the last thing above the press can be a folded gate's cell, so the whole gap stays.
- With no row open (a clear job, every row folded) the press keeps the whole gap under the four cells.

## Not changed

- The leader's one-screen approve on a phone keeps its own press, *Details ▾* and *Hide details ▴*. It unfolds the whole pane, not the gates.
- The affidavit and retainage panes do not draw this box. `LienDesk.tsx`'s notice pane is its only caller.
