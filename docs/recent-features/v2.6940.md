# v2.6940 — The dev seat fixes a payment on a job and combines two jobs: plan_job_payment and plan_job_combine (2026-11-11)

Asked by the owner on 2026-11-11 ("lets add verbs to do it"), after a working session found a bookkeeping fix the seat could not make. An old shell job and the real job for the same work both read paid for the same money, and the bank held one deposit. The shell's payment row had been typed in by hand months later: an amount and a day, no type, no reference and no bank row. The office fixes this in the app by taking the unbacked row off and then combining the shell into the real job. The seat had neither press. Nothing from that session is in this change: the tests run on made-up jobs.

## What was added

Two plan → apply pairs on `dev-mcp` (server 0.65.0, eighty-one write verbs). No wrapper RPC and no migration: each goes through the door the screen uses, as the dev, so the RPCs' own role checks and the tables' RLS hold.

1. **`plan_job_payment` / `apply_job_payment`** (`supabase/functions/_shared/devMcpJobPayment.ts`), three actions.
   - `remove` POSTs `remove_jobs_ledger_payment_and_reconcile`, the RPC behind the Job window's Remove and Accounts Receivable's Unlink and remove. It works the paid figure out again, writes the payment trace, flips a bill back to Billed and moves a Paid job that no longer covers its total back to Billed. A reason is required. The apply then writes it as a note on the job's activity in the dev's name (`jobs_ledger_thread_notes`, the row `plan_activity_note` writes). A row with a bank deposit behind it is refused unless `unlink_bank: true`.
   - `record` inserts one `jobs_ledger_payments` row under RLS with the columns the payments table writes (`paymentInsertRows`), after the job's last. The same amount on the same day refuses the plan unless `duplicate_ok`. Work paid in kind is left to Bill Customer.
   - `link` ties a hand-recorded row to its bank row through `apply_mercury_bank_payment_allocations` with one `{ payment_id }` allocation, Accounts Receivable's own link. A change of type, reference or note is the row's own write under RLS.
   - The plan is the server's read: the job with its total, paid figure and status, every payment row on it, what stands behind the row named (a bank row, a typed type and reference, or nothing), the paid figure and status after, what the write sets off and the note it leaves. It refuses a row Stripe holds, a deposit the bank returned and a deposit with less left than the payment. The hash commits to the job's `updated_at` and every payment row.
2. **`plan_job_combine` / `apply_job_combine`** (`_shared/devMcpJobCombine.ts`): one POST of `migrate_job_ledger_costs_and_delete(p_from, p_to, p_allow_billed)`, then the ask to `photo-save` to move the box photos' files, as the Combine tab's finish does.
   - The plan shows both jobs with their payment rows and bills, an exact count of thirty-two tables the RPC repoints, the target's job total, paid figure and status after, and the prior numbers kept.
   - `stops` lists every reason the combine would stop, each with its fix, where the RPC answers with its first refusal only: the source's status, a payment, a bill or a collect flow on it, a live work order, an open agreement or pay applications on both, and supply shares past 100%. The seat adds three holds of its own: two jobs on different customers, a payment both jobs hold, and under `allow_billed` a source payment with a bank row behind it or a bill Stripe hosts.
   - There is no undo. The plan says so, and the apply needs the plan's hash and `confirm_no_undo: true`, runs the plan again and refuses while a stop stands.

## What the read found about the combine

- **Bills and payments never move in a combine.** The Combine tab refuses a source that holds either. The Delete flow's Reassign to another job… (`p_allow_billed`) deletes them with the source. So a duplicate payment does not double the kept job's paid figure, but a real payment on the source would be lost. The plan says which case it is.
- **The source's job total is added to the target's.** Two cards for one job with the same total would read double after a combine. The plan warns when the two totals are equal.
- **The intended order** is the payment verb first, then the combine. `payment_on_both` names the two rows and says which has a bank row behind it.

## One new read

`restCount` in `dev-mcp/index.ts`: a GET with `Prefer: count=exact` and one row asked for, as the dev. The count is read off `Content-Range`. A count that fails takes only itself out of the plan.

## What the screens do that the verbs do not

Moving a payment to another job (the payment row's Move), applying a deposit to a bill or a job straight from Accounts Receivable, recording work paid in kind, voiding or deleting a bill, undoing a Stripe part payment or unwinding a bill marked paid, and separating a job's lines into a new job.

## Tests

`src/lib/devMcp/devMcpJobPayment.test.ts` and `devMcpJobCombine.test.ts` over `jobMoneyFixture.ts`: a shell with an unbacked payment row beside a real job with a wire. The fake REST refuses a table or a column the generated catalog lacks. The tests read the newest migration bodies of the three RPCs, so a change to a refusal, to the billing guard or to a moved table fails there.
