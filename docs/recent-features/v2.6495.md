## Decisions

- **The window is the thread for an open stage check.** The row press opens it and loads the notes through the same expanded-thread path, so the inbox hook, the realtime refresh and the note draft are unchanged; the inline block stays folded while the window has it, and **Not now** folds it.
- **Reply and close, not a bare Close.** The inbox has always required words to close; the window's press names what it does. The sender never gets it: the row closes itself when they answer, and a sender who is also in Dispatch still answers on the sheet under their own name.
- **The push on a reply is a stage check's alone.** A note on a portal request or a plain task pushes nobody, as before: those requesters are the link minter or the sender's own later self.
- **The settle push reads the trigger's note.** The client sends no words of its own, so the push says what the row says: *Handled: … — Answered on the sheet by Jo Office · Not here*.
- **Call 3 stands**: no text to the group on a high send.
