# v2.5803 — Call in Quo or from my phone: every Call button asks once on a phone and keeps your pick (2026-10-23)

To-do #152 built, on the owner's yes with one change. The proposal (PR #6074, the same day) found that Quo's phone app opens on a link and dials from the company number, that a desk has only the one-time setting that makes Quo the computer's calling app, and that Quo's API cannot place a call for a person. It recommended Quo from the first press for every seat holder, with a switch on Settings → Your account. The owner: *"I think the only thing that we could change in this offering is the user can choose to call with QO or on their own phone."* So the choice is the person's, at the press, and nobody is sent through Quo on a press they did not pick.

- **The kernel** (`src/lib/quo/dial.ts`): `planCall` is the whole button. No seat: `tel:` as it always was, nothing beside it. A seat on a desk: `tel:`, with the desk's words a ▾ away. A seat on a phone: the first press opens the sheet (the link is null), **Call in Quo** or **Call from my phone**, each a link; the kept way then dials at once, the button reads *Call in Quo* only when that is what it does, and the ▾ asks again. `quoDialHref` is Quo's own link, `openphone://dial?number=%2B1…&from=%2B1…&action=call`, `from` the box's number the thread texts from (decision 4), left out while the boxes row is not read so Quo picks the person's number. A number with an extension, or one outside the US and Canada, is one the link cannot carry and stays `tel:`. The seat is the person's own on the Company phone seats card or the one shared by the office (`hasQuoSeat`), since that login may be on their phone too, and the first press asks anyway. `isPhoneDevice` reads an iPhone, an Android, and an iPad that says Mac with a touch screen. The kept pick lives in `localStorage` (`messages_call_through`), read and written like Show Mine · Everyone.
- **The hook** (`src/hooks/useCallThrough.ts`, `lib/quo/dialIo.ts`): one read of the seats row and the boxes row a page, shared by every button; a failed read answers no seat, so the buttons stay `tel:`. The kept pick is one `useSyncExternalStore` value, so a pick in one sheet turns every Call on the page at once.
- **The button** (`src/components/messages/CallButton.tsx`): the site's own look, its glyph, its words (the reply box prints the number, the receipt keeps `TELL_WORDS.call`), and a glyph-only control's label follows the words (*Call in Quo (512) 555-0187* on the thread's ☎). The way to switch back is a ▾ joined to the button's right edge, not a chip beside it: a chip is a second control with its own words on rows that already hold Nudge and Call at phone width, while the ▾ adds 28 px to the button it belongs to and says what it is on hover and to a screen reader (*Call another way*). The sheet is a `ResponsiveModalShell` at 1300, above the customer door's 1100: on a phone the two ways as rows with their notes (*Dials from 512-555-0144. Quo writes the call in the thread.* / *Dials from your own number. Quo does not see the call.*), the kept one marked, and the quiet lines about the kept pick and the store; on a desk the `tel:` link, the Mac and Windows steps, and a link to the guide. The sheet's rows are links, so the tap that picks is the tap that dials.
- **The seven sites**: the call task's **📞 Call** (`FollowUpSections.tsx`), the New lane's **Call back** on a missed call or a voicemail (live now; it was a dead *Call back in Quo* button whose tooltip promised this), Waiting on them's **☎ Call** and **☎ Call them** (`WaitingRow` carries the number and the kind now, not a `tel:`), the thread's ☎ and the numbers under the arrow (`boxForReply(lastWroteTo(allLines), contact)` for the caller ID), the reply box's Call kind, the Pipeline's customer door and the Sent receipt.
- **The Settings card** the proposal drew was not built: the ▾ is on every Call, and a setting far from the press is one more place to drift. **I called** stays on the row for a call made off Quo. The proposal's quiet line *Quo logs this call* after a Quo press, and `call.ringing` / `call.answered` read live, are the later cut named in the to-do.
- **Tests**: the kernel (the link, the device, the seat, the kept pick, every plan, the plain words); the hook (the seat own or shared, the box's number with the team's as fallback, one pick for every button, no sign-in); the button's smoke in each state (no seat, a desk's sheet, the phone's first press with the two links and the kept pick, Quo kept and the phone kept, an extension, a site's words and a glyph-only label); the tests that named the old act.
- **Words**: the guide *call a customer from a task* § Call in Quo or from your phone (the old *On a desk, Quo places the call* overstated); *see who is waiting on us and what we owe them* on Call back; the Glossary's Call task; `docs/MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md`'s Calls paragraph.
- **Nothing on the server**: no migration, no function, no new key. A call made in Quo lands by the webhook and closes the call task as it did since v2.5596.
