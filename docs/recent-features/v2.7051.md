# v2.7051 — Eight ZZ test estimates are deleted (2026-11-12)

Estimates 167, 168, 169, 175, 176, 177, 189 and 190 were made on walks of the app under two ZZ test customers. They were sent, declined or accepted, so they showed in the Estimates lists and in every count read from them, the Bridge's won rate included. The owner saw them there and asked for them to go.

**Why a migration.** `estimates_delete_draft` and `estimates_update_draft` let no signed-in account delete or change an estimate that is not a draft, so the app has no press for this. (A ninth ZZ estimate was an unsent draft and went through the app's own draft delete.)

**The delete.** `20261112200000_delete_zz_test_estimates.sql` deletes a row only when its number is one of the eight, its customer's name starts with `ZZ`, and no job and no bid is tied to it. A second run deletes nothing. It reports its count in a `NOTICE`.

**What goes with them.** Nine `estimate_customer_events` lines and two `estimate_share_links` cascade. Three lines in Messages keep their place and lose their `estimate_id`. The two ZZ customers stay.

**Getting one back.** `estimates` is on the 90-day `deleted_records_archive`, so a dev can restore a bundle with `restore_deleted_records`. The share links are not archived and would not come back. See `docs/migrations/20261112200000_delete_zz_test_estimates.md`.

No client code and no function. After the merge: `supabase db push`.
