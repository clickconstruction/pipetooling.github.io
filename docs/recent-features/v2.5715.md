## Is this the best we can do?

1. **Fewer presses on a phone**: the week's four questions were six scrolls and a count by eye; now four numbers at the top, each one press from its rows.
2. **Plain words**: every tile's label, note and tip runs through `plainWordsFailures`; the guide section passes the sweep.
3. **Do what is ready**: a tile with nothing behind it reads *none* and is no press; a refused estimates read costs the one tile, not the strip; a refused lines read says so in one line.
4. **Reuse**: the longest wait is the queue the panel already read; the failed-text rule is `deliveryMark`; the estimate events fall back to the lines Went out already reads; the answer rule says why notes do not count, as `courtOf` does.
5. **Cut**: a Not delivered filter on Went out (the red rows already stand out; the door scrolls and picks Texts); narrowing the strip by the lens (the strip is the office's read, said under it); a reply-latency fact in the database (the turn rule reads the lines).
