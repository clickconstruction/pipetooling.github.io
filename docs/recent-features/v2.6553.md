# v2.6553 — A check with Dispatch shows on the job: the Pipeline's flag, the Documents row's chip, the question's tag (punch list #245, PR 3, closes the card) (2026-11-05)

Punch list #245's third and last PR. PR 1 (v2.6360) sent a check to Dispatch from its row and drew *With Dispatch* on the sheet; PR 2 (v2.6494, v2.6495) gave Dispatch the context window and closed the request by itself when the check was settled. Until now the office read a check with Dispatch in two places only: the inbox and the sheet. This PR puts it on the three surfaces the office already reads a job from.

## What changed

- **The Pipeline row's mark** (`StagesStageBar.tsx`): while a check on a stage is with Dispatch, the stage's name wears `⚑` in the box's place and its title ends *· with Dispatch* (*· 2 with Dispatch* for more than one), still amber, still the press that opens the row's checklists sheet (#246). `data-stage-with-dispatch` carries the count for tests. The kernel `stageChecklistMarks` (`lib/jobs/stageChecklistMarks.ts`) takes a fourth map, the open requests by fixture id, and puts the count on the stage's state as `withDispatch`; `markGlyph`, `stageWithDispatch` and `withDispatchMarkWords` word the mark. The board's read `useStageChecklistStates` reads the open stage-check requests on the board's jobs beside the answers through `loadOpenStageCheckCounts` (`stageCheckDispatchIo.ts`, chunked like the answers) and fails that read soft on its own, so a refused read of the inbox never costs the board its marks.
- **The Documents read-out's row** (`JobDocumentsChecklists.tsx`): a check with Dispatch wears the sheet's chip under its words, *With Dispatch · 8 min · need a part*, and Dispatch's newest reply under the chip in the sheet's own words. The read-out reads the open requests once through the sheet io's `dispatch.open`, and a Take off or a Skip drops the chip with the row, since the trigger of v2.6494 closes the request.
- **Questions on this job** (`DevelopmentSideCards.tsx`'s `QuestionRow`): a request whose flavour is `stage_check` wears the inbox row's *Stage check* tag beside who asked. The reader `readRequestsWhere` selects `pending_action` on the Dispatch table alone, so the Development page's Questions card wears the tag too.
- The guides *send a check to Dispatch*, *answer a check the crew sent* and *read the Pipeline's money view* say what the office sees.

## Decisions

- **The flag replaces the box.** A check with Dispatch is by its nature unanswered, so the box would say nothing the flag does not; one glyph keeps the names row the width it was. The title carries both facts.
- **The count rides the state.** `StageChecklistState.withDispatch` is optional and set only by the board's kernel, so the sheet, the strip and the read-out keep computing the state as before.
- **The flavour column is read on the Dispatch table only.** The first live read named `pending_action` in the columns both lanes share, and the estimators' table refused the whole read with *column does not exist*; the Questions section drew its failure panel. Each lane now selects its own columns.
- **No new read on the read-out.** The sheet io already carried `dispatch.open`; the read-out reads it once, the way the sheet does, and drops a chip on the presses that close the request.
