# v2.7887 — Workflow page: a failed load says what could not be loaded, and why (2026-11-18)

Three follow-ups left by punch list #397 PR 1 (v2.7838), which stopped the Workflow page from creating a workflow on open. The owner asked for all three on 2026-11-18. No behaviour of a working page changes.

## 1. The unreachable find-or-create fallbacks are gone

`saveStep` and `copyStep` (`Workflow.tsx`), `createFromTemplate` (`useWorkflowTemplates.ts`) and the projection writes (`useWorkflowProjections.ts`) each read `workflow?.id`, fell back to `ensureWorkflow(projectId)` when it was missing, and then said *Workflow not found. Please refresh the page.* Since v2.7838 the page draws those presses only on a workflow, so the fallback could not run, and had it run its sentence would have covered the insert's own reason.

- Each save now reads `workflow?.id` alone. With none in hand it says *This project has no workflow yet, so nothing was saved.* (`NO_WORKFLOW_TO_SAVE_ON`, `src/lib/workflow/workflowStart.ts`) and sends nothing.
- `ensureWorkflow` has one caller left, `startWorkflow`, the **Start a workflow** press. A save can no longer make a workflow.
- `useWorkflowProjections` lost its `projectId` and `ensureWorkflow` arguments, and `useWorkflowTemplates` its `projectId`. `saveStep` lost the block that re-read the workflow to resync state.

## 2. A failed read names what could not be loaded, then its reason

`useWorkflowStepsEngine.ts` built five failures from the result's raw `error.message`: the workflow, the project (twice) and the steps on a load, and the line items after one. A Supabase error is a plain object, so that showed the server's text. A project the viewer cannot read answers `.single()` with code `PGRST116`, and the page drew that answer's message in place of itself. That is read from the code, not seen on a live page.

They now go through `workflowReadFailure(what, error)` in `src/lib/workflow/workflowReadWords.ts`, which is `formatErrorMessage` under a lead per read: *The workflow could not be loaded.*, *The project could not be loaded.*, *The steps could not be loaded.*, *The line items could not be loaded.* The class of the failure picks what follows: no access, a link to nothing, a lost signal alone, or the server's own words after a colon.

Where each lands is unchanged: a first load that leaves nothing to draw replaces the page (`loadError`), a re-read after a write is the banner (`error`), and an access refusal on line items is still swallowed.

## 3. The viewer's role type admits every role

`WorkflowViewerRole` (`useWorkflowRoster.ts`) was a list of six that left out controller, estimator and primary. The role is read off the `users` row with a cast, so a controller's role arrived anyway, and `isAssistantLike` reads it to give a controller the page's office controls. The type is now the app's `UserRole`. `StepFormModal` carried its own copy of the six and takes `WorkflowViewerRole` now. Nothing a person sees changes.

## Tests

`workflowReadWords.test.ts` pins the four leads and what follows a refusal, a missing row, a server error with no sentence of its own and a lost signal. `useWorkflowStepsEngine.render.test.tsx` answers as PostgREST does, with a code: a project the viewer cannot read and a refused read of the workflow say so in plain words under their lead, the steps and the line items keep the server's words after a colon, and a controller reads line items. `useWorkflowProjections.render.test.tsx` holds a write with no workflow in hand: it says the new sentence, checks the workflow before the words and sends nothing.

Eight mutants each failed a named test or the typecheck, with a passing control before and after: each of the four reads back on the raw message, the lead dropped, the old sentence back, a projection written with no workflow, and the role back to a list of six.

## Not covered

- The guard in `saveStep`, `copyStep` and `createFromTemplate` has no test of its own. The page cannot reach it, which is why the fallback went.
- Writes are untouched. `useWorkflowStepWrites.ts`, the template inserts, the projection writes and the lifecycle runner's *Failed to update step* still build their words from the raw message.
- `Projects.tsx`, `Calendar.tsx`, `CustomerForm.tsx` and `EditProjectForm.tsx` each keep a short role list of their own.
- A lost signal on a read says the app's stock sentence, which ends *so nothing was saved*.
- Not walked live. A failed read cannot be staged on the live site without breaking a read.
