# v2.4542 — Submittals: a rows table that fits the bid (2026-10-05)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Finding 11 of the tab review, the second of the three redraws in [`to-dos/submittals/redraws-10-11-12-before-after.html`](../../to-dos/submittals/redraws-10-11-12-before-after.html) (12 was [v2.4537](v2.4537.md)).

## What changed

- **Two fixed shapes** (`rowsTableShape`). A bid built from the takeoff has no schedule to compare to: its table is *Fixture · Product and parts · Their answer · Cut sheet*. On the SpaceX bid that removes Specified (a dash and the fixture's name on all 14 rows), Reason and Lead time (a dash on all 14) and Status (Proposed on 13). A bid with a schedule — tags on the bid, or any row carrying what was specified — keeps *Tag · Specified · Submitted* and reads status, reason, note and lead time in one *Status* cell. The shape changes when the bid gains a schedule, never because one cell was typed.
- **A part is a line with its answer beside it** (`SubmittalRowProducts`, `rowAnswers`): the part and the reviewer's answer are two cells of one grid row under a *Their answer* heading, so *Rejected “TEL145”* sits next to the faucet. A part still waiting reads *no answer yet* when the row is partly answered; a row nobody answered says *No answer yet* once; a row answered as a whole carries its answer on its line. The heading is drawn once any answer exists, as before.
- **The row's verdict moved under the tag**: *1 of 3 rejected · 2 with no answer yet*, who answered, entered by whom, the day — where the eye runs down the tags. The old *Their call* cell was 102 px wide.
- **The counts are filters** (`rowFilterChips`): *All 14 · Need a cut sheet 6 · No product 1 · Sent back 4 · No answer yet 10*. A count shows when it is above zero; *No answer yet* waits for a first answer. Pressing one keeps those rows, hides the order-only group and says *Showing 6 of 14 rows. Show all*. Which chip is on is the table's own state.
- **Said once** over a takeoff table: *13 of 14 rows are Proposed because this bid has no schedule to check against. Type the schedule to change that. Every part comes from National Wholesale.* The house leaves the rows when every row buys from one (`commonHouseId`); a status that is not Proposed shows under the row's tag. One vendor file is no longer named on every row.
- **A row with no product** reads *No product yet* with *Add the product*, where the product goes.
- **⋯ on a draft row** opens *Part of…* and *× Take off…* in place; *Edit*, *Their answer* and *Split* stay out. The buttons column went from 238 px to about 170.
- **On a phone each row is a card** (CSS on the same markup: `.sub-rows-table` in `index.css`), each cell a block under a small label, the answer under its part. The table was a 273 px window onto 1,251 px.
- The sheet column is headed *Cut sheet*.

## Where the build differs from the drawing

- *Split* stays a visible button (it shows only on a row that can split, and it is a nudge); only *Part of…* and × went behind ⋯.
- The row's count (*1 of 3 rejected*) is kept, under the tag, beside the per-part answers.
- The words *sheet needed* are kept (the guide and the walkthrough use them).
