# v2.7617 — Documents: an Agreements tab lists every agreement in one place (2026-11-17)

Punch list #376, PR 3 of five: the tab. The owner looked for agreements on the Documents page and found none. Client only: it stands on the rules (v2.7604) and on the `agreements_on_file` view and its read (v2.7612), which was pushed before this was armed.

## What is new

- **Documents → Agreements** (`?tab=agreements`), after Sent copies, for the office: `src/components/documents/DocumentsAgreementsTab.tsx`. Every agreement on file as one list, newest first under a band per month. A line is a kind tag, the paper's name, who it is with, the job as a link, the state in that kind's own words and the day.
- **Two rows of chips**, both asked of the database: the kind (*All · Job contracts · Warranties · Staff and subs · Partners · Work orders*) and the state (*Sent and signed*, where the tab opens, *Waiting on a signature* with its count, *Signed*, *Voided or ended*).
- **A find box**: a customer, a job number, a person, a company or the paper's name, every word having to match, asked once the typing rests.
- **Show more** reads the next hundred. While more waits the count line says *The newest 100 agreements*.
- **A press opens the window the kind already has**: the Contract window on the record for a signed job paper, the same preview the Jobs tab opens for one still out, the signed record window for a person's signed agreement, People → Contracts on the paper for one still waiting, the work order window for a work order. No new viewer.
- **The empty list** names the Pipeline when nothing is on file, and offers *Clear the search* or *Clear the filters* when nothing matches. **A failed read** draws the retry panel with its reason.
- **A quiet line under the list** points at the Contract sweep on the Pipeline for a job with no contract, and at the Estimates tab for an accepted estimate.
- **`documentsPageTab.ts`** knows `agreements`, and `shownDocumentsPageTab` shows Estimates to a role without the tab that follows an old link.
- **The guide** *find an agreement we sent or signed*.

## Choices worth knowing

- **Who sees it** is `canSeeAgreements`: dev, master technician, assistant, controller. Those are the roles the tables' own policies let read a job contract and a person's agreement. The view runs as its reader, so a role without the tab that asked it directly would still read only what its own policies allow.
- **An unsigned contract's preview opens inside the press.** The tab has only the line, not the contract's row, so it must read before it can draw. `openHtmlWindowWhenReady` opens the window at once and fills it, which a popup blocker allows. A blocked popup says so in words.
- **The two heavy windows are lazy.** The work order window and the signed record window load on the first press that needs one.
- **It takes `embedSearch` already.** Under the Search tab it will draw no box and ask only for the words it is handed. That mount is PR 4.
- **Nothing is renamed.** *Contract* under a job on the Jobs tab and in the job window stays, and the Jobs tab's own contract lines are untouched.
