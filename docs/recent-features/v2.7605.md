# v2.7605 — Estimates: the option editor's moves become a kernel (2026-11-17)

Client only; no migration, no edge function, no screen change. Punch list **#46** row 3 (`to-dos/decomposition-queue.md`): cut 2 of eight on the Estimates page, its second kernel — Stage-A row 3 of the map (`docs/ESTIMATES_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md`). Stacked on v2.7601.

## What shipped

- **`src/lib/estimates/estimateOptionsEditor.ts`** — the editor's four pieces of option state as one value, `{ options, viewedKey, lines, previewSelected }`, and the moves as pure steps over it: `switchViewedEstimateOption` (the typed lines fold into the option being left, then the target's load), `addEstimateOptionToEditor` (the first press turns one estimate into Option 1, starred, and a copy; later presses copy the viewed option, offered the way its source is), `removeViewedEstimateOption` (down to one goes back to a single price; otherwise the star re-seats and the starred option is viewed), `setViewedEstimateOptionKind` (the preview restarts from the default), `patchEstimateOption`, and `syncedEstimateOptionsOf`. The key maker and the stub line are passed in, so a test names its keys.
- **`Estimates.tsx`** — the five handlers call the kernel and set each piece from the answer. A piece a move leaves alone comes back as the same reference, so setting it changes nothing. `syncedEstimateOptions` reads `syncedEstimateOptionsOf`. 5,325 lines (was 5,356).
- **19 tests** (`estimateOptionsEditor.test.ts`): the fold on a switch, the stub line for an empty option, a switch to the viewed or a missing option; the first Add (two options, the copy's lines equal and not shared), a later Add (the copy of the typed lines, the source's kind, the stub line), the cap; Remove down to one from either side, the stub line, the star re-seated or left; rename; Offered as with the star moving and the preview reset.

## What stayed the same, on purpose

- Rename and pitch still go through a functional `setEstimateOptions`, as before, since they fire on every keystroke.
- ★ Recommended and ✓ Start ticked were already kernel calls (`setRecommendedEstimateOption`, `setEstimateAddOnPreticked`) and are untouched.
- `load()` still seeds the four pieces itself; that is the hydration plan, the next kernel.
