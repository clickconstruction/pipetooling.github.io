# v2.8722 — The trade portal's ticks, picks and file picker fit a thumb on a phone (2026-11-27)

Punch list #205, which is retired, so this cites the number and opens no card. The follow-up to v2.8702 (finding 290 of the fixing thread's ledger). Client only. No migration, no edge function, nothing under `supabase/` or `src/lib/gc/`, and no file the edge functions copy. Nothing owed after the merge.

**Taunya's word, 2026-11-27.** Her pick for finding 290 was *Grow every portal press to 44 px on a phone, office GC pages untouched*. v2.8702 grew the buttons and the links. A second read of the portal at 375 px, by the database lane's thread, found four kinds of press it left small. The managing session ruled all four inside her pick: each is a press a thumb lands on. Typing fields stay as they are, since she was not asked about them, and so does the pay application's sheet, which the office opens too.

**What was left small,** measured at 375 px on the scratch page before this change:

| Press | Stood | Where |
|---|---|---|
| A tick's line (its label) | 32 px | *Who gets our emails*, the add-a-person form, what a quote covers and leaves out |
| A pick (`select`) | 30 px | a quote's days, an alternate's adds or takes off, a percent done |
| A fold's line (`summary`) | 17 px | *The whole job*, a statement of work's price and lines |
| A file picker | 21 px | a quote's file, a file for the job |

**The mend.** Four more rules in `phoneThumbPressesCss` (`src/lib/phonePress.ts`), inside the same phone block and under the same shell as v2.8702's, so nothing outside the portal's page is touched.

- A tick or a radio is pressed by its label, so the label takes `min-height: 44px`. The box keeps its size. The rule is marked important because those labels set 32 px in their own style.
- A pick takes `min-height: 44px`.
- A fold's line takes 13 px of padding over and under, with `box-sizing: border-box`, so its words sit in the middle of 44 px. It takes no flex, which would lose its marker.
- A file picker takes 11 px of padding over and under.

No component changes. `GcTradePortalView.tsx` already draws the rule.

**Measured,** on the scratch page that draws the real `GcTradePortalView` over the function's own sample and reaches no server, in a headless Chrome with the page held to 375 px. The count now takes in ticks, picks, folds and file pickers, so it is higher than v2.8702's.

**What it costs.** A job to quote is about 7% longer on a phone, since it holds thirteen ticks. The other pages grow by 2 to 3%.

**Not grown.** A text box, a number box and a date box. The pay application's sheet, which is drawn outside the shell.

**Tests.** The kernel's test holds the four rules' exact words, that a fold's line takes no flex, that no rule names a field a person types in, and that every selector names the shell. A test page applies no screen-width rule, so the heights are the measures above.
