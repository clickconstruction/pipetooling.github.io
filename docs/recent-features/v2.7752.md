# v2.7752 — Job Mode, short: a one-screen home for a superintendent (2026-11-18)

Punch list #384, PR 2 of 3. Client only. No migration and no edge function: the setting and its function landed with v2.7749.

## Why

A superintendent said there is too much on his screen. His daily page is the Job Mode Dashboard on his phone, about six screens and about eighty presses on a working day, and he uses the first screen. The first screen is right. The weight is everything under it. The owner picked a short Job Mode page, set per person.

## What landed

With Job Mode on and the person's Dashboard view set to Short, a superintendent's Dashboard is, top to bottom:

1. **The job card's top, as it is.** Next stop, today's stops, the press bar. Nothing in it moved.
2. **Open this job.** One row that opens the card's lower half in place: the Materials on site link, then the customer, photos, the ticket, stage checklists, job updates and the note box. Open, the row reads **This job** and folds it away.
3. **The pace line.** The project's own headline, ahead or behind, as one row. A press opens the pace card as it is, with **Fold the pace card** under its headline.
4. **Waiting on you.** Your record under that name, moved up under the card. Same lines, same presses.
5. **Tomorrow.** How many stops and when the first is. A press opens the Schedule tab.
6. **Everything else.** One door. Its second line says what is behind it and how many courses are owed. It opens the full Dashboard for the visit, folded on a phone, with **Back to the short page** on top. Job Mode's **Dashboard** tab returns too.

Behind the door: the pinned quick row, Your week, the hours block, the missed-day door, My Schedule's cards and Training. The trial verdict and the customer's words strip stay on top. Nothing is removed from the app.

## How

- `lib/jobMode/jobModeShort.ts` is the rule and every word: `jobModeShortOn` (the view is Short, Job Mode is on, and `shortPageFor` gives the role the Job Mode page), `jobModeShortTomorrow` with `jobModeShortScheduleRead` (the line counts nothing while the schedule is being read or after its read failed), and `JOB_MODE_SHORT_WORDS`.
- `DashboardJobModeCard` takes `short`. The details' job is worked out once for both forms. A job whose papers still close it keeps its papers gate in the open, and with no job on the card the Materials link stays where it was, since it is the only way to the picker.
- `SuperintendentCard` takes `short`. Its hooks still read while it is one row, so the open card draws at once.
- `JobModeShortPage` draws the order. `Dashboard.tsx` hands it the pieces it already owns. The hidden clock mount, the two strips and the two windows became `jobModeTop` and `jobModeWindows`, which both Job Mode pages draw, so the full page's order is what it was.
- **Everything else** sets `jobModeShowFullDashboard` and `shortShowFullDashboard` together, so the visit lands on the full Dashboard and not on the leader's short one. `leaveEverythingElse` clears both, from the button on top and from the bar's Dashboard tab (`DispatchModeFooter`'s `shortHome`, now passed to the Job Mode bar for a superintendent on the short page).
- `useTrainingCoursesOwed` counts the Training card's red rows for the door, and reads only while the short page is on.
- `DashboardYourRecordCard` takes `title`, and its heading wraps under its name on a narrow phone.

## Who it changes

Nobody, until the office sets one superintendent to Short on People. A superintendent on Full, a leader or a dev in Job Mode whatever their view, and field crew, who take no view, keep the page they had.

  - **Waiting on you** drew under the card once the record held a line.
  - Nobody else moved: the sample leader on Short with Job Mode off got the short Dashboard, and with Job Mode on the full card with **Show full dashboard**. The sample helper in Job Mode got the same page with a Short value on the row as without.
