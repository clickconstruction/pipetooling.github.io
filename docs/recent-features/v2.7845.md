# v2.7845 — Lien desk: a bin with no paper has a grey name (2026-11-18)

The owner's ask of 2026-11-18, with a screenshot of the Board and a red box round *Awaiting Master approval*: *"on the lien desk todo, I would like sections with no cards to have their title text be more grey so it's more clear they are there but not active"*. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## Before

- A bin with no paper and no press folds to one line with a dashed edge (v2.7714). Its name stayed the sheet's dark bold (`var(--text-700)`, weight 700), the same as a bin full of chips, so only the edge said the bin was empty. Its zero was already muted.

## What changed

- **A folded bin's name and count are quiet.** `Bin` in `LienBoard.tsx` spreads `LIEN_BOARD_QUIET_HEAD` (`var(--text-muted)`, weight 600) onto the bin's head and its count. It reads the same `folded` flag that draws the dashed edge, so the edge and the grey name cannot disagree.
- **Grey and a step lighter, both.** Grey alone at weight 700 still read as a heading beside the bold names. Weight 600 with the muted token separates it and the name is still plain to read.
- **Contrast, measured on the live desk in headless Chrome.** Light: 4.83 to 1 on the white surface (it was 10.31). Dark: 5.78 to 1 on the dark surface (it was 9.96). Both clear 4.5 to 1 for text this size. A bin with a chip is unchanged at 10.31 and 9.96.
- **Every level that can be empty.** Both columns' flow bins fold by this one rule, on a computer and on a phone with the bins opened, whether the head is a press or plain.

## What it leaves alone

- **An empty bin that offers a live press** (`whenEmpty`, the retainage notice that rides the run) is not folded. Its name and its press stay at full strength, since there is something to do there.
- **A bin whose only papers are held** has chips, so it is not folded and keeps its name. Its count reads a muted bold zero, as before.
- **The closed ends** at each column's foot are drawn only while a job is in them, so they are never empty.
- ***Public jobs · bond claim* and *Off the lien path*** are drawn only while a job is in them (`buildLienPublicBin` and `buildLienOffPathBin` answer null with none), so neither has an empty state to grey. Punch list #390 is folding those two boxes on another branch, and this PR does not touch their files.
- The Board's own empty line, *Nothing is on the desk…*, and a bin's *none* were already muted.

## Tests

- `LienBoard.render.test.tsx`, a new block of three: every bin with no chip carries the quiet name and count with its dashed edge, and every bin with a chip carries neither. The same on a phone with the head as a press. An empty bin with a live press is not quiet, and with a dead press it folds and is.
- Broken on purpose once: with the rule turned off the three fail and the other thirty pass. Restored, all thirty-three pass.
