# v2.7539 — Checklist: add a photo to any task, and see who hears before you start (2026-11-16)

Punch list #369, step B2 of the Task Mode train (tool 5), stacked on B1 (v2.7537). No migration, no edge function.

## Why

A card could take a photo only at a percent, on a Done when… line, or at the finish of a card whose giver had switched the ask on. Read through the dev seat on 2026-11-16, no task photo had ever been taken. So a person had no everyday way to show proof, and whoever looked a task over had nothing to look at. And an open task did not say who would hear of it.

## What changed

- **📷 Photo on every card of Today** (`PHOTO_PRESS_LABEL`), beside **＋ Note**. It opens **Add a photo** (`TaskPhotoSheet`): the photo tile and one press, **Add the photo**, dead until a photo is in. The shot goes to the card's strip as a `progress` photo at where the card stands, or as a `finish` photo on a done card. It finishes nothing and sets no percent. With no signal the shot is kept on the phone and sent later, as on the other sheets.
- **Before the start**, an open card says what is wanted: *Imani hears when it is done. It asks for a photo at the finish.* (`beforeStartWords`). A task nobody hears and that asks for nothing says nothing. A task sent back keeps its amber callout and skips the line.
- **Right after the finish**, the done line adds one green sentence: *Add a photo or a note for Imani if it helps.* (`justDoneWords`). It shows for a task finished in this visit only.

## One thing drawn differently

The mock-up drew a sheet on every Mark done, *Done. Add anything for Imani?*, with **Just done** as its big press. Built, the task finishes at once and the offer is the green sentence beside the two presses. The mock-up named the sheet's cost itself, one more tap on every done, and a round of daily tasks would pay it each time. The offer still reaches every finished task. If the owner wants the sheet, it is the finishing sheet that exists with its photo made optional.

## How it is built

- `useTaskCardSheets` gains `photoPress` and mounts `TaskPhotoSheet` beside the % sheet and the finishing sheet. The send is the hook's own `send`, so the outbox path is the one that exists.
- The `fresh` card always mounts the sheets now, and takes `justDone` from the page. The page keeps the ids finished in this visit (`doneHere`), since a task that is finished moves from Next to Done today and is mounted anew.
- The Map press named for this sheet in the plan comes with step B3.
