# v2.5660 — Pricing: see what a bid's price is made of (2026-10-20)

Punch list #128, PR 3 of the price-breakout train, on PR 1 (v2.5653, the tax rate on the bid) and PR 2 (v2.5654, a row's kind, tags and split, the kernels and the columns). The owner walked the spike on B505 on 2026-10-19 and said build it all; every shape here is the one they picked in the walk. No migration (the columns are PR 2's); no edge function.

- **The split under a row** (`BidsPricingTab`): a chevron before a fixture opens three child rows in the table's own columns — **Material** (the takeoff × 1 + the markup, the row's own markup cell `+ __ %` editable in the note; a per-unit price typed over the dashed cell wins), **Sales tax** (the bid's rate on that material; the words toggle tax-free) and **Labor** (what is left, never typed) — each with cost/unit, sale price/unit, revenue, profit and margin (`rowPriceSplit.ts`). An Equipment row shows one line, all equipment; an unpriced row one muted line. **Expand all** / **Collapse all** under the Fixture header fill or clear the open set, remembered per bid on the device, and each chevron still works alone.
- **The marks are the row's**: seeded from the engine's `select('*')` of `bids_count_rows`, an edit shows at once and is written to its row (`rowMarksIo.saveRowMarks`); kind and split wait behind the sent-bid lock (`guardPricingWrite`), tags are always free. A failed write puts the row back with a toast; before PR 2's push, one toast says the marks save once the database update is in. A row's markup and the bid's chip preview as they are typed and write when left.
- **The three cards** (`BreakdownCardShell`, folding to one line — chevron, a donut, the title, the share bar and the bid's cost · price · profit · margin — remembered per card per device): **Traditional breakdown** (`PriceBreakdownCard` over `buildPriceBreakdown`: Material · Sales tax · Equipment · Labor · the small costs, Cost · Price · Profit · Margin on one line each; the Material line shows the weighted markup across the rows beside the bid's chip, which writes `bids.sov_material_factor`; with no hours, Labor reads *Labor and margin* with an **Open Labor** door), **Kind breakdown** (`KindBreakdownCard` over `buildPricingComposition` — it replaces the composition bar, `PricingCompositionBar.tsx` deleted) and **Tag breakdown** (`TagBreakdownCard` over `buildTagComposition`, each tag's weighted markup, a row with two tags counted in both and said so).
- **The row's chips**: the kind (`RowKindChip`, the app's `SearchableSelect` without search, dashed while it is the guess, Equipment in amber with one line of meaning per kind) and the tags (a round **+** opening a box; Enter adds, the box stays for another, Escape or leaving closes it).
- **The tools beside Margin ›** (one shell, `RowScopePicker` + `useRowScope`: every row, presets, a tag, a kind, each with its count; a button that says what it will do; one Undo): **+% Markup ›** (`MarkupToolPopover`, the default scope the rows still on the bid's number) and **Tax ›** (`TaxToolPopover`, tax-free or taxed; its **Taxed at __ %** cell is the bid's rate — typed it re-taxes at once, left it saves through Bids.tsx's `commitBidTaxPercent`, behind the lock).
- **Small**: the price cards' amounts draw the cents at 70% (`AmountSmallCents` in `PricingCardsRow`); `pricingComposition.ts`'s two comments lose the spike's word.
- **Tests**: `PriceBreakdownCard.render.test.tsx` (the five lines and the bid line, the no-hours state and its door, the chip's live and committed writes, the fold remembered), `BreakdownCards.render.test.tsx` (the tag card's lines, its two-tag note and its empty state; the kind card's buckets); PR 2's kernel tests hold the arithmetic.
