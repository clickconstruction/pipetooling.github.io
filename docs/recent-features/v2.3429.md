# v2.3429 — Demand letter: the invoice goes with it as Exhibit A (2026-09-14)

Migration `20260914243000_job_demand_letters_exhibits.sql` (two columns on `job_demand_letters`, pushed after merge); no edge function. PR 2 of the *Demand letter, itemized* train (`to-dos/demand-letter-itemized/`; PR 1 v2.3425).

## Why

The letter went out alone. The invoice is the evidence — the "systematic record" a sworn-account claim rests on (Tex. R. Civ. P. 185) — and § 53.056(a-3) already says a lien notice may include it. A checkbox would have meant some letters went without it; the mock-up's Refined B made it always, and made the preview show it as the page it will be.

## What shipped

- **Kernel** [`demandLetterPacket.ts`](../../src/lib/jobsDocuments/demandLetterPacket.ts): `buildDemandLetterPacket(letter, exhibits)` merges the letter PDF and each exhibit PDF with pdf-lib (already a dependency), stamps **EXHIBIT A / B / C** in a box top-right of every exhibit page and a footer line (*Exhibit A · Invoice #… · page n of m*), and returns the page counts. `enclosuresLine()` (*Enclosures: Exhibit A — Invoice #867-2608180928, as sent August 18, 2026 (1 page) · Exhibit C — Delivery record (1 page)*) and `exhibitsSentence()` (*The invoice is enclosed as Exhibit A and the delivery record as Exhibit C.*). +3 tests (a real pdf-lib merge).
- **The letter** ([`demandLetter.ts`](../../src/lib/jobsDocuments/demandLetter.ts)): `DemandLetterFields.enclosures`; the sentence under the statement and the Enclosures line at the foot (before the notarial block); `buildDeliveryRecordPdfBlob()` — Exhibit C, one page from the same rows as the letter's Notice History (+1 test).
- **The modal**: **Enclosed** under the statement — *Exhibit A · Invoice #…, as sent …* per covered invoice (the app's physical-invoice PDF from the same document model the statement reads; *always*), *Exhibit B · Signed agreement — <template>, signed <date>* when `job_contracts` has a signed PDF (checkbox, on) else *none on this job*, *Exhibit C · Delivery record — N dated sends and contacts* (checkbox, on). The preview draws each exhibit under the letter: A as the invoice preview with the stamp, B as a card, C as the dated list. **Print packet** opens the merged PDF in a new tab; **Download PDF · N documents** saves it. The packet is built twice so the letter's Enclosures line carries the page counts. **Save & record send** builds the packet for the counts and writes `exhibits` + `debtor_party` (falls back to the old insert shape until the columns are pushed).
- Guide *send a final demand letter*: the Enclosed block, the packet.

## Not in this PR

Documents → Jobs does not list demand letters yet (the record carries the exhibits; the Lien window's *Sent on this job* is the list). The Stripe-hosted rendering is not fetched — Exhibit A is the app's own invoice PDF from the same rows, labeled with the sent date.
