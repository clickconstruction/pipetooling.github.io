# v2.6217 — Every push through a person's hours: the other thirteen senders, one shared door (2026-10-31)

Punch list #141 PR 5 of 5, the last; the card (`to-dos/quiet-hours/README.md` → The plan → PR 5, decision 3's table) is deleted with it. PR 3 (v2.6029) made a person's **Push me** hours real for a customer's reply alone: `messages-notify` held the push, wrote the row as `held`, and the office heard at once when the holder was off. Every other push the app sends went through its own `web-push` loop and read no hours, so a dispatch request at 11 PM still buzzed a phone whose owner had said 6 AM to 8 PM. Now the thirteen go through one helper, each held person gets the same `held` row, and the Dashboard's line names what waited by kind. No migration; fifteen functions to deploy.

- **The helper** ([`_shared/pushHoursSend.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/pushHoursSend.ts)): `loadPushHours` reads every row of `user_push_hours` once, fail-soft (a table that fails to read, or a row that does not parse, is any time, said in the log); `splitPush` is `pushDecision` from the mirrored `messages/pushHours.ts`; `heldRows` and `sentRows` are the `notification_history` rows, the push as it would have gone with the sender's own `template_type`, `channel = 'held'` or `'push'`, the preview cut at two hundred, the sender's extra columns beside (a checklist's `checklist_instance_id`, a work order's `step_id`, `workflow_id`, `project_id`); `sendPushHonouringHours(admin, push, sendTo, opts)` does all of it for one push: the people on are pushed through the sender handed in, the people off get a held row and nothing, and the sent rows are written too unless the caller keeps its own record (`record: 'none'`, for a sender whose row says more than push). A caller that read the hours once passes them (`hours`), so a run of many pushes reads the table once; a caller whose words differ by person passes `payloadFor`. The answer names `sent`, `noDevice` (the people on whose devices took nothing: the caller's email fallback, where it has one, is theirs), `held` and `devices`. The sender ([`_shared/webPushSend.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/webPushSend.ts), `webPushSender`) is the one `web-push` loop the thirteen had each written: every device of one person, a day's TTL, a device the push service says is gone (404, 410) deleted as `paid-job-email` and the bank-return notice already did, any other failure logged with the endpoint's head. `null` when the VAPID keys are not set, and the helper then pushes nobody and still writes the held rows.
- **The thirteen senders**, each with its hold from the card's table: `notify-dispatch-request` (both modes; a held requester has the held row in place of the `push` row the answer always wrote), `notify-estimator-request`, `notify-bid-mark`, `notify-help-feedback`, `notify-lien-approval` (a held leader is not emailed for it either, since the Needs you card carries the notice; the email fallback is unchanged for a leader on with no device), `notify-team-lead-clock`, `remind-bid-followups` (the hours read once per run; the ledger row is written either way, so the day reminds once), `paid-job-email` (Ready to Bill: the held row carries the summary body, so no dollars sit in the history for a summary-tier role; the gear's `test_push` skips the hours), `send-workflow-notification` (the email goes now as before; the function's row reads `email` when the push was held, the helper's held row beside it), `send-checklist-notification` (the Settings test push, tag `test-notification`, skips the hours: the person pressed it to see a push), `send-scheduled-reminders` (a held person gets no email fallback for it either: the person set the time, but hours set later win), `send-report-notification`, and [`_shared/arReturnCaseNotify.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/arReturnCaseNotify.ts) (the email goes now as before), which `ar-returned-checks`, `mercury-webhook` and `stripe-webhook` bundle. Each answer gains `held`.
- **The Dashboard's line** ([`whatWaited.ts`](../../src/lib/messages/whatWaited.ts)): `heldKind` names each sender's kind from the `template_type` its held row carries (*a dispatch request*, *an estimator request*, *a marked bid*, *a help note*, *a lien notice*, *a try-out question*, *a call-again day*, *a Ready to Bill*, *a returned check*, *a work order* for the step templates, *a checklist item*, *a task reminder*, *a report*), *a push* for one not named; the article is *an* before a vowel and *day* pluralises to *days*.
- **Tests**: `pushHoursSend.test.ts` (the split with a row that does not read, the rows with the preview cut and the extra columns, the payload, the hours read fail-soft, the send over a fake service-role client: the people on pushed and recorded, a person with no device named for the fallback, `record: 'none'`, no sender, hours read once for many pushes, a person named twice decided once with a payload per person, a refused write said and not stopping the run, nothing written when everyone is on), `whatWaited.test.ts` (every kind, the article, the plural).
- **Guide**: *settings basics* → *Push notifications* says every kind of push waits the same way and the test push goes at once; *What waited while you were off* says the line names the other pushes by kind.
- **Docs**: `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` (one line in each of the fifteen sections), `MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md` (the push paragraph), `GLOSSARY.md` (the R5 line).
- **Not changed**: `messages-notify` keeps its own reads through the planner (`messagesReplyPush.ts`), since its hold hands the reply on to the office, which no other push does. No kernel changes, but the helper reads `pushHours.ts` from the mirrored copy, so the fifteen functions now bundle it: they join `IMPORTERS` in `scripts/edge-kernels.mjs` (the copy's *Imported by* header regenerates), and from now on a change to any mirrored kernel names them for redeploy too, the three payment webhooks among them.

## Is this the best we can do?

1. **Reuse**: the split is `pushDecision`, the rows are the reply push's rows one `template_type` apart, and the thirteen `web-push` loops that each pruned, logged or did neither are one sender that does both.
2. **Honest**: a held push is recorded and never sent later; the email that went beside a held push is recorded as the email it was; a summary-tier role's held row carries no dollars.
3. **Fail-soft**: the hours read as any time when the table cannot be read, a refused row is logged without stopping the run, and no VAPID keys still writes the held rows, so the Dashboard reads the same.
4. **Cut**: no second kernel, no catch-up push, no per-kind picker; one window for every push, as the card's first cut said.

## Verify

No push reaches your phone; `read_rows notification_history` shows your row with `channel = 'held'` and the sender's `template_type`; the Dashboard's Needs you card reads *While you are off: a dispatch request waits*.
