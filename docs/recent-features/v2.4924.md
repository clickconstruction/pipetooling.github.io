# v2.4924 — GC mode: what a trade partner can do from its portal, in the database (2026-10-08)

P2a of the trade partner portal's real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/PORTAL_REAL_BUILD.md`; plan `to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/portal-p2a.md`, both on branch `spike/gc-mode`). It is the SQL only. P2b brings the submit function and the portal's buttons.

- **Migration `20261008140000_gc_trade_writes`**: twelve `gc_trade_<verb>` functions and four helpers. All are service role only, `SECURITY INVOKER`, dated `public.app_today()`.
  - `got_it`, `set_lang`, `add_person`, `remove_person` and `set_gets` work on the company and its people.
  - `open_plans`, `quote_day`, `submit_quote`, `confirm_quote`, `answer_lines` and `decline` work on an ask and its quotes. Quotes are append only.
  - `ask_question` writes to `gc_plan_questions`, under the closing day.
- **The company check**: each verb takes the link's company first. `gc_trade_ask` locks the ask and refuses another company's (`notYours`), a lost bid and a pass. The award check (`awardedElsewhere`) comes with the Board's B6.
- **Refusals are keys** with plain words in `DETAIL` (decision 11). The plan doc lists the twenty keys.
- **Docs**: `docs/migrations/20261008140000_gc_trade_writes.md`, with the local run and five checks for after the push.
