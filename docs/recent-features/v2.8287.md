# v2.8287 — Job Mode: Turnaway shows once you are on the clock for a job (2026-11-22)

The owner's word of 2026-11-22: a person turns away from a job only once they are already clocked in, so the press need not be on the screen before that. No migration, no edge function.

## What was there

- The phone's press bar always drew **Turnaway**, and drew it dead whenever the card had no current job (`buildJobModePressBar`, `disabled: !hasCurrentJob`).
- The card has a current job only while the person is on the clock for one, so before **On my way** the press was a grey button that did nothing.
- The desk's grid drew the long press, **Turnaway — not ready / not home**, the same way.

## What changed

- `turnawayOffered({ sessionOpen, hasCurrentJob })` in `src/lib/jobMode/pressBar.ts` is the one rule: on the clock, with a current job.
- `buildJobModePressBar` leaves Turnaway out of the small row until then, where it put a dead button. Clocked onto a bid, the row holds **Clock out** alone.
- `DashboardJobModeCard`'s desk grid reads the same rule for its long press.
- `JobModePressBar` draws the small row only when it holds a button, so an empty row leaves no gap over **Leave report**.
- **Leave report** is as it was: always drawn, and dead with its reason without a current job.

## Not changed

- `TurnawayModal`, the report it files, the dispatch alert and the trip charge.
- The other door: **New Report** offers a turnaway for a job on today's schedule, clocked in or not (`src/content/help/turnaway-trip-charges.md`).

## Checked

- `pressBar.test.ts`: the rule's four cases, and the two rows that held a dead Turnaway now hold none.
- `DashboardJobModeCard.pressBar.render.test.tsx`: not clocked in, the press is not in the bar.
- Not seen on the live site: every state past **On my way** needs a write to reach.
