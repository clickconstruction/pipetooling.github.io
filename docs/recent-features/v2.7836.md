# v2.7836 — The Bill tab: bring a bid's stages onto a job that already exists (2026-11-18)

Punch list #397, PR 4 of the train. Client only. No migration and no edge function. Stacked on PR 3.

## Why

PR 3 offers a bid's stages when a job is opened from it. Jobs opened before that, or opened as one line, still sit on *Bid price*. The owner's direction covers them too, by the same offer and with the same care: never where replacing the line would orphan something.

## What changed

- **One link on ① Line Items**, beside Multiple Segment Generator: *Bring B482's stages*. `JobFormFixturesSection` takes it as two optional props and draws nothing without them.
- **`useBringBidStages`** decides, over `bringBidStagesStanding` from PR 2:
  - The cheap half reads the form alone: a bid, a job Waiting or Working, one priced line with a price. A job that fails it is never read.
  - A job that passes is read once: its lines as the database holds them (the crew's percent and the trip are not in the form), any checklist answer or stage window on the line, and a pay application on the job.
  - A read that fails offers nothing. The offer replaces a line, so it is never made on a guess.
  - The saved line must be the one on the form. An unsaved edit that swapped the line offers nothing until it saves.
- **When it is held, it says why and where to go**, under the title row: on a bill, a pay application, the crew's percent, a checklist answer, stage dates, a trip, a tuned budget. Money first.
- **The window in split mode** (`BidCarryWindow`, `mode: 'split'`): *Bring B482's stages onto this job?*, the line they take the place of with its price, the chips and the rows. No *As one line* and no *Start at $0*. **Keep one line** closes it. It says the job saves its lines as they change, since the Edit form saves as it goes.
- **`rowsWithBidStages`** stands the stages where the line stood, keeps every other row in place, and points a discount that named the old line at the stages.
- Each new line's description reads *B482 · Oak Ridge — 30% of the line it took the place of*.
- The guide `bring-a-bids-stages-onto-its-job` gains a section, and the Glossary's Stage kind entry a line.

The link then showed. **Bring 4 stages** was pressed, the form saved by itself, and the rows read back: the old line gone, four lines in its place, each Any time, adding up to the line's price, with the bid's hours. The link was gone after. Every table write in that walk was held to that one job.
- That walk showed one thing worth knowing. A line's labor is a share of its price, so retyping a staged line's price moves its hours with it. That is how every line budget works, not something this PR adds.
