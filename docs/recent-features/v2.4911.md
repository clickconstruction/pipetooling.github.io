# v2.4911 — People → Review: Labor contributors adds up to the job (2026-10-07)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Found on the live look for v2.4909 (punch list #46 row 8) and run down at Punchlist's ask.

## The gap

On JP1007 the Labor contributors window read *Per-person rows total $7,364.00; the job header showed $9,514.00*. The job has one sub sheet: a sheet rate of $50, no miles, and one line with a direct amount of $2,150 and no hours.

- **The header ($9,514) is right.** The job's total labor is the crew time plus each sub sheet costed by `laborJobSubCost`, the Jobs page's rule since v2.2686 (a line's direct $ counts, and a line's own rate overrides the sheet's). Net Revenue and the Jobs page use the same rule.
- **The rows dropped the line.** `buildReviewPersonAllocation` costed each sub sheet's contributor row as its hours × the sheet's rate plus the drive: 0 × $50 = $0. The $2,150 gap is exactly that line. A line with its own rate was also priced at the sheet's.

## The change

[`reviewPersonAllocation.ts`](../../src/lib/people/reviewPersonAllocation.ts): the contributor row costs its sheet with `laborJobSubCost`, the same call that builds the job's total two loops above. Sheets priced by hours at the sheet's rate cost what they did; the drive term is the same formula.

## Left for the owner

The sheet lists four names (three crew members and the sub company), and the window still shows it as one row under all four. Team Summary gives each name an equal share (`laborJobShareForPerson`), while the per-person Review panel counts the whole sheet for each crew member it lists. Which reading is right is on [`owner-decisions-pending.md`](../../to-dos/owner-decisions-pending.md); nothing here changes either.
