# v2.3517 — The Stage Plan drawer mints the GC's portal link (2026-09-16)

Client only. No migration, no edge function. Item 5 of `to-dos/stage-plan-residuals.md`, item 2 of the owner's ready-to-build train.

## Why

Edit Job → Stages → **See it as the customer** opens the drawer that draws the GC's stage card live. Its foot has one door: *Open the portal ↗* when the GC has a minted link, else *Open the sample portal ↗*. A GC who has never been given a link left the office at the sample with no way forward but closing the dialog and finding the globe on the customer. The residual asked for a *Mint the link* door right there.

## What shipped

- **One mint helper** — [`src/lib/portal/mintCustomerPortalLink.ts`](../../src/lib/portal/mintCustomerPortalLink.ts) wraps `mint_customer_portal_link` (audience `all` by default, `rotate` false, throws on an RPC error or a refusal in the payload, resolves the token). `CustomerPortalGlobeButton`'s `mint` now delegates to it, so the globe modal and the drawer cannot drift on the audience default or the error shape. Four node tests.
- **The door** — [`JobFormStagesDrawer`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormStagesDrawer.tsx) takes `gcCustomerId` and `onLinkMinted`. When the portal door is the sample **and** the job has a GC, the footer shows a dashed panel in the globe modal's own idiom: *No portal link yet* · one sentence saying the door above opens the sample and the office decides when to share · **Create their link**. Pressing it mints, flips the globe's tint (`setPortalGlobeState`), reads *Their link is live*, and calls `onLinkMinted`; a refusal shows in place and keeps the button. Nothing is created until it is pressed.
- **The wiring** — `JobFormModal` passes the job's `gcCustomerId` and `stagesGcLinks.refresh`, so the hook re-reads the GC's links and the door flips to *Open the portal ↗* without closing the drawer.
- Help guide *show a GC the stages you plan* → *See it as the customer* gains the paragraph.
- The to-do: item 5 struck; items 3–4 remain owner calls, so the to-do moves to *Needs an owner decision*.

## Verified

- Node: the helper (4). jsdom: the drawer door (3) — offered only with sample + GC, mints and reports, shows a refusal in place; the existing `JobFormStagesGroup` render smokes; `helpGuideContent`; release-notes drift; to-do drift; typecheck; lint.
- Live on prod data: Edit Job → Stages → See it as the customer on a job whose GC has no portal link shows the panel and the enabled button; on a job whose GC has a link the panel is absent and the door reads *Open the portal ↗*. The mint itself was not pressed on a real GC — the render test covers the write, and the globe modal's own flow is unchanged.
