# v2.7062 — Tasks: a map of where your tasks are, at the top of Today (2026-11-12)

Punch list #328, PR 4 of 8. The fourth step closes the loop: a place can be set (v2.7059), seen on a task (v2.7061), and now seen for every task at once.

## What changed

- **Where my tasks are** (`src/components/checklist/TaskPlacesMapCard.tsx`) is the Today tab's first block. Task Mode is the Today tab, so it is the top of Task Mode. It lists the open tasks in the order they are shown: today's, then the outstanding ones. A task done today leaves the map.
- **A numbered dot a place**, and the number is the one the task wears in the list (`taskPlaceDots`). Red is overdue.
- **Tasks at one place share a dot** (`taskPlaceDotGroups`). The dot wears the first number and a plus, *2+*. Places repeat by design, since a place used before is one press, so three tasks at one spot are the usual case and three discs on one point would hide two.
- **A press on a dot** draws a bar under the map with the place's name, **Directions**, and each task with **Show the task**, which scrolls the list to the card and opens it.
- **Tasks with no place** are counted in a line and stay in the list. With no place on any task there is no map at all.
- **Hide map** folds the block to one line, *Where my tasks are · 3 places · Show map*, and the device remembers.
- **Show where I am** asks the phone once, on that press. A dark dot shows the person, and every task's line gains its distance, *250 ft north-east*, for the visit. Nothing is saved or sent. A refusal says the map and Directions still work.
- **Satellite or Streets.** The map opens from above while the dots sit within a mile of each other, and on streets once they spread across town (`taskPlacesDefaultLayer`). The switch is on the map.
- **Sizes**: 220 tall on a phone and 300 at a desk, the Pipeline map's. On a phone the list still starts on the first screen.

The canvas is the app's shared one, `PinsMapCanvas`, through `TaskPlacesMapCanvas.tsx`, which lays the box map's imagery over its streets.

## Three things the walk found

1. **The map started twice.** The imagery layer was loaded lazily inside the map, which suspended a map already up, and Leaflet refuses to start twice in one box. The canvas and its layer are now one file and one load.
2. **The first press on a numbered dot was lost**, on a desk, on every map that uses the shared canvas with the wheel gate. The canvas re-renders on a press and made a fresh icon each time, so the disc was swapped between the press and its release. `PinsMapCanvas` now keeps one icon per look.
3. **In dark mode a view from above was inverted**, here and on the box map: the dark theme inverts map tiles, which suits a street drawing and ruins a photograph. A map showing imagery now wears `.mapImagery` on its box and keeps its tiles as they are.

Also moved on the walk: **Show where I am** sat under the map's own credit line on a phone and could not be pressed. It now sits under the zoom buttons.

## The Task Mode guide

`task-mode.md` gains one line: when an open task has a place, Today opens on the map, with a link to the guide section that shows how to use it. The line was added after v2.7050's rename of that guide had merged.

## Not built here

One task a long way from the rest is drawn and framed with the others, so the rest sit close together until a pinch. The card's plan was a line for the far one, as the Map page does for a far place. It waits until a real case shows the need.
