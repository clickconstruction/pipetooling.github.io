# v2.8116 — Lien desk: the tick for this notice stays on the card (2026-11-20)

The owner's pick of 2026-11-20, out of four ways put to him after the Fable 5.2 audit of punch list #361 (the ledger, v2.7989): pin the tick column to the right edge. Client only.

## What was wrong

Each earlier paper is a column that holds 9.5rem, and this notice is the last column. With two earlier papers the table needs about 785 px, and the card is 665 px wide in a 1,068 px window. The grid slides inside its frame then (`overflow-x: auto`), and the part past the frame's right edge was the whole *This notice* column: no tick on screen, and nothing saying there was more to the right. The ledger's own walk read live notices with one earlier paper at most, so it did not show. No live notice has more than one today. A job with a notice every month will.

## What changed

- [`src/index.css`](../../src/index.css): the head and the cells of this notice's column are `position: sticky; right: 0`, so the papers slide under the column and the tick stays. A cell is opaque under its own tint (the card's surface, the missed row's red, a ticked cell's blue), so nothing shows through.
- [`LienDeskMonths.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskMonths.tsx) reads its frame's own widths with a `ResizeObserver` and sets `data-slides="yes"` on it while the grid is wider than the frame. The column then draws a hairline at its left edge, the sign that papers are under it. A grid that fits draws no line, so a notice with no earlier paper looks as it did.
- Under 560 px of card a row is its own grid and has no column to pin, so the cells go back to `position: static`.
