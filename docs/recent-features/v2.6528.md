# v2.6528 — Archive by the row's id, and the attach door's rules in one kernel (punch list #264, PR 2) (2026-11-04)

Card #264, PR 2 of 2 (PR 1 is [v2.6527](v2.6527.md)). Two leftovers of the users-without-login train (#260): `archive-user` still looked a person up by email and name, which a person with no login may not have and two people may share; and `create-user`'s attach door was forty lines of refusals inside a function that already held two other doors.

## What changed

- **`archive-user` takes the id alone**: `user_id` is required; the lookups by `email` and `name` are gone, and an archived row answers *already archived* by its id. `email` and `name` ride along for the words in the answer. Every caller sends the id: `archiveRequestBody` (`archiveUserDialog.ts`) takes the row (`{ id, email, name }`), `archiveAccount` (`leaveWrites.ts`) takes `userId`, the End employment flow (`PersonDeskLifecycleModal.tsx`) archives by `personKey.userId` and no longer needs an address, the twins' fleet archive sends the twin's id. Redeploy after the merge.
- **The attach door's rules** live in `supabase/functions/_shared/attachLoginDoor.ts`: `attachUserIdOf`, `attachRequestRefusal` (a malformed id, two doors at once), `attachRowRefusal` (no row, a login already there, a sample or twin, an archived person), `attachDoorFields` (the body's address else the row's; the body's password else none; the row's role and name) and `attachCreateRefusal` (the auth server's taken-id words). `create-user` reads them; `src/lib/people/attachLoginDoor.test.ts` holds them. Redeploy after the merge.
- Docs: EDGE_FUNCTIONS (archive-user, create-user).
