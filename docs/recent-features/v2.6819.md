# v2.6819 — A supply house bill's row says what was read off its PDF, and Look shows the paper beside the lines (2026-11-09)

Punch list #298 PR 1b (`to-dos/supply-bill-lines/README.md`, frames B and C of its mock-up), over PR 1's tables and check kernel ([v2.6817](v2.6817.md)). PR 1 laid the ground and showed nothing; this PR is the first screen, and the first writer of a paper from the app.

## What a person sees

- **The Lines cell** on each bill of a house's list (Materials → Supply houses → the house). `rowLinesWords` in `src/lib/materials/lines/paperRows.ts`: *6 lines · adds up* in green; the gap in amber with **Look** (*5 lines · The lines are $12.40 short of the subtotal*); *6 lines · counted as read by Dana* once a person accepted it; *No lines yet* on a bill with a PDF the sweep (PR 2) has not read; *No PDF on this bill* when there is nothing to read. A kit's component lines are not counted.
- **Look** (`src/components/materials/PaperLookModal.tsx`): our copy of the PDF on the left (pdf.js, a page at a time, *Open in Drive* when the row has a link) and the lines read on the right, every number editable. The strip at the top is `gapWords`: *The lines are $12.40 short of the subtotal. Each line's own arithmetic holds, so a line is missing or one was read wrong.* A line whose own arithmetic fails is tinted and marked. Typing in the last row opens another under it. The arithmetic runs again on every keystroke (`recheck` over `checkPaper`), so the strip turns green before Save. **Save N lines** names its count (`saveLinesLabel`); it is dead while nothing changed. **Count it as read anyway** confirms with *Count it as read* and keeps the gap in the paper's note with the person's name.

## What is written, and by whom

- **At Save of a bill filled from its PDF**, the Add invoice window writes the paper and its lines beside the row (`paperSaveFromBillRead`: the reader's answer through `paperFromBillRead`, checked by `checkPaper` against the amount the person saved; `read_by` is `form:<model>`, `read_id` the `supply_bill_reads` row, `file_path` our copy). A refusal never undoes the save; the row then reads *No lines yet*. The client has no text layer, so check 5 (every code found in the paper's text) does not run on this path; PR 2's sweep runs it.
- **Migration `20261109210000_supply_paper_save.sql`** ([docs/migrations/20261109210000_supply_paper_save.md](../migrations/20261109210000_supply_paper_save.md)): PR 1's tables gave the office SELECT and every write to the service role, so two SECURITY DEFINER RPCs open the two presses a person makes: `supply_paper_save(invoice_id, paper, lines)` (one paper per bill through this door; the lines replaced whole; **the sums that decide the state are worked out in SQL**, so a client can turn an adding-up paper off but never the reverse; a person's fix writes `read_by = 'person'`) and `supply_paper_accept(paper_id, note)` (only a paper that is off; a note that says something; the person and the time stamped). Each says the gates a definer passes: office roles, never a training account, a twin or an archived caller.
- **The reads**: `src/lib/materials/lines/paperIo.ts` loads the papers for a house's bills under the SELECT policies and names who accepted. One interface, so the smoke hands the tab a fake.

## Order

The client calls the two RPCs, so `check:calls-ahead` holds the deploy until the migration is pushed. Push right after the merge; no function changes. The types learn the tables on the next `chore(types)` regeneration; until then the io names them as strings.
