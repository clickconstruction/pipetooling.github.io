# v2.6003 — Pricing: the strip names the version your prices land on (2026-10-27)

Wendi's second report of the afternoon on BP545 LAVENDER SPRINGS BLDG 2 (Tommy Gillis, the base at $150,400, the
alternate INCLUDING WATER SOFTENER at *Add $2,000* / $152,400 on the cover letter): "Changed pricing for hosebibs and
it went on alternate on the cover letter instead of base." The letter lists [4] HB1 under Inclusions, the base at
$150,400 and the alternate's *Add $2,000*.

## Which it was

She priced the hose bibs while standing on the alternate without knowing it. The video of the same afternoon
([v2.6000](v2.6000.md)) shows how she got there: the INCLUDING WATER SOFTENER card on the base's row is a door, and a
click on it moves the Workbench onto that version, whose own ★ price is also named WENDI. The $2,000 then landed on
the alternate's own prices. The base never changed: its $150,400 is the figure both versions showed in the video that
morning, and the alternate is now $152,400. The letter is honest.

Nothing leaked from the base to the alternate, and nothing can. An alternate with its own takeoff (v2.2404) is a
bid version with its own count rows and its own copies of every price (v2.2395). The letter's *Add $N* is
`formatAlternateDeltaText(alternateRevenue, baseRevenue)` in
[`coverLetterSamePage.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/coverLetterSamePage.ts): the alternate section's revenue, priced on its
own rows, minus the base's, priced on the base's rows. A base price change moves the headline and moves *Add $N* the
other way by the same amount; it never moves the alternate's own figure. So the only way for *Add $2,000* to appear
with the base unchanged is a change made on the alternate.

For the owner to send Wendi: *The hose bib price went onto INCLUDING WATER SOFTENER because that is the version you
were on. Clicking its card on the Workbench moves you onto it, and until today nothing on the page said so. Each
version keeps its own prices, so the base did not change. The letter shows the base at $150,400 and the alternate as
Add $2,000, which is what the two versions hold. Price the hose bibs again on the base if they belong there. The
strip now names the version you are on, and the saved tick says which version took each price.*

## What changed

- **A version chip on the strip** ([`BidsPricingTab.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/BidsPricingTab.tsx), the sticky
  Revenue / Profit / Margin strip, first in its row): *📐 INCLUDING WATER SOFTENER · alternate* in teal, or *Tommy
  Gillis · base* in green, on any bid with versions; nothing on an unsplit bid. The strip is the one part of the
  Workbench that stays on screen while the grid scrolls, so the name is in view while she types, where the cards
  row (and v2.6000's line over it) is not.
- **The saved tick names the version**: *saved ✓ on INCLUDING WATER SOFTENER* after a typed price on a split bid,
  *saved ✓* as before on an unsplit one. The moment a change lands is the moment the words say where.
- Kernel [`versionOnScreen.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/versionOnScreen.ts): `versionOnScreen(versions, activeId)` → the
  name and whether it is the base or an alternate, null on an unsplit bid; `savedTickWords(version)`.
- The guide [price a bid with the Workbench](../../src/content/help/price-a-bid-with-the-workbench.md), the Send to
  paragraph.

Left as it is: prices do not flow between a base and its own-takeoff alternate, by design (the alternate changes
materials and carries its own prices). Whether a same-GC alternate should take a base price change on the rows the
two share is a design call for the owner, not this fix; the chip and the tick make the landing place visible either
way.
