# v2.3595 — Email reports, Option B: one list by person (2026-09-18)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Closes [`to-dos/email-reports-one-modal/`](https://github.com/clickconstruction/pipetooling.github.io/commits/main/to-dos/email-reports-one-modal) (PR 1 = Option A, v2.3570; this is PRs 2 and 3 of the plan in one). The owner picked **B** on 2026-09-18 over keeping A.

## The shape

`EmailReportsModal` is one list: a row per person who gets report email — **Person** (name, email; *outside address · …* for a non-user) · **Digest** (a chip per schedule they are on: *Yesterday recap · jobs yesterday · all users · with costs*; a dash for an outside address, whose digest cell has no FK to hold) · **Every report** (*every report anyone files*, *reports from Darren, and everyone Abraham leads · paused*) · **Edit**. Under it *+ Add person*, the **Schedules:** line (*Yesterday recap Tue–Sat 3:00 AM · Last week recap Mon 3:00 AM · New…*), and *Preview or send a test* folded. The Dashboard door opens the same list (`initialTab` is gone).

## What shipped

- **Kernel** [`src/lib/reports/emailReportPeople.ts`](../../src/lib/reports/emailReportPeople.ts) (6 tests): `buildEmailReportPeople` folds digest recipient rows and every-report subscriptions into one row per person, sorted by name, chip text through `describeDigestScope` / `describeReportEmailSubscription`; `digestDraftsFor` and **`planDigestRowWrites`** — insert, update or delete **by row id**, only the rows that changed. The old schedule editor's save deleted and reinserted every recipient of the schedule; editing one person there rewrote everyone's rows. [`digestScheduleFields.ts`](../../src/lib/reports/digestScheduleFields.ts) (2 tests) holds the vocabulary both surfaces share (scopes, filters, the `time` bridge, weekdays, *Tue–Sat 3:00 AM*).
- **Data** — [`useEmailReportsData`](../../src/components/jobs/emailReports/useEmailReportsData.ts): roster, schedules, recipient rows, subscriptions (with authors and team leads), team-lead options, in one load; `people` is the fold.
- **The person editor** — [`EmailReportPersonEditor.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/emailReports/EmailReportPersonEditor.tsx): identity (an app user not yet listed, or an outside address); one line per schedule — on/off, scope, filter, costs; the every-report block (the same kernel the old cards saved through: `validateSubscriptionDraft`, `saveReportEmailSubscription`, `deleteReportEmailSubscription`); *Send now* (manual `send-report-email`, unchanged); *Remove* (every digest row and the subscription, after a confirm). Untick Every report and save → the subscription is deleted.
- **The schedule editor** — [`DigestScheduleEditor.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/emailReports/DigestScheduleEditor.tsx): name · enabled · days · 15-minute time, Save / Delete; never touches recipients. **Preview** — [`DigestPreviewToolbar.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/emailReports/DigestPreviewToolbar.tsx): the sandbox as it was (`recurring-job-report-preview`, `-test-send`).
- Deleted: `RecurringDigestsPanel.tsx` (1,031 lines) and `ReportEmailRecipientsPanel.tsx` (506). The modal smoke is rewritten (4 tests).
- Docs and strings: the guide *email reports to owners and managers* (the list, Add a person, Schedules, Send now, Remove); Settings → My email schedule and Email streams "managed from" strings; `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` → send-report-email; `docs/GLOSSARY.md` (the recurring-digest entries point at the Schedules line).
