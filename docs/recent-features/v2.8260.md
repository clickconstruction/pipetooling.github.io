# v2.8260 — Permits, Market: San Antonio's clock says how many applications still wait (2026-11-21)

Punch list #405 ([the card](../../to-dos/permits-market-page/README.md)), the line *San Antonio's clock reads short* on its *Seen while building, not done* list. Taunya said to build it on 2026-11-21.

## What was wrong

Market's clock in San Antonio runs from an application to its permit. It counts only applications that carry both days. An application still waiting has no second day, so it is left out, and the longest waits are the ones missing. The clock read short and did not say so. Its note said *Half of the applications wait 45 days or more*, as if it spoke of every application.

## The fix

- `applicationsWaiting` in `permitMarket.ts` counts the applications filed in the clock's twelve months, how many of them have no issued day, and how many of those were filed over 180 days ago (`LONG_WAIT_DAYS`). It reads the same list the clock reads.
- The application clock carries that count (`MarketClock.waiting`), and `clockWaitingNote` writes the line: *N of the M applications filed in twelve months still wait for a permit. The clock counts only the ones that have their permit, so it reads short. K of them were filed over six months ago. The city does not say whether a long wait is a slow review or a job that stopped.*
- The line draws under the clock's note with its three counts in place, worked out, so bold and no press.
- Nothing waits, or no clock: no line. With nobody past six months, the last two sentences are left off.
- The clock's own note now reads *Half of the applications that have a permit waited N days or more for it.*

The line claims neither a slow review nor a dead project. The city's feed cannot tell the two apart.

## What it reads

Read on prod on 2026-11-21, counts only: 1,833 commercial applications filed in the twelve months, 1,009 on the clock with a middle wait of 50 days, 824 still waiting, and 242 of those filed over six months ago.

## The count is only as fresh as the pull

Building this found that the nightly pull read an application for ten days after it was filed and never again, so one that got its permit later kept reading as waiting. [v2.8256](./v2.8256.md) gives `permit-pull` a second reading by issued day. Until that function is deployed the count runs a little high: on 2026-11-21 the city's own feed had some thirty more applications with a permit than the app held.

## Tests

- `PermitMarket.render.test.tsx`: the line under the clock with two applications waiting, its three counts drawn in place, and no line when every application has its permit.
