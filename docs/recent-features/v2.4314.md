## What changed

- **The step logic** ([`lienReleaseSteps.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienReleaseSteps.ts), tests on job 650's states): six steps — bills · form · amount · details · signed · send — each `done | now | warn | wait`. Steps 2–4 are done once the bills are picked (the app fills them) unless the amount is empty (3 is *now*), an unconditional final is early (3 *warn*) or a printed detail is blank (4 *warn*). The first step that is *now* or *warn* holds the steps after it (`waitsFor`). Step 5 is *now* once nothing above needs anything; step 6 opens when signed. A requested signature folds 1–4 to one line each; signed folds 1–5. A year still being typed does not count as a blank detail — the footer's held line and the issue-time check cover it, and counting it made step 5 vanish and reappear mid-typing (caught in the first test run).
- **The rail** ([`LienReleaseStepRow`](../../src/components/jobs/LienReleaseStepRow.tsx), `.lienStep*` in `index.css`): a dot per step (tick, blue number with a ring, amber "!", grey number), a line and an arrow to the next (green when done, blue leading into the current step), the card with the title, a state label (*Done · You are here · Needs you · Waits for step N · Opens once he signs*) and one plain sentence. A problem in step 1 folds the steps after it to their titles; a problem further down greys them in place inside a disabled `fieldset`, so nothing jumps while it is fixed. `aria-current="step"` on the current one.
- **The window** ([`LienReleaseModal`](../../src/components/jobs/LienReleaseModal.tsx)): 1200 px wide (was 880); the steps column `flex: 0 1 37rem`, the page column sticky beside it (`.lienRelease-preview`), both stacking under 900 px. Above step 1, unnumbered, **Already on this job** (the other live waivers, newest first, *Show all N*; View / Void as before; the waiver being worked on is not in it). Step 1: the bill chips and the *already waived* note (`WaiverCoveredNote` — *Waive the $X already paid instead ›* first when paid money has no unconditional, then *Open the signed one ›*, *Discard this draft*, and *Make it anyway* for when the app has it wrong). Step 2: the form toggles (moved down from the top), the cite, the why line and the *paid and not waived* note (`WaiverPaidNote`, now an outline button). Step 3: the Amount box and `WaiverMathBox` (with *It has settled, go on* on an early final). Step 4: the details as a list (`lien-waiver-details`) with **Change a detail** → the boxes (*Done* back), forced open while one is blank; the property record moved here. Step 5: the leader picker, the title (or *No title on the page · Add his title in Settings ›*, which closes the window and opens the Settings line), two big choices — **✍ He is here, he signs now** (`lien-waiver-sign-now`) and **Send it to his desk** — stacking on a narrow screen, *The record will say: drawn by X on Y’s screen*, and *Signing on paper instead? Print it then Mark issued*; while asked: *Waiting for X to sign · Asked for his signature <date>* with Sign now (when it is you) and Cancel request. Step 6: To / Attached / Sent, **Send to <GC>** (`lien-waiver-send`), Download PDF, Print and the audit sentence. The page header has *Download PDF* until signed; the foot is marked **5 · He signs here** while step 5 is current (`LienWaiverFootPreview` `highlight`) and greyed while step 1 needs a fix. The footer: autosave status, **Discard this draft / Void this waiver** (two clicks — the waiver being worked on left the history box, so its void lives here), and *You are on step N of 6 · …* / *Step N needs you*.
- Guide *send a GC our lien waiver*: new *Follow the six steps*; *Get the leader to sign*, the two notes, *Send it or download it* and *Where the waiver lives* reworded to the steps; `-window.gif` and `-signnow.gif` re-recorded (2.3 MB, 2.0 MB). GLOSSARY amended.
