# v2.3715 — Hiring, the helper try-out loop PR 3: the tally, the nudge and Keep trying on the Try-out card (2026-09-22)

PR 3 of [`to-dos/helper-tryout-loop`](../../to-dos/helper-tryout-loop/README.md) (punch list #24). PR 2 (v2.3650) collected the leaders' verdicts; this is where the office reads them. One migration.

## What the plan had wrong, twice

- **It needs a migration.** The plan sized PR 3 as kernel + card. But a Hiring-board holder cannot read `clock_sessions` (SELECT is pay-approved masters, their assistants, team leads and oneself) and cannot call `trial_helper_supervisors()` (service role only). So "days worked" and "who was asked" are one SECURITY DEFINER RPC, `team_prospect_trial_tally()`, gated inside on the board — `[]` for anyone else.
- **Keep trying was a button that did nothing.** The plan said "nothing changes; the card notes the decision was deferred and by whom". Built instead as *not yet*: it stamps `team_prospects.trial_deferred_at / _by`, the nudge goes quiet — *Keep trying — Maria Sep 20* — and comes back the moment a verdict newer than the stamp lands. It is offered only while the nudge asks a question (hire? / pass? / split); a *waiting on Mike* card has nothing to defer.

## The change

- **Migration [`20260922120000_trial_tally.sql`](../migrations/20260922120000_trial_tally.md)** — the two columns and the RPC: per card on trial, every day up to today the helper clocked, was listed on a block, or has a verdict for (`clocked`, `open`, the day's job), who the rule would have asked that day, every leader's verdict by name and role, and the Keep trying stamp with the presser's name.
- **Kernel [`src/lib/hiring/trialTally.ts`](../../src/lib/hiring/trialTally.ts)** (11 tests) — `buildTrialTally`: days worked and *listed days not clocked*; one line per leader with their **latest answer** (a skip is not an answer but keeps them listed), the note, a *sub* / *helper* tag, *not answered yet* while the answer window (today / yesterday) is open and *never answered* after it; the closed days worked with nobody who could run the job; the counts. `trialNudge`: `TRIAL_NUDGE_THRESHOLDS` = hire at 3 yes and no no, pass at 2 no, **split** when both hold, else *waiting on Mike and Luis* → *nobody who could run the job was on the block — ask Dispatch to put a master on it* → *no days yet — ask Dispatch to put them on a crew* → *1 yes, 1 no — needs another leader*. The deferral quiets a nudge only while it asks and only until a newer answer.
- **The card** ([`TeamProspectsTab.tsx`](../../src/components/prospects/TeamProspectsTab.tsx)) — under the roster line: *4 days worked · 3 leaders*, the leader lines (✓ ✗ ? by name, notes in quotes), one amber line per unled day (*Wed, Sep 16 at J258 · Oak St — no lead listed; ask Dispatch to put a master on the block*), then the nudge chip or the Keep trying stamp. **Keep trying** sits between Hire and Talked today while the nudge asks. The RPC is read with the rest of the board and fails soft: until the migration is pushed the card looks exactly as it did in v2.3627.
- Guide: `team-prospects.md` → *Trying a helper out* gains *Reading the Try-out card* and the third decision. Docs: `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` § Hiring (the tally RPC and the third gate PR 4 must join), `GLOSSARY.md` (the tally, the nudge, Keep trying), `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` (the board entry).
