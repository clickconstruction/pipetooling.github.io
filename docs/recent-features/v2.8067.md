# v2.8067 — Painted maps: a walk says Precise Location looks off even when the phone’s rough reading is an old one (2026-11-20)

Punch list #402, found by the audit of the card. The walk's line is v2.7994. Client only: no migration and no function.

## The scenario

`walkFix` (`src/lib/paintedMap/paintedWalk.ts`) dropped every reading stamped more than 10 seconds before it arrived (`WALK_STALE_FIX_MS`), and it did so before it asked whether the reading was rough. The reading's time is the phone's own stamp, `pos.timestamp`.

Apple's documents say an iPhone with Precise Location off works its rough spot out again only about four times an hour. So the one reading the browser hands the page can carry a stamp minutes old, and no other may follow for a quarter of an hour. The walk then kept no `last` reading at all. The line read *Finding you… This takes a few seconds in the open.* for as long as the person stood there: no *Precise Location looks off.*, no steps to the switch and no circle on the map. That is the phone the card was built for.

**The trigger is from Apple's documents and has not been seen on a phone.** No real iPhone has been walked with the switch off yet. The code path is certain: a rough reading with an old stamp was dropped. The first real walk on an iPhone with the switch off is still the proof, as v2.7994 says.

## What changed

`walkFix` asks whether the reading is rough first. A rough reading becomes `last` with `rough: true` whatever its age. The 10 second guard stays for every other reading.

The guard is there so that an old reading is never painted into the line. A rough reading keeps no point, so taking an old one costs nothing: it adds nothing to the stroke and does not count as a step.

## Who reads `last`

- `PaintedMapWindow` draws the person from it. A rough reading draws nobody for the 4 second wait and the whole circle after it, never a dot.
- `PaintedWalkPanel` reads its margin for the line (`walkSure`).
- `placeOnWalk` starts a place's dot on it only when `readingMovesDot` says so, which is never for a rough reading (v2.8001). The dot starts in the middle of the map.

None of them takes `last` for a fresh spot when it is rough. Nothing outside the kernel reads `state.rough`.

## Not changed

- What happens after **Pause** and **Keep painting**. The walk still keeps its last reading across a pause, as v2.7994 lists under *known*.
- A rough reading with an old stamp now replaces a newer sure one as `last`, as a rough reading with a fresh stamp always did. The next sure reading puts the person back, and the line waits 4 seconds of rough readings before it says *looks off*.

## Checked

- `paintedWalk.test.ts`: a rough reading stamped 5 minutes ago becomes `last` with `rough: true`, adds no point and makes no line (failed before the change, on `expected null to deeply equal`). A reading of 5 m, 35 m, 300 m, 1,000 m or no margin stamped 5 minutes ago is still dropped (passed before and after).
- The other tests that read the walk's rules: `PaintedMapWindow.walk.render.test.tsx`, `PaintedWalkPanel.render.test.tsx` and `PaintedMapHost.render.test.tsx`.
- `paintedWalk.ts` is not copied to the edge functions.
