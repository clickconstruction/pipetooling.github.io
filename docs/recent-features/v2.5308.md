# v2.5308 — Messages: every person you work with on GC projects, and their thread (2026-10-12)

The Messages build's cut 1 screens (wave 2), over the spine (v2.5258), its loaders (v2.5264) and the thread's link and
slots (v2.5287). No migration and no edge function. Guide `find-every-message-with-a-person-in-one-place`.

- **The Messages view** (`GcMessages.tsx`, `/gc?view=messages`, `GC_MESSAGES_HREF`): the fifth pill on GC projects, for
  all five office roles and outside the board's gate. One row per contact in three lines (who and when; their company,
  trade and whose move; the last line), newest conversation first (`listRows.ts`), filtered by All, Customers, Trades
  (with the architects) or Who is this?, searched over names, companies, numbers, addresses and every line's words, and
  folded to a rail of faces this browser keeps.
- **The read** (`messagesIo.ts`'s `loadMessagesView`, only while the view or a thread is open, again after each press):
  the spine's rows through the pure loaders, the records behind them (`Contact.customerId` and `Contact.gcCompanyId`
  are new), the facts' jobs (`factJobs.ts`: a customer's projects in three roles, a trade's asks through `gc_invites`),
  the job tiles (`jobTiles.ts`) and, as a read-side overlay, the emails the app sent to their addresses
  (`loadSentEmailsTo` in `sentCopiesIo.ts`, drawn by `sentLines.ts`; never rows in `messages`, so cut 6 backfills them).
- **The thread window** (`ThreadWindow.tsx`, `?thread=<contact id>&at=<message id>`, a full-screen sheet on a phone):
  the header in four rows (v1.166), the jobs rail (`JobsRail.tsx`: the star on the office's ties through
  `set_default_job`, a tile's filter, ⧉, × with a confirm, **+ add a job**), the lines by day with the time on the last
  line (v1.156) and a repeated job's number alone (v1.158) (`ThreadLine.tsx`), a tile dragged or held onto a line to tie
  it (`tie_message`), the message window (`MessageWindow.tsx`, v1.159), the job picker (`JobPickerWindow.tsx`) and
  **Who is this?** (`WhoIsThisWindow.tsx`, `name_contact`).
- **The slots** (`ThreadSlots`): `headerRight`, `statusRow`, `menuRows`, `aboveBox`, `box` (the reply box, cut 3),
  `underLine`, `lineBody`, `messageWindowFirst` and `side`, each drawn by the window and filled by a later cut through
  `useThreadSlots`, which the window calls itself, so a cut's hook there runs only while a thread is open. A slot that writes on its own (cut 3's send, cut 2's note) calls `useMessagesReread()` after its
  write, and the page reads the view again; with no provider it does nothing.
- **Smokes**: `GcMessages.render.test.tsx`, `ThreadWindow.render.test.tsx`, `MessageWindow.render.test.tsx` and the
  page's pill and window in `GcProjects.render.test.tsx`, all over the spine's rows (`spineMessagesView()`).
