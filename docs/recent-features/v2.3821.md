## The change

- **Kernel** (`gcOnNotice.ts`):
  - `gcNoticeFormClaim(job)`, the one function the run and the preview share since v2.3818, now returns every unnoticed month, the whole `claimAmount` (the claim set by hand when there is one) and the office's full per-month split. There is no stale note any more.
  - `GcNoticeJob` drops `timelyClaim` / `staleClaim` and keeps `timelyMonths` / `staleMonths` / `claimSplitByMonth`. `timelyMonths` still decides who gets a notice (readiness is unchanged: a job with no open window reads `no_months` and is left out).
  - `timelyClaim` and `staleNoteWords` are removed. The run no longer writes `staleNote`; a draft saved before keeps its stored text, and `saveLienDeskDraft` replaces the fields whole on the next save.
  - `summarizeGcOnNotice.claimTotal` and the sort use `claimAmount`. `lienDeskBatches` (the leader's card) keeps reading each saved draft's claim.
- **Preview** (`gcNoticePreview.ts`): the whole balance and every month, with no footnote. The side rail reads *it names every month and claims the whole unpaid balance; a closed month is named as information*.
- **Table** (`GcOnNoticeModal.tsx`, `gcOnNoticeSteps.ts`):
  - The Claim cell shows the balance with *includes Apr, Jun, Jul · windows closed*. `gcNoticeClaimTotals` drops `letterOnly`, and the total is the noticed jobs' balances.
  - The column and the total row read *named as information*.
  - Step 2's intro and Step 3's callout (*One number — the claim on the form, the job's whole unpaid balance*) are reworded, and `{{stale_note}}` is described as blank on these notices.
- **Guide**: *send lien notices from the Lien desk* → step 2 describes the whole-balance claim, with the decision and the counsel question.
