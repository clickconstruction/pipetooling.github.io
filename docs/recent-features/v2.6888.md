# v2.6888 — The Bridge's Monday email is its first screen (punch list #303, PR 5) (2026-11-10)

PR 5, the last of the Bridge's refresh under one story (`to-dos/bridge-one-story/README.md`), on the owner's yes to his call 5, 2026-11-10: the Monday email goes out as the first screen, switched on. Until now the weekly send mailed the Monday sheet, a second reading of the company that the page itself stopped showing in v2.6840, and it had never been sent. No migration. One edge function redeployed.

## What changed

- **The mail is the first screen** (`src/lib/bridge/weekMail.ts`): `WeekMail` is the page's `WeekCallModel` and `FlowStrip` with the week and what was not read. `weekMailSubject` says the call, then cash, then what the field made. `weekMailHtml` draws the three tiles (cash across the row in a week it leads), the strip of pipe as a table since a mail app draws no SVG (the pipe on one bottom line, as tall as the step is wide on the page, the block of waiting work on top, the slowest step red), the call with its moves and the person on each, the last call when one waits, and one press. `weekMailText` is the same in the same order. A proposal lists the first three moves, as the card does at a desk; a recorded call lists every move against today.
- **A mail records nothing**: the press opens the Bridge. It reads *Open the Bridge to make the call* while the page proposes, *Open the Bridge to answer* from Friday, *Open the Bridge* once a call stands.
- **Built from the page's reads** (`sheetBuild.ts`): `buildWeekMailFromReads` replaces `buildSheetFromReads`. It runs the page's chain (cash, receipts, bills, forecast, the five steps, the moves' numbers) into `buildWeekCall` and `buildFlowStrip`. Nobody is signed in on a mail, so a move the page gives to whoever is looking goes out with no person.
- **The same cash as the page** (`cashAccounts.ts`, `cashAccountsIo.ts`): the accounts the owner switched off under the cash chart are left out of the mail's cash, and the cash tile names what is left out. The pure half of `cashAccounts.ts` is copied to the edge functions; the bank read and the save moved to `cashAccountsIo.ts`.
- **The function** (`supabase/functions/bridge-weekly-sheet`): reads `bridge_cash_accounts_out_v1` with the switch and the last run, each account's name from the bank read, and the Done presses on the newest calls' moves (`parseMoveMarks`), then builds and mails the first screen. The cron, the tick, the recipients and the dry run are as they were.
- **The copy of the kernels** (`scripts/edge-kernels.mjs`): `weekMail.ts`, `weekCall.ts`, `flowStrip.ts`, `linkDesk.ts`, `callPeople.ts`, `cashAccounts.ts` and `banking/accountFacts.ts` join it; `mondaySheet.ts`, `courseModel.ts` and `truthCheck.ts` leave it.
- **The words** (`weeklySend.ts`): the switch's card says the app mails the Bridge's first screen. The page's own copy of those words in `doors.ts` is gone, so they have one home again.
- **The catalog's sample** (`weekMailSample.ts`, `teamSampleEmails.ts`, `emailCatalog.ts`): the weekly email's sample runs the mail's own builders on made-up numbers.

## Not in this PR

- `bridge-sheet-email`, the function behind the old *Email me this sheet* press, has had no caller since v2.6840. It is still deployed, and `mondaySheet.ts` stays for its catalog sample. Retiring both is its own PR.
- The mail goes to the owner and the devs, as before. A person named on a move reads it on their Needs you card, not in a mail.
- The floor, the typed cash and the account choice still save for a dev only.
