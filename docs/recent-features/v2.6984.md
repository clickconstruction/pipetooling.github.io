# v2.6984 — Permits: Austin's permits in Williamson County name their owner (2026-11-11)

Punch list #320, PR 3. Austin is filed under Travis County, and the zips of Austin over the county line are in Williamson. Those permits carry the Williamson appraisal district's quick reference id in `tcad_id` (an R and six digits) where the others carry Travis's `geo_id`. Since v2.6974 an R id was simply not asked.

## What was measured (counts only, 2026-11-11)

- Austin's feed, the 75-day window, every kind the pull keeps: 144 of 4,238 rows carry an R id (97 plumbing, 32 houses, 15 commercial), 87 ids on 113 addresses, 70 rows with no point.
- Williamson County publishes the district's parcels on its own map server (`gis.wilco.org`, `public/county_wcad_parcels`). Asked by `QuickRefID` it holds 83 of the 87 ids, each with an owner, a value, a county address and a shape. A POST of 50 ids passed its firewall.
- Through the new kernel, all 113 addresses: **none named an owner before, 112 do after** (108 by the id, 4 by the street address at Williamson), 1 still has no parcel. 7 of the 112 have no mailing address on the layer.
- The district's own page, `search.wcad.org/Property-Detail/PropertyQuickRefID/<id>`, was opened once and holds the record.

## What changed

- **A fifth source**, `WILLIAMSON_PARCELS` in `_shared/permitParcels.ts`: the address in parts (`SStreetNumber`, `SStreetDirectional`, `SStreetName`), the owner in `FullName`, the mailing address on two lines, the DBA, the three market values, the built area (`TotalSqFtLivingArea`), the acres and the use code. The layer has no year built. Its id column is `QuickRefID`.
- **The county by the id**: `ParcelIdColumn.ask` (it was `askable`) hands back the id as the layer keeps it, or null when the id is not the layer's own. Travis turns away an R id; Williamson takes an R and digits and upper-cases it. `sourceForId(home, id)` picks the city's own county when it takes the id, else the first neighbour that does. `NEIGHBOURS` gains Travis → Williamson, Hays and Williamson → Travis.
- **`permit-lookup`**: the id is read off the permit once, the county is picked from it, and the id, the address, the point and then the neighbours are all asked of that county. `factsInCounty` names the county on the facts whenever the layer that answered is not the city's own, and `parcelFactsAtNeighbour` is gone. A kept "no parcel" that asked Travis is asked again, because its address question is no longer today's.
- **Words**: found by the id or the address on a neighbouring county's layer, the sheets say *This lot is in Williamson County.* and the skim card's lot line ends *in Williamson County*. Read at the point, the lines of v2.6980 stand. The guide gained two sentences.

Not built here: a map point for the R rows the city sends without one (68 of 70 would take one from Williamson's shape). That is `permit-pull`, and it is the next PR.

Tests: `permitParcels.test.ts` (the R id, the county picked, Williamson's question, pick and row, the county named), `permitCityDoors.test.ts`. Every id, name and number is made up.

Deploy: `permit-lookup` after the merge, from `main`. No migration.
