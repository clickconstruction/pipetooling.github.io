# v2.3691 — Balances: the doors on residue, overpaid and off-report charges (2026-09-21)

PR 2 of the Balances redesign ([v2.3689](v2.3689.md) named the states; this PR gives each one its action). Client only, no migration — every write is one the app already makes from the Less modal or the payment table.

## What shipped

- **Kernel [`openReports.ts`](../../src/lib/people/openReports.ts)** (+2 tests): `residueSettlementDeduction(row)` — the Less line that closes a residue week (`amount` = what is short, description *Settled · residue (fees or rounding)*); a deduction rather than a paid-note so the report's net then equals what was paid, the journal balance drops the cents, and removing the line reopens the week. `moveOverpaymentWords(plan, week, money)` — the confirm sentence for a move plan, with the no-target variant ("… nothing is open to move it to … file the $20.00 as a credit?").
- **View [`PeoplePayLedgerView.tsx`](../../src/components/people/PeoplePayLedgerView.tsx)** — three doors, each behind the app's confirm dialog:
  - **Mark settled** on a residue row (and *Mark N weeks settled* on the settle line when residue is all that is open): inserts the manual `pay_stub_deductions` line(s) stamped `created_by`, then reloads the stubs.
  - **Move extra** on an overpaid row: runs `moveOverpaymentPlan`'s ops — `pay_stub_payments` updates / deletes on the overpaid week, one insert on the oldest open week (same `paid_at`, memo suffixed *moved from week of …*). With nothing open it deletes/trims the same and opens the offset form pre-filled as an employee credit for the extra.
  - **Take out of a week…** on an off-report charge (and *Take charges out of the open weeks…* on the settle line in charges mode): a chip per open week (*8/23–29 · $762.37 left*) opens the existing [`PayStubLessModal`](../../src/components/pay/PayStubLessModal.tsx) for that report, whose *Apply pending offset* list already holds this person's off-report charges (and enforces the net ≥ 0 / payments ≤ net guards). Saving reloads stubs and offsets, so the charge leaves the strip and lands under its week.
  - New props from [`People.tsx`](../../src/pages/People.tsx): `authUserId`, `showToast`.
- Help guide `see-what-we-owe-each-person` gains the three doors; GLOSSARY's Balances entry names them.

## Verified

typecheck · lint · `npm test` · theme check. Dev server, prod data, no writes made: William's two residue weeks show *Mark settled*; Tristen's four charges show *Take out of a week…* and the settle line's *Take charges out of the open weeks…*, both opening the two-chip week picker; picking a week opens the Less modal for that report with the four charges listed under *Apply pending offset*. Mark settled and Move extra were exercised only to their confirm dialogs (cancelled) — the writes are the Less modal's manual-deduction insert and the payment table's update/delete/insert, both in daily use.
