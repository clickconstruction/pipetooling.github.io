# v2.4150 — Contract wording: a statutory sentence prints at 10 pt (2026-09-29)

Client plus two edge functions (`sign-job-contract`, `share-job-contract` — the shared PDF builder; deploy after merge). No migration. The owner, 2026-09-29, after the Contract Book's service agreement was rewritten (fourteen, then twelve clauses) and the § 53.256 waiver was read against the statute: a mockup of the waiver at 10 pt, then *remove the bold*, then *commit push merge it*.

## Why

Tex. Prop. Code § 53.256 lets an owner waive the list of subcontractors and suppliers only in writing "conspicuously printed in at least 10-point bold-faced type". The agreement's terms print at 12.5 px (9.4 pt) in the print template and at 9 pt in the pdf-lib PDF the signer receives; a plain Book document could not mark one sentence at all. The owner chose the size and left the bold out.

## What shipped

- **[`renderContractBodyToSafeHtml.ts`](../../src/lib/renderContractBodyToSafeHtml.ts)** — `STATUTORY_SENTENCE_RE` and `statutorySpans`: in a plain body, after escaping, each `**…**` becomes `<span class="statutory" style="font-size:max(10pt, 1em)">` — regular weight, the asterisks dropped, a lone `**` left as typed. The only markup a plain body carries. Every consumer of a plain body gets it: the signing page, the record and sweep modals, the Contract Book preview and the print template.
- **[`_shared/jobContractPdf.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/jobContractPdf.ts)** — `TERMS_SIZE` (9) and `STATUTORY_SIZE` (10), `parseStatutoryRuns` (a paragraph → runs), `wrapRuns` (greedy wrap of sized words, the space before a word measured at the size of the word before it); the terms are drawn paragraph by paragraph, a paragraph with a token word by word on one baseline. `contractBodyToPlainText` keeps a plain body's tokens for the builder.
- **[`SettingsContractsTab`](../../src/components/settings/SettingsContractsTab.tsx)** — a plain body renders through `ContractBodyDisplay` too, so the card reads as the customer sees it.
- **Guide** `see-every-contract-side-by-side` → *Change the wording*; `docs/GLOSSARY.md` → *Contracts & terms*; `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` → the two functions.

## Decided here

- **Size, not weight.** The statute names bold too; the owner asked for the bold to go. The span and the PDF run are regular weight.
- **A token, not a format switch.** Switching the Book document to markdown would turn its numbered paragraphs into a list and change how every clause prints; one token in a plain body changes one sentence.
- **`max(10pt, 1em)`** so the sentence never prints smaller than its neighbours where the terms already run above 10 pt (the signing page's 13.6 px).
