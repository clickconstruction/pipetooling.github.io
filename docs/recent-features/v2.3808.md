# v2.3808 — Looking back at an old week no longer fills it with Office blocks (2026-09-24)

Found live 2026-09-24: opening `/schedule-dispatch?week=2026-04-26` as a dev created eleven Office-job blocks (8–4) for the three roster members across Apr 27 – May 1 2026, each stamped `created_by` = the viewer, with tombstones in `dispatch_office_schedule_fills`. The hub's ensure-on-view (v2.1812) called `ensure_office_schedule_blocks(weekStart, weekEnd)` for whatever week was on screen, and the RPC (v2.1810) had no notion of today. The blocks were deleted by hand within the minute (their `removed` rows sit in `schedule_block_events`); the tombstones were left so the week does not refill. Any past week without tombstones would have done the same the next time someone looked back.

## The change

- **Migration [`20260924100000_office_schedule_no_past_fill.sql`](../../supabase/migrations/20260924100000_office_schedule_no_past_fill.sql)** — `ensure_office_schedule_blocks` reads `v_today` as the Chicago date once, returns `{ok: true, created: 0}` when `p_to < v_today`, and otherwise loops from `GREATEST(p_from, v_today)`. Everything else (weekdays only, time off wins, overlap wins, tombstones) is unchanged. This is the guard that holds for every caller. Deploy with `supabase db push` after merge; no client ordering constraint (the old client's wider range is simply clamped).
- **[`lib/dispatchOfficeRoster.ts`](../../src/lib/dispatchOfficeRoster.ts)** — `clampOfficeEnsureRange(from, to, today)`: `null` for a window ending before today (and for an empty or inverted one), otherwise `{from: max(from, today), to}`. Six cases in `dispatchOfficeRoster.test.ts`.
- **[`ScheduleDispatchHubPage`](../../src/components/schedule/ScheduleDispatchHubPage.tsx) `runOfficeEnsure`** — clamps with `todayYmdInAppTz()` before the RPC; a wholly past week makes no call at all, and the once-per-range key is the clamped range, so the current week re-runs from a fresh "today" after midnight without a remount doing anything different.
- [`SCHEDULE_DISPATCH_ARCHITECTURE.md`](../SCHEDULE_DISPATCH_ARCHITECTURE.md) gains the standing-office ensure line under the `loadHub` section.

## Decisions taken

- **Both sides clamp.** The RPC guard is the one that cannot be bypassed (a stale tab, a future caller, the twin); the client clamp saves the round trip and keeps the "created > 0 → quiet reload" path from ever firing on a past week.
- **Today counts as fillable.** A block for today at 8–4 is the crew's working day; the clamp is `< today`, not `<= today`.
- **No cleanup migration.** Retro-filled blocks from before this fix are left in place and listed for the owner rather than deleted — some may be wanted (a real office day recorded late), and a block on a past day feeds clock attribution and overhead reporting.
