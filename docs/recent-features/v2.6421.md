# v2.6421 — The Bridge fits on a phone (2026-11-02)

Fix, no migration, no edge function. v2.6137's run of Settings → What everyone sees (punch list #194) found it on 2026-10-30 and left it: as the dev, The Bridge's Phone 375 frames wore *Scrolls sideways: 424 px in 375* in both themes, with tablet and desk clean. Checked against `origin/main` on 2026-11-02, nothing since had touched it.

## Cause

Three things, each measured from the frame's own document in the lens and on the dev server as the dev at 375 × 812 on real data.

- **The Dial's row** (`BridgeDial.tsx`) was a grid of `minmax(0, 1fr) auto 240px` with 1.6 rem gaps. The number's column is about 170 px (*$43.2k/wk* over a no-wrap unit line), so the number, the 240 px spark and the two gaps needed 436 px of a 330 px panel. The slider's column collapsed to 0 (every mark label stacked on one point at the left edge) and the spark ran from 185 to 425 px, the document's width.
- **Four panels** (*Everything else waits*, *Last week's call*, *This week*, *Fix the reading*) sat in `repeat(auto-fit, minmax(380px, 1fr))` grids on the page. A track's floor is a floor, so in the 359 px column each panel drew 380 wide and ended at 388.
- **A Fix-the-reading row on real data**: its chip *5 jobs worked this window with no % complete* is 294 px and was `nowrap` in a no-wrap row, so the words beside it shrank to 0 px and *Open the jobs* ended at 420. The lens's invented company has no such gap, so the frame does not show this one; the live page did.

## Fix

- **The Dial's row wraps**: a flex row, the slider `flex: 1 1 220px`, the number `0 0 auto` and the spark `0 1 240px` with `maxWidth: 100%`, both hugging the right with an auto left margin. At 768 and 1280 the row draws as before (the slider grows, then the number, then the 240 px spark), since a growing item takes the free space before an auto margin does. At 375 the slider takes the whole row (328 px), and the number and the spark drop under it, each at the right.
- **The page's four auto-fit grids** take `minmax(min(N px, 100%), 1fr)`, the idiom seven other grids in the repo already use, so a track never exceeds its column: the two 380s, the 260 and the 300 (the 300 only mattered under 332 px).
- **The Fix-the-reading row wraps**, its words take `flex: 1 1 8rem`, and the chip loses `nowrap` for `maxWidth: 100%`. A row whose chip, words and door fit on one line draws as before; a chip too long for the line takes it alone, and the words and the door drop under it. A chip wider than the whole row (under about 320 px) wraps its words inside the pill instead of pushing the page.

Nothing the page says changed.

## Tests

- `BridgeDial.render.test.tsx` (+1) and `BridgeReadingGaps.render.test.tsx` (+1): jsdom lays nothing out, so each pins the rule — the row's `flex-wrap`, the slider's and the words' flex bases, the spark's `maxWidth`, the chip with no `nowrap`.
- `e2e/viewport-smoke.spec.ts`: `/bridge` joins the 375 px no-sideways-overflow pages, settling on a quiet network since its words show before its reads land. `docs/E2E_SMOKE.md` lists it. The suite runs after each deploy as the dev test user and does not gate PRs.

## Found, left as it was

- **The Dial's mark labels overlap each other at 375.** The stagger drops a label to a second row only when it sits within 16 points of the one before; at 328 px that is 52 px, while *above here Collect is the link* is about 190 px. They are HTML text, so the lens's overlap check (an SVG drawing's words) does not list them. Before this PR they all sat on one point.
- **This week's rows do not wrap.** Nothing of theirs overflows today; a lever longer than the line would, the way the gap row's chip did.
- **The desks that open on a press** (Collect, Market, Sales, Build, Bill) and the Owed, Hours and Profit panels were not measured at 375; the lens draws the page as it lands.
