# v2.5920 — Legal desk: answer a gap, not only fix it (2026-10-26)

The owner's note of 2026-10-26, after the Burd-walk batch retired (`to-dos/legal-gap-answers/`, mock-up beside it): *"what if work was done without a contract, there is no way to list it or fill it in or make sure that was noted in the narrative."* Every row under *Before this goes to an attorney* was a computed nag whose only control was a door to the surface that would change the fact; a fact that is true and known — the work ran on a handshake, there is no paper to record — had nowhere to live, and the desk ignored the app's own *contract not needed* answer. #166's *Ours with the owner* was the one gap that could be answered; this generalizes it.

- **Migration `20261026210000_legal_gap_answers.sql`**: `legal_matter_gap_answers` — one row per (matter, gap key): the answer (≤ 500), an optional Evidence document on the same matter, who and when. Office-select RLS; writes only through `legal_answer_gap` (the `legal_set_in_house` ensure — the matter is made when the account has none, refused once closed, jobs only added; answering never moves the stage) and `legal_clear_gap_answer`. New table, so the three fences close it (`apply_read_only_write_blocks` / `apply_read_only_stmt_blocks` / `apply_digital_twin_write_blocks`).
- **Kernel** `src/lib/legal/legalGapAnswers.ts`: `gapAnswersByKey` (stored rows win over auto ones), `autoContractGapAnswer` (a job's *contract not needed* mark answers `contract:<jobId>` by itself, worded from its reason and day), `gapAnswerCounts` (every stop is still a stop; an answered warn leaves the to-know-about count; `answered` counts every answer, red ones included), `gapAnswerByline`. `buildLegalPacket` takes `gapAnswers`, hangs each answer on its gap (`LegalGap.answer`) and `readiness` gains `answered`.
- **The desk** (`LegalDeskModal.tsx`, data in `useLegalPacketData.ts`): the count line reads *1 to fix · 4 to know about · 3 answered*; **Answer…** on every gap row opens an inline sheet (the one line, a picker of the matter's Evidence documents); an answered note sits under **Answered — out of the counts, still on the record** with the office's line, the document one press away (it opens Evidence), who and when, **Edit** and **Clear**; a red gap stays in place with the answer riding under it and the sheet says so. An auto answer carries no Clear — *read from the job's contract strip · nothing to retype*.
- **Not built yet** (PR 2, stacked): the carry to the firm — the portal and the printed packet showing each answer beside its gap, and **Insert the answers** in the narrative editor beside *Insert the events*.
