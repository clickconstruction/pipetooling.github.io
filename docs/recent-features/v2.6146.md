# v2.6146 — Your record's items as a week strip (punch list #217, field-8, a prototype) (2026-10-30)

Punch list #217, *Show it, don't say it*: eight scans found 131 places where the app tells instead of shows, and a mock-up page draws each as a before/after pair for the owner to pick from. On 2026-10-30 she asked to see field-8 and field-11 "as prototypes, live, side by side with how each card looks now". This is field-8, shipped as a draft PR for her to look at; field-11 is its own PR. No migration, no edge function.

## The finding

The Dashboard's **Your record** card read *No report on 1318 Sample Retail Shell rough-in · You worked it Thursday (8.5h this week); no field report from you yet*. The 8.5 h and *Thursday* are sums over sessions the card never draws; today's open session counts 0 h, so a tech on the job right now reads as if they were not; and *no field report from you yet* is a week rule (one report covers the job for the pay week) worded as if it were about Thursday.

## What changed

- **The rule for every prototype in this program.** The shape sits behind a per-user switch on its card, remembered on the device under `shape_view_<key>_<userId>` and off by default, so nothing changes until a person turns it on. A dev who opens the page with `?sbs=1` sees today's look and the shape one above the other, labelled *Today* and *With the strip*, on the same live read. The helper is `src/components/prototypes/useShapeView.ts` (`useShapeView(key)` → `{ on, toggle, sideBySide }`, through `useOptionalAuth` so a card outside the auth provider still mounts) and `SideBySide.tsx` (`SideBySide`, `ShapeSwitch`). The rhythm-strips prototype (`claude/217-rhythm-strips…`) writes the same helper under the same names; its branch was not pushed when this one was cut, so the two copies fold together when either merges.
- **`yourRecordWeekStrip.ts`** (`src/lib/`, new, beside `dashboardYourRecord.ts`): the pure kernel. `buildYourRecordWeekStrip` takes the item, the pay week, the instant and the person's sessions and report days, keeps the item's own sessions (the job's, or the clock-open item's own session), and gives seven days, Sunday to Saturday: the closed hours on the job, today's open session's hours so far (`clockedInAt`, a new optional field on `YourRecordSession`), the bar's share of the week's tallest day (never under eight percent when there is something), a report flag, the hours label under a worked day (`8.5h`, `12h`), each day's words for the hover, the week rule as the line under the strip (`lineOf`: *no report from you this pay week*, *nobody has said how far along it is*, *still on the clock from Wednesday*) and the whole strip as one sentence. Seven tests: a closed day, today open, a report day, a week with none, a past-day open clock, the labels, the hours words.
- **`YourRecordWeekStrip.tsx`** (`src/components/dashboard/`, new): the picture. Seven columns in the Legal Timeline strip's shape: a bar in `--timeline-job-4`, hatched for an open session, the report dot in `--text-link` over the day, the hours under a worked day, the day letters under all seven, today's column on a `--bg-blue-tint` wash with its letter in colour. `role="img"` with the strip's sentence as its label and hover; each day's words on its own hover.
- **`DashboardYourRecordCard`**: the head carries the **Week strip** switch (`ShapeSwitch`); on, each item draws the strip and the line in place of the summed sentence, the press unchanged at the end. The read now keeps the sessions and the report days by job (`reports.created_at` → the app's calendar day) beside the items, so the strip and the words come from one read. `data-your-record-shape` is `words`, `strip` or `side-by-side`.
- **Smoke** `DashboardYourRecordCard.render.test.tsx` (new): off by default; on, the strip with today worked and open, the line, the device remembering; a report day's dot; the dev's side by side and a helper's lack of one.
- **Guide** *keep my record straight*: a section on the switch.
- **Docs**: the card's line in `DASHBOARD_SECTIONS_ARCHITECTURE.md`.

## How to open the side by side

On a dev server from this branch, sign in as the dev (`/dev-login?as=1&to=%2Fdashboard%3Fsbs%3D1`) and open `/dashboard?sbs=1`. The card draws only while the dev's own record has an item this pay week; to see it as a helper, use Settings → View as, which has no side by side (the dev check reads the signed-in role), so press the **Week strip** switch there instead.

## What the mock-up asked for that this does not do

- **A hover card with today's hours** over the open bar. The strip uses the browser's hover (`title`) on each day, as the Legal Timeline strip does; today's hours so far are in that hover and in the strip's sentence, not drawn on the bar.
- **The day name under the worked bar** (*Thu · 8.5 h*). On a phone the seven columns are about 37 px wide, so the label is the hours alone and the day's letter sits under every column; two worked neighbours then never overprint.
- **A tick for the current hour on today's bar.** Today's open bar grows with the hours so far at each render (the card re-reads on focus); there is no clock on the card.
