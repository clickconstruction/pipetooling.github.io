# v2.6115 — Banking → Cost batches: the Move cost screen and the batch list with Revert (devs) (2026-10-29)

The owner asked on 2026-10-29 to sort a catch-all job's rows into the right jobs and said the office may need a tool to do so. Until now moving cost between jobs meant an agent hand-building the `cost_batch_apply` payload (`docs/COST_BATCHES.md`, v2.3196): a JSON file, a psql or MCP call, and the two audit tables read by SQL. The catch-all is still live and receiving rows, so the one-off sort will not be the last. This is the screen the contract doc listed under "Not yet built", in Banking, where it said the natural home was.

## What a dev does

Banking → **Cost batches** (`/banking?product=mercury&tab=cost_batches`, the strip's last tab, devs only; `&job=<jobs_ledger.id>` opens it on a job, and the job window's Costs tab has a **Move cost ▸** door that does). Pick the job whose rows are moving — the app's job search — and every cost row on it is read by source: bank allocations (posting day, counterparty, card nickname through `mercury_debit_card_nicknames`, bank category, the transaction and this job's slice, the allocation note), supply-house invoice allocations (house, invoice number, date, PO, amount, share, this job's dollars, paid), clock sessions grouped by person (work date, hours, approved · waiting · rejected), Other job charges (description, amount). Totals per source in each heading and one line over the tables. Tick rows one by one, or by source, by card (the cards present, most rows first; "No card" for ACH, wire and check), or by a date range; untick in the same places. Pick the job they move to, write the reason (kept on the batch and in both notes), the plan's name (`source_ref`) and the note each moved allocation carries (`Move cost <date>` by default). The plan appears as it builds: the label, the ops by kind, the totals by job the way the RPC will report them, what the source job releases, the rows that stay with the reason, and the two notes as they will read. **Dry-run N ops** calls `cost_batch_apply(payload, true)` — every write performed and unwound — and the screen lays the reply's `by_job` beside the plan's own `summarizeCostBatchOps`, every job and field, to the cent; a disagreement is red with its sentence. **Apply N ops** is dead until a dry run of this exact plan matches and the dev ticks *I read the dry run's summary*; any change to the plan puts it back to sleep. On the press the dry run runs once more and the write happens only when it still matches; the batch id is shown with a link to its card, and the rows and the list are read again. Under it, **Batches that touched this job** and **Every batch** (newest 200): label, applied / reverted, when, author, op count, reason, plan, the summary across jobs and by job, **Show N ops** (each op in one line), and **Revert…**, which asks for the word `revert` and a reason before `cost_batch_revert(batch_id, reason)`.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/costBatches/costMoveRows.ts`** (new, pure): the four row shapes, `costMoveRowKey`, `costMoveHold` (an Other charge is never movable — the RPC has no op for it; a rejected session and bid time stay), `costMoveRowDollars` / `costMoveTotals`, the selection helpers (`costMoveKeysBySource`, `costMoveKeysByCard`, `costMoveKeysByDateRange`, `costMoveCardsPresent`, `groupClockRowsByPerson`) and `buildCostMoveOps`: a bank row becomes `allocate` of its slice, positive, with `from_job_id` = the job it is on; an invoice `supply_repoint`; a session `clock_repoint`; a held row is returned under `skipped` with its reason, never dropped.
- **`costMovePlan.ts`** (new, pure): the note bodies in the house form — `<date> — cost batch: N rows moved here from <job> — 381 bank charges ($31,804.23), 10 supply-house invoices and 81 clock sessions. Reason: … Plan: … No estimates in this batch.` on the target, the mirror on the source — the label `Move cost: <source> → <target> (<date>)`, and `buildCostMovePlan`, which hands the whole batch to `buildCostBatchPayload` / `validateCostBatchPayload` and says in words why there is no payload (same job both ways, nothing movable ticked, no reason).
- **`costBatchDryRunCompare.ts`** (new, pure): `parseCostBatchApplyResult` types the RPC's reply; `compareDryRunToPlan` compares every job and field the plan or the dry run names, dollars to the cent and counts exactly, and the op counts; `costBatchPlanSignature` is the fingerprint the screen keeps from the dry run to the apply.
- **`costBatchList.ts`** (new, pure): `cost_batches` and `cost_batch_ops` rows typed, `costBatchOpJobIds` / `costBatchOpTouchesJob` (the target's `job_id` / `to_job_id` / `from_job_id`, a session's `job_ledger_id` before and after, an allocation's job after and the one it replaced), the summary words and per-job lines, `costBatchOpWords`, the typed revert word and `costBatchRevertReady`.
- **`costMoveReads.ts`** (new): the browser's reads as the signed-in dev — the job, the four sources (paged), the ops touching a job (one `or` filter over the jsonb paths, re-checked by the kernel), the lists, the ops of one batch — and the RPC calls: a dry run retried like a read, the apply and the revert sent once (a retry after a lost reply could write twice). **`moveCostHref.ts`**: the screen's address for a job.
- **`src/components/banking/BankingCostBatchesTab.tsx`** (new; zero props, own reads, in the Visuals mold), **`CostBatchJobPicker.tsx`** (`search_jobs_ledger`, billing-only jobs included), **`CostMoveRowsTables.tsx`**, **`CostBatchListPanel.tsx`**. **`src/pages/Banking.tsx`**: the `cost_batches` tab (dev-only in the strip, in the parser's staff fallback and at the panel; the dispatcher skips the master fetch; the nicknames window reads its counts the Card Review way). **`src/components/jobs/JobWindowMoveCostDoor.tsx`** on the job window's tab strip while the Costs tab is up (`JobWindowModal.tsx`, one line).
- **Tests**: `costMoveRows.test.ts` (14), `costMovePlan.test.ts` (9), `costBatchDryRunCompare.test.ts` (6), `costBatchList.test.ts` (7), `BankingCostBatchesTab.render.test.tsx` (the search, the four sources, the holds, the plan's words, a matching dry run, Apply asleep until the summary is read, the batch card's Revert behind the word), `JobWindowMoveCostDoor.render.test.tsx` (the dev's press, the office sees no door).
- **Docs**: `COST_BATCHES.md` ("Not yet built" → the screen), `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` (the Banking row and the Cost batches section), `GLOSSARY.md` (Cost batch), `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` §15, `BANKING_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md` (the tab's row), `twins/APP_DIRECTORY.md` (`/banking`).

No migration, no edge function: the two RPCs and their gates are v2.3196's. A dev in training mode is refused by the RPCs as before.
