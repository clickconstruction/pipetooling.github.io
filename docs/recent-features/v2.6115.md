# v2.6115 — Job window → Costs: every cost row on the job, the Move cost window, and the batch list with Revert (devs) (2026-10-29)

The owner asked on 2026-10-29 to sort a catch-all job's rows into the right jobs and said the office may need a tool to do so; on 2026-10-30 he gave its shape: "see all of the costs for a job and be able to send the cost on one job to another job in an easy way, where perhaps a user clicks on something and a modal appears." Until now moving cost between jobs meant an agent hand-building the `cost_batch_apply` payload (`docs/COST_BATCHES.md`, v2.3196): a JSON file, a psql or MCP call, and the two audit tables read by SQL. The catch-all is still live and receiving rows, so the one-off sort will not be the last. This is the contract doc's "Not yet built" screen, in the owner's shape: the job's own cost view, a click, a window.

## What a dev does

Open a job's window → **Costs**. Under the cost region a dev sees **Every cost row on this job**, by stream: bank allocations (posting day, counterparty, card nickname through `mercury_debit_card_nicknames`, bank category, the transaction and this job's slice, the allocation note), supply-house invoice allocations (house, invoice number, date, PO, amount, share, this job's dollars, paid), clock sessions grouped by person (work date, hours, approved · waiting · rejected), Other job charges (description, amount). Totals per stream in each heading and one line over the tables. A click anywhere on a movable row ticks it, as does its box; a press ticks a whole stream or one person's sessions; tick by card (the cards present, most rows first; "No card" for ACH, wire and check) or by a date range. The bar under the list stays in view on a long list (a catch-all job carries several hundred rows) and says what is ticked. **Move N ticked rows…** opens the **Move cost** window on those rows: pick the job they move to — the app's job search — write the reason (kept on the batch and in both notes), the plan's name (`source_ref`) and the note each moved allocation carries (`Move cost <date>` by default). The plan appears as it builds: the label, the ops by kind, the totals by job the way the RPC will report them, what the source job releases, the rows that stay with the reason, and the two notes as they will read. **Dry-run N ops** calls `cost_batch_apply(payload, true)` — every write performed and unwound — and the window lays the reply's `by_job` beside the plan's own `summarizeCostBatchOps`, every job and field, to the cent; a disagreement is red with its sentence. **Apply N ops** is dead until a dry run of this exact plan matches and the dev ticks *I read the dry run's summary*; any change to the plan puts it back to sleep. On the press the dry run runs once more and the write happens only when it still matches; the window says the batch id, and behind it the job's rows and its batch list are read again. Escape and the window's × close it; the ticks survive. Under the list, **Batches that touched this job**: label, applied / reverted, when, author, op count, reason, plan, the summary across jobs and by job, **Show N ops** (each op in one line), and **Revert…**, which asks for the word `revert` and a reason before `cost_batch_revert(batch_id, reason)`. Banking → **Cost batches** (`/banking?product=mercury&tab=cost_batches`, the strip's last tab, devs only) is the company-wide audit: every batch, newest 200, with the same cards and Revert; `&job=<id>` or the picker adds the batches that touched one job.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/costBatches/costMoveRows.ts`** (new, pure): the four row shapes, `costMoveRowKey`, `costMoveHold` (an Other charge is never movable — the RPC has no op for it; a rejected session and bid time stay), `costMoveRowDollars` / `costMoveTotals`, the selection helpers (`costMoveKeysBySource`, `costMoveKeysByCard`, `costMoveKeysByDateRange`, `costMoveCardsPresent`, `groupClockRowsByPerson`) and `buildCostMoveOps`: a bank row becomes `allocate` of its slice, positive, with `from_job_id` = the job it is on; an invoice `supply_repoint`; a session `clock_repoint`; a held row is returned under `skipped` with its reason, never dropped.
- **`costMovePlan.ts`** (new, pure): the note bodies in the house form — `<date> — cost batch: N rows moved here from <job> — 381 bank charges ($31,804.23), 10 supply-house invoices and 81 clock sessions. Reason: … Plan: … No estimates in this batch.` on the target, the mirror on the source — the label `Move cost: <source> → <target> (<date>)`, and `buildCostMovePlan`, which hands the whole batch to `buildCostBatchPayload` / `validateCostBatchPayload` and says in words why there is no payload (same job both ways, nothing movable ticked, no reason).
- **`costBatchDryRunCompare.ts`** (new, pure): `parseCostBatchApplyResult` types the RPC's reply; `compareDryRunToPlan` compares every job and field the plan or the dry run names, dollars to the cent and counts exactly, and the op counts; `costBatchPlanSignature` is the fingerprint the window keeps from the dry run to the apply.
- **`costBatchList.ts`** (new, pure): `cost_batches` and `cost_batch_ops` rows typed, `costBatchOpJobIds` / `costBatchOpTouchesJob` (the target's `job_id` / `to_job_id` / `from_job_id`, a session's `job_ledger_id` before and after, an allocation's job after and the one it replaced), the summary words and per-job lines, `costBatchOpWords`, the typed revert word and `costBatchRevertReady`.
- **`costMoveReads.ts`** (new): the browser's reads as the signed-in dev — the job, the four streams (paged), the ops touching a job (one `or` filter over the jsonb paths, re-checked by the kernel), the lists, the ops of one batch — and the RPC calls: a dry run retried like a read, the apply and the revert sent once (a retry after a lost reply could write twice).
- **`src/components/jobs/JobWindowCostMovePanel.tsx`** (new): the Costs tab's list for a dev — the reads, the ticks and the helpers, `CostMoveRowsTables`, the sticky move bar, the Move cost window and `CostBatchListPanel` for this job; it tells the window when its own window is open so Escape belongs to the window on top (`JobWindowModal.tsx`: `costMoveMounted`, `costMoveEscBlocked`, the panel under the Costs region's form). **`src/components/costBatches/`** (new folder): **`CostMoveModal.tsx`** (`ResponsiveModalShell` at z 1100 over the job window's 1010: the target, the reason, the plan, Dry-run → compare → Apply, the result and Done), **`CostMoveRowsTables.tsx`** (a click anywhere on a movable row ticks it), **`CostBatchJobPicker.tsx`** (`search_jobs_ledger`, billing-only jobs included), **`CostBatchListPanel.tsx`** (`showEveryBatch`: the job's batches alone in the window, every batch in Banking). **`src/components/banking/BankingCostBatchesTab.tsx`**: the company-wide list with a job filter; **`src/pages/Banking.tsx`**: the `cost_batches` tab (dev-only in the strip, in the parser's staff fallback and at the panel; the dispatcher skips the master fetch; the nicknames window reads its counts the Card Review way).
- **Tests**: `costMoveRows.test.ts` (14), `costMovePlan.test.ts` (9), `costBatchDryRunCompare.test.ts` (6), `costBatchList.test.ts` (7), `CostMoveModal.render.test.tsx` (the target through the search, the plan's words, a matching dry run, Apply asleep until the summary is read, the apply's second dry run then the write, the result and Done), `JobWindowCostMovePanel.render.test.tsx` (the four streams, the holds, a row click and a stream's press tick, Move N rows… opens the window and holds the job window's Escape, the job's batches with Revert behind the word; the office sees none of it), `BankingCostBatchesTab.render.test.tsx` (every batch with Revert, a picked job's batches first).
- **Docs**: `COST_BATCHES.md` ("Not yet built" → the screens), `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` (the Banking row and the Cost batches section), `GLOSSARY.md` (Cost batch), `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` §15, `BANKING_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md` (the tab's row), `twins/APP_DIRECTORY.md` (`/banking`).
