# v2.6179 — Archived bids sit apart from the undecided on a Development page (2026-10-30)

Fix, no migration, no edge function. The second finding of the owner's live walk of the real site's Development page (punch list #208): with the address's pieces shown, the *Bid, undecided* tile counted thirteen bids that nobody is chasing — every one archived off the working board with no send and no outcome (`SPIKE.md`, finding 1, had flagged that the head and the Bid Board's *Not yet won or lost* disagreed).

## Cause

`bidUndecided` (`src/lib/developments/overview.ts`) read *not won or lost*, the customer profile's bucket, so an archived unsent bid counted as undecided and its price into the figure. The Bid Board's pending section counts only sent bids, and `pursuitOutcomeOf` puts an archived unsent bid in none of the four buckets.

## Change

- `bidUndecided` now reads the status word: undecided is *sent* or *working*. Won and lost are decided; archived sits apart. A bid sent and then archived stays undecided, since the answer is still out. Every reader follows: the head's two tiles and `headCounts`, the Money band's chip (`siteMoney.ts`), the Pieces *waiting on* column, the *Mark won* confirm's undecided count.
- The *Bid, undecided* card (`countCards.ts`): archived bids are no longer rows. With none in play the head reads *No bid in play* when any is archived (else *Every bid is decided*), the groups add *Archived · N* after Won and Lost, and the foot says *N archived bids sit apart, not counted*. The tile's sub-line reads *none in play* in the same case.
- The guide `see-everything-on-a-development` says the rule in one line.
- Tests in `overview.test.ts` and `countCards.test.ts`.
