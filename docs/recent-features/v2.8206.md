# v2.8206 — Lien desk: a missed notice that can no longer go stops asking for a month (2026-11-21)

Punch list #361 (the ledger, v2.7989). Seen on the live read of 2026-11-21 that followed v2.8167 and v2.8170, and written on the card as a choice: fold the gates as on a sent notice, or say the window closed. **Taunya's pick, 2026-11-21: fold to one line.** Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

A notice in the Missed pile with no month still due, and no late notice left to send, is a notice nobody can send. Its pane judged it anyway. The gates' bar read *✗ Can't go out yet · 1 blocker*, gate 4 was red with *No month picked* and *Pick at least one month for the notice to name*, the scrolled strip opened with a red *No month picked ›*, and the Months on this job bar read *no months*. No month could be picked there: v2.8125 took the tick column off that card because nothing on it could be ticked. It is the fault v2.6625 fixed on a sent notice, on the other pile.

## What changed

- [`lienDeskGates.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDeskGates.ts): `lienClosedGatesFold({ closedOn, gates, closedMonths, monthShort })` answers the fold's shape with `kind: 'closed'`. The line reads *Window closed Aug 15 · the owner · the GC · the kind · May*, each gate's value in plain words with no tone, as the sent fold says them (one helper, `lienFoldFacts`, words both). With two or more months the head reads *Windows closed, the last Oct 15*. The fourth row for *More details ∨* is *Months whose window closed*. The fold's type gains `kind`, `tail` (*as the notice went* or *nothing sent*) and `monthsHead` (*Jul + Aug*, or *May, closed*).
- [`LienDesk.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDesk.tsx): the desk folds a Missed row when `lienMissedNoticeStillGoes` is false. That is the footer's own test since v2.8170, so the fold arrives with *Window closed · Nothing to sign* and never without it. `closedOn` is the last of the closed months' window days.
- [`lienStripChips.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienStripChips.ts): with a closed fold the first chip is a quiet *Window closed Aug 15 ›* back to the top, and the months chip reads the closed months (*May ›*). No gate chip is drawn.
- [`lienPaneSections.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienPaneSections.ts): the gates bar reads the fold's own `head` and `tail` (*Window closed Aug 15 · nothing sent*), and the months bar its `monthsHead` (*May, closed · $33,500*).
- [`LienDeskGates.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskGates.tsx): `LienGatesSentFold` draws both kinds and carries `data-kind`.

## Not changed

- A missed notice that can still go keeps its four gates: a month still due, or a late notice while the affidavit can still be filed (v2.4708, v2.8170). A notice is still to be sent there, so the gates still judge it.
- The months card, the timeline, the paper and the footer of a missed notice that can no longer go. The footer already said the window closed.
- A sent notice's fold reads as it did.
