# v2.7148 — Needs you counts the card charges a receipt text cannot finish, and each row names its next press (punch list #299, the step after PR 2) (2026-11-13)

The step [`to-dos/ticket-text/README.md`](../../to-dos/ticket-text/README.md) lists after PR 2 (v2.7142), the mock-up's frame T7. PR 2 writes on a charge's request why no text could go; until now that showed only on the charge's line in the Team queue. It sends nothing and texts nobody.

## What changed

- **A Needs you line** (`receipts-to-chase`, the office's, `dashboardNeedsYou.ts`): *N card charges wait on a receipt the text cannot get*, with how many of each kind under it (`receiptChaseDetail`). Its button opens the Team queue (`needsYouDoors.ts`).
- **A Fill them in kind** on Quickfill (`lib/needsYou/fillInReceipts.ts`, `ReceiptChasePresses.tsx`): one row a charge, newest first, in T7's own sentences. *Jordan has no cell on file, and $228.58 at Ridgeway Supply posted to their card.* Each row carries what the app knows and a door: **Add Jordan's cell** (People), **Tie the card** (Banking's cards), **Open the Team queue** (where + Ticket is).
- **Got it** takes the row off the office's list and moves on, with Undo in the trail. Nothing else changes: the link stays open and a receipt that comes later still lands. **Copy the receipt link** makes the charge's link for sending by hand, the Team queue's own press, and stays on the row.
- **Six kinds from four items.** T7 drew four: no cell, a card tied to no one, a STOP, and two texts with no receipt. A card tied to a person who has left is its own sentence beside the second, and a text the phone service turned away three times is a sixth.
- **The database** (`docs/migrations/20261113150000_receipt_requests_office.md`): `office_done_at` and `office_done_by` on `receipt_requests`, the read `receipt_requests_to_chase()` and the write `receipt_requests_set_done()`, each for the office alone.
- Guides: *sort the team's card purchases from the office* (The charges on Needs you), *read the Needs you card* (the new line, the new rows).

## What is not built, and why

- **Text again.** T7 draws it on the fourth item. A second text by a person's press is the owner's call 1 (one reminder, none, or one a day for three days) in another form, and a press that sends a real text wants her word. Copy the receipt link stands in.
- **+ Ticket on the row.** The desk's scan lives on the Team queue's line; the row's door goes there.

## Is this the best we can do?

For the office's morning pass, yes, with two things to know.

- **The row for a missing cell does not leave when the cell is added.** The reason is final for its charge (PR 2), so the charge still has no receipt; the office presses Got it, or sends the link. A line that cleared itself there would hide a charge with no paper.
- **A fuel stop is a row like any other.** Call 5 (every linked card, or field roles only) decides how many rows a week this is. At today's volume, about 80 charges a week, Got it is one press each.
