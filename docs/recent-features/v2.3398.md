# v2.3398 — Pipeline on a map, PR 3: As of — the map rewound to any day (2026-09-14)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Third PR of the train in [`to-dos/pipeline-on-a-map/`](../../to-dos/pipeline-on-a-map/README.md), stacked on v2.3396 and v2.3397. This is the owner's "one addition": "I would like to be able to scroll back in time."

## What shipped

- **Kernel [`jobsMapAsOf.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobsMapAsOf.ts)** (+7 tests): the history shape (light job rows, moves, bills, payments); `statusOnDay` (the last move at or before the day; before a job's first move, that move's `from_status`; with no moves, the status it has now); `moneyOnDay` (each bill out by the day minus the payments dated by then, clamped per bill, plus the oldest bill's age); `jobsMapJobsAsOf` (a job created after the day is not drawn; Collections only when flagged by the day and still flagged now, since the Pipeline clears the flag on paid; the percent is not replayed); `jobsMapSinceThen` + its line (*+41 jobs started · 38 billed · 52 paid · 2 sent to collections*); the slider arithmetic (`JOBS_MAP_AS_OF_FLOOR_YMD` = 2026-02-22, the first recorded move; jumps today · 1 wk · 1 mo · 3 mo · 6 mo · the floor); Play at one day per 150 ms.
- **Loader [`loadJobsMapHistory.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/loadJobsMapHistory.ts)**: four paged reads (every job's light columns with the GC name, every status move, every billed or paid invoice's amount and bill date — the Billed section's own reference, the hand-set date else `billed_at` — outside Stripe test mode, every payment with `paid_on` else `created_at`), all in the company calendar, cached five minutes; nothing loads until the toggle is on.
- **The card**: **⏮ As of** in the header; the As-of row (Play / Pause, the day, days ago, the range slider, jump chips, the floor's sentence at the left end, *replayed from status moves · every job that existed that day, filters aside*); rewound, `jobsMapJobsAsOf` replaces the board's rows as the map's world — pins, chips, the rail's buckets, dollars and ask list all read the day; the since-then strip under the rail. **Rewound, the board's filters do not apply** — they describe today's rows; the strip says so.
- **Openers by id**: a rewound job has no board row, so Open job takes the id (`openJobDetail({ jobId })`), Edit takes the id (`tryOpenEditJob`), and a pin click lands on the row directly when the board has it, else through the # jump's lean lookup by number (a rewound paid job fetches and merges like a typed number would).
- Guide *see the pipeline on a map*: "Scroll back in time".

## Honest limits

The percent done is not replayed (no history of it). A past stint in Collections that has since been paid off is not shown (the flag is cleared on paid). Jobs deleted since the day are gone from the table and cannot be counted. Ready-to-bill on a past day reads from the status move only; drafts are not dated.
