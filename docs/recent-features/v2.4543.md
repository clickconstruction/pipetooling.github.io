# v2.4543 — Submittals: the top of the tab says each thing once (2026-10-05)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Finding 10 of the tab review, the last of the three redraws in [`to-dos/submittals/redraws-10-11-12-before-after.html`](../../to-dos/submittals/redraws-10-11-12-before-after.html) (12 was [v2.4537](v2.4537.md), 11 [v2.4542](v2.4542.md)).

## What changed

- **One header line.** Two lines sat under the bid's name: the sources (*26 fixtures on the takeoff · no schedule yet · choose from the takeoff*) and the revision with its tiles (*Working on Rev 1 · draft · Sep 29 · 14 rows · 13 proposed · 1 missing · 7 of 13 sheets in*). It is one now: *Rev 1 · draft · Sep 29 · Plumbing fixtures & equipment* (plus *started from Rev N*, *package built* and the older-revision warning, as before). Where the rows come from is step 1's to say; how many rows and sheets is step 3's.
- **One help door.** The title's ?, *Words on this page* and the strip's *Walk me through it ▶* became one **? Help** button beside the title (`SubmittalHelpMenu`): a menu with *Walk me through it*, *Words on this page* and *Open the guide*, each with a line saying what it does. On the SpaceX bid the help doors on screen went from 12 to 6.
- **A folded step is one line**: its title and summary. Its sentence and its own ? show once the step is open (`RoadSection`); the ? still starts the walkthrough at that step.
- **An open step drops a summary its contents already say** (`summaryWhenOpen`): step 1 (the cards), step 3 (the table's count chips), step 5 once a room exists (the room line), step 6 once there are answers (the decisions line), step 7 when rows were sent back (the button), step 8 once the log has counts. Step 2's summary is the revision switch and step 4's is its only status, so they stay.
- **The strip is three rows**: the stage pills with *See what the GC sees* beside them, the Next line, its button. The first-visit *New here?* line is unchanged, and while it shows it is the only walkthrough button on the strip.
- **Step 1's folded summary is words only.** Its links moved to the cards: *Add from the takeoff* and *Type or paste the schedule* were already there, and the schedule card gained *Pick the products on Pricing* for a bid with a schedule and nothing picked yet (the header's old *the picks on Pricing* door).
- **"Need a reason" joins the table's count chips** (`rowFilterChips`): an alternate or a design change with no reason is what holds the package, and the header's tiles were the only place an open step 3 counted it.

## Where the build differs from the drawing

- **The revision's name is still written twice**: on the header line and on its switch in step 2. The switch is a control she presses, not a sentence, so it stayed. The drawing had step 2 fold to *started Sep 29 · 14 rows*.
- *Walk me through it* in the Help menu starts at the first step, as the title's ? did; the drawing said it would start at the step she is on. Each open step's own ? does that.
