# v2.6157 — The sub's Owed to you as rows under two bands, a prototype, and a chip that names its day (punch list #217, field-2) (2026-10-30)

Punch list #217, *Show it, don't say it*: the field scan's second finding is the sub's portal head, *Owed to you $3,500 · $2,400 — queued for the Friday pay run · $1,100 — later (see each job below)*, where the head (`queuedNow`: every open sheet whose payable-after is on or before the next run, at any stage) and the cards' chips (`isSubPortalSheetQueued`: stage *customer pay* and any payable-after, printing the run weekday whatever the date) follow different rules, so a card could read *Queued for Friday* while its money sat under *later*. Taunya marked it Build on 2026-10-30 and wrote: *"Could I please see this side-by-side of the before and after as a live prototype? You may need to use test data."* This PR is that prototype, a draft with no auto-merge, plus the one-rule fix for the chips, which ships to every sub.

## What changed

- **`lib/subPortal/subOwedBands.ts`** (new, 11 tests): one rule for where a sheet's open money sits — in the **next run** when its payable-after is on or before the next run, whatever its stage; **later** with its own day when the date is after the run, or when no run is on the calendar; later with no day when it has no date — and from it the head's bands (`subOwedBands`: the next run's rows in payable-after order and their sum, the later rows dated first and undated last, and `unplaced` for money the head counts on no open sheet) and the card's chip (`subOwedChip`). `subOwedDayWords` names a day in either language (*Fri Oct 30*, *vie 30 oct*, *Dec 15*). `withSubOwedShape` and `subOwedShapeFlagFromSearch` carry the page flag along the office's door.
- **The chip, outside the switch** (`pages/SubPortal.tsx`, `SheetCard`): a card's chip comes from the kernel — *Queued for Fri Oct 30* for money in the next run, *Queued for Dec 15* in the quiet colour for money later — and falls back to the stage's chip when the sheet has no date. The rail's fourth dot and the sentence keep `subPortalRailStep`: the dots are the work's stages, and a promised progress payment never jumps the rail ahead of the work. This is the small, clear part of the fix and ships to every sub: the rule it follows is the head's own rule, so the two can no longer disagree, and a chip never names a weekday for a date months away.
- **The bands, behind a flag** (`components/subPortal/SubOwedBandsHead.tsx`): `?shape=1` on any portal link draws *Owed to you* as its rows — the figure, then **This Friday · Oct 30** with each job and its amount and the sum *in the Friday run*, then **Later** with *payable after Dec 15* or *date to come* on each row, and *on no job card* for money the head counts on no open sheet. English and Spanish follow the page's toggle (new strings in `subPortalI18n.ts`). The public page has no signed-in person, so there is no per-user switch; the flag is the switch.
- **The side by side**: `?sbs=1` on the sample token (`/sub?t=sample&sbs=1`) or for a signed-in dev draws **Today** over **With the bands** in the head. On a dev server from this branch: `/sub?t=sample&sbs=1`, or `/sub?demo=1&sbs=1` after `/dev-login?as=1` for the fixture with no network. **Show me on their portal** (`SheetStoryModal`) passes a `?shape=1` or `?sbs=1` the office's own page carries along to the portal.
- **`components/prototypes/SideBySide.tsx`**: this branch's copy of the shared #217 helper, byte for byte the office-round-columns branch's (#6528); whichever lands first wins. The portal reads only `SideBySide` and `sideBySideRequested` from it.
- **Smoke** `SubPortal.owedBands.render.test.tsx` (4) over the DEV demo fixture: today's head and the chips' days; the bands with `?shape=1`; the side by side for a dev with `?sbs=1`; nothing for a sub.
- **Guide** *see what a sub sees on their portal* names the chip's day and the two flags.

## What the mock-up asked for that this PR does not do

- The side by side covers the head only; the cards draw the kernel's chips in every mode, so *Today* in the comparison shows the cards as they now are, not as they were.
- No per-user switch on the public portal, since nobody is signed in there; `?shape=1` is the switch, and the office door carries it.
- A bar of segments per job, which the mock-up called optional, is not drawn: on a phone the rows are the bar.

## Decisions

- Today's head is untouched: its `queuedNow` already follows the kernel's band rule, so the fix is to the chips alone, and the bands are the same money as its two lines, drawn as rows.
- No migration, no function change.
