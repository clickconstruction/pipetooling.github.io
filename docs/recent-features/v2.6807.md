# v2.6807 — The Bridge leaves ZZ test jobs out of its own figures (2026-11-09)

Follow-up to v2.6805 (PR #7484), which gave the assistants' ready-to-bill bar and the contract counts the ZZ test-job rule after the office read a bar of 2 over a section of 1. The same search found the Bridge's loader, `src/lib/bridge/loadBridgeData.ts`, with no rule on any read. The Bill pile and the Dashboard's financials already took the shared ids (v2.5834, v2.5120); the loader itself and the Bill desk's reads did not.

## What a ZZ job could reach (the survey)

Every read in `loadBridgeData` and `bill/loadBillDesk.ts` that returns jobs, bills or payments:

| Read | Feeds | A ZZ job counted? | Now |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `bridge field sessions` → `loadEarnedJobs` | jobs worked, earned revenue, field hours, the *Price jobs…* and no-percent counts | Yes: an approved session on a ZZ job made it a job worked, earning at its contract | Sessions on a ZZ job leave before the jobs are read (ids) |
| `bridge invoices sent` | billed per week (the Bill card's out, the Collect card's in), net position, the cash forecast | Yes: the read carried no job id | Reads `id, job_id`; a ZZ job's bills leave (ids) |
| `bridge payments` | paid per week, net position | Yes | Reads `job_id, invoice_id`; a payment leaves with its job or its bill |
| `bridge rtb`, `bridge collections` | `levers.rtb`, `levers.collections` | Counted, but **nothing draws either figure**: only `levers.bidsDue` is read (`Bridge.tsx`, `sheetBuild.ts`) | Read the names; leave by name or id, so the count is the Pipeline section's rows |
| `bill desk window bills` | the Bill desk's leaks, fast lane and scorecard | Yes: every job's bills in the window | Leave by the label rows' names or the ids |
| `bill desk pile bills`, ready days, labels | the same | Only through the pile, already filtered | Ready days and labels of a dropped job leave too |
| `list_job_promised_pay_dates` | a promise on an AR item | No: it is looked up by the AR items' job ids, already filtered | Unchanged |

## What changed

- **`src/lib/bridge/zzTestJobs.ts`** (new, pure): `bridgeReadyToBill`, `bridgeCollections`, `withoutZzFieldSessions`, `withoutZzBridgeMoney`, `withoutZzBillDeskRows`. Each is the existing rule (`withoutZzTestJobRows`, `withoutZzTestJobMoney` in `src/lib/jobs/zzTestJobVisibility.ts`), not a new one. `zzTestJobIds` is the shared id read; `null` keeps every row, for a dev whose switch shows them. A row that carries its job's names leaves by them too, so a ZZ job made inside the id read's one-minute cache still leaves.
- **`loadBridgeData(db, { zzTestJobIds })`** and **`loadBillDeskReads(db, { …, zzTestJobIds })`**: the option is required, as on `loadBillPileJobs`, so no caller can forget it.
- **`Bridge.tsx`**: the page reads the ids once per user (`loadZzTestJobIds`) while `useZzTestJobsHidden(role)` hides them, and reloads when a dev flips the switch. The Bill desk's read key carries the switch.
- **`sheetReads.ts`** passes the ids it already takes for the Bill pile, so `bridge-weekly-sheet` (the service role, always hiding) builds the Monday sheet by the same rule. The copies under `supabase/functions/_shared/gcKernels/` are regenerated.
- Tests: `zzTestJobs.test.ts` holds the Bridge's Ready to Bill count equal to the Pipeline section's rows for an assistant, an owner, a role still loading, a dev hiding and a dev showing; `bill/loadBillDesk.test.ts` runs the loader on a fake client.

## Release order

Merge, then `supabase functions deploy bridge-weekly-sheet` (`npm run check:edge-drift` names it). Until the deploy the Monday email keeps counting a ZZ job's bills, payments and hours as before; nothing breaks.

## Left as it is

- **Costs.** Bank charges, supply-house bills and sub sheets filed on a ZZ job stay in direct cost: the money left the bank whatever the job is called. The overhead snapshot's field labor is the Overhead tab's shared read and is not touched here.
- **Pay speeds.** `get_billed_customer_pay_speeds` is a server aggregate; a ZZ customer's paid bills still weigh in its medians. That takes a migration.
- **The other Bridge loaders.** `loadBuildPace` (the open jobs and the Build pile), `loadVectorDays` and `loadBridgeVectors` (a person's day on a ZZ job), `loadDemandPace`, the Market and Sales desks and `loadCollectScorecard` (every payment since the scorecard's start) carry no ZZ rule yet.
- **The two unread levers.** `levers.rtb` and `levers.collections` cost two reads on every load and no screen draws them.
