# v2.8300 — A thread on a phone: the person's name has room (punch list #348, the walk of 2026-11-21) (2026-11-22)

v2.8267 named one thing it saw and did not fix: at 320 px wide the first row of a thread's header was 37 px too wide. The owner asked on 2026-11-22 to check that it still needed fixing and to fix it if so. It did, and it was wider than 320. Client only: no migration and no function. Card: [`to-dos/task-windows-phone-leftovers.md`](../../to-dos/task-windows-phone-leftovers.md).

## What was measured first

The header's first row on a ZZ Test person's thread, nobody holding it, in a browser:

| Screen | The row | The name got | ⋯ ended at |
|---|---|---|---|
| 320 px | 333 px in a 296 px row | 0 px | 345, off the screen |
| 375 px | fits | 18 px of 164 | 363 |
| 402 px, an iPhone 17 | fits | 45 px of 164 | 390 |

So on every phone the name read as two or three letters, and on the narrowest the row ran off the screen. The way back is 55 px and call with ⋯ is 89. The who-has-it pill, *Nobody has it · Take it*, was 171.

## The cause

The folded header was drawn on 2026-10-12 (v2.5357, cut 10) with the who-has-it pill as a face alone. That PR gave the header slot a second argument, `ThreadLook { folded }`, and the window has handed it over since. The slot that draws the pill, `headerRight` in `useFollowUpThreadSlots.tsx`, took one argument and never read the look. The fold's own test drew a stand-in slot, so nothing failed.

## What changed

- **[`WhoHasItPill.tsx`](../../src/components/followUp/WhoHasItPill.tsx)** takes `face`. With it the press is the 22 px circle alone in a 28 px control: the holder's initials, or **?** for nobody, on the blue tint when it is yours. The pill's words are its `aria-label` and its hover is as before. A phone has no hover, so with `face` the menu says under its head what the words said about who is off (`offPillTip`). The whole pill and its menu are untouched.
- **[`useFollowUpThreadSlots.tsx`](../../src/components/followUp/useFollowUpThreadSlots.tsx)**: `headerRight(c, look)` passes `face={look?.folded}`. A narrow column at a desk folds the header too, so it gets the face too, as cut 10 drew it.
- **[`src/lib/followUp/whoMenuPlace.ts`](../../src/lib/followUp/whoMenuPlace.ts)** (new): where the pill's menu hangs. It hung from the pill's right edge, or from its left when the right did not fit. A face two thirds across a 375 px screen fits from neither: from the right the menu starts at -14, from the left it ends at 518. `whoMenuPlace` keeps the first choice, and otherwise slides the menu from the pill's left edge until it sits 8 px inside the screen on both sides. `whoMenuWidth` is the menu's own `min(280px, 85vw)`. The pill places its menu on open and again on a resize while it is open, so a phone turned on its side does not leave it where it was.

## After

| Screen | The name gets | The face | ⋯ ends at | The menu |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 320 px | 106 px of 164 | 183 to 211 | 308 | 40 to 312 |
| 375 px | 161 px of 164 | 238 to 266 | 363 | 87 to 367 |
| 402 px | all 164 | 265 to 293 | 390 | 13 to 293 |
| 1,400 px, a desk | as before | none: the whole pill, 171 px | as before | as before |

The menu was opened at each width and no row in it was pressed. Nothing was typed and nothing was sent.

## Tests

- `whoMenuPlace.test.ts`: the three measured places, every phone width from 320 to 600 with the pill anywhere inside the margins, and a screen that cannot be read.
- `WhoHasItPill.render.test.tsx`: the face's initials, its name, its hover and its tint, **?** opening the same menu, the off line in the face's menu and never in the whole pill's.
- `useFollowUpThreadSlots.render.test.tsx`: a folded look gets the face, and a wide look or none gets the whole pill. This is the test that was missing.

## Not claimed

- Measured in a browser pane, not in a simulator or on a phone in a hand.
- Nobody's face is a grey **?**, as the whole pill drew it beside its words. Alone it could read as a help press. It is the owner's pick whether that stays.
- A person off for the night shows on the face only through its menu. The words *off until 6 AM* are not on the header of a phone.
