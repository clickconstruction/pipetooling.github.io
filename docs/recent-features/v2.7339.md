# v2.7339 — Pay calendar: a job on its own calendar is scored on its own payments (2026-11-15)

Punch list #341, PR 3 of five: the job's own record. Taunya picked the rest of the train on 2026-11-15 (*build the entire PR train*). A job on its own Pay calendar had its due day worked out from its own terms, and then the lateness under that day came from the payer's payments on **every** job, replayed through this job's calendar. A big job on its own terms borrowed a record made under other terms. And a payer's summary line in Money waiting and Accounts Receivable read the payer's own calendar alone, so a payer with none, and one job on its own, read as a pace payer while that job's bill was judged by its due day.

## The call this stands on

**The owner's call 2 is not answered.** This PR takes the card's first pick: a job on its own calendar is scored on **that job's payments once it has two, the payer's until then, and the row says which**. The other pick was *always the payer's, as today*. To change it, `JOB_RECORD_MIN_PAYMENTS` in `src/lib/jobs/payCalendar.ts` is the count, and `recordFor` is the one place the choice is made: returning `of: 'payer'` for every read puts it back as it was.

## What changed

- **`recordFor(receipts, read)`** answers the record behind one bill's due day and whose it is: `payer` (the payer's calendar, the payer's payments on every job, as before), `job` (a job's own calendar, scored on that job's payments alone) or `borrowed` (a job's own calendar with fewer than two payments of its own, so the payer's stand in). `calendarSourceFor`'s read now carries the job its row is for (`jobId`), and a receipt carries its job (`PayCalendarReceipt.jobId`; the pay-speeds read has sent it since v2.2288), so no caller hands over anything new.
- **The pay model.** `billedPayModel` scores through `recordFor` and carries `record` beside `lateness`. Its hover says *This job's last 2 payments all landed by the due day.*, or *No payment on this job yet. The record is Larkfield Construction's on other jobs.* before the payer's own sentence. The board's rows, the Payment forecast, Call mode, the lien runway and the job window's bills read it, since each already hands the read over.
- **A billed row's line under its due day** ends with whose record it is (`recordLandsShort`): *Usually lands about Dec 13 · this job's record*, or *Usually lands about Dec 16 · their record on other jobs*. The day still leads, since a narrow row cuts the tail, and the hover carries it whole (`recordLandsWords`). A job with no payment under a payer with none reads *No payment on this job yet*.
- **The Pay calendar window.** On the job's side the amber box scores what is typed on that job's payments once it has two, and its lead reads *This job's record against this.* Until then it says whose record stands in. `loadPayerReceipts` keeps each payment's job.
- **Money waiting and Accounts Receivable, each bill.** A bill is read by `billCalendarRead`, which scores it on the record its calendar calls for and carries the calendar, whether it is the job's own, and the record.
- **Money waiting and Accounts Receivable, the summary line.** `payerCalendarSummary` reads the line from its bills: the bill furthest past its expected day lends the line its calendar and its record. With no bill past its day the line reads the payer's own calendar, as before. `MoneyWaitingRow` and `ArCustomerRow` gain `calendarOwn`. The small line reads *a job's own pay calendar · lands ~6d after the pay day* (`calendarBasisWords`), the hover opens *One of Larkfield Construction's jobs is on its own pay calendar.* (`payerCalendarLead`), and the call opener says *past expected on a job's own pay calendar*.
- **Guides**: *set a payer's pay calendar* gains *The record on a job's own calendar*; *see which customers pay slowly* gains two sentences on a job's own calendar.

## Not changed

- Which calendar a bill reads, and its due day.
- A job that follows its payer, and a payer's own bills: scored on every payment of theirs, as before. A test compares the two.
- A job on its own calendar with fewer than two payments: its expected day is the one it had yesterday. Only the words are new.
- The receipts are the newest 12 a payer the pay-speeds read returns. A job whose two payments are older than its payer's newest 12 still reads the payer's record. The read is not widened here.

## Deploy

No migration. The edge copies of `payCalendar.ts` and `billedDuePay.ts` under `supabase/functions/_shared/gcKernels/` are regenerated. The functions that read them pass a bare calendar, which is scored as the payer's, so a redeploy changes nothing they send; `npm run check:edge-drift` lists them as behind until the next deploy of each.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **Two is a small record.** Two payments on the day make a job read *Usually lands by the due day* with the weight of two. The words say the count, and the owner may want three.
- **The borrowed record is the payer's whole record,** so a job's one payment is in it. The words then say *on all their jobs*, not *on other jobs*.
- **The summary line reads one bill's calendar.** A payer with a late bill on their own calendar and a later one on a job's own shows the worse of the two, and says nothing of the other. Each bill under it still names its own due day.
