# v2.3609 — People → Who's where: the week as crews — who worked with whom, the lead read off the schedule (2026-09-19)

PR 2 of Who's where (PR 1: v2.3607; the to-do `to-dos/whos-where/` closes with this — its record is these two fragments; Todd, 2026-09-18: *"for me to really define team leads, I think I first need to make an org chart, but not in the traditional sense, since many roles are hourly and per job"*; and, on the first drawing's crown-confirm button: *"I want to not have to excessively burden anyone with extra buttons"*). Client only; no migration; nothing written.

## The change

- **Kernel** [`whosWhere.ts`](../../src/lib/people/whosWhere.ts) gains `weekCrews(data, ymds)` (8 tests in `whosWhereWeek.test.ts`): a crew forms around each master or sub present on a field job that week — **the lead, read off the schedule and the clock** (`derivedLead`: the master, else the sub, else nobody); its members are everyone who shared a (day, job) with the lead, with `daysClocked` / `daysListed` / `daysMissed` (listed, did not clock that job) / `daysElsewhere` (clocked another job that day) and a one-line note when the plan and the clock disagree. A person sits on every crew they shared days with. Leftovers who still shared a (day, job) form lead-less crews (union-find); singletons are *alone*; office-only people are *office*; the rest of the roster is *not in*. The office and no-job targets never form crews.
- **The week view** [`WhosWhereWeek.tsx`](../../src/components/people/WhosWhereWeek.tsx): a card per crew — the lead's head with a crown (hollow when they never clock, *listed N* underneath), members with days-together, the jobs line (*J258 · Oak St ×3 · J291 · Elm Ct ×2*), a dashed card and *no lead listed — no master or sub on the block* for a lead-less crew; the rail (Office, Alone this week, Not in · N expandable); the legend with the crew / alone / office / not-in counts.
- **The tab** [`PeopleWhosWhereTab.tsx`](../../src/components/people/PeopleWhosWhereTab.tsx) opens on the week (`ww_view=week`, the default); ◀ ▶ step weeks, *This week* returns; a tap on the day strip opens the day (`ww_view=day`, PR 1's islands, scrubber and lanes) with *⇱ Week* to come back. One week of data serves both views.
- Guide `see-who-was-on-which-job.md` gains *The week: crews, not jobs* and *Why there is no button*; `GLOSSARY.md`, `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`, `PEOPLE_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md` amended.
- `to-dos/helper-tryout-loop/` is un-gated: its verdict card reads `derivedLead` from here.
