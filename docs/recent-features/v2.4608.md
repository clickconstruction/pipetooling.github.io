# v2.4608 — Submittals: open the GC's real review page, as the office (2026-10-05)

Punch list #62 (*See what they see*), PR 1b-ii. Client only: no migration, no edge function. Stacked on #4590 (v2.4606), which is stacked on #4584 (v2.4593). It merges after both, and its to-do status cites v2.4606. It relies on v2.4599 (`submit-submittal-review` refuses writes from the office's preview or session), deployed and proven on 2026-10-05.

## What was missing

The window draws a draft through the page's own pieces (v2.4593, v2.4606). But for anything already shared, the honest view is the real page. It shows the procurement card, the thread and the PDF line that the window does not. Until now the office's only way there was *Copy link* into a private window. Everywhere else in the app, a real page opens from the office as a preview (`withPreviewFlag`: the portal globe, the bid room panel, the legal portal).

## What changed

- **One door, flagged on every path** ([`submittalRoom.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/submittalRoom.ts)): `roomPreviewLink(origin, token)` is the room link through `withPreviewFlag`. The href itself carries `preview=1`, so a copied address carries it too. The function never counts it as an open (since v2.2922) and refuses every write from it (v2.4599). `roomLink` stays the plain link.
- **On the Share step** (`SubmittalRoomPanel`): **Open their page ↗** beside *Copy link*, once the room has been shared. *Copy link* still copies the plain link the office sends.
- **In the window** (`SeeWhatTheGcSees`): **Open their page as it is now · Rev 2 ↗** under the first line, only when the link shows something. A closed room drops the *Rev N*. The window is the draft; the door is their page today, and the label tells them apart. The tab hands the room's token (one prop).
- **On their page** (`SubmittalRoom.tsx`), opened with the flag: *You are looking as the office. Nothing here is counted or saved.*, beside the sample strip, before anyone presses anything.
- **The rule's one home**: `GLOSSARY.md` → *What customers see (Settings)* now holds the three ways the office sees what an outsider sees. They are the real page in preview wherever the record exists, a draft only through the page's own components and kernels, and samples only through the real kernels. `customerSurfaceRegistry.ts` points at it, and the Submittals map links to it.
