# v2.5109 — Stages: the three big row renderers become components (2026-10-09)

Client only; no migration, no edge function, no screen change. Punch list **#46** row 2 (`to-dos/decomposition-queue.md`): the Stages map's step 9 (`docs/JOBS_STAGES_TAB_ARCHITECTURE.md`). It also settles the optional item on the residuals card #21.

## What shipped

- **Three new components**, cut from `jobsStagesRowShared.tsx` by script on exact anchors. Each body is verbatim but for its props: `opts?.x` became `x`.
  - `StagesQuickActionsStack` (was `renderStagesQuickActionsStack`): schedule, week dispatch, call, Dispatch, task.
  - `StagesFieldAndBillingLines` (was `renderStagesFieldAndBillingLines`): Next / Ends, Not scheduled, Done, the bill line and the man-hours chip. The private `whenLine` moved with it.
  - `StagesJobCellActivityFooter` (was `renderStagesJobCellActivityFooter`): the invoice jump chips, the contract and legal chips, the Stripe Resend line and See all.
- **The two tables**: `JobsStagesTable` and `JobsStagesUnifiedTable` keep their wrapper closures, which now render the components. The row files that call them (`StagesUnifiedJobRow`, `StagesUnifiedInvoiceRow`) did not change.
- **`jobsStagesRowShared.tsx`**: 1,661 → 1,063 lines, 25 → 21 functions. The imports only the three used are gone.
- **What changes, all off screen:** each part is now its own component in the React tree. The DOM is the same, and none of the three holds a hook.
- **Docs**: the Stages map (the shared file's row, §9's table, side effects and status, the master summary, step 9); the playbook's Stages row; the decomposition card's row 2, status and `next`; the residuals card #21, whose optional Stages item this was.
