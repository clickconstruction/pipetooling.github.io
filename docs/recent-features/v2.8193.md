# v2.8193 — Job photos and Leave Report: keep a photo's spot on the job (2026-11-21)

Punch list #413, step 2. The second door onto a job's places: a photo that is being added anyway.

## Why

**Photo of a spot** (v2.8191) starts from the job. A crew that is already adding photos to the job, or leaving a report with photos, has the photo in hand and should not have to take it twice. Most photos are plain: progress, a finished fixture. A few show something the next crew must find. Those few get one press.

## What ships

**In the Job photos window**, under each new photo's words: **＋ Keep this spot on the job**, the width of the tile's column and 48 px tall.

**On Leave Report**, under each photo tile: **Keep this spot**. The tile is 96 px wide, so the press is three words and 44 px tall. A screen reader hears *Keep this spot on the job*.

**One press** (`KeepSpotPress`):

1. reads the phone once (`readSpotNow`),
2. reads the photo back from the preview already on the screen (`spotFileFromPreview`), dated by the photo's own time when the screen read one,
3. opens the job's list of places already adding a place, or starts the list when the job has none (`openSpotOnJob`),

and the place sheet opens over the screen with the photo in it. From there it is the same sheet and the same rules as **Photo of a spot**: a good or weak reading sets the dot, a rough one or none moves nothing, and a photo more than five minutes old takes no reading.

**Kept.** When the place is saved, the press reads *✓ Kept on this job* (*✓ Kept* on a report's tile) and takes no second press, so one photo does not make two places. The place sheet tells the press through the spot's `onKept`, and only on a save: a sheet put down leaves the press as it was.

**A plain photo** is untouched. The press is one line to skip, and adding the photo, its words, its stage and the report are as they were.

## The reading is of the press, not of the camera

**Photo of a spot** reads the phone as the camera opens. Here the photo is already on the screen when the press is made, so the phone is read on the press. The person is usually still at the spot. When they are not, the photo's own time says so: past five minutes, no reading is used and the sheet says why.

## Not done here

- **A helper on Leave Report** hands the sheet no address, since the form does not hold one. With a good reading the dot needs none. With a rough reading on a job that has no list yet, the window says *Your photo is ready* and waits for **＋ Place**.
- **No signal:** as in v2.8191, a place set with no signal is not kept on the phone. The card's step 2b.
