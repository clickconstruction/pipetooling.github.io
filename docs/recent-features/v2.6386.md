# v2.6386 — Today's sends holds a text whose step already went (2026-11-02)

PR 2 of three on Taunya's report of 2026-11-02 ("this customer has already been texted but it won't let me click Stop
reminding on this job"); PR 1 is v2.6385. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What the rows said

The homeowner's moved-visit text sat under *ready to send* thirteen days after it was prepared. Read through the dev
seat, the app holds no text to that customer at all: no `messages` line on the contact, the number or the job, and no
`nudge_sent` row — so whatever text the office remembers went outside the app (Quo's webhook records a text sent from
the Quo app by hand, so it was not that either), and nothing in the app could have known. What the read did show is the
gap: the morning run keeps a step back when a reminder went inside its days (`nudgeDispatch.ts`'s `sentLately` over
`nudge_sent`) and when a text for it already waits (`offeredAlready`), but both are the **run's** rules over the
**run's** texts. An office-made offer — a moved visit's Rescheduled text (v2.5597), Send in the morning's — takes
neither road, and the window itself kept no rule, so such an offer sits ready after its step's text went another way:
the run's own text, Remind now from the thread's step card, or another offer on the same step pressed. A visit moved
twice makes two open offers on one step and job, both ready.

## What shipped

- **`offers.ts`'s `withSentHolds(offers, sent)`**: an offer whose contact, step and job got a `nudge_sent` row after it
  was prepared is held — *A text for this step already went to them on Tue Oct 20. Put this one away with Not this
  one.* (`heldSentWords`, the first day it went) — and the older of two open offers on one step and job is held with
  *A newer text for this step is on this list. Put this one away with Not this one.* (`HELD_NEWER`). A text that went
  is the fact that matters, so the hold wins over the run's own; the review hold (`withAnsweredHolds`, v2.6007) is
  applied after it, since an answered review says more.
- **`offersIo.ts`'s `loadOpen`** reads `nudge_sent` for the offers' people since the oldest open offer was prepared
  (`contact_id, step_key, bid_id, project_id, jobs_ledger_id, sent_at`, the office's own read as Follow up's
  `flowNudgesIo.ts` makes it) and hands it to the kernel; a read that fails holds nothing.
- **Guide** *work Today's sends* § When a text is held gains the two reasons. Docs: `GLOSSARY.md` → Today's sends,
  `MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md` (the run's paragraph names the window's own rule).

## Tests

`offers.test.ts`: the hold with its day and Send all skipping it; a text before the offer, on another job, another step
or to someone else holds nothing; the first of two days, and the hold over the run's own; the older of two offers held
and the newer ready; plain words.

## Not in this PR

The stale text itself (a visit on October 22 offered on November 2) is PR 3's: a moved visit's text expires with its
visit. The real row stays ready until then, or until the office presses Not this one.
