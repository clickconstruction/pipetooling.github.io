# v2.5079 — Bids: History puts back a removed row, with what hung on it (2026-10-09)

Bid history PR 5 (punch list #73, `to-dos/bid-history/README.md`), after the owner's call of 2026-10-09: a bid's editors see the bid's OWN removed rows so they can Put them back. Directed by PUNCHLIST. Migration `20261010017000_bid_removed_rows.sql` (`docs/migrations/20261010017000_bid_removed_rows.md`), pushed by PUNCHLIST after the merge; no edge function.

## What shipped

- **The database** (three functions, SECURITY DEFINER; the delete archive keeps its dev-only read):
  - `can_edit_bid(p_bid_id)`: the bids update policy as one check. That is its roles, a primary's own bids, not training mode, and a twin's own bids.
  - `list_bid_removed_rows(p_bid_id)`: the bid's own archive rows still out, for its editors only. Each row is named as History names it, with `in_ledger` when the ledger holds the removal too.
  - `restore_bid_removed_row(p_archive_id)`: one row back with the rows of the same delete that hang on it (a count row's prices, costs, part lines and ticks), parents first. A gone reference is cleared when the column allows it, and otherwise refused in words (*Its count row was removed too. Put that back first.*). The inserts are tagged `put-back`, so the ledger records them as the presser's. A whole bid stays the dev's restore.
- **The History window** reads the bid's removed rows beside its history and lays them over it (`mergeBidRemovedRows`):
  - A removal the ledger holds gains **Put back** by its archive row. The two are paired on table, row and the deleting transaction's time.
  - A removal from before the ledger joins as an archive line, as a dev already saw it.
  - A row whose count row was removed in the same action offers none: that count row's Put back brings it.
  - The result is said at the top (*SUMP is back, with 2 rows that hung on it.*), the history reads again, and the open bid's tabs reload (`BID_HISTORY_PUT_BACK_EVENT`).
  - Someone who cannot edit the bid gets no removed rows and no Put back on a removal. So does a database the function has not reached yet.
- **Captions**: a removed row put back reads *Put back SUMP and what hung on it*.
- **Action tags**:
  - the margin brush's strokes and their Undo (`price-brush`): *Brushed 6 prices*;
  - a price book switch, the copy it makes and the bid's pick (`book-switch`): *Switched the price book, copying 31 prices*.

  Both are a person's press, not the app's. `withBidActionIf` tags a write shared by tagged and untagged paths (`writeUnitPriceOverrideRow`, `cloneTemplateIntoBidAndActivate`, `saveBidSelectedPriceBookVersion`).
- **Out**: undo a whole action is its own PR, at PUNCHLIST's call.
- **The guide** *price a bid with the Workbench* has the removed-row paragraph.
