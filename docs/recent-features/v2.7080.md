# v2.7080 — Demand letter: a short letter no longer puts its signature alone on a second page (2026-11-12)

The owner's ask on the export of the lien letters from the live site (punch list #326's group): *take a look at this page as well, how could we help make it all fit on one page?* After v2.7071 the demand letter was the one paper of the eight still on two pages. Its second page held the signature and nothing else.

## What changed

- **The fit** (`demandLetter.ts`). `buildDemandLetterPdfBlob` draws the letter at the usual setting, `DEMAND_LETTER_SET` (11 pt Times on a 5.4 mm line). When that runs past one page it draws it again down `DEMAND_LETTER_FIT_STEPS` and keeps the first drawing that is one page: closer lines at the same 11 pt, then 10.5 pt, then 10 pt. A letter no step holds on one page keeps the usual setting. It is the lien letters' rule (v2.7071) on the demand letter's own drawer, and the floor is the same 10 pt.
- **The signature's room.** The block asked for 2 mm more than it draws before it would start. It now asks for what it uses, at any setting.
- **The sample** (`paperSamples.ts`). `paymentMethod` is set in front of the dispute line as typed. The sample held a fragment, so the letter read *by card … to the office If you believe…*. It is a whole sentence now. The card's mock-up carries the same sentence.

No word of the letter changed. No migration, no edge function.

## Measured

With the real PDF library, on made-up letters:

| Letter | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| The sample (the short form: four debt lines, one notice) | 2 pages, the signature alone on the second | 1 page at 10.5 pt |
| Today's shape, one bill: statement of account, pay code, fee and interest lines, two notices, two enclosures | 2 pages, the second 60% full | 2 pages, unchanged |
| Today's shape, three bills, four notices, the lien line | 2 pages | 2 pages, unchanged |

## Where it does not reach

- **The letter the Lien window sends today is longer than the sample.** The sample is the short form with no statement of account. A real letter carries the statement, a pay code, the fee and interest lines and its enclosures, and runs about a page and a half. No step holds that on one page, and it is left at the usual setting. Bringing it to one page is a redraw of the letter (the pay code beside the amount box, no repeated balance row on a one-bill statement, a shorter signature block), which is the owner's call and counsel's.
- **The Demand section's keep.** The heading asks for 72 mm before it starts, so a letter of today's shape breaks before *Demand* and leaves the foot of page 1 empty. Not changed here.
- **Browser print.** A recorded letter reopened from the Lien window prints from its HTML page, which this does not change. The sample prints on two sheets there.
- **The sample's shape.** The sample in Settings is still the short form.
