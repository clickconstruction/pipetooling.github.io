# v2.6351 — The Plans look counts only the jobs that build from plans (2026-11-02)

The owner, 2026-11-02, on a real development's Plans look reading *24 jobs on no part*: what does it mean, and should the words change? The chip counted every job in the development that had not named a part. On that site most of the 24 were billing holders and repairs — invoices and draws, mobilizations, crew weeks, change-order holders, a cable repair — which never build from plans, so the chip read as 24 things to fix and the ask to the estimators would have carried the same number. The words were the kernel's shorthand too: *on no part* reads as "not part of the site" to a first-timer.

## Change

- **Kernel `src/lib/plans/sitePlans.ts`** (pure, `sitePlans.test.ts`): `buildSitePlansView` takes each job with the facts the job-kind kernel reads (`SiteJobInput`: `kind`, `hcp_number`, `click_number`, `bid_id`, `project_id`, `revenue`, `status`, `billing_only`); `jobNeedsNoPlans(job)` answers `billing_only` (GC mode's billing job, which carries a project's bills and nothing else) or `service` (`jobKindOfRow`, the office's explicit kind first, punch list #238's rules after) or null — a job of no known kind still counts. `jobsWithNoPart` holds only the jobs that build from plans and name no part; the new `jobsNeedingNoPlans` holds the rest with their reason. The chip and the strip read *N jobs follow no plans yet* (*1 job follows no plans yet*); `sitePlansLeftOutWords` writes the quiet line *22 jobs need no plans: 20 bill only, 2 are service calls*; the all-good words read *every job that builds from plans names a part with a set*; `sitePlansAskCount` follows the smaller count.
- **The loaders**: `fetchDevelopmentPage.ts`'s `JOB_COLUMNS` reads `billing_only` and `kind` (the explicit kind column is read loosely until the types chore carries it, as `jobKind.ts` says); `readDoor.ts`'s `jobOf` maps both when the definer read carries them and leaves them null when it does not (a fact not loaded is skipped, never guessed); `MemberJob` (`overview.ts`) carries both as optional; `DevelopmentPage.tsx` hands the facts to the view.
- **`DevelopmentPartsCard.tsx`** (`DevelopmentPartsCard.render.test.tsx`): the chip's new words, and the quiet line (`site-left-out`) under the chips whenever the count left a job out. The list the chip opens names only the jobs it counts.
- **Docs**: guide *see everything on a development* (the chips paragraph), `GLOSSARY.md` → *Plan sets by part*, `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` → the Development page's route line.

## Not changed

- The chip for the parts with no set, + Part, + New set, the ask's title words (`askTitleForSitePlans`), and what a job's Documents tab offers.
- No migration: `billing_only` has been on `jobs_ledger` since GC mode, and `kind` since v2.6329.
