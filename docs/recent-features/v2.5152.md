# v2.5152 — GC mode: a trade partner sees its job in its portal (2026-10-10)

P5c-1 of the trade partner portal's real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/PORTAL_REAL_BUILD.md`; plan `to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/portal-p5.md`, both on branch `spike/gc-mode`). The job's Building rows, read only. No migration.

- **The slice** (`gc-trade-portal`, `_shared/gcTradePortalSlice.ts`): on the trades **awarded** to the company (never those it was only asked to quote), `submittals`, `submittalHolds`, `submittalRounds`, `rfis` (with `mine`, never who asked, an RFI's cost or change order, nor an RFI on our own work), `rfiHolds` and `punch` (never one taken off); on its statements of work, `draws`, `drawLines`, `lineReports` (each line's newest) and `changeSends`. `changeOrders` widens to the change orders sent to it, carrying `package_id`, `reason` and, on those sent to it only, the `description` it signs. `sows` gains `accepted_on` and `gc` gains `closed_on`. Never who of ours recorded, added or checked a row, nor the email that carried it.
- **The mapper** (`tradePortalState.ts`) reads them through Building's own row mappers: `sowWithDraws` (each line's reported and billed percent, the draws, the pay applications sent back, the charges), `submittalsFromRows`, `rfisFromRows`, `punchItemOf`, and each sent change order's `tradeChange`. `backChargeOf` moves to `backChargeRows.ts`, so the portal's mapper reads `drawRows.ts` without an import loop.
- **The page**: `GcTradePortalJob.tsx` under a signed statement of work: the report (each line's percent done and paid through, the punch list, the submittals it owes, its draws and the totals) and its questions while we build. On a job we are building they take the place of the closed bidding questions. Nothing is pressed yet: the punch list, submittals and questions get their kinds in P5c-2, the report, pay application and waivers in P5c-3.
- **The sample** job gains a report, a paid draw, a punch item, a submittal and an answered RFI.
- **Tests**: `tradePortalSlice.test.ts` (the job's never-sees values and its rows on its awarded trades only), `tradePortalState.test.ts` (through Building's mappers), `tradePortalSample.test.ts`, `GcTradePortal.job.render.test.tsx`.
- **Docs**: `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`, `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`, `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`; guide `share-a-trade-partner-its-portal`.
- **Deploy** (the lead): `gc-trade-portal`.
