# v2.8255 — Messages: the day a customer said is filled in when you write down their word (punch list #129, the leftovers) (2026-11-21)

Punch list #129 (`to-dos/estimator-money-word/README.md`), the card's third follow-up, *the heard-day offer in Messages*. The card asked one thing of it: when the offer is drawn, its press opens the same sheet with the day filled in. Since v2.5649 a customer's own line carries **Write down their word** under it, and the sheet opened with an empty date however plainly the line named one. No migration, no edge function, nothing in the kernels copy.

## What changed

- **The reader** (`src/lib/messages/voicemailRead.ts`): `daySaidIn(text, at, now)` is the day reader that sat inside the voicemail chip's `heardDay`, lifted out so any line's own words can go through it. It reads from the line's own day, so *tomorrow* in Tuesday's text is Wednesday whenever it is read, and `now` only marks a day gone by. `heardDay` calls it and returns what it returned.
- **The kernel** (`src/lib/messages/wordFromLine.ts`): `heardDayOnLine(line, person, now)` gives the day for a customer's own **text** or **voicemail** (the transcript as the line's body keeps it, in quotes), and nothing for a day that has gone by. The words are the kernel's: `heardDayWords` (*They said ⟪day|Fri Nov 27⟫.*, the day marked as a value), `heardDayTip` (the press's hover) and `heardDaySheetLine` (*Fri Nov 27 is read from their text. Change it if they meant another day.*).
- **The slot** (`useFollowUpThreadSlots`' `underLine`): beside the press, *They said* and the day in bold (`ValueSentence`, values in place). The press reads the customer's jobs and lands as before (the line's job, the one job with money open, a pick, or the words that say why not); the sheet then opens with the day.
- **The sheet** (`SetPromisedPayDateModal`): a new `heardYmd`. The date box opens on it, while `initialYmd` stays what the board shows, so **Clear date** and *a new date counts the earlier one as broken* still speak of the board's date. The slot passes the kernel's line as the sheet's `contextLine`.
- **Tests**: `voicemailRead.test.ts` (`daySaidIn` on a text, a day gone by, no words, no day, a bad instant); `wordFromLine.test.ts` (a text, a voicemail's quoted transcript, *tomorrow* read three days on, a day gone by, the day itself, no day from a call, an email, a missed call, our own text or a trade partner, and every word against the plain words rules); `useFollowUpThreadSlots.render.test.tsx` (the day beside the press as a worked-out value, the sheet opening with it and the line, the board's date still answering Clear date; no day under a call, an email or a text that names none); `SetPromisedPayDateModal.render.test.tsx` (`heardYmd` fills the box and is what Save writes).
- **Docs**: the guides *reply to a person from their thread* (**Write down their word**) and *see where a job you bid stands on money*; `MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md`'s slots; the Glossary's R3 line; the app directory's Messages page; the card.

## Read closer than the card

- **A text and a voicemail, not a call or an email.** A call's line is Quo's summary of both sides, so a day in it may be ours. An email's body carries the dates of every mail quoted under it. Both keep the plain press with an empty date.
- **The promise side only.** The reader looks forward (a weekday is the coming one, a date with no year is the next one), so a day read is not before the line, and one that has gone by is dropped. It can only be a day they will pay by; *They say they paid…* keeps its empty day.
- **An offer, never a write.** The press under every customer line already opened the payment sheet; a day beside it adds no claim that the line is about money. A person who knows the text is about a visit does not press, or changes the side or the day in the sheet.
- **Cut 2's row stays undrawn.** `message_promises`' offered row, a voicemail's *Keep the day*, Follow up's *A day was heard* press and `write_down` are still on no screen, and nothing writes an offered row. A promise saved through the sheet is the Pipeline's (`job_payment_promises`), as since v2.5649, so the thread's move line does not read it as *until Fri · payment*. Whether a word written from a line should also mark the line's promise is an owner's call, noted on the card.
- **Once written, the day still shows.** The day is read from the line, not from what is on record, so the same line offers it again; the sheet's own *On record for this bill* list says the word is there.
