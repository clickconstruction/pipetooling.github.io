# v2.3684 — A claim set by hand follows the job: the GC run, the affidavit draft and Collections (2026-09-21)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. The two follow-ups left open by [v2.3682](./v2.3682.md), plus two gaps the exploration for them turned up.

## What shipped

- **Put a GC on notice** — `buildGcOnNotice` ([`gcOnNotice.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/gcOnNotice.ts)) takes `correctionFor(jobId)`; each job's `claimAmount` is `correctedClaim(openBalance, correction).claim`, with `openBalance`, `claimCorrected`, `claimDelta` and `claimOver` beside it, so the sort, the totals, the per-row preview and the approve draft all read the corrected figure with no further change. [`useGcOnNoticeData`](../../src/hooks/useGcOnNoticeData.ts) loads the corrections. [`GcOnNoticeModal`](../../src/components/jobs/GcOnNoticeModal.tsx) shows *set by hand* and the delta under the amount (red, *over the balance — the leader decides*, when over); `approveAll` passes the typed per-month split, sends an over-the-balance claim to the leader as *claim_by_hand* even in spoken-word mode, and stamps a carried correction as looked at — approving the run is a person looking at every claim.
- **Collections** — `collectionsClaimGapWords` in [`lienClaimCorrection.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienClaimCorrection.ts): the part of the app's balance the notice does not claim, named as unsecured. [`JobsStagesTab`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsStagesTab.tsx) composes it into the Collections note line (table and cards) from the desk hook's `claimCorrectionsByJob`, which [`useLienDeskData`](../../src/hooks/useLienDeskData.ts) now loads in light mode too (one indexed select) so the board has it without the desk open.
- **Two gaps closed** — the notice drafted from the Affidavits pane (`draftFields`) and the run's fallback when an approved item has no stored draft (`lienDeskRun.ts`) both claimed the raw balance; both now claim the corrected one.
- Guide *send lien notices from the Lien desk* → "The claim, by hand" says where the correction follows.

## Verified
