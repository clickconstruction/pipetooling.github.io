# v2.7756 — Trips: the ground for putting an Extra trip on a bill (2026-11-18)

## Why

Punch list #391, PR 2 of 4. A trip marked Extra is work past the job's price, and nothing took it to a bill. The owner saw two builds side by side and picked the line: the trip becomes one line among a job's lines, and the Bill tab's own *Bill the N picked* bills it alone or the job's next bill carries it. This PR is the database's part. It carries no client code, so its push can follow the merge at any hour.

## What changed

- **`supabase/migrations/20261118060000_trip_bill_line.sql`** (`docs/migrations/20261118060000_trip_bill_line.md`)
  - `jobs_ledger_fixtures.trip_id`, nullable, `REFERENCES trips(id) ON DELETE SET NULL`. The mark lives on the line, never on the trip. Delete the line and the trip reads not billed again. Delete the trip and the line stays with its words.
  - `jobs_ledger_fixtures_one_line_a_trip`, a unique index where `trip_id` is set. One trip, one line.
  - `trip_put_on_job_line(p_trip_id, p_job_id, p_amount, p_name, p_words, p_dry_run)`, SECURITY DEFINER, shaped like `apply_job_discount`. It takes the four roles that may add a discount to a job, asks that the caller reads the trip and the job, locks the trip then the job, answers a trip that already holds a line as a duplicate, refuses a trip that is not Extra, takes a job of the trip or of its site, inserts one `work` line at the highest position plus one, writes the job total whole from the lines and the riders, links the job to the trip, and logs one financial History row. A dry run writes nothing and answers the same total before and after.
- **`supabase/tests/trips/50_bill_line.sql`** and **`scripts/pgtest-trips.sh`**: a fourth scenario in the trips bed, and this migration applied a second time with the other three.
- **`docs/ACCESS_CONTROL.md`**: who may press, under *Trips*.

## The card's defaults, as built

- **Default 2, who may press:** dev, master_technician, assistant and controller, the roles `apply_job_discount` allows. A primary reads every trip and is refused.
- **Default 5, a trip moved off Extra while its line exists:** allowed. The duplicate answer comes before the billing check, so the trip is still answered with its line.
- **Default 7, a job whose total is not its lines:** `apply_job_discount` has no case for the GC desk's billing job (`gc_projects.billing_job_id`, kept at `gc_owner_billing_revenue`). It would write that job's total from its lines. So this function refuses that job in words, as the card says to when there is no answer to copy.
- **Default 1, a planned trip:** the function does not check that a trip has gone out. The press is drawn only on a trip that is out now or back, in the client that follows.

## Things the card said to check

- **Positions.** The new line takes the highest position plus one, read under the job's row lock. The bed moves a line far down the list and sees the new line land past it.
- **The form's save keeps the mark.** The Job window upserts a line under its own id with the columns it knows, and `trip_id` is not one of them. The bed runs that upsert and the mark stays.
- **A combine.** `migrate_job_ledger_costs_and_delete` moves a job's lines with `UPDATE … SET job_id`, so a moved line keeps its `trip_id`. The bed moves a line the same way.
- **The locks.** `SET lock_timeout = '3s'` first. The column is a catalog change. The foreign key is added `NOT VALID` and validated after. The unique index is partial and empty.
- **Who reads a line** and **a covered line** are the client's, in the next PR.

## Left as `apply_job_discount` leaves it

A job that reads Paid and gains a line still reads Paid until the Job window is closed on it.
