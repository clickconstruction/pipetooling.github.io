# v2.6035 — The text back after a missed call plans on `call.missed` too (2026-10-28)

Punch list #195's live walk, the same afternoon v2.6012 and v2.6018 shipped. The owner called the company number from a phone on a ZZ test record and let it ring out: Quo's greeting answered, nobody picked up, the caller hung up without a message. The missed call landed on the thread (`rang everyone · no voicemail`), but no text went back and nothing was claimed: the line's Quo revision was 1, the function's edge log held one delivery answered 200, and Quo's own delivery log for the subscription (id 43696, enabled, every number, all ten events including `call.completed`) showed exactly one dispatch for the call, `call.missed`, delivered. Quo's reference says `call.completed` fires whenever a call ends and `call.missed` for an unanswered incoming call; in practice a call that reaches the greeting and hangs up gets only `call.missed`. v2.6012 planned on `call.completed` alone, for the voicemail flag it carries, so the commonest missed call never texted.

## What changed

- **`quo-webhook`**: `textBack` runs on whichever of `call.completed` (direction in) and `call.missed` arrives first for a call nobody answered; the claim in `missed_call_replies` keeps the call to one text, so the other event, and Quo's retries, do nothing. The voicemail line goes only when `call.completed` comes first and says a voicemail was left; a caller who leaves one otherwise gets the in-hours or after-hours line. The owner picked this over a one-minute sweep that would have held the text for the voicemail (2026-10-28).
- **Docs and words**: `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` quo-webhook, the kernel's header, the guide *text back a missed call* (the voicemail line's sentence) and this fragment. The card's words are unchanged.
- **Tests**: `missedReply.run.test.ts` gains the call that sends only `call.missed` (the text goes), and the call whose `call.missed` and `call.completed` both arrive (one text).
