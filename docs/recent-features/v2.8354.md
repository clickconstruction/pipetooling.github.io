# v2.8354 — Agreements: every paper with an outside company is on the list (2026-11-23)

Punch list #403, the third piece of the owner's first decision. One migration, `20261123083000_agreements_every_party_paper.sql` ([its page](../migrations/20261123083000_agreements_every_party_paper.md)). No edge function. It stands on v2.8348 (Take back) and v2.8352 (the tab knows *not sent*).

## The decision

The card's pick: the Agreements tab lists every paper that is with an outside party, of any kind and any state, a party on no list among them, and one that is not signed carries **Send** and **Take back**. The owner answered "Yes, all four" on 2026-11-22.

## What changed

- The view `agreements_on_file` lists a party's paper whatever its kind and state. It listed agreements that were sent or signed.
- A W-9, a certificate and a licence with an outside party are lines. A second tag says which.
- A paper made and never sent is a line. It reads *not sent* and shows the day it was made, under the **Not sent** chip and under Search.
- That line carries **Send** for the office, and **Take back** for the leader and a dev. Both were built in the two PRs before this one.
- A party on no list has no page, so the tab is the one screen their W-9 or unsent paper is on.

## What did not change

- Who reads a line. The office's four roles read every one. An estimator reads an outside company's and nobody's on no list, as their policy says.
- A person's own tax forms and unsent papers. They stay on People.
- A trade company's W-9 and certificate kept in GC mode. Only its agreement is a line, as before.
- The list the tab opens on: sent and signed.

## Order of the push

The client first. The tab learned the state in v2.8352, and an older client would call an unsent paper *Voided*. The migration is pushed once that client is live. The view's columns are the same, so no types PR follows.

## The guide

*find an agreement we sent or signed*: the tags gain the second tag, **A paper that was never sent** is a new section, and *What is not on this tab* and *What an estimator sees* say what is true now.
