# v2.7467 — Supply houses: interest and late fees, kept as their own bill and off the job's cost (2026-11-16)

PR 5 of the supply house payments train. The owner, 2026-11-16: "occasionally when we wait more than 30 days interest will be applied to our bills and we may have to pay that as well". His call on how it is kept: a bill of its own, tied to the bill that ran late, and off the job's cost.

## What changes

- **+ The house added interest or a late fee** in **Record a payment**, under the bills: an amount, and **Which bill was late (optional)**. The amount joins **Amount sent**, and the press reads *Record $1,258.60 on 1 bill and interest*.
- **The interest is a bill of its own**, made and paid by that payment in one statement. Its row leads with **Interest on S4398120** (or **Interest or a late fee** when no bill is named), wears an **Interest** chip and says *not a job cost*.
- **Interest not paid yet** is entered with **Add Invoice** and the tick **This is interest or a late fee**, with the same late-bill pick. It then waits with the other bills.
- **Kept off every job's cost, by the database.** An interest bill takes no job and no bid, and a bill on a job cannot be marked interest until the job is off. The form takes the jobs off as it saves. It is not counted as a bill missing its job on Held for suppliers, and an aging cell's window offers it no **Pick a job ›**.
- **The year's line.** Under the Accounts payable total: *Interest and late fees in 2026: $61.20 · Cedar Fork Supply $42.60 · Pecan Supply $18.60*, from every interest bill billed in the year, paid or not.

## How it is held together

Migration `20261117020000_supply_house_interest.sql` (its note is in `docs/migrations/`): `is_interest` and `late_invoice_id` on `supply_house_invoices`, two guards, and `record_supply_house_payment` with one more argument, `p_interest`. An interest row stays `document_kind` `invoice`, so every reader and an older client treat it as the payable bill it is.
