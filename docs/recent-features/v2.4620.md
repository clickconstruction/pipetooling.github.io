# v2.4620 — Help: the supply house and attorney guides match the screens again (2026-10-05)

PR 3 of the lien-guide freshness pass ([v2.4614](v2.4614.md) and [v2.4617](v2.4617.md) are PRs 1 and 2). It fixes facts in six guides about supply houses, an owner's records and the attorney, and changes no structure. Each fix was read against main's code, then given an adversarial second read.

## The fixes, by guide

- **see which supply houses are owed on a lien job**: the card sits under the four *gates* (`LienDeskModal.tsx`). The shared text lists up to twelve jobs and sums the rest, and the email lists all of them (`_shared/lienDeskStatus.ts`).
- **see which paid jobs still owe supply houses**
  - An unallocated invoice gets its job under *Which job* (`SupplyHousesTab.tsx`).
  - After *All*, three filter chips match the row chips, and *Awaiting customer* also lists the Floating jobs (`MaterialsJobAccountsTab.tsx`).
  - The filter chips are drawn plain, in both places the guide shows them.
- **ask a supply house what it shows owed**: the button reads *Save N answers* with more than one answer (`SupplyHouseAskSheet.tsx`). A saved answer's door reads *Change what they told us…* (`MaterialsJobAccountsTab.tsx`).
- **give an owner our records for their property**: *Owed at this property* is the owed line, and one line under it gives the job total and what was paid. *Record it as sent* is faded, not grey (`LienOwnerRecordsModal.tsx`).
- **review a collections account before it goes to your attorney**
  - On a computer the Legal button sits at the Collections header's right, just before *Lien desk* (`JobsStagesTab.tsx`). Billed carries Accounts Receivable on its own header.
  - Most packet lines and section titles carry a door, not all (`legalPacket.ts`, `LegalDeskModal.tsx`).
  - The Paper tab's old lien clock (replaced in v2.3787) is now described as *Where each job stands*, with each job's notice and affidavit dates, and *The paper that went out* (`LegalDeskModal.tsx`).
  - The release button is dark.
  - The account's header shows who asked for a dev.
  - The firm's steps, questions and payments count under *On the matter* as waiting on you, and its fees and costs list under *Attorney fees and costs*.
- **send a customer account to your attorney**
  - The Billed row's *Collections* button; *Flag for collections* is on the phone cards' menu (`StagesUnifiedJobRow.tsx`, `JobsStagesCardList.tsx`).
  - The note is optional (`StagesCollectionsConfirmModal.tsx`).
  - Collections sits below Billed Awaiting Payment, not at the bottom.
  - The Legal button's place, and most items carry a door.
  - The recipients' *Email now* chip and a closed matter's *Settled* chip take the colours the desk draws.
  - The release sheet's fourth state, *Not emailed*.
  - Two buttons are dark.
  - A matter closes as Settled the moment the firm records that step (`submit-legal-portal`).
