# v2.5365 — Job activity shows the customer’s calls, texts and emails (2026-10-12)

Cut 10's J2 for the Messages build (GC Reviews helper 7, directed by the lead session GC Review): screen 5 of `to-dos/gc-mode/messages/phone-mockups.html` on `spike/gc-mode`, in the colors of `customer-messages-prototype.html` v0.3. J1's kernel (v2.5355) draws the lines, and J0's indexes (v2.5353, `20261014011000`) serve the read.

- **`loadJobMessages(job)`** (`src/lib/messages/messagesIo.ts`): reads the lines tied to the job, with cut 5's `duration_seconds`, `summary` and `transcript`. While the job is unpaid (`jobTakesUntiedLines`) it also reads the people put on it (`contact_jobs`) and, for the customer's among them, their lines tied to no job since the job began. A trade's or a supplier's untied line, and an untied team note, stay off. It is read as the signed-in user, so the rows' policy shows only the office team anything.
- **`jobActivityMessagesFromRows`** (`src/lib/jobs/jobActivityMessages.ts`): the rule above, kept pure, with our names from the team's and the person's first name on theirs. `JobActivityMessageFacts` gains `withName` for the thread link.
- **`useJobMessages`** (new): the read when a row's activity opens and again when the job's status moves, null while out. A failed read leaves the feed as it was. Messages has no realtime, so a new line shows on the next open.
- **`JobActivityView`**:
  - For `GC_OFFICE_TEAM` it reads the messages and numbers them with the job's items.
  - The feed waits for both reads, so no line's number moves once it shows.
  - The pills are `JOB_ACTIVITY_FILTERS`, with **Customer** counted by `countJobActivityLines`.
  - Anyone else reads nothing and sees the feed as before.
- **`JobActivityFeed`**:
  - The customer's lines carry an orange tint and bar (`--bg-orange-tint` and `--border-orange`), and hers in a deeper one (`--bg-orange-100` and `--text-orange-700`).
  - The channel is a small word before the words, with an email's subject in bold.
  - *not tied yet* and *only the team sees this* are small words.
  - Every message line opens, to its whole words and **Open the thread with Dana ›** (`messagesThreadHref`).
- **Docs**: the guide `open-job-activity-full-screen`, `GLOSSARY.md`'s Job activity ledger and `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`'s Messages cut 1 paragraph.
