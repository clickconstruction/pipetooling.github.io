# v2.6470 — The Job Summary cost print lists only the card charges its total counts (2026-11-03)

Stage A step 11 of the Jobs page map ([`JOBS_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md`](../JOBS_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md)), after v2.6465 paged the same block. The Job Summary's cost breakdown print (`printJobSummaryCostBreakdown` in `Jobs.tsx`) read a job's card lines with its own inline copy of the per-job loader whenever the row's cache (`jobSummaryMercuryAllocationsByJobId`) had no entry for the job. The print button sits in the expanded row, so that happens when the row's card total is $0 or less (the expanded-row effect loads the lines only above $0) or when Print is pressed before the row's read finishes.

- **The print reads through the row's own loader** (`loadJobSummaryPrintMercuryRows` in the new `lib/jobs/jobSummaryMercuryRows.ts`): the cache when it has the job, else one call to `fetchMercuryJobAllocationsWithAttributionForJob`, mapped by `jobSummaryMercuryRowsFromAllocations`. `useJobSummaryData` now fills the cache through the same mapping, so the two cannot drift again. The 80-line inline read and attribution join leave `Jobs.tsx`.
- **What changes on the paper**: Internal Transfers (`cardChargeAllocationFilter`) drop out of the printed card lines, as they already did from the card total and the open row. Before, a transfer on the job was listed in the Card charges table and counted on a person (or Unassigned) in the Person summary, so a job with a card total also showed "Per-person card totals may not match job card total". A job whose only card line was a transfer printed card charges in the Person summary under a $0.00 total. The lines now carry `linkedToSupplyInvoice` and `isFuel` as the open row's do; the print reads neither today.
- **The parity check, field by field**: the same table, `job_id` filter, `created_at` then `id` order, `fetchAllRows` paging with `withSupabaseRetry` on each page, and the same allocation read label (`job summary print mercury allocations`). The attribution join is the same (person before user, names from `people` and `users`, failing soft to no name), and so is a failed read (the print says "Card charge line detail unavailable."). The loader also selects `mercury_account_id` and `mercury_category`, which the mapping drops, and reads the card-charge exclusions (accounting buckets, invoice links) and the fuel tags and labels. Each of those reads fails soft: a role that cannot read the buckets keeps every line, and fuel stays unflagged.

Tests: the new `jobSummaryMercuryRows.test.ts` runs the real loader against a mocked database. It covers the mapping, a cached row that reads nothing, a read that drops a $500 Internal Transfer and flags an invoice-linked charge, a failed read, and two prints through `buildJobSummaryCostBreakdownHtml`: the card lines add up to the $70 total with no mismatch warning, and a job whose only line is a transfer prints no card charges against anyone. Both prints fail when the transfer is kept.
