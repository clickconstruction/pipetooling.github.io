# v2.3925 — Workflow: what a folded stage card says has tests (2026-09-27)

Client only; no migration, no edge function, no screen change. Punch list **#46** row 9 (`to-dos/decomposition-queue.md`): the Workflow map's step 3, second of three PRs — the stage-card kernels (`docs/WORKFLOW_PAGE_ARCHITECTURE.md` → "Stage-A pure-logic inventory"). Built on v2.3921.

## What shipped

- **`src/lib/workflow/stageCardDefaults.ts`** — `isRowDefaultCollapsed` (pending and finished steps start folded; in progress and rejected start open), `isStepEmpty(step, lineItemCount)` (pending, never started, nobody assigned, no notes, no line items — the gate on type-the-name delete) and `isSectionDefaultExpanded`. `isStepEmpty` closed over the page's `lineItems`; it takes the count now, and the page keeps a one-line wrapper so its two call sites read as before.
- **`src/lib/workflow/stageDisplayItems.ts`** — `buildStageDisplayItems(steps, oldStagesCollapsed)`: every step, or one summary row for the finished steps but the most recent. The rules the tests pin: finished is completed / approved / skipped, not rejected; "most recent" goes by `sequence_order`, not list position; the summary sits where the first old step was and carries the start of the first old step by order, even when that one never started; with fewer than two finished steps nothing is hidden.
- **`src/lib/workflow/stageCardPills.ts`** — `stageCardPills(step, items, now?)` → day count and its `[3 days] ` prefix (days open for a step in progress, start-to-end otherwise), line-item count and total, the two word counts, and whether the Exp pill shows; `wordCount` and `daysPillPrefix` beside it. The item total is `sumAmounts` from v2.3907. The two notes headers ("Notes for Tech (N words)", "Notes for Office (N words)") call `wordCount` instead of repeating the split.
- **`src/pages/Workflow.tsx`** — 4,188 → 4,148 lines. The pill styles, the due-state colouring and every line of JSX stay where they were.
