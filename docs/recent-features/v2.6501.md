# v2.6501 — A person with no login holds their own days and Charged as (punch list #254) (2026-11-04)

Charges by crew (v2.6441–v2.6490) priced a job's labor from crew-days, and a crew-day was built from clock sessions, which belong to a login (`clock_sessions.user_id` → `users` → `auth.users`). Several people on one development's crew were paid from bank rows and never given a login, so they could hold no day and no standing, and the job priced short by every day they were out; the handoff of 2026-11-04 listed it as the fourth open item. The owner's rule (2026-11-04): "we want to be able to track people even if they have no login".

## What changed

- **A People record holds a standing and days** (`20261104180000_people_pay_record_days.sql`, `docs/migrations/20261104180000_people_pay_record_days.md`): `people.charge_standing` with the four values of `users.charge_standing`; `people_pay_record_days` — one row a person, a job and a day, hours and a note naming the source, `origin = 'imported'`, `approved_at` / `approved_by`, `created_by` — readable by `is_dev() OR has_payroll_access()`, with no write policy at all. Two `SECURITY DEFINER` doors: `import_person_days(p_rows jsonb)` (devs only, modeled line for line on `import_clock_sessions`: every row checked, a row that fails named with its reason while the rest go in, a rerun skipping what is there) and `approve_person_days(p_ids uuid[])` (the hours-approval gate, stamping the two columns and nothing else). **Pay never reads these rows.** `charge_standings()` is dropped and made again with `person_id` beside `id`, answering every People record with no login as a row of its own.
- **The standing rule in one kernel** (`src/lib/charges/chargeStandings.ts`): a People record linked to a login reads the login's standing, an unlinked one reads its own (`standingForPerson`, `standingLookupForPeople`); on a crew an unlinked person is keyed `person:<people.id>` (`crewKeyForPerson`, `personIdOfCrewKey`); `standingsById` answers logins alone and `standingsByPersonId` records alone. `standingLookup` on the Job Summary answers an unlinked record by its id and never a login of the same name; `loginLookup` reads the login rows alone.
- **The readers**: `loadCrewSessions` (`jobChargesRead.ts`) reads the job's approved pay-record days beside its approved clock sessions and hands both to `buildCrewDays` as `CrewSessionInput` rows, each pay-record row `imported`, so the Charges window, the Statement of charges and the charged hours statement draw them as *from the pay records* with no second path. The Job Summary (`Jobs.tsx`) reads every row's approved pay-record days in one read (`loadPayRecordDaysForJobs`) and folds them into the breakdown `ledgerChargesFor` prices (`payRecordBreakdown`), keyed by the People record's id so a linked person's days join their clock days on the crew-day; a read that fails leaves the column unpriced with its reason.
- **The screens**: Who counts as what (`WhoCountsAsWhatList.tsx`) lists the People records with no login in a group of their own — *People with no login · their days come from the pay records* — with the same select, writing `people.charge_standing`; one who left stays listed while a standing prices their past days. The person's desk (`PersonDeskAccessSection.tsx`) shows the same *Charged as* row for an unlinked record. The uncharged callout (`UnchargedCallout.tsx`) links a `person:<id>` key to the People record, and the Job Summary's loginless note now reads *Ana has no login. Set their Charged as on the People record, or in Who counts as what.* instead of asking for a link.
- **Docs**: the guide *set what a crew's hour is charged* gains *Count a person who has no login*; the Glossary's crew-day entry; `docs/ACCESS_CONTROL.md`'s standing paragraph and the pay-record table.
- **Tests**: `chargeStandings.test.ts` (the standing rule, the crew key); `crewDays.test.ts` (a day mixing a login and a no-login person as one crew, the pay-record row marked imported); `ledgerCharges.test.ts` (an unlinked person priced by their record's standing, their pay-record days beside the clock's people, the new loginless words, a record never reaching a login by name); render smokes of Who counts as what with an unlinked person, the callout's People-record door and the desk's row; the SQL bed `supabase/tests/typed_hours/60_person_days_scenarios.sql` (the import door's refusals and rerun, the approval door's gate with nothing in the pay, `charge_standings()` answering the record).

## Decisions

- **One rule, one kernel.** A linked record reads the login's standing, so a person who later gets a login is set in one place; the record's own standing speaks only while there is no login.
- **Pay stays out.** The rows are priced to the customer and never paid: no `people_hours` row, no crew-job sync, no reader in payroll.
- **The real crew's rows stay outside the repo.** The owner's draft is imported on her word, later; this PR carries made-up names only.
