# v2.4886 — Help: the email and settings guides match the screens again (2026-10-07)

PR 4 of the help-guides freshness pass after the lien guides (punch list #91, Helper 10). It follows [v2.4860](v2.4860.md), [v2.4882](v2.4882.md) and [v2.4884](v2.4884.md). Two read-only readers checked the card's four Emails and settings guides, plus *put someone in read-only training mode*, against main's code. Punchlist asked for the training-mode rewrite after [v2.4877](v2.4877.md). Every fix was re-read in the code before it was written. Words only.

## The fixes, by guide

- **see what the team sees**
  - The count line reads *26 emails · 26 render live* (`teamEmails.ts`). There have been 26 rows since v2.4311, and every one has rendered live since v2.4182.
  - So the *shows the real one* and *next release* chips and the *Only what doesn't render yet* toggle never appear, and their paragraphs are gone.
  - Three template emails read Email templates & testing: the invitation, the sign-in link and the stage notice. The task reminder's words are fixed in code (`teamSampleEmails.ts`).
  - The four digests keep their real-one buttons under the sample.
  - *Change who gets it →* lands on a card only for the streams that have one. *Contract for signature* opens Contracts & terms. The streams' cards are named as Emails & reports prints them.
- **see your email schedule**
  - The Digests and Every report tabs went in v2.3595. Email reports is one row per person, with Digest chips and an Every report column (`EmailReportsModal.tsx`).
  - The payment lists are bells on the Pipeline's section headers since v2.4527, and also in ☰ Section tools. The Estimates list keeps its ⚙.
  - The subscriptions list gains **Ready to Bill**. The colours are described by kind. A dimmed block can also mean this week's send already went out, and the block's three lines are quoted.
  - Assistants and controllers can set up Email reports too.
- **set up my profile and notifications** (settings basics)
  - **View as…** is dev only, and **Punch list** is dev and leader (`settingsRailDoors.ts`, `punchListAccess.ts`).
  - The name lock shows on sub and helper accounts, with the note quoted as printed.
  - Recent pushes are on **Activity logs** (System), not a Notifications tab. Devs see the emails 25 at a time.
  - There is no in-app *Add to Home Screen* helper; the guide points to the browser's Share menu.
  - The recording runs 20 seconds, not 30. See *Left*.
- **put someone in read-only training mode**
  - Three people can switch it, as `docs/ACCESS_CONTROL.md` → *Read-only (training) mode* has it: a dev, a controller, or a leader approved for pay (`personDeskGates.ts`, the column guard). The guide's roles gain master and controller, and the Account box's hover is quoted.
  - The two places are named: the **Training** box on People → Users → **Account**, wide screens, on a row with a login; and the **Read-only** box on the person desk's **Training mode** row.
  - The + Hire checkbox and **Send sign-in email** are a dev's. The keyword *active accounts* is gone, since that window retired in v2.4348.
  - `docs/ACCESS_CONTROL.md` → *Read-only (training) mode*: the self-flag sentence no longer says only devs can change the flag, which its own *Who* line contradicted.
- **share a customer their portal**
  - Visit requests land in the Dispatch inbox, and bid requests in the Estimator inbox (`submit-portal-request`).
  - A payment with no bill first covers work no sent bill covers, then the oldest sent bill (v2.4534, `_shared/paymentAttribution.ts`).
  - A job in Billed with no bill line shows its remainder. The office view is **Who owes what**.
  - Portal request emails are a dev's, under **Settings → Emails & reports → Portal requests**.
  - The button is **PAY ONLINE** on dark, and **Stop showing** is dark too. The partial chip is *☐ owner sees some bills*.
  - Each job sits under a band with its trade, number and address, and *owner:* beside the AS GC tag. *Jobs on this statement* runs newest first.
  - The Stripe footer prints the full address, and is left off a typed bill-to email or an over-long footer.
  - The example check carries its number and year. An *other* payment shows no method word. *Bill this to us instead* and *Remind the owner for us* are capitalised, under **Ask the office ›**.
