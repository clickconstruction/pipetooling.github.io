# v2.8014 — Contracts and clauses: the ground for a paper built from settings (2026-11-19)

Punch list #389, step 1 of eight, the first half: the migration and the kernel. No screen changes. The clause screens come in the next PR, after this migration is pushed.

**Push**: `20261120050000_paper_settings.sql`, from `main` after the merge. It is safe under the app and the functions deployed today, which call the two replaced functions with the old names and get the defaults.

## What changed

- A Contract Book entry can carry a **settings list** (`contract_template_documents.settings_def`): each setting with its choices from loosest to strictest, the blanks a clause may hold, and the named levels. It is null on every paper today.
- A clause can be tied to one choice of one setting (`when_opts`), and can be one of several wordings of one place in the paper (`slot`).
- The database's assembly, `contract_paper_clauses`, takes a job's picks and typed blanks. For a paper built from settings it keeps the clauses the picks ask for, fills the blanks, and refuses in words when the paper cannot go.
- A paper's revision can be **marked as read by an attorney**, by name and day. The mark names the revision, so a later publish leaves the paper unread again.
- A copy of a paper built from settings is refused when it is made. Nothing carries a job's settings yet, so such a paper goes to nobody until step 3.

## The kernel

`src/lib/contracts/paperSettings.ts` reads and checks a settings list, says whether a clause is kept for a job's picks, fills blanks, and names what stops a paper. `assemblePaper` in `assemble.ts` takes the picks and the typed blanks, and orders a wording where its place is.

The database and the kernel are twins. One list of cases lives in `supabase/tests/paper_settings/20_scenario.sql`. The SQL bed runs it through the database, and `paperSettings.cases.test.ts` reads the same list out of that file and runs it through the kernel.

The twin test found one drift that was already there: the exhibit's trades were trimmed by the database and not by the kernel. The kernel now trims them.

## What it does not do yet

- No screen sets a settings list, ties a clause or marks a read. That is the next PR.
- The edge functions do not pass settings. `clausePaper.ts` takes its own refusal in the next PR, and the three functions that import it are deployed then.
