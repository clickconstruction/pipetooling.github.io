# v2.5196 — GC mode: a trade partner can say it will be late, give its crew and mark its look-ahead, behind the scenes (2026-10-10)

The schedule's PR 14a (`to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/schedule-pr14.md`, on branch `spike/gc-mode`, co-signed by gc 10 and, on the portal's half, gc 3): a trade's four writes from its portal, on PR 3's tables. One migration, `20261010160000_gc_schedule_trade_writes`; no screen. The Portal's P5d-ii adds their kinds to `submit-gc-trade-portal`.

- **`gc_trade_say_late`**: a late notice on the company's own awarded line on a job being built, not done. Under way and done read as `lateDoor` reads them (the actual days, or the line's percent: the open send-back's `we_see`, else the newest report, so a trade that reports through pay applications reads under way). Under way, a new finish; not started, a new start, the bar moving whole and keeping its length. The day after the one it changes and not before today, a reason from the five and a sentence. Returns the notice's id.
- **`gc_trade_keep_day`**: the company keeps its day after the office pushed its notice back, while the notice stands (not taken by a move, not replaced, the bar where it found it).
- **`gc_trade_set_crew_count`**: people a day for its own trade, this week or the next two, 0 to 50; the same as the count that stands keeps nothing.
- **`gc_trade_mark_lookahead`**: its own line done or not done with a reason, this week or last week, on a bar that runs that week, until our superintendent checks it.
- Each is `SECURITY INVOKER`, granted to the service role only, with the link's company first, and raises the portal's keys with their words as `DETAIL`. The eight new keys wait in `gcTradeSubmit.test.ts`'s `WAITING` for P5d-ii. All are records: none touches the plan or its version, and each day is the company's.
