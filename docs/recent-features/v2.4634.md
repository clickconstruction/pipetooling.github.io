# v2.4634 — Lien desk: § Rules opens the rules in a window you can search (2026-10-06)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. The owner (2026-10-05): *when a user clicks on § Rules in the top right hand corner, I would prefer it not open a new page but a searchable modal with the contents of the rules.*

## What changed

- **`LienRulesDoor`** is a button, not a link: it mounts `LienRulesModal` (new) over whichever surface drew it (z 1200, above the Lien window's 1100), keeping its `where` → cite (`LIEN_RULES_DOOR`) so the window opens at the rule for what is on screen.
- **`LienRulesModal`** draws the guide *read the Texas lien rules the app follows* from `HELP_GUIDES` through `helpGuideMarkdownToSafeHtml`, gives the headings their ids (`applyHeadingAnchors`), scrolls to the cite's heading and marks it (`data-rule-opened`), and puts a find box above it. Typing (120 ms after the last key) clears the old marks, wraps every case-insensitive match in `<mark data-find>` and narrows the guide to the sections that hold one, with a count (*7 matches in 3 sections*); Enter / Shift+Enter walk the matches (`data-find-at`); *Show all* clears; Esc closes the window alone, in the capture phase. Links inside: a heading link scrolls, a guide or app link opens in a new tab. The foot keeps *Open the guide on its own page ↗* (`lienRuleHref`).
- **Kernel** `src/lib/helpGuideFind.ts` works on the drawn DOM, never the HTML string: `markFindMatches` (text nodes, skipping existing marks), `clearFindMarks` (unwrap and `normalize`), `narrowToMarked` (a section is a heading and what follows to the next heading of its rank or higher; an `h3` rule under an `h2` group, the group's lead paragraphs its own).

## Tests

- `helpGuideFind.dom.test.ts`: marks and clears (case, short queries, split text rejoined); narrowing keeps a rule under its group heading and hides the rest, off shows all; a group's own lead and a paragraph before the first heading.
- `LienRulesDoor.render.test.tsx`: the door is a button that opens the window at the surface's rule, not a page; the find box marks and narrows the real guide, Enter stands on a match, Show all restores, Esc closes the window and leaves the door.
