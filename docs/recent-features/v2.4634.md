# v2.4634 — Legal portal: the firm reads the facts on file, not a theory (2026-10-05)

Client plus `legal-portal` (its jobs select gains `contract_not_needed_at` and `contract_not_needed_reason`, so *no agreement needed* reaches the firm: redeploy `legal-portal` after merge); no migration. Item 4 of punch list #85 ([`to-dos/legal-portal-firm-ready/`](../../to-dos/legal-portal-firm-ready/README.md)). Stacked on item 3 (v2.4625, `legalFirmWords.ts`). Mockup: `mockup-4-facts-not-theories.html` beside the card — the matter card, Account's jobs, Paper's agreements and the exhibits, before and after, and the words for every fact.

## The defect

The firm's portal pleaded a theory. The matter card said *theory: Signed contract*; Account's Jobs table had a *Basis* column reading *signed contract* or *sworn account holds*; Paper's *Agreements and theory* table said *holds — bill received, GPS evidence, no dispute*; the exhibits said a job without an agreement *proceeds as a sworn account*. Whether a Rule 185 sworn account or a contract claim fits is the litigator's call, and software asserting it invites the first question in the meeting. The *Terms with Click* row showed the office's internal credit posture (*No new work past a broken promise*, *Winding down*), which is not a term the customer agreed to. The firm's print packet (item 1, PR #4601) read the facts back out of the desk's `swornMissing` strings with `startsWith`, so a wording change on the desk would have silently emptied them.

## What changed

- [`legalPacket.ts`](../../src/lib/legal/legalPacket.ts): each job line carries `record: { bill: 'sent' | 'not_sent' | 'none', field: 'gps' | 'no_gps' | 'none', dispute }`. `swornMissing` (the desk's sworn-account rule) is now derived from it, so the two cannot disagree.
- [`legalFirmWords.ts`](../../src/lib/legal/legalFirmWords.ts): `firmJobRecord(j)` → `{ onFile, notOnFile }` and `firmJobRecordWords(j)` → one line, for the page and the printed packet (*signed agreement 2026-04-22 by Pat Sample, bill sent, field record with a GPS location, no dispute logged*; *… Not on file: signed agreement*). An agreement sent and never signed, a bill line never sent, records without GPS, a logged dispute and the office's *no agreement needed* each read as a fact.
- `LegalFirmMatterView`: the theory line goes from the matter card; *Basis* becomes *On file* (facts, then *Not on file* in red); the Paper table is *Agreements* with no sworn column and a footnote pointing to Account; the exhibits' missing-agreement row states the fact only; the *Terms with Click* row is gone.
- The desk keeps its theory, its gap list and its *Terms* row: those are the office's triage.
- Guide `share-your-attorney-their-portal` says what the firm sees on Account; `GLOSSARY.md` → Legal desk says the portal shows the facts, never the theory.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **Soften "sworn account" instead of removing it?** No. It is a conclusion; the three facts behind it are what the firm needs, with their dates.
- **Keep parsing the gap strings?** No. The packet carries the facts as data and one kernel words them. The firm's print packet (item 1) imports `firmJobRecordWords` from `legalFirmWords.ts`; its local copy and its `startsWith` parse are gone, so paper and screen word a job the same way.
- **Show the invoice's terms or due days instead of the credit posture?** The payload's invoice rows carry no terms or due date, and the printed bill says *Upon receipt* for every job. Printing a due date we do not hold would be worse than none, so the row goes. Contract terms, interest and the fee clause are item 14, which adds them to the payload.
- **Say the facts twice, on Account and on Paper?** No. Account's Jobs table carries the record; Paper keeps the paper, with a footnote.
- **Is "crew on site with GPS" true?** No, and the words no longer say it. `hasGps` is only a recorded latitude on a report or clock session; nothing checks it against the job's address. The record says *field record with a GPS location* or *field records, no GPS location*, and adds *N clock sessions awaiting approval* when some are not approved (item 25 counts approved sessions only). Evidence carries the counts.
- **Does "no agreement needed" reach the firm?** It does now. `legal-portal` selects `contract_not_needed_at` and `contract_not_needed_reason`, so the coverage reads `not_needed`; Account, Paper's *Agreement* cell (`firmAgreementWords`) and the exhibits' line all say *no agreement of ours needed, per the office* with the reason, and the exhibits' *No signed agreement on …* leaves those jobs out. Until `legal-portal` is redeployed such a job reads *None on file*.
