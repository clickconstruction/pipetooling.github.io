# v2.5990 — The Legal desk from the Pipeline's ⋯ menu (2026-10-27)

Punch list #182, after the live walk of [v2.5984](v2.5984.md). On a phone with Mobile cards on, the Pipeline drew no door to the Legal desk. The board's cards draw neither the stage bar's scales nor the Collections header's ⚖ Legal, and its ⋯ menu listed the Lien desk only, as it always had. The Dashboard's line and the search reached the desk, but the board did not. The owner, asked on 2026-10-27: "Yes, add a Legal desk row under the Lien desk in the Pipeline's ⋯ tools menu, as its own small PR."

## What shipped

- **The row** (`JobsStagesToolsMenu.tsx`): **Legal desk** under **Lien desk**, wearing the ☰ menu's scales glyph, with the firm's acts awaiting the office as its count, the number the stage bar's scales carry. The desk is a page, so the row is a react-router `Link` to `/legal-desk` with `role="menuitem"`, the way the scales are a link: a ⌘-click opens the desk in a new tab beside the board. A press closes the menu first, as every door in it does. It shows on a computer too, in the same place.
- **The gate** (`JobsStagesTab.tsx`): `gates.legalDesk` is `legalDeskEligible` (`canManageCollections`, the office roles), the set the scales, the ☰ row and the header's ⚖ Legal already use, and the page's own gate. Unlike the scales it does not wait for Collections to have rows, as the Lien desk's row does not wait for notices. The tab passes `legalDeskCount` and `legalDeskTo={LEGAL_DESK_PATH}`.
- **Tests**: `JobsStagesToolsMenu.render.test.tsx` (+1: the row is a link to the page right under the Lien desk, with the scales and the count, and a press closes the menu; the full-gate and gates-off cases name it; the file now renders inside a `MemoryRouter`), `JobsStagesTab.render.test.tsx` (+1: on Mobile cards the ⋯ menu carries the link under the Lien desk). Both new cases fail on the old menu.
- **Words**: the guide *review a collections account before it goes to your attorney* (the ⋯ menu among the doors, the only one on a phone's Mobile cards), `GLOSSARY.md` → Legal desk, `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` → the Pipeline's doors, `JOBS_STAGES_TAB_ARCHITECTURE.md` → the ⋯ tools menu.

## Verify

As the dev on a phone with Mobile cards on: Jobs → Pipeline, press ⋯. **Legal desk** sits under **Lien desk** and opens `/legal-desk`. On a computer the same row is in the same menu, and a ⌘-click on it opens the desk in a new tab.
