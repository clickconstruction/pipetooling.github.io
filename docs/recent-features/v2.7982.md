# v2.7982 — Permits, Market: a place to keep the answer on a builder to call (2026-11-19)

Punch list #405, step two of the train in [`to-dos/permits-market-page/`](../../to-dos/permits-market-page/README.md). One migration, [`20261120031349_builder_call_answers`](../migrations/20261120031349_builder_call_answers.md), and nothing else.

## Why

Market's call list offers a few builders to call each week. A name that stays on the list after it is answered makes the list one nobody reads. So an answer takes the name off, and the next takes its place. The answer has to be the same for every person and on every device, and it needs a day to look again. That is a table and not the browser.

## What changed

- **`builder_call_answers`**, append-only: the builder's key, the answer (`pursue`, `not_for_us`, `back`), a line of why, the day to look again, the city it was given in, the lead a pursue made and its permit, who and when. Read and insert for `user_can_research_permits()`, an insert only as oneself. No update and no delete.

## Why it ships alone

The deploy refuses a client that names a table the database does not have yet (`check:calls-ahead -- --prod`), and it goes on refusing until the migration is pushed. A migration merged with its client would hold every deploy from the merge to the push. Merged alone, with no line of `src` naming the table, it holds nothing. The call list follows after the push.
