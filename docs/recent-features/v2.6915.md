# v2.6915 — Box photos: the Drive files follow any change of job, moved by the sweep (punch list #297, the engine) (2026-11-11)

v2.6885 moved a job's filed box photos into its Drive photo folder when the dev seat moved the job's Materials on site, by an ask of `photo-save` made after the rows landed. Three things were left: a whole-job combine carries the photos by the same rule and nothing asked for it, an ask that failed or ran out of its time was never finished, and a held file was never tried again.

The owner's ask of 2026-11-11 was to offer the move after a combine: do it then and watch, or carry on and let a nightly pass do it. The mock-up (`to-dos/box-photo-files-follow-the-job-combine.html`) weighed that choice against starting by itself, and the owner chose section 2: the files start by themselves, and watching is the choice. This PR is the engine under it. The finish line (the sentence after a combine, the strip with its count and a held file's Try again) is the next PR.

## The shape, and why

The counter tickets' pattern (migration `20261109190000`): the database marks the row when its job changes, and the sweep moves what is marked.

- **The mark is in the database**, not in a caller. A trigger covers a combine, the seat's move and any door added later.
- **The sweep that already runs does the work.** It wakes every five minutes while anything waits, so "later" is minutes, not tonight, and nothing depends on a page staying open.
- **Nightly became "a day later".** The owner's nightly pass is the retry of a held file: one in Drive's trash, or one Drive refused. It is due again 24 hours after the hold, so a file put right in the meantime follows without an ask.
