# v2.4049 — Contract sweep, full screen: the list, the agreement and its paper side by side (2026-09-28)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. PR 2 of 2, after [v2.4045](v2.4045.md) (the toggle in the shell).

## Why

A full-screen button that only stretches the 1100 px window buys little: the paper still sits under the form, so the office still scrolls to check what goes out. Full screen is worth the button when it buys a layout the window cannot hold. Mock-up: artifact `9Cp63b55vq9db8Yka2UsnA`.

## What shipped

- **[`ResponsiveModalShell`](../../src/components/ResponsiveModalShell.tsx)** — full screen, the panel is a flex column and the children sit in one bounded box (`flex: 1 1 auto; min-height: 0`) between the title bar and the footer, so a modal can fill the height instead of scrolling through it. The box is always there (`data-testid="modal-body"`), unstyled in a window, so the toggle never remounts the children — a scope typed before the press survives it.
- **[`JobsContractSweepModal`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsContractSweepModal.tsx)** — children are a function of `{ fullScreen }`. Full screen the grid is `380px · minmax(420px, 1fr) · minmax(400px, 1fr)` with one `minmax(0, 1fr)` row: the list loses its `68vh` cap and scrolls in its column; the pane scrolls on its own (`align-content: start`); the paper — `paper(fullScreen)`, `data-testid="sweep-paper"` — leaves the end of the pane for the third column, a flex column whose iframe grows to the room. In a window, and on a phone, nothing moves.
- **Help guide** `get-a-job-contract-signed`, *Clear the backlog*: *Give it the whole screen.*
- One render case: the paper sits inside the pane in a window; a press on *Full screen* puts it beside the pane in a three-column grid and keeps the pane's fields; *Back to a window* puts it back.

## Decided here

- **The list is a fixed 380 px** full screen: a job row needs no more, and the paper takes what is left. In a window the two columns keep their `5fr / 7fr` split.
- **The pane scrolls, the paper does not.** The form is the thing that grows (a long scope, the signing ways, the filing sheet); the paper is what you are checking.

## Verified

- `JobsContractSweepModal.render.test.tsx` (15), `ResponsiveModalShell.render.test.tsx` (2), `modalFullScreen.test.ts` (4), `helpGuideContent.test.ts`, `releaseNotes.test.ts`; `npm run typecheck`, eslint on the touched files, the theme token check.
- In the browser, as the dev login on production data (read only, 1440 × 900, this worktree's dev server): the button sits beside ⋯ in the window (1100 px, two columns, the paper under the form, the list capped at 68vh). Pressed, the panel fills 1440 × 900 with nothing to scroll, the grid reads `380px 490px 490px`, the list scrolls in its column (364 px more than fits), the pane fits without scrolling, and the paper is 716 px tall with the agreement's iframe at 660 px — letterhead, the work, the amount and payment line, and the terms in view at once. *Back to a window* restores the two columns and the 68vh cap, and the scope box still holds its text. Closed and reopened, the sweep came back full screen (`Back to a window` on the button); left in the window, the key is gone. Both PRs' buttons were exercised on this pass.
- Not verified: a phone width (the shell hides the button there by the same `useIsMobile` the sweep uses), and the light theme.
