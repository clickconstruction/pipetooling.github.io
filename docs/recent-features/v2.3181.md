## Small cents in the expanded row

- Every money render inside the detail row of [`JobsJobSummaryTab`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsJobSummaryTab.tsx) — the "Overhead — the math" summary and day lines, the cost-by-person table (rows, Unassigned, Total), the team labor / card / supply-house drill-down bodies and their footers, the Team labor · Sub labor · Parts · Total bill cost blocks, the parts-per-person table and its footer, the Mercury allocation and invoice-line tables — renders `<SignedAmountSmallCents>` (standalone `` `$${formatCurrency(x)}` `` children) or `<AmountSmallCents>` (a `$` in JSX text followed by `{formatCurrency(x)}`, including the `$` ⏎ `{…}` line-broken form). A script did the rewrite (balanced-paren expression capture, so `Number(x ?? 0)` and multi-line arguments survive); `tsc` proved every replacement landed in a JSX child position.
- Left as plain strings on purpose: `title` / `aria-label` tooltips, the method-B formula sentence (a template with other text), and every hours figure (`formatCurrency(b.hours)` and friends — those are hours, not money). The `JobSummaryChargesTimelineChart` axis labels are unchanged (a chart, not a table).

## "% of total" on the cost-by-person table

- **[`shareOfTotalLabel(rowTotal, grandTotal)`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobSummaryShareOfTotal.ts)** (pure, 3 tests): whole-percent share of the table's Total row; `<1%` for a real share under half a percent; `null` (shown as `—`) when the grand total is missing or zero or the row has nothing.
- New last column **% of total** after **Total** (header tooltip "This row's Total as a share of the Total row"): each person's row total ÷ `personSummaryFooterRowTotal`, the **Unassigned** row's card + supply ÷ the same, the **Total** row `100%`. The denominator is the footer's own figure, so with a person search active the shown rows still add to 100% of the shown total. Muted, tabular digits, `—` while card charges load.
