## What changed

- **A fourth sign in Your last jobs: changed** (`src/lib/paintedMap/paintedMapJobPicker.ts`). `pickerLastOrder` counts a day the person changed a job in the app. It places the job, and it names the job when it is the later day: the row says *changed* over the day. On one day a clock beats the schedule, which beats a change. Each job also keeps `there`, its newest clock or scheduled day, since a job named by yesterday's change may still be the job the start sheet opens on.
- **Coming up**, a new list between Today and Your last jobs: up to four jobs on the person's schedule in the next two weeks, soonest first, each with its day (`pickerSoonWords`: *tomorrow*, a weekday inside a week, then the day). A job under Coming up is not said again under Your last jobs.
- **The reads** (`paintedMapJobPickerIo.ts`, `loadPickerLists`). One more read, `job_activity_events` by `actor_user_id`, newest 200. It fails soft: a read that errors leaves the changed jobs out and the list is still drawn. The schedule's read now runs from two weeks ahead back, 260 rows, and gives both the days ahead and the days behind. `pickerPlan`, a pure kernel, sorts the rows into what to read whole. Eight reads to open where there were seven, and one more the first time on a page for the office job's setting.
- **The index** (`20261119110000_job_activity_by_actor_index.sql`): `job_activity_events_actor_occurred_idx` on `(actor_user_id, occurred_at DESC)`, partial on rows with an actor. The record had none by person, and its read rule runs a function a row. Before the index the read took 1.4 seconds for the heaviest office person and under 0.2 for everyone else walked.
- **The office job is no job to paint for.** `PaintPressJob.office` marks the company's office job (People → Overhead, `overhead_office_job_ledger_id_v1`, read once a page by `loadPaintOfficeJobId`). `paintPressLanding` never lands on it, and no list in the picker offers it. The search still finds it.
- **The guess is held to two weeks** (`PICKER_GUESS_DAYS`). With the office job out of Today, the sheet would have opened for one assistant on a job she clocked onto in August, over six jobs she changed that day. The sheet now opens on a last job only when the person was there in the last two weeks. An older one is asked about, with that job in the list. A job the person only changed, or has coming up, is never the guess.
