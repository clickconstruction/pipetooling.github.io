# v2.7138 — Lien desk Share: a held lien affidavit no longer counts as ready to file (2026-11-13)

Found in passing by the docs catch-up of 2026-11-13 (#7936). The Lien desk's **Share** said *ready to file* of a lien affidavit the Affidavits tab holds.

## What was wrong

The affidavit gained three holds in a week: the general contractor has no address on file (v2.6932, gate `contractor`), nobody answered the homestead question on a house (v2.6937, `homestead_answer`), and the owner of record is a public body (v2.6954, `public_owner`). The batch print learned them (v2.6957). The Share did not: `NEED_BY_GATE` in `src/lib/jobs/lienDeskShare.ts` mapped the four first gates and dropped any other, so a paper closed by one of the three alone reached `liensLine` with no need and was counted ready. The email's own line for that paper read *It is ready to file.*

## The fix

- `LienStatusNeed` (`supabase/functions/_shared/lienDeskStatus.ts`) is now one need for every key `affidavitGates` can return, seven in all.
- `NEED_BY_GATE` is a `Record<LienAffidavitGateKey, LienStatusNeed>` and the words are a `Record<LienStatusNeed, …>` (`NEED_WORDS`). A gate added later does not compile until it has a need, and a need until it has words. Nothing is filtered away any more.
- The words are the desk's own. A fact the office can enter joins the *It needs …* sentence: *the general contractor's address*, *the homestead answer* (`affidavitGateMissingWord`). The public owner is a hold told whole: *It is public property, so no lien can be filed.* (the hold line of `lienAffidavitFootBlockedSentence`).
- `parseLienStatusPayload` accepts the seven needs, at most seven on a lien, and still refuses one it does not know.

## The deploy, and why the page posts two lists

**`send-lien-desk-summary` needs a redeploy** for the email to carry the right count. The share sheet's text and the email's preview are drawn in the browser and are right from the merge.

The function is deployed by hand, after the page. The one running before this change refuses a payload that names a need outside the first four, and the whole email with it. So `sendLienStatusEmail` posts `lienStatusPayloadToPost(payload)`: each lien keeps in `needs` only the four first needs and carries the whole list as `allNeeds`. The old function reads `needs` and sends the email with its old count. The new one reads `allNeeds` when it is there. No press is refused in between.

## Tests

- `lienDeskShare.test.ts`: a paper closed by each of the three gates alone is not ready, a paper with every gate clear is, and every gate the desk can close has a need.
- `lienDeskStatus.test.ts`: the count, each need's words, the parser on the seven needs, and the posted shape read by a function from before the change.

Seen on a screen after the merge (2026-11-13, read-only walks of the live desk, signed in as the dev):

- **The count.** The message counted three fewer papers ready than the old page would have: one on public property and two waiting on the homestead answer. The email's preview said each one's sentence.
- **The contractor's address.** No paper on the desk was held by it that day, so it was seen with every GC's address blanked in the browser alone (the read's answer rewritten on the page, nothing written anywhere). Every paper under a GC then read *It needs the general contractor's address.*, the two contracted with the owner did not, and the message read *None is ready to file yet.*
- **The sent email.** One **Email me a test** went to the dev's own address through the redeployed function. It answered 200 and the send log reads delivered. The page posted the old four-need list beside `allNeeds`, as designed, and the deployed kernel renders that payload with the preview's count and sentences. The delivered mail itself was not opened.
