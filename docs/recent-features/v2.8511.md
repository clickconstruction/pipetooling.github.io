# v2.8511 — Document builder: publishing the insurance sheet asks first (2026-11-24)

Seen beside the #416 walk's fault on the Sub NDA (v2.8439) and left there: the insurance sheet's two publish presses asked nothing first. The card's own walks wrote it twice (*The publish asked nothing first*, *Publishing a job's sheet asks nothing first*). Diane's call of 2026-11-24: these ask too. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## The cause

`FilledBuilder` (`src/components/documentBuilder/FilledBuilder.tsx`) sent each press straight on. **Publish this job's sheet** went to `publishSheet` after the last save. **Publish to N jobs** read every job again and then published the usual and each ready job's sheet.

Reproduced before the change: the two new render tests, run against the old component, found no confirm window after either press.

## What changed

- `jobPublishConfirm` in `src/lib/documentBuilder/sheetScreen.ts` writes the question for one job's sheet, by the revision the press makes. From the second revision on: *Every sub you sent this sheet to sees revision 2 from now on. So does anyone with the link. A publish cannot be taken back. A later revision can only replace it.* The first has nobody reading yet, so it says what revision 1 becomes. The title names the job, and the window's press reads **Publish revision 2**.
- `usualPublishConfirm` writes the question for the usual, from the same plan the press and the rows read: *Your usual becomes revision 2. 1 job gets it now, and each sub sent that sheet sees the change. 1 job is held. Each held job's row says why.* With no job ready it says no job gets it now. The window's press is the bar's own, with its count: **Publish to 1 job**.
- Both ask through the shared `useConfirmDialog`, before the last save, the read of the jobs and the publish. Cancel reads **Not yet** and is the safe answer, so Enter does not publish. **Not yet** publishes nothing and leaves the draft as typed.
- A dead press opens no window: the button is disabled, and each handler reads the press again before it asks.
- When a job changed since the page read it, nothing is published and the press is drawn again, as before. The next press asks again, with the new counts.
- Help: *set the insurance a job's subs must carry* gains the window's steps under **Publish your usual** and **Set one job's sheet**.

## Tests

- `sheetScreen.test.ts`: both questions, for a first publish and a later one, one job and many, jobs held and none ready, each held to the plain-words rules.
- `FilledBuilder.render.test.tsx`: each press opens its window and publishes nothing; **Not yet** publishes nothing and keeps what was typed; a dead press opens no window. Every test that publishes now answers the window.
- `FilledBuilder.movedOn.render.test.tsx`: the publishes that are held because a sheet moved on are still held, through the window.
- Not seen: either window in a browser or on the live site. The render tests are jsdom's. The same shared window was seen live for the Sub NDA on 2026-11-24.
