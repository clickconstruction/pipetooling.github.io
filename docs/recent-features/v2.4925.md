# v2.4925 — GC mode: a trade partner’s portal can take what the company sends (2026-10-08)

P2b-i of the trade partner portal's real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/PORTAL_REAL_BUILD.md`; plan `to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/portal-p2b.md`, both on branch `spike/gc-mode`). It adds the write function and its rules. No database change. Nothing changes on screen: P2b-ii adds the presses.

- **`supabase/functions/submit-gc-trade-portal`** (new, `verify_jwt = false`) takes `POST { token, kind, … }` and checks, in order:
  - the honeypot;
  - the shape (`parseTradeSubmit`);
  - the sample token, which answers ok and writes nothing;
  - the token length;
  - the link (`linkOff`);
  - Spanish held (`spanishHeld`);
  - the hourly cap on free-text writes (`tooMany`).
  Then it calls P2a's `gc_trade_<verb>` with the link's company first. The verb's refusal key passes through with its status (`tradeErrorOf`).
- **`_shared/gcTradeSubmit.ts`** (new, pure) holds:
  - the twelve kinds and their fields;
  - `FREE_TEXT_KINDS` and the cap, `TRADE_HOURLY_CAP` = 10, counted from the questions, the people added, the quotes and the portal contact lines in the last hour;
  - every refusal key with its status;
  - a copy of `PORTAL_SPANISH_ON`, held equal to `portalI18n.ts`'s by a test.
- **`_shared/gcTradeLink.ts`** (new): the link rule, the raw token then its SHA-256 hash, with a turned-off link counting as no link. `gc-trade-portal` moves to it, so the read and the writes resolve a link one way.
- **The words**: 21 new refusal keys in `portalI18n.ts`, in English and Spanish, and `TRADE_ERROR_WORDS` / `tradeErrorWords` in `tradePortalPage.ts`. Where the page already says the same thing (open the plans first, answer each line, the schedule of values must add up), a refusal reads the page's words.
- **Tests**:
  - `gcTradeSubmit.test.ts`: every kind's shape, good and bad; the honeypot and the sample; the Spanish flag; the cap at 9 and 10; and the key mapping.
  - `gcTradeLink.test.ts`: raw first, then the hash, off, and unknown.
  - `tradePortalPage.test.ts`: words for every key, in both languages.
- **Docs**: `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` (a section and a TOC line, and gc-trade-portal's shared rule), `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` (the write path), `config.toml` and the dev-mcp catalog.
- **After merge**, the lead deploys `submit-gc-trade-portal` and redeploys `gc-trade-portal`.
