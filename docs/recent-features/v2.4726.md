Client only. ## What changed

- `LienDeskModal`: the page labels (`data-lien-desk-page-label="cover|notice|pay"`) wear the list's `.lienPileHead` / `.lienPileHeadBtn` and stack the way the pile titles do (v2.4651): the i-th sticks `stripH + i × 30` from the top once passed and `(n−1−i) × 30` from the foot while ahead, where `stripH` is the pinned strip's measured height (`stripRef`, read when `paneScrolled` flips or the job changes, since the strip's chips wrap); `relightPages` on the pane's scroll lights the label whose page is under the reader (`litPage`, the first by default); each label is a button, `scrollToPage` scrolls the pane so that page's label lands under the stack and lights it. The label's extra words (the wording note, the pay summary) ride after it, trimmed with an ellipsis.
