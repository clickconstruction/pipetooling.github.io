# v2.4251 — Procurement log: date boxes read MM/DD, open the calendar on a click, and take a typed 9/23 (2026-09-30)

Grace, with a screenshot of the Procure table's `mm/dd/yyyy` boxes: "make the entry field MM/DD and when the user clicks on it then show the date picker if they are not typing it in. I would like to make this as compact as possible." Built on v2.4243 (the short year and the *N days ago* line, her ask in another session the same day, merged while this was in hand), which kept the browser's date box and showed it over the cell on focus; the box itself could not be narrower than `mm/dd/yyyy` plus the calendar button.

- [`SubmittalProcurementPanel.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/SubmittalProcurementPanel.tsx) → `dateCell`: the box is now a **text box** (`input.procurement-date`, the same label) whose width is what it holds — five characters for `09/23`, more only while a year is typed or has to be said. Beside it, unseen, lies a `<input type="date">` (`.procurement-date-pick`, `tabIndex -1`, `aria-hidden`) that owns the browser's calendar.
  - **A click into the box opens the calendar** (`showPicker()` on the unseen input); a click in a box already in use only places the caret, so after Escape the date can be typed. Tab into a box opens nothing. A browser with no `showPicker` still takes the typed date.
  - **A pick saves at once** (`pickDate` → `readDateBoxEntry` from the shared `dateBoxEntry.ts`, v2.4253). **A typed date saves on Enter or when the box is left** (`commitTypedDate`); Escape drops what was typed; a box left as it was writes nothing. What does not read as a date is dropped with a toast — so the v2.4239 rule (a half-typed date is never written) now holds by construction, and the `typingIn` / `validity.badInput` handling it needed for the browser's box is gone.
  - On a touch screen (`@media (pointer: coarse)` in `index.css`) the unseen date input takes the tap, so the phone's own picker opens and no keyboard comes up.
- [`procurementLog.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/procurementLog.ts): three pure kernels, tested in `procurementLog.test.ts`; the plausibility rule they lean on is the shared `isPlausibleDate` (`src/lib/dateBoxEntry.ts`, v2.4253).
  - `nearestYearFor(month, day, asOf)` — the year that puts a month and a day nearest today (12/20 typed in January is last month; 01/05 typed in December is next month).
  - `readTypedLogDate(text, asOf)` — `9/23`, `09-23`, `9.23`, `9 23`, `0923` take the nearest year; `9/23/27` and `9/23/2027` say their own; nothing typed is empty; a day that does not exist or a year outside the log's window is bad.
  - `logDateRead(iso, asOf)` — what the box shows: `09/23` when a typed month and day would read back as the same date, `09/23/27` when the year has to be said, every digit (`02/12/0001`, in red, with *check the year*) for a year outside the window. Whatever it shows reads back through `readTypedLogDate` as the same date (pinned by a round-trip test).
- `index.css`: `.procurement-date-slot` and its two inputs replace v2.4243's rest/overlay rules and v2.4238's calendar-button rule (there is no calendar button in the box any more).
- Her follow-up while it was being built: "Released, Ordered, Lead, Expected, Required, Float, Delivered … all be centered and their contents centered." `thCenter` / `tdCenter` on those seven columns; the date slot is centred in its cell (`margin: 0 auto`) and the date reads centred in its box. Item, Submittal, PO and Note stay left.
- Kept from v2.4243: the soft line under each date (*house said*, *2 days ago*, *in 12 days*). `shortDateYear` is removed with its last caller.
- Guide *build a submittal package* → the procurement log's two date paragraphs.
