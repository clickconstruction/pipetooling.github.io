# v2.7542 — Checklist: the groundwork for moving, closing and handing on your own task (2026-11-16)

Punch list #369, step C1 of the Task Mode train, stacked on B3 (v2.7541). The migration alone, with its bed. It has no screen: nothing calls the new functions until steps C2 to C7.

## What changed

- **`20261117120000_task_doer_acts.sql`** (documented in `docs/migrations/`): six more kinds of line in a task's thread, a stuck line anyone who reads the task may write, one gate and four narrow functions:
  - `task_move_day`: the person doing a one-time task moves its due day.
  - `task_no_longer_applies`: closes a one-time task without doing it, with a reason.
  - `task_hand_on`: one person for another on the same task, so its thread stays.
  - `task_add_map`: a map the caller painted, onto a task that has none.
- **A bed for it**, `supabase/tests/task_doer_acts/10_acts.sql` with `scripts/pgtest-task-doer-acts.sh` and its line in `scripts/sql-beds.txt`: 71 assertions on the whole schema as eight made-up people.

## Is this the best we can do

- **A function per act, not a wider policy.** The person doing a task may not write `checklist_items`, and widening that policy would let them rename a task or change who hears. Each function is one write and refuses the rest, the shape `add_task_place_from_the_spot` set in v2.7067.
- **A close is a completion.** A new column for *closed, not done* would have to be read by every list that hides finished work: Today, Still open, Review, Manage, My Inbox and the reminders. A completion with a `dropped` line after it leaves every one of those lists by itself, and the screens that care tell the two apart by that line.
- **So a roadmap task and a vehicle task are refused.** Completing either mirrors onto its roadmap task or its service, which would then read done. The tasks in the owner's screenshot wore a roadmap tag, so the close will not clear those: they can be moved to another day or marked stuck, and the roadmap's lead takes them off the roadmap. The fragment of step C4 says this on the screen's side.
- **A hand-on swaps the person.** The office's FWD copied the task and deleted the first day row with its thread. Step C7 points FWD at this function.
