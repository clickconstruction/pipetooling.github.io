## What it does

`/lien-desk` has a row of its own above the desk (`LienDeskPage`'s grid is now `auto minmax(0, 1fr)`). It holds [`LienPayoffs`](../../src/components/jobs/LienPayoffs.tsx): one line, **Payoffs**, with what waits on a release and the press **Someone paid ›**. The press opens a window of every live filed affidavit under its place. `/lien-desk?payoffs=1` opens the window at once.

Each lien is one row: the job, the affidavit's amount, the county and instrument number, three small rounds for the three steps, one sentence and one press.

| Where the lien stands | The sentence | The press | Where it lands |
|---|---|---|---|
| `money`: the job's sent bills still owe | *$5,160.50 is still open on this job's bills.* (*Part paid.* first when money came in) | **Record the payment ›** | Edit Job on its Bill tab |
| `release`: the bills read paid, no release saved | *Paid Nov 24 by check 1184. The release is owed.* and, while the check clears, *The check clears about Dec 1.* in amber | **Make the release ›** | the Lien window on Release of record |
| `county`: a release is saved, the county's day is not | *The release was made Nov 24. Sign it before a notary. Then record it at the county.* | **Open the release ›** | the same tab |
| `done`: the release carries the county's day | *Released. The county recorded it Dec 3 as 2026-0431955.* | none | behind the *Released* fold |

The line under a place says what pays its liens off, or how many releases are owed there. Places that need a press come first: a release owed, then one out for its stamp, then money still owed.

The window writes nothing. It stays open under the door it sends the office through and reads again when the desk does, so the next step is there on the way back.

## Why it reads the filings and not the desk's lists

The desk's list functions keep a job only while `revenue − payments_made > 0`. A job paid to zero leaves them with its lien still on record, and nothing asked for the release. [`lienPayoffIo.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienPayoffIo.ts) reads the live affidavits and releases of record first, then the jobs they sit on with their bills and payments, and works out what the sent bills still owe with the desk's own rule, `lienBilledOpen`.

## The rule

[`lienPayoff.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienPayoff.ts), pure, 22 tests. `lienPayoffStep` gives the step, the marked sentence, the warning and the press. `releaseForAffidavit` finds a lien's release by the link it saves (`fields.releasesFilingId`), then by the instrument number an older release copied from its affidavit, then, on a job with one filed lien, by being the job's only release. A job with two filed liens never hands one loose release to both. `lienPlaceKey` puts jobs at one property together however they spell its address, and whatever customer each sits under: a lien sits on the property, so a place with two customers names both. `lienPayoffPlaces` and `lienPayoffStrip` build the window and the desk's line. Every sentence is held to `plainWords.ts` by the test.

A check still clearing only warns. Whether a release waits for a check to clear is the card's first call for the owner, not yet made.
