## What a look is

The same layouts in other colours: the ground, the accent (the pipes, the ring, the panels, the name on the card) and the ink of the logo's two words where they sit on the accent. Nothing moves. `src/lib/brandKit/looks.ts` holds `THEMES` and `lookLayouts(id)`; the look in use is the same objects as before, untouched. The navy looks take the drawn card front, navy where the pipe photo was, since that photo is tinted black.

## Who does what

- **A developer draws a look**: one entry in `THEMES`.
- **The owner approves**: Settings → People & teams → *Card and footer looks* (`CardLooksCard.tsx`, a dev's card). Each look is drawn with his own facts, with a switch, a default, and how many people use it. The default cannot be switched off. The setting is one `app_settings` row, `card_looks_v1`.
- **A person picks**: *Your card and footer* gets a *Look* row when more than one look is offered: each offered look drawn with their own facts, as a radio group. The pick saves at once (`users.card_look`) and both pieces redraw.
- **The office picks for a teammate** on that teammate's card. *Cards and footers for the team* draws each person in their own look, front included.
- **A look switched off** moves nobody's row. `resolveLook` draws a pick that is no longer offered as the default.

## The rules, held by tests

`looks.test.ts`: every look keeps the bones (the same rows on the same baselines, the same ring, the same pipes, the same logo place), and every pair of its colours reads: white lines on the ground at 4.5 to 1 or better, the name in the accent and the logo's words on the panel at 3 to 1 or better. The setting parses back safely: an unknown look is dropped, one always stays on, the default is always one that is on.
