# v2.2892 — Superintendent's Schedule board: grey "busy" placeholders, a true manpower count, no dead time-off buttons (2026-09-05)

Journey-map Tier-2 #23 (J18-F2 / N2, "the superintendent's board is silently trimmed but fully armed"). `job_schedule_blocks_select` scopes a superintendent's reads to assigned projects — by design (ACCESS_CONTROL: "assigned projects only") — but the board never said so: booked people looked free (the master's week showed 1 of 6 busy cells), Expected Manpower read **45** person-hours where the truth was **83**, and 63 "off" buttons rendered that `pay_staff_bulk_insert_user_time_off` refuses for that role. RLS stays the guard; the UI now tells the truth about what it is not showing.

**Server — migration [`20260905200000_schedule_hidden_block_counts.sql`](../migrations/20260905200000_schedule_hidden_block_counts.md).**

- `public.is_superintendent()` — new role helper (mirrors `is_controller()`).
- `public.schedule_hidden_block_counts(p_start date, p_end date) → (user_id, day, hidden_count, hidden_hours)` — rows only where the caller's RLS hides at least one block. **SECURITY INVOKER**: its own `job_schedule_blocks` aggregate runs under the caller's policies (the visible set); it subtracts that from the totals returned by the SECURITY DEFINER helper `_schedule_total_block_counts` (RLS bypassed, aggregates only). Hidden = total − visible, server-side; nothing re-implements the policy predicate, and no job identity or times leave the server. Gate on both: `is_superintendent() OR is_master_or_dev() OR is_assistant()` (assistant-like includes controller). `hidden_hours` was added beyond the planned `(user_id, day, hidden_count)` shape because Expected Manpower is person-hours — a bare count could not produce "83".

**Client.**

- [`lib/scheduleHiddenBlocks.ts`](../../src/lib/scheduleHiddenBlocks.ts) (new, tested): `fetchScheduleHiddenBlockCounts` (RPC, PostgREST numerics normalized), `buildScheduleHiddenByCell` (→ `hubPersonDayKey` map), `scheduleHiddenManpowerForDayKeys`, `formatManpowerWithHidden` (`38 + 45 → { total: '83', detail: '38 on your projects' }`), `scheduleHiddenBlocksTotal`, `scheduleHiddenPlaceholderTitle`.
- [`lib/scheduleDispatchEditRoles.ts`](../../src/lib/scheduleDispatchEditRoles.ts): view and edit sets are now separate constants (`CAN_VIEW_SCHEDULE_DISPATCH_ROLES` gates the page; `CAN_USE_SCHEDULE_DISPATCH_EDIT_ROLES` gates block edits — same five roles today); new `CAN_WRITE_SCHEDULE_TIME_OFF_ROLES` + `canWriteTimeOff(role)` mirror the RPC gate: dev, master_technician, assistant, controller. Tested as a role matrix that names the RPC.
- [`ScheduleDispatchHubPage.tsx`](../../src/components/schedule/ScheduleDispatchHubPage.tsx): `loadHub` Phase A also calls the RPC **only when `role === 'superintendent'`** (office roles: `[]`, zero behavior change); hidden user ids join the roster and the "only with blocks this week" set; `canTimeOff = canEdit && canWriteTimeOff(role)` gates the empty-cell `off` button, the undo chip handler, and the assign-picker "not coming in" option. Telemetry `recordNavClick(uid, role, 'schedule_hidden_blocks', '#<count>')` once per non-quiet superintendent load.
- [`ScheduleDispatchHub.tsx`](../../src/components/schedule/ScheduleDispatchHub.tsx): new `hiddenByCell` / `hiddenBlockCounts` props (shell → `HubPeoplePanel` → `HubPeopleDayCell`). `HubHiddenBusyPlaceholders` draws one dashed grey `busy` box per hidden block (cap 6 + "+N more"; tokens `--bg-muted` / `--border` / `--text-muted`; tooltip "Busy on work outside your projects (N blocks) — details are hidden"). A cell with only hidden blocks is no longer an empty cell: no `+`/`off` bar, the corner add-triangle instead (the super can still schedule the person on their own job — the double-book risk is now visible, not silent). Expected Manpower stats line: `**83** person-hours · 38 on your projects · 4 jobs · 6 people · 3 busy elsewhere`; the empty-selection copy becomes "No blocks on your projects for Wed · 45 person-hours busy elsewhere (4 people)"; the week footer gains "· 38 on your projects". With nothing hidden every string is byte-identical to before.

**Placeholders vs banner.** Placeholders were chosen over the alternative "showing your projects only" banner: they land in the exact cell the super is about to double-book, and they carry the manpower correction for free.
