# v2.2233 — Pay speeds breakdown: per-customer payment receipts (2026-08-24)

The Pay speeds breakdown modal's customer rows (both tiers) are now expandable: clicking a row reveals the measurable payments behind its median as "receipt" chips — `(+16) 05/01–05/17` (gap pill + billed→paid dates, hover spells out the math). The gap pill is judged against the company median via `receiptGapTone` (green ≤, amber above, red ≥ 2×; neutral without a company stat). Zero-receipt expansions explain themselves: v2 payloads (pre-migration reloads) get a "reload once the updated lookup is live" hint, true zero-sample customers get "no invoice-linked payments in the last 12 months". The old optional `onOpenCustomerBills` row click (never passed by the live caller) moves into the expanded panel as a "See these bills on the board →" link.
