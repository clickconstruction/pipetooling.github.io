# v2.3825 — The owner's portal shows the lien notice on their property (2026-09-25)

Punch list #45, PR 2 (`to-dos/owner-portal-property-view/`). The owner's portal had nothing to say about a lien notice on their house. It said *"You're all paid up"*, and a shared bill told them *"Not yours to pay"*, which is the opposite of what the notice asks them to do. The owner's call (2026-09-25): the notice card shows **on its own** once a notice is recorded, since the owner already holds the paper. Client and one edge function, no migration.

## The change

- **Kernel** `supabase/functions/_shared/portalPropertyNotices.ts` (new; Deno module, vitest-tested):
  - `buildPortalPropertyNotices({ jobs, filings, viewerCustomerId })` takes the recorded `notice_53_056` filings (not voided) on jobs where the viewer is the customer, a GC is on the job and money is still open.
  - It makes one card per paper: a packet covering several jobs at one property is one card with its `printed_claim`.
  - Each card carries the owner send's `sent_on` (falling back to the filing day), the claim, the months, and the GC's (`originalContractorName`), claimant's and signer's (`contactPerson`) names from the saved fields. Newest first.
  - `portalNoticeMonthsWords` writes the months out (*April, June, July and August 2026*).
- **`customer-portal`** reads those filings for the viewer's owner jobs and returns `propertyNotices`. **Redeploy required.**
- **Page** (`CustomerPortal.tsx`, `PortalPropertyNoticeCard.tsx`, `portalPayload.ts`):
  - The card follows counsel's owner letter: *Notice on your property · mailed …*; the address; *We did work on your property under {GC}. {GC} has not paid us {claim} for work in {months}*; the § 53.056 sentence; *You did not hire us, and this is not a lawsuit*; *You may hold back*; the three clean ways to finish it (*We cannot take a joint check*); and a *Call {signer} · {phone}* `tel:` button.
  - The paid-up line reads *Nothing is billed to you directly* whenever a notice or an owner-shared bill exists.
  - `PortalSharedBillsCard` gains a noticed wording (*On your property, billed to your builder*; rows *on the notice above*). Without a notice the old wording stands.
  - `parsePortalPayload` reads `propertyNotices` leniently: a function from before this change sends none.
- **Docs**: *share a customer their portal* → *When a lien notice has gone to the owner*; `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` → customer-portal; counsel is asked to read the card's words (`to-dos/owner-decisions-pending.md`).
