# v2.4650 — Records for an owner: they sign on their portal (2026-10-06)

Migration `20261006040000_esign_consents_owner_records.sql` ([doc](../migrations/20261006040000_esign_consents_owner_records.md)); new edge function `sign-owner-records`; `customer-portal` changed. PR 1 of punch list #86 ([`to-dos/owner-records-portal/`](../../to-dos/owner-records-portal/README.md)), shape B, on the owner's three answers of the same day.

## What changed

- **The office offers.** [`LienOwnerRecordsModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienOwnerRecordsModal.tsx): under check 1, **Offer it on their portal ›** opens one box for a second name allowed to sign and **Offer it and copy the link**, which mints the customer's portal link (`mintCustomerPortalLink`, audience `all`), writes `file.offer { at, by, alsoAllowed }` and copies `/portal?t=…` to the clipboard. Check 1 then reads *Offered on their portal <day>. Waiting for them to sign.* and **Copy the portal link ›** stays. An owner with no customer row gets no button.
- **The owner signs.** `customer-portal` sends `ownerRecords` (the offered, unsent request for the link's customer; never the packet). [`PortalOwnerRecordsCard.tsx`](../../src/components/portal/PortalOwnerRecordsCard.tsx) shows the acknowledgment's lines, asks for the name *as the county lists the owner* (the hint names it, and turns red while the letters differ), and signs through the shared `ContractAcceptSignatureForm` (type or draw, the e-sign consent line and box). [`sign-owner-records`](../EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md#sign-owner-records) checks the link, the request, the name ([`_shared/ownerNameMatch.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/ownerNameMatch.ts): letters only — case, spaces, punctuation and accents stripped — against the roll's owner or the second name), keeps a drawn signature in the `sent-documents` bucket, files the consent on `esign_consents` as `lien_owner_record_request` (the migration), and writes `file.request` (`how: 'portal'`) and `file.acknowledgment` (name, mode, ink path, consent instant). Signed, the card thanks them and says the office sends the records once it has checked our contract.
- **The window reads it.** [`ownerRecords.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/ownerRecords.ts): `OwnerRecordsHow` gains `portal`; the file gains `offer` and the portal fields on `acknowledgment`; check 4 reads *Signed on their portal <day> by <name>, typed / drawn* and has no **Change**. `ownerRecordsAllowedNames` is the one list of names the portal accepts.

## Not in this PR

PR 2 (*On their portal* as a way to record it as sent) and PR 4 (the owner's one **Download** after the office sends). Until then the office prints or downloads the packet and hands it over, as today.

## Deploy

`supabase db push` (the constraint), then `supabase functions deploy sign-owner-records customer-portal`. Deploy the client first or together: the window writes `file.offer`, which an old function ignores.
