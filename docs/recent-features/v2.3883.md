# v2.3883 — Dashboard on a phone: office order (2026-09-27)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Punch list **#30**, PR 4a-3 (`to-dos/taunya-mobile.md`; the mock-up `to-dos/taunya-mobile-pr4-inbox-day.html`). Builds on [v2.3881](v2.3881.md) and [v2.3882](v2.3882.md). Everything here is gated on `isMobile && isPhoneOfficeRole(role)` — a desktop, a field role, a dev and a master see the Dashboard as it was.

## What shipped

- **`ClockInOutButton`** — `oneLine`: while clocked in, the row is *Clocked in · 3:12* with Focus and Clock out as 40 px buttons on the same line, instead of two 48 px buttons that wrap at 375 px. Same handlers, same disabled rules; a salaried person has no Clock out, as before. The clocked-out state is unchanged.
- **My Schedule** hides when today and tomorrow are both empty and the read has landed (`hideEmptyMySchedule`).
- **Needs You as a door** — `DashboardPinnedQuickRow` takes `needsYouDoor`; with Dispatch Mode on the card is one line, *Needs you · 10 · <the first item>* → *Inbox ›*, to `/dispatch-mode/inbox` where the deck is. With Dispatch Mode off the card stays.
- **The folds** (`PhoneFold`, now with `spaced`): Who's in (*7 in*), Crew Day, My Inbox (*due today 2 · overdue 1*), Team inboxes (*Dispatch 5 open · oldest 4 d*), Recent reports, Billing pipeline (*2 ready · 71 billed*), My Time. A section that does not render for the role or is empty gets no fold line (each fold reads the gate the section dock already used). The bodies stay mounted, so nothing loads differently.
- **The section dock** (the floating chips) is off on this view — the fold lines are the index, and a chip cannot scroll to a folded section.
- **`src/lib/dashboard/phoneFolds.ts`** — `whosInFoldHeadline`, `billingPipelineFoldHeadline`, `needsYouDoorWords`, `hideEmptyMySchedule`.
- **The guide** *start-here-in-the-office* → *Your day*.

## Left, on the card

Crew Day, Recent reports and My Time fold by name only (their counts live inside the sections); the Job Report and Tally squares still draw for office roles. Both are leftovers L5 and L6 on the to-do.
