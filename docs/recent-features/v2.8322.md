# v2.8322 — Papers: the office can send a trade company its paper to sign (2026-11-22)

Punch list #403, step 5, the second PR. One edge function and its kernel, the client's copy of the role list, and their tests. No screen and no migration.

## What changed

- **The company branch takes the office's four roles.** `send-contract-for-signature` has a branch for a trade company's own paper (`sendCompanyPaper`). It asked `GC_TRADE_EMAIL_ROLES`, a dev alone. It asks `companyPaperCallerRefusal` now, which takes a dev, a leader, an assistant and the controller (`COMPANY_PAPER_SEND_ROLES`, the same four as `SIGNING_SEND_ROLES`).
- **An archived account is refused first.** The person path already did this. The company branch did not, and it writes with the service role, so no fence in the database stood behind it.
- **The email can come from the name a sub knows.** `trade_email.from: 'office'` sends it as *Click Plumbing and Electrical*. Any other value keeps *Click Construction*, so GC mode's sends are unchanged.
- **The client has its copy.** `canSendCompanyPaper` in `src/lib/gc/access.ts`, held to the function's list by `access.test.ts`.

## Who can do what they could not before

- A leader, an assistant and the controller can send a trade company its own paper for signature, through this function. Only a dev could.
- An archived dev can no longer send one. Nothing refused them before.
- Nobody else gains anything. An estimator, a primary, a superintendent, a sub and a helper are refused as before. So are a training account and a digital twin, whatever their role.

## What stays as it was

- `GC_TRADE_EMAIL_ROLES` is still a dev alone. Every other email to a trade partner asks it: an invitation to quote, a statement of work, a draw, a closeout, a schedule change.
- GC mode's company window still offers **Send a paper** to a dev alone (`canUseGcBoardWrites`), since `gc_send_paper` is a dev's.
- The person path is untouched, and its pin against main still holds.
- No screen offers the office this send yet. Step 6 puts it on People.

## A choice worth a look

The brief said to widen `GC_TRADE_EMAIL_ROLES`. That list gates every email to a trade partner, so widening it would have opened invitations, draws and schedule emails to three more roles. The card asks only for a company's paper, so this PR gives the company branch its own list and leaves the other alone.

## Proof

- `src/lib/companySigningSend.test.ts`: the four roles pass, every other role is `officeOnly`, a training account and a twin are `readOnly`, and the sender's name follows `from`.
- `src/lib/contractSigningSendCaller.test.ts`: the branch refuses an archived caller, then asks who the caller is, before it reads the paper, and the function no longer names `GC_TRADE_EMAIL_ROLES`.
- `src/lib/gc/access.test.ts`: the client's list equals the function's.
- `src/lib/paperCallerReadGates.test.ts` and the person path's pin pass unchanged.
