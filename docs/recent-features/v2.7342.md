# v2.7342 — Tasks: a place can carry the painted map that leads to it (2026-11-15)

Punch list #345, step 5, the second of two PRs. The card drew it: a task's place gains the painted map that leads to it, so the dot comes with its way in. Taunya picked it on 2026-11-15: "I want to build both, also a tasks place carries it's painted map". The column and its gate went in with v2.7340. This is the screen.

## What changed

**The person who gives the task**

- **Where is it?** has a new press under the place's name: **＋ Add the way in**. It lists the painted maps that come within a mile of the ring, nearest first, each with its length, its places and how near it comes: *Reaches this place*, *Comes within 120 ft*. A press on one puts it on the place.
- The map's name then shows above **Use this place**, with the line *Whoever gets the task can open this map.* The ✕ beside it takes the way in off and leaves the map where it is.
- **A place chip that has a way in wears the paint's stroke.** A press on it gives the task the place and the map together, so a way in is added once for a place and comes back with it.
- The same sheet serves the New task window, Manage's Edit and a roadmap task, since all three set a place through it.

**The person who gets the task**

- The place's name on the task wears the paint's stroke.
- Opened, the place's picture draws the orange line, and so does the map opened full size. A line under the picture says *The way in is painted: The way in for deliveries, about 830 ft.*
- **Directions** becomes **Directions to the start** and opens the phone's maps app at the line's first point. A maps app can route to a gate on a road. It cannot follow a painted line.
- **Open the map** opens the painted map's own window: its places with their words and photos, its lengths, and Add to it.

## How it is built

- `taskPlace.ts`: a place carries `mapId`. `TASK_PLACE_SELECT` asks for `painted_map_id`, `taskPlaceColumns` writes it with the place and writes null with no place, and a changed way is a changed place (`taskPlaceDraftChanged`). So the three windows that save a place needed no change of their own.
- `taskPlaceWay.ts`, pure (+13 tests): `waysNearPlace` offers the maps by how near they come, measured to the nearest spot on a line and not to its nearest corner, since a long straight drive passes a place between two corners. `taskPlaceWayWords` and `taskPlaceWayStart` are the card's words and where Directions goes.
- `TaskPlaceSheet` reads the painted maps only on the press, with `loadPaintedMapsEvery`, the read the Map page's layer already makes. `TaskPlaceBlock` reads the one map with `loadPaintedMap` when the card opens and hands it to `OnSiteMap`'s `painted`, which the box map already draws.
- The types and the dev seat's catalog gain `painted_map_id` here, spliced by hand from the pushed migration.

## Is this the best we can do

- **Pick, do not paint, from this sheet.** Painting a new map from a task is part of Taunya's other ask, a painted map on a checklist item, which is designed on the card and waits on her yes. Until then a way in is painted from a job's address or the search, then picked here. The empty list says so.
- **A way in goes with its place.** Take the place off the task and the map goes too. A map on a task with no place is that same ask.
- **Nearest first, a mile at most, six at most.** A list of every map the company has would be read by nobody. A map across town is not this place's way in.
- **A map that is gone, or one the reader may not open, costs nothing.** The block draws the place as it did, with Directions to the place.
- **On the roadmap's own card the line draws and Open the map does not show.** The painted map's window sits under the roadmap's windows, so the press would open something nobody could see. The task on Today has the press. Lifting the layer is for the roadmap PR of the next ask.
