# v2.4714 — AIA G702-G703: a change after the workbook went out is named (2026-10-06)

Second of the three PRs behind the owner's *build C* (the first, [v2.4710](v2.4710.md), opened the window on the job's history). The mock-up's critique had said that "changed since it went out" read from timestamps would be a guess: a Save that changed nothing would be flagged, and nothing recorded what the form said when the workbook left. Migration `20261006236000_sent_documents_source_snapshot.sql` ([doc](../migrations/20261006236000_sent_documents_source_snapshot.md)); no edge function.

## The change

- **The copy carries the figures.** `SentFiling.sourceSnapshot` → `sent_documents.source_snapshot` (jsonb, object or null), written with the row and never changed; `SentCopy.sourceSnapshot` reads it back. A database without the column drops it on write and the read goes again without it (`sentCopiesIo.ts`).
- **Generate files the snapshot**: `payApplicationSnapshot(saved)` — the application number, the period, the four typed amounts per line, and the G702's contract sum to date, completed and stored, retainage percent and held, earned less retainage and payment due — beside the workbook, when the save took.
- **Kernel** `changedAfterWentOut(app, wentOut)` in [`aiaPayApplicationHistory.ts`](../../src/lib/aiaPayApplicationHistory.ts): the saved application against the newest workbook that carries a snapshot; lines matched by id, then by label, a line on one side only named as a new line or a line taken off; then the totals that moved; null when no workbook carries a snapshot or nothing moved. `changedAfterWords` draws it: *Changed after it went out Sep 2: Top Out this period $12,078.40 → $11,323.50 · payment due $10,870.56 → $10,191.15*.
- **The history line and the Documents tab row** show the warning under the application, with *The GC has the Sep 2 workbook. Generate it again to send the change, or open it and put the amounts back.*
