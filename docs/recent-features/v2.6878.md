# v2.6878 — Labels: copies of each box, and a window that reads top to bottom (punch list #304) (2026-11-10)

The owner, with the labels window printing to his P-touch: *I like this tab and I like that it works but I think it could flow better. And sometimes boxes have multiple sides. I would like the ability to set the number of copies of each, so if we were to say 2, instead of printing 1 2 3 we would print 1 1 2 2 3 3.*

## What changed

- **Copies of each** (`lib/materials/tapeLabel.ts`: `TAPE_COPIES_MAX` 6, `clampTapeCopies`, `repeatEach`; `labelPrefs.ts`: `readTapeCopies` / `writeTapeCopies`, key `pt.labels.tapeCopies`): a minus, the count and a plus under the box chips, tape only. `repeatEach` keeps a box's copies together, 1 1 2 2 3 3, so a pair goes on one box before the next pair is in hand. Straight to the printer, the run is the drawn strips repeated. Through the print window, a box's copies are extra strips drawn only on the paper (`.on-site-tape-copy`), each after its own box; the screen draws every box once. The press names boxes times copies (`tapePressLabel`), the line under the title says *3 boxes, 2 strips each. 6 strips.* (`labelCountWords`), and **Print 1 test strip** is one strip whatever the count. A single box's window (a box's **Label** press) has it too.
- **The order** (`LabelsPrintModal`): what it prints on, then which boxes and how many, then the strips, then the press. The statuses and the presses moved from above the boxes to a foot that stays at the window's bottom edge while the strips scroll (`position: sticky`; a 92-box job no longer scrolls the press away once it is below the chips). **Export the labels as a CSV** is a quiet press at the foot's right.
- **One line for the printer**: on a computer that printed tape, the bar that said *Brother tape · 24 mm. This computer printed tape last on Nov 10.* now carries the printer's own words once it is connected (*PT-D610BT · 24 mm tape · ready*), where a second line under it said nearly the same. **Use the print window instead** sits under **Printer setup** there; while the print window is the route, **Print straight to the P-touch** is on the bar.

## The calls made

- **Copies are remembered on the device.** A crew that labels two sides labels two sides every time; the press always names the total, so a remembered 2 cannot print a surprise.
- **No copies on the paper sheet.** A sheet carries lines to write counts on; two of them would be two tallies.
- **1 1 2 2, never 1 2 1 2.** The owner's order, and the one that keeps a box's strips in one hand.

## Checked

Kernel tests for `repeatEach`, `clampTapeCopies` and the count words; render smokes for both routes (the run's order straight to the printer, the paper's copies after their own box through the print window, the foot, the single line). Walked on localhost without a printer: a 2-box job and a 92-box job, the foot flush with the window's bottom at both ends of the scroll. Not checked: a real run of copies on the printer.
