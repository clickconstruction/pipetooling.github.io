# v2.7936 — A primary reads the photos of the jobs they have a claim on (2026-11-19)

Punch list #400, PR 3 of 5 (`to-dos/primary-job-doors.md`), on the owner's call 2. One migration and one edge function. It stands on v2.7906 and ships after it.

## Before

- The gate that lets a primary write a field report on any job also opened what the crew had saved on that job. So a primary on no job could read every job's photo notes and their files, the first look tied to a job, the crew's position and the job's Drive photos.
- No list on a primary's screens showed them: Documents → Photos already holds to the jobs they account-manage. The hole was at the API.

## What changed

- **Photo notes** (`20261119102000_primary_scope_job_photos.sql`): a primary reads their own shot, the notes of a job whose reports they read, and the notes of a job whose crew or schedule they are on. The files in the bucket follow.
- **A first look tied to a job** follows the same rule, with its files.
- **The crew's position** (`list_job_crew_position`) answers a primary for the jobs whose reports they read.
- **The job's Drive photos**: the `job-photos` edge function asks a primary for the same claim.
- **The bed.** `npm run test:pg:primary-scope-job-photos` reads every door as five people, on the whole schema.

## What it leaves alone, and why

- Writing. A primary still files a report with photos on any job, and still adds a photo note on any job and reads it back.
- The stage checklist. The card proposed fencing its answers too. The report form reads the job's answers before it writes one, so a fence there would stop a primary saving a report on a job that is not theirs. The checklist tables stay open, and holding them is a separate piece of work.
- Every other role.

## Deploy

After v2.7906 is merged and pushed: merge, `supabase db push`, then `supabase functions deploy job-photos`. Deployed before the two migrations are live, the function would refuse a primary on every job.
