# v2.5856 — The Bridge: the Collect card's weeks count only the bills past their day (2026-10-25)

Punch list #177, PR E, on [v2.5855](v2.5855.md); it closes calculation 3 of to-do #107 (*Collect's pile counts invoices that are not late*). It changes what the red says, so it merges alone.

## What was wrong

`Bridge.tsx` fed the Collect stage `fin.data.ar.total`, the Dashboard's whole billed-not-paid figure, and `stageFlow` divided it by the window's collected-per-week. A bill inside its payer's usual pace is not waiting on Collect yet, so the card read 10.2 weeks on 2026-10-25 where the bills actually past their day cleared in under five.

## The rule

`CollectStageInput` gains `notDueUsd`. The page feeds Collect from the Collect desk's pile (`buildCollectPile`, [v2.5848](v2.5848.md)), which now loads with the page rather than when the modal opens: the pile is the bills past their day by each payer's own pace (`late` + `broken` + `doubtful`), `notDueUsd` is what is not yet due or promised with a day ahead, and the count is the past-due count. The Dashboard's total stands in until the pile is read. `stageFlow` draws the headline on the sum, the sub lines *$140k past its day on 47 invoices · grey: $157k not yet due*, the weeks on the past-due pile, and the clear words *to collect what is already late*.

The grey is money, not a guess: `StageReading.pile.greyKind` is `waiting` on Collect and `guess` on Build and Bill, and only `guess` takes the thin-reading gate (`BUILD_GUESS_GATE`) and the *set the %s* badge. The stages' caption and the help guide say so.
