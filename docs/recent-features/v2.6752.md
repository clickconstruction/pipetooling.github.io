# v2.6752 — Word to every trade: one email to every company on a job, from the project's card (punch list #205, PR 6) (2026-11-08)

Punch list #205 (`to-dos/trade-portal-vs-jobtread.md`), row 2, the owner's mark of 2026-11-08: the word to every trade on a job. PR 6 of six, stacked on PR 5 (v2.6748). Client only, over `gc-trade-email`: no function change, no migration.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/gc/wordToTrades.ts`** (new, pure, with its test): `wordRecipients(state, project)` (every company awarded a trade on the job through one of its own invites, each once, with its trades), `wordSubject` (*A word from Click on {project}*), `wordLines` (the text's paragraphs), `wordEmailKey(projectId, subject, text)` = `word:<projectId>:<sha256>` (the shape of `datesEmailKey`), so a second press of the same words answers `already` and sends nothing.
- **`src/lib/gc/wordToTradesIo.ts`** (new): `sendWordToTrades` sends one `gc-trade-email` per company, kind `nudge`, `groups: ['job']`, `projectId`, `lang: tradeMailLang(partner.lang)`, the subject and the lines under the one key; keeps going past a refusal and names each company with `gcTradeEmailRefusal`, with the ones that already had the words apart.
- **`GcWordToTrades.tsx`** (new, the pattern of `GcTellTrades.tsx`): the window from the card: the companies by name and trade (*· Español* for a Spanish one), a subject, the words, and **Tell N companies** (`countedLabel`), dead until words are typed and a company is awarded; afterwards `batchOutcomeWords`' line (*Sent 4 emails. 1 did not send.*), who it went to, who already had the words, and each refusal with its reason.
- **The card** (`cardHead.ts`): the door `word`, **Word to every trade**, in the work set after Draws, for `can.word` on a job being built that is not lost; `GcProjects.tsx` gates it by `canSendGcTradeEmail(role)` and opens the window (`wordProjectId`, local state, as the window writes no URL).
- **Tests**: `wordToTrades.test.ts` on main's test state (Fair Oaks Shops, Building D's six companies); `cardHead.test.ts` (the door's place and gates); `GcWordToTrades.render.test.tsx` (the count on the button, dead until typed, one send for every company, the refused and the already-told named, plain words).
- **Docs**: `gc-trade-email`'s **Doors** paragraph in `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`; the guide *email a trade partner from GC mode* (**A word to every trade on a job**); `docs/PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`'s schedule entry; the card `to-dos/trade-portal-vs-jobtread.md` brought to what shipped.

## Verify

Open a job being built on `/gc`, press **Word to every trade**: the button counts the awarded companies, one email goes to each (one row each in `gc_trade_messages`, kind `nudge`), and a second press of the same words says they already had it and sends nothing. Each company's portal shows the email in its Messages thread.
