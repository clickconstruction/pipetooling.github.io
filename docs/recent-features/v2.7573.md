# v2.7573 — Materials on site: a box's pin remembers who set it, when and how sure (2026-11-17)

Punch list #372, *Find a lost box*, step 1 of 5. The owner's word of 2026-11-17: *Build all the steps*, after he confirmed that a crate is a cage or a box from Materials on site. This is the database half of the **Where is it?** sheet. No screen changes.

## Why

A box can carry a pin on the site map. The pin was two numbers on the box's row and nothing else, so the app could not say *pinned three weeks ago by Jordan*. Boxes get moved by other trades, and a pin with no day reads as the truth. The sheet that comes next always says the day and the person.

## What ships

Migration `20261117170000_box_pin_remembers.sql` ([its page](../migrations/20261117170000_box_pin_remembers.md)): four nullable columns on `job_containers` (`pinned_at`, `pinned_by`, `pinned_by_name`, `pin_accuracy_m`) and one trigger, `job_containers_stamp_pin`. The trigger stamps the caller and the time when a pin is set or moved, clears all four when the pin is taken off, and puts a pin's facts back when a write tries to change them without the pin. No new table, policy or grant.

The card planned three columns. The name is the fourth: the crew's roles cannot read `users`, so the row keeps the name beside the id, as a box move does.

**An older client is safe.** Today's **Pin on map** sheet sends the point alone. The trigger gives such a pin the caller, the server's time and no margin, so the app that is live now starts leaving dated pins the moment this is pushed.
