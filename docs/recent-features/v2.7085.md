# v2.7085 — Tasks: a simpler New task window (2026-11-12)

Punch list #331, PR 2 of 4. The window the header's **Task** button opens was called *Add checklist item:* and showed every field open: 1,267 px on a 390 by 844 phone, the day 711 px down, the bottom Send below the screen's edge. Of the 137 tasks made in it, 96 used none of its eight extras. Grace saw the card and the pressable mock-up (`to-dos/new-task-window/`) and said to build it as drawn. Same fields, same save, no migration.

## What changed

- **The name:** *New task*.
- **The order:** the words, **For**, **When**, **Where**, **Add**, then a bar with the sentence and Send. The words box is three lines tall.
- **For** (`TaskFacesRow`): a row of faces from `taskFaces`. Me, anyone picked from the full list, up to eight people given to last (newest first, in place of three names in the order the rows came back), then a face for each crew. **Someone else** is pinned at the end and opens the full list in place, with the crew row and its *New crew* and *Edit* under it. A phone slides the row sideways; a desk wraps it.
- **When** (`TaskWhenBar`): one joined bar, *Today · Tomorrow · Pick a day · Repeats*. Tomorrow is stored as a dated task for the next day. The date box and the weekdays open under the bar only when asked for. The four-week calendar no longer opens for Tomorrow.
- **Where**: `TaskPlaceRow` gains `variant="shelf"` (*＋ New* first, a lit place leading, one line that slides) and `TaskSpotPhotoField` gains `variant="press"` (one button that holds the picture and takes it off). The Edit window keeps the older shapes.
- **Add** (`TaskAddRow` over `taskAddRow`): *Remind, Tell someone else, Link, Due by, Done when…, Photo at the finish, Just for the day*. A press opens that one thing under the row; a thing that is set is lit and shows its value. *Stays on the list until done* is still the default and is now turned off with *Just for the day*.
- **The bar** (`TaskSendBar`): `newTaskSentence` in green, the **Tell me** bell (the old *When it's done, notify · Me* tick), and a Send that names who gets it. Send is dead with the one thing missing named: *Type the task first.* Cancel is gone; the × closes.
- **The card is a column**: head, the form that scrolls, the bar. On a phone its height is capped at the room the keyboard leaves (`useVisualViewportHeight`), so the bar sits above the keyboard. The second Send at the top stays on a phone until a walk on a real phone shows the bar holds.
- `ChecklistCrewTagsRow` gains `onCrewsLoaded`, so the window can draw the crews as faces while the row itself stays in the full list.
- `.ntwShelf` in `index.css`: the sideways row with its faded end. A class, because a scrollbar and a mask cannot be set inline.

## What a walk in a drawing cannot show

Headless Chrome has no keyboard, so the last row was checked by shrinking the visible height. A real phone still has to show it. The faces do not hold everyone: replayed on the record, Me and the last four people covered 87 of 137 tasks, and the rest press **Someone else** first.
