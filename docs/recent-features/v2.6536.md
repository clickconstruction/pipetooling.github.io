# v2.6536 — The material location report keeps every word on the paper (punch list #268) (2026-11-05)

A fix to [v2.6533](v2.6533.md), found by the owner's *please test with screenshots on multiple viewports*. Gun Dog's real rows passed at all seven widths; a made-up dense site fed to the report's three reads at the browser did not. No migration, no edge function.

## The defect

**Last recorded** sat in a 126px column with `white-space: nowrap`, 112px of room. *Nov 30 · Malachi* is 100px and fit, which is why the live walk of v2.6533 passed. *Nov 30 · Joseph Parga* is 135px, *Oct 29, 2025 · Malachi* 136px and an agent's stamp *Nov 1 · Cowork agent · MCP as Robert* 227px. Each ran past the box, and three things followed:

- **The paper lost the words.** The PDF's text stopped at the margin, at *Oct 1 · Cowork agen*.
- **Chrome shrank the whole print to about 85%** to try to fit the overflow (rows 15.3pt where the page sets 18pt): the shrink v2.6432 removed for the manifest, by another road. A cell that runs past the sheet widens the print layout as the page behind once did.
- **On a phone the table ran past the window**, 124px at 375 wide and 179px at 320, and Last recorded took a sideways scroll.

No row on prod was cut when this shipped: the ledger's only `by_name`s are *Malachi* and *Robert* and the surface is days old. The year joins the date once a line is older than the printed year, so from January 1, 2027 a plain *Nov 1, 2026 · Malachi* would have clipped, as would the first two-word name or the first agent row.

## What changed

`SiteMaterialsReportModal.tsx` alone, with its smoke.

- **Last recorded wraps.** The column is 156px (`LAST_RECORDED_PX`), so a two-word name or an older year stays on one line, and a longer stamp takes a second. `overflow-wrap: anywhere` on it and on Part breaks a word only when the word alone is wider than the cell.
- **A phone stacks a line**, a screen rule alone (560px and under): the part with its count and unit, Last recorded under them, the Last recorded head dropped. The first cut kept four columns and sized them to their words, and at 320 wide that left the part about a word's width (*cemen / t, qt*); the screenshots caught what the overflow measures passed. The print keeps the four-column table at every width.
- **Escape closes the window**, through `useModalStackEntry` in the Ask sheet's shape (capture phase, marked handled), so a window opened over it keeps the press.

## Test

- **The smoke** gains two cases (8 tests): Escape, with a window on the stack holding it, and the two cells free to wrap, with the phone rule a screen rule only. Four mutants each fail one: Last recorded back on one line, the part losing its wrap, Escape ignoring the stack, Escape doing nothing.
- **Seven viewports, read-only, on the dev server through the repo's Playwright** (320, 375, 812 on its side, 768, 1024, 1400, 1920; every write refused at the browser): both groupings, Gun Dog's rows and the dense site, no sideways scroll, no clipped cell, no element past the paper, the controls in view and on top, Print, Close, the backdrop and Escape all working, no console error. A read over 1,000 rows paged and summed to the fixture (286 lines, 19,178 on hand). The PDFs read from the running app are Letter with one foot and a page number on every sheet, their text identical from every width, no word past the margin, every long word whole, including a 90-character part with no spaces.
- **The dense paper went from 11 sheets to 13**: it prints at full size now. Gun Dog's real paper is one sheet at the same size as before.

## Decisions

- **The stack is a phone's alone.** The paper is the record, and its four columns fit Letter; a phone's window only previews it.
- **A box taller than a sheet still starts on a fresh sheet**, then splits with its heads repeated, and can leave the sheet before it short. It takes more lines in one box than a sheet holds, and it keeps a box's lines together as far as they can go.

## Left

The two chips in the window's head are 23px tall against 36px for Print and Close; not raised here. Safari, a real iPhone or Android and the browser's own print dialog remain unwalked.
