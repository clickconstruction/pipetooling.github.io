# v2.3656 — Lien desk in dark mode: the notice on the page is readable, and so are the buttons (2026-09-20)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Owner-reported from a screenshot, 2026-09-20: *"This page is hard to read on dark mode."* Measured on prod data with a contrast walk over the open desk (WCAG AA wants 4.5:1) — four failures, two causes.

## The causes

- **The paper, 1.1:1.** The cover-note and notice frames are `data-theme="light"` wrappers, which re-pin the *tokens* to their light values. `color` itself is not a token: it inherits as a computed value, so the dark page's `#f3f4f6` arrived inside the white frame, and `filingDocHtml` sets a color only on its muted and contact lines — which is why the address block was the one readable part. Every `data-theme="light"` wrapper that did not set its own `color` had the same latent bug.
- **Filled buttons, 1.2–2.5:1.** `btn()` used `--text-green-800`, `--text-amber-800` and `--text-link` as *fills* under a white label. Those are text tokens: in dark mode they lighten (`#86efac`, `#fcd34d`, `#60a5fa`) so they read on a dark ground, which is the opposite of what a fill under white needs.

## What shipped

- [`index.css`](../../src/index.css) — `[data-theme='light']:not(:root) { color: var(--text-base) }`, with the reason in the theme-token comment. One rule fixes the desk and every other light-pinned surface; inline colors still win.
- [`LienDeskModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskModal.tsx) — `FILL` (primary `#2563eb`, green `#166534`, amber `#92400e`: the tokens' light values, so light mode is pixel-identical) feeds `btn()` and the Notices / Affidavits switch; the two paper frames share `paperStyle`.
- [`LienDeskOwnerPane.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskOwnerPane.tsx), [`LienDeskRunModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskRunModal.tsx) — **Use** and **Record the run** take the same literal blue; the grey chip's text goes from `--text-muted` to `--text-700` (4.1 → above 4.5 on `--bg-muted`).

## Verified

- Dev server on prod data, dark mode, job 883: the contrast walk over the open desk returns no run under 4.5:1 (was 15 paper runs at 1.1, two footer buttons, the switch, Use, the chip); the paper's computed color is `rgb(26, 26, 26)` from the stylesheet alone.
- Light mode: fills are the same hexes the tokens resolved to.
