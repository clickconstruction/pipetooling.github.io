# v2.7864 — Pay: one form sets a set week, its days and its weekly pay (2026-11-18)

The press that sets a salaried week, the last step of the per-person week. The owner asked on 2026-11-18 and passed the mockup the same day.

## Why

To put someone on a weekly figure, the office typed an hourly wage, ticked Salaried and set the dates, as three separate edits, after dividing the weekly figure by 40 by hand. The card said *No — logged hours × wage* or *Yes — schedule-driven sessions*. A week of anything but 8 hours on five days could not be entered.

## What ships

**One form.** On a person's sheet, *Pay & schedule* shows how the person is paid and one press, **Edit pay setup**. The form holds:

- **Paid**: *By the hour* or *A set week*.
- By the hour: the wage and the office rate.
- A set week: seven boxes, Sunday to Saturday, a blank box being a day off, with four quick picks (*8 h, five days*, *6 h, five days*, *Four tens*, *Short Friday*). Then the weekly pay, which the form divides by the week's hours to the cent. *Comes to* shows the hours in the week, the wage and what a Monday costs a job. *Still clocks in and out* is the old *also record hours*.
- **Employment**: the start and the end.

**One save.** *Save pay setup* writes the dates on the roster row, then the pay row with its week, then runs the schedule's side step when a set week is turned on or off. Turning one off asks first, as before. When the dates land and the pay row does not, the toast says which.

**What it says before saving.** A line beside Save: *Was by the hour at $10.00. Now $300.00 a week, 6 h Mon to Fri.* Above it, when they apply:

- A weekly pay that does not divide to the cent says what it will really pay, since a wage is kept to the cent.
- A set week with no start date says how many earlier weeks, counted from the last pay report, payroll would show as owed.
- A change to the money says past days on jobs are priced again and pay reports already made keep their amounts.

Save is dead with nothing to save or with a field to fix, and the field is named in one line.

## The kernel

`src/lib/people/paySetupEdit.ts`, pure: `draftFromFacts` opens the form from what is stored, `paySetupPlan` reads what was typed and answers either the one field to fix or the write, what it comes to, the line and the warnings. `parseWeekBoxes`, `payWords` and `wholeWeeksSince` are its small rules.

## What does not change

The Pay lens, the Hire form and the Employment tab still tick Salaried and type a wage. They leave the week alone: the Pay lens's saves leave the column out, so a week set on the sheet is not written back over.

The workday schedule still opens from its own press and still draws an 8-hour block. Taking each day's length and start from the week is the next step, with the template's two checks.

A pay change still has no start date of its own: a new wage or week re-prices past days. The form now says so.
