# v2.7945 — Lien desk: the mail sheet has boxes for each certified mail number (2026-11-19)

## Why

The owner, 2026-11-19, with the mail sheet of *Filed liens: copies to mail* in front of him: move Pages and Mail by in front of the name and address, put the certified mail number on a second line under each envelope "with 5 boxes of four digits each like certified mail works", let the name not wrap, and show the street on one line and the city, state and ZIP on the next, "since this is what will match what a user puts in the mail".

The number had been a blank at the end of the line, 114 points wide, and the name and address shared a 162-point column in the middle, where a two-owner name wrapped and the address wrapped wherever the line ran out.

## What changed

- **Columns** (`LIEN_SERVE_SHEET_WORDS.columns`): #, Job, Copy to, Pages, Mail by, then Name and address on the envelope, which takes the rest of the line (272 points). *Certified mail number* is no longer a column.
- **The number's boxes** (`drawLienMailSheet`): under each envelope's line, right against the margin, five boxes of 54 by 15 points with a tick between the digits, labelled `LIEN_SERVE_SHEET_WORDS.numberLabel`. The counts are `LIEN_CERTIFIED_NUMBER_GROUPS` and `LIEN_CERTIFIED_NUMBER_DIGITS`. An envelope's line and its boxes never split across two pages.
- **The name** is drawn on one line: it shrinks from 8.5 points to no less than 7 before it wraps (`fitSize`).
- **The address** (`lienEnvelopeAddressLines`, on each row as `addressLines`): the street, then the city, state and ZIP. It reads `street, city, ST zip`, `street, city, ST, zip`, `street, city ST zip`, a trailing country, and an address typed on lines. One it cannot split stays whole on one line, so nothing is guessed onto the wrong line. The row keeps `address` as it was.
- The sheet's second step reads *Write its number in the boxes under its line.*
- The cover letters and the stamped copies behind the sheet are unchanged.

A run of 22 envelopes has a mail sheet of three pages where it had two, since each envelope now takes a line for its number.

## Tests

`lienServePackets.test.ts`: the address split in each form it reads and the ones it leaves whole, the new columns and step. `lienServePdf.test.ts`: five boxes under each envelope on one line ending at the right margin, the label once an envelope, a long name drawn once and smaller, the city under the street, the fallback to the address as typed, and the head in the new order.
