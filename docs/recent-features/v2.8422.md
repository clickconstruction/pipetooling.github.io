# v2.8422 — Bills sent: a check shows its number, never a long code (2026-11-23)

Found on the Bills sent walk of 2026-11-23 (punch list #422). No migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

`billPaymentWayWords` put a payment's `reference_number` after the word *Check*. A check matched to a bank deposit can carry the deposit's own id there, so the paid line read *Check* followed by 36 characters of letters and dashes. On By day paid the deposit's heading said it once and each bill under it said it again. On a phone it wrapped in the middle. The spreadsheet's *Paid how* held it in 97 rows of one day's file. A number typed as *ck# 3045* read *Check ck# 3045*.

## What changed

- `checkNumberOf(reference)`, new and pure, in `src/lib/jobs/billsAndPayments.ts`: what is left once a leading word for check and a leading `#` are gone, when it starts with a digit and is at most 12 characters. Anything else is no number.
- `billPaymentWayWords` and By day paid's `reference` read it. A check with no readable number says *Check · bank deposit*, and a deposit's heading says *One deposit · 2 bills*.
- The job window's payment line says *check 3045* for a reference typed *ck# 3045*, and keeps a reference that is no number as the person typed it.
- Nothing stored changes. The reference stays as it is on the row.
