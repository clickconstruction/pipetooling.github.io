# v2.8035 — Job Mode on a phone: the page no longer shows under Turnaway, Leave report and Clock In (2026-11-20)

The owner's walk on an iPhone, 2026-11-20: in Job Mode the press bar (Turnaway, then Leave report and Clock In) left a strip between itself and the tab bar, and the page scrolled through it. In the screenshot a Training row reads between Clock In and the tabs. No migration and no edge function.

## The cause

`--app-bottom-chrome` is what every piece of fixed chrome sits on. It was written as the tab bar's 60 px plus `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`.

The tab bar is not that tall. Its 60 px is a `minHeight` over a border box (`* { box-sizing: border-box }`), so the inset, which is its bottom padding, is counted inside the 60. With no inset the bar is 60 px and the sum is right, which is why a browser never showed it. On an installed iPhone the inset is 34 px and the bar stands at its content plus the inset: 83.78 px, where the sum said 94. Everything on the chrome floated 10.22 px above the bar, and nothing was drawn in between.

## The fix

- **The tab bar measures itself.** `DispatchModeFooter` (both the Dispatch and the Job Mode bar) publishes its height on the root as `--app-tab-bar`, from a `ResizeObserver` on its border box and on a window resize, and takes it back when it leaves.
- **The chrome reads it.** `src/lib/bottomChrome.ts` is the one place the offsets are written: `bottomChromeCss` (Layout's `--app-bottom-chrome`: the measured bar, the old sum until a measurement exists, `0px` with no bar), `pageBottomPaddingCss` (`<main>`'s padding), `onTabBarBottomCss` and `onTabBarKeyboardSlideCss` (the press bar's offset and its slide while the keyboard is up).
- **Whole pixels, rounded down.** `tabBarHeightPx` rounds 83.78 to 83. Rounded up, the press bar's bottom edge would stand 0.22 px above the tab bar's top and a 3x screen would show a hairline of the page. Rounded down, the two overlap by less than a pixel.
- **The press bar laps the tab bar's border.** It sits 1 px into the tab bar (`ON_TAB_BAR_LAP_PX`), covers the tab bar's top border and draws that line again as its own bottom border in `--chrome-border`. The seam is one crisp line whether the tab bar's height is whole or not.
- **The page pads by one pixel more** than the chrome and the press bar, which pays back the part pixel the rounding took, so the foot of the page never ends under the tab bar.

Every other reader of `--app-bottom-chrome` (the section dock, the Person desk, the Lien desk's windows, the pricing calculator's float) moves down the same 10 px on an iPhone and sits on the tab bar.

## Verified

Walked on the dev server with Playwright as a phone, with the home indicator's inset set through Chrome's `Emulation.setSafeAreaInsetsOverride`, at 3x.

| Case | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| 440 wide, 34 px inset, light and dark | 10.22 px strip, the page drawn in it | no strip |
| 320 wide, 34 px inset | 10.22 px strip | no strip |
| 440 wide, no inset | no strip | no strip |
| Larger text, labels wrapped, 34 and 21 px insets | 10.22 px strip | no strip |
| Dispatch Mode's bar, 34 px inset | page padded 94 px under an 83.78 px bar | padded 84 px |

The count behind *no strip*: the page was painted one loud colour, and the pixels of that colour from the press bar's top edge to the foot of the screen were counted. Zero in every case. As a control the old sum was put back, and the same count found the strip (27 rows of device pixels, the 10 px less the lap).

The last piece of the page stops above the press bar's top edge in every case. With the keyboard up, both bars leave the screen whole.

## Not in this PR

`ScheduleBlockSheet` and `HubPeoplePhoneBoard` keep their own copy of the old sum (`BOTTOM_INSET`). It costs them 10 px of spare room on an iPhone and shows nothing through, so they are left for a pass on those screens.
