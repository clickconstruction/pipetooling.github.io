# v2.5857 — The Bridge: the cash forecast expects a GC's money at the GC pace (2026-10-25)

Punch list #177, PR F, on [v2.5856](v2.5856.md); decision 1 of the Collect desk, applied to the forecast. It moves the cash line, so it merges alone.

## What was wrong

The Bridge's receipts band (`Bridge.tsx`, the `receipts` memo) scheduled every open bill through `billedExpectedPayModel`: a promise, else the payer's own median, else the company's 7-day median. A first-job GC's pay application therefore read as money due a week after it was marked billed, then as late, and the forecast expected $142k that was weeks away on 2026-10-25.

## The rule

`src/lib/bridge/collect/collectReceipt.ts` — `collectReceiptFor(bill)`: the receipt for a pile bill, its expected day by the Collect desk's pace rule ([v2.5848](v2.5848.md): the payer's own median, else the GC pace here for a GC by record, by a pay application or by a hand-marked commercial bill, else the segment median, else the company's), with the words naming the source and the pace the late grace rests on. The page reads each AR row's pile bill by key once the pile is in and falls back to the old model until then; the late grace and the doubtful line (`scheduleReceipt`) are unchanged. The forecast's caption and the help guide name the new steps.

The AR window's pace chips are unchanged: they show a customer's own pace or none, which is true. Moving `billedExpectedPayModel` itself to the GC pace would move every surface that reads it (the Dashboard's AR window, the Customer Hub, the forecast email) and belongs to its own PR with its own live check; the to-do carries it.

## Tests

`collectReceipt.test.ts`: each source's words and basis, a promise, a bill with no day.

## Verify

The Bridge as the dev, **Cash** opened: the receipts list shows *the GC pace here ~30d* on Structura's $103k with its day near the end of October, not *company pace ~7d* in early October; the cash line's low point moves accordingly.
