# v2.4789 — Legal portal: a short address opens (2026-10-07)

Two edge functions redeployed (`legal-portal`, `submit-legal-portal`); no migration. Found the minute [v2.4756](v2.4756.md) went live: the Demo link was changed to `snell-law-e77` from the window, and the probe answered 400 *Missing token*.

## The defect

Both portal functions kept the key's floor from the hex days: `legal-portal` refused a token under 16 characters before any lookup, and `submit-legal-portal` the same. v2.4756's address is a name part and three characters — `snell-law-e77` is 13 — so a short address was refused as missing. The 12-character tails of v2.4750 and the 64-hex keys were long enough and never tripped it.

## What changed

The floor is 5 in both functions, the server's own minimum for an address (`legal_portal_address_problem`: 5 to 40 characters). The ceiling stays 128. Nothing else moved; the guess gate still counts a miss.

## Why no test caught it

The functions have no unit tests, and the render and kernel tests of v2.4756 stop at the RPC call. The live check after the deploy is what found it. A small pin would be a `legal-portal` test under `src/lib/legal/` that asserts the floor; left for the next change to that function.

## Deploy

`supabase functions deploy legal-portal` and `supabase functions deploy submit-legal-portal`.
