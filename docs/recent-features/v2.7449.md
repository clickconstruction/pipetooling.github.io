# v2.7449 — Lien desk: an approved affidavit moves to Held when a fact stops it (2026-11-16)

Punch list #335, the second of the two *Also seen* items the owner answered on 2026-11-14 (*a closed gate moves an approved paper to Held, with the gate's reason*), with his call of 2026-11-16 on the approval: **it stands once the gate is answered**.

## What was wrong

`pileFor` in `src/lib/jobs/lienDeskAffidavits.ts` read a paper's stored status before its gates. An affidavit approved before a fact came to hold it (the homestead question unanswered, a homestead, a public owner, an address gone) stayed in **Ready to file**. Its To do row, its Accounts Receivable chip and the Lien window's header all read *File the affidavit*, while the window's Lien affidavit tab listed the closed gate and drew no paper, and the batch print left it out.

## What changed

- **The pile** (`pileFor`): an `approved` paper is in **Ready to file** only while every gate is clear. With a gate closed it is in **Held**. Only the pile moves: the stored status stays `approved`, nothing is written, and once the gate is answered the same paper is back in Ready to file with the leader's approval as it was. A paper waiting on the leader stays where it is.
- **Two kinds of hold in one pile.** `lienAffidavitGateHeld(entry)` (pile `held`, status `approved`) tells a gate's hold from the leader's own (status `held`); `lienAffidavitHoldingGate` names the gate. Every reader of the pile asks it:
  - **To do** (`lienNextUp.ts`): the row is the hold's own row, *Affidavit approved, held · …* with the gate's question and its press (`lienGateHoldWords`), the late notice's door when the notice is the one gate left, no press on public property, and *The approval stands.* on its second line. The leader's hold still reads *Affidavit held* with *Review the hold*.
  - **The Board** (`lienBoard.ts`): the chip stands in **Held** with `gateHeld`, opens the property record at the missing line (or the notices), and its card's stages left are *Clear what holds it* and *Notarize it and file it with the county*, where a leader's hold reads *… approves it* first.
  - **The Lien window's header and the desk's timeline** (`lienWindowDeskAffidavit` in `lienTimelineDesk.ts`): the paper is handed over as held by its fact, so the header reads *Answer the homestead question, then file the affidavit*, not *Held … waits on its hold*. The billed row and the phone chip follow through the act table (`lienOneReading.test.ts` holds the four to one step).
  - **The affidavit pane** (`LienDeskAffidavitPane.tsx`): the footer reads *Approved, and held.* with the gate's sentence, *Once that is answered the paper goes back to Ready to file by itself, and the approval stands.* and the gate's door. *Release the hold and approve* is the leader's hold's alone.
  - **Print for notarization** (`lienAffidavitPrintPlan`): the plan reads approved papers from Ready to file and from a gate's Held, so a held paper is still left out with its reason on the bar's amber press and the help sheet, as since v2.7209.
  - **Redraw on the short form** (`lienAffidavitRedrawPlan`): an earlier-form paper a gate holds is still redrawn, as while it sat in Ready to file.
  - **The lien month** (`lienMonthRhythm.ts`): a paper a gate holds counts as left to file; the leader's hold counts on neither side, as before.
- The list row already names what is missing in Held (*· missing the homestead answer*), since the gate marks are drawn in the two early piles only.
