# v2.2271 — Review queue refreshes on same-screen completions (2026-08-25)

Owner-reported: completing a task in Outstanding-by-person didn't surface it
in the "Checklist review — sign off completed work" fold just above until a
manual reload. `ChecklistReviewInboxSection` loaded once on mount and only
refetched after its own dismiss/reopen.

Fix — a new cross-surface window event, sibling to `checklist-item-saved`:

- **`checklist-instance-completed`** (detail: instanceId) is dispatched after
  a confirmed-successful completion by both engines: the shared
  `completeChecklistInstance` kernel (`src/lib/checklistCompleteInstance.ts` —
  covers every ✓ Complete panel: Today cards, Review rows, Manage activity)
  and the Review tab's dev row-✓ `markComplete` (`src/pages/Checklist.tsx`).
- `ChecklistReviewInboxSection` registers a `loadRef`-pattern listener for it
  **and for `checklist-item-saved`** (edits/deletes change queue membership
  too). `onCountChange` rides the reload, so the fold badge and the
  To-sign-off tile update in the same breath.
- Also heals the Dashboard, where the Teams Inbox queue and My Inbox card
  share a screen.

Extracted components must keep dispatching/listening to this event (add it to
the cross-surface contract alongside `checklist-item-saved` — see
`CHECKLIST_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md` quirk #5).
