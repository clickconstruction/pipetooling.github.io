# v2.7271 — The job window on a phone: the name first, one tab strip, every door under ⋯ (2026-11-14)

Punch list #355, PR A ([`to-dos/job-window-phone.md`](../../to-dos/job-window-phone.md), the mock-up [`to-dos/job-window-phone-directions.html`](../../to-dos/job-window-phone-directions.html), direction A as the owner picked it after twenty-nine passes on the drawing). Client only: no migration, no edge function, and no kernel that is copied to the edge functions changed.

## Before

The owner opened a job on his iPhone and said the top line wraps into two, the window is not easy to follow, and the buttons are not easy to press. Measured at 375 px: the seven tab pills, the ? and the ✕ ran onto two lines (78 px) before the job's name; the title block held eight presses of 20 to 31 px; the Job tab ran 4,350 px before the bar. Nineteen days of `ui_nav_clicks` on the window said the crew's bar is the live part and six of the eight icons were never pressed by anyone, on any screen.

## What changed

- **[`src/lib/jobs/jobWindowPhone.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobWindowPhone.ts)** (new, pure, tests beside it): `headerScrollState` says when the header has scrolled away (the ✕ floats) and when the reader is coming back up (the strip is drawn again); `jobWindowSheetRows` draws the ⋯ sheet's rows from the doors the pane reports, Share and Calendar first since they are the two anyone pressed, the ? card last; `jobWindowMoneyLine` folds the three tiles to one line that ends in the bar's money verb (Bill it, Send bill…, Mark paid, or nothing); `jobAccountsLine` the job accounts to one line ending in Add job account; `accountQuietWords` the house and the day of the newest packet.
- **`JobWindowModal.tsx`**: on a phone the title row (the name, the status chip, a quiet line with the number, the trade, the customer and the kind) is drawn first with ⋯ above ✕ stacked at the right, 44 px each; the seven tabs are 40 px pills on one line that scrolls sideways (`overflowX`, a mask fade at the edge, the active tab scrolled into view on change); both sit inside `.job-window-body`'s scroll, so a flick gives the whole screen to the job; a scroll listener runs `headerScrollState`, which floats a ✕ one press down from the corner once the head is gone and makes the strip `position: sticky` while the reader scrolls back up. The strip carries `data-navtrack="job-window"`, so each tab press now counts. The ⋯ opens `JobWindowMoreSheet` (new, over `PhoneSheet`), whose rows are 48 px and carry the same `data-navtrack`, so the count the icons had goes on under the new names. The desk is unchanged.
- **`DetailJobModal.tsx`** (pane mode on a phone): the action row is hidden (`display: none`; its handlers and satellite states stay) and the pane reports `JobPanePhoneDoors` through `onPhoneDoorsChange` (which doors are open for this viewer, the trade, and the press that opens one: share through `useShareJob`, the calendar, the supply-house packet, the money story, the paid report, send as a task, the change order); the three money tiles are one Money line (a tap opens the Bill tab, the act presses the bar's verb through `onPhoneVerb`) and the job accounts one line (the strip's chips compact when there are entries, *None yet* otherwise, Add job account at the end); the Street View banner draws on a phone as on a desk, on every tab, Edit included, and the *street view ▸* fold is gone; `JobPhotosStrip` gets `banner` (one row of 72 px tiles that flicks sideways, Add a photo first, each photo newest first with its day, a tap opening `JobPhotosViewer` at that photo through its new `openAt`; Add a photo opens `JobPhotosWindowLazy`, whose first tile is the camera for the field roles, and the strip reads again when it closes); `JobPaintedMapsStrip` gets `addCentred` (Paint a map as centred blue words, no box, still 40 px).
- **`jobTarget.ts`**: the line is the hours target on every screen: *Hours target · none yet*, *How many hours the job should take. The crew's hours count against it once it is set.*, **Set the hours** / **Change the hours**; `JobTargetLine` draws ⏱ for 🎯.
- **Three renames on every screen**, the owner's words: *Share with supply house* is **Add job account** (the icon, `SupplyHouseShareModal`'s title, the ledger's tip, the Dispatch errand's press, Job account PO line's tip, the journey's sublabel, the legend's row; the guide is `add-a-job-account-at-a-supply-house`, its old slug kept in `helpGuideFormerSlugs.ts`); *Send paid-in-full email* is **Email the paid-in-full report** (the icon and `PaidJobEmailSendModal`'s title); *Write up a change* is **Make change order** (the icon, `QuickEstimateWizard`'s title, the Settings switch *Make change order from the field*, My Schedule's square and link, the Job Mode card's link, the guides).
