`twin-mcp` only (server **1.4.6**), **redeploy after merge**. No migration, no client change. The owner's call of **2026-10-09**, sent by Punchlist: the harness takes the money out of a question raised on a sealed shadow.

A shadow run seals the twin's takeoff from the estimator until it is scored, but its questions went through whole. On 2026-09-29 b499 asked about the unsent b494: *SpaceX BA02 restrooms, Bastrop: an $800k restroom fit-out about 50 miles from the office…* The same day b496 asked about b490: *a kitchen TI around $80k about 55 miles from the office.* Both put the robot's size guess in front of the estimator before the bid was priced. Both are answered, and this does not rewrite filed questions.

## What changed

- **`_shared/twinQuestionShape.ts`:**
  - `scrubMoney(text)` returns the text without its dollar figures and how many went. Money is a `$` amount (`$800k`, `$1.2M`, `$12,500.00`, `$ 800 thousand`), digits with a money word (`800 dollars`, `12 grand`, `2 million`), digits with a k or M suffix that is not a unit (`800k`, but not `12k sf`), and spelled-out sums (`eight hundred thousand`, `two million dollars`, `fifty grand`). A figure used as an adjective after *a* or *an* goes with its article re-picked, so *an $800k restroom* reads *a restroom*. Any other figure reads `[amount withheld]`.
  - `questionTouchesSealedShadow` says whether a question is on a sealed shadow: its bid is the shell or the reference of an unscored shadow run, or its words name one of their numbers (`b494`, `bp494`, `bid 494`, `#499`). Any twin's run counts.
- **`twin-mcp` `ask_question`:**
  - `loadSealedShadows` reads the unscored `twin_shadow_runs` and both bids' numbers. A failed read counts as sealed, because an over-scrub is safe and a leak is not.
  - On a sealed shadow the question, each choice and the pick are scrubbed before the shape gate and the insert, in both lanes. The reply tells the robot how many figures went and to keep its guess in `add_bid_note` on its shell. A refusal after a scrub says the amounts were removed first, since two choices that differed only by an amount merge into one.
  - The tool's description says so, and `serverInfo` reads 1.4.6.
- **Not scrubbed:** questions on a bid with no unscored shadow run, `add_bid_note` (it lands on the robot's own sealed shell), and `finish_price_matrix` asks, whose figures are supply houses' quoted prices rather than the robot's guess.

On prod, 9 of the 14 shadow runs are unscored and so sealed today, b499 on b494 and b496 on b490 among them.
