## What changed

- **[`src/lib/jobs/arDepositTrail.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/arDepositTrail.ts)** (new, +test) — `buildArDepositTrail` turns one deposit's RPC rows into parts: where the money is now (`→ **jobs** when by who`, one part per day-and-person, the jobs joined), where it was (`was ~~job~~ when by who`), the bank's failure (`bank failed it 9/23`) and the take-off (`taken off 9/24 by Taunya`, or `taken off, marked returned …` on a returned deposit). A removal and a live apply within five minutes are a move: no take-off part. Rows with no actor say **the app**. `lastTouchedAt` is the latest of every time on the trail (PR 3 orders All by it). `arTrailWhenWords` says *today 4:02 PM* on the app's calendar day, else *9/29*; `bankFailedAtFromRaw` reads Mercury's `failedAt`.
- **`ArDepositRow.tsx`** — `ArDepositTrailLine`, one line under the meta line, the live jobs bold and the old ones struck; `trail` is a new optional prop.
- **`BankPaymentsModal.tsx`** — beside the list, one read of `list_ar_deposit_trails` for the rows on screen and the All rows behind a search (500 ids a call), grouped per deposit and built with the deposit's own flags (marked returned, the bank's return); cleared and re-read on every list refresh. The RPC is called `as never` until the types regenerate; *could not find the function* switches the read off for the visit, so the client ships before the push.

## Decisions taken

- **The archive's `deleted_at` is the removal time**, not the history's *Payment removed* event: it is exact by construction and does not wait on `20260930213000` reaching prod.
- **Who applied it comes from the history**, which is where the app already records it; the 13 rows this month with no actor there read *the app* until PR 4 names the path.
- **No trail is no line.** A deposit nothing ever happened to shows nothing new.
