## What changed

Title, paragraphs (the conditional language on the final payment), `checkFrom` used, no through date; `unconditional_final` prefills the whole covered amount. `LIEN_WAIVER_FORM_CITES` names each form's subsection; `lienWaiverWhy` is the one line under the toggles. `isLienWaiverFormType` / new `isConditionalLienForm` in [`lienReleaseTracking.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienReleaseTracking.ts) — clearance now waits on either conditional form.
- **Two questions, not four buttons.** `lienWaiverToggles` / `lienWaiverFormFrom` map the grid; the window's form row is *Conditional | Unconditional* · *Progress | Final*, the cite beside it, the why line under it. `pickLienWaiverForBill(job, invoice)` answers both from a bill — settled when the payments applied to it reach its amount; final when it is the last minted line and the minted lines together bill the job's revenue — with the two facts as words (*Not settled yet · Bill 2 of 3 · not the last*). ## Design calls

- **One signature per instrument, drawn.** No stored auto-signature; the leader draws each time. On someone else's screen a typed name would be their keyboard, so the pad is the only way there.
- **The signer is the leader; the device is named beside him, never in his place.** `signer_user_id` stays the leader. The lien desk's *he is standing over me* is a different thing — a declaration about a spoken word — and keeps its recorder because the leader never touched the screen. A signature needs none.
- **The bill picks the form; the office can override.** The toggles stay live on a draft, and the cite line says when the choice differs from the bill's (*the bill would pick Conditional · progress*).
- **Recipient read server-side.** The function accepts only the job's customer email or its GC's billing email, both read by the function; the client's `customer_email` is checked against them.
