# v2.7601 — Estimates: the send plan and the recipient rule become a kernel (2026-11-17)

Client only; no migration, no edge function, no screen change. Punch list **#46** row 3 (`to-dos/decomposition-queue.md`): cut 2 of eight on the Estimates page, its first kernel — Stage-A rows 2 and 5 of the map (`docs/ESTIMATES_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md`). Stacked on v2.7596.

## What shipped

- **`src/lib/estimates/estimateSendPlan.ts`** — `estimateSendPlan({ customerEmail, override, isChangeOrder, totalCents, role })` answers one of three: `refuse` with its reason (no address, or one that is not an address), `sheet` (a normal estimate above $0 sent by an office role opens Tell the customer), or `confirm` with the title, the message that names the recipient and the button's words. `estimateRecipientEmail(customerEmail, override)` is the address rule: the customer's own email wins, the typed one stands in. `SEND_EMAIL_RE` moved with them.
- **`Estimates.tsx`** — `sendToCustomer` asks the plan and acts on it; the step rail's *email present* tick, the draft save's `customer_email` and the Email preview's To line read `estimateRecipientEmail`. The `GC_OFFICE_TEAM` import left the page. 5,356 lines (was 5,362).
- **12 tests** (`estimateSendPlan.test.ts`): the recipient from each source, neither, and whitespace; the address pattern; no address; a bad address; every office role above $0 gets the sheet; a typed address reaches the sheet; a role outside the office gets the confirm; a change order asks *Send for signature?*; a $0 total says so and the press reads *Send anyway*, for an estimate and a change order; a credit change order has no $0 note.

## What stayed the same, on purpose

- The order in `sendToCustomer`: the held-date stop, the customer, the customer's re-read, then the plan. The `discard_guard_shown` click is still recorded once for a send that reaches the sheet or the confirm, and never for a refusal.
- The Email preview's To line still shows a typed address on a draft with no customer picked.
- The hydration's own use of the rule (a customer with an email clears the typed address) is not touched; it leaves with the hydration plan.
