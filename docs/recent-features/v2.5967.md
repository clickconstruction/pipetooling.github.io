# v2.5967 — What everyone sees checks every screen: contrast, sideways scroll, broken images and errors (2026-10-26)

Punch list #194, PR 4 ([`to-dos/experiences-lens/`](../../to-dos/experiences-lens/README.md); the sealed frame v2.5960, the tab v2.5963). The checks a style change needs, on every screen a role can open.

- **The checks** ([`lensAudit.ts`](../../src/lib/lens/lensAudit.ts), 4 tests) are the four that `scripts/visual-qa.mjs` runs from a terminal. The tab runs them in the frame's own document once the frame settles:
  - text under 3:1 against its effective background, skipping elements on a background image and the lens mark;
  - a page that scrolls sideways;
  - a broken image;
  - the errors the frame caught.

  Each frame wears **Clean** or what it found, and *What the check found* lists the texts with their ratios.
- **Settling**: a frame settles once its app has booted (its first asks are in) and its asks stop growing, three seconds in, or eight at most. A check that cannot read a screen, or a screen that draws nothing in sixteen seconds, reports that as a finding, so a sweep never waits on one.
- **The errors**: the seal keeps what the frame's console and window catch on `window.__lens.errors`: `console.error`, uncaught errors and unhandled rejections, at most 50. The real console still prints, and circular values are read safely.
- **Every screen** is a fourth *Across*. It lays out every screen the role can open, one card each, once per address, under the held state, width and theme.
  - **Check every screen** draws the first six unchecked screens (`sweepWindow`). Each check makes room for the next, so the whole list is checked while you watch.
  - The count line adds up what the checks found. *Screens to look at* lists each flagged screen and scrolls to its card.
- **Words**: the guide gains *Check every screen*; the Glossary's *The lens's sealed frame*; the to-do's contact-sheet line, which said frames load as you scroll and now says how the sweep works.
