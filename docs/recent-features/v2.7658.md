# v2.7658 — Tell the dev: a wrench on every page sends a note with the page you are on (2026-11-17)

Punch list #380, PR 2 of six: the wrench, its box and Fix Mode. It stands on v2.7650's ground: the widened `help_feedback` table and the kernels in `src/lib/tellTheDev/`.

## What a person sees

- **The wrench.** At a desk it floats bottom left, with the words Tell the dev until that person has opened it once. On a phone it sits in the header beside Search, because Job Mode's bars own a phone's bottom edge and toasts its top right.
- **The box.** Three starts, the rest of the sentence, the list under *Goes with your note*, and Send to the dev. At a desk it opens above the wrench with the page still in view. On a phone it is a sheet.
- **Fix Mode.** A row in the gear menu, drawn as its neighbours are, and a row in the phone dock's More sheet. On until switched off, per person per device. Off draws no wrench and hears no door.
- **Notes to the dev.** The inbox called Help feedback, on the Dashboard's Teams inbox card and in the Checklist's review. Each note shows its kind, its page and its state, with Open that page and Copy as a brief. Close is now Answer and close.

## How it is built

- **`TellTheDevRoot`** is mounted once in `Layout`. Every door asks through a window event (`requestTellTheDev`), so a door outside the shell reaches the same box.
- **The page is read inside the press.** The page key, the address, the open window's title, the screen and the trouble memory are taken when the event fires, before a menu or a window can close under the pointer.
- **The open window** is asked of the page (`openWindowTitle`): the last element that declares itself a dialog, by its label. The box's own window is marked and never counts.
- **The desk box takes its own Escape**, so a window open under it stays open.
- **The version** is the newest release note's, loaded when the box first opens and never at boot.
- **The trouble memory is fed** from two places: an error toast's words (`ToastContext`) and a refused call (`wrapFetchRememberingRefusals` in `supabase.ts`). A lost signal feeds neither.
- **A resend counts once.** The note's id is made on the client, so a second landing meets its own row.
- **Opens and sends are counted** in `ui_nav_clicks`: `tell_the_dev_open` with the door, `tell_the_dev_sent` with the kind. That is the test's meter.
