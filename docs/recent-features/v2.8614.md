# v2.8614 — Bring back: a sub with no wage on file skips the Wage row (2026-11-26)

Punch list #389, finding 188 of the walk of 2026-11-25 (sitting 19, walk B), briefed by the fixing thread as part 1 of `389-bring-back-wage-row-and-the-pay-log.md` and answered by the owner on 2026-11-26: skip the row for a subcontractor, way A. Client only. No migration, no edge function. Part 2 of that brief, a wage changed on a screen leaving no line on the pay log, is a migration and is not in this PR.

**Read.** **Bring back…** on an archived ZZ Test sub opened its checklist. Team and Paperwork took **Leave open…** with a reason. The Wage row took only a wage: Call 3 of the window (v2.6523) builds it with `canLeaveOpen: false` for everyone, and **Keep** is offered only when a wage above zero is on file, so a person with no pay setup had **Change…** alone and the finish stayed grey until a wage was typed. The walker stopped and asked, and the sub came back with a one-dollar wage made up for the walk. A subcontractor is paid through sub labor and may never have an hourly wage.

**The mend.**

- `kindNeedsWage(kind)` in `lib/people/bringBack.ts`: everyone but a `sub`. The facts carry it as `needsWage`, read beside `needsTeam` from the person's kind (`bringBackFacts.ts`).
- `buildBringBackChecklist`: when `needsWage` is false and no wage above zero is on file, the Wage row is `skipped`, reads *Not needed for their kind*, has no press and does not hold the finish, exactly as Team is for a kind that works in no team. A sub with a wage someone set keeps Call 3, Keep or Change, since that wage is on their row and should be looked at.
- The guide *bring back a teammate who left* says so on its Wage card. The glossary's Bring back entry has the line.

**Where it differs from the brief:** the brief's words were *for a person whose kind is a subcontractor, the Wage row reads Not needed for their kind and is skipped*. Built for a sub with no wage on file; a sub with a wage on file keeps the row, so a wage on their row is never passed over in silence. Nobody has asked for the wider reading.

## What did not change

- Call 3 for every other kind: the Wage row is pressed, Keep or Change, and never left open.
- The pay row is not written for a skipped row: `planBringBackWrites` writes a wage only after **Change…**.
- The pay log. Part 2 of the brief is its own lane.

## Tests

- `bringBack.test.ts`: a sub with no pay setup or an empty pay row reads the skipped row and the finish counts four open; a sub with a wage keeps Call 3; `kindNeedsWage` on sub, helper, assistant and null.
- `PersonDeskBringBackModal.render.test.tsx`: a sub with no pay setup draws *Wage · Not needed for their kind* with no Keep or Change, the button reads one fewer open, and the finish writes no pay row.
