## The page

`/developments/:id` (`src/pages/DevelopmentPage.tsx`), reached from the house line on a bid (`BidPreviewModal`) and a job (`DetailJobModal`), and from the Pipeline's and the Bid Board's house filters once PR 3 lands their param. The reads are one fetch (`src/lib/developments/fetchDevelopmentPage.ts`): the development, its bids and jobs, the bids and jobs at the same address that are not in it, the requests on them, the sends, the plan filings, the stage events, bills, payments, the job's and bid's thread notes, the customers' contact log and the pay-speed RPC the Pipeline's billed rows use. Every rule is a pure kernel in `src/lib/developments/` with a unit test beside it; the components in `src/components/developments/` are the shapes.

- **The head**: the customer and the GC with their doors, the people on site, and the five count tiles (Waiting, Bids, Jobs, Bid undecided, Open on bills). Rest on a tile and its card drops under it (`countCards.ts`, `useHoverCards`): the records behind the number grouped by the count's own rule, eight rows then *and N more*, the board's door in the foot; a press pins it, Escape or a press elsewhere closes, a phone opens it as a sheet. An empty count shows the kinds it would hold and the press that fills it.
- **The strip**: five looks, History, Pieces, Money, Plans, Questions, each with its count and what in it waits; a press jumps; each look folds and the fold is remembered per device (`devicePrefs.ts`).
- **The *not in it* line**: what shares the site's address and is not in the development, as two chips (*21 jobs*, *13 bids*) whose cards list them by stage or status with *Show all N in Pieces ›* in the foot; the spike's *Show the N here* toggle draws them in the table until PR 3's *Put the N in* lands.
- **History** (`whatChanged.ts`, `history.ts`, `DevelopmentFeed`): one stream, newest first, of what the records did and what people wrote: sends and contacts, won and lost, each plan filing numbered on its bid (*set 2 · replaces set 1 of Oct 3*), stage moves, bills and payments, the job's and the bid's thread notes, the customers' contact log, the questions and their answers. *You last looked* is a line inside it and *Mark as looked* moves it. Chips narrow by kind (*Plans* alone is the history of plans) and a Pieces row's *history ›* narrows to that piece; the search covers every line; the undated notes a record carries sit in an *On record* block; a line past 260 characters folds behind *more ›*, which opens the whole of it in a window (`DevelopmentLineWindow`). The entries people keep at the time something happened, their table, the composer and the MCP verbs, are PR 2h and PR 2m.
- **Pieces** (`pieces.ts`, `DevelopmentPieces`, `DevelopmentPairCells`): each bid with the job it became, the plans on it and what it waits on; the gap cells offer *Pick the job*, *New job from bid*, *Not a match* and *Pair JPxxx* from the likely pairs by address and name (`pairing.ts`); **Pair bids with jobs** turns the table into the tool; the paid jobs fold with their sum.
- **Money** (`siteMoney.ts`, `DevelopmentMoney`): the band (percent done only while every live job with a value has one, else *N jobs have no percent on record ›*, a press that sorts by percent and lights them), the sections by stage with their sums, sortable inside each, the billed rows with the lien clock and the expected pay day from the customer's pay speed.
- **Plans** (`plans.ts`): every set newest first with the pieces on it, a chip for the pieces without plans that opens them, *Ask the estimators about the N*.
- **Questions**: the open questions on the site's pieces with their age, and, with none, the kinds a site waits on with their presses (the asks themselves are PR 4's).

## Words and rules kept

The page tells nothing it can show: empty states are the kinds with their state and the press that fills them (`EmptyState` where a sentence remains); every error names its reason (`formatErrorMessage`); theme tokens throughout; the plain-words rules hold the guide. The spike's device-only history and its composer were left out of this PR on purpose.
