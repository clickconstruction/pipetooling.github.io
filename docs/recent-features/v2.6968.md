# v2.6968 — The dev seat writes a bid's takeoff: plan_bid_takeoff and apply_bid_takeoff (2026-11-12)

PR 3 of the bid train (`to-dos/mcp-servers.md` → The bid train), after the counts pair (v2.6967). The owner, 2026-11-11: "fill out all of the line items for counts, takeoffs, labor, pricing."

## What was added

One plan → apply pair on `dev-mcp` (ninety-one write verbs). No wrapper RPC and no migration: the rows are the ones the Takeoffs tab writes, as the dev under the tables' RLS.

- **`plan_bid_takeoff` / `apply_bid_takeoff`** (`supabase/functions/_shared/devMcpBidTakeoff.ts`): `takeoff = { bid_id, rows: [{ fixture, group?, lines?, replace?, materials_each?, materials_from? }], version_id? }`, up to 100 rows and 400 lines a call.
- **A part line** is the tab's own save (`persistTakeoffRoughPartLine`): the quantity for one counted unit, the unit price, where the price came from, how the part is sold, its place after the row's last line.
- **The price**: left out, the parts book's lowest price with its price row, a tie to the smaller id. No price in the book is $0 and the plan says so. A typed price forgets the price row.
- **The quoted cost each** is `bid_count_row_custom_costs`, one a row, upserted on the bid and the count row as QuoteCompare's Apply writes it. `null` clears it.
- **The plan** states one unit's materials and the bid's materials before and after through `combinedMaterials` from the kernel copy: before tax, with the order rounding. It is the Takeoffs tab's Materials tile and what Pricing sees.

## Three choices worth knowing

1. **One line a part on a row.** The tab lets a part sit twice on a row and the table has no key. The seat changes the line that carries the part, adds a part the row lacks, and holds a part several lines carry. `replace: true` rewrites the row's lines whole, and a replace with a held line holds the whole row so nothing is lost.
2. **The quoted cost has one source.** The table's CHECK allows `quoted` alone. No screen lets a person type a row's materials cost by hand: the writers are the quote compare window and twin-mcp. A cost off a paper proposal lands as quoted, with who quoted it in `house_name` and the dev in `applied_by`.
3. **A row with both** part lines and a quoted cost reads two ways, as it does today: Pricing costs the row from the quoted cost, and the Takeoffs tab, Labor and the approval PDF total the lines. The plan warns.

## What the screen does that the verb does not

An assembly expanded into its parts or bought as one bundle line. A part made new in the parts book and a price filed in it. The drag order of a row's lines. One line removed without a replace. The tax rate, the stage split and the takeoff book.
