# v2.7244 — A won bid waits to learn whether it has a job before it offers to open one (2026-11-13)

Client only. No migration, no edge function, and no kernel that is copied to the edge functions changed. Found on the live walk of v2.7201.

The Job block on a bid (`BidWonJobActions`) asks `useJobsOpenedFromBid` whether the bid has a job. The hook returned `{ jobs, loaded }` and the block never read `loaded`. So until the lookup answered, a bid that had a job read as having none, and the block drew the doors that start one: **Open the job** and **Ask Dispatch to open it** for the office, **Ask Dispatch to open the job** for a primary, **Send to Dispatch** for an estimator. On the walk a script pressed **Open the job** the instant the block appeared, on a bid whose job existed, and New Job opened filled in from the bid. Nothing was created. A person on a slow connection could do the same. An estimator reads no job row, so for that role the lookup is the only way the block learns a job exists.

## What changed

- **The rule** (`wonMomentActions.ts`): `wonMomentActions` takes `jobsLoaded`. When it is `false` the answer holds no door that starts a job or hands one to Dispatch (`create`, `create_another`, `ask_dispatch`, `send_draft`, named by `startsOrHandsOffAJob`). `open_existing` stays, since it needs a job the caller already holds. Left out, the rule is as it was.
- **The block** (`BidWonJobActions.tsx`): `jobsLoaded` is true when the caller passed `knownJob`, and otherwise when the block's own lookup has answered. The full shape and the `compact` link both draw from the same actions, so both wait. The Bid Board's rows pass `knownJob` and draw at once, as before. Edit Bid's Job block, Followup's You won it strip and the package sends' won rows look the job up themselves, so they wait.
- **The hook** (`useJobsOpenedFromBid.ts`): the answer keeps the bid it was read for, and `loaded` is true only for that bid. Before, a block moved from one bid to another without a remount kept the first bid's answer, and `loaded`, until the second read landed. A read that throws still answers, with no job, so a failed lookup ends with the doors drawn as before. A refetch after a job is created keeps the old answer until the new one lands, as before.

While it waits, the full shape draws nothing unless an open Dispatch to-do gives it the amber chip. The block appears when the answer does.

## Tests

- `wonMomentActions.test.ts` (15, three new): no door for any role while the lookup is out, `open_existing` alone when a job is held, and the same doors as before once it answers.
- `BidWonJobActions.render.test.tsx` (17, eight new), with the lookup held open by the test: an office role, a primary and an estimator see no door before the answer, a bid with a job never shows one that starts a job, a bid with no job shows them after, the `compact` link waits too, a lookup that rejects still ends with the doors, a block moved to another bid waits again, and `knownJob` draws at once with no lookup. With the rule taken out of the block, seven of the eight fail.

## Verify

Not walked in the app. The render tests hold the lookup open and press nothing until it answers, which is the moment the walk hit.
