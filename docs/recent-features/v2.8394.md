# v2.8394 — People → Contracts: a sub's company has its own papers (2026-11-23)

Punch list #403, step 6, PR 2 of 3: the company's papers and its send. It stacks on v2.8381 (PR 1, the company rows). Client only: it calls `gc_company_paper` (the four roles since v2.8313) and `send-contract-for-signature`'s company branch with `from: 'office'` (the four roles since v2.8322). No migration and no edge function, so nothing is owed after the merge.

## What was there

v2.8381 drew a company's row with its workers under it, and the company's own papers were still kept off the tab: a business's agreement and W-9, filed under `gc-company:<id>`, showed nowhere on People.

## What changed

- **The company's papers, once.** Under the company's row, the newest copy of each of its papers, with a line such as *Version 2 · signed Nov 14 by Pat Sample, Owner* or *Form · sent Nov 18 · not opened*, and its state chip (`companyPaperLines`, `companyPaperDetail`, `src/lib/people/subCompanyPapers.ts`). A company with none says what fills it.
- **Send for signature.** A window (`SubCompanyPaperWindow.tsx`) offers the subs' papers from the Contract Book (`audience = 'sub'`), holding one the company has signed with its reason. It opens on the worker with a title and their roster address, and takes someone else by name. The send keeps the signer at the company as a person who gets its contracts (`gc_company_people`, matched by address so nobody is added twice), makes or refreshes the copy (`gc_company_paper`), names the signer on it (`signer_name`, `signer_title`, `signer_email`, only while the copy is not signed), then calls the company branch with `from: 'office'`, so the email goes out under the name a sub knows. A copy signed between the read and the press is not sent again.
- **Enter from paper.** The same window takes who signed, their title, the day (not after today) and an optional link to the scan, makes or refreshes the copy, and marks it signed with *Entered from paper.* No function is called and nothing is emailed. A form's boxes are not keyed here: the paper entry function files a person's paper and refuses a company's name by the table's own check, so keying a company's W-9 is left for later.
- **Each worker's own papers** stay on their row and now say *signing for* the company, read from the paper's own `signing_for`.
- **Foot:** *The same papers are on Documents → Agreements.* under a company's last worker.

## Defaults for the owner's read

- Papers already filed under a person stay where they are. No data is moved.
- The office's four roles do the pressing.
- An estimator's chip is unchanged.
- The send mails the signer and anyone else at the company who gets its contracts, as the company branch always has.
- Enter from paper keeps the scan as a link and keys no form's boxes.
