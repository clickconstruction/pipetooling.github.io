# v2.7830 — Materials on site: By person shows who changed a box (2026-11-18)

The fourth PR of the train on seeing what one person does with the material on a site. Client only. It reads the tables of `20261118140000_on_site_box_changes.sql` (v2.7825), so that migration is pushed before this deploys.

## What landed

- **The trail in the list.** The By person look now carries the changes that are not ledger rows: a box retired, brought back, changed (its kind, where line or words, with the old value and the new) or renamed, and a part tie or a site price taken off, each with who did it. They sort into the same days as the presses and take the same person and kind filters.
- **Its own read**, [`sitePeopleTrailIo.ts`](../../src/lib/materials/sitePeopleTrailIo.ts): `job_container_changes` and `job_container_names` over the site's boxes, `on_site_removed_answers` on the site and on its jobs, read when the look opens and never with the desk. The two new tables are read untyped until the types regenerate after the push.
- **A read that fails is said, not hidden.** The presses come from the store and still draw. A line over the figures says the box changes are not in the list, gives the database's reason and offers **Try again**.
- **The kernel** (`sitePersonActs.ts`) takes the three lists as optional inputs and gains `outsideProcess`, the rule a later PR applies to a watched person. `pressAnswers.ts` is new and unused by a screen yet: what taking presses back would do to the numbers, summed with `containerLines` twice.

## The limits

- The trail starts at the push. A box retired or edited before it recorded no person, so it is not in the list.
- A box change wears no chip. Whose process it breaks is the watch's to say, in a later PR.

## Tests

`sitePersonActs.test.ts` (the trail's words, a box that is gone, the kinds, `outsideProcess`), `pressAnswers.test.ts`, `sitePeopleTrailIo.test.ts` (what a removed answer said), and two new cases in `SitePeopleView.render.test.tsx`: the trail joins the list once its read answers, and a failed read says so, keeps the presses and reads again.
