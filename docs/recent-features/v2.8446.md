# v2.8446 — Bills sent: a chip with nothing to chase says so (2026-11-24)

One fault of the Bills sent page found on the live test of 2026-11-24 (punch list #422, problem 9). No migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

Three chips of By day sent list what still wants someone: **Did not arrive**, **Not paid** and **No send on record**. With nothing under one, the list answered as it does for a search that missed: *No sends match the filters. Clear the filters*. On the live page **Did not arrive** held nothing, which is the good state, and it read like a miss.

## What changed

- `billsSentEmptyGood(route, typed)`, new and pure, names the three chips whose empty is good, with who the list would be waiting on and why there is nothing. It answers null for a chip that only sorts the sends, and whenever a search is typed.
- The list draws that state through `EmptyState` over `emptyWords`' *waiting* kind, the repo's words for an empty that is good:
  - **Did not arrive**: *Nothing waiting on a resend. No send on an open bill bounced.*
  - **Not paid**: *Nothing waiting on a payment. Every bill on this list is paid in full.*
  - **No send on record**: *Nothing waiting on a send. Every bill the app made has a send on record.*
- Each offers **See every send**, which clears the chip.
- A search that finds nothing under one of these chips still reads *No sends match ‘…’.*
- The guide says what the empty chip reads.
