# v2.3624 — Sign-in page: "More apps" lists the rest of the Tooling family (2026-09-19)

Owner-directed follow-up to v2.3622. Client only; no migration.

## The change

- **The list** — new [`lib/toolingFamily.ts`](../../src/lib/toolingFamily.ts): `FEATURED_APPS` (the two tiles with marks) and `MORE_APPS` (ten more). Only apps with a LIVE public URL are listed — a dead link on the sign-in page is worse than a missing one. Left out on purpose: LoanTooling (empty repo), testing-pipetooling (a test copy), and **ChecklistTooling + SVGTooling, which have no GitHub Pages site published** (both 404 at every candidate URL on 2026-09-19; enable Pages on those repos and add them back). Unit test: unique keys and URLs, https with a trailing slash, names and blurbs present, marks only on the featured two, this app never listed.
- **The disclosure** — [`ToolingFamilyStrip.tsx`](../../src/components/ToolingFamilyStrip.tsx): a native `<details>` under the two tiles. The summary is a frosted pill ("More apps" + a count badge, a chevron that flips when open); the list expands in place as two columns of glass tiles (one column under 480px), so nothing floats over the photo or gets clipped by the stack. No React state; keyboard and screen-reader behaviour come with the element.
- **Styles** — appended to [`authPublicLanding.css`](../../src/components/authPublicLanding.css), the same `rgba(255,255,255,.14)` + `blur(10px)` glass as the trade tiles.
- **Tests** — `SignIn.render.test.tsx`: the disclosure exists, its ten links are https + new tab + `noopener`, and the unlisted apps never appear; the v2.3622 test now scopes its "two links" check to the featured row.
