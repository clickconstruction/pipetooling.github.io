## Who gets a copy, and who does not

`signedCopyHold` in `_shared/personPaperSignedCopyEmail.ts`:

- **Gets one:** the signer of an agreement, when the paper kept an address. That is a paper emailed since v2.7986, a person's or an outside party's.
- **Never:** an hours statement, a W-9, a certificate, a licence. Their PDF holds a tax number or is payroll's.
- **None:** a paper signed in person with no address on it, a paper sent before the send kept addresses, and a trade company's paper, whose send keeps no signer.
- **A two-party form** sends nothing when the signer signs: the PDF is not finished until the office signs its half.
- **Once.** `signed_copy_sent_at` is stamped, and a paper is signed once.

## It never costs a signature

The copy runs after the signature has committed, inside a try, and waits eight seconds at most. A PDF that cannot be drawn or an email that fails is logged and the signer still reads *Thank you*. The paper's PDF is then made the first time the office opens it.
