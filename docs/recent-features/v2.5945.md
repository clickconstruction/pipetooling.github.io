`BilledAwaitingPaymentSection`'s smoke fails between 7pm and midnight Chicago time on main too: `touchYmd` in `lib/quickfill/leanRead.ts` reads the UTC date, so a lean logged that evening reads *leaned on -1d ago*. With the clock pinned to noon its three cases pass, the new fold assertion with them. The fix belongs in its own PR.

## Is this the best we can do?

Most presses are the surface's own control, threaded through a prop where the list sits under the page that owns it. Three are links instead, because the opener lives on another page: the Jobs grid's New Job opens `/jobs?tab=stages&newJob=true`, the deep link the Jobs page already reads; an empty dispatch roster links People → Users, where the crew is added; and Dispatch mode's Home falls back to the Jobs page when the app's New Job window is not mounted. Seven lists keep words alone because their press needs a choice first, so the words name the form: Generate PO, the office roster's Add, Create recurring share, Add rule, Add Personal Time Off, the Monday email's Save, and a scope book's Save the set. The geocode review window names the map behind it, since no reset handler exists in the window. The GC board's *A bid comes here once we win it.* and *A won job comes here once work starts.*, and the roster's *adds the crew*, are written from the code's flow, not the owner's words; they are the first place to ask him.
