# v2.6545 — Themes on the record: a catalog of the patterns to look for across a person’s file, with what counts and counsel’s words (2026-11-05)

Punch list #269, PR T1. A large record on a person is read by pattern, not by date: counsel asks what shows dishonesty, what shows interference with the business, and how each piece proves it. The HR file and a person's matter held the facts and the exhibits with nothing to gather them by, so the answer was a search through the whole record each time. A theme is that gathering word, named once for the company in plain words, with one line on what counts and counsel's words beside it. Migration `20261106000000_legal_themes.sql` ([`docs/migrations/`](../migrations/20261106000000_legal_themes.md)): push right after the merge.

## What changed

- The schema: `legal_themes` (the catalog: name, what counts, counsel's words, retired never deleted, the agent's proposal until kept) and `legal_theme_marks` (a theme on an HR entry, a piece of evidence, a matter document or a matter event, with why it fits, how it proves the theme, who marked it, proposed until kept, removed with a reason and never deleted; the trigger holds a mark to what it was when made). Both a dev's; the HR agent reads and proposes only.
- `supabase/functions/_shared/legalThemes.ts`, the kernel the app and the dev seat share (the door is `src/lib/legal/legalThemes.ts`): the four ways a mark proves a theme and their words, `themeDraftProblem` (a name, under the caps, not a standing theme's name whatever the case), `sortThemes` (standing by name, retired after), `marksByTheme`, `markCountWords`, `proofCountsWords` (*2 by a document · 0 by their own words · 1 firsthand · 0 relayed*), `proposedCountWords` and `markIsProposed`. `legalThemesIo.ts` reads the catalog and the marks (a table not pushed yet reads as empty) and adds, edits, retires, brings back and keeps a theme.
- Settings → People & teams → **Themes on the record** (`LegalThemesSettingsBlock.tsx`, drawn for a dev): the standing themes by name, each with its live mark count and the proof behind them, Edit in place, Retire after a confirm that says the marks keep their meaning, the agent's proposals dashed with Keep, and the retired themes folded under the list with Bring back. *+ Add a theme* opens the form: name, what counts, counsel's words if any.
- The guide *keep private employee files* gains *Name a theme to look for*; `HR_FILES.md` → Themes on the record, `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` → Legal desk and the Glossary's HR files entry carry the catalog.
