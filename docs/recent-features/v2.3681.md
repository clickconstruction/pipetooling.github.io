# v2.3681 — Lien desk: a missed month says what is lost and what is not, and the affidavit shows which months it covers (2026-09-21)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Options 1–3 of the missed-month review on job 258, built as drawn in the Claude artifact *Missed Month, Money Kept* after the owner's "go ahead and build options 1 to 3 as mocked up". Option 4 is [v2.3679](./v2.3679.md) (the alarm) + [v2.3680](./v2.3680.md) (the RPC floor). Stacked on v2.3679.

## Why

The owner asked to "roll a prior month's information into the most past month that still counts." The desk's answer is no: a § 53.056 notice states the month the labor was performed, and the sworn affidavit repeats it, so re-dating June's work to July would misstate both (Civ. Prac. & Rem. Code ch. 12). What the owner was really reacting to was the dot's sentence — *the lien right on Jun 2026 work is gone* — which reads as if the money were gone. It is not: the notice's claim amount is the job's whole open balance, so June's money already rides on July's notice. Only the lien as security for June's work is lost. The three options say exactly that, in the three places a person looks.

## What shipped

- **Option 1 — the missed dialog** ([`LienDeskMonths.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskMonths.tsx)): *Window closed · Sep 15 · no notice, no skip on record* (or *· no notice* once noted, with the *Noted* row from v2.3679), the hours, then two lines from `lienMonthMissedWords` — **Lien:** *gone for June 2026 work.* **Money:** *still owed, and on this notice — the claim is the whole $9,800.* When no month is still open the money line says *chase it in Collections; no notice can carry it now.* The v2.3679 *Note it as missed* row stays.
- **Option 2 — the strip** shipped in v2.3679; the mock-up's second pass folded the claim-box line into it, so nothing more here.
- **Option 3 — the affidavit's months** ([`affidavitMonths.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/affidavitMonths.ts), 2 tests; [`LienDeskAffidavitPane.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskAffidavitPane.tsx)): a *Months the affidavit claims* card on every sub job's pane, from the forecast's work months and the desk's month history (`buildLienMonthHistory` over the desk items and the RPC rows). Each month is *on the lien* (notice sent), *window open* (mail by …) or, under **Worked, not noticed**, *unsecured* — window closed with no notice, or skipped by whom. The closing sentence names what the affidavit claims and sends the unsecured share to Collections. The modal passes `workMonths[jobId]` to the pane; a direct-with-owner job (no monthly notice) shows no card.
- Guide *send lien notices from the Lien desk*: the two lines and the card.

## Not done, on purpose

- The affidavit document itself is unchanged: it already names the months from the desk item; the card is the human-readable check beside it.
- No dollars per month — the desk has hours per month, not billing per month; the card says "share of the balance".
