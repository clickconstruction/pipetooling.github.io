# v2.4950 — Accounts Receivable: card disputes and failed bank payments are cases (2026-10-08)

Punch list #76, piece 1 (`to-dos/ar-returned-checks.md`). A bill paid through Stripe read paid whatever came after. A customer can dispute a card charge with their bank, and Stripe takes the money back while the dispute runs and keeps it if they win. A bank debit (ACH) can fail days after the customer pressed Pay. `stripe-webhook` read neither. Both now open a case beside the checks that came back: the same list under **Came back**, the same pane, the same notice.

## What the office sees

- **card disputed** (red).
  - The story: *$500 was paid by card on bill 1 of #878* · *Heron Construction disputed it. They say they did not make the payment.*
  - The stake: *#878 reads paid. Stripe holds the $500 while the dispute runs.*
  - Next: *Answer the dispute in Stripe by Oct 20*, with **Open the dispute in Stripe** (test or live) and **They said…**.
- **dispute lost** (red), once the customer wins.
  - The stake: *#878 reads paid. The $500 is gone.* Next: *Put the bill back, then bill them again.*
  - **Put the bill back** reads back what changes, then presses once (below).
- **bank payment failed** (amber).
  - The stake: *Bill 3 on #878 is still open*, with Stripe's own words. Next: *Ask them for another payment*, with **They said…**.
- The office is told by email and push, through the same `sendReturnCaseNotice` as a returned check. A dispute is told when it opens and again when it is lost. The Dashboard's **Needs you** card counts the cases: *Heron Construction disputed a $500 card payment*, *2 payments came back*.

## The lost dispute: option (b), and why

Punchlist asked for (a) if the existing functions made it one call, else (b). They do not, so this is (b). Neither existing reversal can take back a bill Stripe charged. `reverse-stripe-invoice-out-of-band-payment` needs our own out-of-band mark, and `void-stripe-invoice-for-revert` answers 409 on real money. And nothing comes off a job until a person presses: every case works that way.

So the case offers one press, **Put the bill back** (`put_back_lost_dispute_bill`). In one transaction:
- the payment comes off the job, with a *removed* line in its history naming the dispute and who pressed
- the bill drops its Stripe link and goes back to Ready to Bill (Billed, if another payment still sits on it), to be billed again with a fresh Stripe bill
- a paid job goes back to Billed
- the case closes as *put_back*

Then the client moves the job to Ready to Bill when no billed line remains, as every send-back does. Stripe is not called: its invoice stays paid, and the dispute is Stripe's record.

Bill truth reads payments, so until the press the bill counts as paid. Until then the case is red on top of Came back, on the Dashboard, and in the second notice. Making every money reader treat a lost dispute as unpaid without removing the payment would touch every reader of `payments_made`. If the owner prefers the bill put back on its own, the webhook can call the same function on the lost event: one line.

## The change

- **Migration** `20261009080000_ar_stripe_cases.sql` ([doc](../migrations/20261009080000_ar_stripe_cases.md)):
  - `ar_stripe_cases`
  - `record_ar_stripe_case` (service role)
  - the self-closes: taken_off, paid, voided
  - `close_ar_stripe_case`, `reopen_ar_stripe_case`, `put_back_lost_dispute_bill`
  - `list_ar_return_cases` with a third branch and a `stripe_case` column. The return type changes, so the function is dropped and made again.
- **`stripe-webhook`** takes `charge.dispute.*`, reading the charge in the event's mode to find its invoice, and `payment_intent.payment_failed`, for a bank debit on an invoice only. `_shared/stripeArCase.ts` (new, pure) builds the arguments and words Stripe's reason codes. It is registered as a staff-only sender on What customers see.
- **The notice** (`_shared/bankReturnedDeposits.ts`, `_shared/arReturnCaseNotify.ts`):
  - three situations: `stripe_dispute`, `stripe_dispute_lost`, `stripe_debit`
  - `arCaseNoticeDue` decides whether a notice is due, now or a second time when lost
  - `arCaseNoticeClaim` says where the once-only claim goes: `notified_at`, then `lost_notified_at`
  - the hourly `ar-returned-checks` uses both
- **The client**:
  - `arReturnCase.ts` gets `arStripeCaseView` and the next steps *answer_dispute*, *put_back*, *new_payment*. No replacement deposit is offered.
  - `ArReturnCasePane` adds the Stripe link, the put-back read-back and the footer words.
  - `BankPaymentsModal` closes a Stripe case through its own RPC, and runs the put-back then the Ready to Bill sync.
  - `useArReturnCases` reads no trail for a Stripe case. `dashboardNeedsYou` gets the titles.
