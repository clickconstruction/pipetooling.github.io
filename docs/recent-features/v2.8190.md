# v2.8190 — Sent copies: a sheet from the Document builder is under Contracts and estimates, with its job (2026-11-21)

Punch list #404. The review of 2026-11-20 listed it: *a sent sheet's copy carries no job, and Sent copies files it under Other.* Taunya asked for it on 2026-11-21. No migration.

## What was wrong

`document-builder-send` filed each copy with a kind, a title and who it went to, and nothing else. On Documents → Sent copies the copy sat under **Other**, and no job's own list of papers showed it.

## The fix

- **The group.** The kinds `insurance_sheet` (a printed sheet), `insurance_sheet_email` and `secrecy_builder_email` sort under *Contracts and estimates* (`sentKindGroup`, `src/lib/sent/sentCopies.ts`). A copy filed before this moves with them, since the group is read from the kind.
- **A sheet's job.** Sent copies knows a job as a row of `jobs_ledger`, and a sheet is about a GC project. A project that bills through a job (`gc_projects.billing_job_id`) now files its sheet's copy under that job. The function reads it in the read of the project it already made.
- **A builder's job and customer.** A Sub NDA sent to a GC files under the job it is about, when it is about one, and under the customer it went to.

`sentCopyJobIds` in `_shared/documentBuilderSend.ts` holds the rule.

## What it does not reach

- A GC project with no billing job files under no job. Its copy is found by its title, which names the project. Sent copies has no column for a project, and adding one is its own piece of work.
- The printed sheet's copy gets its job with the job list's own change, which is where the page first reads a project's billing job.
- Copies filed before this keep the job they had, which is none.

## Tests

- `sendSentCopy.test.ts`: both kinds are under contracts, the rule for a job, and the function's source names the job on each copy it files and the customer on a GC's.
- `sentCopies.test.ts` and `sheetScreen.test.ts`: the three kinds' group.

`document-builder-send` is deployed after the merge.
