# v2.4478 — Submittals: Choose what the GC sees knows what is already approved (2026-10-03)

Finding 3 of the owner's tab review ([`to-dos/submittals/tab-review-before-after.html`](../../to-dos/submittals/tab-review-before-after.html), https://claude.ai/artifact/SJ3skkPrsSDEugjmXe4h2k). Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

Seen on BP398, Rev 4 — the draft *Rev 4 from the 1 row sent back* built, so it holds Shower/tub combos alone while Kitchen sinks and Toilets stand approved on Rev 3 with orders on the log. *Choose what the GC sees…* opened with both lit **GC sees it** (the bid remembers them ticked, and `startingPick` knew only "on the draft" or "as remembered"), the bar read *2 rows go on Rev 4*, and **Update Rev 4** was live before anything was touched. One press put two approved fixtures back in front of the GC. Under the rows, *Left out · 7 from the takeoff · not submitted, not ordered* counted them too.

## What changed

- **The candidate** carries `standsOn: { rev, whole }` ([`takeoffCandidates.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/takeoffCandidates.ts)): set by the tab from the rows `rowsThatStand` already reads for the procurement log (`standing`), by count row. `whole` is false when only some of the row's parts were approved.
- **The kernel** ([`takeoffPicks.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/takeoffPicks.ts)): `standsApproved(c, picks)` — off the draft, standing, and not clicked. `planTakeoffPicks` skips such a fixture: no row, and no write to what the bid remembers. `pickCounts` gains `stands`. `standsWords`, `standsLine`, and `pickChangeWords` for a fixture asked again.
- **The window** ([`SubmittalTakeoffPicker`](../../src/components/bids/SubmittalTakeoffPicker.tsx)): where the three buttons were, *✓ Approved on Rev 3* (or *Part approved on Rev 3*) and **Ask again…**, with *It stays on Rev 3 and on the procurement log. It is not on Rev 4.* under the name. Ask again sets the fixture to *GC sees it* with the three buttons back and the line *Approved on Rev 3. It goes on Rev 4 and the GC is asked again.* **Keep the approval** undoes it. *GC sees all with a product* leaves a standing fixture alone. The group headings and the foot count *N approved earlier*.
- **The tab**: `takeoffLeftOut` leaves standing fixtures out; the line under the rows adds *2 approved on Rev 3*.

## Decided here

- **Asked again is a new row.** It comes on from the takeoff like any fixture; the newest revision then speaks for its tag (`rowsThatStand`), so the log reads it as waiting on the GC until they answer. That is what asking again means.
- **A part-approved row is shown as such**, not as approved: the estimator may want the rest answered.
