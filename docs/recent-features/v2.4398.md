# v2.4398 — Lien instruments on a phone: the paper gets the window (2026-10-02)

No migration, no edge function. **The ask (Robert, 2026-10-02)**, with a screenshot of the Lien instruments window on his iPhone: "the bottom section is very hard to scroll through and read, can you help me make the top part partially collapsable". Plan, then *is this the best we can do?*, then a mock-up, then the same question. Canvas: https://claude.ai/artifact/Hda7XFget9LBPaHsJjA4bJ (row 1 the window as it was, measured; row 2 the first pass with its faults; row 3 the proposal). Robert: "I like this, please help me build it".

## What was wrong

Measured at 390 × 844 on the dev server against production data, job 273. The window's card was 776 px tall. The title bar with the stacked steps took 521 px (the steps alone 426) and the five doors wrapped onto three lines, 136 px. On the § 53.056 notice tab the paper got **120 px** for 1,782 px of content; on Demand letter, whose foot is 194 px, the letter got **0 px**. The notice tab had no × and no Cancel: the only way out was a press outside the window.

The first pass folded the steps behind a *Show the timeline* line and put the doors on one scrolling row. Its faults: the fold hid the next step and the days left, which is what the window is for; it hid a closed window; opening it pushed the paper back to 120 px; five doors on one row ran off the edge with no cue; and folding by scroll position (the desk's notice pane does) makes the list jump under the finger.

## What shipped

- **The folded strip's words** ([`lienTimeline.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienTimeline.ts), `lienTimelineFoldSummary`, kernel-tested): the Next sentence whole (the days are in it), `waiting on us`, and `N windows closed`, where a folded node counts every month it holds and a month skipped on purpose is not counted. The aside and the unknown-kind warning ride along as notes; the warning's sentence is now one constant (`LIEN_KIND_UNKNOWN_WORDS`) the strip prints too.
- **[`LienWindowFoldedSteps`](../../src/components/jobs/LienWindowFoldedSteps.tsx)** (new): the strip is one button, at least 56 px tall. A press drops the steps (`LienTimelineStrip`, `layout="list"`, `withNext={false}`, so the sentence is not said twice) in a panel **over** the paper, ending inside the card (measured against `[data-lien-window-card]`). The strip, the grey behind the steps and Escape put them away. The grey sits inside the card, so a press on it closes the steps and never the window. It always opens folded: there is no state to remember.
- **[`LienInstrumentsModal`](../../src/components/jobs/LienInstrumentsModal.tsx)**, at 640 px and under (`useIsMobile`): the title, § The rules and a 44 px × share one line; the steps are the folded strip; the papers are one `tablist` bar that fills the width (a fourth paper, Release of record, makes it scroll, the cut edge faded until the end is in view); lientooling.com is the foot of the dropped steps. Above 640 px the window is as it was, plus a ×.
- Guide [`file-a-lien-and-never-miss-its-deadlines`](../../src/content/help/file-a-lien-and-never-miss-its-deadlines.md): a section *On a phone*.

One change from the mock-up: the days stay inside the Next sentence instead of a chip of their own, so the strip prints the kernel's sentence as it is and no second place has to agree with it.
