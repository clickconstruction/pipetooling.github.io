# v2.4068 — Fuel is its own line in the Job window (2026-09-28)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Punch list **#52** (`to-dos/fuel-is-a-job-cost.md`), PR 2.

## Why

The owner (2026-09-28): what matters most is how much a job cost and when fuel landed on it. Job Summary has drawn fuel as a grey line under Parts since v2.2725; the Job window folded it into Card charges and Parts.

## The change

- **`src/lib/jobs/jobCardCostLines.ts`** (new) — `clampCostLinesToCounted` (the clamp Job Summary did inline: each cost line takes what is left of the card charges that count, in manager order; `Jobs.tsx` now calls it), `jobCardCostLines` (one job's card lines → cost lines, with the same exclusions as the card total and the same classifier as Job Summary: the accounting label's tag, else the bank category's), `costLinesTotal`, `cardCostLinesSubtitle`.
- **`fetchJobMaterialsCostSnapshot`** — the card read now asks for each transaction's `mercury_category`; the snapshot reads the tags and the accounting labels of the job's transactions and carries `cardCostLines` and `cardTagByTxId`. Tags and labels are office-staff reads: any failure leaves the card charges as one line, as before.
- **The Job tab's Costs card** — ⛽ Fuel & gas as its own line after Parts; Parts shows the rest (its caption says so). The margin does not move.
- **Costs tab → Where the money went** — ⛽ Fuel & gas as its own row; the card row reads *Other card charges*; the direct total does not move. Both open the card charges list.
- **The card charges lists** (the Costs tab's and the read-only one) — each fuel charge carries a ⛽ marker, and the row's subtitle says *includes ⛽ Fuel & gas $x*.
- Any other tag the office marks *show as a cost line* is drawn the same way.

Left for a follow-up: Job Summary's print still folds fuel into parts (and does not take off invoice-linked card charges).
