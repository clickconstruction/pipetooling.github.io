# v2.5113 — GC projects: the database side of paying a certified bill by card, with a 3% card fee (2026-10-09)

Migration `20261010026000_gc_owner_card_bills` (doc: `docs/migrations/20261010026000_gc_owner_card_bills.md`); no edge function, no screen. GC mode's Owner Billing O8a, from the owner's answer to the Stripe Pay question: the customer turns a certified GC bill into a card payment from their portal, the turn adds a 3% credit card fee (counsel's okay, 2026-10-09), and staff never turn one. The plan is `to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/owner-billing-o8.md` on `spike/gc-mode`, whose SQL block this migration is byte for byte but for the version. O8b (the `gc-card-bill` function, the portal's **Pay by card**, the staff convert guard) and O8c (the office's side and the three emails) follow.

## What shipped

- **`gc_owner_card_bills`**: one row per bill turned to card, with the base, the rate (3) and the fee, `pending`, `on_card` or `undone`. The money team reads it; the three functions write it. The three fences close it.
- **`gc_card_bill_begin` / `gc_card_bill_finish`**, the service role only, for `gc-card-bill`'s portal door. Begin refuses an interest bill, a paid bill, a bill on Stripe, a bill with any payment, a bill taken back to a check bill and a second press, then writes the pending row at 3% of the bill, rounded to the cent. Finish writes the Stripe invoice onto the bill: the total, the Stripe columns, and the fee as a `fee_lines` rider, *Credit card fee (3%)*, naming its `card_bill`.
- **`gc_card_bill_undo`**: the money team takes a card bill back to a check bill while no payment is on it, refused in words for a training account, a digital twin and anyone else. As the service role it clears a pending row whose Stripe invoice was never made.
- **The fee is a rider, not GC revenue.** `job_rider_fees` (v2.5091) counts a `card_bill` entry. `gc_owner_billing_revenue` becomes the contract, the interest billed and the billing job's riders, so a card payment, which carries its fee, never marks the billing job paid while a bill is open. No GC figure reads it; O8c's mapper reads a card bill at its amount less its fee. It also counts a returned check fee on a GC bill now, which v2.5091 left to the GC crew.
- **Edit Job's twin**: `riderFeeLineCents` (`src/lib/jobs/arReturnCaseFee.ts`) sums the entries that name a case or a card bill, and `jobFormRiderFeesDollars` reads it, so a billing save keeps a card fee as it keeps a returned check fee. `returnedCheckFeeCents` still counts only the returned check fees.
- **Docs**: `BILLING_FLOWS.md` (the GC billing job's bills on card, its revenue with the riders, the rider sentence), `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` (the Owner Billing door's service-role functions and the card table).

## Tests

- **SQL bed** `supabase/tests/gc_owner_billing/90_card_bills.sql` (42 checks), run by `scripts/pgtest-gc-owner-billing.sh`, which also re-applies the migration. It covers every refusal, the fee to the cent with a half cent, the rider and the revenue, the controller's certificate keeping the riders, back to a check bill, and the early-paid case: every bill paid but one card fee leaves the job not paid with that fee owed. Five mutants of the migration were each caught locally.
- `arReturnCaseFee.test.ts` (`riderFeeLineCents`, +3), `jobFormMoneyTotals.test.ts` (the card fee rides, +1), `jobRiderFeesSql.test.ts` (job_rider_fees counts `card_bill`, the revenue adds the riders, finish writes the rider, +1).
