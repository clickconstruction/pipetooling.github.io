# v2.7983 — Sub papers: the Send press emails a sub their papers (2026-11-19)

Client only: no migration, and `send-sub-paper-email` is not changed or redeployed. Punch list #350, step 5 of the six under the card's *Where it stands*, its second half: the press. The function it calls merged in v2.7506 and has been live since 2026-11-17. Grace's go of 2026-11-19 is the go to land the press. It is not a go to send a paper to a real sub.

The press was first built on the branch `claude/350-send-press` (2026-11-16, never merged), on top of an older copy of steps 3 and 4. This change is cut from fresh `main`. It brings that branch's hunks over and adds what is listed under *Past the branch*.

## What the office sees

- **Send to <name>…** at the foot of the papers window, beside Print and Download the PDF, for a dev, a master technician, an assistant and a controller. An account in training gets no press, and neither does a superintendent.
- **The send step.** *To* is the sub's name and the email on their roster row. *They will get* is the message as the sub reads it, with a chip for each file. *When you press Send* lists the three things the press does. The press names its act with its count: *Send 3 papers by email*.
- **After Record payment** the window that follows reads *Send the payment record…*, and it opens the papers window on the send step with that sending's record.
- **Once it went** a green line says how many papers went and to which address, and the press turns into *Done*. *Back to the paper* brings Send back.

## How the send works

`SubPapersWindowConnected.tsx` hands the view `canSend`, `sendBy`, `emailDoor`, `startOnSend` and `onSend`.

1. **The address.** The window reads `people.email` for the person and shows it. It hands the function no address. The function reads the roster again under the caller's policies, so a paper about one sub cannot go to another address.
2. **The numbers.** Every paper is registered through `mint_sub_paper` before it is drawn. The page never gives out a number.
3. **The PDFs.** Each paper is drawn in the browser by `drawSubPaperPdf`, the kernel the preview reads, and posted as base64.
4. **The email and the copies.** `send-sub-paper-email` sends one message as the company and files each paper as a sent copy of its own (`sub_statement_of_account`, `sub_work_statement`, `sub_payment_record`) on the person and its jobs. The window files nothing itself on a send.

## Past the branch

- **One set, one set of numbers** (`registerSubPaperSet` in `subPaperRegister.ts`). The branch registered the statement of account first, so its rows named a sheet *Sheet of Nov 3, 2026* while the file beside it was `WS-2057-1.pdf`. Now every work statement is registered first. The payment records and the statement of account are built again over the sheets as numbered (`numberedSheet`), and only then registered. The statement of account is last, so a refusal ahead of it spends no `SA-` number. Print and Download go through the same function with a set of one, and read as they did. The fingerprint leaves a sheet's name out, so no revision is made by this.
- **Twelve papers an email** (`SUB_PAPER_SEND_MAX`, `subPaperSendSet` in `subPaperSend.ts`). The function refuses more than 12. The window sends the paper it is on and the first 11 that ride along, oldest first, and a yellow line names the rest for the next email. The branch would have posted them all and shown the function's refusal.
- **Done, not a second Send.** `onSend` answers whether the papers went. The step then shows *Done* until the person goes back to the paper.
- **No answer is not a no** (`subPaperSendUnknownWords`). When the request leaves and no answer comes back, the function may have mailed the papers already. The window claims neither: *No answer came back, so it is not known whether the email went. Look under Documents → Sent copies before you send again.* A refusal the function answered still reads *Nothing was sent*.
- **The window stays while a press is under way.** Close and a click outside wait for the answer, so a result is never missed and a window opened again cannot send a second time.
- **A double click sends once.** The connected window holds a ref while a send is under way, since a second click can land before the dead press is drawn. One email goes, and one statement number is taken.
- **The period holds while the window is open.** The view keeps the last statement as it stood when the window opened. Before this, making a statement registered it, the register was read again, and *Since the last statement* moved to start the day after the paper just made: a second press drew a period from tomorrow to today. That was on `main` for Print and Download since v2.7552.
- **Who may send reads one predicate.** `isOfficeRole` (`doorRoles.ts`), the client's mirror of `is_office_staff()`, which the mint and the function hold too. The branch kept a set of its own.
- **The To line** names a phone only where a text is offered. With email alone it reads the name and the address.
- **The message reads straight.** A statement to date read *Here is your statement for To date, Nov 19, 2026.* It reads *Here is your statement to date, Nov 19, 2026.* now. The words were on `main`, and no email had gone with them.
- **A roster that could not be read is not a sub with no email** (`emailUnread`). When the window's own read of the address fails, the step reads *the address on their roster row*, the press stays live, and the function decides. The green line then names the address it went to.
- **A press that cannot go lists nothing it would do.** *When you press Send* is left out while the press is dead.
- **The files' names.** The chips show each file as it is named before its number, with a line under them: *A paper with no number yet takes one as it is made, and its file is named for it.*
- **A statement number that went nowhere is named** (`subPaperNotSentWords`). A statement of account takes a new number every time it is made, so when the email fails after the papers were registered the red line reads *SA-0007 took its number and did not go. The next send takes a new one.* A work statement keeps its number for its sheet and a payment record carries its sending's, so neither is spent.

## A sub with no email

The press is dead and reads *Cannot send yet*. A yellow line says *<name> has no email on the roster, so there is nowhere to send it.* and then *Print or download the paper for now. A dev can add an email to their roster row.* The paper is not held up: *Back to the paper* has Print and Download.

Read on prod on 2026-11-19, counts only: 24 active people of kind `sub`, 11 with an email on the roster row and 13 without. Two of the 13 have an address on the login tied to their row, and the function does not read it. Every one of the 24 is tied to a login row, so the office's own roster edit does not reach them (`office_update_non_login_people`), and the desk's email change does not copy onto an empty roster row (`change-user-email`). The view takes its door as a prop (`emailDoor`, words and an optional press) so a real door is one change away. Whether the function should fall back to the login's address, the rule `quickSendWorkOrder` already uses, is asked of Grace: it needs a redeploy.

## Found, not changed here

A superintendent reaches **Jobs → Subs → Pay** (the Pay view is theirs, with no payments list) and is shown *Statement of account…*, *Work statement…* and *Payment record…* there, as since v2.7453. The window opens for them on figures with no payments in them, since the payments table hides its rows from that role. The register refuses them, so no paper is made: *Only the office roles who see sub pay can make a sub paper.* The card's rule is that a superintendent gets no press, so those three presses want the same gate as Send. That is a change to the Pay view of its own and is left for one.

## Not built

- **A texted link.** The function refuses `by: 'text'`. The kernel keeps the words of the texted link and the copied link, and the view offers only the ways it is handed, which is email.
- **Confirm a work statement** stays behind `SUB_PAPER_CONFIRM_LIVE`, off.
- **The six sentences for counsel** stay drafts and print marked. `SUB_PAPER_COUNSEL_PENDING` is still true.
