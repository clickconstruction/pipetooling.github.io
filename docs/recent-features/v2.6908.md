# v2.6908 — Drop a supply house's quote on Takeoffs and it reads itself (2026-11-10)

## Decisions

- **A sheet read twice is not read twice.** The file's hash is kept. The same file opens the read that exists, where it was left.
- **The house is written once it is settled**, and it cannot change under a filed row.
- **A person's pick outlives the window.** The part she picks for a row is kept on the row.
- **The file itself is not kept**, only its rows, its page numbers and its hash. The row shows as printed, and its page is named.
- **Not in this PR**: one press for every ready row, a kit row teaching its number with no price, and taking a whole sheet back (PR 5); fixtures onto the takeoff (PR 6); a scan or a photo, which needs the reader that looks at the picture.

## Not proven on a real sheet

The reader was built from the estimator's screenshot and a made-up quote in the same layout. No real quote was read: a search of company mail for one was refused, rightly, without the owner's word. The first real sheet dropped is the measure, and nothing files without a press.
