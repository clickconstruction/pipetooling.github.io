# v2.8170 — Lien desk: a missed notice that can still go late no longer says the lien right is gone (2026-11-21)

The second of two older oddities the live walk of 2026-11-21 wrote on punch list #361's card (the ledger, v2.7989). It was read in the code and never seen live. Client only: no migration, no edge function. No rule about a lien right is new here: the rule is v2.4708's, and this makes the places that did not follow it agree with it.

## Confirmed, with the inputs

A sub job with one work month, May 2026, commercial. Its § 53.056 window closed Aug 15 with nothing sent and nothing noted. No notice item is on the job. The job's § 53.052 affidavit is not filed and its last day, Sep 15, is still ahead. Today is Sep 14. The desk puts the job in the Missed pile with no month due. On that one pane, before this change:

- the path read *Send the May notice late, then file the affidavit — tomorrow*, with the press *Send the late notice ›*
- the gates read *Ready to go out · All 4 clear*
- Months on this job read *a late notice can still go · the affidavit by Sep 15* and *May closed with nothing sent — claimed on this notice late; the affidavit can follow by Sep 15*, with May ticked late
- the footer's head read *Window closed · Nothing to sign*, and the footer *The window closed on May 2026 with no notice — the lien right on that work is gone*, with no send
- the May stop, pressed on the timeline, read *The window closed with nothing sent. The lien right on that work is gone; the money is still owed.*

## Which sentence is right

v2.4708's rule, the owner's call of 2026-10-06: a sub job whose every § 53.056 window closed with nothing sent is lien gone only once the affidavit's own window has closed too. While that window is open, a late notice can go out and the affidavit follow it. The timeline, the months card, the To do row (*Draft it late*) and the Dashboard's line (*send the late notice by Sep 15*, which sends the office to the Missed pile to draft it) all follow that rule. The footer and the stop note did not. They are older than the rule and v2.4708 did not reach them: the footer drew the draft's presses on a missed notice only while a month was still due.

## What changed

- [`lienPaneSections.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienPaneSections.ts): `lienMissedNoticeStillGoes({ dueMonths, lateUntil })` is the one test. A notice in the Missed pile is still a notice to send while a month is due, or while `lateUntil` is set. `lateUntil` is the desk's own late day since v2.4708: the affidavit's last day while every window closed unsent, nothing was noticed and the affidavit is not filed. `lienNoticeFootHead` takes `lateUntil` and reads *Sign and send · Not signed yet* there.
- [`LienDesk.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDesk.tsx): the missed notice's footer is `LienDeskDraftFooter` while the test holds. So the late notice the pane already drafts, with the closed months ticked late, has its send. Past the affidavit's day the footer is unchanged: the closed-window line, under *Window closed · Nothing to sign*.
- [`lienStopPaper.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienStopPaper.ts): `lienClosedNoticeStopNote(next)` words a closed notice stop in the three stop windows (the desk's notice pane, its affidavit pane, the job's Lien window). While the timeline's next step is `late_notice` it reads *The window closed with nothing sent. A late notice can still go, and the affidavit can follow it.* Otherwise it reads as before.

The send itself is not new, by the code: a touch on the same pane (a tick, the release, a wording edit) already saves a draft naming the closed months, and the row then moves to To draft with this same footer. The footer was the one place that hid it.

## Not changed

The months card, the timeline, the gates, the Dashboard and the Pipeline. *Note it as missed* is still on the closed month's row. A missed notice with nothing due and the affidavit's day passed, the only kind on the live desk on 2026-11-21, reads as it did.
