# v2.5988 — A sent estimate shows every option it offered (2026-10-27)

Punch list **#206** PR 2 (`to-dos/estimate-extend-pdf-options.md`), the office's third ask from the
City of Seguin case: *"When this estimate was sent it had 2 options but I'm unable to see the 2nd
option on this screen."* Client only; no migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

Before acceptance `line_items_snapshot` holds the recommended option's lines (the owner's decision 3
of v2.2457) and the sent view's *Line items* table drew only those. `options_snapshot` was on the
row all along — `send-estimate-to-customer` never writes it and nothing clears it on expiry — but on
a sent row only two places read it: the accepted record's names of what they passed on, and the
folded **Customer experience → Page** rehearsal at the bottom of the page. So an estimate sent with
two options read as one, and once it expired the office remade it from its notes.

## What shipped

- **`src/lib/estimates/estimateOfferedOptionsView.ts`** — `estimateOfferedOptionsView(options,
  formatMoney, { accepted })`: one block per option in offered order (`Option 2 · HDPE water line`,
  the money with a `+` on an add-on, the chips *★ Recommended* / *Add-on* / *Starts ticked*, the
  trimmed pitch, the lines, and *The Pipeline's number until they decide.* under the recommended one
  while the customer has not decided), `offeredOptionsCountWords` (*2 options · choose one* · *4
  options · 2 add-ons* · *3 options · tick any*), the words in `OFFERED_OPTIONS_WORDS`. A
  single-option estimate is `active: false`. 8 tests.
- **`src/components/estimates/EstimateOfferedOptions.tsx`** — the blocks, each over the page's own
  `EstimateLineItemsTable`; on an accepted row a `<details>` fold **Every option as sent** with the
  count. Render smoke: the blocks, the star and note, the empty single-option case, the fold.
- **`Estimates.tsx`** — the `rowOfferedOptions` memo (a CO never has options); the read-only header
  draws the component in place of the *Line items* section when the row offered 2+ options; the
  accepted branch adds the fold under the total line.
- **Docs**: the guide *offer options on an estimate* gains *What the office sees after the send*;
  the Glossary's estimate-options entry; the Estimates map's sent-view dossier.
