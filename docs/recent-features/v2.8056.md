# v2.8056 — Share stage dates with a GC is back under the GC picker on a saved job (2026-11-20)

A fix found on the walk of punch list #382's GC page (PR 9, v2.8018). The job's **Share stage dates with this GC** switch (`JobGcStageSharingSwitches`, v2.2933) is mounted under the GC picker in the Edit tab's **GC/Builder** row, `JobFormEditFactRows`.

## What was wrong

Since v2.2933 the switch sat only in `JobFormCustomerSection`, which the job form mounts for a new job alone. The switch needs the job's id, and a new job has none, so it showed nowhere. The help guide told people to tick it under the GC picker, and no saved job could. On 2026-11-20 no job on prod had `gc_shares_stage_dates` on.

Two GC-facing features wait on that switch: the stages a GC is offered (the Stage Plan), and what we need before each stage (#382 PR 9).

## What changed

- The existing component is mounted as it is. It reads and saves `gc_shares_stage_dates` and `gc_auto_offer_next` on the spot, with its own words and defaults. It is off on every job until a person turns it on.
- It shows on a saved job with a GC, to the roles that manage the GC's portal. That is the gate of the portal's globe in the same form: dev, master technician, assistant and controller.
- The guide *show a GC the stages you plan* says where it is: the job's Edit tab, the GC/Builder row.

No migration and no edge function.

## Tests

`JobFormEditFactRows.render.test.tsx`: the switch under the picker for an assistant on a saved job with a GC, off. None for a primary, none with no GC, none on an unsaved job.
