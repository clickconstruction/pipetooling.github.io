# v2.7177 — Materials on site: the boxes read again by themselves (2026-11-13)

Punch list #344, PR 4. From the review of Materials → On site: *the store never reads again*.

## What was wrong

`loadJob` returned from the cache once a job had been read, and `loadDesk` once the desk had, for as long as the page stayed up. The only reads after the first were a refused write and the error panel's Retry. So the office's desk left open showed the morning's numbers all day, and a second phone on a box capped **Used** at, and offered *40 is right* from, a number the first phone had already changed.

## The change

- `src/lib/materials/onSiteFresh.ts`: the ages (60 seconds on coming back, 20 on opening a box, five minutes on the timer) and `readIsStale`.
- **The store**: `refreshJob` and `refreshDesk` read again when the last read is older than the age asked. `loadJob` and `loadDesk` take `quiet`: nothing reads as loading, and a read that fails leaves the last good read on the screen with no error, since that read is still true as of its time and the next refresh tries again. A job never read, one being read, and one whose last read failed are left to their own screens. `jobLoadedAt` says when a job was last read.
- **The hooks**: `useOnSiteJob` and `useOnSiteDesk` ask when the page comes back to the front, the window takes the focus, the signal returns, and every five minutes while the page stays in front. Every on-site screen reads through one of the two.
- **The phone**: opening a box asks with the shorter age.
- The presses still on their way are drawn over the new read, as over any (`withPending`, v2.7173).

Client only.

## Tests

`onSiteFresh.test.ts` (3). Four more cases in `onSiteStore.write.test.ts`: a read old enough brings what another phone did and never reads as loading, one too new is not made; a press that still waits stays through the read; a quiet read that fails keeps the last good read with no error, while a person's own Retry says the failure; a job never read is left alone.

## Not changed

Two phones can still press against one box inside the same few seconds. The second press lands as its own row and the next count settles the line; a lock across phones is on #344's list of what not to build.
