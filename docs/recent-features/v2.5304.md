# v2.5304 — A trade's portal says when its next pay application is due (2026-10-12)

This is wave 2 of the Messages build, cluster 7, PR 5 of 5 (GC Reviews helper 7, directed by the lead session GC Review). It adds the access point "the trade's portal naming the period" from cut 9 of `to-dos/gc-mode/messages/BUILD_PLAN.md` on `spike/gc-mode`.

- **`tradeBillDay(today, cutoffDays)`** in `src/lib/gc/billDay/trades.ts`: the bill day a pay application sent today goes on, and its cut-off. It is the next 25th whose cut-off is today or later, so a pay application after the cut-off goes on next month's bill, as the period rule (`periodFrom`) reads it.
- **The pay application's door** (`GcTradePortalPayApp.tsx`) on a job being built gains a line: *Send pay application 3 by Mon Oct 19. It goes on our bill of Oct 25.* The words are the new `dueBy` key in `buildingWords.ts`, English and Spanish, with Spanish held with the portal's. A new pay application opens with **Period ends** at that bill day. The trade can change it, and a resend keeps what they typed.
- **The cut-off reaches the portal**: `gc-trade-portal` reads `app_settings.gc_bill_day_cutoff_days` with the service role. The slice carries it as `billDayCutoffDays`, a day count, never money. `tradePortalState` hands it to the page, which provides it through `PortalBillDayContext` (`gcTradePortalBillDay.ts`). A slice from an older function, or the office's preview, reads the default, 6. `tradePortalSample.test.ts`'s reviewed list of slice keys gains it.
- **Deploy**: `gc-trade-portal` is redeployed after this merges (`docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`); the page ships first and reads the default until then. `deno check` gives the same 504 errors on the function as on `main`'s, none new.
- **Docs**: the office guide says the portal names the day. APP_DIRECTORY's `/t/:token` line gains a sentence.
