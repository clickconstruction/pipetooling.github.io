# v2.4425 — Pricing: "Bids like this" waits until the bid has a price (2026-10-02)

Found by the after-deploy walk of v2.4418 on six real bids. B82 (City of Seguin, no price on the Workbench yet) drew the block with one chip, *City of Seguin · no decided bids yet*, and a *Compare* button that opened the margin scale with no pointer on it.

## What changed
- [`bidsLikeThisView`](../../src/lib/bids/bidsLikeThis.ts) returns null when it has no chip to show: no bid this size, no record for the GC, and no margin to place. The margin scale alone no longer brings the block up.
- The *no decided bids yet* chip needs a size chip or a margin chip beside it. A GC with decided bids still shows its record on an unpriced bid.
- Tests: two kernel cases and one render case (B82's shape).

The other five bids of the walk read as designed: B159, B375, B398, B403, B490.

No migration, no edge function.
