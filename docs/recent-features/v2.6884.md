# v2.6884 — Lien affidavit: a homestead paper opens with the § 53.254(f) notice (2026-11-10)

The second PR of the short-form train (punch list #305). The owner, 2026-11-10, once the short form and its one-press redraw were live: build the homestead notice. The reading of the statute is the app's, not counsel's.

## What changed

- **`HOMESTEAD_AFFIDAVIT_NOTICE`** in `src/lib/jobsDocuments/lienFilingDocuments.ts`: the statute's own words, *NOTICE: THIS IS NOT A LIEN. THIS IS ONLY AN AFFIDAVIT CLAIMING A LIEN.*
- **A `topNotice` block** on `FilingDocBlock`: centred, bold, **12 point** stated outright in the HTML (`font-size:12pt`, `data-top-notice`) and in the PDF, so it clears the statute's 10-point boldface whatever size the paper around it is drawn at. The text twin prints the line as it is.
- **`LienAffidavitFields.homestead`**: when set, `buildShortLienAffidavitBlocks` puts the notice first, above the title. Every other block is the same paper. The earlier ten-paragraph form is not touched: a paper keeps the form it was drafted on.
- **`buildLienAffidavitFieldsForJob`** takes `homestead` and sets the field on the short form only when it is true, so a stored copy of a paper that is not a homestead reads as before. The four draws hand it the property record's flag (`customer_addresses.homestead`): the Affidavits pane, the paper preview behind a Do now row, a timeline stop's window (`buildLienDeskAffidavitFields`) and the Lien window's Mechanic's lien tab (`buildLienWindowAffidavitFields`).
- The flag alone decides it, never the property kind. The § 53.056 notice prints its § 53.254(g) statement on every residence because a stale flag there costs nothing; an affidavit that says it is not a lien on a house that is not a homestead would be wrong.

## Not changed

- The desk still holds every homestead (`affidavitGates`), and the Lien window draws no affidavit while a gate is closed. So today the notice is seen on the Lien desk's own paper, on a property someone marked homestead. No property on the desk was marked on 2026-11-10.
- `homestead` is `boolean NOT NULL DEFAULT false`: a property nobody asked about reads as not a homestead and prints no notice. The hold that asks the question is the next PR, and it needs a column.
