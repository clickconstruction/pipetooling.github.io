# v2.5089 — Overhead: the hints say recorded time and send wages to the Pay lens (2026-10-09)

Client words and one link. No migration, no edge function, no computation changes.

## What was stale

Four texts on People → Overhead had not caught up with two earlier changes.

- **Recorded time (v2.3261).** The pool and the Method A/C denominators count every *recorded* clock session: clocked out, not rejected, not revoked, approved or still pending. `isRecordedClockSession` in [`overheadDailyLabor.ts`](../../src/lib/overheadDailyLabor.ts) is the filter, called by `buildOverheadDailyLabor` (the pool) and `buildOtherJobsLaborByDay` (the A/C denominators) from `loadOverheadPoolSnapshot`. The strip's Pending approvals hint and the lens modal's what-if line already said so. The lens cards' hover rule, Method A's tooltip and the modal's Watch-outs still said approved-only.
- **The Pay lens (v2.3702).** Wages are set on People → Users → Pay (`USERS_TAB_PAY_LENS_PATH` in [`usersTabLens.ts`](../../src/lib/people/usersTabLens.ts)). The People pay config modal is gone. The strip's Unpriced hours hint still said *People → Pay config*, and the cell modal's no-wage chip said *set in Pay config* while linking to Payroll (`/people?tab=pay_stubs`).

## The change

- **[`PeopleOverheadTab.tsx`](../../src/components/people/PeopleOverheadTab.tsx)**
  - `lensInclusionRule`, appended to all three lens cards' `title`, now reads *Sessions counted: recorded — clocked out, not rejected, not revoked, whether approved or still awaiting approval.*
  - Method A's title says *recorded clock hours*, not *approved*.
  - The Unpriced hours hint reads *Set wages in People → Users → Pay.*, and its `title` names People → Users → Pay instead of pay config.
- **[`OverheadLensModal.tsx`](../../src/components/people/OverheadLensModal.tsx)** — the Watch-outs pending line keeps its two shapes. On A and C: *it already counts in this denominator as recorded time, and a rejection takes it out*. On B: *it doesn't touch this lens, but A and C already count it as recorded time*. It used to say the time was missing until someone worked the queue.
- **[`OverheadPeopleCellModal.tsx`](../../src/components/people/OverheadPeopleCellModal.tsx)** — the no-wage chip links to `USERS_TAB_PAY_LENS_PATH` and reads *set on the Pay lens*. Every Overhead viewer has pay access (dev, or a pay-approved master), so the lens opens for them.
- **Guides:** *keep the overhead numbers accurate*: the example strip quotes the new hint. *read the Overhead tab*: *Behind any cell* names People → Users → Pay among the flags' destinations.
- **Docs:** [`PEOPLE_CONTRACTS_OVERHEAD_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md`](../PEOPLE_CONTRACTS_OVERHEAD_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md) gotcha 17 loses its copy-drift note.
