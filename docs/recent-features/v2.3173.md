# v2.3173 — Trades served: a supply house says which trades it serves, and restricted estimators see only theirs (2026-09-08)

PR 6 of six from [`to-dos/supply-house-directory/`](../../to-dos/supply-house-directory/README.md) — the last one; the to-do is complete. Migration `20260908191317_supply_house_service_types.sql` — **push after the client deploys** (the client treats the absent table as "no tags").

## Why

One of the four estimators carries a trade restriction (`users.estimator_service_type_ids`), and the company quotes plumbing, electrical and HVAC from different counters. Without a trade on the house, a plumbing-only estimator's new directory listed Elliott Electric beside Ferguson, and the RFQ picker offered every house to every bid. The to-do's rule, decided by the owner: **an untagged house is visible to everyone** — tagging narrows, it never hides by default.

## What shipped

- **`supply_house_service_types`** (new link table: `supply_house_id`, `service_type_id`, PK on both, cascade both ways). Reads for the six Materials roles; writes behind `can_manage_supply_house_directory()`; both training-mode read-only blocks and the twin fence attached. **Seeded minimally**: CED and Elliott Electric Supply → Electrical, Garner Heating & Air Conditioning → HVAC; every other house stays untagged.
- **[`supplyHouseTrades.ts`](../../src/lib/materials/supplyHouseTrades.ts)** (new kernel, 6 test groups): `tradesByHouse`, `houseServesAnyTrade` (untagged → true), `defaultTradeSelection` (a restricted viewer's own trades, else all; never empty), `filterHousesByTrades` (no selection or every trade = no filter; a subset keeps untagged + matching), `tradeNamesFor`, `housesForBidTrade` (the picker's default + how many it hid).
- **Vendor form → Trades served** chips (Plumbing / Electrical / HVAC from `service_types`), with the hint *Estimators restricted to a trade only see houses that serve it. Leave all off to show it to everyone.* `useSupplyHouseEditor` syncs the link rows after every save (diff-based: delete what was unticked, insert what was ticked); before the push the reads and writes 404 and are treated as nothing to sync.
- **Directory → trade chips** beside the search, for both audiences. They start on the estimator's restricted trades (`defaultTradeIds` from `estimator_service_type_ids`, passed by `Materials.tsx`) or on all three; clicks win after that. Tagged houses show their trades under the name (desktop) or in the card subtitle (phone).
- **Send price requests** (`RfqComposeModal`) reads the bid's `service_type_id` and defaults to houses that serve it or are untagged: *Plumbing supply houses · 3 vendors hidden · **show all***; the link flips to *only Plumbing*. Nothing is hidden when the bid has no trade.
