# v2.6706 — Job Mode names every job on a superintendent's day (2026-11-08)

A fix. Job Mode (`DashboardJobModeCard`) reads today's visits from `job_schedule_blocks` with the job embedded (`jobs_ledger(hcp_number, click_number, job_name, job_address, service_type_id)`). The embed runs under the reader's own policies on `jobs_ledger`, and a superintendent has no read policy of their own there: a job opens to them only through *Users can read jobs ledger linked from own clock sessions*, once they clock onto it (the same trap `20260902165836_superintendent_thread_note_access.sql` worked around for thread notes). So the first visit on a new job came back with its embed empty, and the header, the day rail and the On my way sheet drew `J— · —` with no address. Found on the live walk of punch list #234 (2026-11-08) as the Superintendent sample; on prod, a superintendent's October schedule held three visits on team jobs they never clocked onto, each one empty to their own read.

## What changed

- `src/lib/jobMode/scheduledJobLabels.ts` (new kernel): `fillScheduledJobLabels(blocks, known)` gives a visit whose job read came back empty (no number, no name, no address) the label fields of the same job from a list the caller already holds, never replacing a field the visit's own read filled, and hands back the same array when nothing changed so the memos downstream hold.
- My Schedule's labels and job meta (`useDashboardSubSchedule`) fall back to the same rows: the label map takes them after the team and ready-to-bill lists, and `withKnownScheduledJobMeta` (`src/lib/dashboardSubSchedule.ts`) fills the address, number and pictures link of a scheduled job its own read gave no row for. My day (`DashboardMyDayEditorModal`) reads the same labels, so its visits stop saying only *Job*.
- Job Mode's Customer line (`JobModeDetailsSection`) settled on *Loading…* for good when the job read gave no row or failed. It now says *Shows once you clock in here.* for no row and the failure's reason (`formatErrorMessage`) for a failed read.
- The Dashboard hands Job Mode `knownJobs`: every row its three definer reads already return, the team's jobs (`list_assigned_jobs_for_dashboard`), the ready-to-bill ones and a superintendent's project jobs (`list_superintendent_jobs_for_dashboard`), through `useDashboardAssignedJobs`' new `jobsForVisitLabels`. ZZ test jobs stay in that list, since Job Mode draws ZZ visits too and the hiding rule is for the Dashboard's lists, not a visit's label. Job Mode fills both the live day and the day kept on the device (#183 PR 3) before the rail and the header read them, and keeps the filled day.

## Read closer

- No policy changes: opening `jobs_ledger` rows to superintendents would also change every policy that checks `EXISTS (SELECT … FROM jobs_ledger …)` under the caller, so the fix stays on the client with reads the person already has.
- Left: a visit dispatch sets on a job the superintendent is neither on the team of nor runs the project for (dispatch adds no team row) still reads empty until they clock on. A definer read of the label fields keyed on the visit's assignee would close it, with a migration.
- My Schedule already took its labels from the team's jobs first, so before this it was blank only for ZZ test jobs (hidden from those lists for every role but a dev), a superintendent's project jobs and the moment between a self-scheduled add and the next read.
