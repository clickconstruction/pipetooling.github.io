# v2.6482 — A thread's messages keep their room on every screen (2026-11-03)

The live pass on every viewport after the short-window train (v2.6474, v2.6476, v2.6477, v2.6478) reached clicktooling.com. Laptops were right; three screens still squeezed the person's lines to their 112 px floor under an open Bid it? card: 1440×900 and 1920×1080 (tall, so nothing folded), a 768×1024 tablet, and a 375×812 phone (left out of the fold on purpose). On the phone a long sender line also pushed the email 494 px wide on a 375 px screen.

## What changed

- `isThreadBeside` (`src/lib/messages/threadFold.ts`) no longer needs a short window: a thread column of 900 px or wider, at a desk, puts the sort card beside the lines at any height.
- `threadFolds`: the sort card folds to one row wherever it sits over the lines (every screen that is not beside); the reply box folds on a short window or a phone. `ThreadWindow` hands both to the slots as `OpenThread.foldCard` and `OpenThread.foldBox`; `NewLaneSlot` and `ReplyBox` read them in place of `short` (`replyBoxFolded` takes `foldBox`).
- The lines keep `max(7rem, 33%)` on every window, not only a short one.
- The lines' grid has one `minmax(0, 1fr)` column, so an unbreakable address or link wraps inside the line instead of widening the column past the screen.
- On a phone the folded card's head takes its own line over its presses, and the folded box's one-line field its own line under the kinds (`flex-wrap`, a 14rem basis).
- Guides: the card's two places move into *Read a thread*; the phone section gains the folded box; *Sort one* and *Open the reply box* name the phone.

## Decisions

- **The width decides where the card sits; the height only decides the header and the box.** A tall desk has room for the whole box and the five-row header, but never for a 400 px card over the lines.
