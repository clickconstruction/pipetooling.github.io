# v2.4827 — GC mode: the tables for the job’s records while we build (2026-10-07)

U1 of the Building lane's real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/BUILDING_REAL_BUILD.md`, on branch `spike/gc-mode`): the job's records while we build, as tables, in `supabase/migrations/20261008030000_gc_building_records.sql` (`docs/migrations/20261008030000_gc_building_records.md`).

- Ten tables:
  - `gc_daily_logs`, one a day per project, with `gc_daily_log_crews` (each trade on site and how many) and `gc_daily_log_delays` (what held work up, in the look-ahead's five reasons);
  - `gc_punch_items`, with the trade's word it is fixed, our check and the times it was sent back;
  - `gc_submittals`, with `gc_submittal_holds` (the scope lines each holds) and `gc_submittal_rounds` (each time it was sent, its Drive link, and the architect's answer);
  - `gc_rfis`, with `gc_rfi_holds`, the company that asked (`gc_companies`) and the answer with what it changes;
  - `gc_weekly_reports`, every send kept as it went, append only.
- Files stay in Google Drive: a log, a punch item and a submittal round keep a link, never a copy.
- Inspections are not here. They are the schedule's bars and failures, which Building reads.
- Dev only while it is built: one policy a table for `(SELECT public.is_dev())`, `anon` revoked, and the three house sweeps (read-only training mode and the twin fence).
- Pushed after the Board's B1 (`20261008020000_gc_company_record`), which makes `gc_companies`. Nothing reads or writes these yet: the writes and the first windows are U3 to U5. The types come in the PR after the push.
