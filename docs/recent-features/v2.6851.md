# v2.6851 — Materials: the tabs sit in three named groups, with On site, Print labels and Scan a label (punch list #304) (2026-11-10)

The owner, 2026-11-10, with the Parts Book on screen and the empty right end of its strip circled: should the Materials page carry doors to the on-site tools and to label printing. He could not remember where label printing lives. No press on the page led there; the shortest route was five presses through Jobs. After the mock-up he named the groups himself (*Purchase, Plan, Sort*) and said to build it. Built as the card's direction D on its picks: Plan first, and Track as the third word, which is one constant.

## What changed

- **`lib/materials/materialsGroups.ts`** (new): the strip's three groups and their order (Plan: Parts Book, Assembly Book, PO Builder · Purchase: Purchase Orders, PO Generator, Supply houses, Held for suppliers · Track: On site), the groups a role sees, the two presses, the third group's badges, and where a job's row leads a role. `MATERIALS_GROUP_WORDS.track` is the third word.
- **`lib/materials/materialsTabs.ts`**: `on-site` joins `MATERIALS_TABS` for every role that opens the page, and the list now runs in the strip's order. `isMaterialsOfficeRole` replaces three copies of the same comparison.
- **`MaterialsGroupStrip`** (new): a caption over each group, a hairline between groups, a badge on a caption only when its group needs a hand. Print labels and Scan a label are presses beside On site, never tabs. Under 640 px the three words are chips and the open group's tabs sit under them.
- **`MaterialsOnSiteTab`** (new): one search across every job (`lib/materials/onSiteFind.ts` over the on-site store's desk load, no new read on the server). Under it the office gets `JobsMaterialsTab`, the desk Jobs → Materials mounts, with a row's **Labels** and **Open on the phone screen**. An estimator, a primary and a superintendent get their jobs with boxes as doors to the Tally's On site tab, with no money drawn.
- **`PrintLabelsWindow`** (new): asks *Which job?* with the jobs that hold a box, then opens `LabelsPrintModal` on the store's rows. One job skips the question. Open to every role that opens On site.
- **`ScanLabelWindow`** (new): the words, and the typed code, which opens `/b/<code>`.
- **`JobsMaterialsTab`**: `driveBackfill` (off on the Materials page, whose job list is every job) and `rowDoors`; `?label=all` opens every box's label; the desk's empty line names its next press. Its words left from the spike are gone (*Spike: rows live on this device only*, *fill it with demo data*).
- **Search**: a door *Find a part on any job* to `/materials?tab=on-site`.
- The badge reads `useMaterialsOnSiteNeeds`, the Needs you card's own figures. Plan and Purchase wear no badge yet.
