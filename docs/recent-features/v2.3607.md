# v2.3607 — People → Who's where: the day as floating heads on job islands, with a time-of-day scrubber (2026-09-18)

PR 1 of [`to-dos/whos-where/README.md`](../../to-dos/whos-where/README.md) (Todd, 2026-09-18: *"a timeline of who is on what job in the form of floating heads … flip through the time both of day and of date"*). Client only; no migration; nothing written.

## The change

- **Kernel** [`src/lib/people/whosWhere.ts`](../../src/lib/people/whosWhere.ts) (pure, 28 tests): sessions and blocks normalised to minutes of the company wall clock (`sessionMinutes` keeps an open session open and clamps cross-midnight work to 1440 on its clock-in date, as `teamBoard.ts` does; `pgTimeMinutes` reads `time_start` loosely); `islandsAt(data, day, minute)` → one island per job with a head per person — **solid** = a session spanning the minute, **hollow** = a block spanning the minute with no session anywhere for that person (a person clocked in elsewhere is never hollow; their solid head carries `listedAt`); a no-job rail for sessions with no target; `notIn` for roster people with nothing that day; `dayLanes` → one row per job with in-bars and hollow listed-bars, each disagreement named (*never clocked* · *clocked at …* · *listed at …*); `dayHeadCounts` for the week strip; `scrubberDomain` (5 am–7 pm, widened to whole hours around the day, an open session to midnight); `roleRing` (master · sub · helper · office).
- **Loader** [`fetchWhosWhereWeek.ts`](../../src/lib/people/fetchWhosWhereWeek.ts): one company week of `clock_sessions` (rejected and revoked out; **pending kept**, as the crew deck decided in v2.2827; **open kept**, as the Dashboard clock strip reads them) and `job_schedule_blocks` (with `shared_block_group_id`, for PR 2), both paged with `fetchAllRows` past PostgREST's silent 1,000-row cap; the active roster with devs; the office job id from `app_settings`. Labels through the ledger prefix map (`formatJobLedgerShortLine`); the office job reads *Office*.
- **The tab** [`PeopleWhosWhereTab.tsx`](../../src/components/people/PeopleWhosWhereTab.tsx), `?tab=whos_where`, in the People group after Day book, gated like Day book (`canSeeDayBook`: dev, master, assistant, controller). ◀ ▶ and a Sunday–Saturday strip with head counts; a range slider over the day's domain with two-hour ticks and ▶ Play (five minutes a step); islands in an auto-fill grid; the rail (*Clocked in, no job*; *Not in that day · N*, expandable); the legend; the lanes with a playhead and a clickable time axis. The URL carries `ww_day`. Assistants are floored by `assistant_hours_window_weeks_v1` like the Hours tab.
- Guide [`see-who-was-on-which-job.md`](../../src/content/help/see-who-was-on-which-job.md); `PEOPLE_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`, `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`, `GLOSSARY.md` updated.
