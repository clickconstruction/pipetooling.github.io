# v2.7061 — Tasks: see where a task is, and get there (2026-11-12)

Punch list #328, PR 3 of 8. v2.7059 let the giver set a place. This is the doer's side.

## What changed

- **The place on the task.** On the Today tab's cards and on the Outstanding rows, a task with a place wears the place's name under it (`TaskPlaceLine`). A task with no place draws nothing new: no empty line and no nudge.
- **Opened** (`TaskPlaceBlock` in `src/components/checklist/TaskPlaceBlock.tsx`):
  - **A picture of the spot from above**, 150 tall. It is still, so a finger on it scrolls the page and never the map (`OnSiteMap` gains `still`). A press opens the same map full size, where it drags, zooms and switches to streets.
  - **Directions** opens the phone's maps app at the point, through `openInExternalBrowser`.
  - **How far am I?** is the one press that asks the phone where it is (`requestMyFix`, a bare reading with no words of its own). It answers *250 ft north-east of you. Your phone is sure within 30 ft.* and puts a dot for the person on the picture. The reading lives in the open card and nowhere else.
  - **Who placed it and when**, with *Your location stays on this phone.*
- **The three honest cases**, each with its own words:
  - Refused: *Location is off for this app. The map and Directions still work.* One answer is lost, the distance.
  - No signal: the tiles do not come (`onTilesFailed`), and the block says *The map did not load. There is no signal here.* How far am I? still answers.
  - No reading in time: *Could not find you. Try again in the open, or use Directions.*
- **A read of its own.** `useTaskPlaces` reads the places of the tasks on the tab beside the day's read and never inside it (`loadTaskPlacesForItems`). A place that cannot be read costs the place line and never the list.

The giver's row now says what is true since this PR: *The person sees this place on the task, with directions.*
