# v2.3578 — Pay sends carry their source; five functions are the one door (2026-09-17)

PR 1 of the Cash App backfill train. The owner, 2026-09-17, after a by-hand reconciliation of four people's Cash App and Mercury sends against the app's pay records: *"Rather than reconcile, now that we have fields for the Cash App transaction, perhaps we should use this ledger to backfill the transactions in the app with the correct information. I also do not mind adding these functions."* Decisions the same day: auto-correct a recorded amount to the send when within **$5**; a Mercury send is identified by its **payroll flag**, no Mercury alias table.

## What the by-hand pass taught

- The app's write contract for a Cash App payment lived in component code: the memo format (`Cash App #D-… "note"`), noon paid_at, the queue lane update, the payment link. An agent had to copy each by hand.
- "Where someone stands" is **net** (gross − deductions + additional lines), the formula the database already enforces on payments. Recomputing it from gross overstated one person's balance by a thousand dollars.
- One send can pay three weeks (a `1200 sent` memo on five rows) and one recorded payment can merge two sends. The old link — the Cash App row pointing at one payment — could not say either.
- Tristen is paid through Taunya's Cash App; a `"Week − 500"` note means an advance was netted; client-direct rows are in no bank feed.

## Migration [`20260917200000_pay_sources.sql`](../migrations/20260917200000_pay_sources.md)

- `pay_stub_payments.source_kind` / `source_id`, one row per send per report (partial unique index). The link now runs payment → send.
- `pay_position(person)` — the one read: net · paid · remaining per report, pending offsets, the Cash App queue.
- `record_pay_send(kind, id, person, amount, paid_on, note, dry_run)` — oldest-open-first, split at week edges, leftover to an advance offset, queue lane set, idempotent on the source.
- `link_pay_send`, `split_pay_payment`, `set_cashapp_lane` — the backfill primitives.
- Every function: payroll access or the service role; SECURITY INVOKER.

## Client — [`paySources.ts`](../../src/lib/people/paySources.ts)

`PAY_SOURCE_KINDS`, `paySendMemo()` (byte-equal to the SQL `pay_send_memo` and to the reconcile modal's `cashAppPaymentMemo` for Cash App), `paySourceLabel()`, `PAY_SEND_AUTOCORRECT_USD = 5` with `withinPaySendAutocorrect()`. Nothing user-facing changes in this PR; the Payments view still derives its chip from the memo until PR 3.
