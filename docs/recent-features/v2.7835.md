# v2.7835 — Subs: a sheet waiting on the customer is tied to one bill (2026-11-18)

Punch list #388, step 1 of 3 (`to-dos/sub-page-customer-paid/README.md`). One migration, `20261118180000_sub_sheet_draw_invoice.sql`, and the office's screens. The sub's page and the `sub-portal` function do not change.

## Why

A sub's sheet waits at **Waiting on customer** until the customer pays. The card's step 2 lets the sub's page say the customer paid. The owner's call 2b (2026-11-17) is that the page speaks for one bill only, the one the sheet is tied to, so it never says paid on a guess. A sheet was tied to a job and to no bill. This PR makes the tie.

## What landed

- **The tie.** `people_labor_jobs.draw_invoice_id` points at one of the job's bills. Deleting the bill clears it.
- **Made by itself.** A sheet that arrives at Waiting on customer takes the job's one open bill, when the job has exactly one. A bill is open until it is paid, so a draft counts. The database does this in a trigger on the sheet, so every writer of the stage gets it and `set_sub_sheet_stage` is unchanged.
- **The pick.** With several open bills the office is asked **Which bill does this sheet ride on?** before the move. The window lists the job's bills, open ones first, and **No bill**. It leans to the one sent bill when the others are drafts. **Cancel** stops the move. **Request the draw** moves the sheet and saves the pick through `set_sub_sheet_draw_invoice`.
- **Four doors, one window.** The rail on Jobs → Subs → Pay, the sheet's step on Subs → Work, the sheet story, and the *Shown on the sub's portal* box all ask through `useDrawBillPick`.
- **The row.** A waiting sheet's row on Jobs → Subs → Pay says *Rides on bill 2 of 3* or *No bill tied* under its rail. The line opens the same window to pick, change or clear the tie.
- **Off the stage, no tie.** A move back clears it. Moving the sheet to another job drops it.

## What it does not do

- **No backfill.** Sheets already waiting on the customer keep no tie until the office picks one from the row. The one open bill a job has today may not be the bill that sheet's draw was requested on.
- **Nothing on the sub's page.** The window's words say only that the sheet waits on the bill's payment. Step 2 adds what the sub reads.

## Either side of the push

The client reads the new column in a read of its own. Before the migration is pushed that read is refused, the move goes on as it did, and no row shows a tie. The pick's save is the one call that needs the push, and it is reached only from a window that opens after a good read of the column.
