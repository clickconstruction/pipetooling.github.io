# v2.8762 — The New task window no longer waits on a read of the role, and a failed read no longer takes it away (2026-11-27)

Punch list #369. Finding 347 of the fixing thread's ledger, from the simulator thread's sitting 6 of 2026-11-27 (SE, 375 by 667, iOS 26.4, Safari, v2.8752, five ZZ Test tasks sent and deleted). Client only. No migration, no edge function. Nothing owed after the merge.

**As seen.** After a task was sent with the keyboard up, **＋ New task** opened nothing, 4 sends of 4, through three presses over two minutes. The filter chip beside it took a tap at once. A reload brought the press back. With the keyboard put away before Send, 1 of 1, it opened in under 8 seconds. Once a window opened, 14 letters were typed, and the window was gone before the keyboard's Done was reached.

**How the cause was found.** The brief could not find it in the code, and neither could a first read here. A local probe was built: a made-up Manage list with the app's real open and close (`ChecklistAddModalProvider`), its real window frame (`TaskWindowShell`) and its real page scroll lock (`BodyScrollLockSentinel`), and a readout of every tap. The simulator thread read it on the SE: the window opened again 3 times of 3. So the frame, the open and close, and the scroll lock were ruled out, and the fault had to be in what the real window has and the probe did not.

**The cause.** `ChecklistAddModal.tsx` drew nothing unless the person's role could give a task, and it knew the role from its own read alone, made again on every open, with no second try:

- The first open waited on that read before it drew. That is the 8 seconds.
- A read that failed answered no row, and the window wrote that down as no role. An open window then drew nothing. That is the window that vanished while it was typed in.
- The app still held the window as open. So a press of **＋ New task** changed nothing, no new read was made, and nothing could draw until a reload. That is the dead press, through every press.

A read that loses the sign-in lock is such a failure, and that lock was lost again and again on this simulator the same day (findings 323 and 343). A send is several writes in a row, and Manage reads its list again as the window closes, so the next open's read starts among them.

**Shown, not guessed.** `ChecklistAddModal.roleRead.render.test.tsx` opens the window, closes it, and opens it again with the role's read failing. On `main` the window is gone while the app holds it as open, and the test fails. With this change it passes.

**Not shown.** That the role's read is what failed on the live site after those four sends. The page has no log of it. The simulator's walk below settles it.

**The mend.**

- The role the app already holds for the signed-in person stands in until the window's own read answers (`newTaskWindowRole` in `src/lib/tasks/newTaskWindow.ts`). The app never draws a signed-in page without that role, so the window opens at once.
- A read that fails keeps the role it had.

The window's own read still wins once it has answered, so a role that may not give a task still gets no window.

**The older tests' wait.** `ChecklistAddModal.render.test.tsx` waited for the one person's name to be drawn once. That moment was the gap between the window drawing and its people landing, which is gone. The name is drawn twice once the people land, on a face and in the list under it, with or without this change. The wait now takes either.

**Tests.** `newTaskWindow.test.ts`: the role held stands in, the read wins, and it is unknown only when neither is known. `ChecklistAddModal.roleRead.render.test.tsx`: open, close and open again; the first open draws at once though its read fails; a later open's failed read does not take the window away; and what the app holds and what is drawn agree. The window's other 73 test files in the checklist and task folders pass.
