# v2.4285 — Lien waivers: one signature block, signed by the leader of record (2026-10-01)

No migration, no edge function. The first real waiver of the GC train ([v2.4274](v2.4274.md)–[v2.4280](v2.4280.md)) — job 650, Schertz Station, conditional final, Malachi at the assistant's desk — came out with *By: Robert* (the signed-in user), a blank *Title: ____*, a typed name in the cursive face in place of his hand, and four label lines repeating the header. ## What changed

- **The foot** ([`lienWaiverRelease.ts`](../../src/lib/jobsDocuments/lienWaiverRelease.ts) `buildLienWaiverFoot` → `LienWaiverFoot { name, company, title | null, signed | null }`, `buildLienWaiverFootHtml`, `lienWaiverFootLines`, the PDF's `foot` block): the signature (drawn PNG, or the typed name in Great Vibes) above one rule; under it **`<name>`, `<company>`** on one line, the title when set (an empty title leaves the line out — never a blank), and *Signed September 30, 2026* (the day signed in the app calendar, `LienWaiverSignature.signedYmd`; the fields' signature date when a row predates it; *Signed ______* unsigned). `buildLienWaiverSignatureLines` and the *Date / Contractor / By / Title* stack are gone from the print page, the copy-for-email text, the PDF (`buildLienWaiverPdfModel` ends in the foot; the whole block moves to a fresh page rather than split), the window's preview, the sign modal and the leader's Waivers to sign seat (the React twin [`LienWaiverFootPreview`](../../src/components/jobs/LienWaiverFootPreview.tsx)), and the journeys' sample paper.
- **The grey lines** ([`lienReleaseLifecycle.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienReleaseLifecycle.ts) `lienReleaseSignatureAuditLine`): one sentence — *Drawn by Malachi Whites in ClickTooling on Sep 30, 2026 at 9:19 PM CT, on Robert’s screen, consent recorded.* (*Typed by …* when typed; rows from before the signing loop keep *Signed electronically in ClickTooling on …*) — then `LIEN_WAIVER_ESIGN_LINE`, the two statutes, a shade lighter. The row now feeds the sentence its `signer_printed_name` and `signer_signature_mode`.
- **One reading of a signed row** — `lienReleaseRowSignature(row, onDeviceOf?)` replaces five hand-built `{ mode: 'type', … }` literals (the window's print/PDF, the Documents page, the cleared-releases queue, the signature inbox, the email's regenerated PDF): the mode is the row's so the sentence tells the truth; a drawn re-rendering shows the printed name in the cursive face because the PNG is not on the row — the stored signed PDF carries the ink.
- **The signer of record** ([`LienReleaseModal`](../../src/components/jobs/LienReleaseModal.tsx)): *Signed by* is the leader picked under **Signed by the leader**, else the company's signer, else the session's name — never the keyboard. His title rides along when he is the company's signer. Both lines follow the pick until someone types in them (`signerTouchedRef`); the labels read *Signed by (the leader)* and *His title*.
- **Settings → Invoices** ([`PhysicalInvoiceIssuerDevSettingsBlock`](../../src/components/settings/PhysicalInvoiceIssuerDevSettingsBlock.tsx); `PhysicalInvoiceIssuer.signerName` / `signerTitle`, optional in the stored JSON — no migration, the same `app_settings` row): **Signs for the company** — his name and *His title* — with the line it prints read back. He is the leader the window's picker opens on (then the job's master, then anyone).
- **The pad locks on every door** — `signerOfRecord`: the row's `signer_user_id` (else the picked leader) is passed to the sign modal as the present signer whenever it is not the signed-in user, so *Sign it ›* from the Bill tab locks to drawing exactly as *He signs now* does. `signLienRelease` already refused a typed name for a present signer; now no path reaches it unlocked.

## Design calls

- **Settings, not a per-person field.** A title column on `users` meant a migration and a Settings chore per leader that would sit empty — the way *Title* went blank in the first place. One line beside the company name is typed once and also picks the default leader.
- **One column.** The print page, the PDF and the email render three ways; a side-by-side date column wraps differently in each. The date is the block's last line.
- **No verification code in the footer.** A GC's bookkeeper matches waivers to checks by amount and date, both already in the body.
