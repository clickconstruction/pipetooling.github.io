# v2.3363 — Legal desk: the Mark attorney ready sheet previews the firm's view (2026-09-12)

The one prototype item the build missed (owner asked for it by name on the round-2 prototype): a read-only preview of what the attorney will see, on the release sheet itself.

## What shipped

- `src/components/jobs/legal/LegalFirmMatterView.tsx` — the firm's matter view extracted from `LegalPortal.tsx`: matter card (payer, address, theory, note from Click, total demand, print), the five-tab strip, `FirmMatterTab`, and the exhibits card. `acts` is a slot: the portal injects `FirmActs`; the preview passes nothing. Constants and styles live in `legalFirmMatterViewShared.ts` (kept out of the component file for fast refresh).
- `LegalPortal.tsx` renders `FirmMatterView` and dropped its private copy of the renderer.
- `LegalDeskModal.tsx` — on the ready sheet, **Preview what the firm sees ↗** opens a light-pinned overlay (`data-theme="light"`, the portal's paper) above the sheet with `FirmMatterView` on the desk's own packet. The packet already carries the office's curation (`theirWord.timeline[].shared`), so held entries are left out by the same filter the portal applies; the sheet's note is shown as *From Click*; the matter's stored entries (if it was released before) show on Fees & steps. Signed-agreement PDF links are not minted for the preview (they are per-open signed URLs on the portal); the row reads "in the printed packet".
- Help guide *review a collections account before it goes to your attorney*: the Two exits section names the preview.

No migration, no edge function.
