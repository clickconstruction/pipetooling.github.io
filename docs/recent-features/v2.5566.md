# v2.5566 — Training: read a course at your own pace, and pass its checks (2026-10-15)

Punch list #111, PR 3 of six (PR 1 v2.5564 the lessons and kernels, PR 2 v2.5565 the tables). The course page `/training/<slug>`, drawn as the mock-up's second spot (https://claude.ai/artifact/LGSMRUFYFb9BvTqRPkcjvG): a page of its own, not a window, because a two-hour read on a phone wants a URL, scrolling and a resume. No migration; no edge function.

- **`src/pages/Training.tsx`** (lazy route `training/:slug` in `App.tsx`; the lessons ride in this chunk through `courseRegistry.ts`, never in the main chunk): `pageGate` waits for the role, then anyone signed in may read a course that is on and a dev may read one that is off as a preview (*Preview · switched off*). The header carries the course's title, the progress chip (*12 of 31*: lessons done plus the final check), the person's standing in its colour (`courseStanding` + `standingWords`) and the *not a card* line. The rail lists the parts and their lessons with a tick on each one done and the current one lit; on a phone (`useIsMobile`) it folds behind a **Lessons** button. The lesson on screen is pinned once chosen (`?lesson=<key>` wins, else `lessonToOpen`), so passing it never jumps the page. Reads fail soft until the migration is on prod (`trainingIo.ts`, `isNotThereYetError`), an unknown slug and a course that is off are each said in words.
- **`components/training/LessonCheck.tsx`**: the four questions with their options shuffled per attempt (`shuffleOptions`); a pick answers at once, a right one says why in one line, a wrong one says why and offers **Try again** with the options reshuffled and the try counted; when all four are right the page records the lesson once through `training_pass_lesson(slug, key, tries)` and **Next lesson ›** opens. **`FinalCheck.tsx`**: `drawFinalCheck` with a seed, every question answered before **Hand it in**, `gradeFinalCheck` against the bar, a pass recorded once through `training_finish_course(slug, content_version, score, total, pass, answers)` and said in the header's standing, a miss shows the score and **Try again** draws afresh. **`Chip.tsx`** and **`trainingUi.tsx`** hold the shared chips, buttons and `inlineBold` (the lessons' `**…**` as the only markup that passes).
- **`src/lib/training/trainingIo.ts`**: `loadTrainingCatalog`, `loadMyTraining`, `passLesson`, `finishCourse`, with the rows typed by hand until the types regenerate after the push; the client is reached untyped for that reason alone.
- **Tests**: `Training.render.test.tsx` over a fixture course (opens on the first lesson not passed with the rail's ticks and the final check locked; four right picks record once and open Next lesson, a wrong pick offers Try again and counts a try; off said so to a helper and previewed by a dev; the final check draws, grades and records; an unknown slug). **Guide** *take a training course* (Field Work). **Docs**: `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` §23, `docs/twins/APP_DIRECTORY.md`.

Not run live before the merge: the page reads `training_courses` on every load, so a course shows only once PR 2's migration is on prod and a dev turns it on. The live walk (a helper reads lesson 19, passes it, finishes the course) follows the push and the Dashboard card (PR 4).
