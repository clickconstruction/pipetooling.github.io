## What changed

- **Kernel `src/lib/jobs/jobNextLine.ts`** (11 tests): nothing on the board computed a job's "one line" before — the labels came from six separate chips on a 600 px card. `jobNextLine(input)` takes the readings those chips already make (`progressPaymentForJob`, `stagesBillSentPctAlert`, the follow-up queue's quiet days, `billedExpectedPayModel`, `jobContractChipLabel`, the stage bar's caption, the upcoming block, `crewClause`) and picks **one chip, first match wins**: no bid value (red) · set % done (red) · quiet N d (red ≥ 14, else amber) · N d past expected (amber, Billed) · no contract (amber) · draw N ready (green) · $X done, not billed (green, Working). The grey **line** is the next block and its crew (else the crew clause), then one fact: the signed agreement, `bill T+N`, or `open 24 min`. `needsMe` = a red or amber chip; `today` = a block today or someone on site. `advanceConsequence(stage, …)` writes the line on top of a confirmation: the move, the money, the agreement, the schedule that stays.
- **`useJobFollowupQuietDays`** — quiet days per job from the same queue math and candidates cache as the Follow-ups badge; loads only while the phone board is up.
- **`StagesPhoneRow`** — two lines and one chip; tap opens the job, the chip opens its fix, press-and-hold (`useLongPress`) opens the ⋯ sheet, a right swipe (the People phone row's `classifySwipe`) uncovers the stage's forward verb and on release confirms: `'sheet'` (the row's own sheet — Waiting → Working, which had no window) or `'own'` (the stage's action opens the window the desktop uses). `touch-action: pan-y` keeps vertical scrolling native.
- **`JobsStagesPhoneStrip`** — the jump strip as sticky stage chips (stuck under the page's own sticky tab strip, measured from `[data-jobs-page-tabs]`), the All / Needs me / Today row, and the stage's money line.
- **The card lists** (`JobsStagesCardList`, `JobsStagesUnifiedCardList`) take `phoneRows?: StagesPhoneRowsMode` (`stagesPhoneRowsMode.ts`): with it they render `StagesPhoneRow`s through the same `onAction` / `onJobAction` / `onInvoiceAction` and the same ⋯ sheet they already wire. **Bug fixed (since v2.1241):** the bill rows' big button wore the tables' send-back labels while firing the forward action; it now shows `actionLabel`, and the ⋯ sheet's send-back item carries the invoice labels.
- **`JobsStagesTab`**: `phoneBoard = isMobile && stagesMobileCards`. One stage at a time = the first open section (`stagesSectionOpen`), so the section prefs and the fetch-on-expand effect carry it unchanged; picking a chip closes the rest. Section headers hide (`.stagesPhoneBoard [data-stages-section-header]`) unless a search is on, when every stage shows with its name. The map + `PipelineOverview` render inside a fold with `order: 99` in a flex column (closed by default, `jobs-stages-phone-overview-open` per device). The Working move carries `consequence` into `StagesReadyForBillingConfirmModal`.
- Help guide `work-the-pipeline-from-my-phone` (new), GLOSSARY *Pipeline on a phone*.
