# v2.7710 — App updates: the chunk recovery's reload waits for a write (2026-11-17)

Client only. No migration, no edge function. The follow-up [v2.7600](v2.7600.md) named.

## Why

A tab on an older build that moves to a page it has not visited asks for a chunk the deploy replaced. [`chunkLoadRecovery.ts`](../../src/lib/chunkLoadRecovery.ts) answers with one guarded full reload, and it fired the instant the chunk failed. A write still in flight at that moment was cut the way v2.7600's incident was: sent, done on the server, never heard by the page.

v2.7600 makes this path more travelled. An automatic update that stands down leaves the tab on the old build under the new worker, and SKIP_WAITING cannot be taken back. The next move reloads through the quiet-moment gate when nothing is in flight. When a write is in flight the gate refuses, the page's chunk is gone, and this recovery was what reloaded, over that write.

## What ships

- **`decideChunkReloadWait`**, pure: `reload` or `wait` with when to look again.
  - Nothing in flight: reload at once, as before.
  - A write in flight: wait for it. After it comes back, stay `CHUNK_RELOAD_SETTLE_MS` (1.5 seconds), because a write is often the first of a chain.
  - There is no standing down, unlike the update's quiet moment. The page that lost its chunk cannot draw, so after `CHUNK_RELOAD_WAIT_MAX_MS` (30 seconds) the reload goes whatever is in flight.
- **`startChunkRecoveryReload`** still claims the guard and marks the reload as on its way at once, so both boundaries and the preload handler in `main.tsx` draw *Updating app…* while the save finishes. The reload itself goes through the wait. It reads the writes from `unsavedWork.ts` (every non-GET request through the Supabase client), and a test hands in its own `WriteWatch`.
- `RouteChunkBoundary` and `main.tsx` are untouched: both already call `startChunkRecoveryReload`. The fallback's own **Reload** press is the person's and waits for nothing.
- Help guide `app-updates`, `TROUBLESHOOTING.md` and `APP_SHELL_ARCHITECTURE.md` amended.

## Tests

- `chunkLoadRecovery.test.ts` +8: the rule's four answers, then the wait run on fake timers against a stand-in for the write count (a write waited out and its beat, a second write behind it, a write that never returns, a write that begins after the reload has gone).
- `RouteChunkBoundary.render.test.tsx` +1: a save in flight through the counted fetch when the chunk is lost. *Updating app…* draws at once and the reload comes after the save and its beat.

## Not in this change

- Writes that do not go through the Supabase client are not counted, here or in the update's gate.
- `ForceReloadContext`, the developer's reload of every open tab, still reloads at once. It is a deliberate press on everyone and has its own five second guard.
