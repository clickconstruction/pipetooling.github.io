## What shipped

- **`lib/jobs/jobFormCostGates.ts`**, new, pure:
  - `jobFormDeleteGate` — what the delete window reads. A job with cost on it (parts, a job charge with an amount or words, team labor with dollars or hours, a sub sheet) is reassigned, not deleted. A cost read that failed is treated the same, so a delete never slips past an unverified cost. A read in flight says *Checking costs…*.
  - `jobFormLaborLinkGates` — which labor line on the Costs table says *Open on Jobs →*. The team door is not for the office, a superintendent or a primary. The sub door is for all but a primary, and needs the job's number and a sheet.
  - `jobFormEffectiveHcp` — the number typed in the form, else the saved one.
- **`JobFormModal.tsx`**: ten memos are two calls, and the Costs table's *Other job charges* total reads the gate's own sum. 3,796 → 3,751 lines. Nothing else in the form moved: not the autosave engine, the close guard, hydrate, reset or `createJob`.
- **Tests**: `jobFormCostGates.test.ts`, 22 cases. `JobFormModal.costGates.render.test.tsx`, 9 cases on the form itself with the two cost hooks as stand-ins: the delete window's Delete, Reassign and Checking costs branches, and the doors by role. The render test passes unchanged on the form as it was before the cut, and seven wiring mutants die (a dropped failed-read flag, a dropped team row, a fixed role, a dropped loading flag, a fixed job number, dropped charges, a dropped supply total).

## What the tests pinned, unchanged

- A card refund alone takes the parts total below zero, and that is not a cost.
- A job charge with words and no amount is a cost. So are two charges that net to nothing.
- A team-labor row with no dollars and no hours earns the *Open on Jobs →* door and is not a cost.
