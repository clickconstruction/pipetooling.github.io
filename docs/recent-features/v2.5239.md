# v2.5239 — The Bridge: a card behind every bar on the top line (link PR 8) (2026-10-11)

PR 8 of the weakest-link build (`to-dos/bridge-weakest-link/README.md`). Dev only. No migration.

- **The kernel** (`src/lib/bridge/weekCard.ts`, `buildWeekCards`): one card per headline week, read from the recorded weekly grid the top line already draws — earned and field wages from the week's company bucket, the contribution and hours from the headline week, the people from the rows with field hours in that column, the sessions from a count the page makes by pay week, dollars an hour, the three jobs with the most earned (from the bucket's per-job split) with a bar scaled to the biggest and "N more" for the rest, and the foot: the week against the 8-week average with "the best of the 8" when it is, or, for a waiting week, what it reads on recorded time against approved only with the door to the schedule.
- **The bars** (`BridgeHeadline.tsx`): each bar is a button when cards are passed; hover, focus or press opens the card under it (`WeekCardPopover`, anchored to the bar, shifted at the edges); a press pins it, a second press lets go. Without cards the bars keep their old title text.
- **The page**: the headline memo now keeps the two grids and builds the cards beside the headline; sessions are counted by the pay week of their work date (office sessions left out).
- **Tests**: `weekCard.test.ts` (2: a settled week, a waiting week), `BridgeHeadline.cards.render.test.tsx` (1).
- **Docs**: guide *read the Bridge* (the top line section gains the card); the Glossary's Bridge entry; the to-do's status line.
