## Deploy

Client only, with the merge. The cards read PR 1's tables, so they work once
`20261024160000_email_box_count.sql` is pushed (it is pushed when PR 1 merges); before that the card
says the read failed in words. The first count still waits on the owner's admin step (the
`gmail.metadata` scope on the delegation row, in PR 2's fragment and the to-do) and his switch.

## Is this the best we can do?

- The list is the control: nothing is read that a row does not name, and the person sees the same row
  the owner does, in their own words.
- A person may take themselves off the count, and the owner sees that they did; the owner may not
  read a person's rows unless he names himself the lead, and the card says who the lead is.
- Not built here: the station (PR 4) and the trend in the history window (PR 5).
