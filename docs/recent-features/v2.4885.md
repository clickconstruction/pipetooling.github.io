# v2.4885 — AIA G702-G703: put the amounts back as they went out (2026-10-07)

Punch list #92, item 3 (`to-dos/aia-pay-application-follow-ups.md`), after item 1 (v2.4883). Client only. Since v2.4714 the history line and the Documents tab row have named each amount a later save moved against the newest workbook that carries its figures. Their words said *open it and put the amounts back*, but the form had no such press.

## The change

- **Kernel** `withWentOutAmounts(form, snapshot)` (`aiaPayApplicationHistory.ts`), the shape of `withCarriedAmounts`. Each line takes back its four typed amounts, matched by id, then by label. A line added since comes off, a line taken off since comes back, and the lines read in the workbook's order. A matched line keeps its labor split and stage. The G702 takes back the retainage percent and previous certificates (earned less retainage minus the payment due). The original contract sum and the change-order fields are not in the snapshot, so they stay; a difference there stays named.
- **Window** (`AiaG702G703Modal.tsx`): on an open application with a workbook, the form is read against the newest snapshot as it is typed (`payApplicationWriteFromForm` → `changedAfterWentOut`). An amber note says *Changed after it went out Oct 2*, lists each amount (*went out … now …*) and carries **Put the amounts back**. The press rebuilds the form and leaves it unsaved, with a toast: *The amounts are back as they went out Oct 2. Save to keep them.* The note goes once nothing differs.
