# v2.4058 — Bids → Pricing: the price cards row reads from its own hooks (2026-09-28)

## What shipped

- **`src/hooks/usePricingCardsData.ts`** — the three reads behind the price cards row, moved out of `BidsPricingTab` as they were:
  - `useGcNamesById(bidVersions)` — the names of the GCs the bid's versions go to (G1, v2.2154).
  - `useScenarioCardRevenues(...)` — each price scenario's revenue for its card, each priced on its own bid version's count rows (v2.3841).
  - `useAlternateVersionData(...)` — each same-GC alternate version's ★ revenue on its own counts, and its own takeoff materials on a rough-model bid (v2.2404).
- **`src/lib/bids/pricingCardsData.ts`** — the pure half: `gcIdsToLoad`, `gcNameForVersion` (its own GC, an ellipsis while loading, else the bid's GC, its builder, "the GC"), `needsOtherBidVersionRows`, `countRowsByOtherBidVersion`.
- **`BidsPricingTab.tsx`** — calls each hook where its effect stood, so the tab's effects keep their order; `gcNameForVersion` is now a thin wrapper over the kernel. 4,064 → 3,963 lines; 66 → 63 `useState`; 11 → 8 effects.

Part 2 — the row's handlers and its JSX — is the next PR.
