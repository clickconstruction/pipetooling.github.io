# v2.4091 — Where the checks went: the sheet prints on three pages, not seven (2026-09-28)

Client only. The first real print (RMC- Dudley Mason, 11 payments, 22 jobs) ran to seven pages with page 1 blank under its heading; the same content now prints on three. Follows [v2.4050](v2.4050.md) (the sheet) and [v2.4053](v2.4053.md).

## What changed (`src/lib/jobsDocuments/gcChecksAppliedReport.ts`)

- **Page breaks.** `section { page-break-inside: avoid }` forced any table longer than a page onto a fresh page (hence the blank first page and the orphan row + total on page 3). Now only rows stay whole (`tr { break-inside: avoid }`), headings keep their table (`break-after: avoid`), and the header row repeats (`thead { display: table-header-group }`).
- **Columns.** `table-layout: fixed` with a `<colgroup>`: the job column gets a third; Received and Amount get room for "May 19, 2026" and "$17,600.00". **Was on** and **Retainage held** are drawn only when a row would fill them.
- **A check's lines group under the job**, named once, invoices indented beneath; *job paid in full* on the job line, *paid in full* on a bill only when the job is not.
- **Paid by as dates** (`paidByWords`): "#48102 Sep 10 · #48211 Sep 24", "check May 19 · check Jun 4"; four or more read "4 payments, Oct 10 – Mar 10". *Last applied* the same way. The kernel's `GcCheckJob.paidBy` now carries `{ label, receivedYmd, noNumber }` per check.
- **Open, then Paid in full** as two tables, each with its total; the open total (which matches the statement) sits under the open rows.
- **Quieter words.** A check without a number reads *check, no number* in grey on paper (the office's orange chip stays on screen). Money no sent bill needed reads *on the job, not tied to an invoice*. A job name that repeats the street ("Service Visit — 9703 Lenox Hl (HCP 858)") reads as the number alone (`checksJobLabel`).
- Margins 0.75in on screen, 0.5in in print.

## Verified

Rendered from an RMC-shaped fixture (10 payments, 22 jobs) and printed with headless Chrome: three pages, no orphan rows, header rows repeated, every amount inside its cell.

## Not changed

The browser's own "about:blank" footer comes from the print window; it goes away with the browser's *Headers and footers* box unticked, or when the sheet is built as a PDF like the unpaid-invoices print — a later step if asked.
