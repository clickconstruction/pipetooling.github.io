# v2.3756 — Where everyone is: the clocked-in map (2026-09-23)

Punch list #31 (`to-dos/clocked-in-map.md`, mock-up approved 2026-09-22). The owner's ask: *"see everyone who is clocked in on a map based on the job they are working on … a map button to the header in the same style on this dashboard area … the map should open as a modal."* Client only — no migration, no RPC, no edge function: every address the map needs is already embedded on the strip's session rows, and the two shared pins canvases and the geocode hook exist.

## What shipped

- **The button** — `Map` joins the control cluster on the Currently In bar of [`DashboardTeamActiveClockStrip`](../../src/components/DashboardTeamActiveClockStrip.tsx), first in the row, in the cluster's own button style; it shows whenever the Currently In table does, so the Dashboard, People → Hours and Quickfill mounts all get it. The header's last column widens its reserve by the button's width (`mapChrome`). No count on the button: the bar already says *(7)*.
- **The kernel** — [`src/lib/clockedInMap.ts`](../../src/lib/clockedInMap.ts) (pure, tested): `buildClockedInMap` regroups the strip's `sessions` by place (`job:<id>` | `bid:<id>` | `office`), people per stop earliest first with since / elapsed / memo, stops most people first, the legend; `placeClockedInStops` turns geocoded stops into `MapCanvasPin`s whose `label` is the head count and splits the rest into *unmapped* (an address the cache and geocoder have not placed) and *no address*; the unmapped line, the phone summary line, the header stamp, miles from the office, the directions and bid links. Office detection is by the overhead office job (`overhead_office_job_ledger_id_v1`, read once per page by the new [`useOverheadOfficeJobId`](../../src/hooks/useOverheadOfficeJobId.ts)) or by the office anchor's address key.
- **The modal** — [`ClockedInMapModal`](../../src/components/map/ClockedInMapModal.tsx), mounted once inside the strip (lazy). Desktop: map left, the stops rail right; a stop row selects its pin, a pin selects its row; the popup carries the people and *Open job* (the strip's own `openJobDetailFromSessionEmbeds`, closing the map) or *Open bid* (the strip's Bid Board deep link) and *Directions*. The office anchor draws with one 25-mile ring; the office row lists its people and is not a pin. *Not on a job* lists each loose session with the strip's `AssignSessionJobPopover` (above the modal at z 1250; a schedule-implied row reads *Salary schedule* instead). The footer carries the unmapped line with job links and *Open the full map ›*. Phone: full-screen sheet, the summary line, the selected stop as a tinted bar with two 44 px buttons, the rest listed beneath. Esc, × and the scrim close; an Esc while the Assign door is open closes only the door (the map listens in the capture phase — the popover closes itself from a document listener and React flushes that at the microtask checkpoint before a bubble listener would run, so a bubble listener saw it already gone and closed both). Provider: Google when the key loads, OSM otherwise — the same sticky fallback as the jobs map.
- **A badge on a pin** — `MapCanvasPin.label` (optional) in [`mapCanvasTypes.ts`](../../src/lib/map/mapCanvasTypes.ts); [`PinsMapCanvas`](../../src/components/map/PinsMapCanvas.tsx) draws a labelled pin as a DivIcon disc with the text (the selected one wears the link-blue ring), [`PinsMapGoogleCanvas`](../../src/components/map/PinsMapGoogleCanvas.tsx) as a size-up circle symbol with a marker label. Unlabelled pins are untouched, so the Dashboard and Bid Board maps draw as before.
- **Docs** — help guide *see where everyone clocked in is right now*; a row in `docs/DASHBOARD_SECTIONS_ARCHITECTURE.md`; the to-do's status. No `APP_DIRECTORY` change (no new route).

## Decisions kept from the mock-up

A pin is a place, not a person · office sessions on the diamond, never a pin · nobody is placed by guess or by phone GPS · jobs blue, bids violet, office navy (one legend across the three maps) · live from the strip's own tick, no new subscription · whoever sees Currently In sees the map · not in scope: GPS breadcrumbs, "last seen".
