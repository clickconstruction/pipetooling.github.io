# v2.8643 — Task Mode on a phone: a task's words get the width, and its presses sit under them (2026-11-26)

Punch list #369, finding 148 of the simulator's sitting 4 (2026-11-25), part 2 of the fixing thread's brief `369-new-task-window-seen-once-and-the-done-row.md`. Asked on 2026-11-26 *Card #369: build the done row's phone layout now, with a drawn before and after in the PR?*, the person's answer was the pick: *Yes, build and arm*. Client only. No migration, no edge function. Nothing owed after the merge.

**Read.** At 402 points wide, Task Mode's done row put its four presses (**Note**, **Photo**, **Map**, **Follow-up**) two by two at the right, and the words got about 75 to 165 points. The done line wrapped to four lines and the green line under it to four more. The waiting row under **Then** has the same shape. In `ChecklistInstanceCard.tsx` a row that is not the **Next** card drew its presses in a box beside the words, `maxWidth: '55%'`, wrapping. The **Next** card already drew the same presses in a row of their own under the words.

**The mend.** On a phone (`useIsMobile`, the app's 640 px line) a row takes the **Next** card's shape: the presses sit in a row of their own under the words, at the right, and the words have the row's whole width. The rule is one pure function, `taskPressesUnderWords(lead, phone)` in `src/lib/tasks/taskPillStyle.ts`. At a desk a row keeps its presses beside its words in the 55% box. The **Next** card is as it was at every width.

The guide *check off my daily tasks* says where the presses sit on a phone.
