# v2.8196 — Punch list: a place to keep each person's "working on it" ticks (2026-11-21)

One migration, [`20261121193000_punch_list_ticks`](../migrations/20261121193000_punch_list_ticks.md). No screen changes.

## Why

Taunya asked on 2026-11-21 for a checkbox beside each card on `/punch-list`, in her words: *"a checkbox that when I can click next to each punchlist item for myself to know if I am working on it or not, I would like the checkbox to be persistant between sessions if I unclick it or click it, and when a card is moved from ready to build to close out or any section to another the checkmark should be wiped"*.

The page is behind a sign-in (a dev or a master), and she reads it at a desk and on a phone. A tick kept in the browser would stay on one device, so the ticks are kept in the database. This is the place to keep them. The checkbox is the next PR.

## What changed

- **`punch_list_ticks`**: one row per person and card, by the card's number. It holds the section the card was in when the person ticked it, and the newest version in the build of the page that made the tick.
- A person reads and writes only their own rows, and only while they can open the page. Nobody sees another person's ticks, a dev included.
- An untick deletes the row.
- The four fences close the file.

## Why the section is kept

The cards live in the repo, and a card changes section when its file changes and the site is built again. The database never hears of the move. So each tick remembers the section it was made in, and the page compares that with the card's section today. A tick whose section no longer matches is not shown, and the page deletes it.

## Why the build's version is kept

A phone can hold an old copy of the page for a while after a deploy. To that copy, a card filed since looks like a card that left the list. The version on each tick lets a page delete only what a newer build than the tick's has seen move.

## What the fences mean here

- A person in training mode can open the page if they are a dev or a master, and reads their own ticks. A tick or an untick is refused, and the checkbox will say why and go back as it was.
- An archived person reads nothing, and meets the Away note before the page.
- A twin has no way to the page and writes nothing.
