## What shipped

- **`src/lib/bids/copyPricesPlan.ts`** (pure, 15 tests): what sat between the handler's reads and its writes.
  - `copyPricesCrossesVersions`: a source on another version is paired onto this version's rows; a source with no version, or on the version on screen, prices the rows as they are.
  - `copyPricesPlan`: which price lands on which row. A row nothing prices is skipped, a saved $0 copies like any other, and a priced row with no one counterpart here is dropped and counted.
  - `copyPricesOutcomeWords`: the four sentences the copy says afterwards, each with its tone.
- **`BidsPricingTab.tsx`**: the handler keeps its reads, the pricing of the source, the per-row writes, the reload and the freeze. The pairing is `rekeyRowMap`, the one v2.7720 gave the clone re-key, so `mapCountRowsByFixture` and `alternateScopeKey` are no longer imported here. 13 lines out.

## What stays as it was

- **The order.** The writes run in the source's row order, one at a time, through `writeUnitPriceOverrideRow`. A write that fails stops the copy and says why, with no reload, no freeze and no toast.
- **The counts.** A dropped price is counted whether or not a later write fails, and it is read only when every write lands.
