# v2.8146 — Document builder: every press is big enough for a thumb on a phone (2026-11-20)

Punch list #404, left by the review of 2026-11-20 under *Against the mock-up*. No migration and no function.

## What was wrong

The presses of the Document builder tab set their own heights inline, each component its own: 24 px for a line's **×** and **Add a line**, 28 to 32 px for the small presses, 36 to 40 px for **Publish** and the send's main press. Measured at 375 px on the live tab, signed in and pressing nothing that writes:

- Insurance requirements for subs: 18 presses, all 18 under 44 px.
- Sub NDA: on its first page, 7 of the editor's own presses under 44 px, the step bar's dashes aside.

## The fix

One rule in `index.css`, at 720 px and under: every `button` and `select` inside `.documentBuilderTab` has a least height of 44 px, and a button a least width of 44 px with it, so a line's **×** is a square. The rule is marked important, because an inline style outranks a class. `DocumentsBuilderTab.tsx` draws the tab inside that one element, which draws nothing of its own.

So no press was raised by hand, and the send's and the sheet's components were not edited: `BuilderSendList.tsx`, `SheetSendCard.tsx`, `SheetPaper.tsx` and `FilledBuilder.tsx` are as they were. A press added to the tab later is 44 px on a phone with no further work.

Two things beside the rule:

- The stops under a level slider were 32 px on *The basis* and 44 px on a topic's page, in a row of that fixed height. The row is 44 px on both (`DocumentBuilderSlider.tsx`), at every width, so the slider sits at one height through the flow.
- **Try again** on the public page at `/d/<code>` goes from 40 to 44 px.

Measured at 375 px after: all 18 presses of the insurance builder at 44 px, every press of the Sub NDA's editor at 44 px or over on the disclaimer and on a topic's page, none under 44 px wide, and no sideways scroll.

## Left as it was, on purpose

- The step bar's dashes (`.documentBuilderStep`, 24 px). They draw the paper's shape, one dash a step, and eleven presses of 44 px would not fit a phone's width. **Back** and **Next** under them move a step and are 44 px.
- The Documents page's own row of tabs, at 42 px. It is the page's, not the builder's.
- A trade's own page. Its sheet's ways in are links in a line of words, not presses.
- Text boxes and tick boxes. They are not presses.

## Tests

- `DocumentsBuilderTab.render.test.tsx`: the open builder's presses are all inside `.documentBuilderTab`, the eleven dashes carry the class the rule leaves out, and the rule is read out of `index.css` with its `!important`.
- `DocumentBuilderFlow.render.test.tsx`: the five stops are 44 px on *The basis* and on a topic's page.
