# v2.8010 — Before we start: ask the customer about every open item in one message (2026-11-19)

Punch list #382, PR 6 of eleven: Ask them, every ask kept, and the Needs you line. It writes `start_check_asks` (v2.7997). No migration and no edge function. **Nothing goes out by itself**, and nothing here reaches the GC's page.

## What a person sees

- **An open stage** with something open on them ends with **Ask (contact) about N**.
- **The Schedule look's banner** has **Ask (contact) about all N**, for every stage whose day to decide is near.
- **The panel** holds the ask in a box where any word can be changed. It is `askText`: one message to one person, a numbered list under each stage, and *A photo would settle it.* after a bullet that asks for one. Under the title: *One message for 2 stages. Change any word. Nothing goes out by itself.*
- **Text it** and **Email it** open the customer's own box in Messages right there, filled with the ask and tied to the job (`JobCustomerBox`). Nothing is sent in that press. The box's send window is where a person sends it.
- **Copy** copies the words.
- **Sent it another way?** **I texted it**, **I emailed it** and **I called** keep the ask without sending anything.

## Every ask is kept

A send from Messages' box, or one of the three presses, writes one row for each open bullet the ask covered, with the day, how it went and who it was for. One press is one `batch_id`, however many stages it covers.

- An open bullet's line then reads *Asked Nov 17 by text. No answer yet.*
- A stage whose every open bullet was asked in the last two days stops offering the ask as what comes next (`stageStanding`, `ASKED_FRESH_DAYS`).

## The Needs you line

The office's Needs you has a line when a customer owes us answers on a stage whose day to decide is near: *Ray owes us 6 answers on 2 stages*, or *Customers owe us 9 answers on 4 stages* across sites. **Open the list** opens the first site's Schedule look, or the job's window for a job on no site.

The count is the Schedule look's own. `startAnswersOwed` reads the same rows through the same rules (`stageFromRows`, `stagesToAsk`, `noticeFor`), so the line and the banner cannot disagree. The first read is the bullets on them still on a list, and with none nothing else is read. A site that reads the default notice has its address placed for its distance, and only those sites.

## Who gets a message

The box in Messages opens the thread of the job's GC, or of its customer when the job has no GC: the customer record's own numbers and addresses. The name in the ask is the first name of the GC's contact on the bid. Those can be two people. The office reads who the box is addressed to before it sends.

A job with no customer to message offers **Copy** and the three presses alone.

## What is not here, on purpose

- **The ask does not point to their page.** *Or tap to answer on your page.* joins with PR 9.
- **No wasted-trip sentence** (the owner's call 5, on its default).
- **A hold note** is PR 7.
