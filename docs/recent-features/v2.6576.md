# v2.6576 — A rental booked before its agreement number: the pre-use check asks for a photo of the paper first (2026-11-06)

Punch list #271, PR 1 of three. Micah's question of 2026-11-06, after the block editor learned to add a rental (v2.6573): "Sometimes we won't know the rental agreement until after the tech or helper picks it up in the morning … perhaps that tech should be able to take a photo of the rental agreement on their phone that the office can see." He chose photo first, the office types the number; this PR is the phone's half. No migration: `machine_check_photos.shot` takes any name up to 80 characters, and the `photo-save` sweep files every row into the machine's Drive folder as it does the gauges and the four sides.

## What changed

- `src/lib/machines/machines.ts`: `AgreementState` (`none | photo | typed`) and `agreementState(machine, checks)`, a machine of ours reading `typed`; `agreementDue(machine, kind, checks)`, true only for a rental's pre-use check with neither number nor photo; `agreementPhotos(checks, machineId)` newest first with the check each was taken on; `agreementPhotoWords` (*photo in*, then *photo filed* once the sweep has copied it); `AGREEMENT_SHOTS`, the tiles' words and the file words for `agreement`, `agreement_more` and `return_slip`; `mintRentalLabel(from, agreementNo)`; `rentalWords` takes the state and reads *agreement not in yet* or *agreement photo in · number not typed yet* instead of *agreement* followed by nothing. `machines.test.ts` holds each.
- `src/components/machines/MachineCheckSheet.tsx`: for a rental whose agreement is not in, the pre-use check opens with **The rental agreement** under the rental line, two tiles (*The agreement*, *Back page or return slip*) through the same `takePhoto`, and the sign press reads *Photo of the agreement first* and holds until one is in. The stop stays on the check that took the photo and is gone from later ones, whose rental line reads the number and *photo filed*. The leaving check of a rental gains the *Return slip* tile. `ShotTile` takes its words, so any shot can use it. Two render tests in `MachineCheckSheet.render.test.tsx`: the stop and the press on an empty walk, the return slip on the leaving check.
- `src/components/machines/MachineStops.tsx`: the block editor's add form mints the rental's label through `mintRentalLabel`, the one home for *Rental 48812-3* and *Sunbelt rental*.
- The guide *check a machine before you run it*, section *A rented machine*: the stop, the press's words, the return slip.
