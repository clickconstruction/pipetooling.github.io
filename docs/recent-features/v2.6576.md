# v2.6576 — A rental booked before its agreement number: the pre-use check asks for a photo of the paper first (2026-11-06)

Punch list #271, PR 1 of three. Micah's question of 2026-11-06, after the block editor learned to add a rental (v2.6573): "Sometimes we won't know the rental agreement until after the tech or helper picks it up in the morning … perhaps that tech should be able to take a photo of the rental agreement on their phone that the office can see." He chose photo first, the office types the number; this PR is the phone's half. No migration: `machine_check_photos.shot` takes any name up to 80 characters, and the `photo-save` sweep files every row into the machine's Drive folder as it does the gauges and the four sides.

## What changed

- `src/lib/machines/machines.ts`: `AgreementState` (`none | photo | typed`) and `agreementState(machine, checks)`, a machine of ours reading `typed`; `agreementDue(machine, kind, checks)`, true only for a rental's pre-use check with neither number nor photo; the agreement filed page by page, `agreementShot(page)` (`agreement`, then `agreement_p2`, `agreement_p3`…), `agreementPage(shot)` and `agreementTiles(photos)`, every page taken in order and one empty tile for the next, since an agreement can run to several pages and the condition report is often not the front (Taunya, 2026-11-06); `agreementPhotos(checks, machineId)` newest check first with the check each was taken on; `agreementPhotoWords` (*photo in*, then *photo filed* once the sweep has copied it); `RETURN_SLIP_SHOT` and its words; `mintRentalLabel(from, agreementNo)`; `rentalWords` takes the state and reads *agreement not in yet* or *agreement photo in · number not typed yet* instead of *agreement* followed by nothing. `machines.test.ts` holds each.
- `src/components/machines/MachineCheckSheet.tsx`: for a rental whose agreement is not in, the pre-use check opens with **The rental agreement** under the rental line, the tiles from `agreementTiles` (*The agreement*, then *Page 2*… and always one *Another page*) through the same `takePhoto`, and the sign press reads *Photo of the agreement first* and holds until the first page is in. The stop stays on the check that took the photos and is gone from later ones, whose rental line reads the number and *photo filed*. The leaving check of a rental gains the *Return slip* tile. `ShotTile` takes its words, so any shot can use it. Two render tests in `MachineCheckSheet.render.test.tsx`: the stop, the press and the next page's tile on an empty walk, the return slip on the leaving check.
- `src/components/machines/MachineStops.tsx`: the block editor's add form mints the rental's label through `mintRentalLabel`, the one home for *Rental 48812-3* and *Sunbelt rental*.
- The guide *check a machine before you run it*, section *A rented machine*: the stop, the press's words, the return slip.
