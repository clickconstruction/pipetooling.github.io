# v2.8674 — The sign-in page: Continue with Google sits under Sign in, in Google's own dress (2026-11-26)

Punch list #447. The owner, the day Google went live: the sign-in screen "looks terrible and needs to be improved". Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

v2.8670 drew Continue with Google by borrowing the magic-link offer's box: a pale blue panel above the form, holding a blue-outlined press and a line of blue words, set flush against the Email field. It read as a notice, not as a way in, and it pushed the form most people use down the card.

## What changed

- **Order.** Email, Password, **Sign in**, then a thin rule with "or" in it, then **Continue with Google**. The rule is a `role="separator"`.
- **Dress.** A white press with a grey border and Google's four-colour G, the size and radius of Sign in. The G is an inline SVG (`GoogleMark` in `src/pages/SignIn.tsx`), so the page asks for no image.
- **Styles.** Two new classes in `authPublicLanding.css`, `__signin-or` and `__signin-google`. The magic-link offer keeps its own box, which now only the magic link uses.
- The press, its "Opening Google…" state, the refusal words above the form and the switch that shows or hides it are as they were.

Looked at in the browser at desktop and phone width against the live project's settings.
