# v2.7490 — Lien desk: the title bar's door to the run is retired (2026-11-16)

Punch list #362. With the two prints standing under the Board's tiles (v2.7483), the title bar's run door (`data-lien-desk-run`: *Print and mail 15 · 3 of 18 mailed*, a meter, and *1 for Sam to sign*) was a third door to one place and the widest thing on the title line. The owner agreed to retire it and then asked: *"should we retire the title bar's run? does it serve another purpose we aren't thinking about?"*

## What it did besides open the run, as he was told

- It was the only way to print the run from By date and By job. By paper has its own door on the pile's head and a notice's footer; By date has one for the mail numbers only, on printed notices; By job has none.
- It was the only thing that showed the run's progress from those views: the meter, the count mailed, and who it waited on.
- Once everything had printed it read *Enter mail numbers*, which kept the second half of the run in view on every view.

The session's advice was to take it off To do and keep a small press on the other three views. **The owner's call: *"Retire it everywhere"*.**

## What changed

- **`LienDesk.tsx`**: the door is not drawn on any view on a computer. Its meter, its small words and the count of what mailed this week went with it (`runPill`, `runTail`, `runMeter`, `mailedRecent`), and so did the trick that kept its width on By paper's Lien affidavits (`runHiddenByView`, `lastRunWidth`).
- **The title line's measuring kernel** (`src/lib/jobs/lienDeskTitleBar.ts`): the `run` part is gone from `LIEN_DESK_TITLE_LINE_PARTS` (it was budgeted at 463 px), from `LienDeskTitleLineParts`, `lienDeskTitleLineNeeds`, `lienDeskTitleLineFit` and `readLienDeskTitleLine`. The line needs about 1,039 px for all five tools where it needed 1,323 on the day it was measured, and 831 for the four presses where it needed 1,115. A 1,300 px window now holds all five on the title line.
- **The door's styles** (`.lienRunDoorBar` and its kin in `index.css`) are gone.
- **A phone is unchanged**: the run is still the first row of *More*, with its count on the More button (`lienDeskMoreRows`, `runDoorWords`).

## What it costs, said plainly

- **By job has no door to the run.** The office opens To do to print.
- **By date can open the run only on a printed notice**, for its mail numbers.
- **Nothing on By date, By paper or By job says how far the run is.** The Board's tiles on To do do.

## Left in place, on purpose

`runDoorPill` and `runDoorTail` in `lienDeskRun.ts` and `lienRunPill` in `lienRunChecklist.ts`, with their tests, have no caller now. They stay until PR #8382 (the run window's redraw) lands, because it rewrites both files and deleting beside it would conflict. Card #362 names the cleanup.
