## What changed

- **Kernel** [`procurementLog.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/procurementLog.ts): `ProcurementItemSource` takes `partKey`, `partOrder`, `orderOnly`, `quantity` and the part's own `stage`; `ProcurementRecord` and `ProcurementRow` carry `partKey` (a part's line is keyed `part:<procure_key>`). `buildProcurementLog` gives a row with parts one line per part under its tag, in the row's order, each with its own record (`byPart`), stage (the part's, else the tag's — the carrier's Rough In apart from the china's Trim Set), quantity and decision; a tag's own line that holds something typed before it had parts is kept as *the fixture, as logged before its parts*. `gcProcurementRows` leaves the order-only lines off the GC's copies. `procurementSections(rows, lens)`: **To order** (*Order now · <house>* groups of released, unordered lines, the soonest order-by first; then *Waiting on the GC*, *On order*, *Delivered*), **By tag**, **By house** (no house last).
- **Reads** [`procurementLogIo.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/procurementLogIo.ts): `procurementItemsFrom(…, parts)` turns a row with parts into a source per part — the part's name, house, lead time (else the row's), stage, `procure_key`, the fixtures counted on the takeoff × the part's quantity, and its call (its own, else the row's; an order-only part takes the row's, so it is released with its fixture). `procurementRecordFromRow` reads `part_key`.
- **Panel** [`SubmittalProcurementPanel.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/SubmittalProcurementPanel.tsx): *To order · By tag · By house* (remembered per device, `submittals_procure_lens`; To order to start), section heading rows with a tick-all, a tick per line, a **Qty** column, the part lines indented under their tag in *By tag*, *order only, not on the GC's copy* under such a line, and a bar for the ticked lines — one *Ordered* date and one *PO* → **Mark N ordered**, or **Mark N delivered today** (each through the panel's own write queue; a part's line is inserted with `part_key`). The To order line: *N lines are approved and not ordered. The first must be ordered by 10/27.*
- **The GC's copies**: the printed sheet, *Send update*'s snapshot and its changes read `gcRows`; the CSV / Google Sheets keep every line and add **Qty** and **GC sees** (*no, order only*) — 17 columns.
- **The GC's room**: `get-submittal-room` passes each part's `procure_key` (`RoomPart.procureKey`) and each record's `part_key`; the room's Procurement card shows a line per part the GC sees.
