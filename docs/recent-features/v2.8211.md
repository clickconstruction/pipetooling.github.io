# v2.8211 — Maps: Google's own shop and landmark labels are off (2026-11-21)

One of the two calls the audit of punch list [#387](../../to-dos/painted-maps-on-google.md) left open. Taunya picked it on 2026-11-21 from three choices shown as before and after pictures: *Every place label off*. Client only: no migration and no function.

## What was wrong

Google's photograph with street names and its road map both draw Google's own labels: shops, restaurants, theatres, landmarks, churches, park names, bus stops. On the live walks there were about ten of them around two dots at 390 px and about sixteen at 1280 px. One dot sat half over a landmark's icon, and on a painted map a park's name and its one-line description sat beside the line. They could not be pressed (`clickableIcons={false}`), so they were only in the way.

## What changed

One list, `GOOGLE_MAP_STYLES` in `src/lib/map/googleMapLook.ts`, handed to Google's own `styles` option by both canvases:

- `PaintedMapGoogleCanvas`, the painted map opened to be read.
- `PinsMapGoogleCanvas`, which every map of dots draws on through `PinsMapFrame`: the person's task map, the team's, the Dashboard's jobs, the Bid Board's, the Jobs page's, the clocked-in map, the home map window and the prospects map.

The list has two rules: the labels of `poi` off and the labels of `transit` off. `labels` is the icon and the words, so on the road map a park keeps its green and loses only its name. Roads and the names of towns and neighbourhoods are not named in the list, so Google draws them as before. With the place labels gone Google fits more street names into the same view.

The permit card's small still map (`PermitSkimGoogleStill`) is not one of the two canvases and is left as it is: it is a whole part of town at zoom 11, where Google draws almost no place label.

Nothing about when or how often a Google map is made changed. The option is a constant, and the map library compares options by value, so it is set once when the map is made.

## How it was worked out

- **No Map ID.** None of the app's maps has one (`PaintedMapGoogleCanvas` says why: it is a setting in Google's console the app does not have). Google's reference says `styles` is the option for a map with no Map ID, and is not available with one or on a vector map. The live map answers `RASTER` to `getRenderingType()`.
- **Over the photograph.** The reference says that on `hybrid` the styles reach labels and geometry only, which is all that is asked.
- **With `colorScheme`.** Google's pages do not say whether `styles` holds beside the dark scheme. Seen on the live site: it does, on the road map in dark.
- **The library.** `@vis.gl/react-google-maps` 1.10.0 passes `styles` through to the map and to `setOptions`, and keeps `colorScheme` for itself.
- **Not seen locally, and why.** Google's script with no key draws its watermarked map and ignores `styles` altogether: ten plain maps with every mix of map type, scheme and styles all kept their labels. So a local page cannot show this, and the pictures came from the live site.

## Seen

On clicktooling.com, 2026-11-21, before the merge, under the deploy's own key: the task map on Checklist → Today with two made-up tasks on public ground downtown (`ZZ Test walk 387 labels A` and `B`). The three choices were set on the live map through `setOptions` from the page, which is the option this change hands the map when it is made. The shipped build is walked after the merge, and the card says what that walk saw.
