# v2.4487 — AIA G702-G703: the paper beside the form, and nothing left from another job (2026-10-04)

The owner, 2026-10-04, opening the AIA window on Jobs → Pipeline: *"Instead of the current view on AIA G702-G703 we offer this modal as it is on the right hand side and a preview on the left hand side."* And, once the preview showed what the download really held: *"FIELDS should not retain the Mission Hills value."* Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

The window fills a bundled workbook (`public/templates/aia-g702-g703-mission-hills.xlsx`) that was saved from a real job. `fillAiaG702G703Workbook` skipped every field the form left empty, so the cell kept that job's value. Opened on job *Water Sample Test* (no contract sum, no project number), the download read contract sum **$123,600**, **$68,000** this period, **$18,228** of stored materials, project no. **240393cz**, period to **March 31st 2026**. Eleven boxes in all.

The computed cells leaked too. ExcelJS writes each formula back with the result Excel cached when the template was saved, so with `$48,500` typed as the contract the file still carried `H22 = 123600`, `H24 = 86228`, `H42 = 81916.6`. Excel recalculates on open; a viewer that does not (a phone's mail preview, Quick Look) showed those.

Nobody could see any of it before pressing Generate and opening the file.

## The change

- **Kernel** [`aiaG702G703Preview.ts`](../../src/lib/aiaG702G703Preview.ts): `buildAiaPreview(values)` returns every mapped box (`typed` or `blank`; the G703 header boxes K2 / K4 / K5 follow their G702 box) and the sheet's math — G703 line 001 and its totals row, the change order summary, G702 lines 1–9 — with the workbook's own formulas. `formatAiaMoney` / `formatAiaPercent` are the workbook's two number formats.
- **Paper** [`AiaG702G703Paper.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/AiaG702G703Paper.tsx): both pages drawn from the kernel inside a `data-theme="light"` wrapper, scaled to the pane with `zoom` off a `ResizeObserver`. A box is a button (`data-aia-cell`, `data-aia-source`); the sheet's math is plain ink; the long printed paragraphs are grey bars; lines 002–003 are drawn as the workbook shows them (`$0.00`, `#DIV/0!`) and 004–036 are one note.
- **Window** [`AiaG702G703Modal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/AiaG702G703Modal.tsx): from 1000 px (`useMatchMedia`) the paper sits left of the form, which keeps its fields, order and footer; under that a **Form / Preview** pair in the header swaps them. Focusing a field highlights its box and scrolls the paper to it; pressing a box focuses its field (`aia-field-<key>`), opening **Change Orders** when the box lives there. The sentence at the top no longer names Mission Hills and says an empty field is empty in the download.
- **Fill** [`fillAiaG702G703Workbook.ts`](../../src/lib/fillAiaG702G703Workbook.ts): an empty (or unreadable) field sets its cell to `null`. The three G703 boxes that are formulas onto the G702 page are left alone when empty so `materializeG703Mirrors` still copies the G702 box. `stampFormulaResults` writes the kernel's numbers as the cached result of each formula cell (G702 H20 H22 H24 F28 F31 H34 H36 M41 H42 F45 F51 G51 H51 H52; G703 H / I / J / K on rows 13 and 49, D–G on 49) and sets `fullCalcOnLoad`.

## Tests

- `aiaG702G703Preview.test.ts` (8): an empty form downloads none of the template's job — every mapped cell empty, no string in either sheet containing its words, the cached totals 0; the kernel agrees with Excel's cached numbers when given the template job's own inputs; a typed application's preview and download agree cell for cell; the G703 header follows G702 unless typed; the nine lines on a typed case; no percent on a zero scheduled value; the two formats.

## Two things to know

- ExcelJS writes no cached result for a `0` and does not read `fullCalcOnLoad` back, so neither is asserted on a round trip.
- The form still has no box for *from previous application* (G703 column D) or *less previous certificates* (G702 line 7): both are 0 in every download, so a second application for a job is not right without editing the file. That is the next piece — applications the job remembers, numbered, with a way to bring in the ones already sent (mockup: https://claude.ai/artifact/QiMb8rAMgou9ir8sLj8QJw).
