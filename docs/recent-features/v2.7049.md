# v2.7049 — Bills sent: one list of every bill that left, who got it and the minute it went (2026-11-12)

Punch list #327, PR 1 of seven. The master asked for a ledger he can open at any time that shows which bills have gone out, to whom, and when exactly. The design pass found the record already exists: since 2026-10-05 every bill the app emails files a `sent_documents` row with the time to the second, the sender, each address and the copy as it went, and his role can read it. It was only on Documents → Sent copies under the Bills chip, a list made for finding a paper: no amount, no address on the line, no total. So this PR adds no table and writes nothing. It adds one screen that reads the records the app keeps.

## What changed

- **The kernel**, `src/lib/billing/billsSent.ts`, pure, 17 tests. `billsSentLines` turns sent copies into lines, newest first:
  - Every send is its own line. Which send of its bill a line is comes from every send on record of that bill, so a second send whose first is off the page still reads *2nd send. First sent Tue, Nov 3.*
  - A copy to the copy list (`bill_copy`) folds into the send it rode with (the same bill, within five minutes) as *copy to*. One with no send near it stands alone.
  - `counts` is true on the first send on record of a bill that has an amount. A statement never counts. `billsSentDays` bands the lines by day in the company's calendar with the bills that first went that day and their total; `billsSentTiles` gives today and the week since Sunday.
  - The bill's number is the one the payer read, taken from the email's own title (`billNumberFromTitle`). The amount is the bill's (`jobs_ledger_invoices.amount`), a statement's is its own row's total (`gc_statement_emails`, tied by the Resend id), a pay application's is the figure filed with its copy (`source_snapshot`).
  - What the app cannot know is said on the line: a printed pay sheet, a statement printed, a pay application saved as a file.
  - `billsSentFilter` is the search (every typed word, over the bill, the job, the payer, the addresses, the job's street and the sender) and the route chip.
- **The reads**, `src/lib/billing/billsSentIo.ts`: the newest 300 sends whose kind begins `bill` or is `gc_statement`, `gc_statement_print` or `pay_application`, then the bills, the jobs, the statement rows and every other send of the same bills, 80 ids to a request. The first read throws so the screen can offer Retry; the rest are best effort.
- **The screen**, `src/components/billing/BillsSentLedger.tsx`: two tiles (Today, This week), the search box, the chips All · Email · Printed · Statement, a sticky band per day, the lines, **Copy as sent** (the kept copy in its own window, through `openSentCopy`), **Show older sends**, and a foot line that says the times are Central and the list starts 2026-10-05. On a phone the time sits above the line and the address stays on it. A read that fails draws the retry panel.
- **The page**, `src/pages/BillsSent.tsx` at `/bills-sent`, gated by `canOpenBillsSent` (the sent copies' own readers: dev, master, assistant, controller) through `pageGate`.
- **A door**: Documents → Sent copies shows one line under the Bills chip that leads to the page.
- **The guide** *see which bills went out and who got them*.

## Not changed

- No table, no policy, no function. Nothing is written.
- Sent copies, the billed row's stamp, Messages.
- What the list leaves out is said in its foot and in the guide: a send before 2026-10-05, a bill entered from Housecall Pro, a text with a pay link, and a bill that was made and never sent. Those are PR 2. Delivered and bounced are PR 3.

## Checked

Walked on the live data through a local copy with every write refused: 69 lines under 24 days, each emailed line with its address and a copy, 3 marked as a second send, no element past the edge at 375, 768 and 1400 wide.
