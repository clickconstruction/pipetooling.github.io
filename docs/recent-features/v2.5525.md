# v2.5525 — Job Mode: Message the customer reads their own thread (2026-10-14)

Two findings from the prod field walk after the residential build, by Customer communication helper 10, at cc's ask. The walk ran as the Sample primary and the Sample helper on the ZZ test job at 390 px.

- **Whose thread** (`src/lib/jobModeMessageCustomer.ts`, `ownContactId`): the sheet read the customer's contacts with `.limit(1)` and no order, so with two contacts at one customer it could show either one's answer. The ZZ customer has a named contact and an own one, and the sheet showed the own one's text by luck. `quo-send`'s tech path takes the own contact, the one with no name (`contact_find_or_make`). The sheet now reads every contact at the customer and picks the same one. With no own contact it takes the newest; with none, none.
- **`src/lib/jobModeMessageCustomerIo.ts`**: `loadCustomer` reads `id, name, created_at` and hands them to `ownContactId`.
- **Job updates after a note** (`MessageCustomerDoor`, `DashboardJobModeCard`, `JobModeDetailsSection`): the primary's Something else note landed, yet the card's Job updates showed it only after a reload. The door takes an `onDone`, the card counts it, and `JobModeDetailsSection` takes the count as `updatesRev` and calls `useJobThreadNotesForModal`'s `reload` when it goes up. A text sent from the sheet counts too.
- **Tests**:
  - `jobModeMessageCustomer.test.ts`: a named person at the customer is never picked while the own contact is there, in either order; the newest stands in when none is own.
  - `MessageCustomerSheet.render.test.tsx`: the door tells the card once the note went and reads the thread again.
- Not here: the helper's note is refused by the thread notes' policy (a migration, next PR), and a tech has no door to the customer's lines on Job activity (the PR after).
