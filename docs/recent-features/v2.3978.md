## What is new

`src/pages/Bids.render.test.tsx` grows from 14 cases to 29. The page smoke of v2.3962 walked the `?tab=` keys on an empty account; these put one bid on the account and follow a link to it:

- **`?tab=bid-board&bidId=`** — the row is ringed (its landing count reads 1) and the `bidId` leaves the URL.
- **A bid that is not on the account** — the link keeps its `bidId` while the page looks for the bid's trade, and nothing is ringed.
- **`?tab=submission-followup&bidId=`** — lands on By status, `bidId` dropped.
- **`?tab=builder-review&bidId=`** on a bid with no customer — says "This bid is not linked to a customer…", `bidId` dropped.
- **The nine workflow tabs** (`counts` … `lien-release`) with a `bidId` — the tab opens on that bid and the link stays as it is.
- **A superintendent's link to Pricing on a bid** — told so, lands on the board.
- **`?openBidEdit=1&bidId=`** — the bid's window opens and the flag leaves the URL.
