# v2.7437 — Lien papers: the signature prints the day, with no time and no line of how it was signed (2026-11-16)

Taunya's ask, relayed through one of her sessions: remove "at 1:00 AM CT" and "Typed by Malachi Whites with his signing code in ClickTooling on November 16, 2026 at 1:00 AM CT, on Robert's screen." from all documents we release from the lien desk. Client only: no migration, and nothing in the database changes. The edge copy of one kernel is regenerated; no edge function draws a lien paper.

## What a paper prints now

| on the paper | before | now |
|---|---|---|
| the frame's last line | *Signed November 16, 2026 at 1:00 AM CT* | *Signed November 16, 2026* |
| the grey lines under the frame | the sentence of how it was signed, then the statute line | the statute line alone |
| the signature, the printed name and title, the record code | printed | printed, unchanged |

The statute line reads right alone: *Binding as a signature in ink under the ESIGN Act (15 U.S.C. § 7001) and the Texas UETA (Bus. & Com. Code ch. 322).* It names no person and no time, so it leaned on nothing in the sentence that came before it.

**A pen record.** Its frame already read *Signed by pen October 9, 2026*, with no clock, and keeps it. Its sentence (*Signed by pen by … on the paper as printed. Recorded by Taunya, who watched him sign. Confirmed by …*) is no longer printed. Its own line stays: *The signature is in ink on the paper as mailed; this frame is the record of it.*

**A release signed before a notary.** Its page draws no ink, so one short line still says where the signature is: *Signed before a Notary Public of the State of Texas. The signed copy is on file.* It replaces *… the signed copy was filed in ClickTooling on Oct 28, 2026 at 10:05 AM CT.* This one is the session's call: with no line at all the page would show a name, a day and the e-signature statutes and nothing of the notary.

## What stays in the app

- The row: `signed_at`, `signed_on_device_of`, `signer_signature_mode`, `signed_recorded_by`, `signed_witness_how`, `signed_leader_confirmed_at`. Untouched.
- **The Lien desk, under the notice.** Before this change the clock of a notice's signature could be read nowhere but on the paper: the footer's chip says the way, the day and whose screen, and no screen said the time. So the pane now draws the record's sentence under the notice's page, outside the paper: *The signing record, kept here and not printed. Typed by Malachi Whites with his signing code in ClickTooling on October 23, 2026 at 2:14 PM CT, on Robert's screen.* (`data-lien-desk-signature-record` in `LienDesk.tsx`). The pane's page itself shows what the paper prints.
- The footer chips, **Not me**, the To do and run words and the run's checklist read as before.
- **The Release of Lien window** already showed a release's sentence in two places (the signed step's look and the line under **Download PDF**) and still does. The Bids tab's foot note reads it off the row.

## How it is held

- `FilingSignature` (`filingSignatureType.ts`) has no `auditLine`, and `LienWaiverSignature` has none either, so a renderer has nothing to print. `toFilingSignature` drops the record's sentence; `LienNoticeSignature` keeps it for the desk.
- `lienSignedWords(mode, day)` in `lienNoticeSignature.ts` writes the frame's last line. `lienReleaseRowSignature` keeps its second parameter, unread, so every caller's call stands.

## Copies already out

- **Stored files stay as they went.** A release's signed PDF is stored when it is signed (`<release>/signed.pdf`) and is the file every later download and email sends when it is there. A notarized copy is the notary's own file. A courtesy email's saved copy under Sent from this job is a stored file. A certified packet mailed through Docsmit left as a PDF.
- **Redrawn from the row, so a reprint now differs from what went before this change:**
  - a notice or retainage notice still in the run, approved or printed and not yet recorded. A packet printed or mailed yesterday carried the clock and the sentence; a reprint today does not.
  - a signed release's page in the window and its **Print**, which are always drawn from the row. For a release signed before this change, the stored signed PDF (what **Download PDF** and the email send) still carries the old sentence, and the page beside it no longer does. A release with no stored PDF is rebuilt from its row everywhere, the email included.
- **Not affected:** a recorded filing's *As it went out* page is redrawn from the filing row with no signature frame at all, before and after.
