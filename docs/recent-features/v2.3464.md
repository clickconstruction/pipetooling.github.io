# v2.3464 — Submittals stage 1c: the reason at the pick, the lead time, the estimator's override (2026-09-14)

Client + one migration (`20260915025302_bid_quote_line_status_override.sql`, [`docs/migrations/`](../migrations/20260915025302_bid_quote_line_status_override.md)); no edge function. Stage 1's last PR ([`to-dos/submittals/README.md`](../../to-dos/submittals/README.md) → PR 1c): v2.3460 put the Specified column on the compare and derived a status per pick; this PR asks the estimator the question the GC asks later — *why this one?* — the moment the price is tapped, and gives the derived status a human override.

## What shipped

- **Schema**: `bid_quote_lines.product_status_override` (`superseded · equal · design_change`, null = derived). The four v2.3460 columns (`alternate_reason_kind` / `_note`, `lead_time_days`, `availability`) are written for the first time here.
- **Kernel** [`src/lib/submittals/leadTime.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/leadTime.ts): the four presets (In stock · 1 wk · 2 wk · 4+ wk), `parseLeadTime` ("3 wk", "10 days", "stock"; a bare number ≤ 12 reads as weeks), `describeLeadTime` (the row's short form), `availabilityFor` (0 → in_stock, positive → lead_time, a discontinued reason wins), `pickAnnotationPatch` (the five-column patch, note trimmed, days capped at 730). Tests.
- **Compare kernel** [`quoteCompare.ts`](../../src/lib/rfq/quoteCompare.ts): `CompareQuoteLine` carries the five stored fields; every `CompareRowCell` gains `annotation` (`annotationOf`: a kit reads its `kit` line, else the first line carrying any field). Test.
- **The compare** ([`QuoteCompareModal`](../../src/components/bids/QuoteCompareModal.tsx)):
  - The status chip on a picked row is a door (`product-status-door`) to the **PickAnnotationDialog**: *Against the schedule* (As derived · Superseded · Equal · Design change), *Why this pick* (Long lead time · Discontinued · In stock · Or-equal clause · Cost · Other — shown for alternates and design changes; the chips read as reasons so "Equal" the reason never sits beside "Equal" the status), *Lead time* (the presets or a typed value; a bad entry disables Save), a note. Save writes `pickAnnotationPatch` onto **every line of the picked cell** (a kit's lines take it together, like `picked`).
  - **Tapping a price that differs from the schedule opens the dialog on its own** (`maybeAskWhy`: a fresh pick whose derived status needs a reason and has none). Unpicks never ask.
  - The row's second line: the chip, then `· long lead time · 2 wk`, or an amber **· why?** when a reason is owed. The override only holds while that pick stands — an unpicked row derives again.
  - The *Against the schedule* line now counts `3 without a reason` (the reason kind feeds `statusCounts`), and the footer strip adds *N picks off the schedule with no reason yet — tap the status on the row to say why*. Mark reviewed is not blocked (owner's call later, per the plan).
  - Loads the widest line shape first (`LINE_COLS_ANNOTATED`), then the kit shape, then the legacy one — a checkout ahead of either push keeps its kit columns.
- **Guide**: [`get-supply-house-prices-on-a-bid.md`](../../src/content/help/get-supply-house-prices-on-a-bid.md) → Step 3 gains the reason and lead-time lines and an example.
- **Glossary**: the Submittals vocabulary (stage 0's item, not done in v2.3460): specified product · submitted product · alternate · superseded · equal · design change · missing · accessory · cut sheet · submittal package · revision.
