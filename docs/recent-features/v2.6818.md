# v2.6818 — A ticket meets its charge: the office sees the ticket on the charge and sorts with its job first (punch list #295, PR 3) (2026-11-09)

## What changed

- **The match is written by whichever screen runs first** (`src/lib/tickets/ticketMatchIo.ts`): the tech's My tickets over their own card's charges, pending rows included (`list_my_linked_mercury_transactions_for_tally`), or the office's Team queue over everyone's posted charges (`list_card_charges_window` and the To sort list). `runTicketMatches` runs `ticketMatch.ts` and writes each `made` match where the row has none yet; the table's guard and its one-per-charge index keep two screens from writing two. `ask` answers are drawn as a question, never written.
- **The Team queue** (`TallyTeamQueue.tsx`, `TallyTeamDayCard.tsx`): a charge with a ticket wears the green chip *Ticket · JP1042 first* (`ticketChipWords`), opening the ticket; the ticket's job leads the line's chips as the new `ticket` rule in `tallySortSuggestion.ts` (`ticketJobByCharge` on the day's input, confidence *likely*, the words *the ticket says so*); a loose match asks *Jordan's ticket from 10:02 AM: is it $228.58 at Ridgeway Supply?* with **It is** / **It is not**; **Sort the day** marks the tickets on the sorted charges used, so their takers no longer move them.
- **The Tickets tab** (`TeamTicketsList.tsx` over `src/lib/tickets/ticketOffice.ts`'s `officeTicketRows`): what needs a person first, newest first: a question or a pick among the charges that fit, a ticket that disagrees with where the charge went (**Change it to JP1042** rewrites the split and marks the ticket used; **Leave it on JP1038** marks it used), a ticket with no job or for no one job (**Pick a job**), a ticket with no charge (**Pick the charge…** among the holder's charges within four days, **Paid another way…**), a ticket on account.
- **The tech's side**: My tickets runs the matcher too, reads *Matched to $228.58 at Ridgeway Supply*, and answers the one-charge question; the purchase card wears the ticket chip, and the sort window puts the ticket's job first (*Your ticket says so*).
- Guides: *sort the team's card purchases from the office* gains the ticket and the Tickets tab; *take a photo of a supply house ticket* says what the office does. `GLOSSARY.md` → *Counter ticket*, `JOB_TALLY_ARCHITECTURE.md`, the #295 card.
