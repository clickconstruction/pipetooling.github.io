# v2.7826 — Pay: a salaried week can be the days and hours the person works (2026-11-18)

The database half of a per-person salaried week. The owner asked on 2026-11-18. No screen changes.

## Why

A salaried person is paid, costed and scheduled as a flat 8 hours on each weekday at the hourly wage, and nothing on a weekend. A person on salary for a 30-hour week, 6 hours on five days, could only be entered as 8 hours at a lower wage. The pay came out right. The wage on the card, the man-hours on a job, the week's target and the end of the shift came out wrong. A short Friday, four ten-hour days or a Saturday could not be entered at all.

A salary is three facts: the weekly pay, the hours in each day and the days worked. The app held one shape for all three.

## What ships

Migration `20261118160000_salaried_week_hours.sql` ([its page](../migrations/20261118160000_salaried_week_hours.md)):

- `people_pay_config.salaried_week_hours`, the paid hours of each day of the person's week: seven numbers, Sunday first, 0 for a day off. It defaults to 0, 8, 8, 8, 8, 8, 0.
- `people_pay_config_salaried_week_hours_check`, which holds a week to seven numbers, none missing, each from 0 to 16 in quarter hours.
- `salaried_hours_on(week, day)`, the one place SQL asks how many hours a salaried person is paid on a day.

Every row reads 8 hours on the five weekdays, so nothing changes for anyone until a week is set. Nobody new reads or writes anything.

## One field, not two

The first cut of this PR held a single day length. The owner then asked for different hours on different days. A day length with a week added beside it would be two fields that can disagree, so the PR was reshaped before it merged: the week is the only field, and the same hours every day is a week whose days match.

## The method, in three steps

1. This column, then the shared rule, payroll and the Pay & schedule card reading it.
2. The cost readers and the five functions that carry the 8.
3. The card setting the week and the weekly pay, and the workday schedule taking each day's length from it. The card takes the weekly amount and works out the wage. The wage and the week are stored, and the weekly figure is always worked out from them.

Left out on purpose: a pay change that starts on a date. A change to a wage or a week re-prices past days, and it is its own piece of work.

## The bed

`supabase/tests/salaried_week/10_scenario.sql`: a new row's week, seven weeks the check takes, ten shapes it refuses, each day of a week read through the rule, and who may call it.

## Order

Additive and idempotent. The push can come any time after this merges. The client that follows must not merge before the push: it names the column in the read of the pay row.
