# v2.5415 — Messages: who hears a customer's reply as a push, and when, behind the scenes (2026-10-12)

Residential build, R5 (integration 13; the after state is card 13 of `customer-messages-integrations-mockups.html` on spike/gc-mode). A kernel, `src/lib/messages/notifyRule.ts`, pure, with no screen, no migration and no function change. **`messages-notify`** will call it: that function already sends the clause approvals' pushes (v2.5317), and it takes the reply push as a new body shape, so this is not a new function. Its SQL comes with R5's SQL PR, which adds `app_settings.messages_office_wait_minutes` and a nullable `notification_history.message_id`.

- **`pushesFor(inbound, has, settings, sent)`** gives the pushes one line that came in should make, each with its recipients, an audience (`tech` or `office`), a title (the person's name), a body, a link, a device `tag` (one per person) and a due time.
  - `has` is `whoHasReply`'s answer (`hasRule.ts`, #5561) or `routeReply`'s for a legal line (`legalLines.ts`, v2.5404, `reason: 'legal'`). It is written out as the fields it reads until #5561 lands.
  - **A tech has it:** the tech hears at once. The rest of the office hears after the wait (`messages_office_wait_minutes`, 15 to start).
  - **The office has it:** the seat's pick hears at once and the rest after the wait. With no pick, only the wait.
  - **Closed:** the office hears at once, with the after-hours answer: *Dana wrote after hours. The app answered with the hours we open.*
  - **A legal-line reply:** the office alone, at once.
  - **No push:** for a supply house's or a candidate's line, for our own line, for an answered call, or for a push already in `sent` for that line.
  - The kernel never reads roles: the function resolves the office (the Customers seat, else the office team) and passes the ids in `settings.officeUserIds`.
- **The words.** The body is the customer's own words, cut to 140 characters, an email by its subject. Ours pass `plainWordsFailures`: *Nobody answered Dana in 15 minutes. It is on Needs a reply.*, the two after-hours lines, *Called and nobody picked up.*, *Left a voicemail.*
- **Links.** The office's link opens the thread at the line, `/gc?view=messages&thread=<contact>&at=<message>` (`threadUrl`). A tech's push carries `url: null`: a tech cannot open `/gc`, and R5's screens give Job Mode's thread a URL.
- **`pushDue(push, now)`**; **`answeredBefore(line, outs, by)`** drops the office's push after the wait when a text, an email or a call went out to them after the line (a team note or an event does not count).
- **`pushSentRow(push, reached)`** gives the `notification_history` rows, as the notify functions write them today: `recipient_user_id`, `template_type` (`message_reply` for at once, `message_reply_wait` for after the wait), `title`, `body_preview`, `channel: 'push'`, plus `message_id`, which is how `sent` keeps a line from pushing twice.
- **Tests** (`notifyRule.test.ts`, 12) cover Dana's reply at 9:04 AM to Luis's text: Luis at once; the office at 9:19 unless answered; the office at once at 5:10 PM; nothing for a supply house; a legal reply to the office alone; the seat pick; no second push; the plain words. Two pins were broken on purpose and each failed its test:
  - skipping the legal branch failed *sends a legal-line reply to the office alone, at once*;
  - dropping the `sent` check failed *never pushes the same line twice*.
- **Docs**: none beyond this fragment. `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` changes when `messages-notify` takes the new body.
