# v2.3693 — One send, oldest week first: split a payment across the open weeks (2026-09-21)

PR 3 of the Balances redesign ([v2.3689](v2.3689.md) the table, [v2.3691](v2.3691.md) the doors). Client only, no migration. The upstream friction the redesign kept running into: payments are recorded against one report, so one $5,000 Cash App send meant four Record dialogs and arithmetic to land the remainder on the right week — and Malachi's Sep 16 advance ended up on the week of Aug 2 for no reason.

## What shipped

- **Kernel [`openReports.ts`](../../src/lib/people/openReports.ts)** (+4 tests): `splitSend(amount, rows, edits)` — the oldest-first fill as the starting point, every typed box clamped to `[0, balance]` and rounded to cents, `total` and `leftover` (negative when the boxes add up past the send); `splitPaymentMemo(memo, i, n, total)` — `… · 2 of 4 from $5,000.00`, the shape the statement already prints for Tristen's Apple Pay pair; `allocateOldestFirst` / `splitSend` take any `{ stubId, balance }` (`Splittable`), so the Cash App picks and the Balances rows share them.
- **[`PaymentSplitEditor.tsx`](../../src/components/pay/PaymentSplitEditor.tsx)** — the one editor: *Applies to · oldest first / Left / This send*, boxes prefilled from the kernel, settled on blur to what the kernel kept, a footer with the total, the payment count and the leftover ("$872.40 left over — nothing open takes it; file it as an advance or a credit" / "The boxes add up $600.00 past the send").
- **Cash App reconcile → Record** ([`CashAppReconcileModal.tsx`](../../src/components/people/CashAppReconcileModal.tsx)): a **Split oldest first** button beside Save when the person has more than one open report. It swaps the one-report select for the editor; Save inserts one `pay_stub_payments` row per week that takes a share (memo = `cashAppPaymentMemo` + the `i of n` suffix, so rule (a) of the matcher still reads the id), links the transaction to the first payment (`pay_stub_payment_id`), lane *recorded*. *One report* goes back to the old editor; nothing about the single-report path changed.
- **Balances settle line** — *Record one payment, oldest first…* opens [`RecordSplitPaymentModal.tsx`](../../src/components/pay/RecordSplitPaymentModal.tsx): amount (prefilled with what the open reports need), paid-on date, memo, the same editor over the person's open rows; Save writes the parts and reloads the stubs. The per-row *Record payment* still opens the parent's one-report modal.
- Help: `see-what-we-owe-each-person` (the settle line's button) and `reconcile-cash-app-payments` (Split oldest first); GLOSSARY's Cash App reconcile entry names the split.

## Verified

typecheck · lint · `npm test` · theme check. Dev server, prod data, no writes: Malachi's settle line opens the split modal prefilled with $27,878.80 landing as 14 payments; typing $5,000 fills the three oldest weeks (2,309.20 · 2,309.20 · 381.60 of the Jun 21 week) with nothing left over; $30,000 shows $2,121.20 left over. The Cash App lane had nothing in review to exercise live; its split path is the same kernel and editor.
