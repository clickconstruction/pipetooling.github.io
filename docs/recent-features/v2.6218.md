# v2.6218 — Blue buttons take one deeper blue, so their white words read (2026-10-31)

Punch list #194's last sweep, the finding [v2.6190](v2.6190.md) left in passing. The app's classic blue button filled itself with the literal `#3b82f6` under white words. That reads 3.68:1 in both themes: over the lens's 3:1 floor, so no read listed it, and under the 4.5:1 every other coloured button now clears. Colour only; no word changed.

## The pick

Taunya picked the blue on 2026-10-31 from a side-by-side of four shades. It showed the header's *Task* and *Bid*, a *Save*, picked weekday chips and a count, on the app's light and dark grounds.

| Shade | White on it | |
|---|---|---|
| `#3b82f6` | 3.68:1 | today |
| `#1e6ff5` | 4.52:1 | the closest to today over 4.5:1 |
| **`#2563eb`** | **5.17:1** | **picked**: the press blue v2.6068's chips, v2.6186's presses and v2.6190's link fills already wear |
| `#1d4ed8` | 6.70:1 | deeper |

Every press is now one blue, and its white words stay.

## What changed

- **A codemod**, `scripts/codemods/press-blue-sweep.mjs`, parsed with the TypeScript compiler. It reads styles and tone maps (`{ bg, fg }`, `{ fill, ink }`, `{ background, foreground }`), ternaries and same-file spreads. Where white words sit on a `#3b82f6` fill, the fill becomes `#2563eb`, and so does any `#3b82f6` in the same element's borders, so the press keeps one edge. In the stylesheets, a rule that fills with `#3b82f6` under white words takes the same rewrite. It is re-runnable: on a rebase conflict, run it on the new main.
- **The run**: 463 styles in 233 files and 2 stylesheet rules. Among them are the header's *Task* and *New Bid*, the Save and Add presses in the windows across Jobs, Bids, People, Pay and Settings, the picked chips and the counts. A Project's 5-and-up people count is one of them.
- **By hand**:
  - The robot console's four copy and queue presses take their white words from `BTN_PRIMARY` in `twinConsoleStyles.ts`, another file, so the codemod cannot read them.
  - Prospects' *Convert* moved with its rule. Its hover, which filled with `#2563eb`, steps deeper to `#1d4ed8`, and its shadow takes the new blue's tint.
- **CI holds it**: `npm run check:press-blue` runs the codemod with `--check` and fails on a `#3b82f6` fill under white words.
- **The pin**: `readablePairs.test.ts`'s white-on-`#2563eb` line (v2.6186) now names these presses too.

## Left as it was

- `#3b82f6` where it carries no words: lines, borders, rings, focus outlines, dots, map pins, stage bands and icons. Blue words keep their colour too.
- **Found in passing**:
  - A Project's 4-people count draws white on `#60a5fa`, 2.54:1, one step lighter on the same heat scale (`peopleCountColor`).
  - The signed agreement email's button is `#3b82f6` under white in the mail's own HTML, outside the app.
