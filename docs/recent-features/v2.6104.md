# v2.6104 — Legal desk: the Paper tab opens on the lien calendar, with the next step's button and the deadlines as chips (2026-10-29)

Punch list #214 (`to-dos/legal-desk-show-not-tell/`, the owner's pick of 2026-10-29: all four sections, in order), PR 1 of 4. The read that started it: on the Legal desk the lien clock was told three times — the Paper tab's *Next on the path* and *Waiting on* label pair over the step row, and the same deadline again as a sentence under *Before this goes to an attorney* (*Lien window open on 891 — § 53.056 notice due 2026-11-16 (18 days) — File the notice before you refer; …*). The calendar that shows it — the strip's **Windows** view (v2.3815, v2.4652: every paper a bar on one axis, today a line, the closed windows struck) — was behind the Steps · Windows switch, remembered app-wide and defaulting to Steps, and the desk's strip had no door on its verdict band, where the Lien window's has one (v2.4693).

## What changed

- **The desk opens on the calendar** ([`useLienTimelineView.ts`](../../src/hooks/useLienTimelineView.ts)): the remembered view has two scopes, `window` (the Lien window and every other strip, Steps as before) and `desk` (the Legal desk's Paper tab, Windows), each under its own storage key; `LienTimelineStrip` takes `viewScope` and its switch writes to that scope. The Lien window's choice and the desk's no longer move together.
- **The next step's door** ([`legalPaperNextDoor.ts`](../../src/lib/legal/legalPaperNextDoor.ts), pure, tested): the verb and the place for each `LienTimelineNext.kind` — a notice or the retainage notice open the **Lien desk** on the job (*Draft the notice…*, *Draft the retainage notice…*), since that is where they are drafted and sent; the affidavit, its service and the release open the **Lien window** on their tab (*Go to the affidavit…*, *Record the service…*, *Record the release…*); the suit, a gone lien and nothing draw no button. The Paper tab passes it as the strip's `nextDoor`, and `LegalDesk`'s `onOpenLienInstruments` takes the door; the page navigates to `?liendesk=1&liendeskJob=…&kind=notice` or `lienWindowHref(job, tab)`.
- **The deadline as a chip** ([`legalPacket.ts`](../../src/lib/legal/legalPacket.ts)): `LegalGap` gains `chip` and `brief`, filled on the two lien gaps — `§ 53.056 notice · due 2026-11-16 · 18 days` with *on 891 · file it before you refer*, and `§ 53.052 affidavit · by 2026-12-15 · 47 days` with the original-contractor or late-notice words. The desk draws the chip in the label's place (`data-legal-gap-chip`, the long form on hover); the office print and the firm's keep `label` and `detail` as they were.
- **Guide** *review a collections account before it goes to your attorney*: the Paper bullet says the calendar opens first, Steps is a press away, and the next step's button opens where the paper is made; the gaps section shows the chip.
- **Tests**: `legalPaperNextDoor.test.ts` (new, the three groups); `legalPacket.test.ts` (+4, the chip and brief on both lien gaps); `LienTimelineStrip.render.test.tsx` (+1, the desk scope opens on Windows, remembers its own choice, and leaves the window's alone).

## What the mock-up asked for that this does not do

The mock-up (`to-dos/legal-desk-show-not-tell/mockup.html`, section 1) drew the rail with a lane gutter and the next press as a sentence with a verb; the strip's calendar already names each paper on its row and the verdict band already carries the words, so this PR changes neither. The lane gutter is a redraw of the calendar's name column; it waits for a reason.
