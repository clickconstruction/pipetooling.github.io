## Is this the best we can do?

- The price is the assembler's own column and nothing else: the scope, the dates and the retainage stay as the draft holds them, and the send, with its record number and the frozen snapshot, stays the board's one press.
- The hint is read the assembler's way, so the deck and the assembler offer the same number.
- Not pressed live: the drafts on prod are real subs' work. The save ran through the injected io; the deck was walked live on the real rows as the dev, read-only.
