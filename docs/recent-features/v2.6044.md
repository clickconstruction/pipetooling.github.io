## The verbs (dev-mcp 0.9.0)

- **`plan_history_entry(line)` / `apply_history_entry(line, plan_hash)`**: the house's write pair over `dev_history_entry_write`, the fifteenth write. `line` is `{ development_id, happened_at (an ISO timestamp with its offset, the email's time), source (email | call | text | on_site | letter | note), body (whole, under 20,000), agent_label (the robot's name as the line shows it), job_id? | bid_id? | customer_id? (at most one), link_url? }`. The plan replies with the line as it will read; apply runs the dry run again and writes only when it still matches, once per plan (`_shared/devMcpWrites.ts`, tested in `src/lib/devMcp/devMcpWrites.test.ts`). The row lands with `via = 'mcp'` and the robot's name, so the page draws it with the *via MCP* mark and the robot in the mark's title.
- **`get_history(development_id, job_id?, bid_id?, since?, limit?)`**: a site's history as people wrote it, every body whole, through `dev_history_read` as the reader (RLS applies): the kept lines, the job's thread notes and the bid's thread notes, newest first, with who wrote each. The records' own lines (sends, stage moves, bills, payments) stay with `read_rows` on their tables.
