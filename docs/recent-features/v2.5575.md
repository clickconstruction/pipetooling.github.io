# v2.5575 — Training: the card says the size of a course, the page counts lessons and the final check apart, and a pass says the day (2026-10-16)

Punch list #111's walkthrough of 2026-10-16 (headless WebKit as the dev on prod data, the policy flipped to devs for the walk and put back) found nine small things; this PR is the first of three (the page and the card), with the mock-up of all nine at `to-dos/training/followups.html`. No migration; no edge function.

- **`src/lib/training/courseFacts.ts`** (new): `TRAINING_COURSE_FACTS` reads every course's `course.md` eagerly (the frontmatter only, a few hundred bytes a course) and counts its lesson files without loading them, so the Dashboard can say *30 lessons of about five minutes · about 3 hours in all* (`courseSizeWords`) without the lesson text riding in the main chunk (the 6 MiB PWA cap). `course.md` carries `minutes: 176`; `Course.minutesStated` reads it and the content test holds it equal to the lessons' and the final check's sum, so the stated number cannot drift.
- **`src/components/dashboard/DashboardTrainingSection.tsx`**: the sub line before the first press is the size line; started reads *N of 30 lessons done* (it said *Started · N lessons done*, and the old *Required for your role · not started* said nothing the red verdict did not).
- **`src/pages/Training.tsx`**: the header chip reads *N of 30 lessons* and a green *Final check passed* chip joins it after the pass (`courseProgress` gains `lessonsDone`, `lessons` and `finalPassed`; `done`/`of` still count the final check as the last step for older readers). The standing chip draws only when there is a pass to date: with no pass it said *Not done yet* in red beside *0 of 31*, two readings of one fact. The Lessons sheet button on a phone says *Lessons · N of 30*.
- **`src/components/training/FinalCheck.tsx`**: a pass reads *Passed. N of M right. Recorded, due again Oct 16, 2027.* (`recordedLine` from the standing's `dueOn`) and offers **‹ Back to the Dashboard** (`onHome`) beside *Read a lesson again* (`onReadAgain`, the first lesson). Before, a pass left the person on a page of graded questions with no door.
- **`LessonCheck.tsx` and `FinalCheck.tsx`**: each question is a `role="group"` box labelled by its heading, the question inside the box (a `fieldset`'s legend sat on the border, half in and half out). **`src/index.css`**: `.trainingPress`, `.trainingRow` and `.trainingOption` are 44 px tall on a coarse pointer; the page's lesson rows, prev/next and final-row buttons, the card's press and every answer row carry them.
- **Tests**: `courseContent.test.ts` (the progress shape; the stated minutes), `Training.render.test.tsx` (the chip words, no standing chip before a pass, the pass line with the day, the green chip, Back to the Dashboard lands on the Dashboard), `DashboardTrainingSection.render.test.tsx` (the size line and the started line).

Left for the next two PRs: the questions (seven lesson checks whose wrong options gave the answer away or whose asks repeated in the final bank, drawn from the owner's unused question pool) and the office polish (a Preview link and a Clear for the retake day in Settings → Training; the training cap in the Users row).
