# v2.6823 — The Bridge leaves ZZ bids out: the robots' shadow bids are no asks (2026-11-09)

Follow-up to v2.6813 (PR #7504), which gave the Bridge's remaining loaders the ZZ test-job rule and asked whether the bids should follow their own. The yes came the same day. The rule is the bid picker's **Hide robots** (`isZzBidName`, `src/lib/bids/bidPickerRobots.ts`, v2.4511: a project name that starts with the word ZZ), used nowhere else until now.

## Why

On 2026-11-09 Market read **71 bids opened** in the 8-week window. Prod held 84 bids named ZZ since June, 83 of them the robots' `ZZ Shadow …` and `ZZ Twin …` rows, and about 36 of those fell inside the window: half the ask count was robots. They carry no sent date and no value, so the dollars barely moved; the count, the per-week asks, the relationship book's new names and never-answered did.

Checked live on the page with the dev switch flipped both ways: Market's bids opened 71 → 35 and asks 104 → 68; the window's win share read 3% → 1%.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/bridge/zzTestJobs.ts`**: `withoutZzBridgeBids(rows, zzTestJobIds)` drops bid rows by `isZzBidName` on `project_name`. The viewer's `zzTestJobIds` stands for the switch, so null (a dev who shows ZZ rows) keeps every bid, and the Bridge needs no second switch. The picker keeps a ZZ bid marked by or for the person; the Bridge reads for no person, so every ZZ bid leaves.
- **`loadDemandPace.ts`**: the three `bids` reads add `project_name`; a ZZ bid is no ask, no bid sent, won, lost or awaiting.
- **`market/loadMarketDesk.ts`**: the bids read and the first-bids read (which adds `project_name`) drop ZZ bids, so a robot's bid makes no asker's first-bid day and no line in its lifetime.
- **`sales/loadSalesDesk.ts`**: the open reads, the decided read and the bids behind the span's jobs drop ZZ bids: in neither pile.
- **`loadBridgeVectors.ts`**: bids sent and bids won by estimator drop ZZ bids.
- **`loadBridgeData.ts`**: `bridge bids due` adds `project_name`; a ZZ bid is no bid due in the cash forecast's levers.
- `isZzBidName` moves to `supabase/functions/_shared/zzTestJob.ts`, beside `isZzTestJob`; `bidPickerRobots.ts` and `zzTestJobSweep.ts` re-export it. The kernel copy's index may reach nothing under `lib/bids/` (`edgeKernels.test.ts`, the customer-portal upload size), and the ZZ convention has one file.
- Estimates are not touched: they have no ZZ rule anywhere in `src`.
- Tests: the kernel in `zzTestJobs.test.ts`; the loaders in `loadDemandPace.test.ts`, `sales/loadSalesDesk.test.ts`, `loadBridgeVectors.test.ts` and `market/loadMarketDesk.test.ts` (new). The gcKernels copies are regenerated.
