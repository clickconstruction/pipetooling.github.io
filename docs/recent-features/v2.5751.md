# v2.5751 — Bids: a bid left to you says so on the card, and a sent bid shows the robot's ring while it works (2026-10-22)

Two gaps in the Bid Board's robot layer (punch list #142, v2.5732), both seen in the live tests of 2026-10-23. After *Leave this one to me* on b510 the robot obeyed within two minutes (the heartbeat's `orders: 'leave b510'`, the shell voided) but the card drew only a faint robot nobody could press, and the way back was the bid's Edit tab tick. And when the 21:00 round worked b497's coverage shadow after send (36 minutes of real work) the icon sat at the one-step working fill, because `progressBidIds` covered unsent bids only, and its tap opened the comparison modal with no way to the run.

- **`src/lib/bids/robotLeftToYou.ts`** (pure, `robotLeftToYou.test.ts`): `robotLeftToYou(state)` (the `off`/`opt-out` state alone), `robotLeftTitle(project)` (*Left to you. Robots leave X alone. Tap to let the robot back in.*, now the row state's title), `robotLeftLabel`, the two sheet sentences (one per state) and the toast; every sentence passes `plainWordsFailures`.

- **`useBidRobotLayer`**: `robotLeftBid` / `setRobotLeftBid`, `letRobotBackIn` (clears `bids.robot_opt_out` optimistically, reverts on a refused write, reloads the progress), `robotStatusReadRun` / `openRobotStatusRun`; `progressBidIds` now comes from the kernel's selector: every undecided bid with a run or a pairing, sent or not.
