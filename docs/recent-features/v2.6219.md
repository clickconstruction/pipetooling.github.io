# v2.6219 — Phone first: a report photo answers a stage checklist question (punch list #189, PR 6) (2026-10-31)

Punch list #189's PR 6, the plan's item b.3: *Where #164's checklist has a question that asks for a photo, that question is a chip too. The photo is its answer.* Built on PR 5b's camera-first report (v2.6083) and #164's checklist tables (v2.6077) and sheet (v2.6084). One additive migration, no edge function.

## What changed

- **The chips** (`ReportPhotoTiles`): under a new tile, after the stage chips, **Or it answers a checklist question** and one chip per live question that asks for a photo on one of the job's stages, *Rough In · Did the water test hold?* (`checklistPhotoChips` in the new kernel `src/lib/photos/reportPhotoChecklist.ts`: each stage's kind by `stageChecklistKindOf`, its template, `asksPhoto` only; a long question cut on a word). Pressing one puts the photo on the question's stage and marks the tile *Answers: Did the water test hold?* (`tileAnswering`); a stage chip pressed after lets the question go (`tileOnStage`). A job with one open stage left, its other lines paid, asks when a question chip is there and names that stage too, so a plain photo still has a press (`tileAsk`). A one-line job reads as no stage lines on the report (`useJobLineProgress`), so it offers no chips at all; its photo question is answered on the sheet (the card's follow-ups). The questions are read once per open (`loadQuestions`); a failed read offers no chips and the form never waits.
- **The write at Save** (`AdditionalReportModal`): after `attachReportPhotos` ties the shots to the report, `checklistAnswerWrites` makes one write per answered question from the attached shots, the latest shot a question winning, named as the sheet names a photo (*2026-10-31 Rough In · Did the water test hold.jpg*, `checklistPhotoName`); `answerQuestionsWithReportPhotos` (`stageChecklistSheetIo.ts`) makes the `stage_checklist_answers` row for the stage and question when there is none and writes `report_photo_id`, `photo_file_name`, `photo_taken_at` and `photo_bytes`, `photo_storage_path` cleared. Best-effort: a write that fails is a warning toast with its reason (*Report saved, but the photo did not answer its question. …*) and the report stands saved. The pick stays the crew's: the question counts as answered once they press Yes or No on the sheet, as v2.6077 planned.
- **The migration** `20261031150000_checklist_answer_report_photo.sql` (`docs/migrations/20261031150000_checklist_answer_report_photo.md`): `stage_checklist_answers.report_photo_id` → `report_photos`, and two triggers so the answer's photo columns stay true without a second copier: an answer that takes a report photo reads its Drive standing as it stands (and a photo taken on the sheet itself takes the question back), and the report photo's `drive_file_id`, `filed_at` and `fail_why` are mirrored onto every answer that names it when the `photo-save` sweep writes them; a report photo deleted by hand leaves its question unanswered again. The sweep is unchanged.
- **The sheet** (`StageChecklistSheet`): a photo that came from a report draws its thumbnail from the report bucket while it waits (`waitingPhotoUrl` takes `{ storagePath, reportPhotoId, jobId }`) and reads *Taken on a field report. On its way to the customer's Drive folder.*, then *In the customer's Drive folder.* once the sweep files it. The answers are read with every column, so the client reads `report_photo_id` once the push has made it and null before.
- **The report**: the caption a question photo is named by in Drive is the stage and the question (`reportPhotoCaption`: *2026-10-31 Luis Ortega · Rough In · Did the water test hold.jpg*), and **Stage progress** counts the answers after the photos (`photosSentence`: *2 photos on Rough In, 1 answers a checklist question*).
- **Guides**: *take photos for a report* gains **Answer a checklist question with the photo**; *fill a stage checklist on the phone* says a report photo can answer the question too. The Glossary's **Stage checklist** and `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`'s table line carry the column.

## Decisions

- **A migration after all.** The brief said none unless the answer table truly cannot hold a photo reference. It cannot: its photo columns are bound to the `stage-checklist-photos` bucket, which the checklist sweep reads by `photo_storage_path`, and no column could name a `report_photos` row. Copying the bytes into the checklist bucket would have put two files in Drive for one shot; writing the answer with no link would have left it waiting forever with no thumbnail. One nullable column and two small triggers keep one copy and one truth.
- **Triggers, not a sweep change.** The mirror could have lived in `photo-save`'s report pass, which would have meant an edge deploy beside the push and a second place that knows the column. The triggers ride with the migration and close the race where the sweep files a shot between the send and the Save.
- **The answer is written at Save, not at the pick**, like the stage (`attachReportPhotos`): a form closed without saving discards its shots and no answer ever named them.
- **The write survives the gap before the push**: the answers are read with `*`, the write fails with the column's name in a warning toast, and the report with its photos is saved either way. `check:calls-ahead` cannot see a column, so this PR carries the label's meaning by hand: push right after the merge.
