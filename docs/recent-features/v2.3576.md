# v2.3576 — Customer payments: Move to job… on Edit Job → Payments received (2026-09-17)

PR 3 of the payment move/remove train in `to-dos/sub-payment-move-remove/README.md` (Taunya, 2026-09-17: *"delete a payment that was applied to the wrong job"*; v2.3562 did the sub sheets). Drawn in `before-after.html`'s PR 3 board. One migration; no edge function.

## The row

Every saved payment row in [`JobFormPaymentsTable.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormPaymentsTable.tsx) carries **Move to job…** beside Remove (beside *Unlink and remove* on a bank-linked row — the deposit link moves with the payment, so Accounts Receivable still reads it as applied, on the new job). The rule worth keeping, from the to-do: **a payment a sent bill already counted does not move** — the button stays but reads *A sent bill counted it — unlink it from the $4,720 bill first*. A Stripe bill's payment shows no Move at all; an unsaved draft has nothing to move yet. `paymentMoveBlock` / `paymentMoveBlockText` in [`jobPaymentMove.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobPaymentMove.ts) are the rule.

## The dialog — [`JobPaymentMoveModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobPaymentMoveModal.tsx)

Search the destination the way *Reassign costs* does (`search_jobs_ledger`, this job excluded); picking one reads its contract and paid total; a **What changes** panel lists both jobs' paid and open before → after (`planJobPaymentMove`) and says *paid in full* when the destination will be; a reason seeded *wrong job*. **Move $2,400 to J922** calls one RPC.

## The migration — [`20260917180000_jobs_ledger_payment_events.sql`](../migrations/20260917180000_jobs_ledger_payment_events.md)

`jobs_ledger_payment_events` (kind `moved`, the snapshot, the actor, the reason; `is_office_staff()` policies) and `move_jobs_ledger_payment(p_payment_id, p_to_job_id, p_reason)` — the same access check as `remove_jobs_ledger_payment_and_reconcile`, on both jobs; refuses a sent or Stripe-hosted bill's payment server-side too; re-points the live row (same id, dates, amount, memo, type, reference, deposit link; last slot on the destination; unlinked from an unsent bill on the way out), reconciles that bill and the source job's *paid* status the way a removal does, and returns both jobs' `payments_made`. The B3 cache trigger already recomputes both jobs on a `job_id` change.

## The trace

[`useJobPaymentTrace`](../../src/hooks/useJobPaymentTrace.ts) reads the events touching a job (fail-soft before the push) and `jobPaymentTraceLines` draws the grey lines under Payments received: *$2,400.00 moved → J922 · Michael Palmer · Taunya · wrong job* on the job it left, *moved here from …* on the one it reached.
