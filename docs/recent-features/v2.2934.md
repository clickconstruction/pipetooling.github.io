## Kernel — `supabase/functions/_shared/stageAsk.ts` (tested from `src/lib/subs/stageAsk.test.ts`)

`askState` (none / open / accepted / proposed), `askProblem` (both days, order, not behind today, under four months), `answerPatch` (accept → the asked span with `window_by = 'gc'`; propose → the office's span, `window_by = 'office'`, the why), `pickStillFits`, `askLine`.

## The GC's side

- `submit-portal-request` gains **`stage_window`** (`stageId` — a window or a bundle id — `start`, `end`, `note`): GC / merged links only, the window must be offered on a job that shares dates with this GC; writes `asked_*` on every window of the bundle; a `dispatch_requests` row (`pending_action: 'gc_stage_ask'`, the window ids and dates in `pending_payload`) with the GC's name in the title; `notify-dispatch-request` fires.
- `customer-portal` / `gcStages`: entries carry `asked` (`open` / `accepted` / `proposed` + the office's note) and `rescheduling` (the order's `change_requested_at`).
- [`PortalStageAsk`](../../src/components/portal/PortalStageAsk.tsx) (+render test) under every stage on the GC's Stages card: **Ask for other dates** → two dates + why → **Ask**; then the state line; the chip reads **Re-scheduling** while the sub re-picks. Not offered once a stage is in inspection or passed.

## The office's side

Subs → Work, the Window cell on an open ask: "GC asked Sep 22 – Oct 2 · “framing slipped” · now Sep 8 – Sep 19" with **Accept Sep 22 – Oct 2** and **Answer with…** (two dates + a why the GC reads → **Propose**). `answerGcAsk` writes `answerPatch` onto the window (a bundle together) and, when the fulfilling order's pick no longer fits, stamps `change_requested_at / _note` on it ("The window moved to … Pick your days inside it."). An answered proposal reads under the chip as "office answered the GC's ask for …".

## The sub's side

`sub-portal` carries `dates.changeRequested`; the sheet card shows **The office needs new dates** with the why and opens the calendar at once; **Confirm new dates** → `pick_dates`, which skips the day-before rule while a change request is set and clears it on success. Strings en / es.
