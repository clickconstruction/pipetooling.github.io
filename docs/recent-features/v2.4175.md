# v2.4175 — The Contract window, PR 2 of 5: the paper is the form (2026-09-29)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. After [v2.4154](v2.4154.md) (the rail). The second design pass: *the first pass still shows every fact twice, a form on the left and a preview on the right. The office reads the agreement as the customer will and fixes what is wrong in place.*

## What shipped

- **[`JobContractPaper.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobContractPaper.tsx)** — presentational: the agreement laid out as the customer's page and the PDF lay it out (letterhead from the issuer, heading, *Job #N · for &lt;signer&gt; · date*, property, the work, not included, dates, the price line, the payment sentence, *Terms · &lt;document&gt;* with clause count and date, the two signature frames — Customer, and Contractor with the license line). Every line that edits is a button until pressed (`Editable`): the view hovers with a dashed edge and a ✎; pressed, the editor takes its place — a textarea for the work (one line per item), an input for the signer's name, not included and the note, the two dates with *Done*, the payment presets as chips with *Done* and the custom line. Empty things are ghost lines (*+ not included…*, *+ start and estimated completion*, *+ a line the customer reads before the work*) that become fields only when pressed. The amount is read-only with its source and door and the drift line (v2.3707, unchanged); the terms heading carries *read all* and *edit the wording*. Locked (out for signature, or signed), the same paper renders read-only — no ghosts, no editors, the frozen amount, the signature and audit line in place of the frames. `data-theme="light"` and tokens, as the signing page.
- **[`JobContractModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobContractModal.tsx)** — the left column is the paper; the field grid (Standard terms, Scope, Not included, Amount, Payment, Dates, Note) is gone; *Preview as customer* becomes *Open full size* (the same print window); the document select returns under the paper only when the Book holds two or more customer documents. Every test id the rail and reopen tests read is kept (`contract-amount*`, `contract-terms-*`, `contract-download-pdf`).
- **The sent rail's `Group` row helper moves to module level.** Declared inside the component it was a new type every render, so React remounted the rail's buttons on each state change — the Edit & re-send test found its button detached when an in-flight fetch landed between finding it and pressing it, and a real press could lose focus the same way.
- **Tests** — `JobContractPaper.render.test.tsx` (4: the layout and the ghosts; editing in place — the work's textarea, a ghost's field, the payment chips, the name; the terms doors, the drift line and the built-in note; locked). `JobContractModal.rail.render.test.tsx` reads the terms row on the paper.
- **Guide** `get-a-job-contract-signed` → *The agreement*; **`docs/GLOSSARY.md`** → *Contract window*.

## Decided here

- **One document, not a form and a preview.** The sweep shows the paper beside a list of jobs; a single job needs no list, so the paper can be the editor.
- **Press to edit, not always-on inputs.** Inputs styled as text still read as a form; a button that becomes a field keeps the page a page, and a phone gets one editor at a time.
- **The select stays out of the paper.** One customer document is a fact printed on the terms heading; two or more is a choice, shown under the paper.
