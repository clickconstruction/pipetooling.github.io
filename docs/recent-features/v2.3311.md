# v2.3311 — Pay ledger: Balances header as two centered lines, chips centered on one line (2026-09-11)

Client only. Two owner asks on People → Pay → Payroll → **Ledger**, same PR: (1) the four roster filter chips (`All 22` / `We owe 11` / `Owes us 5` / `Even 6`) wrapped, with `Even 6` alone on a second line; (2) the totals sentence `We owe $28,508.30 across 11 · owed to us $8,211.85 across 5 · 6 even` should read as two lines — `We owe … across 11` / `owed to us … across 5` — and both the totals and the chips should be centered.

## Why it wrapped

The roster column is fixed at 300px (`PeoplePayLedgerView` grid: `300px minmax(0, 1fr)`), 271px inside the panel padding. Measured in the browser, the four chips at `0.55rem` side padding and a `0.35rem` gap needed 277px.

## What changed

[`PeoplePayLedgerView.tsx`](../../src/components/people/PeoplePayLedgerView.tsx), the chip row: side padding `0.55rem → 0.4rem`, gap `0.35rem → 0.25rem`, `flexWrap: nowrap` and `whiteSpace: nowrap` on the chips so the row is one line by construction. Same font, weight, radius, colors and pressed state. Now needs ~253px, leaving room for three-digit counts. On phones the roster pane is full width, so nothing changes there.

**Selected chip is outlined** (third ask): the pressed chip's border turns `var(--text-link)` with an `inset 0 0 0 1px` box-shadow of the same color — a 2px blue ring with no layout shift — on top of the existing muted fill + strong text. Unpressed chips are unchanged.

**Totals paragraph**: `textAlign: center`, a `<br />` between the two directions, and the trailing `· N even` dropped — the Even chip directly below carries that count. The chip row gets `justifyContent: center`.

Help: `see-what-we-owe-each-person.md` quotes the totals line; updated to the two-line form.
