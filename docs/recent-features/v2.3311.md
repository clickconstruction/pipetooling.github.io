# v2.3311 — Pay ledger: the roster's balance chips fit on one line (2026-09-11)

Client only. Owner ask: on People → Pay → Payroll → **Ledger**, the four roster filter chips (`All 22` / `We owe 11` / `Owes us 5` / `Even 6`) wrapped, with `Even 6` alone on a second line.

## Why it wrapped

The roster column is fixed at 300px (`PeoplePayLedgerView` grid: `300px minmax(0, 1fr)`), 271px inside the panel padding. Measured in the browser, the four chips at `0.55rem` side padding and a `0.35rem` gap needed 277px.

## What changed

[`PeoplePayLedgerView.tsx`](../../src/components/people/PeoplePayLedgerView.tsx), the chip row only: side padding `0.55rem → 0.4rem`, gap `0.35rem → 0.25rem`, `flexWrap: nowrap` and `whiteSpace: nowrap` on the chips so the row is one line by construction. Same font, weight, radius, colors and pressed state. Now needs ~253px, leaving room for three-digit counts. On phones the roster pane is full width, so nothing changes there.

No help-guide change: the chips and their behavior are as documented in `see-what-we-owe-each-person.md`.
