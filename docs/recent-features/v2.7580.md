# v2.7580 — Materials on site: a place can carry the way to it (2026-11-17)

Punch list #372, *Find a lost box*, step 4 of 5, the database half. The owner's word of 2026-11-17: *Build all the steps*. No screen changes.

## Why

**Where is it?** (v2.7575) draws a straight line to a box's pin, and a straight line can cross a fence. A painted map (#345) knows the gate, because a person drew it. One way painted once to a place serves every box that stands in it.

## What ships

Migration `20261117180000_place_painted_way.sql` ([its page](../migrations/20261117180000_place_painted_way.md)):

- `job_places.painted_map_id`, the painted map that is the way to the place.
- `job_places_update`, an update policy on the gate the job's boxes use. A place had no update door before.
- `job_places_guard_way`, a trigger that opens that door for one column. A direct caller changes the way and nothing else, and the way must be a map the writer can open on the place's own job or its development.
- `on_site_job_in_development`, a yes-or-no helper the guard needs, because a helper on a crew cannot read the ledger.

Nobody new reads anything. A map on a job is already read by the people who read the job's places.

`docs/ACCESS_CONTROL.md` gains this rule and the one v2.7573 owed it: a box's pin remembers who set it.
