# v2.8372 — Dashboard: the Lien desk card names the leader for the office (2026-11-23)

Punch list #423, the one piece of its plan that v2.8339 left out. The owner's word on 2026-11-23, asked whether to fix it before closing the thread: *Yes, fix it here*. No migration and no edge function.

## What was there

The Lien desk card (v2.8339) reads the Board's three tiles off the Dashboard's own read of the desk. Its middle count says *wait on you* to a dev or a leader. For the office, an assistant or a controller, it used the tile's own words, and the Dashboard built its Board with no leader's name, so the tile read *waiting on the leader*. The Board on the Lien desk names him, so the card and the page its press opens said different words. The plan said the office would read his name. v2.8339's fragment gave a reason for leaving it out that did not hold: the desk names one person, the master most of its papers wait on.

## What changed

- **One rule for who the leader is.** `lienBoardLeaderUserId` in `src/lib/jobs/lienBoard.ts`: the master most of the desk's papers wait on, each paper counted once, notices then affidavits, a tie to the master met first. The desk's `boardLeaderName` in `useLienDeskBoard.ts` reads it there now, with the same answer as its own tally gave.
- **The name, read for one person.** `useNeedsYouInputs` picks that person off the Dashboard's light read of the desk, for the office alone, and `useLienDeskLeaderName` reads his one row. `lienDeskLeaderNameOf` in `lienDeskCard.ts` names him as the desk's own list does: an active account that is not a dev, a sample, a twin or the walk tester, his name as the row holds it. The name goes to `buildLienBoard` as the desk hands it, so the tile reads *waiting on* and the name.
- **No read for the leader.** A dev and a leader read *wait on you*, so for them no id is picked and nothing is read.
- **A failed read changes nothing.** With no name the card says *waiting on the leader*, as before.
- **A long name wraps.** On a phone the card's middle and last counts now wrap their words inside their columns. The day's count keeps its width. Before, the first two kept theirs, and a long name could have pushed the card past the screen's edge.

## Tests

- `lienBoard.test.ts`: the leader is the master on the most papers, a job with two papers counts twice, a tie goes to the master met first, a job with no master is skipped, and none at all is null. The Board's tile names the person handed in.
- `lienDeskCard.test.ts`: the name is trimmed; an archived person, a sample, a twin, the walk tester and a dev are named by no one; a blank name is null; the office reads *waiting on* and the name and the leader still reads *wait on you*.
- `useLienDeskLeaderName.render.test.tsx` (5): one read by the id, no read without one, null for a person the desk would not name, null when the read fails, and a name read for an older id never shown.
- `DashboardLienDeskCard.render.test.tsx`: the named middle count, and only the day's count kept from shrinking.
- The Lien desk, Dashboard and phone layout suites: 143 files, 1,689 tests, all passing, the desk's naming among them.
