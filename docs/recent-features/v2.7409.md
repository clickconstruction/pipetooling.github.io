# v2.7409 — Documents: a Painted maps tab lists every painted map (2026-11-15)

Punch list #345. Taunya, after seeing the Painted maps section on a job's Documents tab on 2026-11-15: *I'd also like to see a tab on the documents page for these items.*

## What changed

- **The Documents page has a Painted maps tab,** `/documents?tab=painted-maps`, after Photos. Everyone who opens the page sees it.
- **It lists every painted map the reader can open,** newest first. Four columns: the map, where it is, what is on it, and who painted it and when.
- **Where** is the job's number and name, *Juniper Ridge, every lot* for a development's map, or *On no job yet*. A job the reader cannot see by name reads *On a job*.
- **A row is a door,** as on a job: the name opens the painted map's own window on the map as it is now. The map's own words sit under its name.
- **Paint a map** at the tab's head starts one through the start sheet that exists.
- **A box to find a map** shows from seven maps up. It looks in the name, the home, the words and the painter, and a search with no match offers *Clear the search*.
- The list reads again when a map is made, renamed, moved, added to or thrown away anywhere in the app.

## Why a list of every map belongs here now

v2.7393's fragment said a list of every painted map belongs to the search and the Map page. Both find a map you already know of. Neither is a list to read down, and a map on no job had no list at all. This tab is that list.

## How it is built

- `paintedMapLibraryRows`, `findPaintedMapLibrary` and `paintedMapLibraryCountWords` in `paintedMapDocuments.ts`, pure (+4 tests).
- `DocumentsPaintedMapsTab.tsx`: `loadPaintedMapsEvery` for the maps, the read the Map page's layer makes, then `loadPaintedMapsForSearch` for each home in words and `loadPainterNames`. The last two failing cost their words and never the list.
- `documentsPageTab.ts` learns `painted-maps`. No new table, column, policy or function: row security decides the list.

## Is this the best we can do

- **Two reads of the maps table** (the wholes, then the homes). The second is the search's own and small. One read with the homes joined would be a new query for one tab.
- **The newest 400 only,** the layer's cap. The count line says so at the cap.
- **No Share press on a row yet.** It comes with the list of who opened each link, on the card's plan.
- **No small picture of each map.** A picture a row is a canvas a row. The name and what is on it are enough to pick one.
