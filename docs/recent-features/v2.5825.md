# v2.5825 — Quickfill Physical inbox: Comes back, the tray's routines and the mark's note (2026-10-24)

Punch list #158, PR 4 of the build and the last (the table v2.5815, the deck v2.5818, the folds v2.5821). The press that makes a kind of paper stop arriving, the list of what it made, and a mark that says what was decided.

- **Comes back** on a paper (Task's roles) opens the header's Task window on repeat with the title filled in: `ChecklistAddModalPreset.when` (`'today' | 'date' | 'repeat'`) is new, and `ChecklistAddModal` opens on it. Once the task is saved (`onSaved(id)`, v2.5818) the paper closes with outcome `routine` and the item's id; a cancelled window leaves the paper on the deck.
- **The tray's routines** under the folds: every paper with outcome `routine` whose item still exists, read by `OfficeInboxIo.routines` (the items' repeat rule and assignees, and the next open instance from today) and worded by `routineRows` / `routineRepeatWords` / `nextDateWords` (*Pay and sort the toll bills · every Fri · Marisol · next Fri*, *30 days after done*, *next Nov 1*), soonest next day first, with **Edit in Checklist ›** to `/checklist?tab=manage`. A routine whose task was deleted leaves the list. The hook's list now keeps every routine paper as well as the open, waiting, someday and recently decided ones.
- **The mark's note**: `useReportQuickfillSectionMetric` takes an optional fifth argument, `markNote`, in the same call as the count so one cleanup clears both; the provider's new `getMarkNote` reads it and `markSectionUpToDate` writes it as the event's `note_text` when nobody typed a note. The deck reports `markAutoNote(summary, decidedCounts(papers, today, calendarYmdInAppTzFromIso))`: *0 undecided*, *2 undecided · oldest 4 d · 1 task · 1 filed*. Texts and Email still mark with their typed note.
- **The day it came**: the add line gains **Came**, a date that defaults to today and stops at today (`normalizeCameOn`, which reads a blank, unreadable or future day as today), so a paper that sat in the tray before it was typed keeps its real day and its true age. The build asked for each paper typed once with the day it came; until now the deck could only say the day it was typed.
- **The default door**: the owner answered "build it all" without picking where a decided paper lands, so the build takes the proposal's recommendation, a Checklist task. It is one constant, `PHYSICAL_INBOX_DEFAULT_DOOR` (`'task'`), and the deck draws that door first and filled; `'request'` puts Ask (dispatch or estimating) first instead. The owner's own Todo Tooling inbox has no connection from the app, so its stand-in is a Task with the owner picked as the assignee.
- **The one-time sort** is data, not code, and the office's to do: the four routines the proposal named (toll bills every Friday; insurance cards, registrations, licenses and certificates each 30 days after done) and the three projects (lawsuit papers, demand letters, extra phones, each typed with the day it came and filed or decided). Each routine is a real repeating task for a teammate, which the build's session was not allowed to create on prod; the steps are in the to-do's README. The Checklist repeats by weekday or by days after done, so a monthly look is *30 days after done*.
- **Words**: the guide *clear the physical inbox* gains Comes back, the routines and the note. `docs/GLOSSARY.md`, `docs/QUICKFILL_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/CHECKLIST_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md` and `docs/PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` amended; the to-do reads *built*.
- **Tests**: kernel cases for the repeat words, the next day and the routines block; render smokes for Comes back through a real `ChecklistAddModalProvider` (the window opens on repeat, the paper leaves, the routine lists), the default door drawn first, and the count and note the station hands a real `QuickfillSectionMetricsProvider`.
