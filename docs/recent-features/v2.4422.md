# v2.4422 — Lien instruments on a phone: recording a notice or a lien opens as its own page (2026-10-02)

No migration, no edge function. Left open by [v2.4414](v2.4414.md) (*Not done*). Robert, 2026-10-02: "should we do the same for the other two papers?"

## What was wrong

Measured at 390 × 844 on the dev server against production data, job 273, Lien instruments → § 53.056 notice → **Save & record sends…**. Unlike the demand letter's panel this box is inside the tab's scroll, so it did not squeeze the paper. Its faults were the controls: two selects 28 px tall, four text boxes 30 px, two dates 32 px, **Back** and **Record both sends** 29 px, all in 12.5 px type (an iPhone zooms the page on a field under 16 px), and the saved copy's *note (optional)* box ran past the box's right edge. The lien's **Record filing…** and **Record service…** boxes are built the same way.

## What changed

- **[`LienRecordSheet`](../../src/components/jobs/LienRecordSheet.tsx)** (new): the sheet's shell, lifted from v2.4414's demand sheet. A title line with ‹ Back and ×, a summary of what is being recorded, the step's fields as children, an optional note, one full-width button. [`lienRecordSheetStyles.ts`](../../src/components/jobs/lienRecordSheetStyles.ts) holds the field (full width, 48 px, 16 px type) and its label. [`DemandRecordSendSheet`](../../src/components/jobs/DemandRecordSendSheet.tsx) is now this shell plus its own fields; its props and its look are unchanged.
- **[`LienFilingTabs`](../../src/components/jobs/LienFilingTabs.tsx)**, at 640 px and under (`useIsMobile`), draws a sheet instead of the box for three steps, over the same state and the same writes:
  - **Record the sends** (`notice_sends`): the claim and the job on top; an **Owner** block and an **Original contractor** block, each with how it went, the tracking number and the day; the saved copy's link and note; **Record both sends**.
  - **Record the filing** (`affidavit_filing`): the unpaid amount and the job; County, Recording #, Filed on, the saved copy; the serve-by sentence; **Record filing**.
  - **Record service** (`affidavit_service`): the job and the day service is due; Served on; **Record service**.
  The tab returns the sheet beside its body, so the sheet is a direct child of the window's card; a new `onClose` prop gives the sheet its ×.
- **[`index.css`](../../src/index.css)**: `[data-lien-window-card]:has(> [data-lien-record-sheet])` holds the card at its full height while any sheet is up. It replaces the inline height v2.4414 set for the demand sheet alone.
- **A loop that could not yet happen**: `invoiceDocs` defaulted to a fresh `[]` each render, which re-ran the pay-page effect, whose `setPayAssets({})` rendered again without end. The window always passes the list, so production never hit it; the new test, which does not, hung on it. The default is now one module constant.
- Guide [`file-a-lien-and-never-miss-its-deadlines`](../../src/content/help/file-a-lien-and-never-miss-its-deadlines.md): two lines in *On a phone*.

## Verified

- **Record the sends**, live, same job and size, read only — **Record both sends was not pressed**. The sheet is a direct child of the card and covers it (34 to 810). Every field is 48 px tall in 16 px type and stays inside the sheet; Back and × are 44 px. The summary reads *§ 53.056 notice · $17,585.00 / Dudley (Lennox) · 273*. The sheet scrolls (900 px of content in 724). With the paper scrolled to 200, Back removes the sheet and leaves the paper at 200.
- **Record the filing** and **Record service**: by the render test only ([`LienFilingTabs.render.test.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienFilingTabs.render.test.tsx), new, 4 cases). No job on hand clears the affidavit's gates: all three on the desk's Affidavits tab read *Needs the property facts*.
