# v2.5868 — Quickfill: three fixes from the first live look (2026-10-26)

The close-out of punch list #176 (`to-dos/quickfill-needs-you-review/`). The train landed on 2026-10-25 ([v2.5837](v2.5837.md), [v2.5839](v2.5839.md), [v2.5841](v2.5841.md), [v2.5843](v2.5843.md), [v2.5844](v2.5844.md), [v2.5845](v2.5845.md)), the `outcome` migration was pushed after [v2.5837](v2.5837.md), and the types were regenerated (#6140). The first live look, read-only on prod data as the dev on a worktree Vite at main the night of 2026-10-25, found three things.

## What changed

- **Today's lines counts the viewer's own lines.** `QuickfillNeedsYouSection` reported every line on the card as the station's count, the owner's calls included, so an assistant could never reach **Done** on the station while the owner had approvals waiting, and the mark's `outstanding_count` mixed her lines with his. It now reports `groupNeedsYouItems(items, role).yours.length` ([v2.5843](v2.5843.md)'s actor kernel). The station also reports a Next line, the first of the viewer's lines in the card's own words (`todaysLinesNextLine` in `lib/quickfill/nextLine.ts`), through a new `onNextLine` prop and the page's `QuickfillMetricReporter`.
- **The presses wait while a station counts.** A station's count is null while its body loads, and for a beat after the page paints before its reporter registers at all, and the bar read either as a station with no count: **Looked · this station has no count**, lit, and a press then saved a mark with no count and no outcome. `markPresses(count, noun, loading)` now returns **Done** and **Worked it** dimmed with *counting…* while the count is not in, and **Not sure** lit. The wrapper counts a station as counting while its metric is loading or not yet in the metrics context; `QuickfillRoundScreen` takes `countLoading`, which the page reads off the context the same way for the Roller and the round. Every station that draws the presses has a reporter, so a station with no entry is one whose count has not arrived.
- **The weekend reads as a weekend.** On a Saturday or a Sunday with no marks, `officeRoundState` returned *not yet* and the Dashboard card said **No round yet today**, as if one were due. The *not yet* state now carries `weekend`; the card's chip reads **Weekend**, its headline **Last round Fri, Oct 23 12:16 PM · 23 stations · 12 still open**, and its line *No round is due on the weekend.*
- **The three casts go.** With `outcome` in `src/types/database.ts` (#6140), the Quickfill page's events read, the Mark history window and the office-round card read the column through the typed client again.
