# v2.5074 — GC mode: change a bar in its form, and move one part of a split bar (2026-10-09)

PR 8b of the schedule's real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/SCHEDULE_REAL_BUILD.md`, PR 8, on branch `spike/gc-mode`), after 8a's moves. A dev's until the schedule's PR 10. No migration and no edge function: every write is PR 5's and 6b's.

## The change

- **The bar's form** ([`GcActivityEditor.tsx`](../../src/components/gc/GcActivityEditor.tsx)), ported from the prototype's `ActivityEditor` with its words: a bar pressed opens it above the bar's card for those who may move a bar. Its days, **Not before** and **Must finish by** (G-36), what it waits on with a gap after each (G-35; below zero side by side, G-82), what moves out with it on Save, and what a slip of 5 or 10 days would do (G-80). **Save, and say why** hands the change to **Why it moved** (8a), so a change is a move like a drag: `planMove` with the form's limits, `moveRecord`, `saveScheduleMove`.
- **The days it really ran** (G-55): **Keep the real days** saves them at once through 6b's `setActualDates`, a record with no version. A refusal shows under the line in its words.
- **A part of a split line** (G-39): a part dragged on the chart (`onMovePart`, main's `partMoveOf`) opens Why it moved with the part's days before and after and what its line does (`GcPartMoveLines`, ported). The new kernel `partMovePress` ([`scheduleWindow.ts`](../../src/lib/gc/schedule/scheduleWindow.ts)) is the prototype reducer's `moveActivityPart`: the line's span moves like any bar, or only the part moves when the span holds, and the move keeps the parts' days before and after for Undo and Redo.
- Left for PR 9 on purpose: the trade's papers line (G-77), the place (G-83), the job's own bar's buttons (G-38) and an inspection's pass in the form. The what-if copy stays PR 11's.
- The record's empty line takes back the prototype's *or press one to change its dates*.
