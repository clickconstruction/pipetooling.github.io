# v2.3752 — Pipeline rows show the next two weeks on the calendar (2026-09-23)

Taunya (assistant, on the desktop Pipeline, 2026-09-22): *"It would be easier if I could see when the job is to be complete / scheduled."* The row already knew: the Crew & Dates `j: T-1 (wed)` line was the later of the last approved clock day and the last calendar block (T-minus meaning *future*), and the green NEXT line inside the ≥1100 px activity box showed the next block only when one existed. Nothing said *not scheduled*, nothing said *which day the plan ends*, and the code had to be learned. The mock-up (four options, drawn 2026-09-23) picked the two-week strip; this is its first cut. Client only, no migration.

## The change

- **[`stagesScheduleStrip.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/stagesScheduleStrip.ts)** (new kernel, tested) — `buildTwoWeekStrip({ todayYmd, bookedYmds })`: ten weekday cells from this week's Monday, booked / today / past / weekStart flags, a weekend cell only when a block sits on it, `laterCount` for booked days beyond next Sunday. `deriveStagesWhen(...)` picks the row's state: **scheduled** (next window + names, `endsYmd`, visit count), **unscheduled** (amber on Working, muted on Waiting; the last worked day, or the last *booked, no hours* day when a past block was never clocked), **done** (100 %, or a status past Working, with nothing booked). `describeStagesWhen` is the one-sentence hover / screen-reader label.
- **[`stagesUpcomingSchedule.ts`](../../src/lib/stagesUpcomingSchedule.ts)** — the query that feeds the NEXT line already fetched every future block per job and kept only the first; `pickNextUpcomingAppointmentPerJob` now also carries `bookedYmds` (distinct days, ascending), `lastYmd` and `visitCount` on the same entry. The NEXT window and its assignee merge are unchanged; no new request.
- **[`jobsStagesRowShared.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/jobsStagesRowShared.tsx)** — `renderStagesScheduleStripCells` (one button into the Job Calendar; cell size per surface through `--strip-cell`) and `stagesWhenForJob`; `renderStagesFieldAndBillingLines` draws the strip, then the words (NEXT / ENDS, or the *Not scheduled* flag + *Last* + an **Assign work…** door gated on `canOpenJobScheduleModal`, or *Done*), then the untouched `b:` and man-hours lines. The `j:` sessions-vs-schedule tooltip survives as part of the strip's title. 10 px cells: 10 × 10 + 9 gaps + the 3 px week break = 112 px, inside the 140 px column's 116 px of content.
- **[`JobsStagesCardList.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsStagesCardList.tsx)** — the `job T-1 (wed)` chip is replaced by the 11 px strip plus one chip: **→ Fri Sep 25** when the plan runs past the next visit, **done · Mon Sep 21**, or **not scheduled** (+ **Assign work**). The green NEXT chip at the card foot stays — it carries the time window and the crew. The render test now expects a strip on every card (an empty strip with *not scheduled* is the message, where a "job —" placeholder used to be dead width).
- **[`index.css`](../../src/index.css)** — `.stagesStrip*` / `.stagesWhen*` classes (hover and focus rings need a class; cells use `--text-link` for booked, `--text-700` outline for today, `--bg-amber-tint` / `--border-amber` for the flag).
- **Guide** *see what days a job is on people's calendars* — opens with the strip and its three states; GLOSSARY's `j:` / `b:` entry, `JOBS_STAGES_TAB_ARCHITECTURE.md` and `JOBS_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md` amended.

## Not in this cut (PR 2)

Worked ticks — a ✓ on the days someone actually clocked this week — need one batched read of approved `clock_sessions` for the board's job ids and a new map threaded through the two tables, the card list and the expanded-thread row. Until then, past cells draw fainter and the *Last* line carries the fact. The Job Summary tab's expanded header still shows its `j:` line. The stage-header pills (*Not scheduled 14* / *This week* / *Later*) and the next-first sort from the mock-up's option D are a separate PR.
