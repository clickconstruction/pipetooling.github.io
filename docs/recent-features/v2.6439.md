# v2.6439 — Job photos: a photo offers Open the job once (2026-11-03)

Punch list #244's follow-up, found on the live walk of PR 3 (v2.6365) on the ZZ Test job: the photo viewer drew **Open the job** twice on a job photo, and in the Job photos window a report photo's **Open the report** opened the job window.

## What changed

- **The rule** `sourceDoorOpensJob` (`lib/photos/uploadedPhotos.ts`): a checklist photo's and a job photo's own door is *Open the job*.
- **The viewer** (`UploadedPhotoViewer.tsx`): the general *Open the job* shows only when the caller offers one (`onJob` may now be null) and never beside a door of the photo's own that already opens the job. A checklist photo with no door of its own now takes the general one instead of none.
- **The window** (`JobPhotosWindow.tsx`): its own door is the thread, for a text's picture alone (`openThread`, offered only when the thread has a path); every other photo's one door is *Open the job*, offered only when the window has a job to open. The windows PR 4 (v2.6368) opens inside the job window and on the Job Mode card pass no job to open, so they draw no dead press.
- Documents → Photos, where a job photo also showed two, keeps its props and now shows one. A development's photos (`DevelopmentPhotosLook.tsx`) offer their own door for a checklist photo alone, so nothing changes there.

## Tests

`UploadedPhotoViewer.render.test.tsx` (new: each door combination), `JobPhotosWindow.doors.render.test.tsx` (new: a report photo and a job photo, with and without a job to open), `uploadedPhotos.test.ts` (the rule).
