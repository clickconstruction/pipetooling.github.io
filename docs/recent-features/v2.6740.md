# v2.6740 — Materials on site: the Procure step's door to the job's cages and boxes (2026-11-08)

Punch list #209 (`to-dos/materials-on-site/README.md`), the second leftover. The design gave the office three doors to Jobs → Materials — the Job window's tab, the Pipeline tools menu's row, and the Procure step's On site note once the bid has a job — and v2.6043 built the first two. No migration, no edge function.

The cages and boxes on the site:*, and the press **Materials on site**.
- **The panel** (`SubmittalProcurementPanel.tsx`): one quiet line under the four steps and their share bar, drawn only when the log's stage-dates read found the bid's job (`jobId`, the same read that places the Required column), with a `Link` to the tab; the render smoke reads it on the bid that is job j1.
- **Docs**: the guide *see what is on every job site and what walked away* names the door; `MATERIALS_ON_SITE_ARCHITECTURE.md` (The screens → the doors, The writes, The gaps); the card's status, next, ver, As built, The plan and Left list; the release note and this fragment.

## Read closer than the card

- **The step stays a button.** The On site step is a filter press (`aria-pressed`), so the door is its own line under the strip, not a link inside the button — a link inside a button is two presses on one target.
- **The count is the log's, not the ledger's.** The line names how many procurement lines are marked delivered; the ledger's own numbers are one press away. Reading the on-site store here would load a job's whole ledger onto the Bids page for a sentence.
