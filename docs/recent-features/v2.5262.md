# v2.5262 — Messages: Bill day’s texts to a trade in Spanish (2026-10-12)

This is the Spanish follow-up to v2.5248, the Messages build's cluster 7 (GC Reviews helper 7, directed by the lead session GC Review). Helper 8 drafted the Spanish and proposed where it lives. Bill day's texts to a trade now come from main's trade words table, `src/lib/gc/portalI18n.ts`, beside the portal's own `m*` email keys and behind its gate. `PORTAL_SPANISH_ON` stays off until a native speaker reads the drafts. A caller passes `tradeMailLang(company.lang)`, as the trade emails do. There is no screen and no table.

- **`portalI18n.ts`** gains seventeen `bd` keys in both languages: the draw ask (`bdAskDraw`, `bdDrawDue`, `bdWaiverToo`, `bdSendIt`, `bdSendThem`), the waiver ask (`bdAskWaiver`, `bdPaidThatDraw`, `bdUploadIt`), the reminder on a draw sent back (`bdWentBack`, `bdResendIt`), the send-back note (`bdBackLine`, `bdBackStored`, `bdBackPhoto`, `bdBackAgain`) and the draws a waiver is for (`bdDrawOne`, `bdDrawsMany`, `bdDrawFinal`). A key that may open a text starts in lower case, and the text capitalizes it when there is no first name to greet.
- **`src/lib/gc/billDay/asks.ts`**: `AskTo` gains `lang`, and `sendBackNote` takes one. `owedDrawWordsIn(lang, owed)` writes the draws in the trade's language. In English it is `ownerBilling.owedDrawWords` word for word, and a test holds the two equal. The dates come from `pWeekday` and `pDate` ("lun 19 oct", "28 sep"). The superintendent's text stays English, since she is ours.
- **Changed from helper 8's drafts**: Spanish contracts "de el" to "del", so the draw words carry their own *de*. "The unconditional waiver for draw 2" reads *La renuncia incondicional del pago 2*, and two draws and the final read *de los pagos 1 y 2 y del pago final*.
- **What stays English**: the office's own note on a draw sent back, and the superintendent's note in the send-back note. A Spanish text carries them in the words they were typed in.
- **Tests**: `asks.test.ts` gains three: the draw words against `owedDrawWords` in English and in Spanish; the draw and waiver asks in Spanish, with and without a name; and the reminder and send-back note keeping the typed notes. `portalI18n.test.ts` already holds every key, the `bd` keys among them, to English and Spanish with the same blanks.
- **The edge copy**: `supabase/functions/_shared/gcKernels/lib/gc/portalI18n.ts` is regenerated (`npm run gen:edge-kernels`). No function reads the new keys yet, so redeploying the functions that bundle the copy changes nothing they do. Until then `check:edge-drift` lists them as behind: `gc-trade-portal`, `gc-trade-email`, `submit-gc-trade-portal`, `submit-portal-request` and `send-contract-for-signature`.
