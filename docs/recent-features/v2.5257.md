# v2.5257 — Messages: each step’s usual and How it is going, read from what was done (2026-10-12)

Wave 1 of the Messages build, cluster 6, second of three kernels: `src/lib/flows/going.ts` and its test. Stacked on v2.5253 (`flows/board.ts`), whose lines it reads, and on v2.5254 (the flow's types), whose `FactId` and `FlowDef` it uses. No table, no screen, no edge function, no migration.

**What it lifts.** The prototype's How it is going tab and the usual (v1.102; `goingHtml`, `usualWords`, `FUNNEL`, `GOING_DONE` and `STICKS` in `prototype.html` v1.178 on `spike/gc-mode`), and `MESSAGES_PLAN.md` piece 9. Exports: `usualDays`, `usualByStep` (the input the boards take as `BoardContext.usual`), `usualBar` (the step card's bar), `median`, `quarterStart`, `sticksOf`, `sticksWords`, `goingRows`, `funnel` and `FUNNELS`.

**Changed from the prototype, and why:**
- *Every number is a read.* The prototype typed each step's `usual`, the done counts, the funnel and the where it sticks sentences as fixed numbers. Here they come from `StepSpan`s, one per time a company went through a step: from, to, and the fact that turned true last. The funnel comes from `FunnelRun`s, one per company per job, with the facts that turned true. Wave 2's loader makes both from the stamps, the same ones `flows/state.ts` reads.
- *The usual* is the middle of the done ones over the last 365 days, with no usual under five done, as the plan says. The prototype never computed it.
- *Where it sticks* is generated only in the one form the data can say: "their certificate of insurance, 7 of 9 times", the paper's name from `flows/facts.ts`. It shows on a step that waits on two facts or more, all of them (join 'and'), and a tie goes to the first fact in the step's list. A fork, such as Compared or, since v2.5279, Asked to quote, ends on whichever fact turns true. So "last to turn true" says nothing there, and it shows none. The prototype's hand-written sentences ("7 never quoted, 4 of them never opened the plans", "3 of 5 declines said price") need facts the app does not keep, so they are left out (item 6 of the cluster's report).
- *On it now and the longest* read the board's lines, so How it is going and By step always agree. The prototype counted people on one page and lines on the other. The longest carries its line's tone, so one with its day still ahead is never marked over usual (the lead's call 4).
- *The architect's counts are rounds, not a funnel.* Six plan sets, fourteen questions and nine addenda drawn with arrows read as "→ 233%". They are counts with no arrows (`rounds: true`).
- *The ones that fell out* are a rule, not a number. For the trades, not quoted is a run that ended without a quote. For the customers, declined is one that had our bid and ended without a yes.

**Tests** run over the board fixtures' demo companies. The prototype's own numbers become fixtures: Papers in's usual of 3 days with the certificate last most often, and the trades' funnel of 24 asked, 17 quoted, 6 carried and 5 signed, with 7 not quoted (71%, 35% and 83%).
