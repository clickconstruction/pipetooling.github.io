# v2.8334 — Dashboard: a quiet Back to top at the foot of the page (2026-11-23)

The owner's ask of 2026-11-23, on a screenshot of the foot of a leader's Dashboard on his phone: *a soft back to top button*. He circled the empty band between the last card and the bottom bar. A leader's Dashboard is a long scroll, and the only way back up was to drag. No migration, no edge function.

## What was there

Nothing. The app had no back-to-top anywhere. The Dashboard jumps to its top in three places of its own (leaving *Everything else*, twice opening it), each a plain `window.scrollTo(0, 0)`, so the window is what scrolls and its top is the page's top, under the phone's top bar in the old and the new phone layout alike.

## What changed

- **A quiet press under the last card**, in the band he circled: an up arrow and *Back to top*, centred, in muted words, 44 px tall. It is in the page's own flow. It is not a filled button and it floats over nothing, so it never sits on a card or on the bottom bar.
- **Only on a long page.** It draws when the page is two screens tall or more (`backToTopShown`), read again as the page's cards load and fold. A short Dashboard does not end on a press that does nothing.
- **It glides, or jumps.** The window scrolls smoothly to the top, and at once for a person whose device asks for less motion (`backToTopScrollBehavior`).
- **Focus goes to the top too.** The Dashboard's root takes focus from the press (`data-dashboard-top`, `tabIndex={-1}`, no ring), so a keyboard and a screen reader are at the top with the eye. Focus moves at once and without a scroll of its own, so it never cuts the glide short.
- **Every role, by the length rule.** He named the leader's Dashboard. The page is one component for every role, and the rule already keeps the press off a short one, so it is not held to leaders. Narrowing it to leaders is one condition if he wants it.
- **The full page only.** The Job Mode page and the short view return earlier in `Dashboard.tsx` and are short by design. *Everything else* is the full page, so it gets the press.
- **At a desk too**, above the section bar, when the page is long there.

The pieces: `src/lib/backToTop.ts` (the rules), `src/components/BackToTop.tsx` (the press, not tied to the Dashboard), mounted under Training in `Dashboard.tsx`. A press is counted as `dashboard` · `#back-to-top`.

## Not built, and offered

The bottom bar's **Dashboard** tab, pressed while already on the Dashboard, could go to the top as well, which is what phones teach people to expect. The bar can take it: that press already carries a signal for a person on the short view (`SHORT_DASHBOARD_HOME_STATE`, which leaves *Everything else* and goes to the top), so the same press could do it for everyone. It was not asked for and is not built.

## Checked

- `backToTop.test.ts`: two screens or more, a page with no layout, the two ways of going up.
- `BackToTop.render.test.tsx`: the press on a long page and none on a short one, the scroll it calls (smooth, and instant for reduced motion), focus on the top, and the press following the page as it grows and folds.
- Live, on the Dashboard at a phone's width through the dev login, by hand: the press sat centred under Training in the band he circled, and one press took a page about nineteen screens tall to its top with focus on the top and no ring.
- Not seen live: the press above the bottom bar, since the account looked at shows no bottom bar at that width. The page's own bottom padding keeps its foot above the bar, as it does for Training.
