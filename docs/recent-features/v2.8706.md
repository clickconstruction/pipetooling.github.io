# v2.8706 — Send a paper: the site's presses are a fingertip tall on a phone (2026-11-27)

Finding 294 of the fixing ledger, from a reads-only walk of the live site at 375 px on 2026-11-27 (punch list #439, piece 3's site line). Client only.

## What was wrong

In the email window of Documents → Agreements → Send a paper, the site's line draws **Name a site…**, or **Change the site…** and **Take the site off**. Each stood 32 px tall at 375 px. The window's ×, its choice rows, **Cancel** and **Send email** already stood 44, and the print box's own line passed at 46. These three were the ones left.

## What changed

`PaperSiteLine.tsx` reads `useNarrowViewport640` and spreads `paperPressFloor` over its two link presses, the floor the rest of the Agreements tab uses (`agreementsPhonePress.ts`). At 640 px and under each press is 44 px tall. At a desk the floor adds nothing, so the line looks as it did.

The caller is not touched: the line asks the width itself.

## Tested

`PaperSiteLine.render.test.tsx`, 3 smokes: the three presses at 44 px on a phone, at 32 px at a desk, and no press at either width once the paper's row is written. The 15 cases of `AgreementsSendPaper.site.render.test.tsx` pass unchanged.

Not seen live. The height is read from the style in a smoke, not measured in a browser.
