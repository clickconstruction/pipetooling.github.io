# v2.3018 — Test-gap audit, pass 29: the job materials-cost snapshot gets its first tests (2026-09-07)

Follow-up to v2.3017. Tests only; no kernel, client, migration or edge-function change.

## Why this one

`fetchJobMaterialsCostSnapshot.ts` (188 lines) is the one read behind every materials-cost surface on a job: Edit Job's parts cost, Job Detail's materials section, the charges timeline, combine / separate and migrate. It pulls four sources — the supply-invoice total RPC, the supply-invoice allocations, the Mercury card allocations, and the tally parts RPC — each failing soft on its own. Nine callers, no test.

## What the tests found

Nothing wrong. Contracts now pinned that were only implied:

- The supply total comes from the RPC row for this job only; the allocation lines compute `amount × pct ÷ 100`, read PostgREST's embedded invoice and house whether they arrive as an object or a one-element array, cut the invoice date to a day, and skip an allocation whose invoice is gone.
- Mercury lines keep the allocation's own amount (not the transaction's), the note, the posted day and counterparty, and the debit card parsed from the raw payload; an allocation whose transaction is gone still lists with nulls.
- Tally lines price a part at `price_at_time × qty` and a part-less line (null or blank part id) at `fixture_cost × qty`; blank part and creator names read as null; rows for other jobs are filtered out of the company-wide RPC result.
- Each source fails alone: the invoice RPC, the Mercury read and the tally RPC raise their own flag and leave the rest loaded; a failed allocation-lines read leaves the RPC total standing with no flag. Nothing filed comes back empty, not failed.

## What shipped

- **`fetchJobMaterialsCostSnapshot.test.ts`** (4) — a recording Supabase stand-in (tables and `rpc`) routed by name with per-source throws, the raw-card parser mocked. Covers the four asks and their mapping, the two totals helpers, the empty job, and the four failure modes.
