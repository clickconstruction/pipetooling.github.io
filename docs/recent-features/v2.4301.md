# v2.4301 — Contracts: file a signed paper from the bill, for the jobs it names (2026-10-01)

**The ask (Grace, 2026-10-01),** looking at job 251's View bill: "With contracts do we have an area to link to a contract that has already been signed in the app or uploaded to google docs? I would like to add it to this view." Then, on the first mock-up: "I don't think a contract should ever cover every job with someone. But I do think a contract could cover multiple jobs that are specific." Mock-up canvas: https://claude.ai/artifact/5EJ7wdYZuM9y7ND73ipzK6 (six boards; approved "build all of it").

**What was true.** The bill strip (`JobContractStrip`, strip variant) offered only Send contract. The paste-the-Drive-link field existed on Edit Job's Contract row alone (`variant="inline"`, v2.3669). Coverage is per job, and builders sign one paper that names several jobs, which is why the first Drive pass filed 2 of 104 (`to-dos/owner-decisions-pending.md`, *Customer-level (master) agreements*). Read live 2026-10-01: 2 signed `job_contracts` rows company-wide; Michael Palmer has 15 jobs as customer or GC and none signed. The owner decision is answered narrower than it was asked: a paper covers the jobs it names, never the customer.

## What changed

- **Migration** `20261001180000_job_contracts_covers_group.sql`: `job_contracts.covers_group_id` (`docs/migrations/20261001180000_job_contracts_covers_group.md`). Every covered job still has its own signed paper row; the rows of one paper share the id.
- **Kernel** [`jobContractCovers.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobContractCovers.ts) (+ test): `coversGroupKey`, `joinJobNumbers`, `contractCoversLine` (*Covers jobs 251, 825 and 843*), `buildCoverableJobs` (the customer's other jobs, open first by number, paid last, a job another signed contract covers greyed), `coversSheetWords` (the sentence and the button: *File for 3 jobs*), `coversEditDiff`, `groupPapers` / `isLivePaperFiling` (a live signed `paper` row with a link or a scan), `siblingPaperOffers` / `siblingOfferWords`, `jobStatusWord`, `jobNumberSortKey`.
- **Writes** [`jobContractCoversWrite.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobContractCoversWrite.ts): `loadPartyJobs` (the customer's jobs as customer or GC), `fileContractForJobs` (one `fileSignedJobContract` per job, so a live draft or a handed-over copy converts in place, then the group id), `addJobToPaper` (a new row copying the paper's record; a scan is copied to the new row's folder, the old path reused if the copy is refused), `voidPaperRows`, `saveCoveredJobs`.
- **The sheet** [`AddJobContractSheet.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/AddJobContractSheet.tsx) (+ render test): the link (or *Have a scan or photo instead?*), *Which jobs does it cover?* with this job fixed and every other job a tick, *Signed by* / *Signed on* (`FinishedDateInput`; blank = today), the sentence, and the counted button. `mode="edit"` changes a paper's jobs.
- **The strip** (`JobContractStrip`, strip variant only — the inline Edit Job row keeps its own field): *Add the contract* beside *Send one to sign*; *Nothing on file for this job*; a signed paper that covers more than this job adds *Covers jobs …*; a job with nothing on file shows [`JobContractSiblingOffer`](../../src/components/jobs/JobContractSiblingOffer.tsx) for papers on the same customer's other jobs (*Open ↗* · *Add this job to it*). Papers load through [`useJobContractPapers`](../../src/hooks/useJobContractPapers.ts).
- **Customer page** [`CustomerAgreementsCard.tsx`](../../src/components/customers/CustomerAgreementsCard.tsx), above *Their journey*, office roles (`canSeeWhatCustomersSee`): each paper with its job chips (each opens the job), *Open ↗*, *Change the jobs…*, *Take it off…* (asks once, voids every row of the paper), *Add one*, and *Open jobs with no agreement* (by the coverage kernel, so an accepted estimate or a Not needed answer is not listed).
- **Docs**: guide *get a job contract signed* (new *One signed paper for several jobs*; the bill bullet); `GLOSSARY.md` (Signed paper for several jobs).

## Not done

- The Edit Job Contract row (inline variant) keeps its one-job field; the bill and the Customer page are where several jobs are filed.
- A contract signed in the app (link, in person) is never offered to another job: it names its own job.
