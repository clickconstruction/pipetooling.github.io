# v2.5721 — Settings: the office can be open around the clock (2026-10-21)

Punch list #141 PR 1 of 5 (`to-dos/quiet-hours/README.md` → The plan → PR 1, decision 1 B; the owner's yes of 2026-10-21 on the second pass). The owner had set the Office hours card to every day, 12:00 AM to 11:59 PM, because the company is 24/7 right now, and asked for a checkbox that says so. Every day 00:00 to 23:59 was a lie by a minute: `officeOpen` was false from 11:59 PM to midnight, so a reply in that minute had nobody, and the line read *Every day, 12:00 AM to 11:59 PM*. No migration. One mirrored kernel changed, so three functions redeploy.

- **The kernel** ([`hasRule.ts`](../../src/lib/messages/hasRule.ts), mirrored): `OfficeHours.always` (default false; `parseOfficeHours` reads only the boolean `true`, so every row written before the switch reads as before). Under it `officeOpen` is true at every instant, `nextOpening` is the instant itself, `lastClosing` is null, `autoAnswerDue` is false, `whoHasReply` never answers *nobody* (the tech's hold, then the office's pick, at 3 AM as at 3 PM), `hasHover` and `handOffEvent` never take the closed branch (*Passed from Luis to the office.* with no opening time). The tech's hold still ends at `techUntil`. The days and times stay in the row under the switch.
- **The card's kernel** ([`officeHoursCard.ts`](../../src/lib/messages/officeHoursCard.ts)): `always` on the draft, in `draftFrom`, `sameDraft` and `serializeOfficeHours` (which writes the days and times as the draft holds them, so the switch off puts the old hours back); `draftProblem` skips the day and time checks while it is on; `hoursLine` reads *Open around the clock*; `techHourLine` reads the tech's hour back; the words *The office is open around the clock*, *Whoever has a reply hears at once.* and *The office hears after the reply push wait, at 3 AM as at 3 PM.* The card says nothing about the after-hours text, which has no sender (v2.5709 chose that too).
- **The card** ([`OfficeHoursSettingsCard.tsx`](../../src/components/settings/OfficeHoursSettingsCard.tsx)): the switch row first, the On/Off pill the Reply push card uses (`role="switch"`, green when on); the day toggles and the two time inputs hidden while it is on; the read-back line, the two lines under it while on, and the tech's-hour line for everyone, readers included; **Save the hours** lit only when the draft changed and is good; **Put them back** undoes a press.
- **The switch card on Messages** ([`switchCard.ts`](../../src/lib/messages/switchCard.ts) `switchWhys`, drawn by `SwitchOnCard.tsx`): the Office hours row's why reads *the office is open around the clock* while the switch is on.
- **The edge copy** (`supabase/functions/_shared/gcKernels/lib/messages/hasRule.ts`): regenerated (`npm run gen:edge-kernels`), read by `messages-notify` (the reply push, through `messagesReplyPush.ts`) and `quo-send` (the tech's hold, through `quoTechSend.ts`); `messages-nudge-dispatch` imports the copy too.
- **Tests**: `hasRule.test.ts` (open at 3 AM on a Sunday, the next opening is the instant, no last close, no answer due, never *nobody*, the hover and hand-off words, the old JSON reads as before), `officeHoursCard.test.ts` (the round trip with and without `always`, the switch off restores the kept hours, the new words plain), `OfficeHoursSettingsCard.render.test.tsx` (the switch on hides the fields and saves `always: true` with the kept hours, off restores them, Put them back, a reader sees the lines), `switchCard.test.ts` and `SwitchOnCard.render.test.tsx` (the why), `officeHoursCardIo.test.ts`, `edgeKernels.test.ts`.
- **Guide**: *turn on the reply push* → *Set the office hours* reads the tech's hour; new *Open around the clock* says the switch and what it means.
- **Docs**: `MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md` (the switches table's Office hours row, the `hasRule.ts` line), `GLOSSARY.md` (the R5 line), `SETTINGS_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md` (row 7e, Phone and texts, which gains the card).

## Deploy

Merge; CI deploys the client. Then, before the owner presses the switch: `supabase functions deploy messages-notify`, `supabase functions deploy quo-send` and `supabase functions deploy messages-nudge-dispatch` (`npm run check:edge-drift` names them). An old bundle ignores `always` and reads the kept hours, so a press before the redeploy would change nothing on the edge. The order is safe the other way too: a redeployed function reads `always` as false until the owner presses the switch.

## Is this the best we can do?

1. **Plain words**: the switch says the one true thing, and every reader of the JSON can say it in words instead of inferring 24/7 from a pair of times.
2. **Fewer presses**: one press and a save, where seven days and two times stood.
3. **Reuse**: the On/Off pill is the Reply push card's; the days, the times and the save are v2.5709's, untouched; the kernel keeps one boolean.
4. **Nothing lost**: the days and times wait under the switch, so trying 24/7 for a month costs nothing to undo.
5. **Cut**: no field for the tech's hour or the after-hours number (a line reads the hour back; the fields are a later small PR), and nothing promised about the after-hours text, which has no sender.
