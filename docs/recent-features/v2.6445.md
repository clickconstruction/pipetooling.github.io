# v2.6445 — Charges by crew, PR 3: the Job Summary's Charges and Premium columns, and the Charges window with adjustments (punch list #254) (2026-11-03)

The owner, 2026-11-03: the Job Summary should say what each job is charged at beside what it cost, and *"a dedicated modal where when a user clicks on charges they can see a breakdown of everything and an easy way to adjust."* PR 1 (v2.6441) gave the facts a home; this PR puts them on the ledger and opens the window. The card `to-dos/charges-by-crew.md` and its canvas hold the ask and the artboards (G and H).

## What changed

- **Two columns on the Jobs view** (`JobsJobSummaryTab.tsx`, after `%`): **Charges** and **Premium**, sortable (`charges` · `premium` in `jobSummaryLedgerView.ts`), behind the profit gate, with the footer and every group row summing them (`JobSummaryCutByPanel.tsx`, `JobSummaryTotals.chargesUsd` / `premiumUsd` / `chargedJobs`). A shortfall reads red with the word under it; a cell with something unpriced carries a `?` and the reason in its title; the detail row spans 17 columns.
- **Priced from what the row already holds** (`src/lib/charges/ledgerCharges.ts`, `ledgerChargesFor`): the team labor breakdown's hours by person and work date become crew-days, each person's standing read from `users.charge_standing` by the name key the ledger already bridges with; the row's supply-house, tallied and typed sums take the markup; the job's rule and markup come from its own columns; the open adjustments come from one read for every row (`loadChargeAdjustmentsForJobs`). No new read per row; the rate card and the standings are read once when the tab opens (`Jobs.tsx`), and a read that fails leaves the column unpriced.
- **The chip** (`JobSummaryLedgerToolbar.tsx`, `rateCardChipWords`): *Charged at $285.00 the crew’s hour (master with a helper) · 4 of 7 rows set · materials 25% over cost*, outside the search fold.
- **The Charges window** (`src/components/charges/ChargesWindow.tsx`, over `jobChargesRead.ts` and `jobCharges.ts`): the job's rule and markup at the top, which write to the job (`saveJobChargeRule`); four tiles — labor, materials, charges, against the revenue — each as the record and with the adjustments in hand; the crew-days by week with a tick to keep each day and its shape as a chip, the reason under a changed one; the material lines with a tick and **Pass through**; the adjustments log; **Revert to the record** (a confirm, the saved rows stamped reverted, never deleted) and **Save N adjustments** (`batchWords`-style, dead with nothing pending or a reason missing). A read that fails names its reason with **Try again**. The window reads line by line what the column reads from sums, and the two agree because a line adjustment keeps the line's cost (`job_charge_adjustments.amount`).
- **The table** (migration `20261103080000_job_charge_adjustments.sql`): `job_charge_adjustments` — day_shape · day_off · line_pass_through · line_off, the target, the shape, the amount, the reason, who, when, reverted when — RLS for `has_payroll_access()`, no DELETE, the three fence calls.
- **Money words** (`src/components/charges/Money.tsx` over `moneyParts`): the cents at three quarters the dollars' size on every tile and row of the window; the ledger's own cells already print small cents.
- **Kernels and tests**: `premium.ts` (premium · shortfall · even, the office words), `adjustments.ts` (rows read, indexed, applied, worded), `jobCharges.ts` (a job whole, record beside adjusted), `ledgerCharges.ts`; `crewDays.ts` grew day overrides. Smokes: the window (tiles, a pending chip change and a pass-through saved with reasons as the signed-in user, a failed read, a read-only viewer); the ledger's tests still green with the new fields optional.

## Decisions

- **Charges on the ledger come from sums; the window prices lines.** Dated material lines exist only for expanded rows, so the column uses the row's sums and the adjustments' kept costs, and the window reads the lines. They agree to the cent.
- **Premium is the contract price against the charges**, not the earned share the Revenue column shows mid-job, and it is office-only.
- **Adjustments never touch a session or an invoice**, and reverting stamps, never deletes.
- **Edit Job's fold waits** (PR 4): the window writes the job's rule and markup, which is where the office is when it decides.
