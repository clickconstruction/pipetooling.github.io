# v2.6648 — The lien month across the top of the Lien desk (punch list #235, PR 2 of four) (2026-11-07)

PR 1 (v2.6645) wrote the owner's rhythm down as a rule and a kernel. This PR draws it where the office works: a bar across the top of the Lien desk's Do now, five zones sized by their days, each reading what the desk already knows, one press per zone that narrows the list to that zone's work. The mock-up beside the card (`to-dos/lien-month-rhythm/mockup.html`) drew it four ways; this is the desk's two, a computer and a phone.

## Change

- **`LienMonthBar.tsx`** (new): the bar over `LienMonth`. The head names the month and the day. The grid's columns are the zones' days (`5fr 4fr 4fr 2fr 15fr` in November), a today mark at `todayPct` above it. Each zone wears its label in the desk's own tone (reminders violet, notices blue, affidavits red, catch-up amber, the quiet zone dashed and grey), its days, `lienMonthZoneLine`'s head and sub (*1 to send · 1 sent* / *2 coming · 1 to draft, 1 to approve* / *all 9 sent* with a tick / *1 slipped · 1 sent* red with the first job's name / *9 reminders · 6 notices · 2 liens* for next month), a green progress line while it is the zone now, and `lienMonthZonePress` as its press (*Show 12 reminders ›*). A zone with nothing to open is a plain box. A pressed zone reads `aria-pressed`, its press turns to *Show all ›*, and the head's right end says *Showing N of M · Reminders, days 1 to 5* with **Show all** — or *Nothing on the list is Liens's work right now*. On a phone the zones stack as rows, no today mark, the zone today is in saying *today · 1 to 5*.
- **`LienDeskModal.tsx`**: `serveDue` is its own memo (Do now's record-service rows and the month's copies both read it); `lienMonth` is `buildLienMonth` over the queue's entries, the affidavits, `courtesyByJob` and `serveDue`; `monthZone` state; `nextUpRowsShown` filters `nextUpRowsFound` through `lienMonthZoneHasRow` when a zone is pressed, and the find box counts the shown rows. The Do now view's grid gains a row for the bar above the find box.
- **`lienMonthRhythm.ts`**: a drafting row that keeps the notice that went this month (`sentItem`, v2.6624) counts that send as done beside what is left (one more test).
- **Tests**: `LienMonthBar.render.test.tsx` (the five zones at 1,400 and 375 wide, the today mark, a pressed zone's line and Show all, the slipped reading) and the kernel's.
- **Guide** `send-lien-notices-from-the-lien-desk`: *The lien month*. `docs/GLOSSARY.md` gains the term under *Lien desk*; `docs/LIEN_DESK_ARCHITECTURE.md` carries the note.

## What it never does

Send, file, approve or move anything. The owner's words: an office policy, not an app rule. PR 3 is the Dashboard card; PR 4 moves the courtesy notice's clock onto the rule and finishes the guides.
