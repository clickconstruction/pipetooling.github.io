# v2.2726 — The Bridge, plain: net position history + cash forecast, typed cash, floor (2026-09-03)

Owner feedback on v2.2677: the black line wasn't a position, the projection didn't drop when bills came due, "misses port by $X" had no date, and the target/destination made no sense. Root cause: an accrual profit line described in cash language. Rebuilt around two plain charts (mock-up "The Bridge, Plain" approved).

## Client only — no migration (two new `app_settings` keys, written from the page)

- **Net position** — [`lib/bridge/netPosition.ts`](../../src/lib/bridge/netPosition.ts) (+3 tests): `buildNetPositionHistory` walks backwards from today's cash + AR − AP using dated flows (bank net flow by day, invoices sent, payments received, supply invoices dated/paid); `cashTodayFromAsOf` rolls a typed cash figure forward through bank flows since its as-of day. Payroll/sub payables carried flat in history (stated on the page).
- **Cash forecast** — [`lib/bridge/cashForecast.ts`](../../src/lib/bridge/cashForecast.ts) (+3 tests): cash today, then bills on due dates and receipts on expected dates plus a daily drain; past-dated events land on the first forecast day; returns the lowest point, floor verdict, days under floor. `upcomingFridays` for payroll.
- **Loader** additions: bank flows (both signs, non-internal), invoices sent (test-mode excluded, Chicago-bucketed), `jobs_ledger_payments` by `paid_on`, supply invoices dated/paid, unpaid sub sheets by `payable_after` (14-day default), payroll weekly estimate = 8-week field + office labor ÷ 8, daily drain = office parts ÷ 90, `get_billed_customer_pay_speeds` + `list_job_promised_pay_dates` (fail-soft), settings `bridge_cash_on_hand_v1` (JSON `{usd, asOfYmd}`) and `bridge_cash_floor_usd_v1` (default 5000). `saveBridgeCashOnHand` / `saveBridgeFloor`.
- **Receipts** are built on the page from the finance hook's AR items (already collections-excluded) through the existing `billedExpectedPayModel`: promise → customer median → company median; 45-day default when no history at all.
- **Page**: relabeled without ship words (Daily profit rate · Money owed · Crew hours · Net position · Cash · Bills coming due · Receipts expected · What would change it · Data gaps); destination/target UI removed (the `bridge_target_usd_v1` row is simply unused now); levers listed with sized effects, no checkboxes. [`components/bridge/BridgeCashCharts.tsx`](../../src/components/bridge/BridgeCashCharts.tsx) replaces the course chart (kept in the tree, unused).
- Help guide `the-bridge.md` rewritten.
