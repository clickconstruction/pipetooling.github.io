# v2.6955 — Lien affidavit: a flag when a sworn month has no timely notice behind it (2026-11-11)

Punch list #314. Two papers of the office's first signed batch swore April through August on a house where the first § 53.056 notice was mailed in late September, and the record showed that notice going to the owner alone. Nothing on the desk said so: the affidavit's notice gate asks only whether a notice is recorded on the job.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/jobs/lienAffidavitNoticeCheck.ts`** (pure). For each month the paper swears, `lienAffidavitNoticeCheck` reads the recorded § 53.056 filings that name it: the first day a copy went to the owner and the first day one went to the original contractor, against that month's notice day (`noticeDeadlineForMonth`: the 15th of the second month after the work on a house, the third on any other property, rolled off a weekend). It lists the months whose owner notice went late or is not on record, the months with no contractor copy on record, and the months whose contractor copy went late.
- **`lienAffidavitSwornMonths`** in `lienNoticeDraft.ts`: the months a job's affidavit swears, as keys. The short form's words are now drawn from it, so the check and the paper read one list. `lienWindowSwornMonths` is the Lien window's reading of the same.
- **Where it speaks**: an amber block under the gate list on the Affidavits pane (`data-lien-affidavit-notice-check`) and under the gates on the Lien window's Mechanic's lien tab (`data-lien-window-notice-check`). It opens *Ask the attorney before this is filed.* and then one line for each kind of question.

## A flag, never a hold

The owner approved a hold here. It is built as a flag, for three reasons, and is one line to turn into a gate if he and counsel want that:

1. The desk's standing rule is that a late notice counts while the affidavit's window is open (the notice gate's own label says so). A hold would reverse that rule by a side door.
2. What a late notice, or a missing copy to the contractor, leaves standing is counsel's question, not the app's reading.
3. A gate has no override. A paper stopped by a wrong reading days before its last day to file would cost the lien.

The record can also simply be short: a copy that went to the contractor but was never typed in reads as missing here. Recording it on the § 53.056 tab clears the line.

## Prod, read through the dev seat on 2026-11-11

Of the 11 affidavit jobs under a GC due 2026-11-16, five carry a notice mailed 2026-09-22 with no copy to the original contractor on record, and on those the months before August were past their notice day when it went.
