# v2.3977 — Settings → Contracts & terms: every contract a customer signs, side by side (2026-09-27)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Punch list **#50** ([`to-dos/contracts-and-terms`](../../to-dos/contracts-and-terms/README.md)), PR 1 of 7 — the plan ships in this PR.

## Why

The app offers customers contract wording from Estimates, Bids and Jobs, kept five different ways: one Settings text, per-record boxes, built-in wording in code, the Contract Book, and page state. Nothing listed them, so "what are we sending, and when did it last change" had no place to be asked. *What customers see* answers it by the journey; this answers it by the document.

## What shipped

- **[`customerContractCatalog.ts`](../../src/lib/contracts/customerContractCatalog.ts)** — the registry: 18 entries in two groups (11 *contracts customers accept or sign*, 7 *notices customers receive*). Each names its source (`contract_book` · `app_setting` · `code` · `per_record` · `settings_block`, a Settings form this tab cannot print as one text), its edit door, the *What customers see* steps it shows on, the public routes it is served on, and where the customer's copy is kept. Pure functions resolve the wording (`resolveContractTexts`), the door a role gets (`contractEditAction`), the count line, the compare pick (`toggleCompare`, three at most) and the two guards.
- **[`SettingsContractsTab.tsx`](../../src/components/settings/SettingsContractsTab.tsx)** — `settings-contracts`, Company zone, gate `canSeeWhatCustomersSee`. Mounted while open, so it re-reads on every visit. Reads the `app_settings` keys the catalog names and the `contract_template_documents` rows with `audience = 'customer'`; writes nothing of its own. A Book document opens `StandardTermsEditModal` on its card (`fromSettings`: the reach line names no job). Ticked cards fill **Side by side** at the top of the tab; a bar pinned to the foot of the tab (*Comparing 2 · Read side by side ↑ · Clear*) keeps it in reach from the cards below.
- **Doors, as callbacks** — `onOpenEditor(tabId, anchorId)` switches tab, opens the collapsed editor and scrolls; `onOpenStep(ref)` sets `?tab=settings-what-customers-see&step=<journey>/<step>`. Callbacks and not links, because the rail changes the tab without changing the URL and the same link pressed twice would do nothing.
- **Three anchors** on Bids & materials — `#settings-estimate-public-terms`, `#settings-estimate-cx-defaults`, `#settings-bid-cover-letter-defaults` — in `SETTINGS_HASH_ANCHOR_TO_TAB`; the deep-link effect opens the section. `BidCoverLetterDefaultsSettingsBlock` takes `openSignal`. `#settings-contract-<id>` lands one card.
- **What customers see** reads `?step=` and scrolls to it; an open step lists **The wording on it** (`contractEntriesForStep`), each name opening its card.
- **Search** — four entries in `SETTINGS_SEARCH_INDEX`. **Guide** — `see-every-contract-side-by-side`; one sentence added to `see-what-customers-see`.

## The guards

- `contractCatalogProblems` — every public route the surface registry gives to a homeowner, a GC or an owner has a catalog entry or a reason in `CONTRACT_ROUTE_REASONS` (`/pay/:id`, `/submittal`); every step and route an entry names exists.
- `contractPinProblems` — the catalog repeats seven sentences that live in page and builder source (`EstimateAcceptBody.tsx`, `JobContractSign.tsx`, `BidRoom.tsx`, `coverLetter.ts`, `bidBasis.ts`). The test reads those files; a sentence changed on the page fails CI until it is changed here.

## Found on the way (not changed here)

- The bid room can publish with no Terms and no Exclusions; the sweep sends old wording after a standard-terms edit; a change order signed in the bid room records no consent. Each is its own PR in the train (2, 3, 4).
- `app_settings` has no date column, so five of the texts read *No date is kept*. PR 5–6 give them a history.
- The estimate terms and the job agreement's terms disagree on when interest starts (45 days from the invoice; 30 days after the due date) — in production, read side by side on the tab. The answer is the attorney's.
- The bid room's change-order checkbox sentence is not pinned yet — PR 4 changes that card.
