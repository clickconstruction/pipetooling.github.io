# v2.3563 — Pricing tab decomposition, PR 4: the Price book drawer is its own component (2026-09-17)

Client only, behavior-preserving. PR 4 of the Pricing train in `to-dos/engineering-hygiene.md` — region P4 of [`docs/BIDS_PRICING_LABOR_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md`](../BIDS_PRICING_LABOR_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md), re-mapped 2026-09-17 and measured before the cut: 254 lines, seven state pairs, five props, eight tab functions, no supabase call in the JSX.

## What moved

[`BidsPriceBookDrawer.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/BidsPriceBookDrawer.tsx) — the right-hand `role="dialog"` panel the Workbench opens: the book chips with the ★ on the book feeding the bid and the rename pencil on the browsed one, *Add book*, the explicit **Use <book> on this bid** button with its *Browsing only* caption (v2.2396), the per-person default line, the two frozen-copy captions (the bid's own copy vs a shared book with no copy yet, v2.2444), the Combined / Stage price toggle beside *Add entry* (v2.2386), the search, the entries table in both modes, and the footer line. The derived names (`drawerName`, `defaultName`, `bidBookName`), the write-target read and the two style helpers moved with it, verbatim.

Props are grouped the way the Labor book panel's are: **`books`** (the two version lists, the browsed / current / default template ids, the chip row's expand state, the switch-busy flag, and the browse and Use doors), **`entries`** (the rows, the search, the display mode), **`offer`** (the v2.2444 door across — the pending offer, its busy flag, *apply* and *leave alone*), and **`doors`** (the four P5 form openers and close).

**What deliberately stayed in the tab.** `applyPendingBookOffer` and the offer's planning — a sent bid must not re-price because someone tidied the book, and that rule lives beside the write, so the drawer only renders the amber card and reports the two verbs. The Escape listener and the close-time reset of the offer stay in the tab's effect too: they run whether or not the drawer is mounted. `loadTemplateEntries` and every write path are untouched.

Two book ids stay separate on purpose: `browsedTemplateId` (what the drawer reads) and `currentTemplateId` (what feeds the bid). A chip click only browses; switching the bid is the Use button, as v2.2396 made it.

Numbers: `BidsPricingTab.tsx` 5,422 → 5,205 lines.
