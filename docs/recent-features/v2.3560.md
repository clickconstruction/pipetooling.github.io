# v2.3560 — The Punch list says which rows still need a drawing: waiting on a mock-up · not required (2026-09-17)

Client + tooling + docs. PR 3 of the board's move into the app. No migration.

## Why

The owner wanted before/after drawings for every item and first asked for a ritual to produce them. The better answer was his own second thought: *make the UI show it, and when there is not one, show "waiting on mock-up" or "mock-up not required"*. A state on the row needs no ritual — the gap is visible every time anyone opens the board, and the toggle makes the undrawn work one click.

## What shipped

**Three states, derived** (`mockupState` in `todoBoard.ts`): **has** — any `.html` beside the to-do (the ▣ chips, as before); **waiting** — none, and nothing says one isn't needed; **not required** — the to-do's front matter says `mockup: not required — <why>` (or the row is a standing list). `parseMockupField` reads the field; `renderFrontMatter` writes it back; `BoardItem` carries `mockup` and `mockupNote`.

**The board**: a row with no drawing shows an amber dashed **▢ waiting on a mock-up** chip at the head of its links line, or a quiet **— mock-up not required — <why>**; a **Waiting on a mock-up** toggle beside the Show filters narrows every group to those rows and carries the count (`waitingOnMockupCount`, `rowVisible`'s fourth argument). The README index's Links cell carries the same words in italics.

**Seven to-dos marked not required** in this PR, each with its reason: the two live tests, the Stages/Pricing decomposition, the per-GC column retirement, person identity phase E, the Pipeline load-speed pass, the Customer Waiting `tel:` sweep. Today's board: 13 have a drawing, 10 need none (7 + the 3 standing lists), **17 wait** — 4 of the 12 Ready rows, all 12 Gated rows, and one Waiting row.

Docs: the field in `to-dos/README.md`'s front-matter example and in step 2 of the add / change / retire recipe; the help guide's row.
