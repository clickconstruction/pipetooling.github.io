# v2.7266 — Lien affidavit: the paper says why its months line is empty (2026-11-14)

Punch list #356 part 1 ([`to-dos/affidavit-months-line-notice-misses.md`](../../to-dos/affidavit-months-line-notice-misses.md)). Client and the dev seat: `supabase/functions/_shared/devMcpAffidavitDraft.ts` and the copied kernels under `_shared/gcKernels/` change, so `dev-mcp` is redeployed after the merge. No migration.

## Before

`lienAffidavitSwornMonths` cuts the months the recorded § 53.056 notices name to the job's own work span (`lienWorkSpan`). On a job with no clock hours and a last day set by hand, the span is that day's month alone, so a notice recorded by hand for several jobs at a property that names earlier months leaves nothing: the short form printed a blank where its months go, and the *Ask the attorney before this is filed* line (`lienAffidavitNoticeCheck`, punch list #314) stayed silent, because it checks only the months the paper names. Two drafts saved on 2026-11-14 printed that blank.

## What changed

- **[`src/lib/jobs/lienAffidavitNoticeCheck.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienAffidavitNoticeCheck.ts)**: the check takes `work`, the job's own work months, and answers `empty: { work, named }` when the paper swears no month while a live notice names some. `lienNoticeCheckLines` opens with three sentences (`lienNoticeCheckEmptyLines`): *No recorded notice names a month this job was worked.* *Its work was in September 2026.* *The notice on record names April, June, July and August 2026.* `lienNoticeCheckFlags` is true for it, `lienNoticeCheckRowLine` (the batch sheet's row) says the first sentence, and `lienNoticeCheckShortLines` (the notice record's box) reads the two lists as spans. Not raised when the paper names a month, when no notice is on record (the affidavit's own gate holds then), on a job contracted with the owner, or when the caller passes no work months.
- **`lienAffidavitWorkMonths`** in [`lienAffidavitNotices.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienAffidavitNotices.ts) is the span's months, from the same span the sworn months are cut to, over the new `lienWorkSpanMonths` in `lienWorkSpan.ts`; `lienWindowWorkMonths` in `lienWindowAffidavit.ts` is the Lien window's reading of it.
- The five readers pass the work months: the desk's affidavit pane (`LienDeskAffidavitPane`), the batch help sheet's rows (`LienDesk.tsx`), the Lien window's affidavit tab (`LienFilingTabs`), the notice record (`lienNoticeRecord`, whose *No notice.* line already said it on its own table and whose flags now agree) and the dev seat's `plan_affidavit_draft`, whose `no_month` carries the same sentences before *The paper prints a blank where its months go.*
- The guide *file a lien and never miss its deadlines* says when the line speaks.

## Not changed

- No hold, no gate, no change to the paper's words. Part 2 of the card, months set by hand with counsel's reason (the owner's door 1 of 2026-11-14), is its own PR.
- The Lien window's older amber line, *No recorded notice names 2026-09 (the last work month)*, stays beside the new one; it speaks of the last month alone.
