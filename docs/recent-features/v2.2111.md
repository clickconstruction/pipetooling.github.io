# v2.2111 — Partner ledger week cards derive from the journal (2026-08-22)

Owner bug report: the "Your ledger" week cards didn't reconcile. Bryan's live week showed `Week opened +$967.60` → *nothing posted* → `you owe Click $1,008.13` — a $1,975.73 jump with no lines. Three defects, one root cause: **the week cards were built from a different data model than Full ledger.**

**Root cause.** `buildWeekCards` consumed statement stubs only and ran the opening/closing chain *backwards* from `summary.balance`, which the server (`get_my_partner_summary`) defines as posted stubs + additions − deductions − payouts. Charge offsets with `pay_stub_id IS NULL` (back-charges, personal card charges, damages) are **not** in that balance — they were bolted onto the headline via `netPosition(closing, pending_offsets.net)` only on the live card. So: (a) the ±$1,975.73 of unattached charges was in the total but had no line anywhere, (b) openings were derived rather than observed and drifted from the Full-ledger chain, (c) `p_weeks: 8` capped history at 8 statements even though `partner_ledger_payload` allows 520.

**Fix.** New `buildJournalWeekCards(summary, stubs, offsets)` in [`partnerWeeks.ts`](../../src/lib/partnerLedger/partnerWeeks.ts) is a weekly **view over the same journal Full ledger uses** (`partnerStubsToJournal` → charges-at-date). Journal rows group by company week via new pure helper `weekStartYmd` (Sunday-start, UTC date math, garbage passes through); each card's `closing` is literally the last row's running balance, and `opening` chains **forward from $0** at the start of history — so every card's opening equals the previous card's closing by construction, and both match Full ledger to the penny. Labor rows expand into the stub's stamped rate tiers (zero-hour tiers still skipped, v2.2100); payouts book in the week they were *paid*, not the stub's week; weeks with charges but no statement now render as their own card. The live card = journal rows already dated this week + the so-far hour lines, closing = posted chain + `gross_so_far` (no `netPosition` wedge — charges are in the chain now).

[`DashboardPartnerLedgerSection.tsx`](../../src/components/dashboard/DashboardPartnerLedgerSection.tsx): one `p_weeks: 520` fetch now feeds **both** the cards and Full ledger (previously cards fetched 8 weeks eagerly and Full ledger re-fetched 520 lazily) — the two views are structurally incapable of disagreeing, and the lazy loading state is gone. Print statement uses new `signedBalance` so a negative opening/closing prints with its minus sign. `buildWeekCards` is deleted; `netPosition` remains for `PartnershipLedgerTab`.

Verified live in partner view on Bryan's real prod data: live week opens −$1,008.13 → closes $1,008.13 "you owe Click"; every ‹ Older step's opening equals the prior closing (…−$1,295.09 / −$641.59 / −$1,570.09 / $3.85 / $60.25…); the 4/12 week shows the −$1,238.65 car-repair charge and both payouts as lines; history walks back to the true first week (2026-03-22, opening $0.00) where ‹ Older correctly disables. Kernel tests rewritten (18 in `partnerWeeks.test.ts`, incl. a Bryan-shaped case: payout in a later week than its stub, back-charge in a statement-less week). Client-only — no migration.
