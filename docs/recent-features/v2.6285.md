# v2.6285 — Days on the job: the range is one quiet menu, and an empty card says what fills it (2026-11-01)

The owner, on the job window's History tab for a job with no clock-ins: "I do not like how the text
'Whole job / Last 90d / Last 365d / Dates…' looks against everything else." The four range pills were
drawn at the tab strip's own size and blue, so a footnote card read as a second tab bar, the legend
drew under an empty card, and "No days worked in this range." named nothing to do.

- **One word, `Whole job ▾`** (`JobHistoryCrewCalendar.tsx` → `RangeMenu`): a native `<select>` styled
  quiet on the card's label line, at the label's size. Choices: Whole job · Last 90 days · Last 365
  days · Pick dates… (`crewRangeWords`). A phone opens its own picker; nothing wraps. Pick dates… still
  shows the From / To inputs under the line.
- **The legend draws only under a calendar** (`data-crew-legend`, gated on `calendar.months.length`).
- **An empty range is an `EmptyState`** with words from the kernel (`crewEmptyWords`): whole job → *No
  days on this job yet. The first clock-in on this job starts the calendar.*; last 90 / 365 days → *No
  days worked in the last 90 days. Whole job shows every day worked.* with Whole job a press back to the
  whole job; Pick dates → *No days worked between these dates. Whole job shows every day worked.*
  `crewSummaryWords` now returns '' for an empty range instead of the old sentence.
- The alternative shown to the owner, a small segmented strip at the label's size, was not chosen: it
  shows every choice at a glance but costs a row on a phone and stays the one loud thing in an empty card.
- The guide *job-window-tabs* describes the menu in plain words.

Verified on the dev server: the real job window for 178 PLUM renders the empty card; a fixture page
rendered empty, three days and a year of days at phone and desktop width, light and dark.
