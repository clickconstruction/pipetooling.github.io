# v2.8499 — An update never reloads the app over words someone typed (2026-11-24)

Punch list #85. The gap v2.8471 named and left (`docs/recent-features/v2.8471.md`, *What is left*: *The update's own reload does not read typed words yet*). Diane said on 2026-11-24 that the update's own reload should hold for typed words. Her rule is the one v2.8471 was built on: words someone typed stay until they delete them or press send. Client only: no migration and no edge function.

## What was wrong

The app reloads itself onto a new build at a quiet moment (`src/lib/autoReload.ts`, v2.3740): first paint before any touch, a move to another page or tab, or idle, which is a tab hidden five minutes or untouched half an hour. Four things held it: a cursor in a field, an open window, unsaved work a component declared (`useHoldsUnsavedWork`) and a write in flight.

Words typed into a box and then left were none of the four. The cursor is elsewhere, no window is open, and only a handful of forms declare a hold. So a message typed, the phone put down, and five minutes later the tab reloaded under it. With several deploys a day there is nearly always an update waiting.

## Now

Both of the update reload's looks read `pageHoldsTypedText()` (`src/lib/typedText.ts`, v2.8471), the check the recovery reload and the window cure already read.

| The look | With typed words on the page |
|---|---|
| `decideAutoReload`, at each quiet moment | no reload, at first paint, on a move and when idle. It is the first hold asked, before unsaved work, writes, a cursor or a window, so it is the reason named. |
| `decideReloadLanding`, when a decided reload would fire | stands down to the pill. It does not wait and then reload, however long the decision has waited. |

Nothing counts time or tries. A tab hidden two weeks over a typed message has not reloaded.

The second look matters for a draft the app brings back by itself. A page can be judged quiet at first paint, and its draft lands a moment later with no touch to stand the reload down.

When the words are sent or cleared, the next quiet moment reloads as before: the next move, the next minute check of an untouched tab, or the tab's next return from five minutes hidden.

What counts as typed words is v2.8471's rule, unchanged: a `textarea` or a box edited in place with words in it, whoever put them there, and a one-line field the person typed into. A search box, a field the app filled, a read-only box and spaces alone do not count.

## The pill's own Reload

**Reload** on the pill is the person's press, and it still reloads at once over typed words (`asked` in `decideReloadLanding`). The guide says to send or clear them first.

This change makes that press matter more. While typed words hold the update, the pill is what the person sees, so the pill now shows most often on exactly the pages that hold words. Whether that press should say so first is the owner's call, and it is not built here.

## Not proved

- The live site. This is a built app served from this machine, against the same server.
- A real deploy. The new version was the same build with one more line on its service worker file, which the browser treats as a new worker.
- A real report or message box in the app: the note box was the test's own. A real one may save a draft as it is typed, and the look writes nothing.
- A tab the browser itself hid. The page was told it was hidden; the browser kept it in front, so its timers were not slowed.
- Chromium only.

## What is left

- **The pill's Reload over typed words**, above.
- **A page that shows a saved note in a box holds the update while it is open.** The check cannot tell a saved note from a draft, so it holds for both. The pill shows there, and the next move to a page without one reloads.
- **A press a person makes is theirs**, as in v2.8471: **Reload** on a page that could not load and the gear menu's **Hard Reload** reload over typed words too.
