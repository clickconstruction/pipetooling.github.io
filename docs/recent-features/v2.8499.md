# v2.8499 — An update never reloads the app over words someone typed (2026-11-24)

Punch list #85. The gap v2.8471 named and left (`docs/recent-features/v2.8471.md`, *What is left*: *The update's own reload does not read typed words yet*). Diane said on 2026-11-24 that the update's own reload should hold for typed words. Her rule is the one v2.8471 was built on: words someone typed stay until they delete them or press send. Client only: no migration and no edge function.

## What was wrong

The app reloads itself onto a new build at a quiet moment (`src/lib/autoReload.ts`, v2.3740): first paint before any touch, a move to another page or tab, or idle, which is a tab hidden five minutes or untouched half an hour. Four things held it: a cursor in a field, an open window, unsaved work a component declared (`useHoldsUnsavedWork`) and a write in flight.

Words typed into a box and then left were none of the four. The cursor is elsewhere, no window is open, and only a handful of forms declare a hold. So a message typed, the phone put down, and five minutes later the tab reloaded under it. With several deploys a day there is nearly always an update waiting.

## Now

Both of the update reload's looks read `pageHoldsTypedText()` (`src/lib/typedText.ts`, v2.8471), the check the recovery reload and the window cure already read.

| The look | With typed words on the page |
|---|---|
| `decideAutoReload`, at each quiet moment | no reload, at first paint, on a move and when idle. It is the first hold asked, before unsaved work, writes, a cursor or a window, so it is the reason named. |
| `decideReloadLanding`, when a decided reload would fire | stands down to the pill. It does not wait and then reload, however long the decision has waited. |

Nothing counts time or tries. A tab hidden two weeks over a typed message has not reloaded.

The second look matters for a draft the app brings back by itself. A page can be judged quiet at first paint, and its draft lands a moment later with no touch to stand the reload down.

When the words are sent or cleared, the next quiet moment reloads as before: the next move, the next minute check of an untouched tab, or the tab's next return from five minutes hidden.

What counts as typed words is v2.8471's rule, unchanged: a `textarea` or a box edited in place with words in it, whoever put them there, and a one-line field the person typed into. A search box, a field the app filled, a read-only box and spaces alone do not count.

## The pill's Reload warns first, and shows each place

Diane, 2026-11-24: *The pill's Reload should warn first when words are typed. But when a user hits the reload button, it should take the user to the areas where the words are typed on the screen before the user hits confirm. These areas show up highlighted for a second, and if there are several, it takes the user between them on their page.*

The hold above makes this press matter more. While typed words hold the update, the pill is what the person sees, so the pill now shows most often on exactly the pages that hold words. Its **Reload** emptied them at once.

**The press** (`src/lib/updatePillWarn.ts`). With nothing typed, **Reload** reloads as before. With typed words on the page it reloads nothing:

| The pill | Its line | Its first press |
|---|---|---|
| ready | *A new version is ready.* | **Reload** |
| showing | *Reloading clears what you typed.* | **Reload anyway**, dead |
| shown | *Reloading clears what you typed.* | **Reload anyway**, live |

**Not now** is there throughout. It keeps the words and puts the pill away for its ten minutes.

**The walk** (`src/lib/typedTextTour.ts`). `typedTextFields()` names the places that hold typed words, top of the page first. The walk brings the first one not yet shown to the middle of the screen and rings it in the pill's orange. It waits for the page to stop moving, rings every other typed place that can be seen with it, and holds the rings a second. Then it goes on to the next place not yet shown.

- **A stop is a screenful.** A form with six short fields is one stop. A note at the top of a long page and a message at its foot are two.
- **The second counts from when the place has landed.** A smooth scroll down a long list takes over a second.
- **The ring is an attribute**, `data-typed-words-shown`, drawn by one rule in `index.css`. It sits inside the box, so a parent that clips its children cannot hide it. The walk changes no field's words, focus or style.
- **A place that cannot be brought to the screen** (a field in a folded section) is passed over. The warning still shows.
- With less motion asked for (`prefers-reduced-motion`), the page jumps to each place.

**Reload anyway** is dead until every place has been shown, and never for less than a second. It is dead by `aria-disabled`, so the press keeps its focus and a keyboard's next key still lands on the pill.

**A touch anywhere off the pill** ends the warning: the rings lift and the pill reads **Reload** again. The person went back to their words, and the next press on **Reload** looks at the page afresh.

The warned pill's presses are 44 tall. **Not now** is the press that keeps the words, and a thumb must find it.

### What the built app showed, and what it changed

Three faults showed only in a browser, and each is mended and held by a test.

- **The ring lifted as the place arrived.** The first walk waited a fixed 450 ms for the scroll. Bills sent is 51,000 pixels long at a desk and 70,000 on a phone, and the scroll took 1.4 seconds. The walk now reads the place's position until it stops moving.
- **A double press landed on Not now.** The warned pill is laid out afresh, and on a phone the second press of a double press on **Reload** fell on **Not now** and put the pill away. A press on the pill inside 400 ms of the warning is ignored (`updatePillIgnoresPress`).
- **The pill sat on the words.** The last box on a page cannot be brought to the middle, and a message box is usually at the foot, where the pill is. When a place being shown is in the foot of the screen, the pill goes to the top (`updatePillSide`), measured once the place has landed.

The warned pill was also fitted to half the screen (a box fixed at `left: 50%`), three rows deep on a phone. It is `max-content` wide now: one line and one row of presses.

## Seen in the built app

Twice on 2026-11-24, each with a headless Chromium 149 at 1,280 by 800 and at 390 by 844, signed in with a session from the dev-login account, with the build's own service worker in control. The build's `dist` was served locally. A new version was the same build with one more line on its service worker file: the browser found a new worker, installed it and left it waiting, and the pill showed. Read only: nothing in the app was pressed but its own navigation and the pill. The note boxes were the test's own, typed into by the keyboard.

**The hold** (the first commit, built with `npm run build`, its `tsc -b` included).

- **A quiet page, the tab hidden.** It reloaded by itself at 302 seconds, onto the new worker. This is the control.
- **Words on the page, the tab hidden 348 seconds, then looked at again.** Nothing reloaded, hidden or on return. The same document, the words still in their box, the pill showing.
- **The words cleared, then a move to another page.** It reloaded by itself.

**The pill** (the whole change, built with `vite build`). Words in two boxes, one above the page and one below its foot, on Bills sent.

- **A move to another page with the words still there** reloaded nothing in ten seconds. No automatic reload had run in that tab, so the one-minute loop guard was not what held it.
- **Reload pressed, and pressed again 120 ms later on the same spot.** Nothing reloaded. The pill read *Reloading clears what you typed.* with **Reload anyway** dead. The top box was ringed at once (`3px solid rgb(249, 115, 22)`) for 0.9 seconds. Then the foot box was ringed and the page went to it. It landed at 2.3 seconds, the pill went to the top of the screen at 2.6, and the ring lifted at 3.6, when **Reload anyway** came alive. The pill was still there: the second press had been ignored.
- **A touch off the pill.** *A new version is ready.* with **Reload** again. Both boxes still held their words.
- **Reload again, the walk, then Reload anyway.** The app reloaded onto the new worker.
- **One box, Reload, then Not now.** One stop of a second, then the pill went. The same document, the words still in their box.

The warned pill is 466 by 93 at the desk and 358 by 93 on the phone, 16 from each edge. Its presses are 44 tall. Nothing was wider than the screen, and the page threw no error.

## Not proved

- The live site. This is a built app served from this machine, against the same server.
- A real deploy. The new version was the same build with one more line on its service worker file.
- A real report or message box in the app: the note boxes were the test's own. A real one may save a draft as it is typed, and the look writes nothing.
- A tab the browser itself hid. The page was told it was hidden and the browser kept it in front, so its timers were not slowed.
- A place inside an open window, or inside a list that scrolls by itself. The walk uses the browser's own `scrollIntoView`, which moves those too, and it was not looked at.
- The walk with less motion asked for, and the pill by keyboard alone.
- Dark. The looks were in light.
- Chromium only.

## What is left

- **A page that shows a saved note in a box holds the update while it is open**, and the pill's **Reload** warns over it. The check cannot tell a saved note from a draft, so it treats both as words. The next move to a page without one reloads.
- **The other presses a person makes are theirs**, as in v2.8471: **Reload** on a page that could not load and the gear menu's **Hard Reload** reload over typed words, with no warning. Mended in v2.8513: both ask first and walk the page through each place.
- **The ready pill's presses are 30 tall**, as before. Only the warned pill's were made 44.
- **Two typed places at the top and the foot of one screen**: the pill stays at the foot and may cover the lower one.
