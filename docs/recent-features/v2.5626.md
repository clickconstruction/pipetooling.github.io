# v2.5626 — The customer's door on the Pipeline row (2026-10-18)

Punch list #123 (the to-do and its three mock-ups, PR #5847). The owner asked whether the Pipeline makes it easy to message a job's customer, read the three mock-ups, pressed on *is this the best we can do*, and said build the two better shapes: the customer's name as the door, and the stage move offering the text. This PR is the first. Building it found that the row already carried a door the mock-ups had missed: the speech bubble beside the customer's name (residential R2, v2.5451, `MessagesButton.tsx`), which leaves the board for the Messages page. So the name keeps its own press (the customer's record), and the bubble becomes the door: it opens the customer's sheet over the board. No migration; no edge function.

- **The sheet** (`src/components/jobs/JobCustomerSheet.tsx`, a `ResponsiveModalShell` at 480 px, a full-screen sheet on a phone): the customer's name as the title, the job (*#2148 · Helotes re-pipe*) and the number and email on file under it, whose word came last on the job, and the rows **Call** (a `tel:` link with the number), **Text**, **Email**, **Open their thread** (`contact_for_customer`, then the Messages page at `?thread=`, the sheet closing first) and **Their record** (the customer profile window, `useCustomerProfileModal`, the row left off where the page has none). Text and Email swap the rows for the job window's own box (`JobCustomerBox`, R2's, in the customer's orange, tied to the job) under a Text · Email pair and **‹ Back**; a send asks the board to read the job again, so the activity line shows it.
- **The kernel** (`src/lib/messages/customerDoor.ts`): `customerDoorRows` from the facts the row already holds (`customer_phone`, `customer_email`; no mobile greys Call and Text with *No mobile on file. Email instead.*, no email greys Email the same way, the words shorter when both are missing); `lastWordOnJob` over the job's own lines (`loadJobMessages`, J2's read: the newest call, voicemail, text, email or portal line in or out; notes, events and team lines are ours alone) and `lastWordWords` (*Waiting on us: Dana wrote last, yesterday.* / *You wrote last, today at 8:44 AM.*, `whenWords`). A read that fails leaves the line off.
- **The door**: `MessagesButton` takes `onPressInstead`, and the two customer-line renderers in `jobsStagesRowShared.tsx` (the desktop rows' and the cards') pass the row context's new `onOpenCustomerDoor(job)`; `JobsStagesTab` holds the open job and draws the sheet beside its other windows, and the three tables carry the prop as they carry `onOpenJobContract`. Where no door is given (the job window's header, Customers, Estimates) the bubble keeps its own press to the Messages page. The bubble's gate is the sheet's: `canOpenMessages`, the office team; a tech's row has no bubble and nothing changes for them.
- **Tests**: `customerDoor.test.ts` (the rows by what is on file, the newest word and its side, notes ignored, the words); `JobCustomerSheet.render.test.tsx` (the title, the job line, the waiting line, every row, Text greyed with its reason, the box on Text with Back, the thread and record doors); `jobsStagesRowShared.customerDoor.render.test.tsx` (the bubble opens the door from both lines and reads nothing; with no door it keeps its press).
- **Docs**: the guide *text a customer from the job* gains **From the Pipeline row**; the Glossary's R2 line, the page map's doors paragraph and `MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md`'s surfaces table carry the sheet.

Next: the second shape, the stage move's **Tell the customer** offer through `updateJobStatus` with its Flows step; then #123 closes.
