# v2.4730 — Contracts: interest starts 45 days from the invoice date on every text (2026-10-06)

Punch list #50 (`to-dos/contracts-and-terms/README.md`): the one question the tab's first read left for the attorney. The estimate terms and the bid terms (`websiteTerms.ts`, Texas Property Code ch. 28) charge interest on an invoice unpaid 45 days from the invoice date; the job service agreement charged it 30 days after the due date. The owner's answer, 2026-10-06: *make them all 45 days*.

## What changed

- **The built-in agreement terms** (`DEFAULT_JOB_CONTRACT_TERMS_PLAIN` in [`jobContractDocument.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobContractDocument.ts)), used when the Contract Book holds no customer document, now read in clause 3: *Balances unpaid 45 days from the invoice date accrue interest at the lesser of 1.5% per month or the maximum allowed by law, plus reasonable costs of collection.*
- **The office's Service agreement in the Contract Book** (Settings → Contracts & terms, the customer document dated 2026-09-29) said *A balance not paid within 30 days after it is due bears interest…*. It was edited through the tab's own Edit door to *A balance not paid within 45 days of the invoice date bears interest…*; the Book's history keeps the earlier version. New agreements print the new wording. An unsent draft goes out with the current terms (v2.3965); an agreement already signed keeps the words its customer agreed to.
- The seed migration `20260920190000_seed_customer_standard_terms.sql` carries the same sentence, because `jobContractTermsSeed.test.ts` holds it equal to the built-in text. It is already applied on prod, where the Book document is the text the app prints; the amended seed matters only to a fresh database, which now seeds the 45-day clause.
