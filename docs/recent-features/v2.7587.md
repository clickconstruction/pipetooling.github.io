# v2.7587 — Checklist: Forward opens on Pick a person (2026-11-17)

The step v2.7563 left open. That release gave My Inbox its own Forward window, `TaskForwardWindow`, and left the Checklist's two Forward windows drawn in the page.

## Why

A look at the live site on 2026-11-17 showed the Checklist's window with a name in **Assign to**, a blue **Forward** press, and the words *Required: Assignee* in red beside it. The list had no placeholder row, so the browser showed the first person while the page held no pick. Review's window opened on the person who already had the task, and a press there asked the database to hand the task to the same person.

## What changed

- **Today's and Review's Forward draw `TaskForwardWindow`**, the window My Inbox draws. It opens on *Pick a person*. The press is dead until a name is picked, then reads **Forward to** that name.
- **Review's list leaves out the person the task is with** (`forwardTakers(users, fwdFromUserId)`). The office may still take the task themselves. Today's list leaves out the viewer, as My Inbox's does.
- **A refusal stays in the window in the database's words.** Both pages wrote it to the page's error line behind the window until now.
- The *Required:* line is gone from both, with `fwdTitle`, `fwdAssigneeId`, `fwdMissingFields` and `fwdCanSubmit`. The window holds its own pick. `Checklist.tsx` is 155 lines shorter.

No database change, and the call is the same: `handOnTask` → `task_hand_on`, then `tellForwardTaker`.

## Checked

- `Checklist.todayForward.render.test.tsx` (3, new): on the page as an office role, **Forward** opens the window with nobody picked, a dead press, the viewer left out and no *Required* words; a picked name is on the press and the press calls `task_hand_on` with that person; a refusal stays in the window.
- `TaskForwardWindow.render.test.tsx` (5) and the page's two other render tests still pass.
- Both windows seen drawn in a local run of the branch, as a dev, then cancelled: each opens on *Pick a person* with a dead press and no *Required* words. Today's list leaves out the viewer. Review's leaves out the person the task is with and offers the viewer.
