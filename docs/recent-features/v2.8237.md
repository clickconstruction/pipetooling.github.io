# v2.8237 — Dispatch: a request on a ZZ test job rings no phone (2026-11-21)

Punch list #382, finding 6 of the audit of PR 10 (v2.8110), on Taunya's go of 2026-11-21. One edge function, `notify-dispatch-request`, and one shared kernel. No migration, no client change. **The function is redeployed only on a go that names it.**

## The fault: a walk could ring the office

A No on arrival reaches Dispatch as a request, with a push to every member of the Dispatch group. The audit could not press a No on production, because nothing held a ZZ test job out of that push.

On a closer read the gap was the whole inbox's and not this feature's. `notify-dispatch-request` pushed the group for every new request, whatever job it named: a stage check, a request from a phone, a portal request, a No on arrival. The Needs you line, the Pipeline and a saved report's two sends all hold a ZZ test record back. This push did not.

## The fix

`_shared/reportSubject.ts` gains `dispatchRequestPushSkip(db, request)`. It reads the request's job or bid by name with the read the report sends use, and answers with the same reason, `zz_test_job`, when the name is a ZZ name by the one rule (`isZzTestJob`, or a ZZ project name on a bid).

`notify-dispatch-request` asks it before the fan-out to the group. On a ZZ record it sends nothing and answers `skipped: 'zz_test_job'` with `push_sent: 0`.

Two things are left as they were:

- **The line stays in the inbox.** A walk reads its own request there, and removes it after.
- **The requester's own pushes still go**: *Dispatch answered*, *reopened* and *replied*. They reach the person who filed the request, who on a ZZ job is the walker.

A request on no job and no bid, such as the morning license sweep's, is read as no subject and rings as before.

## Checked

`reportSubject.test.ts` has three new tests: a ZZ job is held by one read of the job, a ZZ bid is held, and a real job and a request on nothing both ring. `deno check` on the function is CI's edge-bundle step. Not checked on production: the function is not deployed yet.
