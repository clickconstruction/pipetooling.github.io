# v2.6835 — Permits: Kept, Pursuing and Closed, a hand-off, builders with a mark, and the count (punch list #302, PR 6 to 8) (2026-11-10)

The rest of the first build of `to-dos/permit-skim/`: where an answered permit sits, who it is for, how a kept one is closed out, the builder behind a name with our mark, and the count that says whether the skim turns into work. One migration, tested on the local bed before the push.

## What changed

- **Migration `20261110090000_permit_skim.sql`** (`docs/migrations/20261110090000_permit_skim.md`): `permits.builder_key`, `builders`, `builder_marks`, three columns and a fifth verdict (`back`) on `permit_verdicts`, `research_permits` reading a put-back as unanswered, and the reads `research_permits_v2`, `research_builders`, `permit_funnel`, `permit_skim_wait`. Bed `supabase/tests/permit_skim/` (`npm run test:pg:permit-skim`, in `scripts/sql-beds.txt`).
- **`supabase/functions/permit-pull`** over `_shared/permitFeed.ts` and `_shared/permitLookup.ts` (`builderKeyOf`): every row it upserts carries its builder key, and after the feeds it fills the key on building permits already held, 500 a round, read by id so a name that keys to nothing is passed once. Deployed ahead of the migration it drops the column from the upsert and carries on.
- **`src/lib/research/permitBuilders.ts`** (new, pure, `permitBuilders.test.ts`): `placeOf` (Skim, Kept, Pursuing, Closed, and why a permit is closed; a plumbing permit on the feed closes a kept permit, never a pursued one), `placeCounts`, `keptRows` (the ones to look at again today first, then the longest kept; Mine, Nobody's yet), `closedRows` with its search, the five Not pursuing reasons and their `np_` codes, what a pursue is for, the builder's name, kinds and chips (`neverBid`: three or more permits, never priced, not a name that files for others), the mark's hold, the card's builder line, the nudge's words and the count's tiles and lines.
- **`permitResearchIo.ts`**: `loadResearchPermits` reads v2 and falls back to the older read while the function is missing; `recordVerdict` takes a reason code, a person and a purpose, named only when set; `putBackPermit`, `loadPermitHistory`, `loadResearchers`, `loadBuilders`, `markBuilder`, `saveBuilder`, `loadPermitsOfBuilder`, `loadPermitFunnel`.
- **`ProspectsResearchTab`**: the step strip (Skim, Kept, Pursuing, Closed with their counts; Builders; Ledger and Map), one read of every permit shared by the deck, the strip and the lists, patched in place when an answer is written.
- **`PermitSkimDeck`**: the Who line shows the builder's mark with its reason and how busy the builder is; a name that files for others says so; the bar after a keep reads *Kept. For?* with the team's names; a keep and a pass carry who and why.
- **`PermitPlaceList`** (new): Kept (chips, Hand to, Put back, the strip that says a kept permit got a plumber), Pursuing (who, what for, the lead), Closed (Passed, Has a plumber, Not pursuing, a search, Put back).
- **`PermitBuildingSheet`**: opened from Kept it shows What we did and takes **Pursue…** and **Not pursuing…** at its foot. **`PermitCloseOutSheet`** (new) is the two taps of a pursue and the one tap of a drop.
- **`PermitBuilders`** (new): the Builders list and a builder's sheet (the mark with its reason, the kind, our bids, the plumber they use, their permits, a Google press).
- **`PermitCountStrip`** (new): the month's six numbers and who skimmed, why permits were passed and what the pursues are for.
- The answer bar and the active step used `var(--text)` as a fill, a token that does not exist, so they drew with no background; both fill with `var(--text-strong)` now. Found on the live look.
- Guide `research-permits-for-work-to-bid`, `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` (permit-pull), `docs/ACCESS_CONTROL.md`, `docs/PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`.
