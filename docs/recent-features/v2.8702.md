**Read.** The portal draws its presses with `Btn` of `src/components/gc/gcUi.tsx`, which pads 0.35rem over 0.85rem words and knows nothing of the screen's width, so a press was about 30 px at 375 px as at a desk. 134 files draw `Btn`, most of them the office's GC pages. The portal also draws about a dozen buttons of its own, from 24 px (the reasons under **We will be late**) to 40, and links drawn as words, 15 to 22 px tall: **Call**, **Text**, **Email**, **Directions ↗**, **Open ↗**.

**The mend.** `Btn` is not changed, so its other uses cannot move.

- `src/lib/phonePress.ts` gains `phoneThumbPressesCss(shell)`: one style rule, for a screen of 640 px and under, that names a shell on every selector.
  - A button in the shell stands 44 px tall. The rule is marked important, since several of the portal's buttons set a smaller height in their own style.
  - A button in a line of words carries `data-press-in-line`. It grows the same and its margin gives the growth back, as `thumbInLine` does. The thread's *more* press is the one such button.
  - A link is drawn as it was. Fifteen pixels of padding over and under it grow what a finger can hit to 44 px or more, and the same margin taken back keeps a block link where it stood. A link in a line of words takes no room for its padding, so no line moves.
- `GcTradePortalView.tsx`, the portal's shell, carries the mark `data-trade-portal` and draws the rule once.
