# v2.7384 — Job notes: the groundwork for tagging a teammate (2026-11-15)

Punch list #363, PR 1 of six: the kernel. Taunya asked on 2026-11-15 to tag a teammate with `@name` in a job note and have it reach that person, and settled the card's eight calls the same day. Client only, and nothing imports it yet: no screen, no migration, no edge function.

## What is new

- **`src/lib/jobs/noteTags.ts`** (pure, new), with 44 tests in `noteTags.test.ts`:
  - `tagQueryAtCaret` finds the `@` being typed at the caret. The `@` must open the note or follow a space, and only letters may follow it. `arrive @ 8:45`, `materials @$300` and an email address open no list.
  - `rankTaggable` builds the list under the box: the person who asked first (an answer box), then the job's crew, then the office, each by name. It matches the start of any word of a name, without regard to case or accents, and leaves out people already picked and the writer.
  - `insertPick` puts `@Full Name ` in the words where the `@` was being typed, or at the caret when the @ button is pressed cold. `removePick` takes one back out.
  - `mentionIndex` and `liveTags` say which picks are still in the words, so a name the writer deleted is no tag. `@Chris Bell` is not found inside `@Chris Bellamy`.
  - `noteBodyParts` cuts a posted note into plain words and name chips from the names its tag rows carry.
  - `waitingOnWords` writes the grey line under a note, `waiting on Dana`, with the name whole when two people share a first name.
  - `workingDaysWaited`, `tagIsLate` and `tagAgeWords` hold the age rule. `oldestFirst` and `sectionSplit` order the section and hold it to three shown.

## Rules it settles

- **A tag is a pick, never a parse** (call 4). The kernel only ever looks for `@` plus a full name it was handed. It never guesses a person from a typed word.
- **Amber after two working days** (call 3) counts each Monday to Friday after the day the tag was posted, up to and counting today. A tag posted on a Wednesday is amber on Friday. One posted on a Friday is amber on Tuesday. Holidays are not counted out.
- **A person renamed after a note was posted** draws no chip on that note, and the note still reads whole. PR 2 decides whether the tag row keeps the name as it was written.
