# v2.7648 — Materials on site: press the job number to open the job (2026-11-17)

The owner's ask of 2026-11-17, on a screenshot of a job's page on Jobs → Materials with the job's number ringed: *I would like to be able to click on a job number and have it open the job modal, allowing us to get rid of the open the job button to the right.* Client only, no migration.

## What was there

The head of a job's page read the job as one line of words, `1091 · the job's name`, with the address under it and a button **Open the job** pinned at the far end of the line. The number was text.

## What changed

- `onSiteJobHead` in `src/lib/materials/onSiteJobWords.ts` splits the head in two: `press`, the job's number, and `rest`, the name read beside it. A job with no number is opened by its name, and a job the page could not name by the words the page has for it, so the head always holds one press. `openLabel` is what the press is called (*Open job 1091*).
- `JobsMaterialsTab.tsx` (`JobView`) draws the number as a press inside the heading (`materials-job-open`, `headPress`: the heading's own type with the dotted line a box's name wears) that calls `onOpenJobDetail`, and the **Open the job** button is gone. Both mounts follow, Jobs → Materials and Materials → On site, since they are one component.
- **Open the job** on a row of the *Drive folders* backfill and on the search across every job are other presses and stay.

## Tests

- `onSiteJobWords.test.ts`: the number is the press, a job with no number is opened by its name, a job the page could not name by its fallback.
- `JobsMaterialsTab.retireAll.render.test.tsx`: the number opens the job with the job's id, the heading still reads as the number and the name, and no **Open the job** button is drawn.

## Docs

The guide *see what is on every job site and what walked away* → *Read the row* says the number is a press. `docs/MATERIALS_ON_SITE_ARCHITECTURE.md` → The screens gains the head's own line.
