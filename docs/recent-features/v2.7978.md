# v2.7978 — Permits: a Market view says whether the companies we bid build here (2026-11-19)

Step one of punch list #405 ([the card](../../to-dos/permits-market-page/README.md)). Builders is where a name on a permit is looked up. Market is the opinion beside it: what the permits mean for us. This step ships the two parts that need no research, the table of companies we bid and the clock. The call list, who holds the work and how much is being built are step two.

## What the office sees

**Market** is a view on Prospects → Permits, on the strip beside **Builders**. It rides the address (`view=market`), so a reload and a link come back to it.

- **Do the companies we bid build here?** One row a company we priced a job for in two years: our bids, our wins, the commercial permits its names pulled in twelve months in every city the pull reads, and what that reads as.
  - *Builds, and we win* · *Builds, we never win* (three or more bids, no win) · *Builds, one try* or *two tries* (too early to say) · *No permit on record*.
  - Three chips filter it: **Builds here**, **No permit on record**, **Tie a name**.
  - The small words under a company say where its permits are, each a press that opens the builder's sheet Builders opens.
  - A customer with no permit on record offers **Tie a name**, which opens **Find their names on permits**. A tie made there writes the answer a builder's sheet writes, and the tied name's permits then count for the company.
- **The clock.** The middle wait and the middle half of the days between two dates, as a bar. Application to permit where a city's applications carry an issued day. Building permit to the plumber's permit where the city's permits name plumbers.
- **Two tiles**: how many of the companies we bid pulled a permit, and the clock's middle wait.

## How each number is counted

The card's *How each number is counted* is the definition. As built (`permitMarket.ts`):

- **A company we bid:** every bid made in the last 730 days, under its customer's name and under the old builders list's name on it, each keyed as permits are. One bid under two names of one key counts once. A win is an outcome of won or started.
- **Left out:** a practice bid and a ZZ customer (the ZZ convention), and a customer named for a bid platform, which is the bucket a platform's bids sit under until a GC is chosen (`isBidPlatformBucket`).
- **The permits:** each city's Builders count (`research_builders`: commercial building permits in twelve months) under the company's key, plus the count under every name a person tied to the customer for all of its permits (`answerBook`).
- **Tie a name's count:** our customers with no permit on record where a name on the permits begins theirs, or is begun by it. That is the *likely* grade a builder's sheet asks *Same company?* on (`nameGrade`). A looser likeness is not counted, and nothing is tied on any likeness.
- **The clock, application to permit:** commercial applications submitted in twelve months that carry an issued day. An application still waiting has no second day, so the newest months read a little short.
- **The clock, building permit to the plumber's permit:** the tab's rows of twelve months with a plumbing permit that counts (`plumber_permit_on`, set by `permit_address_plumbing_counts`). A plumbing permit dated before the building permit reads as no wait.
- **The quarter marks** are whole days. Fewer than five jobs draw no clock.

## The reads

No migration. The view reads, once when it opens: our bids of two years with their names (one page today), `research_builders` for each city, and the answers on names Builders already reads. A city's applications for the clock are read per city. Picking another city reads only those.

## Where it differs from the card, and why

- **The clock's applications are read off `permits`, not off the rows the tab holds.** The tab's read leaves out the rows read back as history, and most of San Antonio's year is history.
- **The plumber clock runs to the newest plumbing permit, not the first.** The read keeps one day per job. A job with two plumbing permits is measured to its later one. Measuring to the first needs a column on the read.
- **Tie a name opens Find their names on permits.** The card names the door Builders has. A builder's sheet ties from the name's side. A row here is a company, so the door is the one that starts from the customer. Both write the same answer.
- **A fourth reading, too early to say,** is on the mock-up and not in the card's list of three. One or two bids with no win is not *we never win*.
- **The built count of companies is lower than the first read's**, as the card says it would be: practice bids and the bid platform's bucket are left out.
