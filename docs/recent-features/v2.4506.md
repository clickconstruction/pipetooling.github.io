# v2.4506 — AIA G702-G703: our address prints on two lines (2026-10-04)

The owner, 2026-10-04: the contractor address defaulted to `5501 Balcones Dr A141, Austin, TX 78731` and he wants the street on one line and the city under it. Settings already holds it that way (Settings → the company block → **Address (multiline)**, `app_settings.physical_invoice_issuer_v1.addressText`, two lines in prod); the AIA prefill joined the lines with a comma, and a line break typed into the form's box reached the workbook as one cell with no wrap. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## The change

- **Prefill** [`aiaG702G703Template.ts`](../../src/lib/aiaG702G703Template.ts): `issuerAddressLines` keeps the lines Settings holds (blank lines dropped) instead of joining them. The form's **CONTRACTOR ADDRESS** box was already a textarea and the paper already draws line breaks.
- **Kernel** `aiaContractorBlockRows(address, license)`: the rows under the contractor's name — the address's lines, then the license line. One address line is the sheet as it always printed (D11 address, D12 license). A third line and on folds onto the second (`AIA_CONTRACTOR_ADDRESS_ROWS = 2`).
- **Fill** [`fillAiaG702G703Workbook.ts`](../../src/lib/fillAiaG702G703Workbook.ts) `placeContractorBlock`: the name, the address rows and the license line down column D. Three rows sit where the template has them (D10:D12). A two-line address with a license line makes four, and there is no row below (13 is the ruled line), so the block starts one row up at D9, beside FROM, with D9:F9 dressed like row 10. No row height changes: the page is the length it was.
