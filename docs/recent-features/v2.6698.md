## What happened on job 891

The subcontract was the GC's own form, e-signed by our RMP and never countersigned, and the GC's notice of termination relied on it. *Already have their contract?* would have filed it as signed by the customer, which was false, so the PDF went onto Evidence and the desk kept reading *No signed agreement on 891 — proceeds as a sworn account*. The clauses that decided the dispute were typed into the narrative by hand.

## What changed

- **The record** (`src/lib/jobs/theirPaper.ts`, the kernel): a `job_contracts` row with `kind = 'their_paper'` and `status = 'signed'`, its signer frames and `signed_at` left empty so no reader that knows only the frames mistakes it for a contract the customer signed and the activity bridge writes nothing for it. The new columns: `paper_title`, `our_signer_name`, `our_signed_on`, `our_signed_how`, `their_signed_on` (null = not countersigned), `relied_on_on` + `relied_on_what` (the owner's flag), `key_terms` (`[{ key, clause, words }]` over the eight term keys). `theirPaperLine` is the one line every surface draws (*Their paper · signed by us only · they rely on it*; *signed by both* once they countersign), `theirPaperWords` the sentence a hover and a print read, `theirPaperDraftProblem` the form's rule (a signer is required; a relied-on flag needs its what and its day; a link must be a link).
- **The coverage** (`jobContractCoverage.ts`): a new kind `their_paper` after a signed agreement of ours and before an acceptance of ours; not a contract gap (the GC's paper governs the job, as *Not needed · GC job* already said); the chip's tone is the blue of a record (`JobContractChip`). The batch readers keep `JOB_CONTRACT_COVERAGE_COLUMNS` (a column cannot be seen by `check:calls-ahead`, so a wider select would break every Pipeline read between the deploy and the push) and read the paper's own columns by id only for rows whose kind says so (`withTheirPaperColumns`, `theirPaperIo.ts`): a read from before the push finds no such row and asks nothing more.
- **The door** (`TheirPaperSheet.tsx`): *Their paper…* on the contract strip while nothing of ours is on file or the job is marked not needed; *Edit the record* once one is. The Contract window keeps the row out of its own rows and draws a band naming the paper with the same door, so the Pipeline chip and the desk's *Contract…* land on it too. The save is `saveTheirPaper` (insert or update, the file into the contract bucket, a `recorded` event); the payload is cast until the `chore(types)` PR carries the columns.
- **The desk's reading** (`src/lib/legal/legalContractBasis.ts`): `agreementCountsForSuit` is the rule — ours signed, or their paper once countersigned or relied on. `legalPacket.ts` reads it for the theory (basis *their paper, signed by us only, relied on (their notice of termination, Oct 2, 2026); backup: sworn account*), the gap (`theirPaperGapWords`: a note when the sworn account holds, a stop when it does not, each saying what makes the paper count), the steps (the day we signed with the terms, the day they relied on it) and the *Signed agreements* exhibit. The Paper tab's Agreements table reads the line in blue while it counts and amber until it does, with *Basis: … · …* under the table when a job has their paper; `coverageText`, the packet print, `firmAgreementWords`, `contractStateWords` and `exhibitDetails` read the paper as it is, never as signed by the customer.
- **The narrative** (`LegalNarrativeEditor.tsx`): *Insert the contract's terms* pastes `theirPaperNarrativeMarkdown` — the paper in one line, one list item per term — under *The contract*, with the same manners as the other two inserts (never twice).
- **The firm** (`_shared/legalMatterShape.ts`): `MATTER_CONTRACT_COLUMNS` adds `kind`, `created_at` and the paper's columns, so the portal's coverage kernel tells their paper from ours. **Redeploy `legal-portal` after the push**; until then the old function sends no kind and the firm reads a their-paper row as none on file (its `signed_at` is empty), never as signed.
