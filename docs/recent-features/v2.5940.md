# v2.5940 — Billed Awaiting Payment counts a lean's age on Chicago's calendar (2026-10-26)

Found while testing punch list #187 live on the evening of 2026-10-26: `BilledAwaitingPaymentSection.render.test.tsx` failed on main, and on the station a lean logged at 7:30 PM read *leaned on -1d ago*. `leanRead.ts`'s `touchYmd` took the date off the touch's UTC timestamp while `todayYmd` is Chicago's (`todayYmdInAppTz`), so from 7 PM to midnight Chicago (5 to midnight in winter) every touch of the evening was a day ahead of today and every older touch's age read one day short. It now reads the touch's day with `calendarYmdInAppTzFromIso`, the same calendar as today. The failure also turned CI red for every pull request in those hours, since CI runs after midnight UTC.

- **Test**: `leanRead.test.ts` +1, a lean at 7:30 PM Chicago (00:30 UTC the next day) reads *leaned on today* and a lean twelve Chicago days old reads *no lean in 12d*; it and the section's render test pass in UTC and in America/Chicago.
- The vehicle asks kernel counts whole 24-hour spans and was not affected.

No migration, no edge function.
