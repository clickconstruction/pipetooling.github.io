# v2.4469 — Sub work orders: a signature, an offer or a report in the evening keeps its day (2026-10-03)

One of the instant-day fixes that follow [v2.4452](v2.4452.md); [v2.4458](v2.4458.md) fixed the same days on the sub's own portal. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

The office's side of sub work orders cut days out of `timestamptz` columns by their first ten characters, so anything stamped after 7 pm CDT (6 pm CST) read as the next day:

- **A work order's coverage** ([`workOrderCoverage.ts`](../../src/lib/subWorkOrders/workOrderCoverage.ts)): `signedOn` from `step_commitments.signed_at` (else `accepted_at`) and `sentAt` from `offered_at`. Every reader takes them from here: the board's *signed this month* tile ([`workOrderBoardRows.ts`](../../src/lib/subWorkOrders/workOrderBoardRows.ts)), the Subs tiles' Offers and Signed queues and their counts ([`subsTileQueues.ts`](../../src/lib/subs/subsTileQueues.ts)), the rail's *sent* and *signed* lines and its nudge after a quiet offer ([`sheetRail.ts`](../../src/lib/subWorkOrders/sheetRail.ts), [`standingMove.ts`](../../src/lib/subs/standingMove.ts)), the job window's work order strip and the Subs work view's *offer out since*. The sub portal's statement already read `signed_at` in Central (v2.4458), so the office and the sub disagreed on an evening signature.
- **The sheet's story** ([`sheetStory.ts`](../../src/lib/subWorkOrders/sheetStory.ts)): an offer's *waiting 7 days*, the day an undated sheet started, the progress report's day, and the days at the walk-through from the stage event's `occurred_at` or `stage_changed_at`, through the file's `ymd`, which also reads `date` columns.
- **The walk-through rule** ([`subSheetStageDerived.ts`](../../src/lib/subSheetStageDerived.ts)): a hand move dated after the window's end keeps the sheet at work. A move on the window's last evening read as the next day, so the window never moved the sheet on.
- **A sheet with no date of its own** (`people_labor_jobs.job_date` is null): the board's sheet date, the Sheet date box on Sub Labor ([`JobsSubLaborTab.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsSubLaborTab.tsx)), the crew P&L's sheet day ([`JobsCrewPnlTab.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsCrewPnlTab.tsx)) and the story window's *Sheet dated* ([`SheetStoryModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/SheetStoryModal.tsx)) fell back to `created_at`'s UTC day, and so did each owed sheet on the Dashboard's money owed ([`useSubLaborDueTotal.ts`](../../src/hooks/useSubLaborDueTotal.ts)).
- **One-line readers**: the sub's progress chip (*reported … from their portal*, `progress_at`) and a GC's ask on the stage calendar (`job_stage_windows.asked_at`) in [`JobsSubsWorkView.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsSubsWorkView.tsx); the Offers queue's *opened Sep 5 · 3×* ([`OffersQueue.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/subsTiles/OffersQueue.tsx)); the issued day on an offered work order reopened for editing ([`WorkOrderAssemblerModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/WorkOrderAssemblerModal.tsx)); and a sheet's earlier waivers, *sent* from `person_contract_documents.sent_at` ([`LienWaiverSendModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienWaiverSendModal.tsx)).
- **A sub lien waiver's settle guess** ([`lienWaiverPick.ts`](../../src/lib/subWorkOrders/lienWaiverPick.ts)): a payment with no `payment_date` counts its five days from `created_at`.

The reverse case was on the work order panel ([`SubSheetWorkOrderPanel.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/SubSheetWorkOrderPanel.tsx)): *signed the Master Subcontract Agreement on …* read the MSA's `signed_at`, a `date`, through `new Date(…)`. A bare day read that way is midnight UTC, the evening before in Central, so it printed the day before.

## What changed

Each instant reads `calendarYmdInAppTzFromIso(iso)` from [`dateUtils.ts`](../../src/utils/dateUtils.ts) (`APP_CALENDAR_TZ`). The `date` columns keep their slice: `job_date`, `payment_date`, `payable_after`, `offer_expires_at` and `person_contract_documents.signed_at`. The panel prints the MSA's day with its own `fmtYmd`, as the assembler and quick send already do. `sheetStory.ts`'s `ymd` now says it is for date columns.

## Tests

- `workOrderCoverage.test.ts` and `workOrderBoardRows.test.ts` (+1 each): a signature at `2026-10-01T00:30:00Z` (7:30 pm CDT on Sep 30) reads Sep 30, and one at 7:30 pm CDT on Aug 31 is not in September's tally; `2026-12-02T00:30:00Z` (6:30 pm CST) reads Dec 1; noon UTC reads its own day; an undated sheet made in the evening reads the day before.
- `sheetStory.test.ts` (+3): an offer sent at 7:30 pm CDT on Aug 29 is *waiting 7 days* on Sep 5; an undated sheet made then starts Aug 20; a Done tapped at 8 pm CDT on Sep 3 is 2 days at the walk-through.
- `subSheetStageDerived.test.ts` (+1): a hand move at 8 pm CDT on the window's last day is inside it, so the window fires the next day; at noon on the day after, it stands.
- `lienWaiverPick.test.ts` (+1): a payment recorded at 7:30 pm CDT on Sep 2 is 5 days old and settled on Sep 7.
- `subsTileQueues.render.test.tsx` and `JobsSubLaborTab.render.test.tsx` (+1 each): *opened Sep 5 · 3×* and the Sheet date box; new `useSubLaborDueTotal.test.ts`: the Dashboard's owed sheets.

All 10 fail on main. The work view's chip and ask, the assembler's issued day, the waiver list, the story window, the crew P&L and the MSA line change one expression each; their render smokes load no such rows.

## Checked against prod

Read only, from the worktree dev server: no work order has been sent or signed yet, no GC has asked for a stage window, and all 81 sheets carry their own date. 65 of the 79 sheet payments have no payment day; 2 were recorded in a Central evening, 103 days ago, so no waiver guess changes. The one signed master agreement read a day early on the work order panel until this ships.
