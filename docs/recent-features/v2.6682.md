## What changed

A day ahead of today is refused with *plan a step instead*; a document not live on the same matter is named in the refusal.
- **Grammar** (`_shared/devMcpWrites.ts`): `plan_matter_event` / `apply_matter_event` join `WRITE_VERBS` (fifty-one verbs, forty-three over eighteen RPCs); `writeRpcBody` checks the event's shape as the office form's `eventDraftProblem` does (a `YYYY-MM-DD` day, a body under 500, notes under 4,000, document ids as uuids, duplicates folded) and POSTs `{ p, p_dry_run }` like the others; `alreadyApplied` says the event is on the Timeline and that an event is never edited.
- **Server** (`dev-mcp/index.ts`, 0.40.0): the two tools with their descriptions, the two case labels on the write branch, the seat's own description names the pair. Plan → hash → apply, one apply per plan, as every write.
- **Tests** (`devMcpWrites.test.ts`, +4): the allowlist gains the RPC and checks the migration's gates, the `legal_event_add` call with source `mcp`, the clock and the count; the grammar's valid and refused shapes; the plan reply and the repeat-apply words; the server's tools, labels and version.
