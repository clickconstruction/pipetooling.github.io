# v2.3523 — Job Summary → Capacity: recorded time off comes off the available hours (2026-09-16)

Client only. No migration, no edge function. Item 1 of `to-dos/job-summary-follow-ups.md`; item 4 of the owner's ready-to-build train.

## Why

Capacity (v2.2828) counted every leader and helper on the roster at 8 hours a weekday and said so in its own footnote: *PTO and holidays aren't subtracted yet, so a holiday week reads low.* A week someone was out read as slack the office did not have.

## What shipped

- **Kernel** — [`buildCapacitySeries`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobSummaryCapacity.ts) takes an optional `timeOff` list (`{ personId, startYmd, endYmd }`). On the roster source, a field person recorded off on a weekday comes out of that day's available hours; the week and the totals carry `hoursOff`. Headcount (`people`) stays the roster on the busiest weekday — time off comes off the hours, not the crew. The clocked fallback ignores time off (it has no roster to subtract from). Three new tests: two days off mid-week, a day off before the person joined, the fallback.
- **The source** — [`useFieldRoster`](../../src/hooks/useFieldRoster.ts) reads `user_time_off` for the ledger's window and maps each row to the person through `people.account_user_id`. The to-do named the salary work-schedule day overrides; those describe a working pattern, and the recorded days off live in `user_time_off` (58 rows on prod since April), which `resolveCalendarWorkday` already treats as the first word. Unreadable time off reads as none and the footnote says so.
- **The view** — a **Time off** tile (hours that came off, or *no recorded days off in the window*), a dashed cap above a week's outline bar for the hours that came off, a legend entry, the hours in the hover title, and the footnote rewritten: *less the days off recorded on People → Hours. Company holidays have no record in the app, so a holiday week still reads low.*
- Help guide *see whether the crew was full*: the Time off tile and the corrected watch-out.

Not done, honestly: **holidays**. Nothing in the app records a company holiday, so there is nothing to subtract; a holidays record would be its own small train (a table, a Settings row, the kernel reads it like time off). The to-do says so.

## Verified

- Kernel tests (10), `helpGuideContent`, release-notes drift, to-do drift, typecheck, lint.
- Live on prod data: Jobs → Job Summary → Capacity over a window holding recorded days off shows the Time off tile with the hours, the dashed cap on those weeks, and the recomputed utilization; the Dashboard's under-60% watch and the Ahead view still build (they pass no time off and are unchanged).
