# v2.3777 — One lien notice for the jobs at a property, on purpose (2026-09-23)

Punch list **#35** (`to-dos/lien-notice-sent-by-hand/`), PR 3 of 3. Client only — `packet_id` and `printed_claim` landed with v2.3763's migration.

## Why

The run already shares the owner's envelope across the jobs at one property (v2.3720) but prints one § 53.056 form per job. Taunya's Lenox paper of 2026-09-22 folded 273, 858 and 866 into one form — PR 2 records that after the fact; this PR lets the run print it that way on purpose. It is a tick, off by default, because the form's claim changes when jobs combine and that is the owner's call (the to-do's blocker).

## The change

- **Kernel [`lienNoticeCombine.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienNoticeCombine.ts)** (3 tests): `combineKey` (the owner's name and address plus the original contractor — a missing name, address or GC never combines), `combineNoticesByProperty` (the biggest claim leads and its form, cover page and recipients carry; the months are the union; the claim is the sum; `parts` remember each job's own share, item and months; the reference strip reads *Jobs #273, #858, #866*), `combineSummary`, `combinedFilingPayloads` (one `job_lien_filings` row per part on one `packet_id`, the job's share as `amount`, the paper's months and total as `months_covered` / `printed_claim`, the sends and the saved copy on each; a plain notice records as before).
- **The run modal** ([`LienDeskRunModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskRunModal.tsx), two smokes): the tick appears only when combining would change something (*2 jobs would print as 1 notice (1 combined)*); the shown notices drive the envelopes, the cover sheet, the printed packet and the record; a combined notice carries every part's invoices and pay codes (v2.3758) under the lead job's id, so the pay page lists every bill; the row lists the parts and their shares; a method or tracking typed on an envelope reaches every part (the patch is keyed by item and recipient, not by row index).
- **The record** ([`lienDeskRunIo.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDeskRunIo.ts)): a combined notice inserts its rows in one call, marks each part's desk item sent against its own filing, clears each part's one-shot claim correction, and counts each part as recorded.
- Guide *send lien notices from the Lien desk* (Sending the run); GLOSSARY → Lien desk; the #35 to-do closes its plan — what is left is the office recording the Lenox paper through PR 2's door and the owner's two calls.

## Decisions taken

- **Same owner, same address, same GC — nothing less.** Two jobs at one address under different GCs are two notices; the statute's notice is to the owner about one original contractor's contract.
- **The unit of record stays the job.** The GC run's *noticed* reading, the Customer review's *on notice since* chip and the affidavit's sworn notice all key on the job's own filing.

## Verified

No approved notices sit on prod's desk today, so the run itself could not be opened live; the smoke covers the fold and the record call.
