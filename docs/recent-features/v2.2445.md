# v2.2445 — Book-edit carry covers the whole bid (2026-08-28)

Follow-up review of v2.2444 (fresh pass over the shipped code, checked against BP384's
prod data). Three findings, all fixed:

1. **"Use $13.00 on this bid" wrote only the viewed price option.** A bid's other
   pricings (alternates, other GC packets) each hold their own frozen copy — BP384 has
   three, all with `Ft of Water Line` at $12, and the PlanHub packet's ★ letter is one of
   them. Pressing the button fixed one copy and left the sent packet stale, recreating
   the original confusion one level down. New kernel fn `planSiblingCarry`: the apply now
   also updates same-fixture entries in the bid's other pricings **whose prices equal the
   viewed copy's exact stale values** — identical values are inherited values; anything
   re-priced on purpose (a VE'd entry) won't match and is left alone. The banner counts
   the reach before the press ("2 more price options on this bid hold the same $12 and
   will update with it"); one `UPDATE … IN` covers them all. Adding stays single-copy
   (nothing re-prices from an unassigned entry).
2. **Cross-book update offers were a foot-gun.** Editing ANY book with a bid open (say
   RSmeans) offered "Use $6,150.00 on this bid" for a bid pricing from WENDI.
   `planBookEditBidOffer` now takes `editedBookFeedsThisBid`: *update* offers fire only
   from the book the bid's lineage resolves to (or when no lineage resolves at all);
   *add* offers still fire from any book — importing a missing entry across books is
   exactly the Default-book case v2.2444 was built for.
3. **The entry add/edit form rendered at z-index 50 under the drawer's 70** (pre-existing
   since the drawer rework) — on narrow screens the form opened behind the drawer that
   opened it. Now 80.

Also: the offer plan is now async (it fetches sibling entries), guarded by a token so a
plan that resolves after the pricing/drawer context moved on is dropped, not shown.
+7 kernel tests (17 total on `bookEditBidOffer`). No migration.
