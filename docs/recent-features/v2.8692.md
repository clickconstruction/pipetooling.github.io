**Read.** *Papers before a job opens* (punch list #389) is set one job at a time: one row a job, one copy a person a row. Nothing could require a paper of a site. And the database could not tell which jobs are at an address, since the rule that cleans an address lived in the app alone.

**The shape.** Feed the gate that is there. Turning secrecy on for a site writes that gate's own row on every job at the site, a trigger adds it to each new job, and the lock counts a person as signed for every job of a site once they signed for one. The chip, the prompt, clock-in, Needs you and the emails then work as they do.

**What it makes.** `address_key(text)`, the app's address rule in SQL. A table `site_secrecy`, one row a site and a paper. A column `job_required_papers.site_secrecy_id`. Three presses for the office: `site_secrecy_preview`, `site_secrecy_turn_on`, `site_secrecy_turn_off`. Two triggers, on a job's address or development and on a Same place answer. Nine of the gate's functions restated, each with the site's rule added and nothing else.

**The person's calls**, relayed 2026-11-27: one signature a person for the whole site. The office picks our own people's paper from the Contract Book. A sub with a login is an outside company, is not asked this paper and is not shut out by it. The four office roles turn it on and off, and the estimator reads. Turning it off opens the jobs at once and keeps every signature, and turning it on again asks only those who never signed.

**Calls made here, each open to a flip.** A site's paper is one of plain words, clauses or a form, not one built from settings. A job at another spelling is at the site only once it is answered Same place. The code name is kept now and used from piece 7. The paper's words are kept as they stood when the site went on, so a job that joins later takes the words the others signed. A job that already requires the same paper by itself keeps its own row. A trigger never refuses a job's save: a sync that fails is logged.

**Nothing changes for a job with no site row.** Each restated function reaches the site's rule only on a row whose `site_secrecy_id` is set, and no row has one until an office press.
