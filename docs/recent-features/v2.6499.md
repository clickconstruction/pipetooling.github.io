# v2.6499 — The Development page’s cost timeline draws value created and prints (2026-11-04)

Punch list #257, PR 3 of 3, after PR 1 (v2.6493, #7012) drew the Money look's Timeline and PR 2 (v2.6498) its amber band. The train's last two pieces: the blue line and the print.

- **Value created on the right axis**, behind the shared view's own *Value created (right axis)* toggle, off by default as on the Job Summary. `useSiteValueEvents` (new) reads every field report and every hand-set percent on the site's jobs in one chunked, paged read each by job id (`reports` by `job_ledger_id`, `job_pct_events` with `source = 'manual'`), only while the view is open; `siteValueEventsFromRows` builds them as the Job window does (`buildJobValueEvents`, `buildJobManualPercentEvents`), tagged with the job. The kernel's `siteValueSeries` writes the site's value onto the rows: at each bucket every job's newest percent so far times its price, summed; on one day a hand-set beats a report (`newestPercentEvent`'s rule); a job with no dated percent but a current one adds it at the last bucket, as the kernel does for one job, by the Scoreboard's own percent rule (`scoreboardPct`: a paid job at 100, else the Job Summary chain over the job's own percent and the page's bills), so the line ends where the Scoreboard's Earned stands apart from its assumed halves, and the scope line counts those jobs; a job with no price adds nothing. The 🚩 markers and the tooltip's lines (*JP603 · Report by Dana — 40% complete*) are the shared view's. The key's words are the site's (`valueWords`, a new prop): *each job’s % done × its price, summed*; with no priced job carrying a percent it says so.
- **Print or save as PDF**: a press under the chart opens a print window (`openHtmlPrintWindow`, the Job Costs tab's own door) with the site's name, the customer, the scope line, the chart's ends in words (`siteTimelineEndWords`), the chart's own SVG as the screen drew it (the theme's variables made literal, landscape page) and the key, stamped with who printed it and when (`siteTimelinePrintHtml`, pure). A blocked pop-up says so beside the press.
