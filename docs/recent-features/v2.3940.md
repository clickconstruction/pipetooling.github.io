## Found while pinning

The number box reads jobs and bids differently. A job matches when its number **is** the digits or **starts with** them; a bid matches when its number **contains** them. Typing `375` finds job 3751 but not job 1375 — and does find bid 1375. Pinned as it is and recorded as quirk #22 on the map; making the two agree is a behavior change and would be its own PR.
