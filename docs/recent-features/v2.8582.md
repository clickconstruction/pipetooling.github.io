# v2.8582 — A slow reload no longer shows the sign-in page to someone who is signed in (2026-11-25)

Punch list #406, from the simulator's third sitting on 2026-11-25. Read in the code and held by a test. Not reproduced on a device after the change. No migration, no edge function.

**Seen**, in an iPhone simulator on a starved Mac, one **Hard Reload** of three from Settings, as the dev, signed in the whole time:

- 0.8 s *Loading…*, 4.5 s *Taking too long? Fix the app*.
- 8.3 s the sign-in page.
- 10.5 s the app's header and *Loading…*, 11.3 s the Dashboard. Not Settings, and not the Bridge, where a dev lands.
- About 95 s later a red toast: the app was busy renewing the sign-in.

**The cause.** `src/hooks/useAuth.ts` arms an 8-second watchdog on the first session check (v2.1051), so a wedged auth client cannot hold the app on *Loading…* for ever. Only `getSession()`'s own answer settled that watchdog. But auth-js tells the listener the recovered session before `getSession()` gets the sign-in lock, and a renewal can hold that lock past 8 seconds. So the watchdog fired on a person the listener had already handed over, and wiped them: user, role and name.

Each frame followed from that:

- **The sign-in page.** `ProtectedRoute` saw no user.
- **The page was lost.** The route had already seen the listener's user, so the sign-in page's memory of where to return read *leave* and kept no address.
- **The Dashboard, for a dev.** The watchdog cleared the role, but the hook still held the person as one whose role was resolved. When `getSession()` answered, no role gate was raised, so the landing ran with no role, and no role lands on the Dashboard.

**The mend.**

- The listener notes whether it last handed over a session. When the watchdog fires and it has, the watchdog opens the gate and leaves the person as they are.
- Where the watchdog does sign the person out, it also forgets whose role was resolved, so a session that arrives later raises the role gate.
- The 8 seconds and the freeze rule (`appHealthHoldsGate`) are as they were.

## What did not change

- A start-up that tells the app nothing in 8 seconds still shows the sign-in page, and a late session still signs the person in. That path keeps the address and returns the person to it. Its flash of the sign-in page on a slow phone is a design call nobody has made, and is not this fault.
- With a session from the listener and no answer from `getSession()`, the app still reads *Loading…* until the 8 seconds are up. Opening the gate the moment the listener hands a session over would be faster. It is a wider change to the gate and was not asked.
- The red toast is the app's own honest line for a held lock.

## Tests

- `useAuth.watchdog.render.test.tsx`, new, written first and seen to fail: `getSession()` never answers, the listener hands a session at 1 s, the clock runs past 8 s. Before the mend the user was gone. Now the person stays, with their role and name, and the gate is open. A late answer from `getSession()` changes nothing.
- The same file: a start-up that told it nothing is signed out at 8 s, and a late session signs the person in behind the role gate. A listener that said signed in and then signed out leaves nobody to keep.
- `useAuth.render.test.tsx` passes as it was.

## Not walked

The look is the simulator's, with no write: several **Hard Reload** presses on a loaded Mac, none through the sign-in page, each back on the page it was pressed from.
