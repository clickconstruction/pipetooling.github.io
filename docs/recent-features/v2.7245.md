# v2.7245 — The Bridge: Last worked says the day and how many days ago, with the people under it (2026-11-14)

Client only. No migration, no edge function, and no kernel that is copied to the edge functions changed. A follow-up to v2.7221, which added the **Last worked** cell to the list *Set a percent on the jobs without one*.

The owner, of that cell: "On last worked, instead of seeing September 22 comma clocked -- Malachi, I would like to see 'Sept 22, [number of days ago]' And then in soft text below it, who contributed to it".

## What changed

- **The cell's words** (`pctFacts.ts`, `lastWorkedCell`, `daysAgoWords`): the day and how long ago it was, counted in calendar days: *today*, *yesterday*, *53 days ago*. The month is written the way the rest of the page writes it.
- **Who was out there**: the people on that last day, the most hours that day first, in the list's usual short form (two names, or two and *N more*). Asked whether "who contributed" meant everyone who has worked the job or the last day's crew, the owner first kept everyone and then said: "Actually, just the last people to go out there". Everyone with hours on the job is one press away, on the Hours figure.
- **The schedule still shows.** The word *clocked* is gone from the cell. A day the schedule alone names says so in the soft words: *Scheduled for Ana and Lee*. A job nobody has worked says *Not worked yet*, with the next scheduled day under it.
- **The table** (`callDive.ts`, `BridgeMoveTable.tsx`): a cell may carry soft words under its own (`sub` on a cell). At a desk they sit under the day on one line, cut with the whole text on hover, and the row is no taller. On a phone they take a line of their own under the row's facts.
- **The window** (`BridgePctRun.tsx` draws `pctFactsRows`): the **Last worked** fact reads the day with how long ago it was, then *Clocked by* or *On the schedule for* as before.

## Tests

- `callDive.test.ts` (13) and `BridgeMoveTable.render.test.tsx` (14): the soft words on the cell, drawn under the day at a desk and on their own line on a phone, and none for a job nobody has worked.
- `BridgePctRun.render.test.tsx` (8): the window's sentence.
