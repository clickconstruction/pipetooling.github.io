# v2.7919 — Dev seat: a lien filing's replies say the copy to mail is the stamped scan (2026-11-19)

## Why

Since v2.7916 *Print the copies to mail* mails the filing's saved copy, the clerk's stamped scan, and draws nothing in its place. The dev seat's `plan_lien_filing` still described the print it replaced: four of its lines said the copy is redrawn from the record and told the caller to mail a photocopy of the scan. A session reading a lien through the seat would have passed that on as advice about legal paper.

## What changed

Words only. No verb, argument, write or server version changed.

- **A filing already on record that differs from the paper** (`copy_to_mail`): says the copy in the envelope is the filed paper whatever the record says (`LIEN_COPY_IS_THE_SCAN_WORDS`). Where the difference is one the cover letter states (the claim, the property address or the owner: `lienLetterDiffersWords`), it adds that the letter would be wrong and names the window's tick that leaves it out. A difference in the months, the notice day or the legal description reaches neither the copy nor the letter, and the reply no longer suggests it does.
- **A held plan** (`held_because`): the same two sentences, with the letter's as "recorded as it stands".
- **A snapshot with no owner, property or amount**: the key `no_copy_to_print` is now `no_cover_letter`, and says the stamped copy prints with no letter in front.
- **A filing planned with no scan and no link**: the plan's `writes.file` line adds that the print holds the lien until a saved copy is added.
- `plan_lien_filing`'s description in the tool list and the seat's README say the same.

## Deploy

`dev-mcp` is redeployed from `main` after the merge. Until then the seat answers with the old words; nothing it writes differs.

## Tests

`devMcpLienFiling.test.ts`: the held plan and the on-record check read the new sentences, a months-only difference carries no word about the letter, the three letter fields are named when they differ, a filing with no saved copy is named as held at the print, and the server's description and the README carry no "redraws" or "mail a photocopy".
