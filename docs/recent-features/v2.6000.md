# v2.6000 — Pricing: an alternate with its own takeoff stays on screen when you open it (2026-10-27)

Wendi sent a 7.6-second screen recording of Bids → Pricing on BP545 LAVENDER SPRINGS BLDG 2 with two lines: "IT DONT
WORK" and "I WANT IT TO STOP DISAPPEARING BECAUSE I AM TRYING TO WORK ON IT". Read frame by frame (4 a second, and every
frame around the two moments it changes), it shows this. The bid goes to Tommy Gillis as two versions: the base,
named Tommy Gillis, and INCLUDING WATER SOFTENER, an alternate with its own takeoff (v2.2404). She starts on the
alternate: its chip is lit on the Send to strip and the Workbench shows one price card, WENDI $150,400. At 1.8 s she
clicks the base chip; the row reloads with two cards, WENDI (★ the price on their letter) and INCLUDING WATER SOFTENER
(📐 own takeoff · On their letter · alternate). At 2.85 s, with the hand cursor resting on the alternate's card, she
clicks it, the way every other card on the row is clicked to work on a price. Within one frame the card is gone, the
Tommy Gillis chip goes dim, the INCLUDING WATER SOFTENER chip lights, and the row reloads to the one card it had at the
start. At 5.3 s she clicks the base chip again, at 6.2 s the alternate card again, and it vanishes again. No value was
typed and none was lost. Not the Labor tab's autosave gate (v2.5863) and not the version-ready reload bar: the bar is
never on screen and the flip is instant on the click, not after a round trip.

## The mechanism

- The own-takeoff alternate's card on [`PricingCardsRow`](../../src/components/bids/PricingCardsRow.tsx) is a door:
  its click is `onOpenAlternate`, which the Pricing tab wires to `onSwitchBidVersion` → the engine's
  `switchActiveVersion`. A click on the card is the same as a click on the version's chip up top. The card's only
  word about that was a hover tooltip, and every other card on the row is clicked to *view* a price.
- `sameGcAlternateVersions` lists same-GC alternates other than the active one. On the alternate that is nothing: the
  alternate is the active version and the base is not an alternate. So the row held only the alternate's own prices.
- A new version copies every price with its name (v2.2395), so the alternate's own ★ is also called WENDI. After the
  click nothing on the Workbench carried the words INCLUDING WATER SOFTENER except the lit chip on the Send to strip.
  The app had taken Wendi exactly where she wanted to go and did not say so, and the thing she clicked was nowhere on
  the screen when she got there.

## What changed

- **The row says where you are.** On an own-takeoff alternate the tray opens with a line, *📐 You are on INCLUDING
  WATER SOFTENER, an alternate with its own takeoff. The cards below are its prices. The base is the first card.*
  (`alternateOnScreenWords` in [`ownTakeoffAlternates.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/ownTakeoffAlternates.ts), held to
  `plainWordsFailures`).
- **The base rides the alternate's row as a card**, the mirror of the alternate's card on the base's row:
  `sameGcBaseVersions` (same kernel) names the same-GC base while the active version is an alternate; the row draws it
  first, with a green *base* tag, its ★ revenue on its own counts, its margin with its own materials swapped in
  (`alternateCardNumbers`, the delta reading *vs the alternate*), and the foot *On their letter · base*. A click opens
  the base. [`useAlternateVersionData`](../../src/hooks/usePricingCardsData.ts) reads the base's numbers the same way it
  reads an alternate's.
- **Every version card says *Open ›*** at its top right, so a card that moves you onto another version looks different
  from a price card. The two kinds share one render (`versionCard`) in `PricingCardsRow`; the alternate card's words
  and doors are unchanged.
- `BidsPricingTab` passes the row the version on screen and its base versions; the base cards count toward the row's
  layout, so a row with one price and a base card is the tray, never the one-line band.
- The guide [price a bid with the Workbench](../../src/content/help/price-a-bid-with-the-workbench.md) says what the card
  does and what you see on the far side.
