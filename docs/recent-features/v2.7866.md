## What changed

- **The Saved band**, `PaintedMapSavedBand.tsx` over `src/lib/paintedMap/paintedMapSaved.ts`, in the map's window (`PaintedMapWindow.tsx`). It shows once a save lands in the window: a line drawn or walked, a line's shape, or a place. It reads *✓ Saved on J2417 · Cedar Hollow 12* and, under it, what is on the map and who can open it. A map for every lot and a map on no job each say their own words. A map opened later, whose job's words the window does not hold, reads *Saved on its job*.
- **Text it from my phone…** under the band, on a phone, for whoever may share the map (`paintedMapSavedOffersText`, the same rule as **Share**). It is the Share sheet's own press (#394): it uses the link this phone holds, or makes one, then opens the phone's Messages with the words written. A phone that will not open Messages after the wait gets *The link is ready. Press Open Messages.* and the press reads **Open Messages**. A link this phone holds that was ended elsewhere is not sent: the band reads the map's links once and makes a new one.
- **One blue press.** While the band carries the text press, **Draw a line** is plain.
- **Back in view.** A save scrolls the panel to its top, so after a place is added the band and its press are on screen.
- The band says the map's summary, so the window's own summary line steps aside while the band shows.
- A job's own list of places (#393) draws no band.
