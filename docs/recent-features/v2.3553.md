# v2.3553 — Job accounts on the Bid Board, PR 1b: the lens and one email per house (2026-09-17)

Client only. No migration, no edge function — the RPC exists (`list_bid_job_account_strip`, v2.3451). Second slice of [`to-dos/bid-board-job-accounts/`](../../to-dos/bid-board-job-accounts/README.md) after the row's chips (v2.3520); the strip steps (PR 2) stay unbuilt. Mock-up: the Claude artifact *Job Accounts Lens, Before and After* (2026-09-17), drawn on the board as it is after 1a.

## Why

The row's chips answer "where does this bid's account stand" one bid at a time. Working the pile — three jobs at Ferguson, a first parts run tomorrow — meant reading every row, and asking Curly meant three emails for three properties. The owner asked for the estimator to manage job accounts from the board; this is the list and the one email.

## What shipped

- **Where it lives.** A sixth entry in the Followup group's segmented control, **Job accounts**, beside Waiting to hear — the same chrome and the same tab-key pattern (`'job-accounts'` in the union, `BIDS_TABS`, the superintendent redirect list, `EGG_BIDS_TABS`; deep link `?tab=job-accounts`). The button wears the count of bids whose job still has a house missing. The Bid Board's **"N missing job accounts"** header note (Won and Started or Complete) is now a door to it.
- **One read for the page.** `Bids.tsx` owns the `useBidBoardJobAccountStrips` call for every won or started bid and hands it to the board (new optional prop `jobAccountStrips`; the board's own read switches off when the page's is given), the control's count, and the lens — one RPC call, not three.
- **The lens** ([`BidsJobAccountsLens`](../../src/components/bids/BidsJobAccountsLens.tsx)): the rollup (*3 jobs · 2 houses · 1 won bid with no job yet*), **Only my bids** (estimator or account manager), then one band per house — the job-accounts rep from the strip with email and phone, or the sheet's honest *no job-accounts rep with an email on file* line with the Supply houses pointer — and its rows: **first parts run** (`bids.estimated_job_start_date`; red inside three days, amber inside ten; *no date · won Sep 13* when the bid has none), the J#### pill and project with the bid, GC and bidder, what is missing (*Ferguson · none yet · quoted this bid* / *expects one per property* / *Ferguson · asked · Sep 12 · Curly opens them*), and the actions: **Mark opened…** and **Not needed** (the existing `MarkJobAccountOpenedModal`, `initialMode` per button), or **Open the job first** on a won bid with no job (`openNewJob({ prefillBidId })`). A requested account stays listed until the house opens it.
- **One email per house.** **Ask Curly for both** (`askRepLabel`) opens a band under the house: a tick per row (rows with a job and nobody asked; asked rows and no-job bids are shown greyed and left out), the note composed by `composeRepEmailForProperties` — the single sheet's voice, the ticked properties as numbered blocks each with its own GC and owner line, the start weeks joined (*first parts runs are the weeks of Sep 21 and Sep 28*); one property falls back to `composeRepEmail`. **Email from my inbox** (mailto) or **Copy for email**, then **Sent — log both** runs the shared write once per job.
- **The shared write** [`logJobAccountAskByEmail`](../../src/lib/jobs/logJobAccountAsk.ts): the `requested` upsert (`ignoreDuplicates`) and the send-log row with the bid, lifted out of `JobAccountRepEmailSheet` (which now calls it) so the sheet and the lens log the same way.
- **Kernel** [`jobAccountsLens.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/jobAccountsLens.ts) (+8 tests): `buildJobAccountsLens` (rows per missing house, a no-job bid under the houses the strip names for it, groups biggest first, rows by start date then the older win, Only my bids), `emailableRows`, `daysUntil` / `describeStart` / `startTone`, `askRepLabel`, `lensRollup`. `composeRepEmailForProperties` (+3 tests) in `jobAccountRepEmail.ts`.
- Guide *open a job account when you win a bid* → "Work the pile from Followup".
