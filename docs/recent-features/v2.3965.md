# v2.3965 — Contract sweep: an unsent draft goes out with the current standard terms (2026-09-28)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. A fix to the standard-terms lever of [v2.3642](v2.3642.md).

## Why

A `job_contracts` draft stores a copy of its terms (`body_html`, `body_format`, `template_document_id`, `template_name`, `template_version_date`), and `send-job-contract` / `share-job-contract` send what the row holds. After the office edited the standard terms from the sweep, every draft that already existed kept the old wording and sent it:

- `saveJobContractDraft` stripped the five terms columns from every update of an existing draft, so the re-save the terms-edit `onSaved` asked for ("save it now") wrote nothing.
- `quickSendJobContract` reused a live draft as it stood.
- The hand-off (*Download to print*) stamped the draft as it stood unless something had been typed.
- The pane's preview and the draft PDF read `draftRow.body_html`, so the office previewed the old wording too.

The Contract window never had the fault: `JobContractModal`'s `buildRowPayload` writes the terms of the draft's own document on every save and before every send.

## The rule

A draft is not a snapshot. Until it is sent, a draft written from a Book document follows that document's current wording — and only ever forward: a caller holding an earlier `template_version_date` than the draft carries (a sweep left open while the terms were edited in the Contract window, the Contract library or Settings) leaves the draft's newer terms alone. The dates are `YYYY-MM-DD` and are compared as strings; the same date, or a date missing on either side, decides nothing, and the caller's copy is written when it differs.

| The job's live row | Saving / sending with document A |
| --- | --- |
| none | a draft is inserted with A's terms (unchanged) |
| draft written from A, wording behind | the five terms columns are written from A |
| draft written from A, wording current | no terms are written |
| draft written from A at a later version date than the caller's copy of A | no terms are written — the draft is ahead of the caller |
| draft written from another document | its terms are left alone — the office chose that in the Contract window |
| draft written from the built-in wording (`template_document_id` null) | its terms are left alone |
| sent, signed or voided | never touched |

## What shipped

- **`src/lib/jobs/jobContractDraftTerms.ts`** (pure, 25 tests): `draftTermsToWrite(existing, incoming)` — the terms to write, or null; `jobContractTermsInEffect(existing, incoming)` — what the agreement goes out with; `jobContractTermsFromTemplate(template)` — the one place a Book document (or the built-in wording) becomes the five columns; `pickJobContractTerms`.
- **`jobContractDraftWrite.ts`** — `saveJobContractDraft` adds `draftTermsToWrite`'s answer to the update of an existing draft; `refreshJobContractDraftTerms({ existing, template })` is the terms-only write used before a draft goes out, and throws when it fails. `buildJobContractDraftPayload` reads its terms through `jobContractTermsFromTemplate`. 11 new mocked-client tests.
- **`jobContractQuickSend.ts`** — a reused draft goes through `refreshJobContractDraftTerms` before the function is invoked; a failed refresh returns `{ ok: false }` and nothing is sent. Its first tests (8) pin the order read → refresh → send, and that a stale template leaves a newer draft as it reads.
- **`JobsContractSweepModal.tsx`** — the preview and the draft PDF read `jobContractTermsInEffect`; the hand-off refreshes a draft nothing was typed on; the terms-edit `onSaved` marks the pane dirty only when the selected draft was written from the edited document, and its comment says what happens to the others. One new render case.
- Help guide `get-a-job-contract-signed`, *Two kinds of terms*: the example says what an edit does to agreements that have not gone out.

## Found on the way (not changed)

- A draft written from document B stays on the wording it stored when the sweep is sending with document A, even after B itself is edited; it is refreshed only from the Contract window, which always saves with the draft's own document.
- `fileSignedJobContract` writes the sweep's chosen terms over a draft it converts to a signed paper record, whatever document the draft was written from.
- The pane's Terms row names a draft's stored `template_name` until the draft is next saved.
- Two edits on one day share a version date, so a sweep left open across the second of them can still write the first over a draft saved on the second; the document carries no time of day to order them by.
