# v2.8153 — Document builder: publishing your usual sheet no longer writes over a teammate's changes to a job (2026-11-20)

Punch list #404, the last of the four saving and publishing faults the review of 2026-11-20 found and did not fix. It stands on v2.8143 (the draft's number in the database) and v2.8148 (a save and a publish name it). No migration and no function.

## What was wrong

**Publish to N jobs** published the usual and then, for each job that was ready, wrote that job's sheet again laid over the new usual. It took each job from the page as it was loaded (`publishUsual`, `FilledBuilder.tsx`): the plan of who is ready, the job's own part, and the revision to publish from.

A page open since the morning knew nothing a teammate had done since. A teammate who had changed a job's place and not yet published it had a draft waiting. The page still read that job as clean, so it published to it, and the write set the job's `draft` back to its old published copy. The teammate's change was gone, with nothing said. The write named the job's revision, which a saved draft does not move, so nothing refused it.

## The fix

`publishUsualToJobs` (`sheetsIo.ts`) does the batch, and the screen calls it.

- **Each job is read at the press.** The plan is made from the sheets as the database holds them at that moment (`usualPlanFor`). A job with a draft a teammate saved since is a job with changes of its own waiting: held, as it always should have been, and the draft is kept. A job a teammate published since goes out from that newer copy, as its next revision.
- **Each write names the number just read** (`draft_no`). A change that lands between the read and the write is refused, the job is named afterwards with the reason, and the teammate's draft stands.
- **A job this page holds words for is held too.** Words typed here whose save did not land are changes of its own waiting. A sheet that moved on somewhere else, with the choice on its panel not yet made, is held with its own reason (`usualPublishPlan`, a third reason, `moved`).
- **Do what is ready.** The usual goes to every job that is ready, the outcome counts the jobs held, each held job's row says why, a job that fails is named with its reason, and the rest still go. When the jobs cannot be read at all, the outcome says the usual was published and went to no job yet, with the reason. Each job is then behind the usual, and its own publish brings it up.
- **The page takes each job as it now stands**, but for a job it holds words of its own for, which stay as typed.

`FilledBuilder.tsx` belongs to another session tonight. This PR takes the batch's loop out of it and leaves the call and the outcome.

## The count on the press

The press's count is made from what the page knows. When a teammate changed a job since the page loaded, the press can read *Publish to 3 jobs* and go to two, and the outcome says so: *Published to 2 jobs. Held 1.*, and the job's row says why. Reading the jobs again each time the standing sheet is opened would keep the count closer, and is not done here.

## Tests

- `sheetsIo.test.ts`, *the usual, published to every job*, six tests against the stand-in client: a draft a teammate saved since is kept and the job held, a copy a teammate published since is the one that goes out, a draft that moves between the read and the write is refused and named while the rest go, a job the page holds words for is held, and jobs that do not read stop the batch with the reason.
- `sheet.test.ts`: the plan's third reason, and its words as plain words.
- `FilledBuilder.movedOn.render.test.tsx`: the page loads, a teammate's draft arrives, and the press publishes the usual, does not write the job, and lists it as held. This one fails on the old code, which wrote the job.
