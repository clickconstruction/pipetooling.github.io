# v2.5366 — Lien desk: a notice the leader signed with a pen has a record (pen train, PR 1) (2026-10-12)

Taunya's question on 2026-10-12: a packet printed Oct 9, the leader signed the papers with a pen, she mailed them, and Record the mailing held three envelopes as *Unsigned* with no way to say he had already signed the paper. The signing rule (v2.5086) shipped the day after that packet printed, so every packet printed before it sits in the same spot. The plan is `docs/DOCSMIT_LIEN_RUN_PLAN.md`, section *Signed with a pen*; the playable mock is tab 6 of `docs/prototypes/lien-docsmit-prototype.html`.

**The rule.** A signature is the record, not the mark (`lienNoticeSignature.ts`, v2.5077). A pen signature is the leader's own act on the paper as printed; the record names who signed, when, who wrote it down and how they know, and the frame's record ID already on the paper ties the page to the row. It mirrors *Approve on his word*: the office writes it down, the leader can confirm.

**Migration `20261013100000_lien_pen_signature.sql`** (`docs/migrations/20261013100000_lien_pen_signature.md`): `signer_signature_mode` widens to `type | draw | pen`; three nullable columns join the seven — `signed_recorded_by`, `signed_witness_how` (`watched | told | page`) and `signed_leader_confirmed_at`; the guard gains the pen rule (a witness word is required, the recorder is the signed-in user, the device column is cleared, and only the signer himself confirms) and is otherwise as 20261010020000 left it. `database.ts` is regenerated in a follow-up chore once the columns exist in prod.

**Kernel** (`src/lib/jobs/lienNoticeSignature.ts`): `LienSignatureMode` and `LienPenWitnessHow`; the ten columns; `lienSignatureModeOf`, `lienPenWitnessHowOf`, `lienPenWitnessWords`; `LienNoticeSignature.pen` (who recorded it, how, confirmed when); `lienNoticeSignatureFromRow` takes `recordedByName`; the audit line for a pen signature reads *Signed by pen by Robert Douglas on October 9, 2026, on the paper as printed. Recorded by Taunya, who watched him sign.* with *Confirmed by … from his own sign-in on …* once he has; `signedWords` is *Signed by pen October 9, 2026* (no clock: the office types the day). The clearing patch nulls all ten. Six new tests.

**The frame** (`lienFilingDocuments.ts`): `FilingSignature.mode` takes `pen`; the tag reads *Signed by pen*, the mark is the name in plain type (no cursive, no ink), and the line under the frame is `FILING_PEN_LINE` (*The signature is in ink on the paper as mailed; this frame is the record of it.*) in place of the ESIGN statute line, in both the HTML and the PDF renderers.

**Decisions (owner, 2026-10-12):** the photo of the signed page is asked for, not required; *he told me* counts on its own; the leader's confirmation is not needed. PR 2 adds the pane on the run's held line and the notice footer with the help guide section; PR 3 the leader's confirm and the Needs you count.
