# v2.4884 — Help: the billing guides match the Bill tab again (2026-10-07)

PR 3 of the help-guides freshness pass after the lien guides (punch list #91, Helper 10). It follows the start-here guides ([v2.4860](v2.4860.md)) and Track a GC ([v2.4882](v2.4882.md)). Three read-only readers checked the four Bills guides on the card against main's code, plus *choose who gets the bill*, whose leftovers the card also held. Every fix was re-read in the code before it was written. Words only.

## The fixes, by guide

- **turn a non-Stripe bill into a Stripe bill**
  - The bill sits under **② Bills and payments** on the job window's **Bill** tab (`JobFormSegmentsBar.tsx`). **⚡ Make Stripe bill** is an item in the bill row's ⋯, since v2.3478. On a GC bill, the ⋯ is at the end of the waiver row.
  - The window is *Make this a Stripe bill*, with a Billed date row and **⚡ Create Stripe bill** in Stripe purple (`ConvertBillToStripeModal.tsx`). The portal button is **PAY ONLINE** on dark.
  - **Send Email invoice** lives inside **View**. On a Pipeline row it reads *stripe Resend*. The row's own **Email** opens a draft instead (`HostedStripeBillPanel.tsx`, `StripeInvoiceSharePanel.tsx`).
  - The bill is due on receipt. The greyed-out reasons gain *no linked customer* and *no amount*. Unlinking a payment is the payment line's ⋯, since v2.4293.
- **sub labor outstanding**
  - The pay-run day is a dev's, in **Settings → Jobs & billing**, under *Sub portal · pay schedule* (`SubPortalPaySettingsBlock.tsx`). There is no Settings → Sub portal.
  - Ready needs a signed agreement and every bill on the job paid (`subLaborPayRun.ts`).
  - The Pay when example gets these fixes:
    - *customer paid · 2 of 2 bills*
    - **After inspection**
    - an **On hold** row
    - the ledger's full order of piles
    - the filter chips as one neutral pill each
  - The row ⋯ gains **Lien waiver…**, and **Payment…** shows only while money is due. The expanded row's buttons are named. Editing a payment is inside **Edit sheet**. The tile and Pay figures print cents.
- **sort the bank feed**
  - Sorted charges are job costs on the job window's **Costs** tab (v2.3361, `JobCostsTabCharts.tsx`), not the Bill tab. Crew P&L reads no card charges, so it is dropped.
  - Reconciliation checks a **Window** of 3, 6 or 12 months. Each check leaves a receipt under **Last reconciled** (v2.2949). New transactions arrive in about a second, and the half-hourly sync only fills gaps.
  - Drag Sort's other way is **Quick Sort**. Card Review is *read-only except Assign to*, and a Category Review window can fix a label. The button is **Apply rules to transactions**.
  - Moneyfill opens for a dev or a controller, and the Quickfill chip reads *Close week: clear* when the queues empty. The role chip reads **Leader**. Assistants match deposits in **Accounts Receivable**.
- **sort the pipeline by when jobs were added** and **sort the pipeline by percent complete**
  - The Sort group holds four orders since v2.3788. The guide gains *Next scheduled first* and its **⇅ Next first** button. Percent complete sits in the menu, and the header click still switches to it (`jobsStagesSortMode.ts`).
  - Both guides drop primary from their roles, since primaries never reach the Pipeline (`Jobs.tsx`).
- **choose who gets the bill**
  - The guide uses the Edit and Bill tabs, the **Bills** list with its Draft chip, and the payer named on the row's second line. The chip is gone since v2.3478.
  - The Billing email row has its own **Save**, and blank reads *same as email*. *Pays as GC by default* applies whenever that GC is picked on a job still on This customer.
  - Changing **Bills go to** moves an open bill with no payer of its own (`billVisibility.ts`, `gcReviewRollup.ts`). Sent paper never changes.
  - Moving a sent bill a builder asked about is **Send back**, then **Bill to ▾** on the new draft. Bill to ▾ draws on drafts only. A sent bill's sharing is the row's ⋯.
  - Two words the sweep left open are settled. *pretest* gets its plain word, "the water test that checks the pipes for leaks before the foundation is leveled" (`_shared/testReport.ts`). *the portals* drops "signs in to". They open from a private link with no sign-in.
- **reconcile the books against bank statements**: assistants lost Banking in v2.3305, so the roles line drops them.
