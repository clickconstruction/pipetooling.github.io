# v2.5929 — The Bridge: the Build desk behind its card (2026-10-26)

Punch list #192: the owner asked for a desk behind every stage card, one press from the card the way the Collect desk opens since v2.5890. The Build desk is the approved hybrid *Hours in, work out* (`to-dos/bridge-stage-desks/mockups/build-desk.html`, the 2026-10-26 design run): a 14-day per-job ledger of clock hours in against percent movement out, worst divergence first, that explains the Build card's by-hours against by-percents gap row by row. Its siblings ship beside it (Bill v2.5930, Market v2.5931, Sales v2.5932); this PR also amends the Glossary, the page map and the twins' app directory for all four.

## The kernel (`src/lib/bridge/build/buildDesk.ts`)

`buildBuildDesk` takes what the page already holds — the Build reads (`BuildPaceInput`: the open jobs and every percent point the Build card walks), the top line's 13 pay weeks of closed sessions (`VectorSession[]`, counted the way `fieldPace.ts` counts them: approved or still waiting, on a job, not the office, not a bid), the card's two paces and its weeks — plus the desk's own reads once they land. For every open job: `hours` is its counted session hours in the last `BUILD_DESK_DAYS` days, today included; `quietDays` is the days since its newest counted session anywhere in the 13 weeks, or *no hours in 13 weeks* when it has none (never a guessed number); `pctNow` is the job's percent, else its newest point; `pctFrom` is the newest point before the window, else `buildPace.ts`'s rule (zero for a job created in the window, the first in-window point otherwise, so a job with no point in the window moved nothing); `movedUsd` is the change times the contract, `leftUsd` what is left of it. The groups: **moved** when money moved, **ate** when hours went in and nothing moved, **stalled** when no hours went in, **finished** at 100 % (listed last, *Done work is Bill's row now*, a **Bill it ›** door). A job with no price reads *Nh earning zero* whichever group it falls in. The order: ate by hours, stalled by money idle (the largest wears *stalled · biggest money idle*), moved by money moved, finished. The crew chips read the Schedule's blocks over the next `BUILD_DESK_CREW_DAYS` days as (assignee, day) crew-days: *Nobody scheduled, all week* on a job with money left and no block, else *Malachi + 2 · 12 crew-days · Mon to Thu*. A moving row says *expects about N hours more* from `expectedHoursForJob` less the lifetime hours, floored at zero and omitted where it would rest on the half-done guess. The headline, the verdict (a sentence about nothing is dropped), the paces sub-line, the three tiles, each row's why-line, the moved fold's line, the no-% line and the footnote are all written here, and every sentence passes `plainWordsFailures`.

## The reads (`src/lib/bridge/build/loadBuildDesk.ts`)

Two reads the page does not make, on the desk's first open, over the open jobs' ids and chunked: `job_schedule_blocks` (`job_id, work_date, assignee_user_id`) from today through the look-ahead with the assignees' names from `users`, and `loadEarnedJobs` for each job's lifetime approved hours. Read-only; nothing new on the server.

## The desk

`BridgeBuildDesk.tsx` matches the Collect desk's chrome: the label line, the headline (red while Build is the link), one bold verdict, the muted paces line, the three tiles, then one row per open job whose head is a button that opens the why-line, the crew chip and the doors (**Open the job ›**, **Open the Schedule ›** on a stalled row, **Price the job ›** on a no-price row, **Bill it ›** on a finished row, and **Set the % ›** on every priced row, which mounts `FieldPctUpdateModal` the way the percents prompt does and hands the save to the page's `savePct`, so the Build reads patch in place and the desk recomputes). The moved rows past the first three fold under *N more jobs moving, $X · show*. While the desk's reads are in flight the rows area says *Reading the Build desk…* and the header draws from what the page knows; a failed read shows one line and the rows read without the chips. The footnote at the foot names every read. `Bridge.tsx` adds the `build` entry to the cards' `desks` once the Build reads and the sessions are in, holds the reads state after the demand memo and the kernel memo after the stage flow (the paces live there), and renders the desk after the Collect desk.

## The owner's calls, built to defaults

- `BUILD_DESK_DAYS = 14` — the ledger's window and the days a job can go quiet before it reads stalled. The owner can change it.
- `BUILD_DESK_CREW_DAYS = 7` — how far ahead the crew chips read the Schedule, today included. The owner can change it.

No write beyond **Set the %**; the week's call is recorded on the link desk, never here (the design run's cross-desk ruling).

## Verify

Open `/bridge` as the dev. The Build card ends in **Break it down ›**. Press it: the headline reads the hours in and the work out over the last 14 days, the bold line names the jobs that ate hours and the jobs that saw nobody, and the three tiles sum them. The rows open on a press; a stalled row carries **Open the Schedule ›** and a red *Nobody scheduled, all week* chip when the Schedule has nobody on it this week. Press **Set the % ›** on a priced row, save a percent: the row's % and moved figures change at once, the Build card's grey or pile moves with them, and no reload is needed. Esc, the × and **Close** close the desk.
