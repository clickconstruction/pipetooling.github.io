Nothing on a screen yet: the kernels and the strip the board (PR 3) and the superintendent's phone card (PR 4) both draw, so the office and the field read one number.

## What landed

- **`src/lib/jobs/superintendentLine.ts`** — the kernel. `superintendentLine(input)` reads done percent against hours percent of the labor budget into days ahead or behind at the crew day (the mean of the worked days when there are two or more, else `crewHoursPerDay`, 24 h assumed); money leading the work by ten points or more outranks the hours (*Spending N points ahead of the work.*), spend over three times the money budget reads *The money budget looks wrong.*; no percent, under 8 h in or no budget is too early; the proof line stacks the certified percent, the checklist count and the crew's own percent as provisional; the move when behind is the open checklist, else *Set the %*. `laborDaysOf(events)` groups the charge timeline's team-labor events by work date. `targetWords` names who moved the expected hours, from what and why; the day is the company calendar's (`calendarYmdInAppTzFromIso`), where the spike read the browser's.
- **`src/lib/jobs/stageShares.ts`** — `stageSharesOf(lines)`: a staged job's labor budget cut by stage, in-order stages by sequence, then any-time lines, then the lines with hours and no stage folded into one trailing piece whose percent is weighted by hours; fewer than two pieces draws nothing. **Changed from the spike:** each line arrives with its `laborHours` and `laborUsd` already read, so the kernel needs nothing of #234's `lineBudgets.ts`, which is still on PR #6745 and not on `main`; the board reads each line's labor through `calcLineBudget` in PR 3.
- **`src/lib/jobs/expectedHoursRange.ts`** — new, the slider's rule lifted out of the spike's card so PR 3 takes it as a kernel: the floor is the hours in, the ceiling the labor budget's edge, the step one crew day when the job read one; within an hour of the edge the slider is pinned with *Over the labor budget by N h.* or *At the labor budget's edge.* and the lever words; `clampExpectedHours` holds the − / + presses and the typed box to the same range.
- **`src/components/jobMode/PaceStrip.tsx`** — the strip: the track is the labor budget; one amber segment per work date as wide as its hours (red when behind), each a press and a hover target; the days left as ticks at the crew day over the empty rest (skipped under 2% of the track); the work done as a blue 4 px mark with its chip (`+N d`, `−N d`, `on pace`, `money ahead`, `budget?`) and `N d left` at the right while there is room; the money as a thin line, red when it leads; the expected hours as a dashed mark with the rest hatched, only when short of the budget; the stages as pieces with a 12 px hover bar where each ends and `StageEndCard` off the cursor (*End of Rough In · stage 1 of 3 · Budget to reach here 56 h · $2,538 · Rough In alone … · The crew passed here on Oct 22, 80 h in.*, or *N h short of here*, or *No hours on the job yet*; dollars only when every stage carries them); `stageWords` names each piece under the track for the large view; the legend `done N% · hours N% · N days, tap one · N stages, hover one · money N%`. The one neutral literal the theme check caught (a stage's percent in the large view) now reads `var(--text-link)`.
- **`src/components/jobMode/DayDetailsModal.tsx`** — `DayDetailsBody` (the hours and money first, then who clocked and for how long, the subs, what was bought, the report's percent), `DayHoverCard` (pinned beside the pointer, flipping above when low on the screen, taking no clicks) and `DayDetailsModal` (the same in `ResponsiveModalShell` for a phone's tap).
