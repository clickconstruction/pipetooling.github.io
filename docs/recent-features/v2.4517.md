# v2.4517 — Bids: Mark and For someone are two small icons after the title (2026-10-04)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. The owner, on an open bid: "I don't super love how big the mark and for someone is… remove the words and just [leave] the icons… `[icon|icon]` where it looks like two buttons put together and when the user hovers over one or the other, only that one illuminates before it's clicked."

## What changed

- **One control of two icons** after the open bid's title on the nine workflow tabs: the ring (Mark) and the person with a plus (For someone…), joined, one border, a divider between. 66 × 28 px where the two worded buttons took about 150 px.
- **Each half lights on its own**: hover or keyboard focus washes only that half violet. The For someone half stays lit while its form is open.
- **The words moved to the name and the hover line.** The ring's accessible name is *Mark* / *Marked today*; its hover line reads *Marked today. Press to clear the mark.* The person's name is *For someone…*. The ring is filled, on a light wash, when the bid is marked — the visible *Marked · today* text is gone from the title.
- Nothing else about marking changed: the form, the sent status beside the control, the strip under the title.

## How it is built

- [`BidMarkControls.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/BidMarkControls.tsx) — `BidMarkTitlePair` wraps `BidMarkButton` and `BidMarkForSomeone` in a `role="group"`; both triggers are icon-only `bid-mark-pair__btn`s with `aria-label`s.
- [`src/index.css`](../../src/index.css) — `.bid-mark-pair` / `.bid-mark-pair__btn` replace `.bid-mark-toggle--title`. The pair does not clip its children (the form hangs under the right half), so the halves carry the rounded corners.
- [`BidWorkflowTabTitleWithPreview.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/BidWorkflowTabTitleWithPreview.tsx) draws the pair.

## Tests and checks

`BidMarkRequests.render.test.tsx` (+1: the group, the two names, no text on either half, the marked ring's name and hover line); the For someone case passes unchanged (it finds the button by name). Checked in the app on Counts with BP403 open, dark theme: only the hovered half lights, and the form opens under the right half.
