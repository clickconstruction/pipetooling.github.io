# v2.5294 — Contracts: a paper built from clauses is signed as its clauses (2026-10-12)

Cut 4c's screens, PR B (GC Reviews helper 9, directed by the lead session GC Review): the sign page's version and exhibit line, over the clause tables on prod since v2.5269 and the Settings tab of v2.5285. The prototype's `paperHtml`, `signPage` and `doSign` (Messages prototype v1.178 on `spike/gc-mode`). Two edge functions change, and the lead deploys them from the deploy checkout after this merges.

- **The read** (`get-contract-for-signer`): when a copy's library document is built from clauses (live clauses at revision 1 or more, and no form), the response carries `clause_paper: { revision, en, es }`. It comes from `contract_paper_clauses()` for the company's trades, read through the new `_shared/clausePaper.ts` (`clausePaperFor`). The between line is the GC frame's in each language: *Click Construction and Hillside Excavation*, *Click Construction y …*. A person's paper draws none.
- **The page** (`ContractAccept.tsx`):
  - An **English** / **Español** switch.
  - The clauses drawn by `ClausePaper`, with the exhibit heading and the version line, and no second heading under the page's own.
  - The paper's body, when it has one, folded under **The full agreement**.
  - The consent and the sign form's words in Spanish when the paper is read in Spanish (`clauseSign.ts` → `CLAUSE_SIGN_WORDS`).
  - Signing sends `paperRevision` and `paperLang`.
- **The sign** (`accept-contract`): before anything is stored it puts the paper together again. It signs only at the revision the page showed, else `409 paper_changed`: *The paper changed since you opened it. Reload to read the new version.* A library document with no clauses costs one small read and nothing more. When the page showed clauses, a read that fails answers 500 rather than signing without the freeze; a page that showed the body signs as before. The signature's own update writes `paper_revision` and `clause_versions` (the paper with its title and between line), which the trigger keeps as signed. A paper not built from clauses signs exactly as before.
- **The record** (`PersonContractSignedRecordModal`): a copy that kept clauses draws them, with the version line, and folds its body under **The full agreement**. A copy with none draws its body as before.
- **For later** (the next piece, cluster 9's own):
  - A follow-up migration gives `gc_sows` and `gc_owner_contract_sends` a `paper_id` to a library document, so a statement of work and our contract freeze their clauses too. Until then one master agreement per trade in the packets is the bridge.
  - The trade portal's *Your papers* gains the version line. Its read goes through `gc-trade-portal` and the shared `companyPapers` kernel, which is more than a few lines.
