# v2.6996 — Lien notices: a help sheet in front of a notice printed from the Lien window, and the last day to mail on the run's first page (2026-11-12)

Punch list #314, the last of the card: the help sheets for the § 53.056 notice and the § 53.057 retainage notice. The card sketched one cover sheet for each. Read against how a notice goes out today, that is two different jobs, so this is two changes.

## How a notice prints today

- **On the Lien desk's run.** Every approved notice, monthly and retainage, prints in one packet. Its first page is *Today's mail* (`lienRunPaper.ts`, v2.4971): a checklist written to the person at the desk, a divider before each envelope, a foot on every page. A retainage notice prints nowhere else.
- **From a job's Lien window.** The **§ 53.056 notice** tab has a **Print** press for one notice mailed by hand, then **Save & record sends…**. That print had nothing in front of it.

## What changed

- **The Lien window's notice has a cover sheet.** `lienHelpNoticeSheetHtml` (`src/lib/jobs/lienHelpSheet.ts`) is one page in the affidavit sheets' own shell: the striped edge, the band (*Help sheet · do not mail this page*), the job's facts with **Mail by**, what the app checked, the two envelopes with their addresses, and eight steps with a box to tick each. A notice has no notary and no blank, so the sheet has no picture of the paper and no list of blanks.
- **What the app checked.** `lienHelpNoticeChecks` answers four things the app can see: the owner has a mailing address, the original contractor has one, the last day to mail and whether it has passed, and how many invoices print behind the notice. A missing address or a day that has passed is marked `ask`, and the sheet says *Do not mail this one yet* above the reader's own check.
- **The last day to mail.** `lienHelpNoticeMailBy` takes the months the notice names and the property's kind and returns the earliest of their days (`noticeDeadlineForMonth`), since a notice mailed after it is late for that month. The step prints the day in bold. A day that has passed is not printed as a day to mail by.
- **The steps say what the app cannot do.** The window prints one notice, so step 1 says to copy every page for the second set. Step 5 has a line for each number from the green slips, because **Save & record sends…** asks for both.
- **A code to record.** `lienHelpNoticeRecordCode(jobId)` (`lienHelpGuideCode.ts`) draws `lienWindowHref(jobId, 'notice')`. On a phone it opens the job's Lien window on the notice tab. It sits in the corner of the steps box with *Opens the notice tab* under it, as the batch sheet's does. The guide's code keeps the band's corner.
- **The same box turns it off.** **Print the help sheet first** is now under the notice tab's presses too. It is the affidavit's choice (`lienHelpSheetChoice.ts`), kept on the computer. The copy filed on the job is the notice and what is enclosed, never the sheet.
- **Today's mail says by when.** `RunNotice` gained `mailBy`: the desk entry's `earliestDeadline` for a monthly notice and the retainage entry's `deadline` for a retainage notice. Notices combined at one property carry the earliest of their days (`lienNoticeCombine.ts`). `runMailByWords` puts one line in the page's opening words: *Mail every envelope by …* when the run has one day, or *Mail the first envelopes by …* with each row showing its own. A day that has passed is counted and marked in red: *The last day has passed for 2 envelopes. Ask before you mail them.*
- **A code on Today's mail.** `lienRunGuideCode()` opens the notices guide's share page. `runPacketHtml` takes it as its last argument and the run window hands it in.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **No second sheet in the run.** The owner's complaint that made *Today's mail* was a packet of 154 pages for 18 envelopes. A cover sheet per notice would add a page per notice to say what page 1 and the dividers already say. What page 1 did not say was by when, so that is what it gained. The packet's page count is unchanged.
- **The retainage notice's facts.** The card wanted the day our contract ended and counsel's letter named as page 1 of the owner's copy. The page already lists each envelope's documents in order, counsel's letter first. The day our contract ended is on the notice's own reference strip, and the day that follows from it, the last day to mail, is now on the page.
- **No code to record on the run.** The run window has no phone layout, and the page already says to type the numbers at the desk. A code that lands on a screen built for a computer would be a worse door than the sentence.
- **Signing.** The card's step said *sign*. A notice on the run is signed in the app by the job's leader before it prints, so the page does not say it. A notice printed from the Lien window has a blank line, so its sheet does.
