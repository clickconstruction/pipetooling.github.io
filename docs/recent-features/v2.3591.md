**PR 3 — lock pricing after send.** Once `bid_date_sent` is set, every write keyed to the bid's pricing refuses until the bid is being *revised*:

- Kernel [`pricingLock.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/pricingLock.ts) (11 tests): `pricingLockState` → `open | locked | revising`; the revised set lives in `sessionStorage` under `bids_pricing_revise_v1` (`readRevisedBids` / `writeRevisedBid`, junk-tolerant) — a reload keeps it, a new tab or tomorrow does not, and nothing is stored on the bid; `pricingLockedMessage` and `pricingLockChipText` carry the sent day (`Jul 1`, `Sep 22, 2025`).
- [`BidsPricingTab.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/BidsPricingTab.tsx): `guardPricingWrite()` is the first line of the eleven v2.3481 write sites (the brush's arm, stroke and undo; assign and remove an entry; the typed price; the solver's Apply and its typed price; Fill from book; copy prices from a scenario; an entry added straight into the bid's pricing) and of `applyPendingBookOffer` (the v2.2444 *Use $X on this bid* door — the tab's own comment already said a sent bid must not re-price because someone tidied the book). A shared-book edit in the drawer is not a pricing write and is not gated. When locked, the write returns after a warning toast naming the sent date.
- The chip, above the sent-vs-today line: `🔒 Sent Jul 1 · pricing locked` with **Revise…**; while revising, an amber `✎ Revising a bid sent Jul 1` with **Lock again**. Theme tokens throughout.
- Docs: the Workbench guide's *Price from the book* section, `docs/BIDS_SYSTEM.md` → Price Book templates.

Not built: a per-bid stored flag (the plan's other shape). A session-scoped unlock is enough for the ten post-send drifts in the report to become deliberate acts, and it leaves nothing to migrate.
