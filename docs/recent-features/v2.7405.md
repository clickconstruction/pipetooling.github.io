# v2.7405 — Box labels: the page a scan opens is the company's card (2026-11-15)

With the sealed page live (v2.7379, v2.7390), Taunya asked whether it could be better and more professional. Nine drawings beside the page as it was, each change hers, ended at [`to-dos/prts-box-page/company-card.html`](../../to-dos/prts-box-page/company-card.html), and this builds it. No migration. One edge function changed: `box-read`, redeployed after the merge. The link `prts.co/b/<code>` and the code are untouched.

## Why

The page read as a screen from an app. The biggest words on it were the box's name, which means nothing to a GC or another trade, and a line across the top named the address and *Not signed in*. Taunya sees the label on a site as a place to be seen, so the company leads and the box sits right under it.

## The page

- **One navy plate.** The company's mark, name and tagline. Then *Our cage on this site*, the box's name with its kind (*Cage ZZ1*) large, its code beside it, and *Counted and photographed by its crew.* The line that read `prts.co/b/…` and *Not signed in* is gone.
- **Ask for a bid** opens an email to the bids box, which the app reads, with the scanned box in the words. **Call (512) 360-0599** says Call.
- **Moved, damaged or in the way?** opens a sheet: *Let us know. Send us a photo and we will work to make it right.*, **Send a text**, **Send an email**, *Not now*. A line in Spanish sits under the row.
- **Work with us** (*Always hiring motivated teammates.*) opens the same sheet under its own words.
- **Sign in to see what is inside** stands on its own in an orange outline: *For Click's crew. You stay signed in.*
- The website and the company's email at the foot.

The two presses sit side by side on a phone 390 wide and one over the other on a narrower one, so the words never wrap inside a press. Before this, *Ask for a bid* wrapped onto two lines at that width.

## The function

`box-read` answers `office: { phone, email?, bidsEmail? }`. `email` is the Email of Settings' company block, where the two sheets' emails go. `bidsEmail` is the bids box's address from Settings → Phone and texts, where a bid ask lands in the app. Both come from the one settings read the number already made. An address Settings does not hold, or one a mail link cannot take (`boxPageEmail`), is left out and the press that needs it is not drawn. It still reads nothing a box holds, and `&find=` and `&photo=` still answer 401.

## The kernel

`boxPageSheet` builds a row's sheet: its words and the two ways, each null when the office has no number or address for it. `boxPageBidHref` picks the bid's way: the bids box, then the company's email, then a text. `boxPageMailHref`, `boxPageOursWords`, `boxPageNameWords` and `boxPageKnowLine` are the words. `boxPageAddressWords` and `boxPageSiteWords` went with the lines they drew.

## The order

Either order works. A page that loads before the function is deployed gets the number alone: the bid is a text as it was, the sheets offer the text only, and the foot gives the website. A phone holding a build from before this version reads the new answer as it read the old one. `box-read` is deployed right after the merge and the page proved signed out on a ZZ Test box.
