# v2.8207 — Permits, Market: an answered name's reason ends with one full stop (2026-11-21)

Punch list #405 ([the card](../../to-dos/permits-market-page/README.md)). Seen while drawing Market's values in place (v2.8149) and left on the card: **an answered name's line can end a reason with two full stops.** Taunya said to build it on 2026-11-21.

## What was wrong

Under **Answered** on Market, each name has a line: what was said, the reason, who said it and when, and until when it stays off the list. A pursue keeps its reason with a full stop, *Pursued for this job.* The line added its own after the reason, so it read *Pursuing, Pursued for this job.. Eve, Nov 19.*

## The fix

`standingWords` in `permitCallList.ts` takes the full stops and spaces off the end of a reason before it writes the line, then ends the sentence once.

- A reason kept with a full stop, with none, or with several reads the same: one full stop.
- A reason that ends on a question mark or an exclamation mark keeps it, and the line adds no full stop after it.
- A reason that is only full stops is no reason, and the line reads as it does with none.

Nothing stored changed. The reason is kept as it was typed or stocked. Only the line's words changed.

## Tests

- `permitCallList.test.ts` holds the cases above.
- `PermitCallList.render.test.tsx` had the two full stops in what it expected of the line. It expects one.

Client only. No migration.

## The card

The line is marked fixed.
