# v2.4211 — The alternate gets an answer: accepted alternates on the win (2026-09-30)

Alternates round two, PR 2 (PR 1 is the migration `20260930003000_bids_accepted_alternate_tags.sql`, [doc](../migrations/20260930003000_bids_accepted_alternate_tags.md)). The review after round one: the customer says yes or no to the alternate and the bid had nowhere to put it — the value stayed the base, the job's budget snapshotted the base, and the alternate's rows stayed in every downstream number either way. Mock-up (the seven gaps, before / after): artifact `XxRk2ZykkPBeWMZwLZsCzF`.

## What changed

- **The kernel** — [`alternateAcceptance.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/alternateAcceptance.ts): `offeredAlternates(bid)` (each alternate with the add-on the letter last stamped and whether it was taken), `agreedValueFromAcceptance` (the sent base plus the accepted add-ons), `isDeclinedRow` / **`jobScopeRows(rows, bid)`** (every row until the win, then the base plus the accepted alternates), `acceptanceWords`.
- **Edit Bid → Won** gets a checklist, *Which alternates did they take?* — one box per offered alternate with its add-on; ticking writes `accepted_alternate_tags` (the form's new value, cleared unless the outcome is won or started) and fills **Agreed value** with the sent base plus the accepted add-ons; the sent value stays.
- **A per-GC packet marked Won** asks the same, one yes / no per alternate, then writes the list and the agreed value ([`acceptedAlternatesWrite.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/acceptedAlternatesWrite.ts)).
- **The Bid Board** shows the agreed value once a won bid's customer took an alternate, with a green *alt taken* chip (hover: agreed vs sent); a won bid without one reads *alt declined*.
- **Takeoffs**: a declined alternate's rows stay on the sheet with a grey *ALT · declined* mark and leave *Materials on this bid*, the book fill and the schedule of values. **Labor**: the sync minds the job's rows, so a declined alternate's labor rows go and the hours are the job's. **Pricing**: the declined rows wear the grey mark; prices stay as sent.

## Decided here

- **The answer is one fact through two doors.** The Won dialog and the packet's Won write the same two fields; the bid room's signature will write them too (round two PR 3), and when the room signed first the dialog shows its answer already ticked.
- **Declined rows stay on the bid**, out of the job. A customer who comes back for the break room later is common; deleting the rows would lose the price they were quoted.
- **The server-side snapshots** (`snapshot_job_budget_from_bid`, `bid_estimate_breakdown`) still sum every takeoff line; the labor side is right through the sync, the materials side follows in round two's next migration.
