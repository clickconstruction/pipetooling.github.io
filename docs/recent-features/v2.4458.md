# v2.4458 — Sub portal: a stage, a progress report and a signature read their own day (2026-10-02)

One of the instant-day fixes that follow [v2.4452](v2.4452.md). No migration. [`_shared/subPortalStatement.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/subPortalStatement.ts) is bundled by `sub-portal` and `submit-sub-portal`; both are redeployed after merge.

## What was wrong

Three days on a sub's job card were cut out of a `timestamptz` by its first ten characters, so anything after 7 pm CDT (6 pm CST) read as the next day:

- `stageChangedOn` from `people_labor_jobs.stage_changed_at` (`buildSubSheets`);
- `progress.on` from `people_labor_jobs.progress_at` (`buildSubSheets`);
- `agreement.signedOn` from `step_commitments.signed_at`, else `accepted_at` (`attachSheetAgreements`).

## What changed

All three read through `ymdInAppTzFromIso`, the file's own wrapper of `todayYmdInAppTz` that the payment trace lines have used since v2.3625 ("an evening move is not tomorrow's"). No new helper.

Every writer stamps a real instant: `now()` in the migrations' RPCs, `new Date().toISOString()` in `submit-sub-portal` and the office's sheet form. Of the two `signed_at` columns in play, `step_commitments.signed_at` is a `timestamptz`; `person_contract_documents.signed_at` (the sub's documents) is a `date`, and its reader keeps the day as stored. A sweep of everything `sub-portal` and `submit-sub-portal` bundle, and of the client's sub portal page, found no other instant read as a UTC day: the other slices are day arithmetic on UTC-midnight dates.

## Tests

`subPortalStatement.test.ts` (+3): a stage moved and a progress report sent at `2026-10-03T00:30:00Z` (7:30 pm CDT, also in PostgREST's `+00:00` shape) read Oct 2; an order signed, or accepted with no signature, then reads Oct 2; 6:30 pm CST in December reads Dec 1; noon UTC reads its own day; a missing time reads none. All three fail on main's kernel. The 13 sub portal suites pass (82 tests).

## Checked against prod

Read only, as the dev account: none of the 81 sheets it can see carries a stage or progress time, and it reads no signed sheet order (`step_commitments` returns no rows to it, which may be its row access). Nothing visible reads differently today.
