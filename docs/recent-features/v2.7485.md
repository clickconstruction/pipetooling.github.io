# v2.7485 — Lien desk: one property whole, the kernel (2026-11-16)

PR 1 of 3. The kernel and the rows it needs; no screen yet.

## Why

An office teammate opened an owner's envelope in the run window. It held one job, at a property with seven jobs on the desk, and she could not tell why. The envelope was right: six of the seven had their notice out already. The desk thinks by paper and by job, and no view shows one property whole. The owner asked for one (2026-11-16) and picked the shape: a property card first, then a *Group by property* picker on By job, with one quiet line for an owner's other property.

## What the live desk showed

A read-only count of the desk's jobs by property record decided the design. A handful of records carry several jobs and most carry one. Almost every job has a record. Owners rarely hold two properties, so there is no owner view.

So the client can group what it already reads. No table, no RPC, no migration.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/jobs/lienPropertyPapers.ts`** (new, pure, 24 tests). It holds no rule of its own for a paper's state:
  - `buildLienProperties(rows)` gathers By job's book by `jobs_ledger.customer_address_id`, never by a typed address. The jobs with no record gather under the key `''`. `lienPropertyOf` and `lienPropertyOfJob` find one.
  - `lienPropertyNoticeLine` and `lienPropertyAffidavitLine` say where one job's papers stand: the desk's pile in the Board's words (`lienBoardPileWords`), the day a notice went in the To do row's words (`lienAffidavitNoticeTag`), a filed affidavit and its served copy from the timeline's own steps. A sub job with nothing on record reads *No notice on record*; no reason is guessed.
  - `lienPropertyTally` is the property's one line. Since v2.7488 `lienPropertyTallies` counts each kind of paper apart: *4 notices sent Oct 14 · 2 on Sep 22 · 1 to print and mail*, and *6 lien affidavits ready to file*.
  - `lienNotInRunGroups` and `lienNotInRunWords` said why a property's other jobs are not in a run. They left in v2.7488 before any caller: the run window's envelope line hands the tally the jobs that are not in the envelope, so there is one wording.
  - `sameOwner` names an owner's other property only when the owner's name and mailing address both match.
- **`lienBoard.ts`**: `lienBoardPileWords(kind, pile)`, a pile's name as the Board says it, for a reader that holds a desk entry and no Board.
- **`lienTimelineBook.ts`**: each row keeps what the build already found and dropped: `notice` and `affidavit` (the desk's queue entries) and `noticeSentOn` (the owner sends on the job's recorded notices). `LienBookJob` carries `addressId`, `propertyAddress`, `ownerMailingAddress` and `publicStanding`, filled in `lienTimelineBookAssemble.ts`.
- **`useLienTimelineBook.ts`**: the filings read takes `notice_53_056` beside the affidavits and releases. The same trip, a few more rows. The firm's portal hands no sends, and the property line falls back to the months' own send days there.
