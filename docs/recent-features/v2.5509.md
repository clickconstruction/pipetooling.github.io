# v2.5509 — Hours: a salaried person who clocks in before their start keeps that punch as their day (2026-10-13)

The owner's case of 2026-10-13: Malachi, on a continuous 8:00 workday template, clocked in by hand at 7:10 with *Run copper test*; at 8:00 the salary sync opened the day's template row beside it, both ran open all day, and the team strip summed them, 16.75 h with the **Overlap** chip. Migration `20261014200000_salary_sync_continuous_open_punch_guard.sql` (`docs/migrations/20261014200000_salary_sync_continuous_open_punch_guard.md`); **db push** once merged. No edge function, no screen.

- **The cause**: `salary_sync_one_user_clock_sessions`' continuous branch guarded its canonical insert only against indexed `salary_schedule` children, never against an open or finished hand punch. The split branch has had the half-open any-origin check since `20270403180000`; only continuous templates could double up. `SALARY_CLOCK_SESSIONS.md` said "if nothing is open", which the body never did.
- **The change**: the continuous branch skips the canonical NULL-index row, inside the window and at catch-up, while any non-rejected, non-revoked session of the user's overlaps `[t_start, t_end)` in half-open form (`clocked_in_at < t_end AND (clocked_out_at IS NULL OR t_start < clocked_out_at)`, on the day by `work_date` or by the clock-in's civil date in the template's zone), an open one counting whatever `p_now` is, since the :00 cron can tick in the same second as the start. The early punch is the day's row. A punch that ended at or before the start does not block, a day with no punch opens and closes as before, and an indexed child still blocks the canonical row. The body is otherwise `20260922001000`'s.
- **What does not change**: the sync still never closes a punch, so an early bird who forgets to clock out runs to the 23:59 CT net (`auto_clock_out_open_sessions_eod`), one row now, not two. Payroll for a flagged salaried person stays on the flat 8/0 rule; costing reads the punch. A punch that starts *after* the sync already opened the day's row still overlaps it, as before; that comes from the punch button showing for a templated person whose pay config does not flag them salaried (`self_salary_clock_state` reads `is_salary && has_template`), a pay-config fix rather than a sync one. Days already doubled before the push stay until someone fixes them in the day editor.
