# v2.5730 — Robots: the heartbeat says where the robot is, in words (2026-10-22)

Robot rounds, PR 1 of 7 (punch list #142, `to-dos/robot-rounds/`). The owner's ask: a loop on his home Mac Mini that watches the app and works the robots' queues, and a signal every five minutes so a person sees what is happening before it is done. The signal first. The `heartbeat` verb existed (v2.2483: bid, stage, state, a note, at start, block and done, shown as PULSE chips to devs); this PR gives it a shape a person can read, a cadence, the key that sent it, and the two switches the later PRs press.

- **Migration `20261022052738_twin_heartbeat_progress`**: `twin_runs.state` admits `idle`; `twin_runs` gains `credential_id`, `progress jsonb` and `is_round`, with two partial indexes; `twin_credentials` gains `last_seen_at`, `stopped_at` and `stopped_by`. Additive. **Push first, then deploy `twin-mcp`.**
- **`supabase/functions/_shared/twinHeartbeat.ts`** (one copy, tested from `src/lib/twins/twinHeartbeat.test.ts`): the four states, the seven steps (`picked_up` … `questions`) and their words (*Picked it up*, *Plans filed*, *Reading the sheets*, *Counting*, *Pricing*, *Sealed*, *Questions for you*), `normalizeHeartbeatProgress` (known keys, trimmed and capped, an unknown step dropped), `heartbeatWords` (*Reading the sheets, 12 of 31 sheets.*, *Waiting on you: the plans.*, *Nothing to do. 88 rounds found nothing.*) and `heartbeatDetailWords` (*Wrote: … Next: …*). The words pass `plainWordsFailures` under test.
- **twin-mcp v1.5.0**: `heartbeat` takes `progress` (step, read, wrote, waiting_on, next) and `round`; its description carries the five-minute rule. On a sealed shadow the note and every string in the shape go through `scrubMoney`, the questions' rule (a failed read scrubs anyway). Consecutive `idle` heartbeats from one key collapse into one row whose `ended_at` moves and whose `progress.rounds` counts the stretch. The reply is JSON with `orders`: `carry on`; `stop` when the key's `stopped_at` is set and the caller said `round: true` (the stop reaches rounds only, never a Code session a person started); `leave bNNN` when the shell's human bid carries `robot_opt_out` (a person pressed *Leave this one to me*). `resolveTwin` stamps `twin_credentials.last_seen_at` on every verb, at most once a minute, swallowed before the push. `get_work_state.latest_heartbeat` carries `progress`.
- **The ledger** (`twinConsoleDisplay.ts`, `TwinRunsLedger.tsx`): `describeTwinRun` takes the row's shape and prints the same sentence the bid will, then *Wrote* and *Next*, then the free note; an old row reads as before; an idle row wears a ROUND chip. The ledger selects the new columns.
- **Not in this PR**: the bar on the bid, the ring on the icon and the reader estimators can call (PR 2); the Console's status rows and the Stop press (PR 3); the Dashboard's silence items (PR 4); the rounds kickoff and the five-minute rule in the skill (PR 5).
