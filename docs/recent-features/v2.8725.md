# v2.8725 — The Bridge: the pipe's steps are sized straight to their dollars (2026-11-27)

Punch list #318, call 6 (`to-dos/market-sales-won-rates/README.md`). The call asked whether the pipe's widths should be sized straight to the dollars, so the five steps look less alike. The card's pick was no, since lighting the narrowest step (#321) answers the same worry. Taunya, 2026-11-27, asked *why not?*, read the reasons, and answered: *Size to dollars*.

## The change

- **A step's width is its dollars a week over the most any step moves** (`stripThick` in `src/lib/bridge/flowStrip.ts`). A step that moves half the dollars of the widest is half as wide, and a quarter is a quarter.
- **Before**, the width was 0.3 plus 0.7 times the square root of that share: the way a pipe's bore goes with its flow, with a floor. A step moving a quarter of the widest drew at 0.65 of its width, so the five steps read much alike.
- **The floor is 0.08**, down from 0.3 (`STRIP_MIN_THICK`). A step that moves next to nothing, or does not read, is still a line of pipe.
- **One rule in one place.** The page's strip (`buildFlowStrip`) and the strip with Market at its worth (`withMarketWorth` in `flowStripDown.ts`) both call `stripThick`. They each wrote the formula out before.
- **Not changed:** the words under each step, which sit under the pipe and not in it, so a thin step keeps its words; the blocks of waiting work; the lit step; which step is the narrowest.
