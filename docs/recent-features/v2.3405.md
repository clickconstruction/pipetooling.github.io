# v2.3405 — The Lien desk: § 53.056 notices due per unpaid work month, drafted by the office, approved by the leader or sent on his word (2026-09-14)

Migration `20260914180000_lien_desk.sql` ([`docs/migrations/20260914180000_lien_desk.md`](../migrations/20260914180000_lien_desk.md)); no edge function. First of the train in [`to-dos/lien-desk/`](../../to-dos/lien-desk/README.md) (the plan of record and the mock-ups; the run and the affidavit kind follow).

## Why

Owner, 2026-09-14: "now that we know what days people were working on bills that have not been paid, it seems logical for us to make it as easy as possible to send liens … a notice on an office member's dashboard that a lien is due … an assistant drafts and then it goes to the master unless they select something to bypass the leader's click approval because the leader instructed them orally … its own modal with multiple entry points." Texas Property Code ch. 53 counts from the month labor was furnished: a sub job needs one § 53.056 notice per unpaid month (15th of the 3rd month after; 2nd residential). v2.3400 put that per-month state on the forecast; the Lien window and the Dashboard card still keyed on the last work month and opened a per-job window nobody visited in time. Live that morning: six sub jobs with June's notice due the next day, $99,088 open, none with an owner of record on file.

## What shipped

- **Kernel [`lienDesk.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDesk.ts)** (+10 tests): `buildLienDeskQueue` folds the RPC rows and the stored items into one entry per job — due months, missed months, the earliest deadline, severity (red ≤ 7d, amber ≤ 14d), the GC's standing rule, and the pile (`needs_owner` · `to_draft` · `awaiting` · `ready` · `held` · `sent` (30 days) · `missed`); `submitOutcome` (a "send" rule approves on submit, "hold" parks with a re-ask date, and a live promise always comes back to the leader with the reason — first notice to this GC, held before, no rule); `holdUntilFor` (the promise date, or three days before the deadline, never earlier than today); `summarizeLienDeskForNeedsYou`.
- **[`lienNoticeDraft.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienNoticeDraft.ts)**: the § 53.056 fields from the job (the Lien window's own recipe), `describeNoticeMonths` ("June, July and August 2026"), the cover note, and the draft's stored shape. **[`lienDeskIo.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDeskIo.ts)**: every write — save draft, submit, send on the leader's word, approve, hold, pull back, skip, mark sent, set the standing rule, and `syncLienDeskAfterRecord` (a recorded notice naming every month the item carries sends the item).
- **Hook [`useLienDeskData`](../../src/hooks/useLienDeskData.ts)**: the RPC + items (light, for the counts) and, while the desk is open, the jobs, the GCs with their rule and email, the property records and owner overrides, the promises, and which GCs we have noticed or held before.
- **[`LienDeskModal`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskModal.tsx)** (+4 render cases): pile pills, the list (severity dot, job · GC, dollars, the deadline chip, the months, the state line), the pane — *Before it can go out* (owner of record with **Find the owner ›** into Edit Job → Property record, the GC and its address, property kind, the months with approved hours), *Months this notice names* (checkboxes; one notice may carry several; the crew's hours per month from the v2.3400 kernel), the document (the same `buildLienNoticeBlocks` the Lien window prints, in a light frame), *Send* (recipients, the courtesy email, the cover note) — and the footer by state and role: the office's **Send for approval ▸** (with the sentence saying where it goes and why), **The leader said to send it ▸** (who / when / phone · in person · text), **Skip these months…** (the forfeit named, a reason kept), the leader's *What you're deciding* (open with the GC, their word, months and hours, what a hold costs), **Approve & next ▸**, **Hold — they promised…** / **Hold — I'll call first**, the standing rule radios, **Not what I said** on a spoken-word send, and *Sent on your word* in the header.
- **Doors**: the Dashboard's Needs You keys `lien-notice-draft` (office; red inside a week) and `lien-notice-approve` (master / dev; "Decide") — replacing the per-job `lien-notice-window` card — and Quickfill's twin, both opening `?tab=stages&liendesk=1`; the ⏱ **Lien desk** button beside ⚖ Legal on the Collections header (with the count); a **Collections** group in the section hamburger (`lien-desk`); a row at the top of the Pipeline tools ⋯; the forecast's **Send notice…** now opens the desk on that job; `openLienDesk` on the Stages tab's handle.
- **The Lien window** gains `noticeMonths`: a notice sent from the desk records `months_covered` per month it named (the last-month-only bug for desk sends), and the reference strip reads *Work months 2026-06, 2026-07, 2026-08*. Recording it moves the desk item to *Sent*.
- Guides: new *send lien notices from the Lien desk*; *file a lien and never miss its deadlines* and *see when a customer will pay* point at it. `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` (the approve / spoken-word split), `GLOSSARY.md` (Lien desk, standing rule).

## Decisions taken as proposed (the owner can flip)

30-day lead time; a new GC asks each time; the cover note on by default; the spoken-word send for assistant and controller (a master approves outright); held items re-ask three days before the deadline; a job with a GC set is a sub job (a builder entered as the customer with no GC stays direct — the forecast panel prompts to set the GC).
