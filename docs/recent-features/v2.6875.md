# v2.6875 — A bid in Austin shows what the city has on its address (punch list #307, PR 8) (2026-11-10)

About 15% of recent bid sites are in Austin. Its open-data feed names the contractor, with a trade and a phone, which San Antonio's does not.

## What changed

- **`supabase/functions/_shared/permitFeed.ts`**: `austinPageUrl` (Socrata, the issued construction permits, `permittype in ('BP','PP')`, by issue day, with an end on a read back) and `permitRowFromAustin`. A building permit keeps its work class in our words (*New commercial*, *Commercial remodel*, *New house*). Austin has one plumbing type, so `austinPlumbingType` tells an irrigation or a fire-line permit from the building's plumbing by the work class, in the words `plumbingTypeOf` reads. The contact is *Person, Company* from the contractor, the applicant when no contractor is named, so `builder_key` is the company. The declared value, the area, the point and the applied and issued days come across; the description stands in for a project name; the phone stays in `raw`.
- **`supabase/functions/permit-pull`**: the run reads a list of feeds (`FeedSpec`): San Antonio's two and Austin's one, each with its own page and row. Austin's rows land under city `Austin`, source `atx-issued`, and its runs in `permit_pulls`.
- **`src/lib/research/bidSite.ts`**: `canonOfStreet` drops a unit on the feed's side too (Austin writes *UNIT 143* and *BLDG 3 UNIT 347* on the street), and a service road written *SVRD SB* is read as the road itself.
- **`BID_PERMIT_CITIES`** (`permitResearch.ts`): San Antonio and Austin, the cities a bid's address is looked up in. `RESEARCH_CITIES`, the Permits tab's own, stays San Antonio: Austin has no skim.
- **`BidGcPermits`**: one chip per city with the day its records start. **`BidGcTiedNames`** reads a name's permits in every city and claims no span across two.
- Guide `see-what-the-city-has-on-a-bids-address`, `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` (permit-pull), `docs/PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`.

## Tests

`permitFeed.test.ts` (the page, a commercial building permit, the plumbing types, a house, the applicant's fallback, the other trades skipped), `bidSite.test.ts` (five Austin addresses against the feed's forms), the panels' render test (both cities named).

## Release order

Merge, then `supabase functions deploy permit-pull`. No migration. The first run reads Austin's last 75 days, at night or on Pull now.
