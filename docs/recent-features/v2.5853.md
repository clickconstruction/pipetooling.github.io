## Two rules the live data settled

Opened on prod the day it was built, the desk read SpaceX's $103k at the commercial 12 days, late since Oct 6: the office had never saved a pay application for it, only marked the bill billed by hand, so decision 1's pay-application test found nothing. A commercial payer's hand-marked bill is how a pay application is recorded today, so it reads as a GC too (`gcByHandMarked`, on by default, in the footnote). And the GC pace over every GC on record read 10 days, pulled down by small GCs paying $250 post tests in a day; it now rests on the GC payers in the pile, the ones we are collecting from now (`gcPaceFromIds`): 30 days from nine GCs on 2026-10-25, so SpaceX reads due Oct 24.
