# v2.7389 — Jobs → Subs: a teammate's own sheet is owed (PR 3 of the sub labor train) (2026-11-15)

Client only, no migration. Punch list #346, on the kernels of v2.7383. Taunya's pick of 2026-11-15: a sheet that names one teammate and no sub is "owed to the teamate". Jobs → Subs read such a sheet as *Payroll*, owed to nobody, and no payroll code reads a sheet, so its open money was in no figure. This change moves the Pay view, the Ledger and the payment window onto her rule. People → Subs moved in v2.7388.

## What landed

- **The Pay view** (`JobsSubLaborTab.tsx`). The row builder asks `sheetOwedTo` whose the sheet is. A sheet with one teammate and no sub is no longer `crew`: it counts in *owed*, groups under the teammate with the *Team · sub labor* chip, and its *Pay when* comes from `teamSheetPayWhen`, so it is Ready unless held or dated. The first tile reads *Owed for sub labor* and counts people when a teammate is owed, and the ready tile's words name a teammate's own sheet. *What we owe ›* on a person's group opens their row on People → Subs.
- **The pay run** (`subLaborPayRun.ts`, + 2 tests): `PayRunSheet.team` and `PayRunSub.team`, true for a row whose every sheet is a teammate's own.
- **The Ledger** (`SubPaymentsLedger.tsx`, `SubLedgerBySub.tsx`): `crew` is now "owed to nobody" (`sheetOwedToNobody`), so a teammate's own sheet has a balance, and it wears *team* where it wore *crew*, on a line, in the Paid to picker and on a statement's head.
- **Sendings** (`sendingSheets.ts`, + a new test file, 4): `sendingSheetsFrom` takes the rule. A teammate's sheets keep their key, so they share a sending and can hold a credit.
- Guides `record-sub-labor-on-a-job`, `sub-labor-outstanding`, `see-every-sub-payment-by-date` and `assemble-a-sub-work-order` no longer say a sheet with a teammate on it pays through payroll.

## One copy, in part

v2.7383 said the Pay view would move onto `sheetStanding` here. It moves onto the two rules that decide the money, `sheetOwedTo` and `teamSheetPayWhen`, and keeps its own row builder, because a row also needs the rail, the next move and the stage view that `sheetStanding` does not hold. That sentence in the v2.7383 fragment is amended.

## Left as it is

The *Crew pay* filter chip stays. It now matches only a sheet with no name on it. The dev seat's `sheetSentTo` and Banking's `bankSideSheets` still read the old way and move in the next change, with a `dev-mcp` deploy.

## Tests

The Pay view's smoke (9), the Ledger's (16 and 7) and the payment window's (12) pass unchanged. New: two pay-run tests and four sending tests for the rule. No smoke presses the Pay view to read the *Team · sub labor* chip.
