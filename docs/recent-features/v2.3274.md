# v2.3274 — Robot price matrix, PR 4 of 5: the compare grid reads kits, options and the robot's picks (2026-09-11)

Client only; no migration (the columns landed with v2.3263). Plan: [`docs/PRICE_MATRIX_PLAN.md`](../PRICE_MATRIX_PLAN.md); canvas boards 3 and 4.

## Why

PR 3's pricer writes structured quotes — kit subtotals with unpriced components, a carrier from the second sheet attached to its fixture, size lists as option groups, a reason on every pick. Until now the compare grid showed those as a pile of same-named lines with the first one's price. This PR makes the grid Wendi already uses read them honestly, and makes her corrections teach the robot.

## What shipped ([`QuoteCompareModal`](../../src/components/bids/QuoteCompareModal.tsx))

- **Kit rows.** A fixture priced as a kit shows one $/each (kit subtotal + separately priced parts, via `aggregateKit`) and a ▸ that expands it into one sub-row per part — role, the vendor's description, each house's price or *in kit*, the page it came from, and *not quoted* where a house has no such part. The cell's title spells the basis ("$1,010.00 kit + carrier $338.41").
- **Incomplete kits.** A house that skipped a part another house priced reads *incomplete · missing carrier* and cannot be picked or starred — the honesty rule from the RFQ plan extended to kits.
- **Option groups.** A cell whose lines are alternatives with none chosen reads *needs a choice* with the price range; the row tints amber and the **Settle strip** under the grid lists every such row. Tapping it opens a chooser (label + price per option); the choice writes `option_chosen` on that line (the others cleared) and a `fixture_component_corrections` row (`choose_option`). The row then prices normally.
- **The Robot column** (only when a robot has worked the bid) says why each pick was made, *needs your choice*, *no complete kit quoted*, or *you changed this · robot had: …* when the estimator overruled it. The footer adds *robot picked N, you changed M*.
- **The banner** when robot quotes exist: how many quotes it read, rows picked of total, rows to settle, incomplete kits, the request's summary, and the houses whose quotes were already expired when read.
- **Picks are kit-aware.** Picking a structured cell picks every line of that kit together and stamps `pick_source = 'human'`; unpicking a robot pick keeps its reason on the lines (so the column can say *you changed this*) and records `unpick` / `repick` corrections with the request id and who did it. Lots keep their atomic toggle.
- **Client-ahead tolerance:** the modal selects the kit columns first and falls back to the legacy line shape if PostgREST refuses them.
- **Kernel** [`compareRobotSummary.ts`](../../src/lib/rfq/compareRobotSummary.ts) (4 tests): robot vs human picks, overrules, rows needing a choice, incomplete cells, the Robot column text, range and missing-role words. `quoteKits` / `quoteCompare` carry `pickSource` through.
- **Render smokes:** the NWS shape — kit $/each with ★, the other house incomplete with *missing carrier*, expand shows the carrier price and *in kit*; *needs a choice* with the range, Settle writes `option_chosen` + a correction; unpicking the robot's kit clears all three lines and records the overrule.
- **Help:** *get supply house prices on a bid* gains **Kits, options, and the robot's picks** with the RPZ example.
