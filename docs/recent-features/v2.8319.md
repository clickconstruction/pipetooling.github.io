# v2.8319 — Every write-down of a bill shows on the job's activity (2026-11-22)

Punch list #85, the follow-up to v2.8305. That build found the job's activity held a bill's first write-down only, and the owner said to log each one. One migration, pushed after the merge. No client change and no edge function change.

## Before

- The invoice trigger `jobs_ledger_invoices_to_activity` wrote its `invoice_write_down` line when `agreed_write_down_at` went from empty to set, and a guard allowed one such line a bill.
- A second write-down wrote no line. The bill's own columns hold the newest write-down's note, time and person, so from the third write-down on the steps in between were kept nowhere.
- A write-down on a Stripe bill runs as the service role, so its line had nobody on it. On prod three of the four written-down bills are Stripe's, and all five write-down lines have nobody on them.

## What changed

- **A line for every write-down** (`20261123001000_write_down_activity_each_step.sql`): the trigger writes one whenever the stamp is new or has moved. Each writer stamps `now()`, so each write-down is its own step. The guard is one line a bill a stamp, so a replayed save writes nothing twice.
- **The line is the step's own.** Its *before* is the amount the row held, not the kept first amount, so a second step reads `$450.00 → $400.00`. `detail` carries the step in numbers: `previous_amount`, `amount`, and `first_amount`, the amount before the bill's first write-down. A first write-down's line reads as it always did.
- **The person.** The line takes the signed-in person, else `agreed_write_down_by`, the way the send line took `sent_by_user_id` in v2.3711. A person with no users row leaves the line with nobody and the write-down still goes through.
- **The bed** grows a second scenario, `30_each_step.sql`. It commits, since a write-down must be a transaction of its own for its stamp to move. Three write-downs on each writer leave three lines, and a replayed stamp or a fee leaves none.

## What it leaves alone

- The triggers themselves, the created, billed and sent lines, and every reader. The feed keys each line by its row, so several lines for one bill draw as several lines.
- Old lines. No bill on prod had been written down twice, so each written-down bill already has its one line and nothing is backfilled. The five lines already written keep nobody on them; four of their bills name the person, and putting them on the lines is a data fix for the owner to run, not part of this migration.
- No table of write-downs, as the owner directed.

## Deploy

Merge, then `supabase db push`. Nothing reads the new `detail` keys yet, so the push is safe on either side of the client deploy. Docker is not available on the build Mac, so CI's `sql-beds` is the whole-schema proof; both scenarios passed first on a local Postgres 15 over stubs.
