# v2.8319 — Every write-down of a bill shows on the job's activity (2026-11-23)

Punch list #85, the follow-up to v2.8305. That build found the job's activity held a bill's first write-down only, and the owner said to log each one. One migration, pushed after the merge. No client code changes and no edge function changes.

## Before

- The invoice trigger `jobs_ledger_invoices_to_activity` wrote its `invoice_write_down` line when `agreed_write_down_at` went from empty to set, and a guard allowed one such line a bill.
- A second write-down wrote no line. The bill's own columns hold the newest write-down's note, time and person, so from the third write-down on the steps in between were kept nowhere.
- A write-down on a Stripe bill runs as the service role, so its line had nobody on it and the feed read *System*.

## What changed

- **A line for every write-down** (`20261123080500_write_down_activity_each_step.sql`): the trigger writes one whenever the stamp is new or has moved. Each writer stamps `now()`, so each write-down is its own step. The guard is one line a bill a stamp, so a replayed save writes nothing twice.
- **The line is the step's own.** Its *before* is the amount the row held, not the kept first amount, so a second step reads `$450.00 → $400.00`. A write-down after a fee starts from the amount the fee left. One made at the amount the bill already stands at is still a line, with that amount on both sides and its new note, since the bill's own note is replaced by it. `detail` carries the step in numbers: `previous_amount`, `amount`, and `first_amount`, the amount before the bill's first write-down. A first write-down's line reads as it always did.
- **The person.** The line takes the signed-in person, else `agreed_write_down_by`, the way the send line took `sent_by_user_id` in v2.3711. A person with no users row leaves the line with nobody and the write-down still goes through.
- **The bed** grows a second scenario, `30_each_step.sql`. It commits, since a write-down must be a transaction of its own for its stamp to move. Five write-downs through the in-app writer and three through the Stripe writer are a line each, and a replayed stamp or a fee leaves none. A bill that arrives already written down has one line, from its kept amount.
- **The words on the screen** are the trigger's summary, drawn as it is under the **Write-down** tag. `jobActivityLine.test.ts` holds three write-downs of one bill to three numbered lines, each keyed by its own row, all under Billing, each on its own day.
- **The guide** *give a customer a discount* says a write-down leaves a line each time.

## What it leaves alone

- The triggers themselves, the created, billed and sent lines, and every reader. No screen changes: the feed already draws a line per row.
- Old lines. No bill on prod had been written down twice, so each written-down bill already has its one line and nothing is backfilled. The lines already written keep nobody on them. Putting the person on them would be one guarded update, and the owner's word on 2026-11-23 was to leave it, so no data fix is part of this change.
- No table of write-downs, as the owner directed.

## The migration's stamp

The file was cut as `20261123001000`. Main has since taken four later files, all on prod, so it was re-stamped `20261123080500` from main's latest, as `CLAUDE.md` rules. A plain `supabase db push` refuses a file older than the newest applied one.

## Deploy

Merge, then `supabase db push`. The client calls nothing new and reads none of the new `detail` keys, so the push is safe on either side of the Pages deploy. Between the merge and the push a second write-down behaves as it does today: the bill is right and no second line is written. Docker was not running on the build Mac, so CI's `sql-beds` is the whole-schema proof. Both scenarios passed first on a local Postgres 15 over stub tables, with the two writers' migration and this one applied as they are.
