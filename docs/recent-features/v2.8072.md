# v2.8072 — Developments, Schedule: a stage’s press always has its words (2026-11-20)

Found by the Fable 5.2 audit of punch list #382, *Before we start*. No migration and no edge function.

## What was wrong

A stage card on a development's Schedule has one press, named by what comes next. `pressWords` in `DevelopmentScheduleCard.tsx` ended with `return label ?? 'Open'`. The fallback caught a missing label and let an empty one through.

The kernel hands an empty one. For a stage whose only open bullets are ours, `stageStanding` sets `nextLabel` to the first such bullet's `door`, the name of its own press. A bullet ticked by hand always has one (*Mark it done*). A bullet the app reads has `door: ''` wherever there is no screen to open for it:

- on every load, while it reads *Reading it now.*
- for good when the job came from no bid, the seat cannot read the source, the job is on no development, the city has no records for the address, or the read failed.

**The scenario.** A stage's bullets on the customer all have a yes. Its one bullet of ours reads the order log, on a job with no bid. The day to decide is still ahead, so the card reads *Ray is done. 1 thing of ours is not ready.* Its press was drawn with an empty label: a blue pill with no words, and no name for a screen reader.

## The fix

`pressWords` returns `label?.trim() || 'Open'`. A press with nothing to name opens the stage, as it does for a stage with nothing to do yet. The press's action was always to open the stage, so only its words change. `st.nextLabel` is printed nowhere else in the component.

The kernel is left alone on purpose: `startChecks.ts` is copied to the edge functions, and a change there forces a redeploy.

## The test

`DevelopmentScheduleCard.render.test.tsx` gains *the one press has words when what comes next is a bullet of ours with no press of its own*. It seeds a stage whose bullets on the customer all have a yes and one bullet of ours with `reads: 'order_log'` and no reader. It asserts the card's press reads **Open**, and that no button in any card is without words or a label.

Run both ways: it failed before the fix, where the press was an empty `<button>`, and it passes after. The file's other 16 tests pass both ways.
