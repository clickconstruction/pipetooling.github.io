# v2.4926 — GC mode: change orders to the customer, for a dev (2026-10-08)

Owner Billing's O3-ui in the GC mode real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/OWNER_BILLING_REAL_BUILD.md` → O3, on branch `spike/gc-mode`). It is the window over O3's change-order functions (v2.4915, migration `20261008110000`, applied and verified on prod) and their generated types (#4953). It is dev only, behind the dev door, like the rest of `/gc`.

- **`src/components/gc/GcChangeOrders.tsx`** (`GcChangeOrdersWindow`) opens from **Change orders · N** on a won project's card on `/gc` (`?changes=<project id>`, so a link opens it). It is not offered on a bid or a lost job.
  - The header reads what is signed, the days added and how many wait on the customer.
  - **New change order:** what changed, the reason, whose work (a trade's package or our own crew), the days, and added work or a credit. The price starts at `changeOrderPrice(project, cost)` (the cost plus the job's fee) until the office types its own. A credit reads "saves us $X".
  - A **draft** has **Send for signature** and **Delete the draft**. A **sent** one has the day they answered with **They signed** and **They declined**, and the words to send from the office's own email (the app does not email change orders yet). A **signed** one has its percent done, in the kernel's steps.
  - A **time extension** (G-141) reads its lines through `timeExtensionLines`.
- **Every press goes through O3's functions:** `gc_draft_change_order`, `gc_send_change_order` and `gc_answer_change_order` (`p_how = 'office'`). The percent done and the draft's delete are plain writes the RLS and the keep-what-went trigger already police. A refusal shows in the database's own words. They live in `src/lib/gc/gcIo.ts` beside the Board's reads: `loadGcChangeOrders`, `draftChangeOrder`, `sendChangeOrder`, `answerChangeOrder`, `setChangeOrderPct`, `deleteChangeOrderDraft`.
- **`src/lib/gc/changeOrderRows.ts`** gains `ChangeOrderDraft` (the shape `gc_draft_change_order` reads) and `withChangeOrders(state, rows)`. It lays each project's change orders over the Board's `boardProjectFromView` result, so the kernels read the real rows.
