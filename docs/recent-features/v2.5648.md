# v2.5648 — Write down their word: a day they will pay by, or that they say they paid (2026-10-19)

Punch list #129, PR 3a of 6, on PR 2 (v2.5647, the money line). The customer's word on paying is one record with two kinds. Migration `20261019080000_paid_claim.sql` (`docs/migrations/20261019080000_paid_claim.md`); no edge function; nothing in the kernels copy. The Messages bridge (the slot's heard day landing on the job) is PR 3b.

- **The record** (`job_payment_promises.kind`, `method`, `amount`): a `promise` (the day is a pay-by day, as before) or a `paid_claim` (the day is the day they say they paid, with how they paid and the amount when not all of it), held by four constraints. `add_job_payment_claim` writes a claim under the promise gate (the office, or the job's people); it never touches the board's date and never marks the job paid.
- **The reads**: the company-wide `list_job_payment_promises()` and `list_payment_promise_records()` answer promises only, so the Lien desk, the legal packet, Owner Billing and the pay-speed kernel never score a claim; the per-job `list_job_payment_promises_for(job)` answers both with their kind, and it is what the sheet's record, the job's trail and the money line read.
- **The kernel** (`lib/jobs/paymentPromises.ts`): `PaymentPromise` gains `kind`, `method`, `amount` (the parse defaults a row to a promise), `PAID_METHODS` and `PAID_METHOD_WORDS`, `promisesOnly`, `claimWords` (*a check in the mail · $1,500*); `voidTakesDateOffBoard` ignores a claim. `promiseActivityEvents.ts` reads a claim as *Says they paid Oct 20, 2026 · a check in the mail — Dana · by text · heard by Theo*; `bidMoneyLine.ts` reads the newest live word of either kind, a claim as *They say they paid · a check in the mail · Oct 20* with no days past.
- **The sheet** (`SetPromisedPayDateModal`, now **Write down their word**): a switch, *They'll pay by…* / *They say they paid…*; the paid side takes the method, the day, the amount (blank for all of it, `parseClaimAmount`), who said it, how, a note, and writes through `add_job_payment_claim`. The record under the form lists both kinds (`PromiseRecordList`, *Says paid Oct 20 · a check in the mail*), and *Never said that* voids either. The Pipeline's chip opens it on the promise side as before (`initialMode`).
- **The press**: **Write down their word** on the money line (`BidMoneyLine.tsx`) while money is open, the sheet on the job with the newest promise's day as its start.
- **Tests**: `paymentPromises.test.ts` (the parse's kind, method and amount, `promisesOnly`, `claimWords`, a claim never takes the date off the board), `promiseActivityEvents.test.ts` (the claim's line), `bidMoneyLine.test.ts` (the claim on the line), `SetPromisedPayDateModal.render.test.tsx` (the switch, the paid side's write with its arguments, the Save held on a bad amount); the SQL bed `scripts/pgtest-paid-claim.sh` over `supabase/tests/paid_claim/`, registered in `scripts/sql-beds.txt`.
- **Docs**: the guide *see where a job you bid stands on money* gains **Write down their word**; the Glossary's payment promise entry; `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`'s promise gate paragraph; `BIDS_SYSTEM.md`'s Job block bullet; the app directory's Bids page; to-do #129's state.
