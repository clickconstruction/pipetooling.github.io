# v2.6661 — A sub’s signed hours statement names their company (punch list #212, residual G, the card’s close-out) (2026-11-07)

The last of the three residuals left on the card (`to-dos/signed-hours-statement/README.md`): `hours_statements.labor_company` (v2.6097, for a sub's company on the attestation) stayed null, since nothing wrote it — the window saved `laborCompany: null` and the dev MCP's wrapper never took the column — so a sub's attestation read *I, <name>, state that we did the work above* with no company, whatever the roster called them. The card is amended here: the residuals shipped, and only the owner's live look is left.

## What changed

- **The window** (`HoursStatementModal.tsx`): for a sheet paper, a **Company** field under Who — *The sub's company, as it signs*, at most 160 characters (the column's own check), with a line saying what it does. The trimmed value goes to the row as `labor_company` and into the snapshot's worker and the kernel's input, so `hoursStatementAttestation` reads *I, Dale Whitfield, for Whitfield Plumbing LLC, state that we did the work above …*, the facts band reads *subcontractor, Whitfield Plumbing LLC* and the signature line *Signature of the worker for Whitfield Plumbing LLC* on the print and the signed PDF alike (both already read `worker.company`; nothing filled it). Opening a saved statement fills the field from its row. Blank, the attestation names the sub alone. On a clock person the field is not drawn and nothing is saved.
- **The dev MCP** (`_shared/devMcpHoursStatement.ts`): `plan_hours_statement`'s `make` takes `labor_company` for a sheet paper and writes it through `dev_hours_statement_write` (which took the column with v2.6654's migration `20261107170000`); on a clock paper it is refused in words, as the window offers the field for a sheet alone.
- **Tests**: the window's render smoke (the field on a sub, the saved row's `laborCompany` and snapshot, the field gone on a clock person), `devMcpHoursStatement.test.ts` (the column and the snapshot's worker on a sheet paper, the attestation's words, the clock refusal).
- **Docs**: the guide *make a signed hours statement* → A sub's sheet lines; the card's `status`, `next`, `size` and *Where it stands*.

## Decisions

- **Typed, not read from the roster.** The roster row's name is the sub's name as the office keeps it — sometimes the company, sometimes the person — so there is no column to read a company from without guessing; the office types it once per paper and the row keeps it.
- **No migration.** The column has been there since v2.6097; v2.6654's replacement of the wrapper took it along, so this is a client and function change alone.
