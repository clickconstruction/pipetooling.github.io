# v2.3733 — Day book PR 6b: the Crew Day email carries each office person's line (2026-09-22)

PR 6b of [`to-dos/day-book`](../../to-dos/day-book/README.md) (punch list #20). One migration, `crew-day-email-dispatch` redeployed, no client change.

## What was missing

PR 6a (v2.3728) gave the Dashboard's Crew Day one line per office person by reading `get_day_book_payload` as the viewer. The email dispatcher runs as the service role, which has no `auth.uid()`, so the Day book RPC refused it and the email had no line.

## The change

- **[`20260922180000_day_book_for_user.sql`](../migrations/20260922180000_day_book_for_user.md)** — the Day book's body moves into `day_book_payload_for(p_uid, p_from, p_to, p_person)` (the same SQL as v2.3732's, with the viewer passed in and the dev · controller gate read off `users.role`, since `is_dev()` only knows `auth.uid()`). Two thin doors call it: `get_day_book_payload` (the app's, as `auth.uid()`, same shape and grants as before) and `get_day_book_payload_for_user(p_user_id, p_day)` (service_role only, the `get_crew_day_payload_for_user` pattern). A recipient the gate refuses gets `forbidden` and the email carries no line.
- **[`_shared/dayBookOneLiner.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/dayBookOneLiner.ts)** — a Deno-safe port over the raw payload rows: one day's events grouped per person and written as the Dashboard writes them (*billed 3 · 4 deposits · moved 1 job to Paid · 2 contracts sent · approved 12 sessions · updated the schedule · sent 1 bid*), five phrases then *+N*, deletions left off. [`dayBookOneLinerShared.test.ts`](../../src/lib/people/dayBookOneLinerShared.test.ts) runs one fixture through both this port and the client kernel and expects the same sentence.
- **`crew-day-email-dispatch`** — `fetchOutcomeLines` after the crew payload; `payload.outcomes` → `PersonLine.outcome`, printed under the person in the HTML row and after the hours in the text version; degrades to no line on any error or an RPC not yet pushed.
- Docs: `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` (the crew-day section), the guide *get a daily crew email*, the to-do.

## Verified
