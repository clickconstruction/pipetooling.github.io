# v2.4008 — Edit Job: "Remove payment?" no longer tells you to press Save (2026-09-28)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Found in the live pass on punch list **#46** row 4 (2026-09-28).

## What was wrong

`JobFormPaymentRemoveConfirm` has two copies: one for a saved line (the remove writes through the RPC at once) and one for a line that was only typed. The second read "The payment line is removed from this form now; click **Save** on the job to update the database." Edit mode has had no Save button since v2.1080 — the billing autosave slice carries the change about a second later.

## What shipped

- The typed-line copy reads: "This payment line was only just typed. It leaves the form now, and the job saves the change by itself in a moment."
- The prop's doc comment says the same.
- `JobFormOverlays.render.test.tsx` gains a case: the typed-line copy is shown, the saved-line copy is not, and nothing says "click".

## Verified

- `JobFormOverlays.render.test.tsx` (7). Seen live before the change on job J1053: the old sentence showed, and the payment was gone from the database three seconds after Confirm with no Save pressed.
