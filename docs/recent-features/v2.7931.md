# v2.7931 — The money desks close to a primary (2026-11-19)

Punch list #400, PR 5 of 5 (`to-dos/primary-job-doors.md`), on the owner's call 1. One migration, pushed after the merge. No client change and no edge function.

## Before

- Three desks named the role in their office lists: the bank payments desk, returned checks and disputes, and the GC review week. So did the payment by payment list behind the pay speeds.
- None of them takes a job. So a primary on no job could read the bank deposits, every unlinked payment and the return cases, and could close out a deposit or a case.
- No screen of a primary's opens any of them. The hole was at the API.

## What changed

- **The role is out of each of those lists** (`20261119104000_primary_money_desks.sql`): twenty-two functions and the read policies of `ar_stripe_cases` and `ar_unbanked_check_cases`.
- **The bed.** `npm run test:pg:primary-money-desks` asks every door as five people, on the whole schema.

## What it leaves alone, and one call for the owner

- The card proposed closing all of "who pays when". The client search it asked for found that the Bill tab of a primary's own job draws its expected-pay dates, its pay calendar and its returned-checks warning from four of those reads: `get_billed_customer_pay_speeds`, `list_job_promised_pay_dates`, `can_read_payment_promises()` and `list_ar_returned_check_payers`. Closing them would blank a working screen. They stay as they were.
- Those four answer for every payer in the company. Holding a primary to the payers of their own jobs needs a rule over payers that does not exist yet. That is a new call for the owner.
- Dev, master, assistant and controller pass every door as before.
- The trips office, the Tally picker and the New-report picker, which name the role on purpose.

## Deploy

Merge, then `supabase db push`. Safe on either side of the client deploy.
