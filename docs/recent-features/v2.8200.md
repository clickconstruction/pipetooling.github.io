# v2.8200 — A job shows its places as rows with photos, and reads the places an earlier job kept at the same address (2026-11-21)

Punch list #413, steps 4 and 5, on the read rule of v2.8195. Taunya's word of 2026-11-21 was to build the whole card.

## Why

The card's strongest reason to keep a spot is the call a year later: a main drain backs up, and the tech sent is on a new job and was not there when the cleanout went in. A place stayed on the one job that set it, so that tech saw nothing. And on the job itself, a place was a count on a line: *Places on this job · 3 places*. Seeing which three took opening a map.

## What ships

**Places kept at this address** (step 4). A job reads the lists of places that earlier jobs kept at the same customer address.

- Each such list is a dashed line under the job's own lines: **Kept at this address**, then how many places and when, such as *2 places · kept Nov 2026*.
- A reader who may read the job that kept the list also reads which job: *from 4135 Sewer line, Nov 2026*. A helper may not read the ledger, and reads the month.
- A press opens that list. It is the earlier job's, so its places are changed by whoever set them and by the office. A new place goes on this job's own list.
- Only a job's list of places follows the address. A painted map of the way in stays its own job's.
- A job with no customer address on file shares nothing. Of the jobs opened since October 1, about three in four have one.
- The line also shows on a sheet that shows a job's maps alone, such as On my way, where a tech looks before driving.
- A read of the address that fails shows no line and no error: the job's own maps still stand.

**Each place as a row** (step 5). In the crew's job window, the plain one with no tabs, under **Places on this job** and under each **Kept at this address** line, each place is a row 64 px tall: its photo, its mark, its name and the start of its words. A list shows four rows and one press for the rest, *＋ 3 more ›*.

The office's tabbed window keeps the lines and draws no rows: its top band sits over every tab, and it has the Map tab (v2.8204) for the same places.

**A row opens its list on that place.** The map goes to the place's dot and its row is picked and in view (`pickPlace` through the door's new `place`).

**A place counts its opens.** A press on a row, a press on a dot and opening a place's photo each tell the database the place was opened (`note_painted_place_opened`). It counts an open by anyone but the place's own setter, and names nobody. The count is not drawn on any screen yet: it is there to answer, in a few weeks, whether anyone comes looking.
