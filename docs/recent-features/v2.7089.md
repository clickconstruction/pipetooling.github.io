# v2.7089 — The Bridge's Build, Bill and Collect windows open on one screen, as Market and Sales do (punch list #334, PR 1) (2026-11-12)

PR 1 of the Bridge train (card #334, `to-dos/bridge-pulse/`), the bottom of its stack. The five step windows were drawn before and after on 2026-11-11 and the owner said: "Like all of your recommendations, build it all." Market and Sales shipped on the frame as v2.6977. These are the other three, with the header's line. Client only. No migration, no edge function, and no kernel that is copied to the edge functions changed.

## What changed

- **The Build window** (`BridgeBuildDesk.tsx`, `build/buildSteps.ts`): opens on *The field put in N hours. Only $X of work shows as done.*, with one line that says where most of the gap is. Three short lists follow, named by the act: the jobs whose percent did not move (**Set the percent**), the jobs with hours and no price (**Set a price**), and the sold jobs nobody is on (**Schedule it**). A row's press opens the job's why line, its crew chip and its doors under the row, and the percent window opens from there. At the foot: **What moved**, **Every open job** (the old verdict, the three tiles and the whole ledger), **How this is counted**.
- **The Bill window** (`BridgeBillDesk.tsx`, `bill/billStepWindow.ts`): opens on *$X of finished work has no bill.*, with the office's weekly pace and the pile said in weeks of it. **Do this week** lists the ready jobs as one row, the two largest single jobs, and the jobs with no price as one row that opens its list. **Did the customer get these?** holds the bills marked billed by hand. One line says how many bills went out the day their job finished. At the foot: **Every leak**, **The pipe, this window**, **What waiting costs** (with Billable today and the rule's table), **How this is counted**.
- **The Collect window** (`BridgeCollectDesk.tsx`, `BridgeCollectCounted.ts`): opens on *$X is late. N customers hold most of it.*, over a bar in three parts: not yet due, late, doubtful. **Call this week** lists the payers, the ones on the sheet first, each with its move and one press (**Record the answer**, **Send the statement** or **Open the customer**). The words to say, the lien paper, the owner and day field and **Where it stands** sit under the row. **Only you can decide** follows. At the foot: the what-if, **What is in the pile** (the tiles, the bar by state, everyone else, what slows Collect), **How this is counted**. The plan line, **Record this as the week's plan**, the scorecard and **Copy the call sheet** are as they were.
- **A leak knows its job's label** (`bill/billDesk.ts`): `BillLeak` gains `label` and, on a remainder its bills cover, `partBilledDays`. The step window's rows are written from them. No figure changed.
- **The header** (`Bridge.tsx`): the line beside the page's name ends *reads the last 8 weeks, since Sep 17*, from `BRIDGE_DAYS_BACK` and the window's first day. The loading line reads *Reading the last 8 weeks…* from the same constant.

Nothing a window printed before is removed. The page hands each window the props it already did.

## Verify

Walked on a dev server as a dev on production data, in headless Chrome, at 375 × 812, 768 × 1024 and 1400 × 900, light and dark. Every write to the database was refused; the only requests refused were the app's own last-seen stamp and its nav-click log. Nothing that records was pressed.

- The header read *reads the last 8 weeks, since* and a day, at every width.
- Pressed **Build** on the pipe: *Build, broken down* opens with its headline and the three lists. Opened each of the three folds in turn, one at a time. Escape closed it.
- Pressed **Bill**: *Bill, broken down* opens with its headline, **Do this week** and **Did the customer get these?**. Opened each of the four folds. The close press closed it.
- Pressed **Collect**: *Collect, broken down* opens with its headline, the bar, **Call this week** and **Only you can decide**. Opened each of the three folds. A press outside the window closed it.
- The page never scrolls sideways, with a window open or shut, and nothing inside a window passes the screen's edge. Every press in the three windows is at least 44 px tall. At 375 wide the windows are 343 px wide.
- No page error at any width.

Not walked: the percent window's save, **Record the answer** and **Record this as the week's plan**, because each one records.
