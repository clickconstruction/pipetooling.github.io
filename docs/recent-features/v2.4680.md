# v2.4680 — Legal desk: everything goes to counsel unless you hold it back (2026-10-05)

Punch list #85, item 29 (lane D). The owner's decision of 2026-10-05: share everything with counsel by default. Records are discoverable either way, and hiding them from our own lawyer only sets up a surprise. Until now an entry dated before the account's first bill was held from the firm unless the office ticked it, and the portal told the firm *0 held* whatever the office did. **No migration**: `held_overrides` keeps its column, its type and its meaning (`true` = held).

## What changed

- **The rule, in both places at once.** The packet kernel (`src/lib/legal/legalPacket.ts`) and `legal-portal`'s held filter now hold an entry only when `held_overrides[key] === true`. An old `false` (share a pre-bill entry) is the default now and changes nothing. `sharedByDefault` stays on `LegalSaidEntry`, always `true`, for the readers that compare.
- **A hold carries a reason.** The desk's Their word tab drops the *goes / held* checkbox and the *Only after the first bill* door. Each row reads *goes* with **Hold back…**, which opens a reason box under the row; **Hold back** stays off until a reason is typed. A held row is struck through with *Held: <reason>* under it and a **Share** button. **Share all** shows while anything is held. The reasons ride inside `held_overrides` under `_reasons` (`withHold`, `heldReasonsOf` in `src/lib/legal/legalMatters.ts`); every boolean reader already skips a non-boolean value, so an old client or function ignores them.
- **The firm sees the count.** `legal-portal` counts what it held per matter and sends it as `heldCount` (hard-coded `0` before). The payload parser keeps it (`LegalPortalMatter.heldCount`), and the firm's Their word tab says *The office held back N entries. Ask the office if you need it.* The desk's release preview reads the same line from its packet.
- **Deploy order is free.** `sharedOverrides` now marks every entry the function sends as shared, so a page still on the old pre-bill rule shows the newly sent entries too, and a new page with the old function shows what it showed before. **Redeploy `legal-portal` after merge.** On its first load after the deploy, a matter already with the firm shows its pre-bill contacts, promises and calls. That is the decision, on purpose.
- Docs: the guide *review a collections account before it goes to your attorney*, GLOSSARY (Legal desk, Legal matter), ACCESS_CONTROL (the curate row), EDGE_FUNCTIONS (`legal-portal`).

## Is this the best we can do?

- *Does the firm see the reason, or only the count?* The count. A hold exists for something that is not the firm's business, such as a family matter or a note about our own staff; printing the reason would print the secret. The count tells counsel to ask, and the office answers in the conversation (item 17).
- *Does this need a migration?* No. A separate `held_reasons` column would be cleaner to query, but nothing queries it: the desk and the function read the whole JSON. Keeping it in `held_overrides` keeps one write (`legal_matter_save_review`) and one source.
- *Should a hold need a reason?* Yes. A reason written when the hold is made is what the office can defend later. The button stays off without one.
- *Should "Only after the first bill" survive as a one-click bulk hold?* No. It is the old default under a new name, and the owner decided against it.
- *What about matters already with the firm?* They change on the next portal load. The release note says so, so nobody is surprised.

## Left out

The firm's print packet (item 1, v2.4615, not on main yet) still says *nothing held back*; it can read `heldCount` from the parsed matter once both land.
