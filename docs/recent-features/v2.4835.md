# v2.4835 — Lien window: record a lien that was filed without the app (2026-10-07)

Client only. Grace, on job 717 (The Learning Experience — HVAC) with its Lien window open on the Mechanic's lien tab: *Need to be able to enter the date the lien was filed.* The tab's gate list read ✗ owner of record with mailing address and ✗ legal description, and under it only *Clear the ✗ items above* — the **Record filing…** door, with its *Filed on* box, was drawn only once every gate cleared (`affidavitReady`). The gates are right for the paper the app draws; they were also holding the record of a paper someone else filed. Meanwhile the timeline read *the affidavit window closed with nothing filed — the lien is gone*.

## What changed

- **`LienFilingTabs`** draws the filing's record box once (`filingRecordBox`: county, recording #, *Filed on*, the saved copy) and offers it from two doors: **Record filing…** behind the gate as before, and **Already filed — record it…** (`data-lien-already-filed`) under the gate list while any gate is ✗ and no affidavit is on file, with one line saying when to use it. The phone sheet (`LienRecordSheet` `affidavit_filing`) no longer requires `affidavitReady`; its second line says *Filed without this tab: record the date and number the County Clerk stamped* in that case.
- The record is the same row as before: `job_lien_filings` kind `affidavit`, `filed_at`, `recording_number`, `county`, `serve_due` five days on (§ 53.055), the saved copy, the fields snapshot of what the app knew. The readers were already right: `hasFiledAffidavit`, the runway's `filedYmd`, the desk's affidavit pile and the timeline's § 53.052 node all take a filing by `filed_at`.
- **Record service…** then follows as it does for a filing recorded behind the gate.

## Tests

`LienFilingTabs.render.test.tsx` (+2): with the gates ✗ on a computer the door is drawn and *Record filing…* is not, the box opens with its three fields, the saved copy and the Record filing button, and Back puts the door back; a phone opens the sheet without the gate with the *filed without this tab* line. The write itself is the existing `insertFiling`, unchanged.

## Live

Dev server on prod data, job 717: the door and its line drawn under the two ✗ gates; pressed, the box opened with Saved copy, County, Recording #, *Filed on* (a date box), Back and Record filing. Nothing recorded — the office types the real date.
