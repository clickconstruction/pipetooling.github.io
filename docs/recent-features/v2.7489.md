# v2.7489 — Lien desk: the run window says which paper it holds (2026-11-16)

Two reports from the office on 2026-11-16, both in the notices' run window, both passed on with the owner's go.

1. On an owner-of-record envelope that listed one job, she asked why it showed one job when the property had six invoices. The envelope was right. The property had seven jobs on the desk: one with a notice approved and printed that day, four whose notice was mailed earlier and now stand at the affidavit, two whose notice went earlier still.
2. On the original contractor's envelope, which listed five jobs' notices, she expected six liens at one property and three at another. The envelope was right again. She was counting lien affidavits while looking at the notices' run. And at the second property she saw one of two jobs, because the other job's name does not say the street.

She thinks by property. The run holds only the notices approved and not yet mailed, and nothing on the screen said so. This sits on v2.7481 and v2.7477.

## What changed

- **The head says what the run holds.** `lienRunWhatWords({ monthly, retainage })` in [`src/lib/jobs/lienRunWords.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienRunWords.ts) words a line under the title (`run-what`): *Monthly notices (§ 53.056), approved and not yet mailed. A job whose notice already went is not here. Lien affidavits are not here either.* A retainage notice in the run is named beside the monthly ones. The **?** beside the title adds where the affidavits print (the Board's *Print N for notarization*) and that their copies go out after the filing (v2.7476).
- **The property's street beside a job's name.** [`src/lib/jobs/lienRunStreet.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienRunStreet.ts): `lienPropertyStreet` reads the first line of the property record's address, and `lienRunStreetBeside(jobName, address)` returns it only when the name does not already say it (the house number and the street's first telling word). The record is the one the job points at, `jobs_ledger.customer_address_id`, never a match on typed addresses. `buildLienDeskRun` and `buildLienRetainageRun` put it on `RunNotice.street`, with `RunNotice.propertyId` beside it. The checklist's row (`run-check-street-<itemId>`) and the envelope card's copy line draw it after the name.
