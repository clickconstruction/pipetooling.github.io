# v2.7324 — The Lien desk's draft footers are one press each (2026-11-15)

Client only. The owner's call of 2026-11-15, reading the drafted papers under his dev sign-in: the leader (Malachi, the master on these jobs) always signs a notice with his typed name and always wet-signs an affidavit before a notary, so the draw pad, the signing code, the pen record (v2.7297) and the word record are exceptions. Until now the notice's draft footer carried *Already mailed? Record it…*, *Save draft*, *Malachi said to send it…*, *He signed the paper…* and the primary, with the signing line's box above them, and the affidavit pane an amber word press beside the green or blue one.

## What changed

- `lienFooter` in `src/lib/jobs/lienFooterMenu.ts` (pure, tested): from the role, the paper, the leader's name (`leaderNameFor(master_user_id)` through `lienLeaderPressWords`), the blocked flag, the office's route (`leader` | `run` | `hold`), the notice's sign state (`{ way, otherScreen, codeReady }`) and whether the pen door is wired, the footer: the one primary (`approve` | `ask` | `run`, with its label and the line beside it), the menus (`okayed` — *He already okayed it ▾* with the word row and, on a notice with months, the pen row; `sign` — *Sign another way ▾* for a leader on a notice, the ways on this screen minus the one in use: `type` and `draw` on his own sign-in, `draw` and `code` on someone else's when his code is set) and the quiet links (`save`, `by_hand` on a notice for the office). Blocked: no primary, no menus — the host draws *Go to gate N ▴* as before. `lienSignWayLine` says what reaches the paper per way.
- `LienFootMenu.tsx`: the menu press (`aria-haspopup="menu"`, `data-lien-foot-menu=<key>`) and its popover above (`role="menu"`, rows `role="menuitem"` with `data-lien-foot-menu-row=<key>`, each row's title as its second line). Opens on the press; the first row takes focus; the arrow keys walk the rows; Escape (captured on `window`, so the pane behind never sees it) and a mousedown outside close it; a row closes it and runs the host's `onPick(key)`, which runs exactly what the old press ran — `setWordOpen`, the pen pane's `run('He signed the paper', …)`, or the sign line's mode.
- `LienDesk.tsx` (the notice's draft footer): `signWay` state (`type` on every job change; on someone else's screen `type` means the line's own default, his code when ready, else his hand); the sign line is drawn only when the way is not `type` (`draftSignLine`, keyed by the way so a pick starts clean, `ownToggle={false}`, `onModeChange` keeps the host's state honest). The state line is the kernel's line (the period dropped when a why follows); the primary carries it as its tooltip. *Save draft* is a `lienFootLink` (`data-lien-desk-save-draft`); *Already mailed?* keeps `lienFootSkip` and `data-lien-desk-by-hand`. The awaiting footer, the phone card and the Ready footer keep their signing lines and doors.
- `LienNoticeSignLine.tsx`: `initialMode`, `ownToggle` and `onModeChange` props; `setMode` on the handle (switching clears the pad and the code, as the toggle did).
- `LienDeskAffidavitPane.tsx` and `LienDeskRetainagePane.tsx`: the amber word press is gone; the menus from the kernel sit beside the one press; the grey line for the leader reads the kernel's line (*Ready to file. You sign it on paper before a notary.* / *Your okay puts it in the run.*), for the office *One line on Malachi's phone.*, with the standing-rule and promise sentences kept. The affidavit pane never drew a signing box; a smoke now holds that.
- Guides *send lien notices from the Lien desk* (the footer paragraph, the word bullet, the by-hand link, Signing a notice, someone else's screen, the pen, the affidavit bullet) and *file a lien and never miss its deadlines* (one clause).
