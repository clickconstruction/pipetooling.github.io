# v2.3696 — Bill tab: the stage chooser goes two-way, In order / Any time (2026-09-21)

Owner, 2026-09-21, looking at job 978's Bill tab: *"I think these three options are confusing, and maybe the order options should only be 'Order' and 'Any'."* Migration [`20260921230000_stage_kind_two_way.sql`](../migrations/20260921230000_stage_kind_two_way.md) (data only). No edge function.

## Why

- **Census** (dev-mcp `read_rows`, 2026-09-21): 16 `order` rows across about seven jobs; three `NULL` rows, two of them discounts that `syncDiscountRows` forces null and **one picked by hand — a $50 "Tip"**; every other row `any`, the v2.3083 backfill default. One human pick in two weeks live.
- **What "—" did that Any did not** was small: the wording on the second line (*not a stage · bills with the final draw*), never turning `ready` on its own, staying out of the crew's stage-picker weights (v2.3192) and off the Edit read-out / GC card. Nothing locked — the rule guides.
- **Why it read as confusing:** a glyph in a radio where the lit default sits in the middle; two questions on one control (does it wait its turn? / is it work at all?); and "Order" directly under a row named *CHANGE ORDER*.

## What shipped

- [`StageKindControls.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/StageKindControls.tsx): `KIND_OPTIONS` is two entries, **In order** / **Any time**; `onChange` hands back a `StageKind`, never null. The dashed "plain" badge stays for discount rows. The hint line under ① Line Items drops the "— is just a line item" clause.
- [`segmentGenerator.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/segmentGenerator.ts) / [`MultipleSegmentGeneratorModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/MultipleSegmentGeneratorModal.tsx): `SegmentGeneratorRow.kind` and the payload's `stage_kind` are `StageKind`; `plainCount` and the *N plain lines · bills with the final draw* summary span are gone; the Stage column is the same two-way selector.
- **Kernel untouched**: [`_shared/stagePlan.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/stagePlan.ts) still builds `plainRows` for null kinds — discounts, and any row a future writer nulls — with the same *not a stage* line. Only the type comment changed.
- **Data**: the migration flips hand-picked nulls on non-discount rows to `any` (one row) and rewrites the column comment. Idempotent; order against the client deploy does not matter.
- **Guides**: *split a job into stages and bill stage by stage* rewritten (two kinds, one question, fees are any-time lines that never turn ready on their own); *schedule a sub across every surface* and *give a customer a discount* renamed the selector. `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` Jobs entry and `HELP_MEDIA_PLAN.md` amended. The recorded GIF still shows the three-way pill; re-record when the next Bill-tab GIF is cut.

## Not in this PR

The chooser still shows on a single-line job, where stage mode can never activate (it needs an In order row and a second priced row to sequence against). Hiding it until a job has two or more priced work rows is a separate decision, filed in the session notes.
