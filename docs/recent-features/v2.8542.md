# v2.8542 — Addresses that look alike: Fix the spelling, and Open the job on an address with no street number (2026-11-24)

Punch list #439, piece 2 of seven, its second part. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

Built on the card's pick, not yet answered: **Same place** records the match and changes no job's address, and **Fix the spelling…** is a second, separate press.

## What was there

v2.8529 lists the pairs of addresses that may be one place and keeps an answer for each. An answer changes no address, so a typo stayed on its job. The **No street number** tab listed addresses with no way to reach their jobs.

## What changed

- **Fix the spelling…** on a pair that is not answered yet, and on one answered **Same place**. It opens `AddressSpellingFixWindow.tsx` over the list.
- **The small window.** *Write it as* offers the pair's two spellings, the busier one picked. *These change* names every job and bid written the other way, each ticked, with the address it has now. The button reads **Change 2 addresses** with its count, and is dead when nothing is ticked. Nothing is written before that press.
- **A number that would change.** When one spelling has a word with a number in it that the other lacks, a unit or a suite, an amber line says *This takes 201 off the address*, or *This puts 201 on the address*, and points at **Not the same**.
- **It goes past a row that fails.** A job the reader may not change stays in the window with its reason, under *1 address did not change. It stays here with the reason.* The ones that took leave the list, and the toast says both counts.
- **After the fix** the two addresses share one cleaned key, so they match silently and the pair leaves the list. The job form's list of addresses on file reads again.
- **Open the job** on a **No street number** row, one press a job, four at most, named *Open J1078* when the address has several. It opens the job's Edit tab with the Job Address box ringed for a moment. The box is ringed and never focused: a focus there reads the lists, and a blur may ask the map.
- **Escape** in the small window closes it alone. The list under it stays.
- **The list sits under the Job window.** It drew at 1100, over the Job window's 1010, so a job opened from it was hidden behind it. It is 1005 now, and Escape in the job closes the job alone.

## How it is built

- `src/lib/addresses/fixSpelling.ts` (pure): `spellingFixPlan` says what a kept side rewrites and which numbers it takes off or puts on, with the words for the button, the caution, the outcome and the job door.
- `addressesOnFile.ts`: an entry now carries `things`, every job and bid at it with its id and the address as written.
- `useAddressLookAlikes.ts`: `fixSpelling` writes `jobs_ledger.job_address` or `bids.address` a row at a time. A write that answers no row is read as a refusal, since row security hides a row instead of raising.
- `jobFormFocusRow.ts`: `'address'` joins the rows the Job window can open on. `JobFormIdentityFields` draws the ring.

## Design calls

- **Two spellings, not a text box.** The fix picks one of the two that are on file. A third spelling is the job form's work.
- **Bids change with jobs.** A bid at the other spelling is listed and ticked like a job, since the site match reads both.
- **No fix on Not the same.** Two places are not one spelling.
- **The answer is left alone.** A pair answered **Same place** and then fixed keeps its row in `address_place_answers`. It joins a key nothing is written under any more, which reads as nothing.
