# v2.7321 — Lien desk: the next step is a press (2026-11-15)

The owner, 2026-11-15, pointing at the verdict band on the affidavit pane: "it should be actionable and better presented." The band read two label-and-sentence rows — *NEXT ON THE PATH Find the owner, then send the Sep notice — tomorrow.* over *WAITING ON us — the owner's name for the notice* — and a press on it did nothing, while the doors sat in the gate rows under it (*Fill in the record ›*, *Send the notice first ›*) and in the footer (*Go to gate 1 ▴*). Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What changed

- **[`src/lib/jobs/lienNextAct.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienNextAct.ts)** (new, with its test): `lienNextActFor` turns the kernel's next step into a named act — `gate:owner`, `gate:legal`, `gate:homestead`, `gate:contractor`, `gate:notice`, `footer`, `retainage`, `serve`, `release`, `legal_desk` — with the press's label (*Find the owner ›*, *Send for approval ›*, *File it ›*), or `none` with whose move it is when it is not ours (the owner's reply to a demand letter, the county, counsel on a public owner or a homestead). It reads the same facts the words do (`noticeState`, the affidavit's state and closed gates, `waitingOn`, `paid`), so the two cannot part. `buildLienTimeline` carries it as `next.act`.
- **`LienTimelineStrip.tsx`**: the verdict band is one row — the tone's mark (✗ ! ✓ ·), the words in bold, the aside, and at the right the act's press when the host hands over `onAct`, else a quiet *Waiting on …* tag when the move is not ours. The *Waiting on* sentence rides in the press's title, and beside the words only when the move is not ours or no press can carry it (the History tab, the Lien window, the law firm's page). *Next on the path* stays as a label read by a screen reader and drawn to nobody. New props `onAct` and `act` (the host's own act in the kernel's place); new marks `data-lien-timeline-act`, `data-lien-timeline-verdict-act`, `data-lien-timeline-mark`, `data-lien-timeline-waiting-tag`. The mini row's one quiet line is as it was.
- **`LienDesk.tsx`** (the notice pane) and **`LienDeskAffidavitPane.tsx`**: each wires `onAct` to the doors it already holds — the property window at the owner, the legal description or the homestead question (`setPaneFix` / `onFixProperty`; Edit Job for a role without the window), the GC's customer record, Monthly notices, the Retainage tab (new pane prop `onShowRetainage`), the Lien window's affidavit tab for the copy and the release, the Legal desk. `footer` runs the footer's main press when it is one clear press — the office's send for approval, the leader's approve, *Open the affidavit tab ›* — and otherwise brings the footer up (`lienFooterFocus.ts`: scroll, focus on its last live press, a ring for a moment; the notice footer now wears `lienDeskFooterSlot` and `data-lien-desk-footer="notice"`). Each pane overrides the kernel's act in the viewer's words: the leader reads *Sign and approve ›* / *I approve it ›* for what the office reads *Send for approval ›*; the office on a paper waiting on him reads *Waiting on Malachi Whites* and no press; a send the gates hold reads *Go to gate 1 ›* and rings the gate.
- Tests: `lienNextAct.test.ts` (every state, plain words on every label, JP372's shape through `buildLienTimeline`); `LienTimelineStrip.render.test.tsx` (the tag with no press when the GC owes a reply); `LienDesk.render.test.tsx` (the band on JP372-like data opens the property window and the office sees the leader's name as a tag; a ready affidavit's *File it ›* opens the Lien window's affidavit tab). Four older assertions that picked a door by its words now say which of the two.
- Guides: *send lien notices from the Lien desk* (*Where a job stands*) and *file a lien and never miss its deadlines* describe the press and the tag.

## Not changed

- The retainage pane draws no timeline strip, so it has no band. The History tab, the Lien window (its own door at the right, v2.4693), the Legal desk's packet tab and the law firm's page read the same one-row band with no press.
- The release of record has no door of its own on the desk; *File the release ›* opens the Lien window on the job.
