# v2.3387 — Contract sweep PR 2: the pane — the agreement beside the queue, Send & next (2026-09-14)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Fourth PR of the train in [`to-dos/contract-sweep-seen-first/`](../../to-dos/contract-sweep-seen-first/README.md) (PR 0 v2.3384 · PR 0c v2.3385 · PR 1 v2.3386).

## Why

"It should be easy for the user … to view what is about to be sent" (owner, 2026-09-13). PR 1 said whether a row was ready; nothing yet showed the document. The only preview was two doors away in the Contract modal and opened a pop-up.

## What shipped

- **Two panes** in [`JobsContractSweepModal`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsContractSweepModal.tsx) (desktop width 1100): the queue on the left (rows are now selectable — job · customer, the amount, address · stage · email, the chips; the per-row buttons and email inputs are gone), the selected job on the right. Selection follows the visible list (first row on desktop; none until a tap on phones, where the pane replaces the list with **← Back to the list**).
- **The pane**: **To** (the signer email, editable, red when it does not parse; locked once a contract is out) · **Terms** (the sweep-wide select, moved here from the footer so its effect is visible) · the agreement in an iframe rendered by `buildJobContractDocumentHtml` — the same HTML the signing page and the PDF use — from `buildJobContractPrefill` with the row's accepted-estimate lines and total, the chosen terms via `renderContractBodyToSafeHtml`, and the letterhead from the invoice-issuer setting. **When the job already has a live draft or sent row** the pane shows that row's fields and terms instead (the send reuses it, never mints a second), with a line saying so and pointing to the full editor.
- **The footer** (kernel additions in [`contractSweepRowState.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/contractSweepRowState.ts), +1 test): `contractSweepPrimary` → `send_next` · `file_theirs` · `blocked`; `contractSweepFooterSentence` → *Emails kcallison@tfharper.com · this customer also has J843 here · then J363* / *the scope is one line — "Work we'll do: Job" · sends as time and materials* / *GC job · Summit GC's subcontract is the agreement* / *No signer email on the job…*. Buttons: **Already signed? File it** and **Open the full editor** (both the Contract modal, the first on its filing sheet) on the left; **Skip**, then per state **Send** + **Send & next** (the primary; lands on the row that followed), or **Send ours instead** + **File their subcontract**, or **Send anyway** + a dimmed Send & next, or **Fix email on the job**.
- Send all under ⋯ is unchanged (the confirm bar still swaps the footer). Closing the Contract modal re-reads the selected job's draft so an edit made there shows in the pane.
- Help guide: "The sweep shows the agreement before it sends".
