# v2.7383 — Sub labor: the kernels for whose a sheet is (PR 1 of the sub labor train) (2026-11-15)

Client only, nothing drawn yet. Punch list #346, cut from the design spike Taunya walked on 2026-11-15. Her picks that day are the rules here: a sheet that names one teammate and no sub is "owed to the teamate"; asked whether a sheet should ever name more than the one person it pays, "No, one person"; the sheet form takes "Only one name"; and "Yes, build it" for the clean-up of the old sheets.

## What was found

- On prod, 9 sheets name one teammate and no sub. Seven were made on 2026-11-13 for one person, with flat amounts and nothing paid: their open money was in no figure anywhere, because every screen read a sheet with no sub as "paid through payroll" and no payroll code reads a sheet.
- 8 sheets name a sub and two or three teammates, all made 2026-08-25 to 2026-09-17. Nobody put them there on purpose: picking a job in the sheet form ticked every person on that job's team.

## What landed

- `src/lib/subWorkOrders/teamSheetRule.ts`: `sheetOwedTo(parties, rule)` answers who a sheet's open money is owed to (`sub`, `team`, `shared`, `payroll`, `nobody`) with the person, the grouping key and whether it counts as money we owe. `TEAM_SHEET_RULE` is `owed`. The old reading, `payroll`, stays as a value so a test can read both; no screen hands it in. Also `sheetOwedToNobody`, `sheetOwedToChip` (*Team · sub labor*), `rodeAlongWords` and `teamSheetsLineWords`.
- `src/lib/subWorkOrders/sheetStanding.ts` (+ test, 8): one sheet's parties, its Pipeline job and the Pay view's words for when it pays, by the same kernels in the same order as the Pay view's own row builder. `teamSheetPayWhen`: a teammate's own sheet has no inspection and no customer bill to wait on, so it is Ready unless the office holds it or gives it a later day.
- `src/lib/people/subOwedBreakdown.ts` (+ test, 18): `buildSubOwedBreakdown` groups sheets by who they are owed to, with the shared and the unowed set apart; `owedView` and `owedCheck` open one person's sheets by job and prove they add up to the Balance due; `buildJobOwedBlock` does the same for one job; `owedTiles`, `owedPersonMatches`, `OWED_SORTS` and `owedCsv` are the page's tiles, search, sort and export.
- `src/lib/subWorkOrders/sheetCrewWords.ts` (+ test, 25): the sheet form's words and picks. `SHEET_ONE_NAME` and `nextCrewPick` make the people step one pick, called *Paid to* (`crewStepTitle`). `SHEET_PRETICKS_JOB_TEAM` is off and `jobTeamOfferWords` offers a job's team instead. `crewStepLine` says whose the sheet is and warns on any second name. `sameLinesOnOtherSheets` finds a line a job already carries on another sheet. `whoseSheetPlan` is the plan for a sheet that names two subs.
- `src/lib/subWorkOrders/rideAlongCleanUp.ts` (+ test, 11): the plan for taking teammates off a sub's sheet. Ready sheets and held ones apart, each hold with its reason and its door; the button and its foot from `batchWords`; a sum that shows no money moves; and what does move, which is the even share of the sheet's cost that People → Review and the Team Summary book to each name.

## Not in this change

No screen reads these kernels yet. The Pay view still builds its rows by its own copy of the steps in `sheetStanding`; it moves onto the kernel with the rule, in the change that follows.
