# v2.4421 — Bids: the day to call again shows on the Bid Board and in Edit Bid (2026-10-02)

Punch list #80, PR 3 of 5. [v2.4420](v2.4420.md) put the call-again day on the Call queue; this carries it to the rest of Bids, so a day set in one place reads the same everywhere.

## What shipped

- **Kernel** ([`bidNextFollowup.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/bidNextFollowup.ts)): `followupChip` (the chip's words, its short form for a narrow column, its tone and its hover text; only for a sent bid standing on a day of its own) and `bidIsParked` (a day of its own still ahead).
- **Bid Board** ([`BidsBidBoardTab`](../../src/components/bids/BidsBidBoardTab.tsx)): under **Last Contact** on a table row (*☎ Jan 5*, *☎ today*, *☎ was Sep 29*) and after it on a card row (*☎ call again Tue, Jan 5, 2027 · J. Rayburn*). Hover says who to ask for and what the bid waits on. Sent, undecided bids only.
- **Edit Bid** ([`BidLogContactControl`](../../src/components/bids/BidLogContactControl.tsx)): a **Call again** line under Last Contact on a sent bid with no answer: the day, who, why, or *No day set*. **Set a day…** / **Change…** opens the three questions and writes a method-less note (`buildFollowupChangeEntry`); **No date** removes the day. **Log contact…** carries the same questions with no Save of their own (`FollowupPickPanel` `actions={false}`); a contact with no day picked writes the row it always wrote. The control now reads the bid row once for both derived values.
- **The old lenses leave a parked bid alone**: `bidNeedsChase` takes `parked` and returns false for it, so **Waiting to hear** reads the bid as caught up (its counts, its pills, its next-bid walk); **By builder** and **By status** skip their red stale tint. The By builder card's bid row wears the chip.
- Help: [`follow-up-with-builders`](../../src/content/help/follow-up-with-builders.md) and [`read-the-bid-board`](../../src/content/help/read-the-bid-board.md). [`GLOSSARY.md`](../GLOSSARY.md) *Call-again day* says where it shows.
