# v2.6916 — A price sheet puts its fixtures on the takeoff (2026-11-10)

Punch list #308, PR 6 of nine. The estimator's nine moves a fixture ended in one thing: an assembly with the house's bundle price, and one bundle line on the bid's fixture. This PR makes that from the sheet, on a press.

## What changed

- **Migration `20261111100000_price_sheet_fixtures.sql`** ([doc](../migrations/20261111100000_price_sheet_fixtures.md)): what a placement leaves on `supply_paper_groups`, `price_sheet_group_place` and `price_sheet_group_unplace`, and `price_sheet_take_back` refusing while a fixture is placed. Push right after the merge.

  - `tagKeys`, `countRowKeys`, `rowsForTag`: a heading's tags folded (`WC-1 & 2` is WC1 and WC2) against each of the bid's rows, by its plan tag and by the tag its fixture name opens with.

  - `findSameItemsAssembly`: an assembly on file with exactly those parts and quantities, never one that nests another. `assemblyName`: the tag, the fixture's words and the house.
  - `fixtureStatus`: `placed`, `ready`, `costed_by_hand`, `no_row`, `waits`, `off` or `no_price`, with the price each (the printed subtotal, else the sum of its priced rows) and the reason in words.

- The guide `read-a-supply-houses-price-sheet` gains *Place the fixtures*.

## Decisions

- **The takeoff wins a tie.** A row with lines is `costed_by_hand`: nothing is written until the person presses *Use the sheet's*, and the batch press leaves it alone.
- **The takeoff line, the assembly and its bundle price are written by the client**, under the caller's own row level security, exactly as the Add Assembly window writes them. The database only remembers the placement. So nobody places onto a bid they could not edit by hand.
- **A made assembly stays in the book when a fixture is taken off.** It is reusable, and deleting one another bid may have picked up is the wrong kind of tidy. The bundle price is put back, or removed when there was none, only while it is still the price the sheet set.
- **Not in this PR**: *Fill from a price sheet…* in the Add Assembly window. The panel covers the case, and the window's own flow is untouched.
