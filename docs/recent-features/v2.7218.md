# v2.7218 — Lien affidavits: the notice record asks at the foot of each block, in grey (2026-11-13)

The owner, 2026-11-13, on the notice records page of a printed packet (punch list #322). Each flagged block opened with a heavy black-bordered box and a solid black **ASK FIRST** label, above the table of what was mailed. His words: *move the box to the bottom of the section, make it less black and more gray so it stands out*. This follows v2.7192, which gave the batch help sheet's flagged row its words.

## What changed

- **The verdict closes the record.** `recordBody` in `src/lib/jobs/lienNoticeRecordSheet.ts` draws `verdict` last for both shapes. A batch block reads head, table, the lines (*Sentences 5 and 6 match the record*, *Missing…*), then the box. The single page in front of one paper reads table, the two boxes (*The paper against the record*, *Missing from the record*), then the box. The facts come first and the question about them closes the block.
- **The box is grey.** It has its own styles, `.lien-record-verdict` in `LIEN_RECORD_STYLES`: a 0.75pt mid-grey border with a 3pt left edge, and a light grey fill when it asks. It no longer rides on `.lien-help-ask`, the cover sheet's 2.5pt black band, and it has no solid black tag. The lead is bold in the page's own ink and the rest is the page's normal weight.
- **The words match the batch row.** The lead is `LIEN_RECORD_ASK`, now the batch sheet's own `LIEN_HELP_BATCH_ASK_LEAD`: *Ask the attorney first.* The notice check's whole lines follow it, so the block still says which copy raised the question, which the row no longer says. The closing *Ask the attorney before this is signed.* is gone: the lead says it.
- **A record that stands has the same spot.** *On record.* (`LIEN_RECORD_ON`) and its sentence sit at the foot in the same box with no fill, where the block used to open with a black **ON RECORD** tag. A reader finds one place on every block, and the fill is what sets a question apart.
- Both guides say where the verdict is: *file a lien and never miss its deadlines* and *send lien notices from the Lien desk*.

## Not changed

- **Which papers are flagged**, and the rule behind the flag. `lienNoticeRecordAsks` and `lienAffidavitNoticeCheck` are untouched. The owner also said the late months may not matter because the bill's day is what counts. That is counsel's question (`to-dos/lien-affidavit-contents/README.md`, question 3), not this change.
- The affidavit, the batch help sheet, the cover sheet for one paper and its black **ASK FIRST** band, the copies of the notices, and the Lien window.

- `lienNoticeRecordSheet.test.ts`: the box's exact words on a single page and in a batch block, the lead once, the order of table, lines and box on both shapes, *On record.* in the same spot, no `lien-help-ask` and no **ASK FIRST** left on a record, and the grey styles.
- With made-up names, printed to PDF in headless Chrome through the page each press opens: a batch of nine and a batch of twelve with their notice records, and the single page for a flagged paper, a paper with a copy missing and a paper that stands.
