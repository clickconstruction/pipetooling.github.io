# v2.4149 — GC Review: every GC under its account man, and the leader by default (2026-09-29)

No migration, no edge function. Punch list #54 (`to-dos/gc-worklist-by-account-man.md`, retired with this PR) — the owner, reading This week's GCs after v2.4097 renamed the groups *Account Man Malachi*: *"Perhaps long term we should sort this by account man and when no account man is listed on a job, the default is the leader."*

## What changed

- **`buildGcWorklist`** (`src/lib/jobs/gcWorklist.ts`) makes one kind of group — the account man's. The `under_line` and `unassigned` kinds are gone; `GcWorklistGroup.kind` with them. A row's account man is `ownerFor(gcId, senders, accountMen, leaderUserId)`: the standing pick, else the account manager on most of its jobs, else the **leader** the caller passes (`leaderUserId`, new) — and the row carries `ownerSource: 'pick' | 'jobs' | 'leader' | null` so the panel can say which. A group with no owner exists only when no leader is known (the Pipeline cards, which pass none and never show a heading) and sorts last.
- **The line still decides the word.** `overLine` stays on the row; `worklistNextStep` asks for the word only over it; `worklistCards` counts every GC over the line by that flag instead of the old group kind. `underLineCount(group)` feeds the heading.
- **`leaderUserIdFrom(users)`** — the one live master account, else null: the same rule `chooseCompanyOwnerUserId` falls back to. `JobsGcReviewModal` passes it from the `users` it already holds; the words-due count and the opened-GC call-sheet lookup read `overLine` instead of the kind.
- **`worklistGroupTitle(group, ownerName, isYou)`** reads the owner: *Account Man Malachi* / *Account Man Taunya (you)*; *No account man yet* only for a null owner (no leader at all).
- **`GcWorklistPanel`**: the heading adds *· 2 under $10,000, word optional* when a section holds small GCs; the row's line reads *account man: Wendi (the leader, by default)* and offers *pick an account man* (not *change*) while the leader is only the default; the assign door is on every row now; **Ask by link** and **Call sheet** show only for a group with a word owed (at least one row over the line); the footer says every GC files under its account man, the leader by default.
- Guide *run your GC statement round* → The week's list; the kernel tests (the grouping, `ownerSource`, the no-leader fallback, `leaderUserIdFrom`) and the panel smoke (the leader's section, no *Under $10,000*, the *word optional* count, no call sheet for an all-optional group).

## Design calls

- **The leader is the one live master**, not the signed-in user: `chooseCompanyOwnerUserId` falls back to the viewer so a fresh environment can save rows, but a worklist that filed nobody's GCs under whoever was looking would say something false. With two live masters the leader is unknown and the old fallback heading returns — honest, and visible.
- **No subtotal line for the small GCs** inside a section (the to-do's open question): the heading's *N under $10,000, word optional* and the row's optional Word step already say it.
- **Ask by link / Call sheet only where a word is owed** — the two panel tests that pinned "never for the ones under the line" keep their meaning: a section of only small GCs has nothing to ask.
