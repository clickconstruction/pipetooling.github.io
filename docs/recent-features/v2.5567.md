# v2.5567 — Dashboard: a Training card under My Time, in one colour (2026-10-15)

Punch list #111, PR 4 of six (the course page is v2.5566). The mock-up's first spot: the card where a person meets Training, at the bottom of the Dashboard under My Time, where they clock in. No migration; no edge function.

- **`src/components/dashboard/DashboardTrainingSection.tsx`**: reads the catalog and the person's own record (`loadTrainingCatalog`, `loadMyTraining`), runs `personStandings` for the role and `trainingVerdict` for the line, and draws one verdict band in the state's colour (*1 course to do* red, *1 course to take again* amber, *You are caught up* green) and one row per course: the title, the line under it (`standingWords`: *Done Oct 3, 2025 · was due Oct 3, 12 days ago*; *Started · 12 lessons done*; *Required for your role · not started*) and the one press (`standingPress`: **Start**, **Continue**, **Take it again**, a quiet **Read it again**), each opening `/training/<slug>`. Renders nothing, and tells the dock so, while nothing is required of the role, no course is on, the tables are not on prod yet or a read fails: the card is a nudge, never an alarm about the app.
- **`src/pages/Dashboard.tsx`**: the mount under My Vehicle with its `dash-training` anchor, and the dock entry *Training* after *Me*, visible only when the card drew (`trainingDockVisible`, reported by the card like My Inbox's).
- **Tests**: `DashboardTrainingSection.render.test.tsx` (the three colours and their presses, Start opening the course, Continue with the lessons done, nothing drawn for a role the course is not required of, an off course or missing tables, the dock told either way).
- **Docs**: `DASHBOARD_SECTIONS_ARCHITECTURE.md` (a row in the master table and the role table), `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` §23.

Not run live before the merge: the card shows only once the migration is on prod and a dev turns the course on. Then the walk: a helper signs in, reads the red card, presses Start, passes the course, and reads the green line with the day it is due again.
