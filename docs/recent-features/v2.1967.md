# v2.1967 — Partner ledger: charges-at-date (2026-08-21)

Owner decision, simplifying the pending story: on the office Ledger tab, **charge-type offsets (backcharge / damage / utility_overage) book at their `occurred_date` and move the running balance immediately**, whether or not a statement has attached them yet. The headline becomes one number again (posted math now includes charges; for Bryan it equals the old "settle-up" figure, −$1,008.13).

Mechanics ([`PartnershipLedgerTab.tsx`](../../src/components/partnerships/PartnershipLedgerTab.tsx) + kernel [`partnerLedgerJournal.ts`](../../src/lib/partnerLedger/partnerLedgerJournal.ts)): `buildPartnerJournal` gains a `charges` input (dated, signed, kind deduction/addition; 1 new test — 12 in file). The tab fetches ALL of the person's offsets plus `pay_stub_deductions.person_offset_id`, books every charge-type offset at its date, and **excludes statement deductions that mirror one of those offsets** so attachment never double-counts (deductions from positive-type offsets — profit-share reversals — and manual deductions keep booking on the statement week; positive-type unattached offsets keep the old pending treatment). Statement generation and the v2.1955 charge picker are untouched — attachment remains the paper record and no longer changes a charge's ledger date.

Consistency: the office headline now always equals the partner card's "with pending charges" figure (stub balance + pending net), since attached charges live inside stub math on the RPC side and unattached ones are the pending net. Client-only — no migration.
