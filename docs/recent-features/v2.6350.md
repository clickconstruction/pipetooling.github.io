# v2.6350 — A stage's checklist stays with its line when a job is split or combined (punch list #237) (2026-11-02)

The session that built #237 PR 1b read this gap from the code and left it on the card: splitting or combining a job moves a stage line, and the line's checklist answers, job items and skips keep the old `job_id`. The card asked for its own PR before the office types questions. Prod held no answer, question or report photo when this was written, so nothing has been lost yet.

## What changed

- **Migration `20261102143000_stage_checklist_rows_follow_their_line.sql`** (`docs/migrations/20261102143000_stage_checklist_rows_follow_their_line.md`): the trigger `jobs_ledger_fixtures_carry_checklist`, so a line that moves carries its rows in `stage_checklist_answers`, `stage_checklist_templates` and `stage_checklist_skips`; the trigger `stage_checklist_answers_on_stage_job`, so an answer's job is always its line's job; `migrate_job_ledger_costs_and_delete` restated with `report_photos` moving beside `reports`; and a repair of any row an earlier split stranded.
- **The words**: the combine guide lists stage checklists among what moves, and the Separate tab says a line's checklist goes with it.
- **The bed**: `supabase/tests/stage_checklist_items/30_stranded_rows.sql` and `40_follow_the_line.sql`, run by `scripts/pgtest-stage-checklist-items.sh`.

## What the bed showed

The scenario answers a stage, adds job items and skips, then splits and combines as the people who would press them, and compares every row against a record taken before the moves. Run with no migration ([the red run](https://github.com/clickconstruction/pipetooling.github.io/actions/runs/41482171938), 2026-11-02), it confirmed what the card read from the code. After the split the new job's sheet read *answers 0, items 0, skips 0* where it should read 6, 1 and 1, and eleven rows sat on the old job. After the first combine eighteen rows were gone or changed: nine answers, four job items, four skips, and the company row whose `promoted_from` was cleared. The report photo went with them, and a press naming the deleted job was refused. With the migration ([the green run](https://github.com/clickconstruction/pipetooling.github.io/actions/runs/41482178736)) nothing is lost: 51 checks after the first scenario's 141.

## Decisions

- **A trigger on the line, not a line in each function.** The two function bodies are long and get restated, and the next restatement or a third door could forget. A line that moves carries its rows, whatever moved it. The combine is restated once anyway, for the report photos, which belong beside the reports and not on the line.
- **Only `job_id` changes.** The stamp and `updated_at` stay as they were, since nobody answered anything. The bed compares each whole row but its job.
- **A late press lands, an authoring press refuses.** A sheet opened before the move writes the job it was opened on. An answer takes its job from its line, since the crew's press is true and the line says where it belongs, and an outbox replay gets the same. An item or a skip naming the wrong job keeps its refusal, *That stage is not on this job.*: someone authoring should reopen and look. A side effect, on purpose: the gate now asks about the line's real job, so a field user who names their own job answers no stage on another job.
- **A report's photos follow the report, in a combine.** Every combine was deleting the source's `report_photos` rows while their reports moved, whatever a checklist did. An answer named by one lost its photo through the delete mirror, which is why this is here and not on a later card. A split leaves reports on the old job, so the photo stays there and the moved answer keeps naming it.
- **Nothing moves in the bucket.** A waiting shot keeps its old path. The sweeps read the path from the row and now file it under the right job. Until the sweep runs, the sheet may draw no thumbnail for it, since the bucket's gate reads the job from the path. The answer and the shot are both safe.

## What this does not cover

- Deleting a job outright, or moving its costs to a bid, still takes its stage lines and their checklists. No line is left to carry them to.
- **The job form's billing save deletes and re-inserts every line of the job with new ids** (`writeBillingSlice`, `fixtureInsertRows`), and `ON DELETE CASCADE` on `fixture_id` takes every checklist row on the job with them. A split and a combine are now safe, but an edit to a job's Specific Work is not. The card records it, and it needs its own PR before the office types questions.
- A combine still deletes rows in the other tables that cascade from the source job and that it does not move: 53 on prod's catalog on 2026-11-02, `job_box_photos`, `job_containers` and `job_contracts` among them. Some of them are by design. This PR moved only what an answer needs.
