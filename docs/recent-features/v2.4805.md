# v2.4805 — Map: the records the map cannot place, in one sheet (2026-10-07)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. PR 4 of the Map page refresh ([`to-dos/map-page-refresh/`](../../to-dos/map-page-refresh/README.md)), after [v2.4804](v2.4804.md).

## Why

Four addresses failed to geocode on every visit and their progress list sat in the page header, pushing the map down; nothing listed the records with no address at all (66 of them, most with "." for an address); the far pins were a block under the map; and the Google re-check hid behind a Debug corner.

## What changed

- **[`mapPageUnplaced.ts`](../../src/lib/map/mapPageUnplaced.ts)** (pure): `mapPageNotFound` groups the records with no coordinates by address with the geocoder's reason (`geocodeAddressRows`' error words) and counts the addresses still being placed; `unplacedLine` reads *Placing 4 addresses…* while the geocoder runs, else *70 records have no map location yet*; `farShortLine`, `unplacedName`.
- **[`useMapPageData`](../../src/hooks/useMapPageData.ts)** keeps the records it used to skip — no address, or one too short to key — as `unplaced` (kind, id, name, the link, the address when there is one).
- **[`MapAddressSheet`](../../src/components/map/MapAddressSheet.tsx)**: *Records the map cannot place* — **No address** (each record a link, *has only "."* when the field holds a stub), **Could not be found** (the address, the reason, *Check on Google Maps ↗*, the records), **Far from the office** (the address, the count and miles, *Show on the map*, **Re-check with Google** for dev / master / the assistant-like roles — `geocode-one` `refresh_google_only`, as the review window does; the sheet says whether Google moved it or agrees, and reloads when it moved), and *Review every geocode…* to the old `MapGeocodeReviewModal`. A row's record opens where it lives.
- **The page**: the line under the map (`data-map-misses`) — *70 records have no map location yet · fix their addresses · 2 addresses far from the office · check them* — opens the sheet; the far addresses here are over every placed record, whatever the chips show (the far pins kept out of the fit are still over the chips' selection). The geocoding progress list, its *Open* chooser, the *Resolving addresses…* line, the far list block and the Debug corner are gone.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **One place for every miss.** No address, not found and far away are three ways the same question reads — *where is this?* — and the sheet answers all three with the record one click away.
- **The header is the map's again.** What the geocoder is doing is one quiet line under the map, not a list over it.
- **Not done:** typing the address in the sheet itself (the Bid Board's sheet saves bids in place; jobs and estimates would need their own writes) — the record is one click away and the form has the address rules; the four addresses the geocoder re-asks on every visit are still re-asked (a cached miss belongs to `geocode-address-batch`); the Precincts chip (PR 5).
