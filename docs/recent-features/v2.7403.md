# v2.7403 — People → Subs: take teammates off a sub's sheet (sub labor train) (2026-11-15)

Client only, no migration. Punch list #346, on the kernel of v2.7383 (`rideAlongCleanUp.ts`) and the page of v2.7388. Taunya, 2026-11-15, on the clean-up of the old sheets: "Yes, build it".

## Why

Picking a job in the sheet form used to tick every person on that job's team (fixed in v2.7394). On prod that left 8 sheets that name a sub and two or three teammates. The teammate is not paid from the sheet. Their name lists the sheet on their own screens, and People → Review and the Team Summary book an even share of the sheet's cost to each name (the owner's call of 2026-10-09).

## What landed

- `PeopleSubsTab.tsx`: a line under the roster from `rideAlongOfferWords`, with *Take the teammates off…*. It draws only when a sub's sheet names a teammate.
- `RideAlongCleanUpWindow.tsx`: the sheets as a list, each with who keeps it and who comes off (`rideAlongRowWords`). Every ready sheet starts ticked. A sheet with two subs, or a sub the roster cannot find, is held with its reason and a door, and a hold never stops the rest. The button and its foot come from `batchWords`. The window shows that no money moves and what does move in reviews (`rideAlongPlan`).
- The save, one sheet at a time: `people_labor_jobs.assigned_to_name` becomes the sub's name, the database's own trigger (`sync_people_labor_job_assignees`) rebuilds the sheet's people from it, and the sub's row is tied by id as the sheet form does. A sheet the database refuses is listed afterwards with its reason and a door (`batchOutcomeWords`), and the run goes on past it.
- Guide `review-your-subs`: a section on taking teammates off a sub's sheet.

## Running it

Nothing runs by itself. The 8 sheets change when someone with the page presses the button. The press moves sheet cost from the teammates' Review panels onto the subs, so the owner should hear of it first.

## Tests

`RideAlongCleanUpWindow.render.test.tsx` (5): the ticks, the hold, the count on the press, a run of only what is ticked, a dead press with nothing ticked, the held sheet's door, and the words after a run that one sheet refused. `PeopleSubsTab.render.test.tsx`: the several-names test also reads the offer line and opens the window.
