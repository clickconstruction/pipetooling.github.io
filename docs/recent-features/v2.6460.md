# v2.6460 — A combine names the Lien desk draft it sets aside on the target's activity (punch list #252) (2026-11-03)

v2.6458 moved a combine's money and legal rows, and a live Lien desk item of a kind the target already holds live comes over voided while the target's stands (`job_lien_desk_items_live_uniq` allows one per job and kind). The migration's comment, the guide, the card, the fragment and the release note all said the desk still reads such an item. It does not: every desk reader filters `voided_at IS NULL` (`useLienDeskData`, `list_lien_notice_months`, the legal packet, the GC-on-notice board), so the draft left every screen with no trace.

The owner weighed a hold instead and kept the void on 2026-11-03. Two facts decided it. No press clears a live desk item (`lienDeskIo.ts` has pull back, hold, approve, sign and send; only a dev may delete under `job_lien_desk_items_delete_dev`), so a hold would have had no door. And nothing the desk tracks is lost: `list_lien_notice_months` works a job's due months out from its bills and work, its noticed months from `job_lien_filings` and its missed months from `missed` items, and a combine moves all of those; the live draft is only joined for display.

## What changed

- **Migration `20261103170000_combine_names_set_aside_desk_drafts.sql`** (`docs/migrations/20261103170000_combine_names_set_aside_desk_drafts.md`): `migrate_job_ledger_costs_and_delete` restated from 20261103160000's body with one statement before the void. Each live item it sets aside writes a `job_activity_events` row on the target: type `job_combined` (in the client's registry as the *Combined* tag, and written by nothing else, so no client change and no unknown-type drop), financial, the summary *Lien notice draft set aside in the combine — from Job #9103 · Sep 2026, Oct 2026* (the kind's words, the source's number, the months in order, a malformed month skipped), and `detail` with `desk_item_id`, `kind`, `months`, `combined_from_job_id` and a `source_id` for dedupe. The row itself stays, voided, for a dev to restore.
- **The false wording, corrected where it stood**: the guide's desk example now says the draft is set aside, leaves the desk, is named on the job activity, loses no month and can be brought back by a dev; the card's desk row and its opinion line, the v2.6458 fragment, its release note and its migration doc say what the void does. The applied migration 20261103160000 keeps its comment as history; this file's header records the correction.
- **Bed** `supabase/tests/combine_rows/20_scenario.sql`: the source's live notice now carries two months, out of order, and the target's activity must hold one Combined line for it, financial, reading exactly the summary above; no other desk item gets a line. `scripts/pgtest-combine-rows.sh` now re-applies the newest migration that creates the combine, found by its `CREATE OR REPLACE FUNCTION` line and sorted, so the next restatement needs no edit there.
