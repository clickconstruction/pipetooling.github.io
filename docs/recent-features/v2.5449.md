# v2.5449 — Job Mode: message the customer from the job, switched off for now (2026-10-13)

Residential customers in Messages, R5 (the tech's door and Job Mode; `to-dos/gc-mode/messages/RESIDENTIAL_BUILD_PLAN.md` → *R5* on branch `spike/gc-mode`; screen 2 of `customer-messages-prototype.html` v0.8 beside it). Stacked on helper 5's R2 PR 2 (#5612) with #5558's phone kernel and #5590's tech's door. The press ships behind `JOB_MODE_MESSAGES_ON = false` until `quo-send` takes a tech's text (R5's edge PR). The phone's lines read for a tech once `quick_replies_phone_tech` (#5611) is pushed. No migration, no edge function.

- **`src/lib/jobModeMessageCustomer.ts`** (kernel):
  - `phoneLinesFromRows`: the `quick_replies` rows with the phone's columns as `PhoneLine`s.
  - `onPhone`: a line whose English or Spanish needs `{amount}` or `{link}` stays off the phone. A tech sees no money, and the field cannot make the page link yet.
  - `jobModeFills`: the customer's first name, the tech's first name, the job and its address. `window` and `day` come from today's block on the job, and `back day` and `back time` from the next one. A blank with nothing to read stays out, so the fill row asks for it.
  - `sheetRows`: the phone's lines that can go, the step first, six at most, each by `shortName` and `hintFor`. `previewOf`: the line in the customer's language, filled, with the fill row's asks and `fillFirst`'s stop. `replyWaiting` and `waitingAnswer`: the dot and the answer, read from the newest said line.
  - `noteForOffice`: *Please tell Dana: …*. `SHEET_WORDS`: every word the sheet says, through `plainWordsFailures`.
- **`src/lib/jobModeMessageCustomerIo.ts`**: the reads through R5's doors and the two writes.
  - It reads the job's customer, their own contact, their thread's newest 20 lines and the phone's lines, plus the tech's blocks on the job from today on.
  - A tech makes no contact, so a customer with no thread reads as no contact, and the send names the customer.
  - `send` invokes `quo-send` with `from: 'job_mode'`, the customer, the contact, the job, the line and the words. That is the tech path's body for R5's edge PR.
  - `noteToOffice` writes a `jobs_ledger_thread_notes` row, which a tech on the job could already write.
- **`src/lib/messages/jobBox.ts`**: `JOB_MODE_MESSAGES_ON = false` and `jobModeMessagesOffered(role)`, the tech's door (`canMessageCustomersFromJobMode`) once the switch is on. It is the one place who may message a customer from a job is said.
- **`src/components/jobMode/MessageCustomerDoor.tsx`** and **`MessageCustomerSheet.tsx`**:
  - The door sits beside **Hand the phone** on the clocked-in job, with an orange dot when the customer answered.
  - The sheet is `ResponsiveModalShell`, full screen on a phone. It shows their answer, the lines as 56 px buttons with **Something else** last, then the preview in the customer's orange with the fill row and **Send to Dana**, which waits until every blank is filled.
  - Something else is a note box and **Send to the office**.
  - `DashboardJobModeCard` gains the door through `jobModeMessagesOffered` and `profileName` for `{me}`.
