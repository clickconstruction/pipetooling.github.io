# v2.7395 — Lien desk: a signature on a notice is the full name (2026-11-15)

Taunya's ask of 2026-11-15, on a screenshot of a signing line that read *Malachi* in the cursive face: *"I want to make sure that when his name is entered as a signature with his code, it is not just his first name, it is his full name?"* It was just his first name. One migration, pushed after the merge; no edge function.

## Before

A notice's signature carries a printed name, `job_lien_desk_items.signer_printed_name`, which the paper prints in the cursive face above the rule. Every path took the short name the app talks in, `users.name`:

- **His code** (`sign_lien_desk_item_with_code`, v2.5788) read `users.name` in the database.
- **A press or a drawing** (`signLienDeskItem`) wrote the name the screen handed it: `authName`, or `leaderNameFor` on someone else's screen.
- **The pen record** (`recordLienPenSignature`) wrote `leader.name`, the same short name.

Malachi's account is *Malachi*, with *Malachi Whites, Master Plumber (#RMP41130)* in **Full name and title** (`users.notes`). So the paper would have read *Malachi* over the printed line *Malachi Whites, Master Plumber (#RMP41130), Click Plumbing and Electrical*. The Release of Lien window already had the right rule (`lienWaiverSignerName`, v2.5231, and `check_lien_sign_code`, v2.5924); that migration's notes say the notice was left as it was.

Nothing wrong had printed: all 44 notices on the desk were read, and none had been signed in the app.

## What changed

- **[`src/lib/jobs/lienSigner.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienSigner.ts)**: `lienSignatureName(row)` is the one rule. A leader is named by his title line up to the first comma, else his account name. A dev is named by his account name, since a dev's notes are licence numbers. `lienSignatureNameFor(users, userId, fallback)` reads it off the desk's list. `LienSignerUser` gains `role`.
- **[`src/lib/jobs/lienDeskSignIo.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDeskSignIo.ts)**: `lienSignaturePrintedName(signerUserId, given)` reads the signer's own row at the moment of signing. `signLienDeskItem` and `recordLienPenSignature` both write that name, so every screen that signs a notice (the desk, the run, *Put a GC on notice*, the retainage pane, the pen pane) writes the same one whatever it was handed. The name handed over is the fallback when his row cannot be read. `signLienDeskItem` returns the name it wrote.
- **[`src/pages/LienDeskPage.tsx`](../../src/pages/LienDeskPage.tsx)** loads each user's `role` and passes `signatureNameFor`. **[`src/components/jobs/LienDesk.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDesk.tsx)** uses it for the grey name on the signing line and for the run's leader, so the preview is the name that will be written. The words around the line (*Malachi signs here with his code*, *waits on Malachi*) keep the short name.
- **`supabase/migrations/20261116110000_lien_notice_code_full_name.sql`**: `sign_lien_desk_item_with_code` with the same rule for the name it stamps. Its two messages keep the short name. See [`docs/migrations/20261116110000_lien_notice_code_full_name.md`](../migrations/20261116110000_lien_notice_code_full_name.md).
