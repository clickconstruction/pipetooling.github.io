## Not a send, and why

Six files moved from owed to `NOT_A_SEND`: the bid follow-up sheets (for our own account managers), the takeoff sheets (our own; the schedule that goes to the GC is filed from the cover letter tab), the cost estimate and pricing pages (they hold cost and margin), the contract reader in Settings (reading, not sending), and **partnership notices** — kept on the partnership itself (`notice_html`), never in a list the whole office reads. Two prints inside wired files stay plain, each with a comment: the Billed Awaiting Payment report and the job cost breakdown, both our own reports.

`PRINTS_OWED` is empty. From here a new print fails CI until it files or says why not.
