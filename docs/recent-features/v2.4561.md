# v2.4561 — Dashboard: the lien cards open the Lien desk on the right list (2026-10-05)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Rows 8 and 9 of the lien defects card (punch list #83, `to-dos/lien-defects.md` on PR #4528).

## The defects

- **Row 9.** The *mailed notice has no tracking number* card navigates to `?liendesk=1&liendeskPile=sent` from the Dashboard and from Quickfill. `parseStagesDeepLinks` read `liendeskPile` as `missed` or nothing, so the desk opened on every pile.
- **Row 8.** The lien deadlines card draws *N sent notices at day 10+ — letter two ›* as a secondary line with the key `letter-two`. Neither `onSecondary` handler had a branch for it: the line looked like a link and did nothing.

## The fix

- [`stagesDeepLinks.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/stagesDeepLinks.ts): `liendeskPile` accepts any `LienDeskPile` (`needs_owner`, `to_draft`, `awaiting`, `ready`, `printed`, `held`, `sent`, `missed`); anything else is no pile. `StagesLienDeskLink.pile` is typed to match.
- [`DashboardPinnedQuickRow.tsx`](../../src/components/dashboard/DashboardPinnedQuickRow.tsx) and [`QuickfillNeedsYouSection.tsx`](../../src/components/quickfill/QuickfillNeedsYouSection.tsx): `letter-two` opens the desk on `sent`, the pile whose footer sends letter two.

The tracking card keeps `sent`, not `printed` as the defects card suggested: its rows are recorded filings with no number (`lienDeadlines.ts` `trackingOwed`), and its own words send the reader to the notice's Sent row.

## Tests

`stagesDeepLinks.test.ts`: the pile case now reads `to_draft`, `sent` and `printed`, and drops a name the desk does not have.
