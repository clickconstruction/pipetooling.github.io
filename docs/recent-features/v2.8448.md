# v2.8448 — Bills sent: By day paid opens where the money is (2026-11-24)

One fault of By day paid found on the live test of 2026-11-24 (punch list #422, problem 10). No migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

This month opened on today, whatever today held. Each morning that was an empty box, *Nothing came in on …*, with no press under it. An earlier empty day offered **Go to today**, which landed on the same empty today. An older month already opened on its newest paid day.

## What changed

- `billsPaidLastPaidDay(lines, month, todayYmd)`, new and pure: the newest day of a month that money came, among the lines the chip leaves, and never a day that has not come. A credit in kind is no money.
- `billsPaidOpeningDay` opens every month on that day. Today is that day once something has come in today. A month with no money yet opens on today, or on its last day when it is an older month.
- `billsPaidEmptyDay` gives every empty day a press, and the press says where it goes (`BillsPaidEmptyNext`: its words and a chip, a day or a month):
  - **Show every way that day**, when the chip hid what came in, as before;
  - else **Go to Mon, Nov 23**, the newest day money came, by name;
  - else, with nothing in the month, **Open November 2026**: this month from an older one, the month before from this one.
- Today keeps its ring and its tile. A press on today's cell still shows today.
- The guide says which day a month opens on and what an empty day offers.

## Tests

`billsPaidDays.test.ts`: the opening day on a morning with nothing in, on a day money came, with a credit alone, in a month with none; the last paid day; each of the empty day's presses. `BillsPaidByDay.render.test.tsx`: with the clock held at Tue, Nov 24 and one payment on Monday, the view opens on Monday, today's cell shows the empty box, and **Go to Mon, Nov 23** returns. The kernel cases failed before the change.

Not drawn in a browser. The report's other idea, a short list of the week's days under an empty box, is not built.
