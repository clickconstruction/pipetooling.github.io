# v2.6768 — The bill text carries a short Click Plumbing link that never expires (2026-11-08)

Punch list #290 (`to-dos/short-bill-link.md`), PR 1 of two. The owner's ask of 2026-11-08, on the text the share row sends with a Stripe bill: *I don't like the long stripe link users get via sms when their bill is due to be paid. Can you help me turn it into a short click plumbing link? This also helps guarantee the bill won't expire.*

## What was true

- The text (`buildStripeInvoiceSmsText`) carried the row's `hosted_invoice_url`, Stripe's own link, about 160 characters, which [expires 30 days after the bill's due date](https://docs.stripe.com/invoicing/hosted-invoice-page) (30 days after finalizing with no due date, never past 120 days). After that the customer sees Stripe's *link expired, contact the business* page; the recovery page appears only for invoices Stripe itself emailed, and `create-stripe-invoice` finalizes without emailing.
- The app already had a door that never expires: `/pay/<bill id>` (v2.3754), whose `pay-link` function asks Stripe for the bill's current link each time it is opened and answers open, paid or void. The bill email's Pay now has carried it since v2.4020. The text and Copy link had not.
- The `portal-link-shell` Worker on `my.clickplumbing.com` read `/pay/<id>` as a portal slug.

## What changed

- **The Worker** (`scripts/cloudflare/portal-link-shell.worker.js`, mirrored from the dashboard edit): before the slug check, a path under `/pay/` answers a 301 to `https://clicktooling.com/pay/<the rest>`, lower-cased, as `/e/` does for an estimate. No shell and no preview card: a bill is private.
- **`BILL_SHORT_ORIGIN`** and `billShortUrl` (`src/lib/portal/portalShortOrigin.ts`, beside the estimate's), and the kernel **`billTextLink`** (`src/lib/billing/billTextLink.ts`, with its tests): the short pay page when the bill's id is at hand, else Stripe's own link. 69 characters against about 160.
- **The share row** (`StripeInvoiceSharePanel`): Text's words, Copy link (all three layouts and the text door's head) and the Email draft's *view and pay your invoice here* line carry the short link. **Customer pay page**, the office's own press, still opens Stripe. The Bills tab and View bill already handed the bill's id; the panel after Bill Customer makes a Stripe bill now does too (`ledger_invoice_id` on its result). **Collect payment**'s Copy payment link carries the same short link; Open payment page still opens Stripe.
- Words: the guides *ready-to-bill-pipeline* (the chase cluster's link sentence) and *turn a bill into a Stripe bill*; `docs/DOMAIN_CUTOVER.md`'s line on the short domain.

- The bill email (already on `/pay/<id>`), the QR code and the portal's Pay button are unchanged.
