- **The card charges by the one card rule** (`_shared/devMcpComposites.ts`, dev-mcp server 0.23.0): `get_job` reads the rule's two lookups as the window's `loadCardChargeExclusions` does. They are each card transaction's Banking → Accounting label (`mercury_transaction_drag_sort_assignments`, then `mercury_drag_sort_labels.default_key` through `overheadPartsAccountingBucketFromDefaultKey`) and the transactions on a supply-house invoice (`mercury_transaction_supply_house_invoice_links`). A lookup that fails takes nothing out, as the window's does, and `card_rule.note` names it.
