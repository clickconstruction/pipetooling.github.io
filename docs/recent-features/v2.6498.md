# v2.6498 — The Development page’s cost timeline shows the overhead landed on the site (2026-11-04)

Punch list #257, PR 2 of 3, after PR 1 (v2.6493, #7012) drew the Money look's Timeline with its red and green lines.

- **The amber band**: `useSiteOverhead` keeps each job's day lines beside its share (`lines` on the read, `SiteOverheadRead` in `scoreboard.ts`; the Scoreboard ignores them), and the page hands them to the Timeline's jobs (`overheadDaysFromLines` in `siteTimeline.ts`, `overheadDays` on `SiteTimelineJob`). `buildSiteTimeline` merges every job's days into the Job Summary kernel's band: the fill between cost and true cost, two jobs' shares on one day summed, the trailing bucket while the site stays open, the tooltip's *Overhead to date* and *True cost* lines, all the shared view's own. The key says *since Feb 19, 2026, where office cost begins* (`overheadSinceWords(OVERHEAD_POOL_FIRST_YMD)`), the pool's first day, as the Job window's chart says it when the window's floor cuts off a job's history.
- **The reads**: the day ledger, the page's heaviest read, now starts when the Timeline opens as it does when the Scoreboard opens, through the same session cache, so the two views charge one ledger. The chart draws its lines as soon as the costs and the clock are in and says *The overhead ledger is still reading. The amber band lands with it.* until the ledger lands; a failed ledger leaves the band off and the line says so, never a false zero.
- **Parity**: the site's end overhead equals the sum of the jobs' own `endOverhead`, and a chip narrows the band to that job's days (`siteTimeline.test.ts`).
