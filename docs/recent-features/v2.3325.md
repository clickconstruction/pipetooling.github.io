# v2.3325 — Legal portal PR 5: the firm runs its own inbox (2026-09-11)

Migration `20260911204604_legal_firm_recipients.sql` ([`docs/migrations/20260911204604_legal_firm_recipients.md`](../migrations/20260911204604_legal_firm_recipients.md)); **new edge function `legal-notify-dispatch`** (cron + public confirm/unsubscribe links); `submit-legal-portal` and `legal-portal` changed (**redeploy all three after the push**). Stacked on v2.3322 (PR 4). Last of five.

## Why

The owner's rule: the firm decides who at the firm hears from us and under what circumstances — not the office guessing which paralegal wants what. The simplification agreed before the build: one choice per person (now or digest) plus only-my-matters, with confirm-by-click and one-click unsubscribe as the two non-negotiables; the per-trigger matrix waits for a firm that asks for it.

## What shipped

- **Recipients** (`legal_firm_recipients`) with `mode`, `scope`, digest weekday + time, confirm and unsubscribe token hashes, `paused_at`, `removed_at`. **Queue** (`legal_notification_queue`) fed by triggers: a matter released (`referred`), an office `answer` entry, a matter pulled back.
- **[`legal-notify-dispatch`](../../supabase/functions/legal-notify-dispatch/index.ts)** — cron `POST` (`X-Cron-Secret`, `1-56/5` lane): drains the queue to *now* recipients (scope `mine` = named as the matter's handling person), then sends each *digest* recipient one email on their weekday once their time has arrived (open matters + events since their last digest; `last_digest_at`). A paused firm sends nothing. `GET ?confirm=` / `?unsubscribe=` serve the two links as plain HTML pages. Sends via `sendEmailViaResend`; portal link from `APP_ORIGIN`.
- **`submit-legal-portal`** gains `recipient_add` (12-person cap, one confirmation email with a hashed token), `recipient_rules`, `recipient_stop` / `recipient_resume`, `recipient_resend`.
- **`legal-portal`** returns `recipients` + `firmPaused`; the page's **Notifications** panel lists people with the right-away / digest switch, weekday chips, time, scope, stop/resume, resend, and the add form (honeypot).
- **Kernel** `releaseRecipients(firm, handling, recipients)` (+1 test) → buckets `now` · `digest` · `unconfirmed` · `stopped`, the firm row as the fallback with no people yet.
- **Desk** — the Mark attorney ready sheet lists who hears by bucket; **✉ Firm's emails** on the header: read-only table + **Remove** (`legal_firm_recipient_remove`) + **Pause all / Resume** (`legal_firm_set_paused`).
- Email catalog: `legal_referral`, `legal_digest`, `legal_recipient_confirm`. REPORT_SUBSCRIPTIONS inventory rows. Help guide `manage-who-at-the-law-firm-gets-emails`; EDGE_FUNCTIONS; ACCESS_CONTROL.

## Verified

Legal suites, typecheck, lint, theme. Live after push + deploy: add a person on the portal → confirmation email → click → confirmed; mark an account ready → the *now* recipient's email arrives within a tick; a digest recipient's Monday 07:00 fires once; unsubscribe pauses; the office pause holds the queue.

## Notes for the next session

- Digest `digested_at` is stamped when the firm's last eligible digest recipient of the day has been sent — good enough for one firm; revisit if two firms with different days ever share a queue.
- Wording is fixed (catalog `hardcoded`); moving it to editable templates is the `change-the-wording-of-an-outgoing-email` path.
- The per-trigger Now/Digest/Off matrix from the prototype is deliberately not built.
