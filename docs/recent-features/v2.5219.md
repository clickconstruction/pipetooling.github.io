# v2.5219 — The Bridge says how sure its cash forecast is (2026-10-10)

PR 3 of the Bridge's redraw (plan and mock-up at https://claude.ai/artifact/2GciUJPCjaTysjpTEULWyJ). Client only, dev only. No migration, no edge function.

- **What was wrong**: every late invoice landed on one day, today + 14 (`LATE_RECEIPT_GRACE_DAYS`), so on 2026-10-10 41 late invoices worth $227.5k all "arrived" on Oct 24, the same day as $54.1k of sub labor and payroll — and the readout said only *lowest point $15.4k on Oct 12, stays above the floor*. Nothing said the window rested on that one assumption.
- **The spread** (`src/lib/bridge/cashConfidence.ts`, `lateWithinDays`): a late invoice is assumed to land within the customer's own pay pace from today (their median days, else the company's), held to 7–45 days; 14 when no pace is known. `Bridge.tsx` passes it to `scheduleReceipt` as `graceDays`, and the receipt's reason reads *18d late — assumed within 10d by their pace*. `DOUBTFUL_AFTER_DAYS` (60) is unchanged.
- **The band** (`receiptsConfidence`, `BridgeCashConfidence.tsx`): each receipt carries a `basis` — `promised`, `customer`, `company`, `default` or `late` — and the band under the chart shows promised / by pace / late-and-assumed as shares of the window's receipts, with the three amounts and the late count.
- **The day that carries the window** (`carryingDay`, `carryingSentence`): the forecast day with the most receipts when it holds 30% or more of them — *87% of the window's receipts ($227k) land on Oct 24, $227k of it 41 late invoices assumed to land. That day also owes $54.1k. If they slip, that is the day to watch.* — in an amber box under the band; none when receipts are spread.
