# v2.6260 — The report email carries the photos, and the push counts them (punch list #229, PR 5 of six) (2026-11-01)

Punch list #229, PR 5. A report's subscribers, including an outside address such as a GC, got the report's words and no sign of its photos; the email goes at Save, minutes before the `photo-save` sweep files anything, so there was no Drive link to send either. Two edge functions change; no migration. The owner's call 3 on the card, taken as recommended: attach, rather than link.

## What changed

- **`_shared/reportPhotoEmail.ts`** (new, pure, held by `src/lib/reportPhotoEmail.test.ts`): `planReportEmailPhotos` decides how each `report_photos` row rides, in the order they were taken: a shot still in the bucket is **attached** until ten are, or 8 MB in all; one the sweep filed is a **link** to its Drive file; one past the cap, or with no bytes anywhere, is **named**. `reportEmailPhotoFilename` numbers the attachment (*1 Rough In · Luis Ortega.jpg*) and keeps it safe for a mail client; `reportPhotosEmailBlock` draws **Photos · 2 · attached** with one line a photo (*Rough In · Luis Ortega · 303 KB · attached*, *in the customer's Drive folder* as a link, *on its way to the customer's Drive folder, not attached*); `reportPhotoCountClause` is the push's *· 2 photos*; `photoBytesBase64` turns the bytes into what Resend takes, in pieces.
- **`_shared/fieldReportEmail.ts`**: `ReportContent.photos` and the block after the fields, in the HTML and the text; a report with no photos reads as before (its test still refuses the word *Photos* on the sample).
- **`send-report-email`**: `resolveReportContent` returns the content and the attachments, the bytes read once per report with the service role from the `report-photos` bucket, never once per subscription; a shot that does not read is named instead, so the words stay true. `sendReportToSubscription` passes the attachments beside the sent-copy filing, so a report that leaves the company is kept with its pictures (`docs/SENT_COPIES.md`). Both modes, `auto` and `manual`.
- **`send-report-notification`**: one count of `report_photos` by `report_id`, and the body ends *· 2 photos*.
- **Docs**: `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` → both sections; the guide *file and review field reports* says the email carries the photos. The Glossary's **Report photos** entry gains its line in the card's closing docs PR, since five PRs amending one line would conflict in turn.

## Decisions

- **Attach, not link.** The email goes at Save and Drive has nothing yet; a GC on a subscription wants the picture in the mail. The cap keeps a ten-shot report under 8 MB; Resend's own limit is 40 MB.
- **A filed photo links.** A manual Send now days later finds the bucket copy gone and `drive_file_id` on the row; the email says *in the customer's Drive folder* with the file's link rather than nothing.
- **Once per report.** A report with three subscribers downloads its photos once; the base64 is shared across the sends.
- **The sample email stays without photos.** Settings → What the team sees renders `sampleFieldReportContent`, and `fieldReportEmail.test.ts` asserts no *Photos* word on it; a sample with photos is a small follow-up if the owner wants to see the block there.

## Deploy

From the main checkout after the merge: `supabase functions deploy send-report-email` and `supabase functions deploy send-report-notification` (`npm run check:edge-drift` names both). Until the deploy the email goes as before, without photos.
