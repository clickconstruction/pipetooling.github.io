# v2.3258 — Overhead allocation kernel: hours-weighted smoothing + carry for open jobs (2026-09-10)

Client only; no migration. PR A of three. **No user-visible change** — the kernel ships behind the legacy settings; PR B wires Job Summary and the dev dials, PR C the remaining consumers and the app-wide default.

## Why

Owner review of Job Summary's true profit (2026-09-10): "apply overhead to jobs that were not worked but were open … smooth the cost of overhead over a rolling 30 day period … perhaps we put that 30 days on a slider the dev can manage." The Overhead Dials study ran the three constants over the real 12-month ledger (365 days, 180 jobs with field time, 174 status spans, the Jobs table for revenue) and the owner approved the recommendation: **30 days · 20% carry · 14-day idle cap · open = Working → Billed**.

What the study found, and what the kernel therefore does:

- **Spreading over calendar days is wrong; spreading over hours is right.** An even spread gives a one-hour Saturday a whole day's smoothed pool. Each source day's pool is instead shared by the **field hours** of the following N days (the activity slice) and the **open-job counts** of those days (the carry slice). That cut the spread of overhead per field hour across jobs from a CV of 0.52 to 0.30 (0.57 → 0.31 on one- and two-day jobs) and tightened the 10th–90th percentile daily rate from $5–$40 to $8–$18.
- **Past 30 days the window stops mattering** (a +7-day shift moves the average job 20% at 1 day, 11% at 7, 9% at 14, 7% at 21, 6–7% from 30 on) while more cost pushes past today (3% in flight at 30, 6% at 45, 8% at 60).
- **The pool follows field hours, not the open count** (weekly correlation 0.85 vs 0.59; the open count adds nothing once hours are in). Carry is a policy that open jobs pay for being open, so it stays modest.
- **Carry needs an idle cap**: half of all open job-days since January are >14 days past the job's last field time. A 14-day cap drops the typical day's open count from 34 to 19 so a forgotten Working job cannot soak carry for months.

## What shipped

- **`lib/jobs/overheadAllocation.ts`** (+24 tests): `OverheadAllocationSettings` `{ smoothDays 1–60, carryShare 0–1, idleCapDays | null, openDef 'status' | 'worked' }`, `OVERHEAD_ALLOCATION_LEGACY` (1 · 0% · none — today's day-share), `OVERHEAD_ALLOCATION_RECOMMENDED` (30 · 20% · 14 · status), `normalizeOverheadAllocationSettings` (parses stored JSON, clamps every field), `overheadAllocationLabel`, `buildOverheadAllocation(ledger, settings)` → per-day rows (raw pool, landed, activity, carry, unallocated, open/worked counts, $/field-hour, $/open-job, per-job split), per-job shares (`JobOverheadShare` + carry/activity/openDays), and totals with the reconciliation `pool + carriedIn = charged + unallocated + inFlight` (`overheadAllocationReconciles`). Memoized per ledger + settings (a `WeakMap`), since `enrichJobSummaryRows` asks once per row.
  - Fallback rules: a slice with no takers in its window goes to the other side; activity only becomes carry when carry is switched on, so at 0% an open job is never charged — the property that keeps legacy identical.
  - Open (status definition): from the Working move or the first field day, whichever is earlier, to the Billed/Paid move; no history → field days, still-Working → the window end. Idle cap: `days since last field time ≤ cap` or `days since the span start ≤ cap`.
- **`jobDayLedger.ts`**: `JobDayLedger.leadDays?` (the days before the window, zero-filled; consumed only by the allocation), `buildJobDayLedger({ leadStartYmd })` builds them without touching the window's job roll-up, totals or rates; serialized/deserialized with the ledger. `allocateJobOverheadDayShare(ledger, jobId, settings?)`, `jobOverheadByMethod(…, { settings })` and `unallocatedJobDayOverhead(ledger, settings?)` delegate to the allocation for non-legacy settings and keep the original path otherwise (pinned by a job-by-job identity test). `JobOverheadDayLine` gains optional `activityUsd` / `carryUsd` / `openJobs`.
- **`loadJobDayLedger.ts`**: `leadDays` (default `JOB_DAY_LEDGER_LEAD_DAYS` = 60, the slider's ceiling) widens the session and office-parts fetches before the window; invoices, pending counts and prior hours stay window-scoped. Loader test pins both shapes.
- **`useJobSummaryView`**: ledger session cache key bumped to `jobDayLedger:v5:` so older cached ledgers (no lead) reload.
