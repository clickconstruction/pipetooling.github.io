## What the walk found

1. **The name.** The card took the customer's name through `firstName`, whose greeting cuts a name to its first word. A company customer read *Where it stands with Big*. `threadSummary.ts`'s `summaryName` now names them as the customer's door does: the name the customer goes by on their thread, else the customer's full name. The lines follow (`theirName`), and *Our word* passes the name to `owedSentence` as what they go by, so it is never cut either.
2. **Empty jobs.** Every job opened on the walk had no line tied to it, so the card drew three lines of *nothing*, about 190 px at the top of the window. A card with no line, no promise of theirs and nothing we owe is now `empty` and draws one quiet line, *No calls, texts or emails with ‹name› on this job yet, and nothing promised either way.*, with no fold and no presses.
3. **Their word and Our word read too little.** The promise and owed reads covered only the people the job's lines name, so on a job with no tied line they could never show. `threadSummaryIo.ts` now also reads the job's customer's own contact and the contacts of the people at that customer (`contacts.customer_id`, `customer_contact_persons`, `contacts.customer_contact_person_id`), failing soft to the lines' people. The hook's key carries the customer.

And on a phone (`narrow`, the window's `useNarrowViewport640`) each label sits over its words instead of in a 112 px column, and the presses are 36 px tall.

The walk also confirmed: no sideways scroll on the card or the page at any width; the tokens resolve in both themes (the plain text near-black on pale blue in light, near-white on deep blue in dark; the stamp's grey at 4.5:1 or better on its background). The doors to lines could not be seen live, since no job the walk opened had a tied line; the tests cover them.
