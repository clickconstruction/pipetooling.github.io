# v2.4603 — Bill Customer: the lien waiver window opens only when the tick was there (2026-10-05)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. A hazard found re-reading the Bill Customer map (PR #4556, quirk 29).

## The defect

[`SendRecordInvoiceModal`](../../src/components/jobs/SendRecordInvoiceModal.tsx) seeds `waiverAfterSend` true for any job with a GC. [`BillCustomerLienReleaseStrip`](../../src/components/jobs/BillCustomerLienReleaseStrip.tsx) draws **Send the lien waiver with this bill** only when it is given the bill, which the modal passes only on a `kind: 'invoice'` open. On a `kind: 'job'` open of a GC job there was no tick to untick, yet all three send paths called `onSentWantWaiver` and the Release of Lien window opened.

## The owner's call

No tick, no hand-off. The waiver follows the bill only when the tick was drawn and left on, which is what [v2.4275](v2.4275.md) described. A bill sent from an open on the job goes alone; its waiver comes from the Bill tab's cell.

## The fix

- **Kernel** [`lienWaiverRelease.ts`](../../src/lib/jobsDocuments/lienWaiverRelease.ts): `lienWaiverTickForBill(job, invoiceId)` returns the pick for the bill about to go, or null when no tick is drawn (a direct job, no bill named, a bill not on the job).
- The strip draws the tick from it. The modal's three send paths hand off on `waiverHandoff`, which is `waiverAfterSend` and a non-null tick from the same kernel, so the two cannot disagree again.

## Tests

- `lienWaiverRelease.test.ts`: the tick's four null cases and the drawn one.
- `SendRecordInvoiceModal.waiverHandoff.render.test.tsx`: the first assertions on the hand-off. Opened on a GC job, no tick and no hand-off (fails against the old code). Opened on one bill, the tick is on and the send hands off that bill. Unticked, the bill goes alone.
- Guide *send a GC our lien waiver* says when the tick shows.
