# v2.8087 — The GC’s page: every press is a full thumb wide (2026-11-20)

Found by the Fable 5.2 audit of punch list #382 (Before we start). A press-size fix on the card a general contractor answers from a phone. No migration, no function, no words changed.

## What was wrong

The audit read the presses of [`PortalStartChecks.tsx`](../../src/components/portal/PortalStartChecks.tsx) against the 44 px a thumb needs:

- **The tick box was 30 px.** `box(on)` drew the tick as a 30 × 30 `button role="checkbox"`. It is the card's main control, and the card's own plan calls it *a large tick box*.
- **The two words presses were about 16 px tall.** *All of these are ready* and *I am adding a photo* use `linkPress`, which had `padding: 0` and no height.
- **The day and its send were 40 px.** The date field and *Send the day* had `minHeight: 40`.

Only *Yes, N are ready* was 44 px.

## The fix

Every press on the card is now 44 px, and nothing on the card moves:

- **The tick box.** The `button role="checkbox"` is a 44 × 44 press with no border and no fill (`boxPress`), and the 30 px box is drawn inside it as a span (`data-start-box`, `aria-hidden`). The press carries `margin: -7`, so the drawn box sits exactly where the button sat and the words beside it keep their line. `role`, `aria-checked`, `aria-label`, `disabled` and the click stay on the button. A bullet that waits on its photo keeps its plain 30 px box, which was never a press.
- **The words presses.** `linkPress` gains `minHeight: 44`, `display: 'inline-flex'`, `alignItems: 'center'` and `margin: '-14px 0'`. The press grows 14 px above and below its words and the margin takes that back, so the header line and each bullet's box stay the height they were.
- **The day and its send.** `minHeight: 40` becomes `minHeight: 44` on both.

The page stays pinned light and keeps its `portalTheme` constants.

The old and the new styles were laid out side by side in Chromium on a static page at 390 px, two bullets each. It was not walked on a live GC page.

| Piece | Before | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Card height | 223.2 px | 223.2 px |
| Drawn tick box | 30 × 30 at (39.2, 60) | 30 × 30 at (39.2, 60) |
| Tick box press | 30 × 30 | 44 × 44 |
| *All of these are ready* press | 137 × 16 | 137 × 44, its words on the same line |
| *I am adding a photo* press | 126.5 × 16 | 126.5 × 44, its words on the same line |

No two presses overlap: the tick box's press ends 5 px left of the photo press, and a bullet's photo press ends 8 px above the next bullet's tick box press.

## Test

[`PortalStartChecks.render.test.tsx`](../../src/components/portal/PortalStartChecks.render.test.tsx) gains *every press on the card is a full thumb*. It holds that each `role="checkbox"` is a button at least 44 px wide and tall with the 30 px box drawn inside it, that *All of these are ready* and all three *I am adding a photo* presses have `min-height: 44px`, that the day field, *Send the day* and the send press are 44 px, and that a press on the box still ticks it and draws the tick in the box.

Run both ways: against the component as it stood on `main` the test fails (`expected 30 to be greater than or equal to 44`), and with the fix all 8 tests in the file pass. The seven older tests (tick, send, photo, the day, a refusal, crew confirmed, a sample link) are unchanged.
