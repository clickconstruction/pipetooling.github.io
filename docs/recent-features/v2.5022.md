# v2.5022 — GC Review: a GC's statement waits for this week's check (2026-10-09)

Client, plus `send-gc-statement-email` and `gc-statement-email-dispatch` (**redeploy both after merge**). No migration. The owner's call of **2026-10-09**, sent by Punchlist: *GC statements may not go out unchecked*. The owner chose the gate over rewording the promise.

The worklist row's **Send** was already locked until the GC's bills were checked this week. Its title read *Check the bills first — a statement never goes out unchecked*. **Share → Draft Message**, **Copy** and **Print** on the same row were not locked, and neither were the two functions behind them.

## What changed

- **One rule, `_shared/gcStatementGate.ts`:**
  - `GC_STATEMENT_UNCHECKED_WORDS` holds the worklist's words. The row's locked Send, the doors, the send function and the dispatcher all use this one string.
  - `gcCertWeekStartYmd` gives the Monday of the company-calendar week, the certification's key.
  - `gcStatementGate(live, weekCerts)` takes the latest certification of the week and compares it with the GC's statement now, outside Collections, job by job. A cent apart is a change. When nothing is outstanding outside Collections, there is nothing to check: the week's worklist leaves such a GC out, and its statement is not held.
  - `gcStatementRefusedNote` builds the dispatcher's stamp.
- **The doors.** GC Review's Share menu disables **Draft Message**, **Copy** and **Print** while `gcStatementHeld` says so, and shows the words in an amber note.
  - `gcStatementHeld` is new in `gcReviewCertification.ts`. A statement is held unless the GC is certified and unchanged. A GC with no group outside Collections, or nothing owed on it, is not held.
  - Before the week's checks are read, the note says *Reading this week's checks…*. A failed read says so, instead of calling a checked GC unchecked.
  - **Find a check…**, **Mark sent**, the portal links and **Print unpaid invoices** are untouched. The certify-and-send path opens the dialog straight after a check, as before.
- **`send-gc-statement-email`.** For one GC, `readGcStatementGate` (`_shared/gcStatementGateIo.ts`) reads the GC's certifications of the week. It rebuilds the GC's group the dispatcher's way: `get_gc_statement_email_payload` without Collections, each job's total, and the one payment rule.
  - Held: it answers 200 `{ success: false, refused: 'unchecked', error }` before the dedupe and the send. Draft Message shows the error in the dialog.
  - A read that fails answers 503 and sends nothing.
  - Development and whole-report sends are not gated.
- **`gc-statement-email-dispatch`.** A single GC's statement runs the same gate over the group it has just built, with Collections rows left out.
  - Held: the request is stamped `refused: <the words> (not checked this week | changed since it was checked)` with `sent_at`. The response counts it in a new `refused`, and a weekly chain still advances.
  - A read that fails throws to the attempt's retry.
- **Moved, unchanged:** the payload types, `payloadJobIds`, `attachJobTotals` and `applyPaymentRule` went from the dispatcher's `render.ts` to `_shared/gcStatementPayload.ts`. `render.ts` re-exports them, so its importers are untouched.

### Why the server compares job by job

The board keys a job with one open bill by the job. The payload keys the same row by the bill. On prod, for the week of 2026-10-05, every certified GC whose bills had not moved matched job by job (9 of 12). The other three had real changes:

- Done Right Foundation got a $250 bill at 19:54, two hours after its 17:43 check.
- Michael Holub was paid in full on 10-06.
- Heron's job went to Collections on 10-07.

The client keeps its own check bill by bill (`gcGroupCertStatus`), so the doors are as strict as the row's Send. The server can only be the same or more lenient: a move between two bills of one job that keeps the job's total passes it.

## Left as it was

- **Share all**, **Print all** and development statements are not one GC's statement.
- A refused scheduled send is closed like the other skips, rather than waiting in the queue for the check, because waiting rows would crowd the dispatcher's batch of ten. Once the GC is checked, the office sends it from the row.
