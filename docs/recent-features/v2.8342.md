# v2.8342 — Dashboard: the lien month card is gone, so the Lien desk is said once (2026-11-23)

Punch list #423, PR 3 of 3 by the card's numbering, built second. The owner's ask of 2026-11-23: *On the leaders dashboard we see lien desk information in two places and I don't love that … maybe that looks like dropping the second card, the lien month.* He picked option 1 on the card's mock-up, which drops it, and gave the go to build everything the same day. No migration and no edge function.

## What was there

`DashboardLienMonthCard` (v2.6650, punch list #235, PR 3) stood right after the Billing pipeline for a dev, a leader, an assistant and a controller. It drew the lien month's five zones as a bar with today marked, the zone today is in, what slipped, and next month's queue, with **Open the Lien desk ›**.

Read against the Lien desk's card at the top of the page, in the two kernels:

- **The bar was a picture the desk had given up.** The Board took its place on the Lien desk in v2.7332, the owner's ask of 2026-11-15. The Dashboard card was the last place it was drawn, and its door opened a page that used other words.
- **It said one sentence twice.** From day 16 to the month's end today's zone is the quiet zone, so the card's second line and its *Next month* line were the same call, `lienMonthZoneLine(quiet)`.
- **Its counts looked like the top card's and were not.** The top card counts every paper still to send or file by the next day. This card counted next month's deadlines only, left out held papers, and added reminders. *Copies to mail* here meant filed liens, where *to print and mail* at the top means monthly notices.
- **It cost three reads on every Dashboard visit.** `useLienMonth` read the payments behind the sent notices, the promised pay dates and the filed affidavits, beside the desk's light read.

## What changed

- `DashboardLienMonthCard.tsx`, its render test and `useLienMonth.ts` are deleted, and `Dashboard.tsx` no longer imports or draws them.
- The kernel `src/lib/jobs/lienMonthRhythm.ts` stays, with its tests. **§ Rules** on the Lien desk reads its days (`lienMonthRuleLines`), and the desk's rows read its zones through `lienMonthLine.ts`. `buildLienMonth` has no caller left outside its tests. It is kept so the rhythm can be drawn again from the same counts if the owner asks.
- The guide on the courtesy notice no longer says the Dashboard counts an unsent reminder as slipped. It says the Lien desk lists it under **Reminders**.

## What a leader gives up

The picture of the month's zones, the count of reminders, and in a working week the zone's own progress, like *12 to send · 4 sent*. The owner had meant to look at the lien month in a real reminders week, December 1 to 5. He chose to build now.

What takes its place: the Lien desk card beside Needs you (v2.8339) carries a red line while a paper is past its day or will not make it, in the Board's own words. Filed liens whose copies are not mailed are already a line of the Needs you list.
