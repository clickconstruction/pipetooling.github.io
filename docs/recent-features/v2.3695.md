# v2.3695 — Part cash on a Stripe bill: a credit line lowers the pay link, with undo (2026-09-22)

The owner, after v2.3692 shipped: *"most invoices are recorded in stripe, but I would like the ability to record invoices paid in cash like taunya described"* — and, on the gap the v2.3692 fragment named, *"build it with undo in the same PR."* Design and phone mock-ups: artifact `2EdZR45cEoY8pjLQsjPwaq` (owner said yes to all three decisions: the credit line the customer reads, undo in the same PR, checks and wires the same way).

## What was missing

`record-stripe-invoice-out-of-band-payment` only ever called `invoices.pay({ paid_out_of_band: true })`, which pays the whole invoice, so the window locked the amount at the open balance. $1,000 handed over on a $1,500 Stripe bill had no path: a job payment left the bill reading $1,500 open, and the customer's pay link still asked for all of it.

## The change

- **Migration [`20260922023508_stripe_part_payment_credit_note`](../migrations/20260922023508_stripe_part_payment_credit_note.md)** — `jobs_ledger_payments.stripe_credit_note_id text`. Push before deploying the functions.
- **[`record-stripe-invoice-out-of-band-payment`](../../supabase/functions/record-stripe-invoice-out-of-band-payment/index.ts)** — under Stripe's `amount_remaining` (and the app's `invoice.amount − applied`), creates a **credit note on the open invoice** for the amount with memo *Cash received Sep 21 · $1,000.00 — note* (`partPaymentCreditLine`, the `pt_*` metadata keys plus `pipetooling_part_payment`), then inserts the ledger row with the service role (invoice-linked, `stripe_credit_note_id`); an insert failure voids the note. Over the balance → 400. Full balance unchanged. Returns `partial: true`.
- **[`reverse-stripe-invoice-out-of-band-payment`](../../supabase/functions/reverse-stripe-invoice-out-of-band-payment/index.ts)** — new `payment_id`: bill must still be Billed; voids the row's credit note (idempotent), deletes the row with the service role, writes a `stripe_oob_payment_reverts` audit row.
- **Kernel [`stripePartPayment.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/stripePartPayment.ts)** — `stripePaymentPlan(amount, remaining)` → empty | over | full | part (cent tolerance), `stripeCreditLineText` (mirrors the function), `stripePartPaymentNote`, `stripePaymentButtonLabel` (*Record $1,000.00 · $500.00 stays due* / *Record $1,500.00* / *Confirm*), `stripePaymentBlocker`. Five tests.
- **[`BilledPaymentConfirmationModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/BilledPaymentConfirmationModal.tsx)** — on a Stripe bill the amount is editable; the plan drives the blue part-payment note, the amber over note, the button label and validation; a typed hand-off amount prefills up to the balance; the full-balance-only error and the v2.3692 *You typed…* note are gone; a part response skips the webhook wait. New render test (four cases).
- **[`JobFormPaymentsTable.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormPaymentsTable.tsx)** — a locked Stripe row carrying `stripe_credit_note_id` on a still-Billed bill shows **Undo part payment** (`requestUndoPartPayment`). `PaymentRow.stripe_credit_note_id?` is hydrated in `paymentRowsFromJob` (cast until `database.ts` regenerates) and never written by the form's own save path.
- **New [`UndoStripePartPaymentModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/UndoStripePartPaymentModal.tsx)** — reason required; calls the reverse function with `payment_id`; the job form re-reads the job and toasts *Part payment undone. The pay link asks for the full remainder again.*
- **Guides** — *bill a customer and get paid* (the part-payment example, undo) and *see when a customer will pay*.

## Deploy

1. `supabase db push` (the column).
2. `supabase functions deploy record-stripe-invoice-out-of-band-payment` and `… reverse-stripe-invoice-out-of-band-payment` (both keep `verify_jwt = false`). Until the record function is deployed, an amount under the balance answers *Amount must match the full open balance* from the old code — the window shows that error and nothing is written.
3. `chore(types)` regeneration PR afterwards.

## Left open

- The Edit tab's "→ Paid" step (job-level `mark_job_paid`) still records without telling Stripe.
