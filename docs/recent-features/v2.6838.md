# v2.6838 — The Bridge lists every bank account, and you choose which count as cash (punch list #303) (2026-11-10)

The owner's answer to call 2 of `to-dos/bridge-one-story/README.md`, 2026-11-10: the bank the page reads is the whole bank, "but also I would like to know how much is in every account as sometimes I want to account for Accounts Payable in the balance and sometimes I don't". Client and one shared edge file. No migration.

## What changed

- **The bank read carries the account's own name.** `_shared/mercuryAccounts.ts` kept Mercury's `name` ("Mercury Checking ••2222") and dropped `nickname`, the name the owner gave the account in Mercury ("Accounts Payable 2222"), so nobody could tell which account was which. Each account now carries `nickname`, null when it has none. `get-mercury-account-balances` and `bridge-weekly-sheet` share the file.
- **The kernel** (`src/lib/bridge/cashAccounts.ts`, not copied to the edge functions; `mercuryCash.ts` is, and is untouched):
  - `loadBankRead` makes the one call to the bank the page already made and reads one `app_settings` row, `bridge_cash_accounts_out_v1`: the ids of the accounts left out.
  - `buildCashAccounts` lists every account, the fullest first, by its Mercury name, with its balance, what is free to spend when a pending charge makes that differ by a dollar or more, and whether it counts. A row keeps its place when its switch is pressed. Cash on hand is the bank's total less the accounts left out, so the figure is the one the page has always shown until one is switched off. An id the bank no longer has is ignored.
  - Its words: one sentence for wherever cash on hand shows (*Leaves out Accounts Payable 2222, $12.4k.*, or the count when there are several), and two under the list. An empty account left out ahead of the money says so too.
  - `countedCash` is the bank with those accounts taken off, for the page's Monday sheet view.
- **The list** (`src/components/bridge/BridgeCashAccounts.tsx`), under the cash chart's cash row in place of the one line that named the accounts with money and counted the rest: a tick box on each account, *Left out* in amber on a row switched off.
- **The page** (`Bridge.tsx`): cash on hand, and so the forecast, the net position line, the cash tile and the cash rule, is the counted figure. The two lines that compare the bank with the ledger (the typed figure's drift, *Fix the reading*) still read the whole bank. A switch follows at once and saves behind it; a save that is refused says why and holds for the visit, through **Read again** too.
- **The first screen** (`weekCall.ts`, `BridgeWeek.tsx`): the cash tile carries the left-out sentence as an amber link to the list, in every state, and counts toward the first screen's word budget. In a week cash leads with an account left out the tile says *Cash on hand is $18.4k today*, since *The bank has* would no longer be true.
- **Cash leads.** `WEEK_CASH_MAY_LEAD` is `true`. When the forecast goes under the floor and the bank is read, the cash tile takes the row and the call is proposed on Capital: the late invoices, the supply bills due on or before the low point, then the slowest step. The owner's own pick still wins. A cash call still keeps no snapshot of the low point: `calls.ts` is copied to the edge functions, so that waits for the PR that needs it.

## Not in this PR

- **The weekly email counts the whole bank.** `bridge-weekly-sheet` does not read the choice; its switch is off and the train's last PR redraws it as the first screen, where it will.
- **The owner's role cannot save the choice yet.** `app_settings` takes a write on this key from a dev alone, as it does for the floor and the typed cash beside it. A refused save holds for the visit. One migration can open the three keys to `is_master_or_dev()`; that is a permissions call for the owner.

## Verify

Open `/bridge` as the owner or a dev. Under the cash chart, **The bank's accounts** lists each account by the name it has in Mercury. Clear one account's box: the row reads *Left out*, **Cash on hand** drops by its balance, and the cash tile at the top names the account. Tick it again and both go back. With the forecast under the floor, the first screen opens on **Cash comes first this week**.
