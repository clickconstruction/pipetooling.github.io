# v2.7019 — Payment forecast: the Email button says what it sends, and the close button is easier to press (2026-11-12)

The owner's helper sent a picture of the top right corner of the **Payment forecast** window on 2026-11-12: she did not like how the Email button looked. The window opens from **Payment forecast** on the Billed Awaiting Payment header of Jobs → Stages.

## What was wrong

Read in the installed Chrome at 1400 and 375 wide, in both themes:

- The envelope was the text character `✉`, drawn smaller and thinner than the word beside it. The app's buttons on this board wear line icons from `lucide-react` at 16 px (`StagesToolsMenuGlyph`).
- The button read *Email…*, which names neither what goes nor that a window opens first.
- The close press was a bare `×` character with 4 px of padding: 20 × 33 px, on a phone too. The Email button beside it was 86 × 30 with a border, so the pair did not match.
- The intro sentence started 5 px under the buttons and ran the window's full width, so its first line passed under both.

## What changed

All of it is the header block of `BilledPaymentForecastModal.tsx` and one set of classes, `.forecastHead*`, in `src/index.css` (the hover and focus looks need a class).

- **The Email press** wears the library's `Mail` icon at the label's weight and reads **Email this forecast…**. The dots stay because the press opens the *Email payment forecast* window, where the sender picks the teammate and the time. Its accessible name is now its own words, so the old `aria-label` is gone and the two render tests find it by the new name.
- **The close press** is the library's `X` in a 32 px square, the same height as the Email press. Its name is still *Close*.
- **Both** take the muted fill on hover and a 2 px ring on keyboard focus.
- **The header is a grid.** The title and the intro share the left column and the two presses sit in their own columns, so the intro can never run under them. At 1400 wide the intro is still two lines.
- **At 640 px and under** the close press is 44 × 44 beside the title, and the Email press is 44 px tall on its own row under the intro.
- The guide *read the payment forecast* names the button by its new words.

## How it was checked

- `BilledPaymentForecastModal.render.test.tsx` finds the press by its new name, presses it, and still finds none when no sender is passed.
- Live, on a local copy of the app signed in against the real data with every write refused: the window opened at 1400, 768, 660, 375 and 320 wide in light and dark. No sideways scroll at any width. **Email this forecast…** opened *Email payment forecast* and the close press closed the window, at 1400 and 375 in both themes. No email was sent.

## Left alone

- The intro's words. The sentence carries a dash, a pair of parentheses and a semicolon, which `plainWords.ts` would refuse in a guide. A design session is planning pay-by-date terms for this window the same day and will change what the sentence has to say.
- The sibling windows on the same header row draw the same weak close press, each with its own inline copy and no shared piece: the bare `×` in `BilledAgingChartModal.tsx`, `PaidProfitChartModal.tsx`, `BilledReportShareModal.tsx` and `PaymentForecastShareModal.tsx`, and a `✕` with less padding in `PaySpeedsBreakdownModal.tsx` and `PaymentChaseModal.tsx`. Two presses still wear the `✉` character: *✉ Email this weekly…* in `PaySpeedsBreakdownModal.tsx` and *✉ email* in `PaymentChaseModal.tsx`. One shared header close press would fix the six windows at once; that is its own change.
