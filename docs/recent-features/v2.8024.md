# v2.8024 — Document builder: type on the Sub NDA as a GC sees it, and publish a revision (2026-11-19)

Punch list #404, step 7 of ten: edit on the GC's view, and Publish. No migration and no function. `document_builders` came in v2.7988, and its trigger already holds a revision to one set of words.

Every word is still a placeholder that no attorney has read. No clause wording is typed here or kept in the repo: a clause is drawn as grey bars, and its wording stays in the Contract Book.

## What changed

- The Sub NDA opens on two sides. On the right, **what a GC sees**: the flow. On the left, the map, what changed, and what later steps add under them.
- **You type on the GC's view itself.** The disclaimer's title, lines and note, the words beside its box, the basis page's ask and how, the site page, each topic's name, what a level means on a topic, each breach line a level brings, what the site must have, a blank's value, and the review's title are each a field (`BuilderField`, plain text). A list takes **Add a line** and a small **×** on each line.
- **A breach line a stricter level keeps is one rule.** Changed or taken off on the level that brings it, it changes or goes on every stricter level that kept it. Lines a level keeps from the one under it stay grey under *Also applies* and are typed where they start.
- **The map**: a row a topic, a column a level, 28 cells. A press on a cell opens that page at that level on the right, past the disclaimer. A cell shows its words where there is room and is a small square on a phone. A changed cell is marked. Nobody types in the map.
- **Changed since revision 2** lists each page that differs from the published revision, with **Open it** and **Back to revision 2**. A field that differs wears the changed mark.
- The draft saves a moment after the typing rests, and the bar reads *Saving the draft…* then *Draft saved*. Leaving the builder saves what was typed last.
- **Publish revision 3** makes the draft what everyone outside sees. It is dead while a rule of the paper is broken or a new line has no words, and the page lists why. Until a publish, everyone outside sees revision 2.
- **Marks are on** switches the fields off, to read the flow as a GC does.
- The attorney's read is a press on the bar: a name, kept with the published revision. A new revision is unread again.

`frame.ts`, which sized the lone flow to the screen in step 1, is gone with its test: the editor lays the flow out itself.

## What it does not do yet

- The builder's link is step 8, and the send to a GC is step 9. Nobody outside sees a published revision until one of them.
- The levels' names and the lines they are aligned with are not fields. The owner settled them, and a change to them is a change to the card.
- What a person picks on the sliders is still not saved.
