# v2.7010 — Lien affidavit: the county blank at the top prints plain (2026-11-12)

The owner's helper, on a printed affidavit of 2026-11-12: remove *(where signed)* from the page, since the help sheets make it clear. The words sat beside the blank after **COUNTY OF** at the top of the short form since v2.6952, which put them there the day the first notarized batch came back with the property's county written in that blank. The help sheet did not exist yet. Since v2.6953 a sheet prints in front of the paper and lists that blank first among the things to write, with the county it wants.

## What changed

- **The note is gone from the paper**, in `src/lib/jobsDocuments/lienFilingDocuments.ts`: `AFFIDAVIT_VENUE_NOTE`, `jurisdictionNote` and the `jurisdiction` block's `note` are removed, in the HTML, the text twin and the PDF. The top blank now prints as the notary's own blank lower on the page does, a plain line.
- **A paper kept from before prints plain too.** The three renderers no longer read a `note`, so a `jurisdiction` block saved with one draws the county line alone. Nobody has to redraw a paper.
- **The guide copy's mark 1** (`lienHelpMarkPaper` in `src/lib/jobs/lienHelpSheet.ts`) hung from the note's span. It now hangs from the bold county blank itself; the notary's blank is not bold, so the two marks stay apart.
- The guide *file a lien and never miss its deadlines* says the blank prints empty and the help sheet names the county.

## Not changed

- No sworn statement, and nothing else on the paper. The line keeps its height, so the short form still prints on one sheet.
- A paper printed with a county in **Signed in** never drew the note, and prints the county on both lines as before.
- The **Signed in** box's own hint on the desk and on the Lien window still says the notary writes both county lines when it is left blank.

## Is this the best we can do?

One case is weaker than before. The help sheet is a tick box, on by default and kept per computer. Someone who unticks it and leaves **Signed in** blank now gets a paper with nothing on it that says which county the top blank wants, which is the state the first batch was signed in. The fix that removes the question is the one already there: a county in **Signed in**, or in Settings, prints on both lines and leaves no blank to get wrong.
