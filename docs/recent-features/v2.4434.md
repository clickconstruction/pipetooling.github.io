# v2.4434 — Procurement log: tap a count to see which parts it means (2026-10-02)

No migration, no edge function. Wendi, 2026-10-02, on BP375's *Before you can order* box (v2.4383): "tells me 5 parts have no stage but doesnt say what parts they are. can we make this a link to take you to what 5 parts they are". **Tick the 5** ticked them, but on *To order* they sat inside the *Waiting on the GC* folds, one per house, so a ticked line was still out of sight.

## What changed

- **The count is a link.** In each of the three lines (no lead time, no house, no stage) the *N parts* is a blue underlined button ([`SubmittalProcurementPanel`](../../src/components/bids/SubmittalProcurementPanel.tsx), `procurement-blocker-show-<kind>`). A tap narrows the log below to the lines that blocker names; the line adds *Shown below.*; a second tap brings every line back.
- **The short list is never folded.** The lines keep their sections and their order in whichever view is open (To order, By tag, By house), but *Waiting on the GC* lists them outright instead of one fold per house.
- **A strip over the table says what is showing** (`procurement-only`): *5 parts with no stage. The other lines are hidden.* with **Show every line**. The page scrolls it to the middle of the screen so the parts are under it.
- **It follows the work.** The narrowing is the blocker, not a snapshot: a line that gets its stage leaves the list, and when the blocker names nothing the whole log is back and nothing is picked (`rowsForBlocker` in [`procurementLog.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/procurementLog.ts), with `blockerKeys`, `BLOCKER_KINDS`, `BLOCKER_WORDS`).
- **Only the screen's lines narrow.** The header counts, *Nothing is approved and waiting to be ordered*, the update to the GC and the printed sheet still read every line.
- **Tick the N** is unchanged and works on the short list, as do the section's tick-all box and a tap on a line (its row's Edit window, on that part).
- Guide *build a submittal package* and [`GLOSSARY.md`](../GLOSSARY.md) say so.
