# v2.8011 — Tasks and boxes: Use where I am says Precise Location is on, and shows the way to the switch when it looks off (2026-11-19)

Punch list #402, call 8: the same line on a task's **Use where I am** and on the box map. It follows the four PRs of the card (v2.7984, v2.7994, v2.8001, v2.8004). Client only: no migration and no function.

## The wrinkle

Seven screens take one reading of the phone. With Precise Location off, each got the middle of an area a mile or more across and treated it as a spot.

- **Use where I am** flew the task's map there, and the box map's **Use my location** did the same.
- **How far am I?** and **Show where I am** said a distance from there and drew the person's dot there.
- **I found it**, **Pin it here** and **Standing at this task? Add its place** refused the reading, and then said *Try again in the open*. The open sky does not fix a switch.

## What changed

A rough reading, over 1,000 m, is an area and not a spot. It now moves no map, measures no distance, draws no dot and adds no place. The screen draws the Precise Location line with the steps to the switch for this phone, and its own sentence after it.

| Screen | On a rough reading | Its sentence |
|---|---|---|
| A task's **Use where I am** (`TaskPlaceSheet`) | The map is not moved. With nowhere to open, it opens wide to be dragged. | *The map was not moved. Drag it until the ring is on the spot, or turn the switch on and press again.* |
| The box map's **Use my location** (`WhereIsBox`) | The map is not moved. | The same sentence |
| **Standing at this task? Add its place** (`TaskPlaceAddHere`) | Nothing is added. | *A place cannot be added from a reading that wide.* |
| **How far am I?** on a task's place and on a task's map (`TaskPlaceBlock`, `TaskMapBlock`) | No distance, and no dot on the picture. | *How far cannot be told from a reading that wide.* |
| **Show where I am** on the map of my tasks (`TaskPlacesMapCard`) | No dot, and the list gets no distances. A dot from an earlier sure reading stays. | The same sentence |
| The box map's **Show where I am** (`WhereIsBox`) | No dot, no line and no distance. | The same sentence |
| **I found it** and **Pin it here** (`WhereIsBox`, `PinAskStrip`) | Nothing is pinned. | *A7 was not pinned. Move the pin by hand, or turn the switch on and try again.* |

On a desk each sentence stops before the switch, since a desk has none.

**The two presses that set a spot draw the line in every state**, as **Put the dot where I am** does. A task's **Use where I am** and the box map's **Use my location** now read *Precise Location is on. Sure within 15 ft.* on a sure reading, and one amber box on a weak one, ending *Drag the map until the ring is on the spot.* **Standing at this task?** draws it too, for a reading it keeps and for one too wide to keep, which ends *Try again in the open.* The other screens keep their own words for a reading that is a spot.

**The wait.** A press that asks once has no better reading to wait for, and calls a rough one rough at once. The box map's **Show where I am** follows the phone, so it waits the same 4 seconds as the walk, reading *Finding you…* until then.

## Three things the headless walks found

The first two showed on a task's **Use where I am**, whose map takes the room left over by what is under it. The third showed on the box map.

1. **The steps squeezed the map.** The whole line, with five steps, left the map 190 px tall on a phone, under a sentence that says to drag it. On that sheet the steps now wait behind one press, **Show the steps** (`foldSteps`, `PRECISE_STEPS_FOLD_WORDS`). The lead, the two numbers and the sentence stay in the open. Every other screen scrolls, and draws the steps in the open.
2. **The ring landed off the reading.** After a rough answer, the next press flew the map and collapsed the line in one draw. The ring ended 130 ft from the reading, and the sheet said *You moved the ring.*, so the place would have been saved off the spot with no margin. Two faults in the shared map, `OnSiteMap`, both there before this change and never met, because the answer under the press was one line tall either way:
   - `CenterTracker` listened with a new set of handlers each draw. React takes the old set off, runs `FlyTo`, then puts the new set on, so a fly that landed at once was heard by nobody. It now keeps one set for the life of the map.
   - `FlyTo` glided from a box it had measured before the screen changed, and the resize that followed could not correct a glide under way. It now measures first and goes at once, with no glide.

   The same sheet walked again: the ring was within 2 ft of the reading after every press, and the sheet never said the ring was moved.
3. **The box map said *looks off* beside a distance.** With **Show where I am** on, **I found it** on an area drew a second line under the live one, so the steps showed twice. A sure reading after it then drew the distance under *Precise Location looks off.* and *A7 was not pinned.* Now the press's answer stands in for the live line while it stands. The next reading that is a spot takes it back, with **I found it** there to press again. A rough reading leaves it, so the person still reads that nothing was pinned.

## In the code

- `PreciseReadingLine` in `PreciseLocationLine.tsx`: the line for one reading a press asked for. It reads which phone this is itself.
- `OnSiteMap`: `FlyTo` measures and goes at once, and `CenterTracker` keeps one set of handlers. The box map and the Tally's map fly the same way, so their **Use my location** jumps where it used to glide.
- `src/lib/tasks/taskPlaceReading.ts`: each screen's own sentence. `foundRoughNoteWords` in `whereIsBox.ts` is the box map's.
- `taskPlaceFixWords` in `taskPlace.ts` is read by no screen now, since the line says what it said. It is left where it is: that file is copied into the dev seat's function (`gen:edge-kernels`), and taking one dead function out of it would mean a deploy for nothing. It goes with the next real change to that file.
- The task screens' own 150 ft rule is unchanged: a reading wider than that is still not kept as a place or a pin. The line's own edges, 20 m and 100 m, only choose its words.
