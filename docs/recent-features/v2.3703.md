# v2.3703 — Contract sweep: the tabs sit on the list (2026-09-22)

PR 1 of the last four pieces in [`to-dos/contract-sweep-refresh`](../../to-dos/contract-sweep-refresh/README.md) — the owner, on a screenshot of the sweep's header: *"this area doesn't look great, can we improve it?"* Drawn 2026-09-21 (`amount-and-tabs.html`), redrawn 2026-09-22 after asking whether that was the best we could do (`final-pass.html`, which carries the critique). Client only, no migration.

## What was wrong

Three things in one strip. The summary and the filter control said the same numbers side by side (*109 without a contract … 82 need a look* next to *Needs a look · 82*), while the one count that matters — ready to send — was only in the control. The control was a faint outline with no dividers, so the unselected options read as plain text. And it filtered the list on the left while floating over the agreement pane on the right.

## The change — [`JobsContractSweepModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsContractSweepModal.tsx)

- **Tabs on the list.** A `role="tablist"` strip at the top of the job list (sticky while the list scrolls), one `tab` per filter with its count in a pill: **Ready to send · Needs a look · All**, and **📄 In Drive** first when the Drive pass found something — the same rule as before, the same place. Each tab carries its reason as a tooltip (`CONTRACT_SWEEP_FILTER_TITLES`) rather than a hint line under the strip, which would have repeated the chips every row already wears and pushed the list down. *To send* is renamed **Ready to send** to match the green `Ready` chip.
- **The header says only what the tabs cannot.** New kernel `contractSweepHeaderClauses` in [`contractSweepRowState.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/contractSweepRowState.ts): the lead is the dollars of work (*$1,307,528 of work* has no contract on file), or the count of jobs when nothing has an amount — never *$0 of work*; then *N sent this sweep*, *N filed*, *under $2,500 left out*, each only when true. *📄 checking Drive…* stays while the pass runs; the *N look like they are already in Drive* clause is gone — the In Drive tab appearing is that signal — and `driveFindsSummary` went with it.

## Coverage

`contractSweepRowState.test.ts` — the tab label and the header clauses (money lead, empty rest, the jobs-count lead, one clause). The sweep's render tests select the tabs by role and read the header for the money and for the absence of the counts.
