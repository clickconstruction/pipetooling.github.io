# v2.3385 — Contract sweep PR 0c: the contract question on a new job (2026-09-14)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Second PR of the train in [`to-dos/contract-sweep-seen-first/`](../../to-dos/contract-sweep-seen-first/README.md) (PR 0 v2.3384 the floor + Not needed).

## Why

Jobs born from a won bid or an accepted estimate are already covered by the coverage kernel. The backlog grows from hand-made jobs — the New Job form had no contract question, so every one of them landed in the count and waited for a sweep. Asking once, at the moment the job exists, ends the backlog as a recurring event.

## What shipped

- **[`JobContractAfterCreatePrompt`](../../src/components/jobs/JobContractAfterCreatePrompt.tsx)** (+3 render cases): mounted once by [`JobFormModalProvider`](../../src/contexts/JobFormModalContext.tsx), which hands it the id every New Job save produces (after the caller's own `onCreatedJobId`). It fetches the job with its GC embed and the floor, then asks *Does J523 need a contract?* with the job line (name · customer · builder · amount) and three doors plus Later:
  - **Send our agreement** → the Contract modal (prefilled from the job).
  - **File a signed copy** → the Contract modal opened straight onto the *File a signed contract* sheet (new prop `initialFilingOpen`). On a GC job (`gc_customer_id` set and the builder is not the customer row) this door is first and reads **File Summit GC's subcontract** — the builder's paper is the agreement.
  - **Not needed…** → the same three reason chips as PR 0 + free text → **Mark not needed**.
  - **Later — it stays in the count.**
  - No question when the job is already covered (signed, estimate, bid room), already answered, or under the floor; non-office roles never see it.
- **[`jobContractNotNeeded.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobContractNotNeeded.ts)**: `markJobContractNotNeeded` / `clearJobContractNotNeeded` / `dispatchJobContractChanged` — the one write path the Contract modal and the prompt share (the modal's PR 0 inline writes moved here).
- Help guide *get a job contract signed*: "A new job asks once".

## Verified

Render smoke (GC job leads with filing; direct job leads with sending; Not needed writes and closes; covered and under-floor jobs close silently), typecheck, lint, theme check.
