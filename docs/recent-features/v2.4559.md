# v2.4559 — Documents: every paper printed for someone is kept (2026-10-05)

Step 3 of the Sent copies train ([`docs/SENT_COPIES.md`](../SENT_COPIES.md)), whole: the owner's *"printing counts as sending"*. Every print that goes to someone outside the company now files the page as it printed, and `PRINTS_OWED` is empty. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

Each print goes through `printAndFile` ([`sentCopiesIo.ts`](../../src/lib/sent/sentCopiesIo.ts)), which prints as before and files the page. New helper `printWhenReadyAndFile(build, filing)`: for a page that needs a moment to build (a signed waiver reads its stored ink first), it files the page that printed.

## Not a send, and why

Six files moved from owed to `NOT_A_SEND` in [`sentCopiesCoverage.test.ts`](../../src/lib/sent/sentCopiesCoverage.test.ts): the bid follow-up sheets (for our own account managers), the takeoff sheets (our own; the schedule that goes to the GC is filed from the cover letter tab), the cost estimate and pricing pages (they hold cost and margin), the contract reader in Settings (reading, not sending), and **partnership notices** — kept on the partnership itself (`notice_html`), never in a list the whole office reads. Two prints inside wired files stay plain, each with a comment: the Billed Awaiting Payment report and the job cost breakdown, both our own reports.

From here a new print fails CI until it files or says why not.
