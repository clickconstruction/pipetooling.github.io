# v2.2035 — Chase queue: full scopes, unfiltered rows (2026-08-21)

Live verification of v2.2025 against prod caught the card and call mode disagreeing (11 customers · $100,156 vs 9 · $43,882). Two causes, both in [`JobsStagesTab.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsStagesTab.tsx):

- **Scopes**: billed invoices hang on working/waiting jobs too (a part-billed Working job is exactly the bill that falls through cracks — prod had jobs 650/$26,800 and 878/$16,054 in that state), and the board kernel routes them into the billed section only when their job's scope is loaded. Call mode now fetches ALL `NON_PAID_SCOPES` on open and gates its loading state on every one being merged.
- **Filters**: the full-rows queue was built from `stagesBoardLists.billedActiveRows`, which respects hide-groups and live search. It now derives from `buildJobsStagesBoardLists(jobs, '')` — the same money-never-hides perspective as the lean card and the story cards (v2.1915).

Verified live end-to-end against prod after `db push`: card 11 · $100,156 = modal 11 · $100,156; a note-touch on the smallest customer moved them to the Waiting rail (quiet period) and dropped the queue to 10 · $99,749; the test touch row was then deleted (dev-RLS) restoring the original state. Client-only — no migration.

Known cousin (not fixed here): the Payment forecast has the same scope gap — its Past expected bucket undercounts bills on non-billed-status jobs when their scopes aren't loaded.
