# v2.7817 — Lien desk: a lien day that passed stays on the Board until someone answers it (2026-11-18)

The follow-up to the closed end on the Lien desk's Board (v2.7793, v2.7796, v2.7801). The owner's ask of 2026-11-18 was that a paper which can no longer be liened is shown, and the person is offered the chance to correct the date or to let it go. The closed bin and its window do that. What they could not do is keep the job: a lien day nobody answered left the desk by itself seven days later. This PR keeps it. It carries a migration.

## Before

- `list_lien_affidavit_windows` ends with `d.deadline >= public.app_today() - 7`. Seven days after a job's last day to file, the reader stops listing it.
- The desk builds every affidavit entry from that reader's rows. So on the eighth day the job was gone from the Board's closed bin *Lien day passed · not filed*, from the Missed pile of the affidavit list and from the count beside them, with nothing filed and no answer on record.
- The notices' reader has kept an unrecorded closed month since v2.3680. The affidavits' reader had no such rule.

## What changed

- **Migration [`20261118230000_lien_closed_days.sql`](../migrations/20261118230000_lien_closed_days.md).** The reader's body moves, unchanged, into `lien_affidavit_days()` with its two date filters taken off, so the month rules and the role gate live once. `list_lien_affidavit_windows` is those two filters over it: the same arguments, columns, rows and order. `list_lien_closed_days()` is new: the jobs whose day is more than seven days past, with no filed affidavit, still on the lien path. The two never list the same job. Only the two readers may call the shared one.
- **[`src/lib/jobs/lienClosedDays.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienClosedDays.ts)**, pure, with its unit tests. `withLienClosedDays` hands the affidavit queue one list: the windows rows, then the closed days, one row per job. A job both reads name keeps its windows row.
- **[`src/hooks/useLienDeskData.ts`](../../src/hooks/useLienDeskData.ts)** reads `list_lien_closed_days` beside the windows reader and joins the two before `lienDeskKeepRows` and `buildLienAffidavitQueue`. The read answers no rows when the function is not there yet, or on any failed read, so the desk never goes dark for it. It is read in light mode too, so the Dashboard's Board strip counts the papers the Board counts.
- **Nothing else on screen was written.** `pileFor` already puts a paper not filed past its day in `missed`, the Board draws that pile in the closed bin, and `LienClosedWindow` answers it. The job, its property record and its GC are read by the hook's existing joins, since the closed rows carry the windows reader's columns.

## How a job leaves now

A filing is recorded. The last day of work is corrected, so the day moves ahead and the windows reader lists the job again. The job is taken off the lien path (*Keep collecting*). It is marked Uncollectible (*Let it go*). It is paid. Those are the window's three answers and the two ways a job always left. A drafted, approved or held affidavit does not answer it, and neither does a notice.

A job the desk already leaves out stays out: a hidden ZZ test job, a job an open in-kind deal pays, a job on a property answered public, a job off the lien path.

## What does not change

- The other readers of `list_lien_affidavit_windows` keep their seven days and were not edited: the lien book, Materials, the law firm's portal, the dev seat's verbs.
- Where a missed affidavit already showed for its first seven days, it now stays: the Missed pile of the affidavit list, the muted *Lien window closed · lien gone* chip on AR and Stages. Next up still asks nothing for it.
- No paper is filed, voided or deleted. The migration writes nothing.

## On the day of the push

Every job whose lien day passed more than a week ago with no answer appears in the closed bin at once. They were counted read-only on prod before the code was written, and the owner was told the number before the PR was armed. The number is not in the repo.

## Order

The client calls a function only this migration creates. `npm run check:calls-ahead` labels the PR *push right after the merge*, and the Pages deploy holds until the push. Merge and `supabase db push` in one sitting. The PR was opened without auto-merge for that reason.
