# v2.5234 — The Bridge: Build reads by percent complete (link PR 3) (2026-10-11)

PR 3 of the weakest-link build (`to-dos/bridge-weakest-link/README.md`; the proposal in `notes/the-goal-limiting-step.md`'s "Measure Build by the change in percent complete" is what this ships). Dev only. No migration.

- **The kernel** (`src/lib/bridge/buildPace.ts`, `buildBuildPace`): per job, the percent points — hand-set percents from `job_pct_events` and the field reports' "How complete is the job?" answers (`parseReportPct`: the leading number, clamped to 0–100) — walked in date order from the last point before the window (the baseline; a job created in the window starts at 0, a job created before it with no earlier point starts at its first in-window point). Each step's change in percent × the job's contract lands in the pay week of the point (`builtByWeek`); a hand-set and a report on the same day at the same percent count once. Sold per week = the contract on jobs created in the window ÷ weeks. The pile: the contract left on open jobs (waiting to start or working; billing-only jobs and the office job left out) by their percent; a job with no percent is read as half done and counted as the grey share; jobs with no contract are counted, not priced.
- **The reads** (`loadBuildPace.ts`): the open jobs, the pct events and reports since 90 days before the window (paged), the jobs created in the window, and the jobs the points touch that are no longer open (chunked), for their contract.
- **The card**: Build on the Bridge now reads "$362k left to build · on 28 jobs that have a % · grey: 36 jobs have no %", "sales sells $49k a week" over "the field builds $35k a week", and "10 to 14 weeks to build what is already sold" (the range reads the no-percent jobs as half done). It competes for the link by PR 1's rule; today it reads a close second to Bill, which is honest: the two are one problem, the field building faster than the office bills.
- **Tests**: `buildPace.test.ts` (4: the report parse, built per week from deltas with the baseline and the dedupe, the first-point baseline, the pile).
- **Docs**: guide *read the Bridge* (the Build card's reading); the Glossary's Bridge entry; the to-do's status line.
