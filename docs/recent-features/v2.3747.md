# v2.3747 — Lien months: a job with no clock hours is dated from the day it was created (2026-09-23)

Punch list **#32** (URGENT), the owner's ruling of 2026-09-22 on Taunya's Dudley find. One migration (four lien readers rewritten, one helper), no edge function, no new table.

## Why

Every lien reader built a job's months from **approved clock sessions** and dropped the job when there were none. Searching the Pipeline for RMC- Dudley Mason showed $84.6k open across 20 jobs; *Put RMC- Dudley Mason on notice* claimed $54,850 across 6, because the other jobs — billed service visits and trip charges nobody clocked in on — had no month and were silently left out of a notice about to be mailed. The owner: *"this is a bug, those amounts should still be in the lien list with the day of their job creation as a fall back."*

## The rule

- A job with approved sessions keeps its months from the sessions, exactly as before.
- A job with none gets **one month: the month of `jobs_ledger.created_at`** in the company calendar (America/Chicago — the to-do said Denver; `app_today()` is Chicago and the day key follows it), with `approved_hours = 0` and a new column **`month_source`** = `'hours'` | `'job_created'` on every reader.
- The fallback month goes through the same `lien_notice_deadline` / `lien_filing_deadline` as a real one. An old job's creation month is often already closed: the row appears, the balance is listed, the month is named as information — nothing is hidden — and the form still claims the timely months only (counsel, v2.3745).
- Never both: a job with sessions has no fallback row. A job with no `created_at` gets no month (none on prod).

## The change

- **Migration [`20260923170000_lien_months_creation_fallback.sql`](../migrations/20260923170000_lien_months_creation_fallback.md)** — `lien_fallback_month(timestamptz)` (the rule's one home), then `list_gc_unpaid_months`, `list_lien_notice_months`, `list_lien_affidavit_windows` and `list_jobs_owner_to_confirm` each DROP + CREATE with the months CTE `UNION ALL` a `NOT EXISTS`-guarded creation month and the new `month_source` column (a result column cannot be added with CREATE OR REPLACE). The desk's window rules (v2.3412 / v2.3680 / v2.3683) apply to the fallback month unchanged, so the desk queue shows only the fallback months inside its window; the GC run shows them all.
- **Kernels** — [`lienDesk.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDesk.ts): `LienMonthSource`, `monthFromCreation(row)`, `DATED_FROM_CREATION_WORDS` (one wording), `LienDeskMonth.fromCreation`, `LienDeskEntry.datedFromCreation`; [`gcOnNotice.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/gcOnNotice.ts): `GcNoticeMonth.fromCreation`, `GcNoticeJob.datedFromCreation` (`timelyClaim` with zero hours spreads by count, so a single open fallback month carries the whole claim); [`lienDeskAffidavits.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDeskAffidavits.ts): `lastMonthFromCreation`; [`ownerConfirm.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/ownerConfirm.ts): `firstMonthFromCreation`, and the property line reads *due Oct 15 · dated from the job’s creation* / *February's window closed · dated from the job’s creation* instead of promising later months that do not exist. A row from an older RPC (no `month_source`) reads as hours, so the client is safe to deploy before the push.
- **Rows** — the GC modal's months cell carries the line under the chips (`data-testid="gc-notice-dated-from-creation"`), its step-2 description and empty state no longer say "approved hours"; the Lien desk list row carries it after the months; the Months card ([`LienDeskMonths.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskMonths.tsx)) prints it in place of *0 approved hours*; the pane's fourth gate ([`lienDeskGates.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDeskGates.ts)) reads *Dated from creation* instead of *Approved hours*; the affidavit pane's gate heading says *window from August 2026, the month the job was created — it has no clock hours*.
- **Paper trail** — [`lienNoticeDraft.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienNoticeDraft.ts): `LienDeskDraftFields.monthsDatedFromCreation?: true`, written by the GC run's `approveAll`, the desk's draft save and the affidavit pane's draft, kept by `parseLienDeskDraftFields`, and copied into `job_lien_filings.fields` by the run — the notice itself names the month as it names any other (the owner's call that creation stands in for the furnishing month is on the record, not on the form).
- Guide *send lien notices from the Lien desk* (Drafting a notice; A GC in trouble, step 2); `docs/GLOSSARY.md` → Lien desk. `to-dos/lien-months-fallback.md` deleted.
