# v2.8068 — Painted maps: Put the dot where I am says why under the press when your phone gives no reading (2026-11-20)

Punch list #402, found by the audit of the card. It follows v2.8001, which drew the Precise Location line under the press for a reading that came. Client only: no migration and no function.

## The wrinkle

**Put the dot where I am** asks the phone once. When the phone said no, or did not answer inside 8 seconds, two things went wrong.

- **The reason was drawn where nobody was looking.** It went to the window's error line at the top of the panel, above the kind chips, the name and the photo. The press is at the foot of a panel that scrolls. A painter gets a rough reading, reads the alert with the steps under the press, turns the switch on, comes back and presses again. The phone runs out of time. The alert went away, nothing scrolled, and what stood under the press was the line of whatever reading the dot still sat on. In the walk that was *The signal is weak here. Your phone is only sure within 990 ft. Drag the dot onto the spot.*, from two presses before. The press looked as if it had done nothing.
- **The words were another screen's.** They came from the box map: *Could not find you. Drag the map by hand.* and *Location is off for this app. Drag the map by hand, or allow location in the phone's settings.* On this panel dragging the map places nothing. The pill over the map says *Drag the blue dot onto the spot.*

## What changed

- **The panel owns the words** (`placeDotNoFixWords` in `src/lib/paintedMap/paintedPlaceDot.ts`, beside the weak and rough notes). One sentence set for each reason, each saying the reason, that the dot was not moved, and the next press:
  - not in time: *Your phone did not find you in time. The dot was not moved. Drag it onto the spot, or press Put the dot where I am again.*
  - said no: *Location is off for this app. The dot was not moved. Drag it onto the spot, or allow location in the phone's settings and press again.*
  - no location to give: *This device gives no location. The dot was not moved. Drag it onto the spot by hand.*
- **The window asks with `requestMyFix`**, which hands back the reason and leaves the words to the caller, and keeps the reason in its own state (`noFix`) in place of the window's error line. **Start the map where I am**, in the draw panel, still uses the box map's words: there the map is what is dragged.
- **The reason is drawn under the press**, where the line is drawn, as a `role="alert"` box in the amber of the Precise Location line (`data-painted-place-fix="no-reading"`). It stands in for the line: no older reading's line, weak or rough, and no *You moved the dot* is drawn with it.
- **It comes into view** when the press answers, as the rough alert does. A second press that fails the same way brings it into view again, since the press was made twice.
- **It goes** when the next press gets a reading, of any kind, and when the place panel closes.
- A sure, weak or rough reading behaves as before. A dot on an earlier good reading stays on it through a press that fails, and its margin is still saved with the place.

## Checked

- Tests, each failing before the change and passing after: `paintedPlaceDot.test.ts` (the three sentence sets, plain words, none sends anybody to the map), `PaintedPlacePanel.render.test.tsx` (the box under the press for each reason, standing in for a weak, a rough and a moved line, and scrolled to once per answer) and `PaintedMapWindow.render.test.tsx` (the phone says no, runs out of time and has no location; a weak press, then a rough one, then one that runs out of time, then a sure one; the panel closed and opened again).
- The window's test now runs the real `myLocation` module over a stand-in for `navigator.geolocation`, put there for the length of one ask. Before, the module itself was stood in for and could only answer with a reading.
- `PaintedMapWindow.walk.render.test.tsx`: unchanged and green.
- `eslint`, the colour check and the font check: clean.
