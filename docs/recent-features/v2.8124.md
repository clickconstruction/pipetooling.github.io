# v2.8124 — Job Mode, Short view: the row of named squares (2026-11-20)

The day's row (v2.8047) was built for the long Job Mode page, and the Short view of Job Mode (punch list #384) drew no row, on purpose. Whether it should was left as the one call on punch list #409. The owner was shown the Short view drawn three ways side by side, for three kinds of day: no row, the job's two squares alone, and the whole row. On 2026-11-20 he picked the whole row. A client change. No migration and no function.

## What a person sees

A superintendent whose Dashboard view is Short, with Job Mode on, now opens onto the same row of named squares as the long page, above the job card.

- **A stop still owed, or on the way to it:** **Directions**, **Job details**. Before this the Short view had no door to a job's window and no Directions until a job was on the clock.
- **On the job:** **Photo**, **Job details**, and **On site** with its count on a site that holds boxes.
- **No job left today:** **This week** with the hours so far, **Tomorrow** with the count of jobs.
- **Always, at the right end:** **Paint a map**, **My spend**.

Every square opens what it opens on the long page, with one difference. The Short view has no hours card to scroll to, so there **This week** opens the full Dashboard, as **Everything else** does, and lands on **My Time**. **Back to the short page** and the bar's **Dashboard** tab return, as they did.

## What it costs, as the owner was shown

The row is 78 px tall with its gap. On a morning with five stops that moves **Tomorrow** and **Everything else** off the first screen, and the page's presses go from 13 to 17. On a day with no job left the page still fits one screen, and **Tomorrow** is said twice, once by the square and once by the page's own line.

## How it is built

- `JobModeShortPage` takes a `row` and draws it first. The Dashboard hands it `JobModeTopRow` itself, not `DashboardPinnedQuickRow`, so the Short view gets the squares and no pins line.
- `JobModeTopRow` takes two new props, both unset on the long page, which is unchanged. `loadHours` is the week's hours read by the row itself: only while the **This week** square is drawn, so a working day costs no read, and again on every punch. A read that fails keeps the number on the square. `onHours` is where the square goes on a page with no hours card.
- `loadTopRowWeekHours` in `src/lib/jobMode/topRowIo.ts` is that read. It is the hours card's own read and rule: the week's hours for a person on the board, zero for an answer with nothing on the clock, and nothing for nobody to read, which the square draws as **My hours**.
- `Dashboard.tsx`: the Short view's return hands in the row, and one stable loader sits beside the salary probe it reads.

## Walked

On the dev server against the live data, as the one superintendent through the app's own View as, read only: every write to a table was refused in the browser. His view was read as Short by answering that one read, with nothing saved. 390 and 320 px wide, light and dark.

- A day with stops drew **Directions**, **Job details**, **Paint a map** and **My spend**, four squares of one size, with no sideways scroll.
- A day with no job left, drawn by answering the day's reads, drew **This week** with his hours and **Tomorrow** with his count. The number was the one the long page's square and hours card show.
- A press on **This week** left the Short view, showed **Back to the short page**, and put **My Time** on the screen with the week's hours on its line.
