# v2.4208 — Counts import: schedule rows are not counts (2026-09-30)

Alternates round two, PR 8b — the PipeTooling half of CountTooling's ALT-GROUPS rung 2 (the alternate's own water sizing block). No migration, no edge function.

## What changed

- **[`parseCountsImportText`](../../src/lib/bids/parseCountsImportText.ts)** tracks a schedule block: `--- Duct ---` and `--- Water sizing ---` (`COUNT_SCHEDULE_HEADING_RE`) open one, a blank line or the next framed heading ends it, and its rows are neither counts nor "skipped". Before, a duct row (`24×12<TAB>24 ga…`) counted as skipped and a water row (`Cold main<TAB>1″…`) **imported as a fixture × 1**.
- **The alternate's suffixed heading** — `--- Alternate: <name> · Water sizing ---` (or `· Duct`, `COUNT_ALTERNATE_SCHEDULE_SUFFIX_RE`) — names the same alternate (reported once in `alternateGroups`) and opens a schedule block, so it never becomes a second alternate called "<name> · Water sizing".
- Tests in `parseCountsImportText.test.ts`.

CountTooling's side (its PR, same day): `report.js` narrows a page's annotations to one group (`annotationsInGroup`) and emits the alternate's water rows after the whole-plan block; duct has no per-alternate block because a duct run belongs to no group.
