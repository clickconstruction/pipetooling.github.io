# v2.7215 — Lien affidavit: the notice record says whose total a notice total is (2026-11-13)

Punch list #322. The owner read a printed batch and found a claim amount on the notice records page that matched no affidavit in the stack. The figure was right. It was the total of one notice mailed by hand for several jobs at one property, and the line read *Claim (total) on the paper, (share) for this job*. "On the paper" read as this paper, the affidavit behind the page.

## What changed

- **The notice's line** (`lienRecordClaimWords`, called by `lienRecordNoticeWords` in `src/lib/jobs/lienNoticeRecordSheet.ts`): a notice whose paper total is more than the job's amount reads *The notice claimed (total) in all, (share) of it for this job*. A paper total under the job's amount is a figure set by hand, so the two are said apart: *The notice claimed (total), and this job's amount on record is (amount)*. A notice with no paper total reads *Claim (amount)*, as before.
- **One line that says whose figure it is** (`LIEN_RECORD_TOTAL_IN_ALL`): on a single record it follows the envelope line under the notices, and on a batch it closes the lead under the band. It prints only when a notice on the page carries a paper total (`lienRecordHasPaperTotal`), once.
- The line does not say *for several jobs*. The row cannot prove it: `printed_claim` is set whenever the paper's total is not the job's amount, and a notice entered on one job alone has no sibling row to count. So the words say what the total is and what an affidavit claims.
- No sworn word, no table, no edge function. The help sheet, the copies of the notices and the affidavits print as before.

## Checked

- `lienNoticeRecordSheet.test.ts`: the three shapes of the claim words, the line printing once on a single record and once on a batch, and absent where no notice carries a total. The new sentence passes the plain-words rules.
- Read against the live record of the batch that raised it, through the dev seat, read-only: the notice's total equals the open balances of the jobs at the property that the saved copy lists, and five of them are the papers in the stack. No figure is kept here.
