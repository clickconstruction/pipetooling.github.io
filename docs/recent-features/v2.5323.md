Messages, cut 4: the Settings → Flows tab over the flows editor (`src/lib/flows/editor.ts`, v2.5271 and v2.5316), as the prototype v1.178 draws it (`flowsEditorHtml`). It is the office team's (`canEditFlows`, `GC_FLOWS_EDIT_TEAM`), after Contracts and clauses in the Company zone. Its guide is `set-up-the-steps-a-person-goes-through`.

- **The flows by kind:** Customer, Trade partner and Architect, each with The flow, History and How it is going (`src/components/settings/SettingsFlowsTab.tsx`).
- **The flow** (`flows/FlowStepsTable.tsx`): the three lanes and their steps. Each step has its name and Spanish name, and what finishes it, its facts joined with and, or with or on a fork, the join word making the fork. It also has whose move it is with our seat, the nudge, whether it repeats, its papers, its usual, and its lane, with the arrows and Remove. Remove asks first and names who is on the step. "+ a step in <lane>" adds one. Every press is an edit to the draft, or a refusal said in words.
- **The pickers** (`flows/FlowPickers.tsx`): *Finished when* lists the app's facts by group, and a library paper's facts by its name. *Ask for a fact that is missing* writes `flow_fact_wishes`. The paper picker's last row is "+ Add a new paper", which opens helper 6's New paper window (#5476). Its `onSaved` puts the one paper and its one fact on the step, never the join.
- **Before you save · who moves** tries the draft on everyone of the kind (`flow_rows_kind`, read as the boards read it), with the changes in words, Save and Put it back. Save sends the whole flow at once (`flow_save`, 20261013020000), held to the newest History line read, and clears the boards' copy (`clearFlowDefCache`). A refused Save says the server's words, "Someone saved this flow while you edited it…".
- **History** lists every change with who and when, with the flow as first drawn at the bottom. Removed has Put it back. **How it is going** shows this quarter's funnel and one row per step (`going.ts` over `goingInputs.ts`).
