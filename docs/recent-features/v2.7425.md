# v2.7425 — Lien desk: the cards that open on To do stay on the screen (2026-11-15)

Client only. Found on the walk Taunya asked for on 2026-11-15, after the cut-off menu of v2.7421: every pop-up of the Lien desk opened at five widths, read only.

## What was wrong

- **The Board's hover card** (v2.7381) was placed by a guess of its height, 230 px. It stands 244 to 338 px, and 395 with the stages it names since v2.7419. At 1280 x 720 the card of every chip in To send ran 5 to 81 px off the bottom of the screen.
- **A To do row's step card** always opened under its row. On a row at the foot of the screen it opened below the fold: 2 px of a 387 px card showed.

## What landed

- `src/lib/jobs/lienBoardChipCard.ts`: `lienBoardCardPlace` takes the card's real height and the room over the chip. Under the chip when it fits; over it when it fits there; beside it, to the right or else the left, when neither side holds it, kept inside what shows it from top to bottom. `top` is the card's own top now, with no shift of its own height.
- `LienBoard.tsx`: `ChipCard` draws hidden where the guess puts it, measures itself before the paint and is shown where it fits. The room is the screen cut by every box around the Board that cuts its contents (`roomAround`), so the card is not sent up under the frame that scrolls To do.
- `src/lib/jobs/lienStepCardPlace.ts` (new): `lienStepCardOver({ height, under, over })`. Under whenever it fits; over when it does not and there is more room over.
- `LienDeskSteps.tsx`: `useLienStepCard` hands the card its mark's top and the room on each side, inside the box that scrolls the list. `LienStepCardView` measures itself and stands over the mark when the rule says so (`data-side`). A phone's sheet is as it was.

## Verified

`lienBoardChipCard.test.ts` (the measured cases: over at 338 px, beside at 395 px, the left side at the Board's right edge), `lienStepCardPlace.test.ts`, `LienDeskSteps.card.render.test.tsx` (new), `LienBoard.render.test.tsx`, `LienDeskNextUp.render.test.tsx`; eslint on the touched files; typecheck.

On the dev server, read only: all 43 chips' cards at 1440 x 900, 1280 x 720 and 1024 x 768, and 21 at 768 x 1024, stood whole inside the frame, and none covered its own chip. At 1280 x 720: 9 under, 26 over, 8 beside. A step card on a row at y 692 of 720 opened over the row, 299 to 687; one on a row at y 183 opened under it.
