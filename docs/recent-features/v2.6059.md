# v2.6059 — Honest and fast, PR 3: the phone's day kept (2026-10-29)

Punch list #183 ([`to-dos/honest-and-fast/README.md`](../../to-dos/honest-and-fast/README.md)), the third of five PRs, #163's step 4 taken here. During a database freeze the Dashboard's My Schedule card showed two grey bars and then, when the schedule read failed, a warning toast and *No blocks scheduled.*, so a crew member read an empty day; the Job Mode card read *Loading Job Mode…* until its read failed. This PR takes both through PR 2's `useKeptRead` (v2.6031), keyed by the user and the day, so a slow morning draws the day as the phone last read it under the stamp *Your day as read at 7:52 AM · reading again…*, with every press that writes on the day held, and a failed schedule read never lands as an empty day. No migration, no edge function.

## What changed

- **My Schedule's kept day** [`src/lib/dashboard/myScheduleKeptDay.ts`](../../src/lib/dashboard/myScheduleKeptDay.ts) (8 tests): the rows kept are the view model the card draws, one per block with the label, the customer phone and the job meta (pictures link, numbers, address) the card's three reads resolved for it; `myScheduleKeptKey(userId, todayYmd)` reads `u1:my-schedule:2026-10-29`, so yesterday's day is never shown as today's (the owner's call 4); `buildMyScheduleKeptRows` / `unpackMyScheduleKeptRows` / `myScheduleKeptRowIsValid`.
- **The seam** [`useDashboardSubSchedule.ts`](../../src/hooks/useDashboardSubSchedule.ts) (5 render tests): calls `useKeptRead` with `loading` held true until the blocks, the labels and the meta all landed for the rows on screen, derived in render from the rows key each read was made for (`labelsForRows`, `metaForRows`), never from a flag an effect sets, so there is no render in which a half day could be written. While the kept day shows it hands out the kept blocks and maps with `subScheduleLoading` off and `subScheduleKeptAt` set; a failed read sets `subScheduleError` (`recoveryFailureFromError`, the class kept because the new `readScheduleBlocksForAssigneeDateRange` in `jobScheduleBlocks.ts` throws as the client throws) instead of a warning toast and empty rows, and `subScheduleKeptFailed` when a kept day is up. `leaveReportReminderForJobRow` reads the day on screen. The hook no longer uses the toast context.
- **The card** [`DashboardMyScheduleSection.tsx`](../../src/components/dashboard/DashboardMyScheduleSection.tsx) (5 kept-day smokes): three optional props; the root carries `data-kept-read` and the `KeptReadStamp` with the lead `PHONE_DAY_KEPT_LEAD` (*Your day as read at*, added to `lib/keptRead.ts`) sits under the header; while kept, Update % done, Leave Report / Report due, the rearrange pencil and the ask-Dispatch pictures press are disabled with `keptPress` and the hover words *reading again…*, and + Add job, Calendar, the call button, an existing pictures link, the row's detail and Write up a change stay live; a failed read with nothing kept draws `OfflineRetryPanel` (`idempotent`, so a lost signal reads again by itself and a refusal shows its reason with no Retry) in place of the two days. `Dashboard.tsx` and Dispatch Mode's home pass the props, and `hideEmptyMySchedule` (`phoneFolds.ts`) takes `failed`, so the office phone's fold never hides a failed read as an empty day.
- **Job Mode's kept day** [`src/lib/jobMode/keptDay.ts`](../../src/lib/jobMode/keptDay.ts) (7 tests): the rail's facts, each of today's blocks with its job's display fields, whether a closed session visited the job and whether the open session was on it; the key `u1:job-mode:2026-10-29`. The open session's id and travel stamps are never kept: the clock reads live, since Clock In, On my way and Clock out act on it (the card's rule). A session on a bid or on a job off the schedule keeps no current job, so a kept header then reads *Ready to start* on the first unvisited job.
- **The Job Mode card** [`DashboardJobModeCard.tsx`](../../src/components/jobMode/DashboardJobModeCard.tsx) (4 kept-day smokes): `useKeptRead` keyed by `workDateYmd` with the card's own `loading`, which turns false only when every read of the day landed; `picked` and `dayRail` read the day on screen (kept or live), the header and the rail draw while kept in place of *Loading Job Mode…*, and the stamp sits at the top of the card (`keptReadStampTight`, no margin of its own inside the card's flex gap). Held while kept: the three big presses, the rail's tappable rows, Write up a change, Hand the phone, the quiet Clock out, and on a phone the whole press bar through `JobModePressBar`'s new `held` (the bar muted under `keptPress`, each button disabled with *reading again…*). Live: Job calendar, Materials on site, My statement. A failed read over a kept day shows the stamp's Retry and not the panel; with nothing kept, the panel as in v2.5962.
- **Guides**: *know what to do when the app is slow* gains *Your day on the phone*; *clock in and out with Job Mode* names the line under *Your day on the card*.
- **Docs**: `GLOSSARY.md` (The kept read), `DASHBOARD_SECTIONS_ARCHITECTURE.md` (§0, §9), the to-do's status.
