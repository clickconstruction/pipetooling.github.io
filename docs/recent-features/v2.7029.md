# v2.7029 — Messages: No reply needed sits in plain sight, and a thread says when it is done (2026-11-12)

Punch list #324, PR 3 of 5. No migration, no function to deploy.

## Why

The dev looked at the six lanes on 2026-11-12 and concluded a thread could not be closed: *the only options I see are New, Needs you, Needs a reply, We owe them, Waiting on them, Went out.* Closing with no reply had existed since cut 2 (`stateMenu`, `set_state`). It was two presses deep in the thread's ⋯, on a row in Needs a reply and on the Answer all desk. A row in New did not offer it at all. The thread never said it was Done. And the press left no trace: `set_state` wrote the state and nothing else, so nobody could tell who closed a thread or when.

## What changed

- **The press, in sight** (`followUp/noReply.ts`, `useFollowUpThreadSlots.tsx`): **No reply needed** in the thread's status row beside *We owe them…* while the conversation is Open. The ⋯ rows stay.
- **On a desk** (`NewLaneSlot.tsx`): the same press under the sort card on the Sort all desk, where the card is all there is. The sender stays unnamed and New asks again when they next write. Over a thread the status row holds it, so the card is unchanged there. A *Bid it?* card has its own closes and gets none.
- **A trace** (`FollowUpIo.noReplyNeeded`): every door now closes through one io call, `set_state` then `follow_up_event` with *Rosa said on Nov 12 that no reply is needed.* The shape is Heard back's (`waitingOnThem.ts`), and the two regexes do not match each other's lines. The close stands when the line cannot be written.
- **The state, on the thread** (`threadClose`): once Done by the press, a grey chip *Done · no reply needed · Rosa, Nov 12* and **Reopen**. On a thread only filed mail touched, *Done · old mail, filed Nov 9* and **Reopen**. Nothing for Snoozed, which has its own chip, or Done by our reply, where the move line says *Their move*.
- **The move line reads the state** (`CourtOptions.closed`): a Done conversation whose last word is theirs says *Nothing waits*. Before, a thread closed by a note or by the press kept saying *Our move · 2 d* in its header while no lane listed it.

## Not built

The card's section 4 said the same spot would offer **No answer needed** when our word is last and the person is in Waiting on them. The thread's read carries no line keys, so it cannot tell an estimate accepted through our link from any other grey line, and the press would show on threads the lane had already cleared. The lane's **Heard back ▾ → No answer needed** stays the way to close that side.

## How it was checked

- `noReply.test.ts`: the line's words in plain words and read back, each state's piece of the status row, the move line with and without the state.
- `useFollowUpThreadSlots.render.test.tsx`: the press in the status row closes as the presser; the Done chip, Reopen and the closed fact once it is done.
- `SortAllDesk.render.test.tsx`: the press under the card closes the thread and the next person comes up.
- The six existing assertions that expected `setState(id, 'done')` now expect `noReplyNeeded(id, name)`.
