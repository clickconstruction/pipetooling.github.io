## What changed

- **Kernel** [`lienWaiverAmountMath.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienWaiverAmountMath.ts) (tests on 650's numbers): `lienWaiverAmountMath(formType, job, picked, numbered)` reads the same rules `lienWaiverPrefillAmount` fills the box with — **conditional** = each bill less its payments (*Still owed on bill #1*), **unconditional progress** = the payments (*Paid so far*; with nothing recorded, the whole bill and a sentence saying so), **unconditional final** = the bills' face (*The whole job, paid in full*, `tooEarly` while any is owed). Payments are listed oldest first by `paid_on` with `waiverPaymentLabel` (*Check · Transfer · Card · Cash · Payment*). `show` only when the amount differs from the bills' face (or the final is early). `lienWaiverAlreadyCovered` finds a live, minted waiver of the same family (conditional or unconditional) covering a picked bill — furthest along, then newest — and `waiverCoverageSentence` / `waiverCoversSentence` say it (the signed date in the app calendar: Sep 30 9:19 PM CT is Oct 1 in UTC). `lienWaiverPaidUnwaived` is, under a conditional, the payments on the picked bills less live unconditional waivers covering them.
- **The window** ([`LienReleaseModal`](../../src/components/jobs/LienReleaseModal.tsx), [`LienWaiverAmountMath`](../../src/components/jobs/LienWaiverAmountMath.tsx)): each bill chip reads *#1 · $9,022.49 owed* over *of $26,800* / *nothing paid* / *paid in full* and *· waiver on file*. Under the Amount box: the math box; the amber *Bill #1 is already waived.* note with *Open the signed one ›* (the history's View) and *Discard this draft* (voids the unminted draft and closes); the blue *$17,777.51 is paid and not waived yet.* note with *Waive the $17,777.51 paid ›* (switches to unconditional progress and sets the amount — a resumed draft keeps no prefill); a typed-over amount gets *The bills say $X. You typed $Y.* and *Use $X*. Hovering the math marks the amount in the page preview (`MarkedWaiverAmount`); the math never prints. The notes show only while the row is editable.
- Guide *send a GC our lien waiver* gains *Why the amount is less than the bill*; GLOSSARY amended.
