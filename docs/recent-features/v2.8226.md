# v2.8226 — Punch list: see who is working on a card (2026-11-21)

The screen for [v2.8224](./v2.8224.md)'s migration. No migration of its own.

## Why

The tick went live the same day as v2.8199: a checkbox beside each card that means *I am working on this*, seen by one person. Taunya then asked for more, in her words: *"I think it might be a good idea for us to add to the punch list when a user has claimed a card that they are working on, And then they should release that claim after they've not updated the card in 12 hours. Does that make sense? If so, help me add it."*

A second person on another computer is about to work from the list, and nothing on the page said which cards were taken.

## What changed

- **One checkbox, not two.** Ticking a card claims it.
- **A line under the card's name says who is on it**, in both views: *ZZ Sam is working on this. Last update 3 h ago.* The name and the time are drawn apart from the words (values in place).
- **A claim goes quiet after 12 hours with no update.** An update is the newest of three things: the tick, a **Still on it** press by the claimer, and the card file's last change on `main`.
- **A quiet card is free to everyone else.** The line reads *Free to pick up. ZZ Sam had it and went quiet 2 h ago.*
- **The claimer keeps their own tick.** Their card reads *Your claim went quiet 2 h ago. Others see this card as free to pick up.*, with **Still on it** beside it.
- **A second person may tick a quiet card.** The card then reads as theirs. The first person still sees their own quiet tick under that line.
- **Two fresh claims on one card are allowed**, and the card names both: *You and ZZ Sam are working on this.*
- **A move still ends a claim.** A card that changes section shows nobody's claim.
- **Only the claimer takes their tick off.**

## Three calls made here, each Taunya's to change

1. **The claimer's own tick survives the 12 hours.** Her first ask was a tick that is *persistant between sessions*. Her second releases a claim after 12 hours. Read together: the claim is released to everyone else, and her own mark stays, marked as gone quiet. The other reading is that the tick is wiped at 12 hours for everyone. `OWN_TICK_AFTER_QUIET` in `punchListClaims.ts` is the one word that flips it, from `stays` to `wiped`, and the hook then deletes the row. The rule's two readings are both tested.
2. **Two people may hold one card.** Two people do work one train, and a refusal would only make the second person untick a card they are in fact on. A card with two names is plain to read.
3. **A change of the card file by anyone keeps every claim on it fresh.** Her words were *not updated the card*, and the build cannot tell whose commit a change was in a way that matches a person on the page.

## How the page knows a card's last change

The build already replays every commit on `main` that touched `to-dos/` for the burndown. The same read now keeps, for each card still in the folder, the newest commit that touched its file or anything in its folder. A mock-up counts. It ships in the history the page already loads, as `history.touched`, a slug to a time. The page makes no call to GitHub.

Two limits follow.

- The time is as of the last deploy. A change merged since counts after the next one.
- A build with no git history has no times, and a claim is then counted from the tick and **Still on it** alone.

## No job, no expiry

Nothing expires a row. Fresh or quiet is worked out each time the page draws, from the row's time, the card's time and the page's clock. The clock moves once a minute and when the tab comes back to the front.

## Who sees what

- **A dev or a master:** every claim, with the name.
- **Every other role:** cannot open the page, and the table answers them nothing.
- **Training mode:** reads every claim. A tick, an untick or **Still on it** is refused, the box goes back and the toast says why.
- **Archived:** meets the Away note before the page.
- **View as:** shows what that person sees. A press saves as that person. The page deletes nothing by itself.

## What became of *nobody else sees it*

v2.8196 and v2.8199 said so, and the page's own line did. This version retires the sentence. The page now says *Everyone who opens this board sees your name on it.* No tick existed when the read rule widened, so none made under the old words was shown to anyone.

## Stale rows

A person's own row on a card that moved or left the list is deleted by their page, as before. Another person's such row is only hidden, since a person deletes their own. It goes when its owner next opens the board on a newer build.
