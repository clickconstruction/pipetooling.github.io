# v2.8280 — The Bridge: a ZZ test estimate is no ask on Market and in no list on Sales (2026-11-21)

One of the faults punch list #334 wrote down under *Found while building, not built* and never built. No migration. One function is owed a redeploy after the merge.

## What was wrong

v2.7119 (PR 3 of the train) took ZZ test estimates out of the won rate, which was the ask. Its fragment listed what it left: the count of asks, and the Market and Sales windows' own lists. Those three reads asked for no title and no customer, so they could not tell a test estimate from a real one. A viewer who hides ZZ tests still had a test estimate counted in:

- the pipe's Market step: the estimates asked for, a week, and their dollars (`loadDemandPace.ts`);
- the pipe's Sales step: the estimates sent, won and lost in the window, and the estimates still out (the same read);
- the Market window: the asks by week, the window's dollars and the book's row of customers' estimates (`loadMarketDesk.ts`);
- the Sales window: the estimates still out and the estimates' own history, which gives their pace (`loadSalesDesk.ts`);
- the Monday email's asks and estimates, which are the first read again.

Confirmed in the code on `main`, and on the page: each of those reads answered with the one test estimate now on record.

## The rule

There is one rule for a test estimate, `isZzEstimate`, from v2.7119: its customer's name is a ZZ name, or its title is once *Estimate for* is taken off. It now sits with the Bridge's other ZZ rules in `src/lib/bridge/zzTestJobs.ts`, beside a dropper every estimates read uses:

- **`withoutZzBridgeEstimates(rows, hideZzEstimates)`**: the rows without the test estimates when the viewer hides ZZ tests. Every other row is kept as it came. When the viewer shows ZZ tests it hands back the same array.
- **`ZZ_ESTIMATE_FIELDS`**: the two fields the rule reads, `title` and `customers(name)`. Each read now asks for them.
- `loadEstimateWins.ts` hands `isZzEstimate` on, so nothing that took it from there changes.

## Why the reads take the switch by itself

The three loaders already take `zzTestJobIds`, and the bids in them leave whenever that is a set. On the page that set is the ZZ jobs joined to the jobs the left-out list names. So it is a set for a dev who shows ZZ tests as soon as the list names anything, and that dev's ZZ bids leave. That is the card's other fault, still open.

The estimates were not made to follow the set, or this fix would have taken that dev's test estimates away too. Each loader takes a new option, `hideZzEstimates`, which is the viewer's switch and nothing else:

- the page hands it `hideZz` (`useZzTestJobsHidden`);
- the Monday email hands it true whenever it is told to hide ZZ tests, which is always, with a left-out list or without one.

The option is required, so every caller has to say.

## The totals that move

Only for a viewer who hides ZZ tests, and only by the test estimates' own counts and dollars:

| Where | What moves |
|---|---|
| The pipe, Market | estimates asked for a week, and their dollars |
| The pipe, Sales | estimates sent, won and lost dollars; estimates still out, count and dollars |
| The Market window | asks by week, the window's dollars, the customers' estimates row |
| The Sales window | estimates still out; the estimates' pace and same-day split |
| The Monday email | its asks and its estimates, from the same read as the pipe |

On the live database today there is one test estimate inside the window: $200, made, sent and declined on 2026-11-18. So on this merge the asks lose one estimate and $200, the estimates sent lose $200 and the estimates lost lose $200, over the window. The estimates still out do not move, since it is declined. Every other figure on the page stays.

## What does not move, and the tests that hold it

- **No figure for a viewer who shows ZZ tests.** `loadDemandPace.test.ts`, `loadMarketDesk.test.ts` and `loadSalesDesk.test.ts` each read with `hideZzEstimates: false` and get every estimate, by id. The demand test holds that viewer's counts and dollars as literal numbers, test estimates included. Each of the three reads again with `zzTestJobIds` as a set, the way a left-out list makes it, and gets the same estimates.
- **No real estimate for a viewer who hides them.** In each of the three, the read of real and test estimates together equals the read of the real ones alone, whole object to whole object. So do `buildDemand`, `buildMarketDesk` and `buildSalesDesk` over them: the page draws what it would draw had the test estimates never been written.
- **No bid, no job and no asker.** In each of the three, the bids are the same with the switch on and off. On Market so are every asker's first bid, record and name, and every row of the book but the customers' estimates. On Sales so are the open and the decided bids.
- **The rule itself.** `zzTestJobs.test.ts` (5 more): a test leaves by its customer's name or its title; a real estimate is kept as the same object with every field, *Pizza Plaza* among them; the switch off hands back the same array; so does a list with no test in it; and the dropper agrees with `isZzEstimate` row by row.
- **The email.** `sheetReads.test.ts` (1 more): the asks' read is told to hide test estimates with a list and without one, and told to keep them for a reader who keeps ZZ tests though the list makes the ids a set.

## Left, on purpose

- **A dev who shows ZZ jobs still loses the ZZ jobs and bids once the left-out list names anything.** That is the card's last open fault. `hideZzEstimates` is the first half of the shape its fix needs: the switch apart from the ids.
- **The left-out list does not reach the estimates in these three reads.** An estimate for a customer or an address the Bridge leaves out leaves the won rate (`loadEstimateWins.ts`) and still counts as an ask. Nobody asked for that to change, and it would move real rows.
