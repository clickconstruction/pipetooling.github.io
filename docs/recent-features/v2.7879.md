# v2.7879 — Robots: an assistant can make a new robot account (2026-11-18)

A new pair on `dev-mcp` (server 0.105.0, 129 write verbs, 76 naming no RPC): **`plan_twin`** → **`apply_twin`**. The owner, 2026-11-18, after an audit of what an agent can stand up alone with the dev seat and the twin seat: "Yes, build it."

## Why

The audit walked every step from nothing to a first locked shadow. One step had no verb: making the twin's account with its CountTooling seat. It was a press on Settings → System → Digital twins. `plan_account` cannot flag a twin and refuses to touch one.

## What it does

It makes a digital twin's account as the panel's mint button makes it (`DigitalTwinsPanel.mintTwin`).

- `twin = { kind }`. `estimator` is a bid robot. `pricer` is the pricing robot that never bids. Any other key is refused.
- The caller chooses the kind and nothing else. The seat number, the address and the name come from the fleet.
- The plan reads every twin as the dev, archived ones too, and answers the seat it would take, the flags, the other apps' seats and the fleet as it stands with each twin's rung.
- The plan hash commits to the fleet. A twin minted since the plan, by anyone, refuses the apply.
- A seat whose address a users row already wears without the twin flag is refused at the plan. That row is a mint that stopped before its flags, or a person's.

## What apply writes

Each step goes through the door the panel uses, as the dev, so every function's own dev gate holds.

1. `create-user` with `skip_roster_row`, role `estimator`, and a 24-character password made on the server with `crypto.getRandomValues`. The password is sent once and kept nowhere. A twin signs in through `twin-login`, never with it.
2. The twin flags on the users row under the dev's RLS: `is_digital_twin`, `read_only`, and `twin_kind = 'pricer'` for a pricer.
3. For a bid robot, `ct-bridge` `create` at the CountTooling fleet address, then `users.counttooling_user_id`.
4. For a bid robot, `tt-bridge` `create` at the TakeoffTooling fleet address.

Each step answers done or held with its reason (`TwinStep`), and what landed stays. A held seat names its door: the link button on the twin's row. A row left unflagged gets no seat in the other apps, and the closing line says what a dev must do before another plan.

## What it leaves alone

- No key. `mint_twin_setup_code { label, twin }` is the next call.
- No rung. A new twin is read-only, as the panel leaves it.
- Revoking a key, retrying a held seat, the calibration standard and Archive stay presses in the panel.

## One kernel for the seat

`nextTwinSeat` moved to `supabase/functions/_shared/twinSeat.ts` with the seat's name and its addresses in the other two apps. `src/lib/twinConsoleDisplay.ts` re-exports it, so the panel and the seat number a twin by one rule. The panel itself did not change.
