## What changed

- **The kernel** (`src/lib/messages/bodyContacts.ts`): `contactPieces` reads `bodyLinks.ts`'s pieces into text, links and **contact points** — a phone number as written with its extension's digits beside it (*(832) 871-4166 ext. 121*; `+1`, dots and `x44` read too; a ZIP+4 and a date do not), an email address, and a street address (a number, the street, a suite, the city, the state and the ZIP; one the mail client wrapped reads as one line of words). The mail client's `[image: …]` markers fold into the kind of the line and are hidden on a line that holds a press (*phone*, *email*, *company-address* name what follows; a marked address line is the address whole); a marker on a line with nothing pressable stays as it came. A link piece is never read, so a tracking link stays a link. `bodyContactPoints` lists each point once; `canTextPoint` says whether a number can take a text (ten digits of the US and Canada, no extension); `mapHref` is the customer window's Google Maps search. The line a press leaves is one sentence, `contactPressBody`, read back by `contactPressOf`, and `contactForPressValue` finds the thread a line's address opens to among the view's contacts.
- **The viewer** (`src/components/gc/messages/MessageBodyContacts.tsx`): each point's words, then its presses right after them — the house **Call ▾** (`CallButton`, Quo or my phone, the number without the extension so `tel:` dials; `CallButton` gains `onPress`, which hears the dial and the way it went), **Text** where the number can take one, **Email**, **Open the map** in a new tab. Text and Email need the thread's acts; a window with no thread around it draws the words, Call and the map alone. `MessageWindow` takes `contactActs` and draws its words through it.
- **The presses** (`useContactPointActs.tsx` over `bodyContactsIo.ts`): every press writes a gray event line on the thread the press was made in through `follow_up_event`, the thread menu's writer for the customer's say, as the signed-in person (`follow_up_user_name`), held for ten seconds with **Undo** on the toast as Heard back does (v2.6500); the held lines live in a store outside any component (`deferLine`), so Undo survives the viewer closing and the thread changing, and `pagehide` writes what is still held; a refused write says why (`pressProblem`). The lines: *Grace pressed Call on (832) 871-4166 ext. 121 from Glenn's email of Oct 22.*, *… pressed Text on …*, *… pressed Email on …*, *… opened the map for 4150 Bluebonnet Dr. Suite 102 Stafford, TX, 77477 from …*, *… opened a thread for glenn@marrexbuilders.example from …*; the source reads *Glenn's email of Oct 22*, *our email of …*, *a note of Nov 4*. A call Quo places lands its own call line as today: the pressed line names the intent, the call line the fact.
- **Where Text and Email go**: the number or the address is this person's (`normalizeChannel` against the contact's channels) → the viewer closes and this thread reopens with the box woken on that kind; anyone else's → `contact_for_channel` (a read) finds whose it is, else `makeThread` makes one by the press, never by a read, through the migration's `contact_make_for_channel` — a SECURITY DEFINER for the office team (`follow_up_press`), since a signed-in client has no INSERT on `contacts` or `contact_channels` (`20261012010000`) and the edge's placers are the service role's; it answers whose it is first under an advisory lock, else makes a `contacts` row as the edge makes one for an unknown sender (kind `unknown`, named by the address or the number) with its one `contact_channels` row — then `name_contact` as a new person at the same customer (`customers`) or trade partner (`gc_companies`, an architect's thread naming an architect), the address or the number as their name until someone gives a better one — `name_contact` folds only a same-named contact on the record, so the new person stands beside the sender; a thread on a lead, a supply house, a candidate, a teammate or an unnamed sender gets no record, and the new thread's sort card asks *Who is this?* with the domain's customers offered. The line reads *… opened a thread for …* and `EventLine` draws **open it ›** on it while someone holds that address.
- **The box wakes on the kind** (`useThreadWindow.ts`): `?reply=text|email` beside `?nudge=`, `OpenThreadMore.reply`, `startReplyKindOf`; `NudgedReplyBox` hands it to `ReplyBox`'s new `startKind`, which moves the kinds row and wakes a folded box once per ask. `ThreadWindow.onShowContact` takes the `more`, and the two pages pass it to `openThread`.
- **Docs**: the guide *find every message with a person in one place* gains § Call or write to a number in an email's words; `MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md` and the Glossary's Messages entry each gain a clause.
