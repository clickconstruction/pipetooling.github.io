# v2.8085 — Send a paper: a customer search that fails says so (2026-11-20)

Found by the audit of punch list #403. Two windows of Documents → Agreements drew a failed read as "nobody found". No migration and no function.

## What was wrong

- **Who is it with?** (`PaperPartyPicker`, the second window of Send a paper) asks the database for customers as the office types. Its rejection handler set the customers to an empty list and said nothing. The window then listed the roster, the supply houses and **Someone not in any list**, so a lost signal read as "this customer is not in the app".
- **Add to a list** (`PaperPartyAddToList`), under **Someone at a company we already have**, dropped two rejections the same way: the short lists' read (the supply houses) and the customer search. A failed read drew an empty list under the find box.

Either way the next press was a duplicate: a paper sent to someone "not in any list", or a second customer made for one the app already keeps.

## The fix

Both windows now keep the failure and draw it, as the picker already did for its short lists: `recoveryFailureFromError(e, …)` on `OfflineRetryPanel` with `idempotent`, and a retry that bumps an attempt counter in the effect's deps.

- The picker says *The customers did not read.* with the reason, on its own panel (`paper-party-picker-customers`). The roster, the supply houses and the last row still list under it: a failed customer search never takes the window down.
- Add to a list says *The supply houses did not read.* and *The customers did not read.* under its find box, each with its own Retry (`paper-party-add-to-list-houses`, `paper-party-add-to-list-customers`).
- A lost signal reads the shared read sentence and offers **Retry**, and reads again by itself when the signal is back. A refusal shows its reason with no Retry.
- A new keystroke clears the old failure, since it starts a new search.
- A search that truly finds nothing draws as before. No label changed.

## Tests

- `AgreementsSendPaper.render.test.tsx`: *a customer search that fails says so, keeps the other rows, and reads again on Retry*, and *a customer search that is refused says why, and a new keystroke clears it*.
- `PaperPartyAddToList.render.test.tsx`: *a customer search that fails says so under the find box, and reads again on Retry*, *supply houses that do not read say so, and read again on Retry*, and *a refused read names its reason after the window’s own sentence*.

All five failed before the change and pass after it.
