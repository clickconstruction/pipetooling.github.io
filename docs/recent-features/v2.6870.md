# v2.6870 — Permit news on the Bid Board (punch list #307, PR 5) (2026-11-10)

A permit changes what a person does mostly after the bid is sent, and nobody opens a sent bid to look. So the board says it: a strip above the sections and a chip on the row.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/research/bidPermitNews.ts`** (new, pure, `bidPermitNews.test.ts`): `bidPermitNews(bids, permitsByCity, readCities, today)` gives each undecided bid on a street address in a city we read its chip and, when there is news, its line. A general plumbing permit that lists a name other than the building permit's contact is news however old, leads the list and stays while the bid is undecided. A building permit issued, or an application filed, is news for `NEWS_DAYS` (30). A plumbing permit under the building permit's own name is not another plumber. A decided bid, a robot's copy, a town not read and a site with nothing on it get neither.
- **`useBidPermitNews(bids, enabled)`** (new): one read for the whole board through `loadPermitsByHouseNumber`, run again only when the set of house numbers changes. A failed read, or the read not on the database yet, draws nothing.
- **`BidBoardPermitNews`** (new): the strip, *Permit news on your bids* with its count, four lines and the rest behind *See N more*, one press per line: **Open the bid**.
- **`BidsBidBoardTab`**: the strip above the section pills, and `PermitChip` under the GC on the desk row and the phone card. `showPermitNews` is passed by `Bids.tsx` for `canResearchPermits` roles.
- Guide `see-what-the-city-has-on-a-bids-address` (Permit news on the Bid Board), `docs/PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`, `docs/BIDS_BOARD_FORM_ARCHITECTURE.md`.

## Not built as drawn

The mock-up's **Mark lost…** press. The news line has one press, Open the bid, where the outcome is set: a press that says *Mark lost* has to land on the outcome, and the bid form has no such focus yet. The follow-up queue's line is not built either.
