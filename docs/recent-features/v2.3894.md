# v2.3894 — Subs Work on a phone: rows, with each row’s moves in one sheet (2026-09-27)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. The owner's pick of 2026-09-27 on punch list #30's leftover L9: the Work view's cards, about 500 px each, become two-line rows with a sheet — the pattern [v2.3887](v2.3887.md) gave the Pay view.

## What shipped

- **`src/lib/subs/subsWorkPhoneRows.ts`** (new, pure) — `subsWorkPhoneRow` (the row's title — the stage and the sub; where it stands and its window; one amount: what is open, *unpriced* or *paid*; whether the office owes it a move) and `subsWorkMoveIsButton` (the sub's own step and a finished row are words, not buttons).
- **`JobsSubsWorkView.tsx`**, under 768 px — each row of a job's card is a 52 px button. A tap opens `ScheduleSheet` with the row's next move first (from `standingMovesForRow`, run through the same `runMove`), its second move where it has one, *Set a window* / *Change the window*, every item of the row's ⋯ menu, and *Show the whole card*.
- **The card is still there**, folded under its row: the rail, agreed · paid · open, the window cell and the inline forms, exactly as drawn before. It opens by itself when a form is open on the row, a window is being edited, or a builder's ask is waiting on it — so nothing that needs an answer is hidden — and a move taken from the sheet opens it.
- At 768 px and up the table is unchanged.
- **The guide** *sub-labor-outstanding* → *On a phone*.
