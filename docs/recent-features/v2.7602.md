# v2.7602 — Checklist: the ⋮ menu on Manage stays open on a scrolled list (2026-11-17)

Found on a walk of the live site: the ⋮ menu of the last task on Checklist → Manage opened and was gone within a second.

## Why

Three rules met, each right alone.

- `ManageRowMenu` is drawn `position: fixed` with a full-screen backdrop behind it (v2.7185), and it closes on any scroll, since a menu pinned to the screen would be left behind by one.
- `BodyScrollLockSentinel` freezes the page behind any fixed layer that covers the screen (`findBlockingOverlays`). It took the menu's backdrop for an open window.
- The freeze pins the body with `position: fixed`. On a page scrolled down, the document's scroll offset goes to 0 for an instant, and that is a scroll event.

So on a scrolled list the press opened the menu, the lock moved the page, the menu heard a scroll and closed, and the lock let go and put the page back. Logged on the live site: the menu in the page, a document scroll to 0 eleven milliseconds later, the menu gone, a scroll back to where the page was. At the top of the page the offset is already 0, nothing moves, and the menu stayed open, which is why it looked fine on a short list.

## What changed

- The menu's backdrop carries `data-page-scroll="allow"`, the scroll lock's own opt-out. The page under the menu is free to scroll, and a scroll closes the menu, as designed.

## Checked

- `ManageRowMenu.render.test.tsx` (7, one new): with the menu open and its backdrop given the whole screen, `findBlockingOverlays` finds nothing. The same layer without its opt-out is found, so the test would fail without the fix.
- A local run of the branch, as a dev, in headless Chromium: the page scrolled to the bottom, the last task's ⋮ pressed. The menu was still open two seconds later with *Edit*, *Mute notifications* and *Delete*, the page was not frozen, and no scroll event fired. A wheel scroll then closed it.

## Not checked

- Other menus that close on a scroll and draw a full-screen backdrop may share this. None was looked at: `BidsTakeoffTab`'s and `LienBoard`'s are the first to read.
