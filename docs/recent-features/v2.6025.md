# v2.6025 — GC mode: a trade partner’s portal shows the job’s phases and the dates to meet above its own bars (2026-10-28)

PR 6 of six of punch list #204 (`to-dos/schedule-builder/README.md`, retired by this PR; the mock-up's D5), the owner's
call 5: the phases strip and the dates to meet above what the trade portal shows today, never another company's bars.

## The kernel (`schedule/portalSchedule.ts`)

- `PortalSchedule` gains `phases?: PortalPhase[]` (`{name, start, finish, days}`, the schedule's phases in order, each
  spanning the bars and dates drawn in it, `portalPhases`) and `dates?: PortalDate[]` (`{label, on, met}`, the job's
  dates to meet, the earliest first, `portalDates`). Both are optional, so a function deployed before the page, or
  after it, keeps the chart drawing. A schedule drawn with no phases has none; its dates still come.
- `gc-trade-portal` works it out in `_shared/gcTradePortalSchedule.ts` from the same rows: `_shared/gcScheduleRead.ts`
  now selects `gc_schedules.phases`, `gc_schedule_activities.phase` and `gc_schedule_milestones.phase` (v2.6020), and
  reads the milestones for a trade as it did for the customer. The copy of the kernels is regenerated.

## The strip (`GcTradePortalSchedule.tsx`)

Above the bars, on the whole job's own axis: *The job's phases*, one row each with its name, dates and days, a bar
green once past, blue while running; then *Dates to meet*, a diamond on each day with Today, and the dates listed under
them, *met* once met. In Spanish: *Las fases del trabajo*, *Fechas a cumplir*, *cumplida*, *{days} días*
(`portalI18n.ts`). The sample portal's job carries three phases and three dates.

## Tests

`portalSchedule.test.ts`: Summit Roofing's chart on Fair Oaks D drawn from the quick-lube shell carries the eight
phases with their dates and days and the six dates to meet, with nothing of a company, a price or a bar's id; a
schedule drawn without phases has none and lists its dates. `GcTradePortal.schedule.render.test.tsx`: the strip on
the sample above the bars, with no price, in Spanish, and none for a schedule with no phases.
`tradePortalSchedule.test.ts` and `edgeKernels.test.ts` green on the regenerated copy.
