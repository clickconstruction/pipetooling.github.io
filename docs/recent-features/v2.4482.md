## What was wrong

BP375 is built from the takeoff, so thirteen of its fourteen rows are `proposed`. The GC's page would have read *9 rows need a call — 13 are proposed — the plans' schedule was not on the bid*, and every card *What we intend to install; the plans' schedule was not on the bid to compare against.* To an architect that reads as "they bid without the schedule", thirteen times. It was also untrue on BP398, whose schedule holds four tags and simply lacks the fixture. *The plans:* on such a card showed the takeoff's own name for the fixture (*DWH1 & ET*). *Needs a call* and *2 to go* are the office's words.

## What changed

All in the words the room, the preview (*See what the GC sees*) and the tab's *The GC's page will read* line share:

- [`_shared/submittalRoomPayload.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/submittalRoomPayload.ts): a proposed row's `why` is *This is the product we intend to install.* `roomHeadline`: *N products need your answer*. `roomSubline`: *N are products we intend to install* and *N products match the plans…*.
- [`SubmittalRoomView.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/SubmittalRoomView.tsx) (`RoomRowCard`): a proposed row's chip reads *for your review*; its fixture name shows without *The plans:*; *We intend to install:* where *Proposed:* was (a row that differs from the schedule keeps *The plans:* and *Proposed:*). The parts line reads *3 parts · 1 approved · 2 to answer*.
- [`SubmittalRoom.tsx`](../../src/pages/SubmittalRoom.tsx): *N decided · N to answer*.

The office's own side keeps its words: the row still reads Proposed, and *1 approved · 1 to go* on the tab.
