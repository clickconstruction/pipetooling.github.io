# v2.4308 — Lien notices: a job worked over several months reads its earliest notice date (2026-10-01)

No migration, no edge function. Found 2026-10-01 reading the Lien desk live on production data (dev login, nothing written): the Calendar's GC row for Southern Post Construction said *send the notice by Dec 15 · 75 d* for 878 Take 5 – Seguin ($38,625, commercial, worked June through September) and sorted near the bottom, while the Notices tab said *due in 14d* and the Timeline *Approve the Jul + Aug + Sep notice — 14 days*. July's notice is due Oct 15, and the Calendar's own to-do already counted July's flag under *By Oct 15*. Same on 891 Take 5 – Liberty Hill (Burd & Assoc., *by Nov 16*) and 650 ATI Schertz (Loberg Contracting, *by Dec 15*). The Pipeline's Billed rows carried the same date: *Lien notice by Dec 15 · 75 d* on each of 878's three bills.

## Cause

`buildLienPayRunway` dated the § 53.056 notice from the LAST work month only (`noticeDeadlineForMonth(lastWork, kindEff)`), and every reader took `noticeByYmd` / `daysToNotice` from it: the Calendar's GC word (`groupWord`), a job row's word (`rowWord`), the sort (`runway.sortKey`), the phone sentence and the hovers (`runway.lines`), the Pipeline's dates block (`billedDatesLedger`) and the phone chip (`lienRunwayWantsTheChip`). A sub owes one notice per month worked, so the date that matters is the earliest month still owed. The Calendar's flags came from elsewhere — the desk's `useForecastWorkMonths`, every month — so its flags and its words disagreed.

## What changed

- **The runway knows every work month** ([`lienPayRunway.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienPayRunway.ts)): input `workMonths` ('YYYY-MM' months with approved hours), output `noticeMonths` (each month's notice on the runway's own clock, `sent` / `owed` / `closed`). On a sub job the notice date is the earliest month still owed whose window is open. The § 53.052 lien date still counts from the last month, and so does a closed window (the last month's notice passed unsent → lien gone). Without the months (`null`) the last month stands alone, as before. A month after the last work month is left out. The hover names the months (*§ 53.056 notices for July, August and September 2026 are owed to the owner and the GC, the first by Oct 15; …*). The notice mark's check now means nothing is owed (`!noticeOwed`), not only that the last month is on file. Nine tests on 878's and 927's shapes.
- **Where the months come from** ([`useBilledLienClocks`](../../src/hooks/useBilledLienClocks.ts)): a third read — `clock_sessions` for the board's sub jobs, approved and not rejected or revoked (the sessions `last_work_date` counts), paged with `fetchAllRowsChunkedIn` in a stable order and folded to `workMonths` per job. A failed read leaves them `null`, so the runway counts the last month alone instead of every row losing its runway. `JobsStagesTab`'s `lienRunwayFor` passes them, so the Pipeline's rows and the Calendar's rows still read one runway (v2.4101) and agree.
- **The Calendar's flags are the runway's months** ([`lienCalendar.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienCalendar.ts) `noticeFlagsFor`): a hollow flag per month still owed, a check once on file, none for a closed month — the same months on the same clock as the words, so a GC row's date is always its earliest hollow flag, and every flag names its month. `LienCalendarJob.months` and `LienCalendarMonth` are gone, with `lienCalendarRowsWithMonths` and the v2.4152 widening of the desk's `useForecastWorkMonths` to every billed job: the desk's work months cover its own jobs again, one less sessions read when the desk opens. Four kernel tests and one render case on the live shapes.
- **No property kind:** the runway's clock puts an unset kind on the earlier, residential date (its rule since v2.4051), but the forecast hook's months used the commercial date (as the desk's server RPC does). On 963 Knight Springtown Vet the words said Oct 15 while the flag sat on Nov 16. Flags and words now both read the residential clock, the date the key's *Kind not set* entry promises.
- Copy: the board's footer, the work tick's hover and the key's *Last day worked* panel say the lien date counts from the last month and each notice from its own month.
- Guide *read the Pipeline's money view*: the deadline row shows the earliest month still owed. GLOSSARY *Lien calendar* and *Bill dates ledger*; `LIEN_DESK_ARCHITECTURE.md`.

## Not done

- On a phone the Calendar shows *0 jobs* until the phone board's Billed stage has been opened — its rows come from the Pipeline's loaded jobs, and the phone board loads one stage at a time. Unchanged here.
- The *closed* rule still keys on the last month: a job whose last month's notice window closed unsent reads *lien gone* even if an earlier month's notice went out. No live job has that shape; no billed sub job has a notice recorded today.
- The guide *send lien notices from the Lien desk* is on the plain-words legacy list (`plainWordsLegacy.ts`): it already says a notice has to beat its earliest deadline, but still calls the tick *the month every date on the row is counted from*. The gate fails CI on any edit short of a full rewrite, so the plain-words sweep carries that phrase.
