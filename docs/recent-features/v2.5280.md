# v2.5280 — Messages: the database for a change order started from a message (2026-10-12)

Messages, cut 4b's SQL (migration `20261012180000_change_orders_from_messages`, after the spine's `20261012010000`): a change order started from a customer's message, drafted by the office and sent to sign by the money team (decision 11 as the split). On cut 4b's kernel (`src/lib/changeOrders/fromMessage.ts`, v2.5272). No screen, no edge function.

- **`gc_change_orders`** gains `signer_contact_id` and `source_message_id`, one change order a message; **`gc_change_orders_office`** carries the two at its end, so the office draws the message's chip and the order's line back, still never a money column.
- **`gc_draft_change_order_from_message`** is the office's draft, `SECURITY DEFINER` behind `gc_office_team()` in its body, since the table stays the money team's: widening its insert policy could not work, as `gc_draft_change_order` numbers from the rows its caller sees and returns the new row. It refuses, in words, a training account, a twin, anyone outside the office, a message not a customer's or with no job, a job bidding or lost, and a second order from the same message, and returns the id and the number only. Cost and price may stay 0 for the money team to price.
- **`gc_change_order_sends`** keeps each send to sign in `gc_owner_contract_sends`' shape, the money team's (read and insert, no update or delete), with the three fences; **`gc_send_change_order_to_sign`** (`SECURITY INVOKER`) writes it, sending the draft the first time, with its file in the private bucket **`gc-change-orders`**. `src/lib/gc/doors.ts` lists the table as the money team's (Owner Billing), as `doors.test.ts` asks of every GC table.
- **`contacts_fold_change_orders`**: the spine's `contacts_fold` runs it, so who signs follows a contact that is folded instead of going null with its delete. A definer, since the orders are the money team's; outside a trigger, a call from someone signed in off the office team, or a twin, moves nothing.
- **The bed** (`scripts/pgtest-gc-change-orders-from-messages.sh`, in `scripts/sql-beds.txt`): Rosa drafts change order 2 from Priya's text and reads it through the office's view; Maria prices it and sends it to sign. Owner Billing's bed pins the view's fourteen columns. Not pushed: `supabase db push` waits for `main`.
