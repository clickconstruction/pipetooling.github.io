# v2.3581 — `pay:backfill` says where each person stands (2026-09-17)

The owner, 2026-09-17, after the by-hand pass over four people: *"There are other people who have been paid via Cash App. Could we take my Cash App ledger and now compare it against the other people that were not covered earlier?"* The plan already ran over everyone; what it lacked was the per-person reading a person makes of it.

## The section — [`backfillPlan.ts`](../../src/lib/cashapp/backfillPlan.ts) `summarizeBackfillByPerson` / `renderByPerson`

Under the plan, one table row per person:

| Column | Means |
|---|---|
| App open | net − paid over open reports, from `pay_position` |
| Recorded, no send | payments no Cash App or Mercury send backs after every rule — client-direct memos excluded |
| Sent, not recorded | pay/advance sends with no payment (the plan's `record` candidates) |
| Corrections | recorded − sent over the within-$5 links (positive: the app recorded more than went out) |
| Review | sends left for a person — not counted either way |
| Linked | payments the plan links or splits, plus those already carrying a source |
| **Standing** | open + recorded-no-send + corrections − sent-not-recorded: positive still owed, negative ahead |

Then the unbacked rows per person, dated with their memo, so each one can be checked in Mercury or explained. Pure kernel, 1 test; the script fetches `pay_position` per person.

## Reading it

A person's standing is only as good as the aliases: an unnamed counterparty's sends sit in Review and count nowhere. Proxy accounts (Tristen through Taunya) pool at the account holder for unmatched sends. A recorded-no-send row memoed *Mercury* whose transfer the plan could not find is the doubled-record case; one memoed blank on a payday is the one to look at first.
