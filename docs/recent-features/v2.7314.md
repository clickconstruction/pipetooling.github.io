# v2.7314 — Lien desk: the footer bar sits under the paper, and the list runs to the bottom (2026-11-15)

The owner's screenshot of 2026-11-15, Lien affidavits on a computer, job 891: the footer strip — the amber *August 2026's notice is still due* line, the grey *Blocked until every gate above is clear* line with the red *The lien right … ends December 15, 2026*, and *The leader said to file it ▸* / *Approve ▸* — ran under both columns, so the list of liens on the left lost its bottom to a bar about one job. "It should take only the right-hand side, under the pane of the item being worked on, so the left list stays fully visible to its bottom." Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## Before

Since v2.3753 the affidavit and retainage panes have portalled their footer into one slot the desk draws under the two-column grid (`.lienDeskFooterSlot`, `data-lien-desk-footer`), and the notice pane's footer was drawn inline in the same place. The grid above it was `320px 1fr` with `overflow: hidden`, so both columns ended where the strip began.

## What changed

- **[`src/lib/jobs/lienDeskFooterHome.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDeskFooterHome.ts)** (new): `lienDeskFooterHome(oneColumn)` says where the strip sits — `pane` on a computer, with the pane column's style (`grid`, rows `minmax(0, 1fr) auto`, `minHeight: 0`, `overflow: hidden`), and `desk` on a phone or the stacked layout with no column. Tests: the two shapes.
- **`LienDesk.tsx`**: `paneFooter` is the one footer node — the affidavit slot, the retainage slot, or the notice footer (now `data-lien-desk-footer="notice"`) — and `paneColumn(node)` wraps the pane with it in a `data-lien-desk-pane-column` div on a computer; the three two-column branches of the grid draw `paneColumn(affPane)`, `paneColumn(retPane)` and `paneColumn(pane)`, and after the grid the strip is drawn only when the home is `desk`. The portal is as it was: the panes still render into `footerEl`; only the slot element moved, and it still hides when empty. The list column (`withFind`) is untouched, so it scrolls to the desk's bottom edge.
- **`LienDesk.render.test.tsx`**: on a computer the notice, affidavit and retainage footers are each the last child of the pane column, not inside the find column, and the desk draws no strip under the grid; on a phone the strip stays the fold bar's next sibling with no column around the pane. The #352 PR 6 phone tests pass as they were.
- Guide *send lien notices from the Lien desk*: the bar is at the bottom of the pane. [`docs/LIEN_DESK_ARCHITECTURE.md`](../LIEN_DESK_ARCHITECTURE.md): the dated note.

## Checked

On the dev server at 1,600 px: Lien affidavits (job 891 — the amber line, the grey line and the two presses under the pane, the list to the bottom edge), Monthly notices (job 372 — *Don't send yet*, *Save draft* and *Go to gate 1* under the pane, the pile heads stacked at the list's bottom) and Retainage notices (job 891 — the sent line and *Sign and approve ▸* under the pane). At the 375 phone preset: `innerWidth` 375, the fold bar with the footer as its next sibling, no pane column, and one press on the bar hides the footer without unmounting it.

## Not changed

- The footer's words and presses, `.lienDeskFooterSlot`'s look (`index.css`), the panes, the list rows and the gate rows. No CSS changed.
- A phone, and a host that stacks the desk (`layout="stacked"`), keep the strip under the fold bar: its sibling rule in `index.css` needs the footer right after the bar.
