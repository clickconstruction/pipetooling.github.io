# v2.7260 — Setting a job to 100% can move it to Ready to Bill in the same press (2026-11-14)

Client only. No migration, no edge function, and no kernel that is copied to the edge functions changed.

The owner, setting percents from the Bridge: "if we set it to a hundred percent, that probably means we're ready to bill. So we should probably add an option where when someone sets a job to a hundred percent, they can move it to ready to bill from that modal", and then "I want you to think of the best option and I want you to propose it to me". He took the proposal, with one tick for everyone and no hold on a job with no price.

## Before

A save at 100% on a Working job in the % done window (`FieldPctUpdateModal`) opened a second window, `MarkJobReadyToBillPrompt`, with two checkboxes and its own press. Five presses across two windows, and the offer could not be seen until after the save.

## What changed

- **A kernel** (`src/lib/jobs/pctReadyToBill.ts`, new, pure): when the line is offered (the value to save is 100 and the job is Working), Save's words with the move named on them, whether a saved percent is followed by the move (only when it was asked for and the job is still Working after the save), the words when a move is refused, and the line's own words.
- **The window** (`FieldPctUpdateModal.tsx`): at 100% on a Working job one line appears under the control, **Also move to Ready to Bill**, unticked, with *Parts are reported, and the customer knows the work is done.* under it: the two statements the old window asked one at a time. Save reads **Save 100% and move to Ready to Bill** when it is ticked, and **Save · job 100% and move to Ready to Bill** by line. One press files the status report, mirrors the percent as before, then moves the job with `markJobReadyToBill` (`update_job_status`). The second window no longer follows a save here.
- **On a phone** the sheet's body scrolls, and the line appears below its fold. It is brought into view as it appears, so it is not left half under the foot.
- **A refused move** keeps the percent. The window turns to *Saved at 100%* and says *The percent is saved. The job did not move to Ready to Bill.* with the reason, and offers **Move to Ready to Bill** again (the move alone, no second report) or **Leave it in Working**. Either way the page hears that the percent saved.
- **A job already at 100% and still Working** shows the line too. Ticked, Save makes the move alone and files no second report of the same number.
- **The Bridge** (`BridgePctRun.tsx`, unchanged) gets it through the shared window: a ticked save moves the job and the window goes on to the next job on the list. A job with no price is not held, by the owner's choice.

## Left as it was

- **Leave Report** and the New report and Additional report windows still open `MarkJobReadyToBillPrompt` with its two checkboxes after a 100% report.
- The Stages board's own move to Ready to Bill.
- `update_job_status` itself: who may move a job is unchanged.

## Tests

- `pctReadyToBill.test.ts` (5, new): when the line is offered, Save's words, when the move follows a save, the refused words, and the plain-words rule on the line's words.
- `FieldPctUpdateModal.render.test.tsx` (15): the line at 100% only, Save naming the move, a ticked save filing the report then moving with no second window, an unticked save moving nothing, a refused move keeping the percent and moving on a second press with no second report, leaving it in Working, nothing offered on a job that is not Working, a job already at 100% moving by the tick alone, and the same line by line.
- `BridgePctRun.render.test.tsx` (9): a ticked save on the Bridge moves the job and goes on to the next.

## Verify

Walked on the branch's dev server as a dev over production rows, in headless Chrome, from the Bridge's percent list at 1400 wide light and dark and at 375 wide. The save's writes (the status report, the percent, and the move) were answered in the browser with made-up replies, so no job's percent or status changed and no report was filed. The line appeared at 100% and not below, came into view above the foot on a phone, and Save named the move when ticked. A ticked save filed the report, mirrored the percent and asked for the move, then went on to the next job, and the list's count dropped by one. A refused move kept the window with its reason, and leaving it in Working went on to the next job.
