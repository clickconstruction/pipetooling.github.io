# v2.3505 — What customers see: every outside surface on the tab, and a guard so it cannot drift again (2026-09-16)

PR 1 of the nine-PR train in `to-dos/what-customers-see-journeys/README.md` (mock-up beside it; Claude artifact *Every outside surface*). Client only: no migration, no edge function, no role change yet.

## Why

Settings → What customers see (v2.2758 / v2.2760) rendered three audiences and fifteen steps, and had not been told about anything built since: the customer's own service agreement (`/contract/sign` — the tab's "Contract to sign" is the sub's document), the submittal review room (v2.3487), the collections law firm's portal, the supply house quote page, the hazmat notice, the estimate terms page, and the dozen emails behind them. Nothing asked "is it on What customers see?" when a room or a sender shipped — the same drift the smoke suite suffered (v2.3489).

## What shipped

- **[`customerJourneys.ts`](../../src/lib/customerJourneys.ts)** — five audiences. Homeowner gains the terms page, the agreement lane (email · page · reminder · signed), bill by email, hazmat notice, and after a *when it goes wrong* break the demand letter and lien release. General contractor gains the pricing package email, the submittal room and its decided state, the test report email, the statement email, and the notice to the owner of record. New strips **Supply house** (quote request email, quote page, submitted, job account email) and **Collections law firm** (confirm email, confirmed page, portal, now email, digest email). Every added step is `render: { kind: 'soon' }` — the tab's existing *Next release* card — with a note that names the surface and the PR that renders it.
- **[`customerSurfaceRegistry.ts`](../../src/lib/customerSurfaceRegistry.ts)** (+ test) — `CUSTOMER_SURFACES`: every public route in `App.tsx` and every edge function that sends through Resend, each with an audience and either its journey step(s) or an `exempt` reason (sign-in, invites, the office's own reports). `publicRoutesFromAppSource` parses the real route table (top-level routes not wrapped in `ProtectedRoute`); `resendSendersFromSources` reads the real functions folder (both shared helpers and direct calls). The test fails CI on: a route or sender with no entry, an entry naming a step that does not exist, an entry naming a route or function that is gone, an outside audience left exempt, and any `audience: 'customer'` row of the existing [`emailCatalog.ts`](../../src/lib/emailCatalog.ts) whose sender is not placed on a journey. Adding a public page or a customer email now fails the build until it has a place on the tab.
- **[`SettingsWhatCustomersSeeTab.tsx`](../../src/components/settings/SettingsWhatCustomersSeeTab.tsx)** — a count line under the toolbar: *N steps across 5 audiences · R rendered live · S next release · 2 sent by another system*, with one sentence on the guard.
- **`settingsGroups.ts`** — the tab's hint now reads "Every email and page anyone outside the company gets — customers, GCs, subs, supply houses and the collections firm".
- **Help guide** `see-what-customers-see.md` — the five strips, the count, and what a Next release card means.

Two reconciliations the registry forced: `submit-legal-portal` sends the firm's confirm email (the catalog knew; the first draft had it exempt), and `send-bid-pricing-package` sends through a second shared helper the grep had missed, so the GC strip gained the pricing package email.
