# v2.4265 — Lien calendar: the work tick, the grey past a dead lien, quieter rows, and the key as a strip (2026-09-30)

No migration, no edge function. Grace, on the live Calendar tab (2026-09-30): *"on the right it says 'send the notice · 15 d' but what is the 15 d — is that the deadline? and this sheet does not do a good job showing when the work was complete"*, then *"once something can no longer be liened it should be clear that there is nothing to be done after it — show the row grayed out past it"*, then, on the key, *"a shorter set of words, side by side with the words underneath, and if a user clicks on one they see a longer explanation"*. Mock-up: artifact `JD7N5fPjMPGurmEdXmDd5B` — Draft 1 (the first suggestions as a column and labels), Draft 2 (what shipped), the key strip closed and open.

## What changed

- **The runway names its basis** ([`lienPayRunway.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienPayRunway.ts), additive): `basisYmd` — the day every date was counted from (the last approved clock day, or the creation day when there are no hours; `''` with neither) — and `closedBy: 'notice' | 'lien' | null` on a closed row. The basis is computed before the filed-lien return, so a filed row carries it too. Two tests.
- **The work tick** ([`lienCalendar.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienCalendar.ts) `workMarkFor`, a new `work` mark): a grey tick at `basisYmd` with the date under it (*Aug 22*); hollow, amber and worded when the runway is dated from creation (*Aug · no hours (created)*); at the axis's left edge with a *◂* when the day is before it. It leads every row's marks. The axis already reached back to the lien-gone gutter, so most work days land on it; nothing about the scale changed.
- **A dead row goes grey past the day its lien died** (`closedYmdFor`, a new `gone` mark): the notice date when the notice was never sent (`closedBy: 'notice'`), else the lien date. The track stops there; from that day to the right edge the tab draws a flat wash (`var(--bg-subtle)`, a red left edge) carrying *notice not sent by Sep 15* / *lien window closed Aug 17* under the red flag and one grey line — *nothing left to file after this day — the money is still owed*. No ghost lien flag, no future marks. The money reads grey with *still owed* under it. **Fix riding along:** the closed flag and the axis gutter used `lienByYmd` even when the notice window was what shut — a notice-closed row drew its red flag on a lien date still ahead; both now use the day the lien actually died.
- **A job row says only what its GC row does not** (`rowWord`): the GC row's word gains the date — *send 11 notices by Oct 15 · 15 d* (`groupWord`) — and a job owing that same notice shows nothing under its money; a job on a later notice says *notice by Nov 16 · 47 d*; every other state keeps the runway's verdict; a dead row says *still owed*. The dashed *no pay date* dot is drawn on a job row only outside a GC group (`lienCalendarMarks(job, axis, { payMissingDot })`); under a GC its row's one dot speaks. The GC sub-line counts jobs that share one address — *· 4 at one property* (`sharedPropertyNote`) — the run's combine-into-one-notice rule (v2.3777) applies to them.
- **The bracket on a creation-dated row.** `commercialLienByFor` read the raw `lastWorkYmd`, so a "kind not set" job with no hours drew no striped bracket though its flag could sit a month later; it now reads `runway.basisYmd`.
- **The key as a strip** (`LIEN_CALENDAR_KEY` entries gain `short`, `long` and an optional `door`; three glyphs added — `work`, `work_hollow`, `gone`): `KeyStrip` in [`LienDeskCalendarTab.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskCalendarTab.tsx) draws thirteen buttons in one row, the glyph over two-to-four words (*Today · Last day worked · No approved hours · Notice owed · Notice on file · Lien deadline · Pay date they gave · No pay date yet · Room · File first · Kind not set · Jobs on one date · Lien gone*). A tap turns the mark dark (`aria-pressed`) and opens one panel under the row — the long words, and the board's door where the entry names one: *Set kinds, biggest first ›* opens the kinds sheet (office roles), *Draft the notices ›* runs the to-do's first draft action. Tap again, tap another, Esc or × closes it. The fold and its "open on the first three visits" `localStorage` rule are gone; the strip is always there. A row's mark keeps the old sentence (`label`) as its hover.
- **The cream tint is dropped.** A kind-assumed row was tinted amber with nothing in the key saying so; the bracket and the to-do's *Set kinds* line carry it.
- Kernel tests (`lienCalendar.test.ts`: the tick on- and off-axis and from creation, the notice-closed death day, the bracket from creation, the key's short/long/doors, `rowWord`, `sharedPropertyNote`; expectations for the new leading `work` mark and the group word's date), render smokes (the ticks and their words, the quiet rows, the wash, the strip's tap / second tap / Esc / door; the pen tests moved to a direct row, since under a GC the dashed dot is the GC's). Guide *send lien notices from the Lien desk* → Start on the Calendar, GLOSSARY *Lien calendar*, `LIEN_DESK_ARCHITECTURE.md`.

## Design calls

- **The work date goes where the board is already empty.** The first draft added a *Work ended* column, a month under each flag and *◂ Aug 22* at the track's edge — the same date three times, and a fifth column squeezing the name. Every row's past half was blank; the tick uses it, and the flag visibly comes from something.
- **Honesty where the flags come from.** The basis was only a hover; a wrong basis moves every flag on the row. It is now the first thing on the row, and a stand-in (the creation month) is said in amber, not implied.
- **Nothing is drawn after a lien dies.** The owner's ask was a row that reads "nothing more to do here"; a ghost lien flag or a muted future column would read as a to-do. Grey from the death day, one sentence, the money kept (the debt is real; only the lien is gone). The rows stay in the *Lien gone* group at the bottom — the GC row's word (*send 11 notices*) should count only what is still alive.
- **The GC row speaks once.** Twenty identical *send the notice · 15 d* lines were the redundancy Grace asked about; the date now sits on the GC row's word and a job says only what differs.
- **Sixty-four small date labels is still a lot of ink** — kept visible on purpose (the point is catching a wrong basis without hovering); a hover-only variant is the fallback if it reads busy live.
