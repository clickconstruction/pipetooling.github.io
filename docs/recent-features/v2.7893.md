# v2.7893 — Lien desk: the Board's two column headings are grey (2026-11-18)

The owner's pick of 2026-11-18, one of the last five pieces of the Board's drawing (punch list #383, option B) that a live look found still different. He saw each redrawn on the Board as it stands and picked all five; each is its own PR. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## Before

- *Monthly notices* was blue (`var(--text-blue-700)`) and *Lien affidavits* was red (`var(--text-red-700)`). On the Board red is the colour of a late day (a chip due within a day or past, the first tile's figure and line), so a red heading over the right column read as a late column. Blue is the colour of a live press and of a bin's *act here* edge, so the left heading read as a press.

## What changed

- **Both headings are the muted grey.** `column` in `LienBoard.tsx` draws each `h3` in `LIEN_BOARD_COLUMN_HEAD_COLOR` (`var(--text-muted)`), the token a folded bin's name carries. The size, the weight, the capitals and the spacing are as before, so a heading still reads apart from a bin's name.
- **Every layout.** The one `column` function draws both columns side by side on a computer and stacked on a phone, so the change holds for both, and the token follows the theme.

## What it leaves alone

- **The chips, the tiles and the bins.** No colour that carries a meaning moved: red, amber, violet and grey chips, the blue left edge of a bin with a live press, and the tiles' figures are as they were.
- **The list under the Board.** Its *Needs you now* heading is still red.

## Tests

- `LienBoard.headings.render.test.tsx`: both headings carry the muted token on a computer and on a phone, and a late chip under a grey heading is still red.

## Verified

- On the dev server against the live desk in headless Chrome, a computer (1440 px) and a phone (390 px), light and dark: both headings grey, the chips unchanged.
