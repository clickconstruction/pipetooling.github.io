# v2.3989 — Bids: the page's router is its own piece (2026-09-28)

Client only; no migration, no edge function, no screen change. Punch list **#46** row 5 (`to-dos/decomposition-queue.md`): the Bids map's step 7 (`docs/BIDS_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md` → Recommended extraction order).

## What is new

**`src/hooks/useBidsDeepLinks.ts`** — the Bids page's URL router and everything a link to a bid does, moved out of `src/pages/Bids.tsx` verbatim:

- the main router: `?tab=` to the open tab through the role gates, `?tab=…&bidId=` to the row, the card or the workflow tab the link names, `?new=true`;
- the four retries that run once a bid that was not in hand arrives (Bid Board, By status, By builder, Working);
- `?openBidEdit=1`;
- the three appliers, the By builder lens's jump from a status row, and `consumeBidIdParam`;
- the two rings (`useDeepLinkHighlight`) and the Working board's landing.

The effects run in the order they ran in the page: the hook is called where the router stood. The page hands in what the router moves — the tab, the shared bid, the By status selection, the two section-open states — and the two doors into the Bid window.

## What changed in `src/pages/Bids.tsx`

3,951 → 3,610 lines (measured on main, after v2.3982 landed in between). Behavior-preserving. Four `useCallback` dependency arrays gained setters that are now inputs (state setters and a ref — their identities never change). The two effects that read the Bid window's doors keep the dependency lists they had in the page.

## Left, on purpose

`?lostSummary`, `?newBid=`, `?robot=needs`, `?focus=` and `?envelope=` stay in the page: each opens something the page owns (the lost summary window, the Bid form, the robot layer's sheets).
