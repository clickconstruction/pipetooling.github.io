# v2.6853 — Labels: pick Paper sheet or Brother tape first, with the setup steps and a test strip (punch list #304) (2026-11-10)

The owner, 2026-11-10, with the Brother PT-D610BT and tape in hand: should the new Materials view make label printing more apparent and easy to find. The tape format has been in the Labels window since v2.6801, as a chip that read *Tape* beside *Sheet*; he did not know it was there. Its first-time steps were one sentence of grey text that showed only after the chip was pressed, with no word about the cable or the driver. Built from `to-dos/materials-one-story/tape-labels-before-after.html` on his yes.

## What changed

- **`LabelsPrintModal`**: the window's first choice is two named outputs, a radio group: **Paper sheet** (*One Letter page a box, with lines to write on. Any printer.*) and **Brother tape** (*One strip a box for the P-touch, for boxes that sit in the sun.*). The tape width sits under them when tape is the pick.
- **The first time**: tape picked on a computer that never sent tape to the print window lists three steps (the USB cable, Brother's driver, the printer and the paper in the print window) and links the guide's new section *Print a tape label*.
- **Print 1 test strip**: prints the first ticked box alone. The list holds that one strip while the print window is open and is whole again on the browser's `afterprint`.
- **None · Every box** beside the box chips, as the manifest window has.
- **A computer that printed tape opens on tape**: the choice folds to one line (*Brother tape · 24 mm. This computer printed tape last on Nov 10.*) with **Change** and **Printer setup**. `labelPrefs.ts` keeps the day in `localStorage` (`pt.labels.tapePrintedYmd`). It records that a print was asked for on this device; the app cannot see the printer.
- **`tapeLabel.ts`**: the outputs' words, the steps, the test strip's label, the ready line and the count line. `tapeHintWords` is gone with the grey sentence.
- The strip itself, its sizes and the `@page` rule are unchanged.

## Tests

`tapeLabel.test.ts` holds the words. `LabelsPrintModal.render.test.tsx`: the two outputs and the first-time steps with the guide link, the test strip printing one box and the list returning, a second open landing on tape with Change and Printer setup, None and Every box, and one box having neither.

## Not done

The tape format has still never been printed on the real printer; the walk on the PT-D610BT and the PT-E720BT stays on card #294. The app lays out 18 mm and 24 mm tape only.
