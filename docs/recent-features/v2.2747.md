# v2.2747 — The estimate email is a letter now, with a link that works (2026-09-04)

**Why.** The email a homeowner got from Estimates → Send to customer was the Settings body template escaped into HTML with `<br>`s: the accept URL was never wrapped in an `<a>`, so Outlook and Gmail showed a 90-character string to copy by hand; the subject read "…Estimate: Estimate for…"; nothing named the amount, the expiry or the estimate number; From was "ClickTooling" with a no-reply address. Owner picked direction **B — Letterhead** from three mockups (artifact e6f98354), the sibling of the bid-room email (v2.2729).

**What `send-estimate-to-customer` sends now.**
- **Subject** `Estimate #482 — Water heater replacement — $4,380 · Click Plumbing` (change orders: `Change order #482 — … · …`; company from the acceptance-page brand, *Click Plumbing and Electrical* when none). The `estimate_email_subject_template` setting and the per-estimate subject override are no longer read; the Settings form and the estimate's Email override section hide them.
- **Letterhead**: brand banner (when an acceptance-page logo is selected), the title as the heading (or *Your estimate* / *Your change order*), a meta line *Estimate #482 · for-address · send date* (company calendar day).
- **Your template, split**: the body template's first blank-line-separated paragraph opens the email; the remaining paragraphs sign it off below the button; a paragraph holding the accept link (or a stray `{{accept_url}}`) is dropped because the button replaces it. The live template needs no edit to work, though its first paragraph still ends "…with the following link:" — the owner can trim that in Settings.
- **Total box** *Estimate total $4,380.00* (change orders: *Net change to contract*) with *Pricing is good through Sep 18, 2026.* from `valid_until`; with 2+ options the box becomes the option table (*Our recommendation* / *Alternate*, the recommended row tinted), replacing v2.2460's flex-built list.
- **The button**: a table cell with `bgcolor` (survives dark-mode clients) linking the accept URL, plus a plain-URL fallback line; `color-scheme: light only` keeps the white paper.
- **Footer** from the acceptance-page footer setting (address, licence, regulator) joined on one line, plus *Reply to this email to reach <sender>*; **Reply-To** is the sending staff user's email; **From** keeps `EMAIL_FROM`'s verified address but the display name becomes the brand's company.
- `customer_experience_sent.emailSubject` now stores the subject that was actually sent.

**Staff side.** Estimates → Customer experience → **Email** renders the same builder in a sandboxed iframe (From/Reply-To/To/Subject above it, plain text below), for drafts (live title, total, expiry, options, brand) and for sent rows (the stored copy snapshot + the row's values, dated by `sent_at`).

**How.** Dependency-free builder `supabase/functions/_shared/estimateEmailLetterhead.ts`, re-exported by `src/lib/estimateEmailLetterhead.ts` for the app (one builder, no mirror — replaces `estimateEmailHtmlPreview.ts`) and unit-tested in `src/lib/estimateEmailLetterhead.test.ts` (subject variants, template split, link placement, options ladder, CO wording, sender/footer, escaping). `ESTIMATE_EXPERIENCE_SETTINGS_EDITABLE_KEYS` drops the subject key from the dev Settings form. Edge function deployed after merge.

**Not in this cut.** A per-send personal note (direction C) and line items in the email (direction C); a Reply-To that targets the office inbox rather than the sender (no setting for it yet).
