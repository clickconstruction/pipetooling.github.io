# v2.6306 — Find a file: the line where the words appear, with the words in bold (2026-11-02)

The owner's question on v2.6305's Find a file: are the matching words highlighted in a preview? They were not, because Drive's search API names the files that match but never the passage. Now `drive-link`'s `search` reads the first ten hits' text itself and the box draws the line around the words with the words bold. Client plus the function, no migration; stacked on v2.6305 (PR #6751) since both change `drive-link`.

- **The cut** (`supabase/functions/_shared/excerpt.ts`, pure, tested from `src/lib/documents/excerpt.test.ts`): `excerptOf(text, words)` folds whitespace, finds the earliest place from which every searched word occurs within 240 characters (else the first occurrence of any), cuts about 160 characters around it on word boundaries with an ellipsis on a cut side, and marks every occurrence of each word inside the cut (overlaps merged) as `{ text, marks: [start, end][] }`.
- **The read** (`fileText` in `drive-link/index.ts`): a Google Doc, Sheet or Slides is exported as `text/plain`; a PDF under 8 MB and 40 pages comes down `alt=media` and gives its text layer through `npm:unpdf@2.1.0` (a dynamic import inside the read, so a runtime that cannot load it costs an excerpt, never the function's boot); a `text/*`, `.csv`, `.md` or `.txt` file under 2 MB is read as text. Each read races a 3 s timer. A PDF whose text layer is under 20 characters is a scan. The hit carries `excerpt` or `excerptWhy` (`scan` | `kind` | `big` | `slow`); a read that fails costs its excerpt, never the search.
- **The line** (`FindAFile.tsx`): under the folder/day/size line, the excerpt with each marked span in `<mark>` on the amber token; a scan says *Matched inside the scan.*, a big file *Too big to read here.*, anything else draws no line. Kernel `excerptParts` and `excerptNote` in `src/lib/documents/findAFile.ts`.
- Honest limits: a scanned PDF or a photo has no text we can read without our own OCR, which stays out of scope (Drive's own OCR text is not exposed by its API); Drive may match a file on words outside its body (its name, a comment), and that hit then shows no line.
- Tests: `excerpt.test.ts` (tokens, the cut and its boundaries, start and end, the together-window, overlapping marks, nulls), `findAFile.test.ts` (the parse of excerpts and whys, the parts, the notes), `FindAFile.render.test.tsx` (bold spans, the scan note, nothing for a plain hit). Guide *find a file when you do not know where it is* says which files show the line; `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`'s `search` bullet names the read. **Redeploy `drive-link` once this merges.**
