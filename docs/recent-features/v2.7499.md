# v2.7499 — Plans by date: every item opens a card with its details and its presses (2026-11-16)

Punch list #367, PR 2. The owner's ask of 2026-11-16, while the By date view was on the screen: everything about to be built should react, so that resting the pointer on any item shows more details and the ability to act on it. No migration, no edge function.

## What answers

Every item of the view: a mark, a bar, a burst of sets, a lane's name, today's pill, each of the four figures and each line of the dated list. The one-line hover of v2.7494 is gone; the card replaces it.

## How the card comes and goes

- **With a pointer** the card opens after a beat (140 ms; 380 ms on a line of the list, so a pointer reading down the list does not raise one on every line) and stays while the pointer is on the item or on the card, so its presses can be reached. A press on the item, or Enter, pins it: then it stays until Escape, its ×, or a press anywhere else.
- **On a narrow screen** nothing hovers. A press opens the same card as a sheet from the bottom, with a backdrop.
- **Where it stands**: above the item when there is room, else below, always inside the window (`placeItemCard`). A line of the list keeps its words in view: its card stands at the line's right end. The card follows its item when the page scrolls.
- **A row of a card leads to another card**, with **‹ Back** to return: from a figure to the sets it counts, from a crew bar to its jobs, from a part's name to its sets.
- **The item with an open card stands out twice**, a ring on the chart and a bar at the left of its line in the list, so the two read as one thing.

It is a card beside the item, not a window over the page. Unlike the job number's card (`JobChip.tsx`), which takes no pointer events because it only reads, this one is pressed.

## What each card says and offers

- **A set**: the date on its sheets, the day it came, what it replaces, who added it, its memo and what changed. **Open ↗** (its link), **Build from this set** (never on the set already built from, or on one of ours), **See it in By part**, **Ask the estimators**.
- **A bid**: sent, the plans it was priced from, how it ended, the sets since it was sent as rows. **Open the bid**.
- **A crew bar**: its dates and days, each job on it with its own days, most days first. A job's card has **Open the job**.
- **A History line**: its kind, its day, who kept it, what it is about and its words. **Read the whole line** opens the page's line window.
- **A part's name**: the set it builds from, the jobs on it, its sets newest first. **+ New set** opens the parts table's own form on that part. **See it in By part**.
- **A figure**: what the number counts, as rows.
- **Today**: the newest set, the last day on site, and **Write a line**, which opens History's composer.

A card lists eight rows and counts the rest. What a reader may not do is not offered: an estimator's job card says *A job opens for the office and Dispatch.* and draws no dead press.

## The acts are the page's own

The card only reaches them (`actOnPlansByDate` in `DevelopmentPage.tsx`): `openBid`, `openJob`, the Ask sheet, the History composer and line window, the parts table (which now takes `startSetOn` to open its new-set form on a part), and `siteWrites.buildFrom`. **Build from this set** asks first in the confirm window, with the button naming the set; in the parts table the same press writes at once, as before.

## How it is built

- `src/lib/plans/plansByDateCards.ts`, pure: `buildPlansByDateCards(input, model, caps)` gives a card for every key the view draws; `plansByDateClusterCard` for a burst bar.
- `src/hooks/usePlansByDateItemCard.ts`: one card for the whole view, its timers, its pin, its trail and `placeItemCard`.
- `src/components/developments/PlansByDateItemCard.tsx`: the box and the sheet.
- `layoutPlansByDate` now also says where a lane's name and today's pill are (`nameHits`), and which marks a burst bar stands for (`members`). `crewRuns` keeps each job's own days on a bar.
- `readDoor.ts` reads `first_work_date` off the estimator's overview once the database hands it over (v2.7496's migration).

## Verify after deploy

Looked at with made-up rows at 375 and 1280 px, light and dark: a hover, a pin, the lane, figure and list cards, the sheet on a phone. The live page was not walked. Open a development, press **By date**, rest the pointer on a set, press **Build from this set** on an older one and cancel the confirm, then press a part's name and **+ New set**.
