# v2.5617 — Intro to electrical: the two trades under conditions, seven strips (2026-10-18)

Punch list #120, PR 3 of five (PR 2 v2.5616 is The devices). Part 3 of `src/content/training/intro-electrical/`: lessons 19 to 25, each drawn as a strip of three or four frames the way the OSHA lift and the plumbing flush are, `course.md` at 25 lessons and 140 minutes with *3. The two together* in its parts, and seven more bank questions (thirty-six in all). No migration; no edge function.

- **The lessons**: 19 A tripped breaker (the middle, off then on, once, a second trip is a call, a warm breaker is never reset); 20 A GFCI trip at a sink (the cause first, Reset once, the downstream outlets, a tool that trips every GFCI is tagged out); 21 Water on a panel (do not touch, the water off somewhere else, the utility kills it at the meter, the pan and its drain, the electrician reads a wet panel); 22 A wet floor and a cord (the path across the chest; the numbers verbatim from the OSHA refresher's lesson 9, 1, 5, 30, 50 to 150, 200 and 15,000 mA, with `current-scale` drawn again by name under its own caption; a GFCI on every cord; a frayed cord leaves the job); 23 A cut in the bonded pipe (lesson 8 on the day it goes wrong: the jumper on and the cut opens nothing, the jumper off and the person holding both ends is the path; a temporary jumper is the electrician's call); 24 A water heater with no power (the breaker first, once; **the company's rule** from the owner: off and tested dead before any cover, the high-limit pressed once, the elements and the thermostats the plumber's dead, the panel and everything upstream of the disconnect the electrician's); 25 A generator or a solar backfeed (the transfer switch, the backfeed to the lineman, solar whenever the sun is up, the battery in the dark, never assume dead, the sticker at the meter).
- **The strips**: `tripped-breaker-strip` (four: in the middle, off, on and holding, again), `gfci-trip-strip` (four: damp, it trips, the cause, Reset), `water-on-panel-strip` (four: a leak, live, water off somewhere else, call), `wet-floor-strip` (four: a cord, the frame live, with a GFCI, without one and the person is the path), `cut-pipe-strip` (three: at rest, cut with the jumper on, cut with no jumper), `water-heater-no-power-strip` (four: cold with the breaker on, off and tested dead, press once, again and call), `backfeed-strip` (three: the utility off, the backfeed out to the street, live from the other end). The house red marks current where it should not be and nothing else; the green verdict marks the frame that is the right move.
