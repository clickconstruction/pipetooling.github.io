# v2.5689 — Pricing: the strip says when labor hours follow other counts (2026-10-20)

Punch list #137 PR 2 (owner's call 4, 2026-10-20). Labor rows (`cost_estimate_labor_rows`) are
bid-wide, keyed by fixture name, and re-synced to the ACTIVE version's counts only when the
Labor or Takeoffs tab loads (`syncCostEstimateLaborRows`, gated in `Bids.tsx`). A side-by-side
rebid — two versions, different counts — therefore shows a margin whose labor cost follows
whichever version last opened Labor, silently.

- **`laborCountsOutOfStep`** (`src/lib/bids/laborCountsDrift.ts`): the detector is
  `planLaborSync` itself with nothing parked — after any Labor visit the plan is empty by
  construction, so a non-empty plan (renames, parks, mints, count moves, take-backs) means
  exactly "Labor has not seen these counts". No labor rows at all is the setup story, not
  drift, and stays quiet.
- One amber line under the Workbench's totals (beside `PricedMarginLine`): *"Labor hours don't
  match this version's counts, so our cost and the margin may be off"*, with **Open Labor**
  (`onNavigateBidToTab`) as the door. Opening Labor runs the sync; returning to Pricing
  reloads `pricingLaborRows` fresh (the tab-exit effect clears them), so the line clears on
  its own. No write happens from Pricing.
- Shows on any bid whose labor is out of step — a single-version bid whose counts moved since
  Labor last opened earns the same honesty.

Tests: `laborCountsDrift.test.ts` (in-step, count move, new fixture, uncounted labor row,
alternate-group sums, the two quiet cases). No migration.
