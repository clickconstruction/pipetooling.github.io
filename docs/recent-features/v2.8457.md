# v2.8457 — Bills sent: the tiles fill their rows on a phone (2026-11-24)

One fault of the Bills sent page found on the live test of 2026-11-24 (punch list #422, problem 12, its first half). No migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

At 375 px the list can show five tiles. An odd count was drawn in a grid that fits three to a row, so five sat three and two with a hole in the second row.

## What changed

- `tileRow(phone, count)` in `billsSentLook.ts`, new and pure, is the row of tiles over either view. A desk is as it was. On a phone an even count still sits two to a row. An odd count now wraps, and the tiles of the last row grow to fill it.
- A tile carries the width it grows from (`flex: 1 1 5.2rem`), which only a wrapping row reads.
- Both views draw their tiles through it, so By day paid's three tiles and its one tile follow the same rule.

## Measured

A page of five such tiles in headless Chromium at 375 px, 20 px of gutter each side: the first row holds three tiles 107 px wide, the second two tiles 164 px wide that end at the right edge. Three tiles fill one row. One tile is 335 px wide. Nothing runs past the edge.

## Left

The rows can still differ in height: three narrow tiles stack their amounts and stand taller than two wide ones. Tiles of one row are one height.

## Tests

`billsSentLook.test.ts`: the desk's row, the even count, the odd count and the tile's width. `BillsSentLedger.render.test.tsx`: two tiles on a phone sit in two columns, three wrap. The look cases failed before the change.

## Not looked at

Not drawn inside the app. The measure is of the same styles on a bare page.
