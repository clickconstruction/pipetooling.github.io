# v2.5082 — Lien desk: Approve ▸ is now Sign and approve ▸, one press puts the leader’s name on the notice (2026-10-09)

Lien desk signing, PR 2 of 3 (PR 1 is v2.5077, the columns and the papers; PR 3 holds the unsigned at the run). The owner's ask, 2026-10-09: *for each signature, one button and the leader's name shows up in cursive instead of wet signing, because it's easier to approve.* The research behind it is on the board https://claude.ai/artifact/G1zdfx33BchsUV3w5QidjE and in v2.5077's fragment: the strength is in the record (his own sign-in, his own press, bound to this draft), not in the mark. Counsel is comfortable.

## What changed

- **[`LienNoticeSignLine`](../../src/components/jobs/LienNoticeSignLine.tsx):** the notice's own signature line, drawn where it prints as the release's foot is (v2.4335): his name waiting on the line in the cursive face, grey until the press; the contact person and the company under the rule; the day beside them. *Draw instead* turns the line into a `signature_pad` canvas; *Press instead* turns it back. `allowPress={false}` (someone else's screen, PR 3's Leader here) offers the pad alone. The handle gives the press what the line holds.
- **[`lienDeskSignIo.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDeskSignIo.ts) `signLienDeskItem`:** the one write. A pressed signature stores no file; a drawn one's PNG is trimmed and stored best-effort under `desk/<item>/` in the release bucket. The row gets the seven columns with `signed_fields_hash` of the draft as saved, plus `status: 'approved', approval_mode: 'leader'` when `approve` is on. At someone else's screen only a drawing is taken, and the row names whose screen. The guard refuses anything that is not the leader's own act.
- **The desk footer (`LienDeskModal`):** the line sits above the buttons wherever the leader approves — the draft-state footer, the awaiting footer on the computer and the phone card, the held footer — and **Approve ▸ / Approve & next ▸ / Release the hold and approve ▸** read **Sign and approve ▸ / Sign and approve & next ▸ / Release the hold, sign and approve ▸**. The press reads the line (`signPayloadOrThrow`), sets the offer, and calls `signLienDeskItem` with the draft the row holds (`item.fields`, or `draftFields()` right after `ensureDraft`). The pane's own paper (`docHtml`, the cover letter, the preview) prints the signature once the row carries it (`paneSignature`), and the Ready chip reads *signed Oct 9* or *unsigned* (`readyChip`'s sixth argument, `lienChipSigned`).
- **Sign only:** a notice approved on his word reaches Ready unsigned; for the leader the Ready footer shows the line and **Sign ▸** (`signOnly`, `approve: false`).
- **The mark belongs to the draft as signed:** `saveLienDeskDraft`'s update, `pullBackLienDeskItem` and `undoLienDeskApprovals` spread `LIEN_DESK_SIGNATURE_CLEAR`. Take back keeps it, since the approval stands.
- **Put a GC on notice:** *Approve all N and send the run ▸* is **Sign and approve all N and send the run ▸**; `approveAll('leader')` signs each notice with `{ mode: 'type' }` after its draft is saved, so every notice in the batch is its own record. **The retainage pane** signs the same way (`ensureDraft` first, then the press). The affidavit pane is untouched.
- **The ink on the papers:** `useLienDeskSignatureInks` (over [`lienDeskSignatureInk.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDeskSignatureInk.ts), the release's reader for the desk) hands the drawn PNGs to `buildLienDeskRun` / `buildLienRetainageRun` as `opts.inks`, with `leaderNameFor` as `onDeviceNameFor`; the desk and Put a GC on notice both pass them.
