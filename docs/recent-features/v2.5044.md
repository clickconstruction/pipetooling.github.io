# v2.5044 — GC mode: a trade partner’s portal reads its own work, and can answer a charge or ask for a change (2026-10-09)

P4b-i of the trade partner portal (`to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/portal-p4.md` on branch `spike/gc-mode`; P4b split in three, approved 2026-10-09). No migration. `gc-trade-portal` and `submit-gc-trade-portal` are redeployed after it merges; the page ships first and reads an older slice as no work. P4b-ii brings the two blocks and their presses, and P4b-iii the emails and the Needs-you kernels.

- **The slice** (`_shared/gcTradePortalSlice.ts`) carries the company's own work, each field named in `TRADE_PORTAL_FIELDS`:
  - its statements of work (never a cancelled one), the charges to it (never who made or settled them) and the changes it asked for;
  - of a change order a request became, only its part: the number, the status, the days sent and answered, and the cost;
  - the award of each trade it was asked to quote: its own ask, or `elsewhere` when another company holds it (`AWARDED_ELSEWHERE`), so the portal reads that ask as lost and never learns who won.
- **The never-sees test** plants a change order's price to the customer, its percent done and words, another company's award, statement of work, charge, request and change order, a cancelled statement of work, and who made and settled a charge. Five mutants of the builder each fail it.
- **The mapper** (`tradePortalState.ts`) fills the award, the statement of work with its charges, the requests and the change orders (price 0, no words), and reads a slice without the four lists as no work.
- **The presses**: `submit-gc-trade-portal` takes `answer_back_charge` and `ask_change` (P4a's verbs); `ask_change` is a free-text write under the hourly cap. The keys `alreadyAnswered`, `notAwarded`, `noteNeeded` and `descriptionNeeded` have their statuses and their words in English and Spanish, and leave the guard's `WAITING` list.
- **The sample** gains a job of ours: the award, a signed statement of work, an open charge with a photo link, and a change with the customer at the company's part.
- **Docs**: `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` for both functions, `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`'s trade portal section.
