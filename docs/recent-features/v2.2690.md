# v2.2690 — Contract Desk PR 5: automatic reminders and the portal's "Your agreements" (2026-09-03)

The last phase of the Contract Desk build (v2.2679 / v2.2681 / v2.2684 / v2.2685).

## DB (migration `20260903154024` — fragment in docs/migrations/)

- pg_cron job `job-contract-reminders` (hourly at :23) → `net.http_post` to `remind-job-contracts` with `X-Cron-Secret` (vault `PROJECT_URL` / `CRON_SECRET`, the crew-day precedent). Kill switch `app_settings` `job_contract_reminders_disabled_v1 = '1'`.

## Edge functions (deploy after merge)

- **`remind-job-contracts`** (new; see `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`): drains `sent` contracts with reminders on and `next_reminder_at` due; emails the durable link (reply-to = the contract's creator, CC honored, the third reminder says it is the last); advances `reminder_count` / `next_reminder_at` (+3 days, null after 3); logs a `reminded` event; rows with no token / bad email / expired link are taken off the lane; an email failure retries next hour. `send-job-contract` already seeds `next_reminder_at` when the modal's Reminders box is ticked (the default).
- **`customer-portal`**: payload gains `agreements[]` (sent + signed contracts across the customer's jobs, with the durable `signUrl`).

## Client

- **Portal** (`/portal`, `/p/<slug>`): a **Your agreements** card under the ledger — each contract with job, address, terms, amount, then *Waiting for your signature* → **Review & sign** or *✍ Signed by … · date* → **View signed copy**. `portalPayload.ts` parses `agreements` (drops unknown statuses and non-http links; +2 tests).
- Contract modal: the Reminders checkbox copy drops its "later release" note.
- Help guide gains "Reminders and the customer's account page".
