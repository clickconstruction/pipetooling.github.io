# v2.6785 — Research: the Plumber column names a plumber only when the permit does (punch list #292, PR 3) (2026-11-09)

Found on the first live look at the Already have a plumber lens, minutes after PR 2 (v2.6784) landed: the Plumber column read the GC's or the expediter's name on most commercial rows, and Who wins what led with a permit expediter at 23 jobs. In San Antonio's feed a commercial job's trade permits share the building permit's number and, usually, its primary contact, so the plumbing row's contact is the applicant, not the plumber.

## What changed

- **Migration `20261109100000_permit_research_plumber_named.sql`**: `research_permits` replaced in place (same shape). The pairing still marks a row taken when any plumbing permit sits on the number or the address within the job's window (`plumber_permit_on`), but `plumber` is now the contact of a plumbing permit whose name key differs from the building permit's own applicant, a trade-pulled MEP number first; null when every plumbing row was pulled under the applicant's name.
- **`src/lib/research/permitResearch.ts`**: `plumbingOnFile`, `rowLens` and `rowHasPlumber` read the day as well as the name, so an unnamed plumbing permit still puts the row on the taken lens; `plumberWords` gives the cell its three states (named · on file, no plumber named · none yet). `plumberTally` already counted names alone.
- **The tab, the map card and the Decide sheet** draw the three states; the Who wins what strip says what it counts.

Merge, `supabase db push`. Nothing to deploy. The types regen that follows picks up `research_contact_history` and `research_name_key` from PR 2 as well, which the regen in #7446 predated.
