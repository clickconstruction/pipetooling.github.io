## What shipped

- **Kernel** [`lib/jobs/billedDatesLedger.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/billedDatesLedger.ts) — `buildBilledDatesLedger({ todayYmd, row, data, promise, runway, inCollections })` → `{ rows, track, roomLine, full }`. It arranges what two kernels already decide: the money from `billedExpectedPayModel` (the estimate without the promise, then with it), the deadlines from `buildLienPayRunway` (unchanged — the phone chip, the Lien calendar and the Dashboard still read the runway). Rows, oldest first, each with a marker number, a label, a date, *how far from today*, a tone, `bold` for the action row and an `action` (`they-said` / `new-date` / `lien-desk`): ① *Billed*; ② *Expected* or *They said* (a promise **replaces** the estimate row, the estimate becomes the sub-line, so the block is the same height in every state); ③ *Send the notice by …* on a sub job with no § 53.056 notice recorded (bold; red inside a week) or *Notice sent for …* once recorded; ④ *Lien by …*, bold as *File the lien by …* on `file_first` or a `no_pay` window inside three weeks. `closed` → one red *Lien gone* row, `filed` → one green *Lien filed* row, neither with a marker. The track starts at the bill date (today on a shell row), ends a little past the farthest point, carries `todayPct` (the grey fill) and the runway's `gap` (green room ② → ④, red *short* ④ → ②). `roomLine` = *N d of room after they pay* in the `room` state. 6 tests.
- **Component** [`BilledDatesLedger.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/BilledDatesLedger.tsx) — the track (badges numbered to match, filled when done or when the row is the action; a ▲ and *today*; the green or hatched run) over a three-column grid (badge · words · far). The money row opens `onMoney` (They said… / New date…), every deadline row and the room line open `onLienDesk`; `evidence` (the reliability line) draws under the money row; without handlers nothing is a button. 3 render tests.
- **Wiring** (`JobsStagesTab`): `billedLienRunwayRenderer` now builds the ledger and renders it in the runway's slot (under *Left on Job* and the footnote, in the table rows and the invoice-bearing card); `billedBillLineRenderer` returns `{ words: null, hideWords: true, extras }` — only the bank-returned ⚠ and a shell row's *No bill line* / *In Collections* pill (with its own They said…, since a shell has no money row) stay by the buttons. `hideWords` threads `BilledRowBillLine` → the cell → `StagesStageBar`, which then prints nothing under the bar; `WordsOverride` moved to `StagesStageBar.tsx`.
- **Gone**: `LienPayRunway.tsx` (+ render test; the runway kernel and its tests stay), `BilledWordsLine.tsx` and `lib/jobs/billedWordsLine.ts` (+ tests) — v2.4130's words line, superseded.
- **Guides**: *read the Pipeline money view* (the runway section is now the block, row by row), *see when a customer will pay* (Reading the Expected row), *know whether a customer keeps their word*, *run your GC statement round*; GLOSSARY *Bill dates ledger (Pipeline) — was the lien runway*.
