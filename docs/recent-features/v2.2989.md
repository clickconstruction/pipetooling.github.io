# v2.2989 — Test-gap audit, pass 13: the Stages reference dates get their first tests (2026-09-07)

Follow-up to v2.2985, which named this kernel next. Tests only; no kernel, client, migration or edge-function change.

## Why this one

`stagesJobReferenceDates.ts` (134 lines) decides the two dates every Stages line and the Job Detail middle row lean on: the field date `j:` (the later of the last approved clock-session work date and the last scheduled work date, with a tooltip saying which source it came from) and the billing date `b:` (the latest invoice sent, invoice billed, or payment recorded, with a tooltip naming what happened that day). It also folds schedule block rows into a per-job "latest work date" map for the Stages fetch. Five call sites, no test.

## What the tests found

Nothing wrong. Two contracts worth pinning that were only implied:

- A DB timestamp keeps its own calendar date — the first ten characters — whatever the zone suffix says. It is not shifted to the browser's zone. That matches the Stages fetch, which stores calendar days, and it is why `j:` cannot drift by a day at night.
- A same-day tie between an invoice sent, an invoice billed and a payment lists every distinct label once in the tooltip, in that order: "Latest: Invoice sent · Invoice billed · Payment recorded (date)".
