# v2.8288 — The new phone layout: Jobs and People keep their tabs on one row you can slide (2026-11-22)

A third PR behind the **New phone layout** switch of v2.8285. No migration, no edge function.

## The rule it is held to

With the switch off, nothing changes for anyone. With it on, every tab, pill and press that exists today is still on the screen.

## What an audit of the live site found, at 393 × 852

- **Jobs.** The row of tabs was 1,024 px wide in a 306 px window, and the page's title took the right of the same row. The open tab could sit half past the edge, and nothing said more tabs were there.
- **People.** The same row, with the title, for its sections. A dev has six sections, and three sat past the edge unseen. Then seven pills folded onto two rows.
- With the switch on the top bar already names the page (`phoneBarPageName`), so a title inside the page said it twice.

## What the switch changes on the two pages

- **Jobs.** The tabs are one line in `ScrollableTabStrip`, the app's own scrolling row, across the whole width. The title is not drawn.
- **People.** The same for its sections. Its pills are one line in a second `ScrollableTabStrip` where they were two folded rows. Search and the filter chips are as they were.
- **The open tab is whole in view** when the page opens, when the tab changes, when the row's tabs land late and when the screen changes width.

Nothing else moves. Every tab and pill keeps its words, its order, its press and its place in the keyboard order, and a role sees the tabs it saw.

### The title, and the role dock

The title leaves only while the top bar is saying the same word. Layout is the one place that knows which bar it drew, so it hands the answer down in `PhoneBarPageNameContext`, and `phoneTabRows` (`src/lib/phoneLayout/phoneTabRows.ts`) reads it.

An assistant or a controller in Dispatch Mode has the role dock, whose row keeps its icons and names no page. There the title stays at the right of the row, as it was, and the tabs beside it are still the one scrolling line.

While it is undrawn the title is still an `h1` a screen reader reads, hidden the way the app hides a label it still says.

## What the pictures changed

Each of these was built, looked at on a phone's screen at 393 × 852 on live data, and changed because of what the picture showed.

1. **The open tab sat under the fade.** `ScrollableTabStrip` brings the open tab into view with the browser's `scrollIntoView`, flush against the edge. With Materials open the filled tab's right end washed out into the fade and nothing of Job Summary showed. People's *Who's where* pill did the same. Now the strip takes `activePeek`: the row alone is moved, through the app's own `scrollLeftToShow`, so the open tab is whole and the start of the next tab shows beside it. The peek is 56 px for a tab and 44 px for a pill, past the padding, so letters show and not a blank.
2. **The fade fell on a blank and said nothing.** As the Sample assistant the Jobs row ended on *Billing* with five tabs past it. The edge fell in the padding between two tabs, so the fade, which is the row's own colour, was invisible. At other widths the same happens to other roles. Now the strip takes `edgeMarks`: a small arrow in each fade, at any edge with more past it. It is drawn, not pressed, so a touch goes through it to the tab under it.
3. **The arrow sat on top of a cut word.** On People it was drawn over the *w* of *Who's where*. A fade that carries an arrow is now 32 px wide and the row's own colour over its last 40%, so a cut word ends before the arrow.
4. **An arrow pointed at nothing.** At 375 px the Jobs row moves 10 px to show the tab after Pipeline. That hid only padding of the first tab, and a left arrow appeared. An edge now says nothing until more than a tab's padding is hidden (`fadeSlack`, 24 px for a tab and 12 px for a pill).
5. **A pill was 27 px tall.** One row of pills is also a row to catch with a thumb and slide. On the one-line row each pill is drawn as it was inside a press 44 px tall, the height of a press of the top bar.

### An arrangement tried and not kept

A pill row that starts on the open pill, so the open pill is always first. With Spending open it hides Users, Subs and Person, and the pill a person has just pressed jumps to the left edge from under the thumb. The row that moves as little as it can keeps the first pills in sight and leaves the pressed pill where it was pressed. The first was kept.

The three sections and the pills as one row was weighed and not built: a dev has six sections and seven pills, which is thirteen presses on one line with the open pill far from its section.

Measured in the browser pane on live data. The figure is the top of the first job on Jobs → Pipeline and of the first person on People → Users. A sample account's figures carry the 24 px View-as line.

| Who | First job, off | First job, on | First person, off | First person, on |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dev | 526 px | 457 px | 386 px | 286 px |
| Sample leader | 413 px | 344 px | 410 px | 310 px |
| Sample assistant, on the role dock | 447 px | 375 px | 444 px | 341 px |

The bars of v2.8285 account for 69 px of each (72 px on the role dock). The rest is this PR: nothing on Jobs, where the title shared the tabs' row, and 31 px on People, where two folded rows of pills became one.

On Jobs the gain is sideways. The tabs' window is 377 px wide where it was 306 px, and it says when there is more.

At 375 × 812 with the switch on, `innerWidth` and `scrollWidth` were both 375 on Jobs and on People, for the dev, the Sample leader and the Sample assistant.

## How it is built

- `src/lib/jobs/jobsPageTabs.ts` (new) is the Jobs row: the tabs a pair of roles has, in order, with their words and the two bars. The page decided this inside its markup before, one condition a button. One list now draws the row off and on the switch.
- `src/components/jobs/JobsPageTabStrip.tsx` and `src/components/people/PeoplePageTabRows.tsx` (new) draw the rows both ways. `Jobs.tsx` and `People.tsx` lose the markup and gain the two hooks.
- `src/components/ScrollableTabStrip.tsx` gains `activePeek` and `edgeMarks`. Both are off unless asked for, so Bids and the job's notes draw what they drew.
- `src/contexts/PhoneBarPageNameContext.tsx` (new), provided by `Layout.tsx` around the page.
- `src/lib/phoneLayout/phoneTabRows.ts` (new) holds the rule for the title and the sizes.

## What holds "nothing changes with the switch off"

- `JobsPageTabStrip.render.test.tsx`: off, the row's markup is the markup `Jobs.tsx` drew at v2.8285, character for character, for ten roles, eleven open tabs and both widths (220 draws). The old markup is kept whole in the test.
- `PeoplePageTabRows.render.test.tsx`: the same for People, for three sets of sections and every open view (40 draws).
- `jobsPageTabs.test.ts`: the kernel answers the row the page's old conditions drew for every pair of twelve role values (144 pairs).
- `ScrollableTabStrip.render.test.tsx` (new, the strip had no test): without the two new props the strip calls `scrollIntoView` as before, draws the plain 28 px fade and no arrow.
- `Jobs.newPhoneLayout.render.test.tsx` and `People.newPhoneLayout.render.test.tsx`: at a desk with the tick stored, and on a phone with it off, the pages draw the old rows and their title.
- `Layout.phoneBarPageName.render.test.tsx`: for nine roles in three modes, the page is told the bar names it exactly when the bar draws the name, and never with the switch off or at a desk.

With the switch on, the same files hold that every tab and pill of every role keeps its words, order, lit one and press, that the presses are the rows' only keyboard stops in the rows' order, and that the title is undrawn only while the bar names the page.

## Walked

On `localhost` against live data, signed in as a dev and through View as, read only. No press that writes was pressed. The switch and the theme were set in this browser's own storage and taken off after.

- Jobs and People as the dev, the Sample leader and the Sample assistant, at 393 × 852 with the switch off and on, light and dark, and at 375 × 812 on.
- Jobs with Materials open and with Inspections open, the last tab of the row.
- A desk at 1280 px with the tick stored: the old rows with their titles, no scrolling line, no arrow.

The pictures were taken with the page's transitions switched off for the picture. The pane was hidden, a hidden page does not advance a transition, and a tab's 0.12 s fill was caught half way.

Not seen live, held by tests alone: the Sample superintendent's two tabs, a controller, an estimator and a primary. An iPhone's own momentum scroll and its Home Screen inset were not seen, since the pane has neither.
