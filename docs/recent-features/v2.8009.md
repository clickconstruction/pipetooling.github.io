# v2.8009 — Document builder: send a job's insurance sheet to its trades (2026-11-19)

Punch list #404, step 5 of ten: the send. One new edge function, `document-builder-send`, and a change to `gc-trade-portal`. No migration: `document_builder_sends` came in v2.7988 and is pushed.

**Deploy**, from `main` after the merge: `document-builder-send`, `gc-trade-portal` and `document-builder-link` (its code now comes from a shared file). A trade's page reads no sheets until `gc-trade-portal` is up, and draws nothing new until then.

## What changed

- A job's sheet shows a card, **Send the sheet to this job's trades**: one row a trade company asked to quote on the job (`gc_trade_packages` → `gc_invites` → `gc_companies`), with where the send stands.
- A row's chips say *Sent Nov 20 to* a name, *Not opened yet* or *Opened Nov 21*, and *Changed since they opened it* once a newer revision is published.
- **Send it to them** on a row opens a box: who at the company it goes to (the people on the company record with an email), a note, and the press **Send to** a first name. The second press on a sent row is **Send it again**, or **Tell them it changed**.
- The one press says its count, *Send to 3 trades who are ready*. It acts on every trade not yet sent to whose page is on. The rest are listed under it, each with its reason, and one that fails is named afterwards while the others still go.
- **A send never turns a page back on.** A trade whose page was turned off on purpose is refused, by the function itself and not only by the screen. **Turn their page on** is its own press.
- **A trade that never had a page** gets one only from a send to that trade alone, and the box says so before the press. The one press holds them.
- **An unchecked revision** goes only after the box **Send it anyway** is ticked. The warning is the insurance agent's read mark: *Not checked by your insurance agent*.
- **Take it off their page** ends a live send. The sheet leaves the trade's page, and the row reads as not sent.
- The email is the trade email's own frame (`buildGcTradeEmail`) with one press to the trade's page. Its lines carry the note and the sheet's own link for their insurance agent.
- Each send leaves a line on the company's thread (`gc_trade_messages`, kind `coi`) and a sent copy of kind `insurance_sheet_email`, written only after the email went.

## The trade's page

`gc-trade-portal`'s answer gains `sheets`: each live send to this company, with the sheet as published, its revision, the job, the names it must list and the sheet's link code. The page draws them on its papers, after the certificate form (`TradePageSheets`):

- **What your certificate must show**, a card a job, with *It must name* the names, **Open the sheet**, **Send it to my insurance agent** (a `mailto:` with the sheet's link) and **Open it on its own page to print**.
- *New* until they open it, and *Changed since you opened it* after a newer revision.
- Opening a sheet posts `opened` to `document-builder-send` with the page's own token. The day and the revision are kept on the send. A signed-in teammate's look and a preview are not counted.
- The words are English and Spanish in a file of their own (`tradePageSheets.ts`), kept out of the files the edge copy generates from.

## The order the function checks in

The caller (a dev, never an archived account, a training account or a twin). The sheet, which must be published. The read mark. The company, which must be on a trade of this job. The person, who must be on the company's record with that email. The company's page: on is used, none makes one, off refuses. Then the sheet's own link, made when the sheet never had one. Then the email. Only after it went: the send's row, the thread's line and the sent copy.

`{ test: true }` sends the same email to the caller's own address and writes nothing. No screen asks for it yet.
