# v2.5050 — GC mode: the presses behind RFIs and the change order they start (2026-10-09)

U5a of the Building lane's real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/building-u5.md`, on branch `spike/gc-mode`): the RFIs' presses and the change order a cost answer starts, in `supabase/migrations/20261010012000_gc_rfi_writes.sql` (`docs/migrations/20261010012000_gc_rfi_writes.md`). No client code: the RFIs window (U5b) calls them once the types PR after the push has them.

- **`gc_rfis.change_order_id`**: the change order a cost answer started, set null when its draft is deleted, one RFI per change order, only on a cost answer.
- **`gc_add_rfi(r jsonb)`**: an RFI on a job that is ours, numbered one up from the job's last, with its sheets, its trade or our own work, the company that asked by phone (the one awarded the trade), the scope lines it holds and the days before that work it is needed.
- **`gc_send_rfi_to_architect(id, email)`** and **`gc_answer_rfi(id, a)`**: sent today, by email or another way; the answer by the architect (only once sent) or us, with what it changes, and a cost or days when it adds cost.
- **`gc_rfi_change_order(id, words, price)`**: the money team drafts a change order from a cost answer through Owner Billing's own `gc_draft_change_order`, with the client's words and price, and the RFI keeps the link. An answer that adds days only points at the schedule's Ask for the days.
- **`gc_trade_rfi_ask(company, trade, question, sheets)`**: the trade's question from its portal, the service role's only, holding its next unfinished work. A new key, `jobNotBuilding`, waits in `gcTradeSubmit.test.ts`'s WAITING map for the Portal's P5.
- **They refuse**, in words: a training account and a digital twin; a job bidding, lost or closed; a blank question; a trade, a company or a line that is not this job's or this trade's; a send or an answer out of turn; and a change order outside the money team, twice, or from an answer with no cost. All 30 sentences pass `plainWordsFailures`.
- **The SQL bed**: `supabase/tests/gc_building/50_rfis.sql` (50 assertions) runs beside the other Building scenarios, with the press in the harness's `PRESSES` list. It walks RFIs to the architect and back, starts a change order through the real draft, asks as the trade, and refuses a trainee, a twin, an estimator and a signed-in caller of the trade's press.
- `SECURITY INVOKER`, every one: the tables' dev policies decide who may, and the money team's for the change order.
