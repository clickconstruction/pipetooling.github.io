# v2.5958 — A lien waiver from a bid is kept on the bid, and the leader signs it with his code (2026-10-26)

The waiver signing train's PR C. The owner approved two canvases on 2026-10-26: the modernized Bids →
Lien Release tab (claude.ai/artifact/SKC7A628yTEEsQKahKDerv, shipped in v2.5893) and *Sign by his
code* (claude.ai/artifact/USoqD1sAgUyVszvqouk662). v2.5924 brought the leader's code to the Release of
Lien window and v2.5925 the print for a notary. This is the canvas's board 3 in its redrawn form, plus
the record the Bids canvas drew: *every mint is recorded on the bid*. Before it, the tab drew the four
§ 53.284 forms but kept nothing, and an estimator holding an unconditional waiver could only fetch the
leader's laptop.

## What changed

- **Migration `20261027020000_lien_release_bid_scoped.sql`** (`docs/migrations/…md`): `job_lien_releases`
  gains `bid_id` (FK to bids, on delete cascade) and `job_id` turns nullable, with
  `job_lien_releases_job_or_bid_check` holding exactly one set. Three estimator policies (read, insert,
  update) cover a bid's waivers on a bid he can see, never a job's; the office's policies stand. The
  `job_lien_releases_estimator_guard` trigger holds an estimator's row to the paper he made, issued and
  unsigned, except inside `sign_lien_release_with_code` (it names the row in the transaction-local
  `lien.release_code_signing`). `sign_lien_release_with_code` admits an estimator who is not a twin on a
  bid's waiver only, with the signer gate unchanged; `lien_sign_code_status` answers an estimator about a
  leader's code. The activity trigger skips a waiver with no job and names the conditional final form
  as itself (it read *Unconditional · final* since v2.4274). **Push after merge.**
- **Kernel** ([`bidLienWaiver.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/bidLienWaiver.ts)): `BidLienReleaseRow`,
  `bidLienReleaseFields` / `bidLienWaiverSamePaper` / `bidLienReleaseForPaper` (the recorded paper: the
  same form and every printed box, a signed one first), `bidLienReleaseChip` and
  `bidLienReleaseHistory` (Already on this bid), `bidLienWaiverLeader` (the Signed by box, else the
  company signer, else the first leader), `leaderInitials`, `BID_LIEN_WAIVER_CODE_DOOR_ROLES` /
  `bidLienWaiverCodeDoorShown` (the held assistant and estimator), `bidLienWaiverCodeDoorWords`,
  `bidLienReleaseInsert`, and `bidLienWaiverPaper` taking a signature.
- **IO** ([`bidLienReleaseIo.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/bidLienReleaseIo.ts)): the bid's rows with the
  names of the screens they were signed on, the leaders, the mint, the sent stamp.
- **[`BidLienReleaseTab`](../../src/components/bids/BidLienReleaseTab.tsx):** an output (Copy for
  email, Print, Download PDF) records its paper once, best effort, and a print or copy stamps it sent;
  **Already on this bid** lists the live rows as chips that load a paper back on the page. A held
  assistant or estimator sees board 3's white card in the amber hold: the leader's initials,
  *Malachi is with you?*, **Sign with his code** and *Ask him to send it himself ›*. The press records
  the paper with him under the line and opens the sign window; once his code signs it, the hold lifts
  for that paper and the outputs carry his signature. The page's foot reads *Waits for his ink or his
  code* while held and the signature's record after (`TradeWaiverPaperView` gains `footNote`).
- **[`LienReleaseSignModal`](../../src/components/jobs/LienReleaseSignModal.tsx)**: `codeOnly` (the
  code alone, no pad or *Draw instead*, with a line when his code is not set or is locked) and
  `docLabel` (*Malachi Whites signs here — Bid B482*).
- **`notify-lien-approval`**: a bid's `waiver_code_used` reads the bid; the words name *Bid 482 · Take
  5 Seguin*, land on the bid's Lien Release tab and say the waiver sits under *Already on this bid*
  (`lienWaiverCodeUsedPush` gains `bidId`). **Deploy after merge.**
- **Guide** *make a lien waiver from a bid*: *Sign it with his code* and *Already on this bid*.
- **Docs**: `BIDS_SYSTEM.md`'s Lien Release section rewritten (it still described the retired
  invoice wrapper), `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` rows, `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`.
