# v2.6551 — Pipeline: a Checklist left chip in the search bar narrows the board to the jobs with a question left (punch list #246 closed) (2026-11-05)

Punch list #246's PR 2 of two, the one the card left after PR 1 (v2.6362, the door on the row and the sheet beside it): "*Checklist left · N* in the board's search bar, narrowing the sections to the jobs with a question left." The card's reasoning stands: a column, not a filter — the office asks "what is holding Trim Set on these jobs" more often than it asks for one job, and the board's sections keep their order. With both PRs on main the card closes and its folder is deleted. No migration, no edge function.

## What changed

- `src/lib/jobs/pipelineChecklistDoor.ts` (PR 1's kernel) gains the chip: `jobHasChecklistLeft(fixtures, marks)` is the door's `open` kind; `countJobsWithChecklistLeft(jobs, marksFor)` counts the board's jobs with a question left; `filterJobsByChecklistLeft(jobs, on, marksFor)` keeps only them while the chip is on and every job while it is off, in their order; `checklistLeftChip(count, on)` gives the chip its label (*Checklist left · 4*), its title and its accessible name either way, and is null with nothing left and the chip off, so a clean board shows no chip. The count reads the marks the board already holds (v2.6087), so it costs no read.
- `JobsStagesTab.tsx`: the filter chain gains the chip as its last link. `stagesJobsBeforeChecklist` is the chain as it was; the count reads over it so the number holds while the chip is on; `stagesJobsUnderFilters` is that list through the chip. The chip is a display filter like the GC and hidden-groups ones (`stagesHasActiveDisplayFilter`), so the whole non-paid board loads while it is on and the collapsed headers agree with the rows. The state lives for the page; the office presses it when it asks the question.
- `StagesToolsFilters.checklistLeft` (`JobsStagesToolsMenu.tsx`) carries the count and the state to the bar; `JobsStagesCommandBar.tsx` draws the chip after the hidden-groups chip (`data-testid="stages-checklist-left-chip"`, `aria-pressed`): quiet with the count while off, amber with an × while on, one tap each way through `onToggleChecklistLeft`. Nothing hides with it, so a tap clears it, unlike the hidden-groups chip that opens its window.
- The guide *see every checklist on the Pipeline* gains *Show only the jobs with a question left*; `docs/JOBS_STAGES_TAB_ARCHITECTURE.md`'s notes block has the chip's line. `to-dos/pipeline-checklist-door/` is deleted and its Closed line sits at the top of `to-dos/README.md`'s Closed block.

## Decisions

- **The count is jobs, not questions.** The row's door already reads the questions (*3/6*); the chip answers "how many jobs are held", which is what narrows the board.
- **The number holds while the chip is on.** Counting after the chip's own filter would always equal the rows shown; counting before it keeps the chip's number the board's truth either way.
- **Not kept on the device.** The GC, development and Account Man filters live for the page too; the hidden groups are per device because they are many and chosen once. One chip the office presses when it asks the question needs no memory.
