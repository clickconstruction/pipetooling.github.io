# v2.5470 — Messages: Who is this? lists first the people who fit the inbox a message came to (2026-10-13)

Residential Messages, R6 · compose and sort (`to-dos/gc-mode/messages/RESIDENTIAL_BUILD_PLAN.md` → R6, the third PR, on
branch spike/gc-mode). It stands on R6's kernel (v2.5418, `boxes.ts`) and SQL (v2.5428, `20261014003000`, on prod).
It was built on R2 PR 1 (v2.5446). No migration, no edge function.

- `messages/boxes.ts` gains the reads a send needs, pure and light enough for the edge copy:
  - `parseBoxSettings` reads `messages_boxes_v1`, and `boxSetting` takes each empty field from a fallback.
  - `mailboxFor(kind, settings, fallback, wroteTo)` and `fromNumberFor(…)` pick the box they last wrote to, else their
    kind's.
  - `lastWroteTo(lines)` is their newest line in that names a box.
  - `orderHitsByBox(hits, box)` puts the box's own kinds first.
  - The client's fallback is empty (`NO_BOX_FALLBACK`). The edge functions will pass their secrets: QUO_COMPANY_* and
    QUO_PHONE_NUMBER_ID for the team box, GMAIL_IMPERSONATE_USER for bids@.
- `compose.ts` re-exports them. `replyCompany(jobKey, from)` names the chosen box's number and address. The reply box
  passes `from` only once `SENDS_FROM_BOXES` (`replyIo.ts`) is on. It stays off until R6's edge PR makes
  `messages-email` and `quo-send` send from the boxes. Until then an email goes from the app's own sending address, so
  the from line keeps today's words. `loadBoxSettings()` is the read.
- The thread's read selects `messages.mailbox`, and `ThreadMessage.mailbox` carries it. Lines in from bids@ already
  hold `bids` (R6's backfill and its writers).
- *Who is this?* (`WhoIsThisWindow`) takes the box of the number's newest line in. It lists the box's own kinds first:
  trade partners and supply houses on bids@, customers on the company number.
- `thread.ts` is copied to the edge, so `_shared/gcKernels` is regenerated. The change is one optional field.
- Left as they are, the lead's calls:
  - `websiteTerms.ts` is a copy of the Housecall Pro page, so it changes when the page does.
  - The letterhead presets and the lien reply-to wait for the owner's team@ or office@ answer, in a second PR.
- The help guide *find every message with a person in one place* says the new order.
