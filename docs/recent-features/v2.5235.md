# v2.5235 — The Bridge: Market and Sales read from the bids and estimates (link PR 4) (2026-10-11)

PR 4 of the weakest-link build (`to-dos/bridge-weakest-link/README.md`). Dev only. No migration.

- **The kernel** (`src/lib/bridge/demandPace.ts`, `buildDemand`): Market = bids created in the window (invited) and estimates created in the window (requested), counted and by value, per week; Sales = bids sent / won / lost by value, with "won" split into bids marked won (by `outcome_at`) and bids that became a job (by the job's creation day, at the job's contract), estimates sent / accepted / declined by value, and what awaits an answer: a bid or an estimate sent in the last 90 days (`AWAITING_DAYS`) with no answer. Bids sent with no value are counted for Fix the reading.
- **The stage flow** (`stageFlow.ts`, `MarketStageInput` / `SalesStageInput` / `fieldPerWeekUsd`): the feeders read by pace against the field, not by weeks of pile. Market: "45 asks in 8 weeks", "bids $2.4M from GCs" over "estimates $64k from customers", "about $308k a week", "not short" (green) or "short of the field". Sales: "$4.8M awaiting an answer" with the bids and estimates on their own lines, a SENT / WON / LOST table (the won cell's hover says how much is bids that became jobs), "sold $49k of work a week" over "the field builds $35k a week", "ahead of the field · wait" (green) or "behind the field". Sold per week and the field's pace come from PR 3's Build reading.
- **The reads** (`loadDemandPace.ts`): bids created, sent or answered since 90 days before the window (paged), the jobs those bids became (chunked by bid id, billing-only left out), and the estimates over the same span (`doc_kind` estimate only, `total_cents`).
- **The card** (`BridgeStages.tsx`): a flow line may carry its own tail ("from GCs"); the Sales table draws under the pile.
- **Tests**: `demandPace.test.ts` (2), a feeder case in `stageFlow.test.ts` (8 now).
- **Docs**: guide *read the Bridge* (the Market and Sales cards); the Glossary's Bridge entry; the to-do's status line.
