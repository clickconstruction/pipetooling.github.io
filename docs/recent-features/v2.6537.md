# v2.6537 — The property lookup reads a blank situs spelled Null, and Bexar's situs lines without a comma (2026-11-05)

Two edge functions change (**redeploy after merge**): **`property-lookup`** and **`owner-confirm-nightly`** (both import the changed `_shared/parcelPinMatch.ts` and `_shared/txParcelRecord.ts`). No migration. The two defects came out of the read-only audit that followed [v2.6520](v2.6520.md): every `customer_addresses` row the old pin rule filled (39, 33 unique addresses) run through the deployed function on 2026-11-05 and compared with the stored parcel. The audit itself is in the parent session's notes (`parcel-lookup-audit-2026-11-05.md`): **10 rows look wrong** (seven in Blanco's Rockin J Ranch and Natalia's Mustang Estates, small lots where Google's interpolated pin lands next door; nine of the ten had a person's Confirm), 7 right but held, 16 unchanged, 1 replatted, 2 unknown, 3 ZZ seeds.

## What changed

- **A situs the roll spells as the word `Null` reads as blank** ([`_shared/txParcelRecord.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/txParcelRecord.ts) `rollText`, on the situs and the mailing parts; [`_shared/parcelPinMatch.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/parcelPinMatch.ts) `isBlankSitus` as the gate's own guard). Medina and Bastrop parcels (976 CR 7718, 858 FM 1209) came back with `situsAddress: 'Null'`, the gate read a street named "null" and held the parcel; the pin gate carries a blank situs as before.
- **The streets agree when either side's first word appears among the other's words.** Bexar's situs line carries the city with no comma (`150 E SONMERE BLVD SAN ANTONIO, TX 78258`), so its core became `sonmere san antonio` and a typed `150 E Sonmere Blvd 200B` (core `sonmere 200b`) failed the one-core-inside-the-other test against its own parcel. `Oak` against `Main` still holds; the Taylor case still holds.
- The record panel and the nightly are unchanged; the kernel's words carry.
