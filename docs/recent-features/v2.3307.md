# v2.3307 — Bids → Labor: calibration — the book against finished jobs (PR 5 of the Labor refresh) (2026-09-11)

Client only. Rung 5 of [`to-dos/bids-labor-refresh/`](../../to-dos/bids-labor-refresh/README.md); reads the bid ↔ job links the Burn train (v2.3297–v2.3306) creates.

## Kernels — [`laborBookCalibration.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/laborBookCalibration.ts) (+5 tests)

- `bookMultiplier(jobs)` — over the jobs linked to bids that priced with the book: Σ recorded field hours ÷ Σ predicted-to-date (an unfinished job's prediction × its % done). A job qualifies when the book predicted hours for it, it has hours, it is ≥ 25 % done and has ≥ 8 field days; the rest are named with why (`no-prediction` · `no-hours` · `under-25-pct` · `under-8-days`). `bookMultiplierWords`: *book runs ×1.18 light · 3 jobs* / *×0.82 heavy* / *on the money* / *no linked jobs yet* / *no job says yet — 2 linked but too young or unpriced*.
- `entryEvidence(entries, used)` — per book entry, every used job whose count sheet matched it (`matchLaborRow`, so aliases and plan codes count): the hours the book put on that row, the job's actual hours attributed to the row by the book's own share (row predicted ÷ bid predicted), the ratio; median, and a spread — *agree* / *wide* (ratios differ by more than a third) / *none*; words *3 jobs agree* · *2 jobs · wide* · *1 job* · *no jobs yet*.
- `calibratedEntryHours(entry, medianRatio)` — the entry × the median, to the quarter hour.

## Data — [`useLaborBookCalibration`](../../src/hooks/useLaborBookCalibration.ts)

Bids with `selected_labor_book_version_id = book` → `jobs_ledger` by `bid_id` → the bids' cost-estimate labor rows, `get_man_hours_by_job` (the Stages board's RPC; dev · master technician · assistant) for recorded hours, and `clock_sessions` dates for field days. Roles the RPC refuses read *jobs' hours are not yours to read*. Enabled only while the New view shows.

## The New view — [`BidsLaborNewView`](../../src/components/bids/BidsLaborNewView.tsx) (+2 render tests)

- **Book vs jobs** tile: *×1.18 · book runs ×1.18 light · 3 jobs* (or the no-evidence words).
- An **evidence chip** beside each filled row's source chip — green *3 jobs agree*, amber *2 jobs · wide*, muted *no jobs yet* (disabled). Tapping opens the **drawer** under the grid: the entry's book hours, one line per job (done %, book said, crew ran, ratio), *Median ×1.36 → set the entry to 1.25 / 1.25 / 1.25 h* (a *wide* warning when the jobs disagree), **Set** (writes `labor_book_entries` and reloads the book) / **Keep**.
