# v2.8329 — Dashboard: Needs you buttons at the bottom right on a phone, and three clearer names (2026-11-23)

Four changes in one PR, all the owner's of 2026-11-23: the Needs you presses, and three names. The names are at the end, under *Three names*.

## Needs you on a phone

The owner's ask of 2026-11-23, on a screenshot of the Dashboard's Needs you list on his phone: the buttons should be much more uniform and all on the right-hand side, because people read left to right, so the eye reads what to do and then lands on what to press. A layout change to one component. No line's words, title, colour or door changed.

### What was there

One row drew every source, as a wrapping line: the words (240 px wide or more), the figure, the press. At a desk that is one line with the press last. On a phone the three did not fit, so the press fell to a second line under the words, on the left, and the figure stayed alone at the far right. One line looked different, **Today's sends**, and for no reason of its own: its press says **Open**, and on a wide phone that short label still fit beside the figure. There was never a second layout, only a wrap that depended on the label's length and the phone's width.

### The first cut, and why it changed

The first cut put the press in a right-hand column of one width, 104 px, with the words in the column left of it. The owner saw it drawn and did not want it: *I don't like the buttons squeezing the text to the left, because some of the text is multiple lines. I would prefer we instead just keep it like a smaller button in the bottom right.* A line's sentence can run to three or four lines at full width, and the column made it six.

### What changed

On a phone (`useIsMobile`, 640 px and under) every source's Cards row, and a line opened from a fold, is:

- **The words across the row's full width** (`data-needs-you-words`): the title and the sentence, with nothing beside them.
- **One last line** (`data-needs-you-foot`), with the press at its right end (`data-needs-you-press-end`).
- **A small press**, as wide as its own words and the size it was before: a pill of about 32 px. Its touch area is a clear button 44 px tall around the pill, and the button's margins take back the 6 px that adds above and under, so the row is no taller for it.
- **The figure beside the press**, on its left, as **Today's sends** already had it. A figure the title already says as a number of its own is not drawn (`needsYouPhoneFigure`): *14 purchases need a job* shows 14 once. A title with no number, like *Customers*, keeps its figure. The rule reads whole numbers only, so a 9 is never found inside 19, $9,400 or 9%.
- **A line's secondary links share the last line**, on the left. Where the links and the press do not fit one line, the press takes the next one, still at the right.
- **A long label** stays on one line where the row can hold it. It wraps inside its press only when it and its figure are wider than the row, which at 320 px is a label of twenty letters beside a money figure.
- **Each press keeps its tone's colour**, from `needsYouPalette.ts` as before.

The presses are uniform in what they share: one place, one height, one type size. They are not one width. A fixed width is what narrowed the words.

The kernel is `src/lib/dashboard/needsYouRow.ts`. The card's `row` builds the box and the press's tone once and lays them out twice, and both layouts draw a row's words with `NeedsYouRowWords` (v2.8336, punch list #424), so the desk line and the phone row cannot drift in words or colour. A row that shows its values, as **Today's sends** does since v2.8336, draws its own title with the count in it, so the figure rule reads that drawn title and the count is not drawn a second time beside the press.

### What did not change

- **At a desk** the row is the one line it was, the figure drawn and the press last. The press was already on the right there.
- **Walk the list** keeps its drawing.
- **The leader's lien notices** (`LienLeaderSignCardView`, v2.7435) keep their own drawing: a card with a row per notice, approved as drawn.
- **The Fill them in deck** and the folds' own header lines.
- **Quickfill** draws the same component, so its Needs you rows on a phone change with the Dashboard's, including the lines under the deck.
- **The new phone layout** (v2.8289) chooses between the door, Walk the list and Cards. Where it shows Cards, the rows are these.

### Narrow phones

## Three names

Only the words people read changed. Every code name stays: `DashboardCrewDaySection`, `crewDay.ts`, `isCrewDayRole`, the `crew_day` stream, `get_crew_day_payload`, the `dash-crew-day` anchor, `DashboardYourPeopleSection`, `dashboardYourPeople.ts`, `people_week`, `PeopleWeekSheet`, and the comments. No migration.

### Crew Day is now Who worked

Asked what Crew Day meant, the owner was told it is one day of the whole team by person, and that *crew* is wrong because office people are on it. He picked **Who worked**.

- The Dashboard card's title, its phone fold and its dock chip. The envelope reads **Email Who worked**, as do the window it opens, its search box and its toast.
- The end-of-day email: its subject and its text body now open *Who worked — Tue, Sep 29 · …* (`_shared/crewDayEmail.ts`).
- The same email wherever Settings names it, which was *Crew day*: the Email catalog's *Who worked digest* and its subject example, the template's label, What the team sees, the default intro line and the schedule's stream label.
- The card's refusal reads *You don't have access to this day.*
- A *crew day* in lower case, the unit a job's pace and its charges are read in, is a different thing and keeps its name. So the Projects board, the pace strip, the customer timeline, and the guides `charge-by-crew` and `see-where-every-project-stands-and-set-its-expected-hours` are unchanged.

### Your people is now Team's open tasks

The card lists open one-off tasks, one row a person, for the whole team. *People* did not say tasks and *your* suggested direct reports.

- The card's title, its dock chip, and the words when it fails to load.
- Its top line counts *3 tasks done this week*, where it said cards. The rest of the app and the guide say task.
- The customer portal's *Your people* (who to call on a job) is a different thing and keeps its name.

### Your people's week is now The team's week

- The sheet's heading and its preview frame, the Settings rows, the Email catalog, What the team sees, the schedule's stream label, and the email's subject: *The team's week · Nov 2 to 8* (`_shared/peopleWeekEmail.ts`).
- The email log links a send to its stream by its subject. It now reads both names, so a send from before this version still links (`emailLogStreamLink.ts`).
- **The email keeps *cards done*.** Its count is wider than the Dashboard card's: `get_people_week_payload` counts one-off tasks and roadmap task cards, and the card counts one-off tasks alone. So the two lines are not the same number and do not share a noun.
- The presses keep their words: **✉ Get this every Monday** and **✉ Email me their week**.
- The guide keeps its file name and address. Its title changed, and its keywords keep the old words so a search still finds it.

### Docs

`GLOSSARY.md` has a new *Who worked (Dashboard)* entry, and the two renamed entries each say what they were called. `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`, `DASHBOARD_SECTIONS_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`, `REPORT_SUBSCRIPTIONS.md`, `PEOPLE_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `CHECKLIST_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` and the twins' `APP_DIRECTORY.md` use the new screen names and keep the code names. `to-dos/`, earlier fragments, earlier release notes and migrations are left as written.
