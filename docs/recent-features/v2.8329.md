# v2.8329 — Dashboard: Needs you buttons on the right on a phone, and three clearer names (2026-11-23)

Four changes in one PR, all the owner's of 2026-11-23: the Needs you presses, and three names. The names are at the end, under *Three names*.

## Needs you on a phone

The owner's ask of 2026-11-23, on a screenshot of the Dashboard's Needs you list on his phone: the buttons should be much more uniform and all on the right-hand side, because people read left to right, so the left says what to do and the right says what to press. A layout change to one component. No migration, no edge function, and no line's words, title, colour or door changed.

## What was there

One row drew every source, as a wrapping line: the words (240 px wide or more), the figure, the press. At a desk that is one line with the press last. On a phone the three did not fit, so the press fell to a second line under the words, on the left, as wide as its label, and the figure stayed alone at the far right. One line looked different, **Today's sends**, and for no reason of its own: its press says **Open**, and on a wide phone that short label still fit beside the figure. There was never a second layout, only a wrap that depended on the label's length and the phone's width.

## What changed

On a phone (`useIsMobile`, 640 px and under) the Cards row is a two-column grid, the same for every source and for a line opened from a fold:

- **The words on the left** (`data-needs-you-words`): the title, the sentence, and under them a line's secondary links, where they were.
- **The press on the right** (`data-needs-you-press-column`), centred on the row. The column is `NEEDS_YOU_PRESS_COLUMN`, `clamp(5.25rem, 27vw, 6.5rem)`: 104 px from a 390 px phone up, 101 px at 375, 86 px at 320. Every press fills it, is 44 px or taller, and is set in one type size.
- **A long label wraps inside its press.** The column has fixed ends, so no label widens it for the rest. *Open Hiring → Review*, the longest on the list, takes two lines at 104 px and three at 86 px, and that press grows taller. No label is shortened: a press keeps the words that name its act.
- **The figure sits above the press**, centred on it. A figure the title already says as a number of its own is not drawn (`needsYouPhoneFigure`): *14 purchases need a job* shows 14 once. A title with no number, like *Customers*, keeps its figure above the press. The rule reads whole numbers only, so a 9 is never found inside 19, $9,400 or 9%.
- **Each press keeps its tone's colour**, from `needsYouPalette.ts` as before.

**Walk the list** keeps its drawing, one card with the press and **Skip for now** under the words; on a phone those two presses are now 44 px tall as well.

The kernel is `src/lib/dashboard/needsYouRow.ts`. The card's `row` now builds the box, the words and the press's tone once and lays them out twice, so the desk line and the phone grid cannot drift in words or colour.

## What did not change

- **At a desk** the row is the one line it was, the figure drawn and the press last. The press was already on the right there.
- **The leader's lien notices** (`LienLeaderSignCardView`, v2.7435) keep their own drawing: a card with a row per notice, approved as drawn.
- **The Fill them in deck** and the folds' own header lines.
- **Quickfill** draws the same component, so its Needs you rows on a phone change with the Dashboard's, including the lines under the deck.
- **The new phone layout** (v2.8289) chooses between the door, Walk the list and Cards. Where it shows Cards, the rows are these.

## Narrow phones

At 320 px a row is about 259 px wide. The press column takes its floor, 86 px, and the words keep about 127 px: a title runs three or four words a line. What gives at that width is the press column, 18 px narrower than on a wider phone, and a long label's third line.

## Three names

Only the words people read changed. Every code name stays: `DashboardCrewDaySection`, `crewDay.ts`, `isCrewDayRole`, the `crew_day` stream, `get_crew_day_payload`, the `dash-crew-day` anchor, `DashboardYourPeopleSection`, `dashboardYourPeople.ts`, `people_week`, `PeopleWeekSheet`, and the comments. No migration.

### Crew Day is now Who worked

Asked what Crew Day meant, the owner was told it is one day of the whole team by person, and that *crew* is wrong because office people are on it. He picked **Who worked**.

- The Dashboard card's title, its phone fold and its dock chip. The envelope reads **Email Who worked**, as do the window it opens, its search box and its toast.
- The end-of-day email: its subject and its text body now open *Who worked — Tue, Sep 29 · …* (`_shared/crewDayEmail.ts`).
- The same email wherever Settings names it, which was *Crew day*: the Email catalog's *Who worked digest* and its subject example, the template's label, What the team sees, the default intro line and the schedule's stream label.
- The card's refusal reads *You don't have access to this day.*
- A *crew day* in lower case, the unit a job's pace and its charges are read in, is a different thing and keeps its name. So the Projects board, the pace strip, the customer timeline, and the guides `charge-by-crew` and `see-where-every-project-stands-and-set-its-expected-hours` are unchanged.

### Your people is now Team's open tasks

The card lists open one-off tasks, one row a person, for the whole team. *People* did not say tasks and *your* suggested direct reports.

- The card's title, its dock chip, and the words when it fails to load.
- Its top line counts *3 tasks done this week*, where it said cards. The rest of the app and the guide say task.
- The customer portal's *Your people* (who to call on a job) is a different thing and keeps its name.

### Your people's week is now The team's week

- The sheet's heading and its preview frame, the Settings rows, the Email catalog, What the team sees, the schedule's stream label, and the email's subject: *The team's week · Nov 2 to 8* (`_shared/peopleWeekEmail.ts`).
- The email log links a send to its stream by its subject. It now reads both names, so a send from before this version still links (`emailLogStreamLink.ts`).
- **The email keeps *cards done*.** Its count is wider than the Dashboard card's: `get_people_week_payload` counts one-off tasks and roadmap task cards, and the card counts one-off tasks alone. So the two lines are not the same number and do not share a noun.
- The presses keep their words: **✉ Get this every Monday** and **✉ Email me their week**.
- The guide keeps its file name and address. Its title changed, and its keywords keep the old words so a search still finds it.
