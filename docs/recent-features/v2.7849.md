# v2.7849 — Checklist: View as from a person's Task Mode window lands on their Today, not an empty page (2026-11-18)

Punch list #381, found on the first live run of v2.7786's line. No migration and no edge function.

## What was wrong

The line **View as <first name>** signed the dev in and landed on the address the dev pressed from, `/checklist?tab=review`. Checklist took the tab from the address for any role. A crew role is drawn Today and History alone, so the page showed its two tabs with neither picked and nothing under them. The dev had to press **Today** to see the screen the look had just drawn. v2.7786's fragment said such a role was sent to its own Today. It was not.

The same empty page met any role without the office tabs that opened a link to Review or Manage, and a dev who used Settings → View as while on Review.

## What changed

- **The line lands on the person's Today** (`lookViewAsLanding`, `/checklist?tab=today`), for every role. That is the screen the look drew. **Exit** still returns to the page the dev left, since `loginAsUser` keeps that address apart from the landing.
- **Checklist sends a landing it cannot draw to Today.** `officeTabLandsOnToday` in `src/lib/tasks/checklistTabs.ts`: the address names Review or Manage, the role is read, and the role is drawn neither. The page waits for the role before it moves anyone. `hasOfficeChecklistTabs` is now the one place that says who is drawn those tabs.

## Tests

- `checklistTabs.test.ts`, 5 cases: who is drawn the office tabs, who is sent to Today, and that a role not read yet is left alone.
- `taskLook.test.ts`, one more case for the landing.
- `Checklist.reviewLook.render.test.tsx`: the sign-in is told to land on Today, and a helper who opens `/checklist?tab=review` is drawn their Today. With the new rule switched off, that case fails.
