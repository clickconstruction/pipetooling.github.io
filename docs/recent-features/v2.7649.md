# v2.7649 — Find a file: a signed contract says what it is, and opens its record (2026-11-17)

Punch list #377, PR 6 of six: the chip. A filed contract was already found by its words, since it is a Drive file like any other. This says what the hit is and gives the office the door to its record.

## What changed

- **Both searches mark a paper on file.** `drive-link`'s `search` (Drive's own finds) and `search_index` (our index's rows) look up the contract rows whose Drive copy is among the ids found, and mark each such hit `paper: 'signed' | 'voided'`. One read, by id (`_shared/driveLinkPapers.ts` over `contractPapersOnFile` in the rules).
- **What counts.** A signed paper in force marks its file *signed*, whether the app filed it or adopted it. A voided paper marks only the copy the app made. A file the office linked on a record that was later voided is nobody's paper again and wears no chip. When one file stands for several records, a signed one wins.
- **The record goes only to who reads it.** The four roles that read `job_contracts` also get the contract's id and its job's. An estimator gets the mark alone. The owner chose that estimators can find and read a filed contract (the card's call 1); they are still never handed a door to a record the database would refuse them.
- **No migration.** The card planned to change the two search functions in the database. Their answer shape would have had to change, which means dropping and remaking functions the search calls on every keystroke. Marking the hits in the edge function does the same with one indexed read and nothing to push.
- **The box draws it** (`paperChip` in `findAFile.ts`): *Signed contract* in green, *Voided contract* in red, beside the file's name.
- **Open the contract** shows under a hit whose record was sent. `FindAFileSection.tsx`, a new small file, mounts the box on the Documents page's Search tab and owns that door, so the box itself stays free of the Contract window and `Documents.tsx` gains one line.
- **Fail-soft.** A database without the Drive columns, or a refused read, marks nothing. The search answers exactly as before.
- **The guide's line rides with PR 4**, the step that starts filing contracts: until then no contract is in Drive for the chip to sit on.
