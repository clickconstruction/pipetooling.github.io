# v2.4427 — Bids: a phone notification on the morning of a promised call (2026-10-02)

Punch list #80, PR 5 of 5, and the to-do retires with it. The owner's case: "we need to contact them in January", said in October by someone who may not be at the Dashboard that morning.

## What shipped

- **Edge function** [`remind-bid-followups`](../../supabase/functions/remind-bid-followups/index.ts) ([`EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`](../EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md#remind-bid-followups)): hourly from pg_cron; from 8 AM office time it sends one push per bid and day to the bid's account manager, else its estimator, else whoever set the day. *Bid follow-up due today · City of Riverton, BP82 City re-pipe. Ask for J. Rayburn.* It opens the Call queue. Robots' bids, spent days and decided bids remind nobody. Kill switch `bid_followup_reminders_disabled_v1`.
- **Rules** [`_shared/bidFollowupReminder.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/bidFollowupReminder.ts): what is due, who is told, the words, the office hour. Its "day still stands" rule is pinned to the app's (`followupDayStands`) by a parity test.
- **Migration** [`20261002160000_bid_followup_reminders.sql`](../../supabase/migrations/20261002160000_bid_followup_reminders.sql) ([its doc](../migrations/20261002160000_bid_followup_reminders.md)): the ledger table (one row per bid and day, unique, service-role writes only) and the hourly job.
- `supabase/config.toml`: `verify_jwt = false` for the function (the cron secret is the credential).
- Help: [`follow-up-with-builders`](../../src/content/help/follow-up-with-builders.md) says who gets the notification. [`GLOSSARY.md`](../GLOSSARY.md) *Call-again day*.
- The to-do `to-dos/bid-follow-up-date/` is deleted: all five PRs are in (v2.4419, v2.4420, v2.4421, v2.4426, v2.4427). The mock-up stays in git history.

## Tests

- [`bidFollowupReminder.test.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/bidFollowupReminder.test.ts): 16 cases (the office clock across daylight saving, parity with the app's rule on eight cases, due once, a moved day reminds again, robots, the recipient order, the words).
