# v2.7749 — Dashboard view: a superintendent can be set to Short (2026-11-18)

Punch list #384 (Job Mode, short), PR 1 of 3. One migration, `20261118070000_dashboard_view_superintendent.sql`. No edge function.

## Why

A superintendent said his phone page overwhelms him. The owner picked a short Job Mode page, set per person through the Short setting the leader's short Dashboard already has (punch list #365). That setting took a leader's or a dev's account only, and only the office could press it. This PR opens it. The short Job Mode page itself is PR 2.

## What landed

- **The rule** in `lib/dashboard/shortDashboard.ts`. `DASHBOARD_VIEW_ROLES` is the leader, a dev and a superintendent, and `canHaveShortDashboard` reads it. `shortPageFor(role, jobModeOn)` says which short page a person on Short gets: the short Dashboard with Job Mode off, the short Job Mode page with it on for a superintendent, and nothing with it on for a leader or a dev, who keep the full card as #365 decided. `canSetDashboardView` says who presses a row: the office for any row that takes a view, a person for his own.
- **The function.** `set_dashboard_view` is restated from `20261117030000_dashboard_view.sql`. It takes a superintendent's account, and it lets a person set his own row. Its two refusals read *Only the office sets another person's Dashboard view.* and *A Dashboard view is for a leader's or a superintendent's account.* A training account and a twin are refused as before.
- **The pickers follow the rule.** The People desk's **Dashboard view** section is drawn on a superintendent's desk, and it says *you pick* when the viewer is the person. Settings shows the picker to a superintendent under **Your dashboard**.
- **The short Dashboard's door.** For a superintendent the door's second line is *Clock In, your hours, your crew and the full Dashboard.* The leader's line named money, which a superintendent's page does not hold.

## What a superintendent on Short sees today

- **Job Mode off:** the leader's short Dashboard. **Waiting on you** holds only a job note that tagged him, since the lien lines are the leader's by `needsYouActor`. Then My Schedule when the day or tomorrow holds a stop, the office line, **Everything else** and Training. **Clock In** is behind the door, one press further than on the full page. The door opens the full Dashboard folded, with **Back to the short Dashboard** on top.
- **Job Mode on:** the Job Mode page as it is today, since its return comes first. `shortPageFor` already answers `job_mode` for him, and nothing draws it until PR 2.

Nobody is on Short who was not before: every superintendent's `dashboard_view` is NULL.
