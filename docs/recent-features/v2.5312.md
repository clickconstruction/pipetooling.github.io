# v2.5312 — Contracts: papers added to a flow stay out of the Contract Book's lists (2026-10-12)

Messages cut 4, wave 2's F (helper 6), the guard the lead asked for after F1's review. A paper the flow editor adds (`flow_paper_create`, migration `20261013140000_flow_papers`, v2.5306) is a Contract library document with `flow_kind` set, filed under the customer or the sub audience in a "Flow papers" packet. Eleven older reads took the library by audience alone, so each now skips flow papers with `.is('flow_kind', null)`.

- **The one that would have gone wrong**: `openInPersonSigning` in `src/lib/jobs/jobContractInPerson.ts` takes the *first customer document by name* as the terms of the contract a customer signs in person. A customer flow paper named "Bond" or "Certificate" sorts ahead of the service agreement, so its empty body would have become a signed contract's terms.
- **Customer audience**:
  - `JobContractModal.tsx` and `JobsContractSweepModal.tsx` default the terms to the first customer document by name.
  - `get-job-contract`'s sample takes the newest dated customer document, and a flow paper is dated the day it is made. It is an edge function: the lead redeploys it, after the migration is on prod.
  - `SettingsContractsTab.tsx` lists every customer document as the standard terms.
- **Sub audience**:
  - `quickSendWorkOrder.ts` incorporates every sub document by reference into a quick-sent work order (`buildWorkOrderReferences`).
  - `WorkOrderAssemblerModal.tsx`, `SubSheetWorkOrderPanel.tsx` and `ContractScopeLibraryTab.tsx` offer them as the work order's book documents.
  - `PeopleSubsTab.tsx` falls back to the first sub document for the Gen. Cond. pill, and a new packet's first paper has sequence 0.
  - `src/lib/gc/papersIo.ts` matches only by title or by form, so it was safe, but it carries the guard all the same.
- **The guard is tested**: `src/lib/contracts/flowPapersStayOut.test.ts` reads every library read in `src/` and `supabase/functions/`. One that filters by audience without the guard fails CI, so the next reader cannot forget it.
- **Order**: every read names `flow_kind`, so this ships after the migration is on prod. Nothing can make a flow paper until the New paper window (v2.5307) and the flow editor land, and the window waits for this PR.
- The library's own lists by packet (People → Contracts, the Contract library) and the clause library (Settings → Contracts and clauses) still show flow papers, in their "Flow papers" packets. That is where the office finds them.
