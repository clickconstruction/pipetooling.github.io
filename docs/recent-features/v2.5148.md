# v2.5148 — GC projects: the office's notices go out, behind the owner's switch (2026-10-09)

GC mode's Owner Billing O10b, on O10a (v2.5137, `20261010060000`). New function `gc-office-notices`; migration `20261010070000_gc_office_notices_cron` (doc: `docs/migrations/20261010070000_gc_office_notices_cron.md`). The plan is `to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/owner-billing-o10.md` on `spike/gc-mode`.

## What shipped

- **`gc-office-notices`** (`supabase/functions/gc-office-notices/index.ts`, `verify_jwt = false`): the cron mode, hourly at :13, does nothing before 8 AM Central or with the switch off; else it reads `get_gc_office_notices_due()`, and for each notice finds its address, writes its `gc_office_notices` row first (a second tick's insert hits the unique index and skips), sends, and writes the row's `email_send_log_id`. The architect's reminder goes from Click Construction, Reply-To the project manager, and keeps its sent copy on the billing job (`gc_certify_reminder`); ours are staff mail (`gc_office_notice`). `preview` and `test_send` for the money team (never a training account or a twin) write nothing; a test goes to the caller with `[TEST]` and logs as `gc_office_notice_test`.
- **The words** (`supabase/functions/_shared/gcOfficeNotices.ts`): bill day with the pay application to draft and each trade owing an unconditional waiver; the architect's reminder; the project manager's note with the day the architect was reminded, or why not. `gcOfficeNoticesSince` reads the switch's day once for the function and the Settings block.
- **Settings → Jobs & billing → Email the office's GC notices** (`GcOfficeNoticesSettingsBlock`, `src/lib/gc/officeNoticesSetting.ts`), for dev and the owner: turning it on writes today's day, *On since Oct 15. Only pay applications sent from that day get notices.*; **Preview today's notices** and **Email me a test**.
- **Bill the customer**: a sent pay application waiting on the architect says *We reminded the architect on Oct 5.* (`architectRemindedOn`, read from `gc_office_notices` by `loadGcOwnerBillingRows`).
- **Registries**: What customers see (the GC journey's *still waiting on your certificate*, its sample), its line in `personJourney.ts`, What the team sees (*The office's GC notices*, its sample), the email catalog (`gc_certify_reminder`, `gc_office_notice`), the dev-mcp catalog, `config.toml`. **Docs**: `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`, `REPORT_SUBSCRIPTIONS.md`, `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`; the guide `bill-the-customer-on-a-gc-job` (*The reminders the app sends*).
