# v2.4087 — The submittal room shows the GC the procurement log (2026-09-28)

PR 2 of the procurement log (plan https://claude.ai/artifact/7wZt8WtED2n9AMTgA3eDtV), on top of [v2.4083](./v2.4083.md). Edge function + client; no migration.

## What shipped

- **`get-submittal-room`** answers with `procurement` on an open room when the office has any log row or any tag is approved: the `bid_procurement_items` rows **without the PO** (tag, label, lead time, stage, ordered, expected, delivered, note), the takeoff's count rows and stage splits, the job's stage dates (`stageDatesFromJob` from the job made from the bid — its Order fixtures and `job_stage_windows`), and the last update's `sent_at`. Wrapped in its own try/catch, so a room answers as before if the tables are not there yet. Every `RoomRow` now carries `leadTimeDays`.
- **`_shared/procurementStageDates.ts`** — `stageOfStageName` and `stageDatesFromJob` moved out of the client kernel and re-exported from it (the `stagePlan` pattern), so the office's log and the room read the same "required" dates. `tagStagesFrom(countRows, splits, tags)` joined the client kernel (pure; the IO's `loadTagStagesForBid` now calls it).
- **`SubmittalRoom.tsx`** — `ProcurementCard` after the folded rows on the current revision: builds `ProcurementItemSource`s from the room rows (product = proposed, the decision, the lead time), the records with a blank PO, the tag stages, then `buildProcurementLog`; drops rows still awaiting review or not on the submittal. Tag · Status (`statusText`) · Expected · Required · on time / *N d late* / on site / *order by*; "Updated MM/DD by <company>" from `lastUpdateAt`, the headline counts, and a line saying where required comes from (or that it fills once the job's stage schedule is set). `parseSubmittalRoomPayload` passes `procurement` through.
- Tests: the payload twin (`leadTimeDays` on the row), the room page (a case with three rows: on time, on site, 38 d late; KS-1 awaiting review absent; no PO or house anywhere).
