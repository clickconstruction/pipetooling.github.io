# v2.6911 — One press files every ready row of a price sheet (2026-11-10)

Punch list #308, PR 5 of nine. PR 4 opened a sheet and let each card file its own row. This PR adds the press for many rows, per the repo's rule *do what is ready, never all or nothing*, and the way back for a whole sheet.

## What changed

- **Migration `20261111080000_price_sheet_filing.sql`** ([doc](../migrations/20261111080000_price_sheet_filing.md)): `price_row_file` and `price_row_take_back` replaced with a branch for a sheet's row that has no price, and the new `price_sheet_take_back(paper_id)`. No table changes. Push right after the merge.
- **`PriceSheetPanel.tsx`**:
  - **File N rows**, sticky at the head of the panel, labelled by `batchWords`: *File 40 rows · 25 wait ▸*, with the foot that says what stays and what goes. It files the `ready` rows one by one. A row that fails is named with its reason and the rest go on. A row the database holds (a big move, a newer price on file) becomes a question on its own card and is counted as waiting, not failed. The outcome is said with `batchOutcomeWords`.
  - **Confirm N as the same parts**, on the head of *One look, then file*: the rows matched by a model number, a name or the words, filed together after one look.
  - **Take this sheet back…** replaces *Remove this sheet…* once a row is filed. The confirm window names the count on its button, and the outcome says how many prices were changed since and stay.
- **`PriceRowCardView.tsx`**: a kit row (no price of its own) has a press of its own, **Same part**, or **New part in the book** when nothing is on file. Either says which part the row is and keeps the house's number, and writes no price.
- **`priceRowIo.ts`** files a row with a null price when it is a sheet's row; **`priceSheetIo.ts`** gains `takeBackSheet`.
- The guide `read-a-supply-houses-price-sheet` gains *File what is ready* and *Take a sheet back*.

## Why a row with no price is filed at all

A quote's fixture often prints its parts with no price each and one subtotal for the lot. Those rows are most of the sheet. Filing one writes nothing to the price list, and it is still the most useful press on the panel: it is how the house's own number reaches the part, so the same row on the next sheet is ready with no press.
