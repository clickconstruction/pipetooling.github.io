# v2.6077 — Stage checklists: the office writes the questions (punch list #164, PR 2) (2026-10-29)

Punch list #164, PR 2 of five, on the rules of v2.6072. The tables, one additive migration, and the card where the office keeps the company's questions per stage kind. It ships empty by the owner's call (2026-10-29): he writes the words himself, so no placeholder question reaches prod, and the checklists start the day the office types the questions.

## What changed

- **Migration `20261029210000_stage_checklists.sql`** (`docs/migrations/20261029210000_stage_checklists.md`): `stage_checklist_templates` (a question per row: kind, order, the words, whether it asks for a photo, retired when taken off) and `stage_checklist_answers` (one per job stage and question: the pick, the note, who and when, the photo's waiting-room path, its Drive id and `filed_at` once the sweep files it, `fail_why` when it could not), the private bucket `stage-checklist-photos`, RLS (the questions read by everyone signed in and written by the office; a job's answers by `can_report_stage_progress(job_id)`), the `photo-save` cron re-scheduled to call while a checklist photo waits too, and the three fence calls.
- **The editor**, Settings → Jobs & billing → **Stage checklists** (`StageChecklistSettingsCard.tsx`, dev, master and assistant-like roles): the three kinds listed, each empty with *No questions for Rough In yet. Add a question adds the first.*; a row is the words, **Asks for a photo**, the arrows and **Take off**; **Save the questions** writes the diff (`planQuestionSave` in `lib/jobs/stageChecklistEditor.ts`: inserts, updates, retires, every live row re-placed), **Put them back** undoes the typing, an empty question stops the save with its number and kind. The io is `lib/jobs/stageChecklistIo.ts`.
- **The guide** *write the questions for a stage checklist* (Office). `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`, `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` (the child tables, the Settings card) and the Glossary's *Stage checklist*.
- **For #189's PR 6** (a photo answers a checklist question): once this is pushed, the write is the photo columns of the `stage_checklist_answers` row for the job stage's `fixture_id` and the `question_id`, inserted with `pick` null when the crew has not picked yet; the sheet (PR 3) reads it as the photo.

## Decisions

- **A question taken off is retired, never deleted**, so an answer the crew already gave keeps its words. The dev's delete policy is for a mistake, not the editor.
- **The office is dev, master and assistant-like**, the set that keeps the test report's paper; the estimator reads the questions and writes none.
