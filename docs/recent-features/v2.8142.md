# v2.8142 — Document builder: Send it anyway is asked by the usual a job's sheet went out on (2026-11-20)

Punch list #404, left by the review of 2026-11-20. No migration. One function changed: `document-builder-send`, which takes a deploy.

## What was wrong

A send of a job's insurance sheet warns, with the box **Send it anyway**, while nobody has marked the usual sheet as checked by an insurance agent. The mark names one revision of the usual (`document_builders.read_revision`). A job's sheet is published on one revision of the usual (`document_builder_sheets.usual_revision`) and stays on it until the job is published again.

Both the screen and the function asked the mark of the usual's **newest** revision (`read_revision === revision`), not of the revision the job's sheet went out on. Two cases came out wrong.

- The usual's revision 1 went out unchecked, and a job was published on it. Revision 2 of the usual was then published and checked, and the job was held on revision 1. The job's sheet, on words nobody checked, went with no box and no refusal.
- Revision 1 was checked and a job went out on it. Revision 2 was published and not yet checked, and the job was held on revision 1. The job's sheet, on checked words, asked for the box.

## The fix

One rule, in the file the function and the app both read (`_shared/documentBuilderSend.ts`, re-exported by `sendWords.ts`):

- `sheetWentOutChecked(readRevision, sheetUsualRevision)` is true only when the mark names the revision the sheet was published on.
- `sheetUnreadWords({ readRevision, sheetUsualRevision, usualRevision })` is the warning's words, or an empty string. A sheet behind the usual names its revision: *Your insurance agent has not checked revision 1 of your usual sheet. This job's sheet went out on that revision.* A job with no sheet published yet is asked of the usual's newest, the one it would go out on.

The screen: `FilledBuilder.tsx` hands `SheetSendCard` `sheetUnreadWords(...)` in place of `isBuilderRead(rec)`. That is two lines of the file, the import and the prop. The function: `document-builder-send` reads the sheet's `usual_revision` and refuses `unread` (409) by `sheetWentOutChecked` unless `anyway` is true.

## Deploy

`supabase functions deploy document-builder-send` from `origin/main`, on Taunya's go by the function's name. Until then the screen warns by the new rule and the function refuses by the old one. In the first case above the screen now shows the box, which is the fix. In the second the screen shows no box and the old function refuses the send with its `unread` words, so that send waits on the deploy. The same deploy carries v2.8131 and v2.8152.

## Tests

- `sheetCheck.test.ts`: a checked revision then a newer unchecked one, the reverse, no mark, a sheet never published, and the plain-words rules. The function's source is read to hold that it refuses by this rule on the sheet's own usual revision.
- `FilledBuilder.render.test.tsx`: a job that went out on unchecked revision 1 under a checked revision 2 shows the box, with the revision named, and the one press is dead until it is ticked. A job that went out on checked revision 1 under an unchecked revision 2 shows no box, and the press is live.

## Not changed

The mark is one revision, so a usual checked at revision 1 and again at revision 2 leaves a job still on revision 1 reading as unchecked. That errs toward the warning. Keeping every revision's mark would be a column of its own. The bar's own chip and press (**Mark it checked**, *N things differ from the checked usual*) speak of the usual's newest revision, which the draft on the screen sits over, and are as they were.
