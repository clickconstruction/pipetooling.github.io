# v2.5850 — Legal desk: one menu per document, and a day’s burst of documents folds on the Timeline (2026-10-25)

The second of four refinements from the owner's walk of the Legal desk (design spike, 2026-10-25). On job 891 the Evidence tab held twelve documents, each with four buttons, and the Timeline showed the twelve as a wall on the day they were uploaded.

- **One menu per row** (`LegalMatterDocumentsPanel.tsx`): a ⋯ button (`Actions for <title>`) opens a menu with what the row's state allows — *Hold from the firm…* or *Release to the firm*, *Mark sent…*, *Mark final*, *Received…*, *Not sent after all* / *Not received after all*, *Remove*. The hold reason opens in place as before; Escape closes the menu. Read only keeps no menu.
- **The fold** (`LegalTimelinePanel.tsx`): four or more consecutive documents on one day that sit there only because they were added that day (no day of their own) draw as one row, *N documents added to Evidence*, their titles in one muted line, with **Show each** and the Evidence door. A paper with its own day (v2.5846) is never folded. Presentation only; the kernel is untouched.

Tests: `LegalMatterDocumentsPanel.render.test.tsx` (the menu's items and what they do), `LegalTimelinePanel.render.test.tsx` (the fold and its opening). Guide *review a collections account before it goes to your attorney* (the Documents and Plan sections). No migration.
