## Why

Review finding 2: the inbox's Call button hung on an optional form field. PR 1 already makes the intake fall back to the number on file; this PR closes the loop on the customer's side so the number they will be called at is stated, not asked for twice, and the sent state makes the promise the banner holds the office to.

## What shipped

- **Payload** — `PortalPayload.customerPhone` (parsed tolerantly; null when the office has nothing on file). `customer-portal` has returned it since v2.3246 (`_shared/portalCustomerPhone.ts`: `customers.contact_info->>'phone'`, else the newest `jobs_ledger.customer_phone`).
- **Form** (both `RequestCard`s) — *We'll call you at* shows the number on file as a fact with **use a different number**; choosing it reveals the tel field (required, `autoComplete="tel"`) with **Use (512) 555-0142 instead** to go back. No number on file → the field, required. Submit refuses fewer than seven digits: *Please give us a number we can call you back at.* The visit card's sub-line reads *We'll call you back to confirm a time.*
- **Sent state** — *Got it, Jane — thank you. Your request is on our dispatch desk right now. We'll call you at (512) 555-0142 as soon as we can during office hours.* (bid requests: *estimating desk*). The greeting takes the first word of the statement name; a company name keeps its first word too.
- **Sample** — the sample homeowner / GC fixtures carry a number so the Settings preview shows the prefill.
- **Help** — `share-a-customer-their-portal.md` → *When they send a request* now describes the prefill and points at the Customer Waiting guide.
