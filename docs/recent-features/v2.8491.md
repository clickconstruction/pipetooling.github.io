The fourth fault from the walks of punch list #416 (`to-dos/document-builder-whats-left.md`, walk 1c, on clicktooling.com on 2026-11-24). The other three are client words and shipped as v2.8487.

**Seen:** a test company was added, asked, sent the insurance sheet by email, and its ask taken back. **Remove from the bench…** then refused: *ZZ Test trade no page, delete me stays on the bench, since they wrote to us from their portal.* The company had written nothing. **Now:** the refusal reads *‹name› stays on the bench, since we emailed them.* The rule is the same: an email that went out is history, and the company stays.
