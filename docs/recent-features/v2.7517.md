# v2.7517 — Lien desk: the run holds a notice whose paper and bills no longer agree (2026-11-16)

The owner's report of 2026-11-16: a printed lien packet stated two amounts for one job. Its cover letter and its notice form claimed one figure, and its pay codes page, its envelope divider and its invoices totalled a much smaller one. PR 1 of the fix, the protective one: the print's own check. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

- A desk item keeps the notice as drafted in `fields.notice`. The run prints that stored paper whole: `buildLienDeskRun` took `draft?.notice ?? buildLienNoticeFieldsForJob(...)`, and the cover letter fills its amounts from `fields.claimAmount`.
- The pay page, the divider (`RunNotice.amount`, the desk's `open_balance`) and the enclosed invoices read the bills as they stand.
- The desk's own pane re-reads the claim from the bills (v2.3682), so the screen showed today's figure while the run printed, and the leader's signature bound, the stored one. `fields` is rewritten only while the item is a draft.
- A notice drafted before the desk's money became *billed and still open* (v2.4969) therefore still claimed the whole job less payments, and nothing compared the two: no gate, no chip, no hold in the run.

## What changed

- **One rule.** `src/lib/jobs/lienNoticeParted.ts`: `lienNoticeParted(paper, now, source)` sets the stored paper's money beside what the desk would draw today and returns what differs, to the cent: the claim, the retainage named inside it, the split by month. `lienNoticePartedWords` says it with both figures and the gap; `lienNoticePartedHold` adds what lifts it; `runNoticePrintRefusal` is the answer every print route asks.
- **The run's builders compare.** `buildLienDeskRun` now always builds the paper as the desk would draw it today (the claim less a figure set by hand, its split, its retainage) and sets `RunNotice.parted` when a stored draft differs. `buildLienRetainageRun` does the same for the § 53.057 form's one figure. The paper the run carries is still the stored one: the hold stops it, nothing rewrites it.
- **Held from the print.** `runEnvelopeHold` holds an envelope with a parted notice inside, named by job, after the address and the $0 holds. So the packet prints no page of it, the sheet lists it in red, the card reads *Held ·* with the reason, and Docsmit is not offered it.
- **Held from the ticks and the record.** `runNoticeProblems` lists the hold first, so the checklist row is held (`LienRunRow.held`), and *Mark N printed* and *Record N mailings* leave it with the reason.
- **No way round.** The run window's *Print ›* on one copy and *Print this envelope ›* ask `runNoticePrintRefusal` and say the reason instead of printing.
- **One paper for several jobs.** `combineNoticesByProperty` carries the hold from any job on the paper and names that job (`LienNoticeParted.job`).
- **Not once printed.** As with the signature hold (v2.5533), a notice already printed is not held: the paper is out of the printer as it was, and the hold would only stop its record.

## Not in this PR

A held notice has no press that redraws it yet: the next PR adds *Redraw from the bills*, which rewrites the stored paper, clears the signature and asks for it again, and makes the desk's own signing press sign the paper it shows. Until then the route for a held notice is *Just this one ›*, whose window builds the paper from the bills. A printed notice whose bills changed gets its own line in a later PR.
