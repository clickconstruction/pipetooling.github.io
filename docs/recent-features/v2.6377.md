# v2.6377 — The Development page on a phone: Plans as cards, the strip as one row, and a faster read (2026-11-02)

The Gray Fox design spike (`to-dos/developments/SPIKE_GRAYFOX.md`; the owner, 2026-11-02: *make several changes together and then push them out at once*, then *All eight*, then on the strip *I don't like how this looks on mobile*), PR 1 of four: the phone's two overflow faults and the read's waterfall. Candidates A, I and B of the notes. No migration, no edge function.

## Change

- **Plans by part on a phone (A).** `DevelopmentPartsCard.tsx` draws each part as a card under 760 wide (`DEVELOPMENT_PHONE_QUERY` through `useMatchMedia`): the part's name, the set it builds from, *dated X · came Y* on one line, the memo as a paragraph, the jobs on it, the older sets folded and **+ New set** at the foot (`data-testid="site-plans-cards"`, each card `site-part-<id>`). The desk keeps its table (`site-plans-table`); the two shapes draw from one list of the part's pieces, built once. The page's scroll width at 375 wide is 375; the table ran it to 607.
- **The strip on a phone (I).** `DevelopmentJumpStrip.tsx`: under 760 wide the strip is one `nav.developmentPhoneStrip` of five equal `developmentPhoneLook` cells, each the look's name over one figure from `src/lib/developments/lookFigure.ts` (the number that leads the amber words when something waits on the leader, else the count's; money from ten thousand up rounds, *$186,116.84 left* → *$186k*, *$2,197,171* → *$2.2M*, smaller money drops its cents), amber when something waits, the look you are in underlined in `#2563eb`, no cap and no ordinals. The whole words stay on each cell's label and title. It stands 53 px tall where the desk's chips wrapped to about 110. The desk strip is unchanged, and the `.developmentReviewStrip.isLine` rules it wore on a phone are gone.
- **On record wraps (A).** `DevelopmentPage.tsx`: the About label on an On record line is `nowrap` on the desk only.
- **The read (B).** `fetchDevelopmentPage.ts`: the pay-speed read (office only) starts first and is awaited last; the development row and the members are read together; the prior-numbers read joins the fan-out instead of following it; `readUserNamesAndTwins` reads `is_digital_twin` beside the names and the page's data carries `twinUserIds` (PR 2 of the spike reads it for the shadow bids; nothing reads it here). `useSiteCosts.ts` drops `raw` from the `mercury_transactions` embed, which the totals never read: 1.5 MB → 308 KB on Gray Fox. `supabasePaging.ts`'s `fetchAllRowsChunkedIn` takes `{ parallel }` and runs its id chunks that many at a time, results in chunk order; `overheadPartsBucketReads.ts` and `loadCardChargeExclusions.ts` pass 4. Both kernels are in the edge functions' shared copy, so `npm run gen:edge-kernels` rewrote two files under `supabase/functions/_shared/gcKernels/` (`edgeKernels.test.ts` holds the copy equal to a regenerate); `npm run check:edge-drift` reads every function as current after it. Measured on the dev server with Playwright: the Jobs & bids table drew at about 4.1 s against 5.5 s before. Left for later, in the notes: `get_customer_pay_speed` returns every customer and a read by one customer is a migration; the history-entries read carries 279 KB of mail bodies the page shows folded.
