# v2.8452 — Bills sent: a paid line says what is not on record (2026-11-24)

Two faults of By day sent found on the live test of 2026-11-24 (punch list #422, problems 7 and 8). Both are one rule of the paid stamp, so they are one change. No migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

A paid bill's stamp has small words under it: the way, then how long the money took. On two kinds of line those words were empty, and nothing said why.

- **Ten lines read *✓ Paid* alone.** They are bills entered from Housecall Pro: the bill is marked paid and has no payment row, so there is no day and no way.
- **52 paid lines said no span.** Each was paid before its first send on record. The days are never counted backwards, so the span was left out.

## What changed

- A bill marked paid with no payment day reads *Paid*, then `day not on record` (`BILLS_SENT_PAID_NO_DAY`).
- A bill whose payment day is before its first send on record reads `before the first send on record` (`BILLS_SENT_PAID_BEFORE_SEND`) where *in 6 days* would stand.
- A line carries `payRead`: whether its payment state came from the bill's payment rows. When those rows did not read, the stamp says *Paid* alone as before, since a day that was not read is not known to be missing.
- The print and the spreadsheet's plain words follow (`billsSentPayPlain`). The spreadsheet's *Paid on* and *Days to pay* cells stay blank on these lines, as they were.
- The guide names both.

## Left for the owner

The ten Housecall Pro bills still hold no payment. Entering each payment on its job would give the line its day and its way, and would put the money on a day of By day paid. That is the office's record to make, so it is not done here.

## Tests

`billsSent.test.ts`: both words, the plain run for a print, the line whose payments did not read, and a payment with no first send to count from. The two cases that pinned an empty string were the fault as written.
