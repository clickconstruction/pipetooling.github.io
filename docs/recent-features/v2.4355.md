# v2.4355 — Lien desk: the title bar keeps one line on an iPad (2026-10-02)

**The ask (Grace, 2026-10-02).** A follow-up to the Calendar refresh (v2.4340): on a desk about 1,000 px wide the title bar wrapped **Share** onto a line of its own. Shown on the canvas https://claude.ai/artifact/M4y3KLXhYBiyHheAXG8nU8 (board 1, real screenshots at 1024 px, the suggestion built in the page); Grace: "I like your suggestions, please help me build them".

**Why it wrapped.** The title line holds the title, the five tabs (440 px), § The rules, ☎ Someone's calling ›, Share and, absolutely placed, the full-screen toggle and ×. At 1024 px the card is 992 px and the line needed about 926 px plus 119 px of padding, so Share went to a second line and the bar grew from 55 px to 90 px. Hiding the word alone (tried and reverted in v2.4340) saved 45 px, not enough.

## What shipped

- [`LienDeskModal`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskModal.tsx), region A: the title bar's spacing is now the `lienDeskTitleBar` class's column gap (0.9rem, the old 0.5rem gap plus the 0.4rem right margin each child carried, which are gone, so a wide desk looks the same). The tab buttons take their padding from `lienDeskKindTab`, and Share's word is a `lienDeskShareWord` span.
- [`index.css`](../../src/index.css): on a viewport from 641 to 1,131 px (a desk narrower than 1,100 px; a phone keeps its own layout) the gap closes to 0.5rem, the tabs' side padding to 7 px, and the word steps out of sight as a visually hidden span, so the button is still named **Share**. A viewport query on purpose: a container query on the bar would make it the containing block for the phone's fixed Share sheet.
- Guide [`share-where-our-liens-stand`](../../src/content/help/share-where-our-liens-stand.md): Share shows only its square with an arrow on a phone or a narrow screen.

## Walked

Calendar tab, measured: one line (55 px) at 1440, 1172, 1132 (the word shown), 1131, 1080, 1024 and 980 (the icon); two lines from 960 down, as before; 641 px three lines with the icon; the phone unchanged (the 40 px icon beside ×, its sheet still fixed and full width). No sideways scroll at any width. The Notices tab keeps its pile chips on their own second line, by design.
