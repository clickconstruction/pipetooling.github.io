## The change

- **Kernel** (`gcOnNotice.ts`):
  - `gcNoticeJobClaim(months, claim, perMonth)` is the run's claim: `claimSplit` with the office's per-month figures, then `timelyClaim`. `buildGcOnNotice` stores it on every job: `timelyClaim`, `staleClaim`, `timelyMonths`, `staleMonths`, `claimSplitByMonth`.
  - `gcNoticeFormClaim(job, describeMonths)` turns that into what one form says: the months, the claim, the typed split and the stale-month footnote. `approveAll` and `buildGcNoticePreview` both call it, so the two cannot drift again.
  - `summarizeGcOnNotice.claimTotal` sums `timelyClaim`. Jobs sort by the form's claim, with jobs that get no notice last.
  - `lienDeskBatches` sums each saved draft's `notice.claimAmount` (the run saves the timely figure) and falls back to the balance when a draft has none.
- **Preview** (`gcNoticePreview.ts`): claims, names and footnotes from `gcNoticeFormClaim`, and passes `retainageHeld` as the run does. `gcNoticePreviewableJobs` keeps only jobs with a timely month, so *Preview all* is the run's count (12, was 22). The side rail shows the timely claim and *+ $X for the closed months, in the letter only*.
- **Table** (`GcOnNoticeModal.tsx`, `gcOnNoticeSteps.ts`):
  - The Claim cell shows the timely claim with *+ $X in the letter only*. A job with no timely month reads *no notice · every window closed · $N still owed*.
  - `gcNoticeClaimTotals` returns `notices` (rows that get one), `rows`, `total` (timely), `letterOnly`, `leftOut` and `leftOutOwed`. The total row gains its missing cell.
  - *Affidavit by* reads *closed Jun 15* once past, and *—* on a row with no notice sent or to send.
  - Step 2's intro is rewritten without "no 30-day window".
  - The owners status reads *3 owners found (4 jobs) · press Use* (`foundOwners`).
- **Guide**: *send lien notices from the Lien desk* → step 2 describes the Claim column, the left-out rows, the totals and a preview that matches the run.
