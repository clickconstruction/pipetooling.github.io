## What shipped

- **`src/lib/bids/jobImportFromBid.ts`** — the bid import's decisions:
  - `bidImportGcOptions` — one choice per GC packet, and the bid's own GC first when it has no packet of its own.
  - `decideBidImportGc` — one GC goes on silently at its sent value; several go on as the one recorded winner; none, or more than one, asks (and with more than one, the pick records nothing).
  - `bidImportCarry` — the figure offered: the bid's agreed value when above zero, else what the GC was sent, else nothing. `bidImportCarryQuestion` and `bidImportFirstLine` — the question's words and the line item a Yes becomes.
  - `bidImportEffectiveGc` — the job's GC and its name. `bidImportWinWrite` — what a pick writes on the bid. `bidImportLabel` — "B482 · Oak Ridge".
- **`src/lib/jobs/jobImportFromEstimate.ts`** — `estimateImportFixtureRows` (the estimate's lines as line items; one blank row when it has none) and `estimateImportCustomerFields` (the name, contact and day met; only the estimate's email when there is no customer row).
- **The shell's three handlers** (`applyPrefillFromBid`, `handleWinningGcPick`, `applyPrefillFromEstimate`) ask the kernels and keep every read, both questions, the picker, the writes and the toasts, in the order they ran. `JobFormModal.tsx` is 4,239 → 4,189 lines. Behavior-neutral.
- **One quirk written down, not changed:** an agreed value that is not a readable number falls through to what was sent for the figure, but the question still calls it the bid's agreed value and a Yes records nothing on the bid. The test pins it.
- **Tests** — `jobImportFromBid.test.ts` (24) and `jobImportFromEstimate.test.ts` (5).
- The map's Stage-A tables, the shell-logic row and move 7 say what is done and what is left.
