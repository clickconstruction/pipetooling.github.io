# v2.6670 — Job Summary's printed cost breakdown counts card charges as the table does, with fuel on its own line (punch list #52 follow-up) (2026-11-08)

Punch list #52 (`to-dos/fuel-is-a-job-cost.md`, Found along the way) left this open since PR 2 (v2.4068): Jobs → Job Summary's **Print cost breakdown** (`src/lib/jobsDocuments/jobSummaryCostBreakdown.ts`) summed Parts cost as tally + other charges + supply invoices + every card charge, where the table (`Jobs.tsx`, v2.2692) takes off the card charges that are also on a supply-house invoice — the same purchase, counted once under the invoice — so the printed Parts cost read higher than the row it was printed from whenever a job carried an invoice-linked card charge. And the table has drawn ⛽ Fuel & gas under Parts since v2.2725 while the print folded it into Card charges. The owner picked the follow-up on 2026-11-07. Client only; no migration.

- **The input carries the table's two figures**: `cardChargesLinkedToInvoices` (optional, clamped to the card charges as the table clamps it) and `costLines` (the row's `clampCostLinesToCounted` lines; a $0 line is not drawn, as in the table). `JobsJobSummaryTab.tsx` passes both from the summary row it already holds, and `Jobs.tsx`'s `printJobSummaryCostBreakdown` spreads them through. The builder's other inputs and sections are unchanged, so a job with no linked charge and no cost line prints byte-for-byte as before.
- **Parts cost in the Summary table** is tally + other job charges + supply invoices + (card charges − linked), the table's `partsCost`. Under the figure, each cost line reads as the table's sub-line does: `⛽ fuel & gas $85.00`, muted and small (`.cost-line`).
- **The Parts Cost section** keeps *Card charges — $X* over every attached line (the list the office reads to find a charge), then says *$Y of the card charges is also on a supply-house invoice and is counted there, so Parts cost counts $Z of card charges* when Y > 0, then one `h3` per cost line — *⛽ Fuel & gas — $85.00* with *Card charges in this tag (the purchase's accounting label, else the bank's category). Inside Parts cost, not added to it.*
- The Person summary's card column and footer still total every attached card line by attribution, as before: that table is who spent what on the job, not the job's parts cost.

Tests (`jobSummaryCostBreakdown.test.ts`, 4 new): 100 + 300 + 50 + (200 − 120) prints $530.00 and never $650.00, with the counted-there line; a linked figure above the card charges clamps to them and none says nothing; the cost line under Parts cost and as its own `h3`, a $0 line left out; no cost lines prints no cost-line words. The print's words are not a guide's, so no plain-words sweep applies; `theme-tokenize --check` passes (the print is a light paper with its own literal greys, as before).
