- **The press**: *Line on the estimate* in the message window of a customer's own line tied to a Pipeline job (`StartEstimateLine.tsx`). It sits in the thread's `messageWindowFirst` slot after cut 4b's *Start a change order* (`useEstimateLineThreadSlots.tsx`, one line in `threadSlots.tsx`). It reads the job and the estimates its messages point at, and draws the kernel's line: its place on the job's open draft or a new one, the message's first sentence as the description, and a **Line item** name for the office. *Save the line* says the kernel's toast, *Line 2 is drafted on estimate 1044 for Helotes. The money team prices it and sends it.*
- **The chip**: a line on an estimate carries *a line on estimate 1044* (`onEstimateChip`). The message keeps its estimate, not its line, so the number and the price are the save's words alone.
- **Cut 4b's press steps aside on a Pipeline job's line** (`useChangeOrderThreadSlots.tsx`): a change order is a GC job's, so the two presses never show together. Its chip is unchanged.
- **The definer** `draft_estimate_line_from_message` saves the line and stamps the message in one call, for the office team only. It refuses training mode, a twin, a message on an estimate already, the kernel's refusals, a draft that changed since it was read, a draft with options, and a second new draft on a job with one open. It gives the office no new right over estimates.
- **The job's open estimate** is the newest draft a message on the same Pipeline job points at. #5575 read it as `estimates.job_ledger_id`, but that column is unique and is the link to the job an estimate created. The kernel's doc comment says so now (`lineFromMessage.ts`), and the loader fills the job from the messages (`lineFromMessageIo.ts`).
- **A new draft takes `job_ledger_id` only while no estimate holds that job**, so a signed one never makes a second job through `autoCreateJobGuard`.
- **Words**: `hasOptionsWords` for a draft with options. The guide is `put-a-customers-message-on-their-estimate` (new), and the change order guide points to it for a Pipeline job's message. `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` gains the definer's paragraph.
