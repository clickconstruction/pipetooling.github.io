# v2.4509 — Pipeline map: Hide map sits in the top right corner (2026-10-04)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. The owner's ask, from a screenshot at about 1,000 px wide: *Hide map* had dropped to the card's second line, after the chips, *Fit all* and *Cluster*.

## What changed

- **The header row** of [`JobsMapCard.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsMapCard.tsx) was one wrapping flex row of two groups (title · As of · Crews | chips · Fit all · Cluster · Hide map). When the second group did not fit beside the first it wrapped whole, and *Hide map* went with it. The row is now two pieces that never wrap against each other: the wrapping part (both groups, minus the button) and the *Hide map* / *Show map* button, `flex: none`, top-aligned. The button holds the corner at every width.
- **Phone.** The button takes room on the title line, and the title group no longer fit beside it (the title broke onto two lines). On a phone both groups are `display: contents`, so each control wraps on its own: pin, title and *As of* on the first line with *Hide* at the right, *Crews* and *Cluster* under them.
- The Bid Board's and the Dashboard's map cards are untouched.

## Tests

`JobsMapCard.render.test.tsx` (+1): the button is the header row's last piece, the row itself does not wrap, and the wrapping part holds the title and not the button. Seen in the dev preview at 1,500, 1,000 and 375 px.
