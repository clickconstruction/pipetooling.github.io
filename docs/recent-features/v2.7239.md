# v2.7239 — Subs Ledger: match a payment to the bank line it left on (2026-11-13)

Punch list #347, PR 5, the last of the card's plan. PR 4 gave each payment a tick set by hand. This is the tick with its proof: the Mercury transaction the money left on.

## What it is

- **Find bank lines**, a press on the by-date list at a desk. It reads Mercury's outgoing lines from just before the earliest payment shown, on demand: a year of the bank is thousands of rows, and most visits to the ledger need none of them. A line under the filters says how many were read and how many payments have one to match.
- **An offer.** A payment not yet matched gets a picker when a bank line fits: the same amount to the cent, within three days either side, and not already taken by another payment. The closest day leads. A payee that shares a real word with the sub's name breaks a tie.
- **A press matches.** Choosing a line links the payment to it and ticks it. Nothing is matched by itself, however good the fit.
- **A matched payment** shows *bank Nov 12 · PAYEE* in green with **undo**, which takes the match off and clears the tick.
- **One check is one match.** The lines of a sending are one unit, matched against what was sent, and each of them shows the same picker.
- **A tick and a match go together.** Matching ticks. Clearing the tick, by hand or by an edit to the amount or the date, clears the match.

## Code

- `src/lib/subWorkOrders/subPaymentBankMatch.ts`, new, pure: `bankLinesFromRows` (real money out, as Banking reads a row), `matchUnits`, `bankCandidates`, `suggestBankMatches` and the words.
- `src/hooks/useSubPaymentBank.ts`, new: the matches (read when the ledger opens, with each matched line's day and payee), the bank read (paged, capped at 15,000 rows), `match` and `unmatch`.
- `SubPaymentsLedger` takes `onBankChanged`, called after a match or an unmatch so the page reads its ticks again.
- Migration `20261114040000_sub_payment_bank_matches.sql` (see its note in `docs/migrations/`).

## Who can use it

The roles who read Mercury today: dev, master, assistant and controller. They are the roles who open the ledger. A login that cannot read the bank is told so by the read.

## Order of deploy

*Push right after the merge*, after the two migrations before it. Until the push nothing reads as matched, the bank read still works, and choosing a line says matching is not switched on yet.

## Checked

- The migration on a scratch Postgres 15, twice, with a scenario for each rule.
- Kernel tests (13): which rows are money out, a sending as one unit, the three-day window, the tie-break, a taken line, cents, and the words.
- Render tests (7): no bank press for a caller that cannot tick, the read's start day, an offer and its press, a matched line and its undo, a refusal, an unreadable bank, and an untick reading the matches back.
- Walked on the dev build against the live database before the push, read only: the bank read took a few seconds, more than half of the payments were offered a bank line, and more than half of those had exactly one to choose from. No line was chosen.
