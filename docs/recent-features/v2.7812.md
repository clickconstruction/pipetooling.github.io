# v2.7812 — Header: the green phone button is the same size as the buttons beside it (2026-11-18)

The owner sent a phone screenshot of the header in Job Mode on 2026-11-18 with the green phone press circled: *"I don't like how this is bigger than the rest of the buttons to the right of it on mobile"*.

## The cause

The four coloured presses share `headerActionButtonBase` in `Layout.tsx`. Its height is `calc(1rem + 1.25em)` and each icon is `1.25em`, so a press's size follows its type size. The phone is a link (`<a href="tel:…">`) and takes the page's 16 px. The other three are buttons and take the stylesheet's `button { font-size: 0.875rem }`, 14 px. Nothing on the shared style named a type size, so the tag decided it.

Measured in a headless Chrome on the live project, Job Mode on:

| Width | Phone press before | Its three neighbours | Phone press after |
|---|---|---|---|
| 390 × 844 | 36 × 36 px, icon 20 px, top 14, bottom 50 | 33.5 × 33.5 px, icon 17.5 px, top 15.25, bottom 48.75 | 33.5 × 33.5 px, icon 17.5 px, top 15.25, bottom 48.75 |
| 430 × 932 | 36 × 36 px | 33.5 × 33.5 px | 33.5 × 33.5 px |
| 1440 × 900 | 36 × 36 px beside worded presses 33.5 px tall | 33.5 px tall | 33.5 × 33.5 px |

The bell already matched the two to its right, so it did not move. The gaps were 8 px before and are 8 px after. The header keeps its height, 65 px on a phone and 61 px at a desk, and the page still has no sideways scroll.

## What changed

- `headerActionButtonBase` now carries `fontSize: '0.875rem'` (`HEADER_ACTION_BUTTON_FONT_SIZE`), beside the height it scales. Every press that reads the shared style gets the same em, whatever its tag.
- No press has a size of its own. The phone press was not given a one-off number.
- At a desk the phone press was also 2.5 px taller than the worded presses beside it. It is now their height. What sits to its left moves 2.5 px to the right.

## Tests

`Layout.headerPressSize.render.test.tsx` renders the header in Job Mode at a phone's width and at a desk's. jsdom lays nothing out, so it pins the cause: the phone press and its three neighbours each name the same type size, height, padding and icon size on themselves, and none leaves the type size to its tag. It failed twice on purpose: with the type size taken off the shared style, and with a bigger type size on the phone press alone. The drawn sizes were measured in a headless Chrome at 390, 430 and 1440 wide, read only.
