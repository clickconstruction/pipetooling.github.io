# v2.8276 — A place's small presses take a thumb (2026-11-21)

Punch list #413, the close-out. The card's build is v2.8186 to v2.8205. This PR raises the small presses its live walk measured, and marks the card for closing.

## Why

The walk on prod at a phone's width, 2026-11-21, measured every press on the place sheet. The kinds, **＋ Something else** and **Add this place** were 56 px tall, as the card asked. The presses that read as links were not: **Take another** and **Remove the photo** were 32 px tall. On a place's row, **Open the line** was 32 px, and **Change this place** had no height of its own, 16 px. **Change this place** is the way into the sheet for a saved place, and so the way to add its other photos.

## What ships

- The place sheet's link presses are 44 px tall: **Take another**, **Remove the photo**, **Remove** under each of a place's other photos, and **Remove this place** (`quiet` in `PaintedPlacePanel.tsx`).
- On a place's row in the map's window, **Open the line** and **Change this place** are 44 px tall (`PaintedMapWindow.tsx`). The row's small line grows to fit the press.
- Card #413 goes to the close column, with what is built, what is live, what was walked and what was not.

## Not changed

The map window's other link presses stay 32 px, such as **Remove this line** and **Move its corners**. They are the painted map's own (#345), and a sweep of them is its own change.

## Tests

- `PaintedPlacePanel.render.test.tsx`: the five link presses of a saved place with three photos are 44 px.
- `PaintedMapWindow.render.test.tsx`: **Open the line** on a buried place's row, and **Change this place** on a place I set, are 44 px.
