# v2.7794 — Maps: a Time press shows a place on earlier days (2026-11-18)

Punch list #395, step 1 of 7. The owner asked for the older photos of Checklist → Review's map (v2.7693) on every map, behind one press: *a time button … when someone activates it another row appears of toggles and when they leave the screen the toggle goes away*. This step builds the shared pieces and moves Review's map onto them. No other map changes yet.

## What changed on Review's map

- **Time** replaces **Older photos**, beside Satellite and Streets.
- **Time frames one spot.** It moves in close on the open dot and the photo archive draws there. With several dots and none open the row says *Press a dot to pick the spot.* and the map waits. The old press opened on the first dot, whichever that was.
- **A row of dates under the map**, oldest on the left, with **Today** held at the right end. The row is one line and slides sideways, so thirty dates do not push the page down. Two step presses walk it on a computer.
- **Today is today's photograph of the same ground.** A date and Today are one tile layer apart on one canvas, so the two can be flipped without the map moving. The sentence over the row names the day today's photograph was taken, which is rarely today.
- **The press reads the date**: *Time · Mar 2019*, and the date alone on a phone.
- **Leaving.** Time again, Satellite or Streets. The map fits its dots again.
- **A new dot keeps its date.** With a date on show, a press on another dot draws the same release there at once, then lights the photograph that was up there on the day the pressed one first went up.
- **No popup while Time is on.** A pin's popup would sit on the middle of the photograph, so the frame hands the canvas none in Time. The row names the place, and the popup is back when Time is left. Review's map has no popup; the rule is the frame's, for the cards that follow.

## Leaving the screen

Whether Time is on, its spot and its date are state of the map on screen (`useMapTime`, inside `PinsMapFrame`). Nothing writes them anywhere. Another tab, a closed window or the card's own Hide map unmounts the frame, and the map that comes back is the map of today. The dates a walk found are still kept as before, a week on the device.

## One or the other

The archive is Esri's. Google's Maps Platform terms (3.2.3 (e)) keep another provider's map off and away from a Google map, so the Google canvas is down for as long as Time is on, not only while a date shows. That also means a flip between Today and a date opens no new Google map.

## Pieces

- `src/lib/map/mapTime.ts` (tested): the spot rule, Today and the dates, the carried pick, the step, the press's words and the row's sentence.
- `src/hooks/useMapTime.ts`: Time as state. It holds its spot when a map lets go of its open dot on a stray press.
- `src/components/map/MapViewControl.tsx`: Satellite, Streets and Time. On a phone its presses are 34 px tall.
- `src/components/map/MapTimeRow.tsx`: the row. Its layout is measured on the row, not the window (`.mapTimeRow` in `src/index.css`), since a map can be one column of a wide card: the sentence sits over the dates until the row is 860 px wide.
- `src/components/map/PinsMapFrame.tsx` (tested): the provider rule, the switch, the press and the row around the two pin canvases. A card hands it dots and a height. For the maps that follow it also takes `leafletOnly` (a map that never draws on Google), `timeSpot` (a place that is not a dot), `timeDotsWords` (what on the map is not from the photograph's day), `overlay` (a card's own press on the map) and `noTime`.
- `src/components/map/PinsMapImageryCanvas.tsx`: `TaskPlacesMapCanvas`, moved and renamed, since it is now every Time map's canvas. Today's credit line now reads Esri, Vantor, as Esri's own page does.
- `olderPhotos.ts` loses the opening rule and the two presses' words that Time replaces.
