# v2.8115 — Lien desk: Record it… asks before it ticks, and skips a screen with nothing to tick (2026-11-20)

The owner's answers of 2026-11-20 to the Fable 5.2 audit of punch list #361 (the Lien desk's Months card). Three of them change one step, **Record it…** (`LienNoticeAlreadyWent`), so they ship together. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

**Grace should be told about the first one.** It changes how v2.8027, her yes of the same day, opens: the closed months are still offered, and they no longer arrive ticked.

The auditing session walks the step in a browser before this merges.

## Answer 1: on a missed notice the step opens with nothing picked and nothing ticked

### What was wrong

`LienNoticeApplyPane` started with the paper picked when the property had one (`pickedKey`) and with every offered month ticked (`ticked = new Set(open)`). That is right for a draft, whose months are the ones the desk is asking for. Since v2.8027 a missed notice with no month still due is offered its closed months, so those arrived ticked too. One typed reason cleared the warnings for all of them. The record then read every one of those months as noticed, and the affidavit reads the same rows.

### What changed

- [`lienNoticeApply.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienNoticeApply.ts) `lienApplyStartsBlank({ pile, dueMonths })`: true on the missed pile with no month still due, and nowhere else. The months offered there are closed ones the desk is not asking for, so the office says which the paper stood for.
- [`LienDesk.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDesk.tsx) `byHandPane` hands it down as `apply.startBlank`, and `LienNoticeAlreadyWent` hands it to the pane as `startBlank`.
- [`LienNoticeApplyPane.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienNoticeApplyPane.tsx): with `startBlank`, `pickedKey` starts null and `ticked` starts empty. Nothing else in the pane changed. With no paper picked the button already reads *Pick the paper that went* and is blocked. With a paper and no month the check already refuses with *Tick the month this paper stands for.*
- A draft, awaiting, ready, printed or held notice is unchanged: its own months start ticked and a lone paper starts picked.

## Answer 3: with no month to apply, the step goes straight to the by-hand record

### What was wrong

On a notice in Sent the step opened on *Which paper went?* with the notice's own paper on the list. It said *The desk is not asking this job for a month.*, and its Apply was dead. The only way on was *It is not on this list. Record it now ›*.

### What changed

- [`LienNoticeAlreadyWent.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienNoticeAlreadyWent.tsx): when `apply.months` is empty the property's papers are not read at all, and the by-hand record (`LienNoticeByHandPane`) is drawn at once, with no loading box first.
- After the read, the by-hand record is also drawn when every offered month already has a notice on this job (`apply.months.every((m) => ctx.noticedMonths.includes(m))`).
- The two cases that already led there stay: no paper on record at the property, and *It is not on this list*.

## Answer 4: the step lands inside the window however long the read takes

### What was wrong

The desk's effect on `byHandOpen` lands the pane on its last section (`goToSectionRef.current('sign')`), then lands again each time the footer grows. It stops watching after 4 s (`window.setTimeout(() => grows.disconnect(), 4000)`). With the papers read held 6 s the step grew after the watch had stopped, and its Apply ended at 1,087 px of a 900 px window.

### What changed

- `LienNoticeAlreadyWent` gains `onReady`. An effect calls it once the step has its final pane: when the read resolves or fails, and at mount when no read is made. It runs after the commit that drew the pane, so a landing reads the grown step. It is called once per read. The latest callback is kept in a ref, so a new callback on each of the desk's renders does not call it again.
- `LienDesk.tsx` `byHandPane` passes `onReady`, which lands the pane on its last section when the footer is in the pane, through the same `goToSectionRef` the effect uses.
- The 4 s watch is left as it was. It still covers growth after a paper is picked.

## Words

[`send-lien-notices-from-the-lien-desk.md`](../../src/content/help/send-lien-notices-from-the-lien-desk.md) gains two lines.
