# v2.4475 — Billed ages and the Collected chart keep today after 7 pm (2026-10-03)

The "today" half of the instant-day fixes that follow [v2.4452](v2.4452.md), like [v2.4454](v2.4454.md); [v2.4460](v2.4460.md) named the Billed age and left it for this one. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

These counted to today as the UTC date, which is tomorrow every evening after 7 pm CDT (6 pm CST). `npm run check:timezone` catches `new Date().toISOString().slice(0, 10)`; these spelled it another way:

- **Billed ages** ([`jobFormatting.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobFormatting.ts), `calendarDaysSinceDateUtc`): the start was already a Central day, but the end was `now`'s UTC date. Every bill on the Pipeline's Billed board, the printed Billed awaiting payment report, the aging chart and the GC review ([`invoiceBilling.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/invoiceBilling.ts)), and the jobs map's billed age ([`jobsMap.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobsMap.ts)) read a day older in the evening.
- **The Collected chart** ([`stagesHeaderStats.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/stagesHeaderStats.ts), `collectedByDayFromPayments`) ended its 30 days on `now.toISOString().slice(0, 10)`, and the payments read ([`fetchStagesHeaderStats.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/fetchStagesHeaderStats.ts), `collectedWindowStartYmd`) started from it. In the evening the last bar was tomorrow and empty, and today's payments sat one bar early.
- **Cutoffs a number of days back**, `new Date(Date.now() - n days).toISOString().slice(0, 10)`: the robot queue's stale bids ([`BidsRobotQueueTab.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/BidsRobotQueueTab.tsx) and the console's count, [`BidsRobotConsoleTab.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/BidsRobotConsoleTab.tsx), which must agree), the Person Desk's 90-day attendance count ([`PersonDeskRecordsSection.tsx`](../../src/components/personDesk/sections/PersonDeskRecordsSection.tsx)) and the phone dock's page suggestions ([`PhoneDockMoreSheet.tsx`](../../src/components/dispatchMode/PhoneDockMoreSheet.tsx)).
- **Time with us** ([`teamMemberReviews.ts`](../../src/lib/prospects/teamMemberReviews.ts), `formatTenure`): counted months to `now`'s UTC date, so it turned a month on the evening before the day.

## What changed

Each counts to `todayYmdInAppTz(now)` from [`dateUtils.ts`](../../src/utils/dateUtils.ts), and each cutoff is `ymdAddDays(todayYmdInAppTz(), -n)`. `calendarDaysSinceDateUtc` keeps its name and now says what it counts.

## Tests

- `jobFormatting.test.ts` and `invoiceBilling.test.ts` (+1 each): at `2026-10-03T00:30:00Z` (7:30 pm CDT on Oct 2) a bill from Sep 22 is 10 days old, not 11; at `2026-12-02T00:30:00Z` (6:30 pm CST) one from Nov 21 is 10; at noon UTC the count moves on.
- `stagesHeaderStats.test.ts` and `fetchStagesHeaderStats.test.ts` (+1 each): at 7:30 pm CDT on Oct 2 the window runs Sep 3 through Oct 2, and a payment on Oct 2 is in the last bar.
- `teamMemberReviews.test.ts` (+1): a start on Mar 15, 2024 is *2 yr 1 mo* at 7:30 pm CDT on May 14, 2026 and *2 yr 2 mo* at noon on May 15.

All 5 fail on main. The four cutoffs change one expression each.

Read only, from the worktree dev server: 83 bills are open on the Billed board, and each read a day older every evening until this ships. 46 payments fall in the Collected chart's 30 days.
