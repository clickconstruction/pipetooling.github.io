# v2.8678 — Paint a map: a test map thrown away keeps a copy, and Recently deleted puts it back (2026-11-26)

Punch list #443, row 15: one of the take-backs that need a migration, on the owner's answer of 2026-11-25 (*All eight*), its push on its own yes. One migration, one function, and the press that calls it. No edge function.

## The press nothing took back

**Throw this map away** in a painted map's window deleted the `painted_maps` row from the client. Its lines, places, links and the opens of its links went with it by their keys, and the client then swept the map's photos out of the bucket. No painted map table carries the archive trigger, so nothing was kept, and the confirm said so: *This cannot be undone.* The walk of #345 threw its map away on 2026-11-25 and it was gone.

## No fall back

When the function refuses, or the database does not hold it yet, the map stays and the refusal is shown. The client never tries the old delete in its place: that delete sweeps the photos, and the confirm had just said they stay.

## Who sees it

A dev. The door reads the signed-in role (`useAuth`), and View as a sample account is that account, so it takes the old door there. A job's own list of places stays on the old door too: the app gives a list a fixed name, so it is never a test map by its name. The window is unchanged for everyone else, by test.

## What a put-back does not restore

- A task, a roadmap task or a Materials place that carried the map lost its pointer when the map went, and does not get it back.
- A place on another map that ran along one of this map's lines loses its line.
- Nothing else: the map, its lines, places, links and link opens come back as they stood, a place finds its photo, and a link opens at its old address.

## The photos

The door that keeps a copy leaves the map's photos in the bucket. While the map is gone, a file under its id is listed and removed by its uploader and the office and read by nobody else (`20261116234500_painted_map_photos_after_the_map.sql`). When the archive's 90-day purge takes the copy, nothing sweeps the photos: they stay until their uploader or the office removes them, and no screen lists them.
