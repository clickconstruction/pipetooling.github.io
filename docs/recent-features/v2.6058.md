# v2.6058 — Where files go: a bid's folder by the bid pattern, and folders made as needed (punch list #211, PR 3) (2026-10-29)

Punch list #211, the last of three PRs. PR 1 (v2.6056) stored `drive_bid_folder_pattern` and `drive_folders_made` without honoring them; PR 2 (v2.6057) let the office set them on Documents → Where files go. This PR makes the two settings do what the tab says.

## What changed

- **`drive-intake`** names a bid's folder by the office's bid pattern: `readDriveSettings` for the root and the pattern, `bidFolderName(settings.bidPattern, facts)` over the bid's project name, number, customer (read from `customers` only when the pattern asks for it), address and the year it was made in the app's calendar; the project name alone as it ships, which equals the name every bid folder has today. A bid whose `drive_link` is already a Drive folder keeps it (`driveFolderIdFromUrl`), so a changed pattern never makes a second folder for a bid that has one; the audit note reads *reused* for it.
- **`job-drive-folders`** honors `drive_folders_made`: when the choice is as-needed and the job had no folder of its own, the press finds or makes the job's folder (its bid's, found under the root, or made there) and writes `google_drive_link` alone; the answer carries `folders_made` and names `pictures` and `plans` with `source: 'later'` and no url. A press on a job that already has its folder (`kept`) fills in what is missing inside either way, since the office pressed it on that job. Up front is unchanged.
- **`_shared/driveJobFolder.ts` → `photoFolderFor`** makes Customer Pictures under a Pipeline job's folder the first time a photo lands when the choice is as-needed and the job has a folder but no pictures link, and writes `job_pictures_link` on the job (only while it is still empty), so the next photo finds it. Up front, a job with a folder and no pictures link keeps landing in Team only › Photos as before. `photo-save` (the box photos and the Messages photos) and `drive-link` (Save to Drive for a photo) share the function.
- **The client** (`src/lib/jobs/jobDriveFolders.ts`): `JobDriveFolderSource` gains `later`, the answer gains `foldersMade`, `foldersMadeLater` and the words under the press read *The job's folder is ready under Jobs. The folders inside are made as something first lands in them.*; the Deliveries card links only the folders that exist. The guide *set where a job's files go* says what the as-needed choice does; `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` → drive-intake, job-drive-folders, photo-save; `docs/DRIVE_INTAKE_SETUP.md`; the Glossary.

## Decisions

- **As-needed means the job's folder alone, not nothing.** The job's folder is what every sweep and the office's doors key on (`google_drive_link`); the five inside are cheap to make when something first lands. Plans and Sent by us have no sweep that lands in them for a Pipeline job, so under as-needed they are made by the press on the job's window (v2.6055's *fills in what is missing*) or by hand.
- **A press on a job that has its folder fills in everything, whatever the choice.** The batch never sends such a job; a person pressed it on that job, which is the ask for the folders now.
- **The pictures link is written only while it is empty**, so two photos landing at once cannot leave the job pointing at two folders.

## Deploy

From the main checkout after the merge, `npm run check:edge-drift` before and after: `supabase functions deploy drive-intake`, `supabase functions deploy job-drive-folders`, and the two importers of `_shared/driveJobFolder.ts`, `supabase functions deploy photo-save` and `supabase functions deploy drive-link`, together with PR 1's twelve (`docs/recent-features/v2.6056.md` → Deploy), which overlap on four of them. No migration.
