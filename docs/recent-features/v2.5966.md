# v2.5966 — A batch keeps going past a held row and names it (2026-10-27)

Punch list #184, sweep A, PR 2 of the plan in `to-dos/consistency-sweeps/README.md` → *Sweep A*: the eleven batch buttons that still stopped at the first bad row, or went on and said nothing about who. The rule (`CLAUDE.md` → *Do what is ready, never all or nothing*): a batch acts on what is ready, keeps going past a row that fails, lists what it held or what failed with the reason and a door, and its button says exactly what it will do, dead only when nothing is ready. PR 1 (v2.5909) gave the buttons their count; this PR gives the eleven their held lists. Client only: no migration, no edge function change.

## The shared kernel

`src/lib/batchWords.ts` gains two pieces, tested in `batchWords.test.ts`:

- `batchOutcomeWords({ verb, past, done, failed, noun? })` → `{ line, head, tone }` for after the press: *Generated 4 reports. 2 did not generate.* as the toast, and *2 reports did not generate. They stay here with the reason.* as the head over the rows that did not take (*Nothing generated…* and an error tone when none took). Every sentence passes `plainWordsFailures`.
- `batchWords`' foot drops a trailing preposition from the verb, so *Send to 3 · 1 has no email ▸* reads *The 3 ready ones send now.*, not *send to now*.

## Site 7: why the group is the free people

The window inserts one row per person sharing a `shared_block_group_id`, in one atomic insert so a failure never leaves half a crew. The link is what keeps the crew's times and note together afterwards (`docs/GLOSSARY.md` → *job_schedule_blocks*); it does not need the whole pick. Legs are unlinked and removed one by one elsewhere, and the Dispatch board's linked copy already skips a person whose day overlaps. So the group is made of the free people and the insert stays atomic. A held person added later, once their day is clear, is their own block; the Dispatch board's crew window links them.

## Site 2: a half-applied row comes back as its missing half

An invoice that was made but whose payments did not link comes back as those links, and a split whose new payments went in but whose rollup was not removed comes back as the removal alone. A retry never writes a row twice. Re-picking the file, the old door, did not cover the second case: the planner matches the inserted rows by amount and plans nothing for the orphaned rollup.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **The Rules count is the tab's, not the server's.** It reads the pending suggestions the tab loaded, so a suggestion someone else made since can move the count by a few. The press still runs the preflight with a fresh read, so what it inserts is exact; a count that reads zero while the server would find matches stays dead until the pending list reloads.
- **Two sites hold before the press, nine name what failed after it.** The share (no email) and the job Schedule window (an overlap) know a row will not go before anything is written, so they hold it in the button's count. The other sites cannot know a refusal until the database says so; their button counts the rows and the list comes after.
- **The doors are the nearest real fix.** An email is changed only on the person's desk (Change… is the dev's); an overlap is fixed on the other job; a refused row is retried as it is. Where the reason is a permission (*You don't have permission to …*), the retry fails the same way and says so again: that row needs someone with the right, and the list says which row.
- **Left on purpose:** Settings → Dashboard's *Pin for N users* still keeps only the last error of its loop; it was not one of the eleven and goes with the next pass on that tab. The session card for this branch was not dropped, since this PR was built in a worktree that writes nowhere else.
