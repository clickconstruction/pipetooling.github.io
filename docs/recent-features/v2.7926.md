# v2.7926 — A primary reads the test papers of their own jobs (2026-11-19)

Punch list #400, PR 4 of 5 (`to-dos/primary-job-doors.md`). One migration, pushed after the merge. No client change and no edge function.

## Before

- A job's certified test paper (`job_test_reports`) and its PDF in the `job-test-reports` bucket opened to any primary, on any job. The table was made after the fences that hold a primary to their own jobs, and never got one.
- Documents shows a primary their own jobs only, so no screen of theirs listed another job's paper. The hole was at the API.

## What changed

- **One clause** (`20261119103000_primary_test_papers.sql`): the primary leg of `can_reach_job_for_test_report` asks `primary_can_access_job`, the rule every other job child table asks. The table's four policies and the bucket's read policy all ask that function, so the paper and its PDF follow.
- **The bed.** `npm run test:pg:primary-test-papers` reads the gate, the table and the bucket as five people, on the whole schema.

## What it leaves alone

- Dev, master and the assistant-like roles read and write as before.
- A primary still reads the test papers of the jobs they account-manage. A primary never wrote one.
- Family 7 of the card, the schedule share reads, is built by the session that fixes who may call them.

## Deploy

Merge, then `supabase db push`. Safe on either side of the client deploy.
