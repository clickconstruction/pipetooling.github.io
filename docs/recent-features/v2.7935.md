# v2.7935 — Permits: a row says when a plumbing permit at its address is not counted (2026-11-19)

[v2.7922](./v2.7922.md) stopped another tenant's plumbing permit from closing a San Antonio job. It also stopped showing that permit at all. This puts it back as a note, so the skimmer sees it and judges.

## What the office sees

On a row with no plumbing permit of its own, when one or more sit at its street address and do not count:

- **Ledger, the Plumber cell:** under **none yet**, a muted line. *at this address, not counted: Quill Plumbing, Nov 4, and 2 more*.
- **Skim card, under Who:** the long form, with the work the permit names. *A plumbing permit at this address lists Quill Plumbing, Nov 4, for Water heater, suite 210. It is not counted as this job's. 2 more are at this address.*
- **The Decide sheet, the selected pin on the map and The building:** the short line.

The row stays open. If the permit is this job's plumber, **Decide…** then **Has a plumber** closes it by hand.

The work is what the city wrote on the permit. In San Antonio that is often the telling part: a suite number, *annual gas test*, *backflow*, a water heater.

## Where it shows

Only in San Antonio. A plumbing permit is left uncounted by the same test that counts one (`permit_address_plumbing_counts`), and in the other four cities a permit at the address always counts.

## How it is read

`research_permits_v3` is `research_permits_v2` with four columns more ([migration note](../migrations/20261119073000_permit_plumbing_nearby.md)). It reads v2 and joins them on, so nothing live was replaced. `loadResearchPermits` asks for v3 and falls back to v2 until the migration is pushed.

`nearbyPlumbingWords` (`permitResearch.ts`) writes both forms. `plumberWords` carries the short one as the line under **none yet**, which is how the ledger, the Decide sheet and The building get it with no change of their own.

## Not in this change

- The line names the newest uncounted permit and counts the rest. The others are on **The building**, which already lists every permit at the address.
- One applicant's two jobs at one address still tie to each other.
