# v2.6267 — Every health line ends in the press, or says there is none (2026-11-01)

The owner's measure (2026-11-01, the read-and-judge session on the app's "thoughtless decisions"): a status line about the app's own health should move the decision off the person. It says what the app did, what it will do by itself, and the one press if a person is needed, or that there is none. The outbox already read that way (*Red means this needs you, and a resend is the app's job*, v2.6074); this PR brings the other health lines under the same rule. Words and one small kernel; no new read.

- **The health strip** (`src/lib/appHealth.ts`, `appHealthStripWords`): each state gains a next sentence after its lead, between the kept-page line and a dev's runbook line. Slow: *It goes the moment a read lands. Nothing to press.* Unreachable: *Check your signal, then press Retry to open the page again.* Offline: *Clock presses, notes and reports wait on the phone and go out when the signal is back.* The leads are the owner's words and are unchanged. `appHealth.test.ts` holds the lines in every state and the new sentences under `plainWordsFailures`; `AppHealthStrip.render.test.tsx` reads the strip's text with them.
- **The office's round card** (`src/lib/quickfill/roundClose.ts`, `src/components/dashboard/DashboardOfficeRoundCard.tsx`): `roundStartStation(events, stations)` picks the station today's round starts on, the one marked longest ago among those marked at all in the events read (the round's order breaks a tie; with no mark on any, the first station), and `roundStartWords` writes the sentence: *The round did not run Fri, Oct 30. Today's starts on Billed Awaiting Payment, marked last Mon, Oct 27.*, or *The round has not started yet. Today's starts on …* before a round. **Start today's round** (`ROUND_START_PRESS`) opens that station through `quickfillStationHref`; a weekend keeps *No round is due on the weekend.* and the Quickfill link. The card reads its stations from `QUICKFILL_SECTIONS` with the personal ones (`QUICKFILL_PERSONAL_SECTIONS`) and the afternoon ones (`QUICKFILL_AFTERNOON_SECTIONS`, which a morning round never starts on) left out. On a weekend after a miss the sentence reads *The next round starts on …* and the press *Start the round*. `roundClose.test.ts` holds the pick, the tie, the no-mark case and every sentence.
- **Robots have work waiting** (`src/lib/dashboardNeedsYou.ts`, `robot-backlog`): the line ends *The next round takes the top row by itself while the Mac is awake. Nothing to press.* (the rounds' rule since v2.5753); when a matrix has gone quiet or a request is a week old the title reads *A robot needs a person* and the detail ends *The Console row says what it needs.* `dashboardNeedsYou.test.ts` holds both.
- **Texts not delivered** (`src/lib/messages/weekStrip.ts`): the tile's sub reads *Call them, or send it as an email.* while it has a count, the two presses the Went out rows carry (`STRIP_WORDS.notDeliveredSub`); `weekStrip.test.ts` and `WeekStrip.render.test.tsx` read it.
- **The copy was not kept** (`SentCopyRows.tsx`, `AiaG702G703History.tsx`): the span reads *The copy was not kept. The send went out, and this row is the record.*, so the amber words say what happened and that nothing is owed. Filing stays best effort with no retry (`docs/SENT_COPIES.md`), so the sentence promises none.
- **Guides**: *know what to do when the app is slow* quotes the three new sentences; *find a copy of what we sent*, *run the robots from Claude desktop*, *answer everyone waiting on us from one page* and *run the office day with Quickfill* quote theirs.

Not in this PR, the same rule's other rows from the canvas: the Tally banner and the People waiting line drawing their first row, the Bridge's call recording itself, the Monday mail's Friday verdict, and the helper's empty day naming dispatch (that one needs the dispatch phone read, `APP_SETTINGS_KEY_FIELD_DISPATCH_PHONE`, on the My Schedule card).
