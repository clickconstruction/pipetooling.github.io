# v2.6546 — Mark a theme on an HR entry or a piece of evidence as you write it, on a line that stands, or on several lines at once (2026-11-05)

Punch list #269, PR T2. The catalog of themes (v2.6545) had nothing to mark with it. A mark is cheapest at the moment the entry or the evidence is written, when the person knows why it fits and how it proves the theme, and a record that already stands is marked line by line or many lines at once. No migration: the tables are v2.6545's.

## What changed

- `supabase/functions/_shared/legalThemes.ts`: `MarkDraft` (the themes picked, why, how it proves them), `markDraftProblem`, `toggleThemeId`, `markTargetOfLine` (a Timeline line's thing by its `ref.table`: an HR entry, a piece of evidence, a matter document or event; a license or a paper is none), `marksOnTarget`, `themeCountsOnLines`, `linesWithTheme`, `tickedWords` and `markedWords` (*Dishonesty marked on 3 lines. 1 was marked already.*). `legalThemesIo.ts`: `addMarks` (one row a theme a thing, a thing already marked counted as *already*, a person's own mark kept as made), `removeMark` (stamped with the reason) and `keepMark`.
- `src/components/legal/ThemeMarkPicker.tsx`: the strip every composer shows — the standing themes as chips, then *why it fits* and *proves it by* once one is picked; a theme the thing already carries is pressed and still; with no theme on the catalog it says where the first is named.
- `PersonFilePanel.tsx`: the picker in the entry composer and the Add evidence window (the marks written after the row, named in the toast); on the Timeline a tick box on every markable line, the **bulk bar** once one is ticked (*3 lines ticked*, the picker, *Mark 3 lines* from `countedLabel`, Untick), the **Themes row** with counts that filters the line, and on each line its theme chips (why and grade on the tip, **✕** asks the reason, a proposed mark dashed with **Keep**) and **+ theme** opening the picker in place.
- The guide *keep private employee files* gains *Mark a theme on the record*; `HR_FILES.md` → Themes on the record carries the pickers; the card's T2 row is split, the matter's composers becoming T2b.
