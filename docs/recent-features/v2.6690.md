# v2.6690 — Customer means everything to or from the customer: the filter widened, the box's unnumbered rule on the same set (punch list #125, PR 3) (2026-11-08)

Punch list #125 (`to-dos/pipeline-customer-conversation/README.md`), PR 3 of four, stacked on PR 2 (v2.6689): the owner took the card's recommendation on the Customer filter on 2026-11-08. No migration, no edge function.

## What changed

- **One set** `CUSTOMER_EVENT_TYPES` and `isCustomerEvent` in `src/lib/jobActivityEvent.ts` (+1 test): the events the customer got or did — `invoice_sent`, `invoice_stripe_email_sent`, `invoice_resent`, `contract_sent`, `contract_shared`, `contract_viewed`, `contract_signed`, `lien_release_signature_requested`, `lien_release_sent`, `demand_letter_sent`, `lien_notice_sent`, `lien_courtesy_sent`, `hazmat_notice_emailed`, `payment_promise`. Every one is a Billing event. Left out on purpose, as the card says: `invoice_created` and `invoice_billed` (ours until sent), `contract_voided` and `lien_release_voided` (ours unless the void was sent), `lien_release_issued` and `lien_release_signed` (our side), `payment_added` (the payment itself, the card's recommendation), and `contract_reminded` (the cron's own sends, which the box already kept out).
- **The filter** `jobActivityLineMatches` in `src/lib/jobs/jobActivityLine.ts` (+1 test): **Customer** is the customer's messages (`isCustomerSide`) and the events in the set; the events keep their Billing bucket, so Customer is a lens across the buckets. `countJobActivityLines` counts Customer in its own pass, the other five staying disjoint; a line's number was given before any filter, so a signed contract is the same line under Billing and under Customer.
- **The box** `jobActivityBoxFeed.ts` (+2 tests) reads the same set in place of its own `SENT_EVENT_TYPES` (gone): the contract sent, shared, opened and signed, the release asked for and sent, the courtesy and hazmat notices and the customer's word on paying come in unnumbered beside the bill lines; `sentTagOf` gives the tag its word (*Sent*, or *Opened*, *Signed*, *Said* for what the customer did), `sentLineBody` their words (*Contract sent*, *Contract sent again*, *Contract link shared*, *Contract opened by the customer*, *Contract signed*, *Signed contract on file*, *Release sent to sign*, *Lien release sent*, *Courtesy notice sent*, *Hazmat notice emailed*, the promise's own summary), and what the customer did names *The customer* where the row has no actor. `JobsStagesActivityBox.tsx` draws the tag's word.
- **Words**: guide *open a job's activity full screen*'s Filter pills line; the Pipeline map's row for the box.

## Read closer than the card

- **Customer is a lens, not a bucket.** The card asked whether the events should stay under Billing too; the recommendation the owner took keeps them there. So Customer's count can exceed what the other pills add up to, as All already could.
- **The box gains lines on some jobs.** A job whose contract was sent, opened and signed shows three more unnumbered lines in the box than before. Three unnumbered lines take no number, so *check note 3* still never moves; the box scrolls as it did.
