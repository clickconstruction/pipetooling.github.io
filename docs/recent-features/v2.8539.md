# v2.8539 — Bid Board: the missing-address sheet says why, the day, and offers Try again (2026-11-24)

Punch list #441, the one leftover of #408. v2.8479 gave the Map page's list the reason, the day and **Try again** for an address no map service could place. The Bid Board's own sheet, the door behind *N bids have no map location yet* since v2.3205, still said only that the map could not find the address. It now reads the same three, from the same kernel.

## What changed

- **The row says why and when.** Under the address box of a bid whose address could not be placed, the row reads the map services' reason, then *Checked Nov 24. The app asks again by itself on Dec 24.* Each day is drawn as a value. The row's own words above them stay.
- **Try again sits beside Save.** It makes one call to `geocode-address-batch` for the bid's saved address with `retry: true`, through `tryPlaceAgain`, the Map page list's call. While it asks, the press reads *Asking the map services…* and is dead.
  - Placed: the row says *Placed. Its pin is on the map.*, the saved points are read again, and the bid stays in the sheet as *Placed ✓*, as a saved one does.
  - Still no match: the row says to fix the address in the box and save, and its day moves to the day the function answered with.
  - Nothing was kept, or the ask failed: the Map page list's own words, with the reason.
- **Which rows get it.** Every bid with an address and no pin, once the map has stopped looking. A row with no kept miss has no reason or day line and still offers the press. A bid with no address keeps *No address on the bid*, and a row the map is still looking for keeps *Looking for this address on the map…*. Neither has the press.
- **No second read.** `useAddressGeocodeCoords` already read `address_geocode_misses` to decide what to ask. It now hands those rows back as `misses`, adds the ones the function kept while it answered (a failure that carries `checked_at`, v2.8484), and has `reread` for the saved points after a placed **Try again**. The hook's other callers read neither.

## Kernels

- `src/lib/map/unplacedMemory.ts`: `tryAgainWords` takes where the address is fixed, the record or the row's own box, and `keptMissesInAnswer` reads the misses a batch answer says were kept.
- `src/lib/bids/bidBoardMissingAddresses.ts`: a row carries its miss, and `missingAddressMemory` gives the lines under the box, or null where the row offers no **Try again**.

## What differed from the card

- The card's plan gives **Try again** to *a row with a miss*. It is offered on every unplaced row the map is done looking for, with or without a kept miss, as the Map page's list does. A miss is not kept when Google or the Census did not answer, and that row is the one a second ask helps most.
- The card says the sheet reads the standing miss for each row. The hook under the Bid Board's map had already read them, so the sheet reads nothing of its own.
- No migration and no edge function change, as the card said.
