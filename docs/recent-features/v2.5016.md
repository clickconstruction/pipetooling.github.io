# v2.5016 — Robots → Audits: skip a slate for now (2026-10-09)

Client only. No migration, no edge function. This builds the owner's call of 2026-10-09 on *Robots → Audits: can a slate be parked?*. The owner said yes. The queue (v2.4234, punch list #63) lists a re-bid slate's rows under Up next after the rows with questions. The mock-up left *skip these 16 for now* undrawn until the owner said whether a slate can be parked. As asked, this builds only the door, the fold and *Bring it back*.

## The change

- **The kernel** (`src/lib/bids/auditQueue.ts`):
  - `slateKey` is a backtest slate's label, *slate Aug 31* or *re-bid Sep 5*. A shadow has none.
  - `buildAuditQueue` takes the parked keys. A parked slate's workable rows, questions and all, leave Up next and the *Now n of m* count. They gather, in stake order, in `parked`.
  - `upNextSlates` names the slates left in Up next with their counts, in the order they first appear.
  - *Finish audit → next* reads the first unparked row.
- **The lens** (`BidsAuditsTab.tsx`):
  - Up next keeps its stake order. A line under its head names each slate it holds, *slate Aug 31 · 2*, with a *Skip this slate* door.
  - A *Parked* fold sits after *Opens when you send*. Folded, it names its slates. Open, each slate has its count and *Bring it back*, and its rows can still be opened.
  - The open-card pick treats a parked row as not workable, so parking the slate of the card open now opens the next card outside it.
- **Where it is kept:** `useParkedAuditSlates` keeps the parked keys in this device's storage, per signed-in person, as *Past values* keeps its switch. A parked slate is a "not now" in one person's queue. Nothing about the audits changes, and another person still sees the slate.
