# v2.6265 — A report with photos waits on the phone (punch list #229, PR 6 of six; #189's PR 7) (2026-11-01)

Punch list #229, the last PR, on PR 1's hook (v2.6255). Until now a report with photos never queued: Save waited while a shot was on its way, and with no signal the form stayed open with the shot and Retry, which trapped the tech on a bad site. Punch list #189 planned this as its PR 7, held for #163's field report through the queue (v2.6100), which has shipped. No migration, no edge function; the outbox's IndexedDB goes to version 2.

## What changed

- **`lib/offline/keptPhotos.ts`** (new, tested): the shrunk bytes kept on the device in the outbox's own database, a `photos` store beside the presses, by photo id (`outboxStore.ts` opens version 2 and makes both stores; a device with no IndexedDB keeps them in memory for the page's life, as the presses are). `sendKeptPhotos` sends a kept report's unsent photos in order, each marked `sent` on the press as it lands so a later try never sends it twice; a photo whose bytes are gone is taken off the press and the report still goes; the first send that fails stops with its error. `sweep` drops bytes kept over seven days, once a sign-in, so a press a person dropped leaves nothing behind.
- **The press** (`outbox.ts`): `FieldReportPayload.photos`, one `KeptReportPhoto` a shot (its row's facts and whether it is already in the bucket). The bar's waiting line gains *1 photo with it* / *3 photos with them* (`photosWaitingLine`, `keptPhotoCount`).
- **The replay** (`useOutboxReplay.ts`): before landing a field report with unsent photos it sends them (`sendKept`, injectable); a photo that does not send counts as the press's failed try and the report waits with it. **`afterLanding`** ties the sent photos to the landed report (`attachReportPhotos`), writes the checklist answers (`answerQuestionsWithReportPhotos`, PR 6 of #189), drops the bytes, then sends the two notifications, so the email carries the photos.
- **The hook** (`useReportPhotos`): a shot is kept on the device as soon as it is shrunk, sent, and its bytes dropped once sent. A send lost to the signal (`ReportPhotoSendError` with kind `network`, the class `sendReportPhoto` now throws, or the browser saying offline) leaves the tile **kept** (*Kept on the phone. It goes with the report.*), no Retry; any other failure keeps Retry as before. Kept shots ride with the report (`riding`, `reportSaveWords` counts them), `queuedPhotos` is the press's list, `markQueued` tells the hook the press owns them so a close drops nothing, and Remove or a close without saving drops the bytes.
- **The forms** (`AdditionalReportModal`, `NewReportModal`): the press carries the riding photos; with a kept shot it goes to the queue at once (`forceQueue`), the toast reads *Kept on the phone. It goes out when the signal is back.* and the form closes; with every shot sent and the signal up, the live path attaches as before. The `allowed: false` rule is gone.
- **Guides**: *take photos for a report* gains **With no signal**; *keep working when the signal drops* moves the field report with photos from *What needs a signal* to *What waits on the phone*. The Glossary's **Report photos** entry gains its line in the card's closing docs PR, since five PRs amending one line would conflict in turn.
- **Tests**: `keptPhotos.test.ts` (new, 5), `outbox.test.ts` (+2), `afterLanding.test.ts` (+1), `useOutboxReplay.test.ts` (+3); `AdditionalReportModal.camera.render.test.tsx`'s no-signal case now proves the kept tile, Save's words, the press with the photo's facts and nothing sent, attached or thrown away.

## Decisions

- **The shrunk copy waits, never the original.** About 300 KB a shot, so ten shots are 3 MB on the phone, well inside Safari's quota; the original stays in the camera roll.
- **The photos go before the report, on the same press.** The report's row needs no photo, but the attach needs the rows, and one press keeps the order and the single try count; a photo that fails to send fails the press's try as the report's own send would.
- **A kept photo whose bytes are gone is dropped, not the report.** A page reload on a device with no database loses the memory copy; the report's words still matter more than its pictures.
- **A Turnaway and the Edit form keep today's rule.** Neither goes through the outbox (a Turnaway alerts Dispatch at once; an edit is an update, which the queue does not carry), so a shot there with no signal fails with Retry as before.
