# v2.7318 — Lien desk: the scrolled strip says less, and every chip on it is a press (2026-11-15)

The owner, on JP372's notice pane on 2026-11-15, of the strip that pins to the top once the gates scroll away: "I don't like seeing this here because it is non actionable; right now this is not the best we can do." The strip read *✗ Can't go out yet · 1 · Owner of record: Missing · Sep · Find the owner, then send the Sep notice — tomorrow. · Claim $…*, every piece a `<span>`: the verdict repeated the footer's *Don't send yet*, the blocker chip repeated the verdict, the next-step chip repeated the blocker, and none of them did anything when pressed. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What changed

- **[`src/lib/jobs/lienStripChips.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienStripChips.ts)** (new): `lienStripChips(input)` → the chips the strip draws, each `{ key, words, tone, act, title }`. From the gates, the months' words, the timeline's next step, the claim, the wording line and the sent fold. The rules: with blockers, one red chip per blocking gate in `lienGateShortWords`' own words with a capital (*Owner of record missing ›*, *No GC on the job ›*, *No month picked ›*) and no separate *Can't go out yet* — the red chips are the verdict; with none, one green *Ready to go out ›* whose act is `ready`; a check (the kind unknown) is an amber chip to its gate; the next-step chip is dropped when a notice is blocked and the next step is that notice (its *Find the owner, then send…* says what the owner chip says) and kept when the next step is another paper or the notice has nothing blocking it (it adds the day); the months are a quiet press unless the months gate is already a red chip; the claim is a quiet press, amber and *set by hand* when corrected; the wording line is an amber press; a sent notice's fold is one grey *Sent Oct 14 ›* whose act is `top`. Every chip ends in ›. Tests: each rule, and the kernel's own words against `plainWordsFailures`.
- **`LienDesk.tsx`**: the strip draws the chips as `<button class="lienStripChip" data-lien-strip-chip={key} data-tone={tone}>` with the `chip()` look, and `pressStripChip(act)` wires each to the door the gate box already has — `gate:owner` opens `LienPaperPropertyWindow` at the owner (`setPaneFix`, the affidavit pane's `onFixProperty` door) for the office and rings the gate for anyone else, `gate:gc` opens Edit Job, `gate:kind` and `gate:billed` ring their gate (`pickGate`, whose cell scrolls into view and whose kind row holds the switch), `gate:months` and `months` scroll to the month grid, `claim` to the claim box, `wording` to the envelope line, `next` to the path section (`goToSection('path')`), `ready` unfolds a phone's footer, scrolls the pane to its end and focuses the footer's primary, `top` scrolls to the gates. `data-lien-desk-strip`, `data-lien-desk-strip-next` and `data-lien-strip-sent` stay where they were. *Show gates ▴* is unchanged.
- **`src/index.css`**: `.lienStripChip` (no border, pointer, inherited font) underlines on hover and focus so the chip reads as a door.
- **`LienDesk.render.test.tsx`**: on job 650 (owner missing, kind unknown) the scrolled strip is four presses — *Owner of record missing ›* red, *Property kind unknown ›* amber, *Jun + Jul + Aug ›* and *Claim $33,500 ›* quiet — with no *Can't go out yet* and no next chip, and pressing the owner chip opens `lien-paper-property-window`; with the owner on file the strip opens on *Ready to go out ›*, has no gate chip, and keeps the next chip with its day.
- Guide *send lien notices from the Lien desk*: the strip's paragraph. [`docs/LIEN_DESK_ARCHITECTURE.md`](../LIEN_DESK_ARCHITECTURE.md): the dated note.

## Not changed

- The gate box and its doors, the footer and its *Go to gate N ▴*, the Next on the path band and the pane's stacked heads: the strip presses what they already have. The affidavit and retainage panes have no scrolled strip.
