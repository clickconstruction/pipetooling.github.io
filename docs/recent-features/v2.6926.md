# v2.6926 — Permits: the owner of record on a Loop and along an interstate (2026-11-11)

Punch list #292, after v2.6912 went live. Sixteen live permits across the four newer cities were asked through the deployed `permit-lookup`: twelve found their owner. Of the four that did not, two had no parcel under the address at the county and no point from the city, which no spelling fixes. The other two were spellings:

- **LOOP**: Travis keeps the suffix in its own column (`situs_street` "LEVANDER", suffix "LOOP"), so asking for the name "LEVANDER LOOP" found nothing. `LOOP` (and `TRCE`, `TRACE`, `VW`, `HTS`, `GLN`) joined the suffixes `streetParts` and `bareStreet` drop.
- **Interstates**: Guadalupe writes "I H 10 W"; addresses write "IH 10 W", "I-35" or "Interstate Highway 35". `interstateOf` reads every form, `streetParts` keeps the name as "IH n", `nameSpellings` asks all five forms, and `bareStreet` now drops a trailing side ("W") as well as a leading one. Travis's question asks every spelling too, not only the name.
- **Kept answers**: a "no parcel" asked with a question today's code no longer asks (`askedOtherQuestion`) is asked again instead of waiting out its 30 days, so the misses above clear on their own.

Both shapes were asked of the live layers through the new code and found their owner. Tests: `permitParcels.test.ts`.

Deploy: `permit-lookup` after the merge. No migration.
