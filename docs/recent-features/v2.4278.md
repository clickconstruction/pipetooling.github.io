# v2.4278 — Our lien waiver to the GC, PR 4: the GC's room shows every waiver, one pair per bill (2026-09-30)

No migration; one edge function redeploy (`customer-portal`). PR 4 of the train (PR 1 [v2.4274](v2.4274.md), PR 2 [v2.4275](v2.4275.md), PR 3 [v2.4276](v2.4276.md)). Mock-up board 3 of artifact `R9VKFzFEwMcY7oUb4ZyG3v`.

## What changed

- **Kernel** [`_shared/portalWaivers.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/portalWaivers.ts) `buildPortalWaivers` (tested from vitest, [`portalWaivers.test.ts`](../../src/lib/portal/portalWaivers.test.ts)): one row per sent bill the viewer pays (the shared who-pays rule, `effectiveInvoiceParty` / `payerCustomerId`), with the two halves — conditional, unconditional — each *none · signing · signed · sent* from the job's live `job_lien_releases` rows covering the bill (the newest, furthest-along decides), the bill's place (*Bill 2 of 3*), whether it is paid (status, or the payments on it reach its amount) and whether it is the job's last bill. Rows only for jobs under a GC, or bills that already carry a waiver — a homeowner's page shows nothing new.
- **`customer-portal`** reuses the sent bills and payments it already reads for *Your payments*, reads the jobs' releases (`issued · awaiting_signature · signed`, unvoided), and returns `waivers` with each signed PDF as a **signed URL good for an hour** from the private `lien-release-documents` bucket (`createSignedUrl`, the legal portal's pattern); the storage path itself never leaves the function. A read failure leaves the section off, never the page.
- **The page** ([`portalPayload.ts`](../../src/lib/portal/portalPayload.ts) parses `waivers` tolerantly — `[]` from a function without it; [`PortalWaiversSection`](../../src/components/portal/PortalWaiversSection.tsx) under *Your payments*): *Lien waivers*, a table — Bill · Amount · Conditional · Unconditional. A half is a dated PDF pill (*⤓ Sep 30*, *· final* on the last bill), *on its way* (minted, the leader signing — or the conditional not yet sent on an open bill), or the dashed *when your check clears* for the unconditional the check has not earned. Open bills first, then paid.
- The cleared-releases queue's lead says where *Send to the GC* is once the unconditional is signed. Guide *send a GC our lien waiver* gains *What the GC sees*; GLOSSARY and `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` amended.

## Design calls

- **Pre-scoped in the function.** As with *Your payments*, nothing the page would hide can be read from the payload: the who-pays rule runs server-side and the other party's bills are never sent.
- **The GC is told what is coming, not what is owed.** *on its way* and *when your check clears* are the whole vocabulary; no amounts owed, no deadlines, no office notes.
- **Signed URLs, an hour.** The bucket is private; the PDF link is minted per page load like the legal portal's contract copies.

## Deploy

Merge → `bash scripts/deploy-functions.sh customer-portal`. The page renders the section only when the function sends `waivers`; until the deploy the room simply lacks it.
