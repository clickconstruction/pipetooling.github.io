# v2.8253 — Document builder: the ground for checking a certificate against a job's sheet (2026-11-21)

Punch list #404, step 6, the database half. Taunya's call on 2026-11-21 settled the open one: a certificate for each job, not one for each company. The screens come in the PR after the push, as a new call ships alone first.

## What it adds

One migration, `20261122021800_coi_checked_against_a_sheet.sql` ([its page](../migrations/20261122021800_coi_checked_against_a_sheet.md)):

- Seven columns on `person_contract_documents`: the job a certificate was checked for, the sheet's revision and lines it was checked against, who and when, and what was sent back as missing.
- `document_builder_coi_mark_good` and `document_builder_coi_send_back`, a dev alone, on a published sheet, for a company on a trade of that job.
- A certificate checked for one job is never moved to another.

No client calls either function yet, so nothing on any screen changes.
