# v2.7779 — Lien desk: the Ready to file bin says what the print comes with only when it is not the usual stack (2026-11-18)

The owner's ask of 2026-11-18, with a screenshot of the Board's **Ready to file** bin and a red box around its second line: *"I don't understand why we are offering this line. It doesn't seem very actionable as clicking it takes the user to the job. Should we improve it or delete it?"* His call the same day: *"delete the print line in the usual case"*. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## Before

- Every **Ready to file** bin with a paper to print read *Prints with the help sheet and the notice records · Change ›* under its name (v2.7470, in the bin since v2.7708).
- *Change ›* opened By paper's affidavits list at Ready to file. The setting is three small ticked chips in that list's print bar, and By paper opens the first paper in its pane, so the press read as a door to a job.
- The line said the same thing on every visit, since the setting is kept on this computer and starts with the help sheet and the notice records on and the copies of the notices off.

## What changed

- **The usual stack draws no line.** `lienBoardStackNote` in `lienBoardPrints.ts` answers null for `LIEN_BOARD_USUAL_STACK`: the help sheet and the notice records, no copies. The bin is its name, its count, its press and what the print leaves out.
- **A stack someone changed says so**, in the same quiet line: *Prints without the help sheet*, *Prints without the notice records*, *Prints without the help sheet or the notice records*, *Prints with copies of the notices too*, or *Prints without the help sheet, with copies of the notices*.
- **Its press puts the usual pages back where it stands**: *Put it back ›*, *Put them back ›*, *Leave them out ›* or *Use the usual pages ›*. `onBoardPrintNote` in `LienDesk.tsx` sets the three ticks of By paper's print bar as they start, a toast says so, and the desk stays on To do. It no longer changes the view.
- `lienBoardAffidavitPrint` takes `stack` (`LienBoardPrintStack`, three booleans) where it took `with`, a list of page names.
- The print is unchanged, and so are the three ticks on By paper's print bar.

## Files

- `src/lib/jobs/lienBoardPrints.ts`: `LienBoardPrintStack`, `LIEN_BOARD_USUAL_STACK`, `lienBoardStackNote`; `lienBoardAffidavitPrint` takes `stack`.
- `src/components/jobs/LienDesk.tsx`: the stack handed in, `onBoardPrintNote` resets it.
- `src/components/jobs/LienBoard.tsx`: a comment.
- Guide: `read-the-board-on-the-lien-desk`.

- `lienBoardPrints.test.ts`, `LienBoard.render.test.tsx`, `LienDesk.render.test.tsx`, `useLienDeskBoard.render.test.tsx`: 258 tests pass, with new tests for the usual stack, each changed stack and the press that puts it back.
