# v2.4582 — Release of Lien: every way into Unconditional asks first (2026-10-05)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Row 1 of the lien defects card (punch list #83, [`to-dos/lien-defects.md`](../../to-dos/lien-defects.md)). The card held this row for the owner's call; asked 2026-10-05, the answer was **every route**.

## The defect

The warning of v2.4507 (`UNCONDITIONAL_WAIVER_WARNING`) fired only from the step 2 switch (`pickUnconditional`). Three routes reached an unconditional form without it: *Waive the $X paid ›* and *Waive the $X already paid instead ›* (`waivePaid`), a window opened on a preset form by **Issue unconditional** (the Bill Customer strip, the Dashboard queue), and a settled bill picking unconditional by itself on open (`pickLienWaiverForBill`).

## The fix

All in [`LienReleaseModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienReleaseModal.tsx):

- **`waivePaid`** asks first when the form is conditional. *Stay conditional* leaves the form and the amount alone.
- **A window opened on an unconditional form** asks once, after the job's releases are read (`historyReady`), so it is known whether a draft resumes. *Acknowledge* keeps the form. *Stay conditional* closes a window opened by a preset (it was opened for the unconditional), and steps a form the bill picked back to the conditional of the same kind (`conditionalFormOf`: a final stays a final).
- **A resumed draft or a pending signature request is not asked**: that choice was made when it was started.

## Tests

`LienReleaseModal.gcWaiver.render.test.tsx` (+2): a preset window asks, *Stay conditional* closes it, *Acknowledge* keeps it; a window that opens conditional asks nothing. `amountMath` (+1) and `steps`: the Waive links ask, and *Stay conditional* leaves the form.
