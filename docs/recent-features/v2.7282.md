# v2.7282 — The job window on a phone: Documents, and a photo straight into a folder (2026-11-14)

Punch list #355, PR H ([`to-dos/job-window-phone.md`](../../to-dos/job-window-phone.md), pass 3; the mock-up [`to-dos/job-window-phone-tabs.html`](../../to-dos/job-window-phone-tabs.html)). Client only: no migration, no edge function, and no kernel that is copied to the edge functions changed.

## Before

Measured at 375 px, Documents ran 4.7 screens with 41 presses under 32 px: eleven sections each with a 28 px outline press, and a folder row with a 22 px *Add a file* that opened the phone's picker and nothing else. The camera was in the picker but nothing said so, and a shot arrived with its camera name. Stage checklists folded only a ready stage. The AIA application's 33 fields and the Test report's fields sat under 16 px, so an iPhone zoomed the page on every tap, and the Test report's result cards and pressure boxes ran past the right edge.

## What changed

- **[`src/lib/jobs/jobFolderAddFile.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobFolderAddFile.ts)**: `ADD_SHEET_WORDS` (Add ▾ · Add to Plans · Take a photo · Choose a file · Open in Google Drive) and `folderPhotoFileName` (*2026-11-14 Robert · Plans.jpg*, through `photoDriveName`, so the shot is named as the sweep names one). Tests beside it.
- **`JobDocumentsAddFile.tsx`**: on a phone (`useNarrowViewport640`) the press is **Add ▾**, 40 px, and opens a `PhoneSheet` of three 48 px rows. *Take a photo* opens a hidden `capture="environment"` input; the shot is dated by `readTakenAt`, shrunk by `shrinkPhoto`, named by `folderPhotoFileName` with the viewer's name, and put through the same `io.put` as a chosen file, with the *is there · Open in Drive* line after; over 10 MB it is refused in words. *Choose a file* is the picker and the panel as before. *Open in Google Drive* is the folder's link. The desk keeps *Add a file*.
- **`JobWindowDocumentsTab.tsx`**: `data-documents-phone` on the root and a style at 640 px and under: every section's press 40 px, the section titles 17 px, the folder rows 44 px, an empty state at least 44 px.
- **`JobDocumentsChecklists.tsx`**: on a phone every stage folds to one line when the rows first read (a ready stage alone before), the stage's name a 44 px press beside its standing chip; a tap opens the read-out as before.
- **`AiaG702G703Modal.tsx`** and **`TestReportModal.tsx`**: a style at 640 px and under sets the fields to 16 px; the Test report's date and duration, the PASS and FAIL cards, the four pressure boxes and the gas fixture rows stack on a phone.
- **`LienInstrumentsModal.tsx`**: the same 16 px rule; measured on the ZZ dispute-walk job, the Lien window is a full-screen sheet with nothing past the edge and ten fields under 16 px. View bill, measured on the same job's Bill 1, fits the width and needs nothing.

## Is this the best we can do?

The sheet is the right shape: a folder has three acts and a row has room for one word, so the sheet holds the three with a quiet line each, and the camera is one tap deeper than the row, which is where the owner put it. A photo taken here lands beside a photo taken from the banner's Add a photo, since both file into the job's folder by the same names. What is not done: an empty section still reads its sentence under the title rather than *none* on the title row, since each section's empty state names the press that fills it and that press is the thing a first-timer needs; the sentence is one line at 375 px.

## Verify

At 375 px open a job, press Documents: every press is 40 px; a stage is one row with its standing; a folder row ends in Add ▾, which opens the sheet; Take a photo opens the camera on a phone and the shot's line says it is in the folder. New application: the fields do not zoom. Test report: PASS and FAIL stack. At a desk nothing moves.
