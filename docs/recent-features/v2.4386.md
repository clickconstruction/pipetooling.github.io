# v2.4386 — Procurement log: To order folds by house, and cards on a phone (2026-10-02)

Procure layout train, change 4 of 4 (Grace, 2026-10-02; boards *Procure 2 · To order* and *Procure 4 · On a phone* on canvas https://claude.ai/artifact/JtoZEiwKP7zbABzXEy7hH8). On a draft, BP375's To order was one 44-line *Waiting on the GC* list with the tag on every line, and on a phone (or a 768 px tablet) the log was a 760 px table scrolling sideways in a 608 px box.

## What changed (`SubmittalProcurementPanel`, `procurementLog.ts`)
- **To order folds the waiting lines by house** (`foldByHouse`, `houseFoldNote`): under *Waiting on the GC*, one line per house — *Moore Supply · 22 parts · 4 order only*, *Reece · 3 parts*, *No house yet · 19 parts · 10 order only · pick a house to order* (amber) — each with a tick for all its lines and a **Show** / **Hide** button (`.procure-fold`, `aria-expanded`) that opens its lines. *Order now*, *On order* and *Delivered* stay as lines.
- **Cards when the log is narrow**: below 640 px (`useNarrowViewport640`) or whenever the log's own box is narrower than the table's 760 px (`TABLE_MIN_WIDTH`, measured by a callback ref at once and then by a `ResizeObserver`), each line draws as a card — the tick, the part (tag and model wrap; long models break anywhere), a line *qty · house · stage · lead time* (missing ones say *no stage*, *no lead time*), and the status button; its dates open under the card. Headings, the order-only divider and the house folds draw as blocks. renderRow builds the line's pieces once (tick, part, qty, house, stage, lead, status, the dates) and lays them out as a table row or a card; the tbody is drawn through one `block()` helper for both.
- **The By tag tree carries over** (v2.4384): a card keeps its step in the tree, the indent drawn as left padding plus a rule in place of the table's rails, assembly rows and the per-block order-only divider draw as blocks, and a line's dates open under it at the same step.
- **A row with no product** is flagged (`noProduct` on the line, set by `procurementItemsFrom` when the row has no submitted product) so **Before you can order** names *UTILITY SINK has no product yet* with **Open it**, instead of counting it as a part with no house (v2.4383 looked for the *(no product)* label only, but a Missing row reads the plans' words).
