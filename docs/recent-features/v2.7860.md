# v2.7860 — Trips: a trip put back is tied to its line again (2026-11-18)

## Why

Punch list #391 left one hole with no card: after the Undo of a deleted trip, the trip was back with no tie to its old line, and it could be put on a bill a second time. The delete's question said so. The **Deleted trips** fold (v2.7804) made the hole easier to reach, since a trip can now come back days later, from a list, by somebody who never saw that question. The owner chose it as the first of four follow-ups on 2026-11-18.

## What changed

One migration, `20261118203000_trip_line_tie.sql` (`docs/migrations/20261118203000_trip_line_tie.md`). No table, column or index is added.

- **The trip's capture keeps the line it held.** `trips_archive_on_delete` takes the place of the archive's generic capture on `trips`. It writes the same row, with `held_line_id` when a job's line carries the trip. At that moment the mark is still set: the foreign key clears it after the trip's row is gone.
- **A trip made again ties that line.** `trips_settle_archive` reads the id back from the delete nothing has put back and sets the line's `trip_id`, only when the line is still there, tied to no trip, and the trip holds none. Then it settles the archive rows as before.
- **The words.** The question of **Delete this trip** on a trip that holds a line now ends *Undo or Put back ties the trip to it again.* The guide says the same in its two places, and the Glossary has the rule.

## Decisions

- **The archive row, not a column on the lines.** The lines table is the hottest in billing. Remembering the tie on the trip's own archive row costs no DDL there, and the row is already read when a trip is made again.
- **A removed line stays removed.** Somebody took it off the job on purpose. The trip comes back free, and the press is there if the work still needs billing.
- **Nothing in the client reads the tie.** The trip's chip and its door already read the line by `trip_id`, so both are right the moment the trip is back.

## Left as it is

- The fold does not say that a deleted trip held a line. The trip says it once it is back.
- A trip deleted before this migration has no line id in its capture. Put back, it comes back untied, as before.
