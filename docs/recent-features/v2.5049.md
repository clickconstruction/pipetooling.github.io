# v2.5049 — GC mode: the emails about a trade partner’s charges and changes, and what waits on the office (2026-10-09)

P4b-iii of the trade partner portal (`to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/portal-p4.md` on branch `spike/gc-mode`; P4b split in three, approved 2026-10-09), the last of P4b. No migration, no deploy, and nothing calls it yet: Building's back-charge screen, Owner Billing's O3b and U6 send these emails through `gc-trade-email`, and the Board's B2b reads the two kernels for the Needs you line.

- **The email builders** (`src/lib/gc/tradeEmail.ts`), from the prototype's `portalMessages` word for word, without the greeting the function writes, in the company's language:
  - `backChargeEmail(stage, …)`, kind `backCharge` (the people who get pay emails): `sent` (`mBcSubject`, `mBcWhat`, `mBcAnswer`, `mBcOpen`), `settled` (`mBcKept…` or `mBcDropped…`) and `taken` (`mBcTaken…`), each null when the charge is not at that stage;
  - `changeAskEmail(stage, …)`, kind `changeAsk` (the people who get contract emails): `down` (`mCrDown…`), `sent` (`mCrSent…`, with the company's part, the change order's cost, never our price) and `no` (`mCrNo…`);
  - `backChargeEmailKey` and `changeAskEmailKey`, `<id>:<stage>`, so each one sends once per company.
- **The two Needs you kernels**, lifted word for word from the spike (`portal-p4b-iii.lift.json`; `lift-same.cjs` reads every declaration and test the same): `backChargesWaiting.ts` (a charge the company disputed, one with no answer past its answer day, or an agreed or kept one ready to come off an approved draw; late after `BACK_CHARGE_LATE_DAYS`, 7) and `changeRequestsWaiting.ts` (a request we have not answered; late after `CHANGE_REQUEST_LATE_DAYS`, 7), each with the dashboard's line and its direct tests.
- **Tests**: `gcTradeEmail.test.ts` for each builder's stages, words in both languages, keys and the null cases; the two kernels' direct tests.
- **Docs**: `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`'s `gc-trade-email` section names the two builders.
