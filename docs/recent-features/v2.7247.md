# v2.7247 — The dev seat drafts a lien affidavit on the Lien desk (2026-11-14)

The owner's ask of 2026-11-14. A notice had just been put on record for three jobs, and none of them had an affidavit on the desk. The seat could set a last day of work and record a notice, and could not draft the paper. He asked for the verb.

## What changed

- **Two verbs, server 0.87.0**: `plan_affidavit_draft` → `apply_affidavit_draft` (`supabase/functions/_shared/devMcpAffidavitDraft.ts`, wired in `devMcpWrites.ts` and `dev-mcp/index.ts`). One `job_lien_desk_items` row, kind `affidavit`, status `drafted`: the job's last work month, the nine notice values at the claim, the GC's email, the mark for a month dated from the job's creation, and the short form's mark. It is the row `LienDeskAffidavitPane` hands `saveLienDeskDraft`, key for key, and the test holds the two together. A standing draft is refreshed in place with its signature cleared, as the pane does. 0.86.0 is the box edit and part rename's (v2.7241).
- **The desk decides what may be drafted.** The dry run reads the desk's own reader for the job (`list_lien_affidavit_windows`, over GET), fills in the three facts the desk fills in before its queue (the GC's address, a public owner, the homestead answer) and runs `buildLienAffidavitQueue` from the mirror. Only a job in To draft, with every gate behind it, is drafted. A job the desk lists no window for, one a gate still holds (each gate named) and one whose paper is sent for approval, approved, held, filed or missed are refused in words.
- **The plan reads the paper back.** Nobody is looking at the paper from the seat, so the plan says what it would say today, through the paper's own kernels: the months it names (the months the recorded notices name that fall inside the job's work span), the day and the way each notice went to the owner, the claim with any correction, the owner, the county and legal description, the signer. Under it, the desk's own *Ask the attorney before this is filed* lines. A paper that would print a blank says so.
- **A draft alone.** It is not sent for approval, approved, held, printed, signed or filed, and nobody is told. Each is a press on the desk.
- The seat asks the reader to look 400 days ahead. The desk looks 30, so a draft for a later window shows on the desk once its day is within 30 days, and the plan says so.

## Four small moves so the server can read the desk's kernels

The edge copy refuses a kernel that takes types from a file that runs code, and one that reaches a dynamic import. Every old import still stands through a re-export:

- `FilingSignature` lives in `src/lib/jobsDocuments/filingSignatureType.ts`. `lienFilingDocuments.ts` re-exports it.
- `demandDate` lives in `src/lib/jobsDocuments/demandDate.ts`. `demandLetter.ts` re-exports it.
- `describeNoticeMonths` lives in `src/lib/jobs/lienNoticeMonthWords.ts`. `lienNoticeDraft.ts` re-exports it.
- `lienAffidavitSwornMonths` lives in `src/lib/jobs/lienAffidavitNotices.ts`, beside the months and sends it reads. `lienNoticeDraft.ts` re-exports it.

`scripts/edge-kernels.mjs` gains six entries (`lienDeskAffidavits`, `lienAffidavitNotices`, `lienAffidavitNoticeCheck`, `lienClaimCorrection`, `lienProperty`, `lienAffidavitForm`); the copy is 348 files.

## Not the same as the pane in one place

The pane saves its draft on the way to *Send for approval*. The seat stops at the draft. The paper then sits in To draft until a person sends it on.

## Deploy

`supabase functions deploy dev-mcp`, then restart the seat so the new verbs load. No migration.
