# v2.6455 — The SQL beds run in two groups instead of five, so a run takes two build machines and about 9 minutes of build time (2026-11-03)

Since v2.6415 a leg of the SQL beds spends most of its time on two things it does once: about 60 s pulling the 3.1 GB image, and its migrated copy. The 60-odd beds that start from the copy take about 15 s each. With legs of at most 16 beds, a run of 80 paid for the pull and the copy five times and took five of the org's 20 concurrent jobs.

- **The change**: `changes` in `.github/workflows/sql-beds.yml` deals the list into legs of at most 40 beds instead of 16. Today's 80 beds are two legs of 40. The 81st bed makes three legs of 27, since a longer list still adds a leg.
- **The proof**: the same commit (#6950's, with every bed that can use the copy on it) was dispatched three times together on `ci-test/` branches. Only the cap differed, and the runner line was empty. Every run passed all 80 beds with 3,891 `ok:` lines, 63 of them from the copy.

  | Legs (cap) | Runner jobs | Job-minutes | Longest leg (job) | Each leg's copy |
  | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
  | 5 (16) | 5 | 15.5 | 257 s | 54 to 162 s |
  | 3 (27) | 3 | 11.2 | 250 s | 95 to 141 s |
  | 2 (40) | 2 | 8.7 | 319 s | 102 and 200 s |

  Runs 41652331261, 41652339475 and 41652363754.
- **The trade**: a run takes 2 slots instead of 5 and 44% less build time. The org's limit is 20 jobs at once, so the queue's builds and other PRs wait less. When runners are free, a run's beds finish about a minute later: the longest leg took 319 s against 257 s. Three legs would save less, 28%, for no shorter wait.
- **The time limit**: `timeout-minutes` stays 45, and the wrapper still stops any one bed at 20 minutes. With 16 beds a leg was two rounds of that limit, so even a leg where every bed hung finished inside 45 minutes. A leg of 40 is five rounds, so only many beds hanging at once can now outlast 45 minutes. A leg cancelled at the limit fails the `sql-beds` gate like a failed bed. The healthy legs here took at most 319 s.
- **Docs**: the workflow's header, the deal's comment and `docs/AI_CONTEXT.md` say 40.
