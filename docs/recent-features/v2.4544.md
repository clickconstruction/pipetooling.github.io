# v2.4544 — Lien desk: give an owner our records for their property (2026-10-05)

Owners whose GC has not paid us began asking for what we billed and what the GC paid, to go after the GC themselves. The owner's decision after weighing the risk: answer with a packet per property, on a written request, with a signed acknowledgment — and show the payments with their dates and times. One migration (a new table); no edge function.

## The window

- **The door.** `LienDeskModal`'s title bar gains *An owner asked for records ›* (`onOpenOwnerRecords`, office only). [`LienOwnerRecordsModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienOwnerRecordsModal.tsx) is mounted in `JobsStagesTab` beside the desk (z 800); its backdrop stops its click, so the desk stays open behind it.
- **Pick the owner.** The owners of jobs with a notice on the desk, folded by owner and property ([`ownerRecordsDesk.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/ownerRecordsDesk.ts): `lienSameProperty` and the same customer), named by the owner of record when the property has one.
- **The packet** ([`ownerRecords.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/ownerRecords.ts) `buildOwnerPacket`): every job at the saved property for that customer (a job with no saved property reads alone), each bill that went out, each payment under the bill it names with the day paid and the time recorded, payments that name no bill, and three figures a job: the job total, what was paid, and what is still owed — the job total less everything paid, the figure a notice claims. There is no "billed" figure: a payment can land on a job before its bill is written, so billed can read lower than paid.
- **Four checks** (`ownerRecordsSteps`): 1 their request in writing (day, how, from, a link) · 2 our contract with the GC (the lien leader's tick, read from any request for the same GC, so once per GC) · 3 the numbers agree (`ownerPacketNumbers` against each job's newest notice claim: a match, a claim that differs, a job owed with no notice — a warning, never a block) · 4 their signed acknowledgment (day, a link).
- **Record it as sent** needs 1, 2 and 4 (`ownerRecordsMissing`, the owner's call); it stores how it went, by whom, the total and the jobs.
- **The papers** ([`ownerRecordsDocs.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/ownerRecordsDocs.ts)): the cover note, the statement and a one-page acknowledgment, everything typed escaped. **Draft wording**: `OWNER_RECORDS_WORDING_APPROVED` is false, the window says so, and each print asks through the app-wide confirm (*Not yet* the safe answer).
- **Left out, on purpose** is printed on screen: other owners' jobs, what the GC paid on other properties, check images and bank details, our own notes.

## The table

`lien_owner_record_requests` (`supabase/migrations/20261005155515_lien_owner_record_requests.sql`, doc in `docs/migrations/`): one row per request with the owner, the property, the GC, the jobs and `file` (the checks and the send). Office select / insert / update, dev delete, a server stamp, the read-only and twin fences. **Client first**: until the push the window opens, reads and prints, says *Saving is not ready yet*, and offers no check to file. The client talks to the table through casts; `database.ts` is regenerated after the push.

A request is not kept on a notice's `fields` (where the owner's call lives): a draft's fields are rewritten whole on the next approval.
