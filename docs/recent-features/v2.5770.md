# v2.5770 — Pipeline: the owner-sees pill is a small eye with a card on hover (2026-10-23)

Punch list #145 (`to-dos/pipeline-owner-sees/`, retired with this PR; the mock-up lives in git history at the deleting commit). The owner, 2026-10-23, on the Pipeline row's *☐ owner sees $0* pill (v2.3827): "confusing and takes up valuable real estate … Perhaps we should show an eye with a line through it where when the user hovers over it, they can get details about what that means in the form of a temporary modal." Shown three options drawn at the row's measured sizes, the owner took A: "I think what you are building is sound and I think we should build it." Client only, no migration.

## What the pill was

Not the Stage Plan's eye. The *owner sees the bills* switch: on a job billed to the GC whose customer is a different party, the owner's portal lists nothing, so it reads $0 and *all paid up* while the GC owes on their house. Measured live before the change (80 rows, 23 pills): the pill never set a row's height at desktop width, but it widened the identity block by 56–71 px (the notes box shrank by the same) and wrapped the customer line on 18 of 23 rows at laptop width. The cost was width and attention: a bold pill with an unlabelled dollar figure.

## The change

- **Kernel** `src/lib/jobs/ownerSeesCard.ts` (`ownerSeesCard`, `ownerFirstName`, `ownerSeesCardSentences`; 8 tests, every sentence held to `plainWordsFailures`): the switch's state plus the job's open GC bills → the eye (`crossed` / `hollow` / `open`), the tone (grey with nothing billed, amber once open bills exist the owner cannot see, blue when on), the card's words — the fact first (*Nia Okafor's page shows $0 of this job.*), why, the open figure with its label (*Open to Ridgeway Builders · $12,400*), the door (*Show Nia the bills* / *Show Nia the rest* / *Stop showing*), a hint — and the list's short words. `ownerShareChipWords` is gone.
- **The eye** `src/components/icons/EyeIcon.tsx`, lifted from `JobFormStagesGroup.tsx` with a `variant` (`open` / `hollow` / `crossed`), so the Stage Plan's eye and the row's eye are one drawing with one meaning: *on the portal*.
- **`OwnerShareChip.tsx`**: a 15 px eye button in the customer line's tail (`data-owner-share-chip` keeps the state, `data-owner-sees-tone` the tone, the card's title is its name). Hover opens the card after a beat, focus opens it at once, leaving or Esc closes it; the card slides left when it would run past the viewport's right edge and opens above the glyph when there is no room below. The card's door and a click on the eye open the switch's existing window, which now leads with the card's title. With `words` (the portal window's property lines) the eye carries its short words beside it.
- **Guide** *show an owner the bills their GC pays* rewritten for the eye; **glossary** *Share this bill* amended.
