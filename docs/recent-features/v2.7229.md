# v2.7229 — The dev seat records a lien notice that was already mailed (2026-11-13)

The owner's ask of 2026-11-13. One § 53.056 notice had been mailed by hand for several jobs at a property and was on record for one of them, so the other jobs read "no notice" and no affidavit would draft. The Lien desk records such a paper with *Already mailed? Record it…*. The dev seat could not, and he asked for the verbs.

## What changed

- **Two verbs, server 0.85.0**: `plan_notice_by_hand` → `apply_notice_by_hand` (`supabase/functions/_shared/devMcpNoticeByHand.ts`, wired in `devMcpWrites.ts` and `dev-mcp/index.ts`). One `job_lien_filings` row for each job the paper covered, on one packet, typed as the paper went: the day, how, the number, who it went to, the claim and the months as printed, the saved copy. Then each covered job's live notice item on the desk is marked sent with its filing, on the mailing day when that day is past. One hundred and three write verbs.
- **The rows are the form's own.** The dry run builds them with the form's kernel from the mirror (`byHandFilingPayloads`), and the test holds them equal to it. The first job is at the desk's claim (`lien_billed_open`, read over GET), each other job at its open balance, as `LienNoticeByHandPane` passes them. The snapshot each row keeps is the form's nine values for the first job with the claim replaced by the paper's.
- **What the seat refuses**: whatever the form's own list refuses; a job in `also_covers` that is not another unpaid job at the same property; a job that already holds a live notice with the same mailing day and number; a first job whose claim was set by hand, since the seat does not read a corrected claim and the desk is its door.
- **What the plan says** beside the rows: a line when the paper went to one recipient alone, a line for a job with nothing open on sent bills, and the other unpaid jobs at the property the paper is not being recorded on.

## Two kernels moved so the server can read them

The edge copy refuses a kernel that takes types from a file that runs code. Two small moves, with every old import still standing through a re-export:

- `LienNoticeFields` lives in `src/lib/jobsDocuments/lienNoticeFieldsType.ts`. `lienFilingDocuments.ts` re-exports it.
- `buildLienNoticeFieldsForJob`, `homesteadStatementApplies`, `retainageInsideClaim`, `DEFAULT_CLAIMANT_NAME` and `LienNoticeJobFacts` live in `src/lib/jobs/lienNoticeJobFields.ts`. `lienNoticeDraft.ts` re-exports them. The issuer is typed as the two fields the notice reads (`LienNoticeIssuer`), so the kernel pulls no settings code.

`scripts/edge-kernels.mjs` gains four entries (`lienNoticeByHand`, `lienSentAt`, `lienNoticeJobFields`, `lienSigner`); the copy is 334 files.

## Not the same as the form in two places

- A stored draft's own wording is not read. The snapshot is the job's defaults. The paper that went is the saved copy.
- A covered job with no live notice item gets its row and nothing else. The form also runs the desk's sync there, which matters only where an item waits in another state.

## Deploy

`supabase functions deploy dev-mcp`, then restart the seat so the new verbs load. No migration.
