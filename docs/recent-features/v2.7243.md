# v2.7243 — The Bridge: each job with no price says its address (2026-11-13)

Client only. No migration, no edge function, and no kernel that is copied to the edge functions changed. A follow-up to v2.7232, which put the address on the jobs with no percent. Asked whether the jobs under *Price the jobs with no price* should say it too, the owner: "Yes, add it".

## What changed

- **The line** (`callDive.ts`): a job on the list *Price the jobs with no price* carries its street and city under its name, through the same line under a name (`sub`) and the same `streetAndCity` as v2.7232. `BridgeMoveTable.tsx` did not change. A job with no address has no such line.
- **The read** (`pctFactsIo.ts`, `useBridgePctFacts.ts`): the facts read takes a second list, jobs whose address alone is wanted. A job on both lists is read once. The read now also runs when the price move is on the card without the percent move.

## Tests
