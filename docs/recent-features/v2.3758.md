# v2.3758 — Lien notices carry a page of pay codes: one QR code per unpaid bill (2026-09-23)

Punch list #35 (`to-dos/lien-notice-pay-codes/`), PR 2 of 4 — the owner's first ask: *"historically when we sent out lien notices … a page of QR codes … our app fulfills the cover letter and the notice, it does not fulfill the QR codes."* Client only, no migration; the codes carry the `/pay/<id>` address (v2.3754) and are drawn by the kernel View bill's modal uses (v2.3757).

## The page

After the notice, before the enclosed invoices — where the office always put it — under the office's own sentence, **Once these bills are paid, there will be no lien filed.**: one row per unpaid bill (the number and date, the line as the invoice reads it, *Still owed: $…*, the code, the address in words), then the count and the total, then who to call. The owner's copy repeats the cover letter's rule in a box: *pay us directly only if the GC has told you in writing that you may* — asked whether the page was the best we could do, this was the answer: a pay page behind a letter that says *do not pay us on your own* must carry the letter's condition, or it contradicts the sheet in front of it. Counsel's memo says the GC's envelope is "the form and invoices only"; the GC's copy carries no page until the owner (or counsel) says so — `PAY_PAGE_ON_GC_COPY`, one line. A paper bill beside a Stripe bill prints its row with a dashed box and *pay by check to the address above*; a job whose bills are all paper gets no page (the first live look on the desk showed one — a page of codes with no code on it; the enclosed invoices already say what is owed).

## The change

- **[`lienNoticePayPage.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienNoticePayPage.ts)** (8 tests) — `payPageRows(docs)` from the enclosed bills; `payPageBlocks({ rows, assets, copy, copyLabel, gcName, claimantName, contactPerson, phone, extras })` → the letterhead, the notice's reference strip naming the copy, the title, the intro, the owner's callout, a `payRow` per bill, the total, the fine print; empty for a copy the page does not go to or a job with no unpaid bill. `payPageSummary` for the desk's label (PR 3).
- **[`lienNoticePayPageAssets.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienNoticePayPageAssets.ts)** — the codes, built in the browser once the bills are known: the SVG (120 px, the mark inlined) for print and the preview, the PNG (480 px) for jsPDF; a code that cannot be rasterised leaves the PDF row with its box.
- **[`lienFilingDocuments.ts`](../../src/lib/jobsDocuments/lienFilingDocuments.ts)** — two block kinds the shared renderers learn: `callout` (a boxed line; HTML, PDF and the text twin) and `payRow` (the code or a dashed box, the label, the line, the amount, the address in monospace, the note). The first image jsPDF draws in a filing document since the release's signature.
- **[`noticeInvoiceEnclosure.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/noticeInvoiceEnclosure.ts)** — `NoticeInvoiceDoc` carries `stripeInvoiceId`, `openAmount` (what is still owed, from the same payments the enclosure already counts — `unpaidBilledInvoicesWithOpen`) and `description` — `payPageDescription`: a bill with one service line is that line (the first live look showed the Stripe memo, *Paper checks can be sent to…*, where the row wanted *Trip charge to pick up a reissued check*), a bill with many lines reads its scope, else the first line and how many more; never past a row.
- **[`lienDeskRun.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDeskRun.ts)** — `runPayPageBlocks(n, r, rows, assets, phone)`; `runPacketHtml` takes `payPagesByJob` and prints the page after the form, before the invoices, on the copies that carry it. **[`lienDeskRunIo.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDeskRunIo.ts)** — the emailed copy merges the page's PDF behind the form the same way (`payBlocksByJob`). **[`LienDeskRunModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskRunModal.tsx)** builds the rows and codes once the bills load and says *the pay codes page behind the owner's copy (N codes)* in its sentence.
- **[`LienFilingTabs.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienFilingTabs.tsx)** — the Lien window's notice tab prints, downloads and emails the page the same way, from the bills its *Enclose the invoice* box encloses; untick it and the page leaves with the invoices.
- Guides *send lien notices from the Lien desk* (the run's packet) and *file a lien and never miss its deadlines* (step 1).
