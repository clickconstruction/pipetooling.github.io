# v2.5507 — Messages: opening it from a link no longer lands on the Dashboard (2026-10-13)

A fix from the prod walk after the residential build, by Customer communication helper 10, at cc's ask. On prod at v2.5504, under the owner's sign-in, a typed `/messages` went to `/dashboard`, while a `history.pushState` to it opened the page. So every hard load of Messages bounced the office team: a typed link, the reply push's `/messages?thread=…&at=…`, and the bubble's new-tab open. Moving around inside the app worked, which is why nobody saw it.

- **Why**: the page waited for `useAuth`'s `loading`, then read a null role as a refusal. `loading` can end before the users row lands: its 4 s watchdog lets a slow role read go, and a cold load is the slow case. That null role sent the office to the Dashboard. Map hit the same case in v2.833 and treats a null role as still loading. Messages, GC projects and Bridge never did.
- **The fix**: `pageGate` (`src/lib/roleGate.ts`) is the decision for a role-gated page.
  - It waits while auth loads, and while a signed-in person's role has not landed.
  - It opens for a role the page lets in, and sends away any other role, or anyone signed out.
  - `Messages.tsx` shows *Loading…* while it waits. `GcProjects.tsx` and `Bridge.tsx` take the same gate and render nothing while they wait, as they did.
  - The other pages in `src/pages` that redirect behind a role (Schedule dispatch, Moneyfill, Partnerships and Map) already wait for a null role.
- **Tests**:
  - `roleGate.test.ts` covers `pageGate`'s waits, opens and aways.
  - `Messages.render.test.tsx` gains four cases on `/messages?thread=…&at=…`: auth loading stays and keeps the link; a signed-in null role stays (the cold load); a primary goes to the Dashboard; the owner gets the page with the link kept.
  - Breaking the null-role wait on purpose failed both new pins.
