# v2.6330 — Projects: every working project as one picture, with the expected hours as a slider inside the labor budget (punch list #242, PR 3) (2026-11-02)

Punch list #242, PR 3 of five. The page the owner asked for on 2026-11-01: *expose an independent page to the leader and to the superintendents and to the office where we can very quickly see where all of the big jobs are and adjust their expected hours*. Built from the design spike (`origin/spike/projects-board-242`, never merged) on PR 1's kernels and strip (v2.6320) and PR 2's ledger (v2.6321). Client only, no migration, no function.

## What landed

- **`/projects-board`** (`src/pages/ProjectsBoard.tsx`): the read of every Working job with its line items in one query, filtered by the gate (`isWorkingProject`: a stage In order, set or recognized the way the Pipeline reads stages, or priced at $25,000 and up); the bulk cost read the Costs tab makes (`useBidVsActualBurnInputs`), the `job_budgets` rows (`useJobBudgetRows`, new), the stage checklists' count (`useStageChecklistStates`, v2.6077) and the expected hours (`useExpectedHours`). The cards worst first (`sortByPace`). An empty board says *No working projects yet. Open the Pipeline and set a job Working with its stages.*; a failed read draws `OfflineRetryPanel`. The page says `/projects-board`, not the card's `/projects`, which is the Workflows page.
- **The card** (`src/components/projectsBoard/ProjectCard.tsx`): title *J1044 · Name* as a press, *Job ›* to the job window, the `PaceStrip` at 22 px with its legend, the expected-hours row with the slider in the budget's range (`expectedHoursRange`, v2.6320), the source glyph, the pinned words at or over the edge, the reason box once the handle moved (*Keep it* dead until a reason of three characters; *Put it back*), the target line with **back to the budget** for a setter, the one move's words. A drag re-reads the strip and the words live as if the target were set; nothing writes until Keep it. The card's title and every space around a control open it large in `ResponsiveModalShell` (960 wide): the strip at 40 px with the stage names under it, a long slider with − / + one crew day and a typed box, the full reading. Hover a day for `DayHoverCard`; tap it for `DayDetailsModal`.
- **`projectPace`** (`src/lib/jobs/projectsBoard.ts`): the one assembly of a card's numbers — the track from `resolveJobBudget` (bid → typed) else the lines' sum at the company split (`lineBudgets.ts`, #234 PR 1); the stages' shares cut it in order (`stageSharesOf`), each piece at the cost rate in dollars; the money from `spendByComponent` against the direct budget (the baseline's, else price × (1 − target margin)); the target as the line's budget when one is set. PR 4's phone card reads the same function, so the office and the field cannot disagree.
- **The door**: a **Projects** press beside Forecast on the Pipeline's command bar (`JobsStagesCommandBar`, `canSeeProjects`), shown to the roles that may open the board (`canSeeProjectsBoard`: dev, master, assistant, controller, superintendent), not to everyone who sees Forecast, since a primary would only be bounced. `/projects-board` joins `SUPERINTENDENT_PATHS`.
- **Guide** *see where every project stands and set its expected hours* (Jobs & Scheduling); `APP_DIRECTORY.md`, `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`, `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` (the matrix row), the Glossary's *Projects board* and *Expected hours*.
