The owner's ask of 2026-11-18, with a screenshot of the Board's **Ready to file** bin, the amber line *1 held on a property fact · See it ›* underlined, and the empty space after the bin's one chip underlined too: *"if this line is equally served by having the job as a box in red in the category rather than a line I would like it to look like that instead of the line, can you help me look holistically at this and come up with an ideal solution"*. Three options were drawn over made-up jobs, on a computer and a phone, light and dark. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What the look found

- **The line repeated a chip already on screen.** It counted the approved short-form affidavits a gate holds (`affPrintPlan.gated`). Since v2.7449 every one of those is in the pile `held`, so each already stood as a chip in the affidavits' **Held** bin on the heading line. On the live desk that day the line read 1 and Held held one chip, the same paper.
- **The chip was the shorter road.** A press on the Held chip opened the property record at the missing fact: one press. The line's *See it ›* opened By paper narrowed to the held papers, then *Answer it ›*: two.
- **The chip was red because its day had passed**, not because it was held. Chip colour is the day.
- **Held was a bin with no place in the flow.** It stood at the right of a column's heading line and said nothing of what its paper waited on.

## The options

- **A, the line goes.** Nothing new on screen. Held stays on the heading line.
- **B, as the owner drew it.** The held paper as a box in the bin it is stopped at. Drawn first with red boxes, which read 4 beside *Print 2* and gave red two meanings. Redrawn with a hold mark and a count of what can move.
- **C, Held at the end of Ready to file's row.** The Held group moved off the heading line. The notices' Held would have stayed, so the two columns would differ.

## What changed

- **No bin is named Held.** A held paper stands in the bin of the stage it is stopped at (`lienBoardHeldBin`): an approved affidavit a fact holds in **Ready to file**, an affidavit or a notice the leader held in **Awaiting Master approval**, where it waits on him again. Its `stage` is still `held`, so the tiles, the stages left and the at-risk rule are unchanged. `LienBoardBinKey` no longer has a `held` key.
- **A held chip has a dashed edge and a ‖ mark** (`held` on `LienBoardChip`, `data-held`, `data-lien-board-held-mark`). It stands after the chips that can move, with a little air before the first. Its colour is still its day's, so red still means late. Its hover says *held on a property fact* or *held by the leader*.
- **A bin counts what can move** (`lienBoardBinCount`). Ready to file reads 2 beside *Print 2 for notarization* with two held chips after its two. A bin can read 0 and still show a held chip, and it does not fold.
- **The line is gone.** `lienBoardAffidavitPrint` has no line for a held paper. A paper in Ready to file on the earlier form keeps its line, *1 on the earlier form · See it ›*: its chip stands in the bin unmarked, so the line is why the count is more than the press. The desk now counts that line from the Ready to file pile alone, and its door opens By paper at Ready to file.
- **The pair beside the pinned find box still says *1 held*.** The Board is out of view there.
- **The leader's press leaves a held chip alone.** `lienBoardForLeader` drops it from his bins, so *Approve N affidavits* and its *past its day* line count what they did.
- **A held paper's card and row say so**: *Ready to file · held* (`lienBoardHeldWords`), and the other paper's card reads *Its lien affidavit is held in Ready to file.* In the list under the Board the row stands under its stage's heading. By paper keeps its **Held** pile.
