# v2.7831 — New Job from a won bid: bring the price over as stages (2026-11-18)

Punch list #397, PR 3 of the train. Client only. No migration and no edge function. Stacked on PR 2's rule file.

## Why

A job opened from a won bid arrived with one line, *Bid price*. Everything that reads a job's stages reads its lines, so the pace card, the Projects board and the stage checklists had nothing to show on it. The owner picked the direction that fills the lines at the win, by an offer the office accepts.

## What changed

- **One window in place of one question.** New Job from a bid used to ask *Start the job at $X?* with *Carry $X over* and *Start at $0*. `BidCarryWindow` now asks **How should $X come onto the job?** with three answers:
  - **As stages**: a line for each stage, from the split a chip picks. The rows show each line's hours and dollars and add up to the price.
  - **As one line**: what *Carry over* did. One line, *Bid price*.
  - **Start at $0**: what the old No did. It writes nothing.
- **Cancel import** is new. The old question could not be backed out of. It fills nothing in and, when the form was opened for the import, closes it.
- **The press names the act**: *Bring 4 stages · $48,000*, *Carry $48,000 over*, *Start at $0*.
- **Nothing saves in the window.** The answer goes back to `useJobFormImport`, which lays the lines into Line Items. The office changes any name or amount there before **Create Job**.
- **The estimator's draft path is as it was.** A draft from the Dispatch inbox already answered the question, so it brings one line and asks nobody.
- `bidImportCarryQuestion` is gone. `bidImportWhoseFigure` keeps the one phrase the window still needs, whose figure it is.
- The window sits on the modal stack (`useModalStackEntry`), so Escape cancels the import and the job form under it stays open.
- A new guide, `bring-a-bids-stages-onto-its-job`. `turn-a-won-bid-into-a-job` names the new question. The Glossary's Stage kind entry and the project documentation each take a line.

## What wakes up on a job with stage lines

Both are meant, and the owner took them as drawn (the card's call 3):

- The Pipeline row draws the job as stages (`stageRecognition.ts`).
- Rough In, Top Out and Trim Set each take the company's stage checklist, so the row reads *not ready* until the crew answers.
