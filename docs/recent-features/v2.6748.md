# v2.6748 — The trade portal's Messages tab is the company's thread with us, with a reply box (punch list #205, PR 5) (2026-11-08)

Punch list #205 (`to-dos/trade-portal-vs-jobtread.md`), row 2, the owner's mark of 2026-11-08: the thread with its reply box. PR 5 of six, stacked on PR 4 (v2.6746). A read in `_shared`, a write kind and the page's Messages tab; no migration.

## What changed

- **`_shared/gcTradeThread.ts`** (new), the shape of `gcPortalThread.ts` keyed by the company: the company's contact is `contacts` where `gc_company_id` is the company and `name` is null (the row the trade flow reads). `loadTradeThread(admin, companyId)` reads `messages` on it, `direction` in or out, `channel` portal, text or email, the newest 200 oldest first, and keeps a line tied to a `project_id` only when the company has an invite on one of that project's trades (`tradeProjects`); a line tied to a bid or a Pipeline job never passes. The lines leave as `PortalThreadLine` with the writer's first name only. `writeTradeLine` finds or makes the contact row (`insert { kind: 'trade', gc_company_id }`, the loser of a race re-reading), checks the project (`notYours`), holds `TRADE_PORTAL_LINES_PER_HOUR` (20) on portal lines in and inserts the line.
- **`gc-trade-portal`** answers `thread: PortalThreadLine[] | null` beside the slice; a failed read is null. `TRADE_PORTAL_FIELDS.messages` gains `sent_at`.
- **`submit-gc-trade-portal`**: the kind `portal_message` joins `TRADE_SUBMIT_KINDS` with no verb; `parseTradeSubmit` reads `text` (1 to 2,000, `TRADE_MESSAGE_MAX`) and `projectId` (a uuid or null) into `parsed.message`, and the function handles it after the link, before the Spanish hold, answering `{ ok: true, value: line }`; `tooMany` 429, `notYours` 409, `failed` 500. Not under the free-text cap.
- **`src/lib/gc/tradePortalThread.ts`** (new, pure): `parseTradeThread`, `mergedThread` (the thread's lines with each email we sent as one of our email lines, by `sentMessages`' new `at`, an email the thread already holds on the same day not added twice), `tradeThreadRows` (*You · Thu Oct 8 · from the portal*, *Jordan · Thu Oct 8 · by text*, a long line cut at 240 at a word), `urgentName` and `threadJobs`.
- **`GcTradePortalThread.tsx`** (new): the Messages tab when the answer has a `thread`: the intro, the bubbles oldest first (ours on the right, a cut one with **More**), then the box: *About* a `<select>` of the company's jobs not lost (*No job* first, the one job picked when there is exactly one), a textarea of 2,000 and **Send** → `press.send('portal_message', { projectId, text })`; the page's `send` reads the slice again, which refreshes the thread, and the line stays on screen either way (the sample reads the same thread back). Under it *Click answers in office hours. For something urgent on site, call Jordan Reyes.* The tab's label becomes **Messages**; without a thread the tab keeps its read-only list and its old label.
- **Sample**: `gcTradePortalSampleThread(today)`: a text from Dana, an email from Avery, a line from the portal.
- **Tests**: `src/lib/gc/tradeThread.test.ts` (the filters, the newest 200, the first name only, the merge, the rows, who to call); `gcTradeSubmit.test.ts` (the kind's shape and refusals; 33 kinds); `GcTradePortal.thread.render.test.tsx` (the four lines in order, the post and the line kept, a refusal in the company's words, the read-only list without a thread); `tradePortalSample.test.ts`; `tradePortalPage.test.ts`.
- **Docs**: both function sections in `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` (the kind's row, the read's note); `docs/twins/APP_DIRECTORY.md`'s `/t/:token` entry (PRs 2 to 5 together); both portal guides.
