# v2.5759 — Legal Desk: the settlement floor is a slider you open with a tap (2026-10-22)

The owner, in the Legal Desk design spike, on the Fees & steps tab's *The firm may settle at [___] [% of the balance ▾] or above. [Save floor]* row: "instead of typing in a percentage, I would like a slider where I could choose to expand something to see a slider and then hit save and then have the slider disappear. And when I click on something again, the slider appears. Maybe that thing is the percentage?" Then: "I would also like to be able to see or have the ability to enter in a value for the percentage or for the number." Then: "When moving the slider, I want to see whole numbers only."

- **The line** (`SettlementFloorEditor` in `LegalDeskModal.tsx`): *The firm may settle at* **[any amount ›]** *The firm settles without asking us.* — once a floor is saved, **[80% or more ›]** *That is $12,625.46 today. Below that, the firm asks us.* The old sentence and the **No floor** button are gone; the chip says it, and the far left of the slider is no floor.
- **The panel**, on a tap of the chip: a **% box**, a **$ box** and a **slider** that move together, the box typed in last outlined blue; whole numbers while sliding (the % by 1, the $ as whole dollars with commas), a typed amount keeps its cents; *Any amount · 50% · The full balance* under the slider; **Save**, **Cancel**, and the line that says exactly what Save does — *Saves as 67% of the balance, so it moves as payments land.* / *Saves as $12,000.00, whatever the balance becomes.* / *Saves as no floor. The firm never has to ask.* Escape folds it with nothing written; Save folds it after the write.
- **The kernel** (`src/lib/legal/settlementFloorControl.ts`, `settlementFloorControl.test.ts`): `floorChipWords`, `floorTodayWords`, `floorControlStart` (the saved floor read back as both a share and dollars), `floorControlFromPct`, `floorControlTypePct` (digits only; out of range keeps the text and changes nothing), `floorControlTypeUsd` (`$` and commas allowed), `floorControlValid`, `floorControlSave`, `floorControlSavesWords`. The component holds one `FloorControl` and draws it.
- **Words**: the guide *share your attorney their portal* (the settlement authority paragraph).
- Unchanged: `legal_set_settlement_floor` and what the firm's portal reads; a floor saved before reads back the same.

## Is this the best we can do?

1. **The floor itself is the control**: nothing to find, nothing left open. The chip reads as a sentence closed and opens the panel; Save and Cancel both close it.
2. **Three controls, one value**: the slider, the % box and the $ box cannot disagree, and the blue outline says which one Save will believe.
3. **Whole numbers where a thumb moves, cents where a person types**: the slider never shows `$10,573.82` flicker; a typed `$12,000` is kept as typed.
4. **One kernel**: every rule above is pure and tested; the component is state plus drawing.
5. **Cut**: a per-job floor (the matter is the unit), a history of floors (the firm's acts carry it), and a confirm on Save (the line beside Save already says what it does).
