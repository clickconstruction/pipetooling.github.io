# v2.5238 — The Bridge: everything else waits, the usual fixes as presses (link PR 7) (2026-10-11)

PR 7 of the weakest-link build (`to-dos/bridge-weakest-link/README.md`). Dev only. No migration.

- **The kernel** (`src/lib/bridge/waits.ts`): `buildReflexes` sizes the four usual fixes from the page's own numbers — *Hire another plumber* adds 40 field hours at the field's dollars an hour to Build's pace, *Add a field day* adds a day of every field person, *Push for more bids* adds a third to Market's asks, *Cut overhead 10%* moves nothing — each as a dial preset (the stage and the dollars a week). `reflexLine` reads what the press did from the flow before and the dial's reading after: for the field presses, what the office still bills and how much more waits unbilled; for the bids press, that the field still builds what it builds; for overhead, the dollars a day and a week against one finished job billed; and in every case whether the link moved. `otherLevers` is the old list of each link's lever in a few words, without the lit one. Every line passes the plain-words rule.
- **The card** (`BridgeWaits.tsx`, under the desk beside the scorecard): one press per row; the pressed one shows its line; **back to today** in the footer; a "?" in the corner opens the levers list.
- **The page**: a press picks the stage and sets its dial (`dialUsd`) to the preset, so the cards, the dial and the order all show the fix; dragging the dial clears the press; **back to today** on either clears both.
- **Tests**: `waits.test.ts` (3), `BridgeWaits.render.test.tsx` (1).
- **Docs**: guide *read the Bridge* gains "Everything else waits"; the Glossary's Bridge entry; the to-do's status line.
