# v2.6131 — Six pages hold their full width from the first paint (2026-10-30)

The sweep v2.6117 called for: every page wrapper in `src/pages/*.tsx` written as `maxWidth: N, margin: '0 auto'` with no `width`, pinned to its container the way Job Parts Tally was.

## What was happening

A page is a flex item in Layout's column (`main.appMain` → the Outlet's column). A flex item with auto side margins is not stretched: it takes its fit-content width, clamped to the container. So a page whose widest line was shorter than the screen drew narrower than the screen, a loading line drew narrower still, and the page widened as each load landed. On a desk the same page drew as wide as its content, not its max width.

## What changed

Each wrapper adds `width: '100%'`, `minWidth: 0` and `boxSizing: 'border-box'` (the `.pageWrap` trio, v2.1003), with the comment, by one script run over the six files:

- `src/pages/BoxCode.tsx` (`/b/:code`)
- `src/pages/Documents.tsx` (`/documents`)
- `src/pages/JobsAccountsReceivable.tsx` (`/accounts-receivable`)
- `src/pages/Partnerships.tsx` (`/partnerships`)
- `src/pages/PunchList.tsx` (`/punch-list`)
- `src/pages/Training.tsx` (`/training/:slug`, the `WRAP` constant)

**The max widths stay as they were.** `src/index.css` opens with `* { box-sizing: border-box }`, so every wrapper was already border-box and `maxWidth: N` was already its outer width: Punch list drew 1080 outer with 32 px of padding a side, Partnerships 1000 with 16. Adding the padding to the max width, as v2.6117 did for the tally (480 → 512, 760 → 792), widens the desk by that padding rather than keeping it. The tally is left as it is here; whether to take its 32 px back is its own call.

## Left alone, and why

- `src/pages/Checklist.tsx` (`/checklist`): the `maxWidth: 1100` wrapper is the Outstanding tab's, a block child of the page's padded root (`padding: '0.25rem 1.5rem 1.5rem'`), not the flex item. A block fills its parent, so it already holds 311 of 359 at 375 (the root's 24 px padding a side) and 1100 at 1280.
- `src/pages/PartnerStatement.tsx` (`/my-statement`): same shape, the `maxWidth: 760` paper is a block child of the page's padding div, which is the flex item and stretches. Measured full at both widths.
- `src/pages/CustomerPortal.tsx`, `EstimatePublicTerms.tsx`, `GcWordAsk.tsx`, `LegalConfirm.tsx`, `LegalPortal.tsx`, `SubPortal.tsx`, `SubmittalRoom.tsx`, `SupplyHouseQuotePage.tsx` (both wrappers): public pages routed outside Layout, each under its own `data-theme="light"` block shell. `#root` is not a flex container, so the shell is a block and the centred wrapper inside it is a block child that fills; nothing sizes it by content.
- `Moneyfill.tsx`, `Quickfill.tsx`, `GcProjects.tsx`, `Messages.tsx`, `Roadmap.tsx` already carry `width: '100%'`; `Bids.tsx` and `Materials.tsx` sit on `.pageWrap`.
