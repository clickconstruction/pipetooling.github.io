# v2.7682 — Supply houses: Accounts payable keeps counting every bill as the book grows (2026-11-17)

The second half of the twelfth cut of `src/components/SupplyHousesTab.tsx`, step 12 of the *SupplyHousesTab extraction order* in `docs/MATERIALS_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md`. Unlike the eleven before it, this one changes what the page asks the database for. What the page shows is the same today.

**The risk it closes.** The API hands back at most 1,000 rows for a read with no range, with no error. Two reads on this page had none (the map's quirk 22):

- `loadSupplyHouseSummary` read every bill in one request. Past 1,000 bills the book would have been an arbitrary 1,000 of them, and the Accounts payable total, the aging table and the move window would all have been short. It reads through `fetchAllRows` now, ordered by id, a page at a time. Its fallback stands: when the read with `is_interest` is refused it is made again without the column, and a read that fails both ways leaves the book empty, as before.
- `loadSupplyHouseDetail` read the whole `supply_house_invoice_job_allocations` table, every job on every bill in the company, to draw one house. It reads the allocations of that house's bills only now, through `fetchAllRowsChunkedIn` (by bill id, in chunks, paged), after the bills have read. A house with no bills asks for none. The job names it then fetches are that house's jobs, not the company's.

Neither table is near the cap today, so nothing was being cut. This is the fix made before it is needed.

**A read that nothing used.** Opening a house ran one `purchase_order_items` query for each of its purchase orders, with the part, the house and the template joined in, and the page drew only each order's name and status. The read and its local types are gone. A house with many orders opens with that many fewer requests.

**Where.** All three are in `src/components/materials/useSupplyHouseAccountsPayable.ts`, the data hook step 9 made.

**Proof.** Three new cases in `useSupplyHouseAccountsPayable.render.test.tsx` (9 in all), over a stand-in that cuts a read with no range at 1,000 rows as the API does: a book of 1,200 bills is read whole in two pages, opening a house reads the jobs on its own bills only and never asks for its orders' items, and a house with no bills asks for no jobs. The tab's render test (27 cases) and the Materials page's pass unedited.

**Docs.** The map marks step 12 as shipped and strikes the part of quirk 22 this closes. With this the twelve cuts are done; the map's line numbers are re-read in a change of their own once this lands.
