# v2.3951 — Pipeline map: scrolling past it no longer zooms it out (2026-09-27)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Owner ask: on Jobs → Pipeline the map catches the wheel on the way down the page and zooms far out; keep the wheel for the page until the map has been clicked once.

## What shipped

- **`src/lib/map/scrollZoomGate.ts`** (pure, 9 tests): `mapScrollZoomLocked`, `leafletScrollWheelZoom`, `googleScrollwheelOption` over `{ isMobile, afterClick, clicked }`. Locked means a desktop map that asked for the gate and has not been clicked. Phones are never gated: Leaflet's wheel zoom was already off there and Google's `cooperative` gestures already decide.
- **`scrollZoomAfterClick`**, a new optional prop on `PinsMapCanvas` and `PinsMapGoogleCanvas`. Off by default, so the Bid Board map, the Dashboard map and the clocked-in map behave as before.
  - Leaflet: `MapContainer` reads `scrollWheelZoom` only at mount, so a `ScrollZoomGate` child switches `map.scrollWheelZoom` on the map itself. The click is a `pointerdown` on the container in the capture phase, because Leaflet stops a press on its + / − buttons from bubbling.
  - Google: `onPointerDownCapture` on the frame and the `scrollwheel` option, `false` until the click. `gestureHandling` stays `greedy`, so dragging is unchanged. A map without the gate does not send `scrollwheel` at all.
- **`JobsMapCard`** passes the prop to both canvases. The gate is per mount: Hide map → Show map, or a switch between the phone and desktop forms, locks it again.
- Help guide `see-the-pipeline-on-a-map` says it under *Cluster, Fit all, Hide map*.

## Verified

- `scrollZoomGate.test.ts` (9), `JobsMapCard.render.test.tsx` (15, one new: both canvases receive the prop), the Bid Board, Dashboard and clocked-in map render tests.
- In a browser, the Leaflet canvas on a scratch page (not committed): before a click the wheel over the map scrolled the page 200 px and the zoom stayed at 10; after a click on the map the wheel zoomed it and the page stayed put; a click on **+** zoomed in and also opened the gate; without the prop the wheel zoomed with no click, as before.
- Not run: the Google canvas in a browser, and the card inside the live app — this worktree has no env file and no Maps key.
