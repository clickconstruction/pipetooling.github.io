# v2.7459 — Supply houses: pay part of a bill, use a credit memo, keep money on account (2026-11-16)

PR 3 of the supply house payments train (the owner's ask of 2026-11-16; the ground is v2.7450, the window v2.7455). The window paid a bill whole or not at all. A house is paid what its statement says, and a statement nets credit memos, carries part payments and holds money on account.

## What changes

- **Part payments.** Each ticked bill in **Record a payment** has a box. Lowered, it is a part payment, and the line under the bill says what stays open. Typing **Amount sent** fills the oldest bills first, the last one in part.
- **A credit memo is a line to tick.** It lowers the money needed and is recorded as a line below zero, so the payment's lines add up to what was sent. A credit bigger than the bills ticked keeps the press dead, with the reason.
- **Money on account.** What was sent past the bills is kept only when the person ticks **Keep $… on account with** the house. The house's card shows **On account**, and the next payment offers **Use $… on account first**: that money goes on the oldest bills ticked, as lines of the earlier payment, so nothing is counted twice.
- **A bill paid in part reads what is left**, with *$300.00 paid · $512.40 left of $812.40* on its row, on the house's card and on the phone screen.
- **Every total of unpaid bills counts what is left on a bill, not its whole amount**: the house list's balance, the aging table and its cell windows (a row says *left of $812.40*), Held for suppliers (the paid part joins the paid side), the Accounts payable total on the Dashboard and in Settings, the Lien desk's supplier read and the job account move window. Each reads `supply_house_invoices.amount_paid` off the row through `openAmount`.

## Not changed here

The Dashboard's financials loader and the Bridge's due-day read are also kernels that edge functions run from a copy, so a change to either names a redeploy of every function that reaches the copy. They still count a part-paid bill whole until it is paid. That errs toward showing more owed, never less, and is left as a follow-up of its own.

## Code

- `openAmount` and `partPaidWords` in `src/lib/materials/payments/supplyPayment.ts`.
- `jobAccountsFlow.ts` takes `amount_paid` on a bill: the paid part of an unpaid bill is paid, the rest is owed.
- `SupplyHousesTab` passes `parts` to the window, maps unpaid bills to what is left for the summary and the aging table, and hands the card and the phone screen each bill's open amount with its words. `SupplyHouseBillsCard` gains the part-paid line and the **On account** number.
- The guide *record a payment to a supply house* gains three sections.
