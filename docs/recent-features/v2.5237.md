# v2.5237 — The Bridge: the three resources, and what to deal with first (link PR 6) (2026-10-11)

PR 6 of the weakest-link build (`to-dos/bridge-weakest-link/README.md`). Dev only. No migration.

- **The resources** (`src/lib/bridge/resources.ts`, `buildResources`): Decision = the piles that wait on the owner (hours to approve, prices, percents, unsorted bank rows, bids with no value), counted and named in one line, throttling the link when any wait and the link is a pile stage; Capital = the bank's balance and the forecast's lowest point against the floor, always "symptom of the link"; Talent = who clocked field time in the last 7 days and who sent a bill in the window ("7 in the field · 1 billing"), throttling Bill or Collect when one person bills and Build when fewer than five are in the field.
- **The cards** (`BridgeResources.tsx`, under the dial): a one-line head, the action pill, the gauge in its tone, the line, and the badge. Pressing a card opens its desk (`BridgeLinkDesk` already knew the three resources' words); the dial keeps the lit stage's.
- **The order** (`order.ts`, `buildOrder`; `BridgeOrderModal.tsx`): opened from any badge on the eight cards. The rule in one line (`ORDER_RULE`), then five tiers — First (the link), With it (what throttles it), Next (the stage that would be the link after it), Follows (the other pile stages and the symptom resources), Waits (the feeders, then any stage the app cannot read yet) — each row with its rank, badge, the reading that put it there and its first move from the desk's presets. The pressed badge's row is highlighted with a line naming its tier and why. The order follows the dial while it is moved, and the head says so.
- **The page**: the pick is a `CallLink` now (a stage or a resource); the stage cards, the resources and the desk read it; the dial reads the lit stage.
- **Tests**: `resources.test.ts` (3), `order.test.ts` (2), `BridgeResources.render.test.tsx` (2: the cards and the badge; the modal and its overlay).
- **Docs**: guide *read the Bridge* gains "The three resources" and "What to deal with first"; the Glossary's Bridge entry; the to-do's status line.
