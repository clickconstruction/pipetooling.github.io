# v2.7419 — Lien desk: a chip's card names the stages a late paper still has to pass (2026-11-15)

Client only. Taunya's question of 2026-11-15 on the Board's hover card (v2.7381): "what does it mean 3 stages remain?" The card counted the stages and did not name them. Her word the same day: name them.

## What was wrong

A chip that will not make its day said *Will not make its day as it stands. 3 stages left and 1 working day.* The three stages were the bins the paper still has to pass, which only the Board's own layout showed.

## What landed

- `src/lib/jobs/lienBoard.ts`: the stages left are lists of names now, one per bin (`NOTICE_STAGES_LEFT`, `AFFIDAVIT_STAGES_LEFT`), read through `lienBoardStagesLeft(chip, leader)`. A chip's `stepsLeft` is its list's length, so the count the Board races against the working days and the names on the card cannot disagree. The counts are what they were: 4, 3, 2, 1 down each column, and 2 for a held paper, which waits on the leader again.
- `src/lib/jobs/lienBoardChipCard.ts`: the card carries `left`, the named stages of a chip that is at risk, and its risk line reads *Will not make its day as it stands. Still to do, with 1 working day left:*. A paper that will make its day names none; its bin and its press already say what is next. `lienBoardCardHeight` adds a line per stage, so a taller card still goes over a chip it would not fit under.
- `LienBoard.tsx`: the list is an ordered list under the risk line (`data-lien-board-card-left`). `LienDesk.tsx` hands the card the job's own leader by name; with none known the stage reads *The leader*.
- The Board guide's *Point at a chip* says so.

## Verified

`lienBoard.test.ts` (every bin's names, one name per counted stage in all twelve bins, plain words), `lienBoardChipCard.test.ts` (the list on a late paper, none on one that will make it, the leader unknown, no working days left, the taller card's place), `LienBoard.render.test.tsx` (the list drawn on a chip in Needs the owner, none on one with days in hand); eslint on the touched files; typecheck.
