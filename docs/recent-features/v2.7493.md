# v2.7493 — Lien desk: By job grouped by property (2026-11-16)

PR 3 of 3 of *one property whole* (the kernel is v2.7485, the property card v2.7488).

## What a person sees

By job (`/lien-desk?tab=timeline`) has a third picker, **Group**, beside **GC** and **Show**: *None* (as before, the default) and *By property*.

Grouped, the rows the two filters left stand in sections:

- one section per property that holds more than one job, where its first row stood, so the property with the soonest next date leads. Its head is the address (a press that opens the property card), then the owner, the count, the money open and the tally: *Avery and Jordan Pell · 7 jobs · $15,870 open · 4 notices sent Oct 14 · 2 on Sep 22 · 1 to print and mail · 6 lien affidavits ready to file*.
- **One job each**, with the count of properties: most properties hold one job, and their rows read as they did.
- **No property record**, with the count of jobs. Each row keeps its **Set the property ›** door.

A head is the whole property, from the whole book. When a filter hides some of its jobs the head ends *· 2 shown*, so a filter never makes a property look smaller than it is. Under its own head a row drops its *N jobs here ›* line.

On a phone the picker is a thumb tall and the head wraps under the address.
