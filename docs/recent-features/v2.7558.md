# v2.7558 — Lien notices: a notice printed from its Lien window comes out a set per envelope, each behind its divider (2026-11-17)

Two asks of the owner's, 2026-11-16. First, an office teammate printed a § 53.056 notice from its Lien window. The help sheet named two envelopes and told her to copy every page for the second. Second, after scans of a run divider and of that help sheet: *I would like to include a page like the following in front of every notice … put it in front of each bundle for an envelope so there are no discrepancies that would be behind the help sheet but before the packet.* Then he marked up the help sheet line by line, approved a drawing of it, and asked for the weekday beside the days left. Client only, no migration.

## What the window printed

The help sheet, then one set: the notice built fresh from the job, the pay page, the invoices. Step 1 said *Copy every page behind this sheet*. Step 5 had two short blanks for the certified numbers. The desk's *Just this one* opened this window too, until v2.7512 sent it to the run.

## What changed

- **A set per envelope, each behind the run's divider.** `LienFilingTabs.tsx` builds the window's notice as a `RunNotice` and prints through `lienNoticeOneOff.ts`, which runs it through the run's own `runEnvelopes`, `runMailing`, `runCopyPages`, `runDividerHtml` and `runPageFoot`. One name at one address folds into one envelope. An envelope with no mailing address is held with the run's reason. A divider's count and its *Inside:* amount are read off the pages behind it, and a test holds each divider and page equal to what `runPacketHtml` prints for the same notice.
- **No signature from the window.** The notice here is drawn fresh from the job, and the desk's signature is bound to the desk's own approved draft. The `RunNotice` leaves `signature` out (null would hold every envelope as unsigned). The press's line says so before the print, and the sheet keeps the ink step.
- **The press says what it prints:** *Print 2 envelopes*, *Print 1 envelope · 1 held ▸*, dead with nothing to print (`batchWords`). Under it, *Reprint envelope 2 · the original contractor ›* prints one set behind its divider.
- **Filed once.** The copy filed on the job is the sets alone, every envelope once, with no help sheet and no divider.
- **The help sheet, as the owner marked it up** (`lienHelpSheet.ts`): the head *§ 53.056 notice · Job N* (no *before you mail* under *do not mail this page*); the claim's cents a size down; the mail-by day large, its weekday beside the days left from the print day (`lienHelpMailByWords`); who to ask on its own line, to the right; checks for the claim behind each divider and for the signature; a box per envelope that matches its divider, and one for each held; five or six steps, each a bold line with a line under it. Match each divider. Sign in ink, only when unsigned. Stuff and pull the divider. Mail by the day. Record with a photo of each receipt (v2.7557), which merges the old steps 6 and 7 and drops the blanks. Keep the receipts, the green cards and the dividers, which prove the notice was mailed on time for a lien and a lawsuit to collect.

## Still different from the run

The run's front page is *Today's mail*; this window's is the help sheet, on purpose, one door each. The run's notice is the approved draft, signed, with counsel's letter in the owner's set; the window's is drawn fresh, unsigned, with no letter. **Download PDF** on the tab still builds one set.
