# v2.6653 — Report by line: the Pipeline bar shows each line at the number the crew gave it (2026-11-07)

Punch list #130's close-out walk (a helper on a ZZ job with four priced lines, no Order row, through the dev server as BOBBY D): the Leave Report and Update % done doors filed per-line numbers (50 · — · 25 · — then 50 · 100 · 25 · —), the job read 22% then 34%, the reports carried the *Stage progress* sentence, and `jobs_ledger_fixtures.progress_*` held the stamps — but the Pipeline's Progress & payment cell drew the first line full and the Gas line at 72%. No migration; no edge function.

## What was wrong

v2.5665 made `lines` mode fill each segment from its own `progress_pct` — but the fill it set (`fillPct`) has had no reader on the board since v2.4351, when the bar became the **money** bar: each block is a line, and its slices are the money poured onto it (green paid, blue billed, amber done-not-billed, grey the rest). The amber was the job's one done-not-billed figure (% done × bid − paid − billed) poured left to right by `pourMoney`, so the lines' own numbers never reached the screen, and the guide's *What it affects* panel described a fill the board did not draw.

## Change

- **`progressPaymentCell.ts`**: `pourMoney` takes an optional `ownDone` map (each line's done fraction). In `lines` mode, once any priced line carries the crew's number (`ownNumbers`, the v2.5665 rule), the amber on each block is that line's own number's worth past what is paid or billed on it; a line with no number has none; paid and billed still pour as they did (a bill that names a line takes it first). A job with no line numbers pours the job's percent down the lines as before. The segment's `title` already printed the own fill (*Rough In · $3,500 · 50% done*); the block's hover (`blockMoneyWords`) now agrees with it.
- **Tests**: the v2.5665 case asserts the amber per block in both readings; a new case covers a line billed past its number (no amber) and a line said done past its bill.
- **Guide** `set-job-progress-on-stages.md` (*What it affects*): the sentence now says the amber part of each block is the crew's number for that line.
- **Card** `to-dos/report-by-line/README.md`: the walk's record; the count's second reading is what is left.

## Verified

`progressPaymentCell.test.ts`, `jobMoneyBar.test.ts`, the guide tests; the dev server's board on the walk's job after the change: Water heater 50% amber, Gas line full amber, Slab leak repair 25%, Fixture set grey, tick at 34%.
