## What changed

- **[`src/lib/bids/difficultyPaying.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/difficultyPaying.ts)**: the Paying scale read from what the app keeps about how the GC pays. `readDifficultyPaying` takes the GC's own median and sample count and its measured payments (`get_billed_customer_pay_speeds`, as the Pipeline reads them), the GC pace here (`payPace.ts`) and the GC's pay calendar replayed against those payments (`latenessAgainst`'s arithmetic, counted as `lateBillsInYear`), against the rule: a point when the median is past the days (45), or enough bills in the last year (2) landed after their calendar's due day; fewer bills than the minimum (3) reads no point and says so; a refused read says the bills could not be read. The rule is the default here until the Bid Board's price list carries it (PR 4). `difficultyPayingWords` is the line under the scale (*Paid in 52 days, the median of 7 bills. The GC pace is 35 days. 2 bills in the last year went past their calendar.*), `difficultyPayingRuleWords` the rule in a sentence, `difficultyPayingPick` what a save carries (the by-hand point with *By hand: the reason*, else the read with its words), `difficultyPayingDriftWords` the line when the bills and the rating that stands disagree. [`difficultyPayingIo.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/difficultyPayingIo.ts) makes the three reads, each failing on its own.
- **`gc_difficulty_ratings.pick_reasons`** ([the migration](../migrations/20261115010000_gc_difficulty_pick_reasons.md)): a bills scale's point carries its words, and the guard refuses one without. The read point is written into the rating with the numbers behind it, so the rating stays a record with reasons and the chips count it without a live read.
- **The card**: under Paying, the numbers behind the read, the rule, and for the office **Set by hand…** (a point and a reason, **Use this**; *Read from the bills again* takes it off). The read point counts in the total and in the save; when the bills now read a point the rating does not hold, an amber line says so and the save press wakes. An estimator, whose role the pay-speed read refuses, reads *Their bills could not be read, so the point is not known.*
- **From the live walk of PR 2**: *Rate them* stays on the opened row's GC/Builder fact only; on the list rows and the phone cards twenty links on one screen was loud, so those draw the chip alone. The chip on the Bid Board now draws whatever the GC lines show (a bid with one packet to its GC hid it). The Workbench's chip moved out of the Workbench block's portal to the title line beside the *Bids like this* slot, so a bid with nothing to price yet shows it too.
- The guide *rate how a GC is to work with* says how Paying is read and set by hand.

## Not changed

- The rule's numbers are the kernel's defaults until PR 4's price list; nothing is priced.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **Doubted: writing the read point into the rating.** A live read on every screen would always be current, but the chips would need the pay-speed read (refused to estimators) on every board, and a priced bid pins to a snapshot anyway. Kept the write: the rating holds the point with its words, the card shows the live read beside it and wakes the save when they differ, and the next save reads it in.
- **Doubted: counting late bills with no calendar.** Without a pay calendar there is no due day, so the late count is null and only the median rules. The alternative, a guessed net-30, would have put a point on a GC whose own terms are net-45. Kept null.
- **Doubted: a by-hand point.** It is the door back to a number with no reason. Kept it narrow: a reason is required, the words carry *By hand:* so the Workbench and the bid can show it as such, and the read's drift line stays quiet while a by-hand point stands.
- **Left for Micah, with my pick:** the default rule (45 days, 2 late in a year, 3 bills). These are the canvas's numbers. The price list makes them the office's in PR 4; my pick is to leave them until then.
