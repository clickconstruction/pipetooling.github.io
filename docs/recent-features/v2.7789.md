# v2.7789 — Lien desk: By paper's paper tabs ride the title line when it has room (2026-11-18)

The owner's ask of 2026-11-18, with a screenshot of By paper, a red box around the three paper tabs and another around the empty stretch of the title line between the views and the tools: *"in this view, when it can fit, I would like to move this section up into this space, but when it cant fit it should wrap like it has been"*.

## Before

- On By paper the title line held the way back, the views and the tools, with a long empty stretch between the views and § Rules.
- The second line started with *Monthly notices*, *Lien affidavits* and *Retainage notices*. The pile chips followed them, or fell to a third line when the two did not fit together. Lien affidavits, with five or six chips, was three lines in every window under about 1,400 px.

## What changed

- **The paper tabs sit on the title line when the measured line holds them.** `lienDeskPapersFit(width, parts, papers)` in `lienDeskTitleBar.ts` answers `title_line` when the line holds the way back, the views, the paper tabs and the four presses with a gap between each pair, and `second_line` otherwise. No viewport rule and no CSS wrap decides it.
- **A line too narrow keeps them where they were**, leading the second line.
- **The piles and *Put a GC on notice…* stay on the line under.** `lienDeskTitleBarLayout` takes `papersFit` and `piles` and answers where the papers go (`papers`). With the papers up, the second line is the piles alone on Lien affidavits and Retainage notices, and the GC door alone at the right on Monthly notices. A view with neither is one line.
- **The pile line is measured without the papers once they are up** (`lienDeskPileLineFit`'s third argument).
- **The hook** `useLienDeskTitleBarFit` returns `papersFit` and watches the tab row again after it changes lines. `LienDesk.tsx` draws the row in one of two places so the page's order is the order a keyboard walks.
- **A phone is untouched.** It is never measured and its paper tabs are its second row.

## Measured

On the live desk through the dev server, 2026-11-18, light and dark, with that day's counts (16, 11 and 7):

| Window | Monthly notices | Lien affidavits | Retainage notices |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1,440 px | papers up, 2 lines (the GC door under) | papers up, 2 lines, was 3 or 2 | papers up, 2 lines |
| 1,280 px | papers up, 2 lines | papers up, 2 lines, was 3 | papers up, 2 lines |
| 1,024 px | as before, 2 lines | as before, 3 lines | as before, 2 lines |
| 900 px | as before, 2 lines | as before, 4 lines | as before, 2 lines |

A sweep of the window from 1,440 px down to 700 and back, 4 px a step, with no reload: the papers are up from 1,176 px, and also from 1,100 to 1,131, where `index.css` draws the bar tighter (7 px tabs, an 8 px gap, Share as its icon). From 1,132 to 1,175 and under 1,100 they lead the second line. The bar never overflowed and gave the same answer narrowing and widening.

## Tests

- `lienDeskTitleBar.test.ts`: the fit at its edge (1,089 px holds the line, 1,088 does not), the four window widths, longer counts, papers with no width, a bar with no way back, the layout with the papers up on each kind of paper, the pile line measured without them, and a phone.
- `LienDesk.render.test.tsx`: the desk drawn at a bar that holds the papers (one line with no piles, the piles under, the GC door under), one px short of it, with longer counts, too narrow for the presses, and on a phone.
- Each was run against the rule broken three ways (never up, always up, no break under), between two passing controls.

## Not in this change

- *Put a GC on notice…* could join the title line too where there is room, which would make Monthly notices one line in a wide window. Not asked, so not built.
- The band from 1,132 to 1,175 px, where the roomier padding of a wider window puts the papers back under, is left as measured.
