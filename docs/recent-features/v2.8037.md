# v2.8037 — Checklist: Waiting For starts folded, as one row with its count (2026-11-20)

The owner walked the app on an iPhone on 2026-11-20 and circled the whole section: *in the checklist page I would like the waiting for section to be collapsed by default*. Waiting For lists what will be a person's once someone else's step is done. It is reference, not work, and open it ran longer than the day's own tasks (736 px against one task card on the dev's phone-width Today). No migration and no edge function.

## What a person sees

- **Checklist → Today**, under the tasks and *＋ Add a task for me*: one folded row in a quiet dashed box. It reads **⏳ Waiting For**, then *17 tasks behind 6 steps · after "clear back half of floor" — Robert*, with a ⌄ at its end.
- **A press** opens it to the same grouped list as before, the first three steps and *…and N more steps ahead*. The row then reads *yours, once the step ahead clears* and its mark is ⌃. A second press folds it.
- **Folded on every load.** Nothing is remembered, on purpose: the owner asked for folded by default, and the Dashboard's strip does not remember either.
- **Task Mode** shows the same fold, since it draws the same tab. A look at another person's Task Mode can open it too, because the look lets a press with `aria-expanded` through (`lookAllowsPress`).
- With nothing waiting the section still draws nothing.

## One pattern, not two

The Dashboard's My Inbox card has folded this list since v2.2650: a dashed box on `--bg-subtle`, a count and a peek at the first step while folded, ⌄ and ⌃, the group cards on `--surface` once open, and no memory. The Checklist's fold copies that shell and reuses its peek (`buildWaitingForStripSummary`), so the two read alike. The Checklist has room for a second line, so it spells the count out in words (*5 tasks behind 2 steps*) where the strip has a bare pill. The Dashboard's strip is unchanged.
