# v2.7791 — Job Mode: a date in a job name reads as the day and the month and day (2026-11-18)

Punch list #384 (Job Mode, short), from the look. Client only. No migration and no edge function.

## Why

The owner looked at the short Job Mode page as screenshots. The test job on the card is named with the day it was made, *ZZ Test Walk Project 2026-11-08*. He asked for the day of the week, and for the date as the month and day alone. The date is part of the job's name, so the card had nothing of its own to reformat. He picked the rule: a date typed into a job's name is drawn the plain way on this page.

## What landed

- **The rule** is `jobNameWithPlainDates` in `lib/jobNameDate.ts`, pure and tested. A real calendar date written year, month, day with dashes becomes `Sun, Nov 8`. The year is dropped.
- **What is left alone.** Numbers that are not a day on the calendar, a date inside a longer run of digits or dashes such as a part number, and a date written any other way. A name with no date comes back unchanged.
- **Where it is drawn.** On the Job Mode card (`DashboardJobModeCard`): the title's name in all three headers and each row of the day's rail, with the row's spoken label. On the short page's **Tomorrow** line (`plainLabel` in `lib/jobMode/jobModeShort.ts`). In the lines of Your record, which the short page calls **Waiting on you** (`jobWord` in `lib/dashboardYourRecord.ts`).
- **The name is not changed.** Only the words on the screen are. What the card hands to a window (Leave report, On my way, the machine stops) is the name as typed, so nothing saved carries the rewritten form.

## Who it changes

Everyone in Job Mode sees it, on the long page and the short one, since the card's top is one component. Your record on the full Dashboard reads the same way. Today only test jobs are named with a date.

## Not done

- The job window, My Schedule's cards, the pace card's header and the Job updates feed still show the name as typed. They are behind a press on the short page.
- The pattern uses no lookbehind, because an older phone's browser refuses one when the app loads.

## Checked

- `jobNameDate.test.ts`: the words for a date, a date at the end and in the middle of a name, two dates in one name, a name with none, and every case that only looks like a date.
- `jobModeShort.test.ts` and `dashboardYourRecord.test.ts`: the Tomorrow line and a Waiting on you line with a dated name.
- `DashboardJobModeCard.nameDate.render.test.tsx`: clocked in and ready to start, the card says *Sun, Nov 8* and nowhere says *2026-11-08*, and a name with no date is as it was. With the title's call taken out, the first case fails.
- Seen on the dev server at phone width as the sample superintendent, with a day of stops held in the browser. Nothing was written.
