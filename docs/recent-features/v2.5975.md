# v2.5975 — What everyone sees draws every frame after an update, and names the customer's side (2026-10-27)

Punch list #194, the live check against the mock-up ([`to-dos/experiences-lens/`](../../to-dos/experiences-lens/README.md)). The lens landed as #6290 (v2.5960 to v2.5972). Opened on clicktooling.com the morning after, Settings → What everyone sees showed three gaps.

- **Five of six frames came up empty on the first visit after the deploy.** Each empty frame had fetched the 6.6 MB main bundle through the service worker, and the fetch returned 0 bytes after 30 seconds, so the seal never ran and the root stayed empty. The first frame, a moment ahead of the others, loaded in 117 ms. A reload of the page, or of one frame, drew every frame, because by then the bundle sat in memory. Runs on the dev server never showed it.
  - Frames now load one after another (`admittedFrames` in `src/lib/lens/lensTab.ts`): the tab lets in every frame that has loaded and the first one that has not. Once one frame has the app, the rest load from memory. A frame waiting its turn says *Waiting its turn…*. A frame that never fires its load lets the next one in after 4 seconds.
  - A frame whose load fires with no app in it is drawn once more, once: a sealed frame with no `window.__lens`, or a customer page with an empty root, 1.5 seconds after its load.
- **The customer's side had no name on screen.** The words above the card say the customer's pages are under *Who, outside*, but that group carried the name only as an `aria-label`. Its chips ran on from the roles, and *Subcontractor* showed twice. It is now its own row, **Who, outside**, as the mock-up set it apart.
- **On a phone the knobs' names sat in a side column**, centred on rows four lines tall, beside a strip of empty card. Each name now sits beside its buttons and wraps above them where the card is too narrow for both, as the mock-up drew it at 375.

**Checked:** on the live site before the fix, a fresh visit showed 1 frame drawn and 5 empty, with a 30-second fetch of 0 bytes in each. In every drawn frame, 0 requests reached Supabase, nothing was caught, and the mark read *Lens · invented data · Estimator*. On the dev server, signed in through dev-login, the six frames came in one after another and all six were sealed within 5.3 seconds. At 375 every knob's name sits above its buttons with no sideways scroll, and at 1280 the two Who rows sit side by side. The tab's render tests load the frames in turn, and two new tests cover the one retry and the 4-second turn. `lensTab.test.ts` covers `admittedFrames`, and the guide names the row and the loading order.
