# v2.8330 — Your jobs on a map: fold it from its title, and it opens on the office (2026-11-23)

The owner's ask of 2026-11-23, on a screenshot of the Dashboard on his phone: the card should fold by touching its header like Assigned Jobs and the reports, and the map should always open centred on the office at about a hundred miles. One far job had zoomed his map out to half the country, and the jobs near the office were one blob. No migration, no edge function, and no change to the shared map frame or its canvases.

## The fold

- **Before:** a text link at the head's right, *Hide* on a phone and *Hide map* at a desk, *Show map* to bring it back.
- **Now:** the head is the press, as `DashboardGroupCard` draws it for Assigned Jobs: a ▼ or ▶, the pin and the title in one button with `aria-expanded` (`data-jobs-map-fold`). On a phone the button fills the head and is 44 px tall, so a touch anywhere on the bar folds the card. A folded card is the head's band alone.
- **The same at a desk.** The other sections fold at every width, so this one does too, and *Hide map* is gone there as well.
- **The memory is the one it had.** The fold reads and writes `pipetooling_dashboard_jobs_map_hidden`, the key *Hide map* wrote, so a map somebody hid before this version stays hidden.
- **The head's own press sits beside the fold, never inside it.** At a desk the counts and **Fit all** are the fold's siblings, so a press on Fit all leaves the card open. On a phone the head holds the fold alone, and the counts and the press share the line under it.

## The office view

The kernel is `src/lib/map/officeView.ts`, pure.

- **Centre:** the office, from `resolveOfficeAnchor` (the `office_address_v1` setting, else the Map default view's centre).
- **About a hundred miles** is read from the office to the nearer edge of the frame, so it is set from the frame's shorter side. A phone's frame is wider than tall and a narrow column's is taller; the kernel takes whichever is shorter.
- **Whole zoom steps.** Both maps zoom in whole steps and a step doubles the distance, so no frame can show exactly a hundred. The kernel takes the step nearest it. At the card's real sizes that is the same step for both: a phone's 260 px frame shows about 86 miles up and down, a desk's 360 px frame about 119. Every frame lands between 71 and 141.
- **No canvas changed.** Both canvases already take `fitPoints`, the points a fit frames in place of the pins. The kernel answers a box around the office that both canvases' fit lands on its zoom for: as large as the frame less their 28 px padding allows, so it fits at that zoom and not at the next. `officeView.test.ts` reads the 28 off both canvases, so a change to either's padding fails the test. No other map opens differently.
- **The map waits for the office.** `useOfficeAnchorState` (new, beside `useOfficeAnchor`) says when the read has ended, so the map draws once on its first view and never fits the pins and then jumps.
- **A folded card opened again opens on the office view.** The view is a visit's state, not a stored one.

## A job outside the view

- **It is named.** A line under the map reads *1 job is outside the office view. Fit all shows every job.* The count is drawn as a value (`ValueSentence`). It counts against the office view, a named thing, so the words stay true after the person drags the map.
- **One press, two ways.** **Fit all** frames every pin, and the same press then reads **Office view** and comes back. It is in the head at a desk and beside the counts on a phone, which had no Fit all before.
- **The counts count every job**, in view or not, as they did. They sit away from the map's view line so the two are not read as one.
- **Every pin still draws.** The view only frames; nothing is filtered.

## When there is no office

With no office address on record, or a read that fails, there is no office view: the map fits its pins as it did, **Fit all** is the plain re-fit it was, and nothing about an office view is said.

## What was decided, and what is the owner's call

- **Always a hundred miles, as he said.** If every job sits within a few miles of the office the pins will be close together at this zoom, and **Fit all** is one press away. The other reading, *a hundred miles unless every job fits closer in*, was not built: it would make the first view differ from day to day, and he asked for one that does not.
- **No office mark on the map.** The canvases can draw the office as a labelled diamond, as the Pipeline's map does. Its label is always on and sat on the jobs nearest the office, which is where most of them are. Left out.
