# v2.7228 — Paint a map: painted maps on the Map page and the site map (2026-11-13)

Punch list #345, PR 6, the second half, and the last PR the card drew. A painted map so far showed under an address. This puts it on the two maps the app already has, so the way in is seen beside the pins and beside the boxes.

## What changed

- **The Map page** has a chip, **Painted maps**, beside Precincts. On, it draws every painted map the reader can open: the lines in the paint's colour over a white casing, a stretch the phone was not sure of as dots, the places as numbered-order dots. The chip counts the maps. Off, the page reads none of it.
- **A pointer over a line** reads the map's name, its length and how many places it holds. **A press** on a line or a dot opens that painted map in its own window.
- **Materials on site's map** draws the job's own painted maps under the boxes: on the job window's Materials tab (**Map**) and on the phone's site map. A site with no pinned box and no placed address now opens on its painted line.

## How it is built

- `PaintedMapsLayer` is a child of any react-leaflet map. `usePaintedMapsLayer` reads one job's maps or every map, only while its layer is on, and again when a painted map is saved or thrown away. A failed read is an empty layer.
- `paintedMapLayer.ts`, pure: a map as a layer draws it. A map with nothing on it is left out.
- `loadPaintedMapsEvery` reads the newest 400 maps. Their lines and places are now read a page at a time and a hundred maps at a time: one read stops at 1,000 rows, and four hundred ids in one address is longer than an address may be. The same change serves a development with many lots.

## Is this the best we can do

- **Off until asked for.** The Map page is the office's heaviest page. The layer costs nothing until its chip is pressed, as Precincts does.
- **Hidden while court areas are being drawn.** A press on the map means *add a corner* in that mode, and a painted line under the pointer would take the press.
- **No making press on the Map page.** A map made there would be on no job. The search's **Paint a map** already starts one with a job picked, so a second way to make a homeless map would only make more of them.
- **Not on a task's small map.** That map is a picture inside a list and must not take a press. It is the next PR's question, with the task's own place.

## Not built

- **PR 5, a task's place carrying the painted map that leads to it.** It needs a migration on the task tables, and it is better decided after the first maps exist: which map leads to a place is a pick somebody has to make, and nobody has painted one yet.
- **Text it…** from the company's number, and a sub's work order page showing the map: each opens a painted map to someone new, and each is the owner's call.
