# v2.6392 — People → HR: the person file, one Timeline of everything on record, with evidence as dated records (2026-11-02)

The People → Paperwork design spike's first cut (the owner, 2026-11-02: the four sub-tabs are pieces of an incomplete solution; a person needs an evidence timeline and a narrative, reachable from MCP, so the full profile and the actions as they happen are on record). ## What was found

- The HR tab had been a snapshot since the day it shipped: six field reports pending, the oldest two written 2026-08-24, none ever filed or dismissed; two people with a file, every one of 55 entries written 2026-08-24. The dev's Needs You item had stood since day three.
- Contracts, Licenses, Writeups and HR were four tables with three different keys and three audiences, and nothing about a person accumulated anywhere by itself.

## Change

- **The Timeline** (`src/lib/people/personTimeline.ts`, 18 tests): one dated line per thing on record, on the day it is ABOUT, never the day it was written. Lanes Said · Did · Paper · Missed · Ahead. Sources: the HR entries by source; a pending field report as *waiting to be filed* (a filed one is left to the entry it became, a dismissed one keeps its reason); a contract as signed with the signer named and the sent day kept, sent and waiting, or owed, plus the office section's own line and any expiry; a license on file and its expiry (lapsed → Missed, ahead → Ahead); a write-up with its disclosure, a draft as a draft; a no-show; an excused late; started, last day, archived. Month groups, lane and source filters, counts, the fact strip (`personFileFacts`, `personFileFactChips`: reports waiting, licenses expired, papers owed, sent and waiting, no-shows in 90 days, signed, on file, write-ups, entries, evidence), empty words.
- **The reads** (`personTimelineIo.ts`): every source under the viewer's own RLS, each on its own and fail-soft; a source the role may not open is named under the strip (*dev only* · *needs a login* · *needs a roster row*), never guessed at.
- **Evidence as records** (`personEvidence.ts`, 11 tests; `personEvidenceIo.ts`): a piece of evidence = `dated_on` · `title` · `memo` · files. `evidenceDraftProblem` requires all four, the day not ahead of today, a title under 160, a memo under 4,000, at least one file under 10 MB. The record is inserted, each file staged in the private `hr-files` bucket with an attachment row naming the record, then `hr-evidence-drive` (v2.6393) is asked, fail-soft: a function not deployed or a Drive refusal leaves the record *on its way to Drive* / *not in Drive: why* with **Try Drive again**. Until the migration is pushed the table reads as no records (42P01) and a save says why.
- **The panel** (`src/components/people/personFile/PersonFilePanel.tsx`): Timeline | Evidence | Narrative under the fact strip. A line = day · kind chip · words · notes · file chips · by and written · a door to the page that owns the record. A waiting report carries **File to the record** / **Dismiss…** (the queue's own RPCs). **+ Add entry** opens the composer (the day it happened · kind · words · attach files); files attached to an entry become an evidence record on that entry, titled by the entry's first line. Evidence: records newest first (day · title · memo · file chips · Drive state · Open the folder in Drive › or Try Drive again · by · kept), then *Files from before records*. **Add evidence**: the day it is about · Title · Memo · + Pick files / Take a photo (an image over 600 KB shrinks through `shrinkPhoto`) · *Where it goes: People › <person> › <day · title>* · Keep the evidence; the problem line names the missing piece. Narrative: the summary with its freshness and stale banner, then the narrative with its jump list, as before.
- **The HR tab** (`PeopleHrTab.tsx`): the right pane is the panel; the queue folds to one line while a file is open (**Show the queue** unfolds; `HrPendingReportsSection` gained `reloadKey`, `collapsed`, `onExpand`); on a phone the roster and the file take turns (**‹ All people**). A person with no login wears a *no login* chip. Render smoke rewritten (3 tests).
- **Docs**: `HR_FILES.md` (the tab, the evidence), `GLOSSARY.md` → HR files, `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` → People → HR, `PEOPLE_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md` → the hr row, `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` → the People page, the HR report guide's *What happens after you send it*.
