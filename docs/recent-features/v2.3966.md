## What shipped

- **`src/lib/jobs/jobFormSliceWrites.ts`**, each taking `supabase`:
  - `writeBillingSlice` — the job's revenue → its payments, diffed against the ids the form last knew were saved → its line items, deleted and re-inserted one at a time → the discount trail, logged without waiting. It tells the form which payments are saved (`onPaymentsWritten`) before it touches the line items, and which discounts (`onDiscountsWritten`) before it logs — the two points where the form's refs were updated.
  - `writeMaterialsSlice` — delete, then re-insert. `writeTeamSlice` — read, add the missing, remove the dropped.
  - `writeNewJobChildRows` — a new job's payments → materials → line items → team.
  - `autosaveFailureWords` — the four slices' identical "Autosave failed: …".
- **`src/lib/jobs/jobFormCloseSideEffects.ts`** — `closeDateMetBackfillNeeded` and `closeDemoteToBilledNeeded`; the form had each rule twice (whether closing has work to do, and doing it).
- **The shell's** `persistBillingSlice`, `persistMaterialsSlice` and `persistTeamSlice` read their refs, call the writer and toast a failure; `createJob` calls `writeNewJobChildRows`; the close guard asks the two checks. Every autosave slice is still registered in the form, at the place it was. `JobFormModal.tsx` is 3,698 → 3,611 lines. Behavior-neutral.
- **Kept as they are, and pinned by tests:**
  - Nothing is transactional. A failed write stops the sequence where it is and what ran before it stays written — a line item that fails part-way leaves the ones before it written and the payments saved.
  - A new job's child inserts are awaited and their answers are not read: a refused row stops nothing and is not reported.
  - The transactional `TODO(billing)` the map asked for sits at the head of the kernel.
- **Not moved:** the identity slice's writer (one update between two reads of the bid and the dispatch auto-closes) stays inline; only its failure words are shared.
- **Tests** — `jobFormSliceWrites.test.ts` (19) over a client that records every call and can refuse any one of them; `jobFormCloseSideEffects.test.ts` (7).
- The map's save-engine section, Stage-A table and move 9 say what is done and what is left.
