# v2.3885 — Schedule on a phone: the Day tab's block sheet (2026-09-27)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Punch list **#30**, PR 4b-2 (`to-dos/taunya-mobile.md`; the mock-up `to-dos/taunya-mobile-pr4-inbox-day.html`) — with it PR 4 is built. Taunya's one recorded phone complaint was *"I need to move J1004 to Friday"*; from the Day tab a block tap went to the job's week grid, the desktop's screen.

## What shipped

- **`src/components/schedule/ScheduleBlockSheet.tsx`** (new) — `ScheduleSheet` and `ScheduleSheetAction` lifted verbatim out of `HubPeoplePhoneBoard`, and `ScheduleBlockSheet` over them: Open the job · the note row (*Add a note* / *Edit the note* with the note read back) · Copy to techs · Move · Remove, each drawn only when its handler is passed; the Move row's words are the caller's.
- **`HubPeoplePhoneBoard.tsx`** — draws its block sheet through `ScheduleBlockSheet` with the same five verbs and handlers as before; its copy sheet uses `ScheduleSheet`. No change on screen.
- **`QuickfillScheduleSection.tsx`** — `onPhoneBlockTap`: in agenda mode, a tap on a block hands the block's row to the host. Without the prop the tap goes to the job's week, as before.
- **`ScheduleDispatchHub.tsx`** — the Day tab passes it and the shell draws the sheet from the callbacks it already had: Open the job (`onOpenHubJobDetail`), the note (`onRequestEditBlockNote`), **Move or reassign** (`onRequestMoveBlock` → the page's `ScheduleDispatchMoveBlockSheet`, day chips and a person pick) and Remove (`onDeleteBlock`, the page's confirm). The edit verbs need `canEdit`; a reader gets Open the job alone. The Day section re-reads on its own after a write — it already listens to the day's blocks.
- **The guide** *schedule-dispatch* → *The Day view on a phone*.

## Left, on the card

The sheet is on the Schedule page's Day tab only. Quickfill mounts the section bare, with no hub to own the move sheet and the note modal, so a tap there still opens the job's week — leftover L8. Dispatch Mode's Schedule tab is a different screen (its own agenda: a tap on the time edits the block's times and day, a tap on the job opens it) and is not part of this. Press-and-hold to drag a block within the day (drawn as a hint) is not built.
