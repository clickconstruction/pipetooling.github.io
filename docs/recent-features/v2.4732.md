The owner's call on the follow-up question: the edit does **not** change the bill's line in Job details — the bill already went out, Stripe will not change a sent bill's lines, and the enclosed bill must match the one the customer has.

## What changed

- **`payPageDescription`** (`lib/jobs/noticeInvoiceEnclosure.ts`) skips lines `payLineIsNotTheWork` names — a check-mailing or remittance note, a *(migrated)* placeholder — in the service lines and the scope; a bill left with nothing prints no line.
- **`payPageBlocks`** takes `lines` (the office's typed lines by invoice id; `applyPayLines`), and each `payRow` carries `field: payLineField(invoiceId)`. `changedPayLines` keeps only lines that differ from the bill's.
- **`filingDocHtml`**: with marks, a pay row's line is the notice's typed box (`payRowLineHtml`): amber, a ghost *no line · click to add one* when empty, dotted once locked, and *changed on this page only · the bill keeps its own line* with **Back to the bill's line** (`data-reset`). Print and the PDF pass no marks and stay plain.
- **`LienDeskDraftFields.payLines`** (parsed by `parseLienDeskDraftFields`) carries them; `RunNotice.payLines` hands them to `runPayPageBlocks`, so the run prints what the desk shows.
- **`LienDeskModal`**: `payLineEdits` over the draft's lines, an in-place input on `[data-lien-desk-pay]` (Enter keeps, Esc puts back, blur keeps), the same lock as the notice's wording (`wordingLocked`), the page label counts the changes, and a change stamps the draft's `wording` (who and when) for the leader. Before the bills load, a re-save keeps the stored lines.

## Not changed

The bill itself, its Stripe page and the enclosed copy keep their own lines. The Lien window's notice tab and Put a GC on notice draw the pay page from the bills, without the desk's typed lines.
