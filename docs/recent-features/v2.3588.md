# v2.3588 — Takeoffs PR 9: Old retires; One at a time and Sheet are the two views (2026-09-18)

Client + docs. No migration, no edge function. Closes [`to-dos/takeoffs-retire-old.md`](https://github.com/clickconstruction/pipetooling.github.io/commits/main/to-dos/takeoffs-retire-old.md) and the last rung of [`docs/TAKEOFFS_REFRESH_PLAN.md`](../TAKEOFFS_REFRESH_PLAN.md).

## Why

The owner, clearing the punch list on 2026-09-18: *"We can retire the old takeoffs."* The PR 8 census (2026-09-14) had said the parallel run would teach the same lesson a second week — 17 of 19 new bids with counts had no takeoff, and the two that were costed reached 90% coverage in the new views. Old had cost two views' upkeep since v2.2768.

## The change

- **The view switch is two-way** — [`takeoffView.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/takeoffView.ts): `TakeoffView` is `'new1' | 'new2'`, `DEFAULT_TAKEOFF_VIEW` is One at a time, `parseTakeoffView` lands the retired `old` (and anything unknown) there, and `hasStoredTakeoffView` still counts a stored `old` as a pick so that device is not asked again. The storage key `bids_takeoff_view_v1` and the ids stay. The chooser ([`TakeoffViewChooser.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/TakeoffViewChooser.tsx)) shows two cards, keys 1 · 2; the pills ([`TakeoffViewPills.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/TakeoffViewPills.tsx)) lose the Old pill and the `TakeoffByStageNotice`.
- **The tab** ([`BidsTakeoffTab.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/BidsTakeoffTab.tsx)): a Combined bid renders `TakeoffFocusView` or `TakeoffCostRailView` by the stored view; an `exact` bid renders the classic body — the book selector and Apply row, the By Stage mappings table, Create PO / Add to PO — with no pills. Old's Combined sub-branch (the `renderRoughLinesTable` call and its Print Breakdown button) is deleted; `renderRoughLinesTable` itself stays as the editor both views call. The row-jump and fixture-history guards that excluded `old` are gone.
- **What was kept on purpose**: the By Stage editor, `createPOFromTakeoff` and the template-picker portal. The plan's recipe allowed deleting them; 151 legacy `exact` bids still open on that editor, and the plan says By Stage's fate is a separate owner call. Nothing here decides it.
- **Docs**: the two takeoff guides drop the Old pill and renumber the chooser keys; `docs/BIDS_SYSTEM.md` → Takeoffs, `docs/BIDS_TAKEOFF_TAB_ARCHITECTURE.md` (T8 row and the bodies note), `docs/TAKEOFFS_REFRESH_PLAN.md` status line; `to-dos/takeoffs-retire-old.md` deleted; the Labor PR 6 line on `owner-decisions-pending.md` now cites this as the pattern.

## Verified

- `takeoffView.test.ts` (7) and `BidsTakeoffTab.render.test.tsx` (11, three rewritten: the Combined default, a remembered `old` landing on One at a time, an `exact` bid keeping its editor with no pills and no notice); typecheck, lint, the guide and release-note suites, `check:todo-drift`.
- Dev server against prod: a Combined bid with a stored `old` opens One at a time with two pills; the Sheet pill hops and keeps the fixture; a By Stage bid shows its editor and no pills; Print still prints.
