# v2.4631 — Lien desk: Do now shows the steps (2026-10-06)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. The owner (2026-10-05): *on this page it is unclear what the steps are.* Mock-up: artifact WWgei392LAaSzsaCc3CwZD — a first pass (a step marker on every row), its critique, and the refined shape built here, plus the hover card the owner asked for.

## What changed

- **Kernel** `src/lib/jobs/lienNextUpSteps.ts`: `LIEN_STEP_LADDERS` (notice · affidavit · retainage, four rungs each), `lienStepOfRow` (an act → its rung; a row in the mail, letter two, held, missed or to serve stands beside the ladder), `countLienSteps` (rows per rung; the notice and retainage fourth rung is the count the desk hands over, since a GC with several ready is one row), `lienLaddersShown` (notices always, the others when something stands on them), and `lienStepCard` (one row's ladder written out from `LienStepFacts`: owner, months, draft and approval days, cover note, missing affidavit gates, the run count, whether the viewer is the leader; a row beside the ladder gets its state as two rungs).
- **Component** `LienDeskSteps.tsx`: `LienStepRail` (a rail per ladder, `aria-pressed` rungs, counts, *you* on the leader's rung, the fourth rung of notices opens the run), `LienStepMark` (four dots + fraction, a button; hover, focus or tap), `LienStepCardView` (beside the dots on a computer, `role="tooltip"`; a sheet from the bottom on a phone, `role="dialog"` with Close), `useLienStepCard` (placement inside the scrolling host, a 120 ms grace so the pointer can cross onto the card, Esc closes the card alone).
- **`LienDeskNextUp`** takes `ready`, `onOpenRun`, `factsFor`, `viewerIsLeader`; draws the rail above the groups and the mark between the title and the state words (a new 86 px column); a pressed rung dims the rows not on it (`data-dim="yes"`, opacity 0.3). The card's foot carries the row's own button, which closes the card and acts.
- **The desk** builds `stepFactsFor(row)` from what it holds (`jobsById` → `addressesById` + `ownerByJob` through `resolveLienProperty`, the queue / affidavit / retainage entry's `item` and gates, `counts.ready`), passes `ready={{ notice: counts.ready, retainage }}` and `onOpenRun` for the office.
