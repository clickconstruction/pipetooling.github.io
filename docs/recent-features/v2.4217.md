# v2.4217 — The Bridge: Vectors by the day — was each person's day worth it (2026-09-30)

Punch list #69, PR 1 of 4. The owner's ask (2026-09-30): "a view that shows people every day and if their contributions to jobs were profitable or unprofitable every day and every week and every month." Nothing drew a day: Vectors reads one pay week, People → Review one picked period, and no screen adds up a month. The mock-up (`to-dos/vectors-by-the-day-before-after.html`) was approved as drawn; this PR is the kernel and the Days zoom.

## What shipped

- **Kernel [`src/lib/bridge/vectorDays.ts`](../../src/lib/bridge/vectorDays.ts)** (+27 tests). `buildVectorDayCells` reads the Vectors session rows one person × day at a time with the same earned rate per job (contract ÷ expected hours) and the same wage; `foldBuckets` sums days into any period, merging jobs by id and adding red days up; `vectorColumnsFor` draws a month as its days with a week sum after every Saturday (and the month's last day), or thirteen pay weeks, or twelve months; `buildVectorGrid` puts it together — one row per person with field hours, best contribution first, a company row underneath. A bucket keeps its jobs (hours, rate, guessed, red days) so the next PR's popover and Why line need no second read. Two time modes are in the kernel from the start: `approved` (Vectors' rule; the only one wired this PR) and `recorded` (every closed session not rejected or revoked, pending hours reported on the bucket). A salaried person's day costs `salariedFlatDayHours` × wage whatever the clock says — the reading payroll and People → Review price it by — on the field side when the day had field hours, else the office side. An office / bid day has no contribution and no verdict.
- **Loader [`src/lib/bridge/loadVectorDays.ts`](../../src/lib/bridge/loadVectorDays.ts)**: the closed sessions of one period (`loadVectorSessions`, lifted out of `loadBridgeVectors.ts` with the range as its arguments) and the jobs they touched priced by `loadEarnedJobs` (lifted out of `loadBridgeData.ts`, behaviour unchanged — the Bridge calls the same function), so a month cell here and a pay-week row on Vectors read one rate for one job. People and wages come from the Vectors inputs already on the page.
- **Panel [`src/components/bridge/BridgeVectorDaysPanel.tsx`](../../src/components/bridge/BridgeVectorDaysPanel.tsx)** (+render test) under Vectors on `/bridge`: the month grid, six shades by dollars per field hour (`--bg-green-200` … `--bg-red-200`), the amber hatch on a cell that rests on an assumed-half job, grey cells with hours for office / bid days, a dashed border for hours not yet approved (unused until the recorded-time PR), the week-sum and month-total columns with hours and $/h under them, the Field crew row, a legend and the rule in words. Hover a cell for the split by job. ‹ › steps a month (two years back at most); the current month reads *so far*. A name opens the person's period on People → Review through the existing door.
- **Page**: `Bridge.tsx` keeps a month anchor, loads the period's inputs once and caches them per period, and builds the grid from the Vectors people and wages.
- Guide *read the Bridge* gains **Vectors by the day**; `docs/GLOSSARY.md` gains the entry. The to-do card (#69, PR #4143) was still in the queue when this branch was cut; PR 2 stamps its status.

## Why the rules are what they are

The page's *Is this the best we can do?* section, kept: it lives under Vectors, not on People → Review (#15 is retiring that tab into the Bridge); a red day is a job's verdict, so the cell keeps its jobs with rate beside wage; office days stay on the grid, grey; no daily score or rank (days are noisy, and no financial number reaches a teammate — the Bridge is dev-only). The past moves: the rate is contract ÷ (hours to date ÷ % complete), so every new hour and % update re-prices every day on that job; the grid is always as of today and the footnote says so.
