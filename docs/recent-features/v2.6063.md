## Decisions

- **Keep the fill, change the words (A), on all three calls.** The tabs are OSHA safety orange by name; darkening the fill to the lightest same-hue orange that reads with white (`#bb4b00`, option B) turns it brown and parts the buttons from Clock In. The hints took the orange word token (A) over the warning amber (C): the owner's call, one orange family for every orange word.
- **Two constants, not one**: words on the fill and orange words on a light ground are different pairs with different tokens, and a hint site importing a bids constant would read oddly, so the hint sites take the token itself and the guard test, not an import, keeps them honest.
