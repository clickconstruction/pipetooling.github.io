# v2.2729 — The bid-room email is a letter now (2026-09-03)

**Why.** The email a GC received with their bid-room link was a bare paragraph: subject "Proposal — <project>", from "ClickTooling" with no person behind it, option lines built with `display:flex` (mail clients strip it, so the name ran into the price), an `<a>` styled as a button that dark-mode clients rendered as a highlighted link, and no address, validity, footer or plain-URL fallback. Owner picked the "Letterhead" direction from the three mockups.

**What.** `send-bid-room-link` now sends:
- **Subject** `Plumbing proposal — <project> — $56,343 · Click Plumbing` (trade from the service type, amount = the proposed option, company from the header brand). Revised sends: `Revised plumbing proposal — … (rev N)`.
- **Brand banner** (`/brand/click-plum.png` or `click-elec.png` on the public origin — the estimate email's asset), heading, and a meta line: project address · prepared for <GC> · date (company calendar day).
- **Options as a real table**, the proposed row tinted and labelled *Our recommendation*, alternates labelled *Alternate*; a **bulletproof button** (table cell with `bgcolor`) and a plain-URL fallback line; *Pricing is good for N days* when the terms say so (`validityDaysFromTerms`).
- **Signature block** from the sending user (name · Estimator, company · phone · email) and **Reply-To** set to them; footer names the company. Revised sends show the revision note as *What changed in revision N*.
- A `color-scheme: light only` meta so dark-mode clients keep the white paper.

**How.** Pure builder `supabase/functions/_shared/bidRoomLinkEmail.ts` (dependency-free), unit-tested from `src/lib/bidRoomLinkEmail.test.ts` (subject variants, table not flex, bgcolor button, escaping, sender/no-sender, validity, revision note). `index.ts` fetches the sender's `users` row and the revision `note`, and passes `replyTo` to `sendEmailViaResend`. Deployed after merge.

**Not in this cut.** The personal-note textarea (direction B) and the scope-summary lines (direction C); a company address/licence line in the footer (no source for it in the app yet).
