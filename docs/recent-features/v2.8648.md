# v2.8648 — Lien desk: the releases ride the run (2026-11-26)

Piece 5 of punch list #435 (`to-dos/lien-payoff/README.md`), on top of punch list #362's *Out the door* (v2.8634). The office's call of 2026-11-26: *releases print in the day's run*. A release of a filed lien was made on its tab and then left the app: nothing printed it with the day's other papers, and nothing recorded the notary or the copies. Client only. No migration and no edge function.

## What it does

**A card, *Releases*, in the run**, under *Out the door* (`LienRunReleases`, slotted into `LienGetItRight`). It lists every saved release of a filed lien that has an act left, under the act it is on:

| Act | A release is here when | What the office does |
|---|---|---|
| Print for the notary | it is saved and the run has not printed it | one press prints every ready one, a release a sheet |
| At the notary | it is printed and has no notary's day | types the day it was notarized, where it sits |
| Record at the county | it is notarized and the county has not recorded it | **Record the county’s stamp ›** opens the release's own tab |
| Send the copies | the county recorded it in the last thirty days and no copies are on record | types the day the stamped copies went, where it sits |

A line at the top counts them: *3 to print · 1 at the notary · 2 to record · 1 to send*. The card draws nothing while no release has an act left. A viewer outside the lien office sees the list with no press.

**The print.** The press takes its words from `batchWords`: *Print 3 releases for the notary*, or *Print 2 releases for the notary · 1 is short ▸*. It is dead only when nothing is ready. A saved release prints as it was saved, since its `fields` are the paper, as on the Release of record tab. Each printed release files its copy on its job (`sent_documents`, kind `lien_release_of_record`, the kind the tab's own print files) and keeps who printed it and when.

**Held.** A release never goes out on money that is not there:

- *short*: the job's bills owe again and the release did not go out on a typed reason (`releasedUnpaidReason`). *Held. $5,160.50 is open on this job’s bills again.*
- *the check came back*: the bank sent back the job's last payment (piece 6's `moneyBack`). *Held. The check behind it came back.*

A held release does not print, and it is held on every act before the county's. The county's record stands whatever the money does, so the copies are not held. A held line offers **Open the release ›**.

**In Payoffs.** The place's card reads the same facts. Its third stop says how many releases are notarized (*All 2 made, 1 notarized.*). Its fifth stop, *Copies sent*, is lit while a recorded release owes its copies (*1 sent, 1 to send.*) and ticks once they are all out (*Sent Dec 6.*). The card gains a value, **Copies sent**, typed where it sits and kept on every recorded release at the place that still owes them. A place now stays in the open list until its copies are out, where it left for the *Released* fold on the county's day.

## The three facts, and where they live

On the release row's own `fields` (`job_lien_filings`, kind `release_of_record`), beside its link to its affidavit and the county's instrument number (`lienReleaseFacts.ts`):

- `fields.printedForNotary`: who printed it (`by`, `name`) and when (`at`).
- `fields.notarizedYmd`: the day it was signed before a notary.
- `fields.copiesSent`: the day the copies went (`ymd`) and who recorded it.

The county's day stays the row's `filed_at`. The paper's own fields are never touched by a write here. No migration: pieces 2 and 4 kept their facts the same way.

## Where it stops short of the card

1. **Its own print, not the run's one stack.** The run's print merges two documents into one job on measured pages (`lienRunStack.ts`), and a legal paper may not move. The releases print as their own job from the same screen. Joining the stack is a third paper in that merge, with its own measuring.
2. **A release is still made on its tab**, one lien at a time, where the money gate is. The run prints what is saved: it does not draw releases.
3. **Printed is the run's fact.** A release printed from its own tab before this version has no `printedForNotary`, so it stands under *Print for the notary* until it is printed here or its notary's day is typed, which moves it on.
4. **The copies are a day and who typed it.** To whom, how and the tracking number are not kept. The payer's cover letter is a paper, and counsel's answers gate the papers.
5. **Thirty days.** A release the county recorded more than thirty days ago with no copies on record is left alone, so the releases from before this version do not all stand open.
6. **A row with no paper** (a release saved by an older build with only its link) is left out of the print and says *Print it from its own tab.*
7. **The run's step strip does not count releases.** The strip counts the Board's papers.
8. **The reference line on a batch-printed release** is the job and the day. The tab's own print adds the work month when the job has one.
