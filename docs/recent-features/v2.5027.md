# v2.5027 — Submittals: a submittal leaves only through Share (2026-10-09)

Client only. No migration, no edge function. This builds the owner's call of 2026-10-09 on the Submittals residual *retire the Mail Drop habit*: a standing rule that submittals leave only from the tab. "Mail Drop" was the owner's name for the habit of saving the package and emailing it from outside the app. On SpaceX BA-02N, Rev 2 went out on Sep 10 by iCloud Mail Drop, and no copy reached the office. With v2.5026 the app can send the link itself, so the door that recorded an outside send goes. PUNCHLIST chose the door (reading a), and kept the save-to-device doors.

## The change

- **Step 5's "Sent by email on…" door is gone** (`SubmittalRoomPanel.tsx`). It set `bid_submittals.sent_outside_at` on a draft that went out by email or on paper (v2.4705). Its props, its day box and `markSentOutside` in `BidsSubmittalsTab.tsx` are removed.
- **Typing in the GC's first answer no longer sets it either.** `saveAnswer` and `approveAll` called `markSentOutside` once on a draft. Answers typed on a draft now leave it a draft. The room line already says *Not shared from the app · answers typed in* for a room that was never shared.
- **A revision that already carries the day keeps its record.** `describeRevisionChip` still reads *Rev 1 · sent by email · Sep 29*, and step 5's line still reads `sentByEmailLine`. The journey's Share stage still counts it as sent. Nothing reads the column differently, and nothing deletes it.
- **The save-to-device doors stay.** A PDF to read, or to file in a GC's own system, is legitimate. The cut sheet's line changed from *Attach it to your email or text.* to *…, to read or to file in the GC's own system.* (`cutSheetSavedLine` in `rowCutSheet.ts`).

  - The cut sheet paragraph says to read it or file it in the GC's own system.
