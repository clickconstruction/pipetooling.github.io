# v2.7305 — Move everything on site to another job: the office's press (2026-11-15)

Punch list #209, the gap the map held open on purpose ([`docs/MATERIALS_ON_SITE_ARCHITECTURE.md`](../MATERIALS_ON_SITE_ARCHITECTURE.md) → The gaps). One migration, [`20261115040000_on_site_move_job_office.sql`](../migrations/20261115040000_on_site_move_job_office.md); no edge function. **`supabase db push` right after the merge**: the client calls `on_site_move_job`, which only this migration creates.

## Before

A count started on the phone under the wrong job could be moved whole since v2.6808, but only through the dev seat's `plan_materials_move` → `apply_materials_move`, with a dev's plan-then-apply on the owner's word. The map said: build the office's press when the office asks a second time. On 2026-11-14 the owner asked, looking at the Materials desk's site row for Gun Dog, where JP224 (a paid job from July 2025, 91 boxes) and JP1091 (the working job, 2 boxes) hold one site's material.

## What changed

- **The database**: `dev_on_site_move_job`'s body is now `on_site_move_job_run(p, p_dry_run, p_door)`, callable by nobody but its two doors; the seat's door keeps its gate (a dev, no training account, no twin) and calls it; a new door, **`on_site_move_job(p, p_dry_run)`**, admits a dev, a master technician, an assistant or a controller — the roles that may combine two jobs — who can open both jobs, and refuses everyone else, a training account, a twin and an archived caller in words. The plan is the seat's: counts moved per table, places that became one, labels renumbered, where the photos' files are, each job's rows and parts on hand before and after; the body refuses when the rows after do not add up, and a note lands on both jobs in the caller's name.
- **The press**: on a job's Materials page (Jobs → Materials and Materials → On site, the same component) a quiet line under the boxes, **Move everything on site to another job…**, live for the four roles and dead with its reason for the rest (`canMoveOnSite`, `MOVE_JOB_WHO` in `lib/materials/onSiteMoveJob.ts`). The desk's site row carries the same press when two of the site's jobs hold boxes, choosing the source from that site's jobs.
- **The window** (`components/materials/OnSiteMoveJobModal.tsx`): pick the receiving job — the other jobs on the same site first, then **Find another job** — then **Read the plan** runs the dry run and draws it as the confirm window: what moves table by table (`movedRows`), the places that become one and the labels that change, each job's boxes and parts before and after, the photos' files following to the target's Drive folder by the sweep (`photosFollowWords`, which says what is true since v2.6927 where the database's own sentence still says the files stay), and the parts-on-hand warning when a fold changes a line. The button carries the verb and the counts, *Move 92 boxes and 284 presses to JP1091* (`movePressLabel`). The apply runs the same RPC, both jobs are read again, the target's photos are asked to move as a combine asks (`afterCombineBoxPhotos`), and the page says what is now true (`moveDoneWords`). A refusal is said with its reason and nothing moves.
- **The kernel** reads the plan's JSON into the window's rows (`readMovePlan`), held against the seat's own plan fixture in `devMcpWrites.test.ts`, whose text assertions now read the shared body and the office door's gate. `onSiteMoveJob.fixture.ts` carries Gun Dog's plan for the kernel's and the window's tests.
- **The map, the glossary, `docs/ACCESS_CONTROL.md`, `docs/twins/APP_DIRECTORY.md`** say the move has two doors now; the guide *see what is on every job site and what walked away* gains **Move everything on site to another job**.

## Is this the best we can do?

The weight of the act is the reason the press waited: it re-homes every printed box of a job at once. The window keeps that weight — the plan is the apply's own dry run, drawn before anything is written, and the button says the counts — while taking the dev out of the loop for a correction the office can read for itself. Nothing yet stops the mistake at its start: the phone's job picker takes any job the person can reach (the map's gap list).
