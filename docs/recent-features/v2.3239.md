# v2.3239 — Pricing: what the bid is made of (fixtures · pipe · fittings · other), and the typed-price fix under the envelope (2026-09-10)

Client only — no migration, no edge function. Grace: "show how much of the project cost goes into counts vs line feet … expose this tastefully on the page." Mockup, built on b385 Galloway Park's real rows: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/c4b2ce68-8e00-4251-9908-135559f11f4d.

## What shipped

- **Kernel** [`src/lib/bids/pricingComposition.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/pricingComposition.ts) (+7 tests on Galloway's row names): `compositionKind(name)` starts from the audit diff's `parseRowSignature` and adds the rules a real priced bid needed — valves, wyes, tees and cleanouts are **fittings** even when the signature reads them as fixtures ("Ball Valve") or footage on size alone ("4\" Y (Waste)"); a footage row needs a length unit; allowances (`Material/Labor/Travel`, permits, rentals, lump sums) land in **other**, as do names the classifier can't place — never dropped. `buildPricingComposition(rows)` sums revenue, cost, profit, margin, count and rows per bucket with revenue and cost shares and the three biggest rows; a bucket with revenue but no cost has **no margin** (the Workbench's no-cost rows would otherwise read as 100%). `compositionHeadline` → *59 fixtures · 2,448 ft of pipe · 357 fittings*.
- **Bar** [`PricingCompositionBar.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/PricingCompositionBar.tsx), mounted in [`BidsPricingTab`](../../src/components/bids/BidsPricingTab.tsx) directly under the sticky summary strip and above the margin history — the same card grammar as *Where the profit lives*, one level up. Header line in the estimator's units; a **Revenue / Cost** toggle (per device, `pt.pricing.composition.lens`); the stacked bar (labels inside segments ≥ 12%, percent only ≥ 5%); a legend per bucket with rows, the lens's dollars and the margin in the strip's own `mColor` rule; hover a segment or a legend entry for revenue · cost · profit · margin and the three biggest rows, each a jump to its Workbench row (the profit bar's own flash-and-scroll). Feeds from the Workbench's `eff` rows — `countRow.fixture`, `count`, `cost`, `effRevenue` — so solver previews and mid-typing prices move the bar exactly as they move the strip.
- **The typed-price fix** ([`loadPricedTakeoffRows.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/loadPricedTakeoffRows.ts)): the shared priced-rows loader used by the envelope now reads `bid_count_row_custom_prices` (scoped to the bid's `selected_price_book_version_id`; any typed price when none is selected) with the Workbench's own precedence — assignment override → typed price → book entry (`resolveTakeoffUnitPrice`). The Workbench saves most human prices as typed prices (71 of Galloway's 84 rows), so a human reference bid used to read as $0 rows and the diff filed them under *robot added* / *everything else* (b397's envelope: *everything else −$49,051*). [`BidsAuditsTab`](../../src/components/bids/BidsAuditsTab.tsx) drops its inline copy of the loader for the shared one, so the audit card and the envelope price a takeoff identically.
