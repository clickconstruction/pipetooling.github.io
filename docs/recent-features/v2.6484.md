# v2.6484 — The charges screens count only the days a standing would fix, and a failed read leaves the column unpriced (2026-11-04)

Three bugs read off main after the live pass of charges by crew (punch list #254, v2.6441–v2.6461). The crew-day totals' `unpriced` counted every day with no charge — a rate-card row missing, a flat rate not set, a day priced at nothing — and the Charges and Statement of charges windows handed that count to the callout, which blamed the standings for all of them (*N days price at nothing until someone out counts as a master, a tech or a helper*); the hours statement window alone counted the days with no shape. The Job Summary's users and People reads ignored their errors, so a failed read left the standings list empty, priced every crew-day at nothing and named everyone as nobody, though v2.6445 promised a read that fails leaves the column unpriced. And the callout's links sent a controller or a pay-approved master, who open the Charges window through `has_payroll_access()`, to the Settings tab that renders for a dev alone and to a desk row only a dev edits.

## What changed

- **`unchargedDays`** (`src/lib/charges/crewDays.ts` `CrewDaysTotals`, `jobCharges.ts` `JobChargesTotals`, `ledgerCharges.ts` `LedgerCharges`): the days with `shape == null`, beside `unpriced`, which keeps every unpriced day for the premium (`premiumWaitsWords` was right: the premium waits on any of them). `UnchargedCallout` takes `unchargedDays` and the viewer's `role`; the Charges window, the Statement of charges window (a new `viewerRole` prop from `StatementsOfChargesSection`) and the hours statement window hand it the count and the role.
- **A failed standings read is unknown, not nobody** (`src/pages/Jobs.tsx`): the users read selects `id, name, charge_standing, archived_at` (never `*` for a lookup) and, with the People-links read, keeps `chargeStandingsError` through `formatErrorMessage` when it fails; `ledgerChargesFor` takes it as `standingsUnread` and answers `priced: false` with `standingsUnreadWords(reason)` as the note (*Who counts as what could not be read, so nothing is priced yet: …*), so both cells read a dash with the reason in their title and the nobody note names no one. `jobChargesRead.ts`'s lookup selects `id, charge_standing`; it already threw on an error and the window names it.
- **The callout by role** (`src/components/charges/UnchargedCallout.tsx`): a dev keeps the links to Who counts as what and to each desk; anyone else reads the names with their day counts and *Ask a dev to set who counts as what in Settings, under Rates charged by crew.*, with no dead link.
- **The guide** (`src/content/help/charge-by-crew.md`): a dev opens the desk's Charged as row and Who counts as what, the dev's pick saves at once, the callout counts the days a standing would fix, and anyone but a dev asks one. `docs/GLOSSARY.md`'s crew-day entry says the same.
- **Tests**: `crewDays.test.ts` and `jobCharges.test.ts` (one day everyone Not charged plus three days a master alone on a card with no `master_alone` rate: `unchargedDays` 1, `unpricedDays` 4, and a flat rate not set counts as unpriced alone), `ledgerCharges.test.ts` (a failed standings read: `priced` false, no one named, the reason as the note, both cells a dash; the same row with the read answered still names who counts as nobody), `UnchargedCallout.render.test.tsx` (a controller and a pay-approved master get the words and no link; a dev gets the links; nothing draws when everyone counts).

## Out of scope

A master or a controller cannot read archived logins at all (`users`' select policy is `archived_at IS NULL OR is_dev()`), so a departed person's standing differs by viewer: a dev sees their days priced, a controller sees them count as nobody. That takes a definer read of `id, name, charge_standing` for the payroll roles.
