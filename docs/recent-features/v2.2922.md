## "Show me on their portal" (sheet story → the sub's page, scrolled and haloed)

Owner's ask: next to *The sub sees: "Finish up, then tell us below…" with ✓ My work here is done*, a way to jump to the sub's portal and have it scroll to and highlight that very part.

**Story side** ([`SheetStoryModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/SheetStoryModal.tsx)): every row with a `sees` sentence carries a **Show me on their portal ›** link. The kernel ([`sheetStory.ts`](../../src/lib/subWorkOrders/sheetStory.ts)) gained `seesOn: 'sheet' | 'offer' | null` on each row — the Sent row's offer sentence lives on the offer card, the Work / Walk-through / Customer pays / queued sentences on the sheet card. The link opens a tab synchronously (popup blockers), resolves the sub's **token** link via `resolveSubPortalUrl(personId, { tokenOnly: true })` (new option — the custom short address bounces through the Cloudflare worker, which is not trusted to keep query flags), adds `?preview=1` (`withPreviewFlag`) and `?focus=sheet:<laborJobId>` / `?focus=offer:<orderId>`, then lands the tab there. Only offered when the sheet has a single assignee.

**Portal side** ([`SubPortal.tsx`](../../src/pages/SubPortal.tsx)): the page parses `?focus=` with [`subPortalFocus.ts`](../../src/lib/subPortal/subPortalFocus.ts) (`parseSubPortalFocus`, `withSubPortalFocus`, `subPortalFocusDomId`, `isSubPortalFocused`; ids validated, stale params replaced). `SheetCard` wraps the rail → sentence → pay-when → agreement → "My work here is done" block in a div `sp-focus-sheet-<id>`; `OfferCard`'s root gets `sp-focus-offer-<id>`. `useSubPortalFocusPulse` scrolls the focused block to the middle (smooth, or a plain jump under reduced motion) and wears `.sp-focus` for ~5 s — a card-colored gap plus a copper ring (`box-shadow`, no layout shift) and a faint copper wash that fades; reduced-motion viewers get a steady ring; print drops it.

## Binds under → Person Desk Paperwork

"no MSA on file · no COI on file" is a door to the sub's contracts. `SheetStoryFact` gained `link?: 'paperwork'`, set on the Signed row's *Binds under* fact. The modal renders that fact as a dotted-underline button when the viewer may open the Desk (`useOptionalPersonDesk().canOpen`) and the sheet has a single assignee; it calls `desk.open({ personId, displayName, section: 'paperwork' })` (the v2.2810 section landing), so the Desk drawer opens over the story on Paperwork. ## Sub Labor names the subcontractor

Sheets shared between a sub and teammates ("Malachi | Abraham | Michael A | Behar Kraja") mixed the sub's money with the crew's names. New kernel [`sheetParties.ts`](../../src/lib/subWorkOrders/sheetParties.ts) sorts a sheet's people: junction assignees first (then the delimited name column), each resolved against the roster and split by the crew-vs-sub rule (`isRosterSub`, now in [`rosterSub.ts`](../../src/lib/subWorkOrders/rosterSub.ts) to avoid an import cycle; `sheetsNeedingWorkOrder.ts` re-exports it). Roster subs are the **contractors** (names the roster does not know stay contractors, as the ledger always treated them); the rest are **teammates**; a sheet with no sub at all is **crew** and keeps every name. `label` joins the contractors the way the sheet column does, `withLabel` reads "with Malachi, Abraham", `key` is `id:<people.id>` for a lone contractor else the label's name key. The Crew pay tag now means *no roster sub on the sheet* (payroll); the rail's four-dot / no-work-order rule still follows `isRosterSubSheet` (any teammate on the sheet).

## Sub Labor as a pay run

The page's two questions — who is owed what, and what happened on those sheets — now read as a pay run. Kernel [`subLaborPayRun.ts`](../../src/lib/subWorkOrders/subLaborPayRun.ts):

- `sheetPayWhen({ stage, payableAfter, payHoldReason, bills, gap, crew, balance, unpriced, todayYmd })` → the rule one sheet's money is under, in precedence: `unpriced` · `paid` (balance ≤ 0) · `crew` (Payroll) · `hold` (pay_hold_reason) · `gap` (Not payable — nothing signed) · `work` / `walk` (After the walk-through) · at Customer pays: `ready` when the job's bills are all paid or the payable-after date has arrived, `queued` when that date is ahead, else `wait` (Waiting on customer, with the bill fact). Each carries a chip label, a detail, a tone and a rank (payable first).
- `buildSubLaborPayRun(sheets, { payRunDay, todayYmd })` → `tiles` (owed / owedSubs / owedSheets, ready, queued + `queuedDayLabel` "Fri Sep 11" from `app_settings.sub_pay_run_day` via `nextPayRunYmd`, blocked + `blockedReasons`), `subs` (one per sub key: name, `personId` when every sheet names the same lone roster person, teammates, open `sheetCount`, `owed`, `segments` ready/queued/wait/work/hold/gap, `readySheetIds` biggest first + `readyAmount`, the `whyNot` sentence, an `action` — `pay` / `draft` / `note`), and `counts` per filter chip. Crew sheets are counted but never owed (payroll). `PAY_RUN_FILTERS` = due · ready · queued · wait · gap · crew · paid; `payRunFilterMatches`.

[`JobsSubLaborTab.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsSubLaborTab.tsx) rewritten on it: the search beside New Sub Labor; four tiles; **Who's owed** (name first with "with …", the sub-portal **globe** beside the name when `personId` is set, owed, the segmented bar with a legend, the sentence, and Pay / Draft a work order… / a note); filter chips with counts replace *Only show due · No agreement · Subs only* and the Due/Date/Contractor sort; the ledger is grouped under each sub in the pay run's order (group header: name, globe, Crew pay tag, open sheets, teammates, owed, the same Pay button), rows sorted payable-first inside a group. Columns: Job (number · name · address · *with …* · ✍ record id) · Agreed · Paid · Due · Where it stands (rail, unchanged) · **Pay when** (chip + detail; a gap row carries the office's move; waiting, queued and ready rows offer an inline **set / change payable after…** editor that writes `people_labor_jobs.payable_after` and reloads) · ⋯ (Payment… · Back-charge… · Edit sheet · Print · Story…). The expanded row keeps the totals, invoice link, line items and payments and gains the sheet-date input and the four buttons. Clicking a name in Who's owed jumps to the group with a copper edge for a moment. **Pay** opens Make Payment on the sub's biggest ready sheet (per sheet; with several ready the label says "· 2 sheets" and the button moves to the next after saving). Bills come from the Pipeline job's embedded `invoices` (non-draft = out, `paid` = paid). The tab's `subLaborOutstandingByPerson` prop is optional and unused — Jobs.tsx still builds it for its header total.

Decisions taken as the mock stated them: ready = at Customer pays with the bill paid or the date reached and no hold (⋯ → Payment… is the "pay anyway" path); crew sheets stay, tagged, never owed; one payment per sheet; mixed sheets keep the crew-pay rail rule.

## "How do I get paid?" — the sub portal's guide (owner-approved mock-up, artifact db9d90d9)

A copper-edged card at the very bottom of the sub's statement (after the short-address card, before the footer; `data-screen-only`) opens [`SubPortalGuideSheet`](../../src/components/subPortal/SubPortalGuide.tsx): a bottom sheet on phones, a centered dialog ≥760px. Bottom line first — the owner's sentence verbatim: *Do the work and tap ✓ My work here is done. We call it in for inspection. Once it passes, we can trigger a draw from the customer. When their money hits us, if your documents are current with us, it hits you the same day.* — then the four steps as cards with the card's own quoted sentence and You / Us / Sometimes facts (punch lists on Pre-inspection, with "we'll pay someone else and take it off this job"; the occasional advance on Post-inspection; two cases on You're paid), a walking dot that cycles the four rail words every 2.4 s with a caption (`aria-live`, stopped under reduced motion), then chapters: the one button (with the pulsing ✓ button and the confirm line), new work offers, **your documents with us** (COI · W-9 · agreements · where to send your money — two example states), payments & deductions, and two **Coming soon** cards (Pick your days · Ask us from here). **Viewer grants — plans open without CountTooling's email gate** (design: artifact 8a1103f5; CountTooling side on its `feat/view-grant` branch): CountTooling view links need no account but refuse emails outside the company domain, which is the wall a sub's Gmail hits. The sub-portal function now mints a **grant** for every CountTooling plans link it hands out — [`_shared/viewGrant.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/viewGrant.ts): `grantPlansLink(url, { name, email, person }, secret)` → `…&g=base64url(claims).base64url(HMAC-SHA256)`, claims `{ t: <view token>, name, email?, person, via: 'pipetooling-sub-portal', iat, exp: +24h }`, bound to that token; Drive/PDF links and a missing `COUNTTOOLING_VIEW_GRANT_SECRET` pass the bare link through.
