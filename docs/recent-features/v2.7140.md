# v2.7140 — Pay calendar: the last screens read the due day (2026-11-13)

Punch list #323. The docs catch-up pass of 2026-11-13 found four readers that still judged a payer on a Pay calendar by the pace guess, or by no payer's rule at all. Each was confirmed in the code first (`git grep "billedExpectedPayModel("`). None was a choice: neither the card nor the fragments of v2.7028, v2.7040 and v2.7127 says to leave one.

## The rule

`billHeldDay(model)` in `src/lib/jobs/billedDuePay.ts` is the day a screen holds a bill to when it asks *is this past due?* On a Pay calendar it is the due day, the day the contract names: a bill is never past due before it and is the day after. With no calendar it is the expected day, as before. A day the customer named wins either way. A screen that counts money still reads `expectedYmd`, the due day plus the payer's usual lateness.

## The four readers

- **The Development page's Money look** (`developments/siteMoney.ts`). `fetchDevelopmentPage` reads the calendars and the accepted days beside the pace, office roles only, each soft. `datesFor` calls `billedPayModel`. A calendar payer's billed job carries `dates.due` and the cell draws *Due Nov 10 · in 12d* or *2d past* over *pay calendar · lands ~6d after the pay day* (`DevelopmentMoney.tsx`). *Ask for a date*, the *past due* counts on the band, the section and the strip, and the tightest order all read `moneyPastDays`.
- **Quickfill's Billed Awaiting Payment** (`quickfill/leanRead.ts`). It called every bill past due at a flat 30 days from its bill date. `leanDueByBill` gives each bill of a calendar payer its due day, read as Money waiting reads it (`listPayer`, `calendarFor`, `payClockFor`). `leanRows` then takes a customer's worst bill: a calendar bill by its due day, any other by its 30 days. A customer not yet due reads *due Nov 10 · pay calendar* in Not yet past due, and `leanSince` counts one whose due day passed since the last look. The age chip's amber follows the same read.
- **The residential flow's Paid round** (`flows/residentialLoad.ts`). The round's `due` is the held day. The step card and Follow up's nudges read the calendars through `payCalendarIo.ts` in `readPipelineRows`; `residentialRowsOf` indexes them for the loader.
- **They owe us** (`followUp/theyOweUs.ts`). `billDueDay` returns `billedPayModel`'s model and takes the calendar and the accepted day. A row's `dueYmd`, `late` and `daysLate` are the held day's.

The Pipeline's money chip (`usePipelineMoneyOpportunities`) now reads the accepted days beside the calendars it already read, as Call mode does.

## What the build found

- **They owe us has no screen on main.** v2.5398 shipped its rules alone and nothing draws them. Only the kernel moved. The screen that lands later hands in `payCalendars` and `acceptedByInvoice`.
- **The residential flow's kernel runs on the edge too.** `residentialLoad.ts` and `theyOweUs.ts` are in the generated copy (`_shared/gcKernels/`), and the morning run (`messages-nudge-dispatch`) reads the same Paid round to decide a reminder's day. Moving the screen alone would have split the two. So the run reads `payer_pay_calendars` and the accepted days of the bills it read, each soft, and hands them to the same kernel. The copy gained `lib/jobs/billedDuePay.ts` and `lib/jobs/payCalendar.ts`: 318 files and its index, 62,251 lines.
- **`messages-nudge-dispatch` needs a redeploy.** Until then the run holds the pace's day while the step card shows the calendar's. `npm run check:edge-drift` names it, and the other importers whose bundles reach the two new files. No migration.

## The judgment call

Quickfill's 30 days was a different rule from the other three: it was never the pace guess, and nothing on the card records a decision to keep or drop it for a calendar payer. It is dropped for them. The reason is the card's own: a bill in on the 1st and one in on the 30th are due the same day, so a count of days from the bill cannot say which is past due. A bill 16 days old can now read past due, and one 45 days old can read not yet. If the owner wants the flat 30 days back for everyone, it is `billPastDueDays` in `leanRead.ts`.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **Past due is the due day here, the expected day on the Pipeline.** The Pipeline's *Ask for a date* waits for the day their money usually lands. The Development page's count says *past due*, so it goes by the due day, and so does a reminder's day. A payer who always lands 6 days after reads past due for those 6 days on these screens.
- **Quickfill reads no lateness.** It asks one thing, past due or not, so it reads the due day alone and no pay speeds.
- **Dashboard Accounts Receivable still has less to go on.** Its items carry neither the bill's payer nor its id, so it reads the job's customer's calendar and no accepted day. That wants a field on `FinancialItem`. It is on the card.
