# v2.8541 — Lien desk: a job with no clock hours shows the months its affidavit claims (2026-11-24)

A finding of punch list #415, the Lien desk's leftovers from the ledger (#361). Client only: no migration, no edge function. Walk 7 of that card recorded a notice by hand on a ZZ Test job with no hours, then opened the affidavit pane and found no *Months the affidavit claims* card. The owner picked the way on 2026-11-24: draw the card from the job's stand-in month.

## What was wrong

The affidavit pane built the card from the job's clock months alone (`workMonths.months.length`). A job dated from its bill, from its creation or from a last day of work set by hand has no clock month. So it showed no card, whatever notice was on record, and nothing on the pane said which months its paper would claim. The line that rewrites a checked stand-in month's words in that card (v2.7280) could never run.

## What changed

- [`affidavitMonths.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/affidavitMonths.ts): `affidavitMonthRowsWithoutHours` builds the card's rows for a job with no approved clock month. Its months are the months of the paper's own span (`lienAffidavitWorkMonths`), the ones the paper swears from. Each row leads with what the month rests on, *dated from the bill · no clock hours*, *dated from the job's creation · no clock hours* or *from the last day of work set by hand · no clock hours*, where a clock month leads with its approved hours. The rest of the row is the clock month's own: *notice sent*, *notice still open* with its day, *window closed* with its day, or a skip with its name.
- The month's notice day comes from `noticeDeadlineForMonth`, not from the desk's month reader, which drops a month once its day is a week past. A month a live § 53.056 notice names reads *on the lien*.
- `affidavitNoHoursSource` says which of the three the job rests on, off the affidavit row's own flags. A hand day on a job the ledger shows an approved clock day for answers none: that job is dated from hours too, and its card comes from the clock months.
- [`LienDeskAffidavitPane.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskAffidavitPane.tsx): the pane takes the clock months when it has them, and these rows when the job has none. One row builder (`monthRow`) writes both, so the words cannot drift.
- The guide [send lien notices from the Lien desk](/help/send-lien-notices-from-the-lien-desk) has the line.

## Not changed

- A job with approved clock hours. Its card reads as it did, and it still draws once the clock months are read.
- A job contracted with the owner, which owes no monthly notice and has no such card.
- Any paper, any clock and any date. The card says what the paper already swears.
- The job's path. Walk 7 read *no work month yet* on its § 53.056 stop. This change does not touch the path, and that reading was not looked into.

## Verified

- `affidavitMonths.test.ts`: a month from the bill with a notice on record, a month from the job's creation with its window closed, a hand day with its window open, the notice day itself still open, a skip, and the source off the row's flags. The older cases pass unchanged through the shared row builder.
- `LienDesk.render.test.tsx`: the pane draws the card for a job dated from its bill and for one dated from its creation, with no clock months handed to it, and draws none for a job dated from hours until its clock months are read.
- `npm run typecheck`, lint and the Lien test files pass. The full suite is left to CI. Not seen live yet: walk 7 of #415 is its look, on a ZZ Test job.
