## The kernel — [`bidPriceRequests.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/bidPriceRequests.ts)

- `requestStatusFor(row, todayYmd)` → **quoted** when the row has a plugged quote or a quote link; **late N days** when its needed-by is past with nothing in; **waiting** otherwise — a row with no needed-by at all (PR 2's Ask writes none; hand-sent rows never had one) reads waiting, not blank. `requestStatusLabel` prints the chip.
- `PriceRequestSummary` gains `late`; the header line reads *3 houses · 3 requests · 1 quote in · 1 late*.
- `PriceRequestHouse.defaultRep` carries the rep's `phone` (the table reads it from `supply_house_contacts` now).

## The table — [`BidPriceRequestsTable.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/BidPriceRequestsTable.tsx)

Five columns, 24 / 22 / 10 / 22 / rest: **Supply house · Requested · Status · Quote ·** actions.

- **Requested** keeps the date and now carries the how-it-went chip beside it — *by app* or *sent outside* — with the app row's *viewed … / not viewed yet / nudged ×N* and, for a hand-sent row, its request link (*mail.google.com ↗*) under the date. The amber *needed by …* stays; its old *· late* suffix is gone because the chip says it.
- **Status**: one chip — grey *waiting*, amber *late 7d*, green *quote in*.
- **Quote** holds only the quote: *Vendor page* with *quoted Sep 14 · 12 lines* for a plugged quote, the pasted link with its host label, *nothing in yet* on an app row still waiting, and on a **waiting hand-sent row a paste box** — *Paste the quote link…*, saved on Enter or blur through `normalizePastedLink` (a non-link is refused with the edit form's message), writing `quote_url` and `status = 'quoted'`. No PDF drop, per the owner's 2026-09-16 call.
- **Call** on hand-sent rows whose house's default rep has a phone: a `tel:` link through `telHrefFor` (v2.3571) with the number in the tooltip; app rows keep *Nudge*.
- The one-line bug the mock-up exposed is gone with the restructure: the old cell called `linkHostLabel('')` on a hand-sent row with no link and printed the literal word *link*.
