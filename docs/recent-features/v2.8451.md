# v2.8451 — Bills sent: a bill's record goes back to the day it was opened from (2026-11-24)

One fault of the Bills sent page found on the live test of 2026-11-24 (punch list #422, problem 3). No migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

A bill's name on a payment in By day paid opens that bill's record. The record's only way back was **← All bills sent**, which opened By day sent. A leader reading one day's payments lost the day, the month and the chip at each bill. The browser's own Back did return.

## What changed

- `billsSentRecordPath(billId, from)` takes the day of By day paid a record is opened from, and its chip. The address then reads `/bills-sent?bill=…&view=paid&day=2026-11-23&how=stripe`, so a reload or a pasted link keeps where it came from.
- `billsSentRecordBack({ view, day, how })`, new and pure, reads that address: a record opened from a day goes back to that day under its chip, and the press names it, **← What came in Mon, Nov 23**. Any other record goes back to the list as before.
- A day that does not read as a date, or a chip that is not a plain word, is dropped, so a made-up address falls back to the list.
- A payment's bill name in By day paid passes its day and the chip. A record opened from By day sent or the yellow box is unchanged.
- The guide names the back link.

## Tests

`billsSent.test.ts`: the address with a day, with a chip, with *all*, with a bad day; the way back for each, and for a chip that is no word. `BillsPaidByDay.render.test.tsx`: the bill's link carries the day and the chip. `BillsSentLedger.render.test.tsx`: a record at such an address draws **← What came in Tue, Nov 3** to that day and no **← All bills sent**. All three failed before the change.
