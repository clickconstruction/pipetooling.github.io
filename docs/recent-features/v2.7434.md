# v2.7434 — Lien desk: To send for approval, and Approved · to mail (2026-11-16)

Taunya, on the Board: "I don't understand how to send and in the run differ here, what does to send mean?" *To send* was the office sending a due paper to the leader for approval, and *In the run* was an approved notice waiting to be mailed, so "send" was doing two jobs. Offered the two names below, she answered "Yes, rename them". Shown that the list's *Ready to send* pile then sat between them with "send" still in its name, and asked whether it should read *Approved · to print*, she answered "Yes, rename it". Client only: no migration. The edge copy of two kernels is regenerated and no edge function prints these labels.

## The names

| stage | was | is | where |
|---|---|---|---|
| the office sends the paper to the leader | To send | **To send for approval** | the Board's `to_send` bin in both columns; the `to_draft` pile on By paper for monthly notices, lien affidavits and retainage notices |
| an approved notice waits to be mailed | In the run | **Approved · to mail** | the Board's `in_mail` bin; the `printed` pile on By paper's Monthly notices |
| an approved notice waits for the run to print it | Ready to send | **Approved · to print** | the `ready` pile on By paper's Monthly notices |
| an approved retainage notice waits to go out | Ready to send | **Approved · to mail** | the `ready` pile on By paper's Retainage notices |

The first stage means the same thing on all three papers: the footer's press there is **Send for approval ▸**, and the next pile is *Awaiting approval*.

## What each one holds

- The Board's `in_mail` bin holds two of the list's piles: `ready` (approved, not yet printed) and `printed` (printed, the mailing not yet recorded). The list keeps them apart.
- The list's `ready` pile reads **Approved · to print** and its `printed` pile **Approved · to mail**, so the list runs *To send for approval*, *Awaiting approval*, *Approved · to print*, *Approved · to mail*, *Sent · 30d*.
- Retainage has a `ready` pile and no `printed` one: an approved retainage notice goes out with the run and stays in that pile until it is sent. *To print* would be wrong for one already printed, so that pile takes **Approved · to mail**. This one is the session's call, not Taunya's words.
- The Board's retainage line follows: *Retainage · 2 to send for approval · 1 approved to mail* (no dot inside a part, since dots part the line).

## The run keeps its name

- The run window, *The run · 0 of 3 mailed*, *Send the run · 3*, *it goes in the run* and *Nothing is in the run.* are as they were.
- The Board's third tile and the bar over the list still read *3 in the run*. The tile counts what is in the run and opens it. It took its words from the bin's label in lower case; it now holds them itself, so the bar's parts (*21 by tomorrow · 3 waiting on … · 7 in the run*) keep one dot between them.

## Sentences that name the pile

They follow the new name: *Showing 2 rows in To send for approval.*, *Its monthly notice is in To send for approval.*, *1 in To send for approval was never opened.*, *The affidavit goes back to To send for approval on the short form.*, the calendar's *the desk lists it under Approved · to mail*, and a To do row's small line *Approved · to mail · printed · not signed* where it read *In the run · …*. The empty piles say *Nothing is left to send for approval.* and *Nothing approved is waiting to print.* The run's take-back reads *The 19 notices go back to Approved · to print. The approvals stand.*, and the footer's press puts a printed notice *Back in Approved · to print.*
