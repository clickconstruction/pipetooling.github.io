# v2.7434 — Lien desk: To send for Master approval, and Approved · to mail (2026-11-16)

Taunya, on the Board: "I don't understand how to send and in the run differ here, what does to send mean?" *To send* was the office sending a due paper to the leader for approval, and *In the run* was an approved notice waiting to be mailed, so "send" was doing two jobs. Offered the two names below, she answered "Yes, rename them". Shown that the list's *Ready to send* pile then sat between them with "send" still in its name, and asked whether it should read *Approved · to print*, she answered "Yes, rename it". Then, on the first two: "Should we make 1 and 2? 1. To send for Master approval 2. Awaiting Master approval". Told the longer name was at its limit on the lit pile bar beside the count and the link *show every pile*, she first picked a shorter pair and then said "I changed my mind, shorten the link". So the two bins name who approves, with her capital M, and the link is two words. Client only: no migration. The edge copy of two kernels is regenerated and no edge function prints these labels.

## The names

| stage | was | is | where |
|---|---|---|---|
| the office sends the paper to the master | To send | **To send for Master approval** | the Board's `to_send` bin in both columns; the `to_draft` pile on By paper for monthly notices, lien affidavits and retainage notices |
| the paper waits on the master | Awaiting approval | **Awaiting Master approval** | the Board's `awaiting` bin in both columns; the `awaiting` pile on By paper for all three papers |
| an approved notice waits to be mailed | In the run | **Approved · to mail** | the Board's `in_mail` bin; the `printed` pile on By paper's Monthly notices |
| an approved notice waits for the run to print it | Ready to send | **Approved · to print** | the `ready` pile on By paper's Monthly notices |
| an approved retainage notice waits to go out | Ready to send | **Approved · to mail** | the `ready` pile on By paper's Retainage notices |

The first stage means the same thing on all three papers: the footer's press there is **Send for approval ▸**, and the next pile is *Awaiting Master approval*.

## What each one holds

- The Board's `in_mail` bin holds two of the list's piles: `ready` (approved, not yet printed) and `printed` (printed, the mailing not yet recorded). The list keeps them apart.
- The list's `ready` pile reads **Approved · to print** and its `printed` pile **Approved · to mail**, so the list runs *To send for Master approval*, *Awaiting approval*, *Approved · to print*, *Approved · to mail*, *Sent · 30d*.
- Retainage has a `ready` pile and no `printed` one: an approved retainage notice goes out with the run and stays in that pile until it is sent. *To print* would be wrong for one already printed, so that pile takes **Approved · to mail**. This one is the session's call, not Taunya's words.
- The Board's retainage line follows: *Retainage · 2 to send for approval · 1 approved to mail* (no dot inside a part, since dots part the line).

## The run keeps its name

- The run window, *The run · 0 of 3 mailed*, *Send the run · 3*, *it goes in the run* and *Nothing is in the run.* are as they were.
- The Board's third tile and the bar over the list still read *3 in the run*. The tile counts what is in the run and opens it. It took its words from the bin's label in lower case; it now holds them itself, so the bar's parts (*21 by tomorrow · 3 waiting on … · 7 in the run*) keep one dot between them.

## Fit, measured

**The link.** On a list narrowed to one pile, the pile's title carries its name, its count and the way back. That link read *show every pile* and reads **every pile** (`LIEN_PILE_SHOW_EVERY` in [`LienPileHeads.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienPileHeads.tsx)); its hover and its name for a screen reader are *Show every pile*. It has no opposite state: with every pile shown the link is not drawn. The empty pile's press **Show every pile ›** keeps its words, since an empty state names its press in full.

Measured in Chrome on the real markup, the list narrowed to *To send for Master approval* with twelve rows (ZZ test rows):
