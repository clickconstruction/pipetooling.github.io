# v2.3639 — What customers see: the firm's sample portal carries one matter (2026-09-20)

The last item of `to-dos/what-customers-see-residuals.md`, which this PR deletes — the train (v2.3505–v2.3513), its three residuals (v2.3615, v2.3617, this) and the paper-email step (v2.3635) are all in the release notes. One shared edge module; no migration.

`sampleLegalPortalResponse` (`_shared/customerSampleFixtures.ts`, served by `legal-portal` for the sample token) returned `matters: []`, so the tab's **Firm portal** step showed the portal before its first matter. It now returns `sampleLegalMatter(todayYmd)`: a `referred` matter on **Sample Contracting** (the existing sample GC) — one billed job with an $18,400 invoice 124 days old and a $4,000 check against it, a signed `job_contracts` row, three contact entries, a promise 60 days past with no payment after it, two approved GPS clock sessions, one report, one thread note, and one firm fee entry. Every date is `ymdPlusDays(todayYmd, -n)`, so the promise is always broken and the bill is always four months old.

The fixture is a plain literal in a Deno-shared file, so nothing types it against `JobWithDetails`. Two tests hold it to the real thing instead:

- `legalPortalSample.test.ts` — through `parseLegalPortalPayload` and `buildMatterPacket` (the desk's own kernel), on two different "todays": theory `contract`, totals billed 18,400 / paid 4,000 / balance 14,400, the payer's email found, a verdict other than *not worth it*, the promise reading *broken*, three contact lines.
- `LegalPortal.sample.render.test.tsx` — the page itself over a stubbed fetch: the payer and `14,400` draw, and no `NaN` / `undefined` reaches the screen.
