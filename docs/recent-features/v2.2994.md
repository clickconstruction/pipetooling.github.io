# v2.2994 — Test-gap audit, pass 16: the bid Approval PDF gets its first tests (2026-09-07)

Follow-up to v2.2992, which named this builder as the last target of the train. Tests only; no kernel, client, migration or edge-function change.

## Why this one

`bidDocuments/approvalPdf.ts` (586 lines) is the four-page Approval PDF behind **Bids → Submission & Followup → Approval PDF**: Submission and Margins (portrait), Pricing (landscape), Labor (portrait) and the Cover Letter. It fetches its own data (bid versions, count rows for the active version, price-book entries and assignments, custom prices and submission hides, the cost estimate with its labor rows and PO totals, the payment-schedule flag and rows), runs the same pricing and cost arithmetic as the tabs, and draws with jsPDF. It was the largest untested pure module left, and the only test-shaped thing near it was the cover-letter text builder.

## What the tests found

Nothing wrong. Contracts now pinned that were only implied:

- Count rows come from the **active bid version** (`bid_version_id = <active>`), or the null-version rows for an unsplit bid; pricing comes from the bid's **selected price book** only.
- The Margins cost figure is materials + labor + driving + estimator, **without** travel; the Labor page's grand total includes travel when it is non-zero.
- A count row hidden from submission documents is priced into the total but left off the Pricing table and the cover letter's fixture list.
- The cover letter is addressed to the active version's GC override when one exists, otherwise the bid's builder (customer row first, then the legacy builder table); a typed custom amount replaces the priced amount; long links are shortened to 67 characters plus an ellipsis, and blank links print as an em dash.
- Filename: `Approval_<project name>.pdf`, with `Bid` standing in for a nameless bid.

## What shipped

- **`bidDocuments/approvalPdf.test.ts`** (7) — a recording jsPDF stand-in (text calls by page, links, page orientations, the saved filename) and a recording Supabase stand-in routed by table. Scenario A is a split, priced, costed bid with a GC override and schedule of values: section order and orientations; the fetch filters; every page-1 line including the shortened link and the two em dashes; the Pricing table rows, the hidden row's absence and the bold total; the Labor page's materials, labor rows, driving and estimator lines and summary; and the letter's addressee, amount in words, plan date, fixture list and bold headings. Scenario B is an unpriced, uncosted, unsplit bid with a builder-table GC and a custom amount: the three "nothing here" sentences, no links, no signature block, no schedule, the `Bid` fallback title and filename, and the null-version count-row filter.
