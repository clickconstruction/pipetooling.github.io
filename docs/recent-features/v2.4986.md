## The rule, as a table

`src/lib/scheduleDispatch/hubModes.ts` (new) writes the rule down as 29 rows by 11 flags:

- **Rows:** one per thing that changes a mode.
- **Flags:** the five modes, and the flags that travel with them.
  - The modes: placement, linked copy, the placing strip, multi-cell and the picker.
  - The rest: the + menu, the picker's intent and cell, the add-block window, `?placeJob=` and its arm.
- **Cells:** each is `on`, `end` or `keep`, and `hubModeClears(entry)` gives a row's `end` cells.

The table is the page's rule as it stood, gaps included. Six are named on their rows and listed as item 6 of `to-dos/dispatch-residuals.md`:

- a (`urlArm`), b (`openAddBlock`) and c (`tabAway`) leave linked copy on beside another strip. These are bugs.
- d (`weekNav`), e (`openCellPicker`) and f (`escapePlacement`) are owner calls.

A fix is that row, plus the same flip in the test.

## The hook

`src/hooks/useScheduleDispatchHubModes.ts` (new) owns every mode flag, and the picker's open, intent and cell.

- `leave(entry)` is the only place a flag is cleared, and it clears exactly the row's `end` cells.
- These moved from the page verbatim, except that each handler's run of clearing setters is now its row's `leave(...)`:
  - the entry handlers, the Escape effects and the `?placeJob=` arm
  - the week and job-week effects, and `placementSourceBlock`
  - `onCardPlacementPickCell`, `applyHubMultiCellJob` and the three linked-copy applies
- It returns intents and the banners' three clearers, never a flag's setter. The clearers take only `null`. So the page cannot change a mode around the rule, and the type system is the guard.
- `leave` reads the page's two callbacks through refs. So it never changes, and the effects that call it keep the timing they had.

## The page

`ScheduleDispatchHubPage.tsx` goes from 1,935 to 1,448 lines and destructures the same names. The writers it keeps call the hook:

- `openAddBlock`, `closeAdd`, `onHubAssignJobCellPick`, the new-job path, `setHubTab`, `shiftWeek` and `goThisWeek` call `leaveModesFor(...)`, typed to the page's six rows.
- The picker's pick calls `pickJobToPlace`, and a new job with no cell calls `placeNewJob`.
- The picker's search and number query stay in the page; `onPickerOpened` resets them.
- The add-block window stays in the page; `closeAddBlockWindow` shuts it.

`ScheduleDispatchModeBanners`, `ScheduleDispatchMultiCellBar`, the Hub and `useScheduleDispatchNotComingIn` keep their props.

The hook's effects now run together, where the hook is called. Between their old places sat only the Job Detail close and the delete-confirm Escape, and those share no state with them.
