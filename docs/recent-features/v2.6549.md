The office marks a matter's documents and events with themes (v2.6548); the firm had no view of them. No migration. **Redeploy `legal-portal` after the merge.**

## What changed

- `supabase/functions/_shared/legalThemes.ts`: `PortalTheme` and `portalThemesForMatter` — the themes on a matter as counsel reads them: kept marks only (never a proposal, never a removed one), on the documents and entries that travel (a held one stays home with its marks), the words, the proof counts and which documents and entries carry each, most marked first; `portalThemeCountWords` (*6 items · 4 by a document · 1 by their own words · 1 firsthand · 0 relayed*). Never a why, a who or a person id.
- `legal-portal`: reads `legal_themes` and the matters' `legal_theme_marks` fail-soft (missing before the push: no themes) and adds `themes` to each matter's payload; the entries that travel are named once (`travellingEntries`) for the entries list and the themes' read.
- `legalPortalPayload.ts`: `LegalPortalMatter.themes` and `parseLegalPortalThemes` ([] from an older function).
- `LegalFirmMatterView`: **Evidence by theme** above Documents from the office on the Evidence tab (`FirmThemes`); `legalFirmPacketPrint.ts`: `firmThemesSectionHtml`, a block a theme under Section D in the printed packet. The portal page passes the themes to both.
- `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` → legal-portal, `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` → the firm's portal, `HR_FILES.md` → Themes on the record carry the read.
