# v2.4391 — Materials: see what your materials cost now, against February (2026-10-02)

The owner's ask on 2026-10-01: the price book's history shows material prices creeping up, so track it and show it in the app. The plan and its mock-ups are the canvas *Material prices over time* (https://claude.ai/artifact/NojaxDohfxDuP3TkRs2nLb). Grace took all six of its calls on 2026-10-02. This is PR 1 of four: the number, read only. *Check 20 prices* (PR 2), each bid's line with *Refresh prices* (PR 3), and the list of prices that look wrong (PR 4) follow. The market line waits for three months of checks.

## What the data said first
`material_part_price_history` (the `track_price_history` trigger, insert + price change) held 2,488 rows since 2026-01-28. Only 106 were real changes, and some were typo fixes ($438 → $4.38, a $0.56 clamp → $28.06) or a stand-in (a Reece sink at $999,999, on BP338). A plain monthly average read February at −27%. Weighted by bid spend, the 653 part + house pairs the takeoffs use read 99.7 in September against 100 in February. That reading is flat because 9 in 10 of those prices were entered once and never re-checked, not because prices held. So the number ships with how fresh it is, and holds back when too little is fresh.

## What changed
- **The kernel** [`lib/materials/materialPriceIndex.ts`](../../src/lib/materials/materialPriceIndex.ts) (`computeMaterialPriceIndex`):
  - *The basket* is each part + supply house a takeoff line priced from, weighted by Σ quantity × unit price × fixture count over the last 365 days.
  - *Each month* is the spend-weighted geometric mean of every basket price's move since the month before, chained from `MATERIAL_PRICE_BASE_MONTH` = `2026-02` = 100. The current month runs to today.
  - *A big jump is set aside* (`isBigJump`): a move to 1.5× or more, or to half or less, and any price ≤ 0 or ≥ `PLACEHOLDER_PRICE` ($999,999) count as no move.
  - The price at a month's end is the newest event on or before it. Before a pair's first event it is that event's old price, and a pair with no events has kept today's price since before the history began.
  - *A month reads faint* when under 25% of the basket's spend had a price at its end.
  - *Freshness* is each pair's last event (an entry, a change or a confirmation), else the price row's `updated_at`, bucketed ≤ 30 / 31–90 / older by spend. `showNumber` needs half the spend within 90 days.
  - *Moved lately* is each pair's newest ordinary move in 30 days, newest first, three at most, with the open bids that use it.
  - The words (`verdictText`, `changeText`) read inside ±1% as *about the same*.
- **The reads** [`lib/materials/materialPriceIndexIo.ts`](../../src/lib/materials/materialPriceIndexIo.ts) take a year of book-priced lines (`source_material_part_price_id` set, `unit_price` > 0) with their count row, bid, price row, part and house in one embedded select, plus the whole history. Both reads use `fetchAllRows` with a stable order and `withSupabaseRetry`. Left out of the basket:
  - robot parts (`is_robot`)
  - the `🤖 Web Research` house
  - a robot's own bids (`isRobotBid` over the twin user ids, which reads on without them when `users` is hidden)
  - other trades
- **The card** [`MaterialPricesCard`](../../src/components/materials/MaterialPricesCard.tsx) sits atop Materials → Parts Book for the trade pill (`useMaterialPriceIndex`; `MaterialsPartsBookTab` gains `serviceTypeId`). It shows:
  - the verdict, the figure, the line (dashed grey where faint) and *Last 3 months*
  - *How fresh* as a three-part bar with its shares
  - *Moved lately*, with a rise in orange ▲ and a drop in blue ▼
  - *How this works*, which opens the new guide
  
  With too little fresh it says *Not enough fresh prices to say*. A trade with no book-priced takeoffs shows no card. It writes nothing.
- **The guide** *track what your materials cost* (new, plain words).
