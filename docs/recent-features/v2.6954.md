# v2.6954 — Lien affidavit: a hold when the owner is a public body (2026-11-11)

Punch list #314. One paper of the office's first signed batch named an attention line as the owner. The property record held the county roll's two lines as it gave them: a city in the name box and *ATTN …* in the company box, marked a building owner, so the paper printed the company box. The notice's gate asked only the printed name whether it was a public body, so the § 53.056 notice had gone out too, and the affidavit's gates never asked at all.

## What changed

- **`lienOwnerIsPublic(names)`** in `src/lib/jobs/lienDeskAffidavits.ts`: `ownerKind` (the roll's own test: city of, county, ISD, school district, State of Texas, authority, …) over every name the record gives, not the one the paper prints.
- **The affidavit's owner gate has three answers** (`ownerGate`): no owner of record with a mailing address (`owner`), a public owner (`public_owner`, the desk's own sentence `PUBLIC_OWNER_DESK_SENTENCE`), or the owner. The key says which hold it is, so the *Fill in the record* door stays the missing owner's and a public owner offers none.
- **The fact is the desk's, not the RPC's.** `useLienDeskData` reads the property records under the affidavits (`select('*')`, in light mode too, so the Dashboard's count and the desk agree) and fills `owner_public` with `withPublicOwner`. A failed read and a job with no linked record are not judged.
- **Where it speaks**: the gate list on the Affidavits pane, the Lien window's Mechanic's lien tab (its own line, first, and no paper while it is closed), the hold line on a timeline stop, the list row's *missing a private owner*, Do now's row (*public property, no lien attaches*, no press) and the paper preview's record blank (**Talk to counsel**).
- **The notice's gate asks every name too.** `buildLienDeskGates` takes `ownerIsPublic`, which the desk fills from both of the owner's names.
- **An attention line is never the owner.** `lienPropertyOwnerDisplayName` skips a box that opens with *ATTN*, *Attention*, *c/o* or *%* (`lienAttentionLine`), so the paper names the other box.

## What a hold does

It changes the pile, never the paper's words. A paper not yet sent sits in **Needs the property facts**; an approved paper keeps its pile, and its Lien window draws no paper. There is no override: the notice's gate has none either, and the remedy on public property is a claim on the payment bond, which is counsel's.
