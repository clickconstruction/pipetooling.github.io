# v2.8676 — Agreements: a paper can print its site's address for the signer to read (2026-11-27)

Punch list #439, piece 3 of seven, its last part (3e). No migration: the column `site_prints_address` is on prod since `20261126070436_paper_names_a_site.sql` (v2.8642). **Three edge functions owe a deploy after the merge**, in this order: `accept-contract`, `person-paper-pdf`, then `get-contract-for-signer` last.

## What was there

Since v2.8660 **Send a paper** can name the site an outside company's paper is for. The row kept the address as written and a column that says whether the paper prints it, and every send wrote that column false. The signer's page reads through `get-contract-for-signer`, which named its own columns, so nothing of a site reached a signer.

## What changed

- **The box.** Under a named site in the email window: **Print the site's address on the paper**, unticked. The words under it say what the signer reads as it stands. Unticked: *The signer reads nothing about the site.* Ticked: *Above the paper the signer reads Site address:* and the address itself, in bold.
- **A new site unticks it.** The tick is for one address. **Change the site…** and **Take the site off** each clear it.
- **The signer's page.** When the box was ticked, the page shows one line above the paper, *Site address* with the address, in a small frame of its own under who signs. With a clause paper read in Spanish the label reads *Dirección de la obra*. With the box off the page is as it was.
- **The signed PDF.** A paper of plain words or clauses that prints its address opens its words with *Site address: …*, in the language it was signed in. The signer's emailed copy is that PDF. A form's PDF is the filled form and prints none.
- **Only the address.** The function hands over the address as written and nothing else of the site: not its key, not its development, not that the paper names one when the box is off.
- **Written at Send email**, with the row. A send that lands on a copy written before sets that copy's column to the box as it stands, and writes nothing when it already matches.
- **Once the row is written, the site and the box stand.** A send whose email fails after the write is pressed again on the same row. Until now the site's presses stayed live in between, and a change made there showed one thing and sent another. The line now reads *Kept with the paper when Send email was pressed.*, the presses go, and the box is dead as it was sent.

## The order of landing, and why nothing is promised early

The app and the functions deploy apart, so each side reads the other softly.

- **The new app with the old functions** (between the merge and the deploy). The box is drawn dead and unticked, with *The signer's page cannot show an address yet. This stays off until it can.* The app asks `get-contract-for-signer` for its sample paper and reads whether the answer has a `site_address` key (`answersSitePrint`). The old function's has none. So no row is written with the print on, and every signer reads what they read before. Nobody has to hold anything: the box holds itself.
- **The new signer's page with the old function.** The answer has no `site_address`, the page shows no line, and the signature carries no address. As before.
- **The old app with the new functions.** No old window can tick the box. An old signer's page that is still open shows no address, so `accept-contract` turns its signature back as a changed paper (409 `paper_changed`), and the reload reads the new page with the line.
- **The functions' own order.** `get-contract-for-signer` goes last, since the box goes live on it. Were it first, a signer could read the address and sign through the older `accept-contract`, and the PDF made at that signing would lack the line and be kept as it is.

## How it is built

- `supabase/functions/_shared/paperSitePrint.ts` (pure, with no import, read by the app straight from there). `printedSiteAddress` is the one rule: the address only when `site_prints_address` is `true` itself and the row keeps one. `siteShownAtSigning` is the signing's check. `answersSitePrint` reads whether a function knows the rule. `withSitePrintLine` opens a signed paper's words with the line.
- `get-contract-for-signer` selects the two columns and answers `site_address`, an address or null, on every paper. The sample paper answers null.
- `accept-contract` takes the body's `siteAddress` and refuses a paper that prints an address from a page that did not show that address. Checked before anything is stored.
- `_shared/personPaperSignedCopy.ts` reads the two columns with the signed row and draws the line. `accept-contract` and `person-paper-pdf` bundle it.
- `src/lib/addresses/paperSite.ts`: `sitePrintBox` is the box for a site, its state and its words, null for no site and for a development. `sitePrintsAtSend` is what the send writes. `paperSiteColumns` takes the tick and keeps it only beside an address.
- `PaperSiteLine.tsx` (new) draws the site's line and the box. `AgreementsSendPaper.tsx` asks whether the page can show an address each time a site is named (`sitePrintReadyIo.ts`), and sets a reused copy's column (`writePaperPrintsAddress`).
- `ContractAccept.tsx` shows the line with `ValueLines` and sends the address back with the signature.

## Design calls

- **The box is in the email window, under the site.** That is where the site is chosen, and where the office reads the send once more.
- **Off says nothing at all.** The mock-up's *Site address: Given after you sign* line is not drawn: it tells a signer the paper names a site, and it belongs with the code name of piece 7.
- **The signed PDF prints the line too**, as the first of its words. The paper the signer keeps says what they read. This is why `accept-contract` and `person-paper-pdf` owe a deploy beside the signer's function.
- **A signature is taken only from a page that showed the address.** One small check in `accept-contract`, asked only of a paper that prints one.
- **The box holds itself until the function is live**, by asking the function. No setting, and nothing for a person to remember.
- **A form may tick the box.** Its signer reads the address on the page. Its PDF is the form and prints none, and the guide says so.
- **A copy sent again keeps its print as sent.** The site's line is not drawn on *Send it to … again*, so the box is not either.
- **The site's presses go once the row is written.** This changes v2.8660's line for a send that failed after its write. A change there never reached the row, and with the box it would have promised a signer's page the row did not hold.
