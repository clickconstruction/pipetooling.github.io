- **`gc_money_team()`** names who reads and writes our number: dev, the leaders and the controller. **`gc_project_money`** holds the three inputs behind it, one row per GC project.
  - Each project's values are copied from `gc_projects` (the expand step). Those columns stay, unwritten, until B6-a drops them (the contract step).
  - Door 1 had opened them to estimators and assistants. On prod they are all 0, so nothing leaked.
- **The client's copy**: `GC_MONEY_TEAM` and `canSeeGcMoney` in `src/lib/gc/access.ts`. `access.test.ts` reads the newest migration that defines `gc_money_team()` and fails when the two lists differ.
- **The proof**: a role matrix on a local Postgres, with the real training-mode and twin blocks.
  - dev, master and controller read and write.
  - Assistant, estimator, the field and subs read no row and are refused writes.
  - Training mode is refused writes, a twin reads nothing, and `anon` is denied.
  - The doc carries the table and the rolled-back per-role read for prod after the push.
