# v2.5416 — Messages: a lead who texts the office keeps one thread until they book, behind the scenes (2026-10-12)

Residential R8 · 9, the kernel: `src/lib/prospects/leadThread.ts`, pure and tested
(`to-dos/gc-mode/messages/RESIDENTIAL_BUILD_PLAN.md` → R8; card 9 of `customer-messages-integrations-mockups.html` v0.2,
both on branch spike/gc-mode). Built on R0's kinds and loaders (#5565, #5570). No migration, no RPC and no screen. The
Prospects page, `contacts/kernel.ts` and `followUp/queue.ts` are not touched.

**Where a lead lives (the lead's change of 2026-10-13).** Card 9 put a lead's thread on Prospects. Prospects is the
cold-call deck of companies, and its guide says a prospect is a company, so a homeowner who texted would land in the
calling queue as never called. A lead now lives in Messages instead: a contact of kind `lead` with no record until they
book, their thread on Follow up as #5565 already counts it. A `prospects` row is written only when the office presses
**Add to Prospects** on the lead's card.

- `leadFromWhoIsThis(contacts, unknownId, name)`: *Who is this?* → a new lead. The contact becomes a lead under the name
  the line gives (R7's `nameInText`, passed in) or keeps the number, with no record and no job. The thread stays whole.
  Null for a contact that is already named.
- `prospectRowFor(lead, messages, by)`: the row **Add to Prospects** writes: `contact_name` (null while the name is
  still the number), `phone_number`, `email`, `notes` led by how they came in ("Texted the office: …", the first line in),
  `last_contact` (the newest line), `created_by` and `master_user_id`, with `company_name`, `prospect_fit_status` and
  warmth left empty. `prospects` has no source column. `addedToProspects(contacts, leadId, id)` then points the lead at
  the row through `nameContact`, once.
- `leadCardThread(lead, messages, now)`: the card's last three lines with them (team notes and events stay in the
  thread), the thread's link (`/gc?thread=<id>&at=<newest>`, as `useThreadWindow` reads it), whose move, and when they
  last wrote. Nothing of money. `leadNeeds(leads, messages)` is the card's waiting flag. It is not a second count:
  Follow up counts a waiting lead already.
- `bookingRepoint(contacts, leadId, customer)`: booking re-points the lead to the new `customers` row and keeps every
  line. If a contact already is that customer by name, the lead folds into it, the lines and any new number moving
  onto it. When the lead was added to Prospects, it returns that row's id for `markProspectConverted`; otherwise the
  booking is just the customer being made. The card then reads *Became a customer.* with `/customers/<id>`.

Every re-point is a `NamePlan` the caller folds through the spine's `applyNamePlan`. Adding a lead to Prospects and
booking one are `nameContact`'s own rules since #5570, so `addedToProspects` and `bookingRepoint` call it. Only naming a
number a lead with no record is built here, since a `NameTarget` always has a record. The writes wait on R0's SQL:
`name_contact` has to take the kind `lead`, allow a lead with no record, and re-point a lead as #5570's kernel does.

The Glossary clause for a lead waits until the Messages entry's line is quiet. #5565 and others are editing it now.
