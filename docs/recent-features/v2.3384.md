## Why

Owner, 2026-09-13, on the Contract sweep: "I do not love this page. I think it should be easy for the user to upload a contract or to view what is about to be sent" — and, on the first redesign, "is this the best we can do?". The reading: the sweep listed 104 jobs as if every one needed us to send our agreement, but the nudge kernel had no dollar floor (a $450 repair counted like a $123,600 job) and the office had no way to answer "this one does not need paper" (a builder's subcontract on file elsewhere, a service call). Shrinking the count to the jobs worth chasing comes before making the sweep good.

## What shipped

- **Schema**: `jobs_ledger.contract_not_needed_at` / `_by` / `_reason` — the office's answer on the job. The floor is `app_settings.job_contract_floor_cents_v1` (`value_num`, cents; dev-write like every org switch).
- **Coverage kernel** [`jobContractCoverage.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobContractCoverage.ts) (+3 tests): a new coverage kind `not_needed` (precedence: signed → paper → e-signed estimate → bid room → **not needed** → sent → draft → none), the chip words *No contract · not needed* with the reason and date on hover, `CONTRACT_NOT_NEEDED_REASONS`, and **`isContractGap(cov, revenue, floorCents)`** — the one rule the nudge, the card, the No-contract filter and the sweep now share, so the four numbers agree. `filterJobsByContractCoverage` takes the floor.
- **Floor kernel** [`jobContractFloor.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobContractFloor.ts) (+3 tests): `isUnderContractFloor` (an amount below the floor; **no amount is unknown, not small, and stays in the count**), read/write, `formatContractFloor`, `parseTypedFloorToCents`; `parseJobContractFloorCents` in `appSettingsKeys.ts`.
- **Nudge kernel** [`jobContractNudge.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobContractNudge.ts) (+2 tests): `summarizeContractNudge(…, { floorCents })` leaves under-floor and not-needed jobs out of `missing` and `byStage.missing`, and reports `underFloor { count, revenueTotal }`, `notNeeded { count }`, `floorCents`. [`useJobContractsNudge`](../../src/hooks/useJobContractsNudge.ts) selects the new columns and reads the floor; the Dashboard's *N live jobs have no contract on file* item falls with them.
- **The card** [`PipelineContractCoverageCard`](../../src/components/jobs/PipelineContractCoverageCard.tsx): a third line — *Floor $2,500 · 9 small jobs not counted · 3 marked not needed* — with **change** for devs (an inline box; Enter saves, 0 removes the floor). The all-clear line reads *…has an agreement on file, or doesn't need one*.
- **The Contract modal** [`JobContractModal`](../../src/components/jobs/JobContractModal.tsx): **Not needed…** in the footer opens three reason chips (*GC job — their subcontract* · *Service call* · *Warranty / no charge*) and a free-text line; **Mark not needed** writes the job and the modal shows a dashed banner with **Needed after all**. Sending or filing still works on a not-needed job; a signed record still wins in the chip. `onJobChanged` lets the Stages tab reload its rows (the answer lives on the job row, not in the contract scan).
- **The sweep and the filter**: the sweep's rows, the ⋯ menu's *N without*, and the Pipeline's No-contract filter all read `isContractGap` with the floor; the sweep's header says *jobs under $2,500 left out*.
- **Chip**: a `not_needed` tone — faint text, dashed edge — quieter than a gap.
- Help guide *get a job contract signed*: "Not every job needs one" (the floor, Not needed, Needed after all).
