# v2.2811 — People page: six top-level tabs, every view kept as a sub-tab (2026-09-05)

The People page had grown to eighteen tabs for a dev — the strip scrolled on a laptop and on a phone only three tabs fit before the page title. Owner decision (proposal artifact 3ff49cb1 → mockup d7ec155b): keep every view, nest them under six groups, Person under People, Feedback as its own sixth tab.

- **Groups** (`src/lib/people/peopleTabGroups.ts`, pure + tested): **People** (Users · Subs · Person), **Pay** (Hours · Payroll · Offsets · Employment · Overhead), **Paperwork** (Contracts · Licenses · Writeups · HR), **Fleet & Housing** (Vehicles · Housing), **Review** (Review · Scoreboard · Activity), **Feedback** (alone; no second row). `PeopleTab` (the 18 view keys) moved from `People.tsx` into the kernel; `PEOPLE_TAB_LABELS` carries the sub-tab labels (`pay_stubs` → Payroll, as before).
- **Gates unchanged.** `People.tsx` builds a `visiblePeopleTabs` record from the same flags each tab button used (`isDev`, `canOpenPersonDesk`, `canAccessPay`, `canOpenHoursTab`, `canAccessOverheadTab`, `canAccessContracts`, `canAccessLicenses`, `canAccessVehicles`, `canSeeActivityTab`); `visibleTabGroups()` shows a group when any of its views would have shown as a tab, so the office sees four groups and a non-pay-approved master sees People + Paperwork. Render sites and the `?tab=` URL gate effect are untouched apart from the tab-key check collapsing to `isPeopleTab()`.
- **URL contract unchanged.** `?tab=<view key>` still addresses a view; the group is derived (`groupOfTab`), never stored. `?tab=contracts&doc=…`, `?tab=person&id=…`, `?tab=hours&approvals=1`, the `team_costs`/`pay`/`teams` legacy redirects, all keep working.
- **Per-group memory.** Clicking a group lands on the view last used in that group (`landingViewForGroup`), else its first visible view; stored device-locally under `people.tabGroupMemory.v1` (`parseGroupMemory` drops junk and views filed under the wrong group).
- **Second row** renders only when the active group offers more than one visible view (`pageSubTabStyle` in `src/lib/pageTabStyle.ts` — small pills, selected one inverted). Both rows are `role="tablist"` with `aria-selected`.
- Label kept as **Users** (not the mockup's "Roster") — ~80 guide and doc references say People → Users; renaming is a one-line follow-up in `PEOPLE_TAB_LABELS` plus a doc sweep.
- Docs: guide `find-my-way-around-the-people-page`, `manage-employment` wording, `PEOPLE_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md` (groups section), `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`, `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`.
