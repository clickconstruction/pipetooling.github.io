# v2.6981 — First look, the spike: a link with no sign-in that takes photos and a video of the problem (2026-11-11)

Punch list #319 (`to-dos/show-us-the-problem/README.md`). The owner's idea of 2026-11-11: a caller gets a link by text, shows the problem in photos and a short video on a page with no sign-in, and the office prices it before a truck rolls. The card's plan put a spike first, to learn on real phones what no desk can. The owner asked for the spike on `main` so he can try it on his own phone, so this is the spike built to stay: gated to the owner and the developers, and texting nobody.

## What it is

- **The customer's page, `/show/<ten letters>`** (`src/pages/ShowUs.tsx`), outside the sign-in, light, on the portal's paper. *Show us the problem*, who asked, **Take a photo**, **Take a video**, **Choose from your phone**, four tips and the owner's own line under them. It never shows a name, an address, a price or a picture sent before: opened again it shows a count.
- **Kept on the phone first** (`src/lib/firstLook/kept.ts`). Each file is saved in the browser's store the moment it is picked, then sent. With no signal the page says *Saved on this phone* and tries again when the signal is back, when the page is looked at again, and every fifteen seconds. Opened again later, it finds what is still kept and sends it.
- **Straight to storage, never through a function** (`sendFile.ts`, `pieces.ts`, `showUsIo.ts`). The function hands the page a one-file upload address. A photo is shrunk to the Large size and sent in one request. A video past six megabytes goes in pieces of six megabytes (the resumable upload protocol, tus 1.0.0, with the link's key as `x-signature`) and picks up at the piece it stopped on. A video storage will not take in pieces goes whole.
- **The function `first-look`** (`supabase/functions/first-look/index.ts`, `verify_jwt = false`): the page's four asks by the link's code (open, sign, done, send) and the desk's five signed in. On *done* it reads the object's size from the bucket and its first 32 bytes through a one-minute link, and deletes anything that is not a picture or a video. A code that opens nothing counts a miss through the estimate link's guard.
- **The desk, `/first-looks`** (`src/pages/FirstLooks.tsx`), the owner and the developers: **Make a link** (shown once, with a QR, **Copy the link** and **Copy the ask**), and every look with its photos, its video in a player, the customer's two fields, and what the spike measures of each upload. **Close the link** and **Remove this look…**. With `?contact=<a Messages contact's id>` the link is tied to that person, and their send writes one line on their thread.
- **The ground**: migration `20261112060000_first_looks.sql` (`first_looks`, `first_look_files`, the private bucket `first-look-files`), and the pure rules both sides share in `supabase/functions/_shared/firstLook.ts`, re-exported by `src/lib/firstLook/look.ts`.

## What it is not, yet

Nothing here texts anyone: the office copies the ask and sends it from the thread. Nothing files to Google Drive: the files stay in the bucket. The Messages thread has no card for a look, the reply box has no `/show`, an estimate does not remember a look, and a tech sees nothing. Those are PRs 1 to 7 on the card. The short host `my.clickplumbing.com` has no `/show/` line, so the link is on the app's own address. No guide ships, since no office flow is offered yet.

## Found while testing

- **Safari in a private window will not store a file in its own store, and will store its raw bytes.** The walk in Safari's engine kept nothing across a closed page until the store learned the second way. `kept.ts` now tries the file, then its bytes, then memory alone.
- **A write the browser accepts can still be thrown away at the commit.** The store now waits for the commit before it trusts a write.
- **One camera press for both kinds is not safe.** A file field that asks for pictures and video with the camera opens only the photo camera on some phones, so there are two presses, one a kind.
- **A link that never finishes a file could still fill the bucket.** The caps count the upload addresses handed out for each kind (30 photos, 6 videos), not only the files that arrived.
- **The live project's storage limit is 50 MiB a file** (`fileSizeLimit` 52,428,800, read 2026-11-11 through the Management API). The bucket and the page use the same number. A two-minute video needs the limit raised in the Supabase dashboard first.
