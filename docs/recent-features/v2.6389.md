# v2.6389 — Job Summary's header says what each number is for: to date or at 100 %, a Heading for tile, Burn in words, and a billed-in-full flag (2026-11-02)

The layout half of the owner's J804 read ([v2.6388](v2.6388.md) fixed the three bugs). He chose state C of the mock-up: a header where every number says which moment it belongs to, Burn that speaks, the row naming the contradiction it carries, and window-wide chips that step back under a one-job search. Client only; the kernel is the new [`jobSummaryHeaderWords.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobSummaryHeaderWords.ts), and no money math moved.

## What changed

- **Tiles with a moment.** `jobSummaryTileWords` writes each tile's second line. While any shown row is in progress the to-date tiles say *to date* (Gross profit *to date · −53% margin*, True profit *to date · −70% true margin*, Overhead charged *to date · day-share · — of gross*); a Revenue tile over one earned row says the rule itself — *Revenue earned · to date · 40% of $32,600* (or *assumed 50% of …*); over several, *to date · 2 of 3 in progress earned by hours*; with every row finished, *Revenue · contract · every job finished*. Per field hour reads *true profit ÷ 139.7 h*.
- **Heading for.** A seventh tile between True profit and Per field hour: Σ projected true margin at 100 % over the in-progress rows that project (the figure the `<tfoot>` already summed), with the margin it works out to over those rows' contracts — `summarizeJobSummaryRows` grew `projectedContractUsd` for the denominator. Red under zero or under the Target chip. The column header is **Proj. true margin** with *at 100%* under it; the sort key and the arithmetic are unchanged.
- **Burn in words.** `jobSummaryBurnCellWords`: *94% spent · 40% done* · *12% spent · early* · *done* · —. The footing glyph and the hover (footing, lead in points) are as before.
- **Billed in full.** `enrichJobSummaryRows` marks a row `billedInFull` (flag `billed-in-full`) when its invoices, any status, cover the contract (`paidInvoicesCoverContract` over `jobInvoicedTotalUsd`) while its % is under 100 — J804's shape: billed to the contract Oct 12 at 100 %, then a line report took it to 40. The % cell draws a red **billed in full** chip whose hover says the office or the crew is wrong and what to do; the strip counts them (*⚠ 1 job is billed in full but under 100%*, `billedInFullJobs`); and a one-job search's note reads the margin both ways (`jobSummarySearchNote`): *Job 804 is billed in full, but the crew's last report says 40% done. Read at 40% it is under water. Read as finished its margin is 39%.* The rule itself is not changed — the % still decides; the row says what each answer means.
- **Window chips fold.** `jobSummaryWindowChipsFold`: while the search box holds something, the hygiene chips that speak for the whole window — sessions awaiting approval, overhead with nobody to charge, overhead in flight, overhead on jobs off the list — fold into one dashed line that names the window (*Across this year: 15 sessions await approval · $6,517 of overhead in flight · $12,745 on 10 jobs off this list ▾*); a press opens them. The chips about the rows themselves (assumption, under target, write-downs, discounts, collections, no contract $, no %, hours before the window, the reconcile alarm) never fold.
