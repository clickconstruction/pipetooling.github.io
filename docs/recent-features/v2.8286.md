# v2.8286 — The new phone layout: the Job Mode card opens on Call, Directions and Text (2026-11-22)

The second of two PRs behind the **New phone layout** tick (v2.8285). This one re-lays the Job Mode card, the most used screen in the app: seven helpers, every day. No migration, no edge function.

## The rule the job is held to

With the switch off, nothing changes for anyone. With it on, every press that exists today is still reachable. The card's state logic is not touched: which job, which press, which sheet and what each writes are the lines they were.

## The layout

The card is laid out the new way when the switch is on at a phone's width, the card is the full one, and it shows a job's details (`phoneJobCardOn` in `src/lib/phoneLayout/jobCard.ts`).

1. **The header**, as it was: the status line, the job's number and name, the address, the leg.
2. **Call, Directions, Text**, 44 px each, sharing the width.
   - **Call** makes the same call the number's link made: the number as it is kept, handed to the phone. For a subcontractor it is the number of whoever runs the job, as on the old card, and never the customer's.
   - **Directions** opens the same maps address the square opened.
   - **Text** is the card's own Message the customer door (`MessageCustomerDoor`) drawn in that shape, with its orange dot when the customer answered. It is drawn exactly where the door is offered today: to the roles of `jobModeMessagesOffered`, on the job the person is clocked onto. A leader on the same job has no Text. No new way to text a customer was added.
3. **The number as a phone number** on one quiet line (`phoneAsWritten`, over the app's own `phoneContact`).
4. **The trip, a failed read, today's stops.** A day with one stop is one line, *8:00 AM · 1 of 1 today*. The line is still the stop's press, so a stop to head to and a job whose papers are owed are a press away as before. With several stops the list stays, its rows 44 px tall.
5. **Five rows**, 44 px, full width, each opening what it opened: **Materials on site** with its count and the *due a count* and stickers words under its name, **Photos** with its count, **Job updates** with its count, **Job details**, **Job calendar**.
   - **Job updates** opens in place. It is shut until pressed, and the thread stays mounted while shut, so a note half typed is kept.
6. **Photo of a spot**, then **Paint a map**, **Place on this job** and **My spend** on one row.
7. **The quiet presses**: Make change order, Hand the phone, My statement.
8. The rest as it was: a First look, the stage checklists, the machines, the ticket photo, the customer's name, the email as a 44 px press that copies it, Street View.

## The squares fold into the card

Job Mode's top row and the card are drawn by different parts of the Dashboard, and each reads the day for itself. The squares may only leave once the card draws every press they had. So the card says which job it has laid out (`foldedJob.ts`), and the row leaves for that job alone (`jobSquaresFolded`).

| Square | In the card |
|---|---|
| Directions | the first row |
| Job details | a row |
| Photo (on site) | the Photos row, the same window |
| On site (a site with boxes) | the Materials on site row, the same address |
| Paint a map | the row of presses |
| My spend | the row of presses, with its count |
| This week, Tomorrow (a day with no job) | stay as squares |

## Where the old card stays, with the switch on

- **A day with no job**, and a person clocked onto a bid: the card shows no details, so there is nothing to lay out. The squares stay.
- **A job closed by its papers**: the card shows its number and the papers to sign, never its details. The squares stay.
- **The short Job Mode page**: its lower half is already behind one row.

## Nothing under 12 px, no press under 40 px

The card is many pieces, each with its own inline sizes. The pieces this PR lays out are drawn to the floor. For every other piece, in every state, the floor is held once in `index.css` under `.jobModeCardPhone`, a class the card has only while the switch is on:

- a press with no height of its own stands 40 px, and a height written under 40 px is raised to it;
- words written from 0.6rem to under 0.75rem, or at 10 or 11 px, read at 0.75rem;
- a size at or over the floor is left as written;
- a window a press on the card opens is not the card: every window is a dialog, and the floor stops at it.

On the live card this raised the thread's filter pills from 32 px to 40 px and its tags from 10.4 px to 12 px.

## What holds it

- `DashboardJobModeCard.newPhoneLayout.render.test.tsx`: fourteen states of the card are each drawn the old way and then the new way, and a press the old card offers that the new one does not fails the test. The states: no job, ready to start with one stop and with several, on my way, on site, clocked in mid-day, clocked in on the day's one stop, the last job hourly and salaried, a job off the schedule, a bid, a job closed by its papers, a superintendent on site, a subcontractor.
- For the three states the new layout leaves alone, the card's markup is the same string with the switch on and off.
- At a desk the card's markup is the same string with the tick on and off.
- `JobModeTopRow.newPhoneLayout.render.test.tsx`: the squares fold only for the job the card holds, come back when it lets go, and stay with the switch off, at a desk and on a day with no job.
- `jobCard.test.ts`, `foldedJob.test.ts`: the rules and the words.

The shared pieces of the card were lifted into named values and the rail into one function, so both layouts draw the same code. The old layout draws the same markup from them, which the tests above hold.
