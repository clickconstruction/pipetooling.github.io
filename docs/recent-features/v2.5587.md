# v2.5587 — The tech’s bio: the office writes it on the desk, the tech picks the lines in Settings (2026-10-16)

Grace's ask the morning after On my way (#112, v2.5573) shipped, refined over three mock-ups and five calls (punch list #114, `to-dos/tech-about-lines/README.md`): a short bio per tech, "that initial resume we hand over with a plumber", sent once with the first message a tech sends a customer, never a fact rotated into every visit, with the tech able to pick the lines for one customer from the sheet. This is PR 2 of the train, **the ground the office can use at once**: the table, the thread's memory column, the two functions, the kernel, the desk's block and the Settings block. The send itself (the bio as a second text after the first On my way, the sheet's bubble and the Edit bio window, the thread's mark) is PR 3. Migration `20261016030000_tech_bio.sql` (`docs/migrations/20261016030000_tech_bio.md`); no edge function change.

- **The data**: `user_bio_lines` (the user, the English, the Spanish, a place, who added it and when, `removed_at`, `off_by_user_at` the tech's veto), read by the office team and by each person for their own rows, written only through `edit_bio_line` (the office, through the quick replies' own caller check) and `bio_line_say` (the row's owner). `messages.introduces_user_id` is the memory PR 3 stamps on the bio text, indexed with `contact_id`. `bio_opener(user, lang)` gives *A little about Luis: * / *Un poco sobre Luis: * from the first word of the name, the same rule as the client's kernel, so the desk's count and the function's cap agree. The whole bio is capped at **320 characters** in each language, opener included, counted over every live line whichever the tech turned off. The three fences cover the new table.
- **The kernel** `src/lib/bio/bio.ts` (pure, 16 tests): `bioLinesFromRows` (the office's order: place, when added, id), `liveLines`, `usualPicks` (live and not turned off — what the Edit bio window opens with), `bioOpener`, `bioPickedLines` / `bioText` (the opener and the picked lines joined; Spanish takes only the lines that have it; null when nothing goes, so no bio is sent rather than an empty one), `bioLength` / `bioLengthWith` / `leftWords` (the desk's count, before and after an edit), `introduced` (a line out in the thread that names the tech), `BIO_PROMPTS` (six, each starting a sentence by name) and the words, which pass `plainWordsFailures`. `src/lib/bio/bioIo.ts` reads the lines (null while the push is not in) and calls the two functions.
- **The desk** (`src/components/personDesk/sections/PersonDeskBioSection.tsx`, section id `bio` after Field): **Luis's bio** for the office team on a primary, a helper or an office member — the bio as a customer gets it in each language with the count, the lines with the Spanish beside each (*No Spanish yet* where it is missing), *Luis turned this off* on a vetoed line, Edit in place, Remove after a confirm, and **Add a line** with the six prompts, a Spanish box and the count of the whole, refused past the cap before the server is asked. Training mode reads with no buttons. 8 smokes, and the role gates live in `lib/bio/bioGates.ts` (`bioOnDesk`, `yourBioShown`).
- **Settings → Your account → Your bio** (`src/components/settings/YourBioCard.tsx`, after My Profile, for primaries, helpers and the office team): the bio as a customer gets it and one **Say it** / **Don't say this** switch a line, saved through `bio_line_say`; *The office has not written your bio yet* until then. 5 smokes. Both blocks draw nothing while the push is not in.
