# v2.4423 — Lien instruments on a phone: Already sent opens as its own page, and Release of record fits the screen (2026-10-02)

No migration, no edge function. Left open by [v2.4422](v2.4422.md) (*Not done*). Robert, 2026-10-02: "should we do the same for Already sent and Release of record?" Yes for the first; the second has nothing to open, so it is fixed in place.

## What was wrong

Measured at 390 × 844 on the dev server against production data, job 273, Lien instruments → § 53.056 notice → **Already sent — record it…**. The box was 533 px tall and its three-column grid gave each field 120 px: the date read *10/02/202*, the method *certified ma*, the tracking hint *9407 1118 … (c*, the saved copy *Drive link (opti*. The second recipient tick, *original contractor*, ran past the box, and the tab scrolled sideways (370 px of content in 358). The ticks were 13 px squares; **Back** was 29 px and **Record it on 1 job** 27 px.

Release of record draws the same saved-copy grid (`auto minmax(160px, 2fr) minmax(110px, 1fr)`, wider than a phone's column) and a date in 13 px type.

## What changed

- **[`LienNoticeByHandPane`](../../src/components/jobs/LienNoticeByHandPane.tsx)**: a `layout` prop. `'sheet'` draws the step on [`LienRecordSheet`](../../src/components/jobs/LienRecordSheet.tsx) (kind `notice_by_hand`): Sent on, How it went, Tracking, *Who it went to* as two rows to tick, the claim and the months as printed, the saved copy, the other unpaid jobs at the property as rows to tick, the difference line, and what is still needed in red above the one button. It is one component with one state, so the values hold if a phone is turned and the layout changes. `'box'` (the default) is the box as it was; the Lien desk still uses it. `onCloseWindow` is the sheet's ×.
- **The shell**: `action.disabled` greys the button while the step still needs something. [`lienRecordSheetStyles.ts`](../../src/components/jobs/lienRecordSheetStyles.ts) gains `recordSheetTick`, a row to tick, 48 px tall.
- **[`LienFilingTabs`](../../src/components/jobs/LienFilingTabs.tsx)**: passes `layout={isMobile ? 'sheet' : 'box'}`. The pane stays where it is in the tab, inside the scroller; its sheet is `position: absolute` against the card, which the scroller does not clip. On **Release of record** at 640 px and under, the date takes the sheet's field and label, the saved copy is the sheet's stacked pair, and the three buttons are a two-column grid (`data-lien-release-actions`) with **Save release record** across both.
- **[`index.css`](../../src/index.css)**: the card's full-height rule now matches a sheet anywhere inside the card (`:has([data-lien-record-sheet])`), since this one is not a direct child.
- Guide [`file-a-lien-and-never-miss-its-deadlines`](../../src/content/help/file-a-lien-and-never-miss-its-deadlines.md): *On a phone* names the fourth step and says what Release of record does.

## Verified

- **Already sent**, live, same job and size, read only — **the record button was not pressed**. The sheet covers the card top to bottom (34 to 810; a hit test at the top, the middle and the bottom lands on the sheet, so the tab's scroller does not clip it). Seven fields 326 × 48, six rows to tick 48 to 52 px, the button 48 px, nothing past the edge, and the tab under it no longer scrolls sideways. The sheet scrolls (1,199 px of content in 724). With the paper at 250, Back removes the sheet and leaves the paper at 250.
- **Release of record**: by the render test only. No job on hand has a filed lien.
- Render tests: the pane's sheet layout (prefill, what is missing, a second job, the record) and the filing tabs' two new cases.

## Not done
