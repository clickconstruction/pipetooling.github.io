# v2.6079 — The morning run keeps back a reminder with a blank it cannot fill (punch list #124) (2026-10-29)

Punch list #124's open piece, found while #126 PR 4 (PR #6441) seeded the starter quick replies on the Approved (`/estimate`, `{link}`) and Paid (`/remind`, `{amount}` and `{bill}`) steps: the morning run (`src/lib/flows/nudgeDispatch.ts` → `planNudgeRun`, the edge function `messages-nudge-dispatch`) filled only `{first}` and `{job}` and took the filled text, so with a step on **Sends** a team line with any other blank went to the customer with the braces in it, and on **Offers** Today's sends' press sent it the same way. Tell the customer on a Pipeline move (`stageSends.ts`) already kept such a line back as `blanks left`. Now the run does too. No migration.

## What changed

- **`planNudgeRun`** reads `fillLine`'s `left` after filling `{first}`, `{job}` and now `{link}` (the person's portal link the run already reads, `input.links`). `{rate}` is not a blank left: the edge mints it as the text goes (v2.5943). With a step on Sends, a text with a blank left is kept back with the new reason `blanks left`, its words naming the blanks (`the line still has {amount} and {bill}`), after the person's own reasons (no number, STOP, email). With a step on Offers, the prepared text is held on Today's sends (`held`) with `offerHeldBlanks`: *The line still has {amount} and {bill}. Send it from the customer’s thread, where the box fills it, or change the step’s line.* A held row has no Send button, so nothing with braces goes from the Dashboard either.
- **`blanksLeftWords`** is the one home for the words; `stageSends.ts`'s `blanks left` reads it too, and three blanks now read as a list (`{amount}, {job} and {bill}`, `blankNames`). `nudgeLine` treats a team line holding `{link}` as already pointing at the portal, so the upload line is not added twice.
- **`nudgeRunLog.ts`**: the summary counts `blanks left` among the kept reasons (*1 reminder was kept back because the step’s line still has a blank the run cannot fill.*), after the person's reasons; the Flows card's last-run line (`appSends.ts` → `lastRunLine`) says *2 have a blank the run cannot fill* in the same place.
- **The edge function** `messages-nudge-dispatch` changes nothing of its own; the kernel copies under `_shared/gcKernels/` are regenerated (`nudgeDispatch.ts`, `nudgeRunLog.ts`), so it needs a deploy. `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` says so.
- **Tests**: `nudgeDispatch.test.ts` (the `{link}` fill; a text kept back naming its blanks, one and two; the offer held with its door; the person's reason first; `{rate}` left to the mint; the words in plain words and three blanks as a list); `nudgeRunLog.test.ts` (the summary's sentence and its place); `stageSends.test.ts` unchanged and green on the shared words.
- **Guides**: *work Today's sends* → **When a text is held** names the blank and its door; *offer the app's reminders* says a line with such a blank waits on either setting. The #124 card's status, next and blocker.

## Decisions

- **Hold, do not fill.** Filling `{amount}` and `{bill}` in the run means reading each customer's newest bill the way `tellFills` does in the box; that is its own PR, and until it lands the office sends the Paid line from the thread, where the box fills both. `{link}` is filled because the run already holds it.
- **The person's reason comes first.** A missing number or a STOP is about the person and is what the office fixes on the customer; the blank is about the line and shows once the person can be texted.
- **An offer with a blank is held, not kept back**, so the office sees it on Today's sends with the door to the thread, instead of the morning quietly passing it over.
