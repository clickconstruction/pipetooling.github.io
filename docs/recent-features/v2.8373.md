# v2.8373 — On a computer, Task Mode, Dashboard and Job Mode are a list in the gear menu (2026-11-23)

Punch list [#425](../../to-dos/job-mode-offer/README.md). Client only: no migration and no function.

## The ask

The owner, 2026-11-23, looking at the mode row v2.8332 put above the header:

> At the moment on mobile we have task mode, bid mode, and job mode at the top of mobile screens. But I don't think they should exist on desktop that way. I think they should be put in the gear wheel on the top right hand side as a selector.

The row reads **Task Mode · Dashboard · Job Mode**. On a desk it always showed, as a 32 px line above the header on the Dashboard. Three selectors for the gear menu were drawn: a list with one dot, the three thirds inside the menu, and one row with a dropdown. He picked the list.

## What changed

- **The row is a phone's.** `modeRowDrawnHere(role, phone)` in `src/lib/modeRow.ts` is the one answer to *is the row this person's door here*: a phone's width (640 px, the line `ModeRow` already tucked by) and a role the row is offered to. `modeRowDraw` answers `none` past that width, for every role, in every mode. On a phone nothing changed: the thirds, the tuck, the handle, the pull and the two guards are as v2.8332 built them.
- **The list in the gear menu.** `GearModeList` (`src/components/layout/GearModeList.tsx`) draws **Dashboard**, **Job Mode** and **Task Mode** as rows of the menu under *Modes*, a radio group named Mode, with a blue dot on the mode the page is drawn in and that row in bold. The words are the row's own (`MODE_ROW_WORDS`). `gearModesRows` (`src/lib/gearMenuSections.ts`) says which rows: none where the row is drawn, else the three in `GEAR_MODE_LIST_ORDER`, with Job Mode left out for whoever cannot hold it.
- **One rule, two drawings.** A press in the list goes through the row's own kernel: the dot is `modeRowThirdOn`, the writes are `modeRowPressWrites` through `usePageMode`'s `set`, and the landing is `modeRowLandingPath`. So the list on a desk and the row on a phone write the same values and land on the same page. A press on the row already on only closes the menu.
- **Dispatch Mode** keeps its own tick row under the list, with its PO tab. **Dashboard** never writes Dispatch Mode, so a leader or the office keeps the bar, and the dot reads Dashboard for both, as the row's middle third does.
- **The estimator** has no row at any width, so the estimator's two tick rows became the same list at every width.
- **The ways back that stay in sight.** With no row on a desk, the green *Task Mode · Exit* strip is drawn while Task Mode is on, and **Show full dashboard** stands at the foot of the Job Mode page. Both read `modeRowDrawnHere`, where v2.8332 drew them for the estimator alone.
- **The phone dock's More sheet** still draws its own Job Mode and Task Mode switches off `gearModesRows`' `job` and `task`. They show wherever that sheet is drawn and the row is not: a window past 640 px that is too narrow for the header's links.

## Usage rows

`ui_nav_clicks`: control `gear_mode` with target `#full`, `#job` or `#task` for a press in the list. Job Mode switched on from the list records `job_mode_enabled` with `#gear`, as the old tick did.

## Defaults the owner did not rule on

Four calls were built on a default. Each is small to turn.

1. **A tablet** is wider than 640 px, so it has the list and not the row. The line is `modeRowDrawnHere`.
2. **Dispatch Mode stays apart from the list.** As a fourth choice, **Dashboard** would have to switch Dispatch Mode off, and then Dashboard would mean one thing on a phone and another on a desk.
3. **A press lands on the Dashboard.** Dashboard or Job Mode pressed from another page opens the Dashboard, as the row does. The old ticks stayed on the page. An estimator who leaves Task Mode from the list lands on the Dashboard, where the old tick stayed on the checklist. The green strip's **Exit** still stays on the checklist.
4. **The green *New* mark** on Job Mode is not drawn in the list.

## Left as it was

The window that shows another person's Task Mode (`TaskModeLookWindow`, Checklist → Review) still draws the three thirds at its top for a role that has the row. It cannot know which device that person is on. The 32 px height the thirds take past 640 px stays in `index.css` for that window.

## Tests

`modeRow.test.ts`: a desk draws nothing for all nine roles, in every mode, on both of the row's pages, whatever the phone remembers, and `modeRowDrawnHere` on a phone is the role rule. `gearMenuSections.test.ts`: the Modes rows for all nine roles at a phone's width and a desk's, the estimator's list on a phone, Job Mode left out for whoever cannot hold it, the order and each row's tip. `GearModeList.render.test.tsx` (5): the group and its words, the one mark, the tips, what a press says, and nothing drawn for no rows. `ModeRow.render.test.tsx` now draws on a phone, and its desk case holds that nothing is drawn and no open is counted. `Layout.gearSwitches.render.test.tsx`: a leader on a phone has neither row and no list, a leader on a desk has the list over Dispatch Mode, and a press on Job Mode then Dashboard stores each and never writes Dispatch Mode.

## Looked at

Walked on the dev server through the lens frame, over invented data.

- **A leader at 1280 px and 800 px:** no row above the header. The gear menu's *Modes* group read Dashboard with the dot, Job Mode, Task Mode, then Dispatch Mode's tick. Job Mode, Task Mode and Dashboard were pressed in turn, twice round, and from Task Mode straight to Job Mode. Each press closed the menu, moved the dot and landed on the page it named.
- **Job Mode on a desk:** the Job Mode card, and **Show full dashboard** at the foot of the page.
- **Task Mode on a desk:** the checklist with the green *Task Mode · Exit* strip, and a gear menu of Modes, Show and the leaving band.
- **Dispatch Mode:** ticked on, then Task Mode and back to Dashboard. The tick was still on, with the dot on Dashboard and the PO tab under it.
- **A leader on a phone at 390 px:** the row as before, with Job Mode marked New. The gear menu listed Dispatch Mode alone.
- **An estimator at 390 px and 800 px:** no row, the list in the gear menu at both widths, Task Mode with the strip, and Dashboard back to the Dashboard.
- **A sub at 800 px in the dark look:** the dot on Job Mode, which is a sub's first screen, no Dispatch Mode row, and **Show full dashboard** at the foot.

It was not tried on a real tablet or a real phone. A ticked **Dispatch Mode** wraps to two lines in the menu at its narrowest, as it did before this change.
