# v2.4769 — Map: draw the justice precincts (2026-10-07)

Client only; the table is [v2.4768](v2.4768.md)'s migration (push before this is useful). Step 3 of 5 of the owner's justice-court plan (2026-10-06). The owner: *right now you can look on a map and see all of our jobs … draw our own boundary layers so that if addresses fall within them we know what precinct and what county they should be in.*

## What changed

- **A Court areas toggle** on the Map page's toolbar, for the office cohort (dev, master, assistant-like), `data-court-areas-toggle`. On, the polygon tool draws an area instead of a filter (`GeomanDraw` takes `paused`), and [`CourtAreasLayer`](../../src/components/map/CourtAreasLayer.tsx) draws every active area as a tinted polygon with its name on hover, a colour per county, imported ones solid and drawn ones dashed. The drawn shape stays until it is saved or discarded.
- **[`CourtAreasPanel`](../../src/components/map/CourtAreasPanel.tsx)** under the map: the coverage line (*40 of 46 addresses inside a drawn area · 6 outside every area · 2 on a line to settle*, from every pin with a point against the active areas — `courtCoverage`), the name box for the shape just drawn (county, the precinct as the county writes it, a label, where it was drawn from; *Save the area* / *Discard the shape*), the areas by county with *drawn by the office · 2026-10-07* or *imported · …*, a click to fly to one, *Rename*, and *Remove* after a confirm. Remove retires the row (`active = false`) rather than deleting it, so a classification that read it stays explainable.
- **[`courtAreasDraft.ts`](../../src/lib/legal/courtAreasDraft.ts)**: `polygonFromDrawn` (a Feature or geometry, Polygon or MultiPolygon, else null), `courtAreaDraftProblem` (county and precinct required; a precinct drawn twice is refused), `courtCoverage`, `courtAreaSourceWords`, `courtAreasByCounty` (precincts in number order). **[`courtAreasIo.ts`](../../src/lib/legal/courtAreasIo.ts)**: list, insert, update, retire; `court_areas` is cast until the types regenerate.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **Draw, then name.** The shape comes first because the hand is on the map; the words come after, in a box that will not save without a county and a precinct.
- **The coverage line is the to-do list.** It says when the one-time job is done and which pins are still outside.
- **Not done: moving a vertex.** Changing a shape is remove and redraw. Vertex editing with the plugin is the next cut if redrawing proves slow.
