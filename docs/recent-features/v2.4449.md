# v2.4449 — Submittals: the procurement log keeps the rows approved on an earlier revision (2026-10-02)

A pre-existing gap noticed while building the order-only train ([v2.4436](v2.4436.md), *Next*). No migration; one edge-function line (`get-submittal-room`, redeployed after the merge).

## The gap

`newRevision(onlySentBack = true)` builds the next draft from the rows the reviewer marked Revise or Reject: *The rest stand as approved on Rev N.* The procurement log, though, read the newest revision's rows only (`setProcItems` from `items` when the newest revision is selected), and `buildProcurementLog` draws a `bid_procurement_items` record only when a current row's tag or part key matches it. So after a resubmit from the rows sent back, the approved rows of Rev N were off the log entirely — released, still to order, and any order date or PO typed on their lines invisible. The GC's room had the same gap on its Procurement card (`rev.rows` of the current revision), and the edge function hid the card altogether when no record was typed yet, because its gate read the newest revision's approvals only.

## What changed

- **The kernel** ([`standingRows.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/standingRows.ts)): `rowsThatStand(revisions, approved)` over the revisions newest first. The newest revision that holds a tag speaks for it: a tag on the newest is the newest's; otherwise the newest earlier revision that holds it, and the row stands only when it was approved there — whole, or part by part: a part the GC approved is released whatever the rest of its row says. A row never called, or sent back and dropped, was not released and does not stand; a later revision asking about a tag again outranks an older approval. A revision the GC never read (a superseded draft, `revisionWasRead`) asked about nothing, so its rows with no call claim no tag, and an older approval of the same tag still stands; a call entered on it by hand is a real approval, and that row stands.
- **The tab** reads the earlier shared revisions' rows whenever the newest revision's rows reload (`standing` state: items, their parts, and which revision each stands on), hands them to `procurementItemsFrom` as shared rows, and marks each line `standsOnRev`. `partsOf` holds their parts too, so a tap on the line opens the row's Edit window as on any other line; `canEditProduct` is off for a standing row, since the GC approved that product. The confirm message of *Rev N+1 from the rows sent back* now ends *and stay on the procurement log*.
- **The log** ([`SubmittalProcurementPanel`](../../src/components/bids/SubmittalProcurementPanel.tsx)): a standing line says *approved on Rev N* under its product. Everything else reads as before — released, ordered, delivered, its float — and the GC's copies, the update and the snapshot carry it as any released line.
- **The room** ([`SubmittalRoom.tsx`](../../src/pages/SubmittalRoom.tsx)): the Procurement card takes the current revision and the shared ones before it, and lists the standing rows with the same kernel. `get-submittal-room` sends the card when any shared revision has an approval, not only the newest.

## Decided here

- **The log reads across revisions; the resubmit does not carry the approved rows.** The other fix was to carry them onto the new draft as carried rows with their calls kept, as order-only rows already carry. That would put them back on the GC's page, in the package and in every count, and change what *Rev N+1 from the rows sent back* means. An approved row stands on its revision; the log is what was wrong.
- **Approved only.** A row the GC never called on Rev N is not released, so nothing is lost by leaving it off; a row sent back and not carried was the office's choice to drop.

## Not done here

- **A row with no call that a resubmit from the rows sent back leaves behind** is neither released nor asked again, so it is not on the log — the orders typed on such a line stay hidden, as before. Rare on a reviewed revision (the GC calls every row), but a *New revision* carry is the safer door when some rows are still open.
- The log's *released* date reads the UTC day of the call (`decision.at.slice(0, 10)`): a call recorded after 7 pm Central reads tomorrow. Noted by the other Submittals session on the same walk; untouched here.
