# v2.3831 — Bank payments: a note stays with the deposit you wrote it on (2026-09-25)

Found by the 2026-09-25 map sweep ([`AR_PAYMENT_MODALS_ARCHITECTURE.md`](../AR_PAYMENT_MODALS_ARCHITECTURE.md), B10 and the reset-timing note). Money path; client only, no migration.

## The bug

[`BankPaymentsModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/BankPaymentsModal.tsx) keeps one `internalNote` for the window. The `[open, selectedId]` reset effect cleared the allocation lines, the note fold (`noteOpen`), the apply error, the Stripe out-of-band state and the link-steer dismissals when the deposit changed — but never the note. A note typed for deposit A therefore stayed in the box (the textarea shows while the note is non-empty) and went out with deposit B's apply: as `p_note` to `apply_mercury_bank_payment_allocations`, and in the `internal_note` of `record-stripe-invoice-out-of-band-payment` when B closed a Stripe-hosted bill. Apply & next hit it without the user touching the list.

## The change

- The reset effect also runs `setInternalNote('')`, so a new deposit (by click, by Apply & next, or on reopening the window) starts with an empty note. The Stripe-close retry panel stays on the same deposit and keeps its note, as before.

## Verified

- New render smoke `BankPaymentsModal.notePerDeposit.render.test.tsx`: two deposits, a note typed under the first, the second picked → the note box is gone and *Add a note* is back. Fails without the fix. The five other `BankPaymentsModal*` smokes still pass.
