  - `ScopeItem` gains `sheets` and `specs`. `PlanSet` gains `changedSheets`, `changedSpecs` and `addedLines`. Each field is word for word.

  - `GcProject` gains `index`, main's only: the plans' index and manual as they stand after the newest set. It is never `sheets`, which on the prototype is the first set's index.
