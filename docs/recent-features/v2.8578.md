# v2.8578 — Paint a map on an iPhone: the start sheet ends above Safari's bar (2026-11-25)

Punch list #392, from the walk in an iPhone simulator on 2026-11-25, in Safari at 402 by 874 points. No migration, no edge function.

**Seen.** On the Dashboard's **Paint a map** press, the start sheet's foot was behind Safari's bar. On a plain job **Start with a place** was cut, with only its top edge showing at about 765 points. On a job in a development, which adds a row of two chips, **Draw it by hand** sat at the fold and **Start with a place** was out of sight. Whether the sheet scrolled was not tried.

**The cause.** `paintedSheetFrame` (`src/components/paintedMap/paintedSheetFrame.ts`) pinned a phone's scrim to the whole page box, `position: fixed` with `inset: 0`, and let the sheet be as tall as that box. On an iPhone the page box runs under Safari's bottom bar. A sheet shorter than the box has nothing to scroll, so its foot just sat behind the bar. The plan's line that both presses are above the fold at 812 was measured in a desk browser, where the page box and what shows are the same.

**The mend.** On a phone the scrim is the part of the page that shows: it starts where `window.visualViewport` starts and is as tall (`usePaintedSheetBand`, over `visibleBand(useVisualViewportBox())`). The sheet is at most as tall as its scrim and scrolls inside it, as before. So the sheet's foot stands on the top of Safari's bar, and a sheet too tall for the band scrolls to its last press. `PhoneSheet` has sized its backdrop this way since punch list #348.

- **Paint a map** and **Which job is it for?** share the frame, so both mend.
- The band also ends at the top of the keyboard, which the picker's search brings up, and follows the page when an iPhone slides it under the field.
- At a desk, and on a browser that cannot say what shows, the scrim keeps the page box.

## What did not change

- The sheet's content and its height. On the simulator's screen a plain job's sheet is about as tall as the band, so **Start with a place** may still need a short scroll. Making the sheet shorter is a change to the screen the owner approved, and is not in this fix.
- The desk's window.
