# v2.6558 — The Scoreboard's cuts and window: by part of the site, by person, this month or 90 days (punch list #250, PR 4, closes the card) (2026-11-05)

Punch list #250's fourth and last PR, after the board by job (v2.6407), the rows under each job (v2.6457) and the headline (v2.6467). The owner's design of 2026-11-02 named two more cuts on the same grid and a window on it; he took the window's default, all time, as call 5. This PR is those chips.

## What changed

- **The chips** (`CutChips` in `DevelopmentScoreboard.tsx`, over `src/lib/developments/scoreboardCuts.ts`): two rows above the board, *Cut by* job · part of the site · person and *Window* all time · this month · 90 days, in the Money card's view chips' shape; the window's two days read beside its chips (*Nov 1 – Nov 5*) with what the window does on the title. The device remembers both per development (`readScoreboardCut` / `writeScoreboardCut`, `readScoreboardWindow` / `writeScoreboardWindow` in `devicePrefs.ts`; job and all time leave no key).
- **The cut by part of the site** (`scoreboardPartGroups`): the Plans look's parts as rows in their own order, each folded to its three and opening to its nine and its jobs; a part with no job with money on it is left out, the jobs on no part come last as *No part*. A part's days are counted once across its jobs, from the day keys each row now carries (`ScoreboardJob.dayKeys`, the clock's days). The site's fold and foot stay.
- **The cut by person** (`scoreboardPeopleCut`): the grid turned over. Each person who clocked on the site is a row with their hours, days and labor across the jobs and their share of each job's earned dollars by hours, marked ≈ with its title; opened, their jobs with the Lead or Crew tag, each line their measures on that job. Subs, parts and overhead cells stay blank and a line under the board says why. The viewer's row is gold with *You*, as the member rows are. The leader's board carries no dollar of labor. The rows follow the tile sort where a person fills that column, else earned biggest first.
- **The window** (`buildScoreboard` takes `window` and `chargeEvents`): hours, days and labor from each person's days inside it (`byWorkDate`); subs and parts from the dated charge events the Timeline builds over the same cost rows (`siteChargeEventsFromCostRows`, fuel left as a card charge, so no fuel read); overhead from the day ledger's lines inside it; earned by the hours in the window over the job's expected hours, Job Summary's own rule (`earnedRevenueInWindow`), so a paid job earns its contract across its lifetime hours. The site's days are the jobs' days inside the window, each once. An undated charge is in no window. The page builds the events only while a window is on, and keeps the whole-life board for the cost summary print.
- **Where the window is not offered**: the man-hours fallback (a leader the clock is closed to) has no days, so its board shows no window chips and stays whole-life.
- **Guide**: *Read the scoreboard* gains the cuts and the window. The card's folder is deleted with its Closed line at the top of the Closed block; the Glossary's Development page entry and ACCESS_CONTROL's Scoreboard line are amended.

## Decisions

- **The parts keep the Plans look's order, not the sort's.** A press on a tile sorts the jobs under each part; the parts themselves read in the order the office gave them, so the board by part reads like the Plans look.
- **The person cut's earned is a share, and says so.** Earned per person is their hours' share of the job's earned dollars, as Crew P&L splits it; it wears ≈ on every cell and the title names the rule.
- **The print stays whole-life.** The cost summary's heading says the site's life, so a window on the board does not change the paper.
- **A window with no clock is no window.** Subs and parts could be cut by date without the clock, but earned could not, and a board half-cut would mislead; the chips are offered only when the clock's days are in.
