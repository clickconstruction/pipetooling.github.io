## What changed

- **Both boxes are folded to one line until pressed.** `LienBoardFoldBox` wraps `LienPublicBinView` and `LienOffPathBinView`. The line is the box's name, how many jobs it holds and what they owe (`foldWords`, `lienBoxFoldWords`), and **Show ▾**. The whole line is the press, with `aria-expanded`.
- **Open, the box is what it was**, with **Hide ▴** at the end of its head line. A second press folds it.
- **The folded line keeps what needs a person.** A public job whose bond nobody knows wears *get the bond* on its row, and the fold would hide it. So the folded line counts those rows in amber, *1 to get the bond* (`needs`, `lienPublicNeedsWords`). This is the card's own recommendation, not something the owner asked for.
- **Closed for each visit.** The state is the component's own and is not kept: the box is reference, and its line says when something in it waits.
- **A phone** gets the same fold inside **Show the bins**, the line 44 px tall.
