# v2.8230 — Task Mode on a phone: the small presses are a thumb tall (punch list #369, the walk of 2026-11-21) (2026-11-21)

## How

None of these presses has a phone flag to read. A task card's pill is one fixed style object (`TASK_PILL_STYLE`), and the strip, the fold and the bar draw from inline styles with no knowledge of the screen. A hook in each would be five hooks for one fact, so the screen says it instead:

- **`index.css`** sets `--phone-press-min: 44px` at `max-width: 640px`, the checklist's own phone width, and leaves it unset above.
- **[`src/lib/phonePressFloor.ts`](../../src/lib/phonePressFloor.ts)** writes the two values a style takes. `phoneTall(desk)` is a `min-height` of `desk` px at a desk and 44 on a phone. `phoneGiveBack(desk)` is the margin over and under a press that sits in a line: nothing at a desk, and on a phone half of what the press grew by, taken back, so the line stays as tall as it was drawn.
- **The pill** takes `phoneTall(32)`, so every user of `TASK_PILL_STYLE` follows: the card's four, the page's extras beside them, and the choice pills inside the *Can't do it*, no-longer-applies and *Hand it on* sheets.
- **Exit** is a press with no look of its own around the pill it was, as the Lien desk's pile presses are in v2.8221. A bordered pill grown to 44 px would hang out of its 38 px strip.
- **Go through them** and **Fold ▴** take `phoneTall` with `phoneGiveBack`. *Fold ▴* takes a 44 px width too, its words kept to the right edge. The same fold stands on the Pipeline's Tagged notes, which gains it.
