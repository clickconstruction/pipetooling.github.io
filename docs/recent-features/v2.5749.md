# v2.5749 — Jobs: the Costs tab prints as a packet (2026-10-22)

The owner, with a screenshot of the Costs tab on job 891: "I would like to be able to export these details into a neat document … a very neat packet that can be printed on command … It will be printed on 8.5 x 11 paper." Punch list #143 drew the packet from the job's live tab the same afternoon (PR #6011); the owner said build it all, and this PR is the one the to-do planned.

- **The builder** `src/lib/jobsDocuments/jobCostsPacket.ts`: `buildJobCostsPacketHtml(input)`, pure, beside the Job Summary cost-breakdown print. Four Letter pages, light theme, `@page` margin boxes for the running footer and *Page N of M*. Page 1 at a glance: the identity block (company, job name, the window's number pill, address, customer, GC, status, the day it opened, the last field day), the printed stamp (when, by whom), the price and the % done with its source, the margin band and its sentence (true or direct, green or red, *too early* when the kernel says so, the priced line when the bid carries one), the three tiles, **Cost against value earned** as inline SVG (`jobCostsPacketChartSvg`: the price line, the bid figure when one stands for the whole job, the cost line, the value-earned steps, the forecast dash, the two end labels placed so they never collide) and **How the margin builds, by section** with Direct cost, Overhead share, True cost, Price and the margin row. Page 2: the baseline strip (the bid's hours or *what it has taken so far*, and the kept line), **Where the money went** from the tab's own `MoneyRow[]` with the share bars, team labor by person (`jobCostsPacketTeamByPerson`: field days, hours, cost, most cost first), the sub sheets (`jobCostsPacketSubSheets`) and the supply house invoices. Page 3: the card charges by cost-line tag (⛽ Fuel & gas and any other tag the office shows as a cost line), the other card charges, the lines the one card rule left out with their reason, parts from tally, other job charges, the **Pace** rows (burn rate, work left, field days, overhead landed, cash position) and a **Notes** box. Page 4: the 14-day daily bars when they had spend (`jobCostsPacketDailySvg`) and **What happened, by day** (`jobCostsPacketByDay`: one row per dated day with a column per source, the day's total, the cost to date at the end of the day and the payment or % event; the undated charges last, as one row).
- **The events carry who and how many hours** (`JobChargeEvent.who`, `.hours`, set by `buildJobChargeEvents` on team and sub events): the per-person and per-sheet tables group on them instead of parsing a label. Additive; nothing downstream changes.
- **The button**: `JobCostsVerdict` takes `onPrint` and draws **Print** at the right end of its first line; `JobCostsTabCharts` builds the packet from the load it already runs (the inputs, the overhead, the burn model and the verdict it now keeps together, the kept baseline, the resolved budget) plus the lines the Edit Job window hands down as `packet` (`JobCostsPacketLines`: the supply, card and tally lines with the card rule's exclusions and tags, the team labor row, the sub labor data, the other charges), through `JobFormLaborCostPanel` from `JobFormModal`. The card nicknames come from `useMercuryLedgerNicknames`, enabled only when there is something to print. `openHtmlPrintWindow` opens it; a blocked popup toasts *Allow pop-ups to print it.* The button follows the verdict's gate (`showJobCostBreakdownTeamLabor`: dev, master, controller), since without team labor the packet would lie about labor. `baselineKeptWords` moved from the verdict component into `jobCostsVerdict.ts` so both read it.
- **Checks**: `jobCostsPacket.test.ts` builds the fixture through the tab's own path (events → `buildJobBurn` → `buildCostsVerdict`, `buildMoneyRows`) and holds the four sheets, the stamp, the hero in green, red, direct-only and *too early*, the by-section rows, the money rows in the kernel's order with their shares and total, the per-person and per-sheet tables and the label fallback, the card tables by tag and the excluded lines, the by-day rows with the running cost, the payment and % notes and the undated row, the chart's paths and labels, the daily bars' rule, the pace rows, both baseline strips, escaping, and every fixed sentence against `plainWordsFailures`. `JobFormLaborCostPanel.render.test.tsx` presses the button with `window.open` stubbed and reads the packet back, and shows it absent without the lines. The sent-copies ratchet lists the tab under `NOT_A_SEND` as our own cost report, since it holds wages and margin.
- **Docs**: the guides *move between a job's Job, Edit, Bill, and Costs tabs* and *read the cost and value timeline on a job* name the button; `JOB_FORM_MODAL_ARCHITECTURE.md` and `JOBS_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md` name the builder; to-do #143 is closed (folder deleted, the line in `to-dos/README.md`).
