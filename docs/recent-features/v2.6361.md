# v2.6361 — History on the Development page: a ? that says what the marks mean (2026-11-02)

The owner, 2026-11-02, pointing at the glyph column of a development's History: *what do these icons mean?* Nothing on the page said, beyond the hover title on each badge, which a phone never shows. A ? on the card's header now opens the legend.

## What changed

- **The kernel** `src/lib/developments/historyLegend.ts`: `historyMark(kind, source)` gives every `ChangeKind` its glyph and its family (`MarkTone`: grey for words a person wrote, blue for a move on a record, green for money and a yes, red for a no, amber for what went out or came in; a kept entry wears its source's glyph, grey for a note and amber otherwise); `HISTORY_LEGEND`, fifteen rows, each a mark, a name, one line on what the line records and the chip that narrows to it; `legendByChip()` groups them in the chips' own order. `historyLegend.test.ts` holds that every kind and every source has a mark, every row sits under the chip that matches its line (`lineMatchesChip`), the groups run as the chips do, and the words are plain.
- **The feed reads the same table**: `DevelopmentFeed`'s badge now takes its glyph and tone from `historyMark`, so the list and the legend cannot drift; the colours it drew are unchanged.
- **The window** `HistoryLegendWindow.tsx` (a `ResponsiveModalShell` at 1150, the phone's bottom sheet): the intro line, then each chip's rows with the badge, the name and the words, and Close. `HistoryLegendPress` is the round **?** on the History card's header, before the search box, labelled *What the marks mean*. `HistoryLegendWindow.render.test.tsx` opens it, counts the rows, reads a group and closes it.
- **The guide** *see everything on a development in one place* → Follow the site in History says where the ? is and what the colours mean; `docs/twins/APP_DIRECTORY.md` and the Glossary's Development page entry name the press.

## Decisions

- **One table for the badge and the legend.** The badge's switch lived in the component; the legend would have been a second copy of it. The kernel owns the marks and the component maps a tone to the theme's tokens.
- **A window, not a tooltip row.** The owner asked for a modal from a ? icon; a legend strip under the chips would cost every reader a line on every open, and the phone has no hover.

## Deploy

Nothing: no migration, no function.
