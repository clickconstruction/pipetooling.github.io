# v2.7637 — Prospects: the New Prospect and Edit Prospect windows are their own pieces (2026-11-17)

Step 12 of the order in `docs/PROSPECTS_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md`, the second part of the last cut. A pure move: no behaviour changes.

**Edit Prospect.**

- `useEditProspectWindow` holds the window's nine pieces of state (the open flag, the row it edits, the person and company blocks, the four kind boxes, what it opened with), its leave guard, and its writes: Save, Delete with the deck's re-point, and the two warmth presses. The page hands it the caches' setters, the pointer's, the card clock's reset and its one `saving` flag.
- `EditProspectWindow` draws it. Its JSX is the page's block, unchanged.
- The window keeps its two identities (the map's quirk 14): with no row set it edits the deck's card, with one set it edits that row.

**New Prospect.**

- `useNewProspectWindow` holds the window's eight boxes, its leave guard, the double lookup's answer (`companyDouble` over the doubles the lane hooks read), the two ways it opens (the lane's kind, or a sub with a trade filled in) and its save.
- `NewProspectWindow` draws it, with the three kind cards and the line that says where a company already lives.
- The open flag and the picked kind stay in the page: the bench read and the doubles read are keyed on them. The Dashboard's `?newProspect=true` effect and `openDoubleProspect` stay too, since both write the page's tab state.
- After a save on the lane the window reloads the deck, then the list, through one callback (`reloadLane`), in the order the page did.

**The kind's boxes.** `renderKindFields` moved whole to `components/prospects/ProspectKindFields.tsx`. It stays a function the two windows call, so each window's column of boxes is drawn as one list, as it was.

**Size.** `Prospects.tsx` went from 2,722 lines to 2,223: seventeen pieces of state and thirteen functions fewer.

**Proof.** The render smoke grew from 109 cases to 114: New Prospect closing on Escape while untouched and asking once something is typed; New Prospect with nobody named writing nothing; New Prospect picked as another kind saving that kind with its trades and moving to its lane; Edit prospect warming the card and then deleting it behind its question, with the next card up; Edit prospect asking before it throws away a change. All 114 pass on the page before the move and after it.

**Found on the way, not changed.** The deck loads once on any entry to the page, whatever tab the address names: the page's tab state starts as Follow Up, and the deck's loader runs before the address is read. So a link to the Prospect List with a prospect also puts that prospect up on the deck. The map records it as quirk 37.
