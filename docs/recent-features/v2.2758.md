# v2.2758 — Settings → What customers see: every customer surface, live, in the order they meet it (2026-09-04)

**Why.** The surfaces a customer, a GC or a sub sees were previewed in four different places or not at all, and the plain-text estimate email of v2.2747 sat unnoticed for months because nobody looked from the customer's side. Owner picked direction **B — Journeys** from the mockups (artifact a792b62d): one strip per audience, each step a live frame, tap to open it large.

**What (PR 1 of 2).**
- **Settings → What customers see** (dev-only group `settings-what-customers-see`, Company zone; search entry "What customers see"). A toolbar with Phone / Desktop, the sample names, and Refresh all. Three strips — Homeowner, General contractor, Subcontractor — with steps in the order they happen; each step names what sends it, when, and which Settings it reflects. Tap a step to open it large at phone width (390 px) or full width, with **Open in new tab** for pages and the plain-text part for emails.
- **Rendered live in this PR:** the estimate email, the accept page, the thank-you page, the bid-room email, the bid room, the revised-send email, and the signed room with a change order. Thumbnails are the same frames scaled to 40 %, inert. The bill email is named as **sent by Stripe** (not built by the app). The two portals and the contract page show *Next release* cards — PR 2.
- **Sample mode on the public pages.** `/estimate/accept?t=sample` (`sample-done` = the thank-you) and `/bid-room?t=sample` (`sample-done` = signed, change order accepted). The page sends the signed-in user's JWT instead of the anon key; the edge function checks the caller is an office role (`dev`, `master_technician`, `assistant`, `controller`, `estimator`), then returns the fixture laid over the **live** Settings — estimate copy defaults, public terms and footer; bid cover-letter terms and exclusions defaults; brand. No row is read or written, no view is logged, and the pages short-circuit every write (accept, sign, decline, option-viewed) — a walkthrough that saves nothing, under an orange *Sample* banner.
- **Sample emails** are built in the browser by the same builders the senders run (`estimateEmailLetterhead`, `bidRoomLinkEmail`) over the same fixture and the same Settings rows, with the viewer as sender.

**How.** Dependency-free fixture `supabase/functions/_shared/customerSample.ts` (Sam Sample · 100 Sample St; Sample Contracting · Cedar Bend Apartments, two options; Sam's Plumbing LLC) re-exported by `src/lib/customerSample.ts`; response builders `_shared/customerSampleFixtures.ts`; the staff gate `_shared/sampleViewer.ts`; client `src/lib/customerSampleMode.ts` (`publicFunctionHeaders`), `customerJourneys.ts` (the strips, tested), `customerSampleEmails.ts` (tested); `SettingsWhatCustomersSeeTab.tsx`; `SampleModeBanner.tsx`. `get-estimate-for-customer` and `get-bid-proposal-room` gained the sample branch (deployed).

**Not in this cut.** Customer portal, GC portal view, sub portal, contract signing page (PR 2); a Reply-To/office-inbox setting; a way to pick the electrical brand for the sample.
