# v2.4637 — Legal: only approved clock sessions count as evidence (2026-10-05)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Item 25 of punch list #85 ([`to-dos/legal-portal-firm-ready/`](../../to-dos/legal-portal-firm-ready/README.md)), stacked on item 6 (v2.4633). Mockup: `mockup-25-one-evidence-rule.html` beside the card.

## The defect

The packet's Evidence (`buildLegalPacket` in `legalPacket.ts`) counted every clock session that was not rejected or revoked, approved or not, and its last work day dated the lien clock. The Chapter 53 timeline's work months (`workMonthsFromSessions` in `legalLienPaper.ts`) counted approved sessions only. So a job whose sessions were all unapproved showed hours, days worked and an open notice window on Evidence, and *no work day on record* on Paper; a job with one late unapproved day had its lien dates run from a day its own timeline did not count.

## What changed

- **One rule** in the packet: an approved session that was not rejected or revoked is evidence. `LegalEvidenceJob.sessions` counts those; hours, first and last work day, the GPS count, the sworn-account check, the lien clock and the exhibit count read them. The job's own `last_work_date` stays the fallback when none is approved, as in the timeline.
- `LegalEvidenceJob.approvedSessions` is replaced by `awaitingApproval`: live sessions nobody has approved, said and never counted.
- **One wording**, `legalSessionWords(e)`: *2 approved (2 with GPS) · 1 awaiting approval*, used by the desk, the firm's Evidence tab and both prints. The notes under the tables say only approved sessions count.
- The desk's *No field evidence* gap says when sessions are waiting: *approve them in Hours*.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **Count unapproved sessions and let the timeline catch up instead?** No. An approved session is one a person checked. Lien dates a firm files on must rest on records the office stands behind, and the timeline, the lien desk and the § 53.056 forecast already use approved only.
- **Hide unapproved sessions from the firm entirely?** No. *Awaiting approval* tells the firm more evidence is coming and tells the office what to do. The count carries no hours or dates.
- **And the job's last work date when nothing is approved?** It stays the fallback, the same one the timeline uses, so the two still agree.

## Tests

`legalEvidence.test.ts` (1): two jobs, one with approved, unapproved and rejected sessions and one with only unapproved; the evidence counts, hours and days, the session words, the lien clock's last day and status, the timeline's work months, the sworn-account check and the desk's gap all agree.
