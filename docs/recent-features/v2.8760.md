# v2.8760 — A task window on a phone marks itself, so the new version pill leaves its send bar (2026-11-27)

Punch list #410, retired. Finding 348 of the fixing thread's ledger, from the simulator thread's sitting 6 of 2026-11-27 (SE, 375 by 667, v2.8752, seen twice). Client only. No migration, no edge function. Nothing owed after the merge.

**As seen.** The app's bar *A new version is ready.* came up twice over the New task window's send bar and covered **Tell me** and the send press. **Not now** put it away and kept the window.

**Read.** The pill is pinned to the bottom of the screen and moves to the top while a phone sheet is open (`src/lib/phoneSheetOpen.ts`, v2.8507). `PhoneSheet` marks that store for as long as it is drawn. The task window is not a `PhoneSheet`: `TaskWindowShell.tsx` draws its own card with its own bar at the foot, and it never marked the store. So the pill stayed at the bottom, over the bar.

**The mend.** One effect in `TaskWindowShell.tsx`: on a phone the window marks the store while it is drawn, as `PhoneSheet` does. All three task windows draw through it: New task, the task's edit window and the roadmap's add window.

**What it costs.** A sheet rises from the bottom and leaves the top of the screen free. A task window docks to the top, so the top is not free: while the pill waits it stands over the window's title row, with its **×**. The task, the sentence and **Send** stay clear, and **Not now** puts the pill away. That is the better side for it to stand on, since the press at the foot is the one the window is for.

**Reload over a typed task.** The brief feared **Reload** there would throw the typed task away. Since v2.8499 the pill's **Reload** warns first on a page that holds typed words, walks each place and asks again. That is unchanged.

**Other windows that keep their last press at the foot of a phone and are not a `PhoneSheet`.** Two were met in this run and are not changed here: the On site tab's sheet (`OnSiteSheet.tsx`), whose blue Add press stands in its foot, and the Messages thread window, whose reply box and send press are at its foot. Each would need the same one line. The list is not a full sweep.

**Tests.** `TaskWindowShell.render.test.tsx`: on a phone the store reads open while the window is drawn and shut after it closes, and the pill's place is the top; at a desk nothing is marked; a window that turns from a desk to a phone marks it then, and two windows count as open until both close.

**To see it live.** It needs a deploy to land while a task window is open on a phone, which a walk cannot stage. When it happens: the bar stands at the top of the screen, and **Tell me** and the send press are clear.
