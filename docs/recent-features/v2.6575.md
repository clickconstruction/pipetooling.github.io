# v2.6575 — The sign-in title and its line sit together on one plate (2026-11-06)

Grace, 2026-11-06, on the sign-in hero as v2.6503 left it: *"I don't love how this looks. Could you help me come up with a better way to present the title and subtitle?"* Three treatments were drawn beside the live page with the real pipe wordmark and backdrop at 1,100 and 375 (artifact `2gwzZjfe3B3hv2uHzDRpZc`): A, one lockup with a red rule and a quiet caption; B, the same lockup on a glass plate; C, title and tagline side by side. She picked **B**, and changed the promise's last word: *"I also don't like the word paid. Maybe we should use the word moving."*

## What was wrong

The pipe title (v2.3583) ran the full width like a banner, then a second voice arrived: a Playfair line in two weights floating on the photo. The two faces competed, the backdrop's yellow pipe and hands ran under the words, and nothing held the title and its line together as one thing.

## What changed

- **`AuthPublicLandingLayout`**: when a page passes `heroTagline`, the `.letter-header` also carries `letter-header--plate`. The estimate, contract and bid-room pages pass nothing and keep the bare header.
- **`authPublicLanding.css`**: the plated header is a centered `fit-content` block on the trade tiles' glass (`rgba(12,16,22,0.52)`, a 22 % white hairline, `backdrop-filter: blur(10px)`), 16 px radius. Inside it the pipe mark is `min(440px, 100%)` (380 at 768, the plate's width at 480) with no drop shadow, since the plate is the ground. The tagline loses its own shadow and gains three parts.
- **`LandingTagline`**: a 44 × 3 px rule in the handwheels' red `#d21f1f` (the saturated accent kept literal since v2.3583), then *Changes are constant.* as a small spaced caption in the app's sans (0.78 rem, weight 600, 0.2 em tracking, uppercase, 80 % white), then *ClickTooling keeps you moving.* in Playfair at 1.5 rem, weight 600. Under 480 px the caption is 0.66 rem and the promise 1.12 rem.
- **`SignIn.render.test.tsx`**: the case reads the new words, the plate class on the header and the rule.

## Decisions

- **B over A**: Grace's pick; the plate gives white words a steady ground on any photo, which A left to the shadow.
- **Moving, not paid**: her word. The release note and the test carry it; the index.html description tag still reads *Jobs, bids, crews and money — one app for the office and the field.* on purpose, as the search-result line.
- **The caption keeps its period in the text** and reads as a sentence to a screen reader; visually it is a spaced caption.
- **Only the sign-in page is plated.** The plate hangs on the tagline slot, so the customer pages look as before.
- Named for the owner, not changed here: the title says ClickPlumbing.com and links to the company site while the promise says ClickTooling. Leave it, say *Click keeps you moving*, or draw *ClickTooling* as the pipe word on this page alone.
