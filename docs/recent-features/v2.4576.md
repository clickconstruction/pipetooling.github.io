# v2.4576 — Submittals: an arrow shows where the next revision starts (2026-10-05)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Punch list #84, built the day it was written.

The owner drew an arrow on a screenshot of the Submittals steps, from step 7 *Resubmit* up to step 2, and asked: "if this arrow existed visually, would it be accurate? Or would it go to 6 their call?" It is accurate: *Start a Rev 2 draft…* makes Rev 2 and selects it, so step 2 reads **Rev 2**, the work resumes at step 3, and *Their call* for Rev 2 comes only after it is shared. Then: "should we add the arrow with a title like 'next revision' vertically?" — saved as #84, and then "you build it".

## What changed

- **The loop**: a dashed blue line in a new gutter left of the rail, from the middle of step 7's dot up to an arrowhead at step 2's, with **Next revision** written bottom to top beside it.
- **When**: only while Resubmit is the live step on the newest revision (`showNextRevisionLoop` in `submittalJourney.ts`) — rows were sent back and the button is the next thing to do. Starting the draft, or opening an older revision, takes it away.
- **It stretches by itself.** The road is now a CSS grid class (`.submittal-road` in `index.css`: gutter 22px · rail 34px · step) and each `RoadSection` sits in the grid row of its number. The loop is one grid item over rows 2 to 6 that runs 16px on to step 7's dot; nothing is measured, and folding a step moves both ends with the dots.
- **The label stays in view.** With step 3 open the loop on BP375 is 2,400px tall, so a label fixed at its middle would be off screen at both ends. It is sticky at the top and the bottom instead: it rests at the middle when the loop fits, and rides the edge of the screen when it does not.
- **A phone has no arrow.** At 720px and under the gutter and the loop go and the steps keep their two columns.
- **Decoration.** `aria-hidden`, no pointer events. The button's name and the line beside it say in words what it does.

On a desktop every step now sits 32px further right than before, arrow or no arrow, so the steps do not shift sideways when rows come back.

## Not built
