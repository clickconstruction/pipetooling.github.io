# v2.6990 — The dev seat reads a bid's priced total: get_bid.totals through the Pricing tab's own kernel (2026-11-12)

PR 5a of the bid train (`to-dos/mcp-servers.md` → The bid train; the pickup card is #317). Since v2.3645 `get_bid.totals` was a sentence: the priced total "is computed in the useBidPricingEngine hook, not a shared kernel, so this server does not restate it". The reading of 2026-11-11 found the hook only loads rows. The total is `computeBidPricingRows` through `scenarioPricingRows`, both pure.

## What changed

- **The kernel copy** gains, off its index: `lib/bids/scenarioPricingRows.ts` (with `submissionHides` and the engine's types), `legacyTemplatePricing.ts`, `pickDefaultPriceBookTemplateId.ts` and `pricingWriteTarget.ts`, the last for where the price pair (5b) writes.
- **`_shared/devMcpBidPricing.ts`** reads what the Pricing tab reads, as the dev under RLS: the bid's versions and price books, the shared books of its trade, the active version's count rows, the open pricing's entries, assignments, custom prices and hides, and whether the bid has a labor estimate. `openPricing` picks the pricing by the tab's own rule (`pickActiveVersion`, then `deriveActivePricingId`). `bidPricedTotal` hands the rows to `scenarioPricingRows`.
- **`get_bid.totals`** is now `{ priced_revenue_usd, words, pricing, rows, last_stamped, held }`: the grid's revenue total to the cent, the book it came from and its kind (the bid's own copy, a shared book, the robots' book), the count rows priced and not, and the margin the tab last stamped (`bids.priced_*`).

## What the total is not

- Not the Workbench strip's Revenue while someone holds an unsaved solver preview or a price mid-typing in their own browser.
- Not the strip's figure on a fixed-price row: the strip multiplies it by its count and the grid does not. Nothing on screen sets a row fixed today.
- Not cost or margin. Those read the company crew rate when the bid has no labor rate, a figure the browser computes from ninety days of paid hours.

## One pick that depends on the viewer

An unsplit bid with no pricing of its own, no saved pick and no rows on a shared book opens on the viewer's last-used book (`bid_pricing_user_prefs`), else the book named "Default". The seat reads it as nobody's and says so when that branch chose.

## Found while reading

- A custom price is ignored on a row whose fixture name a book entry carries: the kernel prefers the entry.
- The Pricing tab shows its start panel, not the grid, on a bid with no labor estimate. The reply names it.

## Tests

`src/lib/devMcp/devMcpBidPricing.test.ts`, on a made-up bid: the pick on every branch against `deriveActivePricingId`, the total against `scenarioPricingRows` (an override, a custom price, a custom price the entry beats, an unpriced row), a shared book and the viewer-dependent pick, the words where the tab shows no grid, a failed read leaving the figure out, paging past 200 rows. Every column read is held to the generated catalog.
