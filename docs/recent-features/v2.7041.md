# v2.7041 — Lien affidavit: the note on a notice's saved copy prints with the notice record (2026-11-12)

Punch list #322, the same day as v2.7021. The owner asked to see her own job's pages. Its Lien window showed a line beside the notice's saved copy with the USPS receipt and the day the mail was delivered. The notice record said only *Saved copy on file*, and the copy's delivery record did not have the line at all. Those are the certified mail details she asked the packet to carry.

## What changed

- **The notice record** (`lienNoticeRecordSheetHtml`, and each block of `lienNoticeRecordBatchHtml`): a notice whose row has a `document_note` gets one more row under its copies, *Note on the saved copy*, with the note whole. The notice's own line still says *Saved copy on file*; a note with no file behind it says *A note, no saved file* there (`LIEN_RECORD_NOTE_ONLY`) in place of repeating the note.
- **A copy of a notice** (`lienNoticeCopiesHtml`): the note is the last line of the delivery record, under the line for each envelope. The sentence above the notice no longer repeats it: a notice entered after the fact with a note and no file says so (`LIEN_RECORD_COPY_BY_HAND_NOTE`).
- No sworn word, no table, no edge function. The filed and saved copies are the paper alone, as before.

## Checked

- Unit: `lienNoticeRecordSheet.test.ts` (36), with the note escaped, in the table under the notice's last copy, once on the copy page, in a batch block, and absent when the row has none.
- The print for real, on the owner's job (read locally, every write refused): four sheets before and after, the note on the record and in the delivery record, nothing wider than the sheet, pictures loaded 3 of 3. The made-up packets of the mock-up keep their sheet counts (4 and 5).
- Not checked: a note long enough to run four lines or more. The copy page had room for about three more lines on that job; a longer note would push the end of the redrawn notice onto a second sheet.

## Limits

- The day of delivery prints because the office typed it. The record has no field for it, and a notice with no note shows none.
- The mock-up (`to-dos/lien-notice-record/mockup.html`) is redrawn with a made-up note.
