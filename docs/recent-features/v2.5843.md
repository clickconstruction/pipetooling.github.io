# v2.5843 — Needs you: your lines first, everyone else's folded (2026-10-25)

Change 3 of punch list #176 (`to-dos/quickfill-needs-you-review/`), PRs 1 and 3 of its train, after [v2.5837](v2.5837.md), [v2.5839](v2.5839.md) and [v2.5841](v2.5841.md). The owner's thesis was that everything needing attention should flow through Quickfill for assistants and Needs you for every other role. Since v2.5771 the data flowed; what broke it was one office gate for three roles (`isNeedsYouOfficeRole`): the owner's Dashboard card held 31 lines, seven of them his, and the assistant's station held his approvals and signatures among her chores, so she marked the whole station at three.

## What changed

- **An actor on every line.** [`src/lib/needsYouActor.ts`](../../src/lib/needsYouActor.ts): `NEEDS_YOU_ITEM_ACTOR` names, for each of the builder's 70 keys, who presses its button — `assistant` (contracts, job accounts, the move window, vehicles, lost bids, statements, test reports, returned checks, deposits, team purchases, GC review, the lien desk's drafting and tracking, dispatch requests, hours approvals as the preparer, Customers and Today's sends, GC projects, the portal, the law firm), `owner` (approve and sign notices, the run, the suit year, waivers, team reviews, legal review, capacity, typed hours, the three alerts), `controller` (bank labels, clauses), `estimator` (bid follow-ups, money on my bids, stale claims, the robots, Division 22, pricing, submittals), `dev` (roadmap, HR reports, the robot backlog) and `anyone` (your own card purchases). The type makes a missing key a compile error. `needsYouViewerActors(role)` says which actors a role presses for; the dev reads as the owner too, since the owner's account is a dev. `groupNeedsYouItems(items, role)` gives *yours* and the folds, office first, then the controller, the estimators, the owner, a dev; `needsYouWalkItems` walks yours then the folds. The role gate in `needsYouSources.ts` is unchanged; this is a second column.
- **The card.** [`DashboardNeedsYouCard.tsx`](../../src/components/dashboard/DashboardNeedsYouCard.tsx) renders *yours* as rows, then one dashed line per fold: the heading (*The office is on these*, *Waiting on the owner*, …), the count, the first three titles and *N more*, and **Show** / **Hide** (this visit only; each press is a `needs-you` nav click `#fold-<actor>-show`). An opened fold's rows are the same rows with the same buttons and Fill them in. The count badge is the viewer's lines; *N for others* sits beside it. Walk the list walks every line, yours first; Skip and the trail follow the same order. *Nothing waits on you.* when every line is someone else's. On both hosts, since the Quickfill station renders the same card.
- **Today's lines.** The Quickfill station keeps its id `needs-you` (its marks and history stay) and is labelled **Today's lines**; it moves to the head of `SECTIONS`, and `SECTION_INSERT_AFTER['needs-you'] = ''` puts it first for an org that saved its order before the station existed (the org did). The round and the Roller open on it.
