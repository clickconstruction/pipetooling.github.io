# v2.8476 — Takeoffs: every row press stays in reach in a narrow window (2026-11-24)

A fault on **Bids → Takeoffs**, reported by an estimator on 2026-11-24. She works with the plans in one window and the app in another beside it, each about half a screen wide. Her words: "When working in smaller width windows instead of extending to additional screen, i can not get to the delete for a row in takeoffs." No migration, no edge function. This is the first of two changes. The second folds the rail to a strip (mock-up: https://claude.ai/artifact/DayYuapLAAtVDdiUJXMv5F).

## What was wrong

The Sheet's body was one grid, `minmax(0, 1fr) 320px`: the fixtures and their part lines, and the rail headed **What Pricing sees**. The rail kept its 320 px at every width and there was no narrow layout. The line table cannot be narrower than its six columns, so under a 1,240 px window it overflowed its column. Nothing clipped it. The rail comes later in the page and is pinned (`position: sticky`), so it painted over the overflow: Unit price, Qty, Line total and the two presses at the row's end. The page had no sideways scroll at those widths, so no scroll brought them out.

One at a time has the same table beside a fixed list of fixtures (`minmax(220px, 300px) minmax(0, 1fr)`). Nothing covered its presses, but under about 1,215 px the table pushed them past the page's edge and the whole page scrolled sideways.

## Measured before

## What changed

- **`lib/bids/takeoffSheetFit.ts`** (new, pure): what fits beside what, as functions of a measured width. `railFitsBeside` is true from 1,096 px of sheet (760 for the list, 16 between, 320 for the rail), which is a window of about 1,274 px. `focusListFitsBeside` is true from 1,114 px. `takeoffLinesFit` says how the table draws in its box: `roomy` from 760 px, `snug` from 620, `scroll` under that. An unmeasured box reads as roomy, the look the screen had.
- **The Sheet** (`TakeoffCostRailView`) measures itself with `useMeasuredWidth`. Where the rail does not fit beside the list it goes under it, unpinned. Every panel and press of the rail is still there.
- **One at a time** (`TakeoffFocusView`) measures itself the same way. Where the fixtures list does not fit beside the fixture it goes above it, 13.5 rem tall with its own scroll. The list now keeps the fixture you are on in its own view, in both layouts: Done and the arrows used to move past its foot.
- **`TakeoffLinesBox`** (new) is the box around the line table. It measures itself and sets `data-fit`. The rules are in `index.css` under `.takeoffLinesBox`.
  - `snug`: the side padding of the wide cells drawn in, the part field giving up the width a text field asks for by itself, the part column taking the spare width, and the Actions heading dropped. The table's floor goes from 726 px to 587.
  - `scroll`: the box scrolls sideways by itself, never the page. Each row's last cell, the drag handle and the delete, stays pinned at the box's right edge. A field brought into view by Tab stops clear of the pinned cell.
  - A width cannot know the rows. A long amount or a fixture named in one unbroken word can make the table wider than its box. So after each draw the box checks whether the table overflowed anyway and steps one tighter for that width.
- The box keeps `overflow: visible` while the table fits, as it has since v2.1059, because the part search list hangs below its row. Only `scroll` clips, and there the box makes room under the last row while a list is open.

## What it leaves

- Under the list, the rail is a long scroll away. The fold in the next change puts its headline on a strip that stays in view, one press from the rest.
- In `scroll`, under a 620 px box, Line total sits under the pinned presses until the box is scrolled.
- The part column is still 284 px in a roomy table at any window width, because a text field asks for that much and no more. The spare width goes to the other columns. Not touched here.
- Chromium only. Safari and Firefox were not measured.
