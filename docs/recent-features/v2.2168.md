# v2.2168 — People → Payroll → Ledger view (dev-only prototype) (2026-08-23)

Owner: "I really like how the full ledger in partnerships turned out… what one could look like inside People → Payroll — the ledger with chargebacks, offsets and other things all in one place." Ideation + mock-up (artifact "Payroll Ledger") → prototype → critique pass, same turn.

**Shape.** Two levels. (1) A **roster** of everyone with payroll activity, ranked we-owe → owes-us → even, company totals on top, one-line caption per person (`6 unpaid · $13,855 · 4 partial`, `3 charges · 1 credit`, `21 stubs · all paid`), group chips + search; sticky on desktop. (2) The selected person's **journal**: signed/colored balance headline with words (`we owe Malachi` / `Tristen owes us`), an equation of the totals with zero terms dropped, an amber unpaid-reports callout with the "so what" (where the balance lands if they're paid), `+ Charge` / `+ Credit`, kind-filter chips + "Unpaid only", and the Date · Posting · Amount · Balance table (newest first, month separators, kind pill = type, neutral balance column, unpaid rows tinted with a `record payment` link). Labor rows open the stub viewer; charge/credit rows open `PersonOffsetFormModal`. Phone: roster list → journal full-width with "‹ All balances"; stacked rows like the partner Full ledger.

**Kernel** [`src/lib/people/personLedger.ts`](../../src/lib/people/personLedger.ts) (+5 tests): `buildPersonLedger` wraps the person-agnostic `buildPartnerJournal` — stubs (labor at period_end), additional lines, deductions, payments at paid_at, and **every `person_offsets` row at its occurred_date** (credits too: employee credits never attach to a stub, so the partner rule "credits wait for a statement" would lose them; this is the one convention that differs from the partner card). Deductions mirroring a charge offset are skipped (same as partner). Also per-stub pay state (`unpaid` / `partial` / `paid` from net = gross + additions − deductions vs Σ payments), totals, counts, `lastPostingDate`. `buildAllPersonLedgers` (one per distinct trimmed `person_name` across stubs ∪ offsets — payroll is name-keyed, PERSON_IDENTITY_PLAN), `buildPeopleLedgerRoster` (ranking + totals + captions), `ledgerEquationTerms`, `offsetTypeLabel`. Sign: + = we owe them.

**View** [`PeoplePayLedgerView.tsx`](../../src/components/people/PeoplePayLedgerView.tsx); loads `person_offsets` itself and calls the parent's `loadPayStubs()` on mount (the Reports tab used to be the only trigger). Wired in [`People.tsx`](../../src/pages/People.tsx): dev-only **Pay reports | Ledger** segmented switch above the Payroll tab, `?view=ledger&person=<name>` deep link; non-dev unchanged.

**Verified live** on prod data (22 people): we owe $28,508.30 across 11 · owed to us $8,211.85 across 5 · 6 even; Bryan −$1,008.13 matches his partner card; Tristen −$5,997.44 (4 charges Oct–Dec 2025 outweigh 1 unpaid stub); Malachi +$18,742.00 (6 unpaid + 4 partial salary reports — "paying them settles up"). Critique pass shipped in the same PR: roster captions to one short line (whole dollars), kind pill = type with specifics as the label (was "PAID OUT Paid out · CashApp"), balance column neutral (was red on every row for a negative person), unpaid so-what sentence, `record payment` links, sticky roster, switch relabeled to avoid the Reports table's own "Ledger" heading.

**Open for the owner** (also in the mock-up): (1) `person_ledger_notes` — dated notes like the partnership ledger need their own table (migration; follow-up); (2) payments recorded outside `pay_stub_payments` will show as owed — the ledger makes that visible by design; (3) partner vs employee convention for positive offsets (counts at date here, waits for a statement on the partner card) — decide before controllers get the view; (4) the Reports table's h2 still says "Ledger" — rename to "Pay reports" when this graduates. Client-only; no migration.
