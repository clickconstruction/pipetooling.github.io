# v2.3248 — Customer Waiting, PR 3 of 4: the banner that follows the team + the Needs You item (2026-09-10)

Client only; reads the v2.3246 columns through one new app-wide subscription.

## Why

The row (v2.3247) only helps someone already looking at the inbox. The ask was a banner "at the top of the page following them around the app" for the people in that inbox, that goes away when the request is downgraded — and the review added: it must know when a teammate has already called, it must carry the wait, and it should not shout on the inbox page itself.

## What shipped

- **One subscription** — [`CustomerWaitingContext`](../../src/contexts/CustomerWaitingContext.tsx), mounted once around `layoutBody` in `Layout.tsx`. Eligibility = who can act: dev (both inboxes), `dispatch_group_members` (dispatch rows), `estimator_group_members` (estimator rows); digital twins never. Two selects on the partial `*_open_high_idx` indexes (`status = 'open' AND priority = 'high'`, oldest first, cap 50), realtime on both tables (`customer-waiting-dispatch` / `customer-waiting-estimator`, 400 ms debounce — UPDATE events carry the call stamp and the lowering). Exposes `rows`, `inboxHref` (Dispatch Mode's Inbox tab for dev / master / assistant-like dispatch members, else `/dashboard#dash-teams-inbox`), `logCall`. Readers use `useCustomerWaitingOptional()` — null outside the provider, so render tests and hosts without it keep working.
- **Kernel** — [`customerWaiting.ts`](../../src/lib/customerWaiting.ts): `buildCustomerWaitingBanner(rows, now)` → `waiting` (oldest uncalled row leads: name, kind, phone, ticking wait, 90-char snippet of their words, `+N more`) or `called` (every open high row stamped: the most recent call leads, "Sam called Jane Doe 2:14 pm"); `summarizeCustomerWaiting` for the Needs You input; `isInboxRoute`. Tested.
- **Banner** — [`CustomerWaitingBanner`](../../src/components/CustomerWaitingBanner.tsx) in the Layout slot after the training-mode notice (same geometry, `role="status"`, `aria-live="polite"`). Waiting: red tint, pulsing dot, headline + snippet, **Call** (`CallPhoneButton`, logs through the context) and **Open**. Called: amber tint, 📞, who/when + kind + *still open*, **Open**. On `/dispatch-mode/inbox` it collapses to one line ("1 customer waiting · 14 min" / "📞 Sam called …") with no buttons. **Open** navigates to `inboxHref`; when already on the Dashboard it scrolls to the Teams Inbox card.
- **Needs You** — new key `customer-waiting`, tier **0** (`NEEDS_YOU_RANK`), kicker *Customer portal*; red while anyone is uncalled ("Jane Doe is waiting · 14 min" / "3 customers waiting · oldest 1 h 15 min"), amber once every one has been called ("Jane Doe was called — request still open"); action *Open the inbox*. Inputs `customerWaitingEnabled` / `customerWaiting` are fed by both hosts from the context — `DashboardPinnedQuickRow` (Dashboard, Dispatch Mode → Inbox, Job Mode) scrolls to the Teams Inbox card or navigates to `inboxHref`; `QuickfillNeedsYouSection` opens the page's Dispatch station or navigates. Kernel test added.
- **Help** — the banner section appended to `answer-a-customer-waiting-request.md`.

## Notes for the next session

- The banner is deliberately undismissable: the design's answer to "everyone keeps seeing it" is the called state, not a per-viewer dismiss. If that turns out to be too loud for a five-person dispatch group, the next lever is muting the strip for the viewer who made the call (they have the row open anyway), not a dismiss.
- The provider's two membership reads duplicate `useDispatchInbox` / `useEstimatorInbox`'s own; folding the three into one shared eligibility hook is a clean follow-up.
- PR 4 (v2.3249): the portal form prefill + required number + the sent-state promise.
