# v2.5910 — The crew's job window gets the bar on a phone (2026-10-26)

Punch list #189, PR 4 (`to-dos/phone-first/`, plan a.2, train row 4; the owner's pick of 2026-10-26 from the mock-up's section 5). The walk at 375 px found the crew's window the one screen with nothing pinned: `resolveJobWindowMode` hands helpers, subs and superintendents the plain `DetailJobModal`, a centred card that scrolls, with Arrived, Leaving and Post note as 28 px chips under the note box and Close at the very end. The tabbed window's bar (punch list #30, PR 2b) was the right idea at 41 px. No migration, no edge function; the desktop is unchanged.

## What shipped

- **The kernel draws both bars** ([`jobWindowBar.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobWindowBar.ts)): `tabbedJobWindowBar(verb)` is Status ▾ · the job's next verb · Note, `crewJobWindowBar({ stamp, canLeaveReport, canNote })` is Arrived or Leaving · Leave report · Note, each button only while its press can land, and `JOB_WINDOW_BAR_MIN_HEIGHT` is 48. `crewStampOffer` picks the one stamp the viewer is due from their latest mark on the job today: an Arrived stamp or a clock-in says there (offer Leaving), a Leaving stamp or a clock-out says gone (offer Arrived), nothing today offers Arrived. The clock-in counts because most techs reach a job through Job Mode's I'm here, which clocks in and stamps nothing; read from stamps alone, the bar would offer them Arrived at the end of the day and Update Focus would be out of reach. A stamp is the viewer's by its author or by the name its body carries, a clock session by its person's name.
- **One bar component for both windows** ([`JobWindowActionBar.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobWindowActionBar.tsx)): `JobWindowActionBar({ buttons, onPress })` renders what the kernel returns — the growing button takes the row's spare width, the rest size to their words, every button `minHeight: 48`, a label too long for its cell ellipsized rather than pushing Note off the bar — and `JobWindowModal` passes `tabbedJobWindowBar(verb)`, so its three buttons grow from 41 to 48 px with nothing else changed (`aria-haspopup` stays on Status ▾ and a status-move verb only). The new **`JobWindowNoteSheet`** draws the composer on [`PhoneSheet`](../../src/components/PhoneSheet.tsx), the Messages windows' sheet, whose backdrop is as tall as the visual viewport, so the box and Save sit on the keyboard's top; it pads `--app-top-chrome` and the home bar's inset and takes Escape only when it is on top. The note box is focused in the commit (`useLayoutEffect`, so a phone raises its keyboard for the same press) with the caret at the end, at 16 px so iOS does not zoom; Save stands beside it at the bar's height, disabled until there are words.
- **The plain window on a phone** ([`DetailJobModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/DetailJobModal.tsx), `phoneCrewBar = !paneMode && narrowViewport`): the dialog becomes a column that fills the padded box (`height: min(90vh, 100%)`, so the bar sits at the foot of the screen even on a short job), its body a scroller padded 1.25rem, the bar a non-scrolling sibling under it (`safeAreaBottom={false}`, since the card's overlay already pads the inset). Arrived and Leaving keep their handlers — one pair (`stampArrived`, `stampLeaving`) now feeds the composer's chips on a desktop and the bar on a phone, and the chips leave the composer's row on a phone (`jobThreadStampActions` off while the bar holds them); who is offered them is as it was, everyone in the plain window. Leave report opens the same `AdditionalReportModal` the ＋ Report chip opens, now mounted from `fullJob ?? limitedJob`, since the crew's window holds the limited row and a same-job reload keeps it (`detailJob` goes null through every reload, which would unmount a form mid-typing); the chip's own gate is as it was. The bar is gated on the same row, so it never blinks during a reload. Note opens the sheet; a save goes through `threadNotes.submitNote()`, which now resolves `true` once the note is on the thread ([`useJobThreadNotesForModal.ts`](../../src/hooks/useJobThreadNotesForModal.ts)), and the sheet closes on it; a failed post keeps the words and the sheet. `noteSheetOpen` joins `detailEscBlocked`.
- **Words**: Arrived, Leaving, Leave report, Note, Save. The guide *clock in and out with Job Mode* gains **The job window on a phone**; *move between a job's Job, Edit, Bill, and Costs tabs* points the field roles at it; the Glossary's *Job window on a phone* entry and `JOBS_MODALS_ARCHITECTURE.md`'s two Job Detail sentences name the crew's bar.

Left for later, on purpose: the mock-up's four-button tabbed bar (the stamp beside Set % done for a primary on the crew), so the tabbed window keeps Status ▾ · verb · Note at 48 px and its Arrived / Leaving chips stay in the composer; the plain window's Close stays the last thing in its scroll (a tap on the card's gutter still closes it); the gallery strip under the address waits on #164's PR 5.
