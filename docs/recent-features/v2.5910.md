# v2.5910 — The crew's job window gets the bar on a phone (2026-10-26)

Punch list #189, PR 4 (`to-dos/phone-first/`, plan a.2, train row 4; the owner's pick of 2026-10-26 from the mock-up's section 5, whose after draws the tabbed window's bar as Status ▾ · the verb · Leaving · Note, as do flows 7 and 8 of its section 8). The walk at 375 px found the crew's window the one screen with nothing pinned: `resolveJobWindowMode` hands helpers, subs and superintendents the plain `DetailJobModal`, a centred card that scrolls, with Arrived, Leaving and Post note as 28 px chips under the note box and Close at the very end. The tabbed window's bar (punch list #30, PR 2b) was the right idea at 41 px, but carried no stamp, though the masters and primaries who open it are techs too. No migration, no edge function; the desktop is unchanged.

## What shipped

- **The kernel draws both bars** ([`jobWindowBar.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobWindowBar.ts)): `tabbedJobWindowBar(verb, stamp)` is Status ▾ · the job's next verb · the stamp · Note (the verb takes the spare width; on a Paid job, with no verb, the stamp does), `crewJobWindowBar({ stamp, canLeaveReport, canNote })` is Arrived or Leaving · Leave report · Note, each button only while its press can land, both stamps built by one `stampButton`, and `JOB_WINDOW_BAR_MIN_HEIGHT` is 48. `crewStampOffer` picks the one stamp the viewer is due from their latest mark on the job today: an Arrived stamp or a clock-in says there (offer Leaving), a Leaving stamp or a clock-out says gone (offer Arrived), nothing today offers Arrived. The clock-in counts because most techs reach a job through Job Mode's I'm here, which clocks in and stamps nothing; read from stamps alone, the bar would offer them Arrived at the end of the day and Update Focus would be out of reach. A stamp is the viewer's by its author or by the name its body carries, a clock session by its person's name.
- **One bar component for both windows** ([`JobWindowActionBar.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobWindowActionBar.tsx)): `JobWindowActionBar({ buttons, onPress })` renders what the kernel returns — the growing button's track `minmax(0, 1fr)`, the rest `auto`, every button `minHeight: 48`, the verb's words ellipsized (`overflow: hidden`, `min-width: 0`) rather than pushing Note off the bar — with sides of `0.6rem` and gaps of `0.375rem`, so four buttons fit a 375 px phone. Measured on the real component's markup in headless Chromium under the app's stylesheet: at 375 px every row (the six verbs with either stamp, the three-button rows, the crew rows) fits with no sideways overflow, every button 48 px tall, Status ▾ 80 px, the stamp 75 to 78, Note 56 and the verb 124; five verbs show whole and only *Move to Working* ellipsizes; at 320 px everything still fits, the verb cut sooner. The new **`JobWindowNoteSheet`** draws the composer on [`PhoneSheet`](../../src/components/PhoneSheet.tsx), the Messages windows' sheet, whose backdrop is as tall as the visual viewport, so the box and Save sit on the keyboard's top; it pads `--app-top-chrome` and the home bar's inset and takes Escape only when it is on top. The note box is focused in the commit (`useLayoutEffect`) with the caret at the end, at 16 px so iOS does not zoom; Save stands beside it at the bar's height, disabled until there are words.
- **The plain window on a phone** ([`DetailJobModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/DetailJobModal.tsx), `phoneCrewBar = !paneMode && narrowViewport`): the dialog becomes a column that fills the padded box (`height: min(90vh, 100%)`, so the bar sits at the foot of the screen even on a short job), its body a scroller padded 1.25rem, the bar a non-scrolling sibling under it (`safeAreaBottom={false}`, since the card's overlay already pads the inset). Leave report opens the same `AdditionalReportModal` the ＋ Report chip opens, now mounted from `fullJob ?? limitedJob`, since the crew's window holds the limited row and a same-job reload keeps it (`detailJob` goes null through every reload, which would unmount a form mid-typing); the chip's own gate is as it was. The bar is gated on the same row, so it never blinks during a reload.
- **The tabbed window on a phone** ([`JobWindowModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobWindowModal.tsx)): the pane hands the window's bar its half through `onPhoneBarChange` — a `JobPanePhoneBar`, the stamp a viewer on the crew is due (`viewerOnCrew`, the rule that already gated the composer's chips in pane mode) and one press that answers Arrived, Leaving and Note with the pane's own handlers and draft. The press reads the newest handlers through a ref, so the window hears again only when the stamp changes. Note is a direct call rather than the compose event, whose 50 ms timer would land the focus outside the press, where a phone does not raise its keyboard; it moves the window to the Job tab, so the saved note shows in the activity behind the sheet, and the compose event's 'note' mode stays the fallback before the pane reports. 'pct' (Set % done) is as it was. The office, off the crew, keeps Status ▾ · verb · Note.
- **Both windows on a phone**: one `stampDue` feeds both bars; Arrived and Leaving keep their handlers — one pair (`stampArrived`, `stampLeaving`) feeds the composer's chips on a desktop and the bar on a phone, and the chips leave the composer's row on a phone in either window (`jobThreadStampActions` off while a bar holds them); Leaving still opens Update Focus. The Note sheet draws outside the pane's hidden body, so the tabbed window's Note opens it from any tab; a save goes through `threadNotes.submitNote()`, which now resolves `true` once the note is on the thread ([`useJobThreadNotesForModal.ts`](../../src/hooks/useJobThreadNotesForModal.ts)), and the sheet closes on it; a failed post keeps the words and the sheet. `noteSheetOpen` joins `detailEscBlocked`.
- **Words**: Arrived, Leaving, Leave report, Note, Save. The guide *clock in and out with Job Mode* gains **The job window on a phone**, ending on the tabbed window's bar for masters and primaries on the crew; *move between a job's Job, Edit, Bill, and Costs tabs* says the bar's Note opens the note box and the crew's stamp sits before it, and points the field roles at the crew guide; the Glossary's *Job window on a phone* entry and `JOBS_MODALS_ARCHITECTURE.md`'s Job Detail sentences name both bars.

Left for later, on purpose: the plain window's Close stays the last thing in its scroll (a tap on the card's gutter still closes it); the gallery strip under the address waits on #164's PR 5.
