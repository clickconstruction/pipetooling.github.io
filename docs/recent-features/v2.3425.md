# v2.3425 — Demand letter: the letter reads the bill (2026-09-14)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. PR 1 of the *Demand letter, itemized* train (`to-dos/demand-letter-itemized/`). Owner: "I like all these updates, make them" (2026-09-14), after the mock-up and the statute research in the to-do.

## Why

On job 867 the letter's *Details of Debt* read "Service provided: Service Visit — 628 Terrell Rd (HCP 867) · Invoice #1c915885" — the job name and the first eight characters of the invoice row's id — while the bill the customer got read "Service Visit (HCP #867) Additional gas install and sidewalk bore under patio · Qty 1 · $1,710.00", Invoice #867-2608180928, addressed to the GC. The letter went to the homeowner. A sworn-account claim wants the name, date and charge of each item with credits allowed (Tex. R. Civ. P. 185; *Panditi v. Apostle*, 180 S.W.3d 924), and an excessive or mismatched demand forfeits attorney's fees (*Findlay v. Cave*, 611 S.W.2d 57) — so the debt has to be the bill, and the demand has to go where the bill went.

## What shipped

- **Kernel** [`demandLetter.ts`](../../src/lib/jobsDocuments/demandLetter.ts): `DemandStatementInvoice` / `DemandStatementLine` and `buildDemandStatement(job, sources)` — per covered invoice, the number the customer saw (Stripe's `invoice_number` when the bill is hosted, else the document's display number, else `#<sequence>`; never an id fragment), sent / due dates, the lines (Stripe's lines when hosted, else the physical-invoice document's service + material lines, else the memo and the amount), payments applied to that invoice, the balance. `demandDebtorParty(job, invoices)` = `effectiveInvoiceParty` of the covered invoice — the same rule the bill used. A new `statement` block: `statementRows()` feeds the HTML, text, PDF and React renderers (invoice row → lines → *Payments and credits* → *Balance due* / *Balance on this invoice*, a *Balance due* total when several). The Re line reads *Invoice #867-2608180928 · $1,710.00*; the opening names the billed date, the due date and the service address, then *Nothing has been paid* or *$X has been paid and $Y remains*, and "our presentment of the claim"; *Statement of account* replaces *Details of Debt*; *All payments and credits have been allowed* closes it. Snapshots recorded before this (no `statement`) still render the old four-line block. `DemandLetterFields` gains `debtorParty`, `statement`, `serviceAddress`; prefill takes `sources` (+9 tests, 17 in the file).
- **The modal** [`LienInstrumentsModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienInstrumentsModal.tsx): fetches the full job (fixtures for the invoice document), the payer rows (name, address, billing email for the customer and the GC), and `get-stripe-invoice-details` for each hosted bill (fail-soft — the statement falls back to the app's document). *To (the debtor)* is gone: **Who owes it · from the bill** shows the party's name, email and *the GC on the job* / *the customer on the job* / *the payer typed on the bill*, a red *needs a mailing address* chip when the record has none, and on a GC job with a homeowner the sentence *The bill was addressed to the GC, so the demand goes there too. The property owner gets the § 53.056 notice instead* with the tab as its door. The mailing address stays editable (a corrected address survives re-prefill). **What the letter claims · the bill as sent, read-only** lists the statement rows with *Fix it on the bill — the letter re-reads it*. Bill chips read the display number and show the payer when the job's bills go to different payers; picking a bill for another payer starts a letter for that payer (one letter per payer). The preview renders the statement as a table.
- Guide *send a final demand letter*: the statement, who owes it, one letter per payer.
