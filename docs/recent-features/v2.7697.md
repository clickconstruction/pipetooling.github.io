# v2.7697 — Checklist Review: see a person's Task Mode screen in a window (2026-11-17)

Punch list #381, steps 2 and 3 of five. No migration and no edge function. The owner picked the design on 2026-11-17: a look, the press named for what opens, the owner and a dev.

## What is new

- **See <first name>'s Task Mode**, a press on a person's opened card on Checklist → Review, beside *＋ Add task for*. It shows for the people who may act for others there (`canActForOthers`: the owner or a dev).
- **The window** (`TaskModeLookWindow.tsx`). At a desk it draws the person's screen at a phone's width, with its own scroll, beside a side column. On a phone it takes the whole screen and the side column follows the screen. The head says when the day was read. **Read again** mounts the day anew. **Close**, Escape and a press on the backdrop close it.
- **Their screen is Today itself.** The window's child is `ChecklistTodayTab` with a new `look` prop, under the Task Mode strip and with the Today and History bar. Nothing is copied, so the look cannot drift from the screen it shows.

## How Today reads someone else

`ChecklistTodayTab` now holds two ids. `personId` is whose day it is, and every read keys on it: `readPersonDay`, `readPersonAhead`, Waiting For, who hears, and who a note is by. `actorId` is who acts. They are both the signed-in person, except in a look, where `personId` is the person on the card and `actorId` is null. Every write in the tab asks for `actorId` first and finds no one.

The person's role is read from `users` when the window opens. It decides what their phone offers: the office's Upcoming fold and *＋ Add a task for me*.

## How a look is kept a look

Three things, each enough by itself.

1. **`TaskLookGate`** wraps the screen. It stops a click, a double click, Enter or Space, a submit, a change, a paste and a drop on the way down, before the press's own code hears it. A box to type in gives the keyboard back. The stop also covers a sheet a press would draw elsewhere on the page, since React carries the event through the tree and not the page. The rule is `lookAllowsPress` in `src/lib/tasks/taskLook.ts`: a press goes through only when it wears `aria-expanded` and opens no menu or window, or when it is a link that opens a new tab. A press the rule has never heard of is dead.
2. **The tab's own writes** find no `actorId`: mark done, the percent, a line's tick, a note, Forward, the round, Yesterday's answers, Do first, a follow-up, a task for me.
3. **The windows of acts are not mounted** in a look: the doer's sheets, Forward, Mute alerts and the vehicle window.

Three folds gained `aria-expanded`, which they should have had: Still open's *Show N more*, Waiting For's *more steps ahead* and the office's Upcoming.

## What a look does not show

The window lists these in its side column. The Task Mode switch, Do first and the distance to a task are kept on the person's own phone. Tagged notes are read for the signed-in person alone (`useMyNoteTags`), so a look leaves the section out.

Two smaller things differ from the phone. *Where my tasks are* follows the viewer's own Hide map choice, and at a desk it draws at the desk's map height. Forward is not drawn, as on a phone.
