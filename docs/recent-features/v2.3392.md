# v2.3392 — Drive pass: the folder chain, customer-folder names, and what is not a contract (2026-09-14)

Edge function `drive-contract-scan` changed (**already deployed** — the first live pass ran on it); no migration. Follows v2.3390 / v2.3391 the same day.

## What the first live pass taught

Run as dev against the folder the owner shared (v2.3391): 422 contract-looking files, 267 job folders across the two roots. Two facts the mock-up had not assumed:

- **Folders are named by customer**, not by address or job number — `Josh Peterson`, `_Heron Construction`, `_Mason Dudley` (a leading underscore on builders). The matcher's "confident" rule (street or job number in the folder name) never fired; every hit was a *Check* by customer name, and `RMC- Dudley Mason` did not match `_Mason Dudley` at all (word order).
- **Most files sit two or three folders deep** (`_Heron Construction / 105 Dover / …`), and the one-level attribution left 300 of 422 unplaced. Almost all of those turned out to be a bids tree (`Bids-Plumbing (William) / 260529 TAKE 5 … / PROPOSALS`) — proposals, spec divisions and GC sample subcontracts, not signed job contracts.

Result after the fixes below: **1 confident** (J931 Heron Construction — a signed estimate for 105 Dover Rd, filed through the modal as the first live use of the door), 8 to check, 114 unmatched (customers whose jobs are paid or not in the ledger). Of the 104 jobs without a contract, then, only a handful have a signed copy in this folder — the pile is not mainly a Drive filing gap for these jobs.

## What shipped

- **`drive-contract-scan`**: walks each file's parent chain (≤ 5 levels, one cached metadata GET per distinct folder, ≤ 1 500) up to a root's child folder; the chain of names below it rides along as `Job folder / Sub / Deeper` so an address or job-number subfolder still matches; `unattributed_samples` (≤ 40, with their chain) and `folder_lookups` come back for diagnosis. A live scan takes ~50 s.
- **Kernel [`driveContractMatch.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/driveContractMatch.ts)** (+1 test, others extended): `nameWords` / `partyNamesAgree` — a customer folder and a job's customer or GC agree when one's identifying words all appear in the other's, ignoring RMC / LLC / Contracting / Construction / Homes… (`_Mason Dudley` ↔ `RMC- Dudley Mason`); the customer match reads the chain's top segment. `NEVER_CONTRACT` words (sample, template, exhibit, rider, registration, insurance, inspection, report, geotech, specs, division, procurement, budget, schedule, lien, waiver, release, change order) win over "signed" or "subcontract" in the same name; the soft exclusions (invoice, quote, plans, permit, W-9, COI, receipt, photo) still yield to "contract" / "agreement".
