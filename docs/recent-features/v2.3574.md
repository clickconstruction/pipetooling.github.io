# v2.3574 — Job accounts on the Bid Board, PR 2: the won lane on "Where this bid is" (2026-09-17)

PR 2, the last of `to-dos/bid-board-job-accounts/README.md` (PR 1a v2.3520, PR 1b v2.3553); the mock-up's second board, *Two more steps on "Where this bid is"*. Client only.

## The kernel — [`bidFlow.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/bidFlow.ts)

Two step defs after Sent, phase **Won**: **11 Job opened** (proxy: a job created from this bid — the win moment; no door, the row's own *open the job →* pill is that door) and **12 Job accounts** (proxy: every house that quoted the bid or expects an account is open or marked not needed; door `accounts`). `deriveBidFlow` includes them only when the bid's outcome is won or started, so an undecided or lost bid still has ten steps and every existing count and summary reads as before.

The proxy rule as the mock-up wrote it: step 12 is **done** when no house is missing, **next** when the job exists and a house is missing — the step carries a caption, *Reece missing* / *Reece +1 missing* — and **untracked** before the job exists, because the account keys on the job the win moment creates. Step 11 is done once a job exists, next until then. A decided bid's first ten still go quiet; on the won lane the next unfinished step rings, because the job and its accounts are the office's work after the win. `bidFlowSummary` reads *3 of 11 done · next: Job accounts* while the lane has work and *· decided* once it does not.

`BidFlowFacts` gains an optional `jobAccounts` — `undefined` when a surface does not read the strips (the two steps show untracked there: the Counts, Takeoffs, Pricing and Cover Letter strips), `null` while loading, `{ jobId, missingHouses }` when known. [`bidBoardJobAccounts.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/bidBoardJobAccounts.ts) → `jobAccountFlowFacts(rows, loaded)` reads it from the page-wide `list_bid_job_account_strip` rows the Won section already loads (PR 1a); nothing new is fetched.

## The board — [`BidsBidBoardTab.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/BidsBidBoardTab.tsx)

Both strips on a won row (the hairline and the opened row's full strip) take the merged facts. The `accounts` door opens the Job accounts question for the bid's job — the same door the row's *…* chip opens — and is the board's own: the page's door handler ignores it. [`BidFlowStrip`](../../src/components/bids/BidFlowStrip.tsx) draws a step's `detail` as the small caption under its label (the slot the Review stamp already used) and does not offer the door on an untracked Job accounts step.
