# v2.5236 — The Bridge: the dial, exploit before you elevate (link PR 5) (2026-10-11)

PR 5 of the weakest-link build (`to-dos/bridge-weakest-link/README.md`). Dev only. No migration.

- **The kernel** (`src/lib/bridge/dial.ts`): `todayPace` (a stage's pace today, in dollars a week: billed, collected, built, sold, or asked), `dialSpec` (a range from a quarter to three times today, stepped at $1k or $5k, with the exploit / elevate boundary at 1.5× today, `DIAL_ELEVATE_AT`), `applyDial` (the flow with the stage's pace set: Bill feeds Collect's in-rate, Build feeds Bill's and the feeders' pace, Sales feeds Build's, Market scales its asks), and `buildDialReading`: the value with a plain unit under it (jobs, invoices, field hours, asks, from the page's own counts), the marks (today, the pile stops growing, where the link crosses to another stage, where elevating begins; a feeder shows the field's pace), the pile over twelve weeks at the chosen pace and at today's (a feeder shows Build's pile), and three readings — the pile (grows, holds, or is gone by a date), the link (stays, becomes, or moves to another stage, by `buildStageFlow` on the adjusted flow), the money (for Bill, how much of the field's week becomes a bill and how much stays on paper; for Collect, dollars into the bank against today; for Build, what waits unbilled; for Sales and Market, what a dollar more becomes). Every sentence passes the plain-words rule.
- **The component** (`BridgeDial.tsx`): the slider over a track shaded blue for exploit and amber for elevate, with the zone words ("exploit · with the biller we have", "elevate · a second biller"), the marks on two rows where they crowd, the number and unit, the sparkline, the three boxes, and **back to today** once moved.
- **The page**: the stage-flow input is now a memo of its own (`stageInput`); while the dial is moved the cards draw `dialReading.flow` instead of today's, so the red moves with the slider. Each stage keeps its own dial value until reset.
