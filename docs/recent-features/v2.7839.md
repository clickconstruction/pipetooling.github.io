# v2.7839 — Materials on site: a watch on one person (2026-11-18)

The sixth PR of the train on seeing what one person does with the material on a site. Client only. It reads and writes the tables and the function of `20261118150000_person_watches.sql` (v2.7825), so that migration is pushed before this deploys.

## What landed

- **The control**, [`PersonWatchRow.tsx`](../../src/components/personDesk/sections/PersonWatchRow.tsx), a row of the Person desk's Access & account section drawn for a dev on another person's row with a login: a reason, a tick to be on the watch's list, **Turn on the watch**; then when it began and who set it, and **Stop watching** with its own reason. The reads and writes are [`personWatchIo.ts`](../../src/lib/people/personWatchIo.ts).
- **On the By person look.** The look reads the live watches (a dev's read; anyone else gets none and the look draws as it did). `withWatch` marks every act under a watched person's name with why it is outside the process (`outsideProcess`: a count, a part added, a box retired, brought back, changed or renamed, an answer taken off, a take or a use with no note), a box change following the name since it carries no login. Those acts wear a red chip in place of the amber ones, the filter reads **Outside the process only**, the last figure counts them, and a chip beside the name says since when.
- **Boxes opened and not pressed on.** Both box screens, the phone's and the office's window, call `noteBoxOpen` for every person. The database keeps a row for a watched caller alone. For a watched person the look names the boxes opened since the watch began with no press on them (`openedNotPressed`), each a door to its box.
- **Nothing to see for the person watched.** The call that reports an open is made for everyone, is never awaited, and swallows every failure, since an error on it would be the one sign of a watch.

## The limits

- Nobody is told yet. The watch keeps a list for when alerts are sent, and the control says so.
- An open is reported by the screen. Material taken without opening the box in the app leaves no row.
- A watched person in training mode logs no open: the training-mode fence refuses the write.
