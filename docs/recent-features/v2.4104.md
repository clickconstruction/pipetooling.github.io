# v2.4104 — The job's cost timeline counts card charges as the parts cost does, with fuel on its own line (2026-09-28)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Punch list **#52** (`to-dos/fuel-is-a-job-cost.md`), PR 3.

## The mismatch

The cost timeline — the Job window's Costs tab (the chart, Burn's daily spend, *spent so far*) and Job Summary's expanded row — took every card line allocated to the job as a positive amount (`Math.abs`). An Internal Transfer counted as spend, a charge already on a supply-house invoice counted twice, and a refund at the counter added to the cost instead of coming off. The line did not end on the job's parts cost; on a long job, that is the running cost you watch.

## The change

- **`src/lib/jobs/jobCardChargeEvents.ts`** (new, pure) — `cardEventRowsFromSnapshotLines` (the Job window: the one card rule's lookups on the materials snapshot) and `cardEventRowsFromJobSummaryRows` (Job Summary: the per-job read, which already drops Internal Transfers, now leaves out the invoice-linked charges). Both give each charge at its signed cost (`cardChargeCostUsd`) and flag the fuel.
- **`src/lib/jobChargesTimeline.ts`** — a `fuel` source, ⛽ **Fuel**: a card charge in the fuel family's tag is its own stream, on the day it was bought. The budget, Burn and verdict read it as parts, as before.
- **`pickFuelTag`** (`lib/banking/categoryTags.ts`) — the fuel family's tag (the seeded `fuel_vehicle`, else a tag named fuel), now shared by Wheels and the timeline.
- **The reads** — `fetchJobMaterialsCostSnapshot` carries `cardFuelTxIds`; `fetchMercuryJobAllocationsWithAttributionForJob` asks for the bank category and returns `isFuel`; Job Summary's row keeps `linkedToSupplyInvoice` and `isFuel`. Tags and labels are office-staff reads: any failure leaves every charge a plain card charge.
- Help guide *read the cost and value timeline on a job*: the ⛽ Fuel stream and the rule.

Not changed: Burn's daily bars keep fuel inside parts; the Months view still spreads parts by field hours.
