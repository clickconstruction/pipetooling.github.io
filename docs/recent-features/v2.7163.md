# v2.7163 — Bill Customer: one press makes the Stripe bill and emails it (2026-11-13)

Punch list #343, PR 3 of 3: the one press. The owner's word on the card was *build it all*, which is his yes to this one.

## What was wrong

A Stripe bill took two screens. The first press made the bill and marked it billed. The send was on the next screen, behind **Send Email invoice** and *Yes, send it*. A bill could be made and never sent. Bills sent listed 24 open bills with no send on record on the day of the look. PR 1 (v2.7157) made the first press say it sent nothing. This one makes the main press send.

## The change

| Press | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Make the bill and email Dana** (the blue one; *…and email 2 people* with a copy ticked, *…and email it* with no name) | `submitStripeInvoice(true)`: the bill is made as before, then the bill email goes |
| **Make the bill, send later** (the quiet one) | `submitStripeInvoice(false)`: the old press, unchanged, under a truer name |

- **The send is the same send.** `sendStripeBillEmail` (`src/lib/billing/sendStripeBillEmail.ts`) is the call and the reading of its answer that lived inside `StripeInvoiceSendFromStripeButton`. The button now calls it too, so the one press and **Send Email invoice** cannot drift. Edge function `send-stripe-invoice`, unchanged: our email with Pay now and Stripe's PDF, a copy of their own to each address on the row, Stripe's own email as the fallback, and in test mode the email comes to the sender.
- **Where it sits in the submit.** After the make, the promote, the fee folds and the notice emails, and before the job is read back. It runs under the 60 s email timeout.
- **A send that fails never undoes the bill.** `stripeMadeEmail` holds how it went. The made-bill screen reads *The bill is made. The bill email did not go: {reason} Press Send Email invoice below to send it.* A timeout reads *The email may or may not have gone. Check Bills sent before sending again.* A failed promote returns before the send, as it always returned before the made-bill screen, and its error now says the email did not go.
- **The made-bill screen.** With the email sent it shows the send's own line, *Bill email sent to dana@example.com*. The strip that offers the text leads with that line (`justMadeLead` on `HostedStripeBillPanel`) in place of *Nothing has gone to the customer yet*. The Send Email invoice button there already reads sent (`rememberBillEmailSent`). The line about copies going on Send Email invoice shows only when the email has not gone.
- **The box above the presses** (`theyWillGet`): *The bill is made and marked billed. The bill email goes now to dana@example.com. A text is your choice on the next screen. Every send is kept in Bills sent.* Then *Make the bill, send later makes the bill and sends nothing. Use it for a bill you hand over or text.* With copies it adds *1 more person gets a copy of their own.* In the dev's test mode it reads *Test mode: the bill email comes to you, not to dana@example.com.*
- **No second question.** The one press sends at once, as the Physical tab's press does. The box that says so is right above it.

No migration and no edge function change. Client only.
