# v2.2773 — Contract-for-signature email on the sub portal's paper (2026-09-04)

The email `send-contract-for-signature` sends was three lines of unstyled HTML from "ClickTooling" with a bare "Open signing page" link and a subject that named the signer back to themselves. Owner asked for mockups; picked B ("Paper", the sub portal's own identity) over A (the estimate Letterhead sibling) and C (a plain note from the sender).

## What changed

- **One shared builder** — `supabase/functions/_shared/contractSigningEmail.ts` (dependency-free), re-exported to the app by `src/lib/contractSigningEmail.ts` the way the estimate letterhead is. `buildContractSigningEmail(input)` returns `{ subject, text, html, replyTo, fromName }`. Test: `src/lib/contractSigningEmail.test.ts`.
- **The design** — the portal palette from `src/lib/portal/portalTheme.ts` inlined as literals (mail clients): paper `#f6f3ec`, ink `#16283c`, copper `#b0662f`. CLICK. wordmark + "Plumbing, Electrical, and HVAC", right-side "Paperwork to sign · For <person> · <date>", ink rule, copper eyebrow "One document to sign", the document name as the heading, "Sent to you by <sender> · Click Plumbing and Electrical", the staff member's opening message (default: "Here is your agreement to read and sign…"), a three-step card (open / read, about 2 minutes / type or draw your signature), an ink "Read and sign" button (table cell with bgcolor), the raw link + "works until <expiry>", a note band ("Once you have signed, it stays on your page. my.clickplumbing.com/<slug> keeps your jobs, your pay, and your paperwork…" or the no-portal wording "we keep the copy on file"), one Spanish line pointing at the portal's Español toggle, and a reach line (reply to reach <sender first name>, call or text the office [at phone]). Mail-safe rules from the bid-room/estimate emails: tables not flex, explicit colors, `color-scheme: light only`.
- **Subject** — default `Please sign: <document> · Click Plumbing and Electrical`; the per-send subject box still overrides. **From** — display name `Click Plumbing and Electrical` on EMAIL_FROM's verified address; **Reply-To** the sender (their `users` row's email, else the auth email). `email_send_log` rows now carry `emailType: 'contract_for_signature'`.
- **Edge function** `send-contract-for-signature` — after minting the token it reads the sender's `users` row, resolves the signer's portal address read-only (exactly one non-archived `people` row with `person_name`, a `sub_portal_slugs` row, and an unrevoked `sub_portal_links` row → `https://my.clickplumbing.com/<slug>`; anything else → null, nothing minted), computes `expiresYmd` from the 14-day token in the company calendar zone, and builds the mail. The old local clamp/escape helpers are gone (the builder owns clamping: subject 200, intro 4000, control chars stripped). **Redeploy required.**
- **Send dialog** (`PeopleContractsTab`) — `src/lib/contractSendEmailPreview.ts` (the hand-kept mirror) is deleted; the preview calls the shared builder and renders the HTML in a sandboxed iframe with From / Reply-To / Subject above it. New prop `currentUserId` (People.tsx passes `authUser.id`) → sender from the `users` list; the portal address is looked up the same way the function does (office RLS on the two portal tables). Modal widened to `min(640px, 92vw)` with `max-height: 92vh; overflow: auto`. Placeholders name the real defaults.
- **Docs** — guide `send-one-contract-to-one-person.md` gets a "What the email looks like" section; `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` section updated; `emailCatalog` subject example updated.

## Not changed

The signing page, the token/14-day expiry/`sent` status flow, the per-send subject and intro boxes, the sending domain and `EMAIL_FROM` secret, `PORTAL_COMPANY.phone` (still empty → "call or text the office" with no number).
