# v2.3378 — Share this bill, PR 4: Ask the office — a GC asks about a customer's bill from their statement (2026-09-13)

No migration; edge functions `submit-portal-request` and `customer-portal` changed (**redeploy both**). Last of the train in [`to-dos/gc-portal-customers-bills/`](../../to-dos/gc-portal-customers-bills/README.md) (PR 1 v2.3375, PR 2 v2.3376, PR 3 v2.3377).

## Why

A list of other people's debts with no verb is a report, not a tool. The plan named the two things a builder actually wants to do with a shared bill — cover it, or get the owner nudged — and deliberately routed both through the office so the GC's click never emails the owner and never pays the owner's bill.

## What shipped

- **`customer-portal`**: `sharedBills[].jobId` (the visit picker's `requestableJobs` already carries job ids, so this exposes nothing new).
- **`submit-portal-request`**: kind `share_bill_ask` `{ ask: 'bill_me' | 'remind_owner', jobId, amount?, note? }`. The job must name the link's customer as its GC and someone else as its customer, and at least one billed invoice (or the billed shell remainder) must be **shared** with the viewer by `statementRoleFor` — else 403 *That bill is not on your statement*. The function writes the sentence (*Done Right Foundation asks to be billed for J1017 · 4410 Cedar Hollow ($4,420.00) instead of Maria Delgado.* + *Note: …*) into a `dispatch_requests` row — priority high, `job_ledger_id` set, `pending_payload.kind = 'share_bill_ask'` with `ask`, `jobNumber`, `amount`, `ownerName` — under the generic per-link rate limit; push fan-out and the Portal requests email stream as for a visit.
- **The portal** ([`PortalShareAsk`](../../src/components/portal/PortalShareAsk.tsx), mounted by [`PortalSharedBillsCard`](../../src/components/portal/PortalSharedBillsCard.tsx) on the GC's rows only, with the request token): **Ask the office ›** under each shared bill → two chips (*Bill this to us instead* · *Remind the owner for us*, each with a one-line hint), an optional note, *Send to the office*; the confirmation repeats the bill and the promise (*we'll call you during office hours* / *we'll reach out to the owner and let you know*). Sample-mode tokens short-circuit like the promise ask. The owner's card never offers it.
- **The inbox** ([`portalRequestPayload.ts`](../../src/lib/portalRequestPayload.ts), [`requestPriority.ts`](../../src/lib/requestPriority.ts)): kind `share_bill_ask` parses; the Customer Waiting card's pill reads *Portal · about a customer's bill* and the body is the sentence, with the Call button and the GC's number on file. The office acts with **Bill to ▾** on the Bill tab (the invoice's own `bill_to_party` pick) or the Followup tools. +2 tests; +2 render assertions.
- Help: *choose who gets the bill on a job* → *What the builder can ask*; *share a customer their portal* → When they send a request. `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` → submit-portal-request (v2.3378) and customer-portal (v2.3375 note, catching up PR 1's section).

## Verified

Kernel, parser, inbox and portal suites; typecheck, lint, theme check. Live after both deploys: from Done Right's portal on a shared repair bill, *Bill this to us instead* lands in the Dispatch inbox as a Customer waiting card naming the job and the amount; an unshared job answers 403.
