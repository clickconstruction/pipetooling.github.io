# v2.7520 — Lien desk: Redraw from the bills, and the leader signs the paper he reads (2026-11-16)

PR 2 of the fix for a lien packet that stated two amounts (PR 1 is v2.7517, the run's hold). The run now refuses a notice whose stored paper and bills have parted; this gives the desk the way back, and stops a signature from landing on a paper the leader did not see. Client only: no migration, no edge function. Stacked on v2.7517.

## What was wrong

- The pane re-read the claim from the bills (v2.3682) but `approve` and `signOnly` passed `item.fields`, the stored row, to the signing write. The hash the signature binds was of the paper as it was sent for approval, not the one on the screen.
- An approved notice had no press that rewrote its stored paper: *Pull back to draft* is offered on Awaiting and Held only, and a claim set by hand saves into a draft alone.
- `noticeDate` was set once, the day a draft was first made, and never refreshed. A notice approved six weeks later printed the draft's day.

## What changed

- **The band.** `LienDesk` reads `lienNoticeParted` (v2.7517's rule) for any item past a draft and draws a band over the paper (`data-lien-desk-parted`): what parted with both figures, then what happens next, from `lienNoticePartedBand`. Signed: the run holds it, redraw it. Not yet signed: the screen shows today's figures and the leader signs those. Printed: check it before it is mailed, and take it back to redraw it.
- **Redraw from the bills ▸** (`data-lien-desk-redraw`), for the office and the leader, on a live item awaiting approval, approved or held, and not printed (`canRedrawItem`). It asks first (`lienNoticeRedrawConfirm`, the button names the act). `fieldsRedrawn` replaces `fields.notice` with the paper the pane draws, dated today, keeps every other key of the row, and adds `fields.redrawn`: who, when, the claim and the date it carried before. `redrawLienDeskItem` writes it with `LIEN_DESK_SIGNATURE_CLEAR`, filtered on the same rule, and answers false when the row had moved on. The status stands: an approved notice stays approved and reads unsigned, the state *Sign it again…* leaves it in.
- **Signing signs what is shown.** `fieldsToSign` builds `lienNoticeSignFields`: the pane's paper, with the redraw line when its money had parted. When its hash is not the stored row's, the row is rewritten first and the signature binds the new fields; when it is, nothing is written. A row that moved on is not signed, and the press says so. A printed paper is signed as it printed.
- **The date is the day it is signed.** The pane's `noticeFields` takes today's date until the paper is signed, printed or sent (`paperDateStands`); a signed paper keeps the day it carries. The field's hover and the preview's source read *the day it is signed*.
- **The line after.** A redrawn notice with nothing parted keeps a muted line (`data-lien-desk-redrawn`): *Redrawn from the bills by Dana on Nov 16. It claimed $18,400.00 before.*

## Not changed

- The run window, the phone's approval card and Put a GC on notice still sign the stored row. A notice signed there over a parted paper is caught by the run's hold and redrawn here.
- A printed notice is not redrawn in place and a sent one is never touched: the record of what went stays as it went.
- Whether the form's date should be the day signed or the day mailed is counsel's to say; this takes the day signed, since the date is inside what the signature binds.
