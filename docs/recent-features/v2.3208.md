# v2.3208 — Bids on a map: Play tours the section views, two seconds each (2026-09-09)

Client only; no migration. Owner ask, pointing at the map card's header on the Bid Board: a play button that "cycles through the multiple views of the map with one view every two seconds, and the view that is showing being highlighted as a button." The card's views are its section chips (Unsent / Pending / Won / Started / Lost), so the tour walks those.

## What shipped

- [`bidBoardMap.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/bidBoardMap.ts): `BID_BOARD_MAP_TOUR_INTERVAL_MS` (2000), `bidBoardMapTourStops(legend)` (the sections with pins, in board order — an empty section is skipped rather than shown as two seconds of blank map), `bidBoardMapTourNext(current, stops)` (wraps; a standing start or a stop that emptied out restarts at the first), `bidBoardMapTourVisibility(section)` (that section on, the rest off). Unit tests in `bidBoardMap.test.ts`.
- [`BidBoardMapCard`](../../src/components/bids/BidBoardMapCard.tsx): a **Play** button (outline, play glyph) right after the title (the v2.3205 title toggle), shown when at least two sections have pins. Play puts the first stop alone on the map and clears the pin selection; a per-view `setTimeout` (not an interval, so a stop that gains or loses pins mid-tour — the geocoder placing the rest — is seen at the next step) advances the view every two seconds. The lit chip fills with its section color, white text and dot, `aria-current="true"`, and a 3px white underline (`.bid-board-map-tour-fill`, `src/index.css`) runs across it over the two seconds — keyed on the section so it restarts at every stop; `prefers-reduced-motion` gets a static full underline. The map re-frames per view because the home fit already follows the visible pins.
- **Pause holds the view**: the button flips to Pause while running; pressing it leaves the current section alone on the map with nothing lit. A chip click while playing pauses first, then toggles as usual; Hide map pauses too. Fewer than two stops (every other section toggled off, or pins still resolving) stops the tour.
- Render test: Play → Unsent alone, lit → +2 s Pending (Won / Started with no pins skipped) → +2 s Lost (a section that starts hidden still gets its turn) → wraps to Unsent; Pause holds; a chip click while playing pauses and toggles. And no Play with a single populated section.
