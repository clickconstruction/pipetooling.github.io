# v2.4700 — Needs you: vehicles missing insurance, registration or service (2026-10-06)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. The owner's ask, 2026-10-06: a vehicle lacking insurance, registration or service shows on the Needs you card for devs, assistants and controllers. It came out of punch list #52: People → Review prices a company truck's fixed costs from these, and the one company-truck deal had none on file, so its line read $0.

## The owner's choices

- **Which vehicles:** active ones, the fleet board's rule. A person holds it today; the motor pool and unassigned vehicles wait for a holder.
- **When one shows:** when any one of the three is missing; the card says which.
- **Service:** missing only when no service event was ever entered.

## The change

- **`lib/vehicleRecordGaps.ts`** (pure): `vehicleRecordGaps` — per active vehicle, what is missing.
  - *Insurance*: no weekly cost counts today. The vehicle is off a plan, or on one at $0, by the app's own rule (`effectiveWeeklyInsuranceCost`).
  - *Registration*: `weekly_registration_cost` is $0 or blank.
  - *Service*: no `vehicle_service_events` row ever.
  - Most missing first, then by name. `vehicleRecordGapWords` joins the parts with *and*, or *or* after a "no". A vehicle on a plan at $0 is insured, so its part reads *insurance cost*, not *insurance* (`insuranceOnPlan`). The live check found exactly this: a company truck sits on an insurance plan with no weekly cost on it.
- **`hooks/useVehicleRecordGapsNudge.ts`**: the reads, as the viewer. Dev, assistant and controller pass the vehicle tables' "Pay access users" policies.
  - Holds and insurance periods are read only where they reach today.
  - Service is read only for the vehicles a person holds, paged past PostgREST's 1,000-row cap.
  - Holder names come from `users`.
  - The hook is null while loading, failed or empty, and refetches on focus. `loadVehicleRecordGaps` takes its client, so a test hands in a fake.
- **`lib/dashboardNeedsYou.ts`**: a new key, `vehicle-records-missing`.
  - It sits in the hygiene tier (60), gray, kicker *Vehicles*, with action **Open Vehicles**.
  - One vehicle reads *The 2016 Ford F-250 has no insurance cost, registration or service on file*, and the detail names who drives it.
  - Several list the first three, each with its parts, then *And N more*.
  - Both add that Review prices a company truck from these, so a missing one counts as $0 there.
- **`DashboardPinnedQuickRow`**: the gate is `role === 'dev' || isAssistantLike(role)` (assistant, controller). The button opens `/people?tab=vehicles`.
- **`QuickfillNeedsYouSection`**: the same gate, hook and click (the owner asked for it on Quickfill too, 2026-10-06), so Quickfill's Needs you carries the card as well.

Help guides: *work the Needs you list on the dashboard* (the card, where its button goes, what each part means on People → Vehicles), *manage company vehicles* (a pointer to the card). `docs/DASHBOARD_SECTIONS_ARCHITECTURE.md` gains the builder row.
