# v2.4050 — GC Review: print where the checks went, or download it as a CSV (2026-09-28)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. PR 4 of "Where the checks went" (after [v2.4043](v2.4043.md) the kernel, [v2.4044](v2.4044.md) the line under each bill, [v2.4046](v2.4046.md) Find a check). The third shape of the GC's question — *"send us everything since June for our audit"*.

## What shipped

- **`src/lib/jobsDocuments/gcChecksAppliedReport.ts`** (7 tests) — `buildGcChecksAppliedReportHtml(gcName, report, { asOfYmd })`, in the GC statement print's mold: the title (*Structura — where your checks were applied*), the period line (*Since Sep 28, 2025 · as of Sep 28, 2026 · Click Plumbing and Electrical*), a summary strip (payments received, applied to N invoices on M jobs, received but not yet applied, still open and of it the retainage held), **Each payment, and where it sits now** (payment with its mailed / deposited stamps, received, amount, one line per job and invoice with *paid in full* / *job paid in full*, the unapplied remainder as *not yet applied — tell us the invoice*, and the *was on* column), **Where each job stands** (billed, paid by, last applied, retainage held, still open; the open total matches the statement), and a line counting the earlier payments the period leaves out. `buildGcChecksAppliedCsv(report)`: one row per applied line, thirteen columns, quoted where needed, BOM-prefixed at the call site so Excel reads it. `gcChecksCsvFileName`.
- **`GcFindCheckModal`** — under the search box, the sheet's line (*The sheet: 8 payments since Sep 28, 2025 · $113,700.00 · show all 14*), **🖨 Print the sheet** (`openHtmlPrintWindow`; a blocked pop-up says so) and **CSV** (a blob download). The default period is the last twelve months (`addDaysYmd(today, -365)`); *show all* widens it; the search always reads every payment.
- Guide *run your weekly GC statement round* → Find a check; GLOSSARY and the GC Review map carry the sheet.

## Decided here

- **One door, two outputs.** The sheet lives inside Find a check rather than as a second Share-menu line: the person printing it is the person who was just asked about a check.
- **Twelve months by default, everything on request** — a bookkeeper reconciles a year at a time; the job table always counts every payment so billed − paid = open holds whatever the period.
- **Print all (every GC at once) is not built** — it would read every GC's jobs in one go; the ask was per GC.
