# v2.7633 — Estimates: the draft editor's pieces leave the page (2026-11-17)

Client only; no migration, no edge function, no screen change. Punch list **#46** row 3 (`to-dos/decomposition-queue.md`): cut 8 of eight on the Estimates page, its second and last PR (`docs/ESTIMATES_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md`, order step 7). Stacked on v2.7629.

## What shipped

- **`EstimateDraftCustomerSection.tsx`** — the customer on a draft: the search box, and under it the picked customer's email or the Send to box, phone, the Edit customer door and their notes.
- **`EstimateChangeOrderFields.tsx`** — a change order's own fields: what is changing, why, the impact on schedule, the day a response is asked by.
- **`EstimateDraftLineItems.tsx`** — Line items (Impact on cost on a change order): the note a bridged change order carries, the heading with the recent picks, the catalog window, the guided added-work and credit presses, a row per line, the add-line and catalog presses, and the total. The two prompt styles moved with it.
- **`EstimateSentHeader.tsx`** — the top of a sent, declined or superseded estimate: the For line, the logo, the good-through line, a change order's fields, and the options or the lines.
- **`Estimates.tsx`** — mounts the four. 1,781 lines (was 2,393): 612 out.
- **The smoke** — its 24 cases pass on the moved page; three reads were added to them (the customer's name and phone, Add line, a change order's description and its Impact on cost heading).

All four are in `src/components/estimates/`, and the markup of each is the page's own, moved by script.

## Why each is fully controlled

The page's `load()` blanks the whole editor, and Save draft runs it. So nothing a section must keep can live in the section: the customer's notes, the open Send to box, the catalog's open state and the line being focused all stay in the page and are handed in. The customer section takes 33 values that way. That is the cost of the reload, not of the cut; a quiet reload would let each piece own its own state, and it is a change in behaviour, so it is the owner's call.

## What was not made

An `EstimateDraftEditor` wrapper around what is left of the editor. What is left is the customer view, the title editor, the For and Expires slots, the attachment card, Terms and the button row, each a few lines over the page's own state. A wrapper would only pass all of it through.

`src/pages/Estimates.tsx` was 5,423 lines at the start of the day and is 1,781: the router, and the detail page's wiring (the rail, the gates, the customer and option handlers, the send flow, the previews' memos, the windows).
