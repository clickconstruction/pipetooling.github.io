Taunya opened Print blank stickers on job 224 on 2026-11-15, saw strips that read 0003 with the sample code 0000-0000, and was shown that the names were built in the database (PR 7, v2.7312) and on no screen. Her go: "Build PR 8-10, let me know when it's live". Client only: no migration, no edge function. `mint_sticker_run` has taken `p_names` since v2.7312.

## What changed

- **The Names field.** The window's ask opens on one typed field, **Names**, filled with the site's next free name once the site's boxes are read (`nextFreeName`, the rule the Add sheet offers by). `G78` with How many 200 is G78 to G277; a typed range, `G78-300`, sets the count, How many shows it and its presses rest. The line under the field says what was read, and a refusal is said there in the parser's words with the press dead. Cleared, the run carries numbers only, as before. `stickerNamesAsk` in `stickerNames.ts` is the read; the window holds no rule of its own.
- **Two notices, one hold.** `liveNamesNotice`: names that are live boxes on the site are the backfill, said in green and never held. `blankNamesNotice`: names an earlier run printed that are on no box and not lost hold the press, the line says which names, and **Print them anyway** prints the run as asked. The site is the job's development, every job of it, as the box rule reads a site; `fetchSiteStickerNames` in `stickerRunsIo.ts` reads its boxes and its blank named stickers under the caller's own RLS.
- **The press and the mint.** *Print 223 stickers, G78 to G300* (`stickerRunPressLabel` takes the names). `stickerRunRequest` carries the names and takes the count from them; `mintStickerRunArgs` sends `p_names` only when the run has them, so a numbered run calls as it always did.
- **The strip.** A named sticker prints its name where the number printed, and its number small on a line of its own over the address (`tapeStripLines` takes the number; `stickerStrip` hands it). Up to four characters print at the stencil's full size, five to seven at five sevenths of it (`TAPE_NAME_SMALL_RATIO`, `tapeStencilPx`). On 18 mm tape the three small lines do not fit under a full-size name at the old spacing, so `tapeStripBlock` closes them up, six dots under the name and four between lines, and the name keeps its size. The print-window strip draws the same lines and is cut for the name's room (`stripNameChars`).
- **The preview fold** draws the run's names with its real numbers (`stickerPreview` takes the names). **The Runs fold** titles a named run by its names, *Run of 223 · G78 to G300*, with *numbers 0225 to 0447* on the line under, and hands the names back to Reprint and Print the rest. The window's title, its count line, *Print the rest* and the From/to range read by name (`stickerSpanWords`, `stickerReadName`). **The CSV** leads with a Name column, empty on a numbered run.
- The site guide's **Print blank stickers**, the map's window paragraph and the Glossary's sticker sentence.

## The number's place: the picture and the pick

Section 10 put the number "small before the address", drawn when the address was one line of screen type. The address is two lines of a dot font now (v2.7373, v2.7377), so three ways were drawn through the real strip and shown to Taunya with their lengths, made-up names and codes:

| The number | G78, 24 mm | G120, 24 mm | a seven-character name, 24 mm | G78, 18 mm | G120, 18 mm | seven characters, 18 mm |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A · its own line, above the address | 43.2 mm | 49.0 mm | 57.0 mm | 36.5 mm | 41.6 mm | 48.3 mm |
| B · on the first address line | 49.0 mm | 49.0 mm | 57.0 mm | 44.6 mm | 44.6 mm | 48.3 mm |
| C · beside the name | 51.2 mm | 56.9 mm | 64.8 mm | 44.4 mm | 49.4 mm | 56.0 mm |

A numbered sticker, 0159, is 46.7 mm on 24 mm tape and 39.7 on 18. **A is built**, this session's pick: the number is four characters and never the widest line, so it adds no length, where B makes every short name as long as its first address line and C adds about 8 mm to every strip. She had not picked when the rest of the PR was ready; B or C is a change to `tapeStripCanvas.ts` alone.

## The picks

- **The press waits for the site's names.** While they are read the press is dead and the line says *Reading the site's names…*, so nobody prints a numbered run by pressing before the field fills. A read that fails says so, offers no name and checks nothing; a typed range still prints.
- **Print them anyway prints.** The mock-up's second press only lifted the hold. A press that says *Print* prints, so this one mints the run, and the dead press beside it names the count and the range.
- **The Runs fold keeps the numbers on a named run's by-line**, and its *not used yet* count still reads by number. Section 10 keeps the number where it carries news, and a blank sticker in the fold is one of those places.
- **The window is still called Print blank stickers.** The card's plan says the press reads *Print stickers*; the name is on two strips, a guide heading and its links, and a rename is its own small change.
- **Print labels is untouched.** `tapeStripLines` and `TapeStripBitmap` take the number as an optional argument a box's label never passes, and the tests hold that a box's two lines and their spacing are what they were.
