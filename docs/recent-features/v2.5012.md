# v2.5012 — Bids: a labor row whose hours were cleared still shows what they were (2026-10-09)

Client only. No migration, no edge function. This builds the owner's call of 2026-10-09 on punch list #73 ([`to-dos/bid-history`](../../to-dos/bid-history/README.md)). The question was *a labor row whose hours were wiped hides its past*, and the owner chose (a): show the past line on the needs-hours rows too.

## The gap

Past values (v2.4952) drew a stage's earlier hours under its box only in the Labor table's *rows with hours*. A row whose hours are cleared leaves that table for the *rows need hours* list (`BidsLaborNewView`'s queue). The queue drew its own boxes with no past under them. So the earlier hours vanished exactly when they would help. The walk's case was ZZ Test's WH, 2.25 h → 0 h on 2026-10-07. The History window still listed the change with Put back.

## The change

- **The queue** (`src/components/bids/BidsLaborNewView.tsx`): each stage's box now draws `BidCellPast` under it, the same cell keys as the filled rows (`laborCellKeys(row.id, stage, fixture)`).
  - A box with no past draws nothing, so rows keep their height.
  - A re-imported row with no past of its own borrows the removed row's hours by name, in italics.
  - *+N more* opens History searched on the row.
- A sub line has no boxes, so it draws none, as in the table.
