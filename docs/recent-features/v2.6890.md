# v2.6890 — Materials desk: the three presses over a job's boxes are one family (2026-11-10)

The owner, with a job open on Materials → On site and **Add a container**, **Print labels** and **Print the manifest** in view: *can we make these three buttons look better on this screen?* They were three 36 px outlined boxes on a screen of pills (the flow row, List and Map, the filter chips), heavier than anything near them and dropped to a row of their own.

## What changed

- **`lib/materials/materialsPressStyle.ts`** + **`components/materials/MaterialsPress.tsx`** (new): the pill and the glyphs the Materials strip drew for Scan a label and Print labels (v2.6851), lifted out so a second screen can wear them. Two glyphs join the printer and the scan frame: a plus and a page with its lines.
- **`JobsMaterialsTab`**: the three presses are pills at the filter chips' height (32 px) with a glyph each, in a group pushed to the right of the chips' row; they wrap under the chips, still to the right, when the row is short. A print press with no box to print wears the dead-press grey. Names, order and what each opens are unchanged.
- **`MaterialsGroupStrip`**: draws its two presses from the shared file.

## The call made

A chip filters and a glyph acts. All three stay outlined: a filled press here would outshout the counts, which are what the screen is for.
