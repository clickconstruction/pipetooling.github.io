# v2.7582 — Materials on site: send someone to a box as a task (2026-11-17)

Punch list #372, *Find a lost box*, step 5 of 5. The owner's word of 2026-11-17: *Build all the steps*. This is the last step of the train.

## Why

**Where is it?** serves the person who can open the box: the job's crew and the office. The person sent to fetch a box is often neither, such as a driver or a helper from another job. A task already carries a place (#328) and a painted map (v2.7340), and whoever reads the task reads both. So the box's sheet makes the task.

## What ships

**Send someone to it** on the **Where is it?** sheet, on the phone and in the office's box window. The host closes its own sheet or window and opens the New task window with:

- **The words**: *Find A7 at Laydown yard, by the north fence*. A box with no place reads *Find A7, by the north fence*, or *Find A7*.
- **The place**, when the box is pinned: a dot at the pin, named *A7 at Laydown yard*, with the pin's margin.
- **The map**, when the box's place has a painted way (v2.7581): that map, as the way in to the task's place.

The sender picks the person, the day and anything else in the window as it is, and sends. Nothing is sent by the press itself.

`findBoxTask` in [`whereIsBox.ts`](../../src/lib/materials/whereIsBox.ts) builds it. No table, no policy and no migration: the task's own guard lets a task point at a map its giver can open, and the giver reads the box's job.

## The New task window kept a place and dropped its way

The walk found it. The window keeps a task's map apart from its place, and a window opened on a place set the map to none. So the place arrived and its way did not, and **Send** then saved the place with no map. This reached a follow-up task too (punch list #369), which opens on the place of the task it follows.

`ChecklistAddModal` now applies the window's own rule when it opens on a place, `taskMapAfterPlace`: a place that brings a way in makes that the task's map. The place chip wears the way's mark, and Send saves `painted_map_id`.

## Walked

On a dev server against the live database, in a headless Chromium at 390 wide, as the dev account, on a test job's test cage in a made-up place with a short made-up way painted to it. **Send someone to it** opened the New task window on *Find* the cage *at* the place, with the place chip lit and, after the fix above, wearing the way's mark with the way's name in its tip. The task was not sent. The made-up way was thrown away after, and the cage's pin taken off. The made-up place stays on the test job, since a place cannot be removed in the app.

## The train, walked

This is the last step, so the walk of the whole train is set down here. Once steps 1 to 3 were deployed, the sheet and the pin ask were walked **on the live site** at 390 wide, as the dev account, on a test job's test cage: a box nobody had pinned, **I found it**, **Show where I am** from 240 ft away, **Move the pin by hand**, **Remove the pin**, and the ask on a box opened through its label's address. Steps 4 and 5 were walked on a dev server against the live database, as their fragments say, with a second pass for the office's **＋ Paint the way here** and **Take the way off**, the ask on a new box, and a helper's search for *paint* through **View as**. Every test row was put back but one made-up place, which the app cannot remove. Not walked: the ask after a photo, dark mode, and a real phone.

## What the person given the task sees

The task's card, as any task with a place: the dot, **Directions**, **How far am I?**, and the line of the painted way with **Open the map**. They do not see the box's contents, its photo or its other boxes. Those stay behind the job's own gate.

## Not here

- A box nobody pinned makes a task with words and no dot. The first person to find it pins it with **I found it**, if they can open the box.
- The task does not close itself when the box is pinned. The doer ticks it.
- A mark that says a whole box is lost, a route worked out along painted lines, a link for a driver who is not signed in and a tag on the box are the card's calls 5, 4 and 9, each left for after the train.
- Nothing here has been tried on a real phone.
