# v2.5866 — What customers see: the sample portals' remaining sections, through their kernels (2026-10-25)

What customers see #103 (`to-dos/customer-samples-through-kernels.md`), PR 3 of 4 — the last PR of the train, built after PRs 1 (v2.5105), 2 (v2.5110) and 4 (v2.5112) merged. No migration. One edge function's bundle changes: `customer-portal`. With this PR the to-do is retired: every sample section a real portal can show appears on a sample at least once, built through the real kernels (#62's rule, GLOSSARY → *What customers see (Settings)*).

## Before

The real `customer-portal` answer always sent six keys the samples left out — `sharedBills`, `propertyNotices`, `checks`, `promise`, `ownerRecords` and `gcJobs` (the owner sample sent `gcJobs` alone, written by hand). PR 2 took `sharedBills` and `checks`; this PR takes the rest. The visit picker's properties, the test-report titles and the agreements' signer names were hand-written strings beside their kernels.

## What changed (`supabase/functions/_shared/customerSampleFixtures.ts`)

- **The notice on your property** (v2.3825): the homeowner sample gains a third job, *Primary bath remodel* (Job 0989, billed to a GC, nothing billed yet, so it adds nothing to the money sections), and `sampleNoticeFilings` holds the recorded § 53.056 filing about it — the claim as printed ($8,900), two months, the owner send eight days back. The card is `buildPortalPropertyNotices`' output on those rows, the read a real portal makes of `job_lien_filings`.
- **Properties to ask about** (v2.2037): every sample's `requestableProperties` is `buildPortalProperties` on its jobs. The homeowner's three jobs share one address, so the picker still offers one row; the owner sample, which sent `[]`, now offers its property, as a real owner portal does.
- **Test report titles** (v2.3304): the fixture rows carry `testType`/`system`, and `reportLabel`/`title` are `testReportShortLabel`/`testReportTitle` on them — the same strings as before, no longer hand copies.
- **Agreement signer lines** (v2.4596): each sample agreement is a one-frame `SignerFramesRow`; `signerName` is `signerNamesLine(row)` and the new `signingProgress` key is `framesWaitingLine(row) || null`, as the function computes them.
- **The promise** (Their Word PR 2, no kernel — the function reads `job_promised_pay_dates`): the homeowner's statement says they told us to expect payment a week out, `{ promisedYmd, source: 'customer' }` in the answer's shape. The GC and owner send `promise: null`.
- **Records for an owner** (punch list #86, no kernel — the function reads the request's `file` column): the owner sample sends a request offered three days back and signed here yesterday, not yet sent, in the answer's shape. The others send `ownerRecords: null`.
- **The owner's GC jobs** (Owner Billing O7c): `sampleOwnerGcRows` holds the project, its stage, the change order, our contract's send and the job's terms in `loadGcPortalJobs`' read shapes, and the job card is `gcPortalJobs`' output on them — the contract to sign ($187,000, 10% retainage stepping to 5% at half done, 30 pay days), change order 2 and *Accept the work* (the fixture's one progress pay application covers the price today, the kernel's own rule for the press). The schedule and the page are laid on after the read, as `customer-portal` lays them. The homeowner and GC samples send `gcJobs: []`, as the live branch does for them.

## Held to the kernels

`src/lib/portal/samplePortalSections.test.ts` runs each kernel on the sample's own rows and compares with the function's answer (notices, properties, test-report labels, signer lines, the owner's GC job), and parses every sample through `parsePortalPayload`. `samplePortalMoney.test.ts` (PR 2) still holds the money: the notice job never reaches a bill, check or waiver.

## Deploy

PRs 1, 2 and 4's functions were already carried live by later trains. This PR changes `customerSampleFixtures.ts` and so the bundle of every function that imports it; the train's closing deploy run redeploys the ones `npm run check:edge-drift` names after the merge (`customer-portal` first among them — the only one whose answer changes).
