# v2.6538 — The markup on materials shows its margin beside it, and either one can be typed (2026-11-05)

Micah, 2026-11-05, after seeing the markup field in Settings and in a job's Charges window: "in every area where someone can set 'Markup on materials' I would like to be able to side by that set true margin so updating one updates both and a user can choose how they want to enter it but see both values." The markup had been one box with a sentence under it reading the number both ways (v2.6441); a person who thinks in margin had to work the markup out first. No migration; nothing to deploy.

## What changed

- `src/lib/charges/markupMargin.ts`: the kernel. `markupFromMargin` (to two places; null at or past 100%), `marginFromMarkup` (to one place, through `marginOfMarkup`), `pairFromMarkup` (the pair a stored markup draws), `pairTyped(side, raw)` (the other box follows what was typed, or the typed box refuses with `MARKUP_REFUSAL` / `MARGIN_REFUSAL`), and `markupMarginWords` (`25% over cost, a 20% margin on the sale`). The markup stays the stored number, so nothing changes in the database or in what a charges paper prices.
- `src/components/charges/MaterialsMarkupPair.tsx`: the one control every door draws, two number boxes with an `=` between them, `onTyped` on every keystroke for a door that saves with a press and `onCommit` on blur or Enter for a door that saves as you pick, never while a box refuses. On a job door both placeholders name the company's figures (`Company: 25` and `Company: 20`), or `at cost` when the company has none; the company's own door reads `not set`.
- Under the boxes, the bar (`markupBar(cost, markupPct)` in the kernel): the cost in grey and the markup in green as parts of one bar, `the customer pays $1,250.00` over it and `cost, 80% of the sale · margin, 20% of the sale` under it. Micah's second ask the same afternoon, after the screenshots: "make these areas more attractive and do a better job showing instead of telling." The bar draws the markup that stands: what is typed, else the company's on a job door, else none (all cost, `no markup`). It starts from `barCost` where the door has one (the Charges window passes the job's materials cost and names its lines) and from $1,000 of materials otherwise. While a box refuses, the refusal takes the bar's place.
- Settings → Jobs & billing → Markup on materials (`MaterialsMarkupSettingsBlock.tsx`) holds the pair's state and saves the markup it stands for; the press is dead while a box refuses or nothing changed. The long paragraph that explained the number is cut to one line; the bar shows it. Beside a dead press: `The markup is saved as shown.`, or `No markup is set. Materials on a charges paper are at cost until one is.`
- The Charges window (`ChargesWindow.tsx`) and Edit Job → Charging the customer (`JobFormChargingRow.tsx`) draw the compact pair in place of the one box, the label saying whose markup stands (`the company’s`, `this job’s own`, `none`). The row's collapsed value and `jobMarkupWords` read the margin too: `materials 25% over cost, a 20% margin on the sale, the company’s markup`.
- The guide *set what a crew's hour is charged* (`charge-by-crew.md`) describes the two boxes at each door.
