# v2.5120 — The Dashboard's money leaves out ZZ test jobs (2026-10-09)

Punch list #61, PR 2a of 3 (`to-dos/zz-test-jobs-hide.md`). The lead approved the plan on 2026-10-09: the shared ZZ id read, 2a (the switch and the Dashboard's money), then 2b (the other doors). The client only: no migration, no edge function. PR 1 was v2.5116.

## What shipped

- **The Dashboard's money, as the office reads it.** For every role but a dev who shows them, three Dashboard surfaces leave out ZZ test jobs, their bills and their payments. Each figure falls by exactly the test money, and no real job's figure changes. The Pipeline strip, Quickfill and the Dashboard agree again.
  - **The Billed pin** (`useBilledTotal`). Its read carries no names, so the shared ids drop the jobs and their bills before the payments and the unlinked money are read; their money is never fetched. Dashboard.tsx passes `useZzTestJobsHidden(role)` and the signed-in user as the new third and fourth arguments. A failed id read leaves the pin empty rather than counting the test money.
  - **The AR / Unbilled card** (`useDashboardFinancials`). The jobs and bills drop right after the read, so no ZZ bill is ever a billed id and its payments and unlinked money are never read. The dev-only Bridge reads the same hook and follows the dev's switch.
  - **The Ready to bill / Billed lists** (`useDashboardBillingInvoices`). The four list states keep every row they read and hand out the rows without ZZ jobs. So every load path, the refresh and the units built from them follow one rule, and a flip needs no read.
    - **Held until the ids land** (review on #5241). An RPC job row carries no customer name, so until the shared ids arrive both lists hand out nothing and read as loading and not loaded. So the Day book's queue recorder never counts a ZZ customer's job. A failed read keeps them held and says so: *Could not check for ZZ test jobs, so Ready to bill and Billed are held.* with **Try again** (`useZzTestJobIds`: two quiet retries, then failed).
    - A bill drops by its job's names.
    - An RPC job row (`get_jobs_ledger_by_status`, which returns no customer name) drops by the shared ids. That closes the gap the plan named without a migration.
- **The shared ZZ id read (`src/lib/jobs/zzTestJobRows.ts`).** PR 1's fifth read moved here, for every reader.
  - Its server pattern takes a name that starts with ZZ, or one character and then ZZ (`job_name.ilike.zz*,job_name.ilike._zz*,customer_name.ilike.zz*,customer_name.ilike._zz*`; `_` is LIKE's one-character wildcard). The kernel re-checks every row, so a name with a leading space is caught on both sides, which settles the review's trim nit. It no longer pages every Pizza and Jazz (review on #5241). A name with two leading spaces is caught where a screen reads names, not by this read.
  - The sweep keeps its own strict filter, so its counts do not move.
  - `loadZzTestJobRows(userId)` shares one answer per signed-in user for 60 seconds, however many Dashboard cards ask. Failures are never kept. RLS scopes the read, so the answer read as a sample assistant (View as) is never the dev's after Exit, nor anyone's after sign-out (review on #5241).
  - **The sweep clears it** (`invalidateZzTestJobRows`, called after each job it moves). A ZZ job created in the same session can still show on the Dashboard until the minute lapses. The Pipeline is not affected, because it reads names off its own rows.
  - The Pipeline strip keeps its uncached read (`fetchZzTestJobRows`), since its stats carry their own 60-second freshness.
- **The dev's switch (`src/lib/jobs/zzTestJobSwitch.ts`).** It is per device (`jobs-stages-show-zz`) and hidden by default. `useZzTestJobsHidden(role)` reads it through `useSyncExternalStore`, so a flip moves every reader in the tab at once (and in other tabs via the `storage` event). `hidesZzTestJobs(role, devShowsZz)` ignores it for every other role.
  - **The jobs cache** keeps its single choke point. The rows follow a flip with no read, and the strip's stats are read again under a key that carries the choice. It exposes `zzTestJobCount`, the ZZ jobs it holds. A stats read begun before a flip is dropped when it lands and read again under the new choice (review on #5241).
  - **On the Pipeline** (a dev only, while the board holds a ZZ job and always while the switch shows them, so a dev can turn it back off once none are left; review on #5241):
    - Hide groups… gains a **Test jobs (dev only)** line, *ZZ test jobs · N jobs · Hidden/Hide*.
    - The command bar gains a chip: quiet *N ZZ test jobs hidden* while hidden, amber *ZZ test jobs shown* while shown. Either one opens Hide groups….
  - **Quickfill's two money readers** follow the switch too.
- **Docs.** BILLING_FLOWS (One bill truth) and GLOSSARY (ZZ convention) are amended in place. The card's status says 2a shipped and that 2b is next.

## Proof

- **`zzTestJobVisibility.test.ts`, 13 tests.**
  - The role × switch matrix: a dev is hidden by default and shown by the switch; no other role is ever shown.
  - `withoutZzTestJobRows` by name and by ids.
  - A payment drops with its bill when it has no job id.
  - The AR card's buckets fall by exactly the $2,200 test bill.
- **`zzTestJobRows.test.ts`, 5 tests.** The pattern and the kernel re-check (a leading-space name is kept, a stray hit is dropped). One read per minute per user, a second read after the lapse, a fresh read for each other user (View as, Exit, sign-out), a fresh read after invalidate, and a failure that is not cached.
- **`zzTestJobSwitch.test.ts`, 4 tests.** Hidden by default. A flip moves every reader, including a flip from another tab. An assistant is never shown. The Pipeline's line stays while the switch shows them, even with no ZZ job left.
- **`JobsListCacheContext.render.test.tsx`, 7 tests (3 new).**
  - A dev's default hides the jobs and counts them. A flip shows them with no second read and reads the stats again with `excludeZzTestJobs: false`; flipping back hides them.
  - A stray key on an assistant's device shows nothing.
  - A flip while the stats read is out drops that answer and reads again under the new choice.
- **Render tests on the Pipeline.** `JobsStagesCommandBar.render.test.tsx` covers the chip in both states and that it opens Hide groups. `JobsStagesHideGroupsModal.render.test.tsx` shows the line only when passed, and its press reports the flip.
- **`useBilledTotal.render.test.tsx`, new, 4 tests.** Without the option, the pin reads $2,600 and never asks for the ids. With it, the pin reads $500, the ids are read for the signed-in user, and the unlinked-money read is asked about the real job only. A failed id read leaves the pin empty.
- **`useDashboardBillingInvoices.zz.render.test.tsx`, new, 3 tests.** While the ids load, both lists are empty, loading and not loaded. Once they land, a ZZ customer's job and its bill are dropped and the real ones kept. After a failure the lists stay held and the Try again message shows.
- **`useZzTestJobIds.render.test.tsx`, new, 3 tests.** Off with no read for a role that sees ZZ jobs. Loading, then ready for the user. Two retries, then failed until Try again.
- **`fetchStagesHeaderStats.test.ts`** pins the shared read's loose pattern.
- The related suites, eslint, the theme-token check and the full typecheck pass.

## To see it

On the dev server, sign in as an assistant (`/dev-login?as=<assistant>`). The Dashboard's Billed pin and AR card match the Pipeline strip. As the dev, the Pipeline shows *N ZZ test jobs hidden*. Hide groups → ZZ test jobs → **Hide** shows them, the chip turns amber, and the Dashboard's figures move with it on that device.
