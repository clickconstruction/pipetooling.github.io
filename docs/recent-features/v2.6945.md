# v2.6945 — The dev seat changes a bid: plan_bid_update and apply_bid_update (2026-11-11)

Asked by the owner on 2026-11-11, preparing to enter bids for proposals that went out months ago: "I would like to add verbs to not only do those things you mentioned it cannot do, but fill out all of the line items for counts, takeoffs, labor, pricing." The seat could make a bid and read one. It could not change one, so an old proposal could not carry its amount, its sent date or its outcome. This is PR 1 of the bid train, mapped in `to-dos/mcp-servers.md` → The bid train.

## What was added

One plan → apply pair on `dev-mcp` (eighty-three write verbs). No wrapper RPC and no migration: the write is the one Edit Bid makes, as the dev, so the table's RLS and triggers hold.

- **`plan_bid_update` / `apply_bid_update`** (`supabase/functions/_shared/devMcpBidUpdate.ts`): `update = { bid_id, set: { column: value } }` over the columns `buildBidSavePayload` saves, plus the decided day.
- **The write** is one PATCH on `bids` under RLS, read back to say what landed. When the outcome moved, the apply then inserts the line Edit Bid leaves on `bids_submission_entries`, built by the form's own `buildOutcomeChangeBidNoteBody` from the kernel copy.
- **What rides** is written with its driver, as the form's payload derives it and `bidUpdatePrune.ts` groups it. A bid that stops being lost drops `loss_category` and `loss_reason`. One that stops being won drops the alternates taken. A cleared due date drops its time. A GC change nulls `gc_builder_id`. A cleared sent date nulls the eight checklist stamps.
- **The plan** is the server's read of the bid, whether it has versions, the jobs that point at it and each row a new id names. It shows each column before and after, drops a column that already holds its value and refuses a plan that changes nothing. The hash commits to the before values and `updated_at`.
- **What follows the write** is said in the plan: the Board section the bid moves to, the decided day, the labor rows the database mints on a first sent date, the robot shadow scored once a sent bid has a value, which columns History records, and that a win opens no job.

## Three choices worth knowing

1. **The decided day is `outcome_at`.** No screen has a field for it. The trigger `bids_stamp_outcome_at` (migration `20260911235622`) stamps now when the outcome moves and keeps an explicit write, which its own comment calls a back-dated decision. The seat writes the day at noon in the app's calendar. Left out, the database stamps now and the plan says so.
2. **The sent date carries no checklist.** Edit Bid asks three lines before a new sent date and stamps eight columns with the person's word. The seat never gives that word. The date lands as the Cover Letter's Mark sent lands it on a bid with no versions: `bid_date_sent` alone. A date changed here leaves a person's earlier stamps in place and the plan warns they were given for the old date.
3. **A bid with versions is refused its own Sent and Won / Lost.** There the two are recorded per GC and the bid's own are roll-ups, kept by trigger. Edit Bid locks them, so the plan refuses them and names the panel. Open and Started or Complete stay settable, as on the form.

## What the screen does that the verb does not

The sent checklist. Which alternates they took and which option of the letter won. A job, a hand-off to Dispatch or any notice. Per-GC sends and outcomes. The robot's envelope and its revision note on a bid a robot shadowed. The bid number. The bid's counts, takeoff, labor and prices, which are the train's next pairs.

## Kernel copy

`scripts/edge-kernels.mjs` gains three entries the seat imports by path: `lib/outcomeChangeBidNote.ts`, `lib/bidLossCategories.ts` and `lib/bids/submissionSections.ts`. The index exports nothing of them. Its header comment lists the files, so the functions that import the index read as changed until they are deployed again.

## Tests

`src/lib/devMcp/devMcpBidUpdate.test.ts`, 23 tests on a made-up bid. They hold the verb to the app: every column exists on `bids` in the generated catalog, the verb covers every column the form saves but the bid number, last contact and the alternates, the outcomes match the table's check, the stamps match the checklist's, the Board's headings match the tab's, and the History columns match `bid_changes_bid_columns()`.
