# v2.4442 — Submittals: Their answer, a window of its own with one answer per part (2026-10-02)

Built from the mock-up drawn the same day ([`to-dos/submittals/their-answer-before-after.html`](../../to-dos/submittals/their-answer-before-after.html), canvas https://claude.ai/artifact/JSuCp3iTjiDgejAdVj8Brm). Wendi on BP375, rejecting one flush valve out of five parts: "it is not clear that it is just for a record and will not contact anyone, I am scared to add their email here". The owner asked of the section *is this built well, is this the best we can do*, then: "Build it". One migration ([`20261002211435`](../migrations/20261002211435_bid_submittal_people_email_optional.md)); two edge functions to redeploy (`submit-submittal-review`, `send-submittal-reply-email`).

## The six findings, and what answers each

| Finding (the old section, at the foot of the row editor) | Now |
|---|---|
| It sat at the bottom of another job: under the parts, status, note, lead time and cut sheet | A window of its own, [`SubmittalAnswerDialog`](../../src/components/bids/SubmittalAnswerDialog.tsx), opened by **Their answer** on every row |
| It asked for a person and an email; she knew a company | **Who answered** opens on the bid's GC, then the GC's contacts on file, then *Someone else…* with a name and an optional email |
| One answer per Save | One line per part the GC sees, each with its own Approved / Revise / Rejected and note; one Save writes them all |
| Tick boxes hid what each part already had; taking an answer back cleared the whole row | Each line opens on the call the part carries and who made it; a tap on an entered answer takes that one back |
| The reason Save was grey sat away from Save and from the boxes | The reason sits under the picker; the button says what it will do (*Record 5 answers*, *Take back 1 answer*, *Nothing to save*) |
| The words were ours | *What did they say?*, *Who answered*, *The day they answered* |

## What changed

- **Kernel** [`answerEntry.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/answerEntry.ts): `answerLines` (the GC's parts in order, or the row itself), drafts that start on the current calls, `tapAnswer`, `approveOpenLines`, `clearLines`, and `answerWrites`, which returns only the lines that changed, grouped by answer and note, with the parts to take back. A call made on the room or carried from an earlier revision can be changed but not emptied, as before.
- **Who answered** [`reviewerPick.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/reviewerPick.ts): `ReviewerSources` (the bid's GC name and its `customer_contact_persons`), `gcOffered` / `contactsOffered` (neither repeats a person already on the room), `reviewerChoiceFrom` (the GC is a name with no email and the role `builder`), `matchRoomPerson`. `reviewerPickBad` needs a name; an email only has to read as one when typed. The picker's label is *Who answered*, and its line says the email is optional.
- **The tab** ([`BidsSubmittalsTab`](../../src/components/bids/BidsSubmittalsTab.tsx)): `answering` state, the row button, `saveAnswer` (one `enterCallOnParts` per group, `clearEnteredCallsOnParts` with the parts named, one thread line and one `decided` event for the save, and no reviewer made when answers are only taken back), the GC's contacts read once per bid. `roomAndReviewerFor` reads the room's people fresh and matches with `matchRoomPerson`.
- **The row editor** ([`SubmittalItemEditDialog`](../../src/components/bids/SubmittalItemEditDialog.tsx)) loses the section, its eleven pieces of state and its `people` prop. It keeps one line, *Their answer: …*, and **Save and enter their answer…**, which saves the row as Save does and opens the answer window on the saved row, so no edit is left behind.
- **A fixture bought without the GC** (order only, v2.4436) gets no *Their answer* button and no answer line in its editor: nothing of it is on the GC's copy to answer.
- ***They approved all of it…*** stays the one-line entry it was (the owner's third call, taken as drawn) and gains the same *Who answered* choices.
- **Server**: `resolveIdentify` no longer assumes an email, and a visitor who arrives on the personal link of a person named without one is that person and gives it their address. `SubmittalShareModal` skips people with no email when it checks who is already on the room.
- **Types** hand-edited for `bid_submittal_people.email`, and the dev-mcp catalog rebuilt; regenerate after the push.
- **Docs**: the guide *build a submittal package*; `BIDS_SYSTEM.md`, `BIDS_DOCUMENT_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md` (SU-11), `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` (`submit-submittal-review`); the migration note.
