# v2.5839 — Quickfill: every station leads with a Next line (2026-10-25)

Change 2 of punch list #176 (`to-dos/quickfill-needs-you-review/`), after change 1 ([v2.5837](v2.5837.md)). The review found eleven stations leading with a bare count and a question (*Did every prospect hear from us on time?* over a 268 that never moved) and six with no question at all; a count does not say what to do, so it was stamped.

## What changed

- **The Next line.** Under the banner question, on the Roller, the phone round and the Page, one line names the first row to act on and its verb. The body sorts its rows and reports the line beside its count through `useReportQuickfillSectionMetric`'s new sixth argument, `nextLine`; the kernel [`lib/quickfill/nextLine.ts`](../../src/lib/quickfill/nextLine.ts) writes the words and picks the worst row where the body's order is not worst first.
