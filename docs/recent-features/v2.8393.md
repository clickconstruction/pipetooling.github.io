# v2.8393 — Jobs → Pipeline on a phone: the stage chips stay inside the page (2026-11-23)

A small fault the walks found and left: in the iOS Simulator at 393 px with the gear's **New phone layout** on, the white band behind the Pipeline's stage chips (Waiting, Working, Ready, Billed, Coll.) was wider than the page while the cards under it kept their gutter. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## The cause

`JobsStagesPhoneStrip`'s band is a flex item of the phone board's column. Two things let it outgrow that column:

- It bled past it on purpose, `margin: 0 -0.25rem`.
- Nothing kept the chip row's full width out of the widths worked out above it. The chips do not shrink, so the row's min-content is every chip side by side, about 430 px with three-digit counts. Any ancestor that sizes to its content (a flex item with `min-width: auto`, a shrink-to-fit box) is floored at that width.

A harness page with the real strip under such an ancestor reproduces it in Chromium and WebKit: the band and the page pan to 432 px at a 393 px viewport. The signed-in Pipeline was not looked at here, so which ancestor floors it in the app is not pinned down; the fix holds against any.

## What changed

- `src/lib/jobs/stagesPhoneStrip.ts` holds the head's styles. The band has no side margin, `box-sizing: border-box`, `max-width: 100%` and `min-width: 0`. The chip row scrolls sideways with `contain: inline-size`, so its width sizes nothing above it. The All / Needs me / Today row wraps.
- The chips now line up with the cards' gutter instead of standing 4 px outside it.

## Tests

- `stagesPhoneStrip.test.ts`: the band carries no side margin, is capped at its column and may shrink; the chip row scrolls and is contained; the filter row wraps.
- Harness (not committed): the floored case at 393 px measures 432 px before and 393 px after, the band at 8 to 385 px with the cards, in Chromium and WebKit; light and dark at 393 px and 1,280 px.

## Verify after deploy

On a phone or the iOS Simulator at 393 px, New phone layout on, Jobs → Pipeline: the band ends at the same gutter as the cards, and the chips scroll sideways inside it.
