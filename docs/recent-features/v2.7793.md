# v2.7793 — Lien desk: one rule for a window that has closed, and the answers to it (2026-11-18)

PR 1 of the closed end on the Lien desk's Board. The owner's ask of 2026-11-18: *"In the to do visual, should we add a section that shows when a window has closed? So that they're clearly separate? Basically our flowchart is going to become the flowchart that a user navigates through. So if something can no longer be liened, we want to let the user know and then offer the user the opportunity to correct the date if there is a mistake or to let it go where they mark it as either job stage uncollectible or leave it in job stage collections."* Three places for the closed end were drawn on the real Board with made-up jobs, on a computer and a phone, light and dark, with the window and its answers. His pick the same day: *"Build A"*, a closed end at the foot of each column. This PR is the kernel alone: no screen changes, no migration, no edge function.

## Before

- Both columns of the Board have a **Missed** stage, and the two mean different things. The notices' is a job whose every month closed with no notice. The affidavits' is a job whose lien day passed.
- The affidavits' Missed held only a paper nobody had drafted (`pileFor` in `lienDeskAffidavits.ts`). A paper that was sent, approved or held kept its pile when its day passed, so an approved affidavit past its day stood red in *Ready to file* and the batch print counted it.
- No one place said what a person can do about either. The words *the lien right on that work is gone; the money is still owed* stood on a step note, with no press.

## What changed

- **[`src/lib/jobs/lienClosedEnd.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienClosedEnd.ts)**, pure, with its unit tests.
- **Who stands at a closed end.** `lienAffidavitDayPassed`: an affidavit whose day has passed with nothing filed, whatever pile it stands in. `lienNoticeWindowClosed`: a notice whose every month closed, until someone records the months. `lienLateNoticeStillCounts`: the job's lien day is known and ahead.
- **The bins' names**, each for what closed (`LIEN_CLOSED_BIN_WORDS`): *Window closed · no notice* and *Lien day passed · not filed*.
- **Which end** (`lienClosedKind`): `gone` once the lien day has passed, `month` for a closed month while it is ahead. The lien day wins.
- **The window's words** (`buildLienClosedWindow`): what closed and on which day, what is still open, where the day is counted from (approved hours, the bill, the job's creation month or a day set by hand) and how the app counts it, with *This is the app's count. Ask the attorney when it is not clear.*
- **The answers.** A passed lien day: *The date is wrong* (the door to the last day of work, with *Record the filing ›* under it for a paper filed and never recorded), *Keep collecting* as the filled press, *Let it go* as the deliberate one. A closed month: *Send a late notice*, *The date is wrong*, *Record it closed*. A closed month never offers to let the job go.
- **Who may press.** The lien office answers (`canAnswer`). Anyone may open the late notice. A press this viewer cannot make is dead with its reason, and *Uncollectible* is dead on a job that is not billed, since the mark takes a billed job only.
- **What is kept** (`lienClosedReason`): the day, what the office chose and the person's own note, as the reason the existing writes store. Nothing new is stored: *Keep collecting* is the job taken off the lien path, *Let it go* is the Uncollectible mark, *Record it closed* is the noted miss of v2.3679.
- **The dates off the desk's two entries** (`lienClosedDates`): the unrecorded months with their days, the lien day, the last month and its source. The affidavit's own day wins over the notice's reckoning of it.

## What it does not do

- Nothing reads the kernel yet. The Board's two closed bins are PR 2 and the window with its presses is PR 3.
- A closed lien day still leaves the desk by itself seven days later, because the affidavit windows stop listing the job. Keeping it until someone answers takes a migration, which is its own PR.
