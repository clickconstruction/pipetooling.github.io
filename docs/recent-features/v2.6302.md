# v2.6302 — The Development page's Plans look reads by part (punch list #239, PR 3) (2026-11-01)

Client, stacked on PR 2 (v2.6298) over PR 1's tables (v2.6297). The read-out that started the card found the old look counted a job's empty Plans folder as a set (ten of a real site's eleven "sets") and could not list the two real sets that lived as rows on a bid; this look reads the parts instead.

## Change

- **Kernel `src/lib/plans/sitePlans.ts`** (pure, `sitePlans.test.ts`): `buildSitePlansView({ parts, sets, jobs, bids })` — each part in position order with its current set, the replaced one, the older sets, our as-builts and the jobs naming it; the jobs on no part (a part that no longer exists counts as none); the parts with no set; the bids with a pinned set. `sitePlansCountWords` (*5 sets on 4 parts*), `sitePlansNeedWords` (*1 part with no set · 2 jobs on no part*), `sitePlansChipWords`, `sitePlansAskCount`.
- **Io**: `readSite(developmentId)` and `makeSitePart` on `planSetsIo.ts`; the members' job read carries `plan_part_id` (`fetchDevelopmentPage.ts`, `overview.ts`, fail-soft on the estimator's door in `readDoor.ts`).
- **`DevelopmentPartsCard.tsx`** replaces the link-grouped `DevelopmentPlansCard` (`DevelopmentPartsCard.render.test.tsx`): the table Part · Building from · Dated · Came · Memo · Jobs · Older sets; a row's *N older ▸* fold with *Build from this set*, Open ↗ and × → Remove / Keep; *Our as-builts · N* under the row; **+ New set** on a row through the shared `PlanSetForm` (lifted out of `JobPlansSection` into `src/components/plans/`); the chips *N parts with no set* and *N jobs on no part* that open their lists; **+ Part**; **Ask the estimators about the N** (`askTitleForSitePlans`, found again by `plansAskOnRecord`); *Bids pin their own set* under the table. The strip's Plans look reads *5 sets on 4 parts* and the amber need.
- The link-grouped view (`src/lib/developments/plans.ts`) still feeds the Jobs & bids table's Plans cell and History's plan lines; the memo line it drew on the Plans look is gone with the card (a bid's memo lives in Edit Bid, a part's set's memo on its row).
- **Docs**: `GLOSSARY.md` → *Plan sets by part (Jobs)* gains the site look; `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` → the development page line. Guide *see everything on a development* says what the look shows now.
