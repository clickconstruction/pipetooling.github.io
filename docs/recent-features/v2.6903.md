# v2.6903 — Paste a price from a quote or a call, and it files itself on the right part (2026-11-10)

Punch list #308, PR 3 of nine: one price, with no sheet. The owner asked whether a one-off was easy, and it was not. A price by phone or one row out of a mail went back to typing by hand, with nothing to say the part was already on file. This PR makes the card that the sheet reader is built from, and gives it two doors of its own.

## What changed

- **Migration `20261111040000_price_rows.sql`** ([doc](../migrations/20261111040000_price_rows.md)): where a paper came from, what a row did to the book, `material_part_prices.source_line_id`, the RPCs `price_row_file` and `price_row_take_back`, and the parts book roles' read of quote papers. Push right after the merge.
- **`src/lib/materials/priceSheet/pastedRow.ts`** (pure): `parsePastedRow` and `parsePasted` read text with no model. A row is a quantity, the house's item number, the words and a price, any of them missing. A quote's fixture heading opens a group and its `Subtotal … EACH` closes it; a line of words alone is the row above running on. A price may be each, by the hundred (`C`) or by the thousand (`M`). The same shapes come back from the reader in PR 4.
- **`src/lib/materials/priceSheet/rowCard.ts`** (pure): `rowCard` turns a row, its match, the price on file and the paper's day into the card: `ready`, `offer`, `ask`, `pick`, `new`, `not_a_part`, `no_house` or `no_price`, with the chip, the question in words and the book price each. `priceSourceWords` says where a filed price came from.
- **`src/lib/materials/priceSheet/priceRowIo.ts`**: `filePriceRow`, `takeBackPriceRow`, `loadOnFilePrices`, `loadBidAskedHouseIds`, `loadPriceSources`.
- **`src/components/materials/QuickPriceWindow.tsx`**, *One price*: the supply house (the houses this bid asked first, the last one used remembered), the day, where it came from, and a box to paste or type rows. Each row is a card with its own press. A filed row becomes a green line with **Undo**. With a part already known the window asks only the house, the number and the price.
- **Doors**: **Paste a price** in the Takeoffs title row beside the view pills, and on the Parts Book. **A price from a quote or a call…** above *Their numbers* in both price windows. Both price windows show where each price came from under the house's name (`usePriceSources`).
- The guide `paste-a-price-from-a-quote-or-a-call` is new.

## What the database holds, and what the card only shows

- **A move past a quarter** is not filed unless the caller says a person looked (`allow_big_move`). The card shows the old price, the new one and the change, and its press is that word.
- **An older paper** is not filed over a newer price unless `allow_older`. The card offers *Keep the newer price* first.
- **One number means one part at a house**: a row whose number another part has is refused before anything is written, naming the part.
- **A price changed by hand loses its source** (a trigger), so a typed number never names a paper and a take back leaves it alone.
- **A take back** returns the price, its day and its source to what the row replaced, or deletes the price when there was none, and removes the number the row taught. A one-off's paper goes with it.
