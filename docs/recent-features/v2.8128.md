# v2.8128 — Add to a list: a second press after a failure makes nothing twice (2026-11-20)

Found by the audit of punch list #403 (finding 10). One window and its kernel. No migration and no edge function.

## What was wrong

Documents → Agreements → **Add to a list** writes up to three things in a row: a new supply house (or the customer the app's own New customer form saves), the person kept under that company, and the naming of their contact (`name_contact`). When a later write failed, the window said why and stayed open, and its press was live again. Pressing it ran every write again from the start:

- a new supply house was made a second time, with a second rep row;
- a new customer opened the New customer form again, so saving it made a second customer;
- at a company the app already keeps, the person was kept a second time.

## What changed

- `listMadeKey` and `writeOnce` in `src/lib/documents/paperPartyList.ts`. Each write is known by what it was for: the house by its name, the person by the company's record with their name and address, the customer by the name the form opened on. The first press writes and keeps the record. A later press with the same key gets it back and writes nothing. A write that fails keeps nothing, so the next press tries it again.
- `PaperPartyAddToList.tsx` keeps those records while the window is open. A customer the form saved is reused, so the form opens once.
- A company name changed between presses is a new company, and is made as one.
- The guide *find an agreement we sent or signed* says a press after a failure goes on from where it stopped.

## What it does not cover

The records live in the open window. A window closed after a failure and opened again starts over. The house or customer the first try made is then on the list, under *Someone at a company we already have*. Making the three writes one act on the server would close that too. It needs a migration, so it is left for the owner.

## Tests

- `src/lib/documents/paperPartyList.test.ts`: the key and the write-once rule.
- `src/components/documents/PaperPartyAddToList.render.test.tsx`: a press after a failure for each of the three choices, and a changed name making a new house. The three retry cases failed before the change.
