# v2.6384 — Lien desk: gate 4 says when no month is open, instead of asking for a tick (2026-11-02)

PR 5 of the sweep after Taunya's report on job 890 (v2.6376 the GC, v2.6380 the money). No migration, no function.

- **What it showed.** On 890 — a sent notice whose one month (Aug, set by hand) closed Oct 15 and left the months count — gate 4 read **No month picked · ✗** with *Tick at least one month below — the notice has to name the work it covers*, over a Months grid holding only the column of the notice that went out. The gate could not be cleared.
- **The fix.** `buildLienDeskGates` takes `monthsOnJob`, the count of months the job has at all (open or closed; the entry's `months.length`, what the grid draws). With none and nothing picked, gate 4 reads **No month open** with the title *Every month's window has closed — nothing is left for a new notice to name*; `lienGateShortWords` says *no month open* and `lienFootBlockedSentence` *Don't send yet. Every month's window has closed, so no new notice can name a month.* The gate's row in `LienDeskModal` says *No month is open to notice — every window has closed. The notice that went out is in the grid below*, keeping **Months and deadlines ↓**. A job with months and none ticked reads as before, *Dated from creation* label and all; a reader that passes no count reads as before.
- **Left on card #248:** whether a Sent row with nothing due should draw the gates and a verdict at all.
