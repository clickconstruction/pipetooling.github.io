# v2.6578 — A machine check’s photos file into the machine’s Drive folder, as promised (2026-11-06)

Found on the live walk of punch list #271's first PR (v2.6576): after a photo upload the `machine_check_photos` row's `storage_path` stayed null while the object sat in the bucket. The table from `20261106030000_machines.sql` (punch list #270, v2.6555) has SELECT, INSERT and DELETE policies and no UPDATE policy, so the path the phone writes after each upload was refused silently, zero rows and no error, and the `photo-save` sweep (v2.6556), which files the rows with a `storage_path` and no `filed_at`, never filed a machine photo into Drive. The phone drew the photo through a signed URL by its id, so nothing looked wrong. No real photo rows existed on prod at the time, so nothing is lost.

## What changed

- `supabase/migrations/20261106173757_machine_check_photos_update_policy.sql` (`docs/migrations/20261106173757_machine_check_photos_update_policy.md`): `"machine_check_photos update"`, the taker updates their own row while `filed_at` is null and may not mark it filed, the shape of `report_photos_update_own` with the sweep's fence. The sweep writes as the service role as before.
- `src/lib/machines/checksIo.ts` → `addCheckPhoto`: the `storage_path` write reads its row back; when none comes (the policy missing, or a row that is not the caller's), the object is removed from the bucket and the row deleted, and the sheet shows *The photo went up, but its record was refused the path to it. Try again, and tell the office if it keeps happening.* through its usual `formatErrorMessage`, instead of a photo that looks saved and never files.
- The bed `supabase/tests/machine_photos_path/20_scenario.sql` with `scripts/pgtest-machine-photos-path.sh` (`npm run test:pg:machine-photos-path`, in `scripts/sql-beds.txt`): the policy exists for UPDATE on `authenticated`; the taker records the path on their own photo; the taker changes nothing once the sweep has filed it; the taker cannot mark it filed themself (`42501`); another signed-in person updates nothing.

## Deploy

**Push right after the merge** (`supabase db push`), with the person who pushes at hand: from the deploy until the push every machine photo fails loudly on the sheet instead of silently, and after it the next photo taken files into the machine's Drive folder within a few minutes. No types change.
