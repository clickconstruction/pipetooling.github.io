# v2.3539 — Settings: the rail (2026-09-17)

Owner pick **A** from the 2026-09-16 Settings header mock-ups (Claude artifact *Settings Header Options*), with the second pass's cheap wins folded in. Client only.

## Why

The header spent ~450 px before any setting: a big title, *Your role: Dev*, a search box on its own line, then four zones of plain-text tabs (sixteen for a dev) whose labels — *COMPANY — BY PAGE* — were notes to ourselves.

## What shipped

- **[`SettingsRail.tsx`](../../src/components/settings/SettingsRail.tsx)** replaces `SettingsTabBar`. ≥ 900 px: a sticky vertical list beside the setting (`.settingsShell` grid, `index.css`) — the *Settings* title small at the top, the existing search bar, **recent tabs** as chips, the four zones as short headings with one row per page (each carrying its `pagesHint` as a tooltip), the **role note** ("6 more tabs are for masters and devs") in place of *Your role*, and Sign out / Change password at the foot. Under 900 px the same list is a `<select>` with `<optgroup>`s. Items keep `role="tab"` and their labels, so `e2e/settings-tabs.spec.ts` is unchanged.
- **[`lib/settingsRail.ts`](../../src/lib/settingsRail.ts)** (+5 tests): recent tabs (newest first, three, only ids the role can see; `localStorage` key `settings_recent_tabs_v1`, every read and write in try/catch), `landingTab` (the last tab opened here when the URL names none, else the first), `hiddenTabsCount` / `hiddenTabsNote`.
- Zone label *Company — by page* → *Company*; help guide `settings-basics` and `SETTINGS_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md` updated.

## Verified

Typecheck, lint, theme check, build; rail (5), settings-groups, settings-search tests. Live as Robert at desktop width: the rail, search, chips and the role note (none for a dev); every tab opens by click and by chip; a reload lands on the last tab. At 375 px: the select lists every tab under its group, no sideways overflow, the *Settings* word still present for the viewport smoke. Dark theme through the same tokens.
