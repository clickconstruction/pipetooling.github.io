# v2.5895 — The job window opens on where it stands with the customer (2026-10-26)

Punch list #186, item 2A, the owner's pick of 2026-10-26 (*I like it, build it*): the rule-built thread summary from the to-do's mock-up, the first of the three items. The job window's Job tab (`DetailJobModal.tsx`) opens on a card, **Where it stands with Dana**, above the activity header, for the office team (`jobBoxOffered`). Nothing a model wrote: every word is a row a person wrote.

## The card

`src/lib/jobs/threadSummary.ts` (pure, `buildThreadSummary`) builds three lines from the facts the app keeps:

- **Where it stands**: whose word came last and when, from the job's lines (`lastWordLine` over `loadJobMessages`' rows, the word channels only), then the job's stage word and percent (`jobStatusWord`, `pct_complete`): *The last word was Dana’s, a text yesterday. The job is Working at 34%.*
- **Their word**: the newest written promise whose day has not passed on any customer contact of the job (`message_promises` through `heardLinesFromRows`): *Dana promised a decision by Thu Oct 29. Said on Friday.* A day given on a logged call reads *Dana gave Thu Oct 29 on the call.*
- **Our word**: what we owe them, the newest open `contact_owed` row (`openOwed`, `owedSentence`): *We owe Dana a start date by Mon Nov 2. Marked on Friday.*

Each line with a line behind it carries a door, `threadHrefByKind` at the line, which closes the window and opens Messages there. When nothing is written down the line says so (*No day of Dana’s is written down.*, *Nothing we owe is written down.*). The stamp reads *Read 8:10 AM from 41 lines and 12 events.*: the time the read landed, the job's lines, the activity feed's items.

The mock-up's second and third labels, *They are waiting on* and *We promised*, were corrected while building: the second line's content was their promise, so the labels are now **Their word** and **Our word**, which is what the rows are.

## The reads and the presses

`threadSummaryIo.ts`'s `loadThreadSummaryFacts` reads the job's lines as the feed does and then, for the customer contacts those lines name, the two queries Follow up's thread read makes (`contact_owed` open rows, `message_promises` written with a day from today). `hooks/useJobThreadSummary.ts` reads on open, again when a line lands (`useMessagesLive`) and on **Read again**; a read that fails keeps the last card and says so. **Keep as a note** writes `noteBody` (the title with the stamp, then the three lines) through the window's own note path (`submitNoteWithBody`), the only write. The fold is kept per browser (`job_thread_summary_folded`).
