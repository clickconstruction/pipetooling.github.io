# v2.7870 — Time: the row waits before it counts (2026-11-18)

Punch list #395, a fix found on the live walk that followed the train (v2.7794 to v2.7815). Two things the row said were not yet true.

## What was wrong

- **A false sentence for one frame.** The row drew *The archive has no older photo of this spot.* for about 16 ms after a press on Time, then *Looking through the archive…*. A screen learns of a new spot one frame before its walk begins, and for that frame the hook held no walk and was not yet looking, which read as a finished walk that found nothing. The same frame showed the last spot's dates under a new spot's name after a press on another dot.
- **A count that fell.** The sentence over the row counted older photographs as soon as the walk had met them: 15, then 14, 13 and on down to 8 over a few seconds. Two stops taken on one day are one photograph, so the number falls as each day lands.

Both were seen on clicktooling.com in a change-by-change record of the row on a real painted map. The first also made a walk script read the row too early and report an empty archive.

## What changed

- `olderPhotosForAsk` in `src/lib/map/olderPhotos.ts` (tested): what to show for the tile asked about now. An ask that what is held does not answer is a walk under way: nothing found yet, and looking. `useOlderPhotos` keeps the tile its state was walked for and answers through it, so the frame before a walk begins reads as looking.
- `mapTimeCaption` counts only when the archive is no longer being read: the walk is over and every day is in. Until then the sentence names the spot and, once known, the day today's photograph was taken.

## Checked

- Kernel tests for the three cases of an ask, and the frame's test of the sentence while days are still being found.
- A real browser on a local page with made-up dots, recording the row change by change from the press: *Looking through the archive…* at 19 ms, never the empty sentence, and the count once, at the end. The same after a press on another dot.
