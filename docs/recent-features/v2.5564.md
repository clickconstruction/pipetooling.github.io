# v2.5564 — Training: the OSHA 30 refresher's thirty lessons, behind the scenes (2026-10-15)

Punch list #111, PR 1 of six (the to-do `to-dos/training/`, its mock-up at https://claude.ai/artifact/LGSMRUFYFb9BvTqRPkcjvG; Grace, 2026-10-15: a Training section under My Time where the crew completes courses at their own pace, each course with a currency clock managed by a dev in Settings, red when outstanding, yellow when lapsed, green when caught up, the streamlined OSHA 30 as the first course; "I love it, build it"). This PR is the words and the kernels: no screen reads them yet. PR 2 brings the tables (`training_courses`, `training_lesson_passes`, `training_passes`, migration `20261015060000`), PR 3 the course page `/training/<slug>`, PR 4 the Dashboard card, PR 5 the People rail and Users row chips, PR 6 Settings → Training.

- **`src/content/training/osha-30-refresher/`**: `course.md` (the title, the summary, the *not a card* line, the final check's draw of 20 and bar of 14, the nine parts) and thirty lesson files `NN-<slug>.md`, each with frontmatter (`title`, `part`, `minutes`, `from`) and exactly five sections: *Why it matters*, *Must know*, *Traps*, *Numbers* (`**value** label` chips) and *Check yourself* (four questions of four options, one `[x]`, a `> right:` and a `> wrong:` line each), plus `final-bank.md` (the final check's own questions, 24 or more). Built from the owner's capture of the ClickSafety course (`osha30-refresher.md`, 34 units, the capstone, the final-exam bank, Appendix A's numbers, Appendix B's quirks), streamlined for a plumbing and electrical crew: the units met every week stay whole, the ones met only on another trade's site fold together. Every held line passes `plainWordsFailures` (one idea per sentence, none over 20 words, nothing glued), and the wrong options come from the captured quizzes' own lists, never invented.
- **`src/lib/training/courseContent.ts`** (pure): `parseLesson`, `parseQuestions`, `bodySections`, `buildCourseRegistry` (throws on a bad file: a missing section, five options, two right answers, a gap in the lesson numbers, a part with no lesson, a bank under twenty), `contentVersionOf` (FNV-1a over the words, eight hex characters; a pass records it), `lessonPlainWordsFailures`, `appendixBFailures` (`APPENDIX_B_BANNED_ASKS`: no check question stands on a place where the course's key contradicts its lesson), `drawFinalCheck` (one part at a time so every part is asked, options shuffled, a seed replays a draw), `gradeFinalCheck`, `courseProgress`, `lessonToOpen`. **`courseRegistry.ts`** is the one `import.meta.glob` module, imported only by the lazy course page (the lesson text must never ride in the main chunk).
- **`src/lib/training/trainingState.ts`** (pure): the ONE rule behind the colour everywhere, `courseStanding(policy, passes, role, today)` → red (on, required, no pass that counts), amber (the newest pass's day plus `goodForMonths` is within `dueSoonDays` or past), green; `personStandings`, `trainingVerdict` (*1 course to do*, *1 course to take again*, *You are caught up*), `standingWords` (*Done Oct 3, 2025 · was due Oct 3, 12 days ago*), `standingPress` (Start, Continue, Take it again, Read it again), `trainingChip` (the People chip: *training due*, *training lapsed*), `standingCounts`, `ymdAddMonths` (clamped to the month's end), `ymdDiffDays`, `ymdWords`. A pass before `retakeFrom` (the *Everyone takes it again…* stamp) does not count. Days are YYYY-MM-DD; the caller passes `todayYmdInAppTz()`.
- **Tests**: `courseContent.test.ts` (the parser on fixtures, the draw and the grade, then the REAL folder: every lesson's shape, plain words with no exemption list, Appendix B, and the whole course building with no gap and a full bank), `trainingState.test.ts` (the colours at the edges, the retake stamp, a window of 0 months, the words).

Not run live: nothing shows in the app yet. The content test is the gate; a lesson that fails it is rewritten, never exempted.
