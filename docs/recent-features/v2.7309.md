# v2.7309 — The job window on a phone: every small press gets a thumb’s square (2026-11-15)

Punch list #355, the second fix from the viewport sweep after pass 3 ([`to-dos/job-window-phone.md`](../../to-dos/job-window-phone.md)). Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## Before

The sweep of every tab at 375 px counted 86 presses under 40 px that predate the pass: the Job tab's row acts (Open the calendar, Ready to bill, Reply, Write it down, 20 px), the Edit rows' pencils (23 px squares), the Bill tab's draw chips and the line budgets (20 and 25 px), Costs' Print, Freeze the book's hours and Link the bid (23 and 27 px), the accordion heads (38 px), Documents' Index this job's files (28 px). Each one is a thumb's miss. The History tab's range select was 12.5 px, which makes an iPhone zoom the page on focus.

## What changed

- **[`src/lib/jobs/jobWindowPhone.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobWindowPhone.ts)** gains `pressPad`: how far a press's hit area grows on each side so a 44 px square covers it, nothing on a side already 40 px or more, else half the way to 44, rounded up.
- **[`src/components/jobs/jobWindowPressPad.ts`](../../src/components/jobs/jobWindowPressPad.ts)** (new, tests beside it): `padSmallPresses` walks the window's body, measures every button, link, role button and summary, and marks each small one with `data-press-pad` and `--press-pad-x/y`; `watchSmallPresses` runs it now and again 50 ms after the last change under the body or a turn of the screen (a timer, not a frame, since a frame never comes while the page is in the background). Left alone: the tab strip and the title row (a thumb's size already), a press with a press or a field inside it (the pad would take their taps), a press that clips its overflow (a clipped pad is no pad), and anything not drawn.
- **`JobWindowModal.tsx`**: on a phone an effect starts the watcher on `.job-window-body`; the phone stylesheet draws the pad as a `::after` over the mark, `position: relative` on the press and the four insets from the custom properties. Nothing moves and nothing is seen.
- **`JobHistoryCrewCalendar.tsx`**: the range select is 16 px and 40 px tall on a phone.

## Is this the best we can do?

Eighty-six presses across a dozen files could each have grown by hand, and most of them are words at the end of a line where a 44 px box would have broken the line. The pad keeps every line as it is and gives the thumb the square it needs, and a press added next month gets it without anyone remembering. What it cannot reach: a native select (sized where it is drawn, done here) and a checkbox (the Bill tab's four *Pick Draw* boxes stay 20 × 22; a label around them is the next step), and the Bill tab's draw chips clip their overflow, so their pad is skipped. Two neighbouring pads can overlap; the later one in the page wins the overlap, which is the lower or righter press, the one a thumb was reaching for.
