## What changed

- `App.tsx` imported twenty pages with a plain `import`, so all of them were in the chunk every visit loads: the bid room, the submittal room, the estimate's accept and terms pages, the hazmat notice, the customer portal, the sub portal, the pay link, the legal portal and its confirm page, the GC's word ask, the supply house quote, the contract's accept, sign and book preview pages, the customer rating, a kept file's link, the task shortcut, the partner statement and the logbook.
- Each is now `lazy`, as the newer public pages (the box page, the First look, the receipt link, the trade portal) always were. Each route outside the Layout sits in a `Suspense` of its own; the partner statement and the logbook sit in the Layout's.
- Sign in, the reset and invite pages, the dev login and the Away page stay in the entry: they are the first thing a signed-out person sees.
- **The guard.** The twenty files are `ON_DEMAND_PAGES` in `src/lib/entryGraph.ts`, held by the same test as the windows. `ENTRY_SOURCE_BUDGET_BYTES` comes down from 12.5 MB to 8.5 MB: the entry now reaches 7.2 MB of source.
