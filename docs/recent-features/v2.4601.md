# v2.4601 — Help: twenty-two more guides in plain words (2026-10-05)

The plain-words sweep ([v2.4233](v2.4233.md); batches 1–5 [v2.4235](v2.4235.md), [v2.4258](v2.4258.md), [v2.4282](v2.4282.md), [v2.4283](v2.4283.md), [v2.4286](v2.4286.md)), punch list #75 (`to-dos/plain-words-sweep.md`). The last 67 rows of `LEGACY_PLAIN_WORDS_GUIDES` were split into three contiguous slices for three sessions, so the PRs never touch the same rows. This PR is the middle slice, rows 24–45, `share-a-help-guide` through `tally-payroll-marking`. No migration, no edge function, nothing on a screen changes.

## What changed

- **Twenty-two guides rewritten** by the rules in [`src/lib/plainWords.ts`](../../src/lib/plainWords.ts), sentence by sentence (12,401 → 13,659 prose words). Frontmatter, headings, `:::example` panels, mock-UI tokens, links and every number are unchanged, checked mechanically against the previous text. Three bold spans changed words: one lost its parentheses (*two or more cards*), and two that opened with a pronoun now name their noun. A sentence no longer opens with *it* or *they* pointing back across a full stop. Screen text printed with a dash, a `·` or parentheses is quoted in italics, the rule's exemption: 23 spans, most of them bold labels made bold-italic.
- **A plain word beside each trade word** the first time a guide uses it, where the guide had none: backcharge, retainage, pay application, takeoff, lien waiver, contingency, affiant and some thirty more. Where another guide already defines the word, its definition is reused.
- **Their rows leave the legacy list** ([`plainWordsLegacy.ts`](../../src/lib/plainWordsLegacy.ts)).
- Facts that look out of date, and phrases kept word for word because their meaning was unclear, went to the punch list (#75) and are not changed here.
