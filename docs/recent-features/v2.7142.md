## What changed

## The decisions from the parked branch, kept

No new contact row (the text goes to the roster number's own team contact through `quo_place_number`, with its STOP and its Messages thread). A cron every five minutes that runs only when there is something to do, and no step in the bank's webhook. A request claimed before its text goes, so two sweeps never text one charge twice, and a refused text gives the claim back. The fold of one hour, the one reminder, the 48 hours, the trial.

## Four things hardened when the branch was picked up, and why

- **The day the switch went on is the database's clock, not the phone's.** The branch stamped `since` in the browser. A phone whose clock was a day behind would have reached a day back. The trigger `receipt_texts_stamp` now writes `since` itself on any write that switches it on, keeps it on a write that leaves it on for the same people, and the app reads the day back from the row.
- **A trial list that does not read is off, never everyone.** Nobody named means everyone, so the branch's parser turned `only: ["jordan"]` into everyone. The parser, the trigger and the guard now each read such a value as off.
- **The guard counts a trial's own people.** In a trial the other holders' requests wait untouched, and the branch's guard counted them, so the cron would have called every five minutes for two days with nothing to do. It also skips a waiting request whose charge has its ticket.
- **A refusal about every text stops the sweep.** An unregistered number or the day's cap is now read as texts paused, not as three failed tries on each charge.

## Is this the best we can do?

Close, with four limits to know.

- **Usually a reminder that evening, not a prompt at the counter.** The bank posts most charges overnight, hours after the swipe, and the owner's call was *posts, not pending*. The counter is the Tally press.
- **A sweep that dies between its claim and its send leaves a charge marked texted with no text.** The claim is what keeps two sweeps from texting twice, so the gap falls on the side of one text too few. The evening's reminder still goes for that charge.
- **A reason is final for its charge.** Adding the cell or tying the card texts the next charge, not that one. The line says so, and Copy the receipt link is on the same line.
- The link rides `clicktooling.com/r/` until a person adds the `/r/` rule to the short host's Worker in the Cloudflare dashboard (`RECEIPT_SHORT_ORIGIN` is null until then). A shorter link is a cheaper text.

## Proved, and what is not

- The sweep over a stood-in database and sender, 29 tests (`receiptSweep.test.ts`): off and Send texts off touch nothing; a charge is texted once, and once under two overlapping sweeps; never a pending charge, money back, a failed one, a duplicate, one from before the switch, past 48 hours or with its ticket; the fold; each reason written and never texted; a trial; every refusal; 170 charges in a night taken sixty a sweep with no request opened by two links; the reminder in its evening, once, folded, held by a STOP, by a ticket and by the switch.
- The rules and words, 28 tests (`receiptText.test.ts`); the io, 6; the Settings card, 8 render tests; the Team queue's line, 2 more in its render test.
- The migration applied twice on a throwaway Postgres 15 with stub tables, then 58 checks (`supabase/tests/receipt_texts/20_scenario.sql`): who may press, the database's clock in `since`, the values that land as off, every branch of the guard, one cron row. The whole-schema bed `npm run test:pg:receipt-texts` runs in CI; Docker was held by another account on the Mac this was written on.
- **Not seen**: a text on a real phone, Quo's answer to these words, the cron's post reaching the deployed function, a real STOP, the segment count of a first text. All wait for the trial on one card.
