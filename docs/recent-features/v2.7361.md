# v2.7361 — Scores: the owner sets the target on the Bid Board's Pricing rules (2026-11-15)

Punch list #360, PR 3 of the team scorecard's train. The hurdle is break-even plus the profit per paid field hour the owner wants. That target is one row of `app_settings` (`scorecard_hurdle_v1`), and since PR 1 only a dev could change it, from a form on the Scores tab. It is a rule the office sets for pricing, so it moves beside the other one: what a GC's difficulty costs.

## What ships

- **Bid Board → Pricing rules → The score's target** ([`ScorecardTargetRule.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/ScorecardTargetRule.tsx), under the price list in `BidDifficultyPriceModal.tsx`): the dollars of profit on each paid field hour, with what the number does in a sentence. The owner and a dev type it and press **Save the target** (`canSetHurdleTarget`); the press is dead until the number changes and refuses anything that is not dollars from 0 to 1,000 (`hurdleTargetFromText`). Everyone else with the board reads *$25/h … The owner sets the target.* It is one setting with its own press, not a version of the price list: a price version pins the bids priced under it, and nothing pins to the target.
- **The owner's own write** (migration `20261116050000_scorecard_hurdle_owner_policy.sql`, [its fragment](../migrations/20261116050000_scorecard_hurdle_owner_policy.md)): an insert and an update policy on that one key for `is_master_or_dev()`.
- **The Scores tab** loses its form and says *The owner sets the target on the Bid Board, under Pricing rules.* with **Open Pricing rules**. `/bids?tab=bid-board&rules=1` opens the window and drops the flag from the address (`HURDLE_TARGET_HREF`). The window offers **Open the Scores tab** back, to the owner and a dev.
- Guides: *read the team scores and set the hurdle* gains *Set the target*; *set what difficulty costs* points at it. ACCESS_CONTROL and APP_DIRECTORY amended.

## What it does not do

- The target is not versioned. A score for last month read today uses today's target. Call 3 on the card (where the target lives) is still the owner's.
- The controller edits the price list and reads the target: the card's plan gave the target to the owner.
