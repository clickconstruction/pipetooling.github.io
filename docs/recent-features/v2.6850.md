# v2.6850 — Permits: anyone can keep a passed permit, and every card shows its history (punch list #302) (2026-11-10)

The owner: *if anyone passes it moves it to passed and anyone in passed can move it to kept with a history on each card of who moved what and when.* The first half was already so. Two gaps: a passed row could only be put back on the skim (a keep took three presses through Decide…), and *What we did* showed on kept permits alone.

## What changed

- **`lib/research/permitHistory.ts`** (new, pure): `historyWords(ev)` turns one `permit_verdicts` row into a line (*Sam passed, Nov 9: too far*), the day read on the company calendar; `historyVerb` reads the verdict and its code (`np_` stopped pursuing, `sl_` passed again, `back` put it back, a hand-off); `historyReason` drops the stock words every answer is stored with; `cardHistory(events, show, skip)` gives a card its newest lines and how many older ones the sheet holds; `mayKeepFromClosed(row)` and `keepFromPassedReason(row)`.
- **`PermitPlaceList`**: a **Keep** press on every passed and not-pursuing row, by anyone, until the feed pairs the permit with a plumbing permit. It writes `look` with *Kept from Passed, after Sam passed.*
- **`PermitSkimDeck`**: a **History** row on a card that was answered before, the last two moves, with *and N earlier* opening the sheet. A second-look card titles it **Before** and leaves out the pass its amber line already says. One read per top card, fail-soft.
- **`PermitBuildingSheet`**: *What we did* on every permit, each line through `historyWords`; with no answer yet it says so.
- The second look keeps its cap of one look per permit, so its count still reaches zero. The Passed list has no cap.
- No migration: the history is the append-only `permit_verdicts` rows read back.
