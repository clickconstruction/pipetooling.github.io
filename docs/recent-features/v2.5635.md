# v2.5635 — Job activity: a customer's lines are live, and the hazmat fee's events show (2026-10-19)

A review of the Pipeline's Job activity (2026-10-19) found two defects in how it shows a customer's communications, neither needing an owner decision. Texting through Quo has been on since 2026-10-16, so customer texts land in `messages` all day, but a job's feed read them once. And four event types written to `job_activity_events` since July were never drawn. No migration; no edge function; nothing in the kernels copy.

- **Live lines** (`useJobMessages`): the hook now hears the page's one Messages channel (`useMessagesLive`, on `messages` and `contacts` since v2.5572's `20261016010000_messages_realtime.sql`) and reads the job's lines again after a change settles, beside Job Mode's `rev`. Its comment said `messages` was not in the realtime publication; v2.5572 put it there. Realtime and the read both run under the rows' policies, so a tech hears and gets only what R5's door gives them. The lines on screen stay until the new read is in, and a read again that fails keeps them (the first read that fails still shows the feed without them); a line that lands is the newest and takes the next number, so the numbers on screen stay. It backs `JobActivityView`, `DetailJobModal` and `JobModeDetailsSection`, and now `JobCustomerSheet`, whose own one-time read it replaces (its `loadLines` seam passes through as the hook's `load`), so the sheet's last lines and last word follow a reply too.
- **Live dots** (`useBoardDots`): the board's one hook hears the same channel and reads its dots again, so a row's orange waiting dot lights when the customer's text lands and her contact turns to waiting, without a reload of the board. The newest read wins when two are out. The bus holds one channel per page however many feeds, sheets and rows draw, so there is no channel per row; every listener reads after a 400 ms settle, and a tab coming back into focus reads too.
- **The hazmat events** (`JOB_ACTIVITY_EVENT_RENDER`): `jobActivityEventsFromRpc` drops a type the registry does not know. `send-hazmat-notice-email` writes `hazmat_notice_emailed` (not financial) after Resend takes the notice, and `update_/void_/delete_hazmat_fee_incident` (`20260728025542`, never redefined since) write `hazmat_fee_edited` (financial when the amount moved), `hazmat_fee_voided` (financial) and `hazmat_fee_deleted` (financial unless voided first). All four are in the registry now under Billing: the notice as *Emailed* in the Stripe bill email's blue, the fee's edit as *Hazmat* in amber, its void and delete as *Hazmat* in red. `list_job_activity_events` gates them through `can_read_job_activity` (redefined in `20260927233000`): the financial rows to dev, master, assistant, controller and primary on a job they may read, the others to the office and the job's crew too. `hazmat_fee_default` is an `app_settings` key, not an event.
- **Proof**: `jobActivityEvent.test.ts` reads the edge function's and the migration's sources and fails when a type they write is not a registry key; `jobActivityEventsFromRpc.test.ts` keeps the four rows. `jobActivityLive.render.test.tsx` fires a fake channel under the real feed and sheet: the new line shows as Entry 14 with the note and the others keeping theirs, the feed keeps its 13 lines while the read is out and after a read that fails, and the sheet's last lines and last word follow. `useBoardDots.live.render.test.tsx` draws two rows with the real speech bubble on one channel and lights only the waiting customer's. Each fails with its fix taken out.
- **Left for later**: the same scan of the migrations and edge functions finds other types written and never drawn, `legal_change`, `returned_check_fee`, `returned_check_fee_off`, `returned_check_fee_back` and `rtb_paid_job_blocked`; each wants its own call on who sees it.
