# v2.8426 — Bills sent: the payout tile always answers, and a credit marks its day (2026-11-23)

Two faults of By day paid found on the Bills sent walk of 2026-11-23 (punch list #422). No migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

- **The payout tile went missing.** `readStripePayout` answered null in three cases: Stripe has no payout on its way, the function refused, or the call failed. The view drew the tile only on a payout, so on a day Stripe had already paid out, the fourth tile was not there and nothing said why. The walk reported it as a missing tile.
- **A credit in kind left no mark.** A credit is listed under its day and never added. Its day's cell was blank, the same as an empty day. The **Other** chip, offered because the month held the credit, opened a month reading *0 payments* with no day to press.

## What changed

- `billsPaidPayoutOf(body, failed)`, new and pure, tells three answers apart: *coming* with the amount and the day, *none*, and *unread*. `readStripePayout` returns it and never throws.
- The tile is drawn on any answer. *None on its way* and *Stripe did not answer* stand where the amount would (`billsPaidPayoutWords`), each with a title that says more. Until the answer is in, no tile is drawn.
- `BillsPaidCell` carries `credits`, the credits in kind on the day. A day with a credit and no money reads *1 credit* in its cell (`billsPaidCellCredit`), stays white, and a screen reader hears *1 credit in kind, not added*.
- The printed month's grid marks the same day.

## Tests

`billsPaidDays.test.ts`: the three payout answers and their words; the credit mark, its label, the month's cells and the printed cell. `BillsPaidByDay.render.test.tsx`: the tile on *none* and on *unread*; a credit's day marked and a paid day not.

Neither was drawn in a browser. The live screen was not walked.
