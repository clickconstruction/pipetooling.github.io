# v2.2983 — Test-gap audit, pass 11: the Stripe invoice footer presets, the invoice-details parser and the share copy get their first tests (2026-09-06)

Follow-up to v2.2982, which named the Stripe invoice footer next. Tests only — no kernel, client, migration or edge-function change.

## Why these

`stripeInvoiceFooter.ts` (231 lines) is the org-wide footer that goes on every Stripe invoice the office sends: two shipped presets (plumbing, electrical), a sparse override layer in `app_settings` mirrored to the device, a session pin after fetch or save, and the dev-upload path when the org row is missing. It carries a "keep in sync with the Edge copy" note that nothing enforced. `stripeInvoiceDetailsResponse.ts` parses what the `get-stripe-invoice-details` function returns before Bill Customer shows it. `stripeInvoiceShareCopy.ts` is the email and SMS text a customer receives with their pay link. (`voidStripeInvoiceForRevert.ts` is an edge-function call and stays untested by design.)

## What shipped

- **`stripeInvoiceFooter.test.ts`** (16) — through the recording chainable Supabase stand-in, a retry passthrough and a Map-backed `window.localStorage`, with the module's session pin reset before every case: the **edge sync guard** (the 5,000-character cap is the same number on both sides); the shipped presets (open on plumbing, both under the cap, the detector recognises them); the stored-JSON parser (two string fields, capped, everything else dropped); the device mirror before any fetch (overrides apply, a malformed mirror reads as shipped, no window means shipped); **fetch** — an org row wins over the device and is pinned and mirrored, an empty or unparseable row pins "shipped" and clears the mirror, no org row pins the device mirror and a dev uploads it while other roles do not, a failed read reports no row and changes nothing; **save from the dev form** — only fields that differ from shipped are stored (`key`, JSON `value_text`, `onConflict: key`), both-shipped deletes the row and the mirror, a draft over the cap is truncated, a failed upsert propagates and leaves the pin alone; **reset** drops the pin, the mirror and the org row and swallows a delete failure.
- **`stripeInvoiceDetailsResponse.test.ts`** (5) — the full success payload with trimming and blank-to-null, the refusals (not success, no currency, non-numeric total or amount due), `amount_remaining` derived from total − paid and clamped at zero, bad timestamps and dates as unknown, a malformed line failing the whole parse and a missing lines array reading as none.
- **`stripeInvoiceShareCopy.test.ts`** (4) — subject, email body and SMS text with every trim and fallback.

All 25 passed on the first run; the client and edge caps agree today.
