# v2.5046 — Bid vs actual: each job's margin at completion, by section and by stage (2026-10-09)

Client only. No migration, no edge function. This is Burn against the bid, piece 2: "earned value by stage, per-component burn beyond hours, same surface", approved at the owner's sitting of **2026-10-09** and sent by Punchlist. The four choices Punchlist confirmed:

1. **One kernel with the Costs tab**, so the two can never disagree. The lens builds each linked job's verdict with the job's own Costs-tab calls, in their order. It loads only while the lens is open, and only for the roles that read wages.
2. **A Direct column** beside Priced, with the points the job runs under or over the priced margin. A row opens into the Costs tab's by-section table. Overhead is left out, as on the Pipeline card. The direct margin does not depend on it.
3. **Earned value by stage, in hours, for every role.** The billing lines are mapped to a stage by name. A line that names no stage is named and left out. A stage nobody has reported reads *no stage progress reported*, never 0%.
4. **No migration.** Every read uses existing tables and functions.

Data when built (read-only, 2026-10-09): all 18 linked jobs carry staged billing lines (29 Order / Any lines with a price). Not one job anywhere has a stage report (`progress_pct` is null everywhere), and 7 linked bids carry hours by stage. So the dollar burn lights up every linked job today. Earned value by stage waits for crews to report by stage, and its rows say so.

## What changed

- **Shared with the job's Costs tab:**
  - `buildBurnForVerdict` and `readTargetMarginPct` move verbatim from `JobCostsBurnSection.tsx` to `src/lib/jobs/jobBurnForVerdict.ts`. `JobCostsTabCharts` drops its own copy of the target reader and imports both.
  - `useJobChargesTimelineInputs`' body becomes the exported `loadJobChargesTimelineInputs(job, includeTeamLabor)`. The hook calls it unchanged. Its job type is structural (`JobChargesTimelineJob`): exactly the fields it reads.
- **The lens's burn** (`src/lib/bids/bidVsActualBurn.ts`):
  - `bidVsActualJobVerdict` is the Costs tab's sequence: `resolveJobBudget` → the 100% fallback for a billed or paid job with no % → `buildBurnForVerdict` with no overhead → `buildCostsVerdict`. A test builds the Costs tab's own verdict beside it and holds the two equal.
  - `bidVsActualDirectWords` gives *21%* over *$8,550 at completion*, or *too early*, *needs a % complete* or *no price on the job*.
- **The loader** (`useBidVsActualBurnInputs`): one lean `jobs_ledger` read (revenue, %, invoices, billed materials). Then each job goes through `loadJobChargesTimelineInputs` with team labor, four at a time, and the cells fill in as they land. A failed read marks its row only.
- **Earned value by stage** (`src/lib/bids/stageEarnedValue.ts`, `useJobStageEarnedValue`):
  - `bidStageOfLine` maps "Rough in" → rough, "Top out" → top, and "Trim set / Trim out / Finish" → trim. A line naming two stages stands for neither.
  - A stage of several lines takes their value-weighted progress. An unreported line of a reported stage counts as not started.
  - Earned hours = Σ progress × the bid's hours for the stage (`bid_estimate_breakdown.labor_hours_by_stage`), against the recorded hours, with the pace (*1.25× the hours earned*).
  - It is read when a row opens.
- **Bids → Bid Costs → Bid vs actual** (`BidsBidCostsTab`, `BidVsActualRowDetail`):
  - **Direct** for the dollar roles, e.g. *21% · $8,550 at completion · 10 pts under the price*.
  - **▸** opens a row into the by-section table (dollar roles) and the stage table: bid hours, progress, earned, and the billing lines behind each stage.
  - `useBidVsActual` now reads the whole `job_budgets` row (`budgetRowLikeOf`), so the budget resolves as on the Costs tab.
