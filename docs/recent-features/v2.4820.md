# v2.4820 — Legal portal: Start here and the tour (2026-10-07)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. PR A of 2. The owner (2026-10-07), after pitching a collections firm: *make an intake flow that includes the above information curated for a law firm looking at their portal, followed by a guide of how the portal works where we signal where they are in the process, and use short, curt, to the point language.* "The above information" was the talking points for the pitch. Mockup: `to-dos/legal-portal-firm-ready/mockup-start-here.html` (made-up figures). PR B adds step 4, the firm's answers, with its migration and the two functions.

## What changed

- **[`legalPortalStart.ts`](../../src/lib/legal/legalPortalStart.ts)**, the kernel: every line the firm reads and every figure. Step 1's figures (`companyLines`) come from the same book the Lien grid draws (`startBookRows` → `buildLienTimelineBook`): the share of jobs with a GC, the top five counties, the jobs at or under the justice court limit, the open total, what is due in 30 days (the book's `due` lens, the grid's *Due in 30 days*), and the firm's matters and balance. A line with no figure behind it is left out. Step 2 (`matterLines`) and step 3 (`workLines`: the fee and filing cost from the firm row, the courts, the settlement rule, the firm's own acts, the emails). The company's short name is its first word (`companyShortName`), never typed, which the sample test's *Acme Mechanical* holds.
- **[`LegalPortalStartHere`](../../src/components/jobs/legal/LegalPortalStartHere.tsx)**, a new first panel on the portal: the four steps one at a time, a rail across the top (numbered; done steps ticked green; the current one copper; each step clickable), *Step 2 of 4*, Back and *Next: How we work ›*, and *With your firm now: 1 matter. Open Matters ›* on every step. It opens first on a browser's first visit (`legalPortal.startSeen` in localStorage); after that the portal opens on Matters and Start here stays as the first tab.
- **The tour** ([`LegalPortalTour`](../../src/components/jobs/legal/LegalPortalTour.tsx), stops from `portalTourStops`): Matters, each tab the open matter shows (a narrative tab only when the matter has one, so #4831's tab joins by itself), the Lien grid, Notifications. Each stop opens its panel and tab, and the strip rings the part it means (`data-legal-tour` on the page; `.legalTourRing`) and scrolls it into view. The strip docks at the bottom, full width on a phone: segments, *Tour · stop 3 of 8*, the stop's name and one or two lines, Back, Next or Done, ×. Esc ends it; the arrow keys walk it when no box has the cursor. With no matter yet it is three stops.
- **The rules on the portal** ([`LegalPortalRulesSheet`](../../src/components/jobs/legal/LegalPortalRulesSheet.tsx), loaded lazily): the guide *read the Texas lien rules the app follows* opened only inside the signed-in app, so the firm could not read what it would be asked to sign off. The sheet draws the guide's own words (`helpGuideMarkdownToSafeHtml`, the `?raw` file) over the portal. Links within the guide scroll the sheet; links to statutes open a new tab; links to another guide or to an app page read as plain text.
- **The selected panel tab is bold from the first paint.** The tab row set `font: inherit` after `fontWeight`, so the shorthand reset the weight until a click re-applied it; the shorthand now comes first.

## Is this the best we can do?

- **Not an About page.** The first draft was the pitch bullets as one long page beside a help page: nobody reads it, it asks nothing back, and it does not say where the reader is.
- **Live figures, never typed ones.** The repo is public and a pitch's numbers go stale; the kernel reads them from the book, and the sample shows the sample's own.
- **Curated.** The pitch's sales close and pilot stayed in the meeting. What is unfinished is the Lien grid's own note on precincts.
- **The real portal, not screenshots.** Each stop opens the real tab and rings it, so the tour cannot drift from the page.
- **Never in the way.** Start here opens first once per browser; Matters is one tap from every step.
- **Plain words, held by a test.** Every sentence passes `plainWordsFailures`.
