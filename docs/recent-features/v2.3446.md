# v2.3446 — Pipeline: the Progress & payment cell stays in its column (2026-09-14)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Hotfix from the owner's live look at v2.3419–v2.3421 ("some look okay, some do not look very okay", with a screenshot of Ready to Bill and Billed rows).

## What was wrong

[`JobsStagesUnifiedTable`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsStagesUnifiedTable.tsx) (Ready to Bill · Billed Awaiting Payment · Collections) wraps the cell in `display: flex; flex-direction: column; align-items: center`. A flex item sized by `align-items: center` takes its content's width, and the v2.3419 words line is `white-space: nowrap`, so the cell grew to the length of the sentence and overflowed the `<td>` to the right — the bar ran under **Bill Customer** / **Mark Paid**, the legend's dollars landed under the action column, and the `$… bid` figure collided with the buttons. [`JobsStagesTable`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsStagesTable.tsx) (Waiting · Working · Paid) never wrapped the cell that way, which is why the owner's Working rows looked right. v2.3198's caption had the same `nowrap`, but no row in those sections had ever drawn it.

## What shipped

- [`StagesProgressPaymentCell`](../../src/components/jobs/StagesProgressPaymentCell.tsx): the root takes `width: 100%; max-width: 100%; box-sizing: border-box`, so it is bounded by its wrapper (the column) in both tables and the card list.
- [`StagesStageBar`](../../src/components/jobs/StagesStageBar.tsx): the words line wraps to **two lines** then clips (`-webkit-line-clamp: 2`) instead of one line with an ellipsis — the owner's tablet look had cut *Top Out · Tristen on site Sat · 40% set Aug 7 · …* at the percent.

## Verified

Render suites for the cell, the bar and the unified table; typecheck, lint, theme check. Dev-server pass in Chrome on Ready to Bill (Mission Hills) and Billed (Montolongo, Johnson) rows at 1473 px and 834 px: the cell sits inside its column, the buttons are clear, the sentence reads on two lines.
