# v2.4801 — A check on a Stripe bill holds the Stripe close (2026-10-07)

One migration (`20261007213000_held_stripe_marks`), one new edge function (`close-held-stripe-marks`, cron). Punch list #98 (`to-dos/held-stripe-marks/README.md`). Taunya, 2026-10-07, after a check landed on job 186 instead of its own job: *"need to be able to unlink payment"*. The row's ⋯ menu offered only *View the Stripe bill* and *Check didn't clear…*.

## Why the row could not be unlinked

Mark Paid · Check told Stripe "paid" the moment the office typed it (`record-stripe-invoice-out-of-band-payment` → `invoices.pay({ paid_out_of_band })`), and Stripe never reopens a paid invoice. So the app treated the payment as Stripe's: Remove, Unlink and Move to job… were hidden, and the one door left — *Check didn't clear…* (v2.4082) — reverses the mark with a credit note, sends the bill back and makes the office bill the customer again with a new invoice number. Right mechanics, wrong story for a wrong-job check, and it dropped the money instead of carrying it to the right job.

The app already knew a check is not money for seven days: the lien-waiver cell waits that long (`checkClearing.ts`, v2.4330), and Accounts Receivable closes a Stripe bill only after the deposit is matched (v2.1639). Mark Paid was the one door that skipped both.

## What changed

- **Mark Paid · Check at the whole open balance records the row only** (`mark_invoice_paid`, the RPC a non-Stripe bill already used). The bill reads Paid in the app, the job moves to Paid, the Stripe invoice stays open. The window says so under the type (`heldStripeMarkNote`: *Stripe closes the invoice on Oct 14, 7 days after the check's date… Until then this payment can be moved to another job or taken off like any other, and the pay link stays open*). Cash, Wire, ACH, Card (external), Other and any part payment take the old path. Kernel [`heldStripeMark.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/heldStripeMark.ts): `markPaidHoldsStripeClose`, `heldStripeCloseYmd`, `stripeMarkIsHeld`, `paymentRowOnHeldStripeMark`, the two word functions.
- **A held mark is an ordinary row.** The Stripe lock now hangs on *Stripe owns the row* — `stripeOwnsPaymentRow` in `jobFormPaymentPredicates.ts`, true when `stripeHoldsPaymentReason` names a credit note or a bill marked paid in Stripe — instead of *the row is on a Stripe bill*. `paymentSource` reads a held check as *check · typed by hand*; `paymentMoveBlock` offers Move to job… on it (sent or not: the pay link is open and the customer's number unchanged, so the bill just reads Billed again); the form's `mergePaymentRowUpdate` / `planPaymentRemoveRequest` / `paymentRemoveWritesNow` and the table's lock take the edit, open the confirm and write at once; the line's Remove is enabled; `Check didn't clear…` stays hidden until Stripe holds the mark. The line adds *Stripe closes the bill Oct 14, once the check has cleared* (`held-stripe-mark-line`).
- **`move_jobs_ledger_payment`** follows the rule `remove_jobs_ledger_payment_and_reconcile` already had: a Stripe-hosted bill's payment is refused only while Stripe holds it (the same two sentences); otherwise it moves, is unlinked from the bill, and the bill reads Billed again when no longer covered.
- **`close-held-stripe-marks`** (pg_cron `held-stripe-marks-sweep`, daily 11:17 UTC, `X-Cron-Secret`): for every Stripe bill the app shows paid and Stripe still shows open, once every check on it is `CHECK_CLEAR_DAYS` old, the bill is covered, and no linked deposit is `failed` at Mercury, it writes the same `pt_*` metadata Mark Paid used to (date, type, reference, note, `pt_recorded_by` from the row's `created_by`) and pays the invoice out of band; the webhook no-ops on the already-paid row and stamps `stripe_invoice_status`, which the sweep also stamps itself. A Stripe invoice already paid, void or uncollectible just gets its status stamped. `{ dry_run: true, invoice_id }` for a hand run. Kill switch `app_settings.held_stripe_marks_sweep_disabled_v1 = '1'`.

## Decisions taken

- **Close on the check's seven days, not on the bank match.** Matching a deposit to a held row in Accounts Receivable links it (*Link that payment instead*) and does not close Stripe; the sweep does, on the day the lien-waiver cell would also trust the check. One rule, one clock.
- **Checks only.** Cash does not bounce and a wire is the bank's word; they keep closing Stripe at once. A check on the wrong job is the mistake that happened.
- **The pay link stays open for the week.** A customer who has just mailed a check almost never also pays online; if one does, the webhook sees the app row already paid, stamps Stripe's status, and the row locks as Stripe's — the money shows in Stripe and the office sees two payments to sort, as with any double payment today.
- **The stuck case is not solved here.** A bill Stripe already holds (job 186 today) still needs the credit note and a fresh bill; carrying that check to the right job in one press is the next PR in the to-do.
