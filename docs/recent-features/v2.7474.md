## What ships

Migration `20261117040000_sub_papers.sql` ([its page](../migrations/20261117040000_sub_papers.md)), cut from the design spike's snapshot (`claude/sub-labor-spike-82d6a9` at `7616bc399`) and renumbered from `main`:

- **`sub_papers`**, the register: every paper that was made, with its number, its revision and the paper as issued.
- **`sub_paper_job_counters`** and a sequence, the numbers: `WS-<job>-<n>` per job, `SA-<n>` across the company, and a payment record carries its sending's own number as `PR-<n>`.
- **`sub_paper_acks`**, what a sub says back from their portal. Nothing writes it until the portal's step.
- **`mint_sub_paper`** and **`void_sub_paper`**, the only ways a row is written or voided.

A number is never given out twice: a deleted sheet's row stays with its number, and a voided paper keeps its own.

## What differs from the spike's file

- Both functions refuse a training account, a digital twin and an archived account as well as a caller outside the office. A definer function runs past the row fences, and a number given out in training would be a number a real paper can never have.
- The mint takes a transaction lock per sheet and per sending. Two people pressing at the same moment on a sheet with no paper yet would each have found none and each taken a number.
- The tables are read-only by grant as well as by policy, the counters have no grant at all, and execute is revoked from `anon` as well as `PUBLIC`.
