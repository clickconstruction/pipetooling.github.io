# v2.6024 — GC mode: Start from a template draws every row from the start day, and the schedule reads By phase (2026-10-28)

PR 5 of punch list #204 (`to-dos/schedule-builder/README.md`, the mock-up's D3 and D4), the job's side: the kernel of
PR 1 (v2.5995) reaches the first draft, the chart and the list.

## Before the draw (D3)

- `templateDraftPress` (`schedule/scheduleWindow.ts`): a template with rows draws through `rowsDraft` from the start
  day with the rough's stage days for the lines in no row, the schedule carries the template's phases in order
  (`ProjectSchedule.phases`), and the log's line ends *52 rows in 8 phases landed.* A template with lines alone,
  saved before the builder, and the rough's own copy of a template's lines draw as before.
- `GcTemplatePick` says D3's six labelled lines (`rowsFit`, `rowsFitLines`) for a template with rows: the rows and
  phases with the weeks, the rows on the job's trades and the trades with no package yet, ours, the office's, the
  dates to meet and the scope lines in no row. A template with lines alone keeps `templateFitWords`.
- `draftForRpc` (`writes.ts`) sends `phases` and each bar's and date's `phase` to `gc_schedule_draft` (v2.6020), and
  `schedule/rows.ts` reads them back onto the schedule, its activities and its milestones.

## On the job (D4)

- **By phase** (`ganttGroups(bars, 'phase', phases)`, `gantt.ts`): the template's phases in the schedule's order, each
  bar in the phase it was drawn in, the bars in none last under *Not in a phase*. The chart (`GcGantt.tsx`) opens By
  phase when the schedule has phases and offers it beside By trade, By stage and By company; the phone's list
  (`ganttListGroups`) groups by the phases instead of the stages. A phase row reads its start, finish, days and
  percent from its rows as every group does.
- **The list** (`GcGanttList.tsx`) gains a Days column (both ends counted) and a Spare column (`GanttBar.spare`,
  red at none), for the office's view only. **No company yet** (`noCompanyYet` in `gantt.ts`, the owner's call 7)
  shows under a bar drawn from a template's row for a trade the job has no package for, or on a package nobody is
  awarded on yet, as a door to Trade partners at that trade (`tradePartnersHref`).
- An added row drawn onto a trade's package (`addedOf`, `schedule.ts`) carries that package, its trade and its
  company once awarded, as a line of the package reads; the job's own work with no package reads as before.
- The draw's own final inspection (`rowsDraft`, `builder.ts`): when the template has no row for it, it now comes
  after every rowed bar too, never on a weekend when the template counts working days, with the rowed bars nothing
  waits on among its waits, so substantial completion follows the rows. Before, a template of Office rows longer
  than the job's lines put it in the second week.
- **The Templates card** under a building job's chart keeps Save as a template, which saves the schedule's rows
  beside its lines (v2.6022), and opens the Templates page for everything else; its per-template rows, Rename and
  Set it aside live on the page.
