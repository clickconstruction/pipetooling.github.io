# v2.7073 — My email: Tag is now Save to job (2026-11-12)

Putting a conversation from your own mail on a job copies it into the app whole: each mail as a line, its files, and the raw mail. After that nothing depends on Gmail. *Tag* suggested a mark on the mail and a live link back to it, and until v2.7035 the app did put a label there. With the label gone, the owner asked for the honest word, 2026-11-12: *perhaps we should make the button save to job as that would save the email to that job? then it doesn't matter what happens in a user's email?* and *I think we should rename it.*

Words only. Nothing about what the press does has changed.

## The words

| Was | Is |
|---|---|
| **Tag…** | **Save to job…** |
| **Yes** on a guess | **Yes** (unchanged) |
| **To tag · Tagged · All** | **Not saved · Saved · All** |
| *Tagging…* | *Saving…* |
| *Tagged onto JP1077 4 Elm Ct. 1 line and 68 files are on the job now.* | *Saved to JP1077 4 Elm Ct. 1 line and 68 files are on the job now.* |
| **Untag** | **Take off the job** |
| *Untagged. 2 lines came off the job.* | *Taken off the job. 2 lines came off.* |
| *Tag this conversation onto* (the picker) | *Save this conversation to* |
| **Tag it** (a sent mail, on a thread) | **Save it** |
| *… tagged this conversation from their mail.* (the grey line) | *… saved this conversation from their mail.* |

- **The sentence by the press.** The line over the list now reads: *Nothing is kept until you save it. Saving puts the whole conversation on the job for the office to read. Later mail in it follows by itself.* So *save* does not read as private, or as a one-time copy.
- **The grey line** keeps both of its forms from v2.7020: a person's own save, and *… saved this conversation from <Name>'s mail, through the dev seat.* for a save the dev seat made.
- **The confirm** before taking a conversation off the job says what goes, and its red press is **Take off the job**.
- **What a person meets when it fails**: *The save stopped partway.* and *Taking it off the job stopped partway.*, in place of the tag's and the untag's.
- The settings card, the app-placed line's press, and the owner's notice from v2.7068 all read in the new words.

## Two calls made here

**A bid: one press, not two.** The press is **Save to job…** whether the conversation ends up on a job or a bid. The press is drawn before the person has picked where it goes, so it cannot know. The picker lists jobs and bids side by side under *Save this conversation to*, and the notice afterwards names what it landed on, a bid by its own number. A second label, *Save to bid…*, would need the row to guess the kind first, and would be wrong whenever the guess was.

**The guide's address changes, and the old one still works.** The guide is now *save an email to a job from your own mail*, at `/help/save-an-email-to-a-job-from-your-own-mail`. Its address had the old word in it, and a person who searches help for *save* should find it by its name. The old address is kept through `helpGuideFormerSlugs.ts` (v2.7050): a saved link, a bookmark or a texted share page opens the renamed guide. The old words stay in the guide's keywords, so a search for *tag* or *untag* still finds it.

## Left as they are

- **Internal names**: `email_tag_threads`, the `tag` and `untag` actions, `plan_mail_tag`, the `?view=tagged` address, function and file names. Only what a person reads changed.
- **The dev seat's own words.** Its verbs are `plan_mail_tag` and `apply_mail_untag`, and its replies speak of a tag. A developer reads those, beside the verb's name.
- **Lines already on a thread.** A grey line is written once, when the conversation is saved. Lines written before this deploy say *tagged* and stay that way. Stored rows are not rewritten.
- The other guide, *tag an email onto a job from Gmail*, is about forwarding a mail to a shared inbox with a job's number in the subject. It is a different act and keeps its name.

## Tests

Every test that pinned an old word now pins the new one, across the kernels (`view`, `list`, `reader`, `settingCard`, `mineFunction`), the function's own handler on the fakes (the grey line in both forms, the failure words) and the render tests of the tab, the row, the reader, the settings card and the two thread strips. The guide tests hold the renamed guide to plain words and to working links, and `helpGuideFormerSlugs.test.ts` holds the old address to the new guide.

## Deploys

`gmail-mine` from `main` after the merge: it writes the grey line and the failure words. The rest is the client.
