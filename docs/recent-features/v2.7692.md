# v2.7692 — Checklist: Today reads its day through one tested place (2026-11-17)

Punch list #381, step 1 of five. No change on screen, no migration, no edge function.

Checklist → Today read the signed-in person inside `ChecklistTodayTab`, in three inline queries. Nothing else could draw that day. The owner asked for a window on Review that shows another person's Task Mode screen, and that window has to draw the same day the person's phone draws. So the reads now take the person as a value.

## What moved

- **`src/lib/tasks/personDay.ts`**, the rules with no read in them. `orderTodayRows` puts today's tasks in the order the office set for the person, a task with no order last. `olderRowsToRead` names the older open rows whose threads decide what they are. `olderOpenParts` sorts the older open rows into still open, missed and already answered as skipped. The row types `PersonDayRow` and `RoadmapTaskEmbed` moved here from the page.
- **`src/lib/tasks/personDayIo.ts`**, the reads. `readPersonDay(personId, todayYmd)` is today's rows, the older open rows under their cap of 300, the threads of the repeating ones and the person's order. `readPersonAhead(personId, todayYmd)` is the next 30 tasks dated ahead. The select strings are the page's own, unchanged.
- **`ChecklistTodayTab`** calls the two reads with `authUserId`. `loadToday` and `loadUpcoming` keep their names and what they set.

## What did not change

A failed read of today's rows still draws the retry panel with its reason. A miss on a later read still leaves that part empty. The older rows keep their order: a task past its due day first, then oldest first.

## Tests

- `personDay.test.ts`, 9 cases: the order, a task with no order, the three parts, a day sent back, a day reopened and finished again, a skipped day.
- `personDayIo.test.ts`, 5 cases: every read that names a person names the one it was given, the threads are read only for repeating days, a failure stops the read.
- The page's own render tests pass unchanged: `Checklist.today`, `Checklist.todayParts`, `Checklist.todayForward` and `windowInsideRow`, 22 cases.
