# v2.3893 — Dispatch Mode’s Schedule: tap a block for its sheet, with Move or reassign (2026-09-27)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. The owner's pick of 2026-09-27: Dispatch Mode's Schedule gets the same block sheet as the Schedule page's Day tab ([v2.3885](v2.3885.md)). This is the screen the phone dock's Schedule slot opens; it could change a block's time and day, but not whose it is.

## What shipped

- **`DispatchModeSchedule.tsx`** — for someone who can edit the schedule, a tap on a block's job opens `ScheduleBlockSheet`: *Open the job* (the job window), the note (the edit-block window, which holds it), *Move or reassign*, *Remove from the schedule* (the shared confirm). The tap on the time still opens the edit-block window. A reader's tap opens the job, as before; Job Mode's own-schedule view (`selfUserId`) is unchanged.
- **Move or reassign** is `ScheduleDispatchMoveBlockSheet` over the two visible weeks and the dispatch roster. Saving reads the block's own row — and its linked crew's rows when it has one — and goes through `moveScheduleDispatchBlockTo`, the one move path the grids and the hub's sheet use, so a linked crew moves together and the same-person overlap check runs. The roster loads the first time the move sheet or the crew window opens.
- **One tap more to the job for an editor**: the job is now the sheet's first row instead of the tap itself.
- **The guide** *dispatch-mode* → the Schedule tab.
