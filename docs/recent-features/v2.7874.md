# v2.7874 — Lien desk: no window puts the paper tabs back under as it widens (2026-11-18)

v2.7789 put By paper's three paper tabs on the title line where the measured line holds them, and reported one band it left as measured: from 1,132 to 1,175 px of window they dropped back to the second line, though they fit just below and above. The owner, 2026-11-18: *"please take care of"* that band.

## Why the band was there

`index.css` draws the Lien desk's title bar in two ways by the window's width. From 641 to 1,131 px it was the tighter bar: an 8 px column gap, 7 px of side padding on a tab, and Share as its icon. From 1,132 px it was the roomier bar: a 14.4 px gap, 8 px of padding, and Share with its word. The tighter bar's title line held the papers from about 1,100 px. The roomier one needed 1,176. Between the two, a wider window had less room than a narrower one.

## What changed

- **The tighter bar runs to 1,279 px**, where it ran to 1,131. `LIEN_DESK_TIGHT_BAR_MAX_PX` in `lienDeskTitleBar.ts` names the edge, and the media query in `index.css` uses it.
- **Both bars hold the papers at the edge between them.** `LIEN_DESK_TITLE_LINE_WIDEST` holds each bar's longest title line, measured with three-figure counts on every tab: the tighter bar needs 1,064 px of its 1,191 px line at 1,279 px of window, the roomier one 1,139 of its 1,192 at 1,280.
- **The papers' place is still measured.** `lienDeskPapersFit` decides it from the line the bar drew, as before. The width rule only says which bar is drawn.

## What it costs

A window from 1,132 to 1,279 px shows Share as its icon on every view of the desk, and its tabs sit 1 px closer on each side. The word is still the button's name. A window of 1,280 px or wider is as it was.
