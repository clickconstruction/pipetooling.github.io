# v2.2112 — Partner week-card lines reconcile to the journal amount (2026-08-22)

Follow-up to v2.2111 from the owner's "the math does not flow" thread. Walking all 20 of Bryan's week cards in the live app (partner view, DOM-read) showed the chain itself is right — every opening equals the prior closing back to 2026-03-22 — but on three cards the visible lines summed to a cent off the closing: 05-31 (tiers 696.29 vs `gross_pay` 696.28), 04-26 (869.80 vs 869.79), 03-29 (1520.69 vs 1520.70). Root cause: `journalRowToLines` expanded a labor row into the stub's stamped `day_rates` tiers and showed each tier's own rounded amount, while the journal row — and the office Ledger tab — run on `gross_pay`. The tiers are per-day rounded sums, so their total can miss the gross by a penny.

- **Kernel** [`partnerWeeks.ts`](../../src/lib/partnerLedger/partnerWeeks.ts): new exported `reconcileLines(lines, target)` pushes any residual (target − Σ money lines) onto the largest money line, leaving no-amount lines (pending sessions) alone. Labor tiers reconcile to the journal row's `amount`; the live card's so-far lines (field/estimating) reconcile to `gross_so_far`, since the live closing is `posted + gross_so_far`. Invariant now: **opening + Σ lines = closing, to the penny, on every card** (+5 tests, incl. the Bryan 05-31 shape and a multi-tier case).
- `fmtRate`: whole-dollar rates stay `$50`; fractional rates print `$37.50` (was `$37.5`).
- [`DashboardPartnerLedgerSection.tsx`](../../src/components/dashboard/DashboardPartnerLedgerSection.tsx): handoff loose end — a closed card with no `stubId` (charge-only week, possible since v2.2111) no longer offers **Print / save PDF** / **Acknowledge statement** (it printed a "Partner weekly statement" for a week that never had one); it shows "Charges only — no statement was issued this week." Help guide `see-your-partner-ledger` updated.

Client-only — no migration. Hours remain shown to one decimal; `h × rate` is a label, not a formula the reader can reproduce to the cent (stub hours are exact clock time), and the week-to-week chain is what the owner asked to flow.
