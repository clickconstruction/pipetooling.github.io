# v2.7543 — Checklist: draw a map for your task, or leave a point on one (2026-11-16)

Punch list #369, step C2 of the Task Mode train (tool 8, second half), stacked on C1 (v2.7542). The owner's ask: "the same way they can add a note or a photo, they can also draw a map? I think that on top of drawing a map they should also be able to leave a point on the map." No migration of its own: it calls `task_add_map` from C1's.

## What changed

- **🗺 Map on a task with no map**, unlit, beside **＋ Note** and **📷 Photo** (`TaskDoerMapPress`). It shows on an open task and on a done one.
- **A press opens *A map for this task*** (`TaskChoiceSheet`), with three ways in (`taskMapStarts`):
  - **Draw it by hand**: the painting window opens on a line.
  - **Walk or drive it**: the phone paints the line. Left out on a device that cannot say where it is.
  - **Leave a point**: the painting window opens on a place, which takes a name, words and a photo.
- **The painting window is the one that exists** (`paintPaintedMap`). The task's words name the map, and the window opens at the task's place when it has one.
- **When the window closes with a line or a place saved, the map goes on the task** (`addTaskMap` → `task_add_map`). The press then lights and opens the map, as step B3 drew it.
- **Nothing saved is nothing changed.** A refusal from the database shows under the press in its own words.

## The calls that stand

- **Only a task with no map takes one.** A task that has a map keeps it, and the person adds to it. The database holds this.
- **The map is on no job.** It is its painter's and the office's, and whoever reads the task reads it. The office puts it on a job later, as for a map painted from New task (v2.7366).
- **A point is a place on the map, not the task's place.** The task's own place still comes only from where the person stands (v2.7067).

## How it is built

- `src/lib/tasks/taskDoerActs.ts`, the words and small rules of the doer's tools, and `taskDoerActsIo.ts`, their calls. This step adds the map's part of each.
- The flow is `TaskMapPress`'s of v2.7366: the press makes the map's id and reads the map back by that id when the window closes.
- The `fresh` card takes `onMapAdded`, and Today hands it the reload of the tasks' places and maps.
