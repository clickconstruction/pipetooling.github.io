# v2.4558 — Documents: lien emails are kept as they went (2026-10-05)

Step 2 of the Sent copies train ([`docs/SENT_COPIES.md`](../SENT_COPIES.md)), second part: the lien papers and the hazmat notice, on the helper [v2.4557](v2.4557.md) added. No migration. **Three functions to redeploy**: `send-lien-filing-email`, `send-lien-release-email`, `send-hazmat-notice-email`.

## The change

Each function calls `fileSentEmailBestEffort` after its send; none of the three kept the message or the PDF before (a notice kept its `fields`, a release its signed PDF but not the email, a hazmat notice nothing).

- `send-lien-filing-email`: `lien_notice`, or `demand_letter` for `email_type: 'demand_letter'`; the job; `recipient_label` as the name. The filing's row id is not in the request, so the copy is found by the job.
- `send-lien-release-email`: `lien_release`; the job; the customer it went to (the GC when the address is the GC's billing email); the release as its source. Filed after the sent stamp, and whether or not the stamp took: the email went either way.
- `send-hazmat-notice-email`: `hazmat_notice`; the job, its customer, the incident as its source.

`EMAILS_OWED` in `sentCopiesEmailCoverage.test.ts` drops the three (20 left).

## Tests

The ratchet (3). The edge functions have no local type check; the first live send of each after the deploy is the proof, on that job's Documents tab.
