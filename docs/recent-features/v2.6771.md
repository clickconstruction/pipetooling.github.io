- **The sheet on a bill** (`TellCustomerSheet.tsx`): a `bill` prop (`TellBill`: the row, the amount, the link) and a `zIndex` prop for a caller inside another window; the subline *The bill for the water heater · $1,850.00*; *from the company number (512) 555-0100* beside the To chip, read once from the team box through the io's new `loadFromNumber` (`boxSetting('team', loadBoxSettings())`), the words alone while Settings holds none.
- **A first text says who is writing** (`src/lib/tellCustomer/firstTextIntro.ts`, unit-tested): `firstTextReason` runs the box's own opt-out rule (`textOut.optoutReason`) over the thread tail the sheet already reads (`TellContact.lines`, by number); `introPresent` says whether the words name the sender's first name or Click Plumbing; `withOpeningBack` puts the line's first sentence back once.
