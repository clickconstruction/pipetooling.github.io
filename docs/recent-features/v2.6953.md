# v2.6953 — Lien affidavits print with a help sheet, and a guide copy to learn from (2026-11-11)

Punch list #314. The office's first signed batch of short-form affidavits (2026-11-11) could not be filed as it stood: the county blank at the top held the property's county on every sheet, no notary seal showed, and several papers should have been stopped before they printed. The owner asked for help sheets printed with the papers, saw three mock-ups (`to-dos/lien-help-sheets/`), and said: *build it all I like it all.*

## What changed

- **`src/lib/jobs/lienHelpSheet.ts`** (pure): the words and the HTML of three sheets.
  - **A, the cover sheet** (`lienHelpCoverSheetHtml`): one page in front of one paper, with the job, the property, the claim, the file-by day, the stop list, the six blanks, and the steps after the notary with the clerk's county and the two addresses the copies go to.
  - **B, the batch sheet** (`lienHelpBatchSheetHtml`): the blanks and the stop list in a few words, the steps, then a row for each paper with its clerk, who gets a copy, and five boxes: signed, sealed, filed, in the app, mailed. A paper whose property an earlier paper of the batch is on reads *Ask first* (`lienHelpSameProperty`). A paper left out is listed with its reason.
  - **C, the guide copy** (`lienHelpGuideCopyHtml` over `lienHelpMarkPaper`): the paper's own HTML with a numbered mark beside each blank and beside the owner, month and notice lines, the notes down the side, and a band at the top and the foot saying it is never signed or filed.
  - `lienHelpPaperFromFields` reads every fact off the affidavit's own fields, so the sheet and the paper cannot disagree.
- **`prependToFilingPrintHtml`** in `lienFilingDocuments.ts` puts a section first in a print sheet's body and its styles in the head. The paper's HTML after it is untouched.
- **The Affidavits list** (`LienDesk.tsx`): **Print N for notarization** prints the batch sheet first (`lienAffidavitBatchHelpSheet`, `lienAffidavitPrintRun(papers, helpSheet)`), with the earlier-form papers listed as not printed.
- **The Lien window** (`LienFilingTabs.tsx`): **Print for notarization** on a short-form affidavit prints the cover sheet first; **Print a guide copy** prints C alone.
- **Print the help sheet first**, a box beside each press (`lienHelpSheetChoice.ts`), ticked until someone unticks it. The choice is a convenience kept on that computer.

## A help sheet is never the paper

It carries no sworn word and every page says not to file it. A print still counts as a send (`docs/SENT_COPIES.md`), and the copy filed on each job is the paper alone: the batch's `copies` are built without the sheet, and the Lien window files the paper's HTML, not the print's. The guide copy files nothing, since nothing was sent.

## Proof

Headless Chrome on the kernel's own output with made-up facts: the cover sheet is one page with the paper on the next, the guide copy is one page, and a batch of 12 prints the sheet on two pages (every instruction on the first, rows 9 to 12 on the second) and then 12 papers. A batch of eight or fewer fits one page.

## Not in it

- Help sheets for the § 53.056 notice and the retainage notice. The card says how they would read.
- The earlier ten-paragraph form: the sheets are written for the short form's blanks.
- A hold for a public owner, and a flag for a month with no timely notice. Each is its own PR on the card.
