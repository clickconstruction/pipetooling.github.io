# v2.4455 — Where the checks went: a move and a deposit read their own day (2026-10-02)

One of the instant-day fixes that follow [v2.4452](v2.4452.md). No migration. The kernel is shared, so `gc-statement-email-dispatch` is redeployed after merge.

## What was wrong

[`_shared/gcChecksApplied.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/gcChecksApplied.ts) cut two days out of instants by their first ten characters:

- a move's day (`CheckMove.onYmd`) from `jobs_ledger_payment_events.created_at`, with `.slice(0, 10)`;
- a deposit's day (`depositedYmd`) from `mercury_transactions.posted_at`, through the file's `ymd` helper, which slices too. A grep for `_at.slice` misses this one because the slice sits in the helper.

Both columns are `timestamptz`. A move recorded or a deposit posted after 7 pm CDT (6 pm CST) read as the next day: *moved … on Oct 3*, *was on … until Oct 3*, *deposited Oct 3*.

## What changed

Both read `todayYmdInAppTz(new Date(iso))` from [`_shared/appTimeZone.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/appTimeZone.ts). `paid_on` and `sent_on` are `date` columns and keep the slice.

Every writer of `posted_at` lands on the right Central day: the Mercury sync stores Mercury's own `postedAt` instant, and a manual import stamps `<date>T12:00:00Z` (`import-manual-transactions`). The office's deposit row ([`ArDepositRow`](../../src/components/jobs/ar/ArDepositRow.tsx)) and Banking already show `posted_at` in `APP_CALENDAR_TZ`; Find a check now agrees with them.

Where it shows: Find a check (`checkHeadline`, `checkMoveWords`), the printed *Where the checks went* sheet and its CSV (*Deposited*, *Was on*), and the moves under *Your payments* on the customer portal. The portal's payload passes a move's `created_at` through whole ([`portalChecks.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/portalChecks.ts), [`portalPayload.ts`](../../src/lib/portal/portalPayload.ts)), so the page's kernel reads the instant. The portal carries no deposits.

The scheduled GC statement (`gc-statement-email-dispatch`) bundles the kernel, but its payments block prints only the received day (`paid_on`), so what it sends does not change. It is redeployed so the bundle matches the repo.

## Tests

`gcChecksApplied.test.ts` (+4): a move at `2026-10-03T00:30:00Z` (7:30 pm CDT) reads Oct 2, in `checkMoveWords` too; a deposit posted then, in PostgREST's `+00:00` shape, reads *deposited Oct 2* in `checkHeadline`; 6:30 pm CST in December reads Dec 1; noon UTC reads its own day. The three evening cases fail on main's kernel.

Two fixtures stamped a day as midnight UTC, which is 7 pm the evening before in Central: the deposit in `gcChecksAppliedReport.test.ts` and the move in `PortalPaymentsSection.render.test.tsx`. They move to midday and keep their expectations, since they test the sheet and the section, not the zone.

## Checked against prod

Read only, from the worktree dev server: no payment move is recorded yet, and none of the 138 deposits matched to a payment posted in a Central evening. No row reads differently today; the fix is for the next one.
