# v2.7063 — Roadmap: a roadmap task can have a place (2026-11-12)

Punch list #328, PR 5 of 8. A third of the tasks written in the last 90 days came from a roadmap, and they are the ones most often at a spot on a property. Until now only a task written in the Add task window could have a place.

## Where the place lives, and why

On the roadmap task (`checklist_tech_tree_group_tasks`), not on the checklist row made from it. `sync_roadmap_to_checklist` makes that row when the task's turn comes and deletes it when the task falls back behind an earlier one, so a place on the made row would be lost on the round trip. The card reads the roadmap task's place through `roadmap_group_task_id`, the way it already reads the photo switch. **The sync function is not touched**, so nothing the client calls is replaced.

## What changed

- **The columns** (`20261112230000_roadmap_task_places.sql`): the same six and the same three checks as v2.7058, on the roadmap task.
- **Setting it.** The roadmap's Add task window gains the Place row. A task's card window gains it too (`RoadmapTaskPlaceSection.tsx`), and since that window saves each edit by itself, so does a press on a chip. A save that fails says why and puts the row back. Someone who cannot plan the roadmap sees the place and the way there, with no row to change it.
- **Reading it.** `loadTaskPlacesForItems` now reads in up to two steps, each on its own: the tasks' own places, then the roadmap tasks behind the ones with none. A task's own place wins. If the first read fails on the place columns alone, the link is asked for bare, so a roadmap's places still come.
- **The chips.** `loadRecentTaskPlaces` offers the places of tasks and of roadmap tasks together, each table read on its own.

## Tests

- `taskPlaceIo.test.ts` (new, 9): a task's own place, a roadmap task's behind it, the own place winning, each read failing on its own, the chips from both tables, the save's six columns, its six nulls and its refusal.
- `RoadmapTaskPlaceSection.render.test.tsx` (new, 5): the row lit for a planner, a press saving itself, Remove the place, a failed save's words and the row put back, and what someone who cannot plan sees.
- The roadmap card window's two existing test files pass unchanged.
