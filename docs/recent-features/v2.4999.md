# v2.4999 — GC projects: email the certified bill and change orders to the customer (2026-10-08)

Owner Billing's O4b-2 in the GC mode real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/OWNER_BILLING_REAL_BUILD.md` → *Edge functions*, on branch `spike/gc-mode`): `gc-customer-email` (v2.4998) gains two kinds, each behind its own tick, off to start. Guides: [`bill-the-customer-on-a-gc-job`](../../src/content/help/bill-the-customer-on-a-gc-job.md), [`change-our-contract-with-the-customer`](../../src/content/help/change-our-contract-with-the-customer.md). No migration. The function needs redeploying.

- **`certified`** (Bill the customer → **Record the certificate**, tick **Email the customer the bill now**):
  - `gc_record_certificate` runs first, then the email: what the architect certified, what was cut and that it comes back on the next bill (never on the final one), *We expect it by …* (`ownerExpectPaidOn` with the certificate's day, as the window's due chip reads it) and *Reply with the day you will pay.*
  - No Pay button: the certified bill is a plain billed row with no Stripe invoice. `/pay/<id>` answers *not found* for it, and the portal lists it with no PAY ONLINE.
  - When the customer already has a portal link, the function adds *You can see this bill in your portal:* and the link (`loadPortalReturnUrl`, never minted, https only).
  - Turning the bill into a Stripe invoice from the tick is the owner's call, as its own PR.
- **`change_order`** (Change orders → **Send for signature**, tick **Email it to the customer now**):
  - `gc_send_change_order` runs first, then the email. It carries the change, what it adds to or takes off the price (a time extension: *It does not change your price.*) and its days, and they sign by reply.
  - There is no change order form yet, so the words carry it.
  - A change order sent without the tick keeps the words to send from your own email.
- **The function** checks the row each kind is about (`GC_CUSTOMER_EMAIL_SOURCE`):
  - another project's row is `otherProject`;
  - a pay application with no certificate, or one of $0, is `notCertified` for `certified`;
  - a change order not out for signature is `notSent`.
- **Sent copies:** the certified bill files as its own kind, `bill_gc_certified`, under Bills, so the window tells it from the pay application's `bill_gc_pay_app`. A change order files as `job_contract_gc_change_order`, under Contracts, against `gc_change_orders`.
- **The windows read the copies back:**
  - a sent bill shows *Emailed to …* then *The certified bill was emailed to …* (`emailedWords`);
  - a change order shows *Emailed to … on …* in place of the words to send yourself (`loadGcChangeOrderEmails`).
- **The words** (`src/lib/gc/customerEmail.ts`) are the prototype's `customerMessages`, without its portal Pay and portal signing. What customers see shows both as samples (`gc-certified`, with a sample portal link, and `gc-change-order`).
- **Tests:**
  - `customerEmail.test.ts`: the words, a cut, the final one, a credit, a time extension, the facts, each kind's party, source and filed kind, the portal line (https only), the *Emailed to* lines and the new refusals;
  - the two windows' render tests: each tick off to start, the call with it on, and the emailed lines.
- **Also:** the bill-the-customer guide said the certified bill shows on the customer's statement *with Pay*. It now says the bill shows on their statement in their portal.
