# v2.5450 — Messages: quick replies for the tech’s phone, in Spanish, with blanks the app fills (2026-10-13)

Residential R3's screens, PR B of 2 (`to-dos/gc-mode/messages/RESIDENTIAL_BUILD_PLAN.md` → R3 on `spike/gc-mode`), by Customer communication helper 10, directed by cc. It stacks on PR A (v2.5448, the kernel and the IO), which carries GC Review's editor (#5609), #5558, #5588 and #5596. The screens are the residential prototype's (`customer-messages-prototype.html` v0.8: `phoneTab`, `editRow`, `chipsHtml`, `previewHtml`, `flags`, the strip's `markBlanks`). Guide `edit-the-quick-replies-for-the-phone` is new. No table and no function change. Like PR A, it needs migration `20261013210000` on prod first.

- **The Phone tab** (`reply/QuickRepliesPhoneTab.tsx`, new): it draws in place of the table once a line is on the phone.
  - The phone's lines in their places. Each shows its short name (`shortName`), its words filled with the blanks lit, its step and its use.
  - **↑** and **↓** save the places that changed (`movePhone`). **off** saves place 0. Each is one `edit_quick_reply` per line, then the window reads again.
  - A line past six says it waits. The foot says six reach the phone and **Something else** is last.
  - When the last line comes off, the tab goes away and the window falls back to All.
- **The marks**: a row wears **☎ phone** when it is on the phone. A line for customers wears **es**, or a dashed **no es** while it has no Spanish (`lineMarks`).
- **A line opened in place** (`reply/QuickRepliesEditorLine.tsx`). Every field joins the `data-qr-line-fields` section:
  - Under the words, a chip for each blank (`BLANK_CHIPS`), which puts it at the cursor (`insertBlank`). Then a preview of the line as it would go to the thread's person, with what the box leaves to fill (`preview`).
  - Under the tags, the **Spanish** box on a line for customers (`spanishOffered`). Then the tick **On the tech’s phone**, which says why when the line cannot go (`phoneTick`), and **Its short name on the phone**, with the line's own first sentence as its placeholder.
  - A ticked line goes after the last (`nextPhonePlace`). A line moved away from customers comes off the phone on its Save.
  - Save sends R3's parts only when one changed, so a Save of words and tags alone stays the four named arguments.
- **The step picker** (`QuickReplyTagFields`): a kind with two flows lists each flow's steps under its name (Residential customer, GC customer), as the prototype's `stepOptions` did.
- **The strip and Find a reply**: each line reads as it would go (`reply/FilledLine.tsx`, new). Its blanks are filled from what the box knows, and a blank it cannot fill is lit amber.
- **The reply box**:
  - A press puts the line in the box with what it knows filled (`replyFills`): their first name, the signed-in person's first name, and the tied job's name.
  - A text or an email waits while a blank is left. The words under the box say which, in amber (`sendWaits`), and Send stays off until the person fills it. A note never waits.
- **Not yet**:
  - The Spanish on the strip waits for #5589's `Contact.lang`, so the strip reads the English.
  - The address, the drive, the schedule, the page's link and the money wait for their readers in R5 and Job Mode. Until then they stay lit for the person to fill.
  - The add row keeps the words, the tags and Just for me (PR A's note).
- **Kernel**: `src/lib/quickReplies/filling.ts` (new) holds `BLANK_CHIPS`, `insertBlank`, `preview`, `replyFills` and `sendWaits`, with its test.
- **Smokes** (`reply/QuickRepliesPhone.render.test.tsx`, new):

  - the tab's order, a move up in two saves, and off in one;
  - a line waiting past six;
  - a customer line's Spanish, tick and name saved together;
  - a chip at the cursor, and the flow groups in the step picker;
  - a trade partner's line with no Spanish and a disabled tick;
  - a line moved off customers coming off the phone;
  - the strip's lit blank, the box filled on a press, and Send waiting until the blank is filled.
