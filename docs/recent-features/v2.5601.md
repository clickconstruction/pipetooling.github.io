# v2.5601 — Prospects: the person we call comes first, and a homeowner is a person (2026-10-17)

Punch list #117, PR 2 of the train (the columns are v2.5600's). Every prospect is a person we call, sometimes at a company: a homeowner on Jobs to GC, an estimator at a sub, a GC's project manager. The form made the company the record and the person one box under it, so a homeowner was typed as a company, and a sub's estimator had no title and no cell of their own.

- **`src/lib/prospects/personBlock.ts`** (new, pure; 8 tests): `PersonFields` and `CompanyFields`, `blocksFromRow` and `rowFromBlocks` (a person on their own has their name in `company_name` so every reader keeps working, `is_person` true, `contact_name` always the person), `blocksProblem` (a name for one or the other), `prospectTitle`, `personLineParts` (the person unless they are the title, their title, their language), `textAskWords` (*text? not asked* · *OK to text* · *no texts*) and `callNumbers` (the cell first, the office line named beside it).
- **`components/prospects/ProspectPersonForm.tsx`** (new; 2 render tests): the two blocks New Prospect and Edit prospect share, **The person we call** (Name, Title, Cell, Email, Language, May we text?) and **The company** (Company, Office phone, Address, Website, Also reach), with the double line in a slot between them. The company hint reads *leave it empty for a person on their own*, or *the company on the bench* on a sub.
- **`Prospects.tsx`**: both windows hold the two blocks as state, read them from a row and write them back through the kernel; Language moves out of the sub-only kind fields to the person on every kind; the deck reads the seven new columns; the card's title is `prospectTitle`, its second line the person's name, title and language with the texting chip, the phone button dials `callNumbers`' cell with the office line beside it, and **Also reach** shows when filled; the v2.5582 leave guards read the blocks.
