# v2.7323 — Lien desk: the section heads sit closer together (2026-11-15)

The owner, on Monthly notices with JP372's pane scrolled to the paper (2026-11-15): he likes how the grey section titles work, but without making the words smaller they can sit closer together, so the stack over the paper takes less of the pane. Six bars — the five section heads and the first page label — were 180 px of header. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## Measured before (the dev server against prod, 1,600 px, JP372 scrolled to the cover letter)

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| one bar | 30 px | 24 px |
| the uppercase label's line box (0.62rem) | 14.88 px, 7.55 px of air over and under | 11.91 px, 6.05 px |
| the bold fact's line box (0.78rem) | 18.72 px, 5.64 px of air | 14.97 px, 4.52 px |
| the stack over the paper (five heads and the first page label) | 180 px | 144 px |
| the list's pile title at the left | 30 px | 24 px |
| a phone's pinned head | 40 px | 40 px |

The air is measured from the bar's edge to the line box; the glyphs are the same size on both sides of the table. The line box shrank because the bar's line-height is 1.2 now instead of the body's 1.5: every bar holds one line that never wraps, so the 1.5 only padded the box, and at 1.2 the fact's box is 15 px. 24 is the smallest even number that keeps 4 px over and under that box (23 is the floor); the extra pixel keeps the air at 4 when the box rounds.

## What changed

- **One number.** [`src/lib/jobs/lienPhoneLists.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienPhoneLists.ts): `LIEN_PILE_HEAD_H` is 24 with the measure above in its comment. [`lienPaneSections.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienPaneSections.ts): `LIEN_PANE_HEAD_H` *is* that constant (it had its own 30), `LIEN_PANE_HEAD_H_PHONE` the list's 40, and `lienPaneHeadOffsets` answers `height` beside `top` and `bottom`, as `lienPileHeadPlace` does, so [`LienPaneHead.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienPaneHead.tsx) sets the height inline from the kernel on both screens (the phone's inline 40 moved into the kernel). `LienDesk.tsx`: `PAGE_LABEL_H` is `LIEN_PANE_HEAD_H`, the page labels set their height inline too, and on a phone a label is the pinned head's 40 px with its 0.75rem words and the labels stack under that one head (`headsOverPages`) — until now they stacked under five heads' worth that a phone never pins, 150 px, so a stuck label floated under the head with paper between.
- **CSS.** `.lienPileHead` in [`src/index.css`](../../src/index.css): `height: 24px` as the base and `line-height: 1.2`; the font sizes, weights, letter spacing, the padding, the blue inset bar and the right-hand fact and tail are untouched.
- **The list's pile titles** take the same 24: their first bar sits beside the pane's first head at the top of the desk, so a 30 beside a 24 would have read as two kinds of bar. The Notices list's titles hold a 9.92 px label, a count, and in the mailed pile two small presses (18 px tall), all of which fit in the 22 px inside the borders; the Affidavits and Retainage titles that v2.7316 stacks the same way hold the same parts.
- **Tests.** `lienPaneSections.test.ts`, `lienPhoneLists.test.ts` and the three `LienDesk.render.test.tsx` smokes that pinned 30 now pin 24, each with one line saying why; the lit-section thresholds read `400 − 3·H` instead of 310. [`docs/LIEN_DESK_ARCHITECTURE.md`](../LIEN_DESK_ARCHITECTURE.md): the four sentences that said 30.

## Not changed

- The words: every font size, weight and letter spacing is as it was. The phone's pinned head stays 40 (a thumb tall is a tap rule, well over the text floor). The strip, the title bar and the footer are other sessions' surfaces and are untouched.
