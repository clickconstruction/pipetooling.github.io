# v2.6214 — The Questions look on a Development page: two presses, then the questions in tables (2026-10-30)

Feature, no migration, no edge function. The owner on the Gun Dog page's Questions look: *I don't think we built this the right way, I think that these could just be buttons. And if there are outstanding questions, then they should show up in tables below* (punch list #208).

## What it was

With no question the look borrowed the Waiting count's card body: a *Questions* group with two kind rows (*To the estimators · a bid, its plans, its price* and *To Dispatch*) carrying the Ask presses at the right, then *Answers from the customer* and *Payments* groups with their state words. With questions it was a stack of `QuestionRow` cards. The presses sat inside a list that read like data, and the two other groups are the Waiting tile's business, not questions.

## Change

- `src/lib/developments/questionsLook.ts` (new, tested): `QUESTION_LANES` (the two presses and their hints), `questionTables` (one table per lane with any question, open oldest first, answered newest first), `questionAnswer` (the close note, else the newest reply with its author), `questionTableMeta`, `answeredFoldLabel`, `NO_QUESTIONS_WORDS`.
- `DevelopmentQuestionsLook.tsx` (new, render-tested): the two `SmallButton` presses, then per lane a head (*To the estimators · 2 open · 1 answered*) and a table — Asked, Waiting (the age in amber, or *answered* with the day), About (a door), Question, Answer (*nothing yet* in faint type until one comes), Asked by — with the answered rows folded under *Show the N answered* while any is open. A phone draws the same rows as `QuestionRow` cards. No question at all: `EmptyState` with the good-state words.
- `DevelopmentPage.tsx` draws it in the Questions Card with `onAsk={(lane) => openAsk({ lane })}`; `DevelopmentQuestionsCard` is gone from `DevelopmentSideCards.tsx` (`QuestionRow` stays). The Waiting tile's card keeps its kinds.
- The guide *see everything on a development* describes the look.
