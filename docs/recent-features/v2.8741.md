# v2.8741 — The material location report: Close takes its mark out of the address (2026-11-27)

Punch list #330. Finding 325 of the fixing thread's ledger, from the walking thread's sitting 35 of 2026-11-27 (1280 px, reads only). Client only. No migration, no edge function. Nothing owed after the merge.

**As seen.** **Print the material location report** on a site's materials screen goes to `/developments/<id>?report=materials`, where the paper's window opens by itself. After **Close** the address still read `?report=materials`. A reload drew the paper again.

**Read.** `DevelopmentPage.tsx` read the mark and opened the window. The window's `onClose` shut it and never touched the address. No comment said the mark was kept on purpose.

**The mend.** A kernel, `src/lib/materials/siteReportDoor.ts`: `asksForMaterialsReport` reads the mark, and `withoutReport` answers the address without it, or nothing when there is no mark. On close the page shuts the window and puts that address in place with a replace. So Back still goes to the materials screen the person came from, and every other mark in the address stays.

A window opened by the page's own press, with no mark in the address, closes as it did.

**Tests.** `siteReportDoor.test.ts`: the mark is read, comes out, leaves the others, and the address handed in is not changed. `DevelopmentPage.report.render.test.tsx`: the page opened at `?report=materials` draws the window, and after **Close** the address holds no `report`; `?all=1&report=materials` keeps `all=1`; an address with no mark opens no window.

**To see it live.** On a site's materials screen press **Print the material location report**, then **Close**, and read the address: it ends at the site's id. Reload: no paper. The window prints nothing by itself.
