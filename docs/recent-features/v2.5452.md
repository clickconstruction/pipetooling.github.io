count (`follow_up_count` through `followUpNeedsWords` in `src/lib/gc/followUpNeedsIo.ts`), so `gcNeedsYou` and

  `schedule/counts.board.test.ts` lose their `gcNeedsYou` and `phraseFor` lines; their `allPeople` and board pins stay.

  back word for word as at `board-b2b-i.lift.json`'s `pin.spike`, on branch `spike/h2-needsyou-bodies`, which the GC mode
  lead merges into the spike before this lands. The pinned lift pair still compares equal.
