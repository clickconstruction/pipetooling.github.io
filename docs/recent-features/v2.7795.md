# v2.7795 — Painted maps: places kept on the job, on every map of it (2026-11-18)

Punch list #393, reading C, the screens. The column is v2.7792. The owner chose a list of its own on 2026-11-18, *A separate list of pins*, and said of the mock-up, `to-dos/painted-map-pins-on-the-job.html`, *I like what you've mocked up*.

## What changed

- **Under a job's address**, with its painted maps (`JobPaintedMapsStrip`): a press, **＋ Place on this job**, and once the job has a place, a line, **Places on this job**, with how many. The line opens the list. The press opens it already adding a place, or starts the list when the job has none.
- **The list's window** is the painted map window in a places-only shape. It has **＋ Place** in blue, **Share**, and the places with their marks. It has no **Draw a line**, no **Walk or drive a line** and no rename. The first place saves the list.
- **Letters.** A place on the job wears a letter and a map's own place a number, so the two are never taken for each other. A letter is the place's turn in the job's whole list, oldest first, and is the same on every map.
- **On every other painted map of the job**, the job's places are drawn as a second layer with a dashed ring, and listed under **On this job**. They are read there and not changed. **Open Places on this job** closes the map and opens the list. A corner dragged near one catches it, as it catches a map's own place.

## How it is kept

The list is a painted map with `job_list` true (`paintedJobList.ts`). Nothing is a second kind of place: a place on the list has its kind, its photo, its policies and the list's own link, picture and page.

The table holds a job to no count of lists, so a job can have two: from two phones at the same moment, or from two jobs combined. The app writes to the oldest and reads every one as one list, lettered across all of them.

## The calls, as built

1. **The word**: *Places on this job*. Every word of it is in `JOB_LIST_WORDS`, one object, so the other way, *Pins on this job*, is one file.
2. **Told apart** by a letter and a dashed ring.
3. **A link or a copy** of a painted map carries that map's own places and not the job's. The window says so under **On this job**. The Share sheet is untouched, since #394 is rebuilding it.
4. **Who changes one**: as on a painted map. The app offers **Throw this list away** to the office alone, which is narrower than the table.
5. **Materials on site's places**: untouched.
6. **A development's map and a map on no job**: no list.

The owner answered the mock-up with one message and not call by call, so 1 and 3 are built at their picks and are his to change.
