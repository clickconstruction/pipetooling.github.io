# v2.6805 — The ready to bill bar counts the jobs the Ready to Bill section shows (2026-11-09)

Reported by the office on 2026-11-09: the assistants' bar read *2 ready to bill — send them* and the **Get contracts signed** card read *2 ready to bill*, while the Pipeline's Ready to Bill section held one job and the **Ready to ask for** tile counted one job's dollars. Read on prod as the assistant: two `jobs_ledger` rows carried `status = 'ready_to_bill'`, one real job and one ZZ test job a live pass had left in the stage that morning. The board and the strip's money hide ZZ test jobs for every role but a dev who shows them (punch list #61, `zzTestJobVisibility.ts`); two readers of the stage never took the rule.

## What changed

- **`AssistantReadyToBillBanner`** counted the stage with a head count (`select('id', { count: 'exact', head: true })`), which cannot see a name. It now reads `id, job_name, customer_name` for the stage (a handful of rows) and counts through **`readyToBillBannerCount(rows, hideZzTestJobs)`** (`src/lib/readyToBillBanner.ts`), which is `withoutZzTestJobs` when the viewer hides them. The hide flag is `useZzTestJobsHidden(role)`, the same value the jobs cache reads.
- **`summarizeContractNudge`** (`src/lib/jobs/jobContractNudge.ts`) takes **`hideZzTestJobs`** in its options and drops ZZ jobs before it counts, so `missing`, `liveTotal`, `byStage`, `underFloor` and `notNeeded` all leave them out. `useJobContractsNudge(enabled, hideZzTestJobs = false)` selects the two names and passes the flag; its three callers (the Pipeline's card in `JobsStagesTab`, `useNeedsYouInputs`, `PeopleDayBookTab`) pass `useZzTestJobsHidden` of the viewer's role. The card's stage counts open the board filtered to those jobs, and the board already hid them, so the count and the list it opens now agree.
- A row that carries no names is never a test job, so an older caller of the kernel counts as before.

## Tests

- `readyToBillBanner.test.ts`: `readyToBillBannerCount`, and one case per viewer (an assistant, a dev who hides them, a dev who shows them) that holds the bar's count, the card's `byStage.ready_to_bill` (total, missing, dollars) and the section's rows equal over one real job, one ZZ job and one working job.
- `jobContractNudge.test.ts`: every count with and without the flag; a row with no names counts.
- `AssistantReadyToBillBanner.render.test.tsx`: one real job beside two ZZ jobs (one by job name, one by customer name) reads *1 … send it*, and the read asks for the names.

## Not in this change

The Bridge's Ready to Bill lever (`loadBridgeData.ts`, `'bridge rtb'`) reads the stage by id and revenue and has no ZZ rule on any of its reads; it is a separate surface with its own money and is left for its own pass.
