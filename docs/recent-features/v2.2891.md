# v2.2891 — Partnerships: the office Ledger, Timeline and Statements read one journal — and say who owes whom (2026-09-05)

Journey-map Phase 4, Tier-2 #44 (C66 = J26-F2 + J26-F4): the office showed three partner-balance figures — Ledger −$1,008.13 · Timeline +$967.60 · Statements "attaching −$1,975.73" — with nothing on screen saying how they relate, and labor hours differed by 0.01 h on 7 of 18 weeks because the office tabs read `pay_stubs.hours_total` while the partner side sums the stamped rate-tier days. Root cause: three private loaders over the `pay_stubs` family in `PartnershipLedgerTab.tsx`, `PartnershipTimelineTab.tsx` and `PartnershipStatementsTab.tsx`, while the View-as lens and the partner's statement consume `get_partner_ledger_as`.

## One payload, one hook

- `src/hooks/useOfficePartnerLedger.ts` — `useOfficePartnerLedger(partnershipId, personId)`: ONE `get_partner_ledger_as` call (`p_weeks 520`, the same payload `usePartnerLedger` gives the lens and `/my-statement`) shared by all three tabs, plus one metadata select on `person_offsets` (`id, pay_stub_id, person_name`) for what the partner payload deliberately omits — which statement picked a *credit* up, and the payroll name the offset editor writes back. No dollar figure comes from that select. Exposes `status`, `exists` (false when the lens gate hides a paused/ended partnership — the tabs then say so instead of showing a ledger the partner cannot see), `stubs`, `offsets`, `pending`, `split`, `reload`.
- The Ledger tab's six-query loader, the Timeline's five-query loader and the Statements tab's three-query loader are gone; ledger notes stay a local select on `partnership_ledger_notes` because the payload only carries the partner-visible ones and the office edits them here. The Timeline keeps its office-only trails (NCNS, declines, confirmed jobs); statement acknowledgments now come from the payload's `company_ack_at` / `partner_ack_at`.
- `src/pages/Partnerships.tsx` passes `partnershipId` to the Timeline tab.

## The kernel — `src/lib/partnerLedger/partnerBalance.ts`

- `splitPartnerBalance(stubs, offsets, attachments)` / `partnerBalanceFromLedger(payload, attachments?)` → `{ ledgerBalance, postedBalance, attaching, timelineDelta, pendingCount }` with the relationship pinned by test on a fixture reproducing the J26 screen to the cent: **`postedBalance + attaching = ledgerBalance`** (967.60 − 1,975.73 = −1,008.13) and `timelineDelta = attaching`.
  - `ledgerBalance` — the charges-at-date journal (`partnerStubsToJournal`, the partner's Full ledger, row for row) plus credits still pending = the Ledger headline = the partner statement's BALANCE.
  - `postedBalance` — `partnerStubsToPostedJournal(stubs)`: labor, additions, every statement deduction, payouts — only what statements have posted. The Timeline's running column.
  - `attaching` — Σ signed offsets no statement has picked up = the Statements tab's "attaching N of N" figure.
- `pendingPartnerOffsets(stubs, offsets, attachments)` — charges read their attach state from the payload itself (a `pay_stub_deductions.person_offset_id` link means attached — the same rule the charges-at-date journal uses to skip the mirror), so the identity holds even when the attachment select fails; credits reach statements as unlinked additional lines, so they alone need the attachment row and read as attached without it (in neither figure → nothing disagrees). The inherited draft derived pending-ness purely from the side lookup, which let the Ledger count a charge the Statements tab called "nothing pending" whenever the lookup was missing.
- `ledgerHours(stub)` — `tierHoursTotal(stub) ?? round2(hours_total)`: the office reads the stamped rate-tier days, the number the partner's card and Full ledger show (J26-F2). Used by both journals, the Statements archive (now to 0.01 h) and the pay-report drill-in.
- Words: `officeBalanceWords` ("we owe Bryan" / "Bryan owes us" / "even"), `officeBalanceLabel`, `balanceBridgeText` (the one-line bridge, empty when nothing is pending), `balanceConventionTitle` (the tooltip).
- `partnerStubsToJournal` (`partnerWeeks.ts`) now stamps `offset_id` on charge rows so the office drill-in can open the offset editor — the partner's renderers never read it; rows and balances are unchanged (asserted).

## What each tab now says

- **Ledger** — headline `−$1,008.13 · Bryan owes us`, caption `posted we owe Bryan $967.60 · −$1,975.73 not yet on a statement (2) → Bryan owes us $1,008.13` (or "settle-up balance · the same number Bryan's statement shows" when nothing is pending). Tooltip on the number and caption names the convention.
- **Timeline** — new headline: the posted balance with its words and the same bridge caption; the footer names the sign convention. Money rows come from the posted journal, pending charges from the shared payload.
- **Statements** — under "attaching N of N · −$X" a line spelling the bridge; with charges unchecked it says what attaching everything would do instead. Archive hours read `12.86 h`, not `12.9 h`.
- No telemetry. No migration. Dev-gated surface (`get_partner_ledger_as` is `is_dev()`-only, as the tabs already were via payroll RLS).
