# v2.4617 — Help: the lien release and waiver guides match the screens again (2026-10-05)

PR 2 of the lien-guide freshness pass ([v2.4614](v2.4614.md) is PR 1). This PR fixes facts in the three guides about releases and waivers. It changes no structure. Each fix was read against main's code, then given an adversarial second read that compared old and new, sentence by sentence.

## The fixes, by guide

- **give a customer a lien release**
  - The release button sits between share and the orange gavel on Billed Awaiting Payment and Collections rows (`StagesUnifiedJobRow.tsx`, `JobsStagesTab.tsx`).
  - On a phone, press and hold the row; *Release of lien* is in the ⋯ sheet (`StagesPhoneRow.tsx`, `JobsStagesCardList.tsx`).
  - The form comes from the bill the window opened on (`LienReleaseModal.tsx`).
  - *Stay conditional* closes a window opened from *Issue unconditional*.
  - The unconditional final form fills the whole bill (`lienWaiverRelease.ts`).
  - The company block lives in Settings → Jobs & billing → Physical invoice, which only a dev sees (`Settings.tsx`).
  - The audit line says how, by whom and when (`lienReleaseLifecycle.ts`).
  - The property record steps follow the customer's Properties section; *Property legal info* was removed in v2.3009 (`CustomerPropertiesSection.tsx`, `CustomerPropertySheet.tsx`, `CustomerPropertyRecordPanel.tsx`). A county from the city alone reads *guessed from the city — confirm it* (`_shared/txParcelRecord.ts`).
  - The activity steps include *sent* (`jobActivityEvent.ts`), and the window's list is *Already on this job* with *Show all*.
- **send a GC our lien waiver**
  - When step 5 is the one to do, the page marks the signature line.
  - The two *Waive the … paid* buttons ask before going unconditional, as v2.4582 made them (`LienWaiverAmountMath.tsx`, `LienReleaseModal.tsx`).
  - The signer's Settings line is a dev's to fill.
  - The tick is the *Send to … once signed* line (`DashboardLienWaiversToSignModal.tsx`).
- **send a sub the right lien waiver**
  - The path starts at *Jobs → Subs → Pay*, and the button is in the sheet's ⋯ menu (`Jobs.tsx`, `JobsSubsTab.tsx`, `JobsSubLaborTab.tsx`).
  - The form's full name is used.
  - The signed waiver is listed in the sheet's *Lien waiver…* window, on *Already on this sheet*, and its PDF files to People → Contracts (`LienWaiverSendModal.tsx`).

## Held, unchanged

- **For the owner (gap C):** *Cancel request unlocks it* (release guide), and the window's own note quoted in the GC waiver guide, *click Cancel request in step 5 first*. Cancel request sets the release back to issued, which stays locked (`LienReleaseModal.tsx`, `lienReleaseLifecycle.ts`).
- **For the owner (gap D):** *The covered bill lines are selected* (release guide). *Issue unconditional* reselects only the first covered bill (`DashboardLienReleaseQueueModal.tsx`, `LienReleaseModal.tsx`).
- **For the owner (new, gap I):** the GC waiver guide says *Add the unconditional ›* opens the window on that bill and the unconditional. On a bill marked paid it does not. A paid bill is not selectable (`selectableInvoices` keeps billed and ready to bill), so the window opens on *Conditional · progress* without it and asks nothing (`LienReleaseModal.tsx`; the door at `JobFormInvoiceList.tsx`; `lienWaiverCell.ts`).
- **For #4547 (the signer reset):** the release guide's *Signed by* and *draws his signature or types his name* lines. A one-line follow-up comes after #4547 merges.
- **Unsure, kept:** *prefilled from the original*, and the signer opening on the company's signer when the name matches a master.
