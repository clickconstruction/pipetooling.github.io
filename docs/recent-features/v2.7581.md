# v2.7581 — Materials on site: paint the way to a place once, and every box there has it (2026-11-17)

Punch list #372, *Find a lost box*, step 4 of 5, the screens. The owner's word of 2026-11-17: *Build all the steps*. The column and its guard are v2.7580.

## Why

**Where is it?** (v2.7575) draws a straight line to a box's pin. A straight line can cross a fence or a building. A painted map (#345) knows the gate, because a person drew it. Most of a site's boxes share a few places, so one way painted once to a place serves every box that stands in it.

## What ships

- **＋ Paint the way here.** On the phone, under each place in *Places on this site*. On the office page, beside a place's name above its boxes. The painting window opens on a new map on the place's job, named *The way to Level 2*. The painter draws or walks and saves, as on any map.
- **The way comes back onto the place.** When the painting window closes, the map is read back by the id the press made. If a line or a place was saved, the map goes onto the place and the person is told. Nothing saved is nothing changed. This is how a task's **Paint a new one** works.
- **Open the way.** A place with a way offers it in the same two spots. The office also has **Take the way off**, which keeps the map.
- **On a box.** **Where is it?** reads *The way to Level 2 is painted.* with **Open the way**. The phone closes its sheet first, so the map window is not drawn under it.

The kernel is [`placeWay.ts`](../../src/lib/materials/placeWay.ts), the write [`placeWayIo.ts`](../../src/lib/materials/placeWayIo.ts), the press [`placeWayPaint.ts`](../../src/components/materials/placeWayPaint.ts).

## A helper's search offers Paint a map

The card's call 8. A walk of the live app found that a helper who types *paint* was offered the guide and no door to start a map. The door's own gate was open to everyone. The header search dropped every page and window door for a field role, a rule written for #181, before painted maps existed.

`fieldLookupDoors` in [`doors.ts`](../../src/lib/search/doors.ts) now holds that rule in the kernel: a field role's search keeps the guides, and a window marked `forField` that the route guard admits. **Paint a map** is the one window so marked. `doors.test.ts` holds it, where before the test passed while the screen disagreed.

## What differs from the card

- **The guard's refusals carry their own code**, `P0PWY`, set in v2.7580 before it was pushed. The app renders `42501` as one fixed sentence about access, which would have hidden the reason.
- **Take the way off** is the office's alone. The card named no such press. A way that is wrong needs a way out that does not throw the map away.
