# v2.5992 — Extend a sent estimate's good-through date (2026-10-27)

Punch list **#206** PR 1 (`to-dos/estimate-extend-pdf-options.md`), the office's first ask from the
City of Seguin case: *"Need to be able to go back and change the expiration date easier. For now
I'll have to create a new estimate."* One migration (**push after merge**), one redeploy
(`send-estimate-to-customer`, for the shared words), the client.

## What was wrong

`valid_until` was set on the draft and frozen at send; past it the accept page answered *Estimate
expired*, Resend was refused (`pricing_expired`, *Start a new estimate instead*) and would have
re-minted the link anyway — wrong for a city whose five people each held a copy of the one emailed
link. The sent view did not even show the date. The office remade the estimate by hand.

## What shipped

- **Migration `20261027220000_extend_estimate_good_through.sql`** (`docs/migrations/`): the
  `estimate_customer_events` CHECKs and `log_estimate_customer_event` learn `good_through_extended` /
  `extend_estimate_good_through`; **`extend_estimate_good_through(p_estimate_id, p_valid_until)`**,
  gated as `record_estimate_decline`, only a `sent` row not in a bid room, the day today or later in
  `America/Chicago`: sets `valid_until`, pushes `public_token_expires_at` and every
  `estimate_share_links.expires_at` to at least the end of the new day (never earlier; the hash is
  untouched, the owner's call 1), and writes the event with `{from, to, user_id, user_name}` in one
  transaction.
- **`src/lib/estimates/estimateGoodThroughExtend.ts`**: `canExtendEstimateGoodThrough` (sent with a
  send on record, not in a bid room; a past date and an expired link are never blockers),
  `goodThroughState` (*live* / *passed* / *none* with its words), the three presets (+2 weeks, +30
  days, +60 days) over `addCalendarDaysYmd`, `extendDateProblem` (not a date · before today · the
  same day), the lead words, the button label (*Extend good-through to Nov 26, 2026*), the toast,

- **Docs**: the guide *see the email a customer gets with an estimate* gains *Extend the good-through
