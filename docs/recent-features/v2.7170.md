# v2.7170 — Bid Board: a bid sent on a Sunday counts in its own week (2026-11-13)

Punch list #318 left this as a look: whether the Pulse's weeks on the Bid Board read a bid's send day a day early. The close-out check of 2026-11-13 took the look and found the defect. The fix went in on a yes the same day.

## What was wrong

`bids.bid_date_sent` is a `date` column, so the app receives it as `YYYY-MM-DD` with no time. Two kernels passed it to `calendarYmdInAppTzFromIso`, the helper for a moment in time:

- `sentBidsWithWeek` in `src/lib/bids/estimatingPulse.ts`, behind the Pulse's stat strip, its weekly chart and its cards for each person.
- `buildBidBoardWeeklySentSummaries` in `src/lib/bidBoardWeeklySentStats.ts`, behind the weekly sent counts.

JavaScript reads a plain date as midnight UTC. That is the evening before in Chicago, so every bid landed a day early. A company week starts on Sunday, so Monday to Saturday stayed in their own week and only a Sunday send moved: it counted in the week before.

Their tests did not see it. The fixtures gave `bid_date_sent` a time of day, which the column never holds.

## The fix

Both kernels read the send day as written, with `ymdFromDateLike` (`src/utils/dateUtils.ts`). The chart of wins by bid size (v2.6975) and `bidPursuit.ts` already read it that way.

## What changes on the board

Nothing today. A read of production on 2026-11-13 found no bid sent since May 2026 with a Sunday send day. A bid sent on a Sunday from here on counts in the week it was sent.

## Tests

- `estimatingPulse.test.ts` and `bidBoardWeeklySentStats.test.ts`: a bid sent on a Sunday is in the week that Sunday starts, and a Saturday send is in the week before. Both new tests fail on the old read.
- The fixtures in both files now feed a plain date, as the column holds it.

Seen in tests only, not on a screen.

## Not changed

`src/lib/needsYou/fillInLostBids.ts` reads the send day as a moment to count a bid's age in days. It is at most six hours off and sorts into no week, so it was left alone.
