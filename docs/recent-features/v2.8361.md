# v2.8361 — Retry after a lost signal is a thumb-sized press on a phone (2026-11-23)

Punch list #414, the third of its four small parts, built second. The owner's word of 2026-11-23 to build the card's four app-wide things. No migration and no edge function.

## What was there

`OfflineRetryPanel` (J2-F3, Tier-1 #6b) draws the sentence a failed read or write leaves and, when the failure was the network, a **Retry** press. Every crew screen uses it for a read that fails. The press was `minHeight: 36` at every width: 67 by 36 px at 390 px by the audit of punch list #382. A thumb needs 44.

## What changed

- On a phone (`useIsMobile`, the app's 640 px line) the press is 44 px tall with `0.5rem 1.1rem` of room inside. On a desktop it keeps 36 px and its old padding.
- Nothing else about the panel moves: the sentence, *What this means*, the auto-retry on an `online` event for an idempotent read, the telemetry.

## Tests

- `OfflineRetryPanel.render.test.tsx` gains a phone switch (a mocked `useIsMobile`) and one test that measures the press at phone width (`minHeight` 44px, the wider padding) and at desk width (36px).
- `useIsMobile` reads `window.matchMedia` only when the browser has one, as `useNarrowViewport640` does (the same guard as v2.8359's): a render test that drew the panel with no `matchMedia` stub crashed in CI's first run.
- Not looked at on a phone: the proof is the render test.
