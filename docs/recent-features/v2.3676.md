# v2.3676 — Quick time add, PR 4: the chip on My Time's own day (2026-09-21)

PR 4 of [`to-dos/quick-time-add`](../../to-dos/quick-time-add/README.md), the first of the three follow-ups the owner queued on the punch list on 2026-09-21. Client only; no migration, no edge function.

## Why

PR 3 (v2.3663) put the *quick add* chip and the person's sentence on the two places hours are approved. The person who made the quick add looks somewhere else first — their own day on My Time — and there a quick add was indistinguishable from a ten-minute punch: same block, same "What were you working on?" box. The design's rule is that a quick add is *never hidden inside a punch*.

## The change

- [`myTimeDayTimeline.ts`](../../src/lib/myTimeDayTimeline.ts): `DayEditorSession` gains `quick_add_minutes` (optional, `null` when the select left it out); `normalizeDayEditorSession` carries it. One test.
- [`DashboardMyTimeDayEditorModal.tsx`](../../src/components/DashboardMyTimeDayEditorModal.tsx): both session selects add the column.
- [`MyTimeDayClusterForm.tsx`](../../src/components/my-time-day-editor/MyTimeDayClusterForm.tsx) and [`MyTimeDayClusterVisual.tsx`](../../src/components/my-time-day-editor/MyTimeDayClusterVisual.tsx): the segment whose session is a quick add (resolved through `clockSessionRowForSegmentAssign`, the same mapping the adjust and reject controls use) shows [`QuickAddChip`](../../src/components/clock/QuickAddChip.tsx) beside its time range, and its words box reads *What it was — the office reads this when it approves your hours*.
- Guide *add a quick call or email to my hours* → *Who sees it* says My Time shows the chip too.

## Not changed

The time inputs stay as they are: the database guard (v2.3655) already refuses a stretched quick add and says so in its own sentence, and the owner may slide or re-word one.

## Verified

`myTimeDayTimeline`, `helpGuideContent` and `releaseNotes` suites; typecheck and lint on the touched files.
