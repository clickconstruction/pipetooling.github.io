# v2.8755 — Bids: the three price-book forms' state is `usePriceBookForms` (punch list #46, row 1) (2026-11-27)

The third of the medium cuts row 1 of [`to-dos/decomposition-queue.md`](../../to-dos/decomposition-queue.md) names on `BidsPricingTab.tsx`, after `derivePricingWorkbench` (v2.8744) and the split's child rows (v2.8754). A pure move: no number, word or press on the Pricing tab changes. Client only. It owes nothing on prod.

**It moves state and handlers, so its PR opens unarmed.** It is armed once the walks of v2.8744 and v2.8754 have read clean on the live site.

## Why

The price book has three forms: the version form (a book added or renamed), the delete window, and the entry form (an entry added or edited, from the drawer or from a row's assign search). The forms themselves have been components since v2.3564. What they remember stayed in the tab: twenty state values, two effects and eight doors, spread over a thousand lines. No test opened any of the three.

## How it was done, in order

1. **Tests first, on the tab as it stood.** Eight cases joined the render smoke and passed before the move (the first commit):
   - **Add book** opens the version form empty, **Cancel** forgets what was typed, **Save** writes a new book
   - the pencil on a book opens the form on its name, and **Save** renames it
   - **Delete version** asks for the name: a wrong one is refused in words, **Cancel** forgets it, the right one deletes
   - **Add entry** in Stage price adds the three stages up as they are typed, and **Save** writes them to the book being browsed
   - in Combined price a new entry lands in Rough In, and an edited one moves Rough In by the difference
   - the assign search's **Add** opens the entry form on the term, closes the search, and saves to the bid's own price
   - a change of service type closes all three forms
   - every door that opens a form clears the tab's error line
2. **The page's HTML and its writes, in every state.** A scratch copy of the smoke wrote the page and what it had written to the database at the end of each of its 47 cases, twice, to see it was steady. 3.2 MB.
3. **The move, with no change.** [`src/hooks/usePriceBookForms.ts`](../../src/hooks/usePriceBookForms.ts) holds the twenty state values, the two effects in their order, and the eight doors, each as it stood. The tab calls the hook **where the two effects stood**, so they run in the same place among the tab's other effects.
4. **The same tests, the same HTML and the same writes**, byte for byte in all 47 states.

**What stays in the tab:** the four writes, `savePricingVersion`, `confirmDeletePricingVersion`, `savePricingEntry` and `deletePricingEntry`. They reach the tab's freeze chain, its reloads and its book-edit offer, and they read the hook's state.

Nothing else in the tab changed. The tab is 3,973 lines, from 4,069.

## Seen while moving, not changed

- `pricingFormMode` is only ever set to *template*, by **Add book**. Nothing in the tab sets *pricing-blank* or *pricing-clone* any more, so `savePricingVersion`'s two branches for a new pricing, blank and cloned, cannot be reached from the screen. `pricingCloneSourceId` is only ever set to null. Both are kept as they were.
- The effect that adds the stages up leaves `pricingEntryTotal` out of what it watches, as it always did, and the linter's warning moved with it.
- The entry form draws only while the tab has a price book to browse. With a book, the assign search's **Add** opens it whether the drawer is open or shut. With no price book at all it closes the search and opens no window. That is the tab's own rule, in its markup, and is unchanged. Both were read on the mounted tab.

## Proved, and what is not

- **Mutants.** 25 changes to the hook and 3 to the tab's one call were run against the smoke, after a control in which it passes. 26 fail it. Four survived at first. Two were gaps, an edited entry's Rough In and the error line, so the smoke was tightened and the tighter cases were run on the tab as it stood too. Two change nothing a person can see: the delete window is drawn only while it has a book, and its box is emptied by every way out of it.
- Lint and a focused `tsc` on the tab, the hook and the smoke. Every bids test and every test that reads the source tree pass.
- **Nothing was written on prod and no live site was opened.** Every name in the tests is made up.
- Not seen: the forms on the live site. That is the walk below.

## The walk, on a ZZ test bid, reads only

For `walking`, after this is live. **Press no Save, no Delete and no Use on this bid.** Each form is opened, read and left by **Cancel**, which writes nothing.

1. Bids → the ZZ test bid → **Pricing**. Press the book's name on the line under the strip. The **Price book** drawer opens. The console shows no error.
2. Press **›** beside the book's name to show every book, then **Add book**. A window opens, *New template*, with an empty **Name**. Press **Cancel**.
3. Press the pencil beside a book. The window opens as *Edit template name* with that book's name in the box and **Delete version** at the left. Do not press Save.
4. Press **Delete version**. A second window opens, *Delete price option*, with an empty box and a dead **Delete**. Press **Cancel** on it, then **Cancel** on the first window.
5. Press **Add entry**. A window opens, *New entry*, with empty boxes. In Stage price it has Rough In, Top Out, Trim Set and a Total. Press **Cancel**.
6. Press the pencil on an entry's line. The window opens as *Edit entry* with the entry's name and its price, or its three stage prices. Press **Cancel**.
7. Close the drawer. On a grid row press **assign…**, type a word that is in no book, and press **+ Add "…" to the book**. The search closes and the *New entry* window opens with the word in its name box. Press **Cancel**.

Expect each window as it read before this version.
