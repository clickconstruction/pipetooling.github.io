# v2.6918 — Lien desk: send every drafted affidavit for approval, print every ready one (2026-11-10)

Punch list #305. The owner, 2026-11-10: "is it easy for an assistant to re-issue the liens she has already printed this month with the new lien view we've just made". The record showed the office had printed all 13 approved affidavits on the earlier form on 2026-11-09 and 2026-11-10; none was filed. **Redraw N on the short form…** (v2.6867) moves the ready ones in one press, but each then needed **Send for approval ▸** on its own pane and **Print for notarization** in its own Lien window: three or four presses a paper. He asked for the press.

## What changed

Two bars at the top of the Affidavits list, under the redraw bar, for the office roles:

- **Send N for approval…** acts on every paper in **To draft** that has a draft a person made (`status: 'drafted'`, what a redraw leaves). It asks once. The window says where the papers go: *10 are approved at once by a GC’s standing rule and move to Ready to file. 2 go to the leader for approval.* A paper in To draft that nobody has drafted is left, with *Draft it from its own paper*. A paper that fails stays listed under the press with its reason and a door to its row.
- **Print N for notarization** acts on every paper in **Ready to file** on the short form. One print window, each paper starting its own sheet. A ready paper on the earlier form is left out and the line under the press says so.

## How

- **`src/lib/jobs/lienAffidavitBatch.ts`** (pure): the two plans, the words, and `lienAffidavitPrintRun`, which returns the print document and, for each job, the copy to file.
- **The send makes the pane's call.** `lienAffidavitSubmitOutcome` wraps `submitOutcome` with what the desk holds (the GC's rule, a live promise, a notice on record, a hold before); `LienDeskAffidavitPane`'s **Send for approval ▸** now calls it too, so the two cannot drift. `submitLienAffidavits` in `lienDeskIo.ts` is one update per paper and keeps going past a failure.
- **The print is the desk's own paper.** `lienDeskAffidavitFieldsFor` (lifted out of `affidavitStopPages` in `lienStopPaperPages.ts`) fills each job's fields from the desk's data, the recipe the pane, the preview and the Lien window share. `filingDocsPrintHtml` in `lienFilingDocuments.ts` puts several papers in one print sheet, each in a block that starts a page and carries its own leading; `filingDocPrintHtml` is the same body and shell with one paper, its output unchanged.
- **A print counts as a send** (`docs/SENT_COPIES.md`): each job files its own paper alone, kind `lien_affidavit`, title *Lien Affidavit — Job N*, as its Lien window's print does. Nothing is filed when the print window is blocked.

## Decisions

- **Two presses, not one.** Sending can leave a paper with the leader, and the printed set must be the approved set. Each press says its own count at the moment it is pressed.
- **The send leaves a paper nobody drafted.** The batch does not write a first draft for a paper no person has read.
- **The print leaves the earlier form.** The point of the press is the re-issue on the short form; an earlier-form paper prints from its own window or is redrawn first.
- **No confirm on the print.** The Lien window's print has none, and the button states its count.

## Proof

- Chromium's print engine (Playwright `page.pdf`, `pdfinfo`, `pdftotext`) on a batch of 12 sample papers, one contracted with the owner and one on a homestead with a long legal description: 12 sheets, each paper alone on its sheet with its closing and its statute line.
- In the local preview on the real desk, read only: with the short form forced by a local edit that was reverted and never committed, the bar reads *12 affidavits on the short form are ready to file* over **Print 12 for notarization**. Not pressed: a print files a copy on each job.
- Unit tests for the plans, the words and the print run; render tests for both bars, a send that fails, and a blocked print window.

Not observed: the send on real papers (it writes) and a real printer.
