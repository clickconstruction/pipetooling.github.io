# v2.8333 — Bills sent: who has paid, the day and the way, at a glance (2026-11-23)

Punch list #422, the first of three PRs. The owner's ask of 2026-11-23: on Bills sent, show more clearly who has paid and when, so the leader sees it in a way that fits what he already reads there. The card, with the mock-up he approved, is `to-dos/bills-sent-who-paid/`. No migration, no edge function.

## What was there

The page knew when a bill was paid and did not show it well.

- The paid word was the last of five small things in a line's grey row, in the same green and weight as *Delivered*.
- The day came from `bill_send_marks`, which answers by sent copy. A line with no copy had no day and read *Paid since*: a text with the pay link, a Housecall Pro bill, an email from before the copies began.
- That function answers only for a bill whose status is `paid`, so a part payment said nothing.
- The page read how each bill was paid (`paid_how`) and never drew it. For a Stripe payment the word was empty anyway: the webhook writes the row with no `payment_type` and the note *Stripe*.
- Nothing added it up. No tile, no chip, no word on a day's band.

## What changed

- **A stamp under each amount.** *Paid* with the day, *Part paid* with the amount paid so far, or *Not paid*. Under it the way, in the job window's own words (*Card · Stripe*, *Check 4417 · bank deposit*, *Cash · typed by hand*), then how long the money took from the bill's first send, or how long the bill has been out. A bill paid today through Stripe says the time.
- **Every line has its day.** The page reads each bill's own payment rows, so a line with no copy is dated like any other, and a part payment shows.
- **A day's band says what came back**: *1 of 3 paid · $6,820.00 still out*, counted over the same bills as the band's total.
- **A Not paid tile** adds up the bills on the list that are not paid in full, a part-paid bill for what is left of it, and is the press that narrows the list to them. Two chips, **Not paid** and **Paid**, sit after **All**.
- **Whether it arrived moved to the right end of the line**, and is no longer green. The owner asked for the right edge on 2026-11-23. A bounce stays red. Green on the page now means money.
- **Cents are smaller than the dollars** in every amount on the page, the owner's second ask of the same day, through the app's own `AmountSmallCents`.
- **On a phone** the stamp is a bar across the foot of the card.
- **One bill's record** lists each payment on the bill above its sends, and its print does too.
- **Print** says the paid words on each line and the band's second line. **Download CSV** gains four columns: *Paid on*, *Paid how*, *Still out* and *Days to pay*, and its *Paid* column now says *Paid*, *Part paid* or *Not paid*.

## How it is built

- `src/lib/billing/billsPaid.ts`, new and pure: `billPaidState` turns a bill and its payment rows into one state (paid, part paid or open, the newest payment's day and way, what is still out, each payment oldest first). `billPaymentWayWords` words a payment through `paymentSourceFromFacts`, so the page and the job window never name one payment two ways; a test holds the two equal row for row.
- `src/lib/jobs/billsAndPayments.ts`: `paymentSource` now reads its facts off the job's row and hands them to `paymentSourceFromFacts`, the same sort for a screen that holds a payment and its bill and no whole job. `isStripePayment` moved here from `moneyInWeek.ts`, beside `instrumentWord`, so the Money in bar and Bills sent tell a Stripe payment by one test.
- `src/lib/billing/billsSentIo.ts`: `readPaid` reads `jobs_ledger_payments` by `invoice_id` for the bills on the page, which the page's four roles read under the table's own policy. It is all or nothing: a read that fails, or a page that comes back full, answers no states, so no bill is called *Not paid* on half an answer. The bill read gains `stripe_invoice_id`, `stripe_invoice_status` and `external_send_channel`, which say whether Stripe holds a record of a payment.
- `src/lib/billing/billsSent.ts`: a line carries `pay` and `firstYmd`. `billsSentPayWords` writes the stamp, `billsSentPayPlain` the same as one run of words for a print. `BillsSentDay.back` and `billsSentBackWords` are the band's second line, `billsSentTiles` gains `unpaid`, and `billsSentFilter` knows the two new chips. The file imports `billsPaid.ts` by type alone, so the job kernels stay out of the app's first bundle, where this file rides.
- When the payments do not read, a line falls back to what the page knew before: the bill's status and the day on its sent copy's mark.
- `src/components/billing/BillsSentLedger.tsx`: the money column (`PayStamp`, `PayHow`), the phone's bar, the band's line, the tile, the record's payments. An even count of tiles sits two to a row on a phone.
