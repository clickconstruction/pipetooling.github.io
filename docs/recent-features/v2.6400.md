# v2.6400 — The Warranty step reads the paper, and the thread sends its link (PR 3 of 3) (2026-11-02)

The last of the three warranty PRs (v2.6397 the data and the kernels, v2.6398 the window, the page and the functions). The residential flow reads the warranty back, the thread sends its link, and the guide and the docs name the paper once.

## What changed

- **The flow reads it.** `flowPersonIo.ts`'s residential read selects `job_contracts` beside the other Pipeline tables (`PIPELINE_SELECTS.agreements`), **softly**: a role that cannot read the table (a tech on Job Mode) reads no rounds rather than a failed card. The morning run (`messages-nudge-dispatch`) reads the same rows for everyone at once. `warrantyRounds` (v2.6397) turns them into the Warranty step's rounds, so the step card says *Any time in the job. 1 so far, 1 open.* with the round waiting on them and **Remind them**, and the run's reminder for the step (off until the owner turns it on) has a round to count from.
- **`/warranty` and `{warranty}`** (`messages/papers.ts`, `papersIo.ts`, `quickReplies/slash.ts`, `blanks.ts`, `ReplyBox.tsx`): a `WarrantyPaper` is a `job_contracts` row of kind `warranty`, out to sign with a live link or signed, read with the customer's other papers (the link off the row through `jobContractLinkOnRow`, as the window's doors read it); `/warranty` lists them after the lines (*Warranty · J1312 · Water heater · sent Nov 2 · waiting for initials and a signature*), a pick puts in the team's line that holds `{warranty}` or the app's own (*here is our warranty for {job}. It takes a minute to initial each line and sign on your phone: {warranty}*; a signed one goes in as a copy), and `{warranty}` in any line fills at once from the job's own warranty (`defaultWarrantyPaper`, `defaultEstimatePaper`'s rule), with no minting. The run leaves `{warranty}` for the box (*blanks left*), as it leaves `{estimate}`.
- **The guide** `src/content/help/send-a-homeowner-the-warranty.md`: the Paper pick, the four lines to initial, the three ways, `/warranty`, what the homeowner does, what it leaves behind, the Warranty step.
- **Docs**: `GLOSSARY.md` gains *Warranty in place of a contract* (the paper's one home) and the flow entry names the step; `MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md`'s flows section names the aside step and its read; `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`'s Contracts sentence names the Paper pick.
