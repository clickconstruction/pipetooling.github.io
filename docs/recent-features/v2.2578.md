# v2.2578 — Stripe branding icon: yellow-field mark, asset in repo (2026-09-01)

The Stripe hosted-invoice page renders the account **icon** inside a forced
circle on the brand-color header. The old upload (the hand mark, tight-cropped
on white) got the wrench head and "click" sparks clipped by the circle and
floated as a white disc on the `#ffd700` header — reported by the owner from a
live invoice screenshot on 2026-09-01.

Fix applied in the Stripe Dashboard (Settings → Business → Branding), owner
authorized, live mode; sandbox/test mode inherits live branding so no separate
upload:

- **New Icon**: 512×512 PNG, the hand mark from `public/brand/click-plum.png`
  scaled to 58% with even margins, on solid `#ffd700` (the account's exact
  Brand/Accent color). On the yellow header the disc dissolves and only the
  orange hand + black wrench show ("Option C" of the reviewed proposals; the
  runner-up was a white silhouette on Click orange, which reads better on white
  surfaces if this ever needs revisiting).
- **`Prefer logo over icon` re-checked** after the swap — Stripe silently clears
  it when the icon is removed; it was ON before, keeping the full CLICK lockup
  on Checkout. The **Logo** slot (full lockup) was untouched.
- **Asset committed** as `public/brand/stripe-icon-yellow.png` so the exact
  uploaded file is versioned and re-uploadable. It is served with the client
  like the other `public/brand/` files but nothing in the app references it —
  Stripe hosts its own copy.

Known tradeoff: on white surfaces where Stripe shows the icon (some email
clients), it appears as a yellow rounded chip rather than dissolving.

No app code, schema, or edge-function changes.
