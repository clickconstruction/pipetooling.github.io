# v2.6113 — Nothing scheduled today stands on its own line (2026-10-29)

On the phone Dashboard the My Schedule card's empty day read *Nothing scheduled today. + Add job puts a job on your* with *day.* alone on the second line, and Tomorrow the same. The owner's rule (2026-10-29): when the sentence wraps at all, *Nothing scheduled today.* stands alone on the first line and *+ Add job puts a job on your day.* on the second; when everything fits on one line, nothing changes.

- **The shape, not the words**: `EmptyState` (`src/components/EmptyState.tsx`) draws the sentence as two units, each `display: inline-block`: `main` is one, the press with its `tail` and the *or* press the other. Inline blocks move to the next line whole, so the second unit drops as one piece when the two do not fit together; a unit longer than the line wraps inside itself, so nothing overflows. The words from `emptyWords.ts` and the surfaces' own kernels are untouched, and `textContent` reads as before.
- **App-wide, on purpose**: every empty state is drawn by this element, so Bids, Jobs and the rest break the same way at phone width.
- **The pin**: `EmptyState.render.test.tsx` asserts the two units and their `inline-block`, the *or* press inside the second, and a tail with no press as a unit of its own; `DashboardMyScheduleSection.render.test.tsx` asserts the empty day's two units.

**Checked** on the dev server at 375 wide: the My Schedule card's Today and Tomorrow each read *Nothing scheduled …* on one line and *+ Add job puts a job on your day.* on the next.

No migration, no edge function.
