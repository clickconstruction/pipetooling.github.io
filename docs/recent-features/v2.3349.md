# v2.3349 — Who pays the bill, PR 3: split by line — a Pays tag on every work line and one draft per payer (2026-09-11)

Migration `20260911224805_who_pays_split_by_line.sql` ([`docs/migrations/20260911224805_who_pays_split_by_line.md`](../migrations/20260911224805_who_pays_split_by_line.md)); no edge function. Third of the four-PR train (PR 1 v2.3345 the rule, PR 2 v2.3346 the readers).

## Why

The office billed two payers on one site by opening two jobs at the same address (14 pairs in 120 days): hours, costs, Burn and the Job Summary each saw half a job. PR 1's `split` rule let each draft pick its payer by hand; this PR makes the line items say it and carves the drafts.

## What shipped

- **Schema**: `jobs_ledger_fixtures.bill_to_party` (NULL · `customer` · `gc`, CHECK). Discount rows never carry one.
- **Kernel** [`splitByPayer.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/splitByPayer.ts) (+5 tests): `linePayer` (tag, default customer, never a discount row), `planPayerCarves(fixtures, coverage)` — one carve per party with unbilled dollar-bearing rows, GC first, each sized net of dollar coverage through `segmentSelectionNetSummary` (the same math a hand-picked selection bills), `payerTagCounts`.
- **Form state**: `FixtureRow.bill_to_party`; hydrated by `fixtureRowsFromDb`, carried through the billing autosave slice (`bp`) and the delete+reinsert payload, and in the `jobs_ledger_fixtures` embed list.
- **① Line Items** ([`JobFormFixturesSection`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormFixturesSection.tsx)): when the job's *Bills go to* is `split`, every named work row shows a **Pays** toggle under its name — the customer's name | the GC's name — blue for the customer, amber for the GC, locked with the row once it is on a sent bill.
- **Make bills by payer** ([`JobFormSegmentsCreateAction`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormSegmentsBar.tsx)): a button above the segment action on split jobs — *Make 2 bills by payer — Wildflower Springs $8,400 · Tommy Gillis $3,150*. `carveInvoicesByPayer` in the shell runs each carve through `createInvoiceFromSegmentIds` (which now returns the new invoice id), stamps the draft's `bill_to_party`, and refetches; the primary remainder re-syncs after each carve exactly as a manual break-off does, so a fully carved job ends with no primary draft. A stamping failure leaves the draft in place and says to pick the payer with *Bill to ▾*.
- Help: `choose-who-gets-the-bill` gains **Split a job by line**; `GLOSSARY.md` "Bills go to" mentions the line tag.

## Not changed

Line-level tags do nothing on a `customer` or `gc` job (the toggle is hidden; the drafts inherit the job rule). Moving a tagged row between payers after its draft exists does not move it — send the draft back or delete it first, like any other segment link.
