# v2.8113 — Job Mode: This week counts the day you just worked (2026-11-20)

A follow-up to v2.8047, the day's row in Job Mode. That change closed with a note that the hours square had not been seen with a number, since the account it was walked as has no clocked hours. This is that look, and the one fault it found. A client fix in one component. No migration and no function.

## What was wrong

The **This week** square takes its number from the hours card lower on the page (`DashboardMyHoursBlock`, through `onWeek`), so the week is read once. The card read the week when the page was opened and never again. The row itself reads the day again whenever the clock changes.

So a person who opened the app in the morning, clocked in and clocked out on the same page saw the row turn to **This week** and **Tomorrow** with the morning's number: the whole day they had just worked was missing. The card's own line, *This week N of M h so far*, was short by the same hours, so the two agreed with each other and both were wrong. Opening the page again put it right.

## The fix

The hours card now reads its week again when the clock changes, on the signal the clock button already sends and the row already listens to (`useSessionChanges`). One read of one person's sessions on a punch. The card is the same component in My Time on the full Dashboard, so that line is right after a punch too.

## How it was found and proven

Walked on the dev server against the live data, as people who had hours on the clock that week, through the app's own View as. Nothing was written as them: every write to a table was refused in the browser, and an RPC not known to be a read was answered there and never sent. The day's states the people were not in at that hour were drawn by answering the page's own reads differently, with no write.

- **The fault.** One page was opened on a day read as *before the clock*, then the clock's reads were let through as *clocked in*, then as *clocked out*, with the page's own clock signal sent each time. The square and the card both kept the first number. A fresh open of the same page read a day's hours more. With the fix the same three steps end on the number a fresh open reads.
- **What the square draws, 390 and 320 px wide, light and dark.** A person part way through the week, a person over their hours, a person whose answer is fewer hours than they worked, a salaried person on the default week and on a set week of 30 hours, a person with an answer and nothing on the clock (*0 h*), a person with no answer and no hours (*My hours*, the clock icon), and a week of 120.1 hours made up to try the width. In every case the square's number was the number in the card's line. The longest number kept more than 4 px each side inside the square at 320 px. The press brought the hours card into view above the pinned bar.

## Tests

`DashboardMyHoursBlock.render.test.tsx` had no test of `onWeek`, so nothing held the square's number to the card's line: the row's own tests hand it a number. Three tests now do. The card hands on the week its line prints. It hands on nothing with no row to read, and zero for an answer with nothing on the clock. It reads again when the clock changes, which fails without the fix.

## Not in this change

- While a person is clocked in, the number is the hours up to when the page was last read, so it ages until the next punch or the next open. The line says *so far*.
- For a moment after the page opens, before the app knows a person is salaried, the card can ask a salaried person *How many hours a week can you work?* It corrects itself when that read lands. Seen once on a slow load.
- The Short view of Job Mode still draws no row. Whether it should is the owner's call.
