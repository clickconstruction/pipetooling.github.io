# v2.2686 — Review math fixes 1: sub-sheet costing + office-rate overhead wages (2026-09-03)

First batch of the Review-only fixes from the 2026-09-03 math audit (findings 4 and 10). Both change the inputs to the allocation math so Review agrees with the surfaces it shares definitions with; the allocation formulas themselves are untouched.

## Client only — no migration

- **Sub-labor sheets cost like Jobs (finding 4).** Review priced a `people_labor_jobs` sheet as `Σ(count × hrs_per_unit) × sheet labor_rate + drive`, ignoring a line's `labor_rate` override and `direct_labor_amount`; Jobs → Job Summary honors both via [`lib/jobs/subLaborCost.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/subLaborCost.ts). Every Review cost site now calls `laborJobSubCost` — the derive kernel's `laborJobs`, the union loader's lifetime `laborCostByHcp`, and the per-person loader's lifetime `laborCostByHcp`, `personSubLaborCostByHcp`, and period `laborJobs`. The three `people_labor_job_items` selects add `labor_rate, direct_labor_amount`; `TeamLaborItem` carries them as optional fields. Hours are still hours (`is_fixed ? hrs : count × hrs`), so hour totals, the Hours drilldown, and per-hour denominators do not move; a sheet with a direct $ line now raises that job's lifetime labor and lowers everyone else's share of it, exactly as on Jobs.
- **Office/bid hours at the office rate (finding 10).** `derivePersonTeamSummary` prices `overheadHours` at `office_hourly_wage` when `shouldUseDualRate(cfg)` (hourly + office rate on file — payroll's gate, and the one `overheadWageRatesFromConfig` uses for the Overhead pool), else at `hourly_wage`. New row field `overheadWage` (`TeamSummaryRow` + `TeamSummaryBreakdown`); the Overhead-labor drilldown title/body, the popup document, and the ranked view's math drawer read it and say "office rate" when it differs from the field wage. Single-rate and salaried people are numerically unchanged.
- Tests: +2 in `derivePersonTeamSummary.test.ts` (override + direct line reconcile to the job's value created; dual-rate vs salaried pricing); fixtures gain `overheadWage`.
- Verified live as dev: the tab renders and today's roster is unchanged where no sheet uses overrides and nobody carries an office rate.
