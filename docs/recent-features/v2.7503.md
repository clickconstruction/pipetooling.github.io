# v2.7503 — Lien desk: sending a printed notice back takes its signature off, and says so (2026-11-16)

Found while building *Sign it again* (v2.7502). The owner's call on it, 2026-11-16: *"Sometimes we want to use a different signature like a typed signature instead and that is why we send back. I like the idea of wiping the signature when it is sent back."*

## What was wrong

`takeBackLienDeskItems` wrote who took a notice back into the notice's `fields` (`runTakenBack`) and left the signature columns alone. `lienFieldsHash` covers the whole of `fields`, so the stored `signed_fields_hash` stopped matching and `lienSignatureStale` turned true: the chip read *unsigned*, the paper printed no signature, the Board counted the notice as waiting on the leader and the run held it. The row still carried the old signature, and nothing on the screen had said it would go: the run's confirm read *The approvals stand*, and the desk's *Back to Approved · to print* asked nothing. One notice in production went through it (marked printed by accident, taken back four minutes later, then signed a second time on the office's screen). Read only, nothing else was stranded.

## What changed

- **The write** (`takeBackLienDeskItems`, `src/lib/jobs/lienDeskIo.ts`): the take-back clears the ten signature columns (`LIEN_DESK_SIGNATURE_CLEAR`) in the same update as the print stamp. A signed row keeps a second line, `fields.signatureCleared` (v2.7502's record), naming the signature that came off, at the same instant as `runTakenBack`. The approval is not in the patch. A drawing in storage is kept. The row is now unsigned by its columns, not by a hash that stopped matching.
- **The run's confirm** (`runTakeBackConfirm`, `src/lib/jobs/lienRunTakeBack.ts`): takes `signed`, the count of taken-back notices that carry a signature (`runSignedCount`), and adds one line: *Its signature comes off. The leader signs it again before it prints.* / *Their signatures come off…* / *3 of them are signed. Those signatures come off…*. The strip after (`runTakenBackWords`) ends *The leader signs them again. Then print the packet.* `runNoticesTakenBack` shows the notices unsigned in the window at once, so their envelopes are held until he signs.
- **The desk's link** (`backToReady` in `LienDesk.tsx`): on a notice whose signature stands, *Back to Approved · to print* asks first (`takeBackNoticeConfirm`: the job in the title, *Take back the notice* as the button). An unsigned notice goes back without a question, as before. The toast says the notice waits to be signed again.

## Not changed

- **The hash rule.** `lienFieldsHash` and `lienSignatureStale` are as they were, so nothing in the edge mirror moved and no function is redeployed. The other choice put to the owner was to let a signature survive a take-back by leaving the desk's own records out of the hash; he chose the wipe.
- **No migration.** The guard rules on placing and confirming a signature; clearing one passes it.
- **PR #8383's one-row Undo** goes through the same write, so it clears the signature too. Its own confirm (`runTakeBackOneConfirm`) is that PR's to word.

## Still open

No sent notice carried a signature when this was read, so nothing reads wrong yet; the first signed run is in the mail now. It is punch list #370 (`to-dos/lien-mailed-notice-records-and-the-signature.md`).
