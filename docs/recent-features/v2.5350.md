# v2.5350 — Messages: a place for the emails that reach bids@, behind the scenes (2026-10-12)

Cut 6 (email in), PR 2 of 4: the migration `20261013080000_messages_email_in.sql`
(`docs/migrations/20261013080000_messages_email_in.md`), after the kernels (v2.5349). No edge function and no screen;
the migration is pushed by hand after merge, and nothing reads bids@ until the `gmail-sync` function is deployed and the
switch is on.

- **`messages.gmail_id`** (unique, so the reader writes a line once) and **`gmail_thread_id`**, only on an email.
- **`message_attachments`**: a line's files and links in order. A file has our copy in the private
  `message-attachments` bucket, or stays in Gmail when it is over 25 MB or was not copied; a link keeps its url. The
  reader's guess at a trade partner's W-9 or certificate rides on the file, and the office's answer to the offer is
  stamped with who and when.
- **`email_place_address`** (an address to its contact, or one unknown contact for a new address) and
  **`email_write_line`** (the line, its files and a forwarded copy's note as a team note, once): the service role's.
- **`gmail_sync_state`**: the reader's place in Gmail's history and its health (`last_run_at`, `last_ok_at`,
  `last_error`, `last_taken`), for a dev through the dev-mcp catalog.
- **The switch** `gmail_sync_on_v1` ships off; the owner or a dev turns it on or off (its own `UPDATE` policy). **A cron**
  calls `gmail-sync` every 3 minutes while it is on, authorized as `gc-office-notices`'s cron is.
- **The bed**: `npm run test:pg:messages-email`, in CI's SQL beds workflow.

Later, as its own piece: the team's own Gmail replies as our lines, which also closes the gap where an estimator sees
none of the emails we sent.
