# v2.2822 — Users: the clock cell opens Hours approvals; Expand all on the queue (2026-09-05)

Owner ask on the new Users status column (v2.2815): "when I click on the people time button, I would like it to open this modal right away — and I would like an expand all button on it."

- **The hours cell is a door to the queue.** `UsersRailCells` / `UsersNeedsPill` take `openHours`; the Users tab mounts `PeopleHoursApprovalsQueueModal` pinned to that account (`pinUserId`), with the same `ClockSessionEditSplitModal` companion the Desk's Hours section uses, and refreshes the row signals after approvals or edits. Viewers without hours or pay access keep the Desk fallback (`openDesk('hours')`).
- **Expand all / Collapse all** on the approvals queue toolbar: opens every person and every week (or folds them all); the label follows the current state; disabled while loading or empty.
- Help guides "add someone to the roster" and "clear the hours approvals backlog" updated.
