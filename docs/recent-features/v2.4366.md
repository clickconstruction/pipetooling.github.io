# v2.4366 — Submittals: the walkthrough knows about parts (2026-10-02)

Grace, 2026-10-02, after the parts train (v2.4319–v2.4332) and v2.4354: "At the top of the page is a 'Walk me through it ▶' I think this needs to be updated now." The walkthrough (`SUBMITTAL_TOUR_STEPS`, plain words since v2.4123) still described a row as one product, a GC answer per row and "one row per product" on the log — and had no stop for the blue catch-up box, which is how BP375's rows were missing their parts that morning without anyone noticing.

## What changed (`src/lib/submittals/submittalTour.ts`)
- **Step 3. Fix the rows** — a row lists its parts, one per line; each part gets its own house, lead time and stage in Edit.
- **Step 3. Catch a draft up** (new, anchor `submittals-catch-up` on the blue box, `data-tour` in `BidsSubmittalsTab`) — what the box means, **Refresh from the takeoff**, **Make it a part**. It carries no `missingBody`, so the tour walks it only when the box is on the page (the v2.4134 rule the robot's stop uses); on a fresh bid the walk is unchanged. Stage 3's `?` still opens on Fix the rows (`STAGE_STOP_ANCHORS[3]` lists both).
- **Step 3. Add the cut sheets** — a house file that lists parts under each tag: **Read its parts**; otherwise pages to rows as before.
- **Step 6. Their answer** — on a row with parts, they answer each part. **Step 7. Resubmit** — parts already approved stay approved.
- **Step 8. Procure** — each part its own line, **To order**, tick the lines on one order and mark them ordered, tap a line to change its house (v2.4354); its missing words say the log fills in as the GC approves each part.
- Stage sentences: 3 reads *Check each row and its parts…*, 8 *…one line per part…*.
- 14 stops (was 13). Every sentence passes the plain-words rules (`submittalTour.test.ts`); *cut sheet* is still explained the first time it is used.
