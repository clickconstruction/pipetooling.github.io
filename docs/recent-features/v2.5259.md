# v2.5259 — Messages: Papers on this job read as a grid, with the strip before the job’s day (2026-10-12)

Wave 1 of the Messages build, cluster 6, third of three kernels: `src/lib/flows/jobPapers.ts` and its test. Stacked on v2.5253 (`flows/board.ts`), whose lines give the compare that has sat too long, and on v2.5254 (the flow's types), whose `paperRead` each cell reads. No table, no screen, no edge function, no migration.

**What it lifts.** Papers on this job as the prototype draws it at v1.176 (`job-papers-mockups.html` B): `paperCell`, `jobPaperCounts`, `jobPapersHtml`'s rows, ranks, pills and filter, and `jobStripHtml`, from `prototype.html` v1.178 on `spike/gc-mode`. Exports: `gridColumns`, `paperCell`, `jobPaperCounts`, `jobPaperPills`, `jobPaperGrid` and `jobStrip`.

**Changed from the prototype, and why** (numbered as in the cluster's report to the lead):

1. *Every cell reads its own job.* A cell takes `paperRead(reading, paper, jobId)` and the company's place on that step for that job. The prototype read a loop's round from the default job, so Hillside Excavation's Pad B quote showed "late 2 days" from its Boerne round.
2. *Numbers in.* A late cell's days come from the place's `lateDays`, not from parsing "late 2 days". A day on file comes from the reading's YYYY-MM-DD. The prototype added ", 2026" to "Sep 12" and parsed that.
8. *The columns are the flow's papers in its steps' order*, so a paper the flow editor adds becomes a column with no list to edit. The trade's final waiver is a column (the lead's call), where the prototype listed the columns by hand and left it out. A column's word stays shorter than the paper's label: "Insurance", "Pay apps", "Changes". *Runs out soon* is the renewal ask's 30 days: `INSURANCE_ASK_DAYS` in `src/lib/gc/promises.ts`, the one place main keeps it. The portal's `COI_WARN_DAYS` and the schedule's not-ready check read the same constant. `paperSend.ts` keeps a private copy, `INSURANCE_ASK_WITHIN_DAYS`. A paper's own days override it through `soonDays`. No column holds one on main yet. The column is `contract_template_documents.renew_ask_days`, an integer where null means 30, for a paper the flow editor adds (the new paper window's "days before it runs out, ask for the next one"). Wave 2's SQL adds it, and Settings reads and writes it there. The papers the seed knows keep the constant.
9. *The strip's compare line is two sentences*: "Tejas Power's quote is in. The compare has sat 3 days, over the usual 2." The prototype's semicolon failed plain words. A test runs every strip line through `plainWordsFailures`.

Also changed:
- *An our-move paper* on a step on now reads "ours" and its press sends it. The prototype read "their move" for our bid too.
- *A press says what it does*: open a paper on file, ask for a paper they give us, send one we write (`flows/facts.ts`'s `ours`), remind on one that went or is late. The prototype asked for everything that was not on file, our statement of work included.
- *A cell still waiting with its day ahead reads the day*: a quote asked Sep 24 and due Thu reads "due Thu", not "8 d" since it was asked. The prototype counted days since the paper went for every waiting cell.
- *The quotes due line names three companies at most* ("A, B, C and 2 more"), so it stays one short sentence however many trades are asked.
- *"all in"* shows only when there is something in. A job with no papers yet shows no pills, where the prototype said "all in".
- *The strip* says nothing after bid day, where the prototype counted negative days. Our bid having gone says nothing either. The compare line uses the board's over usual, so a quote with its day ahead never makes one (the lead's call 4).
- *Before bill day* takes the gates from `billDay/trades.ts` (helper 7's cluster) as `{ ok, text, small }`, and each line's press stays its gate's own (`{ kind: 'gate', index }`). This kernel never computes a gate.
