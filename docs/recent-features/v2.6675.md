# v2.6675 — Messages: the bids@ reader keeps each email as the sender laid it out (2026-11-08)

Punch list #259 (`to-dos/email-rich-text.md`), PR 1 of 2, on the owner's yes of 2026-11-07. Grace asked to read an email as rich text or as plain words. The reader kept the words (the plain part, else the HTML read as text) and the HTML's `href`s as link rows, and dropped the HTML; there was nothing to draw. Now it keeps the HTML part.

- **The kernel** (`src/lib/emailIn/gmailMessage.ts`, mirrored): `EmailIn.html` is the mail's `text/html` parts as they came, one after the other, trimmed; null when the mail has none or the part is longer than `EMAIL_HTML_MAX` (2,000,000 characters: a newsletter's whole page or an attachment written inline). `htmlPart` is the rule. The words are what they were.
- **The reader** (`supabase/functions/gmail-sync/index.ts`): after `email_write_line` answers written with the line's id, `keepHtml` uploads the part to the private `message-attachments` bucket at `<line id>/body.html` (`text/html; charset=utf-8`, upsert) and writes the path onto `messages.body_html_path`. Best effort: either step failing warns and leaves the line plain, as every line was before. A repeat (written false) keeps nothing. A bare take (v2.6667) keeps it too, since the part came with the message and no file is fetched. The bucket's office SELECT policy covers the path as it covers the files.
- **The migration** (`20261108020000_messages_body_html_path.sql`): one nullable column, `messages.body_html_path`, the marker the viewer reads so it knows an HTML body exists without a bucket list. No back-fill; older lines stay null. Not a column of HTML: a thread's read brings every line's row, and the part most lines never open runs 20 to 200 KB against the few KB the words take.
- **Tests**: `gmailMessage.test.ts` (the part as it came beside the words, none for a plain-only mail, none blank, none past the cap, two parts joined); the `EmailIn` fixtures of `emailLine`, `tagPlan`, `mineFunction`, `devMcpMailSearch` and `devMcpMailTag` carry the field; `src/lib/gc/edgeKernels.test.ts` holds the mirror.
- **Docs**: `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` § gmail-sync, `docs/MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md` (the thread window's lines, the reader paragraph), `docs/migrations/20261108020000_messages_body_html_path.md`.

**Order**: `supabase db push` first, then `supabase functions deploy gmail-sync --no-verify-jwt`. Deployed first, the reader uploads each HTML and warns that the marker would not write, so those lines stay plain for good. `gmail-mine`, `walk` and `dev-mcp` import the kernel and carry the field at their next deploy; none reads it.
