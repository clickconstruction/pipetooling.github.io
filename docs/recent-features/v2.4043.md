# v2.4043 — GC Review: where a GC's checks went is figured in one tested place (2026-09-28)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. PR 1 of the "Where the checks went" train (owner ask, 2026-09-28: a GC's bookkeeper wanted *"a report of where our checks were last applied"* across every job the GC pays on; mock-up artifact `KKRvpT2R41Vc7iewLoKpCY`).

## What shipped

- **`src/lib/jobs/gcChecksApplied.ts`** (pure, 15 tests) — `buildGcChecksReport({ gcId, jobs, events, deposits, sinceYmd })`:
  - **Checks.** The office records a check that covered two jobs as two payment rows with the same number and date; they fold into one check (`ref:<number>|<date>`; a deposit-linked payment with no number folds by deposit; anything else is its own row). Each check: kind (check · ACH · wire · card · other, read from `payment_type`), label (`#48211`, or `check · no number recorded`), received / sent / deposited dates, the sum, one **line per bill it sits on now** (`Invoice 2 of 3`, the address-led job label the statement uses), whether that bill or job is paid in full.
  - **Which bill.** A linked payment is its bill's. An unlinked one splits under the shared oldest-bill-first rule (`paymentAttribution.ts`, v2.3592), and money no sent bill needed is a line of its own, *on the job, no bill yet* — the same reading the portal and the demand letter give.
  - **Whose money.** A line counts only when the GC pays that bill (`effectiveInvoiceParty` + `payerCustomerId`, the shared who-pays rule): a bill the owner pays on a GC's job is left out; a GC entered as the job's customer is counted.
  - **The trail.** A moved payment keeps its row (`move_job_payment` updates `job_id`), so `jobs_ledger_payment_events` rows of kind `moved` that name one of the check's payments become its *was on* list (amount, from, to, day), newest first.
  - **Unapplied.** For a deposit-linked check, the deposit's dollars no job has applied yet.
  - **By job.** Billed (sent bills the GC pays), the distinct check labels oldest first, the last one applied, retainage held (`lien_retainage_held`), still open, paid.
  - **The period.** `sinceYmd` keeps checks received on or after the day (undated ones stay) and counts the rest as `earlierCount`; the by-job roll-up always reads every payment, so billed − paid = open holds.
  - `findChecks(checks, query)` — by number (two or more digits), by amount to the cent (the check's or a line's), by day (`2026-09-24`) or `Sep 24`.
  - `billPaidByWords(job, invoice)` — the line that will go under each bill: *paid in full by #48102 on Sep 10* · *$12,000.00 paid by #48211 on Sep 24 · $1,333.00 still open, the retainage you hold* (only when what is left is within the retainage the job records) · *nothing applied yet* · for a job balance with no bill, *paid $21,750.00 so far by …*.
  - `checkHeadline` / `checkAppliedSentence` / `checkMoveWords` / `checkWasOnWords` — the sentences Find a check and the sheet read out.

## Decided here

- **"Last applied" means the current home plus the trail**, because payments move. The sheet's *was on* column and Find a check's trail come from the events ledger, never from guessing.
- **Undated checks sort last** in a newest-first list — an anomaly, not the newest.
- **One rule for which bill, one rule for whose money** — both imported from the shared kernels the portal and the edge functions already use, so a GC never reads a different answer from a different screen.
