# v2.7898 — Lien desk: the Board's Retainage line opens its list (2026-11-18)

The owner's pick of 2026-11-18, one of the last five pieces of the Board's drawing (punch list #383, option B) that a live look found still different. He saw each redrawn on the Board as it stands and picked all five; each is its own PR. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## Before

- Under the notices' column the Board says retainage in one line, like *Retainage · 1 to send for approval · 6 look held, not on the job*. Retainage has no bin and no chip, so that line was the Board's only mention of it, and it was words alone. A reader who wanted the six jobs had to know to go to By paper and pick Retainage notices.

## What changed

- **The line ends in *Open ›*.** `LienBoard` takes `onRetainage` and draws the line as a row: the words in their own span (`data-lien-board-retainage`, as before), then the door, a text press in the link colour with a 32 px height on a phone. With no handler the line is words alone, as it was.
- **Where it goes.** The desk's `openRetainageList` opens By paper on the Retainage list with no pile picked and nothing in the find box. Every count on the line has its rows there: the piles, and above them the jobs whose retainage looks held and is not on the job. The desk's two older doors to that list, a To do row's, open it the same way at one job.
- **The kernel is a file of its own.** `lienBoardRetainageDoor.ts` holds the door's words and its hint, and `lienBoardRetainageDoor` answers the line with or without a door, or null with no line, so a door never stands alone.

## What it leaves alone

- **The line's words and counts** (`lienBoardRetainageLine`) are unchanged.
- **One door, not one a count.** The two counts on the line live on the one list, so the line has the one press.

## Tests

- `lienBoardRetainageDoor.test.ts`: the door's words, words alone with no door, nothing with no line, and a door for the line that holds only retainage nobody recorded.
- `LienBoard.retainageDoor.render.test.tsx`: the row's words and door, the words' own mark kept, the press handed to the desk, no door with no handler, no line with an empty retainage desk, a phone's press height.
- `LienDesk.render.test.tsx`: on the desk the door opens the Retainage list whole, with the counted job above the piles and no row ringed.

## Verified

- On the dev server against the live desk in headless Chrome, a computer (1440 px) and a phone (390 px, the bins opened), light and dark: the door opens By paper on the Retainage list. Look only: no print, send, approve, remind, record or mail press.
