# v2.5459 — Messages: where a sub is on each of their sheets, read from the work order and the sheet, behind the scenes (2026-10-13)

Residential Messages R8 · 12, PR 3 of 3 (customer communication helper 1, directed by the lead): the sub flow's loader and the sub's lines on their sheet, `customer-messages-integrations-mockups.html` card 12 on `spike/gc-mode`. After #5586 (the sub flow's facts and seed) and #5662 (v2.5490, which moved the residential loader to `residentialLoad.ts`); it reads the two columns v2.5429 added. No migration, no screen.

- **`subStampsFor(rows)`** in `src/lib/flows/residentialLoad.ts`, beside `residentialStampsFor` and for the same reason, since the sheet's money (`subLaborOutstanding`) must not ride into the edge copy of `load.ts`: one sub's `FlowStamps` for `readFlow` with the sub flow. A lane per sheet (`people_labor_jobs.id`), and one per work order with no sheet yet, keyed `order:<step_commitments.id>` (`orderLaneId`); a draft order with no sheet has not been sent and has no lane. The default lane is the newest unpaid sheet, else the newest lane, since the star on `contact_jobs` names a Pipeline job and not a sheet. A skip has no sheet column, so a sub's lanes take none.
- **`workOrderStamps`**: `sub_asked` (`offered_at`; a withdrawn order is a draft with none), `sub_said_yes` (`accepted_at`) and `sub_declined` (`declined_at` while the status says declined; a re-offer clears it), from the lane's newest live order, a cancelled one left out. A sheet with no work order was agreed outside the app: asked on the day it was made, said yes with no day, as `statusStamps` reads a job whose moves were never kept, so a sheet made by hand stands on On site and not on our work order.
- **`sheetDayStamps`**: `sub_on_site` and `sub_done` from `on_site_at` and `done_at`, which `facts.ts` now calls kept.
- **`sheetIsPaid`** and **`sheetPaidStamps`**: `sub_paid` once `subLaborJobBalance` is 0 or less to the cent and money went to the sub, on the newest payment's day. A sheet with no lines and no payments balances to 0 and is not paid.
- **`subLinesOnSheet(sheet, messages, subContactIds, nowIso)`** in `src/lib/messages/sheetLines.ts`: the sub's lines tied to the sheet's job (`pipeline:<job_ledger_id>`), every contact of theirs, oldest first, and `lastWord`, their newest line to us with its snippet and `lineWhen`. It returns parts, never a sentence, since the card's strip joins them with a dot. Two sheets on one job show the same lines; a sheet with no job shows none.
- **Tests**: `residentialLoad.sub.test.ts` and `sheetLines.test.ts` over the mock-up's Manny Reyes on the Oakridge tie-in (1312): the offer and the yes, a withdraw, a decline and a re-offer, a cancelled order, a sheet made by hand, an order before its sheet, a back charge, a sheet owed money, an empty sheet not paid, the default lane, the walk from the work order to the pay, and an unlinked sheet with no lines. `facts.test.ts` moves its pins for the two kept facts.
