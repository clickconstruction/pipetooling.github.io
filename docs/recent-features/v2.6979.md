# v2.6979 — The Bridge reads Market one way on the whole page (punch list #318, PR 4) (2026-11-11)

PR 4 of six for *Market and Sales on the won rates* (`to-dos/market-sales-won-rates/README.md`), items 1 to 4 under *Not built*. Until now the pipe read Market at what the asks are worth while the Market card behind **The flow**, its badge, the dial and the usual fixes still judged the raw dollars at one win share. This PR is new work: the spike did not build it. No migration. Five kernels copied to the edge functions change, so five functions are owed a redeploy.

## What changed

- **The Market card** (`stageFlow.ts`): `MarketStageInput` carries `worth`, the Market amount with what the bids win and what the estimates sell (`MarketWorthInput`, `marketWorthPerWeek`). The card's last two lines read *worth $X a week* and *bids win $A, estimates sell $B*, and the badge compares that amount with what the field builds: *not short* or *short of the field*. Until the worth is read the card says *about $X a week bid* and *what it is worth is not read yet*, and its badge is *wait*: it judges nothing. `marketWinShare`, the one share for bids and estimates over the eight weeks, is gone from the code.
- **Market's pace** (`dial.ts`): `todayPace('market')` is the worth. Until it is read it is still the dollars that came in, so the pipe has a section to draw; `marketPaceIsWorth` says which. The dial for Market is in dollars of worth, labelled *worth a week*, and there is no dial to move until the worth is read. Moving it scales the asks and both parts. Its lines read *Market is worth $X a week at the won rates. The field builds $Y a week. Demand is short of the field.*
- **The pipe's word** (`flowStrip.ts`): with `marketIsWorth` the Market section reads *$X worth*, and *Market. Worth $X a week.* to a screen reader. This is what the pipe drawn across (`?pipe=across`) shows now. Without the flag it reads *asked*, as it did.
- **Push for more bids** (`waits.ts`): sized as a third more of what Market is worth, and its line says *Market is worth $X more a week.*
- **Sales, by the mark alone** (`demandPace.ts`): a bid is won when it is marked won, or marked started or complete, and lost when it is marked lost. A job made from a bid with no mark is no win, and that bid is still awaiting an answer. `startedUsd`, the dollars of unmarked bids that became jobs, is gone from `SalesStageInput`, and with it the note on the Sales card's won figure.
  - **The day of a win.** Most won bids carry no answer day. A marked win is dated by its answer day, else by the day its job was made. The job only places the win in the window. A won bid with neither day is in no week's count.
  - **A won bid with no value** takes its job's revenue, as `bidWins.ts` reads it.
  - **One more read** (`loadDemandPace.ts`): the bids behind the jobs made inside the window. A bid marked won with no answer day can be older than the loader's three reads, and before this it reached no count at all.
- **The Sales window's rates** (`sales/salesDesk.ts`, `Bridge.tsx`): an asker with fewer than five marked bids has each ask read at the won rate for bids its size (`bidSizeRates`, from `BID_SIZES` and each size's `usedRate`); an ask no size has a rate for falls to all bids together. The estimates are read at the estimates' won rate by dollars. The row says *read at the rate for bids this size*, and the footnote says the rule.
- **A bid a job was made from stays in the open pile** until it is marked (`sales/loadSalesDesk.ts`). The read of the jobs behind the open bids is gone with the filter that used it.
- **The Market window's loader** (`market/loadMarketDesk.ts`) no longer reads the jobs made from bids, and `MarketDeskBid.jobCreatedYmd` is gone: nothing used it. The page hands the Market window no win share. Its older header, which only shows when the won rates cannot be read, says *What that is worth is not read yet.*

## What was left as it is

- **Sales' pace line, *sold $X of work a week***, is the contract on the jobs made in the window (`buildPace.ts`), as before. It is work handed to the field, not a count of bids won, so the mark-only rule does not reach it. The card's item names `buildDemand` alone. If the owner wants that line on the marks too, that is one more change.
- **The weekly email** does not hand the kernels a worth yet, so its Market section still reads the dollars that came in under *asked*. PR 5.

## Verify

Walked on the dev server as a dev on production data, in headless Chrome with every write refused, at 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844. Nothing that records was pressed.

- `/bridge?door=flow`: the Market card reads *worth … a week* over *bids win …, estimates sell …*, with the badge *short of the field*. The amount is the one on the pipe's Market row.
- Pressed the Market card: the dial reads *Market · worth a week*, and its three boxes read the amount against the field.
- Pressed **Push for more bids**: *Market is worth … more a week.*
- `/bridge?pipe=across`: the Market section reads *… worth*.
- The Sales window, **Every asker**: the askers with few marked bids read *read at the rate for bids this size*, and the estimates' row reads their own won rate. **How this is counted** says the rule.
- The Market window opens on the same amount.
- No sideways scroll and no page error at either width.
