# v2.8745 — A group of pins on a map names what it holds (2026-11-27)

Punch list #292. Finding 327 of the fixing thread's ledger, from the walking thread's sitting 35 of 2026-11-27 (Prospects → Permits → Map, 1280 px, reads only). Client only. No migration, no edge function. Nothing owed after the merge.

**As seen.** All 32 groups of pins on the permits map read *N bids here — click to zoom in*. They are permits. It is the group's own name, which a hover and a screen reader read.

**Read.** Both canvases, `PinsMapCanvas.tsx` and `PinsMapGoogleCanvas.tsx`, wrote the words and defaulted the noun to `'bids'`. Four maps group their pins. Only the Map page named its groups (*places*). The permits map (`ResearchMapCard.tsx`, both of its callers) and the Jobs map (`JobsMapCard.tsx`) named nothing, so each said *bids*. The Jobs map was read in the code, not seen on a walk. The Bid Board's map was right by the default alone.

**The mend.**

- `clusterNoun="permits"` on the permits map and `clusterNoun="jobs"` on the Jobs map. Each card's pins were read first: the research card draws permits for both callers, and the Jobs map draws jobs.
- The Bid Board's map now says `clusterNoun="bids"` itself.
- The default is no longer *bids*. A kernel in `src/lib/map/clusterPins.ts`, `clusterTitle(count, noun)`, writes the words for both canvases, and a map that names nothing gets *pins* (`CLUSTER_NOUN_UNNAMED`). So the next map cannot say *bids* by accident.

The words around the noun are as they were.
