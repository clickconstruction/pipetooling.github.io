# v2.4595 — What customers see: the sample review room reads like a real one (2026-10-05)

Punch list #62 (*See what they see*), Layer 5 as rescoped on 2026-10-05. No migration. One edge function to redeploy after the merge: `get-submittal-room`, which serves the sample tokens from `_shared/customerSampleFixtures.ts`. Until then the sample shows the old rows; nothing breaks.

## What was wrong

What customers see has shown the GC's review room since v2.3511 (`/submittal?t=sample`, `sample-done`). But `sampleSubmittalRoomResponse` wrote its five `RoomRow`s by hand, so the sample could say what no real room says:

- the to-follow row read *Not in our scope — by others.*, where the room's kernel says *No product yet — to follow.*;
- WC-1's why named two reasons, and a row holds one;
- no row had parts, so the per-part answers of v2.4322 never showed;
- no row was a product we intend to install (v2.4482), the shape of every bid built from the takeoff.

## What changed

- [`_shared/customerSampleFixtures.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/customerSampleFixtures.ts): the sample is seven submittal rows and WC-1's four parts (`RoomItemSource`, `RoomPartSource`), run through `roomRowsFrom` → `roomCounts`, the call `get-submittal-room` makes for a real bid. WC-1's own answer is `rollUpPartDecisions` of its parts, as `submit-submittal-review` writes it. The order-only row (HB-1) and the order-only part (the supply stop) are there so the sample goes through the same filter; neither reaches the page.
- The open room reads *3 products need your answer*: WC-1 and WH-1 differ, SH-1 is a product we intend to install, TP-1 is an accessory, MB-1 has no product yet, L-1 matches. After Alex Sample's review, WC-1's bowl and seat are approved and its carrier is sent back, so the row reads Revise. SH-1 is approved and WH-1 is sent back, and the room reads *All 3 decided — thank you*.
