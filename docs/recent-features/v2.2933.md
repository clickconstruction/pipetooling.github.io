# v2.2933 — The GC sees the stages you offer (2026-09-06)

PR 7 of the three-party scheduling train (prototype: artifact 3a540149). Stacked on v2.2932. The owner's decisions that shaped it: sharing is per job and off by default; the GC sees one stage at a time unless the office deliberately bundles several onto one window; the GC never sees money, paperwork, or a sub's phone.

## Data

- Migration [`20260906090000_gc_stage_offers.sql`](../migrations/20260906090000_gc_stage_offers.md): `jobs_ledger.gc_shares_stage_dates` / `gc_auto_offer_next`; `job_stage_windows.offered_to_gc` / `offered_to_gc_at` / `bundle_id`.

## Kernel — `supabase/functions/_shared/gcStages.ts` (tested from `src/lib/subs/gcStages.test.ts`)

`buildGcStageEntries({ windows, fixtures, orders, sheets })`: offered windows only; a bundle (shared `bundle_id`) is one entry named "Top-out + Trim & final" with the first window; each entry carries `who` (the sub's first name, only once signed), `when` (the pick), `pct` (the sheet's progress), and `state` — `window` → `offered` → `scheduled` → `working` → `inspection` → `passed` — from the fulfilling order and its sheet. `gcStageLine` words it.

## Customer portal

- `customer-portal` function: for `gc` / `all` links, jobs whose `gc_shares_stage_dates` is on **and** whose GC is the viewer; loads offered windows, their line items, the fulfilling orders and sheets; returns `stages: [{ jobId, jobLabel, jobAddress, entries }]`. `PortalJobRow.gc_shares_stage_dates` rides the job select.
- Client `portalPayload.ts`: `PortalPayload.stages` (+ `PortalStageEntry`, parsed defensively). `CustomerPortal.tsx`: a **Stages** card above Your agreements — per job, each entry with its name ("· together" for a bundle), "Behar · Sep 22 – Sep 23" or "Between Sep 22 – Oct 2", and a chip (Planned / Offered to a sub / Scheduled / 50% along / Inspection next / Passed).

## Office

- Edit Job → GC/Builder: [`JobGcStageSharingSwitches`](../../src/components/jobs/JobGcStageSharingSwitches.tsx) under the picker for an existing job — the share switch and, once on, the auto-offer switch; saved on the spot against the job row (never through the form's save-on-close). `JobFormGcPicker` / `JobFormCustomerSection` take `jobId`.
- Subs → Work: a **GC portal** column — "off for this job" (tooltip points at the switch), **Offer to GC** on a windowed row, **Shown** (· bundle) with **Withdraw** (a bundle withdraws together); the job foot's **Offer several together…** multi-select builds a bundle (`bundle_id = crypto.randomUUID()`). Jobs sharing dates are loaded once (`jobs_ledger` where the switch is on, with the GC's name).
- The sheet story's inspection pass (`setStage('customer_pay')`) calls [`offerNextStageAfterPass`](../../src/lib/subs/offerNextStage.ts): the next not-yet-offered window by line-item order (`nextStageToOffer`, tested) is offered on its own when `gc_auto_offer_next` is on, else a `dispatch_requests` row lands — "#1004 · …: a stage passed — offer Top-out to Summit General?" (`pending_action: 'offer_next_stage'`, the window id in `pending_payload`) and `notify-dispatch-request` fires.
