# v2.3819 — Put a GC on notice: the GC's jobs by stage, and the door to mark each one right (2026-09-25)

Punch list **#43** (`to-dos/gc-notice-jobs-band/`), option A. The owner, 2026-09-25, on the run window for RMC- Dudley Mason: *"I would like to see all of a GC's jobs and their line items and progress added to this current view … so we know what jobs of theirs are at what stage and we can dig in to mark some of them more correctly."* On that run seven of the 22 jobs read wrong on the record — J372 in *Waiting* with 80 % done and a draw billed Sep 14, J305 and J436 70–90 % done in *Waiting* with nothing billed, J706 *Waiting* with a paid draw and no percent, J651 with a Top Out draw out and no percent, J800 and J868 billed with no percent — and the window gave no way to see that, let alone reach the field. A notice claims what the record says, so the record is the thing to fix first. Client only, no migration.

## The change

- **Kernel [`gcNoticeJobsBand.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/gcNoticeJobsBand.ts)** (new, tested against the live run's rows): `buildGcNoticeBand(jobs, todayYmd, order)` — one row per job with the stage on record (`gcNoticeBandStageOf`), the line items with the money poured onto them (`pourGcNoticeBandLines`: a line an invoice names takes that invoice's payments as paid and its unpaid remainder as billed, then paid → billed → done-not-billed pour down the lines in order; each line reads paid · billed · part · done · not started, a "(migrated)" placeholder flagged), the four figures (`gcNoticeBandBilledUnpaid` nets each sent bill to its unpaid part), and the **readings** (`gcNoticeBandReadings`): the stage the evidence says (*Waiting, but 80% done and a bill out* · *Waiting, but a draw paid* · *Working, but 100% done and billed* → Status ▾), *set % done* (→ % done; red when a bill is out or the job is billed, the Pipeline's v2.3411 alert), *$X done, not billed* (→ Bill it), *quiet N d* on Waiting / Working after `GC_NOTICE_BAND_QUIET_DAYS` = 30 (→ Open). Groups by stage (Waiting → Working → Ready to bill → Billed → Collections → Paid, biggest open first inside), by open, or by property, each head counting jobs, open money and how many look wrong; one head line (`gcNoticeBandStageWords`, the totals).
- **Hook [`useGcOnNoticeData`](../../src/hooks/useGcOnNoticeData.ts)** gains `workByJob: Record<jobId, GcNoticeBandWork>` — the job's `status` and `pct_complete` beside the desk's columns, and one chunked read each of `jobs_ledger_fixtures`, `jobs_ledger_invoices` and `jobs_ledger_payments` (the three a Pipeline row already carries). A failed read leaves the band's row without lines, never the run without the job.
- **[`GcNoticeJobsBand.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/GcNoticeJobsBand.tsx)** (new) renders between the brief and Step 1 in [`GcOnNoticeModal`](../../src/components/jobs/GcOnNoticeModal.tsx): the head line, the order switch, the groups, and per row the job (number · name, address, last on site), the stage chip with its reading chips (or *✓ reads right*), the lines, the Pipeline's own cell (`progressPaymentForJob` → `StagesProgressPaymentCell view` — stage chips on J651 / J706, the work channel over the money edge), the Job window's tiles (`jobWindowTiles`) and the open figure. *Hide the jobs ▴* and the order are remembered per browser (`gcNoticeBandOpen`, `gcNoticeBandOrder`). On a phone each row is the job and its open figure, then the stage chips, the bar and the lines.
- **The doors.** `GcOnNoticeModalProps.onOpenEditJob`'s `focus` gains `'status' | 'pct' | 'bill' | 'line-items'`, and `onOpenJob` (the Job tab) is new. [`JobsStagesTab`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsStagesTab.tsx) wires them: a row → `openJobDetail`; a chip → `tryOpenEditJob` with `focusRow: 'status'`, or `initialTab: 'bill'` + `focusRow: 'pct'`, or `initialTab: 'bill'`; a line or the bar → `initialTab: 'bill'` + `fixturesSectionHighlight`. The Job window (z 1010) opens over the run window (z 90), which keeps its scroll, its owner lookups and its typed answers; ✕ hands back and the run re-reads on the window's `onSaved` (autosave fires it on every change) — a job moved to Billed regroups and its Step 2 claim follows.
- **Two focus rows in the Job window** — [`jobFormFocusRow.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobFormFocusRow.ts) gains `'status'` (the status stepper on Edit) and `'pct'` (the % done field on Bill); `isJobFormFactRow` keeps `JobFormEditFactRows` to its own two rows. [`JobFormModal`](../../src/components/jobs/JobFormModal.tsx) scrolls to the field once it has a size (the Bill region is display-toggled) and rings it for 4.5 s (`FOCUS_FIELD_RING`, the ③ Payments received ring of v2.3795).
