# v2.8513 — Hard Reload and the could-not-load Reload ask first over typed words (2026-11-24)

Punch list #85. The two presses v2.8499 left (`docs/recent-features/v2.8499.md`, *What is left*: *The other presses a person makes are theirs*). Diane said yes on 2026-11-24 to both warning the way the update pill's **Reload** does. Client only: no migration and no edge function.

## Before

Three presses reload the app at a person's own word. v2.8499 made the update pill's **Reload** warn first over typed words and walk the page through each place that holds them. The other two still reloaded at once:

- **Hard Reload** in the gear menu, which unregisters the service worker, empties the caches and reloads.
- **Reload** on a page that could not load (`RouteChunkBoundary`), which asks the network first (v2.8458) and then does the same.

The second one shows more often than it did. Since v2.8471 typed words hold the recovery reload, so a lost page with typed words on the screen draws its **Reload** instead of reloading by itself.

## Now

Both presses call `askReloadOverWords(reload)` (`src/lib/reloadOverWords.ts`).

| The page | What the press does |
|---|---|
| nothing typed | `reload()` at once, as before |
| words typed | nothing reloads. The ask goes to the bar. |
| no bar on the page | `reload()` at once. A press never does nothing. |

**The bar** (`src/components/ReloadOverWordsBar.tsx`) is mounted once, beside the update pill, and draws only while a press is being asked about. It is the pill's warning as a bar of its own, on the same kernels (`typedTextTour.ts`, `updatePillWarn.ts`):

- *Reloading clears what you typed.* with **Reload anyway** and **Not now**, both 44 tall.
- The page is walked through each place that holds typed words, top first, each ringed in orange for a second.
- **Reload anyway** is dead until every place was shown, and never for less than a second. Then it runs the press's reload.
- **Not now**, or a touch anywhere off the bar, forgets the ask. The words stay.
- The bar goes to the top of the screen while a place under its foot is shown.
- A press or a touch inside 400 ms of the bar showing is the tail of the press that raised it, and is ignored.
- **Not now** takes the focus when the bar shows, so a keyboard's next key lands on the safe press.

It is `role="status"`, not a dialog. The page behind must stay free to scroll, since scrolling it is the walk.

**Hard Reload.** The gear menu closes, as it always did, and the page is what the person is shown.

**Reload on a page that could not load.** The ask comes before the network is asked. **Reload anyway** then sends the one ask of v2.8458, and its answer reloads or says *No signal, so nothing was reloaded.*

One ask at a time. A second press while the bar is up keeps the walk and takes the newer reload.

## Seen in the built app

On 2026-11-24, the whole change built with `vite build` and its `dist` served locally. A headless Chromium 149 at 1,280 by 800 and at 390 by 844, signed in with a session from the dev-login account, with the build's own service worker in control. Read only: the note boxes were the test's own, typed into by the keyboard, and nothing was pressed but the gear menu, the two presses and the bar. Both widths gave the same result.

**Hard Reload**, with words in two boxes, one above the page and one below its foot.

- **Pressed.** Nothing reloaded and the menu closed. The bar read *Reloading clears what you typed.* with **Reload anyway** dead. The top box was ringed (`3px solid rgb(249, 115, 22)`), then the foot box, and the page went to it. The bar went to the top of the screen once the foot box had landed. **Reload anyway** came alive at 3.5 seconds at the desk and 3.0 on the phone.
- **A touch off the bar.** The bar went. Both boxes still held their words.
- **Pressed again, then Not now.** The bar went. The same document, the words still there.
- **Pressed again, then Reload anyway.** The app reloaded through `/?nocache=…` and came back on the same page.
- **With nothing typed.** It reloaded at once.

**Reload on a page that could not load**, with a note typed. `bills-sent`'s file was taken out of the precache and the browser set offline, so the page drew *This page could not load — the app may have just been updated.* The network was then let back.

- **Reload pressed.** The bar showed and the note was ringed for a second. No ask left for the network, and nothing navigated.
- **Reload anyway.** One ask left (`/favicon.svg?signal=…`), the page reloaded, and Bills sent drew.

The bar is 466 by 93 at the desk and 358 by 93 on the phone. Nothing was wider than the screen, and the page threw no error.

## Not proved

- The live site.
- A real report or message box in the app: the note boxes were the test's own.
- A double press on **Hard Reload** in a browser. The menu closes on the first, so the second lands on the page under it. A render test holds that a touch right behind the press is ignored.
- A place inside an open window, less motion asked for, the keyboard alone, and dark.
- A page drawn without the app's shell, where there is no bar and the press reloads at once.
- Chromium only.

## What is left

- **A dev's Global Reload goes over typed words.** The gear menu's **Global Reload** makes every open tab hard-reload at once (`ForceReloadContext`), whatever is typed in it. It is a reload the person in that tab did not ask for, so it falls under the rule. It is also the dev's tool for moving everyone off a bad build at once. Whether it should wait for typed words is the owner's call, and it is not built here.
- **Fix app** (`/fix-cache.html`) leaves the app for a page of its own and warns of nothing.
- **A browser's own refresh** is the browser's.
