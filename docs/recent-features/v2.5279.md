# v2.5279 — Messages: each flow reads the records the app already keeps (2026-10-12)

Messages wave 1, cluster 4, the loader (helper 4). Kernels only: no screen, no table, no migration, no edge function. The query layer that selects the rows comes with cut 4's screens.

- **`src/lib/flows/load.ts`**: main's rows turned into one person's `FlowStamps`, pure (no Supabase client). The row types are written out until the types regen after `20261012010000_messages_spine.sql` and `20261012120000_messages_flows.sql`.
- **One function per fact family**, as `facts.ts` names each fact's `reads`:
  - `inviteSentStamps` (`gc_trade_messages` of kind invite);
  - `portalStamps` (the first visit to a live link, else the press on Got it);
  - `paperStamps` (the company Documents' rules: a signed W-9, the newest signed certificate current through its last day and bad after, a signed agreement or one out to sign);
  - `askRounds` (one round per ask, finished by their quote or their no, due their promised day from the call log, else the bid's day; a job the office tied them to with no ask is one round not opened);
  - `packageStamps` (carried, or not carried by another's quote or a lost mark), `sowStamps`, `drawRounds`, `finalWaiverStamps` (the final draw's unconditional waiver, else the upload);
  - `projectStamps` (asked, bid, yes, declined, contract, start letter, started, closed, the warranty year);
  - `payAppRounds` (due the pay-by days), `certifyRounds`, `changeOrderRounds` (sent ones only);
  - `planStamps`, `addendaRounds`, `questionRounds` (bidding questions due when questions close, RFIs due their needed days), `submittalRoundStamps`;
  - `skipsOf`, `waitsOf` (a pay application out and not certified waits on the job's architect), `offOf`.
- **`flowStampsFor(kind, rows, now)`** puts them together with one `JobStamps` per GC project: the jobs the facts put them on and the ones the office tied them to, the star first.
- **A fix in `readFlow`**: a loop that runs until what follows (`loopEnds: 'next'`) ends only when a later step that runs once reads true. Before, a certified pay application ended the architect's Submittals.
- **A new fact, `quote_declined`** (the lead's call): their no to an ask, read from `gc_invites.status = 'declined'` and `declined_on`, which the office's decline and the trade portal's press both write. It ends the job's lane (`endsLane`), so a trade who declines stays on the board with the ask done instead of vanishing. The trade's ask is now finished by `quote_in` or `quote_declined`. `readFlow` ends a lane when every round of a loop was finished by a way out, as a fork's way out does.
- **`facts.ts`**: the `reads` of `portal_opened`, `not_carried`, `said_yes` and `payapp_paid` name cut 4's columns first.
- **Tests**: `load.test.ts` builds Greg, Elena and Jonah from rows shaped like main's columns and checks `readFlow` reads them as `flowFixtures.ts` does, lane by lane. Tom declining Pad B is pinned from rows and from stamps. Each family's edges are pinned.
