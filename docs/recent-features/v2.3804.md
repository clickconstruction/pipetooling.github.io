## Decisions taken

- **Insert the ledger row before sending.** Mercury delivers at least once, and a later change to the same transaction arrives under a new delivery signature, so `mercury_webhook_events` cannot fold this; the row is the decision to send. The cost: a Resend outage at that moment means no retry — the v2.3795 card is the backstop, and the row's zero counts say it happened.
- **Never on a deposit nobody matched** — there is nothing to take off a job; AR's chip is that case. **Never on a processing failure** (failed, never posted): eleven of prod's seventeen failed check deposits, all simply re-deposited.
- **Only the webhook path.** The Banking page's 90-day sync also writes the status but sends nothing — a return the webhook missed reaches the office through the card.
- **No Deno on the dev box** — the function's TypeScript could not be type-checked locally; the kernel it imports is vitest-covered and the function body follows `paid-job-email`'s push and `notify-help-feedback`'s history writes line for line.
