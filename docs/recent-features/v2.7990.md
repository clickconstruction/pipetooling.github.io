# v2.7990 — Painted maps: read a saved map on Google Maps (2026-11-19)

Punch list [#387](../../to-dos/painted-maps-on-google.md), step 2 of 5: a Google canvas for looking. The owner asked on 2026-11-17 to see Google Maps under a painted map. A saved map opened in its window now draws on Google's map. Drawing still happens on the Leaflet canvas, which stays whole. Client only: no migration and no function.

## What a person sees

- **A saved map opens on Google Maps.** **From above** is Google's photograph with the street names on it (`hybrid`, call 2's pick). **Streets** is Google's road map. The lines, the numbered places and their marks are drawn as before.
- **Draw a line** or **＋ Place** puts the view from above back, on the spot the painter was looking at. The window then keeps that map until it closes.
- **Time** takes Google's map down for the archive and gives it back when Time is left.
- **Make a picture** in the Share sheet takes Google's map down under the picture, and the window keeps the other map after it.
- With no key, or once Google fails on the page, a painted map opens on the Leaflet canvas as it always did.
- The public page `/way`, the picture and the printed page are not on Google.

## The rule, as it stands

`src/lib/paintedMap/paintedMapProvider.ts` (v2.7979) decides, and its reach is now `window` and `looking`. Three things were added to it in this step.

- **One map while something is made** (`paintedMapKeepsLeaflet`). A screen that has left Google's map to draw, to place, to walk or to show a picture keeps the Leaflet canvas until it closes. Time is not making and does go back.
- **A window opened to add a place is placing from its first moment** (`paintedWindowUse(mode, opensOnPlace)`). It waits a moment for the map to say where it is looking, and read as looking it would make a Google map only to take it down.
- **A still is the Leaflet canvas's.** No caller draws one today.

A failure of Google under a painted map is kept for the page and not for the window (`paintedGooglePage.ts`). A painted map's window opens many times in a visit, and a key refused once is refused every time.

## The Google canvas

`src/components/paintedMap/PaintedMapGoogleCanvas.tsx`, lazy, on `@vis.gl/react-google-maps`.

- **Lines** are Google's own lines, stroked from `paintedGoogleShapes.ts`: a dark edge under the paint, dashes 14 on and 7 off for a line drawn by hand, dots for a stretch the phone was not sure of. A Google line has no dash of its own, so a dash is one small drawing repeated along a line that is not drawn.
- **Places and the person's dot** are HTML held on their spot by an overlay, built by the same `placeMarkHtml` the Leaflet canvas uses. A press on a place reaches the caller and not the map.
- **The view** comes from `paintedMapView.ts`: the closest zoom that holds everything with 40 px of room, never past zoom 19, centred on the flat map. It is also what one canvas hands the other.
- **Its doors to Leaflet** are the pins canvas's own (`useGoogleCanvasDoors`, v2.7981).

## The Leaflet canvas

`PaintedMapCanvas` still holds the two presses, Time and its row. It now mounts one map or the other inside its box, and a caller says where it is and what it is for with one new prop, `google`. A caller that hands none gets the Leaflet map exactly as before, which is what `/way` does today.

What changed inside it is small. It remembers the last view either map was on. A Leaflet map that comes up in place of Google's opens on that view and skips its first framing. `FlyTo` does not fly a second time to a place the map was already taken to. Nothing in the drawing layers, the held press, the catch or the magnifying glass was touched.

The Share sheet says when a picture comes onto the screen (`onPictureShown`), before the picture is painted and not after.

## Is this the best we can do

Things that changed while building, each for a reason found on the screen or in Google's pages.

- **The window does not go back to Google after a save.** The card said *mounted when nothing is being drawn*. Built that way, a painter saw the photograph change under them at each save, and the line they had just drawn sat a few feet off on the other photograph. The window now keeps the photograph the line was drawn over.
- **One Google map for the whole visit.** Google bills by the map made. The library can put a map away and bring the same one back (`reuseMaps`), and the canvas asks for that. Measured below: coming back to Google made no request to Google at all.
- **The view is worked out here and not by Google's fit.** Google's fit has no cap, so a line a few feet long opened at the deepest zoom, past where the photograph is sharp.
- **The marks are not Google's markers.** Google's newer marker draws HTML only on a map with a Map ID, a setting in Google's console that the app does not have. Its older marker draws a picture and not HTML. An overlay draws the very marks the Leaflet canvas draws, with no new setting.
- **A second boundary sits outside the lazy file.** The boundary inside the canvas cannot catch its own file failing to arrive on a weak signal. The Leaflet canvas is already loaded by then, so the screen falls back to it.
- **Not built: a note when Google's photograph does not load.** The Leaflet canvas says *The picture did not load* from its tiles' own errors. Google's map gives no such sign the canvas can read.
- **Not changed: Google's own place names.** Its photograph carries the names and signs of shops and rooms. They cannot be pressed, and the road map carries them too.

## Checked in a browser

Headless Chrome 157 on a scratch page that mounts the real canvas over a made-up map on public ground, against Google's own script in its 3.67 build. The key is a deploy secret, so each case stands in for it.

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| The app built with no key | The Leaflet canvas draws: 18 of Esri's tiles, the lines and the four marks. **Streets** and a press on a place work. Not one request went to Google. |
| The app built with a made-up key | The rule answered Google at 76 ms. Google's canvas was up with `hybrid` at 96 ms, with no Leaflet map on the screen. Google painted at 1.3 s and refused the key at 1.9 s (`InvalidKeyMapError`). The screen fell back with its lines and marks. **Streets**, a press on a place and two corners drawn by hand all worked. |
| The same page, the window closed and opened again | Leaflet at once. Google was not tried a second time. |
| Google's canvas alone, loaded with no key at all | Google's own tiles, watermarked. The lines, the dashes, the dots and the marks sat on their spots on the photograph and on the road map. A press on a place, a fly and a refit worked. |
| That canvas taken down and brought back | The same map: 70 requests to Google before and 70 after. |
| The whole path, with Google kept up by taking the key off the script's address | Reading on Google. **Time** brought the archive up on Leaflet with Google off the screen. Leaving Time brought Google back with no new request to Google. **Draw a line** brought Leaflet up on the same spot, the places 0 px from where they were, and a press added a corner. Back to reading, the window stayed on Leaflet. A picture took Google down and it stayed down. |
| Two Google maps on one screen, a pins map and a painted map, and a made-up key | Both painted at 1.3 s. Google refused the key at 1.8 s and both fell back, each saying why once. This is v2.7981's mend, seen on Google's own script. |

At no point were Google's map and the Leaflet map on the screen together.

One number to read with care: on the way into Time the places moved 28 px in the box. That is the Time row taking its room from the map, as it did before this change, and not the view.
