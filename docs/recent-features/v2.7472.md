# v2.7472 — Liens: print the copies a filed lien owes, with a cover letter (2026-11-16)

Punch list #366, step 1 of 4. The owner asked on 2026-11-16 whether the Lien desk makes it easy, after a lien is filed, to draft the letter that goes to the customer by certified mail. It did not: *Record service…* recorded the mailing and nothing drew what goes in the envelope. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What a person did by hand

Between *Record filing…* and *Record service…* the office photocopied the stamped affidavit once per reader, wrote a cover letter in Word or sent none, looked up the owner's mailing address on the property record and the GC's on its customer record, and addressed each envelope. Tex. Prop. Code § 53.055 gives five days for it.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/jobsDocuments/lienServedCopy.ts`** (new, pure): the cover letter (`buildLienServeLetterBlocks`, its three paragraphs in `lienServeLetterParagraphs`), the copy (`buildLienServedCopyBlocks`: the affidavit as `buildLienAffidavitBlocks` draws it from the filing's fields, with a box that says COPY, the county, the day filed and the document number, and that the page shows no signature, seal or stamp), who the envelopes go to (`lienServeRecipients`), what holds a filing (`lienServeHolds`) and the packet as one print document (`lienServePacketHtml`). On a homestead the § 53.254(f) notice stays first on the copy's page and the box follows it.
- **`src/lib/jobs/lienServeFacts.ts`** (new, pure): a `job_lien_filings` row as those builders read it. The copy is the paper as filed, so it reads the row's stored `fields`, never the job as it stands today. The envelope's name and address are today's (the property record's owner, the GC's customer record), with the paper's own when the office holds none.
- **`LienServeCopiesPress.tsx`** (new): **Print the copies to mail**, beside **Record service…** in the Lien window, in front of the gate list and behind it. A tick box leaves the letter out; the copy always prints. The print is filed on the job with `printAndFile` as kind `lien_affidavit_copy`, against the filing's row.
- **A hold has a door.** A filing with no recording number is held (the owner's pick), and the number is typed under the press: until now a filing recorded without one had no place to take it. A missing address names its record. A filing recorded with *Already filed — record it…* while the app's own page had blanks is not the paper that was filed, so the app draws no copy and says to mail a photocopy.

## Words of law

The letter says only what is true of the filing: the county, the day, the document number, the amount, and how to pay and get a release. It makes no threat and gives no advice, and a test holds it to that. It says *affidavit claiming a lien* and never *a lien*, so one letter is true on a homestead too. It never stands in for the copy.

Counsel has read neither the letter nor the question of whether a copy drawn from the record counts as the copy. `LIEN_SERVE_LETTER_READ_BY_COUNSEL` and `LIEN_SERVED_COPY_READ_BY_COUNSEL` are false, each draws an amber line under the press, and the guides say so under **Counsel has not read this.** The five questions for counsel are on the card, `to-dos/lien-serve-filed-copies/`.
