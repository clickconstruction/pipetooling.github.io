# v2.5271 — Messages: the rules for editing a flow in Settings (2026-10-12)

Messages, cut 4: the rules behind Settings → Flows, lifted from the prototype v1.178 (branch `spike/gc-mode`, `to-dos/gc-mode/messages/prototype.html`: `editBegin`, `flowSave`, `flowPutBack`, the step setters, `moveStep`, `addStep`, `removeStep`, `restoreStep`, the History tab and Before you save · who moves). On cluster 4's types and `readFlow` (v2.5254, v2.5260). Kernels only: no screen, no table, no migration, no edge function.

- **`src/lib/flows/editor.ts`**: a draft of a flow from the seed or its `flow_steps` rows (`draftFrom`, `flowFromRows`, `flowRows`); `renameStep` with its Spanish name; `moveStep` up or down inside its lane; `moveToLane` into another lane at a place in it (the owner's afternoon of Oct 2 moved the ask, the bid, the yes and the contract from Once to Per job, and change orders from Any time); `addStep` with its who, seat, facts, papers, join, loop, nudge, usual and Later words, and a `step_key` made from its name (`stepKeyFor`); `removeStep` into Removed and `restoreStep`; `setWho`, `setSeat`, `setNudge`, `setLoop`, `setJoin`, `addFact`, `removeFact`, `addPaper`, `removePaper`, `setUsual`, `setLater`; `putBack`; `saveDraft`, which returns the flow's rows, the removed list, the steps put back and one History line per step that changed (`flow_step_history`: the step as it was and as it became, who and when); `historyLines` and `removedLines` as the tab draws them; `drawnEntry`, the first line of a flow drawn from the seed; `whoMovesPreview`, Before you save · who moves, read with cluster 4's `whoMoves` over a list of people; `savedWords`.
- **Nothing is state**: every edit returns a new draft or a refusal in words, and the draft an edit started from is left as it was. The screen holds the draft while Settings → Flows is open; cut 4's SQL holds what is saved.
- **Refusals**: a step name that fails the plain-words rules, an empty one, one another step has; a fact not in `facts.ts` or a paper not on the list (`isFactId`, `isPaperId`); a fact or paper picked twice; a move past the end of its lane, or to the lane it is in; a step that is not there, or not in Removed.
- **Changed from the prototype**: a step's nudge changes say in one line from the step as saved, as the prototype's own History line does ("Papers in: the nudge from 3 days to 5, a call"), where the prototype's setter wrote "nudge days → 5" per field; whose move reads ours, theirs or both sides instead of the raw `us`; Save writes one History line per step (the SQL's `step_key` and before and after), the step's changes joined, where the prototype wrote one line per save; a seat is a user id with the name in the words; a name another step has is refused.
- **Tests** over the seed and the demo people: the prototype's five History tab lines word for word (the customer flow drawn with ten steps, before change orders came), every edit's words, each refusal, Put it back, the rows read back as written, who moves when Papers in is removed (Tom, from Papers in to MSA signed; Greg the same), and the owner's afternoon replayed with `moveToLane` into the customer flow as it stands, one History line per step.
