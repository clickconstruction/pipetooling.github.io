## The reader (`src/lib/materials/manifest.ts`)

Shown by running the reader on lines of the shapes supply houses print:

| The line | Before | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `1,000 EA 1/2" PEX pipe 0.42 420.00` | dropped, the comma | 1,000 EA, 0.42 each |
| `2 1/2" x 10 PVC pipe` | 2 of `1/2" x 10 PVC pipe` | not read, and listed |
| `1 1/2" PVC 90 25 EA 4.70 117.50` | 1 of `1/2" PVC 90 25 EA` | 25 EA of `1 1/2" PVC 90` |
| `10 EA 2" PVC 90 ST` | dropped as a street address | 10 EA |
| `25 EA 3/4 COPPER 90 ELBOW 117.50` | 117.50 each | no price until the office says what the figure is |
| `25 EA elbow 117.50 4.70` | 117.50 each | 4.70 each |

- **A count** may carry a comma. With no unit, a number past 999 is still a street number.
- **A mixed size**: with no unit and no `×`, a 1, 2 or 3 right before a fraction is not taken for the count. The line then reads name first when it ends in a count and a unit, and is not read otherwise.
- **An address** is a number, words with no digit among them and a street suffix. A part with a size in it is never one.
- **The price**: of two figures, the one that makes the other when multiplied by the count is the unit price. One figure on a line of one piece is its price. One figure on a line of more is kept as `loneFigure` and read as neither. `linePrice(line, 'each' | 'total')` is the office's answer applied. A `0.00` is no figure.
- **`readInvoiceText`** returns the lines and `notRead`: the pasted lines that hold a number and a word and did not read. Headers, totals, addresses and blanks are not listed. `parseInvoiceText` is its `lines`.

## The panel (`JobsMaterialsTab.tsx`)

- **What goes is what the button counts.** `landableLines`: a count above zero and a name, trimmed. The press sent every line on the table, a line typed down to 0 and a line with its name cleared among them. The button's noun now follows its own count.
- **One press, one answer.** `addDelivery` (`onSiteStore.ts`) sends every line as one on-site press, so they land, wait or are refused together. The panel said *landed* before any answer, and each line that failed then said so on its own. It now waits and says one sentence (`deliveryWords.ts`): the lines landed, or they are kept on this device. A refusal is the store's sentence with the reason, and the lines stay on the table.
- **A line with no count or no name stays** after the press, with the invoice's number, to finish or leave out.
- **Not read** lines sit in an amber box above the table, each with **Add it as a line**.
- **One figure**: when any line carries one, the panel asks once, **The price of each** or **The line’s total**, and the Price column follows. Unsaid, those lines land with no price.
- **The same invoice twice**: `landedFromInvoice` finds the job's Delivered rows with that invoice as their source. The panel names it above the button, and the press opens a window whose button reads *Add 6 lines again*.

Client only. No row's shape changes: a delivery is the same `delivered` rows with the invoice as `source`.
