# v2.7235 — Subs pay: one check across several sheets, and the extra kept as a credit (2026-11-13)

Punch list #347. The Record payment window refused an amount past what a sheet owed, and asked the owner what an overpayment to a sub should become. His answer, 2026-11-13: "overpayment -> credit". Card #350 had found the other half of the same shape: one check that pays several sheets was typed once per sheet, with nothing saying it was one check.

## What changes

- **The window takes the whole check.** Up to what the sheet owes it is one payment, as before. Past that, the extra fills the sub's other open sheets, oldest first, and what is still left is kept as their credit. The window lists every line before the press, and a tick switches the fill off so all of the extra is credit.
- **A credit is offered first.** A sub who holds one gets *Use $… on this sheet* at the top of the window. It pays the sheet with a payment whose method is *From credit*. No money leaves.
- **A sub holds a credit only as one person on the roster.** For anyone else an amount past what their sheets need is refused, with the reason and what fits.
- **Every new payment is a numbered sending.** Lines that were one check share the number. A number is never reused.
- **Where it shows:** the Pay row's payment (*sending 12*, *credit $… kept*), the Ledger by date (a green note under the amount, a *From credit* chip, the sending's number, and CSV columns for both), the Ledger by sub, a statement, and under Balance due on People → Subs.
- **Paid means sent.** The Ledger's Paid totals count what left the bank: a payment and the credit kept with it. A payment made from a credit is in no Paid total.
- **A statement:** *Credit kept* takes away from what we owe. *Credit used* changes nothing, since the sheet owes less and so does their credit. The foot says what credit they hold.
- **Edit** on a payment made from a credit keeps its amount and shows no method chips.

## How it is held together

Migration `20261114020000_sub_credit_and_sendings.sql` (see its note in `docs/migrations/`):

- `sub_payment_sendings` holds the number and no amount, so an edit to a row can never leave a stored total out of step.
- `sub_credit_entries` is a ledger: kept above zero, used below. A person's credit is the sum.
- `record_sub_sending` and `apply_sub_credit` each write their rows in one statement.
- A payment that carries a credit stays on its sheet (the move function and a trigger both refuse). One made from a credit keeps its amount. Removing a use puts the credit back. Removing a payment whose credit was already used is refused. An undo restores the row with its sending and its credit.

## Code

- `src/lib/subWorkOrders/subSending.ts`, new, pure: `sendingContext` (the sub's other open sheets oldest first, their credit), `planSubSending` (the lines and the credit, which always add up to what was sent) and the words. `sendingSheets.ts` reads the page's sheets into it by the Pay view's own rule for whose a sheet is.
- `SubLaborPaymentModals`: Record payment plans a sending when the page hands it the two writes. A caller that does not gets the old gate.
- `useSubLaborLedger`: the payments read asks for `sending_id` and falls back twice; reads of `sub_payment_sendings` and `sub_credit_entries` fail soft; `recordSubSending`, `applySubCredit` and `subCreditByPersonId` are new. Each payment carries `sending_no` and `credit_kept`.
- `subPaymentsLedger.ts`: a line gains `fromCredit`, `creditKept`, `sendingNo` and `sent`; `ledgerBySub` and `buildSubStatement` gain the credit.

## Order of deploy

The client calls two functions and reads two tables that only the migration creates, so this is a *push right after the merge* PR. Until the push: every payment still loads, a payment that stays on its sheet is recorded by the plain insert, and an amount past the sheet says it is not switched on yet.

## Checked

- The migration on a scratch Postgres 15: applies twice, and a scenario plays a sending, a credit, its use and every guard.
- Kernel tests for the planner (13) and the ledger's credit cases, among them that the lines and the credit always add up to what was sent.
- Render tests for the window: one line, the fill, the fill switched off, the credit offered, the fallback before the push, a refusal shown, and Edit on a from-credit payment.
- Walked on the dev build against the live database, which does not have the migration yet, read only: the payments read falls back and every sheet draws, the credit read fails soft, and the window on the test sub's sheet lists the sheet and the credit for an amount past it. Record payment was not pressed.

## Not in this PR

- Placing a sending's lines by hand. The fill is oldest first, on or off.
- Turning a sheet that is already paid past its total into a credit.
- The dev seat's `plan_sub_labor_payment` still records one row with no sending.
