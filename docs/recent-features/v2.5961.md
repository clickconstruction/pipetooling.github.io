## The words: one kernel per page family

Each helper takes the surface's own handler, so the words and the press travel together and the component only wires them. Each test fires every press and holds every sentence to plain words (`plainWordsFailures`).

- `src/lib/dashboard/dashboardEmptyWords.ts`: the Dashboard's cards, sections and windows, and the Dispatch, Estimator and Help feedback inboxes. `NEEDS_YOU_EMPTY` keeps the Needs you card's model words, *Nothing waits on you.*; the queues the viewer clears use the kernel's waiting kind, *Nothing waiting on you.*
- `src/lib/checklistEmptyWords.ts`: Today, Upcoming, History, Outstanding, Manage, Review and the roadmap.
- `src/lib/customers/customersEmptyWords.ts`: the customer list, a customer's bids window, the Customer Hub and customer notes. `NEW_JOB_PATH` is the Jobs page's `?newJob=true` deep link.
- `src/lib/estimates/estimatesEmptyWords.ts`: the list, the Pipeline, the Ledger, the three Follow-up lanes and the line-item book. `NEW_ESTIMATE_PATH` is the page's `?newEstimate=true` deep link.
- `src/lib/documents/documentsEmptyWords.ts`: the four ledgers, alone or under the Search tab.
- `src/lib/partnerLedger/partnershipsEmptyWords.ts`, `src/lib/projects/projectsEmptyWords.ts`, `src/lib/calendarEmptyWords.ts` and `src/lib/materials/duplicatePartsEmptyWords.ts`: the smaller pages.

## Wiring the presses

- New optional props carry a handler to the list that is empty: `DashboardArCustomersView.onClearSearch` (the Financials drill window's own search), `PartnershipLedgerTab.onOpenStatements` (the page's Statements tab) and the Documents ledgers' `onClearEmbedSearch` (the Search tab's box). `ChecklistHistoryLedger` takes `empty`, so the phone ledger and the desktop grid say the same words with the same press.
- `EstimateLineItemCatalogModal` lifts its Edit book tab's handler into `openEditBook`, shared by the tab and the empty book's press.
- The three inboxes' folded one-line state read *— empty*. It now reads the waiting words: *Nothing waiting on dispatch.*, *Nothing waiting on an estimator.*, *Nothing waiting on you.*
- One behaviour change: Projects' empty list offers New Project only to the roles the page gives New Project (dev, master, assistant and controller). Before, *Add one* showed to every role. The roadmap tab's Create first roadmap button is now the empty state's press, with the same handler.

## Is this the best we can do?

Three presses are links where a prefilled window would be better. The Customer Hub's New estimate and New Job, and the bids window's New Bid, open the page's own form through its deep link. Those forms take a customer prefill only from a bid or a lead today, so the person picks the customer again. The Documents ledgers link to the page that fills them for the same reason. Generate statement is named, not pressed, because it writes money from the week and the offsets set above it. The area now carries two verb forms for the good state: Needs you's model *Nothing waits on you.* and the kernel's *Nothing waiting on you.* Which one wins is the owner's call. Three sentences still need the owner's words. *Create one above.* for a reports-only person points at a card the section cannot name. AR's *Nothing waiting on a customer.* drops *Every bill is paid.* while Collections holds parked bills. A closed-only Dispatch list says *Nothing closed yet.*, and no screen draws that list today.
