# v2.7506 — Sub papers: the function that emails a sub their papers, alone (sub labor train) (2026-11-16)

Punch list #350, step 5 of the six under the card's *Where it stands*, its first half: the edge function `send-sub-paper-email`, with no press. **It is not deployed, and it is deployed only on Taunya's go that names it.** Nothing in the app calls it until the Send press lands in the papers window.

## What landed

- `supabase/functions/send-sub-paper-email/index.ts`, cut from the design spike's snapshot (`claude/sub-labor-spike-82d6a9` at `7616bc399`) and fitted to `main`. It mails one message with a Work statement, a Payment record, a Statement of account, or several, PDFs attached. [`EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`](../EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md#send-sub-paper-email) has its section.
- `supabase/config.toml`: its entry, `verify_jwt = false`, with the JWT read in the function as the other senders do.
- `src/lib/emailCatalog.ts`: the row `sub_paper`, so the email shows in Settings with the rest and its sends are counted by type.
- **Settings → What customers see**: a card *Sub papers, emailed* on the subcontractor's journey (`customerJourneys.ts`, `customerSurfaceRegistry.ts`), drawn as a sample statement of account from the papers' own kernel on made-up sheets (`paperSamples.ts`, paper `sub-statement-of-account`). On a real sub's strip the step reads where its copies are kept (`personJourney.ts`).

## What the function holds

- **Who.** The office roles who see sub pay. An account in training and an archived account are refused.
- **Where it goes.** The roster's own `people.email` for the person, read under the caller's policies. No address in the body is read, so a paper about one sub cannot be mailed to another address.
- **What.** 1 to 12 papers of the three kinds, each PDF and the whole message under a size cap. A file name loses any path and ends in `.pdf`.
- **A texted link is refused.** It is not built, and it is never sent as an email.

## What differs from the snapshot

- It sends as the company (`COMPANY_EMAIL_FROM`), as every paper that leaves the office does. The snapshot sent as the app.
- **Each paper is kept as a sent copy of its own**, under its own kind, on the person and its jobs. The snapshot filed one row for the whole message under the first paper's kind.
- The person id and each job id must be an id, and the subject and the message are cut to a length.

## Tests

`src/lib/subWorkOrders/sendSubPaperEmail.run.test.ts` runs the function's own handler under vitest with a recorded `fetch` (9 cases): no login, an estimator, a training account and an archived account refused with nothing sent; each refusal's reason; the roster's address used whatever the body names; the office's words escaped; one sent copy per paper; a file name with a path; and a refused email filing nothing. The sender, catalog, journey and bundle tests pass with it.

## Not in this change

The Send press (the next PR) and the deploy. A test paper goes to a test inbox for the test sub on its ZZ Test job, never to a real sub.
