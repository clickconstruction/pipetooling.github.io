# v2.5543 — GC projects: each stage opens on a band like the Jobs pipeline (2026-10-14)

The owner's ask of 2026-10-14: "In jobs pipeline, it is super easy to distinguish where stages stop and end, but in GC projects it's much more difficult." On the Project Board the stage heading (`GcStageHeading`) was a card: `var(--surface)`, a 1 px border, 8 px corners and the same padding as the project rows under it, so a heading and a row were the same kind of object and the eye found no boundary. The Jobs pipeline reads because its band is a different kind of thing from its contents: flat, tinted with the stage's colour, square, with more room above it than under it. Designed on a canvas (https://claude.ai/artifact/Jf7WpMF6pGMYVjWCT6RnD8: the board as it was, the band, and three other ways weighed — a rail down the rows, a plain heading, one box per stage with flat rows) and built from the band the owner chose. No migration, no edge function.

- **`GcBoardStages.tsx`** `GcStageHeading`: the heading is the pipeline's own band — `className="stagesSectionBand"` with `--stage-color` set to the stage's bar colour (`bandColor`), so the class draws the 4 px bar and the `color-mix(… 9%, var(--surface))` tint in both themes; the card's border, corners, background and padding are gone. The title is `.stagesBandTitle`, the count rides in `.stagesBandCount` (the pipeline's pill, the stage's `fg` on it) in place of *(1)*, the worth and the blurb stay. `marginTop: 0.5rem` over the board's 1 rem gap and under the section's 0.5 rem gap: 1.5 rem above a band, 0.5 rem below, the pipeline's rhythm. `data-gc-stage-band=<section>` for the smoke. `GcCustomerHeading` (By customer) takes the same band in blue with the count in its pill (`data-gc-customer-band`). `GcStageSubheading` (the small heading per stage inside a customer's section) is unchanged.
- **Tests**: `GcBoard.render.test.tsx` (+2: the band's class, colour variable, title and pill, blurb, no border or corners, the spacing, five bands; By customer's blue band).
- **Guide** *see where every GC project stands*: the stage heading example shows the count pill.

Left alone: the strip at the top of the board (its pills were already the stage colours); the Each project section under the board.
