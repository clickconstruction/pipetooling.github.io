# v2.4456 — Customer portal: a bill, a notice and a waiver read their own day (2026-10-02)

One of the instant-day fixes that follow [v2.4452](v2.4452.md). No migration. Four `_shared` modules change, all bundled only by `customer-portal`, which is redeployed after merge.

## What was wrong

Four builders behind the customer portal's payload cut a day out of a `timestamptz` by its first ten characters, so anything stamped after 7 pm CDT (6 pm CST) read as the next day:

- [`portalMergedBills.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/portalMergedBills.ts): `billedOn` from `jobs_ledger_invoices.billed_at`, twice: the bills the viewer owes (`buildPortalBills`) and a bill shared with the other party (`buildPortalSharedBills`). The page prints it as *Billed Oct 3* and counts days since billed from it.
- [`portalChecks.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/portalChecks.ts): `invoices[].billed_at` in *Your payments*, which the page's kernel orders bills by.
- [`portalPropertyNotices.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/portalPropertyNotices.ts): a notice's `mailedOn` when the owner send carries no date, from `job_lien_filings.created_at`.
- [`portalWaivers.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/portalWaivers.ts): a waiver half's day from `job_lien_releases.sent_to_customer_at` or `signed_at`, and the bill's `billedYmd`, through the file's `ymd` helper. A grep for `_at.slice` misses these because the slice sits in the helper; a sweep of everything `customer-portal` bundles found them.

## What changed

Each reads `todayYmdInAppTz(new Date(iso))` from [`_shared/appTimeZone.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/appTimeZone.ts). `portalWaivers.ts`'s `ymd` keeps its name and returns null for a missing or unreadable value, as before.

Left alone, because they are calendar days already: `paid_on` and `sent_on` (`date` columns), and a notice's `sends[].sent_on`, which the Lien desk writes as a `YYYY-MM-DD`. [`stagePlan.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/stagePlan.ts) already read `billed_at` in the company's zone.

Every writer of `billed_at` lands on the right Central day: the status trigger stamps `now()`, a day the office types is stamped 18:00 UTC (`billedAtIsoFromYmd`), and `create_billed_shell_invoice` stamps noon Chicago. `customer-portal` reads `billed_at` straight from `jobs_ledger_invoices`, and it is the only caller of these builders, so no caller hands them a bare day.

## Tests

- `portalMergedBills.test.ts` (+2), `portalChecks.test.ts` (+1), `portalPropertyNotices.test.ts` (+1), `portalWaivers.test.ts` (+1): `2026-10-03T00:30:00Z` (7:30 pm CDT), in both `Z` and PostgREST's `+00:00` shapes, reads Oct 2; `2026-12-02T00:30:00Z` (6:30 pm CST) reads Dec 1; noon UTC reads its own day. All five fail on main's kernels.
- The fixtures in `portalMergedBills.test.ts` and `portalBillMembership.test.ts` passed `billed_at` as a bare `YYYY-MM-DD`, which no caller does: read as an instant, that is midnight UTC, the evening before in Central. They now stamp the day at 18:00 UTC, as the office's own entry does, and keep their expectations.

## Checked against prod

Read only, from the worktree dev server: 16 of the 530 billed or paid bills were marked billed in a Central evening. None of them is open today, so no open bill on a portal reads a day late right now; a paid one with a waiver would show its day on *Your papers*. One lien waiver is signed, in daytime.
