# v2.4395 — Takeoffs: see what your materials cost at today’s book, and catch up (2026-10-02)

The last of the four material-prices PRs (canvas *Material prices over time*). It is the plan's step 3, "on every bid", and shipped after v2.4389 retired By Stage, so every bid prices its materials from part lines. Grace's fifth call: one Refresh prices button on an unsent bid, and a sent bid shows the gap only until you press Revise.

## What changed
- **The kernel** [`lib/bids/takeoffPriceDrift.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/takeoffPriceDrift.ts) compares each part line's price as picked with today's price of the book row it came from (`source_material_part_price_id`):
  - A moved price counts toward the gap: (today − priced) × quantity × fixture count.
  - A book price that is now a big jump from the line's, or a stand-in, is left out and grouped as *looks wrong*. `isBigJump` is the same rule What your materials cost uses.
  - A typed price (no source row) or a row the reader cannot see is left alone, but counts in the materials the share is taken of.
  - `refresh` is each moved line at today's price from its own row, never a cheaper house: that is Reset to catalog's job.
- **The reads** [`takeoffPriceDriftIo.ts`](../../src/lib/bids/takeoffPriceDriftIo.ts): `loadBookPrices` (id-chunked and paged, with each row's house and part name) and `loadTakeoffDriftLines` (one version's lines, or an unsplit bid's base).
- **Takeoffs** draws [`TakeoffPriceDriftLine`](../../src/components/bids/TakeoffPriceDrift.tsx) above the Combined views:
  - from the tab's live lines with today's book prices (`useBookPrices`)
  - *N prices on this takeoff moved in the book. At today’s book these materials cost $X less (▼ Y%).*
  - *See the lines* opens a table.
  - **Refresh prices** asks first, then writes each moved line through `updateTakeoffRoughPartLine`. Prices you typed stay.
  
  A sent bid (`pricingLockState` with the session's revised set) gets *Sent Jul 7. Press Revise on Pricing to refresh them.* and no button. An amber note names a price that looks wrong, with *Fix it in Materials*.
- **Pricing** shows the same gap in one line under *Sent … at $X* (`useTakeoffPriceDrift` + `PricingMaterialsTodayNote`), with *See Takeoffs*.
- **New guide:** *refresh a takeoff's prices to today's book*. The glossary's three price truths now say when a takeoff price moves. The Takeoffs and Pricing maps gain their rows.

## Not in the mock-up's words
The mock-up said *Materials priced Jul 23*. A part line keeps when it was made, but not when its price last changed, because `updated_at` is never maintained, and a refresh keeps the row. So the line says how many prices moved, never when.
