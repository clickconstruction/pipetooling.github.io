# v2.7168 — Subs pay: the three dots sit in the middle of their button (2026-11-13)

The owner's screenshot of **Jobs → Subs → Pay** boxed the row's **More** button: its three dots hugged the bottom right corner.

## The cause

The button is 30 × 26 px and set no padding of its own, so it took the default `button` rule's `0.4rem 0.85rem` (`src/index.css`). 13.6 px a side in a 30 px border-box leaves a content box 0.8 px wide. The typed `⋯` (U+22EF, about 15 px wide) cannot fit a line that narrow, so `text-align: center` gives up and the glyph starts at the content box's left edge and runs out to the right; the 16 px line in an 11 px-high content box runs out of the bottom the same way.

Measured on the deployed page at 1400 × 900 (headless Chromium, the dots' own pixels against the button's box): the dots' centre sat **7.4 px right** and **2.3–3.3 px low** of the button's centre, on every row (67, 83 and 92 px tall), light and dark.

## What changed

- `JobsSubLaborTab` (the Pay view's desk table): the button is `inline-flex`, centred both ways, `padding: 0`, and draws lucide's `MoreHorizontal` (18 px, stroke 2.25), the icon the Pipeline's rows use in `StandingMoveCell`. An icon's dots sit in the middle of its own box on every platform; a typed glyph's height depends on which font the system falls back to. Size (30 × 26), border, colours, `aria-label="More"`, the title and the menu are as they were.
- `.jobPaymentLineMore` (the ⋯ beside a payment on a job's Bill tab, `JobFormPaymentLine`): 28 × 26 with the same inherited padding and the same fault (7.3 px right, 1.4 px low by the text box). It gets `display: inline-flex`, centred, `padding: 0`. It keeps its typed glyph: this is a CSS-only change.

After, same measure: **0 px across, 0 px down** on all seven rows of All due and the first rows of Paid, light and dark (the icon's box against the button's box in the page reads 0 / 0 on every row). On a phone the Pay view draws rows, not the table, and has no More button, so there was nothing to fix there.

## The other ⋯ buttons

About twenty other places type a `⋯` or `⋮` into a button. Read one by one, each sets its own padding or centres with flex, and the three closest in shape were measured (`.jobInvoiceMore`, the Checklist row's `⋮`, and `.jobPaymentLineMore` before its fix): only the last was off. A grep for one-line buttons with a fixed width of 16–40 px and no padding or flex of their own finds none left. No shared button is worth a card for this alone: the fault was two buttons that forgot the default padding, not a pattern.

## Tests

`JobsSubLaborTab.render.test.tsx` gains a desk case: the More button keeps its name, holds no typed characters, draws an icon, is `inline-flex` centred with no padding at 30 × 26, and its press opens the menu.
