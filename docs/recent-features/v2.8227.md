# v2.8227 — A box's More sheet: the × is as big as the other presses (punch list #398, the walk of 2026-11-21) (2026-11-21)

The owner's main session walked a box's screen on a 375 px browser on 2026-11-21, looking only, on the ZZ test job's empty box. Every press met the card's bar of 48 px but one: the × on the More sheet. Client only: no migration and no function. Card: [`to-dos/box-screen/README.md`](../../to-dos/box-screen/README.md).

## What changed

The × is the On site tab's own sheet's (`Sheet` in `src/components/tally/OnSiteSheet.tsx`), so every sheet on the tab gains it, not More alone. It is 48 by 48 (`SHEET_CLOSE_STYLE`, over `SHEET_TOUCH_MIN`). The margins give back what it grew by, 5 px over and under and 4.5 at the right, so the title row and the × are drawn where they were.

Read on the dev server at 375 × 812 and 320 × 640, on the test job's empty box: the × is 48 × 48 (was 39 × 38), the title row 38 px (was 37.8) and the whole sheet 509.6 px (was 509.4). No page slides sideways at either width.

## Read and left as they are

- **An empty box still draws the sticky *Take from …* press.** The press shows on every box but Unsorted and a box with a run left open (`TallyOnSiteTab.tsx`); what is in the box is not asked. Hiding it or greying it on an empty box changes what the screen does, so it is a question for the owner, on the card.
- **The More sheet read *Say it better* where the card's design says *Say what is in it*.** Both are right. The row is `sayItBetterPress` (`src/lib/materials/boxLine.ts`): *Say it better* over a box that has a line, the app's or a person's, and *Say what is in it* on a box with none yet. The empty test box reads *Say what is in it*; a box with a part reads *Say it better*. The card's sentence now says both.
