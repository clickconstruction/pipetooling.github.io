# v2.4978 — Lien desk: take back a printed run (2026-10-08)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. PR 1 of 3 for punch list #101 ([`to-dos/lien-printed-run/`](../../to-dos/lien-printed-run/README.md)), built on the owner's yes of 2026-10-08.

## Why

The office printed a run of 19 notices, then the packet's layout changed (v2.4971) before anything was mailed. Printing stamps each notice `printed_at`, the desk reads it as *In the mail · tracking owed*, and the run window opens on step 3. The only ways back were *Print it again* (its hint says lost copy only) or *Back to ready* on each notice, nineteen times.

## What changed

- **Kernel** [`lienRunTakeBack.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienRunTakeBack.ts) (pure, 8 tests): `runTakenBackOf` / `fieldsWithTakeBack` (the line kept on the item's `fields.runTakenBack`: `at`, `by`, `byName`, `printedAt`; the latest only), `canTakeBackItem` (printed, not sent, missed or voided), `runPrintedItemIds` (printed before this sitting or in it), `runTypedTrackingCount`, `runNoticesTakenBack`, and the words: `runTakeBackConfirm`, `runTakenBackWords`, `takenBackChipWords`.
- **The write** `takeBackLienDeskItems(itemIds, { userId, userName })` in [`lienDeskIo.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDeskIo.ts): reads the rows, keeps the ones `canTakeBackItem` allows, and clears `printed_at` / `printed_by` with the line in `fields`, guarded by `printed_at is not null` and `status <> 'sent'`. `clearLienDeskItemPrinted` (Back to ready, v2.4568) now goes through it, so one notice taken back leaves the same line.
- **The run window** ([`LienDeskRunModal`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskRunModal.tsx)): a new `onTakeBack` prop. While any notice in the run has printed, step 1 carries **Take back…** (`run-take-back`). It opens an inline confirm (`run-take-back-confirm`): who goes back, that approvals stand, that the filed copies stay, the typed numbers it drops, and, on a mixed run, that the unprinted ones stay. **Take back N notices** hands the ids over; the window clears their printed day and typed numbers, returns to step 1 and says so (`run-taken-back`). A failure toasts and leaves the run as it was.
- **Both hosts** pass `onTakeBack`: the Lien desk and Put a GC on notice, which shares the window.
- **The ready footer's chip** (`readyChip`, [`lienFootChip.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienFootChip.ts)) takes the line: *Approved · Oct 6 · taken back Oct 8*, the hover *Printed Oct 7, taken back Oct 8 by …. Nothing was mailed.*
- Guide [`send-lien-notices-from-the-lien-desk`](../../src/content/help/send-lien-notices-from-the-lien-desk.md): **Take back a run**.

## Decisions

- The office takes back without the leader (the owner, 2026-10-08): nothing left the building, and the approvals stand.
- Whole run, not one envelope: an envelope can hold several notices and a notice goes in two envelopes. One job keeps *Back to ready*.
- The copies printed stay in Documents: `sent_documents` is append-only, and they did print.
