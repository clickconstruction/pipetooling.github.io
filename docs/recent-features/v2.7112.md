# v2.7112 — The Bridge: a check on every line hands that one thing to one person as a to-do (punch list #334, PR 9) (2026-11-12)

PR 9 of the Bridge train (card #334, `to-dos/bridge-pulse/`), on PR 8 (v2.7109). Client only. No migration, no edge function. One kernel that is copied to the edge functions lost exports nothing there reads (`callPeople.ts`), so the copy was regenerated and no function is behind for it.

The owner refused handing whole moves to people: "assign these directly to someone as a to-do card ... not be vague, but to be extremely specific". Then: "I would like to see the checkmark in light gray and when someone clicks it makes the check blue until the task is complete, so someone can click the box and see a modal of the task and who it's assigned to and when it was assigned, and once it's done the task can go back to able to be assigned." He accepted four additions: the name and age beside a blue check, amber when it has waited too long, who finished it and when kept on the line, and a customer's line counting its projects handed out.

## What changed

- **The card for one thing** (`src/lib/bridge/callTodo.ts`, new, pure): a title that says who, what and how much, one link to the thing, and *Done when…* lines, for one customer, one project's pay date, one job to bill, to price or to set a percent on, or one supply house. `todoLinkPaths` tags the first link with `?todo=<key>`, which is how the page finds the card again.
- **Where a line stands** (`callTodoState.ts`, new, pure): none, open, late, done or covered. Late is past the card's due day, or three days with none. A finish stays named on its line for 14 days. A project under a customer whose own card is open reads as covered.
- **The read** (`callTodoIo.ts`, new): the cards whose `links` overlap the page's tagged addresses, in chunks of 20, with the people on each and the day each was finished. A card's days are the company's days.
- **The check and its window** (`BridgeTodoCheck.tsx`, `BridgeTodoOpen.tsx`, new): grey, blue or amber. A blue check opens a small window with the card, who has it, since when and its lines as ticked so far. **Open the to-do** goes on to the app's own task window (`ChecklistItemEditModal`), and the checks are read again when it saves or closes.
- **The table** (`BridgeMoveTable.tsx`, `callDive.ts`): one column of checks at the right edge, a line under a row for who has it and for how long, and *N of M handed out* on a customer with projects. The card's foot reads *Press a grey check to hand that line to one person. A blue check is with someone.*
- **The Add task window takes more from a preset** (`ChecklistAddModalContext.tsx`, `ChecklistAddModal.tsx`): `doneWhen`, the lines it opens with, and `asksForPhoto`. The Bridge opens it with the tagged link at the app's public address and no photo asked. The person is picked in the window. Nothing is sent until its Send.
- **Gone**: `callPeopleIo.ts` and the list of accounts in `callPeople.ts`. The two reads here look up the few names they need.

## Not in this PR

- **The tag as its own column.** Today a person who deletes the first link from a card loses its check. PR 12 gives the tag a home on the card.
