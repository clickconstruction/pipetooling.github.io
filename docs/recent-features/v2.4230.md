Punch list #63 (`to-dos/bids-robots-usability/`), PR 1 of 6. The owner took the mock-up's five calls as the draft's picks (2026-09-30: "go with the draft's picks, build it all"). No migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

- **The open card was row 32.** `BidsAuditsTab`'s expand effect ran on the first render, before the notes, draft totals and references that `orderPendingByStake` reads had loaded — every signal read zero, so the pick was the oldest pending audit, and the effect then held it for the session.
- **19 ≠ 15.** The Standing rulings panel counted the robots' open estimator questions over the full open list; the Scoreboard's *Your part* counted `useBidRobotLayer`'s rows, loaded with `about_bid_id not null`, so the four bid-less doctrine asks fell out. The Dashboard card never counted questions at all.
- **The coaching strip's "recent runs" were the five oldest audits** (the list is oldest-first and sliced).

## What shipped

- **`pickOpenAudit`** (`src/lib/bids/auditTriage.ts`, +6 tests): nothing opens until the signals are in (`!loading && refsLoaded`); the first workable pending card opens; the pick re-runs as the signals move until the estimator taps a row or a door names one (`pickedByHand`), after which it holds; a held card that stops being workable is let go. `finishAudit` releases the pick so the next card is the queue's top as it now reads.
- **`openEstimatorQuestions`** and **`isOpenEstimatorDecision`** (`standingRulings.ts`, +3 tests): open, the estimator's lane, not a plans ask, not a pre-rule ask — the same three classifiers `groupStandingRulings` builds `openCount` from, so the panel header and the count are one number by construction. `robotQuestionsWaitingCount` delegates to it; `useBidRobotLayer` loads the full open list (the `.not('about_bid_id', 'is', null)` is gone; `openRobotQuestionsByBidId` skips a bid-less row). **`questionMinutesEstimate`**: ¾ minute a question, in fives past a handful (19 → 15).
- **`useBidAuditsPendingCount`** returns `questions` beside `pending`; **the Dashboard card** (`dashboardNeedsYou.ts`, `robotQuestionsWaiting`) leads with them — *19 robot questions (about 15 min) and 31 audits are waiting on you*, detail *Answer the questions first…*, the figure the questions — and reads as before when there are none (+1 test).
- **The coaching strip left Audits.** "51 notes → what they became" belongs beside the Scoreboard's lessons and "recent runs" is already there as *Last 5 scored runs*, correctly ordered.
- Guide *audit a robot bid* → the Dashboard step; the to-do's status.
