# v2.5916 — A voice note drafts the field report (2026-10-26)

Punch list #186, item 1, PR 2 of its plan, the owner's pick of 2026-10-26 (*I like it, build it*; then *finish PR3 and get it on main*). v2.5912 put the phone's own speech into Notes; this turns the words into a draft of the report's fields, with the first model key in the app and the door that holds it. The machine drafts, a person presses: nothing is written until **Save report**.

## The draft on the form

`ReportVoiceNote.tsx` takes a `draft` host on both report forms (`NewReportModal.tsx`, `AdditionalReportModal.tsx`, through `hooks/useReportVoiceDraft.ts`). When the recognizer's words are in, the form sends them with the job's name, its lines with the percent each stands at, and the report's text fields to `report-draft` (`lib/reports/voiceDraft.ts`'s `draftRequest`, `voiceDraftIo.ts`'s `supabaseRequestDraft`). The answer lands through `applyDraft`: Notes gets three lines, what was done, `Parts: …`, `Next: …`, under whatever it held; each line the tech named gets its pick, and the first is opened so its number is in view; a paid line is never moved. Notes wears *Drafted from your voice note. Tap to change.* in blue until typed in (`DraftedMark`), and under the fields **What you said** (**Lo que dijiste** for Spanish) holds the words and the lines it set with the words each came from (`HeardFold`, `draftedLineWords`). The button says *Drafted from 22 seconds of talk. Less than a cent. Read it, then save.* With no key (409 `noKey`) or no answer the words go into Notes as said, as in v2.5912, and the button says which. A draft that lands after the form closed is dropped.

## The function

`report-draft` (`supabase/functions/report-draft/index.ts`, pure parts in `_shared/reportDraft.ts`): any role that may leave a field report, a real account, not read-only. It asks `claude-opus-5-5` at effort `low` for one JSON object held to `DRAFT_SCHEMA` (structured output, `output_config.format` `json_schema`), with Anthropic's default fallback on a refusal (`fallbacks: "default"`, beta `server-side-fallback-2026-07-01`), through `@anthropic-ai/sdk` 1.3.0. The system prompt says only what was said, a percent only for a line clearly named, English notes, the words each percent came from. `readDraft` drops any line not in the request and clamps the numbers. The answer carries `cents` at Opus 5.5's rates. It writes nothing; the audio never reaches it.

## The key

Migration `20261026220000_model_config.sql`: `model_secret_set` / `model_secret_get` / `model_secret_status` and `model_secret_name` (one secret per provider, `model_anthropic_api_key`), the service role's only, on the Quo connection's shape. `model-config` is the owner's and a dev's door (`status`, `set`, `forget`); `_shared/modelKey.ts`'s `loadModelKey` reads Vault first, then `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`. Settings → Phone and texts → **Model keys** (`ModelKeysSettingsCard.tsx` over `models/modelKeys.ts` and `modelKeysIo.ts`), under Inboxes, shows the last four characters, when and by whom, with **Save the key** and **Forget the key**; anchor `settings-phone-texts-model-keys`, a dev-door row.

## Is this the best we can do

- The plan's transcription model (a second provider for the audio) is not built. The phone's own speech feeds the draft, so no audio leaves the phone for us and there is one key, not two. If the words come back wrong on site, a transcription model is the next cut.
- A draft costs about a cent and any report role may ask for one. There is no per-person cap; the function's log names each call.
