# v2.4540 — Put a GC on notice: see the jobs that look wrong and jump to one (2026-10-05)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. The owner's ask on the run window's jobs band: hovering *6 look wrong* should show the six, and clicking one should scroll to it and light it for a moment. One mock-up, built from RMC- Dudley Mason's six, then built the same day.

## What changed

- **The count is a button.** [`GcNoticeWrongList.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/GcNoticeWrongList.tsx) (new) draws *N look wrong ▾* in the band's head line. Pointing at it opens a list anchored under the words after 120 ms and closes it 220 ms after the pointer leaves the pair; a click pins it open (a touch screen and a keyboard have no hover); Esc, a click elsewhere or a pick closes it. Esc is taken in the capture phase, so it closes the list and not the window.
- **The list.** `gcNoticeBandWrongItems(band)` ([`gcNoticeJobsBand.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/gcNoticeJobsBand.ts)): each wrong job once, in the order the band draws its rows, with its stage, what is open and its readings (red before amber). A row reads *372 · Dudley Mason · $26,400 · Waiting · Waiting, but 80% done and a bill out*.
- **The jump.** Picking a job tells [`GcNoticeJobsBand.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/GcNoticeJobsBand.tsx) to unfold the table if it was hidden, then scroll the row to the middle of the window (`scrollIntoView`, instant under reduced motion) and mark it `data-flash` for 2.2 s: an amber edge and two pulses of amber (`tr.gcNoticeBandRow[data-flash]` in `index.css`; a steady tint under reduced motion).
- **Fit.** The list is as tall as the room under the words in the window's scrolling box, never under the window's foot, and scrolls inside itself past that.
- Not changed: the *N look wrong* words on each group's header row, the chips, the rows' own doors.

## Is this the best we can do?

Three things moved from the ask. A hover-only modal cannot be reached by touch or keyboard and blocks the table it points at, so it is a small list under the words that a click also opens. The list says what looks wrong and what is open, so a person can pick the worst one without jumping to each. And a pick opens a hidden table first, so the jump never lands on nothing.

## Tests

`gcNoticeJobsBand.test.ts` (+2): the order, each job once, red before amber, the other orders, the empty case. `GcOnNoticeModal.render.test.tsx` (+1): the list on hover, its rows against the table's, Esc closing only the list, a click pinning it, a pick unfolding the table, scrolling to the row and lighting that row alone. Seen in the dev preview on RMC- Dudley Mason: six jobs listed; picking 651 scrolled to its row and lit it; the light cleared.
