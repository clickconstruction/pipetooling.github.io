# v2.7980 — Documents: a Document builder tab, for a dev only (2026-11-19)

Punch list #404, step 1 of ten: the tab, with the spike's flow in it. Client only: no migration and no function. Nothing here reads or writes the database.

## What is new

- **Document builder** is the last tab on Documents (`?tab=builder`). Only the `dev` role sees it. Any other role that follows a link to it is shown Estimates, the page's opening tab (`shownDocumentsPageTab`).
- The tab lists the builders, one row each, with chips that say where each stands and an **Open** press. There is no **New builder** press: a new kind is added in code, in the registry.
- **Open** takes the tab's whole body and the address keeps which builder is open (`?tab=builder&builder=sub-nda`), so a reload and the browser's Back land where the person was. **‹ Document builder** goes back to the list. Another tab's press drops the builder from the address.
- The one builder today is the **Sub NDA**: the eleven-step flow the owner shaped as a spike on 2026-11-19, on a made-up job. Its chips read *On a made-up job*, *Nothing is saved* and *Not read by an attorney*, which is exactly where it stands.

The spike's files came across from the branch `spike/document-builder` by path and were renamed from `paperBuilder` to `documentBuilder` in folders, files and identifiers. The kernel's own word for one thing built from settings stays *paper* (`PaperDef`, `PaperSettings`). The spike's page at `/paper-builder` and its route did not come across: the flow opens inside the tab. The spike branch is not merged and stays as it is.

The tab is its own lazy chunk, so the flow and the made-up paper are not in the Documents page's bundle for the roles that never see the tab.

## The frame fits a phone

The spike's page measured where the frame starts and ended it at the bottom of the screen. Inside the tab that no longer held on a phone: a dev's tab row wraps to six lines at 375 px, the frame started 501 px down, and its least height put the **Next** press under the fold.

`src/lib/documentBuilder/frame.ts` now decides how the frame is sized. With 480 px or more under where it starts, it ends at the bottom of the screen and the page stays put, as on a computer. With less, it is sized to the screen from the builder's own top, and the open builder scrolls itself to the top of the screen once. The slider and **Next** are then in reach without a scroll.

## The hard lines, kept

- No clause wording is in the repo. A clause is drawn as grey bars.
- Every name is made up, and every meaning, breach line, site line and the disclaimer is a placeholder that the page marks as one. No attorney has read any of it.
- The review's press requires nothing of anyone.
