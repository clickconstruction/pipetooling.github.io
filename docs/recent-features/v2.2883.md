# v2.2883 — The sheet story: click the rail, one row per dot (2026-09-05)

PR 6 of the work-orders one-row-spine train (owner asked for it after the train shipped: "click on the rail and see a modal with the details of each of these for the sub laborer"; mock-up artifact `5de75e66`, approved as drawn).

## Kernel — [`src/lib/subWorkOrders/sheetStory.ts`](../../src/lib/subWorkOrders/sheetStory.ts) (9 tests)

`buildSheetStory({ sheet, money, coverage, rail, order, job, events, paperwork, portal, crewPay, todayYmd })` → seven `SheetStoryRow`s (four for crew pay): `key · label · state · office · chip · facts[] · sees · actions[]`. Facts are `{ k?, text, quote? }` lines; `sees` is the portal's own English sentence for the step (`SUB_SEES`); the first action is the primary button. Nothing new is stored — every line reads what already exists:

| Row | Reads |
|---|---|
| Drafted | the order's `created_at` / `created_by` / `amount`; "nothing drafted" + the handshake figure when there is no order |
| Sent | `offered_at`, `offer_expires_at`, expired; declined + `decline_reason` (quoted) |
| Signed | `signed_at` / `accepted_at`, portal vs paper, typed/drawn, `signer_printed_name`, `record_id`; **Binds under**: MSA signed date, General Conditions standing, COI expiry |
| Work | sheet date / created, line-item summary, days on the current dot |
| Walk-through | the Activity feed's `sub_stage_change` line to `walkthrough` (who, when, portal vs office) with the sub's note quoted; falls back to the sheet's own stage stamp |
| Customer pays | the Pipeline job's status, contract, bills out / paid; `payable_after` ("queued for the pay run") or `pay_hold_reason`; "not in the Pipeline" when the number has no job |
| Paid | every payment / back-charge with date and memo; "$X to go" or "paid in full" |

Day-only dates are formatted as calendar days (no UTC shift).

## Component — [`SheetStoryModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/SheetStoryModal.tsx)

Loads the sheet (items, payments, assignees), its orders (sheet-anchored + job-anchored), the Pipeline job (from the caller's list or one lookup by number) with its invoices, the feed's `sub_stage_change` events for the sheet (`detail->>source_id`), actor names, the single assignee's paperwork (`person_contract_documents`: MSA, COI) and portal link, and the roster (`useRosterSubKinds`) for the crew rule. Header: sub · job · Not in Pipeline / Crew pay / WO chips · Agreed · Paid · Open · sheet date · portal · the big rail. Actions: `draft` / `open_order` / `view_record` / `reoffer` open the assembler; `nudge` resends; `to_walkthrough` / `to_customer_pays` / `back_to_*` call `set_sub_sheet_stage`; `set_payable_after` is an inline date row writing `people_labor_jobs.payable_after`; `open_sheet` hands off to the caller. Escape closes; the assembler stacks above it.

## Doors

`SheetRail` gains `onClick`: the rail becomes a keyboard-reachable door (`role="button"`, Enter/Space), and the current-dot button stops propagation so Sub Labor's stage menu keeps its spot. Wired on **Jobs → Work Orders** (table + phone cards; `onSheetChanged` reloads the board), **Jobs → Sub Labor** (`SubSheetStageCell.onOpenStory`; `onReloadLaborJobs` from `Jobs.tsx` refreshes the ledger after a move), and the **sheet's Work order box** (`SubSheetWorkOrderPanel`, nested inside the editor).
