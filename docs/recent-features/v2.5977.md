# v2.5977 — A bid room chip no longer drags the Bid Board sideways (2026-10-27)

Fix, no migration, no edge function. Found on 2026-10-27 while testing the header search at the phone preset as the estimator twin (`/dev-login?as=twin:estimator`): `/bids?tab=bid-board` was wider than a 375 px screen and the whole page dragged sideways.

## Cause

Not the Health section's weekly chart. That SVG is 1,080 px wide (26 weeks × 40 px + 40), the widest element on the page, which is why it was suspected. It already sits in its own `overflow-x: auto` box, 341 px wide at 375. On the Electrical board, with the chart on screen, `scrollWidth` was exactly 375. Forcing that box to spill took the page to 1,097 px, and the header still kept its hamburger. Its `(max-width: 640px)` query reads the device width, not the page width.

The page was pushed by a GC line on a phone card (`BidBoardGcLines` in `BidBoardGcRows.tsx`). The line is one `nowrap` flex row: sent date (4.2rem) · state pill · *send to Dispatch →* on a won GC · GC name · notes badge · bid room chip. On the one card with a room chip (*rev 1 · opened 2× · 9/3*, 126 px) the fixed items came to 367 px on a 322 px line. The name, the only item that can shrink, went to 0 px and the chip still ran 45 px past the card. The page measured 393 px. The room chips load about 1.8 s after the rows, so the page is 375 wide until they land.

## Fix

- `BidBoardGcLines` takes `phone`, passed only by the phone card (`renderBidBoardCard`). On the phone the line is a wrapping row of two items. The first is the date, pill, Dispatch link, name and badge, still `nowrap` with the name ellipsizing. The second is the room chip. When the two fit, the line looks as before; when they don't, the chip drops to its own line, left-aligned under the date. The desk table's `dense` line is unchanged (same DOM).
- `BidRoomStateChip` takes `fit`: `max-width: 100%` with an ellipsis, so a label longer than the card (a signed room with a long option name, a CO suffix) can't push the page either. Only the phone card passes it.
- **Test**: `BidBoardGcRows.render.test.tsx` (new, 2). On the phone the chip is outside the no-wrap group, in a wrapping line, capped with an ellipsis; in the desk table it stays at the end of one no-wrap line. jsdom can't measure layout, so it pins the structure. It fails with the phone branch turned off.

## Verified

Dev server at the phone preset (375 × 812, mobile emulation), estimator twin, Plumbing, every section open and every capped list shown (290 cards):

- Before: `scrollWidth` 393, `innerWidth` 393; one element past the edge, the room chip (right edge 394).
- After: `scrollWidth` 375 = `innerWidth`; no element past the edge; header shows **Open menu** (phone form). The chip sits on the line under *General contractor*, and the name shows 88 of its 127 px instead of 0.
- Every card opened one at a time (290): nothing in an open card passes the edge. Electrical and HVAC boards: `scrollWidth` 375 before and after.
- The chip given a 418 px label in the page: it stops at the card edge (348 px) with an ellipsis, and the page stays 375.

## Left as is

- **The "Where everyone stands" band.** Name tags are centered on their marker, clamped to 4%–96% of the band. At 375 the right-most tag, a six-letter first name at the 96% clamp, ends at 374 px, 1 px inside. A longer name at that end, or a 320 px phone (page 321 px today), puts the page past the edge. Keeping the tags inside the band changes how a tag sits over its marker on desktop too, so it is a separate change.
- **The post-deploy phone smoke** (`e2e/viewport-smoke.spec.ts`) measures the Bid Board as soon as the words *Bid Board* show. The room chips land later, so a card like this one can pass it.
