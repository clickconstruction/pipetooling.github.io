# v2.7444 — Painted maps: the Share sheet says the right day a link was made (2026-11-16)

Punch list #345. Found on the live walk of v2.7415: a made-up map's link, made at 11:03 PM on Nov 15, read *One link is out, since Nov 16.*

## What changed

- **The sheet's *since* day is the company's day (Central),** as its end day already was. It was the day in UTC, cut from the row's time, so a link made in the evening read as made the next day. It had been so since the sheet first shipped in v2.7223.

## How it is built

- `linkMadeDayWords(madeAt)` in `src/lib/paintedMap/paintedMapLink.ts`, over `calendarYmdInAppTzFromIso`. The sheet calls it in place of `made_at.slice(0, 10)`. No other painted map screen cuts a day from a time.
