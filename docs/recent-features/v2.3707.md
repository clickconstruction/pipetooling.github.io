# v2.3707 — Contract amount: the job's number, read from its line items — never typed (2026-09-22)

PR 3 of the last four pieces in [`to-dos/contract-sweep-refresh`](../../to-dos/contract-sweep-refresh/README.md) — the owner, on a screenshot of the sweep's Amount box: *"if this number is set by the line items, perhaps we should not make it easy to change as a whole number and the user should have to go in and adjust line items."* Drawn 2026-09-21, redrawn in `final-pass.html` after asking whether that was the best we could do. Client only, no migration.

## What was wrong

The amount was set by the line items — `buildJobContractPrefill` took the accepted estimate's total, else `jobs_ledger.revenue`, which the Job form writes as the line items plus rider fees — and then offered a box. Typing in it changed only the agreement: nothing wrote back to the job and nothing compared the two, so a typed $120,000 was a signed contract for $120,000 on a job that bills $123,600, and no screen said so. The full editor had the same box one click away.

## The kernel — [`contractAmountSource.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/contractAmountSource.ts)

`contractAmountSource({ job, acceptedTotalCents, acceptedOn })` → `estimate` (the accepted total, with the acceptance date) · `line_items` (the job's revenue, with the count of named fixture rows) · `none`. `contractAmountSourceLabel` words it (*from the job's 14 line items* · *from the estimate the customer accepted Sep 12* · *the agreement says time and materials, billed at completion*), `contractAmountDoorLabel` names the one door (*Adjust line items ›* · *Open the job ›* · *Add line items ›*), and `contractAmountDrift` says when a draft carries something else. `buildJobContractPrefill` now reads its amount from the kernel rather than restating the order.

**No typed override.** The one case for one — an agreement for the original scope on a job whose line items grew through change orders — has not happened here; a reasoned *Agreement covers a different amount…* is easy to add later and hard to take away once people lean on it. If it is ever wanted, that is the shape.

## The sweep — [`JobsContractSweepModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobsContractSweepModal.tsx) · [`contractSweepRowState.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/contractSweepRowState.ts)

- **The Amount row is a read-out**: the number, its source, the door (`onEditJob` — the sweep stays put; Edit Job's save reloads the jobs and the pane re-reads). The row's amount column and the header's dollars are the job's numbers; `paneEdit` and the per-job overrides no longer carry an amount.
- **A draft typed earlier is a state, not a warning box.** The sweep now reads every gap job's live draft amount (one chunked `job_contracts` query beside the accepted estimates); `assessContractSweepRows` takes `draftAmountCents` and raises **`amount_differs`** (amber, *Amount differs*) when it is not the job's — a draft saying time and materials where the job has a number differs too. Such a row is not Ready, Send all skips it, `contractSweepFooterSentence` says both numbers (*This draft says $120,000.00, the job says $123,600.00 — use the job's number above*), and the primary waits. The pane's amber line names what the draft says and **Use the job's $123,600** puts the job's number on the draft (autosaved) — the flag clears and the row is Ready. Nothing is rewritten in silence: the draft keeps what it carries until that press.
- The accepted-estimate query now also reads `acceptor_consented_at`, for the source line's date.

## The full editor — [`JobContractModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobContractModal.tsx)

The same read-out (the hole would otherwise have moved one click away): the job's number and source while the row is editable, the amber *This draft still says …* line with **Use the job's $…** when the draft differs, and a new optional `onEditJob` prop for the door (wired from the sweep and from the Pipeline; callers without it read *change it on the job*). A sent agreement shows what it went out with, locked as before. The accepted estimate is read once per open regardless of a live row, so the source is known either way.

## Coverage

`contractAmountSource.test.ts` (the three sources and their words, the door, drift). `contractSweepRowState.test.ts` — `amount_differs`, its absence when the draft agrees or there is none, the sentence. The sweep's render tests: the read-out replaces the box on a thin row (typing the scope alone makes it Ready), and a fixture draft at $120,000 on a $123,600 job wears the chip, moves to Needs a look, blocks the primary, and one press saves the job's number and clears it.
