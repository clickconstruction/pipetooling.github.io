# v2.6867 — Lien desk: one press redraws every ready affidavit on the short form (2026-11-10)

The owner, 2026-11-10, once the short form (v2.6855) was live: the liens drafted that week go out on the new version. Twelve affidavits sat in Ready to file on the earlier form, each three presses from the short one. He asked for one press. Punch list #305.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/jobs/lienAffidavitRedraw.ts`** (pure). `lienAffidavitRedrawPlan(entries)`: `ready` is every affidavit in the Ready to file pile whose desk item is on the earlier form (`lienAffidavitFormForItem`), `held` the earlier-form papers in another live pile (the leader's queue, his hold, a draft). A filed or missed paper is in neither. `lienAffidavitRedrawWords` gives the button (*Redraw 12 on the short form…*, the count from `countedLabel`), the confirm window's title, message and `confirmLabel`, and the line that says what is left. `lienAffidavitRedrawOutcome` is `batchOutcomeWords` for the toast and the head over the papers that did not take.
- **`redrawLienAffidavitShort(item, userId)`** in `lienDeskIo.ts`: one update that sets the item back to `drafted`, clears the approval, the word, the hold and any signature as a pull-back does, and writes its fields with `affidavitForm: 'short'`. It matches only an affidavit still `drafted`, `awaiting_approval`, `approved` or `held`, and throws with the reason when no row moved (filed or taken back since the list was read). The pane's own **Redraw on the short form…** now calls it too: it was a pull-back and then a draft save, two writes that could leave a paper pulled back and unmarked.
- **`redrawLienAffidavitsShort(papers, userId)`**: each paper its own write, the batch keeps going past one that fails, and the failures come back with their reasons.
- **`LienDesk.tsx`**: a bar at the top of the Affidavits list (`data-lien-affidavit-redraw`), office roles, shown while a ready earlier-form paper exists or a failure is listed. The press opens the confirm window, runs the batch, toasts the outcome and refreshes the desk. A paper that did not take is listed with its reason and a door to its row.

## Not changed

- Approval is still one paper at a time. A redrawn affidavit lands in To draft; whoever approves it reads the short form first.
- Nothing is redrawn without the press. A paper approved on the earlier form keeps it until then.
