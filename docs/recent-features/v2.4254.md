# v2.4254 — Needs You says when typed hours wait on you; Looks right on hours typed onto approved time (2026-09-30)

Punch list #70, PR 3. [v2.4247](v2.4247.md) put the pencil on every approval list; this is what tells the second person there is something to look at, and the look for the one case an approval cannot cover.

## What changed

- **Needs You card `typed-hours`** ([`dashboardNeedsYou.ts`](../../src/lib/dashboardNeedsYou.ts), rank beside `hours-approvals`, amber): *Hours typed by hand want a second look — Taunya typed 11.0h for Michael A — time the clock did not record. Whoever typed hours cannot approve them, so they wait for you.* It counts only entries the viewer can act on (did not type them; not the viewer's own), has no age gate, and says when some were typed onto hours already approved. [`useTypedHoursWaitingNudge`](../../src/hooks/useTypedHoursWaitingNudge.ts) reads `list_typed_hours_waiting` for the roles that approve hours; the inputs are optional, so Quickfill's Needs You never names them.
- **Look at them** → `/people?tab=hours&approvals=1&typed=1`: the all-weeks queue opens on its **Typed by hand** filter (`startTypedOnly`).
- **Typed onto hours already approved** — a section at the top of the queue. An in-place time change on an approved session keeps its approval (and its hours in pay), so no approval ever looks at it; each row shows the person, the day, the stamp and *3.8h → 4.1h*, with **Looks right** (`confirm_clock_typed_entry`) for anyone but the typist and the person themself, the reason in its place for those two, and **Open day** (the clock-day editor) where the host can open one.
- Kernel: `parseTypedWaitingRow`, `typedWaitingAsEntry`, `summarizeTypedWaiting` in [`typedHours.ts`](../../src/lib/clock/typedHours.ts); loader [`loadTypedHoursWaiting.ts`](../../src/lib/clock/loadTypedHoursWaiting.ts).
