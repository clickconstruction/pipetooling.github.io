# v2.5823 — Quickfill: the Customers line leaves texts to the Texts station (2026-10-24)

Punch list #157's decision 3, the second PR of option A ([`to-dos/quickfill-texts-station/`](../../to-dos/quickfill-texts-station/README.md)), after v2.5816 put the day's text ledger on the Texts station. Until this PR a customer whose unanswered message was a text counted twice on Quickfill: on Needs you's **Customers** line (*N need a reply*, every channel) and on the Texts station's Needs a reply band, so the round's *left with work* said the same text twice and two stations asked for the same reply.

- **The kernel** (`followUp/customerCounts.ts`): `customerMessageCounts(queue, contacts, { textsElsewhere })` takes the ids of the lines that are texts and leaves out a Needs a reply row whose landing line is one of them; a customer who wrote an email, called, left a voicemail or wrote on their portal page still counts, and *we owe* and *to sort* are untouched. The set names lines, not people, so a customer's older text never hides their waiting email. One test.
- **The read and the hook** (`customerCountsIo.ts`, `useCustomerMessagesCounts`): a `textsElsewhere` flag builds the set from the queue's own lines (`channel = 'text'`, `direction = 'in'`); `useNeedsYouInputs` passes `host === QUICKFILL_CUSTOMER_TEXTS_HOST` (`needsYouSources.ts`, where the header says why the line cannot sit in `QUICKFILL_CARRIED_BY_STATION`: it is gated by `gcFollowUp`, not a source of its own). The Dashboard's line is unchanged, line for line.
- **Words**: the guide *work the Needs you list on the dashboard*, the Customers bullet; `docs/DASHBOARD_SECTIONS_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/QUICKFILL_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md` and `docs/GLOSSARY.md` amended; the to-do's status to built.

## Is this the best we can do?

One line of reason, one flag, no second read: the counts come off the same queue the line already built. Still open, named in v2.5816: Quickfill reads Follow up's queue twice on load, once here and once for the Texts station; one page-level read for both is the follow-up.

Quickfill as the dev or an assistant with a customer's text waiting: the Texts station's Needs a reply band holds it and Needs you's Customers line does not count it; the Dashboard's Customers line still does. With a customer's email waiting, both the line and Messages count it, and the Texts station does not.
