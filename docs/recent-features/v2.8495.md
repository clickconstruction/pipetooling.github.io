# v2.8495 — Painted maps: a buried line is drawn in its own color on the Google map (2026-11-24)

A fault found on punch list #432's walk in the iPhone simulator on 2026-11-24. The card is [`to-dos/photo-of-a-spot-whats-left.md`](../../to-dos/photo-of-a-spot-whats-left.md).

## What was wrong

v2.8203 gave a buried place a line of its own, drawn in the color a locator paints for what is buried: blue for water, green for sewer. The window hands that color to the canvas on each line (`PaintedCanvasLine.color`), and the Leaflet canvas reads it.

Since v2.8235 the place windows draw on Google. The Google canvas never read the color. It stroked every line with the one paint color, so a walked water line opened from **Open the line** was orange.

A second fault sat under the first. The canvas keeps a line it has drawn and draws it again only when its points, its unsure stretches or how it was made change. The job's places are read after the map, so a buried line reaches the canvas first with no color and then with one. A kept line was not drawn again for a color alone.

## What ships

In `PaintedMapGoogleCanvas.tsx`:

- **A line's own color is its paint.** `strokeLine` strokes a line that has a color with that color in place of the paint. The dark edge under it stays dark. The dots of a stretch the phone was not sure of take the color too, as on the Leaflet canvas.
- **A color is part of what a line is.** The kept-line check compares the color, so the line is drawn again when its color comes or changes, and not again when nothing changed.
- **The magnifying glass agrees.** Its drawing of each line reads the same color.

A line with no color of its own is the paint's orange, as before. The line in hand while it is drawn or walked is the paint's too: it is tied to its place only once its map closes.

## Not changed

The small orange swatch beside a painted map's name in a list is the mark of a painted map, not the color of its line. It stays orange.

## Where

## Tests

- `PaintedMapGoogleCanvas.render.test.tsx`: a line in a color of its own with its edge still dark and a plain line beside it still the paint's, a kept line drawn again when its color comes and not a third time, and the glass in the line's color. Each of the three fails on the old canvas.

## Verified

By the tests, and by a look on clicktooling.com on 2026-11-24 once the build stamped v2.8495 was live. As the dev, headless at 1200 px, on the ZZ Test job JP1113: a buried water place set by hand, a line of about 810 ft drawn from it, its map saved and closed, then **Open the line**.

- Every line Google was asked to draw was recorded in the page. On the open the line was asked for twice: first with the paint's `#ff5a1f`, and 281 ms later with `#2563eb`, each time over the same dark edge. The first is the line before the job's places are read. The second is the kept line drawn again for its color.
- On the map itself, 1,647 pixels along the line were within a shade of `#2563eb` and none were within a shade of `#ff5a1f`. Before the line was tied to its place, on the map just saved, the same stretch was orange, as built.
- The line's map and the job's list were thrown away after, each through its own confirm, and the job read back with no painted map.

Not looked at: a walked line, the other five colors, the magnifying glass, and a phone.
