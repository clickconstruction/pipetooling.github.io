# v2.2579 — Lien releases from the Stages board (2026-09-01)

Owner request (proposal approved 2026-09-01): issue the drafted waiver-and-release
family — Conditional/Unconditional on Progress Payment, Unconditional on Final
Payment — from the Jobs board, in-app, instead of round-tripping through
lientooling.com.

- **Kernel** `src/lib/jobsDocuments/lienWaiverRelease.ts` (+18 tests): pure
  paragraph model of the three owner-drafted forms → shared builders for
  clipboard HTML/text (`copyRichHtmlToClipboard`), a light-pinned print
  document, and a jsPDF letter (`loadJsPDF` block-walker, same pattern as
  `hazmatFeeNoticePdf`). Prefill maps: amount = open remaining on the selected
  bill lines (conditional/final) or applied payments (unconditional progress);
  through-date = newest `billed_at` → `last_work_date`; owner =
  `job_property_owners` → GC → `customer_name`; company = physical-invoice
  issuer → `ClickConstruction LLC`.
- **Modal** `src/components/jobs/LienReleaseModal.tsx`: form-type switcher,
  multi-select bill-line chips driving amount/through-date, editable fields,
  live serif preview (pinned light), Copy for email / Print / Download PDF.
  Signer prefills from the job master's People "Full name and title" line
  (same fallback as `LienToolingPrefillModal`).
- **Wiring**: `onOpenLienRelease` prop on `JobsStagesUnifiedTable` (blue
  `FileCheck2` button, first in the icon row, both job-bundle and
  standalone-invoice rows) + mobile action-sheet entry in
  `JobsStagesUnifiedCardList` ('Release of lien'). Passed on Ready to Bill,
  Billed Awaiting Payment, Collections, and the Follow-ups deck variants from
  `JobsStagesTab` (state + gate `canCreateHazmatFee` office set: dev /
  master_technician / assistant-like).

No schema changes. Tracking (`job_lien_releases`), the Bill Customer modal
strip, and the payment-cleared → unconditional nudge ship separately (phase 2
of the proposal).
