# v2.5127 — GC mode: close out a trade and close the job, for a dev (2026-10-09)

U6d of the Building lane's real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/building-u6.md`, on branch `spike/gc-mode`): the Closeout window over U6c's presses (`supabase/migrations/20261010041000_gc_trade_closeout.sql`), ported from the prototype's `GcCloseout.tsx`. A dev's on the money team while Building is built. No migration.

- **The Closeout window** (`src/components/gc/GcCloseoutWindow.tsx`), from **Closeout** after **Draws** on the card of a job being built or closed, at `closeout=<projectId>`, behind `canUseGcBuilding` and `canSeeGcMoney`:
  - the customer's retainage on us, and the day the trades' opens (`ownerRetainagePaidOn`, `tradeRetainageOpensOn`), with **Bill the customer** until they pay;
  - what we hold on the trades, what we paid back, how many are closed out, and the punch items open;
  - **Close the job** once `jobCloseout` is ready, with what is left in a list until then (`gc_close_job`);
  - a card per trade with a signed statement of work and its six steps (`tradeCloseout`): **Accept the work** held by its punch list in words (`gc_accept_work`), **Their final pay application came by email** (`gc_final_pay_app_came_in`; its money is the retainage held), **Approve the release** held until 10 days after the customer pays us and by the company's papers (`gc_approve_retainage`), **Mark paid** and **Their final release came in** (U6a's `gc_pay_draw` and `gc_draw_waiver_in`), and the final pay application to read (`GcDrawPayApp`).
- **The page** (`src/pages/GcProjects.tsx`) reads the customer's bills (`loadGcBillingRows`, `billingStateFor`) and the job's punch list when the window opens, and lays them over the board with the draws. Each press reads the draws again; an acceptance reads the board, and a closed job reloads the projects. Mark paid on a release emails the trade with the Draws window's tick (`paidEmail`).
- **The punch list on real data, read only** (`src/lib/gc/punchRows.ts`, `punchIo.ts`): `gc_punch_items` laid over each project's `punch`, so `punchCounts` and `punchWords` hold Accept the work as the SQL does. Its presses and window stay U3b's.
- **The board reads `gc_projects.closed_on`** into `GcProject.closedOn` (`loadGcBoardRows`' dates, `boardProjectFromView`), so a closed job sits with the closed jobs.
- **They signed it** in the Draws window: a change sent to the trade and signed on paper or by email, with its file and Drive link (`gc_trade_change_signed_in`, U6c's amendment 3). The board reads again, so the change's new line shows on their statement of work.
- **Docs**: the guide *close out a trade and close a GC job* (dev), *pay a trade's draw* gains They signed it, `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` (the Closeout window), `GLOSSARY.md` (closeout, retainage release), `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` (the window's gates).
- **Tests**: `GcCloseoutWindow.render.test.tsx` (13: each step's press, the punch hold, the final that came by email, the customer's payment and its 10 days, the job's close, plain words), `punchRows.test.ts`, `closeoutRows.test.ts`, two cases in `GcDrawsWindow.render.test.tsx`, four in `GcProjects.render.test.tsx`.
- **Not done**: the punch list's presses and window (the Building lane's U3b); the trade's own final pay application and waivers from its portal (the Portal lane's P5c); an email to the trade when we accept its work, asking for its final pay application as the prototype's portal to-do `todoFinal` does, and one when its final came in.
