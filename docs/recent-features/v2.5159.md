# v2.5159 — GC mode: a trade partner reports its work and signs its changes in its portal (2026-10-10)

P5c-3b of the trade partner portal's real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/PORTAL_REAL_BUILD.md`; plan `to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/portal-p5.md`, both on branch `spike/gc-mode`). No migration: U6a's verbs (`20261010021000_gc_trade_draws`) and P5c-m's ledger record types (`20261010071000`) are on prod.

- **The kinds** (`submit-gc-trade-portal`, `_shared/gcTradeSubmit.ts`): `sow_report` (`packageId`, `line`, `pct` from 0 to 100) on `gc_trade_sow_report`; `sign_change` (`changeOrderId`) on `gc_trade_sign_change`; `unconditional_waiver` (`drawId`) on `gc_trade_unconditional_waiver`, held (below). None is under the hourly cap.
- **The two signatures** do what `sign_sow` does around the write, typed only: no consent is `consentNeeded` before any write, the printed name is required (their verbs take none), and after the verb `recordEsignConsent` writes the ledger row with the function's time, method `type`, the IP and the browser. `TradeSignature` now carries the paper its row is keyed by: `gc_sow` (the statement of work), `gc_draw` (the draw) or `gc_trade_change` (the change order). `_shared/esignConsent.ts` and `useEsignConsent.ts` gain the two record types.
- **The keys**: `sowNotSigned`, `splitLine` and `notPaidYet` (409), with their words in English and Spanish, off the guard's waiting list. Four wait on P5c-3c: `drawWaiting`, `nothingToBill`, `finalSent`, `finalNotYet`.
- **The presses** (`GcTradePortalJob.tsx`): each line's percent is a picker by tens that never goes below what was billed, while we build the job; a split line is refused in `splitLine`'s words until the schedule's parts reach the portal (P5d). A change we sent shows what it adds or takes off, then **Sign the change**, `/contract/accept`'s form with `lockMode="type"`.
- **The waiver, built and held**: **Sign the unconditional waiver** on a paid draw with its conditional waiver (**Sign the final release** on the final draw) is drawn only when `WAIVER_SIGN_LIVE` in `drawEmail.ts` is true, and it is false. Its paper is the app's own: `tradeWaiverPaper.ts` fills the Release of Lien window's forms (`lienWaiverRelease.ts`, `unconditional_progress` or `unconditional_final`) from the draw, the company, the job and the day its pay application ran through, and the page lays it out as the window does, with the foot taking the typed name. Two plain lines (`uncondLead`, `uncondFinalLead`) sit above the paper as its explanation, never as the document. The function's copy of the flag (`_shared/gcTradeSubmit.ts`, held equal by a test) refuses the kind as `badRequest`, and a paid draw's email leaves out its waiver line.
- **`DRAW_PORTAL_LIVE` is true** (`drawEmail.ts`): a change's email goes, and the Draws window's change hint says they sign it in their portal. The send-back form's hint reads a new `PAY_APP_PORTAL_LIVE`, false until P5c-3c's pay application window, since the portal shows a sent-back pay application only then.
- **New words**: `errSowNotSigned`, `errSplitLine`, `errNotPaidYet`, `changeSignLead`, `changeSignAgree`, `changeConsentNoun`, and for the held waiver `uncondLead`, `uncondFinalLead`, `uncondSignLead`, `uncondFinalSignLead`, `uncondAgree`, `uncondFinalAgree`, `uncondConsentNoun`, `uncondFinalConsentNoun`, `signUncondFinal`, `uncondSigned`, `uncondFinalSigned`.
- **Tests**: `gcTradeSubmit.test.ts` (the three kinds, the ledger keys, no image and a blank name refused on the two signatures, the waiver's hold equal on both sides), `tradeWaiverPaper.test.ts` (both forms filled from a draw), `GcTradePortal.job.render.test.tsx` (the picker's floor, a split line's refusal, the change posting its consent, no waiver press while held, the preview), `GcTradePortal.waiver.render.test.tsx` (the flag turned on: the paper under the plain lines, the name on its foot, the press posting its consent), `drawEmail.test.ts`, `GcDrawsWindow.render.test.tsx`, `tradePortalSample.test.ts`.
- **Docs**: `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`, `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`, `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`; guides `share-a-trade-partner-its-portal`, `see-what-a-trade-partner-sends-from-its-portal`, `pay-a-trades-draw`. The guides say nothing of the waiver while it is held.
- **Deploy** (the lead): `submit-gc-trade-portal`, and for the type the six other importers of `_shared/esignConsent.ts` (`accept-contract`, `accept-estimate`, `sign-bid-room`, `sign-job-contract`, `sign-owner-records`, `submit-sub-portal`), as after P2c-ii.
