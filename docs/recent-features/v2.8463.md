# v2.8463 — A customer's Try again shows its check every time (2026-11-24)

Punch list #85. Two small faults that v2.8431 named and left (`docs/recent-features/v2.8431.md`, *What is left* and *Seen*), mended on Diane's yes of 2026-11-24. Client only: no migration and no edge function.

## What was wrong

**A press that looked dead.** On a page a customer opens from a link (`PublicPage`) that lost its code, every offline **Try again** asks the network (`probeForSignal`). With the network gone and the browser knowing it, the ask is refused in a millisecond or two. *Checking for a signal…* was on the page for 0.4 to 5.1 ms, and in most presses no frame drew it. The first press still changed the words, from *You are offline…* to *Still offline…*. Each press after it went from *Still offline…* to *Still offline…* with nothing a person could see. Seen again on the live site on 2026-11-24: 1.4 ms.

**Dark words on copper.** The app fades every button's fill over 0.12 s (`button` in `src/index.css`) and not its words. When the press went dead its words turned dark at once while its fill was still copper, and the two sat together for the length of the fade.

## What changed

- **The checking look stays half a second on a no.** `PUBLIC_PAGE_CHECKING_SHOWN_MS` is 500, and `publicPageCheckingHoldMs(tookMs)` in `src/lib/publicPageLoad.ts` says how much longer the look stays after a no that took `tookMs`. `PublicPageLost` keeps *Checking for a signal…* and the dead press for that long, then says *Still offline…* with the press live.
- **A yes is never held.** When the network answers, the page takes its loading look and reloads at once.
- **A slow no is not held twice.** A no that took 300 ms is held 200 more. A no that took the half second or longer, such as an ask given up at five seconds, is not held at all. The longest a press can take to answer is still five seconds.
- **The press changes in one step.** Its style carries `transition: 'none'`, so the dead look and the live look each arrive whole.

The ask is untouched: its address, what counts as an answer and its timeout. While the look is held the press is dead, so a press in that half second sends nothing. If the browser's `online` event comes while the look is held, the page reloads by itself as before. If the page goes while the look is held, its timer is cleared.

## Why half a second

It is long enough to read three words and see the press go grey and come back. It is a tenth of the ask's own limit. It was not asked of the owner as a number: Diane's yes was to hold the look for about half a second on a no.

## Seen

On 2026-11-24, on a throwaway harness page on the dev server (never committed, removed before the commit), with a headless Chromium at 390 by 844 and 1,280 by 800, in light and in dark, on the portal's paper and on the landing's photo and white box. A watcher inside the page timed each change of the words and read the press's colours at the moment of each change.

| The network | Presses | *Checking for a signal…* showed for |
|---|---|---|
| gone, and the browser knows it, the first press | 8 | 498 to 501 ms, about 60 frames |
| gone, and the browser knows it, the three presses after | 24 | 500 to 502 ms |
| a no that came after 300 ms | 2 | 500 ms in all |
| the ask never answered | 2 | 5.00 s, with nothing added |

- **The network gone.** The page read *You are offline…*, then *Checking for a signal…* with the press grey and disabled, then *Still offline…* with the press copper and live. Each later press drew the same checking look before it answered. Four presses sent four asks. The same document stayed and nothing was wider than the screen.
- **The phone wrong, the network there.** The page left for its reload 11 to 247 ms after the press, on 8 presses. Nothing was held.
- **The press's colours.** At the change to the checking look the press already read grey fill with dark words. At the change back it already read copper fill with light words. Before this PR the same reading at the change was copper fill with dark words.
