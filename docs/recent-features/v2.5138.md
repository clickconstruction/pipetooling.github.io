# v2.5138 — GC mode: a trade partner signs its statement of work in its portal (2026-10-09)

P2c-ii of the trade partner portal's real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/PORTAL_REAL_BUILD.md`; plan `to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/portal-p2c.md`, both on branch `spike/gc-mode`), on P2c-i's `gc_trade_sign_sow` (migration `20261010043000_gc_trade_sign_sow`). No migration.

- **The slice** (`gc-trade-portal`, `_shared/gcTradePortalSlice.ts`): `sowLines` (`id`, `sow_id`, `position`, `label`, `amount`, `scope_item_id`), only on the company's own statements of work that are not cancelled, and `excluded` on `sows`. The signer's fields never pass. The sample job carries two lines.
- **The mapper** (`tradePortalState.ts`): `sow.sov` from the lines in position order, each line's id its scope item or its own id (the kernels' `SovLine.id`, as U6's `gc_sow_line_of` reads it), `pctBilled` and `pctReported` 0 until U6; `sow.excluded` from the column; `sow.id` (a new optional field on `Sow`).
- **The kind** (`submit-gc-trade-portal`, `_shared/gcTradeSubmit.ts`): `sign_sow` with `sowId`, `printedName`, an optional `signaturePngBase64` and the required `esignConsent`. Not under the hourly cap. No consent is `consentNeeded` (400) before any write. A drawn PNG (512 KiB at most) goes to `contract-signer-signatures/gc-sows/<sow id>/<uuid>.png` and is deleted if the verb refuses. The verb gets the path, the IP and the browser; after it `recordEsignConsent` writes record type `gc_sow` with the verb's consent time. `gc_sow` joins `EsignConsentRecordType` in `_shared/esignConsent.ts` and `src/hooks/useEsignConsent.ts`.
- **The block** (`GcTradePortalSow.tsx`) fills the job's place in `GcTradePortalProject.tsx`: a draft reads `sowDraft`; a sent one the price, the lines and what it will and will not do, then `ContractAcceptSignatureForm` in the company's words, **Sign the statement of work**; a signed one its day. The button shows until B6-b-ii; the verb says `msaFirst` in words.
- **Words** (`portalI18n.ts`, EN and ES): `errSowNotSent`, `errAlreadySigned` ("This is signed already.", any paper), `errConsentNeeded`, and the form's `sowSignLead`, `sowSignAgree`, `sowConsentNoun`; `msaFirst` says `signMsaFirst`. `TRADE_ERROR_WORDS` maps the four keys, and the guard's WAITING loses P2c-i's three.
- **The flip**: `SOW_SIGN_SCREEN_LIVE` is true (`sowEmail.ts`), so **Send to their portal to sign** (shown to `canSend` since O9) offers **Email it now** to a reader who may email a trade (`canEmail`), a box that starts off.
- **Tests**: `gcTradeSubmit.test.ts` (typed, drawn, a PNG that does not decode, no consent), `tradePortalSlice.test.ts` (another company's and a cancelled one's lines never pass), `tradePortalState.test.ts` (lines and excluded), `tradePortalSample.test.ts`, `GcTradePortal.sow.render.test.tsx` (draft, sent, signed; the form needs a name and the consent and posts the body; `msaFirst` in words; the preview posts nothing), `GcTradeSow.render.test.tsx` and `sowEmail.test.ts` (the flip).
- **Docs**: `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` (both functions), `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`, `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`; guides `share-a-trade-partner-its-portal` and `award-a-trade-on-a-gc-project`.
- **New beside the plan's words and kinds**: three words for the form, `sowSignLead` (the disclosure), `sowSignAgree` (the agree box) and `sowConsentNoun` (the consent's noun), and an optional `id` on `Sow` (`gc_sows.id`), which the press names.
- **Deploy** (the lead, after the merge, all eight so `check:edge-drift` reads clean): `gc-trade-portal`, `submit-gc-trade-portal`, and the six other importers of `_shared/esignConsent.ts`, which changed by a type only: `accept-contract`, `accept-estimate`, `sign-bid-room`, `sign-job-contract`, `sign-owner-records`, `submit-sub-portal`.
