# v2.8560 — Task Mode: a task's lines name who hears for every helper (2026-11-24)

Punch list #369, from the walk in an iPhone simulator on 2026-11-25. A dev gave a ZZ test task to a test helper with **Tell me** lit. Read as that helper, the **Next** card had no *hears when it is done* line, and after **Mark done** the line read *Done* and the time alone. The task was saved as told when done. No migration, no edge function.

## The cause

The words were right and were handed no name. `Checklist.tsx` read the names of the people who hear, and of the people in a task's notes, straight from the people table. That table's read policy gives a helper, a sub or a superintendent their own row and the rows of most roles, but not a dev's row, and not a master's unless that master adopted them. So the read came back with no row, the map of names got no entry, and every line that names the giver fell back to nothing. It was the same on a real phone: the simulator was not the cause.

## What changed

- `src/pages/Checklist.tsx`: both reads of names go through `fetchUserDisplayNames`, the names function every signed-in person may call (`get_user_display_names`, on prod since v2.1652). It gives a name, a role and whether the person has left, and nothing else. The one map feeds the done line, the **Next** card's line before the start, the giver's name in the sheets of the doer's tools, and the writers' names in a task's notes, so all of them mend together.
- `src/lib/userDisplayNames.ts`, three small kernels:
  - `withPlainNames` folds the function's rows into the map. A row with no name still reads *Someone*.
  - `withArchivedIds` keeps the ids of the people who have left.
  - `toldName` gives the name for a line that says who is told: none for a person who has left.
- The names function answers for a person who has left, which the table never did for anyone but a dev. A note still names its writer when they have left. A line that says who hears, or who is told, names no one who has left, as before.

## What did not change

- A task with **Tell me** off reads no line about who hears.
- The words themselves (`doneLineWords`, `beforeStartWords`, `justDoneWords`).
- The guide *check off my daily tasks* already says the lines name who hears. It reads true now and was left as written.

## Tests

- `userDisplayNames.test.ts`: the three kernels.
- `Checklist.todayParts.render.test.tsx`: the people table answers no names and the names function does, as it is for a helper. A task with **Tell me** lit reads the giver's name on the **Next** card and on the done line. A task with it off reads neither. A giver who has left is not named.
- `Checklist.reviewLook.render.test.tsx`: the names function is on its list of reads, since the look at a person's Task Mode must write nothing.
